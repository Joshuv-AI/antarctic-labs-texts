Idempotent completion of cubes in posets
Christian Sattler

Abstract
. This note concerns the category □ \Box of cartesian cubes with connections, equivalently the full subcategory of posets on objects [ 1 ] n [1]^{n} with n ≥ 0 n\geq 0 . We show that the idempotent completion of □ \Box consists of finite complete posets. It follows that cubical sets, i.e. presheaves over □ \Box , are equivalent to presheaves over finite complete posets. This yields an alternative exposition of a result by Kapulkin and Voevodsky that simplicial sets form a subtopos of cubical sets.
