On aL(M)wc and oL(M)wc operators between Banach lattices


Abstract
We study almost L(M) weakly compact and order L(M) weakly compact operators in Banach spaces. Several further topics related to these operators are investigated.

2
aL(M)wc

The classes of almost L-weakly compact (
aLwc
) and almost
M-weakly compact (
aMwc
) operators were introduced in
[

]
.
They generalize L- and M-weakly compact operators.
Note that

2.1

We recall several facts on
aLwc
operators.
As weakly compact sets are bounded,
each
Lwc
operator is an
aLwc
operator.
By (
1
), if
S\in\text{\rm W}(Y,X)
and
T\in\text{\rm aLwc}(X,F)
then

TS\in\text{\rm Lwc}(Y,F)
.
Furthermore, each
aLwc
operator
T:X\to F
is bounded. Indeed, otherwise
there exists a norm null sequence
(x_{n})
in
B_{X}
satisfying
\|Tx_{n}\|\geq n

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
, violating the fact that the
Lwc
set

\{Tx_{n}:n\in\mathbb{N}\}
is bounded. Since
\text{\rm Lwc}(X,F)\subseteq\text{\rm W}(X,F)
,
the identity operator
I_{\ell^{1}}
is not
Lwc
.

2.2
Algebraic properties of
aL(M)wc
operators.

Lemma 2.2.1
.

The following holds.

(i)

if
T\in\text{\rm aMwc}(E,F)
and
S\in\text{\rm Mwc}(F,Y)
then
ST\in\text{\rm Mwc}(E,Y)
;

(ii)

if
T\in\text{\rm aMwc}(E,X)
and
S\in\text{\rm L}(X,Y)
then
ST\in\text{\rm aMwc}(E,Y)
;

(iii)

if
T\in\text{\rm aLwc}(F,G)
,
S\in\text{\rm semi-K}(E,F)
, and
F=F^{a}
then

TS\in\text{\rm Lwc}(E,G)
.

Proof.

(i) Let
T\in\text{\rm aMwc}(E,F)
and
S\in\text{\rm Mwc}(F,Y)
.
Then
T^{\ast}\in\text{\rm aLwc}(F^{\ast},E^{\ast})
by
[
8
, Thm.2.5]
,
and
S^{\ast}\in\text{\rm Lwc}(Y^{\ast},F^{\ast})
by
[
17
, Prop. 3.6.11 (i)]
,
and hence
S^{\ast}
is weakly compact.
Then
(ST)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}S^{\ast}\in\text{\rm Lwc}(Y^{\ast},E^{\ast})

by
[
8
, Thm.2.4]
.
Thus
ST\in\text{\rm Mwc}(E,Y)
by
[
17
, Prop. 3.6.11 (i)]
.

(ii) Follows directly from definition of
aMwc
operators.

(iii)
Since
S\in\text{\rm semi-K}(E,F)
, for each
\varepsilon>0

there is
x\in E_{+}
such that
S(B_{E})\subseteq[-x,x]+\varepsilon B_{F}
.
As
F=F^{a}
, the set
[-x,x]+\varepsilon B_{F}
is relatively weakly compact in
F
.
Since
T\in\text{\rm aLwc}(F,G)
, the set
T([-x,x]+\varepsilon B_{F})=TS(B_{E})

is
Lwc
. Hence
TS\in\text{\rm Lwc}(E,G)
.
∎

Proposition 2.2.2
.

For any Banach lattice
E
the following hold.

(i)

\text{\rm Lwc}(E)
is a closed subalgebra of
\text{\rm W}(E)
and is unital
iff the identity operator
I_{E}
is
Lwc
.

(ii)

\text{\rm aLwc}(E)
is a closed right ideal in
\text{\rm L}(E)

(
and hence a subalgebra of
\text{\rm L}(E)
)
, and is unital iff
I_{E}
is aLwc.

Proof.

It is easy to see that both
\text{\rm Lwc}(E)
and
\text{\rm aLwc}(E)
are
closed subspaces of
\text{\rm L}(E)
, and
\text{\rm Lwc(E)}\subseteq\text{\rm W}(E)
.
The closedness of
\text{\rm Lwc}(E)
under composition and
the fact that
\text{\rm aLwc}(E)
is a right ideal in
\text{\rm L}(E)
follow
from
[
8
, Thm.2.4]
.
The conditions on
I_{E}
, those make the algebras
\text{\rm Lwc}(E)
and
\text{\rm aLwc}(E)
unital,
are simply verified.
∎

.

Let

2.4

The next two theorems describes some conditions for

\text{\rm aMwc}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
and
\text{\rm aLwc}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Lwc}(E,F)
.

Definition 2.4.1
.

A Banach lattice
E
has
the positive Schur property (shortly,
E\in(\text{\rm PSP})
)
if positive
w
-null sequences in
E
are norm null (cf.
[

).

Theorem 2.4.2
.

Let
E^{\ast}
be a
KB
-space, let
F
be Dedekind complete,
and let
\text{\rm aMwc}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
. Then either

E\in\text{\rm(PSP)}
or
F=F^{a}
.

Proof.

Suppose
E\not\in\text{\rm(PSP)}
and
F\neq F^{a}
.
We construct a positive
aMwc
operator
from
E
to
F
that is not
Mwc
.
By assumption, there exists a disjoint w-null sequence
(x_{n})
in
E_{+}
with
\|x_{n}\|\not\to 0
.
WLOG,
\|x_{n}\|=1
for all
n
. There exists a sequence
(f_{n})
in
E^{\ast}_{+}
with
\|f_{n}\|=1
,
such that

|f_{n}(x_{n})|\geq\frac{1}{2}\ \ \ \ (\forall n\in{\mathbb{N}}).

(3)

Define a positive operator
T:E\to\ell^{\infty}
by

Tx:=[(f_{n}(x))_{n}]\ \ \ \ (\forall x\in E).

(4)

Since
F
is Dedekind complete
and
F\neq F^{a}
, there exists a lattice embedding

S:\ell^{\infty}\to F
(cf.
[
1
, Thm.4.51]
). Then, for some
M>0
:

\|Sa\|\geq M\|a\|_{\infty}\ \ \ (\forall a\in\ell^{\infty}).

(5)

Since
\ell^{\infty}\in(\text{\rm DPP})
(cf.
[
1
, Thm.5.85]
) then
S\in\text{\rm wDP}(\ell^{\infty},F)

[
1
, p.349]
, that is, for each
g_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}g
in
F^{\ast}

and each
y_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0
in
\ell^{\infty}
:

g_{n}(Sy_{n})\to 0.

Let
(z_{n})
be disjoint bounded in
E
and let
g_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}g
in
F^{\ast}
.
Since
E^{\ast}
has o-continuous norm,
z_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0

in
E
by
[
17
, 2.4.14]
. Then
Tz_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0
in
\ell^{\infty}
.
It follows from (
5
) that
g_{n}(STz_{n})\to 0
. Therefore,
ST\in\text{\rm aMwc}(E,F)
.
By utilizing (
3
), (
4
), and (
5
), we obtain

\|STx_{n}\|=\|S[(f_{k}(x_{n}))_{k}]\|\geq M\|(f_{k}(x_{n}))_{k}\|_{\infty}\geq M|f_{n}(x_{n})|\geq\frac{M}{2}>0.

(6)

It follows from (
6
) that
ST\not\in\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
, and we are done.
∎

(i)

If
F=F^{a}
and
F
has sequentially weakly continuous lattice operations, then

\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)\cap l\text{\rm-Mwc}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
.

(ii)

If
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space then
\text{\rm aDP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
.

Proof.

(i) Let
T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)\cap l\text{\rm-Mwc}(E,F)
.
Take a disjoint
(x_{n})
in
B_{E}
. We need to show
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
As
T\in l\text{\rm-Mwc}(E,F)
,
Tx_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0

in
F
, and by the assumption on
F
,
|Tx_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0
.
If
(f_{n})
is a disjoint bounded sequence in
F^{\ast}_{+}
, then

|f_{n}(Tx_{n})|\leq\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\cdot\|x_{n}\|
. As
\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\to 0
, then

T\in\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
by
[
11
, Cor.2.6]
.

(ii) Let
T\in\text{\rm aDP}_{+}(E,F)
.
Let
(x_{n})
be a disjoint sequence in
B_{E}
. We need to show
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
Since
E^{\ast}
is a KB-space,
x_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0
and hence

Tx_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{\rm w}}}{{\to}}0
in
F
. As
T\in\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
then
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
in
F
.
∎

Remark 4.1.2
.

In general, neither
l
-Lwc nor
l
-Mwc operators are weakly compact.
For example,
T=I_{\ell^{\infty}}
(and hence
T^{\ast}=I_{(\ell^{\infty})^{\ast}}
) is not weakly compact,
yet
T^{\ast}\in l\text{\rm-Lwc}((\ell^{\infty})^{\ast})
by
[
4
, Thm.2]
,
and hence
T\in l\text{\rm-Mwc}(\ell^{\infty})
by
[
4
, Thm.4]
.
Proposition
4.1.1
shows that those
l
-Mwc operators that are almost limited are weakly compact
as they are Mwc.

4.2

The next result shows the intimate relations between the operators that are considered.
We need the following definition.

Definition 4.2.1
.

A Banach lattice
E
has

(i)

the dual positive Schur property (or
E\in\text{\rm(DPSP)}
) if each positive

w
∗
-null sequence in
E^{\ast}
is norm null;

(ii)

the positive Grothendieck property
(or
E\in\text{\rm(PGP)}
) if each positive

w
∗
-null sequence in
E^{\ast}
is weakly null (cf.
[
22
, p. 760]
).

Theorem 4.2.2
.

Suppose
F=F^{a}
.

(i)

If
F\in\text{\rm(PGP)}
then

\text{\rm wDP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
for every
E
.

(ii)

If
E\in\text{\rm(DPSP)}

(
or
E^{\ast}\in\text{\rm(PSP)}
)
then

\text{\rm wDP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
.

(iii)

If
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space, then
\text{\rm wDP}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aMwc}(E,F)

and

\text{\rm DP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aLim}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
.

Proof.

(i) Let
T\in\text{\rm wDP}_{+}(E,F)
, and let
(x_{n})
be a disjoint w-null sequence in
E
.
We need to show
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
. By
[
11
, Cor.2.6]
, we must show
|Tx_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
and
f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0
for each disjoint bounded
(f_{n})
in
F^{\ast}_{+}
.
Since
x_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
and disjoint, then
|x_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
by
[
1
, Thm. 4.34]
.
As
T
is positive,
|Tx_{n}|\leq T|x_{n}|
, and hence
|Tx_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
.

Let now
(f_{n})
be a positive disjoint sequence in
B_{F^{\ast}}
. Since
F=F^{a}
,
f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0

and, as
F\in\text{\rm(PGP)}
,
f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
.
Since
T\in\text{\rm wDP}(E,F)
,
f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0
and, by
[
11
, Cor.2.6, Cor.2.7]
, we have
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.

(ii) Let
0\leq T\in\text{\rm wDP}(E,F)
, and let
(x_{n})
be a w-null disjoint sequence in
E
.
We must show
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
As
(x_{n})
is disjoint w-null,
|x_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
by
[
1
, Thm. 4.34]
.
Then
|Tx_{n}|\leq T|x_{n}|
and thus
|Tx_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
.
Let
(f_{n})
be disjoint in
B_{F^{\ast}}
.
Since
F=F^{a}
,
f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
by
[
17
, Cor. 2.4.3]
,
and hence
T^{\ast}f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
in
E^{\ast}
.
As
E\in\text{\rm(DPSP)}
,
\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\to 0
.
It follows from
|f_{n}(Tx_{n})|\leq\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\cdot\|x_{n}\|
that
T\in\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
.

The case
E^{\ast}\in\text{\rm(PSP)}
is similar and is omitted.

(iii) Let
T\in\text{\rm wDP}(E,F)
.
Take a disjoint
(x_{n})
in
B_{E}
and w-null
(f_{n})
in
F^{\ast}
.
As
x_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
, we have
f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0
by definition of
wDP
-operators.
Thus,
T\in\text{\rm aMwc}(E,F)
by
[
14
, Prop. 2]
.

To prove the second inclusions, first suppose
0\leq T\in\text{\rm DP}(E,F)
.
Let
f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
be disjoint in
B_{F^{\ast}}
.
Then
|f_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
.
As
T
is positive,
|T^{\ast}f_{n}|\leq T^{\ast}|f_{n}|
, and hence
|T^{\ast}f_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
.
Let
(x_{n})
be a disjoint bounded sequence in
E_{+}
.
As
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space,
|x_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
, and therefore
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
By
[
11
, Cor.2.6]
,
T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)
as
\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\to 0
.

Now let
0\leq T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)
. To see
T\in\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
,
take a disjoint w-null sequence
(x_{n})
in
E
(then
|x_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
by
[
1
, Thm. 4.34]
).
We need to show
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
By
[
11
, Cor.2.6, Cor.2.7]
, it suffices to show that

f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0
for every disjoint
(f_{n})
in
(B_{F^{\ast}})_{+}
.
Let
(f_{n})
be disjoint in
(B_{F^{\ast}})_{+}
.
Since
F=F^{a}
,
f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
by
[
17
, Cor. 2.4.3]
.
Since
|x_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
then
T|x_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
,
and hence
|Tx_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
because
|Tx_{n}|\leq T|x_{n}|
.
Since
T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)
, then
\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\to 0
.
It follows from
|f_{n}(Tx_{n})|\leq\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\cdot\|x_{n}\|
that
f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0
, as desired.
∎

Corollary 4.2.3
.

Let
E^{\ast}
and
F
have order continuous norm.
Then the following holds.

(i)

\text{\rm aLwc}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
.

(ii)

\text{\rm DP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
and

\text{\rm aLim}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
.

(iii)

If
F
has
w
-sequentially continuous lattice operations, then

\text{\rm aLwc}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)
.

(iv)

If
E\in\text{\rm(SP)}
, then

\text{\rm oLwc}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm semi-K}(E,F)
.

Proof.

(i) By
[
8
, Prop. 2.3]
,
\text{\rm aLwc}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
.
On the other hand, as
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space,

\text{\rm aDP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm Mwc}(E,F)

by
[
7
, Prop. 2.1]
.

(ii) By
[
7
, Prop. 2.2]
, order bounded
\text{\rm aDP}(E,F)
are
Mwc
.
If, in addition,
F=F^{a}
, then

\text{\rm DP}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aLim}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm aDP}_{+}(E,F)

by Proposition
4.2.2
(iv).

(iii) Let
T\in\text{\rm aLwc}(E,F)
and let
(x_{n})
be a disjoint sequence in
B_{E}
. We need to show
\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
As
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space,
x_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
and
Tx_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
.
Since
F
has weak sequentially continuous lattice operations,

|Tx_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
.
By
[
11
, Cor. 2.6]
, it remains to show
f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0

for each disjoint bounded
(f_{n})
in
F^{\ast}_{+}
.
This holds by the definition of aLwc operators.

(iv) Let
T\in\text{\rm oLwc}_{+}(E,F)
.
Let
(f_{n})
be a disjoint w
∗
-null sequence in
B_{F^{\ast}}
.
Then
|T^{\ast}f_{n}|\stackrel{{\scriptstyle w^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
. If
(x_{n})
is a disjoint bounded
sequence in
E_{+}
,
x_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle w^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
as
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space
and
f_{n}(Tx_{n})=T^{\ast}f_{n}(x_{n})\to 0
, and
|f_{n}(Tx_{n})|\leq\|f_{n}\|\cdot\|Tx_{n}\|\to 0
.
∎

4.3

We finish with weak compactness of limitedly Lwc operators.
Let
T\in l\text{\rm-Lwc}_{+}(E,F)
.
Since
T(E)\subseteq F^{a}
, we may assume
T
takes values
in a Banach lattice with order continuous norm (see proof of Proposition
3.3.2
(i)). This being the case, if
T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)

then
T\in\text{\rm Lwc}_{+}(E,F)
and is weakly compact.

Proposition 4.3.1
.

Let
E^{\ast}
be a
KB
-space,
E\in\text{\rm(w${}^{\ast}$DPP)}
,
and
F\in\text{\rm(d)}
.
Then
l\text{\rm-Lwc}_{+}(E,F)\subseteq\text{\rm W}(E,F)
.

Proof.

Let
0\leq T\in l\text{\rm-Lwc}(E,F)
.
First, we show
T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)
.
By
[
12
, Thm. 3]
, it suffices to show
\|T^{\ast}f_{n}\|\to 0

for each positive disjoint w
∗
-null sequence in
B_{F^{\ast}}
.
Let
(f_{n})
be such a sequence.
Then
|T^{\ast}f_{n}|=T^{\ast}f_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm w}^{\ast}}}{{\to}}0
.
By
[
11
, Cor. 2.6]
, it remains to show
f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0

for each disjoint bounded sequence
(x_{n})
in
E_{+}
.
Let
(x_{n})
be such a sequence.
Since
E^{\ast}
is a
KB
-space,
x_{n}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rm w}}{{\to}}0
. Then
T^{\ast}f_{n}(x_{n})=f_{n}(Tx_{n})\to 0

as a Banach lattice with w
∗
DPP are exactly those
for which
f_{n}(x_{n})\to 0
for each w
∗
-null
(f_{n})

in
E^{\ast}
and w-null
(x_{n})
in
E
.
Thus
T\in\text{\rm aLim}(E,F)
.
Since
T\in l\text{\rm-Lwc}(E,F)
,
T(E)\subseteq F^{a}
by
[
4
, Thm. 2]
.
Then
T\in\text{\rm Lwc}(E,F)
(see
[
10
, Thm. 2.6]
), and hence
T
is weakly compact.
∎
