Abstract
. We prove a "height-free" effective isogeny estimate for abelian varieties of GL 2 \mathrm{GL}_{2} -type. Sketch of proof of Lemma

By the Dickson classification of subgroups of

is absolutely irreducible, has projective image of size

Showing that

is absolutely irreducible for

Suppose

are subquotients of the representation

of characters corresponding to CM

Lemma 5.5

Let

So we conclude that either

or

or

The map

but
\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathfrak{o}/\mathfrak{p}_{j})

\prod_{j=1}^{i}\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathfrak{o}/\mathfrak{p}_{j})\longrightarrow\mathrel{\mkern-25.0mu}\longrightarrow\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathfrak{o}/\mathfrak{p}_{i+1})/N^{\prime}

we have that
\mathbb{Q}_{p}[\rho_{A,p}(\mathop{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/K))]=M_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})
.

(In case
A/K
is a fake elliptic curve we are implicitly taking
C_{K,A}
so large that
p\geq C_{K,A}
implies that
\text{End}_{K}^{0}(A)\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{Q}_{p}\simeq M_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})
, whence we may write
\rho_{A,p}:\mathop{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/K)\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
.)

Since the argument is essentially Serre’s we will be quite brief.

Sketch of proof of Theorem
A.2
.

Suppose otherwise. Then
\mathbb{Q}_{p}[\rho_{A,p}(\mathop{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/K))]\subseteq M_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})
is abelian. Thus
(\rho_{A,p}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p})^{\mathrm{s.s.}}\simeq\alpha\oplus\beta
with
\alpha,\beta:\mathop{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/K)\longrightarrow\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}^{\times}
characters. Said characters automatically arise from
p
-divisible groups since they are subquotients of
\rho_{A,p}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}
, so they are
p
-adic realizations of algebraic Hecke characters. Thus by Brauer-Nesbitt we see that
(\rho_{A,\ell}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})^{\mathrm{s.s.}}\simeq\alpha\oplus\beta
(where now
\alpha
and
\beta
denote the corresponding
\ell
-adic realizations) for all large
\ell
, so without loss of generality by changing
p
we may assume that
\alpha,\beta:\mathop{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/K)\longrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}
and
0\longrightarrow\alpha\longrightarrow\rho_{A,p}\longrightarrow\beta\longrightarrow 0
. Now considering the inertial restrictions of
\alpha
and
\beta
at primes above
p
we see that either
\beta|_{I_{\mathfrak{q}}}=\mathrm{triv}
for all
\mathfrak{q}|(p)
and thus
|\beta(\mathfrak{m})|=1
for all large primes
\mathfrak{m}\subseteq\mathfrak{o}_{K}
, contradicting Weil, or else for some
\mathfrak{q}|(p)
the sequence
0\longrightarrow\alpha|_{I_{\mathfrak{q}}}\longrightarrow\rho_{A,p}|_{I_{\mathfrak{q}}}\longrightarrow\beta|_{I_{\mathfrak{q}}}\longrightarrow 0
splits (via the connected-étale sequence), whence by Serre-Tate
A/K_{\mathfrak{q}}
is CM, another contradiction.
∎
