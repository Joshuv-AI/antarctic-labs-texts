End of the world brane in double scaled SYK
Kazumi Okuyama

Abstract
We study the end of the world (EOW) brane in double scaled SYK (DSSYK) model. We find that the boundary state of EOW brane is a coherent state of the q q -deformed oscillators and the associated orthogonal polynomial is the continuous big q q -Hermite polynomial. In a certain scaling limit, the big q q -Hermite polynomial reduces to the Whittaker function, which reproduces the wavefunction of JT gravity with an EOW brane. We also compute the half-wormhole amplitude in DSSYK and show that the amplitude is decomposed into the trumpet and the factor coming from the EOW brane.

7
Conclusions and outlook

In this paper, we have studied the EOW brane in DSSYK.
We found that the boundary state of EOW brane is a coherent state
of the
q
-deformed oscillator and the associated
orthogonal polynomial is the continuous big
q
-Hermite polynomial
H_{n}(x,a|q)
.
We showed that
\psi_{n}(\theta)
in (
49
) reduces to the
Whittaker function in the triple scaling limit (
52
).
We also computed the half-wormhole amplitude in DSSYK
and found the decomposition (
91
) into the trumpet and the factor

\frac{a^{\mathfrak{b}}}{1-q^{\mathfrak{b}}}
,
which is a complete parallel of the decomposition
of half-wormhole amplitude
(
46
) in JT gravity.

There are many interesting open questions.
Recall that
(L,b)
and
(n,\mathfrak{b})
are related by
(see (
52
) and (
100
))

\displaystyle L=\lambda n+\log\lambda,\quad b=\lambda\mathfrak{b}.

(104)

Thus the bulk geodesic lengths
L
and
b
are quantized in units of
\lambda

and there is a minimal length
\lambda
in the bulk spacetime.
It would be interesting to understand the physical
meaning of this minimal length.
9
9

9

In
Berkooz:2022mfk
it is discussed
that the bulk spacetime of DSSYK becomes a non-commutative
AdS_{2}

with a minimal length scale
\lambda
.

We can generalize our setup by introducing two EOW branes and
consider the amplitude

\displaystyle\frac{\langle B_{a}|e^{\beta T}|B_{b}\rangle}{\langle B_{a}|B_{b}\rangle}=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)\frac{\langle B_{a}|\theta\rangle\langle\theta|B_{b}\rangle}{\langle B_{a}|B_{b}\rangle}e^{-\beta E(\theta)}.

(105)

Then the measure factor becomes

\displaystyle\mu(\theta)\frac{\langle B_{a}|\theta\rangle\langle\theta|B_{b}\rangle}{\langle B_{a}|B_{b}\rangle}=\mu(\theta)\frac{(ab;q)_{\infty}}{(ae^{\pm\mathrm{i}\theta},be^{\pm\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}},

(106)

and the associated orthogonal polynomial is the Al-Salam-Chihara polynomial

Q_{n}(x,a,b|q)
.
This type of boundary condition has been considered
in
sasamoto1999one
in the context of ASEP
(asymmetric simple exclusion process)
under the identification

\displaystyle\langle W|=\langle B_{a}|,\quad|V\rangle=|B_{b}\rangle.

(107)

It would be interesting to pursue this analogy with ASEP further.
One missing ingredient in this analogy is
the relation to the spin chain.
In the context of ASEP, the problem can be mapped to the matrix product state
of open XXZ spin chain and the quantum group naturally arises in the spin
chain picture (see e.g.
Crampe:2014aoa
for a review).
It would be interesting to understand the role of spin chain in DSSYK, if any.

As discussed in
Jafferis:2022wez
, one can construct various amplitudes
of DSSYK by gluing basic building blocks such as the trumpet, in a similar manner as the JT gravity matrix model
Saad:2019lba
.
However, we still do not have a complete set of the “Lego block” of
DSSYK. For instance, we do not know the DSSYK analogue of the Weil-Petersson
volume, which played an important role for the construction of higher genus amplitudes
of JT gravity.
Also, we have only briefly studied the effect of matter loop in section
6
.
Clearly, more work needs to be done to better understand
the structure of DSSYK.

\displaystyle(ae^{\mathrm{i}(\pm\theta_{1}\pm\theta_{2})};q)_{\infty}=\prod_{s_{1},s_{2}=\pm}(ae^{\mathrm{i}s_{1}\theta_{1}+\mathrm{i}s_{2}\theta_{2}};q)_{\infty}.

(110)

The

-binomial coefficient is defined by

\displaystyle\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\

Continuous big
q
-Hermite polynomials

We summarize some useful properties of the
continuous big
q
-Hermite polynomial
H_{n}(x,a|q)
.

H_{n}(x,a|q)
is defined recursively by the three-term recurrence relation

\displaystyle 2xH_{n}(x,a|q)=H_{n+1}(x,a|q)+aq^{n}H_{n}(x,a|q)+(1-q^{n})H_{n-1}(x,a|q),

(116)

with the initial condition
H_{-1}(x,a|q)=0,H_{0}(x,a|q)=1
.
When
a=0
, the continuous big
q
-Hermite polynomial
reduces to the
q
-Hermite polynomial

\displaystyle H_{n}(x,0|q)=H_{n}(x|q).

(117)

Setting
x=\cos\theta
, we can write down the explicit form of

H_{n}(x,a|q)

\displaystyle H_{n}(\cos\theta,a|q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}(ae^{\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{k}e^{\mathrm{i}(n-2k)\theta}

(118)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}(-a)^{k}q^{\frac{1}{2}k(k-1)}H_{n-k}(\cos\theta|q).

When
a=0
, the first line of (
118
) reduces to

\displaystyle H_{n}(\cos\theta|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}e^{\mathrm{i}(n-2k)\theta}.

(119)

An important property of
H_{n}(x,a|q)

is the orthogonality relation

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\frac{\mu(\theta)}{(ae^{\pm\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}}H_{n}(x,a|q)H_{m}(x,a|q)=(q;q)_{n}\delta_{n,m},

(120)

where
\mu(\theta)=(q,e^{\pm 2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}
.
The generating function
of
H_{n}(x,a|q)
is given by

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}H_{n}(\cos\theta,a|q)=\frac{(ta;q)_{\infty}}{(te^{\pm\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}}.

(121)

As a special case of this relation, we find the generation function of
the
q
-Hermite polynomial

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}H_{n}(\cos\theta|q)=\frac{1}{(te^{\pm\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}}.

(122)

Also note that
H_{n}(x,a|q)
satisfies a
q
-difference equation for the parameter

a

floreanini1995algebraic

\displaystyle H_{n}(x,aq|q)-H_{n}(x,a|q)=a(1-q^{n})H_{n-1}(x,aq|q).

(123)

The product of
q
-Hermite polynomials is expanded as
(see e.g.
ismail1988askey
)

\displaystyle H_{n}(x|q)H_{m}(x|q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\min(n,m)}\frac{(q;q)_{n}(q;q)_{m}}{(q;q)_{n-k}(q;q)_{m-k}(q;q)_{k}}H_{n+m-2k}(x|q),

(124)

which is called the linearization formula.
