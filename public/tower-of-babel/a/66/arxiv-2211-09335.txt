Pseudonorms and p p -adic birational Torelli theorem
Chen-Yu Chi

Abstract
. A p p -adic analogue of the pseudonorm version of the birational Torelli type theorem is obtained via a comparison theorem of image closures. Among other results obtained, we have a criterion for existence of rational points of canonically polarized surfaces over finite fields.
