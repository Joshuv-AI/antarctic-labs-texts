Random Matrices and Quantum Hamilton-Jacobi Method
K. Haritha and K.V.S.Shiv Chaitanya 1

Abstract
In this paper, we start with the quantum Hamilton-Jacobi approach and show that the underlying complex pole evolution of the Schrödinger equation is described by the quantum action in terms of a random matrix. The wave function is given by the random matrix probability distribution function. In literature this is known as the famous Cole-Hopf Transformation.
