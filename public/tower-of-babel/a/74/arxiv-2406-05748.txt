Strong stability from vertex-extendability and applications in generalized Turán problems
Wanfang Chen, Xizhi Liu

Abstract
Extending the work of Liu–Mubayi–Reiher [ LMR23 ] on hypergraph Turán problems, we introduce the notion of vertex-extendability for general extremal problems on hypergraphs and develop an axiomatized framework for proving strong stability for extremal problems satisfying certain properties. This framework simplifies the typically complex and tedious process of obtaining stability and exact results for extremal problems into a much simpler task of verifying their vertex-extendability. We present several applications of this method in generalized Turán problems including the Erdős Pentagon Problem, hypergraph Turán-goodness, and generalized Turán problems of hypergraphs whose shadow is complete multipartite. These results significantly strengthen and extend previous results of Erdős [ Erd62 ] , Győri–János–Simonovits [ GPS91 ] , Grzesik [ Grz12 ] , Hatami–Hladký–Kráľ–Norine–Razborov [ HHK+13 ] , Morrison–Nir–Norin–Rzążewski–Wesolek [ MNN+23 ] , Gerbner–Palmer [ GP22 ] , and others. Keywords: vertex-extendability, degree-stability, generalized Turán problems, the Erdős Pentagon Problem.

Given an

,

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
\Gamma\colon\mathfrak{G}^{r}\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
be a

(\delta_{\ast},c_{\ast},C_{\ast})
-smooth, locally

Let us prove some preliminary results before presenting the proof of Theorem

Fact 3.2
.

Let
\Gamma\colon\mathfrak{G}^{r}\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
be a
k
-uniform,
(\delta_{\ast},c_{\ast},C_{\ast})
-smooth, locally monotone map with
k,\delta_{\ast},c_{\ast},C_{\ast}>0
.

(i)

If
\mathcal{H}
is an
n
-vertex
r
-graph with
\Gamma(\mathcal{H})>0
, then for every set

we have, by the locally monotone property, that

\displaystyle\Gamma(\mathcal{H}-S)\geq\Gamma(\mathcal{H})-\sum_{v\in S}d_{\Gamma,\mathcal{H}}(v).

(ii)

For every

, we have, by the smoothness, that

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n-\delta n)\leq\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)+o(\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)).

(iii)

For every

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)}{n}=(1-\delta)\cdot\frac{\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)}{n-\delta n}\geq(1-\delta-o(1))\cdot\frac{\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n-\delta n)}{n-\delta n}.

The following lemma gives an upper bound for the size of

in near extremal
r

Lemma 3.3
.

Let
\Gamma\colon\mathfrak{G}^{r}\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
be a
k
-uniform,
(\delta_{\ast},c_{\ast},C_{\ast})
-smooth, locally
C
-Lipschitz, continuous, and locally monotone map with

\delta_{\ast}\leq\left(\frac{k}{2C_{\ast}}\right)^{2/c_{\ast}}

Let
\varepsilon\in(0,\delta_{\ast}^{\frac{2+c_{\ast}}{2}})
be a real number and
n
be sufficiently large integer.
Suppose that
\mathcal{H}
is an
n
-vertex
r
-graph with
\Gamma(\mathcal{H})\geq(1-\varepsilon)\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)
.
Then for every
\delta_{1}\in[2\varepsilon^{\frac{c_{\ast}}{2+c_{\ast}}},2\delta_{\ast}^{\frac{c_{\ast}}{2}}]
, the set
Z_{\Gamma,\delta_{1}}(\mathcal{H})
has size at most
(\delta_{1}/2)^{\frac{2}{c_{\ast}}}n
.

Proof.

Fix
\delta_{1}\in[2\varepsilon^{\frac{c_{\ast}}{2+c_{\ast}}},2\delta_{\ast}^{\frac{c_{\ast}}{2}}]
.
Let
\delta_{2}\coloneqq(\delta_{1}/2)^{\frac{2}{c_{\ast}}}
.
Simple calculations show that
\delta_{1}\geq 2\delta_{2}^{\frac{c_{\ast}}{2}}
,
k\delta_{1}\delta_{2}/2\geq\varepsilon
, and
\delta_{2}\leq\delta_{\ast}
.
Let
V\coloneqq V(\mathcal{H})
,
Z\coloneqq Z_{\Gamma,\delta_{1}}(\mathcal{H})
, and
U\coloneqq V\setminus Z
.
Suppose to the contrary that
|Z|\geq\delta_{2}n
.
Let
Z^{\prime}\subset Z
be a subset of size
\delta_{2}n
.
It follows from Fact
3.2

(i)
that

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n-\delta_{2}n)\geq\Gamma(\mathcal{H}[U])

\displaystyle>\Gamma(\mathcal{H})-\delta_{2}n\times(1-\delta_{1})k\cdot\frac{\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)}{n}

\displaystyle=\Gamma(\mathcal{H})-\delta_{2}k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)+\delta_{2}\delta_{1}k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)

\displaystyle\geq\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)-\delta_{2}k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)+\left(\delta_{1}\delta_{2}k-\varepsilon\right)\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)

\displaystyle\geq\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)-\delta_{2}k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)+\delta_{2}^{1+\frac{c_{\ast}}{2}}k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)

\displaystyle\geq\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)-\delta_{2}k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)+2C_{\ast}\delta_{2}^{1+c_{\ast}}\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)

contradicting the smoothness of
\Gamma
.

Lemma 3.4
.

Let
\Gamma\colon\mathfrak{G}^{r}\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
be a
k
-uniform,
(\delta_{\ast},c_{\ast},C_{\ast})
-smooth, locally
C
-Lipschitz, continuous, and locally monotone map with
k,\delta_{\ast},c_{\ast},C,C_{\ast}>0
.
Let
\mathfrak{H}
be a hereditary family of
r
-graphs.
Suppose that
\varepsilon>0
is a sufficiently small constant and
\Gamma
is
(3\varepsilon,N_{0})
-vertex-extendable with respects to
\mathfrak{H}
.
Then the following holds for sufficiently large

If an
n
-vertex
r
-graph
\mathcal{H}
with
\delta_{\Gamma}(\mathcal{H})\geq(1-\varepsilon)\cdot\mathrm{exdeg}_{\Gamma}(n)
satisfies
\mathcal{H}-S\in\mathfrak{H}
for some set

of size at most
\varepsilon n/C
, then
\mathcal{H}\in\mathfrak{H}
.

Proof.

Choose a minimal set
S^{\prime}\subset S
with
\mathcal{H}-S^{\prime}\in\mathfrak{H}
.
If
S^{\prime}=\emptyset
, then we are done.
So we may assume that
S^{\prime}\neq\emptyset
.
Fix
v\in S^{\prime}
.
Let
S^{\prime\prime}\coloneqq S^{\prime}\setminus\{v\}
and
\mathcal{H}^{\prime\prime}\coloneqq\mathcal{H}-S^{\prime\prime}

v(\mathcal{H}^{\prime\prime})\geq n-|S|\geq(1-\varepsilon/C)n\geq n/2\geq N_{0}
and by the local

\displaystyle\delta(\mathcal{H}^{\prime\prime})

\displaystyle\geq\delta(\mathcal{H})-\frac{C|S|k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)}{n^{2}}-o(\mathrm{exdeg}_{\Gamma}(n))

\displaystyle\geq(1-\varepsilon)k\cdot\frac{\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)}{n}-\frac{C|S|k\cdot\mathrm{ex}_{\Gamma}(n)}{n^{2}}-o(\mathrm{exdeg}_{\Gamma}(n))

Proof of Lemma

|\mathcal{H}^{\prime}|\geq|\mathcal{H}|-r\left(v(F)\right)^{2}n^{r-1}=|\mathcal{H}|-o(n^{r})
and
\partial_{r-2}\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
is
F
-free.
In particular,

\displaystyle\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H}^{\prime})\geq\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H})-|\mathcal{H}\setminus\mathcal{H}^{\prime}|n^{v(Q)-r}=\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,H_{F}^{r})-o(n^{v(Q)}).

Combined with Fact
4.3
, we obtain

\displaystyle\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,H_{F}^{r})-o(n^{v(Q)})\leq\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H}^{\prime})\leq\mathrm{inj}(\partial_{r-2}Q,\partial_{r-2}\mathcal{H}^{\prime})\leq\mathrm{inj}(n,\partial_{r-2}Q,F).

This proves
\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,H_{F}^{r})\leq\mathrm{inj}(n,\partial_{r-2}Q,F)+o(n^{v(Q)})
, and hence, completing the proof of Lemma
8.3
.

Now we are ready to prove Theorem
8.1
.

Proof of Theorem
8.1
.

Here we present only the proof for the second part of Theorem
8.1
, since the proof for the first part is quite similar and requires only minor modifications.
Fix
\varepsilon>0
.
Let
\delta>0
be sufficiently small and
n
be sufficiently large.
Let
\mathcal{H}
be an
n
-vertex
H_{F}^{r}
-free
r
-graph with
\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H})\geq(1-\delta)\cdot\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,H_{F}^{r})
.
For convenience, let
G\coloneqq\partial_{r-2}\mathcal{H}
.

Applying Fact
8.4
to
\mathcal{H}
, we find a subgraph
\mathcal{H}_{1}\subset\mathcal{H}
with
|\mathcal{H}_{1}|\geq|\mathcal{H}|-\delta n^{r}
such that the graph
G_{1}\coloneqq\partial_{r-2}\mathcal{H}_{1}
is
F
-free.
It follows from Fact
4.3
and Lemma
8.3
that

\displaystyle\mathrm{inj}(\partial_{r-2}Q,G_{1})\geq\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H}_{1})\geq\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H})-\delta n^{r}\cdot n^{v(Q)-r}

\displaystyle\geq\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,H_{F}^{r})-2\delta n^{v(Q)}

\displaystyle\geq\mathrm{inj}(n,\partial_{r-2}Q,F)-3\delta n^{v(Q)}.

Since
\chi(\partial_{r-2}Q)=\chi(Q)=\ell+1
, by the Graph Removal Lemma (see e.g.
[
RS78
]
), there exists a
K_{\ell+1}
-free subgraph
G_{2}\subset G_{1}
such that
|G_{2}|\geq|G_{1}|-\delta n^{2}
. In particular,

\displaystyle\mathrm{inj}(\partial_{r-2}Q,G_{2})\geq\mathrm{inj}(\partial_{r-2}Q,G_{1})-\delta n^{r}\cdot n^{v(Q)-r}\geq\mathrm{inj}(n,\partial_{r-2}Q,F)-4\delta n^{v(Q)}.

By assumption,
K_{\ell+1}
is
\partial_{r-2}Q
-edge-stable with respect to
\mathfrak{K}_{\ell}^{2}
, so there exists a balanced partition
V_{1}\cup\cdots\cup V_{\ell}=V
such that the complete
\ell
-partite graph
K^{2}\coloneqq K^{2}[V_{1},\ldots,V_{\ell}]
satisfies
|G_{2}\triangle K^{2}|\leq\varepsilon n^{2}
.
Let
x_{\ast}\coloneqq\min\left\{|V_{i}|/n\colon i\in[\ell]\right\}=1/\ell-o(1)
.
Let
K^{r}\coloneqq K^{r}[V_{1},\ldots,V_{\ell}]
and
\mathcal{H}_{2}\coloneqq\mathcal{H}_{1}\cap K^{r}
.
Then

\displaystyle|\mathcal{H}_{2}|\geq|\mathcal{H}_{1}|-|G_{1}\setminus K^{2}|\cdot n^{r-2}

\displaystyle\geq|\mathcal{H}_{1}|-\left(|G_{1}\setminus G_{2}|+|G_{2}\setminus K^{2}|\right)n^{r-2}

\displaystyle\geq|\mathcal{H}_{1}|-(\delta+\varepsilon)n^{r}\geq|\mathcal{H}|-\delta n^{r}-(\delta+\varepsilon)n^{r}\geq|\mathcal{H}|-2\varepsilon n^{r}.

Consequently,

\displaystyle\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H}_{2})\geq\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H})-2\varepsilon n^{r}\cdot n^{v(Q)-r}\geq\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,H_{F}^{r})-3\varepsilon n^{v(Q)}.

Combined with Lemma
6.5

(i)
we obtain

\displaystyle|K^{r}\setminus\mathcal{H}_{2}|\leq\frac{\mathrm{inj}(Q,K^{r})-\mathrm{inj}(Q,\mathcal{H}_{2})}{\left(x_{\ast}n/2\right)^{v(Q)-r}}\leq\frac{3\varepsilon n^{v(Q)}}{\left(n/(4\ell)\right)^{v(Q)-r}}\leq 3(4\ell)^{v(Q)-r}\varepsilon n^{r}.

Therefore,

\displaystyle|\mathcal{H}\triangle K^{r}|\leq|K^{r}\setminus\mathcal{H}_{2}|+|\mathcal{H}\setminus\mathcal{H}_{2}|\leq 3(4\ell)^{v(Q)-r}\varepsilon n^{r}+2\varepsilon n^{r}\leq(4\ell)^{v(Q)}\varepsilon n^{r}.

proving the second part of Theorem
8.1
.

Now we are ready to present the proofs for Theorems
2.5
and
2.6
.

9
Concluding remarks

Given an
r
-graph
Q
and a family
\mathcal{F}
of
r
-graphs, let

\displaystyle\mathrm{hom}(n,Q,\mathcal{F})\coloneqq\max\left\{\mathrm{hom}(Q,\mathcal{H})\colon\text{$\mathcal{H}\in\mathfrak{G}^{r}$ is $\mathcal{F}$-free}\right\}.

All results in this paper concerning
\mathrm{inj}(n,Q,\mathcal{F})
also apply to
\mathrm{hom}(n,Q,\mathcal{F})
due to Fact
4.4
. The proofs require only minor adaptations of the current proofs.

Given integers
r>t\geq 1
and a real number
p>1
, we define the
(t,p)
-norm
of
\mathcal{H}
as

\displaystyle\left\lVert\mathcal{H}\right\rVert_{t,p}\coloneqq\sum_{T\in\binom{V(\mathcal{H})}{t}}d_{\mathcal{H}}^{p}(T),

where
d_{\mathcal{H}}^{p}(T)
is shorthand for
\left(d_{\mathcal{H}}(T)\right)^{p}
.
For a family
\mathcal{F}
of
r
-graphs the
(t,p)
-Turán number
of
\mathcal{F}
is

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}_{t,p}(n,\mathcal{F})\coloneqq\max\left\{\left\lVert\mathcal{H}\right\rVert_{t,p}\colon\text{$v(\mathcal{H})=n$ and $\mathcal{H}$ is $\mathcal{F}$-free}\right\}.

Balogh–Clemen–Lidický
[

,
BCL22a
]
initiated the study of

for hypergraph families, with most results obtained through computer-assisted Flag Algebra calculations.
