Limited type subsets of locally convex spaces
Saak Gabriyelyan

Abstract
Let 1 ≤ p ≤ q ≤ ∞ . 1\leq p\leq q\leq\infty. Being motivated by the classical notions of limited, p p -limited and coarse p p -limited subsets of a Banach space, we introduce and study ( p , q ) (p,q) -limited subsets and their equicontinuous versions and coarse p p -limited subsets of an arbitrary locally convex space E E . Operator characterizations of these classes are given. We compare these classes with the classes of bounded, (pre)compact, weakly (pre)compact and relatively weakly sequentially (pre)compact sets. If E E is a Banach space, we show that the class of coarse 1 1 -limited subsets of E E coincides with the class of ( 1 , ∞ ) (1,\infty) -limited sets, and if 1 < p < ∞ 1<p<\infty , then the class of coarse p p -limited sets in E E coincides with the class of p p - ( V ∗ ) (V^{\ast}) sets of Pełczyński. We also generalize a known theorem of Grothendieck.

Lemma 2.1

A locally convex space

Proof 1

Assume that

. Conversely, let

. Take a sequence

in
\tau

is a relatively sequentially compact subset of

. Thus

and
E_{w}
have the same relatively sequentially compact sets.

Assume that

have the same relatively sequentially compact sets. To show that

has the Schur property, let

be a weakly null sequence. Then

is relatively sequentially compact in

and hence also in

also in

. Suppose for a contradiction that there is a

. Since

converging to some point

is also weakly null we have

. But since

for every

Lemma 2.2

Every weakly sequentially precompact subset

is sequentially complete.

Proof 2

Let

Assume that

is in addition a sequentially complete space. Let

is a Cauchy sequence in

. Thus
