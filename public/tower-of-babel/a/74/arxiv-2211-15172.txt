Let

\Lambda_{1}(g)=\sup\left\{\lambda_{1}(ds^{2})Area(ds^{2})\,\big/ds^{2}\,\,{\rm is\,\,a\,\,Riemannian\,\,metric\,\,on\,\,\Sigma}\right\}.

Yang and Yau
[
25
]
gave the upper bound

\Lambda_{1}(g)\leq\left[\frac{g+3}{2}\right]8\pi,

where

denotes the integer part of

(\Sigma,ds^{2})
and the

This idea was first used by Hersch
