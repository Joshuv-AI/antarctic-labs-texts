Summation formulas of q q -hyperharmonic numbers
Takao Komatsu

Abstract
In this paper, several weighted summation formulas of q q -hyperharmonic numbers are derived. As special cases, several formulas of hyperharmonic numbers of type ∑ ℓ = 1 n ℓ p ​ H ℓ ( r ) \sum_{\ell=1}^{n}{\ell}^{p}H_{\ell}^{(r)} and ∑ ℓ = 0 n ℓ p ​ H n − ℓ ( r ) \sum_{\ell=0}^{n}{\ell}^{p}H_{n-\ell}^{(r)} are obtained. Keywords: Hyperharmonic numbers, Stirling numbers, q q -generalizations

In order to consider the weighted summations, we are motivated by the fact that the sum of powers of consecutive integers
1^{k}+2^{k}+\cdots+n^{k}
can be explicitly expressed in terms of Bernoulli numbers or Bernoulli polynomials. After seeing the sums of powers for small
k
:

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell=\frac{n(n+1)}{2},~\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell^{2}=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6},~\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell^{3}=\left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^{2},~\dots\,,

the formula can be written as

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell^{k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k+1}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k+1}{j}B_{j}n^{k+1-j}

(1)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k+1}(B_{k+1}(n+1)-B_{k+1}(1))\quad\hbox{\cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{CFZ}{}{}]}}\,,

(2)

where Bernoulli numbers
B_{n}
are determined by the recurrence formula

\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k+1}{j}B_{j}=k+1\quad(k\geq 0)

or by the generating function

\frac{t}{1-e^{-t}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,,

and Bernoulli polynomials
B_{n}(x)
are defined by the following generating function

\frac{te^{xt}}{e^{t}-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,.

If Bernoulli numbers
\mathfrak{B}_{n}
are defined by

\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathfrak{B}_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,,

we can see that
B_{n}=(-1)^{n}\mathfrak{B}_{n}
. Then

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell^{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k+1}{j}(-1)^{j}\mathfrak{B}_{j}n^{k+1-j}\,.

We recall the well-known Abel’s identity, which is frequently used in the present paper.

Lemma 1
.

(Abel’s identity)
For any positive integer
n
,

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}a_{\ell}b_{\ell}=s_{n}b_{n}+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}s_{\ell}(b_{\ell}-b_{\ell+1})\,.

where

s_{n}=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}a_{\ell}.

In the weight of harmonic numbers
H_{n}
, we have the following formulas.

.

For

,

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell^{k}H_{\ell}=\frac{H_{n}}{k+1}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k+1}{j}B_{j}n^{k+1-j}-\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}(H_{n}-H_{\ell})\ell^{k}\,.

Set
a_{\ell}={\ell^{k}}
and
b_{\ell}={H_{\ell}}
in Lemma
1
. With

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}s_{\ell}(H_{\ell}-H_{\ell+1})

\displaystyle=s_{1}(H_{1}-H_{2})+\cdots+s_{n-1}(H_{n-1}-H_{n})

\displaystyle=1^{k}H_{1}+\cdots+{(n-1)}^{k}H_{n-1}-s_{n-1}H_{n}

\displaystyle=-\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}(H_{n}-H_{\ell})\ell^{k}\,,

formula (
1
) gives the result.
∎

For

,

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\ell^{k}H_{\ell}=\frac{H_{n}}{k+1}(B_{k+1}(n+1)-B_{k+1}(1))-\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}\frac{B_{k+1}(\ell+1)-B_{k+1}(1)}{(k+1)(\ell+1)}\,.

Set

and

Weighted summations of
q
-hyperharmonic numbers

Many types of
q
-generalizations have been studied for harmonic numbers (e.g.,
[
11
,
17
]
). In this paper, a
q
-hyperharmonic number
H_{n}^{(r)}(q)
(see
[
12
]
) is defined by

H_{n}^{(r)}(q)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}q^{j}H_{j}^{(r-1)}(q)\quad(r,n\geq 1)

(3)

with

H_{n}^{(0)}(q)=\frac{1}{q[n]_{q}}

and

[n]_{q}=\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}\,.

Note that

\lim_{q\to 1}[n]_{q}=n\,.

In this
q
-generalization,

H_{n}(q)=H_{n}^{(1)}(q)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{q^{j-1}}{[j]_{q}}

is a
q
-harmonic number. When
q\to 1
,
H_{n}=\lim_{q\to 1}H_{n}(q)
is the original harmonic number and
H_{n}^{(r)}=\lim_{q\to 1}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)
is the
r
-th order hyperharmonic number, defined by

H_{n}^{(r)}=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}H_{\ell}^{(r-1)}\quad\hbox{with}\quad H_{n}^{(1)}=H_{n}\,.

(4)

Mansour and Shattuck
[
12
, Identity 4.1, Proposition 3.1]
give the following identities

\displaystyle H_{n}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}_{q}\bigl(H_{n+r-1}(q)-H_{r-1}(q)\bigr)

(5)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\binom{n+r-j-1}{r-1}_{q}\frac{q^{rj-1}}{[j]_{q}},

(6)

where

\binom{n}{k}_{q}=\frac{[n]_{q}!}{[k]_{q}![n-k]_{q}!}

is a
q
-binomial coefficient with
q
-factorials
[n]_{q}!=[n]_{q}[n-1]_{q}\cdots[1]_{q}
.
Note that the identities (
5
) and (
6
) are
q
-generalization of the identities (
7
) and (
8
), respectively.

\displaystyle H_{n}^{(r)}

\displaystyle=\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}(H_{n+r-1}-H_{r-1})\quad\hbox{\cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Conway}{}{}]}}

(7)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\binom{n+r-j-1}{r-1}\frac{1}{j}\quad\hbox{\cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{BGG}{}{}]}}\,.

(8)

So, we can see the recurrence relation
for
r\geq 1

H_{n}^{(r+1)}=\frac{n+r}{r}H_{n}^{(r)}-\frac{1}{r}\binom{n+r-1}{r}\,.

The generating function of this type of
q
-hyperharmonic numbers is given by

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)z^{n}=\frac{-\log_{q}(1-q^{r}z)}{q(z;q)_{r}}\quad(r\geq 0)

(9)

(
[
12
, Theorem 3.2]
), where

-\log_{q}(1-t)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{t^{m}}{[m]_{q}}

is the
q
-logarithm function and

(z;q)_{k}:=\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}(1-zq^{j})

is the
q
-Pochhammer symbol. When
q\to 1
, (
9
) is reduced to the generating function of hyperharmonic numbers:

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}H_{n}^{(r)}z^{n}=\frac{-\log(1-z)}{(1-z)^{r}}\quad(r\geq 0)\,.

In fact, the same form is given by Knuth
[
10
]
as

\sum_{n=r-1}^{\infty}\binom{n}{r-1}(H_{n}-H_{r-1})z^{n-r+1}=\frac{-\log(1-z)}{(1-z)^{r}}\quad(r\geq 0)\,.

By (
5
), we have

H_{n}^{(r+1)}(q)-\frac{[n+r]_{q}}{[r]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)=-\frac{q^{r-1}}{[r]_{q}}\binom{n+r-1}{r}_{q}\,.

Hence,

H_{n}^{(r+1)}(q)=\frac{[n+r]_{q}}{[r]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-\frac{q^{r-1}}{[r]_{q}}\binom{n+r-1}{r}_{q}\,.

(10)

By replacing
n
by
n+1
and
r
by
r-1
in (
10
), together with the definition in (

), we have

\displaystyle[n+r]_{q}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=[n+1]_{q}H_{n+1}^{(r)}(q)-q^{n+r-1}\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}_{q}\,.

(11)

Mansour and Shattuck
[
12
, Theorem 3.3]
also give the following formula,

H_{n}^{(r)}(q)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}q^{j(r-m)}\binom{n+r-m-j-1}{r-m-1}H_{j}^{(m)}(q).

(12)

When
q\to 1
, (
12
) is reduced to

H_{n}^{(r)}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\binom{n+r-m-j-1}{r-m-1}H_{j}^{(m)}

(see also

]

). When

Theorem 1
.

For nonnegative integers
n
and
k
and a positive integer

, we have

\binom{k+r-1}{k}_{q}H_{n}^{(k+r)}(q)=\binom{n+k}{n}_{q}H_{n+k}^{(r)}(q)-\binom{n+k+r-1}{n}_{q}H_{k}^{(r)}(q)\,.

Remark.

If
r=1
and

is replaced by

in Theorem
1

If

in Theorem

Proof of Theorem
1
.

The proof is done by induction on
k
. When
k=0
, the identity is clear since both sides are equal to
H_{n}^{(r)}(q)
. Assume, then, that the identity has been proved for
0,1,\cdots,k
. We give some explanations for the following calculation. Firstly, by replacing

by
k+r
in (

), we get the first identity. Secondly, by using the inductive assumption, we get the second identity. Thirdly, by replacing

by

and

by

\displaystyle\binom{k+r}{k+1}_{q}H_{n}^{(k+r+1)}(q)

\displaystyle=\binom{k+r}{k+1}_{q}\frac{[n+k+r]_{q}}{[k+r]_{q}}H_{n}^{(k+r)}(q)-\binom{k+r}{k+1}_{q}\frac{q^{k+r-1}}{[k+r]_{q}}\binom{n+k+r-1}{k+r}_{q}

\displaystyle=\frac{[n+k+r]_{q}}{[k+1]_{q}}\binom{n+k}{n}_{q}H_{n+k}^{(r)}(q)-\frac{[n+k+r]_{q}}{[k+1]_{q}}\binom{n+k+r-1}{n}_{q}H_{k}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{q^{k+r-1}}{[k+r]_{q}}\binom{k+r}{k+1}_{q}\binom{n+k+r-1}{k+r}_{q}

\displaystyle=\frac{[n+k+1]_{q}}{[k+1]_{q}}\binom{n+k}{n}_{q}H_{n+k+1}^{(r)}(q)-\frac{q^{n+k+r-1}}{[k+1]_{q}}\binom{n+k}{n}_{q}\binom{n+k+r-1}{r-1}_{q}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{[n+k+r]_{q}}{[k+1]_{q}}\binom{n+k+r-1}{n}_{q}\frac{[k+1]_{q}}{[k+r]_{q}}H_{k+1}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{[n+k+r]_{q}}{[k+1]_{q}}\binom{n+k+r-1}{n}_{q}\frac{q^{k+r-1}}{[k+r]_{q}}\binom{k+r-1}{r-1}_{q}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{q^{k+r-1}}{[k+r]_{q}}\binom{k+r}{k+1}_{q}\binom{n+k+r-1}{k+r}_{q}

\displaystyle=\binom{n+k+1}{n}_{q}H_{n+k+1}^{(r)}(q)-\binom{n+k+r}{n}_{q}H_{k+1}^{(r)}(q)\,.

We used the relation
[n+k+r]_{q}-q^{n}[k+r]_{q}=[n]_{q}
in the final part.
∎

Proposition 3
.

For
|q|<1
, we have

\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{H_{n+1}^{(n+1)}(q)}{H_{n}^{(n)}(q)}=q\,.

Proof.

Since

\frac{(1-q^{2n+1})(1-q^{2n})}{(1-q^{n+1})(1-q^{n})}\to 1\quad(|q|<1,~n\to\infty)

and

\displaystyle\frac{H_{2n+1}(q)-H_{n}(q)}{H_{2n-1}(q)-H_{n-1}(q)}

\displaystyle=\frac{q\left(\dfrac{1}{[n+1]_{q}}+\dfrac{q}{[n+2]_{q}}+\cdots+\dfrac{q^{n}}{[2n+1]_{q}}\right)}{\dfrac{1}{[n]_{q}}+\dfrac{q}{[n+1]_{q}}+\cdots+\dfrac{q^{n-1}}{[2n-1]_{q}}}

\displaystyle\to q\quad(n\to\infty)\,,

from (
5
),

\displaystyle\frac{H_{n+1}^{(n+1)}(q)}{H_{n}^{(n)}(q)}

\displaystyle=\frac{\binom{2n+1}{n}_{q}\bigl(H_{2n+1}(q)-H_{n}(q)\bigr)}{\binom{2n-1}{n-1}_{q}\bigl(H_{2n-1}(q)-H_{n-1}(q)\bigr)}

\displaystyle\to 1\cdot q=q\,.

∎

Theorem 2
.

For positive integers
n
and
r
,

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}[\ell]_{q}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{[n]_{q}[n+r]_{q}}{[r+1]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-\frac{q^{r}[n-1]_{q}[n]_{q}}{([r+1]_{q})^{2}}\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}_{q}

\displaystyle=\frac{[n]_{q}[r]_{q}}{[r+1]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r+1)}(q)+\frac{q^{r-1}}{[r+1]_{q}}\binom{n+r}{r+1}_{q}\,.

(13)

Proof.

Set
a_{\ell}=q^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r}_{q}
and
b_{\ell}=H_{\ell+r-1}(q)
. By using Lemma

, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r}_{q}H_{\ell+r-1}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r}_{q}H_{n+r-1}(q)-\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}\frac{q^{\ell+r-1}}{[\ell+r]_{q}}\binom{\ell+r}{r+1}_{q}

\displaystyle=\binom{n+r}{r+1}_{q}H_{n+r-1}(q)-\frac{q^{r}}{[r+1]_{q}}\binom{n+r-1}{r+1}_{q}\,.

(14)

Hence,

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}{[\ell]_{q}}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}{[\ell]_{q}}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r-1}_{q}(H_{\ell+r-1}(q)-H_{r-1}(q))

\displaystyle=[r]_{q}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r}_{q}(H_{\ell+r-1}(q)-H_{r-1}(q))

\displaystyle=[r]_{q}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r}_{q}H_{\ell+r-1}(q)-[r]_{q}H_{r-1}(q)\binom{n+r}{r+1}_{q}\,.

(15)

With the help of (

) and (

Lemma 2
.

Given summation formulas
\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell}{j}_{q}[c_{\ell}]_{q}=F_{q}(n,j)
for

, one has

\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell-1}{([\ell]_{q})}^{p}[c_{\ell}]_{q}=\sum_{\ell=0}^{p}q^{\binom{\ell}{2}}S_{q}(p,\ell)\cdot{[\ell]_{q}}!\cdot F_{q}(n,\ell)\,.

where

denote the

-Stirling numbers of the second kind.

Proof.

Using (

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell-1}{([\ell]_{q})}^{p}[c_{\ell}]_{q}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell-1}[c_{\ell}]_{q}\sum_{j=0}^{p}q^{\binom{j}{2}}S_{q}(p,j)\cdot([\ell]_{q})_{(j)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{p}q^{\binom{j}{2}}S_{q}(p,j){[j]_{q}}!\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell}{j}_{q}[c_{\ell}]_{q}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{p}q^{\binom{j}{2}}S_{q}(p,j)\cdot{[j]_{q}}!\cdot F_{q}(n,j)\,.

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell-1}{[\ell]_{q}}^{p}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)=A_{q}(p,r,n)H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-B_{q}(p,r,n)\,.

\displaystyle B_{q}(p,r,n)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{p}\frac{q^{\binom{\ell}{2}+r+2p-2}}{[r+\ell]_{q}}S_{q}(p,\ell){[\ell]_{q}!}\binom{r+\ell-1}{\ell}_{q}\binom{r+n-1}{r+\ell}_{q}\,.

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\binom{r+p-1}{p}_{q}\binom{\ell+r-1}{r+p-1}_{q}(H_{\ell+r-1}(q)-H_{r-1}(q))

\displaystyle\quad\quad-\binom{r+p-1}{p}\binom{r+n}{r+p}H_{r-1}

\displaystyle\quad\quad-\frac{q^{r+2p-2}}{[r+p]_{q}}\binom{r+p-1}{p}_{q}\binom{r+n-1}{r+p}_{q}.

\displaystyle\quad-q^{r}[n-1]_{q}[n]_{q}\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}_{q}\frac{q[r+1]_{q}^{2}[n]_{q}-q^{3}[r]_{q}^{2}+[2]_{q}}{[r+1]_{q}^{2}[r+2]_{q}^{2}}\,.

(18)

Note that
[\ell+1]_{q}=1+q\cdot[\ell]_{q}
and
[\ell+2]_{q}=[2]_{q}+q^{2}\cdot[\ell]_{q}
. With the help of Theorem
3
and identities (
13
) and (
18
), we have the following identities.
For positive integers
n
and
r
,

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}[\ell]_{q}[\ell+1]_{q}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{[n]_{q}[n+r]_{q}([2]_{q}[n+2]_{q}+q^{3}[r-1]_{q}[n+1]_{q})}{[r+1]_{q}[r+2]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle\quad-q^{r}[n-1]_{q}[n]_{q}\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}_{q}\frac{[2]_{q}[r+2]_{q}^{2}+q^{4}[r+1]_{q}^{2}[n-2]_{q}}{[r+1]_{q}^{2}[r+2]_{q}^{2}}\,.

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}[\ell]_{q}[\ell+1]_{q}[\ell+2]_{q}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{[n]_{q}[n+r]_{q}\bigl((r+1)(r+2)n^{2}+3(r+1)(r+4)n+2(r^{2}+6r+11)\bigr)}{[r+1]_{q}[r+2]_{q}[r+3]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle\hskip 8.19447pt-q^{r}[n-1]_{q}[n]_{q}\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}_{q}\frac{(r+1)^{2}(r+2)^{2}n^{2}+(r+1)^{2}(r^{2}+16r+34)n+12(3r^{2}+12r+11)}{([r+1]_{q})^{2}([r+2]_{q})^{2}([r+3]_{q})^{2}}\,.

To give a more general result, we need the
q
-unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind
s_{uq}(n,k)
defined by

[\ell]_{q}^{(n)}=[\ell]_{q}[\ell+1]_{q}\cdots[\ell+n-1]_{q}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}s_{uq}(n,k)([\ell]_{q})^{k},\quad(n\in\mathbb{N}).

The
q
-unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind
s_{uq}(n,k)
are well defined since
[\ell+m]_{q}=[m]_{q}+q^{m}\cdot[\ell]_{q}
.

Theorem 4
.

For positive integers
n,p
and
r
,

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}[\ell]_{q}^{(p)}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)=A_{1q}(p,r,n)H_{n}^{(r)}-B_{1q}(p,r,n)\,,

where

\displaystyle A_{1q}(p,r,n)=\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p.m)A_{q}(m,r,n),

\displaystyle B_{1q}(p,r,n)=\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p.m)B_{q}(m,r,n).

Proof.

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}[\ell]_{q}^{(p)}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p,m){[\ell]_{q}}^{m}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p,m)\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{\ell-1}{[\ell]_{q}}^{m}H_{\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p,m)(A_{q}(m,r,n)H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-B_{q}(m,r,n))

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p,m)A_{q}(m,r,n)\right)H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-\left(\sum_{m=0}^{p}s_{uq}(p,m)B_{q}(m,r,n)\right).

∎

2.1
Backward summations

Now we consider backward summations of
q
-hyperharmonic numbers.

Theorem 5
.

For positive integers
n
and
r
,

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{2n-2\ell}[\ell]_{q}H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}(q)\\
=\frac{[n]_{q}[n+r]_{q}}{[r]_{q}[r+1]_{q}}H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-\binom{n+r}{r+1}_{q}\left(\frac{q^{r-1}}{[r]_{q}}+\frac{q^{r}}{[r+1]_{q}}-\frac{q^{n+r-1}}{[n+r]_{q}}\right)\,.

Proof.

Set
a_{\ell}=q^{n-\ell}{H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}(q)}
, and
b_{\ell}=q^{n-\ell}{[\ell]_{q}}
. By using Lemma
1
and
[\ell+1]_{q}-q[\ell]_{q}=1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{2n-2\ell}[\ell]_{q}H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=[n]_{q}\cdot H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}(H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)-H_{n-\ell-1}^{(r+1)}(q))(q^{n-\ell}{[\ell]_{q}}-q^{n-\ell-1}{[\ell+1]_{q}})

\displaystyle=[n]_{q}\cdot H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)(q^{n-\ell}{[\ell]_{q}}-q^{n-\ell-1}{[\ell+1]_{q}})

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}H_{n-\ell-1}^{(r+1)}(q)(-q^{n-\ell}{[\ell]_{q}}+q^{n-\ell-1}{[\ell+1]_{q}})

\displaystyle=q^{n-1}H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}q^{n-\ell-1}H_{n-\ell-1}^{(r+1)}(q)

\displaystyle=H_{n-1}^{(r+2)}(q)\,.

With the help of (
5
), we get the desired result.
∎

, Theorem
5
is reduced to the following.

It is more complicated to get a summation formula for the backward summations of higher power. In the case where
q\to 1
, we have more relations, including the following.

Theorem 6
.

For positive integers
n,p
and
r
,

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}{\ell}^{p}H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}=A_{2}(p,r,n)H_{n}^{(r)}-B_{2}(p,r,n)\,.

where
A_{2}(p,r,n)
and
B_{2}(p,r,n)
satisfy the following relations:

\displaystyle A_{2}(p,r,n)=A_{2}(0,r,n)\left(1+\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p}{j}A_{2}(j,r+1,n-1)\right),

\displaystyle B_{2}(p,r,n)

\displaystyle=B_{2}(0,r,n)\left(1+\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p}{j}A_{2}(j,r+1,n-1)\right)+\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p}{j}B_{2}(j,r+1,n-1)\,,

with the initial values
A_{2}(0,r,n)=\frac{n}{r}
and
B_{2}(0,r,n)=\frac{1}{r}\binom{n+r-1}{r}
.

Nevertheless, we can have a different backward summation formula without weights.

Theorem 7
.

For positive integers
n,p
and
r
,

\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{p(n-\ell)}H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}(q)=C_{q}(p,r,n)H_{n}^{(r)}(q)-D_{q}(p,r,n)\,,

where
C_{q}(p,r,n)
and
D_{q}(p,r,n)
satisfy the following recurrence relation.

\displaystyle C_{q}(p,r,n)=\frac{[n]_{q}}{[r]_{q}}\left(q^{(p-1)(n-1)}+(1-q^{p-1})C_{q}(p-1,r+1,n-1)\right)

\displaystyle D_{q}(p,r,n)

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{r-1}[n]_{q}}{([r]_{q})^{2}}\binom{n+r-1}{r}_{q}\left(q^{(p-1)(n-1)}+(1-q^{p-1})C_{q}(p-1,r+1,n-1)\right)

\displaystyle\quad+(1-q^{p-1})D_{q}(p-1,r+1,n-1).

Proof.

Set
a_{\ell}=q^{n-\ell}{H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}(q)}
and
b_{\ell}=q^{(p-1)(n-\ell)}
. By using Lemma
1
and
[\ell+1]_{q}-q[\ell]_{q}=1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}q^{p(n-\ell)}H_{n-\ell}^{(r)}(q)

\displaystyle=H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}(H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)-H_{n-\ell-1}^{(r+1)}(q))(q^{(p-1)(n-\ell)}-q^{(p-1)(n-\ell-1)})

\displaystyle=H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)(q^{(p-1)(n-\ell)}-q^{(p-1)(n-\ell-1)})

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}H_{n-\ell-1}^{(r+1)}(q)(-q^{(p-1)(n-\ell)}+q^{(p-1)(n-\ell-1)})

\displaystyle=q^{(p-1)(n-1)}H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)+(1-q^{p-1})\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}q^{(p-1)(n-\ell-1)}H_{n-\ell-1}^{(r+1)}(q)

\displaystyle=q^{(p-1)(n-1)}H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)

\displaystyle\quad+(1-q^{p-1})\left(C_{q}(p-1,r+1,n-1)H_{n-1}^{(r+1)}(q)-D_{q}(p-1,r+1,n-1)\right)\,.

With the help of (
5
), we get the desired result.
∎
