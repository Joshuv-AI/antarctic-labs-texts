Abstract
In this paper, the optimization of deploying unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) over a reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RISs)-assisted visible light communication (VLC) system is studied. In the considered model, UAVs are required to simultaneously provide wireless services as well as illumination for ground users. To meet the traffic and illumination demands of the ground users while minimizing the energy consumption of the UAVs, one must optimize UAV deployment, phase shift of RISs, user association and RIS association. This problem is formulated as an optimization problem whose goal is to minimize the transmit power of UAVs via adjusting UAV deployment, phase shift of RISs, user association and RIS association. To solve this problem, the original optimization problem is divided into four subproblems and an alternating algorithm is proposed. Specifically, phases alignment method and semidefinite program (SDP) algorithm are proposed to optimize the phase shift of RISs. Then, the UAV deployment optimization is solved by the successive convex approximation (SCA) algorithm. Since the problems of user association and RIS association are integer programming, the fraction relaxation method is adopted before using dual method to find the optimal solution. For simplicity, a greedy algorithm is proposed as an alternative to optimize RIS association. The proposed two schemes demonstrate the superior performance of 34.85 % 34.85\% and 32.11 % 32.11\% energy consumption reduction over the case without RIS, respectively, through extensive numerical study.

arranging in rows, where

represents the number of elements in set

. The constraint in (

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{Z}_{i}}\max_{j\in\mathcal{U}_{i}}

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{A_{j}^{2}u_{ij}^{2}}\left|h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l\in\mathcal{L}_{i}}\bm{z}_{l}^{*}\bm{\Phi}_{ilj}\right|^{2}

(21)

\displaystyle s.t.

\displaystyle|\bm{z}_{lm}|^{2}=1,\ \ l\in\mathcal{L}_{i},m\in\mathcal{M}

Since

\displaystyle\left|h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\bm{z}_{l}^{*}\bm{\Phi}_{ilj}\right|^{2}=(h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\bm{z}_{l}^{*}\bm{\Phi}_{ilj})^{H}(h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\bm{z}_{l}^{*}\bm{\Phi}_{ilj})

Denote
\bm{\Phi}_{ij}^{(o)(p)}=\bm{\Phi}_{oj}\bm{\Phi}_{pj}^{H}
,
\hat{\bm{\Phi}}_{ioj}=h_{ij}^{LOS}\bm{\Phi}_{oj}
,
\forall o,p\in\mathcal{L}_{i}
.
Problem (

) can be further expressed as:

\displaystyle\min_{\hat{\bm{z}}_{i}}\max_{j\in\mathcal{U}_{i}}\ \

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{A_{j}^{2}u_{ij}^{2}}\left((h_{ij}^{LOS})^{2}+\hat{\bm{z}}_{i}^{H}\bm{Q}_{ij}\hat{\bm{z}}_{i}\right)

(23)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle\left|[\hat{\bm{z}}_{i}]_{n}\right|^{2}=1,n=1,2,\cdots,|\mathcal{L}_{i}|M+1.

(23a)

\bm{\Phi}_{ij}^{(2)(1)}&\bm{\Phi}_{ij}^{(2)(2)}&\cdots&\bm{\Phi}_{ij}^{(2)(|\mathcal{L}_{i}|)}&\hat{\bm{\Phi}}_{i2j}\\

\end{bmatrix}

(24)

\hat{\bm{z}}_{i}=\begin{bmatrix}\bm{z}_{1}^{T}\\
\bm{z}_{1}^{T}\\
\vdots\\
\bm{z}_{L}^{T}\\
1\\
\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb{C}^{|\mathcal{L}_{i}|M+1}

(25)

It can be inferred that

\displaystyle\min_{\hat{\bm{Z}}_{i}}\max_{j\in\mathcal{U}_{i}}\ \

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{A_{j}^{2}u_{ij}^{2}}\left((h_{ij}^{LOS})^{2}+tr(\bm{Q}_{ij}\hat{\bm{Z}}_{i})\right)

(26)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle[\hat{\bm{Z}}_{i}]_{nn}=1,n=1,2,\cdots,|\mathcal{L}_{i}|M+1

(26a)

\displaystyle\hat{\bm{Z}}_{i}\succeq 0

(26b)

Then, substituting (

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}

(30b)

(30c)

where (

\displaystyle\hat{h}_{ij}\leq\left|h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}m_{il}(\bm{h}_{lj}^{RG})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{l}\bm{h}_{il}^{UR}\right|=\left|h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\kappa_{ilj}^{(r)}h_{il}^{LOS}\right|

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{q}_{i},P_{i},\hat{h}_{ij},\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS},\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS}}\ \ \sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

(32)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{\hat{h}_{ij}},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(32a)

\displaystyle\hat{h}_{ij}\leq\left|\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\kappa_{ilj}^{(r)}\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS}\right|,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(32b)

\displaystyle g_{0}^{r}(\bm{q}_{i}-\bm{q}_{k})\geq d_{min},\ \ \forall i,k\in\mathcal{D},i\neq k,

(32c)

\displaystyle\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS}\leq\frac{(k+1)A}{2\pi d_{ij}^{2}}\cos^{k}(\phi_{ij}^{(r)})g(\varphi_{ij}^{(r)})\cos(\varphi_{ij}^{(r)}),\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(32d)

\displaystyle\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS}\leq\frac{(k+1)A}{2\pi d_{il}^{2}}\cos^{k}(\phi_{il}^{(r)})g(\varphi_{il}^{(r)})\cos(\varphi_{il}^{(r)}),\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall l\in\mathcal{L}.

(32e)

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{q}_{i},P_{i},\hat{h}_{ij},\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS},\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS}}\ \ \sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

(33)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{\hat{h}_{ij}},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(33a)

\displaystyle g_{0}^{r}(\bm{q}_{i}-\bm{q}_{k})\geq d_{min},\ \ \forall i,k\in\mathcal{D},i\neq k,

(33b)

\displaystyle g_{1}^{r}(\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS},\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS})\geq\hat{h}_{ij}^{2},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(33c)

\displaystyle g_{2}^{r}(\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS})\geq d_{ij}^{2},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(33d)

\displaystyle g_{3}^{r}(\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS})\geq d_{il}^{2},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall l\in\mathcal{L}.

(33e)

where

\displaystyle g_{1}^{r}(\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS},\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS})=

\displaystyle 2\text{\footnotesize Re}\left\{\left((\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS})^{(r)}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\kappa_{ilj}^{(r)}(\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS})^{(r)}\right)\left(\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\kappa_{ilj}^{(r)}\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle-\left|(\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS})^{(r)}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}\kappa_{ilj}^{(r)}(\hat{h}_{il}^{LOS})^{(r)}\right|^{2},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}

\displaystyle g_{2}^{r}(\hat{h}_{ij}^{LOS})=

\bm{\Theta}_{t}

. In this subsection, We further optimize
\bm{u}_{i}
and
\bm{m}_{i}
with fixed
\bm{q}_{i}
and
\bm{\Theta}_{t}
. Thus, the optimization problem (
13
) can be reduced to:

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{u}_{i},\bm{m}_{i},P_{i}}

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

(37)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}

(37a)

(37b)

(37c)

III-B
1
User Association Optimization

With fixed RIS Association
\bm{m}_{i}
, we adopt the fractional relaxation method to make this combinatorial problem tractable. In this case,
\bm{u}_{i}
can take on any real value in
[0,1]
. Thus, problem (
37
) can be reformulated as:

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{u}_{i},P_{i}}

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

(38)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}

(38a)

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}u_{ij}=1,\ \ \forall j\in\mathcal{U}

(38b)

\displaystyle u_{ij}\geq 0,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}.

(38c)

Then, the dual problem of (
38
) can be given by:

\max_{\bm{\beta}}\ \ D(\bm{\beta})

(39)

where

D(\bm{\beta})=\left\{\begin{aligned} \min_{\bm{u}_{i},P_{i}}\ \ &L(\bm{u}_{i},P_{i},\bm{\beta})\\
s.t.\ \ \ \ &\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}u_{ij}=1,\ \ \forall j\in\mathcal{U}\\
&u_{ij}\geq 0,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}\\
\end{aligned}\right.

(40)

with

\displaystyle L(\bm{u}_{i},P_{i},\bm{\beta})=\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i}+\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}\beta_{ij}\left(\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})}-P_{i}\right)

(41)

and
\bm{\beta}=\{\beta_{ij}\}
is nonnegative relaxation variables with respect to (
38
a).
In order to minimize the objective function of (
39
), which is a linear combination of
u_{ij}
, we may let the smallest assoication coefficient corresponding to the
u_{ij}
be 1 among all the UAVs with the given ground user
j
. Therefore, we can obtain the optimal
u_{ij}^{*}
:

u_{ij}^{*}=\left\{\begin{aligned} 1,\ \ &\text{if}\ \ i=\arg\min_{i\in\mathcal{D}}\frac{\beta_{ij}A_{j}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})}\\
0,\ \ &\text{otherwise.}\end{aligned}\right.

(42)

To achieve the optimal
P_{i}^{*}
from (
40
), we derive the first derivative with respect to
P_{i}
considering that (
40
) is a linear problem with respect to
P_{i}
:

\frac{\partial L(\bm{u}_{i},P_{i},\bm{\beta})}{\partial P_{i}}=1-\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}\beta_{ij}

(43)

Note that the optimal
P_{i}^{*}=+\infty
if
1-\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}\beta_{ij}\textless 0
. To avoid this, we must have
\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}\beta_{ij}\leq 1
. If there are multiple minimum indexes in satisfying
\arg\min_{i\in\mathcal{D}}\frac{\beta_{ij}A_{j}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})}
, we can choose any one of them.

The values of
\beta_{ij}
can be determined by the gradient method. The updating procedure is given by:

\beta_{ij}=\left[\beta_{ij}^{(r)}+\rho\left(\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}^{(r)}}{h_{j}(\bm{q}_{i})}-P_{i}^{(r)}\right)\right]^{+}

(44)

where
[a]^{+}=\max(a,0)
, and
\rho
is a dynamically chosen positive step-size sequence. Thus, we can obtain the optimal
\bm{u}_{i}^{*}
and
P_{i}^{*}
with fixed
\bm{m}_{i}
through optimizing primal variables and dual variables iteratively.

From (
42
), we can see that even though the feasible region of
u_{ij}
is relaxed to be continuous, the optimal solution to problem (
38
) always satisfies the discrete constraints
u_{ij}\in\{0,1\},\forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}
. Hence, the relaxation of
u_{ij}
does not lose optimality to the primal problem.

III-B
2
RIS Association Optimization

We further optimize
\bm{m}_{i}
with fixed
\bm{u}_{i}
. In this subsubsection, we propose two efficient algorithms to tackle this problem.

Dual Method

First, we formulate the following optimization problem respect to
\bm{m}_{i}
:

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{m}_{i},\tilde{h}_{ij},P_{i}}

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

(45)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{\tilde{h}_{ij}},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}

(45a)

\displaystyle\tilde{h}_{ij}\leq\left|h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}m_{il}(\bm{h}_{lj}^{RG})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{l}\bm{h}_{il}^{UR}\right|,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(45b)

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}m_{il}=1,\ \ \forall l\in\mathcal{L}

(45c)

\displaystyle m_{il}\in\{0,1\},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall l\in\mathcal{L}.

(45d)

where
\tilde{h}_{ij},\forall i,j
is a group of new introduced variables. For the optimal solution of problem (
45
), constraint (
45
b) will always hold with equality. Note that (
45
b) is nonconvex, we can rewrite the right hand side of constraint (
45
b) according to (
22
) due to the fact that

:

\displaystyle\left|h_{ij}^{LOS}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}m_{il}(\bm{h}_{lj}^{RG})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{l}\bm{h}_{il}^{UR}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{ij0}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}C_{ijl}m_{il}+\sum_{l=2}^{L}\sum_{v=1}^{l-1}C_{ijlv}m_{il}m_{iv}

(46)

where

\displaystyle C_{ij0}=(h_{ij}^{LOS})^{2}

\displaystyle C_{ijl}=2h_{ij}^{LOS}\text{Re}\{(\bm{h}_{lj}^{RG})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{l}\bm{h}_{il}^{UR}\}+\left|(\bm{h}_{lj}^{RG})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{l}\bm{h}_{il}^{UR}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle C_{ijlv}=2\text{Re}\{(\bm{h}_{il}^{UR})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{l}^{H}\bm{h}_{lj}^{RG}(\bm{h}_{vj}^{RG})^{H}\bm{\Theta}_{v}\bm{h}_{iv}^{UR}\}

(47)

In (
III-B2
), we denote
m_{il}m_{iv}
as
E_{ilv}
. Thus,
E_{ilv}
must satisfies:

\displaystyle E_{ilv}\geq m_{il}+m_{iv}-1,0\leq E_{ilv}\leq 1,

\displaystyle E_{ilv}\leq m_{il},m_{iv},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall i,l\in\mathcal{L},i\neq l.

(48)

Now, problem (
45
) can be reformulated as:

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{m}_{i},\tilde{h}_{ij},E_{ilv}P_{i}}\ \ \sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i},

(49)

\displaystyle s.t.\ \

\displaystyle P_{i}\geq\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{\tilde{h}_{ij}},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U}

(49a)

\displaystyle\tilde{h}_{ij}^{2}\leq C_{ij0}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}C_{ijl}m_{il}+\sum_{l=2}^{L}\sum_{v=1}^{l-1}C_{ijlv}E_{ilv},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall j\in\mathcal{U},

(49b)

\displaystyle E_{ilv}\geq m_{il}+m_{iv}-1,0\leq E_{ilv}\leq 1,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall i,l\in\mathcal{L},i\neq l,

(49c)

\displaystyle E_{ilv}\leq m_{il},m_{iv},\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall i,l\in\mathcal{L},i\neq l,

(49d)

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}m_{il}=1,\ \ \forall l\in\mathcal{L}

(49e)

\displaystyle m_{il}\geq 0,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall l\in\mathcal{L}.

(49f)

where we have relaxed the integer constraints (
45
d) with
m_{il}\in[0,1]
. Thus, problem (
49
) becomes a convex problem which can be tracked through the dual method.

The dual problem of (
49
) is given by:

\max_{\bm{\tau},\bm{\gamma},\bm{\Gamma}}\ \ \hat{D}(\bm{\tau},\bm{\gamma},\bm{\Gamma})

(50)

where

\displaystyle\hat{D}(\bm{\tau},\bm{\gamma},\bm{\Gamma})=

(51)

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{aligned} &\min_{\bm{m}_{i},\tilde{h}_{ij},E_{ilv}P_{i}}\hat{L}(\bm{m}_{i},\tilde{h}_{ij},E_{ilv},P_{i},\bm{\tau},\bm{\gamma},\bm{\Gamma})\\
s.t.\ \ &\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}m_{il}=1,\ \ \forall l\in\mathcal{L},\\
&m_{il}\geq 0,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall l\in\mathcal{L},\\
&0\leq E_{ilv}\leq 1,\ \ \forall i\in\mathcal{D},\forall i,l\in\mathcal{L},i\neq l,\\
\end{aligned}\right.

with

\displaystyle\hat{L}(\bm{m}_{i},\tilde{h}_{ij},E_{ilv},P_{i},\bm{\tau},\bm{\gamma},\bm{\Gamma})=\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}P_{i}-\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}[\tau_{ij}(P_{i}-\frac{A_{j}u_{ij}}{\tilde{h}_{ij}})]

(52)

\displaystyle-\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}[\gamma_{ij}(C_{ij0}+\sum_{l=1}^{L}C_{ijl}m_{il}+\sum_{l=2}^{L}\sum_{v=1}^{l-1}C_{ijlv}E_{ilv}-\tilde{h}_{ij})]

\displaystyle-\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}\sum_{l=2}^{L}\sum_{v=1}^{l-1}\left[\Gamma_{1ilv}(E_{ilv}-m_{il}-m_{iv}+1)+\Gamma_{2ilv}(m_{il}-E_{ilv})+\Gamma_{3ilv}(m_{iv}-E_{ilv})\right]

and
\bm{\tau}=\{\tau_{ij}\}
,
\bm{\gamma}=\{\gamma_{ij}\}
,
\bm{\Gamma}=\{\Gamma_{1ilv},\Gamma_{2ilv},\Gamma_{3ilv}\}
are nonnegative Lagrange multipliers with respect to the corresponding constraints in primal problem (
49
).

To minimize the objective function in (
51
), which is a linear combination of
E_{ilv}
, we must let the positive coefficients corresponding to the
E_{ilv}
be 0:

E_{ilv}^{*}=\left\{\begin{aligned} &1,\ \ \text{if}\ \ \Gamma_{1ilv}-\Gamma_{2ilv}-\Gamma_{3ilv}+\sum_{j\in\mathcal{U}}\gamma_{ij}C_{ijlv}\textgreater 0\\
&0,\ \ \text{otherwise}\end{aligned}\right.

(53)

Due to the constraint
\sum_{i\in\mathcal{D}}m_{il}=1,\forall l\in\mathcal{L}
, we must let the smallest association coefficient corresponding to
m_{il}
be 1 among all the UAVs with given RIS
l
:

m_{il}^{*}=\left\{\begin{aligned} &1,\ \ \text{if}\ \ i=\arg\min_{i\in\mathcal{D}}C_{il}\\
&0,\ \ \text{otherwise}\end{aligned}\right.

The proposed algorithms used to solve problem (

\bm{u}_{i}

V
conclusion

In this paper, we proposed a novel framework of a VLC-enabled UAV multicell network, by leveraging the prevalent RISs for reducing total consuming power of all the UAVs.
An optimization problem was formulated to dynamically optimize UAV deployment, phase shift of RISs and the association of ground users and RISs to achieve the minimum total transmitting power by taking into account traffic and illumination demands.
An alternating algorithm was proposed to solve multiple subproblems iteratively. Numerical results demonstrate the superior performance of our proposed algorithms over the case without RIS in terms of energy consumption reduction.
And by associating each RIS with the closest UAV, we can achieve the minimum transmit power of all the UAVs.
