Barcodes in level and sub-level persistence and Morse-Novikov theory
Dan Burghelea

Abstract
In this note we recall the relations between the barcodes in level and sub-level persistence and make precise their relation with the Morse-Novikov complex of a Morse real- or angle-valued map. The results in this papers are implicit in [ 4 ] and explicit in [ 1 ] , but apparently not well known even to experts.
