New solvable matrix models III
Aleksander Yu. Orlov

Abstract
We present a family of matrix models whose perturbation series in the coupling constants are written as the series in projective Schur functions over strict partitions and are examples of the tau functions of the two-component BKP hierarchy. The case of the usage of the two-component KP hierarchy is also reviewed and generalized.

Hypergeometric tau functions and determinantal formulas.

Here we follow

Let

Consider the following series

This is just a Taylor series for a function

If as

Indeed, take

We obtain

\tau_{r}(n;x)=\sum_{m\geq 0}\frac{\prod_{i=1}^{p}(a_{i}+n)_{m}}{m!\prod_{i=1}^{q}(b_{i}+n)_{m}}x^{m}={{}_{p}F}_{q}\left({a_{1}+n,\dots,a_{p}+n\atop b_{1}+n,\dots,b_{q}+n}\mid x\right)

where

(a)_{n}=a(a+1)\cdots(a+n-1)=\frac{\Gamma(a+n)}{\Gamma(a)}

(5)

is the Pochhammer symbol.

One can prove the following formula

)
(see for instance

]

\tau_{r}(n;X,Y):=\sum_{\lambda}r_{\lambda}(n)s_{\lambda}(X)s_{\lambda}(Y)=\frac{c_{n}}{c_{n-N}}\frac{\mathrm{det}\left[\tau_{r}(n-N+1;x_{i}y_{j},1)\right]_{i,j\leq N}}{\Delta(\mathbf{x})\Delta(\mathbf{y})}

(6)

where
c_{k}=\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}\left(r(i)\right)^{i-k}

r_{\lambda}(n):=\prod_{(i,j)\in\lambda}r(n+j-i),\hskip 10.00002ptr_{(0)}(n)=1

\lambda

\lambda

\mathrm{det}\left[1-X\otimes Y\right]=\sum_{\lambda}s_{\lambda}(X)s_{\lambda}(Y)=\frac{\mathrm{det}\left[(1-x_{i}y_{j})^{-1}\right]_{i,j\leq N}}{\Delta(\mathbf{x})\Delta(\mathbf{y})}
