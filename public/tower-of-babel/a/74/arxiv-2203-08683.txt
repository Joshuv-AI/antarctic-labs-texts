Generalized radius problems for quotient functions having fixed second coefficient
Swati Anand, Naveen Kumar Jain, Sushil Kumar

Abstract
. In this manuscript, we deal with three classes of quotient functions having fixed second coefficient described on open unit disk. The radius of strongly starlikeness, lemniscate starlikeness, lune starlikeness, parabolic starlikeness, sine starlikeness, exponential starlikeness and several other radius estimates for such classes are examined. Relevant relations of obtained radius estimates with the existing estimates are also discussed.

2.
Classes of Quotient Functions

In
[
5
]
, Ali
et al.
discussed radius problem for the functions
f
fulfilling the one of the condition (i)
\RE(f(z)/g(z))>0
(ii)
|(f(z)-g(z))/g(z)|<1
under some restrictions on
g
. These functions are closely associated with close-to-convex functions.
Recently, Sebastian
et al.

[
31
]
computed certain best possible radius estimates for the classes of functions
f\in\mathcal{A}
fulfilling one of the following conditions:
(i)
\RE f(z)/g(z)>0
where
(1+(1/z))g(z)
has positive real part (ii)
|(f(z)-g(z))/g(z)|>0
where
(1+(1/z))g(z)
has positive real part where
g\in\mathcal{A}
, or (iii)
(1+(1/z))f(z)
has positive real part.
Motivated by the aforementioned works, we consider the three classes of quotient functions having fixed second coefficients which are defined as:

The functions
f
and
g
whose Taylor series expansions are given by

(2.1)

f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+\cdots

and

(2.2)

g(z)=z+g_{2}z^{2}+\cdots

such that
(1+z)g(z)/z
and

are in
\mathcal{P}
for all

. Then

\dfrac{1+z}{z}g(z)=1+(1+g_{2})z+\cdots\in\mathcal{P}.

Thus, we get
|1+g_{2}|\leq 2
, that is
-3\leq g_{2}\ \leq 1
. Also,

{f(z)}/{g(z)}=1+(a_{2}-g_{2})z+\cdots\in\mathcal{P}
which gives
|a_{2}-g_{2}|\leq 2
, that is
-5\leq a_{2}\leq 3
. Now, we consider the functions
f
and
g
with fixed second coefficient and having following series expansion

f(z)=z+5bz^{2}+\cdots\,\,\mbox{ and}\,\,g(z)=z+3cz^{2}+\cdots

where
|b|\leq 1
and
|c|\leq 1
.

Definition 2.1
.

For
|b|\leq 1
and
|c|\leq 1
, let
\mathcal{F}_{b,c}^{1}
be defined as

\mathcal{F}_{b,c}^{1}:=\left\{f\in\mathcal{A}_{5b}:\RE\left(\frac{f(z)}{g(z)}\right)>0\,\mbox{and}\RE\left(\frac{1+z}{z}g(z)\right)>0,g\in\mathcal{A}_{3c},z\in\mathbb{D}\right\}.

Let
f
and
g
be two functions given by (
2.1
) and (
2.2
) such that
|(f(z)/g(z))-1|<1
and
\RE((1+z)g(z)/z)>0
. Since

if and only if
\RE(g(z)/f(z))>1/2
and so we have
|a_{2}|\leq 1+|g_{2}|\leq 4
. In the next theorem, we consider the class of functions
f
and
g
with fixed second coefficient whose series expansion is given by

f(z)=z+4bz^{2}+\cdots
and

g(z)=z+3cz^{2}+\cdots
, where
|b|\leq 1
and
|c|\leq 1
.

Definition 2.2
.

For
|b|\leq 1
and
|c|\leq 1
, let the class
\mathcal{F}_{b,c}^{2}
be defined as

\mathcal{F}_{b,c}^{2}:=\left\{f\in\mathcal{A}_{4b}:\left|\frac{f(z)}{g(z)}-1\right|<1\,\,\mbox{and}\,\,\RE\left(\frac{1+z}{z}g(z)\right)>0,\,\,\,g\in\mathcal{A}_{3c},z\in\mathbb{D}\right\}.

We take the function

given by (
2.1
) satisfying
\RE((1+z)f(z)/z)>0
. Then
(1+z)f(z)/z=1+(1+a_{2})z+\cdots\in\mathcal{P}
and hence
-3\leq a_{2}\leq 1
. Next, we consider the class of following functions
f
with fixed second coefficient

f(z)=z+3bz^{2}+\cdots\,\,\,\mbox{where}\,\,\,|b|\leq 1.

Definition 2.3
.

For
|b|\leq 1
, let
\mathcal{F}_{b}^{3}
be defined as

\mathcal{F}_{b}^{3}:=\left\{f\in\mathcal{A}_{3b}:\RE\left(\frac{1+z}{z}f(z)\right)>0,z\in\mathbb{D}\right\}.

The function

so that

It is observed that

satisfies the conditions of Schwarz lemma in

in
\mathbb{D}
and hence

which shows the class
\mathcal{F}_{b}^{3}

In this manuscript, we determine radius estimates of the functions belonging to the classes
