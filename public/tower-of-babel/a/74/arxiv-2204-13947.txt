Limiting distribution of extremal eigenvalues of d-dimensional random Schrödinger operator
Kaito Kawaai, Yugo Maruyama, Fumihiko Nakano

Abstract
We consider Schrödinger operator with random decaying potential on ℓ 2 ​ ( 𝐙 d ) \ell^{2}({\bf Z}^{d}) and showed that, (i) IDS coincides with that of free Laplacian in general cases, (ii) the set of extremal eigenvalues, after rescaling, converges to a inhomogeneous Poisson process, under certain condition on the single-site distribution, and (iii) there are “border-line” cases, such that we have Poisson statistics in the sense of (ii) above if the potential does not decay, while we do not if the potential does decay.
