Light double-gluon hybrid states from QCD sum rules
Niu Su 1, Hua-Xing Chen 1, Wei Chen 2, Shi-Lin Zhu 3

Abstract
We study the double-gluon hybrid states with the quark-gluon contents q ¯ ​ q ​ g ​ g \bar{q}qgg ( q = u / d q=u/d ) and s ¯ ​ s ​ g ​ g \bar{s}sgg . We use the current

\displaystyle if^{T}_{J/\psi}(p^{\alpha}\epsilon^{\beta}-p^{\beta}\epsilon^{\alpha})\,,

\displaystyle\left(f^{T}_{h_{c}}\right)^{2}\epsilon^{\alpha\beta\mu\nu}\epsilon_{\mu}p_{\nu}\epsilon^{\alpha^{\prime}\beta^{\prime}\mu^{\prime}\nu^{\prime}}\epsilon^{*}_{\mu^{\prime}}p_{\nu^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\langle 0|\tilde{J}^{\alpha\beta}|h_{c}(\epsilon,p)\rangle

\displaystyle i\tilde{f}^{T}_{h_{c}}(p^{\alpha}\epsilon^{\beta}-p^{\beta}\epsilon^{\alpha})\,.

III
QCD sum rule analyses

In this section we use the seven currents
J^{\cdots}_{0^{\pm+}/1^{\pm-}/2^{\pm+}/2^{+-}}
to perform QCD sum rule analyses. We use the current
J_{0^{++}}
defined in Eq. (
1
) as an example. Based on Eq. (
16
), we study its two-point correlation function

\Pi(q^{2})=i\int d^{4}xe^{iqx}\langle 0|{\bf T}[J_{0^{++}}(x)J_{0^{++}}^{\dagger}(0)]|0\rangle\,,

(33)

at both the hadron and quark-gluon levels.

At the hadron level, Eq. (
33
) can be expressed by the dispersion relation as

\Pi(q^{2})=\int^{\infty}_{s_{<}}\frac{\rho(s)}{s-q^{2}-i\varepsilon}ds\,,

(34)

where
s_{<}=4m_{q}^{2}
is the physical threshold. The spectral density
\rho(s)\equiv{\rm Im}\Pi(s)/\pi
is parameterized as one pole dominance for the possibly-existing ground state
X\equiv|X;0^{++}\rangle
together with a continuum contribution

\displaystyle\rho_{\rm phen}(s)

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\sum_{n}\delta(s-M^{2}_{n})\langle 0|J_{0^{++}}|n\rangle\langle n|J_{0^{++}}^{\dagger}|0\rangle

(35)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f^{2}_{X}\delta(s-M^{2}_{X})+\rm{continuum}\,.

At the quark-gluon level, Eq. (
33
) can be calculated by the method of operator product expansion (OPE), from which we can extract the OPE spectral density
\rho_{\rm OPE}(s)
. We perform the Borel transformation at both the hadron and quark-gluon levels, and approximate the continuum using
\rho_{\rm OPE}(s)
above the threshold value
s_{0}
, from which we obtain the sum rule equation

\Pi(s_{0},M_{B}^{2})\equiv f^{2}_{X}e^{-M_{X}^{2}/M_{B}^{2}}=\int^{s_{0}}_{s_{<}}e^{-s/M_{B}^{2}}\rho_{\rm OPE}(s)ds\,.

(36)

It can be used to further derive

M^{2}_{X}(s_{0},M_{B})=\frac{\int^{s_{0}}_{s_{<}}e^{-s/M_{B}^{2}}s\rho_{\rm OPE}(s)ds}{\int^{s_{0}}_{s_{<}}e^{-s/M_{B}^{2}}\rho_{\rm OPE}(s)ds}\,.

(37)

In this paper we study the double-gluon hybrid states with the quark-gluon contents
\bar{q}qgg
(
q=u/d
) and
\bar{s}sgg
. We systematically construct twelve double-gluon hybrid currents using the color-octet quark-antiquark field

\bar{q}_{a}\gamma_{5}\lambda_{n}^{ab}q_{b}\,,

and the color-octet double-gluon fields

d^{npq}G_{p}^{\alpha\beta}G_{q}^{\gamma\delta}\,,\,f^{npq}G_{p}^{\alpha\beta}G_{q}^{\gamma\delta}\,.

Since the field
\bar{q}_{a}\gamma_{5}\lambda_{n}^{ab}q_{b}
has the
S
-wave spin-parity quantum number
J^{P}=0^{-}
, these currents may couple to the lowest-lying double-gluon hybrid states.

We apply the method of QCD sum rules to study these currents, and the obtained results are summarized in Table
1
. Note that the five currents
J^{\cdots}_{0^{\pm-}/1^{\pm+}/2^{--}}
vanish due to some internal symmetries between the two gluon fields, but this does not indicate that the double-gluon hybrid states with these quantum numbers do not exist, since more currents can be constructed by combining the color-octet quark-antiquark fields

\bar{q}_{a}\lambda_{n}^{ab}q_{b}\,,\,\bar{q}_{a}\lambda_{n}^{ab}\gamma_{\mu}q_{b}\,,\,\bar{q}_{a}\lambda_{n}^{ab}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}q_{b}\,,\,\bar{q}_{a}\lambda_{n}^{ab}\sigma_{\mu\nu}q_{b}\,,

and the above color-octet double-gluon fields. We shall systematically investigate them in the near future.
