(weakly) ( s , n ) (s,n) -closed hyperideals
Mahdi Anbarloei

Abstract
. A multiplicative hyperring is a well-known type of algebraic hyperstructures which extend a ring to a structure in which the addition is an operation but the multiplication is a hyperoperation. Let G G be a commutative multiplicative hyperring and s , n ∈ ℤ + s,n\in\mathbb{Z}^{+} . A proper hyperideal Q Q of G G is called (weakly) ( s , n ) (s,n) -closed if ( 0 ≠ a s ⊆ Q 0\neq a^{s}\subseteq Q ) a s ⊆ Q a^{s}\subseteq Q for a ∈ G a\in G implies a n ⊆ Q a^{n}\subseteq Q . In this paper, we aim to investigate (weakly) ( s , n ) (s,n) -closed hyperideals and give some results explaining the structures of these notions.
