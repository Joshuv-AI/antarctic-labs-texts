Comparison geometry for integral generalized quasi-Einstein tensor bounds
Sanghun Lee

Abstract
. The purpose of this paper is to extend the mean curvature comparison and volume comparison estimates by Petersen, Sprouse, and Wei to integral generalized quasi–Einstein tensor. Moreover, we use our comparison results to get global diameter estimate.

1.
Introduction

The study of generalized quasi–Einstein manifolds is introduced in
[
6
]
, which is a natural generalization of the Einstein metrics. More precisely, an
n
-dimensional complete Riemannian manifold
M
is a
generalized quasi–Einstein manifold
if there exist three smooth functions
f
,
\mu
, and
\lambda
on
M
satisfying

(1.1)

\displaystyle Ric_{f}^{\,\mu}=\lambda g.

Here,

(1.2)

\displaystyle Ric_{f}^{\,\mu}:=Ric+\mbox{\rm Hess}f-\mu\,df\otimes df,

where
Ric
is the Ricci tensor on
M
and
Hess
f
is the Hessian of
f
. We call (
1.2
) a
generalized quasi–Einstein tensor
. When
\mu=\frac{1}{m}
for a positive integer
m
, the above is called a generalized
m
-quasi–Einstein manifold. We denote
Ric_{f}^{\,m}:=Ric+\mbox{\rm Hess}f-\frac{1}{m}\,df\otimes df
, and we call
Ric_{f}^{\,m}
a
generalized
m
-quasi–Einstein tensor
. The notion of generalized
m
-quasi–Einstein manifold was originated from the study of Einstein warped product manifolds (see
[
2
]
). It plays an important role in the study of the weighted measure (c.f.
[
16
]
). We also note that when
m=\infty
, a generalized
m
-quasi–Einstein tensor becomes

(1.3)

\displaystyle Ric_{f}^{\,\infty}:=Ric_{f}=Ric+\mbox{\rm Hess}f.

In particular, (
1.3
) is called the
Bakry–Emery Ricci tensor
.

In
[
12
]
, Petersen and Wei generalized the classical Bishop-Gromov volume comparison in an integral bound for the Ricci tensor. Before recalling their results, we need some notation. On a Riemannian manifold
M
, let
Ric_{-}
be the smallest eigenvalue for the Ricci tensor
Ric:T_{x}M\rightarrow T_{x}M

and

\displaystyle Ric^{\,H}_{-}:=\left((n-1)H-Ric_{-}\right)_{+}=\mbox{\rm max}\{0,(n-1)H-Ric_{-}\}

for
H\in\mathbb{R}
, the amount of Ricci tensor below
(n-1)H
. Also, we define

(1.4)

\displaystyle\varphi:=\left(m-m_{H}\right)_{+}=\mbox{\rm max}\{0,m-m_{H}\},

where
m
is the mean curvature of the geodesic sphere in the outer normal direction and
m_{H}
is the mean curvature of the geodesic sphere on the model space
M^{n}_{H}
. Here,
M^{n}_{H}
is the
n
-dimensional simply connected space with constant sectional curvature
H
. We notice that if
r(x_{1})=d(x_{1},x_{2})
is the distance function from
x_{2}
to
x_{1}
, then
\Delta(r)=m(r)
. Finally, we introduce a weighted
L^{p}
norm of function
\phi
:

\displaystyle||\phi||_{p}(R)=\sup_{x\in M}\left(\int_{B(x,R)}|\phi|^{p}dv\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Now we recall results of Petersen and Wei .

Theorem 1.1
.

[
12
]

Let
M
be an
n
-dimensional complete Riemannian manifold. For any
p>\frac{n}{2}
,
H\in\mathbb{R}
(assume
r\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
),

\displaystyle||\varphi||_{2p}(r)\leq\left(\frac{(n-1)(2p-1)}{2p-n}||Ric^{\,H}_{-}||_{p}(r)\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Here,
\varphi
is defined in (
1.4
). Furthermore, for any
0<r<R
(assume
R\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
), there exists a constant
C(n,p,H,R)
which is nondecreasing in
R
such that

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V(x,R)}{V_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p}}-\left(\frac{V(x,r)}{V_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p}}\leq C(n,p,H,R)\left(||Ric^{\,H}_{-}||_{p}(R)||\right)^{\frac{1}{2}},

where
V(x,R)
is the volume of ball
B(x,R)
in
M
, and
V_{H}(R)
is the volume of ball
B(O,R)
in the model space
M_{H}
.

Using this comparison theorem, Petersen and Wei extended classical results such as Colding’s volume convergence, Cheerger-Colding splitting theorem, Gromov precompactness theorem, and pinching result. Petersen and Sprouse extended the above comparison results and generalized Myers theorem in
[
11
]
.

On the other hand, Wu generalized Petersen, Sprouse, and Wei results
[
11
,
12
,
13
]
using Bakry–Emery Ricci tensor. In addition, Wu proved generalized Myers theorem, relative volume comparison for annulus, eigenvalue estimate, and volume growth estimate in
[
17
]
.

In this paper, we generalize Petersen, Sprouse, and Wei comparison results using to generalized quasi–Einstein tensor. Moreover, applying comparison results for generalized quasi–Einstein tensor, we prove diameter estimate. Given an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
, where
f
is a smooth real valued function on
M
and
dv
is the usual Riemannian volume element on
M
. For the measure
e^{-f}dv
, the
f
-mean curvature is
m_{f}=m-\partial_{r}f
, where
m
is the mean curvature of the geodesic sphere in the outer normal direction. The self-adjoint
f
-Laplacian with respect to the measure is
\Delta_{f}=\Delta-\nabla f\cdot\nabla
. Note that
m_{f}(r)=\Delta_{f}(r)
, where
r
is the distance function.

Let
Ric^{\,\mu}_{f-}
be the smallest eigenvalue for the generalized quasi–Einstein tensor
Ric^{\,\mu}_{f}:T_{x}M\rightarrow T_{x}M
and

\displaystyle Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}:=\left((n+k-1)H-Ric^{\,\mu}_{f-}\right)_{+}={\rm max}\,\{0,(n+k-1)H-Ric^{\,\mu}_{f-}\}

for
H\in\mathbb{R}
, the amount of
Ric^{\,\mu}_{f}
below
(n-1)H
. We define a function
\varphi
as follows:

(1.5)

\displaystyle\varphi:=(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H})_{+}={\rm max}\,\{0,m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}\},

where
m_{f}=m-\partial_{r}f
and
m^{n+k}_{H}
is the mean curvature of the geodesic sphere in the model space
M^{n+k}_{H}
. Also, we introduce a weighted
L^{p}
norm of function
\phi
on
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
:

\displaystyle||\phi||_{p,\,f}(r):=\left(\int_{B(x,r)}|\phi|^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}dtd\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Consider the quantity as

\displaystyle\bar{k}(p,H,r):=\sup_{x\in M}\left(\frac{1}{V_{f}(x,r)}\int_{B(x,r)}\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}dtd\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Here,
\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)
is the volume element of weighted form
e^{-f}dv=\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)dt\wedge d\theta_{n-1}
in polar coordinate and
d\theta_{n-1}
is the volume element on unit sphere
S^{n-1}
. Clearly,
Ric^{\,\mu}_{f}\geq(n+k-1)H
if and only if
\bar{k}(p,H,r)=0
.

The following is a mean curvature comparison estimate for generalized quasi–Einstein tensor.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
be an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space. Assume that
\mu\geq\frac{1}{k}
for some positive constant
k
. For any
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
,
H\in\mathbb{R}
(assume
r\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
),

(1.6)

\displaystyle||\varphi||_{2p,\,f}(r)\leq\left(\frac{(n+k-1)(2p-1)}{2p-n-k}||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

and

(1.7)

\displaystyle\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\leq(2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\int^{r}_{0}\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

Here,
\varphi
is the function defined in (
1.5
). Furthermore, if
H>0
and
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}<r<\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{H}}
, then we have

(1.8)

\displaystyle||sin^{\frac{4p-n-k-1}{2p}}(\sqrt{H}t)\varphi||_{2p,\,f}(r)\leq\left(\frac{(n+k-1)(2p-1)}{2p-n-k}||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

and

(1.9)

\displaystyle sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}r)\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\leq(2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}

\displaystyle\times\int^{r}_{0}\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

Secondly, we prove the following volume comparison estimate.

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
be an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space. Assume that
\mu\geq\frac{1}{k}
for some positive constant
k
. For
H\in\mathbb{R}
,
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
, and
0<r\leq R
(assume
R\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
),

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,R)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,R)

\displaystyle\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}},

where

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,R)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\displaystyle\int^{R}_{0}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\left(\frac{t}{V^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt

and
V^{n+k}_{H}(R)
is the volume of ball
B(O,R)
in the model space
M^{n+k}_{H}
for
O\in M^{n+k}_{H}
.

Using above theorems, we prove the following relative volume comparison for annulus as follows.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
be an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space. Assume that
\mu\geq\frac{1}{k}
for some positive constant
k
. Let
H\in\mathbb{R}
and
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
. For
0\leq r_{1}\leq r_{2}\leq R_{1}\leq R_{2}
(assume
R_{2}\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
),

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2},R_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},R_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{1},R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1},R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,r_{1},r_{2},R_{1},R_{2})

\displaystyle\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R_{2})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}},

where

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,r_{1},r_{2},R_{1},R_{2}):=\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\left(\int^{R_{2}}_{R_{1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\left(\frac{t}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},t)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt+\int^{r_{2}}_{r_{1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R_{1})\left(\frac{R_{1}}{V^{n+k}_{H}(t,R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt\right).

Finally, we apply the integral comparison results (Theorem
1.2
and Theorem
1.3
). That is, we obtain global diameter estimate for generalized quasi–Einstein tensor.

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
be an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space. Assume that
\mu\geq\frac{1}{k}
for some positive constant
k
. Given
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
,
H>0
, and
R>0
, there exist
D(n+k,H)
and
\epsilon=\epsilon(n+k,p,H,R)
such that if
\bar{k}(p,H,R)<\epsilon
, then
\mbox{\rm diam(M)}\leq D
.

This paper is summarized as follows. In Section 2, we prove Theorem
1.2
. In Section 3, we apply Theorem
1.2
to prove Theorem
1.3
and as corollary we prove volume doubling result when the integral generalized quasi–Einstein tensor bounds. Moreover, using Theorem
1.3
, we obtain relative volume comparison for annulus. In Section 4, we give application of the integral comparison results. In other words, we apply Theorem
1.2
and Theorem
1.3
to get diameter estimate (Theorem
1.5
).

2.
Mean curvaute comparison estimate

In this section, we prove mean curvature estimate for the integral generalized quasi–Einstein tensor. That is, we prove Theorem
1.2
. The proof uses Bochner formula.

Applying the Bochner formula

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\Delta|\nabla u|^{2}=|\mbox{\rm Hess}\,u|^{2}+\langle\nabla u,\nabla(\Delta u)\rangle+Ric(\nabla u,\nabla u)

to the distance function
r(x)=d(x,p)
, we obtain

\displaystyle 0=|\mbox{\rm Hess}\,r|^{2}+\frac{r}{\partial r}(\Delta r)+Ric(\nabla r,\nabla r).

Since
\mbox{\rm Hess}\,\,r
is the second fundamental form of the geodesic sphere and
\Delta r
is the mean curvaure, by the Schwarz inequality, we have

\displaystyle m^{\prime}\leq-\frac{m^{2}}{n-1}-Ric(\nabla r,\nabla r).

And equality holds if and only if the radial sectional curvatures are constant. Hence the mean curvature of the
n
-dimensional model space
m_{H}
satisfies

\displaystyle m^{\prime}_{H}=-\frac{m^{2}_{H}}{n-1}-(n-1)H.

Since
m_{f}=m-\partial_{r}f
and
m^{\prime}_{f}=m^{\prime}-\mbox{\rm Hess}\,f(\partial r,\partial r)
, we have

\displaystyle m^{\prime}_{f}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{m^{2}}{n-1}-Ric(\partial r,\partial r)-\mbox{\rm Hess}\,f(\partial r,\partial r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{\left(m_{f}+\langle\nabla f,\nabla r\rangle(t)\right)^{2}}{n-1}-Ric(\partial r,\partial r)-\mbox{\rm Hess}\,f(\partial r,\partial r).

Using the element inequality
(a+b)^{2}\geq\frac{a^{2}}{\alpha+1}-\frac{b^{2}}{\alpha}
, for all real number
a
,
b
and positive real number
\alpha
, we get

\displaystyle m^{\prime}_{f}\leq-\frac{m^{2}_{f}}{(n-1)(\alpha+1)}+\frac{\left(\langle\nabla f,\nabla r\rangle(t)\right)^{2}}{(n-1)\alpha}-Ric(\partial r,\partial r)-\mbox{\rm Hess}\,f(\partial r,\partial r).

Let
(n-1)\alpha=k
. Then we obtain

\displaystyle m^{\prime}_{f}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{m^{2}_{f}}{n+k-1}+\frac{1}{k}\left(\langle\nabla f,\nabla r\rangle(t)\right)^{2}-Ric(\partial r,\partial r)-\mbox{\rm Hess}\,f(\partial r,\partial r)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{m^{2}_{f}}{n+k-1}+\mu\left(\langle\nabla f,\nabla r\rangle(t)\right)^{2}-Ric(\partial r,\partial r)-\mbox{\rm Hess}\,f(\partial r,\partial r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{m^{2}_{f}}{n+k-1}-Ric^{\,\mu}_{f}(\partial r,\partial r).

We know that
\left(m^{n+k}_{H}\right)^{\prime}=-\frac{\left(m^{n+k}_{H}\right)^{2}}{n+k-1}-(n+k-1)H
. So we compute

\displaystyle\left(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}\right)^{\prime}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{m^{2}_{f}}{n+k-1}-Ric^{\,\mu}_{f}+\frac{\left(m^{n+k}_{H}\right)^{2}}{n+k-1}+(n+k-1)H

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{\left(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}\right)\left(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}+2m^{n+k}_{H}\right)}{n+k-1}

\displaystyle+(n+k-1)H-Ric^{\,\mu}_{f}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{\left(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}\right)\left(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}+2m^{n+k}_{H}\right)}{n+k-1}+Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}.

Note that on the interval where
m_{f}\leq m^{n+k}_{H}
, we have
\varphi=0
and where
m_{f}>m^{n+k}_{H}
, we have
\varphi=m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H}
. Then we obtain

\displaystyle\varphi^{\prime}+\frac{\varphi}{n+k-1}(\varphi+2m^{n+k}_{H})\leq Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}.

Multiplying the above inequality by
(2p-1)\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}
, we get

\displaystyle(2p-1)\varphi^{2p-2}\varphi^{\prime}\mathcal{A}_{f}+\frac{2p-1}{n+k-1}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}+\frac{4p-2}{n+k-1}\varphi^{2p-1}m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}_{f}

\displaystyle\quad\leq(2p-1)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}.

Using

\displaystyle(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f})^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(2p-1)\varphi^{2p-2}\varphi^{\prime}\mathcal{A}_{f}+\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A^{\prime}}_{f}

\displaystyle(2p-1)\varphi^{2p-2}\varphi^{\prime}\mathcal{A}_{f}+\varphi^{2p-1}m_{f}\mathcal{A}_{f},

we can be rewritten as

\displaystyle(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f})^{\prime}-\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H})-\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}m^{n+k}_{H}+\frac{2p-1}{n+k-1}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{4p-2}{n+k-1}\varphi^{2p-1}m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}_{f}\leq(2p-1)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}.

We know that
-\varphi\leq-(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H})
. So we rearrange the above inequality as

(2.1)

\displaystyle(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f})^{\prime}+\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\left(\frac{2p-1}{n+k-1}-1\right)+\varphi^{2p-1}m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}_{f}\left(\frac{4p-2}{n+k-1}-1\right)

\displaystyle\quad\leq(2p-1)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}.

Since
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
and the assumption
r\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
, we have

\displaystyle\varphi^{2p-1}m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}_{f}\left(\frac{4p-2}{n+k-1}-1\right)\geq 0.

Then we obtain

\displaystyle(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f})^{\prime}+\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\left(\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\right)\leq(2p-1)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}.

Integrating the above inequality from
0
to
r
, we get

\displaystyle\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}+\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

This implies that

\displaystyle\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

and

\displaystyle\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

First, by Holder inequality, we have

\displaystyle\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\leq(2p-1)\left(\int^{r}_{0}\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{1-\frac{1}{p}}.

Multiplying the above inequailty by
\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\left(\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{-1+\frac{1}{p}}
, we obtain

(2.2)

\displaystyle\left(\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\leq\frac{(n+k-1)(2p-1)}{2p-n-k}\left(\int^{r}_{0}\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Take a root on both sides. Then we get (
1.6
).

Secondly, we also have

(2.3)

\displaystyle\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\leq(2p-1)\left(\int^{r}_{0}\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int^{r}_{0}\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{1-\frac{1}{p}}.

Combining (
2.2
) and (
2.3
), we immediately yield (
1.7
).

If
H>0
and
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}<r<\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{H}}
, then
m^{n+k}_{H}<0
. We know that

\displaystyle(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f})^{\prime}+\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\left(\frac{2p-1}{n+k-1}-1\right)+\varphi^{2p-1}m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}_{f}\left(\frac{4p-2}{n+k-1}-1\right)

\displaystyle\quad\leq(2p-1)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}.

Multiplying the above inequality by
sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}r)
and integrating from
0
to
r
, we obtain

\displaystyle\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\left(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\right)^{\prime}dt+\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{4p-n-k-1}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\varphi^{2p-1}m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

\displaystyle\qquad\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

Note that

\displaystyle\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\left(\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\right)^{\prime}dt

\displaystyle\quad=-\frac{4p-n-k-1}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)m^{n+k}_{H}\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

\displaystyle\qquad+sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}r)\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}.

So we have

\displaystyle sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}r)\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}+\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

This implies that

(2.4)

\displaystyle sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}r)\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}

\displaystyle\quad\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

and

(2.5)

\displaystyle\frac{2p-n-k}{n+k-1}\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq(2p-1)\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt.

Similar to the above discussion, by Holder inequality, we get

(2.6)

\displaystyle\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\varphi^{2p-2}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\varphi^{2p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{1-\frac{1}{p}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(\int^{r}_{0}sin^{4p-n-k-1}(\sqrt{H}t)\left(Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}\right)^{p}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Substituting (
2.6
) into (
2.5
), then we obtain (
1.8
). Similarly, putting (
1.8
) and (
2.6
) to (
2.4
) immediately yields (
1.9
).

3.
Volume comparison estimates

In this section, we prove volume comparison results using mean curvature estimate. Before proving Theorem
1.3
, we prove volume element comparison estimate when the integral generalized quasi–Einstein tensor bounds.

For an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
,
\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)
is the volume element of the weighted volume form
e^{-f}dv=\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)dt\wedge d\theta
in the polar coordinate. In other words,
\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)=e^{-f}\mathcal{A}(t,\theta)
, where
\mathcal{A}(t,\theta)
is the standard volume element of the metric
g
. Let

\displaystyle A_{f}(x,r)=\int_{S^{n-1}}\mathcal{A}_{f}(r,\theta)d\theta_{n-1}.

Similarly, we also let

\displaystyle A^{n+k}_{H}(r)=\int_{S^{n-1}}\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}(r,\theta)d\theta,

where
\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}
is the volume element in the model space
M^{n+k}_{H}
.

Now we prove volume element comparison estimate using the mean curvature estimate in Section 2.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
be an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space. Assume that
\mu\geq\frac{1}{k}
for some positive constant
k
. For
H\in\mathbb{R}
,
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
, and
0<r\leq R
(assume
R\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
), we have

(3.1)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{A_{f}(x,R)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{A_{f}(x,r)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq\mathcal{C}(n+k,p,H,R)

\displaystyle\quad\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}},

where
\mathcal{C}(n+k,p,H,R):=\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-n-k)(2p-1)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\int^{R}_{0}\left(A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\right)^{-\frac{1}{2p-1}}dt
.

Moreover, if
H>0
and
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}<r\leq R<\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{H}}
, then we obtain

(3.2)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{A_{f}(x,R)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{A_{f}(x,r)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int^{R}_{r}\frac{(\sqrt{H})^{\frac{n+k-1}{2p-1}}}{sin^{2}(t\sqrt{H})}\,dt.

Proof of Theorem
3.1
.

We apply
\mathcal{A^{\prime}}_{f}=m_{f}\mathcal{A}_{f}
and
(\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H})^{\prime}=m^{n+k}_{H}\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}
to compute that

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)}{\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)}{\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}(t)}(m_{f}-m^{n+k}_{H})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)}{\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\varphi.

So we have

(3.3)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{vol(S^{n-1})}\int_{S^{n-1}}\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)}{\mathcal{A}^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)d\theta_{n-1}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\int_{S^{n-1}}\varphi\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)\,d\theta_{n-1}.

By Holder inequality and (
1.7
), we obtain

(3.4)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)\leq

\displaystyle\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(t)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Note that

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)\cdot\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{-1+\frac{1}{2p-1}}=(2p-1)\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Then we get

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-n-k)(2p-1)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(t)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Integrating from
r
to
R
, we have

\displaystyle\left(\frac{A_{f}(R,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{A_{f}(r,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-n-k)(2p-1)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\int^{R}_{0}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}dt.

For
H>0
and
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}<r\leq R<\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{H}}
, by (
1.9
), we obtain

(3.5)

\displaystyle\left(\int_{S^{n-1}}\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}d\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}sin^{\frac{-4p+n+k+1}{2p-1}}(\sqrt{H}t)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(t)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}.

Note that

(3.6)

\displaystyle\int_{S^{n-1}}\varphi\mathcal{A}_{f}(t,\theta)\,d\theta_{n-1}\leq\left(\int_{S^{n-1}}\varphi^{2p-1}\mathcal{A}_{f}\,d\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}A_{f}(t,x)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Putting (
3.5
) and (
3.6
) into (
3.3
), we get

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}sin^{\frac{-4p+n+k+1}{2p-1}}(\sqrt{H}t)

\displaystyle\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(t)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{A_{f}(t,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Separating of variables and integrating from
r
to
R
, we yield

\displaystyle\left(\frac{A_{f}(R,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{A_{f}(r,x)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\int^{R}_{r}\frac{(\sqrt{H})^{\frac{n+k-1}{2p-1}}}{sin^{2}(t\sqrt{H})}\,dt.

Now we prove Theorem
1.3
using Theorem
3.1
above. The argument is similar.

Proof of Theorem
1.3
.

Using

\displaystyle\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}=\frac{\int^{r}_{0}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt}{\int^{r}_{0}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt},

we compute

(3.7)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)=\frac{A_{f}(x,r)\int^{r}_{0}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt-A^{n+k}_{H}(r)\int^{r}_{0}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt}{\left(V^{n+k}_{H}(r)\right)^{2}}.

On the other hand, integrating both sides of (
3.4
) from
t
to
r
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{A_{f}(x,r)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(r)}-\frac{A_{f}(x,t)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\frac{\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}}{\left(A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int^{r}_{t}\left(A_{f}(x,s)\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}ds

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\frac{\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\left(r-t\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(V_{f}(x,r)\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\frac{\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}r^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(V_{f}(x,r)\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

This implies that

\displaystyle A_{f}(x,r)\int^{r}_{0}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt-A^{n+k}_{H}(r)\int^{r}_{0}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}r^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(r)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(V_{f}(x,r)\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Inserting this inequality into (
3.7
) gives

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\frac{\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}}{\left(V^{n+k}_{H}(r)\right)^{2}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times A^{n+k}_{H}(r)\left(V_{f}(x,r)\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}r^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(r)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{r}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}.

Separating of variables, we get

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(r)\left(\frac{r}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}.

Integrating from
r
to
R
, we obtain

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,R)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int^{R}_{r}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\left(\frac{t}{V^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int^{R}_{0}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\left(\frac{t}{V^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt.

Hence, we complete the proof of Theorem
1.3
.

Next, As a corollary we have the volume doubling result.

Corollary 3.2
.

Let
(M,g,e^{-f}dv)
be an
n
-dimensional smooth metric measure space. Assume that
\mu\geq\frac{1}{k}
for some positive constant
k
. For
\beta>1
and
p>\frac{n+k}{2}
, there is an
\epsilon=\epsilon(n+k,p,H,R,\beta)
such that if
\bar{k}(p,H,R)<\epsilon
, then for all
x\in M
and
0<r_{1}<r_{2}\leq R
(assume
R\leq\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{H}}
when
H>0
), we have

(3.8)

\displaystyle\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}{V_{f}(x,r_{1})}\leq\beta\cdot\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1})}.

Proof of Corollary
3.2
.

By Theorem
1.3
, we obtain

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\geq\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-C(n+k,p,H,r_{2})\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(r_{2})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}.

Multiplying
\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1})}{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}
on the above inequality, then we get

(3.9)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{1})}{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\geq\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(1-\sigma(r_{2})\right),

where

\displaystyle\sigma(r_{2})

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,r_{2})V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\frac{1}{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}\int_{B(x,r_{2})}|Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}|^{p}A_{f}\,dtd\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Similarly, we have for
R

(3.10)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}{V_{f}(x,R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\geq\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(1-\sigma(R)\right),

where

\displaystyle\sigma(R)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,R)V^{n+k}_{H}(R)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\frac{1}{V_{f}(x,R)}\int_{B(x,R)}|Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}|^{p}A_{f}\,dtd\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Since
C(n+k,p,H,r)
is increasing in
r
, we obtain

(3.11)

\displaystyle\sigma(r_{2})\leq\sigma(R)\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,R)}{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Note that

\displaystyle\sigma(r_{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,r_{2})V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\sup_{x\in M}\left(\frac{1}{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}\int_{B(x,r_{2})}|Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}|^{p}A_{f}\,dtd\theta_{n-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,r_{2})V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\bar{k}(p,H,r_{2})^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}.

Similarly, we also have

\displaystyle\sigma(R)\leq C(n+k,p,H,R)V^{n+k}_{H}(R)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\bar{k}(p,H,R)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}.

Let
\epsilon(n+k,p,H,R,\beta)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}=\frac{\left(1-\left(\frac{1}{\beta}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\right)}{3C(n+k,p,H,R)V^{n+k}_{H}(R)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}}
. Substituting this on the above inequality, we obtain

\displaystyle\sigma(R)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(n+k,p,H,R)V^{n+k}_{H}(R)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\frac{2}{3C(n+k,p,H,R)V^{n+k}_{H}(R)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}.

Putting this into (
3.10
) yields

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}{V_{f}(x,R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\geq\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

By (
3.11
), we get

\displaystyle\sigma(r_{2})\leq 3\sigma(R).

This implies that

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{1})}{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\geq\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(1-3\sigma(R)\right).

On the other hand, using

\displaystyle\epsilon(n+k,p,H,R,\beta)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}=\frac{\left(1-\left(\frac{1}{\beta}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\right)}{3C(n+k,p,H,R)V^{n+k}_{H}(R)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}},

we have

\displaystyle\sigma(R)\leq\frac{1-\left(\frac{1}{\beta}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}}{3}.

Hence, we can deduce

\displaystyle\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2})}{V_{f}(x,r_{1})}\leq\beta\cdot\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1})},

where
\beta>1
.

Finally, we study the volume comparison estimate for annulus. The idea of proof is similar to Theorem
1.3
.

Proof of Theorem
1.4
.

By (
3.7
), we have

(3.12)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dR}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r,R)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R)}\right)=\frac{A_{f}(x,R)\int^{R}_{r}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt-A^{n+k}_{H}(R)\int^{R}_{r}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt}{\left(V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R)\right)^{2}}.

Similarly, we also have

(3.13)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r,R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R_{1})}\right)

\displaystyle\quad=\frac{-A_{f}(x,r)\int^{R_{1}}_{r}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt+A^{n+k}_{H}(r)\int^{R_{1}}_{r}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt}{\left(V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R_{1})\right)^{2}}.

Integrating (
3.4
) from
t
to
R
yields

\displaystyle\left(\frac{A_{f}(x,R)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(R)}\right)-\left(\frac{A_{f}(x,t)}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int^{R}_{t}A_{f}(x,s)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}ds

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\times(R-t)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(\int^{R}_{t}A_{f}(x,s)\,ds\right)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{A^{n+k}_{H}(t)}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\times R^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}V_{f}(x,t,R)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Multiplying
A^{n+k}_{H}(R)A^{n+k}_{H}(t)
on the above inequality, then we obtain

\displaystyle A_{f}(x,R)A^{n+k}_{H}(t)-A_{f}(x,t)A^{n+k}_{H}(R)

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}R^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times A^{n+k}_{H}(R)V_{f}(x,t,R)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Integrating this inequality from
r
to
R
, we get

(3.14)

\displaystyle A_{f}(x,R)\int^{R}_{r}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt-A^{n+k}_{H}(R)\int^{R}_{r}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times(R-r)R^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R)V_{f}(x,r,R)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times R^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R)V_{f}(x,r,R)^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

On the other hand, similar to the argument, we also get

(3.15)

\displaystyle A^{n+k}_{H}(r)\int^{R_{1}}_{r}A_{f}(x,t)\,dt-A_{f}(x,r)\int^{R_{1}}_{r}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\,dt

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left((2p-1)^{p}\left(\frac{n+k-1}{2p-n-k}\right)^{p-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R_{1})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times R_{1}^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R_{1})V_{f}(x,r,R_{1})^{1-\frac{1}{2p-1}}.

Substituting (
3.14
) into (
3.12
) gives

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dR}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r,R)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R)}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R)\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\frac{R}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R).

Separating of variables, integrating this inequality from
R_{1}
to
R_{2}
and changing the variable
r
to
r_{2}
, we have

(3.16)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2},R_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},R_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2},R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\int^{R_{2}}_{R_{1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\left(\frac{t}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},t)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R_{2})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}.

On the other hand, combining (
3.13
) and (
3.15
), we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r,R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R_{1})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\times A^{n+k}_{H}(R_{1})\left(\frac{R_{1}}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r,R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}.

Integrating the above inequality from
r_{1}
to
r_{2}
, we get

(3.17)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2},R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{1},R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1},R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R_{1})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R_{1})

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int^{r_{2}}_{r_{1}}\left(\frac{R_{1}}{V^{n+k}_{H}(t,R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt.

Adding (
3.16
) and (
3.17
) gives

\displaystyle\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{2},R_{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},R_{2})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}-\left(\frac{V_{f}(x,r_{1},R_{1})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{1},R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{1}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left(\frac{n+k-1}{(2p-1)(2p-n-k)}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{2p-1}}\left(||Ric^{\,\mu,H}_{f-}||_{p,\,f}(R_{1})\right)^{\frac{p}{2p-1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left(\int^{R_{2}}_{R_{1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(t)\left(\frac{t}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r_{2},t)}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt+\int^{r_{2}}_{r_{1}}A^{n+k}_{H}(R_{1})\left(\frac{R_{1}}{V^{n+k}_{H}(t,R_{1})}\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-1}}dt\right)

for
0\leq r_{1}\leq r_{2}\leq R_{1}\leq R_{2}
.

4.
Diameter estimate

In this section, using mean curvature comparison estimate and volume comparison estimates, we prove the global diameter estimate.

Let
p_{1}
and
p_{2}
are two points in
M
and
x_{0}
be a midpoint between
p_{1}
and
p_{2}
. Consider the excess function

\displaystyle e(x)=d(p_{1},x)+d(p_{2},x)-d(p_{1},p_{2}).

Note that
e(x)\geq 0
in
M
and
e(x)\leq 2r
on
B(x_{0},r)
by the triangle inequality. Using this fact, we want to prove our result by contradiction. That is, we will show that the excess function
e
is negative on
B(x_{0},r)
.

From the mean curvature comparison estimate, by using a suitably lare comparison sphere we may choose any large
D
enough so that if
d(p_{1},p_{2})>D
, then we have
\Delta_{f}\,e\leq-K+\psi_{1}
on
B(x_{0},r)
, where
K
is a large positive constant to be determined, and
\psi_{1}
denotes an error term controlled by
C_{1}(n+k,p,H,r)\cdot\bar{k}(p,H,r)
.

Let
\Omega_{j}
be a sequence of smooth star-shaped domains which converges to
B(x_{0},r)-\mbox{\rm Cut}(x_{0})
and
u_{i}
be a sequence of smooth functions such that
|u_{i}-e|<i^{-1}
,
|\nabla u_{i}|\leq 2+i^{-1}
, and
\Delta_{f}\,u_{i}\leq\Delta_{f}\,e+i^{-1}
on
B(x_{0},r)
(see
[
7
]
). Set
h=d^{2}(x_{0},\cdot)-r^{2}
, we have that
h
is a negative and smooth function on
\Omega_{j}
. By Green’s theorem, we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega_{j}}\left(\Delta_{f}\,u_{i}\right)h-\int_{\Omega_{j}}\left(\Delta_{f}\,h\right)u_{i}=\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}h\left(u_{i}\,\nu\right)-\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}u_{i}\left(h\,\nu\right),

where
\nu
is the ourward unit normal to
\Omega_{j}
. Note that

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\,u_{i}\leq-K+\psi_{1}+i^{-1}

and

\displaystyle\left(\Delta_{f}\,h\right)u_{i}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(e+i^{-1}\right)\left(2d\Delta_{f}\,d+2\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 3r\left(2(n+k)+\psi_{2}\right),

where
\psi_{2}
is another error term controlled by
C_{2}(n+k,p,H,r)\cdot\bar{k}(p,H,r)
. Thus, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega_{j}}\left(-K+\psi_{1}+i^{-1}\right)h-3r\int_{\Omega_{j}}\left(2(n+k)+\psi_{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}(i^{-1}-e)(\nu\,h)

\displaystyle-\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}h(2+i^{-1}).

Since
u_{i}\rightarrow e
when
i\rightarrow\infty
, by the dominated convergence theorem, the above inequality becomes

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega_{j}}(-K+\psi_{1})h-3r\int_{\Omega_{j}}(2n+2k+\psi_{2})\leq-2\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}h-\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}e(\nu\,h).

Also, we compute that

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},r)}(-K+\psi_{1})h

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\int_{B(x_{0},r)}Kh+\int_{B(x_{0},r)}\psi_{1}h

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-\int_{B(x_{0},\frac{r}{2})}Kh+\int_{B(x_{0},r)}\psi_{1}h

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},\frac{r}{2})}K\frac{3}{r}r^{2}-\int_{B(x_{0},r)}\psi_{1}r^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{3}{4}r^{2}KV_{f}\left(x_{0},\frac{r}{2}\right)-\int_{B(x_{0},r)}\psi_{1}r^{2}.

So we get

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},r)}(-K+\psi_{1})h-3r\int_{B(x_{0},r)}(2n+2k+\psi_{2})

\displaystyle\quad\geq\frac{3}{4}r^{2}KV_{f}\left(x_{0},\frac{r}{2}\right)-6r(n+k)V_{f}\left(x_{0},\frac{r}{2}\right)-\int_{B(x_{0},r)}r^{2}\psi_{1}+3r\psi_{2}.

By the volume comparison estimate, we obtain

\displaystyle V_{f}\left(x_{0},\frac{r}{2}\right)\geq 2^{-1}\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(\frac{r}{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}V_{f}(x_{0},r).

Moreover, if
\bar{k}(p,H,r)
is small, we also have

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},r)}(r^{2}\psi_{1}+3r\psi_{2})\leq(r^{2}+nr)V_{f}(x_{0},r).

Hence, we get

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},r)}(-K+\psi_{1})h-3r\int_{B(x_{0},r)}(2n+2k+\psi_{2})

\displaystyle\quad\geq r^{2}V_{f}(x_{0},r)\left(\frac{3}{8}\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(\frac{r}{2})}{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}K-1-7r^{-1}(n+k)\right).

Thus for
K>\frac{8}{3}\left(\frac{V^{n+k}_{H}(r)}{V^{n+k}_{H}(\frac{r}{2})}\right)(7r^{-1}(n+k)+1)
, the above inequality is positive. This implies that

\displaystyle-2\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}h-\int_{\partial\Omega_{j}}e(\nu\,h)>0

as
j\rightarrow\infty
. However the first integral term goes to
0
as
j\rightarrow\infty
, while the second ingeral term :
\nu\,h\geq 0
on
\partial\Omega_{j}
for all
j
. This implies that
e
must be nagative on
B(x_{0},r)
, which is a contradiction. So
d(p_{1},p_{2})<D
for some
D
.
