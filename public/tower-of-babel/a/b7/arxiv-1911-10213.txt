Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz for Fishnet CFT
Benjamin Basso, Gwenaël Ferrando, Vladimir Kazakov, De-liang Zhong

Abstract
We present the TBA equations and the Y-system for the exact spectrum of general multi-magnon local operators in the D D -dimensional anisotropic version of the bi-scalar fishnet CFT. The mixing matrix of such operators is given in terms of fishnet planar graphs of multi-wheel and multi-spiral type. These graphs probe the two main building blocks of the TBA approach that are the magnon dispersion relation and the magnon scattering matrix and which we both obtain by diagonalising suitable graph-building operators. We also obtain the dual version of the TBA equations, which relates, in the continuum limit, D D -dimensional graphs to two dimensional sigma models in A ​ d ​ S D + 1 AdS_{D+1} . It allows us to verify a general formula obtained by A. Zamolodchikov for the critical coupling.

TBA for ground state

We turn now to the TBA equations defining the divergent part of the wheel graph on figure

The latter Y functions are part of a larger family of functions

IV
Dual TBA and Y-system

The TBA equations above give us a good handle on the scaling dimension at weak coupling, which is when the massive Y functions are small and the equations are solvable iteratively. \log\xi_{c}^{2}=\int_{0}^{\infty}\left[\frac{D}{2}e^{-t}+\frac{e^{-\delta t}-e^{\delta t}+e^{-\tilde{\delta}t}-e^{\tilde{\delta}t}}{(1-e^{-t})(1+e^{\frac{Dt}{2}})}\right]\frac{\differential t}{t}

(28)

V
Excited States and Asymptotic Bethe Ansatz

The TBA equations can be generalized to the states with an arbitrary number of magnons by the usual trick of the contour deformation
Dorey and Tateo 1996
;
Bazhanov et al. 1997
. The multi-magnon operators
\mathcal{O}_{J,M}
, associated to spiral graphs shown in right panel of figure
1
, are made out of scalar magnons (
l=0
) and obtained by exciting the corresponding Y function. Most of the formulae stay the same, if not for the energy (
12
) and the equations (
13
) that receive additional driving terms. In particular the anomalous dimensions
\gamma_{M}=\Delta-(J\tilde{\delta}+M\delta)
of the multi-magnon states read

\gamma_{M}=\sum_{m=1}^{M}(2\mathrm{i}u_{m}-\delta)-\sum_{l\geq 0}\int p_{l}^{\prime}(u)\mathrm{L}_{1,l}(u)\frac{\differential u}{2\pi}

(31)

with the first term in the rhs coming from the logarithmic poles at
\mathrm{Y}_{1,0}(u_{m})=-1
. The latter conditions are the exact Bethe ansatz equations, which reduce at large
J
and for sufficiently weak coupling to the ABA equations

1=\xi^{2J}e^{-\varepsilon_{0}(u_{j})J}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
k\neq j\end{subarray}}^{M}\mathbb{S}_{0,0}(u_{j},u_{k})\,.

(32)

In this case, since all spins
l,l^{\prime}=0
, the R matrix trivializes.
It should be supplemented with the trace cyclicity condition

\prod_{j=1}^{M}\xi^{2}e^{-\varepsilon_{0}(u_{j})}=1\,.

(33)

This generalizes the
4D
ABA equations of
Caetano et al. 2016
to any dimension
D
and any anisotropy.

As an example, in the simplest
M=1
case, the ABA equation predicts that the anomalous dimension is given by
(2\mathrm{i}u-\delta)
,
where
u
is the solution to

\xi^{2}e^{-\varepsilon_{0}(u)}=1
. Expanding
u
perturbatively in
\xi^{2}
around the classical value
-\mathrm{i}\delta/2
, we find the one-magnon anomalous dimension

\gamma_{M=1}=\frac{-2\xi^{2}}{\Gamma\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)}+2\xi^{4}\frac{\psi(\delta)+\psi(\tilde{\delta})-\psi\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)-\psi(1)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^{2}}+O(\xi^{6})

(34)

which agrees with the direct field theory computation.

As further checks, we considered two-magnon states for
J=5
. The corresponding mixing matrix takes the same form as for
D=4

Caetano et al. 2016
. We have checked that the ABA prediction agrees with the direct diagrammatic computation through 3 loops.

VI
Discussion and prospects

We presented TBA equations for exact spectrum of arbitrary multi-magnon operators in the fishnet CFT in any spacetime dimension
D
. These operators form an important class of local operators of the theory and contain all of the information about the mirror dynamics. There are other types of operators worth being studied, including spinning operators (i.e. with derivatives) and the conjugate scalars
\bar{Z},\bar{X}
. While it should be possible to include the former within the excited state TBA formalism, the latter are more elusive, and relate to the logarithmic property of the fishnet CFTs
Ipsen et al. 2019
;
Gromov et al. 2018b
;
Caetano
.

The most efficient form of the TBA equations is expected to be given by Baxter equations. It would be good to derive them for generic
D
and for a general local operator. This program is already quite advanced in
D=4
case
Gromov et al. 2018b
;
Gromov and Sever 2019c
;
Grabner et al.
, but not for other
D
’s. Our TBA equations should help filling this gap by providing important information about the analyticity conditions and so-called quantization conditions specifying the solutions. The Baxter equations formulation would also be instrumental for a thorough study of the correspondence between fishnet graphs and non-compact sigma models or to reveal relationships with string-bit models in
AdS

Gromov and Sever 2019a
;
Gromov and Sever 2019b
;
Gromov and Sever 2019c
. Another interesting direction concerns the generalisation of our TBAs to FCFT
D
supported on triangular and hexagonal fishnets, or dynamical fishnet like the one found in the context of the 3-coupling strongly twisted version of
\mathcal{N}=4
SYM theory
Gürdogan and Kazakov 2016
;
Kazakov et al. 2019
.

A natural next step in the study of FCFT
D
would be the computations of structure constants and multi-point correlation functions. In the mirror picture, it entails establishing the eigenfunctions of the graph-building operators in terms of Sklyanin separated variables, known so far only in
2D

Derkachov et al. 2001
;
Derkachov et al. 2019
, see also
Gromov et al. 2019
for new developments. The formalism is closely related to the hexagon approach
Basso and Dixon 2017
;
Fleury and Komatsu 2018
;
Eden and Sfondrini 2017
;
Basso et al. 2018
and would permit to put it on a firmer ground for generic
D
. We believe that our two-body eigenfunction (
6
) is an important building block for Sklyanin’s SoV construction for non-compact quantum spin chains with arbitrary number of spins in principal series representations of
SO(1,D+1)
symmetry
Ferrando
, since these spin chains have the same integrability structure as FCFT
D
.

6
) and its
M
-body generalisation in a somewhat different form in the case of
D=4
.
