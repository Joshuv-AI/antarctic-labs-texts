Hamiltonicity of 3-tough ( K 2 ∪ 3 ​ K 1 ) (K_{2}\cup 3K_{1}) -free graphs
Elizabeth Grimm, Andrew Hatfield

Abstract
Chvátal conjectured in 1973 the existence of some constant t t such that all t t -tough graphs with at least three vertices are hamiltonian. While the conjecture has been proven for some special classes of graphs, it remains open in general. We say that a graph is ( K 2 ∪ 3 ​ K 1 ) (K_{2}\cup 3K_{1}) -free if it contains no induced subgraph isomorphic to K 2 ∪ 3 ​ K 1 K_{2}\cup 3K_{1} , where K 2 ∪ 3 ​ K 1 K_{2}\cup 3K_{1} is the disjoint union of an edge and three isolated vertices. In this paper, we show that every 3-tough ( K 2 ∪ 3 ​ K 1 ) (K_{2}\cup 3K_{1}) -free graph with at least three vertices is hamiltonian.
