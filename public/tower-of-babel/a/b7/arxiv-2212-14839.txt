On the Packing Density Bound in 3-Space
Arkadiy Aliev

Abstract
The note shows an easy way to improve E.H. Smith’s packing density bound in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} from 0.53835 ​ … 0.53835... to 0.54755 ​ … 0.54755... .



Notation 1
.

Let
K,L\subset\mathbb{R}^{2}
be two planar convex bodies. We denote their mixed volume by
(K,L)
.

Notation 2
.

We denote the density of the densest lattice packing by translates of
K
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
by
\delta_{L}(K)
.

Lemma 1
.

Let
H\subset\mathbb{R}^{2}
be a convex centrally symmetric hexagon or a parallelogram. Then for a centrally symmetric convex body
C\subset\mathbb{R}^{2}
we have:

(C,H)\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{\delta_{L}(C)}}\sqrt{|C|}\sqrt{|H|}.

Proof.

Let
H=A_{1}..A_{6}
and let
H^{\prime}=A^{\prime}_{1}..A^{\prime}_{6}
be the centrally symmetric hexagon circumscribing
C
, which satisfies the condition:
A^{\prime}_{i}A^{\prime}_{i+1}\parallel A_{i}A_{i+1}
for
i=1,2,3
. Then it is easy to observe that
(C,H)=(H^{\prime},H)
. Further, since
\delta_{L}(H^{\prime})=1
and
C\subset H^{\prime}
we have
\frac{|C|}{|H^{\prime}|}\leq\delta_{L}(C)
. Therefore

(C,H)=(H^{\prime},H)\geq\sqrt{|H^{\prime}|}\sqrt{|H|}\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{\delta_{L}(C)}}\sqrt{|C|}\sqrt{|H|}.

∎

Theorem 1
.

For a centrally symmetric convex body
K\subset\mathbb{R}^{3}
we have
\delta_{L}(K)\geq 0.54755...
.

Proof.

We use the notation and construction from
[
1
]
.
Let
d
be a chord in
K
of maximum length.
C:=K\cap d^{\perp}.
Let
\Lambda\subset\mathbb{R}^{2}
be a lattice such that
C+\Lambda
is the densest lattice packing of translates of
C
,
det\Lambda=|P|
,
\frac{|C|}{|P|}=\delta_{L}(C)
.
H_{K}
is a centrally symmetric hexagon with
|H_{K}|\geq\frac{|P|}{4}
. We may assume that
|H_{K}|=\frac{|P|}{4}
.
h
is the distance between two layers in
the lattice packing of translates of
K
. Then as shown in
[
1
]
we have:

|K|\geq\frac{|H_{K}|(d-h)}{3}+\frac{h}{3}(|C|+(C,H_{K})+|H_{K}|).

Therefore since
\delta_{L}(K)\geq\frac{|K|}{h|P|}
and
\delta_{L}(C)\geq 0.89265...
we have:

\delta_{L}(K)\geq\frac{1}{12}\frac{d}{h}+\frac{1}{3}\left(\delta_{L}(C)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\delta_{L}(C)}\frac{(C,H_{K})}{\sqrt{|C|}\sqrt{|H_{K}|}}\right)\geq\frac{1}{12}+\frac{1}{3}\left(0.89265...+\frac{1}{2}\right)=0.54755...

∎
