Exploration of the hidden charm decays of Z c ​ s ​ ( 3985 ) Z_{cs}(3985)
Qi Wu 1,2,3, Dian-Yong Chen 1,4

Abstract
In the present work, we investigate the decays of Z c ​ s − ​ ( 3985 ) Z^{-}_{cs}(3985) in the D s − ​ D ∗ 0 + D s ∗ ⁣ − ​ D 0 D_{s}^{-}D^{\ast 0}+D_{s}^{\ast-}D^{0} molecular frame. By using an effective Lagrangian approach, the branching ratios of Z c ​ s − → J / ψ ​ K − Z^{-}_{cs}\rightarrow J/\psi K^{-} and Z c ​ s − → η c ​ K ∗ ⁣ − Z^{-}_{cs}\rightarrow\eta_{c}K^{\ast-} are estimated. It is found that the dominating decay mode of Z c ​ s − ​ ( 3985 ) Z^{-}_{cs}(3985) is open charm channel, while, the branching ratio of the hidden charm decay channels is about 20 % 20\% . In particular, the branching ratios of Z c ​ s − → J / ψ ​ K − Z^{-}_{cs}\rightarrow J/\psi K^{-} and Z c ​ s − → η c ​ K ∗ ⁣ − Z^{-}_{cs}\rightarrow\eta_{c}K^{\ast-} are predicted to be an order of 10 % 10\% . Moreover, our estimation indicates that the fraction of the quasi-two-body cascade decay via Z c ​ s ​ ( 3985 ) Z_{cs}(3985) and the total cross section of e + ​ e − → K + ​ K − ​ J / ψ e^{+}e^{-}\to K^{+}K^{-}J/\psi around 4.68 GeV is ( 6.8 − 6.2 + 10.5 ) % \left(6.8^{+10.5}_{-6.2}\right)\% . All the predicted branching ratios in the present work could be tested by further measurements in BESIII and Belle II.

IV
Summary

Benefit from the improvement of experimental techniques and the accumulation of experimental data, increasing charmoniumlike states have been observed experimentally. Among these charmoniumlike states, the ones with charge attracted particular attention because of their exotic nature. Recently, BESIII collaboration observed a charged charmoniumlike state
Z^{-}_{cs}(3985)
in the
K^{+}
recoil-mass spectrum of process
e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow K^{+}(D^{-}_{s}D^{\ast 0}+D^{\ast-}_{s}D^{0})
, which could be assigned as a strange partner of
Z_{c}(3900)
.

Inspired by the observations from the BESIII collaboration, we study the hidden charm decays of
Z^{-}_{cs}(3985)
via triangle loop mechanism by using an effective Lagrangian approach. The branching ratios of
Z^{-}_{cs}\rightarrow J/\psi K^{-}
and
Z^{-}_{cs}\rightarrow\eta_{c}K^{\ast-}
are predicted to be of order
10\%
, and the dominant decay mode of
Z^{-}_{cs}(3985)
is an open charm channel. Our estimations indicate the decay property of
Z_{cs}(3985)
is in line with the one of
Z_{c}(3900)
, which supports
Z^{-}_{cs}(3985)
as a molecular state.

Moreover, based on the available experimental data and the present estimation, we find the fraction of the quasi-two-body cascade decay via
Z_{cs}(3985)
and the total cross section of
e^{+}e^{-}\to K^{+}K^{-}J/\psi
around 4.68 GeV is
\left(6.8^{+10.5}_{-6.2}\right)\%
, which could be tested by further measurements in BESIII and Belle II.
