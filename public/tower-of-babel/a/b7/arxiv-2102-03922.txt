There is also a problem of extreme complexity — to prove Langlands theorems for Shimura varieties (relations between

Analogs of abelian varieties for the function field case are Anderson t-motives (or Anderson modules — their categories are anti-equivalent). It is natural to consider an object (function field analog of a Shimura variety) whose points parametrize these t-motives (for example, Anderson t-motives of a fixed dimension
