Primitive Pythagorean triples and neutrino mixing
Yuta Hyodo, Teruyuki Kitabayashi

Abstract
The primitive Pythagorean triples are the three natural numbers ( a , b , c ) (a,b,c) that satisfy c 2 = a 2 + b 2 c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2} in a right triangle. We constructed a neutrino mixing models related to primitive Pythagorean triples that satisfy the observed values within the 3 ​ σ 3\sigma region for the reactor, solar, and atmospheric neutrino mixing angles, as well as the Dirac CP phase.

III.2
Relationship between the solar mixing angle, atmospheric mixing angle, and primitive Pythagorean triples

The mixing matrix predicting a reactor mixing angle (
\theta_{13}
) of
0
can be written as

\displaystyle U=R_{23}R_{12}.

(16)

R_{23}

(R_{12})
is a rotation matrix for the 2-3 (1-2) plane
[
13
]
; it is denoted by

\displaystyle R_{23}=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}1&0&0\\
0&\cos{\theta_{23}}&\sin{\theta_{23}}\\
0&-\sin{\theta_{23}}&\cos{\theta_{23}}\end{array}\right),

\displaystyle R_{12}=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}\cos{\theta_{12}}&\sin{\theta_{12}}&0\\
-\sin{\theta_{12}}&\cos{\theta_{12}}&0\\
0&0&1\end{array}\right).

The relationship between primitive Pythagorean triples and neutrino mixing angles,
\theta_{12}
and
\theta_{23}
, is as follows:

\displaystyle s_{12}=\frac{a}{c},~~s_{23}=\frac{b}{c}.

(25)

With the relationship provided in Eq. (
25

0
can be written as

\displaystyle U_{\rm{PM}}=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}\frac{b}{c}&\frac{a}{c}&0\\
-\frac{a^{2}}{c^{2}}&\frac{ab}{c^{2}}&\frac{b}{c}\\
-\frac{ab}{c^{2}}&-\frac{b^{2}}{c^{2}}&\frac{a}{c}\end{array}\right).

We call this mixing matrix primitive Pythagorean triple neutrino mixing (PM).

The mixing matrix predicts the vanishing reactor mixing angle. Next, we performed modifications to improve the prediction of the reactor mixing angle.
