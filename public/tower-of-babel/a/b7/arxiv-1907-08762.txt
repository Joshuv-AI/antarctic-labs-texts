Introduction

A topological space

Definition 1.1

A space

A. V. Arhangel’skii introduced the notion of

C

[
4

-normal space is

-normal. We give an example of a

-normal space which is not normal [Example 2.3] and a
CC
-normal space which is not
Ps
-normal [Example 2.6].

Definition 1.2
.

A space

is called

-Tychonoff(

-Tychonoff) space if there exists a Tychonoff space

such that
f\lvert_{K}:K\mapsto f(K)
is homeomorphism for any compact ( Lindelof) subspace

We define

Ps

In last section we make some remarks on

2.
Ps-normal space

Theorem 2.1
.

Every

CC
-normal.

Proof.

Let
X
be
Ps
-normal space and

X

f\lvert_{K}:K\mapsto f(K)
is homeomorphism for each pseudocompact subset

of

f\lvert_{C}:C\mapsto f(C)
is homeomorphism. Hence

is
CC
-normal space. ∎

Also every
CC
-normal space is
C
-normal. It is easy to check that every normal space is

Normal
\Rightarrow

Ps
-normal
\Rightarrow

CC
-normal
\Rightarrow

C
-normal

a)

is

-normal.

b)

-normal and any pseudocompact subspace of

, then

is

Proof.

We prove

(b)

Let

be a normal space and

Let

is

-normal, then

-compact.

Theorem 2.4
.

Ps
-normality is a topological property.

Proof.

Let
X
be a
Ps
-normal and
X
be homeomorphic to

be a normal space and

Let

Theorem 2.5
.

If
X
is pseudocompact but not normal, then

is not
Ps
-normal.

Proof.

Let
X
be pseudocompact but not normal. If possible let there exist a normal space
X
and a bijective mapping
f:X\mapsto Y
such that
f\lvert_{K}:K\mapsto f(K)
is a homeomorphism for any pseudocompact subspace

of

. In particular

is a homeomorphism. This is a contradiction as
Y
is normal and

is not normal. Hence
X
is not
Ps
-normal.
∎

We have seen that every
Ps
-normal space is
CC
-normal. The following example shows that the converse is not true.

Example 2.6
.

Consider
(\mathbb{R},\tau_{c})
, where
\tau_{c}
is the co-countable topology on set of all real numbers

. In Example 2.6,
[

is

(\mathbb{R},\tau_{c})

More generally every irreducible space (i.e a space which has no pair of disjoint nonempty open sets) which is not normal is not

Let

Let

Theorem 2.8

Proof.

Let

Every

Theorem 3.3

If
