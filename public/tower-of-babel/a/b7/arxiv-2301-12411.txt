The phase transition of Rastall AdS black hole with cloud of strings and quintessence
Mehdi Sadeghi, Faramarz Rahmani

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the black hole solution in Rastall theory of gravity in the presence of quintessence and the cloud of strings. Our investigations show that this model meets only second-order phase transition in four dimensions. While both the first and second order phase transitions are seen in five dimensions. Therefore, according to the AdS/CFT duality, the confinement-deconfinement phase transition only occurs in five dimensions for this model.



Keywords:
Phase transition, Rastall theory of gravity, quintessence, cloud of strings.

Black hole solutions in Rastall AdS gravity with the cloud of strings and quintessence

Rastall’s field equation in the presence of quintessence and cloud of strings in
d
dimensions is as follows,

R_{\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{2}(R+\frac{d_{1}d_{2}}{l^{2}})g_{\mu\nu}+{k}\lambda g_{\mu\nu}R=kT_{\mu\nu}.

(1)

Here, quantities

is decomposed to the quintessence and cloud of strings terms as follows,

T_{\mu\nu}=T^{(quin)}_{\mu\nu}+T^{(cs)}_{\mu\nu}.

(2)

On the other hand, the Einstein and Rastall tensors are,

G_{\mu\nu}=R_{\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{2}Rg_{\mu\nu},

(3)

and

H_{\mu\nu}=G_{\mu\nu}+{k}\lambda g_{\mu\nu}R.

(4)

By substituting equations (
3

4

1

H_{\mu\nu}-\frac{d_{1}d_{2}}{2l^{2}}g_{\mu\nu}=kT_{\mu\nu}.

The components of the energy-momentum tensor of the quintessence are given by

(6)

{T_{{x_{i}}}}{{\mkern 1.0mu}{}^{{x_{i}}}}=-\frac{{{\rho_{q}}}}{{{d_{2}}}}({d_{1}}\omega+1),\qquad(i=2,...,d_{2}),

(7)

The factor,
\alpha
is the quintessence parameter related to the density of quintessence field
{\rho_{q}}
. The parameter
\omega

p_{q}=\omega\rho_{q}

-1<\omega<{{-1}\over 3}

The components of energy-momentum tensor for the cloud of strings are as follows

To obtain the black hole solutions, we use the ansatz

ds^{2}=-f(r)dt^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{f(r)}+r^{2}d\Omega^{2}_{d_{2}}.

The form of

f(r)=\kappa-\frac{2m}{r^{d_{3}}}-\frac{2\Lambda}{d_{1}d_{2}}r^{2}-\frac{\alpha}{r^{d_{1}\omega+d_{3}}}-\frac{2a}{d_{2}r^{d_{4}}}.

The event horizons are obtained through the solving of equation

m=\frac{\kappa r_{h}^{d_{3}}}{2}-\frac{\Lambda}{d_{1}d_{2}}r_{h}^{d_{1}}-\frac{\alpha}{2r_{h}^{d_{1}\omega}}-\frac{a}{d_{2}}r_{h}.

(12)

The integration constant

The Hawking temperature of the black hole is obtained through the relation

T=\frac{1}{4\pi}\left(\frac{\partial f(r)}{\partial r}\right)_{r=r_{h}}.

(14)

We shall use this relation in obtaining the entropy of the system through the first law of thermodynamics.

The entropy and surface area of a black hole solution are given by relations

A=\int d^{d_{2}}x\sqrt{-g}|_{r=r_{h},t=cte}=r_{h}^{d_{2}}\Omega_{d_{2}},

5
Conclusion

We introduced the Rastall AdS black hole solution with the cloud of strings and quintessence. We studied the phase transition of this model in four and five dimensions. Our outcomes show that in four dimensions only the second-order phase transition occurs. In other words, we observed the discontinuities in the heat capacity of the system which is related to the second derivative of the Gibbs function. While in five dimensions, the first-order transition is also seen. This means volume and entropy change discontinuously at critical points in five dimensions. According to AdS/CFT correspondence, our results indicate that the confinement-deconfinement phase transition occurs in the field theory side of this model in five dimensions.

As we know, the cosmological constant and the Rastall coupling are related as,

\Lambda=\frac{1}{3}\frac{\rho_{0}}{4k\lambda-2}
.
Thus, the effect of Rastall coupling is included in the cosmological constant which plays the role of the pressure of the system. In fact, there is no essential difference between the phase transitions in the Rastall gravity with the other phase transitions of the AdS black holes. Nevertheless, there are differences in the dual interpretation. The coupling of the dual field of Rastall gravity is stronger than the coupling of the dual field theory of AdS black holes. Because, the ratio of the shear viscosity to entropy density of this model is
