J / ψ J/\psi Pair Hadroproduction at Next-to-Leading Order in Nonrelativistic-QCD at CMS
Li-Ping Sun a

Abstract
We perform a complete study on the J / ψ J/\psi pair hadroproduction at next-to-leading order (NLO) in the nonrelativstic-QCD (NRQCD) framework with the pair of c ​ c ¯ c\bar{c} either in S [ 1 ] 1 3 {}^{3}S_{1}^{[1]} or S [ 8 ] 0 1 {}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]} fock state. It is found that the S [ 8 ] 0 1 {}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]} channel contribution at NLO is essential. Our results indicate that for the CMS, the NRQCD predictions can not describe the experimental data at all, and the total cross section predicted by NRQCD is smaller than the experimental data by an order of magnitude. So new mechanisms are needed to understand the CMS data for J / ψ J/\psi pair production.

relative velocity

Formalism.
—In NRQCD factorization, the cross section of

J/\psi
pair production at the LHC can be expressed as

\displaystyle d\sigma_{p+p\to J/\psi+J/\psi}=\sum_{i,j,n_{1},n_{2}}{\int}dx_{1}dx_{2}{f_{i/p}(x_{1})}{f_{j/p}(x_{2})}

\displaystyle\times~{d\hat{\sigma}^{n_{1},n_{2}}_{i,j}}\langle\mathcal{O}_{n_{1}}\rangle^{J/\psi}\langle\mathcal{O}_{n_{2}}\rangle^{J/\psi}.

(1)

where
f_{i/p}(x_{1,2})
are the parton distribution functions
(PDFs),
x_{1}
and
x_{2}
represent the momentum fraction of initial
state partons from the protons,

\langle\mathcal{O}_{n}\rangle^{J/\psi}
are LDMEs of
J/\psi
with

n={}^{2S+1}L_{J}^{[c]}
in the standard spectroscopic notation for
the quantum numbers of the produced intermediate heavy quark pairs,
and

are partonic short-distance coefficients. In
this letter we set either
n_{1}=n_{2}={}^{3}S_{1}^{[1]}
or

n_{1}=n_{2}={}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]}
in Eq. (
1

In the LO calculation, there are two subprocesses:

g+g{\rightarrow}J/\psi+J/\psi

q+\bar{q}{\rightarrow}J/\psi+J/\psi

To tackle the infrared (IR) divergences in real corrections, the
two-cutoff phase space slicing method
twocut
is employed.
After isolating the soft divergences and collinear divergences, the
cross sections for the
J/\psi
pair production at the NLO can be
expressed as

\displaystyle\sigma_{NLO}=\sigma_{Born}+\sigma_{Virtual}+\sigma_{Real}^{soft}+\sigma_{Real}^{HC}+\sigma_{Real}^{\overline{HC}},

(2)

where
HC
and
\overline{HC}
represent hard collinear and hard
non-collinear contributions, respectively. The soft divergences and
collinear divergences from real corrections will cancel divergences
from virtual corrections, and thus the final NLO contributions are
IR safe.

Because there are two
J/\psi

contributions behave as

is large. However, at
NLO level, there are contributions which give
p_{T}^{-6}
behavior

the NLO contribution will dominate at large

the CMS and ATLAS data, where a relatively large lower
p_{T}
cutoff
is taken

;
ATLAS
. The expectation will be confirmed by our
numerical results shown below.

Summary.
—In the framework of NRQCD factorization, we
evaluate the full NLO
J/\psi
pair production via the

{}^{3}S_{1}^{[1]}
and
{}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]}
channels. We find that NLO
corrections are essential for
J/\psi
pair production, compared to
the LO results. For the CMS, the NLO predictions of total cross
section,
p_{TJ/\psi J/\psi}
distribution, invariant mass
distribution of the
J/\psi
pair, and rapidity difference
distribution of the
J/\psi
pair are much smaller than CMS data by
about an order of magnitude.
This reveals the signal that, in the
J/\psi
pair production
process, the NRQCD NLO result is not the dominant contribution,
there must be some new schemes dominating the process, if the CMS
data are confirmed.
