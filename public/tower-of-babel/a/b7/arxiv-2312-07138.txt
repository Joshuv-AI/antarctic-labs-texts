Hecke algebras for the 1st congruence subgroup and bundles on ℙ 1 \mathbb{P}^{1} I: the case of finite field
Alexander Braverman and David Kazhdan

Abstract
. Let G G be a split reductive group over a finite field 𝐤 \mathbf{k} In this note we study the space V V of finitely supported functions on the set Bun G ⁡ ( ℙ 1 ) 0 , ∞ ​ ( 𝐤 ) \operatorname{Bun}_{G}(\mathbb{P}^{1})_{0,\infty}(\mathbf{k}) of isomorphism classes G G -bundles on the projective line ℙ 1 \mathbb{P}^{1} endowed with a trivialization at 0 0 and ∞ \infty . We show that V V is naturally isomorphic to the regular bimodule over the Hecke algebra A A of the group G ⁡ ( 𝐤 ⁡ ( ( t ) ) ) G(\mathbf{k}((t))) with respect to the first congruence subgroup. As a byproduct we show that Hecke operators at points different from 0 0 and ∞ \infty to generate the ”stable center” of A A . We provide an expression of the character of the lifting of an irreducible cuspidal representation of GL ⁡ ( N , 𝐤 ) \operatorname{GL}(N,\mathbf{k}) to GL ⁡ ( N , 𝐤 ′ ) \operatorname{GL}(N,\mathbf{k}^{\prime}) where 𝐤 ′ \mathbf{k}^{\prime} is a finite extension of 𝐤 \mathbf{k} in terms of these generators. In a subsequent publication we plan to develop analogous constructions in the case when 𝐤 \mathbf{k} is replaced by a local non-archimedian field.
