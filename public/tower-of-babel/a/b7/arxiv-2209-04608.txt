Eigenvalue Fluctuations of 1-dimensional random Schrödinger operators
Takuto Mashiko, Yuma Marui, Naoki Maruyama, Fumihiko Nakano

Abstract
As an extension to the paper by Breuer, Grinshpon, and White [ 2 ] , we study the linear statistics for the eigenvalues of the Schrödinger operator with random decaying potential with order 𝒪 ⁡ ( x − α ) {\cal O}(x^{-\alpha}) ( α > 0 \alpha>0 ) at infinity. We first prove similar statements as in [ 2 ] for the trace of f ⁡ ( H ) f(H) , where f f belongs to a class of analytic functions : there exists a critical exponent α c \alpha_{c} such that the fluctuation of the trace of f ⁡ ( H ) f(H) converges in probability for α > α c \alpha>\alpha_{c} , and satisfies a CLT statement for α ≤ α c \alpha\leq\alpha_{c} , where α c \alpha_{c} differs depending on f f . Furthermore we study the asymptotic behavior of its expectation value.

Lemma 2.5

Lemma 2.9

Remark

Lemma 2.9

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}c_{j}\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\iota(\beta)=0\\
|\beta|\geq\ell_{0}\end{subarray}}p^{j}(\beta)\left(V^{\beta^{i}}-{\bf E}[V^{\beta^{i}}]\right)

\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}

Proof.

We first
note that, by Lemma 4.2 in
[
2
]
,
The random series

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\iota(\beta)\in[1,N]\\

converges a.s. and define a random variable.

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\iota(\beta)\in[1,\infty)\\

with finite variance of which we estimate below.

\displaystyle Var\left(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\iota(\beta)=0\\

\displaystyle\liminf_{N\to\infty}{\bf E}\left[\left(\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\iota(\beta)=0\\
