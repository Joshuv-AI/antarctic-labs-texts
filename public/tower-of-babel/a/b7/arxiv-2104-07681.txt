Light, Long-Lived B − L B-L Gauge and Higgs Bosons at the DUNE Near Detector Preprint: FERMILAB-PUB-21-200-T, MI-TH-218
P. S. Bhupal Dev, Bhaskar Dutta, Kevin J. Kelly, Rabindra N. Mohapatra, Yongchao Zhang

Abstract
The low-energy U ​ ( 1 ) B − L U(1)_{B-L} gauge symmetry is well-motivated as part of beyond Standard Model physics related to neutrino mass generation. We show that a light B − L B-L gauge boson Z ′ Z{{}^{\prime}} and the associated U ​ ( 1 ) B − L U(1)_{B-L} -breaking scalar φ \varphi can both be effectively searched for at high-intensity facilities such as the near detector complex of the Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment (DUNE). Without the scalar φ \varphi , the Z ′ Z{{}^{\prime}} can be probed at DUNE up to mass of 1 GeV, with the corresponding gauge coupling g B ​ L g_{BL} as low as 10 − 9 10^{-9} . In the presence of the scalar φ \varphi with gauge coupling to Z ′ Z{{}^{\prime}} , the DUNE capability of discovering the gauge boson Z ′ Z{{}^{\prime}} can be significantly improved, even by one order of magnitude in g B ​ L g_{BL} , due to additional production from the decay φ → Z ′ Z ′ \varphi\to Z{{}^{\prime}}Z{{}^{\prime}} . The DUNE sensitivity is largely complementary to other long-lived Z ′ Z{{}^{\prime}} searches at beam-dump facilities such as FASER and SHiP, as well as astrophysical and cosmological probes. On the other hand, the prospects of detecting φ \varphi itself at DUNE are to some extent weakened in presence of Z ′ Z{{}^{\prime}} , compared to the case without the gauge interaction.

The Model

We consider the minimal
U(1)_{B-L}
model based on the gauge group
SU(2)_{L}\times U(1)_{Y}\times U(1)_{B-L}
. For the purpose of anomaly cancellation, three right-handed neutrinos (RHNs)
N_{i}
(with
i=1,\,2,\,3
) are naturally introduced. To break the
U(1)_{B-L}
gauge symmetry, a complex singlet scalar field
\phi
is introduced, which carries two units of
B-L
charge. When the
\phi
-field develops a non-vanishing vacuum expectation value (VEV)
\langle\phi\rangle=v_{BL}/2\sqrt{2}
, the
U(1)_{B-L}
gauge symmetry is spontaneously broken, which generates the
Z^{\prime}
boson mass of
m_{Z^{\prime}}=g_{BL}v_{BL}
, where we have defined the covariant derivative as
D_{\mu}\phi=\left[\partial_{\mu}-2ig_{BL}Z^{\prime}_{\mu}\right]\phi
. After the symmetry breaking, expanding the field around its VEV, we obtain

\displaystyle\phi=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\frac{1}{2}v_{BL}+\varphi+i\chi\right)\,.

(1)

The CP-odd component
\chi
is “eaten” by the
Z^{\prime}
boson, while the CP-even component
\varphi
is left as a physical scalar field. In presence of the
(H^{\dagger}H)(\phi^{\dagger}\phi)
term in the scalar potential (
H
is the SM Higgs doublet), the scalar
\varphi
mixes with the SM Higgs
h
, with a mixing angle
\sin\vartheta
.

Given the Yukawa coupling

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{Y}=-y_{N}\phi\overline{N^{C}}N\,,

(2)

with
C
standing here for charge conjugate, the RHNs obtain masses
m_{N}=y_{N}v_{BL}/\sqrt{2}
, which can be used to generate the tiny active neutrino masses via type-I seesaw mechanism
Minkowski:1977sc
;
Mohapatra:1979ia
;
Yanagida:1979as
;
GellMann:1980vs
;
Glashow:1979nm
. For simplicity, we will assume the RHN masses
m_{N}>m_{Z^{\prime}}/2
and
m_{N}>m_{\varphi}/2
such that the decays
Z^{\prime}\to NN
and
\varphi\to NN
are kinematically forbidden, and we can neglect the contributions of RHNs to the DUNE prospects of
Z^{\prime}
and
\varphi
in this paper. We also assume for simplicity that there is no
Z-Z^{\prime}
mixing throughout this paper, which would otherwise potentially affect the decay branching ratios (BRs) of the
Z^{\prime}
boson. A small, loop-induced kinetic mixing between
Z^{\prime}
and the SM
Z
will arise, on the order of
g_{BL}g/(16\pi^{2})
. Since we are interested in
g_{BL}\ll 1
, this mixing is always very small, and we will disregard it for the remainder of this work. We are also going to focus on the mass regime MeV
\lesssim m_{Z^{\prime}}\lesssim
GeV – whether such small gauge couplings and light gauge boson masses are natural or fine-tuned requires a detailed analysis of the scalar potential, which is beyond the scope of this manuscript, and we just take a more phenomenological approach.

Then there are only four free phenomenological parameters in the minimal
U(1)_{B-L}
model, i.e.

\displaystyle m_{Z^{\prime}}\,,\quad m_{\varphi}\,,\quad g_{BL}\,,\quad\sin\vartheta\,,

(3)

which we study to investigate the DUNE prospects. To produce the
Z^{\prime}
boson and/or the
\varphi
scalar at the DUNE experiment, their masses
m_{Z^{\prime}}
and
m_{\varphi}
have to be at or below the GeV-scale. As we will show in Figs.
4
and
5
respectively, the gauge coupling
g_{BL}
and the mixing

Neglecting the RHN channel, the gauge boson

\displaystyle=\frac{c_{\mathfrak{m}}N_{\rm POT}}{A_{\rm Det.}}\epsilon(m_{Z^{\prime}})\mathrm{BR}\left(\mathfrak{m}\to\gamma Z^{\prime}\right),

\displaystyle=\frac{c_{\mathfrak{m}}N_{\rm POT}}{A_{\rm Det.}}\epsilon(m_{Z^{\prime}})\left[2\left(\frac{g_{BL}^{2}}{e^{2}}\right)\mathrm{BR}\left(\mathfrak{m}\to\gamma\gamma\right)\left(1-\frac{m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}{m_{\mathfrak{m}}^{2}}\right)^{3}\right].

3.3
Improving
Z^{\prime}
prospects at DUNE

As discussed in Eq. (
8
), if both
\varphi
and
Z^{\prime}
exist and
m_{\varphi}>2m_{Z^{\prime}}
, then the decay
\varphi\to Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}
may occur. In Section
3.2.2
we discussed how this decay channel modifies limits on the scalar boson in the
(m_{\varphi},\penalty\ \sin^{2}\vartheta)
plane. In this subsection, we will discuss how the additional flux of
Z^{\prime}
from this channel can improve search capabilities in the
(m_{Z^{\prime}},\penalty\ g_{BL})
plane.

Before analyzing this flux contribution, we briefly note that additional associated production of
both

\varphi
and
Z^{\prime}
may occur from some meson decays. In the presence of the gauge coupling of
\varphi
to
Z^{\prime}
bosons, we can in principle have the associated production of
Z^{\prime}
and
\varphi
from meson decays, for instance

\displaystyle\pi^{0}\to\gamma+Z^{\prime}+\varphi\,,\quad K\to\pi+Z^{\prime}+\varphi\,,\quad\rho\to Z^{\prime}+\varphi\,.

(18)

If the scalar
\varphi
is emitted from the
Z^{\prime}
boson line, the partial widths will be proportional to
g_{BL}^{4}

Batell:2009di
. If the scalar
\varphi
is emitted from the quark fermion lines, the widths will be proportional to
\sin^{2}\vartheta g_{BL}^{2}
. Therefore for small
\sin\vartheta
and
g_{BL}
, these associated production channels are highly suppressed compared to the channels above. Therefore, we do not consider these channels in this paper.

Since the main source of
\varphi

Z^{\prime}
– neutral, short-lived meson decays and proton-proton bremsstrahlung), the

coming from
\varphi

m_{\varphi}
,
\sin^{2}\vartheta
,
g_{BL}
, and

4
Conclusion

We have explored the possibility of experimentally searching for a class of BSM scenarios where the SM is extended by a minimal
U(1)_{B-L}
gauge symmetry to explain neutrino masses and possibly provide a portal to DM. We focused on the light
Z^{\prime}
boson and light associated
U(1)_{B-L}
-breaking scalar
\varphi
in the low gauge coupling regime. We discussed how the presence of
Z^{\prime}
and
\varphi
affects the search range at high-intensity facilities such as the near detector complex at DUNE. Focusing primarily on the sub-GeV mass region for
Z^{\prime}
and
\varphi
, the main results of this paper can be summarized as follows:

•

If the scalar
\varphi
decouples, the DUNE prospects of pure
Z^{\prime}
boson is presented in Fig.
4
. Compared to the FASER sensitivities, the DUNE near detector complex can probe a smaller coupling
g_{BL}
as a result of the lower center-of-mass proton-beam/target energy at DUNE (compared to the LHC).

•

As far as the
B-L
breaking scalar
\varphi
is concerned, the presence of
Z^{\prime}
somewhat weakens the search range of its parameters at DUNE, compared to the case without the gauge interaction, as shown in Fig.
5
.

•

With the extra source of
Z^{\prime}
from scalar decay
\varphi\to Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}
, the number of
Z^{\prime}
signal events can be greatly increased for
m_{Z^{\prime}}\lesssim 200
MeV (cf. Fig.
6
), and the DUNE prospects of
g_{BL}
can be improved by up to a factor of 45, as demonstrated in Fig.
7
.

Our results are largely complementary to the other works on
B-L

Z^{\prime}
and
\varphi
searches at DUNE and other facilities such as FASER and SHiP detectors, as well as to the limits from BBN and supernovae.
