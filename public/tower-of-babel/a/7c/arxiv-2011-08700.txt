1.
Introduction and preliminaries

Let
\mathcal{A}
consists of functions
f
that analytic in the open unit disc
{\mathbb{D}}=\{z:|z|<1\}
, with expansion

f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+a_{3}z^{3}+\cdots,

i.e., normalized such that
f(0)=f^{\prime}(0)-1=0
. The famous Bieberbach conjecture from 1914 states that
|a_{n}|\leq n
,
n=2,3,\ldots
, for the univalent functions from
{\mathcal{A}}
. The proof of the conjecture due to de Branges in 1985
[
1
]
is one of the most celebrated results of the twentieth century.
Although, the conjecture is closed it remains an intriguing question to find upper bounds (preferably sharp) of the modulus of the coefficient for functons in various sublasses of univalent functions. One such class, that attracts significant attention in past decades is the class
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
,
0<\lambda\leq 1
,

{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)=\left\{f\in{\mathcal{A}}:\left|\left(\frac{z}{f(z)}\right)^{2}f^{\prime}(z)-1\right|<\lambda,\,z\in{\mathbb{D}}\right\}.

Functions from this class are proven to be univalent but not starlike which makes them interesting since the class of starlike functions is very wide. Overview of the most valuable results is given in Chapter 12 from
[
9
]
.

In
[
5
]
, the authors conjectured
|a_{n}|\leq 1+\lambda+\lambda^{2}+\cdots+\lambda^{n-1}
for the class
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
and
n\geq 2
. In the same paper they proved that the conjecture is valid for
n=3
and
n=4
, while for
n=2
the proof is given in
[
6
]
. For the fifth coefficient the conjecture was proven in
[
7
]
for the range
2/3\leq\lambda\leq 1
. The proofs for the third, fourth and the fifth coefficient rely on the claim from
[
6
]
that for every function
f
from
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
,

(1)

\frac{f(z)}{z}\prec\frac{1}{(1+z)(1+\lambda z)}.

Here ”
\prec
” denotes the usual subordination, i.e.,
F(z)\prec G(z)
for
F
and
G
analytic in
{\mathbb{D}}
, means that there exists function
\omega(z)
, also analytic in
{\mathbb{D}}
, such that
\omega(0)=0
and
|\omega(z)|<1
for all
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
.

Recently, in
[
2
]
, by a counterexample, the authors showed that
f\in{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
does not imply subordination (
1
). So, the cited estimates of
|a_{n}|
, for
n=3,4
and 5, are correct only on the subclass of
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
consisting of functions satisfying the subordination (
1
).

The estimate
|a_{2}|\leq 1+\lambda
is correct and sharp on whole class
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
(see
[
6
]
).

In this paper we study functions
f
from
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
satisfying subordination (
1
) and we extend the conjectured estimate for
n=5
to the range
\lambda_{0}\leq\lambda\leq 1
, where
\lambda_{0}=0.400436\ldots
is the unique positive solution of the equation

9\lambda^{4}-3\lambda^{3}+\lambda^{2}+2\lambda-1=0.

For the proof we will use the following result for the class
{\mathcal{P}}
of Caratheodory functions, that are functions
p
analytic in
{\mathbb{D}}
, of form
p(z)=1+p_{1}z+p_{2}z^{2}+\cdots
with positive real part, i.e.,
\operatorname{Re}p(z)>0
for
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
. The result is due to Leverenz (
[
3
, Theorem 4(b)]
).

Lemma 1
.

Function
p(z)=1+p_{1}z+p_{2}z^{2}+\cdots
has positive real part on the unit disk, if, and only if,

(2)

\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\left\{\left|2z_{j}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}p_{k}z_{k+j}\right|^{2}-\left|\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}p_{k+1}z_{k+j}\right|^{2}\right\}\geq 0

for every sequence
\{z_{k}\}
of complex numbers that satisfy
\lim_{k\to\infty}|z_{k}|^{1/k}<1
.

We will also need the following result by Prokhorov and Szynal
[
8
, Lemma 2, p.128]
.

Lemma 2
.

Let
\omega(z)=c_{1}z+c_{2}z^{2}+c_{3}z^{3}+\cdots
be analytic in
{\mathbb{D}}
with
|\omega(z)|\leq 1
for all
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
. If
\mu
and
\nu
are real numbers such that
2\leq|\mu|\leq 4
and
\nu\geq\frac{1}{12}(\mu^{2}+8)
, then
|c_{3}+\mu c_{1}c_{2}+\nu c_{1}^{3}|\leq\nu
.

2.
Main result

If
p(z)=1+p_{1}z+p_{2}z^{2}+\cdots
is a function from
{\mathcal{P}}
, then there exists function
\omega(z)=c_{1}z+c_{2}z^{2}+\cdots
, analytic in
{\mathbb{D}}
, such that
\omega(0)=0
,
|\omega(z)|<1
for all
z\in D
and

p(z)=\frac{1+\omega(z)}{1-\omega(z)}\left(=1+2\omega(z)+2\omega^{2}(z)+\cdots\right).

After comparing the coefficients we have

(3)

\begin{split}p_{1}&=2c_{1},\\
p_{2}&=2(c_{2}+c_{1}^{2}),\\
p_{3}&=2(c_{3}+2c_{1}c_{2}+c_{1}^{3}),\\
p_{4}&=2(c_{4}+2c_{1}c_{3}+c_{2}^{2}+3c_{1}^{2}c_{2}+c_{1}^{4}).\end{split}

This will help us to prove the main result.

Theorem 1
.

Let
f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+\cdots
belongs to the class
{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
, where
\lambda_{0}\leq\lambda\leq 1
, where
\lambda_{0}=0.400436\ldots
is the unique positive solution of the equation

9\lambda^{4}-3\lambda^{3}+\lambda^{2}+2\lambda-1=0.

If, additionally,
f
satisfies subordination (
1
), then

|a_{5}|\leq 1+\lambda+\lambda^{2}+\lambda^{3}+\lambda^{4},

and the result is sharp.

Proof.

If
f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+\cdots\in{\mathcal{U}}(\lambda)
,
0<\lambda\leq 1
, satisfies subordination (
1
), then

(4)

\frac{f(z)}{z}=\frac{1}{(1-\omega(z))(1-\lambda\omega(z))}=1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1-\lambda^{n+1}}{1-\lambda}\omega^{n}(z),

where
\omega(z)=c_{1}z+c_{2}z^{2}+\cdots
is analytic in
{\mathbb{D}}
,
|\omega(z)|<1
for all
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
, and
\left.\frac{1-\lambda^{n+1}}{1-\lambda}\right|_{\lambda=1}=n+1
for
n=1,2,\ldots
.
From (
4
) we have

(5)

\begin{split}a_{5}&=(1+\lambda)c_{4}+2(1+\lambda+\lambda^{2})c_{1}c_{3}+(1+\lambda+\lambda^{2})c_{2}^{2}\\
&+3(1+\lambda+\lambda^{2}+\lambda^{3})c_{1}^{2}c_{2}+(1+\lambda+\lambda^{2}+\lambda^{3}+\lambda^{4})c_{1}^{4}.\end{split}

On the other side, if we choose
z_{k}=0
for
k>3
in (
2
), we have

\begin{split}&|2z_{0}+p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{0}+p_{2}z_{1}+p_{3}z_{2}+p_{4}z_{3}|^{2}\\
+&|2z_{1}+p_{1}z_{2}+p_{2}z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3}|^{2}\\
+&|2z_{2}+p_{1}z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{2}+p_{2}z_{3}|^{2}+|2z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{3}|^{2}\geq 0.\end{split}

From here, we have that

(6)

\begin{split}&L=:|p_{1}z_{0}+p_{2}z_{1}+p_{3}z_{2}+p_{4}z_{3}|^{2}\\
\leq&R=:(|2z_{0}+p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3}|^{2})\\
&\quad+(|2z_{1}+p_{1}z_{2}+p_{2}z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{2}+p_{2}z_{3}|^{2})\\
&\quad+(|2z_{2}+p_{1}z_{3}|^{2}-|p_{1}z_{3}|^{2})+|2z_{3}|^{2}.\end{split}

If we choose
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
p_{3}
and
p_{4}
from (
3
) and

\begin{split}z_{0}=&\lambda^{2}(1-\lambda)^{2}c_{1}^{3},\\
z_{1}=&\lambda^{2}c_{2}+(3\lambda^{3}-2\lambda^{2})c_{1}^{2},\\
z_{2}=&2\lambda^{2}c_{1},\\
z_{3}=&1+\lambda,\end{split}

then, after some calculations and comparing with (
5
), for
L
defined in (
6
) we have that

(7)

L=4|a_{5}|^{2}.

Also, if we use that
|a+b|^{2}-|b|^{2}=|a|^{2}+2\operatorname{Re}\{\overline{a}b\}
(
a
and
b
are complex numbers), then by (
6
):

(8)

\begin{split}R&=4|z_{0}|^{2}+4\operatorname{Re}\{(p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3})\overline{z_{0}}\}+4|z_{1}|^{2}+4\operatorname{Re}\{(p_{1}z_{2}+p_{2}z_{3})\overline{z_{1}}\}\\
&+4|z_{2}|^{2}+4\operatorname{Re}\{(p_{1}z_{3})\overline{z_{2}}\}+4|z_{3}|^{2}.\end{split}

Considering each term of (
8
) by choosing the same values for
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
p_{3}
,
p_{4}
,
z_{0}
,
z_{1}
,
z_{2}
,
z_{3}
as before, we receive:

\begin{split}|z_{0}|^{2}&=\lambda^{4}(1-\lambda)^{4}|c_{1}|^{6};\\
\operatorname{Re}\{&(p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3})\overline{z_{0}}\}\leq|p_{1}z_{1}+p_{2}z_{2}+p_{3}z_{3}|\cdot|\overline{z_{0}}|\\
&=2|(1+\lambda)c_{3}+(3\lambda^{2}+2\lambda+2)c_{1}c_{2}+(3\lambda^{3}+\lambda+1)c_{1}^{3}|\lambda^{2}(1-\lambda)^{2}|c_{1}|^{3}\\
&=2\lambda^{2}(1-\lambda)^{2}|c_{1}|^{3}(1+\lambda)\left|c_{3}+\left(2+\frac{3\lambda^{2}}{1+\lambda}\right)c_{1}c_{2}+\left(1+\frac{3\lambda^{3}}{1+\lambda}\right)c_{1}^{3}\right|\\

since in this case
2<|\mu|<4
and
\nu\geq\frac{1}{12}(8+\mu^{2})
is equivalent with
\frac{\sqrt{52}-4}{9}=0.356789\ldots\leq\lambda\leq 1
. Further,

\begin{split}|z_{1}|^{2}&=|\lambda^{2}c_{2}+(3\lambda^{3}-2\lambda^{2})c_{1}^{2}|^{2}=\lambda^{4}|c_{2}|^{2}+(3\lambda^{3}-2\lambda^{2})^{2}|c_{1}|^{4}\\
&\quad+2\operatorname{Re}\{\lambda^{2}(3\lambda^{3}-2\lambda^{2})c_{2}\overline{c_{1}}^{2}\};\\
\operatorname{Re}\{&(p_{1}z_{2}+p_{2}z_{3})\overline{z_{1}}\}\\

\begin{split}R&\leq 4\left[\left(\lambda^{2}(1-\lambda)^{2}|c_{1}|^{3}+3\lambda^{3}+\lambda+1\right)^{2}-(3\lambda^{3}+\lambda+1)^{2}\right.\\

Since

\begin{split}R&\leq 4\left[(\lambda^{2}(1-\lambda)^{2}|c_{1}|^{3}+3\lambda^{3}+\lambda+1)^{2}+\lambda^{2}(\lambda^{2}+2\lambda+2)(1-|c_{1}|^{2})^{2}\right.\\

.

\begin{split}R&\leq 4\lambda^{2}(1-\lambda)^{2}|c_{1}|^{3}+3\lambda^{3}+\lambda+1)^{2}\\
