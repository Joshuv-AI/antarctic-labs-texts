Abstract
. It is shown that, apart from the smallest Ree group, a flag-transitive automorphism group G G of a 2 2 - ( k 2 , k , λ ) (k^{2},k,\lambda) design 𝒟 \mathcal{D} , with λ | k \lambda\mid k , is either an affine group or an almost simple classical group. Moreover, when G G is the smallest Ree group, 𝒟 \mathcal{D} is isomorphic either to the 2 2 - ( 6 2 , 6 , 2 ) (6^{2},6,2) design or to one of the three 2 2 - ( 6 2 , 6 , 6 ) (6^{2},6,6) designs constructed in this paper. All the four 2 2 -designs have the 36 36 secants of a nondegenerate conic 𝒞 \mathcal{C} of P ​ G 2 ​ ( 8 ) PG_{2}(8) as a point set and 6 6 -sets of secants in a remarkable configuration as a block set.
