A nonconforming P3 and discontinuous P2 mixed finite element on tetrahedral grids
Xuejun Xu, Shangyou Zhang

Abstract
. A nonconforming P 3 P_{3} finite element is constructed by enriching the conforming P 3 P_{3} finite element space with three P 3 P_{3} nonconforming bubbles and six additional P 4 P_{4} nonconforming bubbles, on each tetrahedron. Here the divergence of the P 4 P_{4} bubble is not a P 3 P_{3} polynomial, but a P 2 P_{2} polynomial. This nonconforming P 3 P_{3} finite element, combined with the discontinuous P 2 P_{2} finite element, is inf-sup stable for solving the Stokes equations on general tetrahedral grids. Consequently such a mixed finite element method produces quasi-optimal solutions for solving the stationary Stokes equations. With these special P 4 P_{4} bubbles, the discrete velocity remains locally pointwise divergence-free. Numerical tests confirm the theory.
