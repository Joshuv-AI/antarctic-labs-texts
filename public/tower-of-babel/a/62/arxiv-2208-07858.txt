2.
Main results

In this section, we classify all pairs of finite dimensional nilpotent Lie algebras
(N,L)
with
s(N,L)=6,7
.

Let
L
be a finite dimensional nilpotent Lie algebra and
N
,
K
be ideals of
L
such that
L=N\oplus K
. Let
\dim N=n
,
\dim K=m
and
\dim N^{2}=d\geqslant 1
.
We have
\dim\mathcal{M}(L)=\dim\mathcal{M}(N)+\dim\mathcal{M}(K)+(n-\dim N^{2})(m-\dim K^{2}),
by Theorem
1.4
. Also

\dim\mathcal{M}(L)=\dim\mathcal{M}(N,L)+\dim\mathcal{M}(K)

(see
[
1
]
and
[
16
]
). Thus

(1)

\dim\mathcal{M}(N,L)=\dim\mathcal{M}(N)+(n-\dim N^{2})(m-\dim K^{2}).

By using Theorem 3.1 of
[
14
]
,
\dim\mathcal{M}(N)\leqslant\frac{1}{2}(n+\dim N^{2}-2)(n-\dim N^{2}-1)+1
.
Therefore

\dim\mathcal{M}(N,L)\leqslant\frac{1}{2}(n+\dim N^{2}-2)(n-\dim N^{2}-1)+1+m(n-\dim N^{2}).

As the above bound for
\dim\mathcal{M}(N,L)
is decreasing with respect to
\dim N^{2}
, there exists an integer
s(N,L)\geqslant 0
in such a way that

\dim\mathcal{M}(N,L)=\frac{1}{2}(n-1)(n-2)+1+m(n-1)-s(N,L).

By replacing
\dim\mathcal{M}(N)=\frac{1}{2}(n-1)(n-2)+1-s(N)
in (
1
), we have

(2)

(n-1)m-(n-\dim N^{2})(m-\dim K^{2})=s(N,L)-s(N).

It is easy to see that

(3)

s(N,L)-s(N)\geqslant m(\dim N^{2}-1),

which implies that
s(N,L)\geqslant s(N)
.

The following theorem determines the structure of all pairs of nilpotent Lie algebras
(N,L)
, when
s(N,L)\leqslant 2
.
