Disconnected contributions to the magnetic polarisability of the neutral pion
Derek Leinweber

Abstract
The magnetic polarisability of the neutral pion has been calculated using the background field method in lattice QCD. These early results do not consider the effect of the disconnected loop contractions arising from the breaking of charge symmetry in a background magnetic field. Recent work in chiral perturbation theory has shown that these quark-self-annihilation contractions provide the leading loop-order contributions to the magnetic polarisability. A first investigation of these contractions in a background magnetic field is presented.

Propagator Methods

The disconnected propagators
S\left(x,x\right)
of Eq.
\left(\ref{eqn:Gpiz}\right)
require a different method to conventional point-to-all propagators; here we use stochastic estimation of the matrix inverse
[
22
,
23
]
.

For each correlator, we construct sets of random noise vectors
\left\{\eta\right\}
with elements drawn from
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
such that averaging over noise vectors gives

\displaystyle\Braket{\eta_{a\alpha}\left(x\right)\,\eta^{\dagger}_{b\beta}\left(y\right)}=\delta_{xy}\,\delta_{ab}\,\delta_{\alpha\beta},

(4)

where
a
,
b
are colour indices,
\alpha
,
\beta
are spin indices and the space-time indices are
x
,
y
. Each noise vector has a corresponding solution vector

\displaystyle\chi=M^{-1}\,\eta,

(5)

where
M
is the fermion matrix. Hence the fermion propagator matrix element is stochastically estimated as

\displaystyle S_{ab;\alpha\beta}\left(x,y\right)\simeq\Braket{\chi_{a\alpha}\left(x\right)\,\eta^{\dagger}_{b\beta}\left(y\right)}.

(6)

The source smearing methods of Ref.
[
34
]
are adopted when smearing disconnected quark propagators. The
SU(3)\times U(1)
eigenmode projection technique of Ref.
[
30
]
is incorporated in the
S\left(x,x\right)
propagator by applying it to the solution vector
\chi
before the disconnected propagator calculation.

We make use of the

6
Conclusion

In this proceedings, we have presented the first preliminary calculation exploring the effect of a background magnetic field to the disconnected loop contractions of the neutral pion. These disconnected loops are the next-leading terms in the magnetic polarisability of the neutral pion and represent an important step towards a complete
ab-initio
calculation of the magnetic polarisability of the neutral pion using lattice QCD and the background field method.

The results presented in this study highlight that these disconnected contributions are in reach of today’s computing efforts and represent an important opportunity to enhance our understanding of the magnetic polarisability of the neutral pion.
