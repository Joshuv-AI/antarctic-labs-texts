Smoothability of relative stable maps to stacky curves
Kenneth Ascher, Dori Bejleri

Abstract




Abstract.
Using log geometry, we study smoothability of genus
0
twisted stable maps to stacky curves relative to a collection of marked points. One application is to smoothing semi-log canonical fibered surfaces with marked singular fibers.

Keywords.
Moduli of curves, moduli of stable maps, log geometry, log stable maps

1.1. Applications to moduli of fibered surfaces

We will need the following results regarding log smooth morphisms.

Remark 3.1

Remark 5.7
.

For the proof of Theorem

is

We use the notation from the introduction. Let

Proposition 6.1

We run through the conditions of Definition

Finally, under this condition, the order of tangency of

to obtain a smoothing with

. So without loss of generality, suppose

.

We proceed by induction on

. The base case is precisely Theorem

. In general, let

be a map in

. Applying Theorem

to

produces a

with tangency

which also satisfies that the curve is smooth along the preimage of

, the generic fiber is contained in

. Therefore, without loss of generality, we may assume that

∎

. By Theorem

, there exists a smoothing of the stable map into the interior of

, we can write
