Perturbation of Schauder frames and besselian Schauder frames of Banach spaces
Samir Kabbaj, Rafik Karkri and Hicham Zoubeir

Abstract
We consider the stability of Schauder frames and besselian Schauder frames under perturbations. Our results are inspirit close to the results of Heil [ 18 ] .

Fundamental results

Theorem 1
.

Let
\mathcal{F}=\left(\left(a_{n},b_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

be a Schauder frame of
E
and

\left(\left(x_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
a paire of

E
. We assume that:

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left\|y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}+\underset{a_{n}\neq 0}{\overset{}{\sum}}2K_{\mathcal{F}}\dfrac{\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}}{\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}}+\underset{a_{n}=0}{\overset{}{\sum}}\left\|b^{*}_{n}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}<1

(1)

Then there exists a sequence
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E^{*}
and a sequence
\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E
such that the following conditions holds:

1.

\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E}\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

2.

\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E^{*}}\left(y_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

3.

\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a Schauder frames of
E
.

Proof
:
Let
x\in E
and
n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}
. We have:

y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}=(y_{n}^{*}-b_{n}^{*})(x)x_{n}+b_{n}^{*}(x)(x_{n}-a_{n})

It follows that:

\left\|y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}\leq\left\|y_{n}^{*}-b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}\left\|x\right\|_{E}+\left|b_{n}^{*}(x)\right|\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}

If
a_{n}\neq 0
, then:

\displaystyle\left|b_{n}^{*}(x)\right|\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\left\|b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}\dfrac{\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}}{\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}}

\displaystyle\leqslant 2K_{\mathcal{F}}\left\|x\right\|_{E}\dfrac{\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}}{\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}}

Hence:

\left\|y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}\leq\left(\left\|y_{n}^{*}-b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}+2K_{\mathcal{F}}\dfrac{\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}}{\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}}\right)\left\|x\right\|_{E}

If
a_{n}=0
. Then,

\left|b_{n}^{*}(x)\right|\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}\leq\left\|b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}

Hence:

\left\|y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}\leq\left(\left\|y_{n}^{*}-b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}+\left\|b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}\right)\left\|x\right\|_{E}

Consequently, we have for each
x\in E
and
n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}

\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}\left\|y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}\leq Q\left\|x\right\|_{E}

where

Q:=\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}\left\|y_{n}^{*}-b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}+\underset{a_{n}\neq 0}{\overset{}{\sum}}2K_{\mathcal{F}}\dfrac{\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}}{\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}}+\underset{a_{n}=0}{\overset{}{\sum}}\left\|b_{n}^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}\right\|_{E}

We have according to the inequality (
1
) then
Q\in[0,1[
. It
follows that the series
\sum_{n}y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}
is convergent in
E
for each

x\in E
. We set

T(x)=\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n},\;\;x\in E

We have then

\displaystyle\left\|T(x)-x\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\left\|\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle\leq\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}\left\|y_{n}^{*}(x)x_{n}-b_{n}^{*}(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle\leq Q\left\|x\right\|_{E}

It follows that
T:E\longrightarrow E
is an isomorphism of Banach spaces. Consequently,

x=\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}y_{n}^{*}(x)T^{-1}(x_{n}),\;\;x\in E

and

x=\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}(y_{n}^{*}\circ T^{-1})(x)x_{n},\;\;x\in E

Let us set

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}w_{n}=T^{-1}(x_{n}),\;\;x\in E\\
z_{n}^{*}=y_{n}^{*}\circ T^{-1},\;\;x\in E\end{array}\right.

Hence

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E}\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\\
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E^{*}}\left(y_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\end{array}\right.

Furthermore,

\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a Schauder frames of
E
.∎

Lemma 1
.

[
20
]

We assume that
E
is a weakly
sequentially complete Banach space and that
\mathcal{F}:=\left(\left(a_{n},b_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
is a besselian paire of
E.
Then for each
x\in E
, the series
\sum b_{n}^{*}\left(x\right)a_{n}
is unconditionally convergent in
E.

Proof.

For each
x\in E
,
y^{*}\in E^{*}
we have:

\displaystyle\overset{+\infty}{\underset{n=1}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}\left(b_{n}^{*}\left(x\right)a_{n}\right)\right|

\displaystyle=\overset{+\infty}{\underset{k=1}{\sum}}\left|b_{n}^{*}\left(x\right)y^{*}\left(a_{n}\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{F}}\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

\displaystyle<+\infty

Hence the series
\sum b_{n}^{*}\left(x\right)a_{n}
is weakly
unconditionally convergent. Then, since
E
is weakly sequentially complete,
the well-known Orlicz’s theorem
(1929)

[
27
, Proposition.4 ,page 59 and page 66]
, entails that the series
\sum b_{n}^{*}\left(x\right)a_{n}

is unconditionally convergent.

\Box

Theorem 2
.

Let
E
be a weakly sequentially complete Banach space and

\mathcal{F}=\left(\left(a_{n},b_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

be a besselian Schauder frame of
E
. Let
\left(\left(x_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

be a paire of
E
for which there exists a constant
\alpha\in[0,1[

such that the following condition holds for each
x\in E
and

y^{*}\in E^{*}

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n}-a_{n})+(y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n})(x)y^{*}(a_{n})\right|<\alpha\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

Then there exists a sequence
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E^{*}
and a sequence
\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E
such that the following conditions holds:

1.

\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E}\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

2.

\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E^{*}}\left(y_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

3.

\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a besselian Schauder frames of
E
.

Proof
: Let
x\in E
and
n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}
. We have:

y^{*}_{n}(x)x_{n}-b^{*}_{n}(x)a_{n}=y^{*}_{n}(x)(x_{n}-a_{n})+(y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n})(x)a_{n}

It follows that we have for all
x\in E
and
y^{*}\in E^{*}
:

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n})\right|

\displaystyle\leq\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|b^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(a_{n})\right|+\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n}-a_{n})+(y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n})(x)y^{*}(a_{n})\right|

\displaystyle\leq(\alpha+\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{F}})\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

Since
E
is a weakly sequentially complet Banach space,
it follows from lemma
1
that the series

\sum_{n}y^{*}_{n}(x)x_{n}
is convergent.
Let us then set:

T(x)=\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}y^{*}_{n}(x)x_{n}

Then we have:

\displaystyle\left\|T(x)-x\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\left\|\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}y^{*}_{n}(x)(x_{n}-a_{n})+(y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n})(x)a_{n}\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\underset{y^{*}\in\mathbb{B}_{E^{*}}}{\sup}\left|\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n}-a_{n})+(y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n})(x)y^{*}(a_{n})\right|

\displaystyle\leq\alpha\left\|x\right\|_{E}

It follows that
T
is a bounded linear operator on
E
and that:

\left\|T-Id_{E}\right\|_{L(E)}\leq\alpha<1

Hence
T
is an isomorphism of Banach spaces. Consequently, the following
identities holds for each
x\in E
:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}x=\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}(y_{n}^{*}\circ T^{-1})(x)x_{n}\\
x=\underset{n=1}{\overset{+\infty}{\sum}}y_{n}^{*}(x)T^{-1}(x_{n})\end{array}\right.

Let us then set

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}w_{n}=T^{-1}(x_{n}),\;\;n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}\\
z^{*}_{n}=y_{n}^{*}\circ T^{-1},\;\;n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}\end{array}\right.

Hence

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E}\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\\
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E^{*}}\left(y_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\end{array}\right.

Furthermore, we have for each
x\in E
and
y^{*}\in E^{*}

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(w_{n})\right|

\displaystyle=\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(T^{-1}(x_{n}))\right|

\displaystyle=\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)(y^{*}\circ T^{-1})(x_{n})\right|

\displaystyle\leq(\alpha+\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{F}})\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\circ T^{-1}\right\|_{E^{*}}

\displaystyle\leq(\alpha+\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{F}})\left\|T^{-1}\right\|_{L(E)}\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

and

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|z^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n})\right|

\displaystyle=\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(T^{-1}(x))y^{*}(x_{n})\right|

\displaystyle\leq(\alpha+\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{F}})\left\|T^{-1}(x)\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

\displaystyle\leq(\alpha+\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{F}})\left\|T^{-1}\right\|_{L(E)}\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

It follows that
\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a besselian Schauder frames of
E
.∎

Corollary 1
.

Let
E
be a weakly sequentially complete Banach space and

\left(\left(a_{n},b_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
be a besselian Schauder frame of
E
.
Let
\left(\left(x_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

be a paire of
E
such that

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left\|y^{*}_{n}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}+\left\|y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}<1

Then there exists a sequence
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E^{*}
and a sequence
\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E
such that the following conditions holds:

1.

\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E}\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

2.

\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E^{*}}\left(y_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

3.

\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a besselian Schauder frames of
E
.

Proof
: Let us set:
\alpha:=\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left\|y^{*}_{n}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|x_{n}-a_{n}\right\|_{E}+\left\|y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n}\right\|_{E^{*}}\left\|a_{n}\right\|_{E}
. We have then
\alpha\in[0,1[
and for each
x\in E
and
y^{*}\in E^{*}
:

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|y^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n}-a_{n})+(y^{*}_{n}-b^{*}_{n})(x)y^{*}(a_{n})\right|<\alpha\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

Consequently, by virtue of the theorem
2
, there exists
a sequence
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E^{*}
and
a sequence
\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
of elements of

E
such that
\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a besselian Schauder frames of
E
.∎

Corollary 2
.

Let
E
be a weakly sequentially complete Banach space and

\left(\left(a_{n},b_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

be a besselian Schauder frame of
E
.
Let
\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

be a sequence of elements of
E
for which there exists a constant

\alpha\in[0,1[
such that the following condition holds for each

x\in E
and
y^{*}\in E^{*}
:

\displaystyle\underset{n=1}{\overset{\infty}{\sum}}\left|b^{*}_{n}(x)y^{*}(x_{n}-a_{n})\right|\leq\alpha\left\|x\right\|_{E}\left\|y^{*}\right\|_{E^{*}}

Then there exists a sequence
\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E^{*}
and a sequence
\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

of elements of
E
such that the following conditions holds:

1.

\left(w_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E}\left(x_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

2.

\left(z_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}\equiv_{E^{*}}\left(b_{n}^{*}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
.

3.

\left(\left(x_{n},z_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

and
\left(\left(w_{n},y_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}

are a besselian Schauder frames of
E
.

Proof
: Let us consider the paire

\left(\left(x_{n},b_{n}^{*}\right)\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}}
of
E
.
We have then for each
x\in E
and
