Introduction

In this paper we deal with the following surface quasi-geostrophic equation with fractional horizontal dissipation and fractional vertical thermal diffusion:

(AQG)_{\alpha,\beta}

\begin{cases}\partial_{t}\theta+u_{\theta}.\nabla\theta+\mu|\partial_{1}|^{2\alpha}\theta+\nu|\partial_{2}|^{2\beta}\theta=0,\\
u_{\theta}=\mathcal{R}^{\perp}\theta,\\
\theta|_{t=0}=\theta^{0}.\end{cases}

Here,
\alpha,\beta\in(0,1)
, and
\mu,\nu>0
are fixed real numbers. The unknown

is a real-valued function defined on
\mathbb{R}^{+}\times\mathbb{R}^{2}

\theta^{0}
as the given initial data. The operator
\mathcal{R}^{\perp}

\displaystyle\mathcal{R}^{\perp}\theta=\left(-\mathcal{R}_{2}\theta,\mathcal{R}_{1}\theta\right)=\left(-\partial_{2}(-\Delta)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\theta,\partial_{1}(-\Delta)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\theta\right),

The operators
|\partial_{1}|^{\gamma}
and
|\partial_{2}|^{\gamma}
are given by

\mathcal{F}(|\partial_{1}|^{\gamma}f)(\xi)=|\xi_{1}|^{\gamma}\mathcal{F}(f)(\xi),\quad\mathcal{F}(|\partial_{2}|^{\gamma}f)(\xi)=|\xi_{2}|^{2\beta}\mathcal{F}(f)(\xi),\quad\forall\xi=(\xi_{1},\xi_{2})\in\mathbb{R}^{2}.

The system

(

We recall some fundamental lemmas concerning Sobolev spaces, beginning with the following classical product rules in homogeneous Sobolev spaces.

Lemma 2.1

(see
[
3
]
)
.

Let

,

(2.1)

Moreover, if
s_{2}<1
, there exists a positive constant

such that for all

and

(2.2)

\|fg\|_{\dot{H}^{s_{1}+s_{2}-1}}\leq C^{\prime}(s_{1},s_{2})\|f\|_{\dot{H}^{s_{1}}}\|g\|_{\dot{H}^{s_{2}}}.

The following lemma represents the injection between the homogeneous Sobolev spaces and Lebesgue spaces .

Lemma 2.2

(see
[
3
]
)

Let

\frac{1}{p}+\frac{\sigma}{2}=\frac{1}{2}.

Then, there is a constant

\|f\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\||\nabla|^{\sigma}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

We also need the following anisotropic interpolation inequalities.

Lemma 2.3

(see

]
)

For

and

(2.3)

\displaystyle\||\partial_{i}|^{zs_{1}+(1-z)s_{2}}f\|_{H^{s}}\leq\||\partial_{i}|^{s_{1}}f\|_{H^{s}}^{z}\||\partial_{i}|^{s_{2}}f\|_{H^{s}}^{1-z},

(2.4)

\displaystyle\||\partial_{i}|^{zs_{1}+(1-z)s_{2}}f\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}\leq\||\partial_{i}|^{s_{1}}f\|_{\dot{H}^{s_{1}}}^{z}\||\partial_{i}|^{s_{2}}f\|_{\dot{H}^{s_{2}}}^{1-z}.

We recall the following important commutator and product estimates:

Lemma 2.4
.

For
s>1
, if
f,g\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{2})
then for any
\alpha\in(0,1)

(2.5)

\displaystyle\||\nabla|^{s}(fg)-f|\nabla|^{s}g\|_{L^{2}}\leq s2^{s}C\left(\||\nabla|^{s+\alpha}f\|_{L^{2}}\||\nabla|^{1-\alpha}g\|_{L^{2}}+\||\nabla|^{s-1+\alpha}g\|_{L^{2}}\||\nabla|^{2-\alpha}f\|_{L^{2}}\right).

Proof.

We start with:

\displaystyle\||\nabla|^{s}(fg)-f|\nabla|^{s}g\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left|\mathcal{F}(|\nabla|^{s}(fg)-f|\nabla|^{s}g)(\xi)\right|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left||\xi|^{s}-|\eta|^{s}\right||\widehat{f}|(\xi-\eta)|\widehat{g}|(\eta)d\eta\right)^{2}d\xi.

We use the elementary inequality:

\left||\xi|^{s}-|\eta|^{s}\right|\leq s2^{s-1}\left(|\xi-\eta|^{s}+|\eta|^{s-1}|\xi-\eta|\right)

Substituting this inequality into our expression, we get:

\displaystyle\||\nabla|^{s}(fg)-f|\nabla|^{s}g\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq s^{2}2^{2(s-1)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\xi-\eta|^{s}|\widehat{f}(\xi-\eta)||\widehat{g}(\eta)|+|\xi-\eta||\widehat{f}(\xi-\eta)||\eta|^{s-1}|\widehat{g}(\eta)|d\eta\right)^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq s^{2}2^{2s}\|f_{1}g_{1}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+s^{2}2^{2s}\|f_{2}g_{2}\|^{2}_{L^{2}},

where

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}(f_{1})(\xi)=|\xi|^{s}|\mathcal{F}(f)(\xi)|

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}(g_{1})(\xi)=|\mathcal{F}(g)(\xi)|

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}(f_{2})(\xi)=|\xi||\mathcal{F}(f)(\xi)|

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}(g_{2})(\xi)=|\xi|^{s-1}|\mathcal{F}(g)(\xi)|

Applying Hölder’s inequality and using Lemma
2.2
, we obtain:

\displaystyle\||\nabla|^{s}(fg)-f|\nabla|^{s}g\|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq s2^{s}\left(\|f_{1}\|_{L^{\frac{2}{1-\alpha}}}\|g_{1}\|_{L^{\frac{2}{\alpha}}}+\|f_{2}\|_{L^{\frac{2}{\alpha}}}\|g_{1}\|_{L^{\frac{2}{1-\alpha}}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq s2^{s}C(\alpha)\left(\||\nabla|^{s+\alpha}f\|_{L^{2}}\||\nabla|^{1-\alpha}g\|_{L^{2}}+\||\nabla|^{2-\alpha}f\|_{L^{2}}\||\nabla|^{s-1+\alpha}g\|_{L^{2}}\right).

This completes the proof.
