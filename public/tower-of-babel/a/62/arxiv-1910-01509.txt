Abstract
MXenes are 2D transition metal carbides with high potential for overcoming limitations of conventional two-dimensional electronics. In this context, various MXenes have shown magnetic properties suitable for applications in spintronics, yet the number of MXenes reported so far is far smaller than their parental MAX phases. In particular, we have investigated the properties of pristine and surface-functionalized ( V 2/3 ​ Zr 1/3 ​ ) 2 ​ CX 2 \text{(}\text{V}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2/3}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2/3}}\text{}}\text{Zr}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{1/3}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{1/3}}\text{}}\text{)}\text{}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{CX}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}} bimetallic and ( V 2/3 ​ □ ​ 1/3 ​ ) 2 ​ CX 2 \text{(}\text{V}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2/3}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2/3}}\text{}}\Box\text{}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{1/3}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{1/3}}\text{}}\text{)}\text{}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{CX}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}} vacancy-ordered MXenes with X = O, F and OH.
