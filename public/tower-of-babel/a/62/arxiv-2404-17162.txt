Abstract
Let A A be a non-trivial Abelian group. A graph G G is A A -vertex magic if there exists a labeling l : V ⁡ ( G ) → A ∖ { 0 } l:V(G)\to A\setminus\{0\} and μ \mu in A A such that w ⁡ ( v ) = ∑ u ∈ N G ​ ( v ) l ⁡ ( u ) = μ w(v)=\sum_{u\in N_{G}(v)}l(u)=\mu for any vertex v v of G G , where N G ​ ( v ) = { u ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) : v ​ u ∈ E ⁡ ( G ) } N_{G}(v)=\{u\in V(G):vu\in E(G)\} is the open neighborhood of the vertex v v in G G . In this paper, we characterize ℤ \mathbb{Z} -vertex magic graphs. We prove that G G is A A -vertex magic if its reduced graph is A A -vertex magic, where A A is a non-trivial Abelian group. We also construct a family of A A -vertex magic graphs using H H -generalized join operation. In addition, we introduce a new type of labeling called A ′ A^{\prime} -vertex magic labeling of graphs and characterize A A -vertex magicness using A ′ A^{\prime} -vertex magicness. .

The above corollary
11
is a generalisation of the following result proved in
[
11
]
which has been proved only for finite Abelian groups and it uses more number of vertices.

Corollary 12
.

(Corollary 2.3,
[
11
]
)
Any graph
G
is an induced subgraph of an
A
-vertex magic graph
H
.

We now state a procedure to
construct infinite classes of
A
-vertex magic graphs, when
|A|>2
. Let
G
be a
A
-vertex magic graph with magic labeling
l
. As
\sim_{G}
is an equivalence relation, join a new vertex
x
in any one of the equivalence class
[v_{i}]
of
G
and join by an edge
x
into each vertex in
N(v_{i})
, the resulting graph is
G^{\prime}
. Then by Lemma
1
, we can relabel the vertices in
[v_{i}]
and
x
whose label sum is equal to
\sum_{v\in[v_{i}]}l({v})
, for the other vertices in
G^{\prime}
we retain the labeling given in
G
by
l
.

The following result proved in
[
11
]

is an immediate consequence of the above construction.

Corollary 13
.

(Theorem 3.1,
[
11
]
)
Let
t\geq 2
. Suppose
G
is an
A
-vertex magic graph of order
n
with a labeling
l
and magic constant
g
. If there exists an edge
uv
in
G
with
l(u)=l(v)=g
, then the graph
G^{\prime}
, obtained from
G
by subdividing the edge
uv
and by attaching
t

x
, is an
A
-vertex magic graph of order
n+t+1
with the same magic constant
g
.

The main difference between our construction and corollary
13
is, it deals with more number of vertices and it is proved under an additional assumption on the magic labeling. We just add only one new vertex and our construction is true for infinite Abelian groups also.

3
Concluding remarks

In this paper we have proved the
A
-vertex magicness of several graphs by considering its reduced graph. These ideas helps us to embed every graph as an induced subgraph of an
A
-vertex magic graph and also to construct infinite families of
A
-vertex magic graphs in a much more simpler way.
