denote the
m
-dimensional eigenspace. Consider the set of all Hermitian forms on
V
that are given by

(23)

\langle\phi,\frac{d}{dt}[(\alpha+tv)*h]\nu\rangle\text{ for }\phi,\nu\in V

as
v
varies within
T_{\alpha}\mathbb{T}^{\beta}
. According to Theorem 1.5
in
[
7
]
,
if there exists
v\in T_{\alpha}\mathbb{T}^{\beta}
such that
the form (
23
) is positive definite
(which we refer to as the BZ condition), then the point

\alpha\in\mathbb{T}^{\beta}
is topologically regular, meaning that for sufficiently small
\delta>0
the set
\mathbb{T}^{\beta}_{\leq\lambda-\delta}
is a strong deformation retract of
\mathbb{T}^{\beta}_{\leq\lambda+\delta}
.
(Here,
\mathbb{T}^{\beta}_{\leq t}=\left\{\alpha^{\prime}\in\mathbb{T}^{\beta}:\ \Lambda_{k}(\alpha^{\prime})\leq t\right\}
and

\lambda=\Lambda_{k}(\alpha)
.)
