On isometric minimal immersion of a singular non-CSC extremal K a ¨ \ddot{a} hler metric into 3-dimensional space forms
Zhiqiang Wei, Yingyi Wu

Abstract
On any compact Riemann surface there always exists a singular non-CSC (constant scalar curvature) extremal K a ¨ \ddot{a} hler metric which is called a non-CSC HCMU (the Hessian of the Curvature of the Metric is Umbilical) metric. In this paper, by moving frames, we show that any non-CSC HCMU metric can not be isometrically minimal immersed into 3-dimensional real space forms even locally. In general, any non-CSC HCMU metric can not be isometrically immersed into 3-dimensional real space forms with constant mean curvature (CMC).

Let

Similarly, for any

Basic equations

Using the Gauss and Weingarten formulas, the basic equations of isometric immersion

\displaystyle R(X,Y,Z,W)=\overline{R}(X,Y,Z,W)+

\displaystyle(\overline{R}(X,Y)Z)^{\perp}=

\displaystyle(\nabla^{\perp}_{X}B)(Y,Z)-(\nabla^{\perp}_{Y}B)(X,Z);

\displaystyle(\overline{R}(X,Y)\xi)^{\perp}=R^{\perp}(

\displaystyle X,Y)\xi+B(A_{\xi}X,Y)-B(X,A_{\xi}Y),

K=c-\frac{4|h|^{2}}{\mu^{2}}.

Let
dh=a\omega+b\overline{\omega}
, then
d\overline{h}=\overline{a}~\overline{\omega}+\overline{b}\omega
, and

\begin{cases}d\omega_{1}^{3}=\sqrt{-1}(b+\overline{b})\omega\wedge\overline{\omega},\\
d\omega_{2}^{3}=(\overline{b}-b)\omega\wedge\overline{\omega}.\end{cases}

Since

\begin{cases}\omega_{1}^{2}\wedge\omega_{2}^{3}=\frac{\mu^{\prime}\mu}{4\sqrt{-1}}(h+\overline{h})\omega\wedge\overline{\omega},\\
\omega_{1}^{2}\wedge\omega_{1}^{3}=\frac{-\mu^{\prime}\mu}{4}(h-\overline{h})\omega\wedge\overline{\omega},\end{cases}

then the Codazzi-equation becomes

\begin{cases}b+\overline{b}=\frac{-\mu^{\prime}\mu}{4}(h+\overline{h}),\\
b-\overline{b}=\frac{-\mu^{\prime}\mu}{4}(h-\overline{h}),\end{cases}

i.e.,

b=-\frac{\mu^{\prime}\mu}{4}h.

To sum up, we get

\begin{cases}K=c-\frac{4|h|^{2}}{\mu^{2}}~~(\textbf{Gauss-equation}),\\
b=-\frac{\mu^{\prime}\mu h}{4}~~(\textbf{Codazzi-equation}).\end{cases}

Differentiating two sides of the Gauss-equation, we get

a\overline{h}+\overline{b}h=-\frac{\mu^{2}[2\mu^{\prime}\mu(K-c)+\mu^{2}]}{16}.

Since
b=-\frac{\mu^{\prime}\mu}{4}h
, then

a=-\frac{\mu^{2}[3\mu^{\prime}\mu(K-c)+\mu^{2}]}{16\overline{h}}=\frac{3\mu^{\prime}\mu h}{4}+\frac{\mu^{2}h}{4(K-c)}.

∎

Lemma 3.3
.

There does not exist a function
h
satisfying the conditions in Lemma
3.2
.

Proof.

Since
dh=a\omega+b\overline{w}
, so

d^{2}h=d(a\omega+b\overline{\omega})=da\wedge\omega+db\wedge\overline{\omega}=0.

Since

\displaystyle da\equiv\frac{\mu^{3}h}{16}[3\mu^{\prime\prime}+\frac{\mu^{\prime}}{K-c}-\frac{\mu}{(K-c)^{2}}]\overline{\omega}~~(mod~\omega),

\displaystyle db\equiv-\frac{\mu^{2}h}{16}[\mu^{\prime\prime}\mu+4(\mu^{\prime})^{2}+\frac{\mu^{\prime}\mu}{K-c}]\omega~~(mod~\overline{\omega}),

\displaystyle da\wedge\omega=\frac{\mu^{3}h}{16}[3\mu^{\prime\prime}+\frac{\mu^{\prime}}{K-c}-\frac{\mu}{(K-c)^{2}}]\overline{\omega}\wedge\omega,

\displaystyle db\wedge\overline{\omega}=\frac{\mu^{2}h}{16}[\mu^{\prime\prime}\mu+4(\mu^{\prime})^{2}+\frac{\mu^{\prime}\mu}{K-c}]\overline{\omega}\wedge\omega,

so

da\wedge\omega+db\wedge\overline{\omega}=\frac{\mu^{2}h}{16(K-c)^{2}}[4\mu^{\prime\prime}\mu(K-c)^{2}+4(\mu^{\prime})^{2}(K-c)^{2}+2\mu^{\prime}\mu(K-c)-\mu^{2}]\overline{\omega}\wedge\omega=0.

Thus

4\mu^{\prime\prime}\mu(K-c)^{2}+4(\mu^{\prime})^{2}(K-c)^{2}+2\mu^{\prime}\mu(K-c)-\mu^{2}=0.

(11)

Suppose

\mu=\sqrt{-\frac{4}{3}(K-K_{1})(K-K_{2})(K+K_{1}+K_{2})}=(-\frac{4}{3}K^{3}+\lambda_{1}K+\lambda_{2})^{1/2},

where
\lambda_{1}=\frac{3}{4}(K_{1}^{2}+K_{2}^{2}+K_{1}K_{2}),\lambda_{2}=\frac{3}{4}K_{1}K_{2}(K_{1}+K_{2})
,
then

\mu^{\prime}=\frac{1}{2}(-\frac{4}{3}K^{3}+\lambda_{1}K+\lambda_{2})^{-1/2}(-4K^{2}+\lambda_{1}),

\mu^{\prime\prime}=-\frac{1}{4}(-\frac{4}{3}K^{3}+\lambda_{1}K+\lambda_{2})^{-3/2}(-4K^{2}+\lambda_{1})^{2}-4K(-\frac{4}{3}K^{3}+\lambda_{1}K+\lambda_{2})^{-1/2},

\mu\mu^{\prime\prime}=-\frac{1}{4}(-\frac{4}{3}K^{3}+\lambda_{1}K+\lambda_{2})^{-1}(-4K^{2}+\lambda_{1})^{2}-4K,

(\mu^{\prime})^{2}=\frac{1}{4}(-\frac{4}{3}K^{3}+\lambda_{1}K+\lambda_{2})^{-1}(-4K^{2}+\lambda_{1})^{2},

\mu\mu^{\prime}=\frac{1}{2}(-4K^{2}+\lambda_{1}),

\mu\mu^{\prime\prime}+(\mu^{\prime})^{2}=-4K,

So

4\mu^{\prime\prime}\mu(K-c)^{2}+4(\mu^{\prime})^{2}(K-c)^{2}\neq\mu^{2}-2\mu^{\prime}\mu(K-c),

that is the identity (
11
) is not true.
∎
