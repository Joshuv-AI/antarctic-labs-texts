Collective order convergence and collectively qualified sets of operators


Abstract
Collective versions of order convergences and corresponding types of collectively qualified sets of operators in vector lattices are investigated. It is proved that collectively order to norm bounded sets are bounded in the operator norm and collectively order continuous sets are collectively order bounded.

In many cases it is necessary to deal with “uniform” or “collective” properties
of a set of operators between vector lattices (shortly, VLs) regarding
the order convergence in their domains. Whereas the corresponding technique
is rather simple for the norm convergence, the case of order convergence
in VLs requires an additional attention. The present note is devoted to investigation
of collective order convergence and its applications to collectively qualified sets of operators in VLs.

Let

2.2
On continuity of order to norm bounded operators.

It is well known that every order bounded operator from a BL to a NL is continuous (cf.,
[
1
, Theorem 1.31]
).
We extend this fact in the following strong collective way.

Theorem 2.1
.

Every collectively order to norm bounded set of operators from a Banach lattice to a normed space is bounded in the operator norm.

Proof.

Let
E
be a BL,
Y
a NS, and
{\cal T}\in\text{\bf L}_{onb}(E,Y)
.
Assume in contrary,
{\cal T}
is not bounded in the operator norm.
Then the set
{\cal T}(B_{E})
, and hence the set
{\cal T}((B_{E})_{+})
is not norm bounded. Thus, there exist
sequences
(x_{n})
in
(B_{E})_{+}
and
(T_{n})
in
{\cal T}
satisfying
\|T_{n}x_{n}\|\geq n^{3}
for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
.
Take
x:=\|\cdot\|-
\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x_{n}}{n^{2}}\in E_{+}
.
Since
{\cal T}\in\text{\bf L}_{onb}(E,Y)
, there exists
M\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
such that

{\cal T}([0,x])\subseteq MB_{Y}
.
Since
\frac{x_{n}}{n^{2}}\in[0,x]
then
\|T_{n}(\frac{x_{n}}{n^{2}})\|\leq M
for every
n\in\mathbb{N}
.
We get a contradiction:

M\geq\Big\|T_{n}\Big(\frac{x_{n}}{n^{2}}\Big)\Big\|\geq n\ \ \ \ (\forall n\in\mathbb{N}).

Therefore, the set
{\cal T}
is norm bounded.
∎

It is worth noting the same argument combined with the Krein – Smulian
theorem (cf.,
[
5
, Theorem 2.37]
) proves norm boundedness of
every collective order to norm bounded set of linear operators from an
ordered Banach space with a closed generating cone to a normed space.

Corollary 2.1
.

Every linear operator from a Banach lattice to a normed space that maps order intervals to norm bounded sets is bounded.
