Reconstruction and Edge Reconstruction of Triangle-free Graphs
Alexander Clifton, Xiaonan Liu, Reem Mahmoud, Abhinav Shantanam

Abstract
The Reconstruction Conjecture due to Kelly and Ulam states that every graph with at least 3 vertices is uniquely determined by its multiset of subgraphs { G − v : v ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) } \{G-v:v\in V(G)\} . Let d ​ i ​ a ​ m ​ ( G ) diam(G) and κ ⁡ ( G ) \kappa(G) denote the diameter and the connectivity of a graph G G , respectively, and let 𝒢 2 := { G : diam ​ ( G ) = 2 } \mathcal{G}_{2}:=\{G:\textrm{diam}(G)=2\} and 𝒢 3 := { G : diam ​ ( G ) = diam ​ ( G ¯ ) = 3 } \mathcal{G}_{3}:=\{G:\textrm{diam}(G)=\textrm{diam}(\overline{G})=3\} . It is known that the Reconstruction Conjecture is true if and only if it is true for every 2-connected graph in 𝒢 2 ∪ 𝒢 3 \mathcal{G}_{2}\cup\mathcal{G}_{3} . Balakumar and Monikandan showed that the Reconstruction Conjecture holds for every triangle-free graph G G in 𝒢 2 ∪ 𝒢 3 \mathcal{G}_{2}\cup\mathcal{G}_{3} with κ ⁡ ( G ) = 2 \kappa(G)=2 . Moreover, they asked whether the result still holds if κ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ 3 \kappa(G)\geq 3 . (If yes, the class of graphs critical for solving the Reconstruction Conjecture is restricted to 2-connected graphs in 𝒢 2 ∪ 𝒢 3 \mathcal{G}_{2}\cup\mathcal{G}_{3} which contain triangles.) In this paper, we give a partial solution to their question by showing that the Reconstruction Conjecture holds for every triangle-free graph G G in 𝒢 3 \mathcal{G}_{3} and every triangle-free graph G G in 𝒢 2 \mathcal{G}_{2} with κ ⁡ ( G ) = 3 \kappa(G)=3 . We also prove similar results about the Edge Reconstruction Conjecture.
