Local Well-posedness for Free Boundary Problem of Viscous Incompressible Magnetohydrodynamics
Kenta Oishi and Yoshihiro Shibata

Abstract
In this paper, we consider the motion of incompressible magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) with resistivity in a domain bounded by a free surface. An electromagnetic field generated by some currents in an external domain keeps an MHD flow in a bounded domain. On the free surface, free boundary conditions for MHD flow and transmission conditions for electromagnetic fields are imposed. We proved the local well-posedness in the general setting of domains from a mathematical point of view. The solutions are obtained in an anisotropic space H p 1 ​ ( ( 0 , T ) , H q 1 ) ∩ L p ​ ( ( 0 , T ) , H q 3 ) H^{1}_{p}((0,T),H^{1}_{q})\cap L_{p}((0,T),H^{3}_{q}) for the velocity field and in an anisotropic space H p 1 ​ ( ( 0 , T ) , L q ) ∩ L p ​ ( ( 0 , T ) , H q 2 ) H^{1}_{p}((0,T),L_{q})\cap L_{p}((0,T),H^{2}_{q}) for the magnetic fields with 2 < p < ∞ 2<p<\infty , N < q < ∞ N<q<\infty and 2 / p + N / q < 1 2/p+N/q<1 . To prove our main result, we used L p L_{p} - L q L_{q} maximal regularity theorem for the Stokes equations with free boundary conditions and for the magnetic field equations with transmission conditions which have been obtained in [ 7 , 16 , 18 ] .
