Molecular beam deposition of a new layered pnictide with distorted Sb square nets
M. Ohno, M. Uchida, Y. Nakazawa, S. Sato, M. Kriener, A. Miyake, M. Tokunaga, Y. Taguchi, M. Kawasaki

Abstract
While the family of layered pnictides A ​ B ​ X 2 ABX_{2} ( A A : rare or alkaline earth metals, B B : transition metals, X X : Sb/Bi) can host Dirac dispersions based on Sb/Bi square nets, nearly half of them has not been synthesized yet for possible combinations of the A A and B B cations. Here we report the fabrication of EuCdSb 2 with the largest B B -site ionic radius, which is stabilized for the first time in thin film form by molecular beam deposition. EuCdSb 2 crystallizes in an orthorhombic P ​ n ​ m ​ a Pnma structure and exhibits antiferromagnetic ordering of the Eu magnetic moments at T N = 15 T_{\mathrm{N}}=15 K. Our successful growth will be an important step for further exploring novel Dirac materials using film techniques.
