Investigations on the flavor-dependent axial charges of the octet baryons
Rui Qi, Jin-Bao Wang, Gang Li, Chun-Sheng An, Cheng-Rong Deng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We have investigated the axial charges of the ground octet baryons within the extended chiral constituent quark model, where all the possible compact five-quark Fock components q ​ q ​ q ​ ( q ​ q ¯ ) ​ ( q = u , d , s ) qqq(q\bar{q})(q=u,d,s) in the baryons are considered. The transition couplings between the three- and five-quark components in the baryons are assumed to be via the P 0 3 {}^{3}P_{0} mechanism, which could reproduce the sea asymmetry in proton very well. The numerical results for the flavor-dependent axial charges of the octet baryons are comparable to those predicted by other theoretical approaches. It is shown that the singlet axial charges of the octet baryons, which should indicate total baryons spin arising from the spin of the quarks, fall in the range 0.45 − 0.75 0.45-0.75 in present model. This is in consistent with the predictions by lattice QCD and chiral perturbation theory. It’s also very interesting that the light quarks spin Δ ​ u \Delta u and Δ ​ d \Delta d in the Λ \Lambda baryon are of small but negative values, which exactly vanish in the traditional three-quark model.

Introduction

The flavor-dependent axial charges of baryons, i.e. the singlet axial charge
g^{(0)}_{A}
,
the isovector axial charge
g^{(3)}_{A}
and the
SU(3)
octet axial charge
g^{(8)}_{A}

are fundamental observables in hadronic physics, since they may provide us information
about the spin structure and properties of baryons. As we know,
g^{(0)}_{A}
should indicate the total baryons spin
arising from the spin of quarks, and
g^{(3)}_{A}
and
g^{(8)}_{A}
should govern the neutron and hyperons
\beta
decays,
and provide a quantitative measure of spontaneous chiral
symmetry breaking in low energy hadronic physics.
In addition, Goldberger-Treiman relation shows that the isovector axial charge of nucleon
could be directly related to the pion decay constant
Goldberger:1958tr
.
Therefore, one may establish a connection between the weak and strong interactions by explicit investigations
on the flavor-dependent axial charges.

During the past years from 1990s, intensively experimental measurements on the axial charges of nucleon have
been performed, triggered by the renowned EMC experiments, which raised up the proton spin puzzle

II.2
Formalism for the axial charges of octet baryons

In this section, we present the formalism for the axial charges

g_{A}^{(0)}
,
g_{A}^{(3)}
and
g_{A}^{(8)}
of octet baryons within the
framework of E
\chi
CQM shown in Sec.
II.1
.

The quark spin contribution
\Delta q
, which is related to the flavour-dependent axial vector current operator, is defined as

\displaystyle\langle B,s_{z}|\int\mathrm{d}x\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{5}q|B,s_{z}\rangle

\displaystyle=s^{\mu}\cdot\Delta q\,,

(9)

where
s^{\mu}
is the baryon states polarization vector, and
\Delta q
is

\Delta q=(q^{\uparrow}+\bar{q}^{\uparrow})-(q^{\downarrow}+\bar{q}^{\downarrow})\,.

(10)

Combinations of different flavor
\Delta f
with
f=u,d,s

lead to flavor-singlet, isovector, and SU(3) octet axial charges of octet baryons, as follows

\displaystyle g^{(0)}_{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta u+\Delta d+\Delta s.

(11)

\displaystyle g^{(3)}_{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta u-\Delta d,

(12)

\displaystyle g^{(8)}_{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta u+\Delta d-2\Delta s.

(13)

In the non-relativistic approximation,
\Delta f
can be gotten directly through the formula:

\Delta f=\langle Bs_{z}|\sum_{j}\,\hat{\sigma}^{z}_{j}\delta_{jf}|Bs_{z}\rangle,

(14)

where
|Bs_{z}\rangle
is the wave function Eq. (
1
) of octet baryons,
\hat{\sigma}^{z}_{j}
is Pauli operator acting on
j
-th quark, and
\delta_{jf}
is a flavor-dependent operator, defined as

\displaystyle\delta_{jf}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if the favour of $j$-th quark is $f$}\\
0&\text{if the favour of $j$-th quark is not $f$}\end{cases}

(15)

Consequently, for each of the octet baryons, one can get

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\Delta f=\frac{1}{\mathcal{N}}\langle qqq,s_{z}|\sum_{j=1,3}\,\hat{\sigma}^{z}_{j}\delta_{jf}|qqq,s_{z}\rangle+\sum_{i}\frac{(C_{i}^{q})^{2}}{\mathcal{N}}

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\langle qqq(q\bar{q}),i,s_{z}|\sum_{j=1,5}\,\hat{\sigma}^{z}_{j}\delta_{jf}|qqq(q\bar{q}),i,s_{z}\rangle,

(16)

where the non-diagonal terms are neglected.

For simplicity, we denote the matrix elements for the three-quark
components and an explicit given
five-quark component in Eq. (
16
) as

\displaystyle\Delta f^{3q}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle qqq,s_{z}|\sum_{j=1,3}\,\hat{\sigma}^{z}_{j}\delta_{jf}|qqq,s_{z}\rangle\,,

(17)

\displaystyle\Delta f_{i}^{5q}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle qqq(q\bar{q}),i,s_{z}|\sum_{j=1}^{5}\,\hat{\sigma}^{z}_{j}\delta_{jf}|qqq(q\bar{q}),i,s_{z}\rangle.

(18)

Accordingly, one can easily obtain the explicit expression of the matrix
elements results for
\Delta u
,
\Delta d
and
\Delta s
of octet baryons
as following

\Delta u=\frac{1}{\mathcal{N}}\Delta u^{3q}+\sum_{i,q}\frac{(C_{i}^{q})^{2}}{\mathcal{N}}\Delta u_{i}^{5q},

(19)

\Delta d=\frac{1}{\mathcal{N}}\Delta d^{3q}+\sum_{i,q}\frac{(C_{i}^{q})^{2}}{\mathcal{N}}\Delta d_{i}^{5q},

(20)

\Delta s=\frac{1}{\mathcal{N}}\Delta s^{3q}+\sum_{i,q}\frac{(C_{i}^{q})^{2}}{\mathcal{N}}\Delta s_{i}^{5q}.

(21)

Finally, considering the
SU(2)
isospin symmetry, one can get
the relations of the spin contributions of quarks with different flavour to spin of the proton
and neutron as

\displaystyle\Delta{u}(p)=\Delta{d}(n),\Delta{d}(p)=\Delta{u}(n),\Delta{s}(p)=\Delta{s}(n)\,,

(22)

and the resulted relations of the axial charges of proton and neutron as

\displaystyle g^{(0)}_{A}(p)=g^{(0)}_{A}(n),g^{(3)}_{A}(p)=-g^{(3)}_{A}(n)\,,

\displaystyle g^{(8)}_{A}(p)=g^{(8)}_{A}(n)\,.

(23)

For the spin of quarks with different flavor and the axial charges
of the other octet baryons, analogous relationship can be obtained.

IV
Summary

To summarize, we investigate the intrinsic five-quark components and the flavor-dependent
axial charges of the octet baryons within the framework of the extended chiral constituent
quark model, in which wave functions of the higher five-quark Fock components in baryons
are taken into account.

The probabilities of the five-quark components in the nucleon are in agreements with other theoretical predictions.
And with the obtained probabilities of the five-quark components
in the wave functions of the octet baryons, we get the pion- and strangeness-baryon sigma
terms consistent with predictions by lattice QCD.

Finally, the present obtained numerical results show that the singlet axial charges of the octet baryons,
which should indicate total baryons spin arising from the spin of the quarks, fall in the
range
0.45-0.75
in present model, it’s in consistent with the predictions by lattice QCD,
chiral perturbation theory, as well the other theoretical approaches.
The present obtained axial charges
g^{(3)}_{A}
and
g^{(8)}_{A}
of the octet baryons are
also comparable to those predicted by lattice QCD and chiral perturbation theory.
It’s also very interesting that the quark spin
\Delta u
and
\Delta d
of
\Lambda

arising from the five-quark components are of small
but nonzero values.

A.3
\Xi
baryon

The isospin wave function of the
\Xi^{+}
baryon is
|1/2,1/2\rangle_{I}
.
Accordingly, for the configurations with
[v]_{F}=[31]_{F_{1}}
,
both the five-quark components with light and strange quark-antiquark
pairs can exist. Accordingly, the flavour decompositions are

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{1}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}|u^{2}s^{2}_{[31]_{F_{1}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle+

(66)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}|uds^{2}_{[31]_{F_{1}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,,

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{1}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|us^{3}_{[31]_{F_{1}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{s}\rangle,

(67)

respectively.

For the configurations with
[v]_{F}=[31]_{F_{2}}
, the
usss\bar{s}
component is the same as the one with
[31]_{F_{1}}
. So we only have to consider the light
five-quark components, which should be

|\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{2}}}=|uds^{2}_{[31]_{F_{2}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,.

(68)

The flavour symmetry
[v]_{F}=[22]_{F}
rules out the five-quark component in

\Xi^{+}
with strange quark-antiquark pair, and
the flavour decomposition for the
uss(q\bar{q})
component is

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[22]_{F}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}|u^{2}s^{2}_{[22]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle-

(69)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}|uds^{2}_{[22]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,.

Finally, for the configurations with
[v]_{F}=[211]_{F}
, only
udss\bar{d}
component exists in

\Xi^{+}
. And

|\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[211]_{F}}=-|uds^{2}_{[211]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,.

(70)

The flavour decompositions of the five-quark components in the
\Xi^{-}

baryon can be obtained by considering the
SU(2)
isospin symmetry as

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{1}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}|uds^{2}_{[31]_{F_{1}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle+

(71)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}|d^{2}s^{2}_{[31]_{F_{1}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,,

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{1}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|ds^{3}_{[31]_{F_{1}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{s}\rangle\,,

(72)

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{2}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|uds^{2}_{[31]_{F_{2}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle\,,

(73)

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[22]_{F}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}|uds^{2}_{[22]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle-

(74)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}|d^{2}s^{2}_{[22]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,,

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle_{I}^{[211]_{F}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|uds^{2}_{[211]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle\,.

(75)

\Lambda

with
[v]_{F}=[31]_{F_{1}}
.
For the configurations with
[v]_{F}=[31]_{F_{2}}
, the light five-quark components should be

\displaystyle|0,0\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{2}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}|u^{2}ds_{[31]_{F_{2}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle+

(76)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}|ud^{2}s_{[31]_{F_{2}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,.

Additionally, the strangeness five-quark component can also exist in
\Lambda
, as

|0,0\rangle_{I}^{[31]_{F_{2}}}=|uds^{2}_{[31]_{F_{2}}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{s}\rangle\,.

(77)

For the five-quark configurations with
[v]_{F}=[22]_{F}
, only the ones with light
quark-antiquark pairs exist in
\Lambda
baryon, and the flavour decomposition is

\displaystyle|0,0\rangle_{I}^{[22]_{F}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}|u^{2}ds_{[22]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle-

(78)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}|ud^{2}s_{[22]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle.

Finally, for the configurations with
[v]_{F}=[211]_{F}
, the light five-quark components should be

\displaystyle|0,0\rangle_{I}^{[211]_{F_{2}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}|u^{2}ds_{[211]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{u}\rangle-

(79)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}|ud^{2}s_{[211]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{d}\rangle\,,

and the strangeness component is

|0,0\rangle_{I}^{[211]_{F}}=|uds^{2}_{[211]_{F}}\rangle\otimes|\bar{s}\rangle\,.

(80)
