Abstract
. In this paper we derive characterizations of boundedness of the subsequences of partial sums with respect to Vilenkin system on the martingale Hardy spaces when 0 < p < 1 0<p<1 . Moreover, we find necessary and sufficient conditions for the modulus of continuity of f ∈ H p f\in H_{p} martingales, which provide convergence of subsequences of partial sums on the martingale Hardy spaces. It is also proved that these results are the best possible in a special sense. As applications, both some well-known and new results are pointed out.
