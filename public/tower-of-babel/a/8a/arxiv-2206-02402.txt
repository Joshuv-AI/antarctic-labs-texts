Generally, valuations are hard to describe explicitly.

In order to treat valuations with positive transcendence defect, we use the induction.

In the Riemann-Zariski space of

What we need to show now is that as the valuation

This is not true unless we use the algebraicity of

3.6 Lemma
. —

For any
f,g\in\mathcal{O}_{X_{\eta}}
, we have

\mathrm{VP}^{(\infty)}\bigl(f+(g^{p}-g)\bigr)(v)=\mathrm{VP}^{(\infty)}(f)(v),\qquad\mathrm{VP}(f)(v)\leq\mathrm{VP}^{(\infty)}(f)(v).

Proof.

We may take
\pi\in\mathcal{O}_{S}\setminus\{0\}
such that
f,g\in\pi^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{S}
.
Let
\mathbf{a}:=\{a_{k}\}_{k\geq 0}
be a sequence in
\pi^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{S}
, and put

v(\mathbf{a}):=\min_{k}\bigl\{0,v(a_{k})\bigr\},\quad v^{(\infty)}(\mathbf{a}):=\min\Bigl\{0,\lim_{m\rightarrow\infty}v\Bigl(\sum_{k=0}^{m}a_{k}^{1/p^{k}}\Bigr)\Bigr\}.

Now, let
\mathbf{b}=\{b_{k}\}_{k\geq 0}
be a sequence in
\pi^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{S}
. Put
\mathbf{b}^{p}:=\{b_{k-1}^{p}\}_{k\geq 0}
(
b_{-1}:=0
).
Then we claim that

v^{(\infty)}(\mathbf{a}+(\mathbf{b}^{p}-\mathbf{b}))=v^{(\infty)}(\mathbf{a}),\qquad v(\mathbf{a})\leq v^{(\infty)}(\mathbf{a}).

If these claims hold, the lemma follows.
Indeed, in order to show the lemma, we take
\{a_{k}\}
(resp.
\{b_{k}\}
) to be the coefficients of

\underline{x}^{p^{k}\underline{n}}
for each
\underline{n}\in\Lambda
in the expansion of
f
(resp.
g
).
Now, to check the first claim, we may compute

\displaystyle(a_{0}-b_{0})+\sum_{k=1}^{m}\bigl(a_{k}+(b_{k-1}^{p}-b_{k})\bigr)^{1/p^{k}}=\bigl(\sum_{k=0}^{m}a_{k}^{1/p^{k}}\bigr)-b_{m}^{1/p^{m}}.

Since
v(b_{m})\geq-v(\pi)
, we have
\liminf_{m\rightarrow\infty}v(b_{m}^{1/p^{m}})\geq 0
, and the first equality follows.
The second inequality is easy to check.
∎

5.9 Corollary
. —

Assume that
X
and
S
are noetherian affine
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-schemes
(
not necessarily of finite type
)
,
and assume we are given a morphism
X\rightarrow S
of finite type.
For any
F\in\mathrm{Fl\acute{E}t}_{d}(X/S)
and
\mathbf{h}\subset\mathcal{O}_{X\times\Delta^{m}}(X\times\Delta^{m})
,

such that for a principal
\mathrm{Mdf}
-sequence

adapted to
(F;\mathbf{h}\vee\mathbf{h}^{\prime})
,

F[\{\mathbf{V}_{i}\}]_{\infty}

.

Proof.

By [EGA IV, 8.9.1], we may find a
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-scheme of finite type
S_{0}
, a morphism of finite type
X_{0}\rightarrow S_{0}
,
functions
\mathbf{h}_{0}\subset\mathcal{O}_{X_{0}\times\Delta^{m}}(X_{0}\times\Delta^{m})
, and a dominant morphism
S\rightarrow S_{0}

such that
X\cong X_{0}\times_{S_{0}}S
and
\mathbf{h}
is the pullback of
\mathbf{h}_{0}
.
Furthermore, by [SGA 4
\frac{1}{2}
, Rapport, 4.6 (i)] and [SGA 4, Exp. IX, 2.7.4],

F
is a pullback of an element of
\mathrm{Fl\acute{E}t}_{d}(X_{S_{0}}/S_{0})
.
Thus, we may assume that
S=S_{0}
, and in particular, we may assume that
S
is finite type over a field
k
of characteristic
p
.

Let
S^{\prime}\rightarrow S
be a morphism, and
Z\subset X_{S^{\prime}}
be any closed subscheme.
By Lemma
1.7
.
3
,
\mathcal{O}_{X}
is a non-constant family of
Z
.
Take a principal
\mathrm{Mdf}
-sequence
\{\mathbf{W}_{i}\}_{i\leq n}
for
(F;\mathbf{h})
.
By Gruson-Raynaud flattening theorem, there exists a modification

such that

\leq d
over

.
