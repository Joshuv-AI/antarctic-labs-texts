Introduction

Singular elliptic problems arise in several context like chemical heterogeneous catalysts, in the theory of pseudoplastic fluid, non-Newtonian fluid and in the study of relativistic matter in magnetic fluid. for more details, see
[
22

10

11

On the other hand, recently, a great deal of works is devoted to the fractional nonlocal problem arising in many fields such as anomalous diffusion in plasma, flames propagation, geophysical fluid dynamics and American options in finances. See
[
8

12
]
and the references therein.

In this work, we continue the study of semilinear singular elliptic problem with the nonlocal operator theory. more precisely, we are interested by the fractional
p
-Laplacian operator which up to normalization functions may be defined as

(-\Delta)_{p}^{s}u(x)\coloneqq\displaystyle 2\lim_{\varepsilon\rightarrow 0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus B_{\varepsilon}(x)}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}(u(x)-u(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dy,\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{N},

\varepsilon

x

\varepsilon

[

17

In the last years, many works are devoted to the quasi-linear problem

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}(-\Delta)_{p}^{s}u=f(x,u)&\quad\mbox{in }\ \Omega\\[8.5359pt]
u=0&\quad\mbox{on }\ \mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega.\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

p=2

When the nonlinearity is discontinuous, the second author study problem (
1.1

is a given function and

.

For

When

. More precisely,

The authors prove the existence and multiplicity result via the nonsmooth critical point theory.

In this paper, we are interested to study the existence of weak solutions for problem (

) with singular absorption term of the following types

2.1
Variational Formulation of the Problem.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be a bounded domain containing the origin and with smooth boundary
\partial\Omega
, for
p\in(1,\infty)
and
s\in(0,1)
, we define the fractional Sobolev space

W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\coloneqq\left\{u\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\penalty\ :\penalty\ \dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|x-y|^{\frac{N}{p}+s}}\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{2N})\right\},

endowed with the norm

\|u\|_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}\coloneqq\left(\|u\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{p}+\left[u\right]_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

where the following term is the Gagliardo semi-norm of u defined as

\left[u\right]_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}\coloneqq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

We shall work in the following closed linear subspace

W_{0}\coloneqq\left\{u\in W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\penalty\ :\penalty\ u=0\penalty\ \mbox{ a.e. in }\penalty\ \mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega\right\},

which can be equivalently renormed by

\|u\|_{W_{0}}\coloneqq\left[u\right]_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}.

Let
1<ps<N
, then there exist a positive constant
c_{H}
such that we present the fractional Hardy inequality, which says that

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\leq c_{H}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy,\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \forall u\in W_{0}.

(2.6)

For more details, see
[
15
]
.

Now, let
\mathbb{C}_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
the space of functions on
\mathbb{R}
that are infinitely differentiable and have compact support contained in
\mathbb{R}^{N}
. If we denote by
\mathbb{D}^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
the closure of
\mathbb{C}_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
with respect to the Gagliardo semi-norm
\left[u\right]_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})},
then for
sp<N,
we define the Sobolev constant
S_{p}
by

S_{p}=\displaystyle\inf_{v\in\mathbb{D}^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}\frac{\left[u\right]_{W^{s,p}}^{p}}{\|v\|_{L^{p_{s}^{*}}}^{p}},

(2.7)

where
p_{s}^{*}=\frac{Np}{N-sp}
is the critical Sobolev exponent.

Also, It is well-known that
(W_{0},\|\cdot\|_{W_{0}})
is a uniformly convex (i.e. reflexive) Banach space, continuously embedded into
L^{p}(\Omega)
for all
p\in[1,p^{*}]
and compactly injected in
L^{p}(\Omega)
for all
p\in[1,p^{*})
, there exist a
c_{p}>0
which is the best constant of the embedding, such that

||u||_{L^{p}(\Omega)}\leq c_{p}||u||_{W_{0}},\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \forall u\in W_{0}.

(2.8)

Next, let us denote by
(W^{*}_{0},\|\cdot\|_{W^{*}_{0}})
the dual space of
(W_{0},\|\cdot\|_{W_{0}})
and we define the nonlinear operators
A_{p,m}:W_{0}\rightarrow W^{*}_{0}
as

\langle A_{p,m}(u),v\rangle=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}(u(x)-u(y))(v(x)-v(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\frac{|u(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{sp}}u(x)v(x)\penalty\ dx,

where
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
denotes the inner product on
W_{0}
and
m=1
or
m=-1.

Lemma 2.1
.

For
u,v\in W_{0}
and there exist a constant
k\geq 1
, then the nonlinear operators
A_{p,m}
are well defined and verify the following:

\langle A_{p,m}(u),v\rangle\leq k\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}

and

\|A_{p,m}(u)\|_{W^{*}_{0}}\leq k\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}.

Proof.
For all
u,v\in W_{0}
, we have

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,m}(u),v\rangle

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}(u(x)-u(y))(v(x)-v(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{sp}}u(x)v(x)\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}|u(x)-u(y)||v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{(N+sp)\left(\frac{p-1+1}{p}\right)}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{(sp)\left(\frac{p-1+1}{p}\right)}}|u(x)||v(x)|\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-1}}{|x-y|^{(N+sp)\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{\left(\frac{N+sp}{p}\right)}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p-1}}{|x|^{(sp)\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)}}\dfrac{|v(x)|}{|x|^{\frac{ps}{p}}}\penalty\ dx.

Then, by Holder inequality we get

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,m}(u),v\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\left(\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ +m\left(\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}\left(\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

For any
\beta\in(0,1)
and
a,b,c,d>0
, we use the following inequality

a^{\beta}c^{1-\beta}+m\penalty\ b^{\beta}d^{1-\beta}\leq(a+m\penalty\ b)^{\beta}(c+m\penalty\ d)^{1-\beta},

by setting
\beta=\frac{p-1}{p}
and

\begin{array}[]{l}a=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\\[11.38092pt]
b=\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\\[11.38092pt]
c=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\\[11.38092pt]
d=\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx,\end{array}

we can deduce that

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,m}(u),v\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\left(\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \times\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Then, according to the fractional Hardy inequality (
2.6
) we get

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,1}(u),v\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\left(\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+c_{H}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \times\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+c_{H}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

\displaystyle\leq(c_{H}+1)\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}

\displaystyle\leq k\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}

\displaystyle<+\infty.

Where
k=c_{H}+1
and
k\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,-1}(u),v\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\left(\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy-\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \times\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy-\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

\displaystyle\leq\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}

\displaystyle<+\infty.

Moreover, respectively we have

\|A_{p,1}(u)\|_{W^{*}_{0}}\leq k\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}

and

\|A_{p,-1}(u)\|_{W^{*}_{0}}\leq\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}.

\square

Lemma 2.2
.

For all
u,v\in W_{0}
and there exist a constant
\mu\leq 1
, the operators
A_{p,m}
satisfy the following inequalities :

\langle A_{p,1}(u)-A_{p,1}(v),u-v\rangle\geq k\left(\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\right)\left(\|u\|_{W_{0}}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}\right)

and

\langle A_{p,-1}(u)-A_{p,-1}(v),u-v\rangle\geq\mu\left(\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\right)\left(\|u\|_{W_{0}}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}\right).

Proof.
By direct computation, we have

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,m}(u)-A_{p,m}(v),u-v\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle A_{p,m}(u),u-v\rangle-\langle A_{p,m}(v),u-v\rangle

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}(u(x)-u(y))((u-v)(x)-(u-v)(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ +m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{sp}}u(x)(u-v)(x)\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ -\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p-2}(v(x)-v(y))((u-v)(x)-(u-v)(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ +m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{sp}}v(x)(u-v)(x)\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx+\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle-\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}(u(x)-u(y))(v(x)-v(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{sp}}u(x)v(x)\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle-\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p-2}(v(x)-v(y))(u(x)-u(y))}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p-2}}{|x|^{sp}}v(x)u(x)\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|u|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx+\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}\penalty\ dxdy+m\int_{\Omega}\dfrac{|v|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\penalty\ dx

\displaystyle\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ -\langle A_{p,m}(u),v\rangle-\langle A_{p,m}(v),u\rangle.

Furthermore, by Lemma
2.1
, there exist
k\geq 1
such that

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,1}(u)-A_{p,1}(v),u-v\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\langle A_{p,1}(u),v\rangle-\langle A_{p,1}(v),u\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-k\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}-k\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|u\|_{W_{0}}

\displaystyle=-k\left(-\frac{1}{k}\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\frac{1}{k}\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}+\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|u\|_{W_{0}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq-k\left(-\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}+\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|u\|_{W_{0}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq k\left(\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\right)\left(\|u\|_{W_{0}}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}\right).

Likewise, according to the fractional Hardy inequality (
2.6
) and Lemma
2.1
we get

\displaystyle\langle A_{p,-1}(u)-A_{p,-1}(v),u-v\rangle

\displaystyle\geq(1-c_{H})\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+(1-c_{H})\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\langle A_{p,-1}(u),v\rangle-\langle A_{p,-1}(v),u\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\mu\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\mu\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|u\|_{W_{0}}

\displaystyle=-\mu\left(-\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\dfrac{1}{\mu}\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}+\dfrac{1}{\mu}\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|u\|_{W_{0}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq-\mu\left(-\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p}-\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p}+\|u\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|v\|_{W_{0}}+\|v\|_{W_{0}}^{p-1}\|u\|_{W_{0}}\right)

where

\square

Proposition 2.1
.

The nonlinear operators
A_{p,m}
have the following properties :

1)

A_{p,m}:W_{0}\rightarrow W^{*}_{0}
are a continuous, bounded and strictly monotone operators, i.e: if
\langle A_{p,m}(u)-A_{p,m}(v),u-v\rangle>0
,
\forall u\neq v
.

2)

A_{p,m}
are a mappings of type
(S)
, i.e: if
u_{n}\rightarrow u
weakly in
W_{0}
and
\displaystyle\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\langle A_{p,m}(u_{n})-A_{p,m}(u),u_{n}-u\rangle\leq 0
, then
u_{n}\rightarrow u
strongly in
W_{0}
.

3)
