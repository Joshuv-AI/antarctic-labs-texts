The homogeneous spectrum of a ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} -graded commutative ring
Mohamed Aqalmoun

Abstract
. Let ℤ 2 := ℤ / 2 ​ ℤ \mathbb{Z}_{2}:=\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} be the additive group with two elements. Proposition 2.1

Let

-graded ring and

(2)

and

-submodule of

Let

-submodule of

with

. Clearly
