A Liouville comparison principle for solutions of semilinear elliptic second-order partial differential inequalities
Vasilii V. Kurta

Abstract
We consider semilinear elliptic second-order partial differential inequalities of the form L ​ u + | u | q − 1 ​ u ≤ L ​ v + | v | q − 1 ​ v ( ∗ ) Lu+|u|^{q-1}u\leq Lv+|v|^{q-1}v\qquad(\ast) in the whole space ℝ n {\mathbb{R}}^{n} , where n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 , q > 0 q>0 and L L is a linear elliptic second-order partial differential operator in divergence form. We assume that the coefficients of the operator L L are measurable and locally bounded such that the quadratic form associated with the operator L L is symmetric and non-negative definite. We obtain a Liouville comparison principle in terms of a capacities associated with the operator L L for solutions of ( ∗ \ast ) which are measurable and belong locally in ℝ n {\mathbb{R}}^{n} to a Sobolev-type function space also associated with the operator L L .
