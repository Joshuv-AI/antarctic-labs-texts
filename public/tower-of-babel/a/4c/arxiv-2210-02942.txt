The Local Isometric Embedding of Two-Metrics of Low Differentiability in Euclidean Three Space.


Abstract
We prove that the isometric embedding of any metric of differentiability class C 1 C^{1} in E 3 E^{3} exists. We use simplified notation for the given metric, namely geodesic parameters, and level parameters for the embedded surface in E 3 . E^{3}. Central to our discussion will be solutions of initial value problems for two first order non-linear partial differential equations. We also make use of the classical theory of linear algebraic systems. We will prove local isometric embedding. An example is given for which the Gaussian curvature of the metric is equal to one but the embedded surface is non-analytic.

Proof of Main Theorem.

in

\bar{X}(\bar{u},\bar{v})

E^{3}

1,0,\bar{G},
i.e., to the components of the given

\omega.

\bar{X}_{\bar{u}}^{2}=(X_{u}u_{\bar{u}}+X_{v}v_{\bar{u}})^{2}=X_{u}^{2}u_{\bar{u}}^{2}+2X_{u}\cdot X_{v}u_{\bar{u}}v_{\bar{u}}+X_{v}^{2}v_{\bar{u}}^{2}

=(x_{u}^{2}+y_{u}^{2})u_{\bar{u}}^{2}+(x_{v}^{2}+y_{v}^{2}+1)v_{\bar{u}}^{2}.

Proof of the existence of the inverse of the prameter change.

Lemma. The Jacobian of the parameter change is not zero locally.

Proof. Define

\displaystyle\lambda(\bar{u},\bar{v})

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\frac{f_{\bar{u}}^{2}}{1-f_{\bar{u}}^{2}}}.
