Existence and uniqueness of fixed point on closed ball in multiplicative
𝐆

Abstract




Abstract

In this article, we investigate some fixed point results satisfying a new generalized
\Delta
-implicit contractive condition in ordered complete multiplicative
\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}-
metric space. Also, some new definitions and fixed point theorems are presented in ordered complete multiplicative
\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}-
metric space. Furthermore, some nontrivial and illustrative examples are given to validate our obtained results.

Keywords:

Multiplicative
\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}-
metric, closed ball, generalized
\Delta
-implicit contraction, fixed point methodology.

Mathematics Subject Classification:
47H10, 55H02.

3
Main Results

Now, we present our main theorem in ordered complete multiplicative
\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}-
metric space.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
(\mathcal{L},\leq,{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}})
be an ordered complete
\>M^{\circ}\,\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}-M^{\bullet}S
. Suppose the mapping
\mathcal{\mathring{F}}:\mathcal{L}\longrightarrow\mathcal{L}
with
\eta\in[0,1)
and
\,\gamma>0,
satisfying the following,

\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\varpi},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\vartheta})}\>\>\leq\>\>\bigg[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu},\check{\varpi},\check{\vartheta})}\>\bigg]^{\eta},

(3.1)

and

{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0})\>\>\leq\>\>(1-\eta)\,\gamma,

(3.2)

for
\check{\nu},\check{\varpi},\check{\vartheta}\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
. If for a non-increasing (
\mathbf{non-inc}
) sequence
\{\check{\nu}_{n}\}\longrightarrow s
implies that
s\preceq\check{\nu}_{n}
. Then, there is a point
\check{\nu}^{*}
in
\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
such that
\check{\nu}^{*}=\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*}
and
\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})=1.
Furthermore, if for any two points
\check{\nu},\check{\varpi}
in
\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
and there exists a point
t\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
such that
\,t\preceq\check{\nu}\>
and
\>t\preceq\check{\varpi}
, that is every two points in
\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
has a lower bound (
\mathbf{LB}
). Then, a point
\check{\nu}^{*}
is unique in
\mathcal{L}
.

Proof.
Let
\check{\nu}_{0}
be any arbitrary point in
\mathcal{L}
and picard sequence
\check{\nu}_{j+1}=\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{j}\preceq\check{\nu}_{j}\>
for all
n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}.
From Ineq. (3.2), we get

{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})\>\>\leq\>\>(1-\eta)\>\gamma\>\>\leq\>\>\gamma,

implying thereby that
\>\check{\nu}_{1}\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}.
By multiplicative triangle inequality, we have

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{2},\check{\nu}_{2})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{2},\check{\nu}_{2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{1},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{1})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>\Big]^{1+\eta},

that is,

\displaystyle{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{2},\check{\nu}_{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big[{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0})\>\Big]^{1+\eta}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\big[\>(1-\eta)\>\gamma\>\big]^{1+\eta}\>\>\leq\>\>\gamma.

Then,
\check{\nu}_{2}\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}.\>
Consider
\>\check{\nu}_{3},\check{\nu}_{4},...,\check{\nu}_{q}\>
for every
q\in\mathbb{N}.\>
Taking Ineq. (3.1) in consideration, we obtain

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{q},\check{\nu}_{q+1},\check{\nu}_{q+1})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{q-1},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{q},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{q})}\>\>\leq\>\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{q-1},\check{\nu}_{q},\check{\nu}_{q})}\>\Big]^{\eta}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{q-2},\check{\nu}_{q-1},\check{\nu}_{q-1})}\>\Big]^{\eta^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>\Big]^{\eta^{\,q}}.\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt\hskip 23.49976pt(3.3)

Using Ineq. (3.1) and Ineq. (3.3), we find

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{q+1},\check{\nu}_{q+1})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{2},\check{\nu}_{2})}\>.\>...\>.\>\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{q},\check{\nu}_{q+1},\check{\nu}_{q+1})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\bigg[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>\bigg]^{1+\eta+...+\eta^{\,q}},

that becomes as follows

\displaystyle{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{q+1},\check{\nu}_{q+1})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\bigg[{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0})\bigg]^{\dfrac{1-\eta^{\,q+1}}{1-\eta}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\bigg[(1-\eta)\,\gamma\>\bigg]^{\dfrac{1-\eta^{\,q+1}}{1-\eta}}\>\leq\>\gamma.

Hence,
\check{\nu}_{q+1}\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}.\>
Thus,
\>\check{\nu}_{j}\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}\>
for all
\>j\in\mathbb{N}.\>
Consequently, Ineq. (3.3) convert to

\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j+1},\check{\nu}_{j+1})}\>\>\leq\>\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>\Big]^{\eta^{\,j}}.

(3.4)

From Ineq. (3.4), we have

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j+k},\check{\nu}_{j+k})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j+1},\check{\nu}_{j+1})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{j+1},\check{\nu}_{j+2},\check{\nu}_{j+2})}\>.\>...\>.\>\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{j+k-1},\check{\nu}_{j+k},\check{\nu}_{j+k})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big[\sqrt[m]{{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>\Big]^{\eta^{\,j}\>\dfrac{1-\eta^{k}}{1-\eta}}\>\longrightarrow 1,\hskip 11.74988pt\>\>j\longrightarrow+\infty.

This means the sequence
\{\check{\nu}_{j}\}
is a
\>M^{\circ}\,\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}-C^{\bullet}\>
sequence in

\>(\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)},{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}})
.

Furthermore, there exists
\>\check{\nu}^{*}\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}\>
with

\lim\limits_{j\longrightarrow+\infty}\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})}\>\>=\>\>\lim\limits_{j\longrightarrow+\infty}\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j})}\>\>=\>\>1.

(3.5)

Now, assume that
\check{\nu}^{*}\preceq\check{\nu}_{j}\preceq\check{\nu}_{j-1},
then

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{j},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{j-1},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j})}\>.\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{j-1},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})}\>\Big]^{\eta}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\lim\limits_{j\longrightarrow+\infty}\bigg(\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}_{j},\check{\nu}_{j})}\>.\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{j-1},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})}\>\Big]^{\eta}\>\bigg)\>\>=\>\>1,

which is a contradiction. Then,
\check{\nu}^{*}=\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*}.\>
By a similar method,
\>\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})=1\>
and hence,
\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*}=\check{\nu}^{*}.
Now,

\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})}\>=\>\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*})}\>\>\leq\>\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})}\>\Big]^{\eta},

which is a contradiction, since
\eta\in[0,1).\>
Thus,
\>\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})=1.

Uniqueness:

Consider
\check{\varpi}^{*}
be another point in
\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
such that
\check{\varpi}^{*}=F\check{\varpi}^{*}.\>
If
\>\check{\nu}^{*}\>
and
\>\check{\varpi}^{*}\>
are comparable, then

\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*})}\>=\>\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\varpi}^{*},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\varpi}^{*})}\>\>\leq\>\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*})}\>\Big]^{\eta},

which is contradiction that tend us to

\>\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*})=1\hskip 11.74988pt\text{implies}\hskip 11.74988pt\check{\nu}^{*}=\check{\varpi}^{*}.\>

Similarly, we can prove
\>\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\varpi}^{*},\check{\varpi}^{*},\check{\nu}^{*})=1.

On the other hand, If
\>\check{\nu}^{*}\>
and
\>\check{\varpi}^{*}\>
are not comparable then there is a point
\>t\in\overline{\mathcal{B}_{\gamma}(\check{\nu}_{0},\gamma)}
which is the
\>\mathbf{LB}\>
of
\>\check{\nu}^{*}\>
and
\>\check{\varpi}^{*}\>
that is
\>t\preceq\check{\nu}^{*}\>
and
\>t\preceq\check{\varpi}^{*}.\>
Furthermore, by argument
\>\check{\nu}^{*}\preceq\check{\nu}_{n}\>
as
\>\check{\nu}_{n}\longrightarrow\check{\nu}^{*}.\>
Thus,
\>t\preceq\check{\nu}^{*}\preceq\check{\nu}_{n}\preceq...\preceq\check{\nu}_{0}
.

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t,\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\check{\nu}_{1},\check{\nu}_{1})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{1},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t,\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0})}\>.\>\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t,\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0})}\>.\>\Big[\sqrt[m]{\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},t,t)}\>\Big]^{\eta},

that is,

\displaystyle\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t,\mathcal{\mathring{F}}t)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0},\mathcal{\mathring{F}}\check{\nu}_{0})\>.\>\Big[\mathbf{G}_{\mathcal{M}}(\check{\nu}_{0},t,t)\Big]^{\eta}
