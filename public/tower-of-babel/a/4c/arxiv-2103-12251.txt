Abstract
Using p p -adic numbers, we partially categorize the cycles of a sizable class of polynomial dynamical systems. In turn, we prove a few results related to the non-trivial cycles of the Collatz map Col : ℤ + → ℤ + {\text{Col}}:\mathbb{Z}_{+}\to\mathbb{Z}_{+} defined by Col ​ ( n ) = { 3 ​ n + 1 , n ​ is odd; n / 2 , otherwise. {\text{Col}}(n)=\begin{cases}3n+1,&n\text{ is odd;}\\ n/2,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases} Proving the non-existence of non-trivial Collatz cycles would reduce the Collatz conjecture to whether the Collatz map can diverge to infinity.
