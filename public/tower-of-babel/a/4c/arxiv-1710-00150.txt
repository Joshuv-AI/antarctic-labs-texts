Abstract
The cross section of the e + ​ e − → Λ c + ​ Λ ¯ c − e^{+}e^{-}~\rightarrow~\Lambda_{c}^{+}\bar{\Lambda}_{c}^{-} process is measured with unprecedented precision using data collected with the BESIII detector at s = 4574.5 \sqrt{s}=4574.5 , 4580.0 4580.0 , 4590.0 4590.0 and 4599.5 4599.5 MeV \mathrm{MeV} . The non-zero cross section near the Λ c + ​ Λ ¯ c − \Lambda_{c}^{+}\bar{\Lambda}_{c}^{-} production threshold is cleared. At center-of-mass energies s = 4574.5 \sqrt{s}=4574.5 and 4599.5 4599.5 MeV \mathrm{MeV} , the higher statistics data enable us to measure the Λ c \Lambda_{c} polar angle distributions. From these, the Λ c \Lambda_{c} electric over magnetic form factor ratios ( | G E / G M | |G_{E}/G_{M}| ) are measured for the first time. In summary, using data collected at
\sqrt{s}=4574.5
,
4580.0
,
4590.0
, and
4599.5~\mathrm{MeV}
with the BESIII detector, the cross sections of
e^{+}e^{-}~\rightarrow~\Lambda_{c}^{+}\bar{\Lambda}_{c}^{-}
have been measured with high precision, by reconstructing
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
and
\bar{\Lambda}_{c}^{-}
independently with ten Cabibbo-favored hadronic decay channels. The most precise cross section measurement is achieved so far at
\sqrt{s}=4574.5~\mathrm{MeV}
, which is only
1.6~\mathrm{MeV}
above the threshold. The measured value is (
236\pm 11\pm 46
) pb, which highlights the enhanced cross section near threshold and indicates the complexity of production behavior of the
\Lambda_{c}
. At
\sqrt{s}=4574.5
and
4599.5~\mathrm{MeV}
, the data samples are large enough to study polar angle distributions of
\Lambda_{c}
and measure the
\Lambda_{c}
form factor ratio
|G_{E}/G_{M}|
for the first time. These results provide important insights into the production mechanism and structure of the
\Lambda_{c}
baryons.
