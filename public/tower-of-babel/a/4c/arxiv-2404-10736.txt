On forcing axioms and weakenings of the Axiom of Choice
Diego Lima Bomfim, Charles Morgan and Samuel Gomes da Silva

Abstract
. We prove forcing axiom equivalents of two families of weakenings of the axiom of choice: a trichotomy principle for cardinals isolated by Lévy, H κ \mathop{\rm H\hskip 0.05pt}_{\kappa} , and DC κ \mathop{\rm DC}_{\kappa} , the principle of dependent choices generalized to cardinals κ \kappa , for regular cardinals κ \kappa . Using these equivalents we obtain new forcing axiom formulations of AC \mathop{\rm AC} . A point of interest is that we use a new template for forcing axioms. For the class of forcings to which we asks that the axioms apply, we do not ask that they apply to all collections of dense sets of a certain cardinality, but rather only for each particular forcing to a specific family of dense sets of the cardinality in question.

Corollary 4.21
.

\mathop{\rm DC}\Longleftrightarrow\mathop{\rm FA}_{\omega}(\Lambda)
.

Proof.

“
\Longrightarrow
”.
\mathop{\rm DC}
implies
\mathop{\rm FA}_{\omega}(\{\hskip 1.0pt\mathbb{P}\thinspace|\thinspace\mathbb{P}\hbox{ is a po}\hskip 1.0pt\})

implies
\mathop{\rm FA}_{\omega}(\Lambda)

“
\Longleftarrow
” Suppose
\mathbb{T}=\mathbb{T}(F)\in{\mathcal{T}_{\omega}}
for some

X\not\prec\kappa
and
F\in{\mathcal{F}}^{X}_{\omega}
. By definition we have

\mathbb{T}=\{\hskip 1.0ptt\in\mathop{\rm Coll}(\omega,X)\thinspace|\thinspace\forall i<\mathop{\rm lh}(t)\thinspace\thinspace t(i)\in F(t\upharpoonright i)\hskip 1.0pt\}
. We can apply the isomorphism of partial orders
defined in Lemma (
4.10
) between
\mathop{\rm Coll}(\kappa,X)
and
\mathbb{Q}^{X}_{\kappa}
to
\mathbb{T}
to obtain an isomorphic subpartial order of

\mathbb{Q}^{X}_{\kappa}
which is a member of
\Lambda
.

\mathop{\rm FA}_{\omega}(\Lambda)

\mathop{\rm FA}({\mathcal{T}_{\omega}},{\mathfrak{L}^{\omega}})

\mathbb{T}\in{\mathcal{T}_{\omega}}

i<\omega

L^{\omega}_{i}

\mathbb{T}
,

\mathop{\rm DC}
by the previous proposition in the case
