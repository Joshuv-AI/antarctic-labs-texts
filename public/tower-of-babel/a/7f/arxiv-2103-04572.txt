On the widths of finite groups (I)
Wujie Shi

Abstract




Abstract:
Let
G
be a finite group,
\pi(G)
be the set of prime divisors dividing the order of G and
\pi_{e}(G)
(spectrum) denote the set of element orders of
G
. We define
w_{o}(G)=|\pi(G)|
the width of order of G and
w_{s}(G)=max\{|\pi(k)|\mid k\in\pi_{e}(G)\}
the width of spectrum of
G
. In this paper, we discuss the cases of
w_{o}(G)
and
w_{s}(G)
are small, prove several new results and give a survey about the two widths of groups. This article is a part of revised based on the author’s plenary talk at “2020 Ural Workshop on Group Theory and Combinatorics”.

Keywords:
Finite groups, quantitative characterization, the width of order, the width of spectrum.

The case of simple groups of Lie type in characteristic 2 were described by [24], later this result was obtained independently by [25, 26]. Unfortunately, all the papers contain some inaccuracies.
