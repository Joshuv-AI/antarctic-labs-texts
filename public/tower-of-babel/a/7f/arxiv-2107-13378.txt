Abstract
. In this study, we define a brief description of the hyperbolic and elliptic rotational surfaces using a curve and matrices in 4-dimensional semi-Euclidean space with index 2. That is, we provide different types of rotational matrices, which are the subgroups of M M by rotating a selected axis in E 4 E^{4} . Also, we choose two parameter matrices groups of rotations and we give the matrices of rotation corresponding to the appropriate subgroup in 4-dimensional semi-Euclidean space. Therefore, we generate surfaces of rotation using Killing vector fields in E 2 4 E_{2}^{4} and we give the Gaussian curvature and the mean curvature of the surfaces of rotation.
