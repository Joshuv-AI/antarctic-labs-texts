The generic étaleness of the moduli space of dormant 𝔰 ​ 𝔬 2 ​ ℓ \mathfrak{so}_{2\ell} -opers
Yasuhiro Wakabayashi

Abstract
. The generic étaleness is an important property on the moduli space of dormant 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} -opers (for a simple Lie algebra 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} ) in the context of enumerative geometry. In the previous study, this property has been verified under the assumption that 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} is either 𝔰 ​ 𝔩 ℓ \mathfrak{sl}_{\ell} , 𝔰 ​ 𝔬 2 ​ ℓ − 1 \mathfrak{so}_{2\ell-1} , or 𝔰 ​ 𝔭 2 ​ ℓ \mathfrak{sp}_{2\ell} for any sufficiently small positive integer ℓ \ell . The purpose of the present paper is to prove the generic étaleness for one of the remaining cases, i.e., 𝔤 = 𝔰 ​ 𝔬 2 ​ ℓ \mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{so}_{2\ell} . As an application of this result, we obtain a factorization formula for computing the generic degree induced from pull-back along various clutching morphisms between moduli spaces of pointed stable curves.

Introduction

Linear differential equations, or more generally flat connections,

p>0
(where
p

For example,
the study of such objects involving the Grothendieck-Katz conjecture

[

Also, characteristic-

[

A key ingredient in these works is

By definition, the

p
-power structures
appearing in certain associated spaces of infinitesimal symmetries.
In particular, some classes of flat connections characterized by
p

Our study primary

As suggested above,
the
generic étaleness
of
\Pi_{\mathfrak{g},g,r}
has great importance
from the viewpoint of enumerative geometry.
This property has also been verified
for
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell-1}
and
\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{p}_{2m}
(cf.
[
Wak3
, Theorem G]
), and we expect the same for general
\mathfrak{g}
’s.
The purpose of the present paper is to prove the generic étaleness for one of the remaining cases, i.e.,
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell}
.
Our main result is described as follows.

Theorem A

(cf. Theorem
4.11
)
.

Let
\ell
be a positive integer with
\frac{p+2}{4}>\ell>3
.
Then,
the stack
\mathcal{O}p_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},g,r}^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}

is étale over the points of

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,r}
classifying totally degenerate curves.
In particular,

\mathcal{O}p_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},g,r}^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}
is generically étale over
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,r}
, i.e., any irreducible component that dominates
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,r}
admits a dense open substack which is étale over
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,r}
.

The above theorem
makes progress toward realizing some correspondences for
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell}
as displayed in the above picture.
In fact, by the resulting generic étaleness, we can apply the previous study of (a kind of) fusion rings associated to
the moduli space of dormant opers
(cf.
[
Wak3
, § 7]
).
Let

\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})
denote the set of
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-rational points in the GIT quotient of
\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell}
by the action of its adjoint group.
Each element
\rho\in\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})^{\times r}\left(=\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})\times\cdots\times\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})\right)
determines the closed and open substack

\mathcal{O}p^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,g,r}

of
\mathcal{O}p_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},g,r}^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}

classifying dormant
\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell}
-opers
of radii

\rho
(cf.
[
Wak3
, Definition 2.32]
).
Then,
\mathcal{O}p^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},g,r}
decomposes into the disjoint union
\coprod_{\rho\in\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})^{\times r}}\mathcal{O}p^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,g,r}
, which implies a decomposition of generic degrees
\mathrm{deg}(\Pi_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},g,r})=\sum_{\rho\in\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})^{\times r}}\mathrm{deg}(\Pi_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,g,r})

under the assumption in Theorem
A
.

As a corollary of Theorem
A
, we obtain an analogue of the Verlinde formula
computing the values
\mathrm{deg}(\Pi_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,g,r})
, as follows.

Theorem B

(cf. Theorem
4.12
)
.

Let
\ell
be a positive integer with
\frac{p+2}{4}>\ell>3
.
Denote by

\mathbb{Y}

the pseudo-fusion ring for dormant
\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell}
-opers with multiplication
\ast
(cf.
[
Wak3
, Definition 7.34]
).
Write
\mathfrak{S}
for the set of ring homomorphims
\rotatebox[origin={c}]{180.0}{$\mathbb{Y}$}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
and write
\mathrm{Cas}:=\sum_{\lambda\in\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})}\lambda\ast\lambda\left(\in\rotatebox[origin={c}]{180.0}{$\mathbb{Y}$}\right)
.
Then, for each
\rho:=(\rho_{i})_{i=1}^{r}\in\mathfrak{c}(\mathbb{F}_{p})^{\times r}
,
the following equality holds:

\displaystyle\mathrm{deg}(\Pi_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,g,r})=\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak{S}}\chi(\mathrm{Cas})^{g-1}\cdot\prod_{i=1}^{r}\chi(\rho_{i}).

(1.6)

In particular, if
r=0
(which implies

), then this equality reads

\displaystyle\mathrm{deg}(\Pi_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\emptyset,g,0})=\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak{S}}\chi(\mathrm{Cas})^{g-1}.

(1.7)

Finally, we remark that, just as in the case of
\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{l}_{2}
(cf.
[
Wak3
, § 7.8]
),
the details of

\mathcal{O}p^{{}^{\mathrm{Zzz...}}}_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,0,3}
’s would be necessary to
explicitly identify
\mathrm{deg}(\Pi_{\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{o}_{2\ell},\rho,g,r})
’s because these values characterize the ring-theoretic structure of

\mathbb{Y}

.
This is one of the issues to be addressed in future research on the enumerative geometry of dormant opers.
