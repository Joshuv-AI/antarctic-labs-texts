Geometric approach to i-quantum group of affine type D \mathrm{D}
Quanyong Chen, Zhaobing Fan

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the structures of Schur algebra and Lusztig algebra associated to partial flag varieties of affine type D \mathrm{D} . We show that there is a subalgebra of Lusztig algebra and the quantum groups arising from this subalgebras via stabilization procedures is a coideal subalgebra of quantum group of affine 𝔰 ​ 𝔩 \mathfrak{sl} type. We construct monomial and canonical bases of the idempotented quantum algebra and establish the positivity properties of the canonical basis with respect to multiplication and the bilinear pairing.



1.
Introduction

In 1990, Beilinson, Lusztig and MacPherson
[
BLM90
]
provided a geometric realization of quantum Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\rm fin}
as convolution algebras on pairs of partial flags over a finite field.
By using multiplication formulas in
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\rm fin}
with divided powers of Chevalley generators, they furhter realized the quantum group
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
in the projective limit of the quantum Schur algebras (as
d\rightarrow\infty
).
More importantly, an idempotented version of quantum group
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
is discovered inside the projective limit as well admitting a canonical basis.
The role of the canonical basis for the idempotented quantum group
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{q}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
is similar to that of Kazhdan-Lusztig
bases
[
KL79
]
for Iwahori-Hecke algebras.
Subsequently, the Schur-Jimbo duality, as a bridge
connecting the Iwahori-Hecke algebra of type
\mathrm{A}
and (idempotented) quantum group
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
, is realized
geometrically by considering the product variety of the complete flag variety and the
n
-step
partial flag variety of type
\mathrm{A}
in
[
GL92
]
.

There have been some generalizations of the BLM-type construction using the
n
-step (partial) flag varieties of affine type
\mathrm{A}
earlier on; see Ginzburg-Vasserot
[
GV93
]
and Lusztig
[
Lu99
,
Lu00
]
.
Some further developments could be found in
[
GRV93
,
Mc12
,
P09
,
VV99
]
.
Also, there is an affine version of Schur-Jimbo duality formed in
[
CP96
]
.
The affine quantum Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}
is by definition the convolution algebra of pairs of flags of affine type
\mathrm{A}
.
There is a natural homomorphism from the idempotented quantum affine
\mathfrak{sl}_{n}
to the affine quantum Schur algebra

\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{q}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_{n})\longrightarrow\mathbf{S}_{n,d},

which is no longer surjective.
The image of this map is denoted by Lusztig subalgebra
\mathbf{U}_{n,d}
and generated by the Chevalley generators.

An
(\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n}),\mathbf{H}_{\mathrm{C}})
-duality was discovered algebraically and categorically in
[
BW13
]
as a crucial ingredient for a new approach to Kazhdan-Lusztig theory of classical types.
Motivated by
[
BW13
]
, Bao, Kujawa, Li and Wang
[
BKLW14
,
BLW14
]
provided a geometric construction of Schur-type algebras
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\imath,\rm fin}
in terms of
n
-step flag varieties of type
\mathrm{B}_{d}
(or
\mathrm{C}_{d}
).
The
\imath
-Schur duality has been realized in
[
BKLW14
]
by using mixed pairs of
n
-step flags and complete flags of type
\mathrm{B}
or
\mathrm{C}
.
They further established multiplication formulas in the Schur algebras
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\imath,\rm fin}
with divided powers of Chevalley generators, which again enjoy some remarkable stabilization properties as
d\rightarrow\infty
.
They showed the quantum algebra arising from the stabilization procedure is a coideal subalgebra
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
of
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
.
The pair
(\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n}),\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n}))
forms a quantum symmetric pair.

The theory of quantum symmetric pairs was systematically developed by Letzter
[
Le99
,
Le02
]
and Kolb
[
Ko14
]
.
Let
\imath
be an algebra involution on the symmetrizable Kac-Moody algebra
\mathfrak{g}
of the second kind
[
Ko14
, §2]
, and
\mathfrak{g}^{\imath}
be the subalgebra of
\imath
-invariants in
\mathfrak{g}
.
For simple Lie algebras
\mathfrak{g}
of finite type, the classification of
\mathfrak{g}^{\imath}
corresponds to the classification of real simple Lie algebras
[
OV90
]
.
The quantum analogue
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{g})
of the enveloping algebra
\mathbf{U}(\mathfrak{g}^{\imath})
is a coideal subalgebra of the quantized enveloping algebra
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{g})
in the sense that the comultiplication
\Delta
on
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{g})
satisfies

\Delta:\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{g})\longrightarrow\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{g})\otimes\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{g}).

The algebra
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{g})
specializes to
\mathbf{U}(\mathfrak{g}^{\imath})
at
q=1
and the pair
(\mathbf{U}_{q}^{\imath}(\mathfrak{g}),\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{g}))
is called a quantum symmetric pair.

The affine Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{c}}
is by the definition the convolution algebra of pairs of flags of affine type
\mathrm{C}

[
FLLLW20
]
.
It admits a canonical basis which enjoys a positivity with respect to multiplication. Denote a subalgebra
\mathbf{U}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{c}}
of
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{c}}
generated by the Chevalley generators.
They introduce a comultiplication homomorphism and transfer map on Schur algebras such that comultiplication homomorphism
[
FLLLW20
, §5]
and transfer map
[
FLLLW20
, §6]
make sense on the level of Schur algebras instead of Lusztig algebras. The algebra
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{c}}
is by definition a suitable subalgebras of the projective limit of the projective system of Lusztig algebras and the comultiplication homomorphisms gives rise to show that
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{c}}
is a coideal subalgebra of
\mathbf{U}_{q}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_{n})
.
The idempotented form of
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{c}}
, denoted by
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{c}}
can be formulated analogous to the idempotented quantum groups
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{n}
in
[
BLM90
,
Lu93
]
.
The canonical basis of
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{c}}
be constructed and its positivity with respect to the multiplication and a bilinear pairing of geometric origin also be established in
[
FLLLW20
]
.

Fan, Li
[
FL14
]
established a new duality between the Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}^{m}
and the Iwahori-Hecke algebra
\mathbf{H}_{\mathrm{D}}
of type
\mathrm{D}
attached to the special orthogonal group
{\rm SO}_{\mathbb{F}}(2d)
algebraically and geometrically.
The algebra
\mathbf{S}^{m}
contains the subalgebra
\widetilde{\mathbf{S}}^{m}
and two additional idempotents, where
\widetilde{\mathbf{S}}^{m}
isomorphic to
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\imath,\rm fin}
of type
\mathrm{C}
.
They provided a geometric construction of the Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}^{m}
in terms of
n
-step flag varieties of type
\mathrm{D}
.
The Schur duality has been realized by using mixed pairs of
n
-step flags and complete flags of type
\mathrm{D}
.
The quantum algebra
\mathbf{U}^{m}
and its idempotented form (canonical basis) are obtained by stabilization and completion process following
[
BLM90
,
BKLW14
]
.

To this end, it is natural to ask what happens to the case of the affine type
\mathrm{D}
.
The purpose of this paper is to provide an answer to this question, as a sequel to
[
FL14
,
FLLLW20
]
.
There is a lattice presentation of the complete and
n
-step flag variety of affine type
\mathrm{D}
in
[
CFW24
]
,
on which the special orthogonal group
{\rm SO}_{F}(V)
(where
F=k((\varepsilon))
) acts on the complete flags and the
n
-step partial flag variety
\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
, which is formulated in this paper, for
n
even.

We parameterize the orbits for the product
\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\times\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
under the diagonal action of the group
{\rm SO}_{F}(V)
by the set
\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}
of signed matrices.
Similarly, denote by
\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}^{ap}
the subset of aperiodic signed matrices in
\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}
.
The Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}

is by the definition the convolution algebra of pairs of the flags in
\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
.
We further establish the Schur duality between the (extended) Hecke algebra
\mathbf{H}_{\widetilde{\mathrm{D}}}
and the Schur algebra
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
algebraically and geometrically.
The algebra
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
admits a standard basis
\{[\mathfrak{a}]\mid\mathfrak{a}\in\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}\}
and a canonical basis
\{\{\mathfrak{a}\}_{d}\mid\mathfrak{a}\in\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}\}
, which enjoys a positivity with respect to multiplication.
We formulate a subalgebra
\mathbf{U}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
of
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
generated by the Chevalley generators.
Our first main result is the following:

Theorem A

(Theorem
3.15
)
.

The algebra
\mathbf{U}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
admits a monomial basis
\{\zeta_{\mathfrak{a}}\mid\mathfrak{a}\in\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}^{ap}\}
.
Similarly, it possesses a canonical basis
\{\{\mathfrak{a}\}_{d}\mid\mathfrak{a}\in\Xi_{\mathfrak{d}}^{ap}\}
, which is compatible with the corresponding bases in
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
under the inclusion
\mathbf{U}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\subset\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
.

Generalizing the constructions in affine type
\mathrm{A},\mathrm{C}
and finite type
\mathrm{D}

[
FL15
,
FLLLW20
,
Lu00
]
, we introduce a subalgebra
\widetilde{\mathbf{S}}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
of
\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
and the comultiplication-like homomorphisms
\widetilde{\Delta}^{\mathfrak{d}}=\widetilde{\Delta}_{d^{\prime},d^{\prime\prime}}^{\mathfrak{d}}:\widetilde{\mathbf{S}}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\rightarrow\widetilde{\mathbf{S}}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\otimes\mathbf{S}_{n,d^{\prime\prime}}
for any composition
d=d^{\prime}+d^{\prime\prime}
.
These further lead to the transfer maps
\phi_{d,d-n}^{\mathfrak{d}}:\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\rightarrow\mathbf{S}_{n,d-n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
, which are shown to preserve the Chevalley generators.
The homomorphisms
\phi_{d,d-n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
make sense on the level of Schur algebras instead of Lusztig algebras.

The algebras
\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
and
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
are by definition the suitable subalgebras of projective limit of the projective system
\{(\mathbf{U}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}},\phi_{d,d-n}^{\mathfrak{d}})\}_{d\geq 1}
, just as
\mathbf{U}_{n}
is a limit algebra for a similar affine type
\mathrm{A}
projective system.
We showed that the family of homomorphisms
\{\widetilde{\Delta}_{d^{\prime},d^{\prime\prime}}^{\mathfrak{d}}\}
gives rise to a homomorphism
\widetilde{\Delta}^{\mathfrak{d}}:\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}\rightarrow\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}\otimes\mathbf{U}_{n}
and an injective homomorphism
\jmath_{n}:\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}\rightarrow\mathbf{U}_{n}
, whose images on the Chevalley generators are explicitly given.

Theorem B

(Proposition
4.14
, Proposition
4.15
)
.

There exists a decomposition of
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
such that
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}=\mathbf{J}_{+}\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}\oplus\mathbf{J}_{-}\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
, where
\mathbf{J}_{+},\mathbf{J}_{-}
are two idempotents, and the algebra
\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
is a coideal subalgebra of
\mathbf{U}_{n}
.
The pair
(\mathbf{U}_{n},\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}})
forms a quantum symmetric pair of affine type in the sense of Lezter and Kolb
[
Ko14
]
.

We formulate idempotented forms of
\mathbf{U}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
, denoted by
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
, which is analogous to the idempotented quantum groups as formulated in
[
FL14
,
FLLLW20
]
.
Following the approach of
[
Mc12
]
in the affine type
\mathrm{A}
setting and
[
FL15
]
in the finite type
\mathrm{D}
setting, we construct canonical basis for
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
and establish its positivity with respect to the multiplication and a bilinear pairing of geometric origin.

Theorem C

(Theorem
4.11
,Theorem
4.13
)
.

The algebra
\dot{\mathbf{U}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
admits a canonical basis
\dot{\mathbf{B}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
. The structure constants of the canonical basis
\dot{\mathbf{B}}_{n}^{\mathfrak{d}}
lie in
\mathbb{N}[v,v^{-1}]
with respect to the multiplication and in

with respect to the bilinear pairing.

(2.1)

J=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_{d}\\

A free

We set

\mathcal{L}^{\sharp}=\{v\in V\mid Q(v,\mathcal{L})\subset\mathfrak{o}\},\ \ \mathcal{L}^{\ast}=\{v\in V\mid Q(v,\mathcal{L})\subset\mathfrak{m}\}.

Let

Denote by

Fix a pair

.
We consider the sets
\mathcal{Y}_{d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
and
\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}
of all collections of lattices in

\displaystyle\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}=

\displaystyle\{L=(L_{i})_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}\mid L_{i}\subset L_{i+1},L_{i}=\varepsilon L_{i+n},L_{i}^{\ast}=L_{n-i},\ \forall i\in\mathbb{Z}\};

\displaystyle\mathcal{Y}_{d}^{\mathfrak{d}}=

Denote by

\displaystyle\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d},1}

\displaystyle=\left\{L=(L_{i})_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}\in\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\mid|\mathcal{M}/L_{r}\cap\mathcal{M}|+|\mathcal{L}/L_{n}\cap\mathcal{L}|={\rm even}\right\};

\displaystyle\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d},2}

\displaystyle=\left\{L=(L_{i})_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}\in\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\mid|\mathcal{M}/L_{r}\cap\mathcal{M}|+|\mathcal{L}/L_{n}\cap\mathcal{L}|={\rm odd}\right\}.

Then

can be decomposed into

We set

(2.2)

\displaystyle\Lambda_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}=\bigg\{\lambda=(\lambda_{i})_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}\in\mathbb{N}^{\mathbb{Z}}\ \Big|\ \lambda_{i}=\lambda_{1-i}=\lambda_{i+n},\forall i\in\mathbb{Z};\sum_{1\leq i\leq n}\lambda_{i}=D\bigg\}.

admits the following decomposition.

\displaystyle\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}=\bigsqcup_{\mathbf{a}=(a_{i})\in\Lambda_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}}\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}(\mathbf{a}),\ \ \text{where}\ \mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}(\mathbf{a})=\left\{L\in\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\mid|L_{i}/L_{i-1}|=a_{i},\forall i\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}.

2.2.
The double centralizer

Let

\mathbf{S}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}

Similarly, we define the free
\mathcal{A}
-modules

\displaystyle\mathbf{V}_{n,d;\mathcal{A}}^{\mathfrak{d}}=\mathcal{A}_{{\rm SO}_{F}(V)}(\mathcal{X}_{n,d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\times\mathcal{Y}_{d}^{\mathfrak{d}})\ \ \ {\rm and}\ \ \ \mathbf{H}_{d;\mathcal{A}}^{\mathfrak{d}}=\mathcal{A}_{{\rm SO}_{F}(V)}(\mathcal{Y}_{d}^{\mathfrak{d}}\times\mathcal{Y}_{d}^{\mathfrak{d}}).
