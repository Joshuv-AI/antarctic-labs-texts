Theta functions and adiabatic curvature on an Abelian variety
Ching-Hao Chang, Jih-Hsin Cheng, I-Hsun Tsai

Abstract
. For an ample line bundle L L on an Abelian variety M M , we study the theta functions associated with the family of line bundles L ⊗ T L\otimes T on M M indexed by T ∈ Pic 0 ​ ( M ) T\in\text{Pic}^{0}(M) . Combined with an appropriate differential geometric setting, this leads to an explicit curvature computation of the direct image bundle E E on Pic 0 ​ ( M ) \text{Pic}^{0}(M) , whose fiber E T E_{T} is the vector space spanned by the theta functions for the line bundle L ⊗ T L\otimes T on M M . Some algebro-geometric properties of E E are also remarked.

Finally let us make the following remarks. The use
of theta functions is instrumental for both proofs of Theorem 1.1 and 1.2.
By contrast, a purely algebraic proof of Theorem 1.1 over any algebraically
closed field (not necessarily of characteristic zero) can be found in
[
10

basically conceptual ones. It seems natural to ask for a more down-to-earth
point of view; such a possibility is hopefully provided in this paper.
Another merit of this paper lies perhaps in our ongoing work, which is to
study certain “spectral bundles” naturally arising from the
above differential geometric setting. These spectral bundles include the
above bundle

expected to be also relevant to analogous problems in the
