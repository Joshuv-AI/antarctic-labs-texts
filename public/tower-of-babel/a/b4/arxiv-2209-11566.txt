Let

has only finitely many indecomposable maximal Cohen-Macaulay
A
-modules upto isomorphism.
To study
(not necessarily commutative) Artin algebra’s
Auslander and Reiten introduced the theory of almost-split sequences.
These are now called AR-sequences.
Later Auslander and Reiten extended the theory of AR-sequences to the case of commutative Henselian isolated singularities.
Good references for this topic are
[
9
]
and
[

]
. Let

denote the full subcategory of maximal Cohen-Macaulay (= MCM)

-modules.

.

Note we can define Grothendieck group of any extension closed subcategory

of

. By
[

, 13.2]
the natural map

and by abuse of notation denote it by

.

Let
A
be a Henselian Cohen-Macaulay local of finite representation type. Set

A
-modules upto isomorphism.
If

. If
