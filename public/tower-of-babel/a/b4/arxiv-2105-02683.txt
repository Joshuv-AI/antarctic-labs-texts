Exploring the new Tsallis agegraphic dark energy with interaction through statefinder


Abstract
In this work, we explore the recently proposed new Tsallis agegraphic dark energy model in a flat FLRW Universe by taking the conformal time as IR cutoff with interaction. The deceleration parameter of the interacting new Tsallis agegraphic dark energy model provides the phase transition of the Universe from decelerated to accelerated phase. The EoS parameter of the model shows a rich behaviour as it can be quintessence-like or phantom-like depending on the interaction ( b 2 b^{2} ) and parameter B B . The evolutionary trajectories of the statefinder parameters and ( ω D , ω D ′ ) (\omega_{D},\omega_{D}^{{}^{\prime}}) planes are plotted by considering the initial condition Ω D 0 = 0.73 \Omega_{D}^{0}=0.73 , H 0 = 67 H_{0}=67 according to Λ \Lambda CDM observational Planck 2018 data for different b 2 b^{2} and B B . The model shows both quintessence and Chaplygin gas behaviour in the statefinder ( r , s ) (r,s) and ( r , q ) (r,q) pair planes for different b 2 b^{2} and B B .

Brief review of the interacting NTADE model

The first Friedmann equation of a flat FLRW is written as

\displaystyle H^{2}=\frac{1}{3m_{p}^{2}}{(\rho_{m}+\rho_{D})},

(3)

where
\rho_{D}
is INTADE energy density and
\rho_{m}
is pressureless matter density.
The fractional energy densities is simply instigate as
\Omega_{n}=\frac{\rho_{n}}{3m_{p}^{2}H^{2}}
for
n=~m~
and
~D.
Utilizing this definition, the energy density parameter for matter and INTADE are
\Omega_{m}=\frac{\rho_{m}}{3m_{p}^{2}H^{2}}
and
\Omega_{D}=\frac{\rho_{D}}{3m_{p}^{2}H^{2}}
respectively.
By putting the energy density parameter for matter and INTADE in Eq. (
2
), we will find
\Omega_{m}=1-\Omega_{D}
and the energy densities ratio
\frac{\Omega_{m}}{\Omega_{D}}=r=\frac{1}{\Omega_{{D}}}-1
.

The conformal time is defined as
dt=ad\eta
leading
\dot{\eta}=\frac{1}{a}
and thus

\displaystyle T=\int_{0}^{a}\frac{da}{Ha^{2}},

(4)

\displaystyle\rho_{D}=B\eta^{2\delta-4}.

(5)

Where
\eta=\Big({\frac{3{H}^{2}\Omega_{{D}}}{B}}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2\delta-4}}

\displaystyle{\dot{\rho}_{D}}=\frac{B(2\delta-4)\eta^{2\delta-5}}{{a}}

(6)

Using the conservation equations given in Eq. (
1
) and Eq. (
2
), consolidating the derivative of Eq. (
3
) with time and using Eq. (
5
) and Eq. (
6
), we get :

\displaystyle\frac{\dot{H}}{H^{2}}=-\frac{3}{2}(1-\Omega_{D})+\frac{(\delta-2)\Omega_{D}}{a\eta H}+\frac{3}{2b^{2}}.

(7)

By Eq. (
7
), the deceleration parameter is

\displaystyle q=-1-\frac{\dot{H}}{H^{2}}=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{3}{2}\Omega_{{D}}-{\frac{(\delta-2)\Omega_{{D}}}{a\eta H}}-\frac{3}{2b^{2}}.

(8)

Considering
Q=3b^{2}H(\rho_{D}+\rho_{m})
the mutual interaction between the dark sectors of Universe
[
22
]
, substituting Eq. (
5
) and Eq. (
6
) into Eq. (
1
) we find the EoS parameter as:

\displaystyle\omega_{D}=-1-{\frac{2\delta-4}{3a\eta H}}-\frac{b^{2}}{\Omega_{D}}.

(9)

Using Eq. (
5
) and Eq. (
7
),we get the derivatives of the energy density parameter

\displaystyle\Omega_{D}^{{}^{\prime}}=\frac{(2\delta-4)\Omega_{{D}}}{a\eta H}+2\Omega_{{D}} (1+q).

(10)

Where, prime and dot gives the derivative with respect to
\log a
and time.

The scenario of interacting new Tsallis agegraphic dark energy leads
