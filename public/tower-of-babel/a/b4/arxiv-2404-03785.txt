The Galois group of a Special Group
Kaique Matias de Andrade Roberto Hugo L. Mariano

Abstract
In this ongoing work, we extend to a class of well-behaved pre-special hyperfields the work of J. Mináč and Spira ( [ 14 ] ) that describes a (pro-2)-group of a field extension that encodes the quadratic form theory of a given field F F : in [ 1 ] it is shown that its associated cohomology ring contains a copy of the cohomology ring of the field F F . Our construction, a contravariant functor into the category of "pointed" pro-2-groups, is essentially given by generators and relations of profinite-2-groups. We prove that such profinite groups Gal ​ ( F ) \mbox{Gal}(F) encode the space of orders of the special group canonically associated to the hyperfield F F and provide a criterion to detect when F F is formally real or not.

Consider a field

\displaystyle z^{-1}(t^{4}[g^{2},h])z

\displaystyle=z^{-1}t^{4}[g^{2},h]z=(z^{-1}t^{4}z)(z^{-1}[g^{2},h]z)

Proof.

Let

Proposition 4.1
.

With the above notation, we have the following.

and that extends to a natural bijection

-modules

Proof.

i -

The desired bijection follows by the bijection

\displaystyle\{M\subseteq\mathcal{W}(I)/\mathcal{V}:M\mbox{ is a maximal open subgroup}\}\cong

\displaystyle\{M\subseteq\mathcal{W}(I):M\mbox{ is a maximal open subgroup}\}.

given by the following rule: lets

This function
\overline{q}

is the intersection of all open normal subgroups we have (via

\mathcal{G}/\phi(\mathcal{G})\cong(\mathcal{W}(I)/\mathcal{V})/(\Phi(I)/\mathcal{V})\cong\mathcal{W}(I)/\Phi(I).

ii -

For
\{i_{0},...,i_{n}\}\subseteq I

\zeta_{I}(i_{0},...,i_{n}):=\{\sigma\in\mathcal{W}(I):\gamma_{i_{0}}(\sigma)+...+\gamma_{i_{n}}(\sigma)=0\}.

We have that
\zeta_{I}(i_{0},...,i_{n})
is a subgroup of
\mathcal{W}(I)
. Now let
\tau,\theta\in\mathcal{W}(I)\setminus\zeta_{I}(i_{0},...,i_{n})
. Then for all

Therefore

\sum^{n}_{p=1}\gamma_{i_{p}}(\theta^{-1}\tau)=\sum^{n}_{p=1}[\gamma_{i_{p}}(\theta)+\gamma_{i_{p}}(\tau)]=\sum^{n}_{p=1}\gamma_{i_{p}}(\theta)+\sum^{n}_{p=1}\gamma_{i_{p}}(\tau)=1+1=0.

Then

which imply

\mathcal{W}(I)/\zeta_{I}(i_{0},...,i_{n})=\{\overline{1},\overline{\tau}\}\cong\mathbb{Z}_{2}.

To verify that

\gamma_{i_{0}}(\sigma)+...+\gamma_{i_{n}}(\sigma)=0\mbox{ iff }\begin{cases}\gamma_{i_{0}}(\sigma)+...+\gamma_{i_{n-1}}(\sigma)=0\mbox{ and }\gamma_{i_{n}}(\sigma)=0\mbox{ or }\\
\gamma_{i_{0}}(\sigma)+...+\gamma_{i_{n-1}}(\sigma)=1\mbox{ and }\gamma_{i_{n}}(\sigma)=1.\end{cases}

In other words,

given by the rule

For surjectivity, let

Final remarks and future works

We list below the next steps towards the development of a Galois theory for the subclass of all standard pre-special hyperfields (or, equivalently, standard pre-special groups), that contains the class of all special hyperfields (or special groups).

1.

Analyse closure of the class of standard pSG under constructions like quotients, (finite) products, directed colimits, and extensions. Moreover, try to provide descriptions of the Galois under some of the main constructions of special groups.

2.

Develop a theory of quadratic extensions for some subcategory of the category of pre-special hyperfields, envisaging to obtain, for each standard pre-special hyperfield

Describe a category of "pointed" pro-2-groups
