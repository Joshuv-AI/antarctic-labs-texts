The problem of computing a 2 2 -T-connected spanning subgraph with minimum number of edges in directed graphs
Raed Jaberi, Reham Mansour

Abstract
Let G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) be a strongly connected graph with | V | ≥ 3 |V|\geq 3 . For T ⊆ V T\subseteq V , the strongly connected graph G G is 2 2 -T-connected if G G is 2 2 -edge-connected and for each vertex w w in T T , w w is not a strong articulation point. This concept generalizes the concept of 2 2 -vertex connectivity when T T contains all the vertices in G G . This concept also generalizes the concept of 2 2 -edge connectivity when | T | = 0 |T|=0 . The concept of 2 2 -T-connectivity was introduced by Durand de Gevigney and Szigeti in 2018 2018 . In this paper, we prove that there is a polynomial-time 4-approximation algorithm for the following problem: given a 2 2 -T-connected graph G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) , identify a subset E 2 ​ T ⊆ E E^{2T}\subseteq E of minimum cardinality such that ( V , E 2 ​ T ) (V,E^{2T}) is 2 2 -T-connected.
