On Barker-Larman Conjecture relative to a convex body with centrally symmetric sections
E. Morales-Amaya

Abstract
Let K ⊂ ℝ n K\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} be a convex body, n ≥ 3 n\geq 3 . We say that K K satisfies the Barker-Larman condition if there exists a ball B ⊂ int ​ K B\subset\text{int}K such that for every supporting hyperplane Π \Pi of B B , the section Π ∩ K \Pi\cap K is a centrally symmetric set. On the other hand, we say that K K satisfies the Montejano condition if there exists a ball B ⊂ int ​ K B\subset\text{int}K such that for every supporting hyperplane Π \Pi of B B , the section Π ∩ K \Pi\cap K is a body of constant width. In this work we prove the following results where, in both cases, K K is an O O -symmetric convex body and B ⊂ int ​ K B\subset\text{int}K is a ball such that O ∉ B O\notin B : 1) If K K is strictly convex body and satisfies the Barker-Larman condition with respect to B B , then K K is an ellipsoid; 2) If K K satisfies the Montejano condition relative to B B , then K K is a ball.

Introduction

Let

Theorem O.
Let

-dimensional sections of

that divide the volume of

is an ellipsoid.

The following is a similar theorem, due to C. A. Rogers
[
8
]

Theorem R.
Let

be a convex body,
n\geq 3
, and

2
-dimensional sections of

Rogers also conjectured that if

was not the centre of

then it is an ellipsoid. This conjecture was first proved by P. W. Aitchison, C. M. Petty, and C. A. Rogers
[
2
]
, when

, and, the general case, by D. G. Larman
[
4
]
. Later a simpler proof was given by L. Montejano and E. Morales-Amaya in
[
6
]
. Such Theorem is known as the False Center Theorem.

In this note we prove, on the one hand, a result in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
which is a variant of the False Center Theorem when, instead of considering concurrent planes, we consider planes tangent to a given sphere. It can be considered as a progress about a problem due to J. A. Barker and D. Larman
[
3
]
. On the other hand, we prove a result in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
which is a variant of a Theorem of L. Montejano which is a characterization of a ball in terms of concurrent section of constant width
[
5
]

In order to present our results we need the following definitions. Let

Theorem 1

Let

,

Theorem 2
.

Let

,

, be a convex body
O
-symmetric and let
