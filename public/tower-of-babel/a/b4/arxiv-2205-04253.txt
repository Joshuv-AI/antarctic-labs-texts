Physics implication from a Z 3 Z_{3} symmetry of matter
Phung Van Dong

Abstract
I show that breaking B − L B-L by one unit of this charge is suitable for neutrino mass generation through an inverse seesaw mechanism, stabilizing a dark matter candidate without supersymmetry, as well as solving the muon anomalous magnetic moment and the W W mass deviation via dark field contributions. The new physics is governed by the residual Z 3 Z_{3} symmetry of B − L B-L isomorphic to the center of the color group, instead of the well-studied matter parity.
