Strong zero-divisor graph of p.q.-Baer ∗ * -rings
Anil Khairnar, Nana Kumbhar, B. N. Waphare

Abstract




Abstract:
In this paper, we study the strong zero-divisor graph of a p.q.-Baer
*
-ring. We determine the condition on a p.q.-Baer
*
-ring (in terms of the smallest central projection in a lattice of central projections of a
*
-ring), so that its strong zero-divisor graph contains a cut vertex. It is proved that the set of cut vertices of a strong zero-divisor graph of a p.q.-Baer
*
-ring forms a complete subgraph. We prove that the complement of the strong zero-divisor graph of a p.q.-Baer
*
-ring is connected if and only if the
*
-ring contains at least six central projections. We characterize the diameter and girth of the complement of a strong zero-divisor graph of a p.q.-Baer
*
-ring. Also, we characterize p.q.-Baer
*
-rings whose strong zero-divisor graph is complemented.

Keywords:

*
-ring, p.q.-Baer
*
-ring, central projections, zero-divisor graph, complement of the graph.

Mathematics Subject Classification:
16W10, 05C25, 13A70, 05C15.

case ii:

Lemma 4.13
.

Let

-ring such that

Proof.

R
contains at least
6
central projections. Let
a,b
are adjacent in
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
.
If

then
C(a)C(b)\neq 0
this gives
C(a)
and
C(b)
adjacent in
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
.
also
aC(a)C(b)=aC(b)\neq 0
and
bC(a)C(b)=bC(a)\neq 0
i.e.
C(a)C(b)
is adjacent to both
a
and
b
in
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
.
If

, then choose central projection

.
If
ef\neq 0
then
e
and
f
are adjacent in
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}

i.e.

and
C(b)f\neq 0

. Hence

and

in

. If

and
f

Theorem 4.14
.

Let
R
be a p.q.-Baer
*
-ring and there is an element
a\in V(\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R))
be such that
a\neq C(a)
. Then
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
is complemented if and only if
R=\mathbb{Z}_{3}\oplus\mathbb{Z}_{3}.

Proof.

If
R=\mathbb{Z}_{3}\oplus\mathbb{Z}_{3}
, then
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)
is a
4
-cycle whose graph complement is complemented. Conversely, suppose
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
is complemented. Since
a\in V(\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R))
be such that
a\neq C(a)
, therefore
a
is not adjacent ot
C(a)
. Hence
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)
is not complete. By Lemma
4.13
,
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
is disconnected. Therefore,
R
contains exactly four central projections. Let
CP(R)=\{0,1,e,1-e\}
,
C_{e}=\{x\in R~|~C(x)=e\}
and
C_{1-e}=\{x\in R~|~C(x)=1-e\}
. If
|C_{e}|>2
, then any three elements in
C_{e}
are connected in
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)^{c}
, hence no element of
C_{e}
has complement, a contradiction. Therefore
|C_{e}|\leq 2
. Similarly
|C_{1-e}|\leq 2
. If
|C_{e}|=|C_{1-e}|=1
, then
\Gamma^{*}_{s}(R)
is a complete graph on two vertices, a contradiction to the assumption. Therefore
|C_{e}|=|C_{1-e}|=2
. As
e
is a central projection,
R=eR\oplus(1-e)R
. Where
|eR|=|(1-e)R|=3
(as
C_{e}=eR\backslash\{0\}
and
C_{1-e}=(1-e)R\backslash\{0\}
). Therefore
R=\mathbb{Z}_{3}\oplus\mathbb{Z}_{3}
.
∎
