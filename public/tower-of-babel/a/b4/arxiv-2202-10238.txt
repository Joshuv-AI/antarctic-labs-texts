Virtual cycles on projective completions and quantum Lefschetz formula
Jeongseok Oh

Abstract
. For a compact quasi-smooth derived scheme M M with ( − 1 ) (-1) -shifted cotangent bundle N N , there are at least two ways to localise the virtual cycle of N N to M M via torus and cosection localisations, introduced by Jiang-Thomas [ JT ] . We produce virtual cycles on both the projective completion N ¯ := ℙ ⁡ ( N ⊕ 𝒪 M ) \overline{N}:={\mathbb{P}}(N\oplus{\mathcal{O}}_{M}) and projectivisation ℙ ⁡ ( N ) {\mathbb{P}}(N) and show the ones on N ¯ \overline{N} push down to Jiang-Thomas cycles and the one on ℙ ⁡ ( N ) {\mathbb{P}}(N) computes the difference. Using similar ideas we give an expression for the difference of the quintic and t t -twisted quintic GW invariants of Guo-Janda-Ruan [ GJR ] .

Appendix A
Five localisations

We review the five localised invariants introduced in
[
JT
]
for a quasi-projective shceme
N
with the symmetric obstruction theory
{\mathbb{E}}_{N}\to\mathbb{L}_{N}
, acted on by a torus
\mathsf{T}:=\mathbb{C}^{*}
with a compact fixed locus
N^{\mathsf{T}}
.
We assume the POT is equivariant, but the symmetricity need not be preserved by
\mathsf{T}
.

The virtual signed Euler characteristic of Ciocan-Fontanine–Kapranov and Fantechi–Göttsche

The virtual Euler characteristic of
N^{\mathsf{T}}
is defined in
[
CK
,
FG
]
by,

\int_{\,[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}}c\,\big(\,{\mathbb{E}}_{N}^{\vee}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}^{\mathrm{fix}}\big)\ \in\ \mathbb{Q}.

The terminology
virtual Euler characteristic
comes from regarding
{\mathbb{E}}_{N}^{\vee}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}^{\mathrm{fix}}
to be the virtual tangent bundle of
N^{\mathsf{T}}
.
In
[
JT
]
, using the virtual
cotangent
bundle
{\mathbb{E}}_{N}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}^{\mathrm{fix}}
, the
virtual signed Euler characteristic

e_{1^{\prime}}\ :=\ \int_{\,[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}}c\,\big(\,{\mathbb{E}}_{N}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}^{\mathrm{fix}}\big)\ \in\ \mathbb{Q}

is considered.

Graber–Pandharipande torus localisation

The virtual cycle
[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}
has a lifting in the equivariant Chow group
A^{\mathsf{T}}_{0}\big(N\big)
. Then one can localise
[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}
by virtual torus localisation
[
GP
]
,

[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}\ =\ \iota_{*}\left(\frac{[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}}{e_{\mathsf{T}}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)}\right),\ \ \ \iota\ :\ N^{\mathsf{T}}\ \hookrightarrow\ N.

Here,
\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}
denotes the virtual normal bundle. The fixed (weight zero) part
{\mathbb{E}}_{N}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}^{\mathrm{fix}}
of the pullback complex
{\mathbb{E}}_{N}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}
is a perfect obstruction theory of
N^{\mathsf{T}}

[
GP
]
. Thus the virtual fundamental class
[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}
is defined. Using the class

\frac{[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}}{e_{\mathsf{T}}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)}\ \in\ A_{0}\big(N^{\mathsf{T}}\big)[t]

which is a polynomial in
t
by degree reason,
we define an invariant

e_{1}\ :=\ \deg\left(\left\{\frac{[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}}{e_{\mathsf{T}}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)}\right\}_{t=0}\,\right)\ \in\ \mathbb{Q}.

Kiem–Li cosection localisation

The pairing with the Euler vector field defines a cosection

\sigma\ :\ \Omega_{N}\ \longrightarrow\ {\mathcal{O}}_{N}

on the cotangent sheaf
\Omega_{N}
of
N
which is the obstruction sheaf
h^{1}\big({\mathbb{E}}_{N}^{\vee}\big)\cong h^{0}\big({\mathbb{E}}_{N}\big)\cong\Omega_{N}
. Then one can localise
[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}
via cosection localisation
[
KL
]

[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}\ =\ \iota_{*}\,[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}_{\sigma}.

It defines an invariant

e_{2}\ :=\ \deg\,[N]^{\operatorname{vir}}_{\sigma}\ \in\ \mathbb{Z}.

Behrend localisation

A weighted Euler characteristic weighted by the Behrend function
\nu_{N}

[
Be
, Definition 1.4]
gives rise to an invariant

e_{2^{\prime}}\ :=\ e\big(N^{\mathsf{T}},\nu_{N}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}\big)\ \in\ \mathbb{Z}.

The signed Euler characteristic

Considering
N^{\mathsf{T}}
as a topological space, it produces an invariant

e_{2^{\prime\prime}}\ :=\ (-1)^{{\mathrm{rank}\,}\big({\mathbb{E}}_{N}|_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}^{\mathrm{fix}}\big)}\cdot e\big(N^{\mathsf{T}}\big)\ \in\ \mathbb{Z}.

Known results

We list here some known results about the five localised invariants.

1.
Kiem-Li and Behrend localised invariants are the same,
e_{2}=e_{2^{\prime}}
, by
[
Ji
, Theorem 5.20]
.

2.
When the symmetricity of
{\mathbb{E}}_{N}
is preserved by
\mathsf{T}
, Graber-Pandharipande and Behrend localised invariants are the same,
e_{1}=e_{2^{\prime}}
. The following is a brief explanation. In this case
[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}
is of degree zero and

e_{\mathsf{T}}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)=(-1)^{{\mathrm{rank}\,}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)},\ \ \deg\,[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}=e\big(N^{\mathsf{T}},\nu_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}\big).

The latter comes from
[
Be
, Theorem 4.18]
. So we have

e_{1}\ =\ (-1)^{{\mathrm{rank}\,}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)}e\big(N^{\mathsf{T}},\nu_{N^{\mathsf{T}}}\big).

Then
e_{1}=e_{2^{\prime}}
comes from
[
LQ
, Theorem 2.4]
.

3.
Combining the above results, we have
e_{1}=e_{2}
when the symmetricity is preserved by
\mathsf{T}
. There is a stronger and more direct proof: by
[
CKL
, Theorem 3.5]
, we obtain an equivalence of cycles

(31)

\frac{[N^{\mathsf{T}}]^{\operatorname{vir}}}{e_{\mathsf{T}}\big(\mathsf{N}^{\operatorname{vir}}_{N^{\mathsf{T}}/N}\big)}\ =\ [N]^{\operatorname{vir}}_{\sigma}.

This induces
e_{1}=e_{2}
immediately.
Note that (
31
) does not require
N^{\mathsf{T}}
to be compact.

4.

For a
(-1)
-shifted cotangent bundle
N
of a compact quasi-smooth derived scheme,
e_{1}=e_{1^{\prime}}
and
e_{2}=e_{2^{\prime}}=e_{2^{\prime\prime}}
are proven in
[
JT
, Theorem 1.2]
. However
[
JT
, Examples 3.1 and 3.2]
show that
e_{1}
may not be equal to
e_{2}
in general.
