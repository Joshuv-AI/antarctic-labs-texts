The

Using the above stated correspondence between simplicial complexes and clutters, we set

We next rephrase the

A\in X
be a face, and define
U_{X}(A):=\{v\in V-A\colon A\in\operatorname{lk}(X;v)\}\cup A
. Observe that if
F\in X
is a facet, then
U_{X}(F)=F
.

Proposition 4
.

v(X)=\min\{|A|\colon A\in X\;\textnormal{and}\;U_{X}(A)\;\textnormal{is a facet of}\;X\}
for every simplicial complex
X
.

Proof.

Since
X=\operatorname{Ind}(\mathfrak{c}(X))
, a vertex
v\in V-A
is a neighbor of a face
A\in X
in
\mathfrak{c}(X)
if and only if
A\notin\operatorname{lk}(X;v)
. Moreover,
N_{\mathfrak{c}(X)}(A)
is a minimal vertex cover in
\mathfrak{c}(X)
if and only if
V-N_{\mathfrak{c}(X)}(A)
is a facet of
X
. However, the set

is clearly equal to
U_{X}(A)
.
∎

A face
A
of a simplicial complex

is called a

if there exists a unique facet containing it. We define

\lambda(X):=\min\{|A|\colon A\;\textrm{is a free face of}\;X\}.

Corollary 5

Proof.

Following Proposition

is the unique facet of

is a free face of

and

is the unique facet containing it, then

.
∎
