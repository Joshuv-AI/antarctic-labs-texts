Revisiting semileptonic B − → p ​ p ¯ ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} decays
C.Q. Geng 1,2,3 , Chia-Wei Liu 2 and Tien-Hsueh Tsai 3

Abstract
We systematically revisit the baryonic four-body semileptonic decays of B − → 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ′ ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} by the perturbative QCD counting rules with 𝐁 {\bf B} representing octet baryons and ℓ = e , μ \ell=e,\mu . We study the transition form factors of B − → 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ′ B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime} in the limit of ( p 𝐁 + p 𝐁 ¯ ′ ) 2 → ∞ (p_{\bf B}+p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{2}\to\infty with the three-body B ¯ → 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ′ ​ M \bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M and B − → p ​ p ¯ ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu} data along with S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) f SU(3)_{f} flavor symmetry. We calculate the decay branching ratios and angular asymmetries as well as the differential decay branching fractions of B − → p ​ p ¯ ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} . In particular, we find that our new result of ℬ ⁡ ( B − → p ​ p ¯ ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ ) = ( 5.21 ± 0.34 ) × 10 − 6 {\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell})=(5.21\pm 0.34)\times 10^{-6} , which is about one order of magnitude lower than the previous theoretical prediction of ( 10.4 ± 2.9 ) × 10 − 5 (10.4\pm 2.9)\times 10^{-5} , agrees well with both experimental measurements of ( 5.8 − 2.3 + 2.6 ) × 10 − 6 (5.8^{+2.6}_{-2.3})\times 10^{-6} and ( 5.3 ± 0.4 ) × 10 − 6 (5.3\pm 0.4)\times 10^{-6} by the Belle and LHCb Collaborations, respectively. We also evaluate the branching ratios and angular asymmetries in other channels of B − → 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} , which can be tested by the ongoing experiments at LHCb and BelleII.



I
introduction

In 2011, the baryonic four-body semileptonic decay of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
(
\ell=e
or
\mu
) was studied
with its decay branching ratio predicted to be
(10.4\pm 2.6\pm 1.2)\times 10^{-5}
in Ref.
Geng:2011tr
.
Both
e
and
\mu
modes were indeed measured by the Belle Collaboration
Tien:2013nga
in 2014 with

{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}e^{-}\bar{\nu}_{e})=(8.2^{+3.7}_{-3.2}\pm 0.6)\times 10^{-6}
and

{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu})=(3.1^{+3.1}_{-2.4}\pm 0.7)\times 10^{-6}

along with the combined value of
{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell})=(5.8^{+2.4}_{-2.1}\pm 0.9)\times 10^{-6}
,
which is about one oder of magnitude lower than the theoretical prediction.
Recently, the LHCb Collaboration has published the observation of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}

with its decay branching ratio determined to be
(5.27^{+0.23}_{-0.24}\pm 0.21\pm 0.15)\times 10^{-6}

Aaij:2019bdu
,
where the first and second uncertainties correspond to statistical and systematic, and the third one is from the branching fraction of the normalization channel,
respectively.
Both statistical and systematic uncertainties of the LHCb data
have significant improvements compared with the previous ones by Belle
Tien:2013nga
.

These decay modes are useful to determine the value of
|V_{ub}|
as the works in the other baryonic modes
Hsiao:2018zqd
as well as the underlying new CP/T violating effects. The main difficulty in both extracting
|V_{ub}|
from
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
and constraining the new CP/T violating effects is how to obtain their hadronic transition amplitude of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}

as it is hard to be calculated via the usual QCD methods, such as the
factorizations and sum rules, which have been widely used in the mesonic decays
of
B^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
(
B_{\ell 4}
)
Feldmann:2018kqr
;
Boer:2016iez
;
Cheng:2017smj
.
Nevertheless, these modes should be considered in the fit for the extraction of
V_{ub}
. Qualitatively speaking,
to reduce the theoretical values for the decay branching ratios of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
, a smaller value of
|V_{ub}|
is needed
besides the form factors. It is similar to the extractions from the exclusive
B
and
\Lambda_{b}
decays, but lower than that from the inclusive B decays.
Clearly, as a baryonic complementary version of
B_{\ell 4}
decays, both theoretical and experimental studies of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
may shed light on the baryonic transition amplitude of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}
, uncover the nature of the QCD dynamics,
and improve the measurement of
|V_{ub}|
.

Because of the rareness of four-body
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
decays with
{\bf B}^{(\prime)}
representing octet baryons,
people have concentrated on the three-body
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M
decays
to extract the baryonic transition from factors in the
{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}
transitions,
where
{\bf B}(\bar{\bf B}^{\prime})
and
M
are octet (anti-)baryons and pseudoscalar or vector mesons, respectively.
There have been several theoretical analyses on the baryonic three-body
B\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M
decays based on the factorization assumptions
Cheng:2001tr
;
Cheng:2001ub
;
Cheng:2002fp
;
Chua:2002yd
;
Chua:2002wn
;
Geng:2005fh
;
Geng:2005fh
;
Chen:2008sw
.
These baryonic
B
decays can be basically classified into current production
\cal C
, transition
\cal T
and hybrid
{\cal C}+{\cal T}

types
Chen:2008sw
, with the quark flow diagrams shown in Fig.

.

Among them, the transition one is the only channel directly related to the
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}
baryonic transition amplitudes. In Ref.
Geng:2011tr
, perturbative QCD counting rules combined with the available data of
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M
decays at the time
were used to fit the form factors and predict
the
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
decays.
Although the prediction of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
motivated its active search, it is clearly disproved by the experiments of
both Belle
Tien:2013nga
and LHCb
Aaij:2019bdu
.
The main reason for such a large prediction is that there were short of relevant data as well as lack of the understanding of the
underlined QCD dynamics for the baryonic transition of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}
.
In this work, we would like to
reanalyze the semi-leptonic decays of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}

with the same strategy as that in Ref.
Geng:2011tr
with the updated data. In addition, we
shall use the flavor symmetry to extend our results to other
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
decays.
This work is the first step to know the property of
{\bar{B}}\to{\bf B}{\bf B}^{\prime}
transition matrix elements. After getting a better understanding of these elements, we can use them for not only improving the measurement of
|V_{ub}|
but probing or constraining the new physics effects, such as the T violating triple momentum correlations due to the rich kinematic structure in the four-body decays of
{\bar{B}}\to{\bf B}{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell\bar{\nu}
.

This paper is organized as follows. In Sec. II, we present our formalism, which contains the effective Hamiltonians and generalized transition form factors.
In Sec. III, we show our numerical results of the form factors fitted by three-body
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M
processes and the latest
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}
result,
and present our predictions of the branching ratios and angular asymmetries in
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
.
We also compare our results of
p\bar{p}
invariant mass spectrum in the
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}
decay with the one measured by the LHCb. We give our conclusions in Sec. IV.

II
Formalism

The effective Hamiltonian for
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
at the quark level is given by

\displaystyle{\cal H}_{eff}=\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ub}\bar{u}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})b{\bar{\ell}}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})\nu_{\ell}\,,

(1)

where
G_{F}
is the Fermi constant and
V_{ub}
represents the element of the CKM matrix.
The transition amplitude of
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
can be easily factorized into hadronic and leptonic parts, written as

\displaystyle{\cal A}(\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell})=\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ub}\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{u}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})|\bar{B}\rangle\bar{\ell}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})\nu_{\ell}\,,

(2)

where
\bar{\ell}
and
\nu_{\ell}
are the usual Dirac spinors and
\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{u}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})|\bar{B}\rangle
is the unknown hadronic transition amplitude. The most general Lorentz invariant forms of the hadronic transitions for the vector and axial-vector currents
can be parametrized by
Geng:2011tr
;
Chen:2008sw

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{u}\gamma^{\mu}b|\bar{B}\rangle=i\bar{u}(p_{\bf B})\left[g_{1}\gamma^{\mu}+ig_{2}\sigma^{\mu\nu}p_{\nu}+g_{3}p^{\mu}+g_{4}(p_{\bf B}+p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{\mu}+g_{5}(p_{\bf B}-p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{\mu}\right]\gamma_{5}v(p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}}),

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{u}\gamma^{\mu}\gamma_{5}b|\bar{B}\rangle=i\bar{u}(p_{\bf B})\left[f_{1}\gamma^{\mu}+if_{2}\sigma^{\mu\nu}p_{\nu}+f_{3}p^{\mu}+f_{4}(p_{\bf B}+p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{\mu}+f_{5}(p_{\bf B}-p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{\mu}\right]v(p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}}),~~\,

(3)

respectively,
where
f_{i}
and
g_{i}
(
i=1,2,\cdots,5
) are the form factors and
p^{\mu}=(p_{B^{-}}-p_{\bf B}-p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{\mu}
.
Inspiring from the threshold effects
HS
, which have been observed in three-body
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M
decays
Aaij:2017vnw
;
Aubert:2006qx
;
Abe:2002tw
, and the pQCD counting rules
Brodsky:1980sx
;
Lepage:1980fj
;
Lepage:1979za
, the momentum dependences of
f_{i}
and
g_{i}
can be assumed to be

\displaystyle f_{i}=\frac{C_{f_{i}}}{t^{n}},\quad g_{i}=\frac{C_{g_{i}}}{t^{n}},

(4)

with
n=3
, where
C_{f_{i},g_{i}}
are constants determined by the branching ratios of the input channels.
Note that
n
relates to the number of hard-gluon propagators as shown in Fig.
2
. In the
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}
transition, two hard gluons produce the valance quarks in the
{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}
pair separately as well as one more hard gluon is needed to speed up the spectator quark in
\bar{B}

Chen:2008sw
. As a result, we can use
C_{f_{i}}
and
C_{g_{i}}
to describe the hadronic form factors in both transition type three-body and semileptonic four-body decays.

Diagram for the

\otimes

With the help of

C_{g_{i}}

C_{RR}

C_{LL}

C_{LR}

\displaystyle C_{f_{1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle m_{\bar{B}}\left(e_{LL}C_{LL}+e_{RR}C_{RR}\right)+\left(m_{\bf B}+m_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}}\right)e_{LR}C_{LR}

\displaystyle C_{g_{1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle m_{\bar{B}}\left(e_{LL}C_{LL}-e_{RR}C_{RR}\right)+\left(m_{\bf B}-m_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}}\right)e_{LR}C_{LR}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-C_{g_{2}}=e_{LR}C_{LR},\quad C_{f_{i}}=C_{g_{i}}=-e_{LR}C_{LR}\quad\text{for }i=3,4,5\,,

(5)

where

,

and

IV
Conclusions

We have systematically revisited the baryonic four-body semileptonic decays of
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
with
\ell=e,\mu
.
We have reduced the ten form factors in the hadronic transition of
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}
into three free parameters
in the heavy quark limit and
t\to\infty
.
We have performed the minimum
\chi^{2}
method to fit the three parameters and the effective color number of
N_{c}^{eff}
with
\chi^{2}/d.o.f=0.28

by using five three-body decays of
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}M
along with the
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}
measurement.
We have obtained a consistent fitting result of
{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell})=(5.21\pm 0.34)\times 10^{-6}
as well as other input channels.
Our
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
decay branching ratio is about one order of magnitude lower than the previous theoretical
prediction of
(10.4\pm 2.9)\times 10^{-5}
in Ref.
Geng:2011tr
, and
agrees well with the experimental data of
(5.8^{+2.6}_{-2.3})\times 10^{-6}
and

(5.3\pm 0.4)\times 10^{-6}
by Belle
Tien:2013nga
and LHCb
Aaij:2019bdu
, respectively.
In addition, our evaluation of the
m_{p\bar{p}}
invariant mass spectrum is also consistent with
that by the LHCb measurement
Aaij:2019bdu
,
demonstrating that the threshold effect and the
t^{-3}
dependence of the form factors from the QCD counting rules is still dominant in the
baryonic four-body semi-leptonic decays, while the other Lorentz invariant variables, such as
(p_{\bar{B}}+p_{{\bf B}})^{2}
, as well as the resonant states are highly suppressed.
Furthermore, we have plotted the differential branching fractions with respect to the kinematic variables
of
m_{\ell\bar{\nu}}
and
\cos\theta_{{\bf B},\ell}
in
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}

to provide the information in the lepton sector and angular distributions, respectively.
We have also used the flavor symmetry to explore the physical observables in other
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
decays.
In particular,
we have found that that the angular asymmetries of
B^{-}\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}
vanish
due to the absence of the chiral-flipping interaction (
e_{LR}=0
). On the theoretical side, the non observed transition modes of the
three-body
{\bar{B}}\to{\bf B}{\bf B}^{\prime}M
and four-body
\bar{B}\to{\bf B}{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell\bar{\nu}
decays can help us to relax
the assumption of the heavy quark limit once they are measured. Otherwise, the lattice simulation would be currently the most trustworthy theoretical method to reliably extract the hadronic form factors. On the experimental side, some of our results in
B^{-}\to{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}

can be tested by the ongoing experiments at Belle-II and LHCb.
Finally, we remark that the theoretical determination of the four-body decays of
{\bar{B}}\to{\bf B}{\bf B}^{\prime}\ell\bar{\nu}
would
provide a valuable opportunity to search for T violating effects from the triple momentum correlations and improve the measurement of
|V_{ub}|
as the works in exclusive
B
and
\Lambda_{b}
decays.

Starting with transition matrix elements of
\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|J^{\mu}_{V}-J^{\mu}_{A}|\bar{B}\rangle
with
J_{V(A)}^{\mu}=\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}(\gamma^{5})b
, we assume that the
\bar{B}
meson state can be approximately expressed by the field operator of free quarks
|\bar{B}\rangle\sim\bar{b}\gamma^{5}q^{\prime}|0\rangle
. Therefore, the matrix elements become

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|J^{\mu}_{V}-J^{\mu}_{A}|\bar{B}(\bar{q}^{\prime}b)\rangle

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma^{5})b\bar{b}\gamma^{5}q^{\prime}|0\rangle

(11)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma^{5})(\not{p}_{b}+m_{b})\gamma^{5}q^{\prime}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma^{5})(\not{p}_{b}-m_{b})q^{\prime}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{q}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}\not{p}_{b}q_{R}^{\prime}|0\rangle-2m_{b}\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\bar{q}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}q_{L}^{\prime}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|J^{\prime\mu}|0\rangle-\langle{\bf B}\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}|\tilde{J}^{\mu}|0\rangle\,,

where
J^{\prime\mu}=2\bar{q}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}\not{p}_{b}q_{R}^{\prime}
and
\tilde{J}^{\mu}=2m_{b}\bar{q}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}q_{L}^{\prime}

with
q_{L(R)}=(1\mp\gamma^{5})/2q
and
\bar{q}_{L(R)}=\bar{q}(1\pm\gamma^{5})/2
.
Note that, by inserting QCD (gluon-quark-antiquark) vertices in the corresponding diagrams, the Dirac structure in Eq. (11) could be altered. However, because of the asymptotic freedom in QCD, we can treat these alterations from QCD vertices as small perturbations, which are negligible in
the limit of
(p_{\bf B}+p_{{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{2}\to\infty
.
In terms of the crossing-symmetry (c.s.), the final state anti-baryon (
\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}
) is transformed as the initial baryon (
{\bf B}^{\prime}
) in the initial state with opposite four-momentum
\tilde{p}_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}=-p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}(p_{\bf B})\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}(p_{\bar{\bf B}^{\prime}})|J^{\prime\mu}(\tilde{J}^{\mu})|0\rangle\xrightarrow{c.s.}\langle{\bf B}(p_{\bf B})|J^{\prime\mu}(\tilde{J}^{\mu})|{\bf B}^{\prime}(\tilde{p}_{{\bf B}^{\prime}})\rangle.

(12)

According to Refs.
Chua:2002yd
;
Brodsky:1980sx
,
the amplitude can be parameterized as

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}|J^{\prime\mu}|{\bf B}^{\prime}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2i\bar{u}_{\bf B}\gamma^{\mu}\not{p}_{b}\left(\frac{1+\gamma^{5}}{2}F^{\prime+}+\frac{1-\gamma^{5}}{2}F^{\prime-}\right)u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2i\bar{u}_{\bf B}^{L}\gamma^{\mu}\not{p}_{b}u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}^{R}F^{\prime+}+2i\bar{u}_{\bf B}^{R}\gamma^{\mu}\not{p}_{b}u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}^{L}F^{\prime-}

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B}|\tilde{J}^{\mu}|{\bf B}^{\prime}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2m_{b}i\bar{u}_{\bf B}\gamma^{\mu}\left(\frac{1+\gamma^{5}}{2}\tilde{F}^{+}+\frac{1-\gamma^{5}}{2}\tilde{F}^{-}\right)u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}\,,

(13)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2m_{b}i\bar{u}_{\bf B}^{R}\gamma^{\mu}u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}^{R}\tilde{F}^{+}+2m_{b}i\bar{u}_{\bf B}^{L}\gamma^{\mu}u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}^{L}\tilde{F}^{-}\,.

In the asymptotic limit of
(p_{\bf B}-\tilde{p}_{{\bf B}^{\prime}})^{2}\to\infty
, the helicity of a particle can be approximately treated as its chirality, so that
the amplitudes with a specific chirality can be written as

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B},L|J^{\prime\mu}|{\bf B}^{\prime},R\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2i\bar{u}_{\bf B}^{L}\gamma^{\mu}\not{p}_{b}\left(e_{LR}F_{LR}\right)u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}^{R}

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B},R(L)|\tilde{J}^{\mu}|{\bf B}^{\prime},R(L)\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2m_{b}i\bar{u}_{\bf B}^{R(L)}\gamma^{\mu}\left(e_{RR(LL)}F_{RR(LL)}\right)u_{{\bf B}^{\prime}}^{R(L)}\,

(14)

where

\displaystyle e_{LR}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B},L|(a^{L}_{q})^{\dagger}a^{R}_{q^{\prime}}|{\bf B}^{\prime},R\rangle,

\displaystyle e_{RR}

\displaystyle\langle{\bf B},R|(a^{L}_{q})^{\dagger}a^{L}_{q^{\prime}}|{\bf B}^{\prime},R\rangle,

(15)

a_{q}^{s}
