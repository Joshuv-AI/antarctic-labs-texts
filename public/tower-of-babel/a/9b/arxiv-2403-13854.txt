On the occurrence of a phase transition in atomic systems Preprint: C2118/02/3.1
Luca Gamberale, Daniele Garbelli

Abstract
We present a simple argument confirming the spontaneous quantum condensation of an electromagnetic field in matter in the case of a multi-level atomic system coupled to a single-mode electromagnetic field in the dipole approximation. The obtained result disproves the validity of a “no-go theorem” and fully rehabilitates theories of spontaneous quantum condensation of the electromagnetic field in matter. Peculiar features of the identified coherent states are outlined.
