Quantum Rényi Entropy Functionals for Bosonic Gaussian Systems
Junseo Lee, Kabgyun Jeong

Abstract
In this study, the quantum Rényi entropy power inequality of order p > 1 p>1 and power κ \kappa is introduced as a quantum analog of the classical Rényi- p p entropy power inequality. To derive this inequality, we first exploit the Wehrl- p p entropy power inequality on bosonic Gaussian systems via the mixing operation of quantum convolution, which is a generalized beam-splitter operation. This observation directly provides a quantum Rényi- p p entropy power inequality over a quasi-probability distribution for D D -mode bosonic Gaussian regimes. The proposed inequality is expected to be useful for the nontrivial computing of quantum channel capacities, particularly universal upper bounds on bosonic Gaussian quantum channels, and a Gaussian entanglement witness in the case of Gaussian amplifier via squeezing operations.

Because most advances in quantum Shannon theory emerge from information theory, there are still reservations regarding the EPI itself—ranging from the classical, Tsallis, and unified EPIs to the unknown quantum perspective. This kind of research may extend our knowledge of original quantum channel capacity problems while providing novel insights into the theory and applications of emerging quantum networks or quantum internet.
