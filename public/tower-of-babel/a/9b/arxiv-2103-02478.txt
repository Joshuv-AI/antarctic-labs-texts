On lower estimations of square-linear ratio for plane Peano curves.
E. V. Shchepin , E. Yu. Mychka

Abstract
It is proved that for any mapping of a unit segment to a unit square, there is a pair of points of the segment for which the square of the Euclidean distance between their images exceeds the distance between them on the segment by at least 3 ⁤ 5 8 3\frac{5}{8} times. And the additional condition that the images of the beginning and end of the segment belong to opposite sides of the square increases the estimate to 4 + ε 4+\varepsilon .
