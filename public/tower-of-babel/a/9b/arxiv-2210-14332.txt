On lax protomodularity for 𝖮𝗋𝖽 \mathsf{Ord} -enriched categories Thanks: The first and third author acknowledge partial financial assistance by the Centre for Mathematics of the University of Coimbra – UIDB/00324/2020, funded by the Portuguese Government through FCT/MCTES
Maria Manuel Clementino, Andrea Montoli, Diana Rodelo

Abstract
. Our main focus concerns a possible lax version of the algebraic property of protomodularity for 𝖮𝗋𝖽 \mathsf{Ord} -enriched categories. Our motivating example is the category 𝖮𝗋𝖽𝖠𝖻 \mathsf{OrdAb} of preordered abelian groups; indeed, while abelian groups form a protomodular category, 𝖮𝗋𝖽𝖠𝖻 \mathsf{OrdAb} does not. Having in mind the role of comma objects in the enriched context, we consider some of the characteristic properties of protomodularity with respect to comma objects instead of pullbacks. We show that the equivalence between protomodularity and certain properties on pullbacks also holds when replacing conveniently pullbacks by comma objects in any finitely complete category enriched in 𝖮𝗋𝖽 \mathsf{Ord} , and propose to call lax protomodular such 𝖮𝗋𝖽 \mathsf{Ord} -enriched categories. We conclude by studying this sort of lax protomodularity for 𝖮𝗋𝖽𝖠𝖻 \mathsf{OrdAb} , equipped with a suitable 𝖮𝗋𝖽 \mathsf{Ord} -enrichment, and show that 𝖮𝗋𝖽𝖠𝖻 \mathsf{OrdAb} fulfills the equivalent lax protomodular properties with respect to the weaker notion of precomma object ; we call such categories lax preprotomodular.



Introduction

The category
\mathsf{OrdGrp}
of preordered groups was recently studied in
[
9
]
, where the authors exhibited several of their algebraic and homological properties. This category differs from the category of internal groups in the category
\mathsf{Ord}
of preorders and monotone maps, since the inversion morphism of the group structure is not necessarily monotone (it is, in fact, anti-monotone). As a consequence, many of the nice algebraic properties of groups fail to hold in that context. Indeed, in
[
9
]
it was shown that
\mathsf{OrdGrp}
shares several algebraic properties with the category
\mathsf{Mon}
of monoids, but fails to share some of the algebraic properties of the category
\mathsf{Grp}
of groups. Similar observations can be made for the category
\mathsf{OrdAb}
of preordered abelian groups and
\mathsf{Ab}
of abelian groups.

This work focuses on the algebraic property of protomodularity
[
6
]
, which in a pointed finitely complete context is equivalent to the validity of the Split Short Five Lemma. The categories
\mathsf{Grp}
and
\mathsf{Ab}
are protomodular while
\mathsf{OrdGrp}
and
\mathsf{OrdAb}
are not (see
[
9
]
). It is as if the preorder structure works against protomodularity. This led us to the following question:

Is there an
\mathsf{Ord}
-enrichment of these categories so that protomodularity is recovered in a lax sense
?

The aim of this work is to give a positive answer to this question for
\mathsf{OrdAb}
; more precisely: there is a preordered structure on morphisms that does work in favour of lax protomodularity in a sense which we explain next.

A finitely complete category
\mathsf{X}
is protomodular
[
6
]
when the pullback functors
\alpha^{*}\colon\mathsf{Pt}_{Y}(\mathsf{X})\to\mathsf{Pt}_{A}(\mathsf{X})
are conservative for any morphism
\alpha\colon A\to Y
. We recall in Theorem
2.1
well-known equivalent conditions expressed by properties on pullbacks which characterise protomodularity. To obtain a lax version of these equivalences for a finitely complete
\mathsf{Ord}
-enriched category
\mathbb{C}
with comma objects and
2
-pullbacks, we replace pullbacks conveniently with comma objects or
2
-pullbacks and the pullback change-of-base functors with comma object change-of-base functors. Since comma objects are defined on ordered pairs of morphisms, we have two possible change-of-base functors, given a morphism
\alpha\colon A\to Y
: the
vertical
comma object functor
V_{\alpha}\colon\mathsf{Pt}_{Y}(\mathbb{C})\to\mathsf{Pt}_{A}(\mathbb{C})
and the
horizontal
comma object functor
H_{\alpha}\colon\mathsf{Pt}_{Y}(\mathbb{C})\to\mathsf{Pt}_{A}(\mathbb{C})
. We show that the lax version of the equivalences in Theorem
2.1
hold for
\mathbb{C}
; see Theorem
2.5
, Corollary
2.6
, Theorem
2.11
and Corollary
2.12
.

Due to the above characterisations, we propose to call a finitely complete
\mathsf{Ord}
-enriched category
\mathbb{C}
which admits comma objects and
2
-pullbacks
lax protomodular
when the comma object functor
V_{\alpha}
is conservative for any morphism
\alpha
in
\mathbb{C}
. We shall call
\mathbb{C}

colax protomodular
when
\mathbb{C}^{\rm co}
is lax protomodular, that is, when
H_{\alpha}
is conservative for any morphism
\alpha
in
\mathbb{C}
.
The interplay between the different ingredients encoded on protomodularity is worked out in Section
2
.

It turns out that
\mathsf{OrdAb}
, equipped with the
\mathsf{Ord}
-enrichment mentioned above, only admits the weaker notion of
precomma objects
, not all comma objects. For this reason it fails to be lax protomodular. However, if we focus instead on precomma objects, vertical (and horizontal) precomma object functors and pullbacks, the equivalences above still hold in this weaker context. We call a finitely complete
\mathsf{Ord}
-enriched category
\mathbb{C}
with precomma objects
lax preprotomodular
(resp.
colax preprotomodular
) when the precomma object functor
V_{\alpha}
(resp.
H_{\alpha}
) is conservative for any morphism
\alpha
in
\mathbb{C}
. We show in Section
3
that
\mathbb{O}\mathsf{rdAb}
is lax preprotomodular but not colax preprotomodular.

In addition, we analyse the behaviour of two different factorization systems in
\mathbb{O}\mathsf{rdAb}
, which correspond to (bijective on objects, fully faithful) and (surjective on objects, monic and fully faithful) factorizations, showing that they are not pullback-stable, and so
\mathbb{O}\mathsf{rdAb}
fails to be
\mathsf{Ord}
-regular.

3.

\mathbb{O}\mathsf{rdAb}
of preordered abelian groups

Both the categories
\mathsf{Grp}

\mathsf{OrdGrp}

are not (see Theorem 4.6 in

\mathsf{OrdAb}

We start by analysing possible
\mathsf{Ord}
-enrichments for the category
\mathsf{OrdGrp}
of preordered groups and monotone homomorphisms. We recall that a preordered group is a (not necessarily abelian) group
(X,+,0)
equipped with a preorder
\leq
such that the group operation is monotone

x\leq y,u\leq v\;\;\Rightarrow\;\;x+u\leq y+v,

for any elements

The preorder of a group

positive cone

X

P_{X}=\{x\in X:0\leqslant x\}

In
\mathsf{OrdGrp}

f(-x)\leq g(-x)

\leq

\mathsf{OrdGrp}

\mathsf{Ord}
-enriched category
\mathbb{O}\mathsf{rdGrp}

For

∎
