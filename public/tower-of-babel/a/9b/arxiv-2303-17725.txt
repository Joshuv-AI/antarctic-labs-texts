enters the asymptotic of
x_{\gamma,m}
, see (
7.10
,
7.13
).

With the assumptions made, the left hand side of Bete Ansatz equations (
6.5
) become

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\frac{1}{N}\log\frac{Q_{1}(x)}{Q_{2}(x)}\;=\;-2\pi\mathsf{i}(\mu+\mathsf{i}\eta)x+\int dx_{0}P(x_{0})\log\frac{\varphi(x-x_{0}+\mathsf{i}\eta)}{\varphi(x_{0}-x+\mathsf{i}\eta)}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle-\int dx_{0}P_{A}(x_{0})\log\varphi(x-x_{0})+\int dx_{0}P_{B}(x_{0})\log\varphi(x_{0}-x)\;.\end{array}

(8.8)

Elementary transformations provide

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\frac{1}{N}\log\frac{Q_{1}(x)}{Q_{2}(x)}\;=\;\mathsf{i}\pi(\eta^{2}+S_{B}-S_{P})\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle+\int dx_{0}(P(x_{0}+\mathsf{i}\eta)+P(x_{0}-\mathsf{i}\eta)-P_{AB}(x_{0}))\log\varphi(x-x_{0})\;,\end{array}

(8.9)

where the normalisation and expectations

\int dx_{0}P(x_{0})\;=\;1\;,\quad\int dx_{0}P(x_{0})x_{0}\;=\;0\;,\quad\int dx_{0}P(x_{0})x_{0}^{2}\;=\;S_{P}\;.

(8.10)

are taken into account. Thus, the Bethe Ansatz equations (
6.5
) in the integral form (
8.9
) give

P(x_{0}+\mathsf{i}\eta)+P(x_{0}-\mathsf{i}\eta)\;=\;P_{A}(x_{0})+P_{B}(x_{0})

(8.11)

for the ground state density
P(x_{0})
. On the solution of (
8.11
) one has
\displaystyle S_{P}=\eta^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(S_{A}+S_{B})
, so that the Bethe Ansatz equations (
6.5
) become the identity

\frac{1}{N}\log\frac{Q_{1}(x)}{Q_{2}(x)}\;=\;\frac{\mathsf{i}\pi}{2}(S_{B}-S_{A})

(8.12)

Solution to (
8.11
) gives the ground state density

P(x)\;=\;\int dx_{0}K(x-x_{0})P_{AB}(x_{0})\;,

(8.13)

where the kernel

K(x)\;=\;\int\frac{\displaystyle\textrm{{\large e}}^{-2\pi\mathsf{i}xy}}{\displaystyle 2\cosh(2\pi\eta y)}dy\;=\;\frac{1}{\displaystyle 4\eta\cosh\left(\frac{\pi x}{2\eta}\right)}\;.

(8.14)

In particular, in the homogeneous symmetric case
P_{AB}(x)=\delta(x-\mu)+\delta(x+\mu)
,

P(x)\;=\;\frac{1}{\displaystyle 4\eta\cosh\left(\frac{\pi(x-\mu)}{2\eta}\right)}+\frac{1}{\displaystyle 4\eta\cosh\left(\frac{\pi(x+\mu)}{2\eta}\right)}\;.

(8.15)

Expressions for
\boldsymbol{\chi}_{\pm}(u)
then become

\frac{1}{N}\log\frac{\boldsymbol{\chi}_{+}(u)\boldsymbol{\chi}_{-}(u)^{*}}{W(u)^{*}}\;=\;\Phi_{1}(x)\;,\quad\frac{1}{N}\log\frac{\boldsymbol{\chi}_{-}(u)\boldsymbol{\chi}_{+}(u)^{*}}{W(q^{2}u)^{*}}\;=\;\Phi_{2}(x)\;,

(8.16)

where

\Phi_{1}(x)\;=\;\int dx_{0}P(x_{0})\log\varphi(x-x_{0}+\mathsf{i}\eta)\;=\;\int dx_{0}P_{AB}(x_{0})\log\varphi_{2}(x-x_{0}+\mathsf{i}\eta)\;,

(8.17)

\Phi_{2}(x)\;=\;\int dx_{0}P(x_{0})\log\varphi(x_{0}-x+\mathsf{i}\eta)\;=\;\int dx_{0}P_{AB}(x_{0})\log\varphi_{2}(x_{0}-x+\mathsf{i}\eta)\;.

(8.18)

Expressions (
8.17
) and (
8.18
) with the homogeneous density (
8.15
) being analytically continued to the stat mechanical regime of imaginary
x
and
\mu
explicitly reproduce the free energy for the Faddeev-Volkov model
[
10
]
. Moreover, for imaginary
\mu
the distribution (
8.15
) becomes unimodal.

The transfer matrix is given by

\frac{1}{N}\log T_{0}(u)\;=\;\int_{\mathbb{R}\pm\mathsf{i}0}dx_{0}P(x_{0})\log(1-\textrm{{\large e}}^{2\pi\mathsf{b}(x-x_{0})})\;=\;\int dx_{0}P_{AB}(x_{0})I_{\pm}(x-x_{0})\;,

(8.19)

where

I_{+}(x)\;=\;\log\frac{(-qu;q^{4})_{\infty}}{(-q^{3}u;q^{4})_{\infty}}\frac{(-\mathsf{i}w;-\mathsf{i}p)_{\infty}}{(\mathsf{i}w;-\mathsf{i}p)_{\infty}}

(8.20)

with

w\;=\;\exp\left(\frac{\pi x}{2\eta}\right)\;,\quad p\;=\;\exp\left(-\frac{\pi\sigma}{2\eta}\right)\;,\quad-\mathsf{i}p\;=\;\exp\left(-\frac{\mathsf{i}\pi\mathsf{b}^{-1}}{2\eta}\right)\;,

(8.21)

and

I_{-}(x)\;=\;I_{+}(x)-2\pi\mathsf{i}y(x)\;,\quad y(x)\;=\;\int_{-\infty}^{x}dx_{0}K(x_{0})\;=\;\frac{1}{2\pi\mathsf{i}}\log\frac{1+\mathsf{i}w}{1-\mathsf{i}w}\;.

(8.22)

These computations allow one to verify condition (
8.7
):

\frac{1}{N}\log\left|\frac{A(q^{2}u)B(u)}{T_{0}(u)T_{0}(q^{2}u)}\right|\;=\;\int dx_{0}P_{AB}(x_{0})\log\tanh\left|\frac{\pi(x-x_{0}-\sigma)}{4\eta}\right|\;<\;0

(8.23)

Note that due to the branch cuts of
\log
in (
8.19
) the expression for
I(x)
must be modified in each strip
k\eta<\textrm{Im}\;x<(k+1)\eta
,
x\in\mathbb{C}
. In particular,
\log T(u)
has no singularities.
Note also,

\frac{1}{N}\log\left|\frac{A(q^{2}u)B(u)}{T_{0}(u)T_{0}(u)_{x\to x+2\mathsf{i}\eta}}\right|\;=\;0\;,

(8.24)

where
\log T_{0}(u)_{x\to x+2\mathsf{i}\eta}
corresponds to the analytical continuation
I_{\pm}(x)\to I_{\pm}(x+2\mathsf{i}\eta)
in (
8.19
).

The results here correspond to the ground state structure of the model. Next step is the study of the low energy excitations.
