Certain graphs with exactly one irreducible T T -module with endpoint 1 1 , which is thin: the pseudo-distance-regularized case
Blas Fernández Andrej Marušič Institute University of Primorska Muzejski trg 2, 6000 Koper, Slovenia blas.fernandez@famnit.upr.si

Abstract
Let Γ \Gamma denote a finite, simple and connected graph. Fix a vertex x x of Γ \Gamma which is not a leaf and let T = T ⁡ ( x ) T=T(x) denote the Terwilliger algebra of Γ \Gamma with respect to x x . Assume that the unique irreducible T T -module with endpoint 0 0 is thin, or equivalently that Γ \Gamma is pseudo-distance-regular around x x . We consider the property that Γ \Gamma has, up to isomorphism, a unique irreducible T T -module with endpoint 1 1 , and that this T T -module is thin. The main result of the paper is a combinatorial characterization of this property.

A natural follow-up to these results involving Terwilliger algebras of non-distance-regular graphs is presented here. For the most recent research where the Terwilliger algebra plays an important role, see for example

It turns out that there exists a unique irreducible

Assume that the unique irreducible

is pseudo-distance-regularized. The main goal of this paper is to find a combinatorial characterization of graphs, which also have a unique irreducible

denote a simple, finite, connected graph. Pick a vertex

Proof.

Let

denote the standard module, and let
T\widehat{x}
denote the trivial

-module. We observe

Assume first that there are no irreducible

-modules with endpoint

is orthogonal direct sum of irreducible

-modules and none of these

To prove the second part of our assertion, recall that if
