Equivariant K K -theory of Springer Varieties
Vikraman Uma

Abstract
. The aim of this paper is to describe the topological equivariant K K -ring, in terms of generators and relations, of a Springer variety ℱ λ \mathcal{F}_{\lambda} of type A A associated to a nilpotent operator having Jordan canonical form whose block sizes form a weakly decreasing sequence λ = ( λ 1 , … , λ l ) \lambda=(\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{l}) . This parallels the description of the equivariant cohomology ring of ℱ λ \mathcal{F}_{\lambda} due to Abe and Horiguchi and generalizes the description of ordinary topological K K -ring of ℱ λ \mathcal{F}_{\lambda} due to Sankaran and Uma [ 15 ] .

Introduction

Fix a positive integer

\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{C}^{n}):=\{\underline{V}:=(0=V_{0}\subset V_{1}\subset\cdots\subset V_{n}=\mathbb{C}^{n})\mid\dim V_{i}=i\penalty\ \penalty\ \mbox{for all}\penalty\ \penalty\ i\}.

Let

The
Springer variety of type
A
associated to

\mathcal{F}

defined as

\{\underline{V}\in\mathcal{F}\penalty\ \penalty\ \mid\penalty\ \penalty\ NV_{i}\subset V_{i-1}\penalty\ \penalty\ \mbox{for
all}\penalty\ \penalty\ 1\leq i\leq n\}.

The Springer variety
\mathcal{F}_{N}
is
seen to be the subvariety of
\mathcal{F}
fixed by the action of the
infinite cyclic group generated by the unipotent element

\mathcal{U}=I_{n}+N\in SL(n,\mathbb{C})
. Moreover, denoting by

\lambda=(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\ldots,\lambda_{l})
the partition of
n

where the
\lambda_{j}
are the sizes of the diagonal blocks of the
Jordan canonical form of
N
, the variety
\mathcal{F}_{N}
depends, up
to isomorphism, only on the partition
\lambda
. This is so, because
two different choices of nilpotent transformations corresponding to
the same partition
\lambda
are conjugates in
GL(n,\mathbb{C})
. For
this reason, we assume that
N
itself is in the Jordan canonical
form:

N=J_{\lambda}:=\textrm{diag}(J_{\lambda_{1}},\ldots,J_{\lambda_{l}})

with
\lambda_{1}\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_{l}
and denote the Springer
variety
\mathcal{F}_{N}
by
\mathcal{F}_{\lambda}
. (Here

J_{p}=(a_{i,j})\in M_{p}(\mathbb{C})
is the matrix where

a_{i,i+1}=1,1\leq i<p,
and all other entries are zero.) If

\lambda=(1,\ldots,1)
, then
N=0
and we have

\mathcal{F}_{\lambda}=\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{C}^{n})=\mathcal{F}
. At the
other extreme, when
\lambda=(n)
,

is a regular nilpotent element
and we see that
\mathcal{F}_{(n)}
is the one-point variety consisting
only of the standard flag

0=E_{0}\subset E_{1}\subset\cdots\subset E_{n}=\mathbb{C}^{n}
where
E_{j}
is
spanned by the standard basis vectors
e_{1},\ldots,e_{j}
for

1\leq j\leq n
.

Note that
\mathcal{F}_{\lambda}
is stable by the action of the
algebraic torus
T^{l}_{\mathbb{C}}\cong(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{l}
contained in

GL(n,\mathbb{C})
consisting of all diagonal matrices which commute
with
N
. We shall denote by
T^{l}=(\mathbb{S}^{1})^{l}
the compact torus
contained in
T^{l}_{\mathbb{C}}
. Denoting the diagonal subgroup of

GL(n,\mathbb{C})
by
T^{n}_{\mathbb{C}}

({\mathtt{t}}_{1},\ldots,{\mathtt{t}}_{n})\in T^{n}_{\mathbb{C}}
belongs to
T^{l}_{\mathbb{C}}
if
and only if
{\mathtt{t}}_{a_{j}+i}={\mathtt{t}}_{a_{j}+1}
for
2\leq i\leq\lambda_{j+1}
,

0\leq j\leq l-1
where

a_{j}:=\lambda_{1}+\cdots+\lambda_{j},1\leq j\leq l-1

.

[
1

5.6.
The lambda operations in equivariant
K
-theory.

We
recall here the lambda operations or the exterior power operations
in equivariant
K
-theory (see
[
4
, Section 1]
).

Let
X
be a compact
G
-space for
G
a compact Lie group. For

x\in K_{G}(X)
we let

\uplambda_{t}(x):=\sum_{i\geq 0}\uplambda^{i}(x)t^{i}

as an element of the
formal power series ring
K_{G}(X)[[t]]
in the indeterminate
t
.
For any
x\in K_{G}(X)
,
\uplambda^{0}(x)=1
. When

x=[\xi]_{G}\in K_{G}(X)
is the class of a
G
-vector bundle
\xi
on

X
of rank
k
,
\uplambda_{t}(x)
is a polynomial of degree
k
, since
the exterior power
\uplambda^{d}([\xi]_{G})=[\Lambda^{d}(\xi)]_{G}=0
for

d\geq k+1
. In particular, when
\xi
is a
G
-line bundle, then

\uplambda_{t}([\xi]_{G})=1+[\xi]_{G}t
.

Let
\xi=\xi_{1}\oplus\cdots\oplus\xi_{k}
where
\xi_{i}
is a

G
-equivariant line bundle on
X
for
1\leq i\leq k
. Then we have

\displaystyle\uplambda_{t}([\xi]_{G})=\prod_{i=1}^{k}(1+[\xi_{i}]_{G}t)
and

\displaystyle\uplambda_{t}([-\xi]_{G})=\prod_{i=1}^{k}(1+[\xi_{i}]_{G}t)^{-1}
. The
last two equalities follow from the identity

\uplambda_{t}(x+y)=\uplambda_{t}(x)\cdot\uplambda_{t}(y).

When
X={\mathcal{F}}_{\lambda}
and
G=T^{l}
then from (
5.37
) and the
above properties of
\uplambda_{t}
we have

(5.46)

\uplambda_{t}([\xi^{\prime}]_{T^{l}})=\prod_{1\leq r\leq s}(1+[L_{r}]_{T^{l}}t)\prod_{1\leq{\ell}\leq{s+1-d}}(1+[\epsilon_{j_{{\ell}}}]_{T^{l}}t)^{-1}

for
d\geq s+1-q

where
q=p_{\lambda^{\vee}}(s)
. Now,
\uplambda_{t}([\xi^{\prime}]_{T^{l}})
is a
polynomial of degree
d-1
since
\mbox{rank}(\xi^{\prime})=d-1
for

d\geq s+1-q
. Thus by comparing the coefficients of
t^{d}
in
(
5.46
) where
d\geq s-q+1
we obtain the following
equation in
K_{T^{l}}({\mathcal{F}}_{\lambda})

(5.47)

\sum_{0\leq k\leq d}(-1)^{d-k}e_{k}([L_{1}]_{T^{l}},\ldots,[L_{s}]_{T^{l}})h_{d-k}([\epsilon_{j_{1}}]_{T^{l}},\ldots,[\epsilon_{j_{s+1-d}}]_{T^{l}})=0.

We have the following proposition
with the notations of Section
1
where

\mathcal{I}_{\lambda}
in particular denotes the equivariant

K
-theoretic Tanisaki ideal (see (
1.2
)).

Proposition 5.10
.

We have a well defined surjective

-algebra homomorphism

which sends

to

for
1\leq i\leq n
.

Proof.

Recall from Theorem
5.3
that
K^{T^{l}}({\mathcal{F}}_{\lambda})
is
generated by

as an
R(T^{l})
-algebra. Furthermore,
recall from Proposition

Recall that we have the augmentation

-equivariant vector bundle to the class of the underlying vector

-structure. (see

[

]
,
[

]
).

Corollary 6.1
.

The map

{\mathrm{F}}:\mathbb{Z}\otimes_{R(T^{l})}K_{T^{l}}(\mathcal{F}_{\lambda})\longrightarrow K(\mathcal{F}_{\lambda})

\uptheta
on the first factor
and by
\mathrm{f}
on the second factor is an isomorphism of

-modules. In other words the Springer variety

\mathcal{F}_{\lambda}
is weakly equivariantly formal for

-theory

[
8
]
.

Proof:
By Theorem
5.1
,

is a free
R(T^{l})
-module of rank

. By
[
15

-module of rank

On the other hand by specializing to

,
that is by tensoring with

over
