Abstract
In this paper we extend some results obtained by Artamonov and Sabitov for quantum polynomials to skew quantum polynomials and quasi − - commutative bijective skew PBW extensions. Moreover, we find a counterexample to the conjecture proposed in [ 6 ] . Keywords: Skew P ​ B ​ W PBW extensions, skew quantum polynomials, Ore domains, valuations, completions.
