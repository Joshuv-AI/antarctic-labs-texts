Definition 1

(Newton Polygon)
.

For a prime

and non zero rational numbers

with
(a,b)=1
and

, the
q
-adic valuation is given by

\nu_{q}\Big(\frac{a}{b}\Big)=j_{1}-j_{2}\quad\text{where}\quad q^{j_{1}}||a,~~~~q^{j_{2}}||b.

We define
\nu_{q}(0)=+\infty
. Let

with
a_{0}a_{n}\neq 0

h(x)
with respect to
q
. A lattice point of the edge is an integer point on the edge other than the end points of the edge.
