II
Mehod

As for the symmetric nuclear matter, Kohno calculated the
g
matrix
by using the Brueckner-Hartree-Fock (BHF) method with chiral N
3
LO 2NFs and NNLO 3NFs
Kohno 2012
.
The framework is applied for positive energies. The resulting non-local chiral
g
matrix is localized
into three-range Gaussian forms by using the localization method proposed
by the Melbourne group
von Geramb et al. 1991
;
Amos and Dortmans 1994
.
The resulting local
g
matrix is referred to as Kyushu

g
-matrix in this paper
Toyokawa et al. 2018
.

The brief formulation of the double folding model itself is shown below.
The potential
U
consists
of the direct part (
U^{\rm DR}
) and the exchange part (
U^{\rm EX}
):

\displaystyle U^{\rm DR}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\hskip-4.26773pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-4.26773pt\sum_{\mu,\nu}\int\rho^{\mu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P})\rho^{\nu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T})g^{\rm DR}_{\mu\nu}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T},

(1)

\displaystyle U^{\rm EX}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\hskip-4.26773pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-4.26773pt\sum_{\mu,\nu}\int\rho^{\mu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P},{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}-{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}})\rho^{\nu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T},{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}+{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}})

(2)

\displaystyle~~\hskip-14.22636pt\times g^{\rm EX}_{\mu\nu}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\exp{[-i{\mbox{\boldmath$K$}}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\cdot{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/M]}d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T},~~~~

where
{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}={\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}-{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}+{\mbox{\boldmath$R$}}

for the coordinate
R
between P and T. The coordinate

{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}

(
{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}
) denotes the location for the interacting nucleon
measured from the center-of-mass of the projectile (target).
Each of
\mu
and
\nu
corresponds to the
z
-component
of isospin.
The original form of
U^{\rm EX}
is a non-local function of
R
,
but it has been localized in Eq. (
2
)
with the local semi-classical approximation
Brieva and Rook 1977a
;
Brieva and Rook 1977b
;
Brieva and Rook 1978
in which
P is assumed to propagate as a plane wave with
the local momentum
\hbar{\mbox{\boldmath$K$}}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})
within a short range of the
nucleon-nucleon interaction, where
M=AA_{\rm T}/(A+A_{\rm T})

for the mass number
A
(
A_{\rm T}
) of P (T).
The validity of this localization is shown in Ref.
Minomo et al. 2010
.

The direct and exchange parts,
g^{\rm DR}_{\mu\nu}
and

g^{\rm EX}_{\mu\nu}
, of the effective nucleon-nucleon interaction
(
g
-matrix) are assumed to depend on the local density

\displaystyle\rho_{\mu\nu}=\sigma^{\mu}\left[\rho^{\nu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}+{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/2)+\rho^{\nu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}-{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/2)\right]

(3)

at the midpoint of the interacting nucleon pair, where
\sigma^{\mu}
is the Pauli matrix of a nucleon in P.

The direct and exchange parts are described by

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636ptg_{\mu\nu}^{\rm DR}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle{\frac{1}{4}\sum_{S}}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{S1}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\hskip 11.9501pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=\pm 1\vskip 5.69046pt\\
\displaystyle{\frac{1}{8}\sum_{S,T}}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{ST}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu}),\hskip 5.69046pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=0\end{cases}

(4)

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636ptg_{\mu\nu}^{\rm EX}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle{\frac{1}{4}\sum_{S}}(-1)^{S+1}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{S1}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\hskip 9.67383pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=\pm 1\vskip 5.69046pt\\
\displaystyle{\frac{1}{8}\sum_{S,T}}(-1)^{S+T}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{ST}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\hskip 5.69046pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=0~~~~~\end{cases}

(5)

where
\hat{S}={\sqrt{2S+1}}
and
g_{\mu\nu}^{ST}
are
the spin-isospin components of the
g
-matrix interaction.
As a way of taking the center-of-mass correction to the phenomenological densities of Ref.
