Gravitational Equivalence Theorem and Double-Copy for Kaluza-Klein Graviton Scattering Amplitudes
Yan-Feng Hang, Hong-Jian He

Abstract
We analyze the structure of scattering amplitudes of the Kaluza-Klein (KK) gravitons and of the KK gravitational Goldstone bosons in the compactified 5d General Relativity (GR). Using a general R ξ R_{\xi} gauge-fixing, we study the geometric Higgs mechanism for the massive spin-2 KK gravitons. We newly propose and prove a Gravitational Equivalence Theorem (GRET) to connect the scattering amplitudes of longitudinal KK gravitons to that of the KK gravitational Goldstone bosons, which formulates the geometric gravitational Higgs mechanism at the scattering S S -matrix level. We demonstrate that the GRET provides a general energy-cancellation mechanism guaranteeing the N N -point longitudinal KK graviton scattering amplitudes to have their leading energy dependence cancelled down by a large power factor of E 2 ​ N E^{2N} ( N ⩾ 4 N\!\geqslant\!4 ) up to any loop order. We propose an extended double-copy approach to construct the massive KK graviton (Goldstone) amplitudes from the KK gauge boson (Goldstone) amplitudes. With these we establish a new correspondence between the two types of energy cancellations in the four-point longitudinal KK amplitudes at tree level: E 4 → E 0 \,E^{4}\!\!\rightarrow\!E^{0}\, in the KK gauge theory and E 10 → E 2 \,E^{10}\!\!\rightarrow\!E^{2}\, in the KK GR theory. Kinematics of KK Scattering

We consider
\,2\!\rightarrow\!2\,
KK scattering process,
with the four-momentum of each
external state obeying the on-shell condition

\,p^{2}_{j}\!=\!-M_{j}^{2}

\,\sum_{j}p_{j}\!=0\,

the physical momenta of the two incident particles

-p_{1}
and
-p_{2}

as an example,
where

denotes any given KK state of level-
n

and has
M_{j}\!=\!M_{n}

For the KK theory, the external particle has
mass

for a given KK-state of level-

we define the momenta as follows:

\displaystyle p_{1}^{\mu}

\displaystyle=-E\left(1,0,0,\beta\right)\,,

\displaystyle p_{2}^{\mu}=-E\left(1,0,0,-\beta\right)\,,

(S1)

\displaystyle p_{3}^{\mu}

\displaystyle=E\left(1,\beta s_{\theta},0,\beta c_{\theta}\right)\,,

\displaystyle p_{4}^{\mu}=E\left(1,-\beta s_{\theta},0,-\beta c_{\theta}\right)\,,

where
(s_{\theta},\hskip 0.85358ptc_{\theta})=(\sin\hskip-0.85358pt\theta,\,\cos\hskip-0.85358pt\theta)

\hskip 0.85358pt\beta\!=\!(1\!-\!M_{n}^{2}/\hskip-0.85358ptE^{2})^{1/2}\hskip 0.85358pt

\displaystyle s=-\left(p_{1}\!+\hskip-0.85358ptp_{2}\right)^{2}\!=4E^{2},~~~~t=-\left(p_{1}\!+\hskip-0.85358ptp_{4}\right)^{2}\!=-\mbox{$\frac{\,{1}\,}{2}$}s\hskip 0.85358pt\beta^{2}(1\!+\!c_{\theta})\hskip 0.85358pt,~~~~u=-\left(p_{1}\!+\hskip-0.85358ptp_{3}\right)^{2}\!=-\mbox{$\frac{\,{1}\,}{2}$}s\hskip 0.85358pt\beta^{2}(1\!-\!c_{\theta})\hskip 0.85358pt.

(S2)

E^{2}\!=\hskip-0.85358ptE^{2}\beta^{2}\hskip-0.85358pt+\hskip-0.85358ptM_{n}^{2}\hskip 0.85358pt
, we define a new set
of mass-independent Mandelstam variables as follows:

\displaystyle s_{0}=4E^{2}\beta^{2},\qquad t_{0}=-\frac{\,s_{0}\,}{2}(1+c_{\theta})\hskip 0.85358pt,\qquad u_{0}=-\frac{\,s_{0}\,}{2}(1-c_{\theta})\hskip 0.85358pt,

(S3)

\hskip 0.85358pts_{0}\!=\!s\hskip-1.42262pt-\hskip-1.42262pt4M_{n}^{2}

(S4)

As we mentioned in the text,
a massive KK graviton has 5 helicity states
(
\lambda\!=\pm 2,\pm 1,0

take the following forms:

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{\pm 2}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=\epsilon_{\pm}^{\mu}\epsilon_{\pm}^{\nu}\,,\qquad\varepsilon_{\pm 1}^{\mu\nu}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\,}\,}\!\left(\epsilon_{\pm}^{\mu}\epsilon_{L}^{\nu}\!+\epsilon_{L}^{\mu}\epsilon_{\pm}^{\nu}\right)\!,\qquad\varepsilon_{L}^{\mu\nu}\!=\frac{1}{\sqrt{6\,}\,}\!\left(\epsilon_{+}^{\mu}\epsilon_{-}^{\nu}\!+\epsilon_{-}^{\mu}\epsilon_{+}^{\nu}\!+2\epsilon_{L}^{\mu}\epsilon_{L}^{\nu}\right)\!,

(S6)

where the helicity indices of each KK graviton are

\,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}=\pm 2,\hskip 0.85358pt\pm 1,\hskip 0.85358pt0\,
