Crossing of phantom divide line in model of interacting Tsallis holographic dark energy
Artyom V. Astashenok, Alexander S. Tepliakov

Abstract
We consider a Tsallis holographic dark energy model with interaction between dark energy and matter. The density of dark energy is taken as ρ d ∼ 3 ​ C 2 / L 4 − 2 ​ γ \rho_{d}\sim 3C^{2}/L^{4-2\gamma} , where C C , γ \gamma are constants. The event horizon is chosen as the characteristic scale L L . The cosmological dynamics of the universe are analyzed, with special attention paid to the possibility of crossing the phantom line w e ​ f ​ f = − 1 w_{eff}=-1 . It is shown that for certain values of parameters this may occur not only once, but also twice.

Introduction

Our universe is expanding with acceleration

The universe began to expand with acceleration about 5 billion years ago. The reason for this acceleration is most likely the so-called “dark energy”. It is known that dark energy is distributed in space with an extremely high degree of homogeneity, has a low density and does not interact appreciably with ordinary matter through the known fundamental types of interaction—with the exception of gravity. In this paper, we will consider the THDE model with the inclusion of a possible interaction between matter and dark energy. We will analyze a simple type of interaction (

The paper is organized in the following way. In Section II, a model of Tsallis holographic dark energy with interaction is described. II
Model Description

Consider a spatially flat universe with the Friedman–Lemaitre–Robertson–Walker metric:

ds^{2}=dt^{2}-a^{2}(t)(dx^{2}+dy^{2}+dz^{2}).

(1)

Here,
t
is cosmic time,
a(t)
is the scale factor. Let us assume that the universe is filled with dark energy and matter with densities
\rho_{de}
and
\rho_{m}
, respectively. The cosmological equations for a given metric can be written in the following form:

H^{2}=\frac{1}{3}(\rho_{m}+\rho_{de})

(2)

\dot{H}+H^{2}=-\frac{1}{6}(\rho_{m}+\rho_{de}+p_{de}).

(3)

In the THDE model, the dark energy density is

\rho_{de}=\frac{3C^{2}}{L^{4-2\gamma}},

(4)

where
\gamma\in[1,2]
. The value
\gamma=1
corresponds to a simple holographic dark energy model, at
\gamma=2
we have the cosmological constant. As a scale
L
, we can consider the event horizon:

L=a\int_{t}^{\infty}\frac{d{t}^{\prime}}{a}.

Other possibilities are the particle horizon

L=a\int_{0}^{t}\frac{d{t}^{\prime}}{a}

and the inverse Hubble parameter

L=1/H.

If dark energy and matter interact with each other, the continuity equations for the corresponding components take the form:

\dot{\rho}_{m}+3H\rho_{m}=Q,

(5)

\dot{\rho}_{de}+3H(\rho_{de}+p_{de})=-Q.

(6)

Here, some function

and analytically obtain an expression for the pressure of dark energy

p_{de}=-\frac{\dot{\rho}_{de}}{3H}-\rho_{de}-\frac{Q}{3H}.

(7)

Then, the parameter of the dark energy equation of state is

w_{de}=\frac{p_{de}}{\rho_{de}}=-\frac{\dot{\rho}_{de}}{3H\rho_{de}}-1-\frac{Q}{3H\rho_{de}}.

(8)

We will also analyze the evolution of
\Omega_{m}
and
\Omega_{de}
, the fractions of matter density and dark energy in the future:

\Omega_{i}=\frac{\rho_{i}}{3H^{2}}.

(9)

In the case of event or particle horizon as scale cutoff, we also add the following equation for

\frac{d}{dt}\frac{L}{a}=\pm 1/a,

(10)

where ‘
\pm

IV
Conclusions

Let us briefly consider some another choices for the function
Q
.

Q=3d^{2}H\rho_{de}^{\alpha}\rho^{\beta},\quad\rho=\rho_{m}+\rho_{de},

(12)

\alpha+\beta=1.

Our analysis shows that for various
\alpha
and
\beta
, the evolution of the universe in principle does not contain principal peculiarities in comparison with the simple case
\alpha=1
,
\beta=0
considered above.

For

Q=3d^{2}H(\rho_{de}-\rho_{m}),

(13)

the situation is similar. The explanation is very simple. From previous calculations, one can see that the fraction of matter tends to a constant value and therefore
\rho_{de}-\rho_{m}\approx\delta\rho_{de}
, where
\delta<1
. This model is close to that considered above with the slightly redefined parameter
d^{2}
.

In the case of

Q=3d^{2}H\rho_{de}^{\alpha}\rho_{m}^{\beta},\quad\alpha+\beta=1,

(14)

the situation is more interesting. The big rip singularity for
C<1
and
\gamma=1
can be eliminated due to the interaction for
\alpha=2
,
\beta=-1
.

We investigated models of Tsallis holographic dark energy with inclusion of interaction between dark energy and matter in the form of a function
Q=3d^{2}H\rho_{de}
. Our calculations show that the interaction can lead to phantomization for dark energy but without a singularity in the future as one can expect for phantom energy with a constant value of
w_{de}=p_{de}/\rho_{de}
. The established equilibrium between holographic energy density and matter density cancels the singularity although the asymptotical value of
w_{de}
can be less than
-1
.
Another interesting issue is that for
C<1
, the future big rip singularity is eliminated due to the interaction. In this case,
w_{de}
tends to a constant value
w_{0}<-1
but the Hubble parameter slowly decreases. For
\gamma>1
, there is a critical value of the coupling parameter
d^{2}
such that big rip singularity does not occur.
For
C>1
and
\gamma\geq 1
, the“quintessence” with
w<-1
due to the interaction is possible: the Hubble parameter decreases and the universe expansion decelerates.

We consider
the epoch of late acceleration in our paper. Note that, in a similar fashion, following the approach developed in

one can consider unification of inflation with dark energy within Tsallis HDE.

In conclusion, we need to say a few words about the coincidence problem in the considered model. In the
\Lambda
CDM model, the observed fact that the present fractions of dark energy and dark matter are of the same order of magnitude shows that we are currently living in a very special period. For Tsallis HDE without interaction, we also need to explain why in the present epoch dark energy and matter density are close to each other. However, interaction in some cases changes the behavior of the relation
