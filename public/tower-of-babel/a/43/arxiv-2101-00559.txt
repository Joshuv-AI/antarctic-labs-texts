Existence and reducibility of the Hilbert scheme of smooth and linearly normal curves in ℙ r \mathbb{P}^{r} of relatively high degrees This work was started when the author was enjoying the hospitality and the stimulating atmosphere of the Max-Planck-Insitut für Mathematik (Bonn). The author was supported in part by National Research Foundation of South Korea (2019R1I1A1A01058457). He would like to thank the referees for several valuable comments and suggestions which enhanced the clarity as well as the readability of the paper significantly. He is also grateful to Dawei Chen and Claudio Fontanari for useful remarks on admissible covers.

Abstract
. Let ℋ d , g , r \mathcal{H}_{d,g,r} be the Hilbert scheme parametrizing smooth irreducible and non-degenerate curves of degree d d and genus g g in ℙ r . \mathbb{P}^{r}. We denote by ℋ d , g , r ℒ \mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r} the union of those components of ℋ d , g , r \mathcal{H}_{d,g,r} whose general element is linearly normal. In this article we show that ℋ d , g , r ℒ \mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r} ( d ≥ g + r − 3 d\geq g+r-3 ) is non-empty in a certain optimal range of triples ( d , g , r ) (d,g,r) and is empty outside the range. This settles the existence (or non-emptiness if one prefers) of the Hilbert scheme ℋ d , g , r ℒ \mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r} of linearly normal curves of degree d d and genus g g in ℙ r \mathbb{P}^{r} for g + r − 3 ≤ d ≤ g + r g+r-3\leq d\leq g+r , r ≥ 3 r\geq 3 . We also determine all the triples ( d , g , r ) (d,g,r) with g + r − 3 ≤ d ≤ g + r g+r-3\leq d\leq g+r for which ℋ d , g , r ℒ \mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r} is reducible (or irreducible).

1.
An overview, preliminaries and basic set-up

Given non-negative integers
d
,
g
and
r\geq 3
, we denote by
\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}
the Hilbert scheme of smooth curves parametrizing smooth irreducible and non-degenerate curves of degree
d
and genus
g
in
\mathbb{P}^{r}
.

In two recent works
[
6
,
23
]
, the authors studied the Hilbert scheme of
linearly normal
curves in
\mathbb{P}^{r}
,
r\geq 3
. As a preliminary attempt toward a reasonable settlement of the
Modified Assertion of Severi
– which was first addressed in
[
8
, p. 489]
and later suggested by A. Lopez in the review of
[
19
]
on MathSciNet; AMS Mathematical Reviews MR1221726(95a:14026) – the main problem treated in
[
6
,
23
]
was focused on the irreducibility of the Hilbert scheme of linearly normal curves as follows.

Modified Assertion of Severi
.

A nonempty
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}
is irreducible for any triple
(d,g,r)
in the Brill-Noether range

\rho(d,g,r)=g-(r+1)(g-d+r)\geq 0,

where
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}
is the union of those components of
{\mathcal{H}}_{d,g,r}
whose general element is linearly normal.

We briefly make a note of the results in
[
6
]
regarding this business as follows.

Theorem 1.1

(Ballico et al.)
.

(1)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r,g,r}
is non-empty and irreducible.

(2)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-1,g,r}
is non-empty and irreducible for
g\geq r+1
and is empty for
g\leq r
.

(3)

Every non-empty
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
is irreducible.

(4)

Every non-empty
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
is irreducible for
g\geq 2r+3

and is non-empty for
g\geq 3r+3
.

By the Riemann-Roch formula, there is no complete linear series of degree
d
and dimension
r
in case
d\geq g+r+1
. Therefore in this range we have

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}=\emptyset

and the Modified Assertion of Severi makes sense only if
g-d+r\geq 0
.

We remark that the results (1) and (2) in Theorem
1.1
are strong, i.e. the range of the genus
g
with respect to the dimension
r
of the ambient projective space
\mathbb{P}^{r}
for which
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}
(
d\geq g+r-1
) is non-empty is sharp. On the other hand,
even though the irreducibility of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}
holds beyond the Brill-Noether range for
d=g+r-2
, the optimal range of
g
(with respect to
r
) for which
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
is non-empty has not been explicitly obtained in
[
6
]
. Instead, the following scattered results have been shown; cf.
[
6
, Remark 2.4]
.

(1)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}=\emptyset
for
g\leq r+2
,
r\geq 3

(2)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}\neq\emptyset
inside the Brill-Noether range
\rho(g+r-2,g,r)=g-2(r+1)\geq 0
,

(3)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}\neq\emptyset
for
some values
of
g\geq r+3
outside the Brill-Noether range.

However for most values of
g
outside the Brill-Noether range, the existence of a component of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
has been left undetermined.

In this paper we determine the full range of the genus
g
for which
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}\neq\emptyset.
We also get the widest possible (and optimal) range of
g
for which

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}\neq\emptyset.

As a by-product, we come up with the complete list of triples
(g+r-3,g,r)
for which
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
is reducible.

The organization of this paper is as follows. After we briefly recall some terminologies and preliminaries in the remainder of this section, we start the next section by showing the existence of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
for every
g\geq r+3
outside the Brill-Noether range.

In the subsequent section, we demonstrate the non-emptiness of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}

in the optimal range
beyond the Brill-Noether range.
We then proceed to determine the irreducibility (or reducibility) of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
for every
g
for which
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}\neq\emptyset
.

For notation and conventions, we almost always follow those in
[
3
]
and
[
4
]
; e.g.
\pi(d,r)
is the maximal possible arithmetic genus of an irreducible and non-degenerate curve of degree
d
in
\mathbb{P}^{r}
.
Following classical terminology, a linear series of degree
d
and dimension
r
on a smooth curve
C
is usually denoted by
g^{r}_{d}
.
A base-point-free linear series
g^{r}_{d}
(
r\geq 2
) on a smooth curve
C
is called birationally very ample when the morphism

C\rightarrow\mathbb{P}^{r}
induced by the
g^{r}_{d}
is generically one-to-one (or birational) onto its image. A base-point-free linear series
g^{r}_{d}
on
C
is said to be compounded of an involution (compounded for short) if the morphism induced by the linear series gives rise to a non-trivial covering map
C\rightarrow C^{\prime}
of degree
k\geq 2
.
Throughout we work over the field of complex numbers.

We recall several fundamental results which are rather well-known; cf.
[
4
]
or
[
2
, §1 and §2]
.
Let
\mathcal{M}_{g}
be the moduli space of smooth curves of genus
g
. Given an isomorphism class
[C]\in\mathcal{M}_{g}
corresponding to a smooth irreducible curve
C
, there exist a neighborhood
U\subset\mathcal{M}_{g}
of the class
[C]
and a smooth connected variety
\mathcal{M}
which is a finite ramified covering
h:\mathcal{M}\to U
, as well as varieties
\mathcal{C}
,
\mathcal{W}^{r}_{d}
and
\mathcal{G}^{r}_{d}
proper over
\mathcal{M}
with the following properties:

(1)

\xi:\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{M}
is a universal curve, i.e. for every
p\in\mathcal{M}
,
\xi^{-1}(p)
is a smooth curve of genus
g
whose isomorphism class is
h(p)
,

(2)

\mathcal{W}^{r}_{d}
parametrizes the pairs
(p,L)
where
L
is a line bundle of degree
d
and
h^{0}(L)\geq r+1
on
\xi^{-1}(p)
,

(3)

\mathcal{G}^{r}_{d}
parametrizes the couples
(p,\mathcal{D})
, where
\mathcal{D}
is possibly an incomplete linear series of degree
d
and dimension
r
on
\xi^{-1}(p)
.

Let
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}
(
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
, respectively) be the union of components of
\mathcal{G}^{r}_{d}
whose general element
(p,\mathcal{D})
corresponds to a very ample (very ample and complete, respectively) linear series
\mathcal{D}
on the curve
C=\xi^{-1}(p)
. By recalling that an open subset of
\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}
consisting of elements corresponding to smooth irreducible and non-degenerate curves is a
\mathbb{P}\textrm{GL}(r+1)
-bundle over an open subset of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}
, the irreducibility of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}
guarantees the irreducibility of
\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}
. Likewise, the irreducibility of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
ensures the irreducibility of

\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}^{\mathcal{L}}
.

The following facts regarding the schemes
\mathcal{G}^{r}_{d}
and
\mathcal{W}^{r}_{d}
are also well known; cf.
[
13
, 2.a]
and
[
4
, Ch. 21, §3, 5, 6, 11, 12]
.

Proposition 1.2
.

For non-negative integers
d
,
g
and
r
, let

\rho(d,g,r):=g-(r+1)(g-d+r)

be the Brill-Noether number.

(1)

The dimension of any component of
\mathcal{G}^{r}_{d}
is at least

\lambda(d,g,r):=3g-3+\rho(d,g,r).

Moreover, if
\rho(d,g,r)\geq 0
, there exists a unique component
\mathcal{G}_{0}
of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}
which dominates
\mathcal{M}
(or
\mathcal{M}_{g}
).

(2)

\mathcal{G}^{1}_{d}
is smooth and irreducible of dimension
\lambda(d,g,1)
if
g>1,d\geq 2
and
d\leq g+1
.

Occasionally we will use the following upper bound of the dimension of an irreducible component of
\mathcal{W}^{r}_{d}
; cf.
[
18
]
.

Proposition 1.3

(
[
18
, Proposition 2.1]
)
.

Let
d,g
and
r\geq 2
be positive integers such that
d\leq g+r-2
and let
\mathcal{W}
be an irreducible component of
\mathcal{W}^{r}_{d}
. For a general element
(p,L)\in\mathcal{W}
, let
b
be the degree of the base locus of the line bundle
L
on
C=\xi^{-1}(p)
. Assume further that for a general
(p,L)\in\mathcal{W}
the curve
C=\xi^{-1}(p)
is not hyperelliptic. If the moving part of
L
is

(a)

very ample and
r\geq 3
, then

\dim\mathcal{W}\leq 3d+g+1-5r-2b
;

(b)

birationally very ample, then

\dim\mathcal{W}\leq 3d+g-1-4r-2b
;

(c)

compounded, then

\dim\mathcal{W}\leq 2g-1+d-2r
.

Remark 1.4
.

(i)

In the Brill-Noether range
\rho(d,g,r)\geq 0
, the unique component
\mathcal{G}_{0}
of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}
(and the corresponding component
\mathcal{H}_{0}
of
\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}
as well) which dominates
\mathcal{M}
or
\mathcal{M}_{g}
is called the “principal component”.

(ii)

In the range
d\leq g+r
inside the Brill-Noether range
\rho(d,g,r)\geq 0
, the principal component
\mathcal{G}_{0}
which has the expected dimension
\lambda(d,g,r)
is one of the components of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
(cf.
[
13
, 2.1 page 70]
) and hence
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
and

\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}^{\mathcal{L}}
are
always non-empty
in the Brill-Noether range with
g-d+r\geq 0
.

Inside the family of plane curves of degree
d
in
\mathbb{P}^{2}
which is parametrized naturally by the projective space
\mathbb{P}^{N}
with
N=\frac{d(d+3)}{2}
, let
\Sigma_{d,g}\subset\mathbb{P}^{N}
be the Severi variety of plane curves of degree
d
and geometric genus
g
.
Denoting by
\mathcal{G^{\prime}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{2}_{d}
the
union of components whose general element
(p,\mathcal{D})
is such that
\mathcal{D}
is birationally very ample on
C=\xi^{-1}(p)
, we
remark that an open subset of the Severi variety
\Sigma_{d,g}
is a
\mathbb{P}\textrm{GL}(3)
-bundle over an open subset of
\mathcal{G}^{\prime}
. Therefore the irreducibility of
\Sigma_{d,g}
implies the

\mathcal{G^{\prime}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{2}_{d}

Indeed the following theorem of Harris is fundamental for our study; cf.
[
4
, Theorem 10.7 and 10.12]
or
[
14

Theorem 1.5
.

\Sigma_{d,g}
is irreducible of dimension
3d+g-1=\lambda(d,g,2)+\dim\mathbb{P}\textrm{GL}(3)
. Equivalently,

\mathcal{G}^{\prime}\subset\mathcal{G}^{2}_{d}
is irreducible of dimension
\lambda(d,g,2).

Our main approach will be to look at the residual linear series of a very ample and complete linear series
g^{r}_{d}
and leverage results on the irreducibility of the Hurwitz spaces and Severi varieties. As a matter of fact, the cases accessible by this strategy are precisely the cases

, which is why these cases are the focus of the present paper.

As was pointed out by the referee, the irreducibility of Hurwitz spaces and Severi varieties is the only essential point at which the characteristic zero
assumption enters the proof.
In this regard, it is worthwhile to let the readers know that there is a preprint by Christ, He and Tyomkin claiming the irreducibility of Severi varieties in positive characteristic; cf.

2.
Existence of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}

In this section we prove the following theorem which provides the full range of the genus
g
for which
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}\neq\emptyset
and irreducible.

Theorem 2.1
.

(i)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
is non-empty, irreducible, generically smooth of the expected dimension

3g-3+\rho(g+r-2,g,r)+\dim\mathbb{P}\textrm{GL}(r+1)

and has the expected number of moduli for every
g\geq r+3
.

(ii)

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}=\emptyset

if
g\leq r+2
.

Proof.

(i) The irreducibility of a non-empty
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
was shown already in
[
6
, Theorem 2.3]
and the existence in the Brill-Noether range
\rho(g+r-2,g,r)=g-2(r+1)\geq 0
is assured by Remark
1.4
(ii). Therefore we work in the range

g\leq 2r+1
which is outside the Brill-Noether range.

We briefly recall the procedure in
[
6
, Theorem 2.3]
showing the irreducibility of
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
(and hence the irreducibility of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
) under the assumption that there exists of a component
{\mathcal{G}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{r}_{g+r-2}
.
The dimension estimate

\dim\mathcal{G}=\lambda(g+r-2,g,r)=4g-2r-5

is carried out by the inequalities in
Proposition
1.3
(a) and Proposition
1.2
(1).
Let
\mathcal{W}\subset\mathcal{W}^{r}_{g+r-2}
be the component containing the image of the natural rational map

{\mathcal{G}}\overset{\iota}{\dashrightarrow}\mathcal{W}^{r}_{g+r-2}
with
\iota(\mathcal{D})=|\mathcal{D}|
.
Denoting by

\mathcal{W}^{\vee}\subset\mathcal{W}^{1}_{g-r}
the locus consisting of the residual series (with respect to the canonical series on the corresponding curve)
of those elements in
\mathcal{W}
, i.e.

\mathcal{W}^{\vee}=\{(p,\omega_{C}\otimes L^{-1}):(p,L)\in\mathcal{W}\},

we let
\widetilde{\mathcal{W}}^{\vee}\subset\mathcal{W}^{1}_{g-r}
be the union of those components
\mathcal{W}^{\vee}
corresponding to
\mathcal{W}
arising from each component
{\mathcal{G}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
.
By the above dimension estimate, we have

\dim\mathcal{W}^{\vee}=\dim\mathcal{W}=\dim\mathcal{G}=4g-2r-5=\lambda(g-r,g,1)=\dim\mathcal{G}^{1}_{g-r}.

It was shown eventually that
\widetilde{\mathcal{W}}^{\vee}
is birationally equivalent to the irreducible locus
\mathcal{G}^{1}_{g-r}
(cf. Proposition
1.2
(2)), hence
\widetilde{\mathcal{W}}^{\vee}
is irreducible and so is
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}
.

In order to demonstrate the existence of a component
{\mathcal{G}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{r}_{g+r-2}
, it is enough to show that for a general
(p,g^{1}_{g-r})\in\mathcal{G}^{1}_{g-r}
– which is non-empty (cf.
[
4
, Proposition 6.8, p.811]
) – the residual
series
\mathcal{D}=|K_{C}-g^{1}_{g-r}|
is a very ample (and complete)
g^{r}_{g+r-2}
on
C=\xi^{-1}(p)
.
Note that
\mathcal{D}=|K_{C}-g^{1}_{g-r}|
is very ample if and only if

\dim|g^{1}_{g-r}+u+v|=\dim|g^{1}_{g-r}|=1

for every
u+v\in C_{2}
. Since

\rho(g+r-2,g,r)=\rho(g-r,g,1)=g-2(r+1)<0,

\mathcal{G}^{1}_{g-r}
dominates the proper irreducible closed sub-locus
\mathcal{M}^{1}_{g,g-r}\subset\mathcal{M}_{g}
generically consisting of general
(g-r)
-gonal curves with a unique base-point-free and complete pencil
g^{1}_{g-r}
. Recall that the Clifford index
e
of a general
(e+2)
-gonal curve of genus
g\geq 2e+2
is computed only by the unique pencil computing the gonality as long as
e\neq 0
, i.e. there does not exist a
g^{s}_{2s+e}
with
2s+e\leq g-1
,
s\geq 2
; cf.
[
5
, Theorem]
or

[
20
, Corollary 1]
.
Taking
e+2=g-r
,
\deg|g^{1}_{g-r}+u+v|=g-r+2=e+4
and hence
\dim|g^{1}_{g-r}+u+v|=1
for any
u+v\in C_{2}
.

Finally one may argue that
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
is generically smooth as follows. Note that a component
\mathcal{H}
of the Hilbert scheme
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}
is generically smooth if and only if the the corresponding component
{\mathcal{G}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{r}_{d}
is generically smooth. Since we have a sequence of birational maps

\mathcal{G}=\widetilde{\mathcal{G}_{\mathcal{L}}}\dashrightarrow\mathcal{W}\dashrightarrow{\mathcal{W}}^{\vee}\overset{\kappa}{\dashrightarrow}\mathcal{G}^{1}_{g-r},

\mathcal{G}
is birationally equivalent to
\mathcal{G}^{1}_{g-r}
which is generically smooth by Proposition
1.2
(2); cf.
[
4
, Proosition 6.8, p.811]
or
[
2
, Proposition 2.7]
. Therefore

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
is generically smooth.

We
recall that a component
\mathcal{H}\subset\mathcal{H}_{d,g,r}
is said to have the expected number of moduli if the image of natural functorial map
\mathcal{H}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi}}{{\dasharrow}}\mathcal{M}_{g}
has dimension

\min(3g-3,3g-3+\rho(d,g,r)).

In our current situation, the image of the irreducible
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-2,g,r}
under the functorial map
\pi
is
\mathcal{M}^{1}_{g,g-r}
which has dimension

3g-3+\rho(g-r,g,1)=3g-3+\rho(g+r-2,g,r)=\dim\mathcal{G}.

The statement (ii) is the just the repetition of the statement
[
6
, Remark 2.4 (i)]
which follows easily from Castelnuovo genus bound.

3.
Existence of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
and irreducibility

In this section we turn to the next case
d=g+r-3
and extend the existence of the Hilbert scheme
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
of linearly normal curves beyond the Brill-Noether range. We show that there exists an explicit range
\Upsilon\subset\mathbb{N}^{\oplus 3}
– which will be specified later at the end of the section – such that

\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{d,g,r}\neq\emptyset\textrm{ if and only if }(d,g,r)=(g+r-3,g,r)\in\Upsilon\subset\mathbb{N}^{\oplus 3}.

We also determine the reducibility (or irreducibility) of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
for every
(g+r-3,g,r)\in\Upsilon
especially in the range

r+5\leq g\leq 2r+2

whereas every non-empty
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{g+r-3,g,r}
is known to be irreducible for
g\geq 2r+3
; cf. Theorem
1.1
or
[
6
, Theorem 2.5]
.
In several places in the proofs - without explicit mention - we use the basic fact that hyperelliptic curves do not carry linear series which are both special and very ample.
We now collect several relevant known results regarding the Hilbert scheme of smooth curves in
\mathbb{P}^{3}
and
\mathbb{P}^{4}
.

Remark 3.1

(ii)

Proof.

(i) Let
C\subset\mathbb{P}^{r}
(

g=r+6
with very ample hyperplane series
\mathcal{D}=g^{r}_{2r+3}
. We may assume that
\mathcal{D}
is complete, otherwise we have

g=r+6\leq\pi(2r+3,r+1)=r+4

which is an absurdity. Consider the residual series
\mathcal{E}=|K_{C}-\mathcal{D}|=g^{2}_{7}
. Note that

g=r+6\gneqq\frac{(e-1)(e-2)}{2}

. Hence
\mathcal{E}
is compounded, with the non-empty base locus

and

is either

(a)

trigonal with
\mathcal{E}=|2g^{1}_{3}+q|
or

(b)

bielliptic with
\mathcal{E}=|\phi^{*}(g^{2}_{3})+q|
, where

E

If

q

\dim|\mathcal{D}-t-s|=\dim|K-2g^{1}_{3}-q-t-s|=\dim|K-3g^{1}_{3}|\geq r-1.

If

\dim|\mathcal{E}+\phi^{*}(u)|=\dim|\phi^{*}(g^{2}_{3}+u)+q|\geq 3,

\mathcal{D}

(1)

For the next case

Theorem 3.4

(i)

(ii)

For

Proof.

(i) We deal with the case

\mathcal{D}=g^{r}_{2r+4}\in\mathcal{G}\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{L}}\subset\mathcal{G}^{r}_{2r+4}

g=r+7\gneqq 21=\frac{(8-1)(8-2)}{2}\quad\textrm{for}\quad r\geq 15.

Let
\delta
be the degree of the base locus
\Delta
of the compounded
\mathcal{E}
. We list up all the possible cases as follows.

(1)

\delta=0
,
\mathcal{E}=2g^{1}_{4}
and
C
is
4
-gonal.
Recall that on a general
4
-gonal curve
C
, there is a unique
g^{1}_{4}
and

|K_{C}-2g^{1}_{4}|=g^{r}_{2r+4}

is very ample by

\mathcal{G}
dominates the irreducible locus
\mathcal{M}^{1}_{g,4}
and
\dim\mathcal{G}=\dim\mathcal{M}^{1}_{g,4}
.
Since

\lambda(d,g,r)=3g-3+\rho(d,g,r)=r+22\lneqq\dim\mathcal{M}^{1}_{g,4}=2g+3=2r+17

the component
\mathcal{G}
dominating
\mathcal{M}^{1}_{g,4}
as well as the corresponding component of
\mathcal{H}^{\mathcal{L}}_{2r+4,r+7,r}
over
\mathcal{G}
-- which we denote

by
\mathcal{H}_{4,0}

(2)

\delta=0
,
C
is a double cover
C\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi}}{{\rightarrow}}E
of a non-hyperelliptic curve

\mathcal{E}=\pi^{*}(g^{2}_{4})=\pi^{*}(|K_{E}|)

Let

Recall that for a covering

and a line bundle

on the base curve
E
of genus

\displaystyle H^{0}(C,\pi^{*}M)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle H^{0}(E,\pi_{*}\pi^{*}M)=H^{0}(E,\pi_{*}(\pi^{*}M\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{C}}\mathcal{O}_{C}))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle H^{0}(E,M\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{E}}\pi_{*}\mathcal{O}_{C}),
