Abstract
. Our goal in this paper is to prove the global existence of a classical solution for the isentropic nozzle flow. Regarding this problem, there exist some global existence theorems of weak solutions. However, that of classical solutions does not have much attention until now. When we consider the present problem, the main difficulty is to obtain the uniform bound of solutions and their derivatives. To solve this, we introduce an invariant region depending on the space variable and a functional satisfying the Riccati equation along the characteristic lines.

Introduction

The present paper is concerned with isentropic gas flow in a nozzle on a half space
{\bf R}_{+}=\left\{x\in{\bf R};x\geq 0\right\}
.
This motion is governed by the following compressible Euler equations:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle{\rho_{t}+m_{x}=-a(x)m,}\\
\displaystyle{m_{t}+\left(\frac{m^{2}}{\rho}+p(\rho)\right)_{x}=-a(x)\frac{m^{2}}{\rho},\quad x\geq 0},\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

where
\rho
,
m
and
p
are the density, the momentum and the
pressure of the gas, respectively. If
\rho>0
,

v=m/\rho
represents the velocity of the gas. For a barotropic gas,

p(\rho)=\rho^{\gamma}/\gamma
, where
\gamma\in(1,5/3]
is the
adiabatic exponent for usual gases. The given function
a(x)
is
represented by

\displaystyle a(x)=A^{\prime}(x)/A(x),

where
A\in C^{2}({\bf R}_{+})
is a slowly variable cross section area at
x
in the nozzle satisfying
A(x)>0

We introduce the Riemann invariants

in this paper, as

Definition 1.1

\displaystyle w=\frac{m}{\rho}+\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta}=v+\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta},\quad{z}=\frac{m}{\rho}-\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta}=v-\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta}\quad\left(\theta=\dfrac{\gamma-1}{2}\right).

These Riemann invariants satisfy the following.

Remark 1.1

\displaystyle v=\frac{w+z}{2},\;\rho=\left(\frac{\theta(w-z)}{2}\right)^{1/\theta},\;m=\rho v.

From the viewpoint of application, let us review (
1.1
). In engineering, nozzles
are useful in various areas. One of the most famous nozzles is the Laval nozzle. It is
a tube that is pinched in the middle, making a hourglass-shape. The Laval nozzle
accelerates a subsonic to a supersonic flow. Because of this property, the nozzle is
widely utilized in some type of turbine, which is an essential part of the modern
rocket engine or the jet engine.

From the mathematical point of view, (
1.1
) is one of typical equations in the inhomogeneous conservation law and is categorized as the quasi-linear hyperbolic equation. Even if initial data are smooth, such a equation
has discontinuities in general.
The pioneer work in this direction is Liu
[
13
]
.
In
[
13
]
, Liu proved the existence of global weak solutions coupled with steady states, by the Glimm scheme, provided that the initial data have small total variation and
are away from the sonic state. Since then, the existence of the global weak solutions for various models have been studied, see
[
2
,
7
,
11
,
14
]
and therein. Recently, the existence theorems that include the transonic state have been obtained. The transonic stationary solutions has studied in
[
8
,
9
,
10
]
.
The author generalize the invariant region theory in
[
6
]
to proved the global existence of bounded weak solutions for the Laval nozzle
[
15
,
18
]
and the general nozzle
[
16
,
17
]
by the compensated compactness. In
[
11
]
, the authors proved the global existence of weak solutions with bounded variation for flows in general nozzle with extra outer forces.

On the other hand, the existence of classical solutions does not receive much attention until now. Recently, the authors in
[
5
]
consider the initial-boundary value problem for (
1.1
) with large
C^{0}
initial-boundary data. They establish the global existence and asymptotic behavior of classical solutions for supersonic flows through the nozzle. The work is based on the local existence, the maximum principle, and the uniform a priori estimates obtained by the generalized Lax transformations. In
[

[

to the ducts depending on both space and time. However, under the a priori estimate in
[

5
