Equivariant K K -theory and refined Vafa-Witten invariants
Richard P. Thomas

Abstract
. In [ MT2 ] the Vafa-Witten theory of complex projective surfaces is lifted to oriented ℂ ∗ \mathbb{C}^{*} -equivariant cohomology theories. Here we study the K K -theoretic refinement. It gives rational functions in t 1 / 2 t^{1/2} invariant under t 1 / 2 ↔ t − 1 / 2 t^{1/2}\leftrightarrow t^{-1/2} which specialise to numerical Vafa-Witten invariants at t = 1 t=1 . On the “instanton branch” the invariants give the virtual χ − t \chi_{-t} -genus refinement of Göttsche-Kool, extended to allow for strictly semistable sheaves. Applying modularity to their calculations gives predictions for the contribution of the “monopole branch”. We calculate some cases and find perfect agreement. We also do calculations on K3 surfaces, finding Jacobi forms refining the usual modular forms, proving a conjecture of Göttsche-Kool. We determine the K K -theoretic virtual classes of degeneracy loci using Eagon-Northcott complexes, and show they calculate refined Vafa-Witten invariants. Using this Laarakker [ La1 ] proves universality results for the invariants.

Definition 5.33

We call a point

Theorem 5.34
.

A connected component

Otherwise the cosection is nowhere zero on

Proof.

Firstly we claim that for any semistable Higgs pair

on
S
the underlying sheaf

is also semistable.

Let

be the first term of its Harder-Narasimhan filtration. Therefore

is semistable with strictly larger reduced Hilbert polynomial (or “Gieseker slope”) than the other graded pieces of the filtration. Since those pieces are also semistable, it follows that

\operatorname{Hom}(F,E/F)\ =\ 0.

Therefore the Higgs field
\phi
preserves

, so

either strictly destabilises
(E,\phi)
or is all of
E
.

In particular each

in the weight space decomposition of

is semistable (and so torsion-free) of the same reduced Hilbert polynomial. Therefore if
\phi\colon E_{i}\to E_{i+1}
is injective and generically surjective, it is an isomorphism.

It therefore follows from Theorem
5.23
that any closed point of the zero locus
Z(\dot{\varphi})
of the cosection is uniform in the sense of Definition
5.33
. Since being uniform is an open condition, while the zero locus
Z(\dot{\varphi})
is closed, a single uniform point makes the whole connected component

uniform.
In this case the cosection vanishes identically on

by Remark

∎

\sum_{k=-1}^{\infty}t^{-k-1}\chi_{-t}(\operatorname{Hilb}^{k+1}S)\hskip 0.6ptq^{k}\ =\ \widetilde{\Delta}(q,t)^{-1}.

By the results of Göttsche-Soergel
[
GS
]
, it is the unique Jacobi cusp form of index 10 and weight 1,

(5.47)

\widetilde{\Delta}(q,t)\ :=\ q\prod_{k=1}^{\infty}(1-q^{k})^{20}(1-tq^{k})^{2}(1-t^{-1}q^{k})^{2}.

It specialises at
t=1
to the modular form
\eta(q)^{24}
.

Taking only powers
k=\frac{r}{d}m
of
q
divisible by the integer
r/d
on both sides of this formula, and substituting
t^{d}
for
t
, gives

\sum_{m}t^{-mr-d}\chi_{-t^{d}}(\operatorname{Hilb}^{\frac{r}{d}m+1}S)q^{\frac{r}{d}m}\ =\ \frac{d}{r}\sum_{j=0}^{r/d-1}\widetilde{\Delta}(e^{2dj\pi i/r}q,t^{d})^{-1}.

Substituting in (
5.46
) gives

Corollary 5.48
.

If
\mathcal{O}_{S}(1)
is generic then

\sum_{n}\mathsf{VW}_{r,n}(t)\hskip 0.6ptq^{n}\ =\ \sum_{d|r}\frac{1}{[d]_{t}^{2}}\frac{d}{r}\,q^{r}\!\sum_{j=0}^{r/d-1}\widetilde{\Delta}\big(e^{2dj\pi i/r}q^{\frac{d^{2}}{r}},t^{d}\big)^{-1},

where
\widetilde{\Delta}
is given by (
5.47
).

When
r
is prime this simplifies to

\sum_{n}\mathsf{VW}_{r,n}(t)\hskip 0.6ptq^{n}\ =\ \frac{1}{[r]_{t}^{2}}\,q^{r}\,\widetilde{\Delta}\big(q^{r},t^{r}\big)^{-1}+\frac{1}{r}q^{r}\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\widetilde{\Delta}\big(e^{2j\pi i/r}q^{\frac{1}{r}},t\big)^{-1},

proving a conjecture of Göttsche-Kool
[
GK3
]
.

Finally we describe the refinement of a simple calculation on general type surfaces
S
from
[
TT2
, Section 6.3]
. For more recent and much more general results we refer to the discussion of Laarakker’s results
[
La2
]
in the Introduction.

Take a surface
S
with
h^{0,1}(S)=0
and
h^{0,2}(S)>0
and charge
\alpha=(2,0,0)\in H^{*}(S)
. The
\mathbb{C}^{*}
-fixed semistable sheaves on
X=K_{S}
are

(a)

(the pushforward from
S
of)
\mathcal{O}_{S}^{\oplus 2}
, and

(b)

(the pushforward from
2S\subset X
of)
I_{C\subset 2S}\otimes K_{S}
, for any
C\subset S\subset 2S\subset X
in the canonical linear system
|K_{S}|
.

Taking sections twisted by
\mathcal{O}(n)
for
n\gg 0
(using in (b) the fact that the pushdown to
S
of
I_{C\subset 2S}\otimes K_{S}
is
\mathcal{O}_{S}\oplus\mathcal{O}_{S}\mathfrak{t}^{-1}
), imposing stability and dividing by the automorphism group of the sheaf, we find the following. The moduli space of
\mathbb{C}^{*}
-fixed stable Joyce-Song pairs has two components,

(a)

\operatorname{Gr}(2,\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))
of pairs with underlying sheaf
\mathcal{O}_{S}^{\oplus 2}
, and

(b)

\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))\times\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))
of pairs with underlying sheaf
I_{C\subset 2S}K_{S}
.

The first component has a trivial
H^{2}(\mathcal{O}_{S})
piece in the obstruction bundle
\curly Ext^{2}_{p_{X}}(I^{\bullet},I^{\bullet})_{\perp}
, so it contributes nothing to the invariants. The second component is shown in
[
TT2
, Section 6.3]
to have fixed obstruction bundle

(5.49)

\mathrm{Ob}\ \cong\ T^{*}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))}

pulled back from
\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))
,
and virtual normal bundle

(5.50)

N^{\operatorname{vir}}\ =\ T_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\mathfrak{t}^{-1}\ \oplus\ T_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))}\mathfrak{t}\ \oplus\ \Gamma(K_{S})\!\otimes\!\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))}(1)\mathfrak{t}^{2}\\
-\ T^{*}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))\times\mathbb{P}(H^{0}(K_{S}))}\mathfrak{t}\ -\ T^{*}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\mathfrak{t}^{2}\ -\ \Gamma(K_{S})^{*}\otimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))}(1)\mathfrak{t}^{-1}.

A generic element of
\operatorname{End}_{0}\Gamma(K_{S})\cong H^{0}(T_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))})
with distinct eigenvalues gives a vector field on
\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))
with
p_{g}(S)
distinct zeros, and so a vector field on
\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))\times\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(K_{S}))
whose zero locus is
p_{g}(S)
distinct
\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))
fibres. By (
5.49
) the corresponding Koszul resolution gives an equality in
K
-theory

\mathcal{O}^{\operatorname{vir}}\ =\ \Lambda^{\bullet}\hskip 0.6pt\mathrm{Ob}^{*}\ =\ (-1)^{p_{g}(S)-1}p_{g}(S)\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))},

so that the refined pairs invariant is

(5.51)

P^{\perp}_{\alpha}(n,t)\ =\ (-1)^{p_{g}(S)-1}p_{g}(S)\cdot\chi_{t}\!\left(\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))),\frac{K_{\operatorname{vir}}^{1/2}}{\Lambda^{\bullet}(N^{\operatorname{vir}})^{\vee}}\right).

On a
\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))
fibre the virtual normal bundle (
5.50
) simplifies to

N^{\operatorname{vir}}\ =\ \Psi-\Psi^{\vee}\ \oplus\ (\mathfrak{t}^{2})^{\oplus p_{g}(S)}\\
-\ T^{*}_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\mathfrak{t}^{2}\ -\ (\mathfrak{t}^{-1})^{\oplus p_{g}(S)},

where
\Psi:=T_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\mathfrak{t}^{-1}
.
Combined with (
5.49
) this means
K_{\operatorname{vir}}
is

K_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\otimes(\det\Psi)^{-2}\mathfrak{t}^{-2p_{g}(S)}\otimes K_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\mathfrak{t}^{2\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))-2}\mathfrak{t}^{-p_{g}(S)}

with square root

K_{\operatorname{vir}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\ =\ K_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\otimes(\det\Psi)^{-1}\mathfrak{t}^{-3p_{g}(S)/2}\mathfrak{t}^{\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))-1}.

So we now calculate (
5.51
) to be

(-1)^{p_{g}(S)-1}p_{g}(S)t^{\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))-1-3p_{g}(S)/2}\frac{(1-t)^{p_{g}(S)}}{(1-t^{-2})^{p_{g}(S)}}\times\\
\chi_{t}\!\left(\frac{\Lambda^{\bullet}\Psi\otimes\Lambda^{\bullet}\hskip 0.6pt(T_{\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))}\mathfrak{t}^{-2})K_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}}{\det\Psi\otimes\Lambda^{\bullet}\Psi^{\vee}}\right)\!.

By two applications of the identity (
2.28
) (and recalling that
\operatorname{rank}\Psi=\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))-1
) this gives

-p_{g}(S)\frac{t^{\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))-1+p_{g}(S)/2}}{(1+t)^{p_{g}(S)}}\chi_{t}(\Lambda^{\bullet}\hskip 0.6pt(\Omega_{\mathbb{P}(\Gamma(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)))}\mathfrak{t}^{2})t^{-2\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))+2}\\
\ =\ -\frac{p_{g}(S)}{[2]_{t}^{p_{g}(S)}}\big[\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n))\big]_{t^{2}}\,.

Since

P^{\perp}_{\alpha}(n,t)\ =\ -p_{g}(S)\frac{[2\chi(\mathcal{O}_{S}(n)]_{t}}{[2]_{t}^{p_{g}(S)+1}}\,.

This fits Conjecture (
5.2

\mathsf{VW}_{\alpha}(t)\ =\ \frac{p_{g}(S)}{[2]_{t}^{p_{g}(S)+1}}\,.
