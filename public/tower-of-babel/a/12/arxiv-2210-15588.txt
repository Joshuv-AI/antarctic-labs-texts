Anatomy of Λ c + \Lambda_{c}^{+} semileptonic decays
Chao-Qiang Geng, Xiang-Nan Jin and Chia-Wei Liu

Abstract
We present a systematic study of Λ c + → ℬ q ​ ℓ + ​ ν ℓ \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to{\cal B}_{q}\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell} with ℬ q = ( Λ , n ) {\cal B}_{q}=(\Lambda,n) and ℓ = ( e , μ ) \ell=(e,\mu) , examining all the possible decay observables based on the homogeneous bag model (HBM) and lattice QCD (LQCD). With the HBM, we find that the branching fractions and polarization asymmetries of the daughter baryon Λ \Lambda are ℬ ( Λ c + → Λ e + ν e , Λ μ + ν μ , n ℓ + ν ℓ ) = ( 3.78 ± 0.25 , 3.67 ± 0.23 , 0.40 ± 0.04 ) % {\cal B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e},\Lambda\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu},n\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell})=(3.78\pm 0.25,3.67\pm 0.23,0.40\pm 0.04)\% and α Λ ( Λ c + → Λ e + ν e , Λ μ + ν μ ) = ( − 82.6 , − 82.3 ) % \alpha_{\Lambda}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e},\Lambda\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu})=(-82.6,-82.3)\% , respectively. We also explore the time-reversal asymmetries due to new physics beyond the standard model. All our results are consistent with the current experimental data, while some of them are accessible to the experiments at BESIII and Belle II.

IV
TIME REVERSAL ASYMMETRIES AND NEW PHYSICS

In general, contaminated by the hadron uncertainties, it is often difficult to draw a sharp conclusion on whether NP is needed to explain the experimental data.
Nevertheless, NP can generate clear signals of T asymmetries, which vanish in the SM and are equivalent to the CP asymmetries based on the CPT theorem. A great advantage of T violation is that a strong phase is not necessary. The simplest T violating observables are studied in Ref.
Timereversal
,
and we highlight some the results here.

Two of the simplest T asymmetries in
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda(\to p\pi^{-})\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell}
are defined as

\displaystyle{\cal T}_{\ell}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{P_{b}}\left(\int^{\pi}_{0}-\int_{\pi}^{2\pi}\right)\Gamma^{\ell}_{\phi_{\ell}}d\phi_{\ell}\quad=-\frac{\pi^{2}}{8\Gamma^{\ell}}\int_{M_{\ell}^{2}}^{M_{-}^{2}}\zeta{\bf\text{Im}}({\cal X}_{7})dq^{2}\,,

\displaystyle{\cal T}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\alpha}\left[\left(\int^{\pi}_{0}-\int_{\pi}^{2\pi}\right)d\Phi\right]\left[\left(\int_{0}^{1}-\int^{0}_{-1}\right)d\cos\theta_{s}\right]\Gamma^{\ell}_{\phi_{\ell},\cos\theta_{s}}

(37)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{2}{3\pi\Gamma^{\ell}}\int_{M_{\ell}^{2}}^{M_{-}^{2}}\zeta{\bf\text{Im}}({\cal X}_{12})dq^{2}\,,

which are closely related to the azimuthal angles. The reason is that they correspond to the triple product asymmetries of the three-momenta in the final states. Keeping in mind
\xi_{\pm}
are real in the SM, it is easy to see that nonvanishing
{\cal T}_{\ell,s}
require NP beyond the SM.

As an illustration, we explore NP with the effective Hamiltonian as

{\cal H}^{\text{NP}}_{eff}=\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{cs}\left[C_{L}\overline{s}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})c+C_{R}\overline{s}\gamma_{\mu}(1+\gamma_{5})c\right]\overline{v}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})u\,.

(38)

V
Summary

We have given a systematical study on all the possible observables in
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
semileptonic decays, including the effects of NP.
The model independent angular distributions with polarized
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
have been presented.
We have found that the BM underestimate
f_{2}
by a factor of two third, where the same underestimations were also found in the magnetic dipole moments of the octet baryons.
The branching fractions and polarization asymmetries have been found to be
{\cal B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e},\Lambda\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu},n\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell})=(3.78\pm 0.25,3.67\pm 0.23,0.40\pm 0.04)\%
,and
\alpha_{\Lambda}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e},\Lambda\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu})=(-82.6,-82.3)\%
from the HBM,
and
\alpha_{\Lambda}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e},\Lambda\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu})=(-87.4\pm 1.0,-87.2\pm 1.0)\%
from the LQCD,
which can be tested in the near future at BESIII and Belle II.
In particular,
{\cal B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e},\Lambda\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu})
and
\alpha_{\Lambda}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e})
are in good agreement with the current experimental data
BESIII:2016ffj
;
BESIII:2022ysa
.
All the angular observables in the SM have been computed by both the HBM and LQCD.
Most of the results from the two approaches have been shown to be consistent.
The effects of NP on the T asymmetries have been explored, found to be
{\cal O}(10\%)
for Im
(C_{R})=0.2
. We stress that nonzero values of T asymmetries in the experiments will be a smoking gun of NP.
