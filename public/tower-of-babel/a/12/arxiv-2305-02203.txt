Abstract

Using this fine tuning we discuss the rainbow gravity effect on Klein-Gordon (KG) oscillators in Som-Raychaudhuri cosmic string rainbow gravity spacetime background. We show that the energies obtained using the first two rainbow functions in (i) completely comply with the rainbow gravity model (on the invariance of the Planck’s energy scale E p E_{p} ).
