Lemma 2.1
.

Let

be subgroups of

(1) If

(3) If

Proof.

(1) By definition,

(2) Obviously,
K\cap N_{\mathscr{F}}(G,H)=K\cap(\underset{T\leq H}{\bigcap}{N_{G}(T^{\mathscr{F}})})\leq\underset{T\leq H\cap K}{\bigcap}{N_{K}(T^{\mathscr{F}})}=N_{\mathscr{F}}(K,H\cap K)

H\leq K
, then

.

(3) Let

, then

for every

, so

normalize

for all subgroups

Theorem 3.4
