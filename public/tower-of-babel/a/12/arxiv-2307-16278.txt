II
The Model

We would like to construct a model of a spherical black hole
whose interior space is filled with matter. Assuming there is nothing else in spacetime, the stress-energy-momentum tensor
T_{\mu\nu}
is zero outside the black hole,
but it is non-zero inside where matter is. Black hole matter is assumed to be an ideal fluid with

\displaystyle T_{\mu\nu}=(p+\rho)U_{\mu}U_{\nu}+pg_{\mu\nu},

(1)

U^{\mu}U_{\mu}=-1

\rho

the pressure determined by the equation of state
p=-\xi\rho

\xi<0

\xi>0
