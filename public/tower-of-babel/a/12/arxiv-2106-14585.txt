Factoring Variants of Chebyshev Polynomials with Minimal Polynomials of cos ⁡ ( 2 ​ π d ) \cos(\frac{2\pi}{d})
D.A. Wolfram

Abstract
We solve the problem of factoring polynomials V n ​ ( x ) ± 1 V_{n}(x)\pm 1 and W n ​ ( x ) ± 1 W_{n}(x)\pm 1 where V n ​ ( x ) V_{n}(x) and W n ​ ( x ) W_{n}(x) are Chebyshev polynomials of the third and fourth kinds. The method of proof is based on previous work by Wolfram [ 12 ] for factoring variants of Chebyshev polynomials of the first and second kinds, T n ​ ( x ) ± 1 T_{n}(x)\pm 1 and U n ​ ( x ) ± 1 U_{n}(x)\pm 1 . We also show that, in general, there are no factorizations of variants of Chebyshev polynomials of the fifth and sixth kinds, X n ​ ( x ) ± 1 X_{n}(x)\pm 1 and Y n ​ ( x ) ± 1 Y_{n}(x)\pm 1 using minimal polynomials of cos ⁡ ( 2 ​ π d ) \cos(\frac{2\pi}{d}) .

The method of solution follows that by Wolfram
[

]

Lemma 1
.

Where

V_{n}(x)^{2}-1=\Psi_{1}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid 2n\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid 2n+2\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)

(10)

W_{n}(x)^{2}-1=\Psi_{2}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid 2n\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid 2n+2\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x).

(11)

Proof.

From equation (
8
), we have

\displaystyle V_{n}(x)^{2}-1=

\displaystyle(U_{n}(x)-U_{n-1}(x)+1)(U_{n}(x)-U_{n-1}(x)-1)\mbox{ from equation (\ref{VU})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n}(x)^{2}-1-2U_{n}(x)U_{n-1}(x)+U_{n-1}(x)^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n+1}(x)U_{n-1}(x)-2U_{n}(x)U_{n-1}(x)+U_{n-1}(x)^{2}\mbox{ from equation (\ref{UTuran})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n-1}(x)(U_{n+1}(x)-2U_{n}(x)+U_{n-1}(x))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n-1}(x)(2xU_{n}(x)-2U_{n}(x))\mbox{ from equation (\ref{Urec})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Psi_{1}(x)U_{n-1}(x)U_{n}(x)\mbox{ from Definition \ref{def1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Psi_{1}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid 2n\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d|2n+2\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)\mbox{ from equation (\ref{UPsi}).}

Similarly, we have

\displaystyle W_{n}(x)^{2}-1=

\displaystyle(U_{n}(x)+U_{n-1}(x)+1)(U_{n}(x)+U_{n-1}(x)-1)\mbox{ from equation (\ref{WU})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n}(x)^{2}-1+2U_{n}(x)U_{n-1}(x)+U_{n-1}(x)^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n+1}(x)U_{n-1}(x)+2U_{n}(x)U_{n-1}(x)+U_{n-1}(x)^{2}\mbox{ from equation (\ref{UTuran})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n-1}(x)(U_{n+1}(x)+2U_{n}(x)+U_{n-1}(x))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{n-1}(x)(2xU_{n}(x)+2U_{n}(x))\mbox{ from equation (\ref{Urec})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Psi_{2}(x)U_{n-1}(x)U_{n}(x)\mbox{ from Definition \ref{def1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Psi_{2}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d\mid 2n\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)\prod_{\mathclap{\begin{subarray}{c}d|2n+2\\
d>2\end{subarray}}}\Psi_{d}(x)\mbox{ from equation (\ref{UPsi})}

as required.

6
Conclusion

We have solved the problem of factoring variants of Chebyshev polynomials of the third and fourth kinds,
V_{n}(x)\pm 1
and
W_{n}(x)\pm 1
, in terms of minimal polynomials for
\cos(\frac{2\pi}{d})
. This was done by applying the method of Wolfram
[
12
]
for factoring
T_{n}(x)\pm 1
and
U_{n}(x)\pm 1
in a similar way. We have shown that there are no generalizations of this factorization to similar variants of Chebyshev polynomials of the fifth and sixth kinds,
X_{n}(x)\pm 1
and
Y_{n}(x)\pm 1
.
