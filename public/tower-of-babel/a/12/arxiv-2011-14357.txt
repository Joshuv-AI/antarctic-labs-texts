Presentations of algebras in chain complexes
Brice Le Grignou

Abstract
. The goal of this article is to describe several presentations of the infinity category of algebras over some monad on the infinity category of chain complexes.



Introduction

Many categories encountered in Algebra are presentable. This means that for such a category
\mathsf{C}
, one can find a small category
\mathsf{D}
and an equivalence relating
\mathsf{C}
to the full subcategory of
\mathrm{Fun}\left(\mathsf{D}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{Set}\right)
spanned by functors
F:\mathsf{D}\to\mathsf{Set}
that satisfy some locality conditions.

Finite products theories also called Props provides many examples of such presentable categories in Algebra. For instance, if
R
is a commutative ring, the category of
R
-modules may be presented as follows. Let
\mathsf{Cart}_{R}
be the full subcategory of
R
-modules spanned by those of the form
R^{n}
for
n\in\mathbb N
. Then the category of
R
-modules is equivalent to the full subcategory of
\mathrm{Fun}\left(\mathsf{Cart}_{R}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{Set}\right)
spanned by functors that preserves finite products.

Moreover, since
R
-modules are equivalent to such products preserving functors, then many categories of algebras in
R
-modules have a similar presentation. Indeed, let us consider a monadic adjunction

\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces U^{M}}

whose associated monad

preserves reflective coequalisers and filtered colimits, and let

be the full subcategory of
\mathsf{A}
spanned by objects of the form

for

. Then the category
\mathsf{A}
is equivalent to the full subcategory of
\mathrm{Fun}\left(\mathsf{Cart}_{M}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{Set}\right)
spanned by functors that preserves finite products.

These results have extensions in the world of

Remark 1

From

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\varinjlim\mathrm{Map}_{\mathsf{Psh}(\mathsf{D})}\left(Y,D\right)}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\varinjlim\mathrm{Map}_{\mathsf{Psh}(\mathsf{D})}\left(X,D\right)}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{Map}_{\mathsf{Psh}(\mathsf{D})}\left(Y,\varinjlim D\right)}

Definition 21

Let

R^{n}_{k}

Proposition 24
.

The
\infty
-category
\mathsf{Ch}_{R}^{\geq 0}
is equivalent to
\mathsf{Psh}_{\Sigma}(\mathsf{Cart}^{(K)}_{R})
.

Proof.

Let
\mathsf{Fun}_{\Sigma}\left((\mathsf{Cart}^{(K)}_{R})^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{sSet}\right)
the full subcategory of
\mathrm{Fun}\left((\mathsf{Cart}^{(K)}_{R})^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{sSet}\right)
spanned by functors that preserves finite products.
We know from
[
Qui67
]
that it admits a combinatorial model structure whose weak equivalences are objectwise Kan-Quillen weak equivalence and whose fibrations are objectwise Kan-Quillen fibrations. Moreover, we know from

Lur17

that the
resulting
\infty

\mathsf{Psh}_{\Sigma}(\mathsf{Cart}^{(K)}_{R})

To conclude, the Dold Kan correspondence, gives us
equivalences of model categories

\mathsf{Fun}_{\Sigma}\left((\mathsf{Cart}^{(K)}_{R})^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{sSet}\right)\simeq{R}\mathrm{-}\mathsf{mod}^{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\times K}\simeq\mathsf{Chain}_{R}^{\geq 0,K}.

∎
