Abstract
. The current research is devoted to find an affirmative answer to an open question related to ( s , r ) − (s,r)- contractive operators which have been introduced by Ovidiu Popescu.

1.
Intoduction

Let
(X,d)
be a metric space,
\mathcal{P}(X)
the set of all nonempty subset of
X
,
\mathcal{CB}(X)
denote the
classes of all non empty, closed and bounded subsets of
X
, and let
\mathcal{CL}(X)
the set of all closed subsets of
X
. Let

T:X\to\mathcal{CB}(X)
be a multi-valued mapping on
X
. A point

x\in X
is called a fixed point of
T
if
x\in Tx
. Let

Fix(T)=\{x\in X:x\in Tx\}
. An element
x\in X
is said to be an

strict fixed point
(endpoint) of
T
, if
Tx=\{x\}
. Let

Fix(T)=\{x\in X:x\in Tx\}
(see
[
4
]
for more detail).

Let
\mathcal{H}
be the Pompeiu-Hausdorff
[
2
]
metric on
\mathcal{CB}(X)
induced by
d
,
that is,

(1.1)

\mathcal{H}(A,B):=\max\Big\{\sup_{x\in B}d(x,A),\sup_{x\in A}d(x,B)\Big\},\quad A,B\in\mathcal{CB}(X).

in which,
d(x,A)=\inf\{d(x,y):y\in A\}

In 2007, Suzuki
[
6
]
introduced the following theorem as a simple generalization of Banach contraction principle.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
(X,d)
be a complete metric space and let
T
be a mapping on
X
. Define a non increasing function
\theta
from
[0,1)
into
(\frac{1}{2},1]
by

\theta(r)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}1&&0\leqslant r<\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\\
\frac{1-r}{r^{2}}&&\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\leqslant r<\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\
\frac{1}{1+r}&&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\leqslant r<1\end{array}\right.

Assume that there exists
r\in[0,1)
such that

(1.2)

\theta(r)d(x,Tx)\leqslant d(x,y)\ \ \ \ implies\ \ \ \ d(Tx,Ty)\leqslant rd(x,y)

for all
x,y\in X
. Then there exists a unique fixed point
z
of
T
. Moreover,
\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}T^{n}(x)=z
, for all
x\in X
.

Definition 1.2
.

Let
(X,d)
be a metric space and
T:X\to\mathcal{CL}(X)
a multi-valued operator.
T
is called Multi-valued Weakly Picard operator (
\mathcal{MWP}
) if for all
x\in X
and
y\in Tx
, there exists a sequence
\{x_{n}\}_{n}
such that:

(a_{1})

x_{0}=x
,
x_{1}=y
,

(a_{2})

x_{n+1}\in Tx_{n}
, for all
n\geqslant 0
,

(a_{3})

the sequence
\{x_{n}\}_{n}
is convergent and its limit is a fixed point of
T
.

In 2013, Popescu
[
1
]
introduced
(s,r)-
contractive multi-valued operators as follows:

Definition 1.3
.

[
1
, Definition 1.7]

Let
(X,d)
be a complete metric space and let
T
be a mapping from
X
into
\mathcal{CB}(X)
.
T
is called an
(s,r)-
contractive multi-valued operator if
r\in[0,1)
,
s\geqslant r
and for every
x,y\in X

D(y,Tx)\leqslant sd(y,x)\ \ \ implies\ \ \ \mathcal{H}(Tx,Ty)\leqslant r\mathcal{M}_{T}(x,y)

in which

(1.3)

\mathcal{M}_{T}(x,y)=\max\left\{d(x,y),D(x,Tx),D(y,Ty),\frac{D(x,Ty)+D(y,Tx)}{2}\right\}

After that, he presented the following results

Definition 1.4
.

[
1
, Definition 4.2]

Let
(X,d)
be a metric space,
Y\in P(X)
and
T:X\to\mathcal{CL}(X)
be a multi-valued operator. Then, we say that the fixed point problem is well-posed for
T
with respect to
\mathcal{H}
if

(a_{1})

SFix(T)=\{z\}
;

(a_{2})

If
\{x_{n}\}\in Y
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
\mathcal{H}(x_{n},Tx_{n})\to 0
as
n\to\infty
, then
d(x_{n},z)\to 0
as
n\to\infty
.

Theorem 1.5
.

[
1
, Theorem 4.4]

Let
(X,d)
be a complete metric space and
T:X\to\mathcal{CL}(X)
be a multi-valued operator. We suppose that:

(i)

T
is an
(s,r)-
contractive multi valued operator with
s\geqslant 1
;

(ii)

SFix(T)\neq\emptyset
.

Then:

(a)

Fix(T)=SFix(T)=\{z\}
,

(b)

the fixed point problem is well-posed for
T
with respect to
\mathcal{H}
if
s>1
.

In Popescu’s paper, the Theorem
1.5
gave rise to the following open question:

Theorem 1.6
.

(Open Question)
Let
(X,d)
be a complete metric space and
T:X\to\mathcal{CL}(X)
be a multi-valued operator. We suppose that:

(i)

T
is an
(1,r)-
contractive multi valued operator;

(ii)

SFix(T)\neq\emptyset
.

Then is the fixed point problem is well-posed for
T
with respect to
\mathcal{H}
if
s=1
?

2.
Main Results

In this section, we want to give affirmative answer to the Open Question
1.6
.

Theorem 2.1
.

(Answer to Open Question
1.6
)
Let
(X,d)
be a complete metric space and
T:X\to\mathcal{CL}(X)
be a multi-valued operator. We suppose that:

(i)

T
is an
(1,r)-
contractive multi valued operator;

(ii)

SFix(T)\neq\emptyset
.

Then:

(a)

Fix(T)=SFix(T)=\{z\}
,

(b)

the fixed point problem is well-posed for
T
with respect to
\mathcal{H}
.

Proof.

Proving
(a)
is analogous to the argument indicated in
[
1
, Theorem 4.4(a)]
and we remain it to the reader. To prove
(b)
, suppose that

\{x_{n}\}\in Y
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
\mathcal{H}(x_{n},Tx_{n})\to 0
as
n\to\infty
. We claim that
d(x_{n},z)\to 0
as
n\to\infty
.

Since
D(x_{n},Tz)=d(x_{n},z)\leqslant d(x_{n},z),(s=1)
, we have

(2.1)

\mathcal{H}(Tx_{n},\{z\})=\mathcal{H}(Tx_{n},Tz)\leqslant r\mathcal{M}_{T}(x_{n},z).

Also,

(2.2)

\begin{array}[]{lll}{\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{T}(x_{n},z)}&=&{\displaystyle\max\left\{d(x_{n},z),D(x_{n},Tx_{n}),\frac{D(x_{n},Tz)+D(z,Tx_{n})}{2}\right\}}\\
&\leqslant&{\displaystyle\max\left\{d(x_{n},z),\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}),\frac{d(x_{n},z)+\mathcal{H}(\{z\},Tx_{n})}{2}\right\}}\\
&\leqslant&{\displaystyle\max\left\{d(x_{n},z),\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}),\frac{2d(x_{n},z)+\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n})}{2}\right\}}\\
&=&{\displaystyle\max\left\{d(x_{n},z),\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}),d(x_{n},z)+\mathcal{H}(\{z\},Tx_{n})\right\}}\end{array}

(
2.1
) and (
2.2
) conclude that

(2.3)

{\displaystyle\mathcal{H}(Tx_{n},\{z\})\leqslant r(d(x_{n},z)+\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}))\leqslant r(\mathcal{H}(\{z\},Tx_{n})+2\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}))}

Therefore, (
2.3
) concludes that

\mathcal{H}(Tx_{n},\{z\})\leqslant\frac{2r}{1-r}\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}).

and so we have

(2.4)

\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathcal{H}(Tx_{n},\{z\})=0.

Moreover,

(2.5)

d(x_{n},z)\leqslant\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n})+\mathcal{H}(Tx_{n},\{z\})\leqslant\frac{1+r}{1-r}\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n}).

Applying (
2.4
) and taking limit on both sides of (
2.5
) and the fact that
\mathcal{H}(\{x_{n}\},Tx_{n})\to 0
as
n\to\infty
, one can obtain desired result and the proof is completed.
∎
