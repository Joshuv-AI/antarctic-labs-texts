Studying strange hadron productions via two-particle correlations in high energy proton-proton collisions Journal: Eur. Phys. J. A
M.N. Anaam e1, addr1, Liang Zheng e2, addr2, addr1, H.M. Alfanda e3, addr1, Zhongbao Yin e4, addr1

Abstract
Strange hadron production in pp collisions at s = 7 \sqrt{s}=7 TeV is studied with Pythia 8 8 and Epos Lhc event generators via the two-particle correlation method. After pairing charged particles as trigger particles with associated strange hadrons ( K S 0 {\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}} and Λ \Lambda ( Λ ¯ \overline{\Lambda} )), the strange baryon to meson per trigger yield ratios Y Δ ​ φ h − ( Λ + Λ ¯ ) / 2 ​ Y Δ ​ φ h − K S 0 {Y_{\Delta\varphi}^{\mathrm{h}-(\Lambda+\overline{\Lambda})}}/{2Y_{\Delta\varphi}^{\mathrm{h}-\mathrm{K}^{0}_{S}}} dependent on transverse momentum ( p T p_{\rm T} ) are investigated within the near- and away-side jet region as well as the underlying event (UE) region. The modified string fragmentation effects of color reconnection and string shoving mechanisms implemented in Pythia 8 8 and the final state evolution effects built in Epos Lhc are compared in this study. It is found that p T p_{\rm T} dependence of the Λ \Lambda / K S 0 {\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}} per trigger yield ratio in UE is similar to that in the inclusive measurements while the in-jet result is smaller. We also show that the Λ \Lambda / K S 0 {\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}} ratio at both near- and away-side jet region in the intermediate p T p_{\rm T} region decreases significantly with trigger p T p_{\rm T} , which suggests the hard process effects might contribute to the strange baryon to meson enhancement at intermediate p T p_{\rm T} . The much higher Λ \Lambda / K S 0 {\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}} ratio in UE and the striking difference between the near and away side of the in-jet results with low trigger p T p_{\rm T} predicted by the Epos Lhc model indicate that the soft process effects induced by the final state evolution further increase the strange baryon production and can be still effective in a higher p T p_{\rm T} region compared to the string fragmentation type models.

Introduction

The transverse momentum
p_{\rm T}
dependence of the strange baryon to meson
\Lambda
/
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
ratio provides a unique way to study the properties of the hot and dense, color-deconfined QCD matter called quark-gluon plasma (QGP) created at the Relativistic Heavy Ion collider (RHIC) and the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). It is observed that a pronouncing enhancement of the strange baryon to meson ratio at intermediate
p_{\rm T}
exists in nucleus-nucleus (
AA
) collisions compared to proton-proton (pp) collisions
ALICE:2013cdo
. It is believed that this anomaly strange baryon enhancement and the shift of the
\Lambda
/
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
peaks towards higher
p_{\rm T}
can be described by the introduction of the parton recombination mechanism to the hadronization process and the hydrodynamic parton radial flow induced due to the formation of the QGP phase. However, the enhanced strange baryon to meson ratio at intermediate
p_{\rm T}
, together with many other QGP like phenomena
ALICE:2016fzo
;
CMS:2010ifv
;
CMS:2016fnw
;
ALICE:2018pal
, has also been observed in high multiplicity pp and
p–Pb
collisions
ALICE:2020wfu
;
ALICE:2016fzo
;
ALICE:2021npz
;
ALICE:2013wgn
.

The similarity between the small systems and the large systems invites a lot of discussions on whether the QGP matter is formed in high multiplicity pp and
p–Pb
collisions at the LHC. Different explanations are modeled in the general purpose Monte Carlo event generators like
Pythia
8

Sjostrand:2014zea
and
Epos Lhc

Pierog:2013ria
. In
Pythia
8

Bierlich:2015rha
;
Bierlich:2016vgw
. The
Epos Lhc
model introduces the final state hydrodynamic evolution to the small system collisions by assuming that the high density region is thermalized within the core-corona picture. These models can offer similar strange baryon enhancement in the inclusive
\Lambda
/
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
ratio at intermediate
p_{\rm T}
in high multiplicity pp or
p–Pb
collisions. To further pin down the origin of this behavior in small systems, it is important to separate the contributions from soft and hard processes. The strange hadron productions have been studied with reconstructed high-
p_{\rm T}
charged particle jets to investigate the contribution of fragmentation of fast moving partons
ALICE:2021vxl
. The enhancement of charm baryon to meson ratio (
\Lambda^{+}_{\rm c}
/
{\rm D}^{0}
) is also observed in high multiplicity pp collisions
ALICE:2021npz
. Considering the charm hadrons are mostly produced through hard processes, the resemblance of
\Lambda^{+}_{\rm c}
/
{\rm D}^{0}
to the strange sector
\Lambda
/
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
uncovered in the experiment indicates the existence of modifications to the hard parton hadronization process in small systems.

Studying strange hadron correlations with other charged hadrons in different kinematic ranges can be useful to expose the effects of jet fragmentation, parton shower and hydrodynamic evolution
ALICE:2021nvv
. From previous studies, it is known that high-
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
particles are more likely to come from hard processes, such as jet fragmentation, while at intermediate
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
region the particle yield contains also contributions from soft processes like multiple parton interactions, parton radiation effects which cannot be easily separated in experiments
Strangeness1
;
Strangeness2
;
ALICE:2016fzo
;
ALICE:2021npz
;
ALICE:2020wfu
. Particles from jet fragmentation are more collimated with the jet axis, resulting in the near-side
(\Delta\varphi=0)
and the back-to-back away-side
(\Delta\varphi=\pi)
peaks, where minijet contributions with the momentum conservation are dominant
1993
. Particles from the soft production process associated with the underlying event (UE) region of the correlation function are more isotropic and only have some minor sensitivity to the hard process. One may expect significant differences between the strange hadron productions associated with low
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
and high
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
particles due to the large variations of the
z
(fraction of parton momentum) regions probed.

The goal of this study is to analyze the strange baryon and meson production in the peak and UE regions in pp collisions in order to map out the underlying physics mechanisms. The relative contribution of hard and soft processes to strangeness production in pp collisions is still not clear and can be studied through techniques involving the two-particle correlations. We explore the baryon-to-meson anomaly at low and high
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
to determine whether it is arising from the soft, collective, UE type physics, or the hard processes involving the jet fragmentation. The study is performed on
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
,
\Lambda
/
\overline{\Lambda}
with transverse momentum within

GeV/

associated with charged hadrons as trigger particles having transverse momentum within
1\,<\,p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}\,<\,6
GeV/
c
and
6\,<\,p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}\,<\,15
GeV/
c
.

2
PYTHIA and EPOS LHC event generators

Pythia
is a general purpose perturbative-QCD based Monte Carlo event generator, widely used for high-energy collider physics with an emphasis on pp collisions.
Pythia
is quite successful in describing various experimental results of pp collisions at LHC energies
ALICE:2013rdo
;
ALICE:2015olq
;
ALICE:2016jjg
;
ALICE:2015olq
. For a better description of data, different parameters of the existing
Pythia
model have been fine-tuned over time.

In this work, the study is performed using
Pythia
8
(version 8.2.4) with hadronization models considering CR and shoving. The hadronization in
Pythia
is treated using Lund string fragmentation model
Metz:2016swz
;
Christiansen:2015yqa
. In the CR picture of the model
Argyropoulos:2014zoa
;
Bierlich:2015rha
, the final partons are considered to be color connected in a way that the total string length becomes as short as possible
Gustafson:2009qz
. Within the multiple parton interaction (MPI) framework
Fedkevych:2020cmd
;
Diehl:2017wew
implemented in
Pythia
8
, a large number of final state string objects can be generated over limited transverse space in high energy collisions. The traditional string fragmentation process is expected to be modified in the high parton density environment due to the inter-string interactions
Christiansen:2015yqa
;
Sjostrand:2014zea
.

The parameters used in this study are the same of new parameters developed for LHC in Ref
Bierlich:2015rha
with respect to the default settings of the Monash Tune.

String shoving model is recently proposed to describe the strangeness enhancement and long-range correlation in high multiplicity pp collisions. The model is implemented in the DIPSY event generator. It introduces a repulsive force which allows the strings overlapped in transverse space to act coherently and generate a flow-like effect in small systems
Bierlich:2016vgw
.

Epos Lhc
is a Monte Carlo event generator for minimum bias hadronic interactions, used for both heavy-ion interactions and cosmic ray air shower simulation. The
Epos Lhc
employs the core-corona picture which describes high energy collisions ranging from small systems to large systems in a unified manner
Pierog:2013ria
. The high density areas are referred to as the core, the low density areas as the corona
Werner:2007bf
. The inclusion of the thermalized core area of the produced medium brings a parameterized hydrodynamic evolution effect to the system. More details about
Epos Lhc
used in this work can find in the Ref.
Pierog:2013ria
.

Correlation function and acceptance correction

The correlation function is defined as a function of the azimuthal angle difference
\Delta\varphi=\varphi_{\mathrm{trig}}-\varphi_{\mathrm{assoc}}
and pseudorapidity difference
\Delta\eta=\eta_{\mathrm{trig}}-\eta_{\mathrm{assoc}}
between trigger and associated particles. Pair acceptance corrections are evaluated with a mixed-event technique, where (
\Delta\varphi
,
\Delta\eta
) distributions with the trigger and the associated particles originated from different events are constructed. The correlation function
C(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)
is extracted using the following procedure to correct for acceptance effects:

C(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)=B(0,0)\frac{S(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)}{B(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)}\quad,

(1)

S(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)
is constructed from particle pairs coming from the same event.

S(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)=\frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{pairs}}^{\mathrm{signal}}}\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}N_{\mathrm{pairs}}^{\mathrm{signal}}}{\mathrm{d}\Delta\varphi\mathrm{d}\Delta\eta}\quad,

(2)

where
N_{\mathrm{pairs}}^{\mathrm{signal}}
is the number of pairs of particles in
S
.
B(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)
is constructed using an event mixing technique, where each particle in the pair comes from a different event and can be expressed as

B(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)=\frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{pairs}}^{\mathrm{mixed}}}\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}N_{\mathrm{pairs}}^{\mathrm{mixed}}}{\mathrm{d}\Delta\varphi\mathrm{d}\Delta\eta}\quad,

(3)

where
N_{\mathrm{pairs}}^{\mathrm{mixed}}
is the number of pairs of particles in
B
. The
B(0,0)
is the per-trigger yield at
(\Delta\varphi,\Delta\eta)=(0,0)
, where the pair acceptance is maximum

5
Summary

Two-particle azimuthal correlations of h–
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
and h–
(\Lambda+\overline{\Lambda})
are studied in pp collisions at
\sqrt{s}=7
TeV to explore the jet fragmentation effects and the interplay between soft and hard process with
Pythia
8
(CR and Shoving) and
Epos Lhc
event generators. The ratios of the per-trigger yields
{Y_{\Delta\varphi}^{\mathrm{h}-(\Lambda+\overline{\Lambda})}}/{2Y_{\Delta\varphi}^{\mathrm{h}-\mathrm{K}^{0}_{S}}}
are calculated within jets and UE region to understand the relative contribution of different mechanisms to the strange baryon to meson enhancement at intermediate
p_{\rm T}
. It is found that strange baryon to meson ratio of the per-trigger yield in the UE region is similar to the measured inclusive results, while the in-jet ratios are generally smaller than the UE results. However, the in-jet ratio is enhanced from high
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
to low
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
in the region of
p_{\mathrm{T,assoc}}
less than
4
GeV/
c
, and it becomes similar in the high
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
bin across all three different model calculations. This behavior indicates that the hard process jet fragmentation effect is important for the understanding of the strange baryon to meson ratio in the intermediate
p_{\rm T}
region. For high enough
p_{\rm T}
such as above
4
GeV/
c
, the hard process effect is dominant.

With the string fragmentation based models implemented in
Pythia
8
, the in-jet per trigger yield ratios are mildly suppressed within the
p_{\mathrm{T,assoc}}
range from
1
GeV/
c
to
4
GeV/
c
in the high
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
bin compared to the low
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
bin and no significant difference can be found at the near side and the away side. The
Epos Lhc
model predicts a much higher
\Lambda
/
{\rm K}^{0}_{\rm{S}}
ratio in UE compared to the string fragmentation models. The in-jet ratio is found to be larger at away side in the
6\,<\,p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}\,<\,15
GeV/
c
bin. This behavior suggests the soft process effects modeled through the final state evolution effects strongly increase the strange baryon production and persists in the jet region when the
p_{\mathrm{T,trig}}
is not very high. Studying the strange hadron productions via the two particle correlation method in the future experimental analysis will be of great interest to understand the appearance of strange baryon anomaly behavior observed in small systems and discriminate different physics mechanisms contributing to the strange baryon enhancement at intermediate
p_{\rm T}
.
