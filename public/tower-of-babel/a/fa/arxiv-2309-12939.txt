Bright beacons? ALMA non-detection of a supposedly bright [OI] 63- μ \mu m line in a redshift-6 dusty galaxy Facilities: ALMA, APEX
Matus Rybak, L. Lemsom, A. Lundgren, J. Zavala, J. A. Hodge, C. de Breuck, C. M. Casey, R. Decarli, K. Torstensson, J. L. Wardlow, P. P. van der Werf

Abstract
We report a non-detection of the [O I] 63- μ \mu m emission line from the z = 6.03 z=6.03 galaxy G09.83808 using ALMA Band 9 observations, refuting the previously claimed detection with APEX by [ 10 ] ; the new upper limit on the [O I] 63- μ \mu m flux is almost 20-times lower. [O I] 63- μ \mu m line could be a powerful tracer of neutral gas in the Epoch of Reionisation: yet our null result shows that detecting [O I] 63- μ \mu m from z ≥ 6 z\geq 6 galaxies is more challenging than previously hypothesised.

II
Observations and data reduction

To exploit the seemingly bright [O I]
63
line in G09.83808, we re-observed it with ALMA Band 9 (Project #2019.1.01427.S). The observations were taken on 2019 December 5 and 9, giving a total on-source time of 99.3 min. The array consisted of 41 twelve-meter antennas with baseline lengths of 15–312 m, providing sensitivity to structure on scales of 0.2–6.0 arcsec. The December 5 observations were of poor quality and were discarded. The December 9 data were taken in outstanding weather conditions, with a precipitable water vapour of 0.3 mm.

The ALMA observations were reduced and imaged using
CASA
v5.6.1
[
13
]

\times
0.41” (natural weighting); the continuum imaging reaches a 1
\sigma
noise level of 0.53 mJy/beam.
