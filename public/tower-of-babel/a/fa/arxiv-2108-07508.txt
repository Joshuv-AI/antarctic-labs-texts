The maximal superconductivity in proximity to charge density wave quantum critical point in Cu x TiSe 2
Tae-Ho Park, Obinna P. Uzoh, Han-Yong Choi

Abstract
Superconductivity emerges in 1 ​ T 1T -TiSe 2 when its charge density wave (CDW) order is suppressed by Cu intercalation or pressure. Since the CDW state is thought to be an excitonic insulator, an interesting question is whether the superconductivity is also mediated by the excitonic fluctuations. We investigated this question as to the nature of doping induced superconductivity in Cu x TiSe 2 by asking if it is consistent with the phonon-mediated pairing. We employed the ab initio density functional theory and density functional perturbation theory to compute the electron-phonon coupling Eliashberg function from which to calculate the superconducting (SC) critical temperature T c T_{c} . The calculated T c T_{c} as a function of the doping concentration x x exhibits a dome shape with the maximum T c T_{c} of 2 − 6 2-6 K at x ≈ 0.05 x\approx 0.05 for the Coulomb pseudopotential 0 ≤ μ ∗ ≤ 0.1 0\leq\mu^{*}\leq 0.1 . The maximal T c T_{c} was found to be pinned to the quantum critical point at which the CDW is completely suppressed and the corresponding phonon mode becomes soft. Underlying physics is that the reduced phonon frequency enhances the electron-phonon coupling constant λ \lambda which overcompensates the frequency decrease to produce a net increase of T c T_{c} . The doping induced superconductivity in Cu x TiSe 2 seems to be consistent with the phonon-mediated pairing. Comparative discussion was made with the pressure induced superconductivity in TiSe 2 .

IV
Summary and concluding remarks

In this paper, we demonstrated that the superconducting
T_{c}
as a function of the Cu intercalation concentration
x
for Cu
x
TiSe
2
is entirely determined by the electron-phonon interaction without a necessity to invoke the exciton mechanism.
We employed the
ab initio
density functional theory and density functional perturbation theory to calculate the electron-phonon coupling Eliashberg function
\alpha^{2}F(\omega)
from which to calculate the
T_{c}
using the Allen-Dynes-McMillan formula.
The maximum
T_{c}
was found to be pinned to the minimum phonon frequency as has been observed and discussed in many classes of materials.
Testardi_RMP1975
;
Moussa_PRB2006

The phonon mechanism of superconductivity for Cu
x
TiSe
2
is not at odds with the excitonic insulator picture of the CDW state. Note that the excitonic and lattice instabilities should appear simultaneously as reported by Kogar
et al.
because they have the same spatial symmetry.
Kogar_Science2017
;
Bok_PRB2021
Within this picture, the excitonic instability drives the CDW formation of the
2\times 2\times 2
periodic lattice distortion. Then, the CDW must be accompanied by the soft phonon mode of the wavevector
L
at the CDW quantum critical point. The critical temperature
T_{c}
becomes maximum at the minimum phonon frequency within the Eliashberg formalism as we obtained here.
Moussa_PRB2006
The superconductivity is phonon mediated conventional pairing and the proximity of the maximal superconductivity and the CDW quantum critical point is not coincidental.
