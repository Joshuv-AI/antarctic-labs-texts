Spin-glass behavior in Shastry-Sutherland lattice of Tm 2 {}_{\textbf{2}} Cu 2 {}_{\textbf{2}} In Preprint: AIP/123-QED
Baidyanath Sahu, R. Djoumessi Fobasso, André M. Strydom

Abstract
The spin – \textendash glass behavior in the ferromagnetic phase of Tm 2 ​ Cu 2 ​ In \mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In} was investigated by dc – \textendash , ac – \textendash magnetization, and non-equilibrium dynamics characterizations. An argon arc – \textendash melted polycrystalline sample of the compound of Tm 2 ​ Cu 2 ​ In \mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In} was found to adopt the Mo 2 ​ FeB 2 \mathrm{Mo_{2}FeB_{2}} – \textendash type tetragonal structure (space group P 4 / m ​ b ​ m P_{4}/mbm ). The temperature variation of dc – \textendash magnetization exhibits a ferromagnetic behavior with Curie temperature T C T_{C} = 32.5 K. The zero field cooled (ZFC) and field cooled (FC) magnetic curves show the thermomagnetic irreversible behavior below T C T_{C} . The field dependence of irreversible temperature follows the Almeida – \textendash Thouless line. The frequency and ac – \textendash driven field dependent anomalies in ac – \textendash susceptibility results indicate the existence of a spin – \textendash glass state in Tm 2 ​ Cu 2 ​ In \mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In} . The time dependence of magnetization further supports the spin – \textendash glass behavior observed in Tm 2 ​ Cu 2 ​ In \mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In} . The frequency dependence of the freezing temperature in the real part of ac – \textendash susceptibility has been analyzed on the basis of a power – \textendash law divergence and Vogel – \textendash Fulcher law. The obtained results reveal that Tm 2 ​ Cu 2 ​ In \mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In} belongs to the ferromagnetic canonical spin – \textendash glass class of compounds.

Summary

In summary, we have successfully prepared a polycrystalline sample of the intermetallic compound
\mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In}
, which crystallize in the
\mathrm{Mo_{2}B_{2}Fe}

\textendash
type of tetragonal structure. The spin
\textendash

T_{f}
= 31.1 K, has been confirmed by dc
\textendash
magnetization and ac
\textendash
susceptibility measurements. The spin
\textendash
glass behavior in
\mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In}
has also been suggested by the magnetic relaxation behavior. The canonical spin
\textendash
glass state in
\mathrm{Tm_{2}Cu_{2}In}
was suggested from the experimental data of frequency dependence of
T_{f}
in ac
\textendash

\textendash

\textendash
glass state due to the geometrically frustration in the structure likely due to the formation of distorted triangles by the magnetic rare-earth ions.
