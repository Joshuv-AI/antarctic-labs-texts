Branched SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) -opers
Indranil Biswas, Sorin Dumitrescu, Sebastian Heller

Abstract
. Branched projective structures were introduced by Mandelbaum [ 22 ] , [ 23 ] , and opers were introduced by Beilinson and Drinfeld [ 2 ] , [ 3 ] . We define the branched analog of SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) –opers and investigate their properties. For the usual SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) –opers, the underlying holomorphic vector bundle is actually determined uniquely up to tensoring with a holomorphic line bundle of order r r . For the branched SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) –opers, the underlying holomorphic vector bundle depends more intricately on the oper. While the holomorphic connection for a branched SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) –oper is nonsingular, given a branched SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) –oper, we associate to it a certain holomorphic vector bundle equipped with a logarithmic connection. This holomorphic vector bundle in question supporting a logarithmic connection does not depend on the branched oper. We characterize the branched SL ⁡ ( r , ℂ ) {\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}}) –opers in terms of the logarithmic connections on this fixed holomorphic vector bundle.

(V,\,{\mathcal{F}},\,D)

•

We recall that for a usual

For usual

–opers, i.e., when

note that any holomorphic

which is uniquely determined by the condition that the connection on

\xi^{\otimes r}\,=\,{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
induced by

has trivial monodromy. For any

{\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}})
–oper
(V,\,{\mathcal{F}},\,D)
, note that

(V\otimes\xi,\,{\mathcal{F}}\otimes\xi,\,D\otimes{\rm Id}_{\xi}+{\rm Id}_{V}\otimes D_{\xi})

is also a usual
{\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}})
–oper. If we fix a usual

{\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}})
–oper

(V_{0},\,{\mathcal{F}}_{0},\,D_{0})

, then for any usual
{\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}})
–oper

(V^{\prime},\,{\mathcal{F}}^{\prime},\,D^{\prime})

so the filtration for a usual

{\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}})
–oper. In the case of

However, for branched

–opers

{\rm SL}(r,{\mathbb{C}})
–oper. More precisely,

Denote the above quotient line bundle

Let

A criterion of Weil and Atiyah says that

Let

F\,\hookrightarrow\,V\,\stackrel{{\scriptstyle D}}{{\longrightarrow}}\,V\otimes K_{X}\otimes{\mathcal{O}}_{X}(S)\,\xrightarrow{\ q_{0}\otimes{\rm Id}_{K_{X}\otimes{\mathcal{O}}_{X}(S)}\ }(V/F)\otimes K_{X}\otimes{\mathcal{O}}_{X}(S)\,,

) it
follows that this composition of homomorphisms is actually

–linear, and

of

for the logarithmic connection

If

, then the composition of homomorphisms in

F\,\hookrightarrow\,V\,\stackrel{{\scriptstyle D}}{{\longrightarrow}}\,V\otimes K_{X}\,\xrightarrow{\ q_{0}\otimes{\rm Id}_{K_{X}}\ }(V/F)\otimes K_{X}\,,

of

.

Assume that the rank

Assume that there is holomorphic line bundle
