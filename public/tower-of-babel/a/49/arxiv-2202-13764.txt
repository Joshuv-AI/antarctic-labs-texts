A Note on “Optimum Sets of Interference-Free Sequences With Zero Autocorrelation Zone”
Qiping Fang, Zilong Wang

Abstract
In this paper, a simple construction of interference-free zero correlation zone (IF-ZCZ) sequence sets is proposed by well designed finite Zak transform lattice tessellation. Each set is characterized by the period of sequences K ​ M KM , the set size K K and the length of zero correlation zone M − 1 M-1 , which is optimal with respect to the Tang-Fan-Matsufuji bound. Secondly, the transformations that keep the properties of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set unchanged are given, and the equivalent relation of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set is defined based on these transformations. Then, it is proved that the general construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set proposed by Popovic is equivalent to the simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set, which indicates that the generation of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set can be simplified. Moreover, it is pointed out that the alphabet size for the special case of the simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set can be a factor of the period. Finally, both the simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set and its special case have sparse and highly structured Zak spectra, which can greatly reduce the computational complexity of implementing matched filter banks.



I
Introduction

Sequence set with good correlation is of considerable interest in many applications in communication and radar system. The ideal sequence set should have perfect impulse-like auto-correlation as well as all-zero cross-correlation for all pairs of sequences. Unfortunately, the famous Welch bound
[
20
]
implies that it is impossible to have impulse-like autocorrelation functions and all-zero cross-correlation functions simultaneously in a sequence set.

While the ideal sequence set is unattainable, an alternate compromise is to require that out-of-phase auto-correlation and cross-correlation of sequences are equal to zero in a finite zone. Such sequences are referred to as
zero correlation zone
(ZCZ) sequences which have found many applications in quasi-synchronous CDMA (QS-CDMA) system
[
7
]
, ranging system
[
6
]
, channel estimation
[
9
]
and spectrum-spreading
[
11
]
. Moreover, ZCZ sequences have been deployed as uplinked random access channel preambles in the fourth-generation cellular standard LTE
[
8
]
.

Generally, an (
N,K,Z_{cz}
) ZCZ sequence set is characterized by the period of sequences
N
, the set size
K
and the length of zero correlation zone
Z_{cz}
, The upper bound given by Tang
et al

[
19
]
implies that the parameters of a ZCZ sequence set must satisfy

K(Z_{cz}+1)\leq N.

This theoretical bound describes the tradeoff between the set size and the length of ZCZ for a fixed period of a sequence set. A ZCZ sequence set reaching the theoretical bound is called optimal.

It is remarkable that the above theoretical bound does not make any restrictions on the behavior of correlation functions outside ZCZ. We take into account ZCZ sequence sets with all-zero cross-correlations, which are referred to as
interference-free
ZCZ (IF-ZCZ) sequence sets in this paper. It’s possibly beneficial when IF-ZCZ sequences are deployed in some interference-limited systems, such as heterogeneous cellular networks (HetNet)
[
18
]
, multi-function radar system envisaged for autonomous cars
[
17
]
. For instance, an envisioned HetNet is composed of many low powered base stations (BS) within the coverage area of conventional BS. With the increasing density of BS in a HetNet, the signals from multiple BS in its neighborhood are superposed to the mobile terminal (MT) leading to severe interference-limited performance. MT may attept to connect to the closest low powered BS but the strongest signal may come from a conventional BS.
On account of some given scenarios of HetNet, the delays of conventional cell signals may lay in a correlation zone whose length is hard to predict. IF-ZCZ sequence set with proper parameters is a good candidate for these scenarios.

A series of papers
[
2
,
14
,
13
]
are devoted to the design of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets. In
[
15
, Equ.2]
, Popovi
\rm{\acute{c}}
presented a general construction including all known constructions of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets. Furthermore, as the important special case of the general construction, Popovi
\rm{\acute{c}}
presented the construction of generalized chirp-like IF-ZCZ sequences that allow particularly simple implementation of the corresponding banks of matched filters.

In this paper, firstly, a simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set is given. Then, we give transformations that keep the properties of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets unchanged. Based on these transformations, we define the equivalent relationship of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets. Then, we prove that the general construction
[
15
]
is equivalent to a simple construction of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets.
From the perspective of the simple construction of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets, the optimal
(KM,K,M-1)
IF-ZCZ sequence set can be interpreted as consisting of element-by-element multiplication of
K
discrete Fourier transform sequences of
N=KM
with a common sequence obtained by periodically extending
K
times a perfect sequence
{\bm{h}}_{a}
of any length
M
. In addition, there is no need to consider other parameters and their limitations.
Moreover, it can be seen from the expression of the simple construction of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets that the alphabet size doesn’t seem to be smaller than the period
N
of the sequences. However, we indicate that the alphabet size of the general construction
[
15
]
can be
KM
, which is a factor of the period
N=KM^{2}
of the sequences. Finally, the simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set and its special case sequences both have sparse and highly structured Zak spectra, which can greatly reduce the computational complexity of implementing matched filter banks.

The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Section
II
, we introduce some basic definitions and notations of IF-ZCZ sequence set and finite Zak transform. A simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets is given in Section
III
. Transformations, equivalent relationships, and proof are given in Section
IV
. In Section
V
, we indicate that the alphabet size of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets can be reduced. The computational complexities of implementing matched filter banks are given in Section
VI
. In Section
VII
, we conclude the paper.

II-A

ZCZ Sequence Set

Let
\bm{\mathcal{S}}=\{{\bm{u}}_{a}:0\leq a\leq K-1\}
be a sequence set with
K
complex sequences of period
N
. We first define the correlation of sequences.

Definition 1
.

Let
{\bm{u}}_{a}=(u_{a}(0),u_{a}(1),\cdots,u_{a}(N-1))
and
{\bm{u}}_{b}=(u_{b}(0),u_{b}(1),\cdots,u_{b}(N-1))
be two complex sequences of length
N
in
\bm{\mathcal{S}}
. The cross-correlation between
{\bm{u}}_{a}
and
{\bm{u}}_{b}
at shift
n
is defined by

\theta_{a,b}(n)=\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}u_{a}(m)u_{b}^{*}(m-n),\qquad 0\leq n\leq N-1

(1)

where
m-n
is taken modulo
N
and the symbol
*
denotes the complex conjugation. When
a=b
, the auto-correlation of
{\bm{u}}_{a}
at shift
n
is denoted by
\theta_{a}(n)=\theta_{a,a}(n)
.

Definition 2
.

A sequence
{\bm{u}}_{a}=(u_{a}(0),u_{a}(1),\cdots,u_{a}(N-1))
is referred to be perfect if

\theta_{a}(n)=0
for
0<n\leq N-1
.

Definition 3
.

The length
Z_{cz}
of zero correlation zone (ZCZ)
of the set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}
is defined by

\displaystyle Z_{cz}=max\{T|\theta_{a,b}(n)=0\ (0\leq|n|\leq T\

\displaystyle\text{for}\ a\neq b\ \text{and}\ 0<|n|\leq T\ \text{for}\ a=b)\}.

\bm{\mathcal{S}}
is referred to as an
(N,K,Z_{cz})
-ZCZ sequence set. It is clear that
|\theta_{{\bm{u}},{\bm{v}}}(-n)|=|\theta_{{\bm{v}},{\bm{u}}}(n)|
. To determine the value of
Z_{cz}
, it suffices to compute the correlation function of sequences
{\bm{u}}_{a}
and
{\bm{u}}_{b}
in set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}
with
0\leq n\leq N-1
, i.e.,

\displaystyle Z_{cz}=max\{T|\theta_{a,b}(n)=0\ (0\leq n\leq T\

\displaystyle\text{for}\ a\neq b\ \text{and}\ 0<n\leq T\ \text{for}\ a=b)\}.

Definition 4
.

An
(N,K,Z_{cz})
-ZCZ sequence set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}
is referred to as
(N,K,Z_{cz})
interference-free (IF) ZCZ sequence set if the cross-correlation of any two distinct sequences in
\bm{\mathcal{S}}
is always zero.

In addition to the traditional method to study the sequences in time and Fourier space, another approach to study the sequences in Zak space was proposed in

II-B

Finite Zak Transform

Suppose for the remainder of this paper that
N=LM
, where
L
and
M
are
positive integers, and set
n=rM+k
,
m=iL+j
for
0\leq k,i\leq M-1

0\leq r,j\leq L-1
.

Definition 5
.

The
L\times M
finite Zak transform (FZT)
[
21
]
of sequence
{\bm{u}}=(u(0),u(1),\cdots,u(N-1))
is defined by

U(j,k)=\sum_{r=0}^{L-1}u(rM+k)\omega_{L}^{rj}.

(2)

If we set
U(j,k)
as the entry of an
L\times M
matrix
{\bm{U}}
at row
j
and column
k
, it is much simple to understand the FZT by the product of the matrices. The sequence
{\bm{u}}
can be re-expressed by an
L\times M
matrix
\bm{A}^{{\bm{u}}}
, where the entry
A^{{\bm{u}}}(r,k)=u(rM+k)
, i.e,

{\bm{A}^{{\bm{u}}}}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}u(0)&u(1)&\cdots&u(M-1)\\
u(M)&u(M+1)&\cdots&u(2M-1)\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
u(N-M)&u(N-M+1)&\cdots&u(N-1)\\
\end{array}\right).

Let
\bm{F}
be the DFT matrix of order
L
. It is straightforward that

\bm{U}=\bm{F}\cdot{\bm{A}^{{\bm{u}}}}.

If
L=N
and
M=1
, the FZT is identical to the DFT. If
L=1
and
M=N
, the FZT of
{\bm{u}}
is identical to the original sequence
{\bm{u}}
. Thus, the FZT is a primary time-frequency representation which can concurrently encodes both the time and the frequency information about a sequence.

Similar to the inverse DFT, we can define the inverse FZT, where

u(rM+k)=L^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}U(j,k)\omega_{L}^{-rj}.

(3)

The sequence
{\bm{u}}
can be recovered from the inverse FZT.

III
A simple construction of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets

Construction
: For positive integers
K
and
M
,
\bm{\mathcal{S}_{1}}
is a set containing
K
sequences with period
N=KM
. Let
{\bm{u}}_{a}
be the
a
th sequence of
\bm{\mathcal{S}_{1}}
, whose
n
th (
n=rM+k

u_{a}(n)=h_{a}(n\ {\rm mod}\ M)\omega_{N}^{an},

(8)

where
0\leq n\leq N-1
and
{\bm{h}}_{a}=(h_{a}(0),\dots,h_{a}(M-1))
is an arbitrary perfect sequence of length
M
for
0\leq a\leq K-1

Theorem 1
.

Sequence
{\bm{u}}_{a}
in the set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}_{1}
has sparse and highly structured
K\times M
Zak spectra:

U_{a}(j,k)=\begin{cases}Kh_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{ak},&\text{if $(j+a)\mod K=0$,}\\
0,&\text{else}\end{cases}

(9)

for
0\leq j\leq K-1,0\leq k\leq M-1
.

Note that the entries of Zak spectra matrix
{\bm{S}}_{a}
are all zeroes except the
(K-a)
th row.

Proof.

Let
L=K
, we first compute the
K\times M
FZT of the sequence
{\bm{s}}_{a}
in construction 1. By the definition of
L\times M
FZT, we have

\begin{split}U_{a}(j,k)&=\sum_{r=0}^{K-1}u_{a}(k+rM)\omega_{K}^{rj}\\
&=\sum_{r=0}^{K-1}h_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{a(k+rM)}\omega_{K}^{rj}\\
&=h_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{ak}\sum_{r=0}^{K-1}\omega_{K}^{r(a+j)}\\
&=Kh_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{ak}\delta(j+a-K),\end{split}

(10)

where
\delta(\cdot)
is the delta function such that
\delta(0)=1
and
\delta(j)=0
for
j\neq 0
.
∎

Theorem 2
.

\bm{\mathcal{S}}_{1}
is an optimal
(N,K,M-1)
IF-ZCZ sequence set.

Proof.

The FZT of the cross-correlation of sequences
{\bm{s}}_{a}
and
{\bm{s}}_{b}
in construction 1 can be calculated by applying the formula (
7
).

If
a\neq b
, the none zero elements must occur in different rows of their respective Zak spectra matrix
{\bm{U}}_{a}
and
{\bm{U}}_{b}
, we immediately have

\Theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a},{\bm{u}}_{b}}(j,k)=0

for
0\leq j\leq K-1,0\leq k\leq M-1
. By using the IFZT in (
3
), we obtain the cross-correlation of
{\bm{s}}_{a}
and
{\bm{s}}_{b}
:

\theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a},{\bm{u}}_{b}}=\bm{0}.

Thus the sequence set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}_{1}
must be interference free.

If
a=b
and
j\neq K-a
, we have Zak spectrum
U_{a}(j,k)=0
for
\forall k
. Then
\Theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}(j,k)=0
follows.

If
a=b
and
j=K-a
, from the Zak spectra in (
17
), we have

U_{a}(j,k)=Kh_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{k}.

Then

\begin{split}\Theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}(j,k)&=\omega_{L}^{-j}\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}U_{a}(j,l)U_{a}^{*}(j,l-k+M)\\
&+\sum_{l=k}^{M-1}U_{a}(j,l)U_{a}^{*}(j,l-k)\\
&=K^{2}\omega_{K}^{a-K}\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}h_{a}(l)\omega_{N}^{al}h_{a}^{*}(l-k+M)\omega_{N}^{-a(l-k+M)}\\
&+K^{2}\sum_{l=k}^{M-1}h_{a}(l)\omega_{N}^{al}h_{a}^{*}(l-k)\omega_{N}^{-a(l-k)}\\
&=K^{2}\omega_{N}^{ak}\sum_{l=0}^{M-1}h_{a}(l)h_{a}^{*}(l-k)\\
&=NK\omega_{N}^{ak}\delta(k),\end{split}

which means the entries of
\Theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}
are all zeroes except
\Theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}(K-a,0)
.

By using the IFZT in (
3
), the periodic auto-correlation of
{\bm{s}}_{a}
is given below.

\begin{split}\theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}(k+rM)&=K^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}\Theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}(j,k)\omega_{K}^{-rj}\\
&=N\delta(k)\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}\delta(j+a-K)\omega_{K}^{-rj}\\
&=N\omega_{K}^{ra}\delta(k).\end{split}

(11)

In other word,

\theta_{{\bm{u}}_{a}}(n)=\begin{cases}N\omega_{K}^{\frac{na}{M}},&\text{if $M\mid n$,}\\
0,&\text{else}.\end{cases}

So the length of ZCZ in
\bm{\mathcal{S}}_{1}
is
M-1
, which satisfy the Tang-Fan-Matsufuji bound.
∎

IV
Transformation of IF-ZCZ sequence sets

We consider simple operations which leave IF-ZCZ properties and sequence length invariant.
An optimal
(N,K,M-1)
IF-ZCZ sequence set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}=\{{\bm{u}}_{a}:0\leq a\leq K-1\}

u_{a}(n=rM+k)=h_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{(t_{a}K+a)k+rMa}=(h_{a}(k)\omega_{M}^{t_{a}k})\omega_{N}^{an}.

(14)

By definition, the
n
th element of the auto-correlation of

is defined as

\begin{split}\theta_{a}(n)&=\sum_{k=0}^{M-1}h_{a}(k)\omega_{M}^{t_{a}k}h_{a}^{*}(k-n)\omega_{M}^{-t_{a}(k-n)}\\
&=\omega_{M}^{t_{a}n}\sum_{k=0}^{M-1}h_{a}(k)h_{a}^{*}(k-n)\end{split}

where
0\leq n,k\leq M-1
. Since
\{h_{a}(k)\}
is a perfect sequence, we have
\sum_{k=0}^{M-1}h_{a}(k)h_{a}^{*}(k-n)=0
for
0\leq k\leq M-1
. It is obvious that
\theta_{a}(n)=0
for
0<n\leq M-1
. This means that
\{h_{a}(k)\omega_{M}^{t_{a}k}\}
is perfect. Therefore, sequences (
14
) can be simplified by

u_{a}(n=rM+k)=h_{a}(k)\omega_{N}^{an}.

which is exactly the expression of optimal IF-ZCZ sequences in the simple construction.
∎

Compared with the general construction, there is no need to consider the parameters

As an important special case of the general construction
[

From the expression of the simple construction of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets, it seems that the alphabet size of the sequence sets is not less than period

For positive integers

. Let

Theorem 4
.

Sequence
{\bm{s}}_{a}
in the set
\bm{\mathcal{S}}_{2}
has sparse and highly structured
KM\times M
Zak spectra:

U_{a}(j,k)=\begin{cases}KM,&\text{if $j=KM-K\sigma_{a}(k)-a$,}\\
0,&\text{else}\end{cases}

(16)

where
0\leq j\leq KM-1,0\leq k\leq M-1
.

Proof.

By the definition of
KM\times M
FZT, we have

\begin{split}U_{a}(j,k)&=\sum_{r=0}^{KM-1}u_{a}(rM+k)\omega_{KM}^{rj}\\
&=\sum_{r=0}^{KM-1}\omega_{KM}^{r(K\sigma_{a}(k)+a+j)}\\
&=KM\delta(j+K\sigma_{a}(k)+a-KM).\end{split}

(17)

for
0\leq j\leq KM-1,0\leq k\leq M-1
.
∎

Note that for sequence
{\bm{s}}_{a}
, the position
(j,k)
of the none-zero elements in matrix
\bm{U}_{a}
must satisfy
j+K\sigma_{a}(k)+a=KM
. For each column
k

such that

j=KM-K\sigma_{a}(k)-a,

so there is only one none-zero element in each column of matrix
\bm{U}_{a}
. Moreover, for each row
j
, there is a unique none-zero element in this row if and only if

j+a\equiv 0\ (\mbox{mod}\ K).

Otherwise, the elements in row
j
must be all zeroes.

.

\bm{\mathcal{S}_{2}}
is an optimal

Computational Complexity

Both constructions
\bm{S}_{1}
and
\bm{S}_{2}
with sparse and highly structured Zak have certain advantages in terms of the implementations complexity of the corresponding banks of matched filters. In order to demonstrate these advantages, the total number of complex multiplications and complex additions will be used as the implementation complexity evaluation. Typically the DFT/IDFT is performed by some Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) algorithm. The usual approximation of the number of complex multiplications and complex additions in an
N
-points FFT/IFFT algorithm is
(N/2){\rm log_{2}}N
and
N{\rm log_{2}}N
, respectively.

VII
Conclusion

It has been shown that all known constructions of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets are included in the general construction
[
15
]
. We have proven that the general construction is equivalent to a simple construction of optimal IF-ZCZ sequence sets. It is implied that the alphabet size for the special case of the simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set can be a factor of the period. The computational complexity of implementing matched filter banks based on the simple construction of the optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set and its special case can be reduced.
Whether there is an optimal IF-ZCZ sequence set that is not equivalent to the general construction
[
15
]
is an open question.
