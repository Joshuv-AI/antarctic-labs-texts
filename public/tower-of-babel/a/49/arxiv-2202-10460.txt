Dispersion Characteristics of Accelerated Spacetime-Modulated Media
Christophe Caloz

Abstract
This paper opens up the field of nonuniform-velocity SpaceTime-Modulated (STM) metamaterials, with the canonical example of an STM metamaterial of constant proper acceleration or, equivalently, hyperpolic acceleration. Combining tools of General Relativity and Classical Electrodynamics, it derives the dispersion relation of this exotic medium and reports its fundamental physics, whose most striking feature is the bending of light in the direction opposite to the direction of the modulation.
