Polaronic transport and thermoelectricity in Mn 3 Si 2 Te 6 single crystals
Yu Liu, 1,∗ Zhixiang Hu, 1,2 Milinda Abeykoon, 3 Eli Stavitski, 3 Klaus Attenkofer, 3,† Eric D. Bauer, 4 and C. Petrovic 1,2

Abstract
We carried out a comprehensive study of the structural, electrical transport, thermal and thermodynamic properties in ferrimagnetic Mn 3 Si 2 Te 6 single crystals. Mn and Te K K -edge X-ray absorption spectroscopy and synchrotron powder X-ray diffraction were measured to provide information on the local atomic environment and the average crystal structure. The dc and ac magnetic susceptibility measurements indicate a second-order paramagnetic to ferrimagnetic transition at T c T_{c} ∼ \sim 74 K, which is further confirmed by the specific heat measurement. Mn 3 Si 2 Te 6 exhibits semiconducting behavior along with a large negative magnetoresistance of -87% at T c T_{c} and relatively high value of thermopower up to ∼ \sim 10 mV/K at 5 K. Besides the rapidly increasing resistivity ρ ⁡ ( T ) \rho(T) and thermopower S ⁡ ( T ) S(T) below 20 K, the large discrepancy between activation energy for resistivity E ρ E_{\rho} and thermopower E S E_{S} above 20 K indicates the polaronic transport mechanism. Furthermore, the thermal conductivity κ ⁡ ( T ) \kappa(T) of Mn 3 Si 2 Te 6 is notably rather low, comparable to Cr 2 Si 2 Te 6 , and is strongly suppressed in magnetic field across T c T_{c} , indicating the presence of strong spin-lattice coupling, also similar with Cr 2 Si 2 Te 6 .
