The primary objective of Broudy and Lovejoy is to consider various arithmetic properties satisfied by

Theorem 10
.

For all
n\geq 0
,

S\big(256n+171\big)=S\big(4(64n+43)-1\big)\equiv S\big(64n+43\big)\pmod{16}.

Proof.

In (
4.5
), we noted that

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}S(64n+43)q^{n}\equiv-\frac{f_{2}^{4}f_{3}^{8}}{f_{1}^{2}f_{4}^{2}f_{6}}+8qf_{2}^{2}f_{6}^{6}\pmod{16}.

We next need to determine a similar congruence modulo 16 for the generating function dissection for
S(256n+171)
.
Using (
4.5
) and the same kinds of dissection tools that have already been demonstrated, we can easily show that

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}S(128n+43)q^{n}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{f_{4}^{5}f_{6}^{20}}{f_{1}f_{2}^{2}f_{3}^{5}f_{8}^{2}f_{12}^{8}}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{f_{4}^{5}f_{6}^{20}}{f_{2}^{2}f_{8}^{2}f_{12}^{8}}\left(\frac{1}{f_{1}f_{3}}\right)\left(\frac{1}{f_{3}^{4}}\right)\pmod{16}.

Using Lemmas
1
and
4
, we can then show that

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}S(256n+171)q^{n}\equiv-\frac{f_{2}^{10}f_{3}^{4}f_{6}^{5}}{f_{1}^{6}f_{4}^{4}f_{12}^{2}}-4q\frac{f_{2}^{4}f_{3}^{6}f_{12}^{2}}{f_{1}^{4}f_{6}}\pmod{16}.

Therefore, in order to prove this theorem, we want to prove

\displaystyle-\frac{f_{2}^{4}f_{3}^{8}}{f_{1}^{2}f_{4}^{2}f_{6}}+8qf_{4}f_{6}^{6}\equiv-\frac{f_{2}^{10}f_{3}^{4}f_{6}^{5}}{f_{1}^{6}f_{4}^{4}f_{12}^{2}}-4q\frac{f_{2}^{4}f_{3}^{6}f_{12}^{2}}{f_{1}^{4}f_{6}}\pmod{16}.

To do so, we begin by noting that

\displaystyle\frac{f_{2}f_{6}^{2}}{f_{1}^{2}f_{12}}\equiv 1\pmod{2}.

Thus,

\displaystyle 8qf_{4}f_{6}^{6}\equiv 8q\frac{f_{2}f_{4}f_{6}^{8}}{f_{1}^{2}f_{12}}\pmod{16}.

Hence, it is sufficient to prove that
L(q)\equiv 0\pmod{16}
where

(4.6)

\displaystyle L(q):=-\frac{f_{2}^{4}f_{3}^{8}}{f_{1}^{2}f_{4}^{2}f_{6}}+8q\frac{f_{2}f_{4}f_{6}^{8}}{f_{1}^{2}f_{12}}+\frac{f_{2}^{10}f_{3}^{4}f_{6}^{5}}{f_{1}^{6}f_{4}^{4}f_{12}^{2}}+4q\frac{f_{2}^{4}f_{3}^{6}f_{12}^{2}}{f_{1}^{4}f_{6}}.

We then make use of the Alaca–Alaca–Williams parameterization. Substituting the relations in Lemma
5
into
L(q)
, we have

\displaystyle L(q)\cdot\frac{f_{4}^{2}f_{6}}{f_{3}^{2}}=16qs^{4}t^{2}(1+qt)^{3}(1+2qt)(1+4qt).

Since the series
f_{4}^{2}f_{6}/f_{3}^{2}
is invertible in the ring
\mathbb{Z}/16\mathbb{Z}[[q]]
, while the right-hand side of the above vanishes in the same ring, we conclude that

\displaystyle L(q)\equiv 0\pmod{16},

as required.
∎

Theorem

Theorem 11
.

For all
\alpha\geq 0
and
n\geq 0
,

S\left(2^{5+2\alpha}n+\left(2^{5+2\alpha}-\frac{2^{2+2\alpha}-1}{3}\right)\right)\equiv 0\pmod{16}.

Proof.

We prove this theorem by induction on
\alpha

\alpha=0,1,2
have already been proved in Theorem
9
above. We now use the case
\alpha=2
as our base case in a proof by induction.
Thus, we assume that, for some
\alpha\geq 2
and all

S\left(2^{5+2\alpha}n+\left(2^{5+2\alpha}-\frac{2^{2+2\alpha}-1}{3}\right)\right)\equiv 0\pmod{16}.

We then want to prove that

S\left(2^{5+2(\alpha+1)}n+\left(2^{5+2(\alpha+1)}-\frac{2^{2+2(\alpha+1)}-1}{3}\right)\right)\equiv 0\pmod{16}.

It is important to note that, for all
\alpha\geq 2
,

2^{5+2\alpha}n+\left(2^{5+2\alpha}-\frac{2^{2+2\alpha}-1}{3}\right)\equiv 43\pmod{64}.

This will allow the induction step below to go through via Theorem

.
Indeed,

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!S\left(2^{5+2(\alpha+1)}n+\left(2^{5+2(\alpha+1)}-\frac{2^{2+2(\alpha+1)}-1}{3}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=S\left(2^{5+2(\alpha+1)}n+\left(2^{5+2(\alpha+1)}-\frac{2^{2+2(\alpha+1)}-4}{3}\right)-1\right)

\displaystyle=S\left(4\left(2^{5+2\alpha}n+\left(2^{5+2\alpha}-\frac{2^{2+2\alpha}-1}{3}\right)\right)-1\right)

\displaystyle\equiv S\left(2^{5+2\alpha}n+\left(2^{5+2\alpha}-\frac{2^{2+2\alpha}-1}{3}\right)\right)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{16},

where Theorem
