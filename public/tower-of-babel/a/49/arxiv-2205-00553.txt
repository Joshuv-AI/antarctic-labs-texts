Full exceptional collections for anticanonical log del Pezzo surfaces
Giulia Gugiatti, Franco Rota

Abstract
. Motivated by homological mirror symmetry, this paper constructs explicit full exceptional collections for the canonical stacks associated with the series of log del Pezzo surfaces constructed by Johnson and Kollár in [ JK01 ] . These surfaces have cyclic quotient, non-Gorenstein, singularities. The construction involves both the GL ⁡ ( 2 , ℂ ) \GL(2,\mathbb{C}) McKay correspondence, and the study of the minimal resolutions of the surfaces, which are birational to degree two del Pezzo surfaces. We show that a degree two del Pezzo surface arises in this way if and only if it admits a generalized Eckardt point, and in the course of the paper we classify the blow-ups of ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} giving rise to them. Our result on the adjoints of the functor of Ishii–Ueda [ IU15 ] applies to any finite small subgroup of GL ⁡ ( 2 , ℂ ) \GL(2,\mathbb{C}) .

5.3.
Exceptional collections and tilting

Let
\mathcal{D}
be a triangulated category. We say that
T\in\mathcal{D}

generates

\mathcal{D}
if, for every
E\in\mathcal{D}
, we have that
\Hom(T,E)=0
implies
E\simeq 0
, and that
T

classically generates

\mathcal{D}
if the smallest full triangulated subcategory containing
T
, closed under isomorphisms and direct summands, is
\mathcal{D}
itself.
An object
T\in\mathcal{D}
is
partial tilting
if
\Hom(T,T[l])=0
for all
l\neq 0
. We say that
T
is a
tilting
object if, moreover, it generates
\mathcal{D}
.

Generally speaking, tilting objects produce equivalences between
\mathcal{D}
and derived categories of modules over their endomorphism algebra. More precisely, suppose
\mathcal{D}=D^{b}(\Coh(Z))
where
Z
is a smooth Deligne-Mumford stack with finite stabilizers and projective coarse moduli space. Then we have:

Proposition 5.12
.

Let
T
be a tilting complex in
\mathcal{D}
and denote by
\Lambda=\Hom(T,T)
its endomorphism algebra. Then, the functor
\dR\Hom_{\mathcal{O}_{Z}}(T,-)
defines an equivalence of triangulated categories

\mathcal{D}\xrightarrow{\sim}D^{b}(\mathrm{mod-}\Lambda)

with inverse
-\otimes^{\dL}_{\Lambda}T
.

Proof.

For schemes, this is
[
HVdB07
, Theorem 7.6]
.
If
\mathcal{X}
is a smooth DM stack,
D(\QCoh(Z))
is compactly generated (this is shown for the more general class of
perfect
stacks in
[
BZFN10
, Prop. 3.9]
15
15

15

Smooth DM stacks with finite stabilizers have affine diagonal, and hence are perfect. Our setting is analogous to that of
[
AU15
, Lemma 4.1]
, which follows a similar argument.
). Then,
T
generates
D(\QCoh(Z))
if and only if it classically generates
\mathcal{D}

[
BvdB03
, Theorem 2.1.2]
, and the standard proof (see e.g.
[
Bae88
, §3]
) suffices to conclude.
∎

If
(E_{1},...,E_{n})
is a strong full exceptional collection in
\mathcal{D}
, then
\mathcal{E}=\oplus_{i=1}^{n}E_{i}
is a tilting complex. If the collection is not strong,
\Lambda_{\mathcal{E}}=\Hom(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{E})
is a
\mathrm{dg}
-algebra, and one may still obtain an equivalence, analogous to Prop.
5.12
, between
\mathcal{D}
and the derived category of
\mathrm{dg}
-modules over
\Lambda_{\mathcal{E}}
. Alternatively, if the exceptional collection only admits maps in degrees
0
and
1
, we obtain an explicit tilting complex from the so called
universal extension trick
, which we briefly recall here:

Lemma 5.13
.

Assume that
(E_{1},...,E_{n})
is a collection of objects of
\mathcal{D}
such that:

(i)

each
E_{i}
is partial tilting;

(ii)

the sum
\oplus_{i=1}^{n}E_{i}
generates

;

(iii)

if
i<j
, then
\Hom(E_{i},E_{j}[l])=0
for all
l\neq 0,1
;

(iv)

if
i<j

\Hom(E_{j},E_{i}[l])=0

.

admits a tilting complex.

Proof.

We direct the reader to

Proceed by induction on
