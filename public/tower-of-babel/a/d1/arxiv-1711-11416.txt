New estimates for the zeta function
Durmagambetov A.A

Abstract
In the paper the Riemann’s functional equation reduced to a Riemann–Hilbert boundary value problem, and the integral Hilbert transforms arising in its solution allow the calculation of an exact lower bounds for the zeta function.

Introduction

This study is concerned with the properties of modified zeta functions. Riemann’s zeta function is defined by
the Dirichlet series

\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{s}},\,\ s=\sigma+it,

(1)

which is absolutely and uniformly convergent in any finite region of the complex
s

If

\zeta(s)=\prod_{j\in N}\left[1-\frac{1}{p_{j}^{s}}\right]^{-1},

(2)

\zeta(s)

However, the most profound properties of

\zeta(s)

\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)=\pi^{-(1-s)/2}\Gamma((1-s)/2)\zeta(1-s)

(3)

it equation is called Riemann’s functional equation.
