Observations and predictions from past lightcones
Martin Lesourd

Abstract
. In a general Lorentzian manifold M M , the past lightcone of a point is a proper subset of M M that does not carry enough information to determine the rest of M M . That said, if M M is a globally hyperbolic Cauchy development of vacuum initial data on a Cauchy surface S S and there is a point whose past lightcone contains S S , then the contents of such a lightcone determines all of M M (up to isometry). We show some results that describe what properties of M M guarantee that past lightcones do indeed determine all or at least significant portions of M M . Null lines and observer horizons, which are well known features of the de-Sitter spacetime, play a prominent role.
