Multiscale Loop Vertex Expansion for Cumulants, the T 3 4 T^{4}_{3} Model
V. Rivasseau

Abstract
We consider a particular model of a tensor field theory of rank 3 perturbed by a quartic term, nicknamed the T 3 4 T^{4}_{3} model. The method we use is the multi-scale loop vertex expansion. We prove analyticity and Borel summability of the cumulants up to finite order.
