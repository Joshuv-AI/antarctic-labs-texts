Iwasawa theory of fine Selmer groups over global fields
Sohan Ghosh, Somnath Jha, Sudhanshu Shekhar

Abstract
. The p ∞ p^{\infty} -fine Selmer group of an elliptic curve E E over a number field F F is a subgroup of the classical p ∞ p^{\infty} -Selmer group of E E over F F . Fine Selmer group is closely related to the 1st and 2nd Iwasawa cohomology groups. Coates-Sujatha observed that the structure of the fine Selmer group of E E over a p p -adic Lie extension of a number field is intricately related to some deep questions in classical Iwasawa theory; for example, Iwasawa’s classical μ \mu -invariant vanishing conjecture. In this article, we study the properties of the p ∞ p^{\infty} -fine Selmer group of an elliptic curve over certain p p -adic Lie extensions of a number field. We also define and discuss p ∞ p^{\infty} -fine Selmer group of an elliptic curve over function fields of characteristic p p and also of characteristic ℓ ≠ p . \ell\neq p. We relate our study with a conjecture of Jannsen.

is finite.

Thus we are further reduced to show

given by

such that

, where

is a subquotient of
H^{0}(H,H^{2}_{\mathrm{Iw}}(T_{p}E/F_{\infty})^{\vee})
and

.
Now by an argument similar to
[

, Page 562, last paragraph]
which uses Nekovar’s spectral sequence
[

, Proposition 8.4.8.3]
, we get that

.

Then from the proof of
[
Ka1
, Theorem 5.1]
, it suffices to show that for every
\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[\Gamma]]
subquotient

of

and for every

,

is finite. Notice that, by our assumption,
H^{2}_{\mathrm{Iw}}(T_{p}E/F_{\mathrm{cyc}})
is
\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[\Gamma]]
torsion and hence
T(k)
is
\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[\Gamma]]
torsion. We fix a topological generator
\gamma
of
\Gamma
.
Now from the structure theorem of finitely generated torsion modules over

does not divide characteristic ideal of

is finite, for every

The finiteness of

is equivalent to the finiteness of

, again by equation (
4
). As

is finite for every

∎
