b → s ​ γ b\to s\gamma , electron EDM and electroweak baryogenesis: a study in general 2HDM
George Wei-Shu Hou

Abstract
We study inclusive b → s ​ γ b\to s\gamma decay in the context of electron EDM and baryogenesis. The general 2HDM (i.e. without Z 2 Z_{2} ) that possesses an extra set of Yukawa matrices can drive electroweak baryogensis via λ t ​ Im ​ ρ t ​ t \lambda_{t}{\rm Im}\rho_{tt} , where ρ t ​ t \rho_{tt} is the extra diagonal top Yukawa coupling, with the e e EDM constraint evaded by an exquisite flavor cancellation mechanism. We touch upon the current status of direct search for exotic H H , A A and H + H^{+} scalars at the LHC, while for the plethora of flavor observables in g g 2HDM, we focus on b → s ​ γ b\to s\gamma , pointing out chiral enhancement of a ρ t ​ t ​ ρ b ​ b \rho_{tt}\rho_{bb} effect in g 2HDM, which can bring in a CPV phase. We first explore the inclusive ℬ ⁡ ( b → s ​ γ ) {\cal B}(b\to s\gamma) rate, then showcase the progress that Belle II can make in the future, illustrating a potential 3 σ \sigma effect for the inclusive B + → X s + ​ γ B^{+}\to X_{s}^{+}\gamma vs B 0 → X s 0 ​ γ B^{0}\to X_{s}^{0}\gamma CPV rate difference, Δ ​ A CP \Delta A_{\rm CP} . Especially if e e EDM emerges swiftly, perhaps one should pursue further upgrade beyond Belle II.

Summary

Rather than accepting the “

” fate, we advocate

,

H^{+}

verified at the LHC, which would be

Thrown in as bonus would be a bunch of FPCP processes
[

In this talk we covered only the

process, the well known probe
of
H^{+}
in SUSY-type 2HDM-II. We illustrate the power of

to probe both

. Currently, the inclusive rate is the best measured, but we advocate
that Belle II can measure

in the future to possibly provide
a crosscheck on
e
