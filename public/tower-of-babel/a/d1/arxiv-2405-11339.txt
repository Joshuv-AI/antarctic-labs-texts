LOWEST-ORDER NONSTANDARD FINITE ELEMENT METHODS FOR TIME-FRACTIONAL BIHARMONIC PROBLEM
Shantiram Mahata, Neela Nataraj, Jean-Pierre Raymond

Abstract
In this work, we consider an initial-boundary value problem for a time-fractional biharmonic equation in a bounded polygonal domain with a Lipschitz continuous boundary in ℝ 2 \mathbb{R}^{2} with clamped boundary conditions. After establishing the well-posedness, we focus on some regularity results of the solution with respect to the regularity of the problem data. The spatially semidiscrete scheme covers several popular lowest-order piecewise-quadratic finite element schemes, namely, Morley, discontinuous Galerkin, and C 0 C^{0} interior penalty methods, and includes both smooth and nonsmooth initial data. Optimal order error bounds with respect to the regularity assumptions on the data are proved for both homogeneous and nonhomogeneous problems. The numerical experiments validate the theoretical convergence rate results.



Key words:
Time-fractional equation, biharmonic, lowest-order FEM, smooth and nonsmooth initial data, semidiscrete, error estimates

AMS subject classifications:
35R11, 65M15, 65M60

of Lemma
4.6
in (
4.21
) show

\displaystyle\mathinner{\lVert\varphi_{2}^{\prime}(s)\rVert}\lesssim\mathinner{\lVert s^{2}\varphi^{\prime}(s)\rVert}+\mathinner{\lVert s\varphi(s)\rVert}\lesssim s^{1-\alpha}\|u_{0}\|.

This shows

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\mathinner{\lVert\varphi_{2}^{\prime}(s)\rVert}^{2}\,ds\lesssim t^{3-2\alpha}\|u_{0}\|^{2}.

Step 2 (Smooth data).
For
f\neq 0
and
u_{0}\in D(A)
, follow the steps to bound
\|\widehat{\varphi_{1}}(t)\|^{2}
for smooth data in the
Step 3
in Lemma
4.6
and recall (
4.20
) to arrive at

\displaystyle\|\varphi_{2}(t)\|^{2}\lesssim\Big(t^{2}\mathinner{\lVert u_{0}\rVert}_{D(A)}

\displaystyle+B(\alpha\epsilon/2,1-\alpha)t^{2-\alpha(1-\epsilon/2)}\mathinner{\lVert f(0)\rVert}_{D(A^{\epsilon/2})}

\displaystyle\quad+\epsilon^{-1}t^{3-\alpha(1-\epsilon/2)}\mathinner{\lVert f^{\prime}\rVert}_{L^{\infty}(0,T;D(A^{\epsilon/2}))}\Big)^{2}.

It is easy to establish a similar bound for
\int_{0}^{t}\|\varphi_{2}(s)\|^{2}\,ds
.
The approach in
Step 1
of this lemma and the stability properties in (
2.21
) with
p=0
, (
2.22
), (
2.25
) with
q=\epsilon
, and (
2.26
) show

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\|\varphi_{2}^{\prime}(s)\|^{2}\,ds\lesssim\Big(h^{2}t^{\alpha/2-2}B_{1}(\epsilon,t)\Big)^{2}.

The definition of
f_{2}
and (
4.8
) establish

\displaystyle\|f_{2}(t)\|^{2}+\int_{0}^{t}(\|f_{2}(s)\|^{2}+\|f_{2}^{\prime}(s)\|^{2})\,ds\lesssim\Big(t^{2}\|f(t)\|+t\|f\|_{L^{2}(0,t;L^{2}(\Omega))}+t^{2}\|f^{\prime}\|_{L^{2}(0,t;L^{2}(\Omega))}\Big)^{2}.

A combination of
Steps
1 and 2 establishes the assertion.
∎

We are now ready to prove Theorem
4.1
.

Proof of Theorem
4.1
.
A substitution of the bounds from

4.5
-
4.8
in the key inequality from Lemma

and algebraic manipulations establish

\displaystyle\mathinner{\lVert\theta(t)\rVert}+t^{\alpha/2}\|\theta(t)\|_{h}\lesssim\begin{cases}h^{2\gamma_{0}}t^{-\alpha}\mathinner{\lVert u_{0}\rVert}+h^{2}t^{-(\alpha+1)/2}\mathinner{\lVert u_{0}\rVert},\;\;\text{ for }\;u_{0}\in L^{2}(\Omega),f=0,\\

6
Concluding remarks

In this paper, we have studied an initial-boundary value problem for a time-fractional biharmonic problem with clamped boundary conditions in a bounded polygonal domain with a Lipschitz continuous boundary in
\mathbb{R}^{2}
. After defining a weak solution, we have proved the well-posedness result of the problem and derived several regularity results of the solutions of both homogeneous and nonhomogeneous problems which are useful in the error analysis. Using an energy argument a spatially semidiscrete scheme that covers various popular lowest-order nonstandard piecewise quadratic finite element schemes (e.g., the Morley, discontinuous Galerkin, and
C^{0}
interior penalty) is developed and analyzed. The convergence analysis is carried out for smooth and nonsmooth initial data cases, including the initial data
u_{0}\in L^{2}(\Omega)
. Numerical results are provided to validate the theoretical convergence rates of the discrete solution. One future direction is the convergence analysis for time discretization, which will be addressed in another work.

-
(H4)
from

(H1)

Theorem A.5

(Energy norm bound)
.

Under the hypotheses
(H1)
–
(H3)
, the Ritz projection
\mathcal{R}_{h}
satisfies

\displaystyle\|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{h}\lesssim h^{\gamma}\|u\|_{H^{2+\gamma}(\Omega)},\;\;\gamma\in[0,\gamma_{0}]\text{ with }\gamma_{0}\in(1/2,1].

Proof.

Let
\zeta_{h}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=I_{h}I_{\rm M}u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u
and split the error
u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u
as a sum of three terms given by

u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u=(u-I_{\rm M}u)+(I-I_{h})I_{\rm M}u+\zeta_{h}.

Lemma
A.4
(i) and (
A.5
) show

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{h}&\lesssim|\!|\!|u-I_{\mathrm{M}}u|\!|\!|_{\mathrm{pw}}+\|\zeta_{h}\|_{h}.\end{aligned}

(A.7)

For

, the coercivity of

from (

) and (

) reveal

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &\|\zeta_{h}\|_{h}^{2}\lesssim a_{h}(\zeta_{h},\zeta_{h})=a_{h}(I_{h}I_{\rm M}u,\zeta_{h})-a_{h}(\mathcal{R}_{h}u,\zeta_{h})=a_{h}(I_{h}I_{\rm M}u,\zeta_{h})-a(u,Q\zeta_{h})\\
&=a_{\mathrm{pw}}(u,(I-J)I_{\mathrm{M}}\zeta_{h})-a_{\mathrm{pw}}(I_{\mathrm{M}}u,I_{\mathrm{M}}\zeta_{h})+a_{h}(I_{h}I_{\rm M}u,\zeta_{h})\end{aligned}

with (
A.3
) in the last step.
Lemma
A.2
(vi) plus
(
A.3
) show

a_{\mathrm{pw}}(I_{\mathrm{M}}u,(I-J)I_{\mathrm{M}}\zeta_{h})=0
.
This, (

), and
(H2)
(i) in the above displayed expression show

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} \|\zeta_{h}\|_{h}^{2}&\lesssim a_{\mathrm{pw}}(u-I_{\mathrm{M}}u,(I-J)I_{\mathrm{M}}\zeta_{h})+(a_{\mathrm{pw}}+b_{h})((I_{h}-I)I_{\mathrm{M}}u,\zeta_{h})\\
&\quad+((a_{\mathrm{pw}}+b_{h})(I_{\mathrm{M}}u,\zeta_{h}-I_{\mathrm{M}}\zeta_{h})+c_{h}(I_{h}I_{\mathrm{M}}u,\zeta_{h}))=\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}T_{1}+T_{2}+T_{3}.\end{aligned}

(vii), and (

Theorem A.6

(Weaker and piecewise Sobolev norm bound)
.

Under the hypotheses
(H1)
-
(H4)
, the Ritz projection
\mathcal{R}_{h}
satisfies

\displaystyle\mathinner{\lVert u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\rVert}_{H^{s}(\mathcal{T})}\lesssim h^{2-s}\mathinner{\lVert u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\rVert}_{h}\text{ for all }2-\gamma\leq s\leq 2\text{ and for all }\gamma\in(0,\gamma_{0}].

In particular, we have

\|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|\leq\|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{H^{2-\gamma}(\mathcal{T})}\lesssim h^{\gamma}\mathinner{\lVert u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\rVert}_{h}\text{ for all }\gamma\in(0,\gamma_{0}].

The proof is based on duality arguments. Here we sketch only the main ideas and for details, we refer to
[
10
, Section 6]
.
Note that the
L^{2}
estimates in the last displayed expression cannot be improved further for
P_{2}
approximations
[
9
]
.

Outline of the proof.
For
u\in V
and
v=Q\mathcal{R}_{h}u\in V
, the duality of
H^{-s}(\Omega)
and
H^{s}_{0}(\Omega)
reveals

\displaystyle\|u-v\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}=\sup_{0\neq\mathcal{F}\in H^{-s}(\Omega)}\frac{\mathcal{F}(u-v)}{\|\mathcal{F}\|_{H^{-s}(\Omega)}}=\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}(u-v).

A corollary of the Hahn-Banach theorem ensures that the supremum is attained for some
\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}\in H^{-s}(\Omega)\subset V^{*}
with
\|\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}\|_{H^{-s}(\Omega)}=1
. The functional
a(z,\cdot)=\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}\in V^{*}
has a unique Riesz representation
z\in V
in the Hilbert space
(V,a)
;
z\in V
is the weak solution of
\Delta^{2}z=\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}
. By virtue of the elliptic regularity
[
5
]
, see also Remark
2.1
, we have
z\in V\cap H^{4-s}(\Omega)
, with
\|z\|_{H^{4-s}(\Omega)}\lesssim\|\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}\|_{H^{-s}(\Omega)}\lesssim 1
for all
2-\gamma\leq s\leq 2
and all
\gamma\in(0,\gamma_{0}]
.
Hence

\displaystyle\|u-v\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}=a(u-v,z)\text{ and }|\!|\!|z-I_{\mathrm{M}}z|\!|\!|_{\mathrm{pw}}\lesssim h^{2-s}\|z\|_{H^{4-s}(\Omega)}\lesssim h^{2-s}.

(A.9)

For
z_{h}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=I_{h}I_{\mathrm{M}}z\in V_{h}
and
\xi\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=Qz_{h}\in V
, the definition of the Ritz projection
\mathcal{R}_{h}
(cf. (
3.4
)) and elementary algebra show the key identity
[
10
, Lemma 6.3]
:

\displaystyle a(u-v,z)=a(u-v,z-\xi)+a_{\mathrm{pw}}(\mathcal{R}_{h}u-v,\xi-z_{h})+a_{\mathrm{pw}}((I-J)I_{\mathrm{M}}\mathcal{R}_{h}u,z_{h}-I_{\mathrm{M}}z_{h})

\displaystyle+a_{\mathrm{pw}}((I-I_{\mathrm{M}})\mathcal{R}_{h}u,I_{\mathrm{M}}z_{h}-\xi)+a_{h}(\mathcal{R}_{h}u,z_{h})-a_{\mathrm{pw}}(\mathcal{R}_{h}u,I_{\mathrm{M}}z_{h})+a_{\mathrm{pw}}((I-I_{\mathrm{M}})\mathcal{R}_{h}u,z_{h}).

All the terms on the right-hand side of the identity above can be bounded in modulus by a constant times

\mathinner{\lVert u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\rVert}_{h}|\!|\!|z-I_{\mathrm{M}}z|\!|\!|_{\mathrm{pw}}
. The proof of this part is skipped for brevity; we refer to Lemmas 6.4 and 6.5 in
[
10
]
for details. Hence with (
A.9
), we have

a(u-v,z)\lesssim h^{2-s}\mathinner{\lVert u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\rVert}_{h}.

To conclude the proof, split
u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u
as

u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u=(u-v)+(v-\mathcal{R}_{h}u)
.
Invoke (
A.9
) and the above displayed bound to establish

\|u-v\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}\lesssim h^{2-s}\|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{h}.

The definition of the Sobolev-Slobodeckii semi-norm shows

\|u-v\|_{H^{s}(\mathcal{T})}^{2}=\sum_{K\in\mathcal{T}}|u-v|_{H^{s}(K)}^{2}\leq|u-v|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}^{2}\text{ for }\;1<s<2.

This and the last displayed estimate provide

\displaystyle\|u-v\|_{H^{s}(\mathcal{T})}\lesssim h^{2-s}\|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{h}\text{ for all }2-\gamma\leq s\leq 2.

Since
\mathcal{R}_{h}u\in P_{2}(\mathcal{T})
, Lemma
3.5
provides the estimate

\|v-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{H^{s}(\mathcal{T})}\lesssim h^{2-s}\|u-\mathcal{R}_{h}u\|_{h}.

A triangle inequality and a combination of the last two estimates conclude the proof.

\Box
