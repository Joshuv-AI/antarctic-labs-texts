Probabilistic Modelled Optimal Frame for Erasures under Spectral and Operator Norm
Shankhadeep Mondal

Abstract
Error occurs in data transmission process when some data are missing at the time of reconstruction. Finding the best dual frame or a dual pair that minimizes the reconstruction error when erasure occurs,is a deep-rooted problem in frame theory. The main motivation behind this paper is to characterize the optimal dual under the spectral and operator norms. Here we give several equivalent conditions for which the canonical dual is an optimal dual frame for a given frame. We also go on to characterize the set of dual pairs which attains the optimal value.

In the transmission process, data erasure arises from buffer overflows at the routers. Some times, error occurs from the bad global conditions of the network such as conjunction, capacity of transmission channel etc. In these cases, erasures of different elements usually generate some probabilistic irregularities. Probability of a bad channel failure is usually larger than the probability of a good channel failure.

The following theorem gives an equivalent condition between probabilistic optimal dual and probabilistic spectrally optimal dual for 1-erasure for a given parseval frame
F.
This may be not true for any general frame.

Theorem 4.5

Let
F=\{f_{i}\}_{i=1}^{N}
be a perseval frame for
H
and let
\{q_{i}\}_{i=1}^{N}
be a weight number sequence given by (
2.2
). TFAE :

(i)

The canonical dual is an 1-erasure probabilistic optimal dual of
F.

(ii)

The canonical dual is an 1-erasure probabilistic spectrally optimal dual of
F.

Proof 12

Suppose the canonical dual is an 1-erasure probabilistic optimal dual of
F.
Then for any dual
G=\{g_{i}\}_{i=1}^{N}
of
F
satisfy :

\displaystyle{max_{i=1}^{N}\;q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}\;\;\leq\;\;max_{i=1}^{N}\;q_{i}\|f_{i}\|\;\|g_{i}\|}

(4.12)

Let
G^{\prime}=\{g^{\prime}_{i}\}_{i=1}^{N}=\{f_{i}+u_{i}\}_{i=1}^{N}
be a dual frame of
F
such that

\displaystyle{max_{i=1}^{N}\;q_{i}|\langle f_{i},g^{\prime}_{i}\rangle|\;\;<\;\;max_{i=1}^{N}\;q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}}

(4.13)

Let
c=max_{i=1}^{N}q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}.

Consider
\Lambda_{1}=\{i:q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}=c\}
and
\Lambda_{2}=\{1,2,...,N\}\setminus\Lambda_{1}.

From (
4.13
) we have for all
i\in\Lambda_{1}

q_{i}|\langle f_{i},g^{\prime}_{i}\rangle|=q_{i}|\langle f_{i},f_{i}+u_{i}\rangle|=\left|q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}+q_{i}\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle\right|=\left|c+q_{i}\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle\right|<c

This implies
\mathrm{Re}\left(q_{i}\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle\right)<0
, and hence
\mathrm{Re}\left(\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle\right)<0
, for all
i\in\Lambda_{1}.

As
\mathrm{Re}(\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle)<0
, for all
i\in\Lambda_{1}.
we can take
\varepsilon_{1}>0
small enough such that for all
i\in\Lambda_{1}

q_{i}^{2}\|f_{i}+\varepsilon_{1}u_{i}\|^{2}\|f_{i}\|^{2}=q_{i}^{2}\left(\|f_{i}\|^{2}+\varepsilon_{1}^{2}\|u_{i}\|^{2}+2\varepsilon_{1}\textit{Re}\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle\right)\|f_{i}\|^{2}<q_{i}^{2}\|f_{i}\|^{4}=c^{2}

Therefore for all
i\in\Lambda_{1}

q_{i}\|f_{i}+\varepsilon_{1}u_{i}\|\;\|f_{i}\|<c

(4.14)

As
q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}<max_{j=1}^{N}q_{j}\|f_{j}\|^{2}
for all
i\in\Lambda_{2},
then we can choose
\varepsilon_{2}>0
small enough such that

q_{i}^{2}\|f_{i}+\varepsilon_{2}u_{i}\|^{2}\|f_{i}\|^{2}=\left(q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}+q_{i}\varepsilon_{2}^{2}\|u_{i}\|^{2}+2q_{i}\varepsilon_{2}\textit{Re}\langle f_{i},u_{i}\rangle\right)q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}<max_{j=1}^{N}\;q_{j}^{2}\|f_{i}\|^{4}=c^{2}.

Hence for all
i\in\Lambda_{2}

q_{i}\|f_{i}+\varepsilon_{1}u_{i}\|\;\|f_{i}\|<c

(4.15)

Take
\varepsilon:=min\{\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}\}

Therefore the dual frame
G^{\prime\prime}=\{f_{i}+\varepsilon u_{i}\}_{i=1}^{N}
of
F
satisfies

q_{i}\|f_{i}+\varepsilon u_{i}\|\;\|f_{i}\|<c=max_{i=1}^{N}q_{i}\|f_{i}\|^{2}\;\;\;\textit{for all }1\leq i\leq N.

This implies

Which is a contradiction.

Conversely, let the canonical dual is 1-erasure probabilistic spectrally optimal dual of

of

\square
