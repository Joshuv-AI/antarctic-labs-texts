Lemma 2.2

The number of vertices at a fixed distance from a broadcasting vertex has been previously studied; we refer to the work of

For a fixed vertex

which counts the number of vertices a distance of

The formula for the coordination sequence of a vertex is entry
[

]
and is
given by

and thereafter
a(3k)=8k
,
a(3k+1)=8k+3
,

.
The coordination sequence begins with

1,3,5,8,11,13,16,19,21,24,27,29,32,35,37,\ldots.

We adapt the definition of the coordination sequence for our purposes as follows.

Definition 3.1
.

For

th entry in the coordination sequence. That is,
