Theoretical study of the process D s + → π + ​ K S 0 ​ K S 0 D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S} and the isovector partner of f 0 ​ ( 1710 ) f_{0}(1710)
Xin Zhu, De-Min Li, En Wang, Li-Sheng Geng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We present a theoretical study of a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) , the isovector partner of f 0 ​ ( 1710 ) f_{0}(1710) , in the process D s + → π + ​ K S 0 ​ K S 0 D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S} . The weak interaction part proceeds through the charm quark decay process: c ​ ( s ¯ ) → ( s + d ¯ + u ) ​ ( s ¯ ) c(\bar{s})\to(s+\bar{d}+u)(\bar{s}) , while the hadronization part takes place in two mechanisms, differing in how the quarks from the weak decay combines into π ​ K ∗ \pi K^{*} with a quark-antiquark pair q ​ q ¯ q\bar{q} with the vacuum quantum numbers. In addition to the contribution from the tree diagram of the K ∗ ⁣ + → π + ​ K S 0 K^{*+}\to\pi^{+}K^{0}_{S} , we have also considered the K ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ K^{*}\bar{K}^{*} final-state interactions within the chiral unitary approach to generate the intermediate state a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) , then it decays into the final states K S 0 ​ K S 0 K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S} . We find that the recent experimental measurements on the K S 0 ​ K S 0 K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S} and π + ​ K S 0 \pi^{+}K^{0}_{S} invariant mass distributions can be well reproduced, and the proposed mechanism can provide valuable information on the nature of scalar f 0 ​ ( 1710 ) f_{0}(1710) and its isovector partner a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) .

In this section, we will present the formalism for the decay

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{b}

\displaystyle+({\rm exchange~term~with}~p_{2}\leftrightarrow p_{3}),

II.3

We can write the total decay amplitude of
D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S}

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{M}_{a}+\mathcal{M}_{b},

(27)

is

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}\Gamma}{dM_{K_{S}^{0}K_{S}^{0}}{dM_{{\pi}K_{S}^{0}}}}=\frac{M_{K_{S}^{0}K_{S}^{0}}M_{{\pi}K_{S}^{0}}}{128\pi^{3}m_{D_{s}^{+}}^{3}}(|\mathcal{M}_{a}|^{2}+|\mathcal{M}_{b}|^{2}),

(28)

where the interference between
{\cal M}_{a}
and
{\cal M}_{b}

In Ref.
BESIII:2021anf

and
K^{*+}

(D^{+}_{s}\to\bar{K}^{0}K^{*+})

(1.8\pm 0.2\pm 0.1)\%
, which deviates from the CLEO result of Br
(D^{+}_{s}\to\bar{K}^{0}K^{*+})=(5.4\pm 1.2)\%

CLEO:1989zcg
. In this work, since the interference term is not included, we use the CLEO result to determine the value of the coupling constant
g_{D_{s}\bar{K}K^{*}}

Finally, one can easily obtain
{d\Gamma}/{dM_{K_{S}^{0}K_{S}^{0}}}
and

\displaystyle\left(E_{\pi^{+}}^{\ast}+E_{K_{S}^{0}}^{\ast}\right)^{2}-

\displaystyle\left(\sqrt{E_{\pi^{+}}^{\ast 2}-m_{\pi^{+}}^{2}}+\sqrt{E_{K_{S}^{0}}^{\ast 2}-m_{K_{S}^{0}}^{2}}\right)^{2},

, the vector-vector intermediate states were produced at the first step with both the external and internal

IV
Summary

In summary, we have studied the Cabibbo-favored process of
D_{s}^{+}\rightarrow\pi^{+}K_{S}^{0}K_{S}^{0}
.
By considering the decay mechanism of internal
W^{+}
emission, and hadronization of the
s\bar{d}
or
u\bar{s}
with
q\bar{q}
with the vacuum quantum numbers, we obtain
\pi^{+}K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
in the first step, then the transition of
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}\to K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S}
proceeds following final-state interactions of the
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
pair in the chiral unitary approach where the
a_{0}(1710)
state is dynamically generated. In addition, the tree diagram of
K^{*+}\to\pi^{+}K^{0}_{S}
is also taken into account.

We have calculated the
K^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S}
and
\pi^{+}K^{0}_{S}
invariant mass distributions, which are in good agreement with the experimental measurements of BESIII
BESIII:2021anf
. We have found that the
K^{*}
plays an important role in the
a_{0}(1710)
peak region. Our study shows that the BESIII measurements support the
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
molecular nature of the
a_{0}(1710)
and
f_{0}(1710)
states and they overlap strongly in the data.

For the reproduction of the
a_{0}(1710)
peak, it is found that the contributions from both the
tree diagram as shown in Fig.
4
and the
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
final-state interaction as shown in Fig.
2
are crucial. In addition, within the proposed mechanism, it is expected that the charged
a^{+}_{0}(1710)
signal can show up in the
K^{+}K^{0}_{S}
invariant mass distribution of the
D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{0}K^{+}K^{0}_{S}
decay, which should be checked by future experimental measurements.
