Spin correlation in two-proton emission from 6 Be
Tomohiro Oishi

Abstract
This paper presents a theoretical evaluation of spin correlation in the two-proton ( 2 ​ p 2p ) radioactive emission. The three-body model of 6 Be with the proton-proton interaction, which is adjusted to reproduce the experimental energy release, is utilized. Time-dependent calculation is performed to compute the coupled-spin state of the emitted two protons. The spin-correlation function S S as the Clauser-Horne-Shimony-Holt (CHSH) indicator is evaluated as | S | ≅ 2.65 |S|\cong 2.65 . Namely, the 2 ​ p 2p -spin correlation beyond the limit of local-hidden-variable (LHV) theory is suggested. This correlation is sensitive to the proton-proton interaction. The short-lived (broad-width) 2 ​ p 2p state has the weaker spin correlation. In parallel, the core-proton interactions do not harm this correlation during the time-dependent decaying process. The CHSH measurement can be a novel probe into the effective nuclear interaction inside finite systems. Two-proton emitters can provide a testing field for identical-particle entanglement.

II
Formalism and Model

I consider the coupling of two protons, i.e., identical spin-
1/2
fermions, and simulate the CHSH examination.
First, the four options of measurement by the two observers, so-called “Alice” and “Bob” conventionally, are introduced:

\displaystyle\hat{A}_{1,\theta}(1)\otimes\hat{B}_{1,\theta}(2),~~\hat{A}_{2,\theta}(1)\otimes\hat{B}_{1,\theta}(2),

\displaystyle\hat{A}_{1,\theta}(1)\otimes\hat{B}_{2,\theta}(2),~~\hat{A}_{2,\theta}(1)\otimes\hat{B}_{2,\theta}(2).

(1)

Namely, Alice observes the first fermion with one chosen from the two options,
\hat{A}_{1,\theta}
and
\hat{A}_{2,\theta}
.
Bob does the second fermion with one chosen from
\hat{B}_{1,\theta}
and
\hat{B}_{2,\theta}
.
Those operators including the parameter angle
\theta
are given as

\begin{array}[]{l}\hat{A}_{1,\theta}(1)=\hat{\sigma}_{z}(1),\\
\hat{A}_{2,\theta}(1)=\hat{\sigma}_{z}(1)\cos 2\theta+\hat{\sigma}_{x}(1)\sin 2\theta,\end{array}

(2)

for Alice, whereas

\begin{array}[]{l}\hat{B}_{1,\theta}(2)=\hat{\sigma}_{z}(2)\cos\theta+\hat{\sigma}_{x}(2)\sin\theta,\\
\hat{B}_{2,\theta}(2)=\hat{\sigma}_{z}(2)\cos\theta-\hat{\sigma}_{x}(2)\sin\theta,\end{array}

(3)

for Bob.
For an arbitrary two-fermion state
\ket{\Psi(1,2)}
, their expectation values are obtained:

\Braket{A_{i}B_{j}}=\Braket{\Psi(1,2)\mid\hat{A}_{i,\theta}(1)\otimes\hat{B}_{j,\theta}(2)\mid\Psi(1,2)}.

(4)

Then the CHSH indicator
S
is determined as

S=\max\left\{\left|S_{-+++}\right|,\left|S_{+-++}\right|,\left|S_{++-+}\right|,\left|S_{+++-}\right|\right\},

(5)

where

\displaystyle S_{-+++}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\Braket{A_{1}B_{1}}+\Braket{A_{2}B_{1}}+\Braket{A_{1}B_{2}}+\Braket{A_{2}B_{2}},

\displaystyle S_{+-++}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Braket{A_{1}B_{1}}-\Braket{A_{2}B_{1}}+\Braket{A_{1}B_{2}}+\Braket{A_{2}B_{2}},

\displaystyle S_{++-+}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Braket{A_{1}B_{1}}+\Braket{A_{2}B_{1}}-\Braket{A_{1}B_{2}}+\Braket{A_{2}B_{2}},

\displaystyle S_{+++-}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Braket{A_{1}B_{1}}+\Braket{A_{2}B_{1}}+\Braket{A_{1}B_{2}}-\Braket{A_{2}B_{2}}.

(6)

In FIG.
1
,
S
is calculated for the spin-singlet state
\ket{\uparrow\downarrow-\downarrow\uparrow}/\sqrt{2}
.
At
\theta=\pi/4,~3\pi/4,~5\pi/4
, and
7\pi/4
, it has the maximum value,
S=2\sqrt{2}
(Tsirelson’s bound)
Cirel’son 1980
.
This situation resembles Sakai’s experiment
Sakai et al. 2006
.
In the following sections,
\theta=\pi/4
.
As well known, when the initial state is one of the Bell states
\left\{\ket{e_{n}}\right\}
, one finds
S=2\sqrt{2}
.
These states read
Nielsen and Chuang 2000

\displaystyle\ket{e_{1}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\uparrow\uparrow+\downarrow\downarrow},~~~~~\ket{e_{2}}=\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\uparrow\uparrow-\downarrow\downarrow},

\displaystyle\ket{e_{3}}=\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\uparrow\downarrow+\downarrow\uparrow},~~~~~\ket{e_{4}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\uparrow\downarrow-\downarrow\uparrow}.

(7)

They can be used as basis for an arbitrary total-spin state of two spin-
1/2
fermions.

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\ket{d_{2}}

In addition, the actual

\displaystyle\Psi(\bm{r}_{1}\sigma_{1},\bm{r}_{2}\sigma_{2})

\displaystyle\Braket{\bm{r}_{1}\sigma_{1},\bm{r}_{2}\sigma_{2}\mid\Psi(1,2)}

For computing the expectation values in Eq. (

\displaystyle\Braket{A_{i}B_{j}}

\displaystyle\hat{H}_{3B}=\hat{h}(\bm{r}_{1})+\hat{h}(\bm{r}_{2})+v_{pp}(\bm{r}_{1},\bm{r}_{2})+\frac{\bm{p}_{1}\cdot\bm{p}_{2}}{m_{C}},

\displaystyle\hat{h}(r_{i})=-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\frac{d^{2}}{dr_{i}^{2}}+\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\frac{l(l+1)}{r_{i}^{2}}+V(r_{i}),

\displaystyle v_{nucl}(d)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[V_{R}e^{-a_{R}d^{2}}+V_{S}e^{-a_{S}d^{2}}\right]\hat{P}_{S=0}

(15)

\displaystyle+\left[V_{R}e^{-a_{R}d^{2}}+V_{T}e^{-a_{T}d^{2}}\right]\hat{P}_{S=1}.

\hat{P}_{S=1}

That is,

(16)

where

The

-emitting process is simulated with the time-dependent method

;

\ket{\Psi(t)}=\exp\left[-it\frac{\hat{H}_{3B}}{\hbar}\right]\ket{\Psi(0)},

The decaying state

\ket{\Psi_{d}(t)}=\ket{\Psi(t)}-\beta(t)\ket{\Psi(0)},

(18)
