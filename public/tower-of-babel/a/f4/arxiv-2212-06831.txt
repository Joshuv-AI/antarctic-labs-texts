Abstract
. In this work we prove analogues of Bessel inequality and Riesz-Fisher theorem in Hilbert spaces with respect to sequences. We apply our generalized Bessel inequality to the Hilbert spaces associated with the Normal, Beta, Gamma and certain discrete probability distributions to show how to generate certain type of inequalities for special functions systematically.

33C20; 33C05; 33C10; 33C45.

1.
Introduction

Let
\left\{\phi_{n}(x)\right\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
be an orthonormal
sequence of functions in the Hilbert space
L^{2}(a,b)
, then the
infinite matrix
\left(a_{m,n}\right)_{m,n\in\mathbb{N}}

(1.1)

a_{n,m}=\int_{a}^{b}\phi_{m}(x)\overline{\phi_{n}(x)}dx=\delta_{m,n},\quad n\in\mathbb{N}

defines the identity operator on the Hilbert space
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})
,
and for any
f\in L^{2}(a,b)
the Bessel inequality for the orthonormal
system is given by

(1.2)

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|\int_{a}^{b}f(x)\overline{\phi_{n}(x)}dx\right|^{2}\leq\int_{a}^{b}\left|f(x)\right|^{2}dx.

In
[
4
]
Bellman proved that, under a compact perturbation
to the identity operator on
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})
,

(1.3)

\left(a_{m,n}\right)_{m,n\in\mathbb{N}}=\left(\delta_{m,n}\right)_{m,n\in\mathbb{N}}+\left(b_{m,n}\right)_{m,n\in\mathbb{N}},\quad\sum_{m,n=1}^{\infty}\left|b_{m,n}\right|^{2}<\infty,

certain modified version of Riesz-Fisher theorem and Bessel inequality
are still valid in
L^{2}(a,b)
.

In this work we show that different versions of modified Riesz-Fisher
theorem and Bessel inequality hold on any Hilbert space under the
condition

(1.4)

\sup_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|=\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|<\infty.

We apply our generalized Bessel inequality to the Hilbert spaces associated
with the Normal, Beta, Gamma and certain discrete probability distributions
to show how to generate certain type of inequalities for special functions
systematically. It is clear that these are not the only possible choices
for the Hilbert spaces and sequences. For example, one can choose
the Hilbert space to be any
L^{2}
spaces associated with orthogonal
polynomials on the real line, and the sequence to be functions from
a generalized moment problem,
[
1
,
5
,
8
]
.

On the Hilbert space

(1.5)

\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})=\left\{\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigg|\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|x_{n}\right|^{2}<\infty,\ x_{n}\in\mathbb{C}\right\}

we denote

(1.6)

\left\langle\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty},\left\{y_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\right\rangle_{\ell^{2}}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x_{n}\overline{y_{n}},

and

(1.7)

\left\|\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=\sqrt{\left\langle\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty},\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\right\rangle_{\ell^{2}}}

for its inner product and norm. Given any bounded linear operator

A
on
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})
, its operator norm is given by

(1.8)

\left\|A\right\|_{2}=\sup_{x\neq 0,\,x\in\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})}\frac{\left\|Ax\right\|_{\ell^{2}}}{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}}=\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\left\|Ax\right\|_{\ell^{2}}.

A complex infinite matrix
\left(a_{m,n}\right)_{m,n\in\mathbb{N}}

is called symmetric if

(1.9)

\overline{a_{m,n}}=a_{n,m},\quad m,n\in\mathbb{N}.

2.
Main Results

Lemma 1
.

Let
A=\left(a_{m,n}\right)_{m,n=1}^{\infty}
be any
symmetric matrix such that

(2.1)

C=\sup_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|=\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|<\infty.

Then
A
defines a bounded self-adjoint operator on
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})

satisfies

(2.2)

\left\|A\right\|_{2}=\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\left\|Ax\right\|_{\ell^{2}}\leq C.

Furthermore, if it satisfies any of the following three additional
conditions:

(2.3)

\sum_{m,n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{n,m}\right|^{2}<\infty,

(2.4)

\lim_{N\to\infty}\sup_{m\geq N+1}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\right)=0,

or

(2.5)

\lim_{N\to\infty}\sup_{n\geq 1}\left(\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left|a_{n,m}\right|\right)=0,

then
A
is a compact self-adjoint operator on
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})
.

Proof.

For any
N\in\mathbb{N}
the operator norms of
A^{(N)}=\left(a_{m,n}\right)_{m,n=1}^{N}

on
\ell^{1}(\mathbb{C}^{N}),\ell^{2}(\mathbb{C}^{N}),\ell^{\infty}(\mathbb{C}^{N})

satisfy
[
7
]

\left\|A^{(N)}\right\|_{1}=\max_{1\leq n\leq N}\sum_{m=1}^{N}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\leq C,\quad\left\|A^{(N)}\right\|_{\infty}=\max_{1\leq m\leq N}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\leq C

and

\left\|A^{(N)}\right\|_{2}\leq\sqrt{\left\|A^{(N)}\right\|_{1}\cdot\left\|A^{(N)}\right\|_{\infty}}\leq C.

Then for any
N\in\mathbb{N}
and
x=\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty},\,y=\left\{y_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\in\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})
,

\sum_{m,n=1}^{N}\left|a_{m,n}x_{m}\overline{y_{n}}\right|\leq C\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}\cdot\left\|y\right\|_{\ell^{2}}<\infty.

Let
N\to\infty
in the above inequality to obtain

\sum_{m,n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}x_{m}\overline{y_{n}}\right|=\sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}x_{m}\overline{y_{n}}\right|\leq C\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}\cdot\left\|y\right\|_{\ell^{2}}<\infty.

Then by Fubini’s theorem,

<x,Ay>_{\ell^{2}}=<Ax,y>_{\ell^{2}}

and

\left|<x,Ay>_{\ell^{2}}\right|=\left|<Ax,y>_{\ell^{2}}\right|\leq C\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}\cdot\left\|y\right\|_{\ell^{2}}

hold for any

. Thus
A
is self-adjoint
with
\left\|A\right\|_{2}\leq C
,
[
2
]
.

For each
N\in\mathbb{N}
, let

A^{(N)}=\left(a_{m,n}^{(N)}\right)_{m,n=1}^{\infty},\quad a_{m,n}^{(N)}=\begin{cases}a_{m,n},&1\leq m\leq N,\\
0,&m\geq N+1.\end{cases}

For
x\in\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N}_{0})
the
m

A^{(N)}x

is

Then the range of

is

is a compact operator on
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{N})
,
[
2
]
.

If (
2.3
) is satisfied, then

\lim_{N\to+\infty}\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{n,m}\right|^{2}=0

and

\displaystyle\left\|A-A^{(N)}\right\|_{2}^{2}=\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\left\|\left(A-A^{(N)}\right)x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}=\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left|\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{m,n}x_{n}\right|^{2}

Since compact operators form a closed ideal in the norm limit, then

]

Similarly, observe that

\displaystyle\left\|A-A^{(N)}\right\|_{2}^{2}=\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\left\|\left(A-A^{(N)}\right)x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}=\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left|\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{m,n}x_{n}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sqrt{\left|a_{m,n}\right|}\cdot\left(\sqrt{\left|a_{m,n}\right|}\left|x_{n}\right|\right)\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\right)\cdot\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\cdot\left|x_{n}\right|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{m\geq N+1}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\right)\cdot\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\left(\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\cdot\left|x_{n}\right|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{m\geq N+1}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\right)\cdot\sup_{\left\|x\right\|_{\ell^{2}}=1}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left|a_{n,m}\right|\right)\cdot\left|x_{n}\right|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle=\sup_{m\geq N+1}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|a_{m,n}\right|\right)\cdot\sup_{n\geq 1}\left(\sum_{m=N+1}^{\infty}\left|a_{n,m}\right|\right).

If (
2.4
) or (

) is satisfied, then we have

Therefore, under (

) or (

is still

Theorem 2
.

Let
\left\{\phi_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}
be a

\mathcal{H}

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|(f,\phi_{n})\right|^{2}\leq C\left\|f\right\|^{2}.

Proof.

For any
N\in\mathbb{N}

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left|(f,\phi_{n})\right|^{2}=\left(f,\sum_{n=1}^{N}(f,\phi_{n})\phi_{n}\right)\leq\left\|f\right\|\cdot\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{N}(f,\phi_{n})\phi_{n}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|f\right\|\cdot\sqrt{\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{N}(f,\phi_{n})\phi_{n}\right\|^{2}}=\left\|f\right\|\cdot\sqrt{\left(\sum_{m=1}^{N}(f,\phi_{m})\phi_{m},\sum_{n=1}^{N}(f,\phi_{n})\phi_{n}\right)}

\displaystyle=\left\|f\right\|\cdot\sqrt{\sum_{m,n=1}^{N}a_{m,n}(f,\phi_{m})\overline{(f,\phi_{n})}}.

Notice that

0\leq\sum_{m,n=1}^{N}a_{m,n}(f,\phi_{m})\overline{(f,\phi_{n})}\leq C\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left|(f,\phi_{n})\right|^{2},

The following is an analogue of the Riesz-Fisher theorem.
