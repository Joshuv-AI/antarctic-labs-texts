Interpreting dark matter solution for B − L B-L gauge symmetry
Phung Van Dong

Abstract
It is shown that the solution for B − L B-L gauge symmetry with B − L = − 4 , − 4 , + 5 B-L=-4,-4,+5 assigned for three right-handed neutrinos respectively, reveals a novel scotogenic mechanism with implied matter parity for neutrino mass generation and dark matter stability. Additionally, the world with two-component dark matter is hinted.
