Evidence of a higher nodal band α \alpha + 44 Ca cluster state in fusion reactions and α \alpha clustering in 48 Ti
S. Ohkubo

Abstract
In the nucleus 48 Ti, whose structure is essential in evaluating the half-life of the neutrinoless double- β \beta decay ( 0 ​ ν ​ β ​ β 0\nu\beta\beta ) of 48 Ca, the existence of the α \alpha cluster structure is shown for the first time. A unified description of scattering and structure is performed for the α \alpha + 44 Ca system. By using a global potential, which reproduces experimental α \alpha + 44 Ca scattering over a wide range of incident energies, E α E_{\alpha} =18 - 100 MeV, it is shown that the observed α \alpha + 44 Ca fusion excitation function at E α E_{\alpha} =9 -18 MeV is described well. The bump at E α E_{\alpha} =10.2 MeV is found to be due to a resonance which is a 7 − 7^{-} state of the higher nodal band with the α \alpha + 44 Ca cluster structure in 48 Ti. The local potential α \alpha + 44 Ca cluster model locates the ground band of 48 Ti in agreement with experiment and reproduces the enhanced B ⁡ ( E ​ 2 ) B(E2) values in the ground band well. This shows that collectivity due to α \alpha clustering in 48 Ti should be taken into account in the evaluation of the nuclear matrix element in the 0 ​ ν ​ β ​ β 0\nu\beta\beta double- β \beta decay of 48 Ca.
