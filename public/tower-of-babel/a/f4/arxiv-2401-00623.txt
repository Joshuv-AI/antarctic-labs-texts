1.
Introduction and main results

In this article, we investigate the existence of solutions for the following generalized Chern-Simons-Schrödinger
(
CSS
in short) system/equation with supercritical exponential growth

(1.1)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle-\Delta u+\lambda u+A_{0}u+\sum\limits_{j=1}^{2}A_{j}^{2}u=f(u),\\
\displaystyle\partial_{1}A_{2}-\partial_{2}A_{1}=-\frac{1}{2}|u|^{2},\penalty\ \partial_{1}A_{1}+\partial_{2}A_{2}=0,\\
\displaystyle\partial_{1}A_{0}=A_{2}|u|^{2},\penalty\ \partial_{2}A_{0}=-A_{1}|u|^{2},\end{array}\right.

under the constraint

(1.2)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|u|^{2}dx=a^{2},

where
a\neq 0
,
\lambda\in\mathbb{R}
is known as the Lagrange multiplier
and
f\in\mathcal{C}^{0}(\mathbb{R})
denotes the nonlinearity that fulfills the
supercritical exponential growth in the Trudinger-Moser sense at infinity which would be specified later.

In recent years, the following time-dependent CSS system in two spatial dimension

(1.3)

\begin{cases}\text{i}D_{0}\psi+(D_{1}D_{1}+D_{2}D_{2})\psi+g(|\psi|^{2})\psi=0,\\
\partial_{0}A_{1}-\partial_{1}A_{0}=-\text{Im}(\overline{\psi}D_{2}\psi),\\
\partial_{0}A_{2}-\partial_{2}A_{0}=\text{Im}(\overline{\psi}D_{1}\psi),\\
\partial_{1}A_{2}-\partial_{2}A_{1}=-\frac{1}{2}|\psi|^{2},\\
\end{cases}

is usually exploited to describe the non-relativistic dynamics behavior
of massive number of particles in Chern-Simons gauge fields,
where i stands for the imaginary unit,

\partial_{0}=\frac{\partial}{\partial t}
,
\partial_{1}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{1}}
,
\partial_{2}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{2}}

for
(t,x_{1},x_{2})\in\mathbb{R}^{1+2}
,
\psi:\mathbb{R}^{1+2}\to\mathbb{C}
is the complex scalar field,
A_{j}:\mathbb{R}^{1+2}\to\mathbb{R}
denotes the gauge field,

D_{j}=\partial_{j}+\text{i}A_{j}
is the covariant derivative for
j=0,1,2

and the function
g
is the nonlinearity.

This model plays an important role in
the study of the high-temperature superconductor, Aharovnov-Bohm scattering, and quantum Hall effect,
we refer the reader to
[
25
,
26
,
27
]
. Moreover, there are some further physical motivations
for considering CSS system (
1.3
), see e.g.

[
18
,

,

]

For
j=0,1,2
, we always deal with
A_{j}(t,x)=A_{j}(x)
for all
(t,x_{1},x_{2})\in\mathbb{R}^{1+2}
.
If the standing wave ansatz
\psi(t,x)=e^{\text{i}\lambda t}u(x)
with a given
\lambda\in\mathbb{R}

for
u:\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}
, then (
1.3
) reduces to

(1.4)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle-\Delta u+\lambda u+A_{0}u+\sum_{j=1}^{2}A_{j}^{2}u=f(u),\\
\displaystyle\partial_{1}A_{2}-\partial_{2}A_{1}=-\frac{1}{2}|u|^{2},\\
\displaystyle\partial_{1}A_{0}=A_{2}|u|^{2},\penalty\ \partial_{2}A_{0}=-A_{1}|u|^{2},\end{array}\right.

where
f(u)=g(|u|^{2})u
. Let us suppose that the gauge field
A_{j}
satisfies the Coulomb gauge condition

\sum_{j=0}^{2}\partial_{j}A_{j}=0
, then
(
1.4
) becomes the original CSS equation (
1.1
), namely

(1.5)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle-\Delta u+\lambda u+A_{0}u+\sum_{j=1}^{2}A_{j}^{2}u=f(u),\\
\displaystyle\partial_{1}A_{0}=A_{2}|u|^{2},\penalty\ \partial_{2}A_{0}=-A_{1}|u|^{2},\\
\displaystyle\partial_{1}A_{2}-\partial_{2}A_{1}=-\frac{1}{2}|u|^{2},\penalty\ \partial_{1}A_{1}+\partial_{2}A_{2}=0.\end{array}\right.

Since
\partial_{1}A_{0}=A_{2}|u|^{2}
and
\partial_{2}A_{0}=-A_{1}|u|^{2}

in (
1.5
), there holds

\Delta A_{0}=\partial_{1}\big(A_{2}|u|^{2}\big)-\partial_{2}\big(A_{1}|u|^{2}\big),

which leads to

(1.6)

A_{0}[u](x)=\frac{x_{1}}{2\pi|x|^{2}}\ast\big(A_{2}|u|^{2}\big)-\frac{x_{2}}{2\pi|x|^{2}}\ast\big(A_{1}|u|^{2}\big).

Analogously, adopting
\partial_{1}A_{2}-\partial_{2}A_{1}=-\frac{1}{2}|u|^{2}
and

\partial_{1}A_{1}+\partial_{2}A_{2}=0
in (
1.5
) to deduce that

\Delta A_{1}=\partial_{2}\bigg(\frac{|u|^{2}}{2}\bigg)\penalty\ \text{and}\penalty\ \Delta A_{2}=-\partial_{1}\bigg(\frac{|u|^{2}}{2}\bigg).

From which,
the components
A_{j}
for
j=1,2
in (
1.5

(1.7)

A_{1}[u](x)=\frac{x_{2}}{2\pi|x|^{2}}\ast\bigg(\frac{|u|^{2}}{2}\bigg)=-\frac{1}{4\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{(x_{2}-y_{2})u^{2}(y)}{|x-y|^{2}}dy,

(1.8)

A_{2}[u](x)=-\frac{x_{1}}{2\pi|x|^{2}}\ast\bigg(\frac{|u|^{2}}{2}\bigg)=\frac{1}{4\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{(x_{1}-y_{1})u^{2}(y)}{|x-y|^{2}}dy.

When there is no misunderstanding, for simplicity we shall write shortly

As we all know, the mass of each solution to the Cauchy problem for system (

This

S(a)\triangleq\bigg\{u\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{2}):\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|u|^{2}dx=a^{2}\bigg\}.

Moreover, from mathematical perspectives, the consideration of normalized solutions turns out to be also
meaningful since it benefits from understanding dynamical properties of stationary solutions to
equations like (

Remark 1.3

In light of the nonlinearity

The critical exponential case has been studied in

the following three important contributions which can be regarded as a partial motivation to
contemplate the problems behind this article.

(1) We do not require any compact condition which allows us to study a wider class of CSS equations.
Explaining more precisely, to overcome the loss of compactness, authors in
[

look for solutions
in the radially symmetric subspace

[

(2) Although with the above two types of compact imbedding results in hands, authors in

[

\displaystyle=-A_{1}[u](x)-\frac{t}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{x_{2}-y_{2}}{|x-y|^{2}}u(y)v(y)dy-t^{2}A_{1}[v](x)

leading to

\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{A_{1}[u+tv](x)-A_{1}[u](x)}{t}=-\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{x_{2}-y_{2}}{|x-y|^{2}}u(y)v(y)dy.

Arguing as a very similar way, by means of (
1.8
), there holds,

\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{A_{2}[u+tv](x)-A_{2}[u](x)}{t}=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{x_{1}-y_{1}}{|x-y|^{2}}u(y)v(y)dy.

From which, we apply the Fubini’s theorem jointly with (
1.6
) to deduce that

\begin{gathered}\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{1}{2t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\sum_{j=1}^{2}\left(A_{j}^{2}[u+tv]-A_{j}^{2}[u]\right)|u|^{2}dx\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ =\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{1}{2t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\sum_{j=1}^{2}\left(A_{j}[u+tv]+A_{j}[u])(A_{j}[u+tv]-A_{j}[u])\right)|u|^{2}dx\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ =\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{1}[u]\left(-\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{x_{2}-y_{2}}{|x-y|^{2}}u(y)v(y)dy\right)u^{2}dx\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ +\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{2}[u]\left(\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{x_{1}-y_{1}}{|x-y|^{2}}u(y)v(y)dy\right)u^{2}dx\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ =\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(-\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{x_{2}-y_{2}}{|x-y|^{2}}A_{1}[u](x)u^{2}(x)dx\right)u(y)v(y)dy\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ +\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{(x_{1}-y_{1})}{|x-y|^{2}}A_{2}[u](x)u^{2}(x)dx\right)u(y)v(y)dy\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ =\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{0}[u](x)u(x)v(x)dx.\hfill\\
\end{gathered}

Due to
E(u_{t_{u}})=\max_{t>0}E(u_{t})
for all
u\in S(a)
by Lemma
2.5
,
then

\displaystyle\Psi(u+tv)-\Psi(u)

\displaystyle=E\big((u+tv)_{t_{u+tv}}\big)-E(u_{t_{u}})\leq E\big((u+tv)_{t_{u+tv}}\big)-E(u_{t_{u+tv}})

\displaystyle=\frac{t_{u+tv}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\big[|\nabla(u+tv)|^{2}+(A^{2}_{1}[u+tv]+A^{2}_{2}[u+tv])|u+tv|^{2}]dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ -\frac{t_{u+tv}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\big[|\nabla u|^{2}+(A_{1}^{2}[u]+A_{2}^{2}[u])|u|^{2}\big]dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ -t_{u+tv}^{-2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[F(t_{u+tv}u+tt_{u+tv}v)-F(t_{u+tv}u)]dx

\displaystyle=\frac{t_{u+tv}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[2t\nabla u\nabla v+t^{2}|\nabla v|^{2}+(A_{1}^{2}[u+tv]+A_{2}^{2}[u+tv])(2tuv+t^{2}|v|^{2})]dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ +\frac{t_{u+tv}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(A_{1}^{2}[u+tv]+A_{2}^{2}[u+tv]-A_{1}^{2}[u]-A_{2}^{2}[u])|u|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ -t_{u+tv}^{-2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f(t_{u+tv}u+\sigma_{t}tt_{u+tv}v)tt_{u+tv}vdx,

where
\sigma_{t}\in(0,1)
is determined by the Intermediate Value theorem. Also, we adopt

E\big((u+tv)_{t_{u+tv}}\big)=\max_{t>0}E\big((u+tv)_{t}\big)
to get

\displaystyle\Psi(u+tv)-\Psi(u)

\displaystyle=E\big((u+tv)_{t_{u+tv}}\big)-E(u_{t_{u}})\geq E\big((u+tv)_{t_{u}}\big)-E(u_{t_{u}})

\displaystyle=\frac{t_{u}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\big[|\nabla(u+tv)|^{2}+(A^{2}_{1}[u+tv]+A^{2}_{2}[u+tv])|u+tv|^{2}]dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ -\frac{t_{u}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\big[|\nabla u|^{2}+(A_{1}^{2}[u]+A_{2}^{2}[u])|u|^{2}\big]dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ -t_{u}^{-2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}[F(t_{u}u+tt_{u}v)-F(t_{u}u)]dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ +\frac{t_{u}^{2}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(A_{1}^{2}[u+tv]+A_{2}^{2}[u+tv]-A_{1}^{2}[u]-A_{2}^{2}[u])|u|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ -t_{u}^{-2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f(t_{u}u+\bar{\sigma}_{t}tt_{u}v)tt_{u}vdx,

.

\displaystyle=E^{\prime}({u_{t_{u}}})[v_{t_{u}}]=E^{\prime}(\zeta(u))[v_{t_{u}}]

which is the desired result. The proof is completed.

Lemma A.5.
Let
u,v\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{2})
and suppose
that
{\rm supp}{\hskip 1.42271pt}u_{t}\cap{\rm supp}{\hskip 1.42271pt}v=\emptyset
for all
t>0
,
then

\lim_{t\to 0^{+}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{j}^{2}[u_{t}+v]|u_{t}+v|^{2}dx-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{j}^{2}[u_{t}]|u_{t}|^{2}dx-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{j}^{2}[v]|v|^{2}dx\right)=0.

Proof.

According to (
1.7
) and (
1.8
), for all
t>0
, there holds

A_{j}[u_{t}+v]=A_{j}[u_{t}]+A_{j}[v],\penalty\ j=1,2,

and

|u_{t}+v|^{2}=|u_{t}|^{2}+|v|^{2}.

It follows from some simple calculations that

\begin{gathered}\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{j}^{2}[u_{t}+v]|u_{t}+v|^{2}dx-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{j}^{2}[u_{t}]|u_{t}|^{2}dx-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}A_{j}^{2}[v]|v|^{2}dx\right|\hfill\\

\ \ \ \ \leq|A_{j}[u_{t}]|_{\widehat{r}}^{2}|v|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}+|A_{j}[v]|_{\widehat{r}}^{2}|u_{t}|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}+2|A_{j}[u_{t}]|_{\widehat{r}}|A_{j}[v]|_{\widehat{r}}|u_{t}|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}+|A_{j}[u_{t}]|_{\widehat{r}}|A_{j}[v]|_{\widehat{r}}|v|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ =t^{2}|A_{j}[u]|_{\widehat{r}}^{2}|v|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}+t^{\frac{4}{\widehat{r}}}|A_{j}[v]|_{\widehat{r}}^{2}|u|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}+2t^{1+\frac{4}{\widehat{r}}}|A_{j}[u_{t}]|_{\widehat{r}}|A_{j}[v]|_{\widehat{r}}|u_{t}|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}+t|A_{j}[u_{t}]|_{\widehat{r}}|A_{j}[v]|_{\widehat{r}}|v|_{\frac{2\widehat{r}}{\widehat{r}-2}}^{2}\hfill\\
\ \ \ \ \to 0\hfill\\
