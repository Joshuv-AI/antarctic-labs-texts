The continuous dependence for the Navier-Stokes equations in B ˙ p , r d p − 1 \dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}
Weikui Ye, Wei Luo, and, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
In this paper, we mainly investigate the Cauchy problem for the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations in homogeneous Besov spaces B ˙ p , r d p − 1 \dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r} with 1 ≤ p < ∞ , 1 ≤ r ≤ ∞ , d ≥ 2 1\leq p<\infty,\ 1\leq r\leq\infty,\ d\geq 2 . Firstly, we prove the local existence of the solution and give a lower bound of the lifespan T T of the solution. The lifespan depends on the Littlewood-Paley decomposition of the initial data, that is Δ ˙ j ​ u 0 \dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0} . Secondly, if the initial data u 0 n → u 0 u^{n}_{0}\rightarrow u_{0} in B ˙ p , r d p − 1 \dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r} , then the corresponding lifespan T n → T T_{n}\rightarrow T . Thirdly, we prove that the data-to-solutions map is continuous in B ˙ p , r d p − 1 \dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r} . Therefore, the Cauchy problem of the Navier-Stokes equations is locally well-posed in the critical Besov spaces in the Hadamard sense. Moreover, we also obtain well-posedness and weak-strong uniqueness results in L ∞ ​ L 2 ∩ L 2 ​ H ˙ 1 L^{\infty}L^{2}\cap L^{2}\dot{H}^{1} .

Introduction

In this paper, we mainly investigate the Cauchy problem of the incompressible Navier-Stokes (
NS
) equations:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}u_{t}-\Delta u+u\nabla u+\nabla P=0,\\
div~u=0\\
u|_{t=0}=u_{0},\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

where the unknowns are the vector fields
u=(u_{1},u_{2},...,u_{d})
and the scalar function
P
. Here,
u
is the velocity, respectively, while
P
denotes the pressure.

In the seminal paper
[
25
]
, J. Leray proved the global existence of finite energy weak solutions to the (
NS
) equations. Yet the uniqueness and regularity of such weak solutions are big open questions in
the field of mathematical fluid mechanics except the case when the initial data has special
structure. For instance, with axi-symmetric initial velocity and without swirl component,
O. A. Ladyzhenskaya
[
24
]
and independently M. R. Ukhovskii and V. I. Yudovich
[
26
]
proved the existence of
weak solution along with the uniqueness and regularity of such solution to the (
NS
) equations.
Another popular topic is the partial regularity of the weak solution and the typical work was established by Caffarelli, Kohn and Nirenberg
[
6
]
.
They proved that the singular set
S
to the suitable weak solution satisfies that
P^{1}(S)=0

P^{1}

. Lin
[
7

proposed a simplified proof of the Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg theorem.
The investigation on the weak solutions to (
1.1
) is based on the following energy inequality:

\int u^{2}dx+\int^{T}_{0}\int|\nabla u|^{2}dx\leq\int u^{2}_{0}dx,

for any
T>0
and
u_{0}\in L^{2}
. The energy inequality implies the existence and partial regularity of the weak solutions. However,
T. Buckmaster and V. Vical
[
2
]
proved that the weak solutions with bounded kinetic energy are not unique. This result means that the regularity of solutions is important to study the uniqueness.

In order to study the regularity, it is always transform the system (
1.1
) into the following equations

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}u_{t}-\Delta u=-\mathbb{P}(u\nabla u),\\
u|_{t=0}=u_{0},\end{array}\right.

(1.2)

where
\mathbb{P}=I+\nabla(-\Delta)^{-1}div
is the Leray project operator and the initial data satisfies
div~u_{0}=0
. Using the heat kernel and Duhamel’s principle, one can rewrite the
NS
equations into

u=e^{t\Delta}u_{0}-\int^{t}_{0}e^{(t-s)\Delta}\mathbb{P}div~(u\otimes u)ds.

The function
u
satisfying the above equations is called a mild solution. The investigation for the mild solution is based on Banach’s contraction principle. The key point is to look for the estimates for the bilinear operator

B(u,v)=\int^{t}_{0}e^{(t-s)\Delta}\mathbb{P}div~(u\otimes v)ds,

in some suitable Banach spaces
X
. If the norm
\|e^{t\Delta}u_{0}\|_{X}
is small and
\|B(u,v)\|_{X}\leq C\|u\|_{X}\|v\|_{X}
, then one can prove the existence and uniqueness by virtue of the Banach contraction principle.

On the other hand, J. Bourgain and N. Pavlović

The main difficulty is that the initial data maybe large and has low regularity. If the initial data

\displaystyle\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r})\cap\widetilde{L}^{4}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})\cap\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}}_{p,r})\cap\widetilde{L}^{\frac{4}{3}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})\cap\widetilde{L}^{1}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+1}_{p,r})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}_{0}-u^{\infty}_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}+C_{1}(\|u\|_{\widetilde{L}^{4}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\frac{4}{3}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}+\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{4}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}\|v\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\frac{4}{3}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})})

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}_{0}-u^{\infty}_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}+C_{1}(2E_{0})^{\frac{1}{2}}(2a)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\frac{4}{3}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}+2aC_{1}\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{4}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}_{0}-u^{\infty}_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}+\frac{1}{2}(\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\frac{4}{3}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}+\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{4}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,r})}),

(3.21)

where
C_{1}(2E_{0})^{\frac{1}{2}}(2a)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq\frac{1}{2}
and
2aC_{1}\leq\frac{1}{2}
are based on (
3.3
). Then we have

\displaystyle\|u^{n}-u^{\infty}\|_{L^{\infty}_{T}\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}\cap L^{1}_{T}\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+1}_{p,r}}\leq C\|u^{n}_{0}-u^{\infty}_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}\rightarrow 0,\quad n\rightarrow\infty.

(3.22)

This completes the proof of continuous dependence.

Finally, combining Steps 1-2, we obtain the local well-posedness of the (NS) equation. This completes the proof of Theorem
1.1
.

∎

Similarly, we give the proof of Theorem
1.3
.

The proof of Theorem
1.3
:

Proof.

For
u_{0}\in\bar{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,\infty}
, By the definition of
\bar{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,\infty}
, there exists
j_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
such that

\lim_{|j|\geq j_{0}}2^{\frac{d}{p}-1}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}f\|_{L^{p}}\leq\frac{1}{4}a.

Then letting
T_{0}=\frac{a}{4}\frac{1}{2^{2j_{0}}E_{0}}
and
T_{1}=\frac{a^{2}}{4^{2}}\frac{1}{2^{2j_{0}}E^{2}_{0}}
, we have

\displaystyle\|e^{t\Delta}u_{0}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{1}_{T_{0}}\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+1}_{p,\infty}}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}2^{(\frac{d}{p}+1)j}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}\int_{0}^{T_{0}}e^{-2^{2^{j}}t}dt

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{|j|\leq j_{0}}2^{(\frac{d}{p}+1)j}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}T_{0}+\sup_{|j|>j_{0}}2^{(\frac{d}{p}-1)j}(1-e^{2^{2j}T_{0}})\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}

\displaystyle\leq 2^{2j_{0}}{T_{0}}\|u_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}+\sup_{|j|>j_{0}}2^{(\frac{d}{p}-1)j}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}a,

(3.23)

and

\displaystyle\|e^{t\Delta}u_{0}\|_{L^{2}_{T_{1}}\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}}_{p,\infty}}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}(2^{\frac{d}{p}j}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}(\int_{0}^{T_{1}}e^{-2^{2^{j}}t}dt)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{|j|\leq j_{0}}2^{\frac{d}{p}j}T^{\frac{1}{2}}_{1}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}+\sup_{|j|>j_{0}}2^{(\frac{d}{p}-1)j}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}(1-e^{2^{2j}T_{0}})^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq 2^{j_{0}}T^{\frac{1}{2}}_{1}\|u_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}+\sup_{|j|>j_{0}}2^{(\frac{d}{p}-1)j}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}u_{0}\|_{L^{p}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}a.

(3.24)

Setting
T=\min\{T_{0},T_{1}\}
, the proof is then similar to that of Theorem
1.1
. So we can prove that the Cauchy problem (
1.2
) is locally well-posed in the Hadamard sense.
∎

Finally, we consider the Leray weak solutions with extra conditions on the initial data.

The proof of Theorem
1.5
:

Proof.

Set
u_{0}:=u^{\infty}_{0}
. Applying Theorems 1-2, we can obtain some local well-posed solutions
u^{n}
(
n\in\mathbb{N}\cap\{\infty\}
) in
E^{p}_{T}
(or
\dot{E}_{T}
) for a common lifespan
T=T_{u_{0}}
independent of
n
. They are also the Leray weak solutions. Moreover, for any
n\in\mathbb{N}\cap\{\infty\}
we have

\displaystyle\quad\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(B^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,1})}\leq 2E_{0},

\displaystyle\quad\|u^{n}\|_{A}\leq 2a,\quad A:={\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}}_{p,r})\cap\widetilde{L}^{1}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+1}_{p,r})},

(3.25)

and

\displaystyle a\leq\min\{\frac{1}{16C^{2}_{1}E_{0}},(\frac{\sqrt{E_{0}}}{4C_{1}})^{\frac{2}{3}},\frac{1}{4C_{1}}\}\leq\frac{1}{4C_{1}},

(3.26)

where
E_{0}:=\|u_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-1}_{p,r}}
,
C_{1}\geq C
,
C
is the constant of Lemma
2.4
. In fact, we can choose
C_{1}
much bigger such as
C_{1}=48C
.

Setting
w=u^{n}-u^{\infty}
, then we have

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}w_{t}-\Delta w=-\mathbb{P}div[w\otimes u^{n}+u^{\infty}\otimes w],\\
divw=0,\\
w|_{t=0}=u^{n}_{0}-u_{0}.\end{array}\right.

(3.27)

By Lemma
2.4
, we have

\displaystyle\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})}\leq C(\|u^{n}_{0}-u_{0}\|_{L^{2}}+\|w\otimes u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|u^{\infty}\otimes w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}).

(3.28)

Applying the Bony decomposition, we have:

(1) Setting
\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}_{1}}
with
2<p_{1},p^{\prime}_{1}<\infty
, then

\displaystyle\|T_{u^{n}}w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1}_{\infty,\infty})}\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1-\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1+\frac{d}{p}}_{p,\infty})}\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1+\frac{d}{p}}_{p,\infty})}(\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})}).

(3.29)

(2) Setting
\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{p_{2}}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}_{2}}
with
2<p_{2},p^{\prime}_{2}<\infty
and
p\leq p_{2}<\frac{d}{2}p_{1}<\infty
, then

\displaystyle\|T_{w}u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p_{2}}-\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{p_{2},\infty})}\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p_{1}}-\frac{d}{p_{2}}}_{p^{\prime}_{2},2})}

\displaystyle=\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p_{2}}+\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1}_{p_{2},\infty})}\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1}_{p,\infty})}(\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})}).

(3.30)

(3) Setting
\frac{1}{p_{4}}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{p_{3}}
with
p\leq p_{3}<\infty
and
p_{4}<2
, then

\displaystyle\|R(w,u^{n})\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|R(w,u^{n})\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p_{3}}}_{p_{4},2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p_{3}}-\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{p_{3},\infty})}\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}-\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{p,\infty})}\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{2}{p_{1}}}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1}_{p,\infty})}(\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})}).

(3.31)

Combining interpolation inequality with (

), we deduce that

\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1}_{p,\infty})}\leq\|u^{n}\|^{\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}}_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}}_{p,\infty})}\|u^{n}\|^{1-\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}}_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+1}_{p,\infty})}\leq(2a)^{\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}}2E_{0}^{1-\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}}.

Set

for this

.
Combining (
3
), (
3
) and (
3
) yields that

\displaystyle\|w\otimes u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq 3\|u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{p^{\prime}_{1}}_{T}(\dot{B}^{\frac{d}{p}+\frac{2}{p^{\prime}_{1}}-1}_{p,\infty})}(\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})})

\displaystyle\leq 12a(\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})}).

(3.32)

Similarly for

\displaystyle\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{\infty}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{1}_{2,2})}

\displaystyle\leq C(\|u^{n}_{0}-u_{0}\|_{L^{2}}+\|w\otimes u^{n}\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})}+\|u^{\infty}\otimes w\|_{\widetilde{L}^{2}_{T}(\dot{B}^{0}_{2,2})})

Since
