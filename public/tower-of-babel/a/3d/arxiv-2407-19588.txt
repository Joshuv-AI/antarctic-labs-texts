A pair of small Fermi surfaces, chiral charge-density-wave quantum criticality, and BEC-type finite-momentum pairing instabilities in TiSe 2 Preprint: APS/123-QED
Jin Mo Bok, B. J. Kim, Ki-Seok Kim

Abstract
Superconductivity (SC) near charge-density-wave (CDW) quantum criticality in materials with small Fermi surfaces suggests a novel mechanism of SC such as pair-density-wave (PDW) SC and Bose-Einstein condensation (BEC) beyond the conventional Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (BCS) mechanism of SC. Recent research by Kim et al . [arXiv:2312.11979] proposes how chiral CDW ordering arises in TiSe 2 , characterized by a pair of small Fermi surfaces. Interaction-driven electronic quantum fluctuations described by a polarization bubble between the pair of small Fermi surfaces give rise to UV (ultraviolet) symmetry forbidden but dynamically generated linear electron-lattice couplings at IR (infrared), resulting in a chiral symmetry breaking without chiral instability in either the charge or the lattice sector. This mechanism has been substantiated through Raman spectroscopy, inelastic x-ray scattering (IXS), group theory, and many-body calculations in the random phase approximation (RPA). The emergence of SC in this material, induced by pressure or doping, particularly, combined with the pair of small Fermi surfaces and the possible chiral CDW quantum criticality, indicates that conventional interpretations on thermodynamics and magnetic responses based on the BCS theory may not be sufficient. In this study, we propose that BEC-type SC appears, driven by chiral CDW quantum critical fluctuations, which provides a robust explanatory framework for this phenomenon. As an inevitable consequence, we demonstrate that electrons in the p p and d d orbitals form interorbital Cooper pairs with finite center-of-mass momentum, reminiscent of Fulde-Ferrell and Larkin-Ovchinnikov (FFLO) or PDW state. Employing a group theoretical and tight-binding approach to the interorbital pairing, we find that the possibility of unconventional pairing symmetries is restricted, except for the orbital-selective s s -wave pairing symmetry. These findings suggest that a distinct superconducting mechanism, behaving conventionally, may operate in materials exhibiting exotic CDW with small Fermi surfaces.

\displaystyle D^{\alpha\beta}_{\text{RPA}}(i\Omega_{m},\bm{q})=\frac{1}{2}\Big\langle a_{\Gamma_{7}}^{\alpha}(i\Omega_{m},\bm{q})a_{\Gamma_{7}}^{\beta\dagger}(i\Omega_{m},\bm{q})\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\Bigg\{\Big(\Omega_{m}^{2}+\Omega_{\bm{q}}^{2}\Big)\delta_{\alpha\beta}-\frac{\lambda^{2}}{\Omega_{\bm{q}}}\Bigg(\frac{\delta_{\alpha\beta}}{V}\Big(\frac{\delta_{\beta\beta^{\prime}}}{V}+\Pi^{\beta\beta^{\prime}}\Big)^{-1}\Pi^{\beta^{\prime}\beta}

\displaystyle-\Pi^{\alpha\beta}\Bigg(\frac{\delta_{\beta\beta^{\prime}}}{V}+\frac{\delta_{\beta\gamma}}{V}\Big(\frac{\delta_{\gamma\gamma^{\prime}}}{V}+\Pi^{\gamma^{\prime}\beta^{\prime}}\Big)^{-1}\Pi^{\beta^{\prime}\beta}\Bigg)^{-1}\Pi^{\beta^{\prime}\beta}\Bigg)\Bigg\}^{-1}.

Multiple layers of inversion in Eq. (

II.3
Experimental constraints in approaching the QCP

Through IXS experiments, pristine TiSe

is observed to have a phonon mode at 7.4 meV at 300K, which completely softens at 200K.
By fixing this as an experimental constraint, we can determine the bare phonon frequency,

and EPC,

for a given Coulomb interaction,
V
from the CDW instability condition of Eq. (

We then decrease

while keeping the phonon and EPC fixed, moving towards the vicinity of the QCP.
As mentioned earlier, there are two ways to reach the QCP in experiments: doping and pressure.
Doping refers to an increase in the number of electron carriers,
while pressure, as multiple experiments have shown, mainly signifies an increase in hole carriers.
We consider applying pressure to reach the QCP as a desirable way for understanding superconductivity driven by chiral CDW fluctuations.
Therefore, we aim to reach the QCP by increasing the number of hole carriers.
Of course, whether increasing electron carriers or hole carriers, according to the excitonic insulator scenario,

decreases due to the enhanced screening effect.
In our calculations, simply reducing V and shifting the valence band towards

suffices.
That is,

and

We first choose

corresponding to the QCP and determine

to make

zero.
The parameters between QCP and pristine were determined by evenly dividing

and
