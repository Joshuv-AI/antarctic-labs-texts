Dual Nature of Magnetism Driven by Momentum Dependent f f - d d Kondo Hybridization
Byungkyun Kang, Yongbin Lee, Liqin Ke, Hyunsoo Kim, Myoung-Hwan Kim

Abstract
Intricate nature of magnetism in uranium-based Kondo lattices is a consequence of correlations between U-5 f f and conduction electrons. Using linearized quasiparticle self-consistent GW plus dynamical mean-field theory, we demonstrate a crossover from incoherent to coherent f f - d d Kondo cloud in the paramagnetic phase of UTe 2 with reduced volumes, USbTe and USbSe. As the transition occurs, we observe an augmented f f - d d coherence and Pauli-like magnetic susceptibility, with a substantial frozen magnetic moment of U-5 f f persisting. We show that momentum dependent f f - d d hybridization is responsible for the magnetic moments arising from the renormalized f f electrons’ van Hove singularity. Our findings provide a unique perspective to explain the dual nature of magnetism and the long-range magnetic ordering induced by pressure in UTe 2 .

Theoretical studies have proposed the presence of orbital-selective Mott phases (OSMP) to explain the duality observed in multi-band correlated systems
Anisimov et al. 2002
;
Stepanov 2022
;
Kugler and Kotliar 2022

The OSMP has been employed to explain the dual nature of Fe-based compounds.
For example, Kim et al.

Unlike

These factors can affect the hybridization channels, leading to a varying screening of the U-5
f

According to the mutichannel Kondo model

;
Nozieres and Blandin 1980
,
Kondo systems can be classified into three types (under, fully, over-screened) based on the local magnetic moment raised by impurity spin

),

An example of this is the Anderson lattice model of uranium monochalcogenides, where UTe is modelled with

Thomas et al. 2011

However, the RKKY mechanism may not be applicable to coherent U-5

;
Doniach 1977
.

In a Bloch electrons system, van Hove singularity (VHS)
Van Hove 1953
can be attributed as a source of magnetisation.
As the Fermi energy approaches a VHS of the electronic density of states (DOS), the DOS diverges, allowing for weak interactions to have a significant influence on the electronic behavior. This can result in instabilities in charge and spin susceptibilities, leading to substantial enhancement of ferromagnetism
Fleck et al. 1997
;
Hlubina et al. 1997
and antiferromagnetism
Lin and Hirsch 1987
.
The VHS has been associated with the local magnetic moment in a transition metal
Hausoel et al. 2017
and graphene bilayer
Liu et al. 2019
.
By using density functional theory (DFT) combined with dynamical mean field theory (DMFT), Hausoel et al.
Hausoel et al. 2017
recently investigated the paramagnetic phase spectrum of Ni metal. Their results revealed a van Hove magnet at the L point of the Brillouin zone, characterized by a large effective mass and temperature-dependent magnetic susceptibility.
This feature has not been previously observed in U-5
f
Kondo lattice.

In this work, we scrutinized the alteration from disordered to ordered
f
-
d
Kondo hybridization in the paramagnetic phase of Kondo lattices of UTe
2
, UTe
2

We discovered that UTe

This feature was employed to investigate the emergence of long-range magnetic ordering in UTe
2

Investigating the magnetism of UTe
2
under pressure.

To gain a better understanding of the origin of long-range magnetic ordering of UTe
2
at 1.5 GPa, we performed electronic structure calculations of UTe

in its orthorhombic phase with reduced volumes.
It was demonstrated in the preceding section that UTe

The temperature dependent
\chi_{J_{Z}}
shown in Fig.

\chi_{J_{Z}}
with decreasing volume is in agreement with the experimental observation of a decrease in

Similar to USbSe, this coherent

-

Our findings suggest that UTe
2

We discuss other potential causes of long-range magnetic ordering in two distinct scenarios of UTe
2
: fully-screened and under-screened.
In the fully-screened case, the net local magnetic moment is zero. To induce long-range magnetic ordering, the local magnetic moment must appear. Fig.
3
(c) shows that the magnetic moment of U-5
f
decreases with decreasing volume, which is in agreement with the experimental observation of a decrease in effective moments under pressure reported in Ref.
Li et al. 2021

Consequently, the reduced magnetic moment of U-5
f
will remain fully-screened under pressure, with no impurities’ magnetic moment to be coupled.

In the case of under-screened UTe
2
, non-zero U-5
f
magnetic moments can interact through either exchange or RKKY interaction.
It is reasonable to assume negligible direct exchange coupling between neighboring U-5
f
moments, as the
f
-
f
hybridization is negligible due to the small overlap of wave functions between U-5
f
. This is supported by our findings that
f
-
d
hybridization is dominant at low energy.
We also suggest that RKKY interaction can be suppressed under pressure.
Doniach’s work
Doniach 1977
suggests that the transition from an antiferromagnetic to a Kondo-like state is driven by the competition between the binding energy of a Kondo singlet,
W_{K}\sim N(0)^{-1}e^{-1/N(0)J_{0}}
, and that of the RKKY antiferromagnetic state,
W_{AF}\sim J_{0}^{2}N(0)
, where
J_{0}

Conclusion.

By altering the chemical composition or reducing the lattice parameters, uranium-based Kondo lattices are brought into a state of coherent
