A joint explanation of W W -mass and muon g − 2 g-2 in 2HDM
Xiao-Fang Han 1 , Fei Wang 2 , Lei Wang 1 , Jin Min Yang 3,4 , Yang Zhang 2

Abstract
Since both W W -mass and muon g − 2 g-2 can be affected by the mass splittings among extra Higgs bosons ( H , A , H ± ) (H,~A,~H^{\pm}) in a 2HDM, we take a model with μ \mu - τ \tau LFV interactions to examine the two anomalies reported respectively by CDF II and FNAL. We obtain the following observations: (i) Combined with theoretical constraints, the CDF W W -mass measurement disfavors H H or A A to degenerate in mass with H ± H^{\pm} , but allows H H and A A to degenerate. The mass splitting between H ± H^{\pm} and H / A H/A is required to be larger than 10 GeV. The m H ± m_{H^{\pm}} and m A m_{A} are favored to be smaller than 650 GeV for m H < 120 m_{H}<120 GeV, and allowed to have more large values with increasing of m H m_{H} . (ii) After imposing other relevant experimental constraints, there are parameter spaces that simultaneously satisfy (at 2 ​ σ 2\sigma level) the CDF W W -mass, the FNAL muon g − 2 g-2 and the data of lepton universality in τ \tau decays, but the mass splittings among extra Higgs bosons are strictly constrained.
