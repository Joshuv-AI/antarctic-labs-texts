Radon-Nikodym theorem with respect to ( ρ , q ) (\rho,q) -measure on ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p}
Dongkyu Lim

Abstract
. Araci et al. introduced a p p -adic ( ρ , q ) (\rho,q) -analogue of the Haar distribution. By means of the distribution, they constructed the p p -adic ( ρ , q ) (\rho,q) -Volkenborn integral. In this paper, by virtue of the Mahler expansion of continuous functions, the author gives the Radon-Nikodym theorem with respect to the p p -adic ( ρ , q ) (\rho,q) -distribution on ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} .

1.
Introduction

The main object in this paper is the
p
-adic distribution

\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{N}\mathbb{Z}_{p})=\frac{\rho^{p^{N}}}{[p^{N}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}

which was introduced by Araci
et al.

[
1
]
. The
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-distribution is an analogue of Haar distribution based on
(\rho,q)
-numbers. They used it to construct the
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-Volkenborn integral of a function
f\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
as follows:

I_{\rho,q}(f)=\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}f(x)d\mu_{\rho,q}(x)=\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\rho^{p^{N}}}{[p^{N}]_{\rho,q}}\sum_{x=0}^{p^{N}-1}f(x)\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{x}.

The application of the
(\rho,q)
-Volkenborn is an effective way to deduce many important results in
(\rho,q)
-numbers and polynomials.

The original motivation of this paper is the following theorem of Radon-Nikodym.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
[
2
]
, p. 101, Theorem 17.10]
Let
\mu
and
\nu
be measures on a
\sigma
-algebra in a set
X
. If
\mu
is
\sigma
-finite, the following two assertions are equivalent:

(1)

\nu
has a density with respect to
\mu
.

(2)

\nu
is
\mu
-continuous.

The above theorem was proved in 1930 by O. Nikodym
[
14
]
. H. Lebesgue proved the theorem in 1910 for the case where
\mu
is the Lebesgue-Borel measure. J. Radon pushed things further in a fundamental work which appeared in 1913. So Theorem
1.1
is called the theorem of Lebesgue-Radon-Nikodym.
The Radon-Nikodym theorem is a very important tool in the study of Measure Theory since it permits us to decompose a given measure in terms of two others that keep some properties from each other. It is a deep result which demands a previous study of several concepts to the definition of measures like the
\sigma
-algebras, exterior measures, etc.
Various proofs of the Radon-Nikodym theorem can be found in many books on measure theory, analysis, or probability theory. Usually, they use the Hahn decomposition theorem for signed measures, the Riesz representation theorem for functionals on Hilbert spaces, or the martingale theory, (see
[
2
,
3
,
8
,
9
,
16
,
17
,
18
]
).

The
p
-adic numbers were first introduced by K. Hensel in 1897, though with hindsight some of Kummer’s earlier work can be interpreted as implicitly using
p
-adic numbers. The
p
-adic numbers were motivated primarily by an attempt to bring the ideas and techniques of power series methods into number theory. Their influence now extends far beyond this. For example, the field of
p
-adic analysis essentially provides an alternative form of calculus.
The one important tool of these investigations is
p
-adic integral (Volkenborn integral) which is firstly described by Arnt Volkenborn
[
19
,
20
]
in about 1972. The
p
-adic integral was used in mathematical physics, for instance, the functional equations of the Zeta functions, Stirling numbers, and Mahler theory of integration with respect to the ring
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
together with Iwasawa’s
p
-adic
L
functions
cf.

[
13
]
.

The
q
-calculus in the field of special functions was investigated in the last two decades
(see, e.g.,
[
4
,
5
,
6
,
10
,

,

-adic

-adic

[

,

,

[

-adic

The
(\rho,q)
-integer was introduced to generalize or unify several forms of
q

2.
Preliminary

In this section, we give the necessary definitions and notations.

Let
p
be a fixed odd prime number. Throughout this paper, the
symbol
\mathbb{Z}
,
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
,
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
, and
\mathbb{C}_{p}
denote the
ring of rational integers, the ring of
p
-adic integers, the field
of
p
-adic rational numbers, and the completion of algebraic
closure of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
, respectively. Let
\nu_{p}
be the normalized
exponential valuation of
\mathbb{C}_{p}
with
|p|=p^{-\nu_{p}(p)}=p^{-1}
.

Suppose that
d
is an positive number with
(d,p)=1
. Set

X:=X_{d}=\varprojlim_{N}\mathbb{Z}/dp^{N}\mathbb{Z}\ \ \text{and}\ \ X_{1}=\mathbb{Z}_{p}

and

a+dp^{N}\mathbb{Z}_{p}=\{x\in X|x\equiv a\!\!\!\!\pmod{dp^{N}}\}

where
a\in\mathbb{Z}
lies in
0\leq a<dp^{N}
.

Definition 2.1
.

A function
f
is a uniformly differentiable, denoted by
f\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
, if for a given function
f:\mathbb{Z}_{p}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}_{p}
, there exists a continuous function
F_{f}(x,y)\rightarrow\mathbb{C}_{p}

F_{f}(x,y)=\frac{f(x)-f(y)}{x-y},

for all
x,y\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
,
x\neq y
.

In this paper, we assume that
\rho
and
q\in\mathbb{C}_{p}
with
|\rho-1|<p^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}
and
|q-1|<p^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}
as an indeterminate. The
q
-analogue of a fixed number
x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
is that

[x]_{q}=[x:q]=\frac{1-q^{x}}{1-q}.

Observe that
\lim\limits_{q\rightarrow 1}[x]_{q}=x
for arbitrary
x
with
|x|\leq 1
.

The
(\rho,q)
-integer was introduced to generalized or unify several forms of
q
-oscillator algebras well-known in the pyhsics literature related to the representation theory of single parameter quantum algebras, in which the
(\rho,q)
-integer is given by

[n]_{\rho,q}=\frac{\rho^{n}-q^{n}}{\rho-q}

which is a native generalization of the
q
-number, such that

\lim\limits_{\rho\rightarrow 1}[n]_{\rho,q}:=[n]_{q}.

Note that the notation
[n]_{\rho,q}
is symmetric. We also use the following notation for
(\rho,q)
-series:

\displaystyle{n\brack k}_{\rho,q}=\frac{[n]_{\rho,q}!}{[n-k]_{\rho,q}![k]_{\rho,q}!},\quad[n]_{\rho,q}!=[n]_{\rho,q}[n-1]_{\rho,q}\ldots[2]_{\rho,q}[1]_{\rho,q}.

Definition 2.2
.

[
13
]

A
p
-adic distiribution
\mu
on
X
is
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
-linear vector space homomorphism from the
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
-vector space of locally constant functions
X
to
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
. If
f:X\rightarrow\mathbb{Q}_{p}
is locally constant function, instead of writing
\mu(f)
for the value of
\mu
at
f
, we usually write
\int f\mu
.

First, we recall from
[
1
]
the
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
.

Definition 2.3
.

For
N\geq 1
,
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-distribution
\mu_{\rho,q}
on
X
is defined by

\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{N}\mathbb{Z}_{p})=\frac{\rho^{p^{N}}}{[p^{N}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}.

Definition 2.4
.

[
1
]

The
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-Volkenborn integral of a function
f\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
is defined by

I_{\rho,q}(f)=\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}f(x)d\mu_{\rho,q}(x)=\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\rho^{p^{N}}}{[p^{N}]_{\rho,q}}\sum_{x=0}^{p^{N}-1}f(x)\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{x}.

Remark 2.1
.

In the limit as
\rho\rightarrow 1
in Definition
2.4
becomes the
q
-Volkenborn integration
[
11
]
.

Recall from
[
1
]
that the Caritz’s-type
(\rho,q)
-Bernoulli numbers
\beta_{n:a}(\rho,q)
may be represented by

\beta_{n:a}(\rho,q)=\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\rho^{ax}[x]^{n}_{\rho,q}d\mu_{\rho,q}(x).

Specially,

\beta_{0,a}(\rho,q)=\frac{k\log{\rho}}{\log{\rho q}}.

By the meaning of the extension of
({\rho,q})
-Volkenborn integral, we consider the below weakly (strongly)
p
-adic
({\rho,q})
-invariant distribution
\mu_{\rho,q}
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
.

Definition 2.5
.

If
\mu_{\rho,q}
is satisfied the

\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq\delta_{n,\rho,q},

where
\delta_{n,\rho,q}\rightarrow 0
and

, then

.
If

p
-adic
({\rho,q})
-distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
.

.

A distribution
\mu_{\rho,q}
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
is called

-admissible if

|\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{N}\mathbb{Z}_{p})|\leq\left|\frac{\rho^{p^{N}}}{[p^{N}]_{\rho,q}}\right|c_{n},

where

.

Remark 2.2
.

If
\mu_{\rho,q}
is

-admissible on

, then we see that
\mu_{\rho,q}
is a weakly
p
-adic
({\rho,q})
-invariant distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
.

Let
C(\mathbb{Z}_{p},\mathbb{C}_{p})
be the space of continuous function on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
with values in
\mathbb{C}_{p}
, provided with norm
||f||_{\infty}=\underset{x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}}{\sup}|f(x)|
. The difference quotient
\bigtriangleup_{1}f
of

is the function of two variables given by

\bigtriangleup_{1}f(m,x)=\frac{f(x+m)-f(x)}{m},

for all
x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
and
m\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
with
m\neq 0
.

Definition 2.7
.

A function
f:\mathbb{Z}_{p}\longrightarrow\mathbb{C}_{p}
is said to be a Lipschitz function if there exists a constant
M>0
such that for
all
m\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}\setminus\{0\}
and

,

|\bigtriangleup_{1}f(m,x)|\leq M.

Here

is called the Lipschitz constant of

. The
\mathbb{C}_{p}
-linear space consisting of all Lipschitz function is denoted by Lip

.

Remark 2.3
.

\mathbb{C}_{p}
-linear space is a Banach space with respect

||f||_{1}=||f||_{\infty}\vee||\bigtriangleup_{1}f||_{\infty}
.

3.
Radon-Nikodym’s type theorem with respect to
(\rho,q)
-measure on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}

Let
\mu_{\rho,q}
be a weakly
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-invariant distribution, then
\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{N}\mathbb{Z}_{p})
exists for all
x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, and converges uniformly with respect to
x
. We define the
(\rho,q)
-analogue of Radon-Nikodym derivative of
\mu_{\rho,q}
with respect to
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-invariant distribution as follows:

f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(x)=\lim\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}[p^{N}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{N}\mathbb{Z}_{p}).

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
\mu_{\rho,q}
is strongly
(\rho,q)
-invariant distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Then
f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}\in Lip(\mathbb{Z}_{p},\mathbb{C}_{p})
.

Proof.

Let
x,y\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
with
|x-y|_{p}\leq p^{-m}
. Then
x+p^{m}\mathbb{Z}_{p}=y+p^{m}\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Hence, we note that

\displaystyle|f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(x)-f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(y)|=

\displaystyle|f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(x)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(y)+[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(y+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle+[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{m}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{m}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(y+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle+[p^{m}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(y+p^{m}\mathbb{Z}_{p})|.

Since
\mu_{\rho,q}
is strongly
(\rho,q)
-invariant distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, we have

\displaystyle|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{m}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(x+p^{m}\mathbb{Z}_{p})|\leq Cp^{-m},

for some constant
C
and
m<n
.
For
n\gg 0
, we obtain

\displaystyle|f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(x)-f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(y)|\leq C_{1}p^{-m}=C_{1}|x-y|,

where
C_{1}
is positive real constant. Thus this completes the proof.
∎

For any positive integer
a
and
n
with
a<p^{n}
, and
f\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
, let us define

(3.1)

\displaystyle\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})=\int_{a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p}}f(x)d\mu_{\rho,q}(x),

where the integral is the
(\rho,q)
-Volkenborn integral on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
.

Theorem 3.2
.

For
f,g\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
, we have

(1)

\tilde{\mu}_{\alpha f+\beta g,\rho,q}=\alpha\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}+\beta\tilde{\mu}_{g,\rho,q},

(2)

|\tilde{\mu}_{\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})|\leq\|f\|_{1}\left|\left(\frac{q}{\rho}\right)^{a}\right|\left|\frac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\right|.

Proof.

From (
3.1
), these follow from the observation that

\displaystyle\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle=\lim_{m\to\infty}\dfrac{{\rho}^{p^{m}}}{[p^{m+n}]_{\rho,q}}\sum_{x=0}^{p^{m}-1}f(a+p^{n}x)\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a+p^{n}x}

\displaystyle=\lim_{m\to\infty}\dfrac{{\rho}^{p^{m}}}{[p^{m}]_{\rho,q}}\sum_{x=0}^{p^{m-n}-1}f(a+p^{n}x)\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{p^{n}x}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}\lim_{m\to\infty}\dfrac{{\rho}^{p^{m}-n}}{[p^{m-n}]_{\rho^{p^{m}},q^{p^{m}}}}\sum_{x=0}^{p^{m-n}-1}f(a+p^{n}x)\left(\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{p^{n}}\right)^{x}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}f(a+p^{n}x)d\mu_{\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}}}(x).

∎

Corollary 3.1
.

For
f\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
, we have

\displaystyle\big|\tilde{\mu}_{f,{\rho,q}}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq M\|f\|_{\infty},

where
\|f\|_{\infty}=\sup_{x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}}|f(x)|
and
M
is some positive constant.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
P(x)\in\mathbb{C}_{p}[[x]_{\rho,q}]
be an arbitrary
({\rho,q})
-polynomial with

\sum a_{i}[x]_{\rho,q}^{i}
. Then
\mu_{P,{\rho,q}}
is a strongly
p
-adic
({\rho,q})
-distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
and for all
x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
,

\displaystyle f_{\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}}(x)=\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}P(x)\beta_{0:k}(\rho,q),

where
k
is the degree of
P(x)
.
Furthermore, for
g\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}g(x)d\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(x)=\beta_{0:k}(\rho,q)\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}g(x)P(x)d\mu_{\rho,q}(x),

where the second integral is
(\rho,q)
-Volkenborn integral.

Proof.

Since
P(x)\in\mathbb{C}_{p}[[x]_{\rho,q}]
be an arbitrary

({\rho,q})
-polynomial with
\sum a_{i}[x]_{\rho,q}^{i}
, then

\mu_{P,{\rho,q}}
is strongly
(\rho,q)
-measure on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Without a loss of generality, it is enough to prove the
statement for
P(x)=[x]_{\rho,q}^{k}
.

Let
a
be an integer with
0\leq a<p^{n}
. Then we have

\displaystyle\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})=\dfrac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}\lim_{m\to\infty}\dfrac{\rho^{p^{m-n}}}{[p^{m-n}]_{\rho^{p^{m}},q^{p^{m}}}}\sum_{i=0}^{p^{m-n}-1}[a+ip^{n}]_{\rho,q}^{k}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{p^{n}i}

and

\displaystyle[a+ip^{n}]^{k}_{\rho,q}

\displaystyle=\left(q^{a}[ip^{n}]_{\rho,q}+\rho^{ip^{n}}[a+{\rho,q}]\right)^{k}

\displaystyle=\left(q^{a}[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}[i]_{{\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n}}+\rho^{ip^{n}}[a]_{\rho,q}\right)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}q^{al}[p^{n}]^{l}_{\rho,q}[i]^{l}_{{\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n}}\rho^{ip^{n}(k-l)}[a]^{k-l}_{\rho,q}.

This implies that

\displaystyle\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}\{[a]^{k}_{\rho,q}\beta_{0:k}({\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n})+q^{a}[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}k\beta_{1:k-1}({\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n})+\cdots

\displaystyle+[p^{n}]^{k}_{\rho,q}q^{ak}\beta_{k:0}({\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n})\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\frac{P^{(i)}(a)}{i!}\beta_{i:k-i}(\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}})[p^{n}]^{i}_{\rho,q}q^{ai},

where
P^{(i)}(a)=\left(\frac{d}{d[x]_{\rho,q}}\right)^{i}P(x)\big|_{x=a}
. Thus we acquire

\displaystyle[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}\{[a]^{k}_{\rho,q}\beta_{0:k}({\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n})+[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}q^{a}k[a]^{k-1}_{\rho,q}\beta_{1:k-1}({\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n})+\cdots

\displaystyle+[p^{n}]^{k-1}_{\rho,q}q^{ak}\beta_{k:0}({\rho^{p}}^{n},{q^{p}}^{n})\}.

This can be written in the form

\displaystyle\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}

\displaystyle(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle\equiv\dfrac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}[a]_{\rho,q}^{k}\beta_{0:k}(\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}})+\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}q^{a}[a]^{k-1}_{\rho,q}\beta_{1:k-1}(\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}})\pmod{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}

\displaystyle\equiv\dfrac{1}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}P(a)\beta_{0:k}(\rho,q)\beta_{0:k}(\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}})+q^{a}k[a]^{k-1}_{\rho,q}\beta_{1:k-1}(\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}})\pmod{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}.

Let
x
be an arbitrary in
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
with
x\equiv x_{n}\pmod{p^{n}}
and
x\equiv x_{n+1}\pmod{p^{n+1}}
, where
x_{n}

and
x_{n+1}
are positive integers such that
0\leq x_{n}<p^{n}

and
0\leq x_{n+1}<p^{n+1}
. Then

\displaystyle\big|

\displaystyle[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(x+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{P,\rho,q}(x+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|

\displaystyle=\biggl|\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\frac{P^{(i)}(x_{n})}{i!}\beta_{i:k-i}(\rho^{p^{n}},q^{p^{n}})[p^{n}]^{i}_{\rho,q}q^{ai}-\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\frac{P^{(i)}(x_{n+1})}{i!}\beta_{i:k-i}(\rho^{p^{n+1}},q^{p^{n+1}})[p^{n}]^{i}_{\rho^{p},q^{p}}[p^{n}]^{i}_{\rho,q}q^{ai}\biggl|.

Therefore, we obtain

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(a+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq Cp^{-\nu_{p}(\rho^{p^{n}}-q^{p^{n}})},

where
C
is a positive some constant and
n\gg 0
.

Let

\displaystyle f_{\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}}(a)=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p}).

Then, we have

\displaystyle f_{\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}}(a)=\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{a}P(a)\beta_{0:k}(\rho,q).

Furthermore, since
f_{\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}}(x)
is continuous on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, it follows for all
x\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}

(3.2)

\displaystyle f_{\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}}(x)=\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{x}P(x)\beta_{0:k}(\rho,q).

Suppose that
g\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
. From (
3.2
), we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}g(x)d\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(x)

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=0}^{p^{n}-1}g(i)\tilde{\mu}_{P,\rho,q}(i+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\frac{{\rho^{p}}^{n}}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}

\displaystyle=\beta_{0:k}(\rho,q)\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{i=0}^{p^{n}-1}g(i)\biggl(\frac{q}{\rho}\biggl)^{i}P(i)\frac{(\rho^{p})^{n}}{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}g(x)P(x)d\mu_{\rho,q}(x).

The proof of Theorem
3.3
is complete.
∎

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
\mu_{\rho,q}
be a strongly
({\rho,q})
-measure on

\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, and assume that the
({\rho,q})
- Radon-Nikodym
derivative
f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
is a continuous function
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Suppose that
\mu_{1,\rho,q}
is the strongly

({\rho,q})
-measure associated to
f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}
, then
there exists a
({\rho,q})
-measure
\mu_{2,\rho,q}
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
such that

\displaystyle\mu_{\rho,q}=\mu_{1,\rho,q}+\mu_{2,\rho,q}.

Proof.

Let
f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n,\rho,q}{x\brack n}_{\rho,q}
be
the
(\rho,q)
-Mahler expansion of continuous function
f
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, then we note that
\lim_{n\to\infty}|a_{n,\rho,q}|=0
.

Suppose that

\displaystyle f_{m}(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{m}a_{i,\rho,q}{x\brack i}_{\rho,q}\in\mathbb{C}_{p}[[x]_{\rho,q}].

Then it is easily checked the following inequality

\displaystyle\|f-f_{m}\|_{\infty}\leq\sup_{m\leq n}|a_{n,\rho,q}|.

Writing
f=f_{m}+f-f_{m}
, it follows that

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|

\displaystyle\leq\max\{\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|,

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f-f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f-f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\}.

We conclude by Theorem
3.3
that

(3.3)

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f-f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq\|f-f_{m}\|_{\infty}\leq C_{1}p^{-\nu_{p}(\rho^{p^{n}}-q^{p^{n}})},

where
C_{1}
is some positive constant. For
m\gg 0
, we have
\|f\|_{\infty}=\|f_{m}\|_{\infty}.
Accordingly,

(3.4)

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n+1}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq C_{2}p^{-\nu_{p}(\rho^{p^{n}}-q^{p^{n}})},

where
C_{2}
is also some positive constant. This implies, by virtue of (
3.3
) and (
3.4
),

\displaystyle\big|f(a)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|

\displaystyle\leq\max\{\big|f(a)-f_{m}(a)\big|,\big|f_{m}(a)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|,\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f-f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\}

\displaystyle\leq\max\{\big|f(a)-f_{m}(a)\big|,\big|f_{m}(a)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|,\|f-f_{m}\|_{\infty}\}.

If we fix
\epsilon>0
and fix
m
such that
\|f-f_{m}\|\leq\epsilon
, then for
n\gg 0
, we have

\displaystyle\big|f(a)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq\epsilon.

Hence, we acquire

\displaystyle f_{\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}}(a)=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})=\beta_{0:m}(\rho,q)\biggl(\frac{\rho}{q}\biggl)^{a}f(a),

where
m
is the degree of
f_{m}(x)
. Let
m
be the sufficiently large number such that
\|f-f_{m}\|_{\infty}\leq p^{-n}.
Then we have

\displaystyle[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle=[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})+[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\tilde{\mu}_{f-f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle=[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{f_{m},\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\rho}{q}\biggl)^{a}f_{m}(a)\beta_{0:m}(\rho,q)\pmod{[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}}.

For
g\in UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
, we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}g(x)d\tilde{\mu}_{f,\rho,q}(x)=\int_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\beta_{0:m}(\rho,q)f(x)g(x)d\mu_{\rho,q}(x).

Assume that
f
is the function from
UD(\mathbb{Z}_{p},\mathbb{C}_{p})
to
Lip(\mathbb{Z}_{p},\mathbb{C}_{p})
. By the definition of

\mu_{\rho,q}
, we easily see that
\mu_{\rho,q}
is a strongly
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
and for
n\gg 0

\displaystyle\big|f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(a)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq C_{3}p^{-\nu_{p}(\rho^{p^{n}}-q^{p^{n}})},

where
C_{3}
is some positive constant.
If
\mu_{1,\rho,q}
is associated strongly
(\rho,q)
-measure on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, then we have

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{1,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(a)\big|\leq C_{4}p^{-\nu_{p}(\rho^{p^{n}}-q^{p^{n}})},

where
n\gg 0
and
C_{4}
is some positive constant. Consequently, it follows that

\displaystyle\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{1,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|

\displaystyle\leq\big|[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})-f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(a)\big|+\big|f_{\mu_{\rho,q}}(a)-[p^{n}]_{\rho,q}\mu_{1,\rho,q}(a+p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p})\big|\leq K,

where
K
is some positive constant. Therefore,
\mu_{\rho,q}-\mu_{1,\rho,q}
is a
p
-adic
(\rho,q)
-distribution on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. The proof of Theorem
3.4
is complete.
∎
