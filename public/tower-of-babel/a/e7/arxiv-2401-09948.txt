Abstract
. We consider the existence and uniqueness of a minimizer of the extremal problem for weighted combined energy between two concentric annuli and obtain that the extremal mapping is a certain radial mapping. Meanwhile, this in turn implies a Nitsche type phenomenon and we get a 1 | w | λ − \frac{1}{|w|^{\lambda}}- Nitsche type inequality ( λ ≠ 1 \lambda\neq 1 ). As an application, on the basis of the relationship between weighted combined energy and weighted combined distortion, we also investigate the extremal problem for weighted combined distortion on annuli. This extends the result obtained by Kalaj in [ 10 ] .

Introduction

For two positive and distinct constants
r
and
R
, let

\mathbb{A}_{1}=\{z:1\leq|z|\leq r\}\quad\text{and}\quad\mathbb{A}_{2}=\{w:1\leq|w|\leq R\}

be rounded annuli in the complex plane
\mathbb{C}
.
we consider the class
\mathfrak{H}(\mathbb{A}_{1},\mathbb{A}_{2})
of all orientation preserving homeomorphisms

h
from
\mathbb{A}_{1}
onto
\mathbb{A}_{2}
keeping orders of the boundaries, namely,

|h(z)|=1\quad\text{for}\quad|z|=1\quad\text{and}\quad|h(z)|=R\quad\text{for}\quad|z|=r.

[formula]

For
z=te^{i\theta}
, the normal and tangential derivatives of
h
are

h_{N}=h_{t}\quad\text{and}\quad h_{T}=\frac{1}{t}h_{\theta}.

[formula]

The derivatives
h_{z}
and
h_{\overline{z}}
are expressed as

h_{z}=\frac{e^{-i\theta}}{2}(h_{N}-ih_{T})\quad\text{and}\quad h_{\overline{z}}=\frac{e^{i\theta}}{2}(h_{N}+ih_{T}).

[formula]

We find the Hilbert-Schmidet norm of the differential matrix

|Dh|^{2}=2(|h_{z}|^{2}+|h_{\overline{z}}|^{2})=|h_{N}|^{2}+|h_{T}|^{2},

[formula]

and the Jocobian determinant of
h

J(z,h)=|h_{z}|^{2}-|h_{\overline{z}}|^{2}=\Im(\overline{h_{N}}h_{T}).

[formula]

In
[
6
]
Iwaniec and Oninnen have considered the
n-
harmonic energy between two annuli. When

, in
[
5
]
they gave a new proof. For a similar problem but for non-circular annuli we refer to the paper
[
8
]
and its generalization in
[
11
]
. In 2020, Kalaj
[
10
]
has generalized the harmonic energy to the combined energy. He studied the following extremal problem

\inf_{h\in\mathfrak{H}(\mathbb{A}_{1},\mathbb{A}_{2})}\iint_{\mathbb{A}_{1}}\left(a^{2}|h_{N}|^{2}+b^{2}|h_{T}|^{2}\right)dz

[formula]

and proved that the minimizer is certain radial mapping (see Theorem 3.1 in
[
10
]
). Very recently, Yang, Tang and Feng
[
19
]
furthered (1.6) to the weighted case and discussed the following the extremal problem

\inf_{h\in\mathfrak{H}(\mathbb{A}_{1},\mathbb{A}_{2})}\iint_{\mathbb{A}_{1}}\left(a^{2}|h_{N}|^{2}+b^{2}|h_{T}|^{2}\right)\frac{1}{|h(z)|^{4}}dz.

[formula]

In this note, we mainly investigate the mapping in
\mathfrak{H}(\mathbb{A}_{1},\mathbb{A}_{2})
which minimizes the following extremal problem
for weighted combined energy

\mathbb{E}_{\lambda}[h]=\iint_{\mathbb{A}_{1}}\left(a^{2}|h_{N}|^{2}+b^{2}|h_{T}|^{2}\right)\frac{1}{|h(z)|^{2\lambda}}dz,

[formula]

The harmonic energy has close relationship to Nitsche phenomena. In 1962, Nitsche
[
16
]
conjectured that a necessary and sufficient condtion for
existence of a harmonic homeomorphism between two annuli
\mathbb{A}_{1}
and
\mathbb{A}_{2}
is the following inequality

r\leq R+\sqrt{R^{2}-1}\quad\text{or}\quad R\geq\frac{1}{2}(r+\frac{1}{r}).

[formula]

After various lower bounds for

(
[
14
]
,
[
18
]
,
[
17
]
), the Nitsche conjecture was finally solved by Iwaniec, Kovalev and
Onninen
[
7
]
in 2011.

Recall that, given a Riemannian metric
\rho
on
\mathbb{A}_{2}
, a
C^{2}
homeomorphism
h
is said to be harmonic with
respect to
\rho
(or
\rho-
harmonic) if

h_{z\overline{z}}(z)+\left(\log\rho^{2}\right)_{w}\circ hh_{z}h_{\overline{z}}=0.

On the basis of Nitsche conjecture, Kalaj
[
13
]
proposed the so-called
\rho-
Nitsche conjecture as follows

\rho-
Nitsche conjecture

If there exists a
\rho-
harmonic homeomorphism of the annuli
\mathbb{A}_{1}
onto
\mathbb{A}_{2}
, then

r\leq\exp\left(\int_{1}^{R}\frac{\rho(s)}{\sqrt{s^{2}\rho^{2}(s)-\alpha_{0}}}ds\right),

[formula]

where

\alpha_{0}=\inf_{1\leq s\leq R}(\rho^{2}(s))s^{2}
.

It should be pointed out that the
\rho-
Nitsche conjecture corresponds to the classical Nitsche conjecture when
\rho=1
. In
[
12

a partial result is proved on the
\rho-
Nitsche conjecture. In particular, when
\rho(w)=|w|^{-2}

r\leq R+\sqrt{R^{2}-1}.

[formula]

According to this property, Feng and Tang
[
4

gave a positive answer when
\rho(w)=|w|^{-2}

Recently, Kalaj

R\geq\cosh(\frac{b}{a}\log r)=\frac{1+r^{\frac{2b}{a}}}{2r^{\frac{b}{a}}}.

[formula]

Notice that when

2.
weighted combined energy of radial mapping

For mappings of annuli, it is natural to examine the radial mappings.
Assume

h(z)=H(t)e^{i\theta}\quad\text{for}\quad z=te^{i\theta},

where
H:[1,r]\rightarrow[1,R]
is continuous strictly increasing and satisfies the boundary conditions (1.1). We obtain that

\mathbb{E}_{\lambda}[h]=2\pi\int_{1}^{r}\frac{a^{2}t^{2}\dot{H}^{2}(t)+b^{2}H^{2}(t)}{tH^{2\lambda}(t)}dt=2\pi\int_{1}^{r}F(t,H(t),\dot{H}(t))dt.

[formula]

Then

\frac{\partial F}{\partial H}=-\frac{2\lambda a^{2}t\dot{H}^{2}(t)}{H^{2\lambda+1}(t)}-\frac{(2\lambda-2)b^{2}}{tH^{2\lambda-1}(t)},

\frac{\partial F}{\partial\dot{H}}=\frac{2a^{2}t\dot{H}(t)}{H^{2\lambda}(t)},

and

\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial\dot{H}}\right)=\frac{2a^{2}\dot{H}(t)}{H^{2\lambda}(t)}+\frac{2a^{2}t\ddot{H}(t)}{H^{2\lambda}(t)}-\frac{4\lambda a^{2}t\dot{H}^{2}(t)}{H^{2\lambda+1}(t)}.

According to the Euler-Lagrange equation

\frac{\partial F}{\partial H}=\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial\dot{H}}\right),

[formula]

we get

a^{2}t^{2}H(t)\ddot{H}(t)+a^{2}tH(t)\dot{H}(t)+(\lambda-1)b^{2}H^{2}(t)=\lambda a^{2}t^{2}\dot{H}^{2}(t).

[formula]

Set
t=e^{x}
,
y=H(t)=H(e^{x})
, then
y^{\prime}=\dot{H}(t)e^{x}
. Furthermore,

y^{\prime\prime}=\ddot{H}(t)e^{2x}+\dot{H}(t)e^{x}.

Then

\dot{H}(t)=\frac{y^{\prime}}{e^{x}}\quad\text{and}\quad\ddot{H}(t)=\frac{y^{\prime\prime}-y^{\prime}}{e^{2x}}.

[formula]

We put (2.4) into (2.3) and obtain

a^{2}yy^{\prime\prime}+(\lambda-1)b^{2}y^{2}=\lambda a^{2}y^{\prime 2}.

[formula]

Moreover, set
y^{\prime}=\zeta
, then

y^{\prime\prime}=\frac{d\zeta}{dy}\frac{dy}{dx}=\zeta^{\prime}\zeta.

[formula]

According to (2.5) and (2.6), we have

a^{2}\zeta\zeta^{\prime}+(\lambda-1)b^{2}y=\frac{\lambda}{y}a^{2}\zeta^{2}.

[formula]

Thus

a^{2}\zeta\zeta^{\prime}-b^{2}y=\frac{\lambda}{y}(a^{2}\zeta^{2}-b^{2}y^{2}).

[formula]

Let

\omega=a^{2}\zeta^{2}-b^{2}y^{2},

[formula]

[formula]

Thus, for

[formula]

Furthermore,

[formula]
