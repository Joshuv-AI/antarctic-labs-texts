A-type antiferromagnetic order and magnetic phase diagram of the trigonal Eu spin-7/2 triangular-lattice compound EuSn 2 As 2
Santanu Pakhira, M. A. Tanatar, Thomas Heitmann, David Vaknin, D. C. Johnston

Abstract
The trigonal compound EuSn 2 As 2 was recently discovered to host Dirac surface states within the bulk band gap and orders antiferromagnetically below the Néel temperature T N ≈ 24 T_{\rm N}\approx 24 K. Here the magnetic ground state of single-crystal EuSn 2 As 2 and the evolution of its properties versus temperature T T and applied magnetic field H H are reported. Included are zero-field single-crystal neutron diffraction measurements versus T T , magnetization M ⁡ ( H , T ) M(H,T) , magnetic susceptibility χ ⁡ ( H , T ) = M ⁡ ( H , T ) / H \chi(H,T)=M(H,T)/H , heat capacity C p ​ ( H , T ) C_{\rm p}(H,T) , and electrical resistivity ρ ⁡ ( H , T ) \rho(H,T) measurements. The neutron-diffraction and χ ⁡ ( T ) \chi(T) measurements both indicate a collinear A-type antiferromagnetic (AFM) structure below T N = 23.5 ​ ( 2 ) T_{\rm N}=23.5(2) K, where the Eu 2+ spins S = 7 / 2 S=7/2 in a triangular a ​ b ab -plane layer (hexagonal unit cell) are aligned ferromagnetically in the a ​ b ab plane whereas the spins in adjacent Eu planes along the c c axis are aligned antiferromagnetically. The χ ⁡ ( H a ​ b , T ) \chi(H_{ab},T) and χ ⁡ ( H c , T ) \chi(H_{c},T) data together indicate a smooth crossover between the collinear AFM alignment and an unknown magnetic structure at H ∼ 0.15 H\sim 0.15 T. Dynamic spin fluctuations up to 60 K are evident in the χ ⁡ ( T ) \chi(T) , C p ​ ( T ) C_{\rm p}(T) and ρ ⁡ ( H , T ) \rho(H,T) measurements, a temperature that is more than twice T N T_{\rm N} . The ρ ⁡ ( H , T ) \rho(H,T) of the compound does not reflect a contribution of the topological state, but rather is consistent with a low-carrier-density metal with strong magnetic scattering. The magnetic phase diagrams for both H | c H\parallel c and H | a ​ b H\parallel ab in the H H - T T plane are constructed from the T N ​ ( H ) T_{\rm N}(H) , χ ⁡ ( H , T ) \chi(H,T) , C p ​ ( H , T ) C_{\rm p}(H,T) , and ρ ⁡ ( H , T ) \rho(H,T) data.
