Div-curl problem for non-solenoidal flows with infinite energy and no-slip boundary condition
A. V. Gorshkov

Abstract
In the paper we study div-curl problem in exterior domain with given condition on infinity and no-slip boundary condition. We will derive moment relations on vorticity and divergence for uniqueness solvability of the problem and present L 2 L_{2} -estimates for solutions. Keywords: div-curl problem, no-slip condition, flow past a solid.

First, we study (

1.4

1.1
) - (

B_{r_{0}}
as Fourier series
in polar coordinates

\varphi

\displaystyle\mathbf{v}(r,\varphi)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\mathbf{v}_{k}(r)e^{ik\varphi},

\displaystyle\rho(r,\varphi)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\rho_{k}(r)e^{ik\varphi},

\displaystyle w(r,\varphi)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}w_{k}(r)e^{ik\varphi}.

Here
\mathbf{v}(r,\varphi)=(v_{r},v_{\varphi})
- vector filed in polar coordinates,
\mathbf{v}_{k}(r)=(v_{r,k},v_{\varphi,k})
,
\rho_{k}
,
w_{k}
, - are the Fourier coefficients.

The Fourier coefficients for exterior flow
\mathbf{v}_{\infty}
are given by the formulas:

\displaystyle v^{\infty}_{r,k}=\frac{\delta_{|k|,1}}{2}(v^{\infty}_{1}-ikv^{\infty}_{2}),

\displaystyle v^{\infty}_{\varphi,k}=\frac{\delta_{|k|,1}}{2}(v^{\infty}_{2}+ikv^{\infty}_{1}),

and so we have the relation between
v^{\infty}_{\varphi,k}
and
iv^{\infty}_{r,k}
:

\displaystyle v^{\infty}_{\varphi,k}=\operatorname{sign}(k)iv^{\infty}_{r,k}.

Here
\delta_{|k|,1}={\begin{cases}1,|k|=1,\\
0,|k|\neq 1.\end{cases}}

All the Fourier coefficients of the external flow are zero, with the exception of
k=\pm 1
. For horizontal flow
\mathbf{v}_{\infty}=(v_{\infty},0)

\displaystyle v^{\infty}_{r,k}=\frac{\delta_{|k|,1}}{2}v_{\infty},

\displaystyle v^{\infty}_{\varphi,k}=ik\frac{\delta_{|k|,1}}{2}v_{\infty}.

Equations (
1.1
), (
1.2
) in polar coordinates are written as:

\displaystyle{\frac{1}{r}}{\frac{\partial}{\partial r}}\left(rv_{r,k}\right)+{\frac{ik}{r}}v_{\varphi,k}=\rho_{k},

\displaystyle{\frac{1}{r}}{\frac{\partial}{\partial r}}\left(rv_{\varphi,k}\right)-{\frac{ik}{r}}v_{r,k}=w_{k}.

For non-zero
k
the solution of this system with condition at infinity (
1.4
) is determined by the following formula:

\displaystyle v_{r,k}=\operatorname{sign}(k)\frac{ir^{-|k|-1}}{2}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+1}\left(w_{k}(s)-i\operatorname{sign}(k)\rho_{k}(s)\right)\operatorname{ds}

(2.1)

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~+\operatorname{sign}(k)\frac{ir^{|k|-1}}{2}\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{-|k|+1}\left(w_{k}(s)+i\operatorname{sign}(k)\rho_{k}(s)\right)\operatorname{ds}+v^{\infty}_{r,k}

\displaystyle v_{\varphi,k}=\frac{r^{-|k|-1}}{2}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+1}\left(w_{k}(s)-i\operatorname{sign}(k)\rho_{k}(s)\right)\operatorname{ds}

(2.2)

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~-\frac{r^{|k|-1}}{2}\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{-|k|+1}\left(w_{k}(s)+i\operatorname{sign}(k)\rho_{k}(s)\right)\operatorname{ds}+v^{\infty}_{\varphi,k},~~

where
\operatorname{sign}(k)=\begin{cases}1,~k>0\\
0,~k=0\\
-1,~k<0\end{cases}
,
\delta_{|k|,1}
- Kronecker delta.

For
k=0
using no-slip condition (
1.3

\displaystyle v_{r,0}=\frac{1}{r}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s\rho(s)\operatorname{ds},

\displaystyle v_{\varphi,0}=\frac{1}{r}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}sw(s)\operatorname{ds}.

Since the conditions of zero-circularity (
1.5

\int_{r_{0}}^{\infty}s\rho(s)\operatorname{ds}=0
and
\int_{r_{0}}^{\infty}sw(s)\operatorname{ds}=0

\mathbf{v}-\mathbf{v}_{\infty}

2.1

2.2

k=0
.

These formulas have an integral representation with singular kernels:

\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{B_{r_{0}}}\frac{\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}}{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}|^{2}}\rho(\mathbf{y})\operatorname{d\mathbf{y}}+\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{B_{r_{0}}}\frac{(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})^{\perp}}{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}|^{2}}w(\mathbf{y})\operatorname{d\mathbf{y}}+\mathbf{v}_{\infty},

(2.3)

The kernels in the formula above are the gradient
\nabla_{\mathbf{x}}

\nabla_{\mathbf{x}}^{\perp}=(-\partial_{x_{2}},\partial_{x_{1}})

G(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})=\frac{1}{2\pi}\ln|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}|.

Formulas (
2.1

2.2

\int_{r_{0}}^{\infty}s^{-|k|+1}\left(w_{k}(s)+i\operatorname{sign}(k)\rho_{k}(s)\right)\operatorname{ds}=2ikv^{\infty}_{r,k}=2v^{\infty}_{\varphi,k}.

(2.4)

These relations are the conditions of orthogonality

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{B_{r_{0}}}\frac{w+i\rho}{z^{k}}d\mathbf{x}=\begin{cases}0,~k\geq 0,~k\neq 1,\\
2iv^{\infty}_{r,k},~k=1,\end{cases}

where

.

The singular integrals involved in formula (
2.3
) are the operators of Calderon–Zygmund type
[
6
]
and for

\displaystyle\|\nabla\mathbf{v}(\cdot)\|_{L_{p}}\leq C\left(\|\rho\|_{L_{p}}+\|w\|_{L_{p}}\right).

(2.5)

From the Hardy-Littlewood-Sobolev inequality for

, satisfying
\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{2}

\|\mathbf{v}-\mathbf{v}_{\infty}\|_{L_{q}}\leq C\left(\|\rho\|_{L_{p}}+\|w\|_{L_{p}}\right).

The following lemma says, that on the assumption of zero flux and circularity the
L_{2}
-norm of
\mathbf{v}(\cdot)-\mathbf{v}_{\infty}
is finite:

Lemma 2.1
.

Let conditions (
1.5
), (
1.6
) are satisfied and
\rho,w\in L_{2,N}(B_{r_{0}})
with
N>1
. Then the following estimate holds:

\displaystyle\|\mathbf{v}(\cdot)-\mathbf{v}_{\infty}\|_{L_{2}(B_{r_{0}})}\leq C\left(\|\rho\|_{L_{2,N}(B_{r_{0}})}+\|w\|_{L_{2,N}(B_{r_{0}})}\right).

Proof.

We estimate the first term in (
2.1
), (
2.2
) involving
w
for
k\neq 0
,
|k|\neq N-1
:

\displaystyle\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+1}w_{k}(s)\operatorname{ds}\right|=\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}\frac{s^{|k|+\frac{1}{2}}}{(1+s)^{N}}w_{k}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+\frac{1}{2}-N}w_{k}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle r^{-|k|-1}\sqrt{\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|2k|+1-2N}\operatorname{ds}}\sqrt{\int_{r_{0}}^{r}w^{2}_{k}(s)(1+s^{2})^{N}s\operatorname{ds}}\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C}{\sqrt{|2|k|-2N+2|}}\|w_{k}(\cdot)\|_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}\left(\frac{1}{r^{N}}+\left(\frac{r_{0}}{r}\right)^{k+1}\cdot\frac{1}{r_{0}^{N}}\right).

Then we have
L_{2}
-estimate:

\displaystyle\int_{r_{0}}^{\infty}\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+1}w_{k}(s)\operatorname{ds}\right|^{2}rdr\leq

\displaystyle\int_{r_{0}}^{\infty}\frac{C^{2}}{|2|k|-2N+2|}\|w_{k}(\cdot)\|^{2}_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}\left(\frac{1}{r^{N}}+\left(\frac{r_{0}}{r}\right)^{k+1}\cdot\frac{1}{r_{0}^{N}}\right)^{2}rdr\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C^{2}}{||k|-N+1|}\|w_{k}(\cdot)\|^{2}_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}\left(\frac{r_{0}^{-2N+2}}{2N-2}+\frac{r_{0}^{2-2N}}{2k}\right).

For
|k|=N-1
:

\displaystyle\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+1}w_{k}(s)\operatorname{ds}\right|=\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}\frac{s^{|k|+\frac{1}{2}}}{(1+s)^{N}}w_{k}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle\left|r^{-|k|-1}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{|k|+\frac{1}{2}-N}w_{k}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle r^{-|k|-1}\sqrt{\int_{r_{0}}^{r}s^{-1}\operatorname{ds}}\|w_{k}(\cdot)\|_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}\leq\frac{\sqrt{\ln r-\ln r_{0}}}{r^{|k|+1}}\|w_{k}(\cdot)\|_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}.

The similar estimate is valid for
\rho
. And so, the first term in (
2.1
), (
2.2
) for
k\neq 0
belongs to
L_{2}(r_{0},\infty;r)
.

Second term in (
2.1
), (
2.2
) we estimate in similar way:

\displaystyle\left|r^{|k|-1}\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{-|k|+1}w_{k}(s)\operatorname{ds}\right|=\left|r^{|k|-1}\int_{r}^{\infty}\frac{s^{-|k|+\frac{1}{2}}}{(1+s)^{N}}w_{k}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle\left|r^{|k|-1}\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{-|k|+\frac{1}{2}-N}w_{k}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle r^{|k|-1}\sqrt{\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{-|2k|+1-2N}\operatorname{ds}}\sqrt{\int_{r}^{\infty}w^{2}_{k}(s)(1+s^{2})^{N}s\operatorname{ds}}\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{N}\sqrt{|2|k|+2N-2|}}\|w_{k}(\cdot)\|_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}.

For
k\neq 0
it also belongs to
L_{2}(r_{0},\infty;r)
.

Lets study the case
k=0
. We have

\displaystyle v_{\varphi,0}=\frac{1}{r}\int_{r_{0}}^{r}sw_{0}(s)\operatorname{ds}=-\frac{1}{r}\int_{r}^{\infty}sw_{0}(s)\operatorname{ds},

and

\displaystyle|v_{\varphi,0}|=\left|\frac{1}{r}\int_{r}^{\infty}sw_{0}(s)\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle\left|r^{-1}\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{\frac{1}{2}-N}w_{0}(s)(1+s)^{N}\sqrt{s}\operatorname{ds}\right|\leq

\displaystyle r^{-1}\sqrt{\int_{r}^{\infty}s^{1-2N}\operatorname{ds}}\sqrt{\int_{r}^{\infty}w^{2}_{0}(s)(1+s^{2})^{N}s\operatorname{ds}}\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{N}\sqrt{2N-2}}\|w_{0}(\cdot)\|_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}.

In a similar way

\displaystyle|v_{r,0}|\leq\frac{1}{r^{N}\sqrt{2N-2}}\|\rho_{0}(\cdot)\|_{L_{2,N}(r_{0},\infty;r)}.

So,

,

will belong to

. Then, summing up by

, and using Parseval’s equality for Fourier series, we obtain the inequality required in the lemma. The lemma is proved.

Div-cirl problem in exterior domain

Let
\Phi
be a Riemann mapping from
\Omega
into
B_{r_{0}}
such that

\displaystyle\Phi(p)=p+O\left(\frac{1}{p}\right),

where
p=y_{1}+iy_{2}\in\mathbb{C}
.

Then for
z=x_{1}+ix_{2}\in\mathbb{C}
the inverse transform
\Phi^{-1}(z):B_{r_{0}}\to\Omega
satisfies

\displaystyle\Phi^{-1}(z)=z+O\left(\frac{1}{z}\right).

Also, suppose that

\displaystyle\left(\Phi^{-1}\right)^{\prime}(z)=1+O\left(\frac{1}{z^{2}}\right).

(3.1)

We change the variables
\mathbf{y}=(y_{1},y_{2})\in\Omega
onto
\mathbf{x}=(x_{1},x_{2})\in B_{r_{0}}
in the system (
1.1
), (
1.2
).

Vector field
\mathbf{v}(p)
in
\Omega
with
p=y_{1}+iy_{2}
defines vector field
\hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{x})
in
B_{r_{0}}
:

\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{y})=D\Phi^{t}\hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{x}(\mathbf{y})).

with Jacobian matrix

D\Phi=\begin{pmatrix}&\operatorname{Re}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{1}}&\operatorname{Re}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{2}}\\
&\operatorname{Im}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{1}}&\operatorname{Im}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{2}}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}&\operatorname{Re}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{1}}&-\operatorname{Im}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{1}}\\
&\operatorname{Im}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{1}}&\operatorname{Re}\Phi^{\prime}_{x_{1}}\end{pmatrix}.

Then

\displaystyle\hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{x})={D\Phi^{-1}}^{t}\mathbf{v}(\Phi^{-1}(\mathbf{x}))={D\Phi^{-1}}^{t}\mathbf{v}(\operatorname{Re}\Phi^{-1}(x_{1}+ix_{2}),\operatorname{Im}\Phi^{-1}(x_{1}+ix_{2}))

(3.2)

is the vector field in
B_{r_{0}}
. And
\hat{\rho}(\mathbf{x})=\rho(\Phi(\mathbf{x}))
,
\hat{w}(\mathbf{x})=w(\Phi(\mathbf{x}))
are the divergence and vorticity functions in
B_{r_{0}}
correspondingly.

From the Cauchy–Riemann relations

\displaystyle\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}=\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}},

\displaystyle\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}=-\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}

we will have

\displaystyle\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{y}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{y})=\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}=\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{2}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{2}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{2}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{2}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}=\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\left(\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{2}}\right)

\displaystyle-\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\left(\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}-\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{2}}\right)=\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}v(\mathbf{x})-\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}v(\mathbf{x}),

and

\displaystyle\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{y}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{y})=\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}-\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}=\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial v_{2}}{\partial x_{2}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}-\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}-\frac{\partial v_{1}}{\partial x_{2}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}v(\mathbf{x})+\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}v(\mathbf{x}).

Finally the system (
1.1
), (
1.2
) goes to

\displaystyle\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})-\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=\hat{\rho}(\mathbf{x}),

\displaystyle\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})+\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=\hat{w}(\mathbf{x}).

Then

\displaystyle\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=\frac{\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}}{\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}\right)^{2}}\hat{\rho}(\mathbf{x})+\frac{\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}}{\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}\right)^{2}}\hat{w}(\mathbf{x}),

\displaystyle\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=-\frac{\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}}{\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}\right)^{2}}\hat{\rho}(\mathbf{x})+\frac{\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}}{\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}\right)^{2}}\hat{w}(\mathbf{x}).

Using the representation of the complex derivative
\Phi^{\prime}(p)
:

\Phi^{\prime}(p)=\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}+i\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}=\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}-i\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{2}}.

we will have

\operatorname{curl}\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{x}}+i\operatorname{div}\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{x}}=\frac{\Phi^{\prime}(p)}{|\Phi^{\prime}(p)|^{2}}\hat{w}+i\overline{\frac{\Phi^{\prime}(p)}{|\Phi^{\prime}(p)|^{2}}}\hat{\rho}.

Now we calculate the divergence of the field
\hat{\mathbf{v}}={D\Phi^{-1}}^{t}\mathbf{v}(\Phi^{-1}(\mathbf{x}))
.

Using Jacobian matrix

D\Phi^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}&\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}&-\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\\
&\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}&\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\end{pmatrix}=\frac{1}{|\Phi^{\prime}|^{2}}\begin{pmatrix}&\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}&\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\\
&-\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}&\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\end{pmatrix}

and its transpose

D\Phi^{-1}={D\Phi^{-1}}^{t}=\begin{pmatrix}&\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}&\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\\
&-\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}&\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\end{pmatrix}=\frac{1}{|\Phi^{\prime}|^{2}}\begin{pmatrix}&\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}&-\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\\
&\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}&\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}.\end{pmatrix}

we will have

\displaystyle\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}\hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{x})=\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}+\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{|\Phi^{\prime}|^{2}}\left(\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}+\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)\hat{w}+\frac{1}{|\Phi^{\prime}|^{2}}\left(\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}+\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)\hat{\rho}=

\displaystyle\left(\left(\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\right)^{2}\right)\hat{\rho}=|{\Phi^{-1}}^{\prime}|^{2}\hat{\rho}

and

\displaystyle\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}\hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{x})=\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\operatorname{curl}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}-\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\operatorname{div}_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{v}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{|\Phi^{\prime}|^{2}}\left(\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{1}}{\partial y_{1}}-\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}\right)\hat{w}+\frac{1}{|\Phi^{\prime}|^{2}}\left(-\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{1}}-\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\frac{\partial x_{2}}{\partial y_{2}}\right)\hat{\rho}=

\displaystyle\left(\left(\frac{\partial y_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial y_{2}}{\partial x_{1}}\right)^{2}\right)\hat{w}=|{\Phi^{-1}}^{\prime}|^{2}\hat{w}.

Then the system (
1.1
)-(
1.4
) transfers to new one, defined in
B_{r_{0}}
:

\displaystyle\operatorname{div}~\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=|{\Phi^{-1}}^{\prime}|^{2}\hat{\rho}(\mathbf{x})

(3.3)

\displaystyle\operatorname{curl}~\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=|{\Phi^{-1}}^{\prime}|^{2}\hat{w}(\mathbf{x})

(3.4)

\displaystyle\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})=0,~|\mathbf{x}|=r_{0}

(3.5)

\displaystyle\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x})\to\mathbf{v}_{\infty},~|\mathbf{x}|\to\infty.
