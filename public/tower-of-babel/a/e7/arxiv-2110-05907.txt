Long time asymptotic behavior for the nonlocal nonlinear Schrödinger equation in the solitonic region
Gaozhan Li, Yiling YANG, Engui Fan

Abstract
In this paper, we extend ∂ ¯ \overline{\partial} -steepest descent method to study the Cauchy problem for the nonlocal nonlinear Schrödinger (NNLS) equation with weighted Sobolev initial data i ​ q t + q x ​ x + 2 ​ σ ​ q 2 ​ ( x , t ) ​ q ¯ ​ ( − x , t ) = 0 , \displaystyle{\rm i}q_{t}+q_{xx}+2\sigma q^{2}(x,t)\overline{q}(-x,t)=0, q ​ ( x , 0 ) = q 0 ​ ( x ) , \displaystyle q(x,0)=q_{0}(x), where q 0 ∈ H 1 , 1 ​ ( ℝ ) q_{0}\in H^{1,1}(\mathbb{R}) . Based on the spectral analysis of the Lax pair, the solution of the Cauchy problem is expressed in terms of the solution of a Riemann-Hilbert problem, which is transformed into a solvable model after a series of deformations. We further obtain the asymptotic expansion of the solution to the Cauchy problem for the NNLS equation in the solitonic region. The leading term is soliton solutions, the second term is the interaction between solitons and dispersion, the error term comes from a corresponding ∂ ¯ \bar{\partial} -problem. Comparing to the asymptotic results on the classical NLS equation, the major difference is the second and third terms in asymptotic expansion for the NNLS equation were affected by a function depends on the scattering data and the stationary phase point.

The NNLS equation was first proposed

PT symmetric and non-Hermitian physics has been

In recent years, much work on the mathematical structure and properties for the

The inverse scattering transform (IST) method was first used to solve the NNLS equation by Ablowitz and Musslimani

The long-time asymptotics for the NNLS equation with rapidly decaying initial data was first considered due to Rybalko and Shepelsky

The major difference from classical NLS equation is that the NNLS equation has different symmetries on their Jost functions and
scattering data. Also the NNLS equation admits two scattering coefficients

The nonlinear steepest descent method was first developed by Deift and Zhou to investigate the long-time behavior of mKdV equation

Later this method is further generalized into a powerful

In this paper, we would like to extend above

Compared with work

Moreover, to our knowledge
