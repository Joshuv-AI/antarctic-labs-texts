On natural invariants and equivalence of differential operators
Valentin Lychagin, Valeriy Yumaguzhin

Abstract
. We give a description of the field of rational natural differential invariants for a class of nonlinear differential operators of order k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 on a smooth manifold of dimension n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 and show their application to the equivalence problem of such operators.

In papers

[
12
]
, we used the Levi-Civita connection,
defined by the symbols of second order operators, and the quantization,
associated with this connection, found the field of rational differential
invariants for both linear and a class of nonlinear second order

problem.

The case of differential operators of order

on two-dimensional
manifolds was elaborated in
[
14
]
, where we used the Wagner connection
instead of the Levi-Civita and associated with this connection quantization.

All these results were based on the existence of connections associated with

[

to
differential operators of order

in arbitrary dimension, that have

In this paper, we removed all above restrictions on the symbols and

\mathrm{GL}
-orbits of their symbols

This paper is organized in the following way. At first, we are recalling the

extension of the field of rational functions in one variable. Then we pay
attention to the fact that
\mathrm{GL}
-invariants of symbols are natural

-invariants of
n

Remark, that nowadays there are various algebraic methods for

The crucial result here says that it is enough to have

The application of the Rosenlicht theorem

4.
The principle of n-invariants and equivalence of differential
operators

We say that natural invariants

We would like to avoid of using of these coordinates

and will write

Namely, remind that the Tresse derivatives, we write down them as
