\displaystyle f=\sum_{j=-\infty}^{\infty}P_{j}f\quad{\rm at\ least\ in\ the}\ L^{2}\ {\rm sense}.

Definition 3.1(

Let

\displaystyle\widehat{If}(\xi)=m_{N}(\xi)\hat{f}(\xi),

(3.2)

We first give some results on the
I
-operator and the modified energy
E(Iu(t),Iv(t))

\displaystyle\quad E_{w}(Iu(t),Iv(t))=\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}[\mu|\nabla Iu|^{2}+\lambda|\nabla Iv|^{2}+\lambda\mu|Iu|^{2}|Iv|^{2}]dx

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}[|\nabla Iu|^{2}+|\nabla Iv|^{2}+|Iu|^{4}+|Iv|^{4}]dx

\displaystyle\lesssim\|Iu\|^{2}_{\dot{H}^{1}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}+\|Iv\|^{2}_{\dot{H}^{1}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}+\|Iu\|^{2}_{\dot{H}^{1}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\|u\|^{2}_{\dot{H}^{\frac{1}{2}}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}+\|Iv\|^{2}_{\dot{H}^{1}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\|v\|^{2}_{\dot{H}^{\frac{1}{2}}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})},

\displaystyle E_{w}(Iu(0),Iv(0))\lesssim C(\|u_{0}\|_{H^{s}_{x}},\|v_{0}\|_{H^{s}_{x}})N^{2(1-s)}.

(3.4)

Meanwhile,

\displaystyle\|u(t)\|^{2}_{H^{s}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}+\|v(t)\|^{2}_{H^{s}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\lesssim E_{w}(Iu(t),Iv(t))+M_{w}(Iu(t),Iv(t)).

(3.5)
