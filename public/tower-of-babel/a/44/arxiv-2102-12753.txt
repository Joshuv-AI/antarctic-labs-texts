Abstract




A crank for bipartitions with designated summands

Abstract.

Andrews, Lewis and Lovejoy introduced the
partition function
PD(n)
as the number of partitions of
n
with designated summands. A bipartition of
n
is an ordered pair of partitions
(\pi_{1},\pi_{2})
with the sum of all of the parts being
n
.
In this paper, we introduce a generalized crank named
the
pd
-crank for bipartitions with designated summands and give some
inequalities for the
pd
-crank of bipartitions with designated summands
modulo 2 and 3. We also define the
pd
-crank moments weighted by
the parity of
pd
-cranks
\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)
and show the positivity
of
(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)
. Let
M_{bd}(m,n)
denote the number of bipartitions of
n
with designated summands with
pd
-crank
m
. We prove a monotonicity property of
pd
-cranks of bipartitions with designated summands and find that the sequence
\{M_{bd}(m,n)\}_{|m|\leq n}

is unimodal for
n\not=1,5,7
.

Keywords
: bipartition with designated summands;
pd
-crank;
moment; monotonicity

AMS Classifications
: 11P81, 11P83, 05A17, 05A20

1
Introduction

A partition
\lambda
of a positive integer
n

is a finite nonincreasing sequence of positive integers
\lambda=(\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{l})
such that

|\lambda|=\sum_{i=1}^{l}\lambda_{i}=n
. Let
p(n)
denote the number of
partitions of
n
. There are two vital statistics in the theory
of partitions named Dyson’s rank
[
9
]
and the Andrews–Garvan–Dyson crank
[
3
]
.
The rank
[
9
]
of a partition
\lambda
,
denoted by
r(\lambda)
, is defined as the largest part of
\lambda

minus the number of the parts. The rank can be used to provide combinatorial
interpretations for the following two Ramanujan’s famous congruences

\displaystyle p(5n+4)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{5},

(1.1)

\displaystyle p(7n+5)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{7}.

(1.2)

The crank
[
3
]
of a partition
\lambda
of
n>1

is defined as

c(\lambda)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\lambda_{1},&\text{ if}\ \ n_{1}(\lambda)=0,\\[5.0pt]
\mu(\lambda)-n_{1}(\lambda),&\text{ if }\ n_{1}(\lambda)>0,\end{array}\right.

where
n_{1}(\lambda)
denotes the number of parts equal to
one in
\lambda
and
\mu(\lambda)
denotes the number of
parts in
\lambda
larger than
n_{1}(\lambda)
.
The crank can be used to provide combinatorial
interpretations for congruences (
1.1
), (
1.2
) as well as

\displaystyle p(11n+6)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{11}.

(1.3)

Let
M(m,n)
denote the number of partitions with
crank
m
. For
n\leq 1
and
m\neq\pm 1,0
, we set
M(m,n)=0
. For
n=1

and
m=\pm 1,0
, we define

M(-1,1)=M(1,1)=1,\ M(0,1)=-1.

The generating function of
M(m,n)
is obtained by Andrews and Garvan
[
3
,
11
]
as given by

\displaystyle C(z;q)=\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M(m,n)z^{m}q^{n}=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(zq;q)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q;q)_{\infty}},

(1.4)

where here and throughout this paper,

(a;q)_{\infty}
stands for the
q
-shifted factorial

(a;q)_{\infty}=\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-aq^{n-1}),\,\,|q|<1.

Let
M(m,t,n)
denote the number of
partitions of
n
with crank congruent to
m
modulo
t
.
Setting
z=-1
in (
1.4
), we have

\displaystyle C(-1,q)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M(0,2,n)-M(1,2,n))q^{n}=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)^{2}_{\infty}}.

(1.5)

Andrews and Lewis

[
4
]
proved that

\displaystyle M(0,2,2n)

\displaystyle>M(1,2,2n),

(1.6)

\displaystyle M(1,2,2n+1)

\displaystyle>M(0,2,2n+1),

(1.7)

for
n\geq 0
. The above two inequalities imply that the signs of the coefficients of

\displaystyle\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(-q;q)^{2}_{\infty}}
are alternating.

Later, to study the higher-order spt-function
spt_{k}(n)
, Garvan
[
12
]
introduced the
k
-th symmetrized moments
\mu_{k}(n)
of cranks of partitions of
n
. He defined the
k
-th symmetrized moments
\mu_{k}(n)
of cranks of partitions of
n
as

\displaystyle\mu_{k}(n)=\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}{m+\lfloor\frac{k-1}{2}\rfloor\choose k}M(m,n).

(1.8)

Since the fact that
M(m,n)=M(-m,n)
[
3
, (1.9)]
, it is easy to see that
\mu_{2k+1}(n)=0
.
In a recent work, Ji and Zhao
[
14
]
introduced the
crank moments weighted by the parity of cranks as given by

\displaystyle\mu_{2k}(-1,n)=\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}{m+k-1\choose 2k}(-1)^{m}M(m,n).

(1.9)

Let
k=0
in (
1.9
), we get

\mu_{0}(-1,n)=M(0,2,n)-M(1,2,n).

Ji and Zhao
[
14
]
proved
the following positivity property of
(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k}(-1,n)
.

Theorem 1.1
.

(​​
[
14
, Theorem 1.2]
)

For all
n\geq k\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k}(-1,n)>0.

(1.10)

This property implies the two inequalities (
1.6
) and (
1.7
) of Andrews and Lewis
[
4
]
.

Recently, Ji and Zang
[
15
]
proved the following
monotonicity property of cranks of partitions.

Theorem 1.2
.

(​​
[
15
, Theorem 1.7]
)

For all
n\geq 44
and
1\leq m\leq n-1
, we have

\displaystyle M(m-1,n)\geq M(m,n).

(1.11)

Considering Theorem
1.2
and the symmetry
M(m,n)=M(-m,n)
,
the following corollary is true.

Corollary 1.3
.

(​​
[
15
, Corollary 1.8]
)

For
n\geq 44
, we have

M(1-n,n)\leq\cdots\leq M(-1,n)\leq M(0,n)\geq M(1,n)\geq\cdots\geq M(n-1,n).

This means the sequence
\{M(m,n)\}_{|m|\leq n-1}

is unimodal for
n\geq 44
.

In this paper, we aim to study bipartitions with designated summands.
In
[
5
]
, Andrews, Lewis and Lovejoy
studied the number of
partitions with designated summands which are defined on ordinary partitions by tagging exactly one part of each part size. Let
PD(n)
denote the number of partitions of
n
with designated summands. There are ten partitions of
4
with designated summands:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{ccccc}4^{\prime},&3^{\prime}+1^{\prime},&2^{\prime}+2,&2+2^{\prime},&2^{\prime}+1^{\prime}+1,\\[5.0pt]
2^{\prime}+1+1^{\prime},&1^{\prime}+1+1+1,&1+1^{\prime}+1+1,&1+1+1^{\prime}+1,&1+1+1+1^{\prime}.\end{array}

Hence we have
PD(4)=10
.
Andrews, Lewis and Lovejoy
[
5
]

derived the generating function of
PD(n)
as given by

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}PD(n)q^{n}=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})_{\infty}}.

(1.12)

Andrews, Lewis and Lovejoy
[
5
]
obtained
a Ramanujan-type congruence for the partition function
PD(n)
as

\displaystyle PD(3n+2)\equiv 0\pmod{3}.

(1.13)

By introducing the
pd
-rank for partitions with designated summands, Chen, Ji, Jin and the second author
[
8
]
gave a combinatorial interpretation of the congruence (
1.13
).

A bipartition
\pi
of
n
is an ordered pair of partitions

(\pi_{1},\pi_{2})
with
|\pi_{1}|+|\pi_{2}|=n
.
Let
p_{-2}(n)
denote the number of bipartitions of
n
.
The generating function of
p_{-2}(n)
is

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_{-2}(n)q^{n}=\frac{1}{(q;q)^{2}_{\infty}}.

A bipartition with designated summands means a bipartition

\pi=(\pi_{1},\pi_{2})
for which
\pi_{1}
and
\pi_{2}
are both
partitions with designated summands. Here
\pi_{1}
and
\pi_{2}
are allowed to have one part size tagged in common. Let
PD_{-2}(n)

denote the number of bipartitions of
n
with designated summands.
We get the generating function of
PD_{-2}(n)
as given by

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}PD_{-2}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(q;q)^{2}_{\infty}(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}}.

(1.14)

Recently, arithmetic properties of bipartitions with designated summands
have drawn a number of interest, see, for example
[
16
,
13
]
.

The main objective of this paper is to investigate bipartitions with designated summands from three aspects. First, we introduce a generalized crank named
the
pd
-crank for bipartitions with designated summands and establish some
inequalities for the
pd
-crank of bipartitions with designated summands
modulo 2 and 3. Second, we define the
pd
-crank moments weighted by
the parity of
pd
-cranks
\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)
and show the positivity
property of
(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)
. Finally, we prove a monotonicity property of
pd
-cranks of bipartitions with designated summands.

2
The
pd
-crank and its inequalities

In this section, we first introduce a generalized
crank which called
pd
-crank for bipartitions with designated summands.
The definition of the
pd
-crank relies on the construction of the following bijection
\Delta
which Chen, Ji, Jin and the second
author established in
[
8
]
.

Theorem 2.1
.

(​​
[
8
, Theorem 3.1]
)
There is a
bijection
\Delta
between the set of partitions of
n
with designated summands and the set of vector partitions
(\alpha,\beta)
with
|\alpha|+|\beta|=n
,
where
\alpha
is an ordinary partition and
\beta
is a partition into parts
\not\equiv\pm 1\pmod{6}
.

Chen, Ji, Jin and the second
author gave a combinatorial proof of the above theorem
which illustrates the construction of the bijection
\Delta
, see
[
8
, Theorem 3.1]
.
Under the map
\Delta
, Chen, Ji, Jin and the second author defined
the
pd
-rank of a partition
\lambda
with designated summands
in terms of the pair of partitions
(\alpha,\beta)
.

Definition 2.2
.

(​​
[
8
, Definition 3.2]
)

Let
\lambda
be a partition with designated summands and let
(\alpha,\beta)=\Delta(\lambda)
. Then the
pd
-rank of
\lambda
, denoted
r_{d}(\lambda)
, is defined by

\displaystyle r_{d}(\lambda)=l_{e}(\alpha)-l_{e}(\beta),

(2.1)

where

l_{e}(\alpha)
is the number of even parts of
\alpha
and
and
l_{e}(\beta)
is the number of even parts of
\beta
.

We are now ready to give the definition of the
pd
-crank for
a bipartition
(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})
with designated summands under the map

\Delta
.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})
be a bipartition with
designated summands and let

\left((\alpha_{1},\beta_{1}),(\alpha_{2},\beta_{2}))=(\Delta(\lambda_{1}),\Delta(\lambda_{2})\right).

Then the
pd
-crank of
(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})
, denoted
r_{bd}(\lambda)
, is defined by

\displaystyle r_{bd}(\lambda)=l(\alpha_{1})-l(\alpha_{2}),

(2.2)

where

l(\alpha)
is the number of parts of
\alpha
.

Let
M_{bd}(m,n)
denote the number of bipartitions of
n
with designated summands with
pd
-crank
m
,
and
M_{bd}(k,t,n)
denote the number of bipartitions with designated summands of
n
with
pd
-crank congruent to
k
modulo
t
.
According to the Definition
2.3
, it is clear that

\displaystyle M_{bd}(m,n)=M_{bd}(-m,n).

(2.3)

Following the results of Andrews and Lewis
[
4
]
, we
investigate the inequalities of the
pd
-crank of bipartitions with designated summands modulo
2
and
3
. By the definition of the
pd
-crank of
bipartitions with designated summands, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M_{bd}(m,n)z^{m}q^{n}=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(zq;q)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q;q)_{\infty}(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}}.

(2.4)

The following theorem shows that the sequence
M_{bd}(0,2,n)-M_{bd}(1,2,n)

alternates in sign.

Theorem 2.4
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,2,2n)

\displaystyle>M_{bd}(1,2,2n),

(2.5)

\displaystyle M_{bd}(1,2,2n+1)

\displaystyle>M_{bd}(0,2,2n+1).

(2.6)

Proof.
Setting
z=-1
in (
2.4
), we get

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{bd}(0,2,n)-M_{bd}(1,2,n))q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}^{2}(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}(q;q)^{2}_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})^{4}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}}=\frac{f^{2}_{6}f^{2}_{1}}{f^{4}_{2}f^{2}_{3}},

(2.7)

where here and throughout this paper,

f_{k}
is defined by

f_{k}=(q^{k};q^{k})_{\infty}.

By the following
2
-dissection(​​
[
18
, Lemma 2.6]
)

\displaystyle\frac{f_{1}}{f_{3}}

\displaystyle=\frac{f_{2}f_{16}f^{2}_{24}}{f^{2}_{6}f_{8}f_{48}}-q\frac{f_{2}f^{2}_{8}f_{12}f_{48}}{f_{4}f^{2}_{6}f_{16}f_{24}},

we can deduce that

\displaystyle\frac{f_{1}^{2}}{f_{3}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{f_{2}^{2}f_{16}^{2}f^{4}_{24}}{f^{4}_{6}f_{8}^{2}f_{48}^{2}}+q^{2}\frac{f_{2}^{2}f^{4}_{8}f_{12}^{2}f_{48}^{2}}{f_{4}^{2}f^{4}_{6}f_{16}^{2}f_{24}^{2}}-2q\frac{f_{2}^{2}f_{8}f_{12}f_{24}}{f_{4}f^{4}_{6}}.

(2.8)

Applying (
2.8
) into (
2
), we get

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{bd}(0,2,n)-M_{bd}(1,2,n))q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{f_{6}^{2}}{f^{4}_{2}}\left(\frac{f_{2}^{2}f_{16}^{2}f^{4}_{24}}{f^{4}_{6}f_{8}^{2}f_{48}^{2}}+q^{2}\frac{f_{2}^{2}f^{4}_{8}f_{12}^{2}f_{48}^{2}}{f_{4}^{2}f^{4}_{6}f_{16}^{2}f_{24}^{2}}-2q\frac{f_{2}^{2}f_{8}f_{12}f_{24}}{f_{4}f^{4}_{6}}\right),

which implies the coefficient of
q^{n}
in (
2
) is positive(negative) when
n
is even(odd). This completes the proof.

Considering the
pd
-crank of bipartitions with designated summands modulo
3
, we have the following results.

Theorem 2.5
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,3,6n)

\displaystyle>M_{bd}(1,3,6n),

(2.9)

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,3,6n+1)

\displaystyle<M_{bd}(1,3,6n+1),

(2.10)

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,3,6n+2)

\displaystyle>M_{bd}(1,3,6n+2),

(2.11)

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,3,6n+3)

\displaystyle>M_{bd}(1,3,6n+3),

(2.12)

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,3,6n+4)=M_{bd}(1,

\displaystyle 3,6n+4)=M_{bd}(2,3,6n+4),

(2.13)

\displaystyle M_{bd}(0,3,6n+5)

\displaystyle<M_{bd}(1,3,6n+5).

(2.14)

Proof.
Substituting

\displaystyle\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M_{bd}(m,n)\zeta^{m}q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(\zeta q;q)_{\infty}(\zeta^{-1}q;q)_{\infty}(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{3}_{\infty}}.

Using the

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{bd}(0,3,n)-M_{bd}(1,3,n))q^{n}

3
The
pd
-crank moments weighted by the parity of
pd
-cranks

As a natural analog to the
2k
-th crank
moments
\mu_{2k}(-1,n)
weighted by the parity of cranks due to Ji and Zhao
[
14
]
, we define the
2k
-th
pd
-crank moments of bipartitions with designated summands weighted by the
parity of
pd
-cranks as given by

\displaystyle\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)=\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}{m+k-1\choose 2k}(-1)^{m}M_{bd}(m,n).

(3.1)

In this section, we aim to prove the following positivity property of
(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)
.

Theorem 3.1
.

For
n\geq k\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)>0.

(3.2)

It is worth mentioning that Theorem
3.1
reduces to
Theorem
2.4
when
k=0
in (
3.2
).

With the aid of the proof of Theorem 1.3 of Ji and Zhao
[
14
]
and applying Andrews’
j
-fold generalization of
q
-Whipple’s theorem

[
2
]
, we obtain the generating function of
\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)

as

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)q^{n}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}(-q;q)^{2}_{\infty}}\sum_{n_{k}\geq n_{k-1}\cdots\geq n_{1}\geq 1}\frac{(-1)^{k}q^{n_{1}+n_{2}+\cdots+{n_{k}}}}{(1+q^{n_{1}})^{2}(1+q^{n_{2}})^{2}\cdots(1+q^{n_{k}})^{2}}.

(3.3)

Considering the proof of Theorem 1.4 of Ji and Zhao
[
14
]
,
we find that the generating function (
3
) is equivalent to

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)q^{n}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})^{2}_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})^{2}_{\infty}(-q;q)^{2}_{\infty}}\sum_{m_{k}>m_{k-1}>\cdots>m_{1}\geq 1}\frac{(-1)^{m_{k}}m_{1}(m_{2}-m_{1})\cdots(m_{k}-m_{k-1})q^{m_{k}}}{(1-q^{m_{1}})(1-q^{m_{2}})\cdots(1-q^{m_{k}})}.

(3.4)

Using the facts that

\displaystyle(-q^{3};-q^{3})_{\infty}

\displaystyle=(-q^{3};q^{6})_{\infty}(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty},

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}(-q;q^{6})_{\infty}(-q^{5};q^{6})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})^{2}_{\infty}(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}(-q^{3};q^{6})_{\infty}}\sum_{m_{k}>m_{k-1}>\cdots>m_{1}\geq 1}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}\cdot m_{1}(m_{2}-m_{1})\cdots(m_{k}-m_{k-1})q^{m_{k}}}{(1-(-q)^{m_{1}})(1-(-q)^{m_{2}})\cdots(1-(-q)^{m_{k}})}

we get

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\mu_{2k,bd}(-1,n)q^{n}

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt\times\sum_{m_{k}>m_{k-1}>\cdots>m_{1}\geq 1}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}\cdot m_{1}(m_{2}-m_{1})\cdots(m_{k}-m_{k-1})q^{m_{k}}}{(1-(-q)^{m_{1}})(1-(-q)^{m_{2}})\cdots(1-(-q)^{m_{k}})}.

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt\times\sum_{m_{k}>m_{k-1}>\cdots>m_{1}\geq 1}\frac{(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}\cdot m_{1}(m_{2}-m_{1})\cdots(m_{k}-m_{k-1})q^{m_{k}}}{(1-(-q)^{m_{1}})(1-(-q)^{m_{2}})\cdots(1-(-q)^{m_{k}})}

Given

4
A monotonicity property of

In this section, we investigate the following monotonicity property of

which leads to the unimodality of the sequence

with

Theorem 4.1
.

For
m\geq 1
and
n\geq 0
,

M_{bd}(m-1,n)\geq M_{bd}(m,n),

(4.1)

except for
(m,n)=(1,1),(1,5)
or
(1,7)
.

Fu and Tang
[
10
]
studied a generalized crank named

-crank for

-colored partitions. Let

denote the number of
k
-colored partitions of
n
with

-crank

. The generating function of

is given by

\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M_{k}(m,n)z^{m}q^{n}=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}^{2-k}}{(zq;q)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q;q)_{\infty}}

(4.2)

for

.

Now we provide a proof for Theorem

.

.

Setting

in (

) and applying it to (
2.4
),

\displaystyle\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M_{bd}(m,n)z^{m}q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}^{2}}{(zq;q)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q;q)_{\infty}(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}^{2}(q^{3};q^{3})_{\infty}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}^{2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}^{2}(q^{3};q^{3})_{\infty}^{2}}\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}M_{2}(m,n)z^{m}q^{n}.

It is clear that

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{bd}(m-1,n)-M_{bd}(m,n))q^{n}

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}^{2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}^{2}(q^{3};q^{3})_{\infty}^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{2}(m-1,n)-M_{2}(m,n))q^{n}.

According to
[

,

.

.

For

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{bd}(0,n)-M_{bd}(1,n))q^{n}

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt=\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}^{2}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}^{2}(q^{3};q^{3})_{\infty}^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(M_{2}(0,n)-M_{2}(1,n))q^{n}.

Let

Noting that

\displaystyle\frac{(q^{6};q^{6})_{\infty}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{3})_{\infty}}
