Abstract
. This paper shows that if R R is a homomorphic image of a Cohen-Macaulay local ring, then R R -module M M is sequentially generalized Cohen-Macaulay if and only if the difference between Hilbert coefficients and arithmetic degrees for all distinguished parameter ideals of M M are bounded.
