Asymptotic behavior of angular integrals in the massless limit
Fabian Wunder

Abstract
We investigate the small-mass asymptotics of a class of massive d d dimensional angular integrals. These integrals arise in a wide range of perturbative quantum field theory calculations. We derive expressions characterizing their behavior in the vicinity of the massless limit for all cases with up to two denominators. The results established in this work are applicable to phase-space calculations where an integration over virtuality including the massless limit is required.

II
Asymptotic behavior in the massless limit

The main tool for the extraction of the asymptotic behavior will be the
two-point splitting lemma

(
V. E. Lyubovitskij, F. Wunder and A. S.
Zhevlakov 2021
)
.
Using the notation

\displaystyle\Delta_{k}(v_{i},v_{j})\equiv\frac{1}{v_{i}\cdot k\,\,v_{j}\cdot k},

(5)

it states that for any two vectors
v_{1}
and
v_{2}
, we can choose any scalar
\lambda
and construct the linear combination

{v_{3}=(1-\lambda)v_{1}+\lambda v_{2}}

to obtain the identity

\displaystyle\Delta_{k}(v_{1},v_{2})=\lambda\,\Delta_{k}(v_{1},v_{3})+(1-\lambda)\,\Delta_{k}(v_{2},v_{3})\,.

(6)

This allows us to express a given angular integral in terms of other angular integrals where a new auxiliary vector
v_{3}
has been inserted.
By choosing appropriate values for
\lambda
, the vector
v_{3}
can be given desirable properties, most importantly being massless.
This idea has been fruitfully employed in reference
(
V. E. Lyubovitskij, F. Wunder and A. S.
Zhevlakov 2021
)
for the calculation of the all-order
\varepsilon
-expansion of the double-massive integral.

II.1
Asymptotic form of the massive one-denominator integral

We start with the investigation of the massive one-denominator master integral

Finally, we consider the double-massive two-denominator master integral,

By means of recursion relations derived from IBP identities these results extend to all two-denominator angular integrals with integer coefficients.
In the construction of the asymptotic expansion the two-point splitting lemma proved to be an immensely useful tool.
It allowed for the extraction of the massless limits in terms of suitable massless angular integrals.

Appendix A
Order
\varepsilon^{2}
coefficients of the double-massive integral

The explicit form of the order
\varepsilon^{2}
coefficient functions
f_{0}
and
f_{1}
of the massless respectively single-massive two-denominator integrals appearing in Eq. (
II.3
) are

\displaystyle f_{0}(v)=\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(1-\frac{2}{v}\right)-\mathrm{Li}_{2}\!\left(1-\frac{v}{2}\right)\log\frac{v}{2}-\frac{1}{6}\log^{3}\frac{v}{2}

(23)

and

\displaystyle f_{1}(\omega^{+},\omega^{-})=2\,\mathrm{S}_{1,1,1}(\omega^{+},\omega^{-})-2\,\mathrm{Li}_{3}(\omega^{+})

\displaystyle\qquad+2\,\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(\frac{\omega^{+}}{\omega^{+}-1}\right)-2\,\mathrm{Li}_{2}(\omega^{+})\log(1-\omega^{+})

\displaystyle\qquad-\frac{1}{3}\log^{3}(1-\omega^{+})+(\omega^{+}\leftrightarrow\omega^{-})\,,

(24)

where
\mathrm{S}_{1,1,1}
denotes the double Nielsen polylogarithm
(
V. E. Lyubovitskij, F. Wunder and A. S.
Zhevlakov 2021
)

\displaystyle\mathrm{S}_{1,1,1}(x,y)=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{t}\log(1-x\,t)\log(1-y\,t)\,.

(25)

This generalized polylogarithm of weight
3
and depth
2
can be expressed in terms of classical polylogarithms
Frellesvig et al. 2016
;
Duhr and Dulat 2019
.
For
0<y<x<1
it holds

\displaystyle\mathrm{S}_{1,1,1}(x,y)=-\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(1-x\right)-\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(1-y\right)+\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)

\displaystyle\,\,+\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(\frac{1-x}{1-y}\right)-\mathrm{Li}_{3}\!\left(\frac{y(1-x)}{x(1-y)}\right)-\mathrm{Li}_{2}(y)\log(1-x)

\displaystyle\,\,+\mathrm{Li}_{2}(1-x)\log(1-y)+\mathrm{Li}_{2}\!\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\log\!\left(\frac{1-x}{1-y}\right)

(26)

\displaystyle\,\,-\frac{1}{2}\log^{2}(1-y)\log\!\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)+\frac{\pi^{2}}{6}\log(1-y)+\zeta_{3}\,.

The specific value needed for the double massless limit is
\mathrm{S}_{1,1,1}(1,0)=0
, which can be trivially read off from the integral representation (
25
).
