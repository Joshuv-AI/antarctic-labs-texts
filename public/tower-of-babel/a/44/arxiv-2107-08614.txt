Perfectness of Kirillov-Reshetikhin Crystals B r , s B^{r,s} for types E 6 ( 1 ) E_{6}^{(1)} and E 7 ( 1 ) E_{7}^{(1)} with a minuscule node r r
Toya Hiroshima

Abstract
. We prove the perfectness of Kirillov-Reshetikhin crystals B r , s B^{r,s} for types E 6 ( 1 ) E_{6}^{(1)} and E 7 ( 1 ) E_{7}^{(1)} with r r being the minuscule node and s ≥ 1 s\geq 1 using the polytope model of KR crystals introduced by Jang [ 11 ] .

1.
Introduction

Kirillov-Reshetikhin (KR) modules are an important class of finite-dimensional representations for affine quantum group
U_{q}^{\prime}(\mathfrak{g})
without the degree operator
[
5
]
.
We denote a KR module by
W^{r,s}
, where
r
is a node of the Dynkin diagram except
0
and
s\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}
.

There is a conjecture that a KR module
W^{r,s}
admits a crystal base
[
8
,
9
]
, which is called a Kirillov-Reshetikhin (KR) crystal and denoted by
B^{r,s}
.
The conjecture has been solved affirmatively for all nonexceptional types
[
21
]
, for types
G_{2}^{(1)}
and
D_{4}^{(3)}

[
19
]
, and for all affine types with
r
being adjacent to
0
or in the orbit of
0
under a Dynkin diagram automorphism
[
16
]
.
Recently, it has been shown that KR module
W^{r,s}
has a crystal base whenever its classical decomposition is multiplicity free in all affine types
[
3
]
and has a crystal pseudobase (crystal base modulo signs) in types
E_{6,7,8}^{(1)}
,
F_{4}^{(1)}
, and
E_{6}^{(2)}
when
r
is near adjoint (the distance from
0
is precisely
2
)
[
20
]
.

On the other hand, the notion of perfect crystal was introduced in
[
15
]
.
Hatayama et al. conjectured that KR crystals
B^{r,s}
under some condition on
s
are perfect crystals
[
9
]
.
This conjecture was proved for all nonexceptional types
[
6
]
, for type
G_{2}^{(1)}
with
r=1

[
24
]
and
D_{4}^{(3)}
with
r=1

[
17
]
.
However, it is still an open problem for another affine types.
In this paper, we prove that KR crystals
B^{r,s}
of types
E_{6}^{(1)}
and
E_{7}^{(1)}
with
r
being minuscule nodes (
r=1,6
for
E_{6}^{(1)}
and
r=7
for
E_{7}^{(1)}
), whose combinatorial structures were constructed in
[
2
,
13
]
, are perfect crystals of level
s
.
The polytope realization of KR crystals, which was introduced in
[
11
,
12
]
, plays a crucial role.

This paper is organized as follows.
In Section 2 we give the necessary background on crystals.
In Section 3 we review PBW crystals and work by Salisbury-Schultze-Tingley
[
23
]
.
In Section 4 we summarize the facts on crystals of the quantum nilpotent subalgebras of type
E_{6,7}^{(1)}
, which are presented in
[
11
]
.
Section 5 is devoted to the proof of perfectness.

Let
\mathfrak{g}
be an affine Lie algebra of type

\Lambda_{i}

(i\in I)
.
Let

be the simple roots and simple coroots respectively.
Let
\delta=\sum_{i\in I}a_{i}\alpha_{i}
be the null roots and

the canonical central element associated to
\mathfrak{g}
, where

and

Let

be the weight lattice,

the dual weight lattice, and

The canonical pairing

is defined by
\langle\alpha_{i}^{\vee},\alpha_{j}\rangle=A_{i,j}
, where
A=(A_{i,j})_{i,j\in I}
is the generalized Cartan matrix.
We write

.
Let

be the derived subalgebra of

.
Let
\mathfrak{g}_{0}
is the underlying finite-dimensional simple Lie algebra with index set
I_{0}=I\backslash\{0\}
and fundamental weights
\bar{\Lambda}_{i}

(i\in I_{0})
.
The weight lattice is given by

\bar{P}=\bigoplus_{i\in I_{0}}\mathbb{Z}\bar{\Lambda}_{i}
and the dual weight lattice is denoted by
\bar{P}^{\vee}
.
Let
(\quad|\quad):\bar{P}\times\bar{P}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}
denote the symmetric bilinear form defined by
(\alpha_{i}|\alpha_{j})=a_{i}^{\vee}a_{i}^{-1}A_{i,j}
.
Set
\theta=\sum_{i\in I_{0}}a_{i}\alpha_{i}
the maximal root of
\mathfrak{g}_{0}
.
Note that
\alpha_{0}=-\theta\in P_{cl}
(
a_{0}=1
).

Let

be the quantized enveloping algebra of

over

.
A

-crystal (

-weighted

-crystal) is a set

\mathrm{wt}:B\rightarrow P
, Kashiwara operators

, and maps
\varepsilon_{i},\varphi_{i}:B\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}\cup\{-\infty\}
for

subject to the conditions:

(1)

\varphi_{i}(b)=\varepsilon_{i}(b)+\langle\alpha_{i}^{\vee},\mathrm{wt}(b)\rangle
for
i\in I
and
b\in B
.

(2)

if
e_{i}b\in B
, then

(a)

\varepsilon_{i}(e_{i}b)=\varepsilon_{i}(b)-1
,

(b)

\varphi_{i}(e_{i}b)=\varphi_{i}(b)+1
, and

(c)

\mathrm{wt}(e_{i}b)=\mathrm{wt}(b)+\alpha_{i}
.

(3)

if

, then

(a)

\varepsilon_{i}(f_{i}b)=\varepsilon_{i}(b)+1
,

(b)

\varphi_{i}(f_{i}b)=\varphi_{i}(b)-1
, and

(c)

\mathrm{wt}(f_{i}b)=\mathrm{wt}(b)-\alpha_{i}
.

(4)

f_{i}b=b^{\prime}
if and only if

for

and
i\in I
.

(5)

if
\varphi_{i}(b)=-\infty
for
b\in B
, then

.

We say that the
U_{q}(\mathfrak{g})
-crystal

is

when
\varepsilon_{i}(b)=\max\left\{k\mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}e_{i}^{k}b\neq 0\right\}
and
\varphi_{i}(b)=\max\left\{k\mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}f_{i}^{k}b\neq 0\right\}
for
b\in B
.
Let
B
be a regular

-crystal.
We set
\varepsilon(b)=\sum_{i\in I}\varepsilon_{i}(b)\Lambda_{i}
and
\varphi(b)=\sum_{i\in I}\varphi_{i}(b)\Lambda_{i}
for
b\in B
.
The weight function is given by
\mathrm{wt}(b)=\varphi(b)-\varepsilon(b)
for
b\in B
.
We define the tensor products of
U_{q}(\mathfrak{g})
-crystals
B_{1}
and
B_{2}
as the crystal
B_{2}\otimes B_{1}
that is the Cartesian product
B_{2}\times B_{1}
with the crystal structure
[
4
]

\displaystyle e_{i}(b_{2}\otimes b_{1})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}e_{i}b_{2}\otimes b_{1}&\text{if }\varepsilon(b_{2})>\varphi(b_{1}),\\
b_{2}\otimes e_{i}b_{1}&\text{if }\varepsilon(b_{2})\leq\varphi(b_{1}),\end{cases}

\displaystyle f_{i}(b_{2}\otimes b_{1})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}f_{i}b_{2}\otimes b_{1}&\text{if }\varepsilon(b_{2})\geq\varphi(b_{1}),\\
b_{2}\otimes f_{i}b_{1}&\text{if }\varepsilon(b_{2})<\varphi(b_{1}),\end{cases}

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{i}(b_{2}\otimes b_{1})

\displaystyle=\max(\varepsilon_{i}(b_{2}),\varepsilon_{i}(b_{1})-\langle\alpha_{i}^{\vee},\mathrm{wt}(b_{2})\rangle),

\displaystyle\varphi_{i}(b_{2}\otimes b_{1})

\displaystyle=\max(\varphi_{i}(b_{2}),\varphi_{i}(b_{1})+\langle\alpha_{i}^{\vee},\mathrm{wt}(b_{2})\rangle),

\displaystyle\mathrm{wt}(b_{2}\otimes b_{1})

\displaystyle=\mathrm{wt}(b_{2})+\mathrm{wt}(b_{1}).

A
U_{q}^{\prime}(\mathfrak{g})
-crystal (

-weighted

-crystal) and

The quantized enveloping algebra

Let

For

Let

Let

Denote by

R(w_{0})=\left\{\mathbf{i}=(i_{1},\ldots,i_{N})\mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}w_{0}=s_{i_{1}}\cdots s_{i_{N}}\right\}

Given

]

Then the set

-basis of

, which is called the

.
The

-lattice of

is independent of the choice of
\mathbf{i}
, which is denoted by

.
If
\pi:L(\infty)\rightarrow L(\infty)/qL(\infty)
is the canonical projection, then the induced crystal
\pi(\mathbf{B}_{\mathbf{i}})
is a
\mathbb{Q}
-basis of
L(\infty)/qL(\infty)
and also independent of the choice of
\mathbf{i}
, which is denoted by

.
We identify

, which we call the

.

Let

be given with length

.

such that

.

-subspace of

We call this subalgebra the

associated to

.
