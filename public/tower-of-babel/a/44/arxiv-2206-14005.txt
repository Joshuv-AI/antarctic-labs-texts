Zero energy states of Dirac equation in ( 2 + 1 ) (2+1) -dimensional curved spacetime
Choon-Lin Ho, Pinaki Roy

Abstract
We consider Dirac equation in ( 2 + 1 ) (2+1) dimensional curved spacetime in the presence of a scalar potential. It is then shown that the zero energy states are degenerate and they can be obtained when the momentum k y k_{y} in the y y direction satisfies certain constraints involving the mass parameter and the scalar potential coupling.
