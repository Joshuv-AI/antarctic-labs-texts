Definition 2.1

A

Note that local trivializations
\varphi_{\alpha},\varphi_{\beta}

As direct consequences, the transition maps satisfy:

\displaystyle\varphi_{\alpha\alpha}(x)

\displaystyle=\text{id}_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\quad\text{for}\quad x\in U_{\alpha};

\displaystyle=\text{id}_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\quad\text{for}\quad x\in U_{\alpha}\cap U_{\beta};

\displaystyle\varphi_{\alpha\gamma}(x)\varphi_{\gamma\beta}(x)\varphi_{\beta\alpha}(x)

\displaystyle=\text{id}_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\quad\text{for}\quad x\in U_{\alpha}\cap U_{\beta}\cap U_{\gamma}.

(x,v)\sim(y,w)\ :\Longleftrightarrow\ x=y\ \text{and}\ w=\varphi_{\beta\alpha}(x)v\ (x\in U_{\alpha},y\in U_{\beta},v,w\in\mathbb{R}^{n})\,.
