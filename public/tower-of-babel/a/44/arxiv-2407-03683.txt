Ruslan K. Muharlyamov, Tatiana N. Pankratyeva, Shehabaldeen O.A. Bashir

Abstract

The Horndeski theory allows anisotropy to grow over time. The question arises about isotropization. In the theory under consideration, a zero scalar charge imposes a condition on the anisotropy level, namely its dynamics develops in a limited region.
