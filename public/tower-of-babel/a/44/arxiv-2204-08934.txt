φ − \varphi- fixed point theorems in C ∗ − C^{\ast}- algebra valued partial metric spaces
Hafida Massit 1∗ and Mohamed Rossafi 2

Abstract
. In this present article, we etablish some existence results of φ − \varphi- fixed point of a mapping in a C ∗ C^{\ast} -algebra valued metric spaces and we deduce some fixed point theorems in C ∗ C^{\ast} -algebra valued partial metric spaces. Non-trivial examples are further provided to support the hypotheses of our results.

Definition 2.2

[
5

Let

\ p:X\times X\rightarrow\mathbb{A}

-algebra valued metric on

(i)

\theta\preceq p(x,y)

-algebra valued partial metric space.

If we take
