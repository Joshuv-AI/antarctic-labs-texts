Complete weight enumerators for several classes of two-weight and three-weight linear codes
Canze Zhu, Qunying Liao

Abstract




Abstract.

In this paper, for an odd prime
p
, by extending Li et al.’s construction
[
17
]
, several classes of two-weight and three-weight linear codes over the finite field
\mathbb{F}_{p}
are constructed from a defining set, and then their complete weight enumerators are determined by using Weil sums. Furthermore, we show that some examples of these codes are optimal or almost optimal with respect to the Griesmer bound. Our results generalize the corresponding results in
[
17
,
15
]
.

Keywords.

Linear codes; Complete weight enumerators; Character sums; Weil sums

1
Introduction

Let
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}
be the finite field with
p^{m}
elements and
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}^{*}=\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}\backslash\{0\}
, where
p
is an odd prime and
m
is a positive integer. An
[n,k,d]
linear code over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
is a
k
-dimensional subspace of
\mathbb{F}_{p}^{n}
with minimum distance
d
. In addition, the weight enumerator and complete weight enumerator are the important parameters for a linear code
[
21
]
, especially, few-weight linear codes have better applications
[
1
,
2
,
22
,
6
,
3
]
. Motivated by Ding et al’s work
[
8
]
, a number of two-weight or three-weight linear codes have been constructed from defining sets
[
7
,
23
,
11
,
12
,
13
,
14
,
15
,
17
,
19
,
20
,
24
,
16
]
.

In 2015, Ding et al. gave a method to construct a class of two-weight or three-weight linear codes via the trace function from defining sets
[
8
]
. Let

D=\{d_{1},d_{2},\cdots,d_{n}\}\subseteq\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}^{*}
and
\mathrm{Tr}_{m}
denote the trace function from
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}
onto
\mathbb{F}_{p}
, a
p
-ary linear code is defined by

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{D}=\big\{\mathbf{c}(x)=\big(\mathrm{Tr}_{m}(xd_{1}),\mathrm{Tr}_{m}(xd_{2}),\cdots,\mathrm{Tr}_{m}(xd_{n})\big)~\big|~x\in\mathbb{F}_{p}^{m}\big\}.

Motivated by the above construction, Li et al. defined a linear code

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{\bar{D}}=\big\{\mathbb{c}(a,b)=\big(\mathrm{Tr}_{m}(ax+by)\big)_{(x,y)\in D}~\big|~(a,b)\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}\times\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}\big\}

(1.1)

with
\bar{D}\subseteq\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}^{2}

[
17
]
. Later, Jian et al. obtained several classes of two-weight and three-weight linear codes
\mathcal{C}_{\bar{D}}
from
(\ref{C})
by choosing the defining set

\displaystyle\bar{D}=\big\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m}}^{2}~\big|~\mathrm{Tr}_{m}(x^{2}+y^{p^{u}+1})=0\big\},

(1.2)

where
u
is a positive integer
[
15
]
.

In this paper, we define a linear code

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{D_{\lambda}}=\big\{\big(\mathrm{Tr}_{m_{1}}(ax)+\mathrm{Tr}_{m_{2}}(by)\big)_{(x,y)\in D_{\lambda}}~\big|~(a,b)\in\mathrm{F}_{p^{m_{1}}}\times\mathrm{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}\big\}

(1.3)

with

\displaystyle D_{\lambda}=

\displaystyle\big\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{1}}}\times\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}\backslash\{(0,0)\}~\big|~\mathrm{Tr}_{m_{1}}(x^{2})+\mathrm{Tr}_{m_{2}}(y^{p^{u}+1})=\lambda\big\},

(1.4)

where
\lambda\in\mathbb{F}_{p}
,
m_{1}
and
m_{2}
are positive integers.
We determine the parameters and the complete weight enumerators of
\mathcal{C}_{D_{\lambda}}
basing on Weil sums. In
addition, for some examples,
\mathcal{C}_{D_{\lambda}}
is optimal or almost optimal
with respect to the Griesmer bound
[
9
]
. Obviously,
if
m_{1}=m_{2}
and
\lambda=0
, then
D_{\lambda}=\bar{D}
and
\mathcal{C}_{D_{\lambda}}=\mathcal{C}_{\bar{D}}
. Thus, we extend Li et al.’s construction
[
17
]
, and generalize the corresponding results in
[
17
,
15
]
.

This paper is organized as follows. In section 2, some related basic notations and
results of Weil sums are given. In section 3, the complete weight enumerators of several
classes of two-weight and three-weight linear codes are presented. In section 4, the proofs of the main results are given. In
section 5, some examples are obtained by using Magma, which are accordant with the main results. In
section 6, we conclude the whole paper.

2.2
Weil sums

It is well known that Weil sums are defined by
\sum\limits_{x\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}}\chi(f(x))
with
f(x)\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}[x]
, and there are many results for the Weil sum
[
4
,
5
]

\displaystyle S_{m_{2},u}(a,b)=\sum_{x\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}}\chi(ax^{p^{u}+1}+bx)~(a\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}^{*},~b\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}).

Lemma 2.2

If
\frac{m_{2}}{v}
is odd, then

\displaystyle S_{m_{2},u}(a,0)=G_{m_{2}}\eta_{m_{2}}(a).

If
\frac{m_{2}}{v}
is even, then

\displaystyle S_{m_{2},u}(a,0)=\begin{cases}p^{s},\quad&\text{if}~\frac{s}{v}~\text{is even and}~a^{\frac{p^{m_{2}}-1}{p^{v}+1}}\neq(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}};\\
-p^{s+v},\quad&\text{if}~\frac{s}{v}~\text{is even and}~a^{\frac{p^{m_{2}}-1}{p^{v}+1}}=(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}};\\
-p^{s},\quad&\text{if}~\frac{s}{v}~\text{is odd and}~a^{\frac{p^{m_{2}}-1}{p^{v}+1}}\neq(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}};\\
p^{s+v},\quad&\text{if}~\frac{s}{v}~\text{is odd and}~a^{\frac{p^{m_{2}}-1}{p^{v}+1}}=(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}}.\end{cases}

Lemma 2.3

Fixed
a\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}
, then the equation

\displaystyle a^{p^{u}}X^{p^{2u}}+aX=0

is solvable in
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}^{*}
if and only if both
\frac{m_{2}}{v}
is even and
a^{\frac{p^{m_{2}}-1}{p^{v}+1}}=(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}}
. Furthermore, there are exactly
p^{2v}-1
non-zero solutions in this case.

Remark 2.1

By Lemma
2.3
, it is easy to see that
f(X)=a^{p^{u}}X^{p^{2u}}+aX
is a permutation polynomial over
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}^{*}
if and only if
\frac{m_{2}}{v}
is odd, or both
\frac{m_{2}}{v}
is even and
a^{\frac{p^{m_{2}}-1}{p^{v}+1}}\neq(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}}
.

Lemma 2.4

Suppose that
f(X)=a^{p^{u}}X^{p^{2u}}+aX
is a permutation polynomial over
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}
, then,
f(X)=-b^{p^{u}}
has an unique solution in
\mathbb{F}_{p^{m_{2}}}
. Furthermore,

\displaystyle S_{m_{2},u}(a,b)=\begin{cases}G_{m_{2}}\eta_{m_{2}}(a)\zeta_{p}^{\mathrm{Tr_{m_{2}}}(-ax_{0}^{p^{u}+1})},\qquad&\text{if}~\frac{m_{2}}{v}~\text{is odd};\\
(-1)^{\frac{s}{v}}p^{s}\zeta_{p}^{\mathrm{Tr_{m_{2}}}(-ax_{0}^{p^{u}+1})},\qquad&\text{if}~\frac{m_{2}}{v}~\text{is even}.\end{cases}

Lemma 2.5

For the non-permutation polynomial
f(X)=a^{p^{u}}X^{p^{2u}}+aX

6
Conclusion

Note that codes in
[
17
,
15
]
are always even dimension. In this paper, we construct several classes of two-weight and three-weight linear codes with any dimension over the finite field
\mathbb{F}_{p}
(
p
is an odd prime) by extending the construction in
[
17
,
15
]
, and we determine their complete weight enumerators by using Weil sums. Furthermore, according to the Griesmer bound, some examples of these codes are optimal or almost optimal, respectively.
