T.T. Hong, L.T.T. Phuong, T.Phong Nguyen, N.H.T. Nha, L. T. Hue

Abstract
The lepton flavor violating (LFV) decays h , Z → e b ​ e a h,Z\to e_{b}e_{a} , and e b → e a ​ γ e_{b}\to e_{a}\gamma are discussed in a class of general 3-3-1 models adding heavy neutral leptons and singly charged Higgs bosons to accommodate experimental data of neutrino oscillation and ( g − 2 ) e a (g-2)_{e_{a}} anomalies of charged leptons through the inverse seesaw mechanism. We show that the models with the minimal number of inverse seesaw neutrinos cannot reach the 1 ​ σ 1\sigma range of ( g − 2 ) μ (g-2)_{\mu} data due to the experimental upper bounds on decay rates of ( τ → μ ​ γ ) (\tau\to\mu\gamma) and ( μ → e ​ γ ) (\mu\to e\gamma) . Features of LFV decays are presented in detail, focusing on the regions of parameter space satisfying the 1 ​ σ 1\sigma ranges of ( g − 2 ) e , μ (g-2)_{e,\mu} data.
