Generalization of the basis theorem for the D D -type Coxeter groups
Sawsan Khaskeia, Robert Shwartz

Abstract
The O ​ G ​ S OGS for non-abelian groups is an interesting generalization of the basis of finite abelian groups. The definition of O ​ G ​ S OGS states that every element of a group has a unique presentation as a product of some powers of specific generators of the group, in a specific given order. In case of the symmetric groups S n S_{n} there is a paper of R. Shwartz, which demonstrates a strong connection between the O ​ G ​ S OGS and the standard Coxeter presentation of the symmetric group, which is called the standard O ​ G ​ S OGS of S n S_{n} . In this paper we generalize the standard O ​ G ​ S OGS of S n S_{n} to the finite classical Coxeter group D n D_{n} . We describe the exchange laws for the generalized standard O ​ G ​ S OGS of D n D_{n} , and we connect it to the Coxeter length of elements of D n D_{n} .

The fundamental theorem of finitely generated abelian groups states the following:
Let

a_{1},a_{2},\ldots a_{n}

|a_{k}|

i_{k}\in\mathbb{Z}
, where
a_{k}
has infinite order in

Where, the meaning of the theorem is that every abelian group

(where

.

Definition 1.0.1

In contrast to finitely generated abelian groups, the existence of an
