Definability in the group of infinitesimals of a compact Lie group
M. Bays & Y. Peterzil

Abstract
We show that for G G a simple compact Lie group, the infinitesimal subgroup G 00 {G^{00}} is bi-interpretable with a real closed convexly valued field. We deduce that for G G an infinite definably compact group definable in an o-minimal expansion of a field, G 00 {G^{00}} is bi-interpretable with the disjoint union of a (possibly trivial) ℚ \mathbb{Q} -vector space and finitely many (possibly zero) real closed valued fields. We also describe the isomorphisms between such infinitesimal subgroups, and along the way prove that every definable field in a real closed convexly valued field R R is definably isomorphic to R R .

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
(G;*)
be an infinite definably compact group definable in a
sufficiently saturated o-minimal expansion

Then
({G^{00}}(M);*)
is bi-interpretable with the disjoint union of a
(possibly trivial) divisible torsion-free abelian group and finitely many
(possibly zero) real closed convexly valued fields.

To indicate why this is the correct statement, let us note that it can not be
strengthened to bi-interpretability with a single real closed valued field as
in Theorem

is just a divisible torsion free abelian group and thus does not

-definable but not

Note that Theorem

applies in particular to an arbitrary compact
