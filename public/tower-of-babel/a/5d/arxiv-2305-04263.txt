Conformal super Virasoro algebra: matrix model and quantum deformed algebra
Fridolin Melong †,‡

Abstract
. In this paper, we construct the super Virasoro algebra with an arbitrary conformal dimension Δ \Delta from the generalized ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -deformed quantum algebra and investigate the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -deformed super Virasoro algebra with the particular conformal dimension Δ = 1 . \Delta=1. Furthermore, we perform the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -conformal Virasoro n n algebra, the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -conformal super Virasoro n n -algebra ( n n even) and discuss a toy model for the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -conformal Virasoro constraints and ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -conformal super Virasoro constraints. Besides, we generalized the notion of the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -elliptic hermitian matrix model with an arbitrary conformal dimension Δ \Delta . Finally, we deduce relevant particular cases generated by quantum algebras known in the literature.
