1.
Introduction

For a function
P:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R}
and
D=(\partial_{1},\ldots,\partial_{d})
,
consider the periodic initial value problem

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}i\,\partial_{t}u+P(D)u=0,&x\in\mathbb{T}^{d},\quad t\in\mathbb{R},\\
u(x,0)=f(x),&x\in\mathbb{T}^{d},\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

whose solution we denote by

e^{itP(D)}f(x)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\widehat{f}_{n}\,e^{2\pi i(n\cdot x+P(n)t)}.

(1.2)

Carleson’s problem of convergence to the initial datum consists in finding
s
for which

\lim_{t\to 0}e^{itP(D)}f(x)=f(x)\qquad\text{ for almost every }x\in\mathbb{T}^{d},\qquad\forall f\in H^{s}(\mathbb{T}^{d}),

(1.3)

and it is well-known that (
1.3
) is equivalent to proving the maximal estimate

\big\lVert\sup_{t>0}|e^{itP(D)}f|\big\rVert_{L^{2}(\mathbb{T}^{d})}\lesssim\lVert f\rVert_{H^{s}},\qquad\forall f\in H^{s}(\mathbb{T}^{d}).

(1.4)

Thus, up to losing the endpoint information, the objective is to determine the critical exponent

s_{P}(\mathbb{T}^{d})=\inf\big\{\,s\,:\,\text{ the maximal estimate }\eqref{eq:Maximal_Estimate}\text{ holds }\,\big\}.

(1.5)

For the Schrödinger equation,
corresponding to
P(n)=|n|^{2}
,
the best result available is

\frac{d}{2(d+1)}\leq s_{|n|^{2}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})\leq\frac{d}{d+2},

(1.6)

obtained in
[
22
, Prop 1 in Section 2]
and
[
8
, Prop. 3.1 and 3.2]
.
The problem is also open for more general symbols.
In this article, we focus on polynomial symbols,
including

that corresponds to the power Laplacian
P_{2k}(D)=(-\Delta)^{k}
in the equation.
We give a lower bound for

in Section
1.1

and we discuss the state of the art for upper bounds in Section

.

1.3.

1.

Proof.

The basic idea boils down to controling the overlap among the balls in the union of

For a collection of sets

\Big(\sum_{j}|E_{j}|\Big)^{2}=\lVert f\rVert_{L^{1}}^{2}\leq|\operatorname{supp}(f)|\,\lVert f\rVert_{L^{2}}^{2}\leq\Big|\bigcup_{j}E_{j}\Big|\,\sum_{j,k}|E_{j}\cap E_{k}|.

The number of pairs of sets

which implies

We use this argument in the case of

Lemma 2.4

Let
