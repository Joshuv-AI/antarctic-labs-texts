Geometrical and maximal u u -entropy measure-theoretic structures of maps with c c -mostly expanding center and c c -mostly contracting center


Abstract
In this paper, we classified ergodic maximal u u -entropy measures among the set of partially hyperbolic diffeomorphisms with ( E u ​ u , A ) (E^{uu},A) -Markov partition, c c -mostly contracting center and c c -mostly expanding center in the C 1 C^{1} -topology. More specifically, ergodic maximal u u -entropy measures can be divided into c c - u u -states and c c - c ​ u cu -states. Due to the existence of c c -mostly expanding center, ergodic maximal u u -entropy measures of type c c - u u -states are trivial. For ergodic maximal u u -entropy measures of type c c - c ​ u cu -states, by noting that its structure is similar to that of physical measures, with the help of the skeleton we obtain their properties of supports and basins, and variation of number in some sense. Consequently, the ergodic measures of maximal u u -entropy of type c c - c ​ u cu -states are finite in the sense that the measure supports are consistent. On the certain environment, union of the basins of ergodic measures of maximal u u -entropy of type c c - c ​ u cu -states is dense in whole space and number of c c - c ​ u cu -state is upper semicontinuous in the sense of support. Finally, we provide propositions for qualitative construction examples, which also explains that there are many such examples here. Combined with our results, new example we give shows that for such diffeomorphisms, • they can have any given number ergodic measures of maximal u u -entropy of type c c - c ​ u cu -states in the sense of support; • there is uncountable ergodic measure of maximal u u -entropy of type c c - c ​ u cu -states whose support is the same; • union of the basins of ergodic measures of maximal u u -entropy of type c c - c ​ u cu -states is dense in whole space for different c c - c ​ u cu -states in the sense of support; • any given number of equivalence classes can disappear under any small perturbations(i.e, number of c c - c ​ u cu -state is upper semicontinuous in the sense of support) where each equivalence class consists of uncountable ergodic measures of maximal u u -entropy of type c c - c ​ u cu -state. Some examples of C 1 + C^{1+} can also be applied to construction of any given number physical measures of diffeomorphisms with mostly expanding center and mostly contracting center in [ 34 ] , and then used to study finiteness, the upper-semicontinuity of number, the statistically stability and continuity of physical measures. By the way, the reason of such construction has also been explained and also explains the correlation between maximum u u -entropy measure of type c c - c ​ u cu -states and physical measure in some sense.
