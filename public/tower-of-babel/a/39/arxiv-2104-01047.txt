Theoretical study in the B ¯ 0 → J / ψ ​ K ¯ ∗ 0 ​ K 0 \bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0} and B ¯ 0 → J / ψ ​ f 1 ​ ( 1285 ) \bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285) decays
DaZhuang He, Xuan Luo, YiLing Xie, Hao Sun

Abstract
We study the decay processes of B ¯ 0 → J / ψ ​ K ¯ ∗ 0 ​ K 0 \bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0} and B ¯ 0 → J / ψ ​ f 1 ​ ( 1285 ) \bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285) to analyse the f 1 ​ ( 1285 ) f_{1}(1285) resonance. By the calculation within chiral unitary approach where f 1 ​ ( 1285 ) f_{1}(1285) resonance is dynamically generated from the K ∗ ​ K ¯ − c . c . K^{*}\bar{K}-c.c. interaction, we find that the K ¯ ∗ 0 ​ K 0 \bar{K}^{*0}K^{0} invariant mass distribution has a clear broad peak. Such broad peak has been understood as the signal of the f 1 ​ ( 1285 ) f_{1}(1285) . Finally, we obtain a theoretical result R t = Γ B ¯ 0 → J / ψ ​ K ¯ ∗ 0 ​ K 0 / Γ B ¯ 0 → J / ψ ​ f 1 ​ ( 1285 ) R_{t}=\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}/\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)} which is expected to be compared with the experimental data.



I
Introduction

One of the main goals of the hadron physics is to unravel the nature of mesons or baryons.
With the discovery of more and more new particles, the traditional quark model is not enough for us to understand the nature of particles.
The general examples are
a_{0}(980)
and
f_{0}(980)
, and several perspectives were presented about the nature of these:

q\bar{q}
states
Li et al. 2000
;
Godfrey and Napolitano 1999
, glueballs, multiquark states or meson-meson molecules
Klempt and Zaitsev 2007
.

In very low energy regions, the strong interactions of the light pseudoscalar particles can be simplified by considering global symmetry.
Thus, based on the chiral symmetry of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) and the concept of effective field theory(EFT),
a powerful theoretical tool Chiral Perturbation Theory (
\chi PT
) has been proposed
Pich 1995
;
Bernard et al. 1995
.
The
\chi PT
can be used for the study of QCD at low energies(
\leq 500
MeV) by means of chiral Lagrangians using fields related to mesons and baryons.
Further, the application of the unitary approaches in
\chi PT
, unitary chiral approach(
U\chi PT
), provides a possibility to study strong interactions
at higher energy(
\geq 500
MeV) by
\chi PT
. The
U\chi PT
has been successfully applied to study meson-meson and meson-baryon interactions,
and also gives a new perspective to understand the nature of particles, since several resonances can be understood as dynamically generated.
Such as, the
a_{0}(980)
and
f_{1}(1285)
have been viewed as dynamically generated from the interaction of
\bar{K}K
,

\pi\eta

Oller and Oset 1997
;
Locher et al. 1998
;
Xie and Geng 2016
;
Molina et al. 2020
;
Duan et al. 2020
;
Wang et al. 2020
and
K^{*}\bar{K}-c.c.

Xie and Oset 2016
;
Miyahara et al. 2015
respectively.
As for the baryon case, the
\Lambda(1405)
can be shown as dynamically generated from the interaction of

\bar{K}N
and
\eta\Lambda

Oller et al. 2000
;
Xie et al. 2018
;
Roca et al. 2015
;
Miyahara et al. 2015
,
\cdots
.
In this paper, we focus on the

as dynamically generated.

The

It’s hard for

The main decay modes of

In Refs.

, the amplitude for
\mathcal{U}_{J/\psi K^{*0}\bar{K}^{0}}
has a minus sign compared with Eq.(
19
).

With the above results, now the invariant mass distribution
d\Gamma/dM_{inv}
can be written
Zyla et al. 2020

\displaystyle\frac{d\Gamma}{dM_{inv}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum C_{s}^{2}\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{M_{inv}}{8M_{\bar{B}^{0}}^{3}}

(20)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\int_{M_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}}^{min}}^{M_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}}^{max}}|\mathcal{U}|^{2}M_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}}dM_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}},

the
\sum C_{s}^{2}
represents the total polarization structure, which can be written as

\displaystyle\sum C_{s}^{2}=3+\frac{\vec{p}_{J/\psi}^{2}}{m_{J/\psi}^{2}}+\frac{\vec{p}_{\bar{K}^{*0}}^{2}}{m_{\bar{K}^{*0}}},

(21)

where we make the approximation
Liang et al. 2018
;
Sakai et al. 2018
;
Oset and Ramos 2010
that the three-momenta of the vector mesons in the loop are on-shell and small compared to their masses. And we have

\displaystyle\vec{p}_{J/\psi}=

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}(M_{\bar{B}^{0}}^{2},M_{J/\psi}^{2},M_{inv}^{2})}{2M_{\bar{B}^{0}}},

(22)

\displaystyle\vec{p}_{\bar{K}^{*0}}=

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}(M_{inv}^{2},M_{k^{0}}^{2},M_{\bar{k}^{*0}}^{2})}{2M_{inv}},

where
\lambda(x,y,z)=x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}-2xy-2yz-2xz
is the Kählén function.

In the Eq.(
20
),
M_{\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}
and
M_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}}
are interrelated,
thus for specific
M_{\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}

is defined as

\displaystyle M_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}}^{max}=

\displaystyle\sqrt{(E_{\bar{K}^{*0}}+E_{J/\psi})^{2}-\left(\sqrt{E_{\bar{K}^{*0}}^{2}-M_{\bar{K}^{*0}}^{2}}-\sqrt{E_{J/\psi}^{2}-M_{J/\psi}^{2}}\right)^{2}},

\displaystyle M_{J/\psi,\bar{K}^{*0}}^{min}=

\displaystyle\sqrt{(E_{\bar{K}^{*0}}+E_{J/\psi})^{2}-\left(\sqrt{E_{\bar{K}^{*0}}^{2}-M_{\bar{K}^{*0}}^{2}}+\sqrt{E_{J/\psi}^{2}-M_{J/\psi}^{2}}\right)^{2}},

where
E_{\bar{K}^{*0}}=(M_{inv}^{2}-M_{K}^{2}+M_{\bar{K}^{*0}}^{2})/2M_{inv}
and
E_{J/\psi}=(M_{\bar{B}^{0}}^{2}-M_{inv}^{2}-M_{J/\psi}^{2})/2M_{inv}

are the energies of
\bar{K}^{*0}
and
J/\psi
in the
\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}
rest frame.
The Dalitz plot for
M_{\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}
and

\displaystyle T_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)}=V_{P}C_{s}^{\prime}G(M_{f_{1}})g_{f_{1}}

where the polarization factor
C_{s}^{\prime}

The coupling of

K^{*}\bar{K}

g_{f_{1}}\epsilon_{i}(f_{1})\epsilon_{i}(K^{*})
,
by contracting the two polarization vectors
\epsilon_{i}(K^{*})\epsilon_{j}(K^{*})=\delta_{ij}

\displaystyle\sum C_{s}^{\prime 2}=3+\frac{\vec{p}_{J/\psi}^{2}}{m_{J/\psi}^{2}}+\frac{\vec{p}_{f_{1}}^{2}}{m_{f_{1}}^{2}}.

(25)

where
\vec{p}_{f_{1}}=\vec{p}_{J/\psi}=\lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}(M_{\bar{B}^{0}}^{2},M_{J/\psi}^{2},M_{f_{1}}^{2})/2M_{\bar{B}^{0}}
is the three momentum of
f_{1}
and
J/\psi
in the

rest frame.

Then, the partial decay width of
\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)
can be written

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{V_{P}^{2}}{8\pi}\frac{g_{f_{1}}^{2}G^{2}(M^{2}_{f_{1}})|\vec{p}_{J/\psi}|}{M_{\bar{B}^{0}}^{2}}

(26)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\left(2+\frac{\left(M_{\bar{B}^{0}}^{2}-M_{J/\psi}^{2}-M_{f_{1}}^{2}\right)^{2}}{4M_{J/\psi}^{2}M_{f_{1}}^{2}}\right)

By considering
\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)

\displaystyle R_{\Gamma}=\frac{d\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}/dM_{inv}(\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0})}{\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)}}

(27)

NUMERICAL RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

With the former formulae, we present, in FIG.

as a function of the invariant mass

of the
\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}

As mentioned above, the unknown parameter
V_{P}
cancels, therefore it does not appear in the final result of the ratio
R_{\Gamma}
.
Meanwhile, we consider the following renormalization scales
\mu=1100\text{MeV},\ 1200\text{MeV},\ 1300\text{MeV}

and corresponding subtraction constants
a(\mu)=-1.66,\ -1.62,\ -1.59
.
A straightforward relation between
a(\mu)
and

is given by
Oller and Meissner 2001

a_{i}(\mu)=-2\log{\left(1+\sqrt{1+\frac{M_{i}^{2}}{\mu^{2}}}\right)}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{m_{i}}{M_{i}}\right).

(28)

The range of the invariant mass

of

In principle, the range of the invariant mass

2182
MeV(

200-300

We define the parameter

\displaystyle R_{t}=\frac{\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}}{\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)}}=\int_{m_{\bar{K}^{*0}}+m_{K^{0}}}^{m_{\bar{B}^{0}}-m_{J/\psi}}R_{\Gamma}\ dM_{inv},

.

.

On the other side, the processes

\frac{2}{3}
,

, respectively.

.

Furthermore, as mentioned, in order that the chiral unitary approach can work well,

.
As for the relation between

We expect that future experimental observation of the invariant mass distribution

would provide valuable information on the mechanism of the
\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}

V
SUMMARY

In the present work, we have shown the distribution of the invariant mass
\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}
in the decay
\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}

to investigate the basic nature of the
f_{1}(1285)
resonance. In the frame of chiral unitary theory, the
f_{1}(1285)
resonance is dynamically generated
from the
K^{*}\bar{K}-c.c.
interaction, further we get the theoretical prediction

R_{\Gamma}=\frac{d\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}/dM_{inv}(\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0})}{\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)}}

as a function of invariant mass
M_{inv}(\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0})
. The result of
R_{\Gamma}
reproduces the peak of the mass distribution of
M_{inv}(\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0})
,
which is caused by the production of the
f_{1}(1285)
state.
Finally, we can obtain a theoretical result
R_{t}=\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi\bar{K}^{*0}K^{0}}/\Gamma_{\bar{B}^{0}\to J/\psi f_{1}(1285)}

which is possible to be compared with the experimental result
R_{e}
.
We expect that there will be more valuable data in future experimental observations, which could advance our understanding of
f_{1}(1285)
.
