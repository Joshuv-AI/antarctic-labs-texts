Logarithmic corrections to black hole entropy and holography
Aritra Ghosh, Chandrasekhar Bhamidipati, Sudipta Mukherji

Abstract
We compute logarithmic corrections to the black hole entropy S bh S_{\rm bh} in a holographic set up where the cosmological constant Λ \Lambda and Newton’s constant G D G_{D} are taken to be thermodynamic parameters, related to variations in bulk pressure P P and central charge c c . In the bulk, the logarithmic corrections are of the form: 𝒮 = S bh − k ​ ln ⁡ S bh + ⋯ \mathcal{S}=S_{\rm bh}-k\ln S_{\rm bh}+\cdots arising due to fluctuations in thermodynamic volume, induced by a variable Λ \Lambda , in addition to energy fluctuations. We explicitly compute this coefficient k k for the BTZ black hole and show that the result matches with the one coming from the logarithmic corrections to the Cardy’s formula. We propose an entropy function in the CFT, which exactly reproduces the logarithmic corrections to black hole entropy in arbitrary dimensions.
