The Generalization of Schottky Inequality and Its Applications
Shiyu Chen, Junyi Hu

Abstract
This article used Bloch function to derive Schottky inequality, obtained its generalization by using elliptic integral deviation function and demonstrated its applications.

Theorem 1(Classical Schottky Inequality)
: If a function

f\left(z\right)

is holomorphic in

\left|z\right|<1

and the solution is neither 0 nor 1, then

\ln\left|f\left(z\right)\right|\leq\left[C+\max\left\{\ln\left|f\left(0\right)\right|,0\right\}\right]\frac{1+\left|z\right|}{1-\left|z\right|}-C

(6)

C=\left[\min\left\{\rho_{0,1}\left(z\right)\mid\left|z\right|=1\right\}\right]^{-1}

named Landau constant.

\rho_{0,1}\left(z\right)\left|{\rm d}z\right|

is the Poincare metric in

D\left(0,1\right)\backslash\left\{0\right\}

. The best numerical value of Landau constant was confirmed in later 1970s
by J.A. Hempel
[
2
]
:

C=\frac{1}{4\pi^{2}}\Gamma^{4}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)=4.3768796\cdots

(7)

: Due to the Generalized Schwarz Lemma, we have:

\rho_{0,1}\left(f\left(z\right)\right)\left|f^{{}^{\prime}}\left(z\right)\right|\leq\frac{2}{1-\left|z\right|^{2}}

(8)

\left|f\left(z\right)\right|<1
. We

\frac{f^{{}^{\prime}}\left(te^{i\theta}\right)}{f\left(te^{i\theta}\right)}\left[C-\ln\left|f\left(te^{i\theta}\right)\right|\right]^{-1}\leq\frac{2}{1-t^{2}},t\in\left[0,r\right]
