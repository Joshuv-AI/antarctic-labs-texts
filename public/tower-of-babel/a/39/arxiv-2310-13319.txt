A.7
.
Categorification

Let us now return to the considerations of
Section
4
and provide some evidence for why

\mathcal{A}=\left<A_{1}^{\mathrm{log}},\ldots,A_{12}^{\mathrm{log}}\right>^{\bot}\neq 0
. To reach contradiction with
\mathcal{A}=0
we interpret
both
K
- groups for

and
\mathfrak{S}
in terms of the
Grothendieck groups of the bimodules considered in

A.4
— at the end of the day the
latter two groups will not be isomorphic (we will write

\overline{K}_{0}

is trivial

\overline{K}_{0}(S)=\overline{K}_{0}(\mathfrak{S})

4.13

). More precisely, we argue that

\widetilde{L}
, corresponding to the

- multiple fibre

of

, gives a non - trivial
torsion element in
\overline{K}_{0}(\mathfrak{S})
, whereas

\overline{K}_{0}(S)\simeq\mathbb{Z}^{12}
.

15)

15)

This should be
compared with invariants constructed in
[

in order to
distinguish rational cyclic group quotients from irrational ones
(all over
\mathbb{Q}
). See also
[
33
]
for further general
outlook.

To
\widetilde{L}

with the local system on

A.9
.
A - side

This final subsection discusses some analogies with the Seiberg –
Witten theory on
4
- manifolds. Here
moduli spaces of
instantons
on
S
will play the role of geometric counterparts
for
D^{b}(S)
. Let us recall the relevant notions.

First of all, if
M
is the
4
- manifold underlying
S
, then

K_{S}
corresponds to a
\text{spin}^{c}
- structure on
M
(same
also applies to
\mathfrak{S}
being homeomorphic to
M
). This yields
a decomposition of the complexified tangent bundle
T_{S}\otimes\mathbb{C}=(W^{-})^{*}\otimes W^{+}
and the (gauge class of) Dirac operator

D_{S}:H^{0}(S,W^{+})\to H^{0}(S,W^{-})
. Here
W^{\pm}
are rank two
vector bundles satisfying
\bigwedge^{2}W^{\pm}=-K_{S}
. Fixing a
rank
2
vector bundle
\mathcal{V}
on
S
with Chern classes

c_{i}(\mathcal{V})
one obtains a moduli space of twisted Dirac
operators
D_{S}^{a}:H^{0}(S,\mathcal{V}\otimes W^{+})\to H^{0}(S,\mathcal{V}\otimes W^{-})
for various Hermitian
connections
a
on
\mathcal{V}
. Imposing further restrictions on
the solutions of the equations
D_{S}^{a}\star=0
, as well as on
the curvature of
a
, yields various moduli spaces of

topological instantons
whose geometric characteristics
provide Donaldson, SW, etc. invariants of
M
, specific to the
smooth structure induced by
S
.

Example A.10

(cf.
[
50
, Ch. 1, §2]
)
.

Consider a collection of vectors
e_{i}\in\text{Pic}\,S
satisfying: (i)
e_{i}\equiv K_{S}\mod 2
; (ii)

K_{S}^{2}-4c_{2}(S)\leqslant e_{i}^{2}\leqslant 0
; (iii)
e_{i}\cdot K_{S}=0
. The
hyperplanes (‘‘ walls ’’)
e_{i}^{\bot}
divide
\Pic S\otimes\mathbb{R}
into a system of chambers
\mathcal{C}_{k}
. Then we can form
the moduli space
J_{k}^{l}
of Dirac operators, corresponding to
vector bundles
\mathcal{V}
with
c_{1}(\mathcal{V})=c_{1}(S)
and

c_{2}(\mathcal{V})=c_{2}(S)+k
as above, which are also
semistable with respect to polarization belonging to the

k^{\text{th}}
chamber
\mathcal{C}_{k}
. Imposing further a

quasiparabolic structure
on
\mathcal{V}
with respect to
some curve
C\subset S
, one gets a family of moduli spaces

J_{k,\alpha}^{l}
of
\alpha
- parabolic vector bundles,
\alpha\in[0,1/2]
. These
J_{k,\alpha}^{l}
also turn out to be
birationally isomorphic.

Going back to the categorical picture let us say first that the
choice of a spin structure and topological data as above parallels
that of a numerically exceptional collection (cf.
Remark
2.9
). Further, the exceptionality
condition for collections of objects in
D^{b}
may be considered as
an analog of Dirac or Seiberg – Witten equations, which brings in
a similarity between
D^{b}
and various ‘‘ Jacobians ’’
J_{k}^{l}

(see Example
A.10
). We expect the properties of
the latter might guide the properties of the former. Of most
interest to us is the behavior under the logarithmic
transformations (cf. Section
4
).

Namely, we propose that the ‘‘ core ’’
D^{b}(S)
is preserved
under these transformations (cf. the discussion at the end of
(
A.7
)), which is reminiscent of the
above birationality of
J_{k,\alpha}^{l}
.

.

Representing one log transform
on a minimal elliptic surface as a

one finds that the SW invariants are preserved (see e. g.

[
