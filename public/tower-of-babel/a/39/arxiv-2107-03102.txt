Abstract
. In a Dyck path a peak which is (weakly) higher than all the preceding peaks is called a strict (weak) left to right maximum. We obtain explicit generating functions for both weak and strict left to right maxima in Dyck paths. The proofs of the associated asymptotics make use of analytic techniques such as Mellin transforms, singularity analysis and formal residue calculus.

General introduction

A Dyck path is a lattice path in the first quadrant, that starts at the origin (0,0) with an up step (
u=(1,1)
) and thereafter only up and down (
d=(1,-1)
) steps are allowed under the conditions that it may not go below the
x
-axis and that it may terminate only if the end point is on the
x
-axis. A Dyck path with
n

(2n,0)
, see the definition in
[
15
]
. Such a Dyck path is said to have length
2n
. For a detailed study of properties of Dyck paths see
[
6

[

Given an arbitrary Dyck path, we mean by a
strict left to right maximum
, any peak (successive pair of the form
ud
) in the Dyck path which is above all steps to its left. A
weak left to right maximum
is a peak which is greater than or equal to all peaks to its left.

A standard combinatorial problem is the accounting for the number of left to right maxima in combinatorial structures such as permutations and words over a fixed alphabet. In this paper we focus on obtaining a generating function for the number of left to right maxima in Dyck paths. This is a bivariate generating function which tracks the number of up steps by
z
and the number of left to right maxima by
x

We also obtain a generating function for the total number of left to right maxima in Dyck paths with
n
up steps.

Left to right maxima in Dyck paths

We start this section by referring to the paper
[

\displaystyle C(h):=\frac{z^{h}\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}}{\lambda_{1}{}^{h+2}-\lambda_{2}{}^{h+2}}

(2.1)

and

\displaystyle A(h):=\frac{\lambda_{1}{}^{h+1}-\lambda_{2}{}^{h+1}}{\lambda_{1}{}^{h+2}-\lambda_{2}{}^{h+2}},

(2.2)

where
\lambda_{1}

\displaystyle{\lambda_{1}=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}}{2};\lambda_{2}=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}}{2}}.

(2.3)

As explained in the introductory section, we consider a sequence of possibly empty Dyck paths of height
\leq h
for
h=1,2,\ldots\ .
At the end of each path in the sequence, we have a single up step that leads to the next left to right maximum and eventually to the first overall maximum of the entire Dyck path. We let
x
count the number of left to right maxima attained by the Dyck path. This leads to our first theorem:

Theorem 1
.

The generating function
for the number of left to right maxima tracked by
x

r
and length tracked by
z
is

\displaystyle F(x,z,r):=z^{r}xC(r)\prod_{h=1}^{r-1}(1+x(A(h)-1)).

(2.4)

So, the total number of left to right maxima for Dyck paths of fixed height
r
is found by differentiating the above function with respect to
x
and setting
x=1
. The derivative at this point is given by

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial x}F(x,z,r)\Big|_{x=1}

\displaystyle={z^{r}\text{ }C(r)\prod_{h=1}^{r-1}A(h)+z^{r}C(r)\prod_{h=1}^{r-1}A(h)\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}\frac{A(i)-1}{A(i)}}

\displaystyle=z^{r}\text{ }C(r)\prod_{h=1}^{r-1}A(h)\left(1+\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}\frac{A(i)-1}{A(i)}\right)

\displaystyle=z^{r}C(r)\prod_{h=1}^{r-1}A(h)\left(r-\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}\frac{1}{A(i)}\right)

(2.5)

Note that

z^{r}C(r)\prod_{h=1}^{r-1}A[h]
telescopes to become

\frac{2^{3+2r}z^{2r}\left(1-4z^{2}\right)}{\left(-\left(1-\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}\right)^{1+r}+\left(1+\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}\right)^{1+r}\right)\left(-\left(1-\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}\right)^{2+r}+\left(1+\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}\right)^{2+r}\right)}

but the full generating function becomes very complicated as a function of

To simplify this generating function, we substitute

\displaystyle z^{2}=\frac{u}{(1+u)^{2}}

(2.6)

in (

\displaystyle T(r):=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}F(x,z,r)\Big|_{x=1}=\frac{(1-u)^{2}u^{r}(1+u)}{\left(1-u^{1+r}\right)\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}\left(r-\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}\frac{1-u^{2+i}}{(1+u)\left(1-u^{1+i}\right)}\right).

(2.7)

The full generating function for the total number of left-to-right maxima in all Dyck paths of length

\displaystyle Tot(u):=\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}T(r).

(2.8)

2.1.
Formula for total number of left-to-right maxima

In this section, we will obtain an exact formula for the total number of left-to-right maxima in terms of a well-known arithmetic function, namely the divisor function
d(r)
. Note that

\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{u^{r}}{1-u^{r}}=\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}d(r)\,u^{r}

.

To read off coefficients from equation (
2.21
), we observe that for any formal power series
f(z)

[z^{2n}]f(z)=[u^{n}](1-u)(1+u)^{2n-1}f(z(u)).

This can be justified by using formal residue calculus, see for example
[
11
]
. Therefore

\displaystyle[z^{2n}]Tot(z)

\displaystyle=[u^{n}](1-u)(1+u)^{2n-1}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{(1-u)u^{r}}{1-u^{1+r}}

\displaystyle=[u^{n}](1-u)(1+u)^{2n-1}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}(d(r+1)-d(r))u^{r}

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=1}^{n}(d(r+1)-d(r))\left(\binom{2n-1}{n-r}-\binom{2n-1}{n-r-1}\right).

(2.22)

Thus we have shown:

Theorem 4
.

The total number of left-to-right maxima in Dyck paths of semi-length
n
is given by

\sum_{r=1}^{n}(d(r+1)-d(r))\left(\binom{2n-1}{n-r}-\binom{2n-1}{n-r-1}\right).

3.
Asymptotics for strict left to right maxima

In this section we find the asymptotic expression for the total number of strict left to right maxima in Dyck paths.
We will follow the approach used to study the height of planted plane trees by Prodinger in
[

]
. For related asymptotic calculations concerning the height of trees and lattice paths, see

First, we extract coefficients of
z^{n}
in

. That is we find

[z^{n}]\frac{1-u}{u}\sum_{r=2}^{\infty}\frac{u^{r}}{1-u^{r}}.

When

is in terms of

, by (

) the function

which is mapped to

. To study this further we set

\frac{1-u}{u}=e^{t}(1-e^{-t})=t+\frac{t^{2}}{2}+\frac{t^{3}}{6}+\cdots\,\,.

To estimate the harmonic sum

as

, we take the Mellin transform of

]

Thus

By using the Mellin inversion formula, , we have

f_{1}(t)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{2-i\infty}^{2+i\infty}f_{1}^{*}(s)\,t^{-s}\,ds
(again see
[

]
). By computing residues

(3.2)

Let

From (

) and (

)

\displaystyle g_{1}(t)\sim-\log(t)-1+\gamma+\left(\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{2}(-1+\gamma-\log(t))\right)t+\cdots.

Let

In terms of the

\displaystyle g_{1}\left(2y+\frac{2y^{3}}{3}+\cdots\right)

\displaystyle\sim(-1+\gamma-\log(2)-\log(y))+\frac{1}{2}(1+2\gamma-2\log(2)-2\log(y))y

\displaystyle\qquad-\frac{y^{2}}{3}+\cdots.

Replacing

\displaystyle-1+\gamma-\log(2)-\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1-4z^{2}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\left(1+2\gamma-2\log(2)-\log\left(1-4z^{2}\right)\right)\sqrt{1-4z^{2}}

To use singularity analysis, see

∎

Now, we simplify Theorem

\left(1-u^{2}\right)^{2}\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\frac{1-u^{1+i}}{1-u^{2+i}}\sum_{r=i}^{\infty}\frac{u^{r}}{\left(1-u^{1+r}\right)\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}.

\frac{\left(1-u^{2}\right)^{2}}{(1-u)u}\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\frac{u^{i+1}}{1-u^{i+2}}.

This is then combined with the single sum which simplifies to

\displaystyle\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{(1-u)u^{r}\left(1-u^{2}\right)(1-r)}{\left(1-u^{1+r}\right)\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}+\frac{\left(1-u^{2}\right)^{2}u^{1+r}}{(1-u)u\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}\right).

(4.6)

In order to further simplify (
4.6
) we replace
\infty
by finite
M
and then apply partial fractions to the summand of the first term which splits up as

\displaystyle\frac{(-1+r)(1-u)(1+u)}{u\left(1-u^{1+r}\right)}+\frac{(-1+r+1)(1-u)(1+u)}{u\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}-\frac{(1-u)(1+u)}{u\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}.

(4.7)

This is telescoping and simplifies to

\displaystyle\frac{(-1+M+1)(1-u)(1+u)}{u\left(1-u^{2+M}\right)}+\sum_{r=1}^{M}\left(\frac{\left(1-u^{2}\right)^{2}u^{1+r}}{(1-u)u\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}-\frac{(1-u)(1+u)}{u\left(1-u^{2+r}\right)}\right).

(4.8)

Now replace
M
by
\sum_{r=1}^{M}1
, then reversing the previous replacement, letting
M

\infty

Theorem 8
.

The simplified generating function

\displaystyle WTot(u)=\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left(1-u^{2}\right)u^{r}}{1-u^{2+r}}.

4.1.
Formula for total number of weak left-to-right maxima

In this section, we again obtain an exact formula for the total number of left-to-right maxima in terms of the divisor function
d(r)
.
To read off coefficients from Theorem
8
, as before

[z^{2n}]f(z)=[u^{n}](1-u)(1+u)^{2n-1}f(z(u)).

Therefore

\displaystyle[z^{2n}]WTot(z)

\displaystyle=[u^{n}](1-u)(1+u)^{2n-1}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{(1-u^{2})u^{r}}{1-u^{2+r}}

\displaystyle=[u^{n}](1-u)(1+u)^{2n-1}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}(d(r+2)-d(r))u^{r}.

From this it follows that:

Theorem 9
.

The total number of weak left-to-right maxima in Dyck paths of semi-length
n
is given by

\sum_{r=1}^{n}(d(r+2)-d(r))\left(\binom{2n-1}{n-r}-\binom{2n-1}{n-r-1}\right).
