2.2.
Weil divisors and divisorial sheaves

In this section
X
will denote a normal variety over a field
\mathbf{k}
. A coherent sheaf
\mathcal{F}
on
X
is called
reflexive
if the natural map
\mathcal{F}\to\mathcal{F}^{**}
is an isomorphism, where
\mathcal{F}^{**}
denotes the double dual. We say that a coherent sheaf
\mathcal{L}
on
X
is
divisorial
if it is of rank one and reflexive. It is well known that Weil divisors on
X
correspond bijectively to divisorial sheaves through the assignment
L\mapsto\mathcal{O}_{X}(L)
. The set of divisorial sheaves forms a group with the reflexified tensor product
\mathcal{L}_{1}[\otimes]\mathcal{L}_{2}\coloneqq(\mathcal{L}_{1}\otimes\mathcal{L}_{2})^{**}
. In particular, if
\mathcal{L}_{i}=\mathcal{O}_{X}(L_{i})
for some divisor
L_{i}
, we have
\mathcal{L}_{1}[\otimes]\mathcal{L}_{2}=\mathcal{O}_{X}(L_{1}+L_{2})
, and
\mathcal{L}_{1}\simeq\mathcal{L}_{2}
if and only if
L_{1}\sim L_{2}
. Throughout the rest of the paper we will often confuse between a divisorial sheaf and its associated Weil divisor; for example we will write
H^{i}(X,L)
rather than
H^{i}(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(L))
.
Similarly, we denote by
|L|
the associated linear series. More generally, when
V
is a subspace of
H^{0}(X,L)
, we denote by
|V|\subseteq|L|
the corresponding linear series. This notation extends naturally to
\mathbf{Q}
-divisors: given a
\mathbf{Q}
-divisor
L
we denote by
|L|_{\mathbf{Q}}
the set of all effective
\mathbf{Q}
-divisors

such that
L^{\prime}\sim_{\mathbf{Q}}L
, and refer to it as the
\mathbf{Q}
-linear series
of
L
. The
\mathbf{Z}_{(p)}
-linear series

is similarly defined.
Let
f\colon X\to Y
be a contraction of normal varieties with normal general fibre
X_{y}
. Let
L
be a divisor on
X
and let
V\subseteq H^{0}(X,L)
be a subspace. We will denote by

the linear series generated by the image of

. The

Remark 2.3
.

A reflexive sheaf

on a normal variety is determined in codimension one. If
