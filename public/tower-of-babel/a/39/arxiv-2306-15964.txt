Theoretical study of scalar meson a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) in the η c → K ¯ 0 ​ K + ​ π − \eta_{c}\to{\bar{K}}^{0}K^{+}\pi^{-} reaction
Yan Ding, Xiao-Hui Zhang, Meng-Yuan Dai, En Wang, De-Min Li, Li-Sheng Geng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We investigate the process η c → K ¯ 0 ​ K + ​ π − \eta_{c}\to{\bar{K}}^{0}K^{+}\pi^{-} by taking into account the S S -wave K ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ {K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}} and ρ ​ ω \rho\omega interactions within the unitary coupled-channel approach, where the scalar meson a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) is dynamically generated. In addition, the contributions from the intermediate resonances K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) − → K ¯ 0 ​ π − K_{0}^{*}(1430)^{-}\to{\bar{K}}^{0}\pi^{-} and K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) 0 → K + ​ π − K_{0}^{*}(1430)^{0}\to K^{+}\pi^{-} are also considered. We find a significant dip structure around 1.8 GeV, associated to the a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) , in the K ¯ 0 ​ K + {{\bar{K}}^{0}K^{+}} invariant mass distribution, and the clear peaks of the K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) K_{0}^{*}(1430) in the K ¯ 0 ​ π − {\bar{K}}^{0}\pi^{-} and K + ​ π − K^{+}\pi^{-} invariant mass distributions, consistent with the BABAR measurements. We further estimate the branching fractions ℬ ⁡ ( η c → K ¯ ∗ 0 ​ K ∗ ⁣ + ​ π − ) = 5.5 × 10 − 3 \mathcal{B}(\eta_{c}\to\bar{K}^{*0}K^{\ast+}\pi^{-})=5.5\times 10^{-3} and ℬ ⁡ ( η c → ω ​ ρ + ​ π − ) = 7.9 × 10 − 3 \mathcal{B}(\eta_{c}\to\omega\rho^{+}\pi^{-})=7.9\times 10^{-3} . Our predictions can be tested by the BESIII and Belle II experiments in the future.

IV
Summary

Assuming the
a_{0}(1710)
as a
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
molecular state, we have investigated the process
\eta_{c}\rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}\pi^{-}
taking into account the contribution from the
S
-wave
\omega\rho^{+}
and
\bar{K}^{*0}K^{*+}
interactions, as well as the contribution from the intermediate resonance
K^{*}_{0}(1430)
.
We predicted one dip structure around 1.8 GeV in the
\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}
invariant mass distribution, which is in agreement with the
BABAR
measurements
BaBar:2015kii
. It should be pointed out that a similar dip structure also appears around 1.8 GeV in the
K^{+}K^{-}
invariant mass distribution of the process
\eta_{c}\to K^{+}K^{-}\pi^{0}
of the
BABAR
measurements
BaBar:2014asx
. Furthermore, we predicted the
K^{+}\pi^{-}
and
\bar{K}^{0}\pi^{-}
invariant mass distributions of the process
\eta_{c}\rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}\pi^{-}
, and found clear peaks of the resonance
K_{0}^{*}(1430)^{0,-}
, consistent with the
BABAR
measurements
BaBar:2015kii
. In addition, we have also plotted the Dalitz plots of the process
\eta_{c}\rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}\pi^{-}
, and shown the possible signals of the
a_{0}(1710)
and
K^{*}_{0}(1430)
.

Finally, we have estimated the branching fractions
\mathcal{B}(\eta_{c}\to\bar{K}^{*0}K^{\ast+}\pi^{-})=5.5\times 10^{-3}
and
\mathcal{B}(\eta_{c}\to\omega\rho^{+}\pi^{-})=7.9\times 10^{-3}
, which are reasonable by comparing with the experimental data. Our theoretical predictions could be tested by the BESIII and Belle II experiments in the future, and the precise measurements of the process
\eta_{c}\rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}\pi^{-}
could shed light on the nature of the scalar
a_{0}(1710)
.
