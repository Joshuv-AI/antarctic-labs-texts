A conjecture of Baruah and Begum on the smallest parts function of restricted overpartitions
Dazhao Tang

Abstract
. In 2017, Andrews, Dixit, Schultz and Yee introduced the function spt ¯ ω ​ ( n ) \overline{\textrm{spt}}_{\omega}(n) , which denotes the number of smallest parts in the overpartitions of n n in which the smallest part is always overlined and all odd parts are less than twice the smallest part. Recently, Baruah and Begum established several internal congruences and congruences modulo small powers of 5 5 for spt ¯ ω ​ ( n ) \overline{\textrm{spt}}_{\omega}(n) . Moreover, they conjectured a family of internal congruences modulo any powers of 5 5 and two families of congruences modulo any even powers of 5 5 . In this paper, we confirm three families of congruences due to Baruah and Begum.

1.
Introduction

A partition of a nonnegative integer
n
is a finite sequence of weakly decreasing positive integers whose sum is
n
. Let
p(n)
denote
the number of partitions of
n
. The generating function of
p(n)
is given by

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p(n)q^{n}=\dfrac{1}{(q;q)_{\infty}},

where here and throughout this paper, we adopt the following customary notation:

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}:=\prod_{j=1}^{n}(1-aq^{j-1}),\quad n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty\}.

For notational convenience, we denote
E(q^{j})=(q^{j};q^{j})_{\infty}
.

In 1919, Ramanujan
[
13
]
discovered that
p(n)
satisfies some beautiful congruences modulo powers of
5
,
7
and
11
. One of them
is that for any
\alpha\geq 1
,

\displaystyle p(5^{\alpha}n+\delta_{\alpha})\equiv 0\pmod{5^{\alpha}},

where
\delta_{\alpha}
is the least nonnegative integer such that
24\delta_{\alpha}\equiv 1\pmod{5^{\alpha}}
. In 2004, Corteel and Lovejoy

[
11
]
introduced the notion of overpartitions in order to give a combinatorial proof of some basic hypergeometric transformation
formulas. An overpartition of
n
is a finite weakly decreasing sequence of natural numbers whose sum is
n
in which the first occurrence
(equivalently, the final occurrence) of a number
may
be overlined. The smallest parts function
\textrm{spt}(n)
, counting the
total number of appearances of the smallest parts in all partitions of
n
, was introduced by Andrews
[
1
]
. He also proved some
congruences modulo
5
,
7
and
13
for
\textrm{spt}(n)
. Since then, this function and some analogous functions have been investigated
in many works, see the survey of Chen
[
6
]
for more details.

In
[
2
]
, Andrews, Dixit, Schultz and Yee introduced the function
\overline{\textrm{spt}}_{\omega}(n)
, which denotes the number of
smallest parts in the overpartitions of
n
in which the smallest part is always overlined and all odd parts are less than twice the
smallest part. They proved that

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\overline{\textrm{spt}}_{\omega}(n)q^{n}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{q^{n}(-q^{n+1};q)_{n}(-q^{2n+2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(1-q^{n})^{2}(q^{n+1};q)_{n}(q^{2n+2};q^{2})_{\infty}}.

Moreover, they
[
2
]
derived several congruences modulo

3
,
4

5
and
6
for
\overline{\textrm{spt}}_{\omega}(n)
.
Soon after, Wang
[
15

[

\displaystyle\quad-D\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\infty}v_{i}x^{i+4}-E\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\infty}v_{i}x^{i+5}

\displaystyle=v_{1}x+\big(v_{2}-Av_{1}\big)x^{2}+\big(v_{3}-Av_{2}-Bv_{1}\big)x^{3}

\displaystyle\quad+\big(v_{4}-Av_{3}-Bv_{2}-Cv_{1}\big)x^{4}+\big(v_{5}-Av_{4}-Bv_{3}-Cv_{2}-Dv_{1}\big)x^{5},

from which we obtain the representation for

. According to (

obtain (
