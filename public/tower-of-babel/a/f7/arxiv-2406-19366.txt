On the torsion locus of the Ceresa normal function
Matt Kerr, Salim Tayou

Abstract
. We prove that the positive-dimensional part of the torsion locus of the Ceresa normal function in ℳ g \mathcal{M}_{g} is not Zariski dense when g ≥ 3 g\geq 3 . Moreover, it has only finitely many components with generic Mumford-Tate group equal to GSp 2 ​ g \mathrm{GSp}_{2g} ; these components are defined over ℚ ¯ \overline{\mathbb{Q}} , and their union is closed under the action of Gal ⁡ ( ℚ ¯ / ℚ ) \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q}) . More generally, we study the distribution of the torsion locus of arbitrary admissible normal functions.

Let
