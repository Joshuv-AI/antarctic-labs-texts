Generalized Numerical Radius Inequalities for Schatten p-Norms
Jana Hamza, Hassan Issa

Abstract
. In this paper, we present various inequalities for generalized numerical radius of 2 × 2 2\times 2 block matrices for Schatten p-norm. Moreover, we give a refinement of the triangle inequality for the Schatten p-generalized numerical radius.

1.
Introduction

Consider the space
B(H)
of bounded linear operators over a Hilbert space
H
. For
A\in B(H)
, the numerical radius, the usual operator norm, and the Schatten p-norm, are denoted by
\omega{(A)}
,
\left\|A\right\|
and
\left\|A\right\|_{p}
respectively.

A norm
\left|\left|\left|\cdot\right|\right|\right|
on
B(H)
is said to be unitarily invariant if
\left|\left|\left|UAV\right|\right|\right|=\left|\left|\left|A\right|\right|\right|
, where
A\in B(H)
and
U,V\in B(H)
being unitary, and weakly unitarily invariant if
\left|\left|\left|UAU^{\ast}\right|\right|\right|=\left|\left|\left|A\right|\right|\right|
where
A\in B(H)
and
U\in B(H)
being unitary. It is known that
\left\|A\right\|_{p}
is unitarily invariant.

We should note that if
B(H)
is the space of
n\times n
complex matrices,
M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then for
A,B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
we have

\left|\left|\left|A\oplus A^{\ast}\right|\right|\right|=\left|\left|\left|A\oplus A\right|\right|\right|

(1.1)

and

\left|\left|\left|A\oplus B\right|\right|\right|=\left|\left|\left|\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right|\right|\right|\hskip 5.0pt\mbox{(see \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{[11]}{}{}]})}.

(1.2)

Moreover, we have

\left\|A\oplus B\right\|_{p}=(\left\|A\right\|_{p}^{p}+\left\|B\right\|_{p}^{p})^{\frac{1}{p}},

(1.3)

and

\left\|A\oplus A\right\|_{p}=2^{\frac{1}{p}}\left\|A\right\|_{p}.

(1.4)

The numerical radius
\omega{(\cdot)}
is defined by
\omega{(A)}=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}A)\right\|
, where
A\in B(H)
. Due to its importance, the numerical radius has been generalized several times, and their last was given by Abu-Omar and Kittaneh
[
2
]
in 2019, in which they generalized it on
B(H)
. This generalization is denoted by
\omega_{N}(\cdot)
, and is defined by
\omega_{N}(A)=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}N(Re(e^{i\theta}A))
. It was proved by the authors in
[
2
]
that
\omega_{N}(\cdot)
generalizes the numerical radius
\omega(\cdot)
if
N
is the usual operator norm.

In this paper, we are interested in studying the space of
n\times n
complex matrices,
M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
. We should note that
\omega_{N}(\cdot)
has the following two important properties, see
[
1
]
.

Property 1.1
.

The following properties hold:

a)

The norm
\omega_{N}(\cdot)
is self adjoint.

b)

If the norm
N(\cdot)
is weakly unitarily invariant, then so is
\omega_{N}(\cdot)
.

In
[
6
]
, Bhatia and Kittaneh where able to prove the following theorem that relates the Shatten p-norm of an
n\times n
block matrix by that of its block entries.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
T\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
such that
T=[T_{ij}]
,
1\leq i,j\leq n
and
1\leq p\leq\infty
then

n^{2-p}\left\|T\right\|_{p}^{p}\leq\overset{n}{\underset{i,j=1}{\sum}}\left\|T_{ij}\right\|_{p}^{p}\leq\left\|T\right\|_{p}^{p},

(1.5)

for
2\leq p\leq\infty
; and

\left\|T\right\|_{p}^{p}\leq\overset{n}{\underset{i,j=1}{\sum}}\left\|T_{ij}\right\|_{p}^{p}\leq n^{2-p}\left\|T\right\|_{p}^{p},

(1.6)

for
1\leq p\leq 2
.

Motivated by the results of Bhatia and Kittaneh in
[
6
]
, and those of Aldalabih and Kittaneh in
[
3
]
, we aim in this paper to prove some generalized numerical radius inequalities for partitioned general
2\times 2
block matrices considering the case when
N
is taken to be the Schatten p-norm. We denote this norm by
\omega_{p}(\cdot)
and call it the Schatten p-generalized numerical radius. We emphasize on finding such inequalities for the off-diagonal part of block matrices. We also provide an application of this norm in which we give a refinement of the triangle inequality for the Schatten p-generalized numerical radius. The following Lemma was proved by the authors in
[
1
]
, and will be used in our work.

Lemma 1.3
.

Let
A
,
B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&B\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)=2^{\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}\left(B\right)

for all p.

2.
General
2\times 2
Block Matrices Inequalities

In this section we give bounds for the generalized Schatten p-numerical radius of general
2\times 2
block matrices. We give emphasis for
2\times 2
block diagonal matrices. Most of the results in this section, generalize those presented in
[
3
]
.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
A,B

\in

M
{}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
then for all
p
, we have

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)=2^{\frac{1}{p}-1}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}\right\|_{p}.

Proof.

By equations (
1.1
), (
1.2
) and (
1.4
), we have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|\begin{bmatrix}0&e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}\\
e^{i\theta}B+e^{-i\theta}A^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|\begin{bmatrix}e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}&0\\
0&(e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast})^{\ast}\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|\begin{bmatrix}e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}&0\\
0&e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}2^{\frac{1}{p}}\left\|e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=2^{\frac{1}{p}-1}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B^{\ast}\right\|_{p}.

∎

Proposition 2.2
.

Let
A,B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then the following inequality holds for all
p

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}A&0\\
0&B\end{bmatrix}\right)\leq\left(\omega_{p}^{p}(A)+\omega_{p}^{p}(B)\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}A&0\\
0&B\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|Re\left(e^{i\theta}\begin{bmatrix}A&0\\
0&B\end{bmatrix}\right)\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|\begin{bmatrix}Re(e^{i\theta}A)&0\\
0&Re(e^{i\theta}B)\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left(\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}A)\right\|_{p}^{p}+\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}B)\right\|_{p}^{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by equation (\ref{eqn5}))}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\omega_{p}^{p}(A)+\omega_{p}^{p}(B)\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

as required.
∎

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
A=[A_{ij}]
be a
2\times 2
block matrix, then

\omega_{p}^{p}(A)\leq\frac{1}{2^{p-2}}\underset{i,j=1}{\overset{2}{\sum}}\omega_{p}^{p}(a_{ij})

(2.1)

for
2\leq p\leq\infty
, and

\omega_{p}^{p}(A)\leq\underset{i,j=1}{\overset{2}{\sum}}\omega_{p}^{p}(a_{ij})

(2.2)

for
1\leq p\leq 2
, where

a_{ij}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}A_{ij}\hskip 108.405pti=j\\
2^{-\frac{1}{p}}\begin{bmatrix}0&A_{ij}\\
A_{ji}&0\end{bmatrix}\hskip 36.135pti\neq j.\end{array}\right.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}A)\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\left\|\begin{bmatrix}Re(e^{i\theta}A_{11})&\frac{1}{2}(e^{i\theta}A_{12}+e^{-i\theta}A_{21}^{\ast})\\
\frac{1}{2}(e^{i\theta}A_{21}+e^{-i\theta}A_{12}^{\ast})&Re(e^{i\theta}A_{22})\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p},

then by inequality
(
1.5
) and Lemma
2.1
, we get

\displaystyle\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}A)\right\|_{p}^{p}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2^{p-2}}\overset{2}{\underset{i,j=1}{\sum}}\left\|(Re(e^{i\theta}A))_{ij}\right\|_{p}^{p}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2^{p-2}}\left(\underset{i=j}{\sum}\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}A_{ij})\right\|_{p}^{p}+\underset{i\neq j}{\sum}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left\|e^{i\theta}A_{ij}+e^{-i\theta}A_{ji}^{\ast}\right\|_{p}\right)^{p}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2^{p-2}}\left(\underset{i=j}{\sum}\omega_{p}^{p}(A_{ij})+\underset{i\neq j}{\sum}\left(2^{-\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A_{ij}\\
A_{ji}&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\right)^{p}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2^{p-2}}\underset{i=j}{\sum}\omega_{p}^{p}(a_{ij}).

Then

\displaystyle\omega_{p}^{p}(A)

\displaystyle=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\sup}\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}A)\right\|_{p}^{p}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2^{p-2}}\underset{i=j}{\sum}\omega_{p}^{p}(a_{ij})

for
2\leq p\leq\infty
. The second inequality is proved in a similar manner, using Lemma
2.1
, and inequality (
1.6
).
∎

3.

2\times 2

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
A,B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then

a)

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
e^{i\theta}B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

for all
\theta\in\mathbb{R}.

b)

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&B\\
A&0\end{bmatrix}\right).

for all
p
.

Proof.

Let
U=\begin{bmatrix}I&0\\
0&e^{i\frac{\theta}{2}}I\end{bmatrix}
, then
U
is unitary. Then by Property
1.1
we have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(U\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}U^{\ast}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(e^{-i\frac{\theta}{2}}\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
e^{i\theta}B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
e^{i\theta}B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

which ends the proof of (a). Now to prove the equality (b), consider

U=\begin{bmatrix}0&I\\
I&0\end{bmatrix}
, then
U
is unitary.

Then by Property
1.1
we have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(U\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}U^{\ast}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&B\\
A&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

which ends the proof.
∎

The next theorem gives upper and lower bounds for
\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)
in terms of
\omega_{p}(A+B)
and
\omega_{p}(A-B)

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
A,B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then

\frac{\max\left(\omega_{p}(A+B),\omega_{p}(A-B)\right)}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}\leq\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\leq\frac{\omega_{p}(A+B)+\omega_{p}(A-B)}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}

for all
p
.

Proof.

By Lemma
1.3
, we have

\displaystyle 2^{\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}(A+B)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A+B\\
A+B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&B\\
A&0\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&B\\
A&0\end{bmatrix}\right)+\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by triangle inequality).}

\displaystyle=2\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by Lemma \ref{abcd}),}

then

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\geq\frac{1}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}\omega_{p}(A+B),

(3.1)

replacing
B
by
-B
in inequality (
3.1
), we get

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
-B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\geq\frac{1}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}\omega_{p}(A-B),

taking
\theta=\pi
in Lemma
3.1
, we get

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
-B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}\omega_{p}(A-B),

(3.2)

therefore, by the estimations (
3.1
) and (
3.2
), we get

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\geq\frac{\max\left(\omega_{p}(A+B),\omega_{p}(A-B)\right)}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}.

Now for the second inequality, consider
U=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}I&-I\\
I&I\end{bmatrix}
,
where
I
is the
n\times n
identity matrix, then
U
is unitary, and thus by Property
1.1
, we get

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}\right)=

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(U\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B&0\end{bmatrix}U^{\ast}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}-(A+B)&A-B\\
-(A-B)&A+B\end{bmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}-(A+B)&0\\
0&A+B\end{bmatrix}\right)+\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A-B\\
-(A-B)&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\right)\hskip 10.0pt

(by triangle inequality)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(2^{\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}(A+B)+2^{\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}(A-B)\right)

\displaystyle\mbox{(by Proposition \ref{prop}, Lemma \ref{abcd} and Lemma \ref{lemma1.4})}.

as required.

.

Let

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
A,B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then

\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\

Consider
U=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}I&I\\
-I&I\end{bmatrix}
,

is the

U
is unitary, we have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\

\displaystyle\omega_{p}\left(U\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}A+B&A-B\\

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}A+B&A+B\\

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}A+B&A+B\\

(by triangle inequality)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(2\omega_{p}(A+B)+2^{\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}(2B)\right)\mbox{(by Remark \ref{rk2}, Lemma \ref{abcd} and Lemma \ref{lemma1.4})}

∎

4.
An Application

In this section we present an application, which is a refinement of the triangle inequality for the generalized Schatten p-numerical radius. The following remark is presented by ”Yamazaki” in
[
14
]

Remark 4.1
.

Let
T\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, we have

\omega_{p}(T)=\underset{\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}=1}{sup}\left\|\alpha Re(T)+\beta Im(T)\right\|_{p}.

for all
p
. Then

\left\|T+T^{\ast}\right\|_{p}\leq 2\omega_{p}(T).

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}(T)=

\displaystyle\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}T)\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|e^{i\theta}T+e^{-i\theta}T^{\ast}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|\cos(\theta)T+i\sin(\theta)T+\cos(\theta)T^{\ast}-i\sin(\theta)T^{\ast}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|\cos(\theta)Re(T)-\sin(\theta)Im(T)\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\underset{\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}=1}{sup}\left\|\alpha Re(T)+\beta Im(T)\right\|_{p}.

Take
\theta=2\pi
, then

\displaystyle\omega_{p}(T)

\displaystyle\geq\left\|\cos(2\pi)Re(T)-\sin(2\pi)Im(T)\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left\|T+T^{\ast}\right\|_{p}

so,
\left\|T+T^{\ast}\right\|_{p}\leq 2\omega_{p}(T)
.
∎

The next theorem is a refinement of the triangle inequality for the generalized Schatten p-numerical radius.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
A,B\in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})
, then

\left\|A+B\right\|_{p}\leq 2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\leq\left\|A\right\|_{p}+\left\|B\right\|_{p}.

for all
p
.

Proof.

Let
T=\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}
.
We have

\displaystyle\left\|\begin{bmatrix}0&A+B\\
A^{\ast}+B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}^{p}=

\displaystyle\left\|(A+B)\oplus(A^{\ast}+B^{\ast})\right\|_{p}^{p}\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by equation (\ref{eq2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|(A+B)\oplus(A+B)\right\|_{p}^{p}\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by equation (\ref{eq1}))}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\left\|A+B\right\|_{p}^{p}.\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by equation (\ref{eqn6}))}

(4.1)

Then,

\displaystyle 2\left\|A+B\right\|_{p}^{p}=

\displaystyle\left\|\begin{bmatrix}0&A+B\\
A^{\ast}+B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}^{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|T+T^{\ast}\right\|_{p}^{p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2^{p}\omega_{p}^{p}(T)\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by Remark \ref{rk3})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2^{p}\omega_{p}^{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right).

Thus,

\left\|A+B\right\|_{p}\leq 2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right).

For the second inequality, we have

\displaystyle\omega_{p}(T)=

\displaystyle\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|Re(e^{i\theta}T)\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|\begin{bmatrix}0&e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B\\
e^{-i\theta}A^{\ast}+e^{i\theta}B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{sup}\left\|e^{i\theta}A+e^{-i\theta}B\right\|_{p}\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by same argument as (\ref{eq}))}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}}\left(\left\|A\right\|_{p}+\left\|B\right\|_{p}\right).\hskip 30.0pt\mbox{(by triangle inequality)}

Therefore,

\left\|A+B\right\|_{p}\leq 2^{1-\frac{1}{p}}\omega_{p}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&A\\
B^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}\right)\leq\left\|A\right\|_{p}+\left\|B\right\|_{p}.

∎
