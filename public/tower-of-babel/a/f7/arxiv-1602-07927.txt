Nonstandard Deformed Oscillators from 𝒒 \boldsymbol{q} - and 𝒑 , 𝒒 \boldsymbol{p,q} -Deformations of Heisenberg Algebra
Alexandre M. GAVRILIK † and Ivan I. KACHURIK †‡

Abstract
For the two-parameter p , q p,q -deformed Heisenberg algebra introduced recently and in which, instead of usual commutator of X X and P P in the l.h.s. of basic relation [ X , P ] = i ​ ℏ [X,P]={\rm i}\hbar , one uses the p , q p,q -commutator, we established interesting properties. Most important is the realizability of the p , q p,q -deformed Heisenberg algebra by means of the appropriate deformed oscillator algebra. Another uncovered property is special extension of the usual mutual Hermitian conjugation of the creation and annihilation operators, namely the so-called η ⁡ ( N ) \eta(N) -pseudo-Hermitian conjugation rule, along with the related η ⁡ ( N ) \eta(N) -pseudo-Hermiticity property of the position or momentum operators. In this work, we present some new solutions of the realization problem yielding new (nonstandard) deformed oscillators, and show their inequivalence to the earlier known solution and the respective deformed oscillator algebra, in particular what concerns ground state energy.

Physically, deformations of the Heisenberg algebra and the

What concerns the particular existing variants of deformed Heisenberg algebra (DHA),

We mean the option that the creation and annihilation

Accordingly, the position operator

The latter fact was uncovered in the framework of

That was found in closed form in

However, further analysis shows that this solution given by the DSF

-deformed Heisenberg algebra

\displaystyle XP-qPX={\rm i}\hbar.

(2.1)

Below, in the first subsection we mainly follow

We require that the equality (

From DHA to DOA

Like in

\displaystyle X\equiv f(N)a^{-}+g(N)a^{+},\qquad P\equiv{\rm i}\big(k(N)a^{+}-h(N)a^{-}\big)

\displaystyle H(N)=f(N)k(N+1)+qh(N)g(N+1),

\displaystyle G(N)=g(N)h(N-1)+qk(N)f(N-1).

The DSF

\displaystyle N|n\rangle=n|n\rangle,\qquad|n\rangle=\frac{(a^{+})^{n}}{\sqrt{\Phi(n)!}}|0\rangle,\qquad a^{-}|0\rangle=0,

\displaystyle a^{+}|n\rangle=\sqrt{\Phi(n+1)}|n+1\rangle,\qquad a^{-}|n\rangle=\sqrt{\Phi(n)}|n-1\rangle.

The DSF is obtainable from the above functions

\displaystyle{\Phi(n)}=\frac{G(n-1)!}{H(n-1)!}\left(\frac{1}{H(0)}+\sum^{n-1}_{j=1}\frac{H(j-1)!}{G(j)!}\right).
