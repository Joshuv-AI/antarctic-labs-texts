Nowhere vanishing holomorphic one-forms on varieties of Kodaira codimension one
Feng Hao

Abstract
. Based on the celebrated result on zeros of holomorphic 1-forms on complex varieties of general type by Popa and Schnell, we study holomorphic 1-forms on n n -dimensional varieties of Kodaira dimension n − 1 n-1 . We show that a complex minimal smooth projective variety X X of Kodaira dimension κ ⁡ ( X ) = dim X − 1 \kappa(X)=\dim X-1 admits a holomorphic 1-form without zero if and only if there is a smooth morphism from X X to an elliptic curve. Furthermore, for a general smooth projective variety (not necessarily minimal) X X of Kodaira codimension one, we give a structure theorem for X X given that X X admits a holomorphic 1-form without zero.

This indicates that the existence of nowhere vanishing holomorphic 1-forms encodes much algebro-geometric information of irregular smooth complex projective varieties. 2.2.
Quasi-étale morphism

A finite morphism
f:X^{\prime}\to X
between normal varieties is called quasi-étale if it is étale in codimension one, see e.g.
[
GKP16
]
; if
X
is smooth, then any quasi-étale morphism
f\colon X^{\prime}\to X
is étale.
In particular, quasi-étale morphism
f
is ramified at most at singular points of
X
.

2.3.
\mathbb{Q}
-factorialization

Let
X
be a terminal minimal variety with canonical divisor
K_{X}
a
\mathbb{Q}
-cartier divisor. By
[
BCHM10
, Corollary 1.4.3]
, there is a
\mathbb{Q}
-factorialization (not necessarily unique)
\sigma:X^{\prime}\to X
, i.e. a proper birational morphism which is an isomorphism in codimension one such that
X^{\prime}
is
\mathbb{Q}
-factorial, terminal and
K_{X^{\prime}}
is nef.

2.4.
Popa-Schnell’s result on holomorphic 1-forms

We recall the following celebrated theorem by Popa and Schnell
[
PS14
, Theorem 2.1]
, which helps us to reduce our arguments for main theorems to cases of generically isotrivial Iitaka fibrations.

Theorem 2.1

(Popa-Schnell)
.

Let
X
be a smooth projective variety, and
f\colon X\to A

be a morphism to an abelian variety. If
H^{0}(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(mK_{X}-f^{*}L))\neq 0
for some integer
m\geq 1

and some ample divisor
L
on
A
, then
Z(\omega)
is nonempty for every
\omega
in the image of the map
f^{*}\colon H^{0}(A,\Omega_{A}^{1})\to H^{0}(X,\Omega_{X}^{1})
.
