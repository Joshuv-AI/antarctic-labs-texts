A Second-Order Nonlocal Approximation to Manifold Poisson Models with Neumann Boundary Thanks: This work was supported by NSFC grant 12071244, 11671005.
Yajie Zhang, Yanzun Meng, Zuoqiang Shi

Abstract
. In this paper, we propose a class of nonlocal models to approximate the Poisson model on manifolds with homogeneous Neumann boundary condition, where the manifolds are assumed to be embedded in high dimensional Euclid spaces. In comparison to the existing nonlocal approximation of Poisson models with Neumann boundary, we optimize the truncation error of model by adding an augmented function involving the second order normal derivative along the 2 ​ δ 2\delta layer of boundary, with 2 ​ δ 2\delta be the nonlocal interaction horizon. The 2nd normal derivative is expressed as the difference between the interior Laplacian and the boundary Laplacian. The concentration of our paper is on the construction of nonlocal model, the well-posedness of model, and its second-order convergence rate to its local counterpart. The localization rate of our nonlocal model is currently optimal among all related works even for the case of high dimensional Euclid spaces.

2.
Construction of Nonlocal Model

In this work, our purpose is to find a second-order nonlocal approximation to the following Poisson problem:

\begin{cases}-\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}u({x})+\lambda({x})u({x})=f({x})&{x}\in\mathcal{M},\\
\frac{\partial u}{\partial\bm{n}}({x})=0&{x}\in\partial\mathcal{M},\end{cases}

(2.1)

here
\mathcal{M}
is an open, bounded, smooth
m

\mathbb{R}^{d}
, with
\partial\mathcal{M}
be a smooth
(m-1)
dimensional submanifold.
\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}
is the Laplace-Beltrami operator on
\mathcal{M}
, and the function
f\in L^{2}(\mathcal{M})
,
\lambda\in C^{2}(\mathcal{M})
with
\lambda_{1}\geq\lambda({x})\geq\lambda_{0}
for some constant
\lambda_{1}>\lambda_{0}>0
and all
{x}\in\mathcal{M}
.

{rmrk}

(
2.1

-\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}u=f
in our previous work
[
38
]
added by
\lambda u
. In comparision to the previous model, we should remove the restrictions
\int_{\mathcal{M}}u({x})d\mu_{{x}}=0

\int_{\mathcal{M}}f({x})d\mu_{{x}}=0

[
38
]
. (
2.1

, and when the regularity of

This work can be extended into the non-homogeneous Neumann boundary case where
\frac{\partial u}{\partial\bm{n}}=h\in L^{2}(\partial\mathcal{M})

To start the construction of a nonlocal model, we will first introduce the interaction kernel function
R_{\delta}({x},{y})=C_{\delta}R\big(\frac{|{x}-{y}|^{2}}{4\delta^{2}}\big)
that appeared in (
1.2
). A better choice of

will result in much less work in the error analysis. To this end, we make the following assumptions on

:

(1)

Smoothness:
R\in W^{2,+\infty}[0,+\infty)
, i.e., for any
r\geq 0

\big|D^{2}R(r)\big|\leq C
;

(2)

Nonnegativity:
R(r)>0
for any
r\geq 0
;

(3)

Compact support:
R(r)=0
for any
r>1
;

(4)

Nondegenearcy:
\exists\ \delta_{0}>0
so that
R(r)\geq\delta_{0}>0
for
0\leq r\leq 1/2
.

These assumptions are very mild, almost all the smooth functions in the literature satisfy these conditions. The first condition makes
R
not blow up near the origin, while the fourth condition assures a non-degenerate interval for
R
. Despite that the removal of third condition will make the problem more interesting, we keep this condition to avoid complication in the error analysis.
A frequently used kernel function
R
is the following:

R(r)=\begin{cases}\frac{1}{2}(1+\cos\pi r),&0\leq r\leq 1,\\
0,&r>1.\end{cases}

(2.2)

Based on

{R}(r)=\int_{r}^{+\infty}\underline{R}(s)ds
,
\bar{R}(r)=\int_{r}^{+\infty}R(s)ds
,
\overset{=}{R}(r)=\int_{r}^{+\infty}\bar{R}(s)ds
and their corresponding kernel functions

\underline{R}_{\delta}({x},{y})=C_{\delta}\underline{R}\big(\frac{|{x}-{y}|^{2}}{4\delta^{2}}\big),\ \bar{R}_{\delta}({x},{y})=C_{\delta}\bar{R}\big(\frac{|{x}-{y}|^{2}}{4\delta^{2}}\big),\ \overset{=}{R}_{\delta}({x},{y})=C_{\delta}\overset{=}{R}\big(\frac{|{x}-{y}|^{2}}{4\delta^{2}}\big).

(2.3)

It is clear that
\underline{R}

\delta_{0}

where the error function

For the term

5.
Conclusion and Future Work

In this paper, we propose a nonlocal approximation of the manifold Poisson model with Neumann boundary. The well-posedness and the second order localization rate is analyzed.
The application of our nonlocal model is that, such model can be easily discretized and be solved numerically by meshless scheme like point integral method(PIM, see
[
28
]

[
39
]
), while this solution can be regarded as the numerical solution of the local Poisson model as well. Compared to the traditional manifold finite element method(MFEM), one advantage of PIM is that only local mesh is required so that we do not need a global mesh like MFEM.
In our subsequent work, we will implement several numerical examples to illustrate the rate of convergence on the other side. Besides, we will try to seek for accurate nonlocal approximations of some other PDEs, for instance, the elliptic transmission problems, the Stokes problems, the Diffusion-Reaction problems, and the Poisson problems with polygonal domains.
