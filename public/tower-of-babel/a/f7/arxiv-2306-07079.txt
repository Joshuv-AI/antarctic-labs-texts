Braids act on configurations of lines
Vassily Olegovich Manturov

Abstract
Similar pictures appear in various branches of mathematics. Sometimes this similarity gives rise to deep theorems. Mentioning such a similarity between hexagonal tilings, cubes in 3-space, configurations of lines and braid groups, we prove that braids act on configurations of lines.
