A Criterion for Categories on which every Grothendieck Topology is Rigid
Jérémie Marquès

Abstract
Let 𝖢 \mathsf{C} be a small Cauchy-complete category. The subtoposes of [ 𝖢 op , 𝖲𝖾𝗍 ] [\mathsf{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{Set}] are sometimes all of the form [ 𝖣 op , 𝖲𝖾𝗍 ] [\mathsf{D}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{Set}] where 𝖣 \mathsf{D} is a full subcategory of 𝖢 \mathsf{C} . This is the case for instance when 𝖢 \mathsf{C} is finite, an Artinian poset, or the simplex category. In order to unify these situations, we characterize the small categories 𝖢 \mathsf{C} such that for every X ​ ∈ ​ 𝖢 X∈\mathsf{C} , every subtopos of [ 𝖢 / X op , 𝖲𝖾𝗍 ] [\mathsf{C}_{/X}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathsf{Set}] is induced by a subcategory of 𝖢 / X \mathsf{C}_{/X} . We provide two equivalent characterizations. The first one uses a two-player game, and the second one combines two “local” properties of 𝖢 \mathsf{C} involving respectively the poset reflections of its slices and its endomorphism monoids.
