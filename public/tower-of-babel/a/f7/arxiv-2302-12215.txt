A coloring of the plane without monochromatic right triangles
Balázs Bursics, Péter Komjáth

Abstract
We give a full, correct proof of the following result, earlier claimed in [ 1 ] . If the Continuum Hypothesis holds then there is a coloring of the plane with countably many colors, with no monocolored right triangle.

Notation. Definitions.

We use the notation and terminology of axiomatic set theory.
The ordinals are von Neumann ordinals,
\omega_{1}

Theorem.

(CH)
There is
f:\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\omega
with no monochromatic right angles.

Proof.
Using CH, we decompose
\mathbb{R}^{2}
and the set
{\cal E}
of planar lines and circles
into the increasing, continuous sequence of sets as

\mathbb{R}^{2}=\bigcup\{H_{\alpha}:\alpha<\omega_{1}\}
and
{\cal E}=\bigcup\{{\cal E}_{\alpha}:\alpha<\omega_{1}\}
such
that
H_{0}={\cal E}_{0}=\emptyset
and

(1) if
x\neq y\in H_{\alpha}
, then their connecting line and Thales circle
are in
{\cal E}_{\alpha}
,

(2) if
x,y,z\in H_{\alpha}
are not collinear, then their circuit is in
{\cal E}_{\alpha}
,

(3) if
e_{0}\neq e_{1}\in{\cal E}_{\alpha}
, then
e_{0}\cap e_{1}\subseteq H_{\alpha}
,

(4) if
x\in C\in{\cal E}_{\alpha}
,
C
is a circle, then the antipodal point of
x

in
C
is in
H_{\alpha}
,

(5) if
L\in{\cal E}_{\alpha}
is a line,
x\in H_{\alpha}\cap L
, then the line
L^{\prime}

perpendicular to
L
with
x\in L^{\prime}
also contained in
{\cal E}_{\alpha}
.

This can be done by the usual Skolem-type closing arguments.

We are going to construct the coloring
f:\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\omega
and the function

\varphi:{\cal E}\to[\omega]^{\omega}
by transfinite recursion on
\alpha<\omega_{1}
for

H_{\alpha+1}-H_{\alpha}
and for
{\cal E}_{\alpha+1}-{\cal E}_{\alpha}
, satisfying the following:

(6)
f|(H_{\alpha+1}-H_{\alpha})
is injective,

(7) if
x\in H_{\alpha+1}-H_{\alpha}
,
e\in{\cal E}_{\alpha}
,
x\in e
, then
f(x)\in\varphi(e)
,

(8) if
C\in{\cal E}
is a circle,
i\in\varphi(C)
,
x,y\in C
,
f(x)=f(y)=i
,
then
x,y
are not antipodal,

(9) if
C\in{\cal E}
is a circle,
i\notin\varphi(C)
, then

|f^{-1}(i)\cap C|\leq 2
,

(10) if
L\in{\cal E}
is a line,
i\notin\varphi(L)
, then

|f^{-1}(i)\cap L|\leq 1
,

(11) if
L,L^{\prime}
are perpendicular lines,
\{x\}=L\cap L^{\prime}
, then

f(x)\notin\varphi(L)\cap\varphi(L^{\prime})
.

Claim 1.

There is no right triangle monocolored by
f
.

Proof.
Assume that
x,y,z
form a right triangle with the right angle at

i=f(x)=f(y)=f(z)
.
Let
C
be the circle around
x,y,z

If
i\notin\varphi(C)
, then we obtain a contradiction with (9).
If
i\in\varphi(C)
, then we get a contradiction with (8), as
x,z
are
antipodal.

By (3).

Assume that

\varphi|({\cal E}_{\alpha+1}-{\cal E}_{\alpha})

Clearly (6) is satisfied, and also

Next we define
\varphi

{\cal E}_{\alpha+1}-{\cal E}_{\alpha}

If

Finally, we define
\varphi

Let
