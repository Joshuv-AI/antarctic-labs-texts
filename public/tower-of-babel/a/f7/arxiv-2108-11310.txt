On the matrix version of new extended Gauss, Appell and Lauricella hypergeometric functions
Ashish Verma, Ravi Dwivedi

Abstract
Inspired by certain interesting recent extension of the gamma, beta and hypergeometric matrix functions, we introduce here new extension of the gamma and beta matrix function. We also introduce new extensions of the Gauss hypergeometric matrix function, confluent hypergeometric matrix function, Appell matrix function and Lauricella matrix function of three variables in terms of the new extended beta matrix function. Then we investigate certain properties of these extended matrix functions such as the integral representations, differential formulae and recurrence relations. Keywords : Matrix functional calculus, Gauss hypergeometric function, Appell functions, Lauricella hypergeometric functions. AMS Subject Classification : 15A15; 33C65.

The new extended gamma and beta matrix functions

In this section, we consider the generalization of gamma and beta matrix functions. For positive stable matrices
A
,
B
,
\mathbb{X}
and
\mathbb{Y}
, we define the new extension of gamma matrix function as

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X})=\int_{0}^{\infty}{}_{1}F_{1}(A;B;-tI-\mathbb{Y}t^{-1})\,t^{\mathbb{X}-I}.

(3.1)

Let
A
,
B
,
\mathbb{X}
,
\mathbb{Y}
and
\mathbb{Z}
be positive stable matrices in
\mathbb{C}^{r\times r}
. Then, we introduce the new extension of beta matrix function, denoted by
\mathfrak{B}_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z})

\displaystyle\mathfrak{B}_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z})=\int_{0}^{1}{}_{1}F_{1}\left(A;B;-\frac{\mathbb{Y}}{t(1-t)}\right)\,t^{\mathbb{X}-I}(1-t)^{\mathbb{Z}-I}dt.

(3.2)

From Equations (
3.1
) and (
3.2
), the particular case is given by
\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,A)}(\mathbb{X})=\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}}(\mathbb{X})
,
\Gamma_{\bf{0}}^{(A,A)}(\mathbb{X})=\Gamma(\mathbb{X})
,
\mathfrak{B}_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,A)}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z})=\mathfrak{B}_{\mathbb{Y}}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z})
and
\mathfrak{B}_{\bf{0}}^{(A,A)}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z})=\mathfrak{B}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z})
.

We now give some results related to new extended gamma and beta matrix functions.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
A
,
B
,
A-B
,
\mathbb{X}
and
\mathbb{Y}
be positive stable matrices in

\mathbb{X}
and
\mathbb{Y}
are commuting. Then the new extended gamma matrix function can also be presented in the integral form as

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mathbb{\mathbb{Y}}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X})=\ \Gamma(B)\Gamma^{-1}(A)\Gamma^{-1}(B-A)\int_{0}^{1}\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}{\mu}^{2}}(\mathbb{X})\ \mu^{A-\mathbb{X}-I}\ (1-\mu)^{B-A-I}d\mu.

(3.3)

Proof.

Using the integral representation of confluent hypergeometric matrix function
[
7
]
, we have

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X})=\Gamma(B)\Gamma^{-1}(A)\Gamma^{-1}(B-A)\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{1}u^{\mathbb{X}-I}e^{-ut-\frac{\mathbb{Y}t}{u}}\ t^{A-I}\ (1-t)^{B-A-I}dtdu.

(3.4)

Consider the following transformation
v=ut

\mu=t
and the Jacobian of the transformation
J=\frac{1}{\mu}
, we have

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X})=\Gamma(B)\Gamma^{-1}(A)\Gamma^{-1}(B-A)\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{1}v^{\mathbb{X}-I}e^{-v-\frac{\mathbb{Y}\mu^{2}}{v}}\,dv\ \mu^{A-\mathbb{X}-I}\ (1-\mu)^{B-A-I}d\mu.

(3.5)

The convergence of matrix functions permit us to interchange the order of integration, Equation (

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X})

\displaystyle=\Gamma(B)\Gamma^{-1}(A)\Gamma^{-1}(B-A)\int_{0}^{1}\left[\int_{0}^{\infty}v^{\mathbb{X}-I}e^{-v-\frac{\mathbb{Y}\mu^{2}}{v}}\,dv\right]\ \mu^{A-\mathbb{X}-I}\ (1-\mu)^{B-A-I}d\mu

\displaystyle=\Gamma(B)\Gamma^{-1}(A)\Gamma^{-1}(B-A)\int_{0}^{1}\Gamma_{\mathbb{Y}{\mu}^{2}}(\mathbb{X})\ \mu^{A-\mathbb{X}-I}\ (1-\mu)^{B-A-I}d\mu.

(3.6)

It completes the proof of (
3.3
).
∎

Theorem 3.3
.

For the positive stable matrices

,

Proof.

From the definition of new extended beta matrix function, we have

\displaystyle\mathfrak{B}_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X}+I,\mathbb{Z})+\mathfrak{B}_{\mathbb{Y}}^{(A,B)}(\mathbb{X},\mathbb{Z}+I)

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{1}{}_{1}F_{1}\left(A;B;-\frac{\mathbb{Y}}{t(1-t)}\right)[t^{\mathbb{X}}(1-t)^{\mathbb{Z}-I}+t^{\mathbb{X}-I}(1-t)^{\mathbb{Z}}]dt

∎

New extended Gauss hypergeometric and confluent hypergeometric matrix functions
