CDF W W boson mass and muon g − 2 g-2 in type-X two-Higgs-doublet model with a Higgs-phobic light pseudoscalar Preprint: APCTP Pre2022 - 006
Jinheung Kim, Soojin Lee, Prasenjit Sanyal, Jeonghyeon Song

Abstract
The recent measurement of the W W boson mass by the CDF collaboration adds an anomaly to the long-standing discrepancy in the muon anomalous magnetic moment, Δ ​ a μ \Delta a_{\mu} . Although type-X in the two-Higgs-doublet model provides an attractive solution to Δ ​ a μ \Delta a_{\mu} through a light pseudoscalar A A , the model confronts the exotic Higgs decays of h → A ​ A h\to AA and the lepton flavor universality data in the τ \tau and Z Z decays. To save the model, we propose that the light pseudoscalar be Higgs-phobic. Through the random scanning over the entire parameter space, we perform a comparative study of the Higgs-phobic type-X with and without the CDF m W m_{W} measurement, called the CDF and PDG cases respectively. Both cases can explain the two anomalies as well as all the other constraints, but have significant differences in the finally allowed parameter space. For example, a small region with almost degenerate masses of new Higgs bosons around 100 GeV is allowed only in the PDG case. The cutoff scale of the model is also studied via the analysis of renormalization group equations, which reaches up to 10 5 ​ GeV 10^{5}~{\rm GeV} ( 10 7 ​ GeV 10^{7}~{\rm GeV} ) in the CDF (PDG) case. Since the dominant decay modes are A → τ ​ τ A\to\tau\tau , H → Z ​ A H\to ZA , and H ± → W ± ​ A H^{\pm}\to W^{\pm}A in most of the viable parameter space, we propose the 4 ​ τ + V ​ V ′ 4\tau+VV^{\prime} states as the golden discovery channel at the LHC.

VI
Conclusions

The recent measurement of the
W
boson mass by the CDF collaboration
requests new physics beyond the SM: the Peskin-Takeuchi parameters
significantly deviate from the SM expectation like

S_{\rm CDF}=0.15\pm 0.08
and
T_{\rm CDF}=0.27\pm 0.06
with
U=0
.
Another anomaly from the muon anomalous magnetic moment has been around for some time.
Type-X in the 2HDM is one of the most attractive solutions for the muon
g-2
via a light pseudoscalar boson.
Since the ordinary type-X suffers from
h\to AA
and the lepton flavor universality data in the
\tau
and
Z
decays,
we have proposed the Higgs-phobic pseudoscalar in type-X.

Through random scanning of the model parameters,
we impose the theoretical and experimental constraints step by step:
step I is for the muon
g-2
and theoretical stabilities;
step II is for the oblique parameters before and after the CDF
m_{W}
measurement;
step III applies the Higgs precision data and the direct search bounds at high energy colliders;
step IV includes the global
\chi^{2}
fit to
\Delta a_{\mu}
and the LFU data.
The most important consequence is that the Higgs-phobic type-X can explain
not only
m_{W}^{\rm CDF}
and
\Delta a_{\mu}
anomalies
but also all the other constraints, including the LFU observables.

Our main results are summarized as follows:

1.

The muon
g-2
anomaly requires light
M_{A}
and large
t_{\beta}
.

2.

The PDG and CDF cases share some common features:

•

The theoretical constraints and the electroweak oblique parameters put
the upper bounds on
M_{H,H^{\pm}}\lesssim 600{\;{\rm GeV}}
.

•

The LFU data plays the essential role in the curtailment of the parameter space,
eliminating most of the region with
M_{A}\gtrsim 38{\;{\rm GeV}}
and
t_{\beta}\gtrsim 70
.

3.

There exist meaningful differences between the PDG and CDF cases:

•

Only in the PDG case,
a small region where
M_{A}\simeq M_{H^{\pm}}\simeq M_{H}\simeq 100{\;{\rm GeV}}

survives to the last step, called the PDG-island.

•

The PDG-island accommodates both the right-sign and wrong-sign tau lepton Yukawa coupling,
while outside the PDG-island only the wrong-sign
\tau
Yukawa coupling is allowed.

•

The lower bound on
M_{H^{\pm}}
for
M_{A}\lesssim 38{\;{\rm GeV}}
is different,
M_{H^{\pm}}\gtrsim 250{\;{\rm GeV}}
in the PDG case
but
M_{H^{\pm}}\gtrsim 300{\;{\rm GeV}}
in the CDF case.

•

The cutoff scale in the PDG case can go higher than in the CDF case,
the former up to
10^{7}{\;{\rm GeV}}
and the latter to
10^{5}{\;{\rm GeV}}
.

4.

We propose the
4\tau
states associated with
ZW
or
WW
as the golden discovery modes at the LHC for the CDF case,
because of the background-free environment.
