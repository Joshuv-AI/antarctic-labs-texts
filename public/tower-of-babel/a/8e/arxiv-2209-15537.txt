Conclusions

We have studied whether the discrepancy between isospin-rotated
\sigma(e^{+}e^{-}\to\eta\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
and
d\Gamma(\tau^{-}\to\eta\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\nu_{\tau})/dm_{\eta\pi\pi}
data can be explained by heavy new physics beyond the SM. Within an effective field theory approach for the NSI (assuming left-handed neutrinos), and using the bounds obtained previously on the corresponding new physics couplings, we have shown that it is impossible to explain this tension between
e^{+}e^{-}
and
\tau
data by heavy new physics. Future measurement of the
\tau
decay channel at Belle-II will shed light on the origin of this controversy. As a by-product of our analysis, we have developed the formalism needed to study NSI in three-meson tau decays (see ancillary file), which can be useful for other decay channels where hadronization is more complicated.
