Study of the neutrino-oxygen cross sections of the charged-current reaction 16 O( ν ¯ e , e + \bar{\nu}_{e},e^{+} ) 16 N(0 MeV, 2 − 2^{-} ) and the neutral-current reaction 16 O( ν , ν ′ \nu,\nu^{\prime} ) 16 O(12.97/12.53 MeV, 2 − 2^{-} ), producing high-energy γ \gamma rays
Makoto SAKUDA, Toshio SUZUKI, Ken’ichiro NAKAZATO, Hideyuki SUZUKI

Abstract
In the previous work, we discussed the cross section and the detection of 4.4-MeV γ \gamma rays produced in the neutrino neutral-current (NC) reaction 16 O( ν , ν ′ \nu,\nu^{\prime} ) 16 O(12.97 MeV and 12.53 MeV, 2 − 2^{-} ) in a water Cherenkov detector at the low energy below 100 MeV. In this report, we further investigated both the charged-current (CC) reaction 16 O( ν ¯ e , e + \bar{\nu}_{e},e^{+} ) 16 N(0 MeV, 2 − 2^{-} ) and the NC reaction 16 O( ν , ν ′ \nu,\nu^{\prime} ) 16 O(12.97 MeV and 12.53 MeV, 2 − 2^{-} ), producing high-energy γ \gamma rays, in which the more solid identification of the reactions can be applied via the coincidence method.

F(E_{\nu})=\frac{1}{4\pi d_{\rm SN}^{2}}\frac{E_{\nu}^{\rm tot}}{\langle E_{\nu}\rangle}f(E_{\nu}).

(4)

Summary

In the previous work, we discussed the detection of a single 4.4-MeV

16

\nu,\nu^{\prime}
)

In this report, we have further examined both the

16

)
16

[
55
]
in the Earth.

Even though the cross sections of these CC/NC reactions are small, the application of the present work for the future SN neutrino events by water Cherenkov detectors has some unique features. Above all, the coincidence method can be applied for the more solid identification in the former CC reaction. In the latter NC reaction, the large visible energy of 12.97 MeV and 12.53 MeV can be produced and the coincidence method can be also used if the multiple-ring reconstruction can be developed
[
54
]
. Further, it is important for the study of SN physics to have the detection channels which have significant sensitivities at as low neutrino energy as 10 to 20 MeV, where the majority of the neutrino energy spectra from SN bursts lie. These neutrino-oxygen reactions have the lowest energy thresholds (11.44 MeV and 12.97 MeV) among the neutrino-oxygen reactions, which correspond to the energy levels of the
T
=1 ground states of
16

16

T

We also note that the delayed coincidence method to this reaction can be applied in the Hyper-K detector, even without the neutron tagging method using Gd. The recent study by the Hyper-K Collaboration on the detection of the SN neutrino events

Our study for the coincidence method including the high energy
