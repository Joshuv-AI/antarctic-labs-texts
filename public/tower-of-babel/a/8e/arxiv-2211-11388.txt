Hunting light Higgses at the LHC in the context of the 2HDM Type-I
C. H. Shepherd-Themistocleous

Abstract
We show the reinterpretation of existing searches for exotic decays of the Standard Model (SM)-like Higgs, H → a ​ a ​ ( h ​ h ) H\to aa(hh) , in various final states, in the framework of the 2-Higgs Doublet Model (2HDM) Type-I. We then explore a new search for such light Higgses, a a and h h , at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) Run 3 for an integrated luminosity of 300 fb − 1 \text{fb}^{-1} . After performing a scan over the model parameters, we found that the inverted scenario of Type-I offers a new promising signal in the form of the following cascade decays: H → Z ∗ ​ a → Z ∗ ​ Z ∗ ​ h → b ​ b ¯ ​ μ − ​ μ + ​ j ​ j H\to Z^{*}a\to Z^{*}Z^{*}h\to b\overline{b}\mu^{-}\mu^{+}jj . We investigate then its significance through a full Monte Carlo (MC) simulation down to the detector level.

5
Conclusion

Within the framework of the 2HDM Type-I, we have reviewed the search for exotic Higgs decays,
H\to aa(hh)
, in different final states, performed at Run 2 with an integrated luminosity of
35.9~\text{fb}^{-1}
to draw the actual sensitivity of these experiments to the Type-I parameter space. In doing so, we have found that the accessible parameter space is already excluded by previous experimental searches. We have thus suggested an alternative signature to search for light Higgses,
pp\to H\to Z^{*}a\to Z^{*}Z^{*}h\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}jjb\overline{b}
, offering potential discovery at the LHC.
