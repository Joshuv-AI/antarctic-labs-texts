Type of local von Neumann algebras in abelian quantum double model
Yoshiko Ogata

Abstract
We show that the local von Neumann algebras on convex areas of the frustration-free ground state of abelian quantum double models are of type I ​ I ∞ II_{\infty} .

The quantum double models were introduced by Kitaev in

]

They have substantial importance as the examples of topological phases.
An astonishing property of these models is the existence of quasi-particles, called anyons.
Such anyons can be mathematically formulated as superselection sectors

[

[

q\neq 0
.

Lemma 6.2
.

Let
\omega
be a state on
q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q

such that

\displaystyle\omega(x):=\left\langle\Omega_{0},x\Omega_{0}\right\rangle,\quad x\in q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q.

(133)

Then
\omega
is a faithful tracial state on
q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q
.

We first show for any
A,B\in{\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma},\rm{loc}}:=\cup_{M\in{\mathbb{N}}}{\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}_{M},\rm{loc}}

that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\langle\Omega_{0},q\pi_{0}(A)q\pi_{0}(B)q\Omega_{0}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Omega_{0},q\pi_{0}(B)q\pi_{0}(A)q\Omega_{0}\right\rangle.\end{split}

(134)

From Lemma
6.1
, for any
A,B\in{\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma},\rm{loc}}
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left\langle\Omega_{0},\pi_{0}\left(A\right)\pi_{0}\left({Q_{N}}\right)\pi_{0}\left(B\right)\Omega_{0}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Omega_{0},\pi_{0}\left({Q_{N}}A{Q_{N}}B{Q_{N}}\right)\Omega_{0}\right\rangle\\
&=\varphi_{0}\left(Q_{N}AQ_{N}BQ_{N}\right)=\varphi_{0}\left(Q_{N}BQ_{N}AQ_{N}\right)\\
&=\left\langle\Omega_{0},\pi_{0}\left({Q_{N}}B{Q_{N}}A{Q_{N}}\right)\Omega_{0}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Omega_{0},\pi_{0}\left(B\right)\pi_{0}\left({Q_{N}}\right)\pi_{0}\left(A\right)\Omega_{0}\right\rangle\end{split}

(135)

for
N
large enough.
Taking
N\to\infty
limit, we obtain

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\langle\Omega_{0},\pi_{0}\left(A\right)q\pi_{0}\left(B\right)\Omega_{0}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Omega_{0},\pi_{0}\left(B\right)q\pi_{0}\left(A\right)\Omega_{0}\right\rangle,\end{split}

(136)

proving (
134
).
From (
134
), we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\omega\left(qxqyq\right)=\left\langle\Omega_{0},qxqyq\Omega_{0}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Omega_{0},qyqxq\Omega_{0}\right\rangle=\omega\left(qyqxq\right),\end{split}

(137)

for any
x,y\in\pi_{0}\left({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}}\right)^{\prime\prime}
.
Hence
\omega
is a tracial state on
q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q
.

In order to show that
\omega
is faithful, suppose that

x\in q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q

satisfies
\omega(x^{*}x)=0
.
Then for any
y\in q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|xy\Omega_{0}\right\|^{2}=\omega\left(y^{*}x^{*}xy\right)=\omega\left(x^{*}xyy^{*}\right)=\omega\left(xyy^{*}x^{*}\right)\leq\left\|y\right\|^{2}\omega\left(xx^{*}\right)=\left\|y\right\|^{2}\omega\left(x^{*}x\right)=0.\end{split}

(138)

This means

\displaystyle xy\Omega_{0}=0,\quad\text{for all}\quad y\in q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q.

(139)

Furthermore, because

\displaystyle x\in q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q\subset\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}\subset\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\setminus\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime},\quad q\in\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}\subset\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\setminus\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime},

(140)

we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&x\pi_{0}\left(A\right)\pi_{0}\left(B\right)\Omega_{0}=\pi_{0}\left(B\right)x\pi_{0}\left(A\right)\Omega_{0}=\pi_{0}\left(B\right)xq\pi_{0}\left(A\right)q\Omega_{0}=0,\\
&A\in{\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}},\quad B\in{\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\setminus\hat{\Gamma}}.\end{split}

(141)

Hence we obtain
x=0
, proving the faithfulness.

\square

Lemma 6.3
.

Let
\Gamma
be a convex cone in
{\mathbb{R}}^{2}
with apex
\bm{a}_{\Gamma}\in[0,1]\times[0,1]

given by

L_{1}^{\Gamma}:=\bm{a}_{\Gamma}+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\bm{e}_{\theta_{1}}
,

L_{2}^{\Gamma}:=\bm{a}_{\Gamma}+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\bm{e}_{\theta_{2}}
,
with
0\leq\theta_{1}<\frac{\pi}{2}
,
0<\theta_{2}-\theta_{1}<\pi
.
Then

\pi_{0}\left({\mathcal{B}}_{\widetilde{\left(\Gamma\cap{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\right)}}\right)^{\prime\prime}

is not type
III
.

Let
q
be the projection in
\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}
given above.
Suppose
p\in\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}
is a projection
such that
p\sim q
in
\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}

and
p\leq q
. We have
p=v^{*}v
,
q=vv^{*}
for some
v\in\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}
.
Because
p\leq q
,
v=qvq\in q\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}q
.
We then have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\omega\left(q\right)=\omega(vv^{*})=\omega(v^{*}v)=\omega(p)\end{split}

(142)

and for
q-p\geq 0
we have

\omega(q-p)=0
.
By the faithfulness of
\omega
, we have
p=q
.
Hence
q
is finite in
\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}
.
Because
\pi_{0}({\mathcal{B}}_{\hat{\Gamma}})^{\prime\prime}
has a non-zero finite projection,
it is not type
III
.
Note the difference between
\hat{\Gamma}
and

\widetilde{\left(\Gamma\cap{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\right)}

is at most finite.
Therefore,
\pi_{0}\left({\mathcal{B}}_{\widetilde{\left(\Gamma\cap{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\right)}}\right)^{\prime\prime}

is not type
III
either.

\square
