Abstract
We present the results of a search for the b → d ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − b\to d\ell^{+}\ell^{-} flavor-changing neutral-current rare decays B + , 0 → ( η , ω , π + , 0 , ρ + , 0 ) ​ e + ​ e − B^{+,0}\to(\eta,\omega,\pi^{+,0},\rho^{+,0})e^{+}e^{-} and B + , 0 → ( η , ω , π 0 , ρ + ) ​ μ + ​ μ − B^{+,0}\to(\eta,\omega,\pi^{0},\rho^{+})\mu^{+}\mu^{-} using a 711 711 fb -1 data sample that contains 772 × 10 6 772\times 10^{6} B ​ B ¯ B\bar{B} events. The data were collected at the Υ ⁡ ( 4 ​ S ) \Upsilon(4S) resonance with the Belle detector at the KEKB asymmetric-energy e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} collider. We find no evidence for signal and set upper limits on branching fractions at the 90 % 90\% confidence level in the range ( 3.8 − 47 ) × 10 − 8 (3.8-47)\times 10^{-8} depending on the decay channel. The obtained limits are the world’s best results. This is the first search for the channels B + , 0 → ( ω , ρ + , 0 ) ​ e + ​ e − B^{+,0}\to(\omega,\rho^{+,0})e^{+}e^{-} and B + , 0 → ( ω , ρ + ) ​ μ + ​ μ − B^{+,0}\to(\omega,\rho^{+})\mu^{+}\mu^{-} .

In summary, we have searched for the rare decays
B^{+,0}\to(\eta,\omega,\pi^{+,0},\rho^{+,0})e^{+}e^{-}
and
B^{+,0}\to(\eta,\omega,\pi^{0},\rho^{+})\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
, which involve
b\to d\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
transitions, using a
711
fb
-1
data sample of Belle. We find no evidence for the signal in any of the decay channels and set
90\%
confidence-level upper limits on the branching fractions in the range
(3.8-47)\times 10^{-8}
, depending on the decay channel. The world’s best limits are obtained. This is the first search for the channels
B^{+,0}\to(\omega,\rho^{+,0})e^{+}e^{-}
and
B^{+,0}\to(\omega,\rho^{+})\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
. Our branching fraction results for
B^{+}\to\pi^{+}e^{+}e^{-}
and
B^{0}\to\rho^{0}e^{+}e^{-}
, though statistically limited, are consistent with measurements of the
B^{+}\to\pi^{+}\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
and
B^{0}\to\rho^{0}\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
branching fractions from LHCb
LHCb1
;
pipimumu
within
1-2\sigma
. These results are consistent with lepton-flavor-universality in
b\to d\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
transitions. These results will constrain the development of BSM models for
b\to d\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
transitions.
