Counterexamples to two conjectures on mean color numbers of graphs
Wushuang Zhai, Yan Yang

Abstract
The mean color number of an n n -vertex graph G G , denoted by μ ⁡ ( G ) \mu(G) , is the average number of colors used in all proper n n -colorings of G G . For any graph G G and a vertex w w in G G , Dong (2003) conjectured that if H H is a graph obtained from a graph G G by deleting all but one of the edges which are incident to w w , then μ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ μ ⁡ ( H ) \mu(G)\geq\mu(H) ; and also conjectured that μ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ μ ⁡ ( ( G − w ) ∪ K 1 ) \mu(G)\geq\mu((G-w)\cup K_{1}) . We prove that there is an infinite family of counterexamples to these two conjectures. Keywords: chromatic polynomial; coloring; mean color number. Mathematics Subject Classification (2020): 05C15, 05C30, 05C31.

(1)

For any

(2)

By applying

Counterexamples to Conjectures
1.2

1.3

For graphs

]
defined

\tau(G_{1},G_{2},\lambda)=P(G_{1},\lambda)P(G_{2},\lambda-1)-P(G_{1},\lambda-1)P(G_{2},\lambda)

(3)

to compare
\mu(G_{1})
with
\mu(G_{2})
. From Theorem

Lemma 2.1

(
[
4
]
)
.

For any
n
-vertex graphs
G_{1}
and

\mu(G_{1})<\mu(G_{2})

.

For more than two graphs, Dong proved the following Lemma.

Lemma 2.2

(
[
4
]
)
.

For any
n
-vertex graphs
G_{1}
,
G_{2}
and

, if
\tau(G_{1},G_{2},n)<0
and
\tau(G_{2},G_{3},n)<0
, then
\tau(G_{1},G_{3},n)<0
.

Now we give the counterexamples to Conjecture
1.2
.

Construction 2.3
.

Let
G_{0}
be a graph which contains a subgraph
K_{k}

(k\geq 2)
and
|V(G_{0})|=k+s-2

(s\geq 2)
. Let
G_{1}
be a graph obtained from
G_{0}
by adding two new vertices

and

to

(i\geq 1,t\geq 0)

K_{k}
, joining

to

(j\geq 1)
vertices in
K_{k}
, joining
u
to
v
, and satisfying
|N_{G_{1}}(u)\cup N_{G_{1}}(v)|=k+2
,

.

Theorem 2.4
.

Let
G_{0}
and
G_{1}
be graphs as constructed in the Construction
2.3
, and
G_{2}=(G_{1}-v)\cup K_{1}
. If

, then
\mu(G_{1})<\mu(G_{2})
.

Proof.

Let
k+s=n
. Both
G_{1}
and
G_{2}
are
n
-vertex graphs. From the construction of
G_{1}
, we have
N_{G_{1}}(u)\cup N_{G_{1}}(v)=V(K_{k})\cup\{u,v\}

and
i+j=k
. Firstly, we compute the chromatic polynomials

and

.
In the graph
G_{1}-uv
, the degrees of

and

are

and

respectively,

P(G_{1}-uv,\lambda)=P(G_{0},\lambda)(\lambda-i-t)(\lambda-j).

Because

, we have

P(G_{1}/uv,\lambda)=P(G_{0},\lambda)(\lambda-k).

By using the edge deletion-contraction formula, we have

\displaystyle P(G_{1},\lambda)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(G_{1}-uv,\lambda)-P(G_{1}/uv,\lambda)

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(G_{0},\lambda)(\lambda-i-t)(\lambda-j)-P(G_{0},\lambda)(\lambda-k).

One can also compute that

P(G_{2},\lambda)=P(G_{0},\lambda)\lambda(\lambda-i-t).

(5)

Combining

,
(4)
,
(5)
and

, we have

\displaystyle\tau(G_{1},G_{2},n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(G_{1},n)P(G_{2},n-1)-P(G_{1},n-1)P(G_{2},n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(G_{0},n)\big((n-i-t)(n-j)-(n-k)\big)P(G_{0},n-1)(n-1)(n-1-i-t)

\displaystyle-P(G_{0},n-1)\big((n-1-i-t)(n-1-j)-(n-1-k)\big)P(G_{0},n)n(n-i-t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(G_{0},n)P(G_{0},n-1)\big(j^{3}+2(s-t-1)j^{2}+(t^{2}+2t-2ts+s^{2}-s)j+s^{2}-s+(t-j)i\big).

Because

If

, then

,
\mu(G_{1})<\mu(G_{2}).

∎

Lemma 2.5
.

Let
G_{0}
be a graph with
n-1
vertices,
G=G_{0}+w

be the graph obtained from

\tau(G,H,n)<0
.

Proof.

It is easy to compute that

and
P(H,\lambda)=(\lambda-1)P(G_{0},\lambda)
. So we have

\displaystyle\tau(G,H,n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(G,n)P(H,n-1)-P(G,n-1)P(H,n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle nP(G_{0},n)(n-2)P(G_{0},n-1)-(n-1)P(G_{0},n-1)(n-1)P(G_{0},n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-P(G_{0},n)P(G_{0},n-1)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle 0.

∎

Theorem 2.6
.

A counterexample to Conjecture

Let

From Lemma

is also a counterexample to Conjecture

∎
