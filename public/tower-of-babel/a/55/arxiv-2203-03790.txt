Electronic Structure of ThPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}} : an impact of the U ​ 5 ​ f \mathrm{U}~5f states in the electronic structure of UPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}} Preprint: APS/123-QED
Shin-ichi Fujimori, Yukiharu Takeda, Hiroshi Yamagami, Jiří Pospíšil, Etsuji Yamamoto, Yoshinori Haga

Abstract
The electronic structure of ThPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}} , which is isostructural to the heavy fermion superconductor UPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}} , was investigated by photoelectron spectroscopy. The band structure and Fermi surfaces of ThPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}} were obtained by angle-resolved photoelectron spectroscopy (ARPES), and the results were well-explained by the band-structure calculation based on the local density approximation. The comparison between the ARPES spectra and the band-structure calculation suggests that the Fermi surface of ThPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}} mainly consists of the Al ​ 3 ​ p \mathrm{Al}~3p and Th ​ 6 ​ d \mathrm{Th}~6d states with a minor contribution from the Pd ​ 4 ​ d \mathrm{Pd}~4d states. The comparison of the band structures between ThPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}} and UPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}} argues that the U ​ 5 ​ f \mathrm{U}~5f states form Fermi surfaces in UPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}} through hybridization with the Al ​ 3 ​ p \mathrm{Al}~3p state in the Al \mathrm{Al} layer, suggesting that the Fermi surface of UPd 2 ​ Al 3 \mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}} has a strong three-dimensional nature.



I
Introduction

The coexistence of unconventional superconductivity and antiferromagnetic ordering with relatively large magnetic moment (0.85
\mu_{\mathrm{B}}/\mathrm{U}
) is the most characteristic feature of the heavy fermion compound
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}

Geibel et al. 1991
.
There are many electronic structure studies for
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
; however, the nature of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
state in
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
remains contradictory.
The dHvA study reported that the observed dHvA branches are mostly explained by the band structure calculation treating the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states as being itinerant
Inada et al. 1999
.
Its electronic structure has also been studied by photoelectron spectroscopy
Fujimori et al. 2007
;
Fujimori et al. 2010
;
Fujimori et al. 2012a
;
Fujimori et al. 2014
;
Fujimori et al. 2016
, and the itinerant nature of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states has been reported.
A recent Compton scattering study has also reported that the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states have an itinerant character at temperatures lower than
T=20~\mathrm{K}

Koizumi et al. 2019
, which is consistent with the ARPES study
Fujimori et al. 2007
.
Meanwhile, a resonant photoemission study on
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
has also suggested the itinerant nature of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
state, but there exists the correlated satellite structure due to the strong electron correlation effect
Fujimori et al. 2019
.
In contrast, the transport properties of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
are very similar to those of heavy fermion Ce-based compounds, and they have been essentially understood based on the very localized
\mathrm{U}~5f
picture.
For example, the temperature dependence of the magnetic susceptibility has been interpreted based on the crystalline electric field scheme
Grauel et al. 1992
.
To understand these physical properties consistently, the dual model of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states has been proposed for this compound
Zwicknagl et al. 2003
.
In this scenario, the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states are formally divided into two subsystems: an itinerant
f^{1}
component and a localized
f^{2}
component.
The result of the recent point contact spectroscopy study on
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
was also interpreted along with this scenario
Jaggi et al. 2017
, but the microscopic information of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states is still lacking.
To understand the electronic structure of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
, it is essential to identify the contribution of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
state to gain a greater insight regarding its nature in this compound.

In the present study, we have studied the electronic structure of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
, which is the
f^{0}
-reference compound of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
, by photoelectron spectroscopy.
We compared the angle-resolved photoelectron spectroscopy (ARPES) spectra between
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
and
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
, and the contribution from the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states to the band structure of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
was clarified.
Furthermore,
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
is a superconductor with
T_{\mathrm{C}}=0.2~\mathrm{K}

Maple et al. 1995
.
It is the only case where an uranium heavy fermion superconductor has an isostructural counterpart of the thorium compound, which is also superconductor.
Thus,
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
is in itself an important target material to study its electronic structure.
We found that the band structure and the topology of the Fermi surface of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
are essentially explained by the band-structure calculation based on the local density approximation.

II
Experimental Procedure and Band-Structure Calculation

The
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
single crystal was grown by Czochralski method by pulling speed of
12~\mathrm{mm/h}
in a tetraarc furnace under Ar protective atmosphere.
Final shape of single crystal was a cylinder with a diameter of
2-3~\mathrm{mm}
and a length of
15~\mathrm{mm}
.
The high quality of single crystal was confirmed by Laue method showing sharp spots and RRR ratio = 37 (the extrapolated value
\rho_{0}=0.7~\mathrm{\mu\Omega/cm}
) after 2 weeks annealing at
900\mathrm{{}^{\circ}C}
in an evacuated quartz ampule.
Photoemission experiments were performed at the soft X-ray beamline BL23SU of SPring-8
Saitoh et al. 2012
.
The overall energy resolution in angle-integrated photoemission (AIPES) experiments at
h\nu=800~\mathrm{eV}
was approximately
150~\mathrm{meV}
, and that in the ARPES experiments at
h\nu=660~\mathrm{eV}
was approximately
90~\mathrm{meV}
.
Clean sample surfaces were obtained by cleaving the samples
in situ
perpendicular to the
c
axis under ultra-high vacuum conditions.
The vacuum during the course of measurements was typically
<2\times 10^{-8}~\mathrm{Pa}
, and the sample surfaces were stable for the entire duration of the measurements (about 2 days) since no significant changes had been observed in the ARPES spectra during these periods.
The positions of the ARPES cuts in the momentum space were determined by assuming a free-electron final state with an inner potential of
V_{0}=12~\mathrm{eV}
.

In the band-structure calculations, relativistic linear augmented plane wave (RLAPW) calculations
Yamagami 1998
within the LDA
von Barth and Hedin 1972
were performed, treating all
\mathrm{U}~5f
electrons as itinerant.
In this approach, the Dirac-type Kohn-Sham equation has been formulated, and the spin-orbit interaction is exactly taken into account
sup
.
To compare the results of the calculation with the ARPES spectra, we have simulated the ARPES spectral functions on the basis of the band-structure calculation.
In the simulation, the following effects were taken into account: (i) the broadening along the
k_{\perp}
direction due to the finite escape depth of the photoelectrons, (ii) the lifetime broadening of the photohole, (iii) the photoemission cross sections of orbitals, and (iv) the energy resolution and the angular resolution of the electron analyzer.
The details are outlined in Ref.
Fujimori et al. 2012b
.

In Fig.

The three dimensional shape of the calculated Fermi surfaces of

The experimental energy dispersions of

In particular, the very flat features at
E_{\mathrm{F}}
originate from the renormalized
\mathrm{U}~5f

The comparison between the ARPES spectra of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
and
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
indicates that the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states are strongly hybridized with the non-
f
dispersive bands in
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
which correspond to the calculated bands 15-18 of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
.
These calculated bands have the enhanced contribution from the
\mathrm{Al}~3p
states, suggesting that the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states are strongly hybridized with the
\mathrm{Al}~3p
states in
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
.
The crystal structure of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
consists of alternating stacks of
\mathrm{U}
–
\mathrm{Pd}
and
\mathrm{Al}
layers along the
c
axis, and the presence of the enhanced
\mathrm{U}~5f
–
\mathrm{Al}~3p
hybridization suggests that the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states have a strong three-dimensional nature similar to the case of heavy fermion compound
\mathrm{URu_{2}Si_{2}}

Fujimori et al. 2021
.
As a result, the Fermi surface of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
should also have a strong three dimensional nature due to the enhanced
\mathrm{U}~5f
–
\mathrm{Al}~3p
hybridization.
Previous ARPES studies of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}

Fujimori et al. 2010
;
Fujimori et al. 2014
have shown a cylindrical Fermi surface along the
\mathrm{\Gamma}
–
\mathrm{A}
line.
The Fermi surface has an enhanced contribution from the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states, but the present result suggests that it also has the enhanced contribution from the
\mathrm{Al}~3p
states.

Note that the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states have a strong electron correlation effect, and the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states in
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
have incoherent peak distributed at approximately
E_{\mathrm{B}}=0.2-1~\mathrm{eV}

Fujimori et al. 2019
.
Thus, the experimental bands around this binding energy should have the contribution from the incoherent component of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states.
This is consistent with the dual nature of the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states in
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
as proposed theoretically, where the heavy quasi-particle band is described by the effective renormalized theory while the high-energy structure is explained by the multiplet side bands arising from the Hund’s rule
Zwicknagl and Reese 2007
;
Zwicknagl
.
Experimentally, there are not non-dispersive but dispersive bands in this binding energy region, and thus the energy dispersions in
E_{\mathrm{B}}=0.2-1~\mathrm{eV}
are hybridized with the incoherent
\mathrm{U}~5f
states.
It is theoretically proposed that the localized multiplet bands are hybridized with dispersive non-
f
bands
Zwicknagl
, and the experimental spectra are consistent with the theory.

IV
Conclusion

In the present study, we investigated the electronic structure of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
using photoelectron spectroscopy, and the results were compared with the band-structure calculation and the spectra of the isostructural heavy fermion superconductor
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
.The
\mathrm{Pd}~4d
states in the valence-band spectrum of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
were found to be shallower than those in the valence-band spectrum of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
by approximately
200~\mathrm{meV}
, suggesting that the
\mathrm{U}~5f
states are involved in the valence-band structure of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
, and are not impurity-like contributions.
The Fermi surface and the band structure of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
obtained by ARPES were well explained by the band-structure calculation.
The electronic structure in the very vicinity of
E_{\mathrm{F}}
is dominated by contributions from the
\mathrm{Al}~3p
and
\mathrm{Th}~6d
states with minor contributions from the
\mathrm{Pd}~4d
states.
The comparison between the ARPES spectra of
\mathrm{ThPd_{2}Al_{3}}
and
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
suggests that the the electronic structure of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
in the very vicinity of
E_{\mathrm{F}}
is dominated by the enhanced
\mathrm{U}~5f
–
\mathrm{Al}~3p
hybridization.
This indicates that the electronic structure of
\mathrm{UPd_{2}Al_{3}}
has a three-dimensional nature.
