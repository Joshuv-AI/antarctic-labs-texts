Abstract
This is the first of two papers on the fine structure of HOD in models of the Axiom of Determinacy ( 𝖠𝖣 \sf{AD} ). Let M ⊨ 𝖠𝖣 + + 𝖵 = 𝖫 ⁡ ( ℘ ⁡ ( ℝ ) ) M\vDash\sf{AD}^{+}+V=L({\wp}(\mathbb{R})) . [ 13 ] shows that under a natural hypothesis on the existence of iteration strategies, the basic fine structure theory for pure extender models goes over to HOD M \mbox{HOD}^{M} . In this paper, we prove a fine condensation theorem, quite similar to Theorem 9.3.2 of Zeman’s book [ 16 ] , except that condensation for iteration strategies has been added to the mix. In the second paper, we shall use this theorem to show that in HOD M \mbox{HOD}^{M} , □ κ \square_{\kappa} holds iff κ \kappa is not subcompact.

The hod mice used in this work have the

Putting the strategy information in this way

“premouse” becomes very complicated, and indeed it is not clear how to
extend the definition of rigidly layered hod pairs much past that given in

[
