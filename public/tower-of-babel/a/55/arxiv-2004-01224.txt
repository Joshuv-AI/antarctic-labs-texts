A group-theoretic generalization of the p p -adic local monodromy theorem
Shuyang Ye

Abstract
Let G G be a connected reductive group over a p p -adic local field F F . We propose and study the notions of G G - φ \varphi -modules and G G - ( φ , ∇ ) (\varphi,\nabla) -modules over the Robba ring, which are exact faithful F F -linear tensor functors from the category of G G -representations on finite-dimensional F F -vector spaces to the categories of φ \varphi -modules and ( φ , ∇ ) (\varphi,\nabla) -modules over the Robba ring, respectively, commuting with the respective fiber functors. We study Kedlaya’s slope filtration theorem in this context, and show that G G - ( φ , ∇ ) (\varphi,\nabla) -modules over the Robba ring are “ G G -quasi-unipotent”, which is a generalization of the p p -adic local monodromy theorem proven independently by Y. André, K. S. Kedlaya, and Z. Mebkhout.

For

Lemma 4.9
.

Let
(g,X)\in\mathbf{B}^{\varphi,\nabla}(G,R)
. We then have
\big([n]_{*}(g),X\big)\in\mathbf{B}^{\varphi^{n},\nabla}(G,R)
for all
n\in\mathds{N}
.

Proof.

We show by induction on
n
that

X+\dlog\big([n]_{*}(g)\big)=\muu(\varphi^{n},t)\Ad\big([n]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n}(X)\big).

There is nothing to show when
n=1
. We now assume by the induction hypothesis that

X+\dlog\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)=\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\Ad\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n-1}(X)\big),

We notice that
\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)=\muu\varphi(\muu)\cdots\varphi^{n-2}(\muu)
, and hence

\partial(\varphi^{n-1}(f))=\muu\varphi(\muu)\cdots\varphi^{n-2}(\muu)\varphi^{n-1}(\partial(f))=\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\varphi^{n-1}(\partial(f)),~~~~~\forall f\in R,

which implies that

\dlog(\varphi^{n-1}(g))=\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\varphi^{n-1}(\dlog(g)).

On the other hand, since
X+\dlog(g)=\muu\Ad(g)(\varphi(X))
, we have

\varphi^{n-1}(X)+\varphi^{n-1}(\dlog(g))=\varphi^{n-1}(\muu)\Ad\big(\varphi^{n-1}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n}(X)\big).

We now compute

\displaystyle X+\dlog\big([n]_{*}(g)\big)

\displaystyle=X+\dlog\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)+\Ad\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\dlog(\varphi^{n-1}(g))\big)

\displaystyle=\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\Ad\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n-1}(X)\big)

\displaystyle\;\;\;\;+\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\Ad\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n-1}(\dlog(g))\big)

\displaystyle=\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\Ad\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n-1}(X)+\varphi^{n-1}(\dlog(g))\big)

\displaystyle=\muu(\varphi^{n-1},t)\Ad\big([n-1]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n-1}(\muu)\Ad\big(\varphi^{n-1}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n}(X)\big)\big)

\displaystyle=\muu(\varphi^{n},t)\Ad\big([n]_{*}(g)\big)\big(\varphi^{n}(X)\big),

which proves the lemma.
