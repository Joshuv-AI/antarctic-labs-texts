Normalizers in the non-pointed context: a weak case of extremal decomposition
Dominique Bourn

Abstract
The aim of this work is to point out a strong structural phenomenon hidden behind the existence of normalizers through the investigation of this property in the non-pointed context: given any category 𝔼 \mathbb{E} , a certain property of the fibration of points ¶ 𝔼 : P ​ t ​ ( 𝔼 ) → 𝔼 \lx@paragraphsign_{\mathbb{E}}\colon Pt(\mathbb{E})\to\mathbb{E} guarentees the existence of normalizers. This property becomes a characterization of this existence when 𝔼 \mathbb{E} is quasi-pointed and protomodular. This property is also showed to be equivalent to a property of the category G ​ r ​ d ​ 𝔼 Grd\mathbb{E} of internal groupoids in 𝔼 \mathbb{E} which is a kind of opposite, for the monomorphic internal functors, of the comprehensive factorization.

Corollary 7.2

Let
