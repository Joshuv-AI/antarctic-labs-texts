Abstract
In this work, I present for the first time the analysis concerning the growth of matter fluctuations in the framework of f ⁡ ( R , T ) f(R,T) modified gravity where I presume f ⁡ ( R , T ) = R + λ ​ T f(R,T)=R+\lambda T , where R R denote the Ricci scalar, T T the trace of energy momentum tensor and λ \lambda a constant. I first solve the Friedman equations assuming a dust universe ( ω = 0 \omega=0 ) for the Hubble parameter H ⁡ ( z ) H(z) and then employ it in the equation of matter density fluctuations δ ⁡ ( z ) \delta(z) to solve for δ ⁡ ( z ) \delta(z) and the growth rate f ⁡ ( z ) f(z) . Next, I proceed to show the behavior of f ⁡ ( z ) f(z) and δ ⁡ ( z ) \delta(z) with redshift for some values of λ \lambda with observational constraints. Finally, following the prescription of growft41 , I present an analytical expression for the growth index γ \gamma which is redshift dependent and the expression reduces to 3 / 5 3/5 for λ = 0 \lambda=0 , which is the growth index for a dust universe.
