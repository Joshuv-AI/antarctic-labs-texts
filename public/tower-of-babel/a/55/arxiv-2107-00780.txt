Coproduct for affine Yangians and parabolic induction for rectangular W W -algebras
Ryosuke Kodera, Mamoru Ueda

Abstract
We construct algebra homomorphisms from affine Yangians to the current algebras of rectangular W W -algebras both in type A. The construction is given via the coproduct and the evaluation map for the affine Yangians. As a consequence, we show that parabolic inductions for representations of the rectangular W W -algebras can be regarded as tensor product representations of the affine Yangians under the homomorphisms. The same method is applicable also to the super setting.

Introduction

W
-algebras form an important class of vertex algebras.
They have been widely studied with connections to various areas of mathematics and mathematical physics such as two-dimensional conformal field theories, integrable systems, and four-dimensional gauge theories.
We refer to Arakawa’s ICM talk
[
A2
]
and references therein for recent developments.
The goal of this paper is to relate
W
-algebras for rectangular nilpotent elements in type A to certain quantum groups, called affine Yangians.

The
W
-algebra
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
is defined by the quantized Drinfeld-Sokolov reduction
[
FF
,
KRW
]

from the following data:
a reductive Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}
over
\mathbb{C}
,
an invariant symmetric bilinear form
\kappa
on
\mathfrak{g}
,
and a nilpotent element
f
in
\mathfrak{g}
.
Here we omit to mention a choice of a grading of
\mathfrak{g}
.
It admits the Miura map, an injective homomorphism from
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
to the universal affine vertex algebra
V^{\tilde{\kappa}}(\mathfrak{g}_{0})
where
\mathfrak{g}_{0}
denotes a Levi subalgebra of
\mathfrak{g}
determined by a grading of
\mathfrak{g}
, and
\tilde{\kappa}
is a bilinear form on
\mathfrak{g}_{0}
induced from
\kappa
with an appropriate shift.
Thus we can regard
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
as a vertex subalgebra of
V^{\tilde{\kappa}}(\mathfrak{g}_{0})
.

In this paper, we consider the case
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{gl}_{N}
for
N=ln
with
l\geq 1
and
n\geq 3
, and
f
is a nilpotent element whose Jordan form corresponds to the partition
(l^{n})
.
We call them rectangular
W
-algebras because of the shape of the Young diagram corresponding to the partition
(l^{n})
.
Recently the rectangular
W
-algebras have been intensively studied; see
[
CH1
,
CH2
,
EP
,
R
]
for instance.

For a suitable choice of
\kappa
(see Section
2
), the universal affine vertex algebra

is isomorphic to the tensor product of
l
copies of the universal affine vertex algebra of
\mathfrak{gl}_{n}
.
Arakawa-Molev
[
AM
]
constructed elements
W_{i,j}^{(r)}
(
r=1,\ldots,l
and
i,j=1,\dots,n
) in
V^{\tilde{\kappa}}(\mathfrak{g}_{0})\cong V^{\tilde{\kappa}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})^{\otimes l}
and proved that they generate the rectangular
W
-algebra
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
.

Similar, and more refined story has been known for the corresponding finite
W
-algebras.
The rectangular finite
W
-algebra for
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{gl}_{N}
and
f=(l^{n})
is regarded as a subalgebra of
U(\mathfrak{g}_{0})\cong U(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})^{\otimes l}
.
Ragoucy-Sorba
[
RS
]
first noticed a relation between the rectangular finite
W
-algebras and Yangians of finite type both in type A.
Later Brundan-Kleshchev
[
BK1
]
extended Ragoucy-Sorba’s result; they constructed surjective algebra homomorphisms from shifted Yangians to finite
W
-algebras for arbitrary nilpotent elements in
\mathfrak{gl}_{N}
and determined their kernels.
In
[
BK1
, Section 12]
, it was observed that in the case of rectangular type, the homomorphism is related to the coproduct and the evaluation map for the Yangian
Y(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})
.

Our main theorem is an affine analog of this observation, which asserts that the composition of the coproduct and the evaluation map for the affine Yangian
Y(\hat{\mathfrak{sl}}_{n})
gives an algebra homomorphism to the current algebra of the rectangular
W
-algebra
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
.
It answers affirmatively to an question raised by Genra in
[
Ge2
, Introduction]
.

We associate with a vertex algebra

Let

We assume that the condition

Let

We obtain a similar result for the super setting in Theorem

Our work is motivated by the Alday-Gaiotto-Tachikawa (AGT) correspondence for parabolic sheaves on
\mathbb{P}^{1}\times\mathbb{P}^{1}

There are various versions of AGT correspondence and we only refer to the original paper
[

]
and
[

It has been shown that the affine Yangian

We conjecture that our map

induces an action of

on the equivariant cohomology of affine Laumon spaces and that the action is one predicted by the AGT correspondence.
This conjecture would give an affine analog of results of Braverman-Feigin-Finkelberg-Rybnikov
[
BFFR
]
and Nakajima
[
Nakaj
]
in the case of rectangular type.
We note that Negut studies the
K
-theory version in
[
Ne1
,
Ne2
]
by a different approach.

We are missing the

Rectangular
W
-algebras

The
W
-algebra
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
is defined by the quantized Drinfeld-Sokolov reduction from the data consisting of a reductive Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}
over
\mathbb{C}
equipped with a
\mathfrak{g}
-invariant symmetric bilinear form
\kappa

\mathfrak{g}
.
In this paper, we do not use its definition but a characterization by the Miura map, which will be recalled in Section

See

]

Let

be the complex general linear Lie algebra consisting of

We denote by

-entry is

Put

Then we have a decomposition

Fix

.
We define an invariant symmetric bilinear form

\kappa(X,Y)=\begin{cases}k\tr(XY)&\text{if }X,Y\in\mathfrak{sl}_{N},\\
(k+N)\tr(XY)&\text{if }X,Y\in\mathfrak{z}_{N},\\
0&\text{if }X\in\mathfrak{sl}_{N}\text{ and }Y\in\mathfrak{z}_{N}.\end{cases}

(2.1)

The explicit value of
\kappa
is

\kappa(E_{i,j},E_{p,q})=\delta_{iq}\delta_{jp}k+\delta_{ij}\delta_{pq}.

We assume

with nonzero positive integers

As vector spaces, we have an isomorphism

\mathfrak{gl}_{l}\otimes\mathfrak{gl}_{n}\cong\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{gl}_{N},\qquad E_{s,t}\otimes E_{i,j}\mapsto E_{(s-1)n+i,(t-1)n+j}.

(2.2)

We define a

-grading of
\mathfrak{gl}_{l}
by
\deg E_{s,t}=t-s
and denote by
(\mathfrak{gl}_{l})_{j}
its degree

It induces a
\mathbb{Z}
-grading of
\mathfrak{g}
by

\mathfrak{g}_{j}=(\mathfrak{gl}_{l})_{j}\otimes\mathfrak{gl}_{n}

using the identification (
2.2
).
In particular, we have
\mathfrak{g}_{0}\cong\mathfrak{gl}_{n}^{\oplus l}
.
We set

E_{i,j}^{[s]}=E_{(s-1)n+i,(s-1)n+j}

(2.3)

for

and

\begin{split}f=\left(\sum_{s=1}^{l-1}E_{s+1,s}\right)\otimes I_{n}=\sum_{s=1}^{l-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}E_{sn+i,(s-1)n+i}=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccc}O&&&&O\\
I_{n}&O&&&\\
&I_{n}&\dots&&\\
&&&O&\\
O&&&I_{n}&O\end{array}\right].\end{split}

(2.4)

The Jordan form of the nilpotent element

corresponds to the partition

of rectangular type.

From the data (

) above, we can define the rectangular

-algebra

as a vertex algebra.
It is isomorphic to the tensor product

\mathcal{W}^{k}(\mathfrak{sl}_{N},f)\otimes V^{k+N}(\mathfrak{z}_{N}),

where
\mathcal{W}^{k}(\mathfrak{sl}_{N},f)
is the
W
-algebra associated with

and

,

\displaystyle W_{i,j}^{(2)}

Remark 4.3
.

The elements

are slightly different from ones in the literature.

(i)

Let

(ii)

The current algebra

Example 4.4

We have

Parabolic induction

Let us recall the parabolic induction for the rectangular
W
-algebras (see
[
Ge2
, Introduction, Theorem A and B]
for a general result).

Choose integers
l_{1},l_{2}\geq 1
satisfying
l=l_{1}+l_{2}
.
Set
N_{1}=l_{1}n
and
N_{2}=l_{2}n
so that
N=N_{1}+N_{2}
.
We also take
k_{1}
and
k_{2}
so that

k+N=k_{1}+N_{1}=k_{2}+N_{2}

(5.1)

holds.
Since the parameter
\alpha
is defined by
k+N=\alpha+n
, the equality (

) means that
\alpha
is unchanged if we replace
(k,l,N)
with
(k_{i},l_{i},N_{i})
(

).

For

(

), we define an invariant symmetric bilinear form
\kappa_{i}
on
\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{i}}
in a way similar to (
2.1
), and a nilpotent element
f_{i}
as in (
2.4
) corresponding to the partition (
l_{i}^{n}
).
Associated with the data,
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{1}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{1}},f_{1})
and
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{2}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{2}},f_{2})
are defined.
We have the Miura maps

\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{i}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{i}},f_{i})\to V^{\tilde{\kappa}_{i}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})^{\otimes l_{i}}

for
i=1,2
.
Since
\alpha
is taken uniformly for
(k,l,N)
and
(k_{i},l_{i},N_{i})
,

on
\mathfrak{gl}_{n}
is the same as
\tilde{\kappa}
.
Hence
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{1}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{1}},f_{1})\otimes\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{2}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{2}},f_{2})
can be regarded as a subalgebra of

by the tensor product of the Miura maps.
Then it turns out that
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{1}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{1}},f_{1})\otimes\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{2}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{2}},f_{2})

\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
inside

.
We denote the inclusion by
\Delta_{l_{1},l_{2}}
:

\Delta_{l_{1},l_{2}}\colon\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)\to\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{1}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{1}},f_{1})\otimes\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{2}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{2}},f_{2}).

The generators given in Section
4
are related by
\Delta_{l_{1},l_{2}}
as follows.
Divide the left-hand side
(\tau+A^{[1]})\cdots(\tau+A^{[l]})
of (
4.3
) into two blocks:

(\tau+A^{[1]})\cdots(\tau+A^{[l_{1}]})\ \text{ and }\ (\tau+A^{[l_{1}+1]})\cdots(\tau+A^{[l]}).

Then we can similarly define generators for
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{1}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{1}},f_{1})
and
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa_{2}}(\mathfrak{gl}_{N_{2}},f_{2})
, and we can relate them to the generators

of
\mathcal{W}^{\kappa}(\mathfrak{g},f)
.
This gives a description of

in terms of generators.
We give its explicit form for

as follows:

\Delta_{l_{1},l_{2}}(W_{i,j}^{(1)}(m))=W_{i,j}^{(1)}(m)\otimes 1+1\otimes W_{i,j}^{(1)}(m),

\begin{split}\Delta_{l_{1},l_{2}}(W_{i,j}^{(2)}(m))&=W_{i,j}^{(2)}(m)\otimes 1+1\otimes W_{i,j}^{(2)}(m)+\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{a=1}^{n}W_{i,a}^{(1)}(m+r)\otimes W_{a,j}^{(1)}(-r)\\
&\qquad-(m+1)l_{2}\alpha W_{i,j}^{(1)}(m)\otimes 1.\end{split}

We will relate
\Delta_{l_{1},l_{2}}
to the coproduct of the affine Yangian in Corollary
10.2
and
10.3
as a consequence of the main result of this paper.

Assume

Let

be entries of the Cartan matrix of type

.

Definition 6.1
.

The affine Yangian
Y(\hat{\mathfrak{sl}}_{n})=Y_{\hbar,\varepsilon}(\hat{\mathfrak{sl}}_{n})
is the algebra over
\mathbb{C}
generated by

with parameters

subject to the relations:

[H_{i,r},H_{j,s}]=0,\qquad[X_{i,r}^{+},X_{j,s}^{-}]=\delta_{ij}H_{i,r+s},\qquad[H_{i,0},X_{j,s}^{\pm}]=\pm a_{ij}X_{j,s}^{\pm}\quad\text{ for any $i,j$,}

\displaystyle[H_{i,r+1},X_{j,s}^{\pm}]-[H_{i,r},X_{j,s+1}^{\pm}]=\pm\dfrac{a_{ij}}{2}\hbar\{H_{i,r},X_{j,s}^{\pm}\},

\displaystyle[X_{i,r+1}^{\pm},X_{j,s}^{\pm}]-[X_{i,r}^{\pm},X_{j,s+1}^{\pm}]=\pm\dfrac{a_{ij}}{2}\hbar\{X_{i,r}^{\pm},X_{j,s}^{\pm}\}\quad\text{for $(i,j)\neq(0,n-1),(n-1,0)$,}

[H_{n-1,r+1},X_{0,s}^{\pm}]-[H_{n-1,r},X_{0,s+1}^{\pm}]=\mp\dfrac{\hbar}{2}\{H_{n-1,r},X_{0,s}^{\pm}\}+\left(\dfrac{n}{2}\hbar+\varepsilon\right)[H_{n-1,r},X_{0,s}^{\pm}],

[H_{0,r+1},X_{n-1,s}^{\pm}]-[H_{0,r},X_{n-1,s+1}^{\pm}]=\mp\dfrac{\hbar}{2}\{H_{0,r},X_{n-1,s}^{\pm}\}-\left(\dfrac{n}{2}\hbar+\varepsilon\right)[H_{0,r},X_{n-1,s}^{\pm}],

[X_{n-1,r+1}^{\pm},X_{0,s}^{\pm}]-[X_{n-1,r}^{\pm},X_{0,s+1}^{\pm}]=\mp\dfrac{\hbar}{2}\{X_{n-1,r}^{\pm},X_{0,s}^{\pm}\}+\left(\dfrac{n}{2}\hbar+\varepsilon\right)[X_{n-1,r}^{\pm},X_{0,s}^{\pm}],

\sum_{w\in\mathfrak{S}_{1-a_{ij}}}[X_{i,r_{w(1)}}^{\pm},[X_{i,r_{w(2)}}^{\pm},\dots,[X_{i,r_{w(1-a_{ij})}}^{\pm},X_{j,s}^{\pm}]\dots]]=0\quad\text{for $i\neq j$.}

Set

X_{i}^{\pm}(z)=\hbar\sum_{r\geq 0}X_{i,r}^{\pm}z^{-r-1},\quad H_{i}(z)=1+\hbar\sum_{r\geq 0}H_{i,r}z^{-r-1}.

\begin{split}&[W_{j,j}^{(2)}(0),A_{i}+B_{i}]=\sum_{m\geq 0}\Big(-W_{i,j}^{(2)}(-m-1)W_{j,i}^{(1)}(m+1)+W_{i,j}^{(1)}(-m-1)W_{j,i}^{(2)}(m+1)\Big)\\
&\ +(l-1)\alpha\sum_{m\geq 1}mW_{i,j}^{(1)}(-m)W_{j,i}^{(1)}(m)+(l-1)\sum_{m\geq 1}m\Big(W_{j,j}^{(1)}(-m)W_{i,i}^{(1)}(m)-W_{i,i}^{(1)}(-m)W_{j,j}^{(1)}(m)\Big).\end{split}

We may assume
i<j
.
Then, by Lemma
B.1
,
B.2
, and (
11.2
), we have

\begin{split}[D_{i},D_{j}]&=[W_{i,i}^{(2)}(0),W_{j,j}^{(2)}(0)]-[W_{i,i}^{(2)}(0),A_{j}+B_{j}]+[W_{j,j}^{(2)}(0),A_{i}+B_{i}]+[A_{i}+B_{i},A_{j}+B_{j}]\\
&=-W_{i,i}^{(2)}(0)+W_{j,j}^{(2)}(0)+W_{i,j}^{(2)}(0)W_{j,i}^{(1)}(0)-W_{j,i}^{(1)}(0)W_{i,j}^{(2)}(0)\\
&=-W_{i,i}^{(2)}(0)+W_{j,j}^{(2)}(0)+[W_{i,j}^{(2)}(0),W_{j,i}^{(1)}(0)].\end{split}

This is equal to
0
by Lemma

.
∎

and
