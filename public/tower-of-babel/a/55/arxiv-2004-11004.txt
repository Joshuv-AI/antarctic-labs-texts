Valuation rings as limits of complete intersection rings
Dorin Popescu

Abstract
. We show that a valuation ring containing its residue field of characteristic p > 0 p>0 is a filtered direct limit of complete intersection 𝐅 p {\bf F}_{p} -algebras. Key words : immediate extensions, pseudo convergent sequences, pseudo limits, ultrapowers, smooth morphisms, Henselian Rings. 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 13F30, Secondary 13A18,13L05,13B40.



Introduction

The Zariski Uniformization Theory
[
31
]
plays an important role in Desingularization Theory.

Theorem 1
.

(Zariski) Let
V
be a valuation ring containing a field
L\supset{\bf Q}
. Then
V
is a filtered union of its regular local subrings essentially of finite type over
L
.

This theorem says in fact that
V
is a filtered union of its smooth
L
-subalgebras of finite type and
the following theorem is connected with it.

Theorem 2
.

[
21
, Theorem 2]
If
V\subset V^{\prime}
is an immediate extension of valuation rings containing
\bf Q

then
V^{\prime}
is a filtered colimit of smooth
V
-algebras.

An inclusion
V\subset V^{\prime}
of valuation rings is an
immediate extension
if it is local as a map of local rings and induces isomorphisms between the value groups and the residue fields of
V
and
V^{\prime}
.
We remind that a filtered colimit is a limit indexed by a small category that is filtered
(see
[
28
, 002V]
or
[
28
, 04AX]
). A filtered union is a filtered direct limit in which all objects are subobjects of the final colimit,
so that in particular all the transition arrows are monomorphisms.

The above theorem does not hold when the valuation ring extension is algebraic and contains a field of positive characteristic (see
[
19
, Example 3.13]
inspired from
[
17
]
and
[
19
, Remark 6.10]
), but we got the following result.

Theorem 3
.

[
25
, Theorem 4]
Let
V\subset V^{\prime}
be an immediate extension of valuation rings containing a field and
K\subset K^{\prime}
its fraction field extension. If
K^{\prime}=K(x)
for some algebraically independent system of elements
x
over
K

then
V^{\prime}
is a filtered union of smooth
V
-subalgebras of
V^{\prime}
.

As in
[
23
, Proposition 18]
(see also
[

For a Henselian valuation ring
