Two-loop massive QCD and QED helicity amplitudes for light-by-light scattering
Ajjath A H a , Ekta Chaubey b , Hua-Sheng Shao a

Abstract
We present the analytic and compact two-loop helicity amplitudes for QCD and QED corrections to the light-by-light scattering process with massive internal fermions. We express the master integrals either in terms of multiple polylogarithms or in terms of iterated integrals with dlog \mathrm{dlog} one-forms. We also elaborate on optimising the analytic results for each phase-space region. This makes the numerical evaluation of the scattering amplitudes fast, stable and suitable for phenomenological applications.

Conclusion

In this paper, we have presented the compact and analytic two-loop helicity amplitudes of the LbL process
\gamma\gamma\to\gamma\gamma
keeping the full dependence of the internal fermion mass. The results remain simple enough even at two loops to be written in a few pages thanks to the numerous symmetries of the process. They could serve as a benchmark for developing novel multi-loop techniques, such as the recent local unitarity approach

\displaystyle x_{ij}=-\frac{4(w-z)^{2}}{(1-w^{2})(1-z^{2})},\quad\frac{s_{jk}}{m_{f}^{2}}=x_{jk}=-\frac{(w-z)^{2}}{wz},\quad s_{ik}=-s_{ij}-s_{jk}.
