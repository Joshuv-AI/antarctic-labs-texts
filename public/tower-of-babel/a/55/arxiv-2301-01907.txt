Graphic Elementary Lift of Cographic Matroids


Abstract
. A matroid N N is a lift of a binary matroid M M , if N = Q \ X N=Q\backslash X when Q / X = M Q/X=M for some binary matroid Q Q and X ⊆ E ⁡ ( Q ) X\subseteq E(Q) and is called an elementary lift of M M , if | X | = 1 |X|=1 . A splitting operation on a binary matroid can result in an elementary lift. An elementary lift of a cographic or a graphic matroid need not be cographic or graphic. We intend to characterize the cographic matroids whose elementary lift is a graphic matroid.
