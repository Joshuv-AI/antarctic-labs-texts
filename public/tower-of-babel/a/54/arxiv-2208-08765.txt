Theorem 5.1

Let
1<p<\infty
,

and rewrite it in the following equivalent form:

where

\displaystyle+\displaystyle\frac{c_{1}x}{\pi}\int_{-1}^{1}u^{\prime}(y)dy=c_{0}u(x)+\displaystyle\frac{c_{1}}{\pi}\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^{2})u^{\prime}(y)\displaystyle\frac{1-xy}{y-x}\displaystyle\frac{dy}{1-y^{2}}

\displaystyle=c_{0}u(x)+\frac{c_{1}}{\pi}\left[\mathfrak{D}_{G}u\ast_{G}\displaystyle\frac{1}{y}\right](x)=f_{0}(x),\qquad x\in G,

(83)

where

is the convolution (see (
3

G

By applying the Fourier transformation

(84)

5.1
means invertibility (Fredholmness) of the operator

It is clear, that
