DIFFERENTIAL DIFFUSION MODEL WITH TWO EQUILIBRIUM STATES

Abstract




Keywords:

discrete Markov diffusion, evolutionary process, classification of equilibriums, stochastic approximation

Summary

The difference diffusion model with two equilibrium states is given by a stochastic equation with two components: the predicted one, which is determined by the regression function of increments with two equilibriums, and the stochastic one, which is the martingale difference.

We propose a classification of zones of influence of equilibriums according to asymptotic properties of trajectories of statistical experiments.

We study asymptotic behavior of statistical experiments, determined by the sums of
N
sample values, as
N\to\infty
.

The principle ”stimulation and restraint”

The frequency probabilities
P_{+}(k)
of sample values
\delta_{n}^{+}(k)
,
k\geq 1
, are determined by
evolutionary processes
(EP), which dynamics by the stages
k\geq 0

From a mathematical point of view, the principle ”stimulation and restraint”  is given by the linear function of frequencies of values
p_{\pm}
(
0\leq p_{\pm}\leq 1
,
p_{+}+p_{-}=1
) of the sampling values
\delta_{n}^{\pm}(k)
:

\pi_{+}p_{+}-\pi_{-}p_{-}

with two directing parameters
\pi_{\pm}
, which, without reducing the generality, also satisfy the properties of frequencies:

0<\pi_{\pm}<1\ ,\ \ \pi_{+}+\pi_{-}=1.

It follows that the principle ”stimulation and restraint”  is expressed by
frequency fluctuations
(FF)

\pi_{+}p_{+}-\pi_{-}p_{-}=p_{+}-\pi_{-}.

The linear component of RFI can also be represented in the following form:

\pi_{-}p_{+}-\pi_{+}p_{-}=p_{+}-\pi_{+}.

The product of two frequency fluctuations

(p_{+}-\pi_{+})(p_{+}-\pi_{-})=(p_{-}-\pi_{-})(p_{-}-\pi_{+})

(1.1)

generates a nonlinear RFI with two equilibriums
\pi_{\pm}
. Completion of the construction of frequency RFI taking into account the boundary absorption states 0, 1 determines the increments of frequency probabilities:

V^{\pm}(p_{\pm})=\mp V\cdot p_{\pm}(1-p_{\pm})(p_{\pm}-\pi_{\pm})(p_{\pm}-\pi_{\mp})\ ,\ \ V>0.

(1.2)

There takes place a balance condition:

V^{+}(p_{+})+V^{-}(p_{-})\equiv 0\ ,\ \ p_{+}+p_{-}=1.

(1.3)

According to the principle of ”stimulation and restraint” , linear RFI can also be as follows:

\pi_{\pm}p_{+}+\pi_{\mp}p_{-}=\pm\pi(p_{+}-\overline{\pi}_{\mp})\ ,\ \ \overline{\pi}_{\mp}:=\mp{\pi}_{\mp}/\pi\ ,\ \ \pi:=\pi_{+}-\pi_{-}.

Without reducing the generality, we will consider
\pi:=\pi_{+}-\pi_{-}>0
. Then the equilibriums

\overline{\pi}_{\mp}=\mp{\pi}_{\mp}/\pi
are outside the interval
(0,\,1)
:

\overline{\pi}_{-}:=-\pi_{-}/\pi<0\ ,\ \ \overline{\pi}_{+}:=\pi_{+}/\pi>1.

Hence the equilibrium states determined by equilibriums
\overline{\pi}_{\mp}
, ”stimulate”  or ”restrain”  dynamics of frequency probabilities, without dividing the range of values - the interval
(0,\,1)
on additional areas of influence.
Such equilibriums are also considered in Maslov’s economic studies
[
4

5

The martingale property (by
k\geq 0
) of stochastic components (4.1) and (4.2)
allows to characterize them in more detail. Namely, the normalized martingales (4.1) and (4.2) are determined by Bernoulli distributions of possible frequency values
\nu_{N}^{\pm}
at a given distribution
p_{\pm}

\clubsuit
in frequency representation

\begin{split}&P\{\,\Delta\mu_{N}^{\pm}(p_{\pm})=\nu_{N}^{\pm}/N\,|\,S_{N}^{\pm}(k)=p_{\pm}\,\}\\
&=\frac{N!}{\nu_{N}^{+}!\nu_{N}^{-}!}(p_{+})^{\nu_{N}^{+}}(p_{-})^{\nu_{N}^{-}}\ ,\ \ \nu_{N}^{+}+\nu_{N}^{-}=N\ ,\ \ 0\leq\nu_{N}^{\pm}\leq N.\end{split}

(6.1)

\clubsuit
in binary representation

Conclusions

The statistical experiments determined by linear regression functions of increments, which reflect the basic principle of interaction ”stimulation - restraint”  are investigated.

It turns out that two linear growth regression functions have equilibriums in the interaction zone. The main attention is paid to statistical experiments with two equilibriums generated by the product of two linear regression functions of increments, with one of the equilibriums being attractive and the other repulsive.

Such a scheme of constructing the regression function of increments generates three zones of influence with two equilibriums.
