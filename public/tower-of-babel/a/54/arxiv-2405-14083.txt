Log motivic nearby cycles
Doosung Park

Abstract
. We define the log motivic nearby cycles functor. We show that this sends the motive of a proper smooth scheme over the fraction field of a DVR to the motive of the boundary of a log smooth model assuming absolute purity, which is unconditional in the equal characteristic case. In characteristic 0 0 , we show that the ∞ \infty -categories of motives over the standard log point and rigid analytic motives are equivalent, and we relate log motivic nearby cycles functor with Ayoub’s motivic nearby cycles functor.

Introduction

For simplicity of exposition,
we restrict to the DVR case in the introduction even though we can formulate the results in a more general context.
Let
\mathcal{O}_{K}
be a DVR with fraction field
K

Let

This is a motivic version of Grothendieck’s nearby cycles functor in
[
13
, Exposé XII]
,
which aims to understand the limit behavior at the “boundary”
\Spec(k)
of
\Spec(K)
.

Even though
\Psi^{\mathrm{Ayo}}
is defined without assuming that
\mathcal{O}_{K}
has equal characteristic
0
,
many theorems about
\Psi^{\mathrm{Ayo}}
assume this, e.g.,

[
1
, Théorème 3.3.46]
,
[
3
, Scholie 1.3.26]
,
[

]
, and
[
5
]
, or are proven for étale motives, e.g.,
[
2

.
A fundamental reason for this assumption is that
a log smooth model does not need to exist except in equal characteristic
0
even assuming resolution of singularities,
see Proposition
2.5
, Remark
2.6
, and
[
1
, Proposition 3.3.40, Remarque 3.3.41]
.
When working with the étale topology,
one can instead use the de Jong alterations in the mixed characteristic case,
see the proof of
[
2
, Théorème 10.9]
.

The analogue of
\Psi^{\mathrm{Ayo}}
for Grothendieck rings of varieties is
the motivic reduction map

\mathrm{MR}\colon\mathbf{K}(\mathrm{Var}_{K})\to\mathbf{K}(\mathrm{Var}_{k})

due to Nicaise-Shinder
[

,
whose construction was inspired by the motivic nearby fiber due to Denef-Loeser
[

]
and the motivic volume map due to Hrushovski-Kazhdan
[
17
]
.
Again, the equal characteristic

.

The purpose of this paper is to explore the motivic limit behavior using logarithmic geometry.
Let
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\dagger}
be the log ring
\mathcal{O}_{K}
with the standard log structure,
and let
k^{\dagger}
be the log ring
(k,\mathbb{N}\oplus k^{*})
,
whose spectrum is the standard log point.
The
log motivic nearby cycles functor
is

\Psi^{\log}:=i^{*}j_{*}\colon\mathrm{SH}(K)\to\mathrm{SH}(k^{\dagger}),

where
i\colon\Spec(k^{\dagger})\to\Spec(\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\dagger})
and
j\colon\Spec(K)\to\Spec(\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\dagger})
are the obvious strict immersions.
We refer to
[
24
, 3.1.5]
and
[
18
, (8.2.1)]
for the log nearby cycles functors in the non-motivic context.

For a proper smooth scheme
X
over
K
,
a

is a proper log smooth log scheme

over
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\dagger}
such that

.
In this case,

is vertical over
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\dagger}
.
We have noted that a log smooth model (with projective

) does not need to exist except in equal characteristic

.
However,

allows us to compute

(See Proposition

In particular,

Proposition 2.5

Remark 2.6
.

Proposition
2.5
does not hold in general if
X
is the spectrum of a DVR of equal characteristic

or mixed characteristic.
We only give an example for the equal positive characteristic case.
Assume that

is the spectrum of

with the standard log structure,
where

is a field of characteristic

.
Consider

,
which is finite étale over

.

If there exists a proper log smooth morphism

such that

,
then

is log étale.
Furthermore,

\underline{V}
is the spectrum of the integral closure of

in

Theorem 2.7

Let
