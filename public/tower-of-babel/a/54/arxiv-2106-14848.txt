Abstract
Let G G be a graph with vertex set V V , and let k k be a positive integer. A set D ⊆ V D\subseteq V is a distance- k k dominating set of G G if, for each vertex u ∈ V − D u\in V-D , there exists a vertex w ∈ D w\in D such that d ⁡ ( u , w ) ≤ k d(u,w)\leq k , where d ⁡ ( u , w ) d(u,w) is the minimum number of edges linking u u and w w in G G . Let d k ​ ( x , y ) = min ⁡ { d ⁡ ( x , y ) , k + 1 } d_{k}(x,y)=\min\{d(x,y),k+1\} . A set R ⊆ V R\subseteq V is a distance- k k resolving set of G G if, for any pair of distinct x , y ∈ V x,y\in V , there exists a vertex z ∈ R z\in R such that d k ​ ( x , z ) ≠ d k ​ ( y , z ) d_{k}(x,z)\neq d_{k}(y,z) . The distance- k k domination number γ k ​ ( G ) \gamma_{k}(G) ( distance- k k dimension dim k ( G ) \dim_{k}(G) , respectively) of G G is the minimum cardinality of all distance- k k dominating sets (distance- k k resolving sets, respectively) of G G . The distance- k k location-domination number , γ L k ​ ( G ) \gamma_{L}^{k}(G) , of G G is the minimum cardinality of all sets S ⊆ V S\subseteq V such that S S is both a distance- k k dominating set and a distance- k k resolving set of G G . Note that γ L 1 ​ ( G ) \gamma_{L}^{1}(G) is the well-known location-domination number introduced by Slater in 1988. For any connected graph G G of order n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 , we obtain the following sharp bounds: (1) γ k ​ ( G ) ≤ dim k ( G ) + 1 \gamma_{k}(G)\leq\dim_{k}(G)+1 ; (2) 2 ≤ γ k ​ ( G ) + dim k ( G ) ≤ n 2\leq\gamma_{k}(G)+\dim_{k}(G)\leq n ; (3) 1 ≤ max ⁡ { γ k ​ ( G ) , dim k ( G ) } ≤ γ L k ​ ( G ) ≤ min ⁡ { dim k ( G ) + 1 , n − 1 } 1\leq\max\{\gamma_{k}(G),\dim_{k}(G)\}\leq\gamma_{L}^{k}(G)\leq\min\{\dim_{k}(G)+1,n-1\} . We characterize G G for which γ L k ​ ( G ) ∈ { 1 , | V | − 1 } \gamma_{L}^{k}(G)\in\{1,|V|-1\} . We observe that dim k ( G ) γ k ​ ( G ) \frac{\dim_{k}(G)}{\gamma_{k}(G)} can be arbitrarily large. Moreover, for any tree T T of order n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 , we show that γ L k ​ ( T ) ≤ n − e ​ x ​ ( T ) \gamma_{L}^{k}(T)\leq n-ex(T) , where e ​ x ​ ( T ) ex(T) denotes the number of exterior major vertices of T T , and we characterize trees T T achieving equality. in

G
and each vertex of

A vertex subset

9

Slater

Theorem 3.1

Proposition 4.4
.

For

Proposition 4.5
.

For
m\geq 2
, let
G=K_{a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m}}
be a complete
m
-partite graph of order

\gamma_{L}^{k}(G)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\dim_{k}(G)+1=n-1&\mbox{ if }k=1\mbox{ and }G=K_{1,n-1},\\

Proof.

Let

be a complete

-partite graph of order

, where

, and let

. Note that
\textnormal{diam}(G)\in\{1,2\}
, where
\textnormal{diam}(G)=1
if and only if

and
\gamma_{L}^{k}(K_{n})=\dim_{k}(K_{n})=n-1
, for any

, by Theorems

(d) and
3.1
. If

, then
\gamma_{L}^{k}(G)=\dim_{k}(G)=\dim(G)
by Observations
4.1
and
4.2
(b). So, suppose

resolving set of

is also a distance-

dominating set of

. If

and
\gamma_{L}^{1}(K_{1,n-1})=n-1=\dim_{1}(K_{1,n-1})+1

Theorem 5.1
.

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
G
be a connected graph with
e\in E(G)
, and let
k\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
. Then

(a)

\gamma_{L}^{1}(G)-2\leq\gamma_{L}^{1}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{1}(G)+2

(b)

\gamma_{L}^{2}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{2}(G)+2

(c)

\gamma_{L}^{k}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{k}(G)+3

Proof.

Let
k\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
. By Theorem
3.1
, we have
\dim_{k}(G)\leq\gamma_{L}^{k}(G)\leq\dim_{k}(G)+1
and
\dim_{k}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{k}(G-e)\leq\dim_{k}(G-e)+1

For (a), note that
\gamma_{L}^{1}(G-e)-\gamma_{L}^{1}(G)\geq\dim_{1}(G-e)-(\dim_{1}(G)+1)\geq-2

\gamma_{L}^{1}(G)-\gamma_{L}^{1}(G-e)\geq\dim_{1}(G)-(\dim_{1}(G-e)+1)\geq-2

\gamma_{L}^{1}(G)-2\leq\gamma_{L}^{1}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{1}(G)+2

For (b), note that

\gamma_{L}^{2}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{2}(G)+2

For (c), for any

\gamma_{L}^{k}(G-e)\leq\gamma_{L}^{k}(G)+3
. ∎
