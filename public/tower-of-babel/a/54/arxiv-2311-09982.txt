Introduction

In this paper, we study the global in time existence and long time behavior for the initial value problem

(1.1)

\begin{cases}u_{t}+(b(t,x)u^{k+1})_{x}=u_{xx},\quad x\in\mathbb{R},\,t\in(0,\infty),\\

u_{0}

[

In this work we consider drifts

and exponent

(1)

(1.2)

u_{0}\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R});\end{cases}

(2)

has a weak derivative in

(1.3)

\begin{cases}b\in L^{\infty}_{\text{loc}}((0,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}),\\
b_{x}\in L^{\infty}_{\text{loc}}((0,\infty),L^{p,\infty}(\mathbb{R})),\ p\in(1,\infty],\end{cases}\begin{cases}k\geq\frac{1}{2},\\
u_{0}\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}),\\

1.1.
Structure of the paper

This article is organized as follows.

In Section
2
we introduce some definitions, notations, and well known results on Lorentz spaces (Section
2.1
): comparisons, embeddings, interpolations, Hölder’s inequalities and convolutions estimates. Since we are using multiplication/convolutions operators and embedding, the Lorentz space setting more or less gives the same estimates as for
L^{p}
. We also recall a special case of Gagliardo-Niremberg inequality and prove a useful estimate on the heat kernel.

In Section
3
we recall the local existence and uniqueness of the solutions via Duhamel’s principle. The assumption

and that

enters only in this section, and are needed if we let

to be unbounded. The results in this section are standard, and independent on the main theorems of the paper.

The main idea of the paper is contained in Section
4
, where we deal with the global existence of the solutions. Differently from the approach of
[
1
]
, we use a standard rescaling the solutions about the blow-up point at time
T
, Section
4.1
. By means of energy estimates for the truncated solution (Lemma
4.1
) and Gagliardo-Niremberg inequality (Lemma
4.2
), we show that in the new variables
(\tau,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{+}\times\mathbb{R}
that the rescaled solution decays the
L^{2}
-norm as the Heat kernel
\tau^{-\frac{1}{4}}
(Lemma
4.3
). This fact will lead to a uniform estimate on the
L^{2}
-norm of the original solution
u
(Corollary
4.4
), and by adapting the estimate
[
1
, Theorem 3.8]
to our case we deduce that
\|u\|_{\infty}
is bounded, Theorem
4.5
of Section
4.4
. This concludes the proof of the first part of Theorem
1.1
.

In Section
4.5
we study the critical cases, and show that the fact that the norm of
b
remains constant under rescaling leads to a uniform decay rate, Theorem
4.7
.

In Section
5
we provide examples of solutions blowing up in finite time for

above the critical value. The ideas are taken from
[
4
]
and adapted to our situation. Theorem
5.2
corresponds to the second part of Theorem
1.1
for

and

, while the other case

and

. As observed already in

In Section

we discuss the long behavior of solutions, proving Theorem

. The proof of the main result of this section, Theorem

2.1.
Lorentz spaces

We briefly recall the definition and some results about Lorentz space, see
[
3
]
.

Let
f^{*}
be the symmetric decreasing rearrangement defined by

f^{\ast}(x)=\inf\Big\{\alpha>0:\mathcal{L}^{1}\big(\big\{|f|>\alpha\big\}\big)|\leq x\Big\},

and let
f^{**}(x)
be the function defined by

f^{**}(x)=\frac{1}{x}\int_{0}^{x}f^{*}(y)dy.

Define

(2.1)

\|f\|_{p,q}=\begin{cases}\left(\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\big[x^{\frac{1}{p}}f^{\ast\ast}(x)\big]^{q}\,\frac{dx}{x}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}&q\in[1,\infty),p\in(1,\infty)\\
\sup\limits_{x>0}\,x^{\frac{1}{p}}f^{\ast\ast}(x)&q=\infty,p\in[1,\infty].\end{cases}

Note that

\|f\|_{1,\infty}=\|f^{*}\|_{1}=\|f\|_{1},\quad\|f\|_{\infty,\infty}=\lim_{x\searrow 0}f^{**}(x)=\|f\|_{\infty}.

By Hardy’s inequality

\bigg[\int_{0}^{\infty}\bigg(x^{1/p-1}\int_{0}^{x}|f(t)|dt\bigg)^{q}\frac{dx}{x}\bigg]^{1/q}\leq p^{\prime}\bigg[\int_{0}^{\infty}\big(x^{1/p}|f(x)|\big)^{q}\frac{dx}{x}\bigg]^{1/q},\quad\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1,\ p\in(1,\infty),

and some fairly easy computations, one can verify that for
1<p<\infty
the above definition is equivalent to

(2.2)

|||f|||_{p,q}=\begin{cases}\left(\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\big[x^{\frac{1}{p}}f^{*}(x)\big]^{q}\,\frac{dx}{x}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}&q\in[1,\infty),p\in[1,\infty),\\
\sup\limits_{x>0}\,x^{\frac{1}{p}}f^{*}(x)&q=\infty,p\in[1,\infty].\end{cases}

For
p=q=\infty
the equivalence is elementary. For
p=1,q=\infty
the quantity in (
2.2
) is the weak-
L^{1}
norm, while (
2.1
) corresponds to the
L^{1}
-space: the
L^{1}
-norm in the above definition is realized for
p=q=1
. It is elementary to deduce that for
p\in(1,\infty)

(2.3)

\|f^{k}\|_{p,q}\leq p^{\prime}|||f^{k}|||_{p,q}=p^{\prime}|||f|||_{kp,kq}^{k}\leq p^{\prime}\|f\|_{kp,kq}^{k}.

It is immediate to check that

\|f^{k}\|_{\infty,\infty}=\|f^{k}\|_{\infty}=\|f\|_{\infty}^{k}=\|f\|_{\infty,\infty}^{k}.

Definition 2.1

(Lorentz space)
.

The
Lorentz space
L^{p,q}
is the space of (equivalence classes of) measurable functions
f
such that
\|f\|_{p,q}<\infty
. It is a Banach space with the norm
\|\cdot\|_{p,q}
.

We will use the following results.

Proposition 2.2

(
[
3
, Lemma 2.2]
)
.

Let
1<p<\infty
, then

\|f\|_{p}\leq\|f\|_{p,p}\leq p^{\prime}\|f\|_{p},\qquad\text{where}\quad\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1.

Proposition 2.3

(
[
3
, Lemma 2.5]
)
.

Suppose
1<p<\infty
and
1\leq q<r\leq\infty
, then

\|f\|_{p,r}\leq\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{r}}\|f\|_{p,q}.

Another elementary estimate we use is the following.

Lemma 2.4
.

For
p_{1}<p<p_{2}
and
q,q_{1},q_{2}\geq 1
it holds

\|f\|_{p,q}\leq\bigg(\frac{1}{\frac{q}{p}-\frac{q}{p_{2}}}+\frac{1}{\frac{q}{p_{1}}-\frac{q}{p}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}\bigg(\frac{p}{q_{1}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q_{2}}\frac{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}}\bigg(\frac{p}{q_{2}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q_{1}}\frac{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p}}{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}}\|f\|_{p_{1},q_{1}}^{\frac{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}}\|f\|_{p_{2},q_{2}}^{\frac{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p}}{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}}.

Proof.

The fundamental estimate is that, being
f^{**}
decreasing, then

(2.4)

\frac{q}{p}\bar{x}^{\frac{q}{p}}(f^{**}(\bar{x}))^{q}\leq\int_{0}^{\bar{x}}\big[x^{\frac{1}{p}}f^{**}(x)\big]^{q}\frac{dx}{x}\leq\|f\|_{p,q}^{q}.

Hence we can write for
q<\infty

\begin{split}\|f\|^{q}_{p,q}&=\bigg[\int_{0}^{\bar{x}}+\int_{\bar{x}}^{\infty}\bigg]\big[x^{\frac{1}{p}}f^{**}(x)\big]^{q}\frac{dx}{x}\\
\big[\text{(\ref{Equa:f**_estima}) for $p_{1},p_{2}$}\big]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{\frac{q}{p}-\frac{q}{p_{2}}}\bigg(\frac{p}{q_{2}}\bigg)^{\frac{q}{q_{2}}}\|f\|^{q}_{p_{2},q_{2}}\bar{x}^{\frac{q}{p}-\frac{q}{p_{2}}}+\frac{1}{\frac{q}{p_{1}}-\frac{q}{p}}\bigg(\frac{p}{q_{1}}\bigg)^{\frac{q}{q_{1}}}\|f\|^{q}_{p_{1},q_{1}}x^{\frac{q}{p}-\frac{q}{p_{1}}}.\end{split}

One can directly check that the same estimate holds for
q=\infty
as

\|f\|_{p,\infty}\leq\bigg(\frac{p}{q_{2}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q_{2}}}\|f\|_{p_{2},q_{2}}\bar{x}^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}+\bigg(\frac{p}{q_{1}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q_{1}}}\|f\|_{p_{1},q_{1}}x^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{p_{1}}},

and similarly for
q_{1}=\infty
and/or
q_{2}=\infty
.

Optimizing w.r.t. to
\bar{x}
,

\bar{x}^{\frac{1}{p_{1}}-\frac{1}{p_{2}}}=\frac{\big(\frac{p}{q_{1}}\big)^{\frac{1}{q_{1}}}\|f\|_{p_{1},q_{1}}}{\big(\frac{p}{q_{2}}\big)^{\frac{1}{q_{2}}}\|f\|_{p_{2},q_{2}}},

one obtains the statement.
∎

Theorem 2.5

(Hölder’s inequality in Lorentz spaces,
[
3
, Theorem 3.4, Theorem 3.5]
)
.

If

\|fg\|_{p,q}{\leq p^{\prime}}\|f\|_{p_{1},q_{1}}\|g\|_{p_{2},q_{2}}.

If

then

\|fg\|_{1}\leq\|f\|_{p_{1},q_{1}}\|g\|_{p_{2},g_{2}}.

Corollary 2.6
.

If

,

|f(x)-f(x^{\prime})|\leq p^{\prime}\|f\|_{p,\infty}|x-x^{\prime}|^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}.

For

We have by the last formula of Theorem

\begin{split}|f(x)|&=\bigg|\int f_{x}(x^{\prime})1\!\!\mathrm{I}_{(0,x)}dx^{\prime}\bigg|\\
&\leq\|f_{x}\|_{p,\infty}\|1\!\!\mathrm{I}_{(0,x)}\|_{1-\frac{1}{p},1}=\|f_{x}\|_{p,\infty}p^{\prime}|x|^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}.\qed\end{split}

Theorem 2.7

(
[
3

)

If

2.3.
Heat kernel

Recall that the heat kernel in
\mathbb{R}
is

G(t,x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi t}}e^{-{x^{2}/(4t)}}.

In the following we will use the estimate below.

Proposition 2.9
.

It holds

\|G_{x}\|_{p,1}\leq Cp^{\prime}t^{{1}/{2p}-1}
.

Proof.

We estimate

|G_{x}(t)|\leq\frac{C}{t}\mathbf{1}_{[-\sqrt{2t},\sqrt{2t}]}(x)+\mathbf{1}_{\mathbb{R}\setminus[-\sqrt{2t},\sqrt{2t}]}(x)|G_{x}(t)|,

thus

\begin{split}\|G_{x}(t)\|_{p,1}&=\int\bigg[x^{-1-1/p^{\prime}}\int_{0}^{x}(G_{x}(t,y))^{*}dy\bigg]dx\simeq p^{\prime}\int G^{*}_{x}(t,y)y^{-1/p^{\prime}}dy\simeq p^{\prime}t^{\frac{1}{2p}-1}.\qed\end{split}

3.
Local existence and uniqueness

For the sake of completeness, in this section we prove some classical results of local existence and uniqueness for
L^{\infty}\cap L^{1}
-solutions of (
1.1
). Define the integral operator
\Phi[u]
by Duhamel formula

\begin{split}u\mapsto\Phi[u](t)&=G(t)\ast u_{0}+\int_{0}^{t}G_{x}(t-s)\ast(b(s)u^{k}(s))\,ds.\end{split}

Notice that
\|u(t)\|_{1}=\|u_{0}\|_{1}
, as required being the PDE in conservation form.

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
b\in L^{\infty}_{t}L^{p,\infty}_{x}
,
k\geq 0
,
u_{0}\in L^{\infty}\cap L^{1}(\mathbb{R})
and
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
. Then
u\mapsto\Phi[u]
is a contraction in the set

S=\left\{u\in L^{\infty}([0,\bar{t}]\times\mathbb{R})\cap C([0,\bar{t}],L^{p^{\prime}}(\mathbb{R})):\|u\|_{\infty}\leq r,\,u(0)=u_{0}\right\},

with
\bar{t}
sufficiently small and
r\geq 2\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}
. In particular, there is a unique bounded solution for (
1.1
), and it belongs to the space
L^{\infty}((0,\bar{t})\times\mathbb{R})\cap C([0,\bar{t}],L^{p^{\prime}}(\mathbb{R}))
.

We first prove that
t\mapsto\Phi[u](t)
is continuous in
L^{\infty}
when
u\in S
and
t>0
: this will be useful later on, and shows also the continuity of
t\mapsto\Phi[u](t)
in every integral norm
\|\cdot\|_{q}
,
q\in[1,\infty]
for
t>0
.

Lemma 3.2
.

For every
u\in L^{\infty}_{t,x}((0,\bar{t})\times\mathbb{R})
the function
t\mapsto\Phi[u](t)
is continuous in
L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})
for
t>0
.

Proof.

Compute

\begin{split}\|u(t+\delta)-u(t)\|_{\infty}&=\bigg\|(G(t+\delta)-G(t))\ast u_{0}+\int_{0}^{t+\delta}G(t+\delta-s)\ast b(s)u^{1+k}(s)ds\\
&\qquad-\int_{0}^{t}G(t-s)\ast b(s)u^{1+k}(s)ds\bigg\|_{\infty}\\
\big[\text{Theorem \ref{Theo:holder_lorentz}}\big]\quad&\leq\|G(t+\delta)-G(t)\|_{1}\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+C\int_{t}^{t+\delta}\|G(t+\delta-s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}\|b(s)u^{1+k}(s)\|_{p,\infty}ds\\
&\qquad+C\int_{0}^{t}\|G(t+\delta-s)-G(t-s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}\|b(s)u^{1+k}(s)\|_{p,\infty}ds\\
\big[\text{Proposition \ref{prop:appx}}\big]\quad&\leq\|G(t+\delta)-G(t)\|_{1}\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}\\
&\quad+C\|b\|_{p,\infty}\|u\|_{\infty}^{k+1}\bigg(\delta^{\frac{1}{2p^{\prime}}}+\int_{0}^{t}\|G(t+\delta-s)-G(t-s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}ds\bigg).\end{split}

The last terms converge to
0
as
\delta\to 0
uniformly in every interval of the form
[t_{0},t_{1}]
,
t_{0}>0
.
∎

Proof of Proposition
3.1
.

We start by deducing the uniform continuity in
L^{1}
as
t\to 0
: this will give the continuity in time for
\|\cdot\|_{q}
,
q\in[1,\infty)
. From Theorem
2.7
in the first inequality we obtain

\begin{split}\|u(t)-u_{0}\|_{1}&\leq\|G(t)\ast u_{0}-u_{0}\|_{1}+\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{1}\|b(s)u^{1+k}(s)\|_{1}ds\\
\big[\text{Theorem \ref{Theo:holder_lorentz}}\big]\quad&\leq\|G(t)\ast u_{0}-u_{0}\|_{1}+\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{1}\|b(s)\|_{p,\infty}\|u^{1+k}(s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}ds\\
\big[\text{Lemma \ref{Lem:triv_estim_p_p'}}\big]&\leq\|G(t)\ast u_{0}-u_{0}\|_{1}+C\|b\|_{p,\infty}r^{k+1/p}\int_{0}^{t}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-s}}ds,\end{split}

which converges to
0
uniformly once
u_{0}
is fixed. Hence, by Lemma
3.2
above,
t\mapsto\Phi[u](t)
is uniformly continuous in
L^{1}
.

We next prove that
\Phi(S)\subset S
: indeed using again Theorem
2.5
in the first inequality, it holds

\begin{split}\|\Phi[u](t)\|_{\infty}&\leq\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}\|b(s)u^{k+1}(s)\|_{p,\infty}\,ds\\
&\leq\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}\|b(s)\|_{p,\infty}\|u(s)\|_{\infty}^{k+1}\,ds\\
\big[\text{Proposition }\ref{prop:appx}\big]\quad&\leq\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+Ct^{\frac{1}{2p^{\prime}}}\underset{s\in[0,t]}{\textnormal{ess-sup}}\,\|u(s)\|^{k+1}_{\infty}\\
\big[2\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}\leq r\big]\quad&\leq\frac{r}{2}+C\bar{t}^{\frac{1}{2p^{\prime}}}r^{k+1}.\end{split}

Taking
\bar{t}\ll 1
it holds that
\Phi(S)\subset S
.

Finally the same computations show that
\Phi[u]
is a contraction in
C([0,\bar{t}],L^{p^{\prime},1}(\mathbb{R}))
:

\begin{split}\|\Phi[u](t)-\Phi[v](t)\|_{p^{\prime},1}&\leq C\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}\|b(s)\|_{p,\infty}\|u^{1+k}(s)-v^{1+k}(s)\|_{p^{\prime},1}ds\\
&\leq C\|b\|_{p,\infty}t^{\frac{1}{2p^{\prime}}}r^{k}\|u-v\|_{C_{t}L^{p^{\prime},1}_{x}},\end{split}

so that for
\bar{t}\ll 1
the statement follows. The case
p=\infty
follows by Hölder’s inequality because
\|b\|_{\infty,\infty}=\|b\|_{\infty}
, and gives a contraction in
C_{t}L^{1}_{x}
.
∎

For the second case, i.e. Conditions (
1.3
), we start by proving that the second moment
M_{2}(u(t))
is bounded if the solution
u
of (
1.1
) belongs to
L^{\infty}_{t,x}
.

Lemma 3.3
.

If
\|u\|_{L^{\infty}_{t,x}}\leq r
, then

\int(1+x^{2})|u(t)|dx\leq\bigg(\int(1+x^{2})|u_{0}|dx\bigg)e^{C(1+r^{k})t}.

Proof.

Using the estimates by Corollary
2.6

\begin{split}&|b(x)|\leq\|b_{x}\|_{p,\infty}x^{{1/p^{\prime}}}+|b(0)|\leq C\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\leq C(1+|x|),\end{split}

by (
1.1
) it holds

\begin{split}\frac{d}{dt}\int(1+x^{2})u\,dx&=\int(1+x^{2})(u_{x}-bu^{k+1})_{x}dx\\
&=-2\int xu_{x}dx+\int 2xbu^{k+1}dx\\
&\leq 2\int u\,dx+2\int|xb|u^{k+1}\,dx\\
&\leq 2\|u\|_{1}+C\int(1+x^{2})u^{k+1}\,dx\\
&\leq C(1+\|u\|_{\infty}^{k})\int(1+x^{2})|u|dx.\end{split}

Since
\|u\|_{\infty}\leq r
, one integrates the differential inequality to obtain the statement.
∎

Corollary 3.4
.

If
u(t)\in L^{\infty}
and
M_{2}(u_{0})<\infty
, then
t\mapsto\Phi[u](t)
is uniformly continuous in the interval
t\in[t_{0},t_{1}]
with
t_{0}>0
,

Proof.

Following the same line as in Lemma
3.2
above,

\begin{split}\|&u(t+\delta)-u(t)\|_{\infty}\\
&\leq\|G(t+\delta)-G(t)\|_{1}\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+\int_{0}^{\delta}\|G_{x}(\delta-s)\|_{2}\|b(t+s)u^{1+k}(t+s)\|_{2}ds\\
&\quad+\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t+\delta-s)-G_{x}(t-s)\|_{2}\|b(s)u^{1+k}(s)\|_{2}ds\\
&\leq\|G(t+\delta)-G(t)\|_{1}\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+C\Big(\|u^{1+k}\|_{L^{2}_{t,x}}+\sup_{[t_{0},t_{1}]}E(u(t))^{\frac{1}{2}}\|u\|_{\infty}^{\frac{1}{2}+k}\Big)\delta^{\frac{1}{4}}\\
&\quad+C\Big(\|u^{1+k}\|_{2}+\sup_{[t_{0},t_{1}]}E(u(t))^{\frac{1}{2}}\|u\|_{\infty}^{\frac{1}{2}+k}\Big)\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t+\delta-s)-G_{x}(t-s)\|_{2}ds,\end{split}

where we recall that
E(u)
is the second moment of
u
. As in the previous case, the r.h.s. converges to
0
uniformly for
t\geq t_{0}
.
∎

Proposition 3.5
.

Assume that
b(t,x=0)
is uniformly bounded,
b_{x}\in L^{\infty}_{t}L^{p,\infty}_{x}
,
k\geq 0
and
u_{0}\in L^{\infty}\cap L^{1}(\mathbb{R})
with bounded second order moment
E(u_{0})
. Then
u\mapsto\Phi[u]
is a contraction in the set

S=\Big\{u\in L^{\infty}([0,\bar{t}]\times\mathbb{R})\cap C([0,\bar{t}],L^{2}(\mathbb{R})),\|u\|_{\infty}\leq r,u(0)=u_{0}\Big\},

with
\bar{t}
sufficiently small and
r>2\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}
. In particular, there is only one bounded solution for (
1.1
), and it belongs to the space
C([0,\bar{t}],L^{2}(\mathbb{R}))
.

Proof.

Following the same line of the proof of Proposition
3.1
we study first the continuity for
t\searrow 0
:

\begin{split}\|u(t)-u_{0}\|_{1}&\leq\|G(t)\ast u_{0}-u_{0}\|_{1}+\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{1}\|b(s)u^{1+k}(s)\|_{1}ds\\
&\leq\|G(t)\ast u_{0}-u_{0}\|_{1}+C\int_{0}^{t}\|G_{x}(t-s)\|_{1}\big[(\|b(s,0)\|_{\infty}+1)\|u^{k}(s)\|_{\infty}+\|u^{k}(s)\|_{\infty}E(u(s))\big]ds\\
&\leq\|G(t)\ast u_{0}-u_{0}\|_{1}+Cr^{k}\big[(\|b(x=0)\|_{\infty}+1)+\sup_{[0,t]}E(u_{0})\big]\sqrt{t},\end{split}

which converges to
0
as
t\to 0
. Hence, together with Corollary
3.4
we obtain the uniform continuity in every
L^{p}
-space.

Next,

\begin{split}\|\Phi[u](\bar{t})\|_{\infty}&\leq\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\|G_{x}(\bar{t}-s)\|_{2}\|b(s)u^{k+1}(s)\|_{2}\,ds\\
&\leq\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+C\big(\|b(x=0)\|_{L^{\infty}_{t}}+1+\sup_{[0,\bar{t}]}E(u(t))^{\frac{1}{2}}\big)\|u\|_{\infty}^{k+\frac{1}{2}}\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\|G_{x}(\bar{t}-s)\|_{2}ds\\
&\leq\|u_{0}\|_{\infty}+C(1+e^{C\bar{t}(1+r^{k})/2})r^{k+\frac{1}{2}}\bar{t}^{\frac{1}{4}}\\
&\leq\frac{r}{2}+C(1+e^{C\bar{t}(1+r^{k})/2})r^{k+\frac{1}{2}}\bar{t}^{\frac{1}{4}},\end{split}

Taking
\bar{t}\ll 1
it holds that
\Phi(S)\subset S
.

Finally, for positive solutions,

\begin{split}\int(1+x^{2})\big(\Phi[u](\bar{t},x)-\Phi[v](\bar{t},x)\big)&\leq\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\|G_{x}(\bar{t}-s)\|_{2}\|b(s)(u^{1+k}(s)-v^{1+k}(s))\|_{1}ds\\
&\leq C\Big((\|b(0)\|_{L^{\infty}_{t}}+1)(\|u\|_{L^{\infty}_{t,x}}^{k-\frac{1}{2}}+\|v\|_{L^{\infty}_{t,x}}^{k-\frac{1}{2}})\Big)\|u-v\|_{C_{t}L^{2}_{x}}\bar{t}^{\frac{1}{4}}\\
&\quad+\big(E(u)^{1/2}\|u\|^{k-\frac{1}{2}}_{L^{\infty}_{t,x}}+E(v)^{1/2}\|v\|^{k-\frac{1}{2}}_{L^{\infty}_{t,x}}\big)\|u-v\|_{C_{t}L^{2}_{x}}\bar{t}^{\frac{1}{4}},\end{split}

so that for
\bar{t}\ll 1

\Phi
is a contraction.
∎

Remark 3.6
.

The condition
E(u_{0})<+\infty
that we used in Proposition
3.5
will not play a role in the rest of the paper, in the sense that the estimates obtained are independent on
E(u_{0})
. The same can be said for the condition
k\geq\frac{1}{2}
. Clearly for the blow-up it is more interesting to study the PDE for large
k
.

4.
Global existence in the subcritical and critical case

In order to prove the global existence we consider a standard rescaling about a possible blow-up at point
(T,\hat{x})
, where w.l.o.g. we assume that
\hat{x}=0
. We will show that the rescaled solutions decrease to
0
with the appropriate speed in
L^{2}
-norm at time
T
in the critical and subcritical case, so that the original unscaled solution is bounded by using the estimates contained in
[
1
]
.

4.1.
Rescaled variables

Set

t=T(1-e^{-\tau}),

and define

\begin{split}v(\tau,y)&=\sqrt{T}e^{-\tau/2}u\Big(T(1-e^{-\tau}),\sqrt{Te^{-\tau}}y\Big),\\
\tilde{b}(\tau,y)&=(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-k}{2}}b\Big(T(1-e^{-\tau}),\sqrt{Te^{-\tau}}y\Big).\end{split}

The rescaled equation for
v
is then

v_{\tau}+\frac{1}{2}(yv)_{y}+(\tilde{b}v^{1+k})_{y}=v_{yy},\quad v(\tau=0)=\sqrt{T}u_{0}(\sqrt{T}y).

We observe that

\|\tilde{b}(\tau)\|_{p,\infty}=(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-k-1/p}{2}}\big\|b(T(1-e^{-\tau}))\big\|_{p,\infty},

hence in particular in the critical and subcritical case it holds

(4.1)

\sup_{\tau>0}\|\tilde{b}(\tau)\|_{p,\infty}\leq T^{\frac{1-1/p-k}{2}}\sup_{t\in(0,T)}\|b(t)\|_{p,\infty}\leq CT^{\frac{1-1/p-k}{2}},\qquad\text{when}\quad k\leq 1-\frac{1}{p}=\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}.

In the same way, for
b_{x}\in L^{p,\infty}(\mathbb{R})

(4.2)

\|\tilde{b}_{y}(\tau)\|_{p,\infty}=(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-k-1/p}{2}}\big\|b(T(1-e^{-\tau}))\big\|_{p,\infty},

and then

(4.3)

\sup_{\tau>0}\|\tilde{b}_{y}(\tau)\|_{p,\infty}\leq T^{\frac{2-1/p-k}{2}}\sup_{t\in(0,T)}\|b_{x}(t)\|_{p,\infty}\leq CT^{\frac{2-1/p-k}{2}},\qquad\text{for}\quad k\leq 2-1/p.

Moreover we observe that

(4.4)

\|v(\tau)\|_{2}^{2}=\sqrt{T}e^{-\frac{\tau}{2}}\|u(T(1-e^{-\tau}))\|_{2}^{2}.

4.2.
Entropy dissipation

If
\eta:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is a smooth
C^{1,1}
-function, then

(4.5)

\begin{split}\frac{d}{d\tau}\int\eta(v)&=\int\eta^{\prime}v_{\tau}=\int\eta^{\prime}(v)v_{yy}+\int\eta^{\prime}\bigg(-\frac{1}{2}yv-{\tilde{b}}v^{k+1}\bigg)_{y}.\end{split}

Here we consider the entropy
\eta_{a}
given by

\eta_{a}(v)=\begin{cases}{v^{2}/2}&0\leq v\leq a,\\
a\left(v-{a}/{2}\right)&a<v<\infty,\end{cases}

and denote

v_{a}=v\wedge a.

The parameter
a
will be chosen later on to be sufficiently small.

Lemma 4.1
.

Assume the exponent are critical or subcritical, i.e. Conditions (
1.2
) with
k\leq 1-\frac{1}{p}
or Conditions (
1.3
) and
k\leq 2-\frac{1}{p}
. Then for every time
\bar{\tau}
there exists a constant
a=a(p,k,\frac{e^{\bar{\tau}}}{T})
such that it holds

(4.6)

\frac{d}{d\tau}\int\eta_{a}(v(\tau,y))\,dy\leq-\frac{\|v_{a,y}(\tau)\|^{2}_{2}}{2}-\frac{\|v_{a}(\tau)\|_{2}^{2}}{8}

for
\tau\geq\bar{\tau}
. If
k=1-\frac{1}{p}
in (
1.2
) or
k=2-\frac{1}{p}
in (
1.3
) then
a=a(p,k)
is independent on
\bar{\tau},T
.

Proof.

By integration by parts, Equation (
4.5
) becomes

(4.7)

\frac{d}{d\tau}\int\eta_{a}(v)dy=-\|v_{a,y}\|^{2}_{2}-\frac{1}{4}\|v_{a}\|^{2}_{2}+\int v_{a,y}\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}.

Assume first Conditions (
1.2
), and
let

\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=\frac{1}{2},\quad 2<p<\infty.

Fixed
p>2
, choose
2<\tilde{p}^{\prime}<p^{\prime}
such that
(1+k)\tilde{p}^{\prime}>2
(so the constants below do not depend on
\tilde{p}^{\prime}
)
and compute

(4.8)

\begin{split}\bigg|\int v_{a,y}\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\bigg|&\leq\|\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\|_{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\big[\text{Proposition \ref{prop:equiv_L_p}}\big]\quad&\leq\|\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\|_{2,2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\big[\text{Theorem \ref{Theo:holder_lorentz}}\big]\quad&\leq C\|\tilde{b}\|_{p,\infty}\|v_{a}^{k+1}\|_{p^{\prime},2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\big[\text{(\ref{Equa:estima_scal}) and (\ref{Equa:tildeb_Lp:subcr})}\big]\quad&\leq C(p)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v_{a}\|_{(k+1)p^{\prime},(k+1)2}^{k+1}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\big[\text{Proposition \ref{prop:inclusion_lorentz} and Lemma \ref{Lem:triv_estim_p_p'}, $p^{\prime}>\tilde{p}^{\prime}>2$}\big]\quad&\leq C(p)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v\|_{\infty}^{(k+1)(1-\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a}\|_{(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime},(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}^{(k+1)\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\big[\text{Proposition \ref{prop:equiv_L_p}}\big]\quad&\leq C(p)T^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v\|_{\infty}^{(k+1)(1-\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\|v_{a}\|_{(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}^{(k+1)\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\Big[\|v_{a}\|_{(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}\leq C(p,k)\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}}\Big]\quad&\leq C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v\|_{\infty}^{(k+1)(1-\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{(\frac{k+1}{2}-\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}})\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}+1}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{(\frac{k+1}{2}+\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}})\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\\
\Big[\|v_{a}\|_{\infty}\leq\|v_{a}\|_{1}^{\frac{1}{3}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{\frac{2}{3}}\Big]\quad&\leq C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{(k+1)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}+1}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{(\frac{k+1}{2}+\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}})\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\\
\bigg[\alpha\beta\leq\frac{\alpha^{\gamma}}{2}+2^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}}\beta^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}},\gamma=\frac{2}{(k+1)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}+1}\bigg]&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{1-(k+1)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2\frac{\frac{k+1}{2}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}{1-(k+1)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}\\
\big[\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}\leq\|v_{a}\|_{\infty}\|v_{a}\|_{1}\leq a\big]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{1-(k+1)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}\\
&\qquad\cdot a^{\frac{\frac{k+1}{3}(2+\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-1}{1-(k+1)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}\\
\bigg[a=\frac{1}{\bar{C}(p,k)}\bigg(\frac{e^{\bar{\tau}}}{T}\bigg)^{\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}-k}{\frac{k+1}{3}(2+\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-1}}\bigg]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{8}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}.\end{split}

Substituting (
4.8
) into (
4.7
) we get (
4.6
).

The case
p=\infty
, i.e.
b\in L^{\infty}_{t,x}
, can be treated in a much simpler way, because
\|\tilde{b}\|_{\infty,\infty}=\|b\|_{\infty}
: by following the same lines as above

\begin{split}\bigg|\int v_{a,y}\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\bigg|&\leq\|\tilde{b}\|_{\infty}\|v_{a}^{k+1}\|_{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
&\leq C(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-k}{2}}\|v_{a}\|_{(k+1)2}^{k+1}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}\\
\big[\|v_{a}\|_{2(k+1)}\leq C(k)\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{2(k+1)}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2(k+1)}}\big]\quad&\leq C(k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-k}{2}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{1+\frac{k}{2}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{1+\frac{k}{2}}\\
\bigg[\alpha\beta\leq\frac{1}{2}\alpha^{\gamma}+2^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}}\beta^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}},\gamma=\frac{2}{1+\frac{k}{2}}\bigg]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+C(k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-k}{1-\frac{k}{2}}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2\frac{1+\frac{k}{2}}{1-\frac{k}{2}}}\\
&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1-k}{1-\frac{k}{2}}}a^{\frac{2k}{1-\frac{k}{2}}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}\\
\bigg[a=\frac{1}{\bar{C}(k)}\bigg(\frac{e^{\bar{\tau}}}{T}\bigg)^{\frac{1-k}{2k}}\bigg]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{8}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}.\end{split}

Assume instead (
1.3
) and
k\leq 2-\frac{1}{p}
: using here
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
,
1<p<\infty
, and a fixed
1<\tilde{p}^{\prime}<p^{\prime}
such that
(2+k)\tilde{p}^{\prime}>2
, we have

(4.9)

\begin{split}\bigg|\int v_{a,y}\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\bigg|&\leq\frac{1}{k+2}\bigg|\int\tilde{b}_{y}v_{a}^{k+2}\bigg|\\
\big[\text{Theorem \ref{Theo:holder_lorentz}}\big]\quad&\leq C(k)\|\tilde{b}_{y}\|_{p,\infty}\|v_{a}^{k+2}\|_{p^{\prime},1}\\
\big[\text{(\ref{Equa:tildeby_Lp}),(\ref{Equa:estima_scal})}\big]\quad&\leq C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v_{a}\|_{(k+2)p^{\prime},k+2}^{k+2}\\
\big[\text{Proposition \ref{prop:inclusion_lorentz} and Lemma \ref{Lem:triv_estim_p_p'}, $1<\tilde{p}^{\prime}<p^{\prime}$}\big]\quad&\leq C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v\|_{\infty}^{(k+2)(1-\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\|v_{a}\|_{(k+2)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}^{(k+2)\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\\
\Big[\|v_{a}\|_{(k+2)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}\leq C(p,k)\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{(k+2)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{(k+2)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}}\Big]\quad&\leq C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v\|_{\infty}^{(k+2)(1-\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{(\frac{k+2}{2}-\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}})\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{(\frac{k+2}{2}+\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}})\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\\
\big[\|v_{a}\|_{\infty}\leq\|v_{a}\|_{1}^{\frac{1}{3}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{\frac{2}{3}}\big]\quad&\leq C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{(k+2)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{(\frac{k+2}{2}+\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}})\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}}\\
\bigg[\alpha\beta\leq\frac{1}{2}\alpha^{\gamma}+2^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}}\beta^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}},\gamma=\frac{2}{(k+2)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}\bigg]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+C(p,k)(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2-(k+2)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}\\
&\qquad\cdot\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2\frac{\frac{k+2}{2}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}{2-(k+2)(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{6}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}\\

\bigg[a\leq\frac{1}{\bar{C}(p,k)}\bigg(\frac{e^{\tau}}{T}\bigg)^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{\frac{k+2}{3}(2+\frac{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})-2}}\bigg]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{8}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}.\end{split}

so that (

Again the case

\begin{split}\bigg|\int v_{a,y}\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\bigg|&\leq\frac{1}{k+2}\bigg|\int\tilde{b}_{y}v_{a}^{k+2}\bigg|\\

\bigg[a=\frac{1}{\bar{C}(k)}\bigg(\frac{e^{\bar{\tau}}}{T}\bigg)^{\frac{2-k}{2k}}\bigg]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{8}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2},\end{split}

This concludes the proof.
∎

Set now

e^{\bar{\tau}}=T,\quad\bar{a}=a\bigg(p,k,\frac{e^{\bar{\tau}}}{T}\bigg)=a\big(p,k,1),

Lemma 4.2

Proof.

We consider the case

, the other case being completely similar. By Lemma

and Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality we have for

1\leq\bar{x}\leq\bigg(\frac{\beta+k-1}{\alpha+k-1}\bigg)^{\frac{k}{\beta-\alpha}}.

Proposition 5.1
.

Fix a positive measurable function
f:\mathbb{R}\to[0,\infty)
.

(1)

If

(5.3)

\left(\frac{k}{k-(1-\alpha)}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1+\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1+\alpha}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1+\alpha}}\leq\bar{x},

then

(5.4)

m\leq 2\left(\frac{k}{k-(1-\alpha)}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1+\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1+\alpha}}E^{\frac{k-(\alpha+1)}{3k+1+\alpha}}.

(2)

If

(5.5)

\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1-\beta}}\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1\beta)}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1+\beta}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1-\beta}}\geq x,

then

(5.6)

m\leq 2\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\beta)}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1+\beta}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1+\beta}}E^{\frac{k-(1-\beta)}{3k+1+\beta}}.

Proof.

Case 1.
We compute

\begin{split}m&=\int fdx=\int_{-R}^{R}fdx+\int_{|x|>r}fdx\\
&\leq\int_{-R}^{R}f|x|^{\frac{1-\alpha}{k+1}}\frac{1}{|x|^{\frac{1-\alpha}{k+1}}}dx+\frac{1}{R^{2}}\int_{|x|>R}|x|^{2}fdx\\
&\leq\left(\int_{-R}^{R}f^{k+1}|x|^{1-\alpha}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\int_{-R}^{R}\left(\frac{1}{|x|}\right)^{\frac{1-\alpha}{k}}\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{R^{2}}E\\
\big[\text{if }R\leq\bar{x}\big]\quad&\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}}f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\alpha)}\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}R^{\frac{k-(1-\alpha)}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{R^{2}}E.\\
\end{split}

Choosing

R=\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\alpha)}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1+\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1+\alpha}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1+\alpha}}\leq\bar{x},

we obtain

\begin{split}m&\leq 2\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\alpha)}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1+\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1-\alpha}}E^{\frac{k-(1-\alpha)}{3k+1+\alpha}},\end{split}

which is estimate (
5.4
).

Case 2.
Similarly to the previous case, we compute

\begin{split}m&=\int fdx=\int_{|x|\leq M}fdx+\int_{|x|>M}fdx\\
&\leq\int_{|x|\leq M}f|xb|^{\frac{1}{1+k}}\frac{1}{|xb|^{\frac{1}{1+k}}}dx+\frac{1}{M^{2}}E\\
\big[\text{if }\bar{x}\leq M\big]\quad&\leq\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\int_{|x|\leq\bar{x}}\left(\frac{1}{|x|}\right)^{\frac{1-\alpha}{k}}dx+\int_{\bar{x}<|x|\leq M}\left(\frac{1}{|x|}\right)^{\frac{1-\beta}{k}}\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{M^{2}}E\\
\big[(\ref{Equa:bar_x_cond})\big]\quad&\leq\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\beta)}\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}M^{\frac{k-(1-\beta)}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{M^{2}}E.\end{split}

Choosing

M:=\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\beta)}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1+\beta}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1+\beta}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1+\beta}},

we conclude that

\begin{split}m&\leq 2\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\beta)}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1+\beta}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1+\beta}}E^{\frac{k-(1-\beta)}{3k+1+\beta}},\end{split}

which is (
5.6
).
∎

Theorem 5.2
.

Assume (
1.2
) and
k>1-\frac{1}{p}
. If
E(u_{0})
is sufficiently small and
b
satisfying (
5.1
) and (
5.2
),
then the solution of (
1.1
) blows up in finite time.

Proof.

The choice of the constant
\bar{x}
covers both cases (
5.3
) and (
5.5
): indeed

\bar{x}^{3k+1+\beta}\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\beta)}\right)^{k}\leq\left(-\int u^{k+1}xb\right)^{-1}E^{k+1}\leq\bar{x}^{3k+1+\alpha}\left(\frac{2k}{k-(1-\alpha)}\right)^{k},

if and only if (
5.2
) holds.
Then by Proposition
5.1
and computing
\frac{dE}{dt}
by (
1.1
), one obtains

\begin{split}\frac{dE}{dt}&\leq 2m-2\int xbu^{k+1}dx\\
&\leq 2m-C\min\bigg\{\frac{m^{\frac{3k+1+\alpha}{2}}}{E^{\frac{k-(1-\alpha)}{2}}},\frac{m^{\frac{3k+1+\beta}{2}}}{E^{\frac{k-(1-\beta)}{2}}}\bigg\}.\end{split}

The exponents

\frac{k-(1-\alpha)}{2},\frac{k-(1-\beta)}{2}>0

by the choice of
\alpha,\beta
, which gives the blow-up in finite time because of the ODE

\dot{y}=a-\frac{b}{y^{\gamma}},\quad\gamma>0

has a solution converging to
0
in finite time like

y(t)\simeq(T-t)^{\frac{1}{1+\gamma}}

if the initial data is
<(b/a)^{1/\gamma}
. This last condition reads as

E(t=0)\lesssim\min\big\{m^{\frac{3k+\alpha-2}{k-(1-\alpha)}},m^{\frac{3k+\beta-2}{k-(1-\beta)}}\big\}.

The final observation is that since
m(u)
is constant, the fact that
E(u)\to 0
forces
u
to blow up in
L^{\infty}
as the Hölder inequality implies.
∎

5.2.
Case (
1.3
)

Let
\alpha\in(0,1]
,
k>1+\alpha
and consider a smooth odd bounded function
b
with
b_{x}\in L^{p}
such that for

(5.7)

-\min\{x^{\alpha},(\bar{x})^{\alpha}\big\}\leq b(x)\leq-\min\{x^{\alpha},(2\bar{x})^{\alpha}\},\quad\bar{x}\geq 1,

and
(1-\alpha)p<1
.

Proposition 5.3
.

For every measurable positive function
f:\mathbb{R}\to[0,\infty)
the following holds.

(1)

If

(5.8)

\left(\frac{2k}{k-(\alpha+1)}\right)^{-\frac{k}{k+1-\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1-\alpha}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1-\alpha}}\leq\bar{x},

then

m\leq 2\left(\frac{2k}{k-(\alpha+1)}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1-\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1-\alpha}}E^{\frac{k-(\alpha+1)}{3k+1-\alpha}}.

(2)

If

(5.9)

\left(\frac{2k}{k-1}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1}}\geq\bar{x},

then

m\leq 2\left(\frac{2k}{k-1}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1}}E^{\frac{k-1}{3k+1}}.

Proof.

Case 1.
Define

(5.10)

R^{\prime}:=\left(\frac{2k}{k-(\alpha+1)}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1-\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1-\alpha}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1-\alpha}},

and compute by Hölder inequality

\begin{split}m&=\int fdx=\int_{|x|\leq R^{\prime}}fdx+\int_{|x|>R^{\prime}}fdx\\
&\leq\left(\int f^{k+1}|x|^{\alpha+1}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\int_{|x|\leq R^{\prime}}\frac{1}{|x|^{\frac{1+\alpha}{k}}}dx\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{{R^{\prime}}^{2}}E\\
\big[(\ref{eq:cond:1:c:2})\big]\quad&=\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\frac{2k}{k-(\alpha+1)}\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}{(R^{\prime})}^{\frac{k-(\alpha+1)}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{{(R^{\prime})}^{2}}E\\
\big[(\ref{Equa:def_R'})\big]\quad&=2\left(\frac{2k}{k-(\alpha+1)}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1-\alpha}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1-\alpha}}E^{\frac{k-(\alpha+1)}{3k+1-\alpha}},\end{split}

which is the first estimate.

Case 2.

Define

(5.11)

M^{\prime}:=\left(\frac{2k}{k-1}\right)^{-\frac{k}{3k+1}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{-\frac{1}{3k+1}}E^{\frac{k+1}{3k+1}},

and compute

\begin{split}m&=\int fdx=\int_{|x|\leq M^{\prime}}fdx+\int_{|x|>M^{\prime}}fdx\\
&\leq\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\int_{|x|\leq M^{\prime}}\frac{1}{|xb|^{\frac{1}{k}}}dx\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{(M^{\prime})^{2}}E\\
\big[(\ref{eq:syst:b:2}),(\ref{eq:cond:2:c:2})\big]\quad&\leq\left(\int f^{k+1}|x|^{\alpha+1}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(2\int_{0}^{\bar{x}}\frac{1}{|x|^{\frac{1+\alpha}{k}}}dx+2\int_{\bar{x}}^{M^{\prime}}\frac{1}{|x(2\bar{x})^{\alpha}|^{\frac{1}{k}}}dx\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{(M^{\prime})^{2}}E\\
\big[\bar{x}\geq 1\big]\quad&\leq\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|dx\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}\left(\frac{2k}{k-1}\right)^{\frac{k}{k+1}}(M^{\prime})^{\frac{k-1}{k+1}}+\frac{1}{(M^{\prime})^{2}}E\\
\big[(\ref{Equa:def_M'}\big]\quad&=2\left(\frac{2k}{k-1}\right)^{\frac{2k}{3k+1}}\left(\int f^{k+1}|xb|\right)^{\frac{2}{3k+1}}E^{\frac{k-1}{3k+1}}.\end{split}

This is the second estimate in the statement.
∎

Remark 5.5
.

Observe that if
u
is bounded then

\frac{\|u\|_{1}^{3}}{\|u\|_{\infty}^{2}}\lesssim E,

so that

\frac{m^{\frac{2k-(1-\alpha)}{2}}}{E^{\frac{k-(1-\alpha)}{2}}}\gtrsim m^{1-\alpha-\frac{k}{2}}\|u\|_{\infty}^{k-(1-\alpha)},

\frac{m^{\frac{3k+1}{2}}}{E^{\frac{k-1}{2}}}\gtrsim\|u\|_{\infty}^{k-1}m^{2}.

Hence if

This is also easily verified directly by the estimate (obtained as in (

\begin{split}\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_{2}^{2}&\leq-\|u_{x}\|_{2}^{2}+C\|b\|_{p,\infty}\|u_{x}\|_{2}\|u\|_{(k+1)\tilde{p}^{\prime}}^{k+1}\\
&\leq-\|u_{x}\|_{2}^{2}+C\|u\|_{1}^{\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}}\|u_{x}\|_{2}\|u\|_{\infty}^{k+1-\frac{1}{\tilde{p}^{\prime}}}.\end{split}

Thus

is bounded if

where we have used Gagliando-Niremberg inequality. Hence using again the bound (

A completely similar estimate can be done for the case

6.
Long behavior of solutions

In this last section, we discuss the long behavior of the solutions when there are uniform bounds on the drift
b
:

(1)

b\in L^{\infty}\big((0,\infty),L^{p,\infty}(\mathbb{R})\big)
for the case (
1.2
),

(2)

b_{x}\in L^{\infty}\big((0,\infty),L^{p,\infty}(\mathbb{R})\big)
,

b\in L^{\infty}_{\text{\rm loc}}((0,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}\big)
for the case (
1.3
).

We prove the following theorem.

Theorem 6.1
.

The following holds.

(1)

If
k<1-\frac{1}{p}
, then there are drifts
b=b(x)\in L^{p,\infty}
admitting a stationary solution; similarly, if
k<2-\frac{1}{p}
, there are drift
b_{x}(x)\in L^{p,\infty}_{x}
admitting a stationary solution.

(2)

If
k\geq 1-\frac{1}{p}
,
b\in L^{\infty}_{t}L^{p,\infty}_{x}
or
k\geq 2-\frac{1}{p}
,
b_{x}\in L^{\infty}_{t}L^{p,\infty}_{x}
, every uniformly bounded solution
u(t)
decays to
0
as
t^{-\frac{1}{2}}
in the
L^{\infty}
-norm.

Proof.

Point (
1
).
Define the function

u(x)=\frac{1}{(1+x^{2})^{\frac{1-1/p}{2k}}},

which is a stationary solution to (
1.1
) in
L^{1}
if
k<1-\frac{1}{p}
and the drift
b
is given by

b(x)=-\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}}{k}\frac{x}{(1+x^{2})^{\frac{1+1/p}{2}}}.

Being
b(x)\sim|x|^{-1/p}
for
|x|\gg 1
, we have that
b\in L^{p,\infty}(\mathbb{R})
.

For the case
b_{x}\in L^{p,\infty}
, one can similarly show that

u(x)=\frac{1}{(1+x^{2})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}}{2k}}},

which gives the drift

b(x)=-\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}}{k}\frac{x}{(1+x^{2})^{\frac{1}{2p}}}.

Point (
2
).
We have only to consider the supercritical case, being
k=1-\frac{1}{p}
studied in Section
4.5
.

For the case
b\in L^{\infty}_{t}L_{x}^{p,\infty}
, we observe that

bu^{1+k}=(bu^{k-1+\frac{1}{p}})u^{1-\frac{1}{p}}=\hat{b}u^{1-\frac{1}{p}},

with

\|\hat{b}\|_{p,\infty}\leq\|u\|_{\infty}^{k-1+\frac{1}{p}}\|b\|_{p,\infty}.

Hence the analysis of the critical case can be applied here, deducing that
\|u(t)\|_{\infty}\leq\frac{C}{\sqrt{t}}
.

For the case
b_{x}\in L^{p,\infty}
, we follow the computations of (
4.9
):

\begin{split}\bigg|\int v_{a,y}\tilde{b}v_{a}^{k+1}\bigg|&\leq\frac{1}{k+2}\bigg|\int\tilde{b}_{y}v_{a}^{k+2}\bigg|\\
&\leq C\|\tilde{b}_{y}\|_{p,\infty}\|v_{a}^{k+2}\|_{p^{\prime},1}\\
&\leq C\|\tilde{b}_{y}\|_{p,\infty}\|v_{a}\|_{\infty}^{k-2+\frac{1}{p}}\|v_{a}^{2-\frac{1}{p}+2}\|_{p^{\prime},1}\\
\big[\|v(\tau)\|_{\infty}\lesssim(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{1}{2}}\big]\quad&\leq C(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{2-\frac{1}{p}-k}{2}}(Te^{-\tau})^{\frac{k-2+\frac{1}{p}}{2}}\|v_{a}\|_{(2-\frac{1}{p})p^{\prime},2}^{2+2-\frac{1}{p}}\\
\big[\text{as in (\ref{m:eq:f2})}\big]\quad&\leq\frac{1}{2}\|v_{a,y}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{8}\|v_{a}\|_{2}^{2}.\end{split}

Hence one can repeat the same analysis as in the critical case
k=2-\frac{1}{p}
, replacing
\|b_{x}\|_{p,\infty}
with
\|b_{x}\|_{\infty}\|u\|_{\infty}^{k-2+\frac{1}{p}}
. In particular one obtains the decay
\|u(t)\|_{\infty}\lesssim t^{-\frac{1}{2}}
.
∎
