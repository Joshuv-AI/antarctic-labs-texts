0-Auslander correspondence
Xiaofa Chen

Abstract
. We prove an analogue of Auslander correspondence for exact dg categories whose H 0 H^{0} -category is 0 0 -Auslander in the sense of Gorsky–Nakaoka–Palu.



2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
18G25, 18E30, 16E30, 16E45

Let
k
be a commutative ring.
We denote by
\mathrm{dgcat}
the category of dg
k
-categories and by
\mathrm{Hqe}
the localization of
\mathrm{dgcat}
with respect to the quasi-equivalences, cf.
[
11
]
.
For a dg category
\mathcal{A}
, we denote by
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
its 0-homology category, i.e. the category with the same objects as
\mathcal{A}
and whose morphism spaces are given by
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})(A_{1},A_{2})=H^{0}(\mathcal{A}(A_{1},A_{2}))
. We denote by
\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{A})
its derived category. The dg category
\mathcal{A}
is
pretriangulated
if the essential image of
the Yoneda embedding
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})\rightarrow\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{A})
is a triangulated subcategory.
For a dg category
\mathcal{A}
, we denote its
pretriangulated hull
by
\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{A})
, cf.
[
2
,
5
,
3
]
. Put
\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{A})=H^{0}(\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{A}))
.
We refer to
[
4
]
for the definition and the fundamental properties of an
exact dg category
.
Each pretriangulated category endowed with the class of all graded-split exact sequences is an exact
dg category. Also each dg category
\mathcal{A}
such that
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
is an additive category is an exact dg category when endowed with the class of all split exact sequences.
A full dg subcategory
\mathcal{B}
of an exact dg category
\mathcal{A}
which is stable under extensions inherits from
\mathcal{A}
a canonical exact structure.
By
[
4
, Theorem 6.1]
, for each connective exact dg category
\mathcal{A}
, there exists a universal exact morphism
F:\mathcal{A}\rightarrow\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
in
\mathrm{Hqe}
from
\mathcal{A}
to a pretriangulated dg category
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
which induces a quasi-equivalence of exact dg categories from
\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{A}
to
\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{D}^{\prime}

for an extension-closed dg subcategory
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}
of
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
. Put
\mathcal{D}^{b}(\mathcal{A})=H^{0}(\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A}))
. For a triangulated category
\mathcal{T}
and full subcategories
\mathcal{C}
and
\mathcal{D}
of
\mathcal{T}
, we denote by
\mathcal{C}\ast\mathcal{D}
the full subcategory of
\mathcal{T}
on the objects
X
which admits a triangle
C\rightarrow X\rightarrow D\rightarrow\Sigma C
in
\mathcal{T}
where
C\in\mathcal{C}
and
D\in\mathcal{D}
.

It is shown in
[
4
]
that if
\mathcal{A}
is an exact
dg
k
-category, then the category
H^{0}({\mathcal{A}})
inherits a natural extriangulated
structure in the sense of
[
9
]
. An object
A\in\mathcal{A}
is
projective

(resp.
injective
) if it is projective (resp. injective)
in the extriangulated category
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
. We define an exact dg
category
\mathcal{A}
to be
0
-Auslander
if
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
is
0
-Auslander in
the sense of
[
7
]
. The following theorem is
a
0
-dimensional analogue of higher Auslander–Iyama correspondence

[
1
,
8
]
. It is inspired by recent work of
Fang–Gorsky–Palu–Plamondon–Pressland
[
10
]
.

Theorem 1

(0-Auslander correspondence)
.

There is a bijective correspondence between the following:

(1)

equivalence classes of connective exact dg categories
\mathcal{A}
which are
0
-Auslander;

(2)

equivalence classes of pairs
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
consisting of

- a connective additive dg category
\mathcal{P}
and

- a dg subcategory
\mathcal{I}\subset\mathcal{P}
such that
H^{0}(\mathcal{I})
is covariantly finite in
H^{0}(\mathcal{P})
.

The bijection from
(1)
to
(2)
sends
\mathcal{A}
to the pair
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
formed by the full dg subcategory
\mathcal{P}
on the projectives of
\mathcal{A}
and its full dg subcategory
\mathcal{I}
of projective-injectives. The inverse bijection sends
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
to the
\tau_{\leq 0}
-truncation of the dg subcategory
(\mathcal{P}*\Sigma\mathcal{P})\cap\mathrm{ker}\,\mathrm{Ext}^{1}(-,\mathcal{I})
of
\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{P})
.

We will prove the theorem after Proposition
6
.
Clearly, under the correspondence of the theorem,

a)

the exact dg categories in
(1)
whose corresponding extriangulated category is moreover reduced correspond to the pairs in
(2)
where
\mathcal{I}=0
;

b)

the exact dg categories in
(1)
with the trivial exact structure (so that each object is both projective and injective) correspond to the pairs in
(2)
where
\mathcal{I}=\mathcal{P}
.

For a pair
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
in
(2)
, we denote by
\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
the dg subcategory
\mathcal{P}*\Sigma\mathcal{P}\cap\mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{Ext}^{1}(-,\mathcal{I}))
of
\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{P})
and by
\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
(resp.
\mathcal{J}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
) be the full dg subcategory of
\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
whose objects are the direct summands in
H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
of objects in
H^{0}(\mathcal{P})\subset H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
(resp.
H^{0}(\mathcal{I})\subset H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
).
Clearly
\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
is stable under kernels of retractions in
\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{P})
.
Below we will show that
H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
is
weakly idempotent complete
, i.e. retractions of epimorphisms possess kernels, cf. the proof of Theorem
1
.

For a connective exact dg category
\mathcal{A}
, we denote by
\overline{\mathcal{A}}
the full dg subcategory of
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
consisting of objects in the closure under kernels of retractions of
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
in
\mathcal{D}^{b}(\mathcal{A})
. Clearly
\overline{\mathcal{A}}
is stable under extensions in
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
and the thus inherited exact structure induces a quasi-equivalence
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\overline{\mathcal{A}})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
.

Definition 2
.

Two connective exact dg categories
\mathcal{A}
and
\mathcal{B}
are equivalent if there is a quasi-equivalence
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{B})
inducing a quasi-equivalence
\overline{\mathcal{A}}\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\overline{\mathcal{B}}
.

Two pairs
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
and
(\mathcal{P}^{\prime},\mathcal{I}^{\prime})
in
(2)
are equivalent if there is a quasi-equivalence
\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{P}^{\prime})
inducing quasi-equivalences
\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P}^{\prime},\mathcal{I}^{\prime}}
and
\mathcal{J}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{J}_{\mathcal{P}^{\prime},\mathcal{I}^{\prime}}
.

Remark 3
.

A connective exact dg category
\mathcal{A}
is
0
-Auslander if and only if
\overline{\mathcal{A}}
is
0
-Auslander.

\mathcal{P}
(resp.

\mathcal{A}

Let
X\in\overline{\mathcal{A}}
. Then we have
Y
and
Z
in
\mathcal{A}
such that
Y\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}X\oplus Z
. We have a conflation in
\mathcal{A}

M\rightarrow P\rightarrow Y

where
P
is projective in
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
. Then we have the following diagram in
\mathcal{D}^{b}(\mathcal{A})

Proof of Theorem
1
.

By Remark
3
, the property of being 0-Auslander is invariant under the equivalence relation introduced in Definition
2
.
Notice that for a 0-Auslander exact dg category, the inclusion
\mathcal{A}\rightarrow\overline{\mathcal{A}}
induces a quasi-equivalence
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\overline{\mathcal{A}})
and
\mathcal{D}^{b}_{dg}(\mathcal{A})
is quasi-equivalent to the pretriangulated hull of the full dg subcategory of
\mathcal{A}
on the projectives.
Therefore the map sending the equivalence class of
\mathcal{A}
to the equivalence class
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
is well-defined.

For a pair
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
in (2), we claim that
H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
is weakly idempotent complete. We have a quasi-equivalence
\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{pretr}(\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
. Since
H^{0}(\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
is stable under kernels of retractions in
\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
, we see that
H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}},\mathcal{J}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}})
is weakly idempotent complete by Remark
4
. It is not hard to check that
\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}=\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{Q}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}},\mathcal{J}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}}
. Therefore
H^{0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}})
is weakly idempotent complete and the map sending the equivalence class of a pair
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
to the equivalence class of
\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
is well-defined.

Combining Lemma
5
and Proposition
6
, we see that the above maps are inverse to each other.
∎

Recall that an extriangulated category is
algebraic
, if it is equivalent as an extriangulated category to
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
for an exact dg category
\mathcal{A}
, cf.
[
4
, Proposition-Definition 6.20]
. Since
H^{0}(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{A})
and
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
are equal as extriangulated triangulated categories, we may assume in the above definition that
\mathcal{A}
is connective. Inspired by recent results of Fang–Gorsky–Palu–Plamondon–Pressland
[
6
]
, we have the following example which relates an algebraic 0-Auslander extriangulated category to a homotopy category of two-term complexes.

Example 7
.

Let
(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I})
be a pair in Theorem
1
(2) where
\mathcal{P}
is weakly idempotent complete. Put
\mathcal{A}=\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},\mathcal{I}}
. Let
\mathcal{J}
be the full dg subcategory of injectives in
\mathcal{A}
.
Then we have a canonical equivalence of extriangulated categories
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})/(\mathcal{J}\rightarrow\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{K}^{[-1,0]}(H^{0}(\mathcal{P})/[\mathcal{I}])
.

Proof.

We keep the same notations
\mathcal{P}
and
\mathcal{I}
for the corresponding subcategories
H^{0}(\mathcal{P})
and
H^{0}(\mathcal{I})
of
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})
.
Let
\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{I}
be the dg quotient. By
[
4
, Theorem 6.35]

\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{I}
carries a canonical exact structure whose corresponding extriangulated category is
H^{0}(\mathcal{A})/[\mathcal{I}]
which is again 0-Auslander.
So we may replace
\mathcal{A}
by
\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{I}
and assume
\mathcal{I}=0
. Then
\mathcal{J}=\Sigma\mathcal{P}
and
\mathrm{Hom}_{H^{0}(\mathcal{A})}(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{J})=0
.

The canonical dg functor
\mathcal{P}\rightarrow H^{0}(\mathcal{P})
induces a canonical exact dg functor

\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{P},0}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}_{H^{0}(\mathcal{P}),0}

which is quasi-dense.
By taking 0-homology on both sides, it induces a canonical functor

H^{0}(\mathcal{A})\rightarrow\mathcal{K}^{[-1,0]}(H^{0}(\mathcal{P})).

In
\mathcal{K}^{[-1,0]}(H^{0}(\mathcal{P}))
we have
\mathrm{Hom}(\Sigma\mathcal{P},\mathcal{P})=0
. Therefore it induces a canonical functor

F:\mathcal{C}=H^{0}(\mathcal{A})/(\mathcal{J}\rightarrow\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathcal{K}^{[-1,0]}(H^{0}(\mathcal{P})/[\mathcal{I}])=\mathcal{C}^{\prime}.

Let
X
and
Y
be objects in
\mathcal{A}
. Assume we have triangles in
\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{P})

(0.0.9)

P_{1}\xrightarrow{f}P_{0}\rightarrow X\rightarrow\Sigma P_{1}

and

(0.0.10)

Q_{1}\rightarrow Q_{0}\rightarrow Y\rightarrow\Sigma Q_{1}.

We have

\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{H^{0}(\mathcal{A})}(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{\prime}}(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{P})

and

\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{P},\Sigma\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{H^{0}(\mathcal{A})}(\mathcal{P},\Sigma\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{\prime}}(\mathcal{P},\Sigma\mathcal{P})=0.

We apply the cohomological functor
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{P})}(-,\mathcal{P})
to the triangle
0.0.9
and we get a long exact sequence of Hom spaces in
\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{P})

\ldots\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}(\Sigma P_{1},\mathcal{P})\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}(X,\mathcal{P})\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}(P_{0},\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Hom}(f,\mathcal{P})}\mathrm{Hom}(P_{1},\mathcal{P})\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}(X,\Sigma\mathcal{P})\rightarrow 0.

By direct inspection we see that
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,\mathcal{P})
is isomorphic to the kernel of the map
\mathrm{Hom}(f,\mathcal{P})
and that
\mathrm{Hom}_{H^{0}(\mathcal{A})}(X,\Sigma\mathcal{P})=\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,\Sigma\mathcal{P})
. Therefore we have

\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{\prime}}(FX,F\mathcal{P})

and
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,\Sigma\mathcal{P})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{\prime}}(FX,F\Sigma\mathcal{P})
.
Now from the
\mathbb{E}
-triangle in
\mathcal{C}
given by
0.0.10
, we have a long exact sequence

\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Q_{1})\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Q_{0})\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)\rightarrow\mathbb{E}(X,Q_{1})\rightarrow\mathbb{E}(X,Q_{0})\rightarrow\mathbb{E}(X,Y)\rightarrow 0.

We have
\mathbb{E}(X,Q_{1})\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{H^{0}(\mathcal{A})}(X,\Sigma Q_{1})=\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,\Sigma Q_{1})
. By the Five-Lemma, we see that the functor
F
is fully faithful and is an equivalence of extriangulated categories.
∎
