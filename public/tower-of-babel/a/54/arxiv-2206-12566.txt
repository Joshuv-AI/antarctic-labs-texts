Isoparametric submanifolds in Hilbert spaces and holonomy maps
Naoyuki Koike

Abstract
Let π : P → B \pi:P\to B be a smooth G G -bundle over a compact Riemannian manifold B B and c c a smooth loop in B B of constant seed a ( > 0 ) a(>0) , where G G is compact semi-simple Lie group. In this paper, we prove that the holonomy map hol c : 𝒜 P H s → G {\rm hol}_{c}:\mathcal{A}_{P}^{H^{s}}\to G is a homothetic submersion of coefficient a a , where s s is a non-negative integer, 𝒜 P H s \mathcal{A}_{P}^{H^{s}} is the Hilbert space of all H s H^{s} -connections of the bundle P P . In particular, we prove that, if s = 0 s=0 , then hol c {\rm hol}_{c} has minimal regularizable fibres. From this fact, we can derive that each component of the inverse image of any equifocal submanifold in G G by the holonomy map hol c : 𝒜 P H 0 → G {\rm hol}_{c}:\mathcal{A}_{P}^{H^{0}}\to G is an isoparametric submanifold in 𝒜 P H 0 \mathcal{A}_{P}^{H^{0}} . As the result, we obtain a new systematic construction of isoparametric submanifolds in a Hilbert space.

Set

\begin{array}[]{c}\hskip 11.38092pt\Lambda^{H^{s}}(\mathfrak{g}):=\{u\in H^{s}([0,1],\mathfrak{g})\,|\,u(0)=u(1)\}\\

Proposition 3.2.

as

as

for the simplicity.
Take

. According to Lemma 3.1,

is expressed as

\displaystyle{A:=\int_{0}^{1}\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(t),\xi(t)}}\,dt}
for some

Then we have

\begin{array}[]{l}\hskip 14.22636pt\displaystyle{(g_{s})_{\omega}(A,A)=\langle A,\square_{\omega}^{s}(A)\rangle_{0}}\\
\displaystyle{=\langle\int_{0}^{1}\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(\bar{t}),\xi(\bar{t})}}\,d\bar{t},\,\,\square_{\omega}^{s}\left(\int_{0}^{1}\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(\hat{t}),\xi(\hat{t})}}\,d\hat{t}\right)\rangle_{0}}\\

\displaystyle{=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\,\int_{0}^{1}\langle\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(t),\xi(t)}((c\circ z)^{\prime}(t)),\,\,}\\
\hskip 42.67912pt\displaystyle{\square_{\omega}^{s}\left(\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(t),\xi(t)}}\right)_{c(z(t))}((c\circ z)^{\prime}(t))\rangle_{{\rm Ad}(P)_{c(z(t))}}\,dt}\\
\displaystyle{=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\,\int_{0}^{1}\langle\xi(t),\,\,\left(\square_{\omega}^{s}\left(\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(t),\xi(t)}}\right)\right)_{\sigma(t)}(\sigma^{\prime}(t))\rangle_{\mathfrak{g}}\,dt}\end{array}

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle{=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\,\int_{0}^{1}\langle\xi(t),\,\,\sigma^{\ast}\left(\square_{\omega}^{s}\left(\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(t),\xi(t)}}\right)\right)_{t}\left(\frac{d}{dt}\right)\rangle_{\mathfrak{g}}\,dt}\\
\displaystyle{=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\,\langle\xi,\,\square_{\mu_{c}(\omega)}^{s}(\xi)\rangle_{0},}\end{array}

\displaystyle((\mu_{c})_{\ast\omega}(A))(t)

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{1}(\delta_{\eta_{\sigma^{\prime}(\bar{t}),\xi(\bar{t})}})_{\sigma(t)}(\sigma^{\prime}(t))\,d\bar{t}

\begin{array}[]{l}\hskip 14.22636pt\displaystyle{(g_{s}^{o})_{\mu_{c}(\omega)}((\mu_{c})_{\ast\omega}(A),(\mu_{c})_{\ast\omega}(A))}\\

\displaystyle{=\langle\xi,\xi\rangle_{s}^{\mu_{c}(\omega)}=\langle\xi,\square_{\mu_{c}(\omega)}^{s}(\xi)\rangle_{0}.}\end{array}

[formula]

From

and
