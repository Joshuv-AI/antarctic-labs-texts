Spectral theory of p p -adic unitary operator
Zhao Tianhong

Abstract
The p p -adic unitary operator U U is defined as an invertible operator on p p -adic ultrametric Banach space such that | U | = | U − 1 | = 1 \left|U\right|=\left|U^{-1}\right|=1 . We point out U U has a spectral measure valued in projection functors , which can be explained as the measure theory on the formal group scheme. The spectrum decomposition of U U is complete when ψ \psi is a p p -adic wave function. We study the Galois theory of operators . The abelian extension theory of ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} is connected to the topological properties of the p p -adic unitary operator. We classify the p p -adic unitary operator as three types: Teichmüller type , continuous type , pro-finite type . Finally, we establish a framework of p p -adic quantum mechanics , where projection functor plays a role of quantum measurement.
