The Littlewood-Paley-Stein inequality for Dirichlet space tamed by distributional curvature lower bounds
Syota Esaki Zi Jian Xu, Kazuhiro Kuwae

Abstract
The notion of tamed Dirichlet space by distributional lower Ricci curvature bounds was proposed by Erbar-Rigoni-Sturm-Tamanini [ 27 ] as the Dirichlet space having a weak form of Bakry-Émery curvature lower bounds in distribution sense. In this framework, we establish the Littlewood-Paley-Stein inequality for L p L^{p} -functions and L p L^{p} -boundedness of q q -Riesz transforms with q > 1 q>1 , which partially generalizes the result by Kawabi-Miyokawa [ 38 ] .

Kato class, smooth measures of Dynkin class,
Littlewood-Paley-Stein inequality, Riesz transform, Wiener space, path space with Gibbs measure, RCD-space,
Riemannian manifold with boundary, configuration space,

In this section, we prove Theorem

The original idea is due to Meyer

]

in this paper.
Though we have a good core

in proving Theorem

.

]

We prove Theorem

for

and
\phi^{\prime\prime}(r)\leq 0
for
r\in[0,+\infty[
.
Let
\mathfrak{m}_{Y}
be the
3
-dimensional Lebesgue measure restricted to
Y
and

\sigma_{\partial Y}
the
2
-dimensional Hausdorff measure on
\partial Y
. Denote by

\mathscr{E}_{Y}
the Dirichlet form on
L^{2}(Y;\mathfrak{m})
with Neumann boundary conditions.
The smallest eigenvalue of the second fundamental form of
\partial Y
can be given by

\displaystyle\ell(r,\phi)=\frac{\phi^{\prime\prime}(r)}{(1+|\phi^{\prime}(r)|^{2})^{3/2}}(\leq 0),

for
r\leq 1
and
\ell=0
for
r\geq 2
. It is proved in
[
27
, Theorem 4.6]
that
the Dirichlet space
(Y,\mathscr{E}_{Y},\mathfrak{m}_{Y})
is tamed by

\displaystyle\kappa=\ell\sigma_{\partial Y}.

Since
|\kappa|=|\ell|\sigma_{\partial Y}\in S_{K}({\bf X})
(see
[
27
, Lemma 2.34, Theorem 2.36, Proof of Theorem 4.6]
),
we can apply Theorems
1.2
and
1.3
for
(Y,\mathscr{E}_{Y},\mathfrak{m}_{Y})
.

Example 5.7

(
Configuration space over metric measure spaces
)
.

Let
(M,g)
be a complete smooth Riemannian manifold without boundary.
The configuration space
\Upsilon
over
M
is the space of all locally finite point measures, that is,

\displaystyle\Upsilon:=\{\gamma\in\mathcal{M}(M)\mid\gamma(K)\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}\quad\text{ for all compact sets}\quad K\subset M\}.

In the seminal paper Albeverio-Kondrachev-Röckner
[
1
]
identified a natural geometry on
\Upsilon
by lifting the geometry of
M
to
\Upsilon
. In particular, there exists a natural gradient
\nabla^{\Upsilon}
, divergence
{\rm div}^{\Upsilon}
and
Laplace operator
\Delta^{\Upsilon}
on
\Upsilon
. It is shown in
[
1
]
that the Poisson point measure
\pi
on

\Upsilon
is the unique (up to intensity) measure on
\Upsilon
under which the gradient and divergence become
dual operator in
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\pi)
. Hence, the Poisson measure
\pi
is the natural volume measure on
\Upsilon

and
\Upsilon
can be seen as an infinite dimensional Riemannian manifold. The canonical Dirichlet form

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}(F)=\int_{\Upsilon}|\nabla^{\Upsilon}F|_{\gamma}^{2}\pi(\mathrm{d}\gamma)

constructed in
[
1
, Theorem 6.1]
is quasi-regular and strongly local
and it induces the heat semigroup
T_{t}^{\Upsilon}
and a Brownian motion
{\bf X}^{\Upsilon}
on
\Upsilon
which can be identified with the independent infinite particle process. If
{\rm Ric}_{g}\geq K
on
M
with
K\in\mathbb{R}
, then
(\Upsilon,\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},\pi)
is tamed by

\kappa:=K\pi
with
|\kappa|\in S_{K}({\bf X}^{\Upsilon})
(see
[
26
, Theorem 4.7]
and
[
27
, Theorem 3.6]
).
Then one can apply Theorems
1.2
and
1.3
for
(\Upsilon,\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},\pi)
.

More generally, in Dello Schiavo-Suzuki
[
23
]
, configuration space
\Upsilon
over proper complete and separable metric space
(X,{\sf d})
is considered. The configuration space
\Upsilon
is endowed with the
vague topology

\tau_{V}
, induced by duality with continuous compactly supported functions on
X
, and with a reference Borel probability measure
\mu
satisfying
[
23
, Assumption 2.17]
, commonly understood as the law of a proper point process on
X
. In
[
23
]
,
they constructed the strongly local Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon}
defined to be the
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\mu)
-closure of a certain pre-Dirichlet form on a class of certain cylinder functions and prove its quasi-regularity for a wide class of measures
\mu
and base spaces (see
[
23
, Proposition 3.9 and Theorem 3.45]
). Moreover, in
Dello Schiavo-Suzuki
[
24
]
, for any fixed
K\in\mathbb{R}
they prove that a Dirichlet form
(\mathscr{E},D(\mathscr{E}))
with its
carré-du-champ
\Gamma
satisfies
{\sf BE}_{2}(K,\infty)
if and only if the Dirichlet form
(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},D(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon}))
on
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\mu)
with its carré-du-champ
\Gamma^{\Upsilon}
satisfies
{\sf BE}_{2}(K,\infty)
.
Hence, if
(X,\mathscr{E},\mathfrak{m})
is tamed by
K\mathfrak{m}
with
|K|\mathfrak{m}\in S_{K}({\bf X})
, then
(\Upsilon,\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},\mu)
is tamed by
\kappa:=K\mu
with
|\kappa|\in S_{K}({\bf X}^{\Upsilon})
.
Then one can apply Theorems
1.2
and
1.3
for
(\Upsilon,\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},\mu)
under the suitable class of measures
\mu
defined in
[
23
, Assumption 2.17]
.
