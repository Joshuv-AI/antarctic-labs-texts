Continuous generalized atomic subspaces for operators in Hilbert spaces
Mohamed Rossafi, Fakhr-dine Nhari, Abdeslam Touri

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the concept of continuous g − g- atomic subspace for a bounded linear operator and gives several useful continuous resolution of the identity operator on a Hilbert space by implies the theory of continuous g − g- fusion frames. Moreover, we introduce the concept of continuous frame operator for a pair of continuous g − g- fusion bessel sequences.

Introduction

Discret frames were introduced by Duffin and Schaeffer in 1952
Duf
for studing some profound problems in nonharmonic Fourier series, after the fundamental paper
DGM
by Daubechies, Grossman and Meyer, frame theory began to be widely used, particularly in the more specialized context of wavelet frames and Gabor frames.

The concept of generalization of frames was proposed by G. Kaiser
KAI
and independently by Ali, Antoine and Gazeau
GAZ
to a family indexed by some locally compact space endowed with a Radon measure. These frames are known as continuous frames. Gabrado and Han in
GHN
called these frames Frames associated with measurable spaces, Askari-Hemmat, Dehghan and Radjabalipour in
AHDR
called them generalized frames and in mathematical physics they are referred to as Coherent states
COH
.

A continuous g-frame (or simply a c-g-frame) was firstly introduced by Abdollahpour and Faroughi in
AF
and is an extension of g-frames and continuous frames. Recently, continuous g-frames in Hilbert spaces have been studied intensively.

Fusion frames were considered by Casazza, Kutyniok and Li in connection with distributed processing and are related to the construction of global frames
CCO
. The fusion
frame theory is in fact more delicate due to complicated relations between the structure
of the sequence of weighted subspaces and the local frames in the subspaces and also due
to the extreme sensitivity to changes of the weights.

Recently, E.Alizadeh, A. Rahimi, E. Osgooei, M. Rahmani have presented continuous
K-g-
fusion frame in
EAEM
. For more detailed information on frame theory, readers are recommended to consult:
Assila
;
Ghiati
;
Karara
;
Massit
;
Rossafi
;
Kabbaj
;
Chouchene
.

Continuous resolution of the identity operator in continuous
g-
fusion frame

Theorem 5
.

Let
\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
be a continuous
g-
fusion frame for
U
with bounds
C,D
and
S_{\Lambda}
be its associated continuous
f-
fusion frame operator. Then the family
\{v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is the continuous of the identity operator on
U
, where
T_{w}=\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}S^{-1}_{\chi}
,
\forall w\in\Theta
. Furthermore, for all
f\in U
we have,

\frac{C}{D^{2}}\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\leq\frac{D}{C^{2}}\|f\|^{2}.

Proof.

We have
S_{\chi}
is invertible, then for each
f\in U

\displaystyle f=S_{\chi}S_{\chi}^{-1}f

\displaystyle=\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}S_{\chi}^{-1}fd\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}d\mu(w).

Therefore,
\{v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous resolution of the identity operator on
U
.

Moreover, for each
f\in U
we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}(f)\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}S_{\chi}^{-1}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq D\|S_{\chi}^{-1}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq D\|S_{\chi}^{-1}\|^{2}\|f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{D}{C^{2}}\|f\|^{2}.

On the other hand

C\|S_{\chi}^{-1}f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),

since for each
f\in U

\|f\|^{2}=\|S_{\chi}S_{\chi}^{-1}f\|^{2}\leq\|S_{\chi}\|^{2}\|S_{\chi}^{-1}f\|^{2},

then

\|S_{\chi}\|^{-2}\|f\|^{2}\leq\|S_{\chi}^{-1}f\|^{2},

therefore

C\|S_{\chi}\|^{-2}\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),

hence

\frac{C}{D^{2}}\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w).

∎

Theorem 6
.

Let
\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
be a continuous
g-
fusion frame for
U
with bounds
C,D
and let
T_{w}:U\rightarrow U_{w}
be a bounded operator such that
\{v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous resolution of the identity operator on
U
. Then

\frac{1}{D}\bigg|\bigg|\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}fd\mu(w)\bigg|\bigg|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in U.

Proof.

Let
f\in U
and set
g=\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}fd\mu(w)
. Then

\displaystyle\|g\|^{4}

\displaystyle=\langle g,g\rangle^{2}=\langle g,\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}fd\mu(w)\rangle^{2}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\int_{\Theta}v(w)\langle\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}g,v(w)T_{w}f\rangle d\mu(w)\biggr)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}g\|^{2}d\mu(w)\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq D\|g\|^{2}\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),

so

\frac{1}{D}\|g\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),

hence

\frac{1}{D}\bigg|\bigg|\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}fd\mu(w)\bigg|\bigg|\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w).

∎

Theorem 7
.

Let
\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
be a continuous
g-
fusion frame with bounds
C,D
and let
T_{w}:U\rightarrow U_{w}
be a bounded operator such that
\{v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous resolution of the identity operator on
U
. If
T_{w}^{\ast}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}=T_{w}
, then

\frac{1}{D}\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\leq DE\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in U.

Where
E=\sup_{w\in\Theta}\|T_{w}\|^{2}<\infty
.

Proof.

Because
\{v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous resolution of the identity on
U
, then

f=\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}fd\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in U.

For each
f\in U
, using theorem (
6
), we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{D}\|f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{D}\bigg|\bigg|\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}T_{w}fd\mu(w)\bigg|\bigg|\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T^{\ast}_{w}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|T_{w}\|^{2}\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq E\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq ED\|f\|^{2}.

∎

Theorem 8
.

\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous
g-
fusion frame for
U
if the following conditions hold:

(1)

For all
f\in U
there exists
A>0
such that

\int_{\Theta}\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\leq\frac{1}{A}\|f\|^{2}.

(2)

\{v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous resolution of the identity operator on
U
.

Proof.

We have
\{v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous resolution of the identity operator on
U
, then for each
f\in U

f=\int_{\Theta}v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}fd\mu(w).

By Cauchy-Schwartz inequality, we get

\displaystyle\|f\|^{4}

\displaystyle=\langle f,f\rangle^{2}=\langle\int_{\Theta}v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}fd\mu(w),f\rangle^{2}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\int_{\Theta}v(w)\langle\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f,\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\rangle d\mu(w)\biggr)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\int_{\Theta}\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{A}\|f\|^{2}\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),

then

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in U.

On the other hand

\displaystyle\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq B\int_{\Theta}\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{B}{A}\|f\|^{2},

where
B=\sup_{w\in\Theta}v^{2}(w)
and hence,
\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous
g-
fusion frame.
∎

Theorem 9
.

\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous

\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}

Proof.

Assum that

K

\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous
g-

B>0
such that

\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in U.

For any
f\in U
we get

\|K^{\ast}f\|=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg|\langle K^{\ast}f,g\rangle\bigg|=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg|\langle f,Kg\rangle\bigg|.

By definition (
4
), for
g\in U
there exits
\phi\in L^{2}(\Theta,U_{0})
such that

Kg=\int_{\Theta}v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\phi(w)d\mu(w)\quad and\quad\|\phi\|_{L^{2}(\Theta,U_{0})}\leq C\|g\|_{U}\quad for\quad some\quad C>0.

Therefore

\displaystyle\|K^{\ast}f\|

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg|\langle f,\int_{\Theta}v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\phi(w)d\mu(w)\rangle\bigg|=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg|\int_{\Theta}v(w)\langle\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f,\phi(w)\rangle d\mu(w)\bigg|

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg(\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}\bigg(\int_{\Theta}\phi(w)\|^{2}d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\|\phi(w)\|\bigg(\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg(\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}C\|g\|

\displaystyle=C\bigg(\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}},

so

\frac{1}{C^{2}}\|K^{\ast}f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w).

Conversely if
\{F(w),\chi_{w},v(w)\}_{w\in\Theta}
is a continuous
K-g-
fusion frame for
U
with bounds
A,B
and
T_{\chi}
is the corresponding synthesis operator.

Then obviously

is a continuous
g-
fusion bessel sequence in
U

f\in U

A\|K^{\ast}f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Theta}v^{2}(w)\|\chi_{w}\pi_{F(w)}f\|^{2}d\mu(w)=\|T_{\chi}^{\ast}f\|^{2},

implies
AKK^{\ast}\leq T_{\chi}T_{\chi}^{\ast}

1

K(f)=T_{\chi}L(f)=T_{\chi}(\phi)=\int_{\Theta}v(w)\pi_{F(w)}\chi_{w}^{\ast}\phi(w)d\mu(w)

\|\phi\|_{L^{2}(\Theta,U_{0})}=\|L(f)\|_{L^{2}(\Theta,U_{0})}\leq C\|f\|_{U},\quad for\quad some\quad C>0.

Therefore,

g-
atomic subspace of

K
