Quasi-lisse vertex (super)algebras
Hao Li

Abstract
. We study the twisted arc spaces and the vertex superalgebras. We obtain new finiteness conditions for the convergence of genus-0 and genus-1 n n -point correlation functions of quasi-lisse vertex (super)algebras.

The organization of this paper is as follows.
In Section 2, we recall the definition and basic notations of vertex operator (super)algebras. In Section 4, we introduce the definition of quasi-lisse vertex (super)algebras and show that it has only finitely many simple ordinary

Definition 2.1

Let

A vertex superalgebra contains the following data: a vector space of states
V

\mathbf{1}\in V_{\overline{0}},

•

Define the

The reason why we only have surjectivity for

3.1.

We recall the concept of module of a Poisson algebra.

Definition 3.1

Let

Appendix B
Modular forms

In this and the next sections, we review some basics of modular forms and fix the notations. For more details, readers can refer to
[
Zhu96
]
,
[
MT10
]
and
[
Zag08
]
.

The
Eisenstein series

G_{2k}(\tau)

(k\in\mathbb{Z}_{+})
are series

(118)

\displaystyle\begin{split}G_{2k}(\tau)&:=\sum_{(m,n)\in\mathbb{Z}^{2}\setminus\{(0,0)\}}\frac{1}{(m+n\tau)^{2k}}\\
&=(2\pi i)^{2k}(-\frac{B_{2k}}{(2k)!}+\frac{2}{(2k-1)!}\displaystyle\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}}\frac{n^{2k-1}q^{n}}{1-q^{n}}),\end{split}

where
B_{k}
is the
k
th Bernoulli number. Denote
\frac{G_{2k}(\tau)}{(2\pi i)^{2k}}
by
\mathbb{E}_{2k}(\tau).
For
k\geq 2
,
G_{2k}(\tau)
is a modular form of weight
2k
for modular group
SL_{2}(\mathbb{Z}),
i.e.,

\displaystyle G_{2k}(\frac{a\tau+b}{c\tau+d})=(c\tau+d)^{2k}G_{2k}(\tau).

On the other hand,
G_{2}(\tau)
is a quasi-modular form obeying the following transformation law

\displaystyle G_{2}(\frac{a\tau+b}{c\tau+d})=(c\tau+d)^{2}G_{2}(\tau)-2\pi ic(c\tau+d).

One can renormalize
G_{2k}(\tau)

(k\in\mathbb{Z}_{+})
so that its constant term is
1
, and name them
E_{2k}(\tau)
. The
q
-series expansion of
E_{2k}(\tau)
is

(119)

\displaystyle E_{2k}(\tau)=1-\frac{4k}{B_{2k}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n^{2k-1}q^{n}}{1-q^{n}},

where
q=e^{2\pi i\tau}
.
The polynomial algebra generated by
E_{2}(\tau)
,
E_{4}(\tau)
and
E_{6}(\tau)
over
\mathbb{C}
is the algebra of
quasi-modular forms
. The ring of all holomorphic modular forms on
\mathbb{H}
is a graded algebra
\displaystyle A=\oplus_{k}A_{k}=\mathbb{C}[E_{4}(\tau),E_{6}(\tau)],
where
A_{k}
is the subspace of
A
spanned by all weight
k
elements.

Given a function
f
on the upper half plane, we denote by
\tilde{f}(q)
the
q
-expansion of
f
.

Given an element
f\in A_{k},
one defines the
Serre derivation
of weight
k
,
\partial_{k}
, on it:

\displaystyle\partial_{k}f:=f^{\prime}+k\mathbb{E}_{2}(\tau)f.

The Serre derivation preserves the holomorphy and modularity of
f
, i.e.,
\partial_{k}f\in A_{k+2}
. The
i
th iterated Serre derivation of weight
k
is
\partial_{k}^{i}=\partial_{k+2(i-1)}\circ\cdots\circ\partial_{k+2}\circ\partial_{k}
with
\partial_{k}^{0}=1.
A modular linear differential equation (MLDE) of weight
k
is a linear differential equation

\partial_{k}^{n}f+\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}g_{j}\partial_{k}^{j}f=0,

where
g_{j}\in A
and the weight of
g_{j}
is
2n-2j
for each
0\leq j\leq n-1
. The solutions of MLDE belong to
A.

Since we will work with
q
-series later, we define formal Serre derivation
\partial_{k}
and formal iterated Serre derivation
\partial^{i}
of weight
k
by

(120)

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\partial_{k}f(q)=q\frac{d}{dq}f(q)+k\tilde{\mathbb{E}}_{2}(q)f(q),\\
&\partial^{i}(f(q))=\partial_{k+2i-2}(\partial^{i-1}(f(q))),\end{split}

where
f\in A_{k}
and
\partial^{0}={\rm id}
.
