Unbalanced multi-drawing urn with random addition matrix II
Rafik Aguech, Wissem Jedidi ⋆, Olfa Selmi †

Abstract
At at stage n ≥ 1 n\geq 1 , we pick out at random m m balls, say k k white balls and m − k m-k black balls. We inspect the colors and then we return the balls, according to a predefined replacement matrix, together with ( m − k ) ​ X n (m-k)\;X_{n} white balls and k ​ Y n k\;Y_{n} black balls. Under the assumption that the seqence ( X n , Y n ) (X_{n},\;Y_{n}) is bounded and i.i.d. Aguech & Selmi R&O proved a strong law of large numbers (SLLN) and a central limit theorem (CLT) on the proportion of white balls. Here, we extend the last results as follows. In a first step, we obtain the same results under the assumption that ( X n , Y n ) (X_{n},\;Y_{n}) have the same distribution and that 𝔼 ⁡ [ X 1 2 + Y 1 2 ] < ∞ \mathbb{E}[X_{1}^{2}+Y_{1}^{2}]<\infty . In a second step, removing the assumption of identical distribution and assuming that 𝔼 ⁡ [ X n 2 + Y n 2 ] < ∞ \mathbb{E}[X_{n}^{2}+Y_{n}^{2}]<\infty for all n n , we prove the SLLN and CLT for the total number of balls in the urn.
