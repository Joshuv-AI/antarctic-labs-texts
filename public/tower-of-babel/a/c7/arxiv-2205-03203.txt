In this work, we explore the hadronic coupling constants

of the exotic states

We write down the three-point correlation functions, and accomplish the operator product expansion up to the vacuum condensates of dimension-5, and neglect the tiny gluon condensate contributions, just like in our previous works, and obtain the spectral densities through dispersion relation, then we acquire the rigorous quark-hadron duality below the continuum thresholds

and
