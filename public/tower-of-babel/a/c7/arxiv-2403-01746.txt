New supersymmetric A ​ d ​ S 6 AdS_{6} black holes from matter-coupled F ⁡ ( 4 ) F(4) gauged supergravity


Abstract
We study supersymmetric A ​ d ​ S 6 AdS_{6} black holes from matter-coupled N = ( 1 , 1 ) N=(1,1) gauged supergravity coupled to three vector multiplets and S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) × S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) SO(3)\times SO(3) gauge group. This gauged supergravity admits two supersymmetric A ​ d ​ S 6 AdS_{6} vacua preserving all supersymmetries with S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) × S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) SO(3)\times SO(3) and S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) diag SO(3)_{\textrm{diag}} symmetries. By considering a truncation to S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) diag ⊂ S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) diag SO(2)_{\textrm{diag}}\subset SO(3)_{\textrm{diag}} invariant sector, we find a number of new supersymmetric A ​ d ​ S 2 × ℳ 4 AdS_{2}\times\mathcal{M}_{4} solutions by performing a topological twist along ℳ 4 \mathcal{M}_{4} . For ℳ 4 \mathcal{M}_{4} being a product of two Riemann surfaces Σ × Σ ~ \Sigma\times\widetilde{\Sigma} and a Kahler four-cycle, the twist is implemented by S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) diag SO(2)_{\textrm{diag}} gauge field while, for ℳ 4 \mathcal{M}_{4} given by a Cayley four-cycle, the twist is performed by turning on S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) diag SO(3)_{\textrm{diag}} gauge fields. We give numerical black hole solutions interpolating between A ​ d ​ S 2 × ℳ 4 AdS_{2}\times\mathcal{M}_{4} near horizon geometries and asymptotically locally A ​ d ​ S 6 AdS_{6} vacua. Among these solutions, there are solutions interpolating between both of the supersymmetric A ​ d ​ S 6 AdS_{6} vacua and near horizon geometries. The solutions can also be interpreted as holographic RG flows from five-dimensional SCFTs to superconformal quantum mechanics.

We have constructed new supersymmetric

F(4)

SO(3)\times SO(3)

SO(2)_{\textrm{diag}}
gauge field, we find a number of black hole solutions with
AdS_{2}\times H^{2}\times H^{2}
and
AdS_{2}\times\mathcal{M}^{-}_{4}
