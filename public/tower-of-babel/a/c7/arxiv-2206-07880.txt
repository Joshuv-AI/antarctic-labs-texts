Gradient type estimates for linear elliptic systems from composite materials
Youchan Kim, Pilsoo Shin

Abstract
. In this paper, we consider linear elliptic systems from composite materials where the coefficients depend on the shape and might have the discontinuity between the subregions. We derive a function which is related to the gradient of the weak solutions and which is not only locally piecewise Hölder continuous but locally Hölder continuous. The gradient of the weak solutions can be estimated by this derived function and we also prove local piecewise gradient Hölder continuity which was obtained by the previous results.



1.
Introductions

In this paper, we study linear elliptic systems from composite materials.
First, we describe our model problem in this paper. For composite materials, the physical characteristics of the medium are divided into a finite number of components or subregions. So let
\displaystyle\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}

\displaystyle(n\geq 2)
be a bounded domain and
\displaystyle\Omega_{1},\cdots,\Omega_{l}\subset\Omega
be the mutually disjoint subregions (of
\displaystyle\Omega)
with
\displaystyle\Omega_{0}:=\Omega\setminus(\Omega_{1}\cup\cdots\cup\Omega_{l})
. Here, the subregions
\displaystyle\Omega_{0}
,
\displaystyle\Omega_{1}
,
\displaystyle\cdots
,
\displaystyle\Omega_{l}
represent each component of a composite material
\displaystyle\Omega
. Since the physical characteristics are regular in each component
\displaystyle\Omega_{0},\cdots,\Omega_{l}
, we consider the following linear elliptic systems.

For
\displaystyle C^{1,\gamma}
-domains
\displaystyle\Omega_{1}
,
\displaystyle\cdots
,
\displaystyle\Omega_{l}
and
\displaystyle\Omega
, let
\displaystyle u\in W^{1,2}\left(\Omega,\mathbb{R}^{N}\right)
be a weak solution of

(1.1)

\partial_{\alpha}\left[A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x)\partial_{\beta}u^{j}\right]=\partial_{\alpha}F_{\alpha}^{i}\quad\text{ in }\quad\Omega,

for
\displaystyle 1\leq\alpha,\beta\leq n
and
\displaystyle 1\leq i,j\leq N
, where

(1.2)

\lambda|\xi|^{2}\leq A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x)\xi_{\alpha}^{i}\xi_{\beta}^{j}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\big|A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x)\big|\leq\Lambda\qquad\big(x\in\Omega,\ \xi\in\mathbb{R}^{nN}\big),

for some positive constant
\displaystyle\lambda
and
\displaystyle\Lambda
. Because the physical characteristics are regular in each subregion
\displaystyle\Omega_{0},\cdots,\Omega_{l}\subset\Omega
, we assume that
\displaystyle A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta},\,F_{\alpha}^{i}\in C^{\mu}(\Omega_{k})

\displaystyle\big(k\in\{0,\cdots,l\}\big)
for any
\displaystyle 1\leq\alpha,\beta\leq n

\displaystyle 1\leq i,j\leq N

The regularity theory related to composite materials is motivated by the numerical observation
[
2

that the gradient bound
\displaystyle|Du|
is independent of the distance between the subdomains for certain homogeneous isotropic linear systems of elasticity. Bonnetier and Vogelius
[
3

considered a geometric structure that two touching disk inside a disk to obtain a gradient boundedness of the weak solution. Then Li and Vogelius
[
9

obtained global Lipschitz regularity and global piecewise gradient Hölder continuity for linear elliptic equations in general geometry, say mutually disjoint subdomains
\displaystyle\Omega_{1}
,
\displaystyle\cdots

\displaystyle\Omega_{l}
inside the domain
\displaystyle\Omega

[

extended
[

\displaystyle Du
is Hölder continuous in each
\displaystyle\Omega_{k}
for any
\displaystyle k\in\{0,\cdots,l\}
and local gradient piecewise Hölder continuous means that
\displaystyle Du

\displaystyle\Omega_{k}\cap\Omega^{\prime}

\displaystyle k\in\{0,\cdots,l\}
and
\displaystyle\Omega^{\prime}\subset\subset\Omega

In

9
]

Definition 1.4

\|\varphi_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})},\|\varphi_{l}\|_{L^{\infty}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})}\leq c_{2},

and

\varphi_{k}\leq\varphi_{l}\text{ in }Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime}.

Then we have that

(2.1)

|D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})|\leq 3\rho^{-1}\left(\rho^{1+\gamma}c_{1}+2c_{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma+1}}[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma+1}}

for any
\displaystyle x^{\prime}\in Q_{r}^{\prime}
.

Proof.

Fix
\displaystyle x^{\prime}\in Q_{r}^{\prime}
. Choose
\displaystyle y^{\prime}\in Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime}
with

(2.2)

y^{\prime}=x^{\prime}-\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})}{|D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})|}\left(\frac{[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]}{\rho^{1+\gamma}c_{1}+2c_{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma+1}}\rho.

Then by Taylor expansion of
\displaystyle\varphi_{l}-\varphi_{k}
with respect to
\displaystyle x^{\prime}
,

\displaystyle

\displaystyle[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]-[\varphi_{l}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})]

\displaystyle\geq[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]\cdot(x^{\prime}-y^{\prime})

\displaystyle-\left([D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}]_{C^{\gamma}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})}+[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}]_{C^{\gamma}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})}\right)|x^{\prime}-y^{\prime}|^{1+\gamma}.

Since
\displaystyle\varphi_{l}(y^{\prime})\geq\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})
, we find from (
2.2
) that

\displaystyle\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})

\displaystyle\geq\rho|D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})|\left(\frac{[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]}{\rho^{1+\gamma}c_{1}+2c_{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma+1}}

\displaystyle-\frac{\rho^{\gamma+1}\left([D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}]_{C^{\gamma}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})}+[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}]_{C^{\gamma}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})}\right)[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]}{\rho^{\gamma+1}c_{1}+2c_{2}}.

So with that
\displaystyle[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}]_{C^{\gamma}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})},[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{l}]_{C^{\gamma}(Q_{r+\rho}^{\prime})}\leq c_{1}
, we absorb the last term in the right-hand side to the left-hand side to find that

3[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]\geq\rho|D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{0}(x^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{1}(x^{\prime})|\left(\frac{[\varphi_{l}(x^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(x^{\prime})]}{\rho^{1+\gamma}c_{1}+2c_{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma+1}},

and so the lemma holds.
∎

Estimate on

For the composite cube

.

For this subsection, we employ the letter
\displaystyle c\geq 1
to denote any constants that can be explicitly computed in terms such as
\displaystyle n
,
\displaystyle\kappa
,
\displaystyle\sup_{k\in K_{+}}\|D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}(Q_{2r}^{\prime})}
and the number of elements in the set
\displaystyle K
.

We first handle the case when the two points belong to a same region.

Lemma 2.3
.

Under the assumption (
2.5
) and (
2.6
), we have that

\big|\pi^{\prime}(y)-\pi^{\prime}(z)\big|\leq c\kappa\left(\frac{|y-z|}{R}\right)^{2\mu}\qquad\left(y,z\in\overline{Q_{2r}^{k}}\cap Q_{2r}\right),

for any
\displaystyle k\in K
.

Proof.

Let
\displaystyle y=(y^{1},y^{\prime})\in\overline{Q_{2r}^{k}}\cap Q_{2r}
and
\displaystyle z=(z^{1},z^{\prime})\in\overline{Q_{2r}^{k}}\cap Q_{2r}
. Then

(2.7)

\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})\leq y^{1}\leq\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})\qquad\text{and}\qquad\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})\leq z^{1}\leq\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime}).

To prove the lemma, we take

(2.8)

w=\frac{y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}[\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})]+\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})\in\mathbb{R}.

Then we claim that

(2.9)

w\in[\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime}),\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})]\qquad\text{and}\qquad|(w,z^{\prime})-y|+|(w,z^{\prime})-z|\leq c|y-z|^{\gamma}.

By a direct calculation using (
2.8
), we have from (
2.7
) that

w-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})=\frac{y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}[\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})]\geq 0

and

\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-w=\frac{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-y^{1}}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}[\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})]\geq 0

which implies that

(2.10)

w\in[\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime}),\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})].

To prove the second inequality of
(
2.9
)
, recall from (
2.8
) and (
2.7
) that

w=\frac{y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}\left([\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})]-[\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})]\right)+\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})+y^{1},

and

\left|\frac{y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}\right|\leq 1.

So we obtain that

(2.11)

|w-z^{1}|\leq|\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})|+2|\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})|+|y^{1}-z^{1}|\leq c|y-z|,

and

(2.12)

|w-y^{1}|\leq|\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})|+2|\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})|\leq c|y-z|.

So the claim (
2.9
) holds from (
2.10
), (
2.11
) and (
2.12
).

To estimate
\displaystyle\pi^{\prime}(y)-\pi^{\prime}(z)
, with (
2.8
), we make the following computations that

\displaystyle\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})[z^{1}-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}

\displaystyle=\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})[z^{1}-w+w-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}

\displaystyle=\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})[z^{1}-w]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}+\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})[y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})]}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})},

and

\displaystyle\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})[\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-z^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}

\displaystyle=\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})[\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-w+w-z^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}

\displaystyle=\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})[\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-y^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}+\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})[w-z^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}.

By the definition of
\displaystyle\pi^{\prime}(y)
and
\displaystyle\pi^{\prime}(z)
in (
1.5
),

\displaystyle\pi^{\prime}(z)-\pi^{\prime}(y)

\displaystyle=\left[\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})[z^{1}-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}+\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})[\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-z^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}\right]

\displaystyle-\left[\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})[y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})]}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}+\frac{D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})[\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-y^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}\right].

So it follows that

\displaystyle\pi^{\prime}(z)-\pi^{\prime}(y)

\displaystyle=\frac{[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})][z^{1}-w]}{\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})}

\displaystyle+\frac{[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})][\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-y^{1}]}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}

\displaystyle+\frac{[D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})][y^{1}-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})]}{\varphi_{k+1}(y^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(y^{\prime})}.

It only remains to estimate the right-hand side of the above equality. By (
2.5
),

(2.13)

|D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-D_{x^{\prime}}\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})|\leq\kappa\left(\frac{|\varphi_{k+1}(z^{\prime})-\varphi_{k}(z^{\prime})|}{R}\right)^{2\mu}.

From (
2.7
) and (
2.9
), we have that

(2.14)

So by (
2.6
), (
2.13
) and (

), we obtain that

\big|\pi^{\prime}(z)-\pi^{\prime}(y)\big|\leq c\kappa\left[\left(\frac{|z^{1}-w|}{R}\right)^{2\mu}+\left(\frac{|y^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|}{R}\right)^{2\mu}\right],

and the lemma follows from (
2.11
).
∎

With Lemma

, we now obtain Hölder continuity of

in

.

Under the assumption (

3.

In this section, we obtain gradient estimates for when the coefficients are measurable in one variable. The results in this section is based on
[
6
]
and
[
7
, Lemma 3.5]
, but we write this section for the convenience of the readers. For the extension to nonlinear problems, see
[
4
]
. For this section, we employ the letter
\displaystyle c\geq 1
to denote any constants that can be explicitly computed in terms such as
\displaystyle n
,
\displaystyle N
,
\displaystyle\lambda
and
\displaystyle\Lambda
.

Assume that

(3.1)

\lambda|\xi|^{2}\leq A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})\xi_{\alpha}^{i}\xi_{\beta}^{j}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\left|A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})\right|\leq\Lambda\qquad\left(x\in Q_{2},\ \xi\in\mathbb{R}^{nN}\right),

for some positive constant
\displaystyle\lambda
and
\displaystyle\Lambda
.
Let
\displaystyle h\in W^{1,2}\left(Q_{2},\mathbb{R}^{N}\right)
be a weak solution of

(3.2)

D_{\alpha}\left[A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}h^{j}\right]=0\quad\text{ in }\quad Q_{2}.

We first have the following energy estimate in the following lemma.

Lemma 3.1
.

Under the assumption (
3.1
), let
\displaystyle h
be a weak solution of (
3.2
). Then

\int_{Q_{\rho}}|Dh|^{2}\,dx\leq\frac{c}{\rho^{2}}\int_{Q_{2\rho}}\big|h-(h)_{Q_{2\rho}}\big|^{2}\,dx,

for any
\displaystyle\rho\in(0,1]
.

Proof.

Fix
\displaystyle\rho\in(0,1]
. In view of (
3.2
),

(3.3)

D_{\alpha}\left[A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}\left(h^{j}-(h^{j})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right)\right]=0~\text{ in }~Q_{2\rho}.

Choose a cut-off function
\displaystyle\phi\in C_{c}^{\infty}(Q_{2\rho})
with

(3.4)

0\leq\phi\leq 1,\qquad|D\phi|\leq c\rho^{-1}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\phi=1~\text{ in }~Q_{\rho}.

We test (
3.3
) by
\displaystyle\left[h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right]\phi^{2}
to find that

\displaystyle

\displaystyle\int_{Q_{2\rho}}\left\langle A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}\left[h^{j}-(h^{j})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right],D_{\alpha}\left[h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right]\right\rangle\phi^{2}\,dx

\displaystyle=-\int_{Q_{2\rho}}\left\langle A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}\left[h^{j}-(h^{j})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right],\left[h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right]2\phi D_{\alpha}\phi\right\rangle\,dx.

Then by (
3.1
) and Young’s inequality, we have

\int_{Q_{2\rho}}\big|Dh\big|^{2}\phi^{2}\,dx\leq c\int_{Q_{2\rho}}\big|h-(h)_{Q_{2\rho}}\big|^{2}|D\phi|^{2}\,dx,

and the lemma follows from (
3.4
).
∎

We have the following higher order estimate in the following lemma.

Lemma 3.2
.

Under the assumption (
3.1
), let
\displaystyle h
be a weak solution of (
3.2
). Then for any integer
\displaystyle p\geq 0
, we have that

\int_{Q_{1}}\big|D_{x^{\prime}}^{p}Dh\big|^{2}\,dx\leq c(p)\int_{Q_{2}}\big|h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\big|^{2}\,dx.

Proof.

If
\displaystyle p=0
then the lemma holds from Lemma
3.1
. So we assume that
\displaystyle p\geq 1
.

Let
\displaystyle q\in\{0,\cdots,p\}
and
\displaystyle\rho_{q}=1+\frac{1}{q+1}
. Fix
\displaystyle(0,\xi^{\prime})\in\mathbb{N}^{n}
with
\displaystyle|(0,\xi^{\prime})|=q
. Then we have that

(3.5)

D_{\alpha}\Big(A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}\left[D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h^{j}-(h^{j})_{Q_{2\rho}}\right)\right]\Big)=0\text{ in }Q_{2}.

Choose a cut-off function
\displaystyle\phi\in C_{c}^{\infty}(Q_{\rho_{q}})
with

(3.6)

0\leq\phi\leq 1,\qquad|D\phi|\leq c(q)\qquad\text{and}\qquad\phi=1\text{ in }Q_{\rho_{q+1}}.

We test (
3.5
) by
\displaystyle D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2}}\right)\phi^{2}
to find that

\displaystyle

\displaystyle\int_{Q_{\rho_{q}}}\big\langle A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}\left[D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h^{j}-(h^{j})_{Q_{2}}\right)\right],D_{\alpha}\left[D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2}}\right)\right]\big\rangle\phi^{2}\,dx

\displaystyle=-\int_{Q_{\rho_{q}}}\big\langle A_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}(x^{1})D_{\beta}\left[D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2}}\right)\right],\left[D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h^{i}-(h^{i})_{Q_{2}}\right)\right]2\phi D_{\alpha}\phi\big\rangle\,dx.

Then by (
3.2
) and Young’s inequality,

\int_{Q_{\rho_{q}}}\big|DD_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h-(h)_{Q_{2\theta}}\right)\big|^{2}\phi^{2}\,dx\leq c\int_{Q_{\rho_{q}}}\big|D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h-(h)_{Q_{2\theta}}\right)\big|^{2}|D\phi|^{2}\,dx,

whence we have from (

) that

\int_{Q_{\rho_{q+1}}}\big|DD_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\right)\big|^{2}\,dx\leq c(q)\theta^{-2}\int_{Q_{\rho_{q}}}\big|D_{\xi^{\prime}}\left(h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\right)\big|^{2}\,dx.

Since
\displaystyle\xi^{\prime}\in\mathbb{N}^{n-1}
was arbitrary chosen, we find that

\int_{Q_{\rho_{q+1}}}\big|DD_{x^{\prime}}^{q}\left(h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\right)\big|^{2}\,dx\leq c(q)\int_{Q_{\rho_{q}}}\big|D_{x^{\prime}}^{q}\left(h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\right)\big|^{2}\,dx.

Also since
\displaystyle q\in\{0,\cdots,p\}
was arbitrary chosen, if we apply induction, we get that

\int_{Q_{1}}\big|DD_{x^{\prime}}^{p}h\big|^{2}\,dx\leq\int_{Q_{\rho_{p+1}}}\big|DD_{x^{\prime}}^{p}h\big|^{2}\,dx\leq c(p)\int_{Q_{2}}\big|h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\big|^{2}\,dx.

and this complete the proof.
∎

Lemma 3.3
.

Under the assumption (
3.1
), let
\displaystyle h
be a weak solution of (
3.2
). Then

|h(x)-h(y)|^{2}\leq c|x-y|\int_{Q_{2}}\big|h-(h)_{Q_{2}}\big|^{2}\,dx,

for any
\displaystyle x,y\in Q_{1}
.

Proof.

By using an approximation argument, we may assume that
\displaystyle h\in C^{1}(Q_{2})
. To use the Sobolev type embedding, we take an integer
\displaystyle p>\frac{n}{2}
.

Let
\displaystyle(x^{1},x^{\prime}),(y^{1},y^{\prime})\in Q_{1}

Also for any fixed

\displaystyle x^{\prime}

\left|h(x^{1},x^{\prime})-h(y^{1},x^{\prime})\right|^{2}\leq c|x-y|\sum_{0\leq q\leq p}\int_{Q_{1}}\left|D_{x^{\prime}}^{q}D_{1}h(z^{1},z^{\prime})\right|^{2}\,dz^{1}dz^{\prime}.

\displaystyle x^{\prime}
-variable and then in
\displaystyle x^{1}
-variable, we obtain that

\left|D_{x^{\prime}}^{q}h(x^{1},z^{\prime})\right|^{2}\leq c\int_{(-1,1)}\left|D_{x^{\prime}}^{q}h(z^{1},z^{\prime})\right|^{2}+\left|D_{x^{\prime}}^{q}D_{1}h(z^{1},z^{\prime})\right|^{2}\,dz^{1}\qquad(0\leq q\leq p),

\displaystyle z^{1}\in(-1,1)

By combining (
3.7

3.8

Now using Lemma
