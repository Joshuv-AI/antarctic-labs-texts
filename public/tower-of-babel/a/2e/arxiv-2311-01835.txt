Ilya Karzhemanov and Anna Lekontseva

Abstract
. We study surjective (not necessarily regular) rational endomorphisms f f of smooth del Pezzo surfaces X X . We prove that under certain natural non - degeneracy condition f f can have degree bigger than 1 1 only when ( − K X 2 ) > 5 (-K_{X}^{2})>5 . Some structural properties of f f in the case X = ℙ 2 X=\mathbb{P}^{2} are also established.
