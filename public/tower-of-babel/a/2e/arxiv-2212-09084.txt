Congruence subgroups and crystallographic quotients of small Coxeter groups
Pravin Kumar, Tushar Kanta Naik, Mahender Singh

Abstract
. Small Coxeter groups are precisely the ones for which the Tits representation is integral, which makes the study of their congruence subgroups relevant. The symmetric group S n \mathchar 29011_{\mathchar 29038} has three natural extensions, namely, the braid group B n \mathchar 28994_{\mathchar 29038} , the twin group T n \mathchar 29012_{\mathchar 29038} and the triplet group L n \mathchar 29004_{\mathchar 29038} . It turns out that twin groups are related to doodles on the 2-sphere, which were introduced by Fenn and Taylor
