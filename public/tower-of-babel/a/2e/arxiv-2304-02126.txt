Digital Shadows of Safety for Human Robot Collaboration in the World-Wide Lab
Mohamed Behery, Gerhard Lakemeyer

Abstract
The wwl ( wwl ) connects the ds of processes, products, companies, and other entities allowing the exchange of information across company boundaries. Since ds are context- and purpose-specific representations of a process, as opposed to dt which offer a full simulation, the integration of a process into the wwl requires the creation of ds representing different aspects of the process. hrc ( hrc ) for assembly processes was recently studied in the context of the wwl where bt were proposed as a standard task-level representation of these processes. We extend previous work by proposing to standardise safety functions that can be directly integrated into these bt . This addition uses the wwl as a communication and information exchange platform allowing industrial and academic practitioners to exchange, reuse, and experiment with different safety requirements and solutions in the wwl .

The

Entities in the

Previous work

Since robotics and particularly

Task level representations of different robotic activities have been in development over the past several decades.
They usually involve modeling the domain, e.g.,

Recent approaches have shown combinations and transformations between these systems and rule based systems

However, these paradigms are not modular enough to allow code reuse and exchange due to their dependence on problem, domain, and robot descriptions.

Modifying the tree requires less effort and time compared to other graph-based models such as

In addition to storing, sharing, and querying task level description, we allow human operators to exchange safety functions such as checking battery, joint velocities, or whether a human is in the cell of the robot.
Each of these functions can have it’s own interface to the middle-ware (e.g., ROS) of a robot allowing it to read battery or joint state or other perception information such as 3D point cloud data.
As a parameter, they can take topic names of these types of inputs and connect to them directly, since they are condition nodes, their return status encoding whether the system is in a safe state.
Since
