High-order numerical integration on regular embedded surfaces
Gentian Zavalani and Michael Hecht

Abstract
We present a high-order surface quadrature (HOSQ) for accurately approximating regular surface integrals on closed surfaces. The initial step of our approach rests on exploiting square-squeezing –a homeomorphic bilinear square-simplex transformation, re-parametrizing any surface triangulation to a quadrilateral mesh. For each resulting quadrilateral domain we interpolate the geometry by tensor polynomials in Chebyshev–Lobatto grids. Posterior the tensor-product Clenshaw-Curtis quadrature is applied to compute the resulting integral. We demonstrate efficiency, fast runtime performance, high-order accuracy, and robustness for complex geometries.

3
Interpolation in the square

Our high-order format is based on interpolation in tensor-product Chebyshev–Lobatto nodes in the square
\square_{2}
. We start by defining:

Definition 2
(Lagrange polynomials
MIP
;
minterpy
)

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
G_{2,n}=\oplus_{i=1}^{2}\mathrm{Cheb}_{n}
be the tensorial Chebyshev–Lobatto grid, where
\mathrm{Cheb}_{n}=\left\{\cos\left(\frac{k\pi}{n}\right)\;:\;0\leq k\leq n\right\}
,
indexed by a multi-index set
A_{2,n}=\{\alpha\in\mathbb{N}^{2}:\|\alpha\|_{\infty}\leq n\}^{2}
.
For each
\alpha\in A_{2,n}
, the tensorial multivariate Lagrange polynomials are

L_{\alpha}(x)=\prod_{i=1}^{2}l_{\alpha_{i},i}(x)\,,\quad l_{j,i}(x)=\prod_{k=0,k\neq j}^{n}\frac{x_{i}-p_{k,i}}{p_{j,i}-p_{k,i}}\,.

(8)

The Lagrange polynomials are a basis of the polynomial space
\Pi_{2,n}=\mathrm{span}\{\mathrm{x}^{\alpha}=x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}x_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}:\alpha\in A_{2,n}\}
induced by
A_{2,n}
.
Since the

L_{\alpha}(p_{\beta})=\delta_{\alpha,\beta}

for all
\alpha,\beta\in A_{2,n}
,
p_{\alpha}\in G_{2,n}
,
we deduce that
given a function
f:\square_{2}\to\mathbb{R}
, the interpolant
Q_{G_{2,n}}f\in\Pi_{2,n}
of

in
G_{2,n}
is

Q_{G_{2,n}}f=\sum_{\alpha\in A_{2,n}}f(p_{\alpha})L_{\alpha}.

(9)
