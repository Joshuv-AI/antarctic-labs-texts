Introduction

The

.

[

Let

Conjecture

is wide open. The only case for which we know tight bound is when

. Thomassen
[
9
]
and, independently,

[

]

showed that

Even the upper bound on

[

showed that

. One may see the detailed story on the study of Wegner’s conjecture in
[

]
.

The maximum average degree of a graph

Note that if

Recently, following problem was considered in
[

]

(
[

]
)

For each integer

For

[

]
conjectured that

Furthermore, Hocquard, Kim, and Pierron

.

is

-degenerate. So in general we have the following bounds on

[

Does there exist
