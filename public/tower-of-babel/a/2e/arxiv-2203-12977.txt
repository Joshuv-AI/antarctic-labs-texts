Our result, together with Proposition

Lemma 3.2

We have already noticed after Definition

given by

We recall that

of sheaves with singular support in

We remark that

[

Cha+09

ELZ02

KS18

ZC05
]

(1)

(2)

where the limit is that of the directed system given by the

It is a well-known result that persistence modules have a decomposition as sum of barcodes : this is Gabriel’s
theorem (
[

]
on quivers in the finite case and Crawley-Boevey’s theorem (
[
Cra15
]
) in the

is useless here.) We remark that the stratification consists only of a finite set of
points in
\mathbb{R}
and the connected components of its complement, where

is constant. This finite set is thus the
projection to

of

. If

.

In
[
KS18
]
it is explained how to go from the case of a finite stratification to the case of a locally

is at most countable). By
[

]
