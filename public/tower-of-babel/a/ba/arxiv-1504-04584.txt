Diophantine Equations and Congruent Number Equation Solutions
Mamuka Meskhishvili

Abstract
By using pairs of nontrivial rational solutions of congruent number equation C N : y 2 = x 3 − N 2 ​ x , C_{N}:\;\;y^{2}=x^{3}-N^{2}x, constructed are pairs of rational right (Pythagorean) triangles with one common side and the other sides equal to the sum and difference of the squares of the same rational numbers. The parametrizations are found for following Diophantine systems: ( p 2 ± q 2 ) 2 − a 2 \displaystyle(p^{2}\pm q^{2})^{2}-a^{2} = □ 1 , 2 , \displaystyle=\square_{1,2}\,, c 2 − ( p 2 ± q 2 ) 2 \displaystyle c^{2}-(p^{2}\pm q^{2})^{2} = □ 1 , 2 , \displaystyle=\square_{1,2}\,, a 2 + ( p 2 ± q 2 ) 2 \displaystyle a^{2}+(p^{2}\pm q^{2})^{2} = □ 1 , 2 , \displaystyle=\square_{1,2}\,, ( p 2 ± q 2 ) 2 − a 2 \displaystyle(p^{2}\pm q^{2})^{2}-a^{2} = ( r 2 ± s 2 ) 2 . \displaystyle=(r^{2}\pm s^{2})^{2}. Keywords. Diophantine equations, Pythagorean triangles, parametrization, congruent number equation, congruent curve. 2010 AMS Classification. 11D25, 11D41, 11D72, 14G05, 14H52.

Introduction

By using rational solution
(x,y)
of congruent number equation

C_{N}:\;\;y^{2}=x^{3}-N^{2}x,

it is possible to construct a rational right triangle
(a,b,c)
with area
N
[1]

\displaystyle a

\displaystyle=\sqrt{x+N}-\sqrt{x-N}\,,

(1)

\displaystyle b

\displaystyle=\sqrt{x+N}+\sqrt{x-N}\,,

\displaystyle c

\displaystyle=2\sqrt{x}\,.

This construction is not possible from every solution. From (
1
) follows: a rational right triangle exists, if and only if solution
x
satisfies:

x=\square\,,\quad x+N=\square\,,\quad x-N=\square\,.

(2)

These conditions we call Fibonacci conditions, because Fibonacci was the first to find such a number:

x=\Big(\frac{41}{12}\Big)^{2},\;\;N=5.

Congruent equation solutions which satisfy (
2
) we call Fibonacci solutions, respectively. Fibonacci solution has three properties:

x=\Big(\frac{L}{K}\Big)^{2},\;\;K=2M,\;\;(L,N)=1,

where
L
,
K
and
M
positive integers (as well
N
).

If there exists even one nontrivial
(y\neq 0)
rational solution of
C_{N}
, that means there exists infinitely many rational solutions. Among them are infinitely many Fibonacci and non-Fibonacci solutions. The tangent and the secant methods can be used to construct infinitely many solutions from the original. Draw the tangent line to
C_{N}
curve at original point
(x,y)
, this line will meet the curve in a second point [1]:

\bigg(\Big(\frac{x^{2}+N^{2}}{2y}\Big)^{2};\frac{(x^{2}+N^{2})(x^{4}+N^{4}-6x^{2}N^{2})}{8y^{3}}\bigg).

Now using a secant is possible to produce a new rational point and so forth.

In this paper is shown the usage of congruent equation solutions to solve Diophantine systems, including Fibonacci and non-Fibonacci solutions as well. Through them are constructed pairs of rational right (Pythagorean) triangles with one common side and the other sides equal to the sum and difference of the squares of the same rational numbers
p^{2}\pm q^{2}
.

2
Common Legs, Hypotenuses
\boldsymbol{p^{2}\pm q^{2}}

The shared (common) legs denote
a=a_{1}=a_{2}
and hypotheses
c_{1,2}=p^{2}\pm q^{2}
. This case is equivalent to solving Diophantine system:

\begin{cases}(p^{2}+q^{2})^{2}-a^{2}=\square\,,\\[8.5359pt]
(p^{2}-q^{2})^{2}-a^{2}=\square\,.\end{cases}

(3)

We try to find nontrivial integer solutions of system (
3

a_{1}b_{1}c_{1}a_{2}b_{2}c_{2}\neq 0,

or in system notations:

apq\neq 0,\quad p\neq q.

By using rational parametrization formulas for unit hyperbola:

\frac{p^{2}+q^{2}}{a}=\frac{1+\xi^{2}}{2\xi}\,,\quad\frac{p^{2}-q^{2}}{a}=\frac{1+\zeta^{2}}{2\zeta}\,,

(4)

where
\xi
and
\zeta
are arbitrary nontrivial rationals. After simplification (

\displaystyle p^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{a(\xi+\zeta)(1+\xi\zeta)}{4\xi\zeta}\,,

(5)

\displaystyle q^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{a(\zeta-\xi)(1-\xi\zeta)}{4\xi\zeta}\,.

From (
5

\displaystyle(\zeta^{2}-\xi^{2})\big(1-(\xi\zeta)^{2}\big)

\displaystyle=\square\,,

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\xi}{\zeta}\Big)(\xi\zeta)\Big(1-\Big(\frac{\xi}{\zeta}\Big)^{2}\Big)\big(1-(\xi\zeta)^{2}\big)

\displaystyle=\square\,.

By using Property 2 in [2] it is possible to express
\xi
and
\zeta
by arbitrary nontrivial different rational solutions
x
and
z

(xz=\square)
of arbitrary
C_{N}
congruent number equation:

\frac{\xi}{\zeta}=\frac{x}{N}\,,\quad\xi\zeta=\frac{z}{N}\,.

Accordingly,

\xi=\frac{\sqrt{xz}}{N}\,,\quad\zeta=\sqrt{\frac{z}{x}}\,.

(6)

Substitute (
6

5
)

\displaystyle p^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{a}{4N\sqrt{xz}}\,(x+N)(z+N),

\displaystyle q^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{a}{4N\sqrt{xz}}\,(x-N)(z-N).
