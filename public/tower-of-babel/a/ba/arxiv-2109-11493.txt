Asymptotic stability analysis of Riemann-Liouville fractional stochastic neutral differential equations
Arzu Ahmadova, Nazim I. Mahmudov

Abstract
The novelty of our paper is to establish results on asymptotic stability of mild solutions in p p th moment to Riemann-Liouville fractional stochastic neutral differential equations (for short Riemann-Liouville FSNDEs) of order α ∈ ( 1 2 , 1 ) \alpha\in(\frac{1}{2},1) using a Banach’s contraction mapping principle. The core point of this paper is to derive the mild solution of FSNDEs involving Riemann-Liouville fractional time-derivative by applying the stochastic version of variation of constants formula. The results are obtained with the help of the theory of fractional differential equations, some properties of Mittag-Leffler functions and asymptotic analysis under the assumption that the corresponding fractional stochastic neutral dynamical system is asymptotically stable. Keywords: Riemann-Liouville fractional derivative, fractional stochastic neutral dynamical systems, existence and uniqueness, asymptotic stability, continuity of mild solutions in p p th moment

4
Asymptotic stability analysis of Riemann-Liouville fractional neutral differential equations

We assume that
\mathscr{D}
is a space of all
\mathbb{R}^{n}
-valued
\mathbb{F}_{t}
-adapted process
\varphi
satisfying the following norm defined by

\|\varphi\|_{\mathscr{D}}^{p}=\sup_{t\geq 0}\textbf{E}\|\varphi(t)\|^{p}.

where
\varphi
is continuous in
t
such that
\textbf{E}\|\varphi(t)\|^{p}\to 0
as
t\to 0
.
It is clear that
\mathscr{D}
is a Banach space with respect to the norm
\|\cdot\|_{\mathscr{D}}
.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
p\geq 2
be an integer and suppose that Assumptions
2.1
-
2.4
hold. Then, the mild solution of (
2.5
) is asymptotically stable in the
p
th moment.

Proof.

First, we show that
\Psi(\mathscr{D})\subset\mathscr{D}
. Let

. We have

\displaystyle\textbf{E}\|(\Psi X)(t)\|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\|t^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}(t^{\alpha}A)\rho\|^{p}+6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\|g(t,X(t))\|^{p}

\displaystyle+6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}A(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)g(\tau,X(\tau))\mathrm{d}\tau\right\rVert^{p}

\displaystyle+6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)b(\tau,X(\tau))\mathrm{d}\tau\right\rVert^{p}

\displaystyle+6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)\sigma(\tau,X(\tau))\mathrm{d}W(\tau)\right\rVert^{p}.

(4.1)

Now we estimate the terms on the right-hand-side of (
4
). By Assumption
2.1
and according to the Lemma
2.2
(i), first we have

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\|t^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}(t^{\alpha}A)\rho\|^{p}\to 0,\quad\text{as}\quad t\to\infty.

For
X(t)\in\mathscr{D}
and for any
\epsilon>0
, there exists a
t_{1}>0
such that
\textbf{E}\|X(t)\|^{p}\leq\epsilon
for
t\geq t_{1}
. Therefore,

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\|g(t,X(t))\|^{p}

\displaystyle=6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\|g(t,X(t))-g(t,0)+g(t,0)\|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\|g(t,X(t))-g(t,0)\|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}L^{p}_{g}\textbf{E}\|X(t)\|^{p}\to 0,\quad\text{as}\quad t\to\infty.

For the fourth term, by Assumptions
2.2
and
2.4
:

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}A(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)g(\tau,X(\tau))\mathrm{d}\tau\right\rVert^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left(\int_{0}^{t}\|A\|(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}\|E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)\|\|g(\tau,X(\tau))\|\mathrm{d}\tau\right)^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}L_{g}^{p}\|A\|^{p}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}\left((t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}\|E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)\|\right)^{\frac{p}{p-1}}\mathrm{d}\tau\right\rVert^{p-1}\int_{0}^{t}\textbf{E}\|X(\tau)\|^{p}\mathrm{d}\tau

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}L_{g}^{p}\|A\|^{p}\left(\int_{0}^{t}\left(\tau^{\alpha-1}\|E_{\alpha,\alpha}(\tau^{\alpha}A)\|\right)^{\frac{p}{p-1}}\mathrm{d}\tau\right)^{p-1}\int_{0}^{t}\textbf{E}\|X(\tau)\|^{p}\mathrm{d}\tau\to 0,\quad\text{as}\quad t\to\infty.

Similarly, applying Hölder inequality, Assumptions
2.2
and
2.4
, we also have

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)b(\tau,X(\tau))\mathrm{d}\tau\right\rVert^{p}

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left(\int_{0}^{t}(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}\|E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)\|\|b(\tau,X(\tau))\|\mathrm{d}\tau\right)^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}L_{b}^{p}\left(\int_{0}^{t}\left(\tau^{\alpha-1}\|E_{\alpha,\alpha}(\tau^{\alpha}A)\|\right)^{\frac{p}{p-1}}\mathrm{d}\tau\right)^{p-1}\int_{0}^{t}\textbf{E}\|X(\tau)\|^{p}\mathrm{d}\tau\to 0,\quad\text{as}\quad t\to\infty.

For the sixth term of (
4
), we arrive at

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}(t-\tau)^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}((t-\tau)^{\alpha}A)\sigma(\tau,X(\tau))\mathrm{d}W(\tau)\right\rVert^{p}

\displaystyle\leq 6^{p-1}C_{p}L_{\sigma}^{p}\left(\int_{0}^{t}\left[\tau^{2\alpha-2}\|E_{\alpha,\alpha}(\tau^{\alpha}A)\|^{2}\right]^{\frac{p}{p-2}}\mathrm{d}\tau\right)^{\frac{p-2}{2}}\int_{0}^{t}\textbf{E}\|X(\tau)\|^{p}\mathrm{d}\tau\to 0,\quad\text{as}\quad t\to\infty.

\displaystyle 6^{p-1}M^{p}T^{p(\alpha-1)}\delta

\displaystyle+6^{p-1}\Big[L_{g}^{p}+L^{p}_{g}\|A\|^{p}M^{p}\left(\frac{p-1}{p\alpha-1}\right)^{p-1}T^{p\alpha-1}

\displaystyle+L_{b}^{p}M^{p}\left(\frac{p-1}{p\alpha-1}\right)^{p-1}T^{p\alpha-1}+C_{p}L_{\sigma}^{p}M^{p}\left(\frac{T^{2\alpha-1}}{2\alpha-1}\right)^{p/2}\Big]\epsilon<\epsilon
