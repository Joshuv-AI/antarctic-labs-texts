Abstract
Seamless situational awareness provided by modern radar systems relies on effective methods for mot ( mot ). This paper presents a graph-based Bayesian method for nonlinear and high-dimensional mot problems that embeds particle flow. To perform operations on the graph effectively, particles are migrated towards regions of high likelihood based on the solution of a partial differential equation. This makes it possible to obtain good object detection and tracking performance with a relatively small number of particles even if object states are high dimensional and sensor measurements are very informative. Simulation results demonstrate reduced computational complexity and memory requirements as well as favorable detection and estimation accuracy in a challenging 3-D mot scenario.

VI
Conclusion

We presented a graph-based Bayesian method for challenging nonlinear and high-dimensional
mot
problems that relies on particle filtering. Particle degeneracy is avoided by performing operations on the graph using particle flow. Our numerical results demonstrate reduced computational complexity and memory requirements as well as favorable detection and estimation accuracy in a 3-D
mot
scenario. The introduced approach is expected to be particularly appealing for large scale machine perception problems
[
25
]
. Possible directions of future research also include graph-based methods with embedded particle flow for simultaneous localization and object tracking
[
26
]
, extended object tracking
[
27
]
, and information-seeking control
[
28
]
.
