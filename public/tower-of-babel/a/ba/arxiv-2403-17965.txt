Abstract.

I considered solving of the system of linear equations

a{}_{\boldsymbol{{\color[rgb]{1,0.3,0.6}\it j}}}^{\boldsymbol{{\color[rgb]{1,0.3,0.6}\it i}}}{}_{s0}x^{\boldsymbol{{\color[rgb]{1,0.3,0.6}\it j}}}a{}_{\boldsymbol{{\color[rgb]{1,0.3,0.6}\it j}}}^{\boldsymbol{{\color[rgb]{1,0.3,0.6}\it i}}}{}_{s1}=b^{\boldsymbol{{\color[rgb]{1,0.3,0.6}\it i}}}

over non-commutative associative algebra.
I considered examples in quaternion algebra.
I considered also Newton’s method to solve the equation

f(x)=a

over non-commutative associative algebra.
