Some examples of algebraic surfaces with canonical map of degree 20
NGUYEN BIN

Abstract
In this note, we construct two minimal surfaces of general type with geometric genus p g = 3 p_{g}=3 , irregularity q = 0 q=0 , self-intersection of the canonical divisor K 2 = 20 , 24 K^{2}=20,24 such that their canonical map is of degree 20 20 . In one of these surfaces, the canonical linear system has a non-trivial fixed part. These surfaces, to our knowledge, are the first examples of minimal surfaces of general type with canonical map of degree 20 20 .
