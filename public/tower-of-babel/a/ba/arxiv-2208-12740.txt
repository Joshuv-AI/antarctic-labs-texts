Blending type Approximations by Kantorovich variant of α \alpha -Schurer operators
Nadeem Rao, Mamta Rani, Adem Kiliçman, Pradeep Malik, Mohammad Ayman-Mursaleen *

Abstract
. In the present manuscript, we present a new sequence of operators, i . e . i.e. , α \alpha -Bernstein-Schurer-Kantorovich operators depending on two parameters α ∈ [ 0 , 1 ] \alpha\in[0,1] and ρ > 0 \rho>0 for one and two variables to approximate measurable functions on [ 0 : 1 + q ] , q > 0 [0:1+q],q>0 . Next, we give basic results and discuss the rapidity of convergence and order of approximation for univariate and bivariate of these sequences in their respective sections. Further, Graphical and numerical analysis are presented. Moreover, local and global approximation properties are discussed in terms of first and second order modulus of smoothness, Peetre’s K-functional and weight functions for these sequences in different spaces of functions.

4.
Pointwise Approximation results

Here, we consider the Lipschitz type space
[
19
]
as

\displaystyle Lip_{M}^{k_{1},k_{2}}(\gamma)\!:=\!\Big\{f\!\in\!C_{B}[0,1+p],q>0:|f(t)\!-\!f(u)|\!\leq\!M\frac{|t\!-\!u|^{\gamma}}{(t\!+\!k_{1}u\!+\!k_{2}u^{2})^{\frac{\gamma}{2}}}:u,t\in(0,\infty)\Big\},

where
M\geq 0
is a real valued constant number,
k_{1},k_{2}>0,

\rho>0
and
\gamma\in(0,1]
.

Theorem 4.1
.

For
f\in Lip_{M}^{k_{1},k_{2}}(\gamma)
, one yield

\displaystyle|K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}(f;u)-f(u)|\leq M\Bigg(\frac{\eta_{n}^{*}(u)}{k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}\Bigg)^{\frac{\gamma}{2}},

(4.1)

where
u>0
and
\eta_{n}^{*}(u)=K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}(\psi_{u}^{2};u)
.

Proof.

For
\gamma=1
, we have

\displaystyle|K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}(f;u)-f(u)|

\displaystyle\leq K_{n,\lambda}^{\gamma}{(|f(t)-f(u)|)}(u)

\displaystyle\leq MK_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}\left(\frac{|t-u|}{(t+k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}}};u\right).

Since
\frac{1}{t+k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}<\frac{1}{k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}
for all
t,u\in(0,\infty)
, we get

\displaystyle|K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}(f;u)-f(u)|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{M}{(k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}}}(K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}((t-u)^{2};u))^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq M\Bigg(\frac{\eta_{n}^{*}(u)}{k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}\Bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

This implies that for
\gamma=1,
this result stand good. Now, for
\gamma\in(0,1)
and using Hölder’s inequality with
p=\frac{2}{\gamma}
and
q=\frac{2}{2-\gamma}
, one obtain

\displaystyle|K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}(f;u)-f(u)|

\displaystyle\leq\left(K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}((|f(t)-f(u)|)^{\frac{2}{\gamma}};u)\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq M\left(K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}\left(\frac{|t-u|^{2}}{(t+k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2})};u\right)\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{2}}.

Since
\frac{1}{t+k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}<\frac{1}{k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}
for all
t,u\in(0,\infty)
, we obtain

\displaystyle|K_{n,\lambda}^{\rho}(f;u)-f(u)|

\displaystyle\leq M\Bigg(\frac{\mathcal{P}_{n}^{\mu,\nu}\left(|t-u|^{2};u\right)}{k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}\Bigg)^{\frac{\gamma}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq M\Big(\frac{\eta_{n}^{*}(u)}{k_{1}u+k_{2}u^{2}}\Big)^{\frac{\gamma}{2}}.

Hence, we arrive at the desired result.
∎
