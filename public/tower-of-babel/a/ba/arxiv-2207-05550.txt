Abstract
. For a measurable space ( X , 𝒜 X,\mathcal{A} ), let ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) be the corresponding ring of all real valued measurable functions and let μ \mu be a measure on ( X , 𝒜 X,\mathcal{A} ). In this paper, we generalize the so-called m μ m_{\mu} and U μ U_{\mu} topologies on ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) via an ideal I I in the ring ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) . The generalized versions will be referred to as the m μ I m_{\mu_{I}} and U μ I U_{\mu_{I}} topology, respectively, throughout the paper. L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) stands for the subring of ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) consisting of all functions that are essentially I I -bounded (over the measure space ( X , 𝒜 , μ X,\mathcal{A},\mu )). Also let I μ ( X , 𝒜 ) = { f ∈ ℳ ( X , 𝒜 ) : for every g ∈ ℳ ( X , 𝒜 ) , f g is essentially I I_{\mu}(X,\mathcal{A})=\big\{f\in\mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}):\,\text{for every}\,g\in\mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}),fg\,\,\text{is essentially}\,I - bounded } \text{bounded}\big\} . Then I μ ​ ( X , 𝒜 ) I_{\mu}(X,\mathcal{A}) is an ideal in ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) containing I I and contained in L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) . It is also shown that I μ ​ ( X , 𝒜 ) I_{\mu}(X,\mathcal{A}) and L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) are the components of 0 0 in the spaces m μ I m_{\mu_{I}} and U μ I U_{\mu_{I}} , respectively. Additionally, we obtain a chain of necessary and sufficient conditions as to when these two topologies coincide. In particular, it is proved that they coincide if and only if the three sets ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) , L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) , and I μ ​ ( X , 𝒜 ) I_{\mu}(X,\mathcal{A}) coincide and this holds when and only when ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) , with either topology, is connected, which is further equivalent to each of these two topological spaces being a topological ring as well as a topological vector space. Furthermore, it is easy to verify that L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) is a pseudonormed linear space regardless of the choice of I I . Assuming I I is a real maximal ideal in ℳ ⁡ ( X , 𝒜 ) \mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A}) , we show that L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) is complete. We construct two examples of spaces of the form L I ∞ ​ ( μ ) L_{I}^{\infty}\left(\mu\right) , each of which is not only non-separable but is also based on a non-trivial ideal I I .

Suppose

constitutes a base for the closed sets of some topology, familiarly known as the Stone-topology on

We now define two binary relations ‘

’ and ‘

-topology =

-topology =

The following theorem essentially says that, these two classifications of ideals in

and
\cal{I}/_{\approx}

Theorem 4.5
.

For two ideals

and

\cal{I}
,
I\sim J
if and only if
I\approx J
if and only if
I=J
.

Proof.

If
I=J
, then it is trivial that

and
I\approx J
. To prove the non-trivial part of this theorem assume that

and hence

for some subset

of
\widehat{X}
. Therefore

for some point

cl
{}_{\widehat{X}}Z(f)

{}_{\widehat{X}}Z(f)\cap X\subseteq\bar{A}\cap X

Let

-topology. To get the desired contradiction we shall check that

for each

on the other hand

and hence

cl
{}_{\widehat{X}}Z(g)
. But as

is an open neighbourhood of

\widehat{X}
. This surely implies that

-topology =
m_{I}
-topology on

in the

\subseteq

. The theorem is completely proved.
