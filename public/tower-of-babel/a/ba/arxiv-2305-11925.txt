C-CLASS FUNCTIONS AND FIXED POINTS FOR WEAKLY CONTRACTIVE MAPPINGS IN RECTANGULAR b-METRIC SPACES
Mohamed Rossafi 1∗ , Abdelkarim Kari 2 and Arsalan Hojjat Ansari 3

Abstract
. In this paper, inspired by the concept of generalized weakly contractive mappings in metric spaces, we introduce C-Class function and fixed point theory for weakly contractive in the setting of rectangular b b -metric spaces and established existence and uniqueness of fixed points for the mappings introduced.
