Pentactions and action representability in the category of reduced groups with action
Tamar Datuashvili, Tunçar Þahan

Abstract
A notion of pentaction of any object in the category 𝐫𝐆𝐫 ∙ \mathbf{rGr}^{\bullet} of reduced groups with action is introduced. The operations are defined in the set 𝖯𝖾𝗇𝗍𝖺𝖼𝗍 ⁡ ( A ) \mathsf{Pentact}(A) of pentactions of an object A A of 𝐫𝐆𝐫 ∙ \mathbf{rGr}^{\bullet} . It is proved that if an object A A is perfect with zero weak stabilizer in the sense defined in the paper, then 𝖯𝖾𝗇𝗍𝖺𝖼𝗍 ⁡ ( A ) \mathsf{Pentact}(A) is an object of 𝐫𝐆𝐫 ∙ \mathbf{rGr}^{\bullet} , it has a derived action on A A , the object A A is action representable and 𝖯𝖾𝗇𝗍𝖺𝖼𝗍 ⁡ ( A ) \mathsf{Pentact}(A) represents all actions on A A . Mathematics Subject Classification (2020): 08A99, 08C05, 22F05. Keywords: Group with action, pentaction, action representability

Theorem 3.14

{\mathbb{b}}^{\left({\mathbb{b}^{\prime}}^{\mathbb{b}^{\prime\prime}}\right)}
and
{\mathbb{b}}^{\mathbb{b}^{\prime}}
.

We have to show

{a}^{\left({b}^{\left({b^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime\prime}}\right)}\right)}={a}^{\left({b}^{b^{\prime}}\right)}.

The both sides are equal to
{a}^{b}
by the definition of action operation in
\mathsf{Pentact}(A)
.

It is left to show

{\left({b}^{\left({b^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime\prime}}\right)}\right)}^{a}={\left({b}^{b^{\prime}}\right)}^{a},

for any elements of
{\mathbb{b}}^{\left({\mathbb{b}^{\prime}}^{\mathbb{b}^{\prime\prime}}\right)}
and
{\mathbb{b}}^{\mathbb{b}^{\prime}}
, and for any
a\in A
. The left side is equal to

{\left({b}^{\left(\left({b^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime\prime}}\right)\cdot a\right)}\right)}^{\left({b^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime\prime}}\right)}={\left({b}^{a}\right)}^{\left({b^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime\prime}}\right)}={\left({b}^{a}\right)}^{b^{\prime}}.

The right side is equal to

{\left({b}^{\left(b^{\prime}\cdot a\right)}\right)}^{b^{\prime}}={\left({b}^{a}\right)}^{b^{\prime}},

by Corollary
3.6
since

is perfect. For the dual actions the corresponding equalities are proved similarly, which ends the proof of Theorem

∎
