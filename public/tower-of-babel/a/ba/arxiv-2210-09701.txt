Abstract
We design an operator from the infinite-dimensional Sobolev space 𝑯 ⁡ ( curl ) \bm{H}(\mathrm{curl}) to its finite-dimensional subspace formed by the Nédélec piecewise polynomials on a tetrahedral mesh that has the following properties: 1) it is defined over the entire 𝑯 ⁡ ( curl ) \bm{H}(\mathrm{curl}) , including boundary conditions imposed on a part of the boundary; 2) it is defined locally in a neighborhood of each mesh element; 3) it is based on simple piecewise polynomial projections; 4) it is stable in the 𝑳 2 \bm{L}^{2} -norm, up to data oscillation; 5) it has optimal (local-best) approximation properties; 6) it satisfies the commuting property with its sibling operator on 𝑯 ⁡ ( div ) \bm{H}(\mathrm{div}) ; 7) it is a projector, i.e., it leaves intact objects that are already in the Nédélec piecewise polynomial space. This operator can be used in various parts of numerical analysis related to the 𝑯 ⁡ ( curl ) \bm{H}(\mathrm{curl}) space. We in particular employ it here to establish the two following results: i) equivalence of global-best, tangential-trace- and curl-constrained, and local-best, unconstrained approximations in 𝑯 ⁡ ( curl ) \bm{H}(\mathrm{curl}) including data oscillation terms; and ii) fully h h - and p p - (mesh-size- and polynomial-degree-) optimal approximation bounds valid under the minimal Sobolev regularity only requested elementwise. As a result of independent interest, we also prove a p p -robust equivalence of curl-constrained and unconstrained best-approximations on a single tetrahedron in the 𝑯 ⁡ ( curl ) \bm{H}(\mathrm{curl}) -setting, including h ​ p hp data oscillation terms.

3.1
A stable local commuting projector in

We need to first recall (a slightly modified version of) the projector

Definition 3.1

(A stable local commuting projector in
\bm{H}(\mathrm{div})
)
.

Let

(3.1)

2.

Define a Raviart–Thomas polynomial
\bm{\sigma}_{h}^{\bm{a}}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-1.00006ptT}}\hskip-2.5pt_{p}({\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}})\cap\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{div},{\omega_{\bm{a}}})

(3.2)

3.

Extending

by zero outside of the patch subdomain
{\omega_{\bm{a}}}

from (

). In Step

, we simply project
\bm{w}
to the

space elementwise, under the divergence constraint. The intermediate field
\bm{\tau}_{h}
is close to

but does not in general lie in

, possibly having a discontinuous normal trace. This is corrected patchwise in Step
2
: a cut-off of

is made by the hat function
\psi^{\bm{a}}
, the canonical elementwise projector
\bm{I}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}
from (

projection (
2.10
) of

onto piecewise
p

:

\begin{split}\nabla{\cdot}\bm{P}_{h}^{p,\mathrm{div}}(\bm{w})&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{eq_sigma}}}{{=}}\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\sigma}_{h}=\sum_{{\bm{a}}\in\mathcal{V}_{h}}\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\sigma}_{h}^{\bm{a}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{eq_sigma_a}}}{{=}}\sum_{{\bm{a}}\in\mathcal{V}_{h}}\Pi_{h}^{p}(\nabla{\cdot}(\psi^{\bm{a}}\bm{w}))\\

(3.4)

using the linearity of the divergence operator, the linearity of the elementwise
L^{2}
projection (
2.10
), and the partition of unity by the hat functions (
2.1
).

\bm{H}_{0,\mathrm{N}}(\mathrm{curl},\Omega)
, is composed of simple piecewise polynomial projections, and is defined locally, in a neighborhood of each mesh vertex. The operator

is, however, not defined over the entire

The purpose of Step

and other piecewise polynomials, which in particular justifies the use of the canonical elementwise Raviart–Thomas and Nédélec projectors

At the continuous level,

3.3

Theorem 3.9

(Equivalence of local-best and global-best approximations in
\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl})
on general domains and including data oscillation)
.

Let
\bm{v}\in\bm{H}_{0,\mathrm{N}}(\mathrm{curl},\Omega)
, a simplicial mesh
{\mathcal{T}_{h}}
of
\Omega
, and a polynomial degree
p\geq 0
be fixed. Then

\begin{split}{}&\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({\mathcal{T}_{h}})\cap\bm{H}_{0,\mathrm{N}}(\mathrm{curl},\Omega)\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{v}_{h}=\bm{P}_{h}^{p,\mathrm{div}}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\end{subarray}}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|^{2}+\sum_{{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}\bigg(\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\|_{K}\bigg)^{2}\\
\approx{}&\sum_{{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}\bigg[\min_{\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}^{2}+\bigg(\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\|_{K}\bigg)^{2}\bigg],\end{split}

(3.21a)

and

\begin{split}{}&\min_{\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({\mathcal{T}_{h}})\cap\bm{H}_{0,\mathrm{N}}(\mathrm{curl},\Omega)}\bigg[\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|^{2}+\sum_{{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}\bigg(\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\|\nabla{\times}(\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h})\|_{K}\bigg)^{2}\bigg]\\
\approx{}&\sum_{{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}\bigg[\min_{\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}^{2}+\bigg(\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\|_{K}\bigg)^{2}\bigg],\end{split}

(3.21b)

where the hidden constants only depend on the shape-regularity parameter
\kappa_{{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}
of the mesh
{\mathcal{T}_{h}}
and the polynomial degree
p
.

The constraint in (
3.21a
) uses the projector
\bm{P}_{h}^{p,\mathrm{div}}
from Definition
3.1
. By the projection property of
\bm{P}_{h}^{p,\mathrm{div}}

.
Thus (

) indeed extends
[
14
, Theorem 2]
to the case when the datum

has a non-polynomial curl,

. Moreover, the proofs in

]

).

Remark 3.10

of the mesh
{\mathcal{T}_{h}}

Appendix A
p
-robust equivalence of constrained and unconstrained best-approximation in
\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl})
on a tetrahedron

In this Appendix, we extend
[
14
, Lemma 1]
to functions
\bm{v}
with nonpolynomial curl, in the spirit of
[
26
, Lemma A1]
. This is a consequence of the breakthrough result of Costabel and McIntosh in
[
20
, Proposition 4.2]
. Interestingly enough, the constant hidden in the inequality is here independent of the polynomial degree
p
.

Lemma A.1

(Equivalence of constrained and unconstrained best-approximation on a tetrahedron)
.

Let a polynomial degree
p\geq 0
, a tetrahedron
{K}
, and an arbitrary
\bm{v}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})
be fixed. Let

\bm{\tau}_{h}:=\arg\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{w}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}\hskip-2.04861pt_{p}({K})\\
\nabla{\cdot}\bm{w}_{h}=0\end{subarray}}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{w}_{h}\|_{K}.

(A.1)

Then

\begin{split}\min_{\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}&\leq\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{v}_{h}=\bm{\tau}_{h}\end{subarray}}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}\\
&\lesssim\Big(\min_{\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}+\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K}\Big),\end{split}

(A.2)

where the hidden constant only depends on the shape-regularity
\kappa_{K}:=h_{K}/\rho_{K}
of
{K}
.

Proof.

The first inequality is obvious, since the second minimization set has an additional curl constraint. In order to show the second one, denote respectively by

\bm{\iota}_{h}:=\arg\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{v}_{h}=\bm{\tau}_{h}\end{subarray}}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}

(A.3)

and

\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}:=\arg\min_{\bm{v}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.63885pt_{p}({K})}\|\bm{v}-\bm{v}_{h}\|_{K}

(A.4)

the constrained and unconstrained minimizers. We then need to show

\|\bm{v}-\bm{\iota}_{h}\|_{K}\lesssim\|\bm{v}-\widetilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}+\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K},

(A.5)

where
\lesssim
means inequality up to a constant only depending on the shape-regularity
\kappa_{K}
.

Using (
A.1
) and (
4.2
),
\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\in\{\bm{w}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-1.00006ptT}}\hskip-2.5pt_{p}({K});\,\nabla{\cdot}\bm{w}_{h}=0\}
.
Thus, we can use
[
20
, Proposition 4.2]
, cf. also the reformulation in
[
12
, Theorem 2]
, stipulating the existence of
\bm{\varphi}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.99997pt_{p}({K})
with
\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}_{h}=\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}
such that

\|\bm{\varphi}_{h}\|_{K}\lesssim\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}=\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\end{subarray}}\|\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}.

(A.6)

Shifting now the right-hand side of (
A.6
) by
\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}
, we arrive at

\|\bm{\varphi}_{h}\|_{K}\lesssim\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}=\bm{\tau}_{h}\end{subarray}}\|\bm{\varphi}-\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}.

A primal–dual equivalence as in,
e.g.,
[
13
, Lemma 5.5]
implies (as in Section
2.3
,
\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})
is composed of those
\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})
that verify
\bm{\varphi}{\times}\bm{n}_{K}=0
on
\partial{K}
in the weak sense (
2.3
))

\displaystyle\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}=\bm{\tau}_{h}\end{subarray}}\|\bm{\varphi}-\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}={}

\displaystyle\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}=1\end{subarray}}\big\{(\bm{\tau}_{h},\bm{\varphi})_{K}-(\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h},\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi})_{K}\big\}

\displaystyle\leq{}

\displaystyle\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}=1\end{subarray}}\big\{(\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\varphi})_{K}-(\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h},\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi})_{K}\big\}

\displaystyle+\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}=1\end{subarray}}(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\varphi})_{K}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}=\nabla{\times}\bm{v}\end{subarray}}\|\bm{\varphi}-\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}+\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K},

where, to estimate the second term on the middle line, we have used the technical result of Lemma
A.2
below.

Consequently, since
\bm{v}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{curl},{K})
satisfies the curl constraint above,

\|\bm{\varphi}_{h}\|_{K}\lesssim\|\bm{v}-\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}+\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K}.

(A.7)

Now note that
(\bm{\varphi}_{h}+\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h})\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.99997pt_{p}({K})
with
\nabla{\times}(\bm{\varphi}_{h}+\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h})=\bm{\tau}_{h}
. Thus,
\bm{\varphi}_{h}+\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}
belongs to the minimization set in (
A.3
), and the minimization property (
A.3
) of
\bm{\iota}_{h}
implies
\|\bm{v}-\bm{\iota}_{h}\|_{K}\leq\|\bm{v}-(\bm{\varphi}_{h}+\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h})\|_{K}
. Thus, by virtue of the triangle inequality and using (
A.7
), we altogether infer

\displaystyle\|\bm{v}-\bm{\iota}_{h}\|_{K}

\displaystyle\leq\|\bm{v}-(\bm{\varphi}_{h}+\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h})\|_{K}\leq\|\bm{v}-\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}+\|\bm{\varphi}_{h}\|_{K}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\bm{v}-\tilde{\bm{\iota}}_{h}\|_{K}+\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K},

i.e., (
A.5
), and the proof is finished.
∎

Lemma A.2

(
hp
data oscillation)
.

Let the assumptions of Lemma
A.1
be verified. Then

\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})\\
\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}=1\end{subarray}}(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\varphi})_{K}\lesssim\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K},

where the hidden constant only depends on the shape-regularity
\kappa_{K}:=h_{K}/\rho_{K}
of
{K}
.

Proof.

Fix
\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})
with
\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}=1
. From (
2.15
), there exists
\bm{\psi}\in{\bm{H}}^{1}({K})\cap\bm{H}_{0}(\mathrm{curl},{K})
such that
\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi}=\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}
and

\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{K}\leq\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{K}=1.

Since
\bm{\tau}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-1.00006ptT}}\hskip-2.5pt_{p}({K})
with
\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\tau}_{h}=0
, there exists
\bm{w}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{N}}\hskip-1.99997pt_{p}({K})
such that
\nabla{\times}\bm{w}_{h}=\bm{\tau}_{h}
. Thus, by the Green theorem,

\displaystyle(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\varphi})_{K}

\displaystyle=(\nabla{\times}(\bm{w}_{h}-\bm{v}),\bm{\varphi})_{K}=(\bm{w}_{h}-\bm{v},\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi})_{K}

\displaystyle=(\bm{w}_{h}-\bm{v},\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi})_{K}=(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\psi})_{K},

and we conclude by the following Lemma
A.3
.
∎

Lemma A.3

(
hp
data estimate)
.

Let the assumptions of Lemma
A.1
be verified. Let
\bm{\psi}\in{\bm{H}}^{1}({K})
. Then

|(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\psi})_{K}|\lesssim\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K}\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{K},

where the hidden constant only depends on the shape-regularity
\kappa_{K}:=h_{K}/\rho_{K}
of
{K}
.

Proof.

Let
q\in H^{1}_{0}({K})
be such that

(\nabla q,\nabla v)_{K}=(\bm{\psi},\nabla v)_{K}\qquad\forall v\in H^{1}_{0}({K}).

(A.8)

Defining
\bm{\chi}:=\bm{\psi}-\nabla q
, we have

\bm{\chi}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{div},{K})\text{ with }\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\chi}=0.

(A.9)

Moreover, by the Green theorem, the right-hand side in (
A.8
) can be equivalently written as
(\bm{\psi},\nabla v)_{K}=-(\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\psi},v)_{K}
. Thus, since
{K}
is convex, the elliptic regularity shift, see, e.g.,
[
38
, Chapter 3]
, gives
q\in H^{2}({K})
with

\|\nabla(\nabla q)\|_{K}\lesssim\|\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\psi}\|_{K}\leq\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{K},

where
\lesssim
means inequality up to a constant only depending on the shape-regularity
\kappa_{K}
. Thus, in addition to (
A.9
), there also holds
\bm{\chi}\in{\bm{H}}^{1}({K})
with

\|\nabla\bm{\chi}\|_{K}\leq\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{K}+\|\nabla(\nabla q)\|_{K}\lesssim\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{K}.

(A.10)

Now, since from (
A.1
)
\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v}\in\bm{H}(\mathrm{div},{K})
with
\nabla{\cdot}(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v})=0
, there follows by the Green theorem

(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\nabla q)_{K}=0.

Consequently,

(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\psi})_{K}=(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\chi})_{K}.

(A.11)

Let respectively

\bm{\chi}_{h}:=\arg\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{w}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}\hskip-2.04861pt_{p}({K})\\
\nabla{\cdot}\bm{w}_{h}=0\end{subarray}}\|\bm{\chi}-\bm{w}_{h}\|_{K}

and

\tilde{\bm{\chi}}_{h}:=\arg\min_{\bm{w}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}\hskip-2.04861pt_{p}({K})}\|\bm{\chi}-\bm{w}_{h}\|_{K}

be the constrained and unconstrained Raviart–Thomas approximations of
\bm{\chi}
.
The Euler–Lagrange conditions of (
A.1
) allow us to subtract
\bm{\chi}_{h}
(but not
\tilde{\bm{\chi}}_{h}
) in (
A.11
), so that the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and, crucially, the
p
-robust constrained–unconstrained
\bm{H}(\mathrm{div})
equivalence of
[
26
, Lemma A1]
(note that
\nabla{\cdot}\bm{\chi}=0
) lead to

\begin{split}(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\chi})_{K}&=(\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v},\bm{\chi}-\bm{\chi}_{h})_{K}\leq\|\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v}\|_{K}\|\bm{\chi}-\bm{\chi}_{h}\|_{K}\\
&\lesssim\|\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v}\|_{K}\|\bm{\chi}-\tilde{\bm{\chi}}_{h}\|_{K}.\end{split}

(A.12)

Now, since
\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-1.00006ptT}}\hskip-2.5pt_{p}({K})
contains by (
2.9
) polynomials up to degree
p
in each component and since the minimization for
\tilde{\bm{\chi}}_{h}
is unconstrained, the
hp
approximation bound (
2.17
) gives

\|\bm{\chi}-\tilde{\bm{\chi}}_{h}\|_{K}\lesssim\frac{h_{{K}}}{p+1}\|\nabla\bm{\chi}\|_{K}.

(A.13)

Finally, in addition to (
A.1
), let

\tilde{\bm{\tau}}_{h}:=\arg\min_{\bm{w}_{h}\in\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}\hskip-2.04861pt_{p}({K})}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{w}_{h}\|_{K},

i.e., from (
2.11b
),
\tilde{\bm{\tau}}_{h}=\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})
. It follows once again from the
p
-robust constrained–unconstrained
\bm{H}(\mathrm{div})
equivalence of
[
26
, Lemma A1]
(note that
\nabla{\cdot}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})=0
) that

\|\bm{\tau}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v}\|_{K}\lesssim\|\tilde{\bm{\tau}}_{h}-\nabla{\times}\bm{v}\|_{K}=\big\|\nabla{\times}\bm{v}-\bm{\Pi}_{\bm{\mathcal{R\hskip-0.70004ptT}}}^{p}(\nabla{\times}\bm{v})\big\|_{K}.

(A.14)

Thus, the desired result is a combination of (
A.11
), (
A.12
), (
A.13
), and (
A.14
) together with (
A.10
). ∎

Appendix B
Broken regular decomposition in a patch

Let
{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}
be a vertex patch for a mesh vertex
{\bm{a}}\in\mathcal{V}_{h}
as in Section
2.2
. Recall the local spaces (
2.6
) and (
2.7
), including the notation
{\gamma_{\mathrm{D}}}
standing for the faces sharing the vertex
{\bm{a}}
and lying in
\overline{{\Gamma_{\mathrm{D}}}}
. Then there holds:

Lemma B.1

(Broken regular decomposition in a patch)
.

Let
\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},{\omega_{\bm{a}}})
. Then there exists
\bm{\psi}\in\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},{\omega_{\bm{a}}})
with

\bm{\psi}|_{K}\in{\bm{H}}^{1}({K})\qquad\forall{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}

(B.1a)

such that

\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi}=\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}

(B.1b)

and

\displaystyle\|\bm{\psi}\|_{\omega_{\bm{a}}}

\displaystyle\lesssim h_{\omega_{\bm{a}}}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{\omega_{\bm{a}}},

(B.1c)

\displaystyle\Bigg\{\sum_{{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}}\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{K}^{2}\Bigg\}^{1/2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}\|_{\omega_{\bm{a}}},

(B.1d)

where the hidden constants only depend on the shape-regularity parameter
\kappa_{{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}
of the mesh
{\mathcal{T}_{h}}
.

Proof.

Our proof involves a reference patch. For a fixed shape-regularity parameter
\kappa_{{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}
,
there is a maximal number of tetrahedra
|{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}|
in each vertex patch
{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}
.
Therefore, there exists a finite number of ways these tetrahedra can be connected together
in the patch, and we may, for each value of
\kappa_{{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}
, define a finite set

R
of references patches of unit diameter in such a way that each vertex patch

{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}
in the mesh is the image of an element
\widetilde{\mathcal{T}}\in\textup{R}
through
a piecewise affine map that preserves connectivity.

Let
\widetilde{\mathcal{T}}\in\textup{R}
be the reference patch associated with the given

{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}
, and denote by
\mathcal{A}:\widetilde{\omega}\to{\omega_{\bm{a}}}
the corresponding piecewise affine map,
where
\widetilde{\omega}
is the open subdomain corresponding to
\widetilde{\mathcal{T}}
.
Then, for each
{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}
, we denote by

\big(\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}\bm{v}\big)|_{K}:=\mathbb{J}^{\rm-T}_{{K}}\bm{v}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1},\qquad\big(\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}\bm{v}\big)|_{K}:=(\det\mathbb{J}_{{K}})^{-1}\mathbb{J}_{{K}}\bm{v}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1},

(B.2)

the curl- and divergence-preserving Piola mappings associated with
\mathcal{A}
,
where
\mathbb{J}_{{K}}
is the (constant) Jacobian matrix of
\mathcal{A}
on
{K}
,
see e.g.
[
29
, Section 7.2]
. It is standard that

\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}
maps
\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},\widetilde{\omega})
into
\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},{\omega_{\bm{a}}})
,
where
\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},\widetilde{\omega})
embeds essential boundary conditions on

\mathcal{A}^{-1}({\gamma_{\mathrm{D}}})
.
\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}
and
\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}
are
bijective, and we denote by
(\mathcal{A}^{\rm c})^{-1}
and
(\mathcal{A}^{\rm d})^{-1}

their inverses. Finally, we have the commuting property

\nabla{\times}(\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}\cdot)=\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}(\nabla{\times}\cdot)
.

Consider
\bm{\varphi}\in\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},{\omega_{\bm{a}}})
and let

\widetilde{\bm{\varphi}}:=(\mathcal{A}^{\rm c})^{-1}(\bm{\varphi})\in\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},\widetilde{\omega})
.
We can employ (
2.15
) (where we take
\bm{w}
in
{\bm{H}}^{1}_{0,\mathrm{D}}(\omega)
) on the reference
patch
\widetilde{\omega}
, where
C_{\mathrm{L},\widetilde{\omega}}
is now a generic constant only
depending on
\kappa_{{\mathcal{T}_{h}}}
due to the above discussion. Therefore, there exists

\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\in{\bm{H}}^{1}(\widetilde{\omega})
, with
\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}=0
on

\mathcal{A}^{-1}({\gamma_{\mathrm{D}}})
if
{\gamma_{\mathrm{D}}}
includes at least one face and of componentwise
mean value zero on
\widetilde{\omega}
otherwise such that

\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}=\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\varphi}}\quad\text{ and }\quad\quad\|\nabla\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}\leq C_{\mathrm{L},\widetilde{\omega}}\|\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\varphi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}.

We also note that we have

\|\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}\lesssim\|\nabla\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}\lesssim\|\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}=\|\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\varphi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}

(B.3)

by the Poincaré–Friedrichs inequality (
2.16
), since
\widetilde{\omega}
is of unit diameter.

We then introduce
\bm{\psi}:=\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}(\widetilde{\bm{\psi}})\in\bm{H}^{\dagger}(\mathrm{curl},{\omega_{\bm{a}}})

and claim that it satisfies all the requirements in (
B.1
).
Indeed, elementwise, the Piola mapping
\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}
amounts to an affine
change of coordinates and a multiplication by a constant matrix. Therefore, it is
clear that it preserves smoothness elementwise and (
B.1a
) follows from

\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\in{\bm{H}}^{1}(\widetilde{\omega})
. Moreover, (
B.1b
) holds true since

\displaystyle\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi}

\displaystyle=\nabla{\times}\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}(\widetilde{\bm{\psi}})=\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}(\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\psi}})=\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}(\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\varphi}})=\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}(\nabla{\times}((\mathcal{A}^{\rm c})^{-1}(\bm{\varphi})))

\displaystyle=\mathcal{A}^{\rm d}((\mathcal{A}^{\rm d})^{-1}(\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}))=\nabla{\times}\bm{\varphi}.

The estimates (
B.1c
) and (
B.1d
) then follow from
(
B.3
) and the usual scaling arguments due to the
structure (
B.2
) of the Piola mappings.
Indeed, for each
{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}
, we have, on the one hand,

\displaystyle\|\bm{\psi}\|_{{K}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\|\mathcal{A}^{\rm c}(\widetilde{\bm{\psi}})\|_{{K}}^{2}=\|\mathbb{J}_{K}^{-T}\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1}\|_{{K}}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim h_{K}^{-2}\|\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1}\|_{K}^{2}\lesssim h_{K}^{-2}|{K}|\|\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2}\lesssim h_{K}\|\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2},

where
\widetilde{K}=\mathcal{A}^{-1}({K})
. On the other hand, it is true that

\begin{split}\|\nabla{\times}\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2}&=\|(\mathcal{A}^{\rm d})^{-1}(\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi})\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2}=\|(\det\mathbb{J}_{{K}})\mathbb{J}_{K}^{-1}(\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi})\circ\mathcal{A}\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2}\\
&\lesssim(\det\mathbb{J}_{K})^{2}h_{K}^{-2}\|(\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi})\circ\mathcal{A}\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2}\lesssim\frac{(\det\mathbb{J}_{K})^{2}h_{K}^{-2}}{|{K}|}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi}\|_{{K}}^{2}\\
&\lesssim h_{K}\|\nabla{\times}\bm{\psi}\|_{K}^{2},\end{split}

(B.4)

so that (
B.1c
) follows from (
B.3
).

Finally, we establish (
B.1d
) by directly differentiating in
(
B.2
). Specifically, we have

\partial_{\ell}\bm{\psi}=\mathbb{J}_{K}^{-T}\partial_{\ell}(\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1})=\mathbb{J}_{K}^{-T}\partial_{\ell}(\mathcal{A}^{-1}_{j})(\partial_{j}\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1}),

so that

|\partial_{\ell}\bm{\psi}|\leq h_{K}^{-2}|\nabla\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\circ\mathcal{A}^{-1}|

and

\|\partial_{\ell}\bm{\psi}\|_{K}^{2}\lesssim h^{-4}_{K}|{K}|\|\nabla\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{K}}^{2}.

We therefore arrive at

\sum_{{K}\in{\mathcal{T}_{{\bm{a}}}}}\|\nabla\bm{\psi}\|_{{K}}^{2}\lesssim h_{{\omega_{\bm{a}}}}^{-1}\|\nabla\widetilde{\bm{\psi}}\|_{\widetilde{\omega}}^{2},

and the conclusion follows from (
B.3
) and
(
B.4
).
∎
