What is a motive, though?

It is never clear how much background the audience has to the theory of motives. In an attempt to ground the talk in the “real world” we begin with a result from the origins of the theory. This doesn’t have much to do with the main theorems of the talk, but hopefully will give the reader some kind of picture to associate with the words “motive” and “motivic cohomology”.
