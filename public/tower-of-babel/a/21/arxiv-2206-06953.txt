A

An automorphism of

Example 1.3

Let

-
(q^{2},q,q^{1/2})

-transitive, flag-transitive automorphism group.

Let

,

by
[
17
, Table 8.1]
. Hence

is a
2
-
(q^{2},q,\lambda)

acts
2
-transitively on

. Also,

acts flag-transitively on

,
since

. Moreover
r=\left[G_{0}:G_{0,B}\right]=[SL_{2}(q):SL_{2}(q^{1/2})]=q^{1/2}(q+1)

contains
(2,q-1)

, and when

but not in

∎

Example 1.4
.

Let

be a

-dimensional

-space, with

-

Let

,

2
-
(q^{3},q^{3/2},q)

∎
