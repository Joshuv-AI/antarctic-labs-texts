Planar Turán Numbers of Cycles: A Counterexample
Daniel W. Cranston, Bernard Lidický, Xiaonan Liu, Abhinav Shantanam

Abstract
The planar Turán number ex 𝒫 ​ ( C ℓ , n ) \textrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(C_{\ell},n) is the largest number of edges in an n n -vertex planar graph with no ℓ \ell -cycle. For ℓ ∈ { 3 , 4 , 5 , 6 } \ell\in\{3,4,5,6\} , upper bounds on ex 𝒫 ​ ( C ℓ , n ) \textrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(C_{\ell},n) are known that hold with equality infinitely often. Ghosh, Györi, Martin, Paulo, and Xiao [arxiv:2004.14094] conjectured an upper bound on ex 𝒫 ​ ( C ℓ , n ) \textrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(C_{\ell},n) for every ℓ ≥ 7 \ell\geq 7 and n n sufficiently large. We disprove this conjecture for every ℓ ≥ 11 \ell\geq 11 . We also propose two revised versions of the conjecture.
