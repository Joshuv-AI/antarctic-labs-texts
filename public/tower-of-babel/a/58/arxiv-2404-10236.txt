Dark photon - dark energy stationary axisymmetric black holes
Marek Rogatko

Abstract
Using Ernst formalism, stationary axisymmetric black hole solution in Einstein- dark matter - dark energy gravity has been elaborated. The dark sector was chosen as dark photon concept, where an auxiliary U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) -gauge field coupled to ordinary Maxwell one was introduced, while dark energy was modelled by the existence of positive cosmological constant. Refining our studies to the case of vanishing cosmological constant, the uniqueness theorem for the black hole in question has been proved.

IV.2
Uniqueness of Solutions

It was revealed in Ref.

\partial_{r}\Big[P^{-1}\partial_{r}P\Big]+\partial_{z}\Big[P^{-1}\partial_{z}P\Big]=0,

where by

we have denoted
3\times 3
Hermitian matrices with unit determinants.
Additionally it happens that for any constant, invertible matrix
A
, the matrix
APA^{-1}
is the solution of the relation (
77
),
enabling one to create all the transformations referred to the Ernst’s system of partial differential equations.

Let us assume that the matrix
P

<<{\cal D}>>

{\cal M}
, with boundary
\partial{\cal D}
. Suppose, further that we have
two different matrix solutions of the equation (

P_{1}
and
P_{2}

conditions, and consider
the difference between the aforementioned relations satisfies the equation of the form

\nabla\Big(P_{1}^{-1}\Big(\nabla Q\Big)P_{2}\Big)=0,

(78)

where one sets
Q=P_{1}P_{2}^{-1}
.
Multiplying the equation (
78

and taking the trace of the result, we achieve the following outcome:

\nabla^{2}q=Tr\Bigg[\Big(\nabla Q^{\dagger}\Big)P_{1}^{-1}\Big(\nabla Q\Big)P_{2}\Bigg],

(79)

where we set
q=TrQ
. Hermicity and positive definiteness of the matrix
P

P=M~M^{\dagger}
, which in turn
yields

\nabla^{2}q=Tr\Big({\cal J}^{\dagger}{\cal J}\Big),

(80)

Defining homographic change of the variables, for the previously defined quantities connected with both gauge fields, provided by

(82)

<\xi_{\delta}~{\bar{\xi}}^{\delta}>=-1+\sum\limits_{\gamma}\xi_{\gamma}~{\bar{\xi}}^{\gamma},\qquad\gamma=1,\dots,q.

(83)

In the case under consideration

Moreover, for the brevity of the final notion, we change the notation in the relation (

\Phi_{(\tilde{F})}=E_{(\tilde{F})}+i~B_{(\tilde{F})},\qquad\Phi_{({\tilde{B}})}=E_{({\tilde{B}})}+i~B_{({\tilde{B}})}.

V
Conclusions

In our paper we have elaborated the stationary axisymmetric solution to Einstein-Maxwell
dark matter
-
dark energy
black hole solution.
The

dark photon

dark energy

The obtained metric components of the rotating Kerr-like
dark matter
-
dark energy

(like in Kerr dS spacetime
[
47
,
48
]

imprints its presence by square of electric Maxwell and
dark photon
charges,
square of magnetic charges of both sectors and mixing electric-magnetic charges pertaining to
visible
/
dark
and
dark
/
visible
sectors.

Then, we restrict our attention to the case of asymptotically flat solution and rearrange the adequate Ernst equations into the form of matrix equation.
Choosing the domain of outer communication
<<{\cal D}>>
as a rectangle in two-dimensional manifold with coordinates
(r,~z)
, one shows that the two matrix
solutions of the underlying equations being subject to the same boundary and regularity conditions are equal in
<<{\cal D}>>
.
Thus, one can draw the conclusion that Kerr-like
dark matter
stationary axisymmetric black hole solution to Einstein-Maxwell
dark photon

gravity, authorizes the only stationary axisymmetric solution in the theory under inspection, having non-zero
A_{0},~A_{\phi},~B_{0},~B_{\phi}
