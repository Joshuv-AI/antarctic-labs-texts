Pursuit of new physics within the Λ c → Δ ​ K \Lambda_{c}\to\Delta K decays
Di Wang

Abstract
We point out the C ​ P CP asymmetry in the chain decay of Λ c + → Δ + K ( t ) ( → π + π − ) \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}) is determined by the branching fractions of Λ c + → Δ + ⁣ + ​ K − \Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{++}K^{-} , Λ c + → Δ + ​ K S , L 0 \Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K^{0}_{S,L} and Λ c + → Δ 0 ​ K + \Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{0}K^{+} modes under the isospin symmetry. The ambiguities from loop-induced quantities and S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) F SU(3)_{F} breaking hadronic effects are avoided. Once the C ​ P CP asymmetry in the Λ c + \Lambda_{c}^{+} decays into Δ + \Delta^{+} and neutral kaons is confirmed by experiments, we can check if it is beyond the Standard Model, or verify the C ​ P CP -violating effect resulted from the interference between the Cabibbo-favored and the doubly Cabibbo-suppressed amplitudes with the neutral kaon mixing. Future measurements of branching fractions play a critical role in reducing the uncertainties.



I
Introduction

CP
asymmetry in heavy quark weak decay provides a window to test the Standard Model (SM) and search for new physics (NP).
Charmed hadron system is the only platform to probe
CP
asymmetry in the up-type quark decay in hadron.
The LHCb collaboration reported the discovery of
CP
asymmetry in the charm meson decays in 2019
Aaij:2019kcg
,

\displaystyle\Delta A_{CP}

\displaystyle\equiv A_{CP}(D^{0}\to K^{+}K^{-})-A_{CP}(D^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})

\displaystyle\quad=(-1.54\pm 0.29)\times 10^{-3}.

(1)

And the
CP
asymmetries of single decay modes were measured recently
LHCb:2022vcc
.
In theoretical aspect, the ambiguity of penguin topology results in great difficulty in evaluating
CP
asymmetry of the singly Cabibbo-suppressed (SCS) decay.
The Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) inspired approaches do not work well in charm scale.
Damaged by the almost exact cancellation between the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa (CKM) matrix elements
V_{cd}^{*}V_{ud}
and
V_{cs}^{*}V_{us}
, the penguin topologies fail to extract from branching fractions.
There are two controversial viewpoints for the observed
CP
asymmetry in charm in literature, regarding it as a signal of new physics
Buras:2021rdg
;
Calibbi:2019bay
;
Chala:2019fdb
;
Dery:2019ysp
or the non-perturbative QCD enhancements to penguin
Wang:2022nbm
;
Bediaga:2022sxw
;
Wang:2021rhd
;
Schacht:2021jaz
;
Li:2019hho
;
Cheng:2019ggx
;
Grossman:2019xcj
.

Two sum rules of direct
CP
asymmetry in the linear
SU(3)_{F}
breaking were proposed in Ref.
Muller:2015rna
for avoiding predicting individual
CP
asymmetries.
In these sum rules, the primary uncertainties induced by penguin topologies are eliminated.
To uncover new physics, some coefficients of the sum rules, including the strong phases of tree topologies, need to be determined by the global fit of branching fractions.
However, there are too many parameters in the global fit
Muller:2015lua
. The uncertainties are hard to reduce.
Moreover, the
CP
asymmetry sum rules are broken by the second-order
SU(3)_{F}
breaking effects which turn out to be non-negligible in charm decay

;
Gronau:2015rda

For the purpose of circumventing penguin topology, the

|A_{CP}^{\rm dir}|\leq 1.1\%\,\,(95\%\,\,\rm C.L.)

If the future data exceed the upper bound, it might be a signal of new physics.
But a QCD enhancement of the penguin annihilation
PA

Nierste:2015zra

Besides, the theoretical uncertainties cannot be well controlled since the tree-level decay amplitudes are arisen from the
SU(3)_{F}

The similar problem also appears in the

and
D^{0}\to K^{0}_{S}\overline{K}^{*0}

;
Cheng:2021yrn

And a large penguin annihilation cannot be excluded either.

To establish a ”smoking gun” signal of new physics in charm, one need reliable SM predictions.
With the helplessness of controlling the theoretical uncertainties of charmed meson system, we turn to study the charmed baryon decay. The

Once the
CP

\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})

II
CP
asymmetry

In this section, we discuss
CP
asymmetry in the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
mode.
The mass eigenstates of neutral kaons
K_{S}^{0}
and
K_{L}^{0}
are linear combinations of the flavor
eigenstates
K^{0}
and
\overline{K}^{0}
,

|K_{S,L}^{0}\rangle=\frac{1+\epsilon}{\sqrt{2(1+|\epsilon|^{2})}}|K^{0}\rangle\mp\frac{1-\epsilon}{\sqrt{2(1+|\epsilon|^{2})}}|\overline{K}^{0}\rangle,

(2)

where
\epsilon
is a complex parameter characterizing the
CP
asymmrtry in the kaon mixing with

|\epsilon|=(2.228\pm 0.011)\times 10^{-3}
and
\phi_{\epsilon}=43.52\pm 0.05^{\circ}

PDG
.
In experiments, a
K^{0}_{S}
candidate is reconstructed by its decay into two charged pions at a time close to its lifetime. Not only
K^{0}_{S}
, but also
K^{0}_{L}
serve as the intermediate states in the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
mode through the
K^{0}_{S}-K^{0}_{L}
oscillation
Grossman:2011zk
.
The time-dependent
CP
asymmetry in the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
mode is defined by

A_{CP}(t)\equiv\frac{\Gamma_{\pi\pi}(t)-\overline{\Gamma}_{\pi\pi}(t)}{\Gamma_{\pi\pi}(t)+\overline{\Gamma}_{\pi\pi}(t)},

(3)

where

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\pi\pi}(t)

\displaystyle\equiv\Gamma(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})),

\displaystyle\overline{\Gamma}_{\pi\pi}(t)

\displaystyle\equiv\Gamma(\Lambda^{-}_{c}\to\Delta^{-}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})).

(4)

We write the ratio between
\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K^{0})
and
\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}\overline{K}^{0})
as

\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K^{0})/\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}\overline{K}^{0})=r\,e^{i(\phi+\delta)},

(5)

with the magnitude
r
, the relative strong phase
\delta
, and the weak phase
\phi=(-6.2\pm 0.4)\times 10^{-4}
in the SM
PDG
.

Similarly to the
D
meson decay, the time-dependent
CP
asymmetry of
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
decay is derived to be

\displaystyle A_{CP}(t)\simeq\left(A_{CP}^{\overline{K}^{0}}(t)+A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(t)+A_{CP}^{\rm int}(t)\right)/{D(t)},

(6)

with

\displaystyle A_{CP}^{\overline{K}^{0}}(t)

\displaystyle=2e^{-\Gamma_{K^{0}_{S}}t}\mathcal{R}e(\epsilon)-2e^{-\Gamma_{K}t}\Big(\mathcal{R}e(\epsilon)\cos(\Delta m_{K}t)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\mathcal{I}m(\epsilon)\sin(\Delta m_{K}t)\Big),

(7)

\displaystyle A_{CP}^{\rm int}(t)

\displaystyle=-4\,r\cos\phi\sin\delta\Big[e^{-\Gamma_{K^{0}_{S}}t}\mathcal{I}m(\epsilon)-e^{-\Gamma_{K}t}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\times\Big(\mathcal{I}m(\epsilon)\cos(\Delta m_{K}t)-\mathcal{R}e(\epsilon)\sin(\Delta m_{K}t)\Big)\Big],

(8)

\displaystyle A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(t)

\displaystyle=e^{-\Gamma_{K^{0}_{S}}t}\,2\,r\sin\delta\sin\phi,

(9)

\displaystyle D(t)

\displaystyle=e^{-\Gamma_{K^{0}_{S}}t}(1-2\,r\cos\delta\cos\phi),

(10)

where the average of widths is
\Gamma_{K}\equiv(\Gamma_{K^{0}_{S}}+\Gamma_{K^{0}_{L}})/2
, and the differences of widths and masses are
\Delta\Gamma_{K}\equiv\Gamma_{K^{0}_{S}}-\Gamma_{K^{0}_{L}}
and
\Delta m_{K}\equiv m_{K^{0}_{L}}-m_{K^{0}_{S}}
, respectively.
The first term in Eq. (
6
), which is independent of the hadronic parameters
r
and
\delta
, is
CP
asymmetry in the neutral kaon mixing
Grossman:2011zk
.
The second term is direct
CP
asymmetry induced by the interference between the tree level CF and DCS amplitudes.
The third term is the interference between the CF and DCS amplitudes with the neutral kaon mixing, a new
CP
-violating effect pointed out in Ref.
Yu:2017oky
.
Measurements of
CP
asymmetries depend on time intervals selected in experiments.
The time-integrated
CP
asymmetry in the limit of
t_{1}\ll\tau_{S}\ll t_{2}\ll\tau_{L}
is

\displaystyle A_{CP}(t_{1}\ll\tau_{S}\ll t_{2}\ll\tau_{L})\simeq\left(A_{CP}^{\overline{K}^{0}}+A_{CP}^{\rm dir}+A_{CP}^{\rm int}\right)/{D}

\displaystyle~~~~~=\frac{-2\mathcal{R}e(\epsilon)+2\,r\sin\phi\sin\delta-4\,\mathcal{I}m(\epsilon)\,r\cos\phi\sin\delta}{1-2\,r\cos\phi\cos\delta}.

(11)

In the SM,
A_{CP}^{\overline{K}^{0}}
is well determined by parameter
\epsilon
.

A_{CP}^{\rm int}
and
A_{CP}^{\rm dir}
are estimated to be
\mathcal{O}(10^{-4})
and
\mathcal{O}(10^{-5})
, respectively.

According to Eqs. (
6
)
\sim
(
II
), there are two hadronic inputs in evaluating the time-dependent and time-integrated
CP
asymmetries,
r
and
\delta
.
If
r
and
\delta
are well determined from the branching fractions, the theoretical prediction for the
CP
asymmetry in the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
mode will be very precise.
On the other hand, if there exists a beyond-SM weak phase in the DCS or CF transitions, the
CP
asymmetry will differ from the SM prediction.
Unlike the pure CF or DCS modes or the semileptonic modes, the relative strong phase between
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K^{0}
and
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}\overline{K}^{0}
possibly serves as the strong phase between the SM amplitude and the NP amplitude.
It gets rid of the potential scenario that ultraviolet new physics is hidden due to absence of the relative strong phase.

III
Extraction of hadronic parameters

In the
\Lambda_{c}\to\Delta K
modes, the initial state
\Lambda^{+}_{c}
is an isospin singlet, the final states (
\Delta^{++}
,
\Delta^{+}
,

,

,

) and (
\overline{K}^{0}
,
K^{-}

There are two topologies contributing to the

W
-exchange diagram

W

1

Topological decompositions of the

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{++}K^{-})

\displaystyle=-V_{cs}^{*}V_{ud}\,E^{\prime}_{1},

(12)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}\overline{K}^{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}V_{cs}^{*}V_{ud}\,E^{\prime}_{2},

(13)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K^{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}V_{cd}^{*}V_{us}\,C^{\prime}_{1},

(14)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{0}K^{+})

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}V_{cd}^{*}V_{us}\,C^{\prime}_{2}.

(15)

The opposite sign in Eq. (
12
) and Eq. (

|K^{-}\rangle=-|s\bar{u}\rangle
and
|\overline{K}^{0}\rangle=|s\bar{d}\rangle

\Lambda^{+}_{c}

and

is

or

And the difference between

and
C^{\prime}_{2}
is an exchange of
u\leftrightarrow d
as spectator quarks.
If the isospin breaking is neglected, i.e.,
u(\bar{u})=d(\bar{d})
, we get
E^{\prime}_{1}=E^{\prime}_{2}=E^{\prime}
and
C^{\prime}_{1}=C^{\prime}_{2}=C^{\prime}
.

The sub-leading terms are negligible since they are at order of

\displaystyle\delta=\pm arc\,cos\left(\left|R(\Lambda^{+}_{c},\Delta^{+})/2r\right|\right).

The tree interference induced
CP
asymmetry in charm decay is highly sensitive to new physics due to the tiny weak phase
\phi

Once the
CP
asymmetry in the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Delta^{+}K(t)(\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})

Physics beyond the Standard Model would be confirmed indirectly by high-precision measurements without the possible mockery from low-energy QCD.
Even if no signal of new physics is observed in the

IV
Summary

The
CP
asymmetry of
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
decaying into
\Delta^{+}
and neutral kaons can be extracted from the branching fractions of several
\Lambda_{c}\to\Delta K
decays under the isospin symmetry.
It might be served as a clear hint of new physics in charm sector.
