Supplemental material for “Theoretical bound of the efficiency of learning”
Shanhe Su , 1 , ∗ ,^{1,\;*} Ousi Pan, Shihao Xia, Jincan Chen, Chikako Uchiyama

Abstract
The supplementary information is organized as follows. In Sec. I, on the basis of the Markovian master equation and the time derivative of Shannon’s entropy, the Clausius inequality of the bipartite system is derived. In Sec. II, by following the calculation for the bipartite system, the Clausius inequality of a subsystem will be revealed based on the coarse-grained dynamics. The rate of entropy production of the subsystem will be splited into three components, including the entropy-production rate, time derivative of the Shannon entropy, and rate of information learned. In Sec. III, the lower limit of the rate of entropy production of the subsystem is evaluated, and is transformed to obtain the universal upper bound for the efficiency of learning. The basic thermodynamics quantities for the DQD system and the cellular network are presented in Sec. IV.



I
Thermodynamics of a bipartite system

The Clausius inequality of the bipartite system
Z
can be identified
by utilizing the Markovian master equation [Eq. (1) in the main
text]. First, the time derivative of the Shannon entropy
S=-\sum_{z}p(z)\ln p(z)

of system
Z

Nielsen2010
;
Landi2021
yields

\displaystyle\dot{S}

\displaystyle=-\sum_{z}\dot{p}(z)\ln p(z)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}\left[W_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}p(z^{\prime})-W_{z^{\prime}z}^{v}p(z)\right]\ln p(z)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}J_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}\ln p(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}J_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}\ln\frac{p(z^{\prime})}{p(z)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}J_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}\ln\left[\frac{p(z^{\prime})W_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}}{p(z)W_{z^{\prime}z}^{v}}\right]-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}J_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}\ln\left(\frac{W_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}}{W_{z^{\prime}z}^{v}}\right)

\displaystyle=\dot{\sigma}-\dot{S_{r}}\text{.}

(1)

For a Markov process with the local detailed-balance condition,

\dot{S_{r}}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}J_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}\ln\left(\frac{W_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}}{W_{z^{\prime}z}^{v}}\right)

(2)

is the rate of entropy change of the environment, which is associated
with the energy flow to the environment
Schaller2014
;
Shiraishi2019
.
As
\dot{\sigma}
is the sum of the rates of entropy change in the
system and the environment, i.e.,

\dot{\sigma}=\dot{S}+\dot{S_{r}}\text{,}

(3)

one figures out that
\dot{\sigma}
is the rate of entropy production.
By combining Eqs. (
1
) and (
3
),
\dot{\sigma}

has the following equivalent form
Schnakenberg1976

\dot{\sigma}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{z,z^{\prime},v}J_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}\ln\left[\frac{p(z^{\prime})W_{zz^{\prime}}^{v}}{p(z)W_{z^{\prime}z}^{v}}\right]\geq 0\text{.}

(4)

It is always non-negative because of the form

II
Thermodynamics of the subsystems

In this section, the Clausius inequality of the subsystems will be
revealed based on the coarse-grained dynamics [Eq. (3) in the main
text]. First of all, the rate of information learned
is calculated by the time derivative of the mutual information
I
.
The quantity
I
representing the correlation between subsystems

X
and
Y
is expressed as

I=\sum_{x,y}p(z)\ln\frac{p(z)}{p(x)p(y)}\geq 0\text{,}

(5)

where
p(x)=\sum_{y}p(z)=\sum_{y}p(x,y)
and
p(y)=\sum_{x}p(z)=\sum_{x}p(x,y)

are the marginal probabilities of the states of subsystems
X
and

Y
, respectively. It follows from Jensen’s inequality
that
I
is always nonnegative
Cover1999
. The time derivative
of
I
is divided as

\dot{I}=\dot{I}^{X}+\dot{I}^{Y}

(6)

with

\dot{I}^{X}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{p\left(y|x\right)}{p\left(y|x^{\prime}\right)}

(7)

and

\dot{I}^{Y}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,y,y^{\prime},v}J_{yy^{\prime}\mid x}^{v}\ln\frac{p\left(x|y\right)}{p\left(x|y^{\prime}\right)}.

(8)

Note that

J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}=W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p(x^{\prime},y)-W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)

(9)

is the current due to the transition of
X
from state
x^{\prime}

to state
x
provided that subsystem
Y
is at state
y
. Similarly,

J_{yy^{\prime}\mid x}^{v}=W_{yy^{\prime}\mid x}^{v}p(x,y^{\prime})-W_{y^{\prime}y\mid x}^{v}p(x,y)

(10)

is the current due to the transition of
Y
from state
y^{\prime}

to state
y
when subsystem
X
is at state
x
. As explained in
the main text,
\dot{I}^{X}
and
\dot{I}^{Y}
describe the rates
of learning between
X
and
Y
.

In order to relate the Clausius inequality of a subsystem to the rate
of information learned, we start from the evolution of the Shannon
entropy
S^{X}=-\sum_{x}p(x)\ln p(x)
of subsystem
X
. Differentiating

S^{X}
with respect to time and inserting the master equation of
coarse-grained states of
X
[Eq. (3) in the main text] lead

\displaystyle\dot{S}^{X}

\displaystyle=-\sum_{x}\dot{p}(x)\ln p(x)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{x,x^{\prime},v}\left[V_{xx^{\prime}}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime}\right)-V_{x^{\prime}x}^{v}p\left(x\right)\right]\ln p_{x}

\displaystyle=-\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln p(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{p(x^{\prime})}{p(x)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{p(x^{\prime},y)W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{p(x,y)W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{p(x^{\prime})p(x,y)}{p(x)p(x^{\prime},y)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{p(x^{\prime},y)W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{p(x,y)W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}

The conditional probabilities

p\left(y|x\right)=p\left(x,y\right)/p(x)

Under the condition of local detailed balance,

\dot{S_{r}}^{X}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}

III
the effectiveness of the informational learning

In this section, we derive the limit for the efficiency of learning.
First, the upper bound of
\left|\dot{S_{r}}^{X}\right|
is evaluated
as follows

\displaystyle\left|\dot{S_{r}}^{X}\right|=

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}\sqrt{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)+W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)}\frac{J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{\sqrt{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)+W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)}}\right|

\displaystyle\overset{(a)}{\leq}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}\left(\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}\right)^{2}\left[W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)+W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)\right]}\sqrt{\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}\frac{\left(J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\right)^{2}}{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)+W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)}}

\displaystyle\overset{(b)}{\leq}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}\left(\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}\right)^{2}\left[W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)+W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)\right]}\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}J_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}\left(\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}\right)^{2}\left[W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)+W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}p(x,y)\right]}\sqrt{\dot{\sigma}^{X}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\sum_{x,x^{\prime},y,v}\left(\ln\frac{W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}}{W_{x^{\prime}x\mid y}^{v}}\right)^{2}W_{xx^{\prime}\mid y}^{v}p\left(x^{\prime},y\right)}\sqrt{\dot{\sigma}^{X}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\mathcal{A}^{X}\dot{\sigma}^{X}},

(18)

where the Cauchy-Schwartz inequality
Shiraishi2018
;
Funo2019

and the inequality
\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{x+y}\leq\frac{x-y}{2}\log\frac{x}{y}

for non-negative
x
and
y
have been applied in
\left(a\right)

and
\left(b\right)
, respectively. With the
help of the inequality Eq. (
18
), it is natural to obtain
the lower limit on the entropy-production rate of subsystem
X
[Eq.
(10) in the main text]. At the steady state, using Eq. (8) in the
main text and squaring Eq. (
18
) on both sides, we obtain
the following relation

\left(\dot{S_{r}}^{X}\right)^{2}\leq\mathcal{A}^{X}\left(\dot{S_{r}}^{X}-\dot{I}^{X}\right).

(19)

By considering that
\dot{S_{r}}^{X}\geq\dot{I}^{X}\geq 0
, Eq. (
19
)
obviously satisfies

\dot{S_{r}}^{X}\leq\mathcal{A}^{X}\left(1-\frac{\dot{I}^{X}}{\dot{S_{r}}^{X}}\right).

(20)

At the last step, by introducing the definition of the efficiency
of learning, Eq. (
20
) is transformed to reach the universal
upper bound for the efficiency of learning, i.e.,

\eta^{X}=\frac{\dot{I}^{X}}{\dot{S_{r}}^{X}}\leq 1-\frac{\dot{S_{r}}^{X}}{\mathcal{A}^{X}}\text{.}

(21)
