A new method for overconvergence of ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -modules
Heng Du, Tong Liu

Abstract
. We show all Laurent F F -crystals over p p -adic fields are overconvergent.



1.
Introduction

One aim in the study of
p
-adic Hodge theory is to relate good categories of
p
-adic Galois representations of
\mathrm{Gal}_{\mathbb Q_{p}}
with certain categories of semilinear algebraic objects over some big rings. Since it is usually easier to work with those semilinear algebraic objects when studying Galois representations in general coefficients, and when constructing or deforming Galois representations. When we want to consider all
p
-adic Galois representations of
\mathrm{Gal}_{\mathbb Q_{p}}
over
\mathbb Z_{p}
-lattices, then the semilinear algebra category one should use is the category of étale
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules over
\mathbf{A_{\mathbb Q_{p}}}
. Here the latter category is a certain linear algebra category over the ring

\mathbf{A}_{\mathbb Q_{p}}\coloneqq\{f=\sum_{i\in\mathbb Z}a_{i}X^{i}\mid a_{i}\in\mathbb Z_{p}\text{ and }\lim_{i\to-\infty}a_{i}=0\}

equipped with a semilinear action
\varphi
and
\mathbb Z_{p}^{\times}
. When
K
is a
p
-adic field, here in this paper we will refer to the case that
K
being a finite extension of
\mathbb Q_{p}
, the above equivalence between
p
-adic Galois representations and étale
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules are generalized using the field of norms construction of Fontaine-Wintenberger
[
FW79
]
. It turns out that étale
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules over
\mathbf{A}_{\mathbb Q_{p}}
always descent to so-called overconvergent étale
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules and this was first established by Cherbonnier-Colmez in
[
CC98
]
. In the case when
K=\mathbb Q_{p}
, their result implies every étale
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules over
\mathbf{A}_{\mathbb Q_{p}}
descent to the Robba ring
\mathbf{A}_{\mathbb Q_{p}}^{\dagger}
, the subring consisting of functions
f
that converges on some annulus
(r,1)
with
r
sufficiently closed to
1
.

There are two paralleled directions of generalizing the theory of étale
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules and the corresponding overconvergent theories, to a family version or to a relative version. To be more precise, the family version usually refers to Galois representations in general coefficients, cf.
[
Dee01
]
,
[
BC08
]
, and recently
[
Por22
]
. On the other hand, the relative version refers to replacing Galois groups with étale fundamental groups of some geometric objects over the base
p
-adic field, cf.
[
AB08
]
and
[
KL19
]
. Other than the works we mentioned, people also ask similar questions to
(\varphi,\tau)
-modules developed in
[
Car13
]
, and the overconvergence of
(\varphi,\tau)
-modules were obtained in
[
GL20a
]
and
[
GP21
]
. Moreover, there is the theory about overconvergence for Lubin-Tate tower which is considered by Berger in
[
Ber16
]
.

In
[
KL19
]
, Kedlaya-Liu tried to give an axiomatic approach to the theory of overconvergence of
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules, and their idea is that overconvergence is closely related to a property of a perfectoid finite étale tower which they called decompleteness. Using such an idea, they show the decompleteness of the cyclotomic tower, which recovers the result of Cherbonnier-Colmez. Their formalism also succeeds in dealing with relative situations that show relative toric towers are decompleting, and this result plays an important role in the construction of the Riemann-Hilbert functor in
[
LZ17
]
. Kedlaya-Liu expects their formalism to apply to the Kummer tower as well, which will reprove the overconvergence of
(\varphi,\tau)
-modules, cf. Conjecture 6.2.6. in
loc.

cit
. However, we will show that the overconvergent period rings defined by Kedlaya-Liu in Kummer’s case are too small and very few
(\varphi,\tau)
-modules are overconvergent in their sense, cf. the Appendix
A
.

This paper gives a sheaf theoretic approach to the overconvergence of
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules. We will use the theory of prisms developed by Bhatt-Scholze in
[
BS22
]
and
[
BS21
]
. Let
K
be a finite extension of
\mathbb Q_{p}
, and let

be a smooth formal scheme over
\mathcal{O}_{K}
. Consider the absolute prismatic site

over
X
, which equipped with the structure sheaf
\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}
and ideal sheaf

, also define
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{E},{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}=\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}[1/\mathcal{I}_{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}]^{\wedge}_{p}

[

]
, they define the category of Laurent

-crystals consisting of crystals in finite locally free
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{E},{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}
-modules together with a Frobenius semilinear endomorphism which is étale, i.e., the linearization of the Frobenius semilinear endomorphism is an isomorphism, then they can show

(

Prisms are considered as the deperfection of perfectoid rings. We know the overconvergent theory is relatively easy over perfect period rings. Actually, the underlying étale
\varphi
-modules already give an equivalence of categories in many cases, cf.
[
KL19
]
. And we can show (
1
) fits into the following diagram:

Idea of proof.
We will define the overconvergent period sheaves
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{E},{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}^{\dagger,n}
and
\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{E},{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}^{\dagger}
over

in §
2
, to understand sections of these sheaves, we developed a “Noetherian approximation” method in §

. Then we can define the category of overconvergent Laurent
F
-crystals and we can show that overconvergent Laurent
F
-crystals can be understood using overconvergent étale
\varphi
-modules with “overconvergent” descent isomorphisms in §
2
. Given a
\mathbb Z_{p}
-Galois representation, we will get an overconvergent étale
\varphi
-module from the classical overconvergent results, then the question is how to find the overconvergent descent isomorphism. We will use the idea in
[

]
. Briefly speaking, our slogan is that the descent isomorphism should come from the

-action, where

is a specific Galois group element that comes from the

-module theory of Caruso in
[

]
. To apply our strategy, we will also use some computations made in
[

]

-modules. However, in
[

]
they can only get the overconvergent

-action in some perfect overconvergent period rings, the main obstruction of to “deperfect” the

-action is because in

-modules, one can only see the
