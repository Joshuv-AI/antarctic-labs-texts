Abstract
This paper considers two logics. The first one, 𝐊 ​ 𝖦 𝗂𝗇𝗏 \mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{inv}} , is an expansion of the Gödel modal logic 𝐊 ​ 𝖦 \mathbf{K}\mathsf{G} with the involutive negation ∼ 𝗂 {\sim_{\mathsf{i}}} defined as v ( ∼ 𝗂 ϕ , w ) = 1 − v ( ϕ , w ) v({\sim_{\mathsf{i}}}\phi,w)=1-v(\phi,w) . The second one, 𝐊 ​ 𝖦 𝖻𝗅 \mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{bl}} , is the expansion of 𝐊 ​ 𝖦 𝗂𝗇𝗏 \mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{inv}} with the bi-lattice connectives and modalities. We explore their semantical properties w.r.t. the standard semantics on [ 0 , 1 ] [0,1] -valued Kripke frames and define a unified tableaux calculus that allows for the explicit countermodel construction. For this, we use an alternative semantics with the finite model property. Using the tableaux calculus, we construct a decision algorithm and show that satisfiability and validity in 𝐊 ​ 𝖦 𝗂𝗇𝗏 \mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{inv}} and 𝐊 ​ 𝖦 𝖻𝗅 \mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{bl}} are 𝖯𝖲𝗉𝖺𝖼𝖾 \mathsf{PSpace} -complete.

6
Conclusion

We presented two expansions of the Gödel modal logic:
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{inv}}
— with the involutive negation and
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{bl}}
— with bi-lattice connectives. We showed that they can be faithfully embedded into one another and also obtained the
\mathsf{PSpace}
-completeness of their validity via a tableaux calculus that we constructed for an alternative semantics following
[
16
,
17
]
.

Our next steps are as follows. First, we want to provide complete Hilbert-style axiomatisations of
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{inv}}
and
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{bl}}
both over crisp and over fuzzy frames. Second, we plan a more systematic study of the correspondence theory of
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{inv}}
and
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{bl}}
. Third, in
[
31
,
32
]
hypersequent calculi for
\Box
- and
\lozenge
-fragments of
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}
are presented. It makes sense to come up with terminating hypersequent calculi for the full
\mathbf{K}\mathsf{G}
and its expansions constructed in this paper as well as in
[
5
,
6
]
.

In parallel with these tasks, we intend to build off the work of
[
10
]
to engineer Gödel description logics that are able to record subtle features of roles. The foregoing work supports multi-lateral treatments of roles, allowing a description language to track the verification, falsification, and informativeness of a role’s holding between two terms. Planned future work will develop these intuitions, exploring both their formalisation and potential applications.

Theorem
5.1
(soundness)

We show that if
\mathfrak{M}
realises the premise of a rule, then it also realises one of its conclusions. Soundness follows from here since no closed branch is realisable.

Proof

We consider
\Rightarrow_{\triangleright}
(with
\triangleright
being
>
) and
\xcancel{\Box}_{\approx}
(other rules can be tackled similarly).

Let
w:\mathbf{1}:\chi\rightarrow\psi>\mathfrak{X}
be realised by
\mathfrak{M}
and
\real(\mathfrak{X})=z
, i.e.,
v_{1}(\chi\rightarrow\psi,w)>z
. Then, there are two options. First,
v_{1}(\psi,w)>z
. In this case, the left conclusion is realised. Second, it is possible that
v_{1}(\chi\rightarrow\psi,w)=1
and
z<1
. Hence,
v_{1}(\chi,w)\leq v_{1}(\psi,w)
, whence there is some
c
s.t.
v_{1}(\psi,w)=c
and
v_{1}(\chi,w)\leq c
. Thus, the right conclusion is realised.

Assume that
w:\mathbf{1}:\xcancel{\Box}\psi\approx\mathfrak{X}
be realised by
\mathfrak{M}
and
\real(\mathfrak{X})=z
. Then
v_{1}(\xcancel{\Box}\psi,w)=z
. If
z=1
, then
\real(\mathfrak{X})=\real(1)=\real(w:\mathbf{1}:\xcancel{\Box}\psi)
(i.e., the left conclusion is realised). Otherwise,
z\in T_{1}
and
z=\max\{x\in T_{1}(w)\mid x\leq\inf\limits_{w^{\prime}\in W}\{wR^{+}w^{\prime}\rightarrow v_{1}(\psi,w^{\prime})\}\}
. Thus, there is
w^{\prime}
s.t.
v_{1}(\phi,w^{\prime})<w\mathsf{R}^{+}w^{\prime}
and
z\leqslant v_{1}(\psi,w^{\prime})<\mathsf{s}(z)
with
\mathsf{s}(z)=\min\{y\in T_{1}\mid y>x\}
. Hence, the right conclusion is realised.
