1.
Introduction

A central binomial coefficient has the form
\binom{2n}{n}
with

n\in\mathbb{N}=\{0,1,\ldots\}
.
The well-known Fibonacci sequence
{F}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
is defined by

F_{0}=0,F_{1}=1,{\rm and}\ F_{n+1}=F_{n}+F_{n-1}\ {\rm for}\ n=1,2,3,\ldots.

Let
p
be an odd prime.
Sun and Tauraso

[
24
]
proved for any
m\in\mathbb{Z}
with
p\nmid m
,

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{p^{a}-1}(-1)^{k}\binom{2k}{k+d}

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{d-1+\delta_{p,5}}F_{2(|d|-(\frac{p^{a}}{5}))}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p),

(1.1)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{p^{a}-1}\frac{\binom{2k}{k+d}}{m^{k}}

\displaystyle\equiv u_{p^{a}-|d|}(m-2)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p),

(1.2)

\displaystyle|d|\sum_{k=0}^{p^{a}-1}\frac{\binom{2k}{k+d}}{km^{k-1}}

\displaystyle\equiv 2(-1)^{d}+v_{p^{a}-|d|}(m-2)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p),

(1.3)

where
|d|\in\{0,\ldots p^{a}\}
with
a\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
,
\delta_{i,j}
is the Kronecker delta, and the two linear recurrences
\{u_{n}(x)\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
and
\{v_{n}(x)\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
of polynomials are defined as follows:

u_{0}(x)=0,\ u_{1}(x)=1,{\rm and}\ u_{n+1}(x)=xu_{n}(x)-u_{n-1}(x)\ (n=1,2,\ldots),

v_{0}(x)=2,\ v_{1}(x)=x,{\rm and}\ v_{n+1}(x)=xv_{n}(x)-v_{n-1}(x)\ (n=1,2,\ldots),

and

\ u_{-1}(x)=xu_{0}(x)-u_{1}(x)=-1,v_{-1}(x)=xv_{0}(x)-v_{1}(x)=x.

[
25

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{p^{a}-1}\binom{2k}{k+d}

\displaystyle\equiv\left(\frac{p^{a}-|d|}{3}\right)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p),

Along the same lines of the proof of Theorem

\displaystyle\frac{q^{2+3d}(1-q^{d+2})}{1-q^{d}}T(n,d+2)=-T(n,d)+q^{d}\frac{(1+q^{n})(1-q^{2d+2})(q;q)_{2n-1}}{(1-q^{d})(q;q)_{n+d+1}(q^{2};q)_{n-d-2}},

\displaystyle\frac{q^{2+3d}(1-q^{d+2})}{1-q^{d}}S(n+1,d+2)+S(n+1,d)=q^{d}\frac{(1+q^{n+1})(1-q^{2d+2})(q;q)_{2n+1}}{(1-q^{d})(q;q)_{n+d+2}(q^{2};q)_{n-d-1}}.

).

, respectively. Clearly,
