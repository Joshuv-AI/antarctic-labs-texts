Categorical Symmetry of the Standard Model from Gravitational Anomaly
Pavel Putrov, Juven Wang

Abstract
In the Standard Model, some combination of the baryon 𝐁 \bf B and lepton 𝐋 \bf L number symmetry is free of mixed anomalies with strong and electroweak s ​ u ​ ( 3 ) × s ​ u ​ ( 2 ) × u ​ ( 1 ) Y ~ su(3)\times su(2)\times u(1)_{\tilde{Y}} gauge forces. However, it can still suffer from a mixed gravitational anomaly, hypothetically pertinent to leptogenesis in the very early universe. This happens when the total “sterile right-handed” neutrino number n ν R n_{\nu_{R}} is not equal to the family number N f N_{f} . Thus the invertible 𝐁 − 𝐋 \bf B-L symmetry current conservation can be violated quantum mechanically by gravitational backgrounds such as gravitational instantons. In specific, we show that a noninvertible categorical 𝐁 − 𝐋 \bf B-L generalized symmetry still survives in gravitational backgrounds. In general, we propose a construction of noninvertible symmetry charge operators as topological defects derived from invertible anomalous symmetries that suffer from mixed gravitational anomalies. Examples include the perturbative local and nonperturbative global anomalies classified by ℤ \mathbb{Z} and ℤ 16 \mathbb{Z}_{16} respectively. For this construction, we utilize the anomaly inflow bulk-boundary correspondence, the 4d Pontryagin class and the gravitational Chern-Simons 3-form, the 3d Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev-type topological quantum field theory with a framing anomaly corresponding to a 2d rational conformal field theory with an appropriate rational chiral central charge, and the 4d ℤ 4 TF \mathbb{Z}_{4}^{\rm TF} -time-reversal symmetric topological superconductor with 3d boundary topological order.

Conclusion

In this work. we have shown that although
an
invertible
symmetry can suffer from mixed gravitational anomalies
under gravitational backgrounds (such as gravitational instantons),
still a certain
noninvertible
counterpart of an infinite discrete subgroup of
this original broken symmetry can be revived as a noninvertible categorical symmetry. We have constructed the noninvertible symmetry charge operators as topological defects,
specifically for the case of a mixed
{\rm U}(1)
-gravitational anomaly
Eguchi and Freund 1976
;
Alvarez-Gaume and Witten 1984

and a mixed
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
-gravitational anomaly
Tachikawa and Yonekura 2018
;
Garcia-Etxebarria and Montero 2019
;
Hsieh 2018
;
Guo et al. 2020
;
Hason et al. 2020
;
Wan and Wang 2020
.
Built upon the previous construction based on the mixed gauge anomaly pioneered in
Choi et al. 2022
;
Cordova and Ohmori 2022
,
our construction can be regarded as a natural extension to the mixed gravitational anomaly counterpart.
Meanwhile, thanks to the previous systematic classification of the anomalies and the corresponding cobordism class of the Standard Model (SM)
Garcia-Etxebarria and Montero 2019

Wang and Wen 2020

Davighi et al. 2020
;
Wan and Wang 2020
