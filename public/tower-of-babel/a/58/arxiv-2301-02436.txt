Vertex-Critical ( P 5 , c ​ h ​ a ​ i ​ r ) (P_{5},chair) -Free Graphs
Shenwei Huang, Zeyu Li

Abstract
Given two graphs H 1 H_{1} and H 2 H_{2} , a graph G G is ( H 1 , H 2 ) (H_{1},H_{2}) -free if it contains no induced subgraph isomorphic to H 1 H_{1} or H 2 H_{2} . A P t P_{t} is the path on t t vertices. A chair is a P 4 P_{4} with an additional vertex adjacent to one of the middle vertices of the P 4 P_{4} . A graph G G is k k -vertex-critical if G G has chromatic number k k but every proper induced subgraph of G G has chromatic number less than k k . In this paper, we prove that there are finitely many 5-vertex-critical ( P 5 , c ​ h ​ a ​ i ​ r ) (P_{5},chair) -free graphs.
