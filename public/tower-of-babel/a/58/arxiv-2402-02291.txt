Construction of continuous K-g-Frames in Hilbert C ∗ C^{\ast} -Modules
Abdelilah Karara 1 , Mohamed Rossafi ∗2 , Mohammed Klilou 2 and Samir Kabbaj 1

Abstract
. In this work, we provide some constructions and the sum of new continuous K-g-frames in Hilbert C ∗ C^{\ast} -Modules. We provide certain necessary and sufficient conditions for some adjointable operators on ℋ \mathcal{H} , under which new continuous K-g-frames can be retrieved from those that already exist. Additionally, we discuss the sum of continuous K-g-frames, discover some of their characterizations, and offer some adjointable operators to construct new continuous K-g-frames from the previous ones.

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda}{2}\left(\left\langle K_{1}^{*}\left(\alpha_{1}\Theta_{1}+\alpha_{2}\Theta_{2}\right)^{*}g,K_{1}^{*}\left(\alpha_{1}\Theta_{1}+\alpha_{2}\Theta_{2}\right)^{*}g\right\rangle\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\left\langle K_{1}^{*}\left(\alpha_{1}\Theta_{1}-\alpha_{2}\Theta_{2}\right)^{*}g,K_{1}^{*}\left(\alpha_{1}\Theta_{1}-\alpha_{2}\Theta_{2}\right)^{*}g\right\rangle\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\lambda}{2}\left\langle K_{1}^{*}\mathcal{Q}^{*}g,K_{1}^{*}\mathcal{Q}^{*}g\right\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\lambda}{2}\left\|K_{1}^{\dagger}\right\|^{-2}\left\langle\mathcal{Q}^{*}g,\mathcal{Q}^{*}g\right\rangle.

Since
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{Q})\supseteq\mathcal{R}\left(K_{2}\right)
, consequently applying Lemma
1.8
, there exists
\alpha>0
such that

K_{2}K_{2}^{*}\leq\alpha\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{Q}^{*}.

,

consequently, for every
g\in\mathcal{H}_{2}

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda}{2}\alpha^{-1}\left\|K_{1}^{\dagger}\right\|^{-2}\left\langle K_{2}^{*}g,K_{2}^{*}g\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Omega}\left\langle\left(\alpha_{1}\Upsilon_{\xi}\Theta_{1}^{*}+\alpha_{2}\Phi_{\xi}\Theta_{2}^{*}\right)g,\left(\alpha_{1}\Upsilon_{\xi}\Theta_{1}^{*}+\alpha_{2}\Phi_{\xi}\Theta_{2}^{*}\right)g\right\rangle\mathrm{d}\nu(\xi)

\displaystyle\leq\left(\alpha_{1}^{2}B_{1}\left\|\Theta_{1}^{*}\right\|^{2}+\alpha_{2}^{2}B_{2}\left\|\Theta_{2}^{*}\right\|^{2}\right)\left\langle g,g\right\rangle.
