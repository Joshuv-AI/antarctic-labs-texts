Reductions of certain 3-dimensional crystalline representations
Tong Liu

Abstract
. We compute reduction ρ ¯ \bar{\rho} of 3-dimensional irreducible crystalline representations ρ \rho of G ℚ p G_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}} with Hodge-Tate weights { 0 , r , s } \{0,r,s\} satisfying 2 ≤ r ≤ p − 2 , 2 + p ≤ s ≤ r + p − 2 . 2\leq r\leq p-2,\ \ 2+p\leq s\leq r+p-2. If ρ ¯ \bar{\rho} is irreducible then we show that the generic fiber of crystalline universal deformation ring of ρ ¯ \bar{\rho} is connected.
