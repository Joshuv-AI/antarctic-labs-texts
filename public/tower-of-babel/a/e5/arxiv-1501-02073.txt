Numerical results a quantum waveguide with Mixed boundary conditions
M. Raissi 1

Abstract
. This article is devoted to the numerical study of the existence of the eigenvalues of the Hamiltonian describing a quantum particle living on three dimensional straight strip of width d d in the presence of an electric field of constant intensity F F in the direction perpendicular to the electron plane. We impose Neumann boundary conditions on a disc window of radius a a and Dirichlet boundary conditions on the remaining part of the boundary of the strip.
