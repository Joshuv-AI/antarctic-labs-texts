Cohomologies and deformations of differential algebra morphisms Thanks: L. Du is the corresponding author.
Lei Du, Yanhong Bao

Abstract
. This paper studies the formal deformations of differential algebra morphisms. As a consequence, we develop a cohomology theory of differential algebra morphisms to interpret the lower degree cohomology groups as formal deformations. Then, we prove the Cohomology Comparison Theorem of differential algebra morphisms, i.e., the cohomology of a morphism of differential algebras is isomorphic to the cohomology of an auxiliary differential algebra. Finally, we can give a minimal model for morphism of differential algebras with weight=0.

Cohomology of differential algebra morphisms

Definition 2.1
.

(

Let

Definition 2.2

Denote by

Definition 2.3

\displaystyle d_{M}(ax)

\displaystyle=xd_{A}(a)+d_{M}(x)a+\lambda d_{M}(x)d_{A}(a),

\displaystyle a\triangleright m=(a+\lambda d_{A}(a))m,m\triangleleft a=m(a+\lambda d_{A}(a)),m\in M,a\in A.

For distinction, we let

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
\psi:(M,d_{M})\rightarrow(N,d_{N})
be a morphism of
(A,d_{A})
-bimodules, then
\psi:({}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd},d_{M})\rightarrow({}_{\rhd}N_{\lhd},d_{N})
is a morphism of
(A,d_{A})
-bimodules.

Proof.

Let
{}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd}
and
{}_{\rhd}N_{\lhd}
be
(A,d_{A})
-bimodules. It suffice to show that
\psi:({}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd},d_{M})\rightarrow({}_{\rhd}N_{\lhd},d_{N})
is a morphism of
(A_{\star},d_{A})
-bimodules.

Since we have
d_{N}\circ\psi=\psi\circ d_{M}
, it follows that

\displaystyle\psi(a\rhd m)

\displaystyle=\psi((a+\lambda d_{A}(a))x)

\displaystyle=\psi(ax)+\lambda\psi(d_{A}(a)x)

\displaystyle=a\psi(x)+\lambda d_{A}(a)\psi(x)

\displaystyle=(a+\lambda d_{A}(a))\psi(x)

\displaystyle=a\rhd\psi(x).

Similarly,
\psi(x\lhd a)=\psi(x)\lhd a

\psi
is a morphism of
(A,d_{A})
-bimodules.
∎

3.
Deformations of differential morphisms

In this section, we study formal deformations of a differential algebra morphisms and consider the
rigidity of differential algebra morphisms.

Let
(A,\mu_{A},d_{A})
and
(B,\mu_{B},d_{B})
be differential algebras of weight
\lambda
and
\phi:(A,d_{A})\rightarrow(B,d_{B})
be a morphism of differential algebras. For
X=\{A,B\}
, define

\displaystyle\mu_{X,t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\mu_{X,i}t^{i},~~~\mu_{X,0}=\mu_{X},

\displaystyle d_{X,t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}d_{X,i}t^{i},~~~d_{X,0}=d_{X},

\displaystyle\phi_{t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i}t^{i},~~~\phi_{0}=\phi.

(\mu_{A,t},d_{A,t},\mu_{B,t},d_{B,t},\phi_{t})
is called a
1
-parameter formal deformation of
\phi:(A,d_{A})\rightarrow(B,d_{B})
, if
(A[[t]],\mu_{A,t},d_{A,t})
,
(B[[t]],\mu_{B,t},d_{B,t})
are
\mathbb{F}[[t]]
-differential algebras of weight
\lambda
and
\phi_{t}:A[[t]]\rightarrow B[[t]]

Power series
(\mu_{A,t},d_{A,t},\mu_{B,t},d_{B,t},\phi_{t})
is a 1-parameter formal deformation of

if and only if for any

\displaystyle\mu_{A,t}(a_{1}\otimes\mu_{A,t}(a_{2}\otimes a_{3}))

\displaystyle=\mu_{A,t}(\mu_{A,t}(a_{1}\otimes a_{2})\otimes a_{3}),

\displaystyle d_{A,t}\mu_{A,t}(a_{1}\otimes a_{2})

\displaystyle=\mu_{A,t}(d_{A,t}(a_{1})\otimes a_{2})+\mu_{A,t}(a_{1}\otimes d_{A,t}(a_{2}))+\lambda u_{A,t}(d_{A,t}(a_{1})\otimes d_{A,t}(a_{2}))\Big),

\displaystyle=\mu_{B,t}(\mu_{B,t}(b_{1}\otimes b_{2})\otimes b_{3}),

\displaystyle d_{B,t}\mu_{B,t}(b_{1}\otimes b_{2})

\displaystyle=\mu_{B,t}(d_{B,t}(b_{1})\otimes b_{2})+\mu_{B,t}(b_{1}\otimes d_{B,t}(b_{2}))+\lambda u_{B,t}(d_{B,t}(b_{1})\otimes d_{B,t}(b_{2}))\Big),

(7)

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
(\mu_{A,t},d_{A,t},\mu_{B,t},d_{B,t},\phi_{t})
be a 1-parameter formal deformation of

Proof.

We only need to show that

\displaystyle\varrho^{2}\big((\mu_{A,1},\mu_{B,1},\phi_{1}),(d_{A,1},d_{B,1},0)\big)
