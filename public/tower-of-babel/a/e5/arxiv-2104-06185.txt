A General Formula for Asymptotes of Rational Polynomial Functions
Lam Mason, Asterios Skodras, askodras@sch.gr

Abstract




We propose a formula for finding the horizontal, oblique or curvilinear asymptote of any rational polynomial function of any positive degree, as a sum of matrix determinants formed directly from the coefficients of the terms in the given polynomial. This formula provides a new means of computing asymptotes in addition to the standard methods of Euclidean division and the evaluation of limits.

Keywords:
horizontal asymptote, oblique asymptote, curvilinear asymptote, rational function, asymptotic polynomial

1
Introduction

It is well known, and explained in many textbooks [1, 2, 3, 4], that the
horizontal
,
oblique
or
curvilinear
asymptote of a function
f:D\to f(D),D\subseteq\mathrm{R}
is a straight line or a curve whose distance from the graph of
f(x)
approaches zero as
x
approaches infinity. In the case of a rational polynomial function
\frac{a(x)}{b(x)}
, where deg(
b(x))\leq\deg(a(x))
, the expression for the asymptote is a polynomial with degree deg
\left(a(x)\right)
-deg
\left(b(x)\right)
. Currently one of the most common methods of finding the asymptote of a rational polynomial function is by
Euclidean polynomial division
, where the numerator of the polynomial is divided by denominator and the fractional terms are discarded.

Example 1.1
Consider the function

f(x)=\frac{8x^{3}+7}{x-4}

(1)

By Euclidean division,

f(x)=8x^{2}+32x+128+\frac{519}{x-4}

(2)

Hence, the asymptote of this function is

g(x)=8x^{2}+32x+128

(3)

Another popular method, specifically used for finding
oblique
asymptotes, is by evaluating limits. The oblique asymptote of a function
f(x)
takes the form

y=ax+b

(4)

where

a=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{f(x)}{x}

(5)

and

b=\lim_{x\to\infty}\big(f(x)-ax\big)

(6)

Example 1.2
For the function

f(x)=\frac{5x^{3}+13x^{2}+3x+9}{4x^{2}+5x+7}

(7)

we have

a=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{5x^{3}+13x^{2}+3x+9}{4x^{3}+5x^{2}+7x}

(8)

a=\frac{5}{4}

(9)

and

b=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{5x^{3}+13x^{2}+3x+9}{4x^{2}+5x+7}-\frac{5}{4}x\\

(10)

b=\frac{27}{16}

(11)

Thus, the corresonding oblique asymptote is given by

g(x)=\frac{5}{4}x+\frac{27}{16}

(12)

In this paper, we show that the asymptote can alternatively be expressed as the following sum:

g(x)=\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-k}}x^{k}-\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{2}}x^{k-1}+\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&a_{n-2}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{3}}x^{k-2}+\dots+(-1)^{k-1}\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(k-1)}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(k-1)}\\
0&b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-k-(k-1)+1}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{k}}x+(-1)^{k}\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-k}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-k}\\
0&b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-k-k+1}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{k+1}}

(13)

where
b_{i}=0
for
i<0
, for any rational polynomial function

f(x)=\frac{a_{n}x^{n}+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\dots+a_{1}x+a_{0}}{b_{n-k}x^{n-k}+b_{n-k-1}x^{n-k-1}+\dots+b_{1}x+b_{0}}

(14)

where
n>k
and
n,k\in\mathbb{Z}
.

2
Proof by Induction

For a rational polynomial function

f(x)=\displaystyle\frac{\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}x^{r}}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}}

(15)

the equation for its corresponding asymptote would be in the form of

g(x)=\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{k-\alpha}

(16)

for some coefficients
\theta_{\alpha}
.

We first derive the expression for
\theta_{0}
, the coefficient of
x^{k}
. By the definition of an asymptote,

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(f(x)-g(x)\right)=0

(17)

Plugging in our expression for
g(x)
gives

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(f(x)-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{k-\alpha}\right)=0

(18)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(f(x)-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=1}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{k-\alpha}-\theta_{0}x^{k}\right)=0

(19)

Dividing both sides by
x^{k}
,

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k}}-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=1}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{-\alpha}-\theta_{0}\right)=0

(20)

Since

\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=1}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{-\alpha}

(21)

tends to 0 as
x\to\infty
, we have

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k}}-\theta_{0}\right)=0

(22)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k}}\right)-\theta_{0}=0

(23)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k}}\right)=\theta_{0}

(24)

Expanding
f(x)
, we have

\theta_{0}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{f(x)}{x^{k}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}x^{r}}{x^{k}\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}x^{r}}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=k}^{n}b_{i-k}x^{i}}

(25)

\theta_{0}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{a_{n}x^{n}}{b_{n-k}x^{n}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-k}}=\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-k}}

(26)

Now, we prove that

\theta_{\alpha}=\left(-1\right)^{\alpha}\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-\alpha}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-\alpha}\\
0&b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-k-\alpha+1}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{\alpha+1}}

(27)

where
\alpha\leq k\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
,
b_{i}=0
for
i<0
, and we do so by strong induction.

Let
P(\alpha)
be the proposition stated above. Then, to find
\theta_{\alpha}
for
\alpha=1
, we begin with

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(f(x)-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{k-\alpha}\right)=0

(28)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(f(x)-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=2}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{k-\alpha}-\theta_{1}x^{k-1}-\theta_{0}x^{k}\right)=0

(29)

Dividing both sides by
x^{k-1}
,

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-1}}-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=2}^{k}\theta_{\alpha}x^{-\alpha+1}-\theta_{1}-\theta_{0}x\right)=0

(30)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-1}}-\theta_{1}-\theta_{0}x\right)=0

(31)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-1}}-\theta_{0}x\right)=0

(32)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-1}}-\theta_{0}x\right)-\theta_{1}=0

(33)

\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-1}}-\theta_{0}x\right)=\theta_{1}

(34)

Expanding
f(x)
and referring back to our definition of
\theta_{0}
yields

\theta_{1}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-1}}-\theta_{0}x\right)=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}x^{r}}{x^{k-1}\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}}-\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-k}}x\right)

(35)

\theta_{1}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}b_{n-k}x^{r}-\displaystyle\sum_{i=k}^{n}a_{n}b_{i-k}x^{i}}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=k-1}^{n-1}b_{i-k+1}b_{n-k}x^{i}}\right)

(36)

\theta_{1}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\left(a_{r}b_{n-k}x^{r}-a_{n}b_{n-k-1}\right)x^{n-1}}{b_{n-k}{b_{n-k}x^{n-1}}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{a_{n-1}b_{n-k}-a_{n}b_{n-k-1}}{b_{n-k}^{2}}

(37)

\theta_{1}=\frac{a_{n-1}b_{n-k}-a_{n}b_{n-k-1}}{b_{n-k}^{2}}=-\frac{a_{n}b_{n-k-1}-a_{n-1}b_{n-k}}{b_{n-k}^{2}}

(38)

\theta_{1}=-\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\\
\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{2}}

(39)

Thus,
P(\alpha)
is true.
Now, suppose
P(\alpha)
is true for all
\alpha\leq\varphi-1\leq k-1\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
. We will then show that
P(\varphi)
is true, i.e.

\theta_{\varphi}=\left(-1\right)^{\varphi}\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-\varphi}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-\varphi}\\
0&b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-k-\varphi+1}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi+1}}

(40)

for
\varphi-1<\varphi\leq k\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
.

First we have

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-\varphi}}-\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{\alpha}x^{\varphi-\alpha}\right)

(41)

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{f(x)}{x^{k-\varphi}}-\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-k}}x^{\varphi}-(-1)\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\\
\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{2}}x^{\varphi-1}-\dots-(-1)^{\varphi-1}\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&\dots&a_{n-(\varphi-1)}\\
b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-k-(\varphi-1)}\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi}}x\right)

(42)

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}x^{r}}{x^{k-\varphi}\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}}-\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-k}}x^{\varphi}-\dots-\frac{(-1)^{\varphi-1}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&\dots&a_{n-{\varphi-1}}\\
b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-{\varphi-1}}\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi}}x\right)

(43)

Let

\theta_{\alpha}^{{}^{\prime}}=(-1)^{\alpha}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&\dots&a_{n-\alpha}\\
b_{n-k}&\dots&b_{n-k-\alpha}\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}

(44)

Then, combining the fractions gives

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}x^{r}}{x^{k-\varphi}\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}}-\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{\varphi-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-\alpha}\theta_{\alpha}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{\varphi-\alpha}}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi}}\right)

(45)

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}x^{r}-\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}\right)\left(\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{\varphi-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-\alpha}\theta_{\alpha}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{\varphi-\alpha}\right)}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=k}^{n-\varphi}b_{i-k+\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}x^{i}}

(46)

We show that all the terms in the numerator where the powers of
x
are greater than
n-\varphi
vanish using the following result:

Lemma 2.1
In the expression

\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}x^{r}-\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}\right)\left(\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=0}^{\varphi-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-\alpha}\theta_{\alpha}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{\varphi-\alpha}\right)

(47)

the terms where the powers of
x
are greater than
n-\varphi
all sum to zero.

By this result, we are left with

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1}{b_{n-k}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}x^{n-\varphi}}\left(a_{n-\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}-\left(b_{n-k-\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(\varphi-1)}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{\varphi-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{\varphi-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)\right)x^{n-\varphi}

(48)

\theta_{\varphi}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi+1}}\left(a_{n-\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}-\left(b_{n-k-\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(\varphi-1)}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{\varphi-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{\varphi-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)\right)

(49)

To simplify this expression for
\theta_{\varphi}
, we make use of the following result:

Lemma 2.2

\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}=a_{n-j}b_{n-k}^{j}-\left(b_{n-k-j}b_{n-k}^{j-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(j-1)}b_{n-k}^{j-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(50)

which gives us

\theta_{\varphi}^{{}^{\prime}}=a_{n-\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}-(b_{n-k-\varphi}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(\varphi-1)}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{\varphi-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{\varphi-1}^{{}^{\prime}})

(51)

Hence, we have

\theta_{\varphi}^{{}^{\prime}}=\frac{\theta_{\varphi}^{{}^{\prime}}}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi+1}}

(52)

\theta_{\varphi}^{{}^{\prime}}=(-1)^{\varphi}\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(\varphi-2)}&a_{n-(\varphi-1)}&a_{n-\varphi}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(\varphi-2)}&b_{n-k-(\varphi-1)}&b_{n-k-\varphi}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&0&\dots&0&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-k}^{\varphi+1}}

(53)

Hence,
P(\varphi)
is true.

This means
P(\varphi)
is true for all
\alpha\leq k\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
, thus completing the proof.

Proof of Lemmas

In this section we prove the two lemmas mentioned previously.

3.1
Proof of Lemma 2.1

The terms in the expression

\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}\right)\left(\displaystyle\sum_{a=0}^{\varphi-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-\alpha}\theta_{\alpha}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-\alpha}\right)

(54)

where the powers of
x
are greater than
n-\varphi
are

\left(b_{n-k-0}x^{n-k-0}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-z}\theta_{z}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-z}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-1}x^{n-k-1}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-1)}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-(z-1)}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-2}x^{n-k-2}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-2)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-(z-2)}\right)+\dots+\left(b_{n-k-z}x^{n-k-z}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-0}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-0}\right)

(55)

for integers
0\leq z\leq\varphi-1
. Thus, in order to prove that the terms in the expression

\sum_{r=0}^{n}a_{r}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}x^{r}-\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n-k}b_{i}x^{i}\right)\left(\sum_{\alpha=0}^{\varphi-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-\alpha}\theta_{\alpha}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-\alpha}\right)

(56)

all sum to zero for powers of
x
greater than
n-\varphi
, we need to show that

a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}x_{n-z}=\left(b_{n-k-0}x^{n-k-0}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-z}\theta_{z}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-z}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-1}x^{n-k-1}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-1)}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-(z-1)}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-2}x^{n-k-2}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-2)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-(z-2)}\right)+\dots+\left(b_{n-k-z}x^{n-k-z}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-0}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}x^{k-0}\right)

(57)

i.e.

a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}=\left(b_{n-k-0}x^{n-k-0}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-z}\theta_{z}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-1}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-1)}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-2}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-2)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\dots+\left(b_{n-k-z}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-0}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(58)

To do this, we refer to Lemma 2.2 for our expansion of
\theta_{z}^{{}^{\prime}}
, which gives us

\theta_{z}^{{}^{\prime}}=a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{z}-\left(b_{n-k-z}b_{n-k}^{z-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(z-1)}b_{n-k}^{z-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(59)

Plugging this expansion onto the RHS gives

\text{RHS}=\left(b_{n-k-0}\right)\left\{b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-z}\left[a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{z}-\left(b_{n-k-z}b_{n-k}^{z-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(z-1)}b_{n-k}^{z-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)\right]\right\}+\left(b_{n-k-1}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-1)}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-2}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-2)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\dots+\left(b_{n-k-z}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-0}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(60)

\text{RHS}=a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}-\left(b_{n-k-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(z-1)}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-(z-1)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-z}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-1}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-1)}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-2}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-(z-2)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\dots+\left(b_{n-k-z}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1-0}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(61)

\text{RHS}=a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}-\left(b_{n-k-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(z-1)}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-(z-1)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}b_{n-k}^{\varphi-z}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-1}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-z}\theta_{z-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\left(b_{n-k-2}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-(z-1)}\theta_{z-2}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)+\dots+\left(b_{n-k-z}\right)\left(b_{n-k}^{\varphi-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(62)

\text{RHS}=a_{n-z}b_{n-k}^{\varphi}

(63)

as desired.

3.2
Proof of Lemma 2.2

We begin with

\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}=(-1)^{j}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-2)}&a_{n-(j-1)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-2)}&b_{n-k-(j-1)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&0&\dots&0&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}

(64)

Dividing both sides by
(-1)^{j}
,

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-2)}&a_{n-(j-1)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-2)}&b_{n-k-(j-1)}&b_{n-k-j}\\

0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&0&\dots&0&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}

(65)

Expanding the determinant by taking the bottom row elements gives

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=-b_{n-k}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-2)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-2)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-3}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-2}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k-1}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-2)}&a_{n-(j-1)}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-2)}&b_{n-k-(j-1)}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}

(66)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=-b_{n-k}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-2)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-2)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-3}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-2}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(67)

Expanding the determinant again, we get

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=-b_{n-k}\left(-b_{n-k}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-3)}&a_{n-j}\\

\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-4}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-3}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k-2}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-3)}&a_{n-(j-2)}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-3)}&b_{n-k-(j-2)}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-3}\end{vmatrix}\right)+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(68)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=-b_{n-k}\left(-b_{n-k}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-3)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-3)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-4}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-3}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}\right)+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(69)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=b_{n-k}^{2}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-3)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-3)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-4}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-3}\end{vmatrix}-b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(70)

Expanding once again gives

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=b_{n-k}^{2}\left(-b_{n-k}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-4)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-4)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-5}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-4}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k-3}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-4)}&a_{n-(j-3)}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-4)}&b_{n-k-(j-3)}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-2}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}\end{vmatrix}\right)-b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(71)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=b_{n-k}^{2}\left(-b_{n-k}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-4)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-4)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-5}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-4}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-3}}\right)-b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(72)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=-b_{n-k}^{3}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&\dots&a_{n-(j-4)}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-1}&\dots&b_{n-k-(j-4)}&b_{n-k-j}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k-1}&b_{n-k-5}\\
0&0&\dots&b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-4}\end{vmatrix}+b_{n-k}^{2}{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-3}}-b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{\varphi-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(73)

Repeating this process yields

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-j}\\
b_{n-k}&b_{n-k-j}\end{vmatrix}+(-1)^{j-2}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k-(j-1)}\frac{\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{1}}+(-1)^{j-3}b_{n-k}^{j-3}b_{n-k-(j-2)}\frac{\theta_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{2}}+\dots+(-1)^{2}b_{n-k}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\frac{\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-3}}+(-1)^{1}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+(-1)^{0}b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(74)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}\left(a_{n}b_{n-k-j}-a_{n-j}b_{n-k}\right)+(-1)^{j-2}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k(j-1)}\frac{\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{1}}+(-1)^{j-3}b_{n-k}^{j-3}b_{n-k-(j-2)}\frac{\theta_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{2}}+\dots+(-1)^{2}b_{n-k}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-3}}+(-1)^{1}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+(-1)^{0}b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(75)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}a_{n}b_{n-k-j}-(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}a_{n-j}b_{n-k}+(-1)^{j-2}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k-(j-1)}\frac{\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{1}}+(-1)^{j-3}b_{n-k}^{j-3}b_{n-k-(j-2)}\frac{\theta_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{2}}+\dots+(-1)^{2}b_{n-k}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\frac{\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-3}}+(-1)^{1}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta{j-2}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+(-1)^{0}b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(76)

Since
a_{n}=\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}
, we have

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}b_{n-k-j}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}-(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}a_{n-j}b_{n-k}+(-1)^{j-2}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k-(j-1)}\frac{\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{1}}+(-1)^{j-3}b_{n-k}^{j-3}b_{n-k-(j-2)}+\dots+(-1)^{2}b_{n-k-2}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\frac{\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-3}}+(-1)^{1}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\frac{\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-2}}+(-1)^{0}b_{n-k-1}\frac{\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j-1}}

(77)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=(-1)^{j}b_{n-k}^{j-1}a_{n-j}b_{n-k}+(-1)^{j-1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}b_{n-k-j}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{j-3}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k-(j-1)}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{j-5}b_{n-k}^{j-3}b_{n-k-(j-2)}\theta_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+(-1)^{5-j}b_{n-k}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{3-j}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{1-j}b_{n-k-1}\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}

(78)

\frac{\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}{(-1)^{j}}=(-1)^{j}b_{n-k}^{j-1}a_{n-j}b_{n-k}+(-1)^{j+1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}b_{n-k-j}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{j+1}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k-(j-1)}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{j+1}b_{n-k}^{j-3}b_{n-k-(j-2)}\theta_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+(-1)^{j+1}b_{n-k}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{j+1}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{j+1}b_{n-k-1}\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}

(79)

Multiply both sides by
(-1)^{j}
,

\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}=(-1)^{2}b_{n-k}^{j-1}a_{n-j}b_{n-k}+(1)^{2j+1}b_{n-k}^{j-1}b_{n-k-j}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{2j+1}b_{n-k}^{j-2}b_{n-k-(j-1)}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+(-1)^{2j+1}b_{n-k}^{2}b_{n-k-3}\theta_{j-3}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{2j+1}b_{n-k}b_{n-k-2}\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+(-1)^{2j+1}b_{n-k-1}\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}

(80)

\theta_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}=a_{n-j}b_{n-k}^{j}-\left(b_{n-k-j}b_{n-k}^{j-1}\theta_{0}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-(j-1)}b_{n-k}^{j-2}\theta_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}+\dots+b_{n-k-2}b_{n-k}\theta_{j-2}^{{}^{\prime}}+b_{n-k-1}\theta_{j-1}^{{}^{\prime}}\right)

(81)

as desired.

Examples

4.1
Oblique Asymptote

For the linear function

f(x)=\frac{{a_{n}x^{n}}+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1}x+a_{0}}{b_{n-1}x^{n-1}+b_{n-2}x^{n-2}+\cdots+b_{1}x+b_{0}}

(82)

the formula for its oblique asymptote would be

y=\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-1}}x-\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}\\
b_{n-1}&b_{n-2}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-1}^{2}}

(83)

where
b_{i}=0
for
i<0
.

Example 4.1
Referring back to the function from Example 1.2:

\displaystyle f(x)=\frac{5x^{3}+13x^{2}+3x+9}{4x^{2}+5x+7}

(84)

The corresponding asymptote would be

y=\frac{5}{4}x-\frac{\begin{vmatrix}5&13\\
4&5\end{vmatrix}}{4^{2}}

(85)

\displaystyle y=\frac{5}{4}x+\frac{27}{16}

(86)

4.2
Curvilinear Asymptote

For the quadratic function

f(x)=\frac{{a_{n}x^{n}}+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1}x+a_{0}}{b_{n-2}x^{n-2}+b_{n-3}x^{n-3}+\cdots+b_{1}x+b_{0}}

(87)

the formula for its curvilinear asymptote becomes

y=\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n-2}}x^{2}-\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}\\
b_{n-2}&b_{n-3}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-2}^{2}}x+\frac{\begin{vmatrix}a_{n}&a_{n-1}&a_{n-2}\\
b_{n-2}&b_{n-3}&b_{n-4}\\
0&b_{n-2}&b_{n-3}\end{vmatrix}}{b_{n-2}^{3}}

(88)

where
b_{i}=0
for
i<0
.

Example 4.2
The curvilinear asymptote of the function

\displaystyle f(x)=\frac{x^{4}-2x^{3}+3x-9}{2x^{2}-5}\\

(89)

would be

y=\frac{1}{2}x^{2}-\frac{\begin{vmatrix}1&-2\\
2&0\end{vmatrix}}{2^{2}}x+\frac{\begin{vmatrix}1&-2&0\\
2&0&-5\\
0&2&0\end{vmatrix}}{2^{3}}

(90)

\displaystyle y=\frac{x^{2}}{2}-x+\frac{5}{4}

(91)

(92)

1&-4\end{vmatrix}}{1^{2}}x+\frac{\begin{vmatrix}8&0&0\\

0&1&-4\end{vmatrix}}{1^{3}}

(93)

\displaystyle y=8x^{2}+32x+128
