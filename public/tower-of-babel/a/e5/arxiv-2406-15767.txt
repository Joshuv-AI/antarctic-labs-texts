Reducibility points and characteristic p p local fields I Simple supercuspidal representations of symplectic groups.
Corinne Blondel, Guy Henniart, Shaun Stevens

Abstract
. Let F F be a non-Archimedean local field with odd characteristic p p . Let N N be a positive integer and G = Sp 2 ​ N ⁡ ( F ) G=\operatorname{Sp}_{2N}(F) . By work of Lomelí on γ \gamma -factors of pairs and converse theorems, a generic supercuspidal representation π \pi of G G has a transfer to a smooth irreducible representation Π π \Pi_{\pi} of GL 2 ​ N + 1 ⁡ ( F ) \operatorname{GL}_{2N+1}(F) . In turn the Weil–Deligne representation Σ π \Sigma_{\pi} associated to Π π \Pi_{\pi} by the Langlands correspondence determines a Langlands parameter ϕ π \phi_{\pi} for π \pi . That process produces a Langlands correspondence for generic cuspidal representations of G G . In this paper we take π \pi to be simple in the sense of Gross and Reeder, and from the explicit construction of π \pi we describe Π π \Pi_{\pi} explicitly. The method we use is the same as in a previous paper, where we treated the case where F F is a p p -adic field, and π \pi a simple supercuspidal representation of G = Sp 2 ​ N ⁡ ( F ) G=\operatorname{Sp}_{2N}(F) . It relies on a criterion due to Mœglin on the reducibility of representations parabolically induced from GL M ⁡ ( F ) × G \operatorname{GL}_{M}(F)\times G for varying positive integers M M . We extend this criterion to the case when F F has any positive characteristic. The main new feature consists in relating reducibility to γ \gamma -factors for pairs.
