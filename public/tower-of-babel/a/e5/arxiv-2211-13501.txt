Dynamical branes on expanding orbifold and complex projective space
Muneto Nitta, Kunihito Uzawa

Abstract
We construct some new dynamical p p -brane solutions to gravity theories on curved backgrounds. We discuss the relations between dynamical branes, a new time-dependent solution on complex projective space ℂ ​ P n \mathbb{C}{\rm P}^{n} and the static p p -branes on the orbifold ℂ n / ℤ n {\mathbb{C}}^{n}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n} .

the equation

(32)

For example, we require that the functions

and

\bar{K}(\theta)

\frac{\left(1+\bar{\rho}^{2}\right)^{3}}{\bar{\rho}^{3}}\partial_{\bar{\rho}}\left(\frac{\bar{\rho}^{3}}{1+\bar{\rho}^{2}}\partial_{\bar{\rho}}\bar{H}\right)=0\,,~~~~~~\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\partial_{\theta}\left[\frac{4\left(1+\bar{\rho}^{2}\right)}{\bar{\rho}^{2}}\sin\theta\,\partial_{\theta}\bar{K}\right]=0\,.

(33)

The solution to these equations is

\displaystyle\bar{H}(\bar{\rho})=\bar{c}_{1}\left(-\frac{1}{2\bar{\rho}^{2}}+\ln\bar{\rho}\right)+\bar{c}_{2}\,,~~~~~\bar{K}(\theta)=\bar{c}_{3}\ln\tan\frac{\theta}{2}+\bar{c}_{4}\,,

(34)

where
\bar{c}_{i}~(i=1\,,\cdots\,,4)
are constants.

h_{0}
and

.

(36a)
