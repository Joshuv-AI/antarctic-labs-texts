Constraining the Active-to-Heavy-Neutrino transitional magnetic moments associated with the Z ′ Z^{\prime} interactions at FASER ν \nu .
Kingman Cheung a,b,c , C.J. Ouseph a,b

Abstract
We investigate the effects of the transitional magnetic dipole moment of the active-to-heavy-neutrino associated with a new neutral gauge boson Z ′ Z^{\prime} on neutrino-nucleon scattering at the ForwArd Search ExpeRiment- ν \nu (FASER ν \nu ). We consider the neutral-current neutrino-nucleon scattering ( ν ​ A → N ​ A \nu A\rightarrow NA ) as the production mechanism of the heavy neutrino N N at FASER ν \nu and estimate the sensitivity reach on the magnetic moment coupling μ ν α \mu_{\nu_{\alpha}} for a range of heavy neutrino mass ( 1 ​ GeV < M N < 70 ​ GeV 1\,{\rm GeV}<M_{N}<70{\rm GeV} ). In this study, we consider three benchmark models, in which the heavy neutrino is coupled to L e L_{e} , L μ L_{\mu} or L τ L_{\tau} doublet, respectively.

Introduction

Heavy neutrinos beyond the current known species of active neutrinos
are predicted in a number of grand unified theories (GUT), such as GUT
based on
SO(10)
, and are motivated as the natural explanation why
the active neutrinos are so light compared with other matter fermions.
One of the natural models is the seesaw model

;
Mohapatra:1979ia

in which the heavy neutrinos
are right-handed neutrinos of mass
O(10^{11-14})
GeV, that explains
the order of active mass to be
y(vev)^{2}/M_{N}
, where the
vev\sim 10^{2}

GeV is the electroweak vacuum expectation value. There are other
extensions
Dev:2009aw
;
Deppisch:2004fa
of the ideas of the
seesaw mechanism that predict intermediate mass
neutrinos and can also be as light as a few GeV’s. Here in this work we
focus on the heavy neutrinos of mass in the range of
1-70
GeV.

It is well known that a large number of hadrons are produced along the

During the flight, these hadrons will decay thus producing neutrinos

The neutrinos so produced can pass through hundred meters of rock and concrete between the ATLAS IP and the FASER detector, whereas most other particles will be either deflected or absorbed before reaching FASER.
FASER is unique in terms of the energy range of the neutrinos

Other similar setups, such as DUNE near detector, have lower

We identify the events of heavy neutrinos through its decays

\nu

II
The Heavy Neutrino Model

The new matter content includes a heavy neutrino
N
and a

U(1)
gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
.
The effective Lagrangian describing the interactions of
N
and
Z^{\prime}

with the standard model (SM) gauge bosons
Drewes:2015iva
;
Shrock:1978ft
;
FMMF:1994yvb
;
Pascoli:2018heg
;
Alva:2014gxa
;
Atre:2009rg
and other particles
He:1991qd
;
Hewett:1988xc
;
Leike:1998wr
;
Langacker:2008yv
;
Lee:2008zzl
can be written as

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\rm eff}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha=e,\mu,\tau}\Biggr[\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}\overline{N}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\nu_{\rm\alpha}Z^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}-\frac{g}{\sqrt{2}}V_{\alpha N}\overline{N}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}l_{\alpha}W_{\mu}^{+}-\frac{g}{\cos\theta_{w}}V_{\alpha N}\overline{N}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\nu_{\alpha}Z_{\mu}+\rm H.c.\Biggr]

(1)

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\sum_{q,\nu,l}\left[g_{\rm q}\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}q+g_{\nu}\bar{\nu}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\nu+g_{l}\bar{l}\gamma^{\mu}l\right]Z^{\prime}_{\mu}\;,

where
g=e/\sin\theta_{w}
,
\theta_{w}
is the Weinberg angle,

Z^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}
is the field strength of the
Z^{\prime}
boson,
the sum over
q=u,d
,

\nu=\nu_{e},\nu_{\mu},\nu_{\tau}
and
l=e,\mu,\tau
, and the mixing parameter

V_{\alpha N}
denotes the mixing between
\nu_{\alpha}
and
N
.
Here
\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}
denotes the coupling of the active-to-heavy-neutrino transitional magnetic dipole moment, for which the first term in the Lagrangian Eq. (
1
) is valid up to a cut-off energy scale
\Lambda
.
An interpretation of
\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}
above the electroweak scale requires
a Higgs insertion so that the transitional magnetic dipole interaction
described in Eq. (
1
) originates from a dimension-6 operator.
The
\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}
’s are therefore envisioned to be of the order of

1/\Lambda^{2}
in form of
\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}\sim\frac{e.vev}{\Lambda^{2}}
.

The effective Lagrangian consists of two widely used beyond the SM models:
(i) the renormalizable
Z^{\prime}
model with flavor-conserving quark, lepton and neutrino interactions
He:1991qd
;
Hewett:1988xc
;
Leike:1998wr
;
Langacker:2008yv
;
Lee:2008zzl

and (ii) an effective/simplified extension by introducing a right-handed (RH) neutrino, which is a singlet under the SM gauge symmetry
Pascoli:2018heg
;
Alva:2014gxa
;
Atre:2009rg
, with
a transitional dipole moment connecting the active and the heavy neutrino.
Without loss of generality, we assume that
q=u,d
have equal coupling strength
g_{q}
with the
Z^{\prime}
,
\nu=\nu_{e},\nu_{\mu},\nu_{\tau}
have equal strength
g_{\nu}
with the
Z^{\prime}
, and equal coupling strength
g_{l}
for

l=e,\mu,\tau
.

V
Conclusions

In this paper, we have studied the active-to-heavy-neutrino transitional magnetic moment which arises from the
Z^{\prime}
boson interactions between
the heavy and active neutrinos at FASER
\nu
.
We consider the neutrino-nucleon scattering as the production process of the heavy neutrino, and we see that if the heavy neutrino can decay
into charged leptons or hadrons, it can be identified with negligible
backgrounds inside the FASER
\nu
detector.
We investigated the advantage of FASER
\nu
in a wide mass range
search for the heavy neutrino
N
and
Z^{\prime}
to determine the flavor dependence of the coupling between the active neutrinos and the
heavy neutrino, for which we found that FASER
\nu
is sensitive to
\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}
because of large incoming neutrino flux.

In this study we have considered three benchmark models in which
the heavy neutrino couples to
L_{\mu}
,
L_{e}
or
L_{\tau}
SM doublet.
Among all the three models, the BM-I in which the heavy neutrino couples
to
L_{\mu}
has the best sensitivity reach compared to the other two.
We obtained sensitivity curves by considering two different final states: (i) charged leptons and (ii) charged leptons and hadrons.
The second final state provides better sensitivity reach on
\mu_{\nu_{\alpha}}
.
While the FASER
\nu
can achieve the best sensitivity at small

M_{Z^{\prime}}
and
M_{N}
regime, the sensitivity limits get weaker
with the increment of heavy neutrino and
Z^{\prime}
masses. We did not include antineutrinos in our study; if we had included antineutrinos, the sensitivity curves could be improved by a factor of 2.

could be displaced from the production vertex.
