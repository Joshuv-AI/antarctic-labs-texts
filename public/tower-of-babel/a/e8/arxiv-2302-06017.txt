On the q q -binomial identities involving the Legendre symbol modulo 3 3 .
Alexander Berkovich

Abstract
. I use polynomial analogue of the Jacobi triple product identity together with the Eisenstein formula for the Legendre symbol modulo 3 3 to prove six identities involving the q q -binomial coefficients. These identities are then extended to the new infinite hierarchies of q q -series identities by means of the special case of Bailey’s lemma. Some of the identities of Ramanujan, Slater, McLaughlin and Sills are obtained this way.

In this section, we present three new polynomial identities. The proof of these identities is left as an exercise for a motivated reader.

\sum_{j=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{j}\left(\frac{j+1}{3}\right)q^{j\choose 2}{2L+1\brack L-j}_{q}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{(q^{3};q^{3})_{L-1}}{(q)_{L-1}}(2+q^{L}(1+q)-q^{2L+1}),&L>0\\
