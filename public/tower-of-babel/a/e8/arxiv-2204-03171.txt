Representations and cohomologies of differential 3-Lie algebras with any weight


Abstract
The purpose of the present paper is to study cohomologies of differential 3-Lie algebras with any weight. We introduce the representation of a differential 3-Lie algebra. Moreover, we develop cohomology theory of a differential 3-Lie algebra. We also depict the relationship between the cohomologies of a differential 3-Lie algebra and its associated differential Leibniz algebra with weight zero. Formal deformations, abelian extensions and skeletal differential 3-Lie 2-algebras are characterized in terms of cohomology groups

Moreover, combining the Snake Lemma, we have the following long

\cdot\cdot\cdot\longrightarrow H_{DO_{\lambda}}^{n-1}(d_{\mathfrak{g}},d_{V})\longrightarrow H^{n}_{3-D_{\lambda}}(\mathfrak{g},V)\longrightarrow H^{n}_{3-Lie}(\mathfrak{g},V)\longrightarrow H_{DO_{\lambda}}^{n}(d_{\mathfrak{g}},d_{V})\longrightarrow\cdot\cdot\cdot.

3.2
Relation between the cohomologies of differential 3-Lie
algebras and differential Leibniz algebras

We have constructed
cohomological groups of
(\mathfrak{g},d_{\mathfrak{g}})
with
coefficients in its representation
(V,\rho,d_{V})
. Since any

-Lie algebra
(\mathfrak{g},d_{\mathfrak{g}})

(L=\wedge^{2}\mathfrak{g},d_{L})

and associated differential Leibniz
algebra
(L=\wedge^{2}\mathfrak{g},d_{L})

the relation between them like the case of algebras, unfortunately,
we fail to do that. We only discuss their relationship in the case
of
\lambda=0

In the remaining part of this section, we always put
\lambda=0
.

Let
(\mathfrak{g},d_{\mathfrak{g}})
be a differential 3-Lie algebra
with weight
\lambda=0
and
(\rho,V,d_{V})
be its representation.
Then
(L=\wedge^{2}\mathfrak{g},d_{L})

\rho^{L},~\rho^{R}:L\longrightarrow\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V)

\rho^{L}(x,y)(f)(z)=\rho(x,y)f(z)-f([x,y,z])

and

\rho^{R}(x,y)(f)(z)=f([x,y,z])-\rho(x,y)f(z)-\rho(y,z)f(x)-\rho(z,x)f(y),

for any
x\wedge y\in L

(V,\rho^{L},\rho^{R})
is a
representation of
Leibniz algebra
L

[

,
10
]

Proposition 3.5.
Let
(V,\rho,d_{V})
be a representation of differential 3-Lie
algebra
(\mathfrak{g},d_{\mathfrak{g}})
, and
(L=\wedge^{2}\mathfrak{g},d_{L})

algebra with
(\mathfrak{g},d_{\mathfrak{g}})

(\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V),\rho^{L},\rho^{R},\psi)

differential Leibniz
algebra
(L,d_{L})
, where
\psi:\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V)\longrightarrow\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V)

\psi(f)=d_{V}f-fd_{\mathfrak{g}}.

[formula]

Proof.

We only need to check that

\psi\rho^{L}(X)=\rho^{L}(d_{L}(X))+\rho^{L}(X)\psi

and

\psi\rho^{R}(X)=\rho^{R}(d_{L}(X))+\rho^{R}(X)\psi.

In fact, for any
f\in\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V)
and
X=x\wedge y\in L,~z\in\mathfrak{g}
, in view of (3.3) and (3.4),

\displaystyle\rho^{L}(d_{L}(X))f(z)+\rho^{L}(X)\psi(f)(z)-(\psi\rho^{L}(X)f)(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\rho(d_{\mathfrak{g}}x,y)f(z)-f([d_{\mathfrak{g}}x,y,z])+\rho(x,d_{\mathfrak{g}}x)f(z)-f([x,d_{\mathfrak{g}}y,z])+\rho(x,y)d_{V}f(z)

\displaystyle-d_{V}f([x,y,z])-\rho(x,y)fd_{\mathfrak{g}}(z)+f([x,y,d_{\mathfrak{g}}z])-d_{V}(\rho(x,y)f(z)-f([x,y,z]))

\displaystyle+\rho(x,y)fd_{\mathfrak{g}}(z)-fd_{\mathfrak{g}}([x,y,z])

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.

It follows that

\psi\rho^{L}(X)=\rho^{L}(d_{L}(X))+\rho^{L}(X)\psi.

Analogously,

\psi\rho^{R}(X)=\rho^{R}(d_{L}(X))+\rho^{R}(X)\psi.

∎

It is natural to discuss cohomologies of differential Leibniz
algebra
(L,d_{L})
with coefficients in the representation

(\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V),\rho^{L},\rho^{R},\psi)

Let

\displaystyle\partial_{Leib}(f)(X_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{n+1})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\rho^{L}(X_{1})f(X_{2},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{n+1})+\sum_{i=2}^{n+1}\rho^{R}(X_{i})f(X_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{i-1},X_{i+1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{n+1})

\displaystyle+\sum_{i<j}(-1)^{j+1}f(X_{1},,\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{i-1},[X_{i},X_{j}]_{F},X_{i+1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{j-1},X_{j+1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{n+1}).

for any
X_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,X_{n+1}\in L
and
f\in C_{\hbox{Leib}}^{n}(L,\hbox{Hom}(\mathfrak{g},V))
.

Now we are ready to give the cohomology of differential Leibniz
algebra

with coefficients in the representation

Given a linear map

The linear map

\scriptstyle{\bar{\Theta}}

\displaystyle\sum_{i+j=k,~i,~j\geq 0}(\pi_{i}(\pi_{j}(v,w,x),y,z)+\pi_{i}(x,\pi_{j}(v,w,y),z)+\pi_{i}(x,y,\pi_{j}(v,w,z))

(\mathfrak{g}[[t]],\pi_{t},\varphi_{t})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\varphi_{1}([x,y,z])+[x,y,\varphi_{1}(z)]+\lambda[d_{\mathfrak{g}}x,y,\varphi_{1}(z)]+\lambda[x,d_{\mathfrak{g}}y,\varphi_{1}(z)]+\lambda^{2}[d_{\mathfrak{g}}x,d_{\mathfrak{g}}y,\varphi_{1}(z)]

\displaystyle+[y,z,\varphi_{1}(x)]+\lambda[d_{\mathfrak{g}}y,z,\varphi_{1}(x)]+\lambda[y,d_{\mathfrak{g}}z,\varphi_{1}(x)]+\lambda^{2}[d_{\mathfrak{g}}y,d_{\mathfrak{g}}z,\varphi_{1}(x)]
