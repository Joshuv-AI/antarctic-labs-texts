The Bernoulli property for counter-twisting linked twist maps
J. Myers Hill

Abstract
We prove the Bernoulli property for a class of counter-twisting linked twist maps. These compose orthogonal linear shears on the torus, orientated in the opposite sense to their co-twisting counterparts (where the shears reinforce one another). Compared to previous studies we focus on the parameter space corresponding to weak shears, near the critical parameter below which hyperbolicity is lost and the map is non-mixing. The approach developed to deal with this situation appears applicable to a broad range of non-uniformly hyperbolic examples.

The fundamental obstacle in the counter-twisting setting is outlined in
[

]
. While hyperbolicity provides the expansion needed to prove mixing properties, it is tempered by the folding effect of the

[

[

5

Background results

By
[

]

H
is

]

(
MR

For almost any

, there exist

such that for all

and

H^{m}\gamma_{u}(z)\cap H^{-n}\gamma_{s}(\zeta)\neq\varnothing

and
H^{-n}\gamma_{s}(\zeta)
, growing these images up to some tangible size where intersections may be inferred.

A uniformly hyperbolic induced map forms the basis for this expansion. The canonical choice is the return map
H_{S}:S\to S
,
z\mapsto H^{r}(z)
, where

denotes the return time of
z
to
S=P\cap Q
under
H
. It decomposes as the composition
H_{S}=G_{S}\circ F_{S}
of returns under
F
then
G
so

for some naturals

depending on

. The return time of
z
to
S
is then given by

. Recall from
[

]
the cone

, defined by vectors

with

. Similarly defining

and
