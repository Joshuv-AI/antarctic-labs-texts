Abstract
. This paper presents a commutative complex oriented cohomology theory that realizes the Buchstaber formal group law F B F_{B} localized away from 2. It is shown that the restriction of the classifying map of F B F_{B} on special unitary cobordism ring localized away from 2 defines a four parameter genus, studied by Hoehn and Totaro.
