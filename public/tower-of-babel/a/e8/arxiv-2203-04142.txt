A Reply to “On Salum’s Algorithm for X3SAT”
Latif Salum

Abstract
This paper gives a reply to “On Salum’s Algorithm for X3SAT” [ 1 ] .



The authors confuse the definition of the scope
\psi(r_{i})
, beyond the scope
{\phi}^{\prime}(r_{i})
, and the formula
{\varphi}=\psi\wedge{\phi}
. Also, they claim that “it is easy to see that X3SAT formulas with empty minterms are equivalent to 3CNF formulas (by simply replacing the
\odot
notational element with
\vee
)”. I did not understand what the authors meant here. The formula
(a\odot b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot x\odot y)\wedge(c\odot x\odot\overline{y})
is totally different from the formula
(a\vee b\vee c)\wedge(b\vee x\vee y)\wedge(c\vee x\vee\overline{y})
.

In particular, the authors find
\color[rgb]{1,0,0}{\phi}=(a\wedge\overline{b}\wedge\overline{c})\wedge(x\odot y)\wedge(x\odot\bar{y})
, after
Scope

\!(a,{\phi})
runs. They next run
Scope

\!(\overline{a},{\phi})
over
\color[rgb]{1,0,0}{\phi}
, and detect the conflict with
a
already in the minterm. The authors also run
Scope

\!(x,{\phi})
over
\color[rgb]{1,0,0}{\phi}
, and find
\color[rgb]{1,0,0}{\phi}=(a\wedge\overline{b}\wedge\overline{c}\wedge x\wedge\bar{y}\wedge y)
. These are totally wrong.

The correct way of the executions is as follows.

{\varphi}=\psi\wedge{\phi}
.
\psi
is empty and
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}{\phi}=(a\odot b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot x\odot y)\wedge(c\odot x\odot\overline{y})
.

Scope

\!(a,{\phi})
:
\color[rgb]{1,0.5,0}{\phi}(a):=a\wedge{\phi}
. Then,
{\phi}(a)=\psi(a)\wedge{\phi}^{\prime}(a)
, as
Scope

\!(a,{\phi})
terminates.
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}\psi(a)=a\wedge\overline{b}\wedge\overline{c}
and
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}{\phi}^{\prime}(a)=(x\odot y)\wedge(x\odot\bar{y})
.

Scope

\!(\overline{a},{\phi})
:
{\phi}(\overline{a}):=\overline{a}\wedge{\phi}
.
{\phi}(\overline{a})=\psi(\overline{a})\wedge{\phi}^{\prime}(\overline{a})
.
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}\psi(\overline{a})=\overline{a}
and
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}{\phi}^{\prime}(\overline{a})=(b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot x\odot y)\wedge(c\odot x\odot\overline{y})
.

Scope

\!(x,{\phi})
:
{\phi}(x):=x\wedge{\phi}
.
\psi(x)\Rightarrow\overline{b}\wedge x\wedge\overline{y}\wedge\overline{c}\wedge y
. Thus,
x
is incompatible, because
\psi(x)\Rightarrow\overline{y}\wedge y
. As a result,
\overline{x}
is necessary. That is,
{\varphi}\leftarrow\overline{x}\wedge{\phi}
.

{\varphi}=\psi\wedge{\phi}
, where
\psi=\overline{x}
and
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}{\phi}=(a\odot b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot y)\wedge(c\odot\overline{y})
.

Scope

\!(a,{\phi})
:
\color[rgb]{1,0.5,0}{\phi}(a):=a\wedge{\phi}
.
\psi(a)\Rightarrow a\wedge\overline{b}\wedge\overline{c}\wedge y\wedge\overline{y}
. Thus,
\overline{a}
is necessary.

{\varphi}\leftarrow\psi\wedge{\phi}
, where
\psi=\overline{x}\wedge\overline{a}
and
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}{\phi}=(b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot y)\wedge(c\odot\overline{y})
.

Scope

\!(b,{\phi})
:
{\phi}(b):=b\wedge{\phi}
.
\psi(b)=b\wedge\overline{c}\wedge\overline{y}
and
{\phi}^{\prime}(b)
is empty.

Scope

\!(\overline{b},{\phi})
:
{\phi}(\overline{b}):=\overline{b}\wedge{\phi}
.
\psi(\overline{b})=\overline{b}\wedge c\wedge y
and
{\phi}^{\prime}(\overline{b})
is empty.

Likewise,
c,\overline{c},y,\overline{y}
are decided to be compatible. Then, the current formula becomes
{\varphi}=\overline{x}\wedge\overline{a}\wedge\color[rgb]{0,0,1}(b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot y)\wedge(c\odot\overline{y})
, which is satisfiable. Note that
\overline{x}\wedge\overline{a}
is fixed in any satisfying assignment. Next, a satisfying assignment is constructed over
\color[rgb]{0,0,1}{\phi}=(b\odot c)\wedge(b\odot y)\wedge(c\odot\overline{y})
. See also this short presentation
[
2
]
.
