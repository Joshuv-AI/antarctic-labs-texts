Fractional black 𝒑 p -branes on orbifold ℂ 𝒏 / ℤ 𝒏 {\mathbb{C}}^{n}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}
Muneto Nitta, Kunihito Uzawa

Abstract
The recent discovery of an explicit solution of a black hole on the resolved orbifold ℂ n / ℤ n {\mathbb{C}}^{n}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n} makes it possible to investigate the existence of p p -branes on the orbifold. In particular, it is possible with reasonable precision to verify the prediction that an M2-brane on ℂ 4 / ℤ 4 {\mathbb{C}}^{4}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{4} in eleven dimensions and a D3-brane on ℂ 3 / ℤ 3 {\mathbb{C}}^{3}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{3} in ten dimensions have a family of black p p -branes on the orbifold ℂ n / ℤ n {\mathbb{C}}^{n}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n} . These solutions are extremal and have regular horizons S 2 ​ n − 1 / ℤ n S^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n} without any naked singularity, with near horizon geometries AdS p + 2 × S 2 ​ n − 1 / ℤ n {}_{p+2}\times S^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n} .

The terminology “

Since they have the mass saturating a lower bound

Four-dimensional charged black holes are

In many instances it

The

Here we use the ADM formula in our case, and
derive the expression for the ADM mass

M=\frac{V_{p}\,V_{{S}^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}}}{\kappa^{2}}\frac{c_{1}}{c_{0}}(D-p-3)\left(\frac{p}{p+1}c_{0}^{-\frac{1}{p+1}}+\frac{D-p-2}{D-p-3}\,c_{0}^{\frac{1}{D-p-3}}\right)\,,

(18)

where
V_{p}
is the volume of the “longitudinal”
space spanned by the world volume of the
p
-brane
and
V_{S^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}}
is the volume
of
S^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}
.

The charge density per a unit
p
-volume is defined by

q=\frac{1}{2\kappa^{2}}\int_{{S}^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}}\ast F_{(p+2)}\,.

(19)

Then, the total amount of the charge carried by
the solution becomes

\displaystyle|Q|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle V_{p}\,|q|=V_{p}\sqrt{\frac{2(D-2)(D-p-3)}{p+1}}\,\frac{c_{1}\,V_{{S}^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}}}{2\kappa^{2}}

(20)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{0}\,\sqrt{\frac{D-2}{2(p+1)(D-p-3)}}\left(\frac{p}{p+1}c_{0}^{-\frac{1}{p+1}}+\frac{D-p-2}{D-p-3}\,c_{0}^{\frac{1}{D-p-3}}\right)^{-1}M\,.

Since the mass
M
and the charge
Q
are proportional
to each other, this is extremal or BPS.

A black
p
-brane in flat space
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}

has a ADM mass and charge density, given by

M_{1}=\frac{V_{p}\,V_{{S}^{2n-1}}}{\kappa^{2}}\frac{c_{1}}{c_{0}}(D-p-3)\left(\frac{p}{p+1}c_{0}^{-\frac{1}{p+1}}+\frac{D-p-2}{D-p-3}\,c_{0}^{\frac{1}{D-p-3}}\right)\,,

(21)

and

\displaystyle|Q_{1}|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle V_{p}\,|q_{1}|=V_{p}\sqrt{\frac{2(D-2)(D-p-3)}{p+1}}\,\frac{c_{1}\,V_{{S}^{2n-1}}}{2\kappa^{2}}

(22)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{0}\,\sqrt{\frac{D-2}{2(p+1)(D-p-3)}}\left(\frac{p}{p+1}c_{0}^{-\frac{1}{p+1}}+\frac{D-p-2}{D-p-3}\,c_{0}^{\frac{1}{D-p-3}}\right)^{-1}M_{1}\,,

respectively. Here we define the charge density per a
unit
p
-volume:

q_{1}=\frac{1}{2\kappa^{2}}\int_{{S}^{2n-1}}\ast F_{(p+2)}\,.

(23)

This black
p
-brane can live also on the
orbifold as well.
The geometry is not significantly modified
if it is far away from the orbifold singularity
where the geometry is asymptotically flat.

Since the volumes are

V_{{S}^{2n-1}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}}\,=(1/n)V_{{S}^{2n-1}}
,
our solution has
1/n
mass and charge densities
of the conventional one,

M=\frac{1}{n}M_{1}\,,\quad|Q|=\frac{1}{n}|Q_{1}|\,,

(24)

and thus we call it a
fractional

p
-brane.
The unit
p
-brane
(M_{1}\,,Q_{1})
,
which is supposed to be minimally quantized by the
Dirac quantization condition, can exist
outside the orbifold singularity of
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}
,
while fractional one cannot exist and is stacked at the singularity
for the consistency with the Dirac quantization condition.
Such fractional objects stucked at orbifold singularities
are common among
Yang-Mills instantons
Nakajima
;
Nakajima2
,
vortices
Kimura:2011wh
,
and D-branes

Douglas:1996sw
;
Douglas:1997de
;
Eto:2004vy
.

4
Discussions

We conclude with some comments on the properties
of the solutions we have found, and potential applications.
It is interesting to discuss the supersymmetry of
the extremal
p
-brane solutions. It was pointed out in
Refs.
Dabholkar:1989jt
;
Dabholkar:1990yf
;
Stelle:1996tz
;
Argurio:1998cp
that the extremal
p
-brane
solutions are supersymmetric.
While we have not considered supersymmetry transformation
laws, it appears likely that some extremally charged
black
p
-branes on the orbifolds are supersymmetric.
This implies the existence of new varieties of solutions
for black holes on orbifolds.

We have discussed extended black hole solutions in

D
dimensions, since this is the interest for
supergravity. One can clearly modify the derivation
in section
2
to obtain charged black
p
-branes
with a scalar field in any dimension. It is an interesting
question whether or not there exist black
p
-branes
in
D
dimensions if we add non-trivial scalar field.
Although there exist spacetimes with this causal
structure (taking the product of
(p+2)
-dimensional spacetime
and
(D-p-2)
-dimensional space in the
D
-dimensional
solution), the associated energy momentum tensor is usually
unphysical due to giving rise to naked singularity in the
background
Argurio:1998cp
.

There are many open questions regarding the dynamics of
black
p
-branes on the orbifolds. Even more interesting,
studying a way in which the classical solutions interact,
for example by constructing solutions with intersecting

p
-branes
Argurio:1997gt
,
is relevant for checking the consistency with
the interactions of these objects. Another important issue
involving intersections of
p
-branes on the orbifolds is
looking for supersymmetric extremal black holes with a
non-vanishing horizon area.
When one can identify these
p
-brane configurations
in supergravity, mainly systems involving M-brane and
D-branes
Polchinski:1996na
, one can obtain a
microscopic counting of states definitely giving the
semiclassical black hole entropy
Strominger:1996sh
;
Callan:1996dv
.

In Sec.
2
, we have constructed
general solutions whose transverse directions
are general Ricci-flat manifolds.
Thus, replacing the orbifold geometries by
more general Ricci-flat manifolds can give
further black holes and black
p
-brane solutions.
Recalling a fact that the resolved orbifolds

{\mathbb{C}}^{n}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}
are
complex line bundles over
{\mathbb{C}}{\rm P}^{n-1}
,
one can consider Ricci-flat metrics on complex
line bundles over other homogeneous Kähler manifolds
G/H

Higashijima:2001vk
;
Higashijima:2001fp
;
Higashijima:2002px
.
In fact, D3-branes on six-dimensional
resolved and deformed conifolds were constructed in
Refs.
PandoZayas:2000ctr
;
PandoZayas:2001iw
.
Black
p
-branes on higher dimensional deformed conifold

Higashijima:2001yn
should be possible.
Also, by replacing
{\mathbb{C}}{\rm P}^{n-1}
in this paper by
the quadric surface

G/H=
SO(
n
)/[SO(
n-2)\times
U(1)]

Higashijima:2001de
,
one could construct black
p
-branes on
conifolds for which a horizon would be
[SO(
n
)/SO(
n-2
)]/
{\mathbb{Z}}_{n-2}
.
For more general homogeneous Kähler manifolds
G/H
,
one can expect more general geometries with exotic horizons.
