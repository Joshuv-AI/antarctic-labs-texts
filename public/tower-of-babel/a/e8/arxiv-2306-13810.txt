Analysis of a mixed finite element method for stochastic Cahn-Hilliard equation with multiplicative noise
Yukun Li, Corey Prachniak, Yi Zhang

Abstract
This paper proposes and analyzes a novel fully discrete finite element scheme with the interpolation operator for stochastic Cahn-Hilliard equations with functional-type noise. The nonlinear term satisfies a one-side Lipschitz condition and the diffusion term is globally Lipschitz continuous. The novelties of this paper are threefold. First, the L 2 L^{2} -stability ( L ∞ L^{\infty} in time) and the discrete H 2 H^{2} -stability ( L 2 L^{2} in time) are proved for the proposed scheme. The idea is to utilize the special structure of the matrix assembled by the nonlinear term. None of these stability results has been proved for the fully implicit scheme in existing literature due to the difficulty arising from the interaction of the nonlinearity and the multiplicative noise. Second, the higher moment stability in L 2 L^{2} -norm of the discrete solution is established based on the previous stability results. Third, the Hölder continuity in time for the strong solution is established under the minimum assumption of the strong solution. Based on these, the discrete H − 1 H^{-1} -norm of the strong convergence is discussed. Several numerical experiments including stability and convergence are also presented to validate our theoretical results.

(1)

\displaystyle du=\Bigl[-\Delta\Big(\epsilon\Delta u-\frac{1}{\epsilon}f(u)\Big)\Bigr]dt+\delta g(u)\,dW_{t}

(2)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}=\frac{\partial}{\partial n}\Big(\epsilon\Delta u-\frac{1}{\epsilon}f(u)\Big)=0

(9)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}=\frac{\partial w}{\partial n}=0

\displaystyle u=u_{0}

The goal of the paper is to design a numerical scheme for stochastic Cahn-Hilliard equations with functional-type noise, and hence to explore some numerical properties of the proposed scheme. Here the nonlinear term is not globally Lipschitz continuous and only satisfies a one-side Lipschitz continuity, which interacts with the diffusion term to add another layer of difficulty in designing numerical schemes. We are aiming to establish some stability results, higher moment bounds, and finally the convergence rates for the proposed numerical scheme.

Lemma 1
.

Let

be the strong solution to problem (
7
)–(
10

Then for any

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\|u(s)-u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right]+\epsilon\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{t}^{s}\|\Delta(u(\xi)-u(t))\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\,d\xi\right]\leq C_{1}(s-t),

\displaystyle C_{1}=C\sup_{t\leq\xi\leq s}\mathbb{E}\left[\|\Delta u(\xi)\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\right]+C\sup_{t\leq\xi\leq s}\mathbb{E}\left[\|u(\xi)\|^{6}_{H^{1}}\right].

Proof.

Consider the functional
\psi
defined as:

\psi(u(s))=\|u(s)-u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.

The first two Gateaux derivatives are:

\displaystyle D\psi(u(s))(\nu_{1})

\displaystyle=2(u(s)-u(t),\nu_{1}(s)),

\displaystyle D\psi(u(s))(\nu_{1},\nu_{2})

\displaystyle=2(\nu_{1}(s),\nu_{2}(s)).

Applying Itô’s formula to

yields

\displaystyle\|u(s)-u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=2\int_{t}^{s}(u(\xi)-u(t),-\Delta(\epsilon\Delta u(\xi)-\frac{1}{\epsilon}f(u(\xi))))\,d\xi

\displaystyle\quad+\delta^{2}\int_{t}^{s}(g(u(\xi)),g(u(\xi)))\,d\xi

\displaystyle\quad+2\int_{t}^{s}(u(\xi)-u(t),\delta g(u(\xi)))\,dW_{\xi}.

Now after using integration by parts twice on the first integral, we have

\displaystyle\|u(s)-u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=2\int_{t}^{s}(\Delta(u(\xi)-u(t)),-\epsilon\Delta(u(\xi)-u(t)))\,d\xi

\displaystyle\quad+2\int_{t}^{s}(\Delta(u(\xi)-u(t)),-\epsilon\Delta u(t))\,d\xi

\displaystyle\quad+2\int_{t}^{s}(\Delta(u(\xi)-u(t)),\frac{1}{\epsilon}f(u(\xi)))\,d\xi

\displaystyle\quad+\delta^{2}\int_{t}^{s}(g(u(\xi)),g(u(\xi)))\,d\xi

\displaystyle\quad+2\int_{t}^{s}((u(\xi)-u(t)),\delta g(u(\xi)))\,dW_{\xi}.

For the nonlinear term, by using the embedding theorem, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{t}^{s}\|f(u(\xi))\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\,d\xi\right]

\displaystyle\qquad=\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{t}^{s}\int_{\mathcal{D}}u^{6}(\xi)-2u^{4}(\xi)+u^{2}(\xi)\,dx\,d\xi\right]

\displaystyle\qquad\leq C\sup\limits_{t\leq\xi\leq s}\mathbb{E}\left[\|u(\xi)\|^{6}_{H^{1}}\right](s-t).

Then upon taking the expectation on both sides as well as applying the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, the Young’s inequality, and the Gronwall’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\|u(s)-u(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right]+\epsilon\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{t}^{s}\|\Delta(u(\xi)-u(t))\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\,d\xi\right]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\sup_{t\leq\xi\leq s}\mathbb{E}\left[\|\Delta u(\xi)\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\right](s-t)+C\sup_{t\leq\xi\leq s}\mathbb{E}\left[\|u(\xi)\|^{6}_{H^{1}}\right](s-t).

The conclusion is proved.
∎

Theorem 3
.

, respectively, we get

\displaystyle(u^{n}_{h}-u^{n-1}_{h},u^{n}_{h})+\tau(\nabla w^{n}_{h},\nabla u^{n}_{h})

\displaystyle=\delta(g(u^{n-1}_{h}),u^{n}_{h})\Delta W^{n},

\displaystyle\epsilon(\nabla u^{n}_{h},-\nabla\Delta_{h}u^{n}_{h})+\frac{1}{\epsilon}(I_{h}f^{n},-\Delta_{h}u^{n}_{h})

\displaystyle(\nabla w^{n}_{h},\nabla u^{n}_{h})

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n}_{h}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}-\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n-1}_{h}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n}_{h}-u^{n-1}_{h}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\epsilon\tau\|\Delta_{h}u^{n}_{h}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\frac{C\tau}{\epsilon^{3}}\|u^{n}_{h}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\frac{\epsilon\tau}{2}\|\Delta_{h}u^{n}_{h}\|^{2}_{L^{2}}.

6
Conclusion

In this manuscript, we design a numerical scheme for the stochastic Cahn-Hilliard equation with multiplicative noise. The scheme utilizes the interpolation operator to handle the interaction between the drift term and the diffusion term, and is proven to maintain some stability results and higher moment results. Based on these stability results, we construct a probability one set such that the error estimates in the discrete
H^{-1}
-norm hold on this set.

Future work will remove the probability one set, i.e., establishing the error estimates in the whole probability space. The key step is to prove the higher moment bounds for
H^{1}
-norm. This is an open question for this type of stochastic Cahn-Hilliard equation and some new techniques need to be brought in.
