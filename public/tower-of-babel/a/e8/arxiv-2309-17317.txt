Groups Γ n 4 \Gamma_{n}^{4} : algebraic properties
Styrt O. G

Abstract




In the paper, groups
\Gamma_{n}^{4}
closely connected with braid groups are researched from algebraic point of view.
More exactly, for
n\geqslant 7
, it is proved that
\Gamma_{n}^{4}
is a nilpotent finite
2
-
group with
4
-
torsion
and that its subgroup
(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}
is central.

Key words
: groups
\Gamma_{n}^{4}
, braid groups.

\bigl\{M^{\prime}\subset M\colon|M^{\prime}|=k\bigr\}

Introduction

The paper
[
1
]
is devoted to researching groups
\Gamma_{n}^{4}
that are closely connected with braid groups.
The group
\Gamma_{n}^{4}
is defined in
[
1
, § 2]
by

•

the generators
d_{(ijkl)}
for all pairwise distinct
i,j,k,l\in[n]
;

•

the relations 1–4.

For convenience, denote by
S
the generator set and by
s_{ik}^{jl}
the generator
d_{(ijkl)}
. For
P\in C_{[n]}^{4}
,
set
S_{P}:=\bigl\{s_{ik}^{jl}\colon\{i,j,k,l\}=P\bigr\}\subset S
. Then, the relations
1
–
4
have the following sense:

1.

all elements of
S
are involutions;

2.

if
P,Q\in C_{[n]}^{4}
and
|P\cap Q|\leqslant 2
, then
[S_{P},S_{Q}]=\{e\}
;

4.

s_{ik}^{jl}=s_{ki}^{jl}=s_{ik}^{lj}=s_{jl}^{ik}
— so, each element
s_{ik}^{jl}
is uniquely defined by the
unordered
pair
\bigl\{\{i,k\},\{j,l\}\bigr\}
of the disjoint
unordered
pairs
\{i,k\}
and
\{j,l\}
;

3.

if
\{i,j,k,l,m\}\in C_{[n]}^{5}
, then
s_{ik}^{jl}s_{il}^{jm}s_{jl}^{km}s_{ik}^{jm}s_{il}^{km}=e
, i. e.

s_{ki}^{lj}s_{il}^{jm}s_{lj}^{mk}s_{jm}^{ki}s_{mk}^{il}=e
.

Hence, the condition
3
means that any pairwise distinct numbers
k_{1},\ldots,k_{5}\in[n]
satisfy

s_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}}\cdot s_{k_{2}k_{3}}^{k_{4}k_{5}}\cdot s_{k_{3}k_{4}}^{k_{5}k_{1}}\cdot s_{k_{4}k_{5}}^{k_{1}k_{2}}\cdot s_{k_{5}k_{1}}^{k_{2}k_{3}}=e.

(1)

The main result of the paper is the following theorem.

Theorem 1
.

If
n\geqslant 7
, then

•

the subgroup
(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}
of
\Gamma_{n}^{4}
is central
;

•

(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}\cong(\mathbb{Z}_{2})^{p}
and
\Gamma_{n}^{4}/(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}\cong(\mathbb{Z}_{2})^{q}
, where
p\leqslant C_{n}^{3}
and
q\leqslant 3\cdot C_{n}^{4}
.

Corollary 1
.

If
n\geqslant 7
, then

•

\Gamma_{n}^{4}
is a nilpotent finite
2
-
group
;

•

\bigl[\Gamma_{n}^{4},(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}\bigr]=\{e\}
;

•

a^{4}=e
for each
a\in\Gamma_{n}^{4}
.

2
Proofs of the results

In this section, Theorem
1
is proved.

For
a,x,y\in\Gamma_{n}^{4}
, write
x\sim_{a}y
if the following (obviously, equivalent) conditions hold:

1)

x^{-1}y\in Z(a)
;

2)

xax^{-1}=yay^{-1}
;

3)

[x,a]=[y,a]
;

4)

[a,x]=[a,y]
.

It is clear that, with
a
fixed, the
binary
relation
\sim_{a}
on
two
elements
x,y
is an equivalence.

From now, we will assume that
n\geqslant 6
.

Lemma 1
.

If
P\in C_{[n]}^{4}
,
K\in C_{P}^{3}
, and
s\in S_{P}
,
then
s_{1}\sim_{s}s_{2}
for all
s_{1},s_{2}\in\bigcup\limits_{{k^{\prime}\notin P}}S_{K\sqcup\{k^{\prime}\}}
.

\square\quad
For
k^{\prime}\notin K
and
k\in K
, set
t_{k^{\prime}k}:=s_{k^{\prime}k}^{ij}
where
\{i,j\}=K\setminus\{k\}
.

Note that
\bigl|[n]\setminus P\bigr|=n-4\geqslant 2
. Thus, it suffices to prove for any
distinct
k_{1},k_{2}\in[n]\setminus P
and distinct
i,j\in K
the relation
t_{k_{1}i}\sim_{s}t_{k_{2}j}
.

In
K
, take arbitrary distinct elements
k_{3},k_{5}
and denote the rest one by
k_{4}
.
Each subset
Q
of type
\bigl(K\setminus\{k\}\bigr)\sqcup\{k_{1},k_{2}\}
(
k\in K
) satisfies

Q\in C_{[n]}^{4}
and
P\cap Q\subset Q\setminus\{k_{1},k_{2}\}=K\setminus\{k\}
that implies
S_{Q}\subset Z(s)
. By (
1
),

t_{k_{2}k_{3}}^{-1}t_{k_{1}k_{5}}=s_{k_{2}k_{3}}^{k_{4}k_{5}}\cdot s_{k_{3}k_{4}}^{k_{5}k_{1}}=s_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}}\cdot s_{k_{5}k_{1}}^{k_{2}k_{3}}\cdot s_{k_{4}k_{5}}^{k_{1}k_{2}}\in Z(s)
.
∎

Lemma 2
.

Take any subset
K\in C_{[n]}^{3}
. Set
S_{(i)}:=S_{K\sqcup\{i\}}

(
i\notin K
)
.
Then all elements of type
[s_{i},s_{j}]

(
s_{i}\in S_{(i)}
,
s_{j}\in S_{(j)}
,
i,j\notin K
,
i\neq j
)

are the same involution
c^{(K)}
that is central for
n\geqslant 7
.

\square\quad
Set
M:=[n]\setminus K
. Clearly,
|M|=n-3\geqslant 3
.
If
k_{0}\in M
and
s\in S_{(k_{0})}
, then applying Lemma
1
to
P:=K\sqcup\{k_{0}\}

gives for any
s_{1},s_{2}\in\bigcup\limits_{{k^{\prime}\in M\setminus\{k_{0}\}}}S_{(k^{\prime})}
the relations
s_{1}s_{2}\in Z(s)
,

[s_{1},s]=[s_{2},s]
, and
[s,s_{1}]=[s,s_{2}]
.
Therefore:

•

If
\{i,j\}\in C_{M}^{2}
, then all elements
[s_{i},s_{j}]
(
s_{i}\in S_{(i)}
,
s_{j}\in S_{(j)}
)
are the same element
c^{(K)}_{ij}
. It is obvious that
c^{(K)}_{ji}=(c^{(K)}_{ij})^{-1}
.

•

If
k,i,j\in M
and
i,j\neq k
, then
c^{(K)}_{ik}=c^{(K)}_{jk}
and
c^{(K)}_{ki}=c^{(K)}_{kj}
.

Since
|M|\geqslant 3
, all elements
c^{(K)}_{ij}
(
i,j\in M
,
i\neq j
) are the same element
c^{(K)}=(c^{(K)})^{-1}
.
It remains to prove that
[c^{(K)},\Gamma_{n}^{4}]=\{e\}
for
n\geqslant 7
.

Suppose that
n\geqslant 7
,
P\in C_{[n]}^{4}
, and
s\in S_{P}
; show that
c^{(K)}\in Z(s)
.

There exist distinct numbers
k_{1},k_{2}\in M
such that
\bigl(|M\setminus P|\geqslant 2\bigr)\Rightarrow(k_{1},k_{2}\notin P)
.
Take any elements
s_{i}\in S_{(k_{i})}
(
i\in[2]
). Then
c^{(K)}=[s_{1},s_{2}]=(s_{1}s_{2})^{2}
.

Assume that
c^{(K)}\notin Z(s)
.

We have
s_{1}s_{2}\notin Z(s)
and
s_{i}\notin Z(s)
for some
i\in[2]
. Hence,

\Bigl|\bigl(K\sqcup\{k_{i}\}\bigr)\cap P\Bigr|\geqslant 3
. Thus,

•

|K\cap P|\geqslant 2
;

•

if
k_{i}\notin P
, then
|K\cap P|\geqslant 3
, i. e.
P\supset K
.

Note that
|M\setminus P|=n-|K\cup P|\geqslant 7-|K\cup P|=|K\cap P|\geqslant 2
. Therefore,
k_{1},k_{2}\notin P
,
P\supset K
.
So,
P=K\sqcup\{k_{0}\}
(
k_{0}\in M
,
k_{0}\neq k_{1},k_{2}
). Hence,
s_{1}s_{2}\in Z(s)
, a contradiction.

Thus, if
n\geqslant 7
, then
[c^{(K)},S]=\{e\}
and, therefore,
[c^{(K)},\Gamma_{n}^{4}]=\{e\}
.
∎

From now, assume that
n\geqslant 7
.

Due to Lemma
2
, the subgroup
H\subset\Gamma_{n}^{4}
generated by the involutions

c^{(K)}
(
K\in C_{[n]}^{3}
) is central; hence,
H\lhd\Gamma_{n}^{4}
and
H\cong(\mathbb{Z}_{2})^{p}
,
p\leqslant C_{n}^{3}
.
Denote by
\pi
the factoring homomorphism
\Gamma_{n}^{4}\twoheadrightarrow\Gamma_{n}^{4}/H
; write
\overline{x}
for
\pi(x)
.

Lemma 3
.

If
P,Q\in C_{[n]}^{4}
and
P\neq Q
, then
[\overline{S}_{P},\overline{S}_{Q}]=\{e\}\subset\Gamma_{n}^{4}/H
.

\square\quad
We have
[S_{P},S_{Q}]\subset H
since

[S_{P},S_{Q}]=\begin{cases}\{e\},&|P\cap Q|\leqslant 2;\\
\{c^{(P\cap Q)}\},&|P\cap Q|=3.\end{cases}\qed

Lemma 4
.

All elements
\overline{s}\in\Gamma_{n}^{4}/H

(
s\in S
)
pairwise commute.

\square\quad
By Lemma
3
, it suffices to prove that
[\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}},\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{4}}^{k_{3}k_{2}}]=e

for any pairwise distinct
k_{i}
. Consider the set
P\in C_{[n]}^{4}
of these
k_{i}
and an arbitrary number
k_{5}\notin P
.
For distinct
i^{\prime},i\in[4]
, set
r_{i^{\prime}i}:=\overline{s}_{k_{5}k_{i}}^{ll^{\prime}}
where
\{l,l^{\prime}\}=P\setminus\{k_{i},k_{i^{\prime}}\}

(so,
\{k_{5},k_{i},l,l^{\prime}\}=\bigl(P\sqcup\{k_{5}\}\bigr)\setminus\{k_{i^{\prime}}\}
). It follows from Lemma
3
that

[r_{i^{\prime}i},r_{j^{\prime}j}]=e
whenever
i^{\prime}\neq j^{\prime}
. By (
1
),
\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}}=r_{14}r_{21}r_{34}r_{41}
.
Replace
k_{2}
and
k_{4}
:
\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{4}}^{k_{3}k_{2}}=r_{12}r_{41}r_{32}r_{21}
.
Hence,
\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}}\cdot\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{4}}^{k_{3}k_{2}}=r_{14}r_{12}r_{34}r_{32}
.
Now replace
k_{1}
and
k_{3}
:
\overline{s}_{k_{3}k_{2}}^{k_{1}k_{4}}\cdot\overline{s}_{k_{3}k_{4}}^{k_{1}k_{2}}=r_{34}r_{32}r_{14}r_{12}
,
i. e.

\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{4}}^{k_{3}k_{2}}\cdot\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}}=r_{34}r_{32}r_{14}r_{12}=r_{14}r_{12}r_{34}r_{32}=\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{2}}^{k_{3}k_{4}}\cdot\overline{s}_{k_{1}k_{4}}^{k_{3}k_{2}}
.
∎

By Lemma
4
, the group
\Gamma_{n}^{4}/H
is generated by pairwise commuting involutions
\overline{s}
(
s\in S
);
hence,
\Gamma_{n}^{4}/H\cong(\mathbb{Z}_{2})^{q}
,
q\leqslant|S|\leqslant 3\cdot C_{n}^{4}
. In particular,
(\Gamma_{n}^{4}/H)^{\prime}=\{e\}
, i. e.
(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}\subset H
.
Since
c^{(K)}\in(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}
for each
K\in C_{[n]}^{3}
, then
H=(\Gamma_{n}^{4})^{\prime}
.

Thus, we completely proved Theorem
1
and, hence, Corollary
1
.
