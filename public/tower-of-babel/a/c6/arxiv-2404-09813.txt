Four center integrals for Coulomb interactions in small molecules
Garry Goldstein

Abstract
In this work we make some progress on studying four center integrals for the Coulomb energy for both Hartree Fock (HF) and Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations for small molecules. We consider basis wave functions of the form of an arbitrary radial wave function multiplied by a spherical harmonic and study four center Coulomb integrals for them. We reformulated these Coulomb four center integrals in terms of some derivatives of integrals of nearly factorable functions which then depend on the Bessel transform of the radial wave functions considered.

\displaystyle\times\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{b}J_{l_{b}}\left(\left[\mathbf{K}_{a}^{2}+\mathbf{K}^{2}-2\left|\mathbf{K}_{a}\right|\left|\mathbf{K}\right|u_{a}\right]^{1/2}r_{b}\right)R_{l_{b}}\left(r_{b}\right)r_{b}^{2}}{\left[\mathbf{K}_{a}^{2}+\mathbf{K}^{2}-2\left|\mathbf{K}_{a}\right|\left|\mathbf{K}\right|u_{a}\right]^{l_{b}/2}}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{d}J_{l_{d}}\left(\left[\mathbf{K}_{c}^{2}+\mathbf{K}^{2}-2\left|\mathbf{K}_{c}\right|\left|\mathbf{K}\right|u_{c}\right]^{1/2}r_{d}\right)R_{l_{d}}\left(r_{d}\right)r_{d}^{2}}{\left[\mathbf{K}_{c}^{2}+\mathbf{K}^{2}-2\left|\mathbf{K}_{b}\right|\left|\mathbf{K}\right|u_{c}\right]^{l_{d}/2}}

(20)

II.5
A global co-ordinate
change

Now we use the co-ordinates:

\displaystyle K_{a}

\displaystyle=\mathbf{K}_{a}

\displaystyle K_{c}

\displaystyle=\mathbf{K}_{c}

\displaystyle K

\displaystyle=\mathbf{K}

\displaystyle U_{a}

\displaystyle=\left[\mathbf{K}_{a}^{2}+\mathbf{K}^{2}-2\left|\mathbf{K}_{a}\right|\left|\mathbf{K}\right|u_{a}\right]^{1/2}

\displaystyle U_{c}

\displaystyle=\left[\mathbf{K}_{c}^{2}+\mathbf{K}^{2}-2\left|\mathbf{K}_{c}\right|\left|\mathbf{K}\right|u_{c}\right]^{1/2}

(21)

Now we have that the matrix of the transformation is upper triangular
so the determinant of the Jacobian is given by:

\det\left(\mathcal{J}\right)=\frac{K^{2}K_{a}K_{c}}{U_{a}U_{c}}

(22)

As such we have that:

\displaystyle I_{abcd}

\displaystyle=4\pi\left(-i\right)^{l_{b}+l_{d}}i{}^{l_{a}+l_{c}}\sum D_{abcd}^{m,n,p}\frac{\partial^{m_{x}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{a}^{x}}\frac{\partial^{m_{y}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{a}^{y}}\frac{\partial^{m_{z}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{a}^{z}}\frac{\partial^{n_{x}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{c}^{x}}\frac{\partial^{n_{y}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{c}^{y}}\frac{\partial^{n_{z}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{c}^{z}}\frac{\partial^{p_{x}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{b}^{x}}\frac{\partial^{p_{y}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{b}^{y}}\frac{\partial^{p_{z}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{b}^{z}}\times

\displaystyle\times\sum_{L_{a}M_{a}}\sum_{L_{c},M_{c}}\sum_{L,M}i^{L_{a}}Y_{L_{a}M_{a}}\left(\widehat{\mathbf{R}_{a}-\mathbf{\mathbf{R}}_{b}}\right)i^{L_{c}}Y_{L_{c}M_{c}}\left(\widehat{\mathbf{R}_{c}-\mathbf{\mathbf{R}}_{d}}\right)i^{L}Y_{LM}\left(\widehat{\mathbf{R}_{b}-\mathbf{\mathbf{R}}_{d}}\right)\times

\displaystyle\times\int\frac{1}{\left(2\pi\right)^{5}}\frac{U_{a}U_{c}}{K^{2}K_{a}K_{c}}\times J_{L_{a}}\left(K_{a}\left|\mathbf{R}_{a}-\mathbf{R}_{b}\right|\right)J_{L_{c}}\left(K_{c}\left|\mathbf{R}_{c}-\mathbf{R}_{d}\right|\right)J_{L}\left(K\left|\mathbf{R}_{b}-\mathbf{R}_{d}\right|\right)\times

\displaystyle\times\left(\frac{2}{\pi}\right)^{4}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{a}J_{l_{a}}\left(K_{a}r_{a}\right)R_{l_{a}}\left(r_{a}\right)r_{a}^{2}}{K_{a}^{l_{a}}}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{c}J_{l_{c}}\left(K_{c}r_{c}\right)R_{l_{c}}\left(r_{c}\right)r_{c}^{2}}{K_{c}^{l_{c}}}\times

\displaystyle\times\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{b}J_{l_{b}}\left(U_{a}r_{b}\right)R_{l_{b}}\left(r_{b}\right)r_{b}^{2}}{U_{a}^{l_{b}+2}}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{d}J_{l_{d}}\left(U_{c}r_{d}\right)R_{l_{d}}\left(r_{d}\right)r_{d}^{2}}{U_{c}^{l_{d}+2}}

(23)

This factorizes to:

\displaystyle I_{abcd}

\displaystyle=4\pi\left(-i\right)^{l_{b}+l_{d}}i{}^{l_{a}+l_{c}}\sum D_{abcd}^{m,n,p}\frac{\partial^{m_{x}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{a}^{x}}\frac{\partial^{m_{y}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{a}^{y}}\frac{\partial^{m_{z}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{a}^{z}}\frac{\partial^{n_{x}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{c}^{x}}\frac{\partial^{n_{y}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{c}^{y}}\frac{\partial^{n_{z}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{c}^{z}}\frac{\partial^{p_{x}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{b}^{x}}\frac{\partial^{p_{y}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{b}^{y}}\frac{\partial^{p_{z}}}{\partial\mathbf{R}_{b}^{z}}\times

\displaystyle\times\sum_{L_{a}M_{a}}\sum_{L_{c},M_{c}}\sum_{L,M}i^{L_{a}}Y_{L_{a}M_{a}}\left(\widehat{\mathbf{R}_{a}-\mathbf{\mathbf{R}}_{b}}\right)i^{L_{c}}Y_{L_{c}M_{c}}\left(\widehat{\mathbf{R}_{c}-\mathbf{\mathbf{R}}_{d}}\right)i^{L}Y_{LM}\left(\widehat{\mathbf{R}_{b}-\mathbf{\mathbf{R}}_{d}}\right)

\displaystyle\times\int\frac{dK_{a}}{4}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{a}J_{l_{a}}\left(K_{a}r_{a}\right)R_{l_{a}}\left(r_{a}\right)r_{a}^{2}}{K_{a}^{l_{a}+1}}J_{L_{a}}\left(K_{a}\left|\mathbf{R}_{a}-\mathbf{R}_{b}\right|\right)

\displaystyle\times\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dK_{c}}{4}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{c}J_{l_{c}}\left(K_{c}r_{c}\right)R_{l_{c}}\left(r_{c}\right)r_{c}^{2}}{K_{c}^{l_{c}+1}}J_{L_{c}}\left(K_{c}\left|\mathbf{R}_{c}-\mathbf{R}_{d}\right|\right)

\displaystyle\times\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dK}{2\pi}\frac{1}{K^{2}}J_{L}\left(K\left|\mathbf{R}_{b}-\mathbf{R}_{d}\right|\right)

\displaystyle\times\int_{\left|K_{a}-K\right|}^{K_{a}+K}\frac{dU_{a}}{4}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{b}J_{l_{b}}\left(U_{a}r_{b}\right)R_{l_{b}}\left(r_{b}\right)r_{b}^{2}}{U_{a}^{l_{b}+1}}

\displaystyle\times\int_{\left|K_{c}-K\right|}^{K_{c}+K}\frac{dU_{c}}{4}\frac{\int_{0}^{\infty}dr_{d}J_{l_{b}}\left(U_{c}r_{d}\right)R_{l_{d}}\left(r_{d}\right)r_{d}^{2}}{U_{c}^{l_{d}+1}}

(24)

This is our main result as it presents the Coulomb four center integrals
in a nearly factorable form.

In this work we have presented some formulas for four center integrals
