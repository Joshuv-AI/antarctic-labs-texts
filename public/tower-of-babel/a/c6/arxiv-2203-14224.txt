Tests of the Z c Z_{c} -like Laplace Sum Rule (LSR) results using FESR at NLO
R.M. Albuquerque, S. Narison, D. Rabetiarivony

Abstract
In this note, we use local duality Finite Energy Sum Rule (FESR) to test the validity of the Laplace sum rules (LSR) results truncated at the dimension-six condensates for the estimates of the masses and couplings of the Z c Z_{c} -like ground states in Ref. Zc by taking the example of the D ∗ ​ D D^{*}D molecule configuration. We confirm the existence of an eventual ( D ∗ ​ D ) 1 (D^{*}D)_{1} radial excitation with a mass around 5700 MeV and coupling of 197(25) keV to the current which may mask the eventual Z c ​ ( 4430 ) Z_{c}(4430) radial excitation candidate (named ( D ∗ ​ D ) 0 (D^{*}D)_{0} in Ref. Zc ) having a relatively small coupling f ( D ∗ ​ D ) 0 f_{(D^{*}D)_{0}} =46(56) keV. We add more explanations on the estimates in Ref. Zc from LSR and comment the results in Ref WANG .
