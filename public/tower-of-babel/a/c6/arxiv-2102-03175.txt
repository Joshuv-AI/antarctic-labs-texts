Studies of b-hadrons at LHCb
*

Abstract
A large data set collected at the LHCb experiment in proton-proton collisions during Runs 1 and 2 of the Large Hadron Collider has opened a unique possibility to study heavy beauty hadron states and to broaden knowledge of their spectroscopy and production. Recent results on searches for new excited b \mathrm{b} -hadron states, and studies of b \mathrm{b} -hadron production, are reviewed. In particular, the observation of new excited Ω b − {\Omega^{-}_{\mathrm{b}}} states, the observation of two new narrow Λ b ​ ( 6146 ) 0 {{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{b}}(6146)^{0}} and Λ b ​ ( 6152 ) 0 {{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{b}}(6152)^{0}} states, the observation of new Λ b ∗ ∗ 0 {{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{b}}^{**0}} state consistent with the Λ b ​ ( 2 ​ S ) 0 {{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{b}}(2\mathrm{S})^{0}} prediction, a precise measurement of the mass and width of Λ b ​ ( 5912 ) 0 {{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{b}}(5912)^{0}} and Λ b ​ ( 5920 ) 0 {{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{b}}(5920)^{0}} states, and a measurement of the B c − {{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{c}}^{-}} meson production fraction and B c − − B c + {{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{c}}^{-}}-{{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{c}}^{+}} production asymmetry in 7 and 13 TeV of proton-proton collisions are presented.

Introduction

Over the last few years numerous outstanding results have been obtained within the analyses of
\mathrm{b}
-hadrons
.
Among those there are observation of pentaquark and tetraquark resonances, observation of series of excited heavy baryons and many others.
Still, many of the conventional states remain unobserved, parameters of some of the known hadron states are poorly measured and on top of that there are a number of the states which do not fit into the conventional quarkonium spectrum.
Hence study of
\mathrm{b}
-hadrons
spectroscopy is of particular interest in the modern high energy physics.

The results described below are based on the data samples collected by the
LHCb
experiment in proton-proton (
\mathrm{p}\mathrm{p}
) collision at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) with centre-of-mass energies
\sqrt{\rm s}=7
and
8\,\text{\,Te\kern-1.00006ptV}
corresponding to a total integrated luminosity of
3\,\text{\,fb}^{-1}
(
LHC
Run 1) and with centre-of-mass energy
\sqrt{\rm s}=13\,\text{\,Te\kern-1.00006ptV}
corresponding to a total integrated luminosity of
6\,\text{\,fb}^{-1}
(
LHC
Run 2).

5
Conclusion

A significant contribution to the knowledge of beauty hadron spectroscopy is provided by the LHCb experiment.
In particular,
the new excited
{\Omega^{-}_{\mathrm{b}}}
and
{\Lambda_{\mathrm{b}}^{0}}
states are observed, mass and width of newly observed states are measured.
The production fraction of
{{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{c}}^{-}}
meson with respect to
{{\mathrm{B}}^{+}}
and
{\mathrm{B}^{0}}
mesons and
{{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{c}}^{-}}-{{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{c}}^{+}}
production asymmetry are measured in
\mathrm{p}\mathrm{p}
collisions at centre-of mass energies of 7 and 13
TeV
.
