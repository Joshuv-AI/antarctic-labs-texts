Dynamics of valence fluctuations in the extended periodic Anderson model
Pramod Kumar and N. S. Vidhyadhiraja

Abstract
We have investigated the valence transition in the extended periodic Anderson model (EPAM) within the framework of dynamical mean field theory. Previous theoretical studies have indicated the presence of this transition in parameter ranges that are experimentally inaccessible. In this work, we show that, inclusion of dynamics beyond static mean-field brings the valence transition in physically relevant regimes. We show the strong dependence of the quasiparticle-weight scale across the sharp valence crossover. The density of states also exhibit concomitant large scale spectral weight transfers, which can be observed in experiments.

Many rare earth inter-metallic compounds, which are also heavy fermions can be tuned easily to a quantum critical point by application of external perturbations like magnetic field and pressure

Model and formalism

The EPAM extends the usual PAM by considering an additional Coulomb interaction between localised electrons and conduction electrons. In the standard second quantised notation, the model may be represented by the following Hamiltonian:

\displaystyle\hat{H}=

\displaystyle\epsilon_{c}\sum_{i\sigma}c_{i\sigma}^{\dagger}c_{i\sigma}^{\phantom{{\dagger}}}-t\sum_{(ij),\sigma}c_{i\sigma}^{\dagger}c_{j\sigma}^{\phantom{{\dagger}}}+V\sum_{i\sigma}(f_{i\sigma}^{\dagger}c_{i\sigma}^{\phantom{{\dagger}}}+{\rm h.c.})

\displaystyle+\sum_{i\sigma}(\epsilon_{f}+\frac{U_{f}}{2}f_{i{\bar{\sigma}}}^{\dagger}f_{i{\bar{\sigma}}}^{\phantom{{\dagger}}})f_{i\sigma}^{\dagger}f_{i\sigma}^{\phantom{{\dagger}}}+U_{\scriptscriptstyle{fc}}\sum_{i\,\sigma\sigma^{\prime}}n_{i\sigma}^{c}n_{i\sigma^{\prime}}^{f}

(1)

The first two terms correspond to the site and kinetic energy of the conduction electrons.
The third term hybridises the local (
f
) and itinerant (
c
) electrons. The fourth term
intra-
f
-orbital Coulomb repulsion. The last term represents Coulomb repulsion of
c
- and
f
- electrons. Within DMFT
[
10
,
11
]
, which is exact in the limit of infinite dimensions
D
, the hopping
t_{ij}
must be rescaled as
t_{ij}\propto t_{*}/\sqrt{D}
. Decoupling the inter-orbital interaction term within Hartree approximation renormalizes
the conduction and localised electron site energy as
\tilde{\epsilon_{c}}=\epsilon_{c}+U_{\scriptscriptstyle{fc}}\langle n_{f}\rangle
and
\tilde{\epsilon_{f}}=\epsilon_{f}+U_{\scriptscriptstyle{fc}}\langle n_{c}\rangle
where
\langle n_{c}\rangle
and

are average occupancies for conduction and localised orbitals respectively. A point to note is that the occupancies,
\langle n_{c}\rangle
and

\langle n_{f}\rangle

full interacting Green’s function, and hence must be obtained self-consistently. In this
sense, the present approximation is equivalent to carrying out a skeleton expansion
of the
U_{\scriptscriptstyle{fc}}
term about the PAM upto the first order.

The retarded Green’s functions within DMFT for the localised and conduction electrons
are given respectively by
[
15
]

\displaystyle G^{f}(\omega)=\left[\omega^{+}-\tilde{\epsilon_{f}}-\Sigma(\omega)-\frac{V^{2}}{\omega^{+}-\tilde{\epsilon_{c}}-S(\omega)}\right]^{-1}

(2)

\displaystyle G^{c}(\omega)=\left[\omega^{+}-\tilde{\epsilon_{c}}-S(\omega)-\frac{V^{2}}{\omega^{+}-\tilde{\epsilon_{f}}-\Sigma(\omega)}\right]^{-1}

(3)

where
\Sigma(\omega)
is the momentum independent
f

functions are given by
D^{\alpha}(\omega)=-{\rm Im}G^{\alpha}(\omega)/\pi

and the occupancies are given by
n_{\alpha}=2\int^{0}_{-\infty}d\omega\,D^{\alpha}(\omega)
.

S(\omega)

is the Feenberg self-energy, that may be calculated self-consistently through following relations
[
15
]
)

\displaystyle G^{c}(\omega)=H[\gamma]=\int^{\infty}_{-\infty}\frac{\rho_{0}(\epsilon)}{\gamma(\omega)-\epsilon}=\frac{1}{\gamma(\omega)-S(\omega)}

(4)

where

The local

The PAM in various regimes like Kondo Lattice (KL) and mixed valent (MV) regime has also been studied using LMA. We refer the reader to our earlier works on LMA as applied
to the PAM for more details of the implementation

4
Conclusions

The extended periodic Anderson model has been proposed as one of the prime
candidate Hamiltonian for describing valence transitions and a new form
of quantum criticality in heavy fermion and mixed-valent systems. However,
the critical point found in theoretical investigations
lies in model parameter regimes that would not be physically realizable. We present some
preliminary investigations of the EPAM within DMFT using a highly accurate local moment approach as the impurity solver. Our results clearly suggest that the inclusion of
dynamical fluctuations beyond static mean field theories are crucial to making the
critical point
physically accessible. The low energy Kondo scale changes significantly across the valence
crossover, and the single-particle spectra also show concomitant large scale
weight transfers. These changes must be easily observable in angle
resolved photoemission spectroscopy experiments. Finite temperature investigations
are ongoing and will be important to resolve issues such as the temperature
dependence of the valence changes across the quantum critical point under
the second superconducting dome in CeCu
2
Si
2
.
