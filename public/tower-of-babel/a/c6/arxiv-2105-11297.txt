Abstract
. We say that a set of pairs of disjoint cycles Λ ⁡ ( G ) \Lambda(G) of a graph G G is linked if for any spatial embedding f f of G G there exists an element λ \lambda of Λ ⁡ ( G ) \Lambda(G) such that the 2 2 -component link f ⁡ ( λ ) f(\lambda) is nonsplittable, and also say minimally linked if none of its proper subsets are linked. In this paper, (1) we show that the set of all pairs of disjoint cycles of G G is minimally linked if and only if G G is essentially same as a graph in the Petersen family, and (2) for any two integers p , q ≥ 3 p,q\geq 3 , we exhibit a minimally linked set of Hamiltonian ( p , q ) (p,q) -pairs of cycles of the complete graph K p + q K_{p+q} with at most eighteen elements.
