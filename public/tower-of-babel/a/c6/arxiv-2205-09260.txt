Abstract
In this paper, we study the relationship of Gorenstein projective objects among three Abelian categories in a recollement. As an application, we introduce the relation of n n -Gorenstein tilting modules (and Gorenstein syzygy modules) in three Abelian categories. For a recollement R ⁡ ( 𝒜 ′ , 𝒜 , 𝒜 ′′ ) R(\mathscr{A}^{\prime},~\mathscr{A},~\mathscr{A}^{\prime\prime}) of Abelian categories, we show that a resolving subcategory in 𝒜 \mathscr{A} induce two resolving subcategories in 𝒜 ′ \mathscr{A}^{\prime} and 𝒜 ′′ \mathscr{A}^{\prime\prime} . On the other hand, we also prove that two resolving subcategories in 𝒜 ′ \mathscr{A}^{\prime} and 𝒜 ′′ \mathscr{A}^{\prime\prime} can induce a resolving subcategory in 𝒜 \mathscr{A} . Moreover, we give the size relationship between the relative global dimensions of three Abelian categories. 2000 Mathematics Subject Classification: 18A40 16E10 18G25 Keywords : Gorenstein projective objects, Recollement, n n -Gorenstein tilting modules, Resolving subcategories.
