Remarks on additive representations of natural numbers
Runbo Li

Abstract
. For two relatively prime square-free positive integers a a and b b , we study integers of the form a ​ p + b ​ P 2 ap+bP_{2} and give a new lower bound for it, where a ​ p ap and b ​ P 2 bP_{2} are both square-free, p p denotes a prime, and P 2 P_{2} has at most two prime factors. We also consider some special cases where p p is small, p p and P 2 P_{2} are within short intervals, p p and P 2 P_{2} are within arithmetical progressions and a Goldbach-type upper bound result. Our new results generalize and improve the previous results.
