Magnetic phase diagram in three-dimensional triangular-lattice antiferromagnet Sr 3 CoTa 2 O 9 with small easy-axis anisotropy Preprint: APS/123-QED
Iori Nishizawa 1, Nobuyuki Kurita 1, Hidekazu Tanaka 1,2, Takayuki Goto 3

Abstract
We report the results of low-temperature magnetization and specific heat measurements of Sr 3 CoTa 2 O 9 powder, in which Co 2+ ions with effective spin–1/2 form a uniform triangular lattice in the a ​ b ab plane. It was found that Sr 3 CoTa 2 O 9 undergoes successive antiferromagnetic transitions at T N1 = 0.97 ​ K T_{\rm N1}\,{=}\,0.97~{\rm K} and T N2 = 0.79 ​ K T_{\rm N2}\,{=}\,0.79~{\rm K} at zero magnetic field. As the magnetic field increases, both T N1 T_{\rm N1} and T N2 T_{\rm N2} decrease monotonically. The obtained magnetic field vs temperature phase diagram together with a sharp magnetization anomaly at a saturation field of μ 0 ​ H s = 2.3 {\mu_{0}}H_{\rm s}\,{=}\,2.3 T indicates that Sr 3 CoTa 2 O 9 is described as a spin–1/2 three-dimensional triangular-lattice antiferromagnet with a weak easy-axis anisotropy. We discuss the characteristics of the magnetic phase diagram, which approximates the phase diagram for the magnetic field perpendicular to the c c axis.
