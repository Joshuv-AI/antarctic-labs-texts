Abstract
We study the near horizon 2d gravity theory which captures the near extremal thermodynamics of BTZ with excess mass δ ​ M \delta M and excess angular momentum δ ​ J = ℒ ​ δ ​ M \delta J=\mathcal{L}\,\delta M over extremality. We find that the Jackiw-Teitelboim theory is able to capture such departures from extremality with an additional parameter ℒ \mathcal{L} relating it to the near extremal BTZ configuration. We are able to show this by obtaining new simultaneous near horizon near extremal limits of the BTZ geometry parametrized by ℒ \mathcal{L} . The resulting Jackiw-Teitelboim theory captures the near extremal thermodynamics of such BTZ geometries provided we identify the temperature T H ( 2 ) T^{(2)}_{H} of the A ​ d ​ S 2 AdS_{2} geometry in the Jackiw-Teitelboim theory to be T H ( 2 ) = T H / ( 1 − μ ​ ℒ ) T^{(2)}_{H}=T_{H}/(1-\mu\,\mathcal{L}) where T H T_{H} is the BTZ temperature and μ \mu its chemical potential with μ ​ ℒ < 1 \mu\,\mathcal{L}<1 .

Conclusions & Discussions

In this paper we see that the JT theory describing the near horizon dynamics of near extremal BTZ is more capable at describing the departures form extremality than previously explored in literature
i.e.
the constant angular momentum departures from extremality. This is made possible by writing the metric in terms of in-out going null geodesics with angular momentum per unit energy
\mathcal{L}
(
51
) first used in
Malvimat:2021itk
and then taking a simultaneous near extremal near horizon limit generalizing similar previously known limits
Gupta:2008ki
;
Sen:2008yk
. The thermodynamics of the JT theory obtained -which we refer to as JT
L
; about such a near horizon metric parametrized by
0\leq\mathcal{L}<1
is consistent with the near extremal thermodynamics of BTZ where the excess mass and angular momentum are related by
\Delta J=\mathcal{L}\,\Delta M
. We also verify that the behaviour of excess mass and entropy above extremality in the JT
L
theory matches that of the relevant near extremal BTZ configuration. Thus, we also find that the expected form of the first law of black hole thermodynamics is reproduced by the near horizon description. A crucial requirement for this match in the thermodynamics is to identify the near horizon
AdS_{2}
throat temperature as
T_{H}/(1-\mu\,\mathcal{L})
. As a consequence we see that a computation of the 4pt OTOC of 2d probe scalar fields along the lines of
Jensen:2016pah
;
Maldacena:2016upp
exhibits an exponential scrambling behaviour with a Lyapunov index
\lambda_{L}=2\pi T_{H}/(1-\mu\,\mathcal{L})
. We therefore are able to see partially the near horizon or the IR dynamics which gives rise to the sawtooth like behaviour in the OTOC in a state dual to rotating BTZ
Mezei:2019dfv
;
Craps:2020ahu
and the scrambling of mutual information controlled by
\lambda_{L}>2\pi T_{H}

Malvimat:2021itk
. This near horizon description also demonstrates how the generalized bound on the growth of chaos as derived in
Halder:2019ric
can be viewed holographically.
