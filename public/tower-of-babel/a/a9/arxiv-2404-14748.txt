LHC di-lepton searches for Z ′ Z^{\prime} bosons which explain measurements of b → s ​ l + ​ l − b\rightarrow sl^{+}l^{-} transitions Journal: Physics Letters B
Ben Allanach

Abstract
Several current measurements of b → s ​ l + ​ l − b\rightarrow sl^{+}l^{-} processes are in tension with Standard Model predictions, whereas others (e.g. R K R_{K} and R K ∗ R_{K^{\ast}} ) are in reasonable agreement with them. We examine some recent Z ′ Z^{\prime} models that fit the data as a whole appreciably better than does the Standard Model, confronting the models with ATLAS resonance searches in the e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} and μ + ​ μ − \mu^{+}\mu^{-} channels. Both channels constrain the models; we find that the searches rule out less than one fifth of the allowed range of M Z ′ M_{Z^{\prime}} . We estimate that the HL-LHC can roughly double the current sensitivity in M Z ′ M_{Z^{\prime}} , but covering the whole parameter space would require a more powerful machine, such as a 100 TeV p ​ p pp collider.
