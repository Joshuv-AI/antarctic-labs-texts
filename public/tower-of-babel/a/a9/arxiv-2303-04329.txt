Abstract
A recent letter Cai2022 within a phenomenological dark matter framework with a massive graviton in the external state argued that a divergence with increasing centre-of-momentum energy arising from the longitudinal polarizations of the graviton. In this letter we point out that in processes such as graviton-photon production from matter annihilation, f ​ f ¯ → G ​ γ f\bar{f}\to G\gamma , no such anomalous divergences occur at tree-level. This then applies to other tree-level amplitudes related by crossing symmetry such as γ ​ f → G ​ f \gamma f\to Gf , G ​ f → γ ​ f Gf\to\gamma f , γ ​ f ¯ → G ​ f ¯ \gamma\bar{f}\to G\bar{f} , f → f ​ G ​ γ f\to fG\gamma and so on. We show this by explicitly computing the relevant tree-level diagrams, where we find that delicate cancellations ensure that all anomalously growing terms are well-regulated. Effectively at tree-level this is consistent with the operation of a Ward identity associated with the external photon for such amplitudes. The same tree-level results apply if the photon is replaced by a gluon. These results have important consequences for model construction in areas including dark matter, gravity and high-energy physics.
