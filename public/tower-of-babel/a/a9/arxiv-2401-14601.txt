A stabilizer free weak Galerkin method with implicit θ \theta -schemes for fourth order parabolic problems
Shanshan Gu, Qilong Zhai

Abstract
In this paper, we combine the stabilizer free weak Galerkin (SFWG) method and the implicit θ \theta -schemes in time for θ ∈ [ 1 2 , 1 ] \theta\in[\frac{1}{2},1] to solve the fourth-order parabolic problem. In particular, when θ = 1 \theta=1 , the full-discrete scheme is first-order backward Euler and the scheme is second-order Crank Nicolson scheme if θ = 1 2 \theta=\frac{1}{2} . Next, we analyze the well-posedness of the schemes and deduce the optimal convergence orders of the error in the H 2 H^{2} and L 2 L^{2} norms. Finally, numerical examples confirm the theoretical results.

\displaystyle 2\tau\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|\left\|\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}\right\|+\tau^{\frac{5}{2}}\Big(\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{sss}\right\|^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left\|\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\tau}{\varepsilon}\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{4}4\tau\left\|\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\frac{\tau^{4}}{4}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{sss}\right\|^{2}ds+\frac{\varepsilon}{4}4\tau\left\|\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\tau}{\varepsilon}\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|^{2}+\frac{\tau^{4}}{4\varepsilon}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{sss}\right\|^{2}ds+2\varepsilon\tau\left\|\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2},

Next, similar to the proof for the case
\theta\in(\frac{1}{2},1]
, we can get (
4.43
).

For the equation

\displaystyle(\overline{\partial}\eta^{n},v_{0})+(\Delta_{w}(\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}),\Delta_{w}v)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-(\overline{\partial}\rho^{n},v_{0})-(\overline{\partial}u(t_{n})-(\theta u_{t}(t_{n})+(1-\theta)u_{t}(t_{n-1})),v_{0}),\qquad\forall v\in V_{h}^{0},

we set
v=\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}
and arrive at

\displaystyle(\overline{\partial}\eta^{n},\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1})+(\Delta_{w}(\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}),\Delta_{w}(\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-(\overline{\partial}\rho^{n},\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1})-(\overline{\partial}u(t_{n})-(\theta u_{t}(t_{n})+(1-\theta)u_{t}(t_{n-1})),\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}).

From
\theta\eta^{n}+(1-\theta)\eta^{n-1}=(\theta-\frac{1}{2})(\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1})+\frac{1}{2}(\eta^{n}+\eta^{n-1})
, the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, and the Young inequality, we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+(\theta-\frac{1}{2})|\!|\!|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|^{2}-|\!|\!|\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-(\overline{\partial}\rho^{n},\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1})-(\overline{\partial}u(t_{n})-(\theta u_{t}(t_{n})+(1-\theta)u_{t}(t_{n-1})),\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|+\left\|\overline{\partial}u(t_{n})-(\theta u_{t}(t_{n})+(1-\theta)u_{t}(t_{n-1}))\right\|\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{2\tau}{\varepsilon}\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{8\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+\frac{2\tau}{\varepsilon}\left\|\overline{\partial}u(t_{n})-(\theta u_{t}(t_{n})+(1-\theta)u_{t}(t_{n-1}))\right\|^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{8\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{2\tau}{\varepsilon}\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|^{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{4\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+\frac{2\tau}{\varepsilon}\left\|\overline{\partial}u(t_{n})-(\theta u_{t}(t_{n})+(1-\theta)u_{t}(t_{n-1}))\right\|^{2}.

When
\theta\in(\frac{1}{2},1]
, selecting
\varepsilon=\frac{1}{2}
and using (
4.21
), (
4.45
), we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+(\theta-\frac{1}{2})|\!|\!|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|^{2}-|\!|\!|\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 4\tau\left\|\overline{\partial}\rho^{n}\right\|^{2}+\frac{1}{8\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+4\tau^{2}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle Ch^{2(k+\alpha-2)}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds+\frac{1}{8\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+4\tau^{2}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds,

which show

\displaystyle\frac{7}{8\tau}\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}+(\theta-\frac{1}{2})|\!|\!|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|^{2}-|\!|\!|\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle Ch^{2(k+\alpha-2)}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds+4\tau^{2}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds.

With
\left\|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}\right\|^{2}\geq 0
and
|\!|\!|\eta^{n}-\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2}\geq 0
, we get

\displaystyle|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|^{2}\leq|\!|\!|\eta^{n-1}|\!|\!|^{2}+Ch^{2(k+\alpha-2)}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds+4\tau^{2}\int_{t_{n-1}}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds,

which implies

\displaystyle|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|^{2}\leq|\!|\!|\eta^{0}|\!|\!|^{2}+Ch^{2(k+\alpha-2)}\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds+4\tau^{2}\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds.

Since

\displaystyle|\!|\!|\eta^{0}|\!|\!|=|\!|\!|U^{0}-E_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|\leq|\!|\!|U^{0}-Q_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|+|\!|\!|Q_{h}u(0)-E_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|\leq|\!|\!|U^{0}-Q_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|+Ch^{k-1}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}},

we have

\displaystyle|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|\leq

\displaystyle~|\!|\!|U^{0}-Q_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|+Ch^{k-1}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}+Ch^{k+\alpha-2}\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle~+C\tau\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Combining the estimates
\rho^{n}
and
\eta^{n}
, we know

\displaystyle|\!|\!|U^{n}-Q_{h}u(t_{n})|\!|\!|\leq

\displaystyle|\!|\!|\rho^{n}|\!|\!|+|\!|\!|\eta^{n}|\!|\!|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|\!|\!|U^{0}-Q_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|+Ch^{k-1}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}+Ch^{k-1}\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle+Ch^{k+\alpha-2}\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}+C\tau\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|\!|\!|U^{0}-Q_{h}u(0)|\!|\!|+Ch^{k-1}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}+Ch^{k-1}\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{s}\right\|_{\max{\{k+1,4\}}}^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle~+C\tau\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{n}}\left\|u_{ss}\right\|^{2}ds\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

When
\theta=\frac{1}{2}
, using (
4.22
) and the same process, we can get (
4.44
). So, the theorem has been proved.

6
Concluding remarks and ongoing work

In this paper, we propose the semi-discrete numerical scheme and full-discrete numerical scheme for the
fourth order parabolic problem by the SFWG method and the implicit
\theta
-schemes where
\frac{1}{2}\leq\theta\leq 1
. Based on the schemes, we analyze the well-posedness and the convergence of the errors in
H^{2}
and
L^{2}
norms. Finally, we use numerical results to confirm the accuracy of the theoretical results.

In the future work, we are going to study other WG corresponding methods to deal with the fourth order parabolic equation and apply the WG methods combining the implicit
\theta
-schemes to solve other time-dependent partial differential equations.
