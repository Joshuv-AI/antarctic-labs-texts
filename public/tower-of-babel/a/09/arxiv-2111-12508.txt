3
internally enriched double categories

As oppose to the external enrichments that internalize the notion of category in the 2-category of
\mathbb{C}
-enriches category, we are trying to internalized the notion of
|\mathbb{C}|
-enriched category into the 2-category of categories.

Intuitively, a
\mathcal{C}
-enriched category is given by a set
\mathbb{V}
of objects, a function
\mathbb{E}:\mathbb{V}\times\mathbb{V}\rightarrow|\mathcal{C}|
, a unit
U_{X}:\mathcal{C}(\mathbb{1},\mathbb{E}(X,X))
and a composition
\odot_{x,y,z}:\mathcal{C}(\mathbb{E}(X,Y)\otimes\mathbb{E}(Y,Z),\mathbb{E}(X,Z))
, plus standard coherence diagrams.

The immediate generalization is to replace set by categories and functions by functors. However, such a notion is too restrictive due to the strictness of
\mathbb{E}
. Indeed, such a definition require
\mathbb{E}(id_{X},id_{Y})=id_{\mathbb{E}(X,Y)}
, which, intuitively, means that the globular horizontal category will be trivial. Similarly, the strictness on the composition do not make sense either.

However, one can use a lax functor for
\mathbb{E}
and resolve this issue. Additionally, since we are working with double categories, we know that lax functors make way more sense in the context of double categories rather that 2-categories, thus we will require
\mathcal{C}
to be a double category (that we call
\mathcal{D}
). Finally, having access to a new “kind” of morphisms in
\mathcal{D}
, we can distinguish those used as target of
\mathbb{E}
and those used for the unit
U
and composition
\odot
, allowing a richer framework.

,

•

the arrow category is the Grothendieck construction
\mathbb{A}=\int\mathbb{E}

\left|\int\mathbb{E}\right|:=\left\{(X,Y,u)\middle|X,Y\in|\mathbb{V}|,u\in\mathcal{V}(\mathbb{1},\mathbb{E}(X,Y))\right\},

\left(\int\mathbb{E}\right)((X,Y,u),(X^{\prime},Y^{\prime},v)):=\left\{(f,g,\phi)\middle|\begin{matrix}f\in\mathbb{V}(X,X^{\prime}),\ g\in\mathbb{V}(Y,Y^{\prime}),\\

Remarks :

•

For
(\mathrm{Set},\mathrm{Set})
-enriched double categories, the Grothendieck construction is known to give only framed categories, but this is not true for any enrichment, in particular, we will see that the Grothendieck construction over
(\mathtt{Span},\mathrm{Set})
-enriched double categories is an equivalence of category.

•

The transversal double category cannot be easily defined as we did not define enrichments where vertical category are enriched.

•

The diagonal and globular categories can be defined as (2-)categories by looking at the underlying non-enriched double category. But enriched versions are unknown.

\mathcal{D}

\mathcal{D}
