Effective-range expansion of the T c ​ c + T_{cc}^{+} state at the complex D ∗ ⁣ + ​ D 0 D^{*+}D^{0} threshold
Mikhail Mikhasenko

Abstract
Evaluation of the effective-range parameters for the T c ​ c + T_{cc}^{+} state in the LHCb model is examined. The finite width of D ∗ D^{*} leads to a shift of the expansion point into the complex plane to match analytical properties of the expanded amplitude. We perform an analytic continuation of the three-body scattering amplitude to the complex plane in a vicinity of the branch point and develop a robust procedure for computation of the expansion coefficients. The results yield a nearly-real scattering length, and two contributions to the the effective range which have not been accounted before.

The LHCb model of the

The construction is inspired by the model-independent approach based on three-body unitarity

Specifically, the

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}(s)=\frac{g^{2}}{m^{2}-s-g^{2}\Sigma(s)}\,.

\displaystyle\rho_{\text{tot}}=\rho_{\pi^{+}D^{0}D^{0}}+\rho_{\pi^{0}D^{+}D^{0}}+\rho_{\gamma D^{+}D^{0}}\,.

\displaystyle\Sigma(s)=\Sigma_{0}+\frac{s}{2\pi i}\int_{s_{\text{thr}}}^{\infty}\frac{i\rho_{\text{tot}}(s^{\prime})\,ds^{\prime}}{s^{\prime}(s^{\prime}-s-i0)}\,.

Appendix A
T_{cc}^{+}
amplitude

The LHCb model of the
T_{cc}^{+}
lineshape is build on the assumption of isoscalar, axial-vector quantum numbers of the state.
Three coupled channels
\pi^{+}D^{0}D^{0}
,
\pi^{0}D^{+}D^{0}
,
\gamma D^{+}D^{0}
are considered.
The decays of
T_{cc}^{+}
to these states proceed via the
D^{*}
resonances in two different particle pairs for all channels.
In the model, couplings of
T_{cc}^{+}
to
D^{*+}D^{0}
and
D^{*0}D^{+}
are equal by the absolute value and opposite by the sign.
The squared
T_{cc}^{+}
decay matrix element is cast to a bilinear form,

\displaystyle\mathfrak{M}_{f}^{2}(s,m_{12}^{2},m_{13}^{2})=\mathfrak{F}_{f}^{\dagger}\,\mathfrak{X}_{f}(s,m_{12}^{2},m_{13}^{2})\,\mathfrak{F}_{f}\,,

(23)

where
\mathfrak{F}
is a two-element vector of
D^{*}
amplitudes,

\mathfrak{X}(s,m_{12}^{2},m_{13}^{2})
is a two dimensional squared matrix of functions polynomial in mandelstam variables,

m_{12}^{2}
and
m_{13}^{2}
and
s
. Specifically,

\displaystyle\mathfrak{F}_{\pi^{+}D^{0}D^{0}}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}\mathfrak{F}^{+}(m_{12}^{2})\\
\mathfrak{F}^{+}(m_{13}^{2})\end{pmatrix}\,,

\displaystyle\mathfrak{F}_{\pi^{0}(\gamma)D^{+}D^{0}}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}\mathfrak{F}^{+}(m_{12}^{2})\\
\mathfrak{F}^{0}(m_{13}^{2})\end{pmatrix}\,,

where
D^{*}
propagators are paramertized by the relativistic Breit-Wigner amplitude:

\displaystyle\mathfrak{F}^{c}(m^{2})=\frac{1}{m_{D^{*c}}^{2}-m^{2}-im_{D^{*c}}\Gamma_{D^{*c}}}\,,

(24)

and
c
indicates the change,
c=+,0
. The PDG values
ParticleDataGroup:2020ssz
are used masses and widths of
D^{*+}
and
D^{*0}
.

The symmetric squared matrices
\mathfrak{X}
are expressed via the invariant functions,
A
,
B
,

The form of the elements of the

\displaystyle\left(\mathfrak{X}_{\pi^{+}D^{0}D^{0}}\right)_{11}=\left(\mathfrak{X}_{\pi^{0}D^{+}D^{0}}\right)_{11}

(25)

\displaystyle\left(\mathfrak{X}_{\pi^{+}D^{0}D^{0}}\right)_{22}=\left(\mathfrak{X}_{\pi^{0}D^{+}D^{0}}\right)_{22}

\displaystyle\left(\mathfrak{X}_{\pi^{+}D^{0}D^{0}}\right)_{12}=\left(\mathfrak{X}_{\pi^{0}D^{+}D^{0}}\right)_{12}

For
