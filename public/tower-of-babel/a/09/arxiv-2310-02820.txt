Abstract
Based on the Peccei-Quinn (PQ) assignment, the PQ charge operator in the 3-3-1 model with Cosmological Inflation is constructed in terms of diagonal generators T 3 T_{3} and T 8 T_{8} of the S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) L SU(3)_{L} subgroup. PQ charge of neutral scalars equal ± 2 \pm 2 , while for charged scalars, it vanishes. The couplings of axion with fermions are presented. To have correct kinetic term for the axion, the PQ scale f a f_{a} is to be VEV of the singlet scalar boson, namely, f a = v ϕ f_{a}=v_{\phi} . The photon couples to charged particles only, while in this model, the axion does not couple to charged scalar/gauge bosons. The point is worth emphasizing that the axion has doubly derivative coupling with scalar playing the role of inflaton. The new effects mainly happen in the energy region from 10 7 ​ GeV 10^{7}\,\penalty\ \mathrm{GeV} to 10 11 ​ GeV 10^{11}\,\penalty\ \mathrm{GeV} . The chiral effective Lagrangian as usually provides axion mass consistent with model-independent prediction.
