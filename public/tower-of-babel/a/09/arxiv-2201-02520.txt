Four Blocks Cycles C ⁡ ( k , 1 , 1 , 1 ) C(k,1,1,1) in Digraphs
Zahraa Mohsen 1,2

Abstract
A four blocks cycle C ⁡ ( k 1 , k 2 , k 3 , k 4 ) C(k_{1},k_{2},k_{3},k_{4}) is an oriented cycle formed by the union of four internally disjoint directed paths of lengths k 1 , k 2 , k 3 , k_{1},k_{2},k_{3}, and k 4 k_{4} respectively. El Mniny [ 2 ] proved that if D is a digraph having a spanning out-tree T with no subdivisions of C(k, 1, 1, 1), then the chromatic number of D is at most 8 3 ​ k 8^{3}k . In this paper, we will improve this bound to 18 ​ k 18k .

Introduction

Throughout this paper, all the graphs are considered to be simple, that is with no loops and no multiple edges. By giving an orientation to each edge of a graph
G
, we obtain an oriented graph called a digraph, denoted by
D
. Reciprocally, the graph obtained from a digraph
D
by ignoring the directions of its arcs is called the underlying graph of
D
, and denoted by
G(D)
. The chromatic number of a digraph
D
, denoted by
\chi(D)
, is the chromatic number of its underlying graph. In
[
3
]
, Cohen et al. proved that for any two digraphs
D_{1}
and
D_{2}
, we have
\chi(D_{1}\cup D_{2})\leq\chi(D_{1})\times\chi(D_{2})
.

Let
D
be a digraph. An out-tree
T
of
D
is a sub-digraph of
D
whose vertices are of in-degree 1 except for one vertex of in-degree 0, which is called the root of
T
.

Let
T
be a spanning out-tree of a digraph
D
rooted at
r
. For a vertex
x
of
D
, there is a unique
rx
-directed path in
T
, denoted by
T_{[r,x]}
. The level of
x
with respect to
T
, denoted by
l_{T}(x)
, is the length of this path. For a non-negative integer
i
, denote
L_{i}(T)
to be the set of all the vertices having a level
i
in
T
.

For
x\in V(T)
, the ancestors of
x
are the vertices that belong to
T_{[r,x]}
. For an ancestor
y
of
x
, we will write
y\leq_{T}x
and we will denote by
T_{[y,x]}
the directed path in
T
from
y
to
x
. For two vertices
x
and
y
of
T
, the least common ancestor,
z
, of
x
and
y
is the common ancestor of
x
and
y
having the highest level in
T
. Let
D^{\prime}
be a sub-digraph of a digraph
D
and
T
be a spanning out-tree of
D
. Let
x\in V(D^{\prime})
,
x
is said to be a minimal in
D^{\prime}
for
\leq_{T}
if
\forall v\in V(D^{\prime})
satisfying
v\leq_{T}x
, we have
x\leq_{T}v
. Moreover,
x
is said to be a maximal in
D^{\prime}
for
\leq_{T}
if
\forall v\in V(D^{\prime})
satisfying
x\leq_{T}v
, we have
v\leq_{T}x
.

An arc
(x,y)
of
D
is said to be forward (resp. backward) with respect to
T
if
l_{T}(x)<l_{T}(y)
(resp.
l_{T}(x)\geq l_{T}(y)
). For two adjacent vertices

and

the arc between

A maximal out-tree

of

is a spanning out-tree for which for any backward arc with respect to

, there exists a

-directed path in

. We can easily see that for a maximal out-tree

of a digraph

,

is stable in

for all

.

A digraph
D
is said to be strong if for any two vertices

and
v
of
D
, there is a directed path from
u
to
v
. One can easily notice that every strong digraph

has a spanning out-tree. In deed, suppose not and let

be an out-tree of

with maximal number of vertices. Then there exists a vertex

. Since

such that
(y,x)\in A(D)
. Let

, an out-tree of

with

, a contradiction.

In
[
2
]
, El Mniny proved that any digraph
D
that has a spanning out-tree admits a maximal out-tree. Consequently, every strong digraph admits a maximal out-tree.

Given an oriented path
P
(resp. otiented cycle
C
), a block is a maximal directed subpath of
P
(resp.
C
). We denote by

(resp.

) the oriented path (resp. oriented cycle) formed of n blocks of lengths
k_{1},k_{2},\cdots,
and

respectively. Moreover, for a block

of an oriented cycle

which is directed from
x
to
y
, we denote by

(resp.
y
) the source (resp. the sink) of
B
, and we write

as

. Moreover,
x
and

are called the ends of

. In general, for an oriented cycle

with s blocks, we will denote by
B_{i}
’s the blocks of
C
, such that
B_{2i}
has ends

and
y_{i}
for all

and

has ends

and

for all

.

A subdivision of a digraph

is a digraph

obtained by replacing each arc
(x,y)
of

xy
-directed path of length at least 1.

Let
k_{1},k_{2},k_{3},
and
k_{4}
be positive integers. Cohen et al.
[
3
]
proved that any strong digraph with no subdivisions of
C(1,1,1,1)
has chromatic number less than 24 . El Mniny
[
2
]
proved that for any

has a chromatic number at most

. In this paper, we are going to improve the bound established in
[
2
]
by proving that if D is a digraph having a spanning out-tree T with no subdivisions of C(k, 1, 1, 1), then the chromatic number of D is at most
18k
.

In our proof, we used for the first time in such investigations what we call a wheel in order to study the chromatic number of the digraphs. A wheel is a graph made up of a chordless cycle and a vertex adjacent to at least three vertices of the cycle, such vertex is called universal. In
[
1
]
, Thomassen et al. proved that every graph with no wheel as a subgraph is 3-colorable. This result allowed us to get lower bounds for the chromatic number of the studied digraph,
D
, by proving that some subdigraphs of
D
contain no wheels.

Theorem 2.1

Suppose to the contrary that

D_{i}^{1}
contains a wheel of cycle say

and a universal vertex

.

can’t be a cycle of 4 blocks or more, since else

, a contradiction.
Then

is a cycle of 2 blocks. Denote by

(resp.

) the vertex of

with in-degree (resp. out-degree) zero. We will reach a contradiction finding a subdivision of

and its neighbors. Denote by

,
x_{2}
,

three neighbors of

on

\notin
