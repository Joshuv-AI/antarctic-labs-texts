Four-thirds law of energy and magnetic helicity in electron and Hall magnetohydrodynamic fluids
Yanqing Wang, Otto Chkhetiani

Abstract
In this paper, by exploiting the feature of the Hall term, we establish some local version four-thirds laws for the dissipation rates of energy and magnetic helicity in both electron and Hall magnetohydrodynamic equations in the sense of Duchon-Robert type. New 4 / 3 4/3 laws for the dissipation rates of magnetic helicity in these systems are first observed and four-thirds law involving the dissipation rates of energy for the Hall magnetohydrodynamic equations generalizes the work of Galtier.

2
Four-thirds laws in electron magnetohydrodynamic system

This section is devoted to the study 4/3 laws for the dissipation rates of energy and magnetic helicity in
electron magnetohydrodynamic equations (
1.4
). Before we begin the proof, we recall some vector identities as follows,

\displaystyle

\displaystyle\nabla(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B})=\vec{A}\cdot\nabla\vec{B}+\vec{B}\cdot\nabla\vec{A}+\vec{A}\times\text{curl}\vec{B}+\vec{B}\times(\nabla\times\vec{A}),

(2.1)

\displaystyle\nabla\times(\vec{A}\times\vec{B})=\vec{A}\text{div}\vec{B}-\vec{B}\text{div}\vec{A}+\vec{B}\cdot\nabla\vec{A}-\vec{A}\cdot\nabla\vec{B},

\displaystyle\vec{A}\cdot(\nabla\times\vec{B})=\text{div}(\vec{B}\times\vec{A})+\vec{B}\cdot(\nabla\times\vec{A}),

which will be frequently used in this paper.
Combining this and the divgence-free condition
\mathrm{div}\,\vec{j}=0
, one obtains

\displaystyle b\cdot\nabla b=\frac{1}{2}\nabla|b|^{2}+\vec{j}\times b,

(2.2)

\displaystyle\nabla\times(\vec{j}\times b)=b\cdot\nabla\vec{j}-\vec{j}\cdot\nabla b,

which turns out that

\displaystyle\nabla\times[(\nabla\times b)\times b]=\nabla\times[\vec{j}\times b]=\text{div}(b\otimes\vec{j})-\text{div}(\vec{j}\otimes b),

(2.3)

\displaystyle\nabla\times[(\nabla\times b)\times b]=\nabla\times[\vec{j}\times b]=\nabla\times[\text{div}(b\otimes b)-\nabla\frac{1}{2}|b|^{2}]=\nabla\times[\text{div}(b\otimes b)].

(2.4)

Hence, we get two equivalent forms of EMDH equation (
1.4
)

\displaystyle b_{t}+\text{div}(b\otimes\vec{j})-\text{div}(\vec{j}\otimes b)=0,

(2.5)

\displaystyle b_{t}+\nabla\times[\text{div}(b\otimes b)]=0.

(2.6)

2.1
Exact relation of energy in the EMHD system

Proof of Theorem
1.1

b_{t}^{\varepsilon}+\text{div}(b\otimes\vec{j})^{\varepsilon}-\text{div}(\vec{j}\otimes b)^{\varepsilon}=0.

After multiplying the above equation by

2.5

\partial_{t}(bb^{\varepsilon})+\text{div}(b\otimes\vec{j})^{\varepsilon}b+\text{div}(b\otimes\vec{j})b^{\varepsilon}-\text{div}(\vec{j}\otimes b)^{\varepsilon}b-\text{div}(\vec{j}\otimes b)b^{\varepsilon}=0.

(2.7)

Likewise,

\partial_{t}(bb^{\varepsilon})+b\cdot\{\nabla\times[\text{div}(b\otimes b)]^{\varepsilon}\}+b^{\varepsilon}\cdot\{\nabla\times[\text{div}(b\otimes b)]\}=0.

(2.8)

With the help of identity
\eqref{VI}_{3}

\displaystyle-2\pi\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{3}\varphi^{\prime}(r)dr\lim_{\varepsilon\rightarrow 0}\int_{\partial B}\frac{\zeta\cdot[b(x+\zeta r\varepsilon)-b(x)][b(x+\zeta r\varepsilon)-b(x)]^{2}\frac{d\sigma(\zeta)}{4\pi}}{r\varepsilon}

\displaystyle-\frac{3}{2}S(b,b,b),

where the definition of

was used.

This achieves the proof of this theorem.
