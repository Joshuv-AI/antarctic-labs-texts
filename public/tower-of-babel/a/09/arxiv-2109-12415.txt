. Let M M be a closed, oriented, simply connected 6-manifold. Introduction

It is natural to attempt to decompose a given topological space

is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of spheres, Moore spaces, and

In this paper we study the homotopy types of the suspensions of certain 6-manifolds after localization away from 2 and give splitting results analogous to those above. All our manifolds are topological. For those interested in smooth manifolds we note that the PL and smooth categories coincide in dimension 6, so that a closed 6-manifold

vanishes. Moreover, two smooth 6-manifolds are diffeomorphic if and only if they are homeomorphic

(although there are smooth 6-manifolds which are tangentially homotopy equivalent but not homeomorphic).

A homotopical test for a null homotopy

From this point onwards

Lemma 2.1
.

For

be an

-connected space. If

Proof.

The lemma follows from the Hurewicz Theorem.

Lemma 2.2

For

\pi_{n}(P^{n}(p^{r})),\pi_{n}(P^{n-1}(p^{r}))

There is an exact sequence

Theorem 5.11
.

Let
M
be a closed, orientable, simply connected 6-manifold with homology as in (
1
), and let
P
be a principal
SU(n)
-bundle over
M
with
c_{2}(P)=(0,\ldots,0)
and
c_{3}(P)=l
. Suppose
H_{*}(M)
has no torsion. If
\mathcal{P}^{1}:H^{2}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)\to H^{6}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)
is trivial, then

\textstyle\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(M,SU(n))\simeq_{(\frac{1}{2})}\mathcal{G}_{l}(S^{6},SU(n))\times\prod^{b}_{i=1}(\Omega^{4}G\times\Omega^{2}SU(n))\times\prod^{2d}_{k=1}\Omega^{3}SU(n).

If
\mathcal{P}^{1}:H^{2}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)\to H^{6}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)
is non-trivial, then

\textstyle\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(M,SU(n))\simeq_{(\frac{1}{2})}\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(C^{\prime},SU(n))\times\prod^{b-1}_{i=1}(\Omega^{4}G\times\Omega^{2}SU(n))\times\prod^{2d}_{k=1}\Omega^{3}SU(n),

where
C^{\prime}
is a CW-complex with one 2-cell, one 4-cell and one 6-cell.

Suppose the torsion group
T\cong\mathbb{Z}/m
and
\mathcal{P}^{1}:H^{2}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)\to H^{6}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)
is trivial. Then

\displaystyle\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(M,SU(n))

\displaystyle\simeq_{(\frac{1}{2})}

\displaystyle\textstyle\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(C^{\prime\prime},SU(n))\times\prod^{b-1}_{i=1}\Omega^{4}G\times\prod^{b}_{i=1}\Omega^{2}SU(n)\times

\displaystyle\Omega^{3}\{m\}SU(n)\times\textstyle\prod^{2d}_{k=1}\Omega^{3}SU(n)

where
C^{\prime\prime}
is a CW-complex with one 6-cell and 5-skeleton

.

Proof.

Suppose
H_{*}(M)
has no torsion and
\mathcal{P}^{1}:H^{2}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)\to H^{6}(M;\mathbb{Z}/3)
is trivial. By Theorem
5.7
we have
\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(M,SU(n))\simeq_{(\frac{1}{2})}\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(C_{\jmath},SU(n))\times\prod^{b}_{i=1}\Omega^{2}SU(n)\times\prod^{2d}_{k=1}\Omega^{3}SU(n)

\textstyle\mathcal{G}_{\vec{0},l}(C_{\jmath})\simeq\mathcal{G}_{l}(S^{6})\times\prod^{b}_{i=1}\Omega^{4}SU(n).
