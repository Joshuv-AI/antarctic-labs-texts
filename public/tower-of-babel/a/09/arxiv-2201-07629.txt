Comment on “Explicit Analytical Solution for Random Close Packing in d = 2 d=2 and d = 3 d=3 ” Preprint: APS/123-QED
Patrick Charbonneau, Peter K. Morse

Abstract




A recent letter proposes a first-principle computation of the random close packing (RCP) density in spatial dimensions
d=2
and
d=3

Zaccone 2022
. This problem has a long history of such proposals
Williams 1998
;
Jalali and Li 2004
;
Kamien and Liu 2007
;
Song et al. 2008
;
Jin et al. 2010
, but none capture the full picture. Reference
Zaccone 2022
, in particular, once generalized to all
d
fails to describe the known behavior of jammed systems in
d>4
, thus suggesting that the low-dimensional agreement is largely fortuitous.

The crux of the proposed scheme is to evaluate the radial distribution function
g(r,\widehat{\varphi})
at interparticle contact
r=\sigma^{+}
for a (scaled) volume fraction
{\widehat{\varphi}=2^{d}\varphi/d}

Parisi and Zamponi 2010
, and to construct an invariant
{g_{0}=z\sigma/[d^{2}\widehat{\varphi}g(\sigma^{+},\widehat{\varphi})]}
for
z
kissing contacts. Given the functional form
g(r,\widehat{\varphi})
, then
\widehat{\varphi}
and
z
can be evaluated at a known point to obtain an implicit expression,
\widehat{\varphi}(z)
, that holds for other packings. The scheme uses crystal close packings (CCP), for which
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{CCP}}
and
z_{\mathrm{CCP}}
are known, as reference. It is then possible to solve for the marginal-stability condition,
z_{\mathrm{RCP}}=2d
, which holds at jamming, to determine
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{RCP}}
. The only caveat identified in Ref.
Zaccone 2022
is that one must choose a liquid structure expression that is defined from RCP to CCP. The two options suggested—the Carnahan-Starling (CS) equation of state
Song et al. 1989
;
Charbonneau et al. 2011
and the Percus-Yevick (PY) closure relation—both fairly accurately describe the
d=3
liquid structure. (Another caveat is that these liquid state descriptions do not capture singular features of
g(r,\widehat{\varphi})
at RCP
Charbonneau et al. 2017
.)

Because PY fails to describe essential contact features of
g(r,\widehat{\varphi})
in
d>3
liquids
Charbonneau et al. 2021a
, the dimensional generalization here focuses on the CS form. Its one parameter,
A(d)
, is known numerically for
d=2
-
13
, and asymptotically scales as
A(d)\sim 3^{(d+1)/2}\sqrt{2/(d\pi)}

Charbonneau et al. 2021a
. Equating the invariant in both the RCP and crystalline phases yields

\frac{\varphi_{\mathrm{CCP}}}{z_{\mathrm{CCP}}}\frac{1+A(d)\varphi_{\mathrm{CCP}}}{(1-\varphi_{\mathrm{CCP}})^{d}}=\frac{\varphi_{\mathrm{RCP}}}{2d}\frac{1+A(d)\varphi_{\mathrm{RCP}}}{(1-\varphi_{\mathrm{RCP}})^{d}},

(1)

from which
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{RCP}}
can be straightforwardly obtained in
d=2-10
using CCP properties
Conway and Sloane 1998
;
Charbonneau et al. 2021b
. Because the method is independent of the crystal chosen, we also consider
D_{d}
(generalized FCC) lattices, with
{\widehat{\varphi}=2^{d/2-1}\pi^{d/2}/[d\Gamma(d/2+1)]}
and
{z=2d(d-1)}

Conway and Sloane 1998
, to obtain the high
d
asymptotic scaling
{\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{RCP}}\sim(2\sqrt{\pi})^{d-1}e^{d/2}d^{-(5+d)/2}}\rightarrow 0
. Comparing either of these results with RCP determined using a variety of numerical methods
Skoge et al. 2006
;
Charbonneau et al. 2011
;
Morse and Corwin 2014
;
Charbonneau and Morse 2021
shows a marked discrepancy for
d>4
(Fig.
1
). In principle,
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{RCP}}
should trend as these results, but its scaling is nowhere near them. The quantitative proximity in
d=2
-
4
must therefore be considered accidental.

\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{th}}^{\mathrm{PY}}

(black), which converges to the
d\rightarrow\infty
prediction

. Note that the densest crystal density,
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{CCP}}

(turquoise), grows increasingly distant from jamming with
d
.

The key weakness of the approach of Ref.
Zaccone 2022
is that it uses liquid state forms outside of their domain of validity. CS and PY capture the equilibrated liquid branch, but not the crystal branch ending at
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{CCP}}
nor the out-of-equilibrium fluid branch terminating at
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{RCP}}
. (See Refs.
Mangeat and Zamponi 2016
;
Parisi and Zamponi 2010
for a careful discussion.) In low
d
, the relative proximity between
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{RCP}}
and
\widehat{\varphi}_{\mathrm{CCP}}
results in relatively small deviations from either form, but as
d
increases these quantities separate significantly, thus revealing more pointedly the physical inadequacy of the proposed scheme. By contrast, the approach of Ref.
Mangeat and Zamponi 2016
, which treats metastability more carefully, properly captures the dimensional trend, and could in principle be quantitatively improved to systematically close the gap with low-dimensional experiments and simulations.
