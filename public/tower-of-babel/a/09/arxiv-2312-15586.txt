Abstract
. We first introduce the notion of C ​ M CM - τ \tau -tilting free algebras as the generalization of C ​ M CM -free algebras and show the homological properties of C ​ M CM - τ \tau -tilting free algebras. Then we give a bijection between Gorenstein projective τ \tau -rigid modules and certain modules by using an equivalence established by Kong and Zhang. Finally, we give a partial answer to Tachikawa’s first conjecture by using Gorenstein projective τ \tau -rigid modules.

It is obvious that CM-free algebras are CM-

Let

is not CM-

-tilting free. Then there is a indecomposable Gorenstein projective

\mathsf{mod}\hskip 0.72229pt

Corollary 3.11

Let
\Lambda
be an algebra. Then
\Lambda
is Gorenstein CM-

5.
Tachikawa’s Conjecture

In this section, we try to characterize self-injective algebras in terms of Gorenstein projective
\tau
-rigid modules. As a result, we can give a partial answer to Tachikawa’s first conjecture.

Recall from

that a module

\mathsf{mod}\hskip 0.72229pt
is called

if
{\rm Ext}_{\Lambda}^{i}(M,\Lambda)=0
for any
i\geq 1

\Lambda

\mathsf{mod}\hskip 0.72229pt

if
{\rm Ext}_{\Lambda}^{i}(\mathbb{D}\Lambda,N)=0
for

\Lambda

\mathbb{D}\Lambda

\Lambda
is self-injective.

To show the main result in this section, we need the following result in
[

Proposition 5.1
.

Let
\Lambda

(1)

\Lambda
is a
1
-Gorenstein algebra.

(2)

\mathbb{D}\Lambda
is
\tau
-rigid

(3)

\Lambda
is
\tau^{-1}
-rigid.

Note that a self-injective algebra should be a

-Gorenstein algebra, by Proposition
5.1
it is natural to study Tachikawa’s first conjecture in terms of
\tau
-rigid modules.
In the following we give our main result in this section which is a partial answer to Tachikawa’s first conjecture.

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
\Lambda

(1)

\Lambda
is self-injective.

(2)

\tau

(3)

Every cotilting module is semi-Gorenstein projective
\tau
-rigid.

(4)

\mathbb{D}\Lambda
is Gorenstein projective.

(5)

Every coltilting module is Gorenstein projective.

(6)

\Lambda
is semi-Gorenstein-injective
\tau^{-1}
-rigid.

(7)

Every tilting module is semi-Gorenstein injective
\tau^{-1}
-rigid.

(8)

\Lambda
is Gorenstein-injective.

(9)

Every tilting module is Gorenstein-injective.

(a) Since
(1)\Leftrightarrow(4)
is from the definitions of Gorenstein projective modules and self-injective algebras, we only show
(1)\Leftrightarrow(2)
,
(2)\Leftrightarrow(3)
, and
(4)\Leftrightarrow(5)

(1)\Rightarrow(2)
Since
\Lambda
is self-injective, then
\mathbb{D}\Lambda
is projective and hence semi-Gorenstein projective and
\tau
-rigid.

(2)\Rightarrow(1)
Since
\mathbb{D}\Lambda
is
\tau
-rigid and
|\mathbb{D}\Lambda|=|\Lambda|
, one gets that
\mathbb{D}\Lambda
