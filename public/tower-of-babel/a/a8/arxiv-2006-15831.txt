The Injective Dimension Of The Quotient Category
Mod(A)/\mathcal{M}_{0}

In this section, we prove that over an independent S-ring of Krull type, the injective dimension
of the quotient category
Mod(A)/\mathcal{M}_{0}
is exactly equal to
\sup_{\omega\in\Omega^{t}}inj.dim(\Re_{\omega})
.

We note that:

-

The thick subcategory
\mathcal{M}_{0}
of
Mod(A)
is localizing, since for
all
A
-module
M
: among the subobjects of
M
belonging to
\mathcal{M}_{0}
there is a maximal one (see Corollary 1, p. 375 of
[
2
]
).

-

Mod(A)/\mathcal{M}_{0}
is a category with injective
envelopes, and every injective object of this category is
isomorphic to an object
TI
where
I
is a codivisorial injective

A
-module (see Corollary 2, p. 375 of
[
2
]
).

-

If
A
is a S-ring of Krull type, then
TJ
is an
injective object of
Mod(A)/\mathcal{M}_{0}
for every injective

A
-module
J
(see Corollary 3, p. 375 of
[
2
]
).

We begin with a proposition.

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
A
be a ring of Krull type,
\Omega
a defining family for
A
,
M,N
two any

A
-modules, and let
f
be any
A
-homomorphism from
M
to
N
.
Then:

1)

Suppose that

f_{\omega}:M_{\omega}\longrightarrow N_{\omega}
is an essential
extension of
M_{\omega}
for all
\omega\in\Omega
. Then the
homomorphism

\overline{f}:M/M^{c}\longrightarrow N/N^{c}

\overline{m}\longrightarrow\overline{f(m)}

is an
essential extension of
M/M^{c}
.

2)

Conversely, suppose that
f
is an essential extension of
M
. Then

f_{\omega}:M_{\omega}\longrightarrow N_{\omega}
is an essential
extension of
M_{\omega}
for all
\omega\in\Omega^{t}
, in the
following cases:

(i)
M
is codivisorial and
A
is
an independent ring of Krull type.

(ii)
A
is an
independent S-ring of Krull type.

Proof.

1)

It is
easily seen that
f(M^{c})\subset N^{c}
, and then
\overline{f}

is well defined, but since
f_{\omega}
is injective for all

\omega\in\Omega
, it follows easily that
\overline{f}
is also
injective. On the other hand, let
\overline{H}
be a submodule of

N/N^{c}
(
N^{c}\subset H
) such that
\overline{H}\cap\overline{f}(M/M^{c})=0
. Then
\overline{H}_{\omega}\cap(\overline{f}(M/M^{c}))_{\omega}=0
, but since
N^{c}_{\omega}=0
, it follows that
H_{\omega}\cap f(M)_{\omega}=0
, and then

H_{\omega}=0
for all
\omega\in\Omega
; i.e.
H\in\mathcal{M}_{0}
. Therefore
H\subset N^{c}
. Thus
\overline{H}=0
. This implies that
\overline{f}
is an essential extension of

M/M^{c}
.

2)

The two statements follow easily from
[
7
]
Proposition 1.9 and from Proposition
1.8
, respectively.

∎

.

Let

be an independent S-ring of Krull type. If
M
is
an
\Re_{\omega}
-module for some
\omega\in\Omega^{t}
, then
inj.dim_{\Re_{\omega}}(M)=inj.dim_{A}(M)
.

Proof.

Follows immediately from Proposition
1.8
.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
A
be a ring of Krull type, and let
M
be any

A
-module. Then

inj.dim_{A}(\oplus_{\omega\in\Omega}M_{\omega})\leq\sup_{\omega\in\Omega}gl.dim(\Re_{\omega}).

Proof.

The assertion is trivial if
\sup_{\omega\in\Omega}gl.dim(\Re_{\omega})=\infty
. Suppose that
\sup_{\omega\in\Omega}gl.dim(\Re_{\omega})=n
, then by considering
M_{\omega}
as an
\Re_{\omega}
-module, we can find an exact sequence

0\longrightarrow M_{\omega}\longrightarrow(E_{0})_{\omega}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow(E_{n})_{\omega}\longrightarrow 0

where
(E_{i})_{\omega}
is an injective
\Re_{\omega}
-module for all
i\in\{1,...,n\}
. But since
\Re_{\omega}
is a quotient ring of
A
, we have

(E_{i})_{\omega}
is an injective
A
-module for all

, and then
\oplus_{\omega\in\Omega}(E_{i})_{\omega}

is an injective
A
-module (see
[
6
]
, Corollary 2.9.).
Hence, the assertion follows from the fact that the sequence

0\longrightarrow\oplus_{\omega\in\Omega}M_{\omega}\longrightarrow\oplus_{\omega\in\Omega}(E_{0})_{\omega}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow\oplus_{\omega\in\Omega}(E_{n})_{\omega}\longrightarrow 0

∎

Proposition 2.4

Let
A
be a ring of Krull type, and

\Re_{\omega}

If

1)

If

Let

0\longrightarrow TM\longrightarrow C_{0}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow C_{m}\longrightarrow 0

an injective resolution of

Trivial.

Theorem 2.5

Let

be an independent S-ring of Krull type. Then

, let
M

A

2), (ii),
