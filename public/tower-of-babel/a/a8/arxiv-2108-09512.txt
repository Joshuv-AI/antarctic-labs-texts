Thermodynamic analysis of magnetic phase transitions in the ferromagnetic superconductor UGe 2 at ambient pressure


Abstract
We study the possibility to apply phenomenological approach to the description of magnetic transitions in UGe 2 at ambient pressure with the help of Landau free energy expanded to 8 t ​ h 8^{th} order in magnetisation. The analysis shows that for certain values of parameters in front of M 4 M^{4} , and M 6 M^{6} terms in the free energy there is possibility for the appearance of two successive phase transitions between the low-magnetisation and high-magnetisation phases with the same magnetic structure. We establish the relation of the parameters in Landau energy, for which the phase transition from the disordered to low-magnetisation phase is of second order as the experimental data shows. We have also found the relations between the Landau coefficients which result in transition of crossover type between the low- and high-magnetisation phases when temperature is lowered.

4
Concluding remarks

In this paper we apply the phenomenological Landau approach to UGe
2
for the description of magnetic phase transitions at ambient pressure far from superconducting transition. The aim is to find a model, by which the occurrence of two different magnetic phases with same magnetic structure but different magnetisations can be described. We propose expansion of Landau free energy in magnetisation up to
M^{8}
, for which by general theory it has been proven that for particular values of Landau parameters, there is a possibility for isostructural transition to occur,
[
18
]
.
[
19
]
,
[
20
]
. We found the relations between the parameters of Landau energy for which there is possibility of second order phase transition to low-magnetization phase with subsequent transition of crossover type at temperature lowering to high-magnetization phase with the same magnetic structure. As shown there is strong dependence between the magnitudes of parameters
u
and
w
, see Eq. (
6
), and obviously for the appearance of isostructural transition from low- to high-magnetisation phase
w<0,u>0
and
u=w^{2}/3

The nature of magnetic phase transitions from experimental point of view at

Here we propose a general thermodynamic approach which does not depend on the undelying microscopic theories but we consider that at this first stage of our study to make direct comparison with experiment is preliminary. As far as it is generally accepted that the spin fluctuations are responsible for occurrence of superconductivity of p-type, it will be of great interest also to consider their role on magnetic transitions within this phenomenological approach. Recent experiments at ambient pressure
