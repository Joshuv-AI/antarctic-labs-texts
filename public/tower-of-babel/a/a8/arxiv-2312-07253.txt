Geometric invariants of K ​ 3 K3 surfaces with purely non-symplectic automorphisms
Dominik Burek

Abstract
. We give new relations between geometric invariants of K ​ 3 K3 surfaces with purely non-symplectic automorphisms of order 4 and 6. Our approach is based on a comparison of two methods of computation of formulas for the Euler characteristic of higher dimensional generalized Borcea-Vosin Calabi-Yau manifolds constructed in [ Bur21 ] .

Primary 14J32; Secondary 14J40, 14E15

1.
Introduction

Automorphisms of a
K3
surfaces over
\mathbb{C}
were intensively studied in the recent years. Especially important are automorphisms of order
d
which acts on the period as a multiplication by a primitive root of unity of degree
d
i.e.
(purely) non-symplectic automorphisms of order
d
.

Non-symplectic automorphisms have been investigated in several papers. A non-symplectic automorphisms of prime order have been classified by M. Artebani, A. Sarti, S. Taki in
[
AST11
]
. Some authors have started to investigate non-symplectic automorphisms of composite order: D. Al Tabbaa and A. Sarti in
[
TS17
]
(order 8), J. Dillies in
[
Dil12
]
(order 6), A. Sarti and A. Garbagnati in
[
GS13
]
(order
2p,
where
p
is a prime) and J. Keum in
[
Keu15
]
(order 66). Also, S. Brandhorst in
[
Bra19
]
pursued the question when an automorphism determines a
K3
surface up to an isomorphism.

The study of purely non-symplectic automorphism on
K3
surfaces involves, among other things, the description of the fixed points locus of this automorphism, this set is a disjoint union of finitely many isolated points and curves. This leads to several very important numerical invariants. Some of them are related i.e. topological or holomorphic Leftchetz’s formulas give precise relation.

K3
surfaces with purely non-symplectic automorphism have been used in several constructions of Calabi–Yau 3-folds. One of them was the construction given independently by C. Borcea (
[
Bor97
]
) and C. Voisin (
[
Voi93
]
). They constructed families of Calabi-Yau 3-folds using a non-symplectic involutions of
K3
surfaces and elliptic curves. A. Cattaneo and A. Garbagnati in
[
CG16
]
observed that the Borcea-Voisin construction can be generalized by allowing non-symplectic automorphism of orders
3,

4
or
6.
On the other hand S. Cynk and K. Hulek in
[
CH07
]
studied where one can extend the classical construction of Borcea-Voisin as far as taking the product of elliptic curves and resolving the quotient in a crepant way.

In
[
Bur21
]
we investigated higher dimensional construction of Calabi-Yau manifolds which generalizes the classical Borcea-Vosin construction by merging ideas of Cynk-Hulek and Cattaneo-Garbagnati. More precisely: Let
E_{d}
be an elliptic curve with a not period preserving automorphism of order
d\in\{2,3,4,6\}
and
S_{d}
be a
K3
surface with purely non-symplectic automorphism of order
d.
Consider the following group

G_{d,n}:=\{(m_{1},m_{2},\ldots,m_{n})\in\mathbb{Z}_{d}^{n}\colon m_{1}+m_{2}+\ldots+m_{n}=0\}\simeq\mathbb{Z}_{d}^{n-1}

acting naturally on
S_{d}\times E_{d}^{n-1}
. Then the quotient variety
(S_{d}\times E_{d}^{n-1})/G_{d,n}
admits a crepant resolution of singularities
Y_{d,n}
. Consequently
Y_{d,n}
is an
(n+1)
-dimensional Calabi-Yau variety.

In this note we shall derive new relations among invariants of
K3
surfaces with purely non-symplectic automorphisms of order 4 and 6. Our approach is based on varieties
Y_{d,n}
constructed in
[
Bur21
]
. We shall compute Euler characteristic of
Y_{d,n}
in two different ways: by using Hodge numbers of
Y_{d,n}
computed in
[
Bur21
]
and by using orbifold Eulera Characteristic. Since our approach is indirect, the relations have no clear geometrical origin.

The paper is organised as follows. In the sections 2 we review ”stringy” Euler characteristic formula and both topological and holomorphic Lefschetz’s numbers. In the section 3 we recall classical Borcea-Voisin construction and its generalizations. The section 4 is devoted to collect invariant of
K3
surfaces with purely non-symplectic automorphisms. In the section 5 we shall compare two methods of computation of formulas for the Euler characteristic of
Y_{d,n}.

2.2.
Topological and holomorphic Lefchetz’s numbers

In the following section we recall basic information about topological and holomorphic Lefschetz numbers. We refer to
[
GH94
]
and
[
Pet86
]
.

Let
X
be a compact oriented manifold and
f\colon X\to X
any continuous map. The intersection number of the graph
\alpha_{f}
of
f
and diagonal
\Delta_{X}
in
X\times X
is equal to the so called
topological Lefschetz number
given by the following formula:

\mathcal{L}_{\operatorname{top}}(f):=\sum_{q\geq 0}(-1)^{q}\operatorname{tr}\left(f^{*}\colon H^{q}(X)\to H^{q}(X)\right).

2.2 Theorem

(
[
Uen76
]
)
.

Let
X
be a compact complex manifold and
f\colon X\to X
a holomorphic map with non-degenerate fixed locus then

(2.3)

\mathcal{L}_{\operatorname{top}}(f)=\sum_{C\in\operatorname{Fix}(f)}e(C),

where the sum is taken over connected components of
\operatorname{Fix}(f).

Formula
2.3
holds in particular when
f^{n}=\operatorname{id}
for some
n\geq 1
(see
[
Car57
]
).

The map
f
acts not only on the de Rham cohomology of
X
but on the Dolbeault cohomology too. Therefore there is a hope that the action of
f
on
H^{*,*}(X)
will be reflected in local properties of
f
around the fixed point locus.

2.3 Definition
.

Let
X
be a compact complex manifold and
f\colon X\to X
a holomorphic map. The number

\mathcal{L}_{\operatorname{hol}}(f):=\sum_{q\geq 0}(-1)^{q}\operatorname{tr}\left(f^{*}|H^{0,q}(X)\right)

is called
holomorphic Lefschetz number
.

According to
[
AS68a
]
and
[
AS68
]
the holomorphic Lefschetz number can be computed in different way. For our purpose assume that

X
has dimension
2
and let
G
be a finite group of automorphisms of
X.

2.4 Theorem

(
[
AS68a
]
,
[
AS68
]
)
.

For any
g\in G
the following formula holds

\mathcal{L}_{\operatorname{hol}}(g)=\sum_{j\in J}a(P_{j})+\sum_{k\in K}b(C_{k}),

where sets
\{P_{j}\}_{j\in J}
and
\{C_{k}\}_{k\in K}
denote fixed points and fixed curves in
\operatorname{Fix}(g)
and

a(P):=\frac{1}{\det({1-g}|\mathcal{T}_{g})},\quad\textup{where }\mathcal{T}_{P}\textup{ is a tangent space at $P$,}

b(C):=\frac{1-g(C)}{1-\zeta}-\frac{\zeta C^{2}}{(1-\zeta)^{2}},\quad\textup{where }\zeta\textup{ is an eigenvalue of $g$ on the normal bundle of $\operatorname{Fix}(g).$}

3.
Borcea-Voisin construction

3.1.
Classical Borcea-Voisin construction

One of the many reasons behind the interest in non-symplectic automorphisms of
K3
surfaces is the mirror symmetry construction of C. Borcea (
[
Bor97
]
) and C. Voisin (
[
Voi93
]
). They independently constructed a family of Calabi-Yau threefolds using a non-symplectic involutions of
K3
surfaces and elliptic curves. Moreover C. Voisin gave a construction of explicit mirror maps.

3.1 Theorem

(
[
Bor97
,
Voi93
]
)
.

Let
E
be an elliptic curve with an involution
\alpha_{E}
which does not preserve
\omega_{E}
. Let
S
be a
K3
surface with a non-symplectic involution
\alpha_{S_{6}}
. Then any crepant resolution of the variety
{{}^{\textstyle E\times S}\big/_{\textstyle\alpha_{E}\times\alpha_{S_{6}}}}
is a Calabi-Yau manifold.

3.2.
Generalized Borcea-Voisin construction

In
[
CG16
]
A. Cattaneo and A. Garbagnati generalized the Borcea-Voisin construction allowing a non-symplectic automorphisms of a
K3
surfaces of higher degrees i.e.
3,

4
and
6.

3.2 Theorem

(
[
CG16
]
)
.

Let
S_{d}
be a
K3
surface admitting a purely non-symplectic automorphism
\alpha_{S_{d}}
of order
d=3,4,6.
Let
E_{d}
be an elliptic curve admitting an automorphism
\alpha_{E_{d}}
of order
d.
Then
{{}^{\textstyle S_{d}\times E_{d}}\big/_{\textstyle\alpha_{S_{d}}\times\alpha_{E_{d}}^{n-1}}},
is a singular variety which admits a crepant resolution of singularities

\textstyle{{}^{\textstyle S_{d}\times E_{d}}\big/_{\textstyle\alpha_{S_{d}}\times\alpha_{E_{d}}^{d-1}}}

\textstyle\textstyle\mkern-1.5mu\AC

. In particular

\textstyle{{}^{\textstyle S_{d}\times E_{d}}\big/_{\textstyle\alpha_{S_{d}}\times\alpha_{E_{d}}^{d-1}}}

\textstyle\textstyle\mkern-1.5mu\AC

is a Calabi-Yau threefold.

3.3 Definition
.

A crepant resolution of
{{}^{\textstyle S_{d}\times E_{d}}\big/_{\textstyle\alpha_{S_{d}}\times\alpha_{E_{d}}^{d-1}}}\simeq{{}^{\textstyle S_{d}\times E_{d}}\big/_{\textstyle\mathbb{Z}_{d}}}
is called a
Calabi-Yau 3-fold of Borcea-Voisin type.

In
[
Bur20
]
we investigated higher dimensional construction of Calabi-Yau manifolds which generalizes the classical Borcea-Vosin construction by merging ideas of Cynk-Hulek (
[
CH07
]
) and Cattaneo-Garbagnati. Precisely, we proved the following:

3.4 Theorem

(
[
Bur20
]
)
.

Let
E_{d}
be an elliptic curve with an automorphism of order
d
and
S_{d}
be a
K3
surface with purely non-symplectic automorphism of order
d\in\{2,3,4,6\}.
Consider the following group

G_{d,n}:=\{(m_{1},m_{2},\ldots,m_{n})\in\mathbb{Z}_{d}^{n}\colon m_{1}+m_{2}+\ldots+m_{n}=0\}\simeq\mathbb{Z}_{d}^{n-1}

acting naturally on

. Then the quotient variety
{{}^{\textstyle S_{d}\times E_{d}^{n-1}}\big/_{\textstyle G_{d,n}}}

. Consequently

is an

.

We call the resulting variety

(n+1)
-dimensional Calabi-Yau manifold of Borcea-Voisin type.

The following relation holds among parameters attached to

\displaystyle 0=-N+k+b+2a,

\displaystyle 0=-20+2r+2m-n_{1}-n_{2}-2k+2g(D),
