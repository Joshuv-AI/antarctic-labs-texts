On the Structure of Graded Lie Superalgebras
Antonio J. Calderón Martín

Abstract
We study the structure of graded Lie superalgebras with arbitrary dimension and over an arbitrary field 𝕂 . We show that any of such algebras 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} with a symmetric G G -support is of the form 𝔏 = U + ∑ j I j {\mathfrak{L}}=U+\sum\limits_{j}I_{j} with U U a subspace of 𝔏 1 {\mathfrak{L}}_{1} and any I j I_{j} a well described graded ideal of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} , satisfying [ I j , I k ] = 0 [I_{j},I_{k}]=0 if j ≠ k j\neq k . Under certain conditions, it is shown that 𝔏 = ( ⨁ k ∈ K I k ) ⊕ ( ⨁ q ∈ Q I q ) , {\mathfrak{L}}=(\bigoplus\limits_{k\in K}I_{k})\oplus(\bigoplus\limits_{q\in Q}I_{q}), where any I k I_{k} is a gr-simple graded ideal of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} and any I q I_{q} a completely determined low dimensional non gr-simple graded ideal of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} , satisfying [ I q , I q ′ ] = 0 [I_{q},I_{q^{\prime}}]=0 for any q ′ ∈ Q q^{\prime}\in Q with q ≠ q ′ q\neq q^{\prime} . Keywords : Graded Lie superalgebras, Infinite dimensional Lie superalgebras, Structure Theory. 2000 MSC : 17B70, 17B65, 17B05.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
{\mathfrak{L}}
be a graded Lie superalgebra with
{\mathcal{Z}}({\mathfrak{L}})=0
and
{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}=\sum\limits_{g\in\Sigma_{G}}[{\mathfrak{L}}_{g},{\mathfrak{L}}_{g^{-1}}]
. If
I

ideal of
{\mathfrak{L}}
such that
I\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}
, then
I=\{0\}.

Proof.

Suppose there exists a nonzero graded ideal
I
of
{\mathfrak{L}}

such that
I\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}
. We have
[I,\bigoplus\limits_{g\in\Sigma_{G}}{\mathfrak{L}}_{g}]\subset I\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}
, therefore
[I,\bigoplus\limits_{g\in\Sigma_{G}}{\mathfrak{L}}_{g}]\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}\cap(\bigoplus\limits_{g\in\Sigma_{G}}{\mathfrak{L}}_{g})=0
. The fact

{\mathcal{Z}}({\mathfrak{L}})=0
implies
[I,{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}]\neq 0
.
Taking account
{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}=\sum\limits_{g\in\Sigma_{G}}[{\mathfrak{L}}_{g},{\mathfrak{L}}_{g^{-1}}]
, there exists
g_{0}\in\Sigma_{G}
such
that
[I,[{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}},{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}^{-1}}]]\neq 0
. By
writing
I=I^{\bar{0}}\oplus I^{\bar{1}}
with
I^{\bar{i}}=I\cap{\mathfrak{L}}^{\bar{i}}
,
i\in{\hbox{\hueca Z}}_{2}
, and taking into account
equation (
1
) we have
[I^{\bar{i}},[{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}}^{\bar{j}},{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}^{-1}}^{\bar{k}}]]\neq 0
for some

{\bar{i}},{\bar{j}},{\bar{k}}\in{\hbox{\hueca Z}}_{2}
. Super Jacobi identity
gives us now that either
0\neq[I^{\bar{i}},{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}}^{\bar{j}}]\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}}\cap{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}
or
0\neq[I^{\bar{i}},{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}^{-1}}^{\bar{k}}]\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{g_{0}^{-1}}\cap{\mathfrak{L}}_{1}
, a contradiction. Therefore
I=\{0\}.

∎
