Lower bound on the radii of light rings in traceless black-hole spacetimes
Shahar Hod

Abstract
Photonspheres, curved hypersurfaces on which massless particles can perform closed geodesic motions around highly compact objects, are an integral part of generic black-hole spacetimes. In the present compact paper we prove, using analytical techniques, that the innermost light rings of spherically symmetric hairy black-hole spacetimes whose external matter fields are characterized by a traceless energy-momentum tensor cannot be located arbitrarily close to the central black hole. In particular, we reveal the physically interesting fact that the non-linearly coupled Einstein-matter field equations set the lower bound r γ ≥ 6 5 ​ r H r_{\gamma}\geq{6\over 5}r_{\text{H}} on the radii of traceless black-hole photonspheres, where r H r_{\text{H}} is the radius of the outermost black-hole horizon.
