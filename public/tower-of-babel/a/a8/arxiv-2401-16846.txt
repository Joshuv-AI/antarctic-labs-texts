Introduction

In Matrix Analysis, it is clear that a given block matrix
\begin{pmatrix}X&Y\\
Y^{*}&Z\end{pmatrix}
defined on
\mathcal{H}_{1}\oplus\mathcal{H}_{2}
is a positive block matrix if and only if
X
and
Z
are positive operators as well as there is an operator

which is a contraction with
Y=X^{1/2}DZ^{1/2}.

A fundamental construction due to Gelfand-Naimark-Segal (GNS), see
[
20

-algebra

is characterized by a Hilbert space
\mathcal{H},

\mathcal{H},

such that

for all

Let

be a

-algebra, then a linear mapping

where

and

Completely positive maps, are generalization of positive linear functionals, and their decompositions have applications to Quantum Statistical Mechanics, Operator Algebras, Quantum Information Theory, and other related research areas. Stinespring
[
30
]
gave a characterization of all
B(\mathcal{H})
-valued completely positive maps and this result was further extended for
\mathcal{B}
-valued completely positive maps by Paschke
[
18
]
where

is a von Neumann algebra the proof is similar to that of the GNS construction, but instead of a Hilbert space here we get a Hilbert
\mathcal{A}
-
\mathcal{B}
-bimodule. Considering the block completely positive maps (by taking

) Paulsen
[
19
]
proved that every bounded operator on a Hilbert space is completely polynomially bounded if and only if it is similar to a contraction and hence gave a partial answer to a question mentioned by Halmos
[
11
]
which inquires if each polynomially bounded operator on a Hilbert space is similar to a contraction.

Let

be a completely positive map and

2.

In this section, we will discuss the structure of block completely positive definite kernels. Suppose that
\mathcal{B}
is a unital
C^{*}
-algebra with

is a projection and

(2.1)

b=\begin{pmatrix}pbp&pbq\\
qbp&qbq\end{pmatrix}\in\begin{pmatrix}p\mathcal{B}p&p\mathcal{B}q\\
q\mathcal{B}p&q\mathcal{B}q\end{pmatrix}.

Definition 2.1
.

Suppose that
\mathcal{A}
and
\mathcal{B}
are two unital
C^{*}
-algebras and
p_{1}\in\mathcal{A}
and

respects the above block decomposition over

that is, for every

and

(2.2)

\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}(a)=\begin{pmatrix}\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}(p_{1}ap_{1})&\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}(p_{1}aq_{1})\\
\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}(q_{1}ap_{1})&\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}(q_{1}aq_{1})\end{pmatrix}\in\begin{pmatrix}p_{2}\mathcal{B}p_{2}&p_{2}\mathcal{B}q_{2}\\
q_{2}\mathcal{B}p_{2}&q_{2}\mathcal{B}q_{2}\end{pmatrix}.

Suppose that
\mathfrak{K}:S\times S\to B(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})
is a block kernel. Then there exist

\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{11}:p_{1}\mathcal{A}p_{1}\to p_{2}\mathcal{B}p_{2},

\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{12}:p_{1}\mathcal{A}q_{1}\to p_{2}\mathcal{B}q_{2},\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{21}:q_{1}\mathcal{A}p_{1}\to q_{2}\mathcal{B}p_{2},

\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{11}&\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{12}\\
\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{21}&\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}_{22}\end{pmatrix}\quad\quad for\quad\sigma^{\prime},\sigma\in S.

Lemma 2.2
.

Suppose that
\mathcal{A}
and
\mathcal{B}
are two unital

-algebras and we denote

\mathfrak{L}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}(a)=\langle\mathfrak{j}_{1}(\sigma),Va\mathfrak{j}_{2}(\sigma^{\prime})\rangle.
Then the block kernel

\mathfrak{L}^{*}&\mathfrak{K}_{2}\end{pmatrix}:S\times S\to B(M_{2}(\mathcal{A}),M_{2}(\mathcal{B}))

Proof.

Let
\mathfrak{k}(\sigma)=V\mathfrak{j}_{2}(\sigma)\in F_{1}

\sigma^{\prime},\sigma\in S

\mathfrak{K}^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}\begin{pmatrix}a_{11}&a_{12}\\

\langle\mathfrak{k}(\sigma^{\prime}),a_{21}\mathfrak{j}_{1}(\sigma)\rangle&\langle\mathfrak{k}(\sigma),a_{22}\mathfrak{k}(\sigma^{\prime})\rangle\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\
0&\langle\mathfrak{j}_{2}(\sigma),a_{22}(I_{F_{2}}-V^{*}V)\mathfrak{j}_{2}(\sigma^{\prime})\rangle\end{pmatrix}

for all

a_{21}&a_{22}\end{pmatrix}\in M_{2}(\mathcal{A}).

It is easy to verify that
\begin{pmatrix}a_{11}&a_{12}\\
a_{21}&a_{22}\end{pmatrix}\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}\langle\mathfrak{j}_{1}(\sigma),a_{11}\mathfrak{j}_{1}(\sigma^{\prime})\rangle&\langle\mathfrak{j}_{1}(\sigma),a_{12}\mathfrak{k}(\sigma^{\prime})\rangle\\

is a CPD-kernel over
