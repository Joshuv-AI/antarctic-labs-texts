Abstract
We prove and generalize several recent conjectures of Z.-W. Sun surrounding binomial coefficients and harmonic numbers. We show that Sun’s series and their analogs can be represented as cyclotomic multiple zeta values of levels N ∈ { 4 , 8 , 12 , 16 , 24 } N\in\{4,8,12,16,24\} , namely Goncharov’s multiple polylogarithms evaluated at N N -th roots of unity.

Amongst Zhi-Wei Sun’s recent conjectures
[
