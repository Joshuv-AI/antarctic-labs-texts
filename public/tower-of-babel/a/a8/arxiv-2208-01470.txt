Extremal numbers of disjoint triangles in r r -partite graphs
Junxue Zhang

Abstract
For two graphs G G and F F , the extremal number of F F in G G , denoted by ex ( G , F ) (G,F) , is the maximum number of edges in a spanning subgraph of G G not containing F F as a subgraph. Determining ex ( K n , F ) (K_{n},F) for a given graph F F is a classical extremal problem in graph theory. In 1962, Erdős determined ex ( K n , k ​ K 3 ) (K_{n},kK_{3}) , which generalized Mantel’s Theorem. On the other hand, in 1974, Bollobás, Erdős, and Straus determined ex ( K n 1 , n 2 , … , n r , K t ) (K_{n_{1},n_{2},\dots,n_{r}},K_{t}) , which extended Turán’s Theorem to complete multipartite graphs. In this paper, we determine ex ( K n 1 , n 2 , … , n r , k ​ K 3 ) (K_{n_{1},n_{2},\dots,n_{r}},kK_{3}) for r ≥ 4 r\geq 4 and 10 ​ k − 4 ≤ n 1 + 4 ​ k ≤ n 2 ≤ n 3 ≤ ⋯ ≤ n r 10k-4\leq n_{1}+4k\leq n_{2}\leq n_{3}\leq\cdots\leq n_{r} . Keywords: extremal numbers; multipartite graphs; disjoint triangles
