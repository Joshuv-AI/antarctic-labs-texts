1.
Introduction

For any positive integer
n
, let
\sigma(n)
be the sum of all
positive divisors of
n.
In this paper, we always assume that
x
is a real number,
m
and
n
are positive integers,
p
is a prime,
p_{n}
is the
n
-th
prime,
\phi(n)
denotes the Euler totient function.
Jarden
[
4
, p.65]
observed that
\phi(30n+1)>\phi(30n)
for all
n\leq 10^{5}
and later the inequality was calculated to be true up to
10^{9}.
However, Newman
[
8
]
proved that there are infinitely many
n
such that
\phi(30n+1)<\phi(30n)
and the smallest one is

\frac{p_{385}p_{388}\prod_{j=4}^{383}p_{j}-1}{30},

which was given by Martin
[
7
]
. For related research, one may refer
to
[
3
,
6
,
9
,
5
,
11
]
. It is certainly natural to consider the analogous problem for the
sum of divisors function. Recently, Pongsriiam
[
10
, Theorem 2.4]

proved that
\sigma(30n)-\sigma(30n+1)
also has infinitely many sign changes. He found that

\sigma(30n)>\sigma(30n+1)
for all
n\leq 10^{7}
and posed the following relevant problem.

Problem 1.1
.

(
[
10
, Problem 3.8(ii)]
)
Is it true that

\sum_{n\leq K}\sigma(30n)>\sum_{n\leq K}\sigma(30n+1)

for all
K\in\mathbb{N}?

Recently, Ding, Pan and Sun
[
2
]
solved several problems of Pongsriiam. Inspired by their ideas, we answer affirmatively the above Problem
1.1
.
In fact, we prove a slightly stronger result.

Theorem 1.1
.

For every integer
1\leq k\leq 29,\ (k,30)=1,
we have

\sum_{n\leq K}\sigma(30n)>\sum_{n\leq K}\sigma(30n+k)

for all
K\in\mathbb{N}.

2.
Estimations

Let

\beta_{0}=\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d^{2}},

where
B_{0}(d)
denotes the number of solutions, not counting multiplicities, of the congruence
30m\equiv 0\pmod{d}.
For every integer
1\leq k\leq 29,\ (k,30)=1,
let

\beta_{k}=\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d^{2}},

where
B_{k}(d)
denotes the number of solutions, not counting multiplicities, of the congruence
30m+k\equiv 0\pmod{d}.
By the
Chinese reminder theorem, both
B_{0}(d)
and
B_{k}(d)
are multiplicative. Note that for
p\nmid 30
we have
B_{0}(p^{\alpha})=1
and
B_{k}(p^{\alpha})=1
for any positive integer
\alpha
. It is obvious that
B_{0}(p^{\alpha})=p
and
B_{k}(p^{\alpha})=0
for
p=2,3,5
and any positive integer
\alpha
. It follows that

B_{0}(d)\leq 30
and
B_{k}(d)\leq 1
for any positive integer
d.

By
[
1
, Theorem 11.7]
and

\frac{\pi^{2}}{6}=\zeta(2)=\prod_{p}\left(1-p^{-2}\right)^{-1},

we have

\beta_{0}=\prod_{p}\left(1+\frac{B_{0}(p)}{p^{2}}+\frac{B_{0}(p^{2})}{p^{4}}+\cdots\right)=\frac{5}{3}\frac{11}{8}\frac{29}{24}\prod_{p\nmid 30}\left(1-p^{-2}\right)^{-1}=\frac{319\pi^{2}}{1080}

and

\beta_{k}=\prod_{p}\left(1+\frac{B_{k}(p)}{p^{2}}+\frac{B_{k}(p^{2})}{p^{4}}+\cdots\right)=\prod_{p\nmid 30}\left(1-p^{-2}\right)^{-1}=\frac{8\pi^{2}}{75}.

We always assume that
x\geq 1000,

1\leq k\leq 29,\ (k,30)=1
in the following Lemmas.

It could be checked that there would be large oscillations of the error terms which influence the main terms if one tries to calculate directly those sums asked in problem
1.1
. Therefore, we manipulate the weighted sums firstly and then transform them to the original sums via summations by parts.

Lemma 2.1
.

We have

\sum_{m\leq x}\frac{\sigma(30m)}{30m}=\beta_{0}x+g(x),

where

-30\log 30x-32<g(x)<30\log 30x+32.

Proof.

By the definition of
B_{0}(d),

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}\frac{\sigma(30m)}{30m}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}\sum_{d\mid 30m}\frac{1}{d}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{1}{d}\sum_{m\leq x\atop 30m\equiv 0\hskip-7.11317pt\pmod{d}}1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d}\left(\frac{x}{d}+\alpha_{0}(x,d)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle x\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d^{2}}+\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d}\alpha_{0}(x,d)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta_{0}x-x\sum_{d>30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d^{2}}+\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d}\alpha_{0}(x,d)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta_{0}x+g(x),

where
-1\leq\alpha_{0}(x,d)\leq 1
and

g(x)=-x\sum_{d>30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d^{2}}+\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d}\alpha_{0}(x,d).

By
[
1
, Theorem 3.2]
,

0\leq\sum_{d>30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d^{2}}\leq 30\sum_{d>30x}\frac{1}{d^{2}}\leq 30\left(\frac{1}{30x}+\frac{30x-[30x]}{(30x)^{2}}\right)

and

0\leq\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{B_{0}(d)}{d}\leq 30\sum_{d\leq 30x}\frac{1}{d}<30(\log 30x+1).

It follows that

-30\log 30x-32<g(x)<30\log 30x+32.

This completes the proof of Lemma
2.1
.
∎

Lemma 2.2
.

We have

\sum_{m\leq x}\frac{\sigma(30m+k)}{30m+k}=\beta_{k}x+h_{k}(x),

where

-\log(30x+k)-2<h_{k}(x)<\log(30x+k)+2.

Proof.

By the definition of
B_{k}(d),

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}\frac{\sigma(30m+k)}{30m+k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}\sum_{d\mid 30m+k}\frac{1}{d}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{1}{d}\sum_{m\leq x\atop 30m+k\equiv 0\hskip-7.11317pt\pmod{d}}1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d}\left(\frac{x}{d}+\alpha_{k}(x,d)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle x\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d^{2}}+\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d}\alpha_{k}(x,d)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta_{k}x-x\sum_{d>30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d^{2}}+\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d}\alpha_{k}(x,d)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta_{k}x+h_{k}(x),

where
-1\leq\alpha_{k}(x,d)\leq 1
and

h_{k}(x)=-x\sum_{d>30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d^{2}}+\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d}\alpha_{k}(x,d)

By
[
1
, Theorem 3.2]
,

0\leq\sum_{d>30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d^{2}}\leq\sum_{d>30x+k}\frac{1}{d^{2}}\leq\frac{1}{30x+k}+\frac{30x+k-[30x+k]}{(30x+k)^{2}}

and

0\leq\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{B_{k}(d)}{d}\leq\sum_{d\leq 30x+k}\frac{1}{d}<\log(30x+k)+1.

It follows that

-\log(30x+k)-2<h_{k}(x)<\log(30x+k)+2.

This completes the proof of Lemma
2.2
.
∎

Lemma 2.3
.

We have

\sum_{m\leq x}\sigma(30m)>15\beta_{0}x^{2}-2000x\log 30x.

Proof.

Let

S(x)=\sum_{m\leq x}\frac{\sigma(30m)}{30m}.

By
[
1
, Theorem 3.1]
,

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}\sigma(30m)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}30m(S(m)-S(m-1))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 30[x]S([x])-\sum_{m\leq x-1}(30(m+1)-30m)S(m)-30S(0)

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle 30(x-1)(\beta_{0}x-30\log 30x-32)-30\sum_{m\leq x-1}(\beta_{0}m+30\log 30m+32)

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle 30\beta_{0}x^{2}-900x\log 30x-960x-15\beta_{0}x^{2}-900x\log 30x-960x

\displaystyle\quad-30\beta_{0}x+900\log 30x+960

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle 15\beta_{0}x^{2}-2000x\log 30x.

This completes the proof of Lemma
2.3
.
∎

Lemma 2.4
.

We have

\sum_{m\leq x}\sigma(30m+k)<15\beta_{k}x^{2}+100x\log(30x+k).

Proof.

Let

T_{k}(x)=\sum_{m\leq x}\frac{\sigma(30m+k)}{30m+k}.

By
[
1
, Theorem 3.1]
,

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}\sigma(30m+k)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq x}(30m+k)(T_{k}(m)-T_{k}(m-1))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(30[x]+k)T_{k}([x])-\sum_{m\leq x-1}(30(m+1)+k-30m-k)T_{k}(m)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle(30x+k)(\beta_{k}x+\log(30x+k)+2)-30\sum_{m\leq x-1}(\beta_{k}m-\log(30m+k)-2)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle 30\beta_{k}x^{2}+30x\log(30x+k)+60x-15\beta_{k}(x-2)^{2}+30x\log(30x+k)

\displaystyle\quad+60x+k\beta_{k}x+k\log(30x+k)+2k

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle 15\beta_{k}x^{2}+100x\log(30x+k).

This completes the proof of Lemma
2.4
.
∎

3.
Proof of Theorem
1.1

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.

For every integer
1\leq k\leq 29,\ (k,30)=1,
by Lemmas
2.3
and
2.4
, we have

\sum_{m\leq x}\sigma(30m)>15\beta_{0}x^{2}-2000x\log 30x>15\beta_{k}x^{2}+100x\log(30x+k)>\sum_{m\leq x}\sigma(30m+k)

provided that
x\geq 1000.
For every integer
1\leq k\leq 29,\ (k,30)=1,
it is easy to verify that

\sum_{m\leq K}\sigma(30m)>\sum_{m\leq K}\sigma(30m+k)

for every positive integer
K<1000
by programming.

This completes the proof of Theorem
1.1
.
∎
