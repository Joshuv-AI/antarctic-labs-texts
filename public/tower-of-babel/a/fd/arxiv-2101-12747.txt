over the semi-open intervals

defined by
\tau(x):=\frac{2^{\lfloor\frac{x-1}{\alpha}\rfloor}}{3^{x}}
has the following properties:

•

The graph of
\tau
is a set of disjoint, congruent and parallel exponential branches:

.

•

\alpha

.

•

The range of

\lim_{x\rightarrow(1+n\alpha)^{-}}\tau(x)=\frac{1}{6}
.

•

\tau

.

Each interval

for some

. Thus for

. The projection

(38)
