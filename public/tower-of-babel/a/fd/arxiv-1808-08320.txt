L p L_{p} and almost sure convergence of estimation on heavy tail index under random censoring
Yunyi Zhang, Jiazheng Liu, Zexin Pan, Dimitris N. Politis

Abstract
In this paper, we prove L p , p ≥ 2 L_{p},\ p\geq 2 and almost sure convergence of tail index estimator mentioned in [ 1 ] under random censoring and several assumptions. p p th moment of the error of the estimator is proved to be of order O ⁡ ( 1 log m ​ κ / 2 ⁡ n ) O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{m\kappa/2}n}\right) with given assumptions. We also perform several finite sample simulations to quantify performance of this estimator. Finite sample results show that the proposed estimator is effective in finding underlying tail index even when censor rate is high.

We first provide several crucial lemma that will be frequently used in the following proof.

Lemma 1
.

Suppose
X
and
Y
satisfy A1 and A2, then

\gamma_{Z}=\frac{\gamma_{X}\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}

Proof.

Because of independents, we have, for arbitrary large

P_{Z}(x)=L_{X}(x)L_{Y}(x)x^{-\alpha_{X}-\alpha_{Y}}

Therefore,
\gamma_{Z}=1/(\alpha_{X}+\alpha_{Y})=\frac{\gamma_{X}\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}

|\frac{xL_{Z}^{{}^{\prime}}(x)}{L_{Z}(x)}|\leq|\frac{xL_{X}^{{}^{\prime}}}{L_{X}}|+|\frac{xL_{Y}^{{}^{\prime}}}{L_{Y}}|=O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{\kappa}n}\right)

(14)

and the result is proved.
∎

The next one is introduced to provide a bound for the slow varying function.

\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(\log t)^{n\alpha_{Z}-\frac{(n+1)^{1-\kappa}}{D(1-\kappa)}}}\leq\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{4^{n\alpha_{Z}-\frac{(n+1)^{1-\kappa}}{D(1-\kappa)}}}\leq\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^{n\alpha_{Z}}}<\infty

(36)

And we prove the result.

Case 2:
\kappa=1
. If
\kappa=1
, from
31
, we have
\frac{L_{Z}(\eta_{n})}{L_{Z}(t)}\leq\left(1+\frac{(n+1)\log\log t}{\log t}\right)^{C}
, correspondingly, combine with
29
and
30
, for large
t
, we have

\displaystyle|\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t)}\int_{t}^{\infty}\frac{P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy-\gamma_{Z}|\leq\frac{C\gamma_{Z}^{2}}{\log t}\Sigma_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\log^{n\alpha_{Z}}t}\left(1+\frac{(n+1)\log\log t}{\log t}\right)^{C}

(37)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C\gamma_{Z}^{2}}{\log t}\left(2^{C}+\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(n+2)^{C}}{2^{n\alpha_{Z}}}\right)

Since
\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(n+2)^{C}}{2^{n\alpha_{Z}}}<\infty
, the result is proved.

Case 3:
\kappa>1
. If
\kappa>1
, from
31
, we have
\frac{L_{Z}(\eta_{n})}{L_{Z}(t)}\leq\exp(2C/(\kappa-1))
, combine with
30
and we prove the result.
∎

Now, we start to prove the
L_{p}
convergence of statistics
\widehat{\zeta}
.

Theorem 4
.

Suppose A1,A2 and
m\geq 2
then we have

||\widehat{\zeta}-\gamma_{Z}||_{m}=O\left(\frac{1}{s(n)\sqrt{n}}+\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))\sqrt{n}}+\frac{1}{\log^{\kappa}t(n)}\right)

(38)

Proof.

From definition of
\widehat{\zeta}
(see
10
) and Minkowski inequality,

\displaystyle||\widehat{\zeta}-\gamma_{Z}||_{m}\leq||\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)}{y}dy-\gamma_{Z}\right)\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}||_{m}+

(39)

\displaystyle\gamma_{Z}||\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}||_{m}+\gamma_{Z}Prob\left(\widehat{p}(t(n))<s(n)\right)^{1/m}

For the second and the third term, from
26
we know that these term is of order
O\left(\frac{1}{s(n)\sqrt{n}}+\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))\sqrt{n}}\right)=o(1)
. For the first term, from Cauchy inequality and Minkowski inequality, it is less than

||\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}||_{2m}\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}||\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy||_{2m}+|\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy-\gamma_{Z}|\right)

(40)

From
26
,
||\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}||_{2m}=O\left(1\right)
, from integral version Minkowski inequality and lemma
17
, we have

\displaystyle||\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y))}{y}dy||_{2m}\leq\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{||\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)||_{2m}}{y}dy

(41)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{C_{2m}P_{Z}^{1/2m}(y)}{\sqrt{n}y}dy

From lemma
15
, choose
\epsilon=\alpha_{Z}/2
, for sufficiently large

and thus the integration is less than
\frac{4mC_{2m}}{\alpha_{Z}\sqrt{n}}t(n)^{-\alpha_{Z}/4m}=o(1/\sqrt{n})
. Thus,

\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}||\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy||_{2m}=O\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))\sqrt{n}}\right)

(42)

For the second term in
40
, use lemma
28
and we prove the result.
∎

Similarly, if in addition we assume A3, from
9
we know that the convergence rate is of
O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{\kappa}n}\right)
and otherwise the convergence rate becomes
O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{\kappa}\log n}\right)
.

.

Proof for theorem

.

We choose
\mu=\nu=m/2
in theorem 1 of
[

]

20
and
38
, since exists constant
C

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}|\widehat{\zeta}-\gamma_{Z}|^{2\nu}=||\widehat{\zeta}-\gamma_{Z}||_{m}^{m}\leq\frac{C^{m}}{\log^{m\kappa}n}

3
Almost sure convergence of tail index estimator

In this section, we try to prove the almost sure convergence of the tail index estimator under assumption A1-A3. We first introduce two lemma.

Theorem 5
.

Suppose A1-A3, and
t(n),s(n)
are chosen as in
9
, then we have

\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}\to_{a.s.}\frac{\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}

(46)

Proof.

From Borel-Cantelli lemma
[
19
]
, it suffices to show that, for
\forall\epsilon>0
,

\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}Prob\left(|\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}-\frac{\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}|>3\epsilon\right)<\infty

(47)

Since

\displaystyle Prob\left(|\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}-\frac{\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}|>3\epsilon\right)

(48)

\displaystyle\leq Prob\left(|\left(\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}-\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\right)\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}|\geq\epsilon\right)

\displaystyle+Prob\left(|\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}-\frac{\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}|\geq\epsilon\right)

\displaystyle+Prob\left(|\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}\geq\epsilon\right)

We will separately discuss these 3 terms below.

For the first term, notice that for
\forall k>1
, from mean value inequality and Minkowski inequality, we have

\displaystyle Prob\left(|\left(\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\right)\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)|\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}\geq\epsilon\right)

(49)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\epsilon^{k}}||\left(\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\right)\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}||_{k}^{k}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2^{k}\epsilon^{k}}||\left(\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\widehat{p}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{p}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\right)^{2}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}||_{k}^{k}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2^{k}\epsilon^{k}}\left(||\frac{\widehat{q}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}||_{2k}^{2}+\frac{1}{s(n)^{2}}||\widehat{p}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{p}(t(n))||_{2k}^{2}\right)^{k}

From lemma
17
,
||\widehat{q}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))||_{2k}^{2}=O\left(\frac{P_{Z}^{1/k}(t(n))}{n}\right)
and
||\widehat{p}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{p}(t(n))||_{2k}^{2}=O\left(\frac{P_{Z}^{1/k}(t(n))}{n}\right)
, choose
k>\max\left(\frac{1}{1/2-\beta/\gamma_{0}},\frac{1}{1-2c}\right)
then the convergence of summation of first term is proved.

For the second term, notice that it is smaller than

Prob\left(|\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}-\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\geq\epsilon-|\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}-\frac{\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}|\right)

(50)

From
23
, for sufficiently large
n
,
|\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}-\frac{\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}|\leq\epsilon/2
, and
\frac{\mathbf{E}\widehat{q}(t(n))}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\leq\frac{2\gamma_{Y}}{\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y}}
. Since for
\forall k>1

\displaystyle Prob\left(|\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}-1|\geq\frac{(\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y})\epsilon}{4\gamma_{Y}}\right)

(51)

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{4\gamma_{Y}}{\epsilon(\gamma_{X}+\gamma_{Y})}\right)^{k}\left(\frac{1}{s(n)}||\widehat{p}(t(n))-\mathbf{E}\widehat{p}(t(n))||_{k}+Prob(\widehat{p}(t(n))<s(n))\right)^{k}

From
26
and similar to
49
, choose sufficiently large
k
and we know that summation of this term converges.

For the third term, similar with
49
and we prove can prove the convergence of summation. Since
47
is true, almost sure convergence is proved as well.
∎

Theorem 6
.

Suppose A1-A3 and suppose
s(n)
and
t(n)
are chosen as in
9
, then we have

\widehat{\zeta}\to_{a.s.}\gamma_{Z}

(52)

definition of
\widehat{\zeta}
is in

.

Proof.

From Borel-Cantelli
[
19
]
lemma, it suffices to show that, for
\forall\epsilon>0
,

\Sigma_{n=1}^{\infty}Prob\left(|\widehat{\zeta}-\gamma_{Z}|\geq 4\epsilon\right)<\infty

(53)

Since the above term is less than

|\widehat{\zeta}-\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)}{y}dy|+|\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy|+|\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy-\gamma_{Z}|

According to lemma
28
, for sufficiently large

\displaystyle\Sigma_{n=n_{0}}^{\infty}Prob\left(|\widehat{\zeta}-\gamma_{Z}|\geq 4\epsilon\right)

(55)

\displaystyle\leq\Sigma_{n=n_{0}}^{\infty}Prob\left(|\widehat{\zeta}-\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)}{y}dy|\geq\epsilon\right)+Prob\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy|\geq\epsilon\right)

For the second term, notice for arbitrary

\displaystyle Prob\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy|\geq\epsilon\right)\leq\frac{1}{\epsilon^{k}}\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}||\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy||_{k}\right)^{k}

\displaystyle=O\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}^{k}(t(n))n^{k/2}}\right)

Choose

The first term of

is less than

\displaystyle Prob\left(\frac{\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1|\geq\frac{\epsilon}{2}\right)

(57)

\displaystyle+Prob\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)\left(\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy\right)|\geq\frac{\epsilon}{2}\right)

According to lemma
28
, For large

, so for sufficiently large

\displaystyle Prob\left(\frac{\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1|\geq\frac{\epsilon}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq Prob\left(\frac{|P_{Z}(t(n))-\widehat{p}(t(n))|}{s(n)}\geq\frac{\epsilon}{4\gamma_{Z}}\right)+Prob\left(\widehat{p}(t(n))<s(n)\right)

From

\displaystyle Prob\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}(t(n))}|\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy|\geq\frac{\epsilon}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq Prob\left(\frac{1}{P_{Z}^{2}(t(n))}|\int_{t(n)}^{\infty}\frac{\widehat{p}(y)-P_{Z}(y)}{y}dy|^{2}\geq\frac{\epsilon}{2}\right)

\displaystyle+Prob\left(\left(\frac{P_{Z}(t(n))\mathbf{1}_{\widehat{p}(t(n))\geq s(n)}}{\widehat{p}(t(n))}-1\right)^{2}\geq\epsilon/2\right)

From

Proof for theorem

Theorem 7
.

Distribution with density
62
satisfies condition A2

Proof.

Slow varying part of distribution
62

L_{K}(x)=C_{f_{K}}x^{\alpha_{K}}\int_{x}^{\infty}y^{-\alpha_{K}-1}\log^{\beta_{K}-1}y\ dy

\displaystyle|\frac{xL_{K}^{{}^{\prime}}(x)}{L_{K}(x)}|=\frac{|(\alpha_{K}\int_{x}^{\infty}y^{-\alpha_{K}-1}\log^{\beta_{K}-1}y\ dy)-x^{-\alpha_{K}}\log^{\beta_{K}-1}x|}{\int_{x}^{\infty}y^{-\alpha_{K}-1}\log^{\beta_{K}-1}y\ dy}

(65)

\displaystyle=\frac{|\beta_{K}-1|\int_{x}^{\infty}y^{-\alpha_{K}-1}\log^{\beta_{K}-2}y\ dy}{\int_{x}^{\infty}y^{-\alpha_{K}-1}\log^{\beta_{K}-1}y\ dy}

(66)

and thus assumption A2 is satisfied with
\kappa=1
.
