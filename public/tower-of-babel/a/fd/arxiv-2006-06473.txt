Bottom of the L 2 L^{2} spectrum of the Laplacian on locally symmetric spaces
Jean-Philippe ANKER and Hong-Wei ZHANG

Abstract
. We estimate the bottom of the L 2 L^{2} spectrum of the Laplacian on locally symmetric spaces in terms of the critical exponents of appropriate Poincaré series. Our main result is the higher rank analog of a characterization due to Elstrodt, Patterson, Sullivan and Corlette in rank one. It improves upon previous results obtained by Leuzinger and Weber in higher rank.

G

\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{k}\oplus\mathfrak{p}

G
. The Killing form of
\mathfrak{g}
induces a
K
-invariant inner product
\langle\,.\,,\,.\,\rangle
on
\mathfrak{p}
, hence a
G

G/K

\mathfrak{a}
in
\mathfrak{p}

\mathfrak{a}
with its dual
\mathfrak{a}^{*}
by means of the inner product inherited from
\mathfrak{p}
.
Let
\Gamma
be a discrete torsion-free subgroup of
G
that acts freely and properly discontinuously on

. Then
Y\!=\Gamma\backslash X
is a locally symmetric space, whose Riemannian structure is inherited from
X

the joint Riemannian distance on

Let

Consider the classical Poincaré series

\displaystyle P_{s}(xK,yK)=\sum\nolimits_{\gamma\in\Gamma}e^{-sd(xK,\gamma yK)}\qquad\forall\,s>0,\,\forall\,x,y\in G

and

.
Recall that
\delta(\Gamma)\in[0,2\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|]

\displaystyle\delta(\Gamma)=\limsup_{R\to+\infty}\frac{\log N_{R}(xK,yK)}{R}\qquad\forall\,x,y\in G,

where
N_{R}(xK,yK)=|\{\gamma\in\Gamma\,|\,d(xK,\gamma yK)\leq R\}|
denotes the orbital counting function. Finally, let
\Delta_{Y}
be the Laplace-Beltrami operator on

Theorem 1.1
.

In the rank one case (

\displaystyle\lambda_{0}(Y)=\begin{cases}\|\rho\|^{2}&\qquad\textnormal{if}\;\;0\leq\delta(\Gamma)\leq\|\rho\|,\\
\|\rho\|^{2}-(\delta(\Gamma)-\|\rho\|)^{2}&\qquad\textnormal{if}\;\;\|\rho\|\leq\delta(\Gamma)\leq 2\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|.\end{cases}

This result was extended in higher rank as follows by Leuzinger

Theorem 1.2

In the general case (

\bullet

Upper bound:

\displaystyle\lambda_{0}(Y)\leq\begin{cases}\,\|\rho\|^{2}&\qquad\textnormal{if}\;\;0\leq\delta(\Gamma)\leq\|\rho\|,\\
\,\|\rho\|^{2}-(\delta(\Gamma)-\|\rho\|)^{2}&\qquad\textnormal{if}\;\;\|\rho\|\leq\delta(\Gamma)\leq 2\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|.\end{cases}

\bullet

Lower bound:

\displaystyle\lambda_{0}(Y)\geq\begin{cases}\,\|\rho\|^{2}&\qquad\textnormal{if}\;\;0\leq\delta(\Gamma)\leq\rho_{\text{\rm min}},\\
\,\max\hskip 1.42262pt\{0,\|\rho\|^{2}-(\delta(\Gamma)-\rho_{\text{\rm min}})^{2}\}&\qquad\textnormal{if}\;\;\rho_{\text{\rm min}}\leq\delta(\Gamma)\leq 2\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|.\end{cases}

In other terms,

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\,\lambda_{0}(Y)=\|\rho\|^{2}&\textnormal{if}\;\;\delta(\Gamma)\in\bigl[\hskip 0.56905pt0,\rho_{\text{\rm min}}\bigr],\\[5.0pt]
\,\lambda_{0}(Y)\in\bigl[\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|^{2}\!-(\delta(\Gamma)\hskip-1.42262pt-\hskip-1.42262pt\rho_{\text{\rm min}})^{2},\|\rho\|^{2}\hskip 0.56905pt\bigr]&\textnormal{if}\;\;\delta(\Gamma)\in\bigl[\hskip 0.85358pt\rho_{\text{\rm min}},\|\rho\|\bigr],\\[5.0pt]
\,\lambda_{0}(Y)\in\bigl[\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|^{2}\!-\hskip-1.42262pt(\delta(\Gamma)\hskip-1.42262pt-\hskip-1.42262pt\rho_{\text{\rm min}})^{2},\|\rho\|^{2}\!-\hskip-1.42262pt(\delta(\Gamma)\hskip-1.42262pt-\hskip-1.42262pt\|\rho\|)^{2}\hskip 0.56905pt\bigr]&\textnormal{if}\;\;\delta(\Gamma)\in\bigl[\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|,\|\rho\|\hskip-1.42262pt+\hskip-1.42262pt\rho_{\text{\rm min}}\bigr],\\[5.0pt]

As for the Green function, the heat kernel

Proposition 2.1

Proof.

Notice first that (

The

\displaystyle d^{\hskip 0.85358pt\prime}(xK,yK)=d^{\hskip 0.85358pt\prime}(yK,xK)

follows from

\displaystyle(y^{-1})^{+}=-w_{0}.y^{+}\quad\text{and}\quad-w_{0}.\rho=\rho,

where

denotes the longest element in the Weyl group. Let us check the triangular inequality

\displaystyle d^{\hskip 0.85358pt\prime}(xK,yK)\leq d^{\hskip 0.85358pt\prime}(xK,zK)+d^{\hskip 0.85358pt\prime}(zK,yK).

By

According to Lemma

\displaystyle x^{+}+(y^{-1})^{+}-(y^{-1}x)^{+}

belongs to the cone generated by the positive roots.
∎

Lemma 2.2
.

For every
x,y\in G
, we have the following inclusion

(2.3)

\displaystyle\operatorname{co}[W.(xy)^{+}]\subset\operatorname{co}[W.(x^{+}+y^{+})]

between convex hulls.

Proof.

The inclusion (
2.3
) amounts to the fact that

\displaystyle x^{+}+y^{+}-(xy)^{+}

belongs to the cone generated by the positive roots or, equivalently, to the inequality

(2.4)

\displaystyle\langle\lambda,(xy)^{+}\rangle\leq\langle\lambda,x^{+}\rangle+\langle\lambda,y^{+}\rangle\qquad\forall\,\lambda\in\overline{\mathfrak{a}^{+}}.

It is enough to prove (
2.4
) for all highest weights
\lambda
of irreducible finite-dimensional complex representations
\pi:G\longrightarrow\text{GL}(V)
with
K
-fixed vectors.
According to Weyl’s unitary trick (see for instance
[
Kna02
, Proposition 7.15]
), there exists an inner product on

such that

\displaystyle\textstyle\begin{cases}\pi(k)\text{ is unitary}&{\forall}\,k\in K,\\
\pi(a)\text{ is self-adjoint}&{\forall}\,a\in\exp\mathfrak{a}.\end{cases}

As
\lambda
is the highest weight of

\displaystyle e^{\langle\lambda,(xy)^{+}\rangle}=\|\pi(xy)\|\leq\|\pi(x)\|\|\pi(y)\|=e^{\langle\lambda,x^{+}\rangle}e^{\langle\lambda,y^{+}\rangle}=e^{\langle\lambda,x^{+}+y^{+}\rangle}.

∎

\displaystyle s\,d^{\hskip 0.85358pt\prime}(xK,yK)\leq d^{\hskip 1.13809pt\prime\prime}_{s}(xK,yK)\leq s\,d(xK,yK)\qquad\forall\,s>0,\,\forall\,x,y\in G.

\displaystyle P^{\hskip 1.13809pt\prime\prime}_{s}(xK,yK)=\sum\nolimits_{\gamma\in\Gamma}e^{-d^{\hskip 1.13809pt\prime\prime}_{s}(xK,\gamma yK)}\qquad\forall\,s>0,\,\forall\,x,y\in G

\displaystyle\delta^{\hskip 0.56905pt\prime\prime}{(\Gamma)}=\inf\{s>0\,|\,P^{\hskip 1.13809pt\prime\prime}_{s}(xK,yK)<+\infty\}.

It follows from (

\displaystyle 0\leq\delta{(\Gamma)}\leq\delta^{\hskip 0.56905pt\prime\prime}{(\Gamma)}\leq\delta^{\hskip 0.56905pt\prime}{(\Gamma)}\leq 2\hskip 0.56905pt\|\rho\|.
