Covariant Differential Calculus Over Monoidal Hom-Hopf Algebras
SERKAN KARAÇUHA

Abstract
. Concepts of first order differential calculus (FODC) on a monoidal Hom-algebra and left-covariant FODC over a left Hom-quantum space with respect to a monoidal Hom-Hopf algebra are presented. Then, extension of the universal FODC over a monoidal Hom-algebra to a universal Hom-differential calculus is described. Next, concepts of left(right)-covariant and bicovariant FODC over a monoidal Hom-Hopf algebra are studied in detail. Subsequently, notion of quantum Hom-tangent space associated to a bicovariant Hom-FODC is introduced and equipped with an analogue of Lie bracket (commutator) through Woronowicz’ braiding. Finally, it is proven that this commutator satisfies quantum versions of the antisymmetry relation and Hom-Jacobi identity.

Definition 2.2

A first order differential calculus over a monoidal Hom-algebra

(A,\alpha)
-Hom-bimodule

Remark 2.3
.

(1)

In the above definition, the second condition, i.e.
d\circ\alpha=\gamma\circ d
, is equivalent to the equality
d(1)=0

(2)

By the compatibility condition for Hom-bimodule structure of

(3)

By using the Leibniz rule and the fact that

\displaystyle(a\cdot db)\cdot c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(d(ab)-da\cdot b)\cdot c=d(ab)\cdot c-(da\cdot b)\cdot c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d(ab)\cdot c-\gamma(d(a))\cdot(b\alpha^{-1}(c))=d(ab)\cdot c-d(\alpha(a))\cdot(b\alpha^{-1}(c)),

and

\displaystyle\alpha(a)\cdot(db\cdot\alpha^{-1}(c))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha(a)\cdot(d(b\alpha^{-1}(c))-b\cdot d(\alpha^{-1}(c)))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha(a)\cdot d(b\alpha^{-1}(c))-\alpha(a)\cdot(b\cdot d(\alpha^{-1}(c)))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha(a)\cdot d(b\alpha^{-1}(c))-(ab)\cdot d(c).

Hence,
\Gamma=A\cdot dA=dA\cdot A

3.
Universal Differential Calculus of a Monoidal Hom-Algebra

In the theory of quantum groups, a differential calculus is a substitute of the de Rham complex of a smooth manifold for arbitrary algebras. In this section, the definition of differential calculus over a monoidal Hom-algebra (abbreviated, Hom-DC) is given and the construction of the universal differential calculus of a monoidal Hom-algebra (universal Hom-DC) is outlined.

Definition 3.1
.

A
graded
monoidal Hom-algebra is a monoidal Hom-algebra
(A,\alpha)
together with subobjects
A_{n},n\geq 0
(that is, for each
k
-submodule
A_{n}\subseteq A
,
(A_{n},\alpha|_{A_{n}})\in\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}(\mathcal{M}_{k})
) such that

A=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}A_{n},

1\in A_{0}
, and
A_{n}A_{m}\subseteq A_{n+m}
for all
n,m\geq 0
.

Definition 3.2
.

A differential calculus over a monoidal Hom-algebra
(A,\alpha)
is a graded monoidal Hom-algebra
(\Gamma=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\Gamma^{n},\gamma)
with a linear map
d:\Gamma\to\Gamma
, in
\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}(\mathcal{M}_{k})
, of degree one (i.e.,

(1)

,

(2)

for
\omega\in\Gamma^{n},\omega^{\prime}\in\Gamma

(3)

\Gamma^{0}=A
,
\gamma|_{\Gamma^{0}}=\alpha
, and
\Gamma^{n}
is a linear span of the elements of the form

a_{0}(da_{1}(\cdots(da_{n-1}da_{n})\cdots))
with
a_{0},\cdots,a_{n}\in A
,

.

A

of
(\Gamma,\gamma)
is a Hom-ideal
\mathcal{I}
of the monoidal Hom-algebra
(\Gamma,\gamma)
(that is,

is a subobject of

Let us write

. Let

be a differential Hom-ideal of a
(A,\alpha)
-Hom-DC

. Then,

of

In the rest of the section, the construction of the universal differential calculus on a monoidal Hom-algebra
(A,\alpha)
is discussed. Let
(A,\alpha)
be a monoidal Hom-algebra with Hom-multiplication
m_{A}:A\otimes A\to A
. The linear map

, in

\displaystyle a\cdot db+da\cdot b

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\cdot(1\otimes\alpha^{-1}(b)-\alpha^{-1}(b)\otimes 1)+(1\otimes\alpha^{-1}(a)-\alpha^{-1}(a)\otimes 1)\cdot b

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{-1}(a)1\otimes\alpha(\alpha^{-1}(b))-\alpha^{-1}(a)\alpha^{-1}(b)\otimes 1

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle 1\otimes\alpha^{-1}(a)\alpha^{-1}(b)-\alpha(\alpha^{-1}(a))\otimes 1\alpha^{-1}(b)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\otimes b-\alpha^{-1}(ab)\otimes 1+1\otimes\alpha^{-1}(ab)-a\otimes b=1\otimes\alpha^{-1}(ab)-\alpha^{-1}(ab)\otimes 1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d(ab).

For any

, we get

\displaystyle(d\circ\alpha)(a)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d(\alpha(a))=1\otimes a-a\otimes 1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\alpha\otimes\alpha)(1\otimes\alpha^{-1}(a)-\alpha^{-1}(a)\otimes 1)=(\alpha\otimes\alpha)(da),

meaning

\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}(\mathcal{M}_{k})

4.

4.1.

Let

(H,\alpha)

\Delta:H\to H\otimes H,h\mapsto h_{1}\otimes h_{2}

2.4

Definition 4.1

A FODC
(\Gamma,\gamma)

(\Gamma,\gamma)
is a left-covariant FODC over the left Hom-quantum space
(H,\alpha)
with left Hom-coaction
\varphi=\Delta
in Definition
2.4
.

Remark 4.2

According to Proposition
2.5

(H,\alpha)
-Hom-FODC

\phi:\Gamma\to H\otimes\Gamma
in
\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}(\mathcal{M}_{k})

h,g\in H
,

(4.4)

\phi(h\cdot dg)=\Delta(h)(id\otimes d)(\Delta(g)).

In the proof of Proposition
2.5

\phi

(\Gamma,\gamma)
on
(H,\alpha)
and satisfies

\phi(\alpha(h)\cdot(\omega\cdot g))=\Delta(\alpha(h))(\phi(\omega)\Delta(g))

for
h,g\in H
and
\omega\in\Gamma
. From this it follows that
(\Gamma,\gamma)
is a left-covariant
(H,\alpha)
-Hom-bimodule.

Let
(\Gamma,\gamma)
be a left-covariant
(H,\alpha)
-Hom-FODC with derivation

(\Gamma,\gamma)
is a left-covariant
(H,\alpha)
-Hom-bimodule, and then by adapting the structure theory of left-covariant Hom-bimodules, which is discussed in Lemma 4.7 and Proposition 4.9 in
[
11

we summarize the following results. We have the unique projection

\varrho\in\Gamma

Obviously, it is in
\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}(\mathcal{M}_{k})

4.2.

In the theory of Lie groups, if
