Conformal Ricci collineations associated to the Levi-Civita connection on three-dimensional Lorentzian Non-unimodular Lie groups

Calvaruso and Cordero classified three-dimensional Lorentzian Lie groups into
\{G_{i}\}_{i=1,\cdots,7}
equipped with a left-invariant Lorentzian metric
g
in
[
10
,
12
]
(see Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 2.2 in
[
9
]
), and geted corresponding Lie algebra
\{\mathfrak{g}\}_{i=1,\cdots,7.}
. In
[
9
]
, Batat studied algebraic Ricci solitons of three-dimensional Lorentzian Lie groups, similar to his calculations, we compute the Ricci tensor of
\{G_{i}\}_{i=1,\cdots,7}
associated to the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla^{L}
. We define the curvature tensor of the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla^{L}
:

(2.1)

R^{L}(X,Y)Z=\nabla^{L}_{X}\nabla^{L}_{Y}Z-\nabla^{L}_{Y}\nabla^{L}_{X}Z-\nabla^{L}_{[X,Y]}Z.

The Ricci tensor of
(G_{i},g)
associated to the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla^{L}
is defined by

(2.2)

\rho^{L}(X,Y)=-g(R^{L}(X,e_{1})Y,e_{1})-g(R^{L}(X,e_{2})Y,e_{2})+g(R^{L}(X,e_{3})Y,e_{3}).

where
g(e_{i},e_{j})=0,i\neq j;~g(e_{i},e_{i})=1,i=1,2;~g(e_{3},e_{3})=-1.
Let

(2.3)

Ric^{L}(X,Y)=\frac{\rho^{L}(X,Y)+\rho^{L}(Y,X)}{2}.

we get the symmetric Ricci tensor.

We know that for vector
X,Y,V
, the Lie derivative of the Ricci tensor
Ric^{L}
associated to
V
can be defined:

(2.4)

(L_{Z}Ric^{L})(X,Y):=Z[Ric^{L}(X,Y)]-Ric^{L}([Z,X],Y)-Ric^{L}(X,[Z,Y])

(G_{i},g)
admits left-invariant Ricci collineations associated to the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla^{L}
if and only if it satisfies

(L_{V}Ric^{L})(X,Y)=0
.
Further, conformal Ricci collineations can be discribed:

Theorem 2.1
.

(G_{i},g)
admits conformal Ricci collineations associated to the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla^{L}
if and only if it satisfies

(2.5)

(L_{V}Ric^{L})=2\lambda g,

where
V=\lambda_{1}e_{1}+\lambda_{2}e_{2}+\lambda_{3}e_{3}
is a left-invariant vector field and
\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\lambda_{3}
are real numbers.

2.1 Conformal Ricci collineations of
G_{1}

By
[
9
]
, we have the following Lie algebra of
G_{1}
satisfies

(2.6)

[e_{1},e_{2}]=\alpha e_{1}-\beta e_{3},~~[e_{1},e_{3}]=-\alpha e_{1}-\beta e_{2},~~[e_{2},e_{3}]=\beta e_{1}+\alpha e_{2}+\alpha e_{3},~~\alpha\neq 0.

where
e_{1},e_{2},e_{3}
is a pseudo-orthonormal basis, with
e_{3}
timelike.

Lemma 2.2
.

The Ricci tensor of
(G_{1},g)
associated to the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla^{L}
is determined by

(2.7)

\displaystyle Ric^{L}(e_{1},e_{1})=-\frac{\beta^{2}}{2},~~~Ric^{L}(e_{1},e_{2})=-\alpha\beta,~~~Ric^{L}(e_{1},e_{3})=\alpha\beta,

\displaystyle Ric^{L}(e_{2},e_{2})=-2\alpha^{2}-\frac{\beta^{2}}{2},~~~Ric^{L}(e_{2},e_{3})=2\alpha^{2},~~~Ric^{L}(e_{3},e_{3})=-2\alpha^{2}+\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}.

Let
V=\lambda_{1}e_{1}+\lambda_{2}e_{2}+\lambda_{3}e_{3}
is a left-invariant vector field, we can get the Lie derivative of the Ricci tensor

associated to
V

Lemma 2.3
.

(2.8)

\displaystyle(L_{V}Ric^{L})(e_{1},e_{1})=-3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{2}+3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{3},

\displaystyle(L_{V}Ric^{L})(e_{1},e_{2})=\frac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{1}-3\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{2}+3\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{3},

\displaystyle(L_{V}Ric^{L})(e_{1},e_{3})=-\frac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{1}+3\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{2}+\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{3},

\displaystyle(L_{V}Ric^{L})(e_{2},e_{2})=6\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{1}-3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{3},

\displaystyle(L_{V}Ric^{L})(e_{2},e_{3})=-6\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{1}+\dfrac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{2}+\dfrac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{3},

\displaystyle(L_{V}Ric^{L})(e_{3},e_{3})=6\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{1}-3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{2}.

Then, if
V
is a conformal Ricci collineation associated to the Levi-Civita connection, by Lemma 2.3 and Theorem 2.1, we have the following equations:

(2.9)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}-3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{2}+3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{3}=2\lambda\\[2.0pt]
\dfrac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{1}-3\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{2}+3\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{3}=0\\[2.0pt]
-\dfrac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{1}+3\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{2}+\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{3}=0\\[2.0pt]
6\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{1}-3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{3}=2\lambda\\[2.0pt]
-6\alpha^{2}\beta\lambda_{1}+\dfrac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{2}+\dfrac{3}{2}\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{3}=0\\[2.0pt]

case

(2.10)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}-3\alpha\beta^{2}\lambda_{2}=2\lambda\\[2.0pt]
