Abstract
Perturbed angular correlation spectroscopy combined with ab-initio electronic structure calculations is used to unravel the structural phase transition path from the low-temperature polar structure to the high-temperature structural phase in Ca 3 ​ Ti 2 ​ O 7 \text{Ca}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}\text{Ti}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{O}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}} , a hybrid improper ferroelectric. This procedure explores the unique features of a local probe environment approach by monitoring the evolution of the electric field gradient tensor at the calcium sites. The local environments, observed above 1057 K, confirm a structural phase transition from the A ​ 2 1 ​ a ​ m A2_{1}am symmetry to an orthorhombic A ​ c ​ a ​ a Acaa symmetry in the Ca 3 ​ Ti 2 ​ O 7 \text{Ca}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}\text{Ti}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{O}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}} crystal lattice, disagreeing with the frequently reported avalanche structural transition from the polar A ​ 2 1 ​ a ​ m A2_{1}am phase to the aristotype I ​ 4 / m ​ m ​ m I4/mmm phase. Moreover, the EFG temperature dependency, within the A ​ 2 1 ​ a ​ m A2_{1}am temperature stability, is shown to be sensitive to the recently proposed Ca 3 ​ Ti 2 ​ O 7 \text{Ca}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}\text{Ti}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{O}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}} ferroelectric polarization decrease within the 500-800 K temperature range.

Methodology

II.1
Experimental Details

Polycrystalline samples of

Computational Details

;

IV
Conclusions

PAC spectroscopy, combined with
ab-initio
calculations, has proven to be a valuable tool for probing the octahedral rotations of the hybrid improper ferroelectric
\text{Ca}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}\text{Ti}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{O}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
. Local probe experimental data was acquired in a broad temperature range, and
ab-initio
calculations were conducted to account for Cd ions substitutionally occupying the Ca sites in the
\text{Ca}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}\text{Ti}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{O}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
crystal lattice across distinct structural symmetries. The results revealed that Cd probes occupy both Ca rock-salt and Ca perovskite sites in the low temperature
A2_{1}am
phase. The EFG temperature dependency at the rock-salt calcium sites, within the
A2_{1}am
temperature stability, is also shown to be sensitive to the recently proposed
\text{Ca}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}\text{Ti}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{O}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
ferroelectric polarization sharp decrease within the 500-800 K temperature range. Furthermore, the analyses indicate that the structural phase transition of the low-temperature polar phase
A2_{1}am
, as the temperature increases, occurs at a temperature above 1057 K, leading to a crystalline structure of orthorhombic symmetry, which belongs to the space group
Acaa
, contrary to what has often been reported as an avalanche structural transition to the
I4/mmm
aristotype high-symmetry tetragonal phase. The
A2_{1}am
and the
Acaa
structural phases are shown to coexist up to 1219 K.
