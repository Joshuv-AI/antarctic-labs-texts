Abstract

We give an explicit upper and lower bounds for ℜ ∑ ρ 1 / ( s − ρ ) \Re\sum_{\rho}1/(s-\rho) , where the sum runs over the zeros of ζ ⁡ ( s ) \zeta(s) on the line 1 / 2 + i ​ t 1/2+it . For the complex

The functions

Zeros in the strip

The Riemann

The function

Lemma 8
.

If
a,\,b,\,\alpha>0
, then the following inequality holds

\int_{\alpha}^{t}\frac{\log\frac{u}{2\pi}\,du}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2}}\geqslant\frac{1}{ab}\log\left(\frac{t}{2\pi}\right)\arctan\left(\frac{b(t-\alpha)}{a}\right)-\kappa,

when
t>t_{0}\geqslant\alpha
, where
t_{0}
and constant
\kappa>0
are both sufficiently large and
\kappa
is independent on
t
.

In particular, if
a=1/2,\,b=1
and
\alpha=14.134725\ldots
, then the provided inequality holds if
t>23
and
\kappa=0.135
.

We set up the function

F(t)=\int_{\alpha}^{t}\frac{\log\frac{u}{2\pi}\,du}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2}}-\frac{1}{ab}\log\left(\frac{t}{2\pi}\right)\arctan\left(\frac{b(t-\alpha)}{a}\right)+\kappa

and show that
t
derivative
F^{\prime}(t)\geqslant 0
for

. Indeed, according to the Leibniz integral rule (see for example Mackevičius
[
12
]
or Spivak
[
20
]
)

\displaystyle F^{\prime}(t)

\displaystyle=2b^{2}\int_{\alpha}^{t}\frac{(u-t)\log u/2\pi\,du}{(a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2})^{2}}+\left(\frac{1}{a^{2}}-\frac{1}{a^{2}+b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2}}\right)\log\frac{t}{2\pi}

\displaystyle-\frac{\arctan(b(t-\alpha)/a)}{abt}.

The last integral is

\displaystyle 2b^{2}\int_{\alpha}^{t}\frac{(u-t)\log u/2\pi\,du}{(a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2})^{2}}=-\int_{\alpha}^{t}\log\frac{u}{2\pi}\,d\,\frac{1}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\log(\alpha/2\pi)}{a^{2}+b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2}}-\frac{\log(t/2\pi)}{a^{2}}+\int_{\alpha}^{t}\frac{du}{u(a^{2}+b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2})},

where

\displaystyle\int_{\alpha}^{t}\frac{du}{u(a^{2}+b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2})}=\frac{b^{2}}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}\int_{\alpha}^{t}\left(\frac{1}{b^{2}u}+\frac{2t-u}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2}}\right)du

\displaystyle=\frac{\log(t/\alpha)}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}+\frac{b}{a}\cdot\frac{t}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}\arctan\left(\frac{b(t-\alpha)}{a}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}\log\left(1+\frac{b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2}}{a^{2}}\right).

Therefore

\displaystyle F^{\prime}(t)=\frac{1/2}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}\log\left(\left(\frac{t}{\alpha}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{bt(t-\alpha)}{a\alpha}\right)^{2}\right)-\frac{\log(t/\alpha)}{a^{2}+b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2}}

\displaystyle-\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{1}{t}\cdot\frac{1}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}\arctan\left(\frac{b(t-\alpha)}{a}\right).

For
t\geqslant\alpha+a/b
, it holds that

\frac{bt(t-\alpha)}{a\alpha}\geqslant\frac{t}{\alpha},

and

\displaystyle F^{\prime}(t)

\displaystyle\geqslant\frac{\log\sqrt{2}}{a^{2}+b^{2}t^{2}}-\frac{(\alpha(2t-\alpha))\log(t/\alpha)}{(a^{2}+b^{2}t^{2})(a^{2}+b^{2}(t-\alpha)^{2})}

(7)

\displaystyle-\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{1}{t}\cdot\frac{1}{b^{2}t^{2}+a^{2}}\arctan\left(\frac{b(t-\alpha)}{a}\right).

The positive term of the right-hand side of inequality (

vanishes as

while the two negative terms as

if

t_{0}
is sufficiently large.

We next check whether
F(t_{0})\geqslant 0
. It is easy to see that

\lim_{t\to\alpha^{+}}F(t)=\kappa>0.

Therefore, due to continuity of

for at least

if
\kappa

is dependent on

For the particular case

Lemma 9
.

If
t>1
, then

\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{t}<\arctan t<\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{2t}.

The first inequality of (

\frac{\pi}{2}=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{1+x^{2}}=\int_{0}^{t}\frac{dx}{1+x^{2}}+\int_{t}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{1+x^{2}}>\arctan t+\int_{t}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{2}+x^{2}}=\arctan t+\frac{1}{2t}.

Lemma 10
.

Let

Then

For the lower bound, by elementary calculation and Lemmas

\displaystyle\int_{\alpha}^{\infty}\frac{\log(u/2\pi)\,du}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2}}=\left(\int_{\alpha}^{t}+\int_{t}^{\infty}\right)\frac{\log(u/2\pi)\,du}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u-t)^{2}}

\displaystyle<\left(\frac{\pi}{ab}+\frac{1}{2b^{2}}\right)\log\frac{t+1}{2\pi}+\frac{\log(t+1)}{b^{2}t}=\tilde{B}(t).

Lemma 11
.

Let
\alpha>0
and
b>a\geqslant 0
be a constants. For

\displaystyle\int_{\alpha}^{\infty}\frac{\log(u/2\pi)\,du}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u+t)^{2}}=\left(\int_{\alpha}^{t}+\int_{t}^{\infty}\right)\frac{\log(u/2\pi)\,du}{a^{2}+b^{2}(u+t)^{2}}
