Near-threshold scaling of resonant inelastic collisions at ultralow temperatures
Rebekah Hermsmeier 1 , Adrien Devolder 2 , Paul Brumer 2 , and Timur V. Tscherbul 1

Abstract
We show that the cross sections for a broad range of resonant inelastic processes accompanied by excitation exchange (such as spin-exchange, Förster resonant, or angular momentum exchange) exhibit an unconventional near-threshold scaling E Δ ​ m 12 E^{\Delta m_{12}} , where E E is the collision energy, Δ ​ m 12 = m 1 ′ + m 2 ′ − m 1 − m 2 \Delta m_{12}=m_{1}^{\prime}+m_{2}^{\prime}-m_{1}-m_{2} , and m i m_{i} and m i ′ m_{i}^{\prime} are the initial and final angular momentum projections of the colliding species ( i = 1 , 2 i=1,\,2 ). In particular, the inelastic cross sections for Δ ​ m 12 = 0 \Delta m_{12}=0 transitions display an unconventional E 0 E^{0} scaling similar to that of elastic cross sections, and their rates vanish as T Δ ​ m 12 + 1 / 2 T^{\Delta m_{12}+1/2} . For collisions dominated by even partial waves (such as those of identical bosons in the same internal state) the scaling is modified to σ inel ∝ E Δ ​ m 12 + 1 \sigma_{\text{inel}}\propto E^{\Delta m_{12}+1} if Δ ​ m 12 \Delta m_{12} is odd. We present accurate quantum scattering calculations that illustrate these modified threshold laws for resonant spin exchange in ultracold Rb + Rb and O 2 + O 2 collisions. Our results illustrate that the threshold scaling of collision cross sections is determined only by the energetics of the underlying process (resonant vs. exothermic) rather than by whether the internal states of colliding particles is changed in the collision.

In conclusion, we have shown that the near-threshold scaling of the ICS for resonant inelastic EE processes (such as spin exchange) is given by
\sigma_{\text{inel}}\propto E^{\Delta m_{12}}
and only depends on the difference between the combined angular momentum projections
\Delta m_{12}
in the incident and final collision channels. For
\Delta m_{12}=0
the scaling of the inelastic ICS is the same as that of the elastic ICS, i.e.,
\sigma_{\text{inel}}\propto E^{0}
. We have presented rigorous quantum scattering calculations to demonstrate the scaling for ultracold O
2
+ O
2
collisions in the experimentally accessible regimes.

Our work resolves the long-standing controversy concerning the threshold behavior of EE cross sections and confirms the correctness of the calculations reported in Refs.
Glassgold et al. 2005
;
Furlanetto and Furlanetto 2007
. More generally, it suggests that it is the energetics of a collisional EE process (resonant vs. exothermic) rather than the change in the internal states of collision partners, that determines the process’ threshold behavior.
Our results demonstrate a universal
T^{\Delta m_{12}+1/2}
suppression of a wide class of resonant EE processes at ultralow temperatures, which could be observed experimentally for, e.g., spin-exchange atom-atom collisions in optical tweezers
Schmidt et al. 2019
, atom-ion collisions in an optical lattice setup, which allows for high collision energy resolution
Ben-shlomi et al. 2021
, as well as in cold and ultracold collisions of Yb atoms
Uetake et al. 2012
, Ti atoms
Lu et al. 2009
, Rydberg atoms
Ryabtsev et al. 2010
;
Nipper et al. 2012
and polar molecules
Matsuda et al. 2020
;
Liu et al. 2020b
.
