Abstract
. In this paper, we mainly investigate on the finite order transcendental entire solutions of two Fermat types delay-differential and one Fermat type c c -shift equations, as these types were not considered earlier. Our results improve those of [ 13 ] in some sense. In addition, we also extend some recent results obtained in [ 18 ] . A handful number of examples have been provided by us to justify our certain assertion as and when required.
