2.5.3
New vectorfield method

To derive decay estimates for solutions of wave equations on a Kerr background one has to substantially refine the classical vectorfield method.
The new vectorfield method is an extension of the classical method which compensates for the lack of enough Killing and conformal Killing vectorfields in Kerr by introducing, new, cleverly designed, vectorfields whose deformation tensors have coercive properties in different regions of spacetime, not necessarily causal.
The method has emerged in the last 20 years in connection to the study of boundedness and decay for the scalar wave equation in Schwarzschild and Kerr, see section
3.2
for more details.

2.6
