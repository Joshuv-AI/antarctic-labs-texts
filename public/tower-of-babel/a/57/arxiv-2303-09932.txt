De Rham logarithmic classes and Tate conjecture
Johann Bouali

Abstract
We introduce the notion of De Rham logarithmic classes. We show that the De Rham class of an algebraic cycle of a smooth algebraic variety over a field of characteristic zero is logarithmic and conversely that a logarithmic class of bidegree ( d , d ) (d,d) is the De Rham class of an algebraic cycle (of codimension d d ). Moreover for smooth projective varieties, we show that there are non trivial logarithmic classes of bidegree ( p , q ) (p,q) for p ≠ q p\neq q . For smooth algebraic varieties over a p p -adic field, we also give an analytic version of this result. We deduce, from the analytic version, the Tate conjecture for smooth projective varieties over fields of finite type over ℚ \mathbb{Q} and over p p -adic fields for ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} coefficients, under good reduction hypothesis.

Introduction

1.1
De Rham logarithmic classes

In this work, we introduce in a first part for
X
a noetherian scheme, the notion of logarithmic De Rham cohomology classes
which are for each
j\in\mathbb{Z}
, the elements of the subgroup

H^{j}OL_{X}(\mathbb{H}^{j}_{et}(X,\Omega^{\bullet}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits}))\subset\mathbb{H}^{j}_{et}(X,\Omega_{X}^{\bullet})=:H^{j}_{DR}(X)

of the De Rham cohomology abelian group, where
OL_{X}:\Omega^{\bullet}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits}\hookrightarrow\Omega_{X}^{\bullet}

is the subcomplex of abelian sheaves on
X
consisting of logarithmic forms introduced in definition
2
.
Our main result is that if
X
is a smooth algebraic variety over a field of characteristic zero, a de Rham cohomology class
which is logarithmic of type
(d,d)
is the class of an algebraic cycle of codimension
d
.
More precisely, let
X
be a smooth algebraic variety over a field
k
of characteristic zero.
To each algebraic cycle
Z\in\mathcal{Z}^{d}(X)
, we associate its De Rham cohomology class
[Z]\in H^{2d}_{DR}(X)
,
which is by definition, as for any Weil cohomology theory, the image of the fundamental class
[Z]\in H^{2d}_{DR,Z}(X)

by the canonical morphism
H^{2d}_{DR,Z}(X)\to H^{2d}(X)
.
In section 3, we show (c.f. theorem
2
(i)) that

•

for
Z\in\mathcal{Z}^{d}(X)
,
[Z]\in H^{2d}_{DR}(X)
is logarithmic of bidegree
(d,d)
, that is

[Z]=H^{2d}OL_{X}([Z]_{L})\in H^{2d}_{DR}(X),\;[Z]_{L}\in H^{d}_{et}(X,\Omega^{d}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits})

where
H^{d}_{et}(X,\Omega^{d}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits})\subset\mathbb{H}^{2d}_{et}(X,\Omega^{\bullet}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits})
is the canonical subspace,
as all the differentials of
\Omega^{\bullet}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits}
vanishes since by definition a logarithmic form is closed.
This fact is a consequence of the fact that
motivic isomorphisms applied to De Rham cohomology preserve logarithmic classes (c.f. proposition
1
).
The key point is that logarithmic De Rham forms
on algebraic varieties over
k
are closed, (trivially)
\mathbb{A}_{k}^{1}
invariant
and compatible with the transfers maps induced by finite morphisms
of algebraic varieties and in particular finite correspondences.
Since
\Omega^{l}_{\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits}\subset\Omega^{l}
are (trivially)
\mathbb{A}_{k}^{1}
invariant
presheaves with transfers on the category of smooth algebraic varieties over
k
, by a theorem of Voevodsky
the cohomology presheaves of
\mathbb{A}_{k}^{1}
invariant presheaves with transfers are
\mathbb{A}_{k}^{1}
invariant.

•

Conversely, we show (c.f. theorem
2
(ii)),
that a logarithmic class
w\in H^{2d}_{DR}(X)
of bidegree
(d,d)
is the De Rham class of an algebraic cycle
(of codimension
d
).
In the case
X
projective, we also get a vanishing result (c.f. theorem
2
(iii)’) :

H^{2d}OL_{X}(H^{d-k}_{et}(X,\Omega^{d+k}_{X,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits}))=0
for
k>0
.

The proof works as follows :
A logarithmic class of bidegree
(p,q)
is locally acyclic for the Zariski topology of
X
since
it is the etale cohomology of a single sheaf. This allows us to proceed by a finite induction
using the crucial fact that the purity isomorphism for De Rham cohomology preserve logarithmic classes
(c.f. proposition
6
). The proof of proposition
6
follows from the fact
that the purity isomorphism is motivic (c.f.
[
5
]
, see proposition
1
), that
the Euler class of a vector bundle of rank
d

over an algebraic variety is logarithmic of bidegree
(d,d)
(c.f. proposition
5
)
and that the motivic isomorphisms applied to De Rham cohomology preserve logarithmic classes
(c.f. proposition
4
). At the final step, we use, for simplicity,
the fact that for a scheme
Y
,
H^{1}_{et}(Y,\Omega^{1}_{Y,\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits})=H^{1}(Y,O^{*}_{Y})
is the Picard group of
Y
.

For
X
an algebraic variety over a
p
-adic field, we also introduce the notion of
logarithmic analytic De Rham cohomology classes. In proposition
8

we give an analytic analogue of theorem
2
,
that is if
X
is a smooth projective variety over a
p
-adic field with good reduction,

•

a logarithmic analytic De Rham cohomology class of type
(d,d)
is the class of a codimension
d
algebraic cycle,

•

there are no non trivial logarithmic analytic De Rham cohomology classes of type
(p,q)
for
p\neq q
, i.e.

H^{2d}OL_{\hat{X}}(H^{d-k}_{pet}(X,\Omega^{d+k}_{\hat{X}^{(p)},\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits,\mathcal{O}}))=0
for
k\neq 0
.

The proof of proposition

is motivic and similar to the proof of theorem
2
:
a logarithmic analytic class of bidegree
(p,q)
is acyclic for the pro-etale topology on each open subset

such that there exists an etale map
U^{\mathcal{O}}\to T^{d_{U}}\subset\mathbb{A}_{O_{K}}^{d_{U}}
since
it is the pro etale cohomology of a single subsheaf of the De Rham complex (proposition
2
),
this allows us to proceed by a finite induction
using the crucial fact that the purity isomorphism for De Rham cohomology preserve logarithmic analytic classes
since the purity isomorphism is motivic (c.f.
[
5
]
, see proposition

).
The GAGA comparison theorem for de Rham cohomology of
X
induce,

1.2
Tate conjecture

Let
X
be a smooth projective variety over a field
k
of finite type over
\mathbb{Q}
.
Let
p
be a prime number unramified over
k
such that
X
has good reduction at
p
,

\sigma_{p}:k\hookrightarrow\mathbb{C}_{p}
a fixed embedding and
\bar{k}
the algebraic closure of
k
inside
\mathbb{C}_{p}
.
We show the Tate conjecture for
X
with
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
coefficient (c.f. theorem
3
).
The proof works as follows.
Denote by
\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}\subset\mathbb{C}_{p}
the
p
adic completion of
k
with respect to
\sigma_{p}
.
We then consider a smooth proper scheme
X^{\mathcal{O}}_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}}
over
O_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}}

which is an integral model of
X
, i.e. such that

X^{\mathcal{O}}\times_{O_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}}}\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}=X_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}}
.
Let
\alpha\in H_{et}^{2d}(X_{\bar{k}},\mathbb{Z}_{p})(d)^{G}
be a Tate class of
X

In proposition
9
, we show,
using a result of
[
6
]
together with results of
[
9
]
on the pro-etale topology,
that
\alpha
gives by the
p
-adic crystalline comparison isomorphism

H^{2d}R\alpha(X):H_{et}^{2d}(X_{\mathbb{C}_{p}},\mathbb{Z}_{p})\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\mathbb{B}_{cris,\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}}\xrightarrow{\sim}H^{2d}_{DR}(X)\otimes_{k}\mathbb{B}_{cris,\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}},

an analytic logarithmic de Rham class

w(\alpha):=H^{2d}R\alpha(X)(\alpha)\in H^{2d}OL_{X}\mathbb{H}_{pet}^{2d}(X_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}},\Omega^{\bullet\geq d}_{\hat{X}^{(p)}_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}},\mathop{\rm log}\nolimits,\mathcal{O}}).

Proposition
8
then implies that
w(\alpha)=[Z]\in H^{2d}_{DR}(X_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}})
,
is the de Rham cohomology class of an algebraic cycle

Z\in\mathcal{Z}^{d}(X_{\hat{k}_{\sigma_{p}}})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_{p}
of codimension
d
.
By the
p
-adic crystalline comparison isomorphism, we get
\alpha=[Z]\in H_{et}^{2d}(X_{\mathbb{C}_{p}},\mathbb{Q}_{p})
.
Since
\alpha
is
G
invariant, we get

\alpha=[Z]=[Z^{\prime}]\in H_{et}^{2d}(X_{\mathbb{C}_{p}},\mathbb{Q}_{p}),Z^{\prime}:=(1/\#gZ)\sum_{g\in G\cdot Z}gZ\in\mathcal{Z}^{d}(X)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_{p}.

Note that for a
p
-adic field
K\subset\mathbb{C}_{p}
and
X
a smooth projective variety over
K
,

\mathop{\rm dim}\nolimits H_{et}^{j}(X_{\mathbb{C}_{p}},\mathbb{Z}_{l})^{G}>\mathop{\rm dim}\nolimits H_{et}^{j}(X_{\mathbb{C}_{p}},\mathbb{Z}_{p})^{G}
for
l\neq p
in general.
Hence it is not clear whether Tate conjecture holds for smooth projective varieties over finite fields
k_{0}
,
since the base change for etale cohomology for
f:X\to O_{K}
,
O_{K}=W(k_{0})

only holds for
\mathbb{Z}_{l}
coefficients,
l\neq p
.

Let
X
be a smooth projective variety over
\mathbb{C}
.
Then
X
is defined over a subfield
k\subset\mathbb{C}
of finite type over
\mathbb{Q}
, that is
X=X_{k}\otimes_{k}\mathbb{C}
.
Take an isomorphism
\mathbb{C}\simeq\mathbb{C}_{p}

with
p\in\mathbb{N}
a prime number such that
X_{\mathbb{C}_{p}}
has good reduction at
p
.
As Tate conjecture holds for
X_{k}
with
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
coefficients, the standard conjectures holds for
X

and any absolute Hodge class of
X
is the class of an algebraic cycle (corollary
2
).
