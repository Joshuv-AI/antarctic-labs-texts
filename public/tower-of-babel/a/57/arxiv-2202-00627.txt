Introduction and main results

Many partition functions have infinite products as generating function.
Numerous researchers have investigated
their asymptotic behaviors and combinatorial properties.
Let

Lemma 3.1
.

Let

m_{1}+\ldots+m_{k}=n\geq 2

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 3^{n/3}\text{ for }n\equiv 0\mod 3,

\displaystyle\max_{k\leq n}\max_{\begin{subarray}{c}m_{1},\ldots,m_{k}\geq 1\\

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\cdot 3^{\left(n-4\right)/3}\text{ for }n\equiv 1\mod 3,

\displaystyle\max_{k\leq n}\max_{\begin{subarray}{c}m_{1},\ldots,m_{k}\geq 1\\

\displaystyle 2\cdot 3^{\left(n-2\right)/3}\text{ for }n\equiv 2\mod 3.

Proof.

Let

and

so

We can assume that

. Note that we
