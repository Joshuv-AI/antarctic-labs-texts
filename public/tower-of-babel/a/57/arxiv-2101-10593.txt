Characterization of the pseudo-scaling functions on Vilenkin group
Prasadini Mahapatra

Abstract
The study of wavelets, originated from it’s applications in diverse fields, was combined together by it’s Mathematical properties. Initially, all the wavelets and it’s variants were explored in the real space ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} . But now, these are being studied in different abstract settings. The present paper also contributes to this extension. Vilenkin groups, introduced by F. Ya Vilenkin, form a class of locally compact abelian groups. In this paper, Parseval frame multiwavelets associated to multiresolution analysis (MRA) are characterized in L 2 ​ ( G ) L^{2}(G) , where G G is the Vilenkin group. Further, we introduce the pseudo-scaling function along with a class of generalized low pass filters and study their properties in Vilenkin group.

Preliminaries

Vilenkin group

x=(x_{j})=(...,0,0,x_{k},x_{k+1},x_{k+2},...),

(z_{j})=(x_{j})\oplus(y_{j})\Leftrightarrow z_{j}=x_{j}+y_{j}(\text{mod}\>p),\text{ for }j\in\mathbb{Z}.

\theta

.
Let

U_{l}=\{(x_{j})\in G:x_{j}=0\>\text{for}\>j\leq l\},\qquad l\in\mathbb{Z},

\{U_{l}\}_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}
of zero, then we can determine neighbourhood system of every point

, which in turn generates a topology on

.

Let

and
\ominus
denotes the inverse operation of
\oplus
. The Lebesgue spaces

\mu(U)=1

The group dual to

is denoted by

and consists of all sequences of the form

\omega=(\omega_{j})=(...,0,0,\omega_{k},\omega_{k+1},\omega_{k+2},...),

where
\omega_{j}\in\{0,1,...,p-1\}
, for
j\in\mathbb{Z}
and
\omega_{j}=0
, for
j<k=k(\omega)
. The operations of addition and subtraction, the neighbourhoods

and the Haar measure

for

are defined as above for

\chi(x,\omega)=\exp\bigg(\frac{2\pi i}{p}\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}{x_{j}w_{1-j}}\bigg),\quad x\in G,

for some

.

Let

be a discrete subgroup in

be an automorphism on

(Ax)_{j}=x_{j+1}

Let

be defined by

\lambda(x)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}{x_{j}p^{-j}},\qquad x=(x_{j})\in G.

It is obvious that the image of

is the set of non-negative integers

. For every

The generalised Walsh functions for

For positive integers

1.2.1
Refinable function and mask

Definition 1.1
.

Let

be the set of all compactly supported functions in
L^{2}(G)

\phi\in L_{c}^{2}(G)
is said to be a refinable function, if it satisfies an equation of the type

\phi(x)=p\sum_{\alpha=0}^{p^{n}-1}{a_{\alpha}\phi(Ax\ominus h_{[\alpha]})}.

(1)

The above functional equation is called the refinement equation. The generalized Walsh polynomial

m(\omega)=\sum_{\alpha=0}^{p^{n}-1}{a_{\alpha}\overline{W_{\alpha}^{*}{(\omega)}}}

(2)

is called the mask of the refinement equation (or the mask of its solution
\phi
).

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
\phi\in L_{c}^{2}{(G)}
be a solution of the refinement equation, and let
\widehat{\phi}(\theta)=1
. Then,

\sum_{\alpha=0}^{p^{n}-1}{a_{\alpha}}=1,\qquad\text{supp }\phi\subset U_{1-n},

and

\widehat{\phi}(\omega)=\prod_{j=1}^{\infty}{m(B^{-j}\omega)}.

Moreover, the following properties are true:

1
.
\widehat{\phi}(h^{*})=0
, for all
h^{*}\in H^{\perp}\setminus{\{\theta\}}
(the modified Strang-Fix condition),

2
.
\sum_{h\in H}{\phi(x\oplus h)}=1
, for almost every
x\in G
(the partition of unit property).

Definition 1.3
.

A set
M\subset U^{*}
is said to be blocked (for the mask
m
) if it coincides with some union of the sets
U_{n-1,s}^{*}
,
0\leq s\leq p^{n-1}-1
, does not contain the set
U_{n-1,0}^{*}
, and satisfies the condition

T_{p}{M}\subset M\cup\{\omega\in U^{*}:m(\omega)=0\},

where

T_{p}M=\bigcup_{l=0}^{p-1}\{B^{-l}\omega_{[l]}+B^{-1}(\omega):\omega\in M\}.

1.2.2
Multiresolution analysis

Definition 1.4
.

A collection of closed subspaces
V_{j}\subset L^{2}{(G)},j\in\mathbb{Z}
, is called a Multiresolution analysis (MRA) in
L^{2}{(G)}
if the following hold:

(i)
V_{j}\subset V_{j+1}
, for all

(ii)
\overline{\cup_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}V_{j}}=L^{2}{(G)}
and
\cap_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}V_{j}=\{0\}

(iii)
f(\cdot)\in V_{j}\Leftrightarrow f(A\cdot)\in V_{j+1}
, for all
j\in\mathbb{Z}

(iv)
f(\cdot)\in V_{0}\Rightarrow f(\cdot\ominus h)\in V_{0}
, for all
h\in H

(v) there is a function
\phi\in L^{2}{(G)}
such that the system
\{\phi(\cdot\ominus h)|h\in H\}
is an orthonormal basis of
V_{0}
.

The function
\phi
in condition (v) is called a scaling function of the MRA
(V_{j})_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}

For

\phi_{j,h}(x)=p^{j/2}{\phi(A^{j}{x}\ominus h)},\qquad j\in\mathbb{Z},\;\;h\in H

and the system

forms an orthonormal basis of
V_{j}
, for every

.

Theorem 1.5
.

A function

is a scaling function for an MRA of

if and only if

\sum_{h\in H^{\perp}}|\hat{\phi}(\omega\oplus h)|^{2}=1

,   for a.e

\hat{\phi}(B\omega)=m(\omega)\hat{\phi}(\omega)
,   for a.e
\omega\in G^{*}
.

A function
