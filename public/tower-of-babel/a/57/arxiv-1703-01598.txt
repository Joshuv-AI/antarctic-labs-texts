SLOCC classification of n qubits invoking the proportional relationships for spectrums and for standard Jordan normal forms
Dafa Li 1,2

Abstract
We investigate the proportional relationships for spectrums and for SJNFs (Standard Jordan Normal Forms) of the matrices constructed from coefficient matrices of two SLOCC (stochastic local operations and classical communication) equivalent states of n n qubits. Invoking the proportional relationships for spectrums and for SJNFs, pure states of n n ( ≥ 4 \geq 4 ) qubits are partitioned into 12 groups and 34 families under SLOCC, respectively. Specially, it is true for four qubits.

In Theorem 1, we demonstrate that for two SLOCC equivalent states, the
spectrums and SJNFs of the matrices
S_{q_{1}q_{2}}^{(n)}

relationships. Invoking the proportional relationships, we partition pure

respectively.

SLOCC operations, the spectrums, CPs, SJNFs of

invariant. The invariance can be used for SLOCC classification of
n
