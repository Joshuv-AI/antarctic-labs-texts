To B B or not to be in single-trace T ​ T ¯ T\bar{T} holography


Abstract
Superstring theory on black-strings backgrounds, corresponding to deformed, rotating BTZ black holes, formed by p p fundamental strings or negative strings, is inspected. For non -rotating black strings, in the positive case, it was shown in [ 1 ] that the excitation energy of a probe long string, plus its contribution to the black-string background, is in harmony with single-trace T ​ T ¯ T\bar{T} holography, if the B B -field vanishes at the origin. Here, we investigate the superstring on deformed, rotating BTZ black holes, for both the positive and negative cases, and find that for a particular value of the B B -field, the harmony with single-trace T ​ T ¯ T\bar{T} holography persists.
