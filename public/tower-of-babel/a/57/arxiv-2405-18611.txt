A better bound on blow-up rate for the superconformal semilinear wave equation
Mohamed Ali Hamza

Abstract
We consider the semilinear wave equation in higher dimensions with superconformal power nonlinearity. The purpose of this paper is to give a new upper bound on the blow-up rate in some space-time integral, showing a | log ⁡ ( T − t ) | q |\log(T-t)|^{q} improvement in comparison with previous results obtained in [ 16 , 18 ] .



Keywords:
Semilinear wave equation, finite time blow-up,
blow-up rate,
superconformal exponent.

AMS classification :
35L05, 35L67, 35B20.

Introduction

1.1
Motivation of the problem

This paper is devoted to the study of blow-up solutions for the
following semilinear wave equation:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\partial_{t}^{2}u=\Delta u+|u|^{p-1}u,\\
\\
(u(x,0),\partial_{t}u(x,0))=(u_{0}(x),u_{1}(x))\in H^{1}_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\times L^{2}_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^{N}),\\
\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

in space dimension
N\geq 2
, where
u(t):x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow u(x,t)\in\mathbb{R}
,

p_{c}<p<p_{S},

(1.2)

p_{c}\equiv 1+\frac{4}{N-1}
is the conformal critical exponent and
p_{S}\equiv 1+\frac{4}{N-2}
if
N\geq 3

is the Sobolev critical exponent.

The Cauchy problem of equation (
1.1
) is solved in

H^{1}_{loc}\times L^{2}_{loc}
. This follows from the finite speed
of propagation and the wellposedness in
H^{1}\times L^{2}
,
valid whenever
1<p<p_{S}
. Combining the blow-up solutions for the associated ordinary differential equation of (
1.1
) and
the finite speed of
propagation, we conclude that there exists a
blow-up solution
u(t)
of (
1.1
) which depends non-trivially on the space variable.
Numerical simulations of blow-up are given by Bizoń
et al.
in
[
3
,
4
,
5
]
.

If
u
is an arbitrary blow-up solution of (
1.1
), we define
a 1-Lipschitz curve
\Gamma=\{(x,T(x))\}

such that
the domain of definition of
u
is written as

D=\{(x,t)\;|\;t<T(x)\}.

(1.3)

The justification of this fact can be found in Alinhac

[
1
]
, where such a set is referred to as the
maximal influence domain
.
In fact, for any
x_{0}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}
, we derive a solution
which is defined in
K_{x_{0},t_{0}}
, the backward light cone
defined by

K_{x_{0},t_{0}}=\{(\xi,\tau)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}\times[0,t_{0})\;|\;|\xi-x_{0}|<t_{0}-\tau\}.

Denoting by
T(x_{0})
the supremum of such a
t_{0}
, we fall into one of
these two situations:

- either for some
x_{1}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}
,
T(x_{1})=+\infty
, and in that case,
the solution if defined for all
x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}
and
t\geq 0
. In other
words,
T(x_{0})=+\infty
, for any
x_{0}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}
;

- or, for all
x_{1}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}
,
T(x_{1})<+\infty
, and this is the case we
are interested in. In particular, the domain of definition of
u

is the union of backward light cones of the type
K_{x_{0},t_{0}}
, and
such a union is clearly of the form (
1.3
) with
x\mapsto T(x)

is
1-
Lipschitz. See also Sasaki
[
22
]
for a related definition of the
domain of definition in some similar setting.

The time

\bar{T}=\inf_{x\in{{\mathbb{R}}^{N}}}T(x)
and
\Gamma
are called the blow-up
time and the blow-up graph of
u
.
A point
x_{0}
is non characteristic
if there are

\delta_{0}\in(0,1)\mbox{ and }t_{0}<T(x_{0})\mbox{ such that }u\;\;\mbox{is defined on }{\mathscr{C}}_{x_{0},T(x_{0}),\delta_{0}}\cap\{t\geq t_{0}\},

where
{\cal C}_{\bar{x},\bar{t},\bar{\delta}}=\{(x,t)\;|\;t<\bar{t}-\bar{\delta}|x-\bar{x}|\}
. If not,
x_{0}
is said to be characteristic.

When the exponent

is conformal or subconformal, i.e. when

1<p\leq p_{c},

Merle and Zaag proved in
[

,
21
,
20

that the blow-up rate of equation (

) is
C(T-t)^{-\frac{2}{p-1}}
, the solution of the associated ODE
u"=u^{p}
.
More precisely,
they proved that if

is a solution of (
1.1
)
with blow-up graph
\Gamma:\{x\mapsto T(x)\}
and

is a
non-characteristic point, then, for all

\displaystyle 0<\varepsilon_{0}(p)\leq(T(x_{0})-t)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}\frac{\|u(t)\|_{L^{2}(B(x_{0},T(x_{0})-t))}}{(T(x_{0})-t)^{\frac{N}{2}}}

(1.6)

\displaystyle+(T(x_{0})-t)^{\frac{2}{p-1}+1}\Big(\frac{\|\partial_{t}u(t)\|_{L^{2}(B(x_{0},T(x_{0})-t))}}{(T(x_{0})-t)^{\frac{N}{2}}}+\frac{\|\partial_{x}u(t)\|_{L^{2}(B(x_{0},T(x_{0})-t))}}{{(T(x_{0})-t)^{\frac{N}{2}}}}\Big)\leq K_{0},

,

,

in

In

]
, using a highly non-trivial perturbative method, we could obtain the blow-up rate for the

\partial_{t}^{2}u=\Delta u+|u|^{p-1}u+f(u)+g(\partial_{t}u),\,\,\,(x,t)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N}\times[0,T),

under the assumptions

In fact, we proved a similar result to

We would like to mention the result of Donninger and Schörkhuber in

[

in 3 space dimensions in the subcritical range

Later, they extended their results to the superconformal range

Later, by establishing

Let us focus now on the superconformal and Sobolev subcritical range
(

), i.e. when

(1.8)

To the best of our knowledge, the first available result is due to

Furthermore, if
