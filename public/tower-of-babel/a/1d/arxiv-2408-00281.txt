The “Galois Correspondence” for n n -Stacks
Yuxiang Yao

Abstract
. We prove an essentially surjective Galois-correspondence-like functor for n n -stacks. More specifically, it gives an essentially surjective functor from the ∞ \infty -category of n n -stacks of finite sets with an action of the fundamental group of X X to the ∞ \infty -category of Deligne-Mumford n n -stacks étale over a connected scheme X X .



1.
Introduction

In this note, we will introduce an essentially surjective Galois-correspondence-like functor for
n
-stacks. Our motivation is to have an explicit write-up for such a functor for Deligne-Mumford stacks that are finite étale over a nice scheme
X
. But the literature actually deduces a more general version for Deligne-Mumford
n
-stacks rather than only for usual stacks. So we instead build the note for a more general situation. Let’s first state our main theorem for groupoids below:

Theorem 1

(Galois Correspondence for
n
-groupoids)
.

Let
\eta
be a geometric point in a connected scheme
X
. There is an exact equivalence of categories of fibrant objects

F_{\eta}:n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FEt}_{X})\xrightarrow{\sim}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FinSets}).

Here
n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FEt}_{X})
is the
1
-category of
n
-groupoids in
\mathrm{FEt}_{X}
, the category of finite étale (surjective) covers over
X
; and
\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FinSets})
is the category of
n
-groupoids in finite sets with the étale fundamental group,
\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)
, acting on them.

This will be proved in Section
4
. Then a weaker version of the above result for
n
-stacks can be considered as a corollary of the theorem above by simplicial localization. This will be concluded in Section
4
after the proof of the main theorem and a bit more prerequisites. Note that
n
-groupoids can be considered as a nice device for presenting stacks, see
[
P2013
]
. Also, note that we cannot say the following functor is an equivalence of simplicial categories. The subtleties will be explained in the Proof of Corollary

Corollary 2
.

Let
\eta
be a geometric point in a connected scheme

G^{\mathcal{L}}_{\eta}:\pi^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}_{1}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Stacks(FinSets)}\rightarrow\mathrm{DM}\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Stacks}/\mathrm{\acute{e}tale}\text{ }\mathrm{over}\text{ }X

Here,

is given by the simplicial localization of étale
n

\mathrm{Et}(X)

\pi^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}_{1}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Stacks(FinSets)}

\pi^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}_{1}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FinSets})

Note that the category DM-

The following functor, called the fiber functor,

The Category of

For this section, we take Behrend and Getzler

Definition 4

(Kernel pairs and Effective Epimorphisms)
.

(1)

The

of a morphism

X\times_{Y}X\rightrightarrows X.

(2)

The map

is an

if

is the coequalizer of this pair

From now, we will work in the category

with the following axioms:

Definition 5

(Category with covers and finite limits)
.

A

\mathcal{C}
with covers and finite limits
consists of a category
\mathcal{C}
with a subcategory of covers satisfying the following five axioms:

(\mathrm{C0})

\mathcal{C}
has finite limits.

(\mathrm{C1})

The category
\mathcal{C}
has a terminal object
e
, and the map
X\to e
is a cover for every object
X\in\mathcal{C}
.

(\mathrm{C2})

Pullbacks of covers along arbitrary maps exist and are covers.

(\mathrm{C3})

If
g
and
f
are composable, and
f
and
gf
are both covers, then
g
is also a cover.

(\mathrm{C4})

Covers are effective epimorphisms.

Remark 6
.

This is a union of the axioms in “the category with covers” in Wolfson
[
W16
]
and the “descent category” in Behrend and Getzler
[
BG17
]
. Then we are able to make use of results in both papers for the above category

. Those categories we care about in this note will also fortunately fit into the two categories in both papers.

We define that
[k]=\{0<1<\cdots<n\}
is a nonempty linearly ordered set, and
\Delta
is a category whose objects are such

, and morphisms are nondecreasing maps; define
\Delta^{k}:=\mathrm{Mor}_{\Delta}(-,[k])
. A simplicial object in
\mathcal{C}
is a functor
X_{\bullet}:\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal{C}
, and a simplicial set
S
is a simplicial object in
\mathcal{C}=\mathrm{Sets}
. Equivalently, a simplicial set
S
can be written as the colimit of its simplices

S\cong\text{colim}_{\Delta^{k}\to S}\Delta^{k}.

We will also need the following simplicial subsets of
\Delta^{k}
:

•

The boundary
\partial\Delta^{k}\subset\Delta^{k}
can be defined by
(\partial\Delta^{k})([m])=\{\alpha\in\mathrm{Mor}_{\Delta}([m],[k])\>:\>\alpha\text{ is not surjective}\}
.

•

The
i
-th horn
\Lambda_{i}^{k}\subset\partial\Delta^{k}
can be defined as
(\Lambda_{i}^{k})([m])=\{\alpha\in\mathrm{Mor}_{\Delta}([m],[k])\>:\>[k]\not\subseteq\alpha([m])\cup\{i\}\}
.

Let
X
be a simplicial object in
\mathcal{C}
, in other words, a functor
X:\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal{C}
; and let
S
be a simplical set. Dual to the colimit expression of a simplicial set, we define

\textrm{Hom}(S,X):=\lim_{\Delta^{k}\to S}X_{k}.

Definition 7

(
n
-groupoids, Definition 2.5, Wolfson)
.

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty\}
. A
n
-groupoid
is a simplicial object

such that for all
k>0
and

,
\Lambda_{i}^{k}(X)
exists in
\mathcal{C}
and the induced map

\lambda_{i}^{k}(X):X_{k}\to{\rm{Hom}}(\Lambda_{i}^{k},X)

is a cover, and it is also an isomorphism for

.

Here the map
\lambda_{i}^{k}(X)
is induced from the simplicial subset
\Lambda_{i}^{k}\subset\Delta^{k}
.

Definition 8

(
n
-Hypercovers, Definition 2.6, Wolfson)
.

(1)

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty\}
. A map

of simplicial objects in
\mathrm{s}\mathcal{C}
is an
n
-hypercover
if, for all
k\geq 0
, the map

\mu_{k}(f):X_{k}\to{\rm{Hom}}(\partial\Delta^{k},X)\times_{{\rm{Hom}}(\partial\Delta^{k},Y)}Y_{k}

is a cover for all
k
, and it is an isomorphism for
k\geq n
.

Here, the pullback on the right is given by two induced maps to
{\rm{Hom}}(\partial\Delta^{k},Y)
from (1)
f:X\to Y
and (2)
\partial\Delta^{k}\subset\Delta^{k}
; and the induced map
\lambda_{k}(f)
is determined by the map
f_{k}:X_{k}\to Y_{k}
and another map
X\to{\rm{Hom}}(\partial\Delta^{k},X)
induced again by
\partial\Delta^{k}\subset\Delta^{k}
.

(2)

We refer to an
\infty
-hypercover simply as a
hypercover
.

4.
The Main Theorem and Its Corollary

We now prove our main theorem.

Theorem 20

(Galois Correspondence for
n
-groupoids)
.

There is an equivalence of categories

F_{\eta}:n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FEt}_{X})\xrightarrow{\sim}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FinSets}).

Proof.

By Theorem
3
,

F^{\mathrm{Sch}}_{\eta}:\mathrm{FEt}_{X}\to\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}\mathrm{FinSets}

is a category equivalence. By Lemma
13
, the functor

n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(F^{\mathrm{Sch}}_{\eta}):n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FEt}_{X})\to n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}\mathrm{FinSets})

is an equivalence. By Lemma
19
, there is an equivalence

G:n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}\mathrm{FinSets})\xrightarrow{\sim}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\eta)\text{-}n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(\mathrm{FinSets}).

Define
F_{\eta}=G\circ\left(n\text{-}\mathrm{Grpds}(F^{\mathrm{Sch}}_{\eta})\right)
, this gives the equivalence we want.

Finally, since
F^{\mathrm{Sch}}_{\eta}
in Theorem
3
preserves covers, by Lemma
13
,
F_{\eta}
is an equivalence.
∎
