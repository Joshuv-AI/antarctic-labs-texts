W-exchange contribution in hadronic decays of bottom baryon
Fayyazuddin

Abstract




Department of Physics, Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad, Pakistan.

The nonleptonic decays of
\varLambda_{b}
that are dominated by

W
exchange are studied. In particular, the decay modes
\varLambda_{b}\to\varDelta^{0}D^{0},\varDelta^{-}D^{+},\Sigma^{*-}D_{s}^{+}
,

\Lambda_{b}\to\Sigma_{c}^{*+}\pi^{-},\Sigma_{c}^{*0}\pi^{0},\Xi_{c}^{*0}K^{0}

and
\varLambda_{b}\to\Sigma_{c}^{+}\pi^{-}
are analyzed. In an
another aspect, the decay
\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda_{c}^{+}\pi^{-}

in the factorization anstaz is studied. It is shown that factorization
contributes to parity violating (s-wave) amplitude
A
only. Hence
factorization gives asymmetry parameter
\alpha=0
. However, the
dominant contribution to parity conserving (p-wave) amplitude
B

comes from
W
exchange, i.e., from baryon pole, giving asymmetry
parameter
\alpha=-0.77
.

1
Introduction

In the standard model (SM), two body hadronic decays for the heavy flavor are analyzed in terms of the effective Hamiltonian
[
1
,
2
]

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}_{eff}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle V_{cb}V^{*}_{cs}[a_{1}(\bar{s}c)_{V-A}(\bar{c}b)_{V-A}+a_{2}(\bar{c}c)_{V-A}(\bar{s}b)_{V-A}]

(1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle V_{cb}V^{*}_{ud}[a_{1}(\bar{d}u)_{V-A}(\bar{c}b)_{V-A}+a_{2}(\bar{c}u)_{V-A}(\bar{d}b)_{V-A}]

(2)

where

\displaystyle a_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{1}+\frac{1}{3}C_{2}:\quad\quad\quad\quad\text{tree diagram}

\displaystyle a_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{2}+\frac{1}{3}C_{1}:\quad\quad\quad\quad\text{color suppressed tree diagram}

(3)

The Hamiltonian
1
arises from the transition
b\to c+s+\bar{c}
and
2
from the transition
b\to c+d+\bar{u}
. Only the Hamiltonian
1
is relevant for the decays of bottom baryon
\Lambda_{b}
which is studied in this paper. Both these Hamiltonian correspond to the decays which are not Cabibbo suppressed.

It is clear that
(\bar{c}b)_{V-A}
and
(\bar{s}b)_{V-A}
in Eq. (
1
) belong to the singlet and triplet representations of
SU(3)
, respectively. Thus in the factorization ansatz, only possible decays of
\Lambda_{b}
belonging to
\bar{3}
representation of
SU(3)
are
\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda_{c}^{+}D^{-}_{s},\;\Lambda_{c}^{+}\pi^{-}
for the first term in Eq. (
1
). For the second term, since
\bar{3}\times 3=8+1
, the possible decay mode of
\Lambda_{b}
is
\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda J/\psi
, as
\Lambda
belongs to octet representation of
SU(3)
.

Hence for the decay
\Lambda_{b}\left(\frac{1}{2}^{+}\right)\to\mathcal{B}_{c}^{*}\left(\frac{3}{2}^{+}\right)+P_{\pi}
and
\Lambda_{b}\left(\frac{1}{2}^{+}\right)\to\mathcal{B}^{*}\left(\frac{3}{2}^{+}\right)+P_{D}
, where
\mathcal{B}_{c}^{*}\left(\frac{3}{2}^{+}\right)
and
\mathcal{B}^{*}\left(\frac{3}{2}^{+}\right)
belong to sextet and decuplet representations of
SU(3)
, respectively, the above framework is not applicable.

For these decays the dominant contribution comes from
W
exchange.

W
exchange in the non-relativistic limit is encoded in the effective
Hamiltonian
[
3
,
4
,
5
]
:

\mathcal{H}_{W}^{PC}=\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\underset{i\neq j}{\sum}\alpha_{i}^{-}\gamma_{j}^{+}(1-{\bf\sigma}_{i}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{j})\delta^{3}(r),

(4)

where

\displaystyle\alpha_{i}^{-}\left|u\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\left|d\right\rangle,\quad i=1,2\text{ to be summed over $i$}

\displaystyle\gamma_{j}^{+}\left|b\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\left|c\right\rangle,\ j=3.

Note that
\varLambda_{b},\varLambda_{c}^{+},\Xi_{c}^{0}
belong
to anti-symmetric representation
\bar{3}
of
SU(3)
, with spin
wave function

\chi_{\text{MA}}^{\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left|\left(\uparrow\downarrow-\downarrow\uparrow\right)\uparrow\right\rangle,

(5)

where as
\Sigma_{c}^{0},\Sigma_{b}^{+}
belong to symmetric representation
of
6
of
SU(3)
with spin wave function

\chi_{\text{MS}}^{\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\left|-\left(\uparrow\downarrow+\downarrow\uparrow\right)\uparrow+2\uparrow\uparrow\downarrow\right\rangle.

(6)

From the above equation, it follows

\displaystyle\left[\alpha_{1}^{-}\gamma_{3}^{+}\left(1-{\bf\sigma}_{1}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{3}\right)+\alpha_{2}^{-}\gamma_{3}^{+}\left(1-{\bf\sigma}_{2}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{3}\right)\right]\left|\varLambda_{b}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\sqrt{6}\left|\Sigma_{c}^{0}\right\rangle,

(7)

\displaystyle\left[\alpha_{2}^{-}\gamma_{3}^{+}\left(1-{\bf\sigma}_{2}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{3}\right)+\alpha_{1}^{-}\gamma_{3}^{+}\left(1-{\bf\sigma}_{1}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{3}\right)\right]\left|\Sigma_{b}^{+}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\sqrt{6}\left|\varLambda_{c}^{+}\right\rangle,

(8)

\displaystyle\left[\alpha_{2}^{-}\gamma_{3}^{+}\left(1-{\bf\sigma}_{2}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{3}\right)+\alpha_{1}^{-}\gamma_{3}^{+}\left(1-{\bf\sigma}_{1}\cdot{\bf\sigma}_{3}\right)\right]\left|\Sigma_{b}^{+}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=3\sqrt{2}\left|\Sigma_{c}^{+}\right\rangle.

(9)

The following comment is in order with respect to Eq. (
8
)
and Eq. (
9

6\times 8=\bar{3}+6+15+24.

W
exchange is relevant when one considers baryon-pole contributions
(Born terms) involving the matrix elements of the form
\left\langle\mathcal{B}_{c}\left|\mathcal{H}_{W}^{PC}\right|\mathcal{B}_{b}\right\rangle,

which can be evaluated in the non-relativistic quark model (NQM) by
using Eq. (
4
). The form of Hamiltonian Eq. (
4
)
lead to non-zero matrix elements
[
3
]

\left\langle\Sigma_{c}^{0}\left|\mathcal{H}_{W}^{PC}\right|\Lambda_{b}\right\rangle=\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)\sqrt{6}d^{\prime},

(10)

\left\langle\Lambda_{c}^{+}\left|\mathcal{H}_{W}^{PC}\right|\Sigma_{b}^{+}\right\rangle=\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)\sqrt{6}d^{\prime},

(11)

\left\langle\Sigma_{c}^{-}\left|\mathcal{H}_{W}^{PC}\right|\Sigma_{b}^{+}\right\rangle=\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)3\sqrt{2}d^{\prime}.

(12)

[

(13)

4
Hadronic Weak Decays
[
7
]

For the hadronic weak decay
\mathcal{B}^{\prime}\left(p^{\prime}\right)\to\mathcal{B}\left(p\right)+\pi\left(k\right)
,
with
p^{{}^{\prime}}=p+k
, the transition matrix can be written as

T=\frac{1}{\left(2\pi\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}\sqrt{\frac{mm^{\prime}}{2k_{0}p_{0}p_{0}^{\prime}}}\bar{u}\left(p\right)\left(A-B\gamma_{5}\right)u\left(p^{\prime}\right)

(37)

where
A
and
B
are the parity violating (s-wave) and parity conserving
(p-wave) amplitudes, respectively. The Decay rate is given by

\Gamma=\frac{k}{4\pi m^{\prime}}\left[\left(p_{0}+m\right)\left|A\right|^{2}+\left(p_{0}-m\right)\left|B\right|^{2}\right],

(38)

and the asymmetry parameter

\alpha=\frac{2kA^{*}B}{\left[\left(p_{0}+m\right)\left|A\right|^{2}+\left(p_{0}-m\right)\left|B\right|^{2}\right]}.

(39)

4.1
Factorization

For the decay
\Lambda_{b}\left(p^{\prime}\right)\to\Lambda_{c}^{+}\left(p\right)\pi^{-}\left(k\right)
,
the factorization gives

\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)a_{1}\left(-f_{\pi}\right)k^{\mu}\bar{u}\left(p\right)\gamma_{\mu}\left(g_{V}-g_{A}\gamma_{5}\right)u\left(p^{\prime}\right).

(40)

Thus

\displaystyle A_{\text{fact}}

\displaystyle=-\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)a_{1}f_{\pi}\left(m_{\Lambda_{b}}-m_{\Lambda_{c}}\right)g_{V},

(41)

\displaystyle B_{\text{fact}}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)a_{1}f_{\pi}\left(m_{\Lambda_{b}}+m_{\Lambda_{c}}\right)g_{A}.

(42)

The quark model gives
g_{V}=1,\quad g_{A}=0,
implying
B_{\text{fact}}=0.

Now

a_{1}=C_{1}+\frac{1}{3}C_{2},\quad C_{1}=1.009,\quad C_{2}=-0.257,

giving
a_{1}=0.92.
The
g_{V}=1
, is in the limit
m\to 0
, i.e.,

g_{V}\left(0\right)=1
. To get
g_{V}
for finite masses, the following
prescription is used

g_{V}=\left(1+\frac{m_{d}}{m_{b}}\right)\left(1-\frac{m_{c}}{m_{b}}\right)g_{V}\left(0\right)=0.739

(43)

and

a_{1}g_{V}=0.680

(44)

by using
m_{d}=0.336
GeV,
m_{c}=1.45
GeV,
m_{b}=4.66
GeV.
Hence from Eqs. (
41
) and (
44
), we have

A=A_{\text{fact}}=-9.47\times 10^{-8},

(45)

and from Eqs. (
38
), using
k=2.342
GeV,
p_{0}+m_{\Lambda_{c}}=5.558

GeV, we have

\text{Br}\left(\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda_{c}^{+}\pi^{-}\right)=4.0\times 10^{-3},

(46)

to be compared with the experimental value

\text{Br}\left(\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda_{c}^{+}\pi^{-}\right)=\left(4.9\pm 0.4\right)\times 10^{-3}.

(47)

For the decay
\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda_{c}^{+}D_{s}^{-}
, we can write

A=A_{\text{fact}}=-\left(\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ud}V_{cb}\right)a_{1}g_{V}f_{D_{s}}\left(m_{\Lambda_{b}}-m_{\Lambda_{c}}\right)=1.92\times 10^{-7}

(48)

GeV.

Although,
