Abstract

The aim of this paper is to obtain the existence of unique solution to nonlinear Cauchy-type problem. We consider the implicit nonlinear Cauchy-type problem with
\psi-
Hilfer fractional derivative. The Banach fixed point theorem is used to obtain the existence of a unique solution whereas the generalized Gronwall inequality is used to discuss continuous data dependence of the solution. The results obtained herein are supported with illustrative example.

Keywords:

\psi
-Hilfer derivative, Cauchy-type problem, existence of solution, fixed point theory, generalized Gronwall inequality.

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle A(t)+\frac{M}{1-M^{\ast}}\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\int_{a}^{t}\psi^{\prime}(s)(\psi(t)-\psi(s))^{\alpha-\epsilon-1}\left|u^{\ast}(s)-u(s)\right|ds,

An example.
