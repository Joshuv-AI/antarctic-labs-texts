Local sections of arithmetic fundamental groups of p p -adic curves
Mohamed Saïdi

Abstract
We investigate sections of the arithmetic fundamental group π 1 ​ ( X ) \pi_{1}(X) where X X is either a smooth affinoid p p -adic curve , or a formal germ of a p p -adic curve , and prove that they can be lifted (unconditionally) to sections of cuspidally abelian Galois groups. As a consequence, if X X admits a compactification Y Y , and the exact sequence of π 1 ​ ( X ) \pi_{1}(X) splits , then index ​ ( Y ) = 1 \text{index}(Y)=1 . We also exhibit a necessary and sufficient condition for a section of π 1 ​ ( X ) \pi_{1}(X) to arise from a rational point of Y Y . One of the key ingredients in our investigation is the fact, we prove in this paper in case X X is affinoid, that the Picard group of X X is finite .

\psi_{n}:\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{0})@>d>>M=\oplus_{i=1}^{t}(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z}).

Claim 2

\text{ker}(\psi_{n})
is
finite

Proof of Claim 2

First we prove that the kernel of the map

\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n-1})
is finite for
n\geq 2
. Write
I_{n}
for the sheaf of ideals of
O_{S}
defining
S_{n}
. We have an exact sequence of sheaves on
S_{n}
:

1\to 1+(I_{n-1}/I_{n})\to O_{S_{n}}^{\times}\to O_{S_{n-1}}^{\times}\to 1

which induces an exact sequence in cohomology

H^{1}(S_{n},1+(I_{n-1}/I_{n}))\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n-1})\to H^{2}(S_{n},1+(I_{n-1}/I_{n})).

Further the truncated exponential map
\alpha\mapsto 1+\alpha
induces an isomorphism of sheaves
I_{n-1}/I_{n}\overset\sim\to{\rightarrow}1+(I_{n-1}/I_{n})
[
(I_{n-1}/I_{n})^{2}=0
], hence
H^{2}(S_{n},1+I_{n-1}/I_{n})=0
and the map
\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n-1})
is surjective.
Moreover,
H^{1}(S_{n},I_{n-1}/I_{n})
is finite. Indeed

H^{1}(S_{n},I_{n-1}/I_{n})
is a finitely generated
B_{n}
-module with finite support since the morphism
\lambda_{n}^{-1}(Z_{n}\setminus\{z_{1},\ldots,z_{t}\})\to Z_{n}\setminus\{z_{1},\ldots,z_{t}\}
is affine and

R^{1}(\pi_{n})_{*}(I_{n-1}/I_{n})
is the sheaf associated to the
B_{n}
-module
H^{1}(S_{n},I_{n-1}/I_{n})
, here
\lambda_{n}:S_{n}\to Z_{n}
is the proper
morphism induced by
\lambda
.
This shows the kernel of the map

\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n-1})
is finite for all
n\geq 2
. A similar argument shows that the kernel of the map
\operatorname{Pic}(S_{1})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{0})
is finite.
Hence, using Claim 1,
\text{ker}(\psi_{n})
is finite.

This finishes the proof of Claim 2.
∎

In light of Claim 2, and in order to prove assertion
(A)
, it suffices to prove that the cokernel of the composite map

M\overset\text{def}\to{=}\oplus_{i=1}^{t}(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z})@>\beta>>\operatorname{Pic}(S)\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{n})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S_{0})@>d>>M=\oplus_{i=1}^{t}(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z})

is finite. The latter follows from the nondegeneracy of the intersection pairing
(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z})\times(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z})\to\mathbb{Z}
on each fibre

E_{i}
(cf. [Shafarevich], Lemma in page 69 and the discussion in page 71 after this Lemma),
1\leq i\leq t
.

This finishes the proof of assertion
(A)
.
∎

Proof of assertion {\bf(B)}

Let
J
be an ample invertible
O_{S}
-ideal such that
\text{Supp}(O_{S}/J)=S_{0}
. The existence of such

J
follows from the facts that
H_{r}
is affine (cf. Proof of Assertion A),
1\leq r\leq s
, the intersection pairing
(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z})\times(\oplus_{j=1}^{n_{i}}\mathbb{Z})\to\mathbb{Z}
on each fibre
E_{i}
is negative definite (cf. [Shafarevich], Lemma in page 69 and the discussion in page 71 after this Lemma), and the numerical criterion of ampleness on curves.
More precisely,
\forall\ 1\leq i\leq t
, one can find a divisor
D=\sum_{j=1}^{n_{i}}m_{ij}D_{i,j}
with
m_{i,j}<0
and
D.D_{i,j}>0
for all
1\leq j\leq n_{j}
.

For
m\geq 1
, let
S^{\prime}_{m}
be the closed subscheme of
S
defined by the sheaf of ideals
J^{m}
. To prove Assertion B it suffices to prove that there exists
m_{0}>0
such that the map

\operatorname{Pic}(S^{\prime}_{m+1})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S^{\prime}_{m})

is an isomorphism for any
m>m_{0}
. We have an exact sequence of shaves on
S^{\prime}_{m+1}
:

1\to J^{m}/J^{m+1}\to O_{S^{\prime}_{m+1}}^{\times}\to O_{S^{\prime}_{m}}^{\times}\to 1

where the map
J^{m}/J^{m+1}\to O_{S^{\prime}_{m+1}}^{\times}
maps a local section
\alpha
to
1+\alpha
,
which induces an exact sequence in cohomology

H^{1}(S^{\prime}_{m+1},J^{m}/J^{m+1})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S^{\prime}_{m+1})\to\operatorname{Pic}(S^{\prime}_{m})\to 0.

Now there exists
m_{0}>0
such that
H^{1}(S^{\prime}_{m+1},J^{m}/J^{m+1})=0
if
m\geq m_{0}
by [EGA III], première partie, Proposition 2.2.1.

This finishes the proof of assertion
(B)
.
∎

This finishes the proof of Proposition 4.1.
∎

§5. Compactification of formal germs of
p
-adic curves

In this section we use the following notations:
K
is a complete discrete valuation field with valuation ring
R
, uniformising parameter
\pi
,
and with perfect residue field
\ell\overset\text{def}\to{=}R/\pi R
. Further,
A
is a
two dimensional normal complete local ring

containing
R
with maximal ideal
\mathfrak{m}_{A}
containing
\pi
and residue field
\ell=A/{\mathfrak{m}}_{A}
.
We assume that
X\overset\text{def}\to{=}\operatorname{Spec}(A\otimes_{R}K)
is geometrically connected. Given a finite extension
L/K
we write
O_{L}
for the valuation ring of
L
,

A_{L}\overset\text{def}\to{=}A\otimes_{O_{L}}L
,
A_{O_{L}}\overset\text{def}\to{=}A\otimes_{R}{O_{L}}
, and
A_{O_{L}}^{\operatorname{nor}}
the normalisation of
A_{O_{L}}
in its total ring of fractions.

Proposition 5.1 (Compactification of formal germs of $p$-adic curves)

We use the above notations. There exists a finite extension
L/K
, a flat, proper, connected, and normal
O_{L}
-relative curve

Y\to\operatorname{Spec}O_{L}
, a closed point
y\in Y
, and an isomorphism
\hat{O}_{Y,y}\overset\sim\to{\rightarrow}A_{O_{L}}^{\operatorname{nor}}
where
\hat{O}_{Y,y}
is the completion of the local ring
O_{Y,y}
of
Y
at
y
.

Proof

By the main result in [Epp], Introduction, there exists a finite extension
L/K
with uniformising parameter
\pi_{L}
such that

A_{O_{L}}^{\operatorname{nor}}/\pi_{L}A_{O_{L}}^{\operatorname{nor}}

is reduced. Note that
A_{O_{L}}^{\operatorname{nor}}
is a normal two dimensional complete local ring with perfect residue field (cf. [Bourbaki], Chapter IX,
\lx@sectionsign 4
, Lemma 1, and our assumption that
X
is geometrically connected).
Without loss of generality we will assume that
A/\pi A
is reduced. We show there exists a proper, flat, connected, and normal relative
R
-curve

Y\to\operatorname{Spec}R
, a closed point
y\in Y
, and an isomorphism
\hat{O}_{Y,y}\overset\sim\to{\rightarrow}A
.

First,
A/\pi A
is a (reduced) one dimensional complete local ring with residue field
\ell
, hence is isomorphic to a quotient

\ell[[x_{1},\ldots,x_{t}]]/{\mathfrak{a}}
of a formal power series ring
\ell[[x_{1},\ldots,x_{t}]]

over
\ell
(cf. [Bourbaki], Chapitre IX,
\lx@sectionsign 3
). It then follows from [Artin], Theorem 3.8, and basic facts on the theory of algebraic curves, that there exists a proper and reduced
connected (but not necessarily irreducible)
\ell
-curve
Z
, a closed point
y\in Z
, and an isomorphism
\hat{O}_{Z,y}\overset\sim\to{\rightarrow}A/\pi A
where

\hat{O}_{Z,y}
is the completion of the local ring
O_{Z,y}
of
Z
at
y
. Moreover,
Z
is non-singular outside
y
.
There exists a rational function
f
on
Z
which defines a finite generically separable morphism
f:Z\to\mathbb{P}^{1}_{\ell}

such that
y=f^{-1}(\infty)
(cf. [Harbater-Stevenson], proof of Theorem 3). Thus, by considering the completion of the morphism
f
above
\infty
, we obtain a finite generically separable
morphism
\bar{g}:\operatorname{Spec}(A/\pi A)\to\operatorname{Spec}(\ell[[t]])
where
t
is a local parameter at
\infty
.
This morphism lifts to a finite morphism
g:\operatorname{Spf}A\to\operatorname{Spf}(R[[T]])
of formal schemes (cf. loc. cit., Lemma 2).
Let
\widetilde{Z}\to Z
be the morphism of normalisation and
\{x_{1},\ldots,x_{m}\}\subset\widetilde{Z}
the pre-image of
y
.
There is a one-to-one correspondence between the set
\{\mathfrak{p}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{p}_{m}\}\subset\operatorname{Spec}A
of prime ideals of height
1
containing
\pi
and the set
\{x_{1},\ldots,x_{m}\}
,

\mathfrak{p}_{i}
corresponds to
x_{i}
,
1\leq i\leq m
. The composite morphism
\widetilde{Z}\to Z\to\mathbb{P}^{1}_{\ell}
induces, by completion above
\infty
, finite separable morphisms

\bar{g}_{i}:\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Fr}(\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}})\to\operatorname{Spec}\ell((t))
where
\operatorname{Fr}(\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}})
is the fraction field of the completion
\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}}

of the local ring
O_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}}
of
\widetilde{Z}
at
x_{i}
,
1\leq i\leq m
(with the above notations
t=T\mod\pi
).

Consider the formal closed unit disc
D=\operatorname{Spf}R<\frac{1}{T}>
with parameter
\frac{1}{T}
and its special fibre
D_{\ell}=\operatorname{Spec}\ell[\frac{1}{t}]
(
D_{\ell}\overset\sim\to{\rightarrow}\mathbb{A}^{1}_{\ell}
).
By a result of Gabber and Katz (cf. [Katz], Main Theorem 1.4.1) there exists, for
1\leq i\leq m
, a finite cover
\bar{h}_{i}:C_{i}\to D_{\ell}
with
C_{i}
connected, which only (tamely) ramifies above the point
\frac{1}{t}=0

and such that the completion of
\bar{h}_{i}
above
t=0
is generically isomorphic to the cover
\bar{g}_{i}:\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Fr}(\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}})\to\operatorname{Spec}\ell((t))
.
Using formal patching techniques (cf. [Saïdi4], 1.2) one can lift the covers
\bar{h}_{i}
to finite covers
h_{i}:Y_{i}\to D
which only ramify
above the point
\frac{1}{T}=0
,
1\leq i\leq m
[outside
\frac{1}{T}=0

the existence of such a lifting follows from the theorems of lifting of étale covers (cf. [Grothendieck], Exposé I, Corollaire 8.4). In a formal neighbourhood of
\frac{1}{T}=0
such a lifting is possible under the tameness condition: étale locally near
\frac{1}{t}
the cover
\bar{h}_{i}
is defined by an equation
y^{s}=\frac{1}{t^{e}}
, where
s\geq 1
is an integer prime to the
characteristic of
\ell
, and one lifts to the cover defined by
Y^{s}=\frac{1}{T^{e}}
].
For
1\leq i\leq m
, let
\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}_{i}}
be the completion of the localisation
A_{\mathfrak{p}_{i}}
of
A
at
{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}
. Thus,

\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}_{i}}
is a complete discrete valuation ring with uniformising parameter
\pi
(recall
A/\pi A
is reduced)
and residue field
\operatorname{Fr}(\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}})
. Let
B
be the completion of the localisation of
R[[T]]
at
\pi
. Thus,
B
is a complete discrete valuation ring with residue field

\ell((t))
. The finite cover
g:\operatorname{Spf}A\to\operatorname{Spf}(R[[T]])
induces, by pull-back to
\operatorname{Spf}B
, finite covers
g_{i}:\operatorname{Spf}\hat{A}_{\mathfrak{p}_{i}}\to\operatorname{Spf}B
which (by construction) lift the covers

\bar{g}_{i}:\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Fr}(\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}})\to\operatorname{Spec}\ell((t))
,
1\leq i\leq m
. Further, the cover
h_{i}:Y_{i}\to D
induces, by pull-back to
\operatorname{Spf}B
, a finite cover

\tilde{h}_{i}:\operatorname{Spf}B_{i}\to\operatorname{Spf}B
which by construction lifts the cover
\bar{g}_{i}:\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Fr}(\hat{O}_{\widetilde{Z},x_{i}})\to\operatorname{Spec}\ell((t))
. Thus, the covers
\tilde{h}_{i}:\operatorname{Spf}B_{i}\to\operatorname{Spf}B

and

are isomorphic since
\bar{g}_{i}
is generically separable.
Using formal patching techniques (cf. loc. cit.) one can patch the covers
g:\operatorname{Spf}A\to\operatorname{Spf}(R[[T]])
and

h_{i}:Y_{i}\to D
,
1\leq i\leq m
, to construct a finite cover
Y\to\mathbb{P}^{1}_{R}
in the category of formal schemes with

normal, connected, proper, and flat over
\operatorname{Spf}R
.
The special fibre
Y_{\ell}\overset\text{def}\to{=}Y\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}\ell
of
Y
consists of
m
irreducible components which intersect at the point
y
and is (by construction) non-singular outside
y
. The formal curve

is algebraic by formal GAGA and (by construction)
\hat{O}_{Y,y}\overset\sim\to{\rightarrow}A
as required.
