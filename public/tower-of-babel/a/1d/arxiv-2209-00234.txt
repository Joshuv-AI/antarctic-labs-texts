Mock theta functions and characters of N=3 superconformal modules IV
Minoru Wakimoto

Abstract




Abstract

In this paper we obtain explicit formulas for mock theta functions

\Phi^{[m,s]}(\tau,z_{1},z_{2},t)

(m\in\frac{1}{2}{\mathbf{N}},s\in\frac{1}{2}{\mathbf{Z}})

by using the coroot lattice of the Lie superalgebra
D(2,1,a)

and the Kac-Peterson’s identity.
As its application, we study the branching functions of tensor products
of N=3 modules and prove the formula conjectured in
[
17
]
.
