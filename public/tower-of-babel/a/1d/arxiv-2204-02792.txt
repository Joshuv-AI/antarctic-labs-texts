Theoretical analysis of the leptonic decays 𝑩 → ℓ ​ ℓ ​ ℓ ​ 𝝂 ¯ ℓ \bm{B\to\ell\ell\ell\bar{\nu}_{\ell}} : Identical leptons in the final state
Mikhail A. Ivanov a and Dmitri Melikhov a,b,c

Abstract
We study the effects of the identical leptons in the final state of the B + → ℓ + ​ ℓ − ​ ℓ + ​ ν ¯ ℓ B^{+}\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}\ell^{+}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} decay. The amplitude of the process is described by the same form factors as the amplitude of the B → ℓ ​ ℓ ​ ℓ ′ ​ ν ¯ ℓ ′ {B\to\ell\ell\ell^{\prime}\bar{\nu}^{\prime}_{\ell}} decay for non-identical leptons in the final state. However, the differential distributions are strongly different, as the B + → ℓ + ​ ℓ − ​ ℓ + ​ ν ¯ ℓ {B^{+}\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}\ell^{+}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}} amplitude contains both the direct ( M a M_{a} ) and the exchange ( M b M_{b} ) diagrams. We calculate a number of the differential distirbutions. In particular, we propose an interesting observable that can be readily measured experimentally – the differential distribution over the invariant mass of the pair of leptons of the same charge, ℓ + ​ ℓ + \ell^{+}\ell^{+} . The good news is that the interference between M a M_{a} and M b M_{b} , d ​ ℬ a ​ b d{\cal B}_{ab} , is found to be at the level of less than 1% in all considered differental distributions and therefore can be neglected in the full kinematical region of this decay.
