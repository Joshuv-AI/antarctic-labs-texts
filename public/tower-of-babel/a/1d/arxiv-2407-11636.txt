T-Duality and 𝑻 ​ 𝑻 ¯ {T\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muT\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu} -like Deformations of Sigma Models
Daniele Bielli, Christian Ferko, Liam Smith, Gabriele Tartaglino-Mazzucchelli

Abstract
We initiate the study of the interplay between T-duality and classical stress tensor deformations in two-dimensional sigma models. We first show that a general Abelian T-duality commutes with the T ​ T ¯ {T\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muT\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu} deformation, which can be engineered by a gravitational dressing. Then, by using an auxiliary field formulation of stress tensor deformations of the principal chiral model (PCM), we prove that non-Abelian T-duality and arbitrary T ​ T ¯ {T\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muT\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu} -like flows also commute for theories in this class. We argue that all such auxiliary field deformations of both the PCM and its T-dual are classically integrable.

Despite the numerous applications of this framework, our present understanding of the space of quantum field theories is fundamentally incomplete. duality

\displaystyle S^{(0)}_{\rm sm}=-\frac{1}{2}\int\mathrm{d}^{2}x\,\Big\{e\,g^{\mu\nu}\Phi_{\mu\nu}+\mathcal{T}\Big\}\,,

\displaystyle\Phi_{\mu\nu}:=G_{IJ}\partial_{\mu}\phi^{I}\partial_{\nu}\phi^{J}\,,~~~{\cal T}:=\varepsilon^{\mu\nu}B_{IJ}\partial_{\mu}\phi^{I}\partial_{\nu}\phi^{J}\,.

III
Non-Abelian T-duality and Auxiliary Field Sigma Models

In this section we extend the T-dualisation procedure of
T\overline{T}
deformed models to the non-Abelian setting, exploiting the infinite family of integrable deformations introduced in
Ferko:2024ali
. We start by briefly reviewing the AFSM construction. These are
2d
sigma models on a flat Lorentzian worldsheet
\Sigma
, whose target-space geometry is a Lie group G with Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}
, which are characterized by the pullback to
\Sigma
of the left-invariant Maurer-Cartan form
j:=\texttt{g}^{-1}\mathrm{d}\texttt{g}
. The standard PCM action is

S_{\text{PCM}}\!:=\!\int_{\!\Sigma\!}\!\mathrm{d}^{2}\!\sigma\mathcal{L}_{\text{PCM}}\,\,\,\text{with}\,\,\,\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{PCM}}\!:=-\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{tr}(j_{+}j_{-})\,.

(9)

Here

are worldsheet lightcone coordinates, in terms of which the metric and its inverse reduce to
\eta_{+-}=\eta_{-+}=-2
and
\eta^{+-}=\eta^{-+}=-\tfrac{1}{2}
while
\mathrm{tr}
is the trace of the Lie algebra generators in some representation. The infinite family of deformed models is described by the Lagrangian

\mathcal{L}_{\text{PCM}}^{\text{E}}:=\mathrm{tr}\Big[\tfrac{1}{2}j_{+}j_{-}\!+\!v_{+}v_{-}\!+\!j_{+}v_{-}+j_{-}v_{+}\Big]\!+\!E(\nu)\,,

(10)

where
v
is the auxiliary field, a Lie-algebra-valued 1-form, and
E
is an arbitrary function of
\nu:=\mathrm{tr}(v_{+}v_{+})\mathrm{tr}(v_{-}v_{-})
.
The equations of motion (EOM) of the model read

\displaystyle\delta_{\texttt{g}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{PCM}}^{\text{E}}\!\equiv\!0~

\displaystyle\leftrightarrow~\partial_{\!+\!}\mathfrak{J}_{\!-\!}\!+\!\partial_{\!-\!}\mathfrak{J}_{\!+\!}\!=\!-2([v_{\!+\!},j_{\!-\!}]\!+\![v_{\!-\!},j_{\!+\!}])\,,

\displaystyle\delta_{v_{\!\pm\!}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{PCM}}^{\text{E}}\!\equiv\!0~

\displaystyle\leftrightarrow~j_{\pm}\!=\!-v_{\pm}\!-\!2E^{\prime}v_{\mp}\mathrm{tr}(v_{\pm}v_{\pm})\,,

(11)

with
E^{\prime}:=\tfrac{\mathrm{d}E}{\mathrm{d}\nu}

\mathfrak{J}_{\pm}:=-(j_{\pm}+2v_{\pm})

9

.
In this language, the non-linearities of the deformation are encoded in the arbitrary interaction function
E
. Analogously to the gravitational dressing of the previous section, the T-dualisation introduced in

;
delaOssa:1992vci

Buscher:1987sk
;

{}_{L}\times
G

\subseteq
G

of the left sector of the isometry group via a minimal coupling

j\quad\rightarrow\quad j^{\omega}:=\texttt{g}^{-1}(\mathrm{d}+\omega)\texttt{g}=j+\texttt{g}^{-1}\omega\texttt{g}\,.

(12)

The connection
\omega

\omega\!\rightarrow\!h^{-1}\!\omega h\!+\!h^{-1}\!\mathrm{d}h

\mathcal{L}_{\omega}\!=\!\mathrm{tr}\Big[\tfrac{1}{2}j^{\omega}_{+}j^{\omega}_{-}\!+\!v_{+}v_{-}\!+\!j^{\omega}_{+}v_{-}\!+\!j^{\omega}_{-}v_{+}\Big]\!+\!E(\nu)\,.

(14)

In the previous equation we exploited the relation

(15)

In lightcone coordinates, the multiplier term (

\displaystyle=\tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{tr}\Bigl[(\partial_{+}X-2v_{+})\frac{1}{1-\mathrm{ad}_{X}}(\partial_{-}X+2v_{-})\Bigr]

\displaystyle+\mathrm{tr}(v_{+}v_{-})+E(\nu)\,.
