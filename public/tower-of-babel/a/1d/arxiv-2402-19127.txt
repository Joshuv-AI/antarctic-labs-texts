Hankel Determinants of convoluted Catalan numbers and nonintersecting lattice paths: A bijective proof of Cigler’s Conjecture
Markus Fulmek

Abstract
. In recent preprints, Cigler considered certain Hankel determinants of convoluted Catalan numbers and conjectured identities for these determinants. In this note, we shall give a bijective proof of Cigler’s Conjecture by interpreting determinants as generating functions of nonintersecting lattice paths: this proof employs the reflection principle, the Lindström–Gessel–Viennot–method and a certain construction involving reflections and overlays of nonintersecting lattice paths. Shortly after the bijective proof was presented here, Cigler provided a shorter proof based on earlier results.

Lemma 3
.

If
\mathcal{F}
is a folded survivor, then
\overline{\mathcal{F}}
cannot contain free kinks strictly above the forbidden

Consider again the set

Lemma 4
.

If
\mathcal{F}
is a folded survivor which contains a column

{\color[rgb]{0,1,0}G}{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}
,

of type
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}

.

c^{\prime}
:
But it is impossible for a column of type
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}

{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}
must

3.6.5
, a column

which is
ascending adjacent to the right of

must
be of type
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}B}{\color[rgb]{0,1,0}G}

Our considerations make clear that certain distributions of colours for
the terminal points (which are not also initial points) are
impossible

for folded survivors:

Corollary 1
.

If
\mathcal{F}
is a folded survivor, then the sequence of terminal points which are
not also initial points

•

may start with a sequence of consecutive blue points,

•

and every blue point not belonging to this starting sequence must be immediately followed
by a green point.

(In other words: after the first green terminal point, there can never follow a pair
of consecutive blue terminal points.)
