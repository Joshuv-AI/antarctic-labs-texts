The Dirichlet problem for the Laplacian in Lipschitz domains. Abstract
Chérif Amrouche a, Mohand Moussaoui b

Abstract
The main purpose of this paper is to address some questions concerning boundary value problems related to the Laplacian and bi-Laplacian operators, set in the framework of classical H s H^{s} Sobolev spaces on a bounded Lipschitz domain of ℝ N . \mathbb{R}^{N}. These questions are not new and a lot of work has been done in this direction by many authors using various techniques since the 80’s. If for regular domains almost every thing is elucidated, it is not the case for Lipschitz ones and for s s of the form s = k + 1 / 2 s=k+1/2 , with k k integer. It is well known that this framework is delicate. Even in these cases many results are well established but sometimes not satisfactory. Several questions remain posed. Our main goal through this work is on one hand to give some improvements to the theory and on another one by using techniques which do not require too intricate calculations. We also tried to obtain maximal regularity for the solutions and as far as we can optimality of the results.

The first result is about traces of functions belonging to Sobolev spaces, which is crucial in the study of boundary value problems. We know that if
u\in H^{s}(\Omega)
with
s>1/2
then the function
u
has a trace which belongs to
H^{s-1/2}(\Gamma)
. Moreover, if
v\in H^{1/2}(\Omega)
, in general this function
v
may have no trace. In the following theorem, we see that an additional condition on
\nabla v
allows to obtain a trace for the function
v

Theorem 0.1
(Trace Operator in
H^{1/2}(\Omega)
)

i) The linear mapping
\gamma_{0}:u\mapsto u_{|\Gamma}
defined on
\mathscr{D}(\overline{\Omega})
can be extended by continuity to a linear and continuous mapping, still denoted
\gamma_{0}
, from
E(\nabla;\,\Omega)
into
L^{2}(\Gamma)
, where

\displaystyle E(\nabla;\,\Omega)\ =\ \left\{\,v\in H^{1/2}(\Omega);\ \nabla v\in[\textit{{H}}^{\,1/2}(\Omega)]^{\prime}\,\right\}.

ii) The Kernel of
\gamma_{0}
is equal to
H^{1/2}_{00}(\Omega)
. In particular, we have the following property:

v\in H^{1/2}_{00}(\Omega)\Longrightarrow v=0\quad in\;L^{2}(\Gamma).

As a consequence we get immediately the following results: let

v\in H^{3/2}(\Omega)\quad\mathrm{with}\quad\nabla^{2}v\in[\textit{{H}}^{\,1/2}(\Omega)]^{\prime},

then

v_{|\Gamma}\in H^{1}(\Gamma)\quad\mathrm{and}\quad\frac{\partial v}{\partial\textit{{n}}}\in L^{2}(\Gamma).

Moreover, we have the following property:

v\in H^{3/2}_{00}(\Omega)\Longrightarrow v=0\quad\mathrm{in}\;H^{1}(\Gamma)\quad\mathrm{and}\quad\frac{\partial v}{\partial\textit{{n}}}=0\quad\mathrm{in}\;L^{2}(\Gamma).

The notation
\nabla^{k}v
denotes the derivatives
\partial^{\alpha}v
with
|\alpha|=k
. The vector fields and the spaces of vector fields are denoted by bold fonts.

\mathscr{H}_{X}=\{v\in X;\;\Delta v=0\;\mathrm{in}\,\,\Omega\},\quad\quad\mathscr{H}^{\circ}_{X}=\{v\in\mathscr{H}_{X};\;v=0\;\mathrm{on}\;\Gamma\}

Theorem 0.2
(
H^{2}
and
H^{3}
-Regularity)

The operators

\Delta:H^{2}(\Omega)\cap H^{1}_{0}(\Omega)\longrightarrow(\mathscr{H}^{\circ}_{L^{2}(\Omega)})^{\bot}

and

\Delta:H^{3}(\Omega)\cap H^{1}_{0}(\Omega)\longrightarrow(\mathscr{H}^{\circ}_{H^{-1}(\Omega)})^{\bot}

In particular we have the following inequalities: there exits a constant

\|v\|_{H^{3}(\Omega)}\leq C\|\Delta v\|_{H^{1}(\Omega)}.

Observe that the space

We now give extensions of the classical Nečas’ property, that will be very useful for the study of the homogeneous Neumann problem and also for the Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator for the Laplacian. Usually,

Theorem 0.7
(Nečas Property)

Let

u\in H^{1}(\Omega)\quad\mathrm{with}\quad\Delta u\in\ [H^{1/2}(\Omega)]^{\prime}.

i) If
u\in H^{1}(\Gamma)
, then
\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial\textit{{n}}}\in L^{2}(\Gamma)
and we have the following estimate

\displaystyle\left|\left|\frac{\partial u}{\partial\textit{{n}}}\right|\right|_{\textit{{L}}^{2}(\Gamma)}\ \leq\ C(\Omega)\left(\inf_{k\in\mathbb{R}}\left|\left|u+k\right|\right|_{H^{1}(\Gamma)}+\left|\left|\Delta u\right|\right|_{[H^{1/2}(\Omega)]^{\prime}}\right),

where the constant
C(\Omega)
depends only on the Lipschitz character of
\Omega.

ii) If
\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial\textit{{n}}}\in L^{2}(\Gamma)
, then
u\in H^{1}(\Gamma)
and we have the following estimate

\displaystyle\inf_{k\in\mathbb{R}}\left|\left|u+k\right|\right|_{H^{1}(\Gamma)}\ \leq\ C(\Omega)\left(\left|\left|\frac{\partial u}{\partial\textit{{n}}}\right|\right|_{\textit{{L}}^{2}(\Gamma)}+\left|\left|\Delta u\right|\right|_{[H^{1/2}(\Omega)]^{\prime})}\right),

where the constant
C(\Omega)
depends only on the Lipschitz character of
\Omega.

iii) If
u\in H^{1}(\Gamma)
or
\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial\textit{{n}}}\in L^{2}(\Gamma)
, then
u\in H^{3/2}(\Omega)
.

In this work we investigate also the case of solutions in the following weighted Sobolev spaces:

Theorem 0.8
(Solutions in Weighted Sobolev Spaces)

The following operators, each being dual to the other,

v\in L^{2}_{1/\sqrt{\varrho}}(\Omega)\quad and\quad\Delta v\in[H^{3/2}_{0}(\Omega)]^{\prime}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v\in H^{1/2}_{00}(\Omega).
