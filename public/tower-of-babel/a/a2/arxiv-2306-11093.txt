On exceptional QP-manifolds

Abstract
The connection between two recent descriptions of tensor hierarchies – namely, infinity-enhanced Leibniz algebroids, given by Bonezzi & Hohm and Lavau & Palmkvist, and the p p -brane QP-manifolds constructed by Arvanitakis – is made precise. This is done by presenting a duality-covariant version of latter. The construction is based on the QP-manifold T ⋆ ​ [ n ] ​ T ​ [ 1 ] ​ M × ℋ ⁡ [ n ] T^{\star}[n]T[1]M\times\mathcal{H}[n] , where M M corresponds to the internal manifold of a supergravity compactification and ℋ ⁡ [ n ] \mathcal{H}[n] to a degree-shifted version of the infinity-enhanced Leibniz algebroid. Imposing that the canonical Q Q -structure on T ⋆ ​ [ n ] ​ T ​ [ 1 ] ​ M T^{\star}[n]T[1]M is the derivative operator on ℋ \mathcal{H} leads to a set of constraints. Solutions to these constraints correspond to 1 2 \frac{1}{2} -BPS p p -branes, suggesting that this is a new incarnation of a brane scan. Reduction w.r.t. to these constraints reproduces the known p p -brane QP-manifolds. This is shown explicitly for the SL ( 3 ) × (3)\times SL ( 2 ) (2) - and SL ( 5 ) (5) -theories. Furthermore, this setting is used to speculate about exceptional ’extended spaces’ and QP-manifolds associated to Leibniz algebras. A proposal is made to realise differential graded manifolds associated to Leibniz algebras as non-Poisson subspaces (i.e. not Poisson reductions) of QP-manifolds similar to the above. Two examples for this proposal are discussed: generalised fluxes (including the dilaton flux) of O ( d , d ) (d,d) and the 3-bracket flux for the SL ( 5 ) (5) -theory.
