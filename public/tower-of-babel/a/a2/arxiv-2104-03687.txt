Nonexpansive fixed point theorems for primitive uniform spaces MSC: 54H25
Lech Pasicki

Abstract
Some known fixed point theorems for nonexpansive mappings in metric spaces are extended here to the case of primitive uniform spaces. The reasoning presented in the proofs seems to be a natural way to obtain other general results.

Definition 1

Let

Definition 2

In particular, if for a metric space

The subsequent extension of

Theorem 4

Let
(X,\cal{B})
be a primitive uniform space, and let
f\colon X\to X
be a mapping.
Assume that
\emptyset\neq Y\subset X
is such that
f(Y)\subset Y
,
x\in\mbox{$c_{\cal{B}}$}(\mbox{$f_{\mid Y}$})
and the following

f(Y\cap B(x))\subset B(f(x)),\quad B\in\mbox{$\cal{B}$}

(4)

holds. Then
f(x)\in\mbox{$c_{\cal{B}}$}(\mbox{$f_{\mid Y}$})
, and
x
is a fixed point for
f
provided that
\mbox{$c_{\cal{B}}$}(\mbox{$f_{\mid Y}$})
is a singleton.

Proof. We have
\mbox{$f^{n+1}(Y)$}\subset\mbox{$f^{n}(Y)$}\subset\cdots\subset f(Y)\subset Y
.
If
\mbox{$f^{n}(Y)$}\subset B(x)
holds, then by (
4
) we obtain

\mbox{$f^{n+1}(Y)$}=f(\mbox{$f^{n}(Y)$}\cap B(x))\subset f(Y\cap B(x))\subset B(f(x)),

i.e.
f(x)\in\mbox{$c_{\cal{B}}$}(\mbox{$f_{\mid Y}$})
.
\quad\square

In
[
4

(X,d)

Proposition 10

Assume that
Z
is a linear space,
A,C,V\subset Z
are nonempy and
V
convex. Then

A\subset V+C
yields
\mbox{$conv\;$}A\subset V+\mbox{$conv\;$}C
.

Proof. Let
x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\in A
be arbitrary, and let

y_{1},\ldots,y_{n}\in C
be such that
\mbox{$x_{i}$}\in V+\mbox{$y_{i}$}
. Then for
x=\sum\mbox{$\alpha_{i}$}\mbox{$x_{i}$}

(a convex combination) we obtain
x\in\sum\mbox{$\alpha_{i}$}(V+\mbox{$y_{i}$})=V+(\sum\mbox{$\alpha_{i}$}\mbox{$y_{i}$})
and, consequently,

\mbox{$conv\;$}A\subset V+\mbox{$conv\;$}C
.
\quad\square

If

is a convex set in a linear topological space

a uniform space
(X,\mbox{$\cal{U}$})

and all
