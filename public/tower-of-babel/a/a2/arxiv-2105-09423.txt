Analytical study of holographic superconductor with backreaction in 4 ​ d 4d Gauss-Bonnet gravity
Debabrata Ghorai, Sunandan Gangopadhyay

Abstract
In this paper we have analytically investigated holographic superconductors in four dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity background. Recently the novel four dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity has been formulated by rescaling the Gauss-Bonnet coupling constant α → α d − 4 \alpha\rightarrow\frac{\alpha}{d-4} and taking the limit d → 4 d\rightarrow 4 , and this predicts several interesting new features. To know the effect of the curvature correction on the ( 2 + 1 ) (2+1) -dimensional superconductors, we have consider the 3 + 1 3+1 -dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity. Using the Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue approach, the critical temperature and the condensation operator values has been investigated in this framework away from the probe limit. We recover the numerical results from our analytical investigation.

In this paper, using the Sturm-Liouville (SL) eigenvalue approach, we have analytically investigated the critical temperature and the condensation operator values away from the probe limit for
4d
EGB gravity background. First, we have computed the
4d
EGB metric solution without backreaction and with backreaction of the gauge field on the spacetime with our basic set up. To incorporate the backreaction on the metric, we have used the gauge field solution. We have then calculated the Hawking temperature from the metric solution, which is interpreted as the critical temperature of the boundary field theory. Near the critical temperature, we have recast the matter field equation in the SL eigenvalue form and estimated the eigenvalues with a trial function. We have then calculated the critical temperature in terms of the charge density. Using the perturbative expansion of the gauge field, we have computed the condensation operator values. We have shown that our analytical results match very well with the numerical findings.

Basic formalism

Our basic starting point is to write down the action for the formation of scalar hair outside an electrically charged black hole in
d
-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime. This reads

\displaystyle S=\int d^{d}x\frac{\sqrt{-g}}{2\kappa^{2}}\left(R-2\Lambda+\frac{\alpha}{2(d-4)}(R^{2}-4R^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}+R^{\mu\nu\lambda\rho}R_{\mu\nu\lambda\rho})+2\kappa^{2}\mathcal{L}_{matter}\right)

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{matter}=-\frac{1}{4}F^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}-(D_{\mu}\psi)^{*}D^{\mu}\psi-m^{2}\psi^{*}\psi

(2)

where

Now our task is to calculate this temperature in terms of the quantities of boundary field theory. To consider the effect of matter field on the spacetime, we first have to calculate the backreacted metric from the above action.

5
The condensation operator: Critical exponent

In this section, we shall investigate the effect of the backreaction on the condensation operator near the critical temperature for
4d
GB gravity. To investigate the critical exponent, we write the field eq.(
12
) in
z
-coordinate near the critical temperature
(T_{c})
substituting eq.(
33
). This leads to

\displaystyle\phi^{\prime\prime}(z)=\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r^{2}_{+}}\mathcal{B}(z)\phi(z)

(47)

where
\mathcal{B}(z)=\frac{2z^{2\Delta_{+}-4}}{r_{+}^{2\Delta_{+}-4}}\frac{F^{2}(z)}{f(z)}~.

We have considered here
f(z)
as the GB gravity metric. We may now expand
\phi(z)
in the small parameter
\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r^{2}_{+}}
as

\displaystyle\frac{\phi(z)}{r_{+}}=\lambda(1-z)+\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r^{2}_{+}}\zeta(z)

(48)

with
\zeta(1)=0=\zeta^{\prime}(1).

Using eq.(
48
) and comparing the coefficient of
\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r^{2}_{+}}
of left hand side and right hand side of the eq.(
47
), we get the equation for the correction
\zeta(z)
near to the critical temperature

\displaystyle\zeta^{\prime\prime}(z)=\lambda\frac{2z^{2\Delta_{+}-4}}{r_{+}^{2\Delta_{+}-4}}\frac{F^{2}(z)}{f(z)}\mathcal{A}_{1}(z)

(49)

where
\mathcal{A}_{1}(z)=(1-z)
.

We rewrite the above equation as

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dz}\left(\zeta^{\prime}(z)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda\frac{2z^{2\Delta_{+}-2}}{r_{+}^{2\Delta_{+}-2}}\frac{F^{2}(z)}{g_{0}(z)}\mathcal{A}_{1}(z)~.

(50)

Using the boundary condition of
\zeta(z)
, we integrate (
50
) between the limits
z=0
and
z=1
. This gives

\displaystyle\zeta^{\prime}(z)\mid_{z\rightarrow 0}=-\frac{\lambda}{r^{2\Delta_{+}-2}_{+}}\mathcal{A}_{2}

(51)

where
\mathcal{A}_{2}=\int^{1}_{0}dz\frac{2z^{2\Delta_{+}-2}F^{2}(z)}{g_{0}(z)}\mathcal{A}_{1}(z)
.

But from eq.(
48
), we get the asymptotic behaviour (near
z=0
) of
\phi(z)
. Comparing the both equations of
\phi(z)
about
z=0
, we obtain

\displaystyle\mu-\frac{\rho}{r_{+}}z

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda r_{+}(1-z)+\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r_{+}}\left\{\zeta(0)+z\zeta^{\prime}(0)+.........\right\}

(52)

Comparing the coefficient of
z
on both sides of eq.(
52
), we obtain

\displaystyle-\frac{\rho}{r_{+}}=-\lambda r_{+}+\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r_{+}}\zeta^{\prime}(0)~.

(53)

Using eq.(
51
), we obtain the relation between the charge density
(\rho)
and the condensation operator
(\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle)

\displaystyle\frac{\rho}{r^{2}_{+}}=\lambda\left[1+\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}}{r^{2\Delta_{+}}_{+}}\mathcal{A}_{2}\right]~.

(54)

Using the expression for the critical temperature and the definition of
\lambda
and simplifying eq.(
54
), we get

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle^{2}=\frac{(4\pi T_{c})^{2\Delta_{+}}}{\mathcal{A}_{2}[3-\frac{1}{2}\kappa^{2}_{i}\lambda^{2}_{\kappa_{i-1}}]^{2\Delta_{+}}}.\left(\frac{T_{c}}{T}\right)^{2}\left[1-\left(\frac{T}{T_{c}}\right)^{2}\right]~.

(55)

Using the fact that
T\approx T_{c}
, we can write

\displaystyle\left(\frac{T_{c}}{T}\right)^{2}\left[1-\left(\frac{T}{T_{c}}\right)^{2}\right]\approx 2\left[1-\left(\frac{T}{T_{c}}\right)\right]

(56)

Using this we obtain the relation between the condensation operator and the critical temperature in general dimension

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle=\beta T^{\Delta_{+}}_{c}\sqrt{1-\frac{T}{T_{c}}}

(57)

where
\beta=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\mathcal{A}_{2}}}\left[\frac{4\pi}{3-\frac{1}{2}\kappa^{2}_{i}\lambda^{2}_{\kappa_{i-1}}}\right]^{\Delta_{+}}.

From this eq.(
57
), we find that the critical exponent is
1/2
which is not affected by the GB gravity, BI coupling parameter and backreaction.
The choice for
m^{2}L^{2}=-2
or
m^{2}L^{2}_{eff}=-2
yields
\Delta_{+}=2
. Eq.(
57
) then becomes

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{O}_{+}\rangle=\beta T^{2}_{c}\sqrt{1-\frac{T}{T_{c}}}~.

(58)

Now
\mathcal{A}_{2}
and
\beta
become

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{2}=\int^{1}_{0}dz\frac{2z^{2}F^{2}(z)}{g_{0}(z)}(1-z)~~~:~~~\beta=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\mathcal{A}_{2}}}\left[\frac{4\pi}{3-\frac{1}{2}\kappa^{2}_{i}(\lambda^{2}|_{\kappa_{i-1}})}\right]^{2}~.

(59)

where
F(z)=1-\tilde{\alpha}z^{2}
with parameter
\tilde{\alpha}
at which the Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue
\lambda^{2}
is minimum. We have shown the condensation operator value without backreaction for
m^{2}L^{2}=-2
and
m^{2}L^{2}_{eff}=-2
in Table

6
Conclusions

The effect of higher curvature corrections on
3+1
-dimensional holographic superconductors has been investigated analytically in the framework of
4d
Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity away from the probe limit using Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue method. We have calculated the critical temperature and the condensation operator values for this holographic superconductor model. In this study, we observe that the critical temperature decreases with increasing value
\alpha
for both cases
m^{2}L^{2}=-2
and
m^{2}L^{2}_{eff}=-2
. However, the critical temperature near the upper bound of GB parameter
\alpha
suddenly increases for
m^{2}L^{2}_{eff}=-2
, and this is quite different from the case
m^{2}L^{2}=-2
. Our analytical results match wonderfully with the numerical findings in
[
54
]
. We also observe that higher values of the GB and the backreaction parameters disfavour the condensation since the critical temperature decreases with increasing value of both parameters except at the Chern-Simons limit of the GB parameter. In future we are planning to study the effects of higher curvature correction on the critical value of the magnetic field in
3+1
-dimensional holographic superconductors models.
