Structure of deformed w 1 + ∞ w_{1+\infty} symmetry and topological generalization in Celestial CFT
Pavel Drozdov, Taro Kimura

Abstract
We investigate a deformation of w 1 + ∞ w_{1+\infty} algebra recently introduced in [ MRSV21 ] in the context of Celestial CFT that we denote by W ~ 1 + ∞ \widetilde{W}_{1+\infty} algebra. We obtain the operator product expansions of the generating currents of this algebra and explore its supersymmetric and topological generalizations.



1
Introduction

Celestial holography provides new powerful tools for studying quantum gravity by applying a holographic principle to asymptotically flat spacetimes (AFS)
[
PPR21
]
.
It was shown in
[
HS19
,
GHPS21
,
DPS19
]
that each of the soft theorems
(the linear relations between scattering amplitudes of soft particles,
i.e.
, the particles with energy
\omega\to 0
) in gauge theory and gravity in AFS is associated with an infinite number of symmetries and conservation laws on the celestial sphere.
The known symmetries do not close under commutation, implying an infinite tower of soft theorems.
Moreover, it has been also argued that the soft theorems in AFS are related to measurable gravitational memory effects via Fourier transform
[
Str18
,
SZ14
]
together with a discussion towards its experimental setups
[
HTLT20
]
.

In the recent works
[
GHPS21
,
Str21
,
HPS22
]
, it has been shown that the soft currents exhibit the so-called
w_{1+\infty}
symmetry
[
Pop91
,
PRS90
]
,
where the infinite tower of soft symmetries can be organized into a single symmetry based on the wedge algebra of
w_{1+\infty}
.
Along this direction, it has been pointed out in
[
MRSV21
]
that one can consider a more general setup, which involves non-minimal couplings in the bulk on the operator product expansions (OPEs) of soft currents in the Celestial Conformal Field Theory (CCFT).
The corresponding algebra of the currents provides a deformation of
w_{1+\infty}
algebra, that we call
\widetilde{W}_{1+\infty}
algebra.
Although
\widetilde{W}_{1+\infty}
algebra bears semblance to
W_{1+\infty}
algebra
[
PRS90
]
, it shows several specific properties that we briefly review in Section
3.1
.

A purpose of this paper is to obtain a deeper understanding of algebraic aspects of
\widetilde{W}_{1+\infty}
algebra.
First, we derive the OPEs for the generating currents of
\widetilde{W}_{1+\infty}
algebra by generalizing the approaches used for
W_{1+\infty}
algebra.
This results in Theorem
3.4
in Section
3.2
.
Secondly, we explore further generalization of
\widetilde{W}_{1+\infty}
algebra.
In particular, we introduce an
\mathcal{N}=2
supersymmetric extention of
\widetilde{W}_{1+\infty}
algebra in Section
4.1
, and we identify a BRST operator (Theorem
4.2
) in Section
4.2
.
Finally, based on the OPEs for
\mathcal{N}=2

\widetilde{W}_{\infty}
algebra together with the BRST operator, we construct the generators of the topologically twisted version
\widetilde{W}_{\infty}
algebra (Theorem
4.3
) in Section
4.1
.
In Section
5
, we conclude by discussing potential issues and future directions.
