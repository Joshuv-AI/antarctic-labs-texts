The generalized Volterra integral operator and Toeplitz operator on weighted Bergman spaces Thanks: † \dagger Corresponding author.
Juntao Du, Songxiao Li † \dagger and Dan Qu

Abstract
. We study the boundedness and compactness of the generalized Volterra integral operator on weighted Bergman spaces with doubling weights on the unit disk. A generalized Toeplitz operator is defined and the boundedness, compactness and Schatten class of this operator are investigated on the Hilbert weighted Bergman space. As an application, Schatten class membership of generalized Volterra integral operators are also characterized. Finally, we also get the characterizations of Schatten class membership of generalized Toeplitz operator and generalized Volterra integral operators on the Hardy space H 2 H^{2} . Keywords : Bergman space; Volterra integral operator; Toeplitz operator

1.
Introduction

Let
\mathbb{D}
be the open unit disk of complex plane
\mathbb{C}
and
H(\mathbb{D})
be the space of all analytic functions on
\mathbb{D}
. A function
\omega:\mathbb{D}\rightarrow[0,\infty)
is called a weight if it is positive and integrable.
\omega
is radial if
\omega(z)=\omega(|z|)
for all
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
.

Let
\omega
be a radial weight. For
r\in[0,1)
, set

\hat{\omega}(r)=\int_{r}^{1}\omega(s)ds.

We say that
\omega
is a doubling weight, denoted by
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
, if there is a constant
C>0
such that

\hat{\omega}(r)<C\hat{\omega}(\frac{1+r}{2})
.

\omega
is called a regular weight, denoted by
\omega\in\mathcal{R}
, if there is a constant
C>0
and
0<\delta<1
such that

\frac{1}{C}<\frac{\hat{\omega}(r)}{(1-r)\omega(r)}<C,\,\,\mbox{ whenever }\,\,\delta<r<1.

From
[
15
]
, we see that
\mathcal{R}\subset\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and
\hat{\omega}\in\mathcal{R}
if
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
. Moreover, for
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and any given
\varepsilon\in(0,1)
,

\omega^{*}(z)\approx(1-|z|)\hat{\omega}(z),~~~\varepsilon<|z|<1.

Here

\omega^{*}(z)=\int_{|z|}^{1}s\omega(s)\log\frac{s}{|z|}ds,\,\,\,z\in\mathbb{D}\backslash\{0\}.

See
[
14
,
15
]
for more details about
\mathcal{R}
and
\hat{\mathcal{D}}
.

Let
d\sigma
denote the normalized length measures on
{\mathbb{T}}
, the boundary of
\mathbb{D}
. For
0<p<\infty
, the Hardy space
H^{p}
consists of all functions
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
such that

\|f\|^{p}_{H^{p}}=\sup_{0<r<1}\int_{{\mathbb{T}}}|f(r\xi)|^{p}d\sigma(\xi)<\infty.

Let
0<p<\infty
and
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
. The weighted Bergman space
A_{\omega}^{p}
, induced by doubling weight
\omega
, consists of all functions
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
such that

\|f\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f(z)|^{p}\omega(z)dA(z)\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<\infty,

where
dA
is the normalized Lebesgue area measure on
\mathbb{D}
.
When
\omega(z)=(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}(\alpha>-1)
, the space
A_{\omega}^{p}
becomes the classical weighted Bergman space
A_{\alpha}^{p}
.
When
\alpha=0
, we will write
A_{0}^{p}
as
A^{p}
.

The Bloch space, denoted by
\mathcal{B}
, is the space of all
f\in H({\mathbb{D}})
such that

\sup_{z\in{\mathbb{D}}}(1-|z|^{2})|f^{\prime}(z)|<\infty.

The little Bloch space
\mathcal{B}_{0},
consists of all
f\in\mathcal{B}
such that

\lim_{|z|\to 1}(1-|z|^{2})|f^{\prime}(z)|=0.

For
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
, let
\mathcal{C}^{1}(\omega^{*})
denote the space consisting of all
g\in H(\mathbb{D})
such that

\displaystyle\sup_{a\in\mathbb{D}}\frac{\int_{S_{a}}|g^{\prime}(z)|^{2}\omega^{*}(z)dA(z)}{\omega(S_{a})}<\infty.

Here

S_{a}=\left\{re^{\mathrm{i\theta}}:|a|<r<1,|\arg a-\theta|<\frac{1-|a|}{2}\right\}

is a Carleson square at
a\in\mathbb{D}
. We say that
g\in\mathcal{C}_{0}^{1}(\omega^{*})
if
g\in\mathcal{C}^{1}(\omega^{*})
and

\lim_{|a|\to 1}\frac{\int_{S_{a}}|g^{\prime}(z)|^{2}\omega^{*}(z)dA(z)}{\omega(S_{a})}=0.

From
[
15
, Proposition 5.1]
, we see that
\mathcal{C}^{1}(\omega^{*})\subseteq\mathcal{B}
and
C_{0}^{1}(\omega^{*})\subseteq\mathcal{B}_{0}
when
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
. In particularly,
C^{1}(\omega^{*})=\mathcal{B}
and
C_{0}^{1}(\omega^{*})=\mathcal{B}_{0}
when
\omega\in\mathcal{R}
.

Suppose
g\in H(\mathbb{D})
. The integral operator
T_{g}
,
called the Volterra integral operator, is defined by

\displaystyle T_{g}f(z)=\int_{0}^{z}f(\xi)g^{\prime}(\xi)d\xi,~\qquad~~~f\in H(\mathbb{D}),~~z\in\mathbb{D}.

The operator
T_{g}
was first introduced by Pommerenke in
[
21
]
. He showed that
T_{g}
is bounded on the Hardy space
H^{2}
if and only if
g\in BMOA
, the bounded mean oscillation analytic function space. In
[
3
]
, Aleman and Siskakis proved that
T_{g}
is bounded on Bergman space
A^{p}
if and only if
g\in\mathcal{B}
.
In
[
1
]
, Aleman and Cima completely characterized the boundedness of Volterra operator on Hardy spaces.
In particular, they showed that
T_{g}
is bounded on
H^{p}
if and only if
g\in BMOA
.
More results about the Volterra operator on some analytic function spaces can be seen in
[
2
,
6
,
7
,
9
,
10
,
12
,
13
,
15
,
22
]
and the referees therein.

In
[
15
]
, Peláez and Rättyä characterized the boundedness, compactness and Schatten class of
T_{g}
between Bergman spaces induced by rapidly increasing weights.
Using Theorem 5 in
[
8
]
, those results can be extended to
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
as follows.

Theorem A
.

Let
0<p,q<\infty,\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and
g\in H(\mathbb{D})
.

(i)

If
0<p<q<\infty
, then
T_{g}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is bounded (compact) if and only if

\sup\limits_{\frac{1}{2}<|z|<1}\frac{(1-|z|)|g^{\prime}(z)|}{(\omega^{*}(z))^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{q}}}<\infty\left(\lim\limits_{|z|\to 1}\frac{(1-|z|)|g^{\prime}(z)|}{(\omega^{*}(z))^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{q}}}=0\right).

(ii)

T_{g}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{p}
is bounded (compact) if and only if
g\in\mathcal{C}^{1}(\omega^{*})
(
g\in\mathcal{C}_{0}^{1}(\omega^{*})
).

(iii)

If
0<q<p<\infty
, then the following conditions are equivalent:

(a)

T_{g}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is bounded;

(b)

T_{g}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is compact;

(c)

g\in A_{\omega}^{s}
, where
\frac{1}{s}=\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p}
.

In 2020, Chalmouks
[
4
]
introduced a natural generalization of the Volterra operator,
that is, for any given
g\in H(\mathbb{D})
and
k,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}
such that
0\leq k<n<\infty
,

T_{g}^{n,k}f(z)=I^{n}(f^{(k)}g^{(n-k)})(z),\,\,\,\,\forall f\in H(\mathbb{D})\mbox{ and }z\in\mathbb{D}.

Here,
I^{n}
is the
n
-th iteration of the integration operator
If(z)=\int_{0}^{z}f(t)dt
.
Obviously, when
n=1
and
k=0
,
T_{g}^{1,0}=T_{g}
.
The following result can be deduced from
[
4
]
directly.

Theorem B
.

Let
0<p,q<\infty,k,n\in\mathbb{Z}
satisfying
0\leq k<n
, and
g\in H(\mathbb{D})
.

(i)

If
0<p<q<\infty
,
T_{g}^{n,k}:H^{p}\to H^{q}
is bounded if and only if

\sup\limits_{z\in\mathbb{D}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p}+n-k}|g^{(n-k)}(z)|<\infty.

(ii)

T_{g}^{n,0}:H^{p}\to H^{p}
is bounded if and only if
g\in BMOA
.

(iii)

If
k\geq 1
,
T_{g}^{n,k}:H^{p}\to H^{p}
is bounded if and only if
g\in\mathcal{B}
.

(iv)

If
0<q<p<\infty
and
\frac{1}{s}=\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p}
,
g\in H^{s}
implies the boundedness of
T_{g}^{n,k}:H^{p}\to H^{q}
;
the boundedness of
T_{g}^{2,0}:H^{p}\to H^{q}
implies that
g\in H^{s}
.

In this paper, motivated by
[
4
,
15
]
, we study the boundedness, compactness and Schatten class membership of the generalized Volterra operator
T_{g}^{n,k}
between
A_{\omega}^{p}
and
A_{\omega}^{q}
when
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and
0<p,q<\infty
. Moreover, we define a new operator, i.e., the generalized Toeplitz operator. We study the boundedness, compactness and Schatten class membership of the generalized Toeplitz operator on
A_{\omega}^{2}
and on the Hardy space
H^{2}
.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we state some preliminary results. In Section 3, the boundedness and compactness of
T_{g}^{n,k}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
are investigated when either
0<p\leq q<\infty
or
2\leq q<p<\infty
and
k=0
. In Section 4, the boundedness, compactness and Schatten
p
-class of generalized Toeplitz operators on
A_{\omega}^{2}
are studied. As an application, we obtain the characterization of Schatten class generalized Volterra operator. In the last section, we also investigate the Schatten class generalized Toeplitz operator and generalized Volterra integral operator on the Hardy space
H^{2}
.

Throughout this paper, the letter
C
will denote constants and may differ from one occurrence to the other.
The notation
A\lesssim B
means that there is a positive constant C such that
A\leq CB
.
The notation
A\approx B
means
A\lesssim B
and
B\lesssim A
.

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, we state some lemmas for the proof of our main results. First, we introduce some notations.
Let

\Gamma_{z}=\left\{re^{\mathtt{i}\theta}\in\mathbb{D}:|\theta-\arg z|<\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{r}{|z|}\right)\right\},\,\,z\in\overline{\mathbb{D}}\backslash\{0\}

and

T_{u}=\{z\in\mathbb{D}:u\in\Gamma_{z}\},\,\,\,u\in\mathbb{D}.

By a calculation, we have

\displaystyle T_{u}\subset S_{u}~~\mbox{ and }~~\omega(T_{u})\approx\omega(S_{u})\approx(1-|u|)\hat{\omega}(u).

(1)

Lemma 1

(
[
15
]
)
.

Suppose
0<p<\infty
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
. Let
\omega
be a radial weight. Then

\displaystyle\|f\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle=\omega(\mathbb{D})|f(0)|^{p}+p^{2}\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f(z)|^{p-2}|f^{\prime}(z)|^{2}\omega^{*}(z)dA(z)

\displaystyle\approx\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}|f^{(k)}(0)|^{p}+\int_{\mathbb{D}}\left(\int_{\Gamma_{z}}|f^{(n)}(u)|^{2}\left(1-\frac{|u|}{|z|}\right)^{2n-2}dA(u)\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}\omega(z)dA(z)

\displaystyle\approx\int_{\mathbb{D}}|(Nf)(z)|^{p}\omega(z)dA(z).

Here,
(Nf)(z)=\sup\limits_{u\in\Gamma_{z}}|f(u)|
.

Let
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
and
k\in\mathbb{N}
. The Paley-Littlewood
G_{k}
-function of order
k
is defined by

G_{k}(f)(e^{i\theta})=\left(\int_{0}^{1}|f^{(k)}(re^{i\theta})|^{2}(1-r)^{2k-1}dr\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

In
[
4
]
, Chalmoukis proved the following result, which gave an equivalent norm on

.

Lemma 2
.

Let
k\in\mathbb{N}
,
0<p<\infty
and
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
with
f^{(i)}(0)=0
,
0\leq i<k
. Then,

\|f\|_{H^{p}}^{p}\approx\int_{0}^{2\pi}\left(G_{k}(f)(e^{i\theta})\right)^{p}d\theta.

Suppose

The following lemma is always used to investigate the compactness of linear operators between Bergman spaces induced by doubling weights. See
[

Lemma 5
.

Suppose
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and
0<p,q<\infty
. If
T:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is bounded and linear,
T
is compact
if and only if
\lim\limits_{j\to\infty}\|Tf_{j}\|_{A_{\omega}^{q}}=0
whenever
\{f_{j}\}
is bounded in
A_{\omega}^{p}
and converges to 0 uniformly on any given compact subset of
\mathbb{D}
.

Theorem 1.34 in
[
22
]
plays a very important role when we investigate the Schatten class operator on a Hilbert space.

Lemma 6
.

Suppose
T
is compact on a Hilbert space
H
and
\{\lambda_{j}\}
is the decreasing singular value sequence of
T
.

(i)

For each
j\geq 1
, we have

\lambda_{j+1}=\inf\left\{\|T-F\|_{H\to H}:F\in\mathcal{F}_{j}(H)\right\},

where
\mathcal{F}_{j}(H)
is the set of all linear operators on
H
with rank less than or equal to
j
.

(ii)

For each
j\geq 0
, we have

\lambda_{j+1}=\min_{f_{1},f_{2},\cdots,f_{j}\in H}\max\left\{\|Tf\|_{H}:\|f\|_{H}=1,\langle f,f_{i}\rangle_{H}=0,i=1,2,\cdots,j\right\}.

3.
Generalized Integral operators

Theorem 1
.

Suppose
0<p<q<\infty
,
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
,
k,n\in\mathbb{Z}
satisfying
0\leq k<n
, and
g\in H(\mathbb{D})
.

(i)

T_{g}^{n,k}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is bounded if and only if

\displaystyle\sup_{a\in\mathbb{D}}\frac{(1-|a|)^{n-k}|g^{(n-k)}(a)|}{(1-|a|)^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{q}}\hat{\omega}(a)^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{q}}}<\infty.

(2)

(ii)

T_{g}^{n,k}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is compact if and only if

\displaystyle\lim_{|a|\to 1}\frac{(1-|a|)^{n-k}|g^{(n-k)}(a)|}{(1-|a|)^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{q}}\hat{\omega}(a)^{\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{q}}}=0.

(3)

Proof.

(i).

Suppose that
T_{g}^{n,k}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}
is bounded. For any
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
and
r\in(0,1)
, let

M_{q}(r,f)=\left(\int_{0}^{2\pi}|f(re^{\mathtt{i}\theta})|^{q}d\theta\right)^{\frac{1}{q}},\,\,\,\,M_{\infty}(r,f)=\sup_{\theta\in[0,2\pi)}|f(re^{\mathtt{i}\theta})|.

From the well known facts that

M_{\infty}(r,f)\lesssim\frac{M_{q}(\frac{1+r}{2},f)}{(1-r)^{\frac{1}{q}}},\,\,\,M_{q}(r,f^{\prime})\lesssim\frac{M_{q}(\frac{1+r}{2},f)}{1-r},

we have

M^{q}_{\infty}(r,f^{(n)})\lesssim\frac{\|f\|^{q}_{A_{\omega}^{q}}}{(1-r)^{nq+1}\hat{\omega}(r)}.

Suppose
\gamma
is large enough. For any
a\in\mathbb{D}
, let
F_{a}(z)=\left(\frac{1-|a|^{2}}{1-\overline{a}z}\right)^{\gamma}
.
By Lemma
4
, we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{|g^{(n-k)}(a)|}{(1-|a|)^{k}}\approx|(T_{g}^{n,k}F_{a})^{(n)}(a)|\lesssim\frac{\|T_{g}^{n,k}F_{a}\|_{A_{\omega}^{q}}}{(1-|a|)^{n+\frac{1}{q}}\hat{\omega}(a)^{\frac{1}{q}}}\lesssim\frac{\|T_{g}^{n,k}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\omega}^{q}}}{(1-|a|)^{n+\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p}}\hat{\omega}(a)^{\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p}}},

(4)

which deduces (
2
).

Suppose that (
2
) holds. We will divide the proof into three cases.

Case 1:
q=2
.
By Lemma
1
, Fubini’s Theorem and (
1
), we have

\displaystyle\|T_{g}^{n,k}f\|_{A_{\omega}^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\approx\int_{\mathbb{D}}\left(\int_{\Gamma_{z}}|f^{(k)}(u)g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}\left(1-\frac{|u|}{|z|}\right)^{2n-2}dA(u)\right)\omega(z)dA(z)

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{\mathbb{D}}\left(\int_{T_{u}}|f^{(k)}(u)g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}\left(1-|u|\right)^{2n-2}\omega(z)dA(z)\right)dA(u)

\displaystyle\approx\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f^{(k)}(u)|^{2}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{2n-2}\omega(S_{u})dA(u).

(5)

Here and henceforth, for any given
\beta\in{\mathbb{R}}
,

\displaystyle d\mu_{\beta,k}(u)=|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{\beta}\omega(S_{u})dA(u).

(6)

Then, for any fixed
0<r<\infty
and any
z\in\mathbb{D}
, by (
1
) and
\hat{\omega}\in\mathcal{R}
, we have

\displaystyle\mu_{2n-2,k}(\mathbb{D}(z,r))

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{D}(z,r)}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{2n-2}\omega(S_{u})dA(u)

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{u\in\mathbb{D}(z,r)}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}\int_{\mathbb{D}(z,r)}(1-|u|)^{2n-2}\omega(S_{u})dA(u)

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{u\in\mathbb{D}(z,r)}\left(|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{2n}\omega(S_{u})\right)

\displaystyle\approx(1-|z|)^{2k}\omega(S_{z})^{\frac{2}{p}}\sup_{u\in\mathbb{D}(z,r)}\frac{|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{2n}\omega(S_{u})}{(1-|u|)^{2k}\omega(S_{u})^{\frac{2}{p}}}.

Thus, by (
2
),

\displaystyle\frac{\mu_{2n-2,k}(D(z,r))}{(1-|z|)^{2k}\omega(S_{z})^{\frac{2}{p}}}\lesssim\sup_{u\in\mathbb{D}}\frac{|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{2n}\omega(S_{u})}{(1-|u|)^{2k}\omega(S_{u})^{\frac{2}{p}}}<\infty.

(7)

Then Lemma
3
deduces that
D^{k}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu_{2n-2,k}}^{2}
is bounded. So,
\|T_{g}^{n,k}f\|_{A_{\omega}^{2}}\lesssim\|f\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}}
.

Case 2:
2<q<\infty.
For any
f\in A_{\omega}^{p}
and
\|h\|_{L_{\omega}^{\frac{q}{q-2}}}\leq 1
, let

\displaystyle(M_{\omega}h)(z)=\sup_{z\in S_{a}}\frac{\int_{S_{a}}|h(\eta)|\omega(\eta)dA(\eta)}{\omega(S_{a})},

(8)

\displaystyle J_{f,h}=\int_{\mathbb{D}}|h(z)|\left(\int_{\Gamma_{z}}|f^{(k)}(u)|^{2}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}\left(1-\frac{|u|}{|z|}\right)^{2n-2}dA(u)\right)\omega(z)dA(z),

(9)

and

x=2n-2k-2+k(p+2-\frac{2p}{q}),\,\,\,\,\,\,y=2n-2k-2.

Then,

\displaystyle x+2-k(p+2-\frac{2p}{q})=2n-2k\,\,\mbox{ and }\,\,\frac{2qx}{2q+pq-2p}+\frac{(pq-2p)y}{2q+pq-2p}=2n-2.

(10)

Since
T_{u}\subset S_{u}
and
\omega(T_{u})\approx\omega(S_{u})
, Fubini’s Theorem and Hölder’s inequality imply

\displaystyle J_{f,h}\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f^{(k)}(u)|^{2}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}\left(1-|u|\right)^{2n-2}\left(\int_{T_{u}}|h(z)|\omega(z)dA(z)\right)dA(u)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f^{(k)}(u)|^{2}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}\left(1-|u|\right)^{2n-2}(M_{\omega}h)(u)\omega(S_{u})dA(u)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f^{(k)}(u)|^{2+p-\frac{2p}{q}}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{x}\omega(S_{u})dA(u)\right)^{\frac{2q}{(2+p)q-2p}}

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}}|(M_{\omega}h)(u)|^{1+\frac{2q}{p(q-2)}}|g^{(n-k)}(u)|^{2}(1-|u|)^{y}\omega(S_{u})dA(u)\right)^{\frac{pq-2p}{(2+p)q-2p}}.

(11)

Let
\mu_{x,k}
and
\mu_{y,k}
be defined as in (
6
).
Then, for any fixed
0<r<1
and
z\in\mathbb{D}
, similarly to get (

4.

In order to characterize the Schatten class operator
T_{g}^{n,k}
, we define a new kind of Toeplitz operators
\mathcal{T}^{\omega}_{\mu,k}
, which will be called generalized Toeplitz operators.
Let
B_{z}^{\omega}
be the reproducing kernel of
A_{\omega}^{2}
, that is, for any
f\in A_{\omega}^{2}
and

, we have

f(z)=\langle f,B_{z}^{\omega}\rangle_{A_{\omega}^{2}}=\int_{\mathbb{D}}f(w)\overline{B_{z}^{\omega}(w)}\omega(w)dA(w).

Let
\omega_{j}=\int_{0}^{1}t^{j}\omega(t)dt
. By using the standard orthonormal basis
\{z^{j}/\sqrt{2\omega_{2j+1}}\},j\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
, of
A_{\omega}^{2}
, we obtain

B_{z}^{\omega}(w)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\overline{z}w)^{j}}{2\omega_{2j+1}}.

If
k=0,1,2,\cdots
, let
D^{k}
be the
k
-th differential operator and
B_{z}^{\omega,k}=D^{k}B_{z}^{\omega}
.
The generalized Toeplitz operator
\mathcal{T}_{\mu,k}^{\omega}
is defined by

\mathcal{T}_{\mu,k}^{\omega}f(z)=\int_{\mathbb{D}}f^{(k)}(w)\overline{B_{z}^{\omega,k}(w)}d\mu(w).

Obviously, if
k=0
,
\mathcal{T}_{\mu,k}^{\omega}
is the Toeplitz operator
\mathcal{T}_{\mu}^{\omega}
, which was studied in
[
17
,
20
]
.

Lemma 7

\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}

\displaystyle\|g\|_{B_{p,m},*}^{p}

\displaystyle\approx\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},5r)}|g^{(m)}(z)|^{p}(1-|z|)^{mp}\frac{dA(z)}{(1-|z|)^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},5r)}\left(\int_{D(z,r)}|g^{(m)}(\xi)|^{2}\frac{dA(\xi)}{(1-|\xi|)^{2}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}(1-|z|)^{mp}\frac{dA(z)}{(1-|z|)^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},6r)}|g^{(m)}(\xi)|^{2}(1-|\xi|)^{2m}\frac{dA(\xi)}{(1-|\xi|)^{2}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}\int_{D(a_{j},5r)}\frac{dA(z)}{(1-|z|)^{2}}

\displaystyle\approx\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},6r)}|g^{(m)}(\xi)|^{2}(1-|\xi|)^{2m}\frac{dA(\xi)}{(1-|\xi|)^{2}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}<\infty.

Suppose
g\in B_{p,m}
. Let
\xi_{j}\in\overline{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},5r)}
such that
|g^{(m)}(\xi_{j})|=\sup\limits_{z\in\mathbb{D}(a_{j},5r)}|g^{(m)}(z)|
.
By the subharmonicity of
|g^{(m)}|^{p}
, we have

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},5r)}|g^{(m)}(z)|^{2}(1-|z|)^{2m}\frac{dA(z)}{(1-|z|)^{2}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}\lesssim

\displaystyle|g^{(m)}(\xi_{j})|^{p}(1-|\xi_{j}|^{2})^{mp}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{D}(\xi_{j},r)}|g^{(m)}(z)|^{p}(1-|z|^{2})^{mp-2}dA(z)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},6r)}|g^{(m)}(z)|^{p}(1-|z|^{2})^{mp-2}dA(z),

and

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}(a_{j},5r)}|g^{(m)}(z)|^{2}(1-|z|)^{2m}\frac{dA(z)}{(1-|z|)^{2}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}\lesssim\|g\|_{B_{p,m},*}^{p}<\infty.

The proof is completed.
∎
