Leader-Follower dynamics
Hsin-Lun Li

Abstract
. The original Leader-Follower model, proposed in [ 5 ] , categorizes agents with opinions in [ − 1 , 1 ] [-1,1] into a follower group, a leader group with a positive target opinion in [ 0 , 1 ] [0,1] , and a leader group with a negative target opinion in [ − 1 , 0 ] [-1,0] . Leaders maintain a constant attraction to their target, blending it with the average opinion of their group neighbors at each update. Followers, on the other hand, have a constant attraction to the average opinion of their leader group’s opinion neighbors, also integrating it with their group neighbors’ average opinion. This model was numerically studied in [ 5 ] . This paper extends the Leader-Follower model to include a social relationship, variable degrees over time, high-dimensional opinions, and a flexible number of leader groups. We theoretically investigate conditions for asymptotic stability or consensus, particularly in scenarios where a few leaders can dominate the entire population.
