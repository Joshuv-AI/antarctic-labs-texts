Notes on zeta ratio stabilization
Victor Y. Wang

Abstract
. This semi-expository note clarifies the extent to which recent ideas in homological stability can resolve the Ratios Conjecture over 𝔽 q ​ ( t ) \mathbb{F}_{q}(t) . For large fixed q q , a uniform power saving at distance ≥ q − δ \geq q^{-\delta} from the critical line is possible. This implies cancellation-beyond-GRH in arbitrarily large ranges of moduli relative to the family of L L -functions. It has applications to the statistics of low-lying zeros.

Lemma 3.9

(Skew multiplicity formula)
.

Let
\rho\in\mathsf{P}
.

(1)

If
\rho_{1}>K
, then
m^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n)=0
.

(2)

Suppose
\rho_{1}\leq K
and
l(\rho)\leq c[n]/2
.
Then
m^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n)=M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;0)\neq 0
.
If moreover
G=\operatorname{O}
and
K=1
, then
m^{0}_{\rho^{\ast}(n)}(\bm{x};n)=M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;1)\neq 0
.

Proof.

Case 1:
K=0
.

Then (1) is clear, since
m^{0}_{\rho}
is supported on
\rho=\emptyset
.
In (2) we have
\rho=\emptyset
(since
\rho_{1}\leq 0
),
so
m^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n)=1=M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;0)
.

Case 2:
(G,K)=(\operatorname{O},1)
.

By
[
19
]
*Corollary 5.5.6, the
G
-representations
\bigwedge^{i}\mathbb{C}^{c[n]}
are irreducible and pairwise non-isomorphic for
0\leq i\leq c[n]
.
But for
0\leq i\leq c[n]
we have
\mathbb{S}^{G}_{(1^{i})}\mathbb{C}^{c[n]}\neq 0
and
\mathbb{S}_{(1^{i})}\mathbb{C}^{c[n]}=\bigwedge^{i}\mathbb{C}^{c[n]}
, and thus
\mathbb{S}^{G}_{(1^{i})}\mathbb{C}^{c[n]}=\bigwedge^{i}\mathbb{C}^{c[n]}
.
Now (1) is clear, and in (2) we have

m^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n)=x_{1}^{l(\rho)}=M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;0)

and
m^{0}_{\rho^{\ast}(n)}(\bm{x};n)=x_{1}^{l(\rho^{\ast}(n))}=M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;1)
.

Case 3:
G=\operatorname{Sp}
.

Skew Howe duality for
\operatorname{Sp}
(see
[
9
]
*Remark 7 and references within) implies the following:

m^{0}_{\rho}=0
unless
\rho\subseteq(K^{c[n]/2})
, in which case

m^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n)=(x_{1}\cdots x_{K})^{c[n]/2}\chi^{G}_{(c[n]/2-\rho^{\prime}_{K},\dots,c[n]/2-\rho^{\prime}_{1})}(x_{1}^{\pm 1},\dots,x_{K}^{\pm 1})\neq 0.

By the Weyl character formula for
\operatorname{Sp}

[
9
]
*(43), the last character is

\frac{\det(x_{j}^{c[n]/2-\rho^{\prime}_{K-i+1}+K-i+1}-x_{j}^{-(c[n]/2-\rho^{\prime}_{K-i+1}+K-i+1)})_{1\leq i,j\leq K}}{\det(x_{j}^{K-i+1}-x_{j}^{-(K-i+1)})_{1\leq i,j\leq K}}=\frac{\det(x_{j}^{\rho^{\prime}_{i}-c[n]/2-i}-x_{j}^{-(\rho^{\prime}_{i}-c[n]/2-i)})_{1\leq i,j\leq K}}{\det(x_{j}^{-i}-x_{j}^{i})_{1\leq i,j\leq K}}

(where to equate the two fractions, we swap rows
i
and
K-i+1
in the
K\times K
determinants, and multiply each column by
-1
).
By the Weyl denominator formula for
\operatorname{Sp}

[
9
]
*(44),

\det(x_{j}^{-i}-x_{j}^{i})_{1\leq i,j\leq K}=(x_{1}\cdots x_{K})^{-K}D_{K}(\bm{x}).

This completes the proof of (1)–(2).
∎

Lemma 3.10

(Stable multiplicity bound)
.

Let
\rho\in\mathsf{P}
with
\rho_{1}\leq K
.
Then

\lvert M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;0)\rvert\leq\lvert x_{1}\cdots x_{K}\rvert^{c[n]/2}\prod_{1\leq i\leq K}(\lvert x_{i}\rvert+\lvert x_{i}\rvert^{-1}+2)^{c[n]/2+l(\rho)}.

\lvert M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;1)\rvert

Case 1:
K=0
.

Here
\lvert M^{0}\rvert\leq 1
, by Definition
3.8
.
This suffices.

Case 2:
(G,K)=(\operatorname{O},1)
.

Use Definition
3.8
and note that

\lvert x_{1}\rvert^{d}\leq\lvert x_{1}\rvert^{c[n]/2}\max(\lvert x_{1}\rvert,\lvert x_{1}\rvert^{-1})^{c[n]/2+l(\rho)}

for all
d\in[-l(\rho),c[n]+l(\rho)]
.
Taking
d\in\{l(\rho),c[n]-l(\rho)\}
gives the result.

Case 3:
G=\operatorname{Sp}
.

Sort the absolute values
\lvert\rho^{\prime}_{i}-c[n]/2-i\rvert
for
1\leq i\leq K

into a non-decreasing list of nonnegative integers,
u_{1}\leq\cdots\leq u_{K}
.
By Definition
3.8
,
the ratio
\lvert M^{0}_{\rho}(\bm{x};n;0)/(x_{1}\cdots x_{K})^{c[n]/2}\rvert

vanishes unless
0<u_{1}<\dots<u_{K}
,
in which case it equals
\lvert\chi^{G}_{(u_{K}-K,\dots,u_{1}-1)}(x_{1}^{\pm 1},\dots,x_{K}^{\pm 1})\rvert
by the Weyl character formula.
By
[
9
]
*Remark 7, (50)
and the fact that symplectic Schur polynomials have
nonnegative
integer coefficients (with a combinatorial interpretation; see
[
46
]
*Theorem 2.3 or
[
30
]
*(A.3)–(A.4)),
we have

\lvert\chi^{G}_{\lambda}(x_{1}^{\pm 1},\dots,x_{K}^{\pm 1})\rvert\leq\prod_{1\leq i\leq K}\prod_{1\leq j\leq N}(\lvert x_{i}\rvert+\lvert x_{i}\rvert^{-1}+1+1^{-1})

whenever
\lambda\subseteq(N^{K})
.
Since
-c[n]/2-K\leq\rho^{\prime}_{i}-c[n]/2-i\leq l(\rho)-c[n]/2-1
for
1\leq i\leq K
,
we have
u_{K}-K\leq\max(c[n]/2,l(\rho))\leq c[n]/2+l(\rho)
.
Taking

completes the proof.
(A comparable bound on
M^{0}
in terms of eigenvalues and dimensions, not using positivity, is also possible; cf.
[
3
]
*Lemma 11.3.14.)
∎
