The virtual-state character of the 9 Be 1 / 2 + 1/2^{+} state in the 9 Be( γ \gamma , n n ) 8 Be reaction
Myagmarjav Odsuren, Yuma Kikuchi, Takayuki Myo, Masayuki Aikawa, Kiyoshi Katō

Abstract
We study the character of the first excited 1 / 2 + 1/2^{+} state of 9 Be, which is observed as a low-lying sharp peak in the cross section of 9 Be( γ \gamma , n n ) 2 ​ α 2\alpha just above the 8 Be + n n threshold. Using the α \alpha + α \alpha + n n three-body model, we describe the ground and excited unbound states of 9 Be above the α \alpha + α \alpha + n n threshold. Applying the complex scaling method to the three-body model, we find no 1 / 2 + 1/2^{+} resonant solutions with the scaling angle of θ ≤ 15 \theta\leq 15 degrees, while the low-lying peak in the photodisintegration cross section is reproduced in the present calculation. It is found that the low-lying peak is dominantly explained by the 8 Be + n n component. Furthermore, using the analytical continuation of the coupling constant of the three-body interaction for the α \alpha + α \alpha + n n system, we discuss the virtual-state character of the 1 / 2 + 1/2^{+} state.

We investigate the character of the

9

The calculated photodisintegration cross sections into the

The experimental cross section shows the sharp peak just above the

We cannot find any resonance poles for the

From the decomposition of the calculated cross section, it is shown that the

n

1/2^{+}
state.
In addition, the pole trajectory suggests that the pole of the

\alpha
+
\alpha
+
n
