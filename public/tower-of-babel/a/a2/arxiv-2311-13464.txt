Enhancement of stability of metastable states in the presence of Lévy noise
A. A. Dubkov 1 , C. Guarcello 2a,2b , B. Spagnolo 3,1

Abstract
The barrier crossing event for superdiffusion in the form of symmetric Lévy flights is investigated. We derive from the fractional Fokker-Planck equation a general differential equation with the corresponding conditions useful to calculate the mean residence time of a particle in a fixed interval for an arbitrary smooth potential profile, in particular metastable, with a sink and a Lévy noise with an arbitrary index α \alpha . A closed expression in quadrature of the nonlinear relaxation time for Lévy flights with the index α = 1 \alpha=1 in cubic metastable potential is obtained. Enhancement of the mean residence time in the metastable state, analytically derived, due to Lévy noise is found.

The presence of anomalous diffusion can be explained as a deviation of the statistics of fluctuations from the Gaussian law,
giving rise to the generalization of the Central Limit Theorem by Lévy and Gnedenko
Lev37-Gne54
. One of the
manifestations of anomalous diffusion are Lévy flights. These are stochastic processes characterized by the occurrence of
extremely long jumps, so that their trajectories are not continuous anymore. The length of these jumps is distributed according
to a Lévy stable statistics with a power law. The relevance of the Lévy motion appears in many physical, natural, and social
complex systems. The Lévy-type statistics, in fact, is observed in various scientific areas, where scale invariance phenomena
take place, see
Che06
;
Dub08
;
Pad19
and references therein. The Lévy flights are a special class of Markovian processes,
therefore the Markovian analysis can be used to derive the generalized Kolmogorov equation directly from Langevin equation
with Lévy noise
Dub05
.

Metastability, as well as the transition process between metastable states, is a ubiquitous phenomenon in nature affecting
different fields of natural sciences and advancing in its understanding it is a key challenge in complex systems
Fia05-Val18
;
Men13
;
Ser16
;
Gho20
;
Luc22
;
Hru18
;
Mor21
. Experimental
Nos09
;

and theoretical
Bia09
;
Mor21

results show that long-lived metastable states, even if observed in different areas of physics, were not explained.

Recently, the first passage time properties of Lévy flights for random search strategies have been investigated
Pal19
.
Furthermore, noise-induced escape from a metastable state in the presence of Lévy noise governs a plethora of transition phenomena
in complex systems, of physical, chemical and biological nature, ranging from the motion of molecules to climate signals (see
Che06
;
Ser16
;

;
Che07
;

;

how the barrier crossing behavior in different potential profiles
U(x)

L_{\alpha}(t)
with index
\alpha

\frac{dx}{dt}=-U^{\prime}\left(x\right)+L_{\alpha}\left(t\right),

(1)

where

characteristics, there are a lot of numerical results and some analytical approximations

Che07

Bau23

However, exact analytical results for barrier crossing problems in metastable systems in the presence of Lévy noise are lacking.
Obtaining exact analytical results remains an open problem. This Letter aims to answer this question by investigating the barrier crossing
process in a system with a metastable state driven by Lévy noise. Starting from the fractional Fokker-Planck equation corresponding to
Eq. (
1

average time spent by the particle into the potential well (see Fig.

metastable state, which characterizes its stability.

Here, we address the following open questions: (

The anomalous diffusion in the form of Lévy flights, for a particle moving in a potential profile
U(x)
, is described by the
following Fokker-Planck equation with fractional space derivative

\frac{\partial P}{\partial t}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[U^{\prime}\left(x\right)P\right]+D_{\alpha}\frac{\partial^{\alpha}P}{\partial\left|x\right|^{\alpha}}\,,

(2)

where
P\left(x,t|x_{0},0\right)
is the transition probability density, and
D_{\alpha}
is the noise intensity parameter, in the sense that the
size of a cloud of particles undergoing Lévy motion increases with time as
\left(D_{\alpha}t\right)^{1/\alpha}
. Equation (
2
) can
be easily obtained directly from Eq. (
1
)
Dub05
.

The NLRT for a diffusion in a potential profile with a sink, see Fig.
1
, is defined as

T\left(x_{0}\right)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\mathrm{Pr}\left(t,x_{0}\right)dt,

(3)

where

\mathrm{Pr}\left(t,x_{0}\right)=\int_{L_{1}}^{L_{2}}P\left(x,t|x_{0},0\right)dx

(4)

represents the probability to find a particle in the interval
\left(L_{1},L_{2}\right)
at the time
t
, if it starts from some internal point

x_{0}
. Substituting Eq. (
4
) into Eq. (
3
) and changing the order of integration, we arrive at

T\left(x_{0}\right)=\int_{L_{1}}^{L_{2}}Y\left(x,x_{0}\right)dx,

(5)

where

Y\left(x,x_{0}\right)=\int_{0}^{\infty}P\left(x,t|x_{0},0\right)dt.

(6)

Integrating Eq. (
2
) with respect to
t
from
0
to
\infty
and taking into account the initial condition
P\left(x,0|x_{0},0\right)=\delta\left(x-x_{0}\right)
and the asymptotic condition
P\left(x,\infty|x_{0},0\right)=0
(for a potential with a sink), we obtain the following
equation for the function
Y\left(x,x_{0}\right)

\frac{d}{dx}\left[U^{\prime}\left(x\right)Y\right]+D_{\alpha}\frac{d^{\alpha}Y}{d\left|x\right|^{\alpha}}=-\delta\left(x-x_{0}\right).

(7)

To solve Eq. (
7

\widetilde{Y}\left(k,x_{0}\right)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}Y\left(x,x_{0}\right)\,e^{ikx}\,dx.

(8)

For a smooth potential profiles
U\left(x\right)

7

ik\,U^{\prime}\left(-i\frac{d}{dk}\right)\widetilde{Y}+D_{\alpha}\left|k\right|^{\alpha}\widetilde{Y}=e^{ikx_{0}}.

It is convenient to introduce the new function, namely, the derivative of the function
\widetilde{Y}\left(k,x_{0}\right)

with respect to
x_{0}

Differentiating both parts of Eq. (

Substituting

from the backward Fourier transformation into Eq. (

After differentiation of both sides of Eq. (

(13)

One can easily check that, after replacing
k
with
-k

(14)

where

If a sink of the potential profile

After integrating Eq. (

Thus, it is sufficient to solve Eq. (

for an arbitrary Lévy index
\alpha
and an arbitrary smooth potential profile with a sink. For a Cauchy driven noise (
\alpha=1

closed expression in quadratures of the NLRT as a function of the noise intensity parameter, the initial position, and the parameters of the
potential. The interplay between trapping in the metastable state, at small noise intensities, and long jumps of Lévy flights produces a finite
nonmonotonic enhancement of the mean residence time in the metastable state. Our general equations provide a useful tool to describe different dynamical
behaviors in complex systems, characterized by anomalous diffusion and non-exponential relaxation phenomena, such as spatial
extended systems
