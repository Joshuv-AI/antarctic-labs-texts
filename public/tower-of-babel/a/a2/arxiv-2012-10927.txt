A PROOF of Weak Graph Positivity, for a Large Range of the Parameters
Paul Federbush

Abstract
One deals with r r -regular bipartite graphs with 2 ​ n 2n vertices. In a previous paper Butera, Pernici, and the author have introduced a quantity d ⁡ ( i ) d(i) , a function of the number of i i -matchings, and conjectured that as n n goes to infinity the fraction of graphs that satisfy Δ k ​ d ​ ( i ) ≥ 0 \Delta^{k}d(i)\geq 0 , for all k k and i i , approaches 1 1 . Here Δ \Delta is the finite difference operator. This conjecture we called the “graph positivity conjecture”. “Weak graph positivity” is the conjecture that for each i i and k k the probability that Δ k ​ d ​ ( i ) ≥ 0 \Delta^{k}d(i)\geq 0 goes to 1 1 as n n goes to infinity. Here we prove this for the range of parameters where r ≤ 10 , i + k ≤ 100 , k ≤ 27 r\leq 10,i+k\leq 100,k\leq 27 . or i + k ≤ 29 i+k\leq 29 all r r . A formalism of Wanless as systematized by Pernici is central to this effort.
