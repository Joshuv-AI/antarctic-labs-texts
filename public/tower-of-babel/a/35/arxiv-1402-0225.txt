An Intuitionisticaly based Description Logic
Alexandre Rademaker, Edward Hermann Haeusler

Abstract
This article presents i ​ 𝒜 ​ ℒ ​ 𝒞 i\mathcal{ALC} , an intuitionistic version of the classical description logic 𝒜 ​ ℒ ​ 𝒞 \mathcal{ALC} , based on the framework for constructive modal logics presented by Simpson [ 14 ] and related to description languages, via hybrid logics, by dePaiva [ 3 ] . This article corrects and extends the presentation of i ​ 𝒜 ​ ℒ ​ 𝒞 i\mathcal{ALC} appearing in [ 4 ] . It points out the difference between i ​ 𝒜 ​ ℒ ​ 𝒞 i\mathcal{ALC} and the intuitionistic hybrid logic presented in [ 3 ] . Completeness and soundness proofs are provided. A brief discussion on the computational complexity of i ​ 𝒜 ​ ℒ ​ 𝒞 i\mathcal{ALC} provability is taken. It is worth mentioning that i ​ 𝒜 ​ ℒ ​ 𝒞 i\mathcal{ALC} is used to formalize legal knowledge [ 10 , 9 , 8 , 7 ] , and in fact, was specifically designed to this goal.
