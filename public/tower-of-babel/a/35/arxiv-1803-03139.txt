A simple proof for Kazmi et al.’s iterative scheme
E. Soori and Ravi P. Agarwal

Abstract
. In this paper we provide a simple proof of the existence iterative scheme by using two Hilbert spaces due to Kazmi et al. [K. R. Kazmi, R. Ali, M. Furkan, Hybrid iterative method for split monotone …, Numer Algor, 2017].
