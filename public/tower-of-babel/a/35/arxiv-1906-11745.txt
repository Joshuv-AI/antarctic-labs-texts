The Racah Algebra as a Subalgebra of the Bannai–Ito Algebra
Hau-Wen HUANG

Abstract
Assume that 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} is a field with char ⁡ 𝔽 ≠ 2 \operatorname{char}{\mathbb{F}}\not=2 . The Racah algebra ℜ \Re is a unital associative 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} -algebra defined by generators and relations. The generators are A A , B B , C C , D D and the relations assert that [ A , B ] = [ B , C ] = [ C , A ] = 2 ​ D [A,B]=[B,C]=[C,A]=2D and each of [ A , D ] + A ​ C − B ​ A [A,D]+AC-BA , [ B , D ] + B ​ A − C ​ B [B,D]+BA-CB , [ C , D ] + C ​ B − A ​ C [C,D]+CB-AC is central in ℜ \Re . The Bannai–Ito algebra 𝔅 ​ ℑ \mathfrak{BI} is a unital associative 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} -algebra generated by X X , Y Y , Z Z and the relations assert that each of { X , Y } − Z \{X,Y\}-Z , { Y , Z } − X \{Y,Z\}-X , { Z , X } − Y \{Z,X\}-Y is central in 𝔅 ​ ℑ \mathfrak{BI} . It was discovered that there exists an 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} -algebra homomorphism ζ : ℜ → 𝔅 ​ ℑ \zeta\colon\Re\to\mathfrak{BI} that sends A ↦ ( 2 ​ X − 3 ) ​ ( 2 ​ X + 1 ) 16 A\mapsto\frac{(2X-3)(2X+1)}{16} , B ↦ ( 2 ​ Y − 3 ) ​ ( 2 ​ Y + 1 ) 16 B\mapsto\frac{(2Y-3)(2Y+1)}{16} , C ↦ ( 2 ​ Z − 3 ) ​ ( 2 ​ Z + 1 ) 16 C\mapsto\frac{(2Z-3)(2Z+1)}{16} . We show that ζ \zeta is injective and therefore ℜ \Re can be considered as an 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} -subalgebra of 𝔅 ​ ℑ \mathfrak{BI} . Moreover we show that any Casimir element of ℜ \Re can be uniquely expressed as a polynomial in { X , Y } − Z \{X,Y\}-Z , { Y , Z } − X \{Y,Z\}-X , { Z , X } − Y \{Z,X\}-Y and X + Y + Z X+Y+Z with coefficients in 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} .
