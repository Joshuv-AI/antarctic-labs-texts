\prod_{c}(u^{j}_{2})=1\cdot(\Delta(G)+4)\cdot(\Delta(G)+5)
. Since

\prod_{c}(v_{j})\geq 1\cdot 2\cdot(\Delta(G)+4)\cdot(\Delta(G)+5)
,
then
\frac{\prod_{c}(v_{j})}{\prod_{c}(u^{j}_{2})}\geq 2
. Clearly, we have

\Sigma_{c}(v_{j})>\Sigma_{c}(u^{j}_{i})
for each
1\leq j\leq n_{g}
,
1\leq i\leq 2
.

If
n_{h}=3
, then
\Delta(H)\leq 2
,
\chi^{\prime}(H)\leq 3
. We have

\prod_{c}(u^{j}_{3})\leq 2\cdot 3\cdot(\Delta(G)+5)\cdot(\Delta(G)+6)
.
Since
\prod_{c}(v_{j})\geq 1\cdot 2\cdot(\Delta(G)+4)\cdot(\Delta(G)+5)(\Delta(G)+6)
, then

\frac{\prod_{c}(v_{j})}{\prod_{c}(u^{j}_{3})}\geq\frac{5}{3}
. Clearly, we
have
\Sigma_{c}(v_{j})>\Sigma_{c}(u^{j}_{i})
for each
1\leq j\leq n_{g}
,

1\leq i\leq 3
.

Otherwise, i.e.
n_{h}\geq 4
. Clearly,
\chi^{\prime}(H)\leq 4
. We have

\prod_{c}(u^{j}_{n_{h}})\leq 2\cdot 3\cdot 4\cdot(\Delta(G)+n_{h}+2)\cdot(\Delta(G)+n_{h}+3)
.
Since
\prod_{c}(v_{j})\geq 1\cdot 2\cdot(\Delta(G)+4)\cdot(\Delta(G)+5)\cdot\ldots\cdot(\Delta(G)+n_{h}+2)\cdot(\Delta(G)+n_{h}+3)
,
clearly,
\frac{\prod_{c}(v_{j})}{\prod_{c}(u^{j}_{n_{h}})}\geq\frac{5}{2}
.
So we have
\Sigma_{c}(v_{j})>\Sigma_{c}(v^{j}_{i})
for each
1\leq j\leq n_{g}
and
1\leq i\leq n_{h}
.

∎
