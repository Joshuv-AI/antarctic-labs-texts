Abstract
Using e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} collision data collected with the BESIII detector at the BEPCII collider corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 5.2 fb -1 at center-of-mass energies ( s \sqrt{s} ) from 4.009 to 4.600 GeV , the process e + ​ e − → π 0 ​ π 0 ​ ψ ​ ( 3686 ) e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\psi(3686) is studied for the first time. The corresponding Born cross sections are measured and found to be half of those of the reaction e + ​ e − → π + ​ π − ​ ψ ​ ( 3686 ) e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686) . This is consistent with the expectation from isospin symmetry. are described with the MC simulated shapes convolved with a Gaussian function, where the parameters of the Gaussian function are determined in the fit, in order to account for the resolution difference and potential mass shift between the data and MC simulation.
The other backgrounds are described with a linear function.
The fits curves are shown in Fig.

The Born cross section is calculated from

\sigma^{\rm B}=\frac{N^{\rm obs}}{\mathcal{L}_{\rm int}~(1+\delta^{\rm r})~(1+\delta^{\rm v})~\mathcal{B}~\epsilon},

(1)

where
\mathcal{L}_{\rm int}
is the integrated luminosity,
N^{\rm obs}
is the signal yield from the fit,

(1+\delta^{\rm r})
is the ISR correction factor which is obtained by using a QED calculation
ISR
and incorporating the input lineshape
of the cross section which is taken to be the same as that of
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)
from the Belle experiment
Y4360Belle
,

(1+\delta^{\rm v})
is the vacuum polarization factor taken from a QED calculation with an accuracy of 0.5%
VR
,

\mathcal{B}
is the product of the branching fractions in the decay chain, 3.89%, taken from the PDG
PDG
, and
\epsilon
is the detection efficiency.
The numbers used in the Born cross section calculation and the cross sections are summarized in the Supplemental Material
supp
.
The comparison of the Born cross section of
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\psi(3686)
to that of
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)
for the data samples with large luminosities is shown in Fig.
.
The Born cross sections of
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)
are also calculated with the corresponding event yields, and are consistent with the results in Ref.
Y4360Bes
. An alternative fit to the
M_{\rm recoil}(\pi^{+}\pi^{-})
spectra, which have a narrow peak for
e^{+}e^{-}

\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)
and a broad bump depending on c.m. energy for
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\psi(3686)
, is performed. In the fit, the PDF is described by the similar strategy with the
M(\pi^{+}\pi^{-}J/\psi)
spectra. The resulting Born cross sections are consistent with the nominal values for the two processes.

Jpipi

luminosity

In summary, based on a data sample of
e^{+}e^{-}
collision data corresponding to 5.2
fb
-1
at 16 c.m. energy points between 4.009 and 4.600
GeV
collected with the BESIII detector, the Born cross sections for
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\psi(3686)
at these energy points have been measured for the first time. They are found to be half of those for
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)

Y4360Bes
within uncertainties, consistent with the expectation from isospin symmetry. The Dalitz plots of
\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\psi(3686)
are consistent with those in the
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)

Y4360Bes
at all energy points. Furthermore, a structure is observed in
\pi^{0}\psi(3686)
with a mass of (
4038.7\pm 6.5
)
MeV/
c^{2}
at
\sqrt{s}=4.416~\mbox{GeV}
, which confirms the structure in the charged mode. No obvious
Z_{c}(3900)^{0}
state is observed in the fit. The new observed structure may provide insight into the properties of the charged structure observed in
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3686)
as well as the charmoniumlike
Z_{c}
states observed in analogous decay modes and in charmed meson pairs. However, the fit curve does not match the data perfectly. A future larger statistics sample of data and theoretical input incorporating possible interference effects could lead to a better understanding of the structure.
