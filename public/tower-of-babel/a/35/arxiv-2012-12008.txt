Introduction

Witten’s celebrated paper
Witten:1988hf
has shown that the Chern-Simons theory provides a natural framework for quantum invariants of 3-manifolds and knots. The Chern-Simons path integral on a 3-manifold

S=\frac{k}{4\pi}\int_{M_{3}}\text{Tr}\left(A\wedge dA+\frac{2}{3}A\wedge A\wedge A\right)~,

of the gauge group

(1)

\SO

2.1

To this end, let us first recall the generalized volume conjecture

;
Gukov:2008jlx

\mathscr{Q}_{[r]}(K;a,q,t):=\mathscr{Q}_{[r]}(K;a,q,t_{r}=t,t_{c}=1)~,

(11)

where
\mathscr{Q}=\mathscr{P}\textrm{ or }\mathscr{F}
represents Poincaré polynomials of either HOMFLY-PT or Kauffman homology.
If a knot
K
satisfies the exponential growth property
Gukov:2011ry
;
Wedrich:2016smm
(such as thin knots and torus knots)

\mathscr{Q}_{[r]}(K;a,q=1,t)=\Bigl[\mathscr{Q}_{[1]}(K;a,q=1,t)\Bigr]^{r}~,

it is conjectured
(
Kucharski:2017ogk
, Conjecture 4.4)
that the superpolynomial colored by a symmetric representation can be expressed as

\mathscr{Q}_{[r]}(K;a,q,t)=\sum_{l_{1}+l_{2}+\cdots+l_{n}=r}\left[\begin{array}[]{c}r\\
l_{1},l_{2},\ldots,l_{n}\end{array}\right]_{q^{2}}a^{\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_{i}l_{i}}t^{\sum_{i=1}^{n}t_{i}l_{i}}q^{\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}Q_{i,j}l_{i}l_{j}+\sum_{i=1}^{n}q_{i}l_{i}}~,

(12)

where
(a_{i},q_{i},t_{i})
are constants and

is a quadratic form. Here the

\left[\begin{array}[]{c}r\\
l_{1},l_{2},\ldots,l_{n}\end{array}\right]_{q}:=\frac{\left(q;q\right)_{r}}{\left(q;q\right)_{l_{1}}\left(q;q\right)_{l_{2}}\cdots\left(q;q\right)_{l_{n}}}~.

In the large color limit

\displaystyle q=e^{\hbar}\rightarrow 1,\quad r\rightarrow\infty,\quad a=\text{fixed},\quad t=\text{fixed},\quad x=q^{2r}=\text{fixed}~,

(13)

a superpolynomial asymptotes to the form

\mathscr{Q}_{[r]}(K;a,q,t)\overset{r\to\infty,\hbar\to 0}{\underset{x=q^{2r}}{\sim}}\exp\left(\frac{1}{2\hbar}\int\log y\frac{dx}{x}+\cdots\right)~,

(14)

where the integral is carried out on the zero locus of classical super-A-polynomial

\mathscr{A}(K;x,y,a,t)=0.

The ellipsis in (

If

\mathscr{Q}_{\left[r\right]}(K;a,q,t)\sim\int e^{\frac{1}{2\hbar}\left(\widetilde{\mathscr{W}}(K;z_{1},\cdots,z_{n};x,a,t)+\mathcal{O}(\hbar)\right)}dx\prod_{i=1}^{n}dz_{i}~,

where

The leading asymptotic behavior (

\exp\left(z_{i}\frac{\partial\widetilde{\mathscr{W}}(K;z_{1},\cdots,z_{n};x,a,t)}{\partial z_{i}}\right)\bigg|_{z_{i}=z_{i}^{*}}=1~.

(16)

and the zero locus of the classical super-A-polynomial is determined by

\exp\left({x\frac{\partial\widetilde{\mathscr{W}}(K;z_{1}^{*},\cdots,z_{n}^{*};x,a,t)}{\partial x}}\right)=y~.

(17)

Now let us consider the relationship between super-

\SO(N)
(

)

-symbols for all symmetric representations,

\displaystyle\begin{Bmatrix}a&b&e\\

\displaystyle\left(\frac{[2c+N-2]_{q}[2d+N-2]_{q}[2e+N-2]_{q}}{[2]_{q}^{3}\dim_{q}[c]\dim_{q}[d]\dim_{q}[e]}\right)^{1/2}
