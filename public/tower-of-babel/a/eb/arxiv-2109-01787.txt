On the subgroup of B 4 B_{4} that contains the kernel of Burau representation
A. Beridze, L. Davitadze

Abstract
It is known that there are braids α \alpha and β \beta in the braid group B 4 B_{4} , such that the group ⟨ α , β ⟩ \langle\alpha,\beta\rangle is a fee subgroup [ Gor-Lin ] , which contains the kernel K K of the Burau map ρ 4 : B 4 → G ​ L ​ ( 3 , ℤ ⁡ [ t , t − 1 ] ) \rho_{4}:B_{4}\to GL\left(3,\mathbb{Z}[t,t^{-1}]\right) [ Bok-Ves ] , [ Ber-Tra3 ] . In this paper we will prove that K K is subgroup of G = ⟨ τ , Δ ⟩ G=\langle\tau,\Delta\rangle , where τ \tau and Δ \Delta are fourth and square roots of the generator θ \theta of the center Z Z of the group B 4 B_{4} . Consequently, we will write elements of K K in terms of τ i , i = 1 , 2 , 3 \tau^{i},~~i=1,2,3 and Δ \Delta . Moreover, we will show that the quotient group G / Z G/Z is isomorphic to the free product Z 4 ∗ Z 2 Z_{4}*Z_{2} .
