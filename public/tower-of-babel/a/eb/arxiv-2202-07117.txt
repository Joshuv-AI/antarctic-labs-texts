Lemma 2.1

Let

Proof.

Since for

In order to show that

By the Hölder inequality, for

\displaystyle\leqslant\left(\int_{\mathbb{R^{N}}}|u_{n}-u|^{2p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}|B(R)|^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}\leqslant C\|u_{n}-u\|^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}|B(R)|^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}=o_{R}(1),

\displaystyle\leqslant C\|u_{n}\|_{\lambda_{n}}^{\theta p}\left(\int_{A(R)}u_{n}^{2}\,dx+\int_{B(R)}u_{n}^{2}\,dx\right)^{\frac{(1-\theta)p}{2}}<\varepsilon,
