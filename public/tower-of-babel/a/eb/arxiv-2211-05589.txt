Abstract
. A general method of producing correspondences and spectral categories out of symmetric ring objects in general categories is given. As an application, stable homotopy theory of spectra S ​ H SH is recovered from modules over a commutative symmetric ring spectrum defined in terms of framed correspondences over an algebraically closed field. Another application recovers stable motivic homotopy theory S ​ H ​ ( k ) SH(k) from spectral modules over associated spectral categories.

By

Given a finite set

Note that a

Given two maps

6.
Enriched motivic homotopy theory

One of the approaches to Morel–Voevodsky’s stable motivic homotopy theory

over a field

is by means of symmetric

-spectra

, where

(see, e.g.,
[

\mathcal{M}
equipped with the flasque motivic model structure in the sense of
[
23

and then pass to

equipped with the stable model structure. The homotopy category of the latter model category
is denoted by

A genuinely local approach to

, envisioned by Voevodsky in 2001,
is presented in
[
20

framed correspondences and the machinery of framed motives
[
19

In this section we suggest yet another (genuinely local) approach to

and, more generally, a local model for the category of
E
-modules
in

, where

is a symmetric Thom ring spectrum. It is an application of enriched
category theory of spectral categories and spectral modules of Section

17

to construct the theory of

Following

14

, a symmetric

-spectrum

if each motivic space

has the form

We say that a Thom spectrum

in

is strictly greater than

for all

. The
T
-spectrum

is said to be

of even permutations up to

Unless it is specified otherwise

is a symmetric Thom ring

\to(\mathbb{Z}\Fr^{E}(X,V\sqcup W),\mathbb{Z}\Fr^{E}(X,(V\sqcup W)\otimes S^{1}),\ldots)

is a stable equivalence, where
\mathbb{Z}\Fr^{E}(X,V)
is the reduced free Abelian group of the pointed set
\Fr^{E}(X,V)
.
Repeating the proof of
[
15
, Theorem 1.2]
word for word,
\mathbb{Z}(\gamma)
is stably equivalent to the map

\delta:(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V\otimes S^{1}),\ldots)\vee(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W\otimes S^{1}),\ldots)\to\\
\to(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V\sqcup W),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,(V\sqcup W)\otimes S^{1}),\ldots),

where
\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V)=\colim_{n}\mathbb{Z}F^{E}_{n}(X,V)
with
\mathbb{Z}F_{n}^{E}(X,V)
the free Abelian group freely generated by

E
-framed correspondences with connected support
[
14
]
. Since
\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V\sqcup W)=\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V)\times\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W)
,
the map
\delta
equals the stable equivalence of
S^{1}
-spectra

(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V\otimes S^{1}),\ldots)\vee(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W\otimes S^{1}),\ldots)\to\\
\to(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,V\otimes S^{1}),\ldots)\times(\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W),\mathbb{Z}F^{E}(X,W\otimes S^{1}),\ldots).

This completes the proof of the lemma.
∎

The
Cech model category
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{Cech}
associated with
Nisnevich topology is obtained from
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}
by
Bousfield localization with respect to all maps

\eta:\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,\check{U}_{*})\to\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X)
running over the set of finite Nisnevich covers.
It follows from
[
36
, Corollary 5.10]
(see
also
[
5
]
) that
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{Cech}
coincides with the
Nisnevich local model category
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{\nis}
, with
stable weak equivalences defined stalkwise.

We say that a spectral category
\mathcal{O}
is
Cech excisive
if for any finite
Nisnevich cover
\{U_{i}\to X\}
the induced map
\eta:\mathcal{O}(-,\check{U}_{*})\to\mathcal{O}(-,X)

is a local stable weak equivalence.

6.11 Theorem
.

Let
k
be any field. The commutative spectral category
\mathcal{O}^{E}
is Cech excisive.
The Cech model structure coincides with
Nisnevich local model structure on
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}
. This model structure
has all the properties of Theorem
6.2
.
The homotopy category
D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}(k)
of
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{Cech}
is closed symmetric monoidal
compactly generated triangulated with compact
generators being the representables
\{\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X)\mid X\in\mathrm{Sm}/k\}
.
The monoidal product
\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X)\wedge\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,Y)
in
D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}(k)

is isomorphic to
\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X\times Y)
.

Proof.

By Lemma
6.10
each map

\vee_{i_{0},\ldots,i_{n}}\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,U_{i_{0}\ldots i_{n}})\to\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,\check{C}(U_{n}))
is a schemewise
stable equivalence, and hence the realization is. The proof of
[
35
, Theorem 4.4]
shows that the
map
\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,\check{C}(U_{*}))\to\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X)
is a level local equivalence. We see that
\eta

is a local stable weak equivalence. The rest is now straightforward.
∎

The homotopy category
D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}(k)
plays the same role as the derived category

D(\textrm{Shv}_{tr}^{\nis}(\textrm{Sm}/k))
of cochain complexes of Nisnevich sheaves with transfers.
Recall from
[
34
]
that Voevodsky’s category of motives
DM^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)
is the localisation of
D(\textrm{Shv}_{tr}^{\nis}(\textrm{Sm}/k))

with respect to the family
\{\mathbb{Z}_{tr}(-,X\times\mathbb{A}^{1})\to\mathbb{Z}_{tr}(-,X)\mid X\in\textrm{Sm}/k\}
. If
k
is perfect,

DM^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)
is equivalent to the full subcategory of
D(\textrm{Shv}_{tr}^{\nis}(\textrm{Sm}/k))
consisting of
chain complexes with homotopy invariant cohomology sheaves
[
34
]
.
Likewise, localize
\mathsf{Mod}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{Cech}
with respect to the maps

\{\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X\times\mathbb{A}^{1})\to\mathcal{O}^{E}(-,X)\mid X\in\textrm{Sm}/k\}
.
Denote by
D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}_{\mot}(k)
its homotopy category.

6.12 Theorem
.

Let
k
be a perfect field. The homotopy category
D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}_{\mot}(k)
is equivalent to the full triangulated subcategory
DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)

of
D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}(k)
consisting of modules with homotopy invariant sheaves of stable homotopy groups.
The inclusion
DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)\to D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}(k)
has a right adjoint

C_{*}
taking a module
M\in D\mathcal{O}^{E,\mathsf{eff}}(k)
to its Suslin complex
C_{*}(M)
.

Moreover, there is
a triangulated equivalence of compactly generated triangulated categories

DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)\simeq\mathsf{SH}^{\nis}_{S^{1}}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{\Delta}.

Proof.

The proof of the first part is like that of
[
17
, Theorem 3.5]
. One also uses here the fact
that if
k
is perfect then by
[
18
]
(complemented by
[
4
]
in
characteristic 2 and by
[
3
, A.27]
for finite fields) any

\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariant quasi-stable radditive framed presheaf of Abelian
groups
\mathcal{F}
, the associated Nisnevich sheaf
\mathcal{F}_{\nis}
is strictly
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariant.

The equivalence
DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)\simeq\mathsf{SH}^{\nis}_{S^{1}}\mathcal{O}^{E}_{\Delta}
follows from the fact that
both categories are compactly generated by symmetric
E
-framed motives with the same Hom-sets
(as usual we use Lemma
4.1
here).
∎

We call the category
DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)
from the preceding theorem the
triangulated category of
E
-framed motives
.
We finish the paper with the following result saying that
DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)
recovers the category of effective
E
-modules in
SH(k)
.

6.13 Corollary
.

Let
k
be a perfect field. There is a triangulated equivalence of compactly generated triangulated categories

DE^{\mathsf{eff}}(k)\simeq\Mod^{\mathsf{eff}}_{SH(k)}E.

Proof.

This follows from the preceding theorem and Corollary
6.8
.
∎
