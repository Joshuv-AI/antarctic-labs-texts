Abstract
. We prove potential automorphy results for a single Galois representation G F → G ​ L n ​ ( ℚ ¯ l ) G_{F}\rightarrow GL_{n}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}) where F F is a CM number field. is simple, we see that
H\subset\widetilde{F}_{i}^{\prime}
. Now minimality gives
\widetilde{F}_{i-1}^{\prime}\cap H=F
, so that
\Delta_{i}^{m}=\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{F}_{i}^{\prime}/\widetilde{F}_{i-1}^{\prime})\twoheadrightarrow\mathrm{Gal}(H/F)
, thus
\Delta_{i}=PSL_{2}(k)
,

. We claim there exists

such that
\sigma H\subset F_{i}^{\prime}\widetilde{F}_{i-1}^{\prime}
. We may write
\widetilde{F}_{i}^{\prime}
as the composite of

\widetilde{F}_{i-1}^{\prime}
. Now because

\widetilde{F}_{i-1}^{\prime}\cap H=F

. We may pick the smallest

\widetilde{F}_{i-1}^{\prime}

over

Consider the image of
