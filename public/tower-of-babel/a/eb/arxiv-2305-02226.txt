Reiner Czerwinski

Abstract
The 𝐏 {\mathbf{P}} versus 𝐍𝐏 {\mathbf{NP}} problem is still unsolved. But there are several oracles with 𝐏 {\mathbf{P}} unequal 𝐍𝐏 {\mathbf{NP}} relative to them. Here we will prove, that 𝐏 ≠ 𝐍𝐏 {\mathbf{P}}\not={\mathbf{NP}} relative to a 𝐏 {\mathbf{P}} -complete oracle. In this paper, we use padding arguments as the proof method. The padding arguments are not bounded by a computable function. Such as we can use methods from computability theory to separate complexity classes.

2
Computation without Time limit

To analyze, whether
U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}^{U_{{\mathbf{P}}}}
is efficiently computable, we define
two sets analog to
U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
and
U_{{\mathbf{P}}}

without time limit.

\begin{split}D_{{\mathbf{P}}}=\{\langle M,x\rangle\mid\text{ TM }M\text{ accepts }x\}\\
D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}=\{\{\langle M,1^{n}\rangle\mid\exists x\in\{0,1\}^{n}\text{ where TM }M\text{ accepts }x\}\end{split}

So,
\langle M,x\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{P}}}
, if
x\in L(M)
and

\langle M,1^{n}\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
, if
\exists x\in\{0,1\}^{n}
with
x\in L(M)
.

It is obvious, that

•

\langle M,x\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{P}}}
iff
\exists t:\langle M,x,1^{t}\rangle\in U_{{\mathbf{P}}}

•

\langle M,1^{n}\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
iff
\exists t:\langle M,1^{n},1^{t}\rangle\in U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}

Lemma 1
.

If
\langle M,\{0,1\}^{n}\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
, then it is undecidable,
which
x\in\{0,1\}^{n}
is in
L(M)
.

Proof.

Let
M
be an arbitrary TM. Due to Rice’s theorem
[
4
]
every nontrivial
property of the language
L(M)
is undecidable.
Let
x\in\{0,1\}^{n}
arbitrary but fixed. Whether
x\in L(M)
is undecidable.
Also, if
x
is the only element in
\bigl(\{0,1\}^{n}\cap L(M)\bigr)
, is
undecidable, because it is a nontrivial property of
L(M)
. So,
it is undecidable, which element with length
n
is in
L(M)
.
∎

Corollary 1
.

Let
M
be an arbitrary TM and
n\in\mathbb{N}
.
To test whether
\langle M,1^{n}\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
one needs

\theta(2^{n})
accesses to the oracle
D_{{\mathbf{P}}}
in the worst case.

Proof.

Due to lemma
1
it is undecidable for an arbitrary
TM
M
, whether an input
x
with length
n
is in
L(M)
.
But the characteristic function of
L(M)
:

\chi_{L(M)}(x)=\begin{cases}1&x\in L(M)\\
0&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}

is computable with oracle
D_{{\mathbf{P}}}
as a black box. So,
the black box complexity is exponential.
∎

3
Main Result

In this section we conclude from
D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
with oracle
D_{{\mathbf{P}}}

to
U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
with oracle
U_{{\mathbf{P}}}
.

Theorem 1
.

If
M
is an arbitrary TM, then one needs
\theta(2^{n})

accesses to the oracle
U_{{\mathbf{P}}}
to decide whether

\langle M,1^{n},1^{t}\rangle\in U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
in the worst case.

Proof.

Let
M
be an arbitrary TM and
n\in\mathbb{N}
.
The set
\{0,1\}^{n}
is finite. So, there is a

T\in\mathbb{N}
with

\langle M,1^{n},1^{T}\rangle\in U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}\iff\langle M,1^{n}\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{NP}}}

In this case we know for
x\in\{0,1\}^{n}
:

\langle M,1^{n},1^{T}\rangle\in U_{{\mathbf{P}}}
iff

\langle M,x\rangle\in D_{{\mathbf{P}}}
. So, the calculation
of
UNP
with oracle
U_{{\mathbf{P}}}

has the same time complexity as the calculation
of
DNP
with oracle
D_{{\mathbf{P}}}
. Due to lemma
1

this is
\theta(2^{n})
.

Maybe one could argue, that there is a faster calculation than a black box
search when the time limit is shorter.
Assuming there is a faster calculation for

\langle M,1^{n},1^{t}\rangle\in^{?}U_{{\mathbf{NP}}}
, when

\exists x\in\{0,1\}^{n}\text{ with }\langle M,x,1^{t}\rangle\in U_{{\mathbf{P}}}\text{ but }x\in L(M)\text{.}

(1)

Then it would be decidable, if
M
accepts an
x
in more than
t
steps.
But it is not.
∎
