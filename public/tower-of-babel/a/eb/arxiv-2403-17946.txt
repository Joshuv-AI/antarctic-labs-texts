NONLINEAR HEISENBERG-ROBERTSON-SCHRODINGER UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE
K. MAHESH KRISHNA

Abstract




Abstract
: We derive an uncertainty principle for Lipschitz maps acting on subsets of Banach spaces. We show that this nonlinear uncertainty principle reduces to the Heisenberg-Robertson-Schrodinger uncertainty principle for linear operators acting on Hilbert spaces.

Keywords
: Uncertainty Principle, Lipschitz map, Banach space.

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020)
: 26A16, 46B99.

2.
Nonlinear Heisenberg-Robertson-Schrodinger Uncertainty Principle

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space. Recall that the collection of all Lipschitz functions
f:\mathcal{X}\to\mathbb{C}
satisfying
f(0)=0
, denoted by
\mathcal{X}^{\#}
is a Banach space
[
11
]
w.r.t. the Lipschitz norm

\displaystyle\|f\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\coloneqq\sup_{x,y\in\mathcal{X},x\neq y}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|}{\|x-y\|}.

Let
\mathcal{M}\subseteq\mathcal{X}
be a subset such that
0\in\mathcal{M}
. Let
A:\mathcal{M}\to\mathcal{X}
be a Lipschitz map such that
A(0)=0
.
Given
x\in\mathcal{M}
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{\#}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we define
two uncertainties
of
A
at
(x,f)\in\mathcal{M}\times\mathcal{X}^{\#}
as

\displaystyle\Delta(A,x,f)\coloneqq\|Ax-f(Ax)x\|,

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\coloneqq\|fA-f(Ax)f\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}.

Theorem 2.1
.

(
Nonlinear Heisenberg-Robertson-Schrodinger Uncertainty Principle
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space and
\mathcal{M},\mathcal{N}\subseteq\mathcal{X}
be subsets such that
0\in\mathcal{M}\cap\mathcal{N}
. Let
A:\mathcal{M}\to\mathcal{X}
,
B:\mathcal{N}\to\mathcal{X}
be Lipschitz maps such that
A(0)=B(0)=0
. Then for all
x\in\mathcal{M}\cap\mathcal{N}
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{\#}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\nabla(f,A,x)^{2}+\Delta(B,x,f)^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}\left(\nabla(f,A,x)+\Delta(B,x,f)\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)\geq|f(ABx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|.

Proof.

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)

\displaystyle=\|fA-f(Ax)f\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\|Bx-f(Bx)x\|

\displaystyle\geq|[fA-f(Ax)f][Bx-f(Bx)x]|

\displaystyle=|f(ABx)-f(Bx)f(Ax)-f(Ax)f(Bx)+f(Ax)f(Bx)f(x)|

\displaystyle=|f(ABx)-f(Bx)f(Ax)-f(Ax)f(Bx)+f(Ax)f(Bx)\cdot 1|

\displaystyle=|f(ABx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|.

∎

Corollary 2.2
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space and
\mathcal{M},\mathcal{N}\subseteq\mathcal{X}
be subsets such that
0\in\mathcal{M}\cap\mathcal{N}
. Let
A:\mathcal{M}\to\mathcal{X}
,
B:\mathcal{N}\to\mathcal{X}
be Lipschitz maps such that
A(0)=B(0)=0
. Then for all
x\in\mathcal{M}\cap\mathcal{N}
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{\#}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we have

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)+\|fB\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\Delta(A,x,f)\geq|f([A,B]x)|.

Proof.

Note that

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)

\displaystyle\geq|f(ABx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|

\displaystyle=|f(ABx-BAx)+f(BAx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|

\displaystyle=|f([A,B]x)+(fB)[Ax-f(Ax)x]|

\displaystyle\geq|f([A,B]x)|-|(fB)[Ax-f(Ax)x]|

\displaystyle\geq|f([A,B]x)|-\|fB\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\|Ax-f(Ax)x\|

\displaystyle=|f([A,B]x)|-\|fB\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\Delta(A,x,f).

∎

Corollary 2.3
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space and
\mathcal{M},\mathcal{N}\subseteq\mathcal{X}
be subsets such that
0\in\mathcal{M}\cap\mathcal{N}
. Let
A:\mathcal{M}\to\mathcal{X}
,
B:\mathcal{N}\to\mathcal{X}
be Lipschitz maps such that
A(0)=B(0)=0
. Then for all
x\in\mathcal{M}\cap\mathcal{N}
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{\#}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we have

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)+\|fB\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\Delta(A,x,-f)\geq|f(\{A,B\}x)|.

Proof.

Note that

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)

\displaystyle\geq|f(ABx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|

\displaystyle=|f(ABx+BAx)-f(BAx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|

\displaystyle=|f(\{A,B\}x)-(fB)[Ax+f(Ax)x]|

\displaystyle\geq|f(\{A,B\}x)|-|(fB)[Ax+f(Ax)x]|

\displaystyle\geq|f(\{A,B\}x)|-\|fB\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\|Ax-(-f)(Ax)x\|

\displaystyle=|f(\{A,B\}x)|-\|fB\|_{\text{Lip}_{0}}\Delta(A,x,-f).

∎

Corollary 2.4
.

(
Functional Heisenberg-Robertson-Schrodinger Uncertainty Principle
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space with dual
\mathcal{X}^{*}
,
A:\mathcal{D}(A)\to\mathcal{X}
and
B:\mathcal{D}(B)\to\mathcal{X}
be linear operators. Then for all
x\in\mathcal{D}(AB)\cap\mathcal{D}(BA)
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{*}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\nabla(f,A,x)^{2}+\Delta(B,x,f)^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}\left(\nabla(f,A,x)+\Delta(B,x,f)\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)\geq|f(ABx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|.

Corollary 2.5
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space,
A:\mathcal{D}(A)\to\mathcal{X}
and
B:\mathcal{D}(B)\to\mathcal{X}
be linear operators. Then for all
x\in\mathcal{D}(AB)\cap\mathcal{D}(BA)
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{*}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we have

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)+\|fB\|\Delta(A,x,f)\geq|f([A,B]x)|.

Corollary 2.6
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a Banach space,
A:\mathcal{D}(A)\to\mathcal{X}
and
B:\mathcal{D}(B)\to\mathcal{X}
be linear operators. Then for all
x\in\mathcal{D}(AB)\cap\mathcal{D}(BA)
and
f\in\mathcal{X}^{*}
satisfying
f(x)=1
, we have

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)+\|fB\|\Delta(A,-x,f)=\nabla(f,A,x)\Delta(B,x,f)+\|fB\|\Delta(A,x,-f)\geq|f(\{A,B\}x)|.

Corollary 2.7
.

Theorem
1.2
follows from Theorem
2.1
.

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a complex Hilbert space.
A:\mathcal{D}(A)\to\mathcal{H}
and
B:\mathcal{D}(B)\to\mathcal{H}
be self-adjoint operators. Let
h\in\mathcal{D}(AB)\cap\mathcal{D}(BA)
with
\|h\|=1
. Define
\mathcal{X}\coloneqq\mathcal{H}
,
\mathcal{M}\coloneqq\mathcal{D}(A)
,
\mathcal{N}\coloneqq\mathcal{D}(B)
,
x\coloneqq h
and

\displaystyle f:\mathcal{H}\ni u\mapsto f(u)\coloneqq\langle u,h\rangle\in\mathbb{C}.

Then

\displaystyle\Delta(B,x,f)=\Delta(B,h,f)=\|Bh-f(Bh)h\|=\|Bh-\langle Bh,h\rangle h\|=\Delta_{h}(B),

\displaystyle\nabla(f,A,x)

\displaystyle=\nabla(f,A,h)=\|fA-f(Ah)f\|=\|fA-\langle Ah,h\rangle f\|

\displaystyle=\sup_{u\in\mathcal{H},\|u\|\leq 1}|f(Au)-\langle Ah,h\rangle f(u)|=\sup_{u\in\mathcal{H},\|u\|\leq 1}|\langle Au,h\rangle-\langle Ah,h\rangle\langle u,h\rangle|

\displaystyle=\sup_{u\in\mathcal{H},\|u\|\leq 1}|\langle u,Ah\rangle-\langle Ah,h\rangle\langle u,h\rangle|=\sup_{u\in\mathcal{H},\|u\|\leq 1}|\langle u,Ah-\langle Ah,h\rangle h\rangle|

\displaystyle=\|Ah-\langle Ah,h\rangle h\|=\Delta_{h}(A)

and

\displaystyle|f(ABx)-f(Ax)f(Bx)|

\displaystyle=|f(ABh)-f(Ah)f(Bh)|=|\langle ABh,h\rangle-\langle Ah,h\rangle\langle Bh,h\rangle|

\displaystyle=|\langle Bh,Ah\rangle-\langle Ah,h\rangle\langle Bh,h\rangle|=|\langle Ah,Bh\rangle-\langle Ah,h\rangle\langle Bh,h\rangle|.

∎
