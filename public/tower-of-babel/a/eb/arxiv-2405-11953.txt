Abstract
: In this note, we transform the linear order (at order
G
) metric from a system of pointlike bodies source in the post-Minkowskian expansion to the Bondi coordinates. We show that the Bondi 4-momentum and angular momentum coincide with the relativistic definitions of 4-momentum and angular momentum for the system of pointlike bodies. The angular momentum computed at the null infinity is free of supertranslation ambiguity.

The linear metric of pointlike bodies source

The inverse metric of the spacetime with pointlike bodies source in the harmonic gauge in the Minkowski coordinates
x^{\mu}=(x^{0},x^{i})
is given by
[

g^{\mu\nu}=\eta^{\mu\nu}-4G\sum\frac{m}{\Gamma(x)}(v^{\mu}v^{\nu}+\frac{1}{2}\eta^{\mu\nu}),

(1)

where

\Gamma(x)=\sqrt{(x^{\mu}-c^{\mu})(x^{\nu}-c^{\nu})\Pi_{\mu\nu}},\quad\quad\Pi_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+v_{\mu}v_{\nu}.

(2)

The sign
\sum

v^{\mu}

c^{\mu}
are the 4-velocities and initial positions of each particle. The trajectory of the particle with respect to its proper time
\tau

x^{\mu}(\tau)=c^{\mu}+v^{\mu}\tau,\quad\quad v^{2}=-1.

(3)

Each particle is with energy
E

p^{i}
. The 4-momentum of every single particle is defined as
p^{\mu}=(E,p^{i})
. The 4-momentum and angular momentum of the system are the same as the ADM definition
[

]

P^{\mu}_{\text{ADM}}=\sum p^{\mu},\quad\quad J^{i}_{\text{ADM}}={\epsilon^{i}}_{jk}\sum c^{j}p^{k}.

(4)

\begin{split}&D_{A}D_{B}(n\cdot v)=-\gamma_{AB}(v^{0}+n\cdot v),\quad\quad D_{A}D_{B}(n\cdot c)=-\gamma_{AB}(c^{0}+n\cdot c),\\
&D_{A}(n\cdot v)D^{A}(n\cdot v)=-\left[1+(n\cdot v)^{2}+2v^{0}(n\cdot v)\right],\\
&D_{A}(n\cdot v)D^{A}(n\cdot c)=(v\cdot c)-(n\cdot v)(n\cdot c)-v^{0}(n\cdot c)-c^{0}(n\cdot v),\end{split}

\begin{split}g^{uu}&=-4G\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})^{2}},\\
g^{ur}&=-1-4G\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}(-v_{\mu}n^{\mu}v_{i}n^{i}-\frac{1}{2})},\\
g^{uA}&=\frac{4G}{r}\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}v_{\mu}n^{\mu}D^{A}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})},\\
g^{rr}&=1-4G\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}[(v_{i}n^{i})^{2}+\frac{1}{2}}],\\
g^{rA}&=-\frac{4G}{r}\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}v_{i}n^{i}D^{A}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})},\\
g^{AB}&=\frac{1}{r^{2}}\gamma^{AB}-\frac{4G}{r^{2}}\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}D^{A}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})D^{B}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})}-\frac{2G}{r^{2}}\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}\gamma^{AB}}.\end{split}

(7)

Correspondingly, the metric components in the flat-space Bondi coordinates are

\begin{split}g_{uu}&=-1+4G\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}(v_{0}v_{0}-\frac{1}{2})},\\
g_{ur}&=-1+4G\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}(v_{0}v_{\mu}n^{\mu}-\frac{1}{2})},\\
g_{uA}&=4Gr\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}v_{0}D_{A}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})},\\
g_{rr}&=4G\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}(v_{\mu}v^{\mu})^{2}},\\
g_{rA}&=4Gr\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}v_{\mu}n^{\mu}D_{A}(v_{\nu}n^{\nu})},\\
g_{AB}&=r^{2}\gamma_{AB}+4Gr^{2}\sum{\frac{m}{\Gamma}\left[D_{A}(v_{\mu}n^{\mu})D_{B}(v_{\nu}n^{\nu})+\frac{1}{2}\gamma_{AB}\right]}.\end{split}

N_{\bar{A}}=-G\sum{m\frac{2}{(n^{\nu}v_{\nu})^{3}}D_{\bar{A}}(n\cdot c)}+\text{total derivative terms}.

(30)

The total derivative terms can be read from previous equations for the metric components. We omit them here because they will not contribute to the angular momentum at null infinity. It is important to notice that this expression of the angular momentum aspect is the main new result of this note. To obtain that, we have computed

4
Four-momentum at null infinity

The definition of Bondi 4-momentum is

P^{\mu}=\frac{1}{4\pi G}\int_{S}\,m_{b}n^{\mu}\,\text{d}\Omega=\frac{1}{4\pi}\sum{m\int_{S}\frac{n^{\mu}}{(v^{0}-n^{i}v_{i})^{3}}\text{d}\Omega},

(31)

where
\text{d}\Omega
is the integral measure on the celestial sphere. We can select the z-axis in the spherical integral along the direction of the 3-velocity
\vec{v}
. Then
v^{x}=0=v^{y}
and
v^{z}=v=|\vec{v}|
in our arrangement.
The integral for the 4-momentum on the sphere is reduced to

\int_{S}\frac{n^{\mu}}{(v^{0}-n^{i}{v_{i}})^{3}}d\Omega=\int^{2\pi}_{0}d\phi\int^{\pi}_{0}\frac{n^{\mu}\sin{\theta}}{(v^{0}-v\cos{\theta})^{3}}d\theta,

(32)

Then we can integrate for different components as

\displaystyle\int^{2\pi}_{0}d\phi\int^{\pi}_{0}\frac{n^{0}\sin{\theta}}{(v^{0}-v\cos{\theta})^{3}}d\theta=\int^{2\pi}_{0}d\phi\int^{\pi}_{0}\frac{\sin{\theta}}{(v^{0}-v\cos{\theta})^{3}}d\theta=4\pi v^{0},

(33)

\displaystyle\int^{2\pi}_{0}d\phi\int^{\pi}_{0}\frac{n^{z}\sin{\theta}}{(v^{0}-v\cos{\theta})^{3}}d\theta=\int^{2\pi}_{0}d\phi\int^{\pi}_{0}\frac{\cos{\theta}\sin{\theta}}{(v^{0}-v\cos{\theta})^{3}}d\theta=4\pi v,

(34)

and the other two components are zero due to their integration over a period involving
\sin{\phi}
or
\cos{\phi}
. Note also that
v^{x}=0=v^{y}
and
v^{z}=v
. So the final result is

\int^{2\pi}_{0}d\phi\int^{\pi}_{0}\frac{n^{\mu}\sin{\theta}}{(v^{0}-v\cos{\theta})^{3}}d\theta=4\pi v^{\mu}.

(35)

Finally, (
31
) is reduced to

P^{\mu}=\sum{mv^{\mu}},

(36)

which coincides precisely with the ADM 4-momentum.

5
Angular momentum at null infinity

The definition of angular momentum at the leading order in
G
is

J^{i}=\frac{1}{8\pi G}\int_{S}\,Y^{i\bar{A}}N_{\bar{A}}\,d\Omega,

(37)

where
Y^{i\bar{A}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{\bar{A}}}
are three Killing vectors of the celestial sphere, which are related to the normal vector
n^{i}
by

Y^{(i)\bar{A}}=-\epsilon^{\bar{A}\bar{B}}D_{\bar{B}}n^{i}.

(38)

Applying the identity

\epsilon^{AB}D_{A}(n\cdot c)D_{B}(n^{k})=\epsilon^{ijk}n_{i}c_{j},

(39)

we obtain

\displaystyle J^{i}

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{4\pi}\sum{m\int_{S}\frac{\epsilon^{\bar{A}\bar{B}}D_{\bar{A}}(n\cdot c)D_{\bar{B}}n^{i}}{(v^{0}-n^{i}v_{i})^{3}}d\Omega}

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{4\pi}\sum{m\int_{S}\frac{\epsilon^{ijk}n_{j}c_{k}}{(v^{0}-n^{i}v_{i})^{3}}d\Omega}

\displaystyle=\epsilon^{ijk}\sum{mc_{j}v_{k}},

(40)

where the integral on the celestial sphere can be calculated similarly as the 4-momentum. The final result recovers precisely the ADM angular momentum. Here we obtain it from the asymptotic point of view at null infinity.

7
Conclusion

In this note, we obtain the linear metric from a system of pointlike bodies source in the Bondi coordinates near future null infinity. The perturbative metric is at the linear order. The flat-space Bondi coordinates have already had the background Minkowski metric in the Bondi gauge. So the transformation from the flat-space Bondi coordinates to the full Bondi coordinates only involve
{\cal O}(G)
corrections. Hence, the Lorentz frame describing the system of pointlike bodies has never been touched. The linear metric in Bondi gauge involves a supertranslation parametrized by a free function on the celestial sphere. The Bondi mass aspect and angular momentum aspect are specified which do not have supertranslation dependence. The 4-momentum and angular momentum at null infinity are determined by the Bondi mass aspect and angular momentum aspect, respectively. The obtained conserved quantities at null infinity recover precisely the AMD 4-momentum and angular momentum at spatial infinity.
