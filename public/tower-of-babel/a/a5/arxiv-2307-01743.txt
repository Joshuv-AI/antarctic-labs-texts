Regularity theory for nonlocal equations with general growth in the Heisenberg group Thanks: ∗ Corresponding author.
Yuzhou Fang and Chao Zhang ∗

Abstract
. We deal with a wide class of generalized nonlocal p p -Laplace equations, so-called nonlocal G G -Laplace equations, in the Heisenberg framework. Under natural hypotheses on the N N -function G G , we provide a unified approach to investigate in the spirit of De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theory, some local properties of weak solutions to such kind of problems, involving boundedness, Hölder continuity and Harnack inequality. To this end, an improved nonlocal Caccioppoli-type estimate as the main auxiliary ingredient is exploited several times.

1.
Introduction

The objective of this paper is to develop local regularity theory for the weak solutions of a very general class of nonlocal integro-differential problems with non-standard growth in the Heisenberg group
\mathbb{H}^{n}
. The kind of equations we are addressing are the following:

\mathcal{L}u(\xi)=0\quad\text{in }\Omega

(1.1)

with the operator
\mathcal{L}
given by

\mathcal{L}u(\xi):=\mathrm{P.V.}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}g\left(\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\eta)|}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{u(\xi)-u(\eta)}{|u(\xi)-u(\eta)|}\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}},

where
s\in(0,1)
,
\Omega
is a bounded domain in
\mathbb{H}^{n}
for
n\geq 1
, and
Q=2n+2
stands for the homogeneous dimension of
\mathbb{H}^{n}
. Here the symbols
|\cdot|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}
and
\mathrm{P.V.}
denote separately the standard Heisenberg norm and “in the principal value sense”. It is noteworthy that the function
g:\left[0,\infty\right)\rightarrow\left[0,\infty\right)
is continuous and strictly increasing such that
g(0)=0
,
\lim\limits_{t\rightarrow\infty}g(t)=\infty
and

1<p\leq\frac{tg(t)}{G(t)}\leq q<\infty\quad\text{with }G(t):=\int_{0}^{t}g(\tau)d\tau,

(1.2)

where
G(\cdot)
is an
N
-function that carries the
\Delta_{2}
and
\nabla_{2}
conditions (see Section
2
). Let us point out that the necessary condition (
1.2
) firstly appeared in the work of Lieberman
[
37
]
, where the author established the
C^{0,\alpha}
-continuity of weak solutions to (
1.4
) below. Several representative samples, satisfying the requirements above, incorporate the power case
g(t)=t^{p-1}
, the limiting case
g(t)=t^{p-1}\ln(e+t)
as well as
g(t)=t^{p-1}+t^{q-1}
.

Let us first pay attention to the
p
-fractional subLaplacian equations, namely, the particular scenario that
g(t)=t^{p-1}
in (

Integro-differential equations of this type can be seen as a generalization of the fractional subLaplacian in the Heisenberg group at this time, whose integral explanation presented by
[

25

and references therein for more results in the linear situation.

For what concerns the general nonlinear counterpart of (

), that is, the
p
-growth scenario for

demonstrated the interior boundedness along with Hölder continuity for the weak solutions of the fractional

As has been mentioned above, a lot of work concerning the nonlocal problems with general growth has been developed in the context of the Euclidean space, but not very much has been done in the framework of Heisenberg group. In particular, the authors and Zhang

The aim of this paper is to develop a unified approach to the regularity theory of such problems that allows us to establish the boundedness, Hölder continuity and full Harnack inequalities for the weak solutions to Eq. 1.1

3.1

1.2

\mathcal{L}

Before presenting our main results, we introduce a tail space

L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n}):=\left\{u:\mathbb{H}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\text{ is measurable}:\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}g\left(\frac{|u(\xi)|}{(1+|\xi|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}})^{s}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{(1+|\xi|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}})^{Q+s}}<\infty\right\}

and the corresponding nonlocal tail in the ball
B_{r}(\xi_{0})
is defined as

\text{Tail}(u;\xi_{0},r)=\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{r}(\xi_{0})}g\left(\frac{|u(\xi)|}{|{\xi_{0}}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|{\xi_{0}}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}.

Observe that
\mathrm{Tail}(u;\xi_{0},r)<\infty
for any
\xi_{0}\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
and
r>0
, when
u
belongs to
L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
. For no ambiguity, we stress
g^{-1},G^{-1}
mean separately the inverses of
g,G
.

Now we are in a position to state in turn the main contributions of our work. Throughout the forthcoming three theorems we always suppose the condition (
1.2
) is in force. The first result describes the local boundedness of weak solutions.

Theorem 1.1

(Local boundedness)
.

Let
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a weak subsolution of (
1.1
) and
B_{r}:=B_{r}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
. Then for every
\delta\in(0,1]
it holds that

\sup_{B_{r/2}}u\leq Cr^{s}G^{-1}\left(\delta^{\frac{\theta}{1-\theta}}{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{r}}G\left(\frac{u_{+}}{r^{s}}\right)d\xi\right)+r^{s}g^{-1}\left(\delta(r/2)^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},r/2)\right),

where
\theta>1
is from Lemma
3.2
and
C>0
depends only on
n,p,q,s
.

In the theorem above, the presence of the parameter
\delta
allows an interpolation between the local and nonlocal terms, which plays a prominent role in the proof of Harnack inequality below. The second one is the desired interior Hölder regularity for the weak solutions.

Theorem 1.2

(Hölder continuity)
.

Let
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a weak solution to (
1.1
) with local boundedness. Then
u
is locally of the class
C^{0,\alpha}(\Omega)
with some
\alpha\in(0,1)
determined only by the structural constants
n,p,q,s
. To be precise, for any ball
B_{R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
, there exists a
C>1
depending also on
n,p,q,s
such that

\osc\limits_{B_{r}(\xi_{0})}u\leq C\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{\alpha}\left[\sup_{B_{R}(\xi_{0})}|u|+R^{s}g^{-1}(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u;\xi_{0},R))\right]

with
r\in(0,R]
.

Putting together Theorems
1.1
and
1.2
, we could have the Hölder continuity under the natural condition (
1.2
) (without boundedness assumption). We would like to mention that in order to get the oscillation reduction, the pivotal difficulties consist in controlling properly the nonlocal tail of solutions at every iteration step, which is a new element carried by the nonlocality of the operator
\mathcal{L}
, compared to the local case. This demands us to construct very carefully the sequence of shrinking balls
\{B_{r_{j}}(\xi_{0})\}
and the geometric sequence
\{\omega_{j}\}
such that

\osc\limits_{B_{r_{j}}(\xi_{0})}u\leq\omega_{j}.

(1.5)

Generally speaking, if the relation (
1.5
) is inferred, we shall show the accurate estimate of tail

r^{s}_{j}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{\pm}_{j})_{\pm};\xi_{0},r_{j})\leq g\left(C\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j}}\right)

(1.6)

with
\nu_{j}^{+},\nu^{-}_{j}
representing the supremum and infimum of
u
in
B_{r_{j}}(\xi_{0})
respectively. Further, the inequality (
1.6
) leads to the improvement of oscillation in
B_{r_{j+1}}(\xi_{0})
, and we can continue this process step by step. The last one provides a full Harnack inequality for Eq. (
1.1
).

Theorem 1.3

(Harnack inequality)
.

Let
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a weak solution to (
1.1
). For any ball
B_{4R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
, let also
u
be nonnegative in
B_{4R}(\xi_{0})
. Then we infer the following estimate

\sup_{B_{R}(\xi_{0})}u\leq C\left(\inf_{B_{R}(\xi_{0})}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right)

(1.7)

with the constant
C>0
depending only on
n,p,q,s
.

Two direct components of this result are the upper bound estimate (Theorem
1.1
) and the weak Harnack inequality (Lemma
5.3
), where the latter was derived by the so-called expansion of positivity in Section
4

[

R^{s}g^{-1}(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)=\left(R^{sp}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}(\xi_{0})}\frac{u^{p-1}_{-}(\xi)}{|{\xi_{0}}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+ps}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}.

At this stage, our result (

[

3.
Energy estimates and boundedness

This section is devoted to establishing a improved Caccioppoli estimate involving all the information needed to demonstrate (local) regularity properties for weak solutions to (
1.1
) such as boundedness, Hölder continuity together with Harnack estimates. For the notations
\pm
and
\mp
blow, let us point out that subsolution and supersolution always correspond to the upper sign and lower sign, respectively. Besides, for convenience, we introduce several sets

A^{+}(k,r)=B_{r}\cap\left\{u>k\right\},\;A^{-}(k,r)=B_{r}\cap\left\{u<k\right\}

and

A^{+}(k)=\left\{u>k\right\},\;A^{-}(k)=\left\{u<k\right\}.

Proposition 3.1
.

Let u be a weak subsolution
(super\text{-})
to Eq. (
1.1
) and
B_{r}:=B_{r}(\xi_{0})\subset\Omega
. Then for any

\displaystyle\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{\tilde{w}_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{|\tilde{w}_{i}-(\tilde{w}_{i})_{B_{i+1}}|}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}+CG\left(\frac{(\tilde{w}_{i})_{B_{i+1}}}{r_{i+1}^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{|\tilde{w}_{i}-(\tilde{w}_{i})_{B_{i+1}}|}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}+C{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{\tilde{w}_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)\,d\xi.

Observe that

\displaystyle C^{-1}(\theta,q)2^{-q\theta i}G^{\theta-1}\left(\frac{k}{r^{s}}\right)G\left(\frac{w_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq G\left(2^{-i-2}k/r^{s}\right)^{\theta-1}G\left(\frac{w_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)

\displaystyle=G^{\theta-1}\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-\tilde{k}_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)G\left(\frac{w_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{\theta-1}\left(\frac{\tilde{w}_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)G\left(\frac{w_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{\theta}\left(\frac{\tilde{w}_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)

from the monotonicity of
G
. This time it follows from the three displays above that

\displaystyle G^{\frac{\theta-1}{\theta}}\left(\frac{k}{r^{s}}\right)\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{w_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}

\displaystyle\leq C(\theta,q)2^{qi}\left[C\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{|w_{i+1}-(w_{i+1})_{B_{i+1}}|}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}+C{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{w_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right]

\displaystyle\leq C2^{2qi}{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i}}G\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i}}\right)d\xi+C2^{(Q+sq+2q)i}\frac{r^{s}_{i}}{g(k/r^{s})}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},r/2){\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i}}G\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i}}\right)d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C2^{(Q+sq+2q)i}\left(1+\frac{r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},r/2)}{g(k/r^{s})}\right){\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i}}G\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i}}\right)d\xi.

We set

Y_{i}=\frac{{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i}}G(w_{i}/r^{s}_{i})\,d\xi}{G(k/r^{s})},

and obtain

Y_{i+1}\leq Cb^{i}\left(1+\frac{r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},r/2)}{g(k/r^{s})}\right)^{\theta}Y_{i}^{\theta}

with
C=C(n,p,q,s)\geq 1
and
b=2^{\theta(Q+sq+2q)}
. Picking first
k
so large that

k\geq r^{s}g^{-1}\left(\delta(r/2)^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},r/2)\right)

with
\delta\in\left(0,1\right]
, we can discover

Y_{i+1}\leq Cb^{i}\left(1+\frac{1}{\delta}\right)^{\theta}Y_{i}^{\theta}\leq(\delta^{-\theta}C)b^{i}Y_{i}^{\theta}.

If

Y_{0}=\frac{{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{r}}G(u_{+}/r^{s})\,d\xi}{G(k/r^{s})}\leq\left(\delta^{-\theta}C\right)^{\frac{1}{1-\theta}}b^{-\frac{1}{(\theta-1)^{2}}},

(3.12)

then by the iteration lemma (
[
29
, Lemma 7.1]
) there holds that
Y_{i}\rightarrow 0
as
i\rightarrow\infty
. At this moment, we could select

k=r^{s}G^{-1}\left(\delta^{\frac{\theta}{1-\theta}}C^{\frac{1}{\theta-1}}b^{\frac{1}{(\theta-1)^{2}}}{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{r}}G\left(\frac{u_{+}}{r^{s}}\right)d\xi\right)+r^{s}g^{-1}\left(\delta(r/2)^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},r/2)\right),

so that (
3.12
) is true. Eventually, the limit
\lim\limits_{i\rightarrow\infty}Y_{i}=0
implies the desired result.

4.
Hölder regularity

In this part, we are ready to justify that the weak solution,
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
to Eq. (
1.1
), is locally Hölder continuous in
\Omega
. For this aim, we first establish some crucial results; see Lemmas
4.1
–
4.3
.

We now suppose weak solutions are locally bounded and introduce some numbers
\nu^{\pm}
and
\omega
fulfilling

\nu^{-}\leq\inf_{B_{R}(\xi_{0})}u,\quad\nu^{+}\geq\sup_{B_{R}(\xi_{0})}u,\quad\omega\geq\nu^{+}-\nu^{-},

where
B_{R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
.

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a locally bounded weak sub(super)-solution to Eq. (
1.1
). Assume that for some
\beta
and
\gamma
in
(0,1)
, there holds that

\left|\left\{\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\geq\gamma\omega\right\}\cap B_{2r}(\xi_{0})\right|\geq\beta\left|B_{2r}\right|.

(4.1)

Then for some
\sigma\in\left(0,\frac{1}{2}\right]
, we conclude that either

(4r)^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{\pm})_{\pm};\xi_{0},R)>g\left(\frac{\sigma\gamma\omega}{(4r)^{s}}\right)

(4.2)

or

\left|B_{2r}(\xi_{0})\cap\left\{\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\leq\frac{\sigma\gamma\omega}{2}\right\}\right|\leq\frac{C\sigma^{p-1}}{\beta}\left|B_{2r}\right|

with
C>0
depending only on
n,p,q
and
s
, provided that
B_{4r}(\xi_{0})\subset B_{R}(\xi_{0})
.

Proof.

We just show this statement for supersolution, as the case of subsolution is similar. Let

\bar{u}:=u-\nu^{-}\quad\text{and}\quad w:=(\bar{u}-k)

with
k=\sigma\gamma\omega
. Use Proposition
3.1
to have

\displaystyle\int_{B_{2r}}w_{-}(\xi)\left[\int_{B_{2r}}g\left(\frac{w_{+}(\eta)}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right]d\xi

(4.3)

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\frac{4r}{4r-2r}\right)^{q}\int_{B_{4r}}G\left(\frac{w_{-}}{(4r)^{s}}\right)d\xi+C\left(\frac{4r}{4r-2r}\right)^{Q+sq}||w_{-}||_{L^{1}(B_{4r})}\mathrm{Tail}(w_{-};\xi_{0},4r)

\displaystyle\leq CG\left(\frac{k}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{4r}\right|+Ck\left|B_{4r}\right|\mathrm{Tail}(w_{-};\xi_{0},4r),

where we notice that
u-\nu^{-}\geq 0
in
B_{4r}
. Next we deal with the nonlocal tail in the last line.
By means of the properties of
g
and
u-\nu^{-}\geq 0
in
B_{R}
,

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tail}(w_{-};\xi_{0},4r)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}g\left(\frac{\bar{u}_{-}+k}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{B_{R}\setminus B_{4r}}g\left(\frac{k}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C(p,q)\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}g\left(\frac{\bar{u}_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\quad+C(p,q)\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{4r}}g\left(\frac{k}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\mathrm{Tail}(\bar{u}_{-};\xi_{0},R)+C(4r)^{(p-1)s}g\left(\frac{k}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{4r}}\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+sp}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\mathrm{Tail}(\bar{u}_{-};\xi_{0},R)+C\frac{1}{4r}g\left(\frac{k}{(4r)^{s}}\right)

(4.4)

with
C=C(Q,p,q,s)>0
. In view of (
4.3
) and (
4
), we find

\displaystyle\quad\ \int_{B_{2r}}w_{-}(\xi)\left[\int_{B_{2r}}g\left(\frac{w_{+}(\eta)}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right]d\xi

\displaystyle\leq CG\left(\frac{k}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{4r}\right|+Ck\mathrm{Tail}(\bar{u}_{-};\xi_{0},R)\left|B_{4r}\right|

\displaystyle\leq CG\left(\frac{k}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{4r}\right|

\displaystyle\leq C\sigma^{p}G\left(\frac{\gamma\omega}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{4r}\right|,

(4.5)

where the converse of the condition (
4.2
) was applied.

On the other hand, the integral at the left-hand side can be estimated as blow,

\displaystyle\ \quad\int_{B_{2r}}(\bar{u}-k)_{-}\left[\int_{B_{2r}}g\left(\frac{(\bar{u}-k)_{+}}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right]d\xi

\displaystyle\geq\int_{B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\leq\frac{k}{2}\right\}}(\bar{u}-k)_{-}\left[\int_{B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\geq\gamma\omega\right\}}g\left(\frac{(\bar{u}-k)_{+}}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right]d\xi

\displaystyle\geq\frac{k}{2}\frac{1}{(4r)^{Q+s}}g\left(\frac{\gamma\omega-k}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\leq k/2\right\}\right|\left|B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\geq\gamma\omega\right\}\right|

\displaystyle\geq\frac{k}{2}\frac{\beta}{(4r)^{Q+s}}g\left(\frac{\gamma\omega(1-\sigma)}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{2r}\right|\left|B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\leq k/2\right\}\right|

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\sigma\gamma\omega}{C}\frac{\beta}{(4r)^{s}}g\left(\frac{\gamma\omega}{2(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\leq k/2\right\}\right|

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\sigma\beta}{C}G\left(\frac{\gamma\omega}{(4r)^{s}}\right)\left|B_{2r}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\leq k/2\right\}\right|,

(4.6)

where in the third line we need note
|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\leq 4r
and the condition (
4.1
) is used in the penultimate inequality. Therefore, it yields from (
4
) and (
4
) that

\left|B_{2r}\cap\left\{u-\nu^{-}\leq\frac{\sigma\gamma\omega}{2}\right\}\right|\leq\frac{\sigma^{p-1}C}{\beta}\left|B_{2r}\right|

for the positive constant
C=C(n,p,q,s)
.
∎

The next result concerns a De Giorgi-type lemma.

Lemma 4.2
.

Let
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a locally bounded weak sub(super)-solution to (
1.1
). Then there is a constant
\mu\in(0,1)
, depending only on
n,p,q,s
, such that if

\left|B_{2r}(\xi_{0})\cap\left\{\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\leq\gamma\omega\right\}\right|\leq\mu\left|B_{2r}\right|

(4.7)

with some
\gamma\in(0,1)
, then either

r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{\pm})_{\pm};\xi_{0},R)>g\left(\frac{\gamma\omega}{r^{s}}\right)

(4.8)

or

\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\geq\frac{1}{2}\gamma\omega\qquad in\ B_{r}(\xi_{0}),

(4.9)

whenever
B_{2r}(\xi_{0})\subset B_{R}(\xi_{0})
.

Proof.

We just prove this assertion for supersolution. Suppose (
4.8
) is not true, i.e.,

r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{-})_{-};\xi_{0},R)\leq g\left(\frac{\gamma\omega}{r^{s}}\right).

(4.10)

We now justify (
4.9
). For
i\in\mathbb{N}\cup\left\{0\right\}
, define

r_{i}=r(1+2^{-i}),\ B_{i}=B_{r_{i}}(\xi_{0}),\ k_{i}=\frac{k}{2}(1+2^{-i})

with
k=\gamma\omega
, and

w_{i}=(\bar{u}-k_{i})_{-}:=(u-\nu^{-}-k_{i})_{-}.

Through exploiting Proposition
3.1
with
w_{-}:=w_{i},r:=r_{i}
and
\rho:=r_{i+1}
, we have

\displaystyle\quad\ \int_{B_{i+1}}\int_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{|w_{i}(\xi)-w_{i}(\eta)|}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\frac{r_{i}}{r_{i}-r_{i+1}}\right)^{q}\int_{B_{i}}G\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i}}\right)d\xi+C\left(\frac{r_{i}}{r_{i}-r_{i+1}}\right)^{Q+sq}||w_{i}||_{L^{1}(B_{i})}\mathrm{Tail}(w_{i};\xi_{0},r_{i})

\displaystyle\leq C2^{qi}G\left(\frac{k}{r^{s}}\right)|A^{-}_{i}|+C2^{(Q+sq)i}k|A^{-}_{i}|\mathrm{Tail}(w_{i};\xi_{0},r_{i}),

(4.11)

where we utilized the fact
\bar{u}\geq 0
in
B_{i}
, and
A^{-}_{i}
means

A^{-}_{i}:=B_{i}\cap\left\{\bar{u}\leq k_{i}\right\}.

Following the computation of (
4
), we easily get

\mathrm{Tail}(w_{i};\xi_{0},r_{i})\leq C\mathrm{Tail}(\bar{u}_{-};\xi_{0},R)+\frac{C}{r_{i}^{s}}g\left(\frac{k_{i}}{r^{s}_{i}}\right).

(4.12)

By virtue of (
4.10
), (
4
) and (
4.12
),

\int_{B_{i+1}}\int_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{|w_{i}(\xi)-w_{i}(\eta)|}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\leq C2^{(Q+sq+q)i}G\left(\frac{k}{r^{s}}\right)|A^{-}_{i}|.

(4.13)

On the other hand, with the help of Lemma
3.2
, Jensen’s inequality and (
4.13
), there holds

\displaystyle\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{|w_{i}-(w_{i})_{B_{i+1}}|}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}+C{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}\int_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{|w_{i}(\xi)-w_{i}(\eta)|}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}+C{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C2^{(Q+sq+q)i}G\left(\frac{k}{r^{s}}\right)\frac{|A^{-}_{i}|}{|B_{i}|}.

We proceed to estimate

\displaystyle\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{|B_{i+1}|^{\theta-1}}\left(\int_{A^{-}_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}

\displaystyle\geq|B_{i+1}|^{-\frac{1}{\theta}}\left(\int_{A^{-}_{i+1}}G^{\theta}\left(\frac{k_{i}-k_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}}

\displaystyle\geq C2^{-qi}G\left(\frac{k}{r^{s}}\right)\left(\frac{|A^{-}_{i+1}|}{|B_{i+1}|}\right)^{\frac{1}{\theta}},

where the constant
\theta=\theta(Q,s)>1
comes from Lemma
3.2
. Merging the previous two displays arrives at

\frac{|A^{-}_{i+1}|}{|B_{i+1}|}\leq C2^{\theta(Q+sq+2q)i}\left(\frac{|A^{-}_{i}|}{|B_{i}|}\right)^{\theta}.

Set

Y_{i}=\frac{|A^{-}_{i}|}{|B_{i}|}.

We employ the iteration lemma (
[
29
, Lemma 7.1]
) to infer
Y_{i}\rightarrow 0
as
i\rightarrow\infty
, provided

Y_{0}\leq C^{-\frac{1}{\theta-1}}2^{-\theta(Q+sq+2q)\frac{1}{(\theta-1)^{2}}}.

This can be assured by taking
\mu=C^{-\frac{1}{\theta-1}}2^{-\theta(Q+sq+2q)\frac{1}{(\theta-1)^{2}}}
in (
4.7
). Hence we can conclude
\bar{u}\geq\frac{k}{2},\ \text{i.e.,}\ u-\nu^{-}\geq\frac{\gamma\omega}{2}\ \text{in}\ B_{r}
.
∎

As a consequence of Lemmas
4.1
and
4.2
, we derive the following growth lemma.

Corollary 4.3
.

Let
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a locally bouneded weak sub(super)-solution to Eq. (
1.1
). If
B_{2r}(\xi_{0})\subset B_{R}(\xi_{0})
and, for some
\beta,\gamma\in(0,1)
,

\left|B_{r}(\xi_{0})\cap\left\{\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\geq\gamma\omega\right\}\right|\geq\beta|B_{r}|,

(4.14)

then there exists a constant
\tau\in\left(0,\frac{1}{2}\right]
, depending only upon
n,p,q,s
and
\beta
, such that either

r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{\pm})_{\pm};\xi_{0},R)>g\left(\frac{\tau\gamma\omega}{r^{s}}\right)

or

\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\geq\tau\gamma\omega\qquad\text{in}\ B_{r}(\xi_{0}).

Proof.

By (
4.14
), we can see

\left|\left\{\pm(\nu^{\pm}-u)\geq\gamma\omega\right\}\cap B_{2r}(\xi_{0})\right|\geq\frac{\beta}{2^{Q}}|B_{2r}|.

According to Lemmas
4.1
and
4.2
, we could choose
\sigma\in\left(0,\frac{1}{2}\right]
so small that
\frac{C\sigma^{p-1}}{\beta}
is not larger than
\mu
. Then this conclusion follows readily after simple arrangement.
∎

In what follows, we are going to prove the Hölder continuity of solutions, Theorem
1.2
, by induction. The key points of proof are to choose suitably decreasing sequences of radii
\{r_{j}\}
and of bound on oscillation
\{\omega_{j}\}
, and moreover to control precisely the nonlocal tail at each step in the process of iteration.

4.1.
Step 1 of induction

Fix
\xi_{0}\in\Omega
and
B_{R}=B_{R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
. Set

\nu^{+}=\sup_{B_{R}}u,\ \nu^{-}=\inf_{B_{R}}u\quad\text{and}\quad\omega=2\sup_{B_{R}}|u|+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u;\xi_{0},R)\right).

Obviously,

\osc\limits_{B_{R}}u=\nu^{+}-\nu^{-}\leq\omega,

(4.15)

which is the start towards H
\ddot{\text{o}}
lder continuity of
u
. Let
\lambda\in\left(0,\frac{1}{4}\right)
be a number to be fixed later. Suppose with no loss of generality
\nu^{+}-\nu^{-}>\frac{\omega}{2}
. Then one of the following scenarios

\left\{\begin{aligned} \left|\left\{u-\nu^{-}>\frac{1}{4}\omega\right\}\cap B_{\lambda R}(\xi_{0})\right|\geq\frac{1}{2}\left|B_{\lambda R}\right|\\
\left|\left\{\nu^{+}-u>\frac{1}{4}\omega\right\}\cap B_{\lambda R}(\xi_{0})\right|\geq\frac{1}{2}\left|B_{\lambda R}\right|\end{aligned}\right.

must hold true. Assume the first alternative holds, since the other is similar. We this time apply Corollary
4.3
with
\beta=\frac{1}{2},\gamma=\frac{1}{4}
and
r=\lambda R
to discover that either

(\lambda R)^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{-})_{-};\xi_{0},R)>g\left(\frac{\tau\omega}{(\lambda R)^{s}}\right)

(4.16)

or

u-\nu^{-}\geq\tau\omega\quad\text{in }B_{\lambda R}(\xi_{0}),

which together with (
4.15
) indicates

\osc\limits_{B_{\lambda R}}u=\sup_{B_{\lambda R}}(u-\nu^{-})-\inf_{B_{\lambda R}}(u-\nu^{-})\leq(1-\tau)\omega,

where
\tau\in\left(0,\frac{1}{2}\right)
depends only on
n,p,q,s
.

Now we will take a suitable
\lambda
to guarantee (
4.16
) does not occur. Via the definition of
\omega
, the nonlocal tail is estimated as

\displaystyle R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{-})_{-};\xi_{0},R)

\displaystyle\leq CR^{s}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}\left[g\left(\frac{u_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)+g\left(\frac{\nu^{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\right]\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq CR^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)+CR^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right)\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}\frac{R^{s(p-1)}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+sp}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Cg\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right),

where we used

g(a+b)\leq C(p,q)\left(g(a)+g(b)\right)\quad\text{and}\quad g(\tau a)\leq c(p,q)\tau^{p-1}g(a)\quad\text{for }\tau\leq 1.

In order to ensure (
4.16
) does not happen, we select
\lambda
to satisfy

C\lambda^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right)\leq g\left(\frac{\tau\omega}{(\lambda R)^{s}}\right)\Rightarrow g^{-1}\left(C\lambda^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right)\right)\leq\frac{\tau\omega}{(\lambda R)^{s}},

namely, by letting
C\lambda^{s}\leq 1
,

\frac{\tau\omega}{(\lambda R)^{s}}\geq\left(\frac{q}{pC\lambda^{s}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\Rightarrow\lambda\leq\left(\frac{\tau}{C}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{sp}},

(4.17)

where the constant
C>4
depends on
n,p,q,s
and we utilized

g^{-1}(at)\geq\left(\frac{q}{ap}\right)^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}g^{-1}(t)\quad\text{for }0<a\leq 1.

At this point, we have obtained the formal first step for the inductive argument, that is,

\osc\limits_{B_{r_{1}}}u\leq\omega_{1}:=(1-\tau)\omega,

with
r_{1}=\lambda R
for some
\lambda\in\left(0,(\tau/C)^{\frac{p-1}{sp}}\right]
. It is worth pointing out that we shall determine the more accurate range of
\lambda
to meet the demands of iteration process.

4.2.
The recursive process.

Suppose we have confirmed

\osc\limits_{B_{i}}u\leq\omega_{i}:=(1-\tau)^{i}\omega_{0}\quad\text{for }i=0,1,\ldots,j,

(4.18)

where
\omega_{0}=\omega,B_{i}=B_{r_{i}},r_{i}=\lambda^{i}R
. Let also
\nu^{+}_{i}=\sup\limits_{B_{i}}u
and
\nu^{-}_{i}=\inf\limits_{B_{i}}u
. We are ready to show the preceding oscillation estimate still holds for
i=j+1
. The procedure is analogous to Step 1. Without loss of generality, we may suppose
\nu^{+}_{j}-\nu^{-}_{j}>\frac{1}{2}\omega_{j}
. Then one of the following two alternatives

\left\{\begin{aligned} \left|\left\{u-\nu^{-}_{j}>\frac{1}{4}\omega_{j}\right\}\cap B_{\lambda r_{j}}(\xi_{0})\right|\geq\frac{1}{2}\left|B_{\lambda r_{j}}\right|\\
\left|\left\{\nu^{+}_{j}-u>\frac{1}{4}\omega_{j}\right\}\cap B_{\lambda r_{j}}(\xi_{0})\right|\geq\frac{1}{2}\left|B_{\lambda r_{j}}\right|\end{aligned}\right.

must be true. Let us assume the first case holds. Exploiting Corollary
4.3
with
\beta=\frac{1}{2},\gamma=\frac{1}{4}
and
r=\lambda r_{j}
, we obtain that either

(\lambda r_{j})^{s}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{-}_{j})_{-};\xi_{0},r_{j})>g\left(\frac{\tau\omega_{j}}{(\lambda r_{j})^{s}}\right)

(4.19)

or

u-\nu^{-}_{j}\geq\tau\omega_{j}\quad\text{in }B_{j+1}(\xi_{0})=B_{\lambda r_{j}}(\xi_{0})

with the small constant
\tau\in\left(0,\frac{1}{2}\right)
depending only on
n,p,q,s
. This along with (
4.18
) implies

\osc\limits_{B_{j+1}}u=\sup\limits_{B_{j+1}}(u-\nu^{-}_{j})-\inf\limits_{B_{j+1}}(u-\nu^{-}_{j})\leq(1-\tau)\omega_{j}=\omega_{j+1}.

At this moment, our goal is to enforce that (
4.19
) can not occur through choosing properly
\lambda\in\left(0,\frac{1}{4}\right)
. To this end, we evaluate

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{-}_{j});\xi_{0},r_{j})

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}g\left(\frac{(u-\nu^{-}_{j})_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}+\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\int_{B_{i}\setminus B_{i+1}}g\left(\frac{(u-\nu^{-}_{j})_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle=:I_{1}+I_{2}.

For
I_{1}
, it follows from the definition of
\omega
that

I_{1}\leq C\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}\left[g\left(\frac{u_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)+g\left(\frac{|\nu^{-}_{j}|}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\right]\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\leq\frac{C}{R^{s}}g\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right),

where we note
|\nu^{-}_{j}|\leq\omega
. Next let us pay attention to the integral
I_{2}
. For
\xi\in B_{i}
with
i=0,1,\ldots,j-1
, via the inclusion relation of
B_{i}
,

\left(u(\xi)-\nu^{-}_{j}\right)_{-}\leq\nu^{-}_{j}-\nu^{-}_{i}\leq\nu^{+}_{j}-\nu^{-}_{i}\leq\nu^{+}_{i}-\nu^{-}_{i}\leq\omega_{i}.

Hence,

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\int_{B_{i}\setminus B_{i+1}}g\left(\frac{\omega_{i}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\int_{B_{i}\setminus B_{i+1}}g\left(\frac{\omega_{i}r^{s}_{i+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}g\left(\frac{\omega_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)\int_{B_{i}\setminus B_{i+1}}\frac{r^{s(p-1)}_{i+1}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+sp}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\frac{1}{r^{s}_{i+1}}g\left(\frac{\omega_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right).

Finally, we arrive at

r^{s}_{j}\mathrm{Tail}((u-\nu^{-}_{j})_{-};\xi_{0},r_{j})\leq C\left(\frac{r_{j}}{R}\right)^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right)+C\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\left(\frac{r_{j}}{r_{i+1}}\right)^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right).

Notice that, from the definitions of
r_{i},\omega_{i}
,

\displaystyle\left(\frac{r_{j}}{r_{i+1}}\right)^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega_{i}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\right)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{s(j-i-1)}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\frac{r^{s}_{j+1}}{r^{s}_{i+1}}\frac{\omega_{i}}{\omega_{j}}\right)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{s(j-i-1)}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\lambda^{s(j-1)}(1-\tau)^{i-j}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C(1-\tau)^{(q-1)(i-j)}\lambda^{s(j-i-1)+s(p-1)(j-1)}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right)

\displaystyle=C\lambda^{-s}(1-\tau)^{(q-1)(i-j)}\lambda^{sp(j-i)}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right)

and, similarly,

\left(\frac{r_{j}}{R}\right)^{s}g\left(\frac{\omega}{R^{s}}\right)\leq C(1-\tau)^{-(q-1)j}\lambda^{sp-s+spj}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right).

Then,

\displaystyle r^{s}_{j}\mathrm{Tail}\left((u-\nu^{-}_{j})_{-};\xi_{0},r_{j}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\lambda^{-s}\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\lambda^{sp(j-i)}(1-\tau)^{(1-q)(j-i)}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C(1-\tau)^{1-q}\lambda^{sp-s}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right),

where we can find

\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\left[\lambda^{sp}(1-\tau)^{1-q}\right]^{j-i}=\lambda^{sp}(1-\tau)^{1-q}\frac{\left[\lambda^{sp}(1-\tau)^{1-q}\right]^{j}-1}{\lambda^{sp}(1-\tau)^{1-q}-1}\leq 2\lambda^{sp}(1-\tau)^{1-q},

if
\lambda^{sp}(1-\tau)^{1-q}
is restricted by
\frac{1}{2}
, i.e.,

\lambda\leq 2^{-\frac{1}{sp}}(1-\tau)^{\frac{q-1}{sp}}.

(4.20)

To contradict (
4.19
), we need

C(1-\tau)^{1-q}\lambda^{sp}g\left(\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right)\leq g\left(\frac{\tau\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right),

namely, by letting
C(1-\tau)^{1-q}\lambda^{sp}<1
,

\frac{\tau\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\geq\left(\frac{q}{pC(1-\tau)^{1-q}\lambda^{sp}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}\frac{\omega_{j}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\Rightarrow\lambda\leq\tau^{\frac{p-1}{sp}}\left(\frac{q}{pC(1-\tau)^{1-q}}\right)^{\frac{1}{sp}}.

(4.21)

Rechecking the inference process above, especially (
4.17
), (
4.20
) and (
4.21
), we select eventually
\lambda
as

\lambda=\frac{1}{2}\min\left\{\left(\frac{\tau}{C}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{sp}},2^{-\frac{1}{sp}}(1-\tau)^{\frac{q-1}{sp}},\tau^{\frac{p-1}{sp}}\left(\frac{(1-\tau)^{q-1}}{C}\right)^{\frac{1}{sp}}\right\}.

(4.22)

As a mater of fact,
\lambda
is a number in
\left(0,\frac{1}{4}\right)
depending only on
n,p,q,s
. As a consequence, we do infer

\osc\limits_{B_{j+1}}u=\osc\limits_{B_{\lambda^{j+1}R}}u\leq\omega_{j+1}

with
\lambda
determined by (
4.22
), which completes the induction and implies the H
\ddot{\text{o}}
lder continuity of
u
. Indeed, let us fix
0<r\leq R
, and then there is a nonnegative integer
j
such that

\lambda^{j+1}R\leq r\leq\lambda^{j}R,

which results in

j+1\leq\text{ln}\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{\frac{1}{\text{ln}\lambda}}.

Hence,

\omega_{j}=(1-\tau)^{j}\omega\leq(1-\tau)^{-1}(1-\tau)^{\frac{1}{\text{ln}\lambda}\text{ln}\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)}\omega=\frac{\omega}{1-\tau}\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{\alpha}

with

\alpha=\frac{\text{ln}(1-\tau)}{\text{ln}\lambda}.

In addition, we have
B_{r}(\xi_{0})\subset B_{\lambda^{j}R}=B_{j}(\xi_{0})
. Therefore, we arrive at

\osc\limits_{B_{r}}u\leq\osc\limits_{B_{j}}\leq\omega_{j}\leq\frac{\omega}{1-\tau}\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{\alpha}.

Now we have finished the proof of Theorem
1.2
.

4.3

4.1

4.2

4.3

Lemma 5.1
.

Let
B_{4R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
and
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a weak solution of Eq. (

and

\left|B_{R}(\xi_{0})\cap\left\{u\geq t\right\}\right|\geq\beta|B_{R}|

for

and

n,p,q,s
and

R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},4R)>g\left(\frac{\delta t}{R^{s}}\right)

With the help of Lemma
5.1

[
34

Lemma 5.2
.

Let
\beta\in(0,1),t>0,k\in\mathbb{N}
and
B_{16R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
. Assume
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
is a weak supersolution of Eq. (
1.1
). When

u\geq 0\quad\text{in }B_{16R}(\xi_{0})

and

\left|B_{R}(\xi_{0})\cap\left\{u\geq t\right\}\right|\geq\beta^{k}|B_{R}|,

we can find a constant
\delta\in\left(0,\frac{1}{8}\right]
, depending only upon
n,p,q,s
and
\beta
, such that either

R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)>g\left(\frac{\delta^{k}t}{R^{s}}\right)

(5.1)

or

u\geq\delta^{k}t\quad\text{in }B_{R}(\xi_{0}).

The proof of this lemma is almost identical to that of
[
11
, Lemma 6.7]
, because in this process the structure of Eq. (
1.1

\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};x_{0},12r)
in
[
11
, Lemma 6.7]

\eta\in B_{R}(\xi_{0}),r\in\frac{R}{3}
and
u\geq 0
in
B_{12r}(\eta)

\displaystyle(3r)^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\eta,12r)

\displaystyle=(3r)^{s}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{12r}(\eta)}g\left(\frac{u_{-}}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle=(3r)^{s}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{16R}(\xi_{0})}g\left(\frac{u_{-}}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C(Q,p,q,s)R^{s}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{16R}(\xi_{0})}g\left(\frac{u_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq Cg\left(\frac{\delta^{k}t}{R}\right)\leq g\left(\frac{\delta^{k}t}{3r}\right)

with the use of the converse of (
5.1
), which guarantees the reasonable application of Lemma
5.1
in
B_{12r}(\eta)

Based on Lemma
5.2
, we can conclude the following weak Harnack inequality in a rather straightforward way.

Lemma 5.3
.

Let
B_{16R}:=B_{16R}(\xi_{0})\subset\Omega
and
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be a weak solution to Eq. (
1.1
). If

u\geq 0\quad\text{in }B_{16R},

(5.2)

then there are two constants
\epsilon_{0}\in(0,1)
and
C\geq 1
, both of which depend only on
n,p,q,s
, such that

\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{R}}u^{\epsilon_{0}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}\leq C\left(\inf_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right).

Proof.

Assume
u
does not vanish identically in
B_{R}
. Let
\delta\in\left(0,\frac{1}{8}\right]
comes from Lemma
5.2
with
\beta=\frac{1}{2}
. Define

\epsilon_{0}:=\frac{1}{2\log_{\frac{1}{2}}\delta}\in(0,1).

We assert that, for any
t\geq 0
,

\inf_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\right)\geq\delta\left(\frac{|A^{+}(t,R)|}{|B_{R}|}\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}t.

(5.3)

Via the definition of
A^{+}(t,R)
, we just need to verify (
5.3
) for
t\in\left[0,\sup_{B_{R}}u\right)
. Here let
\sup_{B_{R}}u=+\infty
if
u
is not bounded from above in
B_{R}
.

Fixing arbitrarily
t\in\left[0,\sup\limits_{B_{R}}u\right)
, we can find
k=k(t)
as the smallest integer fulfilling

\left|A^{+}(t,R)\right|\geq 2^{-k}|B_{R}|,

(5.4)

i.e.,
k
is an integer such that

\log_{\frac{1}{2}}\frac{\left|A^{+}(t,R)\right|}{|B_{R}|}\leq k<1+\log_{\frac{1}{2}}\frac{\left|A^{+}(t,R)\right|}{|B_{R}|}.

Then we get

\delta\left(\frac{\left|A^{+}(t,R)\right|}{|B_{R}|}\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}\leq\delta^{k}.

(5.5)

If
R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\right)>\delta^{k}t
, then nothing is proved for (
5.3
). Let us consider the case
R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\right)\leq\delta^{k}t
, that is,

R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\leq g\left(\frac{\delta^{k}t}{R^{s}}\right).

At this point, this inequality and (
5.4
) justify the application of Lemma
5.2
, and we derive

u\geq\delta^{k}t\quad\text{in }B_{R}.

From mentioned above, there holds

\inf_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\right)\geq\delta^{k}t,

and by invoking (
5.5
), we can see that (
5.3
) is valid. We now rearrange (
5.3
) to have

\frac{\left|A^{+}(t,R)\right|}{|B_{R}|}\leq\left(\frac{k}{\delta t}\right)^{\epsilon_{0}}

with
k=\inf\limits_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\right)
. Then exploiting this display and Cavalieri’s principle and through some standard calculations, we could deduce

\displaystyle\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{R}}u^{\epsilon_{0}}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\inf_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},16R)\right)\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\inf_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right),

the details of which can be found for instance in
[
11
, Pages 4810-4811]
.
∎

Lemma 5.4
.

Suppose that
u\in H\mathbb{W}^{s,G}(\Omega)\cap L^{g}_{s}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
is a weak solution of Eq. (
1.1
) such that
u\geq 0
in
B_{R}=B_{R}(\xi_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
. Then there holds that

R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},R)\right)\leq C\left(\sup_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right),

where
C>0
depends on
n,p,q,s
.

Proof.

Let
k=2\sup\limits_{B_{R}}u
and
w_{-}=(u-k)_{-}
. We apply Proposition
3.1
with
\rho=\frac{R}{2}
and
r=R
to get

\displaystyle\ \quad\int_{B_{\frac{R}{2}}}w_{-}(\xi)\left[\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}g\left(\frac{w_{+}(\eta)}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right]d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{B_{R}}G\left(\frac{w_{-}}{R^{s}}\right)d\xi+C||w_{-}||_{L^{1}(B_{R})}\mathrm{Tail}(w_{-};\xi_{0},R).

(5.6)

Observe that
w_{-}\leq k
in
B_{R}
by
u\geq 0
in
B_{R}
. Thus we can see

||w_{-}||_{L^{1}(B_{R})}\leq k|B_{R}|\quad\text{and}\quad\int_{B_{R}}G\left(\frac{w_{-}}{R^{s}}\right)d\xi\leq G\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)|B_{R}|.

In addition, the tail is evaluated as

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tail}(w_{-};\xi_{0},R)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}g\left(\frac{u_{-}+k}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}\left[g\left(\frac{u_{-}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)+g\left(\frac{k}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\right]\frac{d\xi}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)+C\frac{1}{R^{s}}g\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right).

Thereby, the right-hand side of (
5
) could be controlled by

C\left[G\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)+\frac{k}{R^{s}}g\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)+k\mathrm{Tail}((u_{-};\xi_{0},R))\right]\left|B_{R}\right|.

We in turn tackle the left-hand side of (
5
). Note that for
\xi\in B_{\frac{R}{2}}(\xi_{0})
and
\eta\in\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}(\xi_{0})
, there holds that
|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\leq|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi_{0}|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}+|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\xi|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\leq 2|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\eta|_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}
. Furthermore, by
k\geq 0
, we discover

g(u_{+})\leq g\left((u-k)_{+}+k\right)\leq C(p,q)\left(g\left((u-k)_{+}\right)+g(k)\right).

Thanks to these two facts, we calculate

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}}w_{-}(\xi)\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}g\left(\frac{w_{+}(\eta)}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\,d\xi

\displaystyle\geq Ck\int_{B_{\frac{R}{2}}}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}g\left(\frac{w_{+}(\eta)}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\eta|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\frac{d\eta}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\eta|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\,d\xi

\displaystyle\geq Ck|B_{R}|\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\setminus B_{R}}\left[g\left(\frac{u_{+}}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\eta|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)-g\left(\frac{k}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\eta|^{s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right)\right]\frac{d\eta}{|\xi_{0}^{-1}\circ\eta|^{Q+s}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}

\displaystyle\geq Ck|B_{R}|\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},R)-C\frac{k}{R^{s}}g\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)|B_{R}|.

In summary, we arrive at

k\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},R)\leq CG\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)+C\frac{k}{R^{s}}g\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)+Ck\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R),

namely,

\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},R)\leq C\frac{1}{R^{s}}g\left(\frac{k}{R^{s}}\right)+C\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)

with
C
depending only on
n,p,q,s
. Now following the computations in
[
17
, page 19]
, we deduce that

R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\xi_{0},R)\right)\leq C\left(\sup_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right).

We complete this proof now.
∎

In the end, we carry out the proof of the nonlocal Harnack estimate given in Theorem
1.3
. For this purpose, we need the forthcoming result obtained by
[

]
.

Lemma 5.5
.

Assume that
F:\left[0,\infty\right)\rightarrow\left[0,\infty\right)
is a nondecreasing function and that the function
t\rightarrow\frac{F(t)}{t}
is nonincreasing. Then we can find a concave function
\bar{F}:\left[0,\infty\right)\rightarrow\left[0,\infty\right)
fulfilling

\frac{1}{2}\bar{F}(t)\leq F(t)\leq\bar{F}(t)\quad\text{for }t\geq 0.

Proof of Theorem
1.3
.
Fix any
\eta\in B_{R}:=B_{R}(\xi_{0})
and
r\in\left(0,2R\right]
. Then the local boundedness result, Theorem
1.1
, gives directly

\sup_{B_{r}(\eta)}u\leq C(2r)^{s}G^{-1}\left(\delta^{\gamma}{\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}G\left(\frac{u}{(2r)^{s}}\right)d\xi\right)+(2r)^{s}g^{-1}\left(\delta r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\eta,r)\right),

where
\gamma=\frac{\theta}{1-\theta}<0
for
\theta>1
given by Lemma
3.2
. Observe that, for
t\geq 0
,

G^{-1}(at)\leq\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}a^{\frac{1}{p}}G^{-1}(t)\quad\text{with }a\geq 1

and

g^{-1}(at)\leq\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}a^{\frac{1}{q-1}}g^{-1}(t)\quad\text{with }0<a\leq 1.

Taking these facts and the tail estimate, Lemma
5.4
, into account, we obtain

\displaystyle\sup_{B_{r}(\eta)}u

\displaystyle\leq C\delta^{\frac{\gamma}{p}}(2r)^{s}G^{-1}\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}G\left(\frac{u}{(2r)^{s}}\right)d\xi\right)+C\delta^{\frac{1}{q-1}}r^{s}g^{-1}\left(r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{+};\eta,r)\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\delta^{\frac{\gamma}{p}}(2r)^{s}G^{-1}\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}G\left(\frac{u}{(2r)^{s}}\right)d\xi\right)

\displaystyle+C\delta^{\frac{1}{q-1}}\left(\sup_{B_{r}(\eta)}u+r^{s}g^{-1}\left(r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\eta,r)\right)\right).

Via taking such a small
\delta>0
that
C\delta^{\frac{1}{q-1}}\leq\frac{1}{2}
, the last display becomes

\sup_{B_{r}(\eta)}u\leq C\delta^{\frac{\gamma}{p}}(2r)^{s}G^{-1}\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}G\left(\frac{u}{(2r)^{s}}\right)d\xi\right)+Cr^{s}g^{-1}\left(r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\eta,r)\right).

Thanks to Lemma
5.5
, we could find a concave function
\bar{G}(t)
fulfilling
\bar{G}(t)\approx G(t^{\frac{1}{q}})
. Then an application of Jensen’s inequality with
\bar{G}^{-1}(t)\approx\left(G^{-1}(t)\right)^{q}
to the previous inequality indicates

\sup_{B_{r}(\eta)}u\leq C\delta^{\frac{\gamma}{p}}\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}u^{q}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}+Cr^{s}g^{-1}\left(r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\eta,r)\right).

For the constant
\epsilon_{0}\in(0,1)
from Lemma
5.3
, employing Young’s inequality we derive

\displaystyle\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}u^{q}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\sup_{B_{2r}}u\right)^{\frac{q-\epsilon_{0}}{q}}\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}u^{\epsilon_{0}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{1}\sup_{B_{2r}}u+C(\delta_{1})\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}}u^{\epsilon_{0}}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}.

Thus, via further picking
\delta_{1}\in(0,1)
so small that
C\delta^{\frac{\gamma}{p}}\delta_{1}=\frac{1}{2}
, the combination of two inequalities above yields

\sup_{B_{r}(\eta)}u\leq\frac{1}{2}\sup_{B_{2r}(\eta)}u+C\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2r}(\eta)}u^{\epsilon_{0}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}+Cr^{s}g^{-1}\left(r^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\eta,r)\right).

Now we would like to make use of the iteration tool, Lemma
2.2
. Set
1\leq\sigma<\tau\leq 2
and
r=(\tau-\sigma)R
. We conclude through a covering argument that

\sup_{B_{\sigma R}}u\leq\frac{1}{2}\sup_{B_{\tau R}}u+\frac{C}{(\tau-\sigma)^{\frac{Q}{\epsilon_{0}}}}\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2R}}u^{\epsilon_{0}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}+CR^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right).

Therefore, apply Lemma
2.2
to deduce

\displaystyle\sup_{B_{R}}u

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\frac{Q}{\epsilon_{0}}\right)\left[C\left({\int\hskip-10.81218pt-}_{B_{2R}}u^{\epsilon_{0}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}}}+CR^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right]

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\inf_{B_{R}}u+R^{s}g^{-1}\left(R^{s}\mathrm{Tail}(u_{-};\xi_{0},R)\right)\right),

where in the last line Lemma
5.3
has been used and the constant
C>0
depends only on
n,p,q,s
.
