On the structure Lie operator of a real hypersurface in the complex quadric
Juan de Dios Pérez, David Pérez-López, Young Jin Suh

Abstract
The almost contact metric structure that we have on a real hypersurface M \displaystyle M\; in the complex quadric Q m = S ​ O m + 2 / S ​ O m ​ S ​ O 2 \displaystyle Q^{m}=SO_{m+2}/SO_{m}SO_{2} allows us to define, for any nonnull real number k \displaystyle k , the k \displaystyle k -th generalized Tanaka-Webster connection on M \displaystyle M\; , ∇ ^ ( k ) \displaystyle\hat{\nabla}^{(k)} . Associated to this connection we have Cho and torsion operators, F X ( k ) \displaystyle F_{X}^{(k)} and T X ( k ) \displaystyle T_{X}^{(k)} , respectively, for any vector field X \displaystyle X tangent to M \displaystyle M . From them and for any symmetric operator B \displaystyle B on M \displaystyle M we can consider two tensor fields of type (1,2) on M \displaystyle M that we will denote by B F ( k ) \displaystyle B_{F}^{(k)} and B T ( k ) \displaystyle B_{T}^{(k)} , respectively. We will classify real hypersurfaces M \displaystyle M in Q m \displaystyle Q^{m} for which any of those tensors identically vanishes, in the particular case of B \displaystyle B being the structure Lie operator L ξ \displaystyle L_{\xi} on M \displaystyle M .

\displaystyle T_{X}^{(k)}L_{\xi}Y-L_{\xi}T_{X}^{(k)}Y=0

for any

\displaystyle Y

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle X\in\mathcal{C}

\displaystyle\lambda\neq k

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}

\displaystyle\phi

\displaystyle k

\displaystyle\phi
