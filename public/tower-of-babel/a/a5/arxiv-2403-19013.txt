Antiferromagnetic domains in a single crystal of the A-type spin-7/2 trigonal topological insulator EuSn 2 As 2
Santanu Pakhira, D. C. Johnston

Abstract
EuSn 2 As 2 is a trigonal A-type antiferromagnetic topological insulator with the moments aligned in the a ​ b ab plane and with a Néel temperature T N = 23.5 T_{\rm N}=23.5 K. Here we report that an EuSn 2 As 2 crystal exhibits a broad peak at H c1 = 1100 H_{\rm c1}=1100 Oe in the field derivative d ​ M a ​ b / d ​ H dM_{ab}/dH of the a ​ b ab -plane magnetization M a ​ b ​ ( H ) M_{ab}(H) at temperature T = 2 T=2 K, demonstrating the presence of trigonal antiferromagnetic domains. We model these M a ​ b ​ ( H , T = 2 ​ K ) M_{ab}(H,\,T=2\,{\rm K}) data and obtain the trigonal anisotropy-energy coefficient K 3 K_{3} that is 13.3 and 3.7 times larger than those we previously reported for single crystals of the trigonal compounds EuMg 2 Sb 2 and EuMg 2 Bi 2 , respectively.
