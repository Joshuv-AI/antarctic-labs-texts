Cohomologies, non-abelian extensions and Wells sequences of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras
Qinxiu Sun, Zhen Li

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate cohomologies and non-abelian extensions of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras. First, we consider Lie comodules and cohomologies of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras. Next, we study non-abelian extensions of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras and classify the non-abelian extensions in terms of non-abelian cohomology group. Furthermore, we explore extensibility of a pair of automorphisms about a non-abelian extension of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras, and derive the fundamental sequences of Wells in the context of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras. Finally, we discuss the previous results in the case of abelian extensions of λ \lambda -weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras.

Introduction

Rota-Baxter operators on associative algebras arose initially in Baxter’s study of the fluctuation theory in probability
[
5
]
,
which can be viewed as an algebraic abstraction of the integral operators.
On the other hand, Rota-Baxter operators on Lie algebras were first considered
by Kuperscmidt in the study of classical
r
-matrices
[
30
]
.
Since then, Rota-Baxter operators have been investigated in connection with
many mathematical and physical branches, including combinatorics
[
22
]
, number theory
[
24
]
, operads and
quantum field theory
[
9
]
.

Rota-Baxter operators with arbitrary weight (also called weighted Rota-Baxter operators) were considered
in
[
1
,
2
]
. They are related with weighted infinitesimal bialgebras, weighted Yang-Baxter equations
[
17
]
, combinatorics of
rooted forests
[
38
]
, post-Lie algebras and modified Yang-Baxter
equations
[
1
]
. Recently, Tang, Bai, Guo and Sheng
[
34
]
developed the deformation and
cohomology theory of
\mathcal{O}
-operators (also called relative Rota-Baxter operators) on Lie algebras,
with applications to Rota-Baxter Lie algebras in mind. Later, Das in
[
11
,
12
]
investigated the cohomologies of Rota-Baxter operators
of arbitrary weights on associative algebras and Lie algebras. Wang and Zhou in
[
36
]
explored cohomology and homotopy theories of Rota-Baxter algebras
with any weight. There are some other related work
concerning cohomologies of Rota-Baxter operators of any weight, see
[
7
,
25
]
and there references.

The notion of a coalgebra is dual to the notion of an algebra. The theory of coassociative coalgebras
has been developed for a long time within the framework of the theory of Hopf algebras. Lie coalgebras were investigated earlier in
[
10
]
by
W. Michaelis. It is well known that the dual of a coassociative coalgebra is
an associative algebra and the dual of a Lie coalgebra is a Lie algebra. Regarding applications, Lie coalgebras are not only closely related with Lie bialgebras and
quantum groups, but also appeared in various fields such as homotopy theory
[
33
]
, noncommutative
geometry
[
20
]
. Specifically, a bialgebra structure for Rota-Baxter
Lie algebras was studied in
[
3
]
. In a recent study, cohomologies of Lie coalgebras were examined by Du and Tan in
[
16
]
. But so far, cohomologies of
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter
Lie coalgebras are still not given. This is our first
motivation for writing the present paper.

Extensions are useful mathematical objects to understand the
underlying structures. The non-abelian extension is a relatively
general one among various extensions (e.g. central extensions,
abelian extensions, non-abelian extensions etc.). Non-abelian
extensions were first developed by Eilenberg and Maclane
[
18
]
,
which induce to the low dimensional non-abelian cohomology group.
Then numerous works have been devoted to non-abelian extensions of
various kinds of algebras, such as Lie (super)algebras, Leibniz algebras, Lie 2-algebras, Lie Yagamuti algebras, Rota-Baxter groups,
Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and Rota-Baxter Leibniz algebras, see
[
6
,
8
,
14
,
19
,
21
,
23
,
27
,
29
,
31
]
and their references.
The abelian extensions of Lie coalgebras were explored
in
[
15
,
16
]
and characterized in terms of coderivations of Lie coalgebras.
But little is known about the non-abelian extension of Lie coalgebras particularly
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter
Lie coalgebras. This is the second motivation for writing the present paper.

Another interesting study related to extensions of algebraic structures is given by the extensibility and inducibility of a pair
of automorphisms. When a pair of automorphisms is inducible? This problem was first
considered by Wells
[
37
]
for abstract groups and further studied in
[
28
,
32
]
.
Since then, several authors have studied this subject further, see
[
4
,
23
,
26
,
31
]
and references therein.
The extensibility problem of a pair of derivations in abelian extensions were investigated in
[
13
,
35
]
.
Recently, the extensibility problem of a pair of derivations and automorphisms was extended to
the context of abelian extensions of Lie coalgebras
[
15
]
. As byproducts, the Wells short exact sequences were obtained for
various kinds of algebras
[
14
,
15
,
21
,
23
,
26
,
28
,
31
]
, which connected various automorphism groups and the non-abelian second cohomology.
Motivated by these results, we study extensibility of a pair of automorphisms in a non-abelian extension of
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter
Lie coalgebras. This is another motivation for writing the present paper. We give a necessary and sufficient condition for
a pair of automorphisms to be extensible. We also derive the analogue of the Wells short exact sequences in the context of
non-abelian extensions of
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we introduce Lie comodules of
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter
Lie coalgebras. In Section 3, we consider cohomologies of
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter
Lie coalgebras. In Section 4, we

we study the problem of when a pair of automorphisms is extensible. We address the necessary and sufficient condition for
a pair of automorphisms to be extensible.
In Section 6, we build Wells short exact sequences in the context of non-abelian extensions of
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter
Lie coalgebras. Finally, we discuss these results in the case of abelian extensions.

\lambda

In this section, we introduce the Lie comodules of
\lambda

We begin with recalling definitions of Lie coalgebras and
\lambda

[

Definition 2.1
.

(i)

A Lie coalgebra is a vector space
C
together with a linear map
\Delta_{C}:C\rightarrow C\otimes C
satisfying

\Delta_{C}=-\tau\Delta_{C},

(2.1)

(I\otimes\Delta_{C})\Delta_{C}-(\Delta_{C}\otimes I)\Delta_{C}+(I\otimes\tau)(\Delta_{C}\otimes I)\Delta_{C}=0.

(2.2)

(ii)

Let
(C,\Delta_{C})
be a Lie coalgebra. A linear operator
R_{C}:C\rightarrow C
is called a
\lambda
-weighted
(\lambda\in k)
Rota-Baxter operator if

\displaystyle(R_{C}\otimes R_{C})\Delta_{C}=(I\otimes R_{C}+R_{C}\otimes I+\lambda)\Delta_{C}R_{C}.

(2.3)

Moreover, a Lie coalgebra
(C,\Delta_{C})
with a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter operator
R_{C}
is called a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebra (Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebra of weight
\lambda
).

Definition 2.2
.

Let
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})
and
(C^{\prime},\Delta_{C^{\prime}},R_{C^{\prime}})
be two
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebras. A homomorphism from
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})
to
(C^{\prime},\Delta_{C^{\prime}},R_{C^{\prime}})
is a Lie coalgebra homomorphism
\varphi:C\rightarrow C^{\prime}
satisfying
R_{C^{\prime}}\varphi=\varphi R_{C}
.

Let
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})
be a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebra.
Denote the set of all automorphisms of
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})

\mathrm{Aut}(C)
.
Obviously,
\mathrm{Aut}(C)
is a group.
A right Lie comodule (corepresentation) of a Lie coalgebra
(C,\Delta_{C})
is a tuple
(M,\rho)
, where
M
is a vector space and
\rho:M\rightarrow M\otimes C
is a linear map satisfying

\displaystyle(I\otimes\Delta_{C})\rho-(\rho\otimes I)\rho+(I\otimes\tau)(\rho\otimes I)\rho=0.

(2.4)

Definition 2.3
.

Let
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})
be a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebra.
A right Lie comodule of
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})
is a triple
(M,\rho,R_{M})
, where
(M,\rho)
is
a right Lie comodule of
(C,\Delta_{C})
and
R_{M}:M\rightarrow M
is a linear map such that

\displaystyle(R_{M}\otimes R_{C})\rho=(R_{M}\otimes I+I\otimes R_{C}+\lambda)\rho R_{M}.

(2.5)

Example 2.1
.

Any
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebra is a right Lie comodule of itself, which is called the adjoint corepresentation.

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
(C,\Delta_{C},R_{C})
be a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie coalgebra. Then
(C^{*},[\ ,\ ]_{C^{*}},R_{C}^{*})
is a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter Lie algebra.

Proof.

It is well known that
C^{*}
is a Lie algebra with the multiplication given by

\displaystyle[f,g]_{C^{*}}=(f\otimes g)\Delta_{C},~\forall~f,g\in C^{*}.

We only need to check that
R_{C}^{*}
is a
\lambda
-weighted Rota-Baxter operator.
Define
R_{C}^{*}(f)=fR_{C},~\forall~f\in C^{*}
. In view of (
2.3
), for all
f,g\in C^{*}
,
we have

\displaystyle[R_{C}^{*}(f),R_{C}^{*}(g)]_{C^{*}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[fR_{C},gR_{C}]_{C^{*}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(fR_{C}\otimes gR_{C})\Delta_{C}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(f\otimes g)(R_{C}\otimes I+I\otimes R_{C}+\lambda)\Delta_{C}R_{C}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(fR_{C}\otimes g)\Delta_{C}R_{C}+(f\otimes gR_{C})\Delta_{C}R_{C}+\lambda(f\otimes g)\Delta_{C}R_{C}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[fR_{C},g]_{C^{*}}R_{C}+[f,gR_{C}]_{C^{*}}R_{C}+\lambda[f,g]_{C^{*}}R_{C}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[R_{C}^{*}(f),g]_{C^{*}}R_{C}+[f,R_{C}^{*}(g)]_{C^{*}}R_{C}+\lambda[f,g]_{C^{*}}R_{C}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{C}^{*}[R_{C}^{*}(f),g]_{C^{*}}+R_{C}^{*}[f,R_{C}^{*}(g)]_{C^{*}}+\lambda R_{C}^{*}[f,g]_{C^{*}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{C}^{*}\big([R_{C}^{*}(f),g]_{C^{*}}+[f,R_{C}^{*}(g)]_{C^{*}}+\lambda[f,g]_{C^{*}}\big).

This finishes the proof.
∎
