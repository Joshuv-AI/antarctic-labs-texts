Abstract

It turns out that as the LQG parameter l l increases, the unstable photon orbit radius x p ​ s x_{ps} , the critical impact parameter u p ​ s u_{ps} , the deflection angle α D ​ ( θ ) \alpha_{D}(\theta) and angular position θ ∞ \theta_{\infty} also increases. Meanwhile, the angular separation s s decreases, and relative magnitude r m ​ a ​ g r_{mag} increases with increasing l l for prograde motion but they show opposite behaviour for the retrograde motion. Using supermassive black holes (SMBH) Sgr A* and M87* as lenses, we compare the observable signatures of LMRBH with those of Kerr black holes.
