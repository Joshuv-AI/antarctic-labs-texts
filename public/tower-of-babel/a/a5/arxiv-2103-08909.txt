Capacity of entanglement in random pure state
Kazumi Okuyama

Abstract
We compute the capacity of entanglement in the bipartite random pure state model using the replica method. We find the exact expression of the capacity of entanglement which is valid for a finite dimension of the Hilbert space. We argue that in the gravitational path integral, the capacity of entanglement receives contributions only from the sub-leading saddle points corresponding to the partially connected geometries.

Introduction

In recent papers
Penington:2019kki
;
Almheiri:2019qdq

is reproduced from the replica computation of the

As argued by Page in

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{H}_{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}_{B}.

(1)

Here the subsystems

and

From the reduced density matrix
\rho_{A}

\displaystyle\rho_{A}=\Tr_{B}|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|,

(2)

we can compute the entanglement entropy
S_{A}

\displaystyle S_{A}=-\langle\Tr\rho_{A}\log\rho_{A}\rangle,

(3)

where the bracket

denotes the ensemble average over the random
pure state
|\Psi\rangle
.
The exact form of

(
42

in

as a function of the dimensions

of the Hilbert spaces (
1
)

\displaystyle d_{A}=\dim\mathcal{H}_{A},\quad d_{B}=\dim\mathcal{H}_{B}.

(4)

As discussed in

,
S_{A}
in the random pure state
model exhibits a similar behavior as the Page curve for the Hawking radiation
from an evaporating black hole.

In a recent paper
Kawabata:2021hac
,
it is argued that the capacity of entanglement
C_{A}
introduced in
qi

is a useful quantity to diagnose the phase transition
around the Page time.

C_{A}
is defined by

\displaystyle C_{A}=\Tr(\rho_{A}K^{2})-(\Tr\rho_{A}K)^{2},

(5)

where
K=-\log\rho_{A}
is the modular Hamiltonian.
In other words,
C_{A}
measures the fluctuation of the modular Hamiltonian.
See e.g.
Nakaguchi:2016zqi
;
Nakagawa:2017wis
;
deBoer:2018mzv
for the study of

in various models.

In this paper, we compute the capacity of entanglement in the random pure state model
using the replica method.
From the ensemble average
of
\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}
over the random pure state,
we can compute the entanglement entropy
S_{A}

and the capacity of entanglement

as
the derivative with respect to the replica number
n
at
n=1

\displaystyle S_{A}

\displaystyle=-\partial_{n}\log\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1}=-\partial_{n}\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1},

(6)

\displaystyle C_{A}

\displaystyle=\partial_{n}^{2}\log\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1}=\partial_{n}^{2}\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1}-(S_{A})^{2}.

We find the exact form of
C_{A}
as a function of the dimensions
d_{A},d_{B}
of the Hilbert
spaces
\mathcal{H}_{A},\mathcal{H}_{B}
.
The exact expression of

in (
46

is the main result of this paper.

As discussed in

;

In the case of the capacity

, it turns out that
the leading saddle point from the fully disconnected or fully connected geometries
does not contribute to
C_{A}
, and it receives non-zero contributions only from the
sub-leading saddle points corresponding to the partially connected geometries.
This is consistent with the
result of
deBoer:2018mzv
that

is a measure of the partial entanglement.

This paper is organized as follows.
In section

In section

Random pure state model

In this section, let us briefly review the random pure state model.
We consider a pure state
|\Psi\rangle
in the bipartite Hilbert space

\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{H}_{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}_{B}
.
This models the black hole evaporation where

A
corresponds to the Hawking radiation while
B
corresponds to the black hole.
In the model discussed in
Penington:2019kki
,

A
and
B
correspond to the end of the world brane and the bulk
JT gravity, respectively.
We can expand the state
|\Psi\rangle
in terms of the orthonormal basis
of
\mathcal{H}_{A}
and
\mathcal{H}_{B}

\displaystyle|\Psi\rangle=\mathcal{N}\sum_{i=1}^{d_{A}}\sum_{\alpha=1}^{d_{B}}X_{i\alpha}|i\rangle_{A}\otimes|\alpha\rangle_{B},

(7)

where
\mathcal{N}
is the normalization factor to ensure the unit norm of
|\Psi\rangle

\displaystyle\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle=1.

(8)

It is useful to regard the coefficient
X_{i\alpha}
in (
7
)
as a component of the
d_{A}\times d_{B}
complex matrix
X

\displaystyle X=(X_{i\alpha}).

(9)

Then the normalization factor

is written as

\displaystyle\mathcal{N}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\Tr(XX^{\dagger})}}.

(10)

We are interested in the reduced density matrix
\rho_{A}

2
)
obtained by tracing out

In terms of the matrix

\displaystyle\rho_{A}=\frac{XX^{\dagger}}{\Tr(XX^{\dagger})}=\frac{W}{\Tr W},

where

The ensemble average over the random pure state

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{O}(W)\rangle=\frac{\int dXdX^{\dagger}\mathcal{O}(W)e^{-\Tr(XX^{\dagger})}}{\int dXdX^{\dagger}e^{-\Tr(XX^{\dagger})}}.

As a distribution of the matrix

The matrix integral (

In the original paper by Page

was computed by

In this paper, we will compute the entropy

and the capacity

using the replica method (
6

To do this, we need the expectation value of the moment
\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}

Fortunately, the exact result of

\displaystyle=\log{d_{A}}-f^{\prime}(1,\alpha),

Next, let us compute the capacity of entanglement

To do this, we need to compute the second derivative of

\displaystyle\partial_{n}^{2}\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1}=

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to 1}\sum_{k=1}^{d_{A}}\frac{\Gamma(d_{A})\Gamma(d_{B}+1+n-k)\Gamma(d_{A}d_{B}+1)}{\Gamma(d_{A}+1-k)\Gamma(d_{B}+1)\Gamma(d_{A}d_{B}+n)}

(44)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\Biggl[N_{n,k}\Bigl(\psi(d_{B}+1+n-k)-\psi(d_{A}d_{B}+n)\Bigr)^{2}

\displaystyle+2\partial_{n}N_{n,k}\Bigl(\psi(d_{B}+1+n-k)-\psi(d_{A}d_{B}+n)\Bigr)

\displaystyle+N_{n,k}\Bigl(\psi_{1}(d_{B}+1+n-k)-\psi_{1}(d_{A}d_{B}+n)\Bigr)+\partial_{n}^{2}N_{n,k}\Biggr],

\psi_{1}(z)=\frac{d}{dz}\psi(z)

5
Conclusions and outlook

In this paper, we have computed
the exact capacity of entanglement
C_{A}
(
46
)
at finite
d_{A},d_{B}
using the replica method (
6
).
At the technical level, the important ingredient
in our computation is the new exact formula (
21
)
of
\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle

written in terms of the Narayana number
N_{n,k}
.
This formula (
21
) makes the relation to the planar limit manifest.
We argued that
C_{A}
vanishes for the fully connected or fully disconnected
geometries, and
C_{A}
is sensitive to the sub-leading contributions
to
\langle\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}\rangle
coming from the partial connected geometries
in the gravitational path integral.
This suggests that
C_{A}
is a good probe of the partial entanglement,
as discussed in
deBoer:2018mzv
.

There are several open questions.
The capacity of entanglement is introduced
in
qi
as an analogue of the heat capacity.
Indeed, if we introduce the modular Hamiltonian

K=-\log\rho_{A}
, the moment
\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}
looks like the partition function

\displaystyle Z_{n}=\Tr\rho_{A}^{n}=\Tr e^{-nK},

(53)

and
n
plays the role of the inverse temperature
\beta
.
In this picture,
our definition (
6
)
of
S_{A}
and
C_{A}
is based on the “annealed” free energy
\log\langle Z_{n}\rangle

\displaystyle S_{A}=-\partial_{n}\log\langle Z_{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1},\qquad C_{A}=\partial_{n}^{2}\log\langle Z_{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1}.

(54)

One could consider
the quenched version of
S_{A}
and
C_{A}
as well

\displaystyle S_{A}^{\text{qu}}=-\partial_{n}\langle\log Z_{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1},\qquad C_{A}^{\text{qu}}=\partial_{n}^{2}\langle\log Z_{n}\rangle\Big|_{n=1}.

(55)

We leave the computation of the quenched version of
S_{A}
and
C_{A}
as an interesting
future problem.

It would be interesting to study the gravitational picture of the
capacity of entanglement.

C_{A}
is related to the quantum fluctuation of the modular Hamiltonian and
the prescription of the gravitational computation of

C_{A}
is proposed in
Nakaguchi:2016zqi
.
We have argued that
C_{A}
receives contributions only
from the sub-leading partially connected geometries
in the replica computation. It would be interesting to related this
picture to the prescription in
Nakaguchi:2016zqi
.
