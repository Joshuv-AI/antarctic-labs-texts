Let

-primary ideal in

If

-stable filtration on

.

.

[
1
]
.

is Cohen-Macaulay, see
[
28

[

is a parameter ideal, see

Narita, see
[

(also see

An ideal
\mathfrak{a}
is said to be normal if
\mathfrak{a}^{n}
is integrally closed for all
n\geq 1
.
In a previous paper
[
24
]
we proved that Itohs conjecture holds when
\mathfrak{a}
is normal and
e_{3}^{\mathfrak{a}}(A)=0
. In fact we proved it more generally for Cohen-Macaulay analytically unramified rings. In this paper we prove Itoh’s conjecture in general. We prove

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a Cohen-Macaulay analytically unramified local ring of dimension
d\geq 3
and let
\mathfrak{a}
be an
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal with
e_{3}^{\mathfrak{a}^{*}}(A)=0
. Then
G_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)^{*}
is Cohen-Macaulay.

Note we are not assuming
A
is Gorenstein in Theorem
1.2
.

Our paper on Itoh’s conjecture for normal ideals gives us a road-map of a possible proof of the general case. However the details are considerably harder to prove in the general case.

We first describe the technique we used in the normal case and then we discuss the changes that we made in the general case.

First technique: Dual filtrations:

We need some preliminaries to get to our discussion.
Let
M
be a finitely generated
A
-module.
Recall an
\mathfrak{a}
-filtration
\mathcal{G}={\{M_{n}\}}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
on
M
is a
collection of submodules of
M
with the properties

(1)

M_{n}\supseteq M_{n+1}
for all
n\in\mathbb{Z}
,

(2)

M_{n}=M
for all
n\ll 0
,

(3)

\mathfrak{a}M_{n}\subseteq M_{n+1}
for all
n\in\mathbb{Z}
.

If

\mathfrak{a}M_{n}=M_{n+1}
for all
n\gg 0
then we say
\mathcal{G}
is
\mathfrak{a}
-
stable
.

Let
\mathcal{R}_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathfrak{a}^{n}
be the extended Rees algebra of
A
with respect to
\mathfrak{a}
.
If
\mathcal{G}=\{M_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
is an
\mathfrak{a}
-stable filtration on
M
then set

\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{G},M)=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}M_{n}
the
extended Rees-module
of
M

with respect to

\mathcal{G}
.
Notice that
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{G},M)
is a finitely generated graded
\mathcal{R}_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)
-module. If
\mathcal{G}
is
\mathfrak{a}
-adic then set
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{G},M)=\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{a},M)
.

Set
M^{*}=\operatorname{Hom}_{A}(M,A)
. For
n\in\mathbb{Z}
set

M_{n}^{*}=\{f\in M^{*}\mid f(M)\subseteq\mathfrak{a}^{n}\}\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{A}(M,\mathfrak{a}^{n}).

It is well-known that
M_{n+1}^{*}=\mathfrak{a}M_{n}^{*}
for all
n\gg 0
.
Note

for
n\leq 0
. Set
\mathcal{F}=\{M_{n}^{*}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
. We called this filtration
the dual filtration
of
M^{*}
with respect to
\mathfrak{a}
. This filtration is
classical cf.
[
30
, p. 12]
.

In
[
24
, 4.4]
we constructed an explicit isomorphism

\Psi_{M}\colon\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{F},M^{*})\quad\longrightarrow\operatorname{\ {}^{*}Hom}_{\mathcal{R}_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)}(\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{a},M),\mathcal{R}_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)).

It was an important observation that the dual filtrations behaves well with respect to the Veronese functor, see
[
24
, 4.8]
.

For the general case we first prove:

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a local Noetherian ring and let
\mathfrak{a}
be an ideal in
A
.
Let

be a finitely generated

\mathcal{G}
be an

-stable filtration on

-stable filtration

\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{H},M^{*})\cong\operatorname{\ {}^{*}Hom}_{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{G},M),\mathcal{R}_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)).

The isomorphism above is existential. However it is an important ingredient in our proof. We call

to be the

on

with respect to

.

In the adic case the good behaviour of dual filtrations with respect to the Veronese functor was just an observation. However in the general case we don’t know whether it is true. We prove a special case which is sufficient for our purposes.

6.
A generalization of a result due to Elias and an application to asymptotic integral closure filtration

By a result of Elias,
[
6
, 2.2]
, it is known that
0ptG_{I^{n}}(A)
is constant for
n\gg 0
for any
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal
I
. We have to extend this result to filtrations. Elias’s proof regreatabbly does not extend to filtrations. We restate Theorem
1.5
for the convenience of the reader. In fact we prove it for modules.

Theorem 6.1
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a local ring. Let
\mathfrak{a}
be an
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal in
A
.
Let
M
be a Cohen-Macaulay
A
-module of dimension
r
.
Let
\mathcal{F}=\{M_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
be an
\mathfrak{a}
-stable filtration on
M
with
M_{n}=M
for all
n\leq 0
.
Then

0ptG_{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M)
is constant for

m\gg 0
.

Proof.

We have nothing to show if
r=0
. So assume
r\geq 1
.
Throughout we take local cohomology with respect to
\mathfrak{M}
the maximal homogeneous ideal of
\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{a}}(A)
.
Let

p=\min\{i|i<r,\ \text{and either}\ H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{-1}\neq 0\ \text{or}\ \ell(H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M)))=\infty\}.

Note
p\geq 1
. For
i=0,\ldots,p-1
set

\displaystyle a_{i}(M)

\displaystyle=\inf\{n\mid H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{n}\neq 0\},

\displaystyle b_{i}(M)

\displaystyle=\sup\{n\mid H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{n}\neq 0\}.

Furthermore set

c_{i}(M)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if}\ H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))=0,\\
b_{i}(M)-a_{i}(M)+2&\ \text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Let
amp(M)=\max\{c_{i}\mod 0\leq i\leq p-1\}
.
We

Claim: For all
m\geq amp(M)
we have
0ptG_{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M)=p
.

We note that for all
m\geq amp(M)
we have for
i=0,\ldots,p-1

H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M))(-1)\cong\left(H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))(-1)\right)^{<m>}=0.

By
5.5.1
it follows that
0ptG_{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M)\geq p
.
If
p=r
then we are done. Assume
p<r
.
Suppose if possible

0ptG_{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M)>p
for some
m>amp(M)
. Then as

0=H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M)(-1))\cong\left(H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))(-1)\right)^{<m>},

it follows that
H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{-1}=0
and
H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{mn-1}=0
for all
n\in\mathbb{Z}
. As
n\mapsto\ell(H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{n}
coincides with a polynomial for
n\ll 0
(see

5.4
(d)), it follows that
H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{n}=0
for
n\ll 0
. Thus
\ell(H^{p}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M)))<\infty
. This contradicts the definition of
p
.
The result follows.
∎

We will need the following result:

Theorem 6.2
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a local ring. Let
\mathfrak{a}
be an
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal in
A
.
Let
M
be a Cohen-Macaulay
A
-module of dimension
r
.
Let
\mathcal{F}=\{M_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}

\mathfrak{a}

with

Proof.

We have nothing to prove if

. So assume

By
5.8
and
5.5.1
it follows that

H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}^{<m>}}(M))=0
for

we have

It follows that
H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{mn-1}=0
for all
n\in\mathbb{Z}
and for

. Fix

. As
n\mapsto\ell(H^{i}(L^{\mathcal{F}}(M))_{n}

∎

6.3
.

Recall an ideal

is said to be asymptotically normal if

is integrally closed for all

.
If

is a asymptotically normal
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal and

that in this case

(here

We give a proof of Theorem

. We restate it for the convenience of the reader.

Theorem 6.4
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a Cohen-Macaulay analytically unramified local ring with

\mathfrak{a}

\mathfrak{m}

7.
An extension of a result due to Hoa

In this section we give an extension of a result of Hoa,
[
12
]
, which is sufficient for our needs.
We restate it for the convenience of the reader.

Theorem 7.1
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a Noetherian local ring of dimension
d\geq 1
and let
\mathfrak{a}
be an
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal. Let
\mathcal{F}=\{\mathfrak{a}_{n}\}_{n\geq 0}
be a multiplicative
\mathfrak{a}
-stable filtration.
Suppose
\mathcal{F}^{<c>}
is
J=\mathfrak{a}_{c}
-adic and that reduction number of
J^{n}
is
<d
for all
n\gg 0
. Then the
a
-invariant of
G_{\mathcal{F}}(A)
is negative.

Proof.

By our assumption we have that the
a
-invariant of
G_{J}(A)
is negative. Suppose if possible
the
a
-invariant of
G_{\mathcal{F}}(A)
is
\geq 0
. Then as local cohomology behaves very well with respect to the Veronese functor we have
H^{d}(G_{\mathcal{F}}(A)^{<c>})_{0}\neq 0
.

Notice we have surjective maps

\mathfrak{a}_{cn}/\mathfrak{a}_{c(n+1)}\rightarrow\mathfrak{a}_{cn}/\mathfrak{a}_{cn+1}\rightarrow 0\quad\text{for all}\ n\geq 1.

So we have a surjective ring homomorphism
G_{J}(A)\rightarrow G_{\mathcal{F}}(A)^{<c>}
. As dimensions of both the rings considered is
d
we get a surjective map

H^{d}(G_{J}(A))\rightarrow H^{d}(G_{\mathcal{F}}(A)^{<c>})\rightarrow 0,

which implies that
H^{d}(G_{J}(A))_{0}\neq 0
, a contradiction. The result follows.
∎
