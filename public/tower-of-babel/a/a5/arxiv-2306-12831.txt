Abstract
. Continuing [ 1 ] , we develop a version of Extender-based Magidor-Radin forcing where there are no extenders on the top ordinal. As an application, we provide another approach to obtain a failure of SCH on a club subset of an inaccessible cardinal, and a model where the cardinal arithmetic behaviors are different on stationary classes, whose union is the club, is provided. The cardinals and the cofinalities outside the clubs are not affected by the forcings.

9.

Assume GCH. Let
\kappa
be a strongly inaccessible cardinal. For each
\gamma<\kappa

\gamma

•

\vec{E}_{\gamma}=\langle E_{\gamma}(\alpha,\beta)\mid\beta<\circ^{\gamma}(\alpha)\rangle
.

•

each

(\alpha,\alpha^{+f_{\gamma}(\alpha)})
extender witnesses
\alpha

\alpha^{+f_{\gamma}(\alpha)}
-strong.

•

\circ^{\gamma}(\alpha)<\alpha
.

•

for
\nu<\kappa

Then we can proceed a similar forcing construction, except that the corresponding Cohen part at
\alpha

.

\mathbb{P}^{\langle f_{\gamma}\mid\gamma<\kappa\rangle}

0<\xi<\kappa
regular and
\gamma<\kappa
, the collection of
\alpha
with
{\rm cf}(\alpha)\geq\xi

Fix
\xi>0

\mathbb{P}
-name of a club subset of
\kappa

\dot{D}
.

\dot{D}
