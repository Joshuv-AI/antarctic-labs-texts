KK-parity non-conservation in UED confronts LHC data


Abstract
Kaluza-Klein (KK) parity can be violated in five-dimensional universal extra dimensional model with boundary-localized (kinetic or mass) terms (BLTs) at the fixed points of S 1 / Z 2 S^{1}/Z_{2} orbifold. In this framework we study the resonant production of Kaluza-Klein excitations of the neutral electroweak gauge bosons at the LHC and their decay into an electron-positron pair or a muon-antimuon pair. We use the results (first time in our knowledge) given by the LHC experiment to constrain the mass range of the first KK-excitation of the electroweak gauge bosons ( B 1 ​ and ​ W 3 1 B^{1}\ \textrm{and}\ W_{3}^{1} ). It is interesting to note that the LHC result puts an upper limit on the masses of the n = 1 n=1 KK-leptons for positive values of BLT parameters and depending upon the mass of ℓ + ​ ℓ − \ell^{+}\ell^{-} resonance.
