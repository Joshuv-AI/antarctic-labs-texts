Finite speed of propagation for the 2- and 3-dimensional multiplicative stochastic wave equation
Immanuel Zachhuber

Abstract
We prove finite speed of propagation for the multiplicative stochastic wave equation in two and three dimensions which leads us to a global space-time well-posedness result for the cubic nonlinear equation in the analogue of the energy space.



1
Introduction

The aim of this paper is to solve the
cubic multiplicative stochastic wave
equation
globally in space and time, which is formally

\displaystyle\partial^{2}_{t}u-\Delta u-u\cdot\xi

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-u^{3}\text{ on }\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}

\displaystyle(u,\partial_{t}u)|_{t=0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(u_{0},u_{1})

and

this equation from
[
9

well-posed in the periodic setting with data in the energy space. Moreover,
Strichartz estimates were shown to hold in 2 dimensions in

[
19

(following the work

[
25
]
on Strichartz estimates on the Schrödiger
analogue of the equation considered here). To be precise, the result from

[

is that until any time

one has a unique solution to

\displaystyle\partial^{2}_{t}u-Hu

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-u^{3}\text{ on }[0,T]\times\mathbb{T}^{d}

\displaystyle(u,\partial_{t}u)|_{t=0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(u_{0},u_{1})\in\mathcal{D}\left(\sqrt{-H}\right)\times L^{2},

with continuous dependence on the data. Here

denotes the continuous

The operator

The main difficulty to get global space-time solutions as opposed to the

Definition 2

(Weighted Besov spaces,

]
)

For
\nu\in\mathbb{R}
we consider the following class of
weights

\langle x\rangle^{\nu}=(1+|x|^{2})^{\frac{\nu}{2}}\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}

and define the weighted

space w.r.t. this weight as

L_{\langle\cdot\rangle^{\nu}}^{p}:=\{f\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{d}):f\cdot\langle\cdot\rangle^{\nu}\in L^{p}\},p\in[1,\infty],

whose norm is defined as

\|f\|_{L_{\langle\cdot\rangle^{\nu}}^{p}}:=\|f\langle\cdot\rangle^{\nu}\|_{L^{p}}.

Moreover, for a Littlewood-Paley decomposition
(\Delta_{i})_{i\geqslant-1},
one defines weighted Besov spaces as

B_{p,q,\nu}^{s}:=\{f\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{d}):\|f\|_{B_{p,q,\nu}^{s}}\char 60\relax\infty\},\text{with the norm }\|f\|_{B_{p,q,\nu}^{s}}:=\left\|\|\Delta_{i}f\|_{L_{\langle\cdot\rangle^{\nu}}^{p}}\right\|_{\ell_{i}^{q}}.

space, moreover we write

We note that one can analogously define these spaces on the torus
