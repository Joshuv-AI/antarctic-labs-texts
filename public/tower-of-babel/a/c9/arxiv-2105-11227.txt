Finite time Convergence of Pinning Synchronization with a single Nonlinear Controller


Abstract
In this paper, we discuss distributive synchronization of complex networks in finite time, with a single nonlinear pinning controller. The results apply to heterogeneous dynamic networks, too. Different from many models, which assume the coupling matrix being symmetric (or the connecting graph is undirected), here, the coupling matrix is asymmetric (or the connecting graph is directed).

Introduction

Synchronization of complex networks has been a hot topic in recent decades. The model in the literature can be described as

\displaystyle\dot{x}_{i}(t)=f(x_{i}(t))+c\sum_{j=1}^{m}a_{ij}x_{j}(t)

(1)

where
x_{i}(t)=[x_{i}^{1}(t),\cdots,x_{i}^{n}(t)]^{T}\in R^{n}
,
i=1,\cdots m
.

The purpose of synchronization is to make
x_{i}(t)-x_{j}(t)\rightarrow 0
for all
x_{i}(t)
,
i=1,\cdots,m
, when
t\rightarrow+\infty
.

In some cases, one hopes to make all
x_{i}(t)-s(t)\rightarrow 0
when
t\rightarrow+\infty
,
i=1,\cdots,m
, and
s(t)
is a solution of

\displaystyle\dot{s}(t)=f(s(t)).

(2)

Pick
\varepsilon>0
, in
[
1
]
, the following coupled network
with a single linear controller

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{cl}\frac{dx_{1}(t)}{dt}&=f(x_{1}(t))+c\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{1j}x_{j}(t)-c\varepsilon(x_{1}(t)-s(t)),\\
\frac{dx_{i}(t)}{dt}&=f(x_{i}(t))+c\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{ij}x_{j}(t),~~~~i=2,\cdots,m\end{array}\right.

was proposed. It can make all
x_{i}(t)-s(t)\rightarrow 0
exponentially, if
c
is chosen suitably.

Another topic is finite time convergence
[
3
]
-
[
9
]
. Finite time consensus was studied in
[
10
]
and
[
11
]
, where the general protocol has the following form:

\displaystyle\dot{x}_{i}(t)=\beta sig(\sum_{j}a_{ij}x_{j}(t))^{\alpha}+\gamma\sum_{j}a_{ij}x_{j}(t)

with
0\leq\alpha<1
,
\beta>0
, and
\gamma\geq 0
, and the network is directed.

On the other hand,
[
12
]
added controllers on all the nonidentical nodes and realized the finite time synchronization;
[
13
]
considered the finite/fixed time cluster synchronization for undirected networks with or without control;
[
14
]
considered the exponential synchronization for switched networks with pinning technique;
[
15
]
studied the finite time cluster synchronization in complex-variable, fractional-order and undirected networks;
[
16
]
and
[
17
]
investigated the finite time synchronization/stabilization for the (drive-response) inertial memristive neural networks with mixed delays. In these models, nonlinear couplings or controllers are introduced. However, the coupling matrices are required symmetric and/or the nonlinear controllers are added to all nodes.

It is natural to raise a question:

Can we design new algorithms with only a single pinning controller to make all
x_{i}(t)-s(t)\rightarrow 0
in finite time for directly connected networks?

In this paper, we give a confirmative answer. We propose simple finite time synchronization models and prove that under mild conditions, they can reach finite time synchronization.

Some basic concepts and background

Denote
x_{i}=[x_{i}^{1},\cdots,x_{i}^{n}]^{T}\in R^{n}
,
i=1,\cdots,m
.
x=[x_{1}^{T},\cdots,x_{m}^{T}]^{T}\in R^{mn}
.
||x_{i}||_{1}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}|x_{i}^{k}|
.
||x||_{1}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}||x_{i}||_{1}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{k=1}^{n}|x_{i}^{k}|
.

Definition 1
.

A coupling matrix
A=(a_{ij})_{i,j=1}^{m}
is defined as a Metzler matrix satisfying
a_{ij}\geq 0
, if
i\neq j
and
a_{ii}=-\sum_{j\neq i}a_{ij}
. We also assume
Rank(A)=m-1
.

Definition 2
.

A coupling matrix with a single pinning control on the first node
\tilde{A}=(\tilde{a}_{ij})_{i,j=1}^{m}
is defined as follows

\tilde{a}_{11}=a_{11}-\varepsilon
,
\varepsilon>0
and

\tilde{a}_{ij}=a_{ij}
otherwise.

It was revealed in
[
1
]
,
-\tilde{A}
is an M-matrix. By M-matrix theory, there are constants
\theta_{i}>0
,
i=1,\cdots,m
, with
\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}=1
and
\theta^{\star}>0
, such that

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\tilde{a}_{ij}<-\theta^{\star},

(6)

for all
j=1,\cdots,m
.

Distributed finite time synchronization algorithms of complex networks with a single nonlinear controller

In this section, we introduce distributed discontinuous and continuous algorithms for finite time synchronization of complex networks with a single nonlinear controller.

Firstly
, we propose following distributed algorithm with a single nonlinear controller

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{dx_{1}(t)}{dt}&=f(x_{1}(t))+c\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{1j}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))-\epsilon(x_{1}(t)-s(t))}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{1j}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))-\epsilon(x_{1}(t)-s(t))||_{1}},\\
\frac{dx_{i}(t)}{dt}&=f(x_{i}(t))+c\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{ij}x_{j}(t)}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{ij}x_{j}(t)||_{1}},~~~~i=2,\cdots,m\end{array}\right.

and prove the following Theorem.

Theorem 1
.

Suppose
||f(x_{i}(t))-f(s(t))||_{1}\leq L||x_{i}(t)-s(t)||_{1}
,
L>0
,
\forall i
. Then, the controlled system (
3
)
can synchronize all
x_{i}(t)
to
s(t)
in finite time with sufficiently large
c
.

Proof

It is clear that system (
3
) can be written as

\displaystyle\frac{dx_{i}(t)}{dt}

\displaystyle=f(x_{i}(t))+c\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))||_{1}},~~i=1,2,\cdots,m

(10)

Denote
y_{i}(t)=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}x_{j}(t)
,
\delta{y}_{i}(t)=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))
. Then, we have

\displaystyle\dot{\delta}y_{i}(t)=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\delta f(x_{j}(t))+c\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\frac{\delta{y}_{j}(t)}{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}},

(11)

where
\delta f(x_{j}(t))=f(x_{j}(t))-f(s(t))
. Define a norm

\displaystyle||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}||\delta{y}_{i}(t)||_{1}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\sum_{k=1}^{n}|\delta{y}_{i}^{k}(t)|,

where
\delta{y}(t)=[\delta{y}_{1}^{T}(t),\cdots,\delta{y}_{m}^{T}(t)]^{T}
,
\delta{y}_{i}(t)=[\delta{y}_{i}^{1}(t),\cdots,\delta{y}_{i}^{n}(t)]^{T}
,

i=1,\cdots,m
.

Because
(\tilde{a}_{ij})
is nonsingular, there are constants

\kappa_{1}
and
\kappa_{2}
such that

\kappa_{1}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}\leq||\delta{x}(t)||_{\{1\}}\leq\kappa_{2}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}

where
||\delta{x}(t)||_{\{1\}}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}||\delta{x}_{i}(t)||_{1}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}||{x}_{i}(t)-s(t)||_{1}
.

Combining
||f(x)-f(y)||_{1}\leq L||x-y||_{1}
, it is easy to see that

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\delta f(x_{j}(t))|

\displaystyle\leq L\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}|\tilde{a}_{ij}|||\delta x_{j}(t)||_{1}

\displaystyle\leq L\max_{i,j}\{|\tilde{a}_{ij}|\}\sum_{j=1}^{m}||\delta x_{j}(t)||_{1}

\displaystyle=L\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{x}(t)||_{\{1\}}

\displaystyle\leq L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}

and

\displaystyle\frac{d||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{dt}=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\frac{d||\delta{y_{i}}(t)||_{1}}{dt}=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{n}\theta_{i}\frac{d|\delta{y_{i}^{k}}(t)|}{dt}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{n}\theta_{i}sign(\delta{y_{i}^{k}}(t))\frac{d\delta{y_{i}^{k}}(t)}{dt}

\displaystyle\leq L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}

\displaystyle+c\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{n}\bigg(\tilde{a}_{ii}sign(\delta{y_{i}^{k}}(t))\frac{\delta{y}_{i}^{k}(t)}{||\delta{y}_{i}(t)||_{1}}+\sum\limits_{j=1,j\neq i}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\frac{sign(\delta{y_{i}^{k}}(t))\delta{y}_{j}^{k}(t)}{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}}\bigg)

\displaystyle\leq L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}

\displaystyle+c\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{n}\bigg(\tilde{a}_{ii}\frac{|\delta{y}_{i}^{k}(t)|}{||\delta{y}_{i}(t)||_{1}}+\tilde{a}_{ij}\frac{|\delta{y}_{j}^{k}(t)|}{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}}\bigg)

\displaystyle=L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}+c\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\frac{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}}{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}}

\displaystyle=L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}+c\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\tilde{a}_{ij}

\displaystyle<L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}-cm\theta^{\star}

In case that
c>\frac{L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{m\theta^{\star}}
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{d||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{dt}

\displaystyle<L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}-cm\theta^{\star}<0.

Denote
\tilde{L}=L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}
, we have

||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}=0

when
t\geq\frac{||\delta{y}(0)||_{1}}{cm\theta^{\star}-\tilde{L}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}
.

Secondly
, we propose following continuous finite time convergence algorithm

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{dx_{1}(t)}{dt}&=f(x_{1}(t))+c\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{1j}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{1j}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))||_{1}^{\alpha}},\\
\frac{dx_{i}(t)}{dt}&=f(x_{i}(t))+c\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{ij}x_{j}(t)}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}a_{ij}x_{j}(t)||_{1}^{\alpha}},~~~~i=2,\cdots,m\end{array}\right.

where
0<\alpha<1
.

By similar derivations, we have

\displaystyle\frac{d||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{dt}

\displaystyle\leq\tilde{L}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}||\delta y_{i}(t)||_{1}+c\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\tilde{a}_{ij}||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}^{1-\alpha}

\displaystyle\leq\tilde{L}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}||\delta y_{i}(t)||_{1}-c\theta^{\star}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}^{1-\alpha}

By Jensen inequality

\displaystyle(\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1})^{1-\alpha}\leq m^{\alpha}(\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}^{1-\alpha})

we have

\displaystyle\frac{d||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{dt}

\displaystyle<\tilde{L}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{j}||\delta y_{i}(t)||_{1}-c\frac{\theta^{\star}}{m^{\alpha}}(\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\theta_{i}||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1})^{1-\alpha}

\displaystyle=\tilde{L}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}-c\frac{\theta^{\star}}{m^{\alpha}}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}^{1-\alpha}

Proposition 1
.

(Lemma 2 in
[
13
]
) Suppose the Lyapunov function
V(x)
is defined on a neighborhood
U
of the origin, and satisfies

\displaystyle\dot{V}(x)\leq-\beta V^{p}(x)+kV(x),0<p<1,

then, in case that
\beta>kV(0)^{1-p}
, the origin is finite-time stable, and the settling time

\displaystyle\bar{t}=\int_{0}^{V(0)}\frac{dV}{\mu(V)}

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{V(0)}\frac{dV}{\beta V^{p}(1-\frac{k}{\beta}V^{1-p})}

\displaystyle=\frac{\ln(1-\frac{k}{\beta}V(0)^{1-p})}{k(p-1)}.

Let
V=||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}
,
k=\tilde{L}
,
p=1-\alpha
,
\beta=c\frac{\theta^{\star}}{m^{\alpha}}
,
c>\frac{\tilde{L}m^{\alpha}}{\theta^{\star}}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}^{\alpha}
, the settling time

\displaystyle\bar{t}=\frac{\ln(1-\frac{\tilde{L}m^{\alpha}}{c\theta^{\star}}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}^{\alpha})}{-\alpha}

and
\delta y(t)=0
if
t>\tilde{t}
. Theorem is proved.

Heterogeneous dynamic networks

As an application of previous results, in this section, we discuss synchronization for heterogeneous dynamic networks.

Given
m+1
systems
\dot{x}(t)=f_{k}(x(t))
,
k=0,1,\cdots,m
, where
f_{k}(x)
,
i=0,1,\cdots,m
, are (maybe different) bounded continuous functions.

Consider following system

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{dx_{1}(t)}{dt}&=f_{1}(x_{1}(t))+c_{02}\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{1j}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{1j}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))||_{1}},\\
\frac{dx_{i}(t)}{dt}&=f_{i}(x_{i}(t))+c_{02}\frac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}x_{j}(t)}{||\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}x_{j}(t)||_{1}},~~~~i=1,2,\cdots,m\end{array}\right.

where
k=1,\cdots,m,

s(t)
is a solution satisfying
\dot{s}(t)=f_{0}(s(t))

Theorem 2
.

Suppose
||f_{0}(x_{i}(t))-f_{0}(s(t))||_{1}\leq L||x_{i}(t)-s(t)||_{1}
.
Algorithm (
4
) can synchronize all
x_{i}(t)
to
s(t)
in finite time.

Proof
Denote
y_{i}(t)=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}x_{j}(t)
,
\delta{y}_{i}(t)=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}(x_{j}(t)-s(t))
.
Algorithm (
4
) can be rewritten as

\displaystyle\dot{\delta}y_{i}(t)=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}[f_{j}(x_{j}(t))-f_{0}(s(t))]+c_{02}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\frac{\delta{y}_{j}(t)}{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}[f_{0}(x_{j}(t))-f_{0}(s(t))]+c_{02}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}\frac{\delta{y}_{j}(t)}{||\delta{y}_{j}(t)||_{1}}+u_{i}(t)

(18)

where
u_{i}(t)=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\tilde{a}_{ij}[f_{j}(x_{j}(t))-f_{0}(x_{j}(t))]
and there is a constant
\tilde{c}>0
such that
||u_{i}(t)||_{1}\leq\tilde{c}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
.

Similar to the proof of Theorem 1, define

\displaystyle||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}||\delta{y}_{i}(t)||_{1}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\theta_{i}\sum_{k=1}^{n}|\delta{y}_{i}^{k}(t)|

we have

\displaystyle\frac{d||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{dt}<L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}-c_{02}m\theta^{\star}+\tilde{c}m

In case that
c_{02}>\frac{L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}+\tilde{c}m}{m\theta^{\star}}
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{d||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{dt}

\displaystyle<L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}-c_{02}m\theta^{\star}+\tilde{c}m<0

Therefore,
||\delta{y}(t)||_{\{\theta,1\}}=0
, when
t>\frac{||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}}{L\kappa_{2}\max_{i}\{|\tilde{a}_{ii}|\}||\delta{y}(0)||_{\{\theta,1\}}-c_{02}m\theta^{\star}+\tilde{c}m}
.

5
Conclusions

In this paper, we propose new distributed pinning synchronization models of complex networks with a single nonlinear pinning controller. One is discontinuous algorithm. The other one is continuous algorithm. All two algorithms can synchronize all states of individual nodes to a specified trajectory in finite time. The result can also apply to heterogeneous dynamic networks. The coupling matrix is asymmetric (or the connecting graph is directed). The method is innovative. A coordinate transform is introduced. Different from Lyapunov function with
L_{2}
norm, here,
L_{1}
norm with weights and without weights are introduced, which is proved to be very effective.
