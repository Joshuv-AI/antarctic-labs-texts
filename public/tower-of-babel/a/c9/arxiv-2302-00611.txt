Abstract
In this note, we prove the Morse index theorem for a geodesic connecting
two submanifolds in a
C^{7}
manifold with a
C^{6}
(conic) pseudo-Finsler metric
provided that the fundamental tensor is positive definite along velocity curve of the geodesic.

1
Introduction, preliminaries and statements of results

1.1. Introduction
.
Ioan Radu Peter proved the Morse index theorem where the ends are submanifolds in Finsler geometry in
[
25
]
.
In this paper we generalize it to (conic) pseudo-Finsler
manifolds with lower smoothness.
Instead of the Cartan connection used in
[
25
]

we employ the Chern connection to introduce the Morse index form,
P
-Jacobi field and the normal second fundamental form
(or shape operator).
In addition, our proof method is different from that of
[
25
]
. For example,
we do not use the (conic) pseudo-Finsler version (Proposition
3.2
) of the so-called index lemma
[
25
, Lemma 4.2]

in Finsler geometry. The Morse index theorem proved in

[
9
, §31]
by R.A. Hermann plays a key role in our proof.

V,W\in W^{1,2}_{P\times Q}(\gamma^{\ast}TM)
it holds that

\displaystyle{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(V,W)={\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q}(\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(V),\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(W))+{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(V),\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(W)).

(2.24)

If
V\in{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q})
, then
V=\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(V)
and
\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(V)=0
.
It follows that
V\in{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q})
and hence
{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q})\subset{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q})
.
Conversely, suppose
V\in{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q})
. (
2.24
) leads to

\displaystyle{\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q}(\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(V),\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(W))+{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(V),\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(W))=0,\quad\forall W\in W^{1,2}_{P\times Q}(\gamma^{\ast}TM).

(2.25)

Taking
W\in W^{1,2}_{P\times Q}(\gamma^{\ast}TM)^{\bot}
so that
W=\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(W)
and
\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(W)=0
, we derive from
(
2.25
) that
\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(V)\in{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q})
and therefore

\displaystyle{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(V),\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(W))=0,\quad\forall W\in W^{1,2}_{P\times Q}(\gamma^{\ast}TM).

(2.26)

Choosing
W=V
we get

0={\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(V),\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(V))=\int^{\tau}_{0}\left(\frac{d}{dt}\frac{g_{\dot{\gamma}}(V,\dot{\gamma})}{g_{\dot{\gamma}}(\dot{\gamma},\dot{\gamma})}\right)^{2}g_{\dot{\gamma}}(\dot{\gamma}(t),\dot{\gamma}(t))dt=L(\dot{\gamma}(0))\int^{\tau}_{0}\left(\frac{d}{dt}\frac{g_{\dot{\gamma}}(V,\dot{\gamma})}{g_{\dot{\gamma}}(\dot{\gamma},\dot{\gamma})}\right)^{2}dt

because
g_{\dot{\gamma}(t)}(\dot{\gamma}(t),\dot{\gamma}(t))=L(\dot{\gamma}(t))\equiv L(\dot{\gamma}(0))
by
[
15
, Proposition 2.3]
.
This implies that
t\mapsto g_{\dot{\gamma}(t)}(V(t),\dot{\gamma}(t))
is constant.
Note that
g_{\dot{\gamma}(0)}(V(0),\dot{\gamma}(0))=0
since
V(0)\in T_{\gamma(0)}P
and
\dot{\gamma}(0)\in TP^{\bot}
.
We obtain that
g_{\dot{\gamma}(t)}(V(t),\dot{\gamma}(t))\equiv 0
and so
V=\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(V)\in{\rm Ker}({\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q})
.
The first equality is proved.

In order to prove the second equality, observe that

{\rm Index}({\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q})\leq{\rm Index}({\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q})
by
the definition of Morse indexes. Moreover, we have known that both are finite.
Let
X_{i}
,
i=1,\cdots,m={\rm Index}({\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q})
, be linearly independent vectors in

W^{1,2}_{P\times Q}(\gamma^{\ast}TM)
such that

{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}
is negative definite on the subspace generated by
X_{1},\cdots,X_{m}
.
Then for any
(c_{1},\cdots,c_{m})\in\mathbb{R}^{m}\setminus\{0\}
and
Y=\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}X_{i}
it holds that

\displaystyle 0>{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(Y,Y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(Y),\textsf{tan}_{\gamma}(Y))+{\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q}(\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(Y),\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(Y))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q}\left(\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{i}),\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{i})\right)+L(\dot{\gamma}(0))\int^{\tau}_{0}\left(\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}\frac{d}{dt}\frac{g_{\dot{\gamma}}(X_{i},\dot{\gamma})}{g_{\dot{\gamma}}(\dot{\gamma},\dot{\gamma})}\right)^{2}dt

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle{\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q}\left(\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{i}),\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{i})\right).

This shows that
\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{i}\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{i})\neq 0
and therefore that

\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{1}),\cdots,\textsf{nor}_{\gamma}(X_{m})
are also linearly independent.
It follows that
{\rm Index}({\bf I}^{\gamma}_{P,Q})\leq{\rm Index}({\bf I}^{\gamma,\bot}_{P,Q})
.
∎
