Abstract
Let G G be a locally compact group and A A be a commutative semisimple Banach algebra over the scalar field ℂ \mathbb{C} . The correlation between different types of BSE - Banach algebras A A , and the Banach algebras L 1 ​ ( G , A ) L^{1}(G,A) are assessed. It is found and approved that M ⁡ ( G , A ) = L 1 ​ ( G , A ) M(G,A)=L^{1}(G,A) if and only if G G is discrete. Furthermore, some properties of vector-valued measure algebras on groups are given, so that M ⁡ ( G , A ) M(G,A) is a convolution measure algebra. Keywords: Banach algebra, BSE - algebra, BED - algebra, C ∗ C^{*} - algebra, measure algebra.
