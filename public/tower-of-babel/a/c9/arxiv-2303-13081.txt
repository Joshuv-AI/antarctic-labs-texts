Physical space approach to wave equation bilinear estimates revisit
Sheng Wang, Yi Zhou

Abstract
In the paper by Klainerman, Rodnianski and Tao [ 6 ] , they give a physical space proof to a classical result of Klainerman and Machedon [ 3 ] for the bilinear space-time estimates of null forms. In this paper, we shall give an alternative and very simple physical space proof of the same bilinear estimates by applying div-curl type lemma of Zhou [ 13 ] and Wang and Zhou [ 11 ] , [ 12 ] . As far as we known, the later development of wave maps [ 1 ] , [ 2 ] , [ 7 ] , [ 8 ] , [ 9 ] , [ 10 ] , and the proof of bounded curvature theorem [ 4 ] , [ 5 ] rely on basic ideas of Klainerman and Machedon [ 3 ] as well as Klainerman, Rodnianski and Tao [ 6 ] .

In a classical paper by Klainerman and Machedon

\displaystyle\|Q\left(\phi,\psi\right)\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{+}\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}

(1.1)

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} &Q_{0}\left(\phi,\psi\right)=\partial_{t}\phi\partial_{t}\psi-\sum\limits_{i}^{n}\partial_{x_{i}}\phi\partial_{x_{i}}\psi,\\
&Q_{\alpha\beta}\left(\phi,\psi\right)=\partial_{\alpha}\phi\partial_{\beta}\psi-\partial_{\alpha}\psi\partial_{\beta}\phi,\\

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\Box\phi\left(t,x\right)=0,\ t>0,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\\

\end{aligned}\right.

(1.3)

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\Box\psi\left(t,x\right)=0,\ t>0,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\\

2
Div-Curl Lemma

Lemma 2.1
.

Suppose that

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\partial_{t}f^{11}+\partial_{x}f^{12}=G^{1},\\
&\partial_{t}f^{21}-\partial_{x}f^{22}=G^{2},\end{aligned}\right.

(2.1)

\displaystyle f^{11},f^{12},f^{21},f^{22}\rightarrow 0,x\rightarrow\infty,

(2.2)

where
\left(t,x\right)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R}^{1}
and each
f^{i,j}\left(i,j=1,2\right)
is a real-value function of
\left(t,x\right)
.

Then we have:

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f^{11}f^{22}+f^{12}f^{21}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\|f^{11}\left(0\right)\|_{L^{1}}+\sup\limits_{0\leq t\leq T}\|f^{11}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\lvert G^{1}\rvert\right)

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\|f^{21}\left(0\right)\|_{L^{1}}+\sup\limits_{0\leq t\leq T}\|f^{21}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\lvert G^{2}\rvert\right)

provided that the right side is bounded.

Proof..

The details of the proof can be referred to in our previous work
[
12
]
, and are repeated here for the convenience of the reader.

\partial_{t}\left(\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}\right)+f^{12}=\int_{-\infty}^{x}G^{1}.

Then

f^{21}\int_{-\infty}^{x}\partial_{t}f^{11}+f^{12}f^{21}=f^{21}\int_{-\infty}^{x}G^{1},

(2.3)

\partial_{t}f^{21}\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}-\partial_{x}f^{22}\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}=G^{2}\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}.

(2.4)

(
2.3
)+(
2.4
):

\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\partial_{t}\left(\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}f^{21}\right)+

\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\left(f^{12}f^{21}-\partial_{x}f^{22}\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}\right)

\displaystyle=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\left(f^{21}\int_{-\infty}^{x}G^{1}+G^{2}\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}\right).

We have:

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f^{11}f^{22}+f^{12}f^{21}=

\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\left(\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}f^{21}\right)\left(0\right)-\left(\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}f^{21}\right)\left(T\right)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\left(f^{21}\int_{-\infty}^{x}G^{1}+G^{2}\int_{-\infty}^{x}f^{11}\right)

\displaystyle:=\mathcal{A}_{1}+\mathcal{A}_{2}+\mathcal{A}_{3},

(2.5)

where

\displaystyle\lvert\mathcal{A}_{1}\rvert

\displaystyle\lesssim\|f^{11}\left(0\right)\|_{L^{1}}\|f^{21}\left(0\right)\|_{L^{1}}+\|f^{11}\left(T\right)\|_{L^{1}}\|f^{21}\left(T\right)\|_{L^{1}},

\displaystyle\lvert\mathcal{A}_{2}\rvert

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{T}\|f^{21}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}\|G^{1}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sup\limits_{0\leq t\leq T}\|f^{21}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}\left(\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\lvert G^{1}\rvert\right),

\displaystyle\lvert\mathcal{A}_{3}\rvert

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{T}\|f^{11}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}\|G^{2}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sup\limits_{0\leq t\leq T}\|f^{11}\left(t\right)\|_{L^{1}}\left(\int_{0}^{T}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\lvert G^{2}\rvert\right).

Based analysis above, we complete the proof.
∎

Application of div-curl lemma

Integrate in

(3.3)

Let

(3.4)

we rewrite (

\partial^{2}_{t}v-\partial^{2}_{x_{1}}v+\Delta_{y}v=2\Delta_{y}v.

(3.5)

Multiply (

.

For solution

and

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{16}\left(D^{+}(u)+D^{-}(u)\right)\left(D^{+}(v)+D^{-}(v)\right)+\frac{1}{8}\left(D^{+}(u)+D^{-}(u)\right)E_{2}(v)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{4}E_{2}(u)E_{2}(v)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{8}\left(D^{+}(u)+D^{-}(u)\right)E_{2}(v)+\frac{1}{8}\left(D^{+}(v)+D^{-}(v)\right)E_{2}(u)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{4}E_{2}(u)E_{2}(v)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{8}D^{+}(u)D^{-}(v)+\frac{1}{8}D^{-}(v)D^{+}(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}E_{1}(u)E_{2}(v)+\frac{1}{4}E_{1}(v)E_{2}(u)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{8}D^{+}(u)D^{-}(v)+\frac{1}{8}D^{-}(u)D^{+}(v)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{16}\left(D^{+}(u)+D^{-}(u)\right)E_{2}(v)+\frac{1}{16}\left(D^{+}(v)+D^{-}(v)\right)E_{2}(u)

Combining above and noting (
1.8

We note that
Q_{i,j}(\phi_{\mu},\psi_{\lambda})=\partial_{i}(\phi_{\mu}\partial_{j}\psi_{\lambda})-\partial_{j}(\phi_{\mu}\partial_{i}\psi_{\lambda})
. Then by Bernstein inequality and Hölder inequality, if
\mu\sim\lambda
, we have:

\displaystyle\|\left(-\Delta\right)^{-1/2}Q_{i,j}\left(\phi_{\mu},\psi_{\lambda}\right)\|_{L^{2}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}\lesssim\lambda\|\phi_{\mu}\|_{L^{4}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}\|\psi_{\lambda}\|_{L^{4}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}.

By the following Strichartz inequality,

\|\phi_{\mu}\|_{L^{4}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}\lesssim\mu^{\frac{n-3}{4}-\frac{1}{2}}E^{1/2}\left(\phi_{\mu}\right),

(4.14)

\|\psi_{\lambda}\|_{L^{4}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}\lesssim\lambda^{\frac{n-3}{4}-\frac{1}{2}}E^{1/2}\left(\psi_{\lambda}\right),

(4.15)

we can obtain:

\displaystyle\|\left(-\Delta\right)^{-1/2}Q_{i,j}\left(\phi_{\mu},\psi_{\lambda}\right)\|_{L^{2}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda\|\phi_{\mu}\|_{L^{4}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}\|\psi_{\lambda}\|_{L^{4}([0,1]\times\mathbb{R}^{n})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\mu^{\frac{n-3}{2}}E^{1/2}\left(\phi_{\mu}\right)E^{1/2}\left(\psi_{\lambda}\right)

(4.16)

for
\lambda\sim\mu
.
Thus, the proof is done.
∎
