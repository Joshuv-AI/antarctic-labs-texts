7.
A Generalized Bessel-Type Operator Example

As an illustration of these results, we consider the following explicitly solvable generalized Bessel-type equation following the analysis in
[
37
]
(see also
[
30
]
).
Let
a=0
,
b\in(0,\infty)\cup\{\infty\}
, and consider

\begin{split}p(x)=x^{\nu},\quad r(x)=x^{\delta},\quad q(x)=\frac{(2+\delta-\nu)^{2}\gamma^{2}-(1-\nu)^{2}}{4}x^{\nu-2},\\
\delta>-1,\ \nu<1,\ \gamma\geqslant 0,\ x\in(0,b).\end{split}

(7.1)

Then

\displaystyle\begin{split}\tau_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}=x^{-\delta}\left[-\frac{d}{dx}x^{\nu}\frac{d}{dx}+\frac{(2+\delta-\nu)^{2}\gamma^{2}-(1-\nu)^{2}}{4}x^{\nu-2}\right],\\
\delta>-1,\;\nu<1,\;\gamma\geqslant 0,\;x\in(0,b),\end{split}

(7.2)

is singular at the endpoint
x=0
(since the potential,
q
is not integrable near
x=0
), regular at
x=b
when
b\in(0,\infty)
, and in the limit point case at
x=b
when
b=\infty
. Furthermore,
\tau_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}
is in the limit circle case at
x=0
if
0\leqslant\gamma<1
and in the limit point case at
x=0
when
\gamma\geqslant 1
.

Solutions to
\tau_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}u=zu
are given by (cf.
[
49
]
,
[
50
, No. 2.162, p. 440]
)

\displaystyle y_{1,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,x)

\displaystyle=x^{(1-\nu)/2}J_{\gamma}\big(2z^{1/2}x^{(2+\delta-\nu)/2}/(2+\delta-\nu)\big),\quad\gamma\geqslant 0,

(7.3)

\displaystyle y_{2,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,x)

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}x^{(1-\nu)/2}J_{-\gamma}\big(2z^{1/2}x^{(2+\delta-\nu)/2}/(2+\delta-\nu)\big),&\gamma\notin{\mathbb{N}}_{0},\\
x^{(1-\nu)/2}Y_{\gamma}\big(2z^{1/2}x^{(2+\delta-\nu)/2}/(2+\delta-\nu)\big),&\gamma\in{\mathbb{N}}_{0},\end{cases}\ \gamma\geqslant 0,

(7.4)

where
J_{\mu}(\,\cdot\,),Y_{\mu}(\,\cdot\,)
are the standard Bessel functions of order
\mu\in{\mathbb{R}}

(cf.
[
1
, Ch. 9]
).

In the following we assume that

\gamma\in[0,1)

(7.5)

to ensure the limit circle case at
x=0
. In this case it suffices to focus on the generalized boundary values at the singular endpoint
x=0
following
[
34
]
. For this purpose we introduce principal and nonprincipal solutions
u_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(0,\,\cdot\,)
and
\widehat{u}_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(0,\,\cdot\,)
of
\tau_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}u=0
at
x=0
by

\displaystyle\begin{split}u_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(0,x)&=(1-\nu)^{-1}x^{[1-\nu+(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]/2},\quad\gamma\in[0,1),\\
\widehat{u}_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(0,x)&=\begin{cases}(1-\nu)[(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]^{-1}x^{[1-\nu-(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]/2},&\gamma\in(0,1),\\
(1-\nu)x^{(1-\nu)/2}\text{\rm ln}(1/x),&\gamma=0,\end{cases}\\
&\hskip 128.0374pt\delta>-1,\;\nu<1,\;x\in(0,1).\end{split}

(7.6)

Remark 7.1
.

Since the singularity of
q
at
x=0
renders
\tau_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}
singular at
x=0
(unless, of
course,
\gamma=(1-\nu)/(2+\delta-\nu)
, in which case
\tau_{\delta,\nu,(1-\nu)/(2+\delta-\nu)}
is regular at
x=0
), there is a certain freedom in the choice of the multiplicative constant in the principal solution
u_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}
of
\tau_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}u=0
at
x=0
. Our choice of
(1-\nu)^{-1}
in (
7.6
) reflects continuity in the parameters when comparing to boundary conditions in the regular case (cf.
[
34
, Remark 3.12
(ii)
]
), that is, in the case
\delta>-1
,
\nu<1
, and

\gamma=(1-\nu)/(2+\delta-\nu)
treated in
[
29
]
.

\diamond

The generalized boundary values for
g\in\dom(T_{max,\delta,\nu,\gamma})
are then of the form

\displaystyle\begin{split}\widetilde{g}(0)&=-W(u_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(0,\,\cdot\,),g)(0)\\
&=\begin{cases}\lim_{x\downarrow 0}g(x)\big/\big[(1-\nu)[(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]^{-1}x^{[1-\nu-(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]/2}\big],&\gamma\in(0,1),\\[2.84526pt]
\lim_{x\downarrow 0}g(x)\big/\big[(1-\nu)x^{(1-\nu)/2}\text{\rm ln}(1/x)\big],&\gamma=0,\end{cases}\end{split}

(7.7)

\displaystyle\begin{split}\widetilde{g}^{\,\prime}(0)&=W(\widehat{u}_{0,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(0,\,\cdot\,),g)(0)\\
&=\begin{cases}\lim_{x\downarrow 0}\big[g(x)-\widetilde{g}(0)(1-\nu)[(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]^{-1}x^{[1-\nu-(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]/2}\big]\\
\qquad\qquad\big/\big[(1-\nu)^{-1}x^{[1-\nu+(2+\delta-\nu)\gamma]/2}\big],&\hskip-5.69046pt\gamma\in(0,1),\\[2.84526pt]
\lim_{x\downarrow 0}\big[g(x)-\widetilde{g}(0)(1-\nu)x^{(1-\nu)/2}\text{\rm ln}(1/x)\big]\\
\qquad\qquad\big/\big[(1-\nu)^{-1}x^{(1-\nu)/2}\big],&\hskip-5.69046pt\gamma=0.\end{cases}\end{split}

(7.8)

Next, introducing the standard normalized (at
x=0
) fundamental system of solutions

\phi_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,\,\cdot\,,0),\theta_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,\,\cdot\,,0)
of

,
z\in{\mathbb{C}}
, that is real-valued for
z\in{\mathbb{R}}
and entire with respect to
z\in{\mathbb{C}}
by

\displaystyle\begin{split}\widetilde{\phi}_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,0,0)&=0,\quad\widetilde{\phi}_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}^{\,\prime}(z,0,0)=1,\\
\widetilde{\theta}_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,0,0)&=1,\quad\,\widetilde{\theta}_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}^{\,\prime}(z,0,0)=0,\quad z\in{\mathbb{C}},\end{split}

(7.9)

one obtains explicitly,

\displaystyle\phi_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,x,0)=(1-\nu)^{-1}(2+\delta-\nu)^{\gamma}\Gamma(1+\gamma)z^{-\gamma/2}y_{1,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,x),

\displaystyle\hskip 89.626pt\delta>-1,\;\nu<1,\;\gamma\in[0,1),\;z\in{\mathbb{C}},\;x\in(0,b),

(7.10)

\displaystyle\theta_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,x,0)=\begin{cases}(1-\nu)(2+\delta-\nu)^{-\gamma-1}\gamma^{-1}\Gamma(1-\gamma)z^{\gamma/2}y_{2,\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,x),&\hskip-2.84544pt\gamma\in(0,1),\\[2.84526pt]
(1-\nu)(2+\delta-\nu)^{-1}[-\pi y_{2,\delta,\nu,0}(z,x)\\
\quad+(\text{\rm ln}(z)-2\text{\rm ln}(2+\delta-\nu)+2\gamma_{E})y_{1,\delta,\nu,0}(z,x)],&\hskip-2.84544pt\gamma=0,\end{cases}

\displaystyle\hskip 176.407pt\delta>-1,\;\nu<1,\;z\in{\mathbb{C}},\;x\in(0,b),

(7.11)

\displaystyle W(\theta_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,\,\cdot\,,0),\phi_{\delta,\nu,\gamma}(z,\,\cdot\,,0))=1,\quad z\in{\mathbb{C}},

(7.12)

where
