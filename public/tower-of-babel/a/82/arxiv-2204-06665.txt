Abstract
. We develop a new method for addressing certain weakly null systems of wave equations. This approach does not rely on Lorentz invariance nor on the use of null foliations, both of which restrict applications to, e.g., multiple speed systems. The proof uses a class of space-time Klainerman-Sobolev estimates of the first author, Tataru, and Tohaneanu, which pair nicely with local energy estimates that combine the r p r^{p} -weighted method of Dafermos and Rodnianski with the ghost weight method of Alinhac. We further refine the standard local energy estimate with a modification of the ∂ t − ∂ r \partial_{t}-\partial_{r} portion of the multiplier.



1.
Introduction

This article represents a proof of concept for a method of addressing
certain systems of weakly null wave equations that do not satisfy the classical
null condition. This example falls into the class of equations
studied in
[
5
]
. For
simplicity of exposition, we only consider a semilinear system. Unlike

[
5
]
, the methods here do not use the Lorentz
boosts, which is important for similar problems in the setting of
multiple speeds, exterior domains, or stationary asymptotically flat
background geometry. And when compared to the methods of

[
10
]
, which apply to a broader class of weakly null equations,
we believe that our methods are simpler and, as we do not rely on null
foliations, additional applications to multiple speeds systems appear
possible. The current work is most akin to that of

[
6
]
, which is based on the ideas of
[
13
]
and
proves global existence without the use of the Lorentz boosts, but we
believe our method to have added flexibility for other applications.

In three spatial dimensions, it is known that solutions to semilinear
systems of equations of the
form
\Box u=Q(\partial u)
with nonlinearity that vanishes to
second order at the origin can only be guaranteed to exist almost
globally, which means that the lifespan grows exponentially as the
size of the data shrinks. See, e.g.,
[
7
]
for the lower bound
on the lifespan and
[
8
]

and
[
21
]
for counterexamples to global
existence. Based on the fact that the components of the space-time
gradient
\partial u=(\partial_{t}u,\nabla_{x}u)
that are tangent to
the light cone decay faster, the null condition was identified in

[
2
]
and
[
11
]
as a sufficient condition for guaranteeing
small data global existence. This condition requires that at least
one factor of each nonlinear term (at the quadratic level) to be one of
the “good” directions. Einstein’s equations, for example, do not
satisfy this classical null condition, which led to the introduction
of the weak null condition in
[
14
,
15
]
as a possible sufficient
condition for small data global existence. Further evidence
supporting this is given in
[
10
]
.

Here we shall consider a coupled system of equations. One of the
equations satisfies the classical null condition, but the other does
not. The intuition is that the equation satisfying the null
condition has a solution that decays faster, and when that is
plugged into the second equation, this additional decay allows for
an argument to be closed.

We specifically will consider

(1.1)

\begin{cases}\Box u=\partial_{t}u\partial_{t}v-\nabla u\cdot\nabla v,\\
\Box v=\partial_{t}v\partial_{t}u,\\
(u(0,{\,\cdot\,}),\partial_{t}u(0,{\,\cdot\,}))=(u_{(0)},u_{(1)}),\\
(v(0,{\,\cdot\,}),\partial_{t}v(0,{\,\cdot\,}))=(v_{(0)},v_{(1)}).\end{cases}

For simplicity of exposition, we shall take the initial data to be
compactly supported, say within
\{|x|\leq 2\}
.

In order to describe the “good” directions, we shall frequently
orthogonally decompose the (spatial) gradient into radial and angular portions:

\nabla=\frac{x}{r}\partial_{r}+{\not\negmedspace\nabla}.

The directions that are tangent to the light cone are

{\not\negmedspace\partial}=(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r},{\not\negmedspace\nabla}).

By noting that

\partial_{t}u\partial_{t}v-\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u\partial_{t}v-\partial_{r}u(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})v-{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\cdot{\not\negmedspace\nabla}v,

we see that the equation for
u
satisfies the null condition. The
equation for
v
, however, does not. Nevertheless, we shall prove
that solutions to (
1.1
) with sufficiently small
initial data exist globally.

Our main theorem is the following statement of global existence.

Theorem 1.1
.

Suppose that
u_{(j)},v_{(j)}\in C^{\infty}_{c}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
. Then there is a

N\in\mathbb{N}
sufficiently large and
\varepsilon_{0}>0

sufficiently small so that if

(1.2)

\sum_{|\alpha|\leq N+1}\|\partial_{x}^{\alpha}u_{(0)}\|_{L^{2}}+\sum_{|\alpha|\leq N+1}\|\partial_{x}^{\alpha}v_{(0)}\|_{L^{2}}+\sum_{|\alpha|\leq N}\|\partial_{x}^{\alpha}u_{(1)}\|_{L^{2}}+\sum_{|\alpha|\leq N}\|\partial_{x}^{\alpha}v_{(1)}\|_{L^{2}}\leq\varepsilon

with
\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon_{0}
, then (
1.1
) has a
unique global solution
(u,v)\in(C^{\infty}([0,\infty)\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3}))^{2}
.

The methods that we employ are partly inspired by
[
9
]
where
almost global existence was established for equations without a null
condition by pairing a local energy estimate with a weighted Sobolev
estimate that provides decay in
|x|
rather the
t
.
The latter does not require the use of any time dependent vector
fields, which was instrumental in adapting the method of invariant
vector fields to, e.g., exterior domains. The paper
[
3
]

developed the
r^{p}
-weighted local energy estimate. In this variant
of the local energy estimate, the additional decay for the “good”
derivatives manifests itself as much improved weights. In

[
4
]
, an analog of
[
9
]
was established using these

r^{p}
-weighted estimates in order to show global existence for wave
equations with the null condition.

In
[
17
]
, the
r^{p}
-weighted multiplier of
[
3
]
was
combined with a “ghost weight” as in
[
1
]
.
The resulting estimate allowed for additional improvements on the
weight of
(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u
near the light cone. This was
then combined with the space-time Klainerman-Sobolev estimates of

[
20
]
in order to establish long-time existence for systems of
wave equations where the nonlinearity is allowed to depend on the
solution not just its derivative. We rely strongly upon these ideas.
A further modification of the
(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})
component
of the multiplier for typical local energy estimates is introduced here.
This modification, in particular, while requiring a faster decaying
weight also provides a more rapidly decaying weight on the forcing
term.

1.1.
Notation

Here we fix some notation that will be used throughout the paper. We
let

\Omega=x\times\nabla,\quad S=t\partial_{t}+r\partial_{r},\quad Z=(\partial_{t},\nabla,\Omega,S)

denote the admissible vector fields. We will use the shorthand

|Z^{\leq N}u|=\sum_{|\mu|\leq N}|Z^{\mu}u|,\quad|\partial^{\leq N}u|=\sum_{|\mu|\leq N}|\partial^{\mu}u|.

A key property of the vector fields
Z
is that they all preserve
solutions to the homogeneous wave equation since

[\Box,\partial]=[\Box,\Omega]=0,\quad[\Box,S]=2\Box.

It will also be important to notice that

(1.3)

[Z,\partial]\in\text{span}(\partial),\quad|[Z,{\not\negmedspace\partial}]u|\leq\frac{|Zu|}{r}+|{\not\negmedspace\partial}u|.

In the proof of local energy estimates, we will frequently use that

(1.4)

[\nabla,\partial_{r}]=[{\not\negmedspace\nabla},\partial_{r}]=\frac{1}{r}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}.

We, moveover, note that

(1.5)

{\not\negmedspace\nabla}=-\frac{x}{r^{2}}\times\Omega,\quad|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u|\leq\frac{1}{r}|Zu|.

We will often decompose
{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
into (inhomogeneous) dyadic
regions. To that end, let

A_{R}=\{R\leq\langle x\rangle\leq 2R\},\quad\tilde{A}_{R}=\Bigl\{\frac{7}{8}R\leq\langle x\rangle\leq\frac{17}{8}R\Bigr\}.

Similarly, we set

X_{U}=\{(t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3}\,:\,U\leq\langle t-r\rangle\leq 2U\},

with
\tilde{X}_{U}
denoting a similar enlargement.

We shall use a finer refinement, as in
[
20
]
, when necessary.
Because of our assumption that the initial data are supported in

\{|x|\leq 2\}
and because of the finite speed of propagation, it will
suffice to examine
C=\{r\leq t+2\}
. We then consider a dyadic strip

C_{\tau}=\{(t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3}\,:\,\tau\leq t\leq 2\tau,\>r\leq t+2\}.

Away from the light cone
t=|x|
, we further decompose into dyadic
regions in the
r
variable:

C^{R=1}_{\tau}=C_{\tau}\cap\{r\leq 2\},\quad C^{R}_{\tau}=C_{\tau}\cap\{R\leq r\leq 2R\}\text{ when }1<R\leq\tau/4.

We additionally set

\tilde{C}^{R=1}_{\tau}=C\cap\Bigl\{\frac{7}{8}\tau\leq t\leq\frac{17}{8}\tau,r\leq\frac{17}{8}\Bigr\},\quad\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau}=C\cap\Bigl\{\frac{7}{8}\tau\leq t\leq\frac{17}{8}\tau,\frac{7}{8}R\leq r\leq\frac{17}{8}R\Bigr\}\text{ when }1<R\leq\tau/4

to denote slight enlargements, which will accommodate the tails of the
cutoff functions that are used to localize in the sequel. The key
property is that

\langle r\rangle\approx R,\quad t-r\approx\tau\quad\text{ on }C^{R}_{\tau},\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau}\quad\text{ with }\tau\geq 4\text{ and }1\leq R\leq\tau/4.

In the vicinity of the light cone, we instead dyadically decompose in

t-|x|
. To this end, let

C^{U=1}_{\tau}=C_{\tau}\cap\{|t-r|\leq 2\},\quad C^{U}_{\tau}=C_{\tau}\cap\{U\leq t-r\leq 2U\}\text{ when }1<R\leq\tau/4.

As above, we denote a slight enlargement on both scales by

\tilde{C}^{U=1}_{\tau}=C\cap\Bigl\{\frac{7}{8}\tau\leq t\leq\frac{17}{8}\tau,|t-r|\leq\frac{17}{8}\Bigr\},

and

\tilde{C}^{U}_{\tau}=C\cap\Bigl\{\frac{7}{8}\tau\leq t\leq\frac{17}{8}\tau,\frac{7}{8}U\leq t-r\leq\frac{17}{8}U\Bigr\}\text{ when }1<U\leq\tau/4.

These choices give

r\approx\tau,\quad\langle t-r\rangle\approx U\quad\text{ on }C^{U}_{\tau},\tilde{C}^{U}_{\tau}\quad\text{ with }\tau\geq 4\text{ and }1\leq U\leq\tau/4.

With these notations in place, we have

C_{\tau}=\Bigl(\bigcup_{1\leq R\leq\tau/4}C^{R}_{\tau}\Bigr)\cup\Bigl(\bigcup_{1\leq U\leq\tau/4}C^{U}_{\tau}\Bigr)\cup C^{\tau/2}_{\tau}

where

C^{\tau/2}_{\tau}=C_{\tau}\cap\{t-r\geq\tau/2\}\cap\{r\geq\tau/2\}.

On
C^{\tau/2}_{\tau}
, we have
r\approx\tau
and
t-r\approx\tau
.
We may regard this region as either a
C^{R}_{\tau}
or a
C^{U}_{\tau}
region.

The decomposition of
C_{\tau}
into
C^{R}_{\tau}
and
C^{U}_{\tau}
regions

On occasion, we shall use
{\tilde{\tilde{C}}}^{R}_{\tau},{\tilde{\tilde{C}}}^{U}_{\tau}
to denote
an enlargement of
{\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau},{\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau}
respectively. In the sequel, it will be understood that
\tau,R,U
always run
over dyadic values.

In order to localize to such regions, we fix the following notation
for cutoff functions. Let
\chi
be a smooth, nonnegative function
so that
\chi(z)\equiv 1
for
z\geq 1
and
\chi(z)\equiv 0
for
z\leq 7/8
. We also set

\beta(z)=\chi(z)-\chi\Bigl(z-\frac{9}{8}\Bigr)

so that

\beta(z)\equiv 1
for
1\leq z\leq 2
and
\beta(z)\equiv 0
when

z\not\in[7/8,17/8]
.

We will frequently use the mixed norm notation

\|u\|^{p}_{L^{p}L^{q}}=\int_{0}^{\infty}\|u(t,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{q}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})}^{p}\,dt,

with the obvious alteration when
p=\infty
. Unless specified, the
domain of all mixed norms of this type is
{\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
. We also fix the
following local energy norms, which will be discussed more in the next section:

\|u\|_{LE}=\sup_{R\geq 1}R^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times A_{R})},\quad\|u\|_{LE^{1}}=\|(\partial u,u/r)\|_{LE}.

2.
Local Energy Estimates

The integrated local energy estimate

(2.1)

\|u\|_{LE^{1}}^{2}+\|\partial u\|^{2}_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\lesssim\|\partial u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\int_{0}^{\infty}\int|\Box u|\Bigl(|\partial u|+\frac{|u|}{r}\Bigr)\,dx\,dt

is frequently proved by pairing the equation
\Box u
with a
multiplier of the form
\partial_{t}+f(r)\partial_{r}+\frac{f(r)}{r}u
, integrating over a space-time slab, and integrating by parts.
The function
f(r)
needs to be
C^{2}
, bounded, non-negative,
increasing, and satisfy

-\Delta(f(r)/r)\geq 0
, which the function
f(r)=r/(r+R)

appropriately satisfies. See
[
22
]
,
[
18
,
19
]
.
We may rewrite this multiplier as

(2.2)

\partial_{t}+\frac{r}{r+R}\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r+R}=\frac{R/2}{r+R}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)+\frac{r+(R/2)}{r+R}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr),

which has the property that the coefficient of

\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}
is nonnegative and decreasing in

r
,
while the coefficient of
\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}
is
nonnegative and increasing. While there are other requirements, this
is the key
observation to allow for generalizations of the multiplier. In
particular, we shall later consider

(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)+(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr).

Here
\sigma_{U}(z)=z/(U+|z|)
,
\delta>0
, and
0<p<2
.

Multipliers of the form
r^{p}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)
appeared previously in
[
3
]

and
e^{-\sigma(t-r)}\partial_{t}
in
[
1
]
. The
combination of the two as reflected above is originally from

[
17
]
. The change in multiplier on
\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}

provides an additional degree of decay on the forcing term that helps to close the
nonlinear arguments in the sequel.

We first record a corollary of (
2.1
).

Proposition 2.1
.

Suppose
u\in C^{2}({\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and for all
t\in{\mathbb{R}}_{+}
,

|\partial^{\leq 1}u(t,x)|\to 0
as
|x|\to\infty
. Then

(2.3)

\|u\|_{LE^{1}}+\|\partial u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\lesssim\|\partial u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\int_{0}^{\infty}\|\Box u(t,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\,dt.

The proposition follows immediately from (
2.1
) upon applying the
Schwarz inequality to see that

\int_{0}^{\infty}\int|\Box u|\Bigl(|\partial u|+\frac{|u|}{r}\Bigr)\,dx\,dt\leq\Bigl(\|\partial u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|r^{-1}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\Bigr)\int_{0}^{\infty}\|\Box u(t,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\,dt.

A Hardy inequality gives

\|r^{-1}u\|_{L^{2}}\lesssim\|\partial_{r}u\|_{L^{2}},

which permits the first factor above to be bootstrapped.

We will now discuss the mixed
r^{p}
-weighted and ghost weighted
estimates of
[
17
]
, where the former is motivated by

[
3
]
and the latter by

[
1
]
. To begin, we look at a variant of the
Hardy inequality that holds in the space-time norms and yields a
“good” derivative. This, in essence, previously appeared in
[
17
]
.

Lemma 2.2
.

Fix
0<p<2
. Suppose
u\in C^{2}({\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and
for every
t\in{\mathbb{R}}_{+}
,
r^{\frac{p}{2}}|u(t,x)|\to 0
as
|x|\to\infty
. Then,

(2.4)

\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\lesssim\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}.

Proof.

We write

\int_{0}^{T}\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r^{2}}u^{2}\,dx\,dt=-\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p-1}\Bigl[(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(r^{-1})\Bigr](ru)^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt.

Here
d\omega
is the volume form on
{\mathbb{S}}^{2}
.
Integration by parts gives that this is

=-\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r}u^{2}(T,x)\,dx+\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r}u^{2}(0,x)\,dx\\
+(p-1)\int_{0}^{T}\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-2}}{r}u^{2}\,dx\,dt+2\int_{0}^{T}\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r^{2}}u(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\,dx\,dt.

Using that
\frac{1}{1+r}\leq\frac{1}{r}
in the third term and
applying the Schwarz inequality to the last term then shows that

(1-|p-1|)\int_{0}^{T}\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r^{2}}u^{2}\,dx\,dt+\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r}u^{2}(T,x)\,dx\leq\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r}u^{2}(0,x)\,dx\\
+2\Bigl(\int_{0}^{T}\int\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r^{2}}u^{2}\,dx\,dt\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Bigl(\int_{0}^{T}\int(1+r)^{p-1}\Bigl(r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\Bigr)^{2}\,dx\,dt\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Bootstrapping the first factor of the last term and taking a supremum
over
T
then yields (
2.4
).
∎

We next record what, in essence, is the main new estimate of
[
17
]
.

Proposition 2.3
.

Fix
0<p<2
. If
u\in C^{2}({\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and

r^{\frac{p+2}{2}}|\partial^{\leq 1}u(t,x)|\to 0
as
|x|\to\infty
, then

(2.5)

\|\langle r\rangle^{p}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{p}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\sup_{U\geq 1}U^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(X_{U})}\\
\lesssim\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\\
+\Bigl(\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/4}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}+\sum_{U}\Bigl(\sum_{\tau\geq 4U}U\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Proof.

Noting that

(2.6)

\Box u=r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2}-{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\cdot{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\Bigr)(ru),\quad\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u=r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru),

we consider

\int_{0}^{T}\int(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Box u\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\,dx\,dt\\
=\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2}-{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\cdot{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\,dr\,d\omega\,dt

for
0<p<2
, which, using (
1.4
), is equivalent to

\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(1+r)^{p}}{r}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt.

Subsequent integrations by parts then give

(2.7)

\int_{0}^{T}\int(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Box u\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\,dx\,dt\\
=\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl\{\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}+|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\Bigr\}\,dr\,d\omega\Bigl|_{t=0}^{T}\\
+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t)}u^{2}(t,0)\,d\omega\,dt+\frac{p}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p-1}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p}\sigma^{\prime}_{U}(t-r)e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
+\Bigl(1-\frac{p}{2}\Bigr)\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(r+1)^{p}}{r}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
+\frac{p}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(1+r)^{p-1}}{r}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt.

Rearranging the terms, noting that

\sigma_{U}^{\prime}(t-r)\gtrsim\frac{1}{\langle t-r\rangle},\quad\text{ on }X_{U},

and taking a supremum over
T
yields

\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|^{2}_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|^{2}_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}^{2}+\sup_{U}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\langle t-r\rangle^{-\frac{1}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(X_{U})}\\
\lesssim\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\int_{0}^{\infty}\int\langle r\rangle^{p}|\Box u||r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)|\,dx\,dt.

By the Schwarz inequality, we may bound

(2.8)

\int_{0}^{\infty}\int\langle r\rangle^{p}|\Box u||r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)|\,dx\,dt\lesssim\Bigl(\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/4}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\Bigl\{\sum_{U}\Bigl[\sum_{\tau\geq 4U}U\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}^{2}\Bigr]^{\frac{1}{2}}\Bigr\}\Bigl(\sup_{U}U^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(X_{U})}\Bigr).

The second factor of each term may be bootstrapped. Combining what
results with (
2.4
) completes the proof.
∎

We next combine the previous proposition with a modification of the

(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})
portion of the multiplier in (
2.2
). While the new

\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}
terms are easily controlled using the
LE^{1}

norm, the corresponding forcing term comes with an added factor of
decay,
(1+r)^{-\delta}
, when compared to the right side of (
2.1
).

Theorem 2.4
.

Fix
0<p<2
and
\delta>0
. If
u\in C^{2}({\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times{\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and

r^{\frac{p+2}{2}}|\partial^{\leq 1}u(t,x)|\to 0
as
|x|\to\infty
,
then

(2.9)

\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-\frac{1}{2}}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\sup_{U}U^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(X_{U})}\lesssim\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1-\delta}{2}}\Box u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\Bigl(\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/4}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1+p}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}+\sum_{U}\Bigl(\sum_{\tau\geq 4U}U\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Proof.

Using (
2.6
) and the related identity

\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u=r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru),

we begin by considering

\int_{0}^{T}\int(1+r)^{-\delta}\Box u\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\,dx\,dt=\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2}-{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\cdot{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\,dr\,d\omega\,dt

with
\delta>0
.
Integrating by parts and using (
1.4
), this is

=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt-\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(1+r)^{-\delta}}{r}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt.

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and subsequent integrations by
parts give

(2.10)

\int_{0}^{T}\int(1+r)^{-\delta}\Box u\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\,dx\,dt=\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl\{\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}+|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\Bigr\}\,dr\,d\omega\Bigl|_{t=0}^{T}\\
-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}u^{2}(t,0)\,d\omega\,dt+\frac{\delta}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-1-\delta}\Bigl[\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr]^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt\\
-\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl(r^{-1}+\frac{\delta}{2}(1+r)^{-1}\Bigr)|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\,dt.

We now consider a multiplier of the form

(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)+C(1+r)^{p}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t-r)}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr),\quad C\gg 1,

by adding a large multiple of (
2.7
) to (
2.10
). Since

\sigma_{U}
is bounded independently of
U
, for a sufficiently large

C
,

\frac{C}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}e^{-\sigma_{U}(t)}u^{2}(t,0)\,d\omega\,dt-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}u^{2}(t,0)\,d\omega\,dt\geq 0,

and as such, this
r=0
boundary term may be dropped. The nonnegative
contribution

\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)(T,r\omega)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega

may also be omitted, and since
\delta,p>0
, we can simplify by
bounding

\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{-\delta}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)(0,r\omega)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega\leq\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{S}}^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(1+r)^{p}|{\not\negmedspace\nabla}(ru)(0,r\omega)|^{2}\,dr\,d\omega.

What then results from this combination of (
2.10
) and
(
2.7
) is

(2.11)

\Bigl\|(1+r)^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigl\|^{2}_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\delta}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\sup_{U}U^{-1}\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(X_{U})}\\
\lesssim\Bigl\|(1+r)^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)(0,{\,\cdot\,})\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}\Bigr(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}\Bigr)(ru)(0,{\,\cdot\,})\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\Bigl\|(1+r)^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\Bigr\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\\
+\int_{0}^{\infty}\int|\Box u|\Bigl\{(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl|\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\Bigr|+(1+r)^{p}\Bigl|\Bigl(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r}+\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\Bigr|\Bigr\}\,dx\,dt.

We use (
2.8
) and the
fact that the Schwarz inequality allows us to bound

\int_{0}^{\infty}\int|\Box u|(1+r)^{-\delta}\Bigl|\Bigl(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r}-\frac{1}{r}\Bigr)u\Bigr|\,dx\,dt\lesssim\|(1+r)^{\frac{1-\delta}{2}}\Box u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\|(1+r)^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}.

Bootstrapping then gives

\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\sup_{U}U^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})(ru)\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(X_{U})}\lesssim\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}r^{-1}u(0,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1-\delta}{2}}\Box u\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\Bigl(\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/4}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1+p}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}+\sum_{U}\Bigl(\sum_{\tau\geq 4U}U\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box u\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Pairing this with (
2.4
) then completes the proof.
∎

3.
Sobolev Estimates

In this section, we collect our principal decay estimates, which are
variants of the Klainerman-Sobolev estimate
[
12
]
.

On occasion, it will suffice to apply the following standard weighted Sobolev
estimate, which is also from
[
12
]
and follows by applying Sobolev
embeddings in the
r
and
\omega
variables after localizing.

Lemma 3.1
.

For
h\in C^{\infty}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and
R>0
,

(3.1)

\|h\|_{L^{\infty}(A_{R})}\lesssim R^{-1}\|Z^{\leq 2}h\|_{L^{2}(\tilde{A}_{R})}.

Where a finer analysis is necessary, we shall use the space-time
Klainerman-Sobolev estimates of
[
20
, Lemma 3.8]
. We record
these in the following lemma.

Lemma 3.2
.

If
\tau\geq 1
,
1\leq R\leq\tau/2
, and
1\leq U\leq\tau/4
, then

(3.2)

\displaystyle\|w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|\partial_{r}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau})},

(3.3)

\displaystyle\|w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{3}{2}}U^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{U}_{\tau})}+\frac{U^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\tau^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial_{r}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{U}_{\tau})}.

We shall only tersely describe the proof since this result previously
appeared in
[
20
]
.
If
R=1
, (
3.2
) is an immediate consequence of standard
Sobolev embeddings. And if
1<R\lesssim\tau
, then after localizing,
we may apply Sobolev embeddings in
(s,\omega)
and the
Fundamental Theorem of Calculus in
\rho
where

t=e^{s}
and
r=e^{s+\rho}
. This gives that

\beta\Bigl(\frac{e^{s}}{\tau}\Bigr)\beta(\frac{e^{s+\rho}}{R}\Bigr)|w(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega)|\lesssim\Bigl(\int\int\int\Bigl|\partial_{\rho}\bigl|\partial_{s,\omega}^{\leq 2}\Bigl[\beta(\frac{e^{s}}{\tau}\Bigr)\beta(\frac{e^{s+\rho}}{R}\Bigr)w(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega)\Bigr]\bigr|^{2}\Bigl|\,d\rho\,ds\,d\omega\Bigr)^{1/2}.

Relying on the observations that

\partial_{s}(w(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega))=(Sw)(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega),\quad|\partial_{\omega}(w(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega)|\lesssim|(\Omega w)(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega)|,

\partial_{\rho}(w(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega))=(r\partial_{r}w)(e^{s},e^{s+\rho}\omega),

upon changing variables in the integrals, we see that

(3.4)

\|w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R}\|Z^{\leq 2}w\|^{\frac{1}{2}}_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau})}\|\partial_{r}Z^{\leq 2}w\|^{\frac{1}{2}}_{L^{2}L^{2}(\tilde{C}^{R}_{\tau})}.

The estimate (
3.2
) is now an immediate consequence. Moreover,
if
R=\tau/2
, if we replace
w
by
\chi\Bigl(\frac{2(t-r)}{\tau}\Bigr)w
and note that

S\Bigl(\chi\Bigl(\frac{2(t-r)}{\tau}\Bigr)\Bigr)={\mathcal{O}}(1)
, the estimate
for
C^{\tau/2}_{\tau}
also follows.

When
U=1
, the bound (
3.3
) follows from (
3.4
).
Otherwise, with
t=e^{s}
and
t-r=e^{s+\rho}
, a similar application
of Sobolev embeddings yields (
3.3
).

When the estimates of
[
20
]
are applied to
\partial w
, the
decomposition pairs nicely with

(3.5)

2Sw=(t+r)(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})w+(t-r)(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})w,

which will allow us to recover
\Box
to get additional decay out of
the second derivative terms. This represents space-time analogs of
some estimates of
[
13
]
. See, also,
[
16
]

where some similar analyses appeared previously.

Corollary 3.3
.

For
\tau\geq 1
and
1\leq R\leq\tau/2
,
1\leq U\leq\tau/4
, we have

(3.6)

\displaystyle\|\partial w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|\Box Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})},

(3.7)

\displaystyle\|\partial w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|\Box Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}.

Proof.

We apply (
3.2
) to see

(3.8)

\|\partial w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|\partial_{r}\partial Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}.

We notice that

(3.9)

\|\partial_{r}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{R}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}

follows from (
1.4
) and (
1.5
).
Moreover, by applying (
3.5
) with
w
replaced by

(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})Z^{\leq 2}w
and
(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})Z^{\leq 2}w
respectively, we obtain

(3.10)

\|(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})^{2}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{R}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\|(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2})Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})},

and

(3.11)

\|(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})^{2}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{R}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\|(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2})Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}.

Observing that

|\partial_{r}\partial Z^{\leq 2}w|\leq|\partial_{r}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}Z^{\leq 2}w|+|\partial_{r}(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})Z^{\leq 2}w|+|\partial_{r}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})Z^{\leq 2}w|

and subsequently writing
\partial_{r}=\frac{1}{2}\Bigl[(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})-(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})\Bigr]
in the last two terms, we see that

(3.12)

\|\partial_{r}\partial Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\|\partial_{r}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\|(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})^{2}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}\\
+\|(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})^{2}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\|(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2})Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}.

Using this in (
3.8
) and the estimating via (
3.9
), (
3.10
), and (
3.11
) gives

\|\partial w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2})Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{R}_{\tau})}.

Relying upon

(3.13)

\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2}=\Box+\frac{2}{r}\partial_{r}+{\not\negmedspace\nabla}\cdot{\not\negmedspace\nabla}

and (
1.5
)
yields
(
3.6
).

For (
3.7
), we argue similarly. By (
1.4
) and
(
1.5
) (applied to
\partial_{r}Z^{\leq 2}w
), we obtain

\|\partial_{r}{\not\negmedspace\nabla}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}.

And using (
3.5
) with
w
replaced by

(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})Z^{\leq 2}w
and
(\partial_{r}+\partial_{r})Z^{\leq 2}w
respectively, we see
that

\displaystyle\|(\partial_{t}-\partial_{r})^{2}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{U}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}+\frac{\tau}{U}\|(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2})Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})},

\displaystyle\|(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})^{2}Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{\tau}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}+\|(\partial_{t}^{2}-\partial_{r}^{2})Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}.

Using these in (
3.3
) and the
{\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau}
analog of (
3.12
), in combination with (
3.13
) and
(
1.5
) as above, we see that

\displaystyle\|\partial w\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}+\frac{U^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\tau^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial_{r}\partial Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1}{U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau^{\frac{3}{2}}}\|\partial Z^{\leq 3}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})}+\frac{1}{U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}}\|\Box Z^{\leq 2}w\|_{L^{2}L^{2}({\tilde{C}}^{U}_{\tau})},

which completes the proof.
∎

4.
Global Existence

Here we provide the proof of Theorem
1.1
. To do so, we
set
u_{0}\equiv 0
,
v_{0}\equiv 0
and recursively define
u_{k},v_{k}
to solve

(4.1)

\begin{cases}\Box u_{k}=(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})u_{k-1}\,\partial_{t}v_{k-1}-\partial_{r}u_{k-1}(\partial_{t}+\partial_{r})v_{k-1}-{\not\negmedspace\nabla}u_{k-1}\cdot{\not\negmedspace\nabla}v_{k-1},\\
\Box v_{k}=\partial_{t}u_{k-1}\,\partial_{t}v_{k-1},\\
(u_{k}(0,{\,\cdot\,}),\partial_{t}u_{k}(0,{\,\cdot\,}))=(u_{(0)},u_{(1)}),\\
(v_{k}(0,{\,\cdot\,}),\partial_{t}v_{k}(0,{\,\cdot\,}))=(v_{(0)},v_{(1)}).\end{cases}

We will show that the sequences
(u_{k})
and
(v_{k})
converge. The
limits yield the desired solutions
u,v
to (
1.1
).

4.1.
Boundedness

We fix
0<p<1
,
0<\delta<\min\Bigl(p,1-p\Bigr)
, and
N
large enough so that

\frac{N}{2}+3\leq N
. We then
set

(4.2)

M_{k}=\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\|Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{LE^{1}}+\|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+\sup_{\tau}\sup_{R\leq\tau/2}\Bigl(\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)+\Bigl[\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/2}\Bigl(\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{1-\frac{\delta}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}v_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{2}\Bigr]^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
+\sup_{\tau}\sup_{U\leq\tau/4}\Bigl(\tau U^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)+\Bigl[\sum_{\tau}\sum_{U\leq\tau/4}\Bigl(\tau^{1-\frac{\delta}{2}}U^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}v_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{2}\Bigr]^{\frac{1}{2}}.

For any
k\geq 1
, we shall show that

(4.3)

M_{k}\leq C_{0}\varepsilon+CM_{k-1}^{2}

for some fixed constant
C_{0}
. Provided that
\varepsilon>0
is
sufficiently small, a straightforward induction argument then shows
that

(4.4)

M_{k}\leq 2C_{0}\varepsilon

for any
k
.

The product rule gives

(4.5)

|Z^{\leq N}\Box u_{k}|\lesssim|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k-1}||\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}|+|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}||\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}v_{k-1}|.

Hence,

(4.6)

\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\Box Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\lesssim\tau^{-\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{p+\delta}{2}}\Bigl(\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\\
+\tau^{-\frac{1}{2}}R^{\frac{p+\delta}{2}}\Bigl(\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R^{1-\frac{\delta}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)\|Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}\|_{LE^{1}},

and

(4.7)

U^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\lesssim\tau^{\frac{p-1+\delta}{2}}\Bigl(U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\\
+\tau^{\frac{p-1+\delta}{2}}\Bigl(U^{\frac{1}{2}}\tau^{1-\frac{\delta}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)\|Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}\|_{LE^{1}}.

From this, it follows that

(4.8)

\Bigl(\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/2}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1+p}{2}}\Box Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}+\sum_{U}\Bigl(\sum_{\tau\geq 4U}U\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}\lesssim M_{k-1}^{2}.

By (
2.5
) and (
1.2
), along with a Hardy inequality, we get

(4.9)

\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\lesssim\varepsilon+M_{k-1}^{2}.

As the above argument does not rely on the null structure of
\Box u_{k}
and we also have

(4.10)

|Z^{\leq N}\Box v_{k}|\lesssim|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k-1}||\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}|+|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}||\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}v_{k-1}|,

the same arguments show that

(4.11)

\Bigl(\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/2}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1+p}{2}}\Box Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}+\sum_{U}\Bigl(\sum_{\tau\geq 4U}U\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p}{2}}\Box Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|^{2}_{L^{2}L^{2}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}}\lesssim M_{k-1}^{2},

which we shall use later.

It is in the process of bounding
\|Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{LE^{1}}
and
\|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}
that we will need the null condition. A finer
alternative to (
4.5
) that takes care with the good
directions is

(4.12)

|\Box Z^{\leq N}u_{k}|\lesssim|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}\partial v_{k-1}||Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-1}|+|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-1}||Z^{\leq N}\partial v_{k-1}|\\
+|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}\partial u_{k-1}||Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}|+|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}||Z^{\leq N}\partial u_{k-1}|.

We need to consider

\int_{0}^{\infty}\|Z^{\leq N}\Box u_{k}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}}\,ds\leq\sum_{j\geq 0}\int_{0}^{\infty}\|Z^{\leq N}\Box u_{k}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(A_{2^{j}})}\,ds.

To each lower order term in (
4.12
), we apply
(
3.1
) to see that this is

\lesssim\sum_{j\geq 0}\int_{0}^{\infty}2^{-\frac{p-\delta}{2}j}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(A_{2^{j}})}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}\partial v_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(\tilde{A}_{2^{j}})}\,ds\\
+\sum_{j\geq 0}\int_{0}^{\infty}2^{-\frac{p-\delta}{2}j}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(\tilde{A}_{2^{j}})}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}Z^{\leq N}\partial v_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(A_{2^{j}})}\,ds\\
+\sum_{j\geq 0}\int_{0}^{\infty}2^{-\frac{p}{2}j}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1}{2}}Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}\partial u_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(\tilde{A}_{2^{j}})}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(A_{2^{j}})}\,ds\\
+\sum_{j\geq 0}\int_{0}^{\infty}2^{-\frac{p}{2}j}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1}{2}}Z^{\leq N}\partial u_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(A_{2^{j}})}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}(s,{\,\cdot\,})\|_{L^{2}(\tilde{A}_{2^{j}})}\,ds.

By the Schwarz inequality and (
1.3
), this is

\lesssim\Bigl(\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\Bigr)\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\|Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}\|_{LE^{1}}\Bigl(\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\Bigr)

provided
\frac{N}{2}+2\leq N
, which is clearly
{\mathcal{O}}(M_{k-1}^{2})
as desired.
Hence, due to (
2.3
) and (
1.2
), we have shown

(4.13)

\|Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{LE^{1}}+\|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\lesssim\varepsilon+M_{k-1}^{2}.

In order to address the

terms,
we next consider

\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{1-\delta}{2}}Z^{\leq N}\Box v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}.

To the lower order factors in (

) we apply
(
3.1
) to see that this is

\lesssim\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}.

Since

. When combined

), (

), (

this gives

(4.14)

\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}v_{k}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\lesssim\varepsilon+M_{k-1}^{2}.

In order to show (
4.3

Applying (

+R^{\frac{\delta-p}{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}Z^{\leq N}\partial v_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+R^{-\frac{p}{2}}\|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}\partial u_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+R^{-\frac{p}{2}}\|Z^{\leq N}\partial u_{k-1}\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}+2}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}.

4.2.
Convergence

It remains to show that the sequence
(u_{k})
and
(v_{k})
converge. We
set

(4.18)

A_{k}=\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}Z^{\leq N}(v_{k}-v_{k-1})\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r^{-1}Z^{\leq N}(v_{k}-v_{k-1})\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\|Z^{\leq N}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{LE^{1}}+\|\partial Z^{\leq N}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{\infty}L^{2}}\\
+\|\langle r\rangle^{-\frac{1+\delta}{2}}\partial Z^{\leq N}(v_{k}-v_{k-1})\|_{L^{2}L^{2}}+\sup_{\tau}\sup_{R\leq\tau/2}\Bigl(\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}R\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)\\
+\Bigl[\sum_{\tau}\sum_{R\leq\tau/2}\Bigl(R^{\frac{3-\delta}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}(v_{k}-v_{k-1})\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{R}_{\tau})}\Bigr)^{2}\Bigr]^{\frac{1}{2}}+\sup_{\tau}\sup_{U\leq\tau/4}\Bigl(\tau U^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})\|_{L^{\infty}L^{\infty}(C^{U}_{\tau})}\Bigr)\\

We note that

+|\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}u_{k-2}||\partial Z^{\leq N}(v_{k-1}-v_{k-2})|+|\partial Z^{\leq N}u_{k-2}||\partial Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}(v_{k-1}-v_{k-2})|,

and

(4.22)

|\Box Z^{\leq N}(u_{k}-u_{k-1})|\lesssim|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}\partial v_{k-1}||Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}(u_{k-1}-u_{k-2})|+|Z^{\leq N}\partial v_{k-1}||Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}(u_{k-1}-u_{k-2})|\\
+|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-2}||Z^{\leq N}\partial(v_{k-1}-v_{k-2})|+|Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}u_{k-2}||Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}\partial(v_{k-1}-v_{k-2})|\\
+|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}||Z^{\leq N}\partial(u_{k-1}-u_{k-2})|+|Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}v_{k-1}||Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}\partial(u_{k-1}-u_{k-2})|\\
+|Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}{\not\negmedspace\partial}(v_{k-1}-v_{k-2})||Z^{\leq N}\partial u_{k-2}|+|Z^{\leq N}{\not\negmedspace\partial}(v_{k-1}-v_{k-2})||Z^{\leq\frac{N}{2}}\partial u_{k-2}|,

which will be used in place of (

),

)
respectively. Arguing as in the proof of (

that

Provided that

(
