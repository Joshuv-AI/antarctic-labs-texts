Introduction

In this paper we study the local everywhere regularity of the following
integral functional of the Calculus of Variations

(1.1)

\mathcal{F}_{0}\left(u,\Omega\right)=\int\limits_{\Omega}G\left(\left|u^{1}\right|,...,\left|u^{m}\right|,\left|\nabla u^{1}\right|,...,\left|\nabla u^{m}\right|\right)\,dx

\Omega

\mathbb{R}^{n}

n\geq 2

m\geq 1

H.1:

for every

The Caccioppoli inequalities (1.10) and (1.11) are typical of scalar
variational problems with growths of the type q-p; in particular we will
refer to the ideas presented in [14, 29, 32-34] to obtain from the
inequalities (1.10) and (1.11) the boundedness theorem Theorem 1.

The results given in Theorem 1 and Theorem 2 are not obvious, as shown by
the examples developed by E. De Giorgi in [16] and by E. Giusti and M.
Miranda in [26]. In particular, in [16], E. De Giorgi proved that the
function
u:B_{1}\left(0\right)\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}

u^{\alpha}\left(x\right)=x_{\alpha}\left|x\right|^{-\frac{n}{2}+\frac{n}{2\sqrt{\left(2n-2\right)^{2}+1}}}

n>2

F_{E}\left(v,B_{1}\left(0\right)\right)=\int\limits_{B_{1}\left(0\right)}\sum\limits_{i,j=1}^{n}\sum\limits_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{n}A_{\alpha,\beta}^{i,j}\left(x\right)\partial_{i}v^{\alpha}\partial_{j}v^{\beta}\,dx

with

A_{\alpha,\beta}^{i,j}\left(x\right)=\delta_{\alpha,\beta}\delta_{i,j}+\left[\left(n-2\right)\delta_{\alpha,i}+n\frac{x_{\alpha}x_{i}}{\left|x\right|^{2}}\right]\left[\left(n-2\right)\delta_{\beta,j}+n\frac{x_{\beta}x_{j}}{\left|x\right|^{2}}\right]

among all functions taking the value x on the boundary of the unit ball
B_{1}\left(0\right)
. While E. Giusti and M. Miranda in [26] demonstrated
that the function

u(x)=\frac{x}{\left|x\right|}

minimizes the functional

A_{\alpha,\beta}^{i,j}\left(x\right)=\delta_{\alpha,\beta}\delta_{i,j}+\left[\delta_{\alpha,i}+\frac{4}{n-2}\frac{u_{\alpha}u_{i}}{1+\left|u\right|^{2}}\right]\left[\delta_{\beta,j}+\frac{4}{n-2}\frac{u_{\beta}u_{j}}{1+\left|u\right|^{2}}\right]

sufficientely large. We observe that the functionals

examples of non-bounded or non-continuous extremals for vector-type

2.2.
Sobolev Spaces

For completeness we remember that if
\Omega
is a open subset of
\mathbb{R}^{N}
and
u
is a Lebesgue measurable function then
L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)
is the set of the class of the Lebesgue measurable function such
that
\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|u\right|^{p}\,dx<+\infty
and
W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)
is the set of the function
u\in L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)
such that its waek derivate
\partial_{i}u\in L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)
. The spaces
L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)
and
W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)
are Banach spaces with the respective norms

(2.3)

\left\|u\right\|_{L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)}=\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|u\right|^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

and

(2.4)

\left\|u\right\|_{W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)}=\left\|u\right\|_{L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)}+\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\left\|\partial_{i}u\right\|_{L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)}

We say that the function
u:\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n}
belong in
W^{1,p}\left(\Omega,\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
if
u^{\alpha}\in W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)
for every
\alpha=1,...,n
, where
u^{\alpha}
is the
\alpha
component of the
vector-valued function
u
; we end by remembering that
W^{1,p}\left(\Omega,\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
is a Banach space with the norm

(2.5)

\left\|u\right\|_{W^{1,p}\left(\Omega,\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)}=\sum\limits_{\alpha=1}^{n}\left\|u^{\alpha}\right\|_{W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)}

In particular, the following basic results hold.

Theorem 8

(Sobolev Inequality)
.

Let
\Omega
be a open subset of
\mathbb{R}^{N}
if
u\in W_{0}^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)
with
1\leq p<N
there
exists a real positive constant
C_{SN}
, depending only on
p
and
N
,
such that

(2.6)

\left\|u\right\|_{L^{p^{\ast}}\left(\Omega\right)}\leq C_{SN}\left\|\nabla u\right\|_{L^{p}\left(\Omega\right)}

where
p^{\ast}=\frac{Np}{N-p}
.

Theorem 9
.

(Rellich-Sobolev Immersion Theorem) Let
\Omega
be a open bounded subset
of
\mathbb{R}^{N}
with lipschitz boundary then if
u\in W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)

with
1\leq p<N
there exists a real positive constant
C_{IS}
, depending
only on
p
and
N
, such that

(2.7)

\left\|u\right\|_{L^{p^{\ast}}\left(\Omega\right)}\leq C_{IS}\left\|u\right\|_{W^{1,p}\left(\Omega\right)}

where
p^{\ast}=\frac{Np}{N-p}
.

of Sobolev spaces.

\left[\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\left|\nabla\left(\eta\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right)\right|^{q}\,dx\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

\leq 2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\left|\nabla\eta\right|^{q}\left|\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\eta^{q}\left|\nabla\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

and

(8.14)

\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\left|\nabla\eta\right|^{q}\left|\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx

\leq\left(\frac{2}{\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}}\right)^{q}\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\left|\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx

\leq\left(\frac{2}{\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}}\right)^{q}\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right|^{q}\,dx

\leq\left(\frac{2}{\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}}\right)^{q}\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q}\,dx

usinig (8.12), (8.13) and (8.14) we get

(8.15)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq c_{q,n}2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}\left[\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\frac{\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q}}\,dx\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}\left[\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\eta^{q}\left|\nabla\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

Since

\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\eta^{q}\left|\nabla\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx\leq\int\limits_{A_{k_{i+1},\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|\nabla v_{R}^{\alpha}\right|^{q}\,dx

using Caccioppoli Inequality (8.7) it follows

(8.16)

\int\limits_{B_{\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}}\eta^{q}\left|\nabla\left(v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i+1}}{R}\right)_{+}\right|^{q}\,dx

\leq\int\limits_{A_{k_{i+1},\tilde{\varrho}_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|\nabla v_{R}^{\alpha}\right|^{q}\,dx

\leq\frac{C_{1}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx+C_{2}\left(1+\varrho_{i}^{-\epsilon n}k_{i}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{n}+\epsilon}

then by (8.15) and (8.16) we have

(8.17)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq c_{q,n}2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}\left[\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\frac{\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q}}\,dx\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}

\cdot\left[\frac{C_{1}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{\beta q^{\ast}}}\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx+C_{2}\left(1+\varrho_{i}^{-\epsilon n}k_{i}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{n}+\epsilon}\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

Moreover by Young Inequality we get

\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\frac{\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q}}\,dx\leq\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}1+\frac{\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q^{\ast}\beta}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\,dx

and

(8.18)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq c_{q,n}2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}\left[\frac{1}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx+\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}

\cdot\left[\frac{C_{1}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\int\limits_{A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}}\left|v_{R}^{\alpha}-\frac{k_{i}}{R}\right|^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx+C_{2}\left(1+\varrho_{i}^{-\epsilon n}k_{i}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{n}+\epsilon}\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

Moreover it follows

(8.19)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}W_{\alpha,i}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}C_{1}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}W_{\alpha,i}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}C_{2}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left(1+\varrho_{i}^{-\epsilon n}k_{i}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{1-\beta}\left[\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

Since
0<\epsilon<\frac{\beta q^{\ast}}{2n}
,
\frac{1}{2}<\varrho_{i}<1

and

\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i+1},\varrho_{i+1}}^{\alpha}\right)\leq\mathcal{L}^{n}\left(A_{k_{i},\varrho_{i}}^{\alpha}\right)\leq\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}

we get

(8.20)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\left[\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\right]^{1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}W_{\alpha,i}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}\left[\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\right]^{1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left[\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}C_{1}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\left[\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\right]^{1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}W_{\alpha,i}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}C_{2}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left(1+2^{\epsilon ni}k_{i}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left[\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\right]^{1-\beta}\left[\frac{W_{\alpha,i}}{\left(\frac{k_{i+1}-k_{i}}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta}}\right]^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

Since,
\varrho_{i}-\varrho_{i+1}=\frac{1}{2^{1+i}}
and
k_{i+1}-k_{i}=\frac{d}{2^{i}}
, it folows

(8.21)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}}{\left(\frac{1}{2^{1+i}}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}2^{q^{\ast}\beta}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

+\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}C_{1}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{1}{2^{1+i}}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}C_{2}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left(1+2^{\epsilon ni}k_{i}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

Moreover
0<\epsilon<\frac{\beta q^{\ast}}{2n}
and
k_{i}=d\sum\limits_{j=0}^{i}2^{-j}\leq 2d
, then

(8.22)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left(2^{q^{\ast}\beta}+C_{1}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\right)}{\left(\frac{1}{2^{1+i}}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[2^{q^{\ast}\beta}+C_{2}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left(1+2^{q^{\ast}\beta i}d^{q^{\ast}\beta}\right)^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\right]\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

if we choose
d>1
we get

(8.23)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq\frac{c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left(2^{q^{\ast}\beta}+C_{1}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\right)}{\left(\frac{1}{2^{1+i}}\right)^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

+c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left[2^{q^{\ast}\beta}+d^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}C_{2}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\left(1+2^{q^{\ast}\beta i}\right)^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\right]\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

and

(8.24)

W_{\alpha,i+1}

\leq C_{1,q,n,\beta}\,2^{\left(1+i\right)\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

+C_{2,q,n,\beta}\,d^{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}2^{\left(1+i\right)\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}}\frac{W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}{\left(\frac{d}{2^{i}R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}

where
C_{1,q,n,\beta}=c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left(2^{q^{\ast}\beta}+C_{1}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\right)
and

C_{2,q,n,\beta}=c_{q,n}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-1}\left(1+C_{2}^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}\right)2^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}
. Since
0<R<r<1
it follows

(8.25)

W_{\alpha,i+1}\leq\frac{C_{3,q,n,\beta}\,\left[4^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}+1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}\right]^{i}}{\left(\frac{d}{R}\right)^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}}W_{\alpha,i}^{1-\beta+\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}

where
C_{3,q,n,\beta}=\left(C_{1,q,n,\beta}+C_{2,q,n,\beta}\right)4^{q^{\ast}\beta\left(\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}+1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right)}
. Let us consider the inequality
1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}>0
that it is
equivalent to the inequality

\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}\beta^{2}+\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)\beta-1<0

which has as a set of solution
\beta_{1}<\beta<\beta_{2}
where
\beta_{1}=\frac{-\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)-\sqrt{\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)^{2}+4\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}}{2\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}
and
\beta_{2}=\frac{-\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)+\sqrt{\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)^{2}+4\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}}{2\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}
; since
\frac{q}{q^{\ast}}<\beta<\min\left\{\frac{2^{\ast}}{2}\frac{q}{q^{\ast}},1,\beta_{0}\right\}

then after simple algebraic calculation we have

\frac{q}{q^{\ast}}<\frac{-\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)+\sqrt{\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)^{2}+4\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}}{2\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}

We put
\beta_{0}=

\frac{-\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)+\sqrt{\left(1-\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}}{q}\right)^{2}+4\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}}{2\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\right)^{2}}{nq}}
and
we choose

d=C_{4,q,n,\beta}\left[\frac{1}{R^{n+\frac{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}-q^{\ast}\beta}{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}}\int\limits_{B_{R}\left(y_{0}\right)}u_{0,+}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx\right]^{\frac{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}{q^{\ast}\beta\left[1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]}}+1

C_{4,q,n,\beta}=C_{3,q,n,\beta}^{\frac{1}{q^{\ast}\beta\left[1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]}}4^{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta\left[\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}+1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]}{\left[\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]\left[1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]}}

(8.26)

ess-\sup_{B_{\frac{R}{2}}\left(y_{0}\right)}\left(u_{0}^{\alpha}\right)<C_{4,q,n,\beta}\left[\frac{1}{R^{n+\frac{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}-q^{\ast}\beta}{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}}\int\limits_{B_{R}\left(y_{0}\right)}u_{0,+}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx\right]^{\frac{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}{q^{\ast}\beta\left[1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]}}+1

for every

.

Similary we get

(8.27)

ess-\inf_{B_{\frac{R}{2}}\left(y_{0}\right)}\left(u_{0}^{\alpha}\right)>-C_{4,q,n,\beta}\left[\frac{1}{R^{n+\frac{\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{q}-q^{\ast}\beta}{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}}}\int\limits_{B_{R}\left(y_{0}\right)}u_{0,-}^{q^{\ast}\beta}\,dx\right]^{\frac{\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}}{q^{\ast}\beta\left[1-\beta-\frac{\left(q^{\ast}\beta\right)^{2}}{nq}+\epsilon\frac{q^{\ast}\beta}{q}\right]}}-1
