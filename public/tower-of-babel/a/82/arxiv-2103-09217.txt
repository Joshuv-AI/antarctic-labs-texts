Abstract
. Let Λ \Lambda be an Artin algebra. In 2014, T. Adachi, O. Iyama and I. Reiten proved that the torsion funtorially finite classes in mod ⁡ ( Λ ) \mathrm{mod}\,(\Lambda) can be described by the τ \tau -tilting theory. The aim of this paper is to introduce the notion of F F -torsion class in mod ⁡ ( Λ ) \mathrm{mod}\,(\Lambda) , where F F is an additive subfunctor of Ext Λ 1 , \mathrm{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}, and to characterize when these clases are preenveloping and F F -preenveloping. In order to do that, we introduce the notion of F F -presilting Λ \Lambda -module. The latter is both a generalization of τ \tau -rigid and F F -tilting in mod ⁡ ( Λ ) . \mathrm{mod}(\Lambda).

Introduction

Throughout this paper
\Lambda
will be an Artin
R
-algebra. We denote by
\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda)
the category of finitely generated left
\Lambda
-modules and
\mathrm{proj}(\Lambda)
(respectively,
\mathrm{inj}(\Lambda))
denotes the category of finitely generated projective (respectively, injective) left
\Lambda
-modules. Following
[
AR75
]
, we have the usual duality functor
D_{\Lambda}:\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda)\to\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda^{op}),
where
D_{\Lambda}:=\mathrm{Hom}_{R}(-,k)
and

R/\mathrm{rad}(R),
the duality functor

and the Nakayama equivalence

where

For any

, we denote by
\mathrm{gen}(M)
the epimorphic images of finite direct sums of copies of
M,
and by
\mathrm{add}(M)
the class of all the direct summands of finite direct sums of copies of

The study of relative homological algebra over an Artin algebra
\Lambda
is due to M. Auslander and Ø. Solberg
[
AS93
,
ASII93
]
. It has been very useful for studying relative tilting modules through a connection with partial tilting modules and his complemments
[
ASIII93
]
. In those papers, Auslander and Solberg found more general methods that allow us the construction of new subcategories that are contravariantly finite and functorially finite. It is also given a generalization to the relative context of the theory of tilting and cotilting modules giving new relationships between algebras.

All the subfunctors
F
of
\mathrm{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}
to be considered in this paper will be additive. We write
F\subseteq\mathrm{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}
meaning that
F
is an additive subfunctor of

For a given

An

is a
\Lambda
-module

\mathrm{gen}(X)=\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda).
Following
[
AS93
]
, for a given class

which is determined by all the exacts sequences
\eta:0\to A\to B\to C\to 0
such that
\mathrm{Hom}_{\Lambda}(X,\eta)
is an exact sequence
\forall

X\in\mathcal{X}.

Let
\mathcal{C}
be an additive category and
\mathcal{X}
be a class of objects in
\mathcal{C}.
Following
[

]

-precover. Dually, we have the notion of

-

In case that

-exact sequence

Tilting theory started as a module-theoretic interpretation of the reflection functors introduced by I. N. Berstein, I. M. Gelfand and V. A. Ponomarev in the early 1970s. These functors were categorized in
\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda)
by Brenner and Butler
[
BB80
]
, through the tilting modules. When
T
is a tilting module, there is an associated torsion pair
(\mathcal{T},\mathcal{F})
, where
\mathcal{T}=\mathrm{gen}(T).
Moreover, for
\Gamma:=\mathrm{End}_{\Lambda}(T)^{op},
we have the faithful functors
\mathrm{Hom}_{\Lambda}(T,-):\mathcal{T}\to\mathrm{mod}(\Gamma)
and
\mathrm{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}(T,-):\mathcal{F}\to\mathrm{mod}(\Gamma).
The above can be seen as a generalization of Morita equivalence
[
Mo58
]
, which is recovered if
T
is a progenerator. In the case that
\mathcal{T}
is a funtorially finite torsion class, it is proved in
[
AIR14
]
that there is a support
\tau
-tilting module
M
such that
\mathcal{T}=\mathrm{gen}(M),
where
\tau
is the Auslander-Reiten translation functor
[
AR75
]
. Moreover, this assignment defines a bijection between functorially finite torsion classes and support

\tau
-tilting modules.

\mathcal{X}\subseteq\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda).
The right orthogonal class of

Dually, we have the left orthogonal class

of

and the

of

Following

M. Hoshino and S. Smalø proved the following result characterizing the torsion preenveloping classes (containing the injectives)
through the tilting modules.

Theorem 1.1
.

[

(b)

the class of all the
\Lambda
-modules which are finite direct sums of copies of objects in

.

Let

and

\mathrm{(a)}

(respectively
,

(respectively,

-exact sequence

of

(respectively

\mathrm{(b)}

The concept of triangulated category, introduced by J. L. Verdier in
[
Ve96

, has been very useful for studying the category

This is because certain triangulated categories become quite accessible if one applies the methods of representation theory

[

and can be identified with the homotopy category
\mathsf{K}^{-,b}(\mathrm{proj}(\Lambda))
of bounded cochain complexes which are bounded above and have bounded cohomology over the class
\mathrm{proj}(\Lambda).
Silting theory is a topic originating from representation theory of Artin algebras which is an extension of tilting theory in the environment of triangulated categories
[
AMV16
]

We denote by
\mathsf{C}(\mathcal{C})

\mathcal{C}.
The homotopy category of cochain complexes of objects in
\mathcal{C}
is denoted by

The two-terms presilting complexes in

-tilting theory

. For an additive generator

-rigid modules in

For each

For any cochain complex

Let

The following technique allows solving problems in

Proposition 2.1

[

For

\forall\,Z\in\mathrm{add}(M);

\forall\,X\in\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda).

(b)

Let

The class

For an additive generator

\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda),

Consider the class
\mathcal{T}:=\mathrm{gen}(P(1)).
Let us show the following:

(1)

\mathrm{gl.dim}_{F}(\Lambda)=1.
In particular,
\Lambda
is
F
-admissible.

Inded, since
\mathrm{pd}_{F}(X)=0
and
\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda)=\mathrm{add}(X\oplus S(1)),
it is enough to show that
\mathrm{pd}_{F}(S(1))=1.
But, this follows from the Auslander-Reiten quiver since
0\to S(2)\to P(1)\to S(1)\to 0
is an
F
-exact sequence.

(2)

\mathcal{T}
is
F
-torsion and preenveloping but not
F
-preenveloping. In particular, from Theorem

,
\mathcal{T}\neq\mathrm{gen}_{F}(T)
for any
F
-tilting
T\in\mathrm{mod}(\Lambda).
However, by Theorem

, there is some
M\in\mathrm{F}\text{-}\mathrm{presilt}\text{-}\mathrm{esp}(\Lambda)
with
\mathcal{T}=\mathrm{gen}_{F}(M).

Indeed, note firstly that
\mathcal{T}=\mathrm{add}(P(1)\oplus S(1))=\mathrm{gen}_{F}(P(1))
and thus
\mathcal{T}

P(1)

, we get that
\mathcal{T}

\mathcal{I}(F)=\mathrm{add}(S(3))\oplus\mathrm{inj}(\Lambda)\not\subseteq\mathrm{gen}_{F}(P(1)).
Thus, by Lemma

(c), we get that
\mathcal{T}=\mathrm{gen}_{F}(P(1))

F
-preenveloping.

\mathcal{T}\neq\mathrm{gen}_{F}(M)
