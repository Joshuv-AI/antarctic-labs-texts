The electromagnetic form factors in the N f = 4 N_{f}=4 holographic QCD
Hiwa A. Ahmed, Yidian Chen, Mei Huang

Abstract
In this study, we employ a modified soft-wall holographic model with four flavors to investigate the meson spectra, decay constants, electromagnetic form factors, and charge radius of various mesons. We obtain the spectra for vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalar mesons. Decay constants are calculated and compared with experimental and lattice QCD data. The pion and kaon electromagnetic form factors are compared with the experimental data, and a good agreement is achieved for the kaon at low Q 2 Q^{2} . For the charmed mesons, the electromagnetic form factors of the D D and D s D_{s} and electric form factors of the D ∗ D^{*} and D s ∗ D_{s}^{*} are well consistent with the lattice QCD data. Moreover, the electric, magnetic, and quadrupole form factors are predicted for the ρ \rho , K ∗ K^{*} , a 1 a_{1} , K 1 K_{1} , D 1 D_{1} , and D s ​ 1 D_{s1} mesons. Furthermore, the charge radius of the vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalars, including the strange and charmed mesons, are computed.

The holographic QCD model for
N_{f}=4

In this section, we revisit the formalism of the bottom-up holographic QCD model, specifically focusing on the inclusion of the charm quark. The original formalism for the light flavor sector (
N_{f}=2
) can be found in Refs.
Erlich:2005qh
;
Karch:2006pv
for the hard-wall and soft-wall approaches, respectively. Additional works
Abidin:2009aj
;

;
Momeni:2020bmy
;
Chen:2021wzj

Chen:2021wzj
. The five-dimensional metric defined in the Anti-de Sitter (AdS) space is given by,

ds^{2}=\frac{1}{z^{2}}\left(\eta_{\mu\nu}dx^{\mu}dx^{\nu}+dz^{2}\right),

(1)

where
\eta_{\mu\nu}
is the four-dimensional metric in the Minkowski space, and
z
is the fifth dimension of the AdS space, which has an inverse energy scale. It is well-known that in the AdS/CFT model for each operator in the 4D theory (also called boundary theory), there is a corresponding field in the 5D theory (bulk theory). The operators that are important for the chiral dynamics are left- and right-handed currents
J_{R/L\mu}^{a}=\bar{\psi}_{qR/L}\gamma_{\mu}t^{a}\psi_{qR/L}
and quark bilinear
\bar{\psi}_{qL}\psi_{qR}

and
L_{\mu}^{a}

X

Chen:2021wzj
a heavy scalar field
H

V_{H}

\rho
) and heavy charmed meson (
J/\psi
). Now, we have all the ingredients to write down the general five-dimensional action with
SU(4)_{L}\times SU(4)_{R}
symmetry as follows.

\displaystyle S_{M}

\displaystyle=-\int_{\epsilon}^{z_{m}}d^{5}x\sqrt{-g}e^{-\phi}\operatorname{Tr}\left\{\left(D^{M}X\right)^{\dagger}\left(D_{M}X\right)+M_{5}^{2}|X|^{2}\right.

(2)

\displaystyle\left.+\frac{1}{4g_{5}^{2}}\left(L^{MN}L_{MN}+R^{MN}R_{MN}\right)+\left(D^{M}H\right)^{\dagger}\left(D_{M}H\right)+M_{5}^{2}|H|^{2}\right\},

where
D^{M}X=\partial_{M}X-iL_{M}X+iXR_{M}
and
D^{M}H=\partial_{M}H-iV_{HM}+iHV_{HM}
are the covariant derivative of the scalar field
X
and
H

M_{5}^{2}=(\Delta-p)(\Delta+p-4)=-3

\Delta=3

. Despite of introducing the dilaton field
\phi

is inserted as the upper limit of the model. So now, the limits of the integration represent the IR (
z_{m}
) and UV (
\epsilon

is related to the number of color (
N_{c}
) using the AdS/CFT dictionary
g_{5}^{2}=12\pi^{2}/N_{c}

Erlich:2005qh
, where
N_{c}=3
. The gauge field strength
L_{MN}
and
R_{MN}

\displaystyle L_{MN}=\partial_{M}L_{N}-\partial_{N}L_{M}-i\left[L_{M},L_{N}\right],

(3)

\displaystyle R_{MN}=\partial_{M}R_{N}-\partial_{N}R_{M}-i\left[R_{M},R_{N}\right].

To simplify the analysis, the left- and right-handed gauge fields are replaced with the vector (
V_{M}=\frac{1}{2}(L_{M}+R_{M})
) and axial fields ((
A_{M}=\frac{1}{2}(L_{M}-R_{M})

\displaystyle V_{MN}=\partial_{M}V_{N}-\partial_{N}V_{M}-i\left[V_{M},V_{N}\right]-i\left[A_{M},A_{N}\right],

(4)

\displaystyle A_{MN}=\partial_{M}A_{N}-\partial_{N}A_{M}-i\left[V_{M},A_{N}\right]-i\left[A_{M},V_{N}\right],

\displaystyle D^{M}X=\partial_{M}X-i\left[V_{M},X\right]-i\{A_{M},X\}.

The complex scalar field can be decomposed as

X=e^{i\pi}X_{0}e^{i\pi}

(5)

where
X_{0}=\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{diag}\left[v_{u}(z),v_{d}(z),v_{s}(z),v_{c}(z)\right]

-\frac{z^{3}}{e^{-\phi}}\partial_{z}\frac{e^{-\phi}}{z^{3}}\partial_{z}v_{q}(z)-\frac{3}{z^{2}}v_{q}(z)=0.

\displaystyle\left.v_{q}(z)\right|_{z\rightarrow 0}

\displaystyle=2C_{q1}z+

(9)

\displaystyle C_{q1}\left[-\mu^{2}+2\gamma_{E}\mu^{2}+2\mu^{2}\log z+2\mu^{2}\log\mu+\mu^{2}\Psi\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)\right]z^{3}.

10
) up to the second order in the fields

,

, and

(20)

(28)

with the gauge fixing and transverse condition
A_{z}^{a}=0
, and
\partial^{\mu}A_{\mu\perp}^{a}
, respectively. The bulk-to-boundary propagator of the axial vector field
\mathcal{A}^{a}(q^{2},z)
satisfy the bounadry conditions
\mathcal{A}^{a}(q^{2},\epsilon)=0
and
\partial_{z}\mathcal{A}^{a}(q^{2},zm)=0
, in the UV and IR region.

Finally, the mass spectra of the pseudoscalar mesons can be found by solving the coupled equation of motions between the pseudoscalar field
\pi
and the longitudinal part of the axial vector field
\phi
,

\displaystyle q^{2}\partial_{z}\varphi^{a}(q,z)+\frac{2g_{5}^{2}M_{A}^{aa}}{z^{2}}\partial_{z}\pi^{a}(q,z)=0,

(29)

\displaystyle\frac{z}{e^{-\phi}}\partial_{z}\left(\frac{e^{-\phi}}{z}\partial_{z}\varphi^{a}(q,z)\right)-\frac{2g_{5}^{2}M_{A}^{aa}}{z^{2}}\left(\varphi^{a}(q,z)-\pi^{a}(q,z)\right)=0,

with the boundary conditions
\pi^{a}(q^{2},\epsilon)=\phi^{a}(q^{2},\epsilon)=0
and
\partial_{z}\pi^{a}(q^{2},zm)=\partial_{z}\phi^{a}(q^{2},zm)=0
. In the holographic QCD approach, the decay constants for the vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalar mesons are defined by

\displaystyle f_{V^{n}}^{a}=\frac{e^{-\phi}}{g_{5}z}\partial_{z}\psi_{V^{n}}^{a}(z)|_{z\to\epsilon},

(30)

\displaystyle f_{A^{n}}^{a}=\frac{e^{-\phi}}{g_{5}z}\partial_{z}\psi_{A^{n}}^{a}(z)|_{z\to\epsilon},

\displaystyle f_{\pi^{n}}^{a}=-\frac{e^{-\phi}}{g_{5}z}\partial_{z}\varphi^{a,n}(z)|_{z\to\epsilon},

where
\psi_{V^{n}}^{a}(z)
,
\psi_{A^{n}}^{a}(z)
, and
\varphi^{a,n}(z)
are the normalized wavefunctions for the vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalar mesons, respectively.

IV
Three-point interaction Coupling Constants and form factors

The coupling constants and form factors can be obtained from the cubic order of the 5D action in Eq. (
10
),

\displaystyle S^{(3)}=

\displaystyle-\int d^{5}x\left\{\eta^{MN}\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z^{3}}(2\left(A_{M}^{a}-\partial_{M}\pi^{a}\right)V_{N}^{b}\pi^{c}g^{abc}+V_{M}^{a}\left(\partial_{N}\left(\pi^{b}\pi^{c}\right)-2A_{M}^{b}\pi^{c}\right)h^{abc}\right.

(31)

\displaystyle-V_{M}^{a}V_{N}^{b}\pi^{c}k^{abc})+\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{2g_{5}^{2}z}\eta^{MP}\eta^{NQ}(V_{MN}^{a}V_{P}^{b}V_{Q}^{c}+V_{MN}^{a}A_{P}^{b}A_{Q}^{c}+A_{MN}^{a}V_{P}^{b}A_{Q}^{c}\left.+A_{MN}^{a}A_{P}^{b}V_{Q}^{c})f^{bca}\right\}

with the following definitions for
g^{abc}
,
h^{abc}
, and
k^{abc}
,

\displaystyle g^{abc}=iTr\left(\{t^{a},X_{0}\}[t^{b},\{t^{c},X_{0}\}]\right),

(32)

\displaystyle h^{abc}=iTr\left([t^{a},X_{0}]\{t^{b},\{t^{c},X_{0}\}\}\right),

\displaystyle k^{abc}=-2Tr\left([t^{a},X_{0}][t^{b},\{t^{c},X_{0}\}]\right).

In the present work, we are interested in the three-point interactions of the
VVV
,
VAA
, and
V\pi\pi
. The corresponding part of the action to these three-point interactions are

S_{VVV}=-\int_{\epsilon}^{z_{m}}d^{5}\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{2g_{5}^{2}z}\eta^{mp}\eta^{nq}\left(V_{mn}^{a}V_{p}^{b}V_{q}^{c}\right)f^{abc}

(33)

S_{VAA}=-\int_{\epsilon}^{z_{m}}d^{5}\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{2g_{5}^{2}z}\eta^{mp}\eta^{nq}\left(V_{mn}^{a}A_{p}^{b}A_{q}^{c}+A_{mn}^{a}V_{p}^{b}A_{q}^{c}+A_{mn}^{a}A_{p}^{b}V_{q}^{c}\right)f^{abc}

(34)

\displaystyle S_{V\pi\pi}=

\displaystyle-\int_{\epsilon}^{z_{m}}d^{5}x\left\{\eta^{mn}\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z^{3}}\left(2\left(A_{m}^{a}-\partial_{m}\pi^{a}\right)V_{n}^{b}\pi^{c}g^{abc}+V_{m}^{a}\left(\partial_{n}\left(\pi^{b}\pi^{c}\right)-2A_{n}^{b}\pi^{c}\right)h^{abc}\right)\right.

(35)

\displaystyle\left.+\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{2g_{5}^{2}z}\eta^{mp}\eta^{nq}\left(V_{mn}^{a}A_{p}^{b}A_{q}^{c}\right)f^{abc}\right\}

In the AdS/CFT approach, the coupling constants of three-point interaction can be obtained from the three-point correlation functions by functional variation of the 5D action with respect to the sources of the 5D fields
Grigoryan:2007wn
;
Kwee:2007nq
. The relevant coupling constants calculated are given by

g_{VVV}=g_{5}f^{abc}\int_{\epsilon}^{zm}dz\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z}V^{a}(z)V^{b}(z)V^{c}(z),

(36)

g_{VAA}=g_{5}f^{abc}\int_{\epsilon}^{zm}dz\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z}V^{a}(z)A^{b}(z)A^{c}(z),

(37)

g_{V\pi\pi}=g_{5}\int_{\epsilon}^{zm}dz\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z}\left(f^{abc}\partial_{z}\phi^{a}V^{b}(z)\partial_{z}\phi^{a}-\frac{2g_{5}^{2}}{z^{2}}(\pi^{a}-\phi^{a})V^{b}(z)(\pi^{c}-\phi^{c})(g^{abc}-h^{bac})\right).

(38)

Moreover, we study the electromagnetic (EM) form factors of the channels
VVV
,
VAA
, and
V\pi\pi
and the charge radius. The EM form factors of the vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalar mesons are defined by
Ballon-Bayona:2017bwk
;
Grigoryan:2007wn
;
BallonBayona:2009ar

\displaystyle\left\langle V^{a}(p+q),\epsilon^{\prime}\left|J_{EM}^{\mu}(0)\right|V^{a}(p),\epsilon\right\rangle=

\displaystyle-\left(\epsilon^{\prime}\cdot\epsilon\right)(2p+q)^{\mu}F_{V^{a}}^{1}\left(q^{2}\right)

(39)

\displaystyle+\left[\epsilon^{\prime\mu}(\epsilon\cdot q)-\epsilon^{\mu}\left(\epsilon^{\prime}\cdot q\right)\right]\left[F_{V^{a}}^{1}\left(q^{2}\right)+F_{V^{a}}^{2}\left(q^{2}\right)\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{M_{V^{a}}^{2}}\left(q\cdot\epsilon^{\prime}\right)(q\cdot\epsilon)(2p+q)^{\mu}F_{V^{a}}^{3}\left(q^{2}\right).

\displaystyle\left\langle A^{a}(p+q),\epsilon^{\prime}\left|J_{EM}^{\mu}(0)\right|A^{a}(p),\epsilon\right\rangle=

\displaystyle-\left(\epsilon^{\prime}\cdot\epsilon\right)(2p+q)^{\mu}F_{A^{a}}^{1}\left(q^{2}\right)

(40)

\displaystyle+\left[\epsilon^{\prime\mu}(\epsilon\cdot q)-\epsilon^{\mu}\left(\epsilon^{\prime}\cdot q\right)\right]\left[F_{A^{a}}^{1}\left(q^{2}\right)+F_{A^{a}}^{2}\left(q^{2}\right)\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{M_{A^{a}}^{2}}\left(q\cdot\epsilon^{\prime}\right)(q\cdot\epsilon)(2p+q)^{\mu}F_{A^{a}}^{3}\left(q^{2}\right).

\left\langle\pi^{a}(p+q)\left|J_{EM}^{\mu}(0)\right|\pi^{a}(p)\right\rangle=(2p+q)^{\mu}F_{\pi^{a}}\left(q^{2}\right).

(41)

where
\epsilon
and
\epsilon^{\prime}
are the polarization vectors of the initial and final vector mesons, respectively, and
J_{EM}^{\mu}(0)
is the electromagnetic current. The electromagnetic current can be represented by the linear combination of the flavor currents via
Ballon-Bayona:2017bwk

J_{EM}^{\mu}(x)=\sum_{a=(\rho,\omega,J/\psi)}c_{a}J_{a}^{\mu}(x)

(42)

where the coefficients
c_{a}
is a constant depending on the contribution of the quarks in the EM current. The electric (charge), magnetic, and quadrupole form factors of the vector and axial vector mesons can be deduced from the linear combination of the form factors in Eq. (
39
) and Eq. (
40
), respectively.

\displaystyle F_{V^{a}}^{E}=F_{V^{a}}^{1}+\frac{q^{2}}{6M_{V^{a}}^{2}}\left[F_{V^{a}}^{2}-\left(1-\frac{q^{2}}{4M_{V^{a}}^{2}}\right)F_{V^{a}}^{3}\right],

(43)

\displaystyle F_{V^{a}}^{M}=F_{V^{a}}^{1}+F_{V^{a}}^{2},

\displaystyle F_{V^{a}}^{Q}=-F_{V^{a}}^{2}+\left(1-\frac{q^{2}}{4M_{V^{a}}^{2}}\right)F_{V^{a}}^{3}.

\displaystyle F_{A^{a}}^{E}=F_{A^{a}}^{1}+\frac{q^{2}}{6M_{A^{a}}^{2}}\left[F_{A^{a}}^{2}-\left(1-\frac{q^{2}}{4M_{A^{a}}^{2}}\right)F_{A^{a}}^{3}\right],

(44)

\displaystyle F_{A^{a}}^{M}=F_{A^{a}}^{1}+F_{A^{a}}^{2},

\displaystyle F_{A^{a}}^{Q}=-F_{A^{a}}^{2}+\left(1-\frac{q^{2}}{4M_{A^{a}}^{2}}\right)F_{A^{a}}^{3}.

For the elastic case, the electromagnetic form factors obtained as

\displaystyle F_{V^{a}}^{1}=F_{V^{a}}^{2}=F_{V^{a}}=\sum_{n}\frac{f_{V^{a}}g_{VVV}}{M_{V^{a}}^{2}+Q^{2}},

(45)

\displaystyle F_{V^{a}}^{3}=0,

\displaystyle F_{A^{a}}^{1}=F_{A^{a}}^{2}=F_{A^{a}}=\sum_{n}\frac{f_{V^{a}}g_{VAA}}{M_{V^{a}}^{2}+Q^{2}},

(46)

\displaystyle F_{A^{a}}^{3}=0,

F_{\pi^{a}}=\sum_{n}\frac{f_{V^{a}}g_{V\pi\pi}}{M_{V^{a}}^{2}+Q^{2}}

(47)

where
Q^{2}=-q^{2}
. Using the couplings in Eqs. (
36
-
38
) and the definition of the bulk-to-boundary in Eq. (
27
), one can reach the final version of the form factors for the vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalar mesons,

F_{V}(Q^{2})=f^{abc}\int dz\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z}\mathcal{V}^{a}(Q^{2},z)V^{b}(z)V^{c}(z),

(48)

F_{A}(Q^{2})=f^{abc}\int dz\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z}\mathcal{V}^{a}(Q^{2},z)A^{b}(z)A^{c}(z),

(49)

F_{\pi}(Q^{2})=\int dz\frac{e^{-\phi(z)}}{z}\mathcal{V}^{b}(Q^{2},z)\left(f^{abc}\partial_{z}\phi^{a}\partial_{z}\phi^{c}-\frac{2g_{5}^{2}}{z^{2}}(\pi^{a}-\phi^{a})(\pi^{c}-\phi^{c})(g^{abc}-h^{bac})\right).

(50)

Note that the bulk-to-boundary propagator for vector sector
\mathcal{V}^{b}(Q^{2},z)
can be obtained by solving the equation of motion Eq. (

\mathcal{V}^{b}(Q^{2},z)=\Gamma(1+\frac{Q^{2}}{4\mu^{2}})U(\frac{Q^{2}}{4\mu^{2}},0,z^{2}\mu^{2})

(51)

The charge radius can be obtained from the low

F^{(C)}(Q^{2})=1-\frac{1}{6}\left<r^{2}\right>Q^{2}+...,

(52)

then, the charge radius is given by

\left<r^{2}\right>=-6\frac{dF(Q^{2})}{dQ^{2}}|_{Q^{2}\rightarrow 0}.

(53)

VI
Conclusions

In the present work, we performed the four flavors modified soft-wall holographic model to investigate various aspects of mesons, including meson spectra, decay constants, electromagnetic form factors, and charge radii of the vector, axial vector, and pseudoscalar mesons. The model parameters were fitted to experimental meson masses and decay constants, enabling us to obtain the meson spectra for the vector mesons,
\rho
,
K^{*}
,

\pi

,

D

Chen:2021wzj
, our results are significantly improved, especially for the pseudoscalar sector. Moreover, the decay constants were also computed and reported in Table
5

16\%
. In the axial vector sector, the decay constant of the
