ii)

Since all of the odd integers in

to be included into a
\varphi_{2^{\alpha}}
, hence

. Thus

.

Since

and
(u^{{}^{\prime}},v^{{}^{\prime}})
are arbitrary vertices of
\Gamma_{1}
, hence the diameter of

is
2
.
Similarly the diameter of
\Gamma_{2}
is
2
.

and
\alpha\geq 1
. Then
\Gamma

(u,v)

(u^{{}^{\prime}},v^{{}^{\prime}})

\Gamma
.

u=u^{{}^{\prime}}
and

. Since
(u,v)

and
