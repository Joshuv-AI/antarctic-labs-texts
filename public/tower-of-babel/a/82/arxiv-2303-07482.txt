Normal forms for rational 3-tangles
Bo-hyun Kwon, Jung Hoon Lee

Abstract
. In this paper, we define the normal form of collections of disjoint three bridge arcs for a given rational 3 3 -tangle. We show that there is a sequence of normal jump moves which leads one to the other for two normal forms of the same rational 3-tangle.



1.
Introduction

Originally, rational tangles introduced by John Conway (1970,
[
1
]
) meant rational
2
-tangles. J. Conway completed the classification of rational tangles by assigning each rational tangle to the corresponding rational number. Moreover, if two rational tangles are equivalent then their corresponding rational numbers are the same. Based on the definition of rational tangles, we can extend the meaning of the rational tangles to define rational
n
-tangles,
n\geq 2
. For a long time after the rational tangle is defined there are many attempts to classify rational
3
-tangles in advance.
As one of the trials to classify them, the first author
[
2
]
gave an algorithm to compare two rational
3
-tangles. However, it does not give a certain representative of each rational
3
-tangle. In this paper, we define the
normal form
which is a special collection of disjoint three
bridge arcs
of a given rational
3
-tangle
T
. We provide a clue to give a certain representative of each rational
3
-tangle.
