Abstract
. In this work we explore the relationship between the Möbius inverse transformation of an arbitrary sequence and unrestricted partitions identities.

Introduction

A partition of a number
n

\pi=(k_{1},k_{2},\cdots)
comprising non-negative integers, with a finite subset of the
k_{i}
being nonzero.
\pi
is a partition of
n
if and only if

can be expressed as the sum
\sum jk_{j}
, where each positive integer
j
appears with a frequency or multiplicity of
k_{j}
, without consideration of order. The integers
j

\pi=1+2+3+2+1
of 9 is denoted
\pi=(2,2,1,0,0,\cdots)
with
k_{1}=2
,
k_{2}=2
,
k_{3}=1
and
k_{4}=k_{5}=\cdots=0

Within this study, our focus lies on sums represented by
\sum_{\pi\in P(n)}f(\pi)

n
, denoted by
P(n)
.

Four particular functions that we shall discuss are

(1.1)

k(\pi)=\sum_{j}k_{j}

the total number of parts in
\pi
, and

(1.2)

Q(\pi)=\sum_{k_{j}>0}1

the number of distinct parts in
\pi
. For example, the partition
\pi=(2,2,1)
of 9 has
k(\pi)=5
and
Q(\pi)=3
.

The other two function are the
s(\pi)
and

\pi
of n.

2.
Arithmetical interpretation of weighted partitions identities related to the largest and smallest part

Theorem 1
.

Let
a_{n}
an arbitrary sequence of real or imaginary terms,
n
a positive integer number, and

and

real or imaginary numbers. Then

(2.1)

\sum_{n\geq 1}a_{n}\frac{tu\left((1-u)tq^{n+1}\right)_{\infty}}{\left(tq^{n}\right)_{\infty}}q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 1}q^{n}\sum_{\pi\in P(n)}t^{k(\pi)}u^{Q(\pi)}a_{s(\pi)}

where
s(\pi)
is the smallest term in the partitions
\pi

Theorem 2
.

Let
a_{n}
an arbitrary sequence of real or imaginary terms,
n
a positive integer number, and
t
and
u
real or imaginary numbers. Then

(2.2)

\sum_{n\geq 1}a_{n}\frac{tu\left((1-u)tq\right)_{n-1}}{\left(tq\right)_{n}}q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 1}q^{n}\sum_{\pi\in P(n)}t^{k(\pi)}u^{Q(\pi)}a_{l(\pi)}

where

is the largest term in the partitions
\pi

Theorem 3
.

Let
a_{n}
an arbitrary sequence of real or imaginary terms,
n
a positive integer number, and
t
and
u
real or imaginary numbers. Then

(2.3)

\sum_{n\geq 1}a_{n}\sum_{i\geq 0}\frac{tu\left((1-u)tq^{i+1}\right)_{n-1}}{\left(tq^{i+1}\right)_{n}}q^{n+i}=\sum_{n\geq 1}q^{n}\sum_{\pi\in P(n)}t^{k(\pi)}u^{Q(\pi)}\sum_{j=1}^{s(\pi)}a_{l(\pi)-s(\pi)+j}

where

and

are the largest and smallest term in the partitions

, respectively.

Theorem 4
.

Let
a_{n}
and arbitrary sequence of real or imaginary terms ,

or
0
, and

a positive integer number, Then

(2.4)

\sum_{\pi\in P(n)}(t)^{k(\pi)-1}\left(\frac{t-1}{t}\right)^{Q(\pi)-1}\sum_{j=1}^{s(\pi)}a_{l(\pi)-s(\pi)+j}=\sum_{d|n}(t)^{d}\left(\frac{a_{1}}{t}+\frac{a_{2}}{t^{2}}+\frac{a_{3}}{t^{3}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{d}}{t^{d}}\right)

Let
a_{n}
and arbitrary sequence of real or imaginary terms ,

&=\sum_{d|n}(t)^{d}(a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\cdots+a_{d})\end{split}

\displaystyle\begin{split}\sum_{\pi\in P(n)}(t)^{k(\pi)-1}\left(\frac{t-1}{t}\right)^{Q(\pi)-1}&\sum_{j=1}^{s(\pi)}\left(a_{l(\pi)-s(\pi)+j}-ta_{l(\pi)-s(\pi)+j-1}\right)=\sum_{d|n}a_{d}\end{split}
