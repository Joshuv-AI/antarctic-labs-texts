A Composite Order Generalization of Modular Moonshine
Satoru URANO

Abstract
We introduce a generalization of Brauer character to allow arbitrary finite length modules over discrete valuation rings. We show that the generalized super Brauer character of Tate cohomology is a linear combination of trace functions. Using this result, we find a counterexample to a conjecture of Borcherds about vanishing of Tate cohomology for Fricke elements of the Monster.

Introduction

Conway and Norton’s monstrous moonshine conjecture
[

stated the existence of a graded representation

of the real numbers

Frenkel, Lepowsky, and Meurman

7
]
constructed a well-behaved graded representation as a vertex algebra, and Borcherds
[
1
]
showed that this vertex algebra satisfied Conway and Norton’s conjecture.

The original modular moonshine conjectures of Ryba asserted the existence of vertex algebras
{}^{g}V
over finite fields with finite group actions, such that the graded Brauer characters are genus zero modular functions. He also suggested a construction of the vertex algebra

of prime order

that is the largest conjugacy class of order

[
4
]

Borcherds and Ryba reinterpreted the modular moonshine conjectures in terms of Tate cohomology with coefficients in an integral form
V
of the monster vertex algebra. They proved that the 1-st Tate cohomology
\hat{H}^{1}(g,V)
for a cyclic group
\langle g\rangle
generated by any Fricke element

(see Definition

) of odd prime order vanishes. Borcherds proved that if

p
-regular element of
C_{\mathbb{M}}(g)
, then graded Brauer characters

\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}\big(h|{\hat{H}}^{i}(g,V)\big)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}\big(h|{\hat{H}}^{i}(g,V_{n})\big)q^{n-1}
,
i=0,1
, is a linear combination of Hauptmoduls for any non-Fricke element

of odd prime order
[
2
,
3
]
. At the end of
[
2
]
, Borcherds proposed a conjecture that asserts the existence of objects that unify modular moonshine with generalized moonshine. In his conjecture, conditions 2, 4 and 7 together imply
\hat{H}^{1}(g,V)
vanishes for all Fricke elements

. We will call this claim “Borcherds’s conjecture about vanishing of Tate cohomology”.

It is natural to consider Tate cohomology of
V
for elements of composite order, but there are no results in the literature. Tate cohomology does not yield vector spaces over a field when the element has composite order, but Brauer characters are only defined for such objects. Specifically, Tate cohomology yields torsion modules over the ground ring. We can’t use the Brauer character for such modules because it is defined only for representations defined over a field and
p
-regular elements. So, we need to define a generalized Brauer character.

In Section
2
, we introduce some terminology in this paper. In Section
3
, we summarize the modular moonshine conjecture for elements of prime order. In Section
4
, we consider ramified extensions

of the
p

and calculate Tate cohomology. In Section

, to generalize the modular moonshine conjectures, we define a generalized Brauer character and show that the generalized super Brauer character of Tate cohomology is a linear combination of traces. This is a generalization of
[
3

to the composite order case. Using this result, we give a counterexample to Borcherds’s conjecture about vanishing of Tate cohomology. We propose a weaker conjecture about vanishing of Tate cohomology.

Modular moonshine for elements of prime order

In this section, we summarize modular moonshine for elements of prime order

Ryba proposed the modular moonshine conjecture. The existence of a self-dual integral form of the monster vertex algebra was not proved when he suggested it, but Carnahan proved it
[

]
.

Theorem 3.1

(the original modular moonshine conjecture)
.

Suppose that an element
g\in\mathbb{M}
has prime order
p
and lies in conjugacy class
pA
, and that
V
is a self-dual integral form of the monster vertex algebra.
Let
V^{g}
be the set of vectors fixed by

{}^{g}V=\cfrac{V^{g}/pV^{g}}{(V^{g}/pV^{g})\cap(V^{g}/pV^{g})^{\perp}}.

For

, if

is a
p
-regular element of the centralizer

of

\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|^{g}V_{n})q^{n-1}=T_{gh}(\tau).

Here, we may take

when computing Brauer character

.

Let

be a finite group including an element

which are free as

-modules; there are

,
\mathbb{Z}_{p}[G]

By the following lemma, the Tate cohomology for any

Lemma 3.2

(
[

If

We have the following assertion by applying Proposition

Lemma 4.2

5

-Brauer character

In this section, we will define a new notion of generalized Brauer character and prove properties of it. We will give a counterexample to a conjecture of Borcherds about vanishing of Tate cohomology.

Let
p
be a prime number. Let
R
be a discrete valuation ring of mixed characteristic
(0,p)
,
v\colon R\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\cup\{\infty\}
be a surjective valuation on
R
and
K=\operatorname{Frac}(R)
.

We can’t use the Brauer character for
R_{p}/(1-\zeta_{N}^{n})R_{p}
because this is not a vector space over a field. To solve this, we will define a
p
-Brauer character.

Definition 5.1
.

Let
G
be a finite group and
M
be a finite length
R[G]
-module. Let
h
be a
p
-regular element of
G
. Suppose that
M
has a composition series
0=M_{0}\subsetneq M_{1}\subsetneq\dots\subsetneq M_{n}=M
as an
R[h]
-module.
We define the

-Brauer character of

for

\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}(h|M)=\cfrac{1}{v(p)}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\operatorname{Tr}\big(h|\widetilde{M_{i}}\otimes K\big),

where
\widetilde{M_{i}}
is an
R
-free

-module satisfying
M_{i}/M_{i-1}\cong\widetilde{{M}_{i}}\otimes_{R[h]}R/\mathfrak{m}_{R}[h]
.

Lemma 5.2
.

This definition does not depend on our choices of
M_{i}
and
\widetilde{M}_{i}
.

Proof.

For
M_{i}/M_{i-1}\cong\widetilde{{M}_{i}}\otimes_{R[h]}R/\mathfrak{m}_{R}[h]\cong\widetilde{{M}_{i}^{\prime}}\otimes_{R[h]}R/\mathfrak{m}_{R}[h]
, we have
\operatorname{Tr}\big(h|\widetilde{M}_{i}\otimes K\big)=\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|M_{i}/M_{i-1})=\operatorname{Tr}\big(h|\widetilde{M}_{i}^{\prime}\otimes K\big)
. By the Jordan–Hölder theorem, the composition series of
M
is unique up to permutation and isomorphism. That is, for any two composition series
0=M_{0}\subsetneq M_{1}\subsetneq\dots\subsetneq M_{n}=M
,
0=M_{0}^{\prime}\subsetneq M_{1}^{\prime}\subsetneq\dots\subsetneq M_{n^{\prime}}^{\prime}=M
, we have
n=n^{\prime}
and some permutation
\sigma
of
\{1,\dots,n\}
s.t.
M_{i}/M_{i-1}\cong M_{\sigma(i)}^{\prime}/M_{\sigma(i)-1}^{\prime}
. Hence,
\sum_{i=1}^{n}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|M_{i}/M_{i-1})=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|M_{\sigma(i)}^{\prime}/M_{\sigma(i)-1}^{\prime})=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|M_{i}^{\prime}/M_{i-1}^{\prime})
. Therefore the
p
-Brauer character is well-defined.
∎

Note that it does depend on a choice of a prime number

.

We can consider the notion of an irreducible

-Brauer character, so that

is a simple

-module that

is a simple

-module. In particular, an irreducible

-Brauer is an irreducible Brauer character and a character table of

We will prove that the

\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}
satisfies
\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}(h|M^{\prime})=\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}(h|M)+\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}(h|M^{\prime\prime})
.

Lemma 5.3
.

The

Proof.

Let

-modules. Suppose that

has length

and a composition series

and

of

is

is a composition series of

. By injectivity of

for any

Therefore,

\displaystyle\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}(h|M^{\prime})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{v(p)}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}\big(h|\varphi(M_{i})/\varphi(M_{i-1})\big)+\frac{1}{v(p)}\sum_{j=1}^{m^{\prime\prime}}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}\big(h|\psi^{-1}(M^{\prime\prime}_{j})/\psi^{-1}(M^{\prime\prime}_{j-1})\big)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{v(p)}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|M_{i}/M_{i-1})+\frac{1}{v(p)}\sum_{j=1}^{m^{\prime\prime}}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}(h|M^{\prime\prime}_{j}/M^{\prime\prime}_{j-1})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{v_{S}(p)}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\operatorname{Tr}\big(h|\widetilde{A}\otimes_{R}S\otimes\operatorname{Frac}(S)\big)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{kv_{R}(p)}k\operatorname{Tr}\big(h|\widetilde{A}\otimes\operatorname{Frac}(R)\big)=\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p}(h|A).

∎

Theorem 5.7
.

Let
G
be a finite group. Suppose that
g\in\operatorname{Cent}(G)
has order
N
and that
h\in G
is an
N
-regular element. Let
A
be a finitely generated
R
-free
R[\langle g,h\rangle]
-module. Then
\hat{H}^{*}(g,A)=\hat{H}^{0}(g,A)-\hat{H}^{1}(g,A)
is a virtual representation of
\langle h\rangle

\displaystyle\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p_{i}}(h|\hat{H}^{*}(g,A))=\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}a_{k,p_{i}}\operatorname{Tr}\big(g^{k}h|A\big),

\displaystyle a_{k,p_{i}}=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{\sum_{(p_{i},d)=1,d|N}\varphi(N/d)}\left(n_{i}-l-\dfrac{n_{i}-l-1}{p_{i}}\right),&\big(k,p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)=p_{i}^{l},\ 0\leq l\leq n_{i}-1,\\
0,&\big(k,p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)=p_{i}^{n_{i}},\end{cases}

where the prime factorization of
N
is
\prod p_{i}^{n_{i}}
.

Proof.

Suppose

\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p_{i}}\big(h|\hat{H}^{*}(g,A)\big)=\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}a_{k,p_{i}}\operatorname{Tr}\big(g^{k}h|A\big).

It suffices to take
A=R_{n,m}
.
By an inverse discrete Fourier transform and Lemma
5.6
, we have

\displaystyle a_{k,p_{i}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{b=0}^{N-1}\widetilde{\operatorname{Tr}}_{p_{i}}\big(h|\hat{H}^{*}(g,R_{b,m})\big)\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\big(g^{k}h|R_{b,m}\big)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N}\Bigg(n_{i}+\sum_{|g^{b}|=p_{i}}\frac{-1}{\varphi(p_{i})}\zeta_{N}^{-kb}+\sum_{|g^{b}|=p_{i}^{2}}\frac{-1}{\varphi\big(p_{i}^{2}\big)}\zeta_{N}^{-kb}+\dots+\sum_{|g^{b}|=p_{i}^{n_{i}}}\frac{-1}{\varphi\big(p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)}\zeta_{N}^{-kb}\Bigg).

Let
\big(k,p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)=p_{i}^{l}
. If
s>l
, then
\sum_{|g^{b}|=p_{i}^{s}}\zeta_{N}^{-kb}
is a sum of primitive
p_{i}^{s-l}
-th roots of unity and equals an integer multiple of the Möbius function
\mu\big(p_{i}^{s-l}\big)
. If
s\leq l

\sum_{|g^{b}|=p_{i}^{s}}\zeta_{N}^{-kb}=\varphi(p_{i}^{s}).

Hence, when
\big(k,p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)=p_{i}^{n_{i}}
, we have

a_{k,p_{i}}=\frac{1}{N}(n_{i}-1-\dots-1)=0.

When
\big(k,p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)=p_{i}^{l}

\big(N/p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)=\sum_{d|(N/p_{i}^{n_{i}})}\varphi\big(N/p_{i}^{n_{i}}d\big)

\displaystyle a_{k,p_{i}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N}\Bigg(n_{i}-1-\dots-1+\frac{p_{i}^{l}}{\varphi\big(p_{i}^{l+1}\big)}\Bigg)

\displaystyle=\frac{(n_{i}-l)\varphi\big(p_{i}^{l+1}\big)+p_{i}^{l}}{N\varphi\big(p_{i}^{l+1}\big)}=\frac{p_{i}^{l+1}(n_{i}-l)-p_{i}^{l}(n_{i}-l-1)}{p_{i}^{l+1}\varphi\big(p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)\big(N/p_{i}^{n_{i}}\big)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sum_{(p_{i},d)=1,d|N}\varphi(N/d)}\bigg(n_{i}-l-\frac{n_{i}-l-1}{p_{i}}\bigg).
