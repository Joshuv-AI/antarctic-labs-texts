Planar graphs without cycles of length from 4 to 7 are near-bipartite
Lili Hao, Weihua Yang, Shuang Zhao

Abstract




Abstract:

A graph is near-bipartite if its vertex set can be partitioned into an independent set and a set which induces a forest. In this paper, planar graphs without cycles of length from 4 to 7 are shown to be near-bipartite.

Keywords
: Near-bipartite; 3-colorable; Discharging method; Planar graph

In 2001, Borodin and Glebov

The main tool used in this paper is the discharging method which has been utilized in graph theory for more than 100 years. Borodin
[

. By Lemma 2.5(2), there are at least two non-adjacent non-bad vertices in

f

\frac{2}{3}

Subcase 4.1.
If

,

only if

,

Subcase 4.2.

Lemma 3.3
.

ch^{\ast}(f_{0})>0
.

Proof.
Incontrovertibly,
ch^{\ast}(f_{0})\geq ch_{1}(f_{0})
. Let
n_{Fa}(G)
denote the number of
FA
-structures in
G
, then
0\leq n_{Fa}(G)\leq 3

Case 1.

, the case where
f_{0}
gives the most charge is that when
n_{Fa}(G)=1
and all the other vertices on
C_{0}
, except for this

-structure, have degree 2. By R4,

sends
\frac{4}{3}
to
d(f_{0})-2

, which implies

. Besides,

. But
G
has one
10^{+}
-face
f_{1}
containing nine 2-vertices with

as Lemma 3.2. By R5,

.

Case 2.

n_{Fa}(G)=0

f_{0}
sends
\frac{4}{3}
only to 2-vertex by R4. Since
G\ncong G[V(C_{0})]
