Finding Efficient Domination for P 8 P_{8} -Free Bipartite Graphs in Polynomial Time
Andreas Brandstädt, Raffaele Mosca

Abstract
A vertex set D D in a finite undirected graph G G is an efficient dominating set ( e.d.s. for short) of G G if every vertex of G G is dominated by exactly one vertex of D D . The Efficient Domination (ED) problem, which asks for the existence of an e.d.s. in G G , is known to be ℕ ​ ℙ \mathbb{NP} -complete for P 7 P_{7} -free graphs, and even for very restricted H H -free bipartite graph classes such as for K 1 , 4 K_{1,4} -free bipartite graphs as well as for C 4 C_{4} -free bipartite graphs while it is solvable in polynomial time for P 7 P_{7} -free bipartite graphs as well as for S 2 , 2 , 4 S_{2,2,4} -free bipartite graphs. Here we show that ED can be solved in polynomial time for P 8 P_{8} -free bipartite graphs.
