Applications of β \beta -Nijenhuis-Richardson bracket to cohomology of Hom-algebras
Nejib Saadaoui

Abstract
In this paper, we mean by Hom-algebras a triple ( M , d , β ) (M,d,\beta) in which M M is a vector space, d d a bilinear map on M M and β : M → M \beta\colon M\to M (the twisting map). In this work we define the left, right and Lie β \beta -Nijenhuis-Richardson bracket associated to a vector space M M . We use these brackets to construct, in a canonical way, cohomology and deformations of each Hom-algebra structure of M M . Next, we use these chomologies to define the representation and extensions of Hom-algebra ( L , δ , α ) (L,\delta,\alpha) by a Hom-algebra ( V , μ , α V ) (V,\mu,\alpha_{V}) .
