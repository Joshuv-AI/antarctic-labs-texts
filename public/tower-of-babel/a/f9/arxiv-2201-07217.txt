Conditional h h -convexity with applications
Ismail Nikoufar and Davuod Saeedi

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the notion of conditional h h -convex functions and we prove an operator version of the Jensen inequality for conditional h h -convex functions. Using this type of functions, we give some refinements for Ky-Fan’s inequality, arithmetic-geometric mean inequality, Chrystal inequality, and H o ¨ \ddot{o} lder-McCarthy inequality. Many of the other inequalities can be refined by applying this new notion.

Lemma 1

Let
h:[0,1]\to(0,\infty)

Proof

Let

for every

and
\lambda\in[0,1]
.
It is enough to show that

.
We have

\displaystyle F(u,\lambda)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\ln\bigg(\frac{1-u}{u}\bigg)+\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\ln\bigg(\frac{1-v}{v}\bigg)-\ln\bigg(\frac{1-(\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v)}{\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v}\bigg)

\displaystyle=\ln\bigg(\frac{1-u}{u}\bigg)-\ln\bigg(\frac{1-(\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v)}{\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v}\bigg)

\displaystyle\ \ \ +\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\ln\bigg(\frac{1-v}{v}\bigg)-(1-\frac{\alpha}{\beta})\ln\bigg(\frac{1-u}{u}\bigg).

(16)

It is obvious that

for every
\lambda\in[0,1]
.
So,
\frac{1-(\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v}{\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v}<\frac{1-u}{u}
.
It follows that

\ln\bigg(\frac{1-u}{u}\bigg)-\ln\bigg(\frac{1-(\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v)}{\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v}\bigg)\geq 0.

(17)

On the other hand, we have
\frac{v^{\alpha}}{v^{\alpha}+(1-v)^{\alpha}}\leq u
so that

\frac{1-u}{u}\leq(\frac{1-v}{v})^{\alpha}
.
This ensures

.
Since

,
this indicates that

\bigg(1-\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\bigg)\ln\bigg(\frac{1-u}{u}\bigg)\leq\frac{1}{\beta}\ln\bigg(\frac{1-u}{u}\bigg)\leq\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\ln\bigg(\frac{1-v}{v}\bigg).

(18)

The inequalities (

) and (

.

u\leq u^{\beta-\alpha}
.

\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\ln(v)\leq\frac{\beta-\alpha}{\beta}\ln(u).

(22)

We realize from the inequalities (

F(u,\lambda)\geq 0
.

Corollary 3

(Refined Arithmetic-Geometric Mean inequality)

\gamma=\max|a_{i}-a_{j}|
.

\gamma=0
.

Proof

Consider
f(t)=-\ln(t)
on
(0,1]
.

0<a_{i}\leq v\leq 1
, we have
1<\alpha\leq\alpha+\gamma\leq\alpha+1
. Due to Lemma
1
and setting
\beta=\alpha+\gamma
, the function
f
is
g
-conditional
h
-convex
at every

A

2

and
||x||=1

Theorem
2.1
signifies the inequality (

) holds and by the assumptions we have

\displaystyle\langle Ax,x\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{n}q_{i}a_{i},
