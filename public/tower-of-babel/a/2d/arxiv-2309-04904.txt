On real hyperelliptic solutions of focusing modified KdV equation
Shigeki Matsutani

Abstract
. We study the real hyperelliptic solutions of the focusing modified KdV (MKdV) equation of the genus three. Since the complex hyperelliptic solutions of the focusing MKdV equation over ℂ {\mathbb{C}} are associated with the real gauged MKdV equation, we present a novel construction of the real hyperelliptic solutions of the gauged MKdV equation. When the gauge field is constant, it can be regarded as the real solution of the focusing MKdV equation, and thus we also discuss the behavior of the gauge field numerically.

Introduction

It is known that the modified Korteweg-de Vries (MKdV) equation is given by

\partial_{t}q\pm 6q^{2}\partial_{s}q+\partial_{s}^{3}q=0,

(1.1)

where
\partial_{t}:=\partial/\partial t
,

[

Although the MKdV equation is connected with geometry as a helical curve by G. Lamb Jr
[
22

[

Konno, Ichikawa and Wadati found a novel geometrical integrable differential equation by studying the inverse scattering method

[

They called its soliton solution

Following their study, Ishimori revealed the connection between the loop soliton equation and the focusing MKdV equation

The loop soliton equation is regarded as the equation for the deformation of a real curve whose tangential angle

\partial_{t}\theta+\frac{1}{2}\left(\partial_{s}\theta\right)^{3}+\partial_{s}^{3}\theta=0.

In this paper we refer to (

Independently, Goldstein and Petrich found that the iso-metric deformation of a real curve on a plane is connected with the recursion relations of the focusing MKdV hierarchy, and the curve whose curvature obeys the focusing MKdV hierarchy is an iso-metric deformation

In other words, the loop soliton equation was rediscovered as an iso-metric deformation of curves on a plane, although it could be seen as a revival of the study

Since the plane curve obeying (

In this paper, we showed a novel algebro-geometric method to obtain the cross-section

Then we have used the data of the hyperelliptic curves

Further, Theorem
6.1
shows that they are also regarded as cross section of the solutions of the MKdV equation (
1.3
) if the
\partial_{u_{3}}\psi_{{\mathrm{i}}}
is sufficiently small.
We consider that the question of what the conditions should be for it to be small is the next issue.

The ultimate purpose of this study is to find the focusing MKdV equation of higher genus explicitly rather than its cross section as mentioned in Introduction.
It is far from the final result, but this study shows the first step towards the goal.
In other words, finding the real analytical solution of the focusing MKdV equation is difficult but so interesting that we should continue to study this problem.
