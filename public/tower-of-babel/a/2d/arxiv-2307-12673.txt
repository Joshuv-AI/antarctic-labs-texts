Soft-photon theorem for pion-proton elastic scattering revisited
Piotr Lebiedowicz, Otto Nachtmann, Antoni Szczurek

Abstract
We discuss the reactions π ​ p → π ​ p \pi p\to\pi p and π ​ p → π ​ p ​ γ \pi p\to\pi p\gamma from a general quantum field theory (QFT) point of view. We consider the pion-proton elastic scattering both off shell and on shell. The on-shell amplitudes for π ± ​ p → π ± ​ p \pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p scattering are described by two invariant amplitudes, while the off-shell amplitudes contain eight invariant amplitudes. We study the photon emission amplitudes in the soft-photon limit where the photon energy ω → 0 \omega\to 0 . The expansion of the π ± ​ p → π ± ​ p ​ γ \pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma amplitudes to the orders ω − 1 \omega^{-1} and ω 0 \omega^{0} is derived. These terms can be expressed by the on-shell invariant amplitudes and their partial derivatives with respect to s s and t t . The term of order ω − 1 \omega^{-1} is well known from the literature. Our term of order ω 0 \omega^{0} is new. The formulas given for the amplitudes in the limit ω → 0 \omega\to 0 are valid for both real and virtual photons. We also discuss the behaviour of the corresponding cross-sections.



I
Introduction

In this paper we shall study the
\pi p\to\pi p
and
\pi p\to\pi p\gamma
reactions.

Let
\omega
be the energy of the photon in the overall c.m. system. We are interested in the limit
\omega\to 0
, that is, the soft-photon limit. In a seminal paper
Low:1958sn
F. E. Low considered the scattering of a charged on an uncharged scalar particle with photon radiation. He showed that the pole term of order
\omega^{-1}
in the amplitude comes exclusively from the photon emission of the external particles. The extension of this result to reactions with an arbitrary number of external particles was considered in
Weinberg:1965nx
.
This result was extended to spin 1/2 particles
by Burnett and Kroll in
Burnett:1967km
,
and generalized subsequently by Bell and Van Royen

Bell:1969yw
to particles of arbitrary spin.
Up to the present many authors have studied
the soft photon limit; see for instance

Gribov:1966hs
;
Burnett:1967km
;
Bell:1969yw
;
Lipatov:1988ii
;
DelDuca:1990gz
;
Gervais:2017yxv
;
Bern:2014vva
;
Lysov:2014csa
;
Bonocore:2021cbv

There is in general agreement on the pole term proportional to
\omega^{-1}
in the amplitudes for photon emission.

In
Low:1958sn
also an expression for the term of order
\omega^{0}
for the reaction studied there, is given.
In
Lebiedowicz:2021byo
we reconsidered this problem. We studied the reactions

\displaystyle\pi^{-}+\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}+\pi^{0}\,,

\displaystyle\pi^{-}+\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}+\pi^{0}+\gamma\,.

(1.1)

We worked in the framework of QCD and treated electromagnetism to lowest relevant order. As theoretical tools we used the Ward-Takahashi identity
Ward:1950xp
;
Takahashi:1957xn

and the Landau conditions giving the position of singularities in amplitudes;
see
Landau:1959fi
and chapter 18 of
Bjorken:1965

To our surprise, we found a result for the
\omega^{0}
term different from the one given in
Low:1958sn

In Appendices A and B of

\omega^{0}
presented in

;
Lipatov:1988ii
;
Gervais:2017yxv
;
Bern:2014vva
;
Lysov:2014csa

In our present paper we continue our studies of soft-photon production in hadronic reactions.
In

V
Conclusions

In this article we have considered soft-photon production in

\pi^{\pm}
-proton elastic scattering. That is, we studied the reactions

\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p
and
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma

for the photon energy
\omega\to 0
.
The terms of order
\omega^{-1}
in the amplitudes for
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
are well known
Low:1958sn
;
Weinberg:1965nx

and we reproduced them in our calculations.
The evaluation of the terms of order
\omega^{0}
is the central topic of our paper and our result there is new, as far as we know.
The main issues which we had to consider and the main results which we obtained were the following.

(i)

In going from

\displaystyle\quad\pi^{\pm}\,(p_{a})+p\,(p_{b})

\displaystyle\to\pi^{\pm}\,(p_{1})+p\,(p_{2})

(5.1)

to

\displaystyle\quad\pi^{\pm}\,(p_{a})+p\,(p_{b})

\displaystyle\to\pi^{\pm}\,(p^{\prime}_{1})+p\,(p^{\prime}_{2})+\gamma(k)

(5.2)

for
k\neq 0
a corresponding change of the momenta
(p_{1},p_{2})\to(p^{\prime}_{1},p^{\prime}_{2})
has to be taken into account.
Keeping
p^{\prime}_{1}=p_{1}
and
p^{\prime}_{2}=p_{2}

for
k\neq 0
violates energy-momentum conservation;
see
(
3.6
)–(
3.25
)
and Fig.
2
.

(ii)

In the diagrams for (
5.2
) we have to deal with the
off-shell
amplitudes for
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p
;
see Fig.
3
. Whereas the on-shell amplitude
for
\pi^{-}p\to\pi^{-}p
scattering is described
by two invariant amplitudes,

A^{-}(s,t)
and
B^{-}(s,t)
, see (
II
)–(
II
), the off-shell amplitude for
\pi^{-}p\to\pi^{-}p
contains eight invariant amplitudes;
see (
II
)–(
II
).
The same is, of course, true for
\pi^{+}p\to\pi^{+}p
. In the calculation of
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
all these eight off-shell invariant amplitudes have to be taken into account.
It came as a surprise to us that the final result for the terms of order
\omega^{-1}
and
\omega^{0}
in the
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
amplitudes can be completely expressed in terms of the on-shell amplitudes
A^{\pm}(s,t)
,
B^{\pm}(s,t)
,
and their partial derivatives with respect to
s
and
t
;
see (
3.53
)–(
III
).

(iii)

Our results for the amplitudes of
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
were then used to discuss the behavior of the corresponding cross section for
\omega\to 0
; see (
IV
).

(iv)

The formulas
(
3.53
)–(
3.58
)
which we give for the amplitudes in the limit

\omega\to 0
are valid both for real photons (
k^{2}=0
)
and for virtual photons (
k^{2}\neq 0
).

(v)

We emphasize that our results represent a strict theorem in the framework of QCD which applies at all c.m. energies
\sqrt{s}
.
Thus, the theorem can be tested experimentally both at low values of
\sqrt{s}
, available for instance
in the HADES experiment

Rathod_PhD_thesis

at GSI, Darmstadt, and at high energy pion-proton scattering.

We end with an outlook. Using the methods which we have developed
for
\pi p
scattering with soft-photon production one could also tackle proton-proton scattering with soft-photon production. We see no problem of principle there, except that the calculations will be very long and complex. Will there be at the end a theorem analogous to the
\pi p
scattering case? To answer this question will need much more work.
