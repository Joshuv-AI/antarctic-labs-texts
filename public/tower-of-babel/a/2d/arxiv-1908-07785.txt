Analysis of Impulsive φ \varphi –Hilfer Fractional Differential Equations


Abstract
This paper is concerned with the existence and uniqueness, and Ulam–Hyers stabilities of solutions of nonlinear impulsive φ \varphi –Hilfer fractional differential equations. Further, we investigate the dependence of the solution on the initial conditions, order of derivative and the functions involved in the equations. The outcomes are acquired in the space of weighted piecewise continuous functions by means of fixed point theorems and the generalized version of Gronwall inequality.

Existence results

In this section, we derive the existence of solution to the problem (
1.2
) by means of Schaefer’s fixed point theorem.

Theorem 3.1

Assume that:

(
H_{1}
)

The function
f:(a,T]\times\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is continuous and satisfy the following conditions:

(i)

f(\cdot,u(\cdot))\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
for any
u\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right),

(ii)

\left|f(t,u(t))-f(t,v(t))\right|\leq(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,|u(t)-v(t)|
,
t\in J
,
u,v\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right),

(
H_{2}
)

The functions
\mathcal{J}_{k}:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
, (
k=1,2,\cdots,m.
) satisfy the condition

(i)

\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))-\mathcal{J}_{k}(v(t_{k}^{-}))\right|\leq(\varphi(t_{k}^{-})-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,|u(t_{k}^{-})-v(t_{k}^{-})|
,
u\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\left(\mathcal{J},\,\mathbb{R}\right),

(ii)

there exist
\zeta_{k}>0
such that
|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))|\leq\zeta_{k}
,
u\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\left(\mathcal{J},\,\mathbb{R}\right)
.

Then, the problem (
1.2
) has at least one solution in the space
{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right).

Proof:

Consider the operator
\mathbb{F}
defined on
{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
by

(\mathbb{F}u)(t)=\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\left(u_{a}+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right)+\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,u(t)),~\text{ $t\in J$ }.

(3.1)

Then as proved in
[
36
]
,
\mathbb{F}
is mapping from

{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
to itself. Further, our problem of finding the solution to (
1.2
) is reduced to find a fixed point to the operator
\mathbb{F}
. Utilizing Schaefer fixed point theorem, we prove that the operator
\mathbb{F}
has fixed point, and the proof of same given in following four steps.

Step 1:
\mathbb{F}
is continuous.

Let
\{u_{n}\}
be a sequence such that
u_{n}\to u\,\text{in}~{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right).
Then for each
t\in J,

\displaystyle(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\left|(\mathbb{F}u_{n})(t)-(\mathbb{F}u)(t)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\left(u_{a}+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u_{n}(t_{k}^{-}))\right)+(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\,\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,u_{n}(t))\right.

\displaystyle\qquad\left.-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\,\left(u_{a}+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right)-(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,u(t))\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u_{n}(t_{k}^{-}))-\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right|

\displaystyle\qquad+(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\,\left|\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}\left(f(t,u_{n}(t))-f(t,u(t))\right)\right|

Since,
f
and
\mathcal{J}_{k},(k=1,\cdots,n)
are continuous functions, we have

\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u_{n}(t_{k}^{-}))-\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right|\to 0

and

\left|\left(f(t,u_{n}(t))-f(t,u(t))\right)\right|\to 0

as
n\to\infty
.
Therefore,

(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\,\left|(\mathbb{F}u_{n})(t)-(\mathbb{F}u)(t)\right|\to 0\,\text{as}\,n\to\infty,

which gives

\left\|\mathbb{F}u_{n}-\mathbb{F}u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\to 0\,\text{as}\,n\to\infty.

This proves the operator
\mathbb{F}:{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)\to{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)

is continuous.

Step 2:
\mathbb{F}
maps bounded sets into bounded sets.

To prove this we shall show that for
\delta>0
there exists
\eta>0
such that for any
u\in\mathbb{D}_{\delta}=\left\{u\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right):\left\|u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\leq\delta\right\}
one has
\left\|\mathbb{F}u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\leq\eta.

Let
\mathcal{M}^{*}=\sup_{s\in J}|f(s,0)|
and
\zeta=\sum_{k=1}^{m}\zeta_{k}
. Then, using the hypotheses
(H_{1})(ii)
and
(H_{2})(ii)
, for each
t\in J
, we have

\displaystyle(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}|(\mathbb{F}u)(t)|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right|+(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\left|\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,u(t))\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\zeta_{k}+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,\left|f(s,u(s))-f(s,0)\right|\,ds

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,\left|f(s,0)\right|\,ds

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{\zeta}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\left|u(s)\right|\,ds

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{\mathcal{M}^{*}(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,ds

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|+\zeta}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\left\|u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,ds

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{\mathcal{M}^{*}(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,ds

\displaystyle=\frac{|u_{a}|+\zeta}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma+\varrho}}{\Gamma(\varrho+1)}\left(\left\|u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}+\mathcal{M}^{*}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|+\zeta}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{(\varphi(T)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma+\varrho}}{\Gamma(\varrho+1)}\left(\delta+\mathcal{M}^{*}\right)

\displaystyle:=\eta.

This gives
\left\|\mathbb{F}u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\leq\eta.
Thus the proof of the operator
\mathbb{F}
maps bounded sets into bounded set is completed.

Step 3:
\mathbb{F}
is equicontinuous.

Let
u\in C_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
and
t_{1},t_{2}\in J
with
a<t_{1}<t_{2}<T,
we get

\displaystyle\left|(\mathbb{F}u)(t_{2})-(\mathbb{F}u)(t_{1})\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\left(u_{a}+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{2}}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right)+\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t_{2},u(t_{2}))\right.

\displaystyle\qquad\left.-\frac{(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\left(u_{a}+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{1}}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right)-\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t_{1},u(t_{1}))\right|

\displaystyle\leq|u_{a}|\,\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}-\frac{(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\right|

\displaystyle\qquad+\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{2}}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))-\frac{(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{1}}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t_{2}}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}|f(s,u(s))|\,ds

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t_{1}}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}|f(s,u(s))|\,ds

\displaystyle\leq|u_{a}|\,\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}-\frac{(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\right|+\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{2}-t_{1}}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-})\right|

\displaystyle\qquad-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t_{1}}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}\,(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}|f(s,u(s))|\,ds

\displaystyle\leq|u_{a}|\,\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}-\frac{(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\right|+\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{2}-t_{1}}\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-})\right|

\displaystyle\qquad+\left\|f\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\left(\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}-\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}\right)

\displaystyle=\leq|u_{a}|\,\left|\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}-\frac{(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\right|+\frac{(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t_{2}-t_{1}}\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-})\right|

\displaystyle\qquad+\left\|f\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\left\{\frac{\Gamma(\sigma)}{\Gamma(\varrho+\sigma)}\left[(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\varrho+\sigma-1}-(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\varrho+\sigma-1}\right]\right\}.

Therefore

\left|(\mathbb{F}u)(t_{2})-(\mathbb{F}u)(t_{1})\right|\to 0~\text{as}\,\,|t_{1}-t_{2}|\to 0.

This shows that
\mathbb{F}
is equi-continuous on
J

{\mathcal{PC}}_{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}
type of Arzela-Ascoli Theorem

Step 4: Finally, we show that the set

. Then

\displaystyle|u(t)|=|\lambda\,\mathbb{F}u(t)|<|\mathbb{F}u(t)|.

Let

Then, using hypotheses
(H_{1})(i)
and
(H_{2})(i)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-})-\mathcal{J}_{k}(o(t_{k}^{-}))\right|+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\left|\mathcal{J}_{k}(o(t_{k}^{-}))\right|

\displaystyle\qquad+(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\left|\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}\left(f(t,u(t))-f(t,0)\right)\right|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\left|\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,0)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|u_{a}|}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}(\varphi(t_{k})-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}|u(t_{k}^{-})|+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}|\mathcal{J}_{k}(0)|

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,\left|f(s,0)\right|\,ds

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{|u_{a}|}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{m\,\mathcal{N}^{*}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}+\frac{\mathcal{M}^{*}(\varphi(T)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma+\varrho}}{\Gamma(\varrho+1)}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}}{\Gamma(\varrho)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\varrho-1}\,(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\left|u(s)\right|\,ds

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}(\varphi(t_{k})-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}|u(t_{k}^{-})|.

2.3

\displaystyle\mathcal{U}(t)

This shows that the set

is bounded subset of

Hence by Schaefer’s fixed point theorem

\displaystyle\qquad+\|f\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}}\left\{\frac{\Gamma(\sigma)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\varrho+\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma+\varrho)}-\frac{\Gamma(\sigma)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\varrho-\delta+\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma+\varrho-\delta)}\right\}.

(4.9)

where

5
Ulam Stabilities of
\varphi
–HFDE

In this section, we investigate the Ulam–Hyers stabilities of the impulsive
\varphi
-HFDE (
1.2
).

Let
\tilde{u}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
,
\chi>0,~\epsilon>0
and
\theta:J\to\mathbb{R}
be a nondecreasing function. We consider the following inequalities:

\displaystyle\begin{cases}|^{H}\mathbf{D}^{\varrho,\nu;\,\varphi}_{a^{+}}\tilde{u}(t)-f(t,\tilde{u}(t))|\leq\epsilon,~t\in J\\
|\Delta\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\tilde{u}(t_{k})-\mathcal{J}_{k}(\tilde{u}(t_{k}^{-}))|\leq\epsilon,~k=1,2,\cdots,m,\end{cases}

(5.1)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}|^{H}\mathbf{D}^{\varrho,\nu;\,\varphi}_{a^{+}}\tilde{u}(t)-f(t,\tilde{u}(t))|\leq\theta(t),~t\in J\\
|\Delta\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\tilde{u}(t_{k})-\mathcal{J}_{k}(\tilde{u}(t_{k}^{-}))|\leq\chi,~k=1,2,\cdots,m\end{cases}

(5.2)

and

\displaystyle\begin{cases}|^{H}\mathbf{D}^{\varrho,\nu;\,\varphi}_{a^{+}}\tilde{u}(t)-f(t,\tilde{u}(t))|\leq\epsilon\,\theta(t),~t\in J\\
|\Delta\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\tilde{u}(t_{k})-\mathcal{J}_{k}(\tilde{u}(t_{k}^{-}))|\leq\epsilon\,\chi,~k=1,2,\cdots,m.\end{cases}

(5.3)

Definition 5.1

The problem (
1.2
) is said to be Ulam–Hyers (UH) stable if for
\epsilon>0
there exists a constant
C_{m,\varrho}>0
such that, for every solution
\tilde{u}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
of the inequality (
5.1
), there is a unique solution
u\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
to the problem (
1.2
) satisfying

\left\|\tilde{u}-u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\leq C_{m,\varrho}\,\epsilon,~t\in J.

Definition 5.2

The problem (
1.2
) is said to be generalized Ulam–Hyers (GUH) stable if
there exists
\phi_{m,\varrho}\in C(\mathbb{R^{+}},\mathbb{R^{+}})
with
\phi_{m,\varrho}(0)=0
such that for each solution
\tilde{u}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
of the inequality (
5.2
) there is a unique solution
u\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
to the problem (
1.2
) satisfying

\left\|\tilde{u}-u\right\|_{{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)}\leq\,\phi_{m,\varrho}(\epsilon),~t\in J.

Definition 5.3

The problem (
1.2
) is said to be Ulam–Hyers–Rassias (UHR) stable corresponding to
(\theta,\chi)
if for every
\epsilon>0
there exists a real number
C_{m,\varrho,\theta}>0
such that, for every solution
\tilde{u}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)

1.2
) satisfying

(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\left|\tilde{u}(t)-u(t)\right|\leq C_{m,\varrho,\theta}\,\epsilon\,(\theta(t)+\chi),~t\in J.

Definition 5.4

The problem (
1.2
) is said to be generalized Ulam–Hyers–Rassias (GUHR)stable corresponding to
(\theta,\chi)
if there exists a constant
C_{m,\varrho,\theta}>0
such that, for every solution
\tilde{u}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)
of the inequality (
5.2
),there is a unique solution

to the problem (

(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{1-\sigma}\,\left|\tilde{u}(t)-u(t)\right|\leq C_{m,\varrho,\theta}\,(\theta(t)+\chi),~~~t\in J.

Remark 5.1

The function
\tilde{u}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)

) if there is a function
\mathcal{E}\in{\mathcal{PC}}_{{1-\sigma};\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mathbb{R}\right)

with

(2)

{}^{H}\mathbf{D}^{\varrho,\,\nu;\,\varphi}_{a^{+}}\tilde{u}(t)=f(t,\tilde{u}(t))+\mathcal{E}(t),\,t\in J,

(3)

\Delta\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\tilde{u}(t_{k})=\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))+\mathcal{E}_{k},~k=1,2,\cdots,m.

Looking towards the Remark

\displaystyle\qquad+\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,\tilde{u}(t))+\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}\mathcal{E}(t).

(5.4)

Therefore, we have

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{u}(t)-\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\left(\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\tilde{u}(a)+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\mathcal{J}_{k}(\tilde{u}(t_{k}^{-}))\right)-\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,\tilde{u}(t))\right|

\varphi
-HFDE

5

and

\displaystyle|\tilde{u}(t)-u(t)|

\displaystyle=\left|\tilde{u}(t)-\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\sigma-1}}{\Gamma(\sigma)}\,\left(\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{1-\sigma;\,\varphi}\tilde{u}(a)+\sum_{a<t_{k}<t}\mathcal{J}_{k}(u(t_{k}^{-}))\right)-\mathbf{I}_{a^{+}}^{\varrho;\,\varphi}f(t,u(t))\right|
