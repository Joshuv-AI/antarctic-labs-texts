Abstract
In this paper, we mainly consider the stability of Φ S , F , H \Phi_{S,F,H} harmonic map and Φ T , F , H \Phi_{T,F,H} harmonic map from or into Φ \Phi -SSU manifold. We mainly consider the stability of Φ S , F , H \Phi_{S,F,H} harmonic map and Φ T , F , H \Phi_{T,F,H} harmonic map from or into compact convex hypersurface. We also give some Theorems to know when a manifold is Φ S , F , H \Phi_{S,F,H} -stable or Φ S , F , H \Phi_{S,F,H} -unstable.
