Crossed product interpretation of the Double Shuffle Lie algebra attached to a finite Abelian group
YADDADEN Khalef

Abstract
. Racinet studied the scheme associated with the double shuffle and regularization relations between multiple polylogarithm values at N t ​ h N^{th} roots of unity and constructed a group scheme attached to the situation; he also showed it to be the specialization for G = μ N G=\mu_{N} of a group scheme 𝖣𝖬𝖱 0 G \mathsf{DMR}_{0}^{G} attached to a finite abelian group G G . Then, Enriquez and Furusho proved that 𝖣𝖬𝖱 0 G \mathsf{DMR}_{0}^{G} can be essentially identified with the stabilizer of a coproduct element arising in Racinet’s theory with respect to the action of a group of automorphisms of a free Lie algebra attached to G G . We reformulate Racinet’s construction in terms of crossed products. Racinet’s coproduct can then be identified with a coproduct Δ ^ G ℳ \hat{\Delta}^{\mathcal{M}}_{G} defined on a module ℳ ^ G \hat{\mathcal{M}}_{G} over an algebra 𝒲 ^ G \hat{\mathcal{W}}_{G} , which is equipped with its own coproduct Δ ^ G 𝒲 \hat{\Delta}^{\mathcal{W}}_{G} , and the group action on ℳ ^ G \hat{\mathcal{M}}_{G} extends to a compatible action of 𝒲 ^ G \hat{\mathcal{W}}_{G} . We then show that the stabilizer of Δ ^ G ℳ \hat{\Delta}^{\mathcal{M}}_{G} , hence 𝖣𝖬𝖱 0 G \mathsf{DMR}_{0}^{G} , is contained in the stabilizer of Δ ^ G 𝒲 \hat{\Delta}^{\mathcal{W}}_{G} . This yields an explicit group scheme containing 𝖣𝖬𝖱 0 G \mathsf{DMR}_{0}^{G} , which we also express in the Racinet formalism.
