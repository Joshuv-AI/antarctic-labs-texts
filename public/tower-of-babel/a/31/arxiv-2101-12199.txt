Unusual heat transport of the Kitaev material Na 2 Co 2 TeO 6 : putative quantum spin liquid and low-energy spin excitations
Xiǎochén Hóng, 1 , 2 , , {}^{1,2,}{}^{,} Matthias Gillig, 1,∗ Richard Hentrich, 1,∗ Weiliang Yao, 3 Vilmos Kocsis, 1 Arthur R. Witte, 1,4 Tino Schreiner, 1 Danny Baumann, 1 Nicolás Pérez, 1 Anja U. B. Wolter, 1 Yuan Li, 3,5, Bernd Büchner, 1,4,6 and Christian Hess 1,2,6,

Abstract
We studied the field dependent thermal conductivity ( κ \kappa ) of Na 2 Co 2 TeO 6 , a compound considered as the manifestation of the Kitaev model based on the high-spin d 7 d^{7} Co 2+ ions. We found that in-plane magnetic fields beyond a critical value B c ≈ B_{c}\approx 10 T are able to drastically enhance κ \kappa at low temperatures, resulting in a double-peak structure of κ ⁡ ( T ) \kappa(T) that closely resembles the behavior of α \alpha -RuCl 3 . This result suggests that heat transport in Na 2 Co 2 TeO 6 is primarily phononic, and it is strongly affected by scattering from magnetic excitations that are highly tunable by external fields. Interestingly, for magnetic fields B / ⁣ / a \textbf{B}\mathbin{\!/\mkern-5.0mu/\!}\textbf{a} (i.e., along the zigzag direction of the Co-Co bonds), there is an extended field range which separates the long-range magnetic order for B ≤ B c ≈ 10 B\leq B_{c}\approx 10 T and the partially spin-polarized gapped high-field phase for B ≳ 12 B\gtrsim 12 T. The low-energy phonon scattering is particularly strong in this field range, consistent with the notion that the system becomes a quantum spin liquid with prominent spin fluctuations down to energies of no more than 2 meV.
