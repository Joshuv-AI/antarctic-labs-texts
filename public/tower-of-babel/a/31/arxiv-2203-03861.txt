4 ​ D 4D AdS Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet black hole with Yang-Mills field and its thermodynamics
Dharm Veer Singh, Benoy Kumar Singh, Sudhaker Upadhyay

Abstract
We derive an exact black hole solution for the Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity with Yang-Mills field in 4 ​ D 4D AdS spacetime and investigate its thermodynamic properties to calculate exact expressions for the black hole mass, temperature, entropy and heat capacity. The thermodynamic quantities get modification in the presence of Yang-Mills field, however, entropy remains unaffected by the Yang-Mills charge. The solution exhibits P − v P\--v criticality and belongs to the universality class of Van der Waals fluid. We study the effect of Gauss-Bonnet coupling and Yang-Mills charge on the critical behaviour and black hole phase transition. We observe that the values of critical exponents increase with the Yang-Mills charge and decrease with the Gauss-Bonnet coupling constant.

V
Critical Exponents

In this section, we compute the critical exponent, which is a universal property of phase transition. Usually, near the critical point, a Van der Waals like phase transition is characterized by the four critical exponents
\alpha_{0}
,
\beta
,
\gamma
and
\delta
describing the behaviour of the specific
heat, order
parameter
\eta
, the
isothermal compressibility
\kappa_{T}
and the
critical isotherm, respectively. These are defined as

\displaystyle C_{v}=T\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\propto|\tau|^{-\alpha_{0}},\qquad\qquad\eta=v_{l}-v_{s}\propto|\tau|^{\beta},

(37)

\displaystyle\kappa_{T}=-\frac{1}{v}\frac{\partial v}{\partial p}\propto|\tau|^{-\gamma},\quad\qquad|P-P_{c}|_{T_{c}}\propto|v-v_{c}|^{\delta},

(38)

where
\tau=\frac{T-T_{c}}{T_{c}}
and
v_{l}
and
v_{s}
denote specific volume of large and small black holes, respectively.

Now, with the following definitions:

p=\frac{P}{P_{c}},\qquad\qquad t=\frac{T}{T_{c}}=\tau+1,\qquad\text{and}\qquad\lambda=\frac{v}{v_{c}},

(39)

the equation of state translate into so-called law of corresponding states and reads

\frac{8p}{3}=\frac{t}{\lambda}-\frac{t\alpha_{0}}{6\lambda^{3}}-\frac{}{}\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}+\frac{2\alpha_{0}}{\lambda}+\frac{2\nu^{2}}{36\lambda^{4}}\right).

(40)

Let us calculate the critical exponents predicted by the corresponding states (
40
). We define

\qquad\qquad\omega=\frac{v-v_{c}}{v_{c}}=\lambda-1.

To find the critical exponents we expend equation of state near the critical points as following:

(42)

If the black hole undergoes the phase transition from small to large one keeping temperature and pressure

\omega_{s}

Moreover, during the phase transition, the Maxwell’s area law also holds, i.e.,

\displaystyle\int_{\omega_{l}}^{\omega_{s}}\omega dP=\omega\left(a_{11}\tau+a_{30}\omega^{2}\right)dP=0.

Hence we have the order parameter
\eta

VI
Discussions and Conclusions

We have found an exact solution for
4D
EGB black hole in the presence of Yang-Mills field which is a generalization of the solution obtained by Glavan and Lin
gla
. Also, one recovers the Schwarzschild AdS solution for
\alpha\rightarrow 0
,
\nu\rightarrow 0
and
l\rightarrow\infty
.
Furthermore, we have investigated the thermodynamics behaviour of the solution. Here, we have observed that the thermodynamics quantities get modification due to the presence of Yang-Mills charge
\nu
. However, the entropy of the black hole remains unaffected by the Yang-Mills charge and turns out to be the Bekenstein-Hawking area-law for the novel
4D
EGB black hole. In order to discuss the stability of the black hole we derive the heat capacity. The heat capacity

diverges at a critical radius

We also examined the

AdS EGB black hole solution with Yang-Mills charge. We have found that a small-large black hole phase transition occurs for the case where temperature is less than the critical temperature, i.e.,

. The critical exponents are also calculated which increase with the Yang-Mills charge
\nu
and decrease with the GB coupling constant
