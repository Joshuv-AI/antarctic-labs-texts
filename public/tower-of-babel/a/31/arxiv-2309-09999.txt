A renewal approach to prove the Four Color Theorem unplugged Part II: R/G/B Kempe chains in an extremum non-4-colorable MPG
Shu-Chung Liu

Abstract
. This is the second part of three episodes to demonstrate a renewal approach for proving the Four Color Theorem without checking by a computer. The first and the third episodes have subtitles: “RGB-tilings on maximal planar graphs” and “Diamond routes, canal lines and Σ \Sigma -adjustments,” where R/G/B stand for red, green and blue colors to paint on edges and an MPG stands for a maximal planar graph. We focus on an extremum non-4-colorable MPG E ​ P EP in the whole paper. In this second part, we refresh the false proof on E ​ P EP by Kempe for the Four Color Theorem. And then using single color tilings or RGB-tilings on E ​ P EP , we offer a renewal point of view through R/G/B Kempe chains to enhance our coloring skill, either in vertex-colorings or in edge-colorings. We discover many fundamental theorems associated with R-/RGB-tilings and 4-colorability; an adventure study on One Piece, which is either an MPG or an n n -semi-MPG; many if-and-only-if statements for E ​ P − { e } EP-\{e\} by using Type A or Type B e e -diamond and Kempe chains. This work started on May 31, 2018 and was first announced by the author [ 1 ] on Jan. 22, 2020, when the pandemic just occurred.
