Abstract

We have defined almost separable space. We show that like separability, almost separability is
c
productive and converse also true under some restrictions. We establish a Baire Category theorem like result in Hausdorff, Pseudocompacts spaces. We investigate few relationships among separability, almost separability, sequential separability, strongly sequential separability.

We know that separability is not a hereditary property. Niemytzky’s plane is a well known example. Same is true about almost separability as well. Niemytzky’s plane provides an example in this case also.

We now finish our paper giving relations among the different types of separability notions which are defined earlier.

\Rightarrow
Sequentially separable
\Rightarrow
Separable
\Rightarrow
Almost separable.
