An SPDE approach to perturbation theory of Φ 2 4 \Phi^{4}_{2} : asymptoticity and short distance behavior
Hao Shen, Rongchan Zhu, Xiangchan Zhu

Abstract
. In this paper we study the perturbation theory of Φ 2 4 \Phi^{4}_{2} model on the whole plane via stochastic quantization. We use integration by parts formula (i.e. Dyson-Schwinger equations) to generate the perturbative expansion for the k k -point correlation functions, and prove bounds on the remainder of the truncated expansion using PDE estimates; this in particular proves that the expansion is asymptotic. Furthermore, we derive short distance behaviors of the 2 2 -point function and the connected 4 4 -point function, also via suitable Dyson-Schwinger equations combined with PDE arguments.
