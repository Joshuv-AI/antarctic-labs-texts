On the discrete analogues of Appell function F 3 F_{3}
Ravi Dwivedi, Vivek Sahai

Abstract
The present paper is devoted to the study of Appell hypergeometric function F 3 F_{3} from discrete point of view. We mainly introduce two generalized discrete forms of F 3 F_{3} and study their basic properties viz. regions of convergence, difference-differential equations, integral representations, finite and infinite sums, recursion formulae, to name a few. AMS Subject Classification: 33C65, 39A12. Keywords: Appell functions, discrete hypergeometric functions.

The Appell function
F_{3}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{m}\,(a^{\prime})_{n}\,(b)_{m}\,(b^{\prime})_{n}}{(c)_{m+n}}\,\frac{x^{m}\,y^{n}}{m!\,n!},\quad|x|,\,|y|<1;

(1.1)

\displaystyle\varXi_{2}\left(a_{1},b_{1};c;x,y\right)

(1.3)

In this paper, we explore these functions from discrete point of view. In particular, we give two discrete forms of Appell function

\displaystyle F_{{l_{2}}:{l^{\prime}_{2}},{l^{\prime\prime}_{2}}}^{{l_{1}}:{l^{\prime}_{1}},{l^{\prime\prime}_{1}}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}A:&B,&C\\
D:&E,&F\end{array};x,\ y\right)

For convergence of the discrete function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}
, we consider its general term
\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle<\left|\frac{\Gamma(c)}{\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b_{1})\,\Gamma(b_{2})\,\Gamma(-t_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\quad\times\left|\frac{\Gamma(a+m+n)\,\Gamma(b_{1}+m)\,\Gamma(b_{2}+n)\,\Gamma(-t_{1}+mk_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2}+nk_{2})}{\Gamma(c+m+n)\,\Gamma(m+1)\,\Gamma(n+1)}\right|\,|x|^{m}\,|y|^{n}.

(2.11)

The Stirling formula
\lim_{n\to\infty}\Gamma(\lambda+n)=\sqrt{(}2\pi)\,n^{\lambda+n-\frac{1}{2}}\,e^{-n}

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle<\left|\frac{\Gamma(c)}{\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b_{1})\,\Gamma(b_{2})\,\Gamma(-t_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\quad\times\left|2\pi\,(m+n)^{a-c}\,m^{b_{1}-1}\,n^{b_{2}-1}\,(mk_{1})^{mk_{1}-t_{1}-\frac{1}{2}}\,(nk_{2})^{nk_{2}-t_{2}-\frac{1}{2}}e^{-(mk_{1}+nk_{2})}\right|\,|x|^{m}\,|y|^{n}.

(2.12)

N>\left|\frac{2\pi\,\Gamma(c)}{\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b_{1})\,\Gamma(b_{2})\,\Gamma(-t_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|<\frac{N\,(mk_{1})^{mk_{1}-t_{1}-\frac{1}{2}}\,(nk_{2})^{nk_{2}-t_{2}-\frac{1}{2}}}{(m+n)^{c-a}\,m^{1-b_{1}}\,n^{1-b_{2}}\,e^{mk_{1}+nk_{2}}}\,|x|^{m}\,|y|^{n}.

(2.13)

\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}

2.1
Difference equations

Let
\Theta_{t}:=t\,\rho_{t}\,\Delta_{t}
, where
\Delta_{t}f(t)=f(t+1)-f(t)
and
\rho_{t}f(t)=f(t-1)
, be the difference operator. It can be readily verified that

\displaystyle\Theta_{t}\,((-1)^{nk}\,(-t)_{nk})

\displaystyle=n\,k\,(-1)^{nk}\,(-t)_{nk}.

(2.14)

We have the following theorem:

Theorem 2.1
.

The discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}
satisfy the following difference equations:

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{t_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+c-1\right)-k_{1}\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+a_{1}\right)\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+b_{1}\right)\right]\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}=0;

(2.15)

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{t_{2}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+c-1\right)-k_{2}\,(-1)^{k_{2}}(-t_{2})_{k_{2}}\,y\,\rho_{t_{2}}^{k_{2}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a_{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+b_{2}\right)\right]\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}=0.

(2.16)

Proof.

To prove equation (
2.15
), we start with

\displaystyle\Theta_{t_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+c-1\right)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a_{1})_{m}\,(a_{2})_{n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}\,mk_{1}\,(c+m+n-1)

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,\sum_{m,n\geq 0}(a_{1}+m)\,(b_{1}+m)\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\frac{(a_{1})_{m}\,(a_{2})_{n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(mk_{1}+nk_{2})}\,(-t_{1})_{(m+1)k_{1}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+a\right)\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+b_{1}\right)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}.

(2.17)

Thus, we arrive at (
2.15
). Similarly the equation (
2.16
) can be proved.
∎

3
Differential and difference formulae

For difference operator
\Delta_{t}
defined by
\Delta_{t}f(t)=f(t+1)-f(t)
, we have
\Delta_{t}[(-1)^{k}\,(-t)_{k}]=k\,(-1)^{k-1}\,(-t)_{k-1}
. For differential operators
\theta=x\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x},\phi=y\,\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
, we have the following theorem:

Theorem 3.1
.

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, following difference and differential formulae are satisfied by discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}
:

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a_{1})_{r}\,(b_{1})_{r}\,x^{r}}{(c)_{r}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+r,a_{2},b_{1}+r,b_{2};c+r;t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y);

(3.1)

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{2}})^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},1,x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a_{2})_{r}\,(b_{2})_{r}\,y^{r}}{(c)_{r}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2}+r,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c+r;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},1,x,y);

(3.2)

\displaystyle(\theta)^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{rk_{1}}\,(a_{1})_{r}\,(b_{1})_{r}\,(-t_{1})_{rk_{1}}\,x^{r}}{(c)_{r}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+r,a_{2},b_{1}+r,b_{2};c+r;t_{1}-rk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.3)

\displaystyle(\phi)^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{rk_{2}}\,(a_{2})_{r}\,(b_{2})_{r}\,(-t_{2})_{rk_{2}}\,y^{r}}{(c)_{r}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2}+r,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c+r;t_{1},t_{2}-rk_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.4)

Proof.

The proofs of these differential formulae are very elementary and hence we give here only the proof of (
3.1
). The rest can be verified in a similar way. The action of difference operator
\Delta_{t_{1}}
on the discrete function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}
yields

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a_{1})_{m}\,(a_{2})_{n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{m-1}\,m\,(-t_{1})_{m-1}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a_{1})_{m+1}\,(a_{2})_{n}\,(b_{1})_{m+1}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{m}\,(-t_{1})_{m}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n+1}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m+1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=x\,\frac{a_{1}\,b_{1}}{c}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a_{1}+1)_{m}\,(a_{2})_{n}\,(b_{1}+1)_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{m}\,(-t_{1})_{m}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=x\,\frac{a_{1}\,b_{1}}{c}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+1,a_{2},b_{1}+1,b_{2};c+1;t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y).

(3.5)

Now, acting the difference operator twice leads to

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})^{2}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

It completes the proof.

Now, we establish some finite and infinite summation formulae in terms of discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}
in the following theorem.

Theorem 4.1
.

Following summation formulas hold true

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1}+r,b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\frac{(a_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c)_{s}}\,x^{s}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+s,a_{2},b_{1}+s,b_{2};c+s;t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(4.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2}+r;c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

(4.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1})_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+r,a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

(4.3)

Proof.

To prove (
4.1
), we start by applying the Leibnitz rule and get

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}\left[x^{b_{1}+r-1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\left[\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r-s}\,x^{b_{1}+r-1}\right]\,\left[\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{s}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}(-1)^{r-s}\,(1-b_{1}+s)_{r-s}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a_{1})_{s}\,(b_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c)_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+s,a_{2},b_{1}+s,b_{2};c+s;t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\,(-1)^{r-s}\frac{(-1)^{s}\,(1-b_{1}-r)_{r}}{(b_{1})_{s}}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a_{1})_{s}\,(b_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c)_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+s,a_{2},b_{1}+s,b_{2};c+s;t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(b_{1})_{r}\,\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c)_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{1}(a_{1}+s,a_{2},b_{1}+s,b_{2};c+s;t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.4)

Taking into account the equation (
3.8
), we have

\displaystyle x^{b_{1}-1}\,(b_{1})_{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1}+r,b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(b_{1})_{r}\,\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c)_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2}+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c+s;t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.5)

This implies

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1}+r,b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\frac{(a_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c)_{s}}\,x^{s}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+s,a_{2},b_{1}+s,b_{2};c+s;t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.6)

This completes the proof. A similar procedure can be used to get equation (
4.2
). To prove (
4.3
), we start with the right hand side of the equation and thus get

\displaystyle(1-z)^{-a_{1}}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}\left(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},\frac{x}{1-z},y\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}(1-z)^{-(a_{1}+m)}\,\frac{(a_{1})_{m}\,(a_{2})_{n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}.

Using the binomial theorem
(1-z)^{-(a_{1}+m)}=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1}+m)_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}
and identity
(a_{1})_{m+r}=(a_{1})_{m}\,(a_{1}+m)_{r}=(a_{1})_{r}\,(a_{1}+r)_{m}

\displaystyle(1-z)^{-a_{1}}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}\left(a_{1},a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},\frac{x}{1-z},y\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\,\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1})_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\frac{(a_{1}+r)_{m}\,(a_{2})_{n}(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c)_{m+n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1})_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}(a_{1}+r,a_{2},b_{1},b_{2};c;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.8)

It completes the proof.

7
Conclusion

The present paper is based on the study of two distinct discrete forms of Appell function
F_{3}
. We obtain their regions of convergence, difference-differential relations, integral representations, finite and infinite summation formulas, recursion relations etc. We also introduced the discrete analogues of Humbert functions
\varXi_{1}
and
\varXi_{2}
as limiting cases of discrete Appell functions
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{3}
and
\mathcal{F}^{(2)}_{3}
.
The particular cases of the results and identities obtained in this paper lead to the corresponding results for Appell function
F_{3}
and Kampé de Fériet function. A few of them are already available in the literature but some are believed to be new.
