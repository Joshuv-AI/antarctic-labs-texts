On the μ \mu -invariants of residually reducible Galois representations
Anwesh Ray, R. Sujatha

Abstract
. The Iwasawa μ \mu -invariant of the Selmer group of a residually reducible Galois representation arising from a Hecke eigencuspform is studied. Furthermore, certain Iwasawa-invariants refining the μ \mu -invariant are defined and analyzed. As an application, we show that given a reducible mod- p p Galois representation ρ ¯ \bar{\rho} and any choice of integer N ≥ 1 N\geq 1 , there is a modular Galois representation lifting ρ ¯ \bar{\rho} whose associated Selmer group has μ \mu -invariant ≥ N \geq N . This is a refinement of Serre’s conjecture in the residually reducible case.



1.
Introduction

Iwasawa theory of Galois representations associated to elliptic curves and modular forms encodes deep arithmetic information. Greenberg analyzed the algebraic structure of the Selmer groups associated to ordinary Galois representations over the cyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of a number field (see
[
5
]
). An important invariant associated with the Selmer group is its
\mu
-invariant. It is conjectured that when the residual representation is irreducible, then the Iwasawa
\mu
-invariant of
p
-primary Selmer group is zero, see
[
6
, Conjecture 1.11]
. Let
E
be an elliptic curve over a number field
F
and
\operatorname{Sel}(E/F^{\operatorname{cyc}})
be the
p
-primary Selmer group over the cyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
F
. Mazur gave examples of elliptic curves
E
for which the residual representation
E[p]
is reducible as a Galois module and the
\mu
-invariant of
\operatorname{Sel}(E/F^{\operatorname{cyc}})
is positive, see
[
12
, section 10]
. Drinen showed that to every finite Galois submodule
\alpha
of
E[p^{\infty}]
, there is an integer
\delta(\alpha)\geq 0
which contributes to the
\mu
-invariant of
\operatorname{Sel}(E/F^{\operatorname{cyc}})
, see
[
3
, Theorem 2.1]
.

Denote by
\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}
the absolute Galois group
\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})
and fix an embedding
\iota:\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\hookrightarrow\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}
. Let
f=\sum_{n\geq 1}a_{n}e^{2\pi i\tau}
be a normalized Hecke eigencuspform. Associated to
f
is the Galois representation
\rho_{f}:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{2}(K)
, where
K
is the finite extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
generated by
\iota(a_{n})
for positive integers
n
. Let
\mathcal{O}
be the valuation ring of
K
and let
T
be a
\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}
-stable
\mathcal{O}
-lattice and set
\rho_{T}:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{2}(\mathcal{O})
to be the Galois representation on
T
. As is well known, any elliptic curve
E
over
\mathbb{Q}
coincides with an eigencuspform
f
of weight
2
with rational Fourier coefficients. The Galois representation on the
p
-adic Tate module of
E
then coincides with the
p
-adic Galois representation associated to
f
. We prove our results for Selmer groups associated to
p
-ordinary Hecke eigencuspforms of weight
k\geq 2
. The Selmer groups considered here are defined over admissible, pro-
p
,
p
-adic Lie extensions
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
, with Galois group
\operatorname{G}:=\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}/\mathbb{Q})
(see
[
20
, section 2]
). In this case, the Iwasawa algebra
\Lambda(\operatorname{G}):=\mathcal{O}[[\operatorname{G}]]
is a left and right noetherian local domain. Further, it is known that
\Lambda(\operatorname{G})
is an Auslander regular ring
[
23
, Theorem 3.26]
. There is a dimension theory associated to finitely generated modules over such rings.

In this article, we will study the
\mu
-invariant of the
p
-primary Selmer group over
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
. Furthermore, certain refinements of the
\mu
-invariant are defined and studied (see Definition
2.4
). Throughout, it is assumed that the residual representation
\bar{\rho}_{T}:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{2}(\mathcal{O}/\varpi)
is reducible. Note that the
\mu
-invariant depends on the choice of the
\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}
-stable lattice
T
. When the Galois representation arises from an elliptic curve, this lattice is taken to be the
p
-adic Tate module. Our results show that the
\mu
-invariant depends crucially on the residual representation. We classify the residual representation
\bar{\rho}_{T}
into two types:
aligned
or
skew
(see Definition
3.1
). We prove results on the
\mu
-invariants in the two mutually disjoint cases. The first example of an elliptic curve with positive
\mu
-invariant was provided by Mazur
[
12
, Example 2]
. This example is residually aligned. It is shown that if
\rho
is residually reducible and aligned, then
\mu
is positive (cf. Theorem
3.5
), furthermore, it is shown that the
\mu
-multiplicity is
1
(cf. Definition
2.4
and Theorem

).

Here is a brief outline of how our results fit into a broader framework of known results and conjectures on the
\mu
-invariants of Selmer groups of elliptic curves. Greenberg conjectured that any elliptic curve over
\mathbb{Q}
is isogenous to one for which the
\mu
-invariant of the Selmer group is zero. It is shown that an elliptic curve whose Galois representation is residually reducible and aligned is isogenous to one whose Galois representation is residually reducible and skew, see Proposition
3.2
. Indeed, the
\mu
-invariant is positive in the residually aligned case, and in the residually skew case, examples indicate that the
\mu
-invariant vanishes. An explicit example is given in section
7
which illustrates this. This can be viewed as a justification for Greenberg’s conjecture. More generally, we study the case when
\rho_{T/n}:=\rho_{T}\mod{\varpi^{n}}
is
reducible
for an integer
n>0
. This means that there are characters
\varphi_{i,n}:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{1}(\mathcal{O}/\varpi^{n})
lifting
\varphi_{i}
for
i=1,2
, such that
\rho_{T/n}\simeq\left({\begin{array}[]{cc}\varphi_{1,n}&\ast\\
0&\varphi_{2,n}\\
\end{array}}\right)
. It is shown that if
\bar{\rho}_{T}
is aligned and

is such that
\rho_{T/n}
is reducible, then the
\mu
-invariant of the
p
-primary Selmer group of
\rho_{T}
is at least
n
. In practice, it is hard to find examples of Galois representations arising from elliptic curves which are reducible modulo
p^{2}
. However, there are modular forms for which the associated Galois representation is reducible modulo a high power of the uniformizer, for instance, the proof of Theorem
1.1
gives an explicit construction of modular Galois representations that are reducible modulo a high power of the uniformizer. We also prove more precise results for the refined
\mu
-invariants (see Theorem
3.5
).

Here is an interesting application of our analysis of
\mu
-invariants to the deformation theory of Galois representations. Applying the results of Skinner and Wiles
[
21
]
, Hamblen and Ramakrishna in
[
8
]
proved Serre’s conjecture for residually reducible Galois representations which satisfy some additional conditions. Let
\mathbb{F}
be a finite field of characteristic
p
. The ring of Witt vectors
\operatorname{W}(\mathbb{F})
is the valuation ring of the unique unramified extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
with residue field
\mathbb{F}
. Denote by
\bar{\chi}
the mod-
p
cyclotomic character. A character
\kappa:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{1}(\mathbb{F})
is
odd
if
\kappa(c)=-1
, where

denotes complex conjugation. The character
\kappa
is

otherwise.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
N
be a non-negative integer. Let
\bar{\rho}:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{F})
be a Galois representation such that
\bar{\rho}\simeq\left({\begin{array}[]{cc}\varphi&\ast\\
0&1\\
\end{array}}\right)
for a character
\varphi:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{1}(\mathbb{F})
. Assume that the following conditions are satisfied:

(1)

p\geq 5
,
\varphi
is odd,

(2)

\bar{\rho}
is indecomposable,

(3)

\varphi^{2}\neq 1,\varphi\neq\bar{\chi}^{-1},\varphi_{\restriction\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}}\neq 1,\varphi_{\restriction\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}}\neq\bar{\chi}_{\restriction\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}}
and the image of
\varphi
spans
\mathbb{F}
over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
.

Let
k
be a positive integer such that
k\equiv 2\mod{p^{N}(p-1)}
. Then there is a Galois representation

\rho_{T}:\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{2}(\operatorname{W}(\mathbb{F}))

which lifts
\bar{\rho}
such that:

(i)

\rho_{T}
lifts
\bar{\rho}
, i.e.,
\bar{\rho}_{T}\simeq\bar{\rho}
,

(ii)

\rho_{T}
arises from a
p
-ordinary Hecke eigencuspform
f
of weight
k
and a choice of
\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}}
-stable lattice
T
which is residually aligned,

(iii)

the
p
-primary Selmer group associated to
\rho_{T}
has
\mu\geq N
.

Thus one may not only lift a Galois representation
\bar{\rho}
to one arising from a modular form, but one can also arrange the
\mu
-invariant to be as large as possible. The above result is a refinement of Serre’s conjecture in the residually reducible case and significantly extends the main result of Hamblen-Ramakrishna
[
8
]
. It crucially relies on our results on
\mu
-invariants proved in sections
3
and
4
of this paper. We emphasize that our methods provide an explicit lift of the actual representation
\bar{\rho}
, rather than that of the semisimplification of
\bar{\rho}
. This is a stronger statement altogether and is indeed crucial when it comes to showing that the lift has
\mu
-invariant
\geq N
, since the nature of the
\mu
-invariant depends on the actual residual representation
\bar{\rho}
. As a result, we do not work with pseudo-representations, and instead resort to a purely Galois theoretic construction.

The lifting theorem above may be contrasted with the result of Bellaïche and Pollack
[
1
]
, who study the variation of
\mu
-invariants in a Hida family of tame level
N=1
, hence, for Galois representations unramified away from
\{p\}
. Our approach relies on a purely Galois theoretic construction and we make no assumption on the set of primes at which
\bar{\rho}
is ramified.

The paper is organized into 7 sections. Certain preliminary notions are discussed in section
2
. It is in this section that the refined
\mu
-invariants are introduced. In sections
3
and
4
, results are proved that characterize the
\mu
-invariants of Selmer groups associated to residually reducible Galois representations arising from Hecke eigencuspforms. In section
5
, necessary preparations are made for the proof of Theorem
1.1
. In section
6
, Theorem
1.1
is proved. Examples are discussed in section
7
.

Let

Let

Lemma 2.2
.

With notation as above, the choice of

and

Proof.

Suppose that

is an
\mathcal{O}
-module basis for
T

. With respect to this basis, the local Galois representation is given by

\rho_{T}|_{\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}}=\left({\begin{array}[]{cc}\chi^{k-1}\gamma_{1}&\alpha\\
0&\gamma_{2}\\
\end{array}}\right).

Therefore, for
g\in\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}
, we have that

ge_{1}=\chi^{k-1}(g)\gamma_{1}(g)e_{1}\text{ and }ge_{2}=\alpha(g)e_{1}+\gamma_{2}(g)e_{2}.

Consider another choice of
\hat{T}^{+}
and
\hat{T}^{-}

\mathcal{O}
-lattice basis of

\rho_{T}|_{\operatorname{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}}=\left({\begin{array}[]{cc}\chi^{k-1}\hat{\gamma}_{1}&\hat{\alpha}\\
0&\hat{\gamma}_{2}\\
\end{array}}\right).

Writing
\hat{e}_{1}=a\hat{e}_{1}+b\hat{e}_{2}
, one obtains

\begin{split}&g\hat{e}_{1}=\chi^{k-1}(g)\hat{\gamma}_{1}(g)\hat{e}_{1}=a\chi^{k-1}(g)\hat{\gamma}_{1}(g)e_{1}+b\chi^{k-1}(g)\hat{\gamma}_{1}(g)e_{2},\\
&g\hat{e}_{1}=age_{1}+bge_{2}=(a\chi^{k-1}(g)\gamma_{1}(g)+b\alpha(g))e_{1}+b\gamma_{2}(g)e_{2}.\end{split}

Comparing the coefficients of

, we have that

(2.2)

b\chi^{k-1}(g)\hat{\gamma}_{1}(g)=b\gamma_{2}(g).

Let

be in the inertia group of

such that

)

Proposition 6.3
.

Let

The proof is by induction. Let

(1)

\end{array}}\right)

(2)

is of the form
\left({\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{n+1}&\ast\\
0&1\\
\end{array}}\right)
.

Note that each local representation

(\operatorname{Id}+p^{n}\iota(f))r_{n+1}
satisfies the condition
\mathcal{C}_{v}
at each prime
v\in X_{0}
. Note that
r_{n+1}=\left({\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{n+1}&\ast\\
