6
Discussion

In this article we have constructed a new class of the consistent cubic interactions for
\mathcal{N}=2
higher-spin gauge supermultiplets. It was shown, that
\mathcal{N}=2
cubic vertex
(\mathbf{s_{1}},\mathbf{s_{2}},\mathbf{s_{2}})
for
\mathbf{s_{1}}\geq 2\mathbf{s_{2}}
has the universal structure:

S_{int}=\int d^{4}xd^{8}\theta du\;\left(\Psi^{-\alpha(s_{1}-1)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-2)}\mathcal{D}^{+}_{\alpha}+\bar{\Psi}^{-\alpha(s_{1}-2)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-1)}\bar{\mathcal{D}}^{+}_{\dot{\alpha}}\right)\mathcal{J}_{\alpha(s_{1}-2)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-2)},\vskip-5.69046pt

(6.1)

where
\Psi^{-}_{\alpha(s_{1}-1)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-2)}
,
\bar{\Psi}^{-}_{\alpha(s_{1}-2)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-1)}
are the Mezincescu-type higher-spin prepotentials defined by (
3.2
), and
\mathcal{J}_{\alpha(s_{1}-2)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-2)}
are the principle gauge-invariant
\mathcal{N}=2
supercurrents, which satisfy reality and harmonic independence conditions and the conservation equations
\mathcal{D}^{+\alpha}\mathcal{J}_{\alpha(s_{1}-2)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-2)}\approx 0
. We also explicitly constructed spin
\mathbf{s_{1}}
(
s_{1}\geq 2s_{2}
)
\mathcal{N}=2
higher-spin principle supercurrent for an arbitrary
\mathcal{N}=2
off-shell gauge spin
\mathbf{s_{2}}
supermultiplet

\begin{split}&\mathcal{J}_{\alpha(s_{1}-2)\dot{\alpha}(s_{1}-2)}=\;\;(i)^{s_{1}-2s_{2}}\sum_{p=0}^{s_{1}-2s_{2}}(-1)^{p}\frac{\binom{s_{1}-2}{2s_{2}-2+p}\binom{s_{1}-2}{p}}{\binom{s_{1}-2}{2s_{2}-2}}\;\partial^{p}\mathcal{W}_{\alpha(2s_{2}-2)}\partial^{s_{1}-2s_{2}-p}\bar{\mathcal{W}}_{\dot{\alpha}(2s_{2}-2)}\\

To construct vertices we used two types of
