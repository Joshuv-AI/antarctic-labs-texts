K. MAHESH KRISHNA

Abstract




Abstract
: We prove the continuous non-Archimedean (resp. p-adic) Banach space and Hilbert space versions of non-Archimedean (resp. p-adic) Welch bounds proved by M. Krishna. We formulate continuous non-Archimedean and p-adic functional Zauner conjectures.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
84
]
(
Welch Bounds
)
Let
n>d
. If
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
is any collection of unit vectors in
\mathbb{C}^{d}
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}|\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|^{2m}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}|\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|^{2m}\geq\frac{n^{2}}{{d+m-1\choose m}},\quad\forall m\in\mathbb{N}.

In particular,

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}|\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|^{2}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}|\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|^{2}\geq\frac{n^{2}}{{d}}.

Further,

\displaystyle\text{({Higher order Welch bounds})}\quad\max_{1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k}|\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|^{2m}\geq\frac{1}{n-1}\left[\frac{n}{{d+m-1\choose m}}-1\right],\quad\forall m\in\mathbb{N}.

In particular,

\displaystyle\text{({First order Welch bound})}\quad\max_{1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k}|\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|^{2}\geq\frac{n-d}{d(n-1)}.

In this section we derive continuous non-Archimedean Banach space version of results derived in
[
54
]
. Let
\mathbb{K}
be a non-Archimedean (complete) valued field satisfying

\displaystyle\left|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\lambda_{j}^{2}\right|=\max_{1\leq j\leq n}|\lambda_{j}|^{2},\quad\forall\lambda_{j}\in\mathbb{K},1\leq j\leq n,\forall n\in\mathbb{N}.

For examples of such fields, we refer
[

\mathbb{K}
satisfies Equation (
1
). Letter
\mathcal{X}
stands for a
d
-dimensional non-Archimedean Banach space over
\mathbb{K}
. Identity operator on
\mathcal{X}
is denoted by
I_{\mathcal{X}}
. The dual of
\mathcal{X}
is denoted by
\mathcal{X}^{*}

In the entire paper,

Theorem 2.1
.

(First Order Continuous Non-Archimedean Functional Welch Bound)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a
d
-dimensional non-Archimedean Banach space over
\mathbb{K}
. Let
\{\tau_{g}\}_{g\in G}
be a collection in
\mathcal{X}
and
\{f_{g}\}_{g\in G}
be a collection in
\mathcal{X}^{*}
satisfying following conditions.

(i)

For every
x\in\mathcal{X}
and for every
\phi\in\mathcal{X}^{*}
, the map

\displaystyle G\ni g\mapsto f_{g}(x)\phi(\tau_{g})\in\mathcal{X}

is measurable and integrable.

(ii)

The map

\displaystyle S_{f,\tau}:\mathcal{X}\ni x\mapsto\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(x)\tau_{g}\,d\mu_{G}(g)\in\mathcal{X}

is a well-defined bounded linear operator.

(iii)

The operator
S_{f,\tau}
is diagonalizable.

(iv)

\displaystyle\int\limits_{G\times G}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)<\infty.

Then

\displaystyle\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|\right\}\geq\frac{1}{|d|}\left|\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\right|^{2}.

In particular, if
f_{g}(\tau_{g})=1
for all
g\in G
, then

(First order continnuous non-Archimedean functional Welch bound)

\displaystyle\max\left\{|(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(\Delta)|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|\right\}\geq\frac{|\mu(G)|^{2}}{|d|}.

Proof.

We first note that

\displaystyle\operatorname{Tra}(S_{f,\tau})=\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g),

\displaystyle\operatorname{Tra}(S^{2}_{f,\tau})=\int\limits_{G}\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\,d\mu_{G}(h).

Let
\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{d}
be the diagonal entries in the diagonalization of
S_{f,\tau}
. Then using the diagonalizability of
S_{f,\tau}
and the non-Archimedean Cauchy-Schwarz inequality (Theorem 2.4.2
[
64
]
), we get

\displaystyle\left|\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\operatorname{Tra}(S_{f,\tau})|^{2}=\left|\sum_{k=1}^{d}\lambda_{k}\right|^{2}\leq|d|\left|\sum_{k=1}^{d}\lambda_{k}^{2}\right|=|d||\operatorname{Tra}(S^{2}_{f,\tau})|

\displaystyle=|d|\left|\int\limits_{G}\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\,d\mu_{G}(h)\right|=|d|\left|\int\limits_{G\times G}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|

\displaystyle=|d|\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)+\int\limits_{(G\times G)\setminus\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|

\displaystyle\leq|d|\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\left|\int\limits_{(G\times G)\setminus\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq|d|\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|\right\}.

Whenever
f_{g}(\tau_{g})=1
for all
g\in G
,

\displaystyle|\mu(G)|^{2}\leq|d|\max\left\{|(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(\Delta)|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|\right\}.

∎

Theorem 2.4
.

(
Higher Order Continuous Non-Archimedean Functional Welch Bounds
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a
d
-dimensional non-Archimedean Banach space over
\mathbb{K}
. Let
m\in\mathbb{N}
. Let
\{\tau_{g}\}_{g\in G}
be a collection in

and

be a collection in
\mathcal{X}^{*}
satisfying following conditions.

(i)

For every

and for every
\phi\in\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X})^{*}
, the map

\displaystyle G\ni g\mapsto f^{\otimes m}_{g}(x)\phi(\tau_{g}^{\otimes m})\in\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X})

is measurable and integrable.

(ii)

The map

\displaystyle S_{f,\tau}:\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X})\ni x\mapsto\int\limits_{G}f^{\otimes m}_{g}(x)\tau_{g}^{\otimes m}\,d\mu_{G}(g)\in\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X})

is a well-defined bounded linear operator.

(iii)

The operator
S_{f,\tau}
is diagonalizable.

(iv)

\displaystyle\int\limits_{G\times G}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|^{m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)<\infty.

Then

\displaystyle\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|^{m}\right\}\geq\frac{1}{\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|}\left|\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{m}\,d\mu_{G}(g)\right|^{2}.

In particular, if
f_{g}(\tau_{g})=1
for all
g\in G
, then

(Higher order continuous non-Archimedean functional Welch bounds)

\displaystyle\quad\max\left\{|(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(\Delta)|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|^{m}\right\}\geq\frac{|\mu(G)|^{2}}{\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|}.

Proof.

Let
\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{\text{dim}(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))}
be the diagonal entries in the diagonalization of
S_{f,\tau}
. We note that

\displaystyle b\operatorname{dim(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))}=\operatorname{Tra}(bI_{\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X})})=\operatorname{Tra}(S_{f,\tau})=\int\limits_{G}f_{g}^{\otimes m}(\tau_{g}^{\otimes m})\,d\mu_{G}(g),

\displaystyle b^{2}\operatorname{dim(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))}=\operatorname{Tra}(b^{2}I_{\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X})})=\operatorname{Tra}(S^{2}_{f,\tau})=\int\limits_{G}\int\limits_{G}f_{g}^{\otimes m}(\tau^{\otimes m}_{h})f^{\otimes m}_{h}(\tau^{\otimes m}_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\,d\mu_{G}(h).

Then

\displaystyle\left|\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{m}\,d\mu_{G}(g)\right|^{2}=\left|\int\limits_{G}f_{g}^{\otimes m}(\tau_{g}^{\otimes m})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\right|^{2}=|\operatorname{Tra}(S_{f,\tau})|^{2}=\left|\sum_{k=1}^{\text{dim}(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))}\lambda_{k}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq|\text{dim}(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))|\left|\sum_{k=1}^{\text{dim}(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))}\lambda_{k}^{2}\right|=|\text{dim}(\text{Sym}^{m}(\mathcal{X}))||\operatorname{Tra}(S^{2}_{f,\tau})|

\displaystyle=\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right||\operatorname{Tra}(S^{2}_{f,\tau})|=\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\left|\int\limits_{G}\int\limits_{G}f_{g}^{\otimes m}(\tau_{h}^{\otimes m})f_{h}^{\otimes m}(\tau_{g}^{\otimes m})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\,d\mu_{G}(h)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\left|\int\limits_{G}\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{h})^{m}f_{h}(\tau_{g})^{m}\,d\mu_{G}(g)\,d\mu_{G}(h)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\left|\int\limits_{G\times G}f_{g}(\tau_{h})^{m}f_{h}(\tau_{g})^{m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)+\int\limits_{(G\times G)\setminus\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{h})^{m}f_{h}(\tau_{g})^{m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|

\displaystyle\leq|d|\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\left|\int\limits_{(G\times G)\setminus\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{h})^{m}f_{h}(\tau_{g})^{m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})^{m}f_{h}(\tau_{g})^{m}|\right\}

\displaystyle=\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2m}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|^{m}\right\}.

Whenever
f_{g}(\tau_{g})=1
for all
g\in G
,

\displaystyle|\mu(G)|^{2}\leq\left|{d+m-1\choose m}\right|\max\left\{|(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(\Delta)|,\sup_{g,h\in G,g\neq h}|f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})|^{m}\right\}.

∎

Theorem 3.1

Define

\displaystyle\left|\int\limits_{G}f_{g}(\tau_{g})\,d\mu_{G}(g)\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=|d|\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)+\int\limits_{(G\times G)\setminus\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|

\displaystyle\leq|d|\max\left\{\left|\int\limits_{\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{g})^{2}\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,g)\right|,\left|\int\limits_{(G\times G)\setminus\Delta}f_{g}(\tau_{h})f_{h}(\tau_{g})\,d(\mu_{G}\times\mu_{G})(g,h)\right|\right\}
