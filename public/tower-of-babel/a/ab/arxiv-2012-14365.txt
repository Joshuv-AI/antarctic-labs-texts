Abstract
We performed a high resolution study of 0 + 0^{+} states in 134 Ba using the 136 Ba( p , t p,t ) two-neutron transfer reaction. Our experiment shows a significant portion of the L = 0 L=0 pair-transfer strength concentrated at excited 0 + 0^{+} levels in 134 Ba. Potential implications in the context of 136 Xe → \to 136 Ba neutrinoless double beta decay matrix element calculations are briefly discussed.



The even-mass barium isotopic chain (

For example, theory calculations predict an enhanced octupole collectivity around

In the context of fundamental symmetries,
136

136
Xe double beta decay, an attractive candidate to search for the lepton-number-violating neutrinoless double beta (
0\nu 2\beta

In such a scenario, other useful information on lepton-number-violating new physics or the absolute neutrino mass scale, etc. can only be obtained if the nuclear matrix element (NME) for the decay is accurately known

Menéndez 2017
;
Ejiri et al. 2019

To this end, a
variety of many-body techniques are used to evaluate

decay NMEs in several candidate nuclei. These calculations have yielded significantly discrepant results for individual cases
Jonathan Engel and Javier
Menéndez 2017
. Addressing this apparent model dependence in all double beta decaying nuclei remains an important issue. We focus on this aspect here.

The dominant contribution to a
0\nu 2\beta
decay NME arises from the transformation of nucleon pairs that couple to total angular momentum
J=0

Caurier et al. 2008a
;
Šimkovic et al. 2008
;
Iwata et al. 2016
. Such a decay corresponds to spherical superfluid parent and daughter nuclei. The
J\neq 0
contributions to the matrix element arise from higher seniority
Caurier et al. 2008a
;
Šimkovic et al. 2008
components in the wavefunctions, due to broken Cooper pairs of nucleons. These lead to cancellations and effectively reduce the
0\nu 2\beta
decay amplitude. An additional reduction in the NME is expected if there is a seniority mismatch between the initial and the final wavefunctions
Caurier et al. 2008b
. This would be the case if the parent and the daughter have different intrinsic shapes
Alfredo Poves 2013
, that are driven by multipole correlations. It is now established that these collective correlations (other than pairing) play an important role in
0\nu 2\beta
NME calculations
Jonathan Engel and Javier
Menéndez 2017
and could further quench calculated NMEs
Alfredo Poves 2013
. As examples, one can look at two traditionally used many-body methods, the Interacting Shell Model (ISM) and the Quasiparticle Random Phase Approximation (QRPA). In the former, the treatment of correlations is exact, with comparatively smaller valence spaces. On the other hand, the QRPA calculations use larger model spaces, with relatively simpler configurations for the valence nucleons. Here, the pairing between like nucleons is treated via a transformation to the quasiparticle regime, within the Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (BCS) approximation
Avignone et al. 2008
. Such BCS pairing smears the Fermi surfaces in the parent and the daughter (as one would expect in a superfluid), while the RPA correlations admix higher seniority components in the wavefunctions
Caurier et al. 2008b
;
Šimkovic et al. 2008
. In this context,
(p,t)

Freeman et al. 2007
;
Bloxham et al. 2010
;
Thomas et al. 2012
;
Sharp et al. 2019
and
(^{3}{\rm He},n)

Roberts et al. 2013

Broglia et al. 1973
. For reactions on even-even nuclei, strong population of the ground states (relative to excited
0^{+}

Freeman et al. 2007
;
Broglia et al. 1973
.
