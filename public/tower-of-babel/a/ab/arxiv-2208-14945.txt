New solutions to the complex Ginzburg-Landau equations Preprint: Borelli preprint number
Robert Conte, Micheline Musette, Tuen Wai Ng, Chengfa Wu

Abstract
The various régimes observed in the one-dimensional complex Ginzburg-Landau equation result from the interaction of a very small number of elementary patterns such as pulses, fronts, shocks, holes, sinks. We provide here three exact such patterns observed in numerical calculations but never found analytically. One is a quintic case localized homoclinic defect, observed by Popp et alii, the two others are bound states of two quintic dark solitons, observed by Afanasyev et alii.
