Quark masses, CKM angles and
Lepton Flavour Universality violation

Abstract
A properly defined and suitably broken U ⁡ ( 2 ) U(2) flavour symmetry leads to successful quantitative relations between quark mass ratios and CKM angles. At the same time the intrinsic distinction introduced by U ⁡ ( 2 ) U(2) between the third and the first two families of quarks and leptons may support anomalies in charged and neutral current semi-leptonic B B -decays of the kind tentatively observed in current flavour experiments. We show how this is possible by the exchange of the ( 3 , 1 ) 2 / 3 (3,1)_{2/3} vector leptoquark in two U ⁡ ( 2 ) U(2) -models with significantly different values of Lepton Flavour Universality violation, observable in foreseen experiments.
