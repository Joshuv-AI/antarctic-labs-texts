Tensor products of Archimedean Riesz spaces: a representational approach
A.W. Wickstead

Abstract
. In an earler paper Buskes and the author pointed out that given two Archimedean Riesz spaces E E and F F it is relatively simple to construct, from their Ogasawara-Maeda representations, a third Archimedean Riesz space G G and a bi-injective Riesz bimorphism ϕ : E × F → G \phi:E\times F\to G . They further pointed out that the Riesz subspace of G G geneated by ϕ ⁡ ( E × F ) \phi(E\times F) is isomorphic to the Archimedean Riesz space tensor product E ​ ⊗ ¯ F E\ftp F constructed by Fremlin. The proof there relies on the properties of E ​ ⊗ ¯ F E\ftp F proved by Fremlin. In this paper we show that this approach actually gives a simple way to construct and establish the properties of E ​ ⊗ ¯ F E\ftp F and that any choice of G G and ϕ \phi yield isomorphic objects.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let

Proof.

By continuity we may assume that

and

\phi(\cdot,\boldsymbol{1}_{Y})(z):C(X)\to\mathbb{R}

\lambda_{z}\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
and

\phi(f,\boldsymbol{1}_{Y})(z)=\lambda f(x_{z})
for all

\mu_{z}\in\mathbb{R}
and

\phi(\boldsymbol{1}_{X},g)(z)=\mu g(y_{z})

we see that
\lambda_{z}=\mu_{z}

\phi(f,g)(z)=\lambda_{z}f(x_{z})g(y)z)
for all

g(y_{z})=0
then

|\phi(f,g)|(z)\leq\phi(|f|,|g|)(z)\leq\phi(\|f\|_{\infty}\boldsymbol{1}_{X},|g|)(z)=\|f\|_{\infty}\lambda_{z}|g|(y_{x})=0.

Hence

\displaystyle\phi(f,g)(z)

\displaystyle=\phi(f,g(y_{z})\boldsymbol{1}_{Y})(z)+\phi(f,g-g(y_{z})\boldsymbol{1}_{Y})(z)

\displaystyle=g(y_{z})\lambda_{z}f(x_{z})+0=\lambda_{z}f(x_{z})g(y_{z}).

Define
T

is a lattice homomorphism. The uniqueness is clear.
