Loop induced h → γ ​ γ , Z ​ γ , g ​ g h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma,Z\gamma,gg decays in 3-Higgs doublet models
Emine Yildirim

Abstract
Abstract In this paper, we analyze the loop induced decays of neutral Higgs boson h h into pairs of photons, gluons, and Z γ \gamma in 3-Higgs Doublet Models (3HDMs), where all the doublets have non-zero vacuum expectation values. In the 3HDM, there are contributions to these decays from loops of charged scalars H 1 , 2 ± H^{\pm}_{1,2} that are not present in the Standard Model. We find an enhancement in the channel h → Z ​ γ h\rightarrow Z\gamma with respect to the Standard Model prediction in some parameter region while respecting constraints from the measurements of h → γ ​ γ h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma at the LHC. The charged Higgs bosons H 1 , 2 ± H^{\pm}_{1,2} lead to the enhancement of Z ​ γ Z\gamma decay mode in some part of the parameter space of the 3HDM. Then, we discuss the correlations between the decay modes h → Z ​ γ h\rightarrow Z\gamma and h → γ ​ γ h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma . Moreover, we study Type-I and Type-Z of 3HDMs and find that these types of 3HDMs can be distinguished via only the gluonic decays, since the contributions of the H 1 , 2 ± H^{\pm}_{1,2} loops are dominant to those of fermion loops. We also investigate the effects of the model parameters on these loop induced decays. As an interesting phenomenological consequence, the 3HDM is dominantly governed by the parameters: two ratios of vacuum expectation values (VEVs); tan ⁡ γ , tan ⁡ β \tan\gamma,~\tan\beta and the mixing angles of the CP-even Higgs bosons, α 1 , 2 ​ and ​ θ \alpha_{1,2}~\text{and}~\theta .

V
CONCLUSIONS

In this work, we have analyzed the new physics contributions predicted by the 3HDM to the loop induced decays
h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma,~\gamma Z,~gg
. We have found that

•

The new charged Higgs bosons
H^{\pm}_{1,2}
can significantly alter the decay widths
h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma,~\gamma Z
(where h is the lightest CP-even scalar in the 3HDM), since their loop factor is the largest among others.

•

tan
\beta
, tan
\gamma
,
\alpha_{1,2},~\theta
, and
m_{H_{1,2}^{\pm}}
parameters can have significant effects on these decays.

•

We can disentangle the Type-I and Type-Z 3HDM only in the gluonic decays, since
\gamma\gamma
and
Z\gamma
decays are dominated by the
H^{\pm}_{1}
and
H^{\pm}_{2}
bosons loop contributions.

•

h\rightarrow Z\gamma
branching ratio increases considerably compared to its SM value in the part of the parameter space while the
h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma
decay is compatible with the LHC measurement. Our results on the BR
(h\rightarrow Z\gamma)
can be tested at the LHC when
R_{BR}\simeq 1
.

•

A correlation between the
h\rightarrow Z\gamma
and
h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma
decays exist, which could be probed in future experiments at the LHC.

In general, a large enhancement of
\Gamma(h\rightarrow Z\gamma)
in the 3HDM may be obtained with the contributions of the new charged Higgs bosons
H^{\pm}_{1,2}
and probed at the HL-LHC.
