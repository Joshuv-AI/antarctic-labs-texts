Anomalous and Anisotropic Nonlinear Susceptibility in the Proximate Kitaev Magnet α \alpha -RuCl 3 Preprint: APS/123-QED
Ludwig Holleis 1, Joseph Prestigiacomo 2, Zhijie Fan 1, Satoshi Nishimoto 3, Mike Osofsky 2, Gia-Wei Chern 1, Jeroen van den Brink 3,4, B.S. Shivaram 1

Abstract
The leading order nonlinear (NL) susceptibility, χ 3 \chi_{3} , in a paramagnet is negative and diverges as T → 0 T\rightarrow 0 . This divergence is destroyed when spins correlate and the NL response provides unique insights into magnetic order. Dimensionality, exchange interaction, and preponderance of quantum effects all imprint their signatures in the NL magnetic response. Here, we study the NL susceptibilities in the proximate Kitaev magnet α \alpha -RuCl 3 which differs from the expected antiferromagnetic behavior. For T < T c T<T_{c} = 7.5 K and field B B in the ab-plane, we obtain contrasting NL responses in low ( < < 2 T {T} ) and high field regions. For low fields the NL behavior is dominated by a quadratic response (positive χ 2 \chi_{2} ), which shows a rapid rise below T c T_{c} . This large χ 2 > 0 \chi_{2}>0 implies a broken sublattice symmetry of magnetic order at low temperatures. Classical Monte Carlo (CMC) simulations in the standard K − H − Γ {K-H-\Gamma} model secure such a quadratic B {B} dependence of M {M} , only for T {T} ≈ \approx T c {T}_{c} with χ 2 {\chi}_{2} being zero as T {T} → \rightarrow 0. It is also zero for all temperatures in exact diagonalization calculations. On the other hand, we find an exclusive cubic term ( χ 3 {\chi}_{3} ) describes the high field NL behavior well. χ 3 {\chi}_{3} is large and positive both below and above T c {T}_{c} crossing zero only for T {T} > > 50 K. In contrast, for B B ∥ \parallel c-axis, no separate low/high field behaviors is measured and only a much smaller χ 3 {\chi}_{3} is apparent.



I
Introduction

Since the demonstration by Kitaev
KitaevAnnPhys2006
of the existence of a quantum spin liquid state in two dimensions through exact calculations on a honeycomb lattice, there has been an intense experimental search for its realization in real material systems. Aided through insights provided by Jackeli and Khaliullin
JackeliPRL2009
to engineer (bond dependent) exchange interactions, the materials search has identified several promising systems with prominent attention given thus far to two candidates, the iridium oxides and ruthenium chloride
HermannsRev2017
;
SavaryRev2017

When they do order magnetically, the features observed in both microscopic probes such as neutron scattering and macroscopic measurements such as magnetometry and thermal response are highly unusual and contrast dramatically with what is expected from conventional magnets. In their thermal transport
KasaharaNature2018
, thermodynamic
DoNatPhys2017

WellmPRB2018

In the proximate Kitaev spin liquid candidate,

Summary and Outlook

In summary, we have presented measurements of the nonlinear susceptibility in the Kitaev magnet
