Abstract
A search for a new Z ′ Z^{\prime} gauge boson associated with (un)broken B − L B-L symmetry in the keV-GeV mass range is carried out for the first time using the missing-energy technique in the NA64 experiment at the CERN SPS. From the analysis of the data with 3.22 × 10 11 3.22\times 10^{11} electrons on target collected during 2016 - 2021 runs no signal events were found. This allows to derive new constraints on the Z ′ − e Z^{\prime}-e coupling strength, which for the mass range 0.3 ≲ m Z ′ ≲ 100 0.3\lesssim m_{Z^{\prime}}\lesssim 100 MeV are more stringent compared to those obtained from the neutrino-electron scattering data.
