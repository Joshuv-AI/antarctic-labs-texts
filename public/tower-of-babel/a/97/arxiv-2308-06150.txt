On immersions and embeddings with trivial normal line bundles
Gabriel Katz

Abstract
. Let Z Z be a smooth compact ( n + 1 ) (n+1) -manifold. 2.
Comparing bordisms of immersions and embeddings that have trivial normal line bundles

Consider the obvious maps

\mathsf{A}:\mathsf{EMB}(Z,\partial Z)\to\mathsf{IMM}(Z,\partial Z)\text{ \; and \; }\mathsf{A}:\mathsf{EMB}(Z)\to\mathsf{IMM}(Z).

Theorem 2.1
.

•

The map
\mathsf{A}:\mathsf{EMB}(Z,\partial Z)\to\mathsf{IMM}(Z,\partial Z)

Lemma 2.1
.
