Igor Kurchavov, Alexander Petrov

Abstract
The energy shift in molecular spectra due to interaction of nuclear magnetic quadrupole moment ( M M ) with electrons is equal to δ ​ E M = M ​ W M ​ P M \delta E_{M}=MW_{M}P_{M} where W M W_{M} is a constant determined by the electronic structure of the molecule and P M P_{M} is a dimensionless constant. We extended the method for calculation of parity nonconservation effects in triatomic molecules developed in Ref. [A. Petrov and A. Zakharova, Phys. Rev. A 105 , L050801 (2022)] to the case of P M P_{M} constant and applied it to 173 YbOH in the first excited v = 1 v=1 bending mode. Results of our calculations are required for extracting of the M M value from the YbOH experiment.
