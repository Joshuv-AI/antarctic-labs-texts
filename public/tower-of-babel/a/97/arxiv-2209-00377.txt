Error Bound for the Linear Complementarity Problem using Plus Function
Bharat Kumar, Deepmala, A. Dutta, A. K. Das

Abstract
In this article we establish error bound for linear complementarity problem with P P -matrix using plus function. We introduce a fundamental quantity associated with a P P -matrix and show how this quantity is useful in deriving error bounds for the linear complementarity problem of the P P -type. We also obtain (upper and lower) bounds for the quantity introduced.



Keywords:
Linear complementarity problem, plus function, error bound, relative error bound.

Theorem 3.1
:

Let

be a

denote the unique solution
of LCP
(A,q)
and let

be an arbitrary vector. Then,

\frac{1}{1+\|A\|_{\infty}}\|(d-(d-(Ad+q))_{+})\|_{\infty}\leq\|z-d\|_{\infty}\leq\frac{1+\|A\|_{\infty}}{\beta(A)}\|(d-(d-(Ad+q))_{+})\|_{\infty}.

Proof.

Let

and

. Consider the vector

This implies that

Now we show that the vectors

and

satisfy complementarity condition.

If
d_{i}\geq(Ad+q)_{i},

((d-(Ad+q))_{i})_{+}=d_{i}-(Ad+q)_{i}

Then

and

In another way, if
d_{i}\leq(Ad+q)_{i},

((d-(Ad+q))_{i})_{+}=0.

This implies that

s_{i}=0
and
c_{i}=q_{i}-d_{i}+((d-(Ad+q))_{i})_{+}+(Ad)_{i}=(Ad)_{i}+q_{i}-d_{i}\geq 0.

Considering both the cases we obtain the following pair of inequalities and complementarity condition.

s\geq 0,\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ c=q+(A-I)p+As\geq 0,

(3.2)

s^{T}c=0.

(3.3)

Now, for each

we have

(s-z)_{i}(c-l)_{i}=s_{i}c_{i}+z_{i}l_{i}-z_{i}c_{i}-s_{i}l_{i}\leq 0
.

Therefore, we have

0\geq(s-z)_{i}(c-l)_{i}=(d-p-z)_{i}(q+Ap-p+As-q-Az)_{i}=(d-p-z)_{i}(q+Ap-p+Ad-Ap-q-Az)_{i}=(d-p-z)_{i}(-p+A(d-z))_{i}\\

This implies that,

(d-z)_{i}(A(d-z))_{i}\leq(d-z)_{i}p_{i}+p_{i}(A(d-z))_{i}

In particular for the index

(d-z)_{i}(A(d-z))_{i}=\max\limits_{j}(d-z)_{j}(A(d-z))_{j}

Now from the condition (

Hence

(d-z)_{i}(A(d-z))_{i}\geq\beta(A)\|d-z\|^{2}_{\infty}

Therefore,

\beta(A)\|d-z\|^{2}_{\infty}\leq(d-z)_{i}(A(d-z))_{i}\leq(d-z)_{i}p_{i}+p_{i}(A(d-z))_{i}\\
=((I+A)(d-z))_{i}\ p_{i}\leq(1+\|A\|_{\infty})\|p\|_{\infty}\|d-z\|_{\infty}.

Hence
