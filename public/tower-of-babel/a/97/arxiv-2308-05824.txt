Shadowing in non-compact spaces with a global attractor.
Gonzalo Cousillas

Abstract
. This paper studies the behavior of dynamical systems in non-compact spaces, specifically focusing on the concepts of global attractors and shadowing. Let K K be a compact global attractor. We show that the shadowing property holds in certain types of dynamical systems in non-compact spaces if and only if K K is trivial.

1.
Introduction

Dynamical systems are a fundamental concept in mathematics, appearing in a wide range of applications from physics to economics. In a dynamical system, a set of variables evolve over time according to law of movement. The behavior of the system can be studied by examining the long-term behavior of the orbits.

One important concept in the study of dynamical systems is global attraction. A global attractor is a subset of the phase space of a dynamical system satisfying that all orbits eventually approach. The study of attractors has important implications for understanding the long-term behavior of a system.

Much of the existing research on global attractors has focused on compact spaces, where the phase space is bounded. In this paper, we study global attractors on non-compact spaces.

Another important property of dynamical systems that has played an important role is the pseudo-orbit tracing property or shadowing property. See
[
9
,
8
,
6
]
. It allows us to approximate a true orbit with a nearby orbit. This property has important implications for numerical simulations of dynamical systems, as it allows us to approximate the true solution with a simpler, more computationally tractable solution.
In the non-compact setting for the shadowing to be a conjugacy invariant property the usual definition is adapted
[
5
]
.

In this paper, we study the shadowing property and its relation with the existance of a gobal attractor and its topology in non-compact spaces. We obtain the following two main results.

Theorem 1
.

Let
X
be a locally compact metric space,
f:X\to X
a map and
p
a globally asymptotically stable fixed point. Then
f
has the topological shadowing property.

Theorem 2
.

Let
f:{\mathbb{R}}^{n}\to{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
be a homeomorphism such that there exists
K
is a compact global attractor for the dynamical system
(f,X)
.

f
has TSP if and only if
K
is trivial.
