On signed Mordell-Weil groups for abelian varieties.
Jishnu Ray

Abstract
. In this short note, we work in the general framework of supersingular abelian varieties defined over ℚ \mathbb{Q} . Using Coleman maps constructed by Büyükboduk–Lei, we define some new objects called “the multi-signed Mordell-Weil groups" for supersingular abelian varieties, provide an explicit structure of the dual of these groups as an Iwasawa module and prove a control theorem. This recovers the case of elliptic curves over ℚ \mathbb{Q} non-ordinary at the prime p p with a p = 0 a_{p}=0 studied by Antonio Lei.

1.
Introduction

Let
p
be an odd prime,
E
be an elliptic curve over
\mathbb{Q}
with supersingular reduction at
p
. Let
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
be the cyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
\mathbb{Q}
with Galois group
\Gamma
. Write
\mathbb{Q}_{(n)}
for the unique sub-extension of degree
p^{n}
with Galois group
\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_{(n)}/\mathbb{Q})=\Gamma_{n}
. Let
\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[\Gamma]]
be the Iwasawa algebra of
\Gamma
.

Let
A
be a
g
-dimensional abelian variety over
\mathbb{Q}
(its
p
-adic Tate module is of
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-rank
2g
) with good supersingular reduction at
p
and let
A^{\vee}
be the dual abelian variety.
For any subset
\underline{I}\in\{1,...,2g\}
of cardinality
g
, using the theory of Coleman maps, we define the
\underline{I}
-Mordell-Weil group
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
and the
\underline{I}
-Tate-Shafarevich group
\FSha_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
(see Section
3
) which fits into the following exact sequence

0\rightarrow\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})\rightarrow\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})\rightarrow\FSha_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})\rightarrow 0,

where the middle term is the
\underline{I}
-Selmer group defined as in
[
Pon20
, Section 1.6]
.

The definitions of the
\underline{I}
-Mordell-Weil group
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
and
\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})

(and hence that of the
\underline{I}
-Tate-Shafarevich group
\FSha_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
) depend on a choice of a Hodge-compatible basis of the associated Dieudonné module (see Definition
2.1
).
We show that if a certain matrix is block anti-diagonal the groups
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
and
\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
are canonically defined for two indexing sets, namely
\underline{I}=\{1,...,g\}
and
\underline{I}=\{g+1,...,2g\}
(see Proposition
3.7
). Hence the groups

\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
and
\FSha_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
are also canonically defined.
When the abelian variety is an elliptic curve (i.e.
g=1
), then these groups exactly coincide with their canonically defined plus and minus variants, namely the groups
\mathcal{M}^{\pm}(E/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
and
\FSha^{\pm}(E/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
define by Lei in
[
Lei23
, definition 5.2]
.
Our goal in this short note is to prove a control theorem for
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
as
n
grows (Theorem
3.5
). We also make some remarks on the algebraic structure of the Pontryagin dual of
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
(see Proposition
3.6
). These are generalizations of results in
[
Lei23
]
given for elliptic curves.

2.
Signed Selmer groups over the cyclotomic tower

Let
A
be a
g
-dimensional abelian variety defined over
\mathbb{Q}
with good supersingular reduction at
p
. Throughout the article we will need the following notations.

•

For
n\geqslant 1
, let
A[p^{n}]
be the
p^{n}
-torsion points of
A(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})
and let

A[p^{\infty}]=\cup_{n}A[p^{n}].

•

Let
T
be the
p
-adic Tate module associated with the abelian variety
A
with a continuous action of the absolute Galois group
G_{\mathbb{Q}}
. It is a free
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-module of rank
2g
.

•

Let
V=T\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}\mathbb{Q}_{p}
,
V
is a crystalline
G_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}
-representation with Hodge-Tate weights
0
and
1
, both with multiplicity
g
.

•

For
i\geqslant 0
, the Iwasawa cohomology group
H^{i}_{\mathrm{Iw},\mathrm{cyc}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},T)
is defined as the projective limit of
H^{i}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{n}})_{v_{n}},T)
relative to the corestriction maps. Here
v_{n}
is the prime of
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{n}})
above
p
.

•

The Dieudonné module
\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)
associated to
T
(see
[
Ber04
]
) is a free
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-module of rank
2g
.

•

As mentioned in the introduction,
A^{\vee}
will be the dual abelian variety and for a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-module
M
,
M^{\land}
will be the Pontryagin dual
\operatorname{Hom}(M,\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p})
.

The module
\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)
is equipped with a Frobenius after tensoring with
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
and a filtration of
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-modules
(\mathrm{Fil}^{i}\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T))_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}
satisfying

\mathrm{Fil}^{i}\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)=\begin{cases}0&\text{if $i\geqslant 1$,}\\
\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)&\text{if $i\leqslant-1$.}\end{cases}

Definition 2.1
.

Choose an
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-basis
\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{2g}\}
of
\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)
such that
\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{g}\}
is a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-basis of
\mathrm{Fil}^{0}\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)
. Such a basis is called Hodge-compatible.

The matrix of the Frobenius
\varphi
with respect to this basis is of the form

C_{\varphi,p}=C_{p}\left[\begin{array}[]{c|c}I_{g}&0\\
\hline\cr 0&\frac{1}{p}I_{g}\end{array}\right]

for some
C_{p}\in\mathrm{GL}_{2g}(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
and the identity
g\times g
matrix
I_{g}
.
Following
[
BL17
, Definition 2.4]
, for
n\geqslant 1
, we can define

C_{p,n}:=\left[\begin{array}[]{c|c}I_{g}&0\\
\hline\cr 0&\Phi_{n}(1+X)I_{g}\end{array}\right]C_{p}^{-1}\text{ and }M_{p,n}:=(C_{\varphi,p})^{n+1}C_{p,n}\cdots C_{p,1},\hfill

where
\Phi_{n}(1+X)
is the
p^{n}-
th cyclotomic polynomial in
1+X.

Recall that as
p
is odd,

\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})/\mathbb{Q})\cong\Delta\times\Gamma

where
\Delta
is a finite group of order
p-1
.
Set
\mathcal{H}=\mathbb{Q}_{p}[\Delta]\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}\mathcal{H}(\Gamma)
where
\mathcal{H}(\Gamma)
is the set of elements
f(\gamma-1)
with
\gamma\in\Gamma
and
f(X)\in\mathbb{Q}_{p}[[X]]
is convergent on the
p
-adic open unit disk. Let

\mathcal{L}_{T,p}:H^{1}_{\mathrm{Iw},\mathrm{cyc}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},T)\rightarrow\mathcal{H}\otimes\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)

be the Perrin-Riou’s big logarithm map (see
[
LLZ11
, Definition 3.4]
). It interpolates Kato’s dual exponential maps
[
Kat93
, II, §1.2]

\mathrm{exp}^{*}_{p,n}:H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}(\mu_{p^{n}}),T)\rightarrow\mathbb{Q}_{p}(\mu_{p^{n}})\otimes\mathrm{Fil}^{0}\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T).

In
[
BL17
, Theorem 1.1]
, Büyükboduk–Lei showed that the big logarithm map decomposes into Coleman maps in the following way.

\mathcal{L}_{T,p}^{\infty}=(u_{1},\ldots,u_{2g})\cdot M_{T,p}\cdot\begin{bmatrix}\operatorname{Col}_{T,p,1}\\
\vdots\\
\operatorname{Col}_{T,p,2g}\end{bmatrix},

where
M_{T,p}=\underset{n\rightarrow\infty}{\lim}M_{p,n}
is a
2g\times 2g
logarithmic matrix defined over
\mathcal{H}
and for
i\in\{1,...,2g\}
, the maps
\operatorname{Col}_{T,p,i},
are
\mathbb{Z}_{p}[\Delta][[\Gamma]]
-homomorphisms from
H^{1}_{\mathrm{Iw},\mathrm{cyc}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},T)\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{p}[\Delta][[\Gamma]].

For any subset
I_{p}
of
\{1,...,2g\}
, one can define

\displaystyle\operatorname{Col}_{T,I_{p}}:H^{1}_{\mathrm{Iw},\mathrm{cyc}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},T)

\displaystyle\rightarrow\prod_{i=1}^{|I_{p}|}\mathbb{Z}_{p}[\Delta][[\Gamma]],

\displaystyle{z}

\displaystyle\mapsto(\operatorname{Col}_{T,p,i}({z}))_{i\in I_{p}}.

Let
\mathcal{I}
be the set of all subsets of
\{1,...,2g\}
of cardinality
g
. So
\underline{I}\in\mathcal{I}
will be a subset of
\{1,...,2g\}
having cardinality
g
. Given any such
\underline{I}\in\mathcal{I}
and
\mathbf{z}=z_{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge z_{g}\in\bigwedge^{g}H^{1}_{\mathrm{Iw},\mathrm{cyc}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},T),
one can define

\operatorname{Col}_{T,\underline{I}}(\mathbf{z})=\det(\operatorname{Col}_{T,p,i}(z_{j}))_{i\in\underline{I},1\leqslant j\leqslant g}.

For all
n\geqslant 1
, let
H_{p,n}=C_{p,n}\cdots C_{p,1}
. For a pair
\underline{I}
and
\underline{J}
in
\mathcal{I}
, we define
H_{\underline{I},\underline{J},n}
as the
(\underline{I},\underline{J})
-minor of
H_{p,n}
.

Since the prime
p
is totally ramified in
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})
, we will also use it to denote the unique prime of
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})
above
p
. Let
I_{p}
be a subset of of
\{1,...,2g\}
. One defines the subset

H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})_{p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])\subset H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})_{p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])

as the orthogonal
complement of
\ker(\operatorname{Col}_{T,I_{p}})
under the Tate’s local pairing

H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})_{p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])\times H^{1}_{\mathrm{Iw},\mathrm{cyc}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},T)\rightarrow\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p}.

By the inflation-restriction exact sequence it is possible to show that the restriction map

H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})_{p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])^{\Delta}

is an isomorphism; because the order of
\Delta
is
p-1
and
A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}](\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})_{p})
is a finite
p
-group. Using this isomorphism one defines

H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])\subset H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])

as

H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])=H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})_{p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])^{\Delta}.

Definition 2.2
.

For
\underline{I}=(I_{p})\in\mathcal{I}
, the
\underline{I}
-Selmer group of
A^{\vee}
over
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
is defined by

\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})=\ker\Big(H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow\prod_{v\nmid p}{H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{{\infty},v},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}\times\frac{H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{{\infty},p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}{H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{{\infty},p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}\Big).

Remark 2.3
.

This definition of multi-signed Selmer groups can be generalized for any abelian variety over a general number field
F
, unramified at
p
, with good supersingular reduction at every prime
v
of
F
dividing
p
. In this case,
the multi-signed Selmer groups are defined for
\underline{I}=(I_{v})_{v\mid p}
where
I_{v}\subset\{1,...,2g[F_{v}:\mathbb{Q}_{p}]\}
. This is the notation used in page 5 of
[
LP20
]
. In our case, since the base field is
\mathbb{Q}
, we have only one prime
p
and to be consistent with the existing notation, we have written
\underline{I}=(I_{p})
. In our case
\underline{I}
and
I_{p}
are the same.

3.
Signed Mordell-Weil group and signed Tate-Shafarevich group

In this section, we define the signed Mordell-Weil group and the signed Tate-Shafarevich group generalizing
[
Lei23
]
.

Definition 3.1
.

The
\underline{I}
-Mordell-Weil group of
A^{\vee}
over the cyclotomic extension
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
is defined by

\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})=\ker\Big(A^{\vee}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p}\rightarrow\frac{H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}{H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}\Big).

Now, let us define the
\underline{I}
-Mordell-Weil group at the level
\mathbb{Q}_{(n)}
, for which we need to define the local condition at
p
.
For
n\geqslant 0
, we define

H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{(n),p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}]):=H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])^{\Gamma_{n}}.

As mentioned before, this definition is in coherence with
[
Pon20
, Section 1.6]
and
[
Lei23
, Definition 5.2]
.

Definition 3.2
.

We define the
\underline{I}
-Mordell-Weil group and the
\underline{I}
-Selmer group over
\mathbb{Q}_{(n)}
as

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})

\displaystyle=\ker\Big(A^{\vee}(\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_{p}/\mathbb{Z}_{p}\rightarrow\frac{H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{{(n)},p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}{H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{{(n)},p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}\Big),

\displaystyle\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})

\displaystyle=\ker\Big(H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{(n)},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow\prod_{v\nmid p}{H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{{(n)},v},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}\times\frac{H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{{(n)},p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}{H^{1}_{I_{p}}(\mathbb{Q}_{{(n)},p},A^{\vee}[p^{\infty}])}\Big).

Remark 3.3
.

When
A
is an elliptic curves over
\mathbb{Q}
with

, for
\underline{I}=\{1\}

\underline{I}=\{2\}

coincide with Kobayashi’s plus and minus Selmer groups (see

Definition 3.4
.

Let

Proposition 3.6
.

If

Proof.

By definition, the Pontryagin dual of

Let

3.1.
Change of basis

The definitions of
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
and
\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
(and hence that of
\FSha_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
) depend on the choice of a Hodge-compatible basis and hence are not canonically defined. Under certain additional assumptions, one can define them canonically for certain choices of the indexing set
\underline{I}
.
Let
\underline{I}_{1}=\{g+1,...,2g\}
and recall that
\underline{I}_{0}=\{1,...,g\}
.

Proposition 3.7
.

Suppose that
C_{p}
is a block anti-diagonal matrix with respect to the basis
\mathcal{B}_{p}
. For
\underline{I}=\underline{I}_{0}
or
\underline{I}_{1}
, the groups
\mathcal{M}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
,
\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
and
\FSha_{\underline{I}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
are canonically defined.

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{B}_{p}=\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{2g}\}
and
\mathcal{B}_{p}^{\prime}=\{w_{1},\ldots,w_{2g}\}
be a pair of Hodge-compatible bases of
\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{cris},p}(T)
. Let

be the change of basis matrix from
\mathcal{B}_{p}^{\prime}
to
\mathcal{B}_{p}
. This implies that

is block diagonal (see

\hline\cr 0&B_{2,2}\end{array}\right]

where

. Let
\operatorname{Col}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}}_{T,p,i}
be the

. Let
\operatorname{Col}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}}_{T,p}
denote the column vector of Coleman maps
(\operatorname{Col}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}}_{T,p,i})_{i=1}^{2g}
. Similarly define
\operatorname{Col}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}^{\prime}}_{T,p}
as the column vector of Coleman maps defined with respect to the basis
\mathcal{B}_{p}^{\prime}
. An argument analogous to
[

, Proposition 4.17]

\operatorname{Col}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}^{\prime}}_{T,p}=B_{p}\cdot\operatorname{Col}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}}_{T,p}.

Thus,

\begin{bmatrix}\operatorname{Col}_{T,p,1}^{\mathcal{B}_{p}^{\prime}}\\

3.2.
Remarks and examples

•

Note that
C_{p}
will remain block anti-diagonal upon a change of basis. This is because
B_{p}C_{p}B_{p}^{-1}
is block anti-diagonal if
B_{p}
is block diagonal and
C_{p}
is block anti-diagonal.

•

In the case of elliptic curves with
a_{p}=0
, note that Kobayashi’s signed Selmer groups
\operatorname{Sel}_{p}^{\pm}(E/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
are canonically defined. These groups correspond to
\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}_{0}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
and
\operatorname{Sel}_{\underline{I}_{1}}(A^{\vee}/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
for
g=1
(see
[
BL17
, Appendix 4]
). Hence Proposition
3.7
should be seen as a generalization of this phenomenon. Kobayashi showed that the Selmer groups
\operatorname{Sel}_{p}^{\pm}(E/\mathbb{Q}_{\infty})
are
\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[\Gamma]]
-cotorsion and hence the assumption
(Torsion)
is satisfied. Here
r=0
(cf.
[
Lei23
, Remark 3.5]
) and we know that the
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-rank of
E(\mathbb{Q}_{(n)})
is bounded as
n
varies (cf.
[
Lei23
, discussion after eq. (1.1)]
).

•

Note that in the context of abelian varieties of
GL(2)
-type, under some additional hypotheses, one can ensure that the matrix of Frobenius
C_{p}
is block anti-diagonal (see
[
LP20
, Section 3.3]
).
