Jeff Smith’s Theory of Ideals
Robert R. Bruner, Daniel C. Isaksen

Abstract
. Jeff Smith recently proposed a theory of ideals for rings in a triangulated symmetric monoidal category such as ring spectra or DGAs. We show that his definition is equivalent to a ‘central’ R R - R R -bimodule map I → R I\to R .
