Abstract
We study classical M5 brane solutions in the probe limit in the A ​ d ​ S 7 × S 4 AdS_{7}\times S^{4} spacetime geometry with worldvolume 3-form flux. These solutions describe the holography of codimension-4 defects in the 6d boundary dual 𝒩 = ( 0 , 2 ) \mathcal{N}=(0,2) supersymmetric gauge theories. Starting from some half-BPS solutions which follow stricter BPS conditions, we find a general 1 16 \frac{1}{16} -BPS solution. We show how some of the giant-like M5 brane solutions here may be related to the codimension-2 surface operators in the 4d gauge theory.
