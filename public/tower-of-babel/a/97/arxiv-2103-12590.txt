Integrals of polylogarithms and infinite series involving generalized harmonic numbers
Rusen Li

Abstract
In this paper, we give explicit evaluation for some infinite series involving generalized (alternating) harmonic numbers. In addition, some formulas for generalized (alternating) harmonic numbers will also be derived. Keywords: polylogarithm function, generalized harmonic numbers



1
Introduction and preliminaries

Let
\mathbb{Z}
,
\mathbb{N}
,
\mathbb{N}_{0}
and
\mathbb{C}
denote the set of integers, positive integers, nonnegative integers and complex numbers, respectively. The well-known polylogarithm function is defined as

Li_{p}(x):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n^{p}}\quad(\lvert x\lvert\leq 1,\quad p\in\mathbb{N}_{0})\,.

Note that when
p=1
,
-Li_{1}(x)
is the logarithm function
\log(1-x)
. Here, and throughout this paper, we use the natural logarithm (to base
e
). Furthermore,
Li_{n}(1)=\zeta(n)
, where
\zeta(s):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}
denotes the well-known Riemann zeta function. The classical generalized harmonic numbers of order
m
is defined by the partial sum of the Riemann Zeta function
\zeta(m)
as:

H_{n}^{(m)}:=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{1}{j^{m}}\quad(n,m\in\mathbb{N})\,.

For convenience, we recall the classical generalized alternating harmonic numbers
\overline{H}_{n}^{(p)}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(-1)^{j-1}/{j^{p}}
.

It is interesting that infinite series containing harmonic numbers
H_{n}
can be expressed explicitly in terms of the logarithms and polylogarithm functions. For instance, De Doelder
[
1
]
used the integrals

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t,\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t,\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)\log(1-t)}{t^{2}}\mathrm{d}t

and

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)\log(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

to evaluate infinite series containing harmonic numbers of types
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}}{n^{2}}x^{n}
,
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}}{n^{3}}x^{n}
,
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(H_{n-1})^{2}}{n}x^{n}
and
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(H_{n-1})^{2}}{n^{2}}x^{n}
.

When
p=1
, De Doelder
[
1
]
gave the following formulas:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}}{n}x^{n}=\frac{1}{2}\log^{2}(1-x)\quad(\lvert x\lvert\leq 1)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}}{n^{2}}x^{n}=\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-x)+\log(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)-Li_{3}(1-x)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad+Li_{3}(1)\quad(0\leq x\leq 1)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n}H_{n-1}}{n^{2}}x^{n}=\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1+x)-\frac{1}{3}\log^{3}(1+x)-Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad-\log(1+x)Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)+Li_{3}(1)\quad(0\leq x\leq 1)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}}{n^{3}}=\frac{1}{360}\pi^{4}\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}(-1)^{n-1}}{n^{3}}=\frac{1}{48}\pi^{4}-2Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{2}\bigg)-\frac{7}{4}\log(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log(2)-\frac{1}{12}\log^{4}(2)\,.

In this paper, we give explicit evaluation for infinite series involving generalized (alternating) harmonic numbers of types

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}(-x)^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}}(-x)^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}}{n}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}
.
In addition, some formulas for generalized (alternating) harmonic numbers will also be derived.

2
Infinite series containing generalized harmonic numbers

Now we establish more explicit formulas for infinite series
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(p)}}{n^{m}}x^{n}
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
p\in\mathbb{N}
with
p
odd and
\lvert x\lvert\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(p)}}{n}x^{n}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j-1}Li_{j}(x)Li_{p+1-j}(x)+Li_{p+1}(x)\,.

Proof.

Integrating the generating function of
H_{n}^{(p)}
, we can write

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(p)}}{n+1}x^{n+1}=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{p}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=Li_{1}(x)Li_{p}(x)-\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{1}(t)Li_{p-1}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=Li_{1}(x)Li_{p}(x)-Li_{2}(x)Li_{p-1}(x)+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)Li_{p-2}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{j-1}Li_{j}(x)Li_{p+1-j}(x)+(-1)^{k}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{k}(t)Li_{p-k}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\quad(0\leq k\leq p)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j-1}Li_{j}(x)Li_{p+1-j}(x)+(-1)^{p}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{p}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

Note that

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(p)}}{n}x^{n}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n-1}^{(p)}}{n}x^{n}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n^{p+1}}\,,

thus we get the desired result.
∎

Corollary 1
.

Let
p,n\in\mathbb{N}
and
k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
with
0\leq k\leq p
, then we have

\displaystyle H_{n}^{(p)}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{j-1}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{n+1}{\ell^{j}(n+1-\ell)^{p+1-j}}+(-1)^{k}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{1}{\ell^{k}(n+1-\ell)^{p-k}}\,.

In particular, if
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, then we can obtain that

\displaystyle\quad H_{n}^{(2m+1)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{m}(-1)^{j-1}\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{n}{k^{j}(n-k)^{2m+2-j}}+\frac{(-1)^{m}}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{n}{k^{m+1}(n-k)^{m+1}}+\frac{1}{n^{2m+1}}\,,

\displaystyle H_{n}^{(2m)}=\sum_{j=1}^{m}(-1)^{j-1}\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{n}{k^{j}(n-k)^{2m+1-j}}+(-1)^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{j^{m}(n-j)^{m}}+\frac{1}{n^{2m}}\,.

Proof.

The following formula is obtained in the proof of Theorem
1
,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(p)}}{n+1}x^{n+1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{j-1}Li_{j}(x)Li_{p+1-j}(x)+(-1)^{k}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{k}(t)Li_{p-k}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\quad(0\leq k\leq p)\,,

comparing the coefficients on both sides gives the desired result.
∎

Lemma 1
.

(

)
Let
0\leq x<1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1-t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-2\bigg(Li_{4}(x)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{-x}{1-x}\bigg)-Li_{4}(1-x)+Li_{4}(1)-Li_{3}(x)\log(1-x)\bigg)

\displaystyle\quad-2Li_{3}(1-x)\log(x)+2Li_{2}(1-x)\log(x)\log(1-x)-\frac{1}{6}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{2}\log^{2}(x)\log^{2}(1-x)+\frac{1}{3}\log(x)\log^{3}(1-x)-\frac{1}{12}\log^{4}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+2Li_{3}(1)\bigg(\log(x)-\log(1-x)\bigg)\,.

Lemma 2
.

(
[
2
, p.310]
)
Let
0\leq x<1
, then we have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t=-2Li_{4}(x)+2Li_{3}(x)\log(x)-Li_{2}(x)\log^{2}(x)\,.

Lemma 3
.

(
[
1
,
2
]
)
Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{2}(1-x)+2\log(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad-2Li_{3}(1-x)+2Li_{3}(1)\,,

(1)

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{2}(1+x)-\frac{2}{3}\log^{3}(1+x)-2Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)

\displaystyle\quad-2\log(1+x)Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)+2Li_{3}(1)\,.

(2)

De Doelder
[
1
]
only calculated the integral
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t
, we now give explicit expression for the intagral
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t
.

Lemma 4
.

Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=2\bigg(Li_{4}(-x)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{x}{1+x}\bigg)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-Li_{4}(1)-Li_{3}(1)\log(x)

\displaystyle\quad+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{x}{1+x}\bigg)\log(1+x)+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)\log(1+x)+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)\log(x)

\displaystyle\quad+Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)\log(x)\log(1+x)\bigg)+\frac{1}{6}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(1+x)-\frac{1}{2}\log^{2}(x)\log^{2}(1+x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{4}{3}\log(x)\log^{3}(1+x)-\frac{1}{2}\log^{4}(1+x)\,.

Proof.

Following De Doelder’s paper
[
1
]
, we make the substitution
t=\frac{1}{u}-1
and it yields that

\displaystyle\quad\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\int_{\frac{1}{1+x}}^{1}\frac{\big(\log(1-u)-\log(u)\big)^{2}\log(u)}{u}\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle=-\int_{\frac{1}{1+x}}^{1}\frac{\log^{2}(1-u)\log(u)}{u}\mathrm{d}u+2\int_{\frac{1}{1+x}}^{1}\frac{\log(1-u)\log^{2}(u)}{u}\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle\quad-\int_{\frac{1}{1+x}}^{1}\frac{\log^{3}(u)}{u}\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle=-\int_{0}^{\frac{x}{1+x}}\frac{\log^{2}(y)\log(1-y)}{1-y}\mathrm{d}y+2\int_{\frac{1}{1+x}}^{1}\frac{\log(1-u)\log^{2}(u)}{u}\mathrm{d}u

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{4}\log^{4}(1+x)\,.

With the help of Lemmata
1
and
2
, we get the desired result.
∎

Theorem 2
.

Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}x^{n}

\displaystyle=2Li_{4}(x)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{-x}{1-x}\bigg)-Li_{4}(1-x)+Li_{4}(1)-Li_{3}(x)\log(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+Li_{3}(1)\log(1-x)+\frac{1}{24}\log^{4}(1-x)-\frac{1}{6}\log(x)\log^{3}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(1-x)\,,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}(-x)^{n}

\displaystyle=2Li_{4}(-x)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{x}{1+x}\bigg)-Li_{4}(1)

\displaystyle\quad+\log(1+x)Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)+\log(1+x)Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{x}{1+x}\bigg)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(1+x)+\frac{1}{3}\log(x)\log^{3}(1+x)-\frac{1}{4}\log^{4}(1+x)\,.

Proof.

Integrating the generating function of
H_{n}
, we can write

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{(n+1)^{3}}x^{n+1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{u}\int_{0}^{u}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t-\frac{1}{4}\log^{2}(x)\log^{2}(1-x)-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1-t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t\,,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{(n+1)^{3}}(-x)^{n+1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{u}\int_{0}^{u}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t-\frac{1}{4}\log^{2}(x)\log^{2}(1+x)+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

Note that

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}x^{n}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{(n+1)^{3}}x^{n+1}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n^{4}}\,,

and

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}(-x)^{n}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{(n+1)^{3}}(-x)^{n+1}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-x)^{n}}{n^{4}}\,,

with the help of Lemmata
1
,
3
and
4
, we get the desired result.
∎

Theorem 3
.

Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}

\displaystyle=-Li_{2}(x)\log(1-x)-\log(x)\log^{2}(1-x)-2\log(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+2Li_{3}(1-x)-2Li_{3}(1)+Li_{3}(x)\,,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}

\displaystyle=-Li_{2}(-x)\log(1+x)-\log(x)\log^{2}(1+x)+\frac{2}{3}\log^{3}(1+x)

\displaystyle\quad+2\log(1+x)Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)+2Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-2Li_{3}(1)+Li_{3}(-x)\,.

Proof.

Integrating the generating function of
H_{n}^{(2)}
, we can write

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n+1}x^{n+1}=-\log(1-x)Li_{2}(x)-\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n+1}(-x)^{n+1}=-\log(1+x)Li_{2}(-x)-\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

With the help of Lemma
3
, we get the desired result.
∎

Theorem 4
.

Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}}x^{n}

\displaystyle=-Li_{4}(x)-2Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{-x}{1-x}\bigg)+2Li_{4}(1-x)-2Li_{4}(1)+2Li_{3}(x)\log(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad-2Li_{3}(1)\log(1-x)+\frac{1}{2}Li_{2}(x)^{2}-\frac{1}{6}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{3}\log(x)\log^{3}(1-x)-\frac{1}{12}\log^{4}(1-x)\,,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}}(-x)^{n}

\displaystyle=-Li_{4}(-x)-2Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{x}{1+x}\bigg)-2Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)+2Li_{4}(1)

\displaystyle\quad-2Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{x}{1+x}\bigg)\log(1+x)-2Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)\log(1+x)+\frac{1}{2}Li_{2}(-x)^{2}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1}{6}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(1+x)-\frac{2}{3}\log(x)\log^{3}(1+x)+\frac{1}{2}\log^{4}(1+x)\,.

Proof.

Integrating the generating function of
H_{n}^{(2)}
, we can write

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{(n+1)^{2}}x^{n+1}

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{u}\int_{0}^{u}\frac{Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t-\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t+\log(x)\log(1-x)Li_{2}(x)+\frac{1}{2}Li_{2}(x)^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{(n+1)^{2}}(-x)^{n+1}

\displaystyle=\log(x)\int_{0}^{-x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)Li_{2}(-t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\int_{0}^{-x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t+\log(x)\log(1+x)Li_{2}(-x)+\frac{1}{2}Li_{2}(-x)^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)\log^{2}(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

With the help of Lemmata
1
,
4
and Theorem
3
, we get the desired result.
∎

Remark 1
.

It seems difficult to give explicit expressions for infinite series of types

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{4}}x^{n}
and

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{3}}x^{n}
, since the integrals
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t
and
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t
are not known to be related to the polylogarithm functions.

Example 1
.

Some illustrative examples are as following:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}\cdot 2^{n}}=Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{2}\bigg)+\frac{1}{720}\pi^{4}-\frac{1}{8}\log(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{1}{24}\log^{4}(2)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}\cdot 2^{n}}(-1)^{n}=2Li_{4}\bigg(-\frac{1}{2}\bigg)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{3}\bigg)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{2}{3}\bigg)-\frac{1}{90}\pi^{4}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad+\log\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{2}{3}\bigg)+\log\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{3}\bigg)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad+\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log^{2}\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)-\frac{1}{12}\log^{4}\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)-\frac{1}{6}\log(6)\log^{3}\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}(-1)^{n}=2Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{2}\bigg)-\frac{11}{360}\pi^{4}+\frac{7}{4}\log(2)\zeta(3)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad-\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(2)+\frac{1}{12}\log^{4}(2)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{(n+1)2^{n+1}}=-\frac{1}{4}\zeta(3)+\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log(2)-\frac{1}{6}\log^{3}(2)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n\cdot 2^{n}}=\frac{5}{8}\zeta(3)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}(\sqrt{5}-1)^{n+1}}{(n+1)2^{n+1}}=-\frac{2}{5}\zeta(3)-\frac{1}{5}\pi^{2}\log\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\bigg)+\frac{2}{3}\log^{3}\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\bigg)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}(\sqrt{5}-1)^{n}}{n\cdot 2^{n}}=-\frac{2}{5}\zeta(3)-\frac{1}{5}\pi^{2}\log\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\bigg)+\frac{2}{3}\log^{3}\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\bigg)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-1)^{n}=\frac{1}{12}\pi^{2}\log(2)-\zeta(3)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}\cdot 2^{n}}=Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{2}\bigg)+\frac{1}{1440}\pi^{4}+\frac{1}{4}\log(2)\zeta(3)-\frac{1}{24}\pi^{2}\log^{2}(2)+\frac{1}{24}\log^{4}(2)\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}\cdot 2^{n}}(-1)^{n}=-Li_{4}\bigg(-\frac{1}{2}\bigg)-2\bigg(Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{1}{3}\bigg)+Li_{4}\bigg(\frac{2}{3}\bigg)-\frac{1}{90}\pi^{4}\bigg)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad-2\bigg(\log\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{2}{3}\bigg)+\log\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{3}\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad+\frac{1}{2}Li_{2}\bigg(-\frac{1}{2}\bigg)^{2}-\frac{1}{6}\pi^{2}\log^{2}\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)+\frac{1}{2}\log^{4}\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad+\frac{2}{3}\log(2)\log^{3}\bigg(\frac{3}{2}\bigg)\,.

3
Infinite series containing generalized alternating harmonic numbers

Proposition 1
.

Let
p,n\in\mathbb{N}
and
k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
with
0\leq k\leq p
, then we have

\displaystyle\overline{H}_{n}^{(p)}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{j}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{n+1-\ell}(n+1)}{\ell^{j}(n+1-\ell)^{p+1-j}}+(-1)^{k+1}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{n+1-\ell}}{\ell^{k}(n+1-\ell)^{p-k}}\,.

In particular, we have

\displaystyle\overline{H}_{n}^{(p)}=\frac{n+1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{n+1-\ell}}{\ell^{j}(n+1-\ell)^{p+1-j}}\quad(p+n\quad\hbox{even})\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{n+1-\ell}}{\ell^{j}(n+1-\ell)^{p+1-j}}=0\quad(p+n\quad\hbox{odd})\,.

Proof.

Integrating the generating function of
\overline{H}_{n}^{(p)}
, we can write

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(p)}}{n+1}x^{n+1}=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{-Li_{p}(-t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-Li_{1}(x)Li_{p}(-x)+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{1}(t)Li_{p-1}(-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-Li_{1}(x)Li_{p}(-x)+Li_{2}(x)Li_{p-1}(-x)-\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)Li_{p-2}(-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{k}(-1)^{j}Li_{j}(x)Li_{p+1-j}(-x)+(-1)^{k+1}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{k}(t)Li_{p-k}(-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\quad(0\leq k\leq p)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j}Li_{j}(x)Li_{p+1-j}(-x)+(-1)^{p}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{p}(t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

Comparing the coefficients on both sides gives the desired result.
∎

Lemma 6
.

Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(1-t)\log(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{8}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-\sqrt{x})-\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-x)-\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1+x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{3}\log^{3}(1+x)+\frac{1}{2}\log(1-\sqrt{x})Li_{2}(1-\sqrt{x})-\log(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\log(1+x)Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-\frac{1}{2}Li_{3}(1-\sqrt{x})+Li_{3}(1-x)-\frac{3}{2}Li_{3}(1)

\displaystyle\quad+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)\,.

Proof.

We start from

\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t^{2})}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

This integral equals to

\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t+2\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(1-t)\log(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

It is obvious that

\displaystyle\quad\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t^{2})}{t}\mathrm{d}t=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-\sqrt{x})+\log(1-\sqrt{x})Li_{2}(1-\sqrt{x})-Li_{3}(1-\sqrt{x})+Li_{3}(1)\,.

With the help of (
1
) and (
2
), we get the desired result.
∎

Theorem 6
.

Let
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}

\displaystyle=Li_{2}(-x)\log(1-x)-Li_{3}(-x)+\frac{1}{8}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-\sqrt{x})-\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1+x)+\frac{1}{3}\log^{3}(1+x)+\frac{1}{2}\log(1-\sqrt{x})Li_{2}(1-\sqrt{x})

\displaystyle\quad-\log(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)+\log(1+x)Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-\frac{1}{2}Li_{3}(1-\sqrt{x})

\displaystyle\quad+Li_{3}(1-x)+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-\frac{3}{2}Li_{3}(1)\,,

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}

\displaystyle=Li_{2}(x)\log(1+x)-Li_{3}(x)+\frac{1}{8}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-\sqrt{x})-\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1}{2}\log(x)\log^{2}(1+x)+\frac{1}{3}\log^{3}(1+x)+\frac{1}{2}\log(1-\sqrt{x})Li_{2}(1-\sqrt{x})

\displaystyle\quad-\log(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)+\log(1+x)Li_{2}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-\frac{1}{2}Li_{3}(1-\sqrt{x})

\displaystyle\quad+Li_{3}(1-x)+Li_{3}\bigg(\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg)-\frac{3}{2}Li_{3}(1)\,.

Proof.

Integrating the generating function of
\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}
, we can write

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n+1}x^{n+1}=\log(1-x)Li_{2}(-x)+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(1-t)\log(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n+1}(-x)^{n+1}=\log(1+x)Li_{2}(x)+\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(1-t)\log(1+t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t\,.

Note that

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n-1}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}x^{n}}{n^{3}}\,,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n-1}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n^{3}}\,,

with the help of Lemma

∎
