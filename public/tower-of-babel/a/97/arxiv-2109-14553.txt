Abstract
Hybrid organic-inorganic halide perovskites for photovoltaics have attracted research interest due to their unique material properties, but suffered from poor material stability. In this work, we investigated the surface phase diagrams of PbI 2 {{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} and cubic CH 3 ​ NH 3 ​ PbI 3 {{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} ( MAPbI 3 {{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} ) for a better understanding of precursor effect on perovskite synthesis via solid-gas reaction, by density functional theory calculations combined with thermodynamics. Using the devised slab models of PbI 2 {{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} (001) and MAPbI 3 {{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} (100), (110) and (111) surfaces with perfect and various vacant defect terminations, we calculated their formation energies and adsorption energies of CH 3 ​ NH 2 {{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} molecule on the PbI 2 {{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} (001) surfaces under different synthesis conditions of temperature, pressure and pH via chemical potentials of species. Our calculations revealed that the adsorption can be facilitated by including HI or NH 4 ​ I {{\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{4}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{I}}} molecule by dissociation of this additive and formation of CH 3 ​ NH 3 + {{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{+}}}} cation or NH 3 {{\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} - CH 3 ​ NH 3 + {{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{+}}}} complex, which is beneficial for conversion of PbI 2 {{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} to MAPbI 3 {{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} via solid-gas reaction. Furthermore, we found that among different perovskite MAPbI 3 {{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}} surfaces, the MA-terminated (110) and MAI-terminated (100) surfaces are placed on the thermodynamically stable region of chemical potentials at pH values of 1 and 6, being agreed well with the experimental findings. We believe this work gives a fundamental understanding of solid-gas reaction for high-crystallinity perovskite synthesis towards perovskite solar cells with improved stability.

Methods

All calculations within density functional theory (DFT) framework were performed by using the pseudopotential plane-wave method as implemented in the Quantum ESPRESSO (QE, version 6.2) package
[
32
]
.
With additional consideration of scalar relativistic effect for heavy I and Pb atoms, the ultrasoft pseudopotentials of constituent atoms were constructed using the LD1 code and input files from the PSlibrary (version 1.0.0), where the electron configurations are Pb-
5d^{10}6s^{2}6p^{2}
, I-
4d^{10}5s^{2}5p^{5}
, C-
2s^{2}2p^{2}
, H-
1s^{1}
, O-
2s^{2}2p^{4}
and N-
2s^{2}2p^{3}
.
To account for the effect of van der Waals (vdW) interactions between the molecular moieties, we adopted the rev-vdW-DF2 exchange-correlation functional
[
33
,
34
,
35
]
.
We set kinetic cutoff energies as 70 and 700 Ry for the wave function and electron density, and special
k
-point meshes as
6\times 6\times 6
and
2\times 2\times 1
for bulk and surface calculations, respectively.
With these parameters, the total energy of bulk and formation energy of surface converged within 5 meV per atom and 5 mJ/m
2
, respectively.
All atoms were relaxed until the atomic forces converged to
5\times 10^{-4}
Ry/Bohr, and for the lattice optimization, until the stress was less than 0.05 kbar.

As a preliminary step, we optimized the bulk unit cells of rhombohedral
{{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
with space group
P\bar{3}m1
and pseudo-cubic
{{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
with space group
Pm\bar{3}m

Then, we built surface models by cleaving the optimized bulk unit cells along the selected crystallographic directions, considering different terminations.
For
{{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}

When increasing the atomic layers from nine to eleven, the surface energy was found to increase by only 0.01 J/m
2

To consider defective surfaces and different adsorbate coverage, the surface unit cells were set to
2\times 2

1\times 1

The morphology of perovskite surface and interface is modified according to the growth condition, which plays a decisive role in photovoltaic performance of PCSs.
In this sense, we drew the surface phase diagram of cubic

{{\text{I}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}

{{\text{MAI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
and (111)-Pb along with the modified (111)-I surface were treated in this work.

4
Conclusions

In this work, we have systematically investigated
{{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
and cubic
{{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
surfaces by using combined density functional theory and thermodynamic calculations, aiming at a better understanding of precursor effect on perovskite synthesis via solid-gas reaction.
We devised the slab models of
{{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
(001) and
{{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
(100), (110) and (111) surfaces with perfect and various vacant defect terminations, and calculated their formation energies and adsorption energies of
{{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
molecule on the
{{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
surfaces under different synthesis conditions of temperature, pressure and pH through chemical potentials of species.
We revealed that the adsorption of
{{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
molecule can be facilitated by including HI or
{{\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{4}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{I}}}
molecule due to lowering adsorption energies and their being negative even at 300 or 400 K through dissociation of this additive and formation of
{{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{+}}}}
cation or
{{\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
-
{{\text{CH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}\nolinebreak\text{NH}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{+}}}}
complex, which is advantageous for conversion of
{{\text{PbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{2}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
to
{{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
via solid-gas reaction.
Moreover, it was found that among nine different types of the
{{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
low-index surfaces, the MA-terminated (110) and MAI-terminated (100) surfaces occupy the thermodynamically stable region for equilibrium synthesis of bulk
{{\text{MAPbI}_{\vphantom{\text{}}\text{3}}^{\vphantom{\text{}}\vphantom{\text{\smash[t]{2+}}}\text{}}}}
at pH = 1 and 6, being agreed well with the experimental findings.
This work can provide a fundamental understanding of solid-gas reaction for high-crystallinity perovskite synthesis towards high performance perovskite solar cells.
