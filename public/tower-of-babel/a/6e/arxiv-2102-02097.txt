Evolutionary search for cobalt-rich compounds in the yttrium-cobalt-boron system
Takahiro Ishikawa, Taro Fukazawa, Guangzong Xing, Terumasa Tadano, Takashi Miyake

Abstract
Modern high-performance permanent magnets are made from alloys of rare earth and transition metal elements, and large magnetization is achieved in the alloys with high concentration of transition metals. We applied evolutionary search scheme based on first-principles calculations to the Y-Co-B system and predicted 37 cobalt-rich compounds with high probability of being stable. Focusing on remarkably cobalt-rich compounds, YCo 16 \text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}} and YCo 20 \text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}} , we found that, although they are metastable phases, the phase stability is increased with increase of temperature due to the contribution of vibrational entropy. The magnetization and Curie temperature are higher by 0.22 T and 204 K in YCo 16 \text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}} and by 0.29 T and 204 K in YCo 20 \text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}} than those of Y 2 ​ Co 17 \text{Y}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{Co}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{17}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{17}}\text{}} which has been well studied as strong magnetic compounds.

I
Introduction

Rare-earth magnets are strong permanent magnets, which mainly consist of rare-earth elements and 3d transition metals (Fe and/or Co). High Fe/Co concentration gives rise to high magnetization and rare earths are a source of high magnetocrystalline anisotropy which is essential for high coercivity. Rare-earth magnets have been developed since the discovery of large magnetocrystalline anisotropy in an alloy of yttrium and cobalt,
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{5}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{5}}\text{}}

Hoffer and Strnat 1966
, and neodymium magnets are the strongest type of permanent magnet commercially available, and its main phase is formed by
\text{Nd}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{Fe}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{14}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{14}}\text{}}\text{B}
compound
Sagawa et al. 1984
,
which has the saturation magnetization of 1.85 T at 4.2 K, magnetocrystalline
anisotropy field of 5.3 MA/m at room temperature, and Curie
temperature of 586 K
Hirosawa et al. 1986
.

The magnetization is expected to be further increased using iron-richer compounds than
\text{Nd}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{Fe}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{14}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{14}}\text{}}\text{B}
, and

(

In the present study, we search for novel stable compounds with novel Fe/Co-rich rare-earth compounds using composition and crystal structure prediction scheme based on first-principles calculations and evolutionary algorithm. Here, we focused on Y-Co-B system for the following reasons: (i) Y is favorable for theoretical treatment because it has no

As a result, we found 37 cobalt-rich compounds including remarkably Co-rich
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}}

\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}}

For the pure elements, we used the hcp structures for Co and Y, and a rhombohedral
R\bar{3}m
structure for B.
Next, we constructed the preliminary convex hull of Y-Co-B system by performing the structural optimizations for the compounds in the initial set.
Then, by applying evolutionary operators, “mating”, “mutation”, and “adaptive mutation”
Ishikawa and Miyake 2020
to target compounds selected from the compounds whose distance to the convex hull is small (0–4.4 mRy/atom), new compositions and structures with high probability of being stable were created. Repeatedly performing the creation of compounds and the update of the convex hull, we searched for stable compounds.

We combined our evolutionary construction code with the Quantum ESPRESSO (QE) code
Giannozzi et al. 2009
to perform the optimizations of the structures created by the operators. We used the generalized gradient approximation by
Perdew, Burke and Ernzerhof (PBE) for the exchange-correlation functional in the framework of the projector augmented wave (PAW) method
Kresse and Joubert 1999
. We got the PAW potentials from the QE web site
QEw
. The energy cutoff was set at 100 Ry for the wave function and 800 Ry for the charge density.
The maximum number of atoms in calculation cell is 84, and
the
k
-space integration over the Brillouin zone (BZ) was carried out
on 12
\times
12
\times
12, 8
\times
8
\times
8, 6
\times
6
\times
6, and 4
\times
4
\times
4 grids for the structures including 1–4, 5–12, 13–30, and more than 30 atoms in the calculation cell, respectively.

To investigate dynamical and thermodynamical stability of the predicted compounds, we calculated phonon dispersion and vibrational free energy using the Vienna
ab initio
simulation package (VASP)
Kresse and Furthmüller 1996
and the PHONOPY code
Togo and Tanaka 2015
.
Second-order interatomic force constants were computed by the finite-displacement method based on harmonic approximation, as implemented in PHONOPY. Total number of atoms in each supercell is
\sim
100 or larger, which was sufficient to reach the convergence of the vibrational free energy.
The energy cutoff for the wave function was set at 400 eV, and the
k
-point mesh was generated automatically in such a way that the mesh density in the reciprocal space is larger than 450 Å
-3
. The convergence criteria of energy and force minimization were set to 10
-8
and 10
-7
eV, respectively.

For the stable compounds, we calculated the intersite magnetic couplings using the Liechtenstein’s method
Liechtenstein et al. 1987
.
For this purpose, we used AkaiKKR
Aka
,
a first-principles program of Korringa-Kohn-Rostoker (KKR) Green’s
function method, within the local density approximation.
The Curie temperature
T_{\text{C}}
is evaluated from a classical spin model within the mean-field approximation.
Other computational details are the same to the settings
in Ref.
Fukazawa et al. 2018
.

IV
Conclusion

In conclusion, we searched for stable compounds in the Y-Co-B system,
\text{Y}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{\text{$1-x-y$}}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{\text{$1-x-y$}}}\text{}}\text{Co}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{\text{$x$}}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{\text{$x$}}}\text{}}\text{B}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{\text{$y$}}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{\text{$y$}}}\text{}}
, using the evolutionary construction technique of a formation-energy convex hull. Focusing on Co-rich (
0.75\leq x\leq 1
) and low energy (
\Delta H\leq 4.4
mRy/atom) region, we predicted 34 compounds, including novel Co-rich compounds,
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}}
and
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}}
. In addition, we obtained a new stable structure of
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{5}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{5}}\text{}}
, and a low energy path connecting between
\text{Y}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{Co}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{14}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{14}}\text{}}\text{B}
and
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{7}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{7}}\text{}}
. Phonon calculations predict that
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}}
and
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}}
are dynamically stable and the hull distance
\Delta H
is decreased to 1.48 mRy/atom for
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}}
and 2.72 mRy/atom for
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}}
with increase of temperature to 1200 K due to the contribution of the vibrational free energy.
The calculated
M
and
T_{\text{C}}
values are 1.474 T and 1378 K in
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}}
and 1.539 T and 1378 K in
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}}
, which are larger than those in
\text{Y}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{2}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{2}}\text{}}\text{Co}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{17}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{17}}\text{}}
and
\text{YCo}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{12}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{12}}\text{}}
.
We performed the same calculations for
\text{YFe}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{16}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{16}}\text{}}
and
\text{YFe}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\vphantom{\text{2}}\smash[t]{\text{20}}}^{\vphantom{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\hphantom{\text{20}}\text{}}
, and obtained the results that, although the

values are decreased in

This study provides novel Co-rich compounds,
