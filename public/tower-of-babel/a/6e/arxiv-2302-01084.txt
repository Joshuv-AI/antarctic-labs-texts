Introduction

We write
Y(p_{1},p_{2};G)
for the optimal constant (the optimal ratio of both sides) of Young’s convolution inequality on a locally compact group
G
.
The main result of this paper is that
Y(p_{1},p_{2};G)\leq Y(p_{1},p_{2};H)
holds for any closed subgroup
H\subset G
(Theorem
1.2
).
As a corollary, we generalize the estimate of

from above by Beckner
[

]
, Fournier
[

]
, Klein–Russo
[

]
, and Nielsen
[

]
to any connected Lie group

such that the center of the semisimple part is a finite group such as connected linear Lie groups and connected solvable Lie groups.
That is, we have

, where

is the dimension of the maximal compact subgroups of

We set the Hölder conjugate

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1.

The

We define the convolution

of measurable functions

as

\displaystyle\phi_{1}*\phi_{2}(g^{\prime}):=\int_{G}\phi_{1}(g)\phi_{2}(g^{-1}g^{\prime})dg.

(1.2)

In addition, there exists a unique continuous homomorphism

such that

\displaystyle\int_{G}\phi(g^{-1})dg=\int_{G}\frac{\phi(g)}{\Delta(g)}dg.

(1.3)

This

.
Then the notion of the optimal constant

is defined as follows.

Definition 1.1

(
[
KR78
, Section 2]

[
Nie94
]
)
.

For any locally compact group
G

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}}\geq 1,

the optimal constant

\displaystyle Y(p_{1},p_{2};G):=\sup\{\|\phi_{1}*(\phi_{2}\Delta^{1/p_{1}^{\prime}})\|_{p}\mid\phi_{1},\phi_{2}\colon G\to\mathbb{C},\;\|\phi_{1}\|_{p_{1}}=\|\phi_{2}\|_{p_{2}}=1\}.

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}}=1+\frac{1}{p}.

The main result of this paper is the following theorem.

Theorem 1.2
.

Suppose that

The inequality (

Theorem

In addition, if the identity component

Although Corollary

We prove Corollary

Proof of Corollary

In this section, we show Corollary

by using Theorem
1.2
and the argument of Jing–Tran–Zhang
[
JTZ23
]
.
We write
\mathcal{A}
for the set of the connected Lie groups

satisfying the assumption in Corollary

(i.e. the center of the semisimple part of

is a finite group).
We note that any connected solvable Lie group is an element of

.
Let

be the dimension of the maximal compact subgroups for a connected Lie group

We show the following lemma to prove Corollary
1.3
.

Lemma 5.1
.

Suppose that a real number

is defined for each
G\in\mathcal{A}
, and

\displaystyle d(G)\geq d(H)+d(G/H)

holds for any connected closed normal subgroup
H\in\mathcal{A}
of any

with
G/H\in\mathcal{A}
.
For
G\in\mathcal{A}
, we denote by

the inequality

\displaystyle d(G)\geq d(\mathbb{R})\dim G+(d(\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})-d(\mathbb{R}))r(G).

(5.2)

(1)

Suppose that a connected closed normal subgroup
H\in\mathcal{A}
of
G\in\mathcal{A}
satisfies
G/H\in\mathcal{A}
.
If
I(H)
and

I(G)
also holds.

(2)

Every non-trivial connected solvable Lie group
G
satisfies

.

(3)

Furthermore, we assume that

\displaystyle d(\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})=0\leq d(H)\leq d(G)

(5.3)

for any connected closed subgroup
H\in\mathcal{A}
of any
G\in\mathcal{A}
.
Then every
G\in\mathcal{A}
satisfies
I(G)
, that is,

\displaystyle d(G)\geq d(\mathbb{R})(\dim G-r(G)).

(5.4)

Jing–Tran–Zhang generalized the Brunn–Minkowski inequality to any Lie group by essentially using Lemma
5.1

[
JTZ23
, Theorem 1.1]
.
Now we give some examples of Lemma
5.1

(2)
.

Example 5.2
.

(1)

If the equality of (

) holds for any connected closed normal subgroup

of any

, then the equality of

also holds for any connected solvable Lie group

.
Actually, if

with

(2)
.

(2)

Let

for connected Lie group

.
Now we show

.
