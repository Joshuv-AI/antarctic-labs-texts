The Reconstruction of Constant Jerk Parameter with f(R,T) Gravity


Abstract
In this work, we have developed an FLRW type model of a universe which displays transition from deceleration in the past to the acceleration at the present. For this, we have considered field equations of f(R,T) gravity and have taken f ⁡ ( R , T ) = R + 2 ​ λ ​ T f(R,T)=R+2\lambda T , λ \lambda being an arbitrary constant. We have estimated the λ \lambda parameter in such a way that the transition red shift is found similar in the deceleration parameter, pressure and the equation of state parameter ω \omega . The present value of Hubble parameter is estimated on the basis of the three types of observational data set: latest compilation of 46 46 Hubble data set, SNe Ia 580 580 data sets of distance modulus and 66 66 Pantheon data set of apparent magnitude which comprised of 40 SN Ia bined and 26 high redshift data’s in the range 0.014 ≤ z ≤ 2.26 0.014\leq z\leq 2.26 . These data are compared with theoretical results through the χ 2 \chi^{2} statistical test. Interestingly, the model satisfies all the three weak, strong and dominant energy conditions. The model fits well with observational findings. We have discussed some of the physical aspects of the model, in particular the age of the universe.
