A Note on Islands in Schwarzschild Black Holes
Irina Aref’eva, Igor Volovich

Abstract
We consider evaporation of the Schwarzschild black hole and note that island configurations do not provide a bounded entanglement entropy. The same remark is valid also for some other static black holes. A proposal for improving the situation is discussed.

r_{h}\ll(c\,G\,b/24\pi)^{1/3}

Moreover, the Schwarzschild solution in the Kruskal coordinates is singular for small

However the extremization procedure makes

\,2\pi a^{2}/G=8\pi GM^{2}

this term becomes subdominant for small

c\,b/(12GM)

In first scenario,

•

For more special case, Fig.

\displaystyle U=-\sqrt{\frac{r-r_{h}}{r_{h}}}\,e^{-\frac{t-(r-r_{h})}{2r_{h}}},\qquad V=\sqrt{\frac{r-r_{h}}{r_{h}}}\hskip 1.42262pte^{\frac{t+(r-r_{h})}{2r_{h}}}

\displaystyle ds_{2-dim\,part\,Schw}^{2}=-W^{-2}dUdV,\qquad W=\sqrt{\frac{r}{4r_{h}^{3}}}\,e^{\frac{r-r_{h}}{2r_{h}}}\,.

\displaystyle d(\ell_{1},\ell_{2})=\sqrt{\frac{\big(U(\ell_{2})-U(\ell_{1})\big)\big(V(\ell_{1})-V(\ell_{2})\big)}{W(\ell_{1})W(\ell_{2})}}.

S_{n{\cal I}}\simeq\frac{c}{6}\frac{t_{b}}{r_{h}}\,\qquad r_{h}=2GM

In such a case an island is expected to emerge

Time dependence of the entanglement entropy of the evaporating black hole in thermal coordinates

\displaystyle{\mathscr{U}}

\displaystyle=

(4.3)

\displaystyle\frac{\,d{\mathscr{U}}d{\mathscr{V}}}{{\mathscr{W}}^{2}},\qquad{\mathscr{W}}=\frac{1}{B}\left(\frac{{\mathscr{U}}{\mathscr{V}}}{(1-\frac{2GM}{r})}\right)^{1/2},

Conclusion and discussion

We have considered the evaporation of the Schwarzschild black hole and note that, generally speaking, the island
configurations do not provide a bounded entanglement entropy in the end of the black hole evaporation.
Despite the fact that
including
the island and the extremization about its location results in saturation of the entropy for the eternal black hole, the evaporating of black hole ends up by unbounded increasing of the entropy.

One can argue that the formula (
1.2
) applies for a black hole held in equilibrium with radiation, and not for a freely evaporating black hole. The second term
cb/6r_{h}
can be interpreted as the entropy of infalling radiation

In

Depending of the initial state parameters the unboundeness of the entanglement entropy can occur inside or outside of the Planck length.
