Comment on “Improved RSA Technique with Efficient Data Integrity Verification for Outsourcing Database in Cloud”
Chebrolu Deepak Kumar, Lilly Kumari Biswas, Srijanee Mookherji, Gowri Raghavendra Narayan Kurmala, Vanga Odelu

Abstract
In 2022, Neela-Kavitha [Wireless Personal Communications, 123(3):2431–2448, 2022] proposed an improved RSA encryption algorithm (IREA) for cloud environment. In this paper, we review and comment on the correctness of the IREA technique. We prove that the private key generation in the proposed IREA is mathematical incorrect. That is, decryption of the cipher generated by the public key is not possible with the private key. Further, we discuss the possible modifications in IREA to make it correct decryption.

The proposed protocol IREA suffers from correctness error. According to the Euler and Fermat’s little theorem
Niven
et al. 1991
,
M
is relatively prime to
j
, where
j=b\times v
and
b
and
v
are large primes.

M^{(\phi(j))}\equiv 1\pmod{j}.

(1)

Here,
\phi{(j)}
is the Euler’s Totient function which gives the number of positive integers less than
j
which are relatively prime to
j
.
For prime numbers,
b

\phi(b)=b-1

(2)

By the elementary properties of the Euler’s totient function:

\phi(j)=\phi(b)\times\phi(v)=(b-1)\times(v-1)=j-(b+v)+1.

(3)

Since

\phi(j)

Therefore, for some integer

(5)

From Equations

2

and since

M^{k\times\phi(j)+1}\equiv M\pmod{b}

This equality hold for all

Therefore, from Equation

Thus, in the proposed protocol, as per Equation
