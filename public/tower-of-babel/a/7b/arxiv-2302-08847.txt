Probing Baryogenesis with Radiative Beauty Decay and Electron EDM
Wei-Shu Hou, 1 Girish Kumar 1 and Tanmoy Modak 2

Abstract
With the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) running, we should probe electroweak baryogenesis (EWBG) while probing C ​ P CP violation (CPV) with electron electric dipole moment (eEDM). Rooted in the flavor structure of the Standard Model (SM), the general two Higgs doublet model (g2HDM) with a second set of Yukawa couplings can deliver EWBG while surviving eEDM. We point out a chiral-enhanced top-bottom interference effect that makes b → s ​ γ b\to s\gamma decay an exquisite window on EWBG and eEDM, and illustrate the importance of the Δ ​ A C ​ P \Delta A_{CP} observable at Belle II.
