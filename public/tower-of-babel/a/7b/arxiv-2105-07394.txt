Abstract
We construct a B − L B-L model with A 4 × Z 3 × Z 4 A_{4}\times Z_{3}\times Z_{4} flavor symmetry which can accounts for the recent 3 + 1 3+1 active-sterile neutrino data. The tiny neutrino mass and the mass hierarchy are obtained by the type-I seesaw mechanism. The hierarchy of the lepton masses is satisfied by a factor of v H ​ ( v l Λ ) 2 ∼ 10 − 4 ​ GeV v_{H}\left(\frac{v_{l}}{\Lambda}\right)^{2}\sim 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{GeV} of the electron mass compared to the muon and tau masses of the order of v H ​ v l Λ ∼ 10 − 1 ​ GeV \frac{v_{H}v_{l}}{\Lambda}\sim 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{GeV} . The 3 + 1 3+1 active-sterile neutrino mixings are predicted to be 0.015 ≤ | U 14 | 2 ≤ 0.045 0.015\leq|U_{14}|^{2}\leq 0.045 , 0.004 ≤ | U 24 | 2 ≤ 0.012 0.004\leq|U_{24}|^{2}\leq 0.012 and 0.004 ≤ | U 34 | 2 ≤ 0.014 0.004\leq|U_{34}|^{2}\leq 0.014 for normal hierarchy while 0.020 ≤ | U 14 | 2 ≤ 0.045 0.020\leq|U_{14}|^{2}\leq 0.045 , 0.008 ≤ | U 24 | 2 ≤ 0.018 0.008\leq|U_{24}|^{2}\leq 0.018 and 0.008 ≤ | U 34 | 2 ≤ 0.022 0.008\leq|U_{34}|^{2}\leq 0.022 for inverted hierarchy. Sterile neutrino masses are predicted to be 0.7 ≲ m s ​ ( eV ) ≲ 3.16 0.7\lesssim m_{s}\,(\mathrm{eV})\lesssim 3.16 for normal hierarchy and 2.6 ≲ m s ​ ( eV ) ≲ 7.1 2.6\lesssim m_{s}\,(\mathrm{eV})\lesssim 7.1 for inverted hierarchy. For three neutrino scheme the model predicts 0.3401 ≤ sin 2 ⁡ θ 12 ≤ 0.3415 , 0.460 ≤ sin 2 ⁡ θ 23 ≤ 0.540 , − 0.60 ≤ sin ⁡ δ C ​ P ≤ − 0.20 0.3401\leq\sin^{2}\theta_{12}\leq 0.3415,\,0.460\leq\sin^{2}\theta_{23}\leq 0.540,\,-0.60\leq\sin\delta_{CP}\leq-0.20 for normal hierarchy and 0.3402 ≤ sin 2 ⁡ θ 12 ≤ 0.3416 , 0.434 ≤ sin 2 ⁡ θ 23 ≤ 0.610 , − 0.95 ≤ sin ⁡ δ C ​ P ≤ − 0.60 0.3402\leq\sin^{2}\theta_{12}\leq 0.3416,\,0.434\leq\sin^{2}\theta_{23}\leq 0.610,\,-0.95\leq\sin\delta_{CP}\leq-0.60 for inverted hierarchy.

v_{H}\left(\frac{v_{l}}{\Lambda}\right)^{2}\sim 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{GeV}
of the electron mass compared to the muon and the tau masses of the order of
