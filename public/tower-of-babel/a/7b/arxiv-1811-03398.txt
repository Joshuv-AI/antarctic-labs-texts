On the first-order differential subordination and superordination results for 𝒑 \bm{p}\, -valent functions
Vali Soltani Masih, Ali Ebadian, Shahram Najafzadeh

Abstract
. In this paper, we obtain some application of first-order differential subordination, superordination and sandwich-type results involving operator for certain normalized p p - valent analytic functions. Further, properties of p p - valent functions such as; λ \uplambda - spirallike and λ \uplambda - Robertson of complex order are considered.

Definition 1

(

An univalent dominant that satisfies

\mathfrak{q}

By using Lemma

.
