Tewodros Amdeberhan and Shalosh B. Ekhad

Abstract




Classification:

PART I: Theory

The
unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind
\left[{n\atop k}\right]
enumerate permutations of
n
elements with
k
disjoint cycles. They also arise as coefficients of the rising factorial, i.e.,

x(x+1)(x+2)\cdots(x+n-1)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left[{n\atop k}\right]x^{k}.

The
Stirling numbers of the second kind
{n\brace k}
enumerate the number of ways to partition a set of
n
objects into
k
non-empty subsets. They also arise as coefficients of the falling factorial, i.e.,

\frac{x^{k}}{x(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-k)}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}{n\brace k}x^{n}.

Assume
a
and
b
to be non-negative integers. Our specific interest lies in computing the determinants of the following
n\times n
matrices

M_{n}(a,b)=\left(\left[{i+a\atop j+b}\right]\right)_{1\leq i,j\leq n}\qquad\text{and}\qquad N_{n}(a,b)=\left({i+a\brace j+b}\right)_{1\leq i,j\leq n}.

Denote
\beta_{n}(a,b)=\det(M_{n}(a,b))
and
\gamma_{n}(a,b)=\det(N_{n}(a,b))
. Let
[a]=\{1,2,\dots,a\}
be the integer interval. Given a partition
\lambda
, the
Schur functions
can be given by

s_{\lambda}(\xi_{1},\dots,\xi_{a})=\frac{\det\left(\xi_{i}^{\lambda_{j}+a-j}\right)_{1\leq i,j\leq a}}{\det\left(\xi_{i}^{a-j}\right)_{1\leq i,j\leq a}}.

We are now ready to state our results.

Theorem 1.
For
a,b,n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
and
b\leq a
, the sequence
\beta_{n}(a,b)
has a rational generating function, in the variable
q
, with linearly factored denominator having the form

\prod\Sb i_{1}<i_{2}<\dots<i_{b}\\i_{1},i_{2},\dots,i_{b}\in[a]\left(1-\frac{a!}{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots i_{b}}q\right).

Theorem 2.
Denote
(n^{c})=(n,n,\dots,n)\vdash cn
to be a partition of
cn
. Then, we have

\beta_{n}(a,b)=s_{(n^{a-b})}(1,2,\dots,a)\qquad\text{and}\qquad\gamma_{n}(a,b)=s_{((a-b)^{n}}(1,2,\dots,a)).

Proof.
First, observe that the Stirling numbers of the first and second kinds are the respective specializations of

e_{m}
and
h_{m}
. Both assertions follow from the Jacobi-Trudi and Nägelsbach-Kostka identities

s_{\lambda}(\boldsymbol{\xi})=\det(e_{\lambda_{j}^{\prime}+j-i}(\boldsymbol{\xi}))_{1\leq i,j\leq n}=\det(h_{\lambda_{j}+j-i}(\boldsymbol{\xi}))_{1\leq i,j\leq n},

where
\lambda^{\prime}
is the
conjugate
partition to
\lambda
and
e_{m}(\boldsymbol{\xi}),h_{m}(\boldsymbol{\xi})
are the elementary and the complete homogeneous symmetric functions, respectively.

\square

Theorem 3.
Let
A_{i}=(-1)^{a-i}\cdot\frac{i^{a}}{a!}\binom{a}{i}
, for
i\in[a]
. Then, we have the ”explicit” expressions

\displaystyle\hfil\displaystyle\beta_{n}(a+b,b)=(-1)^{\binom{a}{2}}\sum\Sb 1\leq i_{1}<\cdots<i_{a}\leq a+b\,\,\prod_{\ell=1}^{a}A_{i_{\ell}}\cdot\prod\Sb\ell_{u}<\ell_{v}

\displaystyle\ell_{u},\ell_{v}\in\{i_{1},\dots,i_{a}\}(i_{\ell_{u}}-i_{\ell_{v}})^{2}\cdot\prod_{\ell=1}^{a}i_{\ell}^{n-a+1}=\gamma_{a}(n+b,b).

Remark.
R. Stanley informed the first author that our identity for special case
\beta_{n}(a+1,a)
looks like its should be equivalent to Exercise 7.4 in [2] (see also references therein).

PART II: Computations

\beta_{n}(a,b)
, and also the explicit generating functions
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}(a,b)q^{n}
(that are always rational functions of
q
),
for
all

10\geq n\geq a\geq b\geq 0
