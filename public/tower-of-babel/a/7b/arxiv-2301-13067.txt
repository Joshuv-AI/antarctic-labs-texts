Fuzzy Presheaves are Quasitoposes
Aloïs Rosset Roy Overbeek Jörg Endrullis

Abstract
Quasitoposes encompass a wide range of structures, including various categories of graphs. They have proven to be a natural setting for reasoning about the metatheory of algebraic graph rewriting. In this paper we propose and motivate the notion of fuzzy presheaves , which generalises fuzzy sets and fuzzy graphs. We prove that fuzzy presheaves are rm-adhesive quasitoposes, proving our recent conjecture for fuzzy graphs. Furthermore, we show that simple fuzzy graphs categories are quasitoposes.

In this paper, we do the following.

1.

We propose the notion of a
fuzzy presheaf
, which is a presheaf endowed pointwise with a membership function, generalising fuzzy sets and fuzzy graphs.

2.

We show that fuzzy presheaf categories are rm-adhesive quasitoposes, when the membership values are taken in a complete Heyting algebra (
Theorems

We obtain as a corollary the known result that fuzzy sets form a quasitopos, prove our conjecture that fuzzy graph form a quasitopos

3.

We examine the related question whether simple fuzzy graphs form a quasitopos.
Because simple graphs are not presheaves (nor rm-adhesive), our main theorem cannot be applied.
However, we show that (directed and undirected) simple fuzzy graphs are quasitoposes by using the notion of separated elements.

Theorem 2.1

If

is a small category and

is a family of complete Heyting algebras, then
\mathsf{FuzzyPresheaf}(I,\mathcal{L})
is a quasitopos.

In the rest of this section
I
is a small category and

is a family of complete Heyting algebras.
We give a brief high-level overview of the proof.
We tackle the properties of being a quasitopos one by one (
Definition

12
):

•

Section

2.1
:
Finite limits and colimits exist for presheaves, and it suffices to combine them with a terminal (respectively initial) structure to have finite limits (respectively finite colimits) for fuzzy presheaves.

•

Section

2.2
:
We modify the subobject classifier for presheaves by giving all its elements full membership.
This gives us the regular-subobject classifier for fuzzy presheaves.

•

Section

2.3

We combine how exponential objects look like for fuzzy sets and presheaves to obtain exponential objects for fuzzy presheaves, resulting in cartesian closedness.

•

Section

2.4

Lastly, to show that we have a locally cartesian closed category, we show that every slice of a fuzzy presheaf category gives another fuzzy presheaf category, extending on a result from the literature.

In

5

Hence, adding a fuzzy structure to these categories results in quasitoposes by
Theorem

2.1
.
This raises the question whether simple fuzzy graph categories are quasitoposes, and if so, whether it can be shown using
Theorem

2.1

First, simple graphs do not arise as presheaf categories, meaning
Theorem

2.1
cannot be applied.
Here is one way to observe this: presheaves are toposes and in toposes, all monomorphisms are regular.
However, monomorphisms in directed simple graphs

and in undirected simple graphs

36

are regular only if they reflect edges,
7
7

7

A graph homomorphism

if, for every

f_{V}(v)
and
f_{V}(w)
has an
f
-preimage.

which is not always the case.

Nevertheless, we show in this section that directed and undirected simple fuzzy graph categories are quasitoposes, by using a different technique. In more detail, given a
(Lawvere-Tierney) topology

\tau

\tau
-separated elements form a subcategory which is a quasitopos
[
17
, Theorem 10.1]
.
Vigna
[
42

has used this fact to prove that the (directed/undirected) simple graphs are exactly the
\lnot\hskip 1.00006pt\lnot\hskip 1.00006pt

\lnot\hskip 1.00006pt\lnot\hskip 1.00006pt
is a topology. We extend the approach by Vigna, showing that directed and undirected simple fuzzy graphs are quasitoposes.

Because we start from fuzzy multigraphs, which form a quasitopos, we need to work with the more general definition of a
topology on a quasitopos

The result that the separated elements form a quasitopos still holds true with this more general definition of a topology

Remark 5
.

Definition 20

(

)
.

A

\tau
on a quasitopos

is a family of mappings

for each

.
It sends every monomorphism

to another monomorphism
\tau m:A_{1}\rightarrowtail A

(i)

If
m\leqslant m^{\prime}
, then
\tau m\leqslant\tau m^{\prime}
.

(ii)

m\leqslant\tau m
.

(iii)

\tau\tau m\simeq\tau m
.

(iv)

For all

\tau({f}^{\leftarrow}m)\simeq{f}^{\leftarrow}(\tau m)
.

(v)

If
m
is regular, then so is
\tau m
.

Notice that axioms
(i)-(iii)
says that
\tau
is a closure operation.
For instance in
\mathsf{Set}
, given a subset

,
(ii)
requires that
A_{0}\subseteq\tau(A_{0})\subseteq A
.
By
(iv)
and
(v)
the closure operation must commute with pullbacks and preserve regularity.

6
Conclusion

In this paper, we introduced the concept of fuzzy presheaves and proved that they form rm-adhesive quasitoposes.
Furthermore, we showed that simple fuzzy graphs, both directed and undirected, also form quasitoposes.

There are several directions for future work.
The alternative definition of fuzzy presheaves from
Remark

2
, with poset morphisms, can be explored further.
For graphs, it means that the membership of an edge gives a lower-bound for or determines the membership of its source and target.

To obtain fuzzy presheaves, we have added a pointwise fuzzy structure to presheaves.
More generally, different notions of fuzzy categories have been defined
[
40
,
41
]
.
It is natural to wonder if a fuzzy (quasi)topos is also a quasitopos.

Finally, a more abstract question is whether having a fuzzy structure is a particular instance of a more abstract categorical construction.
For instance, one could express an inequality in a poset
\mathcal{L}
via a natural transformations and then say that a diagram is
\leqslant
-commuting when it commutes up to this natural transformation.
Another possibility would be to consider poset-enriched categories for comparing morphisms via inequality.
Fuzzy structures do look like slice or comma categories but do not precisely fall under their scope.
For comma categories, Artin Glueing gives a nice criteria for obtaining new quasitoposes
[
19
]
.
We wonder if more abstract uniform theorems for obtaining quasitoposes can be expressed and proved.
