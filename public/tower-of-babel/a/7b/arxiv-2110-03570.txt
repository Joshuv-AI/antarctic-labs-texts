Frustration induced inversion of magnetocaloric effect and enhanced cooling power in substituted Pyrochlore Iridates
Vinod Kumar Dwivedi, Prabhat Mandal, Soumik Mukhopadhyay

Abstract
We investigate the effect of partial replacement of extended 5 ​ d 5d Ir 4+ sites by localized 3 3 d Cr 3+ moments on the magnetocaloric properties of Y 2 Ir 2 O 7 (YIO) pyrochlore iridates. We find that Y 2 Ir 2-x Cr x O 7 (YICO) undergoes cluster glass transition, possibly due to RKKY like interaction between localized Cr 3+ moments occupying random sites in the pyrochlore network, mediated by 5 ​ d 5d Ir conduction electrons. The coexistence of ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic clusters give rise to the conventional and inverse magnetocaloric effect (MCE). We observe significant enhancement of conventional as well as inverse MCE with substitution. Although the value of conventional MCE and inverse MCE in substituted Iridates are not large, the effect spans over a giant working temperature window, thus leading to orders of magnitude enhancement of cooling power, the value being comparable to standard magnetocalric materials.
