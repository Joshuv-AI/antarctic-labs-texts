In this paper, we consider the singularly perturbed semilinear elliptic equation with critical nonlinear terms

1.1.
Statement of the main result

Now, let us focus on the problem (

In the present paper, we are mainly interested in finding positive solutions of (

(k.0)

\displaystyle 0<\inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}}k\quantity(x)\leqslant\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}}k\quantity(x)<+\infty
