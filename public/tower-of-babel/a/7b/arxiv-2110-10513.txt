Muon g − 2 g-2 , B → K ( ∗ ) ​ μ + ​ μ − B\to K^{(*)}\mu^{+}\mu^{-} anomalies, and leptophilic dark matter in U ​ ( 1 ) μ − τ U(1)_{\mu-\tau} gauge symmetry
P. Ko, Takaaki Nomura, Hiroshi Okada

Abstract
We propose a new class of U ​ ( 1 ) μ − τ U(1)_{\mu-\tau} gauged model that can explain recent flavor anomalies such as muon g − 2 g-2 , b → s ​ μ + ​ μ − b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-} , as well as include a scalar dark matter candidate while satisfying all the phenomenological constraints. For this purpose, we add new vectorlike quarks and leptons as well as two inert singlet scalars, one leptophilic and the other leptophobic. In our model b → s ​ μ + ​ μ − b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-} anomalies can be explained by loop induced interactions among the SM and exotic quarks and Z ′ Z^{\prime} gauge boson. In our numerical analysis, we show allowed region of our model to be narrow, and it would be tested soon, for example by searching for 4 muons and dimuon + missing transverse momentum from p ​ p → μ + ​ μ − ​ Z ′ pp\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}Z^{\prime} and p ​ p → ν μ , τ ​ ν ¯ μ , τ ​ Z ′ pp\to\nu_{\mu,\tau}\bar{\nu}_{\mu,\tau}Z^{\prime} followed by Z ′ → μ + ​ μ − , ν μ , τ ​ ν ¯ μ , τ Z^{\prime}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-},\nu_{\mu,\tau}\bar{\nu}_{\mu,\tau} at the LHC.

Summary and Conclusions

In this paper, we have proposed a
U(1)_{\mu-\tau}
gauged model
to explain recent anomalies; the muon anomalous magnetic moment and
b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}

at the same time, satisfying several constraints. Also, we have considered a complex scalar boson
DM candidate (
\chi_{l}
) that mainly couples to leptons.
In our model
b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
anomalies can be explained by loop induced interactions among quarks and
Z^{\prime}
. We also estimated one-loop diagrams associated with vector-like quark and
Z(\gamma)
vertices that contribute to Wilson coefficients

to check

We then find that

induced by vector-like quark loop can be small, and we can explain
b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}

In our numerical analysis, we have found allowed regions to explain anomalies while satisfying phenomenological constraints.
This model would be test soon in future measurement such as
pp\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}Z^{\prime}(\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-})

We also have the processes
