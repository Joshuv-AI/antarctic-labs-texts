The refined local lifting problem for cyclic covers of order four The author is supported by the Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics and the Simons Foundation Grant Targeted for Institute of Mathematics, Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology.
Huy Dang

Abstract
Suppose ϕ \phi is a ℤ / 4 \mathbb{Z}/4 -cover of a curve over an algebraically closed field k k of characteristic 2 2 , and Φ 1 \Phi_{1} is a nice lift of ϕ \phi ’s ℤ / 2 \mathbb{Z}/2 -sub-cover to a complete discrete valuation ring R R in characteristic zero. We show that there exist a finite extension R ′ R^{\prime} of R R , which is determined by Φ 1 \Phi_{1} , and a lift Φ \Phi of ϕ \phi to R ′ R^{\prime} whose ℤ / 2 \mathbb{Z}/2 -sub-cover isomorphic to Φ 1 ⊗ R R ′ \Phi_{1}\otimes_{R}R^{\prime} . That result gives a non-trivial family of cyclic covers where Saïdi’s refined lifting conjecture holds. In addition, the manuscript exhibits some phenomena that may shed some light on the mysterious moduli space of wildly ramified Galois covers.

Introduction

As the name suggests, the goal of a
lifting problem

0

[

\Gamma

\Gamma

Proposition 4.9

\begin{split}G_{2}=&G_{2,\min}+2\frac{\sum_{0\leq i<n_{2}-m_{1}}a_{n_{2}-i}X^{i}}{X^{\alpha}\prod_{j=1}^{\beta}(X-v_{j})^{2}}+4\sum_{n_{2}-m_{1}\leq i<n_{2}}\frac{a_{n_{2}-i}}{X\prod_{j=1}^{q_{2}-i/2}(X-v_{j})^{2}}\\
