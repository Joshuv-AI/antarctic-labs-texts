1.1
Statement and Discussion of Results

The splitting approach involves the use of singular minimal hypersurfaces. In
[

Inductive Removal of Singularities
Theorem 1 replaces the singular set
\Sigma_{H}
of

by a minimal boundary. The point is that although, in general, the boundary

The straightforward way to apply Theorem 1 is to use

(H^{n},d_{\mathcal{S}})

U

is an
area minimizer with obstacle

\partial U

in
\overline{X_{U}^{n}}

[

•

However, since

[

•

L^{n-1}

\scal>0

3.3
