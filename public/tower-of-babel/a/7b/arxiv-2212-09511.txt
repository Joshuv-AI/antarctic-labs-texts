Logarithmic negativity of the 1D antiferromagnetic spin- 1 1 Heisenberg model with single-ion anisotropy
Vl. V. Papoyan, G. Gori, V. V. Papoyan (Jr.), A. Trombettoni, N. Ananikian

Abstract
We study the 1D antiferromagnetic spin- 1 1 Heisenberg XXX model with external magnetic field B B and single-ion anisotropy D D on finite chains. We determine the nearest and non-nearest neighbor logarithmic entanglement L ​ N LN . Our main result is the disappearance of L ​ N LN both for nearest and non-nearest neighbor (next-nearest and next-next-nearest) sites at zero temperature and for low-temperature states. Such disappearance occurs at a critical value of B B and D D . The resulting phase diagram for the behaviour of LN is discussed in the B − D B-D plane, including a separating line – ending in a triple point – where the energy density is independent on the size. Finally, results for L ​ N LN at finite temperature as a function of B B and D D are presented and commented. Keywords: Heisenberg model, quantum spin chains, antiferromagnetism, entanglement, logarithmic negativity

II
Model and Definitions

The Hamiltonian for the 1D antiferromagnetic spin-
1
Heisenberg
XXX
model with external magnetic field
B
and single-ion anisotropy
D
is given as

\displaystyle H

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle J\sum_{i=1}^{N}\vec{S}_{i}\cdot\vec{S}_{i+1}+D\sum_{i=1}^{N}(S_{i}^{z})^{2}+B\sum_{i=1}^{N}S_{i}^{z}.

(1)

where
J=1
to fix the energy scale,

D
represents uniaxial single-ion anisotropy,

B
is the controllable parameter (periodic boundary conditions are assumed).
The
local
spin vector
\vec{S}_{i}
for each site has the components of the
spin-
1
operators:

\displaystyle S^{x}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\begin{array}[]{lll}0&1&0\\
1&0&1\\
0&1&0\end{array}\right),\hskip 11.38092ptS^{y}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\begin{array}[]{lll}0&-i&0\\
i&0&-i\\
0&i&0\end{array}\right),\hskip 11.38092ptS^{z}=\left(\begin{array}[]{lll}1&0&0\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&-1\end{array}\right).

At thermal equilibrium, the state of the system is determined by
the density matrix

\hat{\rho}(T)=\frac{e^{-{\frac{H}{k_{B}T}}}}{Z}=\sum_{\alpha}\frac{e^{-{\frac{E_{\alpha}}{k_{B}T}}}}{Z}|\psi_{\alpha}\rangle\langle\psi_{\alpha}|,

(12)

where
E_{\alpha}
are the eigenvalue of the
\alpha
-th quantum many body
eigenstate and the partition function is
Z=\sum_{\alpha}e^{-\beta E_{\alpha}}

with
\beta={1/k_{B}T}
(from now on
k_{B}=1
).

For spin-
1
system the
degree of pairwise entanglement, measured in terms of the
negativity
Ne
, can be employed to evaluate the thermal state of
concern
Vidal
;
Wang
.
The negativity of a state
\rho_{ab}
is defined as

\textit{Ne}=\sum_{i}\ |\mu_{i}|,

(13)

where the
\mu_{i}
’s are negative eigenvalues of

\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}
and
T_{1}
denotes the the partial pairwise
transpose with respect to the first system, i.e., for bipartite
system in state
\rho_{ab}
it is defined as

\langle i_{1},j_{2}|\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}|k_{1},l_{2}\rangle\equiv\langle k_{1},j_{2}|\rho_{ab}|i_{1},l_{2}\rangle,

(14)

for any orthonormal but fixed basis. Here
\rho_{ab}
is a partial trace
of density matrix. Definition (
13
) is equivalent to

\textit{Ne}=\frac{\parallel\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}\parallel_{1}-1}{2},

(15)

where
||\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}||_{1}
is trace norm of
\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}

(
||*||_{1}=Tr\sqrt{*^{{\dagger}}*}
). For unentangled states
negativity vanishes, while
Ne
>
0 gives a computable
measure of thermal entanglement.

The logarithmic negativity
LN

is an entanglement measure defined as

\displaystyle LN=\log_{2}\left(\parallel\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}\parallel_{1}\right)=\log_{2}\left(\sum_{k}|\lambda_{k}|\right)

(16)

where the
\lambda_{k}
’s are the eigenvalues of the partially transposed
matrix
\rho_{ab}^{T_{1}}
.
Since values of negativity for nearest and non-nearest neighbors are small
we consider, as entanglement measure, the logarithmic negativity
LN
.
