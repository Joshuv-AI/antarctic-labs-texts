Edge Contraction and Line Graphs
Hany Ibrahim Peter Tittmann University of Applied Sciences Mittweida

Abstract
Given a family of graphs ℋ \mathcal{H} , a graph G G is ℋ \mathcal{H} -free if any subset of V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) does not induce a subgraph of G G that is isomorphic to any graph in ℋ \mathcal{H} . We present sufficient and necessary conditions for a graph G G such that G / e G/e is ℋ \mathcal{H} -free for any edge e e in E ⁡ ( G ) E(G) . Thereafter, we use these conditions to characterize claw-free and line graphs.
