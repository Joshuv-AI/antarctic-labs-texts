On Star-critical ( K 1 , n , K 1 , m + e ) (K_{1,n},K_{1,m}+e) Ramsey numbers
C. J. Jayawardene

Abstract
We say that K n → ( G , H ) K_{n}\rightarrow(G,H) , if for every red/blue coloring of edges of the complete graph K n K_{n} , there exists a red copy of G G , or a blue copy of H H in the coloring of K n K_{n} . The Ramsey number r ⁡ ( G , H ) r(G,H) is the smallest positive integer n n such that K n → ( G , H ) K_{n}\rightarrow(G,H) . Let r ⁡ ( n , m ) = r ⁡ ( K n , K m ) r(n,m)=r(K_{n},K_{m}) . A closely related concept of Ramsey numbers is the Star-critical Ramsey number r ∗ ​ ( G , H ) r_{*}(G,H) defined as the largest value of k k such that K r ⁡ ( G , H ) − 1 ⊔ K 1 , k → ( G , H ) K_{r(G,H)-1}\sqcup K_{1,k}\rightarrow(G,H) . Literature on survey papers in this area reveals many unsolved problems related to these numbers. One of these problems is the calculation of Ramsey numbers for certain classes of graphs. The primary objective of this paper is to calculate the Star critical Ramsey numbers for the case of Stars versus K 1 , m + e . K_{1,m}+e. The methodology that we follow in solving this problem is to first find a closed form for the Ramsey number r ∗ ​ ( K 1 , n , K 1 , m + e ) r_{*}(K_{1,n},K_{1,m}+e) for all n , m ≥ 3 n,m\geq 3 . Based on the values of r ∗ ​ ( K 1 , n , K 1 , m + e ) r_{*}(K_{1,n},K_{1,m}+e) for different n , m n,m , we arrive at a general formula for r ∗ ​ ( K 1 , n , K 1 , m + e ) r_{*}(K_{1,n},K_{1,m}+e) . Henceforth, we show that r ∗ ​ ( K 1 , n , K 1 , m + e ) = n + m − 1 r_{*}(K_{1,n},K_{1,m}+e)=n+m-1 is defined by a piecewise function related to the three disjoint cases of n , m n,m both even and n ≤ m − 2 n\leq m-2 , n n or m m is odd and n ≤ m − 2 n\leq m-2 and n > m − 2 n>m-2 .
