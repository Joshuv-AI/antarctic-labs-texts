In summary, we synthesized nanoparticles of magnetite with sizes along three orders of magnitude, solely modifying the amount of a unselective binding surfactant which also leads to a shape control. We obtained a relationship between the morphology of the nanoparticles and the ratio surfactant/precursor and we have found an equation which treat this dependence including a shape parameter (
\upsilon
