Remarks on J J -hyperideals and their expansion
M. Anbarloei

Abstract
. The aim of this research work is to define and characterize a new class of hyperideals in a Krasner ( m , n ) (m,n) -hyperring that we call n-ary J J -hyperideals. Also, we study the concept of n-ary δ \delta - J J -hyperideals as an expansion of n-ary J J -hyperideals. Finally, we extend the notion of n-ary δ \delta - J J -hyperideals to ( k , n ) (k,n) -absorbing δ \delta - J J -hyperideals.
