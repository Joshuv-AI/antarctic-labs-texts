Constructing abelian varieties from rank 2 Galois representations
Raju Krishnamoorthy, Jinbang Yang, Kang Zuo

Abstract
. Let U U be a smooth affine curve over a number field K K with a compactification X X and let 𝕃 {\mathbb{L}} be a rank 2 2 , geometrically irreducible lisse ℚ ¯ ℓ \overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}_{\ell} -sheaf on U U with cyclotomic determinant that extends to an integral model, has Frobenius traces all in some fixed number field E ⊂ ℚ ¯ ℓ E\subset\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell} , and has bad, infinite reduction at some closed point x x of X ∖ U X\setminus U . We show that 𝕃 {\mathbb{L}} occurs as a summand of the cohomology of a family of abelian varieties over U U . The argument follows the structure of the proof of a recent theorem of Snowden-Tsimerman, who show that when E = ℚ E=\mathbb{Q} , then 𝕃 {\mathbb{L}} is isomorphic to the cohomology of an elliptic curve E U → U E_{U}\rightarrow U .

is supersingular, we deduce that

is also positive. Therefore,

]
).

For the next step: Faltings-Chai have constructed the following Kodaira-Spencer map

(2.2)

\Omega_{\bar{B}/\bar{C}}\otimes\Omega_{\bar{B}^{t}/\bar{C}}\rightarrow\omega_{\bar{C}}(Z),

extending the usual Kodaira-Spencer map over

and after restricting to

is just the sum of the Kodaira-Spencer maps for each

in
\mathcal{H}_{\infty}(\kappa({\mathfrak{p}}))\subset\mathcal{H}(\kappa({\mathfrak{p}}))
. Since
\mathcal{T}_{2}
is infinite and
\mathcal{H}_{\infty}/S
is of finite type, it follows that there exists a finite field extension

and a point
\beta\in\mathcal{H}(K^{\prime})
. In fact, as
\mathcal{H}_{\infty}
has relative dimension
0
, our point
\beta
may be chosen to be compatible with infinitely many of the
\beta_{{\mathfrak{p}}}
, where compatibility is defined in the obvious sense. By definition of
\mathcal{H}_{\infty}
, the point
\beta\in\mathcal{H}_{\infty}(K^{\prime})
corresponds to an abelian scheme
B_{U^{\prime}_{K^{\prime}}}\rightarrow U^{\prime}_{K^{\prime}}
such that
\mathcal{L}|_{U^{\prime}_{K^{\prime}}}
injects into the integral
\mathcal{O}_{M}
cohomology. By taking a Weil restriction, we obtain an abelian scheme
A_{U}\rightarrow U
(of dimension
g[K^{\prime}:K]
) such that
{\mathbb{L}}
injects into the the cohomology of
A_{U}\rightarrow U
. Using Faltings’ semi-simplicity theorem, we conclude that
{\mathbb{L}}
is in fact a summand of the cohomology, as desired.

In general, there exists a finite étale cover
f\colon U^{\prime}\rightarrow U
such that
f^{*}\mathbb{L}
has the following property. Let
C^{\prime}
be the compactification of
U^{\prime}
, and set
D^{\prime}
to be the divisor at infinity. Then for each
\infty\in D^{\prime}
, the lisse
\ell
-adic sheaf
f^{*}\mathbb{L}
has either good reduction at
\infty
or bad, unipotent reduction at
\infty
. There then exists a curve
U^{\prime}\subset V^{\prime}\subset C^{\prime}
, where
f^{*}\mathbb{L}
extends to a lisse
\ell
-adic sheaf
\mathbb{M}^{\prime}
on all of
V^{\prime}

C^{\prime}\setminus V^{\prime}
. Then the above argument applies, producing an abelian scheme

whose cohomology has
\mathbb{M}^{\prime}
as a summand. Restricting to
U^{\prime}
and then applying a Weil restriction of scalars along the finite étale map

, we obtain the desired result.
∎
