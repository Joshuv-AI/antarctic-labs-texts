Sheaves over complexes of groups and developability
Joshua L. Faber

Abstract
We define the notion of a sheaf over a complex of groups. As an application, we give a criterion for the developability of a complex of groups. When the developability is witnessed by a morphism to GL ⁡ ( V ) \mathrm{GL}(V) for some V V , our criterion is a characterization of developability.
