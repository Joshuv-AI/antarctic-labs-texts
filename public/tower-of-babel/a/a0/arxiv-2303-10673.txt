Temperature-dependent Eu spin reorientations in the tetragonal A-type antiferromagnet EuGa 4 induced by small ab -plane magnetic fields
Santanu Pakhira, David C. Johnston

Abstract
The body-centered-tetragonal antiferromagnet EuGa 4 {\rm EuGa_{4}} exhibits A-type antiferromagnetic order below its Néel temperature T N = 16.4 T_{\rm N}=16.4 K in magnetic field H = 0 H=0 where the moments are ferromagnetically aligned in the a ​ b ab -plane with the Eu moments in adjacent Eu planes aligned antiferromagnetically. Previous magnetization versus field M a ​ b ​ ( H ) M_{ab}(H) measurements revealed that the moments exhibit a spin-reorientation transition at a critical field H c ​ 1 H_{c1} where the Eu moments become perpendicular to an in-plane magnetic field while still remaining in the a ​ b ab plane. A theory for T = 0 T=0 K was presented that successfully explained the observed low-field moment-reorientation behavior at T = 2 T=2 K. Here we present a theory explaining the observed T T dependence of M a ​ b ​ ( H , T < T N ) M_{ab}(H,T<T_{\rm N}) in the [1,0,0] direction for H ≤ H c1 ​ ( T ) H\leq H_{\rm c1}(T) from 2 to 14 K arising from a T T -dependent anisotropy energy.
