The heights of symmetric peaks and the depth of symmetric valleys over compositions of an integer
Walaa Asakly, Noor Kezil

Abstract
A composition π = π 1 π 2 ⋯ π k \pi=\pi_{1}\pi_{2}\cdots\pi_{k} of a positive integer n n is an ordered collection of one or more positive integers whose sum is n n . The number of summands, namely k k , is called the number of parts of π \pi . In this paper, we introduce two statistics over compositions of an integer n n with exactly k k parts: heights of symmetric peaks and depths of symmetric valleys over all compositions of n n . We derive an explicit formula for the generating functions of compositions of n n with exactly k k parts according to the number of symmetric peaks (valleys) and the total heights (depths) of peaks (valleys). Additionally, we present a combinatorial proof for the total heights (depths) of peaks (valleys) over all compositions of n n with exactly k k parts. Furthermore, we investigate these statistics in the context of random words, where the letters are generated by geometric probabilities. Keywords : Symmetric peaks, Symmetric valleys, Height of peaks, Depth of valleys, Compositions and Generating functions.

\ell_{1}+\ell_{2}\dots+\ell_{k-3}=n-2b-m-(k-3)
, where
\ell_{1},\ell_{2},\ldots\ell_{k-3}
are nonnegative integers. The depth of the symmetric valley
bmb
is
b-m
. Therefore, the total sum of depths of compositions of
n
with a symmetric valley in the
i

\sum_{m=1}^{\lfloor\frac{n-k+1}{3}\rfloor}\sum_{b=m+1}^{\lfloor\frac{n-m-(k-3)}{2}\rfloor}\binom{n-2b-m-1}{k-4}(b-m).

There are
k-2
options for choosing
i
. Multiplying the above expression by
k-2
yields the desired result.

2.

Let
d_{m}=b-m
. If
\frac{n-m}{2}
is an integer then

.

∎
