On a conjecture on a series of convergence rate 1 2 \frac{1}{2}


Abstract
Sun, in 2022, introduced a conjectured evaluation for a series of convergence rate 1 2 \frac{1}{2} involving harmonic numbers. We prove both this conjecture and a stronger version of this conjecture, using a summation technique based on a beta-type integral we had previously introduced. Our full proof also requires applications of Bailey’s F 1 2 ​ ( 1 2 ) {}_{2}F_{1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) -formula, Dixon’s F 2 3 ​ ( 1 ) {}_{3}F_{2}(1) -formula, an almost-poised version of Dixon’s formula due to Chu, Watson’s formula for F 2 3 ​ ( 1 ) {}_{3}F_{2}(1) -series, the Gauss summation theorem, Euler’s formula for F 1 2 {}_{2}F_{1} -series, elliptic integral singular values, and lemniscate-like constants recently introduced by Campbell and Chu. The techniques involved in our proof are useful, more broadly, in the reduction of difficult sums of convergence rate 1 2 \frac{1}{2} to previously evaluable expressions.
