Electroweak monopoles and magnetic dumbbells in grand unified theories
G. Lazarides, Q. Shafi

Abstract
We use the S ​ U ​ ( 5 ) SU(5) model to show the presence in grand unified theories of an electroweak monopole and a magnetic dumbbell (“meson”) made up of a monopole-antimonopole pair connected by a Z Z -magnetic flux tube. The monopole is associated with the spontaneous breaking of the weak S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L SU(2)_{L} gauge symmetry by the induced vacuum expectation value of a heavy scalar S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L SU(2)_{L} triplet with zero weak hypercharge contained in the adjoint Higgs 24-plet. This monopole carries a Coulomb magnetic charge of ( 3 / 4 ) ​ ( 2 ​ π / e ) (3/4)(2\pi/e) as well as Z Z -magnetic charge, where 2 ​ π / e 2\pi/e denotes the unit Dirac magnetic charge. Its total magnetic charge is 3 / 8 ​ ( 4 ​ π / e ) \sqrt{3/8}(4\pi/e) , which is in agreement with the Dirac quantization condition. The monopole weighs about 700 GeV, but because of the attached Z Z -magnetic tube it exists, together with the antimonopole, in a magnetic dumbbell configuration whose mass is expected to lie in the TeV range. The presence of these topological structures in S ​ U ​ ( 5 ) SU(5) and S ​ O ​ ( 10 ) SO(10) and in their supersymmetric extensions provides an exciting new avenue for testing these theories in high-energy colliders.
