Abstract
. On any finite algebraic extension K K of the field ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} of p p -adic numbers, there exist rational maps ϕ ∈ K ⁡ ( z ) \phi\in K(z) such that dynamical system ( ℙ 1 ​ ( K ) , ϕ ) (\mathbb{P}^{1}(K),\phi) has empty Fatou set, i.e. the iteration family { ϕ n : n ≥ 0 } \{\phi^{n}:n\geq 0\} is nowhere equicontinuous.

1.
Introduction

Let
p
be a prime number and
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
be the field of
p
-adic numbers. Let
\phi\in\mathbb{Q}_{p}(z)
be a rational map of degree at least two, which induces a continuous map on the projective line
\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})
over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
. Recall that the space
\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})
is equipped with the spherical metric.

Let
\mathbb{C}_{p}
be the completion of an algebraic closure of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
. Naturally,
\phi
induces a continuous map from
\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{C}_{p})
to itself, where

\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{C}_{p})
is the projective line over
\mathbb{C}_{p}
with the spherical metic. The Fatou-Julia theory of rational maps
\phi\in\mathbb{C}_{p}
on
\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{C}_{p})
have been well developed by many authors

[
2
,
4
,
8
,
9
,
10
]
. Benedetto
[
3
]
showed that
\phi
has at least one non-repelling fixed point in
\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{C}_{p})
, which is a dynamical stable point. Hence, the Fatou set of the system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{C}_{p}),\phi)
is not empty.

On the other hand, the minimality and minimal decomposition dynamical system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}),\phi)
when
\phi
have good reduction or potential good reduction were investigate in
[
5
,
6
]
. Remark that these dynamical systems have empty Julia set which means that the whole space
\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})
is the Fatou set.

In this note, we construct rational maps
\phi
such that the Fatou set of the system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}),\phi)
is empty.

For the polynomial
f(z)=(z^{p}-z)/p
,
it is known in
[
11
]
that the system
(\mathbb{Z}_{p},f)
is topologically conjugated the one-side full shift system
(\Sigma_{p},\sigma)
. See
[
7
]
for a general study of the such systems. Our idea is to construct a rational map
\phi
satisfying the following two properties:

(1)
\phi
maps the points outside
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
into
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
;

(2)
\phi
is a small perturbation of
f
on
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
such that
(\mathbb{Z}_{p},\phi)
is topologically conjugated to
(\mathbb{Z}_{p},f).

Actually, on each finite extension
K
of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
, we can construct rational maps
\phi
such that the Fatou set of the system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K),\phi)
is empty. Let
K
be a finite extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
and let
d=[K:\mathbb{Q}_{p}]
. Let
e
be the ramification index of
K
over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
(see Section
2
). It is known that
e\mid d
and let
f=d/e
.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
K
be a finite algebraic extension of the field
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
of
p
-adic numbers. For positive integers
m>n
, let

\phi(z)=\frac{z^{p^{f}}-z}{\pi+z^{p^{mf}}-z^{p^{nf}}}.

Then the dynamical system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K),\phi)
has empty Fatou set.

Hence, it follows immediately that there exist rational maps
\phi\in\mathbb{Q}_{p}(Z)
such that the dynamics
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}),\phi)
have empty Fatou set.

Corollary 1.2
.

For positive integer
m>n\geq 1
, let

\phi(z)=\frac{z^{p}-z}{p+z^{p^{m}}-z^{p^{n}}}.

Then the dynamical system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}),\phi)
has empty Fatou set.

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, we recall some notations and some useful lemmas concerning the roots of unity and monomials.
Let
K
be a finite
extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
and let
d=[K:\mathbb{Q}_{p}]

denote the dimension of
K
as a vector space over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
. The
extended absolute value on
K
is still denoted by
|\cdot|_{p}
.
For
x\in K^{*}:=K\setminus\{0\}
,
v_{p}(x):=-\log_{p}(|x|_{p})

defines the valuation of
x
, with convention
v_{p}(0):=\infty
.
One can show that there exists a unique positive integer
e
which
is called
ramification index
of
K
over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
, such that

v_{p}(K^{*})=\frac{1}{e}\mathbb{Z}.

(Sometimes, we write the image of
K^{*}
under
|\cdot|_{p}
as
|K^{*}|_{p}=p^{{\mathbb{Z}}/{e}}
. )

The extension
K
over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
is said to
be
unramified
if
e=1
,
ramified
if
e>1
and

totally ramified
if
e=d
. An element
\pi\in K
is called a

uniformizer
if
v_{p}(\pi)=1/e
. For convenience of notation, we
write

v_{\pi}(x):=e\cdot v_{p}(x)

for
x\in K
so that

v_{\pi}(x)\in\mathbb{Z}
.
Let
\mathcal{O}_{K}:=\{x\in K:|x|_{p}\leq 1\}
, whose elements are called
integers
of
K
. Let

\mathcal{P}_{K}:=\{x\in K:|x|_{p}<1\}
, which is the maximal ideal
of
\mathcal{O}_{K}
. The
residual class field
of
K
is

\mathbb{K}:=\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathcal{P}_{K}
. Then

\mathbb{K}=\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}
, the finite field of
p^{f}
elements
where
f=d/e
. Let
C=\{c_{0},c_{1},\cdots,c_{p^{f}-1}\}
be a fixed
complete set of representatives of the cosets of
\mathcal{P}_{K}

in
\mathcal{O}_{K}
. Then every
x\in K
has a unique
\pi
-adic
expansion of the form

(2
\cdot
1)

x=\sum_{i=i_{0}}^{\infty}a_{i}\pi^{i}

where
i_{0}\in\mathbb{Z}
and
a_{i}\in C
for all
i\geq i_{0}
.

Lemma 2.1

(
[
1
]
, p.56)
.

The integral ring
\mathcal{O}_{K}
contains the
(p^{f}-1)
-th roots of unity. Moreover, for any
x\in\mathcal{O}_{K}\setminus\mathcal{P}_{K}
, the open ball
D(x,1)
contains exact one
(p^{f}-1)
-th root of unity.

For any integer
k\geq 0
, denote by
M_{k}(z)=z^{p^{kf}}
.

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
m>n\geq 1
be two positive integers. Then for any
x\in\mathcal{O}_{K}
,

|M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)|_{p}<|\pi|_{p}.

Proof.

First assume that
x\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{K}
. Then
|M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)|_{p}=|x|_{p}^{p^{nf}}<|\pi|_{p}
.

Secondly, assume
x\in\mathcal{O}_{K}\setminus\pi\mathcal{O}_{K}.
There exists a
p^{f}-1
-th root
\xi
of unity
such that
x=\xi+y
for some
y\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{K}
. Observing that
\xi^{p^{mf}}=\xi^{p^{nf}}=\xi
, we have

\displaystyle M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{p^{mf}}C_{p^{mf}}^{i}y^{i}\xi^{p^{mf}-i}+\sum_{i=1}^{p^{nf}}C_{p^{nf}}^{i}y^{i}\xi^{p^{nf}-i}.

Note that
p\mid C_{p^{kf}}^{i}
for each positive integer
k\geq 1
. Since
y\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{K},
we have

|M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)|_{p}<|y|_{p}\leq|\pi|_{p}.

∎

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
m>n\geq 1
be two positive integers. Then for
x,y\in\mathcal{O}_{K}
with
|x-y|_{p}<1
, we have

|M_{m}(x)-M_{m}(y)|_{p}<|M_{n}(x)-M_{n}(y)|_{p}<|x-y|_{p}.

Proof.

Since
M_{k+1}(z)=M_{k}(z)^{p^{f}}
, we can write

(2
\cdot
2)

\displaystyle M_{k+1}(x)-M_{k+1}(y)=\Big(M_{k}(x)-M_{k}(y)\Big)\sum_{i=0}^{p^{f}-1}M_{k}(x)^{i}M_{k}(y)^{p^{f}-i}.

Since
|x-y|_{p}<1
, it is easy to see
|M_{k}(x)-M_{k}(y)|_{p}<1,

which implies that

(2
\cdot
3)

\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{p^{f}-1}M_{k}(x)^{i}M_{k}(y)^{p^{f}-i}\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{K}.

Hence, (
2

⋅

2
) and (
2

⋅

3
) implies

|M_{k+1}(x)-M_{k+1}(y)|_{p}<|M_{k}(x)-M_{k}(y)|_{p}.

By induction on
m
, for
m>n
we have

|M_{m}(x)-M_{m}(y)|_{p}<|M_{n}(x)-M_{n}(y)|_{p}.

∎

Corollary 2.4
.

Let
x,y\in\mathcal{O}_{K}
with
|x-y|_{p}<1
.
For
k\geq 1
,

|M_{k}(x)-M_{k}(y)|_{p}\leq|\pi|_{p}^{k}|x-y|_{p}.

Lemma 2.5
.

Let
x,y\in\mathcal{O}_{K}
and let
u,v\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{k}
.
If
|u-v|_{p}\leq|x-y|_{p}
, then we have

\Big|\frac{x}{1+u}-\frac{y}{1+v}\Big|_{p}\leq|x-y|_{p}.

Moreover, if
|u-v|_{p}<|x-y|_{p}
, the equality holds.

Proof.

Since
u,v\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{k}
, we have
|1+u|_{p}=|1+v|_{p}=1
. Hence,

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{x}{1+u}-\frac{y}{1+v}\Big|_{p}=|x(1+v)-y(1+u)|_{p}=|x(1+u)-y(1+u)+x(v-u)|_{p}.

The inequality follows, because
|x(v-u)|_{p}\leq|x-y|_{p}
.

If
|u-v|_{p}<|x-y|_{p}
, then
|x(v-u)|_{p}<|x-y|_{p}
. Hence, we get the equality.
∎

3.
Rational maps with empty Fatou set

Let
K
be a finite extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
and let
\phi\in K(z)
be a rational map of degree at least
2
.
Then
\phi
induce a continuous map from the projective line
\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)
over
K
to itself.
Here, we equip the projective line
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K))
with the spherical metric
\rho(\cdot,\cdot)
, i.e.
for
P=[x_{1},y_{1}],Q=[x_{2},y_{2}]\in\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)
,

\rho(P,Q)=\frac{|x_{1}y_{2}-x_{2}y_{1}|_{p}}{\max\{|x_{1}|_{p},|y_{1}|_{p}\}\max\{|x_{2}|_{p},|y_{2}|_{p}\}}.

Consider the dynamical system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K),\phi)
.
The
Fatou set

\mathcal{F}(\phi)
of the system is defined to be the subset of
\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)
consisting of the points having a neighborhood on which the family of iterates
\{\phi^{n}\}_{n\geq 0}
is equicontinuous with respect to the spherical metric. The
Julia set
of the system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K),\phi
is the complement of
\mathcal{F}(\phi)
in
\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)
, denoted by
\mathcal{F}(\phi)
.

In this section, we show that there exist rational maps in
K(z)
with empty Fatou set for their induced dynamical systems on
\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)
.

The following lemma shows that
\phi
is locally scaling on
\mathcal{O}_{K}
.

Lemma 3.1
.

For
x,y\in\mathcal{O}_{K}
with
|x-y|_{p}<1
, we have

|\phi(x)-\phi(y)|_{p}=p^{1/e}|x-y|_{p}.

Proof.

Recalling that
M_{k}(z)=z^{p^{kf}}
, we have

\displaystyle\phi(x)-\phi(y)=\frac{1}{\pi}\cdot\left(\frac{M_{1}(x)-x}{1+\frac{M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)}{\pi}}-\frac{M_{1}(y)-y}{1+\frac{M_{m}(y)-M_{n}(y)}{\pi}}\right).

By Lemma
2.3
, under the assumption
|x-y|_{p}<1
, we have

(3
\cdot
4)

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)}{\pi}-\frac{M_{m}(y)-M_{n}(y)}{\pi}\Big|_{p}=\Big|\frac{M_{n}(x)-M_{n}(y)}{\pi}\Big|_{p}

and

(3
\cdot
5)

\displaystyle|(M_{1}(x)-x)-(M_{1}(y)-y)|_{p}=|x-y|_{p}.

Since
n\geq 2
, by Corollary
2.4
,

\Big|\frac{M_{n}(x)-M_{n}(y)}{\pi}\Big|_{p}<|x-y|_{p},

which together with (
3

⋅

4
) and (
3

⋅

5
) implies that

\Big|\frac{M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)}{\pi}-\frac{M_{m}(y)-M_{n}(y)}{\pi}\Big|_{p}<|(M_{1}(x)-x)-(M_{1}(y)-y)|_{p}.

By Lemma
2.2
, we have

M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x),M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)\in\pi^{2}\mathcal{O}_{K},

which is equivalent to

\frac{M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)}{\pi},\frac{M_{m}(x)-M_{n}(x)}{\pi}\in\pi\mathcal{O}_{K}.

Hence, by Lemma
2.5
, we have

|\phi(x)-\phi(y)|_{p}=|1/\pi|_{p}\cdot|x-y|_{p}=p^{1/e}|x-y|_{p}.

∎

The proof of Theorem
1.1
.

Observe that
m\geq 2
. Then we have

\phi(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)\setminus\mathcal{O}_{K})\subset\pi\mathcal{O}_{K}.

The same argument in Lemma
2.2
implies that

\forall x\in\mathcal{O}_{K},\quad|M_{1}(x)-x|_{p}\leq|\pi|_{p}.

Also, by Lemma
2.2
, we have

\phi(\mathcal{O}_{K})\subset\mathcal{O}_{K}.

Then we shall determine the symbolic dynamics of the dynamical system
(\mathcal{O}_{K},\phi)
.
Denote the reduction modulo
\pi
of
x\in\mathcal{O}_{K}
by
\overline{x}\in\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}
and set

\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}}=(\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}})^{\mathbb{N}}

by which we mean the direct product of a countable copies of
\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}
, each with the discrete topology.
Now we shall show that
(\mathcal{O}_{K},\phi)
is topologically conjugated to
(\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}},\sigma)
, where
\sigma
is the shift
map on
\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}}
, i.e.
\sigma:\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}}\to\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}}
by
(\sigma(x))_{i}=x_{i+1}
for all
x=(x_{i})\in\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}}
.
Define a map
H:\mathcal{O}_{K}\to\Sigma_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f}}}
by

(H(z))_{i}=\overline{\phi^{i}(z)},\quad\forall z\in\mathcal{O}_{K}.

By the same argument in
[
11
, Theorem 1]
, one can see that
H
is a bijective.
It is clear that
H
is continuous and
\sigma\circ H=\phi\circ H
.

Hence, we obtain that the Julia set of dynamical system
(\mathbb{P}^{1}(K),\phi)
is the whole space
\mathbb{P}^{1}(K)
.
∎
