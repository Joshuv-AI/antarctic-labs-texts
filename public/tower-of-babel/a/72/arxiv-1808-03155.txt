Conclusions

Couple of methods of evaluating the expression to count the third
category of generalized necklaces are studied; while doing so, we
formalized the ideas into algorithms. Comparative analysis of
their computational complexity is carried out and found that the
best alternative method suggested in the paper cuts down on the
amount of computation by more than
50\%

arrive at still better efficient methods of evaluating the
expression.
