Observation of T c ​ c T_{cc} and a quark model
Sungsik Noh, Woosung Park

Abstract
The recent discovery of the doubly charmed tetraquark T c ​ c T_{cc} ( u ¯ ​ d ¯ ​ c ​ c \bar{u}\bar{d}cc ) provides a stringent constraint on its binding energy relative to its lowest decay threshold. We use a fully convergent spatial wave function and perform a simultaneous global fit to both the meson and baryon spectra. Our analysis shows that a Yukawa type hyperfine potential leads to a slight bound state for T c ​ c T_{cc} with ( I , S ) = ( 0 , 1 ) (I,S)=(0,1) below its lowest threshold, in agreement with recent experimental findings. We also find that T c ​ c T_{cc} is highly likely to be in a compact configuration.
