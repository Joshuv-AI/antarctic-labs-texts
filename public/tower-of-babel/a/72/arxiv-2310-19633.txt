The Hilb-vs-Quot Conjecture
Oscar Kivinen, Minh-Tâm Quang Trinh

Abstract
. Let R R be the complete local ring of a complex plane curve germ and S S its normalization. We propose a “Hilb-vs-Quot” conjecture relating the virtual weight polynomials of the Hilbert schemes of R R to those of the Quot schemes that parametrize R R -submodules of S S . By relating the Quot side to a type of compactified Picard scheme, we show that our conjecture generalizes a conjecture of Cherednik’s, and that it would relate the perverse filtration on the cohomology of the Picard side to a more elementary filtration. Next, we propose a Quot version of the Oblomkov–Rasmussen–Shende conjecture, relating parabolic refinements of our Quot schemes to Khovanov–Rozansky link homology. It becomes equivalent to the original version under (refined) Hilb-vs-Quot, but can also be strengthened to incorporate polynomial actions and y y -ification. For germs y n = x d y^{n}=x^{d} , where n n is either coprime to or divides d d , we prove the Quot version of ORS through combinatorics. When n = 3 n=3 and 3 ∤ d 3\nmid d , we deduce Hilb-vs-Quot by an asymptotic argument, and hence, establish the original ORS conjecture for these germs.

A.1.

In this appendix, we specify our grading conventions for Khovanov–Rozansky homology; compare them to those of other published works; and illustrate on the smallest examples (unknot, Hopf link, trefoil,
(3,4)
torus knot) to aid the reader’s sanity.
Our exposition closely follows
[
GH
, §1.6]
, but we correct some mistakes:
See
A.1
–
A.2
.

A.2.
Soergel Bimodules

Let
T=\mathbf{G}_{m}^{n}
, and let

(A.1)

\displaystyle\mathbf{S}\vcentcolon=\mathrm{H}_{T}^{\ast}(\mathit{pt})=\mathbf{C}[t_{1},\ldots,t_{n}].

We regard
\mathbf{S}
as a graded ring, with
\deg(t_{i})=2
for all
i
.
Thus the
S_{n}
-action on
T
that permutes coordinates also preserves the grading on
\mathbf{S}
.
Let
s_{i}\in S_{n}
be the transposition that swaps
t_{i}
and
t_{i+1}
.

In the category of graded
\mathbf{S}
-bimodules, we write
(m)
for the grading shift
\mathbf{B}(m)^{i}=\mathbf{B}^{i+m}
.
Let
{\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim}
be the full subcategory generated by the identity bimodule
\mathbf{S}
and the bimodules
\mathbf{S}\otimes_{\mathbf{S}^{s_{i}}}\mathbf{S}(1)
for all
i
under isomorphisms, direct sums, tensor products
\otimes=\otimes_{\mathbf{S}}
, direct summands, and grading shifts.
Objects of
{\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim}
are called
Soergel bimodules
.
We write
\mathsf{K}^{b}({\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim})
for the bounded homotopy category of
{\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim}
.
It is a monoidal additive category whose unit is the complex consisting of the identity bimodule in degree zero.

Let
\mathit{Br}_{n}
be the group of braids on
n
strands up to isotopy.
Any braid
\beta\in\mathit{Br}_{n}
defines an object
\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta}\in\mathsf{K}^{b}({\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim})
called the Rouquier complex of
\beta
.
See,
e.g.
,
[
GH
, §2.1]
for the precise definition.
If
\beta=\beta^{\prime}\beta^{\prime\prime}
in
\mathit{Br}_{n}
, then any sequence of braid moves that transforms
\beta^{\prime}\beta^{\prime\prime}
into
\beta
defines an isomorphism from
\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta^{\prime}}\otimes\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta^{\prime\prime}}
onto
\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta}
.
Thus, the braid group is categorified by the objects
\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta}
under
\otimes
.

Let
\mathsf{Vect}_{2}
be the category of
\mathbf{Z}^{2}
-graded vector spaces that are finite-dimensional in each bidegree, such that the first grading is bounded below and the second is bounded.
Let
\overline{\mathsf{HH}}=\overline{\mathsf{HH}}^{\ast,\ast}:{\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim}\to\mathsf{Vect}_{2}
be the
Hochschild cohomology
functor:

(A.2)

\displaystyle\overline{\mathsf{HH}}^{i,j}(\mathbf{B})=\Ext_{\mathbf{S}\otimes_{\mathbf{C}}\mathbf{S}^{\mathrm{op}}}^{i}(\mathbf{S},\mathbf{B}(j)).

These Ext’s can be computed using a Koszul resolution of
\mathbf{S}
over
\mathbf{S}\otimes_{\mathbf{C}}\mathbf{S}^{\mathrm{op}}
, which shows that the Ext grading sits in degrees
0
through (at most)
n
.

Let
\mathsf{Vect}_{3}
be the category of
\mathbf{Z}^{3}
-graded vector spaces that are finite-dimensional in each tridegree, such that the first grading is bounded below and the other two gradings are bounded.
Let
\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}=\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}^{\ast,\ast,\ast}
be the composition of functors

(A.3)

\displaystyle\mathsf{K}^{b}({\mathbf{S}}\mathsf{Bim})\xrightarrow{\overline{\mathsf{HH}}}\mathsf{K}^{b}(\mathsf{Vect}_{2})\xrightarrow{\mathrm{H}^{\ast}}\mathsf{Vect}_{3}.

Explicitly, the gradings are ordered so that
\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}^{I,J,K}=\mathrm{H}^{k}(\overline{\mathsf{HH}}_{n}^{I,J})
.

The story above can be redone with the quotient torus
T_{0}\vcentcolon=T/T^{S_{n}}
in place of
T
.
Note that
T_{0}
is just the image of
T
along the quotient map
\GL_{n}\to\PGL_{n}
.
Replacing
T
with
T_{0}
entails replacing
\mathbf{S}
with its subring
\mathbf{S}_{0}\vcentcolon=\mathrm{H}_{T_{0}}^{\ast}(\mathit{pt})
.
We write
\cal{T}_{\beta}
,
\mathsf{HH}
,
\mathsf{HHH}
for the objects that respectively replace
\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta}
,
\overline{\mathsf{HH}}
,
\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}
.

Let
L
be the link closure of
\beta
.
In
[
Kh
]
, Khovanov proved that
\mathsf{HHH}(\cal{T}_{\beta})
matches the
reduced
version of the triply-graded homology of
L
proposed in
[
DGR
]
and constructed in
[
KhR
]
, up to an affine transformation of the trigrading.
One can show that

(A.4)

\displaystyle\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}(\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta})\simeq\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}(\bar{\cal{T}}_{\id})\otimes\mathsf{HHH}(\cal{T}_{\beta}),

and that in consequence,
\overline{\mathsf{HHH}}(\bar{\cal{T}}_{\beta})
matches the
unreduced
version of the homology constructed in
[
KhR
]
, up to similar regradings.
