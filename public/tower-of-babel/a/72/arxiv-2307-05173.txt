Diagnostics of Anomalous Conductance Plateaus in Abelian Quantum Hall Regime
Sourav Manna, Ankur Das, Yuval Gefen, Moshe Goldstein

Abstract
Two-terminal conductance quantization in the context of quantum Hall (QH) physics is intimately related to current carried by a discrete number of edge modes. Upon pinching off of a QH bar, one may reach regimes in which some modes which are fully transmitted (while the others are fully reflected), giving rise to quantized conductance plateaus. Here we study an alternative protocol for quantized values of the conductance, which relies on inter-mode equilibration. Concretely this is the result of considering different inequalities between charge equilibration and thermal equilibration lengths on one hand and geometrical lengths on the other hand, and accounting for the possibility of edge reconstruction. Taking the 2/3 state as a prototypical example, we obtain a host of scenarios leading to conductance plateaus at e 2 / 3 ​ h e^{2}/3h (observed previously), e 2 / 2 ​ h e^{2}/2h (recently observed), and 5 ​ e 2 / 9 ​ h 5e^{2}/9h (our prediction). Our approach facilitates distinguishing among various scenarios based on dc current auto- and cross-correlation (shot noise).

;
Kumar et al. 2022
;

Summary and outlook.—

We have proposed a new paradigm for the emergence of unorthodox quantized conductance plateaus in the context of bulk fractional
quantum Hall phases. Our picture relies on the interplay among different equilibration channels. We have shown that it gives rise to the unusual behavior of the current noise, namely
unequal
auto- and cross-correlations
Glattli
.
Furthermore, we have used this feature to distinguish among different edge models of the
2/3
state exhibiting the same
plateau.
Those models originate from
either no equilibration or different degree of charge and
thermal equilibration, while the reconstruction
is present or absent.
Our results include several possible scenarios for the
recently observed
e^{2}/2h

Nakamura et al. 2023
;
Fauzi et al. 2023
, and previously observed
e^{2}/3h

Bid et al. 2009
plateaus and the means to distinguish between them via shot noise. In addition, we predict a possible
5e^{2}/9h
(only in the unequilibrated regime) plateau. Our scheme is realizable with the present day experimental abilities.
The analysis can be extended to other quantum Hall states
Manna and Das 2023
, graphene quantum Hall, and edge reconstructed
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
topological insulators
Khanna et al. 2022b
;
Wang et al. 2017
;
John et al. 2022
.
