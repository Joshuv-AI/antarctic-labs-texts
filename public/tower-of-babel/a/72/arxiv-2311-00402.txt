Introduction

Marston Morse, a mathematician, created Morse’s hypothesis in the 1920s. He was a member of the university at the Institute for Advanced Study, and Princeton University released Topological Methods in the Theory of Functions of a Complex Variable in 1947 as part of the Annals of Mathematics Studies series. A well-known publication by theoretical physicist Edward Witten that connects Morse’s theory to quantum field theory has garnered a lot of attention for this idea during the past two decades.
Morse’s theory and computation of critical groups are useful tools for studying the multiplicity and existence of solutions to nonlinear problems. As far as we know, this method is rarely used in the study of problems of differential equations. For this, we’ll provide a brief overview of the technique.

\displaystyle\displaystyle H:[0,1]\times Y_{1}\backslash 0\to Y_{1}\backslash 0
as

H(t,\mathrm{w}_{n})=(1-t)\mathrm{w}_{n}+t\tau_{1}(\mathrm{w}_{n})\mathrm{w}_{n}.

We have
\displaystyle\displaystyle H(0,\mathrm{w}_{n})=\mathrm{w}_{n},

\displaystyle\displaystyle H(1,\mathrm{w}_{n})=\tau_{1}(\mathrm{w}_{n})\mathrm{w}_{n}\in\psi^{A},
and
\displaystyle\displaystyle H(t,.)_{|\psi^{A}}=id_{|\psi^{A}}
for all
\displaystyle\displaystyle t\in[0,1].
It follows that

\psi^{A}\text{ is a strong deformation retract of }Y_{1}\backslash 0.

(3.34)

We consider the radial retraction
\displaystyle\displaystyle r:Y_{1}\to\mathbb{R}
defined by

r(\mathrm{w}_{n})=\frac{\mathrm{w}_{n}}{\|\mathrm{w}_{n}\|}\text{ for all }\mathrm{w}_{n}\in Y_{1}.

This map is continuous and
\displaystyle\displaystyle r_{|S^{1}}=id_{|S^{1}}.
Therefore,
\displaystyle\displaystyle S^{1}
is a retract of
\displaystyle\displaystyle Y_{1}\backslash 0.

Considering the map defined by

h(t,\mathrm{w}_{n})=(1-t)\mathrm{w}_{n}+tr(\mathrm{w}_{n})\text{ for all }(t,\mathrm{w}_{n})\in[0,1]\times Y_{1}\backslash 0.

Then,
\displaystyle\displaystyle h(0,\mathrm{w}_{n})=\mathrm{w}_{n},

\displaystyle h(1,\mathrm{w}_{n})=r(\mathrm{w}_{n})\in S^{1},
and
\displaystyle\displaystyle h(1,.)_{|S^{1}}=id_{|S^{1}}.
Hence, we refer that

S^{1}\text{ is a deformation retract of }Y_{1}\backslash 0.

(3.35)

Finally, by
3.34
and
3.35
it follow that
\displaystyle\displaystyle\psi^{a}
and
\displaystyle S^{1}
are homotopie equivalent. We already know that the space
\displaystyle Y_{1}
is an infinite dimensional Banach space. From Remark 6.1.13 in
[
31
]
, it follows that the sphere unit
\displaystyle S^{1}
is contractible. So, we have that

\displaystyle H_{k}(Y_{1},\psi^{a})=H_{k}(Y_{1},S^{1})=0\text{ for all },\displaystyle k\in\mathbb{N}.

Finally, we obtain that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\displaystyle C_{k}(\psi,\infty)=\displaystyle H_{k}(Y_{1},\psi^{a})=\displaystyle H_{k}(Y_{1},S^{1})=\displaystyle 0,\text{ for all }k\in\mathbb{N}.

(3.36)

∎

Existence of infinitely non-trivial solutions

Theorem 9
.

Suppose that conditions
\displaystyle(\mathrm{V}),
and
\displaystyle(\mathcal{H}_{1})-(\mathcal{H}_{4})
are satisfied. Then, the problem (
3
) has infinitely non-trivial weak solutions in

Proof.

We suppose that our problem admits three non-trivial solutions

\displaystyle\displaystyle Y_{1}.
That is
\displaystyle\displaystyle K_{\psi}=\{0,\mathrm{w}_{n},\mathrm{v}_{n}\}.
From Morse’s relation, it follows that

C_{n}(\psi,0)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\mathbb{R},&k=m(0),\\
0,&\text{ otherwise },\end{array}\right.\\

where
\displaystyle m(0)
is a Morse index of
\displaystyle 0
. We use Morse’s relation, we get that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{k\geq 0}\rank C_{k}(\psi,\infty)X^{k}+(1+X)Q(X)=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{k\geq 0}\rank C_{k}(\psi,0)X^{k}+\sum_{k\geq 0}\rank C_{k}(\psi,\mathrm{w}_{n})X^{k}+\sum_{k\geq 0}\rank C_{k}(\psi,\mathrm{v}_{n})X^{k}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

3.36
), it follows that

where
\displaystyle\displaystyle\beta_{k}
nonnegative integer and

we have
\displaystyle\displaystyle 2a=1+2\sum_{k\geq 0}\beta_{k}.

leads to a contradiction. Thus, there exist infinitely solutions to the problem (

4
Fundamental Theorem

Theorem 10
.

Suppose that conditions
\displaystyle(\mathrm{V}),
and
\displaystyle(\mathcal{H}_{1})-(\mathcal{H}_{4})
are satisfied. Then, problem (
1
) admits an infinitely weak solutions in
\displaystyle\displaystyle Y_{1}.

Proof.

Let
\displaystyle\displaystyle\{\mathrm{w}_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset Y_{1}
be the sequence of solutions to problem (
3
). So, we have that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\begin{split}&\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{1}p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}\\
\displaystyle&+\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{2}q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}\\
\displaystyle&=\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g_{n}(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))}{(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n})^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}\varphi(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}+\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n})\varphi(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x},\text{ for all }\varphi\in Y_{1}^{*}.\end{split}

(4.1)

We take
\displaystyle\displaystyle\varphi=\mathrm{w}_{n}
in (
4.1
), we have that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{1}p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}+\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{2}q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g_{n}(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))}{(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n})^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}+\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n})\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g_{n}(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))}{(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n})^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}+\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})}d\mathrm{x}.

Combining
\displaystyle(\mathcal{H}_{1})
with
\displaystyle(\mathrm{V}),
it follows that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{1}p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}+\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{2}q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g_{n}(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))}{(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n})^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}+\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})+\frac{1}{n}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})}d\mathrm{x}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathcal{U}}g(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))|^{1-\xi(\mathrm{x})}d\mathrm{x}+\eta_{1}\|\mathrm{w}_{n}\|_{Y_{1}}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathcal{U}}\beta(\mathrm{x})(1+|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))|^{r(\mathrm{x})-1)})|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})|^{1-\xi(\mathrm{x})}d\mathrm{x}+\eta_{1}\|\mathrm{w}_{n}\|_{Y_{1}}.

Since
\displaystyle\displaystyle r(\mathrm{x})-1\leq r(\mathrm{x})-\xi(\mathrm{x})
for all
\displaystyle\displaystyle\mathrm{x}\in\mathcal{U},
we get that

\displaystyle\|\mathrm{w}_{n}\|_{Y_{1}}\leq\frac{\|\beta\|_{\infty}C(r(\mathrm{x}),p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y}),q_{1}(\mathrm{x}),\xi(\mathrm{x}),s_{1},\mathcal{U})}{1-\eta_{1}}.

Hence, the sequence
\displaystyle\displaystyle\{\mathrm{w}_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}
is bounded in
\displaystyle\displaystyle Y_{1}.
Since
\displaystyle\displaystyle Y_{1}
is a reflexive Banach space, up to a subsequence, still denoted by
\displaystyle\displaystyle\{\mathrm{w}_{n}\}
such that
\displaystyle\displaystyle\mathrm{w}_{n}\rightharpoonup\mathrm{w}
weakly in
\displaystyle Y_{1},

\displaystyle\displaystyle\mathrm{w}_{n}\to\mathrm{w}
strongly in
\displaystyle\displaystyle L^{a(\mathrm{x})}(\mathcal{U})
for
\displaystyle\displaystyle 1\leq a(\mathrm{x})<p^{*}_{s_{1}}(\mathrm{x}),
and
\displaystyle\displaystyle\mathrm{w}_{n}\to\mathrm{w}
a.e in
\displaystyle\mathcal{U}.
A similar discussion as in Theorem
4
gives that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\begin{gathered}\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}[\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{1}p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}\\
\displaystyle+\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y})|^{q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{2}q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}]\\
=\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y})|^{p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{1}p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}\\
+\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y})|^{q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{2}q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}.\end{gathered}

Since
\displaystyle\displaystyle\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x})>0,
we get that

\displaystyle|\frac{g_{n}(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))\varphi(\mathrm{x})}{(\frac{1}{n}+\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}|\leq|g(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x}))\varphi(\mathrm{x})|.

From the dominated converge theorem, it follows that

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty}\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g_{n}(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))\varphi(\mathrm{x})}{(\frac{1}{n}+\mathrm{w}_{n}(\mathrm{x}))^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}d\mathrm{x}=\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x}))\varphi(\mathrm{x})}{(\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x}))^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}d\mathrm{x}.

Similarly, we prove that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}_{n}+\frac{1}{n}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})-2}(\mathrm{w}_{n}+\frac{1}{n})\varphi(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}=\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})-2}\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})\varphi(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x}.

(4.7)

Finally, passing to the limit in (
4.1
), we deduce that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\begin{gathered}\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y})|^{p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{1}p(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}+\int_{\mathcal{U}\times\mathcal{U}}\frac{|\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y})|^{q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})-2}(\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})-\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{y}))(\varphi(\mathrm{x})-\varphi(\mathrm{y}))}{|\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y}|^{N+s_{2}q(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{y})}}d\mathrm{x}d\mathrm{y}\\
=\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{U}}\frac{g(\mathrm{x},\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x}))\varphi(\mathrm{x})}{(\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x}))^{\xi(\mathrm{x})}}d\mathrm{x}+\int_{\mathcal{U}}\mathcal{V}(\mathrm{x})|\mathrm{w}|^{\sigma(\mathrm{x})-2}\mathrm{w}(\mathrm{x})\varphi(\mathrm{x})d\mathrm{x},\text{ for all }\varphi\in Y_{1}^{*},\end{gathered}

namely
\displaystyle\displaystyle\mathrm{w}
is a weak solution to (
1
).
