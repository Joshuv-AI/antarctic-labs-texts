The quotient spaces of topological groups with a q q -point Corresponding author: The project is supported by the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province under Grant (No. 2021A1515010381) and the Innovation Project of Department of Education of Guangdong Province (No. 2022KTSCX145).
Li-Hong Xie, Hai-Hua Lin, Piyu Li

Abstract
In this paper, we study the uniformities on the double coset spaces in topological groups. As an implication, the quotient spaces of topological groups with a q q -point are studied. It mainly shows that: (1) Suppose that G G is a topological group with a q q -point and H H is a closed subgroup of G G ; then the quotient space G / H G/H is an open and quasi-perfect preimage of a metrizable space; in particular, G / H G/H is an M M -space. (2) Suppose that G G is a topological group with a strict q q -point and H H is a closed subgroup of G G ; then the quotient space G / H G/H is an open and sequentially perfect preimage of a metrizable space. (3) Suppose that G G is a topological group with a strong q q -point and H H is a closed subgroup of G G ; then the quotient space G / H G/H is an open and strongly sequentially perfect preimage of a metrizable space.
