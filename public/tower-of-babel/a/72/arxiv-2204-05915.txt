Generation of S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) sum rule for charmed baryon decay
Di Wang 1

Abstract
Flavor S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) symmetry is a powerful tool to analyze charmed baryon decays. In this work, we propose an approach to generate S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) sum rules for the singly and doubly charmed baryon decays without writing the Wigner-Eckhart invariants explicitly. The S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) sum rules are computed routinely in several master formulas. Hundreds of S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) sum rules are found to serve as test of the flavor symmetry in the charmed baryon decays.

II
Theoretical framework

In the Standard Model, the tree level effective Hamiltonian of charm decay can be written as
Wang:2020gmn

\mathcal{H}_{\rm eff}={G_{F}\over\sqrt{2}}\sum_{q=d,s}V_{cq}^{*}V_{uq}\sum_{i^{\prime}=1}^{2}C_{i^{\prime}}(\mu)O_{i^{\prime}}(\mu)=\sum_{i,j,k=1}^{3}H^{ij}_{k}O_{ij}^{k},

(1)

where
O_{ij}^{k}
denotes the four-quark operator, and
H
is a
3\times 3\times 3
complex matrix that can be obtained from the map
(\bar{u}q_{1})(\bar{q}_{2}c)\rightarrow V^{*}_{cq_{2}}V_{uq_{1}}
in current-current operators and
V_{cs}\simeq V_{ud}\approx 1
,
V_{cd}\simeq-V_{us}\approx-\lambda

ParticleDataGroup:2020ssz
.
Operator
O_{ij}^{k}
, as the representation of
SU(3)
group, can be decomposed into direct sum of irreducible representations,
3\otimes\overline{3}\otimes 3=3_{p}\oplus 3_{t}\oplus\overline{6}\oplus 15
.
The initial and final states, such as pseudoscalar mesons, can be written as

\displaystyle|M_{\alpha}\rangle=(M_{\alpha})^{i}_{j}|M^{i}_{j}\rangle.

(2)

|M^{i}_{j}\rangle
is the quark composition of meson state,
|M^{i}_{j}\rangle=|q_{i}\bar{q}_{j}\rangle
.
(M_{\alpha})
is the coefficient matrix.
The decay amplitude of the two-body nonleptonic weak decay of charm hadron (taking
D_{\gamma}\to M_{\alpha}M_{\beta}
as example) can be constructed to be

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D_{\gamma}\to M_{\alpha}M_{\beta})

\displaystyle=\langle M_{\alpha}M_{\beta}|\mathcal{H}_{\rm eff}|D_{\gamma}\rangle

\displaystyle~=\sum_{\omega}\,(M_{\alpha})^{n}_{m}\langle M^{n}_{m}|(M_{\beta})^{s}_{r}\langle M^{s}_{r}||H^{jk}_{l}O^{jk}_{l}||(D_{\gamma})_{i}|D_{i}\rangle

\displaystyle~~=\sum_{\omega}\,\langle M_{m}^{n}M^{s}_{r}|O^{jk}_{l}|D_{i}\rangle\times(M_{\alpha})_{m}^{n}(M_{\beta})_{r}^{s}H^{jk}_{l}(D_{\gamma})_{i}

\displaystyle~~~=\sum_{\omega}X_{\omega}(C_{\omega})_{\alpha\beta\gamma},

(3)

in which
\sum_{\omega}
present summing over all the possible full contractions of tensor
\langle M_{m}^{n}M^{s}_{r}|O^{jk}_{l}|D_{i}\rangle

According to the Wigner-Eckhart theorem

;

All the information of initial/final states is absorbed into the Clebsch-Gordan (CG) coefficient
(C_{\omega})_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=(M_{\alpha})_{m}^{n}(M_{\beta})_{r}^{s}H^{jk}_{l}(D_{\gamma})_{i}

If the effective Hamiltonian

If the effective Hamiltonian is decomposed into irreducible representation of
SU(3)

To get
SU(3)
sum rules, a widely used approach in literatures is to write the decay amplitude of each mode decomposed by the Wigner-Eckhart invariants, TDA or IRA, then combine several modes to form a polygon in the complex plane.
The detailed discussions about searching for
SU(3)
sum rules in this way are shown in Refs.
Wang:2017azm
;
Savage:1989qr
;
Jia:2019zxi

SU(3)_{F}
limit are found.
However, this method is laborious.
Ref.
Grossman:2012ry
provides an useful approach to generate
SU(3)
sum rules for the charmed meson decays without computing the Wigner-Eckhart invariants.
The idea is that if there exists an operator
T
under which
TH=0
, the invariance of Hamiltonian is related to the generation of sum rules for the amplitudes.
We can understand it as follows.
If
TH=0
, it follows that

\langle M_{\alpha}M_{\beta}|T\mathcal{H}_{\rm eff}|D_{\gamma}\rangle=\sum_{\omega}\,\langle M_{m}^{n}M^{s}_{r}|O^{jk}_{l}|D_{i}\rangle\times(M_{\alpha})_{m}^{n}(M_{\beta})_{r}^{s}(TH)^{jk}_{l}(D_{\gamma})_{i}=0.

(4)

One can apply the operator
T
to the initial/final states rather than the effective Hamiltonian and expand the results with the initial/final states as bases.
Then the LHS of Eq.
4
is turned into sum of several decay amplitudes. The RHS of Eq.
4
is invariant.
Thereby, Eq. (
4
) is an abstract
SU(3)
sum rule.

It is found in Ref.
Grossman:2012ry
that the effective Hamiltonian is invariant under the operators
T_{-}
and
S
in the
SU(3)_{F}
limit, i.e.,
T_{-}H=0
,
SH=0
.

T_{-}
and
S
are expressed as
Grossman:2012ry

\displaystyle T_{-}=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}0&0&0\\
1&0&0\\
\lambda&0&0\\
\end{array}\right)\quad{\rm and}\quad S=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}0&0&0\\
0&-\lambda&1\\
0&-\lambda^{2}&\lambda\\
\end{array}\right).

S
is a linear combination of
U
-spin operators and
T_{-}
is a linear combination of isospin and
V
-spin operators,

\displaystyle S=-\lambda U_{3}-\lambda^{2}U_{-}+U_{+},\qquad T_{-}=I_{-}+\lambda V_{-}.

(12)

So the
SU(3)
sum rules generated from
S
are
U
-spin sum rules, and the
SU(3)
sum rules generated from
T_{-}
are combination of isospin sum rules and
V
-spin sum rules.

If we consider the linear flavor
SU(3)
breaking, i.e., the NLO corrections of
SU(3)_{F}
symmetry, the invariance of the effective Hamiltonian under
T_{-}
and
S
is broken,
T_{-}H_{\cancel{SU(3)_{F}}}\neq 0
and
SH_{\cancel{SU(3)_{F}}}\neq 0

.
And it seems that such operator under which
H_{\cancel{SU(3)_{F}}}
being invariant is unavailable. So we only consider the flavor
SU(3)
limit in this work.

V
Summary

Flavor
SU(3)
symmetry is a powerful tool to analyze the charmed baryon decays since the factorization scheme based on the expansion of
1/m_{c}
is not always valid.
In this work, we propose a method to compute amplitude sum rules for the singly and doubly charmed baryon decays in the
SU(3)_{F}
limit without writing the Wigner-Eckhart invariants.
Hundreds of
SU(3)
sum rules are derived systematically which can be tested by experiments.
