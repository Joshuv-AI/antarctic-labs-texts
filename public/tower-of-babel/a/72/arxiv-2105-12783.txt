Tautological classes on moduli spaces of hyper-Kähler manifolds. Erratum
Nicolas Bergeron, Zhiyuan Li

Abstract
. This note is an erratum to the paper “Tautological classes on moduli spaces of hyper-Kähler manifolds.” Thorsten Beckman and Mirko Mauri have pointed to us a gap in the proof of [ 1 , Theorem 8.2.1] . We do not know how to correct the proof. We can only recover a partial statement. This gap affects the proof of one of the two main results of [ 1 ] , we explain how to correct it.



1.
Notation

Following notation of
[
1
, Section 3]
we let
\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}
be a connected component of the moduli space of
h
-polarised (or
h
-ample) hyper-Kähler manifolds of dimension
2n

b_{2}=b+3
, with a full
\ell
-level structure. As in
[

we furthermore denote by

\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
the moduli space of
h
-ample
\Sigma

\ell
-level structure. We have a natural forgetful map

(1)

\iota_{\Sigma}:\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}.

Let

\pi_{h}^{\ell}:\mathcal{U}_{h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}\quad\mbox{and}\quad\pi_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}:\mathcal{U}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}

We define the following subalgebras in

:

•

is the subalgebra generated by irreducible components of the images of the maps (
1

\Sigma
;

•

the

\mathrm{R}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell})
is the subalgebra generated by the
\kappa

the

We add the subscript

If

2.
The Leray spectral sequence and cup products: a gap

Following
[
1
, §8.2]
in this section we simply denote by
\pi:\mathcal{U}\to\mathcal{F}
a universal family of lattice polarized hyperkähler manifolds
\pi_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}:\mathcal{U}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
.

Consider the local systems
\mathbb{H}^{j}=R^{j}\pi_{*}\mathbb{Q}
. It follows from
[
3
, Theorem 1.1.1]
that there is a splitting of the Leray filtration in the category of mixed Hodge structure, the degeneration of the Leray spectral sequence for
\pi
therefore gives an isomorphism of mixed Hodge structure

(2)

\mathrm{H}^{k}(\mathcal{U},\mathbb{Q})\cong\bigoplus_{i+j=k}\mathrm{H}^{i}(\mathcal{F},\mathbb{H}^{j}).

The cup product on the LHS of (
2
) induces a cup product on the Leray spectral sequence, and therefore a cup product on the RHS of (
2
). However the isomorphism (
2
) does not preserve the ring structure; see e.g.
[
4
, Prop. 0.4 or Prop. 0.6]
.

In
[
1
, Theorem 8.2.1]
, we claimed that the two cup products on (
2
) agree on the subalgebra generated by low degree classes (
\mathrm{degree}<\frac{1}{2}\dim\mathcal{F}
) up to removing some Noether-Lefschetz loci. Based on this result, we proved Theorem 8.3.1 and Theorem 4.3.1 in
[
1
]
, which implies that

\mathrm{R}_{\hom}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{F}_{h})={\rm NL}^{\bullet}_{\hom}(\mathcal{F}_{h})

when
n<\frac{b_{2}-3}{8}
. In particular, the cohomological tautological conjecture holds for K3 surfaces and
K3^{[2]}
-type hyperkähler manifolds. However, in our ‘proof’, we only show that

Proposition 2
.

Let
\alpha_{1}
and
\alpha_{2}
be two classes in
\mathrm{H}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{U},\mathbb{Q})
. Assume that the sum the degrees of
\alpha_{1}
and
\alpha_{2}
is
<\frac{1}{2}\dim\mathcal{F}
, then

(3)

\alpha_{1}\wedge_{\rm LHS}\alpha_{2}=\alpha_{1}\wedge_{\rm RHS}\alpha_{2},

up to removing some Nother-Lefschetz components.

Proof.

This is a direct consequence of the cohomological generalized Franchetta Conjecture
[
1
, Theorem 8.1.1]
.
∎

For the entire algebra
\mathrm{H}^{\bullet}_{<\frac{1}{2}\dim\mathcal{F}}(\mathcal{U})
, we actually do not know if (
3
) holds (up to removing some NL loci). In other words, we don’t know if the FKM ring is stable by the LHS ring structure in large degree.

3.
The corrected cohomological tautological conjecture

It remains open whether the two cup products coincides (up to some classes support on NL loci) on the entire subalgebra of
{\rm CH}^{\bullet}_{\rm hom}(\mathcal{U})
generated by cycles of small co-dimension. This causes a problem in the proof of
[
1
, Theorem 8.3.1]
and hence of
[
1
, Theorem 4.3.1]
.

As we will show in the next sections we can however prove the following theorem. The bound on
n
is weaker but it is good enough to apply to
K3
surfaces and
K3^{[2]}
-type hyper-Kähler manifolds.

Recall that
\pi_{h}^{\ell}:\mathcal{U}_{h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}
denotes a smooth family of
h
-polarized hyperkähler manifolds over an irreducible quasi-projective variety
\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}
of dimension
b\geq 3
. We define the following conditions to state our main result:

\mathbf{(\ast)}
For every lattice-polarized universal family
\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h}\to\mathcal{F}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h}
, the group of Hodge classes of
degree
\mathbf{2n}
of the very general fiber is spanned by Beauville-Voisin classes.

\mathbf{(\ast\ast)}
For every lattice-polarized universal family
\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h}\to\mathcal{F}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h}
, the group of Hodge classes of
degree
\mathbf{\leq 2n}
of the very general fiber is spanned by Beauville-Voisin classes.

Then we have

Theorem 3
.

Suppose
b\geq\sup\{16n-12,12n-4\}
. Then

\mathrm{DR}^{\bullet}_{\hom}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell})={\rm NL}_{\hom}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}).

Moreover, we have
\mathrm{R}^{\bullet}_{\hom}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell})={\rm NL}_{\hom}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell})
if the condition
(\ast)
holds and

(4)

\mathrm{NL}_{\hom}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell})=\mathrm{R}^{\bullet}_{\rm hom}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell})=\widetilde{\mathrm{R}}^{\bullet}_{\rm hom}(\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}),

if either
b\geq\sup\{16n-8,12n-2\}
or the condition
(\ast\ast)
holds.
In particular, the cohomological tautological conjecture holds for both
K3
surfaces and
K3^{[2]}
-type hyper-Kähler manifolds.

4.1.
Noether-Lefschetz locus

Definition 4
.

Let
\alpha
be a class in
{\rm Hdg}^{2k}(\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h})
. We say that
\alpha
is
supported on the Noether-Lefschetz locus
if there exist finitely many lattices
\Sigma_{j}\supset\Sigma
with
{\rm rank}(\Sigma_{j})={\rm rank}(\Sigma)+1
and classes
\gamma_{j}\in{\rm Hdg}^{2k-2}(\mathcal{U}_{\Sigma_{j},h}^{\ell})
such that

\alpha=\sum\limits_{j}r_{j}(\rho_{j})_{\ast}(\gamma_{j}),

where
r_{j}\in\mathbb{Q}
and
\rho_{j}
denotes the map
\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma_{j},h}\to\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h}
corresponding to the forgetful map
\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma_{j},h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
.

Let
d=\dim\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
. The main results in
[
2
]
and
[
1
]
imply the following

Theorem 5
.

(i)

For every degree
k<\frac{d+1}{3}
or
k>\frac{2d-1}{3}
, we have

{\rm Hdg}^{2k}(\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell})=\mathrm{NL}^{k}_{\rm hom}(\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}).

(ii)

Let
\alpha\in{\rm Hdg}^{2k}(\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma,h})
with
k<\min\left\{\frac{d}{4}+1,\frac{d+1}{3}\right\}.

If the restriction of
\alpha
to the very general fiber of
\pi_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
is zero, then
\alpha
is supported on the Noether-Lefschetz locus.

Proof.

The first part is precisely the main result of
[
2
]
.

The second assertion follows from
[
1
, Theorem 6.4.1]
and
[
1
, §6.2]
. In fact letting

be a finite dimensional
{\rm SO}(2,d;\mathbb{R})
-representation and
\mathbb{E}
be the associated local system on
\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
, it is proved there that

\left(\dagger\right)
for all

, the space
(W_{2i}H^{2i}(\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell},\mathbb{E}))^{i,i}
is spanned

espac
by decorated special cycles in
\mathrm{SC}^{i}(\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell},\mathbb{E})

and, by definition, special cycle classes are generated by pushforwards of decorated fundamental classes of
\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma^{\prime},h}^{\ell}
, where
\Sigma^{\prime}
is a lattice containing
\Sigma
and the pushforward is with respect to the forgetful map
\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma^{\prime},h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
.

We now proceed as in the proof of

If

then by hypothesis we have

and
\left(\dagger\right)
applied to

implies that the component of

in

is a linear combination of decorated Noether-Lefschetz cycle classes. Finally if

the local system
\mathbb{H}^{2(k-i)}
is trivial and we can similarly apply (i) to conclude that all the components of

can be decomposed as linear combinations of decorated Noether-Lefschetz cycle classes.

We are therefore reduced to proving that a decorated Noether-Lefschetz cycle class represents a class in

that is supported on the Noether-Lefschetz locus. To do so consider a forgetful map

{\rm Hdg}^{2a}(\mathcal{U}^{\ell}_{\Sigma^{\prime},h})
whose restriction to the very general fiber is precisely

By induction on

Another key result is the following

Corollary 6
.

Assume that

. Then for any
\alpha\in{\rm CH}^{k}(\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell})

By assumption, the class

belongs to

and its pushforward image

We then have distinguish two cases.

(1)

If

\mathcal{F}_{h}^{\ell}
, that
(\iota_{\Sigma})_{\ast}[\alpha]
lies in

(2)

If

and it follows from Theorem

(i), applied to
\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}

.

∎

4.2.
Inductive step

Throughout this subsection, we simply denote by
\pi:\mathcal{U}\to\mathcal{F}
the universal family
\mathcal{U}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}\to\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma,h}^{\ell}
.
We make use of Theorem

(ii) to investigate the difference between the rings

The following result shows that when

Theorem 7
.

Let
\alpha=\prod\limits_{i}\alpha_{i}\in\mathrm{H}_{\leq 2n}^{2k}(\mathcal{U})
with

\alpha_{i}

\alpha-\beta=\sum\limits_{j}r_{j}(\rho_{j})_{\ast}(\gamma_{j}),

\gamma_{j}\in H^{2k-2}_{\leq 2n-1}(\mathcal{U}_{\Sigma_{j}})

\Sigma_{j}\supset\Sigma

Let
\alpha\in{\rm Hdg}^{2k}(\mathcal{U})
with

5.
Another correction

There are some minor issues in the paper. In section 2.4, we say that the orthogonal group
G_{X}
can act on the entire cohomology ring of
X
via the monodromy action. This is not true is general. There is only a Spin group action on the entire cohomology ring and it does not factor through the orthogonal group in general (e.g. this fails for generalized Kummer varieties). But
G_{X}
does act on the ring of even degree cohomology groups via the LLV action. Two actions agree after taking level structures (passing to a finite index subgroup). See
[
1
, Remark 2.4.1]
. Note that the
G_{X}
action is only needed to construct theta classes in even degrees, so this does not affect our results.
