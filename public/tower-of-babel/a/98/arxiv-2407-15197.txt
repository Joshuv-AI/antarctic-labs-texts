Abstract
. In this paper, we prove the fractional Hardy inequality on polarisable metric measure spaces. The integral Hardy inequality for 1 < p ≤ q < ∞ 1<p\leq q<\infty is playing a key role in the proof. Moreover, we also prove the fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequality on metric measure spaces. In addition, logarithmic Hardy-Sobolev and fractional Nash type inequalities on metric measure spaces are presented. In addition, we present applications on homogeneous groups and on the Heisenberg group.



1.
Introduction

In one of the famous works of G. H. Hardy (see
[
21
]
), Hardy showed the following inequality:

(1.1)

\int_{a}^{\infty}\frac{1}{x^{p}}\left(\int_{a}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}\,dx\leq\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p}\int_{a}^{\infty}f^{p}(x)\,dx,

where
f\geq 0
,
p>1
and
a>0
. This inequality is called the direct integral Hardy inequality. This result has been extensively studied in various works, including
[
8
,
13
,
18
,
27
,
28
,
29
]
, and generalizations to higher dimensions have been established in
[
9
]
. The extension of the direct Hardy inequality to metric measure spaces for
1<p\leq q<\infty
and
0<q<p
with
1<p<\infty
were established in
[
33
]
and
[
34
]
, respectively. Moreover, the reverse integral Hardy inequality for
q<0
with
p\in(0,1)
and
q\leq p<0
were obtained in
[
25
]
and
[
26
]
.

In
[
10
]
and
[
16
]
, the fractional Hardy inequality on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
was studied, yielding the following inequality:

(1.2)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}_{E}}\,dx\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|_{E}^{n+sp}}\,dxdy,

where
s\in(0,1)
,
p>1
is such that
n>sp
, and
|\cdot|_{E}
denotes the Euclidean distance. Also, (
1.2
) was obtained for the case
n<sp
in
[
22
]
. Also, by using the measure of the Euclidean ball
|B(x,r)|=|\sigma_{n-1}|r^{n}
, where
|\sigma_{n-1}|
is the volume of the
n-1
dimensional sphere, we can rewrite (
1.2
) as

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}_{E}}\,dx\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|_{E}^{sp}|B(0,|x-y|_{E})|}dxdy.

Let
(\mathbb{X},d)
be a metric measure space. The measure
|\cdot|
is doubling if there is a constant
C>0
, such that

|B(x,2r)|\leq C|B(x,r)|,\quad x\in\mathbb{X},\,\,r>0,

and
|\cdot|
is reverse doubling, if there are constants
0<k<1
and
0<C<1
such that

|B(x,kr)|\leq C|B(x,r)|,\quad x\in\mathbb{X},\,\,0<r<\text{diam}(\mathbb{X})/2,

where
\text{diam}(\mathbb{X})
is the diameter of
\mathbb{X}
.

For
E\subset\mathbb{X}
and
r>0
, the open
r
-neighborhood of
E
is the following set

E_{r}:=\{x\in\mathbb{X}:d(x,E)<r\}.

The upper Assouad codimension of
E
is denoted by
\overline{\text{co\,}\dim_{A}}(E)
, which is the infimum of all
D\geq 0
for which there is a constant
C>0
such that

\frac{|E_{r}\cap B(x,R)|}{|B(x,R)|}\geq C\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{D}

for all
x\in E
and
0<r<R<\text{diam}(E)
.
Hence, in
[
11
]
, the authors obtained the localised fractional Hardy inequality on metric measure spaces with doubling measures in the following form:

Theorem 1.1

(Theorem 5.2,
[
11
]
)
.

Let
0<s<1
and
p>1
. Assume that the metric measure space
(\mathbb{X},d)
is complete and that
|\cdot|
is reverse doubling. Let
E\subset\mathbb{X}
be a closed set with
\overline{\rm{co\,}\dim_{A}}(E)<sp
and let
u:\mathbb{X}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
be a continuous function such that
u=0
on
E
. Then there exists
C>0
, independent of
u
, such that

\int_{B\setminus E}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}}{d^{sp}(x,E)}dx\leq\int_{3B}\int_{3B}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{d^{sp}(x,y)|B(x,d(x,y))|}dxdy,

whenever
B=B(w,r)
with
w\in E
and
0<r<\rm{diam}(E)
.

On stratified group, Ciatti, Cowling and Ricci proved the fractional Hardy inequality with fractional powers of the sub-Laplacian by using the boundedness of
T_{s}:u\mapsto|\cdot|^{-s}\mathcal{L}^{-\frac{s}{2}}u
on the Lebesgue space, where
|\cdot|
is a homogeneous norm,
0<s<\frac{Q}{p}
(
Q
homogeneous dimension and
p>1
) and
\mathcal{L}
is the sub-Laplacian. On the Heisenberg group, Thangavelu and Roncal (in
[
31
]
) obtained the fractional Hardy inequality in the case
p=2
by using some explicit integral representations for the fractional powers of the conformal invariant fractional sub-Laplacian in the form
\mathcal{L}_{s}=|2T|^{s}\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{\mathcal{L}}{2|T|}+\frac{1+s}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{\mathcal{L}}{2|T|}+\frac{1-s}{2}\right)}
. Furthermore, the authors derived this representation via the heat semigroup generated by
\mathcal{L}_{s}
. Generally, for the fractional sub-Laplacian
\mathcal{L}^{s}
, the fractional Hardy inequality was obtained by establishing a relation between
\mathcal{L}_{s}
and
\mathcal{L}^{s}
using the expressions
U_{s}=\mathcal{L}_{s}\mathcal{L}^{-s}
and
V_{s}=\mathcal{L}^{-1}_{1-s}\mathcal{L}\mathcal{L}^{-s}
. These operators are bounded on
L^{2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
(see
[
31
, Subsection 5.3]
). In
[
1
]
, by using this integral representation, the authors generalised a version of fractional Hardy inequality in the case
Q>sp
. Further investigations have considered fractional Hardy inequalities in different settings, such as homogeneous Lie groups. In addition, in the case of
Q>sp
a fractional version of the Hardy inequality was obtained in
[
24
]
. The main aim of this paper is to obtain the fractional Hardy inequality on polarisable metric measure spaces.

In
[
20
]
, the integer order Hardy-Sobolev inequality was established, given by

\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p^{*}_{\beta}}}{|x|_{E}^{\beta}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{p}{p^{*}_{\beta}}}\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla u(x)|^{p}\,dx,

where,
p^{*}_{\beta}=\frac{(n-\beta)p}{n-p}
,
1<p<n
,
\beta\in(0,p)
, and
\nabla
is the Euclidean gradient. The Sobolev inequality is obtained when
\beta=0
, and the classical Hardy inequality arises when
\beta=p
. The fractional version of the Hardy-Sobolev inequality was introduced in
[
35
]
as follows:

\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p^{*}_{s,\beta}}}{|x|_{E}^{\beta}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{p}{p^{*}_{s,\beta}}}\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}\,dxdy,

where,
p^{*}_{s,\beta}=\frac{(n-\beta)p}{n-sp}
,
0<\beta<sp<n
, and
s\in(0,1)
. Similarly, we get the fractional Sobolev inequality if
\beta=0
and the fractional Hardy inequality if
\beta=p
. We refer to
[
3
,
4
,
12
,
17
]
for this topic.

In this paper, we extend the fractional Hardy and fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequalities to metric measure spaces. We consider a metric space
\mathbb{X}
equipped with a Borel measure
dx
allowing for the polar decomposition at
a\in\mathbb{X}
. This decomposition involves the existence of a locally integrable function
\lambda\in L^{1}_{loc}
, such that for any
f\in L^{1}(\mathbb{X})
we have

(1.3)

\int_{\mathbb{X}}f(x)\,dx=\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma_{r}}f(r,\omega)\lambda(r,\omega)\,d\omega_{r}dr,

for the set
\Sigma_{r}=\{x\in\mathbb{X}:d(x,a)=r\}\subset\mathbb{X}
with a measure on it denoted by
d\omega=d\omega_{r}
.

The condition (
1.3
) can be obtained as the standard polar decomposition formula, if a differentiable structure exists on
\mathbb{X}
. In general, when
\mathbb{X}
does not have a differentiable structure, we can not obtain the function
\lambda(r,\omega)
as the Jacobian of a polar change of coordinates. Let us consider some examples of
\mathbb{X}
for which the condition (
1.3
) holds, and have the different expressions for
\lambda(r,\omega)
:

(I)

Euclidean space
\mathbb{R}^{n}:\lambda(r,\omega)=r^{n-1}
.

(II)

Homogeneous groups
\mathbb{G}
:
\lambda(r,\omega)=r^{Q-1}
, where
Q
represents the homogeneous dimension of the
group. Homogeneous groups have been extensively studied by Folland and Stein
[
15
]
, see also
[
14
]
and
[
32
]
.

(III)

Hyperbolic spaces
\mathbb{H}^{n}:\lambda(r,\omega)=(\sinh r)^{n-1}
.

(IV)

Cartan–Hadamard manifolds: Here, we consider a Riemannian manifold
(M,g)
with sectional curvature denoted as
K_{M}
. A Riemannian manifold is called a Cartan–Hadamard manifold, if it possesses the following characteristics: it is complete, simply connected, and has non-positive sectional curvature, meaning that the sectional curvature
K_{M}\leq 0
for each plane section at every point of
M
. To proceed, we fix a point
a\in M
and denote by
\rho(x)=d(x,a)

the geodesic distance from
x
to
a
on
M
. The exponential map
\exp_{a}:T_{a}M\rightarrow M
is a diffeomorphism, see e.g. Helgason
[
23
]
. Let
J(\rho,\omega)
be the density function on
M
, see e.g.
[
19
]
. Consequently, the polar decomposition
takes the following form:

\int_{M}f(x)dx=\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-1}}f(\exp_{a}(\rho\omega))J(\rho,\omega)\rho^{n-1}\,d\rho d\omega,

so that we have (
1.3
) with
\lambda(\rho,\omega)=J(\rho,\omega)\rho^{n-1}
.

Conditions for (
1.3
) to hold on general metric measure spaces were recently analysed in
[
2
]
.

2.
Main results

Consider
\mathbb{X}
as a metric measure space. For brevity, we denote a ball in
\mathbb{X}
with center
a
and radius
r
as
B(a,r)
, i.e.,

B(a,r):=\left\{x\in\mathbb{X}:d(x,a)<r\right\},

where
d
is the metric on
\mathbb{X}
. Let
a
be a fixed point in
\mathbb{X}
, and we will use the notation
|x|_{a}=d(a,x)
.

Furthermore, let us define
|B(a,r)|
as the measure of the ball
B(a,r)
.

Now, turning our attention to this setting, we recall the integral Hardy inequality on metric measure spaces.

Theorem 2.1

(
[
33
]
Integral Hardy inequality)
.

Let
1<p\leq q<\infty
. And let
\mathbb{X}
be a metric measure space with a polar decomposition at
a
. Let
g,h
be positive measurable functions on
\mathbb{X}
such that
g\in L^{1}(\mathbb{X}\backslash\{0\})
and
h^{1-p^{\prime}}\in L^{1}_{loc}(\mathbb{X})
. Then the following inequality

(2.1)

\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left|f(y)\right|\,dy\right)^{q}g(x)\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\leq C_{H}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left|f(x)\right|^{p}h(x)\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

holds for all measurable functions
f:\mathbb{X}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
if and only if the following condition holds:

(2.2)

D_{1}:=\sup_{x\neq 0}\left[\int_{\mathbb{X}\backslash B(a,|x|_{a})}g(y)\,dy\right]^{\frac{1}{q}}\left[\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}h^{1-p^{\prime}}(y)\,dy\right]^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}<\infty.

Moreover,

D_{1}\leq C_{H}\leq(p^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1},

where
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
.

2.1.
Fractional Hardy inequality

As the main result of this section, we present the fractional Hardy inequality on metric measure spaces with polar decomposition.

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
\mathbb{X}
be a metric measure space with polar decomposition at
a
, and let
s>0
and
p>1
. Assume that
v(x,y)
is a positive measurable function on
\mathbb{X}\times\mathbb{X}
and denote

(2.3)

A(x):=\left(\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}v^{1-p^{\prime}}(x,y)dy\right)^{1-p}.

Suppose that
\left(p^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1}<1
, where

(2.4)

D_{1}=\sup_{x\neq a}\left[\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}\backslash B(a,|x|_{a})}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sp}|B(a,|y|_{a})|^{p}}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sp}}\right)^{1-p^{\prime}}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}\right]<\infty.

Then the following inequality holds

(2.5)

\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)\left|u(x)\right|^{p}}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\,dx\leq\frac{2^{sp}}{(1-\left(p^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1})^{p}}\int_{\mathbb{X}}\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}dx\,dy.

Proof.

By Minkowski inequality, we have

(2.6)

\begin{split}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)\left|u(x)\right|^{p}}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\left|u(x)-\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right.\right.\\
&+\left.\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\left.\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\left|u(x)-\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&+\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}=I_{1}+I_{2},\end{split}

where

I_{1}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{1}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\left|u(x)-\frac{1}{B(a,|x|_{a})}\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

and

I_{2}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

By Hölder inequality with
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
, we have

(2.7)

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}\left(u(x)-u(y)\right)v(x,y)^{\frac{1}{p}}v(x,y)^{-\frac{1}{p}}\,dy\right|^{p}\\
&\leq\left(\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)\,dy\right)\left(\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}v^{1-p^{\prime}}(x,y)\,dy\right)^{p-1}.\end{split}

For
0<r_{1}\leq r_{2}
, we have
\left|B(a,r_{1})\right|\leq\left|B(a,r_{2})\right|
.

Furthermore, using the property that
|y|_{a}\leq|x|_{a}
for all
y\in B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)
together with the triangle inequality, we obtain

(2.8)

d(x,y)\leq|x|_{a}+|y|_{a}\leq 2|x|_{a},

and hence

(2.9)

\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|\leq\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|.

Therefore, we get

(2.10)

\begin{split}I_{1}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\left|u(x)-\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{p}}\left|u(x)\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|-\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(u(x)-u(y)\right)\,dy\right|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{7})}}{{\leq}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)\left(\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}v^{1-p^{\prime}}(x,y)\,dy\right)^{p-1}}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{p-1}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)\,dydx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{1})}}{{=}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{1}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|}\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)\,dydx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{8}),(\ref{9})}}{{\leq}}2^{s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\int_{B\left(a,|x|_{a}\right)}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dydx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq 2^{s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.\end{split}

By assumption (
2.4
), we can observe that the integral Hardy inequality (Theorem
2.1
) applies to functions
g(x)=\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|a|_{a})\right|}
and
h(x)=\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}
. This yields

(2.11)

\begin{split}I_{2}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sp}\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}u(y)\,dy\right|^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\leq C_{H}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)\left|u(x)\right|^{p}}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq\left(p^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)\left|u(x)\right|^{p}}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.\end{split}

Substituting (
2.10
) and (
2.11
) into (
2.6
), and using that
\left(p^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1}<1
, we obtain

\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(x)\left|u(x)\right|^{p}}{|x|_{a}^{sp}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\leq\frac{2^{s}}{1-\left(p^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}v(x,y)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

which completes the proof.
∎

2.2.
Fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequality

In this subsection, we show the fractional Hardy-Sobolev inequality on metric measure spaces with polar decomposition.

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
\mathbb{X}
be a metric measure space with a polar decomposition at
a
,
s>0
, and
1<p,q<\infty
. Assume that
z(x)
and
v(x)
are positive measurable functions on
\mathbb{X}
and denote

(2.12)

V(x)=\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}v(y)\,dy,

(2.13)

A(x):=\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{\frac{q}{p}}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{v^{p}(y)}{z(y)}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}\,dy\right)^{-\frac{q}{p^{\prime}}}V^{q}(x)v(x).

Suppose that
\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1}<1
, where

(2.14)

D_{1}=\sup_{x\neq 0}\left[\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}\backslash B(a,|x|_{a})}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{A(y)v^{-q}(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\right)^{(1-q^{\prime})}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}\right]<\infty.

Then we have

(2.15)

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)\right|^{q}A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sq}}\,dx\\
&\leq\frac{2^{sq}}{(1-\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1})^{q}}\int_{\mathbb{X}}v(y)\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}\,dy.\end{split}

Proof.

We follow the same pattern as in the proof of Theorem
2.2
.
By Minkowski inequality, we get

(2.16)

\begin{split}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(y)\right|^{q}A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\left|u(y)-\frac{1}{V(y)}\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right.\right.\\
&+\left.\left.\frac{1}{V(y)}\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\left|u(y)-\frac{1}{V(y)}\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&+\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)}\left|\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}=I_{1}+I_{2},\end{split}

where

I_{1}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\left|u(y)-\frac{1}{V(y)}\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}},

and

I_{2}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)}\left|\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}.

By Hölder inequality with
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
, we get

(2.17)

\begin{split}&\left|\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left(u(y)-u(x)\right)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\\
&\leq\left(\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}\left(\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left(\frac{v^{p}(x)}{z(x)}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p^{\prime}}}.\end{split}

By using (
2.17
) together with (
2.8
) and (
2.9
), we have

(2.18)

\begin{split}I_{1}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\left|u(y)-\frac{1}{V(y)}\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{12})}}{{=}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)}\left|\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left(u(y)-u(x)\right)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{19})}}{{\leq}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)\left(\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)\left(\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left(\frac{v^{p}(x)}{z(x)}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}\,dx\right)^{-\frac{q}{p^{\prime}}}}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{13})}}{{=}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left(\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}v(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}\left|B(a,|y|_{a})\right|^{\frac{q}{p}}}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{8}),(\ref{9})}}{{\leq}}2^{s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\leq 2^{s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{split}

With regard to assumption (
2.14
), we see that the integral Hardy inequality (see Theorem
2.1
) holds for the functions
g(x):=\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(x)}
and
h(x):=\frac{A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sq}v^{q}(x)}
, and hence we have

(2.19)

\begin{split}I_{2}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}(y,a)V^{q}(y)}\left|\int_{B(a,|y|_{a})}u(x)v(x)\,dx\right|^{q}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\leq C_{H}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)\right|^{q}A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sq}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\leq\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)\right|^{q}A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sq}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{split}

Finally, combining (
2.18
), (
2.19
), and considering that
\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1}<1
, we derive

(2.20)

\begin{split}&\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)\right|^{q}A(x)}{|x|_{a}^{sq}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\leq\frac{2^{s}}{1-\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}},\end{split}

completing the proof.
∎

2.3.
Logarithmic Hardy-Sobolev type inequality

Let us recall logarithmic Hölder inequality on general measure spaces.

Theorem 2.4

(Logarithmic Hölder inequality
[
6
]
)
.

Let
\mathbb{X}
be a measure space. Let
u\in L^{p}\left(\mathbb{X}\right)\cap L^{q}\left(\mathbb{X}\right)\backslash\{0\}
with some
1<p<q<\infty
. Then we have

(2.21)

\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u\right|^{p}}{\left\|u\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}\left(\mathbb{X}\right)}}\log\left(\frac{\left|u\right|^{p}}{\left\|u\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}\left(\mathbb{X}\right)}}\right)\,dx\leq\frac{q}{q-p}\log\left(\frac{\left\|u\right\|^{p}_{L^{q}\left(\mathbb{X}\right)}}{\left\|u\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}\left(\mathbb{X}\right)}}\right).

Now we show the logarithmic fractional Hardy-Sobolev inequality on polarisable metric measure space.

Theorem 2.5
.

Let
\mathbb{X}
be a metric measure space with a polar decomposition at
a
, and let
s>0
,
1<p<q<\infty
. Assume that
v(x)
and
z(x)
are non-negative measurable functions on
\mathbb{X}
. Suppose that
\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1}<1
, where

D_{1}=\sup_{x\neq 0}\left[\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}\backslash B(a,|x|_{a})}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{A(y)v^{-q}(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\right)^{(1-q^{\prime})}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}\right]<\infty,

V(x)=\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}v(y)\,dy,

and

A(x):=\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{\frac{q}{p}}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{v^{p}(y)}{z(y)}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}\,dy\right)^{-\frac{q}{p^{\prime}}}V^{q}(x)v(x).

Under these conditions, we have the inequality

(2.22)

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left(\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}|u|}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right)^{p}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\log\left(\frac{\left(\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}|u|}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right)^{p}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)\\
&\leq\frac{q}{q-p}\log\left(C\frac{\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\right),\end{split}

where C is a positive constant independent of
u
.

Proof.

By logarithmic Hölder inequality (
2.21
) and fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequality (
2.15
), we get

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left(\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}|u|}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right)^{p}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\log\left(\frac{\left(\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}|u|}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right)^{p}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)\leq\frac{q}{q-p}\log\left(\frac{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{q}(\mathbb{X})}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)\\
&\leq\frac{q}{q-p}\log\left(C\frac{\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}}{\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}^{s}}\right\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{X})}}\right),\end{split}

completing the proof.
∎

2.4.
Fractional Nash type inequality

Finally we show the fractional Nash type inequality on metric measure spaces with polar decomposition.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
\mathbb{X}
be a metric measure space with a polar decomposition at
a
, and let
s>0
,
2<q<\infty
. Assume that
v(x)
and
z(x)
are non-negative measurable functions on
\mathbb{X}
. Suppose that
\left(q^{\prime}\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}q^{\frac{1}{q}}D_{1}<1
, where

D_{1}=\sup_{x\neq 0}\left[\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}\backslash B(a,|x|_{a})}\frac{A(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}V^{q}(y)}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{A(y)v^{-q}(y)}{|y|_{a}^{sq}}\right)^{(1-q^{\prime})}\,dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}\right]<\infty,

V(x)=\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}v(y)\,dy,

and

A(x):=\frac{1}{\left|B(a,|x|_{a})\right|^{\frac{q}{2}}}\left(\int_{B(a,|x|_{a})}\left(\frac{v^{2}(y)}{z(y)}\right)\,dy\right)^{-\frac{q}{2}}V^{q}(x)v(x).

Then the following inequality holds

\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}}\right\|^{4-\frac{4}{q}}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\leq C\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}}\right\|^{\frac{2(q-2)}{q}}_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})},

where C is a positive constant independent of
u
.

Proof.

Let us denote
g(x):=A^{\frac{1}{q}}(x)u(x)|x|_{a}^{-s}
. By using Jensen inequality for the convex function
h(u)=\log\left(\frac{1}{u}\right)
, we have

(2.23)

\begin{split}\log\left(\frac{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}{\left\|g\right\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)&=h\left(\frac{\left\|g\right\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)=h\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{1}{|g(x)|}|g(x)|^{2}\left\|g\right\|^{-2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\,dx\right)\\
&\leq\int_{\mathbb{X}}h\left(\frac{1}{|g(x)|}\right)|g(x)|^{2}\left\|g\right\|^{-2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\,dx=\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\log|g(x)|\,dx.\end{split}

Then by the logarithmic Hardy-Sobolev inequality (
2.22
) for
p=2
, we get

(2.24)

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\log|g(x)|\,dx=\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\log\left(\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\right)\,dx\\
&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\log\left(\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)\,dx+\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{|g(x)|^{2}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\,dx\\
&\leq\frac{q}{2(q-2)}\log\left(C\frac{\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}}{\left\|g\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}}\right)+\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\\
&=\frac{q}{2(q-2)}\log\left(C\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\right)\\
&-\frac{q}{q-2}\|g\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}+\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}.\end{split}

By using (
2.23
) and (
2.24
), we have

(2.25)

\begin{split}&2\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}-\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})}-\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}+\frac{q}{q-2}\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\\
&\leq\frac{q}{2(q-2)}\log\left(C\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\right).\end{split}

Therefore, we obtain

\begin{split}&\left(1+\frac{q}{q-2}\right)\log\left\|g\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\\
&\leq\frac{q}{2(q-2)}\log\left(C\left\|g\right\|^{\frac{2(q-2)}{q}}_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\right),\end{split}

and hence

\left\|g\right\|^{4-\frac{4}{q}}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\leq\left(C\left\|g\right\|^{\frac{2(q-2)}{q}}_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\right).

We can write this in the following form

\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}}\right\|^{4-\frac{4}{q}}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{X})}\leq C\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{X}}\frac{\left|u(x)-u(y)\right|^{p}z(x)}{d^{sp}(x,y)\left|B\left(a,\frac{d(x,y)}{2}\right)\right|}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{2}}v(y)\,dy\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\left\|\frac{A^{\frac{1}{q}}u}{|\cdot|_{a}}\right\|^{\frac{2(q-2)}{q}}_{L^{1}(\mathbb{X})},

which completes the proof.
∎

3.
Applications

In this section, we show some consequences of the fractional Hardy and fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequalities on homogeneous Lie groups and on the Heisenberg group.

3.1.
Homogeneous groups.

A Lie group (on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
)
\mathbb{G}
is called homogeneous Lie group, if there is a dilation
D_{\lambda}(x)
such that

D_{\lambda}(x):=(\lambda^{\nu_{1}}x_{1},\dots,\lambda^{\nu_{n}}x_{n}),\,\,\nu_{1},\dots,\nu_{n}>0,\,\,D_{\lambda}:\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n},

and
D_{\lambda}
is an automorphism of the group
\mathbb{G}
for all
\lambda>0
. The homogeneous dimension of the homogeneous group
\mathbb{G}
is denoted by
Q:=\nu_{1}+\dots+\nu_{n}
. We also denote a homogeneous quasi-norm on
\mathbb{G}
by
|x|
, which is a continuous non-negative function

\mathbb{G}\ni x\mapsto|x|\in[0,\infty),

which has the following properties

I)

|x|=|x^{-1}|
for all
x\in\mathbb{G}
,

II)

|\lambda x|=\lambda|x|
, for all
x\in\mathbb{G}
, and
\lambda>0
,

III)

|x|=0
if and only if
x=0
.

Proposition 3.1

(see e.g.
[
15
]
,
[
14
]
, Proposition 3.1.39 and
[
32
]
, Proposition 1.2.4)
.

Let
\mathbb{G}
be a homogeneous group. Then there always exists a homogeneous quasi-norm
|\cdot|
on
\mathbb{G}
which satisfies the triangle inequality (with constant C = 1)

|xy|\leq|x|+|y|

for all
x,y\in\mathbb{G}
.

There is a (unique) positive Borel measure
\sigma
on the unit quasi-sphere
\mathfrak{S}:=\{x\in\mathbb{G}:|x|=1\}
, so that for every
f\in L^{1}(\mathbb{G})
, we have

(3.1)

\int_{\mathbb{G}}f(x)\,dx=\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathfrak{S}}f(ry)r^{Q-1}\,d\sigma dr.

Let us define the quasi-ball centered at
x
with radius
r
in the following form

B(x,r)=\{y\in\mathbb{G}:|x^{-1}y|<r\}.

Let
p>1
and
s\in(0,1)
. For a measurable function
u:\mathbb{G}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
, the Gagliardo seminorm is given by

[u]_{s,p}:=\left(\int_{\mathbb{G}}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|y^{-1}x|^{sp+Q}}\,dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

The fractional Sobolev space
W^{s,p}(\mathbb{G})
on the homogeneous Lie group
\mathbb{G}
is given by

W^{s,p}(\mathbb{G}):=\left\{u\in L^{p}(\mathbb{G}):[u]_{s,p}<\infty\right\},

endowed with the norm

\|u\|_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{G})}:=\|u\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{G})}+[u]_{s,p},\quad u\in W^{s,p}(\mathbb{G}).

Theorem 3.2

(Fractional Hardy inequality)
.

Let
\mathbb{G}
be a homogeneous Lie group of homogeneous dimension
Q
, and let
|\cdot|
be a homogeneous quasi-norm on
\mathbb{G}
. Suppose that
s>0
,
p>1
, and
Q<sp
. Then for all
u\in W^{s,p}(\mathbb{G})
, we have the following inequality

(3.2)

\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\,dx\leq\frac{2^{sp+Q}Q(sp+Qp-Q)^{p}}{|\mathfrak{S}|(sp-Q)^{p}}[u]_{s,p}^{p}.

Proof.

First, let us verify the condition (
2.4
) for
\mathbb{X}=\mathbb{G}
,
a=0
(identity element of
\mathbb{G}
), and
d(x,y)=|y^{-1}x|
with
A(x)=v(x,y)=1
. By noting that
|B(0,|x|)|=\frac{|\mathfrak{S}||x|^{Q}}{Q}
(cf. (
3.1
)), we obtain

\begin{split}\int_{\mathbb{G}\backslash B(0,|x|)}&|y|^{-sp}|B(0,|y|)|^{-p}\,dy\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{polar decomposition of homogenuous groups}}}{{=}}\frac{|\mathfrak{S}|^{-p}}{Q^{-p}}\int_{|x|}^{+\infty}\int_{\mathfrak{S}}r^{-sp-Qp+Q-1}\,d\sigma(y)dr\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle-sp-Qp+Q<0}}{{=}}\frac{|\mathfrak{S}|^{1-p}Q^{p}}{(sp+Qp-Q)}|x|^{-sp-Qp+Q},\end{split}

and

\int_{B(0,|x|)}|y|^{-sp(1-p^{\prime})}\,dy\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{polar decomposition of homogenuous groups}}}{{=}}\int_{0}^{|x|}\int_{\mathfrak{S}}r^{sp^{\prime}+Q-1}\,d\sigma(y)dr\stackrel{{\scriptstyle sp^{\prime}+Q>0}}{{=}}\frac{|\mathfrak{S}|}{sp^{\prime}+Q}|x|^{sp^{\prime}+Q}.

Therefore

\begin{split}D_{1}&=\sup_{x\neq 0}\left[\frac{Q|\mathfrak{S}|^{\frac{1-p}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}{(sp+Qp-Q)^{\frac{1}{p}}(sp^{\prime}+Q)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}|x|^{\frac{-sp-Qp+Q}{p}+\frac{sp^{\prime}+Q}{p^{\prime}}}\right]\\
&=\frac{Q}{(sp+Qp-Q)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(s\frac{p}{p-1}+Q\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}=\frac{Q(p-1)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}{sp+Qp-Q}<\infty.\end{split}

By using
Q<sp
, we have

(p^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1}=\frac{Qp}{sp+Qp-Q}<\frac{Qp}{Qp}=1.

By applying the inequality (
2.5
), and using
|B(0,|x|)|=\frac{|\mathfrak{S}||x|^{Q}}{Q}
, we get

\begin{split}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}}{|x|^{sp}}\,dx&\leq\frac{2^{sp}}{\left(1-\frac{Qp}{sp+Qp-Q}\right)^{p}}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|y^{-1}x|^{sp}|B(0,\frac{|y^{-1}x|}{2})|}\,dxdy\\
&=\frac{2^{sp+Q}Q(sp+Qp-Q)^{p}}{|\mathfrak{S}|(sp-Q)^{p}}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|y^{-1}x|^{sp+Q}}\,dxdy,\end{split}

completing the proof.
∎

Remark 3.3
.

In the Abelian (Euclidean) case
\mathbb{G}=(\mathbb{R}^{n},+)
,
Q=n
, we have the fractional Hardy inequality under the condition
n<sp
.

Theorem 3.4

(Fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequality)
.

Let
\mathbb{G}
be a homogeneous Lie group of homogeneous dimension
Q
, and let
|\cdot|
be a homogeneous quasi-norm on
\mathbb{G}
. Assume that
s>0
,
1<p,q<\infty
, and
Q<sp
. Then the following inequality

(3.3)

\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)|^{q}}{|x|^{sq}}\,dx\leq\frac{2^{sq+\frac{Qq}{p}}Q^{\frac{q}{p}}(sq+Qq-Q)^{q}}{|\mathfrak{S}|^{\frac{q}{p}}(sq-Q)^{q}}\int_{\mathbb{G}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{G}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|y^{-1}x|^{sp+Q}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}\,dy

holds for all
u\in W^{s,p}(\mathbb{G})
.

Proof.

The proof of this theorem follows the similar approach to the one used in proving Theorem
3.2
.
∎

Remark 3.5
.

The inequality (
3.3
), as a special case, implies the Abelian (Euclidean) case
\mathbb{G}=(\mathbb{R}^{n},+)
,
Q=n
, under the condition
n<sq
.

3.2.
Heisenberg group.

The Heisenberg group
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is defined as

\mathbb{H}^{n}:=\{\xi=(x,y,t):x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},t\in\mathbb{R}\}.

Composition law in this group is given by

\xi\circ\xi^{\prime}=\left(x+x^{\prime},y+y^{\prime},t+t^{\prime}+\frac{1}{2}(xy^{\prime}-x^{\prime}y)\right),

where
\xi^{\prime}=(x^{\prime},y^{\prime},t^{\prime})\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
, and
xy
denotes their standard Euclidean inner product. The inverse element in
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is
\xi^{-1}=-\xi
. The homogeneous dimension of the Heisenberg group is
Q=2n+2
.

For
\xi=(x,y,t)\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
, define the Koranyi-Folland distance

d(\xi,0):=((|x|^{2}+|y|^{2})^{2}+|t|^{2})^{\frac{1}{4}}.

For simplicity, we denote
d(\xi,0)=d(\xi)
.

Proposition 3.6

(
[
5
]
, Proposition 5.14.1)
.

Let
\mathbb{H}^{n}
be a Heisenberg group, and let
d(\cdot)
be the Koranyi-Folland distance. Then there exists a constant
\beta\geq 1
such that

d(\xi\circ\xi^{\prime})\leq\beta d(\xi)+d(\xi^{\prime}),\quad\xi,\xi^{\prime}\in\mathbb{H}^{n}.

Let us define the ball
B(\xi,r)
with center
\xi
and radius
r
as follows

B(\xi,r):=\{\xi^{\prime}\in\mathbb{H}^{n}:d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})<r\}.

Also,
\omega_{n}
denotes the Lebesgue measure of
B(0,1)
, i.e.
\omega_{n}:=|B(0,1)|
.

Proposition 3.7

(
[
5
]
, Proposition 5.4.4)
.

Let
\mathbb{H}^{n}
be a Heisenberg group of homogeneous dimension
Q
, and
d(\cdot)
be the Koranyi-Folland distance. Let
f
be a function defined on
B(0,r)
. If
f\in L^{1}(B(0,r))
, we have

(3.4)

\int_{B(0,r)}f(d(\xi))\,d\xi=Q\omega_{n}\int_{0}^{r}s^{Q-1}f(s)\,ds.

For a measurable function
u:\mathbb{H}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
, the Gagliardo seminorm is defined by

[u]_{s,p}:=\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}}{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})^{sp+Q}}\,d\xi^{\prime}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

For
p>1
, and
s\in(0,1)
, the fractional Sobolev space on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is

W^{s,p}(\mathbb{H}^{n}):=\left\{u\in L^{p}(\mathbb{H}^{n}):[u]_{s,p}<\infty\right\},

equipped with the norm

\|u\|_{W^{s,p}(\mathbb{H}^{n})}:=\|u\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{H}^{n})}+[u]_{s,p},\quad u\in W^{s,p}(\mathbb{H}^{n}).

Theorem 3.8

(Fractional Hardy inequality)
.

Let
\mathbb{H}^{n}
be a Heisenberg group of homogeneous dimension
Q
and let
d(\cdot)
be the Koranyi-Folland distance on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
. Suppose that
s>0
,
p>1
, and
Q<sp
. Then for all
u\in W^{s,p}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
, we have the following inequality

(3.5)

\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)|^{p}}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\,d\xi\leq\frac{(\beta+1)^{sp+Q}(sp+Qp-Q)^{p}}{\omega_{n}(sp-Q)^{p}}[u]_{s,p}^{p}.

Proof.

Similarly to the proof of Theorem
2.2
, by using the Minokwski inequality, we have

(3.6)

\begin{split}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)|^{p}}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\left|u(\xi)-\frac{1}{|B(0,d(\xi)|}\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right.\right.\\
&+\left.\left.\frac{1}{B(0,d(\xi))}\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\left|u(\xi)-\frac{1}{|B(0,d(\xi))|}\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&+\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}|B(0,d(\xi))|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}=I_{1}+I_{2},\end{split}

where

I_{1}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\left|u(\xi)-\frac{1}{|B(0,d(\xi))|}\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

and

I_{2}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}|B(0,d(\xi))|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

By using Proposition
3.6
together with the property
d(\xi^{-1})=d(\xi)
, we obtain

(3.7)

d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})\leq\beta d(\xi)+d(\xi^{\prime})\leq\beta d(\xi)+d(\xi)=(\beta+1)d(\xi),

for all
\xi^{\prime}\in B(0,d(\xi))
, where
\beta\geq 1
. Since
|B(0,r_{1})|\leq|B(0,r_{2})|
for
0<r_{1}\leq r_{2}
, we have

(3.8)

\left|B\left(0,\frac{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})}{\beta+1}\right)\right|\leq|B(0,d(\xi))|.

By Hölder’s inequality, we have

(3.9)

\left(\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}\left|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})\right|\,d\xi^{\prime}\right)^{p}\leq\left(\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}\,d\xi^{\prime}\right)\left|B(0,d(\xi))\right|^{p-1}.

Therefore, we obtain

\begin{split}I_{1}&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}|B(0,d(\xi))|^{p}}\left|u(\xi)|B(0,d(\xi))|-\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}u(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&=\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}|B(0,d(\xi))|^{p}}\left|\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}(u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime}))\,d\xi^{\prime}\right|^{p}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{HolderonHisenberg}}}{{\leq}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}|B(0,d(\xi))|}\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}\,d\xi^{\prime}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{trialngleineq},\eqref{measureofbollsonHeisenberg}}}{{\leq}}(\beta+1)^{s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}}{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})^{sp}|B(0,\frac{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})}{\beta+1})|}\,d\xi^{\prime}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq(\beta+1)^{s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}}{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})^{sp}|B(0,\frac{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})}{\beta+1})|}\,d\xi^{\prime}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.\end{split}

By noting that
|B(0,r)|=\omega_{n}\,r^{Q}
(cf. (
3.4
)), we get the following estimate

(3.10)

I_{1}\leq\frac{(\beta+1)^{s+\frac{Q}{p}}}{\omega_{n}^{\frac{1}{p}}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}}{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})^{sp+Q}}\,d\xi^{\prime}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Now, let us check the condition (
2.2
) for the functions
g(\xi)=\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}|B(0,d(\xi))|^{p}}
and
h(\xi)=\frac{1}{d(\xi)^{sp}}
. We have

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}\backslash B(0,d(\xi))}d(\xi^{\prime})^{-sp}|B(0,d(\xi^{\prime}))|^{-p}d\xi^{\prime}=\omega_{n}^{-p}\int_{d(\xi^{\prime})\geq d(\xi)}d(\xi^{\prime})^{-sp-Qp}\,d\xi^{\prime}\\
&=\omega_{n}^{-p}\sum_{k=1}^{+\infty}\int_{2^{k-1}d(\xi)\leq d(\xi^{\prime})\leq 2^{k}d(\xi)}d(\xi^{\prime})^{-sp-Qp}\,d\xi^{\prime}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{polardeconHeisenberg}}}{{=}}Q\omega_{n}^{1-p}\sum_{k=1}^{+\infty}\int_{2^{k-1}d(\xi)}^{2^{k}d(\xi)}r^{-sp-Qp+Q-1}\,dr\\
&=\frac{Q\omega_{n}^{1-p}}{-sp-Qp+Q}\left(1-\frac{1}{2^{-sp-Qp+Q}}\right)d(\xi)^{-sp-Qp+Q}\sum_{k=1}^{+\infty}2^{k(-sp-Qp+Q)}\\
&=\frac{Q\omega_{n}^{1-p}}{sp+Qp-Q}d(\xi)^{-sp-Qp+Q},\end{split}

\int_{B(0,d(\xi))}d(\xi^{\prime})^{-sp(1-p^{\prime})}\,d\xi^{\prime}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{polardeconHeisenberg}}}{{=}}Q\omega_{n}\int_{0}^{d(\xi)}r^{sp^{\prime}+Q-1}\,dr=\frac{Q\omega_{n}d(\xi)^{sp^{\prime}+Q}}{sp^{\prime}+Q}.

Then, we can verify that
D_{1}=\frac{Q(p-1)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}}{sp+Qp-Q}<\infty
, and
(p^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}p^{\frac{1}{p}}D_{1}=\frac{Qp}{sp+Qp-Q}<1
. By applying the integral Hardy inequality (
2.1
), we have

I_{2}\leq\frac{Qp}{sp+Qp-Q}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)|^{p}}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

By putting (
3.10
) and (
3.8
) to (
3.6
), we obtain

\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)|^{p}}{d(\xi)^{sp}}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\leq\frac{(\beta+1)^{s+\frac{Q}{p}}(sp+Qp-Q)}{\omega_{n}^{\frac{1}{p}}(sp-Q)}\left(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\xi^{\prime})|^{p}}{d(\xi^{-1}\circ\xi^{\prime})^{sp+Q}}\,d\xi^{\prime}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

which completes the proof.
∎

Theorem 3.10

(Fractional Hardy-Sobolev type inequality)
.

Let

be a Heisenberg group of homogeneous dimension

Proof.

2.3

2.2
