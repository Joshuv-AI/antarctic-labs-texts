Probing dark energy using anisotropies in the clustering of post-EoR H i distribution
Chandrachud B.V. Dash, Tapomoy Guha Sarkar

Abstract
We propose an anisotropy quantifier of the H i 21-cm signal traditionally used to clock the astrophysics of the reionization era as a post-reionization dark energy diagnostic. We find that the anisotropy probe can be measured at SNR ∼ 10 \sim 10 in both auto-correlation and in cross-correlation with the Ly- α \alpha forest over a wide z z and k − k- range. We propose to use the BAO signature on the anisotropy signal to measure ( H ​ ( z ) , D A ​ ( z ) ) (H(z),D_{A}(z)) . Subsequently, we put constraints on a dark energy model involving a negative cosmological constant on top of a quintessence scalar field and find that such a model is consistent with futuristic observations.
