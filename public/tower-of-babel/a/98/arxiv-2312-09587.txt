Wave Propagation in Pure-Time Modulated Step Media With Applications to Temporal-Aiming
Mourad Sini, Haibing Wang, Qingyun Yao

Abstract
We analyze the propagation of an incident electromagnetic wave in a purely-time modulated medium. Precisely, we assume that the permeability is unchanged while the permittivity has a multiple-step profile in time and uniformly constant in space. For this, we use time-dispersive Lorenz’s model with time-dependent plasma frequency with highly concentrated values near the centers of the steps’ intervals. Under certain regimes linking the number of steps to the contrasts of the permittivity, we can generate effective permittivity having positive and high values on a finite interval of time which behaves as a ’wall’ that kills the forward waves and keeps only the backward waves (i.e. enabling full reflection). But this happens if these high values are away from a discrete set. If these high values are close to the elements of this discrete set, then the effective medium behaves as a ’well’ that absorbs all the forward waves and hence there is no backward waves (i.e. enabling full transmission). Such results are reminiscent to the ’wall’ and ’well’ effects known in the quantum mechanics theory . Keywords: Time-Dependent Media, Electromagnetism, Temporal Aiming, Effective Media, Wall and Well Effects. MSC(2010): 35L05, 35C20.

\Phi(t,\,T_{j})=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\frac{\partial_{t}^{l}\Phi(T_{m},\,T_{j})}{l!}(t-T_{m})^{l}=\Phi(T_{m},\,T_{j})\left(1+\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{(i\kappa)^{l}}{l!}|t-T_{m}|^{l}\right),

we have

\displaystyle\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(t)dt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)dt+\frac{iC\delta^{1-h}}{2\kappa}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{j})\int^{T_{j}+\delta/2}_{T_{j}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(s)ds+O\left(\delta^{3-h}N\right)

(2.41)

\displaystyle+\frac{iC\delta^{-h}}{2\kappa}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{j})\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{(i\kappa)^{l}}{l!}|t-T_{m}|^{l}dt\int^{T_{j}+\delta/2}_{T_{j}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(s)ds.

With

\bigg|\int^{T_{j}+\delta/2}_{T_{j}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(s)ds\bigg|\leq\delta^{\frac{1}{2}}||\widetilde{E}||_{L^{2}(T_{j}-\delta/2,T_{j}+\delta/2)},

and

\displaystyle\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\Big|\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{(i\kappa)^{l}}{l!}|t-T_{m}|^{l}\Big|dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{l!}\left(\frac{\kappa\delta}{2}\right)^{l}\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}dt

\displaystyle(2l!\geq 2^{l})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2\delta\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\kappa\delta}{4}\right)^{l}=O(\delta^{2}),\quad\mbox{if}\ \delta<\frac{1}{\kappa},

(
2.41
) for
m=1,\dots,N
can be simplified to

\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(t)dt=\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)dt+\frac{iC\delta^{1-h}}{2\kappa}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{j})\int^{T_{j}+\delta/2}_{T_{j}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(s)ds+O\left(\delta^{3-h}N\right).

(2.42)

Let

\widetilde{q}_{m}:=\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(t)dt,\quad\widetilde{d}_{m}:=\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)dt.

Based on the invertibility of
\bar{A}
, we have

\widetilde{q}=\bar{A}^{-1}\widetilde{d}+O\left(\delta^{3-h}N\right)\bar{A}^{-1}\widetilde{e},

(2.43)

with
\widetilde{q}:=(\widetilde{q}_{1},\,\widetilde{q}_{2},\,\dots,\,\widetilde{q}_{N})^{T}
,
\widetilde{d}:=(\widetilde{d}_{1},\,\widetilde{d}_{2},\,\dots,\,\widetilde{d}_{N})^{T}
and
\widetilde{e}:=(1,\,1,\,\dots,\,1)^{T}
.
For (
1.8
),
t\in\mathbb{R}
, with (
2.39
), we get

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}(t)-\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C\delta^{-h}\sum_{m=1}^{N}\Phi(t,\,T_{m})\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(t)dt+C\delta^{-h}\sum_{m=1}^{N}\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}(\Phi(t,\,s)-\Phi(t,\,T_{m}))\widetilde{E}(s)ds

(2.44)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C\delta^{-h}\sum_{m=1}^{N}\Phi(t,\,T_{m})\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}(t)dt+O\left(\delta^{2-h}N\right).

Denote
\hat{F}(t):=(\widetilde{\Phi}(t,\,T_{1}),\,\widetilde{\Phi}(t,\,T_{2}),\,\dots,\,\widetilde{\Phi}(t,\,T_{N}))
with
||\hat{F}||_{2}=(\sum_{n=1}^{N}|\widetilde{\Phi}^{2}(t,\,T_{n})|)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq N^{\frac{1}{2}}
.
Coupling (
2.43
) and (
2.44
), according to
||\bar{A}^{-1}||_{2}=O(1)
and
||\widetilde{e}||_{2}=N^{\frac{1}{2}}
, we have

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)+C\delta^{-h}\hat{F}\widetilde{q}+O\left(\delta^{2-h}N\right)

(2.45)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)+C\delta^{-h}\hat{F}\left(\bar{A}^{-1}\widetilde{d}+O\left(\delta^{3-h}N\right)\bar{A}^{-1}\widetilde{e}\right)+O\left(\delta^{2-h}N\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)+C\delta^{-h}\hat{F}\bar{A}^{-1}\widetilde{d}+O\left(\delta^{3-2h}N||\hat{F}||_{2}\,||\bar{A}^{-1}||_{2}\,||\widetilde{e}||_{2}\right)+O\left(\delta^{2-h}N\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)+C\delta^{-h}\hat{F}\bar{A}^{-1}\widetilde{d}+O\left(\delta^{3-2h}N^{2}\right)+O(\delta^{2-h}N).

In addition, we have
\widetilde{d}=\delta E^{in}+O(\kappa\delta^{2})\widetilde{e}
, where
E^{in}:=(e^{i\kappa T_{1}},\,e^{i\kappa T_{2}}\,\dots,\,e^{i\kappa T_{N}})^{T}
, since

\widetilde{d}_{m}=\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)dt=\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}e^{i\kappa T_{m}}dt+e^{i\kappa T_{m}}\int^{T_{m}+\delta/2}_{T_{m}-\delta/2}\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{(i\kappa)^{l}}{l!}(t-T_{m})^{l}dt=\delta e^{i\kappa T_{m}}+O(\delta^{2}).

We get the simple form that

\widetilde{E}(t)=\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)+C\delta^{1-h}\hat{F}\bar{A}^{-1}E^{in}+O\left(\delta^{2-l-h}\right)+O\left(\delta^{3-2l-2h}\right).

The proof is complete.

3
The effective field

In this section, we prove Theorem
1.3
. Consider the integral equation

\widetilde{E}_{eff}(t)-\widetilde{E}_{inc}(t)=\int^{T}_{0}C\delta^{-\alpha}\Phi(t,\,s)\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s)ds,\quad t\in(-\infty,\,\infty),

(3.46)

where
\widetilde{E}_{eff}
is the effective field for
\widetilde{E}
in
\mathbb{R}
and
\alpha=-1+h+l
.
The differential equation with the operator
(\partial_{t}^{2}+\kappa^{2})
corresponding to the integral equation (
3.46
) is

\partial_{t}^{2}\widetilde{E}_{eff}(t)+\kappa^{2}\widetilde{E}_{eff}(t)=-\left[C\delta^{-\alpha}\right]\chi_{[0,\,T]}\widetilde{E}_{eff}(t),\quad t\in(-\infty,\,\infty).

(3.47)

Let
\lambda:=\sqrt{C\delta^{-\alpha}+\kappa^{2}}
with

\lambda=\begin{cases}O(1),\quad&\mbox{if}\ 1-h-l\geq 0,\\
O(\delta^{-\frac{\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if}\ 1-h-l<0.\end{cases}\\

The general solution of the equation (
3.47
) is

\widetilde{E}_{eff}(t)=\begin{cases}C_{1}\,e^{i\kappa t}+C_{2}\,e^{-i\kappa t},\quad&t\in(-\infty,\,0),\\
C_{3}\,e^{i\lambda t}+C_{4}\,e^{-i\lambda t},\quad&t\in[0,\,T],\\
C_{5}\,e^{i\kappa t}+C_{6}\,e^{-i\kappa t},\quad&t\in(T,\,\infty).\end{cases}

(3.48)

Setting
C_{1}=1
and
C_{6}=0
to satisfy the long-time condition (
1.7
), the differential equation (
3.47
) with the transmission conditions

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=0^{-}}=\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=0^{+}},

\displaystyle\partial_{t}\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=0^{-}}=\partial_{t}\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=0^{+}},

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=T^{-}}=\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=T^{+}},

\displaystyle\partial_{t}\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=T^{-}}=\partial_{t}\widetilde{E}_{eff}|_{t=T^{+}},

has the particular solutions with the coefficients and

\displaystyle C_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{(\lambda^{2}-\kappa^{2})(e^{i\lambda T}-e^{-i\lambda T})}{(\lambda+\kappa)^{2}e^{-i\lambda T}-(\lambda-\kappa)^{2}e^{i\lambda T}}=-1+\frac{4\lambda\kappa-i4\kappa^{2}\tan(\lambda T)}{4\lambda\kappa-i2(\lambda^{2}+\kappa^{2})\tan(\lambda T)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}0+o(1),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$,}\\
O(1),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
-1+O(\delta^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\end{cases}

\displaystyle C_{3}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\kappa(\kappa+\lambda)e^{-i\lambda T}}{(\lambda+\kappa)^{2}e^{-i\lambda T}-(\lambda-\kappa)^{2}e^{i\lambda T}}=\frac{2\kappa^{2}+2\kappa\lambda-i2\kappa(\kappa+\lambda)\tan(\lambda T)}{4\lambda\kappa-i2(\lambda^{2}+\kappa^{2})\tan(\lambda T)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2\kappa^{2}+i(\lambda^{2}-\kappa^{2}-2\kappa\lambda)\tan(\lambda T)}{4\lambda\kappa-i2(\lambda^{2}+\kappa^{2})\tan(\lambda T)}=\begin{cases}1+o(1),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
\frac{1}{2}+O(\delta^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\
O(1),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\end{cases}

\displaystyle C_{4}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\kappa(\lambda-\kappa)e^{i\lambda T}}{(\lambda+\kappa)^{2}e^{-i\lambda T}-(\lambda-\kappa)^{2}e^{i\lambda T}}=\frac{-2\kappa^{2}+2\kappa\lambda+i2\kappa(\lambda-\kappa)\tan(\lambda T)}{4\lambda\kappa-i2(\lambda^{2}+\kappa^{2})\tan(\lambda T)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}+\frac{-2\kappa^{2}+i(\lambda^{2}-\kappa^{2}+2\kappa\lambda)\tan(\lambda T)}{4\lambda\kappa-i2(\lambda^{2}+\kappa^{2})\tan(\lambda T)}=\begin{cases}0+o(1),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
\frac{1}{2}+O(\delta^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\
O(1),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\end{cases}

\displaystyle C_{5}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{4\lambda\kappa e^{-i\kappa T}}{(\lambda+\kappa)^{2}e^{-i\lambda T}-(\lambda-\kappa)^{2}e^{i\lambda T}}=\frac{4\lambda\kappa e^{-i\kappa T}}{4\lambda\kappa\cos(\lambda T)+i2(\lambda^{2}+\kappa^{2})\sin(\lambda T)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\pm e^{-i\kappa T}+o(1),\ \mbox{($\pm$ for even or odd $n$)},\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$,}\\
O(1)e^{-i\kappa T},\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{\alpha}{2}})e^{-i\kappa T},\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\end{cases}

where
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
n\gg 1
.

Theorem 3.1

We have the effective field
\widetilde{E}_{eff}
with the estimate

\widetilde{E}(t)-\widetilde{E}_{eff}(t)=\begin{cases}O(\delta^{\frac{1-h+2l}{2}})+O(\delta^{1-h}),\quad&\mbox{if $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
O(\delta^{2-2h-l}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$, $1-h-l<0$ and $l>2\alpha$},\\

Proof.

For
m=1,\,\dots\,N
, the integral equation (
3.46
) at the point
t=T_{m}
can be rewritten as

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{m})-\frac{iC\delta^{-\alpha}}{2\kappa}\int_{0}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{j})\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{j})x_{j}ds

\displaystyle=\widetilde{E}_{inc}(T_{m})+\sum_{j=0}^{N}\int^{T_{j+1}}_{T_{j}}\frac{iC\delta^{-\alpha}}{2\kappa}\left[\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s)\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s)-\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{j})\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{j})\right]ds

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\quad\;+\frac{iC\delta^{-\alpha}}{2\kappa}\int^{T_{1}}_{T_{0}}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{0})\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{0})ds.

(3.49)

Denote
f_{j}(T_{m},\,s):=\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s)\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s)
for

.
With the Taylor series, we have

f_{j}(T_{m},\,s)-f_{j}(T_{m},\,T_{j})=(s-T_{j})\bar{R}_{j}(T_{m},\,s),

where

\displaystyle\bar{R}_{j}(T_{m},s)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\partial_{s}f(T_{m},\,s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\,d\vartheta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\left[\partial_{s}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\right]\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\,d\vartheta

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{1}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\left[\partial_{s}\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\right]d\vartheta,

and

\displaystyle|\bar{R}_{j}(T_{m},s)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Bigg|\int_{0}^{1}i\kappa(\vartheta-1)\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\,d\vartheta\Bigg|

\displaystyle+\Bigg|\int_{0}^{1}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\left[\partial_{s}\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s-\vartheta(s-T_{j}))\right]d\vartheta\Bigg|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\kappa\left(\int_{0}^{1}|\vartheta-1|^{2}d\vartheta\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}||\widetilde{E}_{eff}||_{L^{2}[T_{j},\,T_{j+1}]}+||\partial_{t}\widetilde{E}_{eff}||_{L^{2}[T_{j},\,T_{j+1}]}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}O(\delta^{\frac{l+\alpha}{2}})+O(\delta^{\frac{l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{l}{2}})+O(\delta^{\frac{l-\alpha}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{otherwise.}\end{cases}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}O(\delta^{\frac{1-h}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{otherwise.}\end{cases}

Then, the estimates of the integrals in the right of (
3.49
) are

\displaystyle|I_{A}|

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\Bigg|\sum_{j=0}^{N}\int^{T_{j+1}}_{T_{j}}\frac{iC\delta^{-\alpha}}{2\kappa}\left[\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,s)\widetilde{E}_{eff}(s)-\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{j})\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{j})\right]ds\Bigg|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}O(\delta^{\frac{2-2h+l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\
O(\delta^{\frac{1-h+2l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{otherwise,}\end{cases}

and

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\Bigg|\frac{iC\delta^{-\alpha}}{2\kappa}\int^{T_{1}}_{T_{0}}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{0})\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{0})ds\Bigg|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle O(\delta^{l-\alpha}(C_{3}+C_{4}))=\begin{cases}O(\delta^{\frac{1-h+l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$ and $1-h-l<0$,}\\

) can be rewritten as

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle+\frac{iC\delta^{-\alpha}}{2\kappa}\int^{T_{1}}_{T_{0}}\widetilde{\Phi}(T_{m},\,T_{0})\widetilde{E}_{eff}(T_{0})ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}O(\delta^{\frac{1-h+2l}{2}})+O(\delta^{1-h}),\quad&\mbox{if $1-h-l\geq 0$,}\\
O(\delta^{2-2h-l}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is close to $n\pi$, $1-h-l<0$ and $l>2\alpha$},\\

,

O(\delta^{\frac{3-3h-l}{2}}),\quad&\mbox{if $\lambda T$ is away from $n\pi$, $1-h<l<3(1-h)$}.\end{cases}

The proof is complete.
