K-stable smooth Fano threefolds of Picard rank two Thanks: Throughout this paper, all varieties are assumed to be projective and defined over ℂ \mathbb{C} .
Ivan Cheltsov, Elena Denisova, Kento Fujita

Abstract
. We prove that all smooth Fano threefolds in the families №2.1, №2.2, №2.3, №2.4, №2.6 and №2.7 are K-stable, and we also prove that smooth Fano threefolds in the family №2.5 that satisfy one very explicit generality condition are K-stable.

The valuative criterion for K-stability

To do this, let us remind what are
\beta

Recall that

We set

(respecitvely,

We say that

The simplest way to apply the valuative criterion is to check whether
