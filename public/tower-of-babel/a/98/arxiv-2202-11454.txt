Abstract
In this paper, we introduce ℤ p r ​ ℤ p r ​ ℤ p s \mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}} -additive cyclic codes for r ≤ s r\leq s . These codes can be identified as ℤ p s ​ [ x ] \mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}[x] -submodules of ℤ p r ​ [ x ] / ⟨ x α − 1 ⟩ × ℤ p r ​ [ x ] / ⟨ x β − 1 ⟩ × ℤ p s ​ [ x ] / ⟨ x γ − 1 ⟩ \mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}[x]/\langle x^{\alpha}-1\rangle\times\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}[x]/\langle x^{\beta}-1\rangle\times\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}[x]/\langle x^{\gamma}-1\rangle . We determine the generator polynomials and minimal generating sets for this family of codes. Some previous works has been done for the case p = 2 p=2 with r = s = 1 r=s=1 , r = s = 2 r=s=2 , and r = 1 , s = 2 r=1,s=2 . However, we show that in these previous works the classification of these codes were incomplete and the statements in this paper complete such classification. We also discuss the structure of separable ℤ p r ​ ℤ p r ​ ℤ p s \mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}} -additive cyclic codes and determine their generator polynomials. Further, we also study the duality of ℤ p s ​ [ x ] \mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}[x] -submodules. As applications, we present some examples and construct some optimal binary codes.

\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}

\displaystyle\phi

\displaystyle:\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}\longrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}

\displaystyle u\longmapsto u\penalty\ \mbox{mod}\penalty\ p^{r}.

\displaystyle:\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}

Lemma 4.15

6

In this section, we study the duality of
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic codes. We discuss some results that determine the relationship between
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic codes and their duals.

Let
C
be a
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic code and
C^{\perp}
be its dual. Taking any codeword
\mathbf{u}
of
C^{\perp}
,
\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{u^{\prime}}=0
for all
\mathbf{u^{\prime}}\in C
. Since
\mathbf{u^{\prime}}
is a codeword of
C
, we know that
\mathbf{u^{\prime}}^{(-1)}
is also a codeword. So,
\mathbf{u^{\prime}}^{(-1)}\cdot\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{u^{\prime}}\cdot\mathbf{u}^{(1)}=0
for all
\mathbf{u}\in C
. Therefore,
\mathbf{u}^{(1)}\in C^{\perp}
, hence
C^{\perp}
is also a
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic code. So we obtain the following result.

Proposition 6.1
.

Let
C
be a
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic code. Then its dual
C^{\perp}
is also
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic code.

Proposition 6.2
.

Let

be a
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic code of block length
(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)
and

has the generator polynomials given in Theorem
4.9
. Then,

|C^{\perp}|=p^{\sum_{i=1}^{r}i\deg(\hat{a}_{i}(x))+\sum_{j=1}^{r}j\deg(\hat{b}_{j}(x))+\sum_{k=1}^{s}k\deg(\hat{g}_{k}(x))}.

Proof.

It is known that
|\mathbb{Z}^{\alpha}_{p^{r}}\times\mathbb{Z}^{\beta}_{p^{r}}\times\mathbb{Z}^{\gamma}_{p^{s}}|=|C||C^{\perp}|=p^{r\alpha+r\beta+s\gamma}
and suppose that

for some positive integer

, we have
|C|=p^{\sum_{i=0}^{r-1}(r-i)\mbox{deg}(\hat{a}_{i}(x))+\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}(r-j)\mbox{deg}(\hat{b}_{j}(x))+\sum_{k=0}^{s-1}(s-k)\mbox{deg}(\hat{g}_{k}(x))}

Proposition 6.3

Let

Conclusion

4.11

,
28

29

\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic codes and determine their generator polynomials also. In Theorem
4.19
, we classify some cases of generator polynomials of this family of codes. After that, We determine the minimal generating sets of
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
-additive cyclic codes and their size. In the last section, we discuss the duality of
\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{r}}\mathbb{Z}_{p^{s}}
