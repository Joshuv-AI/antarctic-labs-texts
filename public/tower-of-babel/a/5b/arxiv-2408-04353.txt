Exotic Kondo effect in two one dimensional spin 1/2 chains coupled to two localized spin 1/2 magnets
Igor Kuzmenko 1,2 , Tetyana Kuzmenko 1 , Y. B. Band 1,2,3 and Yshai Avishai 1,4

Abstract
We study an exotic Kondo effect in a system consisting of two one-dimensional XX Heisenberg ferromagnetic spin 1 / 2 1/2 chains (denoted by α = u , d \alpha=u,d for up and down chains) coupled to a quantum dot consisting of two localized spin 1 / 2 1/2 magnets. Using the Jordan-Wigner transformation on the Heisenberg Hamiltonian of the two chains, this system can be expressed in terms of non-interacting spinless fermionic quasiparticles. As a result, the Hamiltonian of the whole system is expressed as an Anderson model for spin 1/2 fermions interacting with a spin-1/2 impurity. Thus, we study the scattering of fermionic quasiparticles (propagating along spin chains) by a pair of localized magnetic impurities. At low temperature, the localized spin 1 / 2 1/2 magnets are shielded by the chain ‘spins’ via the Kondo effect. We calculate the Kondo temperature T K T_{K} and derive the temperature dependence of the entropy, the specific heat, the specific heat and the ‘magnetic susceptibility’ of the dot for T ≫ T K T\gg T_{K} . Our results can be generalized to the case of anti-ferromagnetic XX chains.

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle U_{u}(N+1),

(9)

\displaystyle\mathcal{U}_{u}

\displaystyle\mathcal{U}_{d}\,\exp\big(i\pi\gamma_{d}^{{\dagger}}\,\gamma_{d}\big),

\displaystyle s_{\alpha}^{\pm}(n)

The Hamiltonian

(16)

The energy band
\epsilon_{k}
is an even function of

\epsilon_{-k}=\epsilon_{k}

\frac{\Theta(|v|-|\epsilon|)}{\pi\,\sqrt{v^{2}-\epsilon^{2}}}

IV
Schrieffer-Wolff transformation

The Hamiltonian
H
given in Eqs. (
1
), (
15
), (
19
) and (
21
) is, in fact, an Anderson impurity model Hamiltonian, which is similar to a model used to describe magnetic impurities embedded in metals
[
12
]
. When the temperature
k_{B}T
is much smaller than the quantum dot excitation energy
w
, we can apply the Schrieffer-Wolff transformation to project out the high energy excitations
|0\rangle
and
|s\rangle
of the Anderson impurity model Hamiltonian and obtain an effective low energy model Hamiltonian
[
13
]
:

H=H_{0}+H_{K}.

(22)

Here
H_{0}
is given in Eq. (
15
), and
H_{K}
has the form of an
s
-
d
exchange interaction Hamiltonian used in
the Kondo model
[
9
]
,

H_{K}=J\,\mathbf{S}\cdot\bm{\Sigma},

(23)

where
\mathbf{S}
is the local pseudo-spin density operator of the chains,
\bm{\Sigma}
is a pseudo-spin operator of the dot, and
J
is the exchange coupling:

\displaystyle\mathbf{S}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2(2N+1)}\sum_{k,k^{\prime},\alpha,\alpha^{\prime}}c_{k,\alpha}^{{\dagger}}\bm{\tau}_{\alpha,\alpha^{\prime}}c_{k^{\prime},\alpha^{\prime}},

(24)

\displaystyle\bm{\Sigma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\alpha,\alpha^{\prime}}\gamma_{\alpha}^{{\dagger}}\bm{\tau}_{\alpha,\alpha^{\prime}}\gamma_{\alpha^{\prime}},

(25)

\displaystyle J

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{4t^{2}}{w}.

(26)

Here
\bm{\tau}=(\tau_{x},\tau_{y},\tau_{z})
, which appears in both
\mathbf{S}
and
\bm{\Sigma}
, is a vector of Pauli matrices. Note that the operators

\mathbf{S}
and
\bm{\Sigma}
are not ‘real’ spin operators, that is, they do not represent an intrinsic angular momentum carried by particles. In this context, fermion quasiparticles with
\alpha=d
are designated as ‘pseudo-spin-down’ quasiparticles, and
\alpha=u
as ‘pseudo-spin-up’ quasiparticles. The operators
\mathbf{S}
and
\bm{\Sigma}
permit ‘pseudo-spin-flipping’ transitions, and satisfy the commutation relations of spin operators, therefore they can be considered as pseudo-spin 1/2 operators.

\displaystyle\frac{dj(D)}{d\ln D}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-j^{2}(D),

\displaystyle j(D_{0})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{4t^{2}}{\pi vw}.

(28)

Equation (

where the Kondo temperature

VIII
Conclusions

We have demonstrated that the XX Heisenberg Hamiltonian of two one-dimensional spin 1/2 chains coupled to a quantum dot composed of two localized spin 1/2 magnets can be expressed as an Anderson model Hamiltonian for spin 1/2 fermions interacting with a spin 1/2 impurity. In this context, the chain index
\alpha
plays a role of the fermion spin, where
\alpha=u,d
stands for ‘spin-up’ and ‘spin-down’ states, respectively. At low temperatures, the impurity pseuodospin is screened by the chain pseudo-spins via the Kondo effect. The Kondo temperature
