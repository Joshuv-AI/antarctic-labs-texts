Consider the class of C ∗ {\rm C^{*}} -algebras which are tracially 𝒵 \mathcal{Z} -absorbing (in the sense of Amint, Golestani, Jamali, Phillips’s simple tracially 𝒵 \mathcal{Z} -absorbing or Castillejos, Li, Szabvo’s tracial 𝒵 \mathcal{Z} -stability).
