Special cubic zeros and the dual variety
Victor Y. Wang

Abstract
. Let F F be a diagonal cubic form over ℤ \mathbb{Z} in 6 6 variables. From the dual variety in the delta method of Duke–Friedlander–Iwaniec and Heath-Brown, we unconditionally extract a weighted count of certain special integral zeros of F F in regions of diameter X → ∞ X\to\infty . Heath-Brown did the same in 4 4 variables, but our analysis differs and captures some novel features. We also put forth an axiomatic framework for more general F F .

n_{3}\in\mathcal{N}_{\bm{c}}

Upon writing

\sum_{N\leq n<2N}\lvert S^{\natural}_{\bm{c},0}(n)\rvert\ll_{\epsilon}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}N_{1},N_{2},N_{3}\mid 2N:\\
N/4<N_{1}N_{2}N_{3}<2N\end{subarray}}N^{\epsilon}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1},n_{2}\geq 1:\\
N_{1}\leq n_{1}<2N_{1},\\
N_{2}\leq n_{2}<2N_{2},\;n_{2}\in\mathcal{N}_{\geq}(2)\end{subarray}}n_{2}^{1/2}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{3},d\geq 1:\\

d\in\mathcal{N}_{\geq}(3),\;d\mid n_{3}\end{subarray}}(n_{3}/d)^{1/2}\lvert S^{\natural}_{\bm{c}}(d)\rvert.

Upon summing over
n_{1}
,
n_{2}
,
n_{3}
(for each fixed

\sum_{N\leq n<2N}\lvert S^{\natural}_{\bm{c},0}(n)\rvert\ll_{\epsilon}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}N_{1},N_{2},N_{3}\mid 2N:\\
N_{1}N_{2}N_{3}<2N\end{subarray}}N^{\epsilon}N_{1}N_{2}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d<2N_{3}:\\
d\in\mathcal{N}_{\bm{c}}\cap\mathcal{N}_{\geq}(3)\end{subarray}}(N_{3}\lVert\bm{c}\rVert)^{\epsilon}(N_{3}/d)^{1/2}\lvert S^{\natural}_{\bm{c}}(d)\rvert.

But

for

.

.

∎

Given

),

Definition 8.7
.

For a real number

Similarly define

(by replacing

Using (
8.11
), one may rewrite the left-hand side of (

Lemma 8.8
.

Uniformly over reals

(8.13)

\Sigma_{<Y/P}(X,\Lambda^{\perp}\setminus\mathcal{E}_{2})\ll_{\epsilon}X^{m/2+\epsilon}P^{-1/2}.

We proceed somewhat crudely.
By Proposition
5.1
,
the absolute value of the left-hand side of (

(8.14)

Y^{-2}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{c}\in\Lambda^{\perp}\setminus\mathcal{E}_{2}:\\
\lVert\bm{c}\rVert\leq X^{1/2+\epsilon}\end{subarray}}\,\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{0}n_{1}<M_{2}Y:\\
n_{1}<Y/P\end{subarray}}(n_{0}n_{1})^{(1-m)/2}\lvert I_{\bm{c}}(n_{0}n_{1})\rvert\cdot n_{1}^{1/2}\lvert S^{\natural}_{\bm{c},0}(n_{0})\rvert.

We now examine an individual
\bm{c}\in\Lambda^{\perp}\setminus\mathcal{E}_{2}
.
By Observation
3.10
(1),

So

(8.15)

[

]
*Lemma 3.2.
Upon inserting (

n_{0}
, and then applying Lemma

, we find that the quantity (

\ll_{\epsilon}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\bm{c}\in\Lambda^{\perp}\setminus\mathcal{E}_{2}:\\

But (

(when

Plugging this in, and noting that

\ll_{\epsilon}\sum_{\bm{c}\in\Lambda^{\perp}\setminus\mathcal{E}_{2}:\,\lVert\bm{c}\rVert\leq X^{1/2+\epsilon}}\,\sum_{n_{1}<Y/P}Y^{-2}X^{1+m/2+\epsilon}\lVert\bm{c}\rVert\cdot(M_{2}Y/n_{1})^{1/2}\prod_{k\in\mathcal{K}}\frac{\operatorname{sq}(c_{\min\mathcal{J}(k)})^{1/2}}{\lvert c_{\min\mathcal{J}(k)}\rvert},

in the notation of Definition
