Abstract




1
Prognostics and Health Management

In the realm of modern manufacturing, significant emphasis is placed on improving the reliability, performance, and service life of complex
ES
, which has catalyzed the emergence of
PHM
(
PHM
) as an essential discipline in the past two decades
[
1
,
2
]
.
PHM
includes a range of methods dedicated to the early detection, prognosis, and mitigation of impending faults or failures in
ES
, thereby facilitating sophisticated maintenance strategies and enhancing operational efficiency
[
3
]
.

Central to the efficacy of
PHM
methods is the acquisition and analysis of degradation data, which encapsulates the evolving health condition of
ES
over time
[
3
]
. Degradation data serves as a rich source of information, offering invaluable insights into the underlying degradation processes, failure modes, and performance trends of
ES

[
2
,
4
]
. Through the integration of advanced sensing technologies, data analytics, and machine learning algorithms, researchers and practitioners can harness degradation data to develop prognostic models, optimize maintenance schedules, and make informed operational decisions
[
1
]
.

In the remainder of this paper, the tasks of diagnostics and prognostics are listed, and an overview from
[
5
]
of publicly available degradation data sets is presented. The authors aim to update the overview regularly in the future, as new data sets are continually being published in the dynamic research field of
PHM
.

2
Tasks of Diagnostics and Prognostics

Extensive and representative degradation data enables addressing a specific task—prognosis—within
PHM
, with the subdivision of
PHM
into distinct tasks. Based on
[
1
]
,
[
6
]
, and
[
4
]
, the tasks of diagnostics and prognostics within
PHM
can be subdivided as follows:

•

Fault detection/anomaly detection
: Detect a fault state or anomaly in an
ES
without considering its root cause. This results in a binary classification problem with the states fault or no fault.

•

Diagnosis
: Assign one or more causes to a detected fault state.

•

Health assessment
: Assess the state of degradation or the current risk of failure of an
ES
based on its current condition.

•

Prognosis
: Prediction of the future degradation behavior or the current
RUL
(
RUL
).
