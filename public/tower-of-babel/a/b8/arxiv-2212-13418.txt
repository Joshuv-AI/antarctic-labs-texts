On the self-adjoint differential operator with the periodic matrix coefficients
O. A. Veliev

Abstract
In this paper we consider the spectrum of the self-adjoint differential operator L L generated by the differential expression of order n n with the m × m m\times m periodic matrix coefficients, where n n and m m are respectively odd and even integers and n > 1 . n>1. We prove that the number of gaps in the spectrum of L L is finite and find explicit estimation in term of coefficients for the number of the gaps. Moreover, we find a condition on the norms of the coefficients for which the spectrum is ( − ∞ , ∞ ) (-\infty,\infty) . Besides we investigate the bands of the spectrum and prove that most of the real axis is overlapped by m m bands. Key Words: Self-adjoint differential operator, Spectral bands, Periodic matrix potential. AMS Mathematics Subject Classification: 34L05, 34L20.

Introduction and Preliminary Facts

Let

8, 10]) the spectrum
\sigma(L)
of the operator

is the union of the
spectra of the operators
L_{t}
for

generated in

by the expression (1) and the quasiperiodic
conditions

U_{\mathbb{\nu}}(y):=y^{(\mathbb{\nu})}\left(1\right)-e^{i\pi t}y^{(\mathbb{\nu})}\left(0\right)=0,\text{ }\mathbb{\nu}=0,1,...,(n-1).

(2)

Note that

is the set of vector-valued
functions

with

The norm

and inner product

in

\left\|f\right\|^{2}=\int_{0}^{1}\left|f\left(x\right)\right|^{2}dx,\text{ }(f,g)=\int_{0}^{1}\left\langle f\left(x\right),g\left(x\right)\right\rangle dx,

where

are the norm and inner product in

The

and

if

Let us discuss some preliminary results and describe the results of this
paper. Clearly,
\left(2\pi k+\pi t\right)^{n}
for

is
the eigenvalue of
L_{t}(0)
with multiplicity

E_{k,t}=\left\{ue^{i\pi\left(2k+t\right)x}:u\in\mathbb{C}^{m}\right\}.

(3)

The set

overlap

First of all note that the case when

essentially differ from the case when

then the spectrum of

is

. Moreover, this proof is very
simple and passes through for the case when both

In [12], for this case, we proved that if the matrix

of the gaps in

is finite. Here we prove this statement without any

obtain the following additional results for the case

and

1. We give an explicit estimation for the number of gaps in term of

M={\textstyle\sum\limits_{v=2}^{n}}\sup_{u}\left\|P_{v}u\right\|\leq{\textstyle\sum\limits_{v=2}^{n}}\left({\textstyle\sum\limits_{i,j=1}^{m}}\left\|p_{v,i,j}\right\|\right),

(4)

2. We prove that if

3. We give a definition of the bands and band functions and prove that the

Note that in the

,

is below-bounded self-adjoint operator. Therefore the

can be numerated in the nondecreasing order

and the function

be used for the odd

since then

is not semibounded operator.

4. We prove that most of the real axis is overlapped by

bands.

Finally note that, the method used in this paper is absolutely different from
the methods used in [11] and [12] for the cases

and

respectively.
