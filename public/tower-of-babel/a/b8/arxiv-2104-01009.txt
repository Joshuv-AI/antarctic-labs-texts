Abstract
We study and analyse the conformal transformations of different conservation laws in the spin hydrodynamics framework.

2
Conservation laws

Baryon number conservation law is given by
d_{\alpha}N^{\alpha}(x)=0
, where for perfect-fluid case, net baryon current
N^{\alpha}
is defined as

\displaystyle N^{\alpha}={\cal N}U^{\alpha}=4\sinh(\frac{\mu}{T})\,\,{\cal N}_{(0)}~U^{\alpha}\,,

(1)

with
{\cal N}
and
U^{\alpha}
being the net baryon density and fluid flow vector, respectively.

\mu
,
T
, and
{\cal N}_{(0)}
is baryon chemical potential, temperature, and number density for ideal relativistic gas of classical massive particles
[
1
]
, respectively.

For perfect-fluid dynamics case, energy and linear momentum conservation law is expressed as

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}T^{\alpha\beta}(x)\equiv d_{\alpha}[({\cal E}+{\cal P})\,U^{\alpha}U^{\beta}+{\cal P}g^{\alpha\beta}]=0\,

(2)

with the energy density,
{\cal E}=4\cosh(\frac{\mu}{T}){\cal E}_{(0)}
and pressure,
{\cal P}=4\cosh(\frac{\mu}{T}){\cal P}_{(0)}
,
with
{\cal E}_{(0)}
and
{\cal P}_{(0)}
being the energy density and pressure for ideal relativistic gas of classical massive particles
[
1
]
.

Total angular momentum consists of orbital angular momentum
L^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
and spin angular momentum
S^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
as

\displaystyle J^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle L^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}+S^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}=x^{\beta}T^{\alpha\gamma}-x^{\gamma}T^{\alpha\beta}+S^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}\,.

(3)

where the total angular momentum conservation law is
d_{\alpha}J^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}=d_{\alpha}S^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}+2T^{[\beta\gamma]}=0
.
Symmetric
T^{\mu\nu}
implies the conservation of spin separately, where the spin tensor is given as
[
19
,
72
]

\displaystyle S_{\rm GLW}^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}={\cal C}U^{\alpha}\omega^{\beta\gamma}+{\cal A}U^{\alpha}U^{[\beta}k^{\gamma]}+{\cal B}\big[U^{[\beta}\Delta^{\alpha\delta}\omega^{\gamma]}_{\hphantom{\alpha}\delta}+U^{\alpha}\Delta^{\delta[\beta}\omega^{\gamma]}_{\hphantom{\alpha}\delta}+\Delta^{\alpha[\beta}k^{\gamma]}\big]

(4)

with
\omega^{\alpha\beta}(x)
is spin polarization tensor and
{\cal A}
,
{\cal B}
and
{\cal C}
are defined as

{\cal A}=2{\cal C}-3{\cal B}
,
{\cal B}=-\frac{{\cal E}+{\cal P}}{2Tz^{2}}
, and
{\cal C}=\frac{{\cal P}}{4T}
.

3
Gubser symmetry and conformal weights

Gubser symmetry
[
68
,
69
]
consist of two special conformal
transformations, i.e., (
SO(3)_{q}
), boosts in the direction of
\eta
, i.e., (
SO(1,1)
) and reflection in the
r-\phi
plane, i.e., (
Z_{2}
).
For a system to conserve conformal symmetry, it needs to be invariant under Weyl rescaling
[
73
,
74
,
75
,
68
,
69
]
which implies that tensors of type
(m,n)
transform homogeneously as

\displaystyle{\cal{X}}^{\mu_{1}...\mu_{m}}_{\nu_{1}...\nu_{n}}(x)\,\rightarrow\,\Omega^{\Delta_{\cal{X}}}{\cal{X}}^{\mu_{1}...\mu_{m}}_{\nu_{1}...\nu_{n}}(x)\rightarrow\,e^{-\varphi(x)\Delta_{\cal{X}}}{\cal{X}}^{\mu_{1}...\mu_{m}}_{\nu_{1}...\nu_{n}}(x)\,,

(5)

where
\Delta_{\cal{X}}=[{\cal{X}}]+m-n
is the conformal weight of the quantity
\cal{X}

with
[\cal{X}]
being its mass dimension, and
m
and
n
represents the contravariant and covariant indices, respectively.
For example, the rank 2 dimensionless metric tensor
g_{\mu\nu}
transforms under Weyl rescaling as
[
73
,
69
]

\displaystyle g_{\mu\nu}\rightarrow\Omega^{-2}\,g_{\mu\nu}\,.

(6)

Using Eq. (
6
) and the normalization of flow vector, one can find
\Delta_{U^{\mu}}=1
.
Energy density and pressure have mass dimension
[{\cal E}]\equiv[{\cal P}]=4
, hence one can obtain their conformal weight to be
\Delta_{{\cal E}}=\Delta_{{\cal P}}=4
. Similarly, net baryon density has conformal weight
\Delta_{{\cal N}}=3
, and temperature and baryon chemical potential have the same conformal weight
\Delta_{T}=\Delta_{\mu}=1
, as both have same mass dimension, which is 1.
Conformal weight of energy-momentum tensor can be known using the conformal weight of
{\cal E}
,
{\cal P}
, and
U^{\mu}
, which results to
\Delta_{T^{\alpha\beta}}=6
, since
\Delta_{x^{\beta}}=0
. One can see from Eq. (
3
) that spin tensor should have same conformal weight as
T^{\mu\nu}
, so, canonical spin tensor
[
76
]
, GLW spin tensor
[
72
]
in Eq. (
4
), and HW spin tensor
[
58
,
54
]
, expressed respectively below, should have same conformal weights

\displaystyle S_{\rm C}^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{i}{8}\bar{\psi}\{\gamma^{\alpha},[\gamma^{\beta},\gamma^{\gamma}]\}\psi\,,

(7)

\displaystyle S^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}_{\rm GLW}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{i}{4m}\left(\bar{\psi}\sigma^{\beta\gamma}\overleftrightarrow{\partial}^{\alpha}\psi-\partial_{\rho}\epsilon^{\beta\gamma\alpha\rho}\bar{\psi}\gamma^{5}\psi\right),

(8)

\displaystyle S_{\rm HW}^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{\rm C}^{\alpha,\beta\gamma}-\frac{1}{4m}\left(\bar{\psi}\sigma^{\beta\gamma}\sigma^{\alpha\rho}\partial_{\rho}\psi+\partial_{\rho}\bar{\psi}\sigma^{\alpha\rho}\sigma^{\beta\gamma}\psi\right),

(9)

with

\sigma^{\mu\nu}\!\!=\!\!\frac{i}{2}\left[\gamma^{\mu},\gamma^{\nu}\right]
, where, spinor
\psi
and dual spinor
\bar{\psi}\equiv\psi^{\dagger}\gamma_{0}
have conformal weight
\Delta_{\psi}=\Delta_{\bar{\psi}}=\frac{3}{2}
and gamma matrix have conformal weight
\Delta_{\gamma^{\mu}}=1

[
77
,
78
]
, hence
\Delta_{S_{\rm GLW}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}}=\Delta_{S_{\rm C}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}}=\Delta_{S_{\rm HW}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}}=6
.
In similar way, one can obtain conformal weight of net baryon number current,
\Delta_{N^{\alpha}}=4
, using conformal weight of
{\cal N}
and Eq. (
1
).
We summarize below the transformation rules under Weyl rescaling (for 4D spacetime) using Eq. (
5
)

\displaystyle N^{\alpha}\rightarrow\Omega^{4}~N^{\alpha},\quad T^{\alpha\beta}\rightarrow\Omega^{6}~T^{\alpha\beta},\quad S^{\alpha\beta\gamma}\rightarrow\Omega^{6}~S^{\alpha\beta\gamma}.

(10)

4
Conformal invariance of laws of conservation

For 4D conformal fluid dynamics, we intend to find the conformal transformations of the laws of conservation for net baryon number, energy and linear momentum, and spin, which are given below as

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}N^{\alpha}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\partial_{\alpha}N^{\alpha}+\Gamma^{\alpha}_{\alpha\beta}N^{\beta}=0\,,

(11)

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}T^{\alpha\beta}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\partial_{\alpha}T^{\alpha\beta}+\Gamma^{\alpha}_{\alpha\lambda}T^{\lambda\beta}+\Gamma^{\beta}_{\alpha\lambda}T^{\alpha\lambda}=0\,,

(12)

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}S^{\alpha\beta\gamma}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\partial_{\alpha}S^{\alpha\beta\gamma}+\Gamma^{\alpha}_{\alpha\lambda}S^{\lambda\beta\gamma}+\Gamma^{\beta}_{\alpha\lambda}S^{\alpha\lambda\gamma}+\Gamma^{\gamma}_{\alpha\lambda}S^{\alpha\beta\lambda}=0,

(13)

respectively, where
\Gamma^{\beta}_{\alpha\lambda}
are Christoffel symbols
[
71
]
, and the conformal transformation of the Christoffel symbols is
[
79
,
75
,
71
]

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\lambda\alpha}^{\beta}=\hat{\Gamma}_{\lambda\alpha}^{\beta}+\delta^{\beta}_{\lambda}\partial_{\alpha}\varphi+\delta^{\beta}_{\alpha}\partial_{\lambda}\varphi-\hat{g}_{\lambda\alpha}\hat{g}^{\beta\sigma}\partial_{\sigma}\varphi\,,

(14)

with
\delta^{\beta}_{\lambda}
denoting the Kronecker delta function and
\varphi
being the function of spacetime coordinates.
For 4D spacetime, net baryon number (
11
) conservation equation is conformal-frame independent
[
73
,
74
,
75
,
68
,
69
]
, and since net baryon number has conformal weight
\Delta_{N^{\alpha}}=4
, one obtains

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}N^{\alpha}=\Omega^{4}\hat{d}_{\alpha}\hat{N}^{\alpha}.

(15)

Now putting Eq. (
10
) and Eq. (
14
) in Eq. (
12
),
we obtain the transformation of conservation of energy and linear momentum
[
74
,
75
]

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}T^{\alpha\beta}=\Omega^{6}\left[\hat{d}_{\alpha}\hat{T}^{\alpha\beta}-\hat{T}^{\lambda}_{\hphantom{\alpha}\lambda}\hat{g}^{\beta\delta}\partial_{\delta}\varphi\right]\,.

(16)

One observe that
\hat{T}^{\alpha\beta}
should have trace 0 to be conserved in de Sitter spacetime
[
80
,
81
,
82
]
, and using Eq. (
10
) and Eq. (
14
) in Eq. (
13
), spin conservation law transforms as

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}S^{\alpha\beta\gamma}=\Omega^{6}\left[\hat{d}_{\alpha}\hat{S}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}(\hat{S}_{\lambda}^{\hphantom{\alpha}\lambda\gamma}\hat{g}^{\beta\sigma}+\hat{S}^{\alpha\beta}_{\hphantom{\alpha}\hphantom{\alpha}\alpha}\hat{g}^{\sigma\gamma})\partial_{\sigma}\varphi\right].

(17)

We find from above equation that spin tensor must satisfy the condition
\hat{S}_{\alpha}^{\hphantom{\alpha}\alpha\beta}=0
in order to have conformal invariance of spin conservation law.
It is easy to see that this condition is not satisfied by GLW (
8
) and HW (
9
) definitions, and hence, conformal invariance of Eq. (
17
) breaks.

5
Summary

We have analysed the properties of the laws of conservation for net baryon number, energy-linear momentum, and spin with respect to the conformal transformations. We found the condition required for the conformal invariance of the spin conservation law, which the GLW and HW spin tensors explicitly breaks.
