Radiative and meson decays of Y ⁡ ( 4230 ) Y(4230) in flavor SU(3)
Luciano Maiani, Antonio D. Polosa, Verónica Riquer

Abstract
The charmonium-like exotic states Y ⁡ ( 4230 ) Y(4230) and the less known Y ⁡ ( 4320 ) Y(4320) , produced in e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} collisions, are sources of positive parity exotic hadrons in association with photons or pseudoscalar mesons. We analyze the radiative and pion decay channels in the compact tetraquark scheme, with a method that proves to work equally well in the most studied D ∗ → γ / π + D D^{*}\to\gamma/\pi+D decays. The decay of the vector Y Y into a pion and a Z c Z_{c} state requires a flip of charge conjugation and isospin that is described appropriately in the formalism used. Rates however are found to depend on the fifth power of pion momentum which would make the final states π ​ Z c ​ ( 4020 ) \pi Z_{c}(4020) strongly suppressed with respect to π ​ Z c ​ ( 3900 ) \pi Z_{c}(3900) . The agreement with BES III data would be improved considering the π ​ Z c ​ ( 4020 ) \pi Z_{c}(4020) events to be fed by the tail of the Y ⁡ ( 4320 ) Y(4320) resonance under the Y ⁡ ( 4230 ) Y(4230) . These results should renovate the interest in further clarifying the emerging experimental picture in this mass region.

The study of final states in

The so-called
