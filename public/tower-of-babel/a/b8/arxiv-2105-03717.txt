Introduction

In the research of harmonic analysis and partial differential equations, the maximal operators and Littelwood-Paley
g
-functions paly an important role and were investigated by many mathematicians extensively.
For any integrable function
f
on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
, the Hardy-Littlewood maximal operator is defined as

M(f)(x):=\sup_{Q}\frac{1}{|Q|}\int_{Q}|f(y)|dy,

where the supremum is taken over all cubes
Q\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
. For
f\in BMO(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, Bennett-DeVore-Sharpley proved in
BDS
that
M(f)
is either infinite or belongs to
BMO(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. The boundedness result in
BDS
can be extended to other maximal operators. For example, let
-\Delta
be the Laplace operator:
\displaystyle\Delta=\sum^{n}_{i=1}\frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{i}^{2}}
. Denote by
M_{\Delta}
and
g
the maximal operator and Littlewood-Paley
g
-function generated by the heat semigroup
\{e^{-t(-\Delta)}\}_{t>0}
, respectively, i.e.,

\left\{\begin{aligned} \displaystyle&M_{\Delta}(f)(x):=\sup_{t>0}|e^{-t(-\Delta)}(f)(x)|;\\
&g(f)(x):=\Bigg(\int_{0}^{\infty}|e^{-t(-\Delta)}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dt}{t^{n+1}}\Bigg)^{1/2}.\end{aligned}\right.

(1)

Due to the mean value on “large” cubes may be infinite, the
BMO(\mathbb{R}^{n})
-boundedness of
M_{\Delta}
or
g
holds if
M_{\Delta}(f)<\infty
or
g(f)<\infty
for
f\in BMO(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

However, if the Laplacian
-\Delta
is replaced by other second-order differential operators, the situation becomes different. Consider the Schrödinger
\mathcal{L}=-\Delta+V
in
\mathbb{R}^{n},\ \ n\geq 3,

where
V
is a nonnegative potential belonging to the reverse Hölder class
B_{q}
for some
q>n/2
. Here a nonnegative potential
V
is said to belong to
B_{q}
if there exists
C>0
such that for every ball
B
,

\Bigg(\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}V^{q}(x)dx\Bigg)^{1/q}\leq\frac{C}{|B|}\int_{B}V(x)dx.

In
BDS
, the authors pointed out that for
f\in BMO(\mathbb{R}^{n})
a supremum of averages of
f
over “large” cubes may be infinite, see
(
BDS
, page 610)
. In 2005, Dziubański et.al.
DGMTZ
proved the square functions associated with Schrödinger operators are bounded on the BMO type space
BMO_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
related with
\mathcal{L}
which is distinguished from the case of
BMO(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. See also
LL
and
wyx
for similar results in the setting of Heisenberg groups and stratified Lie groups.

Let
H=-\Delta+|x|^{2}
be the harmonic oscillator. In
bet
, Betancor et al. introduced a
T1
criterion for Calderón-Zygmund operators related to
H
on the BMO type space
BMO_{H}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. Later, Ma et al. in
ma2
generalized the
T1
criterion to the case of Campanato type spaces
BMO_{\mathcal{L}}^{\gamma}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
related with
\mathcal{L}
. As applications, the authors in
ma2
proved that the maximal operators associated with the heat
semigroup
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}}\}_{t>0}
and with the generalized Poisson operators

\{P^{\sigma}_{t}\}_{t>0}(0<\sigma<1)
, the Littlewood-Paley g-functions given in terms of the heat and the Poisson semigroups are bounded on
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Notice that for
\sigma\in(0,1)
, the generalized Poisson operator

\{P^{\sigma}_{t}\}_{t>0}
is expressed as

P^{\sigma}_{t}(f)(x):=\frac{t^{2\sigma}}{4^{\sigma}\Gamma(\sigma)}\int^{\infty}_{0}e^{-\frac{t^{2}}{4r}}e^{-r\mathcal{L}}(f)\frac{dr}{r^{1+\sigma}}.

(2)

Specially, for
\sigma=1/2
,
\{P^{1/2}_{t}\}_{t>0}
is corresponding to the Poisson semigroup
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{1/2}}\}_{t>0}
associated with
\mathcal{L}
.
The main purpose of this paper is to derive the pointwise estimate and regularity properties of the fractional heat semigroup
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
,
\alpha>0
, to prove the boundedness of the maximal function and the Littlewood-Paley
g
-functions generated by
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
on
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
via
T1
theorem, respectively.

When
\mathcal{L}=-\Delta
, the kernels of the fractional heat semigroup
\{e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
can be defined via the Fourier transform, i.e.,

K_{\alpha,t}(x)=(2\pi)^{-n/2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}e^{ix\cdot\xi-t|\xi|^{2\alpha}}d\xi.

(3)

For
\mathcal{L}=-\Delta+V
with
V>0
, the kernels of fractional heat semigroups
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
,
\alpha\in(0,1)
, can not be defined via (
3
).
However, for
\alpha>0
, the subordinative formula (cf.

K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)=\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{s}(x,y)ds,

(4)

where

is a continuous function on
(0,\infty)
satisfying (
19
) below.
In
Li2
, the identity (
4

via the heat kernel

, see Proposition

, the estimates of

goes back to those of the Poisson kernel

, see
(
duo
, Lemma 3.9)
.

We point out that, compared with the case of
\{P^{\sigma}_{t}\}_{t>0}
, some new regularity estimates should be introduced to prove the
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}
-boundedness of the maximal function and Littlewood-Paley
g
-functions generated by
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
. Let

\|P^{\sigma}_{t}(f)\|_{E}\leq C_{\sigma}\int^{\infty}_{0}t^{2\sigma}e^{-\frac{t^{2}}{4r}}\|e^{-r\mathcal{L}}(f)\|_{E}\frac{dr}{r^{1+\sigma}}.

The fact that

-boundedness of the maximal function

Preliminaries

2.1
Schrödinger operators and function spaces

In this paper, let
\delta_{0}=2-n/q
. At first, we list some properties of the potential
V
which will be used in the sequel.

Lemma 1

(
(
she
, Lemma1.2)
)

(i)
For
0<r<R<\infty
,

\frac{1}{r^{n-2}}\int_{B(x,r)}V(y)dy\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{R}\Big)^{\delta}\frac{1}{R^{n-2}}\int_{B(x,R)}V(y)dy.

(ii)

r^{2-n}\int_{B(x,r)}V(y)dy=1
if
r=\rho(x)
.

r\sim\rho(x)
if and only if

r^{2-n}\int_{B(x,r)}V(y)dy\sim 1.

Lemma 2

(
(
she
, Lemma 1.4)
)

(i)
There exist
C>0
and
k_{0}\geq 1
such that for all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

C^{-1}\rho(x)\Big(1+|x-y|/\rho(x)\Big)^{-k_{0}}\leq\rho(y)\leq C\rho(x)\Big(1+|x-y|/\rho(x)\Big)^{k_{0}/(1+k_{0})}.

In particular,
\rho(y)\sim\rho(x)
if
|x-y|<C\rho(x)
.

(ii)
There exists
l_{0}>1
such that

\int_{B(x,R)}\frac{V(y)}{|x-y|^{n-2}}dy\leq\frac{C}{R^{n-2}}\int_{B(x,R)}V(y)dy\leq C\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{l_{0}}.

Lemma 3

(
(
DZ2
, Corollary 2.8)
)

For every nonnegative Schwarz function
\omega
, there exist
\delta>0
and
C>0
such that

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}t^{-n/2}\omega(|x-y|/\sqrt{t})V(y)dy\leq\begin{cases}Ct^{-1}(\sqrt{t}/\rho(x))^{\delta},&t<\rho(x)^{2};\\
Ct^{-1}(\sqrt{t}/\rho(x))^{l_{0}},&t\geq{\rho(x)^{2}},\end{cases}

where
l_{0}
is the constant given in Lemma
2
.

It is well known that the classical Hardy space
H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
can be defined via the maximal function
\sup_{t>0}|e^{-t(-\Delta)}f(x)|
(cf.
15
). In this sense, we can say that the Hardy
space
H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is the Hardy space associated with
-\Delta
. Since 1990s, the theory of Hardy spaces associated with operators on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
has been investigated extensively. In
DZ3
, Dziubański and Zienkiewicz introduced the Hardy space
H^{1}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
related to Schrödinger operators
\mathcal{L}
and obtained the atomic characterization and the Riesz transform characterization of
H^{1}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
via local Hardy spaces. By the aid of Campanato type spaces, the spaces
H^{p}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})(0<p\leq 1)
were introduced by Dziubański and Zienkiewicz
DZ2
. In recent years, the results of
DZ3
;
DZ2
have been extended to other second-ordered differential operators, and various function spaces associated to operators have been established. For further information, we refer the reader to
BDY
;
wyx
;
jia
;
yang
and the references therein.

For a Schrödinger operator
\mathcal{L}
, let
\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}}\}_{t>0}
be the heat semigroup generated by
\mathcal{L}
and denote by
K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
the integral kernels of
e^{-t\mathcal{L}}
. Because the potential
V\geq 0
, the Feynman-Kac formula implies that

0\leq K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t}(x,y)\leq K_{t}(x-y):=(4\pi t)^{-n/2}e^{-\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{4t}}.

The Hardy type spaces
H^{p}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,
0<p\leq 1
, are defined as follows (cf.
DZ2
):

Definition 1

For
0<p\leq 1
, the Hardy type space
H^{p}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is defined as the completion of the space of compactly supported
L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
)-functions such that the maximal function

M_{\mathcal{L}}(f)(x):=\sup_{t>0}|e^{-t\mathcal{L}}(f)(x)|

belongs to
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. The quasi-norm in
H^{p}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is defined as

\|f\|_{H^{p}_{\mathcal{L}}}:=\|M_{\mathcal{L}}(f)\|_{L^{p}}.

Let
f
be a locally integrable function on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
B=B(x,r)
be a ball. Denote by
f_{B}
the mean of
f
on
B
, i.e.,
f_{B}=|B|^{-1}\int_{B}f(y)dy
. Let

f(B,V):=\begin{cases}f_{B},&r<\rho(x);\\
0,&r\geq\rho(x),\end{cases}

where the auxiliary function
\rho(\cdot)
is defined as

\rho(x):=\sup\Bigg\{r>0:\frac{1}{r^{n-2}}\int_{B(x,y)}V(y)dy\leq 1\Bigg\}.

Definition 2

Let
0<\gamma\leq 1
. The Campanato type space
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is defined as the set of all locally integrable functions
f
satisfying

\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}}:=\sup_{B\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Bigg\{\frac{1}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\int_{B}|f(x)-f(B,V)|dx\Bigg\}<\infty.

The dual space of
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,
0\leq\gamma<1
, is the Campanato type space
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
(cf.
(
ma1
, Theorem 4.5)
).

2.2
The
T1
criterion on Campanato type spaces

We denote by
L^{p}_{c}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
the set of functions
f\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,
1\leq p\leq\infty
, whose support
\text{ supp }(f)
is a compact subset of
\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

Definition 3

Let
0\leq\beta<n
,
1<p\leq q<\infty
with
1/q=1/p-\beta/n
. Let
T
be a bounded linear operator from
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into
L^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
such that

Tf(x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K(x,y)f(y)dy,\ \ \ f\in L^{p}_{c}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\ and\ a.e.\ x\notin\text{supp}(f).

We shall say that
T
is a
\beta
-Schrödinger-Calderón-Zygmund operator with regularity exponent
\delta>0
if there exists a constant
C>0
such that

(i)

\displaystyle|K(x,y)|\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n-\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}
for all
N>0
and
x\neq y
;

(ii)

\displaystyle|K(x,y)-K(x,z)|+|K(y,x)-K(z,x)|\leq C\frac{|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n-\beta+\delta}}
when
|x-y|>2|y-z|
.

The following
T1
type criterions on Campanato type spaces were established by Ma et al.
ma2
.

Theorem 2.1

(
(
ma2
, Theorem1.1)
)

Let
T
be a
\beta
-Schrödinger-Calderón-Zygmund operator,
\beta\geq 0
,
0<\beta+\gamma<\min\{1,\delta\}
, with smoothness exponent
\delta
. Then
T
is a bounded operator from
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into
BMO^{\gamma+\beta}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if and only if there exists a constant

C
such that

\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)^{\gamma}\frac{1}{|B|^{1+\beta/n}}\int_{B}|T1(y)-(T1)_{B}|dy\leq C

for every ball
B(x,r)
,
x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
0<r\leq\rho(x)/2
.

When
\gamma=0
, the authors in
ma2
also proved

Theorem 2.2

(
(
ma2
, Theorem1.2)
)

Let
T
be a
\beta
-Schrödinger-Calderón-Zygmund operator,
0\leq\beta<\min\{1,\delta\}
, with smoothness exponent
\delta
. Then
T
is a bounded operator from
BMO_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into
BMO^{\beta}_{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)\frac{1}{|B|^{1+\beta/n}}\int_{B}|T1(y)-(T1)_{B}|dy\leq C

.

By the fundamental solution of Schrödinger operators, Dziubański and Zienkiewicz proved that the heat kernel

Lemma 5

(i)

(
(
DZ3

(ii)

(
(

Assume that

For any

(

Lemma 7

(

Suppose that

for all

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}{K}^{\mathcal{L}}_{t}(x,y)|\leq Ct^{-(n+1)/2}e^{-c{{|x-y|}^{2}}/{t}}\left(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\right)^{-N}.

(6)

By the perturbation theory for semigroups of operators,

\displaystyle K_{t}(x-y)-K_{t}^{\mathcal{L}}(x,y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{t}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K_{t-s}(w-x)V(w)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{s}(w,y)dwds

(7)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{t/2}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K_{t-s}(w-x)V(w)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{s}(w,y)dwds

\displaystyle+\int^{t/2}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K_{s}(w-x)V(w)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t-s}(w,y)dwds.

Similar to
(
DZ2
, Proposition 2.16)
, we can prove the following lemma.

Lemma 8

Suppose that
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n
. There exist constants

such that

\Big|\nabla_{x}K_{t}(x-y)-\nabla_{x}K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t}(x,y)\Big|\leq Ct^{-(n+1)/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\min\Bigg\{\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}},\ \Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\Bigg\}.

Proof

If
t\geq\rho(y)^{2}
, it is easy to see that

\Big|\nabla_{x}K_{t}(x-y)-\nabla_{x}K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t}(x,y)\Big|\leq Ct^{-(n+1)/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}.

If
t<\rho(y)^{2}
, by (
7
), we get

\displaystyle\Big|\nabla_{x}K_{t}(x-y)-\nabla_{x}K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{t}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\nabla_{x}K_{t-s}(x-w)V(w)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{s}(w,y)dwds\Big|

(8)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle I_{1}+I_{2},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} I_{1}&:=\int^{t/2}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla_{x}K_{t-s}(x-w)|V(w)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{s}(w,y)dwds;\\
I_{2}&:=\int^{t/2}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla_{x}K_{s}(x-w)|V(w)K^{\mathcal{L}}_{t-s}(w,y)dwds.\end{aligned}\right.

For
I_{1}
, using Lemmas
7
and
3
, we get

{|x-y|^{2}}/{(t^{1/\alpha}u)}=r
. It holds

\displaystyle\Big|\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}(x-y)\Big)-\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)-\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}(x-z)\Big)\Big|

(20)

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}t^{-n/(2\alpha)}\int^{\infty}_{0}e^{-r}\Big(\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}r}\Big)^{-1-\alpha-(n+1)/2}\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}r^{2}}dr

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{1+1/(2\alpha)}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+1}}.

On the other hand, letting
s/t^{1/\alpha}=\tau
, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}(x-y)\Big)-\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)-\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}(x-z)\Big)\Big|

(21)

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{1/(2\alpha)}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}s^{-(n+1)/2}ds

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{1/(2\alpha)}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-(n+1)/2}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}t^{-n/(2\alpha)}.

It is easy to see that the desired estimate follows from (
20
) and (
21
). The proof of Proposition
10
is finished.

-boundedness via
T1

4.1
Maximal operators for the fractional heat semigroup

Definition 4

Let
0<\gamma\leq 1
. The Campanato type space
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L},L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is defined as the set of all locally integrable functions
f
satisfying

\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L},L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}}:=\sup_{B\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Bigg\{\frac{1}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\int_{B}\|f(x,t)-f(B,V)\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}dx\Bigg\}<\infty.

To prove that
M^{\alpha}_{\mathcal{L}}
is bounded from
BMO_{\mathcal{L}}^{\gamma}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into itself, we give a vector-valued interpretation of the operator and apply Lemma
4
. Indeed, it is clear that
M^{\alpha}_{\mathcal{L}}f=\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}f\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}
. Hence, it is enough to show that the operator
\Lambda(f):=\{e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}f\}_{t>0}
is bounded from
BMO^{\gamma}_{\mathcal{L}}
into
BMO_{\mathcal{L},L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}^{\gamma}
.

By the spectral theorem,
\Lambda
is bounded from
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into
L^{2}_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. The desired result can be then deduced from the following theorem.

Theorem 4.1

Assume that the potential
V\in B_{q}
with
q>n/2
. Let
x,y,z\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

(i)
For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

\Big\|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}\leq\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

(ii)
For
|x-y|>2|y-z|
and any
0<\delta<\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{0}\}
, there exists a constant
C>0
such that

\Big\|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}+\Big\|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,x)-K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,x)\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}\\
\quad\leq\frac{C|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}}.

(22)

(iii)
There exists a constant
C
such that for every ball
B=B(x,r)
with
0<r\leq\rho(x)/2
,

\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}dy\leq C,

and, if
\gamma<\min\{2\alpha,1,\delta_{0}\}
then

\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)^{\gamma}\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}dy\leq C.

Proof

For (i), from (i) of Proposition
2
, we can get

\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Bigg\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N}},\ t^{-n/(2\alpha)}\Bigg\}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}>|x-y|
, then

\displaystyle\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}\leq|x-y|
, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N}}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{|x-y|}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|x-y|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{2\alpha+N}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+N}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

For (ii), from (ii) of Proposition
2
, we obtain

\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Bigg\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N+\delta}},\ t^{-n/(2\alpha)}\Bigg\}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{\delta}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}>|x-y|
, then

\displaystyle\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{n}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta}\leq\frac{C|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}}.

If
|x-y|>\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}
, we can also get

\displaystyle\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N+\delta}}|y-z|^{\delta}\leq\frac{C_{N}|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}}.

The symmetry of the kernel
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
gives the conclusion of (ii).

For (iii), letting
B=B(x,r)
with
0<r\leq\rho(x)/2
, the triangle inequality gives

\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}\leq\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}dz.

We estimate
\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}
. Because
y,z\in B
,
\rho(y)\sim\rho(z)\sim\rho(x).

By Proposition
5
, we split

|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)|\leq S_{1}+S_{2}
,
where

\left\{\begin{aligned} S_{1}:=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(y,w)\Big|dw;\\
S_{2}:=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(z,w)\Big|dw.\end{aligned}\right.

For
S_{1}
, if
|y-w|\leq\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}
, we obtain

\displaystyle S_{1}\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\frac{t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|y-w|)^{n+2\alpha}}dw\leq\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}.

If
|y-w|>\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}
, we can see that

\displaystyle S_{1}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\frac{|y-w|}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\frac{t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|y-w|)^{n+2\alpha}}dw

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\rho(x)^{\delta_{0}}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{|y-w|^{\delta_{0}+n-1}t}{(|y-w|+\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{n+2\alpha}}dw

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\int^{\infty}_{0}u^{\delta_{0}+n-1}(1+u)^{-n-2\alpha}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}},

where we have chosen
\delta_{0}<2\alpha
since
\delta_{0}=2-n/q
,
q>n/2
.
The proof of the term
S_{2}
is similar to that of the term
S_{1}
, so we omit it. Then we can get

\Big|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big|\leq C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}},

which shows that if
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}\leq 2r
,

\Big|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big|\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}>2r
, then
|y-z|\leq 2r<\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}
. Hence, Proposition
2
implies that for
0<\delta<\delta_{0}
,

\Big|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big|\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big|K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,w)-K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,w)\Big|dw\\
\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{\delta}\frac{t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|y-w|)^{n+2\alpha}}dw\leq C\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{\delta}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{\delta}.

(23)

Therefore, if
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}>\rho(x)
, (
23
) gives

|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)|\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta}.

When
2r<\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}<\rho(x)
, we have

|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)|=I+II+III,

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} I&:=\int_{|w-y|>c\rho(y)>4|y-z|}\Big|\Big(K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(y,w)\Big)-\Big(K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(z,w)\Big)\Big|dw;\\
II&:=\int_{4|y-z|<|w-y|<c\rho(y)}\Big|\Big(K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(y,w)\Big)-\Big(K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(z,w)\Big)\Big|dw;\\
III&:=\int_{|w-y|<4|y-z|}\Big|\Big(K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(y,w)\Big)-\Big(K^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,w)-K_{\alpha,t}(z,w)\Big)\Big|dw.\end{aligned}\right.

Noticing that the estimate (
22
) is valid for the classical fractional heat kernel. For
I
, by (
22
), we can get

I\leq C\int_{|w-y|>c\rho(y)>4|y-z|}\frac{|y-z|^{\delta}}{|w-y|^{n+\delta}}dw\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta}.

For
II
, we apply Proposition
8
and the fact that
\rho(w)\sim\rho(y)
in the region of integration.

\displaystyle II\leq C|y-z|^{\delta}\int_{4|y-z|<|w-y|<c\rho(y)}\frac{tdw}{\rho(w)^{\delta}(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|w-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta}.

For
III
, since
|y-z|\leq 2r<\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}},
we have
|w-y|<C\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}
. For
n-\delta_{0}>0
, by Proposition
5
, we obtain

\displaystyle III

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\Bigg(\int_{|w-y|<4|y-z|}\frac{tdw}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|w-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}+\int_{|w-z|\leq 5|y-z|}\frac{tdw}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|w-z|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Bigg)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\int^{5|y-z|/\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}_{0}u^{n-1}du\leq C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{Cr^{n}}{\rho(x)^{\delta_{0}}(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{n-\delta_{0}}}\leq\frac{Cr^{n}}{\rho(x)^{\delta_{0}}r^{n-\delta_{0}}}=C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}.

Thus, when
2r<\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}<\rho(x)
,

\Big|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big|\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta}.

Combining the above estimates, we can get

\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta}.

(24)

Therefore, it holds

\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}dy\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta}\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)\leq C,

which is the first conclusion of (iii).

For the second estimate of (iii), take
\delta\in(\gamma,\ \min\{2\alpha,\ 2-n/q\})
. by (
24
), we have

\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)^{\gamma}\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{\infty}((0,\infty),dt)}dy\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{\delta-\gamma}\leq C.

Similar to Section

, we introduce the following function space:

Definition 5

\displaystyle\quad\leq\frac{C_{N}|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}}.

(iii)

C

\gamma<\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{0},1\}

\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)^{\gamma}\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}dy\leq C.

Proof

For (i), from Proposition
3
, we have

\Big|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Bigg\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N}},\ t^{-n/(2\alpha)}\Bigg\}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big\|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\int^{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{0}\frac{t^{2+2N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2n+4\alpha+4N}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\int^{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{0}\frac{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{4\alpha+4N}}{|x-y|^{2n+4\alpha+4N}}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{|x-y|}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\int^{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{|x-y|}\Big)^{4\alpha+2N}\frac{dt}{t}.

{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}/{|x-y|}=u
. We can see that

\displaystyle\Big\|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\int^{1}_{0}u^{4\alpha+2N-1}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}\geq|x-y|
, we can get

\displaystyle\Big\|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\int^{\infty}_{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}t^{-n/\alpha-1}dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}.

For (ii), By Proposition
3
, we have

\displaystyle\Big\|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{C_{N}t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{2n+4\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{2\delta}dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}|y-z|^{2\delta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{2\alpha-2\delta}}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{2n+4\alpha}}dt.

Let
{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}/{|x-y|}=u
. We obtain

\displaystyle\Big\|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}|y-z|^{2\delta}|x-y|^{-2n-2\delta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{u^{4\alpha-2\delta-1}}{(1+u)^{2n+4\alpha}}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}|y-z|^{2\delta}}{|x-y|^{2n+2\delta}}.

The symmetry of the kernel
D^{L,m}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
gives the conclusion of (ii).

For (iii), let us fix
y,z\in B=B(x_{0},r)
,
0<r\leq\rho(x_{0})/2
. Similar to Theorem
4.1
, we must handle

\Big\|t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}.

We can write

\displaystyle\Big\|t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle M_{1}+M_{2}+M_{3},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} M_{1}&:=\int^{(2r)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big)dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t};\\
M_{2}&:=\int^{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}_{(2r)^{2\alpha}}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big)dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t};\\
M_{3}&:=\int^{\infty}_{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big)dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t}.\end{aligned}\right.

Since
y,z\in B\subset B(x_{0},\rho(x_{0}))
, it follows that
\rho(y)\sim\rho(x_{0})\sim\rho(z)
. By Proposition
3
(iii),

M_{1}\leq C\int^{(2r)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\frac{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}/\rho(x_{0}))^{2\delta}}{(1+\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}/\rho(x_{0}))^{2N}}\frac{dt}{t}\leq C\int^{(2r)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}\frac{dt}{t}=C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}.

Also, by Proposition
3
(ii),

\displaystyle M_{3}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int^{\infty}_{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{2\delta}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int^{\infty}_{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{2\delta}\frac{dt}{t}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}.

It remains to estimate the term
M_{2}
. In this case,
|y-z|\leq 2r\leq\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}\leq\rho(x_{0})
. Then we can use the methods in Theorem
4.1
,

\displaystyle M_{2}=\int^{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}_{(2r)^{2\alpha}}\Big|M_{2,1}+M_{2,2}+M_{2,3}\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} M_{2,1}&:=\int_{|x-y|>c\rho(y)>4|y-z|}\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(x-y)\Big)-\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)-D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(x-z)\Big)dx;\\
M_{2,2}&:=\int_{4|y-z|<|x-y|<c\rho(y)}\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(x-y)\Big)-\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)-D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(x-z)\Big)dx;\\
M_{2,3}&:=\int_{|x-y|<4|y-z|}\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(x-y)\Big)-\Big(D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)-D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(x-z)\Big)dx.\end{aligned}\right.

For
M_{2,1}
, similar to prove (
25
), we also can get

\Big|D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-D^{\mathcal{L},m}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big|\leq\frac{C|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}},

which is valid to
D^{m}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot)
. So we obtain

|M_{2,1}|\leq C\int_{|x-y|>c\rho(y)>4|y-z|}\frac{|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}}dx\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta}.

For
M_{2,2}
, by Proposition
9

and the fact that
\rho(x)\sim\rho(y)
in the region of integration.

\displaystyle|M_{2,2}|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C|y-z|^{\delta}\int_{4|y-z|<|x-y|<c\rho(y)}\frac{t}{\rho(x)^{\delta}(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}dx\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta}.

For
M_{2,3}
, since
|y-z|\leq 2r<\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}},
we have
|x-y|<C\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}
. For
n-\delta_{0}>0
, by Proposition
7
,
we obtain

\displaystyle|M_{2,3}|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\Bigg(\int_{|x-y|<4|y-z|}\frac{tdx}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}+\int_{|x-z|\leq 5|y-z|}\frac{tdx}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-z|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Bigg)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\int^{5|y-z|/\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}_{0}u^{n-1}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{Cr^{n}}{\rho(x_{0})^{\delta_{0}}(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{n-\delta_{0}}}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta_{0}}.

The estimates for
M_{2,i},i=1,2,3
, imply that

M_{2}\leq\int^{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}_{(2r)^{2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}\frac{dt}{t}=C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{r}\Big).

Finally, we can get

\Big\|t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-t^{m}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta}\Big(\log(\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{r})\Big)^{1/2}.

Thus (iii) readily follows.

4.3
Boundedness of Littlewood-Paley
g
-function
\widetilde{g}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha}

By the
L^{2}
-boundedness of Riesz transforms
\nabla_{x}\mathcal{L}^{-1/2}
, we can see that

\|\widetilde{g}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha}f\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\leq C\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{1/2\alpha}\mathcal{L}^{1/2}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}f(x)|^{2}dx\Big)\frac{dt}{t}.

Then by the spectral theorem, we know that
\widetilde{g}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha}
is bounded from
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Theorem 4.3

Assume that the potential
V\in B_{q}
with
q>n
. Let
x,y,z\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
. Then

(i)
For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N};

(ii)
Let
|x-y|>2|y-z|
and
0<\delta<\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{1},1\}
. For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}+\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(y,x)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(z,x)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C_{N}|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-y|^{n+\delta}};

(iii)
There exists a constant
C
such that for every ball
B=B(x_{0},r)
with
0<r\leq\rho(x)/2
,

\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}dy\leq C,

and, if
\gamma<\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{1},1\}
then

\Big(\frac{\rho(x)}{r}\Big)^{\gamma}\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}\Big\|t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-(t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1)_{B}\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}dy\leq C.

Proof

For (i), from Proposition
4
, we have

\Big|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Bigg\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha+1/(2\alpha)}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N+1}},\ t^{-n/(2\alpha)}\Bigg\}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}\leq|x-y|
, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{0}\frac{t^{2+2N/\alpha+1/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2n+4\alpha+4N+2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\int^{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{0}\frac{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{4\alpha+4N+2}}{|x-y|^{2n+4\alpha+4N+2}}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{|x-y|}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\int^{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{|x-y|}\Big)^{4\alpha+2N+2}\frac{dt}{t}.

Let
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}/|x-y|=u
. We can see that

\displaystyle\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\int^{1}_{0}u^{4\alpha+2N+1}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}.

If
\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}\geq|x-y|
, we can get

\displaystyle\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}\int^{\infty}_{|x-y|^{2\alpha}}t^{-n/\alpha-1}dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|^{2n}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(x)}+\frac{|x-y|}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N},

which proves (i).

For (ii), By Proposition
4
, we have

\displaystyle\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{C_{N}t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{2n+4\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{2\delta}dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}|y-z|^{2\delta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}})^{2\alpha-2\delta}}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{2n+4\alpha}}dt.

Let
{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}/{|x-y|}=u
. We obtain

\displaystyle\Big\|\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}|y-z|^{2\delta}|x-y|^{-2n-2\delta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{u^{4\alpha-2\delta-1}}{(1+u)^{2n+4\alpha}}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}|y-z|^{2\delta}}{|x-y|^{2n+2\delta}}.

The symmetry of the kernel
D^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
gives the desired conclusion of (ii).

For (iii), fix
y,z\in B=B(x_{0},r)
with
0<r\leq\rho(x_{0})/2
. Similar to Theorem
4.2
, we need to deal with the term

\Big\|t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

first. Write

\displaystyle\Big\|t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle G_{1}+G_{2}+G_{3},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} G_{1}&:=\int^{(2r)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big)dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t};\\
G_{2}&:=\int^{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}_{(2r)^{2\alpha}}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big)dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t};\\
G_{3}&:=\int^{\infty}_{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)-\widetilde{D}^{\mathcal{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)\Big)dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t}.\end{aligned}\right.

Since
y,z\in B\subset B(x_{0},\rho(x_{0}))
, then
\rho(y)\sim\rho(x_{0})\sim\rho(z)
. It follows from Proposition
4
(iii) that

G_{1}\leq C\int^{(2r)^{2\alpha}}_{0}(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}/\rho(x_{0}))^{1+2\alpha}\frac{dt}{t}\leq C\int^{(2s)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}\frac{dt}{t}=C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}.

Also, we apply Proposition
4
(ii) to deduce that

\displaystyle G_{3}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int^{\infty}_{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{2\delta}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{t}{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}dx\Big|^{2}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int^{\infty}_{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|y-z|}{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}}}\Big)^{2\delta}\frac{dt}{t}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}.

Then for
G_{2}
, following the procedure of the treatment for
M_{2}
in Theorem
4.2
, we obtain

G_{2}\leq\int^{\rho(x_{0})^{2\alpha}}_{(2r)^{2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}\frac{dt}{t}=C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{2\delta}\log\Big(\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{r}\Big).

From the above estimates, we can get

\Big\|t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(y)-t^{1/(2\alpha)}\nabla_{x}e^{-t\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}}1(z)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{\delta}\Big(\log(\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{r})\Big)^{1/2}.

Thus (iii) readily follows.
