Abstract
The work is motivated by the papers [ Ba1 ] , [ Ba2 ] , [ Ba7 ] , [ Ba11 ] , [ Be ] and [ Be-Tu ] . In particular, the strong homology groups of continuous maps were defined and studied in [ Be ] and [ Be-Tu ] . To show that the given groups are a homology type functor, it was required to construct a corresponding shape category. In this paper, we study this very problem. In particular, using the methods developed in [ Ba7 ] , [ Ma3 ] , the strong shape theory of continuous maps of compact metric spaces, the so-called strong fiber shape theory is constructed.
