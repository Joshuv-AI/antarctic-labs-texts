On the 2-adic valuation of ( a b − c d a^{b}-c^{d} )
Luca Onnis

Abstract
In this paper will be proved an inequality regarding v 2 ​ ( a b − c d ) v_{2}(a^{b}-c^{d}) . Using this formula it will be possible to have informations about the divisibility of 2 of this function without computing it. Then, will be studied the behavior of this function when it approaches infinity and there will be a lot of analogies with 2-adic integers.
