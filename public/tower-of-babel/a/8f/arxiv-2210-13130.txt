Introduction

J. Álvarez and M. Milman
AlvarezMilman
introduced a new class of Calderón-Zygmund operators, called
strongly singular Calderón-Zygmund operator
and established continuity of those operators in real Hardy space
H^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. More precisely, a continuous function
K\in C(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\backslash\Delta)
, where
\Delta=\left\{(x,x):\,x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\right\}
is a
\delta
-kernel of type
\sigma
, if there exists some
0<\delta\leq 1
and
0<\sigma\leq 1
such that

|K(x,y)-K(x,z)|+|K(y,x)-K(z,x)|\leq C\dfrac{|y-z|^{\delta}}{|x-z|^{n+\frac{\delta}{\sigma}}},

(1)

for all
|x-z|\geq 2|y-z|^{\sigma}
. A bounded linear operator
T:\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\rightarrow\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is called a
strongly singular Calderón-Zygmund operator
, if it is associated to a
\delta
-kernel of type
\sigma
in the sense

\langle Tf,g\rangle=\int{\int{K(x,y)f(y)g(x)dy}dx}
, for all
f,g\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
with disjoint supports; it has bounded extension from
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
to itself and in addition
T
and
T^{\ast}
extend to a continuous operator from
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
to
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, where

\dfrac{1}{p}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{\beta}{n}
for some
(1-\sigma)\dfrac{n}{2}\leq\beta<\dfrac{n}{2}
.
When
\sigma=1
and
\beta=0
we recover the standard non-convolution Calderón-Zygmund operators (see
Coifman-Meyer
).

The authors in
(
AlvarezMilman
, Theorem 2.2)
established the continuity of those classes of operators in real Hardy spaces
H^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
as follows: under the condition
T^{\ast}(1)=0
, strongly singular Calderón-Zygmund operators associated to a kernel satisfying (
1
) are bounded from
H^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
to itself for every
q_{0}<q\leq 1
where

\frac{1}{q_{0}}:=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\beta\left(\frac{\delta}{\sigma}+\frac{n}{2}\right)}{n\left(\frac{\delta}{\sigma}-\delta+\beta\right)}\ .

(2)

The case
q=q_{0}
is still open, however the conclusion continues to hold replacing the target space by
L^{q_{0}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
(see
(
AlvarezMilmanVectorValued
, Theorem 3.9)
).

In this note, we establish results on continuity of strongly singular Calderón-Zygmund operators on Hardy-Morrey spaces

\displaystyle\left(\int_{C_{j}(z,\ell^{\rho})}{|K(x,y)-K(x,z)|^{r}+|K(y,x)-K(z,x)|^{r}dx}\right)^{\frac{1}{r}}

2
Hardy-Morrey spaces
\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

In this section, we recall and present some properties of Hardy-Morrey spaces. For
0<q\leq\lambda<\infty
, the Morrey spaces, denoted by
\mathcal{M}^{\lambda}_{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, are defined to be the set of measurable functions
f\in L^{q}_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
such that

\displaystyle{\|f\|_{\mathcal{M}^{\lambda}_{q}}:=\sup_{J}|J|^{\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{q}}\left(\int_{J}|f(y)|^{q}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}<\infty},

where the supremum is taken over all cubes
J\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

For any tempered distribution
f\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and any fixed
\varphi\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
with
\int\varphi\neq 0
, consider the smooth maximal function
M_{\varphi}f(x)=\sup_{t>0}\left|(\varphi_{t}\ast f)(x)\right|
, where
\varphi_{t}(x)=t^{-n}\varphi(x/t)
. For any
0<q\leq\lambda<\infty
, we say that
f\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
belongs to Hardy-Morrey space
\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if the smooth maximal function
M_{\varphi}f\in\mathcal{M}_{q}^{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. The functional
\|f\|_{\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}}:=\|M_{\varphi}f\|_{\mathcal{M}_{q}^{\lambda}}
defines a quasi-norm as
0<q<1
and is a norm if
q\geq 1
.

In the same way as Hardy spaces, the Hardy-Morrey spaces have also equivalent maximal characterizations (see
(
JiaWang-HM
, Section 2)
). Clearly, Hardy-Morrey spaces cover the classical Hardy spaces
H^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
when
\lambda=q
and Morrey spaces
\mathcal{M}^{\lambda}_{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if
1<q\leq\lambda<\infty
.

2.1
Atomic decomposition in Hardy-Morrey spaces

Definition 1

(
JiaWang-SingularIntegrals
, Definiton 2.2)
. Let
0<q\leq 1\leq r\leq\infty
with
q<r
and
q\leq\lambda<\infty
. A measurable function
a_{Q}
is called a
(q,\lambda,r)-
atom if it is supported on a cube
Q\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
and satisfies:
(i)
\|a_{Q}\|_{L^{r}}\leq|Q|^{\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\lambda}}
and
(ii)\ \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}x^{\alpha}a_{Q}(x)dx=0

for all
\alpha\in\mathbb{N}_{0}^{n}
such that
|\alpha|\leq N_{q}:=\left\lfloor n\left({1}/{q}-1\right)\right\rfloor
, where
\lfloor\cdot\rfloor
denotes the floor function.

The following lemma is an extension of
(
MT
, Proposition 2.5)
and the proof will be presented for completeness.

Proposition 1

Let
0<q\leq 1\leq r\leq\infty
with
q<r
and
q\leq\lambda<\infty
with
\lambda\leq r
. If
f
is a compactly supported function in
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
satisfying the moment condition

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}x^{\alpha}f(x)dx=0\;\text{ for all }\;|\alpha|\leq N_{q},

(5)

then it belongs to
\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and moreover
\|f\|_{\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}|Q|^{1/\lambda-1/r}
for all cube
Q\supseteq\text{supp}\,(f)
. In particular, if
f=a_{Q}
, then
\|a_{Q}\|_{\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}}\lesssim 1
uniformly.

Proof

Let
J\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
be an arbitrary cube and
Q
a cube such that
\text{supp}\,(f)\subseteq Q
. Split the integral over
J
into
J\cap Q^{\ast}
and
J\setminus Q^{\ast}
. Since the maximal function
M_{\varphi}
is bounded from
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
to itself for every
1<r\leq\infty
, it follows that

\displaystyle\int_{J\cap Q^{*}}|M_{\varphi}f(x)|^{q}dx

\displaystyle\leq\|M_{\varphi}f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|J\cap Q^{\ast}|^{1-\frac{q}{r}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|J\cap Q^{\ast}|^{1-\frac{q}{r}}.

For
r=1
and
0<q<1
, setting
R=\|f\|_{L^{1}}|J\cap Q^{\ast}|^{-1}
and using that
M_{\varphi}
satisfies weak
(1,1)
inequality we get the analogous inequality:

\displaystyle\int_{J\cap Q^{*}}|M_{\varphi}f(x)|^{q}dx\,\simeq\int_{0}^{\infty}\omega^{q-1}\left|\{x\in J\cap Q^{\ast}:\,|M_{\varphi}f(x)|>\omega\}\right|d\omega

\displaystyle\quad\lesssim\,|J\cap Q^{\ast}|\int_{0}^{R}\omega^{q-1}d\omega+\|f\|_{L^{1}}\int_{R}^{\infty}\omega^{q-2}d\omega\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{1}}^{q}\,|J\cap Q^{\ast}|^{1-{q}}.

(6)

If
|Q|<|J|
, since
q/\lambda-1\leq 0
and
1-q/r>0
for all
1\leq r<\infty
, one has
|J|^{{q}/{\lambda}-1}|J\cap Q^{\ast}|^{1-{q}/{r}}\leq|Q|^{1-q/r}
. On the other hand, if
|J|<|Q|
, using that
\lambda\leq r
it follows

\displaystyle{|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}|J\cap Q^{\ast}|^{1-\frac{q}{r}}=|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-\frac{q}{r}}\,\left(\frac{|J\cap Q^{\ast}|}{|J|}\right)^{1-\frac{q}{r}}\leq|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{r}}}.

Hence

|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\int_{J\cap Q^{*}}|M_{\varphi}f(x)|^{q}dx\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{r}}.

To estimate the integral on
J\backslash Q^{\ast}
, using the moment condition (
5
) we write

\varphi_{t}\ast f(x)=\int f(y)\left(\varphi_{t}(x-y)-P_{\varphi_{t}}(y)\right)dy,

where
\displaystyle{P_{\varphi_{t}}(y)=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq N_{q}}{C_{\alpha}\,\partial^{\alpha}\varphi_{t}(x)\,(-y)^{\alpha}}}
denotes the Taylor polynomial of degree
N_{q}
of the function
y\mapsto\varphi_{t}(x-y)
.
The standard estimate of the remainder term (see
(
Stein
, p. 106)
)
yields

\left|\varphi_{t}(x-y)-P_{\varphi_{t}}(y)\right|\lesssim{|y-x_{Q}|^{N_{q}+1}}{|x-x_{Q}|^{-(n+N_{q}+1)}}

and since
\text{supp}\,(f)\subseteq Q
, we have the pointwise control

\displaystyle\left|M_{\varphi}f(x)\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{\ell_{Q}^{N_{q}+1}}{|x-x_{Q}|^{n+N_{q}+1}}\int_{Q}{|f(y)|dy}\lesssim\frac{\ell_{Q}^{N_{q}+1}}{|x-x_{Q}|^{n+N_{q}+1}}\,\|f\|_{L^{r}}\,|Q|^{1-\frac{1}{r}}.

If
|Q|<|J|
, since
N_{q}+1>n\left(1/q-1\right)
, we estimate
|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\int_{J\setminus Q^{*}}|M_{\varphi}f(x)|^{q}dx
by

\displaystyle\|f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|Q|^{q\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}+\frac{N_{q}}{n}+\frac{1}{n}+1\right)-1}\int_{(Q^{\ast})^{c}}|x-x_{Q}|^{-q(n+N_{q}+1)}dx\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|Q|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-\frac{q}{r}}.

Finally, if
|J|<|Q|

\displaystyle|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\int_{J\setminus Q^{*}}|M_{\varphi}f(x)|^{q}dx

\displaystyle\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\,|Q|^{q-\frac{q}{r}}\,\ell_{Q}^{\,-nq}\,\ |J\backslash Q^{\ast}|\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}^{q}\,|Q|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-\frac{q}{r}},

which concludes the proof.

Given
1\leq r\leq\infty
, we denote the atomic space
\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
by the collection of
f\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
such that

f=\sum_{Q\,:\,\text{dyadic}}s_{Q}a_{Q}\,\,\text{ in }\,\,{\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}
,
where
\{a_{Q}\}_{Q}
are
(q,\lambda,r)
-atoms and
\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}
is a sequence of complex scalars satisfying

\|\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}\|_{{\lambda,q}}:=\sup_{J}\left\{\left({|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}Q\subseteq J\end{subarray}}\left(|Q|^{\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{\lambda}}\,|s_{Q}|\right)^{q}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\right\}<\infty.

The functional
\|f\|_{\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,r}}:=\inf\left\{\|\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}\|_{{\lambda,q}}:f=\sum_{Q}s_{Q}a_{Q}\right\}
,
where the infimum is taken over all such atomic representations, defines a quasi-norm in
\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

then
\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,r_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is continuously embedded in
\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,r_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. The converse of this simple embedding is the content of the next result.

Lemma 1

Let
0<q\leq 1\leq r
with
q<r
and
q\leq\lambda<\infty
. Then
\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})=\textbf{at}\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda,\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
with comparable quasi-norms.

Proof

The proof is based on the corresponding theorem for Hardy spaces (see
(
CF
, Theorem 4.10)
). Let
a_{Q}
to be a
(q,\lambda,r)-
atom and we show that
a_{Q}=\sum_{j}s_{Q_{j}}a_{Q_{j}}
, where
\{a_{Q_{j}}\}_{j}
are
(q,\lambda,\infty)-

\|\{s_{Q_{j}}\}_{j}\|_{q,\lambda}\leq C
independently. Consider
b_{Q}=|Q|^{1/\lambda}\,a_{Q}
and since
\int_{Q}|b_{Q}(x)|^{r}dx\leq|Q|

of disjoint dyadic cubes (subcubes of

and
\big|\bigcup_{j}Q_{j}\big|\leq\alpha^{-r}\int_{Q}|b_{Q}(x)|^{r}dx\leq|Q|\,\alpha^{-r}

\mathcal{P}_{N_{q}}
to be the space of polynomials in

with degree at most

and
\mathcal{P}_{N_{q},j}
its restriction to
Q_{j}
. Since
\mathcal{P}_{N_{q},j}
is a subspace of the Hilbert space

, let
P_{Q_{j}}b\mathcal{P}_{N_{q},j}
to be the unique polynomial such that

for all

.

Now we write
b_{Q}=g_{0}+\sum_{j}h_{j}
, where
h_{j}(x)=[b_{Q}(x)-P_{Q_{j}}(b)(x)]\mathds{1}_{Q_{j}}(x)
and
g_{0}(x)=b_{Q}(x)
if

and
g_{0}(x)=P_{Q_{j}}(b)(x)
if

almost everywhere (see

\displaystyle\left(\fint_{Q_{j}}{|h_{j}(x)|^{r}dx}\right)^{{1}/{r}}\leq\left(\fint_{Q_{j}}{|b_{Q}(x)|^{r}dx}\right)^{{1}/{r}}+\left(\fint_{Q_{j}}{|g_{0}(x)|^{r}dx}\right)^{{1}/{r}}\leq c\alpha.

For each

, let

. Applying the previous argument for each

\displaystyle\displaystyle{b_{Q}=g_{0}+(c\alpha)\sum_{j_{0}}{b_{j_{0}}}=g_{0}+c\alpha\sum_{j_{0}}g_{j_{0}}+(c\alpha)^{2}\sum_{j_{0},j_{1}}b_{j_{0},j_{1}}},

\displaystyle{\alpha^{r}\leq\fint_{Q_{j_{0},j_{1}}}{|b_{j_{0}}(x)|^{r}dx}\leq 2^{n}\alpha^{r}}

\displaystyle b_{Q}=g_{i_{0}}+c\alpha\sum_{i_{1}}g_{i_{1}}+(c\alpha)^{2}\sum_{i_{2}}g_{i_{2}}+\cdots+(c\alpha)^{k-1}\sum_{i_{k-1}}g_{i_{k-1}}+(c\alpha)^{k}\sum_{i_{k}}g_{i_{k}}+\cdots\;

and

\displaystyle a_{Q}

\displaystyle=s_{i_{0}}a_{i_{0}}+\sum_{i_{1}}s_{i_{1}}a_{i_{1}}+\sum_{i_{2}}s_{i_{2}}a_{i_{2}}+\cdots+\sum_{i_{k}}s_{i_{k}}a_{i_{k}}+\cdots

(9)

where each coefficient

\displaystyle|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\sum_{Q_{i_{k}}\subseteq J}|s_{i_{k}}|^{q}|Q_{i_{k}}|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}

2.2

Definition 2

Let

with
q<r
,
q\leq\lambda<\infty
, and

. A function

is called a
(q,\lambda,{s},r)-
molecule in
\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, or simply an

molecule, if there exist a cube

such that

(M_{1})\ \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}{|m(x)|^{r}dx}\lesssim\,\ell_{Q}^{\ n\left(1-\frac{r}{\lambda}\right)}\quad(M_{2})\ \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|m(x)|^{r}|x-x_{Q}|^{s}dx\lesssim\,\ell_{Q}^{\ s+n\left(1-\frac{r}{\lambda}\right)}

and also the concelation condition
\displaystyle(M_{3})\ \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}{m(x)x^{\alpha}dx}=0
for all
|\alpha|\leq N_{q}
.

Let

and

Proof

The proof follows the corresponding result for for Hardy spaces
(

. Let
m
to be a
(q,\lambda,{s},r)-
molecule centered in the cube
Q
. For each

, let
Q_{j}:=Q(x_{Q},\ell_{j})
in which
\ell_{j}=2^{j}\ell_{Q}
. Consider the collection of annulus
\{E_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{N}_{0}}
given by

and

for

, and let

in

uniquely determined in

such that

\displaystyle(2^{j}\ell_{Q})^{|\gamma|}|\phi_{\gamma}^{j}(x)|\lesssim 1\quad\text{ and }\quad\frac{1}{|E_{j}|}\int_{E_{j}}\phi^{j}_{\gamma}(x)x^{\beta}dx=\begin{cases}1,&\gamma=\beta\\
0,&\gamma\neq\beta\end{cases}

(10)

where the implicit constant is uniformly on
E_{j}
. Let
{m_{\gamma}^{j}=\fint_{E_{j}}m_{j}(x)x^{\gamma}dx}
and consider
{P_{j}(x)=\sum_{|\gamma|\leq N_{q}}m_{\gamma}^{j}\phi_{\gamma}^{j}(x)}
. Splitting

,
with convergence in
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, we claim that for each
j
,
m_{j}-P_{j}
is multiple of a
(q,\lambda,r)
-atom and
P_{j}
is a finite linear combination of
(q,\lambda,\infty)
-atoms.

For the first sum, since
m_{j}
and
P_{j}
are supported on
E_{j}
, so is
m_{j}-P_{j}
and by definition one has the desired vanish moments up to the order
N_{q}
. It remains show that
m_{j}-P_{j}
satisfies the size estimate. Indeed, from conditions
(M_{1})
and
(M_{2})
it follows that for every

\|m_{j}\|_{L^{r}}\lesssim|E_{j}|^{\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\lambda}}\ (2^{j})^{-\frac{s}{r}+n\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}\right)}.

(11)

Also, from (
10
) it follows
|P_{j}(x)|\leq\Big(\sum_{|\beta|\leq N_{q}}|\phi_{\beta}^{j}(x)|2^{j|\beta|}\Big)\fint_{E_{j}}|m_{j}(x)|dx\lesssim|E_{j}|^{-\frac{1}{r}}\|m_{j}\|_{L^{r}}
, where the implicit constants are independent of
j
. Hence, if we write
(m_{j}-P_{j})(x)=d_{j}\,a_{Q_{j}}(x)
for

d_{j}=\|m_{j}-P_{j}\|_{L^{r}}\,|Q_{j}|^{\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}}
and
a_{Q_{j}}=\frac{m_{j}-P_{j}}{\|m_{j}-P_{j}\|_{L^{r}}}\ |Q_{j}|^{\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\lambda}}
,
for each
j\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, it is clear that
\{a_{Q_{j}}\}_{j}
is a sequence of
(q,\lambda,r)
-atoms supported on
Q_{j}
. Moreover, from (
11
) we have

\|m_{j}-P_{j}\|_{L^{r}}\lesssim\|m_{j}\|_{L^{r}}\lesssim|Q_{j}|^{\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\lambda}}\ (2^{j})^{-\frac{s}{r}+n\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}\right)}.

Hence, since
s>n\left({r}/{q}-1\right)

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|d_{j}|^{q}|Q_{j}|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle\lesssim|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(2^{j})^{q\left[-\frac{s}{r}+n\left(\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{r}\right)\right]}\lesssim|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}.

For the second sum, let
\psi_{\gamma}^{j}(x):=N_{\gamma}^{j+1}\left[|E_{j+1}|^{-1}\phi_{\gamma}^{j+1}(x)-|E_{j}|^{-1}\phi_{\gamma}^{j}(x)\right]
, where

=\sum_{k=j}^{\infty}{\int_{E_{k}}{m_{Q}(x)x^{\gamma}dx}}
.
Then, we can represent
P_{j}
(using the vanish moments (

\displaystyle{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}P_{j}(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}{\sum_{|\gamma|\leq N_{q}}{\psi_{\gamma}^{j}(x)}}}
. The function
\psi_{\alpha}^{j}
is supported on
E_{j+1}
and by construction also satisfies vanish moments conditions up to the order
N_{q}
. It remain to check the size condition.
Since
|\gamma|\leq n\left(1/\lambda-1\right)
and
s>n\left(r/q-1\right)
we have

|N_{\gamma}^{j+1}|\leq|Q_{j}|^{1-{1}/{\lambda}}\ (2^{j}\ell_{Q})^{|\gamma|}(2^{j})^{-{s}/{r}+n\left({1}/{\lambda}-{1}/{r}\right)}
.
The previous estimate and
(2^{j}\ell_{Q})^{|\gamma|}|\phi_{\gamma}^{j}(x)|\leq C
yields for all

\left|N^{j+1}_{\gamma}|E_{j}|^{-1}\phi_{\gamma}^{j}(x)\right|\leq C|Q_{j}|^{-\frac{1}{\lambda}}(2^{j})^{-\frac{s}{r}+n\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}\right)}.

Let
\psi_{\gamma}^{j}=t_{j}\,b_{\gamma}^{j}
, where

and

.
Hence, we can write

\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}P_{j}(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}{\sum_{|\gamma|\leq N_{q}}}t_{j}\,b_{\gamma}^{j}(x)
,
and for each

(q,\lambda,\infty)-

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|t_{j}|^{q}|Q_{j}|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}=|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(2^{j})^{q\left(-\frac{s}{r}+n\left(\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{r}\right)\right)}\lesssim|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}.

Now we ready to announce a molecular decomposition in Hardy-Morrey spaces.

Theorem 2.2

Let
\left\{m_{Q}\right\}_{Q}
be a collection of
L^{r}-
molecules and
\left\{s_{Q}\right\}_{Q}
be a sequence of complex numbers such that
\|\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}\|_{\lambda,q}<\infty
. If the series
f=\sum_{Q}s_{Q}m_{Q}
converges in
\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and
\lambda<r
, then
f\in\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and moreover,
\|f\|_{\mathcal{HM}_{q}^{\lambda}}\lesssim\|\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}\|_{\lambda,q}
with implicit constant independent of
f
.

Proof

Suppose
f=\sum_{Q}s_{Q}m_{Q}
in
\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and
\|\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}\|_{\lambda,q}<\infty
. Since
0<q\leq 1
, for a fixed dyadic cube

we may estimate
\int_{J}|M_{\varphi}f(x)|^{q}dx
by

\displaystyle\sum_{Q\subseteq J}|s_{Q}|^{q}\int_{J}|M_{\varphi}m_{Q}(x)|^{q}dx+\sum_{J\subset Q}|s_{Q}|^{q}\int_{J}|M_{\varphi}m_{Q}(x)|^{q}dx

\displaystyle:=I_{1}+I_{2}.

Estimate of
I_{1}
. From Lemma
2
, write
m_{Q}=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}d_{{}_{j}}a_{Q_{j}}
(convergence in
L^{r}
)
where
\left\{a_{Q_{j}}\right\}_{j}
are
(q,\lambda,r)-
atoms and moreover
\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|d_{j}|^{q}|Q_{j}|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\lesssim|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}
.
It follows from analogous estimates of Proposition
1
that

\displaystyle I_{1}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{Q\subseteq J}|s_{Q}|^{q}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|d_{Q_{j}}|^{q}\int_{J}|M_{\varphi}a_{Q_{j}}(x)|^{q}dx\lesssim\sum_{Q\subseteq J}|s_{Q}|^{q}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}|d_{Q_{j}}|^{q}|Q_{j}|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{Q\subseteq J}|s_{Q}|^{q}|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\lesssim|J|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\,\|\{s_{Q}\}_{Q}\|_{\lambda,q}^{q}.

Estimate of
I_{2}
.
Since
1<r<\infty
and
M_{\varphi}
is bounded on
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, it follows

\displaystyle|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\int_{J}{|M_{\varphi}m_{Q}(x)|^{q}dx}

\displaystyle\leq|J|^{q\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}\right)}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(2^{j}\ell_{Q})^{-s}\int_{E_{j}}{|m_{Q}(x)|^{r}|x-x_{Q}|^{s}dx}\right)^{\frac{q}{r}}

\displaystyle\lesssim|J|^{q\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}\right)}\,|Q|^{q\left(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\lambda}\right)}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}2^{-js}\right)^{\frac{q}{r}}\simeq\left(\frac{|J|}{|Q|}\right)^{q\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-\frac{1}{r}\right)}.

If
r=1
and

, we proceed like in (
6
) and then
|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\int_{J}{|M_{\varphi}m_{Q}(x)|^{q}dx}\lesssim

\displaystyle|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\left[|J|\int_{0}^{|Q|^{1-\frac{1}{\lambda}}|J|^{-1}}\omega^{q-1}d\omega+|Q|^{-1+\frac{1}{\lambda}}\int_{|Q|^{1-\frac{1}{\lambda}}|J|^{-1}}^{\infty}\omega^{q-2}d\omega\right]\lesssim\left(\frac{|J|}{|Q|}\right)^{q\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}-1\right)}.

Fixed a dyadic cube
J
, we point out there exists a subset
N\subseteq\mathbb{N}
such that each cube
J\subset Q
is uniquely determined by a dyadic cube
Q_{k,J}\in\big\{Q\;\text{dyadic}:J\subset Q\text{ and }\ell_{Q}=2^{k}\ell_{J}\big\}
. Hence, we can write

\displaystyle{\sum_{J\subset Q}|s_{Q}|^{q}\left(\frac{|J|}{|Q|}\right)^{\gamma}=\sum_{k\in N}|s_{Q_{k,J}}|^{q}\,2^{-kn\gamma}}

with
\gamma:={1}/{\lambda}-{1}/{r}>0
. Then,

\displaystyle|J|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}\sum_{J\subset Q}|s_{Q}|^{q}\|M_{\varphi}(a_{Q})\|_{L^{q}(J)}^{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{k\in N}\left(|s_{Q_{k,J}}|^{q}|Q_{k,J}|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\right)|Q_{k,J}|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}2^{-kn\gamma}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k\in N}\left(\sum_{Q\subseteq Q_{k,J}}|s_{Q}|^{q}|Q|^{1-\frac{q}{\lambda}}\right)|Q_{k,J}|^{\frac{q}{\lambda}-1}2^{-kn\gamma}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\left\{s_{Q}\right\}_{Q}\|_{\lambda,q}^{q}\sum_{k\in N}2^{-kn\gamma}\lesssim\|\left\{s_{Q}\right\}_{Q}\|_{\lambda,q}^{q}.
