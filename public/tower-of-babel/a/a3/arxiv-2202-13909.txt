Introduction

In this paper,
{\mathbb{D}}
always denotes the open unit disk and
\partial{\mathbb{D}}
denotes the unit circle in the complex plane
\mathbb{C}
. Let
H({\mathbb{D}})
be the space of all analytic functions on
{\mathbb{D}}
. The space
H^{\infty}({\mathbb{D}})
consists of all bounded analytic functions on
{\mathbb{D}}
. The Hardy space
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
consists of all analytic functions
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}z^{n}
on
\mathbb{D}
such that

\|f\|^{2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left|a_{n}\right|^{2}<\infty.

For any
w\in{\mathbb{D}}
, the function
K_{w}={1\over 1-\bar{w}z}
is the reproducing kernel at
w
in
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
such that

\langle f,K_{w}\rangle=f(w)

for any
f\in H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
.

Let
\varphi
be an analytic self-map of
{\mathbb{D}}
. A composition operator
C_{\varphi}
on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
is given by

C_{\varphi}f=f\circ\varphi.

The operator
C_{\varphi}
is automatically bounded on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
. Let
\varphi
be an analytic self-map of
{\mathbb{D}}
and
\psi\in H({\mathbb{D}})
. The weighted composition operator
W_{\psi,\varphi}
is defined by

W_{\psi,\varphi}f(z)=\psi(z)f(\varphi(z)),~~~~~~~z\in{\mathbb{D}},~~~~~f\in H({\mathbb{D}}).

Assuming that
W_{\psi,\varphi}
is bounded on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
, then

W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}K_{w}(z)=\overline{\psi(w)}K_{\varphi(w)}(z)

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
.
In recent decades, the study of composition operators and weighted composition operators has been widely concerned and developed rapidly. See
[
4
,
20
]
and reference therein for general background of them.

For
g\in L^{\infty}(\partial{\mathbb{D}})
, the Toeplitz operator
T_{g}
is given by
T_{g}f=P(fg)
for
f\in H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
, where
P
is the orthogonal projection of
L^{2}
onto
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
.
T_{\psi}f=\psi f
when
\psi\in H^{\infty}
. Therefore, we occasionally write
W_{\psi,\varphi}=T_{\psi}C_{\varphi}
when
\psi\in H^{\infty}
. In addition,

T_{g}^{\ast}K_{w}=\overline{g(w)}K_{w}

for any
w\in{\mathbb{D}}
and
g\in H^{\infty}({\mathbb{D}})
.

A conjugate-linear (anti-linear) operator
C:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}
is called a conjugation if it satisfies

\langle Cx,Cy\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}=\langle y,x\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}

for any
x,y\in\mathcal{H}
and
C^{2}=I_{\mathcal{H}}
, where
I_{\mathcal{H}}
is an identity operator on
\mathcal{H}
.
It is easy to check that
(Jf)(z)=\overline{f(\bar{z})}
is a conjugation on the space
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
be a separable complex Hilbert space and the space of all bounded linear operators on
\mathcal{H}
, respectively. An operator
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
is said to be normal if
TT^{\ast}=T^{\ast}T
. Let

be a conjugation on
\mathcal{H}
and

.

is said to be

-symmetric if

.

is said to be complex symmetric if there exists a conjugation

such that

is
C
-symmetric. Any normal composition operator induced by
\varphi(z)=\alpha z
with
|\alpha|\leq 1
is complex symmetric. In fact, the class of complex symmetric operators contain those normal operators, Hankel operators, binormal operators, compressed Toeplitz operators and the Volterra integration operator. For applications and further information about complex symmetric operators, see
[

,

,

,

,

, Ptak, Simik and Wicher introduced a new interesting class of operators, named

Obviously,

2.
C
-normal composition operators

In this section, we study
C
-normal composition operators on the Hardy space
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
when the conjugation
C
is defined by

\mathcal{A}_{u,v}f(z)=u(z)\overline{f(\overline{v(z)})},\,\,f\in H^{2}({\mathbb{D}}),

where
u:{\mathbb{D}}\to\mathbb{C}
and
v:{\mathbb{D}}\to{\mathbb{D}}
are analytic functions. It is clear that
\mathcal{A}_{u,v}=J
when
u(z)\equiv 1
and
v(z)=z.

Lemma 1
.

[
15
, Lemma 2.11]

Let
u:{\mathbb{D}}\to\mathbb{C}
and
v:{\mathbb{D}}\to{\mathbb{D}}
are analytic functions. Then
\mathcal{A}_{u,v}:H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})\to H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
is a conjugation if and only if either of the following holds.

(i)

There is
\beta,\mu\in\partial{\mathbb{D}}
such that for any
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
,

u(z)=\beta{~and~}v(z)=\mu z.

(ii)

There is
p\in{\mathbb{D}}\setminus\{0\}
and
\beta\in\partial{\mathbb{D}}
such that for any
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
,

u(z)=\beta{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-pz}{~and~}v(z)={p\over\bar{p}}{\bar{p}-z\over 1-pz}.

Lemma 2
.

[
11
, Lemma 2]

Suppose that

\xi_{p}(z)={\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-\bar{p}z}\,\,\,and\,\,\,\tau_{p}(z)={\lambda(p-z)\over 1-\bar{p}z}\in{\rm{Aut}({\mathbb{D}})},

where
p\in{\mathbb{D}}
and
|\lambda|=1
. Then
JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}
is a conjugation on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
if and only if
\lambda p=\bar{p}
.

As a direct consequence of Lemma
1
, there are only two classes of conjugations
\mathcal{A}_{u,v}
on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
. In the first class, choosing
u(z)=1
and
v(z)=\mu z
, we obtain that

J_{\mu}f(z)=\mathcal{A}_{u,v}f(z)=\overline{f(\mu\bar{z})}

is a conjugation on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
. In particular,
J=J_{1}
. In the second class, we choose

u(z)={\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-pz}{~and~}v(z)={p\over\bar{p}}{\bar{p}-z\over 1-pz}.

Applying Lemmas
1
and
2
, we see that
\mathcal{A}_{u,v}=JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}

is a conjugation on
H^{2}({\mathbb{D}})
, where
p\in{\mathbb{D}}\setminus\{0\}
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
\varphi(z)={az+b\over cz+d}
be a linear fractional self-map of
{\mathbb{D}}
. Then
C_{\varphi}
is
J_{\mu}
-normal if and only if
C_{\varphi}
is normal.

Proof.

Assume first that
C_{\varphi}
is
J_{\mu}
-normal. Let

\sigma(z)={\bar{a}z-\bar{c}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}},~~g(z)={1\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}},~~h(z)=cz+d.

For any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
, we have

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}J_{\mu}K_{w}(z)=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}\overline{K_{w}(\mu\bar{z})}=C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}{1\over 1-\bar{\mu}wz}

(1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}K_{\mu\bar{w}}(z)=K_{\varphi{(\mu\bar{w})}}(\varphi(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\displaystyle 1\over 1-{\overline{a\mu}w+\bar{b}\over\overline{c\mu}w+\bar{d}}\cdot{az+b\over cz+d}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\displaystyle(\overline{c\mu}w+\bar{d})(cz+d)\over(\overline{c\mu}w+\bar{d})(cz+d)-(\overline{a\mu}w+\bar{b})(az+b)}.

For
\bar{a}w\neq\bar{c}
, Lemma 2.1 in
[
12
]
yields that

\displaystyle J_{\mu}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}C_{\varphi}K_{w}(z)

(2)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle J_{\mu}\left(-c{\overline{g(w)}\over\overline{\sigma(w)}}K_{\varphi(0)}(z)+h\left({1\over\overline{\sigma(w)}}\right)\overline{g(w)}K_{\varphi({\sigma(w)})}(z)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\bar{c}{g(w)\over\sigma(w)}K_{\mu\overline{\varphi(0)}}({z})+\overline{h({1\over\overline{\sigma(w)}})}g(w)K_{\mu\overline{\varphi({\sigma(w)})}}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\displaystyle\bar{c}\over\bar{c}-\bar{a}w}\cdot{1\over 1-{b\over d}\mu z}+\left({\bar{d}\over\bar{d}-\bar{b}w}-{\bar{c}\over\bar{c}-\bar{a}w}\right)\cdot{1\over 1-\mu z\cdot{(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})w+b\bar{d}-a\bar{c}\over(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)w+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}}}.

Since
C_{\varphi}
is
J_{\mu}
-normal, then

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}J_{\mu}K_{w}(z)=J_{\mu}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}C_{\varphi}K_{w}(z)

(3)

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
. Taking
w=0
, we have

\displaystyle{\bar{d}(cz+d)\over\bar{d}(cz+d)-\bar{b}(az+b)}={d\over d-b\mu z}

for any
z\in{\mathbb{D}}
. Noting that the coefficient of constant in the last equality must be zero, which means that
|b|^{2}d=0
. Thus,
b=0
. Then

\left|d\over a\right|\geq\left|c\over a\right|+1.

(
1
), (
2
) and (
3
) deduce that

\displaystyle{(\overline{c\mu}w+\bar{d})(cz+d)\over(\overline{c\mu}w+\bar{d})(cz+d)-|a|^{2}\bar{\mu}wz}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bar{c}\over\bar{c}-\bar{a}w}+\left(1-{\bar{c}\over\bar{c}-\bar{a}w}\right)\cdot{1\over 1-\bar{\mu}z\cdot{|a|^{2}w-a\bar{c}\over\bar{a}cw+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bar{c}\over\bar{c}-\bar{a}w}-{\bar{a}w\over\bar{c}-\bar{a}w}\cdot{\bar{a}cw+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}\over\bar{a}cw+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}-(|a|^{2}w-a\bar{c})\bar{\mu}z},

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
, which implies that

\displaystyle{\displaystyle(\overline{c\mu}w+\bar{d})(cz+d)(\bar{c}-\bar{a}w)-\bar{c}[(-|a|^{2}\bar{\mu}w+|c|^{2}\bar{\mu}w+\bar{d}c)z+|d|^{2}+\bar{c}d\bar{\mu}w]\over[(-|a|^{2}\bar{\mu}w+|c|^{2}\bar{\mu}w+\bar{d}c)z+|d|^{2}+\bar{c}d\bar{\mu}w](\bar{c}-\bar{a}w)}

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\displaystyle-\bar{a}w(\bar{a}cw+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2})\over\bar{a}cw+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}-(|a|^{2}w-a\bar{c})\bar{\mu}z}

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
. Noting that the coefficient of
z^{2}
in (
4
) must be zero, that is

\displaystyle(-|c|^{2}\overline{a\mu}w^{2}-\bar{a}c\bar{d}w+\bar{c}|a|^{2}\bar{\mu}w)(|a|^{2}w-ac)=0

(5)

for any
w\in{\mathbb{D}}
. Similarly, the coefficient of
w^{3}
in (
5
) must be zero, that is,
\bar{a}|c|^{2}|a|^{2}\bar{\mu}^{2}=0
. Since
|\mu|=1
and
a\neq 0
, we get
c=0
. Therefore,
\varphi(z)=\alpha z
with
|\alpha|\leq 1
and Lemma
3
gives that
C_{\varphi}
is normal.

Conversely, suppose that
C_{\varphi}
is normal. Lemma
3
yields that
\varphi(z)=\alpha z
with
|\alpha|\leq 1
. Then

C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}J_{\mu}K_{w}(z)={1\over 1-|a|^{2}\bar{\mu}wz}=J_{\mu}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}C_{\varphi}K_{w}(z)

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
. So
C_{\varphi}
is
J_{\mu}
-normal. The proof is complete.

Lemma 4

(ii)

(i)

, we have

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}K_{w}(z)=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}J{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-\bar{p}z}\cdot{1\over{1-{\bar{w}\lambda(p-z)\over 1-\bar{p}z}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}J{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-\overline{wp}+(\bar{w}\lambda-\bar{p})z}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-wp+(w\bar{\lambda}-p)z}

\displaystyle{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-wp}C_{\varphi}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}K_{\eta}(z)=\overline{\xi_{p}(\bar{w})}K_{\varphi{(\eta)}}(\varphi(z)).

(ii)

\displaystyle JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}C_{\varphi}^{\ast}C_{\varphi}K_{w}(z)

\displaystyle JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}\left(-c{\overline{g(w)}\over\overline{\sigma(w)}}K_{\varphi(0)}(z)+h({1\over\overline{\sigma(w)}})\overline{g(w)}K_{\varphi({\sigma(w)})}(z)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\overline{\xi_{p}(\bar{z})}\left(-\bar{c}{g(w)\over\sigma(w)}K_{\overline{\varphi(0)}}(\overline{\tau_{p}{(\bar{z})}})+\overline{h({1\over\overline{\sigma(w)}})}g(w)K_{\overline{\varphi({\sigma(w)})}}(\overline{\tau_{p}{(\bar{z})}})\right).

(6)

Proof.

3.
C
-normal weighted composition operators

In this section, we characterize
J_{\mu}
-normal and
JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}
-normal weighted composition operators
W_{\psi,\varphi}
which are induced by linear fractional self-map
\varphi
of
{\mathbb{D}}
and specific function
\psi
, which given by
\psi(z)=\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(z)
.

Theorem 4
.

Let
\varphi(z)={az+b\over cz+d}
be a linear fractional self-map of
{\mathbb{D}}
and
\psi(z)=\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(z)
, where
\beta
is a non-zero constant and
\sigma(z)={\bar{a}z-\bar{c}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}
. Then
W_{\psi,\varphi}
is
J_{\mu}
-normal if and only if

|b|=|c|~and~(\bar{c}d-\bar{a}b)\bar{\mu}=\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d.

Proof.

For any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
, we have

\displaystyle J\mu W_{\psi,\varphi}W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}k_{w}(z)=J\mu W_{\psi,\varphi}\overline{\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(w)}K_{\varphi(w)}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\beta}J\mu\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(z)K_{\overline{\sigma(0)}}(w)K_{\varphi(w)}(\varphi(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\beta|^{2}\overline{K_{\sigma(0)}(\mu\bar{z})}K_{\sigma(0)}(w)\overline{K_{\varphi(w)}(\varphi(\mu\bar{z}))}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\beta|^{2}K_{\sigma(0)}(w)K_{\mu\overline{\sigma(0)}}(z)K_{\overline{\varphi(w)}}(\overline{\varphi(\mu\bar{z})})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}\over 1+{c\over d}w}\cdot{1\over 1+{\bar{c}\over\bar{d}}\bar{\mu}z}\cdot{1\over 1-{aw+b\over cw+d}{\overline{a\mu}z+\bar{b}\over\overline{c\mu}z+\bar{d}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}|d|^{2}\over(d+cw)(\bar{d}+\overline{c\mu}z)-(aw+b)(\overline{a\mu}z+\bar{b})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}|d|^{2}\over[(|c|^{2}-|a|^{2})\bar{\mu}w+(\bar{c}d-\bar{a}b)\bar{\mu}]z+(c\bar{d}-a\bar{b})w+|d|^{2}-|b|^{2}}.

Let
g(z)={1\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}
and
h(z)=cz+d
. Noting that

W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}=T_{g}C_{\sigma}T_{h}^{\ast}T_{\psi}^{\ast}{\rm~and~}\beta K_{\sigma(0)}h=\beta d,

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
, we obtain that

\displaystyle W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}W_{\psi,\varphi}J_{\mu}K_{w}(z)=W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}W_{\psi,\varphi}\overline{K_{w}(\mu\bar{z})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}W_{\psi,\varphi}K_{\bar{w}\mu}(z)=T_{g}C_{\sigma}T_{h}^{\ast}T_{\psi}^{\ast}\psi(z)K_{\bar{w}\mu}(\varphi(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T_{g}C_{\sigma}\left(T_{\beta K_{\sigma(0)}}T_{h}\right)^{\ast}\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(z)K_{\bar{w}\mu}(\varphi(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T_{g}C_{\sigma}|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}K_{\sigma(0)}(z)K_{\bar{w}\mu}(\varphi(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}g(z)K_{\sigma(0)}(\sigma(z))K_{\bar{w}\mu}(\varphi(\sigma(z)))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}\cdot{1\over 1+{c\over d}{\bar{a}z-\bar{c}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}}\cdot{1\over 1-w\bar{\mu}{(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})z+b\bar{d}-a\bar{c}\over(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)z+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}\over(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)z+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}-w\bar{\mu}[(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})z+b\bar{d}-a\bar{c}]}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}\over[(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\bar{\mu}w+(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)]z+(a\bar{c}-b\bar{d})\bar{\mu}w+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}}.

Therefore,
W_{\psi,\varphi}
is
J_{\mu}
-normal if and only if

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}(|c|^{2}-|a|^{2})\bar{\mu}=(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\bar{\mu}\\
(\bar{c}d-\bar{a}b)\bar{\mu}=\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d\\
c\bar{d}-a\bar{b}=(a\bar{c}-b\bar{d})\bar{\mu}\\
|d|^{2}-|b|^{2}=|d|^{2}-|c|^{2},\end{array}\right.

which is equivalent to

|b|=|c|{\rm~and~}(\bar{c}d-\bar{a}b)\bar{\mu}=\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d.

The proof is complete.
∎

Theorem 5
.

Let
p\in{\mathbb{D}}\setminus\{0\}
,
\varphi(z)={az+b\over cz+d}
be a linear fractional self-map of
{\mathbb{D}}
and
\psi(z)=\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(z)
, where
\beta
is a non-zero constant and
\sigma(z)={\bar{a}z-\bar{c}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}
. Then
W_{\psi,\varphi}
is
JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}
-normal if and only if

(|b|^{2}-|c|^{2})p=(-\bar{a}c+\bar{b}d-a\bar{b}+c\bar{d})|p|^{2}

and

(|a|^{2}-|d|^{2})|p|^{2}=(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)\bar{p}-(\bar{a}b-\bar{c}d)p.

Proof.

Assume first that
W_{\psi,\varphi}
is
JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}
-normal. For any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
, we have

\displaystyle JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}W_{\psi,\varphi}W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}k_{w}(z)=JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}W_{\psi,\varphi}\overline{\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(w)}K_{\varphi(w)}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\beta}JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}\beta K_{\sigma(0)}(z)K_{\overline{\sigma(0)}}(w)K_{\varphi(w)}(\varphi(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\beta|^{2}K_{\sigma(0)}(w)\overline{\xi_{p}(\bar{z})}K_{\overline{\sigma(0)}}(\overline{\tau_{p}{(\bar{z})}})K_{\overline{\varphi(w)}}(\overline{\varphi(\tau_{p}{(\bar{z})})})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}\over 1+{cw\over d}}\cdot{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-pz}\cdot{1\over 1+{\bar{c}\over\bar{d}}\cdot{p-\bar{\lambda}z\over 1-pz}}\cdot{1\over 1-{aw+b\over cw+d}\cdot{\overline{a\lambda}(\bar{p}-z)+\bar{b}(1-pz)\over\overline{c\lambda}(\bar{p}-z)+\bar{d}(1-pz)}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}|d|^{2}\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over(d+cw)[\bar{d}(1-pz)+\bar{c}(p-\bar{\lambda}z)]}\cdot{1\over 1-{aw+b\over cw+d}\cdot{\bar{a}p+\bar{b}-\overline{a\lambda}z-\bar{b}pz\over\bar{d}(1-pz)+\bar{c}(p-\bar{\lambda}z)}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}|d|^{2}\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over(A_{1}w+B_{1})z+C_{1}w+D_{1}},

where

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}A_{1}=(a\bar{b}-c\bar{d})p+(|a|^{2}-|c|^{2})\bar{\lambda}\\
B_{1}=(|b|^{2}-|d|^{2})p+(\bar{a}b-d\bar{c})\bar{\lambda}\\
C_{1}=c\bar{d}-a\bar{b}+(|c|^{2}-|a|^{2})p\\
D_{1}=|d|^{2}-|b|^{2}+(d\bar{c}-\bar{a}b)p.\end{array}\right.

Noting that
\beta K_{\sigma(0)}h=\beta d
and

\tau_{p}{(\overline{\varphi(\sigma(z))})}=\lambda{p(a\bar{c}-b\bar{d})\bar{z}+p(|d|^{2}-|c|^{2})-(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\bar{z}+\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d\over(a\bar{c}-b\bar{d})\bar{z}+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}-\bar{p}(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\bar{z}+\bar{p}(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)},

we get

\displaystyle W_{\psi,\varphi}^{\ast}W_{\psi,\varphi}JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}k_{w}(z)=T_{g}C_{\sigma}T_{h}^{\ast}T_{\beta K_{\sigma(0)}}^{\ast}W_{\beta K_{\sigma(0)},\varphi}J\xi_{p}(z)K_{w}(\tau_{p}(z))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\overline{\beta d}T_{g}C_{\sigma}W_{\beta K_{\sigma(0)},\varphi}\overline{\xi_{p}(\bar{z})}K_{\bar{w}}(\overline{\tau_{p}{(\bar{z})}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}T_{g}C_{\sigma}K_{\sigma(0)}(z)\overline{\xi_{p}(\overline{\varphi(z)})}K_{\bar{w}}(\overline{\tau_{p}{(\overline{\varphi(z)})}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}g(z)K_{\sigma(0)}(\sigma(z))\overline{\xi_{p}(\overline{\varphi(\sigma(z))})}K_{\bar{w}}(\overline{\tau_{p}{(\overline{\varphi(\sigma(z))})}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}\bar{d}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}\cdot{1\over 1+{c\over d}\cdot{\bar{a}z-\bar{c}\over-\bar{b}z+\bar{d}}}\cdot{\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over 1-p{(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})z+b\bar{d}-a\bar{c}\over(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)z+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}}}\cdot{1\over 1-w\overline{\tau_{p}{(\overline{\varphi(\sigma(z))})}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}|d|^{2}\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)z+|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}-p(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})z-p(b\bar{d}-a\bar{c})}\cdot{1\over 1-w\overline{\tau_{p}{(\overline{\varphi(\sigma(z))})}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{|\beta|^{2}|d|^{2}\sqrt{1-|p|^{2}}\over(A_{2}w+B_{2})z+C_{2}w+D_{2}},

where

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}A_{2}=(-\bar{a}c+\bar{b}d)p+(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\bar{\lambda}\\
B_{2}=\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d-p(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\\
C_{2}=-(|d|^{2}-|c|^{2})p+(b\bar{d}-a\bar{c})\bar{\lambda}\\
D_{2}=|d|^{2}-|c|^{2}-p(b\bar{d}-a\bar{c}).\end{array}\right.

Therefore,

W_{\psi,\varphi}
is
JW_{\xi_{p},\tau_{p}}
-normal if and only if

(A_{1}w+B_{1})z+C_{1}w+D_{1}=(A_{2}w+B_{2})z+C_{2}w+D_{2}

for any
w,z\in{\mathbb{D}}
.
This is equivalent to

A_{1}=A_{2},B_{1}=B_{2},C_{1}=C_{2},D_{1}=D_{2},

which is equivalent to

(|b|^{2}-|c|^{2})p=(-\bar{a}c+\bar{b}d-a\bar{b}+c\bar{d})|p|^{2}

and

(|a|^{2}-|d|^{2})|p|^{2}=(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)\bar{p}-(\bar{a}b-\bar{c}d)p.

The proof is complete.
∎

.

[
11

Let
\varphi(z)={az+b\over cz+d}

Theorem 6
.

-normal;

(ii)

Theorem 7
.

(ii)

is

-normal and Hermitian if and only if

[(a_{1}-|a_{0}|^{2})^{2}-1]|p|^{2}=-(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2}+1)2Re(a_{0}p).

Let

(i)

(ii)

then

(|b|^{2}-|c|^{2})p=(-\bar{a}c+\bar{b}d-a\bar{b}+c\bar{d})|p|^{2}.

By a simple calculation, we get

(|a|^{2}-|d|^{2})|p|^{2}=[(a_{1}-|a_{0}|^{2})^{2}-1]|p|^{2}\\

and

\displaystyle(\bar{a}c-\bar{b}d)\bar{p}-(\bar{a}b-\bar{c}d)p

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[-(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2})\overline{a_{0}}-\overline{a_{0}}]\bar{p}-[(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2}){a_{0}}+{a_{0}}]{p}

\displaystyle-\overline{a_{0}p}(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2}+1)-a_{0}p(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2}+1)

\displaystyle-(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2}+1)(\overline{a_{0}p}+a_{0}p)

\displaystyle-(\overline{a_{1}}-|a_{0}|^{2}+1)2Re(a_{0}p).

is

Theorem 8
.
