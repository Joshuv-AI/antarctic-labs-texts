Abstract




Abstract
This work concerns with the existence of solutions for the following class of nonlocal elliptic problems

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}(-\Delta)^{s}u+u=|u|^{p-2}u\text{ in }\Omega_{r}\\
u\geq 0\quad\text{ in }\Omega_{r}\text{ and }u\neq 0\\
u=0\quad\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\Omega_{r}\end{array}\right.,

involving the fractional Laplacian operator
(-\Delta)^{s},
where
s\in(0,1),N>2s
,
\Omega_{r}
is the half space with a hole in
\mathbb{R}^{N}
and
p\in\left(2,2_{s}^{*}\right).
The main technical approach is based on variational and topological methods.

Keywords:
Nonlocal elliptic problems, Positive high energy solution, Half space with a hole

AMS
classification: 58J05, 35J60.

1. Introduction

Let
\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}=\left\{\left(x^{\prime},x_{N}\right)\in\mathbb{R}^{N-1}\times\mathbb{R}\mid 0<x_{N}<\infty\right\}
be the upper half space.
\Omega_{r}
is an unbounded smooth domain such that

\overline{\Omega_{r}}\subset\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N},

and

\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}\setminus\overline{\Omega_{r}}\subset B_{\rho}(a_{r})\subset\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}\

with
a_{r}=(a,r)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}
.
Indeed,
\Omega_{r}
is the upper half space with a hole.

We consider the following fractional elliptic problem:

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}(-\Delta)^{s}u+u=|u|^{p-2}u\text{ in }\Omega\\
u\geq 0\quad\text{ in }\Omega_{r}\text{ and }u\neq 0\\
u=0\quad\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\Omega\end{array}\right.,

where
\Omega=\Omega_{r},\ s\in(0,1),N>2s
,
p\in\left(2,2_{s}^{*}\right),
where
2_{s}^{*}=\frac{2N}{N-2s}
is the fractional critical Sobolev exponent and
(-\Delta)^{s}
is the classical fractional Laplace operator.

When
s\nearrow 1^{-},
problem(
1. Introduction
) is related to the following elliptic problem

\displaystyle-\triangle u+u=|u|^{p-1}u,\quad x\in\Omega,\quad u\in H_{0}^{1}(\Omega)

(1.5)

When
\Omega
is a bounded domain, by applying the compactness of the embedding

1.5
). If
\Omega
is an unbounded domain, we can not obtain a solution for problem (

because the embedding

consists of the radially symmetric functions in

By the P.L.Lions’s Concentration-Compactness Principle

By moving Plane method, Gidas-Ni-Nirenberg

\displaystyle u(r)re^{r}=\gamma+o(1)\ as\ r\rightarrow\infty.
