Why does neutron transfer play different roles in sub-barrier fusion reactions 32 S+ 94,96 Zr and 40 Ca+ 94,96 Zr?
V.V.Sargsyan 1,2 , G.G.Adamian 1 , N.V.Antonenko 1 , W. Scheid 3 , and H.Q.Zhang 4

Abstract
The sub-barrier capture (fusion) reactions 32 S+ 90,94,96 Zr, 36 S+ 90,96 Zr, 40 Ca+ 90,94,96 Zr, and 48 Ca+ 90,96 Zr with positive and negative Q Q -values for neutron transfer are studied within the quantum diffusion approach and the universal fusion function representation. For these systems, the s s -wave capture probabilities are extracted from the experimental excitation functions and are also analyzed. Different effects of the positive Q x ​ n Q_{xn} -value neutron transfer in the fusion enhancement are revealed in the relatively close reactions 32 S+ 94,96 Zr and 40 Ca+ 94,96 Zr.
