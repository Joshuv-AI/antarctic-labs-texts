Light Neutralino Dark Matter in the NMSSM
Qiurong Mou, Hongyan Wu, Sibo Zheng

Abstract
The next-to-minimal supersymmetric standard model can host light neutralino dark matter with mass of order GeV scale. It is dominated by the singlino component as a result of approximate Peccei-Quinn symmetry. This paper is devoted to address the question how light such neutralino dark matter can be in the light of the LHC Run 1 data as well as the latest LUX and Xenon1T limits. In particular, we show the sensitivity of parameter space of dark matter mass with respect to Z Z boson and SM Higgs invisible decay.

IV
Conclusion

This work presented a numerical study of NMSSM with heavy soft mass parameters associated with the third generation
but light neutralino DM due to small spontaneously breaking of PQ symmetry.
Naively speaking, such light DM may be already excluded either by DM direct detection facilities or SM
Z
boson and Higgs scalar invisible decay experiments.
The main finding, however, is that both the latest SD and SI limits from LUX and Xenon1T are unable
to exclude light neutralino DM with mass of order several GeVs.
This study also demonstrates that even the further precision test on Higgs invisible decay at the HL-LHC
or LZ experiments fail to exclude such a possibility.

Mass Matrixes

The mass matrixes discussed in this appendix determine both the DM eigenstate mass
and its couplings to CP-even Higgs scalar, CP-odd scalar
A_{1}
,

Firstly, we follow Ref.
0505142
to decompose the Higgs doublet scalars
H^{0}_{u,d}
and singlet scalar
S
into the following fields,

\displaystyle{}H^{0}_{u}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\upsilon_{u}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(H_{uR}+iH_{uI}),

\displaystyle H^{0}_{d}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\upsilon_{d}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(H_{dR}+iH_{dI}),

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle s+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(S_{R}+iS_{I}),

(13)

where
\upsilon^{2}_{u}+\upsilon^{2}_{d}=(174~\text{GeV})^{2}
and

In order to eliminate the Goldstone mode, one rotates the gauge eigenstates from
(H_{uR},H_{dR},S_{R})
to
(H,h,S_{R})
via the orthogonal matrix

with
\tan\beta=\upsilon_{u}/\upsilon_{d}

Under the new basis
(H,h,S_{R})

\displaystyle{}\mathcal{M}^{2}_{S}=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}\frac{A^{2}_{\lambda}}{1+x}+(M^{2}_{Z}-\lambda^{2}\upsilon^{2})\sin^{2}2\beta&-\frac{1}{2}(M^{2}_{Z}-\lambda^{2}\upsilon^{2})\sin 4\beta&-\lambda A_{\lambda}\upsilon\cos 2\beta\\

&*&\lambda^{2}\upsilon^{2}(1+x)\\
\end{array}\right)

Here

.
After diagonalization by an orthogonal matrix

h_{i}
,
and one of them is identified as the SM-like Higgs.
The off-diagonal elements in

determine the mixing effects between these mass eigenstates,
the magnitude of which are severely upper bounded by the Higgs precision measurements at the LHC

With small mixing effects the SM-like Higgs is mainly composed of

Under basis

\displaystyle{}\mathcal{M}^{2}_{P}\simeq\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}\frac{2\mu}{\sin 2\beta}A_{\lambda}&\lambda\upsilon A_{\lambda}\\
&\frac{\lambda^{2}\upsilon^{2}A_{\lambda}\sin 2\beta}{2\mu}\\
