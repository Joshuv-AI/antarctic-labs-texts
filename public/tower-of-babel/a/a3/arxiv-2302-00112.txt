Abstract
The minimum completion (fill-in) problem is defined as follows: Given a graph family ℱ \mathcal{F} (more generally, a property Π \Pi ) and a graph G G , the completion problem asks for the minimum number of non-edges needed to be added to G G so that the resulting graph belongs to the graph family ℱ \mathcal{F} (or has property Π \Pi ). This problem is NP-complete for many subclasses of perfect graphs and polynomial solutions are available only for minimal completion sets. We study the minimum completion problem of a P 4 P_{4} -sparse graph G G with an added edge. For any optimal solution of the problem, we prove that there is an optimal solution whose form is of one of a small number of possibilities. This along with the solution of the problem when the added edge connects two non-adjacent vertices of a spider or connects two vertices in different connected components of the graph enables us to present a polynomial-time algorithm for the problem.
