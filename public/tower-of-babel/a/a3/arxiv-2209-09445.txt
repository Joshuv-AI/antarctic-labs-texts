Mirror symmetric and piecewise analytic potential

Here we explore a new exactly solvable double well potential

and its dual single well potential

\displaystyle V_{\text{D}}(x)=\text{min}[(x+d)^{2},(x-d)^{2}]=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}(x-d)^{2}&x\geq 0\\[2.0pt]

\displaystyle V_{\text{S}}(x)=\text{max}[(x+d)^{2},(x-d)^{2}]=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}(x+d)^{2}&x\geq 0\\[2.0pt]

This paper will be cited as I hereafter.

Since the min, max definitions of

and

are obviously symmetric

These potentials are obviously mirror symmetric

and analytic on either half line
x>0
and

That is, the wavefunctions on either half line are analytic functions.
That is, their wavefunctions

and
\psi_{\text{S}}(x)

\displaystyle-\frac{d^{2}\psi_{\text{D}}(x,E)}{dx^{2}}+V_{\text{D}}(x)\psi_{\text{D}}(x,E)=E\psi_{\text{D}}(x,E),\quad-\frac{d^{2}\psi_{\text{S}}(x,E)}{dx^{2}}+V_{\text{S}}(x)\psi_{\text{S}}(x,E)=E\psi_{\text{S}}(x,E),

\displaystyle\psi_{\text{D}}(x,E)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{\text{D}}^{(+)}(x,E)&x>0\\[2.0pt]

\psi_{\text{S}}^{(-)}(x,E)&x<0\end{array}\right..

Let us assume that the above wavefunctions are piecewise square integrable for both D and S,

\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\psi^{(+)}(x,E)\right)^{2}dx<\infty,\quad\int_{-\infty}^{0}\left(\psi^{(-)}(x,E)\right)^{2}dx<\infty.

(

Like other one-dimensional quantum mechanical systems with piecewise analytic potentials,

(2.6)

]
and

Due to the mirror symmetry of the potentials

\displaystyle{\rm even:}

\displaystyle\quad\psi^{(+)}(x,E)=\psi^{(-)}(-x,E),\qquad\ \ {\rm odd:}

\displaystyle\quad\psi^{(+)}(x,E)=-\psi^{(-)}(-x,E).

(2.7)

The continuity of the wavefunctions and their first derivatives provides the equations determining the eigenvalue

\displaystyle{\rm even:}

\displaystyle\quad\psi^{(+)}(0,E)=\psi^{(-)}(0,E),\qquad\ \ \frac{d\psi^{(+)}(0,E)}{dx}=0,

\displaystyle{\rm odd:}

\displaystyle\quad\frac{d\psi^{(+)}(0,E)}{dx}=\frac{d\psi^{(-)}(0,E)}{dx},\qquad\psi^{(+)}(0,E)=0.

4.1
Confluent hypergeometric functions

It is well known that
the one dimensional Schrödinger equation with the quadratic potential
x^{2}

can be rewritten as an equation of the confluent hypergeometric function
\varphi(z,E)

\displaystyle-\frac{d^{2}\psi(x,E)}{dx^{2}}+x^{2}\psi(x,E)=E\psi(x,E),\quad\psi(x,E)=e^{-x^{2}/2}\varphi(z,E),\quad z\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\text{def}}}{{=}}x^{2},

\displaystyle\hskip 85.35826pt\Longrightarrow z\frac{d^{2}\varphi(z,E)}{dz^{2}}+(b-z)\frac{d\varphi(z,E)}{dz}-a\varphi(z,E)=0,

(4.1)

\displaystyle\hskip 85.35826pta=\frac{(1-E)}{4},\quad b=\frac{1}{2}.

(4.2)

The two fundamental solutions of the above Kummer’s differential equation (
4.1

\displaystyle\varphi_{1}(z,E)

\displaystyle={}_{1}F_{1}(a,b\,;z)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{k}}{(b)_{k}}\frac{z^{k}}{k!},

(4.3)

\displaystyle\varphi_{2}(z,E)

\displaystyle=z^{1-b}{}_{1}F_{1}(a+1-b,2-b\,;z)=z^{1-b}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a+1-b)_{k}}{(2-b)_{k}}\frac{z^{k}}{k!},

The well-known piecewise square integrable combination of the fundamental solutions is

for simplicity of presentation.

Summary and Comments

Some basic facts, expressions and numbers related with the eigenvalues and eigenfunctions
of the piecewise analytic and exactly solvable
potentials
V_{\text{D}}(x)
and
V_{\text{S}}(x)
are explored.
For the applications, the norms of some of the lower lying eigenstates would be needed.
This would require a substantial work.

It is well known that a piecewise linear potential
V_{\text{L}}(x)=g^{3}|x|
is exactly solvable.
It is expected that the potentials
