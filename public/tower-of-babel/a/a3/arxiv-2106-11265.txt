Abstract

Tagging is accomplished through a partial reconstruction of semileptonic decays B s → D s ​ X ​ ℓ ​ ν B_{s}\rightarrow D_{s}X\ell\nu , where X X denotes unreconstructed additional hadrons or photons and ℓ \ell is an electron or muon. With 121.4 fb -1 of data collected at the Υ \Upsilon (5S) resonance by the Belle detector at the KEKB asymmetric-energy e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} collider, we obtain ℬ ⁡ ( B s → D s ​ X ) {\mathcal{B}}(B_{s}\rightarrow D_{s}X) = ( 60.2 ± 5.8 ± 2.3 ) (60.2\pm 5.8\pm 2.3) %, where the first uncertainty is statistical and the second is systematic.

To confirm the 3D fitting procedure and correction to
{\mathcal{B}}_{\rm raw}
, we generated ensembles of simulated data distributions with varied signal content.
Signal and crossfeed distributions were generated by randomly selecting from our large sample of MC-generated signal events.
For background we generated distributions according to those used in the fit, with parameters fixed to the results of the fit to data.
Ensembles of 200 experiments were generated for each of ten branching fractions in the range 10-100%, in 10% increments.
Each distribution was fitted according to our procedure.
The resulting ensemble mean branching fractions, corrected and plotted against input branching fractions, were fitted to a line.
This test was repeated for each of the six
D_{s}
mode combinations, as well as the combined set.
All showed consistency with a unit slope and no systematic shifts.
