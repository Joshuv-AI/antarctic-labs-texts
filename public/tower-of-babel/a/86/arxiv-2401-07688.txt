Fuzzy Set with Hyperbolic Valued Membership Function
Chinmay Ghosh, Sanjib Kumar Datta, Soumen Mondal

Abstract
In this article, we have introdued 𝔻 \mathbb{D} -fuzzy sets. We have discussed the notions of inclusion, union, intersection, complementation and convexity of such 𝔻 \mathbb{D} -fuzzy sets. Also we have proved separation theorem of convex 𝔻 \mathbb{D} -fuzzy sets. Keywords and phrases : Fuzzy set, hyperbolic number, hyperbolic valued metric, convexity, separation theorem.

Let

A

-fuzzy set

\mathbb{D}

\mathbb{D}
-membership” of

in
A.
Thus the nearer the value of

to
1

\mathbb{D}

in
A.

A
\mathbb{D}
-fuzzy set
A
in
X

\mathbb{D}
-membership function

A=\{(x,f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x)):x\in X\}.

Let

be the idempotent representation of

Then
A_{1}=\{(x,f_{A}^{1}(x):x\in X\}
and
A_{2}=\{(x,f_{A}^{2}(x):x\in X\}

two ordinary fuzzy sets.

Note that if
A
is a set in the ordinary sense, its
\mathbb{D}
-membership

f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}1\text{ if }x\in A\\

Theorem 8

If two
\mathbb{D-}
fuzzy sets

and

on the Universal sets

and

are convex then their cartesian product

is also convex on

Proof.
Let
C=A\times B.
Then

\displaystyle f_{C}^{\mathbb{D}}\{\lambda(x_{1},y_{1})+(1-\lambda)(x_{2},y_{2})\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{C}^{\mathbb{D}}\{(\lambda x_{1}+(1-\lambda)x_{2},\lambda y_{1}+(1-\lambda)y_{2})\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\{f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(\lambda x_{1}+(1-\lambda)x_{2}),f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(\lambda y_{1}+(1-\lambda)y_{2})\}

Now since

and

f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}\{\lambda x_{1}+(1-\lambda)x_{2}\}\succeq\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\left\{f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x_{1}),f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x_{2})\right\}

f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}\{\lambda y_{1}+(1-\lambda)y_{2}\}\succeq\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\left\{f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(y_{1}),f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(y_{2})\right\}.

Hence

\displaystyle f_{C}^{\mathbb{D}}\{\lambda(x_{1},y_{1})+(1-\lambda)(x_{2},y_{2})\}

\displaystyle\succeq

\displaystyle\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\{\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\left\{f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x_{1}),f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x_{2})\right\},\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\left\{f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(y_{1}),f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(y_{2})\right\}\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\{\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\left\{f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x_{1}),f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(y_{1})\right\},\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\left\{f_{A}^{\mathbb{D}}(x_{2}),f_{B}^{\mathbb{D}}(y_{2})\right\}\}.

Therefore

f_{C}^{\mathbb{D}}\{\lambda(x_{1},y_{1})+(1-\lambda)(x_{2},y_{2})\}\succeq\min\nolimits_{\mathbb{D}}\{f_{C}^{\mathbb{D}}((x_{1},y_{1})),f_{C}^{\mathbb{D}}((x_{2},y_{2}))\}.
