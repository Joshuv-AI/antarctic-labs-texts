Derivation of breakup probabilities from experimental elastic backscattering data
V.V.Sargsyan 1 , G.G.Adamian 1 , N.V.Antonenko 1 , W. Scheid 2 , and H.Q.Zhang 3

Abstract
We suggest simple and useful method to extract breakup probabilities from the experimental elastic backscattering probabilities in the reactions with toughly and weakly bound nuclei.

There is a direct relationship between the capture,
quasi-elastic scattering and breakup processes, since any losses from

\displaystyle P_{qe}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)+P_{cap}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)+P_{BU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)=

\displaystyle=P_{el}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)+P_{rest}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)+P_{BU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)=1,

Equation (1) can be rewritten as

\displaystyle\frac{P_{el}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}{1-P_{BU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}+\frac{P_{rest}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}{1-P_{BU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}=P_{el}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)+P_{rest}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)=1,

\displaystyle\frac{P_{el}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}{P_{rest}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}=\frac{P_{el}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}{P_{rest}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}=\frac{P_{el}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}{1-P_{el}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}.

\displaystyle P_{BU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)=1-\frac{P_{el}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}{P_{el}^{noBU}(E_{\rm c.m.},J)}.
