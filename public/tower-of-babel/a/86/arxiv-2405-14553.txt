Nonvanishing and Abundance for cones of movable divisors
G. Bini, M.C. Brambilla, C. Fontanari, E. Postinghel

Abstract
. We propose a generalized version of the Log Nonvanishing Conjecture and of the Log Abundance Conjecture for a klt pair ( X , Δ ) (X,\Delta) , that is: if K X + Δ ∈ Mov ¯ k ​ ( X ) K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X) , then K X + Δ ∈ Mov k ​ ( X ) K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X) . 1.
Introduction

Let
X
be a normal projective variety of dimension
n

and fix an integer
k
with
0\leq k\leq n-1
.

The cone
\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)\subset N^{1}(X)_{\mathbb{R}}
is the cone generated
by numerical classes of effective Cartier divisors
D
such that the stable
base locus of
|D|
has codimension at least
k+1
in
X
.
The cone
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
is the closure of
\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
in
N^{1}(X)_{\mathbb{R}}
.

In particular,
\textrm{Mov}^{0}(X)=\textrm{Eff}(X)
is the cone of effective divisors
and
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{0}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Eff}}(X)
is the cone of
pseudoeffective divisors. Next,
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{1}(X)
is just the standard
movable cone. Finally,
\textrm{Mov}^{n-1}(X)=\textrm{Sem}(X)

turn out to be the cone of semiample divisors by the Theorem of Zariski-Fujita (see
for instance
[
6
]
, Remark 2.1.32) and
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{n-1}(X)={\textrm{Nef}}(X)

is the nef cone.

The cones
\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
form a natural filtration:

\textrm{Sem}(X)=\textrm{Mov}^{n-1}(X)\subseteq\textrm{Mov}^{n-2}(X)\subseteq\ldots\subseteq\textrm{Mov}^{1}(X)\subseteq\textrm{Mov}^{0}(X)=\textrm{Eff}(X)

Taking closures, we obtain a similar filtration of the corresponding closed cones.

In
[
3
]
, the cones
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
were denoted by
\mathcal{D}_{k}(X)
and
called
the cones of divisors that are ample in codimension
k
, following the
terminology introduced in
[
10
]
.

If
X
is a Mori Dream Space, then we have
\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
for
k\in\{0,1,n-1\}
by definition and the same holds also for all other values of
k
by
[
3
]
, Theorem 1.1. For an arbitrary
X
, the cones
\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
need not be closed, but the canonical divisor
K_{X}
is expected to behave well at least with respect to
\textrm{Mov}^{0}(X)
and
\textrm{Mov}^{n-1}(X)
. Indeed, we have the following celebrated conjectures in birational geometry:

Conjecture 1
.

(Log Nonvanishing)

Let
(X,\Delta)
be a projective klt pair.
If
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Eff}}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{0}(X)
,
then
K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Eff}(X)=\textrm{Mov}^{0}(X)
.

Conjecture 2
.

(Log Abundance)

Let
(X,\Delta)
be a projective klt pair with
\dim(X)=n
.
If
K_{X}+\Delta\in{\textrm{Nef}}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{n-1}(X)
,
then
K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Sem}(X)=\textrm{Mov}^{n-1}(X)
.

Both conjectures are true for
n\leq 3
by
[
5
]
and
for
K_{X}+\Delta
big and arbitrary
n
by Kawamata-Shokurov
Basepoint free theorem, but are widely open in general (see
for instance
[
8
]
).

In this paper we introduce a natural generalization of the above conjectures
for
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
with
0\leq k\leq n-1
. Namely, we propose the following:

Conjecture 3
.

Let
(X,\Delta)
be a projective klt pair with
\dim(X)=n

and fix an integer
k
with
0\leq k\leq n-1
.
If
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
, then
K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
.

The statement of Conjecture
3
for
1\leq k\leq n-2
is
formally the same as the one for
k=0
and for
k=n-1
, but it turns
out to be substantially weaker. Indeed, the assumption in the case

k=n-1
is very explicit:
K_{X}+\Delta\in{\textrm{Nef}}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{n-1}(X)

means that the degree of
K_{X}+\Delta
on every irreducible curve on
X

is nonnegative, and the same is true also in the case
k=0
: indeed,

K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Eff}}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{0}(X)
means
precisely that the degree of
K_{X}+\Delta
on any irreducible member of a family
of curves covering
X
is nonnegative, by the main result in
[
2
]
.
On the other hand, such a
strong duality
between divisors and
curves on
X
may fail in the intermediate cases
1\leq k\leq n-1
even
for a toric threefold (see
[
10
]
, Example 1). A weaker form
of duality, involving divisors and curves on the (small) birational models of
X
,
still holds for any
k
, at least for Fano type varieties and Mori Dream Spaces (see
[
4
]
, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.1), and more generally
for smooth projective varieties (see

[
9
]
, Corollary 3.3 and Remark 3.4),
but such a formulation is
admittedly less effective than in the two classical cases
k=n-1
and
k=0
.

Our main result concerning Conjecture
3
is the following:

Theorem 1
.

Let
(X,\Delta)
be a projective klt pair with
\dim(X)=n

and fix an integer
k
with
0\leq k\leq n-1
.
Assume that the Log Minimal Model Program, the Log Nonvanishing Conjecture,
and the Log Abundance Conjecture hold for
every pair
(X^{\prime},\Delta^{\prime})
with

f:X\dashrightarrow X^{\prime}
an isomorphism in codimension
k
and
\Delta^{\prime}=f_{*}\Delta
.
If
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
, then
K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
.

As a consequence of Mori theory in dimension three, we obtain the following unconditional
result:

Corollary 1
.

Let
(X,\Delta)
be a projective klt pair with
\dim(X)=3
.
If
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{1}(X)
,
then
K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Mov}^{1}(X)
.

In particular, by taking
K_{X}=0
and by replacing an effective divisor
D
with

\varepsilon D
with
0<\varepsilon<<1
, we deduce for K-trivial threefolds
that an effective divisor lying on the boundary of
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{1}(X)
is indeed movable,
exactly as in the case of Fano varieties (or, more generally, of Mori Dream Spaces):

Corollary 2
.

Let
X
be a projective normal threefold with trivial canonical bundle.
Then we have
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{1}(X)\cap\textrm{Eff}(X)=\textrm{Mov}^{1}(X)
.

More generally, as a consequence of Theorem
1
, the same equality
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{1}(X)\cap\textrm{Eff}(X)=\textrm{Mov}^{1}(X)
holds for projective normal varieties

X
of arbitrary dimension with trivial canonical bundle, provided that the Log Minimal Model Program, the Log Nonvanishing Conjecture, and the Log Abundance Conjecture hold for every small birational modification
X^{\prime}
of
X
. This fact is remarkable because in the statement of the Morrison-Kawamata Cone Conjecture (see for instance
[
7
]
, Conjecture 3.3 (2)) the cone
\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{1}(X)\cap\textrm{Eff}(X)
plays a crucial role.

Finally, since by
[
1
]
the Log Minimal Model Program holds for varieties of log general type, we get:

Corollary 3
.

Let
(X,\Delta)
be a projective klt pair with
\dim(X)=n

and fix an integer
k
with
0\leq k\leq n-1
.
If
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
is big, then

K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
.

We work over the complex field
\mathbb{C}
.

2.
Proof of Theorem
1

Since
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)\subseteq\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{0}(X)=\overline{\textrm{Eff}}(X)
,
by the Log Nonvanishing assumption on
(X,\Delta)
we have
K_{X}+\Delta\in\textrm{Eff}(X)
.

We claim that there exists
f:X\dashrightarrow X^{\prime}
an isomorphism
in codimension
k
(i.e. a birational map with exceptional locus of
codimension at least
k+1
in
X
) with
K_{X^{\prime}}=f_{*}K_{X}

such that
K_{X^{\prime}}+\Delta^{\prime}
is nef, where
\Delta^{\prime}=f_{*}\Delta
.
Indeed, by the Log Minimal Model Program assumption on
(X,\Delta)

there exists a birational map
f:X\dashrightarrow X^{\prime}
with

K_{X^{\prime}}=f_{*}K_{X}
and
K_{X^{\prime}}+\Delta^{\prime}
nef.
If by contradiction there is a subvariety
V
in the exceptional
locus of
f
of codimension at most
k
in
X
, then there is a

k
-moving curve class
C
sweeping out
V
such that
(K_{X}+\Delta).C<0
.
Now, since
K_{X}+\Delta\in\overline{\textrm{Mov}}^{k}(X)
, there exists a sequence of

\mathbb{R}
-divisors
D_{i}\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
converging to
K_{X}+\Delta
;
in particular, we have
D_{i}.C<0
for any
i
sufficiently large.
It follows that
V
is contained in the (stable) base locus of

D_{i}
, a contradiction.

Next, by the Log Abundance assumption on
(X^{\prime},\Delta^{\prime})
, since

f_{*}(K_{X}+\Delta)=K_{X^{\prime}}+\Delta^{\prime}
is nef then it is semiample,
in particular
f_{*}(K_{X}+\Delta)\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X^{\prime})
. Hence we may
conclude by invoking the previous claim and applying the following
Lemma to
D:=K_{X}+\Delta
.

Lemma 1
.

Let
D
be an effective Cartier divisor on a normal projective
variety
X
and let
f:X\dashrightarrow X^{\prime}
be an isomorphism
in codimension
k
. If
f_{*}D\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X^{\prime})
, then
D\in\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
.

Proof.

By contradiction, assume that
D\notin\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X)
, so that
there exists a subvariety
W
in the stable base locus of
D

of codimension at most
k
in
X
. Since by assumption the
exceptional locus of
f
has codimension at least
k+1

in
X
, it follows that
f_{|W}:W\dashrightarrow f(W)

is birational, in particular we have
\dim f(W)=\dim W
.
We claim that
f(W)
is contained in the stable base locus
of
f_{*}(D)
. Indeed, we have
|f_{*}(D)|=f_{*}|D|

since
f
is an isomorphism in codimension
k\geq 1
and
f(W)

is contained in every element of
f_{*}|D|
since

W
is contained in every element of
|D|
.
From the claim it follows that
f_{*}D\notin\textrm{Mov}^{k}(X^{\prime})
,
a contradiction.

∎
