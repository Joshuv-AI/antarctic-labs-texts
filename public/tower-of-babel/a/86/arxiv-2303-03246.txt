5
The Existence in the critical cases

For
\mu\geq\mu_{0}
,
let
\tau\in(-N,2s)
,

v_{\tau}(x)=|x|^{\tau},\hskip 9.24994pt\forall\,x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\}

and a positive integer
m
,

w_{\tau,m}(x)=|x|^{\tau}(-\ln|x|)^{m},\hskip 9.24994pt\forall\,x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\}.

It is known from
[
13
, Lemma 2.1,  2.3]
that
for
\tau\in(-N,2s)
, there holds that

(-\Delta)^{s}v_{\tau}(x)=c_{s}(\tau)|x|^{\tau-2s},\hskip 9.24994pt\ \forall\,x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\}

with

c_{s}(\tau)=2^{2s}\frac{\Gamma(\frac{N+\tau}{2})\Gamma(\frac{2s-\tau}{2})}{\Gamma(-\frac{\tau}{2})\Gamma(\frac{N-2s+\tau}{2})}.

(5.1)

The function
c_{s}:(-N,2s)\mapsto\mathbb{R}

is strictly concave and uniquely maximized at the point
\frac{2s-N}{2}
with the maximal value
2^{2s}\frac{\Gamma^{2}(\frac{N+2s}{4})}{\Gamma^{2}(\frac{N-2s}{4})}.

Moreover,

c_{s}(\tau)=c_{s}(2s-N-\tau)\hskip 18.49988pt{\rm for}\ \ \tau\in(-N,2s)

(5.2)

and

\lim_{\tau\to-N}c_{s}(\tau)=\lim_{\tau\to 2s}c_{s}(\tau)=-\infty.

(5.3)

For
\mu>\mu_{0}
,
-N<\tau_{-}<\tau_{+}<2s
are zero points of

c_{s}(\tau)+\mu=0.

Lemma 5.1

For any positive integer
m
, we have that

{\cal L}_{\mu}^{s}w_{\tau_{\pm},m}(x)=|x|^{\tau_{\pm}-2s}\Big(\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{\pm})(\ln|x|)^{m-i}\Big),

where

c_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{\pm})
is the
i
-th-derivative of
c_{s}
at
\tau=\tau_{\pm}
.

Furthermore,

c_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{+})<0\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm if}\ \,i\ {\rm is\ odd},\hskip 18.49988ptc_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{+})>0\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm if}\ \,i\ {\rm is\ even}

and

c_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{-})>0\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm if}\ \,i\ {\rm is\ odd},\hskip 18.49988ptc_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{-})<0\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm if}\ \,i\ {\rm is\ even}.

Proof.
Fix
x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\}
and let

F(x,\tau)=(-\Delta)^{s}v_{\tau}(x)+\frac{\mu}{|x|^{2s}}v_{\tau}(x)-c_{s}(\tau)|x|^{\tau-2s}\equiv 0\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm for}\ \,\tau\in(-N,2s),

then

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left.\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial\tau^{m}}F(x,\tau)\right|_{\tau=\tau_{\pm}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-\Delta)^{s}w_{\tau_{\pm},m}(x)+\frac{\mu}{|x|^{2s}}w_{\tau_{\pm},m}(x)-\sum^{m}_{i=0}c_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{\pm})|x|^{\tau_{\pm}-2s}(\ln|x|)^{m-i}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{s,\mu}w_{\tau_{\pm},m}(x)-\sum^{m}_{i=1}c_{s}^{(i)}(\tau_{\pm})|x|^{\tau_{\pm}-2s}(\ln|x|)^{m-i},

where we use the fact that
c_{s}^{(0)}(\tau_{+})=c_{s}(\tau_{+})=0
.

Note that

\displaystyle c_{s}(\tau)|x|^{\tau-2s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-\Delta)^{s}v_{\tau}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{C_{N,s}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|x+y|^{\tau}+|x-y|^{\tau}-2|x|^{\tau}}{|y|^{N+2s}}\,dy

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{C_{N,s}}{2}|x|^{\tau-2s}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|e_{1}+z|^{\tau}+|e_{1}-z|^{\tau}-2}{|z|^{N+2s}}\,dz\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm for}\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\},

where
e_{1}=(1,0,\cdots,0)\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
. Thus

c_{s}(\tau)=-\frac{C_{N,s}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|x-e_{1}|^{\tau}+|x+e_{1}|^{\tau}-2}{|x|^{N+2s}}\,dx.

Consequently, for
\tau\in(-N,2s)
, we have for positive integers
n
that

c_{s}^{(2n)}(\tau)=-\frac{C_{N,s}}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|e_{1}-x|^{\tau}(\log|e_{1}-x|)^{2n}+|e_{1}+x|^{\tau}(\log|e_{1}+x|)^{2n}}{|x|^{N+2s}}dx<0,

(5.4)

then
c_{s}^{(2n-2)}(\tau_{+})
is strictly concave, and by the symmetric property (
5.2
), we have that

c_{s}^{(2n-1)}(\cdot)
is strictly decreasing at
\tau=\tau_{+}
and strictly increasing at
\tau=\tau_{-}
, that is

c_{s}^{(2n-1)}(\tau_{+})<0<c_{s}^{(2n-1)}(\tau_{-}).

This completes the proof.
\Box
