On cover-free families of finite vector spaces
Yunjing Shan, Junling Zhou

Abstract
There is a large literature on cover-free families of finite sets, because of their many applications in combinatorial group testing, cryptographic and communications. This work studies the generalization of cover-free families from sets to finite vector spaces. Let V V be an n n -dimensional vector space over the finite field 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} and let [ V k ] q \left[V\atop k\right]_{q} denote the family of all k k -dimensional subspaces of V V . A family ℱ ⊆ [ V k ] q \mathcal{F}\subseteq\left[V\atop k\right]_{q} is called cover-free if there are no three distinct subspaces F 0 , F 1 , F 2 ∈ ℱ F_{0},F_{1},F_{2}\in\mathcal{F} such that F 0 ≤ ( F 0 ∩ F 1 ) + ( F 0 ∩ F 2 ) F_{0}\leq(F_{0}\cap F_{1})+(F_{0}\cap F_{2}) . A family ℋ ⊆ [ V k ] q \mathcal{H}\subseteq\left[V\atop k\right]_{q} is called a q q -Steiner system S q ​ ( t , k , n ) S_{q}(t,k,n) if for every T ∈ [ V t ] q T\in\left[V\atop t\right]_{q} , there is exactly one H ∈ ℋ H\in\mathcal{H} such that T ≤ H T\leq H . In this paper we investigate cover-free families in the vector space V V . Firstly, we determine the maximum size of a cover-free family in [ V k ] q \left[V\atop k\right]_{q} . Secondly, we characterize the structures of all maximum cover-free families which are closely related to q q -Steiner systems.

We may begin with a verification of the relationship between cover-free families of subspaces with odd dimension and those with even dimension. Meanwhile, we need to show that the structures defined by

Lemma 1.3
.

(i)

Suppose that
T\in\left[V\atop t\right]
,
R\in\left[V\atop r\right]
and
T\cap R=\{\textup{{0}}\}
. If
T\leq F\leq V
, then
F\cap(T\oplus R)=T\oplus R\cap F
.

(ii)

Suppose that
T\in\left[V\atop t\right]
,
P\in\left[V\atop p\right]
and
T\cap P=\{\textup{{0}}\}
. If
R\in\left[P\atop r\right]
and
S\in\left[P\atop s\right]
, then
(T\oplus R)\cap(T\oplus S)=T\oplus R\cap S
.

Proof.

With the assumption of (i), it is obvious that
T\oplus(R\cap F)\leq F\cap(T\oplus R)
. Let
{\rm\dim}(F)=k
. Since

\begin{array}[]{rl}{\dim}(F\cap(T\oplus R))&=k+(r+t)-{\dim}(F+R)\\[8.5359pt]
&=k+(r+t)-(k+r-{\dim}(R\cap F))\\[8.5359pt]
&=t+{\dim}(R\cap F)\\[8.5359pt]
&={\dim}(T\oplus R\cap F),\end{array}

this proves (i).

Since
R\cap S\leq R\cap(T\oplus S)
and

\begin{array}[]{rl}{\dim}(R\cap(T\oplus S))&=r+(t+s)-{\dim}(T+R+S)\\[8.5359pt]
&=r+(t+s)-(t+{\dim}(R+S)-0)\\[8.5359pt]
&={\dim}(R\cap S),\end{array}

we have
R\cap(T\oplus S)=R\cap S
. Then using the condition of (ii) and taking
F=T\oplus S
in (i) gives
(T\oplus R)\cap(T\oplus S)=T\oplus R\cap(T\oplus S)=T\oplus R\cap S
.
∎

.

Preliminaries

In this section, we recall a number of basic results and establish several new lemmas in the vector spaces, which are essential for our proofs. For a family

Theorem 2.1
.

(
[

,
[

Suppose

\left[2k-1\atop k\right],&\text{if~$2k-1\leq n\leq 2k$}.\end{cases}\

Moreover, equality holds if and only if one of the following holds:

(i)

4
Concluding remarks

In the present paper, we investigated cover-free families of finite vector spaces. To establish an upper bound of the cardinality of a cover-free family, we first simplified the problem to the case of a cover-free family of
subspaces with even dimension. Then we defined a family
\mathcal{A}(F,T)
of
t
-dimensional subspaces with special properties and made use of a weight function to get a tight upper bound of the cover-free family
\mathcal{F}\subseteq\left[V\atop 2t\right]
. Finally, we succeeded in characterizing the structures of all maximum cover-free families, which are intimately connected to
q
-Steiner systems. Our main result is Theorem
1.2
, which is a generalization of Theorem
1.1
from sets to vector spaces.

It is well-known that a
q
-Steiner system is a type of constant-dimension subspace code with the largest possible size. The present paper disclosed an intimate relationship between
q
-Steiner systems and maximum cover-free families of vector spaces. We think that it is worthwhile to explore possible applications of cover-free families of vector spaces in the field of subspace codes. On the other hand, since the knowledge of
q
-Steiner systems is very limited, the investigation on cover-free families of vector spaces with smaller cardinalities is also interesting.
