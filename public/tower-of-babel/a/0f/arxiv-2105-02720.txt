Superlattice Induced by Charge Order in the Organic Spin Chain (TMTTF) X 2 {}_{2}X ( X = X= SbF 6 , AsF 6 and PF 6 ) Revealed by High Field EPR
Charles-Emmanuel Dutoit, Anatoli Stepanov, Johan van Tol, Maylis Orio, Sylvain Bertaina

Abstract
We have investigated the charge ordering phase of the quasi one dimensional quantum antiferromagnet (TMTTF) X 2 {}_{2}X ( X = X= SbF 6 , AsF 6 and PF 6 ) using high fields/frequencies electron paramagnetic resonance. In addition to the uniform displacement of the counter anions involved in the charge order phase, we report the existence of a superlattice between the spin chains in the direction c c , caused by the space modulation of the charge order. When the field is high enough, the magnetic decoupling of the spin chains allows us to estimate the interaction between the chains, J c < 1 J_{c}<1 mK, three orders of magnitude lower than expected from the mean field theory.

3.1
Charge order transition observed by HF-EPR

The figure

Riera et Poilblanc

anions is not fully uniform and has a small modulation in space. In

3

representations of the unit cell in the CO state. In B the displacement of the
anions
X

no direct effect on the spin, the counter-anion displacement has a similar
effect on the g-tensor. In triclinic symmetry, the the orientation of g-tensor
axes is not fixed by the symmetry and the displacement of
X

of the tensor principal axes

reported for other low-symmetry systems
van Rens et al. 1970

Pilbrow and Wood 1979

configuration B the displacement is stronger than in A, which leads to a more
significant rotation of the g-tensor of the configuration B than in A.

Finally, let’s discuss why the superlattice in the

detected by other techniques. In the introduction to this paper we have
presented several techniques which have permitted he observation of the CO.
However, only few of them such as

case of

measurement of the displacement of the counter anions responsible for the CO was

modulation of the displacement would just be included in the error bars. In our

The low P
