Abstract
. This study delves into a comprehensive examination of the three-dimensional ( 3 ​ D ) (3D) incompressible magneto-hydrodynamic ( M ​ H ​ D ) (MHD) equations in H 1 ​ ( ℝ 3 ) H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3}) . The modification involves incorporating a power term in the nonlinear convection component, a particularly relevant adjustment in porous media scenarios, especially when the fluid adheres to the Darcy-Forchheimer law instead of the conventional Darcy law. It is important to note that these achievements are obtained with smallness conditions on the initial data, but under the condition that β > 3 \beta>3 and α > 0 \alpha>0 . However, when β = 3 \beta=3 , the problem is limited to the case 0 < α < 1 2 0<\alpha<\frac{1}{2} as the above inequality is unsolvable for these values of α \alpha using our method.
