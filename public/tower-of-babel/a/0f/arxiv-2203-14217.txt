Realization of zero-divisor graphs of finite commutative rings as threshold graphs
Rameez Raja, Samir Ahmad Wagay

Abstract
Let R R be a finite commutative ring with unity, and let G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) be a simple graph. The zero-divisor graph, denoted by Γ ⁡ ( R ) \Gamma(R) is a simple graph with vertex set as R R , and two vertices x , y ∈ R x,y\in R are adjacent in Γ ⁡ ( R ) \Gamma(R) if and only if x ​ y = 0 xy=0 . In [ 10 ] , the authors have studied the Laplacian eigenvalues of the graph Γ ⁡ ( ℤ n ) \Gamma(\mathbb{Z}_{n}) and for distinct proper divisors d 1 , d 2 , ⋯ , d k d_{1},d_{2},\cdots,d_{k} of n n , they defined the sets as, 𝒜 d i = { x ∈ ℤ n : ( x , n ) = d i } \mathcal{A}_{d_{i}}=\{x\in\mathbb{Z}_{n}:(x,n)=d_{i}\} , where ( x , n ) (x,n) denotes the greatest common divisor of x x and n n . In this paper, we show that the sets 𝒜 d i \mathcal{A}_{d_{i}} , 1 ≤ i ≤ k 1\leq i\leq k are actually orbits of the group action: A ​ u ​ t ​ ( Γ ⁡ ( R ) ) × R ⟶ R Aut(\Gamma(R))\times R\longrightarrow R , where A ​ u ​ t ​ ( Γ ⁡ ( R ) ) Aut(\Gamma(R)) denotes the automorphism group of Γ ⁡ ( R ) \Gamma(R) . Our main objective is to determine new classes of threshold graphs, since these graphs play an important role in several applied areas. For a reduced ring R R , we prove that Γ ⁡ ( R ) \Gamma(R) is a connected threshold graph if and only if R ≅ F q R\cong{F}_{q} or R ≅ F 2 × F q R\cong{F}_{2}\times{F}_{q} . Conclusion:
In this research article, we presented some new classes of threshold graphs. As discussed above, the construction of new classes of threshold graphs are effective in a sense that it assists to advance in coding theory and spectral analysis. We obtained the results related to reduced and local rings. In fact, we characterized all reduced rings of which zero-divisor graphs are realized as threshold graphs. Furthermore, we obtained classes of threshold graphs realized by some classes of local rings and provided an example which illustrates that a zero-divisor graph associated with some local ring is not always a threshold graph. So, it is interesting to determine all classes of local rings of which zero-divisor graphs are always threshold. This investigation has been included in the paper as a problem for future research work.
