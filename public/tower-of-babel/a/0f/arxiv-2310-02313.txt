Thermal Bekenstein-Hawking entropy from the worldsheet
Indranil Halder and, Daniel L. Jafferis

Abstract
We define and compute the leading sphere diagram contribution to the entropy of the BTZ black hole supported by Kalb-Ramond flux in bosonic string theory. In a winding condensate description, integrating exactly over the constant mode for the radial direction of AdS 3 reduces the problem to one of the correlation functions of winding operators in the free theory. The volume of the residual PSL(2, ℂ \displaystyle\mathbb{C} ) gauge group of the sphere is canceled by the action of conformal transformations on the winding interaction insertions. We formulate a precise version of the replica trick in terms of (infinitesimally) non-integer winding condensates to produce the entropy of the BTZ black hole. The resulting entropy can be calculated from the one-point function of a non-local operator on the worldsheet.

4.3
The area operator

The calculation performed in this note makes it clear that for the purpose of evaluating thermal entropy, we need to only go away from the on-shell background by an infinitesimal amount, in particular, this leads to the fact that entropy comes entirely from one point function of the stringy area operator
