2.3

we thus have

r_{ij}^{D}+r_{ji}^{D}=2\frac{\mathrm{det}L[\{i,j\}^{c},\{i,j\}^{c}]}{\kappa(D)}\leq 2.

c_{ii}+c_{jj}-c_{ij}-c_{ji}\leq 1
. The determinant of a square matrix is the same as the determinant of its transpose, we get

By equations
3.5
,
3.8
and
3.9
, we conclude that

r_{ij}^{D}\leq\frac{n+k}{n+k}=1.

∎

We now present the proof of Theorem
1.6
.

Proof.

Let

be a digraph in

. We prove Theorem

.

.

.
∎
