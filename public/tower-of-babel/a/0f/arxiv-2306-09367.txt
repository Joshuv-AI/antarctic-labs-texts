On asymptotic normality of the total progeny in the positive recurrent Q-processes Thanks: © 2023 Imomov A.A, Nazarov Z.A
Azam A. IMOMOV, Zuhriddin A. NAZAROV

Abstract
. We examine the population growth system called Q-processes. This is defined by the Galton-Watson Branching system conditioned on non-extinction of its trajectory in the remote future. In this paper we observe the total progeny up to time n n in the Q-process. By analogy with branching systems, this variable is of great interest in studying the deep properties of the Q-process. We find that the sum total progeny as a random variable approximates the standard normal distribution function under a second moment assumption for the initial Galton-Watson system offspring law. We estimate the speed rate of this approximation.
