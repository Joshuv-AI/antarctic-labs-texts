Simulation of 1 / f 1/f charge noise affecting a quantum dot in a Si/SiGe structure
M. Kępa, N. Focke, Ł. Cywiński, J. A. Krzywda

Abstract
Due to presence of magnetic field gradient needed for coherent spin control, dephasing of single-electron spin qubits in silicon quantum dots is often dominated by 1 / f 1/f charge noise. We investigate theoretically fluctuations of ground state energy of an electron in gated quantum dot in realistic Si/SiGe structure. We assume that the charge noise is caused by motion of charges trapped at the semiconductor-oxide interface. We consider a realistic range of trapped charge densities, ρ ∼ 10 10 \rho\!\sim\!10^{10} cm -2 , and typical lenghtscales of isotropically distributed displacements of these charges, δ ​ r ≤ 1 \delta r\!\leq\!1 nm, and identify pairs ( ρ , δ ​ r ) (\rho,\delta r) for which the amplitude and shape of the noise spectrum is in good agreement with spectra reconstructed in recent experiments on similar structures.

We employ the simplest model leading to
1/f

having switching rates
\gamma

\gamma

;

Distinct electric fields generated by the TLF in each of its states cause state-dependent shifts of ground state energy of the nearby QD. We define the coupling of the QD to the
n
-th TLF,
\eta_{n}
, as the shift of the energy of the GS orbital of an electron in the QD caused by a switching of the TLF.
We focus on switching events that correspond to a sub-nm motion of a defect charge in the insulating layer between the semiconductor and the metallic gate (the blue region in Fig.
1
). The two locations of the charge corresponding to the two states of the
n
-th TLF are
\mathbf{r}_{n}
and
\mathbf{r}_{n}+\delta\mathbf{r}_{n}
. We have checked that charge tunneling between the insulator and the metal leads to shifts of energy levels of the QD that are too large to be reconciled with the experimentally observed noise levels.
Shehata et al. 2022

The PSD of the noise in GS energy of the electron in the QD is given by a sum of Lorentzian PSDs of the RTNs:

S(f)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{2\eta_{n}^{2}}{\gamma_{n}+(2\pi f)^{2}/\gamma_{n}}\,\,.

(1)

For a large number of TLFs, a flat distribution of
\eta_{n}
, and a log-normal distribution of
\gamma_{n}
, i.e.
p(\gamma_{n})\propto 1/\gamma_{n}
, results in the PSD that is well approximated by the 1/f noise spectrum, i.e.
S(f)\!\approx\!S_{1}f_{1}/f
,
where
S_{1}
is the power of the noise at
f_{1}=1
Hz, which is of the order of
1

\mu
eV
2
/Hz in state-of-the-art Si/SiGe QDs.
Kranz et al. 2020

The motion of the charge away from the dot leads to decrease of

On the other hand, the derivative in

\approx 100

Summarizing, we have calculated electrostatic couplings between a quantum dot defined in realistic Si/SiGe nanostructure, and the charges trapped in the oxide above the semiconductor layer. Starting with reasonable initial ranges parameters for isotropic displacements of these charges,
\delta r
and their densities,
\rho
, we have identified a set of
(\rho,\delta r)
pairs, for which in the typically observed amplitude of
1/f
charge noise at
1
Hz, and its total power, can be reproduced. Deviations from ideal
1/f
shape of charge noise PSDs measured in various Si/SiGe structures
Kafanov et al. 2008
;
Struck et al. 2029
;
Connors et al. 2019
;
Rojas-Arias et al. 2023
;
Struck et al. 2029
;
Yoneda et al. 2022
;
Connors et al. 2022
can be reproduced with
\rho\geq 10^{10}
cm
-2
and
\delta r\!\leq\!0.15\,\mathrm{nm}
.
Note that these numbers might differ from actual values of
\rho
and
\delta r
, as the QDs that work well as spin qubits could be subjected to selection bias, i.e. focusing on QDs with lower-than typical amplitude of noise.
The ranges of parameters found here to be consistent with experiments can be further narrowed down if more information on noise in a given nanostructure - for example on cross-correlations of noises in two QDs
Yoneda et al. 2022
- is available. Note that a recent paper
Rojas-Arias et al. 2023
shows that those correlations can be obtained in a model with
