An inductive approach to generalized abundance using nef reduction
Priyankur Chaudhuri

Abstract
. We use the canonical bundle formula for parabolic fibrations to give an inductive approach to the generalized abundance conjecture using nef reduction. In particular, we observe that generalized abundance holds for a klt pair ( X , B ) (X,B) if the nef dimension n ⁡ ( K X + B + L ) = 2 n(K_{X}+B+L)=2 and K X + B ≥ 0 K_{X}+B\geq 0 or n ⁡ ( K X + B + L ) = 3 n(K_{X}+B+L)=3 and κ ⁡ ( K X + B ) > 0 \kappa(K_{X}+B)>0 .

In
[
10

(Generalized Abundance) Let

They show that this conjecture holds in case

(see
[
10

). The main purpose of this article is to prove the following result.

Theorem 2
.

Let
(X,B)
be an

[

[

[
10
, page 2]
in dimension
\leq d-1
.

Then
K_{X}+B+L\equiv M
for some semiample divisor
M
.

Note that the phrase “Conjecture (
*
) holds in dimension

(resp.
\leq d
)” above means that (
*

(X,B)
such that
\dim X=d

\leq d
).

In case

]

[

]
and Nakayama-Zariski decomposition

. We have the following corollary

Corollary 3
.

Generalized abundance holds (in any dimension) in the following two cases:

(1)

n(K_{X}+B+L)=2
and
K_{X}+B\geq 0
, or

(2)

n(K_{X}+B+L)=3
and
\kappa(K_{X}+B)>0

Note that any divisor of nef dimension

Definition 2.2

(Nef reduction and nef dimension

)
.

Let

(1)

If

\phi

L|_{F}\equiv 0
.

(2)

If
x\in X

C\subset X

such that
\dim(\phi(C))>0
, then
(L\cdot C)>0
.

\phi
is called the
nef reduction map
of
L
and
\dim Y
the
nef dimension

n(L)
of

.

Remark 4
.

Note that if
\phi:X\dashrightarrow Y
is regular over an open subset of
Y

of

such that the exceptional divisor
Ex(\hat{X}/X)
is
\hat{\phi}
-vertical. For example,
\hat{X}

\phi

Notation 5

For a pseudoeffective divisor
