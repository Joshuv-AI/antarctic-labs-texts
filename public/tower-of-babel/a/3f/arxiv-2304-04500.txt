Liouville integrable binomial Hamiltonian system
Mustafa Mullahasanoglu

Abstract
In this study we work on a novel Hamiltonian system which is Liouville integrable. In the integrable Hamiltonian model, conserved currents can be represented as Binomial polynomials in which each order corresponds to the integral of motion of the system. From a mathematical point of view, the equations of motion can be written as integrable second-order nonlinear partial differential equations in 1 + 1 1+1 dimensions.
