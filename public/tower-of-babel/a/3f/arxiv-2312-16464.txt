Strong Shape Invariance of Alexander-Spanier Normal Homology Theory
Anzor Beridze

Abstract
In the paper [Ba-Be-Mdz], using the Alexander-Spanier cochains based on the normal coverings, the exact homology theory H ¯ ∗ N ​ ( − , − , G ) \bar{H}^{N}_{*}(-,-;G) , the so called Alexander-Spanier homology theory, is defined. In the paper, we use the method of construction of the strong homology theory to show that the homology theory H ¯ ∗ N ​ ( − , − , G ) \bar{H}^{N}_{*}(-,-;G) is a strong shape invariant.
