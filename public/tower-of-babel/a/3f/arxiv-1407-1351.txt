Lower bound for the population of hyperfine component μ = 0 \mu=0 particles in the ground state of spin-1 condensates
C. G. Bao

Abstract
An analytical expression for the lower bound of the average number of hyperfine component μ = 0 \mu=0 particles in the ground state of spin-1 condensates (denoted as ρ 0 _ ​ _ \overset{\_\_}{\rho_{0}} ) under a magnetic field has been derived. In the derivation the total magnetization M M is kept rigorously conserved. Numerical examples are given to show the applicability of the analytical expression. It was found that, in a broad domain of parameters specified in the paper, the lower bound is very close to the actual ρ 0 _ ​ _ \overset{\_\_}{\rho_{0}} . Thereby, in this domain, ρ 0 _ ​ _ \overset{\_\_}{\rho_{0}} can be directly evaluated simply by using the analytical expression.

In conclusion, although the derivation of the inequality by Tasaki is
rigorous, the resulting lower bound is too low to be meaningful. On the other hand, although
approximations have been used in this paper (namely, the M-conserved SMA and

(i) The inequality has been generalized for the case with an arbitrary

(ii) The constraint is now species-dependent. Since the g.s. of the Rb

reasonable.

(iii) In a broad domain of parameters frequently accessed in experiments,
the new inequality is applicable. In particular, when
