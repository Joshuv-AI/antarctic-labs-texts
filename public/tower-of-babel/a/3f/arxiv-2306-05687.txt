In a multiplicative hyperring
(R,+,\circ)
a non empty subset
L
of
R
is called a multiplicative set whenever
a,b\in A\Rightarrow a\circ b\cap A\neq\phi
.

We can contract the localization of a multiplicative hyperring
R
as follows:
Let
S
be a multiplicative closed subset of
R
, that is,
S
is closed under the hypermultiplication and contains the identity. Let
S^{-1}R
be the set
(R\times S/\sim)
of equivalence classes where

(r_{1},s_{1})\sim(r_{2},s_{2})\iff\exists s\in S\,\,\text{such that }ss_{1}r_{2}=ss_{2}r_{1}.

Let
r/s
be the equivalence class of
(r,s)\in R\times S
under the equivalence relation
\sim
. The operation addition and the hyperoperation multiplication are defined by

\frac{r_{1}}{s_{1}}+\frac{r_{2}}{s_{2}}=\frac{s_{1}r_{2}+s_{2}r_{1}}{s_{1}s_{2}}=\{\frac{a+b}{c}:a\in s_{1}r_{2},b\in s_{2}r_{1},c\in s_{1}s_{2}\}

\frac{r_{1}}{s_{1}}\cdot{\frac{r_{2}}{s_{2}}}=\frac{r_{1}r_{2}}{s_{1}s_{2}}=\{\frac{a}{b},a\in r_{1}r_{2},b\in s_{1}s_{2}\}.

Note that the localization map
f:R\to S^{-1}R
,
f(r)=\frac{r}{1}
is a homomorphism of hyperrings. It is easy to see that the localization of a hyperideal is a hyperideal.

Proposition 3.11
.

Let
P
be an
I-
prime hyperideal of
R
with

. Then

is an
S^{-1}I-

.

Proof.

\frac{r_{1}}{s_{1}},\frac{r_{2}}{s_{2}}\in S^{-1}R
with
\frac{r_{1}}{s_{1}}\frac{r_{2}}{s_{2}}=\frac{r_{1}r_{2}}{s_{1}s_{2}}\subseteq S^{-1}P-S^{-1}IS^{-1}P=S^{-1}P-S^{-1}(IP)
. For each
n\in r_{1}r_{2},s\in s_{1}s_{2}
, we have
\frac{n}{s}\in\frac{r_{1}r_{2}}{s_{1}s_{2}}
and
\frac{n}{s}=\frac{a}{t}

,

or
