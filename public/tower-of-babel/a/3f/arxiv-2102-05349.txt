Can the nature of a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) be tested in the D s + → π + ​ π 0 ​ η D_{s}^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta decay?
Xi-Zhe Ling, Ming-Zhu Liu, Jun-Xu Lu, Li-Sheng Geng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
From the amplitude analysis of the D s + → π + ​ π 0 ​ η D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta decay, the BESIII Collaboration firstly observed the D s + → a 0 ​ ( 980 ) + ​ π 0 D^{+}_{s}\to a_{0}(980)^{+}\pi^{0} and D s + → a 0 ​ ( 980 ) 0 ​ π + D^{+}_{s}\to a_{0}(980)^{0}\pi^{+} decay modes, which are expected to occur through the pure W W -annihilation processes. The measured branching fraction ℬ [ D s + → a 0 ( 980 ) + ( 0 ) π 0 ​ ( + ) , a 0 ( 980 ) + ( 0 ) → π + ( 0 ) η ] \mathcal{B}[D_{s}^{+}\to a_{0}(980)^{+(0)}\pi^{0(+)},a_{0}(980)^{+(0)}\to\pi^{+(0)}\eta] is, however, found to be larger than those of known W W -annihilation decays by one order of magnitude. This apparent contradiction can be reconciled if the two decays are induced by internal W W -conversion or external W W -emission mechanisms instead of W W -annihilation mechanism. In this work, we propose that the D s + D^{+}_{s} decay proceeds via both the external and internal W W -emission instead of W W -annihilation mechanisms. In such a scenario, we perform a study of the D s + → π + ​ π 0 ​ η D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta decay by taking into account the contributions from the tree diagram D s + → ρ + ​ η → π + ​ π 0 ​ η D^{+}_{s}\to\rho^{+}\eta\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta and the intermediate ρ + ​ η \rho^{+}\eta and K ∗ ​ K ¯ / K ​ K ¯ ∗ K^{*}\bar{K}/K\bar{K}^{*} triangle diagrams. The intermediate a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) state can be dynamically generated from the final state interactions of coupled K ​ K ¯ K\bar{K} and π ​ η \pi\eta channels, and it is shown that the experimental data can be described fairly well, which supports the interpretation of a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) as a molecular state.

IV
Summary

We studied the
D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta
decay recently analysed by the BESIII Collaboration, where the
D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{0}a_{0}(980)^{+}
and
D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}a_{0}(980)^{0}
decay modes are claimed as the
W
-annihilation dominant processes observed for the first time, and their branching fractions, however, are one order of magnitude larger than those of known
W
-annihilation decays. Inspired by Ref.
Hsiao:2019ait
, we proposed that the anomalously large branching ratios of these decay modes can be understood via triangle diagrams. At first, the
D^{+}_{s}
meson decays weakly into either
\rho^{+}\eta
or
K\bar{K}^{*}/K^{*}\bar{K}
. The vector mesons then decay into a pair of pseudoscalar mesons,
\pi\pi
or
K\pi
. One of them interacts with the pseudoscalar meson from the weak decay of
D^{+}_{s}
, and generates dynamically the
a_{0}(980)
state. With the weak decay couplings determined by fitting to the experimental branching fractions, our method predicted both the absolute branching ratio of
D_{s}\to a_{0}\pi
and the
\pi\eta
invariant mass distributions, which are in nice agreement with the BESIII data. For the
D^{+}_{s}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta
decay, the contribution from the tree diagram as shown in Fig.
2
is the most dominant. After the cut of
M_{\pi^{+}\pi^{0}}>1
GeV, the contributions of the triangle diagrams are crucial to produce the
a_{0}(980)
resonance. In addition, we predicted the
K\bar{K}
invariant mass distributions of the
D_{s}\to\pi K\bar{K}
decay, which can be checked by future experimental measurements.

Furthermore, the present work provides a way to estimate the effective weak coupling appearing in the
D_{s}\to K^{*}\bar{K}\to K\bar{K}\pi
vertex. The same mechanism might also be relevant to those of similar processes, such as the
D^{+}\to\pi^{+}K\bar{K}
and
D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}\eta
reactions
