Abstract
. We show that the affine cones over a general Fano-Mukai fourfold of genus g = 7 g=7 , 8 8 and 9 9 are flexible. Equivalently, there is an infinitely transitive action of the special automorphism group on such affine cones. In particular, any Mukai fourfold of genus 7 , 8 7,8 and 9 9 is 𝔸 2 \mathbb{A}^{2} -cylindrical.



1.
Introduction

Studying affine varieties and their automorphism groups is an active area of research. A remarkable property of an affine variety is the infinitely transitive action of the special automorphism group on it. Let
X
be an affine variety over
\mathbb{C}
.
A one-parameter subgroup
H
of
{\rm Aut}(X)
isomorphic as an algebraic group to the additive group
\mathbb{G}_{a}
is called a unipotent one-parameter subgroup. We denote by
{\rm SAut}(X)
the special automorphism group, that is, the subgroup of
{\rm Aut}(X)
generated by all the one-parameter unipotent subgroups.
Following
[
3
]
, the variety
X
is called
flexible
if
{\rm SAut}(X)
acts highly transitively on the smooth locus
{\rm reg}(X)
,
that is, it acts
m
-transitively for any natural number
m
.

In dimension
2
, del Pezzo surfaces of degree
\geq 4
with their pluri-anticanonical polarizations have flexible affine cones (
[
26
,
24
]
).
In higher dimensions, the affine cones over flag varieties of dimension
\geq 2
are flexible by
[
6
]
.
In
[
22
]
, it is shown that the affine cone over the secant (tangential) variety of Segre-Veronese varieties are flexible.
Furthermore, there are several constructions producing new flexible varieties from a given flexible one, e.g. via suspensions or open subsets with complement of codimension
\geq 2
(see
[
3
,
6
,
10
,
16
]
).
Furthermore, several families of examples of Fano varieties of dimension
3
and
4
and Picard number one admitting flexible affine cones have been constructed
[
6
,
5
,
22
,
29
,
30
]
,
but a complete classification is still far from being known.

In this paper we focus on the case in which
X
is a Fano–Mukai fourfold, namely a four dimensional complex smooth Fano variety of index
2
. Given a smooth Fano fourfold
X
with Picard rank
1
, the
index
of

is the integer

such that
-K_{X}=rH
, where

is the ample divisor generating the Picard group:
{\mathrm{Pic}}(X)=\mathbb{Z}H
.
The

is the degree with respect to

. It is known that

and smooth Fano fourfolds of index

are called

; their degrees are even and can be written as

, where

is called the

of

.

Our main theorem is the following.

Theorem 1.1
.

The affine cones over general Mukai fourfold of genus
g=7
,

9
are flexible.

The only known flexible Mukai fourfolds are of genus

According to
