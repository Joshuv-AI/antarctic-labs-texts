{\rm Sel}^{\varphi}(E/F)=\{c\in H^{1}(G_{F},E[\varphi])\mid{\rm res}_{\omega}(c)\in{\rm Im}\ \delta_{\varphi,F_{\omega}}\text{ for every }\omega\in\Sigma_{F}\}.

Setting

, the Tate-Shafarevich group of
E
over

0\longrightarrow{\widehat{E}(F)}/{\varphi(E(F))}\longrightarrow{\rm Sel}^{\varphi}(E/F)\longrightarrow\Sh(E/F)[\varphi]\longrightarrow 0

(2)

In particular, for

In view of Prop.

\widehat{E}(K)\longrightarrow\widehat{E}(K)/\varphi(E(K))\lhook\joinrel\xrightarrow{}H^{1}(G_{K},E[\varphi])\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\sim}}{{\smash{\longrightarrow}\rule{0.0pt}{1.54996pt}}}(L^{*}/L^{*3})_{N=1}
again by

; similarly we view

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{{L^{*}}/{L^{*3}}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{{K^{*}}/{K^{*3}}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{{L_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}}/{L_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3}}}}

(3)

To ease the notation, via the canonical embedding

with

{\rm Sel}^{\varphi}(E/K)=\{\overline{x}\in(L^{*}/L^{*3})_{N=1}\mid\overline{x}\in\text{Im }\delta_{\varphi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}\text{ for all }{\mathfrak{q}}\in\Sigma_{K}\}.

(4)

Similarly, if

{\rm Sel}^{\varphi}(E/K)=\{(\overline{x}_{1},\overline{x}_{2})\in(K^{*}/K^{*3}\times K^{*}/K^{*3})_{N=1}\mid(\overline{x}_{1},\overline{x}_{2})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\varphi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}\text{ for all }{\mathfrak{q}}\in\Sigma_{K}\}.

(5)

These will be our working definitions of the Selmer groups for the rest of the article.

2.
Some algebraic number theory

We obtain some algebraic results in this section which will be used later.

Definition 2.1
.

We define
(A^{*}/A^{*3})_{N=1}
to be the kernel of the norm map
\overline{N}_{L/K}:A^{*}/A^{*3}\to{\mathcal{O}}_{K}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K}^{*3}
induced from the norm map
\overline{N}_{L/K}:L^{*}/L^{*3}\to K^{*}/K^{*3}
. The group

(A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3})_{N=1}
is defined similarly by replacing
A
and
{\mathcal{O}}_{K}
with
A_{\mathfrak{q}}
and
{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
, respectively, in this definition.

In the following proposition we compute the size of
(A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3})_{N=1}
.

Proposition 2.2
.

For
{\mathfrak{q}}\nmid 3
, we have

|(A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3})_{N=1}|=\begin{cases}1,&\text{ if }a\notin K_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*2},\\
3,&\text{ if }a\in K_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*2}.\end{cases}

On the other hand, for
{\mathfrak{p}}\mid 3
, we have

|(A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*3})_{N=1}|=\begin{cases}9,&\text{ if }a\notin K_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*2},\\
27,&\text{ if }a\in K_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*2}.\end{cases}

Proof.

First, assume that
a\notin K_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*2}
i.e.
{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}
is the ring of integers of the field
L_{\mathfrak{q}}
. In this case, cokernel of
N_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}/K_{\mathfrak{q}}}:{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}\to{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}
is a
2
-primary group,
hence
\overline{N}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}/K_{\mathfrak{q}}}:{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}\to{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}
is surjective. Thus, we get

|(A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3})_{N=1}|=\frac{|{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}|}{|{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}|}.

On the other hand, if
a\in K_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*2}
, then
A_{\mathfrak{q}}={\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}\times{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
(see §
1
) and the norm map
N_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}/K_{\mathfrak{q}}}:{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}\times{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}\to{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}
is given by the multiplication of co-ordinates, hence surjective. Therefore,

|(A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3})_{N=1}|=\frac{|{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}\times{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}|}{|{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}|}=|{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}^{*3}|.

Thus, to complete the proof, we compute
|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*3}|
, with
F\in\{K_{\mathfrak{q}},L_{\mathfrak{q}}\}
a field. Let
q
and
|\kappa_{F}|
denote the characteristic and cardinality of the residue field of
F
, respectively. Then

{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}\cong\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{(|\kappa_{F}|-1){\mathbb{Z}}}\times\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{q^{s}{\mathbb{Z}}}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{q}^{r},

where
r=[F:{\mathbb{Q}}_{q}]
and
s=\mathrm{max}\{t\mid F\text{ contains }q^{t}-\text{th roots of unity}\}
.

Consider the primes
q\neq 3
. If
q\equiv 1\pmod{3}
, then
q
splits as a product of two (distinct) primes in
K
, so
|\kappa_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}|=q\equiv 1\pmod{3}
. On the other hand, if
q\equiv 2\pmod{3}
, then
q
is inert in
K
. Then
K_{\mathfrak{q}}
is an unramified quadratic extension of
{\mathbb{Q}}_{q}
, hence
|\kappa_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}|=q^{2}=1\pmod{3}
. Thus for
q\neq 3
,
|\kappa_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}|\equiv 1\pmod{3}
. Since
|\kappa_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}|
is either
|\kappa_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}|
or
|\kappa_{K_{\mathfrak{q}}}|^{2}
, we see that
|\kappa_{L_{\mathfrak{q}}}|\equiv 1\pmod{3}
for
q\neq 3
. We conclude that

|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*3}|=\Big[\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{(|\kappa_{F}|-1){\mathbb{Z}}}:3\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{(|\kappa_{F}|-1){\mathbb{Z}}}\Big]\Big[\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{q^{s}{\mathbb{Z}}}:3\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{q^{s}{\mathbb{Z}}}\Big]\Big[{\mathbb{Z}}_{q}^{r}:3{\mathbb{Z}}_{q}^{r}\Big]=3.

Now if
q=3
, then
{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{*}\cong\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{2{\mathbb{Z}}}\times\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{3{\mathbb{Z}}}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{3}^{2}
implies
|{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{*3}|=3^{3}.

Similarly,
{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{*}\cong\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{8{\mathbb{Z}}}\times\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{3{\mathbb{Z}}}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{3}^{4}
implies
|{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{L_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{*3}|=3^{5}.

Combining all of these, the claim follows.
∎

Recall that for a local field
F
with the ring of integers

{{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}}/{U^{n}_{F}}\cong({\mathcal{O}}_{F}/{\pi^{n}{\mathcal{O}}_{F}})^{*}

Definition 2.3
.

For a local field
F
, define

V_{F}=\{u\in{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}\mid\overline{u}\in{{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}}/{U^{3}_{F}}\text{ satisfies }\overline{u}=\alpha^{3}\text{ for some }\alpha\in{{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}}/{U^{3}_{F}}\}.

It is obvious from the definition that

and

Lemma 2.4
.

For a field
F\in\{K_{\mathfrak{p}},L_{\mathfrak{p}}\}
, we have
|V_{F}/{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*3}|=3
.

Proof.

Note that
|V_{F}/{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*3}|=\frac{|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*3}|}{|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/V_{F}|}.
First we compute
|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/V_{F}|=\frac{|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/U^{3}_{F}|}{|V_{F}/U^{3}_{F}|}.
It is easy to see that
V_{F}/{U^{3}_{F}}\cong\big({\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/U^{3}_{F}\big)^{3}
and so
|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/V_{F}|=\frac{|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/U^{3}_{F}|}{|({\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/U^{3}_{F})^{3}|}.
We know that
\frac{{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}}{U^{3}_{F}}\cong\frac{{\mathbb{Z}}}{(|\kappa_{F}|-1){\mathbb{Z}}}\times\frac{U^{1}_{F}}{U^{3}_{F}}
. The structure of

is well known;
\frac{U_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{1}}{U_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{3}}\cong\big(\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{3\mathbb{Z}}\big)^{\oplus 2}
and
\frac{U_{L_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{1}}{U_{L_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{3}}\cong\big(\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{3\mathbb{Z}}\big)^{\oplus 4}
. Therefore,
|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/V_{F}|=|U^{1}_{F}/U^{3}_{F}|=\begin{cases}9,&\text{ if }F=K_{\mathfrak{p}},\\
81,&\text{ if }F=L_{\mathfrak{p}}.\end{cases}

We have computed
|{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*}/{\mathcal{O}}_{F}^{*3}|=\begin{cases}27,&\text{ if }F=K_{\mathfrak{p}},\\
243,&\text{ if }F=L_{\mathfrak{p}},\end{cases}
(in the proof of Prop.
2.2
).
The result is immediate from these observations.
∎

Proposition 2.6

(1)

There exists a unit

(1)

(2)

Lemma 2.7

Let

Proposition 2.15

Recall

Also by definition, for each

L_{\mathfrak{Q}}(\{\sqrt[3]{x}\mid\overline{x}\in M(S,a)\})=L_{\mathfrak{Q}}

Theorem 3.22

Suppose that

and

and so

4.1.
The cube sum problem

Recall that a cube-free integer
D>2
can be written as a sum of two rational cubes if and only if
\text{rk }E_{-432D^{2}}({\mathbb{Q}})>0
.
The elliptic curve
E_{aD^{2}}:y^{2}=x^{3}+aD^{2}
is a cubic twist by
D
of
E_{a}:y^{2}=x^{3}+a
.
Let
\ell
be a rational prime. The Sylvester’s conjecture predicts that

\text{rk }E_{-432\ell^{2}}({\mathbb{Q}})=\begin{cases}0,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 2,5\pmod{9}\\
1,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 4,7,8\pmod{9}\\
0\text{ or }2,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 1\pmod{9}.\end{cases}

For
\ell\equiv 2,5\pmod{9}
, the proof goes back to the works of Pépin, Lucas and Sylvester.
In
[
DV
]
, for primes
\ell\equiv 4,7\pmod{9}
, assuming that
3
is not a cube modulo
\ell
, it is shown that
\text{rk }E_{-432\ell^{2}}({\mathbb{Q}})=\text{rk }E_{-432\ell^{4}}({\mathbb{Q}})=1
.

[
RZ
]
considers the primes
\ell\equiv 1\pmod{9}
and provides three non-trivial criteria to check whether
\text{rk }E_{-432\ell^{2}}({\mathbb{Q}})
is
0
or
2
(assuming the BSD conjecture).
In
[
MS
,
JMS
]
, the authors produce infinitely many primes
\ell\equiv\pm 1\pmod{9}
that are cube sums.

Sylvester also proved that if
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9}
(respectively, if
\ell\equiv 2\pmod{9}
), then
2\ell
(respectively,
2\ell^{2}
) is not a rational cube sum. If
\ell\equiv 2\pmod{9}
(resp.
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9}
), Satgé
[
Sa
]
proved that
2\ell
(resp.
2\ell^{2}
) is a cube sum. The cube sum problem for
2\ell,2\ell^{2}
for primes
\ell\equiv 1\pmod{3}
and
\ell\equiv 8\pmod{9}
seems to be not covered in the literature.

In Corollaries
4.5
and
4.6
, we study the cube sum problem for
2\ell
and
2\ell^{2}
. For every prime
\ell\geq 5

First, we compute
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
for primes
\ell\equiv 2\pmod{3}
.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
\ell\geq 5
be a prime such that
\ell\equiv 2\pmod{3}
and
\phi:E_{\ell^{2}}\to E_{\ell^{2}}
be the
3
-isogeny given in (
1
). Then

\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=\begin{cases}1,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9},\\
2,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 2,8\pmod{9}.\end{cases}

Proof.

We note that
S_{\ell^{2}}=\{2{\mathcal{O}}_{K},\ell{\mathcal{O}}_{K}\}
and
{\mathcal{O}}^{*}_{S_{\ell^{2}}}=\langle\pm\zeta,2,\ell\rangle=\langle\pm\zeta,2,2\ell\rangle
. Then
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)\subset\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}=\langle(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\overline{\zeta}),(\overline{4},\overline{2}),(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\overline{2\ell})\rangle

and
0\leq\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)\leq 3
.

From Prop.
3.6
(2),
\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}({E}_{\ell^{2}}(K_{2}))\cap\big(A_{2}^{*}/A_{2}^{*3}\big)_{N=1}=\{(\overline{1},\overline{1})\}
and hence
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\overline{\zeta})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
.
Observe that
\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}(0,\ell)=(\frac{1}{\overline{2\ell}},\ \overline{2\ell})=(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})
for all
{\mathfrak{q}}
and hence
(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. Thus,
1\leq\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)\leq 2
for
\ell\equiv 2\pmod{3}
.

We now investigate the remaining generator
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})
of
\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}
. Note that for primes
{\mathfrak{q}}\notin S_{\ell^{2}}\cup\{{\mathfrak{p}}\}
, one has
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})\in\big(A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}/A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3}\big)_{N=1}=\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
(Prop.
3.6
(1)). Thus it reduces to verify whether
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
for
{\mathfrak{q}}\in S_{\ell^{2}}\cup\{{\mathfrak{p}}\}
. Notice that the cubic residues
\big(\frac{2}{\ell}\big)_{3}=\big(\frac{\ell}{2}\big)_{3}=1
, as
\ell\equiv 2\pmod{3}
. Hence,
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})=(\overline{1},\overline{1})=\big({A_{\ell}^{*}}/{A_{\ell}^{*3}}\big)_{N=1}\cap\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\ell}}
. Similarly, using
\big(\frac{\ell}{2}\big)_{3}=1
, we get
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})=(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
. Thus, it remains to check whether or not
(\overline{4},\overline{2})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
.

Let us first consider the primes
\ell\equiv 2,8\pmod{9}
. Then
4\ell^{2}\equiv 2\ell\equiv 1\pmod{3}
, which gives
4\ell^{2},2\ell\in 1+3{\mathbb{Z}}_{3}\subset U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
; whereas
4\ell^{2},2\ell\notin U^{3}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
. Note that
\frac{U^{3}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}\cong\frac{V_{3}}{A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*3}}
by Lemma
2.7
and
\frac{V_{3}}{A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*3}}\subset\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
by Prop.
3.11
. It follows that
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}=\langle(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\overline{2\ell}),(\overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})^{2}},\overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})})\rangle\cong\frac{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
. As
\overline{4}\in\frac{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
, we see that
(\overline{4},\overline{2})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
. Hence,
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=2
, when
\ell\equiv 2,8\pmod{9}
.

Finally, we take
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9}
. In this situation,
\ell^{2}-1\equiv-3\pmod{9}
,
\exists\ \alpha\in 1+3{\mathbb{Z}}_{3}
such that
\ell^{2}-1=-3\alpha^{2}
. Consequently, we obtain a point
P:=(-1,{\mathfrak{p}}\zeta\alpha)\in E_{\ell^{2}}(K_{\mathfrak{p}})
. We compute that
\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}(P)
is
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})
if
\ell\equiv 14\pmod{27}
,
(\overline{\zeta^{2}(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})^{2}},\ \overline{\zeta(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})})
if
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{27}
and
(\overline{\zeta(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})},\ \overline{\zeta^{2}(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})^{2}})
if
\ell\equiv 23\pmod{27}
. As
(\overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})^{2}},\ \overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})})\in\frac{V_{3}}{A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*3}}\subset\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
, we conclude that
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
. In fact,
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}=\langle(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta}),(\overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})^{2}},\ \overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})})\rangle
.
Let
f\big({U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}/{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}\big)
be the isomorphic copy of
{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}/{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
under the isomorphism
f:{U^{1}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}/{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\sim}}{{\smash{\longrightarrow}\rule{0.0pt}{1.54996pt}}}\big({A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*}}/{A_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*3}}\big)_{N=1}
. As
\overline{\zeta}\notin{U^{3}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}/{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
, we see that
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}\cap f\big(\frac{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}\big)\cong\frac{U^{3}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}.
Since
\overline{4}\in\frac{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}\setminus\frac{U^{3}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
, we deduce that
(\overline{4},\overline{2})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
and hence, is not in
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
.

We have shown above for
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9}
that
(\overline{4\ell^{2}},\ \overline{2\ell})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
and
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2}),(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})\notin{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. There are four subgroups of
\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}
of order
9
containing
(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})
.
To conclude that
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=1
, it suffices to show that

\langle(\overline{4\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{2\zeta})\rangle\not\subset{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
and
\langle(\overline{2\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{4\zeta})\rangle\not\subset{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. First we consider
(\overline{4\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{2\zeta})
. As
\big(\frac{2}{\ell}\big)_{3}=1
, we identify
(\overline{4\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{2\zeta})
with
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})
in
\big(\frac{A_{\ell}^{*}}{A_{\ell}^{*3}}\big)_{N=1}.
Recall that
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\ell}}\cap\big(\frac{A_{\ell}^{*}}{A_{\ell}^{*3}}\big)_{N=1}=\{(\overline{1},\overline{1})\}
by Prop.
3.6
(2). Given
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9}
, we have
(\overline{4\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{2\zeta})=(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\ell}}
and so it is not in
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. A similar argument works for
(\overline{2\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{4\zeta})
. This shows
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=1
, when
\ell\equiv 5\pmod{9}
.
∎

In the following theorem, we compute
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
for primes
\ell\equiv 1\pmod{3}

Theorem 4.3
.

Let
\ell\equiv 1\pmod{3}
be a prime and
\phi:E_{\ell^{2}}\to E_{\ell^{2}}
be the
3
-isogeny given in (
1
). Let the prime
\ell
split as
\pi_{\ell}\cdot\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}
in
{\mathcal{O}}_{K}
and
\big(\frac{\cdot}{\cdot}\big)_{3}
be the cubic residue symbol.
Then

\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=\begin{cases}1,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 1,7\pmod{9}\text{ and }\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}\neq 1,\\
2,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 4\pmod{9},\\
3,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 1,7\pmod{9}\text{ and }\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1.\end{cases}

Proof.

We write
\ell=\pi_{\ell}\cdot\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}
such that
\pi_{\ell}=m+n\zeta
. In this case, it is possible to choose
m\equiv 1\pmod{3}
and
n\equiv 0\pmod{3}
. Here,
S_{\ell^{2}}=\{2{\mathcal{O}}_{K},\pi_{\ell},\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}\}
and
{\mathcal{O}}^{*}_{S_{\ell^{2}}}=\langle\pm\zeta,2,\pi_{\ell},\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}\rangle=\langle\pm\zeta,2,\pi_{\ell},2\ell\rangle
. Also
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)\subset\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}=\langle(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta}),\ (\overline{4},\ \overline{2}),\ (\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}}),\ (\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\rangle
and
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)\leq 4
. As in the previous case,
(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
, being the image of
(0,\ \ell)
under
\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
for all
{\mathfrak{q}}
. On the other hand,
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
and hence is not in
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. This gives
1\leq\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)\leq 3
. Moreover, for primes
{\mathfrak{q}}\notin S_{\ell^{2}}\cup\{{\mathfrak{p}}\},

(\overline{4},\ \overline{2}),\ (\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})\in\big({A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*}}/{A_{\mathfrak{q}}^{*3}}\big)_{N=1}=\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
.

First, consider the case
\ell\equiv 1,7\pmod{9}
and
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1
. We now check whether
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})
,
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{q}}}
at primes
{\mathfrak{q}}\in S_{\ell^{2}}\cup\{{\mathfrak{p}}\}
; we start with
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})
. Our assumption
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1
implies
\big(\frac{2}{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1
. Thus,
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})=(\overline{1},\overline{1})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi_{\ell}}}
and
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}}
. Using the law of cubic reciprocity,
\big(\frac{\pi_{\ell}}{2}\big)_{3}=\big(\frac{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}{2}\big)_{3}=1
. This implies
\big(\frac{\ell}{2}\big)_{3}=1
and so,
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})=(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
. Now, following the proof of Theorem
4.1
,
\ell\equiv 2,8\pmod{9}
case, we see that
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}=\langle(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell}),\ (\overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})^{2}},\ \overline{(1+{\mathfrak{p}}^{3})})\rangle\cong\frac{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
.
As
4\in U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
,
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
and thus, it is in
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. Next, we investigate
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})
. Again by the trick of Evan,
\big(\frac{\pi_{\ell}}{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1
, we get
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})=(\overline{1},\overline{1})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}}
. Further,
\big(\frac{\pi_{\ell}}{2}\big)_{3}=1
gives
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})=(\overline{1},\overline{1})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
. These two observations also imply that
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})=(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi_{\ell}}}
. Finally, using
\pi_{\ell}=m+n\zeta
, where
m\equiv 1\pmod{3}
and
n\equiv 0\pmod{3}
, we get
\pi_{\ell}\equiv 1\pmod{{\mathfrak{p}}^{2}}
i.e.
\pi_{\ell}\in U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
. Now using
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}\cong\frac{U^{2}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}{U^{4}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}}
, we have
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})\in\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
and hence
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=3
.

Next, consider the case
\ell\equiv 1,7\pmod{9}
and
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}\neq 1
. We already have
(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. There are
13
distinct subgroups of
\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}
of order
9
containing
(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})
.
There are
13
generators corresponding to order
3
subgroups of
\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}\Big/\big\langle(\overline{4\ell}^{2},\ \overline{2\ell})\big\rangle
in
\Big(\frac{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*}}{A_{S_{\ell^{2}}}^{*3}}\Big)_{N=1}
viz.
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})
,
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta})
,
(\overline{\pi_{\ell}}^{2},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{4\zeta^{2}},\ \overline{2\zeta})
,
(\overline{2\zeta^{2}},\ \overline{4\zeta})
,
(\overline{4\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\pi_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{4\pi_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\pi^{2}_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{\zeta^{2}\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{\zeta\pi_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{\zeta\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{\zeta^{2}\pi_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{4\zeta^{2}\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\zeta\pi_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{2\zeta^{2}\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{4\zeta\pi_{\ell}})
,
(\overline{4\zeta\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\zeta^{2}\pi_{\ell}})
and
(\overline{2\zeta\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{4\zeta^{2}\pi_{\ell}})
. A long and tedious calculation using the theory of cubic residues shows that
(\overline{\zeta}^{2},\ \overline{\zeta}),\ (\overline{\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
, whereas
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2}),\ (\overline{4\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\pi_{\ell}}),\ (\overline{4\pi_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\pi^{2}_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}}
and the rest of the generators are not in
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{{\mathfrak{p}}}}
. So, none of these
13
generators belong to
{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. Thus we conclude
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=1
in this case.

Finally, we consider the case
\ell\equiv 4\pmod{9}
. We already have
(\overline{4\ell^{2}},\ \overline{2\ell})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
. We divide this case into
3
subcases, depending on whether the cubic residue
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}
is equal to
1
,
\zeta
or
\zeta^{2}
. In each of these subcases, using the theory of cubic residues, we examine all the
13
generators mentioned above and observe the following:

If
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1
, then
(\overline{4\pi_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\pi^{2}_{\ell}})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
, whereas
(\overline{4},\ \overline{2})
,
(\overline{\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}}),\ (\overline{4\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\pi_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{{\mathfrak{p}}}}
and the rest of the generators are not in
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}}
.

If
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=\zeta
, then
(\overline{2\zeta\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{4\zeta^{2}\pi_{\ell}})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
, whereas
(\overline{4\zeta^{2}},\ \overline{2\zeta}),\ (\overline{4\zeta^{2}\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\zeta\pi_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi_{\ell}}}
and
(\overline{\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{\pi_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
, while the rest of the generators are not in
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}}
.

If
\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=\zeta^{2}
, then
(\overline{2\zeta^{2}\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{4\zeta\pi_{\ell}})\in{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)
, whereas
(\overline{2\zeta^{2}},\ \overline{4\zeta}),\ (\overline{4\zeta\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\ \overline{2\zeta^{2}\pi_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi_{\ell}}}
and
(\overline{\pi^{2}_{\ell}},\overline{\pi_{\ell}})\notin\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{2}}
, while the rest of the generators are not in
\text{Im }\delta_{\phi,K_{\pi^{\prime}_{\ell}}}
.

Thus, for each of these cases,

\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{\ell^{2}}/K)=2
, when
\ell\equiv 4\pmod{9}
.
∎

.

Theorem 4.4
.

\ell\equiv 1\pmod{3}
be a prime and
\phi:E_{\ell^{4}}\to E_{\ell^{4}}

-isogeny given in (

2,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 7\pmod{9},\\
3,&\text{ if }\ell\equiv 1,4\pmod{9}\text{ and }\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1.\end{cases}

Proof.

In the case, when

\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}\neq 1

\big(\frac{2}{\pi_{\ell}}\big)_{3}=1

Finally, in the case

Remark 4.7

\ell\equiv 7\pmod{12}
. If
\ell\equiv 1\pmod{12}
, then we know that
\big(\frac{p}{\ell}\big)=-1
if and only if
\big(\frac{\ell}{p}\big)=-1
.
It follows that the density of primes
\ell
such that
\ell\equiv 1\pmod{12}
and
\big(\frac{p}{\ell}\big)=-1
is
1/8
. Similarly, the density of primes
\ell
such that
\ell\equiv 7\pmod{12}
and
\big(\frac{p}{\ell}\big)=-1
is again
1/8
. We deduce that the density of the set
T_{p}
is
1/4
.
Now, observe that
S_{16p}=\emptyset
and
S_{16p\ell^{2}}=S_{16p}\sqcup\{\mathfrak{l}\in\Sigma_{K}:\mathfrak{l}\mid\ell\text{ and }p\in K_{\mathfrak{l}}^{*2}\}
. Hence, for all
\ell\in T_{p}
, one has
S_{16p\ell^{2}}=\emptyset
. So by Theorem
3.22
,
\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_{3}}{\rm Sel}^{\phi}(E_{16p\ell^{2}}/K)\in\{h^{3}_{L},\ h^{3}_{L}+1\}.

∎
