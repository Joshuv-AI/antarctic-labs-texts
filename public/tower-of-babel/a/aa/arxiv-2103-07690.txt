Asymptotic separation of solutions to fractional stochastic multi-term differential equations
Arzu Ahmadova, Nazim I. Mahmudov

Abstract
In this paper, we study the exact asymptotic separation rate of two distinct solutions of Caputo stochastic multi-term differential equations (Caputo SMTDEs for short). Our goal in this paper is to establish results on the global existence and uniqueness and continuity dependence of the initial values of the solutions to Caputo SMTDEs with non-permutable matrices of order α ∈ ( 1 2 , 1 ) \alpha\in(\frac{1}{2},1) and β ∈ ( 0 , 1 ) \beta\in(0,1) whose coefficients satisfy a standard Lipschitz condition. For this class of systems, we then show the asymptotic separation property between two different solutions of Caputo SMTDEs with a more general condition based on λ \lambda . Also, the asymptotic separation rate for the two distinct mild solutions reveals that our asymptotic results are general. Keywords: Asymptotic separation, Caputo stochastic multi-term differential equations, existence and uniqueness, continuous dependence on initial conditions, non-permutable matrices

In Section

Theorem 4.1

(Global existence and uniqueness and continuity dependence on the initial values of solutions of Caputo SMTDE)

Suppose that Assumptions

(i) for any
\eta\in\Xi_{0}

(ii) on any bounded time interval

with

\varphi(\cdot,\eta)
depends continuously on
\eta
, i.e.

\lim\limits_{\eta\to\gamma}\sup\limits_{t\in[0,T]}\|\varphi(t,\eta)-\varphi(t,\gamma)\|^{2}_{\text{ms}}=0.

For any

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{\eta}Y=\left(I+t^{\alpha}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha+1}^{A,B}(t)B\right)\eta

\displaystyle+\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)b(r,Y(r))\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle+\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)\sigma(r,Y(r))\mathrm{d}W(r),\quad t>0.

(4.1)

\displaystyle+4\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)Y(r)\mathrm{d}r\right\rVert^{2}

(4.2)

Considering

and using Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, we obtain the following results:

\displaystyle\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)Y(r)\mathrm{d}r\right\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\leq t\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{2\alpha-2}\textbf{E}\|Y(r)\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle\leq\mathscr{M}^{2}T\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{2\alpha-2}\sup_{r\in[0,T]}\textbf{E}\|Y(r)\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle\leq\mathscr{M}^{2}T\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{2\alpha-2}\|Y\|^{2}_{H^{2}}\mathrm{d}r=\mathscr{M}^{2}\frac{T^{2\alpha}}{2\alpha-1}\|Y\|^{2}_{H^{2}},

(4.3)

and

\displaystyle\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)b(r,Y(r))\mathrm{d}r\right\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\mathscr{M}^{2}\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{2\alpha-2}\mathrm{d}r\textbf{E}\int_{0}^{t}\|b(r,Y(r))\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle=\mathscr{M}^{2}\frac{T^{2\alpha-1}}{2\alpha-1}\textbf{E}\int_{0}^{t}\|b(r,Y(r))\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r.

\displaystyle\leq 2\mathscr{M}^{2}\frac{L_{b}^{2}T^{2\alpha}}{2\alpha-1}\|Y\|^{2}_{H^{2}}

\displaystyle+2\mathscr{M}^{2}\frac{T^{2\alpha-1}}{2\alpha-1}\int_{0}^{T}\|b(r,0)\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r.

\displaystyle=\mathscr{M}^{2}\sum_{i=1}^{d}\textbf{E}\left(\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{2\alpha-2}|\sigma_{i}(r,Y(r))|^{2}\mathrm{d}r\right)

\displaystyle=\mathscr{M}^{2}\textbf{E}\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{2\alpha-2}\|\sigma(r,Y(r))\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle\leq\mathscr{M}^{2}T^{2\alpha-2}\textbf{E}\int_{0}^{t}\|\sigma(r,Y(r))\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r.

From Assumption

\displaystyle\|\sigma(r,Y(r))\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\|\sigma(r,Y(r))-\sigma(r,0)\|^{2}+2\|\sigma(r,0)\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2L^{2}_{\sigma}\|Y(r)\|^{2}+2\|\sigma(r,0)\|^{2}.

Therefore, for all

\displaystyle\textbf{E}\left\lVert\int_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)\sigma(r,Y(r))\mathrm{d}W(r)\right\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\mathscr{M}^{2}T^{2\alpha-2}L^{2}_{\sigma}\textbf{E}\int_{0}^{t}\|Y(r)\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle+2\mathscr{M}^{2}T^{2\alpha-2}\int_{0}^{t}\|\sigma(r,0)\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle+2\mathscr{M}^{2}T^{2\alpha-2}\int_{0}^{T}\|\sigma(r,0)\|^{2}\mathrm{d}r.

Asymptotic separation between mild solutions of (

Theorem 5.1
.

Let
\eta,\gamma\in\Xi_{0}
such that
\eta\neq\gamma
. Then for any
\epsilon>0

\limsup_{t\to\infty}t^{\alpha+\epsilon}\|\varphi(t,\eta)-\varphi(t,\gamma)\|_{\text{ms}}=\infty.

Proof.

Assume the contrary, i.e. there exists a positive constant
\lambda>\alpha

\limsup_{t\to\infty}t^{\lambda}\|\varphi(t,\eta)-\varphi(t,\gamma)\|_{\text{ms}}<\infty,

for some
\eta,\gamma\in\Xi_{0}

. There exists constants

and

such that

(5.1)

From (
3
) and the inequality (
2.9
), we have

\displaystyle\|\eta-\gamma\|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{4\mathcal{C}}\|\varphi(t,\eta)-\varphi(t,\gamma)\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{\mathcal{C}}\left\lVert\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)\left(\varphi(r,\eta)-\varphi(r,\gamma)\right)\mathrm{d}r\right\rVert^{2}

, we obtain

\displaystyle\|\eta-\gamma\|^{2}_{\text{ms}}\leq\frac{1}{4\mathcal{C}}\textbf{E}\|\varphi(t,\eta)-\varphi(t,\gamma)\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{\mathcal{C}}\textbf{E}\left(\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\|\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)\|\|\varphi(r,\eta)-\varphi(r,\gamma)\|\mathrm{d}r\right)^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{\mathcal{C}}\textbf{E}\left(\int\limits_{0}^{t}(t-r)^{\alpha-1}\|\mathscr{E}_{\alpha-\beta,\alpha,\alpha}^{A,B}(t-r)\|L_{\sigma}\|\varphi(r,\eta)-\varphi(r,\gamma)\|\mathrm{d}W(r)\right)^{2}.
