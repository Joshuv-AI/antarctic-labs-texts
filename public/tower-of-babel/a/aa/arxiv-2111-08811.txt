Thermal corrections to the bound-electron g g -factor
T. Zalialiutdinov 1, 2 , D. Glazov 1 and D. Solovyev 1

Abstract
The influence of the blackbody radiation field on the g g -factor of light hydrogenlike ions is considered within the framework of quantum electrodynamics at finite temperature for bound states. One-loop thermal corrections are examined for a wide range of temperatures. The numerical results for 1 ​ s 1s , 2 ​ s 2s , 2 ​ p 1 / 2 2p_{1/2} , and 2 ​ p 3 / 2 2p_{3/2} states are presented. It is shown that for excited states finite temperature corrections to the bound-electron g g -factor are close to the level of current experimental uncertainty even at room temperatures and can be discerned within the measurements anticipated in the near future.

E_{a}\sim m_{e}(\alpha Z)^{2}
and

\int\limits_{0}^{\infty}d\omega\omega^{k}n_{\beta}(\omega)\sim(k_{B}^{\mathrm{\;r.u.}}T)^{k+1}
. Then the

) is parametrized as follows

\displaystyle\Delta g_{a}\sim\frac{(k_{B}T)^{4}_{\mathrm{r.u.}}}{\alpha^{5}m_{e}^{4}Z^{6}}\,.

(35)

In particular, the estimation Eq. (
35
) is valid for the
a=1s

np

m_{e}(\alpha Z)^{2}

n_{a}l_{a}

n

E_{n}=E_{n_{a}}

\displaystyle\Delta g_{a}\sim\frac{(k_{B}T)^{2}_{\mathrm{r.u.}}}{\alpha m_{e}^{2}Z^{2}}\,.

(36)

It should be noted that the quadratic behavior in temperature given by the formula (
36

Donoghue et al. 1985

Jun et al. 1991

Elmfors et al. 1996
.

Estimation (

2s
and
2p

III

The one-loop thermal self-energy correction to the bound-electron
g
-factor is given by the formulas (
30
) and (
31
). To sum up the entire spectrum in Eq. (
30
), we use the Schrödinger finite basis set constructed within the nonrelativistic B-spline approach, following the ideas presented in Refs.
Sapirstein and Johnson 1996
;
Shabaev et al. 2004
. In our calculations, we also modify the nonrelativistic energies of
2s
and
2p
states by including the Lamb shift correction
Johnson and Soff 1985
. Within the framework of the Line Profile approach, this can be performed by the accounting of an infinite series of self-energy and vacuum-polarization insertions into the internal electron lines of diagrams in Fig. (
1
). Arising geometric progression leads to the appearance of the corresponding Lamb shifts and levels widths in the energy denominators
Andreev et al. 2008
. As was shown in
Solovyev et al. 2015
;
Jentschura and Haas 2008
, inclusion of the Lamb shift slightly changes the BBR-induced ac-Stark shift in hydrogen. Fine splitting is also taken into account for the
2p_{1/2}
and
2p_{3/2}

An equally interesting aspect of such effects is the search for variations of the fundamental constants: the fine structure constant, the gravitational constant, and the proton-to-electron mass ratio
Dirac, P. A. M. 1937

Flambaum and Kozlov 2009
;
Safronova 2019

Webb et al. 2001

g

. The achieved level of experimental accuracy leads to the electron mass determination with a relative error of about
10^{-11}
. The corrections obtained in present work are close to this value and, can be considered as possible source for future experimental improvements.

IV
Conclusion

The bound-electron
g
-factor is a subject of high-precision measurements and theoretical calculations, which jointly are able to bring various results of fundamental importance. The effect of black-body radiation was considered previously for binding energies showing its importance in Rydberg atoms, atomic clocks, and frequency standards. In this work, we have investigated one-loop thermal correction for
1s
,
2s
,
2p_{1/2}
, and
2p_{3/2}
states of light H-like ions. Currently available experimental values are found to be insensitive to blackbody radiation. However, the potential importance of this effect for
n=2
states in future investigations is demonstrated, including determination of the fundamental constants and search for new physics.
