Determination of matter radius and neutron-skin thickness of 60,62,64 Ni from reaction cross section of proton scattering on 60,62,64 Ni targets
Shingo Tagami, Tomotsugu Wakasa, Masanobu Yahiro

Abstract
Background: In our previous work, we determined matter radii r m ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm m}({\rm exp}) and neutron-skin thickness r skin ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm skin}({\rm exp}) from reaction cross sections σ R ​ ( exp ) \sigma_{\rm R}({\rm exp}) of proton scattering on 208 Pb, 58 Ni, 40,48 Ca, 12 C targets, using the chiral (Kyushu) g g -matrix folding model with the densities calculated with Gogny-D1S-HFB (D1S-GHFB) with angular momentum projection (AMP). The resultant r skin ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm skin}({\rm exp}) agree with the PREX2 and CREX values. As for 58 Ni, our value is consistent with one determined from the differential cross section for 58 Ni+ 4 He scattering. As for p+ 60,62,64 N scattering, σ R ​ ( exp ) \sigma_{\rm R}({\rm exp}) are available as a function of incident energies E in E_{\rm in} , where E in = 22.8 ∼ 65.5 E_{\rm in}=22.8\sim 65.5 MeV for 60 Ni, E in = 40 , 60.8 E_{\rm in}=40,60.8 MeV for 62 Ni, E in = 40 , 60.8 E_{\rm in}=40,60.8 MeV for 64 Ni. Purpose: , Our aim is to determine matter radii r m ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm m}({\rm exp}) for 60,62,64 Ni from the σ R ​ ( exp ) \sigma_{\rm R}({\rm exp}) . Method: Our method is the Kyushu g g -matrix folding model with the densities scaled from D1S-GHFB+AMP densities, Results: Our skin values are r skin ​ ( exp ) = 0.076 ± 0.019 , 0.106 ± 0.192 , 0.162 ± 0.176 r_{\rm skin}({\rm exp})=0.076\pm 0.019,~0.106\pm 0.192,~0.162\pm 0.176 fm, and r m ​ ( exp ) = 3.759 ± 0.011 , 3.811 ± 0.107 , 3.864 ± 0.101 r_{\rm m}({\rm exp})=3.759\pm 0.011,~3.811\pm 0.107,~3.864\pm 0.101 fm for 60,62,64 Ni, respectively.

\displaystyle\rho_{\rm scaling}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}})=\frac{1}{\alpha^{3}}\rho({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}/\alpha)

\displaystyle\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{\langle{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}^{2}\rangle_{\rm scaling}}{\langle{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}^{2}\rangle}}.
