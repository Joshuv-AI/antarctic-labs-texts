Hilbert series of generic ideals in products of projective spaces
Ralf Fröberg

Abstract
If k ⁡ [ x 1 , … , x n ] / I = R = ∑ i ≥ 0 R i k[x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}]/I=R=\sum_{i\geq 0}R_{i} , k k a field, is a standard graded algebra, the Hilbert series of R R is the formal power series ∑ i ≥ 0 dim k R i ​ t i \sum_{i\geq 0}\dim_{k}R_{i}t^{i} . It is known already since Macaulay which power series are Hilbert series of graded algebras [ 12 ] . A much harder question is which series are Hilbert series if we fix the number of generators of I I and their degrees, say for ideals I = ( f 1 , … , f r ) I=(f_{1},\ldots,f_{r}) , deg ⁡ f i = d i \deg f_{i}=d_{i} , i = 1 , … , r i=1,\ldots,r . In some sense ”most” ideals with fixed degrees of their generators have the same Hilbert series. There is a conjecture for the Hilbert series of those ”generic” ideals, see below. In this paper we make a conjecture, and prove it in some cases, in the case of generic ideals of fixed degrees in the coordinate ring of ℙ 1 × ℙ 1 {\mathbb{P}}^{1}\times{\mathbb{P}}^{1} , which might be easier to prove.
