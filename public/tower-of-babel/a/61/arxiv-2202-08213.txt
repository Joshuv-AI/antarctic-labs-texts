A study of anisotropic compact star with MIT bag EoS in f ⁡ ( ℜ , T ) f(\Re,T) modified theory
H. D. Singh, J. Kumar

Abstract
In the current study, we investigated a specific model of anisotropic strange stars specially Her X-1, in the background of modified f ⁡ ( ℜ , T ) f(\Re,T) gravity by choosing f ⁡ ( ℜ , T ) = ℜ + 2 ​ ξ ​ T f(\Re,T)=\Re+2\xi T , where ℜ \Re is Ricci scalar, T T is the trace of the energy- momentum tensor and ξ \xi is a coupling constant. To obtained the solution for the modified field equations, we apply Buchdahl metric to our equations. We consider the case, when the matter is governed by MIT bag model equation of state as P r = 1 3 ​ ( ρ − 4 ​ B ) P_{r}=\frac{1}{3}(\rho-4B) , where B B is bag constant. We calculate the values of unknown parameters using Schwarzschild interior space-time followed by choosing the appropriate values of parameter ξ \xi , K K , β \beta and also tabulated different values of bag constant for different ξ \xi . We examine the physical validity of our model by performing tests such as energy conditions, equilibrium of the forces, the adiabatic index, redshift and some more. The observational results showed that the proposed f ⁡ ( ℜ , T ) f(\Re,T) model satisfies all these tests and are quite acceptable.
