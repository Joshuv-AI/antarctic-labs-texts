Proof of a conjecture on distribution of Laplacian eigenvalues and diameter, and beyond
Leyou Xu, Bo Zhou

Abstract
Ahanjideh, Akbari, Fakharan and Trevisan proposed a conjecture on the distribution of the Laplacian eigenvalues of graphs: for any connected graph of order n n with diameter d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 that is not a path, the number of Laplacian eigenvalues in the interval [ n − d + 2 , n ] [n-d+2,n] is at most n − d n-d . We show that the conjecture is true, and give a complete characterization of graphs for which the conjectured bound is attained. This establishes an interesting relation between the spectral and classical parameters. MSC: 05C50, 05C12, 15A15 Keywords: Laplacian eigenvalue, diameter

. One main technique is to
apply Weyl’s inequality and interlacing theorems to the corresponding matrices.
To simplify the proof of Theorem

Lemma 4.1
.

Let

and

be integers with
3\leq t\leq d-2
and

For positive integers

\displaystyle H_{n,d,t;a,b}

\displaystyle=P_{d+1}\cup K_{a}\cup K_{b}+\{wv_{i}:t-2\leq i\leq t,w\in V(K_{a})\}

\displaystyle\quad+\{wv_{i}:t\leq i\leq t+2,w\in V(K_{b})\},

Figure 3:
The graph

Proof.

Let

. It is easy to see that

.
Let

be the principal submatrix of

corresponding to the vertices of

Then

2.4
,
2.3
and

\displaystyle\mu_{n-d}(H_{n,d,t;a,b})

\displaystyle=\mu_{n-(n-a)+n-d-a}(H_{n,d,t;a,b})

\displaystyle\leq\rho_{n-d-a}(B)

\displaystyle\leq\mu_{n-d-a}(G_{n-a,d,t+1})+\rho_{1}(M)

\displaystyle=n-d-a+2+a=n-d+2.

Suppose that

that

\mathbf{x}

for each

so

. Similarly, from

at
v_{t}
and
v_{t-1}

x_{v_{t-1}}=x_{v_{t-2}}=0
. Thus,
\mathbf{x}
is a zero vector, which is a contradiction. It follows that
\mu_{n-d}(H_{n,d,t;a,b})<n-d+2
.
∎

For integers

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
n
and

be integers with

. Then

Proof.

If

Similarly, if

\mu_{2}(P_{n,t}^{++})<4

Suppose in the following that

Let

2.6
that
\mu_{2}(H)=4

Let

. Then

-1&\mbox{ if }\{i,j\}=\{t+1,n\},\\

\mu_{2}(P_{n,t}^{++})\leq\mu_{2}(H)+\rho_{1}(-S)=\mu_{2}(H)=4
.

Suppose that

. Then there exists a nonzero vector
\mathbf{x}
such that

and

for

From

x_{2}=-3x_{1}

Suppose that

. This shows that

for

.

\mu_{n-d}(G^{\prime}-e)<n-d+2
, where

G^{\prime}=G_{n,d,t}
.

If
e
joins two vertices outside
P
, then
G^{\prime}-e\cong G^{\prime}-v_{t}w
, where
w\in V(G^{\prime})\setminus V(P)
.
If
e
lies on
P
, then
e=v_{t-1}v_{t},v_{t}v_{t+1}
, and
G^{\prime}-e\cong G^{\prime}-v_{t-1}w,G^{\prime}-v_{t+1}w
, where
w\in V(G^{\prime})\setminus V(P)
.
So we may assume
e=v_{t-1}w,v_{t}w,v_{t+1}w
for some vertex
w
outside
P
.

If
d\leq n-3
, then the result follows from Lemma
4.4
.
Suppose that
d=n-2
.
If
e=v_{t-1}w
, then
P_{n}=v_{1}\dots v_{t}wv_{t+1}\dots v_{n-1}
is a path in
G^{\prime}-e
and

G^{\prime}-e-E(P_{n})
has a single edge
v_{t}v_{t+1}
. By Lemma
2.5
,
\mu_{2}(G^{\prime}-e)\leq\mu_{1}(P_{n})<4
.
Similar argument applies if
e=v_{t+1}w
.
If
e=v_{t}w
, then
G^{\prime}-e\cong P_{n,t-1}^{++}
, so we have
\mu_{n-d}(G^{\prime}-e)<n-d+2
by Lemma
4.3
.

Combining the above three cases, we have
\mu_{n-d}(G)<n-d+2
, so

m_{G}[n-d+2,n]<n-d
. This completes the proof.
∎
