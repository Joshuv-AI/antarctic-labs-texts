Partial-wave expansion of Λ ​ N ​ N \Lambda NN three-baryon interactions in chiral effective field theory
M. Kohno, H. Kamada, K. Miyagawa

Abstract
An expression of partial wave expansion of three-baryon interactions in chiral effective field theory is presented. The derivation follows the method by Hebeler et al. [Phys. Rev. C 91 , 044001 (2015)], but the final expression is more general. That is, a systematic treatment of the higher-rank spin-momentum structure of the interaction becomes possible. Using the derived formula, a Λ \Lambda -deuteron folding potential is evaluated. This information is valuable for inferring the possible contribution of the Λ ​ N ​ N \Lambda NN three-baryon forces to the hypertriton as the basis of further studies by sophisticated Faddeev calculations. A microscopic understanding of Λ ​ N ​ N \Lambda NN three-baryon forces together with two-body Λ ​ N \Lambda N interactions is essential for the description of hypernuclei and neutron-star matter.



I
Introduction

Any description of two-body baryon-baryon interactions in which various
degrees of freedom are eliminated or frozen is effective. When the interactions
are applied in many-body systems, the appearance of three-body interactions is
inevitable as induced interactions. The important role of three-body forces (3BFs)
in nuclear physics has been observed in scattering and binding properties of
few-nucleon systems
WGH98
;
SS02
;
PPW01
and also
in heavier nuclei and nuclear matter, in particular in connection with
saturation properties
PPW01
;
APR98
;
BB12
. The recent
development of the construction of baryon-baryon interactions in chiral effective
field theory (ChEFT)
EHM09
;
ME11
provides a systematic way to introduce
three-body (and more-than-three-body) forces in a power-counting scheme and
therefore quantifies the role of 3BFs as opposed to simple phenomenological adjustment.

The inclusions of 3BFs in a microscopic description of nuclei often need partial-wave
expansion in two Jacobi momenta. An efficient method was developed by
Hebeler
et al.

Heb15
for the local 3BFs.
Here the local means that the interaction is a function
of the momentum transfer of each Jacobi momentum except for the cutoff
regularization function that does not depend on angle variables. In their method,
the original eight-dimensional angular integration, though five dimensional
because of the rotational invariance, was reduced essentially to two dimensional.

In this article, following the derivation in Ref.
Heb15
,
a different expression for the partial-wave expansion of 3BFs
is presented, which is more systematic for treating higher-rank coupling of
spin and momentum vectors.

Before discussing the partial-wave expansion, the basic structure of leading-order
3BFs in ChEFT is summarized in Sec. II. The expression of partial wave decomposition
of 3BFs in momentum space concerning the Jacobi momenta
is presented in Sec. III. As an application of the derived expression, a possible role of
the
\Lambda
NN 3BFs in the hypertriton is studied by calculating a
\Lambda
-deuteron
folding potential from
\Lambda NN
3BFs. A summary follows in Sec. IV.

III
Partial-wave expansion

A 3BF is in general a function of the initial and final Jacobi momenta,

V_{3BF}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1})
in the case of the leftmost diagram of Fig. 2
with supressing the spin and isospin indices.
The partial-wave expansion requires integrals of the product of four spherical
harmonics and
V_{3BF}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1})
over the solid angles
related to Jacobi momenta
\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}
,
\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}
,
\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}
, and
\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}
for

V_{3BF}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1})
:

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\{(2\pi)^{3/2}\}^{4}}\int\cdots\int d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}}Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}m_{p}^{\prime}}^{*}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}})

\displaystyle\times Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}m_{q}^{\prime}}^{*}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}})Y_{\ell_{p}m_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})Y_{\ell_{q}m_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})V_{3BF}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}).

(5)

To make the expression compact, the following notation is used in the
subsequent derivation, in which the angular momenta
\ell_{p}
and
\ell_{q}
are coupled
to
L
, and
\ell_{p}^{\prime}
and
\ell_{q}^{\prime}
to
L^{\prime}
, respectively. That is, supplementing the summation

\sum_{m_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}m_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}}\sum_{m_{\ell_{p}}m_{\ell_{q}}}(\ell_{p}^{\prime}m_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}\ell_{q}^{\prime}m_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}|L^{\prime}M_{L}^{\prime})(\ell_{p}m_{\ell_{p}}\ell_{q}m_{\ell_{q}}|LM_{L}),

(6)

the angular integration on a 3BF
V_{3BF}
is expressed as

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{6}}\langle[Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}})]_{M_{L}^{\prime}}^{L^{\prime}}|V_{3BF}

\displaystyle\times|[Y_{\ell_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})]_{M_{L}}^{L}\rangle,

(7)

where the left- and right-angle brackets represent
d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}}
integration.

The angular momentum projection in momentum space postulates a complete
plane-wave basis
GL96
,

\langle\mbox{\boldmath$p$}^{\prime}|p\ell_{p}m_{p}\rangle=\frac{\delta(p^{\prime}-p)}{p^{\prime}p}Y_{\ell_{p}m_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}^{\prime}}).

(8)

The three-body basis states in jj coupling for the total three-body
angular momentum
J
are constructed as

\displaystyle|pq\alpha\rangle\equiv

\displaystyle|pq(\ell_{p}s_{p})j_{p}(\ell_{q}1/2)j_{q}(j_{p}j_{q})JM\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{m_{j_{p}}m_{j_{q}}}\sum_{m_{p}m_{s_{p}}}\sum_{m_{q}m_{s_{q}}}(j_{p}m_{j_{p}}j_{q}m_{j_{q}}|JM)

\displaystyle\times(\ell_{p}m_{p}s_{p}m_{s_{p}}|j_{p}m_{j_{p}})(\ell_{q}m_{q}s_{q}m_{s_{q}}|j_{p}m_{j_{p}})

\displaystyle\times|p\ell_{p}m_{p}\rangle\chi_{p}^{s_{p},m_{s_{p}}}|q\ell_{q}m_{q}\rangle\chi_{q}^{s_{q},m_{s_{q}}},

(9)

where
\chi_{p}^{s_{p},m_{s_{p}}}
and
\chi_{q}^{s_{q},m_{s_{q}}}
represent spin states of the
p

and
q
degrees of freedom, rspectively. The isospin state can be treated separately.
The basis states satisfy the orthonormality condition:

\langle p^{\prime}q^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}|pq\alpha\rangle=\frac{\delta(p^{\prime}-p)}{p^{\prime}p}\frac{\delta(q^{\prime}-q)}{q^{\prime}q}\delta_{\alpha^{\prime}\alpha}.

(10)

For the case of a local 3BF

V_{3BF}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime},\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1})=V_{3BF}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}-\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1},\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}-\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1})
,
the subtle manipulation
Heb15
of adding a radial part to the angle integration
while keeping the absolute value by a delta function is helpful,

\displaystyle d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}}\rightarrow d\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime\prime}\frac{\delta(p_{1}^{\prime\prime}-p_{1}^{\prime})}{{p_{1}^{\prime}}^{2}}\hskip 5.0pt\mbox{and}\hskip 5.0ptd\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}}\rightarrow d\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime\prime}\frac{\delta(q_{1}^{\prime\prime}-q_{1}^{\prime})}{{q_{1}^{\prime}}^{2}}.

(11)

Changing the variables
\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime\prime}
and
\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime\prime}
to
p
and
q
by the shifts
of
\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime\prime}=\mbox{\boldmath$p$}+\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}
and
\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime\prime}=\mbox{\boldmath$q$}+\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}
, Eq. (
7
) is modified as

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{6}}\langle[Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}})]_{M_{L}^{\prime}}^{L^{\prime}}|V_{3BF}

\displaystyle\times|[Y_{\ell_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})]_{M_{L}}^{L}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{({2\pi})^{6}}\int_{0}^{\infty}p^{2}dp\int_{0}^{\infty}q^{2}dq

\displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt\times\langle[Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}(\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}+\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}+\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{M_{L}^{\prime}}^{L^{\prime}}|

\displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt\times\frac{\delta(|\mbox{\boldmath$p$}-\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}|-p_{1}^{\prime})}{{p_{1}^{\prime}}^{2}}\frac{\delta(|\mbox{\boldmath$q$}-\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}|-q_{1}^{\prime})}{{q_{1}^{\prime}}^{2}}V_{3BF}

\displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt\times|[Y_{\ell_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})]_{M_{L}}^{L}\rangle.

(12)

The delta function can be written as follows by using a Legendre polynomial
of the first kind
P_{k}
:

\displaystyle\delta(|\mbox{\boldmath$p$}-\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}|-p_{1}^{\prime})=\frac{p_{1}^{\prime}}{pp_{1}}\delta\left(\cos\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}\mbox{\boldmath$p$}}-\frac{p_{1}^{\prime 2}-p_{1}^{2}-p^{2}}{2p_{1}p}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\pi\frac{p_{1}^{\prime}}{pp_{1}}\sum_{k^{\prime}}P_{k^{\prime}}(c_{p})(-1)^{k}\sqrt{\hat{k^{\prime}}}[Y_{k^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{k^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})]_{0}^{0},

(13)

\displaystyle\delta(|\mbox{\boldmath$q$}-\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}|-q_{1}^{\prime})=\frac{q_{1}^{\prime}}{qq_{1}}\delta\left(\cos\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}\mbox{\boldmath$q$}}-\frac{q_{1}^{\prime 2}-q_{1}^{2}-q^{2}}{2q_{1}q}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\pi\frac{q_{1}^{\prime}}{qq_{1}}\sum_{k}P_{k}(c_{q})(-1)^{k}\sqrt{\hat{k}}[Y_{k}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})\times Y_{k}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{0}^{0},

(14)

where
\hat{k}\equiv 2k+1
,
c_{p}\equiv\frac{p_{1}^{\prime 2}-p_{1}^{2}-p^{2}}{2p_{1}p}
, and

c_{q}\equiv\frac{q_{1}^{\prime 2}-q_{1}^{2}-q^{2}}{2q_{1}q}
.
These
\delta
functions restrict the
p
and
q
integrations as

\displaystyle p_{min}\equiv|p_{1}^{\prime}-p_{1}|\leq p\leq p_{max}\equiv p_{1}^{\prime}+p_{1},

(15)

\displaystyle q_{min}\equiv|q_{1}^{\prime}-q_{1}|\leq q\leq q_{max}\equiv q_{1}^{\prime}+q_{1}.

(16)

The spherical-harmonic functions
Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}(\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}+\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})
and

Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}+\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})
are also expanded as follows:

\displaystyle Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}m_{p}^{\prime}}(\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}+\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})=

\displaystyle\sum_{j_{p}+j_{p}^{\prime}=\ell_{p}^{\prime}}\sqrt{\frac{4\pi(\hat{\ell_{p}^{\prime}})!}{(\hat{j_{p}})!(\hat{j_{p}^{\prime}})!}}\frac{p_{1}^{j_{p}}p^{j_{p}^{\prime}}}{{p_{1}^{\prime}}^{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle\times[Y_{j_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{j_{p}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})]_{m_{p}^{\prime}}^{\ell_{p}^{\prime}},

(17)

\displaystyle Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}m_{q}^{\prime}}(\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}+\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})=

\displaystyle\sum_{j_{q}+j_{q}^{\prime}=\ell_{q}^{\prime}}\sqrt{\frac{4\pi(\hat{\ell_{q}^{\prime}})!}{(\hat{j_{q}})!(\hat{j_{q}^{\prime}})!}}\frac{q_{1}^{j_{q}}q^{j_{q}^{\prime}}}{{q_{1}^{\prime}}^{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle\times[Y_{j_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})\times Y_{j_{q}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{m_{q}^{\prime}}^{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}.

(18)

A straightforward evaluation of the recoupling of these decoupled spherical harmonics
finally gives

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{6}}\langle[Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}})]_{M_{L}^{\prime}}^{L^{\prime}}|V_{TPE}^{\Lambda NN}|[Y_{\ell_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})]_{M_{L}}^{L}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{({2\pi})^{6}}(-1)^{\ell_{p}^{\prime}+\ell_{q}^{\prime}-L^{\prime}}\sum_{JM}(-1)^{M_{L}^{\prime}}(L^{\prime}-M_{L}^{\prime}LM_{L}|JM)\frac{1}{p_{1}^{\prime}q_{1}^{\prime}{p_{1}}{q_{1}}}\int_{p_{min}}^{p_{max}}pdp\int_{q_{min}}^{q_{max}}qdq

\displaystyle\times(2\pi)^{2}\sum_{k^{\prime}k}\hat{k^{\prime}}\hat{k}P_{k^{\prime}}(c_{p})P_{k}(c_{q})\sum_{j_{p}+j_{p}^{\prime}=\ell_{p}^{\prime}}\sqrt{\frac{(\hat{\ell_{p}^{\prime}})!}{(\hat{j_{p}})!(\hat{j_{p}^{\prime}})!}}\frac{p_{1}^{j_{p}}p^{j_{p}^{\prime}}}{{p_{1}^{\prime}}^{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}}\sum_{j_{q}+j_{q}^{\prime}=\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(-1)^{j_{p}+j_{q}}\sqrt{\frac{(\hat{\ell_{q}^{\prime}})!}{(\hat{j_{q}})!(\hat{j_{q}^{\prime}})!}}\frac{q_{1}^{j_{q}}q^{j_{q}^{\prime}}}{{q_{1}^{\prime}}^{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}}\sqrt{\hat{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}\hat{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}\hat{L}\hat{L^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle\times\frac{1}{4\pi}\sum_{L_{p}L_{q}}\sqrt{\hat{j_{p}^{\prime}}\hat{j_{q}^{\prime}}\hat{j_{p}}\hat{j_{q}}}(k^{\prime}0j_{p}^{\prime}0|L_{p}0)(k0j_{q}^{\prime}0|L_{q}0)(k^{\prime}0j_{p}0|\ell_{p}0)(k0j_{q}0|\ell_{q}0)

\displaystyle\times\begin{Bmatrix}\ell_{q}&\ell_{q}^{\prime}&L_{q}\\
j_{q}^{\prime}&k&j_{q}\end{Bmatrix}\begin{Bmatrix}\ell_{p}&\ell_{p}^{\prime}&L_{p}\\
j_{p}^{\prime}&k^{\prime}&j_{p}\end{Bmatrix}\begin{Bmatrix}\ell_{p}^{\prime}&\ell_{q}^{\prime}&L^{\prime}\\
\ell_{p}&\ell_{q}&L\\
L_{p}&L_{q}&J\end{Bmatrix}\int d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}}\;[Y_{L_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})\times Y_{L_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{M}^{J}V_{TPE}^{\Lambda NN}(\mbox{\boldmath$p$},\mbox{\boldmath$q$})

(19)

Observing
P_{\ell}(\cos\widehat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})=(-1)^{\ell}\frac{4\pi}{\sqrt{\hat{\ell}}}[Y_{\ell}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})\times Y_{\ell}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{0}^{0}
, the above expression with
J=0

corresponds to Eq. (6) of Ref.
Heb15
.
It is verified numerically that Eq. (
19
) with
J=0
delivers
the same results as Eq. (6) of Ref.
Heb15
.
The evaluation of
\hat{I}_{L^{\prime}L}V_{3BF}
with
J>0
is straightforward
and transparent.

The angle integration in Eq. (
19
) for 3BFs in the form
of Eq. (
2
) needs

\displaystyle\int d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}}d\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}}\;[Y_{L_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})\times Y_{L_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{M}^{J}[Y_{\ell_{a}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})\times Y_{\ell_{b}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{\mu}^{K}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{K+\mu}\delta_{JK}\delta_{M,-\mu}\delta_{L_{p}\ell_{a}}\delta_{L_{q}\ell_{b}}(-1)^{\ell_{a}+\ell_{b}}.

(20)

This means that the angular momentum
J
in Eq. (
19
) corresponds to
the rank
K
of the tensor-product structure of the angular-momentum coupling
in 3BFs.

For actual calculations of the matrix element of 3BFs in various situations, it is
convenient to define a reduced matrix element in a form similar to the
Wigner-Eckart theorem,

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{6}}\langle[Y_{\ell_{p}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}^{\prime}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}^{\prime}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}^{\prime}})]_{M_{L}^{\prime}}^{L^{\prime}}|V_{TPE}^{(K,\ell_{a},\ell_{b})}(p,q)[Y_{\ell_{a}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}})\times Y_{\ell_{b}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}})]_{\mu}^{K}|[Y_{\ell_{p}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$p$}_{1}})\times Y_{\ell_{q}}(\hat{\mbox{\boldmath$q$}_{1}})]_{M_{L}}^{L}\rangle

V
Summary

We have presented an expression of partial-wave decomposition of 3BFs concerning
the relevant Jacobi momenta. The derivation essentially follows that of Hebeler

et al.

Heb15
, but the final formula differs in that it can systematically treat
the higher-rank spin and angular-momentum tensor-product structure of 3BFs.
Although the consideration is intended specifically for
\Lambda NN
3BFs and one set
of the Jacobi momenta, the formula is general, as far as 3BFs are a function
of the momentum transfer in each Jacobi momentum. Even if a regularization
function is angle-dependent, the 3BFs can be expressed in the form of Eq. (
2
)
and then the result of Eq. (
22
) is applicable.

As an application of the derived expression, the
\Lambda
-deuteron folding potential
from NNLO
\Lambda NN
3BFs is evaluated. At the present stage, the construction
of baryon-baryon interactions in the strangeness
S=-1
sector in ChEFT is practiced
up to the NLO level
NLO13
;
HMN20
. Even at this low order,
it is difficult to unambiguously determine coupling constants because of the lack of
sufficient experimental data, and therefore a plausible assumption of the SU(3) symmetry
has to be called for. In addition, there is no conclusive way to fix the parameters of the
two-pion exchange
\Lambda NN
3BFs that are basically of the NNLO. This means
that experimental and theoretical investigations are needed in the future. It is
essentially important to quantitatively establish the contribution of
\Lambda NN

3BFs in hypernuclei, which is also relevant for the understanding of the appearance
or absence of
\Lambda
hyperons in neutron star matter
MK18
;
GKW20
.

In particular, the investigation of the hypertriton is of fundamental importance.
Before doing full Faddeev
calculations for the hypertriton including
\Lambda NN
3BFs,
it is worthwhile to estimate the possible role of the 3BFs for the hypertriton.
The present folding potential calculation indicates the necessity of considering

\Lambda NN
3BFs in the theoretical study of the hypertriton because the
quantitative estimation of their effects will influence the parametrization of

\Lambda N
two-body interactions.
