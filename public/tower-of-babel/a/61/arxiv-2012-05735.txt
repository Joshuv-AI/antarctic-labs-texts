Probing Top Changing Neutral Higgs Couplings at Colliders
Wei-Shu Hou, Tanmoy Modak

Abstract
The h ⁡ ( 125 ) h(125) boson, discovered only in 2012, is lower than the top quark in mass, hence t → c ​ h t\to ch search commenced immediately thereafter, with current limits at the per mille level and improving. As the t → c ​ h t\to ch rate vanishes with the h h - H H mixing angle cos ⁡ γ → 0 \cos\gamma\to 0 , we briefly review the collider probes of the top changing t ​ c ​ H / t ​ c ​ A tcH/tcA coupling ρ t ​ c \rho_{tc} of the exotic C ​ P CP -even/odd Higgs bosons H / A H/A . Together with an extra top conserving t ​ t ​ H / t ​ t ​ A ttH/ttA coupling ρ t ​ t \rho_{tt} , one has an enhanced c ​ b ​ H + cbH^{+} coupling alongside the familiar t ​ b ​ H + tbH^{+} coupling, where H + H^{+} is the charged Higgs boson. The main processes we advocate are c ​ g → t ​ H / A → t ​ t ​ c ¯ , t ​ t ​ t ¯ cg\to tH/A\to tt\bar{c},\;tt\bar{t} (same-sign top and triple-top), and c ​ g → b ​ H + → b ​ t ​ b ¯ cg\to bH^{+}\to bt\bar{b} . We also discuss some related processes such as c ​ g → t ​ h ​ h cg\to thh , t ​ Z ​ H tZH that depend on cos ⁡ γ \cos\gamma being nonzero, comment briefly on g ​ g → H / A → t ​ t ¯ , t ​ c ¯ gg\to H/A\to t\bar{t},t\bar{c} resonant production, and touch upon the ρ t ​ u \rho_{tu} coupling.

Summary and Prospect

In the past few years, we have verified the Yukawa couplings of
t
,
b
quarks
and
\tau
,
\mu
charged leptons, making extra Yukawa couplings involving
a second Higgs doublet plausible and attractive.
This general 2HDM with extra Yukawa couplings point to
extra Higgs bosons
H
,
A
and
H^{+}
in the sub-TeV range, which is
based on naturalness of the second set of dimension-4 couplings
unique to the Higgs sector: Higgs quartic self-couplings.
The exotic bosons are well hidden so far by fermion mass-mixing hierarchy
and alignment, the smallness of
h
–
H
mixing angle
c_{\gamma}
.

The search for
t\to ch
(and concurrently for
h\to\tau\mu
) is ongoing.
If it does not emerge with full Run 2 data, it would constrain
the product of
\rho_{tc}\,c_{\gamma}
. But given that
c_{\gamma}

is small, sizable
\rho_{tc}
would still be allowed.
In this brief review, we advocate the search for

cg\to tH/A\to tt\bar{c},\,tt\bar{t}
and
cg\to bH^{+}\to bt\bar{b}
at the LHC,
where production, unhampered by small
c_{\gamma}
, depends on sizable
\rho_{tc}
,
while the
tt\bar{t}
and
bt\bar{b}
final states require finite
\rho_{tt}

for
H/A\to t\bar{t}
and
H^{+}\to t\bar{b}
decays.
It would be interesting, therefore, to follow up with full Run 2 data on the

m_{A}\sim 400
GeV excess seen by CMS in
t\bar{t}
resonance search.
Both extra top Yukawa couplings
\rho_{tt}
and
\rho_{tc}

can drive electroweak baryogenesis, providing further impetus for search.
LHC Run 2 data may already be promising,
and Run 2+3 data would be even more revealing,
while HL-LHC may hold the ultimate promise for discovery,
which would open up a new chapter at the intersection of Higgs and flavor physics.
