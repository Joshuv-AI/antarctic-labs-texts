Dark matter, Z ′ Z^{\prime} , vector-like quark at the LHC and b → s ​ μ ​ μ b\to s\mu\mu anomaly
Wei Chao 1 , Hongxin Wang 2 , Lei Wang 2 , Yang Zhang 3

Abstract
In this paper, combining the b → s ​ μ + ​ μ − b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-} anomaly and dark matter observables, we study the capability of LHC to test dark matter, Z ′ Z^{\prime} , and vector-like quark. We focus on a local U ​ ( 1 ) L μ − L τ U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}} model with a vector-like S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L SU(2)_{L} doublet quark Q Q and a complex singlet scalar whose lightest component X I X_{I} is a candidate of dark matter. After imposing relevant constraints, we find that the b → s ​ μ + ​ μ − b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-} anomaly and the relic abundance of dark matter favor m X I < 350 m_{X_{I}}<350 GeV and m Z ′ < 450 m_{Z^{\prime}}<450 GeV for m Q < m_{Q}< 2 TeV and m X R < m_{X_{R}}< 2 TeV (the heavy partner of m X I m_{X_{I}} ). The current searches for jets and missing transverse momentum at the LHC sizably reduce the mass ranges of the vector-like quark, and m Q m_{Q} is required to be larger than 1.7 TeV. Finally, we discuss the possibility of probing these new particles at the high luminosity LHC via the QCD process p ​ p → D ​ D ¯ pp\to D\bar{D} or p ​ p → U ​ U ¯ pp\to U\bar{U} followed by the decay D → s ⁡ ( b ) ​ Z ′ ​ X I D\to s(b)Z^{\prime}X_{I} or U → u ⁡ ( c ) ​ Z ′ ​ X I U\to u(c)Z^{\prime}X_{I} and then Z ′ → μ + ​ μ − Z^{\prime}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-} . Taking a benchmark point of m Q m_{Q} =1.93 TeV, m Z ′ = 170 m_{Z^{\prime}}=170 GeV, and m X I = m_{X_{I}}= 145 GeV, we perform a detailed Monte Carlo simulation, and find that such benchmark point can be accessible at the 14 TeV LHC with an integrated luminosity 3000 fb -1 .

Conclusion

In this paper we study the capability of LHC to test DM,

, and vector-like quark in a local

U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}

b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}

The current searches for jets and missing transverse momentum
