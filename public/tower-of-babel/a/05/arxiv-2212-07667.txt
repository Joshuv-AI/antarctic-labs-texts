Extended Weil representations: the non-dyadic local field cases
Chun-Hui Wang

Abstract
. Let F F be a non-archimedean local field of odd residual characteristic. Let W W be a symplectic vector space over F F . It is known that there are different Weil representations of a Meteplectic covering group Mp ⁡ ( W ) \operatorname{Mp}(W) . By utilizing twisted actions, we reorganize these representations as a representation of PGMp ± ⁡ ( W ) \operatorname{PGMp}^{\pm}(W) , a covering group related to the projective similitude symplectic group.
