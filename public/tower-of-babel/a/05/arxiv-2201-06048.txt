From local to global congruences for automorphic representations
Boyer Pascal

Abstract
Given a irreducible automorphic representation Π \Pi of a similitude group G / ℚ G/{\mathbb{Q}} giving rise to a KHT-Shimura variety, given a local congruence of the local component of Π \Pi at a fixed place p p , we justify the existence of a global automorphic representation Π ′ \Pi^{\prime} with the same weight and the same level outside p p than Π \Pi , such that Π \Pi and Π ′ \Pi^{\prime} are weakly congruent. The arguments rest on the separation of the various contributions coming either from torsion or on the distinct families of automorphic representations, to the modulo l l reduction of the cohomology of Harris-Taylor perverse sheaves.

1.
Introduction

The first appearance of automorphic congruences can be trace back to
Ramanujan’s works on the
\tau
-functions. Now existence and
construction of higher dimension automorphic congruences play an
essential role in particular in the Langlands program.

One possible approach
is to look at the cohomology groups of some
\overline{{\mathbb{Z}}}_{l}
-local system
on a Shimura variety associated to some reductive group
G/{\mathbb{Q}}
,
whose free quotients are expected to be of automorphic
nature. The idea is then to take two (or two families of) such local systems those modulo
l
reduction
is related and be able to relate the modulo
l
reduction of their cohomology
groups. We then face two main problems.

(a)

The torsion may interfere and prevent us to relate the modulo
l
reduction
of the free quotients of the cohomology groups of our two
\overline{{\mathbb{Z}}}_{l}
-local
systems.

(b)

Even if we can manage about the torsion, the

\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}_{l}
-cohomology of our local systems may involve different sorts
of automorphic representations and we would only be able to construct
imprecise automorphic congruences.

In this article we are able, playing with all the Harris-Taylor local systems associated to one
cuspidal representation, to deal with this two problems when
G
is a similitude
group with signature
(1,d-1)
and the associated Shimura variety is of
Kottwitz-Harris-Taylor type. More precisely we first prove the conjecture 5.10
of
[
4
]
which says that the modulo
l
reduction of the torsion
submodule of the cohomology groups of the Harris-Taylor perverse sheaves
only depends on the modulo
l
reduction of the perverse sheaf. Secondly
we are able to separate the contribution of the automorphic representations
according to the shape of their local component at the place considered,
in the following sense.

Main result
:
We start with a irreducible automorphic
representation
\Pi
of
G({\mathbb{A}})
which locally at some place
v
looks like

\mathop{\mathrm{Speh}}\nolimits_{s}\bigl({\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits}_{t_{1}}(\pi_{v,1})\times\cdots{\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits}_{t_{r}}(\pi_{v,r})\bigr)
where

\pi_{v,1},\cdots,\pi_{v,r}
are irreducible cuspidal representations. We then
consider some local congruence
\pi^{\prime}_{v,1}
of
\pi_{v,1}
which we suppose to be
supercuspidal modulo
l
, and we then
construct a irreducible automorphic
representation
\Pi^{\prime}
of
G({\mathbb{A}})
such that

•

locally
\Pi^{\prime}
at the place
v
is isomorphic to
\mathop{\mathrm{Speh}}\nolimits_{s}({\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits}_{t^{\prime}_{1}}(\pi^{\prime}_{v,1})\times\cdots\times{\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits}_{t^{\prime}_{r^{\prime}}}(\pi^{\prime}_{v,r^{\prime}})
with
t^{\prime}_{1}=t_{1}
and
\pi^{\prime}_{v,i}
,
for
i=2,\cdots,r^{\prime}
irreducible cuspidal representations,

•

\Pi^{\prime}
share with

More precisely theorem
6.2
gives a quantitative version in terms of a
equality between multiplicities in the space of automorphic forms.
