Background

Blocks.

Blocks are sometimes but not always formed out of smaller units.
For example, a NAND flash block is made out of smaller units called
pages
.
However, this is not always the case, as operating systems often use pages as virtual blocks.
To make more confusing, a cache block is often a subset of a total data page.
Luckily, there are no paging statements (as opposed to blocking statements in Verilog) because what would they even do?
