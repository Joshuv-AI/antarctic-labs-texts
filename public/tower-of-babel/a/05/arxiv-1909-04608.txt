Probing effects of new physics in Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ {{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}} decays
Martina Ferrillo, Abhijit Mathad, Patrick Owen, Nicola Serra

Abstract
We present, for the first time, the six-fold differential decay density expression for Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ {{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}\ell^{-}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\ell}} , taking into account the polarisation of the Λ b 0 {\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b} baryon and a complete basis of new physics operators. Using the expected yield in the current dataset collected at the LHCb experiment, we present sensitivity studies to determine the experimental precision on the Wilson coefficients of the new physics operators with Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ {{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}} decays in two scenarios. In the first case, unpolarised Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ {{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}} decays with Λ c + {\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c} → \rightarrow p p K + {K}^{+} π − {\pi}^{-} are considered, whereas polarised Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ {{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}} decays with Λ c + {\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c} → \rightarrow p p K S 0 {K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}} are studied in the second. For the latter scenario, the experimental precision that can be achieved on the determination of Λ b 0 {\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b} polarisation and Λ c + {\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c} weak decay asymmetry parameter is also presented.

\displaystyle\mathcal{O}_{{C_{V_{L}}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bar{c}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}b_{L}\,\bar{\ell}_{L}\gamma_{\mu}\nu_{L}\,,

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bar{c}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}b_{R}\,\bar{\ell}_{L}\gamma_{\mu}\nu_{L}\,,

5
Experimental sensitivity

In this section the sensitivity that can be achieved on the Wilson coefficients, by studying the differential decay density in different scenarios, is presented.

In the first case,
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}
baryons are considered to be produced unpolarised. The angular distribution of the
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}
decay is integrated over and the
q^{2}
and
\cos\theta_{l}
distributions are measured.
The expression for the employed fit model is shown in Eq.(
F
) of Appendix
F
. The expected signal yield is determined from Ref.
[
26
]
, where the
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}\rightarrow{p}{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}
decay mode is adopted.
When extrapolated to the current LHCb dataset of 9
\text{\,fb}^{-1}
, this gives 7.5M expected signal candidates
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}}
.
The abundant signal yield suggests that the
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}\rightarrow{p}{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}
decay mode is the most sensitive to NP operators, as favoured by the experimental signature.

For the second scenario the
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}\rightarrow{p}{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}}}

{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}
is foreseen. In this case, the angles

6
Conclusions

In this study a general expression for the effective Lagrangian governing
b\rightarrow c\l\nu_{l}
transitions has been considered, including NP contributions through a complete basis of dimension-six operators and assuming only left-handed neutrinos.
Using this formalism, we presented for the first time an expression for the six-fold differential decay density for polarised
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}\ell^{-}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\ell}}
decays, with subsequent
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}

\rightarrow

p

{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}}
decay.

In addition, we carried out sensitivity studies to determine the experimental precision on the Wilson coefficients which can be achieved through the analysis of
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}}
decays at the
LHCb
experiment.
When considering the integrated Run I and Run II data samples collected at
LHCb
.
The first case considered was the decay channel
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}

\rightarrow

{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}
(
\rightarrow

p

{K}^{-}

{\pi}^{+}
)
\mu^{-}

{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}
, where the 2D distribution in
q^{2}
and
\cos\theta_{l}
was studied.
The second explored scenario focused on
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}

\rightarrow

{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}
(
\rightarrow

p

{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}}
)
\mu^{-}

{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}
decays, including polarisation effects on the production of
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}
. At this purpose, the 4D distributions in
q^{2}
,
\cos\theta_{l}
,
\cos\theta_{p}
and
\phi_{p}
variables were inspected.
Since a missing unreconstructed neutrino in the final state spoils the experimental resolution on
q^{2}
and
\cos\theta_{l}
, in both of the mentioned cases the resolution effects were folded into the fit model through a migration matrix.

The results of the sensitivity studies show that the best precision on the Wilson coefficients can be achieved by probing the
q^{2}
and
\cos\theta_{l}
distributions of
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}

\rightarrow

{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}
(
\rightarrow

p

{K}^{-}

{\pi}^{+}
)
\mu^{-}

{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}
decays collected at
LHCb
, leading to a good sensitivity to
C_{V_{R}}
,
C_{T_{L}}
,
C_{S_{R}}
and
C_{S_{L}}
.
No sensitivity is expected to overall global factors, such as
V_{{c}{b}}
and
C_{V_{L}}
, as the present study is performed on the normalised differential decay distributions.

Although no enhanced sensitivity to the Wilson coefficients could be achieved through the investigation of 4D kinematic distributions of
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}

\rightarrow

{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}
(
\rightarrow

p

{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}}
)
\mu^{-}

{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}
decay channel, they do however provide a prospect of measuring
\alpha_{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}
and
P_{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}
. The parameter
\alpha_{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}
is particularly promising, with a precision which is two orders of magnitude smaller than that measured by the
BESIII
experiment.
The precision on
P_{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}
could also be improved by performing a full six-dimensional angular analysis.
That would require large signal yields expected at the LHCb upgrades and a different treatment of the resolution.

In this paper we have assumed
C\!P
conservation and considered the Wilson coefficients to be real variables.
To further distinguish NP models, one could easily extend the present study allowing Wilson coefficients to be complex to probe
C\!P
violation in
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}}
decays.
The results of the sensitivity studies have been compared to the model-independent constraints obtained in global fits to
B\rightarrow D^{(*)}{\ell^{-}}{{\nu}_{\ell}}
semileptonic decays, presented in Ref.
[
22
]
, and have shown a significant improvement in the achievable experimental precision.
As a consequence, it can be concluded that studying
{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{0}_{b}}\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{\mu^{-}}{{\overline{\nu}}_{\mu}}
decays at the
LHCb
experiment will not only lead to a more precise measurement on
{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}
decay asymmetry parameter, but also allows to place
stringent world leading constraints on the Wilson coefficients of the corresponding NP operators.
