(super)Schwarzian mechanics arXiv: 2111.04643
Nikolay Kozyrev, and Sergey Krivonos

Abstract
In this paper we revisit the construction of supersymmetric Schwarzians using nonlinear realizations. We show that 𝒩 = 0 , 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 {\cal N}=0,1,2,3,4 supersymmetric Schwarzians can be systematically obtained as certain projections of Maurer-Cartan forms of superconformal groups after imposing simple conditions on them. We also present the supersymmetric Schwarzian actions defined as the integrals of products of Cartan forms. In contrast with the previous attempts to obtain the super-Schwarzians within nonlinear realizations technique, our set of constraints does not impose any restriction on the super-Schwarzians.

In this work we re-consider the application of the method of nonlinear realizations to the

{\cal N}

\omega_{P}=\triangle\tau,\omega^{i}_{Q}=d\theta^{i}
.
Here, the forms
\triangle\tau,d\theta^{i}

{\cal N}

The final constraint reads
\omega_{D}=0

We explicitly show that this minimal set of constraints is enough to express
all Cartan forms of the
{\cal N}=0,1,2,3,4
(super)conformal groups in terms of corresponding (super-)Schwarzians and their derivatives.

In the cases of higher supersymmetries the calculations quickly become rather cumbersome. Having at hands the constraints written on the Cartan forms (not on their projections!) it proved useful to use the Maurer-Cartan equations which

{\cal N}

{\cal N}
super-Schwarzian

The idea to use the “boundary” superspace to impose the proper constrains on the Cartan forms was firstly formulated in
AG1

AG2

AG1
;
AG2
;
AG3
;
AG4
;
AG5
