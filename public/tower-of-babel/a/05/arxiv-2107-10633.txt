Abstract
. The paper studies the interpolation properties of net spaces N p , q ​ ( M ) N_{p,q}(M) , when M M is the set of dyadic cubes in ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} , and also when M M is the family of all cubes with parallel faces to the coordinate axes in ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} . It is shown that, in the case when M M is the set of dyadic cubes the scale of spaces is closed with respect to the real interpolation method. In the case, when M M is the set of all cubes with parallel faces to the coordinate axes, an analogue of the Marcinkiewicz-Calderon theorem on cones of non-negative functions is given.

Consider the first term
