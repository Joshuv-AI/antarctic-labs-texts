Peiyu Zhang, Dajun Liu

Abstract
In this article, firstly, we introduce the notion of star modules with respect to a balanced pair and obtain some properties. We mainly give the relationship between n n - 𝒳 {\mathscr{X}} star modules and n n - 𝒳 {\mathscr{X}} tilting modules [ 9 ] , and a new characterization of n n - 𝒳 {\mathscr{X}} tilting modules. MSC2010: 16D90 18G05 18G15 18E40 Keywords : balanced pair, relative star module, relative tilting module.

Let

Recall that an subcategory

Dually, we have the definition of the right
\mathscr{C}
-resolution and covariantly finite subcategory.

The notion of the balanced pair was first introduced by Chen
[
2
]
, the author mainly given a triangle-equivalence
between two homotopy categories of complexes, see
[
2

. Furthermore, the author proved that there exists a
triangle-equivalence between the homotopy category of Gorenstein projective modules and the
homotopy category of Gorenstein injective modules when

Definition 1.1

[
2
]
A pair
({\mathscr{X}}
,
{\mathscr{Y}})
of additive subcategories in an Abelian category
{\mathscr{A}}
is called a balanced pair if the following
conditions are satisfied:

(1)
the subcategory
{\mathscr{X}}
is contravariantly finite and
{\mathscr{Y}}
is covariantly finite;

(2)

{\mathscr{X}}
-resolution

(3)
for each object N, there is a
{\mathscr{Y}}
-coresolution
N\longrightarrow Y^{\bullet}
such that it is exact by applying the
functors
\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathscr{A}}}(X,~-)
for all
X\in{\mathscr{X}}
.

be an Abelian category. A contravariantly finite subcategory
\mathscr{C}
of

is admissible provided that each right

\mathscr{C}

Dually, the notion of coadmissible is defined.
Recalled that a balanced pair
