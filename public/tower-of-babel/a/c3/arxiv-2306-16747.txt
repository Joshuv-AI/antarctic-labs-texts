Spectral extremal graphs for edge blow-up of star forests Thanks: Research was partially supported by the National Nature Science Foundation of China (grant numbers 11871329, 11971298, 1220010800) and Hainan Provincial Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 122QN218)
Jing Wang Zhenyu Ni Liying Kang Yi-zheng Fan

Abstract
The edge blow-up of a graph G G , denoted by G p + 1 G^{p+1} , is obtained by replacing each edge of G G with a clique of order p + 1 p+1 , where the new vertices of the cliques are all distinct. Yuan [J. Comb. Theory, Ser. B, 152 (2022) 379–398] determined the range of the Turán numbers for edge blow-up of all bipartite graphs and the exact Turán numbers for edge blow-up of all non-bipartite graphs. In this paper, we prove that the graphs with the maximum spectral radius in an n n -vertex graph without any copy of edge blow-up of star forests are the extremal graphs for edge blow-up of star forests when n n is sufficiently large. Keywords: Spectral radius; Edge blow-up; Spectral extremal graph AMS (2000) subject classification: 05C50; 05C35

Introduction

Given a simple graph
G=(V(G),E(G))
, let
\delta(G)
and
\Delta(G)
be its minimum and maximum degrees of
G
, respectively. We use

to denote the number of edges in

. For

denotes the set of edges between
V_{1}
and
V_{2}
, and

. For any

, let
N(S)=\cup_{u\in S}\{v:uv\in E(G)\}
,
d_{S}(v)=|N_{S}(v)|=|N(v)\cap S|
.
G\setminus S
denotes the graph obtained from
G
by deleting all vertices in
S
and their incident edges.
G[S]
denotes the graph induced by
S
whose vertex set is
S
and whose edge set consists of all edges of
G
which have both ends in
S
. A set
M
of disjoint edges of
G
is called a
matching
in
G
. The
matching number
, denoted by
\nu(G)
, is the maximum cardinality of a matching in
G
. We call a matching with
k
edges a
k
-matching
, denoted by
M_{k}
.
For two vertex disjoint graphs
G
and

, the
union
of
G
and
H
, denoted by
G\cup H
, is the graph with vertex set
V(G)\cup V(H)
and edge set
E(G)\cup E(H)
. The
join
of
G
and
H
, denoted by
G\vee H
, is the graph obtained from
G\cup H
by adding all edges between
V(G)
and
V(H)
.
K_{r}
denotes the complete graph of order
r
. Let
K_{r}(n_{1},\cdots,n_{r})
be the complete
r
-partite graph with classes of sizes
n_{1},\cdots,n_{r}
. If
\sum_{i=1}^{r}n_{i}

=n
and
|n_{i}-n_{j}|\leq 1
for any
1\leq i<j\leq r
, then
K_{r}(n_{1},\cdots,n_{r})
is called an
r
-partite
Turán graph
, denoted by
T_{r}(n)
. Let
S_{k}
be a star of
k
edges. The
center
of a star is the vertex of maximum degree in the star. A
star forest
is the union of vertex disjoint stars. We use
S_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
to denote
\cup_{i=1}^{q}S_{k_{i}}
, where
k_{1}\geq k_{2}\geq\cdots\geq k_{q}\geq 1
.

Given a family of graphs
\mathcal{F}
, a graph

is called

-

if it does not contain any copy of
F
in
\mathcal{F}
. If
\mathcal{F}=\{F\}
, we use

-free instead of
\mathcal{F}
-free. In 1941, Turán
[
18
]
proved that if

-free of order

, then

, with equality if and only if
G=T_{r}(n)
. The problem of determining the maximum number of edges among all
n
-vertex
\mathcal{F}
-free graphs is usually called the Turán type extremal problem. The
Turán number
, denoted by
\mathrm{ex}(n,\mathcal{F})
, is the maximum number of edges among all
n
-vertex
\mathcal{F}
-free graphs. An
n
-vertex
\mathcal{F}
-free graph with

edges is called an

for
\mathcal{F}
. Denote by
\mathrm{Ex}(n,\mathcal{F})
the set of all extremal graphs for
\mathcal{F}
.

Given a graph
G
and an integer

, the
edge blow-up
of
G
, denoted by
G^{p+1}
, is obtained by replacing each edge of
G
with a clique of order
p+1
, where the new vertices of the cliques are all distinct. Simonovits
[
16
]
and Moon
[
10
]
determined the extremal graphs for the edge blow-up of matchings. Chen, Gould, Pfender and Wei
[
1
]
determined the Turán number for the edge blow-up of stars. There are other extremal results for the edge blow-up of paths, cycles and trees, see
[
6
,
9
,
19
,
11
]
. In 2022, Yuan
[
21
]
determined the range of the Turán numbers for edge blow-up of all bipartite graphs and the exact Turán numbers for edge blow-up of all non-bipartite graphs.

The
spectral radius
of a graph
G
is the spectral radius of its adjacent matrix
A(G)
, denoted by
\rho(G)
.
In 2010, Nikiforov
[
15
]
proposed the following spectral Turán type extremal problem: What is the maximum spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
\mathcal{F}
-free graphs? We denote by
\mathrm{Ex_{sp}}(n,F)
the set of all graphs which attain the maximum spectral radius.
Nikiforov
[
13
]
determined the spectral version of Turán’s extremal result, and proved that if

is a

-free graph on

, with equality if and only if

.
Cioabă, Feng, Tait and Zhang
[
4
]
proved that the graph attaining the maximum spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
S_{k}^{3}
-free graphs is a member of
\mathrm{Ex}(n,S_{k}^{3})
.
In 2022, Zhai, Liu and Xue
[
22
]
determined the unique spectral extremal graphs for
S_{k}^{3}
when
n
is sufficiently large. Let
H_{s,k}
be the graph obtained from s triangles and
k
odd cycles of lengths

5
by sharing a common vertex.
Li and Peng
[
7
]
showed that
\mathrm{Ex_{sp}}(n,H_{s,k})\subseteq\mathrm{Ex}(n,H_{s,k})
for
n
large enough.
Lin, Zhai and Zhao
[
8
]
proved that
\mathrm{Ex_{sp}}(n,\Gamma_{k})\subseteq\mathrm{Ex}(n,\Gamma_{k})
for sufficiently large

,
where
\Gamma_{k}
is the family of graphs consisting of
k
-edge-disjoint
triangles.
Desai, Kang, Li, Ni, Tait and Wang
[
5
]
generalized the result of
[
4
]
to

.
Cioabă, Desai and Tait
[
3
]
raised the following conjecture: Let

be any graph such that the graphs in

are Turán graphs plus

edges. Then for sufficiently large

-free graphs on

[

Recently, Ni, Wang and Kang

proved that if

-free graphs on

.

In this paper, we consider the spectral extremal problem for the edge blow-up of star forest
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
. The following result is obtained.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
p\geq 2
,
q\geq 1
, and
k_{1}\geq k_{2}\geq\cdots\geq k_{q}\geq 1
. For sufficiently large
n
, if
H
has the maximum spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
-free graphs, then

H\in\mathrm{Ex}(n,S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}).

Preliminaries

In this section, we give some notations and useful lemmas.
Let
G
be a simple graph with matching number
\nu(G)
and maximum degree
\Delta(G)
. For two given integers
\nu
and
\Delta
, define
f(\nu,\Delta)=\max\{e(G):\nu(G)\leq\nu,\Delta(G)\leq\Delta\}
. In 1976, Chvátal and Hanson
[
2
]
obtained the following result.

Theorem 2.1

(
[
2
]
)
.

For integers
\nu\geq 1
and
\Delta\geq 1
, we have

f(\nu,\Delta)=\Delta\nu+\left\lfloor\frac{\Delta}{2}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{\nu}{\lceil{\Delta}/{2}\rceil}\right\rfloor\leq\Delta\nu+\nu.

Denote by
\mathcal{E}_{\nu,\Delta}
the set of the graphs obtained the upper bound
in Theorem
2.1
.
Let
H(n,p,q)={K}_{q-1}\vee T_{p}(n-q+1)
and
\mathcal{H}(n,p,q,k_{q}-1)
be a set of graphs obtained from
K_{q-1}\vee T_{p}(n-q+1)
by putting a copy of
{E}_{k_{q}-1,k_{q}-1}\in\mathcal{E}_{k_{q}-1,k_{q}-1}
in one class of
T_{p}(n-q+1)
. Let
h(n,p,q)=e(H(n,p,q))
.
Using Theorem 2.3 in
[
21
]
on graph
G=S_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
, we obtain that

\mathrm{ex}(n,S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}})\leq h(n,p,q)+f(k_{q}-1,k_{q}-1).

Combining with the fact that the graphs in
\mathcal{H}(n,p,q,k_{q}-1)
are
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
-free, we have the following result.

Theorem 2.2
.

If
n
is large enough, then we have

\mathrm{ex}(n,S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}})=h(n,p,q)+f(k_{q}-1,k_{q}-1).

(1)

Moreover, the graphs in
\mathcal{H}(n,p,q,k_{q}-1)
are the only extremal graph for
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
.

Chen, Gould, Pfender and Wei
[
1
]
gave the following lemma.

Lemma 2.1

(
[
1
]
)
.

Suppose that
G
is a
S_{k}^{r}
-free graph, and
V(G)=V_{1}\cup V_{2}\cup\cdots\cup V_{r-1}
is a vertex partition of
G
. Let
E_{cr}(G)=\cup_{1\leq i<j\leq r-1}E(V_{i},V_{j})
be the set of crossing edges of
G
. For any
i\in[r-1]
and
v\in V_{i}
, if

\displaystyle\sum_{j\neq i}\nu(G[V_{j}])\leq k-1\ \ \ \mbox{and}\ \ \ \Delta(G[V_{i}])\leq k-1,

\displaystyle d_{G[V_{i}]}(v)+\sum_{j\neq i}\nu(G[N(v)\cap V_{j}])\leq k-1,

then

\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}|E(G[V_{i}])|-\left(\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq r-1}|V_{i}||V_{j}|-|E_{cr}(G)|\right)\leq f(k-1,k-1).

The following lemma was given in
[
4
]
.

Lemma 2.2

(
[
4
]
)
.

Let
V_{1},\cdots,V_{n}
be
n
finite sets. Then

|V_{1}\cap\cdots\cap V_{n}|\geq\sum_{i=1}^{n}|V_{i}|-(n-1)|\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}V_{i}|

By the Perron-Frobenius theorem,
\rho(G)
is the largest eigenvalue of

.
Let
\mathbf{x}=(x_{1},\cdots,x_{n})^{\mathrm{T}}
be an eigenvector corresponding to

. Then for any

and

,

\rho(G)x_{i}=\sum_{ij\in E(G)}x_{j}.

(2)

By the Rayleigh quotient, we have

\rho(G)=\max_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}\frac{\mathbf{x}^{\mathrm{T}}A(G)\mathbf{x}}{\mathbf{x}^{\mathrm{T}}\mathbf{x}}=\max_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}\frac{2\sum_{ij\in E(G)}x_{i}x_{j}}{\mathbf{x}^{\mathrm{T}}\mathbf{x}}.

(3)

From the spectral version of the Stability Theorem given by Nikiforov
[
14
]
, we get the following result.

Lemma 2.3

(
[
5
]
)
.

Let

Proof of Theorem
1.1

In this section, we suppose that
H
has the maximum spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
-free graphs. Then we will prove that
e(H)=\mathrm{ex}(n,S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}})
for sufficiently large

.
Clearly,
H
is connected. Let
\rho(H)
be the spectral radius of
H
. Then by the Perron-Frobenius theorem, there exist positive eigenvectors corresponding to
\rho(H)
.
Let
\mathbf{x}
be a positive eigenvector corresponding to
\rho(H)
with
\max\{x_{i}:i\in V(H)\}=1
. Without loss of generality, we assume that
x_{z}=1
.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
H
be an
n
-vertex
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
-free graph with maximum spectral radius. Then

\rho(H)\geq\frac{p-1}{p}n+O(1).

Proof.

Let
H^{\prime}
be an
n
-vertex
S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}}
-free graph with
e(H^{\prime})=\mathrm{ex}(n,S^{p+1}_{k_{1},\cdots,k_{q}})
.
By (
1
), we have

\displaystyle e(H^{\prime})

\displaystyle\geq e(T_{p}(n-q+1))+(q-1)(n-q+1)+\binom{q-1}{2}

\displaystyle\geq e(T_{p}(n))+\frac{q-1}{p}n-\frac{(q-1)(p+q-1)}{2p}-\frac{p}{8}.

According to (
3
), for sufficiently
n
, we have

\displaystyle\rho(H)

\displaystyle\geq\rho(H^{\prime})\geq\frac{\mathbf{1}^{\mathrm{T}}A(H^{\prime})\mathbf{1}}{\mathbf{1}^{\mathrm{T}}\mathbf{1}}=\frac{2e(H^{\prime})}{n}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{2}{n}\left(e(T_{p}(n))+\frac{q-1}{p}n-\frac{(q-1)(p+q-1)}{2p}-\frac{p}{8}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{p-1}{p}n+\frac{2(q-1)}{p}-\frac{1}{n}\left(\frac{(q-1)(p+q-1)}{p}+\frac{p}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{p-1}{p}n+O(1).

∎

Lemma 3.4

Suppose that

\varepsilon<\varepsilon_{2}\ll\theta

\displaystyle e(H\setminus L^{\prime})

\displaystyle e(T_{p}(n))-\varepsilon n^{2}-\lfloor\varepsilon_{3}n\rfloor(1-\frac{1}{p}-\varepsilon_{2})n

\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2p}(n-\lfloor\varepsilon_{3}n\rfloor-q+1)^{2}+(q-1)(n-\lfloor\varepsilon_{3}n\rfloor-q+1)+\binom{q-1}{2}+k_{q}^{2}

Lemma 3.6

For each
