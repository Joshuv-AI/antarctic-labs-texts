Introduction

\displaystyle=\eta^{2}-(\xi_{1}b_{1}+\xi_{2}b_{2})\eta+\xi_{1}\xi_{2}(b_{1}b_{2}-c_{1}c_{2})-\frac{1}{4}\Big(\xi_{1}^{2}c_{1}^{2}-2\xi_{1}\xi_{2}c_{1}c_{2}+\xi_{2}^{2}c_{2}^{2})

\displaystyle=\eta^{2}-(\xi_{1}b_{1}+\xi_{2}b_{2})\eta+\xi_{1}\xi_{2}(b_{1}b_{2}-c_{1}c_{2})-\frac{1}{4}(\xi_{1}c_{1}-\xi_{2}c_{2})^{2}.

By (
2.4
), we have
\xi_{1}c_{1}-\xi_{2}c_{2}=0
, and then

{\rm det}\big(B(\xi_{1},\xi_{2},\eta)\big)=g_{1}(\eta)>0\quad\forall\,\,0<\eta<\eta_{0}\leq\tilde{\eta}.

Therefore,
B(\xi_{1},\xi_{2},\eta)
is positive definite for any

0<\eta<\eta_{0}
.

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}u^{2}\frac{|\nabla w|^{2}}{w^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\mu\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p}-p\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w.

This together with (

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}\frac{\chi_{1}u+\chi_{2}v}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle=\chi_{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w+(\chi_{1}-\chi_{2})\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-2}}{w}u\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle\leq\chi_{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w+\frac{p(\chi_{1}-\chi_{2})^{2}}{4\chi_{2}}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}|\nabla(u+v)|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\mu\chi_{2}}{p}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p}-\chi_{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle=\frac{p(\chi_{1}-\chi_{2})^{2}}{4\chi_{2}}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}|\nabla(u+v)|^{2}+\frac{\mu\chi_{2}}{p}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p}.

Therefore, (

Next, by the similar arguments as in the above, we have that

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}\frac{\chi_{1}u+\chi_{2}v}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle=\chi_{1}\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w+(\chi_{2}-\chi_{1})\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-2}}{w}u\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle\leq\chi_{1}\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w+\frac{p(\chi_{2}-\chi_{1})^{2}}{{4\chi_{1}}}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}|\nabla(u+v)|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\mu{\chi_{1}}}{p}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p}-{\chi_{1}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle=\frac{p(\chi_{2}-\chi_{1})^{2}}{4\chi_{1}}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}|\nabla(u+v)|^{2}+\frac{\mu\chi_{1}}{p}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p}.

Therefore, (
4.5
) holds.

Now, assume that
\chi_{1}=\chi_{2}
. Let
\chi:=\chi_{1}
. For any

and

,

\displaystyle\chi\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle=\underbrace{(\chi-\beta)\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w}_{I_{1}}+\underbrace{\beta\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w}_{I_{2}}.

By Young’s inequality, we get that

\displaystyle I_{1}

\displaystyle=(\chi-\beta)\int_{\Omega}\frac{(u+v)^{-p-1}}{w}\nabla(u+v)\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle\leq\frac{p(\chi-\beta)^{2}}{4\beta}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p-2}|\nabla(u+v)|^{2}+\frac{\beta}{p}\int_{\Omega}(u+v)^{-p}\frac{|\nabla w|^{2}}{w^{2}}.

(4.9)

Lemma 5.5

First of all, recall that

\displaystyle E(t)=E(t;u_{0},v_{0})

\displaystyle=\xi_{1}\int_{\Omega}\Big(u(t,x;u_{0},v_{0})-u^{*}-u^{*}\ln\frac{u(t,x;u_{0},v_{0})}{u^{*}}\Big)

\displaystyle\quad+\xi_{2}\int_{\Omega}(v(t,x;u_{0},v_{0})-v^{*}-v^{*}\ln\frac{v(t,x;u_{0},v_{0})}{v^{*}}\Big).

We then have

\displaystyle E^{{}^{\prime}}(t)

\displaystyle=\xi_{1}\int_{\Omega}\Big(1-\frac{u^{*}}{u}\Big)u_{t}+\xi_{2}\int_{\Omega}\Big(1-\frac{v^{*}}{v}\Big)v_{t}

\displaystyle=\xi_{1}\int_{\Omega}\frac{u-u^{*}}{u}\Big(\Delta u-\chi_{1}\nabla\cdot\big(\frac{u}{w}\nabla w\big)\Big)+{\xi_{1}}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})(a_{1}-b_{1}u-c_{1}v)

\displaystyle\quad+\xi_{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{v-v^{*}}{v}\Big(\Delta v-\chi_{2}\nabla\cdot\big(\frac{v}{w}\nabla w\big)\Big)+\xi_{2}\int_{\Omega}(v-v^{*})(a_{2}-b_{2}v-c_{2}u).

(5.8)

Note that

a_{1}=b_{1}u^{*}+c_{1}v^{*}\quad{\rm and}\quad a_{2}=b_{2}v^{*}+c_{2}u^{*}.

This together with (
5.8
) implies that

\displaystyle E^{{}^{\prime}}(t)

\displaystyle=\xi_{1}\int_{\Omega}\frac{u-u^{*}}{u}\Big(\Delta u-\chi_{1}\nabla\cdot\big(\frac{u}{w}\nabla w\big)\Big)-\xi_{1}b_{1}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})^{2}-\xi_{1}c_{1}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})(v-v^{*})

\displaystyle\quad+\xi_{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{v-v^{*}}{v}\Big(\Delta v-\chi_{2}\nabla\cdot\big(\frac{v}{w}\nabla w\big)\Big)-\xi_{2}b_{2}\int_{\Omega}(v-v^{*})^{2}-\xi_{2}c_{2}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})(v-v^{*})

\displaystyle=-\xi_{1}u^{*}\int_{\Omega}u^{-2}|\nabla u|^{2}-\xi_{2}v^{*}\int_{\Omega}v^{-2}|\nabla v|^{2}+\xi_{1}\chi_{1}u^{*}\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{uw}\nabla u\cdot\nabla w+\xi_{2}\chi_{2}v^{*}\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{vw}\nabla v\cdot\nabla w

\displaystyle\quad-\xi_{1}b_{1}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})^{2}-\xi_{2}b_{2}\int_{\Omega}(v-v^{*})^{2}-(\xi_{1}c_{1}+\xi_{2}c_{2})\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})(v-v^{*}).

(5.9)

Next, note that

\xi_{1}\chi_{1}u^{*}\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{uw}\nabla u\cdot\nabla w\leq\xi_{1}u^{*}\int_{\Omega}u^{-2}|\nabla u|^{2}+\frac{\xi_{1}\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla w|^{2}}{w^{2}}

(5.10)

and

\xi_{2}\chi_{2}v^{*}\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{vw}\nabla v\cdot\nabla w\leq\xi_{2}v^{*}\int_{\Omega}v^{-2}|\nabla v|^{2}+\frac{\xi_{2}\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla w|^{2}}{w^{2}}.

(5.11)

By (
2.4
), we have

0<\xi_{i}\leq 1
for
i=1,2
. Then
by (
5.9
), (
5.10
), (
5.11
), and Lemma
5.4

we obtain

\displaystyle E^{{}^{\prime}}(t)

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\frac{\xi_{1}\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}+\frac{\xi_{2}\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla w|^{2}}{w^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad-\xi_{1}b_{1}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})^{2}-\xi_{2}b_{2}\int_{\Omega}(v-v^{*})^{2}-(\xi_{1}c_{1}+\xi_{2}c_{2})\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})(v-v^{*})

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}+\frac{\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)\frac{m^{*}(\mu,\nu,\lambda)}{w^{*}}\frac{1}{\inf_{t\geq\tau,x\in\Omega}w(t,x)}\Big[\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})^{2}+\int_{\Omega}(v-v^{*})^{2}\Big]

\displaystyle\quad-\xi_{1}b_{1}\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})^{2}-\xi_{2}b_{2}\int_{\Omega}(v-v^{*})^{2}-(\xi_{1}c_{1}+\xi_{2}c_{2})\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*})(v-v^{*})

\displaystyle=-\int_{\Omega}(u-u^{*},v-v^{*})A_{\tau}(u-u^{*},v-v^{*})^{\top}

(5.12)

for any
t\geq\tau>0
,
where
A_{\tau}
is the following
2\times 2
symmetric matrix,

A_{\tau}=\left(\begin{matrix}\xi_{1}b_{1}-\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}+\frac{\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)\frac{m^{*}(\mu,\nu,\lambda)}{w^{*}\inf_{t\geq\tau,x\in\Omega}w(t,x)}&\frac{1}{2}\big(\xi_{1}c_{1}+\xi_{2}c_{2}\big)\\
&\\
\frac{1}{2}\big(\xi_{1}c_{1}+\xi_{2}c_{2}\big)&\xi_{2}b_{2}-\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}+\frac{\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)\frac{m^{*}(\mu,\nu,\lambda)}{w^{*}\inf_{t\geq\tau,x\in\Omega}w(t,x)}\end{matrix}\right).

Note that

A_{\tau}=B(\xi_{1},\xi_{2},\eta)

with

\eta=\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}+\frac{\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)\frac{m^{*}(\mu,\nu,\lambda)}{w^{*}\inf_{t\geq\tau,x\in\Omega}w(t,x)}.

Now, by (
2.15
),
we have

\displaystyle a_{\min}-2\mu h(\chi_{1},\chi_{2})

\displaystyle>\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}+\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)^{p}\cdot m_{0}\cdot m_{1}

\displaystyle\geq\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}+\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)^{p}\cdot m_{0}\cdot\Big(\frac{m(\mu,\nu,\lambda)}{\delta_{0}\cdot|\Omega|\cdot w^{*}\cdot\eta_{0}}\Big)^{p}.

This implies that

\displaystyle\frac{\big(a_{\min}-2\mu h(\chi_{1},\chi_{2})\big)^{1/p}\delta_{0}|\Omega|\min\{\nu,\lambda\}}{m_{0}^{1/p}}>\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}+\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\frac{m^{*}}{w^{*}}\frac{1}{\eta_{0}}.

This together with Theorem
2.1
implies that

\Big(\frac{\chi_{1}^{2}u^{*}}{4}+\frac{\chi_{2}^{2}v^{*}}{4}\Big)\frac{m^{*}(\mu,\nu,\lambda)}{w^{*}\inf_{t\geq\tau,x\in\Omega}w(t,x)}<\eta_{0}\quad\forall\,\,\tau\gg 1.

Therefore,
A_{\tau}
is positive definite
for
\tau\gg 1
. Moreover, there is
\epsilon>0
such that (
5.7
) holds.
The lemma is thus proved.
∎

.

Proof of Theorem
2.2
.

(1) First, we prove (

).

, for

(2) Assume that
