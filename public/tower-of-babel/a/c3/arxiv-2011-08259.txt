On the Bezrukavnikov-Kaledin quantization of symplectic varieties in characteristic p p
Ekaterina Bogdanova, Vadim Vologodsky

Abstract
. We prove that after inverting the Planck constant h h the Bezrukavnikov-Kaledin quantization ( X , 𝒪 h ) (X,{\mathcal{O}}_{h}) of symplectic variety X X in characteristic p p is Morita equivalent to a certain central reduction of the algebra of differential operators on X X .

1.2.
Differential operators as a Frobenius-constant quantization.

A basic example of a Frobenius-constant quantization is the following. Let

be a smooth variety over
k

the cotangent bundle to

Applying the Rees construction to the filtered algebra

whose fiber over

and whose fiber over

Explicitly,

is the subalgebra of

generated by

,
{\mathcal{O}}_{Y}
, and

.
The

-curvature homomorphism

sending a function

to

and a vector field

induces an isomorphism between the algebra

and the center
of

can be viewed as a quasi-coherent sheaf on

. The canonical Frobenius-constant quantization of

1.3.
Restricted Poisson structures.

There is a local obstruction to the existence of a central quantization of a symplectic variety
(X,\omega)
. It is observed in
[
BK3
]
that if
f^{p}\in{\mathcal{O}}_{X^{\prime}}\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt\longrightarrow}\limits^{\sim}}{\mathcal{O}}_{X}^{p}
is in the image (
1.1
) then
the restricted power
H_{f}^{[p]}
of the Hamiltonian vector field
H_{f}
is again Hamiltonian:
H_{f}^{[p]}=H_{f^{[p]}}
for some
f^{[p]}\in{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
. For example, it follows that the torus
({\mathbb{G}}_{m}\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m},\omega=\frac{dx}{x}\wedge\frac{dy}{y})
does not admit central quantizations.

A Frobenius-constant structure on
(X,{\mathcal{O}}_{h})
provides a canonical Hamiltonian for
H_{f}^{[p]}
.
In fact, given a Frobenius-constant quantization

(X,{\mathcal{O}}_{h},s)
the formula

(1.3)

f^{[p]}=\frac{1}{h^{p-1}}(\tilde{f}^{p}-s(f))\mod h

defines a restricted structure on the Poisson algebra
{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
, that is the structure of a restricted Lie algebra on
{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
compatible with the commutative product on
{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
such that
H_{f}^{[p]}=H_{f^{[p]}}
.

It is shown in
[
BK3
]
that, for every symplectic variety
(X,\omega)
, giving a restricted structure on
{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
is equivalent to giving a class

[\eta]\in H^{0}_{Zar}(X,\coker({\mathcal{O}}_{X}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle d}}{{\longrightarrow}}\Omega^{1}_{X}))

such that

d([\eta])=\omega.

In one direction, if
\eta\in\Omega^{1}_{X}
,
d\eta=\omega
, then the formula

f^{[p]}=L^{p-1}_{H_{f}}\iota_{H_{f}}\eta-\iota_{H_{f}^{[p]}}\eta

defines a restricted structure on
{\mathcal{O}}_{X}
.
In particular, if
(X,\omega)
admits a restricted structure then
\omega
is exact locally for Zariski topology on
X
.

4.2.

\alpha
by
\hat{\mathbb{W}}
.

In this subsection we shall apply the theory of restricted Lie algebras to study the category of central extensions of the group scheme

Recall (see
e.g.
,
[
GD
, Chapter II, §7]
) that the Lie algebra of a group scheme

over a field of characteristic

is equipped with the
p
-th power operation giving

\Lie H
a restricted Lie algebra structure. We are not aware of a written account of such theory for group ind-schemes. Therefore we shall use the following trick to reduce our problem to the well
documented setup.

For an affine scheme
S
and an affine group scheme
H
over
k
denote by
\underline{\Mor}(S,H)
the
fpqc
sheaf of groups assigning to a scheme

over
k
the group

\Mor(S\times T,H)
.

Lemma 4.9
.

Let
G
be a finite connected group scheme over
k
,
H
a smooth commutative group scheme, and let
S
be an affine scheme. Then the groupoid of central extensions of
G
by
\underline{\Mor}(S,H)
in the category of fpqc sheaves of groups
is equivalent to the category of central extensions of
G_{S}=G\times S
by
H_{S}=H\times S
in the category of group schemes over
S
.

Proof.

Assume we are given a central extension

(4.11)

1\rightarrow H_{S}\rightarrow K\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi}}{{\longrightarrow}}G_{S}\rightarrow 1,

and let us construct a central extension
F
of
G
by
\underline{\Mor}(S,H)
. For any scheme
T
over
\Spec(k)
define
F(T)=\{g\in K(T\times S)|T\times S\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi(g)}}{{\longrightarrow}}G\times S\rightarrow G\text{ factors through the projection to }T\}
. It is easy to see that the resulting

is a sheaf and that
\underline{\Mor}(S,H)
injects into it, so it is left to prove that
F\rightarrow G
is a surjection. Indeed, since the morphism
\pi
is flat and
H_{S}
is smooth we get that
K\rightarrow G_{S}
is formally smooth (
[
Stacks
]
Lemma 29.33.3). Then since the map
\Spec(k)\times S\rightarrow G\times S
is a nilpotent thickening we get that

has a section. Thus

is surjective.

Conversely, if we have a central extension

(4.12)

1\rightarrow\underline{\Mor}(S,H)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle i}}{{\longrightarrow}}F\rightarrow G\rightarrow 1,

define for any
S
-scheme
T
the group

to be
F(T)\times^{\Mor(T\times S,H)}\Mor(T,H)
. Here the map
\Mor(T\times S,H)\rightarrow F(T)
is induced by

and

is defined to be the restriction to the graph of the structure morphism

. Let

be the homomoprhism whose composition with the first projection takes

to the neutral element and whose composition with the second projection is the identity map. This defines an injection of sheaves

, making

into an

-torsor over

representable by a scheme (
[

, Chapter III, Theorem 4.3]
). That is enough.
∎

Corollary 4.10
.

The groupoid of central extensions of

The above groupoids are discrete

Declare the restricted Lie algebra of

to be
\Lie(\Gra_{G}^{0})=h^{-1}k[h^{-1}]

Next we claim that for an extension

∎

Lemma 6.6

For every integer

\lsp(2n)=m^{2}/m^{3}

Proof.

Write
m_{n}^{l}/m_{n}^{l+1}
for

\lsp(2)=m_{1}/m_{1}^{2}

is irreducible for every

Moreover, the representations
m_{1}^{l}/m_{1}^{l+1}

are isomorphic if and only if

To prove the lemma in general, consider
the restriction of the representation of
\lsp(2n)
on

to the Lie subalgebra

\lsp(2)^{\oplus^{n}}\hookrightarrow\lsp(2n)

of the block diagonal matrices. The latter representation decomposes as follows

By the Jacobson density theorem the representation of
\lsp(2)^{\oplus^{n}}
on each summand is irreducible. Moreover, if

then these direct summands are pairwise non-isomorphic.
It follows that any subspace

invariant under the
\lsp(2)^{\oplus^{n}}
-action is the sum of
some
of the summands appearing in (

). Hence, it suffices to prove that
if a

-subrepresentatioin

contains
m_{1}^{i_{1}}/m_{1}^{i_{1}+1}\otimes\ldots\otimes m_{1}^{i_{n}}/m_{1}^{i_{n}+1}
, for some partition
(i_{1},\ldots,i_{n})
of
l
with
i_{1}>0
,
the projection of
W
to
m_{1}^{i_{1}-1}/m_{1}^{i_{1}}\otimes m_{1}^{i_{2}+1}/m_{1}^{i_{2}+2}\otimes\ldots\otimes m_{1}^{i_{n}}/m_{1}^{i_{n}+1}
is nonzero. This reduces the proof to the case

which is shown by
direct inspection.
∎
