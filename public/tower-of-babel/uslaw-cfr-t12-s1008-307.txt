12 C.F.R. § 1008.307 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1008.307
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1008.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1008 (S.A.F.E. Mortgage Licensing Act—State Compliance And Bureau Registration System (Regulation H)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CSBS, AARMR, or the Bureau, as applicable, may charge reasonable fees to cover the costs of maintaining and providing access to information from the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry. Fees shall not be charged to consumers for access to such system and registry. If the Bureau determines to charge fees, the fees to be charged shall be issued by notice with the opportunity for comment prior to any fees being charged.
