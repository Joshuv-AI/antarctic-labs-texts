45 C.F.R. § 1326.100 — Who presides.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1326.100
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1326.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1326 (Developmental Disabilities Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The presiding officer at a hearing must be the Secretary, his or her designee, or another person specifically designated for a particular hearing or hearings.
(b) The designation of a presiding officer must be in writing. A copy of the designation must be served on all parties and amici curiae.
