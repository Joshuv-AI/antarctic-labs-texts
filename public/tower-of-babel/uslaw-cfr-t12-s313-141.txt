12 C.F.R. § 313.141 — Notification to OPM.
Citation: us:cfr:12:313.141
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-313.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 313 (Procedures For Collection Of Corporate Debt, Criminal Restitution Debt, And Civil Money Penalty Debt). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When making a request for administrative offset under § 313.140, the FDIC shall provide OPM with a written certification that:
(a) The debtor owes the FDIC a debt, including the amount of the debt;
(b) The FDIC has complied with the applicable statutes, regulations, and procedures of OPM; and
(c) The FDIC has complied with the requirements of 31 CFR parts 900 through 904, including any required hearing or review.
