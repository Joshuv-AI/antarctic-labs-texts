7 U.S.C. § 7215 — Applicability of payment limitations
Citation: us:usc:7:7215
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7215
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 100 (Agricultural Market Transition); Subchapter II (Production Flexibility Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 1308 through 1308–3 of this title shall be applicable to contract payments made under this subchapter.
