20 C.F.R. § 616.1 — Purpose of arrangement.
Citation: us:cfr:20:616.1
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-616.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 616 (Interstate Arrangement For Combining Employment And Wages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This arrangement is approved by the Secretary under the provisions of section 3304(a)(9)(B) of the Federal Unemployment Tax Act to establish a system whereby an unemployed worker with covered employment or wages in more than one State may combine all such employment and wages in one State, in order to qualify for benefits or to receive more benefits.
