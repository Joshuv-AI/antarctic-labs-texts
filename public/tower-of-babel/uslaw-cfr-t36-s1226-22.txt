36 C.F.R. § 1226.22 — When must agencies transfer permanent records?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1226.22
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1226.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1226 (Implementing Disposition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All records scheduled as permanent must be transferred to the National Archives of the United States after the period specified on the SF 115 in accordance with procedures specified under § 1235.12 of this subchapter.
