33 U.S.C. § 3048 — Continuation on active duty; deferral of retirement
Citation: us:usc:33:3048
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3048
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter III (Separation And Retirement Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of subchapter IV of chapter 36 of title 10 relating to continuation on active duty and deferral of retirement shall apply to commissioned officers of the Administration.
