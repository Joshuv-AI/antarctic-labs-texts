42 U.S.C. § 3618 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:3618
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 45 (Fair Housing); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.
