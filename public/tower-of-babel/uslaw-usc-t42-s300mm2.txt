42 U.S.C. § 300mm–2 — Education and outreach
Citation: us:usc:42:300mm–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300mm–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXXI (World Trade Center Health Program); Part A (Establishment Of Program; Advisory Committee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The WTC Program Administrator shall institute a program that provides education and outreach on the existence and availability of services under the WTC Program. The outreach and education program—
(1)
shall include—
(A)
the establishment of a public Web site with information about the WTC Program;
(B)
meetings with potentially eligible populations;
(C)
development and dissemination of outreach materials informing people about the program; and
(D)
the establishment of phone information services; and
(2)
shall be conducted in a manner intended—
(A)
to reach all affected populations; and
(B)
to include materials for culturally and linguistically diverse populations.
