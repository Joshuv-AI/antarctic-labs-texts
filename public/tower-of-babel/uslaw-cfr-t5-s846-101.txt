5 C.F.R. § 846.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:846.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-846.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 846 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Elections Of Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part identifies the employees who may transfer to the Federal Employees Retirement System (FERS), gives the conditions under which they may transfer, and sets forth the method of computing the annuities of employees who transfer to FERS.
