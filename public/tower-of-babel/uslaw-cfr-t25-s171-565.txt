25 C.F.R. § 171.565 — How will I know if BIA plans to adjust my annual operation and maintenance assessment rate?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.565
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.565
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We provide public notice of our proposed rates annually in the Federal Register.
(b) You may contact the irrigation facility servicing your farm unit.
