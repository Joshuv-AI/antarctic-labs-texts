7 C.F.R. § 789.70 — Protection against claims.
Citation: us:cfr:7:789.70
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-789.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 789 (Agriculture Priorities And Allocations System (Apas)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person will not be held liable for damages or penalties for any act or failure to act resulting directly or indirectly from compliance with any provision of this part, or an official action, even if such provision or action is subsequently declared invalid by judicial or other competent authority.
