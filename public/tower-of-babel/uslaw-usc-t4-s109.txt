4 U.S.C. § 109 — Same; exception of Indians
Citation: us:usc:4:109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section109
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 4 (The States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in sections 105 and 106 of this title shall be deemed to authorize the levy or collection of any tax on or from any Indian not otherwise taxed.
