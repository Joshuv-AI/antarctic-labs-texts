7 C.F.R. § 27.83 — No fees for certain certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:7:27.83
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-27.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 27 (Cotton Classification Under Cotton Futures Legislation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No fee shall be collected for a new cotton class certificate issued in lieu of a prior certificate solely for the purpose of correcting clerical errors therein or for the purpose of substituting a new form applicable to outstanding certificates, or without an application therefor.
