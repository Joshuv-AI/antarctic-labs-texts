36 U.S.C. § 22312 — Distribution of assets on dissolution or final liquidation
Citation: us:usc:36:22312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22312
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 223 (American Symphony Orchestra League). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On dissolution or final liquidation of the corporation, any assets remaining after the discharge of all liabilities shall be distributed as provided by the board of directors, but in compliance with the constitution and bylaws of the corporation.
