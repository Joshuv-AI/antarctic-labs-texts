10 C.F.R. § 2.400 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.400
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes procedures applicable to licensing proceedings which involve the consideration in hearings of a number of applications, filed by one or more applicants pursuant to appendix N of part 50 or part 52 or § 53.1470 of this chapter, for licenses to construct and/or operate nuclear power reactors of identical design to be located at multiple sites.
