15 U.S.C. § 8714 — Limitation on expenditures for administrative expenses
Citation: us:usc:15:8714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8714
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 113 (Concrete Masonry Products Research, Education, And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated to carry out this chapter may not be used for the payment of the expenses or expenditures of the Board in administering the order.
