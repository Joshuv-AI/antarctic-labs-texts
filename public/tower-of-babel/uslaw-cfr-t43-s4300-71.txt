43 C.F.R. § 4300.71 — Under what circumstances can BLM modify, reduce or cancel my permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.71
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BLM may cancel the permit if:
(1) BLM issued it improperly through error as to a material fact;
(2) You fail to comply with any of the provisions of the permit or the regulations of this part; or
(3) Disposal, withdrawal, natural causes, such as drought or fire, or any other reason in § 4300.50 so requires.
(b) BLM will not cancel the permit for failure to comply until BLM has notified you in writing of the nature of your noncompliance, and you have been given at least 30 days to show why BLM should not cancel your permit.
(c) BLM may modify or reduce a permit in accordance with § 4300.50.
