12 U.S.C. § 2202e — Waiver of mediation rights by borrowers
Citation: us:usc:12:2202e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2202e
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter IV (Provisions Applicable To Two Or More Classes Of Institutions Of The System); Part C (Rights Of Borrowers; Loan Restructuring). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No System institution may make a loan secured by a mortgage or lien on agricultural property to a borrower on the condition that the borrower waive any right under the mediation program of any State.
