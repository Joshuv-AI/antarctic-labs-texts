22 U.S.C. § 4715 — Compliance with Congressional Budget Act
Citation: us:usc:22:4715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4715
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 57 (United States Scholarship Program For Developing Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any authority provided by this chapter to enter into contracts shall be effective only—
(1)
to the extent that the budget authority for the obligation to make outlays, which is created by the contract, has been provided in advance by an appropriation Act; or
(2)
to the extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
