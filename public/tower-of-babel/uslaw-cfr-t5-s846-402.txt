5 C.F.R. § 846.402 — Refunds of all CSRS contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:846.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-846.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 846 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Elections Of Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An individual who elects to transfer to FERS is entitled to a refund of all CSRS contributions in accordance with the provisions of part 831, subpart T of this chapter.
(b) An application for refund of FERS retirement contributions under § 843.202 of this chapter is deemed to also be an application for refund of CSRS retirement contributions under part 831, subpart T of this chapter.
