20 C.F.R. § 25.5 — How does OWCP adjudicate claims of non-citizen residents of possessions or territories?
Citation: us:cfr:20:25.5
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-25.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 25 (Compensation For Disability And Death Of Noncitizen Federal Employees Outside The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who is a bona fide permanent resident of any United States possession, territory, commonwealth, or trust territory will receive the full benefits of the FECA, as amended, except that the application of the minimum benefit provisions provided therein shall be governed by the restrictions set forth in 5 U.S.C. 8138.
