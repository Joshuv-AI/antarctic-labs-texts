24 C.F.R. § 242.7 — Maximum mortgage amounts.
Citation: us:cfr:24:242.7
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-242.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 242 (Mortgage Insurance For Hospitals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mortgage shall involve a principal obligation not in excess of 90 percent of HUD's estimate of the replacement cost of the hospital, including the equipment to be used in its operation when the proposed improvements are completed and the equipment is installed.
