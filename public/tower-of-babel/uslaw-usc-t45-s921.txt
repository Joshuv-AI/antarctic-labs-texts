45 U.S.C. § 921 — Office of Rail Public Counsel
Citation: us:usc:45:921
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section921
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 18 (Milwaukee Railroad Restructuring). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of Rail Public Counsel may appear and be heard in the case in the bankruptcy court involving the reorganization of the Milwaukee Railroad, for purposes of representing affected shippers, localities, and municipalities with respect to the proposed abandonment of any line of the Milwaukee Railroad.
