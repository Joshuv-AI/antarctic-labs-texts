11 U.S.C. § 926 — Avoiding powers
Citation: us:usc:11:926
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section926
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 9 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Municipality); Subchapter II (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
If the debtor refuses to pursue a cause of action under section 544, 545, 547, 548, 549(a), or 550 of this title, then on request of a creditor, the court may appoint a trustee to pursue such cause of action.
(b)
A transfer of property of the debtor to or for the benefit of any holder of a bond or note, on account of such bond or note, may not be avoided under section 547 of this title.
