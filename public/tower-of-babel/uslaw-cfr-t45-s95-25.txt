45 C.F.R. § 95.25 — When to request a waiver for good cause.
Citation: us:cfr:45:95.25
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-95.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 95 (General Administration—Grant Programs (Public Assistance, Medical Assistance And State Children'S Health Insurance Programs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State should request a waiver in writing as soon as the State recognizes that it will be unable to submit a claim within the appropriate time limit.
