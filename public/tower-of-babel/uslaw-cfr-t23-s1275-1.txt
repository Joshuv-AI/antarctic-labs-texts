23 C.F.R. § 1275.1 — Scope.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1275.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1275 (Repeat Intoxicated Driver Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the requirements necessary to implement Section 164 of Title 23, United States Code, which encourages States to enact and enforce repeat intoxicated driver laws.
