5 U.S.C. § 5708 — Effect on other statutes
Citation: us:usc:5:5708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5708
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 57 (Travel, Transportation, And Subsistence); Subchapter I (Travel And Subsistence Expenses; Mileage Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not modify or repeal—
(1)
any statute providing for the traveling expenses of the President;
(2)
any statute providing for mileage allowances for Members of Congress;
(3)
any statute fixing or permitting rates higher than the maximum rates established under this subchapter; or
(4)
any appropriation statute item for examination of estimates in the field.
