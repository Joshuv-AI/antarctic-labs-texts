49 C.F.R. § 222.1 — What is the purpose of this regulation?
Citation: us:cfr:49:222.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-222.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 222 (Use Of Locomotive Horns At Public Highway-Rail Grade Crossings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide for safety at public highway-rail grade crossings by requiring locomotive horn use at public highway-rail grade crossings except in quiet zones established and maintained in accordance with this part.
