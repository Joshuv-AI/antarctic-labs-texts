2 C.F.R. § 418.410 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:2:418.410
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-418.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 418 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each agency must take such actions as are necessary to ensure that the provisions herein are vigorously implemented and enforced in that agency.
