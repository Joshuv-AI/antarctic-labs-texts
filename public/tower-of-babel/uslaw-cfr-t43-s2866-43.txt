43 C.F.R. § 2866.43 — How does the BLM calculate rent for passive reflectors and local exchange networks?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2866.43
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2866.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2860 (Communications Uses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BLM calculates rent for passive reflectors and local exchange networks by using the same rent schedules for passive reflectors and local exchange networks as the Forest Service uses for the region in which the facilities are located. You may obtain the pertinent schedules from the Forest Service or from any BLM state or field office in the region in question. For passive reflectors and local exchange networks not covered by a Forest Service regional schedule, the BLM will use the provisions in § 2806.70 of this chapter to determine rent. See the Forest Service regulations at 36 CFR chapter II.
