41 U.S.C. § 1129 — Center of excellence in contracting for services
Citation: us:usc:41:1129
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section1129
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 11 (Establishment Of Office And Authority And Functions Of Administrator); Subchapter II (Authority And Functions Of The Administrator). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall maintain a center of excellence in contracting for services. The center shall assist the acquisition community by identifying, and serving as a clearinghouse for, best practices in contracting for services in the public and private sectors.
