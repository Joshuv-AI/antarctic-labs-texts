29 C.F.R. § 530.405 — General.

Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 530 (Employment Of Homeworkers In Certain Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as specifically provided in these regulations, the “Rules of Practice and Procedure for Administrative Hearings before the Office of Administrative Law Judges” established by the Secretary at 29 CFR part 18 shall apply to administrative proceedings described in this subpart.
