47 C.F.R. § 3.45 — Amount of charges.
Citation: us:cfr:47:3.45
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-3.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 3 (Authorization And Administration Of Accounting Authorities In Maritime And Maritime Mobile-Satellite Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Accounting Authorities may charge any reasonable fee for their settlement services. Settlements themselves, however, must adhere to the standards set forth in these rules and must be in accordance with the International Telecommunication Regulations (ITR) taking into account the applicable ITU-T Recommendations and other guidance issued by the Commission.
