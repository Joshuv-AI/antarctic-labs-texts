14 C.F.R. § 300.5 — Prohibited conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:14:300.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-300.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 300 (Rules Of Conduct In Dot Proceedings Under This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall: (a) Attempt to influence the judgment of a concerned DOT employee by any unlawful means such as deception or the payment of money or other consideration; or
(b) Disrupt or interfere with the fair and orderly disposition of a DOT proceeding.
