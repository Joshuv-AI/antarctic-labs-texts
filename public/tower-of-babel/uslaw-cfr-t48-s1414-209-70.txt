48 C.F.R. § 1414.209-70 — 1414.209-70 Examination of records by the Department of the Interior.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1414.209-70
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1414.209-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1415 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CO shall insert the clause at 1452.215-70, Examination of Records by the Department of the Interior, in all contracts requiring the clause at FAR 52.215-2 Audit and Records, Negotiation, as prescribed in FAR 15.209(b).
