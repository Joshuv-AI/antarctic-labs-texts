6 U.S.C. § 496 — Relation to the National Security Council
Citation: us:usc:6:496
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section496
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IX (National Homeland Security Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may convene joint meetings of the Homeland Security Council and the National Security Council with participation by members of either Council or as the President may otherwise direct.
