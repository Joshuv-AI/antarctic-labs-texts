47 U.S.C. § 1402 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:47:1402
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1402
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 13 (Public Safety Communications And Electromagnetic Spectrum Auctions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each range of frequencies described in this chapter shall be construed to be inclusive of the upper and lower frequencies in the range.
