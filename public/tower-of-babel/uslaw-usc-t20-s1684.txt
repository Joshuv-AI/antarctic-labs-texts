20 U.S.C. § 1684 — Blindness or visual impairment; prohibition against discrimination
Citation: us:usc:20:1684
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1684
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 38 (Discrimination Based On Sex Or Blindness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person in the United States shall, on the ground of blindness or severely impaired vision, be denied admission in any course of study by a recipient of Federal financial assistance for any education program or activity, but nothing herein shall be construed to require any such institution to provide any special services to such person because of his blindness or visual impairment.
