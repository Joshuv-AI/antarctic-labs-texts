29 U.S.C. § 1404 — Alternative method of withdrawal liability payments
Citation: us:usc:29:1404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1404
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance); Part 1 (Employer Withdrawals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A multiemployer plan may adopt rules providing for other terms and conditions for the satisfaction of an employer’s withdrawal liability if such rules are consistent with this chapter and with such regulations as may be prescribed by the corporation.
