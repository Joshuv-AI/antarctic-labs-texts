A nodal basis for the C 1 C^{1} - P 33 P_{33} finite elements on 5D simplex grids
Jun Hu, Shangyou Zhang

Abstract
. We construct a nodal basis for the 5-dimensional C 1 C^{1} finite element space of polynomial degree 33 33 on simplex grids, where the finite element functions are C 1 C^{1} on the 6 4D-simplex faces, C 2 C^{2} on the 15 face-tetrahedra, C 4 C^{4} on the 20 face-triangles, C 8 C^{8} on the 15 edges, and C 16 C^{16} at the 6 vertices, of a 5D simplex.

65N15, 65N30

1.
Introduction

In 2D, the Argyris finite element is one of the first finite elements even constructed,
cf.
[
4
]
.
It is a
C^{1}
-
P_{5}

Here

-
P_{5}

But the Argyris element is

at vertices and

at edges, with the degrees of freedom
of up-to order 2 derivatives at each vertex and 1 normal derivative at each edge.
It is straightforward to extend the Argyris finite element to
C^{1}
-
P_{k}
(
k>5
) finite elements,
as follows.
We introduce
(k-5)
function values at
(k-5)
internal points on each edge; we
introduce first-order normal derivatives at

(k-4)
internal points each edge;
and we introduce
function values at 2D
\dim P_{k-6}
internal points on the triangle.

Also in 2D, in 1970, Bramble and Zlámal
[
2
]
and Ženíšek
[

]

extended the above

-

finite element to

-

for all

, on triangular grids.
In fact, Ženíšek
[
6
]
defined
all
C^{m}
-
P_{k}
finite elements for
k\geq 4m+1
.

In 3D, the first

element was constructed
by Ženíšek in 1973
[
7
]

-
P_{9}

finite element.
By avoiding high-order derivatives in the degrees of freedom of Ženíšek
[
7
]
,
the element was extended to all
C^{1}
-
P_{k}
(

) finite elements
[

]

in 2009.
Also Ženíšek extended the

-

finite element to

-

]

Again high-order derivatives (above the continuity order) were used as degrees of freedom in

That is, the Ženíšek 3D

-

and 3D

A different

The family of

-

In 4D, a family of

Alfeld, Schumaker and Sirvent are the first to

In addition to

Mathematically it is a challenge to find explicit definitions of basis functions for

-

As we can see, in this work, the Bernstein index partition has more and more overlapping

\displaystyle\partial_{\mathbf{x}^{\alpha}}p(\mathbf{x}_{i}),\quad|\alpha|\leq 16,\ i=1,\dots,6,

\displaystyle(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{5},33-|\alpha|),\quad|\alpha|=\alpha_{1}+\cdots+\alpha_{5}\geq 0.

\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{k}C_{n+i,i}=C_{n+k+1,k}.
