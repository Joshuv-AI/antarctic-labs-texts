21 C.F.R. § 332.15 — Combination with non-antiflatulent active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:332.15
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-332.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 332 (Antiflatulent Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An antiflatulent may contain any generally recognized as safe and effective antacid ingredient(s) if it is indicated for use solely for the concurrent symptoms of gas associated with heartburn, sour stomach or acid indigestion.
