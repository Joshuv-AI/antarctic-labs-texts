50 C.F.R. § 11.24 — Final administrative action.
Citation: us:cfr:50:11.24
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-11.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 11 (Civil Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless a notice of request for an appeal is filed in accordance with § 11.25 of this subpart C, the administrative law judge's decision shall constitute the final administrative determination of the Secretary in the matter and shall become effective 30 calendar days from the date of the decision.
