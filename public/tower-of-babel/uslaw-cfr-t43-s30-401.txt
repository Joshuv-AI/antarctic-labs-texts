43 C.F.R. § 30.401 — Who may purchase at probate?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.401
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible purchaser at probate is any of the following:
(a) Any devisee or eligible heir who is receiving an interest in the same parcel of land by devise or descent in the probate proceeding;
(b) Any co-owner;
(c) The Indian Tribe with jurisdiction over the parcel containing the interest; or
(d) The Secretary on behalf of the Tribe.
