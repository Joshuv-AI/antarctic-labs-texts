8 C.F.R. § 1337.10 — Failure to appear for oath administration ceremony.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1337.10
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1337.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1337 (Oath Of Allegiance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant who fails to appear without good cause for more than one oath administration ceremony for which he or she was duly notified shall be presumed to have abandoned his or her intent to be naturalized. Such presumption shall be regarded as the receipt of derogatory information, and the procedures contained in § 335.5 of 8 CFR chapter I shall be followed.
