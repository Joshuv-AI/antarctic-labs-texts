25 C.F.R. § 26.22 — May a tribe integrate Job Placement and Training funds into its Public Law 102-477 Plan?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.22
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, Indian tribes may integrate Job Placement and Training Program funds into their Public Law 102-477 Plan.
