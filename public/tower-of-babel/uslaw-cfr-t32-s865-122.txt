32 C.F.R. § 865.122 — Summary of statistics for Discharge Review Board.
Citation: us:cfr:32:865.122
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-865.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 865 (Personnel Review Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Air Force Discharge Review Board shall prepare and provide to the Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense (Military Personnel and Force Management) DASD(MP&FM), Office of the ASD(MRA&L), a semiannual report of discharge review actions in accordance with § 865.125.
