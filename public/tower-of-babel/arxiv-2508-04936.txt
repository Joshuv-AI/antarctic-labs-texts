Hadronic decay D + → π + ​ η ​ η D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\eta\eta and the a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) and f 0 ​ ( 1370 ) f_{0}(1370) contributions
Wen-Tao Lyu, Le-Le Wei, De-Min Li, En Wang

Abstract
Motivated by the resent BESIII Collaboration measurements of the process D + → π + ​ η ​ η D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\eta\eta , we investigate this process by considering the contribution from the resonance a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) , which is generated by S S -wave pseudoscalar meson-pseudoscalar meson interaction, and the contribution from the intermediate scalar meson f 0 ​ ( 1370 ) f_{0}(1370) . Our results could give a good description of the π + ​ η \pi^{+}\eta and η ​ η \eta\eta invariant mass distributions, which implies that the contribution from the intermediate f 0 ​ ( 1370 ) f_{0}(1370) , neglected in BESIII analysis, could play a role in this process. Furthermore, the predicted Dalitz plots are also consistent with the BESIII measurements. The more precise measurements about this process in future could shed light on the mechanism of this process and the properties of f 0 ​ ( 1370 ) f_{0}(1370) and a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) .

Recently, the BESIII Collaboration has measured the process

using 20.3 fb

of
e^{+}e^{-}
collision data at the center-of-mass energy 3.773 GeV, and found a clear cusp structure in the
\pi^{+}\eta
invariant mass distribution, which should be associated with the scalar
a_{0}(980)

a_{0}(980)

a_{0}(980)

D^{+}\to\bar{K}^{*}_{0}(1430)K^{+}\to a_{0}(980)^{+}\eta
with a significance of
5.8\sigma

MeV, about
80

a_{0}(980)

According to BESIII measurements of the process

\eta\eta
invariant mass distribution is significantly different from the phase space distribution in the region
1300\sim 1500
MeV

Therefore, in this work, we have investigated this process by considering the contribution from the resonance

S

f_{0}(1370)

One can find the our results including the contribution from the intermediate
f_{0}(1370)

\pi^{+}\eta
and
\eta\eta
invariant mass distributions, which supports the molecular nature of the
a_{0}(980)

f_{0}(1370)
plays a sizeable role. Since the properties of the scalar
