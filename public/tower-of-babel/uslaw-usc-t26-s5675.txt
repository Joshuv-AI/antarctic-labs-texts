26 U.S.C. § 5675 — Penalty for intentional removal or defacement of brewer’s marks and brands
Citation: us:usc:26:5675
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5675
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part III (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Applicable To Beer And Brewing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every person other than the owner, or his agent authorized so to do, who intentionally removes or defaces any mark, brand, or label required by section 5412 and regulations issued pursuant thereto shall be liable to a penalty of $50 for each barrel or other container from which such mark, brand, or label is so removed or defaced.
