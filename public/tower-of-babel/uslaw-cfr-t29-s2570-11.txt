29 C.F.R. § 2570.11 — Scope of review.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2570.11
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2570.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2570 (Procedural Regulations Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The review of the Secretary shall not be a de novo proceeding but rather a review of the record established before the administrative law judge. There shall be no opportunity for oral argument.
