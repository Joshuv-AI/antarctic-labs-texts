12 C.F.R. § 151.80 — How do I provide a registered broker-dealer confirmation?
Citation: us:cfr:12:151.80
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-151.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 151 (Recordkeeping And Confirmation Requirements For Securities Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you elect to satisfy § 151.70 by providing the customer with a registered broker-dealer confirmation, you must provide the confirmation by having the registered broker-dealer send the confirmation directly to the customer or by sending a copy of the registered broker-dealer's confirmation to the customer within one business day after you receive it.
(b) Unless you have determined remuneration in a written agreement with the customer, if you have received or will receive remuneration from any source, including the customer, in connection with the transaction, you must provide a statement of the source and amount of the remuneration in addition to the registered broker-dealer confirmation described in paragraph (a) of this section.
