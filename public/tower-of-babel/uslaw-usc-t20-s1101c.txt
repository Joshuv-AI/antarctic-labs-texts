20 U.S.C. § 1101c — Duration of grant
Citation: us:usc:20:1101c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1101c
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter V (Developing Institutions); Part A (Hispanic-Serving Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Award period
The Secretary may award a grant to a Hispanic-serving institution under this subchapter for 5 years.
(b) Planning grants
Notwithstanding subsection (a), the Secretary may award a grant to a Hispanic-serving institution under this subchapter for a period of 1 year for the purpose of preparation of plans and applications for a grant under this subchapter.
