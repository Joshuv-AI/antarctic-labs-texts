5 C.F.R. § 302.108 — Determinations of fitness for employment in an Excepted Service position.
Citation: us:cfr:5:302.108
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-302.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 302 (Employment In The Excepted Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An agency must make fitness determinations for excepted service positions in accordance with the applicable requirements of part 731 of this chapter.
(b) An agency must record its reasons for making fitness determinations under part 731 of this chapter and must furnish a copy of those reasons to an applicant upon their request.
