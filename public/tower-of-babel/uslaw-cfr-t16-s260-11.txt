16 C.F.R. § 260.11 — Ozone-safe and ozone-friendly claims.
Citation: us:cfr:16:260.11
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-260.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 260 (Guides For The Use Of Environmental Marketing Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is deceptive to misrepresent, directly or by implication, that a product, package, or service is safe for, or friendly to, the ozone layer or the atmosphere.
