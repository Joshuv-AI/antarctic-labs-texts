45 C.F.R. § 2516.320 — Is a participant eligible to receive an AmeriCorps educational award?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.320
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. However, service-learning coordinators who are approved AmeriCorps positions are eligible for AmeriCorps educational awards.
