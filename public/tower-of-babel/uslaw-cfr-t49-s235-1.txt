49 C.F.R. § 235.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:235.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-235.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 235 (Instructions Governing Applications For Approval Of A Discontinuance Or Material Modification Of A Signal System Or Relief From The Requirements Of Part 236). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes application for approval to discontinue or materially modify block signal systems, interlockings, traffic control systems, automatic train stop, train control, or cab signal systems, or other similar appliances, devices, methods, or systems, and provides for relief from part 236 of this title.
