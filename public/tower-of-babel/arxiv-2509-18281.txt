Weights for K-motives on stacks
Thiago Landim

Abstract
We construct the Chow weight structure on a full subcategory of the category of K \mathrm{K} -motives over a tame quotient stack in characteristic zero as defined by Hoyois. We also prove that in a quite general case, this full subcategory is exactly the category of geometric K \mathrm{K} -motives. We apply this to give a partial Springer decomposition in the context of K \mathrm{K} -motives.

In this section, we will define a weight structure on the category of

\mathrm{K}
-motives, similarly to

Definition 2.1
.

For any tame quotient stack
\mathscr{X}
, the category of pure
\mathrm{K}
-motives
\mathrm{DK}_{\mathrm{pure}}(\mathscr{X})\subset\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{X})
is the smallest additive subcategory generated under retracts by the collection

\{f_{!}1_{\mathscr{Y}}\mid f\colon\mathscr{Y}\to\mathscr{X}\textrm{ is projective and }\mathscr{Y}\textrm{ is regular Noetherian}\}.

The category of
resolvable

\mathrm{K}
-motives
\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{res}}(\mathscr{X})\subset\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{X})
is the full thick subcategory generated by
\mathrm{DK}_{\mathrm{pure}}(\mathscr{X})
, in other words, the full subcategory generated by
\mathrm{DK}_{\mathrm{pure}}(\mathscr{X})
under finite limits, finite colimits and retracts.

Theorem 2.2
.

For any tame quotient stack
\mathscr{X}
, the category
\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{res}}(\mathscr{X})
admits a bounded weight structure whose heart is
\mathrm{DK}_{\mathrm{pure}}(\mathscr{X})
.

Proof.

By base change and
[
Hoy20
, Remark 5.7]
, the mapping spectrum

\begin{split}\Map(f_{!}1_{\mathscr{Y}},f^{\prime}_{!}1_{\mathscr{Y}^{\prime}})&=\Map(f^{\prime*}f_{!}1_{\mathscr{Y}},1_{\mathscr{Y}^{\prime}})\\
&=\Map(\pi_{2!}\pi_{1}^{*}1_{\mathscr{Y}},1_{\mathscr{Y}^{\prime}})\\
&=\Map(1_{\mathscr{Y}\times_{\mathscr{X}}\mathscr{Y}^{\prime}},\pi_{2}^{!}1_{\mathscr{Y}^{\prime}})\\
&=\mathrm{K}(\mathrm{Coh}(\mathscr{Y}\times_{\mathscr{X}}\mathscr{Y}^{\prime})),\end{split}

is connective by
[
Kha22
, Proposition 3.1]
. Therefore
[
Sos19
, Remark 2.2.6]
implies the existence of the given weight structure.
∎

Remark 2.3
.

The general case for linearly scalloped stacks follows from an induction argument together with

, Theorem 3.9]

4
An Application

Let
\mathbb{k}
be an algebraically closed field of characteristic, let
\mathrm{G}
be a reductive group over
\mathbb{k}
and let
\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}
the nilpotent cone of
\mathrm{G}
. In
[
Ebe24
]
, Eberhardt defined the category of reduced Springer
\mathrm{K}
-motives, in the same way as
[
ES23
]
and proved in
[
Ebe24
, Theorem 5.2]
that

\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{r}}^{\mathrm{Spr}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))=\mathrm{Perf}(\mathscr{H}_{\mathrm{aff},\mathbf{q}}),

where
\mathscr{H}_{\mathrm{aff},\mathbf{q}}
is the affine Hecke algebra associated to
\mathrm{G}
. He then used this equivalence to relate the left-hand side with the unramified local Langlands correspondence. We prove an orthogonality result which implies, in particular, the category of Springer
\mathrm{K}
-motives admits an orthogonal complement. It may be seen as a strengthening of a
\mathrm{K}
-theoretic version of
[
Ach11
, Theorem 5.1]
.

Theorem 4.1
.

There exists an orthogonal decomposition

\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))=\bigoplus_{\mathrm{L}}\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))_{\mathrm{L}}

indexed by conjugacy classes of Levi subgroups. The same recomposition is also true for
reduced

\mathrm{K}
-motives.

Proof.

Before going into the proof, we will explain the notation, which comes from
[
Gun17
, §3]
. For any parabolic subgroup
\mathrm{P}
with Levi subgroup
\mathrm{L}
, we consider the span

\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})\xleftarrow{\pi_{\mathrm{P}}}\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{P}}/(\mathrm{P}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})\xrightarrow{\mu_{\mathrm{P}}}\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}),

where
\mu_{\mathrm{P}}
is a smooth map of relative dimension 0 and
\pi_{\mathrm{P}}
is a projective map. We define the parabolic induction by

\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\coloneqq\mu_{\mathrm{P}!}\pi_{\mathrm{P}}^{*}\cong\mu_{\mathrm{P}*}\pi_{\mathrm{P}}^{!}\colon\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))\to\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))

and the parabolic restrictions

\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\coloneqq\pi_{\mathrm{P}*}\mu_{\mathrm{P}}^{!}\colon\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))\to\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))

and

{}^{\prime}\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\coloneqq\pi_{\mathrm{P}!}\mu_{\mathrm{P}}^{*}\colon\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))\to\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})).

as its right and left adjoints, respectively.

By Proposition
2.4
, the functor
\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}
is weight exact and therefore by
[
Bon14
, Theorem 1.2.3(9)]
the functors
\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}
and
{}^{\prime}\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}
are right and left adjoints, respectively. By
[
Des25
, §3]
, the motivic coefficient system
\operatorname{DK}
satisfies hyperbolic localization over algebraic stacks. As already remark in
[
BYD21
, Proposition 4.3]
, this implies that one has an isomorphism
\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\cong{}^{\prime}\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\overline{\mathrm{P}}}^{\mathrm{G}}
, where
\overline{\mathrm{P}}
is the parabolic opposite to
\mathrm{P}
. In particular, the functors
\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}
and
{}^{\prime}\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}
are weight exact. We may, therefore, restrict the adjoint functors to the heart of the corresponding categories:

\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\colon\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\heartsuit_{w}}\rightleftharpoons\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\heartsuit_{w}}\colon\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}.

Let
\mathrm{L}
be a Levi subgroup of
\mathrm{G}
and denote by
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{X})^{\mathrm{ren}}\coloneqq\operatorname{Ind}(\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{X}))
the Ind-completion of the category of geometric motives
\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{X})
for any tame quotient stack
\mathscr{X}
.

(a)

We denote by
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{\geq\mathrm{L}}
(resp.
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{>\mathrm{L}}
) the full subcategory of graded sheaves killed by parabolic restriction to any
\mathrm{L}^{\prime}
such that
\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\ngeq\mathrm{L}
(resp.
\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\ngtr\mathrm{L}
).

(b)

We denote by
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{\mathrm{L}}
the quotient
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{\geq\mathrm{L}}/\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{>\mathrm{L}}
.

(c)

We denote by
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{\mathrm{cusp}}
the full subcategory of
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}
spanned by cuspidal sheaves.

As in
[
Gun17
, Convention 3.10]
, one may identify the quotient
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{\mathrm{L}}
with the subcategory of
\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}
generated under colimits by
\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\operatorname{DK}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))^{\mathrm{ren}}_{\mathrm{cusp}}
. Therefore, to prove the orthogonality result, it suffices to show that

\Map(\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}M,\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{G}}M^{\prime})=0,

for any pair of non-conjugate Levi subgroups
\mathrm{L}
,
\mathrm{L}^{\prime}
, and for any
M\in\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))_{\mathrm{cusp}}^{\heartsuit_{w}}
and
M^{\prime}\in\operatorname{DK}_{\mathrm{gm}}(\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}/(\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}))_{\mathrm{cusp}}^{\heartsuit_{w}}
. By adjunction, it suffices to understand
\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{G}}
and we will do so following
[
DS23
, §2.5]
. By base change, the functor
\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{G}}
is given by
!
-pulling and
*
-pushing along the following correspondence

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}/(\mathrm{P}^{\prime}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{Q}}/(\mathrm{Q}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}/(\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{G}}/(\mathrm{G}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m}),}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mu_{\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\pi_{\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\pi_{\mathrm{P}}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mu_{\mathrm{P}}}

where
\mathscr{X}
admits a stratification
\mathscr{X}=\bigsqcup_{w}\mathscr{X}_{w}
indexed by
\mathrm{W}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}\backslash\mathrm{W}/\mathrm{W}_{\mathrm{L}}
, by
[
DS23
, §2.5]
.

Similarly, for any representative

of the double coset
\mathrm{W}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}\backslash\mathrm{W}/\mathrm{W}_{\mathrm{L}}
, one may take a lift of

in
\mathrm{G}
as in
[

, Lemma 2.3.1]
and consider the correspondence

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{Y}_{w}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{P}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}/((\mathrm{P}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime})\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}/((\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{P}^{\prime})\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}/({}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}/((\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime})\times\mathbb{G}_{m})}

By
[
DS23
, Theorem 2.5.3]
, there exists a map

over
\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}/(\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})\times\mathscr{N}_{\mathrm{L}}/(\mathrm{L}\times\mathbb{G}_{m})
such that

Indeed, to prove this, it suffices to explain why
[

, Lemma 2.2.3]
is still true for
\operatorname{DK}
.

Since
\operatorname{DK}
is homotopy invariant, by
[

, Theorem 1.1(8)]
, it follows that for any vector bundle

, the map

is fully faithful, and therefore

. Furthermore, let

be a map induced by a morphism

exhibiting
\mathrm{H}
as the reductive quotient of
\mathrm{G}
. Since we are in characteristic zero, there exists a section

and the map
\overline{\sigma}\colon\mathrm{X}/\mathrm{H}\to\mathrm{X}/\mathrm{G}
is a section of
\overline{f}
and is
\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{u}}(\mathrm{G})
-torsor. Since
\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{u}}(\mathrm{G})
is also a vector bundle, using once again homotopy invariance, it follows that pullback with respect to
\overline{\sigma}
is fully faithful and therefore the same is true for
\overline{f}
.

In particular, arguing as in
[

, Proof of Lemma 2.5.1]
, we have for that
\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{G}}M

for any

.

We can now conclude our proof. Let
\mathrm{L}
and
\mathrm{L}^{\prime}
be non-conjugate Levi subgroups of
\mathrm{G}
, and let

let

be cuspidal
\mathrm{K}
-motives of weight zero. In this case, for any

,

\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}}\circ\operatorname{ad}(w^{-1})^{*}M^{\prime}=0,

since

is cuspidal and
\mathrm{L}\cap{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}
is a proper Levi subgroup of
{}^{w}\mathrm{L}^{\prime}
. Therefore

\begin{split}\Map(\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}M,\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{G}}M^{\prime})&=\Map(M,\mathscr{R}_{\mathrm{L}\subset\mathrm{P}}^{\mathrm{G}}\mathscr{I}_{\mathrm{L}^{\prime}\subset\mathrm{P}^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{G}}M^{\prime})\\
&=\Map(M,0)\\
&=0,\end{split}

proving the desired orthogonality.
∎
