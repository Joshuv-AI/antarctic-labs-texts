30 C.F.R. § 1210.200 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1210.200
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1210.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1210 (Forms And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart explains your reporting requirements if you produce coal or other solid minerals from Federal or Indian leases. Included are your requirements for reporting production, sales, and royalties.
