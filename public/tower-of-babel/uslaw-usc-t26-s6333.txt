26 U.S.C. § 6333 — Production of books
Citation: us:usc:26:6333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6333
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter D (Seizure Of Property For Collection Of Taxes); Part II (Levy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a levy has been made or is about to be made on any property, or right to property, any person having custody or control of any books or records, containing evidence or statements relating to the property or right to property subject to levy, shall, upon demand of the Secretary, exhibit such books or records to the Secretary.
