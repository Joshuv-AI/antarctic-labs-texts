32 C.F.R. § 750.35 — Attorney's fees.
Citation: us:cfr:32:750.35
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-750.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 750 (General Claims Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Attorney's fees are limited to 20 percent of any compromise or settlement of an administrative claim, and are limited to 25 percent of any judgment rendered in favor of a plaintiff, or of any settlement accomplished after suit is filed. These amounts are to be paid out of the amount awarded and not in addition to the award. 28 U.S.C. 2678.
