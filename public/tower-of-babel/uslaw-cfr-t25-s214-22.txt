25 C.F.R. § 214.22 — Serving of notices.
Citation: us:cfr:25:214.22
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-214.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 214 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands, Oklahoma, For Mining, Except Oil And Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wherever notice is provided for in this part it shall be sufficient if notice has been mailed to the last known place of address of the party, and time shall begin to run with the day next ensuing after the mailing or from the date of delivery of personal notice; but where the party is outside the State of Oklahoma the officer in charge may, in his discretion, increase the time allowed.
