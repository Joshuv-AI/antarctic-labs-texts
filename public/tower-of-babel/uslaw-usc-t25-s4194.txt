25 U.S.C. § 4194 — Training and information
Citation: us:usc:25:4194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4194
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter VI (Federal Guarantees For Financing For Tribal Housing Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in cooperation with eligible public entities, shall carry out training and information activities with respect to the guarantee program under this subchapter.
