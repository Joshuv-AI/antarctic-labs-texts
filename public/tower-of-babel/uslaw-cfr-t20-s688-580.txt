20 C.F.R. § 688.580 — What effect do payments to YouthBuild participants have on eligibility for other Federal needs-based benefits?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.580
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under § 683.275(d) of this chapter, the Department does not consider allowances, earnings, and payments to individuals participating in programs under title I of WIOA as income for purposes of determining eligibility for and the amount of income transfer and in-kind aid furnished under any Federal or Federally-assisted program based on need other than as provided under the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 301).
