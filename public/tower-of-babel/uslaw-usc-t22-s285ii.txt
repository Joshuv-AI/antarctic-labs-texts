22 U.S.C. § 285ii — Asian Development Fund thirteenth replenishment
Citation: us:usc:22:285ii
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section285ii
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XIV (Asian Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The United States Governor of the Fund may contribute on behalf of the United States $174,440,000 to the thirteenth replenishment of the resources of the Fund, subject to obtaining the necessary appropriations.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
In order to pay for the United States contribution provided for in subsection (a), there are authorized to be appropriated, without fiscal year limitation, $174,440,000 for payment by the Secretary of the Treasury.
