44 U.S.C. § 2701 — Advisory Committee on the Records of Congress
Citation: us:usc:44:2701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2701
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 27 (Advisory Committee On The Records Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is established the Advisory Committee on the Records of Congress (hereafter in this chapter referred to as the Committee).
(b)
The Committee shall be subject to the provisions of chapter 10 of title 5, except that the Committee shall be of permanent duration, notwithstanding any provision of section 1013 of title 5.
