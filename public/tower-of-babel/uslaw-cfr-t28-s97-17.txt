28 C.F.R. § 97.17 — Mandatory restraints to be used while transporting violent prisoners.
Citation: us:cfr:28:97.17
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-97.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 97 (Standards For Private Entities Providing Prisoner Or Detainee Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Companies covered under this part must, at a minimum, require that violent prisoners be transported wearing handcuffs, leg irons, and waist chains unless the use of all three restraints would create a serious health risk to the prisoner, or extenuating circumstances (such as pregnancy or physical disability) make the use of all three restraints impracticable.
