43 C.F.R. § 10010.47 — Pre-decision referrals to CEQ.
Citation: us:cfr:43:10010.47
Creator: Utah Reclamation Mitigation And Conservation Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-10010.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 10010 (Policies And Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon receipt of advice that another Federal agency intends to refer a Commission matter to CEQ, the Commission will immediately meet with that Federal agency to attempt to resolve the issues raised.
(b) Upon any referral of a Commission matter to CEQ by another Federal agency, the Executive Director will be responsible for coordinating the Commission's position.
