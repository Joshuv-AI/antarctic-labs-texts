40 U.S.C. § 6910 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:40:6910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6910
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 69 (Union Station Redevelopment); Subchapter I (Union Station Complex). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts necessary to meet lease and other obligations, including maintenance requirements, incurred by the Secretary of the Interior and assigned to the Secretary of Transportation under this subchapter may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation.
