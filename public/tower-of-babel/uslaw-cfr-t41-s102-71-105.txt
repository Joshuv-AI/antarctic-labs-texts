41 C.F.R. § 102-71.105 — -71.105 Furnishing and installing signs concerning tobacco product restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.105
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agency building managers are responsible for furnishing and installing suitable, uniform signs in the building, and in and around building entrance doorways and air intake ducts.
