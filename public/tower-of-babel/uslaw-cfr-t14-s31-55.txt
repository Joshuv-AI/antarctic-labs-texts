14 C.F.R. § 31.55 — Deflation means.
Citation: us:cfr:14:31.55
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-31.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 31 (Airworthiness Standards: Manned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There must be a means to allow emergency deflation of the envelope so as to allow a safe emergency landing. If a system other than a manual system is used, the reliability of the system used must be substantiated.
