10 U.S.C. § 1001 — Reference to chapter 1219
Citation: us:usc:10:1001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1001
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 51 (Reserve Components: Standards And Procedures For Retention And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of law relating to standards and procedures for retention and promotion of members of reserve components are set forth in chapter 1219 of this title (beginning with section 12641).
