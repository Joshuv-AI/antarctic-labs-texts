48 C.F.R. § 1422.604-2 — 1422.604-2 Regulatory exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1422.604-2
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1422.604-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1422 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The AS/PMB is authorized to request the Secretary of Labor to exempt contracts from the Walsh-Healey Public Contracts Act under FAR 22.604-2(b). A written finding justifying the exemption shall be prepared by the CO and submitted by the HCA to the Director, PAM for further action.
