A. Overview of Information Collection

    Title of Information Collection: FHA Catalyst: Multifamily 
Applications, and Portal + Loan Underwriting System (PLUS).
    OMB Approval Number: 2502-0620.
    Type of Request: Revision of a currently approved collection.
    Form Number: N/A.
    Description of the need for the information and proposed use: FHA 
Catalyst: Multifamily Applications (``Catalyst'') is an existing, web-
based portal that allows FHA multifamily lenders to upload and submit 
applications for FHA multifamily mortgage insurance to HUD. FHA's 
Portal + Loan Underwriting System (PLUS) is a new web-based application 
that will allow FHA multifamily and healthcare lenders to request FHA 
Project Numbers for their applications and view the real-time status of 
their applications. Additional lender portal functionalities will be 
built into PLUS in subsequent phases, and PLUS will later replace the 
multifamily lender portal functionalities that are currently in 
Catalyst, likely in 2027.
    Respondents: Business and other for-profit (MAP Lenders), State and 
Local Government (Housing Finance Agencies), and Government-Sponsored 
Enterprises (Fannie Mae & Freddie Mac)--all FHA-approved mortgagees.
