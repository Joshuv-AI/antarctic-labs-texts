34 C.F.R. § 76.106 — State documents are public information.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.106
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State shall make the following documents available for public inspection:
(a) All State plans and related official materials.
(b) All approved subgrant applications.
(c) All documents that the Secretary transmits to the State regarding a program.
