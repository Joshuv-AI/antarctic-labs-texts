33 U.S.C. § 2234 — Nonapplicability to Saint Lawrence Seaway
Citation: us:usc:33:2234
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2234
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter II (Harbor Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 2231, 2232, and 2233 of this title do not apply to any harbor or inland harbor project for that portion of the Saint Lawrence Seaway administered by the Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation.
