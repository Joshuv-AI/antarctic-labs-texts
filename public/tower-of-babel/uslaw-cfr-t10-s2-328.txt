10 C.F.R. § 2.328 — Hearings to be public.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.328
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.328
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as may be requested under section 181 of the Act, all hearings will be public unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.
