38 U.S.C. § 8131 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:38:8131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8131
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 81 (Acquisition And Operation Of Hospital And Domiciliary Facilities; Procurement And Supply; Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property); Subchapter III (State Home Facilities For Furnishing Domiciliary, Nursing Home, And Hospital Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter—
(1)
The veteran population of each State shall be determined on the basis of the latest figures certified by the Department of Commerce.
(2)
The term “State” includes each Indian tribe (as defined in section 4 of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 5304)) but does not include any possession of the United States.
(3)
The term “construction” means the construction of new domiciliary or nursing home buildings, the expansion, remodeling, or alteration of existing buildings for the provision of domiciliary, nursing home, adult day health, or hospital care in State homes, and the provision of initial equipment for any such buildings.
(4)
The term “cost of construction” means the amount found by the Secretary to be necessary for a construction project, including architect fees, but excluding land acquisition costs.
