25 U.S.C. § 2211 — Governing body of tribe; construction of chapter as not vesting with authority not authorized by tribal constitution or by-laws
Citation: us:usc:25:2211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2211
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 24 (Indian Land Consolidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as vesting the governing body of an Indian tribe with any authority which is not authorized by the constitution and by-laws or other organizational document of such tribe.
