47 C.F.R. § 24.12 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:47:24.12
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-24.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 24 (Personal Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any entity, other than those precluded by section 310 of the Communications Act of 1934, as amended, 47 U.S.C. 310, is eligible to hold a license under this part.
