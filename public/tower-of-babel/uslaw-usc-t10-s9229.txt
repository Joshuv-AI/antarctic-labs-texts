10 U.S.C. § 9229 — Command: commissioned officers in certain designated categories
Citation: us:usc:10:9229
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9229
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 925 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer designated as a medical, dental, veterinary, medical service, or biomedical sciences officer or as a nurse is not entitled to exercise command because of rank, except within the categories prescribed in subsection (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f), or (i) of section 9067 of this title, or over persons placed under his charge.
