10 U.S.C. § 8704 — Disposition of receipts
Citation: us:usc:10:8704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8704
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 865 (Salvage Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts received under this chapter shall be credited to appropriations for maintaining naval salvage facilities. However, any amount received under this chapter in any fiscal year in excess of naval salvage costs incurred by the Navy during that fiscal year shall be deposited into the general fund of the Treasury.
