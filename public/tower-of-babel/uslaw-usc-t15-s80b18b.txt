15 U.S.C. § 80b–18b — Custody of client accounts
Citation: us:usc:15:80b–18b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80b–18b
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter II (Investment Advisers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An investment adviser registered under this subchapter shall take such steps to safeguard client assets over which such adviser has custody, including, without limitation, verification of such assets by an independent public accountant, as the Commission may, by rule, prescribe.
