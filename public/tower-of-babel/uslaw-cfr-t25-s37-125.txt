25 C.F.R. § 37.125 — Can an eligible student living off a reservation attend a school or dorm?
Citation: us:cfr:25:37.125
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-37.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 37 (Geographic Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. An eligible student living off a reservation can attend a day school, on-reservation boarding school, or peripheral dorm.
