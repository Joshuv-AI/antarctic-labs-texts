9 C.F.R. § 2.9 — Officers, agents, and employees of licensees whose licenses have been suspended or revoked.
Citation: us:cfr:9:2.9
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-2.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 2 (Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who has been or is an officer, agent, or employee of a licensee whose license has been suspended or revoked and who was responsible for or participated in the activity upon which the order of suspension or revocation was based will not be licensed, or registered as a carrier, intermediate handler, dealer, exhibitor, or research facility, within the period during which the order of suspension or revocation is in effect.
