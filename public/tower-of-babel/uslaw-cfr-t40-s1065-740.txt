40 C.F.R. § 1065.740 — Lubricants.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1065.740
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1065.740
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1065 (Engine-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Use commercially available lubricating oil that represents the oil that will be used in your engine in use.
(b) You may use lubrication additives, up to the levels that the additive manufacturer recommends.
