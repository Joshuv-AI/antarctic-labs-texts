20 C.F.R. § 229.52 — Age reduction when a reduced age O/M is effective before DIB O/M.
Citation: us:cfr:20:229.52
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-229.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 229 (Social Security Overall Minimum Guarantee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee received a reduced age O/M before the effective date of a DIB O/M, the PIA amount for the DIB O/M is reduced as if the employee had attained retirement age on the effective date of the DIB O/M.
