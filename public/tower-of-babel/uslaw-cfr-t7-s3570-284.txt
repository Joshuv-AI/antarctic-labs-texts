7 C.F.R. § 3570.284 — Review or appeal rights.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3570.284
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3570.284
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3570 (Community Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may seek a review of an Agency decision under this subpart from the appropriate Agency official that oversees the program in question or appeal to the USDA National Appeals Division in accordance with 7 CFR part 11.
