10 U.S.C. § 8868 — Disposition of prize money
Citation: us:usc:10:8868
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8868
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 883 (Prize). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The net proceeds of all property condemned as prize shall be decreed to the United States and shall be ordered by the court to be paid into the Treasury.
