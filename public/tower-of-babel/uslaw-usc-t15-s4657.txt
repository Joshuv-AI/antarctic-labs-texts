15 U.S.C. § 4657 — Prohibition relating to foreign entities of concern
Citation: us:usc:15:4657
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4657
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 72A (Creating Helpful Incentives To Produce Semiconductors For America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter may be provided to a foreign entity of concern.
