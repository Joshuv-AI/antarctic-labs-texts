Correction: Utilization of home-based care and its buffering effects between dementia caregiving intensity and caregiver burden in China
Authors: Yang Zhou, Wallace Chi-ho Chan
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Geriatr 24, 913 (2024)

10.1186/s12877-024-05501-4

In this erratum, we would like to clarify the relationship between our article, Zhou and Chan [1], “Utilization of home-based care and its buffering effects between dementia caregiving intensity and caregiver burden in China”, published in BMC Geriatrics, and our earlier publication, Zhou and Yan (2021), “Burden of family caregivers of the mentally retarded elderly from the perspective of stress process theory” [in Chinese], published in Journal of Zhejiang Gongshang University.

Both articles are based on data from the same survey of dementia caregivers conducted in 2017–2018. The earlier study, Zhou and Yan (2021), focused on identifying correlates of caregiver burden within a descriptive framework based on the stress process model. The later study, Zhou and Chan [1], addressed a different research question by examining the association between caregiving intensity and caregiver burden, and by testing the moderating role of home-based care (HBC) in this relationship. The two papers are therefore conceptually related.

Although the analytic samples differ due to different inclusion criteria (318 cases in Zhou and Chan, 2024 [1]; 271 cases in Zhou and Yan, 2021), they partially overlap. As a result, there is some overlap in the description of the study design, sample characteristics, and certain variables. We acknowledge that the relationship between the two studies and the use of shared data should have been more clearly disclosed, and we apologize for this omission and for any lack of clarity regarding the relationship between the two manuscripts.

The BMC Geriatrics article extends the earlier work by further developing the conceptualization of caregiving intensity and by examining the role of service utilization in shaping caregiver outcomes, providing additional empirical insight into the mechanisms linking care demands, service utilization, and caregiver outcomes.

We acknowledge that the earlier Chinese-language publication was not cited in the BMC Geriatrics article, and that the shared data source and the relationship between the two studies were not explicitly disclosed. We regret this omission and apologize for any confusion it may have caused.

The earlier publication is now cited as follows:

Zhou, Y., & Yan, S. (2021). Burden of Family Caregivers of the Mentally Retarded Elderly from the Perspective of Stress Process Theory [in Chinese]. Journal of Zhejiang Gongshang University, (3), 141–152.
