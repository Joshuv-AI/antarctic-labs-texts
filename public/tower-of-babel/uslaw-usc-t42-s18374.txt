42 U.S.C. § 18374 — Instrument test-beds and venture class missions
Citation: us:usc:42:18374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18374
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 159 (Space Exploration, Technology, And Science); Subchapter VI (Earth Science). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall pursue innovative ways to fly instrument-level payloads for early demonstration or as co-manifested payloads. The Congress encourages the use of the ISS as an accessible platform for the conduct of such activities. Additionally, in order to address the cost and schedule challenges associated with large flight systems, NASA should pursue smaller systems where practicable and warranted.
