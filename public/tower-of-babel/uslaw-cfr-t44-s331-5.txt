44 C.F.R. § 331.5 — Production facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:44:331.5
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-331.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 331 (Preservation Of The Mobilization Base Through The Placement Of Procurement And Facilities In Labor Surplus Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Federal departments and agencies shall give consideration to labor surplus areas in the selection of sites for Government-financed production facilities, including expansion, to the extent that such selection is consistent with existing law and essential economic and strategic factors.
