49 C.F.R. § 386.41 — Stipulations regarding discovery.
Citation: us:cfr:49:386.41
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-386.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 386 (Rules Of Practice For Fmcsa Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise ordered, a written stipulation entered into by all the parties and filed with the Agency Decisionmaker or the administrative law judge, if one has been appointed, may:
(a) Provide that depositions be taken before any person, at any time or place, upon sufficient notice, and in any manner, and when so taken may be used like other depositions, and
(b) Modify the procedures provided by these rules for other methods of discovery.
