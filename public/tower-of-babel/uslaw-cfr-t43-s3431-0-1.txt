43 C.F.R. § 3431.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3431.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3431.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3430 (Noncompetitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to provide procedures for the sale of coal that is necessarily removed in the exercise of a right-of-way issued under Title V of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (43 U.S.C. 1761 et seq.).
