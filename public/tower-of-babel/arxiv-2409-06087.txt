SBV REGULARITY OF ENTROPY SOLUTIONS FOR HYPERBOLIC SYSTEMS OF BALANCE LAWS WITH GENERAL FLUX FUNCTION
Fabio Ancona, Laura Caravenna, Andrea Marson

Abstract
. We prove that vanishing viscosity solutions to smooth non-degenerate systems of balance laws, having small bounded variation, in one space dimension, must be functions of special bounded variation ( SBV \mathrm{SBV} ). For more than one equation, this SBV \mathrm{SBV} -regularity for non-degenerate fluxes is new also in the case of systems of conservation laws outside the context of genuine nonlinearity. For general smooth strictly hyperbolic systems of balance laws, this regularity fails, as known for systems of conservation laws: in such case we generalize the SBV \mathrm{SBV} -like regularity of the eigenvalue functions of the Jacobian matrix of the flux from conservation to balance laws. Proofs are based on extending Oleinink-type balance estimates, with the introduction of new source measure, a localization argument from [ 41 , 15 ] , and observations in real analysis.

Let

\displaystyle[{{\mathfrak{\upsilon}}}_{i}(t)]^{+}(B)\leq C\left(\frac{\mathcal{L}^{1}(B)}{t-s}+\mu^{ICS}([s,t]\times\mathbb{R})\right)

4.

The case of a genuinely nonlinear field of Theorem

It is relevant to extend the

Definition 21
.

Let

be the eigenvalues of a matrix

\pi_{i}^{(k+1)}=\nabla\pi_{i}^{(k)}\cdot r_{i}

(4.1)

\displaystyle\left\{\pi_{i}^{(k)}\right\}_{k=1,\dots,M}\neq 0^{M}\,.

In case of a scalar equation, when

, then
\pi_{1}^{(1)}=f^{\prime\prime}
and
\pi_{1}^{(k)}=f^{(k+1)}
: a flux is called non-degenerate for us if there is a derivative higher than the first which is non-vanishing.
In
[
30
]

was considered piecewise genuinely nonlinear if

and

do not vanish simultaneously, and in
[
34
]
the same condition denoted nongenerate fluxes.

Remark 22
.

When all fields are genuinely nonlinear, then
\pi_{i}^{(1)}\neq 0
for

and this nondegeneracy condition holds.
Piecewise genuinely nonlinear fluxes of
[

satisfy
\pi_{i}^{(1)}

\pi_{i}^{(2)}
do not vanish simultaneously, for

[

]
fluxes were called nondegenerate when

, …,

do not vanish simultaneously, for

With the Whitney topology and when

, the set of functions

satisfying our non-degeneracy condition (

Before relating the non-degeneracy of the flux to the regularity of solutions, we better understand the condition.

Lemma 23
.

Suppose
A
is non-degenerate as in Definition
21
. For each

and any

, the set

Z_{i}\doteq{}\{u\in\Omega\quad:\quad\nabla\lambda_{i}(u)\cdot r_{i}(u)=0\}\cap[-R,R]^{N}

is the union of finitely many disjoint smooth sub-manifolds

Proof.

Since the non-degeneracy assumption grants that

, …,

do not vanish simultaneously, the decomposition can be constructed applying the implicit function theorem to the sets

\bigcap_{k=1}^{\ell}\{\pi^{(k)}_{i}=0\}\cap\{\pi^{(\ell+1)}_{i}\neq 0\}\qquad\text{for }\ell=M-1,\dots,1\,.

Consider now any

and consider any smooth curve

with

In particular, for

identically so that

By the non-degeneracy assumption, necessarily

\displaystyle\left\lvert\left[{{\mathfrak{\upsilon}}}_{i}^{\nu,\mathrm{jump}}(t)\right](a(t))-\left(\widetilde{\lambda_{i}}(t,a(t+))-\widetilde{\lambda_{i}}(t,a(t-))\right)\right\rvert\leq 2\varepsilon_{\nu}+\beta_{\nu}\,.
