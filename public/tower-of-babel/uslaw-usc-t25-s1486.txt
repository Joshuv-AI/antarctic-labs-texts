25 U.S.C. § 1486 — Loans ineligible for guaranty or insurance
Citation: us:usc:25:1486
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1486
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter II (Loan Guaranty And Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans made by any agency or instrumentality of the Federal Government (not including an eligible Community Development Finance Institution), or by an organization of Indians from funds borrowed from the United States, and loans the interest on which is not included in gross income for the purposes of chapter 1 of title 26 shall not be eligible for guaranty or insurance hereunder.
