36 C.F.R. § 1280.44 — Filming, photographing, or videotaping for commercial purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1280.44
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1280.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1280 (Use Of Nara Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are not permitted to film, photograph, or videotape for commercial purposes on NARA property or in NARA facilities.
