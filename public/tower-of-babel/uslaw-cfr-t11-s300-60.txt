11 C.F.R. § 300.60 — Scope (52 U.S.C. 30125(e)(1)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:300.60
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-300.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 300 (Non-Federal Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to:
(a) Federal candidates;
(b) Individuals holding Federal office (see 11 CFR 300.2(o));
(c) Agents acting on behalf of a Federal candidate or individual holding Federal office; and
(d) Entities that are directly or indirectly established, financed, maintained, or controlled by, or acting on behalf of, one or more Federal candidates or individuals holding Federal office.
