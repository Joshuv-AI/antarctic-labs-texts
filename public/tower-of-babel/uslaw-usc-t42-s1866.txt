42 U.S.C. § 1866 — Divisions within Foundation
Citation: us:usc:42:1866
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1866
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16 (National Science Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be within the Foundation such Divisions as the Director, in consultation with the Board, may from time to time determine.
