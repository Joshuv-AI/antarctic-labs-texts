5 C.F.R. § 317.603 — Selection.
Citation: us:cfr:5:317.603
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-317.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 317 (Employment In The Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may make a noncareer or limited appointment without the use of merit staffing procedures. The appointee, however, must meet the qualifications requirements for the position, as determined in writing by the appointing authority.
