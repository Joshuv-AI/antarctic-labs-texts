34 C.F.R. § 104.54 — Education of institutionalized persons.
Citation: us:cfr:34:104.54
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-104.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 104 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient to which this subpart applies and that operates or supervises a program or activity that provides aid, benefits or services for persons who are institutionalized because of handicap shall ensure that each qualified handicapped person, as defined in § 104.3(k)(2), in its program or activity is provided an appropriate education, as defined in § 104.33(b). Nothing in this section shall be interpreted as altering in any way the obligations of recipients under subpart D.
