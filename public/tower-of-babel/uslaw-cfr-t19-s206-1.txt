19 C.F.R. § 206.1 — Applicability of part.
Citation: us:cfr:19:206.1
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-206.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 206 (Investigations Relating To Global And Bilateral Safeguard Actions, Market Disruption, Trade Diversion, And Review Of Relief Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to proceedings of the Commission under sections 201-202, 204, and 406 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 U.S.C. 2251-2252, 2254, and 2436), and sections 301-302 of the United States-Mexico-Canada Implementation Act (19 U.S.C. 4551-4552) (hereinafter USMCA Implementation Act), and the statutory provisions listed in § 206.31 that implement bilateral safeguard provisions in other free trade agreements into which the United States has entered.
