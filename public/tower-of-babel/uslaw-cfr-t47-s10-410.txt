47 C.F.R. § 10.410 — Prioritization.
Citation: us:cfr:47:10.410
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-10.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 10 (Wireless Emergency Alerts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Participating CMS Provider is required to transmit National Alerts upon receipt. National Alerts preempt all other Alert Messages. A Participating CMS Provider is required to transmit Imminent Threat Alerts, AMBER Alerts and Public Safety Messages on a first in-first out (FIFO) basis.
