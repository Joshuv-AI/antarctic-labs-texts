40 C.F.R. § 104.11 — Briefs and findings of fact.
Citation: us:cfr:40:104.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-104.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 104 (Public Hearings On Effluent Standards For Toxic Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the conclusion of the hearing, the Presiding Officer shall set a schedule for the submission by the parties of briefs and proposed findings of fact and conclusions. In establishing the aforesaid time schedule, the Presiding Officer shall consider the time constraints placed upon the parties and the Administrator by the statutory deadlines.
