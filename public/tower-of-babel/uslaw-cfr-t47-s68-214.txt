47 C.F.R. § 68.214 — Changes in other than “fully protected” premises wiring that serves fewer than four subscriber access lines.
Citation: us:cfr:47:68.214
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-68.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 68 (Connection Of Terminal Equipment To The Telephone Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Operations associated with the installation, connection, reconfiguration and removal (other than final removal) of premises wiring that serves fewer than four subscriber access lines must be performed as provided in § 68.215(c) if the premises wiring is not “fully protected.” For this purpose, the supervisor and installer may be the same person.
