21 C.F.R. § 582.3670 — Propylparaben.
Citation: us:cfr:21:582.3670
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-582.3670
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 582 (Substances Generally Recognized As Safe). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Product. Propylparaben (propyl p-hydroxybenzoate).
(b) Tolerance. This substance is generally recognized as safe for use at a level not exceeding 0.1 percent in accordance with good manufacturing or feeding practice.
