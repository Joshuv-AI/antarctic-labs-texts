26 C.F.R. § 513.3 — Interest.
Citation: us:cfr:26:513.3
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-513.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 513 (Ireland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of § 513.2 relating to the degree of liability to Irish tax in the case of dividends are equally applicable with respect to the income falling within the scope of this section.
