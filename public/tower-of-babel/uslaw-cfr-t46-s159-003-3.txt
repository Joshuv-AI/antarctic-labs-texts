46 C.F.R. § 159.003-3 — -3 Acceptance of foreign approvals under a Mutual Recognition Agreement (MRA).
Citation: us:cfr:46:159.003-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-159.003-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 159 (Approval Of Equipment And Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Coast Guard approval issued by a foreign authority in accordance with the provisions of an effective MRA is acceptable for any application where the regulations in this chapter require Coast Guard approval.
