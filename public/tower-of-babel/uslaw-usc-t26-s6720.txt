26 U.S.C. § 6720 — Fraudulent acknowledgments with respect to donations of motor vehicles, boats, and airplanes
Citation: us:usc:26:6720
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6720
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 68 (Additions To The Tax, Additional Amounts, And Assessable Penalties); Subchapter B (Assessable Penalties); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any donee organization required under section 170(f)(12)(A) to furnish a contemporaneous written acknowledgment to a donor which knowingly furnishes a false or fraudulent acknowledgment, or which knowingly fails to furnish such acknowledgment in the manner, at the time, and showing the information required under section 170(f)(12), or regulations prescribed thereunder, shall for each such act, or for each such failure, be subject to a penalty equal to—
(1)
in the case of an acknowledgment with respect to a qualified vehicle to which section 170(f)(12)(A)(ii) applies, the greater of—
(A)
the product of the highest rate of tax specified in section 1 and the sales price stated on the acknowledgment, or
(B)
the gross proceeds from the sale of such vehicle, and
(2)
in the case of an acknowledgment with respect to any other qualified vehicle to which section 170(f)(12) applies, the greater of—
(A)
the product of the highest rate of tax specified in section 1 and the claimed value of the vehicle, or
(B)
$5,000.
