33 C.F.R. § 117.42 — Remotely operated and automated drawbridges.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.42
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon written request by the owner of a drawbridge, the District Commander may authorize a drawbridge to operate under an automated system or from a remote location.
(b) If the request is approved, a description of the full operation of the remotely operated or automated drawbridge will be added to subpart B of this part.
