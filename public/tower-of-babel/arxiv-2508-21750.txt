It is capable of simultaneously describing the spatial and spin components of the wave function, providing an effective description of the internal momentum distribution within the meson. \displaystyle+\,m_{\ell}^{2}\,)+3\,m_{\ell}^{2}\,\bigg(\,1\,-\frac{m_{a_{0}}^{2}}{m_{D}^{2}}\,\bigg)^{2}|\,f_{0}^{D\to a_{0}}(\,q^{2})\,|^{2}\bigg\}.

(6)

To study the behavior of the TFFs in the process
D\to a_{0}(1450)
, we proceed following the standard methodology of sum rules. The derivation begins with the correlation function:

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu}(p,q)=i\int d^{4}xe^{iq\cdot x}\langle a_{0}(1450)(p)|T\{J_{n}(x),j_{n}^{\dagger}(0)\}|0\rangle,

(7)

here, the current operators are defined as

and

, where
q_{1}
and
q_{2}
represent light quarks. In the next step, we derive the TFFs following the standard procedure of QCD sum rules. On the one hand, in the timelike
q^{2}
-region, we insert a complete set of intermediate states with
D
-meson quantum numbers into the correlation function Eq. (
7
). After isolating the pole contribution from the lowest-lying
D
-meson, we obtain the hadronic representation. The contributions from higher resonances and continuum states are then replaced using hadronic dispersion relations, ultimately yielding the hadronic representation of the
D\to a_{0}(1450)
correlation function. On the other hand, in the spacelike
q^{2}
-region, we perform the operator product expansion (OPE) near the light-cone (
x^{2}\approx 0
). In the expansion of the
c
-quark propagator, only the twist-2,-3 contributions are retained, as the higher-twist terms give negligible contributions to the LCSR in practical calculations. By introducing an effective threshold parameter
s_{0}
to separate all continuum and excited states, and applying the Borel transformation, we finally obtain the analytic expressions for the
D\to a_{0}(1450)

\displaystyle f_{+}^{D\to a_{0}}(q^{2})=\frac{m_{c}\bar{f}_{a_{0}}}{m_{D}^{2}f_{D}}\,\int_{u_{0}}^{1}\,du\,e^{(m_{a_{0}}^{2}-s(u))/M^{2}}\bigg\{-\frac{m_{c}}{u}\,\phi_{2;a_{0}}\,(u,\mu)

\displaystyle\quad+m_{a_{0}}\phi_{3;a_{0}}^{p}(u,\mu)+\frac{m_{a_{0}}}{6}\bigg[\,\frac{2}{u}\,\phi_{3;a_{0}}^{\sigma}(u,\mu)-\frac{1}{m_{c}^{2}+u^{2}m_{a_{0}}^{2}-q^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\bigg((\,m_{c}^{2}-u^{2}\,m_{a_{0}}^{2}+q^{2}\,)\,\frac{d\phi_{3;a_{0}}^{\sigma}(u,\mu)}{du}\,-\,\frac{4um_{c}^{2}m_{a_{0}}^{2}}{m_{c}^{2}+u^{2}m_{a_{0}}^{2}-q^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\phi_{3;a_{0}}^{\sigma}(u,\mu)\bigg)\bigg]\bigg\},

(8)

\displaystyle f_{-}^{D\to a_{0}}(q^{2})\,=\,\frac{\,m_{c}\,\,\bar{f}_{a_{0}}}{m_{D}^{2}\,\,f_{D}}\,\int_{u_{0}}^{1}\,du\,\,e^{(\,m_{a_{0}}^{2}\,-\,s\,(u)\,)/\,M^{2}}\,\bigg[\,\frac{\,\,\phi_{3;a_{0}}^{p}(\,u\,,\mu\,)}{u}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{6u}\frac{d\phi_{3;a_{0}}^{\sigma}(u,\mu)}{du}\bigg],

(9)

where the lower limits of the integration are

, the abbreviation

, the

are the mass and decay constant of the

-meson,

-quark, and

We adopt LCHO model to construct the twist-2 LCDA of the

\displaystyle\Psi_{2;a_{0}}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp})=\sum_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}\chi_{2;a_{0}}^{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp})\Psi_{2;a_{0}}^{R}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp}),

\displaystyle\Psi_{2;a_{0}}^{R}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp})=A_{2;a_{0}}\varphi_{2;a_{0}}(x)\exp\Big[-\frac{\mathbf{k}_{\perp}^{2}+\hat{m}_{q}^{2}}{8\beta_{2;a_{0}}^{2}{x}{\bar{x}}}\Big],

;

\mathbf{k}_{\perp}

is introduced to tune the

-direction shape (including endpoint behavior and width) of the twist-2 LCDA for
a_{0}(1450)

Next we present the form of the spin wave function. Referring to the spin wave function of
\pi
-meson, the wave function of the meson of the instantaneous SU(6) quark model is usually transformed into the light cone form to obtain the complete spin wave function of the meson

;

;

a_{0}(1450)
-meson, we take spin

, orbital angular momentum

and

. We take the spin wave function of the instant-form (T) of the rest frame
(\boldsymbol{q}_{1}+\boldsymbol{q}_{2}=0)
,

\displaystyle\chi_{a_{0}}^{T}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\chi_{1}^{\uparrow}\chi_{2}^{\downarrow}-\chi_{2}^{\uparrow}\chi_{1}^{\downarrow}),

(12)

where
\chi_{1/2}^{\uparrow/\downarrow}
is the Pauli spinor of the triplet state, and the four-momenta of the two quarks are respectively:
q^{\mu}_{1}=(q^{0},\boldsymbol{q})
,
q^{\mu}_{2}=(q^{0},-\boldsymbol{q})
,
q^{0}=\sqrt{m^{2}+\boldsymbol{q}^{2}}
. The instant-form
|J,s\rangle_{T}
and the light-cone form
|J,\lambda\rangle_{F}
are related by Wigner rotation, and for hadronic states with total
J=0

|J,\lambda\rangle_{F}=\sum_{s}U_{s\lambda}^{J}|J,s\rangle_{T}
, for a quark with spin-1/2, the Melosh transformation is as follows:

\displaystyle\chi^{\uparrow}(T)=\omega[(q^{+}+m)\chi^{\uparrow}(F)-q_{R}\chi^{\downarrow}(F)],

\displaystyle\chi^{\downarrow}(T)=\omega[(q^{+}+m)\chi^{\downarrow}(F)-q_{L}\chi^{\uparrow}(F)],

(13)

where
\omega=[2q^{+}(q^{0}+m)]^{-1/2},\quad q_{R/L}=q_{1}\pm iq_{2},\quad q^{+}=q^{0}+q^{3}

a_{0}(1450)
-meson can be obtained,

\displaystyle\chi_{a_{0}}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp})=\sum_{\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2}}C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})\chi_{1}^{\lambda_{1}}(F)\chi_{2}^{\lambda_{2}}(F).

(14)

When expressed in terms of the instant-form momentum
q^{\mu}=(q^{0},\boldsymbol{q})
, the component coefficient

can be calculated, and its specific form is as follows:

\displaystyle C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\uparrow,\downarrow)

\displaystyle=+\frac{m}{\sqrt{2(m^{2}+\mathbf{k}_{\perp}^{2})}},

\displaystyle C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\downarrow,\uparrow)

\displaystyle=-\frac{m}{\sqrt{2\big(m^{2}+\mathbf{k}_{\perp}^{2})}},

\displaystyle C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\uparrow,\uparrow)

\displaystyle=-\frac{(k_{1}-ik_{2})}{\sqrt{2(m^{2}+\mathbf{k}_{\perp}^{2})}},

\displaystyle C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\downarrow,\downarrow)

\displaystyle=-\frac{(k_{1}+ik_{2})}{\sqrt{2(m^{2}+\mathbf{k}_{\perp}^{2})}},

(15)

these coefficients satisfy the following normalization relation:
\sum_{\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2}}C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})^{*}C_{0}^{F}(x,\mathbf{k}_{\perp},\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})=1

Using the relationship between the leading-twist LCDA of

•

In the physical sense, the LCSR approach is applicable to low and intermediate
q^{2}
-regions. Thus, for the TFFs of the
D\to a_{0}(1450)
transition process, to obtain reasonable LCSR results, we extrapolate them to all allowed physical regions, i.e.,
0\leq q^{2}\leq(m_{D}-m_{a_{0}})^{2}
. We adopt the simplified series expansion (SSE) to fit the dull analytical results, the SSE is a fast convergent series on the
z(t)
-expansion, and its expansion is as follows
Bharucha:2015bzk
;
Bourrely:2008za
;
Fu:2018yin
,

\displaystyle f_{i}(q^{2})=P_{i}(q^{2})\sum_{k=0,1,2}a_{k}^{i}[z(q^{2})-z(0)]^{k},

(31)

where
f_{i}(q^{2})
stands for
D\to a_{0}(1450)
TFFs,
a_{k}^{i}
are the coefficients, and
P_{i}(q^{2})=\big(1-{q^{2}}/{m_{R,i}^{2}}\big)^{-1}
,
z(t)=({\sqrt{t_{+}-t}-\sqrt{t_{+}-t_{0}}})/({\sqrt{t_{+}-t}+\sqrt{t_{+}-t_{0}}})
, with
t_{+}=(m_{D}-m_{a_{0}})^{2}
,
t_{0}=t_{+}(1-\sqrt{1-(t_{-})/(t_{+})})
.
P_{i}(q^{2})
is a simple pole corresponding to the first resonance in the spectrum and can be used to account for the low-lying resonance, and
m_{R,i}
is the resonance of the
D
-meson. The masses of the low-lying
D
-meson resonances are mainly determined by their
J^{P}
quantum number states, which are
m_{D}^{*}(2007)^{0}
and
m_{D}^{*}(2300)
here. The free parameters
a_{1}^{i}
and
a_{2}^{i}
are also determined to make the goodness of fit
\Delta
of the TFF as small as possible. Conventionally, we require
\Delta<1\%
, and
\Delta
is used to measure the extrapolation quality. Meanwhile, we also present the fitting parameters for the TFFs in Table
2
. After extrapolating the TFFs to the entire
q^{2}
-region, we obtain the behavior of
D\to a_{0}(1450)
TFFs within the full kinematic region of
q^{2}
, as shown in Fig.

. Within the framework of the QCD sum rule approach, we present the predictions for

-region. This is in good agreement with the predictions from the QCDSR (
\mathrm{S2}
)
Cheng:2005nb

Galkin:2025emi

IV
