19 C.F.R. § 200.735-112 — -112 General conduct prejudicial to the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:19:200.735-112
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-200.735-112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 200 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee shall not engage in criminal, infamous, dishonest, immoral, or notoriously disgraceful conduct, or other conduct prejudicial to the Government.
