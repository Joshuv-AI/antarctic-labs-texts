29 U.S.C. § 1843 — Information on employment conditions
Citation: us:usc:29:1843
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1843
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 20 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection); Subchapter IV (Further Protections For Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Workers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each farm labor contractor, without regard to any other provisions of this chapter, shall obtain at each place of employment and make available for inspection to every worker he furnishes for employment, a written statement of the conditions of such employment as described in sections 1821(b) and 1831(b) of this title.
