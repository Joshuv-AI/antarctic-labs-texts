38 C.F.R. § 21.7040 — Categories of basic eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.7040
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.7040
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligibility for basic educational assistance can be established by:
(a) Some individuals who first become members of the Armed Forces or who first enter on active duty as a member of the Armed Forces after June 30, 1985, and
(b) Some individuals who are eligible for educational assistance allowance under 38 U.S.C. chapter 34.
