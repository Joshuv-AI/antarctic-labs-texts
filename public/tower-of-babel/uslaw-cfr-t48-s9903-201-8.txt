48 C.F.R. § 9903.201-8 — 9903.201-8 Compliant accounting changes due to external restructuring activities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9903.201-8
Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9903.201-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9903 (Contract Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contract price and cost adjustment requirements of this part 9903 are not applicable to compliant cost accounting practice changes directly associated with external restructuring activities that are subject to and meet the requirements of 10 U.S.C. 2325.
