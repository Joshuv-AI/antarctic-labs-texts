25 C.F.R. § 211.8 — Government employees cannot acquire leases.
Citation: us:cfr:25:211.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-211.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 211 (Leasing Of Tribal Lands For Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

U.S. Government employees are prevented from acquiring leases or interests in leases by the provisions of 25 CFR part 140 and 43 CFR part 20 pertaining to conflicts of interest and ownership of an interest in trust land.
