27 C.F.R. § 478.60 — Certain continuances of business.
Citation: us:cfr:27:478.60
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-478.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 478 (Commerce In Firearms And Ammunition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensee who furnishes his license to the Chief, Federal Firearms Licensing Center for correction or endorsement in compliance with the provisions contained in this subpart may continue his operations while awaiting its return.
