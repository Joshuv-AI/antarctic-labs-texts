6 U.S.C. § 494 — Other functions and activities
Citation: us:usc:6:494
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section494
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IX (National Homeland Security Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of more effectively coordinating the policies and functions of the United States Government relating to homeland security, the Council shall—
(1)
assess the objectives, commitments, and risks of the United States in the interest of homeland security and to 1 make resulting recommendations to the President;
(2)
oversee and review homeland security policies of the Federal Government and to 1 make resulting recommendations to the President; and
(3)
perform such other functions as the President may direct.
