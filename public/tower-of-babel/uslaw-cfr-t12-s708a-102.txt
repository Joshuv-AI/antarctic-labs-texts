12 C.F.R. § 708a.102 — a.102 Authority to convert.
Citation: us:cfr:12:708a.102
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-708a.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 708a (PART 708a—BANK CONVERSIONS AND MERGERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A credit union, with the approval of its members, may convert to a mutual savings bank or a savings association that is in mutual form without the prior approval of the NCUA, subject to applicable law governing mutual savings banks and savings associations and the other requirements of this part.
