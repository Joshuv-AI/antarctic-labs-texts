12 U.S.C. § 1735e–1 — Use of American materials and products
Citation: us:usc:12:1735e–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1735e–1
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the administration of housing assistance programs, the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall encourage the use of materials and products mined and produced in the United States.
