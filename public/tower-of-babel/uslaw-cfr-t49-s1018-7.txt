49 C.F.R. § 1018.7 — Conversion claims.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1018.7
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1018.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1018 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These procedures are directed primarily to the recovery of money on behalf of the Government. The Board may demand:
(a) The return of specific property; or
(b) Either the return of property or the payment of its value.
