12 U.S.C. § 1884 — Penalties for violations
Citation: us:usc:12:1884
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1884
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 19 (Security Measures For Banks And Savings Associations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A bank or savings association which violates a rule promulgated pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to a civil penalty which shall not exceed $100 for each day of the violation.
