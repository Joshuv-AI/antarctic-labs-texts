16 U.S.C. § 937 — United States Section as agency of United States
Citation: us:usc:16:937
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section937
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 15A (Great Lakes Fisheries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Section shall, for the purposes of these 1 provisions of title 28, relating to claims against the United States and tort claims procedure, be deemed to be an agency of the United States.
