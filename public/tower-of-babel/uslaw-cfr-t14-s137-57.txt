14 C.F.R. § 137.57 — Availability of certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:14:137.57
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-137.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 137 (Agricultural Aircraft Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each holder of an agricultural aircraft operator certificate shall keep that certificate at his home base of operations and shall present it for inspection on the request of the Administrator or any Federal, State, or local law enforcement officer.
