40 C.F.R. § 425.01 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:425.01
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-425.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 425 (Leather Tanning And Finishing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any leather tanning and finishing facility which discharges or may discharge process wastewater pollutants to the waters of the United States, or which introduces or may introduce process wastewater pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works.
