42 C.F.R. § 50.206 — Sterilization of a mentally incompetent individual or of an institutionalized individual.
Citation: us:cfr:42:50.206
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-50.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 50 (Policies Of General Applicability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Programs or projects to which this subpart applies shall not perform or arrange for the performance of a sterilization of any mentally incompetent individual or institutionalized individual.
