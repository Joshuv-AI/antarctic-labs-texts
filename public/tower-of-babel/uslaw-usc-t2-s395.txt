2 U.S.C. § 395 — Death of contestant
Citation: us:usc:2:395
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section395
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 12 (Contested Elections). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the event of the death of the contestant, the contested election case shall abate.
