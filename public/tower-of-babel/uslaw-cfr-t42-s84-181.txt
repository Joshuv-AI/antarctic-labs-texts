42 C.F.R. § 84.181 — Communication performance test—PAPR class PAPR100.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.181
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.181
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Powered air-purifying respirators must be designed to allow for proper communication while worn.
(b) A Modified Rhyme Test 7 will be used to test the wearer's ability to communicate efficiently.
(c) The communications requirement is met if the overall performance rating is greater than or equal to 70 percent.
