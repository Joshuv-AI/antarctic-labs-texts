7 C.F.R. § 633.15 — Payments not subject to claims.
Citation: us:cfr:7:633.15
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-633.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 633 (Water Bank Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any payments due any person shall be determined and allowed without regard to State land and without regard to any claim or lien against any crop, or proceeds thereof, which may be asserted by any creditor, except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section.
(b) The regulations governing setoffs and withholdings, in part 13 of this title, as amended, shall be applicable to this program.
