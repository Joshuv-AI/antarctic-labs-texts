25 C.F.R. § 242.6 — Spawning season.
Citation: us:cfr:25:242.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-242.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 242 (Commercial Fishing On Red Lake Indian Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Walleye and northern pike (or pickerel) shall not be taken during their spawning season except for propagation purposes.
