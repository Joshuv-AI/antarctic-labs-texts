29 C.F.R. § 1614.701 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1614.701
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1614.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1614 (Federal Sector Equal Employment Opportunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements Title III of the Notification and Federal Employee Antidiscrimination and Retaliation Act of 2002 (No FEAR Act), Pub. L. 107-174. It sets forth the basic responsibilities of Federal agencies and the Commission to post certain information on their public Web sites.
