42 U.S.C. § 300j–17 — Estrogenic substances screening program
Citation: us:usc:42:300j–17
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300j–17
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XII (Safety Of Public Water Systems); Part E (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the substances referred to in section 346a(p)(3)(B) of title 21 the Administrator may provide for testing under the screening program authorized by section 346a(p) of title 21, in accordance with the provisions of section 346a(p) of title 21, of any other substance that may be found in sources of drinking water if the Administrator determines that a substantial population may be exposed to such substance.
