14 C.F.R. § 33.8 — Selection of engine power and thrust ratings.
Citation: us:cfr:14:33.8
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-33.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 33 (Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requested engine power and thrust ratings must be selected by the applicant.
(b) Each selected rating must be for the lowest power or thrust that all engines of the same type may be expected to produce under the conditions used to determine that rating.
