40 C.F.R. § 139.20 — Gas turbines.
Citation: us:cfr:40:139.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-139.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 139 (Discharges Incidental To The Normal Operation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements in paragraph (b) of this section apply to discharges from the washing of gas turbine components.
(b) The discharge of untreated gas turbine washwater is prohibited unless infeasible.
