10 U.S.C. § 12315 — Reserves: duty with or without pay
Citation: us:usc:10:12315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12315
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1209 (Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Subject to other provisions of this title, any Reserve may be ordered to active duty or other duty—
(1)
with the pay and allowances provided by law; or
(2)
with his consent, without pay.
Duty without pay shall be considered for all purposes as if it were duty with pay.
(b)
A Reserve who is kept on active duty after his term of service expires is entitled to pay and allowances while on that duty, except as they may be forfeited under the approved sentence of a court-martial or by non-judicial punishment by a commanding officer or when he is otherwise in a non-pay status.
