DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2381-071]


PacifiCorp; Notice of Reasonable Period of Time for Water Quality 
Certification Application

    On February 11, 2026, PacifiCorp submitted to the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (Commission) documentation from the Idaho 
Department of Environmental Quality (Idaho DEQ) that it received a 
request for a Clean Water Act section 401(a)(1) water quality 
certification as defined in 40 CFR 121.5, from PacifiCorp, in 
conjunction with the above captioned project on January 15, 2026. 
Pursuant to the Commission's regulations,\1\ we hereby notify Idaho DEQ 
of the following dates.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 18 CFR 4.34(b)(5)(iii).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Date of Receipt of the Certification Request: January 15, 2026.
    Reasonable Period of Time To Act on the Certification Request: One 
year, January 15, 2027.
    If Idaho DEQ fails or refuses to act on the water quality 
certification request on

[[Page 37102]]

or before the above date, then the certifying authority is deemed 
waived pursuant to section 401(a)(1) of the Clean Water Act, 33 U.S.C. 
1341(a)(1).

(Authority: 18 CFR 2.1)

    Dated: June 16, 2026.
Carlos D. Clay,
Deputy Secretary.
