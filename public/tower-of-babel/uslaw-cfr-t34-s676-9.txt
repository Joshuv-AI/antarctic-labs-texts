34 C.F.R. § 676.9 — Student eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:34:676.9
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-676.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 676 (Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A student at an institution of higher education is eligible to receive an FSEOG for an award year if the student—
(a) Meets the relevant eligibility requirements contained in 34 CFR 668.32;
(b) Is enrolled or accepted for enrollment as an undergraduate student at the institution; and
(c) Has financial need as determined in accordance with part F of title IV of the HEA.
