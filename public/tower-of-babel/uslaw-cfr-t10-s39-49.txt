10 C.F.R. § 39.49 — Uranium sinker bars.
Citation: us:cfr:10:39.49
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-39.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 39 (Licenses And Radiation Safety Requirements For Well Logging). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee may use a uranium sinker bar in well logging applications only if it is legibly impressed with the words “CAUTION—RADIOACTIVE-DEPLETED URANIUM” and “NOTIFY CIVIL AUTHORITIES (or COMPANY NAME) IF FOUND.”
