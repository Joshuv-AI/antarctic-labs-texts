19 C.F.R. § 111.69 — Recommended decision by hearing officer.
Citation: us:cfr:19:111.69
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-111.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 111 (Customs Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After review of the proposed findings and conclusions submitted by the parties pursuant to § 111.68, the hearing officer will make his recommended decision in the case and certify the entire record to the Executive Assistant Commissioner. The hearing officer's recommended decision must conform to the requirements of 5 U.S.C. 557.
