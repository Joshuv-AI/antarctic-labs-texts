25 C.F.R. § 1000.1410 — Are Federal funds available to cover the cost of Tribes/Consortia carrying out environmental responsibilities?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1410
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, funds are available:
(a) For project-specific environmental costs through the construction project agreement or funding agreement that includes the construction project; and
(b) For environmental review program costs through a funding agreement and/or a construction project agreement.
