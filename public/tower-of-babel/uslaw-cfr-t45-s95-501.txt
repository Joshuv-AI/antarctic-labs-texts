45 C.F.R. § 95.501 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:95.501
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-95.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 95 (General Administration—Grant Programs (Public Assistance, Medical Assistance And State Children'S Health Insurance Programs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes requirements for:
(a) Preparation, submission, and approval of State agency cost allocation plans for public assistance programs; and
(b) Adherence to approved cost allocation plans in computing claims for Federal financial participation.
