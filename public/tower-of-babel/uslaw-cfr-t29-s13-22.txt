29 C.F.R. § 13.22 — Paid sick leave.
Citation: us:cfr:29:13.22
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-13.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 13 (Establishing Paid Sick Leave For Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor shall allow all employees performing work on or in connection with a covered contract to accrue and use paid sick leave as required by Executive Order 13706 and this part.
