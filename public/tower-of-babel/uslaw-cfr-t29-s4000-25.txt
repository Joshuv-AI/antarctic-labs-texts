29 C.F.R. § 4000.25 — What if I use the postal service of a foreign country?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4000.25
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4000.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4000 (Filing, Issuance, Computation Of Time, And Record Retention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you send your submission or issuance using the postal service of a foreign country, your filing or issuance date is the date of receipt at the proper address.
