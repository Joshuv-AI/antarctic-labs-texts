13 C.F.R. § 106.200 — Cosponsored Activity.
Citation: us:cfr:13:106.200
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-106.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 106 (Cosponsorships, Fee And Non-Fee Based Sba-Sponsored Activities And Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator (or designee), after consultation with the General Counsel (or designee), may provide assistance for the benefit of small business through Cosponsored Activities pursuant to section 4(h) of the Small Business Act.
