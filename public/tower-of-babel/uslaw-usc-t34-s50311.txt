34 U.S.C. § 50311 — Authorization of a Badge
Citation: us:usc:34:50311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section50311
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 503 (Law Enforcement Congressional Badge Of Bravery); Subchapter I (Federal Law Enforcement Congressional Badge Of Bravery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may award, and a Member of Congress or the Attorney General may present, in the name of Congress a Federal Law Enforcement Congressional Badge of Bravery to a Federal law enforcement officer who is cited by the Attorney General, upon the recommendation of the Federal Board, for performing an act of bravery while in the line of duty.
