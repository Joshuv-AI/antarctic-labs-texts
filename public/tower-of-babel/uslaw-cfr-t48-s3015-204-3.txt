48 C.F.R. § 3015.204-3 — 3015.204-3 Contract clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3015.204-3
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3015.204-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3015 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert clause (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.215-70, Key Personnel or Facilities, in solicitations and contracts when the selection for award is substantially based on the offeror's possession of special capabilities regarding personnel or facilities.
