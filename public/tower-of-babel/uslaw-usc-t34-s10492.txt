34 U.S.C. § 10492 — General authorization
Citation: us:usc:34:10492
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10492
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXII (Support For Law Enforcement Officers And Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may make grants to States and local law enforcement agencies and to organizations representing State or local law enforcement personnel to provide family support services and mental health services to law enforcement personnel.
