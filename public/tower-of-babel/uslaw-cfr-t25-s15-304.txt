25 C.F.R. § 15.304 — When must I file my claim?
Citation: us:cfr:25:15.304
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-15.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 15 (Probate Of Indian Estates, Except For Members Of The Osage Nation And The Five Civilized Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must file your claim before the conclusion of the first hearing by OHA or, for cases designated as summary probate proceedings, as allowed under 43 CFR 30.140. Claims not timely filed will be barred.
