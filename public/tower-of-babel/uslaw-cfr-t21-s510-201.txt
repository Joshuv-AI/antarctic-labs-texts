21 C.F.R. § 510.201 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:21:510.201
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-510.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 510 (New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to tolerances for residues of new animal drugs not approved or conditionally approved for use in the United States, but lawfully used in another country and present in imported, animal-derived food and food products.
