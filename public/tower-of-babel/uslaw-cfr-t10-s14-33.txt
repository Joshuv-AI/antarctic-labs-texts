10 C.F.R. § 14.33 — Officials authorized to act.
Citation: us:cfr:10:14.33
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-14.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 14 (Administrative Claims Under Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The General Counsel or the General Counsel's designee shall exercise the authority to adjust, determine, compromise and settle a claim under the provisions of 28 U.S.C. 2672.
