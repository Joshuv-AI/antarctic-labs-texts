49 C.F.R. § 40.165 — To whom does the MRO transmit reports of drug test results?
Citation: us:cfr:49:40.165
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-40.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 40 (Procedures For Transportation Workplace Drug And Alcohol Testing Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As the MRO, you must report all drug test results to the DER, except in the circumstances provided for in § 40.345 .
(b) If the employer elects to receive reports of results through a C/TPA, acting as an intermediary as provided in § 40.345 , you must report the results through the designated C/TPA.
