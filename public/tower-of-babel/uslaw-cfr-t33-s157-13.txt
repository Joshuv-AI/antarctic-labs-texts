33 C.F.R. § 157.13 — Designated observation area.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each new vessel must have a designated observation area on the weather deck or above that is:
(a) Located where the effluent from each discharge point and manifold described in § 157.11 can be visually observed; and
(b) Equipped with:
(1) A means to directly stop the discharge of effluent into the sea; or
(2) A positive communication system, such as a telephone or a radio, between the observation area and the discharge control position.
