43 C.F.R. § 10010.39 — Elimination of duplication with state and local procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:43:10010.39
Creator: Utah Reclamation Mitigation And Conservation Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-10010.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 10010 (Policies And Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will incorporate in its appropriate program regulations provisions for the preparation of an EIS by a State agency to the extent authorized in section 102(2)(D) of NEPA.
