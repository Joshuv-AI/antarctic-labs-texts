30 C.F.R. § 550.135 — What will BOEM do if my operating performance is unacceptable?
Citation: us:cfr:30:550.135
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-550.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 550 (Oil And Gas And Sulfur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your operating performance is unacceptable, BOEM may disapprove or revoke your designation as operator on a single facility or multiple facilities. We will give you adequate notice and opportunity for a review by BOEM officials before imposing a disqualification.
