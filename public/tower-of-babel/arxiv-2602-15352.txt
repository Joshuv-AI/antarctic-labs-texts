From Blaschke–Santaló-type inequalities to uniform contractions Keywords and phrases: Euclidean space, intrinsic volume, isoperimetric inequality, Brunn–Minkowski inequality, intersections of congruent balls, r r -ball body, Blaschke–Santaló-type inequalities, uniform contraction, Kneser–Poulsen-type results, Alexander’s conjecture. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: 52A20, 52A22.
Károly Bezdek

Abstract
In this short note, we establish Blaschke–Santaló-type inequalities for r r -ball bodies. Building on these inequalities, we somewhat further extend earlier results on analogues of the Kneser–Poulsen conjecture concerning intersections of balls under uniform contractions in Euclidean d d -space. As an immediate corollary, we obtain a proof of Alexander’s conjecture for uniform contractions.

Proof of Theorem
1

We start by recalling Proposition 2.5 of
[
5
]
. (See also Lemma 8 of
[
6
]
for an extended version.)

Lemma 6
.

Let
r>0
and let
d\geq 2
be an integer. If
\emptyset\neq A\subset\mathbb{E}^{d}
is a compact set with
0<{\rm cr}(A)\leq r
, then

A^{r}-{\rm conv}_{r}(A)=\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},r].

(7)

Clearly, the Brunn–Minkowski inequality for intrinsic volumes (see for example, Eq. (74) in
[
13
]
) combined with (
7
) of Lemma
6
implies the following inequality.

Corollary 7
.

Let
r>0
and let
d\geq 2
be an integer. If
\emptyset\neq A\subset\mathbb{E}^{d}
is a compact set with
0<{\rm cr}(A)\leq r
, then

{\rm V}_{k}\left(A^{r}\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}+{\rm V}_{k}\left({\rm conv}_{r}(A)\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}\leq{\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},r]\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}.

(8)

Thus, Corollary
7
and the positive homogeneity (of degree
k
) of
k
-th intrinsic volume imply in a straightforward way that

{\rm V}_{k}\left(A^{r}\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}\leq r{\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},1]\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}-{\rm V}_{k}\left({\rm conv}_{r}(A)\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}.

(9)

Next, according to a well-known theorem of Alexandrov (see Eq. (20) of
[
9
]
) applied to the convex body
{\rm conv}_{r}(A)
(which is by assumption a compact convex set with non-empty interior in
\mathbb{E}^{d}
), one obtains the following inequality
for the corresponding intrinsic volumes:

\left(\frac{{\rm V}_{l}\left({\rm conv}_{r}(A)\right)}{{\rm V}_{l}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},1]\right)}\right)^{k}\leq\left(\frac{{\rm V}_{k}\left({\rm conv}_{r}(A)\right)}{{\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},1]\right)}\right)^{l},

(10)

where equality is attained if and only if
{\rm conv}_{r}(A)
is a ball.

Now, by definition
{\rm V}_{l}\left({\rm conv}_{r}(A)\right)={\rm V}_{l}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},R^{\mathbb{E}^{d}}_{r,l}(A)]\right)
and therefore - using positive homogeneity of intrinsic volumes - (
10
) turns out to be equivalent to the simpler inequality

{\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},R^{\mathbb{E}^{d}}_{r,l}(A)]\right)\leq{\rm V}_{k}\left({\rm conv}_{r}(A)\right),

(11)

where equality is attained if and only if
{\rm conv}_{r}(A)
is a ball of radius
R^{\mathbb{E}^{d}}_{r,l}(A)
.

Finally, (
9
) and (
11
) together with the positive homogeneity (of degree
k
) of
k
-th intrinsic volume imply in a straightforward way that

{\rm V}_{k}\left(A^{r}\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}\leq r{\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},1]\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}-{\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},R^{\mathbb{E}^{d}}_{r,l}(A)]\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}={\rm V}_{k}\left(\mathbf{B}^{d}[\mathbf{o},r-R^{\mathbb{E}^{d}}_{r,l}(A)]\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}={\rm V}_{k}\left(B^{r}\right)^{\frac{1}{k}}.

Here, we have equality if and only if equality is attained in (
9
) and (
11
), i.e.,
{\rm conv}_{r}(A)
is a ball of radius
R^{\mathbb{E}^{d}}_{r,l}(A)
. This completes the proof of Theorem
1
.
