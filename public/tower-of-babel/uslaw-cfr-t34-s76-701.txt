34 C.F.R. § 76.701 — The State or subgrantee administers or supervises each project.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.701
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State or a subgrantee shall directly administer or supervise the administration of each project.
