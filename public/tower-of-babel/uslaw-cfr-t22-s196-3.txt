22 C.F.R. § 196.3 — Grants to post-secondary education institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:196.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-196.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 196 (Thomas R. Pickering Foreign Affairs/Graduate Foreign Affairs Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of State may make a grant to a post-secondary education institution for the purpose of increasing the level of knowledge and awareness of and interest in employment with the Foreign Service, consistent with 22 U.S.C. 3905, not to exceed $1,000,000, unless otherwise authorized by law.
