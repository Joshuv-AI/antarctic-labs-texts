41 C.F.R. § 102-118.130 — -118.130 Audit requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.130
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3726, agencies are required to establish a program to audit all transportation bills.
(b) Agencies may perform either a prepayment or a post payment audit of transportation invoices.
