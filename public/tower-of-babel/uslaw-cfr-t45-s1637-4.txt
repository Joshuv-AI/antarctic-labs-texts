45 C.F.R. § 1637.4 — Change in circumstances.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1637.4
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1637.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1637 (Representation Of Prisoners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, to the knowledge of the recipient, a client becomes incarcerated after litigation has commenced, the recipient must use its best efforts to withdraw promptly from the litigation, unless the period of incarceration is anticipated to be brief and the litigation is likely to continue beyond the period of incarceration.
