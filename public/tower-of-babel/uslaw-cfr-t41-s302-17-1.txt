41 C.F.R. § 302-17.1 — -17.1 Reimbursement for substantially all, and not exactly all, of the additional income taxes incurred as a result of a relocation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-17.1
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-17.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-17 (17—Taxes On Relocation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under 5 U.S.C. 5724b, employees are reimbursed for substantially all, not exactly all, of the Federal, State, and local income taxes incurred as a result of relocation. The withholding tax allowance (WTA) and relocation income tax allowance (RITA) are the two allowances through which the Government reimburses an employee for substantially all of the income taxes that they incur as a result of the relocation.
