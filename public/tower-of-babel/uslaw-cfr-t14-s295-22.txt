14 C.F.R. § 295.22 — Prohibited unfair or deceptive practices or unfair methods of competition.
Citation: us:cfr:14:295.22
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-295.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 295 (Air Charter Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An air charter broker shall not engage in any unfair or deceptive practice or unfair method of competition.
