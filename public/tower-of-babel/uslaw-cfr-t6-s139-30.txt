6 C.F.R. § 139.30 — Conformance with signs and directions.
Citation: us:cfr:6:139.30
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-139.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 139 (Conduct On Federal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person on Federal property must at all times comply with official signs of a prohibitory, regulatory, or directive nature and with the lawful direction of security personnel.
