25 C.F.R. § 900.197 — Does FTCA cover employees of the contractor who are paid by the contractor from funds other than those provided through the self-determination contract?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.197
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.197
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, as long as the services out of which the claim arose were performed in carrying out the self-determination contract.
