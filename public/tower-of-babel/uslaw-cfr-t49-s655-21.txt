49 C.F.R. § 655.21 — Drug testing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:655.21
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-655.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 655 (Prevention Of Alcohol Misuse And Prohibited Drug Use In Transit Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An employer shall establish a program that provides testing for prohibited drugs and drug metabolites in the following circumstances: pre-employment, post-accident, reasonable suspicion, random, and return to duty/follow-up.
(b) When administering a drug test, an employer shall ensure that the following drugs are tested for:
(1) Marijuana;
(2) Cocaine;
(3) Opioids;
(4) Amphetamines; and
(5) Phencyclidine.
(c) Consumption of these products is prohibited at all times.
