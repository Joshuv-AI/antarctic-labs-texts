48 C.F.R. § 4.705-2 — 4.705-2 Pay administration records.
Citation: us:cfr:48:4.705-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-4.705-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 4 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Payroll sheets, registers, or their equivalent, of salaries and wages paid to individual employees for each payroll period; change slips; and tax withholding statements: Retain 4 years.
(b) Clock cards or other time and attendance cards: Retain 2 years.
(c) Paid checks, receipts for wages paid in cash, or other evidence of payments for services rendered by employees: Retain 2 years.
