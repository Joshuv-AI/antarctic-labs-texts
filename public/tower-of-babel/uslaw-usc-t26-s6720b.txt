26 U.S.C. § 6720B — Fraudulent identification of exempt use property
Citation: us:usc:26:6720B
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6720B
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 68 (Additions To The Tax, Additional Amounts, And Assessable Penalties); Subchapter B (Assessable Penalties); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to any criminal penalty provided by law, any person who identifies applicable property (as defined in section 170(e)(7)(C)) as having a use which is related to a purpose or function constituting the basis for the donee’s exemption under section 501 and who knows that such property is not intended for such a use shall pay a penalty of $10,000.
