27 C.F.R. § 31.53 — Wholesale dealers in beer consummating sales at premises of other dealers.
Citation: us:cfr:27:31.53
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-31.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 31 (Alcohol Beverage Dealers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any dealer who has registered as a wholesale dealer in beer for the place from which that dealer conducts selling operations may consummate sales of beer (but not wines or distilled spirits) to other dealers at the purchasers' places of business without having to register again as a wholesale dealer on account of those sales.
