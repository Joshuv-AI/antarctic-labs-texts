16 U.S.C. § 8207 — Coordination with States and Indian Tribes
Citation: us:usc:16:8207
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8207
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 101 (National Fish Habitat Conservation Through Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide a notice to, and cooperate with, the appropriate State agency or Tribal agency, as applicable, of each State and Indian Tribe within the boundaries of which an activity is planned to be carried out pursuant to this chapter, including notification, by not later than 30 days before the date on which the activity is implemented.
