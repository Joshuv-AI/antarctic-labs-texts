25 C.F.R. § 173.4 — Permits subject to existing and future rights-of-way.
Citation: us:cfr:25:173.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-173.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 173 (Concessions, Permits And Leases On Lands Withdrawn Or Acquired In Connection With Indian Irrigation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use by the permittee of any land authorized under this part shall be subject to the right of the Secretary to establish trails, roads and other rights-of-way including improvements thereupon or through the premises, and the right to use same by the public. No interference shall be permitted with the continued use of all existing roads, trails and other rights-of-way and improvements thereon.
