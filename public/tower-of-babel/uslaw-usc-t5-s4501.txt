5 U.S.C. § 4501 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:5:4501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4501
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 45 (Incentive Awards); Subchapter I (Awards For Superior Accomplishments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter—
(1)
“agency” means—
(A)
an Executive agency;
(B)
the Library of Congress;
(C)
the Office of the Architect of the Capitol;
(D)
the Botanic Garden;
(E)
the Government Publishing Office;
(F)
the government of the District of Columbia; and
(G)
the United States Sentencing Commission;
but does not include—
(i)
the Tennessee Valley Authority; or
(ii)
the Central Bank for Cooperatives;
(2)
“employee” means—
(A)
an employee as defined by section 2105; and
(B)
an individual employed by the government of the District of Columbia; and
(3)
“Government” means the Government of the United States and the government of the District of Columbia.
