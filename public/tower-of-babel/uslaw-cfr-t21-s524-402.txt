21 C.F.R. § 524.402 — Chlorhexidine.
Citation: us:cfr:21:524.402

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-524.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 524 (Ophthalmic And Topical Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each gram of ointment contains 10 milligrams chlorhexidine acetate.
(b) Sponsors. See Nos. 054771 and 058829 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use in dogs, cats, and horses—(1) Indications for use. For use as a topical antiseptic ointment for surface wounds.
(2) Limitations. Do not use in horses intended for human consumption.
