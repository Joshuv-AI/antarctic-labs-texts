Thomason-Type Model Structures on Simplicial Complexes and Graphs
Emilio Minichiello

Abstract
. In this paper we show that the Matsushita model structure on loop graphs, which is right-transferred from the Kan-Quillen model structure on simplicial sets, factors through two other right-transferred model structures on simplicial complexes and reflexive graphs. We show that each Quillen adjunction between these right-transferred model categories is a Quillen equivalence. These model structures are analogous to the Thomason model structure on small categories, and we prove that they are all cofibrantly generated and proper. Furthermore we show that all cofibrant simplicial complexes are flag complexes, and all forests are cofibrant.

Definition 2.23

We say that two maps

(3)

Definition 2.39

Given a simplicial complex

In a simplex

Given a simplicial complex

Lemma 2.41

If

Proof.

First let us show that if

Lemma 6.1

n

and proves

We let

U:\mathbf{oCpx}\to\mathbf{Cpx}

denote the forgetful functor. We note that

\text{Re}\cong U\circ\text{oRe}.

Lemma A.14
.

The forgetful functor
U:\mathbf{oCpx}\to\mathbf{Cpx}
preserves colimits.

Proof.

Let
K_{\leq}:I\to\mathbf{oCpx}
be a diagram of ordered simplicial complexes and let
K=U\circ K_{\leq}
. Then

\displaystyle U(\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,K_{\leq}(i))

\displaystyle\cong U(\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,\text{oRe}\,\text{oSing}(K_{\leq}(i)))

\displaystyle\cong U\,\text{oRe}(\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,\text{oSing}(K_{\leq}(i)))

\displaystyle\cong\text{Re}(\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,\text{oSing}(K_{\leq}(i)))

\displaystyle\cong\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,\text{Re}\,\text{oSing}(K_{\leq}(i))

\displaystyle\cong\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,K(i)

\displaystyle\cong\underset{i\in I}{\text{colim}}\,U(K_{\leq}(i)).

∎
