20 C.F.R. § 641.370 — May a State incorporate its 4-year plan for SCSEP into a Combined State Plan under WIOA?
Citation: us:cfr:20:641.370
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-641.370
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 641 (Provisions Governing The Senior Community Service Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A State may include its 4-year plan for SCSEP in its WIOA Combined State Plan according to the requirements in 20 CFR 676.140 through 676.145. For a State that obtains approval of that Combined State Plan under 20 CFR 676.143, the requirements of sec. 103 of WIOA and 20 CFR part 676 will apply in lieu of sec. 503(a) of the OAA and this subpart, and any reference in this part to a “State Plan” will be considered to be a reference to that Combined State Plan.
