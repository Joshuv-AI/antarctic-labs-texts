11 U.S.C. § 1512 — Participation of a foreign representative in a case under this title
Citation: us:usc:11:1512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1512
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter II (Access Of Foreign Representatives And Creditors To The Court). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon recognition of a foreign proceeding, the foreign representative in the recognized proceeding is entitled to participate as a party in interest in a case regarding the debtor under this title.
