45 C.F.R. § 91.16 — Affirmative action by recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:45:91.16
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-91.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 91 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Hhs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Even in the absence of a finding of discrimination, a recipient may take affirmative action to overcome the effects of conditions that resulted in limited participation in the recipient's program or activity on the basis of age.
