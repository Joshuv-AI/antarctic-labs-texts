42 C.F.R. § 442.110 — Certification period for ICF/IID with standard-level deficiencies.
Citation: us:cfr:42:442.110
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-442.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 442 (Standards For Payment To Nursing Facilities And Intermediate Care Facilities For Individuals With Intellectual Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Facilities with standard-level deficiencies may be certified under § 442.101 with a condition that the certification will continue if either of the following applies:
(a) The survey agency finds that all deficiencies have been satisfactorily corrected.
(b) The survey agency finds that the facility has made substantial progress in correcting the deficiencies and has a new plan of correction that is acceptable.
