46 C.F.R. § 148.9 — Right of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.9
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by enforcement of this part by or on behalf of the Coast Guard may appeal the decision or action under Subpart 1.03 of this chapter.
