On Projective representations of special p p -groups
Sumana Hatui

Abstract
. Let G G be a special p p -group. If G G is of rank two, or G G is of maximum rank with | G p | ≤ p |G^{p}|\leq p , then we describe the complex irreducible projective representations of G G .

1.
Introduction and main results

The theory of projective representations was first studied by Schur in
[
19
,
20
,
21
]
. A projective representation is a homomorphism from a group into the projective general linear groups. Such representations arise naturally in the study of ordinary representations of groups and have significant connections to several areas, such as topology, Lie theory, and mathematical physics.
By definition, every ordinary representation of a group is projective, but the converse need not be true. Detailed treatment of this theory can be found in
[
11
]
. We know that the irreducible ordinary representations of abelian groups are one dimensional, which is not valid in the case of projective representations. So the difficulty of the projective representations starts here, and it is not fully understood yet for the abelian groups. For some special cases, it has been studied in
[
14
]
,
[
15
]
,
[
7
]
.

The projective representations of Dihedral groups are also well known in the literature; see
[
11
, Theorem 7.3, page 126]
. For the symmetric groups, the projective representations were initially studied by Schur himself in
[
21
]
and continued by several authors in
[
13
,
16
,
17
,
23
]
. Projective representations of discrete Heisenberg groups over cyclic rings and over rings of order
p^{2}
are described in
[
5
,
4
]
.
Continuing this line of investigation, we study the complex irreducible projective representations of special
p
-groups
G
for the following cases:

(i)
G
is of rank
2
.

(ii)
G
is of maximum rank having
G^{p}=1
or
G^{p}\cong\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
.

A group
G
is called a
special
p
-group
of rank
k
if
G
is a
p
-group of order
p^{n}
having the commutator subgroup
G^{\prime}
is equal to the center
Z(G)
and
G/G^{\prime}
is elementary abelian of order
p^{n-k}
.
Observe that in this case
G^{\prime}\cong(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{k}
,
G^{p}\subseteq G^{\prime}
and
p\leq|G^{\prime}|\leq p^{\frac{1}{2}d(d-1)}
for
d=n-k
.
Special
p
-groups with minimum rank are the extra-special
p
-groups, and their projective representations are described in
[
5
, Corollary 1.5]
. In this article, we consider the special
p
-groups having rank
2
or having maximum rank.

Here we fix some notations before stating our main results.
For a group
G
,
G^{p}
denotes the subgroup generated by the elements
x^{p},x\in G
.
A group
G
is said to be
capable
if there exists a group
H
such that
G\cong H/Z(H)
.
The epicenter of
G
is denoted by
Z^{*}(G)
, which is the smallest central subgroup of
G
such that
G/Z^{*}(G)
is capable.
The second cohomology group
{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
is called the Schur multiplier of
G
.
The set
Z^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
consists of all
2
-cocycles of
G
. For an element
\alpha\in Z^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
, the corresponding element of
{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
will be denoted by
[\alpha]
.
For each
2
-cocycle
\alpha
of
G
,
\mathrm{Irr}^{\alpha}(G)
denotes the set of all inequivalent irreducible complex
\alpha
-representations of
G
.
We define

\text{Proj}(G):=\bigcup_{\{[\alpha]\in{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})\}}\mathrm{Irr}^{\alpha}(G).

The set of all inequivalent irreducible complex ordinary representations of
G
is denoted by
\mathrm{Irr}(G)
.
For
m\geq 1
,
ES_{p}(p^{2m+1})
and
ES_{p^{2}}(p^{2m+1})
denotes the extra-special
p
-groups of order
p^{2m+1}
of exponent
p
and of exponent
p^{2}
respectively. In this article, we always assume
p
is an odd prime.

When we write a presentation of a group, we assume all the commutators
[x,y]
for generators
x,y
, which are not explicitly stated in the presentation, are identity.

Definition 1.1
.

A finite group
G^{*}
is called a representation group of
G
if there is a subgroup
Z\subseteq(G^{*})^{\prime}\cap Z(G^{*})
such that
Z\cong{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
and
G^{*}/Z\cong G
.

It follows by
[
5
, Corollary 3.3]
that there is a bijective correspondence between the sets Proj
(G)
and
\mathrm{Irr}(G^{*})
.

Now we are ready to state our main results.

1.1.
Special
p
-groups of rank
2

Let
G
be a special
p
-group of rank
2
.
An explicit description of the Schur multiplier of
G
and a classification of capable groups among
G
are given in
[
2
]
.
Now, the following result describes the projective representations of

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
G
be a special
p
-group of rank
2
minimally generated by
d

. Then the following holds:

(1)

Suppose

and

is not capable, then there is a bijective correspondence between
\mathrm{Proj}(G)
and
\mathrm{Proj}\big((\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{d}\big)
.

(2)

If
G^{p}=1
with
G
is not capable, or
G^{p}\cong\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
, then for some central subgroup

of order
p

G/Z\cong ES_{p}(p^{2m+1})\times(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{(d-2m)}~\text{ for some }m\geq 1.

(i) If
m\geq 2
, then there is a bijective correspondence between
\mathrm{Proj}(G)
and
\mathrm{Proj}((\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{d})
.

(ii) If

, then there is a bijective correspondence between
\mathrm{Proj}(G)
and
\mathrm{Irr}(G^{*})
, where

\displaystyle G^{*}=\langle x_{1},x_{2},y_{r},1\leq r\leq d-2\mid[x_{1},x_{2}]=z,[z,x_{k}]=z_{k},[y_{r},x_{k}]=w_{rk},[y_{i},y_{j}]=z_{ij},

\displaystyle\hskip 113.81102ptx_{k}^{p}=y_{r}^{p}=z^{p}=1,1\leq i<j\leq d-2,1\leq k\leq 2\rangle.

By
[
6
, Proposition 3]
, we have the following.

Corollary 1.3
.

If
G
is a special
p
-group of rank
2
of order
p^{n},n\geq 8
and
G^{p}=G^{\prime}
, then there is a bijective correspondence between
\mathrm{Proj}(G)
and
\mathrm{Proj}\big((\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{d}\big)
.

1.2.
Special
p
-groups of maximum rank

Let
G
be a special
p
-group of maximum rank, minimally generated by
d
elements and
d\geq 3
. The Schur multiplier of these groups are described in
[
18
]
.
We write
G/G^{\prime}=\langle x_{1}G^{\prime},x_{2}G^{\prime},\cdots,x_{d}G^{\prime}\rangle
and
[x_{i},x_{j}]=y_{ij},~1\leq i<j\leq d

G^{\prime}\cong\bigoplus_{1\leq i<j\leq d}\langle y_{ij}\rangle\cong(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{\frac{1}{2}d(d-1)},

and every element of

can be written uniquely as

\prod_{i=1}^{d}x_{i}^{r_{i}}\prod_{1\leq j<k\leq d}y_{jk}^{s_{jk}}~\text{ for }1\leq r_{i},s_{jk}<p.

We consider the two cases
G^{p}=1
and
G^{p}\cong\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
separately.

1.2.1.
G^{p}=1

In this case, we study projective representations of
G
by describing
2
-cocycles and a representation group of
G

Theorem 1.4
.

Let

be a special

-group of maximum rank having exponent

be minimally generated by

. Then the following holds.

(1)

Every
2
-cocycle of

is cohomologous to a cocycle of the form

\displaystyle\alpha\Big(\prod_{i=1}^{d}x_{i}^{r_{i}}\prod_{1\leq j<k\leq d}y_{jk}^{s_{jk}},\prod_{i=1}^{d}x_{i}^{r^{\prime}_{i}}\prod_{1\leq j<k\leq d}y_{jk}^{s^{\prime}_{jk}}\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\prod_{1\leq i<j<k\leq d}\mu_{ijk}^{r_{i}s^{\prime}_{jk}-r_{k}s^{\prime}_{ij}}\prod_{1\leq i<j<k\leq d}\mu_{jik}^{r_{j}s^{\prime}_{ik}+r_{k}s^{\prime}_{ij}}

\displaystyle\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq d}\mu_{iij}^{r_{i}s^{\prime}_{ij}}\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq d}\mu_{jij}^{r_{j}s^{\prime}_{ij}}

for
\mu_{ijk}\in\mathbb{C}^{\times}
such that
\mu_{ijk}^{p}=1
.

(2)

The group

\displaystyle H_{d}^{*}=\langle x_{i},1\leq i\leq d\mid[x_{j},x_{k}]=y_{jk},[x_{i},y_{jk}]=z_{ijk},z_{rmn}=z_{nmr}z_{mnr}^{-1},x_{i}^{p}=1,y_{jk}^{p}=1,

\displaystyle 1\leq m<n<r\leq d,1\leq j<k\leq d\rangle

1.2.2.
G^{p}\cong\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}

Now we state our main result for the special

-groups of maximum rank with
