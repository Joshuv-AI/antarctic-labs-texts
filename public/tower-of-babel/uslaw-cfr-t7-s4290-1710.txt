7 C.F.R. § 4290.1710 — Agency's authority to collect or compromise claims.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.1710
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.1710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency may, upon such conditions and for such consideration as it deems reasonable, collect or compromise all claims relating to obligations it holds or has guaranteed, and all legal or equitable rights accruing to it.
