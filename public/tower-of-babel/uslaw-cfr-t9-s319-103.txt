9 C.F.R. § 319.103 — Cured beef tongue.
Citation: us:cfr:9:319.103
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-319.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 319 (Definitions And Standards Of Identity Or Composition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In preparing “Cured Beef Tongue,” the application of curing solution to the fresh beef tongue shall not result in an increase in the weight of the cured beef tongue of more than 10 percent over the weight of the fresh uncured beef tongue.
