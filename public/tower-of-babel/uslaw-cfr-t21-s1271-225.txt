21 C.F.R. § 1271.225 — Process changes.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1271.225
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1271.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1271 (Human Cells, Tissues, And Cellular And Tissue-Based Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any change to a process must be verified or validated in accordance with § 1271.230, to ensure that the change does not create an adverse impact elsewhere in the operation, and must be approved before implementation by a responsible person with appropriate knowledge and background. You must communicate approved changes to the appropriate personnel in a timely manner.
