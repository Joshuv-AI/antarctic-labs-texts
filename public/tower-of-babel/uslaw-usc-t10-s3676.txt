10 U.S.C. § 3676 — Guidelines
Citation: us:usc:10:3676
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3676
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart C (Contracting Methods And Contract Types); Chapter 257 (Contracts For Long-Term Lease Or Charter Of Vessels, Aircraft, And Combat Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget and the Secretary of the Treasury shall jointly issue guidelines for determining under what circumstances the Department of Defense may use lease or charter arrangements for aircraft, naval vessels, and combat vehicles rather than directly procuring such aircraft, vessels, and combat vehicles.
