42 C.F.R. § 59.9 — For what purpose may grant funds be used?
Citation: us:cfr:42:59.9
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-59.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 59 (Grants For Family Planning Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any funds granted under this subpart shall be expended solely for the purpose for which the funds were granted in accordance with the approved application and budget, the regulations of this subpart, the terms and conditions of the award, and the applicable cost principles prescribed in 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, subpart E.
