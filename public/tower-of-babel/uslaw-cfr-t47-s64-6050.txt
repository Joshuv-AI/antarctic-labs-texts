47 C.F.R. § 64.6050 — Billing-related call blocking.
Citation: us:cfr:47:64.6050
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-64.6050
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 64 (Miscellaneous Rules Relating To Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Provider shall prohibit or prevent completion of a Collect Calling IPCS communication or decline to establish or otherwise degrade any Collect Calling IPCS communication solely for the reason that it lacks a billing relationship with the called party's communications service provider unless the Provider offers Debit Calling, Prepaid Calling, or Prepaid Collect Calling for IPCS communications.
