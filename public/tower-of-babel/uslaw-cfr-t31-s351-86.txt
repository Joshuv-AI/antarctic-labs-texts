31 C.F.R. § 351.86 — What is the role of Federal Reserve Banks and Branches?
Citation: us:cfr:31:351.86
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 351 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Federal Reserve Banks and Branches are fiscal agents of the United States. They are authorized to perform such services as we may request of them, in connection with the issue, servicing and redemption of Series EE bonds.
