42 C.F.R. § 457.353 — Monitoring and evaluation of screening process.
Citation: us:cfr:42:457.353
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-457.353
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 457 (Allotments And Grants To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States must establish a mechanism and monitor to evaluate the screen and enroll process described at § 457.350 of this subpart to ensure that children who are:
(a) Screened as potentially eligible for other insurance affordability programs are enrolled in such programs, if eligible; or
(b) Determined ineligible for other insurance affordability programs are enrolled in CHIP, if eligible.
