32 C.F.R. § 1703.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1703.1
Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1703.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1703 (Production Of Odni Information Or Material In Proceedings Before Federal, State, Local Or Other Government Entity Of Competent Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the policy and procedures with respect to the production or disclosure of material contained in the files of the ODNI, information relating to or based upon material contained in the files of the ODNI, and information acquired by any person while such person was an employee of the ODNI as part of the performance of that person's official duties or because of that person's association with the ODNI.
