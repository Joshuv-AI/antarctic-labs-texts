Rescission of Amended Final Results

    On July 11, 2007, the Department of Commerce (the Department) 
published amended final results of the administrative review of the 
antidumping duty order on ball bearings and parts thereof from Japan 
for the period May 1, 2000, through April 30, 2001. See Ball Bearings 
and Parts Thereof from Japan: Amended Final Results of Antidumping Duty 
Administrative Review, 72 FR 37702 (July 11, 2007) (Amended Final 
Results). We published the Amended Final Results, which reflected a 
court decision, mistakenly before that decision became final and 
conclusive. Therefore, the Department is rescinding those Amended Final 
Results.
