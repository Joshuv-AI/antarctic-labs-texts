47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2205; MM Docket No. 00-169; RM-9953]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Oswego and Granby, NY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 65 FR 57800 
(September 26, 2000) this document reallots Channel 288A from Oswego to 
Granby, New York and provides Granby with its first local aural 
transmission service. The coordinates for Channel 288A at Granby are 
43-17-44 North Latitude and 76-26-16 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective November 5, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
