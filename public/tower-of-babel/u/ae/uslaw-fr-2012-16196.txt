DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 7940]


Determination Under the Foreign Assistance Act and the Department 
of State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs Appropriations Acts

    Pursuant to Section 654(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, 
as amended (FAA), notice is hereby given that the Deputy Secretary of 
State has made a determination pursuant to Section 620H of the FAA, and 
Section 7021 of the Department of State, Foreign Operations, and 
Related Programs Appropriations, 2012 (Div. I, Pub. L. 112-74), and 
similar provisions in prior-year appropriations acts, and has concluded 
that publication of the determination would be harmful to the national 
security of the United States.
    This notice shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: June 22, 2012.
Vann H. Van Diepen,
Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary of State.
