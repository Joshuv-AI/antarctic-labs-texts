DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans, Notice of Meeting

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. ch. 10, that the Advisory 
Committee on Minority Veterans will meet virtually on August 6, 2025. 
The meeting sessions will begin, and end as follows:

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
               Date                          Time                   Location                  Open session
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
August 6, 2025....................  11:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m.,  Dial in by phone: +1 872-   Yes.
                                     Eastern Standard       701-0185. Phone
                                     Time (EST).            conference ID: 496 198
                                                            177#.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    This meeting is open to the public.
    The Committee on Minority Veterans advises the Secretary of 
Veterans Affairs with respect to the administration of benefits by the 
Department for Veterans who are minority group members, by reviewing 
reports and studies on compensation, health care, rehabilitation, 
outreach, and other benefits and services administered by the 
Department.
    On August 6, 2025, the Committee will receive briefings and updates 
from the Advisory Committee Management Office, Veterans Affairs Deputy 
Chief of Staff, Office of Ethics, Advisory Committee on United States 
Outlying Areas and Freely Associated States, Advisory Committee on 
Tribal and Indians Affairs and Office of Women's Health. The Committee 
will receive public comments from 2:35 p.m. to 3:15 p.m. EST. 
Individuals who speak are invited to submit a 1-2-page summary of their 
comments at the time of the meeting for inclusion in the official 
meeting record. Members of the public may also submit written 
statements for the Committee's review to Mr. Dwayne E. Campbell, 
Department of Veterans Affairs, Center for Minority Veterans (00M), 810 
Vermont Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20420, or email at 
[email protected]. Any member of the public seeking additional 
information should contact Mr. Campbell or Mr. Ronald Sagudan at (202) 
461-6191, or by fax at (202) 273-7092.

    Dated: May 28, 2025.
Jelessa M. Burney,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
