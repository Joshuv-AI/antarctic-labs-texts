DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board Task Force on Logistics Modernization

ACTION: Notice of Advisory Committee Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Defense Science Board Task Force on Logistics 
Modernization will meet in open session on September 25-26, 1995 at the 
Institute for Defense Analyses (IDA), 1801 N. Beauregard Street, 
Alexandria, Virginia.
    The mission of the Defense Science Board is to advise the Secretary 
of 

[[Page 45706]]
Defense and the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition & Technology 
on scientific and technical matters as they affect the perceived needs 
of the Department of Defense.
    Persons interested in further information should call LTC Kerry M. 
Brown at (703) 697-7980.

    Dated: August 29, 1995.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
