SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    NOAA's Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite (GOES) 
Data Collection System (DCS) collects and transmits environmental data 
from remote platforms. NOAA allows other users access to any excess 
capacity on the system if they meet certain criteria, primarily that 
they are sponsored by another government agency and that no other 
adequate common carrier is available. NOAA needs a minimal amount of 
information from applicants to determine if their request for access 
meets the requirements as stated in 15 CFR Part 911(a)-(c).

II. Method of Collection

    Applicants prepare narrative applications. No forms are used.
