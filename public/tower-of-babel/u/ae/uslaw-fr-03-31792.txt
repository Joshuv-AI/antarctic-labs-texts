inal rule.

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense is removing various parts 
(organizational charters) from chapter I, Office of the Secretary of 
Defense. This administrative action removes obsolete information from 
the Code of Federal Regulations and notifies readers of the 
availability of the current version of the DoD documents.

DATES: This rule is effective December 29, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: L. Bynum or P. Toppings, (703) 601-
4722
