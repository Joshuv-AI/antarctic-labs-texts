DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the PBA Study Panel Chairs. The study results 
also will be briefed to the Chief of Staff of the Air Force (CSAF) 
during this period. Much of the discussion and work will be carried on 
at the Top Secret and SCI levels, and the studies will also be 
discussing substantial amounts of contractor-proprietary information. 
The meeting will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 
552b of Title 5, USC, specifically subparagraphs (c)(1) and (4) 
thereof.

DATES: 17-28 June 2002.

ADDRESSES: The Arnold and Mabel Beckman Center, Irvine, CA.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The Air Force Scientific Advisory 
Board Secretariat at (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
