Sony Electronics, Projection Television Picture Tube Division, 
Mt. Pleasant, PA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 7, 2004, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Sony Electronics, Projection 
Television Picture Tube Division, Mt. Pleasant, Pennsylvania.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-40,757A). Consequently, further investigation 
in this case would serve no purpose; and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 16th day of April, 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
