Pine Creek Mine, LLC; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal of 
Petition for Declaratory Order

    On February 12, 2016, Pine Creek Mine, LLC (PCM) filed an 
application for an original license to construct, operate, and maintain 
the proposed 1.5-megawatt Pine Creek Mine Tunnel Hydroelectric Project 
in Inyo County, California. On June 4, 2020, PCM filed a petition for 
an order declaring that the California State Water Resources Control 
Board waived its authority to issue water quality certification for the 
project under Section 401 of the Clean Water Act, 33 U.S.C. 1341(a)(1). 
On September 29, 2020, PCM filed a letter notifying the Commission that 
it was withdrawing its petition for declaratory order.
    No motion in opposition to the notice of withdrawal has been filed, 
and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the withdrawal. 
Accordingly, pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure,\1\ withdrawal of the petition became effective 
on October 14, 2020, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
