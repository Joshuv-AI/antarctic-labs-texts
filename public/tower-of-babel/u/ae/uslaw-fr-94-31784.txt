SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2748; Amendment #3]

 

Texas; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    The above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended, effective 
December 9, 1994, to extend the deadline for filing applications for 
physical damages to January 18, 1995.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for economic injury is July 19, 1995.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: December 20, 1994.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
