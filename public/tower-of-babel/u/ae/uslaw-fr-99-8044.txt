FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 98-22; RM-9183]


Radio Broadcasting Services; DeRuyter and Chittenango, NY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Cram Communications, LLC, 
dismisses its request to reallot Channel 286B from DeRuyter to 
Chittenango, NY, and modify its license for Station WVOA to specify 
Chittenango as its community of license. See 63 FR 11400, March 9, 
1998. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Report and Order, MM Docket No. 98-22, adopted March 17, 1999, and 
released March 26, 1999. The full text of this Commission decision is 
available for inspection and copying during normal business hours in 
the FCC Reference Center (Room 239), 1919 M Street, NW., Washington,
