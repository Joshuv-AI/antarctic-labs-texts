NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Business and Operations Advisory Committee

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Business and Operations Advisory Committee (9556).
    Date/Time: April 26, 2001; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; April 27, 2001; 
8:30 a.m. to 3 p.m.

[[Page 16300]]

    Place: National Science Foundation, 4121 Wilson Boulevard, 
Arlington, VA (Stafford II Conference Center, Room 555).
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Louise McIntire, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230 (703) 292-8200.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice concerning issues related 
to the oversight, integrity, development and enhancement of NSF's 
business operations.
    Agenda:
April 26, 2001
    AM: Introductions: Briefings on CIO and CFO functions
    PM: Discussion: Strategic planning for business and operations
April 27, 2001
    AM: Discussion: Planning for an NSF Academy; meeting with NSF 
Director
    PM: Discussion: Risk assessment planning; wrap up

    Dated: March 20, 2001.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
