Huckleberry Land Exchange With Weyerhaeuser Company, Mt. Baker-

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Revised notice of intent to prepare an environmental impact 
statement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On July 14, 1995, a notice of intent to prepare an 
environmental impact statement (EIS) for the

[[Page 55785]]

Huckleberry Land Exchange with Weyerhaeuser Company was published in 
the Federal Register (60 FR 36257). This notice announced the 
responsible official as Wendy M. Herrett, Director of Recreation, 
Lands, and Mineral Resources, Pacific Northwest Region. The 
responsibility for this Huckleberry Land Exchange EIS and decision has 
been delegated to the Forest Supervisor of the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie 
National Forest.
