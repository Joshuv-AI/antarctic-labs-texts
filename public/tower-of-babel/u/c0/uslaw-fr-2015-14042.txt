National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, July 7, 2015, 11:00 a.m. to 
July 08, 2015, 06:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 2W032/034, Rockville, MD, 20850 which

July 30-31, 2015 from 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to 
the public.
