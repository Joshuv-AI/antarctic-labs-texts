UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE BOARD OF GOVERNORS


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIMES AND DATES: 1 p.m., Monday, June 3, 2002; 8:30 a.m., Tuesday, June 
4, 2002.

PLACE: Washington, DC at U.S. Postal Service Headquarters, 475 L'Enfant 
Plaza, SW., in the Benjamin Franklin Room.

STATUS: June 3--1 p.m. (Closed); June 4-8:30 a.m. (Open)

1. Financial Performance.
2. Strategic Planning.
3. Personnel Matters and Compensation Issues.
Tuesday, June 4--8:30 a.m. (Open)
1. Minutes of the Previous Meeting, May 6-7, 2002.
2. Remarks of the Postmaster General and CEO.
3. Implementation of June 30 Price Changes.
4. Automated Flat Sorting Machine 100 Achievements.
Tuesday, June 4--8:30 a.m. (Open) [continued]
5. Making Information Technology Accessible for Persons with

    a. Northern New Jersey, Teterboro, Processing and Distribution 
Center.
7. Tentative Agenda for the July 1-2, 2002, meeting in Anchorage, 
Alaska.

20260-1000. Telephone (202) 268-4800.
