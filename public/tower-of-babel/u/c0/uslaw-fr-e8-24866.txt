Premier Manufacturing Support Services, Shreveport, LA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 18, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed by the State Workforce Office on behalf of workers of 
Premier Manufacturing Support Services, Shreveport, Louisiana.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, (TA-W-63,793) which expires on September 27, 2010. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 9th day of October, 2008.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
