Colonial Metals Co., Columbia, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 9, 2004, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Colonial 
Metals Company, Columbia, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 17th day of February 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
