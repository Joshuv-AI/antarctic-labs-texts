DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Air Force


Intent To Grant an Exclusive Patent License

    Pursuant to the provisions of Part 404 of Title 37, Code of Federal 
Regulations, which implements Public Law 96-517, the Department of the 
Air Force announces its intention to grant Sherwood Medical Center, a 
Delaware Corporation, an exclusive license under United States Patent 
No. 5,213,099 filed in the name of Lloyd D. Tripp, Jr. for an ``Ear 
Canal Pulse/Oxygen Saturation Measuring Device''.
    The license described above will be granted unless an objection 
thereto, together with a request for an opportunity to be heard, if 
desired, is received in writing by the addressee set forth below within 
sixty (60) days from the date of publication of this Notice. Copies of 
the patent may be obtained, on request, from the same addressee.

[[Page 4733]]

    All communications concerning this Notice should be sent to Mr. 
Randy Heald, Patent Attorney, Secretary of the Air Force, Office of the 
General Counsel, SAF/GCQ, 1501 Wilson Blvd., Suite 805, Arlington VA 
22209-2403, Telephone No: (703) 696-9037.
Carolyn A. Lunsford,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
