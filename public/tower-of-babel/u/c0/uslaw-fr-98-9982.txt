meetings:

and Stroke Special Emphasis Panel (Telephone Conference Call).
    Date: April 30, 1998.
    Time: 1:00 p.m.
    Place: National Institutes of Health, 7550 Wisconsin Avenue, 
Room 9C10, Bethesda, MD 20892.
    Contact Person: Dr. Paul Sheehy, Mr. Phillip Wiethorn,

    Date: May 18, 1998.
    Time: 3:30 p.m.
    Place: National Institutes of Health, 7550 Wisconsin Avenue, 
Room 9C10, Bethesda, MD 20892.
    Contact Person: Dr. Katherine Woodbury, Scientific Review 
Administrator, Scientific Review Branch, NINDS, National Institutes

(301) 496-9223.
    Purpose/Agenda: To evaluate one grant application.

set forth in secs. 552b(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C.
