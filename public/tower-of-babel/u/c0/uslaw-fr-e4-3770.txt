Employment And Training Administration

[TA-W-56,093]


Hamilton Sundstrand, Grand Junction, CO; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 26, 2004 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Hamilton 
Sundstrand, Grand Junction, Colorado.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 6th day of December, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
