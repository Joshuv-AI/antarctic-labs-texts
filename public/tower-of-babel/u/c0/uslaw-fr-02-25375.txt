DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


South Pyramid Timber Sales, Willamette National Forest, Linn 
County, OR

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: August 2, 1999, a Notice of Intent (NOI) to prepare an 
environmental impact statement for the South Pyramid Timber Sales on 
the Sweet Home Ranger District of the Willamette National Forest, was 
published in the Federal Register (64 FR 41912). The 1999 NOI is hereby 
rescinded.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Brian McGinley, Resource Planner, 
Sweet Home Ranger District, 3225 Highway 22, Sweet Home, Oregon 97386; 
phone 541-367-5168.
