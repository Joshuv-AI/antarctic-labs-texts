DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army, Corps of Engineers


Grant of Partially Exclusive License

AGENCY: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, DoD.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Reference the previous Federal Register notice published on 
Thursday, December 7, 2000 (Vol 65, No. 236), page 76613, the notice 
inadvertently announced information under the Supplementary Information 
paragraph.
    On page 76613, column 2, line 44, the information should indicate 
``aqueous sludge dewatering'' vice ``concrete armor unit''.
