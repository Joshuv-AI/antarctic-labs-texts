DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 3370]


Culturally Significant Objects Imported for Exhibition 
Determinations: ``Painting Revolution: Kandinsky, Malevich and the 
Russian Avant-Garde''

AGENCY: United States Department of State.

ACTION: Amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On January 12, 2000, Notice was published on page 1940 of the 
Federal Register (Volume 65, Number 8) by the Department of State 
pursuant to Pub. L 89-259 relating to the exhibit ``Painting 
Revolution: Kandinsky, Malevich and the Russian Avant-Garde.'' The 
referenced Notice is amended as follows. After ``March 31, 2001,'' 
insert the following additional venue: ``and at the Bass Museum in 
Miami Beach, Florida from on or about July 9, 2001, is in the national 
interest.''

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For further information, including a 
list of exhibit objects, contact Jacqueline Caldwell, Attorney-Adviser, 
Office of the Legal Adviser, U.S. Department of State (telephone: 202/
619-6982). The address is U.S. Department of State, SA-44, 301 4th 
Street, S.W., Room 700, Washington, DC 20547-0001.

    Dated: July 18, 2000.
Helena Kane Finn,
Acting Assistant Secretary for Educational and Cultural Affairs, 
Department of State.
