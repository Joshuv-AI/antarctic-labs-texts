DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Indian Affairs


Indian Gaming

AGENCY: Bureau of Indian Affairs, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of approved amendment to Tribal-State Compact.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Section 11 of the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act of 
1988, Pub. L. 100-497, 25 U.S.C. 2710, the Secretary of the Interior 
shall publish, in the Federal Register, notice of approved Compacts for 
the purpose of engaging in Class III (casino) gambling on Indian 
reservations. The Assistant Secretary--Indian Affairs, Department of 
the Interior, through his delegated authority, has approved Amendment 
II to the Amended Gaming Compact Between the Sisseton-Wahpeton Sioux 
Tribe and the State of South Dakota, which was executed on January 13, 
1998.

DATES: This action is effective May 8, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy J. Pierskalla, Acting Director, 
Indian Gaming Management Staff, Bureau of Indian Affairs,Washington, 
D.C. 20240, (202) 219-4068.
