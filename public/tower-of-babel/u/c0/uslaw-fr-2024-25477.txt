COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the U.S. Virgin Islands Advisory 
Committee to the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights; Revision to Meeting 
Date and Time

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Revision to meeting date and time.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register on October 7, 2024, regarding the November 12th 
business meeting of the U.S. Virgin Islands Advisory Committee. The 
notice can be found in FR Doc. 2024-23121, located in the second and 
third columns of page 81039 and continuing into the first column of 
page 81040. This document provides the correct date and time for the 
Committee's next meeting.

Atlantic Time.
