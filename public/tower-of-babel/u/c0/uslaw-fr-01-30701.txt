Nevada Power Company Complainant, v. Morgan Stanley Capital 
Group, Inc. Respondent; Notice of Complaint

December 6, 2001.
    Take notice that on December 4, 2001, Nevada Power Company (NPC) 
filed a complaint with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
(Commission) requesting that the Commission mitigate unjust and 
unreasonable prices in sales contracts between NPC and Morgan Stanley 
Capital Group, Inc. (Morgan Stanley) entered into in the first half of 
2001 for delivery after January 1, 2002.
    NPC requests that the Commission set a refund effective date of 60 
days from the date of filing of its complaint.
    Copies of NPC's filing were served on Morgan Stanley and the Public 
Utilities Commission of Nevada. NPC has requested privileged treatment 
of certain information in the complaint and has filed privileged and 
public copies of the complaint, a request for privileged treatment, and 
a protective agreement.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest this filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions or 
protests must be filed on or before December 24, 2001. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Answers to the complaint shall also be due on or before 
December 24, 2001. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection. This filing may 
also be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' 
link, select ``Docket#'' and follow the instructions (call 202-208-2222 
for assistance). Comments, protests and interventions may be filed 
electronically via the Internet in lieu of paper. See, 18 CFR 
385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the Commission's web site 
under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
