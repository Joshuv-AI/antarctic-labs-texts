DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Oregon; Public Lands Closure

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Medford District, Ashland Resource 
Area.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The notice published on page 4788 in the issue of Thursday, 
February 8, 1996, closing to all public use the Keno Access Road and 
the Howard Prairie Hook Up Road in Jackson County, Oregon is hereby 
rescinded.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Dave Jones, District Manager, Medford District Office, at (541) 770-
2200.

    Dated: August 1, 1996.
David A. Jones,
District Manager.
