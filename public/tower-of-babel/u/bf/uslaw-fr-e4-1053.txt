Tanya Creations, Inc., East Providence, RI; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 2, 2004, in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Tanya Creations, 
Inc., East Providence, Rhode Island.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of April, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
