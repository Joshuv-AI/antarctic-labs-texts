NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management; Renewals

    The NSF management official having responsibility for the U.S. 
National Assessment Synthesis Team (#5219) has determined that renewing 
for an additional eight months is necessary and in the public interest 
in connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the Director, 
National Science Foundation (NSF), by 42 U.S.C. 1861 et seq. This 
determination follows consultation with the Committee Management 
Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Authority for this Committee will expire on September 30, 2000, 
unless renewed. For more information, please contact Karen York, NSF, 
at (703) 306-1182.

    Dated: January 24, 2000.
Karen J. York,
Committee Management Officer.
