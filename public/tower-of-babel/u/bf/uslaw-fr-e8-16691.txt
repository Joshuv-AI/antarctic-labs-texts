FFP Ohio River 3, LLC, FFP Ohio River 4, LLC; Notice of 
Preliminary Permit Applications Accepted for Filing and Soliciting 
Comment, Motions To Intervene, and Competing Applications

July 15, 2008.
    On March 25, 2008, FFP Ohio River 3, LLC and FFP Ohio River 4, LLC 
each filed an application, pursuant to section 4(f) of the Federal 
Power Act, proposing to study the feasibility of the Ohio River 3 and 
Ohio River 4 Projects, to be located on the Ohio River in Vanderburgh 
and Warrick Counties, Indiana and Henderson County, Kentucky.
    The proposed Ohio River 3 Project consists of: (1) 3,840 proposed 
20 kilowatt Free Flow generating units having a total installed 
capacity of 76.8 megawatts, (2) a proposed transmission line, and (3) 
appurtenant facilities. The FFP Ohio River 3, LLC, project would have 
an average annual generation of 336.38 gigawatt-hours and be sold to a 
local utility.
    The proposed Ohio River 4 Project consists of: (1) 1,860 proposed 
20 kilowatt Free Flow generating units having a total installed 
capacity of 37.2 megawatts, (2) a proposed transmission line, and (3) 
appurtenant facilities. The FFP Ohio River 4, LLC, project would have 
an average annual generation of 162.94 gigawatt-hours and be sold to a 
local utility.
