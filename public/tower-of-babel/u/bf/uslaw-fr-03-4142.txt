FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement(s) Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement(s) under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties can 
review or obtain copies of agreements at the Washington, DC offices of 
the Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, NW., Room 940. Interested 
parties may submit comments on an agreement to the Secretary, Federal 
Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within 10 days of the date 
this notice appears in the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 011409-008
    Title: Transpacific Carrier Services, Inc. Agreement
    Parties: Westbound Transpacific Stabilization Agreement; 
Transpacific Space Utilization Agreement; Asia North America Eastbound 
Rate Agreement; Transpacific Stabilization Agreement;American President 
Lines, Ltd.; APL Moller-Maersk Sealand; China Shipping Container Lines 
Co., Ltd.; CMA CGM, S.A.; COSCO Container Lines Company, Ltd.; 
Evergreen Marine Corporation; Hanjin Shipping Co., Ltd.;Hapag-Lloyd 
Container Linie GmbH; Hyundai Merchant Marine Co., Ltd.; Kawasaki Kisen 
Kaisha, Ltd.;Mitsui O.S.K. Lines, Ltd.; Nippon Yusen Kaisha, Ltd.; 
Orient Overseas Container Line, Inc.; P&O Nedlloyd B.V.; P&O Nedlloyd 
Limited; and Yangming Lines
    Synopsis: The amendment adds China Shipping Container Lines Co., 
Ltd. as a party to the agreement.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
