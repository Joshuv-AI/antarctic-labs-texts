COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Additions to the Procurement List; Correction

    In the document appearing on page 40878, FR 98-20501, in the issue 
of July 31, 1998, in the second column, the service listed as 
Facilities Services Support, White Sands Missile Range, White Sands, 
New Mexico should read Facilities Services Support, High Energy Laser 
Systems Test Facility (HELSTF), White Sands Missile Range, New Mexico.
