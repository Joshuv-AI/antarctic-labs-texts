Sperian Protective Gloves, USA, Buffalo, NY; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    In accordance with Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
amended, an investigation was initiated on April 17, 2009 in response 
to a petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of 
Sperian Protective Gloves, USA, Buffalo, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 21st day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
