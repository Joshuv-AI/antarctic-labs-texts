SUMMARY: At the time the original Federal Register and local media 
announcements of our Notice of Intent were published (February 1, 1994, 
59 FR 4680) and revised (May 23, 1994 59 FR 26624) it was expected that 
the draft Eastside Environmental Impact Statement would be available by 
November 1994. It is now expected to have a draft Environmental Impact 
Statement available before the end of calendar year 1995.
