Johnston Industries, Inc., Dewitt, IA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on December

[[Page 7795]]

29, 2003 in response to a petition filed on behalf of workers at 
Johnston Industries, Inc., DeWitt, Iowa.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on January 15, 2004 (TA-W-53,723C) which remains 
in effect. Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of February 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
