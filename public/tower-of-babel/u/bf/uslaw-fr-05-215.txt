SUMMARY: The Coast Guard published a document in the Federal Register 
of December 27, 2004, requesting information about the current status 
of dry cargo operations on the Great Lakes. The document contained an 
incorrect ACTION caption.

U.S. Coast Guard, Environmental Standards Division, telephone: (202) 
267-0713, e-mail: [email protected].

(69 FR 77147), correct the ACTION caption to read:

ACTION: Notice of inquiry.
