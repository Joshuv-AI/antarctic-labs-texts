SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Reporting Requirements Submitted for OMB Review

AGENCY: Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Reporting Requirements Submitted for OMB Review; 
Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Small Business Administration published a document in the 
Federal Register of August 10, 2009, concerning Reporting and 
Recordkeeping Requirements. The document contained an incorrect title.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Curtis Rich, 202-205-7030.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of August 10, 2009, FR document E9-19013, 
Volume 74, Number 152, page 39991, under the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION 
``Title'' should read:

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
    Title: Information for Small Business Size Determination.

    Dated: August 10, 2009.
Curtis B. Rich,
Acting, Chief Administrative Information Branch.
