DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Notice of Opportunity for Public Comment To Dispose of 4.68 Acres 
of Airport Land at Houlton International Airport, Houlton, ME

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Request for public comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is being given that the FAA is considering a request 
from the Town of Houlton to dispose of 4.68 acres of land. The parcel 
is located within the airport industrial park as is not needed for 
aeronautical purposes. There is adequate developable area on the 
airport to meet the future twenty year need for projected activity. The 
airport will obtain fair market value for the disposal of the land and 
the income derived from this disposal will be placed in the airport's 
operation and maintenance funds for the facility.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before September 26, 2018.
