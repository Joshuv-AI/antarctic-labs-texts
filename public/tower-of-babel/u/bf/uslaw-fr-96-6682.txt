DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Meeting of the Semiconductor Technology Council

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under the provisions of Public Law 92-463, the ``Federal 
Advisory Committee Act,'' notice is hereby given that the Semiconductor

[[Page 11387]]

Technology Council will hold forth its fourth meeting. The Council's 
mission is to: link industry and national security needs to 
opportunities for cooperative investments, foster pre-competitive 
cooperation among industry, government and academia, recommend 
opportunities for new R&D efforts and potential to rationalize and 
align on-going industry and government investments. Part of the meeting 
will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 10(d) of the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, and pursuant to the appropriate 
provisions of Section 552b(c) (3) and (4), Title 5, U.S.C. There will 
be an open session from 11:15 to 11:45 a.m. for a discussion of the 
Advanced Research Projects Agency (ARPA) Advanced Lithography Project 
strategy. Registration is required prior to this session.

DATES: March 29, 1996.

ADDRESSES: Hyatt Regency, Room 411, 1325 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, 
VA 22209.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Dr. Lance Glasser, Director, ARPA/ETO, 3701 N. Fairfax Drive, 
Arlington, VA 22203-1714; telephone: 703/696-2213.

    Dated: March 15, 1996.
