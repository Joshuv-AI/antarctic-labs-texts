OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Excepted Service

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This gives notice of positions placed under Schedule A, B, and 
C in the excepted service, as required by Civil Service Rule VI, 
Exceptions from the Competitive Service.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Shivery, Director, Washington 
Service Center, Employment Service (202) 606-1015.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Appearing in the listing below are 1 
Schedule A authority and the individual authorities established under 
Schedule C between January 1, 2002, and January 31, 2002. Future 
notices will be published on the fourth Tuesday of each month, or as 
soon as possible thereafter. A consolidated listing of all Excepted 
Service authorities as of June 30 is published each year.

Schedule A

Social Security Administration

    Temporary and time-limited positions in the Ticket to Work and Work 
Incentives Advisor Panel. No Employees may be appointed after November 
17, 2007. Effective November 19, 2001.
