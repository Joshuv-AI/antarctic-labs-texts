Defense Partnership Council Meeting

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice of Meeting Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  On October 12, 2000, 65 FR 60652, the Department of Defense 
published a notice to announce a meeting of the Defense Partnership 
Council to be held November 9, 2000. This notice is to announce that 
the meeting is cancelled due to conflicts in members' schedules.

Civilian Personnel Management Service, 1400 Key Boulevard, Suite B-200, 
Arlington, VA 22209-5144, (703) 696-1450.
