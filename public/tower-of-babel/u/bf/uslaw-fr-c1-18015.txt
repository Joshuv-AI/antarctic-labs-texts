Correction

    In rule document 01-18015 beginning on page 38892 in the issue of 
Thursday, July 26, 2001, make the following correction:
    On page 38895, in Table 2, in Procedure 2, in the second column, 
the entry ``10,000 total flight cycles or 1,500 500 flight cycles after 
the effective date of this AD.'' is corrected to read ``10,000 total 
flight cycles or 1,500 flight cycles after January 6, 1997 or 100 
flight cycles after the effective date of this AD.''
