OFFICE OF THE UNITED STATES TRADE REPRESENTATIVE


Generalized System of Preferences (GSP); 1995 Annual Review 
Public Hearings Site

AGENCY: Office of the United States Trade Representative.

ACTION: Notice of site for the hearings associated with the 1995 Annual 
Review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces that the hearings for the 1995 Annual 
Review under the Generalized System of Preferences will be held 
November 13 and 14, 1996 at the International Trade Commission, Main 
Hearing Room, 500 E Street, S.W., Washington, D.C. 20436. The hearings 
will begin at 10 am on November 13th.
    For further information contact the GSP Information Center (202) 
395-6971.
Frederick L. Montgomery,
Chairman, Trade Policy Staff Committee.
