DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Modac County RAC Meetings

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of Modoc County RAC meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the authorities in the Federal Advisory Committees 
Act (Pub. L. 92-463) and under the Secure Rural Schools and Community 
Self-Determination Act of 2000 (Pub. L. 106-393), the Modoc National 
Forest's Modoc County Resource Advisory Committee will meet Monday 
October 6, 2003, from 6 to 8 p.m. in Alturas, California. The meetings 
are open to the public.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Agenda topics for the meeting include 
approval of the August 11 minutes, review of Modoc County Board of 
Supervisor's approval of projects for 2003 RAC funds, and 
reprioritization of projects if needed. The meeting will be held at 
Modoc National Forest Office, Conference Room, 800 West 12th St., 
Alturas, California on Monday, October 6, 2003 from 6 to 8 p.m. Time 
will be set aside for public comments at the beginning of the meeting.
