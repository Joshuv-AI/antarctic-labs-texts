DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, 
November 29, 2000, 1 p.m. to November 29, 2000, 3 p.m., NIEHS, 79 T.W. 
Alexander Drive, Building 4401, Conference Room 3446, Research Triangle 
Park, NC 27709 which was published in the Federal Register on October 
19, 2000, FR 203:62741.
    The telephone conference call meeting will be held on January 16, 
2001, from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. at the same location, instead of November 
29, 2000, as previously advertised. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: November 17, 2000.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
