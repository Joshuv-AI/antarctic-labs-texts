DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Former Prisoners of War; Notice of 
Meeting--Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. App. 2, that the meeting of 
the Advisory Committee on Former Prisoners of War (FPOW), previously 
scheduled to be held at the Audie Murphy VA Medical Center, 7400 Merton 
Minter Blvd., San Antonio, TX, on January 11-13, 2016, has been 
cancelled.
    For more information, please contact Mr. Eric Robinson, Designated 
Federal Officer at (202) 443-6016 or via email at 
[email protected].

    Dated: December 15, 2015.
Jelessa Burney,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
