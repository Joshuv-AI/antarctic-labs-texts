CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission.

TIME AND DATE: Tuesday, April 30, 1996.

LOCATION: Room 420, East-West Towers, 4330 East-West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the public.

Matter to be Considered:

Children's Sleepwear

    The Commission will consider whether to issue final amendments 
of the children's sleepwear flammability standards to exempt tight 
fitting sleepwear garments and sleepwear garments intended for 
children younger than six months.

    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East-West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207 (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: April 22, 1996.
Sadye E. Dunn,
Secretary.
