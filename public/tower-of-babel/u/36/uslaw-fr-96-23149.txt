DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. TM97-1-113-000]


Gasdel Pipeline System, Inc.; Notice of Change in Annual Charge 
Adjustment

September 5, 1996.
    Take notice that on August 30, 1996, Gasdel Pipeline System, Inc. 
(Gasdel) tendered for filing and acceptance to be

[[Page 47907]]

a part of its FERC Gas Tariff, First Revised Volume No. 1-A, Fifth 
Revised Sheet No. 5, with a proposed effective date of October 1, 1996.
    Gasdel states that the purpose of this filing is to revise its 
Annual Charge Adjustment surcharge in order to recover the Commission's 
annual charges for the 1996 fiscal year.
    Gasdel states that copies of the filing have been mailed to all 
jurisdictional customers.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedures (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests must be filed as provided in Section 154.210 of the 
Commission's Regulations. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Any person desiring to 
become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
