Community Technology Centers Program

AGENCY: Office of Vocational and Adult Education, Department of 
Education.

ACTION: Notice inviting applications for new awards for fiscal year 
(FY) 2003; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 3, 2003 a notice inviting applications for new awards 
for the Community Technology Centers Program for FY 2003 was published 
in the Federal Register (68 FR 33321).
    On page 33323, in column one, the ``Deadline for Transmittal of 
Applications'' date reads ``June 30, 2003'' and the ``Deadline for 
Intergovernmental Review'' date reads ``August 29, 2003''. These dates, 
respectively, are corrected to read ``July 7, 2003'' and ``September 5, 
2003''.
