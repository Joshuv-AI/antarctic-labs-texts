DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Elko County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Elko County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC) will hold 
its first meeting.

DATES: The meeting will be held on Friday, April 29th, 2011 and will 
begin at 10 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held in the Forest Service office at 
2035 Last Chance Road, Elko, NV 89801.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Doug Clarke, RAC Coordinator, USDA, 
Humboldt-Toiyabe National Forest, Mountain City Ranger District, 2035 
Last Chance Road, Elko, NV 89801 (775) 778-6127; e-mail: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Agenda items include: (1) Remarks by Forest 
Supervisor and Mountain City District Ranger; (2) Review of Secure 
Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act; (3) Role of RAC 
committee members; (4) Selection of RAC Committee Chairman; (5) 
Overview of project selection process; and (6) Public Comment. The 
meeting is open to the public. Public input opportunity will be 
provided and individuals will have the opportunity to address the 
Committee at that time.

    Dated: April 8, 2011.
Jeanne M. Higgins,
Forest Supervisor.
