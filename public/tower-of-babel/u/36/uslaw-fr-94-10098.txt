DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. JD94-05567T Oklahoma-74]


State of Oklahoma; NGPA Notice of Determination by Jurisdictional 
Agency Designating Tight Formation

April 21, 1994.
    Take notice that on March 11, 1994, the Corporation Commission of 
the State of Oklahoma (Oklahoma) submitted the above-referenced notice 
of determination pursuant to Sec. 271.703(c)(3) of the Commission's 
Regulations, that the Sycamore and Hunton Formations, underlying a 
portion of Garvin County, Oklahoma, qualifies as a tight formation 
under section 107(b) of the Natural Gas Policy Act of 1978. The 
recommended area consists of the E/2 of section 8, PW/2 of sections 9 
and 16, the NW/4 and SE/4 of section 21, and the NE/4 of section 28 in 
T3N, R3W, in Garvin County, Oklahoma.
    The notice of determination also contains Oklahoma's findings that 
the referenced formation meets the requirements of the Commission's 
regulations set forth in 18 CFR part 271.
    The application for determination is available for inspection, 
except for material which is confidential under 18 CFR 27.206, at the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street NE., 
Washington, DC 20426. Persons objecting to the determination may file a 
protest, in accordance with 18 CFR 275.203 and 275.204, within 20 days 
after the date this notice is issued by the Commission.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
