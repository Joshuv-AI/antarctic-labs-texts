DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 3 June 1998.
    Time of Meeting: 0800-1600.
    Place: Arlington, VA.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Issue Group panel on 
``Impacts of Precision Guided Munitions on Future Tank and Howitzer 
Capabilities'' will visit the Presidential Tower in Arlington, VA 
for a meeting on the study subject. This meeting will be open to the 
public. Any interested person may attend, appear before, or file 
statements with the committee at the time and in the manner 
permitted by the committee. For further information, please call our 
office at (703) 604-7490.
Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist Army Science Board.
