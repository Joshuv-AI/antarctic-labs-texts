DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Notice of Meeting of the Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and 
Availability

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, HHS.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and Availability will meet 
on April 27, 1998, from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm and on April 28, 1998 from 
8:00 am to 3:00 pm. The meeting will take place in the Kaleidoscope 
Room of the Georgetown Holiday Inn, 2101 Wisconsin Ave. N.W., 
Washington, D.C. 20007. The meeting will be entirely open to the 
public.

[[Page 15422]]

    The Committee will consider blood product shortages. On April 27, 
1998 the committee will review information presented to it by 
representatives of consumers, industry and government agencies. At the 
conclusion of these presentations, the public will be invited to 
comment. Following these presentations, the Committee will consider 
what, if any, recommendations to make to the Department on this matter.
    Prospective speakers should notify the Executive Secretary of their 
desire to address the Committee and should plan for no more than 5 
minutes of comments.
