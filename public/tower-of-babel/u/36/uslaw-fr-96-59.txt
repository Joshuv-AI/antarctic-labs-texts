DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Airport Traffic Control Tower at Delaware County Airport, Muncie, 
IN

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of closing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Notice is hereby given that on December 31, 1995, the Airport 
Traffic Control Tower (ATCT) at Muncie, Indiana will be permanently 
closed. Services to the aviation public in the Muncie area, formerly 
provided by Muncie ATCT, are being provided by the Indianapolis Center 
at Indianapolis, Indiana. This information will be reflected in the FAA 
organization statement the next time it is reissued.
William C. Withycombe,
Acting Regional Administrator, Great Lakes Region.
