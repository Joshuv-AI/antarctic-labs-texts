MERIT SYSTEMS PROTECTION BOARD

5 CFR Part 1201


Notice of Practice and Procedure; Realignment of Regional Offices

AGENCY: Merit Systems Protection Board.

ACTION: Final rule; Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The document on Practice and Procedure; Realignment of 
Regional Offices which was published on August 10, 1995 (60 FR 40744), 
contained an error in the address for the Atlanta Regional Office. This 
document contains the correct address and facsimile number for the 
Atlanta Regional Office and amends the Approved Hearing Locations by 
Regional Appeals for the Atlanta Regional Office.

EFFECTIVE DATE: August 25, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Darrell L. Netherton, Senior Executive for Regional Administration, 
(202) 653-7980.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. 95-19729, on page 40744, Column 
3, in Appendix II to part 1201, item 1 is corrected to read as follows:

Atlanta Regional Office, 401 West Peachtree Street NW., 10th floor, 
Atlanta, Georgia 30308-3519, Facsimile No.: (404) 730-2767 (Alabama, 
Florida, Georgia, Mississippi, South Carolina, and Tennessee)
