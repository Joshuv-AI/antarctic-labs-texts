DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Attendance at the Colorado Public Utilities 
Commission's Fifth Commissioner Information Meeting

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of its staff may attend the Colorado Public 
Utilities Commission's Commissioner Information Meeting (CIM) as noted 
below. Their attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach 
efforts.
    The CIM will be held on March 20, 2018 from 9:00 a.m. until 3:00 
p.m. Mountain Time at the Colorado Public Utilities Commission, Hearing 
Room A, 1560 Broadway, Suite 250, Denver, CO 80202. The phone number is 
(303) 894-2533.
