from specified requirements of Federal Aviation Regulations. The 
purpose of this notice is to improve the public's

information in the summary is intended to affect the legal status of 
the petition or its final disposition.

Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2023-0471.
    Petitioner: 417 Drone Imaging LLC.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  48.110(a)(6) and 
48.110(a)(7).
    Description of Relief Sought: 417 Drone Imaging LLC seeks relief 
from 14 CFR 48.110(a)(6) and 48.110(a)(7) to apply a single 
registration number to all small unmanned aircraft system (sUAS), 
weighing less than 55 pounds, in their system to commercially operate a 
multiple sUAS swarm drone light show.
