FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-256, MM Docket No. 01-301, RM-10207]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Mississippi State, MS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Mississippi Authority for 
Educational Television, licensee of noncommercial station WMAB-TV, 
Mississippi State, Mississippi, substitutes DTV channel *10 for DTV 
channel *38. See 66 FR 54190, October 26, 2001. DTV channel *10 can be 
allotted to Mississippi State in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates (33-21-14 N. and 89-09-00 W.) with a power of 4.3, HAAT of 
349 meters and with a DTV service population of 330 thousand. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 25, 2002.
