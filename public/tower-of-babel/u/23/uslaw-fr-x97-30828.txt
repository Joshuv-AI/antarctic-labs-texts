Correction

    In rule document 97-10481, beginning on page 24690, in the issue of 
Tuesday, May 6, 1997, make the following corrections:


Sec. 172.101  [Corrected]

    1. On page 24702, in the table, insert seven asterisks (*) under 
``n-Amylene''.
    2. On page 24706, in the table, ``Dn-Butyl bromide, see 1-
Bromobutane'' should read ``n-Butyl bromide, see 1-Bromobutane''. Also, 
move the phrase from column (1) to column (2).
    3. On page 24707, in the table, in column (1), ``1'' should read 
``I''.
    4. On the same page, in the table, for the last five entries, 
column 5 should be blank and all other column information is moved over 
one.
    5. On page 24711, in the table, in the fourth entry, in column 
(9B), ``30 I'' should read ``30 1''.


Sec. 173.62  [Corrected]

    6. On page 24725, in the table, in the second line from the bottom, 
``Receptacles'' should removed.
    7. On page 24726, in the table, in the last line, ``No'' should 
read ``Not''.
    8. On page 24727, in the table, in the last line, in both places, 
``Receptables'' should read ``Receptacles''.
    9. On page 24728, in the table, in the second column, remove the 
leaders after ``fibreboard, metal, plastics, wood, dividing, and 
partitions''.


Sec. 173.132  [Corrected]

    10. On page 24732, in Sec. 173.132(c)(3), in the formula, remove 
the ``+'' after the inferior ``A''.


Sec. 173.224  [Corrected]

    11. On page 24734, in Sec. 173.224(b), in the table, in column (5), 
``x5'' should read ``-5''.
    12. On pages 24736 through 24740, in Sec. 173.224, in the table, 
the following entries are corrected to read as follows:
