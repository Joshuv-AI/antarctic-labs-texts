DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


U.S. Air Force Exclusive Patent License

    AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, Air Force Research Laboratory 
Information Directorate, Rome, New York.

    ACTION: Notice of intent to issue a partially exclusive patent 
license.

    SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of part 404 of Title 37, Code 
of Federal Regulations, which implements Public Law 96-517, as amended, 
the Department of the Air Force announces its intention to grant 
Synerji, LLC, a corporation of Delaware, having a place of business at 
106 Genesee St., Utica, New York 13501 a partially exclusive license in 
any right, title and interest the United States Air Force has in: U.S. 
Patent Application No. 13/573,899, filed on December 17, 2012 entitled 
``Method for Context Aware Text Recognition.''
    FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: An exclusive license for this 
patent will be granted unless a written objection is received within 
fifteen (15) days from the date of publication of this Notice. Written 
objections should be sent to: Air Force Research Laboratory, Office of 
the Staff Judge Advocate, AFRL/RIJ, 26 Electronic Parkway, Rome, New 
York 13441-4514. Telephone: (315) 330-2087; Facsimile (315) 330-7583.

Henry Williams,
Civ, Acting Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
