FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 00-8; RM-9788]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Spencer and Webster, MA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Montachusett Broadcasting, Inc. this 
document reallots Channel 255A from Spencer to Webster, Massachusetts, 
and modifies the license of Station WORC-FM to specify Webster as the 
community of license. See 65 FR 4491, published January 27, 2000. The 
reference coordinates for Channel 255A at Webster, Massachusetts, are 
42-02-10 and 71-59-23. With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 16, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Report and Order in MM Docket No. 00-8, adopted March 22, 2000, and 
released March 31, 2000. The full text of this decision is available
