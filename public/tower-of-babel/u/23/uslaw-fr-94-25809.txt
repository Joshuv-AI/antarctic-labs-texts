                Presidential Determination No. 94-58 of September 30, 
                1994

 

Assistance for Independent States of the
                Former Soviet Union

                Memorandum for the Secretary of State

                Pursuant to section 584 of the Foreign Operations, 
                Export Financing, and Related Programs Appropriations 
                Act, 1995 (Public Law 103-306), I hereby determine that 
                the provision of funds to the Government of Russia is 
                in the national security interest of the United States.

                You are authorized and directed to notify the Congress 
                of this determination and to publish it in the Federal 
                Register.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    Washington, September 30, 1994.
