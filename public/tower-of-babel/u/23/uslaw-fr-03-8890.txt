RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Notice of Public Meeting; Sunshine Act

    Notice is hereby given that the Railroad Retirement Board will hold 
a meeting on April 16, 2003, 9 a.m., at the Board's meeting room on the 
8th floor of its headquarters building, 844 North Rush Street, Chicago, 
Illinois 60611. The agenda for this meeting follows:
    (1) Employer Status Determination--Rail Temps, Inc.
    (2) Employer Status Determination--Southern Gulf Railway Company.
    (3) Occupational Disability Task Force Report.
    (4) Management Information Report--Strategic Initiatives Related to 
the President's Management Agenda.
    The entire meeting will be open to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: April 7, 2003.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
