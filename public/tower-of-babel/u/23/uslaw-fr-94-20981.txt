DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Maritime Administration

 

Notice of Change of Name of Approved Trustee

    Notice is hereby given that effective July 1, 1994, American 
National Bank and Trust Company, with offices at 101 East Fifth Street, 
St. Paul, Minnesota 55101-1860, has changed its name to American Bank 
National Association.

    Dated: August 17, 1994.

    By Order of the Maritime Administrator.
Joel C. Richard,
Acting Secretary.
