Employment and Training Administration

Ericsson Inc.; Brea, CA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 23, 2006 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Ericsson Inc., 
Brea, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 30th day of May, 2006.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
