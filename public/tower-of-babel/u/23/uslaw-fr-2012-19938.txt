DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Agency Information Collection Extension; Correction

AGENCY: U.S. Department of Energy.

[[Page 48508]]


ACTION: Notice and Request for Comments; Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy (DOE) published a document in the 
Federal Register of June 11, 2012, announcing the submission of an 
information request to the OMB for the Foreign Travel Management System 
(FTMS). This document corrects an error in that notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Requests for additional information or 
copies of the information collection instrument and instructions should 
be directed to Julie Squires at [email protected].

Correction

    In the Federal Register of June 11, 2012, in FR Doc. 2012-14119, 77 
FR 34367, please make the following correction:
    On page 34367, second column, under the heading SUPPLEMENTARY 
INFORMATION, (1) should read OMB No. 1910-5144;

    Issued in Washington, DC, on August 7, 2012.
Julie Squires,
Director, Office of International Travel and Exchange Visitor Programs.
