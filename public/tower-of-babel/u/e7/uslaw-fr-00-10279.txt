DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On, January 6, 2000 a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 65, No. 4, Page 787, that an application had been filed with the 
Fish and Wildlife Service by Toledo Zoological Gardens, Toledo, OH, for 
a permit (PRT-014704) to import one captive born polar bear (Ursus 
maritimus) for the purpose of public display.
    Notice is hereby given that on March 16, 2000, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.

    Dated: April 20, 2000.
Kristen Nelson,
Chief, Branch of Permits, Office of Management Authority.
