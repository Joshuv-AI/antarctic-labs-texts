Renewal of Two Currently Approved Information Collections;

AGENCY: Corporation for National and Community Service.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Corporation for National and Community Service published a 
document in the Federal Register of November 21, 2003, concerning a 
request for public comments on two information collection activities. 
The document contained an incorrect telephone number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Bruce Kellogg, (202) 606-5000, ext. 
256.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of November 21, 2003, in 68 FR 65681, on 
page 65682, in the first column, correct the FOR FURTHER INFORMATION 
CONTACT caption to read:

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Bruce Kellogg, (202) 606-5000, ext. 
526.
