DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

Veterans Affairs Wage Committee, Notice of Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (Public Law 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App.), that the Veterans Affairs 
Wage Committee has been renewed for a 2-year period beginning April 19, 
1995, through April 19, 1997.

    Dated: September 28, 1995.


[[Page 52729]]

    By Direction of the Secretary.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
