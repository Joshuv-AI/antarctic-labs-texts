Amendment of Certain Designations Pursuant to Section 1(a)(ii)(A) 
of Executive Order 12947

Order 12947 of January 23, 1995, as amended by Executive Order 13099 of 
August 20, 1998, and in consultation with the Secretary of the Treasury
