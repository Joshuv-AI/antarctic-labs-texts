DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:


    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 2-3 June 1998.
    Time of Meeting: 0800-1600, Jun 98; 0800-1400, 3 Jun 98.
    Place: Pentagon--Arlington, VA.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Issue Group panel on 
``De-militarization of the Multiple Launch Rocket System (MLRS) 
Rockets'' will receive fact finding briefings from industry and 
government representatives. This meeting will be open to the public. 
Any interested person may attend, appear before, or file statements 
with the committee at the time and in the manner permitted by the 
committee. For further information, please call our office at (703) 
604-7490.
Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
