Idaho: Filing of Plats of Survey

    The plat of the following described land was officially filed in 
the Idaho State Office, Bureau of Land Management, Boise, Idaho, 
effective 9:00 a.m. August 11, 1997.
    The plat representing the dependent resurvey of a portion of the 
Sixth Standard Parallel North (south boundary), and the survey of tract 
No. 39, unsurveyed T. 30 N., R. 7 E., Boise Meridian, Idaho, Group 928, 
was accepted, August 11, 1997.
    This survey was executed to meet certain administrative needs of 
the U.S. Forest Service. All inquiries concerning the survey of the 
above described land must be sent to the Chief, Cadastral Survey, Idaho 
State Office, Bureau of Land Management, 1387 South Vinnell Way, Boise, 
Idaho, 83709-1657.

    Dated: August 11, 1997.
Duane E. Olsen,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor for Idaho.
