NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
[Notice 95-098]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Estee Lauder Companies of 
Melville, New York 11747, has requested a partially exclusive license 
to practice the invention protected by U.S. Patent No. 4,902,769, 
entitled ``Low Dielectric Fluorinated Poly (Phenylene Ether Ketone) 
film and coating,'' which was issued on February 20, 1990, to the 
United States of America as represented by the Administrator of the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections to 
the prospective grant of a license should be sent to Mr. George F. 
Helfrich, Patent Counsel, Langley Research Center.

DATES: Responses to this Notice must be received by January 5, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. George F. Helfrich, Patent Counsel, NSAS Langley Research Center, 
Mail Code 212, Hampton, VA 23681-0001; telephone (804) 864-3521.
