Amendment to Notice of Closure of Public Land to Certain Uses in 
Yellowstone County, MT

ACTION: Notice; amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published a document in the 
Federal Register of October 14, 1999, closing 765 acres of public land 
to the use of motorized vehicles and mechanized vehicles, the discharge 
of firearms, horseback riding, hang gliding, and rock climbing. This 
notice will amend the closure to the area known as Four Dances Natural 
Area (formerly known as the Sacrifice Cliff or the Larsen property) to 
include closure to the use of Blowguns or Bow & Arrow. This area is 
also closed to killing or any attempt to kill animals without a special 
permit.
