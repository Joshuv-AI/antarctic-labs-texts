Greatbatch Hittman, Inc., Columbia, MD; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 2, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a state agency on behalf of workers at Greatbatch 
Hittman, Inc., Columbia, Maryland.

[[Page 40155]]

    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of June, 2006.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
