FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

AGENCY: Federal Election Commission.

Date and Time: Thursday, July 13, 2017 at 10:00 a.m.

Place: 999 E Street NW., Washington, DC (Ninth Floor).

Status: This Meeting Will Be Open to the Public.

Request by the Sergeant at Arms of the United States House of 
Representatives Regarding the Possible Use of Campaign Funds for 
Residential Security Systems
Draft Advisory Opinion 2017-07: Sergeant at Arms
Draft Advisory Opinion 2017-05: Great America PAC and Committee to 
Defend the President
Proposed Interim Enforcement Policy for ``Volunteer Mail'' Exemption

American Elections
Management and Administrative Matters

at least 72 hours prior to the meeting date.

Telephone: (202) 694-1220.
