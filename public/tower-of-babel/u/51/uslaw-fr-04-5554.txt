DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


State Charter School Facilities Incentive Grants Program (CFDA 
Number: 84.282D)

AGENCY: Department of Education, Office of Innovation and Improvement.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: We published a notice in the Federal Register on February 23, 
2004 (69 FR 8318) inviting applications for new awards for fiscal year 
2004 for the State Charter School Facilities Incentive Grants Program. 
We inadvertently omitted words from the text. This notice corrects that 
error.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of February 23, 2004, in FR Doc. 04-3849, 
on page 8319, column 3, line 8, after the word ``competition'', insert 
the words ``for construction''.
