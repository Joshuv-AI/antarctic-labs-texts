FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Farm Credit Administration Board; Amendment to Sunshine Act 
Meeting

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Government in the Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 
552b(e)(3)), the Farm Credit Administration gave notice on March 8, 
1996 (61 FR 9458) of the special meeting of the Farm Credit 
Administration Board (Board) scheduled for March 12, 1996. This notice 
is to amend the agenda by adding an item to the open session of that 
meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Floyd Fithian, Secretary to the Farm 
Credit Administration Board, (703) 883-4025, TDD (703) 883-4444.

ADDRESSES: Farm Credit Administration, 1501 Farm Credit Drive, McLean, 
Virginia 22102-5090.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This meeting of the Board was open to the 
public (limited space available). The agenda for March 12, 1996, is 
amended by adding the following item:

Open Session

B. Reports

    2. Office of Secondary Market Oversight's Quarterly Report.

    Dated: March 13, 1996.
Floyd Fithian,
Secretary, Farm Credit Administration Board.
