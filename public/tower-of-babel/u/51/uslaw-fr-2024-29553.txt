PENSION BENEFIT GUARANTY CORPORATION


Performance Review Board Members

AGENCY: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation (PBGC) announces the 
appointment of members of the PBGC Performance Review Board.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In accordance with 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), made 
applicable by PBGC's Senior Level Performance Management System, PBGC 
announces the appointment of those individuals who have been selected 
to serve as members of PBGC's Performance Review Board. The Performance 
Review Board is responsible for making recommendations on each senior 
level (SL) professional's annual summary rating, performance-based 
adjustment, and performance award to the appointing authority.
    The following individuals have been designated as members of PBGC's 
2024 Performance Review Board:

1. Ann Orr, Acting Director
2. David Foley, Chief of Benefits Administration
3. Patricia Kelly, Chief Financial Officer
4. Alice Maroni, Chief Management Officer

    Issued in Washington, DC.
Ann Y. Orr,
Acting Director, Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation.
