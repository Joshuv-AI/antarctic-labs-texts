DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

21 CFR Part 175



 

Resinous and Polymeric Coatings

CFR Correction

    In title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 170 to 199, 
revised as of April 1, 1994, in Sec. 175.300, paragraph (d), Table 2, 
the 8 percent alcohol extractant entry for food type VI-A of conditions 
D. and E. was inadvertently removed. The entries should read ``150 deg. 
F, 2 hr.'' and ``120 deg. F, 24 hr.'' respectively.
