Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

CNG Transmission Corporation and Texas Eastern Transmission 
Corporation; Notice of Site Visit

June 3, 1998.
    On June 9 an 10, 1998, the Office of Pipeline Regulation staff will 
conduct a site visit, with representatives of CNG Transmission 
Corporation, of the Market Area Storage Project in Westmoreland County, 
Pennsylvania.
    All interested parties may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
