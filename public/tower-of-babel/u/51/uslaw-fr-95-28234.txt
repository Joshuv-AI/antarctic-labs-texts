SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Specialized Small Business Investment Company (SSBIC) Advisory 
Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Investment Division (SBIC) 
Advisory Council will hold a public meeting on Friday, November 17, 
1995 at 9:00 am at the U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 Third 
Street, S.W., Eisenhower Conference Room (Eighth Floor), Washington, 
D.C. 20416; to discuss matters as may be presented by members, staff of 
the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Darryl K. Hairston, 
Deputy Associate Administrator for Investment, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, 409 Third Street, S.W., Suite 6300, Washington, D.C. 
20416 (202) 205-6510.

    Dated: November 8, 1995.
W. F. Combs,
Acting Director Office of Advisory Council.
