Notice of Extension of Concession Contracts

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Public Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Park Service hereby gives public notice that it 
proposes to extend the following expiring concession contracts for a 
period of up to one (1) year, or until the effective date of a new 
contract, whichever occurs sooner.

DATES: Effective January 1, 2015.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jo A. Pendry, Acting Chief, Commercial 
Services Program, National Park Service, 1201 Eye Street NW., 11th 
Floor, Washington, DC 20005, Telephone: 202-513-7156.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: All of the listed concession authorizations 
will expire by their terms on or before December 31, 2014. Pursuant to 
36 CFR 51.23, the National Park Service has determined the proposed 
short-term extensions are necessary to avoid interruption of visitor 
services and has taken all reasonable and appropriate steps to consider 
alternatives to avoid such interruption. The publication of this notice 
merely reflects the intent of the National Park Service but does not 
bind the National Park Service to extend any of the contracts listed 
below.
