FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 00-2148; MM Docket No. 99-75; RM-9446]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Grants, Milan, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Don Davis, former licensee 
of Station KQEO(FM), now Station KXXQ(FM), Grants, New Mexico, reallots 
Channel 264A from Grants to Milan, New Mexico as the community's second 
local aural transmisson service. See 64 FR 14423 (March 25, 1999). 
Channel 264A at Grants was downgraded from 264C2 by one-step 
application (File No. BMPH-19960226IB) granted on June 13, 1996. 
Channel 264A can be allotted to Milan in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements, with respect to 
domestic allotments, at petitioner's site 5.7 kilometers (3.6 miles) 
south, at coordinates 35-07-09 North Latitude and 107-54-08 West 
Longitude.

DATES: Effective November 6, 2000.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria M. McCauley, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.
