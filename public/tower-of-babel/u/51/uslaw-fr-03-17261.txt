Centers for Independent Living; Notice Inviting Applications for 
New Awards for Fiscal Year (FY) 2003; Correction

SUMMARY: On June 20, 2003, a notice inviting applications for new 
awards for the Centers for Independent Living program for FY 2003 was 
published in the Federal Register (68 FR 36977).
    On page 36977, in column three, the ``Estimated Available Funds'' 
figure reads ``$1,871,862'' and the ``Estimated Average Size of 
Awards'' figure reads ``$103,992''. These figures, respectively, are 
corrected to read ``$1,935,902'' and ``$107,550''. In the table, for 
Indiana, the ``Estimated available funds'' figure reads ``$42,980''. 
This figure is corrected to read ``$107,020''.
