FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2]

 

A Petition for Reconsideration of Actions in Rulemaking 
Proceedings

September 15, 1994.
    A Petition for reconsideration has been filed with respect to the 
Commission's Order listed below. The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room 616, 1919 M Street, NW., 
Washington, DC, by contacting Donna Viert ((202) 418-1725). In 
addition, copies may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. ((202) 857-3800). Oppositions to this petition 
for reconsideration must be filed by October 5, 1994. See Section 
1.4(b) of the Commission's Rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an 
opposition must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing 
oppositions has expired.
    Subject: Specifications as to Pleadings and Documents; Amendment of 
Section 1.49 of the Commission's Rule (FCC 94-181).
    Filed By: David B. Popkin on August 10, 1994.
    Action by the General Counsel.

Federal Communications Commission.
William F. Caton,
Acting Secretary.
