The Lane Company, a Subsidiary of Lane Furniture Industries, 
Inc., a Subsidiary of Furniture Brands International, Altavista, VA; 
Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at The Lane Company, a subsidiary of 
Lane Furniture Industries, Inc., a subsidiary of Furniture Brands 
International, AltaVista, Virginia. The application contained no new 
substantial information which would bear importantly on the 
Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was 
issued.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of June, 2005.
