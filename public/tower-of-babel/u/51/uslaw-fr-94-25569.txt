DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[NM-017-4210-05-RGRP]

 

Modification of Realty Action--Sale of Public Lands in Bernalillo 
County, NM

agency: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

action: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: The Notice of Realty Action for Sale of Lands in Bernalillo 
County, New Mexico, published on pages 42280-42282 of the Federal 
Register, Volume 59, No. 158, on August 17, 1994, is hereby modified to 
delete Competitive Parcel No. 7, NMNM 88815, because of a potential 
conflict with the adjoining land owner. All other terms and conditions 
of the previous Notice remain unchanged. Questions regarding this sale 
may be directed to Joe Jaramillo, Rio Puerco Resource Area Office, 
(505) 761-8779.

    Dated: October 7, 1994.
Sue E. Richardson,
Associate District Manager.
