DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Internal Revenue Service


Cancellation of Open Meeting of the Area 6 Taxpayer Advocacy 
Panel (Including the States of Washington, Hawaii, Alaska, Idaho, 
Oregon, Wyoming, Montana, Utah, New Mexico, Nevada, Las Vegas and 
Colorado)

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The open meeting of the Area 6 Committee of the Taxpayer 
Advocacy Panel to be conducted (via teleconference) has been cancelled.

DATES: The meeting will be held Monday, July 19, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Judi Nicholas at 1-888-912-1227, or 
206-220-6096.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given pursuant to Section 
10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. App. (1988) 
that an open meeting of the Area 6 Committee of the Taxpayer Advocacy 
Panel will be held Monday, July 19, 2004 from 2 p.m. Pacific Time to 3 
p.m. Pacific Time via a telephone conference call published on Monday, 
July 19, 2004, has been cancelled. For further information contact Judi 
Nicholas. Mrs. Nicholas can be reached at 1-888-912-1227 or 206-220-
6096 or write to 915 Second Avenue, M/S W406, Seattle, WA 98174, or 
post comments to the web site: http://www.improveirs.org.

    Dated: July 1, 2004.
Bernard Coston,
Director, Taxpayer Advocacy Panel.
