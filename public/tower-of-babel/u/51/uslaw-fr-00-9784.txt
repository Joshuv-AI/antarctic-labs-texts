SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    This collection of information is necessary to identify original 
export licenses of respondents who request duplicate licenses for lost 
or destroyed licenses. The licensee must submit a letter certifying 
that the original license issued to a licensee has been lost or 
destroyed. They must provide the circumstances under which it was lost 
or destroyed, and if found, will return either the original or the 
duplicate to BXA. All other record keeping requirements pertaining to 
the original license remain in effect for duplicate licenses.

II. Method of Collection

    Written submission.
