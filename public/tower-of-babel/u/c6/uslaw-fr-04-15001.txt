FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 04-1201, MM Docket No. 01-43, RM-10041]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Jackson, MS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of CivCo, Inc., grants the 
petition for reconsideration of the Report and Order, which dismissed 
CivCo's request seeking the substitution

[[Page 39869]]

of DTV channel 9 for station WLBT-TV's assigned DTV channel 51. See 68 
FR 19240 (2003). The Commission grants CivCo's request to substitute 
DTV channel 9 for DTV channel 51 at Jackson. DTV channel 9 can be 
allotted to Jackson, Mississippi, in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates 32-12-49 N. and 90-22-56 W. with a power of 3.2, HAAT of 
610 meters and with a DTV service population of 639 thousand. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective July 26, 2004.
