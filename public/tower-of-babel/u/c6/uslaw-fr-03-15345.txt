EXPORT-IMPORT BANK


Economic Impact Policy

    This notice is to inform the public that the Export-Import Bank has 
received an application to guarantee up to $198 million of equipment 
and other goods and services on the behalf of U.S. exporters to a buyer 
in Egypt. The U.S. exports will enable the Egyptian company to produce 
anhydrous ammonia from natural gas. The Egyptian company will have a 
production capacity of 1,850 metric tons of ammonia per day. It is 
envisioned this new production will be consumed primarily in Jordan and 
India. Interested parties may submit comments on this transaction by e-
mail to [email protected] or by mail to 811 Vermont Avenue, NW., 
Room 1238, Washington, DC 20571, within 14 days of the date this notice 
appears in the Federal Register.

Helene S. Walsh,
Director, Policy Oversight and Review.
