DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine Notice of Meeting Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the virtual meeting of the 
Biomedical Library, Informatics and Data Science Review Committee, June 
15-16, 2023, which was published in the Federal Register on March 23, 
2023, 88 FR 56, Page 17587.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting times to 10:00 
a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on June 15th and 10 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. on June 16th. 
The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 17, 2023.
