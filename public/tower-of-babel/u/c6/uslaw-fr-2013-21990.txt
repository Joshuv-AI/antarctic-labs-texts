DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Research and Innovative Technology Administration


ITS Joint Program Office; Notice of Meeting

AGENCY: Research and Innovative Technology Administration (RITA), U.S. 
Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    This notice announces the cancellation of the Connected Vehicle 
Planning and Policy Stakeholder Meeting. The meeting was scheduled for 
Thursday, September 12, 2013 from 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. E.D.T. in the 
DOT Conference Center at the U.S. Department of Transportation, 1200 
New Jersey Ave. SE., Washington, DC. The ITS Joint Program Office is 
considering rescheduling the meeting for a future date. In the 
meantime, stakeholders may be interested in attending the upcoming 
Connected Vehicle Program Public Meeting on September 24 to 26, 2013. 
For more information, visit http://www.its.dot.gov/meetings/connected_vehicle_policy.htm.
    Questions about the cancellation may be emailed to 
([email protected]).

John Augustine,
Managing Director, ITS Joint Program Office.
