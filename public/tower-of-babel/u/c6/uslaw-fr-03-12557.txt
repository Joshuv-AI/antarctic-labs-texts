Willing B. Wire Willingboro, New Jersey; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 2, 2003 in response to petition 
filed by a state agency representative on behalf of workers at Willing 
B. Wire, Willingboro, New Jersey.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 6th day of May, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
