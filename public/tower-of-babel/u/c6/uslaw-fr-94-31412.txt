DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

NorAm Gas Transmission Company; Notice Of Filing

December 16, 1994.
    Take notice that on December 14, 1994, NorAm Gas Transmission 
Company (NGT) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, 
Fourth Revised Volume No. 1, revised Original Sheet No. 126, to become 
effective February 1, 1995.
    NGT states that the revised tariff sheet describes, in greater 
detail than as originally filed, the area around the Chandler 
Compressor Station in which pool receipts may be designated to any one 
of NGT's three zones, by stating that receipt points in Pittsburg, 
Latimer, Haskell, and Pushmataha Counties in Oklahoma, and receipt 
points on Lines O West, OT-12, and OT-16, will be eligible receipt 
points for all of NGT's zones.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol 
Street, NE., Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Rule 211 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.214). All such 
protests should be filed on or before December 23, 1994. Protests will 
be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action 
to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
