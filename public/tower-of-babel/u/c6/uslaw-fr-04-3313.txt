Manpower, Inc., Roswell, NM; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 29, 2004 in response to a worker 
petition filed by one worker on behalf of workers of Manpower Inc., 
Roswell, New Mexico.
    To be valid, petitions must be filed by three workers, their duly 
authorized representative, or a state agency. The petition regarding 
the investigation has therefore been deemed invalid. Consequently, the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 30th day of January 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
