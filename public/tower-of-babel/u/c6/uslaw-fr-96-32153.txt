DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP97-29-000]


Panhandle Eastern Pipe Line Company; Notice of Technical 
Conference

December 13, 1996.
    In the Commission's order issued November 8, 1996, the Commission 
held that the issues raised by the protestors in the above captioned 
proceeding should be addressed in a technical conference.
    Take notice that the technical conference will be held on Tuesday, 
January 14, 1997, at 10:00 a.m., in a room to be designated at the 
offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, 
NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested parties and Staff are permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
