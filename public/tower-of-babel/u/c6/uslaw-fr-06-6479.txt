DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary of Defense


Termination of Department of Defense Federal Advisory Committees

AGENCY: DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee Act of 
1972, as amended (5 U.S.C. Appendix), the Department of Defense gives 
notice that the DoD Advisory Committee on Military Compensation, was 
terminated effective July 11, 2006.
    This committee has concluded their objectives and provided 
necessary and valuable independent advice to the Government's decision 
makers.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Frank Wilson, DoD Committee Management 
Officer, 703-601-2554.

    Dated: June 20, 2006.
L.M. Bynum,
OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
