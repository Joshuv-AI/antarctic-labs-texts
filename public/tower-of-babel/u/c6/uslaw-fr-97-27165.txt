SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Amendment to Declaration of Disaster #2976]


State of North Carolina; (And Contiguous Counties in South 
Carolina)

    The above-numbered declaration is hereby amended to extend the 
filing deadline for physical damage to November 5, 1997.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for economic injury is May 5, 1998.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: October 5, 1997.
Aida Alvarez,
Administrator.
