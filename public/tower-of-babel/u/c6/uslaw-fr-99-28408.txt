[DA No. 99-2245; MM Docket No. 99-234; RM-9645]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Hunt, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 260A at Hunt, Texas, in response 
to a petition filed by Hunt Radio Broadcasting Company. See 64 FR 
36323, July 6, 1999. The coordinates for Channel 260A at Hunt, Texas, 
are 30-07-18 NL and 99-25-39 WL. There is a site restriction 10.7 
kilometers (6.6 miles) northwest of the community. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated. A filing window for Channel 260A at Hunt 
will not be opened at this time. Instead, the issue of opening a filing 
window for this channel will be addressed by the Commission in a 
subsequent order.

DATES: Effective December 6, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
