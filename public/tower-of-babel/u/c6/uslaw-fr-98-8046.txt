COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Proposed Additions to the Procurement List; Correction

    In the document appearing on page 9999, FR Doc. 98-5095, in the 
issue of February 27, 1998, in the first column, the service listed as 
Janitorial/Custodial, Marine Corps Air Base, Camp Pendleton, California 
should read Janitorial/Custodial, (including Fallbrook Naval Ordinance 
Center), Camp Pendleton, California.
G. John Heyer,
General Counsel.
