DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
 

National Register of Historic Places; Notification of Pending 
Nominations

    Nominations for the following properties being consider for listing 
in the National Register were received by the National Park Service 
before August 13, 1994. Pursuant to Sec. 60.13 of 36 CFR Part 60 
written comments concerning the significance of these properties under 
the National Register criteria for evaluation may be forwarded to the 
National Register, National Park Service, P.O. Box 37127, Washington, 
D.C. 20013-7127. Written comments should be submitted by September 7, 
1994.
Antoinette J. Lee,
Acting Chief of Registration, National Register.
