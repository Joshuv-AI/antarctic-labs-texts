Arsenic Treatment Demonstrations

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (USEPA) plans to 
conduct the second phase of a demonstration program on the treatment 
(reduction and/or removal) of arsenic in drinking water. The notice on 
the first phase of the program was published on

[[Page 19209]]

March 28, 2002 (67 FR 14951). The USEPA recently promulgated a standard 
that limits arsenic concentrations in drinking water to 10 ug/l. 
Through this demonstration program, the USEPA intends to identify and 
evaluate the ability of commercially available technologies and 
engineering or other approaches to cost effectively meet the new 
standard in small water systems (http://www.epa.gov/ORD/NRMRL/arsenic/.
