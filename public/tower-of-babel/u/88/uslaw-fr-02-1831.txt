January 18, 2002.
    a. Date and Time of Meeting: January 24, 2002, 12 noon EST.
    b. FERC Contact: Tom Dean at (202) 219-2778; 
[email protected] or John Costello at (202) 219-2914; 
[email protected].
    c. Purpose of the Teleconference: As follow-up to discussions

the Raquette River, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, the New 
York State Historic Preservation Office, and the Advisory Council on

consultation with the St. Regis Mohawk Tribe.

    A. Introduction, Recognition of Participants, Teleconference 
Objectives
    B. Discussion of PA, Appendices, and License Orders
    C. Summary of meeting
    D. Follow-up actions
    E. Information regarding the teleconference including the toll free 
telephone number will be provided later.
