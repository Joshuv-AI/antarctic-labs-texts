DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration


Modernization Transition Committee (MTC); Meeting

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Date: September 14, 1995 from 8:00 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Place: This meeting will take place at the Portland Hilton Hotel, 921 
S.W. Sixth Avenue, Portland, OR.

Status: The meeting will be open to the public. On September 14, 1995, 
10:15 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. will be set aside for oral comments or 
questions from the public. Approximately 50 seats will be available on 
a first-come first-served basis for the public.

Matters to be Considered: This meeting will cover: A Fire Weather 
Presentation, a briefing on the status of Department of Commerce review 
of the NRC Study, consultation on final Consolidation Certifications 
for WSOs Los Angeles and Galveston, and proposed Consolidation 
certifications for WSOs Oklahoma City, Phoenix, Tulsa and New Orleans.

Contact Person for More Information: Mr. Nicholas Scheller, National 
Weather Service, Modernization Staff, 1325 East-West Highway, SSMC2, 
Silver Spring, Maryland 20910. Telephone: (301) 713-0454.

    Dated: August 21, 1995.
Nicholas R. Scheller,
Manager, National Implementation Staff.
