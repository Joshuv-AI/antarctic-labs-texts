DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-40,307]


Universal Furniture Limited, Goldsboro, North Carolina; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was

[[Page 113]]

initiated on October 29, 2001 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Universal Furniture Limited, 
Goldsboro, North Carolina.
    As active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-38,811A, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 12th day of December, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
