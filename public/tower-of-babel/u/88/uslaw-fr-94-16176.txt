LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION

 

Correction of Notice of Intent To Designate Recipient for the 
Provision of State Support for the Delivery of Legal Services in the 
State of Louisiana

AGENCY: Legal Services Corporation.
ACTION: Correction of notice of intention to award grant.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In a Notice published on May 27, 1994 (59 FR 27585), the Legal 
Services Corporation announced its intention to make a grant to 
Louisiana Legal Consortium, Inc. to provide state support services to 
the Legal Services Corporation's recipient programs in the State of 
Louisiana. The intended grant for the remainder of 1994 was erroneously 
stated as $94,314. The correct amount is $78,596.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: John Tull, Director, Office of Program 
Services, (202) 336-7264.

    Dated: June 28, 1994.

John A. Tull,
Director, Office of Program Services.
