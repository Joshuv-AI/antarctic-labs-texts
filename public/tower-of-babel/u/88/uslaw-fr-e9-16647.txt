770-488-3024 and teleconference.

with provisions set forth in Section 552b(c) (4) and (6), Title 5 
U.S.C., and the Determination of the Director, Management Analysis 
and Services Office, CDC, pursuant to Public Law 92-463.

to ``RFA-DP09-101SUPP09: Health Promotion ans Disease Prevention 
ResearchCenters: Special Interest Project Competitive Supplements

    For More Information Contact: Brenda Colley Gilbert, PhD,
