FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Farm Credit Administration Board; Special Meeting

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the Government in the 
Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 552b(e)(3)), of the special meeting of the Farm 
Credit Administration Board (Board).

[[Page 62246]]


Date and Time: The special meeting of the Board was held at the offices 
of the Farm Credit Administration in McLean, Virginia, on September 26, 
2002, from 3:30 p.m. until such time as the Board concluded its 
business.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jeanette C. Brinkley, Acting Secretary 
to the Farm Credit Administration Board, (703) 883-4009, TTY (703)883-
4056.

ADDRESSES: Farm Credit Administration, 1501 Farm Credit Drive, McLean, 
Virginia 22102-5090.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This meeting of the Board was open to the 
public (limited space available). The matter considered at the meeting 
was:

Open Session

    [sbull] New Business--Other
    [sbull] FY 2003 Revised Budget and FY 2004 Proposed Budget.

    Dated: October 1, 2002.
Jeanette C. Brinkley,
Acting Secretary, Farm Credit Administration Board.
