FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM

Change in Bank Control Notices; Formations of, Acquisitions by, 
and Mergers of Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 97-4154) published on pages 
7783 abd 7784 of the issue for Thursday, February 20, 1997.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis heading, the entry 
for TCF Financial Corporation, Minneapolis, Minnesota, is revised to 
read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis (Karen L. Grandstrand, Vice 
President) 250 Marquette Avenue, Minneapolis, Minnesota 55480-2171:
    1. TCF Financial Corporation, Minneapolis, Minnesota; to acquire 
TCF Minnesota Financial Services, Inc., Minneapolis, Minneosta, and 
thereby engage in holding record title to mortgages securing loans, 
pursuant to Sec.  225.25(b)(1) of the Board's Regulation Y.
    Comments on this application must be received by March 14, 1997.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, February 25, 
1997.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
