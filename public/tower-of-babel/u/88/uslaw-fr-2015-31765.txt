    Place: Virtual meeting via teleconference hosted by NSF.

    The NSF is located at 4201 Wilson Blvd., Arlington, VA 22230.

    Type of Meeting: Closed.
    Contact Person: Charles Liarakos, National Science Foundation, 4201 
Wilson Boulevard, Room 605, Arlington, VA 22230; Tel No.: (703) 292-
8400.

Committee Subcommittee on NEON Scope Impacts.
