Employment and Training Administration

Asia Perez, New York, NY; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 15, 2001 in response to a worker petition, 
which was filed by the company on behalf of workers as Asia Perez, New 
York, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, the 7th day of February 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
