SUPPLEMENTAL INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The Export Export Administration Regulations (EAR) set forth the 
licensing policy for exports and reexports of encryption commodities 
and software to U.S. subsidiaries, insurance companies, health and 
medical end-users, on-line merchants and foreign commercial firms. The 
information required is used in making licensing decisions. A recent 
rulemaking reduced paperwork by eliminating the following: (1) key 
recovery agent requirements; (2) business and marketing plan 
requirement for non-recovery items; and (3) six month progress reviews.
