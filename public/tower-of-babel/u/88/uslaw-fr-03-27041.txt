DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

International Trade Administration


University of California, San Diego; Notice of Withdrawal of 
Application for Duty-Free Entry of Scientific Instruments

    The University of California, San Diego has withdrawn Docket Number 
03-047 an application for duty-free entry of Wave Measurement 
Instrumentation/Equipment. We have discontinued processing in 
accordance with section 301.5(g) of 15 CFR part 301.

Gerald A. Zerdy,
Program Manager, Statutory Import Programs Staff.
