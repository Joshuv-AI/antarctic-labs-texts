Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 124--Gramercy, Louisiana; Authorization 
of Production Activity; Frank's International, LLC (Line Pipe With 
Tubular Joints); New Iberia and Lafayette, Louisiana

    On February 19, 2020, Frank's International, LLC submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facilities within FTZ 124, in New Iberia and Lafayette, Louisiana.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (85 FR 12499, March 3, 2020). On June 
18, 2020, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including Sec.  
400.14.

    Dated: June 18, 2020.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
