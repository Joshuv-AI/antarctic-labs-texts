RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD

Public Hearing

    Notice is hereby given that the Railroad Retirement Board, acting 
through its designated Hearings Examiner, will hold a hearing on May 
22, 1995, 9:00 a.m., at the Board's meeting room on the 8th floor of 
its headquarters building, 844 North Rush Street, Chicago, Illinois, 
60611. The hearing will be held at the request of the CSX Intermodal 
for the purpose of taking evidence relating to status of their company 
under the Railroad Retirement and Railroad Unemployment Insurance Acts.
    The entire hearing will be open to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Thomas W. Sadler, Assistant General 
Counsel (312) 751-4513, TDD (312) 751-4701.

    Dated: April 21, 1995.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
