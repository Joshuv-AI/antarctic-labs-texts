The Lubrizol Corporation, Mountaintop Manufacturing, Mountaintop, 
PA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 7, 2005 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers at The Lubrizol Corporation, Mountaintop 
Manufacturing, Mountaintop, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of April, 2005.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
