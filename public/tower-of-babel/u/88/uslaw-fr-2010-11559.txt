DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Rural Housing Service


Notice of Intent to Hold Public Forums to Solicit Feedback From 
the Public Regarding the Section 523 Mutual Self-Help Housing Program

AGENCY: Rural Housing Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Rural Housing Service, USDA published a document in the 
Federal Register of February 2, 2010, concerning upcoming public forums 
and request for comments regarding the Section 523 Mutual Self-Help 
Housing Program. Notice is hereby given that the forums scheduled for 
June 4, 2010 in Vermont and June 11, 2010 in Iowa have been cancelled.
