Administrative Declaration of a Disaster for the State of Texas

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a notice of an Administrative declaration of a 
disaster for the State of Texas dated 02/13/2020.
    Incident: Watson Grinding and Manufacturing Facility Explosion.
    Incident Period: 01/24/2020.

DATES: Issued on 02/13/2020.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: 04/13/2020.
    Economic Injury (EIDL) Loan Application Deadline Date: 11/13/2020.

ADDRESSES: Submit completed loan applications to: U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Processing and Disbursement Center, 14925 Kingsport 
Road, Fort Worth, TX 76155.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: A. Escobar, Office of Disaster 
Assistance, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street SW, 
Suite 6050, Washington, DC 20416, (202) 205-6734.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given that as a result of 
the

[[Page 10506]]

Administrator's disaster declaration, applications for disaster loans 
may be filed at the address listed above or other locally announced 
locations.

by the disaster:

Primary Counties: Harris

    Texas: Brazoria, Chambers, Fort Bend, Galveston, Liberty, 
Montgomery, Waller.
