SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

A. Purpose

    The GSA requires contractors holding Multiple Award Schedule 
Contracts to identify in their GSA price lists those products that they 
market commercially that have environmental attributes in accordance 
with GSAR clause 552.238-78. The identification of these products will 
enable Federal agencies to maximize the use of these products and meet 
the responsibilities expressed in statutes and executive order.
