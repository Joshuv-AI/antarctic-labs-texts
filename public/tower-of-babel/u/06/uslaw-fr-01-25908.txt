POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Mailing Facility Visit

AGENCY: Postal Rate Commission.

ACTION: Notice of commission visit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Members of the Commission's staff will visit the Moore 
Business Communication Services' Thurmont, Maryland facility on 
November 6, 2001, beginning at 8:30 a.m. The purpose of the visit is to 
attend a training session that reviews the preparation of workshared 
First-Class Mail.

DATES: The visit is scheduled for Tuesday, November 6, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen L. Sharfman, General Counsel, 
Postal Rate Commission, Suite 300, 1333 H Street NW., Washington, DC 
20268-0001, 202-789-6820.

    Dated: October 9, 2001.
Steven W. Williams,
Acting Secretary.
