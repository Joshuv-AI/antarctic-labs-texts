NATIONAL FOUNDATION ON THE ARTS AND THE HUMANITIES

National Endowment for the Arts


Combined Arts Advisory Panel; Notice of Correction

    This is to announce a correction to the meeting notice for the 
Combined Arts Advisory Panel, Design section, published in the Federal 
Register on Friday, May 14, 1999. The open session, previously 
announced as being from 10:45 a.m. to 4 p.m. on June 25th, will, 
instead, be held from 12:45 p.m. to 4 p.m. on June 25th.

    Dated: May 28, 1999.
Kathy Plowitz-Worden,
Panel Coordinator.
