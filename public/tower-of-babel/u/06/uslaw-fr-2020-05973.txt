Notice of Rail Energy Transportation Advisory Committee Meeting

    On March 11, 2020, the Board provided notice of a meeting of the 
Rail Energy Transportation Advisory Committee (RETAC), to be held on 
April 21, 2020. This meeting will be postponed. Notice of the 
rescheduled meeting will be announced at a later date. For further 
information, please contact Kristen Nunnally at (202) 245-0312 or 
[email protected].
