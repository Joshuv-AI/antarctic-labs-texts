DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute of Mental Health; Closed Meetings

Rockville, MD 20857.

5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, Telephone: 301, 443-3936.

Emphasis Panel.

    Place: Parklawn Building, Room 9-101, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, MD 20857.
    Contact Person: Shirley H. Maltz, Parklawn Building, Room 9-101, 
5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, Telephone: 301, 443-3936.

    Committee Name: National Institute of Mental Health Special 
Emphasis Panel.
    Date: December 12, 1995.
    Time: 1 p.m.
    Place: Parklawn Building, Room 9-101, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, MD 20857.
    Contact Person: Shirley H. Maltz, Parklawn Building, Room 9-101, 
5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, Telephone: 301, 443-3936.
    The meetings will be closed in accordance with the provisions 
set forth in secs. 552b(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C. 
Applications and/or proposals and the discussions could reveal 
confidential trade secrets or commercial property such as patentable 
material and personal information concerning individuals associated 
with the applications and/or proposals, the disclosure of which 
would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.

the first meeting due to the urgent need to meet timing limitations 
imposed by the review and funding cycle.
