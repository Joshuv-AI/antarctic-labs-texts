DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


DOE Response to Recommendation 2012-2 of the Defense Nuclear 
Facilities Safety Board, Hanford Tank Farms Flammable Gas Safety 
Strategy; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice; Correction

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy (DOE) published a document in the 
Federal Register of January 22, 2013, announcing the DOE's Response to 
Recommendation 2012-2 of the Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board, 
Hanford Tank Farms Flammable Gas Safety Strategy. This document 
corrects an error in that notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Requests for additional information or 
copies of the information collection instrument and instructions should 
be directed to Steven Petras at [email protected].

Correction

    In the Federal Register of January 22, 2013, in FR Doc. 2013-01132, 
78 FR 4404, please make the following correction:
    On page 4404, first column, add a DATES section before ADDRESSES to 
read as follows:

DATES: Comments, data, views, or arguments concerning the Secretary's 
response are due on or before February 21, 2013.

Mari-Josette Campagnone,
Departmental Representative to the Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety 
Board, Office of Health, Safety and Security.
