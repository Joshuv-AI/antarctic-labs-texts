DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER97-4030-000]


The Cincinnati Gas & Electric Company, PSI Energy, Inc.; Notice 
of Filing

August 26, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 31, 1997, The Cincinnati Gas & Electric 
Company (CG&E) and PSI Energy, Inc. (PSI), (hereinafter collectively 
referred to as Cinergy Operating Companies), tendered for filing 
pursuant to the Commission's November 15, 1996, Order issued in Docket 
Nos. ER96-2504-000 and ER96-2506-000 approving, among other things, 
Cinergy's market-based rate Power Sales Tariff, FERC Electric Tariff, 
First Revised Volume No. 4, Cinergy's revised quarterly transaction 
report for the calendar quarter ending June 30, 1997.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before September 8, 1997. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion 
to intervene. Copies of the filings are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
