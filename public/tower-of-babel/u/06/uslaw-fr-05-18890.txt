Airspace Designations; Incorporation by Reference; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects an error in the effective date contained 
in a Final Rule that was published in the Federal Register on September 
1, 2005 (70 FR 52012). That Final Rule amended Title 14 Code of Federal 
Regulations (14 CFR) part 71 relating to airspace designations to 
reflect the approval by the Director of the Federal Register of the 
incorporation by reference of FHA Order 7400.9N, Airspace Designations 
and Reporting Points.

DATES: These regulations are effective September 15, 2005. The 
incorporation by reference of FAA Order 7400.9N is approved by the 
Director of the Federal Register as of September 15, 2005, through 
September 15, 2006.
