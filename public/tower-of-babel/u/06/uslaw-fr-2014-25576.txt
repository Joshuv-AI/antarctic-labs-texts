SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

[File No. 500-1]


In the Matter of Icon Public Ltd. Co.; Order Withdrawing Trading 
Suspension

October 22, 2014.
    The Securities and Exchange Commission hereby withdraws the trading 
suspension order as to the securities of Icon Public Ltd. Co. 
(``ICLR'') entered October 22, 2014 (``October 22, 2014 Order'').

    This order shall be effective immediately.

    By the Commission.
Kevin M. O'Neill,
Deputy Secretary.
