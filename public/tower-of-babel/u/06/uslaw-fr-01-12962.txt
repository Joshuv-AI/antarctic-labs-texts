Extension of Scoping Period for the Lower Potomac River 
Coordinated Management Plan/Environmental Assessment

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Milwaukee Field Office, Interior.

ACTION: Extension of scoping period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On April 30, 2001, the Bureau of Land Management (BLM), 
Milwaukee Field Office, filed a Notice of Intent to prepare a 
coordinated management plan (CMP) in the Federal Register (66 FR 21412-
3) for the Lower Potomac River project area located in Charles County, 
Maryland. The CMP will be prepared to determine whether and where to 
acquire land within the project area.
    In order to provide the public with a better opportunity to 
identify issues and concerns, the scoping period will be extended to 
June 29, 2001. See the April 30, 2001, notice for more information 
about the project.

DATES: Comments should be postmarked no later than June 29, 2001 to 
ensure the issues they raise are considered in the plan.

ADDRESSES: Comments may be mailed to the following addresses: James W. 
Dryden, Field Office Manager, Milwaukee Field Office, P.O. Box 631, 
Milwaukee, Wisconsin 53201-0631 or Ed Ruda, Project Leader Manager, 
Eastern States Office, 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 
22153.

1663, or by electronic mail at [email protected], or Howard Levine, 
Planning and Environmental Coordinator at (414) 297-4463, or by
