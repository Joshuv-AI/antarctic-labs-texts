Harvard Illinois Bancorp, Inc., Harvard, Illinois; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on February 12, 2010, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of Harvard Savings Bank, 
Harvard, Illinois, to convert to the stock form of organization. Copies 
of the application are available for inspection by appointment (phone 
number: 202-906-5922 or e-mail [email protected]) at the 
Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the 
OTS Central Regional Office, 1 South Wacker Drive, Suite 2000, Chicago, 
Illinois 60606.

    Dated: February 19, 2010.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
