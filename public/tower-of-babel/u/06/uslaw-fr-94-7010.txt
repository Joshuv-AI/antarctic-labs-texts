NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION
 

Advisory Committee for Polar Programs; Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting.

    Name: Advisory Committee for Polar Programs.
    Date and Time: April 14 and 15, 1994, 8:30 a.m.-5 p.m. each day.
    Place: Room 340, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson 
Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Dr. Dennis S. Peacock, Science Section Head, 
Office of Polar Programs, room 755, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230. Telephone: (703) 306-
1033.
    Minutes: May be obtained from the contact person listed above.
    Purpose of Meeting: Serves to provide expert advice to the U.S. 
Antarctic Programs and the Arctic Program, including advice on 
science programs, polar operations support, budgetary planning, and 
polar coordination and information.
    Agenda: The OPP Advisory Committee meets on April 14/15 to 
discuss the following agenda topics--FY 94/95 Budget, Congressional 
Update, Organization and Long Range Planning (LRP). The LRP topic 
will include presentations on Facilities, operations/logistics and 
discussion of science priorities.

    Dated: March 21, 1994.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
