DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. CP90-1372-000 et al.]

 

Altamont Gas Transmission Company; Site Visit

August 18, 1994.
    On August 30, and 31, 1994, members of the environmental staff of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) will conduct aerial and 
ground inspections with the applicant in the southwest portion of 
Wyoming along the route certificated for the Altamont Gas Transmission 
Company in the above docket. This will include the South Pass area of 
the project. All interested persons are welcome to attend; however, 
they must provide their own transportation.
    For further information, contact Robert Arvedlund, Chief, 
Environmental Review and Compliance Branch I, at (202) 208-0091.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
