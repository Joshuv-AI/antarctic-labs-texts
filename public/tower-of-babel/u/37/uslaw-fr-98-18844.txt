POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Announcement of Visit

SUMMARY: Representatives of E-Stamp Corporation will visit the 
Commission to present a briefing on a system for electronic generation 
of postage indicia.

DATES: The date of the visit is Thursday, July 16, 1998, beginning at 
2:30 p.m.

ADDRESSES: Postal Rate Commission (Conference Room), 1333 H Street, NW, 
Suite 300, Washington, DC 20268-0001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Stephen L. Sharfman, General Counsel, Postal Rate Commission, Suite 
300, 1333 H Street, NW, Washington, DC 20268-001, (202) 789-6820.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: During this visit, representatives of E-
Stamp Corporation will brief the Commission on a system for electronic 
generation of postage indicia.

    Dated: July 10, 1998.
Cyril J. Pittack,
Acting Secretary.
