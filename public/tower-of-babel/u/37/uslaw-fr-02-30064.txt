LaGrange Foundry, LaGrange, MO; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 28, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at LaGrange Foundry, LaGrange, Missouri.
    The investigation revealed that there is an existing petition (TA-
42,245) for the subject firm workers which was instituted on October 
15, 2002.
    Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 7th day of November 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
