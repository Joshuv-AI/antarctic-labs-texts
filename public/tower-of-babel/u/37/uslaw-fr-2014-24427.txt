International Trade Administration


Architecture Services Trade Mission to Qatar, November 16-19, 
2014

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Department of Commerce, International Trade 
Administration is amending the Notice published at 79 FR 44157, July 
30, 2014, for the Architecture Services Trade Mission to Qatar, with an 
optional mission stop in Saudi Arabia, from November 16-19, 2014 to 
revise the mission description from executive-led to non-executive led.
