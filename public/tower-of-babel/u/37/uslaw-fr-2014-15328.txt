Self-Regulatory Organizations; NASDAQ OMX PHLX LLC; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Delete the PHOTO Historical Data 
Product From Section IX of the Exchange's Options Fee Schedule

June 25, 2014.
    On May 9, 2014, NASDAQ OMX PHLX LLC (the ``Exchange'' or ``Phlx'') 
filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the ``Commission''), 
pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 
(the ``Act'') \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change 
to delete the PHOTO Historical data product from Section IX of the 
Exchange's Options Fee Schedule. The proposed rule change was published 
for comment in the Federal Register on May 29, 2014.\3\ The Commission 
has not received any comment letters on the proposal. On June 24, 2014, 
the Exchange withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-Phlx-2014-34).
