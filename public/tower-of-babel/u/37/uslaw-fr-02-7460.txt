DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP02-145-001]


Williston Basin Interstate Pipeline Company; Notice of Refund 
Report

March 22, 2002.
    Take notice that on March 15, 2002, Williston Basin Interstate 
Pipeline Company (Williston Basin), tendered for filing with the 
Commission its Refund Report made pursuant to the Commission's Letter 
Order issued February 14, 2002 in Docket No. RP02-145-000.
    Williston Basin states that on March 11, 2002, refunds associated 
with the final reconciliation of the gas supply realignment (GSR) 
amortization account as of January 31, 2002, were sent to applicable 
Rate Schedule FT-1 shippers. These refunds included interest through 
March 11, 2002, in accordance with Section 154.501 of the Commission's 
Regulations.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before March 29, 2002. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection. This filing may also be viewed on the Web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select ``Docket#'' and follow the 
instructions (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). Comments, protests and 
interventions may be filed electronically via the Internet in lieu of 
paper. See, 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the 
Commission's Web site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
