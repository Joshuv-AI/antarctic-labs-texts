DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board


Submission of Comments; Change of Address

    Submission of comments to the Foreign-Trade Zones Board should 
hereafter be directed to Room 4008, rather than to Room 3716, as 
indicated in previous notices. The Foreign-Trade Zones Board office has 
moved from Room 3716 to Room 4008, and all comments and other 
correspondence to the FTZ Board should be submitted to: Foreign-Trade 
Zones Board, U.S. Department of Commerce, 14th & Pennsylvania Avenue 
NW, Room 4008, Washington, D.C. 20230.

    Dated: September 17, 1999.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Acting Executive Secretary.
