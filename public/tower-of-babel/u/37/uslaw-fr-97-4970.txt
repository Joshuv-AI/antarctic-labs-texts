ESI Calistoga GP, Inc. and Caithness Geysers, Inc.; Notice of 
Amendment to Filing

February 24, 1997.
    On February 21, 1997, ESI Calistoga GP, Inc. and Caithness Geysers, 
Inc. tendered for filing an amendment to its January 31, 1997, filing 
in this docket.
    The amendment pertains to the ownership aspects and steam purchase 
agreement of the geothermal small power production facility. No 
determination has been made that the submittal constitutes a complete 
filing.
    Any person desiring to be heard or objecting to the granting of 
qualifying status should file a motion to intervene or protest with the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with rules 211 and 214 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such motions or 
protests must be filed by March 7, 1997, and must be served on the 
applicants. Protests will be considered by the Commission in 
determining the appropriate action to be taken but will not serve to 
make protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to 
become a party must file a petition to intervene. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
