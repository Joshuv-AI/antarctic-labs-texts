FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Sunshine Act Meeting

Agency Holding the Meeting: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve 
System.

Federal Register Citation of Previous Announcement: 62 FR 18629-30, 
April 16, 1997.

Previously Announced Time and Date of the Meeting: 11:00 a.m., Monday, 
April 21, 1997.
