Lending and Investment

Correction

    In proposed rule document 01-27329 beginning on page 55131 in the 
issue of Thursday, November 1, 2001, make the following correction:


Sec. 560.42  [Corrected]

    On page 55138, in the table for Sec. 560.42, in the first column, 
entries (2) and (3) should read as follows:
    ``(2) Other obligations of a governmental entity (e.g., revenue 
bonds) that hold one of the four highest investment grade ratings by a 
nationally recognized rating agency or that are nonrated but of 
investment quality''.
    ``(3) Obligations of a governmental entity that do not qualify 
under any other paragraph but are approved by your Regional Director''.
