SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3528, Amdt. 1]


State of Ohio

    In accordance with the notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective July 
11, 2003, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to establish 
the incident period for this disaster as beginning on July 4, 2003 and 
continuing through July 11, 2003.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is September 15, 2003, and for 
economic injury the deadline is April 15, 2004.


(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: July 23, 2003.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
