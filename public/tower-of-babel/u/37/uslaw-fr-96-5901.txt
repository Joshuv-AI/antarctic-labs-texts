FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-30; RM-8762]


Television Broadcasting Services; Antigo, WI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition filed by Robert 
J. Cox d/b/a Native American Television Company proposing the allotment 
of UHF Television Channel 46 to Antigo, Wisconsin. The channel can be 
allotted to Antigo without a site restriction at coordinates 45-08-54 
and 89-09-00. Canadian concurrence will be requested for the allotment 
of Channel 46 at Antigo.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before April 29, 1996, and reply 
comments on or before May 14, 1996.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner, as follows: Robert J. Cox, Native American 
Television Company, 200 Fillmore Street, Kaukauna, Wisconsin 54130.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
