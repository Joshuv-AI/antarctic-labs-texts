Modernizing Electricity Market Design Notice of Technical 
Conference on Resource Adequacy in the Evolving Electricity Sector: ISO 
New England Inc

    Take notice that the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
(Commission) will convene a Commissioner-led technical conference 
regarding wholesale markets administered by ISO New England Inc. in the 
above-referenced proceeding on Tuesday, May 25, 2021, from 
approximately 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern time. The conference will 
be held remotely. The Commission will issue a supplemental notice 
providing the agenda for the technical conference.
    The conference will be open for the public to attend remotely. 
There is no fee for attendance. Information on this event will be 
posted on the Calendar of Events on the Commission's website, 
www.ferc.gov, prior to the event.
    The conference will be transcribed. Transcripts will be available 
for a fee from Ace Reporting at (202) 347-3700.
    Commission conferences are accessible under section 508 of the 
Rehabilitation Act of 1973. For accessibility accommodations, please 
send an email to [email protected] or call toll free (866) 208-
3372 (voice) or (202) 208-8659 (TTY), or send a fax to (202) 208-2106 
with the required accommodations.
    For more information about this technical conference, please 
contact David Rosner at [email protected] or (202) 502-8479, or 
Emma Nicholson at [email protected] or (202) 502-8741. For legal 
information, please contact Meghan O'Brien at meghan.o'[email protected]

Sarah McKinley at [email protected] or (202) 502-8368.
