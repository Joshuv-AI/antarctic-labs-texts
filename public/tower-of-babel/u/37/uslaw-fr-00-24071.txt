DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Golden Gate National Recreation Area; Extension of Comment Period 
to Notice of Proposed Year-Round Closure at Fort Funston

    A notice of proposed year-round closure at Fort Funston in the 
Golden Gate National Recreation Area was published in the Federal 
Register on July 18, 2000 (65 FR 44546), and amended on July 26, 2000 
(65 FR 45988). The comment period closed on September 18, 2000. This 
document extends the comment period. Public comments on this notice 
must be received by October 6, 2000.

    Dated: September 15, 2000.
Brian O'Neill,
Superintendent, Golden Gate National Recreation Area.
