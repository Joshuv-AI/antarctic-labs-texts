DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Equity and Excellence Commission

AGENCY: U.S. Department of Education.

ACTION: Notice; Advisory Committee Meeting Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Education gives notice of the cancellation 
of the Meeting of the Equity and Excellence Commission scheduled for 
March 29, 2012 and announced in the Federal Register on March 14, 2012 
in Vol. 77 No. 50.
    The meeting will be rescheduled for a date to be announced in the 
future.
