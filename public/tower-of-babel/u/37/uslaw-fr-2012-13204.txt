Approval of Negative Declaration and Withdrawal of Large 
Municipal Waste Combustors State Plan for Designated Facilities and 
Pollutants: Illinois

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: EPA is proposing to approve Illinois' negative declaration and 
request for EPA withdrawal of its 111(d)/129 State Plan to control air 
pollutants from ``Large Municipal Waste Combustors'' (LMWC). On 
February 1, 2012, the Illinois Environmental Protection Agency 
submitted a letter of certification to EPA that the only designated 
facility in the State Plan ceased operation and is completely shut down 
and requested that EPA withdraw the State Plan implementing the 
emission guidelines for LMWCs.
