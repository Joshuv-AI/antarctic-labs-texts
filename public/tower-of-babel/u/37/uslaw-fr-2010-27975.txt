Lancaster & Chester Railroad, LLC--Acquisition and Operation 
Exemption--Line of Lancaster & Chester Railway Company

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board, DOT.

ACTION: Correction to Notice of Exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the Notice of Exemption 
served and published in the Federal Register on Friday, October 15, 
2010 (75 FR 63,532-33). In the notice, noncarrier Lancaster & Chester 
Railroad, LLC (L&C Railroad) seeks to acquire and operate under 49 CFR 
1150.31 approximately 62 miles of rail line owned by Class III rail 
carrier Lancaster & Chester Railway Company. The correction relates to 
the name of the connecting railroad in the description of the rail 
line. The notice incorrectly indicated that L&C Railroad would connect 
with Consolidated Rail Corporation at former Survey Station 0+06 
(milepost SG-346+2210) in Chester County, S.C. Instead, L&C Railroad 
will connect with CSX Transportation, Inc. at the specified station in 
Chester County.
