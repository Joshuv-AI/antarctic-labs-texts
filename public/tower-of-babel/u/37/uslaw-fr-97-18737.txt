FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-3; RM-8945]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Bend, OR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Sunriver Broadcasting 
Company, allots Channel 259A to Bend,

[[Page 38032]]

OR, as the community's sixth local commercial FM service. See 62 FR 
3852, January 27, 1997. Channel 259A can be allotted to Bend in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements without the imposition of a site restriction, at 
coordinates 44-03-30 North Latitude; 121-18-30 West Longitude. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective August 25, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications will open on August 25, 1997, and close on September 25, 
1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
