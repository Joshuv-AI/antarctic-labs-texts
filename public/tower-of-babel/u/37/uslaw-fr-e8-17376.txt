Lakeland Mold Co. Stow, OH; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 27, 2008, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Lakeland 
Mold Co., Stow, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 22nd day of July 2008.
 Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
