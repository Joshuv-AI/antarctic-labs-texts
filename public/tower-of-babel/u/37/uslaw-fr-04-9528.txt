[Public Notice 4663]


Advisory Committee on Labor Diplomacy; Notice of Cancellation of 
Meeting

    The Advisory Committee on Labor Diplomacy (ACLD) has cancelled its 
meeting scheduled for Monday, April 26, 2004 at 9 a.m. in room 1107, 
U.S. Department of State, 2201 C Street, NW., Washington, DC 20520. The 
meeting has been postponed until further notice.

    Dated: April 21, 2004.
Robert Hogan,
Director, Bureau of Democracy, Human Rights and Labor, Department of 
State.
