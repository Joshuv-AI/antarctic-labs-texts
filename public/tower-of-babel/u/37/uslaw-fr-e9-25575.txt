NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Business and Operations Advisory Committee; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Business and Operations Advisory Committee (9556).
    Date/Time:
     November 18, 2009; 1 p.m. to 5:45 p.m. (EST).
     November 19, 2009; 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. (EST).
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, 
Stafford I, Room 375.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Patty Balanga, National Science Foundation, 4201 
Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230 (703) 292-8100.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice concerning issues related 
to the oversight, integrity, development and enhancement of NSF's 
business operations.
    Agenda:

November 18, 2009

Welcome/Introductions;
OIRM/CIO/BFA Updates;
FastLane/Grants.gov/System-to-System Capabilities;
The American Recovery and Reinvestment Act-Reporting/Update;
NSF Workforce Management;
iTrak-Financial System Modernization.

November 19, 2009

NSF Strategic Plan Revision-2010-2015;
Future NSF-2013 Lease Expiration;
Committee discussion and prepare for Meeting with NSF Deputy 
Director;
Discussion with Deputy Director;
Closing Committee Discussion/Wrap-Up.

    Dated: October 20, 2009.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
