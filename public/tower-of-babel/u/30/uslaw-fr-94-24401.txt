GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION
 

Interagency Committee for Medical Records (ICMR) Cancellation of 
Medical Standard Form

agency: General Services Administration.

action: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: Because of low usage the cut sheet version of the following 
Standard Form is cancelled:

SF 502, Medical Record--Narrative Summary (Clinical Resume) (NSN 
7540-00-634-4114)

    The two-part set of the SF 502 is still available from the Federal 
Supply Service.

dates: Effective on October 4, 1994.

for further information contact: Ms. Barbara Williams, General Services 
Administration, (202) 501-0581.

    Dated: September 20, 1994.
Theodore D. Freed,
Chief, Forms Management Branch.
