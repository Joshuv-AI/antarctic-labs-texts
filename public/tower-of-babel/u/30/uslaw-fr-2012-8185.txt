Payment of Premiums

CFR Correction

    In Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 1927 to End, 
revised as of July 1, 2011, on page 933, Sec.  4007.5 is reinstated to 
read as follows:


Sec.  4007.5  Date of filing.

    The PBGC applies the rules in subpart C of part 4000 of this 
chapter to determine the date that a submission under this part was 
filed with the PBGC.
