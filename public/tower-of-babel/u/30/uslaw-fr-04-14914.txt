Hawk Motors; A Division of the Hawk Corporation, Alton, IL; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 20, 2004 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Hawk Motors, a 
division of the Hawk Corporation, Alton, Illinois.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 9th day of June, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
