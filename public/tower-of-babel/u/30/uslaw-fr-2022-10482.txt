LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE:  The Legal Services Corporation's Board of Directors 
will meet remotely on Monday, May 23, 2022. The meeting will commence 
at 4:00 p.m. EDT, and will continue until the conclusion of the Board's 
agenda.

PLACE: 
    Public Notice of Virtual Meeting: LSC will conduct the May 23, 2022 
meeting via Zoom.
    Public Observation: The Board meeting will be open to public 
observation via Zoom. Members of the public who wish to participate 
remotely in the public proceedings may do so by following the 
directions provided below.
