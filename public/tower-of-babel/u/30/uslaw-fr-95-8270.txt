Mississippi River Transmission Corp.; Notice of Proposed Change 
in FERC Gas Tariff

March 30, 1995.
    Take notice that on March 28, 1995, Mississippi River Transmission 
Corporation (MRT) submitted for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, 
Third Revised Volume No. 1, Second Revised Sheet No. 9, proposed to be 
effective April 1, 1995.
    MRT states that the purpose of the filing is to retire Substitute 
First Revised Sheet No. 9 which provided for the disposition of Account 
Nos. 191 and 858 costs, and reserve Sheet No. 9 for future use.
    MRT states that a copy of the filing has been mailed to all of its 
former jurisdictional sales customers and the State Commissions of 
Arkansas, Illinois and Missouri.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capital Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426, in accordance with Sections 385.211 and 385.214 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211, 385.214). 
All such motions or protests should be filed on or before April 6, 
1995. Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
