Federal Management Regulation; Redesignations of Federal

AGENCY:  Public Buildings Service (P), GSA

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  The attached bulletin announces the redesignation of a 
Federal Building.

Expiration Date:  This bulletin expires September 20, 2007. However, 
the building redesignation announced by this bulletin will remain in 
effect until canceled or superseded.

NW., Washington, DC 20405, e-mail at [email protected], (202) 501-
1100.
