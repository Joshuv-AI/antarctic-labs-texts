SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2760; Amdt. #4]


California; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    The above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended, effective 
immediately, to establish the incident period for this disaster as 
beginning on January 3, 1995 and continuing through February 10, 1995.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for economic injury is October 10, 1995. The 
deadline for filing applications for physical damage expired on March 
13, 1995.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: March 14, 1995.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
