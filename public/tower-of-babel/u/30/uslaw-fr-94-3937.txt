DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

North Pacific Fishery Management Council; Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The North Pacific Fishery Management Council's Halibut Working 
Group will hold a meeting on March 10-11, 1994, in NMFS main conference 
room on the 4th floor of the Federal Building, 907 West 9th Street, 
Juneau, AK. The meeting will begin at 1 p.m. on March 10, and adjourn 
by 4 p.m. on March 11.
    The Working Group will begin to develop appropriate elements and 
options for a proposed moratorium on the entry of new charter vessels 
into the halibut fishery off Alaska.
    The meeting is physically accessible to people with disabilities. 
Requests for sign language interpretation or other auxiliary aids 
should be directed to Judy Willoughby, (907) 271-2809, at least 5 days 
prior to the meeting date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Witherell, North Pacific Fishery 
Management Council, P.O. Box 103136, Anchorage, AK 99510; telephone: 
(907) 271-2809.

    Dated: February 15, 1994.
David S. Crestin,
Acting Director, Office of Fisheries Conservation and Management, 
National Marine Fisheries Service.
