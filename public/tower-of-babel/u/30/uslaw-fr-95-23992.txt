FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-85; RM-8482]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Falmouth and Mashpee, MA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document reallots Channel 266A from Falmouth, 
Massachusetts, to Mashpee, Massachusetts, and modifies the license for 
Station WUNZ to specify Mashpee as its community of license in response 
to a petition filed by Leapfrog Radio Partnership. See 59 FR 39317, 
August 2, 1994. The coordinates for Channel 266A at Mashpee are 41-34-
45 and 70-30-45. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 6, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

and Order, MM Docket No. 94-85, adopted September 15, 1995, and
