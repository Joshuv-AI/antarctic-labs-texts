DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Change to Advisory Circular 29-2A, Certification of Transport 
Category Rotorcraft

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance and availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Change 3, Advisory Circular (AC) 29-2A, Certification of 
Transport Category Rotorcraft, was issued to bring the AC up-to-date 
with various rule changes to 14 CFR Part 29. As part of the FAA effort 
to achieve national standardization in rotorcraft certification, the AC 
serves as a ready reference for manufacturers, modifiers, FAA design 
evaluation engineers, flight test engineers, and engineering flight 
test pilots and has been harmonized with the Joint Aviation Authority 
(JAA) to establish common guidance for the U.S. and for JAA member 
nations. The AC material has no legally binding status and must be 
treated as advisory only.

DATES: Change 3, AC 29-2A, was issued by the Rotorcraft Directorate, 
Aircraft Certification Service, on June 1, 1995.
