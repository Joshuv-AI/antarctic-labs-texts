Self-Regulatory Organizations; Nasdaq MRX, LLC; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend Options 7, Section 7 To Add 
Market Data Fees

October 31, 2022.
    On August 25, 2022, Nasdaq MRX, LLC (``MRX'') filed with the 
Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), pursuant to 
Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 \1\ and Rule 
19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to assess market data fees. 
The proposed rule change was published for comment in the Federal 
Register on September 14, 2022.\3\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 15 U.S.C. 78s(b)(1).
    \2\ 17 CFR 240.19b-4.
    \3\ See Securities Exchange Act Release No. 95708 (September 8, 
2022), 87 FR 56457.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    On October 14, 2022, MRX withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-MRX-
2022-14).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \4\ 17 CFR 200.30-3(a)(12).

    For the Commission, by the Division of Trading and Markets, 
pursuant to delegated authority.\4\
