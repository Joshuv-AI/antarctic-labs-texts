DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL16-89-000]


Virginia Electric and Power Company; Notice of Institution of 
Section 206 Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On July 28, 2016, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL16-89-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2012), instituting an investigation into the justness and 
reasonableness of certain aspects of Virginia Electric and Power 
Company's Reactive Service rates. Virginia Electric and Power Company, 
156 FERC ] 61,072 (2016).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL16-89-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: July 29, 2016.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
