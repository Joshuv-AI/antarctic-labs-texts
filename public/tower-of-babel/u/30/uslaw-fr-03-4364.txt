FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-369, MB Docket No. 03-35, RM-10646]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Florence, SC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition filed by SSR 
Communications Incorporated proposing the allotment of Channel 237A at 
Florence, South Carolina, as that community's second FM commercial 
aural transmission service. The coordinates for Channel 237A at 
Florence are 34-12-00 North Latitude and 79-40-45 West Longitude. There 
is a site restriction 7.7 kilometers (4.8 miles) east of the community.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before March 31, 2003, and reply 
comments on or before April 15, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554. In addition to filing comments with the FCC, 
interested parties should serve the petitioner, as follows: SSR 
Communications Incorporated, 5270 West Jones Bridge Road, Norcross, GA 
30092-1628.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
