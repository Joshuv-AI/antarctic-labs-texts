DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Cancer 
Immunopathology and Immunotherapy Study Section, June 14, 2007, 8 a.m. 
to June 15, 2007, 5 p.m. The Madison Loews Hotel, 1177 15th Street, 
NW., Washington, DC 20005 which was published in the Federal Register 
on May 21, 2007, 72 FR 28515-27517.

Marriott Bethesda North Hotel & Conference Center, 5701 Marinelli Road, 
Bethesda, MD 20852. The meeting is closed to the public.
