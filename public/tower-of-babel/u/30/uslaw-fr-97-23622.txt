DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Commonwealth Electric Company, Cambridge Electric Light Company; 
Notice of Filing

August 29, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 8, 1997, Commonwealth Electric Company and 
Cambridge Electric Light Company tendered for filing their first 
quarterly report of transactions under their market-based power sales 
tariffs for the period of April 1, 1997 to June 30, 1997.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before September 5, 1997. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make any protestants parties 
to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a 
motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
