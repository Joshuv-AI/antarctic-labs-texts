DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Invention for Licensing; Government-
Owned Invention

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention listed below is assigned to the United States 
Government as represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available 
for licensing by the Department of the Navy. U.S. Patent Application 
Serial No. 09/898,360 entitled ``Video Converter Board,'' Navy Case No. 
83,093.

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the patent cited should be directed 
to the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Crane Division, Code OCF, building 
64, 300 Highway 361, Crane, IN 47522-5001 and must include the Navy 
Case number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Darrell Boggess, Intellectual 
Property Office, Naval Surface Warfare Center, Crane Division, Code 
OCF, Building 64, 300 Highway 361, Crane, IN 47522-5001, telephone 
(812) 854-1130.
