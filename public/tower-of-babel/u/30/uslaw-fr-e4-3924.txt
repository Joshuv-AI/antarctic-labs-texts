SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On September 30, 2004, the Department of 
Commerce published the final results of the administrative review of 
the antidumping order covering circular welded carbon steel pipes and 
tubes from Taiwan. See Circular Welded Carbon Steel Pipes and Tubes 
From Taiwan: Final Results of Antidumping Duty Administrative Review, 
69 FR 58390 (Final Results). The version published in the Federal 
Register contained a typographical error which is being identified and 
corrected by this Correction notice.
    During the publication process, the title of one of the sections- 
``Assessment'' -was transposed into the chart in the previous section 
that identified the respondent and the final weighted-average margin. 
The necessary correction is as follows:

Final Results of Review
