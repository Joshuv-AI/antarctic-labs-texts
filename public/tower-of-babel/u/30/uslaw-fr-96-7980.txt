DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Family Preservation and Support Program Instruction.
    OMB No.: New Request.
    Description: The 1996 Family Preservation and Support Program 
Instruction is being published to inform the State and eligible Indian 
Tribes of the requirement that they submit for approval an Annual 
Progress and Services Report (APSR), a budget request form (CFS-101), 
and certain information solicited to evaluate the implementation of the 
program at the program at the State and Tribal level. This information 
is required by statute, sections 430-435 of the Social Security Act and 
to meet the evaluation information requirements of the Law. This 
information will be used to monitor the progress of States and eligible 
Indian Tribes under this program and to provide information for 
evaluations currently underway.
    Respondents: State governments.
