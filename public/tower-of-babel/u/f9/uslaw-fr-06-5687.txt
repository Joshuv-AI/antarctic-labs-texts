DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, July 6, 2006, 8 a.m. to July 
6, 2006, 4:30 p.m., Hyatt Regency Hotel on Capitol Hill, 400 New Jersey 
Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20001 which was published in the Federal 
Register on June 9, 2006, 71 FR 33472-33474.
    The meeting will be held at the Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One 
Bethesda Metro Center, 7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814. The 
meeting date and time remain the same. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: June 14, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
