Puget Sound Power & Light Company; Notice of Effective Date of Withdrawal of License Application March 25, 1996. On July 19, 1983, Puget Sound Power & Light Company (Puget Power) filed a license application for the proposed Thunder Creek Project No. 3913, to be located on Thunder Creek in Skagit County, Washington. On March 4, 1996, Puget Power filed a letter withdrawing its license application. No motion in opposition to the withdrawal was filed, and the Commission took no action to disallow the withdrawal. Accordingly, pursuant to Rule 216 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal became effective on March 19, 1996.

DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Project No. 3913-001]

Puget Sound Power & Light Company; Notice of Effective Date of 
Withdrawal of License Application
March 25, 1996.
    On July 19, 1983, Puget Sound Power & Light Company (Puget Power) 
filed a license application for the proposed Thunder Creek Project No. 
3913, to be located on Thunder Creek in Skagit County, Washington. On 
March 4, 1996, Puget Power filed a letter withdrawing its license 
application. No motion in opposition to the withdrawal was filed, and 
the Commission took no action to disallow the withdrawal. Accordingly, 
pursuant to Rule 216 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal became effective on March 19, 1996.

    \1\ 18 CFR 385.216.
