Haemer-Wright Tool & Die, Inc., Saegertown, PA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 1, 2002, in response to a 
worker petition filed on behalf of workers at Haemer-Wright Tool & Die, 
Inc., Saegertown, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioning group of workers have been determined eligible to 
apply for Trade Adjustment Assistance under petition number TA-W-
40,065. Further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and 
the investigation is terminated.
