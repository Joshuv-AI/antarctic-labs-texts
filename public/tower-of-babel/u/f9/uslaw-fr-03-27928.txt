Clayson Knitting Company, Inc., Star, NC; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 17, 2003 in response to a 
worker petition filed a company official on behalf of workers at 
Clayson Knitting Company, Inc., Star, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 30th day of September 2003
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
