NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice (99-028)]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Prospective Patent License.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Patterned Fiber Composites, Inc. 
of Pleasant Grove, Utah has applied for an exclusive license to 
practice the invention described and claimed in U.S. Patent No. 
5,203,435, entitled ``Composite Passive Damping Struts for Large 
Precision Structures,'' which is assigned to the United States of 
America as represented by the Administrator of the National Aeronautics 
and Space

[[Page 6383]]

Administration. Written objections to the prospective grant of a 
license to Patterned Fiber Composites, Inc. should be sent to John H. 
Kusmiss, Assistant Patent Counsel of the NASA Management Office at the 
Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

DATES: Responses to this Notice must be received by April 12, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: John H. Kusmiss, Assistant Patent 
Counsel, NASA Management Office-JPL, 4800 Oak Grove Drive, Mail Station 
180-801, Pasadena, CA 91109-8099; telephone (818) 354-7770.

    Dated: February 2, 1999.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
