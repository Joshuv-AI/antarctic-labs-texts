National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Fellowships in Diabetes Endocrinology and Metabolic Diseases, October 
12, 2023, 10:00 a.m. to October 12, 2023, 07:00 p.m., NIH, 2 Dem, 6707 
Democracy Blvd., Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 21, 2023, 56848.

meeting from 10/12/2023 to 10/11/2023. The meeting is closed to the 
public.
