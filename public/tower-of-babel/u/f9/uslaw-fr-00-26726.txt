Imaging Technologies, Inc., Cookeville, TN; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 18, 2000, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Imaging Technologies Inc., Cookeville, Tennessee.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 26th day of September, 2000.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
