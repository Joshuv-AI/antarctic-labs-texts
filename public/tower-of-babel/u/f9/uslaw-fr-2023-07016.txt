DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 15029-001]


SV Hydro, LLC; Notice of Surrender of Preliminary Permit

    Take notice that SV Hydro, LLC, permittee for the proposed Itasca 
County Pumped Storage Project, has requested that its preliminary 
permit be terminated. The permit was issued on October 28, 2020, and 
would have expired on September 30, 2024.\1\ The project would have 
been located near the City of Marble, Itasca County, Minnesota.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 173 FERC ] 62,047 (2020).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The preliminary permit for Project No. 15029 will remain in effect 
until the close of business, April 28, 2023. But, if the Commission is 
closed on this day, then the permit remains in effect until the close 
of business on the next day in which the Commission is open.\2\ New 
applications for this site may not be submitted until after the permit 
surrender is effective.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \2\ 18 CFR 385.2007(a)(2) (2022).
