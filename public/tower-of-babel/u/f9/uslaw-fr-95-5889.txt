Northwest Pipeline Corp.; Notice of Refund Report

March 6, 1995.
    Take notice that on March 1, 1995, Northwest Pipeline Corporation 
(Northwest), tendered for filing with the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission (Commission) a refund report in the above referenced docket.
    Northwest states that the refund detailed in the instant filing was 
ordered by the Commission on November 26, 1993. The refund was paid on 
January 31, 1995 and is comprised of the remaining ten percent of 1992 
Gas Inventory Charge revenues plus accrued interest through January 31, 
1995.
    Northwest states that a copy of this filing has been served upon 
all affected customers and relevant state regulatory commissions.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol 
Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 
of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such protests 
should be filed on or before March 13, 1995. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
