NATIONAL FOUNDATION ON THE ARTS AND THE HUMANITIES

National Endowment for the Arts


Combined Arts Advisory Panel; Notice of Change

    Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
(Pub. L. 92-463), as amended, notice is hereby given that the time of 
the open session of the Combined Arts Advisory Panel, Music Section 
(Access and Heritage & Preservation categories) has been changed. This 
session will be held from 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., rather than 2 p.m. to 
3:30 p.m., on November 22, 2002, at the Nancy Hanks Center, 1100 
Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Washington, DC, 20506.

    Dated: October 22, 2002.
Kathy Plowitz-Worden,
Panel Coordinator, Panel Operations, National Endowment for the Arts.
