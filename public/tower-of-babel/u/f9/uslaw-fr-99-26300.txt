SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3219; Amendment #1]


Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency dated September 27, 1999, the above-numbered 
Declaration is hereby amended to include Dauphin County, Pennsylvania 
as a disaster area due to damages caused by severe flash flooding 
associated with Tropical Depression Dennis that occurred on September 
6-7, 1999.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous counties of Cumberland, Lancaster, 
Lebanon, and York in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania may be filed 
until the specified date at the previously designated location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 20, 1999 and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 22, 2000.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: October 1, 1999.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
