FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Revised Sunshine Notice To Open Commission Meeting, Thursday, 
October 22, 2009

October 16, 2009.
    The FCC Sunshine Notice dated 10-15-09 inadvertently failed to 
include the following language: The Commission waives the Sunshine 
Period Prohibition on ex parte contacts with the Commission to the 
extent that those contacts are made through the Open Internet Blog, 
http://blog.openinternet.gov. Such contacts take place in a forum that 
is both instantaneously available to all interested parties and will 
not intrude on the Commission's decision making.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subject listed below on Thursday, October 22, 2009, which is 
scheduled to commence at 10:00 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th 
Street, S.W., Washington, D.C.
