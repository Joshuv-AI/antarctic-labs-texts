COMMITTEE FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF TEXTILE AGREEMENTS


Establishment of a New Export Visa Arrangement and New 
Certification Stamp for Outward Processed Goods for Certain Cotton, 
Wool, Man-Made Fiber, Silk Blend and Other Vegetable Fiber Textiles and 
Textile Products Produced or Manufactured in Romania; Amendment

February 2, 2000.
    In the letter to the Commissioner of Customs published in the 
Federal Register on December 14, 1999 (64 FR 69744), insert the 
following sentence on page 69746, 1st column, 6th paragraph, line 20, 
right above ``General Provisions'': ``Any shipment which is declared 
for the Outward Processing Program but found not to qualify may be

[[Page 6176]]

permanently denied entry into the United States.''

Troy H. Cribb,
Chairman, Committee for the Implementation of Textile Agreements.
