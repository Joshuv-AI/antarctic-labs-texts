DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-60-2020]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 90--Syracuse, New York, Authorization of 
Limited Production Activity, PPC Broadband, Inc. (Fiber Optic Cables); 
Dewitt, New York

    On October 8, 2020, PPC Broadband, Inc., (PPC Broadband) submitted 
a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facility within FTZ 90, in Dewitt, New York.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (85 FR 65790-65791, October 16, 2020). 
On February 5, 2021, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that further review of part of the proposed activity is 
warranted. The FTZ Board authorized the production activity described 
in the notification on a limited basis, subject to the FTZ Act and the 
Board's regulations, including Section 400.14, and further subject to 
restrictions requiring that foreign-status tight buffered fiber be 
admitted to the zone in privileged foreign status (19 CFR 146.41) and 
that aramid yarn, swellcoat blockers or equivalent be admitted to the 
zone in domestic/duty paid status (19 CFR 146.43).

    Dated: February 5, 2021.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
