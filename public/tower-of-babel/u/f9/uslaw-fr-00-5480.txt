FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2391]


Petitions for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

March 1, 2000.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and 
published pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full test of these 
documents are available for viewing and copying in Room CY-09A257, 445 
12th Street, S.W., Washington, D.C. or may be purchased from the 
Commission's copy contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-093800. Oppositions 
to these petitions must be filed by March 22, 2000. See Section 
1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an 
opposition must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing 
oppositions has expired.
    Subject: Revision of the Commission's Rules To Ensure Compatibility 
with Enhanced 911 Emergency Calling Systems.
    Number of Petitions Filed: 2.

Federal Communications Commission.
Magalie Roman Salas,
Secretary.
