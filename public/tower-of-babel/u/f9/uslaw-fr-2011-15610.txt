47 CFR Part 73

[DA 11-932; MB Docket No. 09-219; RM-11581]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Brackettville, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division grants a Petition for Rule Making issued at 
the request of RF Services, Inc., licensee of a new FM station at 
Rocksprings, Texas, that requests the deletion of vacant Channel 234A 
at Brackettville to accommodate the hybrid application, which requests 
the substitution of Channel 234C3 for Channel 235C3 at Rocksprings, 
Texas, reallotment of Channel 234C3 from Rocksprings, to Brackettville, 
Texas, and modification of the new FM station authorization. See File 
No. BNPH-20091019AFF.

DATES: Effective July 5, 2011.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth 
Street, SW., Washington, DC 20554.
