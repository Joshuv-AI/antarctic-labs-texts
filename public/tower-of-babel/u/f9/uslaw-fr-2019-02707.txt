DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 1889-088]


FirstLight Hydro Generating Company, FirstLight MA Hydro LLC; 
Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 8, 2019, the Commission issued a notice setting February 
7, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments, motions to 
intervene, and protests on the application for transfer of license and 
substitution of relicense applicant for the Turners Falls Hydroelectric 
Project No. 1889. Due to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies 
between December 22, 2018 and January 25, 2019, the Commission is 
extending the comment period until February 25, 2019.

    Dated: February 11, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
