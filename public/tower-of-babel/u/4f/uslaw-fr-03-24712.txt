Fishing Vessel (F/V) Seven Sons, Cordova, AK; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 19, 2003 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of F/V Seven 
Sons, Cordova, Alaska.
    The petition regarding the investigation has been deemed invalid. 
In order to establish a valid worker group, there must be at least 
three full-time workers employed at some point during the period under 
investigation. Workers of the group subject to this investigation did 
not meet this threshold level of employment. Consequently, the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 27th day of August 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
