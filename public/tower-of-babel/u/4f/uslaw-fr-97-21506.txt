COMMITTEE FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF TEXTILE AGREEMENTS


Adjustment of Import Limits for Certain Wool and Man-Made Fiber 
Textile Products Produced or Manufactured in Romania; Correction

August 8, 1997.
    In the letter to the Commissioner of Customs published in the 
Federal Register on July 23, 1997 (62 FR 39501), column 2, under the 
heading ``Adjusted twelve-month limit,'' the unit of measure for 
Category 410 should be corrected from ``dozen'' to ``square meters.''
Troy H. Cribb,
Chairman, Committee for the Implementation of Textile Agreements.
