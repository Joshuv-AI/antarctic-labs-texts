PROSPECTIVE PAYMENT ASSESSMENT COMMISSION

 

Meetings

    Notice is hereby given of the meetings of the Prospective Payment 
Assessment Commission on Wednesday and Thursday, January 19-20, 1994, 
at the Madison Hotel, 15th & M Streets, Northwest, Washington, DC.
    The Full Commission will convene at 9 a.m. on each day in Executive 
Chambers 1, 2 and 3.
    All meetings are open to the public.
Donald A. Young,
Executive Director.
