Millennium Pipeline Company, L.P. and Columbia Gas Transmission 
Corporation; Notice of Change of Location for Scoping Meeting

March 11, 1998.
    The location for the environmental scoping meeting in Mount Vernon, 
New York, on March 24, 1998, has been changed from the Mount Vernon 
High School to the following facility: Mark Twain Junior High School, 
160 Woodlawn Avenue, Auditorium, Yonkers, New York.

    For further information, call Paul McKee, Office of External 
Affairs, at (202) 208-1088.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
