DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Statement of Organization, Functions, and Delegations of 
Authority; Correction

Correction

    In the Federal Register of January 6, 2012 (77 FR 797), the 
Department of Health and Human Services published a notice titled 
National Institutes of Health Statement of Organization, Functions, and 
Delegations of Authority. On page 797, in the first column, first 
paragraph, correct the National Center for Advancing Translational 
Science (NCATS) to read: National Center for Advancing Translational 
Sciences (NCATS).

    Dated: January 9, 2012.
Jerry Moore,
NIH Federal Register Liaison Officer, National Institutes of Health.
