Southern Company Services; Initiation of Proceeding and Refund 
Effective Date

August 18, 1994.
    Take notice that on August 12, 1994, the Commission issued an order 
in the above-indicated dockets initiating an investment in Docket No. 
EL94-85-000 under section 206 of the Federal Power Act.
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL94-85-000 will be 60 days 
after publication of this notice in the Federal Register.
