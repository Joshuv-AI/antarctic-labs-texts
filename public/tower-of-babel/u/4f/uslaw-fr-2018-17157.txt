DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army, Corps of Engineers


Inland Waterways Users Board; Notice of Federal Advisory 
Committee Meeting

AGENCY: Department of the Army, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, DoD.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The notice of an open meeting scheduled for August 30, 2018 
published in the Federal Register on July 11, 2018 has a new date and 
time. The meeting will now be held on August 28, 2018 at 1:00 p.m.

DATES: The Inland Waterways Users Board will meet from 1:00 p.m. to 
5:00 p.m. on August 28, 2018. Public registration will begin at 12:15 
p.m.
