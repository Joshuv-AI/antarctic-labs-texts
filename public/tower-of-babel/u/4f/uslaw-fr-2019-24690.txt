DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


U.S. Strategic Command Strategic Advisory Group; Notice of 
Advisory Committee Closed Meeting; Cancellation

AGENCY: Office of the Chairman Joint Chiefs of Staff, Department of 
Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Notice of Federal Advisory Committee; cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Monday, October 28, 2019 the DoD published a notice 
announcing a meeting of the U.S. Strategic Command Strategic Advisory 
Group that was to take place on Tuesday, November 19, 2019 to 
Wednesday, November 20, 2019. Due to schedule conflicts, the DoD is 
cancelling the November 19-20 meeting.

ADDRESSES: Mailing address is 901 SAC Boulevard, Suite 1F7, Offutt AFB, 
NE 68113-6030.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  Mr. John L. Trefz, Jr., Designated 
Federal Officer, (402) 294-4102 (Voice), (402) 294-3128 (Facsimile), 
[email protected] (Email).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Due to circumstances beyond the control of 
the Department of Defense and the Designated Federal Officer, the U.S. 
Strategic Command Strategic Advisory Group was unable to provide 
sufficient public notification required by 41 CFR 102-3.150(a) 
concerning the cancellation of its previously noticed November 19-20, 
2019 meeting. Accordingly, the Advisory Committee Management Officer 
for the Department of Defense, pursuant to 41 CFR 102-3.150(b), waives 
the 15-calendar day notification requirement.

    Dated: November 7, 2019.
Aaron T. Siegel,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
