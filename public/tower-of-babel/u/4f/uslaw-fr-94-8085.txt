DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Standards for Electronic Bulletin Boards Required Under Part 284 
of the Commission's Regulations

March 30, 1994.
AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Energy.

ACTION: Notice of filings and opportunity to file comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission has received a filing 
from Working Group 5, on March 24, 1994, submitting a consensus 
proposal for company common codes in capacity release data sets and 
from Working Group 3, on March 25, 1994, submitting a consensus 
agreement on the implementation of standard standards for non-capacity 
release business transactions. The Commission is affording interested 
persons an opportunity to file comments on these filings.

DATES: Comments due by April 6, 1994.

ADDRESSES: Comments should be filed at: Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.
