SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3626]


State of Louisiana; Amendment 2

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency--effective 
October 22, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
reestablish the incident period for this disaster as beginning 
September 13, 2004, and continuing through and including September 26, 
2004.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 15, 2004, and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 15, 2005.
