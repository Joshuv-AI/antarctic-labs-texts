Correction

    In notice document E7-17877, published in the issue of Tuesday, 
September 11, 2007, at 72 FR 51904-51905, make the following 
correction:
    On page 51905, in the fourth paragraph (titled ``Current 
Actions:''), in the third sentence, the phrase ``the tobacco 
manufacturer'' should read ``the brewer''.

Francis W. Foote,
