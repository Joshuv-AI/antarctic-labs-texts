Maritimes and Northeast Pipeline, L.L.C; Notice of Telephone 
Technical Conference

October 6, 2004.
    In an order issued on July 29, 2004,\1\ the Commission directed 
staff to convene a technical conference to discuss Maritimes and 
Northeast Pipeline, L.L.C.'s proposed non-rate modifications to its 
tariff including, but not limited to, revisions to the fuel retainage 
percentage, revisions to the right of first refusal, and the proposed 
action alert. The technical conference was held on September 22, 2004.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Maritimes and Northeast Pipeline, L.L.C., 108 FERC ] 61,087 
(2004).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    A telephone technical conference will be held on Wednesday, October 
13, 2004, beginning at 10 a.m. (EST) to further discuss issues 
concerning Maritimes' proposed modifications to its right of first 
refusal procedures, action alert, and accounting for its fuel tracker. 
The telephone number for the conference is 888-412-7888. The code is 
2360313.
    All interested parties and staff are permitted to call in. For 
further information please contact: David Faerberg at (202) 502-8275 or 
e-mail [email protected].
