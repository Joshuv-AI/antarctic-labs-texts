Federal Home Loan Bank Housing Goals Amendments

AGENCY: Federal Housing Finance Agency.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Housing Finance Agency is making a non-substantive 
change to correct an erroneous amendatory instruction in the final rule 
that published on June 25, 2020, amending the existing Federal Home 
Loan Bank Housing Goals regulation.

DATES: This correction is effective August 24, 2020.
