DEPARTMENT OF TREASURY

Internal Revenue Service


Members of Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The purpose of this notice is to publish the names of those 
IRS employees who will be serving as members on IRS' FY2004 SES 
Performance Review Board(s).

DATES: This notice is effective October 1, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ann Pope, 1111 Constitution Avenue, 
NW., OS:HC:S, Room 3511, Washington, DC 20224, (202) 622-0601.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), this notice 
announces the appointment of members of the Internal Revenue Service's 
Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board. The names and titles 
of the executives serving on this board follow:
