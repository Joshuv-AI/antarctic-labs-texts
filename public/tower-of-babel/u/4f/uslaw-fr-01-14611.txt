Approval and Promulgation of Implementation Plans; IN

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The EPA is proposing to approve revisions to particulate 
matter (PM) regulations for Rolls-Royce Allison (Rolls-Royce), formerly 
Allison Engine Company. This facility is located in Marion County, 
Indiana. The Indiana Department of Environmental Management (IDEM) 
submitted the revised regulations on August 31, 2000 as a requested 
amendment to its State Implementation Plan (SIP). The revisions consist 
of a name change for the company and the addition of a provision that 
allows the facility to use landfill gas as an alternate fuel. These 
requested SIP revisions do not change Rolls-Royce's emissions limits.
