[DA 07-2882; MB Docket No. 05-67; RM-11116, RM-11342]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Clinton, Fishers, Indianapolis, and 
Lawrence, IN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This Report and Order dismisses two Counterproposals as 
unacceptable for consideration. In addition, this Report and Order 
upgrades Channel 230A, Station WWFT (FM), Fishers, Indiana, to Channel 
230B1, reallots Channel 230B1 from Fishers to Lawrence, Indiana, as 
Lawrence's first local aural transmission service, and modifies Station 
WWFT (FM)'s license accordingly. In order to maintain local service at 
Fishers, the Report and Order reallots Channel 238B, Station WFMS (FM), 
from Indianapolis to Fishers, Indiana, and modifies Station WFMS (FM)'s 
license accordingly. To accommodate the reallotment of Channel 230B1 to 
Lawrence, the Report and Order substitutes Channel 229A for Channel 
230A at Station WPFR-FM. Clinton, Indiana, and modifies Station WPFR-
FM's license accordingly. The Media Bureau's Consolidated Database 
System (CDBS) reflects these changes.
