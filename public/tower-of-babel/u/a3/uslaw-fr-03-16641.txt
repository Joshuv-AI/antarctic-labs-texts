ACTION: Notice of issuance of advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Advisory Circular (AC) 
20-146, Methodology for Dynamic Seat Certification by Analysis for Use 
in parts 23, 25, 27, and 29 Airplanes and Rotorcraft. The AC sets forth 
an acceptable means, but not the only means, for demonstrating 
compliance to certain sections of the regulations and the technical 
standard order associated with those regulations. The AC provides 
guidance on how to validate the computer model and under what 
conditions the model may be used in support of certification or TSO 
approval/authorization.

DATES: Advisory Circular 20-146 was issued by the Acting Manager of the 
Aircraft Engineering Division on May 19, 2003.
