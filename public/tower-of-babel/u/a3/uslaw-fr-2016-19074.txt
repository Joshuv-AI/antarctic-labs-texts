DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL16-97-000]


NextEra Energy Transmission West, LLC; Notice of Institution of 
Section 206 Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On August 5, 2016, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL16-97-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2012), instituting an investigation into whether the 
formula rate protocols of NextEra Energy Transmission West, LLC may be 
unjust, unreasonable, unduly discriminatory or preferential. NextEra 
Energy Transmission West, LLC, 156 FERC ] 61,095 (2016).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL16-97-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.
