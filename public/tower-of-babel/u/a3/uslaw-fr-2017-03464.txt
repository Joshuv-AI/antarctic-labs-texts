SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Region III published 
in the Federal Register of December 23, 2016, a Notice of Intent for 
Partial Deletion of the North Penn Area 6 Superfund Site from the 
National Priorities List. The notice referenced the incorrect Docket ID 
Number. This notice provides the correct Docket ID Number.

DATES: February 22, 2017.

[email protected].

the ADDRESSES section, correct the Docket ID Number to read: EPA-HQ-
SFUND-1989-0008.
