Confidentiality of Alcohol and Drug Abuse Patient Records

    0930-0092--Extension, no change--Statute (42 U.S.C. 290dd-2) and 
regulations (42 CFR Part 2) require Federally conducted, regulated, or 
directly or indirectly assisted alcohol and drug abuse programs to keep 
patient records confidential. Information requirements are (1) written 
disclosure to patients, and (2) documenting medical personnel'' status 
of recipients of a disclosure to meet a medical emergency.
