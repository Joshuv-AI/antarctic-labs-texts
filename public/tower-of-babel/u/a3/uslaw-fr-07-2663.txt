DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Human Genome Research Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Genome 
Research Review Committee, June 8, 2007, 1 p.m. to June 8, 2007, 3 
p.m., National Institutes of Health, 5635 Fishers Lane, Bethesda, MD 
20892, which was published in the Federal Register on May 17, 2007, 72 
FR 95, page 27822.
    The GNOM-G telephone conference date is changed from June 8, 2007, 
to June 14, 2007. The meeting time remains 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 23, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
