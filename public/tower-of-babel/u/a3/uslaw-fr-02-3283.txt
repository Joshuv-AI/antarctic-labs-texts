DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Office of the Secretary


Bureau of Indian Affairs, Office of Special Trustee for American 
Indians, Office of Indian Trust Transition; Tribal Consultation on 
Indian Trust Asset Management

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Bureau of Indian Affairs, Office of 
the Special Trustee for American Indians, Office of Indian Trust 
Transition, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of the Secretary, Bureau of Indian Affairs, Office 
of the Special Trustee for American Indians, and the Office of Indian 
Trust Transition gave public notice in the Federal Register of January 
31, 2002, (67 FR 4703) of a tribal consultation meeting in Portland, 
Oregon, to be held on February 14, 2002. The time of the consultation 
meeting was in error. This action corrects that error.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Wayne R. Smith, Deputy Assistant 
Secretary--Indian Affairs, 1849 C Street, NW, MS 4240 MIB, Washington, 
DC 20240 (202/208-7163).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register document published 
on January 31, 2002, there was an error in the scheduled time of the 
consultation meeting. The Department is correcting the document as 
follows:
    In notice document (Federal Register document 02-2303) make the 
following correction:
    On page 4730, in the third column, 16 lines from the bottom of the 
column, the time for the consultation meeting should read ``1:00 p.m.''

    Dated: February 6, 2002.
J. Steven Griles,
Deputy Secretary.
