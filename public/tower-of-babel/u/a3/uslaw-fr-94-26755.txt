DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Federal Aviation Administration
[AC No. 120-44A]


Proposed Advisory Circular on Air Carrier First Aid Programs

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Request for comments on proposed Advisory Circular (AC) Air 
Carrier First Aid Programs.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The proposed AC is intended to provide guidance about first 
aid program resources, subjects, equipment, and pertinent regulations. 
This document adds information about bloodborne pathogen awareness 
programs to AC 120-44 which was published in 1987.

COMMENTS INVITED: Comments are invited on all aspects of the proposed 
AC. Commentators must identify file number AC 120-44A.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before November 28, 1994.

ADDRESSES: Send all comments and requests for copies of the proposed AC 
to: Federal Aviation Administration, Air Transportation Division 
(Attention: AFS-203), 800 Independence Avenue SW., Washington DC, 
20591.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The guidance material contained in this AC 
reflects the material to assist all operators to conduct bloodborne 
pathogen disease awareness programs.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on October 18, 1994.

Thomas C. Accardi,

Director, Flight Standards Service.
