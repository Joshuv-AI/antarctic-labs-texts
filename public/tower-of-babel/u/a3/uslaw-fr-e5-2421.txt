Ametek, U.S. Gauge; Bartow, FL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 25, 2005 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Ametek, U.S. Gauge, 
Bartow, Florida.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 28th day of April, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
