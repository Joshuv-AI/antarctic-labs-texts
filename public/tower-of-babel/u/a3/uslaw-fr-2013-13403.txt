published in the Federal Register on May 22, 2013, 78 FR 30233, 
regarding the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement; 
Government Support Contractor Access to Technical Data (DFARS 2009-
D031). This correction makes two corrections to the amendatory 
language.
