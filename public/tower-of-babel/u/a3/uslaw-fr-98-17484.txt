DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Committee Meeting Notice

AGENCY: United States Army School of the Americas, Training and 
Doctrine Command.

ACTION: Notice; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document withdraws from consideration the Committee 
Meeting Notice published in the Federal Register on June 17, 1998 (Vol. 
63, No. 116 FR 33055-33056). Reason for withdrawal is based on the 
expired membership of this subcommittee which precludes the meeting 
from taking place.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: United States Army School of the 
Americas, Attention: TMD, MAJ Clemente, Room 333, Building 35, Fort 
Benning, GA 31905.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.

Gregory D. Showalter,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
