SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Public Meeting

    The Heartland States Regulatory Fairness Board will hold a Public 
Hearing on June 13, 2000. The meeting hearing will be held at the 
Marriott Hotel located at 1250 74th Street, West Des Moines, IA 50266 
at 1 p.m. The purpose of this hearing is to receive comments and 
testimony from small businesses and representatives of trade 
associations concerning regulatory enforcement or compliance actions 
taken by federal agencies. Transcripts of these proceedings will be 
posted on the Internet. These transcripts are subject only to limited 
review by the National Ombudsman. Contact: John Greiner (312) 353-0880.

Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administration/Public Liaison.
