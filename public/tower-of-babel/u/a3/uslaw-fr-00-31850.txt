DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket Nos. RP00-344-000 and RP00-601-000]


Dominion Transmission, Inc.; Notice of Technical Conference

December 7, 2000.
    Take notice that a technical conference to further discuss the 
various issues raised by Dominion Transmission, Inc.'s (Dominion) Order 
No. 637 compliance filing will be held on Thursday, December 14, 2000, 
at 10 a.m., in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
