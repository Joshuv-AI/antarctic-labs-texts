DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


High Energy Physics Advisory Panel

AGENCY: Office of Science, Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Federal Advisory Committee Act, and Code of 
Federal Regulations, and following consultation with the Committee 
Management Secretariat, General Services Administration, notice is 
hereby given that the High Energy Physics Advisory Panel (HEPAP) has 
been renewed for a two-year period.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. John Kogut at (301) 903-1298 or 
email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Panel will provide advice and 
recommendations to the Director, Office of Science (DOE), and the 
Assistant Director, Directorate for Mathematical and Physical Sciences 
(NSF), on scientific priorities within the field of basic high energy 
physics research.
    Additionally, the Secretary of Energy has determined that renewal 
of the HEPAP is essential to conduct business of the Department of 
Energy and the National Science Foundation and is in the public 
interest in connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the 
Department of Energy by law. The Panel will continue to operate in 
accordance with the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 
adhering to the rules and regulations in implementation of that Act.
