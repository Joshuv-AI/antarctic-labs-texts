OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Notice of Request for Reclearance of Form OPM 1530

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (title 
44, U.S. Code, chapter 35), this notice announces a request for 
reclearance of an information collection. OPM Form 1530, Report of 
Medical Examination of Person Electing Survivor Benefit (CSRS), is used 
to collect sufficient information from the required medical examination 
regarding an annuitant's health. This information is then used to 
determine whether the insurable interest survivor benefits election can 
be allowed.
    Approximately 500 OPM 1530 forms are completed annually. It takes 
approximately 90 minutes to complete this form. The total annual burden 
is 750 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact C. Ronald Trueworthy on (703) 
908-8550.

DATES: Comments on this proposal should be received on or before 
October 28, 1994.
