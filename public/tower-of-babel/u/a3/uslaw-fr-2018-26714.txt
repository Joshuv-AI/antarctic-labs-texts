DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


National Research Advisory Council; Notice of Meeting; 
Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act that the meeting of the National 
Research Advisory Council previously scheduled to be held on Wednesday, 
December 5, 2018, at 1100 First Street NE, Room 104, Washington, DC 
20002, has been cancelled.
    For more information, please contact Ms. Rashelle Robinson, 
Designated Federal Officer at (202) 443-5668, or via email at 
[email protected].

    Dated: December 4, 2018.
LaTonya L. Small,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
