New United Motor Manufacturing, Inc. Fremont, California; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 25, 2006 in response to a 
worker petition filed by the state agency on behalf of workers at New 
United Motor Manufacturing, Inc., Fremont, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of October, 2006.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
