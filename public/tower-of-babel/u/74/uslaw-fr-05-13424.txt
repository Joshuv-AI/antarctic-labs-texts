Sunshine Act Public Announcement Pursuant to the Government in

SUMMARY: The U.S. Parole Commission published a document in the Federal 
Register of June 29, 2005, concerning an open meeting to be held on 
Tuesday, July 5, 2005. The document provides a correction as to the 
date of the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Thomas W. Hutchison, Chief of Staff, 
United States Parole Commission, (301) 492-5990.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of June 29, 2005, in FR Doc 05-12890, on 
pages 37433-37434, correct the TIME AND DATE caption to read:

TIME AND DATE: 2:30 p.m., Monday, July 28, 2005.

    Dated: July 1, 2005.
