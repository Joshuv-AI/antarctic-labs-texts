NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management; Renewals

    The NSF management officials having responsibility for three 
advisory committees listed below have determined that renewing these 
groups for another two years is necessary and in the public interest in 
connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the Director, 
National Science Foundation (NSF), by 42 U.S.C. 1861 et seq. This 
determination follows consultation with the Committee Management 
Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Committees:

Advisory Committee for Environmental Research and Education, 
9487
Proposal Review Panel for Industrial Innovations and Partnerships, 
28164
Proposal Review Panel for Emerging Frontiers in Research and 
Innovation, 34558
    Effective date for renewal is March 3, 2014. For more information, 
please contact Crystal Robinson, NSF, at (703) 292-8687.

     Dated: February 11, 2014.
Suzanne Plimpton,
Acting Committee Management Officer.
