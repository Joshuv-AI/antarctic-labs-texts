Quarterly Rail Cost Adjustment Factor

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board, DOT.

ACTION: Approval of rail cost adjustment factor.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Board has approved the fourth quarter 2011 Rail Cost 
Adjustment Factor (RCAF) and cost index filed by the Association of 
American Railroads. The fourth quarter 2011 RCAF (Unadjusted) is 1.208. 
The fourth quarter 2011 RCAF (Adjusted) is 0.533. The fourth quarter 
2011 RCAF-5 is 0.506. The Board noted an error in the third quarter 
2011 Materials and Supplies Index, which will be accounted for using 
the first quarter 2012 forecast error calculation.

DATES: Effective Date: October 1, 2011.

8339.
