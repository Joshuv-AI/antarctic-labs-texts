NESHAP: National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: 
Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants for Hazardous Waste Combustors; 
Extension of Comment Period

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule; extension of comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: EPA is announcing that the comment period to the proposed rule 
entitled National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: 
Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants for Hazardous Waste Combustors, 
published on March 23, 2006, is being extended until May 8, 2006. In 
the proposed rule, EPA is requesting comment on a revised new source 
particulate matter standard for cement kilns that burn hazardous waste. 
We are also requesting comment on corresponding changes to the new 
source particulate matter standards for incinerators and liquid fuel 
boilers that burn hazardous waste.

DATES: The comment period for this proposed rule is extended from the 
original closing date of April 24, 2006 to May 8, 2006.
