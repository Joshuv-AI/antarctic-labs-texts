DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army; Corps of Engineers


Availability of Draft Environmental Impact Statement for the 
Dallas Floodway Project, in the City of Dallas, Dallas County, TX

AGENCY: Department of the Army, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, DoD.

ACTION: Notice; extension of comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The comment period for the Availability of Draft Environmental 
Impact Statement for the Dallas Floodway Project, in the City of 
Dallas, Dallas County, TX published in the Federal Register on Friday, 
December 19, 2014 (79 FR 75794), required comments be postmarked on or 
before January 18, 2015. The comment period has been extended to 
February 2, 2015.
