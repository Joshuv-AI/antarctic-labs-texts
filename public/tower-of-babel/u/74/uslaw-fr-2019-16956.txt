DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Amended; 
Notice of Meeting.

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Environmental Health Sciences Council, which was published in 
the Federal Register on February 15, 2019, 84 FR, 4502.
    This notice is being amended due to change of meeting location. The 
meeting will be held at Durham Convention Center (Junior Ballroom AB), 
301 Morgan Street, Durham, NC 27701. The meeting is partially Closed to 
the public.

    Dated: August 2, 2019.
Sylvia L. Neal,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
