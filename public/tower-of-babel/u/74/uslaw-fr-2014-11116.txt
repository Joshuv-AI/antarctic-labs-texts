Radio Broadcasting Services; Tohatchi, New Mexico

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of The Navajo Nation, the Audio Division amends 
the FM Table of Allotments, by allotting Channel 268C2 at Tohatchi, New 
Mexico, as a first local Tribal Allotment and a first local service to 
the community. A staff engineering analysis confirms that Channel 268C2 
can be allotted to Tohatchi consistent with the minimum distance 
separation requirements of the Commission's Rules with no imposition of 
a site restriction at reference coordinates 35-54-37 NL and 108-46-26 
WL.
