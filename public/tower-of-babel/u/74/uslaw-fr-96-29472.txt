NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Notice of Meeting To Be Held With Less Than Seven Days Advance 
Notice

TIME AND DATE: 1:00 p.m., Thursday, November 14, 1996.

PLACE: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047, 1775 Duke Street, Alexandria, 
VA 22314-3428.

STATUS: Open.

MATTER TO BE CONSIDERED:

    1. Interim Field of Membership Policy Consistent with the D.C. 
Court of Appeals' July Ruling.

    The National Credit Union Administration Board voted unanimously 
that Agency business requires a meeting be held with less than the 
usual seven days advance notice.
    The National Credit Union Administration notified interested 
parties of the time and place of the meeting as soon as possible after 
the members of the Board voted to hold the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Becky Baker, Secretary of the Board, 
Telephone (703) 518-6304.
Becky Baker,
Secretary of the Board.
