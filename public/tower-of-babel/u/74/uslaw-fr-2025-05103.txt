Supplemental Type Certificate SE03079AT; Original Product Type 
Certificate Numbers E-162

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Surrendered supplemental type certificate.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces Air Repair, Inc. surrendered its 
supplemental type certificates (STC) SE03079AT to the FAA. This action 
is intended to inform all aircraft owners who may possess a product 
affected by these surrendered certificates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Gary Wechsler, East Certification 
Branch, Federal Aviation Administration, 1701 Columbia Avenue, College 
Park, GA 30337; telephone (404) 474-5575; email [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Air Repair, Inc. notified the FAA by letter 
received September 16, 2024, it is voluntarily surrendering the 
following STC:
