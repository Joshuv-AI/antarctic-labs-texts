DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. RP95-185-000 and RP95-185-001]


Northern Natural Gas Co.; Notice of Informal Settlement 
Conference

September 27, 1995.
    Take notice that Commission Staff will convene an informal 
settlement conference in this proceeding on November 1, 1995, at 1 p.m. 
The conference will continue on November 2, if necessary. The 
conference will be held in a hearing room at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, DC.
    Any party, as defined by 18 CFR 385.102(c), or any participant, as 
defined in 18 CFR 385.102(b), may attend. Persons wishing to become a 
party must move to intervene and receive intervenor status pursuant to 
the Commission's regulations, 18 CFR 385.214.

    For additional information, contact Donald Heydt at (202) 208-
0740 or Robert Young at (202) 208-5705.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
