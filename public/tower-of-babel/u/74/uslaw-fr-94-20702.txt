FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-3; RM-8384]

 

Radio Broadcasting Services; Silverton, CO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document deletes vacant and unapplied for Channel 257A at 
Silverton, Colorado, in response to a petition for rule making filed by 
Caren Lacy, permittee of Station KWXA (FM), Channel 259C2, Durango, 
Colorado, to accommodate her application at an electronics site 
designated by the Forest Service for communications installations. See 
59 FR 7237, February 15, 1994. The Notice optionally proposed to allot 
Channel 224A as a substitute for Channel 257A at Silverton. However, no 
expressions of interest in retaining a Class A channel were received, 
and therefore, no substitution is made at that community.
    With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 3, 1994.
