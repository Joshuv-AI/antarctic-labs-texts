Tekas Corporation; Notice of Informal Settlement Conference

October 2, 1995.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Friday, October 13, 1995, at 10:00 
a.m. in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 
20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Michael J. Ahern at (202) 208-0527.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
