DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

Veterans' Advisory Committee on Environmental Hazards; Charter 
Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Department of Veterans Affairs 
Veterans' Advisory Committee on Environmental Hazards has been renewed 
for a 2-year period beginning January 12, 1994, through January 12, 
1996.

    Dated: January 14, 1993.

    By direction of the Secretary:
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
