DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Notice of Public Meeting

    The National Park Service is seeking public comments and 
suggestions on the planning of the 1997 Christmas Pageant of Peace, 
which opens December 4, on the Ellipse (President's Park), south of the 
White House.
    The meeting will be held at 1 p.m., Thursday, November 13, 1997, in 
room 234 of the National Park Service's National Capital Region 
Building, at 1100 Ohio Drive, S.W., in East Potomac Park.
    Persons who would like to comment at the meeting should notify the 
National Park Service by November 10, by calling the White House 
Visitor Center between 9 a.m. and 4 p.m. weekdays, at (202) 208-1631. 
Written comments may be sent to the Park Manager, White House Visitor 
Center, 1100 Ohio Drive, SW., Washington, DC 20242, and can be accepted 
until November 7.

    Dated: November 3, 1997.
Tom Peyton,
Park Manager, President's Park.
