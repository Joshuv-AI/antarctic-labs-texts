DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

International Trade Administration


The President's Export Council: Meeting of the President's Export 
Council

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, U.S. Department of 
Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of an open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The President's Export Council will hold a meeting to discuss 
topics related to export expansion. The meeting will include discussion 
of trade priorities and initiatives, PEC subcommittee activity and 
proposed letters of recommendation to the President. The PEC was 
established on December 20, 1973, and reconstituted May 4, 1979, to 
advise the President on matters relating to U.S. trade. It was most 
recently renewed by Executive Order 13316.
