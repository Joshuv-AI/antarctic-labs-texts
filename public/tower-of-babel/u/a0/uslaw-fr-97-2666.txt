DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[OR-958-0777-54; GP6-0216; OR-22221 (WA)]


Public Land Order No. 7240; Revocation of Secretarial Order Dated 
June 28, 1943; Washington

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Public Land Order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This order revokes in its entirety a Secretarial order which 
withdrew 4.25 acres of public land for the Bureau of Land Management's 
Powersite Classification No. 342. The land is no longer needed for the 
purpose for which it was withdrawn. The land is included in another 
existing withdrawal and remains closed to surface entry and mining. The 
land has been and remains open to mineral leasing.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 4, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Betty McCarthy, BLM Oregon/Washington 
State Office, P.O. Box 2965, Portland, Oregon 97208-2965, 503-952-6155.
    By virtue of the authority vested in the Secretary of the Interior 
by Section 204 of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976, 
43 U.S.C. 1714 (1988), it is ordered as follows:
    1. The Secretarial Order dated June 28, 1943, which established 
Powersite Classification No. 342, is hereby revoked in its entirety:

Willamette Meridian

T. 11 N., R. 4 E.,
    Sec. 2, lot 1.

    The area described contains 4.25 acres in Lewis County.

    2. The land is included in Power Project No. 2016 and remains 
withdrawn from operation of the public land laws, including the mining 
laws. The land has been and continues to be open to applications and 
offers under the mineral leasing laws.

    Signed: January 23, 1997.
Bob Armstrong,
Assistant Secretary of the Interior.
