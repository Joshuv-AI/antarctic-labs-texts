Radio Broadcasting Services; Shiner, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule making; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a petition for rule making filed by 
Elgin FM Limited Partnership requesting the allotment of Channel 232C3 
at Shiner, Texas. See 64 FR 68662, December 8, 1999. Elgin FM Limited 
Partnership withdrew its interest in the allotment of Channel 232C3 at 
Shiner, Texas. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
