47 CFR Part 73

Radio Broadcasting Services; Abilene and Burlingame, KS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a petition filed by MCC Radio, LLC, 
licensee of Station KSAJ(FM), Channel 253C1, Abilene, Kansas, 
requesting the reallotment of Channel 253C1 from Abilene to Burlingame, 
Kansas, as its first local service and modification of the Station 
KSAJ(FM) license accordingly. Channel 253C1 can be allotted to 
Burlingame in conformity with the Commission's rules, provided there is 
a site restriction of 17.7 kilometers (11 miles) northwest of the 
community, using reference coordinates 38-52-29 NL and 95-58-05 WL.

DATES: Effective May 30, 2006.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554.
