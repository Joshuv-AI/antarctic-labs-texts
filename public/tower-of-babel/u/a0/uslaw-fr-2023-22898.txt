SUMMARY: On October 12, 2023, the Department of Education (Department) 
published in the Federal Register a request for information (RFI) on a 
potential new program, From Seedlings to Scale (S2S). We are correcting 
the docket identification number. All other information in the RFI 
remains the same.

DATES: This correction is applicable October 17, 2023.
