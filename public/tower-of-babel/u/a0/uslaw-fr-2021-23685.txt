FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

FEDERAL REGISTER CITATION NOTICE OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: 86 FR 58278.

PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TIME AND DATE OF THE MEETING: Tuesday, October 26, 
2021 at 10:00 a.m. and its continuation at the conclusion of the open 
meeting on October 28, 2021.

CHANGES IN THE MEETING: This meeting also discussed:
    Matters relating to internal personnel decisions, or internal rules 
and practices.
    Information for which disclosure would constitute an unwarranted 
invasion of privacy.
    Information the premature disclosure of which would be likely to 
have a considerable adverse effect on the implementation of a proposed 
Commission action.
