FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-3089, MB Docket No. 02-219, RM-10506]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Lawton, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of KSWO Television Company, 
Inc., substitutes DTV channel 11 for DTV channel 23 at Lawton, 
Oklahoma. See 67 FR 52922, August 14, 2002. DTV channel 11 can be 
allotted to Lawton, Oklahoma, in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates 34-12-55 N. and 98-43-13 W. with a power of 138, HAAT of 
327 meters and with a DTV service population of 431 thousand. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 30, 2002.

418-1600.
