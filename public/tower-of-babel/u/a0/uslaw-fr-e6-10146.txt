Preventing Undue Discrimination and Preference in Transmission 
Service

AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects a paragraph formatting and numbering 
error in a notice of proposed rulemaking that the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission published in the Federal Register on June 6, 
2006. That action proposed amendments to Commission Order Nos. 888 and 
889.

DATES: Effective Date: June 6, 2006.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David D. Withnell, Office of the 
General Counsel, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission at (202) 502-
8421.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Document 06-4904, published on June 6, 
2006 (71 FR 32636) make the following correction:
    On page 32695, in column 3, paragraph nos. 395 and 396 should be 
merged into one paragraph and designated no. 395. Paragraph no. 397 
becomes 396 and the subsequent paragraph numbers are corrected 
accordingly. (The corrected sequence runs from the renumbered paragraph 
no. 396 to the last paragraph in the preamble, which will be paragraph 
no. 499.)
