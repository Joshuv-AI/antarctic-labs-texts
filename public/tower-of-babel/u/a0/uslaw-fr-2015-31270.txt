FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 2015-30856) published on 
page 76287 of the issue for Tuesday, December 8, 2015.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta heading, the entry for 
Seacoast Banking Corporation of Florida, Stuart, Florida, is revised to 
read as follows:
    1. CapGen Capital Group III LP, CapGen Capital Group III LLC, both 
in New York, New York, and Seacoast Banking Corporation of Florida, 
Stuart, Florida; to merge with Floridian Financial Group, Inc., Lake 
Mary, Florida, and thereby indirectly acquire Floridian Bank, Daytona 
Beach, Florida.
    Comments on this application must be received by January 4, 2016.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, December 8, 
2015.
Michael J. Lewandowski,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
