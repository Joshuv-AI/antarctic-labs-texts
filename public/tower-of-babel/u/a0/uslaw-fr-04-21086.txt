SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the final rule, which 
was published in the Federal Register of Wednesday, July 7, 2004 (69 FR 
41130). The final rule related to the Amendment of the Schedule of 
Application Fees.

DATES: Effective on October 1, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Claudette E. Pride, 202-418-1995; E-
mail: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    The final rule that is the subject of these corrections amends the 
Schedule of Applications Fees, 47 CFR 1.1102 et seq., to adjust its 
fees for processing applications and other filings. Section 8(b) of the 
Communications Act, as amended, requires that the Commission review and 
adjust its application fees every two years after October 1, 1991.

Need for Correction

    As published, the final rule contains an error which provides the 
wrong fee amount and payment type code for a license to operate a 
direct broadcast satellite in Sec.  1.1107, Schedule of Charges for 
Applications and Other Filings for the International Service.
