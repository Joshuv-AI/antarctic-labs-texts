FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 04-373, MB Docket No. 03-221, RM-10796]


Television Broadcast Service; Tupelo, MS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of KB Prime Media and United 
Television, Inc., substitutes channel 49+ for channel 35+ at Tupelo, 
Mississippi. See 68 FR 62046, October 31, 2002. TV channel 49+ can be 
allotted to Tupelo, Mississippi, in compliance with Sections 73.610 and 
73.698 at coordinates 33-55-37 N. and 88-33-36 W. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated.
