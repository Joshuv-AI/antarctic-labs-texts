DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Notice of Opportunity for Public Comment To Dispose of 1.93 Acres 
of Land at Eastern Slopes Regional Airport, Fryeburg, ME

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Request for Public Comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is being given that the FAA is considering a request 
from the Town of Fryeburg, ME to dispose of 2.0 Acres of land at 
Eastern Slopes Regional Airport, Fryeburg ME. The land was the site of 
the non-directional beacon located 10.2 nautical miles from the airport 
and was decommissioned and removed from the site. The land is no longer 
needed for aviation purposes and can be disposed without affecting 
future aviation needs of the airport. Ninety percent of the revenue 
generated by the disposal will be returned to the FAA and used for 
future airport grants.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before May 21, 2021.
