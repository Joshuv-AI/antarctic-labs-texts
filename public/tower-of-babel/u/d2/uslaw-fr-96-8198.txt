FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[MM Docket No. 93-17; RM-8170]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Rosendale, NY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
State University of New York, licensee of noncommercial educational 
Station WFNP, Channel *204A, Rosendale, New York, directed to the 
Report and Order in this proceeding which denied its proposal to modify 
the Station license to specify operation on Channel 273A. See 60 FR 
54954, October 27, 1995. This document also dismisses a related 
Emergency Request for Stay of FM Application Window. With this action, 
the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 4, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
