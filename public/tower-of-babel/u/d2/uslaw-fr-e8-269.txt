SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Introduction

    The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is preparing and issuing 
Revision 1 to the Interim Staff Guidance (ISG)-18 document for ``The 
Design and Testing of Lid Welds on Austenitic Stainless Steel Canisters 
as Confinement Boundary for Spent Fuel Storage.'' This ISG document 
provides clarifying guidance to the NRC staff when reviewing licensee 
integrated safety analyses, license applications or amendment requests, 
or other related licensing activities for dry cask storage systems 
under 10 CFR Part 72. The NRC is soliciting public comments on the 
Draft of ISG-18, Revision 1, which will be considered in the final 
version or subsequent revisions.

II. Summary

    The purpose of this notice is to provide the public with an 
opportunity to review and comment on the Draft of ISG-18, Revision 1, 
``The Design and Testing of Lid Welds on Austenitic Stainless Steel 
Canisters as Confinement Boundary for Spent Fuel Storage.''
