DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of the Comptroller of the Currency

[OCC Charter Number 706591]


Commonwealth Bank, F.S.B., Mt. Sterling, Kentucky; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on March 31, 2015, the Office of the 
Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) approved the application of 
Commonwealth Bank, F.S.B., Mt. Sterling, Kentucky, to convert to the 
stock form of organization. Copies of the application are available for 
inspection on the OCC Web site at the FOIA Electronic Reading Room 
https://foia-pal.occ.gov/palMain.aspx. If you have any questions, 
please call OCC Licensing Activities at (202) 649-6260.

    Dated: March 31, 2015.

    By the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency.
Stephen A. Lybarger,
Deputy Comptroller for Licensing.
