North Carolina Electric Membership Corporation v. Duke Energy 
Progress, LLC; Notice Granting Motion To Continue To Hold Proceeding in 
Abeyance

    On March 7, 2022, North Carolina Electric Membership Corporation 
(NCEMC) filed a motion to continue to hold the complaint filed in the 
above-captioned proceeding in abeyance until June 1, 2022. NCEMC 
represents that itself and Duke Energy Progress, LLC (DEP) have been 
negotiating an agreement to implement several changes to the Power 
Supply and Coordination Agreement at issue in this proceeding, but the 
parties need additional time to finalize the agreement. NCEMC asks that 
the Commission delay taking any action in this docket for an additional 
period up to and including June 1, 2022, to allow parties to continue 
to finalize these details. NCEMC also states that DEP supports the 
motion.
    Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that NCEMC's motion is 
granted. As such, the Commission will not take any action on the 
complaint filed in this proceeding until after June 1, 2022.

    Dated: March 16, 2022.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
