COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Proposed Additions to the Procurement List; Correction

    In the document appearing on page 37098, FR Doc. 99-17479, in the 
issue of July 9, 1999, in the second column, the service listed as 
Janitorial/Custodial, Fort Hamilton Proper, Fort Hamilton Manor and 
Fort Hamilton Tenants (less buildings 114 & 116), Fort Hamilton, New 
York should read Janitorial/Custodial, Fort Hamilton Proper, Fort 
Hamilton Manor and Fort Hamilton Tenants, Fort Hamilton, New York.
Beverly L. Milkman,
Executive Director.
