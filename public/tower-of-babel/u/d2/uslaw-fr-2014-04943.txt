DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Disability Compensation, Notice of Meeting

    The Department of Veterans Affairs gives notice under 92 (Federal 
Advisory Committee Act) that the meeting of the Advisory Committee on 
Disability Compensation scheduled to be held at VA Central Office, 810 
Vermont Avenue NW., Washington, DC on March 3-4, 2014 has been 
cancelled.
    For more information, please contact Ms. Nancy Copeland, Designated 
Federal Officer at (202) 461-9684.

    Dated: March 3, 2014.
Jelessa Burney,
Committee Management Officer.
