COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meetings of the Nebraska Advisory Committee

AGENCY: U.S. Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Announcement of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
rules and regulations of the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights 
(Commission) and the Federal Advisory Committee Act that the Nebraska 
Advisory Committee (Committee) will hold a series of meetings via Webex 
on Monday, December 14th, Wednesday, December 16th, Thursday, January 
7th at 9:00 a.m. Central Time for the purpose of gathering testimony on 
the Impact of Native American Mascot names in Nebraska.
