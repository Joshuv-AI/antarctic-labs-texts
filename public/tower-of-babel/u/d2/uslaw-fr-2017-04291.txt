NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[NRC-2017-0054]


Request for a License To Export Radioactive Waste

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Update of export license application and extension of comment 
period; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register on February 16, 2017, 
regarding a request for a license to export radioactive waste. This 
action is necessary in order to provide the correct Agencywide Document 
Access and Management System accession number for the export license 
application.

DATES: The correction is effective March 6, 2017.
