NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

 

Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications and Amendments to 
Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Consideration

    On December 21, 1994 (59 FR 65809), the Federal Register published 
the Biweekly Notice of Applications and Amendments to Operating 
Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Considerations. In the first 
sentence of the last paragraph in the second column, on page 65809, the 
date which reads ``January 13, 1995,'' should be corrected to read 
``January 20, 1995.''

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 22nd day of December 1994.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Frederick J. Hebdon,
Acting Director, Division of Reactor Projects--I/II, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
