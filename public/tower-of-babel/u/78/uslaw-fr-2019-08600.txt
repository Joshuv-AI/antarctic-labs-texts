FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

2018

1. Staff Briefing on Georgia--South Carolina Marine Terminal Operator

2. Staff Briefing on Puerto Nuevo Terminals LLC Cooperative Working 
Agreement, Agreement No. 201292
