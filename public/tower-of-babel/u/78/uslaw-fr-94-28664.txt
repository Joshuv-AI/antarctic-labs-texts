DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. ES95-10-000]

 

MDU Resources Group, Inc., Notice of Application

November 15, 1994.
    Take notice that on November 9, 1994, MDU Resources Group Inc., 
filed an application under Section 204 of the Federal Power Act seeking 
authorization to issue up to $50 million of short-term indebtedness 
through December 31, 1996, with a final maturity date no later than 
December 31, 1997.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protect with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426 in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before December 8, 1994. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make the protestants parties 
to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a 
motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
