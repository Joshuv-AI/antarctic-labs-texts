Known Gilsonite Leasing Area

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Known Leasing Area.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that based upon recent geologic 
information the following lands have been classified as the Bonanza, 
Cowboy, Independent, Little Emma and Wagon Hound Known Leasing Areas 
for gilsonite. Detailed information regarding this action, a 
description of the lands included in the Bonanza, Cowboy, Independent, 
Little Emma and Wagon Hound Known Leasing Areas, and the gilsonite 
Master Title Plats (MTPs) showing the boundaries of the proposed known 
leasing areas, are available to the public in the Public Room of the 
Utah State Office of the BLM.

DATES: Effective Date: December 19, 2006.

ADDRESSES: Inquiries should be sent to the State Director (UT-923), 
Bureau of Land Management, Utah State Office, 440 West 200 South, Suite 
500, Salt Lake City, Utah 84101.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stan Perkes (801) 539-4036.
