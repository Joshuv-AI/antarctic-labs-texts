Government-Owned Inventions, Available for Licensing

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Availability of Inventions for Licensing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The inventions listed below are assigned to the National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration, have been filed in the United 
States Patent and Trademark Office, and are available for licensing.

DATES: June 19, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Guy M. Miller, Patent Counsel, Goddard 
Space Flight Center, Mail Code 750.2, Greenbelt, MD 20771; 301-286-
7351.

Fixture for Fiber Optic Applications;

NASA Case No. GSC 14,106-1: Automated Liquid Helium Transport System;

Optically Polished Surfaces On Bare Aluminum Substrates;

Adjustment of Springback Reflectors;
NASA Case No. GSC 14,205-1: Continuously Variable Planetary 
Transmission(CVPT);
NASA Case No. GSC 14,207-1: Gear Bearings;
NASA Case No. GSC 14,213-1: Estimated Spectrum Adaptive Postfilter

Applications;
