DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Government-Owned Invention; Available 
for Licensing

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The following invention is assigned to the U.S. Government as 
represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available for licensing 
by the Department of the Navy.

U.S. Patent Number 6,562,160 B2 entitled ``Airbag Propellant.''

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the Patent cited should be directed 
to the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Code CAB, 101 Strauss Avenue, 
Indian Head, MD 20640-5035.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. J. Scott Deiter, Head, Technology 
Transfer Office, Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division, 
Code CAB, 101 Strauss Avenue, Indian Head, MD 20640-5035, telephone 
301-744-6111.
