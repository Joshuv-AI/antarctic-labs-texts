47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2081, MM Docket No.01-127, RM-10132]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Pittsburg, KS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Saga Quad States 
Communications, Inc., licensee of station KOAM-TV, substitutes DTV 
channel 13 for DTV channel 30 at Pittsburg, Kansas. See 66 FR 34400, 
June 28, 2001. DTV channel 13 can be allotted to Pittsburg in 
compliance with the principle community coverage requirements of 
Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 37-13-15 N. and 94-42-23 W. 
with a power of 4.2, HAAT of 336 meters and with a DTV service 
population of 357 thousand.
    With is action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 22, 2001.
