DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-93-000]


Southern Natural Gas Company; Notice of Refund Report

December 23, 1997.
    Take notice that on December 19, 1997 Southern Natural Gas Company 
(Southern) tendered for filing a Refund Report.
    Southern states that pursuant to Section 23.3 of the General Terms 
and Conditions of Southern's Tariff the Refund Report sets forth Rate 
Schedule ISS revenues to be refunded to Rate Schedule CSS customers.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest this filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Sections 385.214 and 385.211 of the Commission's 
Rules and Regulations. All such motions or protest should be filed on 
or before December 31, 1997. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person 
wishing to become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
