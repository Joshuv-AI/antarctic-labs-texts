Reporting Requirements for Foreign Gifts and Decorations

AGENCY: Office of Governmentwide Policy, GSA.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Section 101-49.001-5 currently defines the minimal value for 
reporting foreign gifts as $225. Public Law 95-105 requires that at 3-
year intervals following January 1, 1981, minimal value be redefined by 
the Administrator of General Services, after consultation with the 
Secretary of State, to reflect changes in the consumer price index for 
the immediately preceding 3-year period. The required consultation has 
been completed and the minimal value has been increased to $245.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 1996.
FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Martha S. Caswell, Director, Personal Property Management Policy
