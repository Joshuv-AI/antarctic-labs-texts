Fashionland, Inc., Jersey City, New Jersey; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 1, 1999, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers Fashionland, Inc., Jersey City, New Jersey.
    Repeated attempts to contact officials of the subject firm proved 
unsuccessful. Consequently, further investigation in this case would 
serve no purpose; and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 28th day of April, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
