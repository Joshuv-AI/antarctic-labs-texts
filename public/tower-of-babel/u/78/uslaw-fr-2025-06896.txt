Missisquoi, LLC; Notice of Reasonable Period of Time for Water 
Quality Certification Application

    On April 9, 2025, Missisquoi, LLC submitted to the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (Commission) documentation from the Vermont 
Department of Environmental Conservation (Vermont DEC) that it received 
a request for a Clean Water Act section 401(a)(1) water quality 
certification as defined in 40 CFR 121.5, from Missisquoi, LLC, in 
conjunction with the above captioned project on April 2, 2025. Pursuant 
to the Commission's regulations,\1\ we hereby notify Vermont DEC of the 
following.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 18 CFR 4.34(b)(5)(iii).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Date of Receipt of the Certification Request: April 2, 2025.
    Reasonable Period of Time to Act on the Certification Request: One 
year, April 2, 2026.
    If Vermont DEC fails or refuses to act on the water quality 
certification request on or before the above date, then the certifying 
authority is deemed waived pursuant to section 401(a)(1) of the Clean 
Water Act, 33 U.S.C. 1341(a)(1).

    Dated: April 16, 2025.
Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Secretary.
