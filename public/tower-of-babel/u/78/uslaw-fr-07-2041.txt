DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Subcommittee Site Visit of the President's Commission on Care for 
America's Returning Wounded Warriors

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Section 10(a), Public Law 92-462, as amended, 
notice is hereby given of a forthcoming subcommittee site visit of the 
President's Commission on America's Returning Wounded Warriors. The 
purpose of the subcommittee site visit is to gather information.

DATES: Tuesday, 8 May 2007.
    Location: Richmond, Virginia, McGuire Veterans Affairs Medical 
Center, 1201 Broad Rock Blvd, Phone 804-675-5000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Col. Denise Daily, 703-588-0439.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.
