                Sunshine Act Meetings





U.S. CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., Tuesday, December 20, 1994.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the public.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

Protocol Revisions

    The staff will brief the Commission on a final rule revising the 
child-resistant packaging test protocols under the Poison Prevention 
Packaging Act.

    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207 (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: December 14, 1994.
Sadye E. Dunn,

Secretary.
