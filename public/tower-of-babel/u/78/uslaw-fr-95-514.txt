FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD


Employee Thrift Advisory Council; Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), a notice is hereby given of the 
following committee meeting:

    Name: Employee Thrift Advisory Council.

    Time: 10:00 A.M.

    Date: January 24, 1995.

    Place: Fourth Floor, Conference Room, Federal Retirement Thrift 
Investment Board, 1250 H Street, NW., Washington, DC.

    Status: Open.

    Matters to be considered:
    Approval of the minutes of the June 14, 1994, meeting; report of 
the Executive Director on the status of the Thrift Savings Plan; 
legislation; nomination of Council Chairman and election of Vice-
Chairman; and new business.

    Any interested person may attend, appear before, or file statements 
with the Council. For further information contact John J. O'Meara, 
Committee Management Officer, on (202) 942-1660.


    Dated: January 4, 1995.
Roger W. Mehle,
Executive Director, Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.
