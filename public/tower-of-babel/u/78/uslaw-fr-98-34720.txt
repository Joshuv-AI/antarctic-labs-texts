SUMMARY: This notice provides official publication of the legal 
description of Juniper Butte Range withdrawal as required by section 
2903(a)(1) of Public Law 105-261 enacted October 17, 1998.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 31, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jon Foster, BLM Idaho State Office, 
1387 S. Vinnell Way, Boise, Idaho 83709, 208-373-3813.
