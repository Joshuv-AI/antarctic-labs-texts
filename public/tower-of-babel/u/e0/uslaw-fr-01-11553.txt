SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Federal regulations at 50 CFR 229.34 limit the number of nets that 
can be used in certain fisheries in the mid-Atlantic that appear to be 
most closely linked with accidental catch of harbor porpoises. 
Fishermen in these fisheries must obtain and attach numbered tags for 
their nets. Because the number of tags per vessel is capped, the 
tagging program helps to limit the number of nets in use and helps NOAA 
identify the number in use.

II. Method of Collection

    Requests for tags are submitted to NOAA on a paper form.
