NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 04-085]


NASA Advisory Council, Space Science Advisory Committee; Meeting

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, Public 
Law 92-463, as amended, the National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration announces a forthcoming meeting of the NASA Advisory 
Council (NAC), Space Science Advisory Committee (SScAC).

DATES: Wednesday, July 28, 2004, 1 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., Thursday, July 
29, 2004, 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., and Friday, July 30, 2004, 8:30 a.m. 
to Noon.

ADDRESSES: Shelter Pointe Hotel and Marina, 1551 Island Drive, San 
Diego, CA 92106.

[[Page 42464]]


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Marian R. Norris, Office of Space 
Science, National Aeronautics and Space Administration, Washington, DC 
20546, (202) 358-4452.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting will be open to the public up to 
the capacity of the room. The agenda for the meeting includes the 
following topics:
     Program Overview Status
     Subcommittee Reports
     Report on Explorer Program Phasing
     Status of Space Science Education and Public Outreach 
Programs
     Sounding Rockets Program Status
     Annual Review of Science Achievements for FY 2004 
Government Performance Results Act Performance Report
     Update on NASA Reorganization
    It is imperative that the meeting be held on these dates to 
accommodate the scheduling priorities of the key participants. Visitors 
to the meeting will be requested to sign a visitor's register.

R. Andrew Falcon,
Advisory Committee Management Officer, National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration.
