FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-1271, MM Docket No. 01-53, RM-10040]


Television Broadcast Service; Galesburg, IL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Northwest Television, Inc., 
substitutes TV channel 53 for TV channel 67 at Galesburg, Illinois. See 
66 FR 12922, March 1, 2001. TV channel 53 can be allotted to Galesburg 
with a zero offset in compliance with the principle community coverage 
requirements of Section 73.610 and with the criteria set forth in the 
Commission's Public Notice released on November 22, 1999, DA 99-2605. 
The coordinates for channel 53 at Galesburg are (41-18-45 N and 90-22-
45 W.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective July 9, 2001.
