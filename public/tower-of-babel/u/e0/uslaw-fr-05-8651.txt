National Historic Oregon Trail Interpretive Center Advisory Board 
Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management (BLM).

ACTION: Meeting notice for National Historic Oregon Trail Interpretive 
Center Advisory Board

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Historic Oregon Trail Interpretive Center 
Advisory Board will meet June 1, 2005, from 1 to 5 p.m. (PDT) at the 
Best Western Sunridge Inn, One Sunridge Way, Baker City, Oregon.
    Meeting topics may include the long-range marketing plan, education 
and outreach, the strategic plan versus budget, and other topics as may 
come before the board. The meeting is open to the public. Public 
comment is scheduled for 2 to 2:15 p.m.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Additional information concerning the 
National Historic Oregon Trail Interpretive Center Advisory Board may 
be obtained from Debbie Lyons, Public Affairs Officer, Vale District 
Office, 100 Oregon Street, Vale, Oregon 97918, (541) 473-6218 or e-mail 
[email protected].

    Dated: April 26, 2005.
Tom Terry,
Acting District Manager.
