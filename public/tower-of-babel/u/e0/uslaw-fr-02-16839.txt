DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 3615-002]


Drew River Mill, Inc.; Notice of Site Visit

June 28, 2002.
    The staff of the Office of Energy Projects (OEP) will be conducting 
a site visit for the Branch River Mill Project (Project) on August 1, 
2002. Representative(s) of Drew River Mill, Inc. will accompany the OEP 
staff. All interested parties may meet at 9:30 a.m. at the Project dam. 
Attendees must provide their own transportation.


[[Page 44828]]


    For further information, please contact the Commission's Office 
of External Affairs at 1-866-208-FERC.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
