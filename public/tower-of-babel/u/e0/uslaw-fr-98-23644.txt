Cincinnati Gas & Electric Company, et al.; Notice of Comment 
Period

August 26, 1998.
    At the Midwest Electric Pricing Issues conference held August 14, 
1998, in Rosemont, Illinois, interested parties were invited to file 
written observations or comments on the issues addressed at that 
meeting. Any comments should be filed on or before September 14, 1998. 
They should be filed in Docket No. EL98-53-000.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
