NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Nuclear Waste; Revised

    The 104th meeting of the Advisory Committee on Nuclear Waste (ACNW) 
has been rescheduled from October 20-22, 1998 to October 20 and 21, 
1998, at the Longstreet Inn, Conference Room Colorado #2, Stateline 
373, Amargosa Valley, Nevada. Presentations by the Department of Energy 
on Site Characterization and Viability Assessment will be rescheduled. 
The ACNW review of the NRC staff's Format and Content Guide for Reactor 
License Termination has been canceled. The Committee will not hold any 
sessions in Las Vegas as was previously announced.
    All other items pertaining to this meeting remain the same as 
published in the Federal Register on Thursday, September 10, 1998 (63 
FR 48532).
    Further information regarding this meeting can be obtained by 
contacting Mr. Richard K. Major, Chief, Nuclear Waste Branch (telephone 
301/415-7366), between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. EDT.

    Dated: September 29, 1998.
Andrew L. Bates,
Advisory Committee Management OFficer.
