DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

RTCA, Inc., Special Committee 162, Aviation Systems Design 
Guidelines for Open Systems Interconnection; Meeting

    Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
(Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., Appendix I), notice is hereby given for 
Special Committee 162 meeting to be held March 29-31, starting at 9:30 
a.m. The meeting will be held at the Stanford Telecom (STEL): Steve 
VanTrees, Phone: (703) 438-8014.
    The agenda for this meeting is as follows: (1) Chairman's 
introductory remarks; (2) Approval of the summary of the twentieth 
meeting held May 12-13, 1993. RTCA Paper No. 276-93/SC162-166 
(previously distributed); (3) Reports of Related Activities being 
conducted by other organizations; (5) Review and plan the activity of 
Working Group 1, ``ATN MASPS & Router MOPS.''; (6) Plan the activity of 
Working Group 2, ``Rewrite DO-205, Part 1.''; (7) Review industry 
systems management activity; (8) Other business; (9) Date and place of 
next meeting. (7) Date and place of next meeting.
    Attendance is open to the interested public but limited to space 
available. With the approval of the Chairman, members of the public may 
present oral statements at the meeting. Persons wishing to present 
statements or obtain information should contact the RTCA Secretariat, 
1140 Connecticut Avenue, NW., suite 1020, Washington, DC 20036; (202) 
833-9339. Any member of the public may present a written statement to 
the committee at any time.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on February 15, 1994.
    Note: The first day, March 29, the SC-162 plenary will meet. The 
following two days, March 30-31, will be a working session to begin 
drafting the Avionics Router MOPS. The meeting will be held at STEL 
for all three days.
Joyce J. Gillen,
Designated Officer.
