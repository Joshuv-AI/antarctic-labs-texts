[Program Announcement 02191]


Expansion of HIV/AIDS/TB Care and Prevention Activities Among 
People With HIV/AIDS in the Republic of Uganda; Notice of Availability 
of Funds; Amendment

    A notice announcing the availability of Fiscal Year 2002 funds for 
cooperative agreements for the Expansion of HIV/AIDS/TB Care and 
Prevention Activities Among People with HIV/AIDS in the Republic of 
Uganda was published in the Federal Register, June 20, 2002, Volume 67, 
No. 119, pages 42006-42007. The notice is amended as follows: On page 
42006, Column 3, Paragraph ``I. Submission and Deadline'', remove 
``July 17, 2002'' and insert in its place ``August 9, 2002''.

    Dated: July 24, 2002.
Rebecca B. O'Kelley,
Acting Director, Procurement and Grants Office, Centers for Disease 
Control and Prevention.
