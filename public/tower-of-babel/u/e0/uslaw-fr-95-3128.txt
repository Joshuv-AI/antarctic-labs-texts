DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Notice of Merger of Approved Trustee

    Notice is hereby given that Ameritrust Texas, National Association, 
Houston, Texas, changed its name to Texas Commerce Trust Company, 
National Association effective September 28, 1993. Texas Commerce Trust 
Company, National Association merged with and into Texas Commerce Bank, 
National Association, P. O. Box 2558, Houston, Texas 77252-8341, 
effective December 17, 1993, with Texas Commerce Bank, National 
Association as the surviving corporation in the merger.

    Dated: February 2, 1995.

    By Order of the Maritime Administrator.
Murray A. Bloom,
Acting Secretary.
