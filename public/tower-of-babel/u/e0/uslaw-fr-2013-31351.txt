Reservations: Income Deductions and Resource Eligibility was published

associated information collection requirements (ICR) on September 26, 
2013. This document announces approval of the ICR.

DATES: The ICR associated with the final rule published in the Federal 
Register on August 27, 2013, at 78 FR 52827, was approved by OMB on 
September 26, 2013, under OMB Control Number 0584-0293.
