DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the 2003 Science and Technology Review. The 
purpose of the meeting is to allow the SAB and study leadership to 
assess the quality and long-term relevance of scientific research. 
Because contractor-proprietary information will be discussed, this 
meeting will be closed to the public.

DATES: October 21-25, 2002.

ADDRESSES: Holiday Inn, Palmdale, CA.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Major John Pernot, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
