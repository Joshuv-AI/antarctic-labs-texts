Compliance Policy Guides (CPG's); Revocation

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing the 
revocation of six CPG's because they contain outdated regulatory 
guidance. This action is being taken to ensure that FDA's CPG's reflect 
current FDA policy.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 26, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ronald C. Varsaci, Center for Food 
Safety and Applied Nutrition (HFS-22), Food and Drug Administration, 
200 C St. SW., Washington, DC 20204, 202-205-4251.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: FDA is revoking the following six CPG's 
because they contain outdated regulatory guidance:
     (1) CPG 7101.02--``Caffeine, Ingredient in Carbonated Beverages''
     (2) CPG 7105.06--``Orgeat or `Orzata' Sirup, Definition and 
Labeling''
     (3) CPG 7105.08--``Sirup-Labeling--Use of Descriptive Statements''
     (4) CPG 7105.10--``Candy Pills--Representation as Drugs''
     (5) CPG 7128.01--``Bithionol in Cosmetics''
     (6) CPG 7108.19--``Polychlorinated Biphenyls (PCB's) in Certain 
Freshwater Fish''
