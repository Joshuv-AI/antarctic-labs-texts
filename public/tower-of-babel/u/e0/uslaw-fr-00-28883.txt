FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 00-2374; MM Docket No. 00-149; RM-9940]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Smiley, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Smiley Community Radio Company filed a petition for rule 
making proposing the allotment of Channel 280A at Smiley, Texas, as the 
community's first local aural transmission service. See 65 FR 53689, 
September 5, 2000. On October 10, 2000, petitioner filed a request for 
withdrawal. A showing of continuing interest is required before a 
channel will be allotted. It is the Commission's policy to refrain from 
making an allotment to a community absent an expression of interest. No 
other comments were filed. Therefore, at the request of petitioner, we 
will dismiss the instant proposal.
