President's Council on the 21st Century Workforce and the 
Committees on Skills Gap, Demographics and Workplace Issues;

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary of Labor.

ACTION: Notice of postponement of meeting of the President's Council on 
the 21st Century Workforce and meeting of Committees.
