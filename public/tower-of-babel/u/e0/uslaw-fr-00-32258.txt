FEDERAL MINE SAFETY AND HEALTH REVIEW COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Change in Meeting Time and Date

PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TIME AND DATE: 2 p.m., Tuesday, December 12, 2000.

PLACE: Room 6005, 6th Floor, 1730 K Street, NW., Washington, DC.

CHANGES IN THE MEETING: The time and date of the Commission meeting to 
consider and act upon Disciplinary Proceeding, Docket No. D 2000-1, has 
been changed to commence following upon the conclusion of the 
Commission meeting starting at 10 a.m., December 13, 2000, to consider 
the Commission's general procedures for handling requests to vacate 
defaults. No earlier announcement of these changes was possible.

for TDD Relay/1-800-877-8339 for toll free.
