Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 01-20056) published on page 
42229 of the issue for Friday, August 10, 2001.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for Team Financial Acquisition Subsidiary, Inc., Paola, Kansas, was 
published twice. The correct end of the comment period for this 
application was August 1, 2001.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, August 14, 
2001.
