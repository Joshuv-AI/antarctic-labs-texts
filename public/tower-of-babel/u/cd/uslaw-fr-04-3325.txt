International Mill Service, Midland, Pennsylvania; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 24, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by the United Steelworkers of America Local 1212 on 
behalf of workers at International Mill Service, Midland, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 20th day of January, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
