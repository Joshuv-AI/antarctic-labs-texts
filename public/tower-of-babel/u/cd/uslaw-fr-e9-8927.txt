Freescale Semiconductor, Technical Support Engineering, Austin, 
TX; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 23, 2009 in response to a 
worker petition filed on behalf of workers of Freescale Semiconductor, 
Technical Support Engineering, Austin, Texas.
    The petitioners requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 26th day of March 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
