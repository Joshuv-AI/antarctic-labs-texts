Bremner Incorporated, Ripon, WI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 22, 2004 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Bremner Incorporated, Ripon, 
Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 9th day of February 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
