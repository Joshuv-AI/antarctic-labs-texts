DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for 2011 American Eagle Silver Proof and Uncirculated 
Coins

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the re-pricing of the 
2011 American Eagle Silver Proof and Uncirculated Coins. The price of 
the 2011 American Eagle Silver Proof Coins will be lowered from $68.45 
to $58.95, and the price of the 2011 American Eagle Silver Uncirculated 
Coins will be lowered from $60.45 to $50.95.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B. B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.
