Westpoint Stevens, Clemson Finishing Plant, Clemson, SC; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 7, 2004 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at WestPoint 
Stevens, Clemson Finishing Plant, Clemson, South Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 16th day of November, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
