Tax Shelter Regulations; Correction

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Treasury.

ACTION: Correction to final regulations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to final regulations (TD 
9046) which were published in the Federal Register on Tuesday, March 4, 
2003 (68 FR 10161), relating to tax shelter regulations.

DATES: This correction is effective March 4, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Tara P. Volungis or Charlotte Chyr at 
(202) 622-3070 (not a toll-free number).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    The final regulations that are subject to these corrections are 
under sections 6011(a), 6111(d) and 6112 of the Internal Revenue Code.

[[Page 15943]]

Need for correction

    As published, final regulations (TD 9046) contains errors that may 
prove to be misleading and are in need of clarification.
