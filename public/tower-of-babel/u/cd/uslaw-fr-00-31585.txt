DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice No. 3469]


Shipping Coordinating Committee; Subcommittee on Standards of 
Training and Watchkeeping; Notice of Meeting Cancellation

    On November 15, 2000, 65 FR 69118, the United States Department of 
State published notice #3467 to announce a meeting of the Shipping 
Coordinating

[[Page 77417]]

Committee to be held on Thursday, December 14, 2000. The purpose of 
this meeting was to review the agenda items to be considered at the 
thirty-second session of the Subcommittee on Standards of Training and 
Watchkeeping (STW 32) of the International Maritime Organization (IMO).
    This notice is to announce that the meeting is cancelled.
    For further information, please contact Chief, Office of Operating 
and Environmental Standards, U.S. Coast Guard Headquarters, Commandant 
(G-MSO), room 1210, 2100 Second Street, SW, Washington, DC, 20593-0001 
or by calling LCDR Luke Harden at: (202) 267-1838.

    December 6, 2000.
Mira Piplani,
International Transportation and Commercial Officer.
