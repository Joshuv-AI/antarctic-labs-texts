HARRY S. TRUMAN SCHOLARSHIP FOUNDATION


Closing Date for Nominations From Eligible Institutions of Higher 
Education; Notice

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority 
contained in the Harry S. Truman Memorial Scholarship Act, Pub. L. 93-
642 (20 U.S.C. 2001), nominations are being accepted from eligible 
institutions of higher education for Truman Scholarship. Procedures are 
prescribed at 45 CFR 1801.
    In order to be assured consideration, all documentation in support 
of nominations must be received by the Truman Scholarship Review 
Committee,

[[Page 64909]]

2255 North Dubuque Road, P.O. Box 168, Iowa City, IA 52243 no later 
than January 23, 1997.

    Dated: December 1, 1996.
Louis H. Blair,
Executive Secretary.
