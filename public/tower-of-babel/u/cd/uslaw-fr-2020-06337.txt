DEPARTMENT OF STATE

U.S. Department of State Advisory Committee on Private 
International Law: Notice of Cancellation of Meeting

    Due to concerns surrounding the spread of COVID-19, the Department 
of State's Advisory Committee on Private International Law (ACPIL) is 
cancelling its meeting previously scheduled meeting on Friday, April 
17, 2020 in Washington, DC. This meeting will be re-scheduled at a 
later date. The Department of State will issue a Federal Register 
Notice with details.
    For additional information, contact Sharla Draemel 
([email protected]) or Tricia Smeltzer ([email protected]).

Zachary A. Parker,
Director, Office of Directives Management, Department of State.
