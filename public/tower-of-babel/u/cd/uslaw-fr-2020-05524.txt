DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Arthritis ond Musculoskeletal and Skin 
Diseases; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, which was published in the Federal Register on January 
30, 2020, 85 FRN 5461.
    The NIAMS Special Emphasis Panel meeting is being amended due to a 
change in the meeting format. This one-day meeting held on 3/31/2020 
will be a teleconference meeting. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 12, 2020.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
