Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Digestive 
Diseases and Nutrition C Study Section, March 13, 2024, 05:00 p.m. to 
March 15, 2024, 05:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, NIDDK, 
Democracy II, Suite 7000A, 6707 Democracy Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892

9164.

public.
