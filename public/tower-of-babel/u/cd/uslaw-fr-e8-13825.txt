Board of Scientific Counselors, National Center for Environmental 
Research (NCER) Standing Subcommittee Meeting--2008

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of Cancellation of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency, Office of Research and 
Development (ORD), announces the cancellation of a meeting of the Board 
of Scientific Counselors (BOSC) National Center for Environmental 
Research (NCER) Standing Subcommittee. This meeting, a teleconference 
June 24, 2008, was announced in a Federal Register Notice published on 
Friday, May 30, 2008 (Volume 73, Number 105, page 31116). The purpose 
of this meeting was to discuss the subcommittee's draft letter report 
and NCER's next charge question(s), and it will be rescheduled at a 
later date.
