ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-6105-1]


National Advisory Council for Environmental Policy and 
Technology; Notice of Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The charter for the Environmental Protection Agency's (EPA) 
National Advisory Council for Environmental Policy and Technology 
(NACEPT) will be renewed for an additional two-year period as a 
necessary committee that is in the public's interest, and in accordance 
with the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee Act (FACA), 5 
U.S.C. appl.2 section 9(c). The purpose of NACEPT is to provide advice 
and counsel to the Administrator of EPA on issues associated with 
environmental management and policy. It is determined that NACEPT is in 
the public's interest in connection with the performance of duties 
imposed on the Agency by law.
    Inquiries may be directed to Gwendolyn Whitt, Designated Federal 
Officer, NACEPT, U.S. EPA (1601-F), 401 M Street, SW, Washington, D.C. 
20460.

    Dated: May 8, 1998.
Gwendolyn Whitt,
Designated Federal Officer.
