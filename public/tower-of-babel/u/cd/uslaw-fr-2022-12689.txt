FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[FR ID 90341]


Open Commission Meeting Wednesday, June 8, 2022

June 2, 2022.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Wednesday, June 8, 2022, which is 
scheduled to commence at 10:30 a.m.
    Due to the current COVID-19 pandemic and related agency telework 
and headquarters access policies, this meeting will be in an electronic 
format and will be open to the public only on

[[Page 35774]]

the internet via live feed from the FCC's web page at www.fcc.gov/live 
and on the FCC's YouTube channel.
