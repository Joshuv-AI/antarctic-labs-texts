DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Staff Attendance at the Southwest Power Pool, Regional 
State Committee, Members' Committee and Board of Directors' Meetings

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of its staff may attend the meetings of the 
Southwest Power Pool, Inc. Regional State Committee (RSC), Human 
Resource Committee (HR), Finance Committee (FC), Members' Committee and 
Board of Directors as noted below. Their attendance is part of the 
Commission's ongoing outreach efforts.
    The meetings will be held at the Doubletree Warren Place, 6110 
South Yale Place, Tulsa, OK 74136. The phone number is (918) 495-1000. 
All meetings are Central Time.

SPP RSC

April 29, 2019 (8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. CDT)

SPP HR

April 29, 2019 (8:00 a.m.-1:00 p.m. CDT)

SPP FC

April 29, 2019 (8:00 a.m.-11:30 p.m. CDT)

SPP Members/Board of Directors

April 30, 2019 (8:00 a.m.-2:00 p.m. CDT)
