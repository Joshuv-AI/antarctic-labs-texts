Submission for OMB Review: Community Urban County/New York Towns 
Qualification/Requalification Process

AGENCY: Office of the Assistant Secretary for Administration, HUD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The urban county/New York towns qualification/requalification 
process obtains information each year that establishes the 
participating population that is used by HUD in calculating the final 
grant allocation of CDBG funds for all entitlement (which includes 
metro cities and urban counties) and State CDBG grantees for the next 
fiscal year.
