Notice of Public Meeting on Voluntary Initiatives To Combat 
Infringement of Intellectual Property in the Online Environment; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: United States Patent and Trademark Office, Department of 
Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Patent and Trademark Office published a 
notice in the Federal Register of June 22, 2017, concerning a public 
meeting on measuring the impact of voluntary initiatives undertaken to 
reduce intellectual property infringement, scheduled for July 17, 2017, 
at its headquarters in Alexandria, Virginia. This notice announces that 
the July 17, 2017 meeting has been cancelled.
