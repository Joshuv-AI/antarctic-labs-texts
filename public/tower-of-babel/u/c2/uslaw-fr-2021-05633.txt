DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Institute of Standards and Technology


Agency Information Collection Activities; Submission to the 
Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for Review and Approval; Comment 
Request; NIST Associates Information System; Correction

AGENCY: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), 
Commerce.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 15, 2021, the Department of Commerce, published a 30-
day public comment period notice in the Federal Register with FR 
Document Number 2021-05296 (Pages 14313-14314), and on January 7, 2021, 
published a 60-day public comment period notice with FR Document Number 
2021-00056 (Page 1089) seeking public comments for an information 
collection entitled, ``NIST Associates Information System.'' This 
document referenced incorrect information in the DATA section, and 
Commerce hereby issues a correction notice as required by the Paperwork 
Reduction Act of 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For additional information concerning 
this correction, contact Maureen O'Reilly, NIST, Management Analyst, at 
[email protected].

the title of the collection or the OMB Control Number 0693-0067.
