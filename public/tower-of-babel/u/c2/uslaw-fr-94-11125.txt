DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
 

Notice of Disposition of Application for Duty-Free Entry of 
Scientific Instrument

    We have been advised that no tariff was levied on the entry covered 
by Docket Number 94-017 (See notice at 59 FR 13705, March 23, 1994). We 
are treating the docket as a withdrawal pursuant to Sec. 301.5(g) of 
the regulations and have discontinued processing.
Pamela Woods
Acting Director, Statutory Import Programs Staff
