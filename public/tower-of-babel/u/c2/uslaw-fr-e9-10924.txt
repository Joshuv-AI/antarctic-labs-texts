Chromalox Precision, Pittsburgh, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 6, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers of Chromalox Precision, Pittsburgh, 
Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of April 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
