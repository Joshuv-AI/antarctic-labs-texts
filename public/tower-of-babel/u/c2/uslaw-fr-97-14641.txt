Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Montana Power Company; Notice of Informal Settlement Conference

May 30, 1997.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Tuesday, June 10, 1997, at 10:00 
a.m. in a room to be designed at the offices of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Pamela Seeley at (202) 208-0528.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
