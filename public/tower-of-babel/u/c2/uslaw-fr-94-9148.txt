DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-29,470]

 

ICI Fiberite, Greenville, TX; Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 7, 1994, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on February 7, 1994, on behalf of workers at ICI 
Fiberite, Greenville, Texas.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 6th day of April, 1994.
Marvin M. Fooks,
Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
