DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Surface Transportation Board

[STB Ex Parte No. 290 (Sub No. 5) (2003-4)]


Quarterly Rail Cost Adjustment Factor

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board, DOT.

ACTION: Approval of rail cost adjustment factor.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 54932]]

SUMMARY: The Board has approved the fourth quarter 2003 rail cost 
adjustment factor (RCAF) and cost index filed by the Association of 
American Railroads. The fourth quarter 2003 RCAF (Unadjusted) is 1.017. 
The fourth quarter 2003 RCAF (Adjusted) is 0.515. The fourth quarter 
2003 RCAF-5 is 0.490.

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 1, 2003.

8339.
