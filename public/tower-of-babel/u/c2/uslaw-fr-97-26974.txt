DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Women Veterans; Notice of Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (Public Law 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App.), that the Department of 
Veterans Affairs' Advisory Committee on Women Veterans has been renewed 
for a 2-year period beginning September 26, 1997, through September 26, 
1999.

    Dated: September 30, 1997.

    By Direction of the Secretary-Designate.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
