National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Cancer 
Institute Special Emphasis Panel, June 7, 2013, 10:00 a.m. to June 7, 
2013, 1:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, National Cancer 
Institute, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Room 7W640, Rockville, MD, 20850 
which was published in the

[[Page 30933]]

Federal Register on May 14, 2013, 78 FR 11346.
    Meeting has been canceled.

    Dated: May 17, 2013.

Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
