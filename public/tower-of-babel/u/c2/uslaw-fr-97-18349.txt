Pennsylvania-New Jersey-Maryland Interconnection Restructuring; 
Notice of Filing

July 8, 1997.
    Take notice that on June 19, 1997, the Coalition for a Competitive 
Electric Market (CCEM) filed a Capacity Rights Open-Access Transmission 
Tariff for restructuring the PJM Interconnection in accordance with 
Order No. 888. Copies of the filing were served on the regulatory 
commissions of Delaware, the District of Columbia, Maryland, New 
Jersey, Pennsylvania, and Virginia.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before July 23, 1997. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
