DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Clinical Trials and Translational Research Advisory 
Committee, July 10, 2013, 09:00 a.m. to July 10, 2013, 04:00 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, 31 Center Drive, Building 31, C Wing, 
6th Floor, Conference Room 10, Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published 
in the Federal Register on April 24, 2013, 78FR24225.
    The notice is being amended to change the meeting format and time. 
The meeting will be held as a virtual meeting on July 10, 2013, 10:00 
a.m. to 12:00 p.m. The webinar meeting may be viewed at http://videocast.nih.gov/. Please note that the meeting is open to the public 
and you may attend the virtual meeting in person. The meeting will be 
broadcast from the National Institutes of Health, 31 Center Drive, 
Building 31, C Wing, 6th Floor, Conference Room 10, Bethesda, MD 20892.

    Dated: June 4, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
