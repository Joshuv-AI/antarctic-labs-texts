Bureau of Land Management

Notice of Meeting of the Utah Resource Advisory Council (RAC)

SUMMARY: Utah's Resource Advisory Council will be conducting a 2-day 
meeting in Escalante, Utah. A field tour of the Escalante section of 
the Grand Staircase-Escalante National Monument will be taking place on 
March 18, 1997. This will be an all-day tour on which we will be 
discussing issues (i.e. recreation, grazing, archaeology, etc.), 
pertaining to the Monument. The Council will depart from Escalante for 
the Monument at 8:00 a.m. RAC meetings are open to the public; however, 
transportation, meals, and overnight accommodations are the 
responsibility of the participating public.
    Our general meeting of the RAC will take place on March 19, 1997, 
at the Bureau of Land Management's Escalante Field Office, 755 West 
Main, Escalante, Utah. At this time, we will be discussing membership 
and nomination procedures, charter renewal, and an overview of selected 
issues and opportunities. Opportunities for members of the public to 
address the Council will take place March 19, 1996, from 8:00 a.m.-8:30 
a.m.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Any member of the public interested in 
attending the Monument field tour or desiring an opportunity to address 
the Council should contact Sherry Foot, Special Programs Coordinator, 
Bureau of Land Management, 324 South State Street, Salt lake City, 
Utah, 84111; phone (801) 539-4195, by March 10, 1997.
