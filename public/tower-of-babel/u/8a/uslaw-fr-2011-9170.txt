 DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Drug Enforcement Administration

Cynthia M. Cadet, M.D.; Decision and Order

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On Thursday, April 7, 2011, the Drug Enforcement Administration 
published the above-titled Decision and Order, as well as the Decision 
of the Administrative Law Judge (76 FR 19450). In preparing the 
document for publication, the files were merged resulting in the 
footnotes of the Administrative Law Judge's Decision being numbered 
sequentially to follow the footnote numbers of the Decision and Order 
rather than beginning with the number 1 as they did in the ALJ's slip 
opinion.
    Therefore, this notice corrects footnotes 4 through 67 appearing in 
the Decision signed by the U.S. Administrative Law Judge to be 
footnotes 1 through 64 beginning at 76 FR 19451 under the second 
column.

    Dated: April 8, 2011.
Michele M. Leonhart,
Administrator.
