DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Bureau of Transportation Statistics


Advisory Council on Transportation Statistics

AGENCY: Bureau of Transportation Statistics, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) is canceling the 
meeting of its Advisory Council on Transportation Statistics (ACTS) 
scheduled for September 17, 1999, at 10:00 a.m. The meeting was 
announced in the Federal Register on September 10, 1999 (64 FR 49273). 
BTS will announce an alternative date as soon as possible.
Ashish Sen,
Director.
