Proposed Amendment to Class E Airspace; Medford, OR

Correction

    In proposed rule document 94-787 beginning on page 1682 in the 
issue of Wednesday, January 12, 1994, make the following corrections:


Sec. 71.1  [Corrected]

    1. On page 1683, in the second column, in Sec. 71.1, incorporation 
by reference material, in the fourth line from the bottom, ``ANM OR E5 
Medford, OR [Revised]'' should read ``ANM OR E4 Medford, OR 
[Revised]''.
    2. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 71.1, 
incorporation by reference material, the heading ``ANM OR E4 Medford, 
OR'' should read ``ANM OR E5 Medford, OR''.
