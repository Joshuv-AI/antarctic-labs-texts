DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Closed 
Meetings

amended (5 U.S.C. Appendix 2), notice is hereby given of the following 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Special Emphasis Panel (SEP) meetings:

Disease.
    Date: February 25, 1997.
    Time: 8:00 a.m.
    Place: Hyatt Regency Hotel, One Metro Center, Bethesda, Maryland 
20814.
    Contact Person: Ivan Baines, Ph.D., Two Rockledge Center, Room 
7184, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892-7924, (301) 435-0277.

Room 7220, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892-7924, (301) 435-
0266.
    Purpose/Agenda: To review and evaluate grant applications.
    These meetings will be closed in accordance with the provisions
