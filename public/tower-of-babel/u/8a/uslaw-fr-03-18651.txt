Defense Science Board

ACTION: Notice of advisory committee meeting date change.

SUMMARY: On Thursday, June 19, 2003 (68 FR 36772), the Department of 
Defense announced closed meetings of the Defense Science Board Task

meeting has moved to September 8, 2003. The Task Force will meet at 
SAIC, 4001 N. Fairfax Drive, Arlington, VA.
