DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Performance Review Board Members

AGENCY: Department of Veterans Affairs.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4) agencies are 
required to publish a notice in the Federal Register of the appointment 
of Performance Review Board (PRB) members. This notice revises the list 
of members of the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Performance 
Review Boards which was published in the Federal Register on October 
15, 1997 (62 FR 53686).

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 6, 1998.
