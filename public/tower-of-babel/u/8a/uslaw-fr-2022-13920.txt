LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: The Finance Committee of the Legal Services 
Corporation's Board of Directors will meet virtually on July 5, 2022. 
The meeting will commence at 3:00 p.m. EDT and will continue until the 
conclusion Committee's agenda.

PLACE: 
    Public Notice of Virtual Meeting.
    LSC will conduct the July 5, 2022 meeting via Zoom.

[[Page 38428]]

    Public Observation: Unless otherwise noted herein, the Finance 
Committee meeting will be open to public observation via Zoom. Members 
of the public who wish to participate remotely in the public 
proceedings may do so by following the directions provided below.
