COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled for 17 
December 1998 at 10:00 AM in the Commission's offices at the National 
Building Museum (Pension Building), Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F 
Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. 20001. The meeting will focus on 
reviewing designs for the $1 circulating coin.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200. 
Individuals requiring sign language interpretation for the hearing 
impaired should contact the Secretary at least 10 days before the 
meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, D.C., 30 November 1998.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
