DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Government-Owned Inventions; Available 
for Licensing

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention listed below is a CRADA Subject Invention, 
jointly-made under NCRADA-NAWCWDCL-03-111, and is assigned to Reynolds 
Systems, Inc. The United States Government, as represented by the 
Secretary of the Navy, has an undivided interest in this invention. 
U.S. Patent No. 7,661,362: Energetic material initiation device 
utilizing exploding foil initiated ignition system with secondary 
explosive material.
