COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' next meeting is scheduled for 16 
February 1995 at 10:00 a.m. in the Commission's offices in the Pension 
Building, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, NW, Washington, DC 
20001 to discuss various projects affecting the 
[[Page 7588]] appearance of Washington, DC including buildings, 
memorials parks, etc.; also matters of design referred by other 
agencies of the government.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, DC, 30 January 1995.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
