FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties can review 
or obtain copy of the agreement at the Washington, DC offices of the 
Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, NW., Room 940. Interested parties 
may submit comments on an agreement to the Secretary, Federal Maritime 
Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within 10 days of the date this 
notice appears in the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 201159.
    Title: Memorandum of Settlement of Local Conditions in the Port of 
New York and New Jersey.
    Parties: New York Shipping Association, Inc. and the International 
Longshoremen's Association.
    Filing Parties: Andre Mazzola; Gleason & Mathews, P.C.; 26 
Broadway, 17 Floor; New York, New York 10004; and William M. Spelman; 
Lambos &Junge; 29 Broadway, 9th Floor; New York, New York 10006.
    Synopsis: The agreement establishes local conditions for the Port 
of New York-New Jersey under the ILA Master Contract.

    Dated: July 20, 2004.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
