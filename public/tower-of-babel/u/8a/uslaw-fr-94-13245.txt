Proposed Withdrawal; Opportunity for Public Meeting; Colorado; 
Correction

    Summary: This action will correct the original notice to include a 
parcel of land that was incorrectly omitted.
    In 58 FR 51648, 51649, dated October 4, 1993, page 51648, third 
column, under McDonald Creek Cultural Resource Area, T. 10 S., R. 104 
W., sec. 32 is corrected to include SW\1/4\SE\1/4\NE\1/4\. The acreage 
total is corrected to 4,166.23 acres of public lands in Mesa County.
Robert S. Schmidt,
Chief, Branch of Realty Actions.
