DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

 

Justification for Acceptance of an Unsolicited Application

AGENCY: Department of Energy (DOE).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: DOE announces that pursuant to 10 CFR 600.14, it intends to 
award on a noncompetitive basis a Cooperative Agreement to the African 
Electrification Foundation. The purpose of this program is to develop a 
partnership with Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCU's) 
to collaborate in strengthening the electric power system 
infrastructure in Africa. This cooperative agreement will help ensure 
that minority businesses and educational institutions are afforded an 
opportunity to expand their participation in the commercial application 
of energy-related technologies and enhance their competitiveness. 
Eligibility for this award is restricted to the African Electrification 
Foundation.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rufus H. Smith, M-5, U S Department of 
Energy, Oak Ridge, Tennessee 37830, (615) 576-4988.

    Issued in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, on May 24, 1994.

Director, Procurement & Contracts Division, Oak Ridge Operations
