DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Wolf Creek Ditch Special Use Permit, Okanogan and Wenatchee 
National Forests, Okanogan County, WA

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On August 20, 1999, a Notice of Intent (NOI) to prepare an 
environmental impact statement for the Wolf Creek Ditch Special Use 
Permit, was published in the Federal Register (64 FR 45502). This 
project was originally planned as an EIS because of potential 
significant impacts to the Lake-Chelan Sawtooth Wilderness, an

[[Page 16716]]

inventoried Roadless area and fish listed under the Endangered Species 
Act. The proponent has changed the design of the proposed project such 
that an environmental impact statement is not anticipated. The 1999 NOI 
is hereby rescinded.
