FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should

[[Page 62772]]

not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of Freight 
Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, D.C. 20573.

J.E.S. Forwarding Inc., 130A Kinderkamack Road, Montvale, NJ 07645, 
Officer: John E. Staib, Jr., President
A A Shipping, 100 Market Street, Suite 116, Inglewood, CA 90301, Peter 
Mozie, Barbara Mozie, Partnership

    Dated: November 19, 1997.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
