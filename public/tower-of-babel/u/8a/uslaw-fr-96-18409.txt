NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
[Notice (96-076)]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Staged Vibration Corporation, of 
Norfolk, Virginia 23508, has applied for a partially exclusive license 
to practice the invention disclosed in NASA Case No. LAR-15348-1, 
entitled THIN-LAYER COMPOSITE-UNIMORPH PIEZOELECTRIC DRIVER AND SENSOR, 
``THUNDER,'' for which a U.S. Patent Application was filed on April 4, 
1995, by the United States of America as represented by the 
Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. 
Written objections to the proposective grant of a license should be 
sent to Mr. George F. Helfrich, Patent Counsel, Langley Research 
Center.

DATES: Responses to this notice must be received by September 17, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. George F. Helfrich, Patent Counsel, Langley Research Center, 
Hampton, VA 23681-0001; telephone (757) 864-9260; fax (757) 864-9190.

    Dated: July 10, 1996.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
