DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Clinical Center; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Board of 
Governors of the Warren Grant Magnuson Clinical Center, November 21, 
2003, 9 a.m. to November 21, 2003, 12 p.m., which was published in the 
Federal Register on October 20, 2003, FR 68, 202-59946.
    The meeting is being changed from open to partially closed. The 
meeting will be closed from 10:45 to 11:30 in accordance with the 
provisions set forth in section 522b(c)(6), Title 5 U.S.C., as amended 
for discussion of personal qualifications and performance, the 
disclosure of which would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of 
person privacy.

    Dated: November 13, 2003.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
