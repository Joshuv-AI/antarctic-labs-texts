GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Interagency Committee for Medical Records (ICMR); Printed 
Construction Cancellation of Medical Standard Form

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Because of low usage only the cut sheet version of the 
following Standard Form is cancelled: SF 519A, Medical Record--
Radiologic Consultation Request/Report (NSN 7540-00-634-4161).
    The 3-part set of the form (NSN 7540-00-634-4162) is still current 
and available from the Federal Supply Service.

DATES: Effective January 22, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Barbara Williams, General Services 
Administration, (202) 501-0581.

    Dated: January 5, 2001.
Barbara M. Williams,
Deputy Standard and Optional Forms Management Officer.
