Correction

    The Office of Community Services Program Announcement HHS-2005-ACF-
OCS-EN-0018 was published in the Federal Register on June 6, 2005.
    On page 32794 of this announcement, the due date for applications 
is July 21, 2005.
    On page 32800 of this announcement, the due date for applications 
is August 5, 2005.
    The correct due date for applications for this announcement is July 
21, 2005.
