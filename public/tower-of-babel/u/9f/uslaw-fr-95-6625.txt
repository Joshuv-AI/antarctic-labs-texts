INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION
[Investigation No. 337-TA-370]


Certain Salinomycin Biomass and Preparations Containing Same; 
Notice of Designation of Additional Commission Investigative Attorney

    Notice is hereby given that, as of this date, Teresa M.B. Martinez, 
Esq. and Juan S. Cockburn, Esq. of the Office of Unfair Import 
Investigations are designated as the Commission investigative attorneys 
in the above-cited investigation instead of Teresa M.B. Martinez, Esq.
    The Secretary is requested to publish this Notice in the Federal 
Register.

    Dated: March 10, 1995.
Lynn I. Levine,
Director, Office of Unfair Import Investigations.
