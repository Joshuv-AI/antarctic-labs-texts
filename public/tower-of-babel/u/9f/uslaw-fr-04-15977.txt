FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act; Schedule Change; FCC To Hold Open Commission 
Meeting, Thursday, July 8, 2004

    Please note that the time for the Federal Communications Commission 
Open Meeting is rescheduled from 9:30 a.m. to 10 a.m.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Thursday, July 8, 2004, which is scheduled 
to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, SW., 
Washington, DC.
