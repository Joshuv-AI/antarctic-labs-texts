DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. QF93-129-001]

 

Syracuse Power Co.

May 2, 1994.
    On April 6, 1994, the Commission issued a notice of petition to 
intervene and motion to revoke self-certification (notice), in the 
above-docketed proceeding. The notice was published in the Federal 
Register on April 12, 1994 (59 FR 17365), making the due date for 
comments May 12, 1994. By this notice, the date for the filing of 
comments or protests is shortened to and including May 6, 1994.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
