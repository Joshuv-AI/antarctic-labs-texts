FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices, Acquisition of Shares of Bank or 
Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. E8-18107 published on pages 
46005 and 46006 of the issue for Thursday, August 7, 2008.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for The Schifferdecker Limited Partnersip, Girard, Kansas, is revised 
to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City (Todd Offenbacker, Assistant 
Vice President) 1 Memorial Drive, Kansas City, Missouri 64198-0001:
    1. The Schifferdecker Limited Partnership, Girard, Kansas; Mark W. 
Schifferdecker, Girard, Kansas; Susan B. Friesen, Omaha, Nebraska; and 
Joy L. Shoop, Hiawatha, Kansas; as general partners, to acquire control 
of GN Bankshares, Inc., and thereby indirectly aacquire control of The 
Girard National Bank, both in Girard, Kansas.
    Comments on this application must be received by August 21, 2008.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, August 7, 
2008.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
