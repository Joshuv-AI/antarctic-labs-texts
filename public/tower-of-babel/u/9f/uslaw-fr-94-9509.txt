DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force

 

Community College of the Air Force; Meeting

    The Community College of the Air Force (CCAF) Board of Visitors 
will hold a meeting on Tuesday, 10 May 1994 at 8 a.m., in the CCAF 
Commander's Conference Room (Bldg 836), 130 West Maxwell Boulevard, 
Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama. the meeting will be open to the 
public.
    Purpose of the meeting is to review and discuss academic policies 
and issues relative to the operation of the CCAF. Agenda items include 
a CCAF mission briefing, faculty credentials, and reaffirmation of 
CCAF.
    For further information contact Captain Dallas Brooks, (205) 953-
2703, Community College of the Air Force, Maxwell Air Force Base, 
Montgomery, Alabama 36112-6653.
Patsy J. Conner,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
