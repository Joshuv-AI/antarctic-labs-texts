FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Freight Forwarder License

Revocations

    The Federal Maritime Commission hereby gives notice that the 
following freight forwarder licenses have been revoked pursuant to 
section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718) and the 
regulations of the Commission pertaining to the licensing of ocean 
freight forwarders, effective on the corresponding revocation dates 
shown below:

    License Number: 3678.
    Name: Total Transport, Inc.
    Address: 7749 East 11th Street, Tulsa, OK 74112.
    Date Revoked: May 12, 1997.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.

    License Number: 1957.
    Name: Universal Freight Forwarders, Ltd. d/b/a Universal Freight 
Forwarders and Customs Brokers, Ltd.
    Address: 83 South King Street, Suite 205, Seattle, WA 98104.
    Date Revoked: May 2, 1997.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Director, Bureau of Tariffs, Certification and Licensing.
