DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Board of 
Regents, May 12-13, 2020, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at the National 
Institutes of Health, Building 38, Lindberg Room, 8600 Rockville Pike, 
Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on 
February 4, 2020, 85 FR 23, Page 6210.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting location from 
the National Institutes of Health, Building 38, Lindberg Room, 8600 
Rockville Pike, Bethesda, MD 20892 to a virtual meeting. The URL link 
to this meeting is: https://videocast.nih.gov. Any member of the public 
may submit written comments no later than 15 days after the meeting.

    Dated: March 27, 2020.
Ronald J. Livingston, Jr.,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
