SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Minneapolis/St. Paul Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Minneapolis/St. Paul 
District Advisory Council will hold a public meeting on Friday, 
February 16, 

[[Page 2327]]
1996 at 11:30 am at the Sheraton Inn Midway, 400 North Hamline Avenue, 
Saint Paul, Minnesota to discuss matters as may be presented by 
members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Edward A. Daum, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 610-C Square, 100 North 
Sixth Street, Minneapolis, Minnesota 55403, (612) 370-2306.

    Dated: January 18, 1996.
Art DeCoursey,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
