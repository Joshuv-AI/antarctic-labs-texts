Dickten Masch Plastics, LLC, Hattiesburg Plant, a Subsidiary of 
Everett Smith Group, Hattiesburg, MS; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 13, 2006 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Dickten 
Masch Plastics, a subsidiary of Everett Smith Group, Hattiesburg Plant, 
Hattiesburg, Mississippi.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Dated: December 7, 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
