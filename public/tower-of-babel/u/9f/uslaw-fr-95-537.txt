DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
National Institutes of Health


National Center for Human Genome Research; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the time of open portion of 
the meeting January 30 and 31, 1995, of the National Advisory Council 
for Human Genome Research, National Center for Human Genome Research, 
which was published in the Federal Register on December 22, 1994, 59 FR 
66034.
    The open portion of the meeting was to have been from 8:30 to 11:30 
a.m. on Monday, January 30, 1995. The open portion will now be held at 
1:00 to 5:00 p.m.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 93.172, Human 
Genome Research.)

    Dated: January 4, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
