Weather Shield Manufacturing, Inc., Medford, WI; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 17, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of the workers at Weather Shield 
Manufacturing, Inc., Medford, Wisconsin.
    The worker petition submitted did not meet the criteria of three 
petitioners employed at the location for which the petition was 
submitted.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 25th day of November 2008.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
