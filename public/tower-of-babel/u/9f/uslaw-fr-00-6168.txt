Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement; Technical 
Amendments

AGENCY: Deaprtment of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Acting Director of Defense Procurement is making technical 
amendments to the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement to 
update activity names, addresses, and telephone numbers; delete 
obsolete text; update paragraph numbering and cross-references; and 
update certain commodity assignments under the DoD Coordinated 
Acquisition Program.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 16, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Michele Peterson, Defense 
Acquisition Regulations Council, PDUSD (AT&L) DP (DAR), IMD 3D139, 3062 
Defense Pentagon, Washington, DC 20301-3062. Telephone (703) 602-0311; 
telefax (703) 602-0350.
