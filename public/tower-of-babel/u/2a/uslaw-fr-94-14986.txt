DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Notice of Change of Meeting of the Department of Defense (DoD) 
Commission on Roles and Missions of the Armed Forces

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The meeting of the Commission which was announced on Thursday 
June 9, 1994, 59 FR 29784, has been rescheduled for Monday, July 11, 
1994. All other information remains the same.
    For further information contact CDR Gregg Hartung, Director for 
Public Affairs, (703) 696-4230/50.

    Dated: June 15, 1994.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
