DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Highway Administration


Environmental Impact Statement: Henderson and Buncombe Counties, 
North Carolina

AGENCY: Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of intent; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects three typographical errors in the 
FHWA's notice of intent, published on June 7, 2006, at 71 FR 33033. The 
notice of intent advises the public that an Environmental Impact 
Statement (EIS) will be prepared for a road widening project in 
Henderson and Buncombe Counties, North Carolina. There were two 
typographical errors in the SUMMARY section and the incorrect route 
number appeared in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION section. This notice 
provides the correct information.

Background

    On June 7, 2006, at 71 FR 33033, the FHWA issued a notice of intent 
regarding an EIS for a road widening project in North Carolina. The 
June 7, 2006 notice contained three typographical errors. The purpose 
of this notice is to correct these errors. First, in the SUMMARY 
section the word ``advice'' should be ``advise''. Second, in the 
SUMMARY section, the word ``multi-land'' should be ``multi-lane''. 
Finally, in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION section the route, ``NC 255'' 
should read ``NC 225''.
