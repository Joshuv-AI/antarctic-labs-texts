DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Notice of Availability of A Novel Quantum-Well for Exclusive, 
Partially Exclusive or Non-Exclusive Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive or non-exclusive licenses relative to 
a novel quantum-well technology as described in the U.S. Patent 
#5,579,331; ``Delta-strained quantum-well semiconductor lasers and 
optical amplifiers''; Shen, et al.; November 26, 1996. Licenses shall 
comply with 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR 404.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael D. Rausa, U.S. Army Research 
Laboratory, Office of Research and Technology Applications, ATTN: 
AMSRL-CS-TT/Bldg 459, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland 21005-5425, 
Telephone: (410) 278-5028.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.
