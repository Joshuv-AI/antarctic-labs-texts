Western Pacific Fishery Management Council; Public Meetings; 
Correction

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice advises the public that the Western Pacific 
Fishery Management Council (Council) will postpone a meeting of the 
Hawaii Archipelago Regional Ecosystem Advisory Committee in Honolulu, 
HI scheduled for Friday, May 16, 2008.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kitty M. Simonds, Executive Director; 
telephone: (808) 522-8220.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The original notice published in the Federal 
Register on April 24, 2008 (73 FR 22137).
    The Hawaii Archipelago Regional Ecosystem Advisory Committee 
meeting that was scheduled for Friday, May 16, 2008 has been postponed 
until further notice. The Council will publish a Federal Register 
notice when dates for this meeting are set.
    All other previously-published information remains unchanged.
