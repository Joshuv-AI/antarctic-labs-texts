Tennessee Gas and Pipeline; Notice of Proposed Settlement

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice of proposed settlement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under Section 122(h) of the Comprehensive Environmental 
Response, Compensation and Liability Act (CERCLA), the United States 
Protection Agency (EPA) has offered to potentially responsible parties 
an Administrative Order on Consent to settle claims for past and future 
removal actions at the Tennessee Gas and Pipeline Site along the Gulf 
Coast of Texas, Louisiana and Mississippi and extending along three 
routes to markets in the midwestern and northeastern United States. EPA 
will consider public comments on the proposed settlement for thirty 
(30) days. EPA may withdraw from or modify the Agreement should such 
comments disclose facts or considerations which indicate the proposed 
settlement is inappropriate, improper, or inadequate. Copies of the 
proposed settlement are available from: Ms. Paula V. Batchelor, Waste 
[[Page 21211]] Management Division, U.S. EPA, Region IV, 345 Courtland 
Street, NE., Atlanta, Georgia 30365, 404/347-5059 X6169.
    Written comments may be submitted to Ms. Batchelor within 30 days 
of the date of publication.

    Dated: April 24, 1995.
H. Kirk Lucius,
Chief, Waste Programs Branch, Waste Management Division.
