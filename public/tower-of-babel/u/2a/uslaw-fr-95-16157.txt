DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Agency Information Collection Under OMB Review

Title: ACF 106-State JOBS Plan and ACF 107--State Supportive Services 
Plan
OMB: No.: 0970-0108
Description: The information collected is required by the Social 
Security Act to collect program and financial data under the AFDC 
program. The State agency provides a quarterly estimate of the total 
amount and the Federal share of expenditures to be made in 
administering the AFDC program.

Respondents: State governments
