DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Aging Special Emphasis Panel, September 19, 2001, 7 p.m. 
September 20, 2001, 5 p.m. Hallmark Inn, 110 F Street, Davis, CA, 95616 
which was published in the Federal Register on September 19, 2001, 66 
FR 48266.

time. The meeting is closed to the public.
