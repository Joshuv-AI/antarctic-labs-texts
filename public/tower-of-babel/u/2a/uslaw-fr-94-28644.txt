DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
 

Correction to Program Announcement for Cooperative Agreements for 
the National AIDS Education and Training Centers Program for FY 1995

    The Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) announced 
in the Federal Register on October 27, 1994 at 59 FR 53996, that 
applications will be accepted for fiscal year 1995 Cooperative 
Agreements for the National AIDS Education and Training Centers (AETCs) 
Program, authorized under section 776(a), title VII of the Public 
Health Service (PHS) Act, as amended by the Health Professions 
Education Extension Amendments of 1992, Pub. L. 102-408. The following 
is a correction to that notice.

Deadline Date

    The deadline date for receipt of applications has been changed to 
December 21, 1994.

    Dated: November 15, 1994.
Ciro V. Sumaya,
Administrator.
