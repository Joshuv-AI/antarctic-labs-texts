Renfro Corporation, Fort, AL; Dismissal of Application for 
Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Renfro Corporation, Fort Payne, 
Alabama. The application contained no new substantial information which 
would bear importantly on the Department's determination. Therefore, 
dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-57,260; Renfro Corporation Fort Payne, Alabama (August 2, 2005).

    Signed in Washington, DC this 5th day of August, 2005.
Timothy Sullivan,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
