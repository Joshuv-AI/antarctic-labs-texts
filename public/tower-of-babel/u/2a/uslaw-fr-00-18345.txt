Radio Broadcasting Services; Rose Hill, Trenton, and Aurora, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies the petition for reconsideration and 
supplement to petition for reconsideration filed by Conner Media 
Corporation of the Report and Order, 61 FR 66618 (December 18, 1996) 
which allotted Channel 283A to Aurora, North Carolina, and denied a 
mutually exclusive proposal by Station WBSY(FM), Channel 284A, Rose 
Hill, NC to substitute Channel 284C2 for Channel 284A, to reallot the 
upgraded Channel to Trenton, NC, and to modify the license for Station 
WBSY (FM) accordingly. The document affirms the Report and Order's 
decision not to use an alternate channel to remove the conflict between 
the competing proposals because of a short spacing to a vacant 
allotment.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Arthur D. Scrutchins, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.
