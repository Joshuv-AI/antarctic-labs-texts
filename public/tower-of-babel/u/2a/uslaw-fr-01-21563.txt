Right To Appeal; Director, Great Lakes Pilotage

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Direct final rule; confirmation of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 13, 2001, we published a direct final rule (66 FR 
31842). The direct final rule notified the public of the Coast Guard's 
intent to amend its appellate procedures to provide explicit authority 
for appeal of decisions or actions taken by the Director, Great Lakes 
Pilotage. We have not received an adverse comment, or notice of intent 
to submit an adverse comment, on this rule. Therefore, this rule will 
go into effect as scheduled.

DATES: The effective date of the direct final rule is confirmed as 
September 11, 2001.
