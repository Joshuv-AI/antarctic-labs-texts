Price Formation in Energy and Ancillary; Services Markets 
Operated by Regional Transmission; Organizations and Independent System 
Operators; Notice of Filing

    Take notice that PJM Interconnection, L.L.C., Midcontinent 
Independent System Operator, Inc., ISO New England, Inc., New York 
Independent System Operator, Inc., Southwest Power Pool, Inc., and the 
California Independent System Operator Corporation (collectively, RTOs/
ISOs), each separately, filed reports in response to the Order 
Directing Reports issued by the Commission on November 20, 2015.\1\ In 
the Order Directing Reports, the Commission established that public 
comment in response to the RTOs/ISOs' reports may be submitted within 
30 days of the filing of the reports.\2\ Upon consideration, public 
comment in response to all of the RTOs/ISOs' reports may be submitted 
by 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on April 6, 2016.

    \1\ Price Formation in Energy and Ancillary Services Markets 
Operated by Regional Transmission Organizations and Independent 
System Operators, 153 FERC ] 61,221 (2015) (Order Directing 
Reports).
