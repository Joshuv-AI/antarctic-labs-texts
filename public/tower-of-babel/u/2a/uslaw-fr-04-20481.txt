GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION

[FMR Bulletin 2004-B3]


Federal Management Regulation; Redesignations of Federal 
Buildings

AGENCY: Public Buildings Service (P), GSA.

ACTION: Notice of a bulletin.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The attached bulletin announces the redesignations of 8 
Federal buildings.

DATES: Effective Date: This bulletin expires January 9, 2005. However, 
the building redesignations announced by this bulletin will remain in 
effect until canceled or superseded.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paul Chistolini, General Services 
Administration, Public Buildings Service (P), Washington, DC 20405; e-

[[Page 54791]]

mail, [email protected], telephone (202) 501-1100.

    Dated: August 30, 2004.
Stephen A. Perry,

the redesignations of 8 Federal buildings.
