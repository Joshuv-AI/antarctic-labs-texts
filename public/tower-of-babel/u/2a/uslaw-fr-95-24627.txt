SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Hartford District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Hartford District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Monday, December 11, 1995 at 8:30 
a.m. at 2 Science Park Haven, New Haven, Connecticut 06511, to 

[[Page 52025]]
discuss matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Jo-Ann Van Vechten, 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 330 Main Street, 
Hartford, Connecticut, (203) 240-4670.

    Dated: September 28, 1995.
Art DeCoursey,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
