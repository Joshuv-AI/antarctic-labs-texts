SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[License No. 09/09-0365]


VK Capital Company; Notice of Surrender of License

    Notice is hereby given that VK Capital Company, 600 California 
Street, Suite 1700, San Francisco, California 94108-2704, has 
surrendered its license to operate as a small business investment 
company under the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, as amended 
(the Act).
    VK Capital Company, L.P. was licensed by the Small Business 
Administration on February 7, 1986.
    Under the authority vested by the Act and pursuant to the 
regulations promulgated thereunder, the surrender was effective as of 
May 26, 1999, and accordingly, all rights, privileges, and franchises 
derived therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Companies)

    Dated: June 30, 1999.
Don A. Christensen,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
