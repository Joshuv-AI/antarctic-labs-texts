[Program Announcement 99115]


Cooperative Agreement for Strategies to Prevent Genital Herpes 
Infections: Building a National Prevention Program; Notice of 
Availability of Funds; Amendment

    A notice announcing the availability of fiscal year 1999 funds for 
the Strategies to Prevent Genital Herpes Infections: Building a 
National Prevention Program was published in the Federal Register on 
May 18, 1999, (Vol. 64 FR No. 95). The notice is amended as follows:
    On page 26983, second column, paragraph G., sub-paragraph titled 
``Application,'' replace second sentence:
    On or before July 26, 1999, submit application to: Sharron Orum, 
Grants Management Specialist, Grants Management Branch, Procurement and 
Grants Office, Announcement 99115, Centers for Disease Control and 
Prevention (CDC), 2920 Brandywine Rd., Room 3000, Atlanta, GA 30341.

    Dated: May 25, 1999.
John L. Williams,
Director, Procurement and Grants Office, Centers for Disease Control 
and Prevention (CDC).
