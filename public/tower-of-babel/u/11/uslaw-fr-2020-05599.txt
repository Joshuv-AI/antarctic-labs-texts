National Offshore Safety Advisory Committee; Meeting Cancellation

AGENCY: U.S. Coast Guard, Department of Homeland Security.

ACTION: Notice of federal advisory committee meeting; cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On February 6, 2020 (85 FR 25, Page 6962), the U.S. Coast 
Guard published a notice that announced a meeting of the National 
Offshore Safety Advisory Committee, which was scheduled to take place 
on March 24th and March 25th 2020. The Coast Guard is publishing this 
notice to announce that this federal advisory committee meeting has 
been cancelled and will be rescheduled at a later date. The rescheduled 
meetings will be announced in the Federal Register.
