National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Advisory 
Committee to the Director, National Cancer Institute, May 10, 2001, 12 
pm to May 10, 2001, 1 pm, National Cancer Institute, National 
Institutes of Health, 9000 Rockville Pike, Building 31, Room 11A03, 
Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on April 
19, 2001, 66 FR 20152.

open to the public.
