Approval and Promulgation of Implementation Plan; Ohio

AGENCY: United States Environmental Protection Agency (USEPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Environmental Protection Agency (USEPA) 
proposes to conditionally approve a revision to the Ohio State 
Implementation Plan (SIP) to meet the requirements of the USEPA 
transportation conformity rule. The Transportation conformity SIP 
revisions enable the State of Ohio to implement and enforce the Federal 
transportation conformity requirements at the State or local level.

DATES: Comments on this proposed action must be received by June 17, 
1996.

ADDRESSES: Written comments should be sent to: J. Elmer Bortzer, Chief, 
Regulation Development Section, Regulation Development Branch (AR-18J), 
USEPA, Region 5, 77 West Jackson Boulevard, Chicago, Illinois 60604-
3590.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Patricia Morris at (312) 353-8656.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: For additional information, see the Direct
