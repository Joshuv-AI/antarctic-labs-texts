Notice of Public Meeting: Bureau of Land Management Nevada 
Resource Advisory Councils; Postponement

    In the notice document published Monday, February 1, 2016 (81 FR 
5132), a public meeting of the Bureau of Land Management Nevada 
Resource Advisory Councils was announced.
    The BLM Nevada Resource Advisory Council meeting scheduled for 
February 10-11, 2016 has been postponed until the outstanding member 
appointments have been finalized. A new notice will be published when 
the dates have been decided.

Rudy Evenson,
Deputy Chief, Office of Communications.
