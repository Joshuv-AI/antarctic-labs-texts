Department of the Air Force

 

Performance Review Boards; List of Members

    Below is a list of additional individuals who are eligible to serve 
on the Performance Review Boards for the Department of the Air Force in 
accordance with the Air Force Senior Executive Appraisal and Award 
System.

Secretariat
    Mr. Frederic C. Schwartz

Air Force Materiel Command
    Maj Gen (select) Rondal H. Smith
    Mr. Harry E. Schulte

Air Staff and ``Others''
    Lt Gen Marcus Anderson
    Maj Gen Shepperd
    Mr. Arthur J. Myers
Patsy J. Conner,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
