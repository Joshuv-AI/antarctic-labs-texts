DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 19, 2007, 1 p.m. to June 
19, 2007, 1:30 p.m., The Fairmont Washington, DC, 2401 M Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on May 
21, 2007, 72 FR 28515-28517.
    The meeting was cancelled due to administration problems.

    Dated: May 22, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
