FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the 
agreements to the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, 
Federal Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 
20573. Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received 
within 12 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
and the Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited review. Copies of agreements are 
available through the Commission's website (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of Agreements at (202)-523-5793 or 
[email protected].

    Agreement No.: 201348-003.
    Agreement Name: APL/SWIRE Guam, Saipan--S. Korea, Japan Slot 
Charter Agreement.
    Parties: American Presidents Lines, LLC; Swire Shipping Pte. Ltd.
    Filing Party: Conte Cicala, Clyde & Co. US LLP.
    Synopsis: The Amendment modifies the amount of space being 
chartered under the Agreement.
    Proposed Effective Date: 4/10/2023.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC.Agreements.Web/Public/AgreementHistory/34502.
    Agreement No.: 201403.
    Agreement Name: SSPL/NPDL Slot Charter Agreement.
    Parties: Neptune Pacific Direct Line Pte. Ltd.; Swire Shipping Pte. 
Ltd.
    Filing Party: Matthew Thomas, Blank Rome.
    Synopsis: The Agreement would authorize Swire Shipping to charter 
space to Neptune Pacific Direct Line in the trade between the United 
States (including American Samoa) on the one hand, and Tahiti, Samoa, 
Tonga, Fiji, Australia and New Zealand on the other hand.
    Proposed Effective Date: 5/27/2023.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC.Agreements.Web/Public/AgreementHistory/79503.
