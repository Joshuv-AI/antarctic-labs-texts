DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Board of Scientific Advisors, March 21, 2017, 8:30 
a.m. to March 22, 2017, 12:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Building 31 Center Drive, Conference Room 6, Bethesda, MD 20892 which 
was published in the Federal Register on February 22, 2017, 82 FR 
11363.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the meeting date to March 
21, 2017. The location and time remain the same. The meeting is open to 
the public.

    Dated: February 27, 2017.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
