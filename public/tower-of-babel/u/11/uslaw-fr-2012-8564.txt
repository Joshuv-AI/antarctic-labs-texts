NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Proposal Review Panel for Materials Research; Notice of Meeting: 
Correction

SUMMARY: The National Science Foundation (NSF) published in the Federal 
Register on March 30, 2012, a notice of a partially open meeting for 
the Proposal Review Panel for Materials Research, 1203. This 
notice is to correct the ending time of the meeting on April 24, 2012 
being held at the Pennsylvania State University, College Station, PA.

Correction

    On page 19362, column 2, under Dates & Times, please replace 
``April 24, 2012; 5:30p.m.-8:30p.m.'' with ``April 24, 2012; 5:30p.m.-
10:30p.m.''
    On page 19362, column 3, line 3, please replace: ``7 p.m.-8:30p.m. 
Open-Poster Session'' with ``7 p.m.-10:30p.m. Open-Poster Session''.

    Dated: April 5, 2012.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
