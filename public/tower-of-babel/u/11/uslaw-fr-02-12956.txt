DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Office of the Secretary


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request; Correction

May 15, 2002.
    On Monday, May 5, 2002, the Department of Labor (DOL) published a 
notice in the Federal Register (67 FR 30400, May 6, 2002) announcing an 
opportunity to comment on an information collection request (ICR) that 
was submitted to the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for review 
and approval in accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 
(Pub. L. 104-13, 44 U.S.C. Chapter 35). The notice announced an 
opportunity to comment on the ICR for ESA's OFCCP Recordkeeping and 
Reporting Requirements--Supply and Service (OMB control number 1215-
0072).
    The corrections are as follows:
    On page 30400 in the table, the average hours per response for 
Standard Form 100, should read ``3.7'' instead of ``3.8''.
    On page 30400, column one, the Total Burden Hours should read 
``9,967,675'' instead of ``9,967.675''

Ira L. Mills,
Departmental Clearance Officer.
