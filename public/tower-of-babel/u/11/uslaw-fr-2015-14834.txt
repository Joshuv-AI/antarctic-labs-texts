SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Federal Register citation of previous announcement: [80 FR 32638, 
June 9, 2015].

STATUS: Closed Meeting.

PLACE: 100 F Street NE., Washington, D.C.

DATE AND TIME OF PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED MEETING:  June 11, 2015 at 2:00 
p.m.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Additional Item.
    The following matter will also be considered during the 2:00 p.m. 
Closed Meeting scheduled for Thursday, June 11, 2015: A matter related 
to pending litigation
    The General Counsel of the Commission, or her designee, has 
certified that, in her opinion, one or more of the exemptions as set 
forth in 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(3), (5), (7), (9)(B) and and (10) and 17 CFR 
200.402(a)(3), (5), (7), (9)(ii) and (10), permit consideration of the 
scheduled matter at the Closed Meeting.
    Commissioner Stein, as duty officer, voted to consider the items 
listed for the Closed Meeting in closed session, and determined that 
Commission business required consideration earlier than one week from 
today. No earlier notice of this Meeting was practicable.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact the Office of the Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: June 11, 2015.
Brent J. Fields,
Secretary.
