DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Health Resources and Services Administration


Advisory Council; Notice of Meeting; Correction

    In Federal Register Document 00-1032 appearing on page 2634 in the 
issue for Tuesday, January 18, 2000, the February 10-11, 2000, meeting 
dates of the ``National Advisory Council on Migrant Health'' are 
incorrect. The meeting will be held on February 11-12, 2000; 9:00 a.m.-
5:00 p.m.
    All other information is correct as it appears.

    Dated: January 28, 2000.

Director, Division of Policy Review and Coordination.
