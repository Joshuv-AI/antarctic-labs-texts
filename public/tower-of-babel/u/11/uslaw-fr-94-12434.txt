DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Security Federal Savings Association Panama City, FL; Replacement 
of Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Subdivision (C) of section 5 (d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator Security Federal Savings Association, Panama 
City, Florida (``Association''), with the Resolution Trust Corporation 
as sole Receiver for the Association on May 6, 1994.

    Dated: May 17, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
