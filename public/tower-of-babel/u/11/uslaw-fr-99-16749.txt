UNITED STATES INFORMATION AGENCY


Culturally Significant Objects Imported for Exhibition 
Determinations: ``The Treasury of St. Francis of Assisi''

AGENCY: United States Information Agency.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is an amendment to the Notice regarding Culturally 
Significant Objects Imported for Exhibition in the exhibit entitled 
``The Treasury of St. Francis of Assisi.'' This is to amend Federal 
Register Doc. 99-1839, FR Vol. 64, No. 17 (January 27, 1999) by 
inserting the following language after the words ``June 27, 1999'': 
``and at the Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco, CA from on or about 
July 24, 1999, to on or about November 14, 1999.'' is in the national 
interest.

    Dated: June 25, 1999.
Les Sin,
General Counsel.
