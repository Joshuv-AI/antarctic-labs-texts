ASSASSINATION RECORDS REVIEW BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., September 17, 1996.

PLACE: Board of Education, District Board Room H-160, 450 North Grand, 
Los Angeles, CA 90051.

STATUS: Public Hearing.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Testimony on Assassination Records.
    A list of witnesses will be available from the Review Board by 
September 12, 1996. Due to time constraints, the Board will not be able 
to accept unscheduled testimony. The record of this hearing will be 
kept open until October 11, 1996, for those who wish to submit written 
comments.
