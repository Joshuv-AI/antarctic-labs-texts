FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 93-211; RM-8285]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Arizona City, AZ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document substitutes Channel 293A for Channel 292A at 
Arizona City, Arizona, and modifies the license for Station KONZ(FM) 
accordingly to enable it to expand its signal coverage area, in 
response to a petition filed on behalf of Arizona City Broadcasting 
Corporation. See 58 FR 40399, July 28, 1993. Coordinates for Channel 
293A at Arizona City, Arizona, are 32-45-21 and 111-40-13. With this 
action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 25, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
