DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 6572]


Determination To Transfer Title of Selected Aircraft to the 
Government of Colombia

    Pursuant to section 484(a)(2) of the Foreign Assistance Act, as 
amended (``the Act'') and section 1-100(a)(1) of Executive Order No. 
12163, I hereby determine that section 484(a)(1) of the Act (which 
requires that the United States retain title to aircraft made available 
to foreign countries primarily for narcotics-related purposes) should 
not apply to: Six (6) Bell UH-1N helicopters; and one (1) T-65 Ayres 
spray airplane, because retention of title to these aircraft would be 
contrary to the national interest of the United States.
    This determination, together with the Memorandum of Justification 
and aircraft inventory, shall be reported to the Congress, and 
published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: February 26, 2009.
Hillary Rodham Clinton,
Secretary of State, Department of State.
