Airworthiness Directives; General Electric Company (GE) CF6-
80E1A2 and -80E1A4 Turbofan Engines; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; request for comments correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to Airworthiness Directive 
(AD) 2003-26-11 applicable to GE CF6-80E1A2 and -80E1A4 turbofan 
engines with left vertical link bolts part number (P/N) 1304M26P02 
installed, and pylon attachment bolts originally torqued to 450-500 lb 
ft. That AD was published in the Federal Register on January 6, 2004 
(69 FR 494). The SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION paragraph title, first 
sentence, and first three words of the second sentence of that 
paragraph were inadvertently omitted. This document corrects that 
omission. In all other respects, the original document remains the 
same.

EFFECTIVE DATE: Effective January 14, 2004.
