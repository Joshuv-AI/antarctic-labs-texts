ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Withdrawal

AGENCY: U.S. Election Assistance Commission (EAC).

ACTION: Sunshine Act Notice; Notice of Public Roundtable Agenda, 
Withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action withdraws the Sunshine Act Notice published in the 
Federal Register on July 11, 2022, U.S. Election Assistance Commission 
Roundtable Discussion: Disability and the Digital Divide in The Voting 
Process.

DATES: As of July 8, 2022, the Sunshine Act Notice published in the 
Federal Register on July 11, 2022 (87 FR 41117) is withdrawn.
