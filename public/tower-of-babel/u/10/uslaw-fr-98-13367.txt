DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Veterans' Advisory Committee on Environmental Hazards, Notice of 
Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Veterans' Advisory Committee on 
Environmental Hazards has been renewed for a 2-year period beginning 
April 27, 1998, through April 27, 2000.

    Dated: May 12, 1998.

    By direction of the Secretary.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
