Sunshine Act Meeting

    3. Fiscal Year 2000 Comprehensive Statement on Postal Operations.
    4. Report on Capital Metro Operations.
    5. Tentative Agenda for the April 2-3, 2001, meeting in Chicago, 
Illinois.
Tuesday, March 6--10 a.m. (Closed)
    1. Strategic Planning.
Wednesday, March 7--8:30 a.m. (Closed)
    1. Strategic Planning.
