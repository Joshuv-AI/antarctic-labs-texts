NASA STEM Engagement Committee; Meeting

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) announces that the 
planned meeting on November 15, 2024, of the STEM Engagement Committee 
is cancelled.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This meeting was announced in the Federal 
Register on October 23, 2024, at 89 FR 84634. The cancellation of this 
meeting is due to NASA administrative priorities. NASA will announce 
the new dates for this meeting in a future Federal Register notice.

Jamie M. Krauk,
Advisory Committee Management Officer, National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration.
