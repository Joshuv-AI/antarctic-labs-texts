agency: Office of the Secretary, DoD.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: This rule is the fiscal year 1994 revision of the section 
listing DoD contractors receiving contract awards of $10 million or 
more. This part is published to comply with the provisions of section 
1, Public Law 97-295, October 12, 1982; 10 U.S.C. 2397.

effective date: September 30, 1994.

for further information contact: Mr. R.S. Drake, Director, Directorate 
for Information Operations and Reports, Washington Headquarters 
Services, 1215 Jefferson Davis Highway, Suite 1204, Arlington, VA 
22202-4302. Telephone (703) 604-4569.

    Armed forces, Conflict of interests, Government employees, 
Government procurement, Reporting and recordkeeping requirements.
