DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

[T.D. 94-53]

 

License Cancellations

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: General notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to 19 CFR 111.51(a), the 
following Customs broker licenses have been cancelled due to the death 
of the broker. These licenses were issued in various Customs Districts.

Harlan Nelson Naag--license No. 2904
Antonio Ferraioli--license No. 2194
William A. Phelps--license No. 3626
Jose R. Hernandez--license No. 10381
Bufford Struck--license No. 3232
Richard M. Van Sant--license No. 2671
Howard J. Mann--license No. 2253.

    Dated: June 16, 1994.
Philip Metzger,
Director, Office of Trade Operations.
