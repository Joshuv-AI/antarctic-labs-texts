Cancellation of Customs Broker Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Brokers' Licenses Cancellations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    I, as Commissioner of Customs, pursuant to section 641(f) Tariff 
Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1641(f)) and section 111.51(a) of 
the Customs Regulations (19 111.51(a)), hereby cancel the following 
Customs brokers' licenses due to the deaths of the brokers.
