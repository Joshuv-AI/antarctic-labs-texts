DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
 

University of Florida, Notice of Withdrawal of Application for 
Duty-Free Entry of Scientific Instrument

    The University of Florida has withdrawn Docket Number 94-072 an 
application for duty-free entry of a Laser Ablation System. This 
withdrawal constitutes final disposition for Docket Number 94-072 in 
accordance with Sec. 301.7 of 15 CFR part 301.
Pamela Woods,
Acting Director, Statutory Import Programs Staff.
