Food Additives Permitted in Feed and Drinking Water of Animals; 
Selenium Yeast; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is correcting a final 
rule that published in the Federal Register of June 6, 2000 (65 FR 
35823). The document amended FDA's food additive regulations to reflect 
approval of a petition (FAP 2238) filed by Alltech Biotechnology 
Center. The petition proposed to amend the food additive regulations to 
provide for the safe use of selenium yeast as a source of selenium in 
animal feeds intended for use in chickens. The document was published 
with an inadvertent error. This document corrects that error.
    This rule does not meet the definition of ``rule'' in 5 U.S.C. 
804(3)(A) because it is a rule of ``particular applicability.'' 
Therefore, it is not subject to the congressional review requirements 
in 5 U.S.C. 801-808.

DATES: Effective June 6, 2000.
