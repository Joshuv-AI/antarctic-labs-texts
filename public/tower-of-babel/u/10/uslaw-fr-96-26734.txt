DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP96-317-000]


Great Lakes Gas Transmission Limited Partnership; Notice of 
Technical Conference

October 11, 1996.
    Pursuant to the Commission's order, issued on September 12, 1996, a 
technical conference will be held to resolve the issues raised in the 
above-captioned proceeding.
    The conference will be held on Wednesday, October 23, 1996 at 10:00 
a.m. in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
