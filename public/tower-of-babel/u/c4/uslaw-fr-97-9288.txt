Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Shell Gas Pipeline Company; Notice of Proposed Changes in FERC 
Gas Tariff

April 4, 1997.
    Take notice that on April 1, 1997, Shell Gas Pipeline Company 
(SGPC) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Original 
Volume No. 1, the sheets set forth on Appendix B to the filing in 
compliance with the Order in Docket Nos. RP97-138-000 and RP97-138-001 
issued March 6, 1997 to become effective June 1, 1997.
    SGPC states that the amended tariff sheets set forth revisions to 
SGPC's December 2, 1996 and January 17, 1997 tariff filings, made to 
comply with Order No. 587. The amended tariff sheets reflect changes 
for: (1) Certain standards that have been either incorporated verbatim 
or by reference; (2) revised intra-day nominations that allow for later 
effective times; and (3) include an end date to the shipper nomination 
form.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington D.C. 20426, in accordance with 18 CFR 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed by 
April 21, 1997. Protests will be considered by the Commission in 
determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to 
make Protestants parties to the proceeding. Copies of this filing are 
on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection in 
the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
