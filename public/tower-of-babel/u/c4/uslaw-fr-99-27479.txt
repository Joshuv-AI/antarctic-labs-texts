SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Oregon Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Oregon Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Portland, Oregon will hold a public 
meeting from 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Tuesday, October 26, 1999 at 6th 
Floor Conference Room, 1515 Bldg., 1515 SW Fifth Avenue, Portland, 
Oregon to discuss such matters as may be presented by members, SBA 
staff or others present.
    For further information, write or call Donald S. Matsuda, Deputy 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 1515 SW Fifth 
Avenue, Suite 1050, Portland, OR 97201-5494, telephone number (503) 
326-5221.
Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administrator.
