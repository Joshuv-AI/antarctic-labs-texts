DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, June 30, 2017, 8:00 a.m. to 
June 30, 2017, 6:00 p.m., Bethesda North Marriott Hotel & Conference 
Center, 5701 Marinelli Road, Bethesda, MD, 20852 which was published in 
the Federal Register on May 5, 2017, 82 FR 21256.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the meeting start time to 
7:00 a.m. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 21, 2017.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
