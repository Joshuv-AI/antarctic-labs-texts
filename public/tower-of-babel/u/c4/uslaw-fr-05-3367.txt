DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4995]


Department of State Performance Review Board Members (for Non-
Career Senior Executive Employees)

    In accordance with section 4314(c)(4) of the Civil Service Reform 
Act of 1978 (Public Law 95-454), the Executive Resources Board of the 
Department of State has appointed the following individuals to the 
Department of State Performance Review Board (for Non-Career Senior 
Executive Employees): Kara G. LiCalsi, Senior Advisor to the Secretary 
and White House Liaison, Department of State; Christopher B. Burnham, 
Assistant Secretary for Resource Management and Chief Financial 
Officer, Department of State; Brian F. Gunderson, Chief of Staff, 
Office of the Secretary, Department of State.

W. Robert Pearson,
Director General of the Foreign Service and Director of Human 
Resources, Department of State.
