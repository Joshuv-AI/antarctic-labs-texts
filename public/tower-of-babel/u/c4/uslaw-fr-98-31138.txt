DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP99-57-001]


U-T Offshore System; Notice of Compliance Filing

November 17, 1998.
    Take notice that on November 13, 1998, U-T Offshore System (U-TOS) 
tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Third Revised 
Volume No. 1, the following tariff sheets to become effective November 
2, 1998:

Second Revised Sheet No. 46A
Original Sheet No. 46B
Original Sheet No. 46C
Sub Ninth Revised Sheet No. 73
Sub Fourth Revised Sheet No. 73A
Sub Third Revised Sheet No. 73B

    U-TOS asserts that the purpose of this filing is to comply with the 
Commission's July 15, 1998, letter order in the captioned proceeding 
regarding Order No. 587-G. Pipelines must comply with the adoption of 
Version 1.2 of the GISB standards (284.10(b)) and the standards 
regarding the posting of information on websites and retention of 
electronic information (284.10(c)(3)(ii) through (v)).
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed as 
provided in Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceedings. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission 
and are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
