Title 49 CFR Parts 1000 to 1199; Republication

CFR Correction

    Title 49 CFR parts 1000 to 1199, revised as of October 1, 1998, is 
being republished. The earlier issuance contained incorrect text on 
page 265. As corrected, page 265 should include the last two lines of 
Sec. 1180.1(f) and paragraphs (g) and (h). Also omitted was Sec. 1180.2 
introductory text and paragraphs (a) through (d)(2).
