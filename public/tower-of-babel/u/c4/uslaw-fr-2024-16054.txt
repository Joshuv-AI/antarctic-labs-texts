National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, Catalyze 
Product Definition, August 15, 2024, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., National 
Institutes of Health, Rockledge I, 6705 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD

p.m. The meeting is closed to the public.
