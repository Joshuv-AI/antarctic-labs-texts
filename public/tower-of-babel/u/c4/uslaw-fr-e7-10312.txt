The Troxel Company; West Point, MS; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 7, 2007 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at The Troxel Company, 
West Point, Mississippi.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of May 2007.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
