DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On February 3, 1999, a notice was published in the Federal 
Register, Vol. 64, No. 22, Page 5310, that an application had been 
filed with the Fish and Wildlife Service by Michael Carpinito for a 
permit (PRT-007280) to import a polar bear (Ursus maritimus) sport-
hunted trophy taken from the Southern Beaufort Sea polar bear 
population, Canada, for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on March 24,1999, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
