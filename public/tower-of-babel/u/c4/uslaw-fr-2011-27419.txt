JUDICIAL CONFERENCE OF THE UNITED STATES


Hearing of the Judicial Conference Committee on Civil Rules

AGENCY: Judicial Conference of the United States, Advisory Committee on 
Civil Rules.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of open hearing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The following public hearing on proposed amendments to the 
Federal Rules of Civil Procedure has been canceled: Civil Rules 
Hearing, November 7, 2011, in Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Benjamin J. Robinson, Deputy Rules 
Officer and Counsel, Rules Committee Support Office, Administrative 
Office of the United States Courts, Washington, DC 20544, telephone 
(202) 502-1820.

    Dated: October 18, 2011.
Benjamin J. Robinson,
Deputy Rules Officer and Counsel.
