EXPORT-IMPORT BANK


Economic Impact Policy

    This notice is to inform the public that the Export-Import Bank of 
the United States has received an application for a $115 million direct 
loan to support the export of approximately $100 million worth of 
vehicle assembly equipment to India. The U.S. exports will enable the 
Indian company to produce approximately 330,000 vehicles per year. 
Available information indicates that the majority of this new vehicle 
production will be sold in India with the remainder sold in Mexico, the 
Middle East, Africa, and ASEAN regions. Interested parties may submit 
comments on this transaction by email to [email protected] or by 
mail to 811 Vermont Avenue NW., Room 442, Washington, DC 20571, within 
14 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
inclusive of the date of this notification.

Angela Mariana Freyre,
Senior Vice President and General Counsel.
