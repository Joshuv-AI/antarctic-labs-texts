DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Notice of Intent To Rule on Request for Land Use Change From 
Aeronautical to Non-Aeronautical for 7.6 Acres of Land at Lebanon 
Municipal Airport, Lebanon, NH

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Request for public comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is being given that the FAA is considering a request 
from the City of Lebanon, NH to change the land use from Aeronautical 
to Non-Aeronautical use for 7.6 acres of land at Lebanon Municipal 
Airport, Lebanon, NH. The land is not required for aeronautical use and 
can be developed as an extension of the existing business park. The 
expansion would create a new long term revenue source for the airport 
and the proceeds will be deposited in the airport's operations and 
maintenance account.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before July 26, 2021.
