Southwest Power Pool, Inc.; Notice Allowing Post-Technical 
Conference Comments

April 11, 2006.
    A technical conference was convened on Monday, April 10, 2006, from 
1 p.m. to 4 p.m. at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426. The technical 
conference addressed the

[[Page 19887]]

two issues related to Southwest Power Pool, Inc.'s proposed credit 
policy, as discussed in the Commission's February 28, 2006 Order.\1\ 
Specifically, discussion at the technical conference explored SPP's 
proposed total debt to total capitalization and debt service coverage 
scores and SPP's proposed $50,000 unsecured credit floor for not-for-
profit entities. Prior to the technical conference, a notice was issued 
on March 22, 2006, that set forth several questions. The information 
submitted in response to the questions was discussed at the technical 
conference.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

8, 2006.
