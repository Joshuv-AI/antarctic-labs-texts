National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, October 15, 2003, 6:00 PM to 
October 17, 2003, 12:00 PM, The Belvedere Hotel, 319 West 48th Street, 
New York, NY, 10036 which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 26, 2003, 68 FR 51282.
    This meeting is amended due to the change of the meeting location 
to W Hotels of New York, 130 East 39th Street, New York NY 10016. The 
meeting is closed to the public.
