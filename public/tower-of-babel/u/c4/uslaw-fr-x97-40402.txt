Correction

    In final rule document 97-6686 beginning on page 12915 in the issue 
of March 19, 1997 make the following corrections:


Sec. 312.1  [Corrected]

    (1) On page 12923, in Sec. 312.1(b)(3), in the second column, in 
the first paragraph:
    (a) In the sixteenth line ``form'' should read ``from''.
    (b) In the seventh line from the bottom of the paragraph 
``requirements of English'' should read ``requirements for English''.
    (c) In the fourth line from the bottom of the paragraph ``even with 
reasonable modifications to the methods of determining English 
proficiency'' was inadvertently repeated.


Sec. 312.2  [Corrected]

    (3) On the same page, in Sec. 312.2(b)(1), in the second column: 
    (a) In the seventh line ``Untied stated'' should read ``United 
States''.
    (b) In the fourth line from the bottom of the paragraph ``results 
form'' should read ``results from''.
    (2) On the same page, in Sec. 312.2(b)(2), in the third column:
    (a) In the twelth line ``Untied States'' should read ``United 
States''.
    (b) In the seventeenth line ``Neutralization'' should read 
``Naturalization''.
    (c) In the penultimate line ``purpose'' should read ``purposes''.
