DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming Fall Board Meeting of the Scientific Advisory Board. The 
purpose of the meeting is to allow the SAB leadership to meet with the 
Chief and Secretary of the Air Force, the Military Director of the SAB, 
and key members of the scientific and technical communities. Because 
classified and contractor-proprietary information will be discussed, 
this meeting will be closed to the public.

DATES: October 9-10 2002.

ADDRESSES: 1560 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22209.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Colonel Charles Bowker, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
