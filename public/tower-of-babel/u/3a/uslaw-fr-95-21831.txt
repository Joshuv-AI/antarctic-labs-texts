DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Closed

7216, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, Maryland 20892-7924, (301) 
435-0310.

    Date: October 19-20, 1995.

    Place: Holiday Inn, Chevy Chase, Maryland.
    Contact Person: Louis M. Ouellette, Ph.D., Rockledge II, Room 
7216, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, Maryland 20892-7924, (301) 
435-0310.
    Purpose/Agenda: To review and evaluate grant applications.

    These meetings will be closed in accordance with the provisions 
set forth in secs. 552b(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C. 
Applications and/or proposals and the discussions could reveal
