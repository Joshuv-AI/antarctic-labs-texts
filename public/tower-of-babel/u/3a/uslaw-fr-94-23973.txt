DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Division of Research Grants; Notice of Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the meeting of the 
Biomedical Sciences Study Section, Division of Research Grants, 
November 14-16, 1994, which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 22, 1994, 59 FR 43129.
    Due to scheduling and workload problems this meeting has been 
canceled.

    Dated: September 21, 1994.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
