SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Notice of Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Public Law 94-409, that the Securities 
and Exchange Commission will hold an Open Meeting on Wednesday, 
February 13, 2013 at 10:00 a.m., in the Auditorium, Room L-002.
    The subject matter of the Open Meeting will be:
    The Commission will consider whether to approve the 2013 budget of 
the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board and will consider the 
related annual accounting support fee for the Board under Section 109 
of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items.
    For further information and to ascertain what, if any, matters have 
been added, deleted or postponed, please contact: The Office of the 
Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: February 6, 2013.
Elizabeth M. Murphy,
Secretary.
