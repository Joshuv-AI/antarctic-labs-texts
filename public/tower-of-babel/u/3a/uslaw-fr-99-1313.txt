DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans, Notice of Charter 
Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Public 
Law 92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Advisory Committee on Minority 
Veterans has been renewed for a period beginning January 8, 1999, 
through December 31, 1999.

    Dated: January 11, 1999.

    By direction of the Secretary.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
