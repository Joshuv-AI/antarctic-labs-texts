CFR Correction

    In Title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 400 to 699, 
revised as of January 1, 1995, on page 116, in Sec. 401.120, item 9 was 
inadvertently omitted. The correct text, which should precede item 10, 
follows:


Sec. 401.120  Rice endorsement.

* * * * *

9. Contract Changes

    The date by which contract charges will be available in your 
service office is December 31 preceding the cancellation date for 
counties with an April 15 cancellation date and November 30 
preceding the cancellation date for all other counties.
