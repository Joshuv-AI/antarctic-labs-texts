FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-2755, MM Docket No. 01-55, RM-10034]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Fayetteville, AR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Arkansas Educational 
Television Commission, substitutes DTV channel *9 for DTV channel *45 
at Fayetteville. See 66 FR 12751, February 28, 2001. DTV channel *9 can 
be allotted to Fayetteville, Arkansas, in compliance with the principal 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates 35-48-53 N. and 94-01-41 W. with a power of 19, HAAT of 509 
meters and with a DTV service population of 675,000. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 20, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
