Radio Broadcasting Services; Charlotte and Grand Ledge, MI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 68 FR 62554 
(November 5, 2003), this Report and Order substitutes Channel 225A for 
Channel 224A, at FM Station WQTX, Charlotte, Michigan, reallots Channel 
225A to Grand Ledge, Michigan, and modifies Station WQTX's license 
accordingly. The coordinates for Channel 225A at Grand Ledge Michigan 
are 42-42-17 NL and 84-37-20 WL, with a site restriction of 11.5 
kilometers (7.2 miles) southeast of Grande Ledge.

DATES: Effective October 3, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
