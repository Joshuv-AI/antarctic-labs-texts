LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting of the Board of Directors Operations & 
Regulations Committee

Time and Date: The Operations and Regulations Committee of the Legal 
Services Corporation Board of Directors will meet on November 16, 2001. 
The meeting will begin at 2:00 p.m. and continue until the Committee 
concludes its agenda.

LOCATION: Marriott at Metro Center, 775 12th Street, NW., Washington, 
DC.

Status of Meeting: Open.

Matters to be Considered:
    1. Approval of agenda.
    2. Approval of the minutes of the Committee's meeting of September 
7, 2001.
    3. Consider and act on the Draft Notice of Proposed Rulemaking 
(``DNPRM'') on 45 CFR Part 1639 (Welfare Reform). A copy of the DNPRM 
is available on the LSC Web site: www.lsc.gov.
    4. Staff report on public comments received on the Final Report of 
the Regulations Review Task Force.
    5. Staff report on the status of Current Negotiated Rulemakings: 45 
CFR Part 1626 (Restrictions on Legal Assistance to Aliens); and 45 CFR 
Part 1611 (Eligibility).
    6. Consider and act on other business.
    7. Public comment.

CONTACT PERSON FOR INFORMATION: Victor M. Fortuno, Vice President for 
Legal Affairs, General Counsel & Corporate Secretary, at (202) 336-
8800.

Special Needs: Upon request, meeting notices will be made available in 
alternate formats to accommodate visual and hearing impairments. 
Individuals who have a disability and need an accommodation to attend 
the meeting may notify Elizabeth S. Cushing, at (202) 336-8800.

    Dated: November 8, 2001.
Victor M. Fortuno,
Vice President for Legal Affairs, General Counsel & Corporate 
Secretary.
