Bernhardt Furniture Company Corporate Office, Lenoir, NC; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 9, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers of Bernhardt Furniture Company, Corporate 
Office, Lenoir, North Carolina.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 24th day of March 2009
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
