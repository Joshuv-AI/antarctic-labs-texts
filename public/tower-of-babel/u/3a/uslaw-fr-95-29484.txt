[MM Docket No. 91-253; RM-6882]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Tioga, PA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Europa Communications, Inc., 
substitutes Channel 234B1 for Channel 234A at Tioga, Pennsylvania, and 
modifies the license of Station WPHD to specify the higher class 
channel. See 56 FR 42967, September 6, 1991. Channel 234B1 can be 
allotted to Tioga in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements with a site restriction of 10.3 kilometers (6.4 
miles) southwest, at coordinates 41-51-00 NL; 77-13-49 WL, to avoid 
short-spacings to Stations WLVY, Channel 232A, Elmira, NY, WYYY, 
Channel 233B, Syracuse, NY, and WIYN, Channel 234A, Deposit, NY. 
Canadian concurrence in the allotment has been received since Tioga is 
located within 320 kilometers (200 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border. 
With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 2, 1996.
