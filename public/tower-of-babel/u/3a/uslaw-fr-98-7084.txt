DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Meeting

March 13, 1998.
    On March 26, 1998, at 10:00 a.m. in Room 10A-07, Commission staff 
will meet with representatives of the North American Reliability 
Council (NERC), to discuss matters related to NERC's draft Transmission 
Line Loading Relief procedures.
    Any person interested in attending may contact Donald LeKang at 
202-208-1156 for further information.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
