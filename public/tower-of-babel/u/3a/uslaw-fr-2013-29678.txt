FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Notice of a Matter To Be Added to the 
Agenda for Consideration at an Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that the following matter 
will be added to the ``Discussion Agenda'' for consideration at the 
open meeting of the Board of Directors of the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation scheduled to be held at 10:00 a.m. on

[[Page 75345]]

Tuesday, December 10, 2013, in the Board Room on the sixth floor of the 
FDIC Building located at 550--17th Street NW., Washington, DC:

    Memorandum and resolution re: The Resolution of Systemically 
Important Financial Institutions:
    The Single Point of Entry Strategy.

    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Robert E. Feldman, Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at 202-898-7043.

    Dated: December 9, 2013.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
