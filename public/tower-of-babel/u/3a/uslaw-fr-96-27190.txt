ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 5-0 on October 16, the Commission

public.

to U.S.C. 552b(e) and 10 CFR Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules 
that ``Affirmation of Yankee Atomic Electric Company (Yankee Nuclear 
Power Station), Docket No. 50-029-DCOM, Memorandum and Order (Granting 
Motion for Summary Disposition), LBP-96-18'' be held on October 18, and 
on less than one week's notice to the public.
