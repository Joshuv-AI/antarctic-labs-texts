Riverside Gas Storage Co. and Texas Eastern Transmission Corp.; 
Notice of Site Visit

September 22, 1994.
    On September 28, 1994, the Office of Pipeline Regulation staff will 
conduct a site visit with representatives of Riverside Gas Storage 
Company for the facilities proposed in the Riverside Gas Storage Field 
Project. The proposed facilities are near the towns of Carmichaels and 
Nemacolin in Greene County, Pennsylvania.
    On September 29 and 30, 1994, the Office of Pipeline Regulation 
staff will conduct a site visit with representatives of Texas Eastern 
Transmission Corporation for the facilities proposed in the Riverside 
Transportation Project. The proposed facilities are loops or 
replacement sections of Texas Eastern's mainline in Greene, Fayette, 
Bedford, and Bucks County, Pennsylvania. The facilities also include a 
new meter and regulator station in Bucks County, Pennsylvania.
    Parties to the proceeding may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation. For further information, call Steven 
G. Grape, (202) 208-1046.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
