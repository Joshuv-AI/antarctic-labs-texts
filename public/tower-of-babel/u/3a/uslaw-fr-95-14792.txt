FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Clearinghouse Advisory Panel: Renewal of Charter

SUMMARY: The National Clearinghouse on Election Administration 
announces the renewal of the charter for the Clearinghouse Advisory 
Panel.
    The purpose of the Panel is to provide advice and consultation to 
the Clearinghouse with respect to its research programs on election 
administration.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Janet McKee, National Clearinghouse on 
Election Administration, Washington, DC 20463, 202/219-3670.

    Dated: June 13, 1995.
Penelope Bonsall,
Director, National Clearinghouse on Election Administration.
