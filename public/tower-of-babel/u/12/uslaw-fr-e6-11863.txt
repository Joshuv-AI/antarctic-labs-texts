Johnson Controls, Inc.; Interiors Division; Holland, MI; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 7, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Johnson 
Controls, Inc., Interiors Division, Holland, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn at this 
time. Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 29th day of June 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
