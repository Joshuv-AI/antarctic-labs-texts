SUMMARY: This document corrects a notice of final rulemaking concerning 
surety bond provisions of Minerals Management Service (MMS). MMS 
published the final rule in the Federal Register of May 22, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: John V. Mirabella, Engineering and 
Operating Division, at (703) 787-1607.

Correction

    This document corrects the final rule published on May 22, 1997 (62 
FR 27948). On page 27956 in the sixth line of the amendatory language 
number 11, ``paragraph (e), (f), and (g)'' should read ``paragraphs (e) 
and (f).''
