Radio Broadcasting Services; Nekoosa, Wisconsin

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Action in this document allots Channel 288A to Nekoosa, 
Wisconsin, in response to a petition filed by Lyle Robert Evans d/b/a 
The Radio Company. See 61 FR 18541, April 26, 1996. The coordinates for 
Channel 288A are 44-18-33 and 90-03-10. There is a site restriction 
11.9 kilometers (7.4 miles) west of the community. With this action 
this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective February 10, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 288A at Nekoosa, Wisconsin, will open on 
February 10, 1997, and close on March 13, 1997.
