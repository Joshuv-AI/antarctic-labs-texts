NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Aviation Maintenance Repair Facilities: A Public Forum

    A public forum on aviation maintenance repair facilities sponsored 
by the National Transportation Safety Board will be held August 30, 
1999, at the Sheraton Chicago Hotel and Towers, 301 East North Water 
Street, Chicago, Illinois. For more information, contact the Office of 
Research and Engineering Division, telephone (202) 314-6510, fax (202) 
314-6597 or Paul Schlamm, Office of Public Affairs, Washington, DC 
20594, telephone (202) 314-6100.

    Dated: August 18, 1999.
Rhonda N. Underwood,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
