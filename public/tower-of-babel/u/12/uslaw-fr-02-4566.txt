SCG Pipeline, Inc.; Notice of Route and Site Review

February 20, 2002.
    On March 14 and 15, 2002, the staff of the Office of Energy 
Projects (OEP) will conduct a site review of the proposed SCG Pipeline 
Project. The SCG Pipeline Project facilities are proposed for 
construction by SCG Pipeline, Inc (SCG). The proposed pipeline route 
and route alternatives, crossing portions of Chatham and Effingham 
Counties, Georgia and Jasper County, South Carolina, will be inspected 
by automobile.
    Anyone interested in attending the route and site review or 
obtaining further information may contact the Commission's Office of 
External Affairs at (202) 208-1088. Attendees must provide their own 
transportation.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
