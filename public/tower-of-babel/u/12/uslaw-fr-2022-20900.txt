COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Puerto Rico Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Puerto Rico Advisory 
Committee. The meeting scheduled for Wednesday, September 28, 2022, at 
1:00 p.m. (ET) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of 
Wednesday, September 7, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-19268, in the third 
column of page 54671 and the first column of page 54672.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria Moreno, 434-515-0204, 
[email protected].
