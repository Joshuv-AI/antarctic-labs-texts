Digital Television Broadcast Service; Green Bay, WI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of CBS Broadcasting Inc., 
substitutes DTV channel 39c for DTV channel 56 at Green Bay, Wisconsin. 
See 66 FR 66867, December 27, 2001. DTV channel 39c can be allotted to 
Green Bay, Wisconsin, in compliance with the principle community 
coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 44-
20-01 N. and 87-58-56 W. with a power of 1000, HAAT of 364 meters and 
with a DTV service population of 997 thousand. Since the community of 
Green Bay is located within 400 kilometers of the U.S.-Canadian border, 
concurrence from the Canadian government was obtained for this 
allotment. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 6, 2004.
