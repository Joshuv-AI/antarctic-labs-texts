INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION

 

Notice of Intent To Engage in Compensated Intercorporate Hauling 
Operations

    This is to provide notice as required by 49 U.S.C. 10524(b)(1) that 
the named corporations intend to provide or use compensated 
intercorporate hauling operations as authorized in 49 U.S.C. 10524(b).
    1. The parent corporation is Pace American, Inc., 11550 Harter 
Drive, Middlebury, IN 46540.
    2. The wholly owned subsidiaries of Pace American, Inc. and state 
of corporation are as follows:

Pace American of Georgia, Inc.  (in Corporation)
Pace American of Utah, Inc.  (in Corporation)
Pace American Transport, Inc.  (in Corporation)
Vernon A. Williams,
Secretary.
