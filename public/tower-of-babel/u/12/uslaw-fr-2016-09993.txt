DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Notice of Intent To Grant a Partially Exclusive License; AEgis 
Technologies Group, Inc.

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 35 U.S.C. 209(e) and 37 CFR part 
404.7(a)(1)(i), the Department of the Army announces the intent to 
grant a revocable, non-assignable, partially exclusive license to AEgis 
Technologies Inc., Huntsville, Alabama, for the Joint Embedded 
Messaging System (JEMS) government-owned software for efficient 
manipulation of data. Joint Embedded Messaging System (JEMS) routes and 
translates messages and protocols for command and control (C2), 
simulation, and other systems using a configurable application for 
input and output formats, providing interoperability solutions while 
not requiring software modifications for data compatibility.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license can file 
written objections, along with supporting evidence, if any, within 15 
days from the date of this publication.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Office of 
Research and Technology Applications, SDMC-CT (Ms. Susan D. McRae), 
Bldg. 5220, Von Braun Complex, Room 5078, Redstone Arsenal, AL 35898.
