NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Proposal Review Panel for Ocean Sciences; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation (NSF) announces the 
following meeting:
    Name and Committee Code: Proposal Review Panel for Ocean Sciences 
(#10752)--C-DEBI, University of Southern California (Site Visit).

    Date And Time:
January 29, 2018; 8:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m.
January 30, 2018; 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m.

    Place: C-DEBI, University of Southern California, Los Angeles, CA 
90089.

[[Page 837]]

22314. Telephone (703) 292-7585.

Biosphere Investigations (C-DEBI) Science and Technology Center.

Organizational Structure & Partner Involvement to achieve center goals;
