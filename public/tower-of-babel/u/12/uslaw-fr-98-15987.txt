Employment and Training Administration

Estela Tops, Huntington Park, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 16, 1998 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on March 16, 1998 on behalf of workers at Estala Tops, 
Huntington Park, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 12th day of May, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
