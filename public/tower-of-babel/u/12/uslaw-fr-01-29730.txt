Legal Description of Barry M. Goldwater Range Withdrawal, AZ

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice provides official publication of the legal 
description of the Barry M. Goldwater Range withdrawal in Arizona, as 
required by Section 3033(a)(1) of Public Law 106-65 (October 5, 1999).

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 5, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Eugene A. Dahlem at the Bureau of Land 
Management, Phoenix Field Office, 21605 North 7th Avenue, Phoenix, 
Arizona 85027, (623) 580-5525.

    The area described contains 1,733,921 acres, more or less, of 
withdrawn land in Maricopa, Pima and Yuma Counties. The withdrawn area 
consists of approximately 83,675 acres of former State and Private 
lands, in scattered parcels owned by the military, and approximately 
1,650,246 acres of public lands.
