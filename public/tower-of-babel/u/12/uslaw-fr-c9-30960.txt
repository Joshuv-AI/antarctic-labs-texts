Correction

    In proposed rule document 99-30960 beginning on page 67216 in the 
issue of Wednesday, December 1, 1999, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 67217, in the first column, in the first paragraph, in 
the second to last line ``the'' should read ``ten''.
    2. On page 67218, in the table, the second and third entries 
``.043'' and ``.064'' should read ``0.43'' and ``0.64'' respectively.
    3. On page 67220, in the first column, under Regulatory Flexibility 
Act, nine lines from the bottom, ``$447'' should read ``$477''.
