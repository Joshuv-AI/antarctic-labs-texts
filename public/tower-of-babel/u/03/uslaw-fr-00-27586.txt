Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

October 19, 2000.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of 
this document is available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 
12th Street, S.W., Washington, D.C. or may be purchased from the 
Commission's copy contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to 
these petitions must be filed by (November 13, 2000. See Section 
1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an 
opposition must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing 
oppositions have expired.
    Subject: Redesignation of the 17.7-19.7 GHz Frequency Band, Blanket 
Licensing of Satellite Earth Stations in the 17.7-20.2 GHz and 27.5-
30.0 GHz Frequency Bands, and the Allocation of Additional Spectrum in 
the 17.3-17.8 GHz and 24.75-25.25 GHz Frequency Bands for Broadcast 
Satellite-Service Use (IB Docket No. 98-172, RM-9005, RM-9118).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 4.
    Subject: Implementation of Video Description of Video Programming 
(MM Docket No. 99-339).
