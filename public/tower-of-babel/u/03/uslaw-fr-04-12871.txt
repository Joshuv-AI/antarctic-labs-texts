Meridian Automotive Systems, Inc., Lenoir, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 27, 2004, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Meridian 
Automotive Systems, Inc., Lenoir, North Carolina.

[[Page 32048]]

    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 21st day of May, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
