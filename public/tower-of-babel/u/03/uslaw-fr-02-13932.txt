Carter Industries, Inc., Brooklyn, New York; Dismissal of 
Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Carter Industries, Inc., Brooklyn, 
New York. The application contained no new substantial information 
which would bear importantly on the Department's determination. 
Therefore, dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-39,396; Carter Industries, Inc. Brooklyn, New York (May 20, 
2002).

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 18th day of May, 2002.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
