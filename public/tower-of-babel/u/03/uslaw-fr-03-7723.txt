Notice of Cancellation of Customs Broker License

AGENCY: Bureau of Customs and Border Protection, Department of Homeland 
Security.

ACTION: General notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

(19 U.S.C. 1641) and the Customs Regulations(19 CFR 111.51), the 
following Customs broker license and any and all associated local and 
national permits are canceled without prejudice.
