DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

International Trade Administration


Application for Duty-Free Entry of Scientific Instrument

    Pursuant to Section 6(c) of the Educational, Scientific and 
Cultural Materials Importation Act of 1966 (Pub. L. 89-651; 80 Stat. 
897; 15 CFR part 301), we invite comments on the question of whether an 
instrument of equivalent scientific value, for the purposes for which 
the instrument shown below is intended to be used, is being 
manufactured in the United States.
    Comments must comply with 15 CFR 301.5(a) (3) and (4) of the 
regulations and be filed within 20 days with the Statutory Import 
Programs Staff, U.S. Department of Commerce, Washington, DC 20230. The 
application may be examined between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. in room 4211, 
U.S. Department of Commerce, 14th Street and Constitution Avenue, NW, 
Washington, DC.
    Docket Number: 96-109R. Applicant: University of Arkansas for 
Medical Sciences, 4301 W. Markham, Little Rock, AR 72205. Instrument: 
Rapid Kinetics Accessory, Model SFA-20. Manufacturer: Hi-Tech Ltd., 
United Kingdom. Intended Use: See the original notice of this 
resubmitted application which was published in the Federal Register of 
October 30, 1996.
Frank W. Creel,
Director, Statutory Import Programs Staff.
