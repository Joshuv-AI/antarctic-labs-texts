Revocation of Customs Broker's License

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Broker's license revocation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    I, as Commissioner, hereby pursuant to section 641(b)(5), Tariff 
Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1641(b)(5)) and section 111.45(a) of 
the Customs Regulations (19 CFR 111.45(a)), revoke the following 
Customs broker license.
