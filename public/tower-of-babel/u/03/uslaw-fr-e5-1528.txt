Ruskin Company; Clayton, OH; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 16, 2005 in response to a petition filed by a 
company official and the United Steel Workers Union, Local 4545 on 
behalf of workers at Ruskin Company, Clayton, Ohio.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 25th day of March 2005.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
