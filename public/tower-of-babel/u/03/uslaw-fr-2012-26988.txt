National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 27, 2012, 8:00 a.m. 
to November 27, 2012, 3:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Neurosciences Building, 6001 Executive Boulevard, Conference Room C, 
Rockville, MD 20852, which was published in the Federal Register on 
October 10, 2012, 77 FR 61614.
    This notice is amended to change the location, date and time to 
November 26, 2012, 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Executive Plaza North, 6130 
Executive Boulevard, Room 6042, Rockville, MD 20852. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: October 31, 2012.
