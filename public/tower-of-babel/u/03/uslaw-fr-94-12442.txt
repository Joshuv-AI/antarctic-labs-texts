DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Commonwealth Federal Savings Bank, Manassas, VA; Replacement of 
Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Subdivision (C) of section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator for Commonwealth Federal Savings Bank, 
Manassas, Virginia (``Association''), with the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as sole Receiver for the Association on May 6, 1994.

    Dated: May 17, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
