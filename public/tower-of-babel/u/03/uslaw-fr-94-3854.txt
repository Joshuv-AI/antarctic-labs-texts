DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
 

Advisory Committees; Renewals

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announces the renewal 
of two FDA advisory committees by the Commissioner of Food and Drugs. 
This notice is issued under the Federal Advisory Committee Act of 
October 6, 1972 (Pub. L. 92-463 (5 U.S.C. app. 2)).

DATES: Authority for these committees will expire on the date indicated 
below unless the Commissioner formally determines that the renewal is 
in the public interest.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
       Name of the committee                 Date of expiration         
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Food Advisory Committee              December 18, 1995                  
Vaccines and Related Biological      December 31, 1995                  
 Products Advisory Committee                                            
------------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Donna M. Combs, Committee Management 
Office (HFA-306), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, MD 20857, 301-443-2765.
