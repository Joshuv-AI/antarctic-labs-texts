FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[FRS 16687]


Open Commission Meeting by Teleconference, Thursday, April 23, 
2020

April 17, 2020.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
Thursday, April 23, 2020, which is scheduled to commence at 10:30 a.m. 
Due to the current COVID-19 pandemic and related agency telework and 
headquarters access policies, this meeting will be in a wholly 
electronic format and will be open to the public on the internet via 
live feed from the FCC's web page at www.fcc.gov/live and on the FCC's 
YouTube channel. Because of these special circumstances, the first 
three items on the agenda will be considered by the Commission during 
the teleconference meeting while the other items listed below are 
expected to be voted on circulation prior to the meeting.
