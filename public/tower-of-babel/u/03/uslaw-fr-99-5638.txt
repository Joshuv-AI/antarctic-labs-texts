DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board
[Order No. 1023]


Expansion of Foreign-Trade Zone 151; Findlay, OH; Correction

    The Federal Register notice (64 FR 8542, 2/22/99) describing 
Foreign-Trade Zones Board Order 1023 (approved 2/10/99) authorizing 
expansion of Foreign-Trade Zone 151 in Findlay, Ohio, is corrected as 
follows:
    Paragraph 6, Sentence 1, should read ``The application to expand 
FTZ 151-Site 1 and to include Site 2 is approved, * * *''

    Dated: March 2, 1999.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Acting Executive Secretary.
