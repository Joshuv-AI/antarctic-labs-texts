DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Change In Date for the December Commission Open Meeting

    Take notice that the Commission has changed the date for its 
December 2023 open meeting. The meeting will now take place on Tuesday, 
December 19, 2023. The open meeting had been initially scheduled for 
Thursday, December 21, 2023.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ The start time of the meeting remains 10:00 a.m. Eastern 
Time.

    Dated: October 26, 2023.
Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Deputy Secretary.
