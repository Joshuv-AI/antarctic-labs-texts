FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Freight Forwarder License Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, D.C. 
20573.

Zaky Transportation Services, Inc., 8610 N.W. 70 Street, Miami, FL 
33166, Officers: Isaac Wahnich, C.E.O., Robert Wahnich, Director
International Globtrade, Inc. d/b/a/ JAB Forwarding; Legacy Shipping, 
36 S. Wabash Avenue, Suite #602, Chicago, IL 60603, Officers: Spiro 
Jankovich, President, Frederick W. Ampt, Vice President
Southeast Logistics International, Inc., 122 Agape Street, Williamson, 
GA 30292, Officers: Patricia G. Owen, C.E.O., Larry Owens, Chief 
Financial Officer.

Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
