Extension of Scoping Period for Ward Valley Supplemental 
Environmental Impact Statement (SEIS) Until July 15, 1996

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) in California announces an 
extension of the scoping period for the Ward Valley SEIS until July 15, 
1996. Three public scoping workshops were held in Sacramento, San 
Bernardino and Needles, California. This extension is to allow the 
public more time to provide written comments on the scope of the SEIS.
