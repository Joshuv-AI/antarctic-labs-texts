Safety Zones; Recurring Annual Events Held in Coast Guard Sector 
Boston Captain of the Port Zone

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DHS.

ACTION: Notice of enforcement of regulation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard will enforce eleven safety zones within the 
Captain of the Port Boston zone on the specified dates and times. This 
action is necessary to ensure the safety of vessels, spectators and 
participants from hazards associated with fireworks displays and swim 
events. During the enforcement period, no person or vessel, except for 
the safety vessels assisting with the events, may enter the safety 
zones without permission of the Captain of the Port (COTP) or his 
designated on-scene representative.

DATES: The regulations in 33 CFR 165.118 and 33 CFR 165.119 will be 
enforced for the safety zones identified in the SUPPLEMENTARY 
INFORMATION section below for the dates and times specified.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: If you have questions about this 
notice of enforcement, call or email Mark Cutter,

[[Page 27777]]

Sector Boston Waterways Management Division, U.S. Coast Guard; 
telephone 617-223-4000, email [email protected].
