INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION
[Investigation No. 337-TA-370]


Certain Salinomycin Biomass and Preparations Containing Same; 
Change of Commission Investigative Attorney

    Notice is hereby given that, as of this date, Juan S. Cockburn, 
Esq. of the Office of Unfair Import Investigations is designated as the 
Commission investigative attorney in the above-cited investigation 
instead of Teresa M.B. Martinez, Esq. and Juan S. Cockburn, Esq.
    The Secretary is requested to publish this Notice in the Federal 
Register.

    Dated: October 2, 1995.
Lynn I. Levine,
Director, Office of Unfair Import Investigations, 500 E Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20436.
