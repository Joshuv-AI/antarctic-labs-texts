Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2002-11939.
    Petitioner: Civil Air Patrol.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  119.1.
    Description of Relief Sought: Civil Air Patrol (CAP) is requesting 
relief to operate in a third class of missions--C missions under CAP 
72-2 to serve communities, states, and the nation. Additionally, relief 
would allow for CAP to accept tasking directly from a government and 
non-profit organization as a C mission. CAP C missions consist of 
missions directed and funded by the CAP and may support functions such 
as mission pilot evaluations, support to state, county and local 
agencies, and flight academies for cadets.
