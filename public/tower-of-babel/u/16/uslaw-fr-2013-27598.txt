National Institute on Aging; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National 
Institute on Aging Special Emphasis Panel, December 18, 2013, 2:00 p.m. 
to December 18, 2013, 3:15 p.m., National Institute of Aging, Gateway 
Building, 7201 Wisconsin Avenue, Suite 2C212, Bethesda, MD 20892, which 
was published in the Federal Register on November 8, 2013, 67177 FR 
217.
