DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Scoping Meetings for Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator 
Relocation Environmental Assessment (EA)

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of extension of comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 42243]]

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy published on May 31, 2002, in the 
Federal Register, a notice announcing its intent to prepare an EA for 
determining the future location of the Department's Heat Source/
Radioisotope Power System (HS/RPS) assembly and test operations. The 
Department invited comments on the proposed action and scope of the EA 
and announced public meetings to encourage public participation. The 
comment period was to continue for 21 days from the date of the 
publication of the Federal Register notice (till June 21, 2002).
    This Federal Register notice extends the comment period till July 
5, 2002. The comments received after this date will be considered to 
the extent practical.

DATES: The scoping comment period has been extended to July 5, 2002.
