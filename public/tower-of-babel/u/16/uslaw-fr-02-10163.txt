FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-864; MB Docket No. 02-76; RM-10405]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Crisfield, MD

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on the proposed substitution 
of Channel 250A for Channel 245A at station WBEY(FM), Crisfield, 
Maryland. This channel change will allow Station WBEY to avoid ducting 
interference and to operate with maximum Class A FM facilities. 
Coordinates used for this proposal are 37-55-13 NL and 75-41-59 WL.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before June 3, 2002, and reply 
comments on or before June 18, 2002.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 
20554. In addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties 
should serve the petitioner, as follows: Law Office of Lauren A. Colby; 
10 E. Fourth Street; P.O. Box 113; Frederick, Maryland 21705-0113.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
