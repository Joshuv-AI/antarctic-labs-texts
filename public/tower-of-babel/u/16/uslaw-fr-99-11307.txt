SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region 1 Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region 1 Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Augusta, will hold a public meeting 
at 10:00 a.m. on Wednesday, May 26th, 1999 at the Augusta Civic Center, 
Civic Center Drive, Augusta, Maine, to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, 
or others present.
    For further information, write or call Mary McAleney, District 
Director, U.S.

[[Page 24438]]

Small Business Administration, 40 Western Avenue, Augusta, Maine 04330, 
207-622-8378.

Director, External Affairs.
