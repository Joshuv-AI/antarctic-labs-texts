DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On July, 19, 1999, a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 64, No. 137, Page 38687, that an application had been filed with 
the Fish and Wildlife Service by Randy Pope for a permit (PRT-013353) 
to import one polar bear (Ursus maritimus) trophy taken from the 
Lancaster Sound population, Canada for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on October 18, 1999, as authorized by 
the provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended 
(16 U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
    On Aug. 12, 1999, a notice was published in the Federal Register,
