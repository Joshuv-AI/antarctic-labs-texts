DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given to amend the notice of the National Cancer 
Institute Board of Scientific Counselors, Division of Cancer Etiology 
meeting which was published in the Federal Register (60-FR-19600) on 
April 19, 1995.
    The Board was originally scheduled to be open on June 15 from 9 am 
to recess and closed on June 16 from 9 am to adjournment. The Board 
meeting will now be open from 8:30 am to approximately 3 pm on June 15. 
The meeting will be closed on June 15 from approximately 3 pm to recess 
and from 8:30 am to adjournment on June 16.

    [[Page 30092]] Dated: May 31, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
