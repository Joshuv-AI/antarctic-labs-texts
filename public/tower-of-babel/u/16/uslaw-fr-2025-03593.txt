Radio Broadcasting Services; Ethete, Wyoming

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document amends the Table of FM Allotments, of the 
Federal Communications Commission's (Commission) rules, by allotting FM 
Channel 260C0 at Ethete, Wyoming, as a Tribal Allotment. The staff 
engineering analysis indicates that Channel 260C0 can be allotted to 
Ethete, Wyoming, consistent with the minimum distance separation 
requirements of the Commission's rules, with a site restriction of 42 
km (26 miles) north of the community. The reference coordinates are 43-
22-25 NL and 108-36-28 WL.

DATES: Effective April 14, 2025.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2054, [email protected].
