SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3581]


Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

    Beaver and Lawrence Counties and the contiguous counties of 
Alleghany, Butler, Mercer and Washington in the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania; Columbiana, Mahoning, and Trumbull in the State of Ohio; 
and Hancock County in the State of West Virginia constitute a disaster 
area as a result of heavy rain, high winds and flooding that occurred 
from May 18 through May 22, 2004. Applications for loans for physical 
damage as a result of the disaster may be filed until the close of 
business on August 2, 2004 and for economic injury until the close of 
business on March 2, 2005 at the address listed below or other locally 
announced locations:

Small Business Administration, Disaster Area 1 Office, 360 Rainbow 
Blvd., South 3rd Floor, Niagara Falls, NY 14303.
