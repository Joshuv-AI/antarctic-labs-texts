Notice of Relocation of the Bureau of Land Management's Gunnison 
Field Office in Gunnison, CO

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of relocation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management's (BLM) Gunnison Field Office 
moved from 216 North Colorado Street in Gunnison to a new location at 
650 South 11th Street in Gunnison, Colorado 81230. The BLM officially 
closed the office located on Colorado Street at 12 p.m., November 24, 
2010, and reopened at the new office December 6, 2010. The new 
telephone number is (970) 642-4940. Directions to the new office: From 
State Highway 50, turn east on Rio Grande Boulevard, continue 
approximately \1/2\ mile, then turn left on 11th Street. The new office 
is located on the northeast corner of Rio Grande Boulevard and 11th 
Street.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Brian St. George, BLM Gunnison Field 
Office, (970) 642-4940.

Helen M. Hankins,
State Director.
