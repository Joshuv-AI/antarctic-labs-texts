Radio Broadcasting Services; Camdenton and Laurie, MO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Camdenton Community Broadcasters proposed the allotment of 
Channel 265A at Camdenton, Missouri, as the community's first local 
commercial FM service. See 62 FR 10010, March 5, 1997. In response to a 
counterproposal filed by Bott Communications, Inc., we shall allot 
Channel 265C3 at Laurie, Missouri, as a first local service, at 
coordinates 38-08-30 and 92-50-37. There is a site restriction 6 
kilometers south of the community. No allotment will be made at 
Camdenton, Missouri. A filing window for Channel 265C3 at Laurie will 
not be opened at this time. Instead, the issue of opening this 
allotment for auction will be addressed by the Commission in a 
subsequent order.

DATES: Effective June 11, 2001.
