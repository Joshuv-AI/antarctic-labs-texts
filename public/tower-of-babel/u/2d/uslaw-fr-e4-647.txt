DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[ Docket No. IS04-179-000]


Texaco Petrochemical Pipeline LLC; Notice Requesting Briefs

March 16, 2004.
    On February 27, 2004, the Commission issued an order in the above 
captioned docket requesting briefs on a jurisdictional issue raised by 
Texaco Petrochemical Pipeline's proposal to cancel its tariff for the 
transportation of ethylene. See Texaco Petrochemical Pipeline LLC, 106 
FERC ] 61,186 (2004). Specifically, the Commission requested briefs on 
whether the Commission has jurisdiction over the transportation of 
ethylene by oil pipelines. Initial briefs were due 30 days after the 
February 27th Order issued. Since the 30th day falls on March 28, 2004, 
a Sunday, interested parties must intervene and file initial briefs no 
later than March 29, 2004. Reply briefs are due April 8, 2004, 
thereafter.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
