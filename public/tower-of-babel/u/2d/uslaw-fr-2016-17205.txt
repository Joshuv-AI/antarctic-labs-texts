SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On July 7, 2016, the Department of Commerce 
(the Department) published Initiation of Antidumping and Countervailing 
Duty Administrative Reviews; 81 FR 44260 (July 7, 2016) (Initiation 
Notice) in which the Department inadvertently initiated an antidumping 
duty administrative review of Certain Welded Carbon Steel Standard 
Pipes and Tubes from India. The Department did not receive a request 
for review covering the period May 1, 2015, through April 30, 2016, 
with respect to any companies, as such, we are not initiating a review 
with respect to this order. In addition, in the Initiation Notice the 
Department misspelt Overseas Distribution Services Inc. as Overseas 
Distrubution Services Inc.\1\ This notice serves as a correction to the 
Initiation Notice.
