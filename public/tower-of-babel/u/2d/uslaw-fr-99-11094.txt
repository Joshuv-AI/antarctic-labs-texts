Collection; Comment Request

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The New York Export Assistant Center, which is a combined effort of 
the U.S. Department of Commerce, Export-Import Bank, and Small Business 
Administration provides a comprehensive array of export counseling and 
trade finance services to small and medium-sized U.S. exporting firms. 
In 1998, it launched an interactive website, www.nyuseac.org that is 
geared to the needs of New York and New Jersey metropolitan industry. 
One electronic form is proposed to be added to the website in order to 
improve the usefulness of the site. The form will ask U.S. exporting 
firm respondents to provide general background information and identify 
which service(s) they are interested in.

II. Method of Collection

    The form is submitted via internet, telephone, fax, or email.

IV. Request for Comments

    Comments are invited on (a) whether the proposed collection of

Comments submitted in response to this notice will be summarized and/or
