Employment and Training Administration

Voith Fabrics, Inc., Frankfort, Kentucky; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 4, 2002 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by the company on behalf of workers at Voith Fabrics, 
Inc., Frankfort, Kentucky.
    The petitioner requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.
