FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: Federal Election Commission

DATE AND TIME: Tuesday March 11, 2014 at 11:00 a.m.

PLACE: 999 E Street, NW., Washington, DC

STATUS: This meeting will be closed to the public.

ITEMS TO BE DISCUSSED: Information the premature disclosure of which 
would be likely to have a considerable adverse effect on the 
implementation of a proposed Commission action.
    Matters concerning participation in civil actions or proceedings or 
arbitration.
