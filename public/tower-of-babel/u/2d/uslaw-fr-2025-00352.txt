DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RM01-5-000]


Electronic Tariff Filings; Notice of Inability To Add Service 
List Entries for eTariff Filings

    Take notice that filers making eTariff currently are not able to 
add additional names to the service list through FERC Online. The 
account manager in Company Registration is added

[[Page 1996]]

automatically. A solution for this problem is in progress. In the 
interim, if filers want to add additional names to the service list, 
they should file an Intervention through FERC Online.

    Dated: January 3, 2025.
Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Secretary.
