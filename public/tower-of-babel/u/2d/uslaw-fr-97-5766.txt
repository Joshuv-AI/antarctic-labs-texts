DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Privacy Act of 1974: Deletion of Systems of Records Notices

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice to delete Privacy Act systems of records.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is deleting the following 
systems from its inventory of Privacy Act systems of records notices.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 10, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Crystal M. Bush, Privacy Coordinator, 
U.S. Department of Transportation, Washington, DC 20590. Telephone: 
(202) 366-9713.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974, 
the Department of Transportation conducted a review of several of its 
Privacy Act systems of records and determined the following records are 
covered by DOT/ALL 8, Employee Transportation Facilitation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
              System No.                           System name          
------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOT/OST 024...........................  Parking Permit Application Files
                                         and Vanpool Application Files. 
DOT/OST 025...........................  Parking Permit Management       
                                         System.
