DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Posting of Stockyards

    Pursuant to the authority provided under Section 302 of the Packers 
and Stockyards Act (7 U.S.C. 202), it was ascertained that the 
livestock markets named below are stockyards as defined by Section 
302(a). Notice was given to the stockyard owners and to the public as 
required by Section 302(b), by posting notices at the stockyards on the 
dates specified below, that the stockyards are subject to the 
provisions of the Packers and Stockyards Act, 1921, as amended (7 
U.S.C. 181 et seq.).
