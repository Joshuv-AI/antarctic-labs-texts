    On January 3, 2018, Northern Indiana Public Service Company 
(transferor) and Northern Indiana Public Service Company LLC 
(transferee) filed an application for the transfer of license of the 
Norway-Oakdale Hydroelectric Project No. 12514. The project is located 
on the Tippecanoe River in Carroll and White counties, Indiana.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Norway-Oakdale Hydroelectric Project from the transferor to the 
transferee. This transfer of license reflects an internal corporate 
reorganization. The transferor will be merged into newly created 
transferee.
