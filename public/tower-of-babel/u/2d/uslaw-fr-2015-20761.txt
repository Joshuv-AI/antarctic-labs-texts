DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of the Comptroller of the Currency

[OCC Charter Number 706624]


New Buffalo Savings Bank, New Buffalo, Michigan; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on August 12, 2015, the Office of the 
Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) approved the application of New 
Buffalo Savings Bank, New Buffalo, Michigan, to convert to the stock 
form of organization. Copies of the application are available for 
inspection on the OCC Web site at the FOIA Electronic Reading Room 
https://foia-pal.occ.gov/palMain.aspx. If you have any questions, 
please call OCC Licensing Activities at (202) 649-6260.

    Dated: August 12, 2015.

    By the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency.
Stephen A. Lybarger,
Deputy Comptroller for Licensing.
