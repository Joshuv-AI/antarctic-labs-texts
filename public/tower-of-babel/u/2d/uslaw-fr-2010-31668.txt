OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Request for Comments on a Revised 
Information Collection: (OMB Control No. 3206-0142; Standard Form 2808)

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget (OMB) a request for review of a revised information 
collection. This information collection, ``Designation of Beneficiary: 
Civil Service Retirement System (CSRS)'' (OMB Control No. 3206-0142; 
Standard Form 2808), is used by persons covered by CSRS to designate a 
beneficiary to receive the lump sum payment due from the Civil Service 
Retirement and Disability Fund in the event of their death.
    Approximately 2,000 forms will be completed annually. The form 
takes approximately 15 minutes to complete. The annual burden is 
estimated at 500 hours.

4808, FAX (202) 606-0910 or via e-mail to [email protected]. Please 
include a mailing address with your request.
