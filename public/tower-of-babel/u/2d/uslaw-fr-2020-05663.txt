DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Veterans Rural Health Advisory Committee; Notice of Meeting--
Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act that the meeting of the Veterans Rural 
Health Advisory Committee previously scheduled to be held on April 15-
16, 2020, at Raymond G. Murphy VA Medical Center, 1501 San Pedro Dr. 
SE, Albuquerque, NM 87108 has been cancelled. For more information, 
please contact Mr. Thomas Klobucar, Designated Federal Officer at (202) 
632-8581, or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: March 13, 2020.
LaTonya L. Small,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
