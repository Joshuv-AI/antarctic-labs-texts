NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[NRC-2016-0168]


Request To Amend a License To Import Radioactive Waste

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Update of import license application and extension of comment 
period; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register on August 8, 2016, 
regarding request to amend a license to import radioactive waste. This 
action is necessary in order to adequately describe the type of 
radioactive material being requested for import.

DATES: The correction is effective March 6, 2017.
