DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases Special Emphasis Panel, 
February 8, 2001, 8:00 am to February 9, 2001, 5:00 pm, Holiday Inn 
Downtown DC, 1155 14th Street, NW, Washington, DC 20005 which was 
published in the Federal Register on December 19, 2000, 65 FR 79377.
    The meeting will be held at the Bethesda Holiday Inn, Bethesda, 
Maryland. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: January 5, 2001.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
