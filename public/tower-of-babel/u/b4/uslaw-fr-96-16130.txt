DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Sunshine Act Meeting

Board Conference

TIME and DATES: 10:00 a.m., July 3, 1996.

PLACE: Hearing Room A, Surface Transportation Board, 1201 Constitution 
Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20423.

STATUS: The Board will meet to discuss among themselves the following 
agenda items. Although the conference is open for the public 
observation, no public participation is permitted.
