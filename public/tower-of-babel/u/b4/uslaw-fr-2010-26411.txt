SUMMARY: On September 30, 2010 (75 FR 60430) the Department of Defense 
published a notice in the Federal Register announcing an October 21, 
2010, meeting of the Threat Reduction Advisory Committee in Chantilly, 
VA. The Department is now announcing that the October 21 meeting has 
been cancelled.
