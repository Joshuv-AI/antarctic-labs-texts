DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-404-000]


Mississippi River Transmission Corporation; Notice of Technical 
Conference

January 6, 1999.
    Take notice that the Commission staff will convene a technical 
conference as provided by the Commission order in this proceeding 
issued October 14, 1998. The conference will be held on Thursday, 
January 14, 1999, beginning at 10:00 a.m. in a room to be designated at 
the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are invited to attend.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
