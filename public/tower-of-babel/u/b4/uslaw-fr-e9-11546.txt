DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 6617]


In the Matter of the Designation of Revolutionary Struggle aka 
Epanastatikos Aghonas as a Foreign Terrorist Organization Pursuant to 
Section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as Amended

    Based upon a review of the Administrative Record assembled in this 
matter, and in consultation with the Attorney General and the Secretary 
of the Treasury, I conclude that there is a sufficient factual basis to 
find that the relevant circumstances described in section 219 of the 
Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended (hereinafter ``INA'') (8 
U.S.C. 1189), exist with respect to Revolutionary Struggle (aka 
Epanastatikos Aghonas).
    Therefore, I hereby designate that organization and its alias as a 
foreign terrorist organization pursuant to section 219 of the INA.
    This determination shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: April 29, 2009.
Hillary Rodham Clinton,
Secretary of State, Department of State.
