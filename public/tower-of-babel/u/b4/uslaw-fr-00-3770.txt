PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.; Notice of Initiation of Proceeding 
and Refund Effective Date

February 11, 2000.
    Take notice that on February 10, 2000, the Commission issued an 
order in the above-referenced dockets initiating an investigation in 
Docket No. EL00-41-000 under section 206 of the Federal Power Act.
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL00-41-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the Federal Power Act, will be 60 days 
following publication of this notice in the Federal Register.
