Radio Broadcasting Services; Cheyenne and Encampment, WY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition for rule making 
filed by Mountain States Radio, Inc. (``Petitioner''), licensee of 
Station KRRR(FM), Channel 285C2, Cheyenne, Wyoming. The petition 
requests that the Commission allot Channel 285C2 to Encampment, 
Wyoming; substitute Channel 229C2 for Channel 285C2 at Cheyenne, 
Wyoming; and substitute Channel 285C2 for vacant Channel 229A at 
Cheyenne. The coordinates for proposed Channel 285C2 at Encampment are 
41-14-00 NL and 106-56-46 WL, with a site restriction of 13.4 
kilometers (8.3 miles) west of Encampment. The coordinates for proposed 
Channel 229C2 at Cheyenne are 41-08-32 NL and 104-32-21 WL, with a site 
restriction of 23.0 kilometers (14.3 miles) east of Cheyenne. The 
coordinates for proposed Channel 285C2 at Cheyenne are 41-21-25 NL and 
104-40-55 WL, with a site restriction of 26.7 kilometers (16.6 miles) 
northeast of Cheyenne.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before December 16, 2004, and reply 
comments on or before December 31, 2004.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, 445 12th
