Manpower, Inc., Roswell, New Mexico; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 4, 2004, in response to a 
worker petition filed by one worker on behalf of workers of Manpower 
Inc., Roswell, New Mexico.
    To be valid, petitions must be filed by three workers, their duly 
authorized representative, or a State agency. The petition regarding 
the investigation has therefore been deemed invalid. Consequently, the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 6th day of February, 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
