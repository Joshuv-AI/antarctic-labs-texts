Wisconsin Automated Machinery Corp., Oshkosh, WI; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
Section 250(a), Subchapter D, Chapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 U.S.C. 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
September 9, 2002, in response to a petition filed by the International 
Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers Union, District 
10 and a company official on behalf of workers at Wisconsin 
Automated Machinery Corporation, Oshkosh, Wisconsin.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no purpose 
and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 10th day of October 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
