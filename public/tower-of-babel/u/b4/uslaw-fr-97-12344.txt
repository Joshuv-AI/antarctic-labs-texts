FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Deletion of Agenda Item from May 7th Open 
Meeting

May 6, 1997.
    The following item has been deleted from the list of agenda items 
scheduled for consideration at the May 7, 1997, Open Meeting (62 FR 
24653, May 6, 1997as revised in notice published May 8, 1997) and 
previously listed in the Commission's Notice of May 5, 1997.

Item No., Bureau, and Subject

1--Office of General Counsel--Title: Section 257 proceeding to Identify 
and Eliminate Market Entry Barriers for Small Businesses (GN Docket No. 
96-113).
Office of Communications Business Opportunities--Summary: The 
Commission will consider addressing implementation of Section 257.

Federal Communications Commission.
