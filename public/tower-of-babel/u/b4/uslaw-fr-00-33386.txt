Northern Natural Gas Company; Notice Shortening Comment Period

December 26, 2000.
    On December 22, 2000, Reliant Energy Minnegasco (Minnegasco) filed 
a Motion for Extension of Time Within Which to File Comments in the 
above-docketed proceeding. In the filing, Minnegasco requested that the 
period for submitting answers to the extension motion be shortened in 
order to expedite Commission review of the filing. By this notice, the 
date for filing answers to Minnegasco's Motion for Extension of Time is 
shortened to and including December 28, 2000.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
