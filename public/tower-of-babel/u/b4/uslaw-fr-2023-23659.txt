Radio Broadcasting Services; Wharton, Texas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document amends the FM Table of Allotments, of the 
Federal Communications Commission's (Commission) rules, by allotting 
Channel 277C2 at Wharton, Texas, as a second local service. A staff 
engineering analysis indicates that Channel 277C2 can be allotted to 
Wharton, Texas, consistent with the minimum distance separation 
requirements of the Commission's rules, with a site restriction of 2.1 
km (1.3 miles) west of the community. The reference coordinates are 29-
18-26 NL and 96-07-50 WL.

DATES: Effective December 4, 2023.

418-2054, [email protected].
