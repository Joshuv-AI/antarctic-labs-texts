Hewlett Packard, Corvallis, OR, Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 12, 2004 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Hewlett Packard, Corvallis, 
Oregon.
    The petition has been deemed invalid. There were not three 
petitioners from the same firm requesting certification to apply for 
trade adjustment assistance benefits.
    Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of November, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
