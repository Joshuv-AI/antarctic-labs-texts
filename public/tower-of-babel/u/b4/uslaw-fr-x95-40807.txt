Bulk Hazardous Materials; Correction

Correction

    In rule document 95-18764 beginning on page 39267 in the issue of 
Wednesday, August 2, 1995, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 39267, in the second column, in paragraph 6., in the 
correction to paragraph s., in the second line, ``Ethylene glycol'' 
should read ``Diethylene glycol''.
    2. On the same page, in the third column:
    a. In the seventh line from the top, ``GILT'' should read ``GLT''.
    b. In paragraph 11., in the third line, ``DAN'' should read 
``DAH''; and in the seventh line, ``LEO/'' should read ``VEO/''.
    c. In paragraph 14., in the fourth entry, the first line should 
read ``N-(2-Methoxy-1-methyl ethyl)-2-ethyl-6-''.
    d. In paragraph 14., in the sixth entry, in the first line, 
``aide'' should read ``fide''; and in the third line, ``polysulfide'' 
was misspelled.
