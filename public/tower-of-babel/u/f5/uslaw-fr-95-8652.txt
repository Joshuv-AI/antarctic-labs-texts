DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary

32 CFR Part 290


Defense Contract Audit Agency (DCAA) Freedom of Information Act 
Program

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary of Defense, DoD.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This administrative amendment is published to inform potential 
FOIA requestors of the geographical coverage of Wyoming from the 
Western region to the Central region as part of its reorganization. 
This part also authorizes the ``DCAA Label 4'' (For official use only 
coversheet).

EFFECTIVE DATE: (April 10, 1950).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. Dave Henshall, Attn: CMR, Defense Contract Audit Agency, Cameron 
Station, Alexandria, VA 22304-6168, telephone 703-274-4400.

List of Subjects in 32 CFR Part 290

    Freedom of information.

    Accordingly, 32 CFR Part 290 is amended as follows: [[Page 18006]] 

PART 290--DEFENSE CONTRACT AUDIT AGENCY (DCAA) FREEDOM OF 
INFORMATION ACT PROGRAM

    1. The authority citation of part 290 continues to read as follows:

    Authority: 5 U.S.C. 552.

    2. Appendix B to part 290 is amended as follows:
    a. Regional office CALIFORNIA is amended after ``Oregon'' by adding 
the word ``and'', and after ``Washington'' by removing the words, ``and 
Wyoming.''
    b. Regional office TEXAS is amended after ``Wisconsin'' by adding 
the state ``Wyoming''.
    3. Appendix C to part 290 is amended by adding new paragraph (c)(3) 
to read as follows:

Appendix C to Part 290--For Official Use Only

* * * * *
    (c) * * *
    (3) DCAA Label 4, FOUO Cover Sheet. This form may be used to 
further identify FOUO information.
