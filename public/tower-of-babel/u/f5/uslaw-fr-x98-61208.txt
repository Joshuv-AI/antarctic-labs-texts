Modification of Class E Airspace; Valparaiso, IN

Correction

    In rule document 98-30585, beginning on page 63601, in the issue of 
Monday, November 16, 1998, make the following correction:


Sec.  71.1  [Corrected]

    On page 63602, in the second column, in Sec. 71.1, the airspace 
designation is corrected to read as set forth below:
* * * * *
AGL IN E5 Valparaiso, IN [Revised]

Valparaiso, Porter County Municipal Airport, IN

    (Lat. 41 deg.27'15''N., long. 87 deg.00'22''W.)

    That airspace extending upward from 700 feet above the surface 
within an 7.7-mile radius of the Porter County Municipal Airport.
