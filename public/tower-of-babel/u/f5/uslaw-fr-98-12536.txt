SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region III District Advisory Council Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region III District Advisory 
Council, located in the geographical area of Clarksburg, West Virginia, 
will hold a public meeting at 10:30 a.m. on Monday, June 8, 1998, at 
Ponderosa Steak House, Bridgeport, West Virginia, to discuss such 
matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U. S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Jayne Armstrong, State 
Director, U. S. Small Business Administration, 168 West Main Street, 
Clarksburg, West Virginia 26301, telephone (304) 62305631 Ext. 223.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
