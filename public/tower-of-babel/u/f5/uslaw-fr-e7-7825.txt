OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Extension of a 
Currently Approved Information Collection: RI 38-115

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget (OMB) a request for extension of a currently approved 
information collection. RI 38-115, Representative Payee Survey, is used 
to collect information about how the benefits paid to a representative 
payee have been used or conserved for the benefit of the incompetent 
annuitant.
    Approximately 11,000 RI 38-115 forms are completed annually. The 
form takes approximately 20 minutes to complete. The annual estimated 
burden is 3,667 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact Mary Beth Smith-Toomey on 
(202) 606-8358, FAX (202) 418-3251 or via E-mail to [email protected]. Please include a mailing address with your request.
