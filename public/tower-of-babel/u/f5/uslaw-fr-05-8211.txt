FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 05-1023; MB Docket No. 04-316, RM-11047]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Morrison and Sparta, Tennessee

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Clear Channel 
Broadcasting Licenses, Inc., licensee of Station WRKK-FM, Channel 288A, 
Sparta, Tennessee, deletes Channel 288A at Sparta, Tennessee, from the 
FM Table of Allotments, allots Channel 287A at Morrison, Tennessee, as 
the community's first local FM service, and modifies the license of 
Station WRKK-FM to specify operation on Channel 287A at Morrison. 
Channel 287A can be allotted to Morrison, Tennessee, in compliance with 
the Commission's minimum distance separation requirements with a site 
restriction of 3.4 km (2.1 miles) northeast of Morrison. The 
coordinates for Channel 287A at Morrison, Tennessee, are 35-37-27 North 
Latitude and 85-53-37 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective May 23, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Deborah Dupont, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
