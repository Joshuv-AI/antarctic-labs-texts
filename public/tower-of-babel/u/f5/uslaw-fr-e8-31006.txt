FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 08-2721; MB Docket No. 08-115; RM-1145]


Television Broadcasting Services; Omaha, NE

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission grants a petition for rulemaking filed by Mitts 
Telecasting Company, licensee of station KXVO-DT, to substitute DTV 
channel 38 for its assigned post-transition DTV channel 15 at Omaha, 
Nebraska.

DATES: This rule is effective January 29, 2008.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shaun A. Maher, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
