FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Transportation Intermediary License Revocation

    The Federal Maritime Commission hereby gives notice that the 
following Ocean Transportation Intermediary license has been revoked 
pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 
1718) and the regulations of the Commission pertaining to the licensing 
of Ocean Transportation Intermediaries, effective on the corresponding 
date shown below:

License Number: 15865N.
Name: Davis International Trade and Transportation Organization, Inc. 
dba Ditto.
Address: 6610 Tributary Street, Suite 200, Baltimore, MD 21224.
Date Revoked: May 10, 2000.
Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.

Sandra L. Kusumoto,
Director, Bureau of Consumer Complaints and Licensing.
