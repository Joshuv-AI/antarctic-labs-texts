DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 8510]


Department of State Performance Review Board Members

    In accordance with section 4314(c)(4) of 5 United States Code, the 
Department of State has appointed the following individuals to the 
Department of State Performance Review Board for Senior Executive 
Service members:

Robert Goldberg, Chairperson, Director, Office of the United States 
Foreign Assistance Resources, Department of State;
Linda Jacobson, Assistant Legal Advisor, Office of the Legal Advisor, 
Department of State;
Margaret Pollack, Office Director, Bureau of Population, Refugees and 
Migration, Department of State; and
Teddy Taylor, Diplomat in Residence, Bureau of Human Resources, 
Department of State.

    Dated: October 21, 2013.
Hans Klemm,
Acting Director General of the Foreign Service and Director of Human 
Resources, Department of State.
