UNITED STATES INSTITUTE OF PEACE


Notice of Board Meeting

    Agency: United States Institute of Peace.
    Date/Time: Friday, January 17, 2020 (10:00 a.m.-12:30 p.m.).
    Location: 2301 Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20037.
    Status: Open Session--Portions may be closed pursuant to Subsection

and Security Committees; and Reports/Updates from the Front Lines: 
Iran/Iraq, Sudan/Ethiopia, and the Afghanistan Peace Process.
