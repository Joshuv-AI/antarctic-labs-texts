Foreign-Trade Zone 75--Phoenix, Arizona; Withdrawal of 
Application for Subzone Expansion; Conair Corporation, Glendale, 
Arizona

    The City of Phoenix, Arizona, grantee of FTZ 75, submitted an 
application, docketed on May 16, 2017, requesting expanded subzone 
status for the facilities of Conair Corporation (Conair), Subzone 75A, 
located in Glendale, Arizona (82 FR 25239, June 1, 2017). The City of 
Phoenix subsequently requested and obtained approval for the expanded 
subzone status for Conair under the alternative site framework. As a 
result, the City of Phoenix has withdrawn the initial application 
requesting expanded subzone status. For further information, contact 
Christopher Kemp at [email protected] or (202) 482-0862.

    Dated: August 11, 2017.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
