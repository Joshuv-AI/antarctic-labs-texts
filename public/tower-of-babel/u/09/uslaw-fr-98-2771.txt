Notice of Proposed Withdrawal and Opportunity for Public Meeting; 
Oregon; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects an error in the land description 
published in the Federal Register, 62 FR 61342, on November 17, 1997, 
as FR Doc. 97-30061, for a proposed withdrawal.
    On page 61343, paragraph 1 which reads ``Olalla-Thompson Creek Day 
Use Area, T. 30 S., R. 7 W., sec. 5, SW\1/4\NE\1/4\NE\1/4\SW\1/4\,'' is 
hereby corrected to read ``sec. 5, SW\1/4\NE\1/4\SW\1/4\,''.

    Dated: January 23, 1998.
Robert D. DeViney, Jr.,
Chief, Branch of Realty and Records Services.
