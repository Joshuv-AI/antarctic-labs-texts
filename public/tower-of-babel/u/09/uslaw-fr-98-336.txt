DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER98-929-000]


Atlantic City Electric Company; Notice of Filing

December 31, 1997.
    Take notice that on December 4, 1997, Atlantic City Electric 
Company (Atlantic Electric), tendered for filing service agreements 
under which Atlantic Electric will sell capacity and energy to 
Allegheny Power (Allegheny), and NESI Power Marketing, Inc. (NESI), 
under Atlantic Electric's market-based rate sales tariff. Atlantic 
Electric requests the agreements be accepted to become effective on 
December 4, 1997.
    Atlantic Electric states that a copy of the filing has been served 
on Allegheny and NESI.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before January 12, 1998. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make

[[Page 835]]

protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a 
party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on 
file with the Commission and are available for public inspection.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
