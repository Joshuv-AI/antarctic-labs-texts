DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Treasury Advisory Committee on Commercial Operations of the U.S. 
Customs Service

AGENCY: Department Offices, Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the date and location of the next 
meeting and the agenda for consideration by the Treasury Advisory 
Committee on Commercial Operations of the U.S. Customs Service.

DATES: The next meeting of the Treasury Advisory Committee on 
Commercial Operations of the U.S. Customs Service will be held on July 
15, 1994 in Washington, DC. The meeting will be held at 9:30 a.m. in 
room 500 of the conference facility of the Treasury Executive Institute 
at 1255 22nd Street, NW., Washington, DC 20220.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Keith McDaniels, International Trade Intern, Office of Tariff and Trade 
Affairs, Office of the Assistant Secretary (Enforcement), room 4004, 
Department of the Treasury, 1500 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Washington, 
DC 20220. Tel.: (202) 622-1433.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The preliminary agenda to be considered 
during the meeting on July 15, 1994 is as follows:
    1. Customs reorganization.
    2. Implementation of the Customs Modernization Act.
    a. National Customs Automation Plan, including Customs' computer 
hardware and software resources.
    b. Consistency of Customs entry procedures after the Customs 
Modernization Act.
    3. Update on the activities of the Trade Ombudsman. The provisional 
agenda may be modified prior to the meeting.
    The meeting is open to the public. However, it is necessary for any 
person other than an Advisory Committee member who wishes to attend the 
meeting to give advance notice. In order to be admitted to the meeting, 
contact Ms. Theresa Manning at (202) 622-0220 no later than July 8, 
1994.

    Dated: June 17, 1994.
John P. Simpson,
Deputy Assistant Secretary, Regulatory Tariff and Trade Enforcement.
