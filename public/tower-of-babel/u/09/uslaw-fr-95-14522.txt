FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 89-500; RM-6070]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Stephenson, MI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission dismisses a Petition for Reconsideration filed 
by Value Radio Corporation (Value). Value sought reconsideration of the 
action taken by the Chief, Allocations Branch in MM Docket No. 89-500 
on the basis that the action precluded its proposal, in another 
proceeding, for amendment of the Commission's FM Table of Allotments. 
See 56 FR 19039 (April 25, 1991). The Commission dismissed Value's 
petition as moot in light of the fact that Value's rulemaking proposal 
was subsequently approved and was not precluded by the action taken in 
MM Docket 89-500.

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 14, 1995.
