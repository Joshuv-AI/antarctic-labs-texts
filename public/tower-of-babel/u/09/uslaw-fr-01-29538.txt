DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, 
October 3, 2001, 1:00 p.m. to October 3, 2001, 3:00 PM, NIEHS-East 
Campus, 79 T W Alexander Dr., Bldg. 4401, Rm EC-122, Research Triangle 
Park, NC, 27709 which was published in the Federal Register on August 
30, 2001, FR 66: 45861.
    This telephone conference meeting will now be held on December 3, 
2001 at 1:00 pm and Dr. Linda Bass will be the Scientific Review 
Administrator. The meeting is closed to the public.
