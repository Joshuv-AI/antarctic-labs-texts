SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
 

Dallas/Fort Worth District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Dallas/Fort Worth Distict 
Advisory Council will hold a public meeting at 9 a.m. on Wednesday, 
March 30, 1994, in the Forum Room of the South Campus, Tarrant County 
Junior College, 5301 Campus Drive, Fort Worth, Texas, to discuss such 
matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call James S. Reed, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 4300 Amon Carter Blvd., 
suite 114, Fort Worth, Texas 76155, (817) 885-6500.

    Dated: February 24, 1994.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Acting Assistant Administrator, Office of Advisory Councils.
