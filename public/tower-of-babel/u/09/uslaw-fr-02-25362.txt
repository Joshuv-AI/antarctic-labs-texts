Final Data Quality Guidelines

AGENCY: Securities and Exchange Commission.

ACTION: Notice of availability of final guidelines.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Securities and Exchange Commission has posted on its Web 
site at http://www.sec.gov its final data quality assurance guidelines. 
The guidelines describe the Commission's procedures for ensuring and 
maximizing the quality of information before it is disseminated to the 
public, and the procedures by which an affected person may obtain 
correction, where appropriate, of disseminated information that does 
not comply with the guidelines.
