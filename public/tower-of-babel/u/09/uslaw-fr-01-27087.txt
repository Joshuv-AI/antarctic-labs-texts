DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP01-53-001]


Great Lakes Gas Transport, LLC; Notice of Compliance Filing

October 23, 2001.
    Take notice that on December 8, 2000, Great Lakes Gas Transport, 
LLC (GT), formerly Gas Transport, Inc., filed an explanation of 
imbalance trading in compliance with a Commission order issued November 
9, 2000 in Docket No. RM96-1-014, et al. The filing provides an 
explanation of imbalance trading on GT's system.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before October 30, 2001. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection. This filing may also be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select ``Docket#'' and follow the 
instructions (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). Comments, protests and 
interventions may be filed electronically via the Internet in lieu of 
paper. See, 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the 
Commission's web site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
