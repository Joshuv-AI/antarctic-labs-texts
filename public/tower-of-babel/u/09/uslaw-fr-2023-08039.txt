COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Guam Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of virtual business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a virtual business meeting of the Guam 
Advisory Committee. The meeting scheduled for Tuesday, April 18, 2023, 
at 9:00 a.m. (ChST) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register 
of Monday, March 27, 2023, in FR Doc. 2023-06298 in the first and 
second columns of page 18114.

(202) 770-1856.
