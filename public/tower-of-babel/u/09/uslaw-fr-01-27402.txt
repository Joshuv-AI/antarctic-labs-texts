SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to the final rule for the 
Alaska Commercial Operator's Annual Report (COAR) that was published in 
the Federal Register on August 20, 2001.

DATES: Effective November 1, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Patsy A. Bearden, 907-586-7008.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: A final rule was published in the Federal 
Register on 66 FR 43524 (August 20, 2001), to require groundfish 
motherships and catcher/processors to submit annually to the State of 
Alaska, Department of Fish & Game (ADF&G), a COAR.
    Several errors were made in Tables 14A, 15, 16, and 18 to 50 CFR 
part 679. These tables are corrected and reprinted as follows:

List of Subjects in 50 CFR Part 679

    Alaska, Fisheries, Recordkeeping and reporting requirements.

    Accordingly, 50 CFR part 679 is amended by making the following 
corrections:
