PART 4--[CORRECTED]

    In rule document 01-15166 beginning on page 32882 in the issue of 
Monday, June 18, 2001, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 32888, in the second column, in line 17, ``office of 
Hearings and Appeals,'' should read ``Office of Hearings and 
Appeals,''.


Sec. 4.201  [Corrected]

    2. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 4.201, in 
paragraph (3), ``decedent to their heirs,'' should read ``decedent to 
the heirs,''.


Sec. 4.202  [Corrected]

    3. On page 32889, in the first column, in Sec. 4.202, in line 15, 
``devicees'' should read ``devisees''.


Sec. 4.251  [Corrected]

    4. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 4.251(a), in the 
third line, ``many'' should read ``may''.
    5. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 4.251(b)(4), 
``Claims for'' should read ``Claims of''.
    6. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 4.251(d), in the 
last line, ``entirely.'' should read ``entirety.''.


Sec. 4.270  [Corrected]

    7. On page 32890, in the first column, in Sec. 4.270, in the third 
line from the bottom, ``of deceased'' should read ``of a deceased''.


Sec. 4.273  [Corrected]

    8. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 4.273(b), in the 
11th line, ``proceeding'' should read ``proceedings''.

Sec. 4.320  [Corrected]

    9. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 4.320(a), in the
