Correction

Friday, April 16, 1999, make the following correction(s):

paragraph (b)(ii)(A), in the second line, after the word ``of'' add 
``a''.
    2. On the same page, in the second column, in the same section, in 
paragraph (b)(ii)(C), in the fourth line, ``class'' should read 
``class,''.


235.006-70  [Corrected]

    3. On page 18830, in the second column, in section 235.006-70, in 
the heading ''Manufacturing Technology Program'' should read 
``Manufacturing Technology Program.''
    4. On the same page, in the second column, in the same section, in 
paragraph (b), in the first line, ``contract'' should read 
``contracts''.
