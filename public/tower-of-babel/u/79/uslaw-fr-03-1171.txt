DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. P-6564]


Jane A. Horning; Notice of Site Visit

January 13, 2003.
    Jane A. Horning, exemptee, for the Brunswick Creek Hydroelectric 
Project (project), in Washington County, North Plains, Oregon, requests 
to surrender her exemption. On January 30, 2003, the

[[Page 2537]]

staff of the Office of Energy Projects (OEP) will conduct a site visit 
of the project. Representatives of the project will accompany the OEP 
staff. All interested parties may meet at 9 a.m. at the project 
powerhouse located near the Brunswick Creek dam. Attendees must provide 
their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Blake Condo at (202) 502-
8914 or the Commission's Office of External Affairs at (866) 208-FERC.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
