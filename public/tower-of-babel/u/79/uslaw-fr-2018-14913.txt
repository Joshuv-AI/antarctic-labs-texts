Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2018-0325.
    Petitioner: 3GLP, Inc. dba Precision Flight Devices.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Part 21, subpart H; part 27; 
45.23(b); 45.27(a); 61.113; 91.7(a); 91.9(b)(2); 91.9(c); 91.103; 
91.109(a); 91.119; 91.121; 91.151(a); 91.203(a) & (b); 91.405(a); 
91.407(a)(1); 91.409(a)(1) & (2); 91.417(a) & (b).
    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner is requesting relief 
to commercially operate a CW-30, hybrid fixed wing and vertical takeoff 
and land multi-copter, unmanned aircraft (UA) that weighs more than 55 
pounds (lbs.). The proposed operation would allow the petitioner to 
conduct aerial data collection to include remote sensing and measuring 
by an instrument or combination of instruments aboard the UA. 
Specifically, Precision Flight Devices intends to operate the UA at 
less than 30 miles per hour with a maximum takeoff weight of 75 lbs. 
The operations would be conducted within visual line of sight over a 
15.56 square mile area situated at and around Kiana and Nulato 
airfields (8 square miles per airport) in Western Alaska. The 
collection of UA high resolution data and images may require operations 
as close as 200 feet from non-participating persons.
