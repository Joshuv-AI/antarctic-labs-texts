DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Meeting of the Defense Business Board (DBB)

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Meeting notice; cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense published an announcement of a 
meeting of the Defense Business Board (DBB) on October 1, 2009 (74 FR 
50782-50783). The meeting was scheduled for October 22, 2009. This 
meeting has been cancelled. This meeting may be rescheduled and 
announced at a later date.
