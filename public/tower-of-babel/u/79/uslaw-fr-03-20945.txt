Radio Broadcasting Services; Cambria, California

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a Petition for Rule Making 
filed by Daniel R. Feely proposing the allotment of Channel 287A at 
Cambria, California, as the community's third local aural transmission 
service. Channel 287A can be allotted to Cambria at city reference 
coordinates. The reference coordinates for Channel 287A at Cambria, 
California are 35-33-14 NL and 121-05-15 WL.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 22, 2003, and 
reply comments on or before October 7, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street SW., 
Washington, DC 20554. In addition to filing comments with the FCC, 
interested parties should serve the petitioner, as follows: Daniel R. 
Feely, 682 Palisade Street, Pasadena, California 91103.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
