DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Mojave National Preserve Advisory Commission; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given in accordance with the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act that a meeting of the Mojave National Preserve Advisory 
Commission will be held March 23, 1998; assemble at 9:00 a.m. at the 
National Park Service Office Facility, Baker, California.
    The agenda: General Management Plan--Alternatives, re: Cultural 
Resources; Native American Interests; Visitor Use, Services and 
Facilities; Visitor Centers, Wayside Exhibits, and Education; 
Recreational/Day Use Activities; Camping; Campgrounds; Commercial 
Services; Mojave Road; Administrative Operations and Facilities; 
Staffing and Budget; Education and Research; Soda Springs; Granite 
Mountain Reserve.
    The Advisory Commission was established by Public Law 103-433 to 
provide for the advice on development and implementation of the General 
Management Plan.
