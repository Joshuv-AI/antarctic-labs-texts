N.E.W. Hydro, LLC; Wisconsin8, LLC; Notice of Application for 
Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To Intervene, and 
Protests

    On July 13, 2016, N.E.W. Hydro, LLC (transferor) and Wisconsin8, 
LLC (transferee) filed an application for the transfer of license of 
the Weyauwega Hydroelectric Project No. 2550. The project is located on 
the Waupaca River in Waupaca County, Wisconsin. The project does not 
occupy federal lands.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Weyauwega Hydroelectric Project from the transferor to the 
transferee.
