Advisory Circular (AC) 187-1A, Flight Standards Service Schedule 
of Charges Outside the United States

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the availability of AC 187-1A.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Background

    AC 187-1A transmits an updated schedule of charges for services of 
FAA Flight Standards aviation safety inspectors outside the United 
States. The AC has been updated in accordance with the procedures 
listed in 14 CFR Part 187, Appendix A.
