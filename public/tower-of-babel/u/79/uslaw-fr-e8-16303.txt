DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Compliance Workshop; Notice of Comment Period for Workshop on 
Regulatory Compliance

July 10, 2008.
    As announced at the Workshop on Regulatory Compliance held July 8, 
2008, the Commission is providing the opportunity for written comments 
to be filed in this proceeding until July 22, 2008.
    The Commission encourages electronic submission in lieu of paper 
using the ``eFiling'' link at http://www.ferc.gov. Persons unable to 
file electronically should submit an original and 14 copies of comments 
to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
