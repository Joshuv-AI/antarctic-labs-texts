DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Parole Commission


Sunshine Act Meeting

Public Announcement

    Pursuant To The Government In the Sunshine Act (Public Law 94-409) 
[5 U.S.C. Section 552b]

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Department of Justice, United States Parole 
Commission.

TIME AND DATE: 10:30 a.m., Wednesday, September 22, 1999.

PLACE: U.S. Parole Commission, 5550 Friendship Boulevard, 4th Floor, 
Chevy Chase, Maryland 20815.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The following matters have been placed on the 
agenda for the open Parole Commission meeting:
    1. Approval of minutes of previous Commission meeting.
    2. Reports from the Chairman, Commissioners, Legal, Chief of Staff, 
Case Operations, and Administrative Sections.
    3. Approval of FY 2001 U.S. Parole Commission Budget Request.

AGENCY CONTACT: Tom Kowalski, Case Operations, United States Parole 
Commission, (301) 492-5962.

    Dated: September 15, 1999.
Michael A. Stover,
General Counsel, U.S. Parole Commission.
