Radio Broadcasting Services; Cross Hill, SC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a petition filed by Ron 
Moore proposing the allotment of Channel 231A at Cross Hill, South 
Carolina, as the community's first local aural transmission service. 
Channel 231A can be allotted to Cross Hill in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements with a site 
restriction of 14.7 kilometers (9.1 miles) southeast to avoid short-
spacings to the licensed sites of Station WGOR(FM), Channel 230C3, 
Martinez, Georgia, and Station WMUU-FM, Channel 233C, Greenville, South 
Carolina. The coordinates for Channel 231A at Cross Hill are North 
Latitude 34-13-04 and West Longitude 81-51-41.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before July 29, 1996, and reply 
comments on or before August 13, 1996.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner, or its counsel or consultant, as follows: Ron 
Moore, 811-A Montague Ave., Greenwood, South Carolina 29649 
(Petitioner).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon P. McDonald, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
