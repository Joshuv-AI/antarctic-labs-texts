SUMMARY: The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) issued Supplement 1 to 
Revision 1 to Generic Letter 92-01, ``Reactor Vessel Structural 
Integrity,'' on May 19, 1995. This generic letter supplement will be 
available in the NRC Public Document Room under accession number 
9505090312. This generic letter supplement was issued on an expedited 
basis in accordance with NRC procedures. This generic letter supplement 
is discussed in Commission information paper SECY-95-118 which will 
also be available in the NRC Public Document Room.
