SUMMARY: On December 17, 2014, the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) 
published a notice of availability in the Federal Register of the Draft 
Environmental Impact Statement for the Plains & Eastern Clean Line 
Transmission Project (DOE/EIS-0486; Draft EIS). This notice corrects 
the date of the public comment period.
