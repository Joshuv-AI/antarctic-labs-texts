Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Ketchup Caddy, LLC, Philip Mango; Updated Notice of Designation 
of Commission Staff as Non-Decisional

    With respect to an order issued by the Commission on February 21, 
2024, in the above-captioned docket, with the exceptions noted below, 
the staff of the Office of Enforcement are designated as non-decisional 
in deliberations by the Commission in this docket.\1\ Accordingly, 
pursuant to 18 CFR 385.2202 (2023), they will not serve as advisors to 
the Commission or take part in the Commission's review of any offer of 
settlement. Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.2201 (2023), they are prohibited from communicating with advisory 
staff concerning any deliberations in this docket.
