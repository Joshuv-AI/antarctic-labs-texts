OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


SES Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given of the membership of the OPM SES 
Performance Review Board.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mary Lynn Horst, Office of Human 
Resources and EEO, Office of Personnel Management, 1900 E Street, NW, 
Washington, DC 20415, (202) 606-2165.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Section 4314 ( c ) ( 1 ) through ( 5 ) of 
Title 5, U.S.C., requires each agency to establish, in accordance with 
regulations prescribed by the Office of Personnel Management, one or 
more SES performance review boards. The board reviews and evaluates the 
initial appraisal of a senior executive's performance by the 
supervisor, along with any recommendations to the appointment authority 
relative to the performance of the senior executive.

Office of Personnel Management.
