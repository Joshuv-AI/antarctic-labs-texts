Additional Information

    By a vote of 3-0 on May 2, 2018, the Commission determined pursuant 
to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec.  9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that the 
above referenced Affirmation Session be held with less than one week 
notice to the public. The meeting is scheduled on May 3, 2018.
