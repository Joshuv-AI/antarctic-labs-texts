FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Notice of Agency Meeting; Sunshine Act

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that at 10:30 a.m. on 
Friday, March 18, 2005, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation's 
Board of Directors will meet in closed session, pursuant to section 
552b(c)(2), (c)(4), (c)(6), (c)(8), and (c)(9)(A)(ii) of Title 5, 
United States Code, to consider matters relating to the Corporations's 
corporate and supervisory activities.
    The meeting will be held in the Board Room on the sixth floor of 
the FDIC Building located at 550 17th Street, NW., Washington, DC.
    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Ms. Valerie J. Best, Assistant Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-7043.

    Dated: March 11, 2005.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Valerie J. Best,
Assistant Executive Secretary.
