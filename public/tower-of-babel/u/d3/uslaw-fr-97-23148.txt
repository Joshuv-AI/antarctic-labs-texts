Overthrust Pipeline Company; Notice of Tariff Filing

August 26, 1997.
    Take notice that on August 22, 1997, Overthrust Pipeline Company 
(Overthrust) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, First 
Revised Volume No. 1-A, First Revised Sheet Nos. 67A and 67B and 
Original Sheet No. 67C, to be effective September 22, 1997.
    Overthrust explains that the proposed tariff sheets revise Section 
15, Scheduling of Gas Receipts and Deliveries, of the General Terms and 
Conditions of its tariff by implementing a second batch period for 
acceptance and processing of intra-day nominations received after 5:01 
p.m. Central Clock Time each gas day.
    Overthrust states that a copy of this filing has been served upon 
its customers, and the Wyoming Public Service Commission.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 385.211 and 385.214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests must be filed in accordance with Section 154.210 of the 
Commission's Regulations. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
