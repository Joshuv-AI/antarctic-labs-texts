SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On February 19, 2019, the Department of 
Commerce (Commerce) published the preliminary results of the 2016-2017 
administrative review of the antidumping duty order for certain hot-
rolled carbon steel flat products from the Russian Federation. Commerce 
inadvertently stated it intended to issue the final results of this 
administrative review no later than 90 days after the date these 
preliminary results of review were issued, pursuant to section 
751(a)(2)(B) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (the Act). Commerce properly 
intends to publish the final results of this administrative review, 
including the results of its analysis of issues addressed in any case 
or rebuttal brief, no later than 120 days after publication of the 
preliminary results, unless extended.\1\ This notice serves as a 
correction notice.
