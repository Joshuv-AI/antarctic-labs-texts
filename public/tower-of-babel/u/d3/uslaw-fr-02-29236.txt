Radio Broadcasting Services; Jasper, AR, and Milford, UT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division requests comments on a petition filed by 
JEM Broadcasting Company, Inc. proposing the allotment of Channel 245A 
at Jasper, Arkansas, as the community's first local aural transmission 
service. Channel 245A can be allotted to Jasper in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at city reference 
coordinates. The coordinates for Channel 245A at Jasper are 36-00-29 
North Latitude and 93-11-11 West Longitude. The Audio Division also 
requests comments on a petition filed by Larry Jackson proposing the 
allotment of Channel 288C2 at Milford, Utah, as the community's first 
local aural transmission service. Channel 288C2 can be allotted to 
Milford in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements at city reference coordinates. The coordinates for Channel 
288C2 at Milford are 38-23-49 North Latitude and 113-00-36 West 
Longitude.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before December 16, 2002, and reply 
comments on or before December 31, 2002.
