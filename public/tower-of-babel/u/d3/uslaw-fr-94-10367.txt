SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[License #06/06-0298]

 

Southern Ventures, Inc.; Notice of License Surrender

    Notice is hereby given that Southern Ventures, Inc., (``SVI''), 605 
Main Street, Suite 202, Arkadelphia, Arkansas 71923, has surrendered 
its license to operate as a small business investment company under the 
Small Business Investment Act of 1958, as amended (``the Act''). SVI 
was licensed by the Small Business Administration on November 30, 1988.
    Under the authority vested by the Act and pursuant to the 
regulations promulgated thereunder, the surrender of the license was 
accepted on February 28, 1994, and accordingly, all rights, privileges, 
and franchises derived therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Company)

    Dated: April 21, 1994.
Robert D. Stillman,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
