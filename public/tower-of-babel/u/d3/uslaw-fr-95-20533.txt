THE COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' next meeting is scheduled for 19 
September 1995 at 10:00 AM in the Commission's offices in the Pension 
Building, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, N.W., Washington, 
D.C. 20001 to discuss various projects affecting the appearance of 
Washington, D.C., including buildings, memorials, parks, etc.; also 
matters of design referred by other agencies of the government.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral 

[[Page 43171]]
statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, D.C. 27 July 1995.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
