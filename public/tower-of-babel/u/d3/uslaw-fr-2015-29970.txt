DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Howard and Mildred Carter, Allen Rae Carter; Notice of 
Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To 
Intervene, and Protests

    On October 26, 2015, Karl Knuchel, attorney for the estate of 
Mildred Carter, (Howard Carter preceded Mildred Carter in death) 
(transferor) and Allen Rae Carter (transferee) filed an application for 
the transfer of the license of the Pine Creek Hydroelectric Project No. 
8546 to Allen Rae Carter. Mildred Carter, now deceased, was the 
surviving licensee on the project. Pursuant to Mildred Carter's will, 
her estate seeks to transfer the project to her son, Allen Rae Carter. 
The project is located on Pine Creek in Park County, Montana.
    Applicant Contact: For Applicants: Mr. Allen Rae Carter, 33 Eastep 
Lane, Livingston, MT 59047 and Mr. Karl Knuchel, P.C., 101 North E 
Street, P.O. Box 953, Livingston, MT 59047, Phone: 46-222-0135, email: 
[email protected].
    FERC Contact: Patricia W. Gillis, (202) 502-8735.

30 days from the date that the Commission issues this notice. The

at the end of your comments. For assistance, please contact FERC Online
