DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. CP01-361-000]


Northwest Pipeline Corporation; Notice of Site Visit

August 3, 2001.
    On August 13 through August 17, 2001, the Office of Energy Projects 
staff and representatives of Northwest Pipeline Corporation, will 
conduct a site visit of the proposed facilities of the Grays Harbor 
Pipeline Project 2001 in Thurston and Grays Harbor Counties, 
Washington.
    All interested parties may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact the Office of External 
Affairs at (202) 208-1088.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
