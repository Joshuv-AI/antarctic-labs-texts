Radio Broadcasting Services; Buras, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for rule making filed by 
Mountain West Broadcasting proposing the allotment of FM Channel 279C2 
to Buras, Louisiana, as that locality's first commercial FM 
transmission service. Petitioner failed to establish the availability 
of a suitable location for tower construction as the required site 
restriction located 6 kilometers south of the community at coordinates 
29-18-15 NL and 89-32-00 WL to accommodate Channel 279C2 at Buras is in 
marshland. See 64 FR 31172, June 10, 1999. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.
