DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Invention for Licensing; Government-
Owned Invention

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention listed below is assigned to the United States 
Government as represented by the Secretary of Navy and are available 
for licensing by the Department of the Navy.
    U.S. Patent Application Serial No. 09/451,718 entitled ``A Zeus++ 
Code Tool, A Method for Implementing Same and Storage Medium Storing 
Computer Readable Instructions for Instantiating the Zeus++ Tool'', 
filing date: December 1, 1999, Navy Case No. 79694.

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the patent applications cited should 
be directed to the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Dahlgren Laboratory, 
Code CD222, 17320 Dahlgren Road, Building 183, Room 015, Dahlgren, VA 
22448-5100, and must include the Navy Case number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: James B. Bechtel, Patent Counsel, 
Naval Surface Warfare Center, Dahlgren Laboratory, Code CD222, 17320 
Dahlgren Road, Building 183, Room 015, Dahlgren, VA 22448-5100, 
telephone (540) 653-8016.
