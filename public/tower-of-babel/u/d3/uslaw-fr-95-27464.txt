DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Nu Quaker Dyeing, Incorporated, Easton, Pennsylvania; Dismissal 
of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Program Manager of the Office of 
Trade Adjustment Assistance for workers at Nu Quaker Dyeing, 
Incorporated, Easton, Pennsylvania. The review indicated that the 
application contained no new substantial information which could bear 
importantly on the Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of 
the application was issued.

TA-W-31,239; NU Quaker Dyeing, Incorporated, Easton, Pennsylvania 
(October 26, 1995)

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 26th day of October, 1995.
