DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Privacy Act of 1974; Deletion of System of Records

AGENCY: Department of Veterans Affairs.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Notice is hereby given that the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) 
is deleting a system of records entitled ``PROS/KEYS User Permissions 
Database-VA'' (67VA30), which was first published FR Vol. 52, No 155 
dated August 12, 1987. The system of records known as ``PROS/KEYS User 
Permissions Database-VA'' is obsolete. The information was initially 
developed as a means to grant individuals access permissions to the 
resources of the Austin Data Processing Center. The requirement for VA 
to maintain this system of records no longer exists because the PROS/
KEYS Database was replaced by 87VA045, ``Automated Customer 
Registration System (ACRS)-VA'', which was published in FR Vol. 60, No 
239 dated December 13, 1995. 87VA045 was subsequently amended, renamed, 
renumbered and republished in its entirety as 87VA0050P, ``Customer 
User Provisioning System (CUPS)-VA'', in FR Vol. 74, No 156 dated 
August 14, 2009.
    A ``Report of Intention to Publish a Federal Register Notice of 
Deletion of a System of Records'' and an advance copy of the system 
notice have been provided to the appropriate congressional committees 
and to the Director, Office of Management and Budget (OMB), as required 
by 5 U.S.C.

[[Page 19525]]

552a(r) and guidelines issued by OMB (65 CFR 77677), dated December 12, 
2000. This system deletion is effective April 7, 2011.

    Approved: March 18, 2011.
John R. Gingrich,
Chief of Staff, Department of Veterans Affairs.
