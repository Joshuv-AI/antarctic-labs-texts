Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2024-1390.
    Petitioner: Win Win Aviation Inc.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  119.1(e)(6).
    Description of Relief Sought: Win Win Aviation Inc. requests 
relief to conduct military parachute operations in support of U.S. 
Air Force (USAF) rescue squadrons at Lake Roosevelt, Arizona at a 
distance greater than 25 miles from the planned San Carlos Apache 
Airport P-13 departure location. The petitioner asserts that use of 
San Carlos Apache Airport P-13 instead of the Grapevine Airstrip, 
which it presently uses for these operations, will enhance safety 
for its flight crews and the USAF rescue personnel.
