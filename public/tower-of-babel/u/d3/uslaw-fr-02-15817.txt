SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3413]


State of Missouri, (Amendment 3); Disaster Loan Areas

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated June 10, 2002, the above-numbered Declaration 
is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as 
beginning April 24, 2002 and continuing through June 10, 2002.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is July 7, 2002, and for loans 
for economic injury the deadline is February 10, 2003.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: June 13, 2002.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
