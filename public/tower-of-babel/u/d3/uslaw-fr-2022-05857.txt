FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE:  Thursday, March 24, 2022 at 10:00 a.m.

PLACE:  Hybrid meeting: 1050 First Street NE, Washington, DC (12th 
floor) and virtual. Note: Due to the covid-19 pandemic, the FEC's 
hearing room remains closed to visitors for the near term as we
