DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Statement of Organization, Functions, and Delegations of 
Authority

    Part N, National Institutes of Health, of the Statement of 
Organization, Functions, and Delegations of Authority for the 
Department of Health and Human Services (40 FR 22859, May 27, 1975, as 
amended most recently of 65 FR 20477, April 17, 2000, and redesignated 
from Part HN as Part N at 60 FR 56606, November 9, 1995), is amended as 
set forth below to reflect the retitling of the Office of 
Bioengineering and Bioimaging in the Office of the Director, National 
Institutes of Health, as the Office of Bioengineering, Bioimaging, and 
Bioinformatics.
    Section N-B, Organization and Functions, is amended as follows: 
Under the heading Office of Bioengineering, Bioimaging (NAC, formerly 
HNAC), replace the title with: Office of Bioengineering, Bioimaging, 
and Bioinformatics (NAC, formerly HNAC).

    Dated: May 25, 2000.
Ruth Kirschstein,
Acting Director, National Institutes of Health.
