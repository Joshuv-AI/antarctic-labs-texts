MORRIS K. UDALL SCHOLARSHIP AND EXCELLENCE IN NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL 
POLICY FOUNDATIONS


Sunshine Act Meeting

    The Board of Trustees of the Morris K. Udall Scholarship & 
Excellence in National Environmental Policy Foundation will hold a 
meeting beginning at 9:00 a.m. on Thursday, April 22, 1999 at the 
offices of the U.S. Institute for Environmental Conflict Resolution, 
110 South Church, Ste. 3350, Tucson, AZ 85701.
    The matters to be considered will include (1) A report on the U.S. 
Institute of Environmental Conflict Resolution, and (2). A report from 
the Udall Center for Studies and Public Policy and (3) Program Reports. 
The meeting is open to the public.
    Contact Person for More Information: Christopher L. Helms, 110 
South Church, Ste. 3350, Tucson, Arizona 85701. Telephone (520) 670-
5608.

    Dated this 9th day of April, 1999.
Christopher L. Helms,
Director.
