DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Office of the Secretary

 

Glass Ceiling Commission: Notice of Cancelled Closed 
Teleconference Meeting

    Due to scheduling difficulties, the October 5, 1994 closed meeting 
by teleconference has been cancelled. This meeting had been scheduled 
from 1:00 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. This meeting was previously announced in 
the Federal Register on September 26, 1994 at 59 FR 49086.
FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Rene A. Redwood, Executive 
Director, Glass Ceiling Commission, U.S. Department of Labor, 200 
Constitution Avenue, N.W., Room C-2313, Washington, D.C. 20210, (202) 
219-7342.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 4th day of October, 1994.
Rene A. Redwood,
Executive Director, Glass Ceiling Commission.
