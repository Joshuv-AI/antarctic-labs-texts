DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin 
Diseases; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, December 20, 2005, 8:30 p.m. to December 21, 2005, 5 
p.m., Double Tree Rockville, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852 
which was published in the Federal Register on December 21, 2005, FR70: 
75826.
    The meeting will be held February 8-9, 2006 from 8 a.m.-5 p.m. both 
days at the Bethesda Marriott (5151 Pooks Hill Road, Bethesda, MD 
20814). The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: February 6, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
