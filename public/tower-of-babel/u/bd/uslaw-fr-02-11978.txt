47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-1038, MM Docket No. 01-56, RM-10033]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Huntington, WV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of SJL License Subsidiary, LLC, 
licensee of station WOWK-TV, substitutes DTV channel 47 for DTV channel 
54 at Huntington, West Virginia. See 66 FR 12751, February 28, 2001. 
DTV channel 47 can be allotted to Huntington, West Virginia, in 
compliance with the principle community coverage requirements of 
Sec. 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 38-30-21 N. and 82-12-33 W. 
with a power of 895, HAAT of 396 meters and with a DTV service 
population of 1063 thousand. Since the community of Huntington is 
located within 400 kilometers of the U.S.-Canadian border, concurrence 
from the Canadian government has been obtained for this allotment.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective June 24, 2002.
