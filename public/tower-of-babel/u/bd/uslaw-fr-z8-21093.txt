Correction

    In rule document E8-21093 beginning on page 59034 in the issue of 
Wednesday, October 8, 2008, make the following corrections:


Sec.  1045.205  [Corrected]

    1. On page 59205, in the third column, in Sec.  1045.205(q), in the 
fifth line, ``CO2'' should read ``CO2''.


Sec.  1045.315  [Corrected]

    2. On page 59212, in the second column, in Sec.  1045.315(b), the 
equation should read as follows:

``Ci = Max [0 or Ci-1 + Xi - (STD + 
0.25 x [sigma])]''
    3. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec.  1045.315(f), in 
the fourth line, ``5.0 x [sigma]'' should read ``5.0 x [sigma]''.


Sec.  1054.112  [Corrected]

    4. On page 59264, in the first column, in Sec.  1054.112(b)(2), in 
the first line, ``m2 day'' should read ``m\2\/day''.


Sec.  1065.370  [Corrected]

    5. On page 59329, in the first column, in Sec.  1065.370(c), in the 
third line, `` 3% or less'' should read `` 2% 
or less''.
