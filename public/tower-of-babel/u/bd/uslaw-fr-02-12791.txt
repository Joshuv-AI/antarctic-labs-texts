Pacific Trust Bank, Chula Vista, CA; Approval of Conversion 
Application

    Notice is hereby given that on, May 15, 2002, the Director, 
Examination Policy, Office of Thrift Supervision

[[Page 36066]]

(``OTS''), or her designee, acting pursuant to delegated authority, 
approved the application of Pacific Trust Bank, Chula Vista, 
California, to convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of the 
application are available for inspection by appointment (phone number: 
202-906-5922 or e-mail: [email protected]) at the Public 
Reading Room, OTS, 1700 G Street, NW, Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS 
West Regional Office, Pacific Plaza, 2001 Junipero Serra Boulevard, 
Suite 650, Daly City, California 94014-1976.

    Dated: May 17, 2002.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision,
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
