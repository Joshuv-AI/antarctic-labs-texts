J. R. Simplot Company, Don Plant, Pocatello, Idaho; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 20, 2002 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by Paper, Allied-Industrial, Chemical and Energy Workers 
International Union, Local 8-632 on behalf of workers at J. R. Simplot 
Company, Don Plant, Pocatello, Idaho.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 31st day of May, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
