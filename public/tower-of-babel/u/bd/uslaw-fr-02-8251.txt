Synopsis

    1. On August 17, 2001, the Commission released the Flexibility 
Notice of Proposed Rulemaking, 66 FR 47621 (Sept. 13, 2001), on 
proposals to bring flexibility to the delivery of communications by 
mobile satellite service (MSS) providers. One alternative proposal 
under consideration would open portions of the MSS bands for any 
operator to provide a terrestrial service that could either be offered 
in conjunction with MSS or as an alternative mobile service. Additional 
technical comment on this alternative proposal will assist the 
Commission in reaching a decision in this proceeding.
    2. For this reason, the Commission additional technical comment on 
issues pertaining to the alternative proposal for MSS operations.
