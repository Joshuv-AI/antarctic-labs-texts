Notice

    In accordance with Section 309(a) of the Clean Air Act, EPA is 
required to make its comments on EISs issued by other Federal agencies 
public. Historically, EPA met this mandate by

[[Page 22700]]

publishing weekly notices of availability of EPA comments, which 
includes a brief summary of EPA's comment letters, in the Federal 
Register. Since February 2008, EPA has included its comment letters on 
EISs on its Web site at: http://www.epa.gov/compliance/nepa/eisdata.html. Including the entire EIS comment letters on the Web site 
satisfies the Section 309(a) requirement to make EPA's comments on EISs 
available to the public. Accordingly, on March 31, 2010, EPA 
discontinued the publication of the notice of availability of EPA 
comments in the Federal Register.
