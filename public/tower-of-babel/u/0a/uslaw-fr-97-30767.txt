Hearing of the Judicial Conference Advisory Committee on Rules of 
Criminal Procedure

AGENCY: Judicial Conference of the United States, Advisory Committee on 
Rules of Criminal Procedure.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of open hearing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Criminal Rules public hearing scheduled to be held in New 
Orleans, Louisiana, on December 12, 1997, has been canceled. (Original 
notice of hearing appeared in the Federal Register of August 25, 1997.)
