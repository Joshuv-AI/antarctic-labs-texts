NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
[Notice 96-007]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Laser Technology, Inc. of 
Norristown, Pennsylvania, has applied for an exclusive license to 
practice the invention described and claim in a pending U.S. Patent 
Application entitled ``Apparatus and Method for High Speed 
Characterization of Surfaces,'' which is assigned to the United States 
of America as represented by the Administrator of the National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections to the 
prospective grant of the license to Laser Technology should be sent to 
Beth Vrioni, John F. Kennedy Space Center, Mail Code DE-TPO, Kennedy 
Space Center, FL 32899.

DATES: Responses to this Notice must be received within 60 days from 
date of publication in the Federal Register.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Beth Vrioni at (407) 867-2780.

    Dated: January 22, 1996.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
