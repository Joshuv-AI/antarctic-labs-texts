NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION
 

Correction to Notice of Consideration of Issuance of Amendment to 
Facility Operating License, Proposed No Significant Hazards; 
Consideration Determination, and Opportunity for a Hearing

    In the Notice published Friday, May 13, 1994, on page 25133, column 
one, last paragraph, ``amendment dated May 5, 1994'' should be changed 
to read ``amendment dated December 6, 1993, supplemented by letter 
dated May 6, 1994.''

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Theodore R. Quay,
Director, Project Directorate IV-2, Division of Reactor Projects III/
IV, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
