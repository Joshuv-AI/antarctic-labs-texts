ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-10013-89-OP]


National Environmental Justice Advisory Council; Notice of 
Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the Environmental Protection 
Agency (EPA) has determined that, in accordance with the provisions of 
the Federal Advisory Committee Act (FACA), the National Environmental 
Justice Advisory Council (NEJAC) is necessary and in the public 
interest in connection with the performance of duties imposed on the 
agency by law. Accordingly, NEJAC will be renewed for an additional 
two-year period. The purpose of the NEJAC is to provide independent 
advice and recommendations to the Administrator about issues associated 
with integrating environmental justice concerns into EPA's outreach 
activities, public policies, science, regulatory, enforcement, and 
compliance decisions.
