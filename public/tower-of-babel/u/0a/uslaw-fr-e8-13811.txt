Obtaining Guidance on Regulatory Requirements; Notice of 
Compliance Help Desk Implementation

June 12, 2008.
    On May 15, 2008, the Commission issued an order in this proceeding 
directing the implementation of a compliance help desk on the 
Commission's Web site to provide a mechanism to submit questions 
regarding compliance with the statutes, rules, regulations and tariffs 
administered by the Commission. The compliance help desk is operational 
and can be found at http://www.ferc.gov/contact-us/compliance-help-desk.asp. A link to the compliance help desk can also be found on the 
home page of the Commission's Web site, http://www.ferc.gov.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
