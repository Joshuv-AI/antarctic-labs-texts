[TA-W-56,056]


Set Enterprises, Warren, MI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 22, 2004 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at SET 
Enterprises, Warren, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 3rd day of December 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
