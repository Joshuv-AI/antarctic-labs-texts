Fees; Correction

AGENCY: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department of the Interior.

ACTION: Proposed rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the preamble to a proposed rule 
published in the Federal Register of December 2, 2021, regarding Fees. 
The document contained incorrect dates for submitting comments. This 
correction clarifies that comments are due January 31, 2022.

[[Page 2384]]


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael Hoenig, 202-632-7003.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of December 2, 2021, in proposed rule FR 
Doc. 2021-25838, on page 68445, in the first column, change the Dates 
caption to read:
    DATES: Written comments on this proposed rule must be received on 
or before January 31, 2022.

    Dated: January 6, 2022.

General Counsel.
