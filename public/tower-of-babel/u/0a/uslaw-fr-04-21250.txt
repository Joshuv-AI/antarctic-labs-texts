NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Time and Date: 10 a.m., Thursday, September 23, 2004.

Place: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047, 1775 Duke Street, Alexandria, 
VA 22314-3428.

Status: Open.

Matters To Be Considered:
    (1) Request from a Federal Credit Union to Expand its Community 
Charter.
    (2) Requests form two (2) Federal Credit Unions to Convert to 
Community Charters.
    (3) Notice and Request for Comment--Federal Credit Union Bylaws.
    (4) Final Rule: Section 701.36 of NCUA's Rules and Regulations, 
Federal Credit Union Ownership of Fixed Assets.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mary Rupp, Secretary of the Board, 
Telephone: (703) 518-6304
