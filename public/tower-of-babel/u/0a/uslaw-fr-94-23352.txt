Designation of Areas for Air Quality Planning Purposes; State of 
Pennsylvania; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Final rule; amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 21, 1993 (58 FR 67334), EPA took final action to 
redesignate areas as nonattainment for the PM-10 (particles with an 
aerodynamic diameter less than or equal to a nominal 10 micrometers) 
and sulfur dioxide (SO2) national ambient air quality standards 
(NAAQS). This notice corrects the entry for ``Warren County'' in the 
section 107 attainment designations table for ``Pennsylvania--
SO2'' displayed at 58 FR 67345. ``Mead Twp'' shall be classified 
as ``Cannot be classified'' only and not ``Does not meet secondary 
standards''. ``Clarendon Boro'' shall be classified as ``Cannot be 
classified''. ``Warren Boro'' shall be classified as ``Does not meet 
secondary standards'' in addition to the classification of ``Does not 
meet primary standards''. The remainder of the ``Warren County'' entry 
is complete and accurate.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 20, 1994.
