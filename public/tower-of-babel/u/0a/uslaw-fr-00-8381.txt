DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


RTCA, Inc.; Free Flight Steering Committee

Revised Agenda

    The April 13 RTCA Free Flight Steering Committee Meeting announced 
in the Federal Register, 65 FR 16240 (Monday, March 27, 2000), third 
column, has been revised.
    The revised agenda reads as follows: The agenda will include: (1) 
Welcome and Opening Remarks: (a) Recognize Departing Members of the 
Steering Committee; (b) Welcome Incoming Members. (2) Review Summary of 
the Previous Meeting; (3) Reports from FAA on: (c) Free Flight Phase 1 
Baseline Data and Performance Assessments Update; (d) Controller-Pilot 
Data Link

[[Page 17939]]

Communications (CPDLC) Update; (4) Satellite Navigation Users Group 
Report; (5) Report and Recommendations from the Free Flight Select 
Committee; (6) Other Business; (7) Date and Location of Next Meeting; 
(8) Closing Remarks.
    Person wishing to obtain further information should contact RTCA at 
(202) 833-9339 (phone), (202) 833-9434 (fax), or [email protected] (e-
mail).

    Issued in Washington, DC, on March 30, 2000.
Janice L. Peters,
Designated Official.
