DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Office of the Secretary


San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area Advisory 
Committee notice of renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice is published in accordance with Section 9(a)(2) of 
the Federal Advisory Committee Act of 1972 (Public Law 92-463). Notice 
is hereby given that the Secretary of the Interior has renewed the 
Bureau of Land Management's (BLM) San Pedro Riparian National 
Conservation Area Advisory Committee.
    The purpose of the Committee is to provide advice to the Secretary 
of the Interior, through the Director, BLM with respect to the 
preparation and implementation of the comprehensive plan for the long-
range management and protection of the San Pedro Riparian National 
Conservation Area.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Melanie Wilson, Intergovernmental 
Affairs, (640), Bureau of Land Management, 1620 L Street, 4th Floor, 
Washington, D.C. 20240, telephone (202) 452-0377.
