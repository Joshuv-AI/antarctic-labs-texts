SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Small Business Development Center; Advisory Board Public 
Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration National Small Business 
Development Center Advisory Board will hold a public meeting on Sunday, 
January 31, 1999, from 12:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the Adam's Mark Hotel 
in Columbia, South Carolina to discuss such matters as may be presented 
by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present.
    For further information, please write or call Ellen Thrasher, U.S. 
Small Business Administration, 409 Third Street, SW., Fourth Floor, 
Washington, DC 20416, telephone number (202) 205-6817.

    Dated: January 12, 1999.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
