Delegation to States

CFR Correction

    In Title 30 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 700 to End, 
revised as of July 1, 2012, in Sec.  1227.110, on page 907, in the last 
sentence in paragraph (a), the phrase ``www.ONRR.gov'' is corrected to 
read ``www.boemre.gov'' and on page 908, in paragraph (e), the phrase 
``ONRR Associate Director for Minerals Revenue Management'' is 
corrected to read ``Director for Office of Natural Resources Revenue''.
