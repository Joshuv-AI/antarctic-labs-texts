DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
[I.D. 072597B]


Advisory Panel on Highly Migratory Species Fisheries

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of intent; request for nominations; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NMFS is correcting a notice that appeared in the Federal 
Register of Thursday, August 28, 1997 (62 FR 45629) in column one. The 
original notice was published with an incorrect date for receipt of 
nominations. The date for receipt of nominations should be September 
12, 1997. Nominations may be sent to Rebecca Lent, Chief, Highly 
Migratory Species Management Division, 1315 East-West Highway, Silver 
Spring, MD 20910. Nominations may be faxed to (301) 713-1917.


[[Page 46721]]


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Liz Lauck (301) 713-2347.
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. 072597B, appearing on page 45629 
in the Federal Register of Thursday, August 28, 1997, the following 
correction is made:
    1. On page 45629, in the first column, ``Nominations must be 
submitted by September 29, 1997'' is changed to ``Nominations must be 
submitted by September 12, 1997.
    Dated: August 28, 1997.
Gary C. Matlock,
Director, Office of Sustainable Fisheries, National Marine Fisheries 
Service.
