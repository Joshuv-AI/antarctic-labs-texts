SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Revocation of License; Small Business Investment Company

    Pursuant to the authority granted to the United States Small 
Business Administration by the Order of the United States District 
Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, dated June 12, 1986, the 
United States Small Business Administration hereby revokes the license 
of Bartlesville Investment Corporation, a Oklahoma Corporation, to 
function as a small business investment company under the Small 
Business Investment Company License No. 06/10-0139 issued to 
Bartlesville Investment Corporation on February 28, 1964 and said 
license is hereby declared null and void as of February 26,1996.

United States Small Business Administration.

    Dated: June 20, 1996.
Don A. Christensen,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
