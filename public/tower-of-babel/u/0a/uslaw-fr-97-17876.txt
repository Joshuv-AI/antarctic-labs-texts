47 CFR Part 73

Radio Broadcasting Services; Cooperstown, PA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of John Anthony Bulmer, allots 
Channel 299A at Cooperstown, Pennsylvania, as the community's first 
local aural transmission service. See 62 FR 6926, February 14, 1997. 
Channel 299A can be allotted at Cooperstown in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at city reference 
coordinates. The coordinates for Channel 299A at Cooperstown are North 
Latitude 41-29-55 and West Longitude 79-52-14. Since Cooperstown is 
located within 320 kilometers (200 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border, 
concurrence of the Canadian government has been obtained. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective August 11, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 299A at Cooperstown, Pennsylvania, will open 
on August 11, 1997, and close on September 11, 1997.
