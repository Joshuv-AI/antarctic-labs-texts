Winooski One Partnership City of Burlington, Vermont; Notice of 
Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments and Motions 
To Intervene

    On June 20, 2014, Winooski One Partnership (transferor) and the 
City of Burlington, Vermont (transferee) filed an application for 
transfer of license of the Chace Mill Project located on the Winooski 
River in Chittenden County, Vermont.
    The transferor and transferee seek Commission approval to transfer 
the license for the Chace Mill Project from the transferor to the 
transferee.
