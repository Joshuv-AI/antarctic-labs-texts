FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 00-2238; MM Docket No. 99-278; RM-9424]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Susquehanna, PA and Conklin, NY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the joint request of Majac of Michigan, 
Inc., and Equinox Broadcasting Corporation, reallots Channel 223A from 
Susquehanna, Pennsylvania, to Conklin, New York, and modifies Station 
WKGB-FM's license accordingly. We also reallot Channel 263A from 
Conklin, New York, to Susquehanna, Pennsylvania, and modify Station 
WCDW(FM)'s license accordingly. See 64 FR 51284, September 22, 1999. 
Channel 223A can be reallotted to Conklin in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at Station WKGB-
FM's requested site. The coordinates for Channel 223A at Conklin are 
42-06-53 North Latitude and 75-51-16 West Longitude. Additionally, 
Channel 263A can be reallotted to Susquehanna in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at Station 
WCDW(FM)'s requested site. The coordinates for Channel 263A at 
Susquehanna are 42-02-30 North Latitude and 75-41-30 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective November 13, 2000.
