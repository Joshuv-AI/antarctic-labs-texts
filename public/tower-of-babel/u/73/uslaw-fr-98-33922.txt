DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Program Support Center; Statement of Organizations, Functions, 
and Delegation of Authority

    Part P, Program Support Center (PSC), of the Statement of 
Organization, Functions, and Delegations of Authority for the 
Department of Health and Human Services, Chapter P as last amended at 
60 FR 51480, dated October 2, 1995 is being revised. The change is to 
place within the Program Support Center, the Freedom of Information Act 
function associated with the Public Health Service. The change is as 
follows:
    Under Chapter PA, Office of the Director, include the following 
statement:

[[Page 71144]]

    1. Provides responses to requests for records for the Public Health 
Service (PHS) or records involving more than one PHS component, 
including records relating to the PHS components located in the 
regions.

    Dated: December 17, 1998.
Donna E. Shalala,
Secretary.
