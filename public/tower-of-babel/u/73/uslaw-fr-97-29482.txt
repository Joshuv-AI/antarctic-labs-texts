DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Health Resources and Services Administration


Advisory Council; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
National Advisory body are scheduled to meet during the month of 
December 1997:
    Name: HRSA Aids Advisory Committee.
    Time: December 2-3, 1997 9:00 a.m.
    Place: Loews L'Enfant Hotel, 80 L'Enfant Plaza, S.W., Washington, 
D.C. 20024.
    The meeting is open to the public.
    Agenda: The topics to be discussed include the reorganization of 
the Ryan White CARE Act programs; Clinical Guidelines; and Access to 
Combination Therapies and Adherence Issues.
    Anyone requiring information regarding the subject Committee should 
contact Joan Holloway, HIV/AIDS Bureau, Health Resources and Services 
Administration, Room 7-13, Parklawn Building, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, Maryland 20857, Telephone (301) 443-9530.
    Agenda Items are subject to change as priorities dictate.

    Dated: November 3, 1997.
Jane M. Harrison,
Advisory Committee Management Office, HRSA.
