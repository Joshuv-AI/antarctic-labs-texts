Date Change for Special Local Regulation (SLR), Seattle National 
Maritime Week Tugboat Race

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of change in implementation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard announces a change to the effective date for 
the Seattle National Maritime Week Tugboat Race Special Local 
Regulation (SLR) as per 33 CFR 100.1306(c). This year's event will be 
held on Saturday, May 12th, 2001, necessitating this effective date 
change.

DATES: 33 CFR 100.1306 is effective May 12, 2001, from 12 p.m. to 4:30 
p.m.

    Dated: May 3, 2001.
M.D. Dawe,
Commander, U.S. Coast Guard, Commander, Group Seattle.
