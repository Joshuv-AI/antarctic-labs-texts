DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,662]


Leed's; Warren, OH; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 23, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed by workers of Leed's, Warren, Ohio.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of May 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
