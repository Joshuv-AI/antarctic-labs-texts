DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Export Trade Certificate of Review

ACTION: Correction for Notice of Application.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    ``In the Notice of Application for a Certificate of Review for 
United Products of America, Inc., Application No. 95-00004, which was 
printed in the Federal Register on June 7, 1995, page 30064, No. 109, 
Vol 60, the - Date Deemed Submitted was incorrectly listed as April 30, 
1995. In fact, Application No. 95-00004 was deemed submitted on May 30, 
1995, not April 30.''

    Dated: June 30, 1995.
W. Dawn Busby,
Director, Office of Export Trading Company Affairs.
