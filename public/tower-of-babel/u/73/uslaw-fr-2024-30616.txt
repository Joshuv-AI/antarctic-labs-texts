United States Mint


Establish Prices for 2025 United States Mint Numismatic Products

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing new pricing for United 
States Mint numismatic clad products in accordance with the table in 
the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION section.

DATES: The new numismatic product prices will be effective January 2, 
2025.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Derrick Griffin; Branch Chief, Product 
Management; United States Mint, 801 9th Street NW, Washington, DC 
20220; or call 202-354-7579.
