previously approved project by the Susquehanna River Basin Commission 
during the period set forth in DATES.

DATES: June 1-30, 2018.

ADDRESSES: Susquehanna River Basin Commission, 4423 North Front Street, 
Harrisburg, PA 17110-1788.

Township, Northumberland County, Pa.; approval to add Wells H1, H2, and

June 20, 2018.
    2. Furman Foods, Inc., dba Furmano's, Docket No. 20120621-1, Point 
Township, Northumberland County, Pa.; approval to changes in the 
authorized water uses; Approval Date: June 20, 2018.
