COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Rhode Island Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Rhode Island Advisory 
Committee. The meeting scheduled for Wednesday, March 10, 2021 at 12:00 
p.m. (ET) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of 
Thursday, January 21, 2021, in FR Doc. 2021-01151, on pages 6293-6294.
