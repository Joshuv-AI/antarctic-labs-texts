SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The Naval Petroleum Reserves Production Act of 1976 restricts the 
export of any petroleum product produced from crude oil derived from 
National Petroleum Reserves. Under very limited circumstances, 
petroleum products can be exported if an export licenses is obtained. 
This information collection requires the submission of documents to 
support export license applications, or the retention of documents for 
shipments made under applicable License Exceptions of petroleum 
products derived from a naval petroleum reserve.

II. Method of collection

    Submission with BXA form BXA-748P and record retention.
