Grants for Dissertation Awards for Doctoral Candidates for 
Violence-Related Injury Prevention Research in Minority Communities; 
Notice of Availability of Funds; Amendment

    A notice announcing the availability of fiscal year (FY) 2003 funds 
for a grant program for Dissertation Awards for Doctoral Candidates for 
Violence-Related Injury Prevention Research in Minority Communities 
published in the Federal Register on February 7, 2003, Volume 68, 
Number 26, pages 6475-6479. The notice is amended as follows: On page 
6476, first column, Section D. Funds, sub-section Use of Funds, at the 
end of the paragraph, add the following sentence: Indirect costs for 
these trainee-related activities are limited to eight percent.
    On page 6477, second column, Section G. Submission, Date, Time and 
Address, the first sentence should read: The application must be 
received by 4 p.m. Eastern Time April 8, 2003.

    Dated: February 13, 2003.
Sandra R. Manning,
Director, Procurement and Grants Office, Centers for Disease Control 
and Prevention.
