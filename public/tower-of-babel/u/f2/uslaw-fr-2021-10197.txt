CIVIL RIGHTS COMMISSION


Notice of Public Meeting of the Minnesota Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Minnesota Advisory 
Committee. The meeting scheduled for Thursday, May 13, 2021 at 12:00 
p.m. (CST) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of 
Tuesday, May 4, 2021, in FR Doc. 2021-09269, on page 23671.
