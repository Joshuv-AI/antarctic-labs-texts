FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 93-245; RM-8316]

Radio Broadcasting Services; Hayneville, AL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a petition filed by R. J. Miller, 
requesting the allotment of FM Channel 300A to Hayneville, Alabama, as 
that community's first local aural transmission service, based upon the 
petitioner's withdrawal of interest, and the absence of any other 
acceptable expression in pursuing the allotment request in response to 
the Notice. See 58 FR 50313, September 27, 1993. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
