PI, Inc., Custom Molding Division, Athens, TN; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 9, 2007 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of PI, Inc., 
Custom Molding Division, Athens, Tennessee.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated. Further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of November 2007.
 Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
