SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3629]


State of Georgia (Amendment 2)

    In accordance with notices received from the Department of Homeland 
Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency--effective September 24 
and 29, and October 14, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby 
amended to include Clayton and Forsyth Counties as disaster areas due 
to damages caused by Hurricane Ivan occurring on September 14, 2004, 
and continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous county of Spalding in the State of 
Georgia may be filed until the specified date at the previously 
designated location. All other counties contiguous to the above named 
primary counties have previously been declared.
    In addition, Banks, Carroll, Cherokee, Dade, Dawson, DeKalb, Early, 
Elbert, Fannin, Forsyth, Franklin, Fulton, Gilmer, Habersham, Harris, 
Heard, Lumpkin, Madison, Miller, Pickens, Pike, Rabun, Towns, Union, 
Upson, White, and Wilkes Counties in the State of Georgia are also 
eligible under Public Assistance and our disaster loan

[[Page 62496]]

program is available for private non-profit organizations that provide 
essential services of a governmental nature in those counties.
    The Public Assistance number assigned to Georgia is P07208.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 17, 2004, and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 20, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: October 19, 2004.
Cheri L. Cannon,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
