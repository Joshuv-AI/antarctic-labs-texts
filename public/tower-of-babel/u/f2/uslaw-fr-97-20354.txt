FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting

Time and Date: 10:00 a.m. (EDT) August 11, 1997.

Place: 4th Floor, Conference Room 4506, 1250 H Street, NW., Washington 
DC.

Status: Open.

Matters to be Considered:
    1. Approval of the minutes of the July 14, 1997, Board member 
meeting.
    2. Thrift Savings Plan activity report by the Executive Director.
    3. Review of investment policy.
    4. Review of Arthur Andersen semiannual financial review.
    5. Review of KPMG Peat Marwick audit report: ``Pension and Welfare 
Benefits Administration Review of the Thrift Savings Plan Withdrawal 
and Inactive Accounts Operations at the United States Department of 
Agriculture, National Finance Center.''

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Thomas J. Trabucco, Director, 
Office of External Affairs, (202) 942-1640.
Roger W. Mehle,
Executive Director, Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.
