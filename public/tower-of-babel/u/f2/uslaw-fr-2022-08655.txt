Copyright Claims Board: Initiating of Proceedings and Related 
Procedures; Correction

AGENCY: U.S. Copyright Office, Library of Congress.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Copyright Office is issuing a correction governing 
the fee to designate a service agent under the Copyright Alternative in 
Small-Claims Enforcement Act of 2020. The correction reverses an 
inadvertent reservation instruction.

DATES: Effective April 25, 2022.

General Counsel, by email at [email protected], or by telephone at 
202-707-8350.
