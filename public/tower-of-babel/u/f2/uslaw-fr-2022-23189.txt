SUMMARY: The Department of Housing and Urban Development published a 
document in the Federal Register of October 19, 2022, an information 
collection notice concerning the safety standards program for mobile 
home construction. The document contained an incorrect comment closing 
date.

available documents submitted to OMB may be obtained from Ms. Pollard.
