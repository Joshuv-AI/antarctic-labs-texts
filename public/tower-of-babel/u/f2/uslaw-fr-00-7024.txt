Portland General Electric Company and the Confederated Tribes of 
the Warm Springs Reservation of Oregon; Notice of Meeting

March 16, 2000.
    At the request of Portland General Electric Company and the 
Confederated Tribes of the Warm Springs Reservation of Oregon, the 
Commission's staff will hold a meeting on April 4, 2000, at 3:00 p.m. 
in room 62-26, 888 First Street NE, Washington, DC. The purpose of the 
meeting is to discuss issues arising from the pending Global Settlement 
between PGE and the Tribes to resolve the competitive proceeding.
    Other interested parties wishing to attend and participate in the 
meeting are welcome. Questions on the meeting should be directed to 
Hector M. Perez on
