NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice: (21-061)]


Planetary Science Advisory Committee; Meeting

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) 
announces a meeting of the Planetary Science Advisory Committee. The 
meeting will be held for the purpose of soliciting, from the scientific 
community and other persons, scientific and technical information 
relevant to program planning.

DATES: Monday, October 18, 2021, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Eastern Time; 
and Tuesday, October 19, 2021, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Eastern Time
