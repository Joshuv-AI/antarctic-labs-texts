SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Agenda

Thursday, March 27, 2025

    The Enforcement Committee will review the Council's Small sablefish 
release analysis, the Maximum Retainable Amount (MRA) adjustments 
analysis, and other business including the Enforcement Committee Terms 
of Reference. The Small sablefish release analysis evaluates a proposed 
management measure to allow sablefish IFQ/CDQ harvesters using fixed 
gear in the BSAI and GOA to carefully release small sablefish. The MRA 
adjustment analysis evaluates proposed management measures to revise 
the MRA regulations to clarify and/or revise (1) the definition of a 
fishing trip, (2) calculations for MRAs, and (3) applications of MRAs.
    The agenda is subject to change, and the latest version will be 
posted at https://meetings.npfmc.org/Meeting/Details/3085 prior to the 
meeting, along with meeting materials.
