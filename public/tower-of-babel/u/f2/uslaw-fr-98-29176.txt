CENSUS MONITORING BOARD


Notice of Public Meeting

SUMMARY: This notice, in compliance with PL 105-119, sets forth the 
meeting date, time and place for the third business meeting of the full 
Census Monitoring Board. The meeting agenda will include an examination 
of ongoing preparations by the Census Bureau for the 2000 Decennial 
Census.

DATES: The meeting will take place at 10:00 AM, Friday, November 6, 
1998.

LOCATION: The meeting will be held in Room 2203, Rayburn House Office 
Building, Washington, DC 20515.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Contact Michael Miguel, Census Monitoring Board. Phone: 301-457-5080.
Fred T. Asbell,
Executive Director, Congressional Members.
Mark Johnson,
Executive Director, Presidential Members.
