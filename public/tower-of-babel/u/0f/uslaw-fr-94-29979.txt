DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 2125]

 

Delegation of Authority 215; Designation and Delegation of 
Authority Concerning the Department's Ethics Program

    Pursuant to the regulations of the Office of Government Ethics, 5 
C.F.R. 2638.202, and under the authority delegated to me under 
Delegation of Authority No. 198 dated September 16, 1992, I hereby 
designate William M. McQuade, senior Attorney Adviser responsible for 
ethics, as the Alternate Designated Agency Ethics Official for the 
Department of State. I delegate to him the duties referred to in 
Section 2638.203, to be carried out in conjunction with the Designated 
Agency Ethics Official of the Department.

    Dated: November 9, 1994.
Richard M. Moose,
Under Secretary for Management.
