DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Postponement of the Defense Finance and Accounting Service Board 
of Advisors

AGENCY: Department of Defense, Office of the Secretary of Defense 
(Comptroller).

ACTION: Notice of postponement of June 27, 2001 meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Monday, June 18, 2001 (66 FR 32795), the Department of 
Defense published a notice of meeting of the Defense Finance and 
Accounting Service (DFAS) Board of Advisors scheduled for Wednesday, 
June 27, 2001. The meeting has been postponed and will be rescheduled 
and announced at a later date.

    Dated: June 25, 2001.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
