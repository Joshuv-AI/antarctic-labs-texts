Carolina Furniture Manufacturers Inc., Ramseur, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 9, 2008 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Carolina Furniture 
Manufacturers Inc., Ramseur, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 17th day of July 2008.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
