Self-Regulatory Organizations; Chicago Stock Exchange, Inc.; 
Notice of Withdrawal of a Proposed Rule Change To Adopt the Route QCT 
Cross Routing Option

August 2, 2018.
    On March 6, 2018, the Chicago Stock Exchange, Inc. (``Exchange'') 
filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), 
pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 
(``Exchange Act'') \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule 
change to adopt the Route QCT Cross routing option. The proposed rule 
change was published for comment in the Federal Register on March 20, 
2018.\3\ On May 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 19(b)(2) of the Exchange 
Act,\4\ the Commission designated a longer period within which to 
approve the proposed rule change, disapprove the proposed rule change, 
or institute proceedings to determine whether to disapprove the 
proposed rule change.\5\ On June 13, 2018, the Commission instituted 
proceedings under Section 19(b)(2)(B) of the Exchange Act \6\ to 
determine whether to approve or disapprove the proposed rule change.\7\ 
The Commission received one comment letter on the proposed rule 
change.\8\
