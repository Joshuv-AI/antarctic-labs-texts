DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Trunkline Gas Company; Notice of Informal Technical Conference

May 28, 1999.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) will convene 
an informal staff technical conference on June 28, 1999, at 2:00 p.m., 
in Room 3M3, of the Commission's offices at 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C., to discuss Trunkline's answers to staff's data 
requests in the above-captioned proceeding. The conference is open to 
all interested persons.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
