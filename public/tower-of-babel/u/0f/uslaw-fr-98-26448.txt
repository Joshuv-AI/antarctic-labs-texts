DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Gettysburg National Military Park Advisory Commission

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice sets forth the date of the twenty-seventh meeting 
of the Gettysburg National Military Park Advisory Commission.

DATE: The public meeting will be held on October 15, 1998, from 7:00 
p.m. to 9:00 p.m.

LOCATION: The meeting will be held at the Cyclorama Auditorium, 125 
Taneytown Road, Gettysburg, Pennsylvania 17325.

AGENDA: Sub-committee Reports, General Management Plan, Federal 
Consistency Projects Within the Gettysburg Battlefield Historic 
District, Operational Update on Park Activities, and Citizens Open 
Forum.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
John A. Latschar, Superintendent, Gettysburg National Military Park, 97 
Taneytown Road, Gettysburg, Pennsylvania 17325.

    Date: September 21, 1998.
Dr. John A. Latschar,
Superintendent.
