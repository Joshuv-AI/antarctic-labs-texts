DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Notice of Intent To Rule on Request To Release Airport Property 
at Eufaula Municipal Aiport, Eufaula, Oklahoma, Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA published a document in the Federal Register of 
December 5, 2014, concerning request for comments on the release of 
land at Eufaula Municipal Airport under the provisions of Section 125 
of the Wendell H. Ford Aviation Investment Reform Act for the 21st 
Century (AIR 21). The document contained the incorrect state.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathy Franklin, 817-222-5634

Correction

    In the Federal Register of December 5, 2014, in FR Doc. 2014-28562, 
on page 1, in the Notice title, correct the Notice title to read: 
Notice of Intent to Rule on Request to Release Airport Property at 
Eufaula Municipal Airport, EUFAULA, OKLAHOMA.

    Dated: January 8, 2015,
Edward N. Agnew,
Acting Manager, Airports Division, Southwest Region.
