POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--Inbound PRIME Tracked Service 
Agreement

AGENCY: Postal ServiceTM.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing a request with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add Inbound Market Dominant PRIME 
Tracked Service Agreement to the Market Dominant Product List.

DATES: Effective date: January 3, 2017.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher C. Meyerson, (202) 268-
7820.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice that, pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642, on December 23, 
2016, it filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission a Request of 
United States Postal Service to add Inbound Market Dominant PRIME 
Tracked Service Agreement to the Market Dominant Product List. 
Documents are available at www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. MC2017-71 and 
R2017-3.

Stanley F. Mires,
Attorney, Federal Compliance.
