NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

Correction to Director's Decision Under 10 CFR 2.206

    On June 17, 1996 (61 FR 30643), notice of issuance of Director's 
Decision DD-96-06 under 10 CFR 2.206 was published, concerning Indian 
Point Nuclear Generating Units 2 and 3. However, reference to one of 
the licensees, the Power Authority of the State of New York, was 
inadvertently omitted from the heading on page 30643.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 8th day of July 1996.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
William T. Russell,
Director, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
