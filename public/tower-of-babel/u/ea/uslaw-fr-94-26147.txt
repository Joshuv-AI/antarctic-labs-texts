DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
 

Notice of Approval of Applicant as Trustee

    Notice is hereby given that First Trust of New York, National 
Association, with offices at 100 Wall Street, New York, New York 10005, 
has been approved as Trustee pursuant to Public Law 100-710 and 46 CFR 
part 221.

    Dated: October 17, 1994.

    By Order of the Maritime Administrator.
Joel C. Richard,
Acting Secretary.
