Washington Hydro Energy Development Corporation, Skookum Hydro 
Inc.: Notice of Surrender of Preliminary Permits

requested to surrender their preliminary permits because of the status 
of current economic conditions.

issuance of this notice unless that day is Saturday, Sunday, or holiday

applications involving these project sites, to the extent provided for 
under 18 CFR part 4, may be filed on the next business day.
