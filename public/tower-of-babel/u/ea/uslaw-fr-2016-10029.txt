DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Frederick 
National Laboratory Advisory Committee to the National Cancer 
Institute, May 11, 2016, 08:00 a.m. to May 11, 2016, 05:00 p.m., 
National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 
TE406, Rockville, MD, 20850 which was published in the Federal Register 
on April 4, 2016, 81 FR 19210.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the start and end time from 
8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. to 8:30 a.m.-5:15 p.m. The meeting is open to the 
public.

    Dated: April 26, 2016.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
