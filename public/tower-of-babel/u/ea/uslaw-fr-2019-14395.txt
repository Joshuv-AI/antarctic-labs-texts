COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: 1:00 p.m., Thursday, July 11, 2019.

PLACE: Three Lafayette Centre, 1155 21st Street NW, Washington, DC, 9th 
Floor Commission Conference Room.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The following matters will be considered at 
this meeting:
     Enforcement matters; and
     Exempt swap execution facility (SEF) determination.
    In the event that the time, date, or location of this meeting 
changes, an announcement of the change, along with the new time, date, 
and/or place of the meeting will be posted on the Commission's website 
at https://www.cftc.gov.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Christopher Kirkpatrick, 202-418-
5964.

    Authority: 5 U.S.C. 552(b).

    Dated: July 1, 2019.
Christopher Kirkpatrick,
Secretary of the Commission.
