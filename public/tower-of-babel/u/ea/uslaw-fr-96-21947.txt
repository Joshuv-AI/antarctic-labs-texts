ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY
[FRL-5602-2]


Revised Hours for Public Access to the Headquarters Library and 
INFOTERRA

AGENCY: U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that beginning September 3, 1996, the 
Headquarters Library and INFOTERRA will be open to the public from 8:00 
a.m. to 5 p.m. This constitutes an expansion in hours.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Irvin Weiss, Library Manager at 202-260-9388 or Emma McNamara, 
INFOTERRA Manager at 202-260-1522.

    Dated: August 23, 1996.
Irvin Weiss,
Library Manager.
