OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT

 

Request for Reclearance of Form RI 92-22

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (title 
44, U.S. Code, chapter 35), this notice announces a request for 
reclearance of an information collection. Form RI 92-22, Annuity 
Supplement Earnings Report, is used to annually obtain the amount of 
personal earnings from annuity supplement recipients to determine if 
there should be a reduction in benefits paid to the annuitant.
    Approximately 1,500 RI 92-22 forms are completed annually. The form 
requires approximately 15 minutes to complete. The annual burden is 375 
hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact C. Ronald Trueworthy on (703) 
908-8550.

DATES: Comments on this proposal should be received on or before 
September 19, 1994.
