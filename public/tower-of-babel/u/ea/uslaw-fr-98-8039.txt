SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3064, Amendment. 1]


State of New Jersey

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated March 10, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to include Ocean County, New Jersey as a disaster area due to 
damages caused by a severe winter coastal storm, high winds, and 
flooding that occurred February 4-9, 1998.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous county of Monmouth, New Jersey may 
be filed until the specified date at the previously designated 
location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is May 2, 1998 and for economic 
injury the termination date is December 3, 1998.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: March 18, 1998.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
