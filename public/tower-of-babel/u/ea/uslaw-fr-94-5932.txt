COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS

 

Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' next public meeting is scheduled for 
17 March 1994 at 10 a.m. in the Commission's offices in the Pension 
Building, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street NW., Washington, DC 
20001 to discuss various projects affecting the appearance of 
Washington, DC, including buildings, memorials, parks, etc.; also 
matters of design referred by other agencies of the government.
    At the request of the U.S. Mint, the Commission will also hold an 
executive session at 9 a.m., prior to the public meeting, to review 
designs for the 1995/1996 Olympic Centennial Games Commemorative Coins.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, DC, March 8, 1994.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
