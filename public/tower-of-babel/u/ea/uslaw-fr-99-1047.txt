SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region I Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region I Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Hartford, Connecticut will hold a 
public meeting at 8:30 a.m., on Monday, January 11, 1999, Hartford, 
District Office, 330 Main Street, 2nd Floor, Hartford, Connecticut 
06106, to discuss such matters as may be presented by members, staff of 
the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Marie A. Record, District Director, 
U.S. Small Business Administration, 330 Main Street, 2nd Floor, 
Hartford, Connecticut 06106, (860) 240-4700.

    Dated: January 8, 1999.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, External Affairs.
