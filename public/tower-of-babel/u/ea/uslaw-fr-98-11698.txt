DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURAL

Foreign Agricultural Service


Types and Quantities of Agricultural Commodities Available for 
Donation Overseas Under Section 416(b) of the Agricultural Act of 1949, 
as Amended, in Fiscal Year 1998

AGENCY: Foreign Agricultural Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On April 6, 1998, the Secretary of Agriculture determined that 
10,500 metric tons of nonfortified nonfat dry milk packaged in 25 
kilogram bags are available for donation overseas under section 416(b) 
of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, during fiscal year 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Ira Branson, Director, CCC Program Support Division, FAS, USDA, (202) 
720-3573.
