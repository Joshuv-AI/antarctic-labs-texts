DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Lake Mead National Recreation Area; Operation of a Marina at 
Willow Beach

SUMMARY: The National Park Service is issuing a second solicitation 
seeking offers to operate a marina at Willow Beach Site within Lake 
Mead National Recreation Area. This will be a 125 slip marina with 
modest food and store operations along with related services. There is 
no existing operator. This opportunity is fully competitive. The 
existing facility is government-owned. An initial capital investment 
will be required for the rehabilitation of marina facilities. The term 
of the contract has been extended from five to ten years. In addition, 
rather than having to write-off the investment in the new marina 
docking system during the contract term, the concessioner will be 
allowed a possessory interest in that facility (a right to be 
compensated at the end of the ten years) at either the appraised value, 
based on its replacement cost less wear-and-tear and obsolescence or on 
the investment made (whichever is less). The closing day for acceptance 
of offers is September 30, 1997.
