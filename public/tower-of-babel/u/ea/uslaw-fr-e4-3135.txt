Dana Undies, Colquitt, GA; Dismissal of Application for 
Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Dana Undies, Colquitt, Georgia. 
The application contained no new substantial information which would 
bear importantly on the Department's determination. Therefore, 
dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-55,395B; Dana Undies, Colquitt, Georgia (October 28, 2004).

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 4th day of November 2004.
Timothy Sullivan,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
