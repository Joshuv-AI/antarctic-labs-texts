Realty Action; Proposed Sale of Public Land

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The following land has been found suitable for sale under 
Section 203 of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (43 
U.S.C. 1713), at not less than the appraised fair market value of 
$24,000:

San Bernardino Meridian, Imperial County, California

T. 10 S., R. 13 E.,
    Sec. 10, E\1/2\NE\1/4\ (80 acres)

    This land will not be offered for sale for at least 60 days 
following the date of this notice.The sale will be by modified 
competitive procedures. Persons permitted to bid will be limited to the 
adjacent landowners. The petitioner of this proposed sale will be given 
the opportunity to meet the highest bid received. Sale will be by 
sealed bid only. All sealed bids must be submitted to the BLM's El 
Centro Resource Area Office, 1661 South 4th Street, El Centro, CA 92243 
no later than 4:30 p.m., December 2, 1996. Bids must be for not less 
than the appraised $24,000. Each sealed bid must be accompanied by a 
certified check, postal money order, bank draft, or cashier's check 
made payable to USDI/BLM for not less than 10 percent of the amount 
bid. Federal law requires that all bidders be U.S. citizens 18 years of 
age or over, or in the case of corporations, be subject to the laws of 
any State or of the U.S. These lands contain no known locatable or 
salable mineral values, however the property is classified as being 
prospectively valuable for geothermal resources. Locatable and salable 
mineral interests will be conveyed simultaneously with the land. 
Leasable minerals will be reserved to the U.S.

DATES: For a period of 45 days after publication of this notice in the 
Federal Register, interested parties may submit comments to the Area 
Manager, Bureau of Land Management, El Centro Resource Area, 1661 South 
4th Street, El Centro, CA 92243. Objections will be reviewed by the 
State Director, who may sustain, vacate, or modify this realty action. 
In the absence of any objections, this realty action will become the 
final determination of the Department of the Interior.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Linda Self, Realty Specialist, at the 
above address or telephone (619) 337-4426.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Publication of this Notice in the Federal 
Register segregates the public land to the extent that it will not be 
subject to appropriation under the public land laws, including the 
mining laws. The segregative effect of this notice shall terminate upon 
issuance of patent or other conveyance document, publication in the 
Federal Register of a termination of the segregation or 270 days from 
the date of publication, whichever occurs first. The patent, when 
issued, will contain a reservation of ditches and canals to the U.S.

    Dated: September 27, 1996.
