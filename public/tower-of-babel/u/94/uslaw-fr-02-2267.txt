FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 67-2442) published on page 
893 of the issue for Tuesday, January 8, 2002.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas heading, the entry for 
Pubco Bancshares, Inc., Slaton, Texas, is revised to read as follows:
    B. Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas (W. Arthur Tribble, Vice 
President) 2200 North Pearl Street, Dallas, Texas 75201-2272)
    1. Lubco Bancshares, Inc., Slaton, Texas; to acquire 100 percent of 
the voting shares of Shamrock Bancshares, Inc., Shamrock, Texas, and 
thereby indirectly acquire voting shares of Shamrock Delaware 
Financial, Inc., Dover, Delaware, and First National Bank, Shamrock, 
Texas.
    Comments on this application must be received by February 1, 2002.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, January 25, 
2002.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
