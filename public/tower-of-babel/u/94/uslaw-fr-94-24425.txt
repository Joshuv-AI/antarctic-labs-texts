Presidential Determination Under Subsections 
                402(a) and 409(a) of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
                Amended--Emigration Policies of the Russian Federation

                Memorandum for the Secretary of State

                Pursuant to the authority vested in me by sections 
                402(a) and 409(a) of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 
                2432(a) and 2439(a) (the ``Act'')), I determine the 
                Russian Federation is not in violation of paragraph 
                (1), (2), or (3) of subsection 402(a) of the Act, or 
                paragraph (1), (2), or (3) of subsection 409(a) of the 
                Act.

                You are authorized and directed to publish this 
                determination in the Federal Register.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    Washington, September 21, 1994.
