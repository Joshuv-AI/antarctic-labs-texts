DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER98-1440-002]


Central Vermont Public Service Corporation; Notice of Filing

January 8, 1999.
    Take notice that on April 9, 1998, Central Vermont Public Service 
Corporation tendered for filing in compliance with the Commission's 
March 11, 1998, order issued in the above-referenced docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions and 
protests should be filed on or before January 19, 1999. Protests will 
be considered by the Commission to determine the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
