FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-26]

 

Radio Broadcasting Services; Pago Pago, American Samoa

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission rescinds the Report and Order which deleted 
Channel 266C1 from Pago Pago, American Samoa. See 59 FR 59200, November 
16, 1994. Channel 266C1 is not allotted to Pago Pago but to Leone, 
American Samoa. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 19, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 634-6530.
