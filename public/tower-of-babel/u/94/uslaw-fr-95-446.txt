ASSASSINATION RECORDS REVIEW BOARD


Notice

Summary: The Assassination Records Review Board announces the 
commencement of its formal review of records. On January 3, 1995, the 
staff began the review of the records created by the House Select 
Committee on Assassinations. The Review Board will make determinations 
on postponements for these records during its meeting in late January.

Contact person for more information: David G. Marwell, Executive 
Director, Room 208, 600 E Street NW., Washington, DC 20530, Telephone: 
(202) 724-0088, FAX: (202) 724-0457.
David G. Marwell,
Executive Director.
