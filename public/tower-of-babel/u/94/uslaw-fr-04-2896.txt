Radio Broadcasting Services; Benjamin and Mason, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; denial of application for review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies an Application for Review filed by 
Charles Crawford directed to both the Memorandum Opinion and Order in 
MM Docket No. 01-131 and MM Docket No. 01-133 concerning his respective 
proposals for a Channel 257C2 allotment at Benjamin, Texas, and a 
Channel 249C3 allotment at Mason, Texas. See 68 FR 5854, February 5, 
2003. With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.
