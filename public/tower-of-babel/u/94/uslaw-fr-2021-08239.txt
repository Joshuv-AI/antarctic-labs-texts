DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 4428-011]


Walden Hydro, LLC; Notice of Waiver Period for Water Quality 
Certification Application

April 15, 2021.
    On April 2, 2021, Walden Hydro, LLC filed with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission a copy of its application for a Clean Water Act 
section 401(a)(1) water quality certification submitted to New York 
State Department of Environmental Conservation (New York DEC), in 
conjunction with the above captioned project. Pursuant to 40 CFR 121.6, 
we hereby notify the New York DEC of the following:
    Date of Receipt of the Certification Request: April 1, 2021
    Reasonable Period of Time to Act on the Certification Request: One 
year.
    Date Waiver Occurs for Failure to Act: April 1, 2022.
    If New York DEC fails or refuses to act on the water quality 
certification request by the above waiver date, then the agency's 
certifying authority is deemed waived pursuant to section 401(a)(1) of 
the Clean Water Act, 33 U.S.C. 1341(a)(1).

    Dated: April 15, 2021.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
