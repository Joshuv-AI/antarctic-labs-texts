NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications and Amendments to 
Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Consideration

    On February 3, 2004 (69 FR 5200), the Federal Register published 
the ``Biweekly Notice of Applications and Amendments to Operating 
Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Considerations.'' On page 
5216, Southern Nuclear Operating Company, Inc., et al., Vogtle Electric 
Generating Plant, Units 1 and 2, ``Amendment Nos. 130 and 108'' should 
read ``Amendment Nos. 130 and 109.''

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 10th day of February 2004.
    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Ledyard B. Marsh,
Director, Division of Licensing Project Management, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
