LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE: The Finance and the Institutional Advancement Committees 
of the Legal Services Corporation Board of Directors will meet 
virtually on July 11, 2024. The Finance Committee meeting will begin at 
10 a.m. EDT and will continue until the conclusion of the Committee's 
agenda. The Institutional Advancement Committee meeting will begin at 
11 a.m. EDT and will continue until the conclusion of the Committee's 
agenda.

PLACE: 
    Public Notice of Virtual Meeting. LSC will conduct the July 11, 
2024, meetings via Zoom.
    Public Observation: Unless otherwise noted herein, the Finance and 
the Institutional Advancement Committees meeting will be open to public 
observation via Zoom. Members of the public who wish to participate 
remotely in the public proceedings may do so by following the 
directions provided below.
