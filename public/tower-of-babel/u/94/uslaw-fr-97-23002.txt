DEPARTMENT OF STATE


Bureau of Political-Military Affairs; Determination Under the 
Arms Export Control Act

[Public Notice 2596]
    Pursuant to Section 654(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, 
as amended, notice is hereby given that the Secretary of State has made 
a determination pursuant to Section 81 of the Arms Export Control Act 
and has concluded that publication of the determination would be 
harmful to the national security of the United States.

    Dated: August 20, 1997.
Thomas E. McNamara,
Assistant Secretary of State for Political-Military Affairs.
