DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Alternative Medicine; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Council for Complementary and Alternative Medicine, September 
8, 2006, 9 a.m. to September 8, 2006, 4 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, Neuroscience Building, 6001 Executive Boulevard, Rooms C & D, 
Rockville, MD 20852, which was published in the Federal Register on 
July 28, 2006, 71 FR 42860.
    This meeting is being amended due to the start time change for the 
Open session from 2 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. The meeting is partially Closed 
to the public.

    Dated: August 28, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
