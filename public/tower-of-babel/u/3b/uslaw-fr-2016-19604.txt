DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Ninety-Sixth Meeting Special Committee 159 Global Positioning 
System

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), U.S. Department of 
Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: NINETY-SIXTH MEETING Special Committee 159 Global Positioning 
System.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public of a 
meeting of NINETY-SIXTH MEETING Special Committee 159 Global 
Positioning System.

DATES: The meeting will be held October 14-21, 2016, 09:00 a.m.-5:00 
p.m. (unless stated otherwise)

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at: RTCA Conference Rooms, 1150 
18th NW., Suite 910, Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rebecca Morrison at [email protected] 
or (202) 330-0654, or The RTCA Secretariat, 1150 18th Street NW., Suite 
910, Washington, DC 20036, or by telephone at (202) 833-9339, fax at 
(202) 833-9434, or Web site at http://www.rtca.org.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal 
Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., App.), notice is 
hereby given for a meeting of the NINETY-SIXTH MEETING Special 
Committee 159 Global Positioning System. The agenda will include the 
following:
