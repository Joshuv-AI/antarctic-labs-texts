Employment and Training Administration

Mason Companies, Inc., Distribution Center, Chippewa Falls, WI; 
Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Mason Companies, Inc., 
Distribution Center, Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin. The application 
contained no new substantial information which would bear importantly 
on the Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the 
application was issued.

TA-W-57,646; Mason Companies, Inc., Distribution Center Chippewa 
Falls, Wisconsin (September 12, 2005)

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 15th day of September, 2005.
Timothy Sullivan,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
