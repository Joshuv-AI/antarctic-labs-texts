DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. GT96-30-000]


Williston Basin Interstate Pipeline Company; Notice of Filing

November 3, 1995.
    Take notice that on November 1, 1995, Williston Basin Interstate 
Pipeline Company (Williston Basin), tendered for filing revised tariff 
sheets to Second Revised Volume No. 1 of its FERC Gas Tariff.
    Williston Basin states that the revised tariff sheets are being 
filed to update its Master Receipt/Delivery Point List.

[[Page 56589]]

    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before November 13, 1995. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party to the proceeding 
must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with 
the Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
