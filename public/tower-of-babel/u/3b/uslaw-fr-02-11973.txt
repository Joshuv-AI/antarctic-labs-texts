FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-1041, MM Docket No. 02-27, RM-10367]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Springfield, IL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of West Central Illinois 
Educational Telecommunications Corporation, an applicant for a new 
television station to operate on channel *65 at Springfield, 
substitutes DTV channel *36 for channel *65 at Springfield. See 67 FR 
9428, March 1, 2002. DTV channel *36 can be allotted to Springfield, 
Illinois, in compliance with the principal community coverage 
requirements of Sec. 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 39-36-50 N. and 
89-38-58 W. with a power of 100, HAAT of 156 meters and with a DTV 
service population of 448 thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective June 24, 2002.
