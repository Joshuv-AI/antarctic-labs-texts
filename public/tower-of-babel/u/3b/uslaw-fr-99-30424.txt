SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3215]


State of New Jersey; Amendment #3

    In accordance with correspondence received from the Federal 
Emergency Management Agency dated November 9, 1999, the above-numbered 
Declaration is hereby amended to extend the deadline for filing 
applications for physical damage as a result of this disaster to 
December 17, 1999.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for economic injury is June 19, 2000.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: November 12, 1999.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
