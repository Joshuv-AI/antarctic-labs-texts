FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 99-1604; MM Docket No. 99-86; RM-9505]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Fruitland, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed Rule; dismissal of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the request of Mountain West 
Broadcasting to allot Channel 300A to Fruitland, NM, as it is not a 
community for allotment purposes. See 64 FR 14421, March 25, 1999. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Report and Order, MM Docket No. 99-86, adopted August 11, 1999, and 
released August 13, 1999. The full text of this Commission decision is
