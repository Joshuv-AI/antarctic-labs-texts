DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Deschutes Provincial Interagency Executive Committee (PIEC), 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Deschutes PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on April 22, 
1999 at the Jefferson County Fire Hall located on the corner of Adam 
and ``J'' street off of Hwy 97 in Madras, Oregon. A business meeting 
will begin at 9 a.m. and finish at 4 p.m. Agenda items include Hosmer 
Lake Working Group Recommendations, PAC/IAC Summit, Revisit PAC 
Agreements on Ground Rules for Meetings and Subcommittee/Working Group 
Processes/Assignments, The Lower Deschutes Working Group PAC Liaison 
Update, 1999 Program of Work, a Short Course on the Northwest Forest 
Plan, and a public forum from 1:30 p.m. till 2 p.m. All Deschutes 
Province Advisory Committee Meetings are open to the public.
