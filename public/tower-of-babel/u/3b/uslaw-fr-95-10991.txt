Cincinnati Gas & Electric Co. and PSI Energy, Inc.; Notice of 
Initiation of Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

April 28, 1995.
    Take notice that on April 26, 1995, the Commission issued an order 
in the above-indicated dockets initiating a proceeding in Docket No. 
EL95-39-000 under section 206 of the Federal Power Act.

after publication of this notice in the Federal Register.
