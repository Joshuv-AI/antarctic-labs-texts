DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Delegations of Authority

    Notice is hereby given that on January 19, 2001 the Director of 
Child Support Enforcement redelegated to the Deputy Commissioner of 
Child Support Enforcement, all the authorities delegated to the Deputy 
Director/Commissioner of Child Support Enforcement by the Director of 
Child Support Enforcement. This delegation is subject to any 
limitations or conditions contained in the delegations to the Deputy 
Director/Commissioner.

    Dated: January 19, 2001.
Olivia A. Golden,
Director, Child Support Enforcement.
