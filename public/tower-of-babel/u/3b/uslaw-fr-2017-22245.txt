DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 2, 2017, 9:00 a.m. to 
November 2, 2017, 7:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 
9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD 20850 which was published in 
the Federal Register on September 22, 2017, 82 FR 44429.
    This meeting notice is amended to change the meeting date from 
November 2, 2017 to December 7, 2017. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

Melanie J. Pantoja,
