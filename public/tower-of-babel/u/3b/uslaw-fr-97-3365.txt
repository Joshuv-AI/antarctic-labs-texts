Renewal of the Advisory Committee on Voluntary Foreign Aid

agency: United States Agency for International Development.

action: Notice of renewal of advisory committee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: Pursuant to the Federal Advisory Committee Act, the 
Administrator has determined that renewal of the Advisory Committee on 
Voluntary Foreign Aid for a two-year period, beginning January 1, 1997, 
is necessary and in the public interest.
