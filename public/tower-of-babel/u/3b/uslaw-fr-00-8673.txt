DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Availability of U.S. Patents for Non-Exclusive, Exclusive, or 
Partially-Exclusive Licensing

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Adelphi, Maryland, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 37 CFR 404.6, announcement is made of the 
availability of the following U.S. patent for non-exclusive, partially 
exclusive or exclusive licensing. The listed patent has been assigned 
to the United States of America as represented by the Secretary of the 
Army, Washington, DC.
    This patent covers a wide variety of technical arts including: An 
improved liquid explosive composition of nitromethane, a nitromethane 
sensitizer and an energetic compound.
    Under the authority of Section 11(a)(2) of the Federal Technology 
Transfer Act of 1986 (Public Law 99-502) and Section 207 of Title 35, 
United States Code, the Department of the Army as represented by the 
U.S. Army Research Laboratory wish to license the U.S. patent listed 
below in a non-exclusive, exclusive or partially exclusive manner to 
any party interested in manufacturing, using, and/or selling devices or 
processes covered by this patent.
    Title: Liquid Explosive Composition.
    Inventor: John D. Sullivan, Jr.
    Patent Number: 6,007,648.
    Issued Date: December 28, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael Rausa, Technology Transfer 
Office, AMSRL-CS-TT, U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Aberdeen Proving 
Ground, MD 21005-5055 tel: (410) 278-5028; fax: (410) 278-5820.
