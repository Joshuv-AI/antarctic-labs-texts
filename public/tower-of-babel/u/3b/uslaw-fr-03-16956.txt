Radio Broadcasting Services; Meigs and Pelham, GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document reallots Channel 222A from Pelham, Georgia, to 
Meigs, Georgia, and modifies the authorization for Station WQLI to 
specify on operation Channel 222A at Meigs in response to a petition 
filed by Mitchell County Television. See 68 FR 16750, April 7, 2003. 
The coordinates for Channel 222A at Meigs are 31-05-12 and 84-12-10. 
With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective August 7, 2003.

(202) 418-2180.
