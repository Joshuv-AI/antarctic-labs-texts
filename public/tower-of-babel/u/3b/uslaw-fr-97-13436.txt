DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER97-2846-000]


Florida Power Corporation; Notice of Filing

May 16, 1997.
    Take notice that on May 5, 1997, Florida Power Corporation (Florida 
Power) filed an Application for an Order Approving Market-Based Rates 
for Sales Outside of Florida. In its Application, Florida Power 
requests authorization to engage in wholesale, bulk power sales outside 
of Florida at market-determined prices, including sales not involving 
Florida Power's generation or transmission. Florida Power requests an 
effective date of 60 days after this filing, or the date on which the 
Commission issues an order approving Florida Power's application for 
market-based rates, whichever is earlier.

[[Page 28015]]

    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedures (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before May 27, 1997. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
