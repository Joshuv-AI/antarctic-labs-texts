NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Correction to Biweekly Notice; Applications and Amendments to 
Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Consideration

    On April 7, 1999 (64 FR 17021), the Federal Register published the 
Biweekly Notice of Applications and Amendments to Operating Licenses 
Involving No Significant Hazards Consideration. On page 17022, the 
paragraph starting ``By April 23, 1999, the licensee may file'', should 
read ``By May 7, 1999, the licensee may file''.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 13th day of April 1999.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
John A. Zwolinski,
Director, Division of Licensing Project Management, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
