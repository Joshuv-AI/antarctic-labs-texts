SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[License No. 02/02-0650]


Surrender of License of Small Business Investment Company; High 
Peaks Seed Ventures, L.P.

    Pursuant to the authority granted to the United States Small 
Business Administration under Section 309 of the Small Business 
Investment Act of 1958, as amended, and 13 CFR 107.1900 of the Code of 
Federal Regulations to function as a small business investment company 
under the Small Business Investment Company license number 02/02-0650 
issued to High Peaks Seed Ventures, L.P. said license is hereby 
declared null and void.

Paul Salgado,
Director, Investment Portfolio Management, United States Small Business 
Administration.
