FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Open Commission Meeting, Thursday, November 
8, 2001

November 1, 2001.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Thursday, November 8, 2001, which is 
scheduled to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, 
SW., Washington, DC.

[[Page 56108]]

                      Telecommunicatio   Review Spectrum Aggregation

                                         Mobile Radio Services spectrum
                                         aggregation limits and the

                                         to undertake a comprehensive

                                         policies of local radio
                                         ownership.

                                         consider a Memorandum Opinion

                                         television.
