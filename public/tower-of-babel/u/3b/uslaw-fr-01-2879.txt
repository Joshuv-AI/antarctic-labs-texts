DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 3565]


Privacy Act of 1974 as Amended; Removal of a System of Records

    Notice is hereby given that the Department of State is removing a 
system of records, ``Privacy Act Requests Records, STATE-40'' pursuant 
to the provisions of the Privacy Act of 1974, as amended (5 U.S.C. 
552a(r)), and in accordance with the record-keeping practices and the 
reorganization of the Bureau of Administration.
    As reported in Public Notice 3487 dated November 27, 2000 (65 FR 
75761, No. 233, December 4, 2000), the relevant records reflected in 
STATE-40 are now part of ``Information Access Programs Records STATE-
35,'' and STATE-40 consequently has been removed.

    Dated: January 29, 2001.
Patrick F. Kennedy,
Assistant Secretary for the Bureau of Administration, Department of 
State.
