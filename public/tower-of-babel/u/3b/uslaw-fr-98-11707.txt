Enogex Interstate Transmission L.L.C. and Ozark Gas Transmission, 
L.L.C.; Notice of Site Visit

April 28, 1998.
    On May 13, 1998, the Office of Pipeline Regulation (OPR) staff will 
conduct an aerial inspection of the proposed Ozark/NOARK Expansion 
Project in Sebastian, Franklin, Logan, Johnson, Pope, Conway, Van 
Buren, Stone, Izard, Baxter, Sharp, Lawrence, Greene, and Clay 
Counties, Arkansas. The aerial inspection will begin at 9:00 a.m. at 
Mid South Aviation, Inc., North Little Rock Airport, North Little Rock, 
Arkansas. If weather conditions preclude an overflight, the inspection 
will be canceled. A representative of the project sponosrs, Enogex 
Interstate Transmission L.L.C. and Ozark Gas Transmission, L.L.C., will 
accompany the OPR staff.
    All interested parties may attend, although those planning to 
attend must provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.
Robert Arvedlund,
Chief, Environmental Review & Compliance Branch I.
