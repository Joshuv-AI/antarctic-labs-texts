JOINT BOARD FOR THE ENROLLMENT OF ACTUARIES


Advisory Committee on Actuarial Examinations; Meeting

    Notice is hereby given that the Advisory Committee on Actuarial 
Examinations will meet in the Nathan Hale Room at the Sheraton 
Washington Hotel (located at Woodley and Connecticut Avenue, NW, in 
Washington, DC) on Tuesday, March 24 at 10:45 a.m.
    The purpose of this meeting to hold a Discussion Forum on the 
possible restructuring of the enrollment examinations. The panel will 
consists of Joseph A. Applebaum, Joint Board Chairman; Robert I. 
Brauer, Executive Director; and Carl Shalit, Advisory Committee 
Chairman. This meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: March 3, 1998.
Robert I. Brauer,
Advisory Committee Management Officer, Joint Board for the Enrollment 
of Actuaries.
