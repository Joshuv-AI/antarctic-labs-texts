POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Briefing on Data System Changes

AGENCY: Postal Rate Commission.

ACTION: Notice of public briefing; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The starting time for a United States Postal Service briefing 
on a proposed merger of two major data reporting systems (previously 
noticed at 68 FR 52802) has been changed. Instead of beginning at 10 
a.m. on September 17, 2003, the briefing will begin at 2 p.m. in the 
Postal Rate Commission's hearing room. The briefing is open to the 
public and is expected to last about two hours.

DATES: September 17, 2003, at 2 p.m.

ADDRESSES: Postal Rate Commission (hearing room), 1333 H Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20268-0001, Suite 300.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen L. Sharfman, General Counsel, 
202-789-6818.

Steven W. Williams,
Secretary.
