FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Open Commission Meeting; Thursday, February 
20, 2014

Date: February 12, 2014.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Thursday, February 20, 2014. The meeting 
is scheduled to commence at 10:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th 
Street SW., Washington, DC. The Commission is waiving the sunshine 
period prohibition contained in Section 1.1203 of the Commission's 
rules, 47 CFR 1.1203, until 11:59 p.m. on Tuesday, February 18, 2014. 
Thus, presentations with respect to the items listed below will be 
permitted until that time.

need and a way we can contact you if we need more information. Last 
minute requests will be accepted, but may be impossible to fill. Send 
an email to: [email protected] or call the Consumer & Governmental Affairs

    Additional information concerning this meeting may be obtained from
