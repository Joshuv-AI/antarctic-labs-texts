Notice of Realty Action

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In notice document Vol. 63, No. 62 beginning on page 15858 in 
the issue of Wednesday of April 1, 1998, make the following correction: 
The legal land description was written as follows:

Salt Lake Meridian

T.28 S., R.11 E.,
    Sec. 4 W\1/2\NE\1/4\.

    Containing 80 acres more or less.

    The legal description should be changed to read as follows:

Salt Lake Meridian

T.28 S., R.11 E.,
    Sec. 14 W\1/2\NE\1/4\.

    Containing 80 acres more or less.

    Dated: April 21, 1998.
Dave Henderson,
Acting District Manager
