Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-53,192]


Telect, Sugar Hill, GA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 8, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Telect, Sugar Hill, Georgia.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-41,469D, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 24th day of November, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
