Public Land Order No. 7230; Revocation of Executive Order Dated 
January 22, 1912, and Secretarial Orders Dated March 29, 1932, and 
March 17, 1944; Oregon

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Public Land Order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This order revokes in their entirety, one Executive order and 
two Secretarial orders which withdrew 17,557.20 acres of National 
Forest System lands for the Bureau of Land Management's Powersite 
Reserve No. 240, and Powersite Classification Nos. 263 and 348. The 
lands are no longer needed for the purposes for which they were 
withdrawn. The lands remain closed to surface entry, mining, and 
mineral leasing by other overlapping withdrawals.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 24, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Betty McCarthy, BLM Oregon/Washington 
State Office, P.O. Box 2965, Portland, Oregon 97208-2965, 503-952-6155.
