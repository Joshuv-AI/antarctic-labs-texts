ECP Uptown Campus LLC; Notice of Petition for Limited Waiver

    Take notice that on June 1, 2021, pursuant to section 292.205(c) of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's (Commission) Rules and 
Regulations,\1\ implementing the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act 
of 1978 (PURPA), ECP Uptown Campus LLC submitted a petition for a 
limited waiver of the efficiency standard set forth in 18 CFR 
292.205(a)(2) for its cogeneration facility located in Pittsburgh, 
Pennsylvania, for the 2020 calendar year, as more fully explained in 
the petition.
