Quality GearBox, LLC; Notice of Competing Preliminary Permit 
Application Accepted for Filing and Soliciting Comments and Motions To 
Intervene

May 10, 2010.
    1. On April 28, 2010, Quality GearBox, LLC (Quality GearBox) filed 
an application for a preliminary permit, pursuant to section 4(f) of 
the Federal Power Act, proposing to study the feasibility of the 
Harpersfield Low Head Dam Hydroelectric Project No. 13718, to be 
located at the existing Harpersfield Dam, on the Grand River, in 
Ashtabula County, Ohio.
    2. The proposed project would consist of: (1) An existing 8.5-foot-
high by 325-foot-long gravity dam; (2) an existing 19-acre impoundment 
with a storage capacity of 133 acre feet; (3) an existing filtration 
plant to be converted into a powerhouse with three new turbine-
generator units for a total installed capacity of 300 kilowatts; (4) a 
new 300-foot-long, 13.2-kilovolt transmission line; and (5) appurtenant 
facilities. The proposed project would operate in a run-of-river mode 
and would have an estimated average annual generation of 2,000 
kilowatt-hours.
