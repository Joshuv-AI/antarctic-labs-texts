Radio Broadcasting Services; Alamo Community, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Alamo Navajo School Board, Inc., this 
document allots Channel 298A* Alamo Community, New Mexico. As proposed, 
this allotment is reserved for noncommercial educational use. The 
reference coordinates for the Channel 298A* allotment at Alamo 
Community, New Mexico, are 34-25-01 and 107-30-04. See 65 FR 55930, 
published September 15, 2000.

DATES: Effective August 7, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177

Report and Order in MM Docket No. 00-158, adopted June 20, 2000, and
