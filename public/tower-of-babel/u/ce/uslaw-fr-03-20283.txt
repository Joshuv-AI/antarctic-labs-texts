Lower Deck Service Compartments on Transport Category Airplanes; 
Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes corrections to the final rule published in 
the Federal Register on June 19, 2003. That rule amended the 
airworthiness standards for transport category airplanes concerning 
lower deck service compartments.

EFFECTIVE DATE: This correction is effective on August 8, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jayson Claar, telephone (425) 227-
2194.

4, correct ``Amendment No. 110'' to read ``Amendment No. 25-110''.
