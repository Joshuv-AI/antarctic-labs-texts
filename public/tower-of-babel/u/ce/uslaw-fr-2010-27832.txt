DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Air Traffic Procedures Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public that a new 
charter has been issued for the Federal Aviation Administration Air 
Traffic Procedures Advisory Committee (ATPAC). The duties of this 
advisory committee include:
    1. To make recommendations for standardizing, clarifying, and 
upgrading terminology and procedures, as a result of its review of 
present ATC procedures and practices.
    2. To provide advice and make recommendations concerning:
    a. New or significantly revised ATC procedural concepts.
    b. The adequacy of charts, diagrams, and illustrations used to 
convey information concerning the application of ATC procedures and 
their relevance to current, revised, or proposed ATC procedures and 
concepts.
    c. Aviation regulations that have an impact on present, new, or 
significantly revised ATC procedures and concepts.
    3. To act solely in an advisory capacity to accomplish its duties.

DATES: Effective October 29, 2010.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Elizabeth Ray, ATPAC Executive 
Director, 800 Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, DC 20591. Telephone 
(202) 267-9205.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to Section 9(c) of the Federal 
Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App. 2), notice is 
hereby given of the filing of the ATPAC Charter, Effective October 29, 
2010.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on October 29, 2010.
Elizabeth Ray,
Executive Director, Air Traffic Procedures Advisory Committee.
