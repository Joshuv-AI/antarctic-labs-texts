DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 1975]

 

Participation of Private-Sector Representatives on U.S. 
Delegations

1979, the Department is submitting its June 1992 through December 1993 
list of U.S. accredited Delegations which included private-sector 
representatives.

guidelines published in the Federal Register on March 23, 1979.

    Dated: March 21, 1994.

Managing Director, Office of International Conferences.
