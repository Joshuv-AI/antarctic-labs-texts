Cooper Cameron Corp., Cooper Turbocompressor Division, 
Cheektowaga, NY; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 7, 1998 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Cooper Cameron Corporation, 
Cooper Turbocompressor Division, Cheektowaga, New York.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
35,247). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 22nd day of December, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
