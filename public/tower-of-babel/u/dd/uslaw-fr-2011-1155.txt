POSTAL SERVICE


Market Test of Experimental Product: ``Marketing Mail Made Easy''

AGENCY: Postal ServiceTM.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of a market test of an 
experimental product in accordance with statutory requirements.

DATES: January 20, 2011.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Brandy A. Osimokun, 202-268-2982.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3641(c)(1) that it will begin 
a market test of its ``Marketing Mail Made Easy'' experimental product 
on or around February 27, 2011. The Postal Service has filed with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission a notice setting out the basis for the 
Postal Service's determination that the market test is covered by 39 
U.S.C. 3641 and describing the nature and scope of the market test. 
Documents are available at http://www.prc.gov, Docket No. MT2011-3.

Neva R. Watson,
