Information Collection Under Review by the Office of Management

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard published a notice in the Federal Register of 
February 16, 2001, concerning request for comments on several 
collections of information. The notice contained an incorrect docket 
number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barbara Davis, Office of Information 
Management, 202-267-2326.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of February 16, 2001, in FR Doc. 01-3898, 
on page 10768, correct the ``docket number'' to read [USCG 2001-8629] 
in the following places: (1) column 2, the heading; (2) column 2, 
ADDRESSES, line 3; and (3) column 3, Request for Comments, line 6.

    Dated: April 5, 2001.
J. E. Evans,
Acting Director of Information and Technology.
