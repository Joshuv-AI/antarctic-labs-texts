FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

May 8, 2001.

Deletion of Agenda Item From May 10th Open Meeting

    The following item has been deleted from the list of agenda items 
scheduled for consideration at the May 10, 2001, Open Meeting and 
previously listed in the Commission's Notice of May 3, 2001.

Item No: 2.

Bureau: Mass Media.

Subject:   

Title: Cross-Ownership of Broadcast Stations and Newspapers; and 
Newspaper/Radio Cross-Ownership Waiver Policy (MM Docket No. 96-197).

Summary: The Commission will consider a Notice of Proposed Rule Making 
proposing to modify, eliminate, or retain its rule that prohibits 
common ownership of broadcast stations and newspapers within the same 
geographic area.

Federal Communications Commission.
Magalie Roman Salas,
Secretary.
