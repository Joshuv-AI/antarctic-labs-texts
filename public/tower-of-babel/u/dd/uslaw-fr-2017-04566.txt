47 CFR Part 73

[DA 17-186; MB Docket No. 16-371; RM-11777]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Red Lake, Minnesota

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Red Lake Nation, the Audio Division amends 
the FM Table of Allotments by allotting FM Channel 287C1 at Red Lake, 
Minnesota, as a Tribal Allotment and a first local Tribal-owned service 
to the community. A staff engineering analysis indicates that Channel 
287C1 can be allotted at Red Lake, Minnesota, as proposed, consistent 
with the minimum distance separation requirements of the Commission's 
rules with a site restriction 42.4 km (25.34 miles) northwest of the 
community. The reference coordinates are 47-59-00 NL and 95-33-33 WL.

DATES: Effective April 8, 2017.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Adrienne Y. Denysyk, Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2700.
