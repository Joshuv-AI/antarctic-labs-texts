DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

National Assessment Governing Board; Meeting

AGENCY: National Assessment Governing Board.

ACTION: Amendment to published notice of National Assessment Governing 
Board meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given of an amendment to the notice of a 
meeting of the National Assessment Governing Board scheduled for August 
3-5, 1995 published on July 17, 1995, FR60, page 36406. The time of the 
closed session on Friday, August 4, for the Achievement Levels 
presentation has been extended one-half hour to accommodate an 
additional presentation on the subject to be delivered by the Associate 
Commissioner of the National Center for Education Statistics. This 
closed session of the full Board meeting will conclude at 3:00 p.m.

Roy Truby,
Executive Director, National Assessment Governing Board.
