FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Intent To Terminate

    The Commission gives notice that it intends to terminate the 
following agreement pursuant to 46 CFR 501.17(h)(2) thirty days from 
publication of this notice.
    Agreement No.: 011918.
    Agreement Name: Seaboard Marine/Frontier Lines Space Charter 
Agreement.
    Reason for termination: Frontier Liner Services no longer 
registered Vessel Operating Common Carrier.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC.Agreements.Web/Public/AgreementHistory/502.

    Dated: August 5, 2022.
William Cody,
Secretary.
