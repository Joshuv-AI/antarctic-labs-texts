SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Notice of Closure of Receivership and Surrender of Licensee

    Notice is hereby given that First Texas Investment Company (``First 
Texas''), has surrendered its License (No. 06/10-0013) to operate as a 
small business investment company under the Small Business Investment 
Act of 1958, as amended. First Texas was licensed by the Small Business 
Administration on Deceber 11, 1959.
    Pursuant to a Final Order dated June 4, 1993, the receivership was 
terminated. The surrender of the license was accepted on June 4, 1993, 
and accordingly, all rights, privileges, and franchises derived 
therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Companies)

    Dated: October 31, 1995.
Don A. Christensen,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
