FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-105; RM-9046]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Atlanta, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Winn Parish Broadcasting, 
allots Channel 293A to Atlanta, Louisiana, as the community's first 
local aural transmission service. See 62 FR 15869, April 3, 1997. 
Channel 293A can be allotted to Atlanta in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without the 
imposition of a site restriction. The coordinates for Channel 293A at 
Atlanta are 31-48-18 NL and 92-44-36 WL. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective September 2, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications will open on September 2, 1997, and close on October 3, 
1997.
