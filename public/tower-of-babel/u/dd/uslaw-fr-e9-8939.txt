Tyco Electronics Corporation, Jonestown, PA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 13, 2009, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Tyco Electronics Corporation, 
Jonestown, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 26th day of March 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
