The Manufacturing Council: Meeting

manufacturing sector.

Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20230.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  J. Marc Chittum, The Manufacturing 
Council, Room 4043, 1401 Constitution Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 
20230; telephone: 202-482-4501; and e-mail: [email protected].
