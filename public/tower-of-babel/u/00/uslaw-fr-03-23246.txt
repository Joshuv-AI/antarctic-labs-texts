FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2627]


Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

DATE: September 8, 2003.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's Rulemaking proceedings listed in this public notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of this 
document is available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th 
Street, SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's 
copy contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. Oppositions to 
these petitions must be filed by October 14, 2003. See section 
1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an 
opposition must be filed within 20 days after the time for filing 
oppositions have expired.
    Subject: In the Matter of Implementing the Telephone Consumer 
Protection Act of 1991 (CG Docket No. 02-278).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 55.
