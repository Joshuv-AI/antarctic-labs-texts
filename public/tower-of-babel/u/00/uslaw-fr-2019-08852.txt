60-Day Notice of Proposed Information Collection: Pay for Success 
Pilot Application Requirements; Withdrawal

AGENCY: Office of the General Counsel, HUD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On April 25, 2019 at 84 FR 17416, HUD published a 60-day 
notice of proposed information collection entitled, ``Pay for Success 
Pilot Application Requirements'' (FR-7014-N-12) (FR Doc. 2019-08366). 
The notice contained typographical errors and was published 
inadvertently. Today's notice withdraws the 60-day notice published on 
April 25, 2019. HUD will publish a corrected version of the notice in 
the Federal Register at a later date.
