AGENCY: Research and Special Programs Administration (RSPA), DOT.

ACTION: Removal of direct final rule amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document removes an amendment in a direct final rule 
titled, ``Periodic Updates to the Pipeline Safety Regulations'' (63 FR 
7721), published on February 17, 1998, and restores the regulatory text 
that existed prior to the direct final rule. The final rule updated 
references to voluntary specifications

[[Page 38758]]

and standards incorporated by reference, and made various 
clarifications and grammatical corrections. The other amendments made 
by the direct final rule became effective on May 4, 1998.

DATES: The removal of the amendment is effective July 20, 1998.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Partial Withdrawal

    Under Clarifications of the direct final rule, Sec. 192.614(c)(5) 
requires operators to ``Provide for temporary marking of buried 
pipelines in the area of excavation activity before, as far as 
practical, the activity begins.'' RSPA believed that this requirement 
could be confusing to the reader in terms of interpreting the meaning 
of ``as far as practical.'' Therefore, RSPA amended the paragraph to 
require temporary marking of buried pipelines before excavation 
activities begins ``except in emergency situations.''
    Based on a comment to this clarification, RSPA is removing this 
change from this direct final rule. The comment was based on the 
interpretation of the phrase ``as far as practical.'' RSPA did not mean 
to distort the language of the regulation, only to clarify that 
operators would not be responsible for marking in an emergency 
situation. However, based on feedback from the pipeline industry and 
other regulatory parties within RSPA, it was determined that the 
original language better served the reader of the regulation. RSPA 
plans to issue an interpretation of the language in Sec. 192.614 to 
clarify its meaning.
