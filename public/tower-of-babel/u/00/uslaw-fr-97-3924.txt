Notice of Proposed Withdrawal; Utah

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This order will correct the errors in acreage and legal land 
descriptions in Notice of Proposed Withdrawal and Opportunity for 
Public Meeting; Utah.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 21, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael Barnes, Salt Lake District 
Office, (801) 977-4372 or Angela Williams, Utah State Office, (801) 
539-4107.
