DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration


Drug Safety and Risk Management Advisory Committee; Notice of 
Postponement

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is postponing the 
meeting of the Drug Safety and Risk Management Advisory Committee 
scheduled for September 19, 2003, due to Hurricane Isabel. The future 
date of this meeting is to be determined. This meeting was announced in 
the Federal Register of August 5, 2003 (68 FR 46199).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shalini Jain, Center for Drug 
Evaluation and Research (HFD-21), Food and Drug

12535. Please call the Information Line for up-to-date information on 
this meeting.
