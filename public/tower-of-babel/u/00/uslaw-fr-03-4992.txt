DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Submission for OMB Review: Comment Request

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Agency Information Collection Extension.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy (DOE) has submitted information 
collection package 1910-5112, Chronic Beryllium Disease Prevention to 
the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for extension under the 
Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995. The package covers the collection of 
information from DOE and DOE contractors that are subject to the 
Department's Chronic Beryllium Disease Prevention Program (10 CFR part 
850). The regulations contained in the Chronic Disease Prevention 
Program have been promulgated under authority in the Atomic Energy Act 
of 1954, and the Department of Energy Organization Act.

[[Page 10214]]

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: (1) Current OMB Control Number: 1910-5112 
(2) Package Title: Chronic Beryllium Disease Prevention Program (3) 
Summary: A three-year extension is requested to provide DOE employers 
with the information needed to manage chronic beryllium disease 
prevention programs, provide information to employees, and permit 
oversight of their programs by DOE management. (4) Purpose: This 
collection provides the Department with the information needed to 
reduce the number of workers currently exposed to beryllium in the 
course of their work at DOE facilities managed by DOE or its 
contractors; minimize the levels and potential exposure to beryllium; 
and provide medical surveillance to ensure early detection of disease. 
(5) Type of Respondents: DOE and DOE contractor employers of workers 
exposed or potentially exposed to beryllium; current workers. (6) 
Number of respondents: 1,703 annually; (7) Total annual burden hours: 
32,952 hours.
    Statutory Authority: Atomic Energy Act of 1954, 42 U.S.C. 2201.
