SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Women's Business Council; Notice of Cancellation for 
Public Meeting

    In accordance with the Women's Business Ownership Act (Pub. L. 106-
554 as amended) the National Women's Business Council (NWBC) public 
meeting on Capitol Hill, originally scheduled for Tuesday, May 23, 
2006, is being postponed until September 2006. The NWBC Web site will 
be updated shortly with information on the new date, time and location.
    We hope you will be able to join the NWBC in September 2006. If you 
have any questions, please contact the National Women's Business 
Council at 202-205-3850 or [email protected].

Matthew K. Becker,
Committee Management Officer.
