DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, December 5, 
2005, 12 p.m. to December 5, 2005, 3 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in 
the Federal Register on November 16, 2005, 70 FR 69579.

a.m. The meeting is closed to the public.
