Radio Broadcasting Services; Tilden, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 66 FR 38410 
(July 24, 2001), this Report and Order dismisses the Petition for Rule 
Making in MM Docket No. 01-153, proposing to allot Channel 245C3 at 
Tilden, Texas. The proposal was dismissed because it is inconsistent 
with, and untimely filed in relation to, a previously-filed proposal in 
MM Docket No. 00-148.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Deborah A. Dupont, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
