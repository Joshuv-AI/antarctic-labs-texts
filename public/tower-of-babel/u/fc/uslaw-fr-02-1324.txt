OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Excepted Service

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This gives notice of positions placed under Schedule C in the 
excepted service, as required by Civil Service Rule VI, Exceptions from 
the Competitive Service.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Shivery, Director, Washington 
Service Center, Employment Service (202) 606-1015.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Individual authorities established or 
revoked under Schedule C between November 1, 2001, and November 30, 
2001, appear in the listing below. A consolidated listing of all 
authorities as of June 30 is published each year.
