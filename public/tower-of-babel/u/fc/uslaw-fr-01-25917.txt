47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2326, MM Docket No. 00-100, RM-9860]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; San Antonio, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Alamo Public 
Telecommunications Council, licensee of noncommercial station KLRN-TV, 
NTSC channel *9, substitutes DTV channel *8 for DTV channel *20 at San 
Antonio, Texas. See 65 FR 36809, June 12, 2000. DTV channel *8 can be 
allotted to San Antonio in compliance with the principle community 
coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 
(29-19-38 N. and 98-21-17 W.) with a power of 8.3, HAAT of 263 meters 
and with a DTV service population of 1464 thousand. Since the community 
of San Antonio is located within 275 kilometers of the U.S.-Mexican 
border, concurrence of the Mexican government has been obtained for 
this allotment. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 26, 2001.
