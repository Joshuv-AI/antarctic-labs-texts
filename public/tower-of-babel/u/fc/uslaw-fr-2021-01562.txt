JUDICIAL CONFERENCE OF THE UNITED STATES


Committee on Rules of Practice and Procedure; Meeting of the 
Judicial Conference

AGENCY: Judicial Conference of the United States.

ACTION: Committee on Rules of Practice and Procedure, notice of open 
meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Committee on Rules of Practice and Procedure will hold a 
meeting on June 22, 2021 in Washington, DC. The meeting is open to the 
public for observation but not participation. An agenda and supporting 
materials will be posted at least 7 days in advance of the meeting at: 
http://www.uscourts.gov/rules-policies/records-and-archives-rules-committees/agenda-books.

DATES: June 22, 2021, 9 a.m.-5 p.m. (Eastern).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shelly Cox, Management Analyst, 
Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts, Thurgood Marshall Federal 
Judiciary Building, One Columbus Circle NE, Suite 7-300, Washington, DC 
20544, Phone (202) 502-1820, [email protected].

    Authority: 28 U.S.C. 2073.

    Dated: January 19, 2021.
