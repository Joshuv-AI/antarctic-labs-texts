Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board Chairs

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 28, 2011, in FR Doc. 2011-7243, on page 17118, the 
Department of Energy (DOE) published a notice of open meeting 
announcing a meeting on April 13-14, 2011 of the Environmental 
Management Site-Specific Advisory Board Chairs (76 FR 17118). This 
notice announces the cancellation of this meeting.
