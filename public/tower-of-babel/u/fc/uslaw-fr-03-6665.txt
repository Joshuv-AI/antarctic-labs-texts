SUMMARY: The Agricultural Marketing Service is correcting the final 
rule that appeared in the Federal Register of February 12, 2003, which 
amended all Federal milk marketing orders based on evidence received at 
a hearing held May 8-12, 2000, in Alexandria, Virginia. The document 
was published with an inadvertent error in Part 1030 regarding the 
computation of the statistical uniform price for milk. This docket 
corrects the error.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 1, 2003.
