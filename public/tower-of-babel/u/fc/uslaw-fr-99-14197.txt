SUMMARY: This notice corrects an error in the land description 
published as FR Doc. 99-13531 in the Federal Register, 64 FR 28833-4, 
May 27, 1999, for a proposed Bureau of Land Management withdrawal.
    On page 28834, column 1, line 4 from the bottom of the column, 
which reads ``W\1/2\NE\1/4\SW\1/4\SE\1/4\, W\1/2\SW\1/2\SE\1/4\,'' is 
hereby corrected to read ``W\1/2\NE\1/4\SW\1/4\SE\1/4\, W\1/2\SW\1/
4\SE\1/4\.''

    Dated: May 28, 1999.
John E. Moorhouse,
Acting Deputy State Director, Division of Resources.
