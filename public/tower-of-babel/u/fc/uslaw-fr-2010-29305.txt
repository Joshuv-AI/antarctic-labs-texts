DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commissioners and Staff Attendance at FERC/NARUC 
Collaborative on Smart Response Meeting

November 12, 2010.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC or Commission) 
hereby gives notice that members of the Commission and/or Commission 
staff may attend the following meeting: FERC/NARUC Collaborative on 
Smart Response: Omni Hotel at CNN Center, 100 CNN Center, Atlanta, GA 
30303. November 14, 2010 (8:15 a.m.-12:30 p.m.).
    Further information may be found at http://annual.narucmeetings.org/Program.cfm.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
