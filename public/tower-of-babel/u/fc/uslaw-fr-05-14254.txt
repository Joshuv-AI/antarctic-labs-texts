Establishment of Area Navigation (RNAV) Routes; AK

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects several errors in the airspace 
descriptions of a final rule published in the Federal Register on June 
22, 2005 (70 FR 36016), Airspace Docket No. 05-AAL-04.

EFFECTIVE DATE: 0901 UTC, September 1, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ken McElroy, Airspace and Rules, 
Office of System Operations and Safety, Federal Aviation 
Administration, 800 Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, DC 20591; 
telephone: (202) 267-8783.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

History

    On June 22, 2005, Airspace Docket No. 05-AAL-04 was published in 
the Federal Register (70 FR 36016), establishing 33 low altitude area 
navigation routes in Alaska. In that rule, the airspace descriptions 
contained several data points that were in error. This action corrects 
those errors.
