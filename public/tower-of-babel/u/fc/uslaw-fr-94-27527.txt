FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD

 

Open Season; Thrift Savings Plan Elections

AGENCY: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board (Board) in its 
regulation at 5 CFR 1600.2 provides that notice will be given of the 
beginning and ending dates of all open seasons (as defined at 5 CFR 
1600.1) which are subsequent to the open season ending on July 31, 
1987. The Board's next open season will commence on November 15, 1994, 
and will end on January 31, 1995. The election period (as defined at 5 
CFR 1600.1) covered by this open season extends from January 1, 1995 
through January 31, 1995.
