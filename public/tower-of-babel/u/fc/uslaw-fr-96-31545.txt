Notice of Alaska Resource Advisory Council Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Alaska Resource Advisory Council will conduct an open 
meeting Tuesday, January 21, 1997 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Wednesday, 
January 22, 1997 from 8:30 a.m. until 4:00 p.m. The purpose of the 
meeting is to discuss mining issues on the Fortymile Wild and Scenic 
River. The meeting will be held at the BLM Northern District Office, 
1150 University Avenue, Fairbanks, Alaska. Public comments regarding 
mining issues in the Fortymile will be taken from 3-4 p.m. Tuesday, 
January 21. Written comments may be submitted at the meeting.

ADDRESSES: Inquiries about the meeting should be sent to External 
Affairs, Bureau of Land Management, 222 W. 7th Ave., #13, Anchorage, 
Alaska 99513-7599.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Teresa McPherson or Janet Malone at (907) 271-5555.

Tom Allen,
State Director.
