SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Declaration of Disaster 3618; Commonwealth of the 
Northern Mariana Islands; Amendment 1

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency-- effective 
August 26, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning August 21, 
2004 and continuing through August 26, 2004.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is October 26, 2004 and for 
economic injury the deadline is May 27, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).
