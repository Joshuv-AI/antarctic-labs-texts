Federal Acquisition Regulation; Corrections

AGENCIES: Department of Defense (DOD), General Services Administration 
(GSA), and National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Corrections.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Civilian Agency Acquisition Council and the Defense 
Acquisition Regulations Council are issuing corrections to Federal 
Acquisition Circular 90-45 published at 62 FR 224, January 2, 1997, to 
correct miscellaneous editorial and technical errors.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 1997, except for the correction to 
Sec. 33.103, which is effective March 3, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Beverly Fayson at (202) 501-4755, 
General Services Administration, FAR Secretariat, Washington, DC 20405.
