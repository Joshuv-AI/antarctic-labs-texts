DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Nursing Research; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Nursing Research Special Emphasis Panel, November 12, 
2003, 8 a.m. to November 13, 2003, 5 p.m., Bethesda Marriott, 5151 
Pooks Hill Road, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal 
Register on October 3, 2003, FR67; 192;57473-57474.
    The meeting will be held at the Bethesda Marriott Suites at 6711 
Democracy Blvd., Bethesda, Maryland. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: November 20, 2003.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
