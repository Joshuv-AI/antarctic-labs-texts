Employment and Training Administration

Swanson Group/Swanson Aviation LLC, Grants Pass, OR; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 20, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Swanson Group/Swanson Aviation LLC, 
Grants Pass, Oregon.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of May 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
