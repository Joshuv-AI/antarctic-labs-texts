SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2775 Amendment #2]


Louisiana; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    The above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended, effective July 
14, 1995, to extend the deadline for filing applications for physical 
damages as a result of this disaster. The new deadline is August 10, 
1995.
    The termination date for filing applications for loans for economic 
injury remains February 12, 1996.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: July 20, 1995.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
