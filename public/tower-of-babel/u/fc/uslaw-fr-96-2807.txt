DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Due to the partial shutdown of the Federal Government, notice is 
hereby given of a change in the following meeting, as previously 
advertised in the Federal Register.

National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders

    Ad Hoc Clearinghouse Subcommittee of the National Deafness and 
Other Communication Disorders Advisory Council was to have met December 
18, 1995, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m., as a telephone conference call originating 
in Room 3C05, Building 31, National Institutes of Health, 9000 
Rockville Pike, Bethesda, Maryland, as published in the Federal 
Register on December 11, 1995 (60 FR 63537). The meeting has been 
changed to February 16, 1996, 1-3 p.m., in Conference Room 7 of 
Building 31. As previously advertised, the meeting will be a telephone 
conference call and will be open to the public.

    Dated: February 1, 1996.
Susan K. Feldman,
NIH Committee Management Officer.
