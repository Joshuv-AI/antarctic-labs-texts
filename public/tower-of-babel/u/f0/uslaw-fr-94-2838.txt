Road Closure; Arizona

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The road generally known as the Black Hills Back Country Byway 
will be closed on March 19, 1994 between the hours of 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. 
The road is within Graham and Greenlee Counties located in Southeast 
Arizona. Personnel will be on site to ensure the closure. This closure 
is authorized as per title 43 CFR Sec. 8365.1-6; Supplementary Rules.

    The legal description is within the following townships:

Gila and Salt River Meridian, Arizona

T. 5 S., R. 29 E.
T. 5 S., R. 30 E.
T. 6 S., R. 29 E.
T. 7 S., R. 29 E.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: For further information, contact Matt 
Wohlberg, BLM Ranger, Safford District, 711 14th Avenue, Safford, 
Arizona 85546, telephone number (602) 428-4040.

    Dated: January 26, 1994.
Frank L. Rowley,
Acting District Manager.
