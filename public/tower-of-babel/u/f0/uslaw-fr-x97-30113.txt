Correction

    In rule document 96-28650, beginning on page 57946, in the issue of 
Friday, November 8, 1996, make the following corrections:


Sec. 25.807  [Corrected]

    1. On page 57957, in the second column, in Sec. 25.807(f)(2), in 
the second line, ``the airplane do not'' should read ``the airplane 
does not''.
    2. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 25.807(f)(3), in 
the second line, ``airplanes'' should read ``airplane''.
    3. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 25.807(g)(7), in 
the fifth line, ``in'' should read ``is''.
    4. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 25.807(g)(9), in 
the first line, ``of'' should read ``or''.


Sec. 25.810  [Corrected]

    5. On page 57958, in the second column, in Sec. 25.810(d)(3), in 
the fourth line, ``tow'' should read ``two''.
