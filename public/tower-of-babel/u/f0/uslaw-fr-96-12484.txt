Pesticide Reports for Pesticide-Producing Establishments (EPA 
Form 3540-16); 1995 Annual Solicitation

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Time extension for submission of reports.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The EPA announced in the Federal Register (61 Vol. 8221, March 
4, 1996), that because of delays in completing and distributing 
reporting packages, that it would extend the due date for submission of 
annual pesticide production reports (EPA Form 3540-16) for calendar 
year 1995 until May 1, 1996. In another Federal Register document (61 
Vol. 14497, April 2, 1996), EPA corrected the original document of 
March 4, 1996, by stating ``Annual pesticide production reports for 
calendar year 1995 will not be due until two (2) months after the 
reporting packages are mailed out.''
    This notice announces that the 1995 Pesticide Reports for 
Pesticide-Producing Establishments forms (EPA Form 3540-16) will be 
mailed out by May 24, 1996, and are due to be submitted back to the 
Agency by July 24, 1996. If you have not received your reporting 
packages within two weeks from the date of this document, please 
contact your local EPA Regional office.

DATES: Annual pesticide production reports for calendar year 1995 will 
be due July 24, 1996.
