DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Health Resources and Services Administration


Rural Regional Trauma and Emergency Medical Services System 
Demonstration Grant Project in South Central Florida

agency: Health Resources and Services Administration, Public Health 
Service, Department of Health and Human Services.

action: Notice of extension of application due date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: This notice extends the application due date for a Rural 
Regional Trauma and Emergency Medical Services (EMS) Demonstration 
Grant Project in South Central Florida. The application due date for 
the South Central Florida project is extended to May 15, 1995. All 
other aspects of the February 15, 1995 Federal Register Notice remain 
the same.
