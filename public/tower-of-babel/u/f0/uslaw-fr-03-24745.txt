Flightdeck Security on Large Cargo Airplanes

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to the final rule published 
in the Federal Register on July 18, 2003 (68 FR 42874). That rule 
provided an alternative means of compliance to operators of all-cargo 
airplanes that are required to have a reinforced security flightdeck 
door.

EFFECTIVE DATE: This correction is effective on September 30, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joe Keenan, telephone (202) 267-9579.

Correction

    In the final rule FR Doc. 03-18075, published on July 18, 2003, (68 
FR 42874), make the following corrections:
    1. On page 42874, in column 1 in the heading section, beginning on 
line 4, correct ``Amendment Nos. 121-287 and 129-37'' to read 
``Amendment Nos. 121-287 and 129-38.''
