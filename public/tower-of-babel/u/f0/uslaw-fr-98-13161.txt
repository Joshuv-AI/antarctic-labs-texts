DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration

21 CFR Parts 510 and 558


Animal Drugs, Feeds, and Related Products; Change of Sponsor

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is amending the animal 
drug regulations to reflect a change of sponsor for 96 new animal drug 
applications (NADA's) and 4 abbreviated animal drug applications 
(ANADA's) from Hoffmann-La Roche, Inc., to Roche Vitamins, Inc.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 21, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Thomas J. McKay, Center for Veterinary 
Medicine (HFV-102), Food and Drug Administration, 7500 Standish Pl., 
Rockville, MD 20855, 301-827-0213.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Hoffmann-La Roche, Inc., Nutley, NJ 07110, 
has informed FDA that it has transferred the ownership of and all 
rights and interests in approved NADA's and ANADA's to Roche Vitamins, 
Inc., 45 Waterview Blvd., Parsippany, NJ 07054-1298. Accordingly, the 
agency is amending the regulations in 21 CFR parts 510 and 558 to 
reflect the change of sponsor. The agency is also amending the 
regulations in Sec. 510.600(c)(1) and (c)(2) by removing Hoffmann-La 
Roche, Inc., because the sponsor no longer sponsors any approved new 
animal drugs, and by alphabetically adding an entry for Roche Vitamins, 
Inc.
