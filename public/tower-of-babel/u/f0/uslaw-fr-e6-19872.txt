National Endowment for the Arts; Proposed Collection; Comment

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the date for comments regarding the 
National Endowment for the Arts (NEA) proposed data collection for the 
national Survey of Public Participation in the Arts that was published 
in the Federal Register on November 6, 2006 (Vol. 71, No. 214, page 
65007). The correct date for the submission of written comments to the 
office listed in the address section below is on or before January 7, 
2007.

ADDRESSES: Tom Bradshaw, National Endowment for the Arts, 1100 
Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Room 616, Washington, DC 20506-0001, 
telephone (202) 682-5527 (this is not a toll-free number), fax (202) 
682-5677.

Murray Welsh,
Director, Administrative Services, National Endowment for the Arts.
