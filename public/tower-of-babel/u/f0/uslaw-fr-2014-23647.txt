FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Transportation Intermediary License Rescission of Order of 
Revocation

    The Commission gives notice that the Order revoking the following 
Ocean Transportation Intermediary license has been rescinded pursuant 
to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. 40101).
    License Number: 019113N.
    Name: Amoy International, LLC dba Amoy Line dba VMS Lines.
    Address: 14145 Proctor Avenue, Suite 14, City of Industry, CA 
91746.

Licensing Updates.
