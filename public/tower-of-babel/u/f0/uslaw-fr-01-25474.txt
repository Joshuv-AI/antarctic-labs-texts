Price Pfister, Injection Molding Department Pacoima, CA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 11, 2001 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Price Pfister, Injection Molding 
Department, Pacoima, California.
    A negative determination applicable to the petitioning group of 
workers was issued on June 12, 2001 (TA-W-39,111). No new information 
was provided in the petition, which would result in a reversal of the 
Department's previous determination. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 25th day of September, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
