bottom, ``satisfactory'' should read ``satisfactorily''.
    3. On the same page, in the third column, in the first full 
paragraph, in the 14th line from the end, remove ``ensures the 
paragraph,''''.
    4. On page 43574, in the second column, in the 17th line, after 
``services,'' remove ``for'' and add ``five''.


Sec. 25.731  [Corrected]

third line, ``Sec. 24.735'' should read ``Sec. 25.735''.
