B/E Aerospace, Inc., Marysville, WA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 2, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition on behalf of workers at B/E Aerospace, Inc., Marysville, 
Washington.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 23rd day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
