meeting:

    Name of Committee: Board of Visitors, United States Military 
Academy.
    Date of Meeting: 10 February 1998.
    Place of Meeting: Room 418, Russell Senate Office Building, The 
Capitol, Washington, DC.
    Start time of Meeting: Approximately 10:00 a.m.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
For further information, contact Lieutenant Colonel Joseph A. Dubyel, 
United States Military Academy, West Point, NY 10996-5000, phone: (914) 
938-5078.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Proposed Agenda: Election of officers; selection of Executive

identification of areas of interest for 1998.
    All proceedings are open.
