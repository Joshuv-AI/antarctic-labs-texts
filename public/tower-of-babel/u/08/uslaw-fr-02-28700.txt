Corrections to Extension of Time Limit for Final Results of 
Countervailing Duty New Shipper Reviews

    On October 23, 2002, the Department published its notice of Certain 
In-Shell Pistachios (C-507-501) and Certain Roasted In-Shell Pistachios 
(C-507-601) From the Islamic Republic of Iran: Extension of Time Limit 
for Final Results of Countervailing Duty New Shipper Reviews (Extension 
Notice). See 67 FR 65090. In the ``Extension of Final Results of 
Reviews'' section of the Extension Notice, we inadvertently stated that 
the Department is extending the time limits for completion of the final 
results until no later than January 24, 2002. We should have stated 
that the Department is extending the time limits for completion of the 
final results until no later than January 24, 2003. This extension is 
in accordance with section 751(a)(2)(B)(iv) of the Act.
