Approval and Promulgation of State Plans--North Carolina:

Technical Correction

Correction

    In rule document 00-25599 beginning on page 60101 in the issue of 
Tuesday, October 10, 2000, make the following corrections:


Sec. 52.1770  [Corrected]

    1. On page 60102, in Sec. 52.1770 (c), in the table, in the section 
``Subchapter 2D--Air Pollution Control Requirements'', in the first 
line, the column for ``State effective date'' should read ``1/15/98'' 
and the column for ``EPA approval date'' should read ``11/10/99''.
    2. On page 60103, in the same section of the table, in the last 
line, in the column for ``State effective date'', ``Annual Emissions 
Reporting'' should read ``1/15/98''. The column for ``EPA approval 
date'' should read ``11/10/99'' and the column for ``Comments'' should 
read ``*''.
