CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION

16 CFR Part 1700


Poison Prevention Packaging

CFR Correction

    In Title 16 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 1000 to End, 
revised as of January 1, 1996, on page 685, in Sec. 1700.14, paragraph 
(a)(25) was inadvertently omitted. The omitted text should read as 
follows:


Sec. 1700.14  Substances requiring special packaging.

    (a) * * *
    (25) Naproxen. Naproxen preparations for human use and containing 
the equivalent of 250 mg or more of naproxen in a single retail package 
shall be packaged in accordance with the provisions of Sec. 1700.15 
(a), (b), and (c).
