Optional Rider for Proof of Additional NVOCC Financial

determined to extend the comment period in this matter.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Commission by Notice of Proposed 
Rulemaking published January 29, 2004, 69 FR 4271-4273, proposed to 
amend its regulations governing proof of financial responsibility for 
ocean transportation intermediaries. The Commission proposes to allow 
an optional rider for additional coverage to be filed with a licensed 
non-vessel-operating common carrier's proof of financial responsibility 
for such carriers serving the U.S. oceanborne trade with the People's 
Republic of China.
    The American Surety Association and The Surety Association of 
America are seeking a seven-day extension of time to Friday, February 
27, 2004, to file comments. In support of this request, the parties 
advise that they require additional time to complete and submit their 
comments. The Commission has determined to grant the requests. Comments 
are now due on Friday, February 27, 2004.

Bryant L. VanBrakle,
