FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2485: MM Docket No. 00-174; RM-9965]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Kailua-Kona, HI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Nick Koster this document alots Channel 244A 
to Kailua-Kona, Hawaii. See 65 FR 59164, published October 4, 2000. The 
reference coordinates for the Channel 244A allotment at Kailua-Kona, 
Hawaii, are 19-38-26 and 155-59-44.

DATES: Effective December 11, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.
