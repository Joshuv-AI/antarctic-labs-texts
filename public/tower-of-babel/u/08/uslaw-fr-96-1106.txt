SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Hartford District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Hartford District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Monday, February 26, 1996 at 8:30 
am at 2 Science Park, New Haven, Connecticut 06511, to discuss matters 
as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business 
Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Jo-Ann Vechten, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 330 Main Street, 
Hartford, Connecticut 06106, (860) 240-4670.

    Dated: January 19, 1996.
Art DeCoursey,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
