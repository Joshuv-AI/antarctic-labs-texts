DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP01-479-000]


Florida Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Proposed Changes in 
FERC Gas Tariff

July 10, 2001.
    Take notice that on July 5, 2001, Florida Gas Transmission Company 
(FGT) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Third Revised 
Volume No. 1, the following tariff sheets to become effective August 4, 
2001:

Seventh Revised Sheet No. 2
First Revised Sheet No. 209
First Revised Sheet Nos. 210 through 299

    FGT states that the tariff sheets listed above are being filed to 
include a general statement in its Tariff that the ``shipper must hold 
title'' policy is waived to the extent that FGT renders service for 
others using off-system capacity pursuant to its existing Tariff and 
rates.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or a protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with 385.214 or 385.211 of the Commission's Rules and 
Regulations. All such motions or protests must be filed in accordance 
with Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection. This filing may also be viewed on 
the web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select 
``Docket#'' and follow the instructions (call 202-208-2222 for 
assistance). Comments, protests and interventions may be filed 
electronically via the Internet in lieu of paper. See, 18 CFR 
385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the Commission's web site 
under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
