DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-86-000]


Koch Gateway Pipeline Company; Notice of Section 4 Filing

December 12, 1997.
    Take notice that on December 10, 1997, Koch Gateway Pipeline 
Company (Koch Gateway), tendered for filing a section 4 filing 
pertaining to the termination of gathering services associated with the 
abandonment of South Texas facilities granted in FERC Docket No. CP97-
337-001.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Koch Gateway Pipeline Co., 81 FERC para.61,228 (1997).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Koch Gateway proposes no changes to its published tariff therefore 
no revised tariff sheets are included in this filing. Koch Gateway 
filed with the Commission a list of gathering customers affected by the 
abandonment.
    Any persons desiring to be heard or to make any protest this filing 
should file a motion to intervene or a protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, D.C. 20426, in 
accordance with Sections 385.214 and 385.211 of the Commission's Rules 
of Practice and Procedures. All such motions or protests should be 
filed as provided by Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. 
Protest will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not served to make protestants 
parties at the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection in the Public 
Reference Room.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
