Advisory Circular 35.37-1A, Guidance Material for Fatigue Limit 
Tests and Composite Blade Fatigue Substantiation

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance of advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Advisory Circular (AC) 
35.37-1A. Guidance Material for Fatigue Limit Tests and Composite Blade 
Fatigue Substantiation. This advisory circular (AC) provides guidance 
and describes methods, but not the only methods, for demonstrating 
compliance with Sec. 35.37 of Title 14 of the Code of Federal 
Regulations, Fatigue limit tests. This AC also provides methods for the 
fatigue evaluation of propellers and the fatigue substantiation of 
composite propeller blades. Lake all AC material, this AC is not, in 
itself, mandatory and does not constitute a regulation. While these 
guidelines are not mandatory, they are derived from extensive Federal 
Aviation Administration (FAA) and industry experience in determining 
compliance with the applicable regulations.

DATES: Advisory Circular 35.37-1A was issued by the Manager, Engine and
