SUMMARY: This notice announces the reconvening of the hearing which 
began on September 23, 2003, in Tempe, Arizona, to consider proposals 
to amend the producer-handler provisions of the Arizona-Las Vegas and 
Pacific Northwest orders and to consider elimination of the ability to 
simultaneously pool the same milk on the Arizona-Las Vegas milk order 
and on a State-operated order that provides for marketwide pooling. The 
proposals

[[Page 62028]]

seek to, among other things, end the regulatory exemption of producer-
handlers from the pooling and pricing provisions of these two milk 
marketing orders if their Class I route distribution exceeds three 
million pounds of milk per month in either order.

2003.
