Radio Broadcasting Services; Olustee, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule, dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a petition for rule making filed by 
Charles Crawford to allot Channel 252A at Olustee, Oklahoma for failure 
to state a continuing interest in the requested allotment. See 69 FR 
57898, published September 28, 2004. This document therefore terminates 
the proceeding.
