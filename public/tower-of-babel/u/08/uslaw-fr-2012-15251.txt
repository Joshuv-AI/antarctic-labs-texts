DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby gives notice that 
members of the Commission's staff may attend the following meeting 
related to the transmission planning activities of the North Carolina 
Transmission Planning Collaborative:

Transmission Advisory Group

    June 19, 2012, 1:30 p.m., Local Time.
    The above-referenced meeting will be held at:

North Carolina Electric Membership Corporation, 3400 Sumner Boulevard 
Raleigh, North Carolina.

    The above-referenced meeting is open to stakeholders.
    Further information may be found at www.nctpc.org.
    The discussions at the meeting described above may address matters 
at issue in the following proceeding:

Docket No. EC11-60, Duke Energy Corporation and Progress Energy, Inc.

    For more information, contact Michael Lee, Office of Energy Market 
Regulation, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission at (202) 502-8658 or 
[email protected].

    Dated: June 15, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
