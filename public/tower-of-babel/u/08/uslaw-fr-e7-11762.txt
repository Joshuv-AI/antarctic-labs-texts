FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices, Acquisition of Shares of Bank or 
Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. E7-11296) published on page 
32296 of the issue for Tuesday, June 12, 2007.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis heading, the entry for 
Gaylon M. Lawrence, Jr., Franklin, Tennessee, is revised to read as 
follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (Glenda Wilson, Community 
Affairs Officer) 411 Locust Street, St. Louis, Missouri 63166-2034:
    1. Gaylon M. Lawrence, Jr., Nashville, Tennessee; to acquire 
control of Farmers Bancorp, Inc., and thereby indirectly acquire 
control of Farmers Bank and Trust Company, both of Blytheville, 
Arkansas.
    Comments on this application must be received by June 27, 2007.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, June 14, 2007.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
