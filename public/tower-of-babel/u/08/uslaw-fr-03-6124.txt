INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 332-451]


Advice Concerning Possible Modifications to the U.S. Generalized 
System of Preferences, 2002 Review

AGENCY: International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Correction of notice of investigation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission's notice published in the Federal Register on 
March 7, 2003 (68 FR 11143) contained a typographical error that 
incorrectly identified one of the HTS subheadings upon which the 
Commission will provide advice as to the probable economic effect on 
U.S. industries producing like or directly competitive articles and on 
consumers of the elimination of U.S. import duties for all beneficiary 
countries under the GSP. The Commission will provide advice for HTS 
subheading 7202.93.00; it will not provide advice for HTS subheading 
7202.93.00 (pt.).

    By order of the Commission.

    Issued: March 10, 2003.
Marilyn R. Abbott,
Secretary.
