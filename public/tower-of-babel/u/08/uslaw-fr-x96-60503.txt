Correction

    In rule document 96-10157 beginning on page 18900 in the issue of 
Monday, April 29, 1996, make the following corrections:


Sec. 3.23  [Corrected]

    1. On page 18908, in the first column, in Sec. 3.23(b)(3), in the 
second line, insert ``within 30 days after the date on which the 
decision for which reconsideration is being sought was rendered'' after 
``filed'' and in the third through the sixth lines, delete ``on which 
the decision for which reconsideration is being sought was rendered''.


Sec. 3.31  [Corrected]

    2. On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 3.31(b), in the 
sixth line, ``Sec. 3.8(a)(c)'' should read ``Sec. 3.8(a) and (c)''.


Sec. 242.19  [Corrected]

    3. On page 18909, in the third column, in Sec. 242.19, in 
amendatory instruction 24, in the fourth line, ``(6)'' should read 
``(b)''.
