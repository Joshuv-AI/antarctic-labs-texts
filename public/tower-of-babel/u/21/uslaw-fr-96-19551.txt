DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Notice of Performance Review Board Membership

SUMMARY: Per 5 U.S.C. 4314 (c)(4), the Department of the Navy (DON) 
announces the appointment of members to the DON's numerous Senior 
Executive Service (SES) Performance Review Boards (PRB's). The purposes 
of the PRB's is to provide fair and impartial review of the annual SES 
performance appraisal prepared by the senior executive's immediate and 
second level supervisor, to make recommendations to appointing 
officials regarding acceptance or modification of the performance 
rating, and to make recommendations for monetary performance awards. 
Composition of the specific PRB's will be determined on an ad hoc basis 
from among individuals listed below:
