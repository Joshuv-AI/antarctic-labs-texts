DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. OA97-669-000]


IES Utilities Inc., Notice of Filing

August 8, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 14, 1997, IES Utilities Inc., tendered for 
filing its Order No. 888-A compliance filing in the above-referenced 
docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion

[[Page 43522]]

to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with 
Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure 
(18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or protests 
should be filed on or before August 20, 1997. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
