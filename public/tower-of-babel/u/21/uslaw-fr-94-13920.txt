DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Maritime Administration

 

Approval of Applicant as Trustee

Company, with offices at 101 East Fifth Street, St. Paul, Minnesota, 
has been approved as Trustee pursuant to Public Law 100-710 and 46 CFR 
part 221.

    Dated: June 2, 1994.

    By Order of the Maritime Administrator.
James E. Saari,
Secretary.
