Correction

    In rule document 99-7625, beginning on page 14972, in the issue of 
Monday, March 29, 1999, make the following corrections:


Sec. 93.53  [Corrected]

    1. On page 14976, in the second column, in Sec. 93.53, in the first 
paragraph, in the 16th line from the bottom, ``lat. 61 deg.16'13''N.'' 
should read ``lat. 61 deg.17'13''N.''.


Sec. 93.55  [Corrected]

    2. On page 14976, in the third column, in Sec. 93.55(b), in the 
second line, ``an'' should read ``and''.
    3. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 93.55(c), in the 
eighth line, ``Seaward'' should read ``Seward''.
    4. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 93.55(e), in the 
fourth line, ``long. 149 deg.43735''W.'' should read ``long. 
149 deg.37'35''W.''.
    5. On page 14977, in the first column, in Sec. 93.55(f), in the 
second line, ``an'' should read ``and''.
    6. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 93.55(f), in the 
10th line, ``61 deg.40723''N.'' should read ``61 deg.07'23''N.''.
    7. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 93.55(f), in the 
17th line, ``Rod'' should read ``Road''.


Sec. 93.65  [Corrected]

    8. On page 14977, in the third column, in Sec. 93.65(d), in the 
second line, ``AFB;'' should read ``AFB,''.
