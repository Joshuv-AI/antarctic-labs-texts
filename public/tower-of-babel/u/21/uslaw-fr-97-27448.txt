DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Notice of Meeting of the Division 
of Research Grants Advisory Committee

    Pursuant to Pub. L. 92-463, notice is hereby given of the meeting 
of the Division of Research Grants Advisory Committee, November 17-18, 
1997, Building 31C, Conference Room 10, National Institutes of Health, 
Bethesda, Maryland 20892.
    The entire meeting will be open to the public from 8:30 on November 
17 to adjournment on November 18. The meeting will include, among other 
topics, a discussion of some recent experiences and experiments in 
streamlining the peer review system. Attendance by the public will be 
limited to space available.
    The Office of Committee Management, Center for Scientific Review, 
Rockledge 2 Building, Suite 3016, National Institutes of Health, 
Bethesda, Maryland 20892-7778, telephone (301) 435-1124, will furnish a 
summary of the meeting and a roster of the committee members.
    Dr. Samuel Joseloff, Executive Secretary of the Committee, 
Rockledge 2 Building, Suite 3176, National Institutes of Health, 
Bethesda, Maryland 20892-7762, phone (301) 435-0691, will provide 
substantive program information upon request.
    Individuals who plan to attend and need special assistance, such as 
sign language interpretation or other reasonable accommodations, should 
contact the Executive Secretary at least two weeks in advance of the 
meeting.

    Dated: October 10, 1997.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
