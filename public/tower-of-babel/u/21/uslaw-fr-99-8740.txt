FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 98-37; RM-9238]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Frankston and Palestine, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document reallots Channel 244C2 from Palestine, Texas, to 
Frankston, Texas, and modifies the authorization for Station KLIS, 
Palestine, to specify operation at Frankston, in response to a petition 
filed by Nicol/Excel Broadcasting, LLC. See 63 FR 17145, April 2, 1998. 
(On May 30, 1997, the license for Station KLIS, Palestine, Texas, was 
modified to

[[Page 17109]]

specify operation on Channel 244C2 in lieu of Channel 244A, BPH-
97031IC.) The coordinates for Channel 244C2 at Frankston are 32-02-02 
NL and 95-24-30 WL. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 17, 1999.
