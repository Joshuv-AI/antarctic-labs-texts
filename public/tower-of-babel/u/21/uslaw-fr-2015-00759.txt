GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE


Medicare Payment Advisory Commission Nominations

AGENCY: Government Accountability Office (GAO).

ACTION: Notice on letters of nomination.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Balanced Budget Act of 1997 established the Medicare 
Payment Advisory Commission (MedPAC) and gave the Comptroller General 
responsibility for appointing its members. For appointments to MedPAC 
that will be effective May 1, 2015, I am announcing the following: 
Letters of nomination and resumes should be submitted by March 13, 2015 
to ensure adequate opportunity for review and consideration of nominees 
prior to the appointment of new members.

Washington, DC 20548

512-4800.
