American Components, Inc., Dandridge, TN; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 8, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of all workers at American Component, Inc., Dandridge, 
Tennessee.
    The workers' petition regarding the investigation is invalid. Two 
of the three petitioners neglected to sign the petition. Consequently, 
further investigation in this cannot progress, and the investigation 
has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 29th day of April 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
