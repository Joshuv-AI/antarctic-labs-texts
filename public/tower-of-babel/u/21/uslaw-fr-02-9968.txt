DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Trinity County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC); Meeting

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Trinity County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC) will meet 
on April 29, 2002, Weaverville, Calif. The purpose of the meeting is 
voting on road restoration and vegetation reduction projects; forming 
subcommittees to address contracting, grants and agreements; and 
discussing the community model forest concept.

DATES: The meeting will be held on April 29, 2002 from 6:30 to 8:30 
p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Trinity County Office of 
Education conference room, 201 Memorial Drive, Weaverville, Calif.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce Andersen, Designated Federal 
Official, USDA, Shasta-Trinity National Forest, P.O. Box 1190, 
Weaverville, CA 96093. Phone: (530) 623-2121. Email: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting is open to the public. Public 
input opportunity will be provided and individuals will have the 
opportunity to address the committee at that time.

    Dated: April 16, 2002.
James M. Pena,
Deputy Forest Supervisor.
