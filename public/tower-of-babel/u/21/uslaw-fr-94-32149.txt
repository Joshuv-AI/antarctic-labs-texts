Realty Actions; Proposed Direct Sale

December 19, 1994.
    The Notice of Realty Action published in the November 9, 1994, 
edition of the Federal Register (59 FR 55851) is hereby amended as 
follows:
    The appraised fair market value has been determined to be 
$1,200.00.
    The parcel is being offered only to Robert F. Dersham et al. (fee 
owners of Tax Lot 600, Map 1N 3 3) and William Tolke et al. (fee owners 
of Tax Lot 1500, Map 1N 3 11) selling under contract to David VanAsche.
    Sealed written bids, delivered or mailed, must be received by the 
Bureau of Land Management, Salem District Office, 1717 Fabry Road SE, 
Salem, Oregon 97306, prior to 11:00 a.m. on Wednesday, January 11, 
1995.
Dana R. Shuford,
Tillamook Area Manager.
