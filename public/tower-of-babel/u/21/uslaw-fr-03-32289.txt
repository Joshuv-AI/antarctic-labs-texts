Pillowtex Corporation, New York Design and Sales Office, New 
York, NY; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 10, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Pillowtex Corporation, New York 
Design and Sales Office, New York, New York.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-52,559A, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of December, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
