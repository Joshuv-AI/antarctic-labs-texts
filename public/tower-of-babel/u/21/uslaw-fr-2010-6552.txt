Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2010-0101.
    Petitioner: Air Canada.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR 93.123(a).
    Description of Relief Sought:
    Air Canada requests an exemption from the limit for DCA set forth 
in Sec.  93.123(a), to permit the FAA to create commuter slots during 
certain limited hours for Air Canada's use. The proposed commuter slots 
would replace expiring slots currently held by Air Canada and used to 
provide service from DCA to points in Canada. Specifically, Air Canada 
desires one daily slot during each of the 1100, 1200, 1800, 1900, 2000 
and 2100 hours. The proposed exemption would permit Air Canada to 
continue operating its existing services.
