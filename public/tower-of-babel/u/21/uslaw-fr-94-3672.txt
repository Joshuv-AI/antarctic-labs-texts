DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
 

SerCan Timber Sale, Plumas National Forest; Environmental Impact 
Statement Cancellation

    The Plumas National Forest is no longer involved in the paration of 
an Environmental Impact Statement for the SerCan Timber Sale.
    The notice of Intent, published in the Federal Register on March 
23, 1989, is hereby rescinded (54 FR 11981).

11500, Quincy, CA 95971, telephone (916) 283-2050.
