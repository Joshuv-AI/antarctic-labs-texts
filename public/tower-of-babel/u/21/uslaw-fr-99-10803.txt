DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On December 28,1998, a notice was published in the Federal 
Register, Vol. 63, No. 248, Page 71497, that an application had been 
filed with the Fish and Wildlife Service by Dennis Leistico for a 
permit (PRT-006163) to import one polar bear (Ursus maritimus) trophy 
taken from the M'Clintock Channel population, Canada for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on April 7, 1999, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
