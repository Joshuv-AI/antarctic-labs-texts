Digital Television Broadcast Service and Television Broadcast 
Service; Campbellsville and Bardstown, KY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denied.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, by this document, denies the petition for 
rulemaking filed by Louisville Communications, LLC, licensee of station 
WBKI(TV), proposing the reallottment of TV channel 34 and DTV channel 
19 from Campbellsville to Bardstown, Kentucky. See 66 FR 37443, July 
18, 2001. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
