Caribbean Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council's (Council) 
Scientific and Statistical Committee (SSC) will hold a two-day public 
virtual meeting to address the items contained in the tentative agenda 
included in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

DATES: The public virtual meeting will be held on September 16, 2020, 
from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and September 17, 2020, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. 
All meetings will be at Eastern Standard Time.
