Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary of Defense, DoD.

ACTION: Notice to alter a System of Records.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of the Secretary of Defense proposes to alter a 
system of records, DoDEA 27, entitled ``Department of Defense Education 
Activity Research Approval Process'', in its inventory of record 
systems subject to the Privacy Act of 1974, as amended. This system 
will collect, validate eligibility, and maintain an official registry 
file that identifies individuals who apply for, and are granted, access 
to conduct research involving DoDEA students, staff, parents or data. 
Additionally will establish researcher accountability, enable future 
contact with researchers, and support preparation of statistical and 
other aggregate reports on researcher use of DoDEA records.

DATES: This proposed action will be effective on November 26, 2013 
unless comments are received which result in a contrary determination. 
Comments will be accepted on or before November 25, 2013.

ADDRESSES: You may submit comments, identified by docket number and 
title, by any of the following methods:

Drive, East Tower, 2nd Floor, Suite 02G09, Alexandria, VA 22350-3100.
    Instructions: All submissions received must include the agency name

at http://www.regulations.gov as they are received without change,
