DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. RP89-34-012, RP89-257-004, and RP90-2-014]


Williston Basin Interstate Pipeline Company; Compliance Filing

May 10, 1995.
    Take notice that on May 5, 1995, Williston Basin Interstate 
Pipeline Company (Williston Basin), tendered for filing certain revised 
tariff sheets to First Revised Volume No. 1 and Original Volume Nos. 1-
A, 1-B and 2 of its FERC Gas Tariff.
    Williston Basin states that the revised tariff sheets were filed in 
compliance with the Commission's ``Order on Rehearing'' issued April 5, 
1995 in Docket Nos. RP89-34-010, RP89-257-003 and RP90-2-013 as more 
fully described in the filing.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street 
NE., Washington, DC 20246, in accordance with Rule 211 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211). All such 
protests should be filed on or before May 17, 1995. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make the protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Copies of the filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
