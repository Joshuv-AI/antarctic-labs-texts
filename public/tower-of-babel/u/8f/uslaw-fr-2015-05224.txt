ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 3, 2014, the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) issued a 
final rule adopting conservation standards for some classes of walk-in 
cooler and walk-in freezer components. The final rule was published 
with typographical errors to some of the reported values. DOE is 
providing corrections to address these errors. Neither the errors nor 
the corrections in this document affect the substance of the rulemaking 
or any of the conclusions reached in support of the final rule.

DATES: This correction is effective March 6, 2015.
