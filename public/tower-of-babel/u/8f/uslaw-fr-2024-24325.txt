DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Frederick 
National Laboratory Advisory Council, October 23, 2024, 9:00 a.m. to 
October 23, 2024, 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 9606 
Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD, 20850 (Virtual Meeting), which was 
published in the Federal Register on October 1, 2024, FR Doc. 2024-
22438, 89 FR 79937.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting start and end 
times. The meeting will now be held from 12:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. 
instead of from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The meeting is open to the 
public.

    Dated: October 16, 2024.
David W. Freeman,
Supervisory Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee 
Policy.
