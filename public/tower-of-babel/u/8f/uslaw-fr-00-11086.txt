DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Privacy Act of 1974; Amendment of Systems Notice; Appointment of 
New System Manager

AGENCY: Department of Veterans Affairs.

ACTION: Notice; amendment of systems of records.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: As required by the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(e), 
notice is hereby given that the Department of Veterans (VA) is changing 
the System Manager, system number and address in three systems of 
records.

DATES: These amendments are effective on May 4, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Director, Field Liaison Staff (20SA), 
Office of Information Management, Veterans Benefits Administration, VA 
Central Office, 810 Vermont Avenue, NW, Washington, DC 20420, (202) 
273-6947.

    Approved: April 18, 2000.
Togo D. West, Jr.,
Secretary of Veterans Affairs.
