DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Natural Resources Conservation Service


Rehabilitation of Aging Flood Control Dams in Oklahoma

AGENCY: Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS).

ACTION: Notice of availability of record of decision.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: M. Darrel Dominick, responsible Federal official for projects 
administered under the provisions of Section 14 of Public Law 83-566 
(enacted by Section 313 of Public Law 106-472, otherwise known as ``The 
Small Watershed Rehabilitation Amendments of 2000'') in the State of 
Oklahoma, is hereby providing notification that a record of decision to 
proceed with the installation of the Rehabilitation of Aging Flood 
Control Dams in Oklahoma is available. Single copies of this record of 
decision may be obtained from M. Darrel Dominick at the address shown 
below.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: M. Darrel Dominick, State 
Conservationist, Natural Resources Conservation Service, State Office, 
100 USDA Suite 206, Stillwater, Oklahoma, 74074-2655, telephone (405) 
742-1227.


[[Page 72639]]


    Dated: November 22, 2002.
M. Darrel Dominick,
State Conservationist.

Assistance under No. 10.904, Watershed Protection and Flood 
Prevention, and is subject to the provisions of Executive Order 
12372, which requires intergovernmental consultation with State and 
local officials)
