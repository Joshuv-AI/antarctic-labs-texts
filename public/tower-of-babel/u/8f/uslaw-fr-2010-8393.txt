FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 2010-7980) published on page 
17917 of the issue for Thursday, April 8, 2010.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of New York heading, the entry for 
Morgan Stanley, New York, New York, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of New York (Ivan Hurwitz, Bank 
Applications Officer) 33 Liberty Street, New York, New York 10045-0001:
    1. Morgan Stanley, New York, New York; to acquire 100 percent of 
the voting shares of Morgan Stanley Private Bank, N.A., Jersey City, 
New Jersey, as a result of converting Morgan Stanley Trust, Jersey 
City, New Jersey, into a national bank. Morgan Stanley Private Bank, 
N.A., will assume certain deposit liabilities of Morgan Stanley Bank, 
N.A., Salt Lake City, Utah, and will be relocated to Purchase, New 
York.
    Comments on this application must be received by April 26, 2010.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, April 8, 2010.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
