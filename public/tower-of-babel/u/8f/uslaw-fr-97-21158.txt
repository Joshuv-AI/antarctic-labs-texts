NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice (97-107)]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Cannondale, of 9 Brookside 
Place, Georgetown, CT 06829-0122, has applied for a partially exclusive 
license to practice the inventions described and claimed in NASA Case 
Number GSC-13,802-1, entitled ``3-D Sprag Ratcheting Tool,'' and U.S. 
Patent No. 5,482,144, entitled ``Three Dimensional Roller Locking 
Sprag,'' which are both

[[Page 43018]]

assigned to the United States of America as represented by the 
Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. 
Written objections to the prospective grant of a license should be sent 
to Goddard Space Flight Center.

DATES: Responses to this notice must be received by October 10, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Guy M. Miller, Patent Counsel, Goddard Space Flight Center, Mail Stop 
204, Greenbelt, MD 20771, telephone (301) 286-7351.

    Dated: August 4, 1997.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
