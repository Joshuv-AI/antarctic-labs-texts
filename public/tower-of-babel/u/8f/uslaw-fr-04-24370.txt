SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster P065]


State of New York; Amendment 1

    In accordance with notices received from the Department of Homeland 
Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective October 13 and 
22, 2004, the above numbered Public Assistance declaration is hereby 
amended to include Cattaraugus, Niagara, Orange, and Orleans Counties 
in the State of New York as a disaster area due to damages caused by 
severe storms and flooding occurring on August 29, 2004, and continuing 
through September 16, 2004.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 30, 2004.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59008.)

    Dated: October 26, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
