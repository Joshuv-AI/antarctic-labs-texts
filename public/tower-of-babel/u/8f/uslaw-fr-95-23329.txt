INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION
[Investigation No. 337-TA-371]


Notice

    In the matter of Certain Memory Devices with Increased 
Capacitance and Products Containing Same.

    Notice is hereby given that the prehearing conference in this 
matter that was to commence at 10 a.m. on September 18, 1995, in 
Courtroom B (Room 111), U.S. International Trade Commission Building, 
500 E St. S.W., Washington, D.C., and the hearing commencing 
immediately thereafter (60 Fed. Reg. 46626) are postponed until 10 a.m. 
on November 6, 1995, in Courtroom A (Room 100) at the same address.

    The Secretary shall publish this notice in the Federal Register.

    Issued: September 12, 1995.

Sidney Harris,

Administrative Law Judge.
