Midwest Independent Transmission System Operator, Inc.; Midwest 
Independent Transmission System Operator, Inc. and PJM Interconnection, 
LLC, et al.; Midwest Independent Transmission System Operator, Inc. and 
PJM Interconnection, LLC, et al.; Ameren Services Company, et al.; 
Notice Establishing Common Comment Date

May 13, 2005.
    On April 19, 2005, the Commission issued a Notice of Extension of 
Time establishing a common due date to file comments on the March 31, 
2005, filing by the Midwest Independent Transmission System Operator, 
Inc. (Midwest ISO) and the Midwest ISO Transmission Owners 
(collectively, Midwest ISO Applicants). The comment due date was 
extended to coincide with the comment date established for an 
anticipated filing that was to be submitted by Midwest ISO Applicants 
to incorporate lost revenue information that was to be filed by the PJM 
Interconnection, L.L.C. (PJM) transmission owners.
    On May 9, 2005, the Commission issued another notice to establish a 
common comment due date for further filings that had been submitted 
that relate to the March 31, 2005 filing by the Midwest ISO Applicants.
    Subsequently, Midwest ISO Applicants' anticipated filing to 
incorporate lost revenue information that was submitted by the PJM 
transmission owners, was submitted on May 4, 2005, as amended on May 5, 
2005. A notice for this filing was issued on May 12, 2005, with a 
comment due date of May 26, 2005.
    Accordingly, in order to ensure consistent comment deadlines on 
these related filings, notice is hereby given that the due date for 
comments on the filings submitted in the above captioned dockets is 
extended to and including May 26, 2005.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
