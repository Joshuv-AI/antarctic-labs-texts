North American Electric Reliability Corporation; Notice of 
Designation of Commission Staff as Non-Decisional

    With respect to an order issued by the Commission today in the 
above-captioned docket, with the exceptions noted below, the staff of 
the Office of Enforcement are designated as non-decisional in 
deliberations by the Commission in this docket. Accordingly, pursuant 
to 18 CFR 385.2202 (2011), these persons will not serve as advisors to 
the Commission or take part in the Commission's review of any offer of 
settlement. Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.2201 (2011), they are prohibited from communicating with advisory 
staff concerning any deliberations in this docket.
    Exceptions to this designation as non-decisional are:

Larry Gasteiger
Bryan Craig
Roger Morie
David Applebaum
Teresina Stasko
Jeremy Medovoy

    Dated: June 4, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
 Secretary.
