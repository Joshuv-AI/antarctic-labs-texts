Employment and Training Administration

Cooper Crouse-Hinds Myers-Hub, Montebello, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 11, 2003 in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Cooper Crouse-Hinds Myers-Hub, 
Montebello, California.
    A petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 18th day of August, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
