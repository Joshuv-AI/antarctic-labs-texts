INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION
[Docket No. AB-6 (Sub-No. 359X)]

 

Burlington Northern Railroad Co.--Abandonment Exemption--In 
Mobile County, AL

AGENCY: Interstate Commerce Commission.

ACTION: Notice of exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to 49 U.S.C. 10505, the Commission exempts Burlington 
Northern Railroad Company (BN) from the prior approval requirements of 
49 U.S.C. 10903-10904 to permit BN to abandon its car barge service 
between Mobile and Blakely Island, AL, and the Blakely Island track 
between engineering station (ES) 0.00N to ES 58 + 98N and ES 0 + 00S to 
ES 103 + 53S, a total of 3.08 miles. The exemption will be subject to 
standard labor protective conditions.

DATES: Provided no formal expression of intent to file an offer of 
financial assistance has been received, this exemption will be 
effective on May 13, 1994. Formal expressions of intent to file an 
offer1 of financial assistance under 49 CFR 1152.27(c)(2) must be 
filed by April 25, 1994. Petitions to stay must be filed by April 28, 
1994. Requests for a public use condition in conformity with 49 CFR 
1152.28(a)(2) must be filed by May 3, 1994. Petitions to reopen must be 
filed by May 9, 1994

    .\1\ See Exempt. of Rail Line Abandonment--Offers of Finan. 
Assist., 4 I.C.C.2d 164 (1987).
