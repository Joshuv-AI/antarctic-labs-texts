Petitions for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

    Petitions for Reconsideration have been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR section 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room CYA257, 445 12th Street, SW., 
Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, Qualex International (202) 8632893. Oppositions to these 
petitions must be filed by September 19, 2002. See section 1.4(b)(1) of 
the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition 
must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has 
expired.
    Subject: In the Matter of Reallocation of the 216220 MHz, 13901395 
MHz, 14271429 MHz, 14291432 MHz, 14321435 MHz, 16701675 MHz, and 
23852390 MHz Government Transfer Bands (WT Docket No. 028)
    Number of Petitions filed: 5.
