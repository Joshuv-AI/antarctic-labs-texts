DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the rescheduling of the meeting of the Ad 
Hoc Clearinghouse Subcommittee of the National Deafness and Other 
Communication Disorders Advisory Council, the notice of which was 
published in the Federal Register 60 FR 55849 on November 3, 1995. This 
meeting could not be convened on November 16 due to the partial 
shutdown of the Federal Government. It is rescheduled for December 18 
from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., as a telephone conference call 
originating in room 3C05, Building 31, 9000 Rockville Pike, Bethesda, 
Maryland. The meeting will be open to the public, limited to space 
available.

    Dated: December 4, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
