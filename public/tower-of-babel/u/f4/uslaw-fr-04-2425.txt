DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board, Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
committee meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date(s) of Meeting: 5 & 6 February 2004.
    Time(s) of Meeting: 0800-1700, 5 February 2003; 0800-1700, 6 
February 2003.
    Place: Hilton Hotel, Crystal City, VA.
    1. Agenda: The Army Science Board FY04 Summer Studies, Force 
Balance and FCS Urban Operations are holding a plenary meeting on 
5&6 of February 2004. The meeting will be held at the Hilton Hotel 
in Crystal City, VA. The meeting will begin at 0800 hrs. on the 5th 
and will end at approximately 1700 hrs. on the 6th. For further 
information regarding Force Balance, please contact LTC Al Klee at 
(703) 601-0676 or e-mail at [email protected]. For 
FCS Urban Operations, please contact MAJ Al Visconti at (865) 574-
8798 or e-mail at [email protected].

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
