NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice (99-056)]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Rohm and Haas Company of 
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, has applied for an exclusive license to 
practice the invention described and claimed in U.S. Patent No. 
5,658,649, entitled ``Corrosion Resistant Coating,'' (NASA Case No. 
KSC-11600) which is assigned to the United States of America as 
represented by the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration. Written objections to the prospective grant of a 
license should be sent to the Kennedy Space Center.

DATES: Responses to this notice must be received by June 7, 1999.
