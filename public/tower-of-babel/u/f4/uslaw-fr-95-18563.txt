FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 92-245; RM-8026]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Frederiksted, VI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission dismisses the petition for rule making filed by 
Jose J. Arzuaga, proposing the allotment of Channel 298A at 
Frederiksted, Virgin Islands, as its second local FM transmission 
service. See 57 FR 54543 November 19, 1992. The petitioner has 
abandoned its interest in a Class A allotment at Frederiksted, and 
there are no other timely expressions of interest for the channel. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon P. McDonald, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
