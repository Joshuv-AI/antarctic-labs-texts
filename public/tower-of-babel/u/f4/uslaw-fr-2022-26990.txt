NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Notice of Sunshine Act Meeting Cancellation

    The National Transportation Safety Board has cancelled the virtual

[[Page 76090]]

Sunshine Act meeting previously scheduled for Tuesday, December 13, 
2022. The matter scheduled to be considered at the Sunshine Act meeting 
was the Safety Research Report--Alcohol, Other Drug, and Multiple Drug 
Use Among Drivers.
