DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
 

Special Medical Advisory Group (SMAG); Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Special Medical Advisory Group 
(SMAG) has been renewed for a period beginning November 17, 1993, 
through November 17, 1995.

    Dated: May 31, 1994.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
