DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

PJM Interconnection, L.L.C., Duke Energy Ohio, Inc., Duke Energy 
Kentucky, Inc; Notice of Filing

    Take notice that on April 5, 2012, Duke Energy Ohio, Inc. and Duke 
Energy Kentucky, Inc., tendered for filing a Settlement Agreement on 
behalf of themselves and the Indiana Municipal Power Agency, pursuant 
to 18 CFR 385.602, in the above-docketed proceeding. Pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.602(f)(2) initial comments on the settlement agreement should be 
filed on or before April 18, 2012. Reply comments are due on or before 
April 20, 2012.

    Dated: April 12, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
