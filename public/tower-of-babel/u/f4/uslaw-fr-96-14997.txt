NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Public Hearing in Maryland: Railroad Accident

    In connection with its investigation of the collision and 
derailment of a MARC commuter passenger train with AMTRAK Train 29, The 
Capitol Limited, near Silver Spring, Maryland, on February 16, 1996, 
the National Transportation Safety Board will convene a public hearing 
at 9:00 a.m. (local time), on June 26, 1996, at the DoubleTree Hotel, 
1750 Rockville Pike (Route 355), Rockville, Maryland 20852. For more 
information, contact Pat Cariseo, Office of Public Affairs, Washington, 
D.C. 20594, telephone (202) 382-0660.

    Dated: June 7, 1996.
Bea Hardesty,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
