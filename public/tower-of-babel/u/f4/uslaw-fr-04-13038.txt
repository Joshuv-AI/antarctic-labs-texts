[FCC 04-116; MM Docket No. 89-120]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Cuba, Eldon, Lake Ozark, Northwye, 
and Waynesville, MO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration, dismissed.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission dismissed a petition for reconsideration filed 
by Lake Broadcasting, the former licensee, inter alia, of Station 
KBMX(FM), Eldon, Missouri. The Commission held that the petition for 
reconsideration, seeking to upgrade the class of the Eldon station, was 
moot because the revocation of its license had become final and because 
a federal appellate court ruled that no new proceedings regarding the 
revocation were warranted. See 67 FR 21182 (April 30, 2002).

DATES: Effective June 9, 2004.
