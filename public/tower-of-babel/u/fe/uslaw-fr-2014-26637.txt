DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Performance Review Board Membership

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Performance Review Board Membership list published in the 
Federal Register on Friday, October 17, 2014 (79 FR 62432) is amended 
to include the following individual: Mr. Gabriel O. Camarillo, 
Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary of the Army (Acquisition, 
Logistics and Technology), Office of the Assistant Secretary of the 
Army (Acquisition, Logistics and Technology).
    The Department of the Army Performance Review Board will be 
composed of a subset of the individuals listed here and in the Federal 
Register on October 17, 2014.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barbara Smith, Civilian Senior Leader 
Management Office, 111 Army Pentagon, Washington, DC 20310-0111.

Brenda S. Bowen,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
