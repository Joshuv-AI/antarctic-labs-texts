NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Nuclear Waste, Subcommittee Meeting on 
Planning and Procedures; Notice of Meeting

    The ACNW Subcommittee on Planning and Procedures will hold a 
meeting on September 24, 2004, at the Suncoast Hotel (Fairway 2 Room), 
9090 Alta Drive, Las Vegas, Nevada.
    The entire meeting will be closed to public attendance pursuant to 
5 U.S.C. 552b(c) (2) and (6) to discuss organizational and personnel 
matters that relate solely to internal personnel rules and practices of 
ACNW, and information the release of which would constitute a clearly 
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.
    The Subcommittee will continue to discuss self-assessment of ACNW 
performance in CY 2004, potential operational areas for improved 
effectiveness, and other activities related to the conduct of ACNW 
business.
    Further information regarding this meeting can be obtained by 
contacting Mr. Howard J. Larson, Assistant Director for ACNW/Team 
Leader (telephone 301/415-6805), between 7:30 a.m. and 4 p.m. (e.t.).

    Dated: September 9, 2004.
Michael R. Snodderly,
Acting Associate Director for Technical Support, ACRS/ACNW.
