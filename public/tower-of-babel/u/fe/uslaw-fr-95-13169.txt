DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

National Assessment Governing Board; Meeting

AGENCY: National Assessment Governing Board, Education.

ACTION: Amendment to notice of a teleconference meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This amends the notice of a meeting of the Achievement Levels 
Committee of the National Assessment Governing Board published on May 
23, 1995 in 60 FR 27279. The date and time of the June 12, 1995 
teleconference meeting of the Achievement Levels Committee of the 
National Assessment Governing Board has been changed. The Committee 
will meet on June 15, 1995, between the hours of 12 noon to 2 p.m., 
(et). The meeting location is unchanged.

DATES: June 15, 1995.

TIME: 12 Noon to 2 p.m. (et).

LOCATION: 800 North Capitol Street, NW., Suite 825, Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mary Ann Wilmer, Operations Officer, National Assessment Governing 
Board, Suite 825, 800 North Capitol Street, NW., Washington, DC 20002-
4233. Telephone--202-357-6938.

    Dated: May 24, 1995.
Roy Truby,
Executive Director, National Assessment Governing Board.
