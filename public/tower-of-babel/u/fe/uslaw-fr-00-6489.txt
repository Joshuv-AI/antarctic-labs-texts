Airworthiness Directives; General Electric Company (GE) CF34 
Series Turbofan Engines; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to Airworthiness Directive 
(AD) 2000-03-03 that is applicable to certain General Electric Company 
CF34 series turbofan engines that was published in the Federal Register 
on February 7, 2000 (65 FR 5759). Two subpart names and two mandatory 
inspections were inadvertently omitted from the Mandatory Inspection 
Requirements of Table 804. This document corrects this Table by adding 
the subparts and inspections.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 23, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kevin Donovan, Aerospace Engineer, 
Engine Certification Office, FAA, Engine and Propeller Directorate, 12 
New England Executive Park, Burlington, MA 01803-5299; telephone (781) 
238-7743, fax (781) 238-7199.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: A final rule airworthiness directive 
applicable to General Electric Company CF34 series turbofan engines, 
was published in the Federal Register on February 7, 2000 (65 FR 5759). 
In the compliance section, an omission was made in Table 804 of the 
Mandatory Inspection Requirements. This correction adds necessary 
subpart names and inspections to the mandatory inspection requirements:


Sec. 39.13  [Corrected]

    1. On page 5760, in AD 2000-03-03, table 804 is corrected to read 
as follows:
