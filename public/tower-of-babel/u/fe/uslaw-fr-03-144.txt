DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:
    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date(s) of Meeting: 21-23 January 2003.
    Time(s) of Meeting: 0800-1700, 21 January 2003, 0800-1700, 22 
January 2003, 0800-1700, 23 January 2003.
    Place: The Crystal City Marriott, Crystal City, VA.
    Agenda: The Force Protection Study of the Army Science Board is 
holding a meeting on 21-23 January 2003. The meeting will be held at 
The Crystal City Marriott, 1999 Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, VA 
22202. The meeting will begin at 0800 hrs on the 21st and will end at 
approximately 1600 hrs on the 23rd. For further information, please 
contact: Major Bob Grier--703-604-7478 or email 
[email protected].

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
