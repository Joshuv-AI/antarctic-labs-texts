Lambda Electronics, Inc., McAllen, Texas; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 14, 1999, in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Lambda Electronics, Inc., McAllen, Texas.
    On June 14, 1999, the petitioner formally requested that the 
petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case 
would service no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 14th day of June, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
