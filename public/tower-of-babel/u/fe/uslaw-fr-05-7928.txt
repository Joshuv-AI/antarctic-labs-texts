INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 731-TA-814 (Review)]


Creatine Monohydrate From China

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Termination of five-year review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The subject five-year review was initiated in January 2005 to 
determine whether revocation of the antidumping duty order on creatine 
monohydrate from China would be likely to lead to continuation or 
recurrence of material injury to a domestic industry. On April 11, 
2005, the Department of Commerce published notice that it was revoking 
the order effective February 4, 2005 because ``the domestic interested 
parties did not participate in this sunset review'' (70 FR 18366). 
Accordingly, pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 
U.S.C. 1675(c)), the subject review is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 4, 2005.
