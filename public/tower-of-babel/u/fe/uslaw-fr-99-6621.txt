DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Notification of Location and Hours of Operation for Armed Forces 
Discharge Review/Correction Board Reading Room

AGENCY: Army Review Board Agency.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In compliance with DoD Directive 1332.28D3f, the Secretary of 
the Army hereby gives notice of the location, hours of operation and 
similar types of information regarding the Reading Room. Effective 22 
April 1999, the Reading Room will be relocated from Room 2E123, the 
Pentagon to 1941 Jefferson Davis Highway, Crystal Mall #4, Room 227. 
The hours of operation will be each Thursday from 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 
p.m.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ed Troxell, Army Review Board Agency, 
1941 Jefferson Davis Highway, Crystal Mall #4, Arlington, VA 22202.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Discharge Review Board (DRB) documents are 
made available for public inspection and copying, and are located in 
the Reading Room. The documents are indexed in a usable and concise 
format so as to enable the public, the applicant and/or those who 
represent applicants, to locate a decision document that is similar in 
circumstance or reasons for which the DRB or the Secretary concerned 
granted or denied relief.
Gregory D. Showalter,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
