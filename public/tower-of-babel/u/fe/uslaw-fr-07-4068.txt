DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, September 26, 2007, 8 a.m. to 
September 27, 2007, 5 p.m., Bethesda Marriott, 5151 Pooks Hill Road, 
Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on July 
31, 2007, 72 FR 41760.
    The meeting notice is amended to reflect the change in the name of 
the committee from ``SPORE in Lymphoma, Prostate, Breast & Skin 
Cancers'' to ``SPORE in Lymphoma, Prostate, Breast, Skin, Leukemia & GI 
Cancers''. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: August 13, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
