SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Economic Injury Disaster 9X06]


State of Oregon

    Deschutes and Jefferson Counties and the contiguous counties of 
Crook, Harney, Klamath, Lake, Lane, Linn, Marion, Wasco and Wheeler in 
the State of Oregon constitute an economic injury disaster area as a 
result of a wildfire known as the B & B Complex wildfire that began on 
August 19, 2003 and continues to burn. The wildfire caused the closure 
of Highway 20/126 for approximately two weeks and has caused several 
businesses to suffer substantial economic losses. Eligible small 
businesses and small agricultural cooperatives without credit available 
elsewhere may file applications for economic injury assistance for this 
disaster until the close of business on June 28, 2004 at the address 
listed below or other locally announced locations: U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Disaster Area 4 Office, P.O. Box 13795, Sacramento, CA 
95853-4795.
    The interest rate for eligible small businesses and small 
agricultural cooperatives is 3.100 percent.
    The number assigned for economic injury for this disaster is 
9X0600.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59002.)
