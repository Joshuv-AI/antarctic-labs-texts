COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the U.S. Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled 
for 18 October 2018, at 9:00 a.m. in the Commission offices at the 
National Building Museum, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 401 F Street NW, 
Washington DC, 20001-2728. Items of discussion may include buildings, 
parks and memorials. Draft agendas and additional information regarding 
the Commission are available on our website: www.cfa.gov. Inquiries 
regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or oral statements 
should be addressed to Thomas Luebke, Secretary, U.S. Commission of 
Fine Arts, at the above address; by emailing [email protected] ; or by 
calling 202-504-2200. Individuals requiring sign language 
interpretation for the hearing impaired should contact the Secretary at 
least 10 days before the meeting date.

    Dated: September 21, 2018 in Washington DC.
Thomas Luebke,
Secretary.
