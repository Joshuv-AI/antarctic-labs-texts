DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of General Medical Sciences; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory General Medical Sciences Council, May 12, 2021, 08:30 a.m. to 
May 13, 2021, 05:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Natcher 
Building, 45 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in 
the Federal Register on December 31, 2020, 85 FR 86942.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date of the meeting 
from May 12-13, 2021 to May 20, 2021. The meeting is partially Closed 
to the public.

    Dated: February 22, 2021.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
