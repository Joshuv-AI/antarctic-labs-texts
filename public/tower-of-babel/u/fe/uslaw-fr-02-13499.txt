DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP98-39-024]


Northern Natural Gas Company; Notice of Filing of Annual Report

May 23, 2002.
    Take notice that on May 20, 2002, Northern Natural Gas Company 
(Northern) submitted its annual report pursuant to the Commission's 
Order in Public Service Company of Colorado, et al., Docket Nos. RP97-
369-000, et al.
    Northern further states that copies of the filing have been mailed 
to each of its affected jurisdictional sales customers and state 
commissions.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before May 30, 2002. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection. This filing may also be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select ``Docket'' and 
follow the instructions (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). Comments, 
protests and interventions may be filed electronically via the Internet 
in lieu of paper. See, 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the

[[Page 37794]]

instructions on the Commission's web site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
