DENALI COMMISSION


Withdrawal of Denali Commission Fiscal Year 2026 Draft Work Plan

AGENCY: Denali Commission.

ACTION: Notice; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 7, 2025, the Denali Commission published a Federal 
Register notice to allow public comment on the Denali Commission Fiscal 
Year 2026 Draft Work Plan. The notice is hereby withdrawn.

DATES: The document published at 90 FR 19284 on May 7, 2025, is 
withdrawn as of May 15, 2025.
