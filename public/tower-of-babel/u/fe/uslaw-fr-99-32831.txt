DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Southwest Oregon Province Interagency Executive Committee (PIEC) 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Southwest Oregon PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on 
January 6, 2000 at the Bureau of Land Management, 1300 Airport Lane, 
North Bend, Oregon. The meeting will begin at 9:00 a.m. and continue 
until 4:30 p.m. Agenda items to be covered include: (1) Province 
Advisory Committee (PAC) mission and improving PAC effectiveness; (2) 
Water Quality Management Planning update; and (3) Public Comment.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Direct questions regarding this 
meeting to Roger Evenson, Province Advisory Committee Coordinator, 
USDA, Forest Service, Umpqua National Forest, 2900 NW Stewart Parkway, 
Roseburg, Oregon 97470, phone (541) 957-3344.

    Dated: December 14, 1999.
Don Ostby,
Designated Federal Official.
