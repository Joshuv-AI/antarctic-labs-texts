Agency Information Collection Activities: Final Collection; 
Comment Request

AGENCY: Export-Import Bank of the United States.

ACTION: Submission for OMB review and comments request.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Export-Import Bank of the United States (EXIM), as a part 
of its continuing effort to reduce paperwork and respondent burden, 
invites the general public and other Federal Agencies to comment on the 
proposed information collection, as required by the Paperwork Reduction 
Act of 1995.
    This form is to be completed by EXIM borrowers as required under 
certain EXIM long-term guarantee and direct loan transactions in 
conjunction with a borrower's request for disbursement for U.S. goods 
and services. It is used to summarize disbursement documents submitted 
with a borrower's request and to calculate the requested financing 
amount. It will enable EXIM to identify the specific details of the 
amount of disbursement requested for approval to ensure that the 
financing request is complete and in compliance with EXIM's 
disbursement requirements. This form will be uploaded into an 
electronic disbursement portal.

DATES: Comments should be received on or before August 20, 2018 to be 
assured of consideration.

ADDRESSES: Comments may be submitted electronically on 
www.regulations.gov (EIB 18-04) or by email to [email protected], or 
by mail to Mia L. Johnson, Export-Import Bank of the United States, 811 
Vermont Ave. NW, Washington, DC 20571. The form can be viewed at: 
https://www.exim.gov/sites/default/files/pub/pending/eib18-04_itemized_statement_of_payments-us_costs_form_-_final.xlsx.
