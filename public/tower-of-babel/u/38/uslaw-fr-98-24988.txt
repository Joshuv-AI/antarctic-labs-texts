CENSUS MONITORING BOARD


Meeting

AGENCY: U.S. Census Monitoring Board.

ACTION: Notice of Cancellation of Public Hearing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice hereby cancels the Sept. 18 public hearing of the 
U.S. Census Monitoring Board. The original hearing notice was published 
on Sept. 10, 1998 in the Federal Register (FR Doc. 98-24258) Vol. 63, 
No. 175 (63 FR 48467). The hearing will be rescheduled in the near 
future.

FOR MORE INFORMATION CONTACT: Carrie Hyun, Communications Director, 
Presidential Members, U.S. Census Monitoring Board, Phone 301/457-9903 
or Michael Miguel, Press Secretary, Congressional Members, U.S. Census 
Monitoring Board, Phone 301/457-5080.
Mark R. Johnson,
Executive Director, Presidential Members.
