DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, September 24, 2020, 11:00 a.m. 
to September 24, 2020, 2:30 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD, 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on August 11, 2020, 85 FR 48546.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting name from 
``National Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel Provocative Question 
7'' to ``National Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel SEP-7: 
Research Answers to NCI Provocative Questions''. The meeting is closed 
to the public.

    Dated: August 12, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
