FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Additional Item To Be Considered at Open 
Commission Meeting, Thursday, November 3, 2005

November 1, 2005.
    The Federal Communications Commission will consider one additional 
item on the subject listed below at the Open Meeting on Thursday, 
November 3, 2005, which is scheduled to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room 
TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, SW., Washington, DC.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Item No.              Bureau                     Subject
------------------------------------------------------------------------
4...............  Enforcement..........  Title: Review of the Emergency
                                          Alert System (EB Docket No. 04-
                                          296).
                                         Summary: The Commission will
                                          consider a First Report and
                                          Order and Further Notice of
                                          Proposed Rulemaking concerning
                                          the Emergency Alert System
                                          rules.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The prompt and orderly conduct of Commission business permits less 
than 7-days notice be given for consideration of this item.
    Action by the Commission, November 1, 2005. Chairman Martin; 
Commissioners Abernathy, Copps, and Adelstein voting to consider this 
item.
    Additional information concerning this meeting may be obtained from 
Audrey Spivack or David Fiske, Office of Media Relations, (202) 418-
0500; TTY 1-888-835-5322.
