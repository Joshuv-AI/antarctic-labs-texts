INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION

[Ex Parte No. 290 (Sub No. 5) (95-2)]


Quarterly Rail Cost Adjustment Factor

AGENCY: Interstate Commerce Commission.

ACTION: Approval of rail cost adjustment factor and decision.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission has approved a second quarter 1995 rail cost 
adjustment factor (RCAF) and cost index filed by the Association of 
American Railroads. The second quarter RCAF (Unadjusted) is 1.070. The 
second quarter RCAF (Adjusted) is 0.820, a decrease of 0.2% from the 
first quarter 1995 RCAF (Adjusted). Maximum second quarter 1995 RCAF 
rate levels may not exceed 99.8% of maximum first quarter 1995 rate 
levels.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 1, 1995.

5721.
