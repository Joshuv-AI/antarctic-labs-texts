named under FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT. Interested persons are

will be considered by the Engine and Propeller Directorate, Aircraft 
Certification Service, before issuance of the final AC.

[[Page 16516]]

Background

    This AC is on the subject of continued airworthiness of aircraft 
engines type certificated under Title 14 of the Code of Federal 
Regulations, part 33 (14 CFR part 33). The information and guidance 
presented in this AC would provide a method that can be used to 
demonstrate compliance with the requirements of Sec. 33.4 and Appendix 
A to part 33--Instructions for Continued Airworthiness.
    This advisory circular would be published under the authority 
granted to the Administrator by 49 U.S.C. 106(g), 40113, 44701-44702, 
44704 and would provide guidance for the requirements in 14 CFR part 
33.
