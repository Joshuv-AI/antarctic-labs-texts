SUMMARY: The rule entitled Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program: 
Trafficking Controls and Fraud Investigations was published on August 
21, 2013. The ICR for this rule approved the creation of a new 
information collection, which has been assigned the OMB Control Number 
0584-0587. The Office of Management and Budget cleared the associated 
ICR on September 23, 2013. This document announces the approval of the 
ICR.

DATES: The ICR associated with the rule published in the Federal 
Register on August 21, 2013, at 78 FR 51649, was approved by OMB on 
September 23, 2013, under OMB Control Number 0584-0587.
