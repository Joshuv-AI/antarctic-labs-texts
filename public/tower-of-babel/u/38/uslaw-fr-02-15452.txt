Employment and Training Administration

Fashion Sportswear Corp., Fall River, MA; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 13, 2002 in response to a worker petition, which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Fashion Sportswear Corp., Fall River, 
Massachusetts.
    Two of three workers did not complete their contact information 
(name, address, telephone number, and date of separation), as required 
in the TAA petition form. The petition is therefore, deemed invalid. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
