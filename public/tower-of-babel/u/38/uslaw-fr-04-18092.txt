SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3598; Amdt. 1]


State of New Jersey

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency--effective July 
23, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to establish 
the incident period for this disaster as beginning July 12, 2004 and 
continuing through July 23, 2004.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is September 14, 2004, and for 
economic injury the deadline is April 18, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: August 2, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
