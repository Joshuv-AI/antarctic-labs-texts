ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Notice

AGENCY:  United States Election Assistance Commission.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

DATES AND TIME: Thursday, June 30, 2005, 9:30 a.m.-10:30 a.m.

PLACE: New York Marriott Marquis, 1535 Broadway, 16th Floor Sky Lobby, 
New York, NY 10036, (212) 398-1900, (Subway Stop: MTA 42nd Street).
