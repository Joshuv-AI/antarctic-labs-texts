Caribbean Fishery Management Council 185th Public Hybrid Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public hybrid meeting (in-person/virtual).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council (CFMC) will hold the 
185th public hybrid meeting to address the items contained in the 
tentative agenda included in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

DATES: The 185th CFMC public hybrid meeting will be held on December 4-
5, 2024, from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., AST. A closed session will be held 
on December 4, 2024, from 4:45 p.m. to 5:15 p.m., AST, to discuss 
personnel matters. The Council will reconvene on December 5, 2024, from 
9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., AST.
