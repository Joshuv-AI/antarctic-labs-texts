NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act; Notice of Agency Meeting

Time and Date: 1:00 p.m., Wednesday, April 12, 2017.

Place: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047, 1775 Duke Street, Alexandria, 
VA 22314-3428.

Status: Closed.
    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act,'' notice is hereby given that the NCUA Board unanimously 
determined that agency business required holding a closed meeting with 
less than seven days' notice to the public, and that no earlier notice 
of the meeting was possible.

Matters to be Considered: 
    1. Consideration of Supervisory Action. Closed pursuant to 
Exemptions (8), (9) and (9)(ii).

For Further Information: Contact Gerard Poliquin, Secretary of the 
Board, Telephone: 703-518-6304.

Gerard Poliquin,
Secretary of the Board.
