COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS

 

Amendment to Notice of Public Meeting of the Arizona Advisory 
Committee

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the rules and 
regulations of the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights, that a meeting of 
the Arizona Advisory Committee to the Commission announced at FR Doc 
94-2941, 59 FR 5984, published February 9, 1994, will convene at 1 p.m. 
and adjourn at 4 p.m. on February 26, 1994, at the Hyatt Regency Hotel, 
Russell Room, 122 North 2nd Street, Phoenix, Arizona 85004. (This 
amendment is for change of location and time only.)

    Dated at Washington, DC, February 18, 1994.
Carol-Lee Hurley,
Chief, Regional Programs Coordination Unit.
