RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Notification of Meeting

    The railroad Retirement Board hereby gives notice that the Board 
will meet at 10:00 a.m., April 3, 2002, in the Board Room on the 8th 
floor of the agency's headquarters building located at 844 N. Rush 
Street, Chicago, Illinois. The subject to be addressed at this meeting 
is the Selection to Fill Vacancy in the Philadelphia District Office.
    The entire meeting will be closed to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: March 29, 2002.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
