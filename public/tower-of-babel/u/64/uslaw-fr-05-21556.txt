Radio Broadcasting Services; Cimarron, Las Vegas and Pecos, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 69 FR 35561 
(June 25, 2004) this Report and Order reallots Channel 264C3, Station 
KLVF(FM) (``KLVF''), Las Vegas, New Mexico, to Pecos, New Mexico, and 
modifies Station KLVF's license accordingly. The coordinates for 
Channel 264C3 at Pecos, New Mexico, are 35-40-48 NL and 105-32-26 WL, 
with a site restriction of 16.9 kilometers (10.5 miles) northeast of 
Pecos. The Report and Order also allots Channel 296A to Las Vegas, New 
Mexico, at coordinates of 35-36-33 NL and 105-09-31 WL, with a site 
restriction of 5.4 kilometers (3.3 miles) east of Las Vegas, New 
Mexico.

DATES: Effective November 28, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
