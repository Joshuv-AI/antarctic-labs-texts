DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Army


Intent To Prepare a Draft Environmental Assessment (EA) for 
Project Access Improvements on the Snake and Gros Ventre Rivers, 
Jackson Hole, Teton County, Wyoming

AGENCY: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: 59 FR 26214, May 19, 1994 announced that the Draft EA would be 
available on 23 May 1994. Due to unforeseen problems and controversies, 
the draft EA will not be available until mid September 1994.

ADDRESSES: Written comments concerning the draft EA should be addressed 
to the Chief, environmental Resources Branch, Chief of Engineers, Walla 
Walla District, Walla, Walla, Washington 99362-9265.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Ms. Anneli Carlson, (509) 522-6606.

Kenneth L. Denton,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
