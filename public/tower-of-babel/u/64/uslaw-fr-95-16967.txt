FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Security for the Protection of the Public Financial 
Responsibility to Meet Liability Incurred for Death or Injury to 
Passengers or Other Persons on Voyages; Issuance of Certificate 
(Casualty)

    Notice is hereby given that the following have been issued a 
Certificate of Financial Responsibility to Meet Liability Incurred for 
Death or Injury to Passengers or Other Persons on Voyages pursuant to 
the provisions of Section 2, Public Law 89-777 (46 U.S.C. 817(d)) and 
the Federal Maritime Commission's implementing regulations at 46 CFR 
part 540, as amended: Silversea Cruises, Ltd., 110 East Broward Blvd., 
Fort Lauderdale, Florida 33301.

Vessel: SILVER WIND.

    Dated: July 6, 1995.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
