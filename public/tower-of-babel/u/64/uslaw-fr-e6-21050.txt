DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission and Commission Staff Attendance at 
Organization of Midwest ISO States Annual Meeting

December 4, 2006.

members of the Commission and Commission staff may attend the following 
Organization of Midwest ISO States Annual Meeting: December 14, 2006 
(11 a.m.-4 p.m.), Midwest ISO Headquarters, 701 City Center Drive, 
Carmel, IN 46032.
