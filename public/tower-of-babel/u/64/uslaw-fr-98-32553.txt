DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


National Electric and Magnetic Fields Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee 
Act (Pub. L. No. 92-463, 86 Stat. 770), notice is hereby given of a 
meeting of the National Electric and Magnetic Fields Advisory 
Committee.

DATES: Monday, December 14, 1998: 8:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m.

ADDRESSES: Crowne Plaza Hotel, 14th & K Street, N.W., Washington, DC 
20005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Imre Gyuk, EMF Program Manager, 
EE-14, 1000 Independence Avenue, S.W., Washington, D.C. 20585, (202) 
586-1482.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
    Purpose of the Committee: The National Electric and Magnetic Fields 
Advisory Committee (NEMFAC) advises the Department of Energy and the 
National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences (NIEHS) on the 
design and implementation of a five-year, National Electric and 
Magnetic Fields (EMF) Research and Public Information Dissemination 
(RAPID) Program. The Secretary of Energy, pursuant to Section 2118 of 
the Energy Policy Act of 1992, Pub. L. No.102-486, has overall 
responsibility for establishing the national program which includes 
health effects research, development of technologies to assess and 
manage exposures, and dissemination of information.

    Public Participation: The meeting is open to the public. Written
