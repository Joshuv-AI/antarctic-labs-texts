FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Notice of Proposals to Engage in Permissible Nonbanking 
Activities or to Acquire Companies that are Engaged in Permissible 
Nonbanking Activities; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 05-13211) published on page 
38931 of the issue for Wednesday, July 6, 2005.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of New York heading, the entry for 
Fubon Financial Holding Company, Ltd., Taipei, Taiwan, is revised to 
read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of New York (Jay Bernstein, Bank 
Supervision Officer) 33 Liberty Street, New York, New York 10045-0001:
    1. Fubon Financial Holding Co., Ltd., Taipei, Taiwan; to engage in 
limited securities activities through Fubon Securities USA LLC, 
Pasadena, California, and Fubon Asset Management USA, LLC, Arcadia, 
California, pursuant to sections 225.28(b)(6), (b)(7)(i), (b)(7)(ii), 
(b)(7)(iii), (b)(7)(v), and (b)(8)(i) of Regulation Y.
    Comments on this application must be received by July 21, 2005.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, July 6, 2005.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
