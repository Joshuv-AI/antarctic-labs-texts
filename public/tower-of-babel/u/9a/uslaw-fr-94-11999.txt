Importer of Controlled Substances; Registration

Register on March 28, 1994, (59 FR 14426), Sigma Chemical Company, 3500 
Dekalb Street, St. Louis, Missouri, 63118, made application to the Drug

to Section 1008(a) of the Controlled Substances Import and Export Act 
and in accordance with Title 21 Code of Federal Regulations 1311.42, 
the above firm is granted registration as an importer of the basic 
classes of controlled substances listed above.
