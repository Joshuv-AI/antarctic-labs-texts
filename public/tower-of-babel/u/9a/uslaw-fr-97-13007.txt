DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER97-2756-000]


Arizona Public Service Company; Notice of Filing

May 13, 1997.
    Take notice that on April 23, 1997, Arizona Public Service Company 
(``APS'') tendered for filing an amendment to its Open Access 
Transmission Tariff to reflect a Joint Trial Stipulation among the 
participants in the above-referenced proceedings.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedures (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before May 28, 1997. Protests 
will be

[[Page 27243]]

considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
