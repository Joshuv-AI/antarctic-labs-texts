Penske Logistics, Elliston, VA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 28, 2008 in response to a 
worker petition filed by a state agency representative on behalf of 
workers of Penske Logistics, Elliston, Virginia.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of March 2008.
 Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
