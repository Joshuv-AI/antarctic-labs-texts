Lego Systems, Inc., Shows & Events Department, Enfield, 
Connecticut; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 27, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Lego Systems, Inc., Shows 7 
Events Department, Enfield, Connecticut.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 9th day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
