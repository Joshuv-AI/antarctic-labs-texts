Bosch-Rexroth Corporation; Mobile Hydraulics Division Wooster, 
OH; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 18, 2005 in response to petition filed by the 
United Automobile, Aerospace, Agricultural Implement Workers of 
America, Local 1239 on behalf of workers at Bosch-Rexroth Corporation, 
Mobile Hydraulics Division, Wooster, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 1st day of June, 2005.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
