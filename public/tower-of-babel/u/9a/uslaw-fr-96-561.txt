DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Forest Service


Newspaper Used for Publication of Legal Notice, Comment and 
Appeal of Decisions for Pacific Northwest Region, Oregon and Washington

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Revised notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a revision to the notice which appeared in the Federal 
Register on May 11, 1995 (60 FR 25196). This notice listed the 
newspaper of record for the Forest Supervisor decision of the Okanogan 
National Forest (Washington) to be Omak Chronicle (Omak, Washington). 
This revised notice changes the principal newspaper for publication of 
legal notice of decisions (comment and appeal) for the Forest 
Supervisor of the Okanogan National Forest to The Wenatchee World 
(Wenatchee, Washington).

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 15, 1995.
