Office of the Secretary; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Interagency Pain Research Coordinating Committee, July 09, 2018, 02:00 
p.m. to July 09, 2018, 04:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Conference Room 6, Bethesda, MD, 20892

23283.

from 2-4 p.m. to 4-6 p.m. Date has not changed. The meeting is open to 
the public.
