DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management


Lower Snake River District; Notice of Meeting

SUMMARY: The Lower Snake River District Resource Advisory Council will 
hold two meetings to discuss and develop draft statewide standards for 
rangeland health and guidelines for managing livestock grazing on 
public lands. Public comment periods will be held at 1 p.m. on January 
25 and at 8 p.m. on February 15.

DATES: January 25, 1996 beginning at 8:15 a.m.; and February 15, 1996, 
beginning at 6:30 p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meetings will be held at the Idaho State Office of the 
Bureau of Land Management, 3380 Americana Terrace, Boise, Idaho, 83706.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barry Rose, Lower Snake River District 
Office (208-384-3393).
Barry Rose,
Public Affairs Specialist.
