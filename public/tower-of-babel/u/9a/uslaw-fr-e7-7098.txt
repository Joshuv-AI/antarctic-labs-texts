Gildan Activewear Malone, Inc., Bombay, NY; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 27, 2007 in response to a worker 
petition filed a company official on behalf of workers at Gildan 
Activewear Malone, Inc., Bombay, New York.
    The petitioner has withdrawn the petition. Thus, this investigation 
is terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of April 2007.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
