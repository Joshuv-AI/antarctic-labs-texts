Correction

    The final rule published on December 31, 2007 (72 FR 74169) is 
corrected as follows:
    1. In the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION section, on page 74170, in the 
first column, the table is corrected by:
    a. Removing the phrase ``(first time applicants only)'' after the 
phrase ``Passport Card Execution Fee;'' under paragraph (9)(c).
    b. Removing the words ``First Time'' in the line reading ``Total 
First Time Child'' under paragraph (9)(c).
    c. Removing the line ``Total renewal child'' in the first column 
and ``$10.'' in the second column under paragraph (9)(c).
