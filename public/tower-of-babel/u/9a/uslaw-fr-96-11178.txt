UNITED STATES INSTITUTE OF PEACE


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: United States Institute of Peace.

DATE/TIME: Thursday-Saturday, May 16-18, 1996.

LOCATION: Airlie Conference Center, Airlie, Virginia.

STATUS: Open Session--Portions may be closed pursuant to Subsection (c) 
of Section 552(b) of Title 5, United States Code, as provided in 
subsection 1706(h)(3) of the United States Institute of Peace Act, 
Public Law 98-525.

AGENDA: May Board Meeting and Annual Board/Senior Staff Program Review; 
Approval of Minutes of the Seventy-fifth Meeting of the Board of 
Directors; Chairman's Report; President's Report; Committee Reports; 
Approval of Solicited Grants; Selection of 1996-1997 Peace Scholars and 
Senior Fellows; Selection of 1996 National Essay Contest Winners; Other 
General Issues.

CONTACT: Dr. Sheryl Brown, Director, Office of Communications, 
Telephone: (202) 457-1700.

    Dated: May 1, 1996.
Charles E. Nelson,
Vice President for Management and Finance, United States Institute of
