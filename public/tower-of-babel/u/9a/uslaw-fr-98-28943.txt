DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP99-95-000]


CNG Transmission Corporation; Notice of Proposed Changes in FERC 
Gas Tariff

October 23, 1998.
    Take notice that on October 20, 1998, CNG Transmission Corporation, 
(CNG), tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Second 
Revised Volume No. 1, the following revised tariff sheets, with an 
effective date of November 23, 1998:

Sheet Nos. 142A, 153A, 162A and 173A

[[Page 58019]]

Original Sheet No. 309A

    CNG states that the purpose of this filing is to address pagination 
and supercession errors within Volume No. 1 of its tariff. At Sheet 
Nos. 142A, 153A, 162A and 173A, CNG seeks to establish reservation 
pages for sheets that were not effectively superseded by CNG's February 
13, 1998 compliance filing in Docket No. CP96-492. Original Sheet No. 
309A would recapture Sections 11B.4.B, 11B.4.C, and 11.B.4.D from the 
General Terms and Conditions. CNG states that it inadvertently omitted 
these subsections of its tariff during the repagination of this area as 
filed in Docket No. RP97-406-005.
    CNG states that copies of its letter of transmittal and enclosures 
are being mailed to CNG's customers and interested state commissions.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Sections 385.214 or 385.211 of the Commission's 
Rules and Regulations. All such motions or protests must be filed in 
accordance with Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection in the Public 
Reference Room.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
