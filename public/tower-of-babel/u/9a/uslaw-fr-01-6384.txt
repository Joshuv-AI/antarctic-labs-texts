DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Notice of Intent To Grant an Exclusive Patent License

    Pursuant to the provisions of Part 404 of Title 37, Code of Federal 
Regulations, which implements Public Law 96-517, as amended, the 
Department of the Air Force announces its intention to grant Allcomp 
Inc., a company doing business in City of Industry, CA, an exclusive 
license in the right, title and interest the Air Force has in U.S. 
Patent Number 5,752,773 entitled ``High Temperature Rolling Element 
Bearing,'' and in related invention disclosures concerning the same 
technical art.
    A license for this patent and related invention disclosures will be 
granted unless a written objection is received within 15 days from the 
date of publication of this Notice. Information concerning this Notice 
may be obtained from Mr. William H. Anderson, Associate General Counsel 
(Acquisition), SAF/GCQ, 1500 Wilson Blvd., Suite 304, Arlington, VA 
22209-2310. Mr. Anderson can be reached at 703-588-5090 or by fax at 
703-588-8037.

Janet A. Long,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
