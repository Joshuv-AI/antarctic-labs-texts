SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Little Rock District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Little Rock District Advisory Council, will 
hold a public meeting from 9:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., on Monday, May 1, 
1995, at the U.S. Small Business Administration Little Rock District 
Office, located at 2120 Riverfront Drive, Suite 100, Little Rock, 
Arkansas, to discuss such matters as may be presented by members, staff 
of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Valerie J. Coleman, Business 
Opportunity Specialist, U.S. Small Business Administration, at the 
above address, Tele: 501/324-5871, ext. 236.

    Dated: April 20, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Councils.
