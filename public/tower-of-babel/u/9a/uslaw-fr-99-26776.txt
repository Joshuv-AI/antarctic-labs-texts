NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[Docket No. 40-9027-MLA; ASLBP No. 99-757-01-MLA]


Cabot Performance Materials; Notice of Reconstitution

    Pursuant to the authority contained in 10 CFR 2.721 and 2.1207, the 
Presiding Officer in the captioned 10 CFR part 2, Subpart L proceeding 
is hereby replaced by appointing Administrative Judge Alan S. Rosenthal 
as Presiding Officer in place of Administrative Judge Peter B. Bloch.
    All correspondence, documents, and other material shall be filed 
with the Presiding Officer in accordance with 10 CFR 2.1203. The 
address of the new Presiding Officer is: Administrative

[[Page 55777]]

Judge Alan S. Rosenthal, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, U.S. 
Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Washington, DC 20555-0001.

    Issued at Rockville, Maryland, this 7th day of October 1999.
G. Paul Bollwerk III,
Chief Administrative Judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel.
