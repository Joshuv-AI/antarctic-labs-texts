POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--Global Plus 1B and 2B Negotiated 
Service Agreements

AGENCY: Postal Service \TM\.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Postal Service notice of its filing a request with the Postal 
Regulatory Commission to add Global Plus 1B and 2B Negotiated Service 
Agreements to the Competitive Products List pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642.

DATES: January 18, 2011.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Margaret M. Falwell, 202-268-2576.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[reg] hereby 
gives notice that it filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission, on 
December 9, 2010, Requests of United States Postal Service to Add 
Global Plus 1B and 2B Negotiated Service Agreements to the Competitive 
Product List, and Notice of Filing (Under Seal) the Enabling Governor's 
Decisions and Two Functionally Equivalent Agreements for each filing. 
Documents are available at http://www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. MC2011-7, 
MC2011-8, CP2011-39, CP2011-40, CP2011-41 and CP2011-42.
