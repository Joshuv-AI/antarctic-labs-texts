DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4480]


Department of State Performance Review Board Members (At Large 
Board)

    In accordance with section 4314 (c) (4) of the Civil Service Reform 
Act of 1978 (Pub. L. 95-454), the Executive Resources Board of the 
Department of State has appointed the following individuals to the 
Department of State Performance Review Board (At-Large):

Janice H. Brambilla, Senior Advisor, Office of the Under Secretary for 
Public Diplomacy and Public Affairs, Department of State;
David S. Mathias, Assistant Legal Advisor, Office of the Legal Advisor, 
United Nations Affairs, Department of State;
Cathleen E. Lawrence, Executive Director, Bureau of Nonproliferation, 
Department of State;
Lawrence R. Baer (Outside Member), Dean, School of Professional and 
Area Studies, Foreign Service Institute, Department of State;
William E. Todd, Controller/Executive Director, Bureau for 
International Narcotics and Law Enforcement, Department of State;
James L. Millette, Deputy Assistant Secretary, Bureau of Resource 
Management, Department of State.


[[Page 53776]]


    Dated: September 5, 2003.
Ruth A. Whiteside,
Acting Director General of the Foreign Service and Director of Human 
Resources, Department of State.
