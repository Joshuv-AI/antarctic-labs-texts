DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
Geological Survey

 

American Barrick Resources Corp.; Contribution Acceptance

AGENCY: U.S. Geological Survey, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the U.S. Geological Survey has 
accepted from American Barrick Resources Corporation a contribution of 
$10,500 to support geological studies of the occurrence of gold 
mineralization to northern Nevada that are being conducted by 
scientists in the Branches of Central Mineral Resources and Western 
Mineral Resources.

DATES: This notice is effective July 27, 1994.

ADDRESS: Information on the work is available to the public upon 
request at the following location: U.S. Geological Survey, Branch of 
Central Mineral Resources, Denver Federal Center, MS-935, P.O. Box 
25046, Denver, Colorado 80225-0046.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Dr. Charles Thorman of the U.S. Geological Survey, Branch of Central 
Mineral Resources, at the address given above; telephone 303/236-5601.
Benjamin A. Morgan,
Chief Geologist.
