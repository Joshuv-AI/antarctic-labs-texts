FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-1860; MM Docket No. 01-176; RM-10191]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Sykesville, PA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition for rule making 
filed on behalf of Sykesville Broadcasting, requesting the allotment of 
Channel 240A to Sykesville, Pennsylvania, as that community's first 
local aural transmission service. Coordinates used for this proposal 
are 41-03-01 NL and 78-49-21 WL. Sykesville is located within 320 
kilometers (200 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border and therefore, 
concurrence of the Canadian government to this proposal must be 
obtained.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 24, 2001, and 
reply comments on or before October 9, 2001.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 
20554. In addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties 
should serve the petitioner's counsel, as follows: A. Wray Fitch, III, 
Esq., Gammon & Grange, P.C., 8280 Greensboro Drive, 7th Floor, McLean, 
VA 22102-3807.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
