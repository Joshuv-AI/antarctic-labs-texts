DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. RP94-60-000 and RP94-60-001]

 

Transwestern Pipeline Co.; Settlement Conference

March 29, 1994.
    Pursuant to the Commission order which issued on December 30, 1993, 
a settlement conference will be held to resolve the issues raised in 
the above-captioned proceeding. The conference will be held on 
Thursday, April 14, 1994 at 10 a.m. in a room to be designated at the 
offices of the Federal Regulatory Commission, 810 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
