Agilent Technologies, Roseville, California; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 4, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Agilent Technologies, Roseville, California.
    The investigation revealed that one of the three petitioners was 
not an employee of the Roseville, California plant. Only a company 
official or authorized representative such as a union representative 
may submit a Trade Adjustment Assistance petition for more than one 
location of a company.
    Consequently further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 6th day of May, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
