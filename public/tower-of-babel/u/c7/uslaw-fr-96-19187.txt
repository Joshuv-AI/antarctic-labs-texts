SUMMARY: In notice document 96-15013 beginning on page 30033 in the 
issue of

[[Page 39441]]

Thursday, June 13, 1996, make the following correction:
    On page 30035 in the second column, the Geographic Markets listed 
as Project Funding Priorities were the Big Emerging Markets of 
Argentina, the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN--Brunei, 
Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam), 
Brazil, the Chinese Economic Area (Peoples Republic of China, Taiwan 
and Hong Kong), India, Korea (South), Mexico, Poland, South Africa and 
Turkey. Russia and the Newly Independent States were inadvertently 
omitted and should be added to this list of geographic markets.

    Dated: July 23, 1996.
Jerome S. Morse,
Director, Resource Management and Planning Staff, Trade Development.
