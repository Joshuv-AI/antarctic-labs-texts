Wesley Hall Furniture, Inc., Hickory, NC; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 28, 2009, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Wesley Hall Furniture, Inc., 
Hickory, North Carolina.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 6th day of May 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
