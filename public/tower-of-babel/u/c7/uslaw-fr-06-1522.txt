Radio Broadcasting Services; Ely and Spring Creek, Nevada

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule, dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of the Petitioner, this document dismisses a 
petition for rulemaking filed by Ruby Radio Corporation, licensee of 
Station KCLS(FM), Ely, Nevada, proposing the substitution of Channel 
269C1 for Channel 269C3 at Ely, the reallotment of Channel 269C1 from 
Ely to Spring Creek, Nevada, as the community's first local 
transmission service, and the modification of the license for Station 
KCLS(FM) to reflect the new community.
