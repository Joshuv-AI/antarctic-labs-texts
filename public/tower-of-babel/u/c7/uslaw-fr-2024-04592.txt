DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke Special Emphasis Panel, 
March 19, 2024, 9 a.m. to March 19, 2024, 6 p.m., National Institutes 
of Health, Neuroscience Center, 6001 Executive Boulevard, Rockville, MD 
20852, which was published in the Federal Register on February 23, 
2024, FR Doc 2024-03818, 89 FR 14080.
    This notice is being amended to change the date of this one-day 
meeting to a two-day meeting March 19, 2024, to March 20, 2024. The 
meeting time remains the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: February 29, 2024.
Lauren A. Fleck,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
