DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 5861]


International Security Advisory Board (ISAB) Meeting Notice--
Cancellation of July 12 Meeting

    The closed meeting of the International Security Advisory Board 
(ISAB) originally scheduled for July 12, 2007, and published as Public 
Notice 5759 in the Federal Register of June 14, 2007 (Volume 72, Number 
114, Pages 32939-32940), has been cancelled.
    For more information, contact Brandy Buttrick, Deputy Executive 
Director of the International Security Advisory Board, Department of 
State, Washington, DC 20520, telephone: (202) 647-9336.

    Date: July 3, 2007.
George W. Look,
Executive Director, International, Security Advisory Board, Department 
of State.
