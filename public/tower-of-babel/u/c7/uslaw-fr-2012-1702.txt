DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Alternative Medicine; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Council for Complementary and Alternative Medicine, February 
3, 2012, 8:30 a.m. to February 3, 2012, 4 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was 
published in the Federal Register on December 21, 2011, 76 FR 79202.
    This meeting has been amended so that the open session will begin 
at 10 a.m. instead of 10:45 am. The public comment period will be from 
3:30 p.m. to 3:45 p.m. The meeting is partially Closed to the public.

    Dated: January 20, 2012.
Jennifer S. Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
