Multi-Form, Inc., DBA Dack Industries, Bristol, CT; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 26, 2004, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Multi-
Form, Inc., dba Dack Industries, Bristol, Connecticut. The petition was 
filed more than one year after the subject firm was closed. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 27th day of February, 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
