Detroit Edison Company; Notice of Initiation of Proceeding and

December 2, 2003.

order in the above-referenced dockets initiating an investigation in 
Docket No. EL04-29-000 under section 206 of the Federal Power Act.

pursuant section 206(b) of the Federal Power Act, will be 60 days 
following publication of this notice in the Federal Register.
