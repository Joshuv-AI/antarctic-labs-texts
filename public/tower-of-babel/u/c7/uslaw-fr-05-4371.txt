Register of February 1, 2005, concerning an announcement of a committee

Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463). The meeting time has now been changed.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Taunya King, SERDP Program Office, 
901 North Stuart Street, Suite 303, Arlington, VA or by telephone at 
(703) 696-2124.
