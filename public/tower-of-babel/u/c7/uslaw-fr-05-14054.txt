47 CFR Part 73

Radio Broadcasting Services; Grand Isle and St. Albans, VT and 
Tupper Lake, NY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 70 FR 3667 
(January 26, 2005), this Report and Order upgrades Channel 272A, 
Station WLFE-FM, St. Albans, Vermont, to Channel 272C3, reallots 
Channel 272C3 to Grand Isle, Vermont, and modifies Station WLFE-FM's 
license accordingly. To accommodate the foregoing changes, this Report 
And Order substitutes Channel 271C3 for Channel 272A at FM Station 
WRGR, Tupper Lake, New York. The coordinates for Channel 272C3 at Grand 
Isle, Vermont are 44-44-07 NL and 73-30-57 WL, with a site restriction 
of 17.4 kilometers (10.8 miles) west of Grand Isle. The coordinates for 
Channel 271C3 at Tupper Lake, New York, are 44-07-21 NL and 74-31-52 
WL, with a site restriction of 12.6 kilometers (7.8 miles) southwest of 
Tupper Lake.

DATES: Effective August 8, 2005.

418-2180.
