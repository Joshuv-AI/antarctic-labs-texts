Radio Broadcasting Services; Big Sur, Chualar, and Dos Palos, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a counterproposal to allot Channel 240A 
to Big Sur, California, as its first local aural service, at reference 
coordinates 36-15-28 and 121-49-28. The document also denies a 
rulemaking petition to reallot and change the community of license for 
Station KSKD(FM), Channel 240A, from Dos Palos to Chualar, California, 
because this would not result in a preferential arrangement of 
allotments. See 66 FR 51361, published October 9, 2001.

DATES: Effective March 22, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Andrew J. Rhodes, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
