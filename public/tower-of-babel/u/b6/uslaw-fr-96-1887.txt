DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Request for Public Comments Regarding OMB Clearance Entitled 
Federal Acquisition and Community Right-to-Know

AGENCIES: Department of Defense (DOD), General Services Administration 
(GSA), and National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense, General Services Administration, 
and National Aeronautics and Space Administration have decided to 
withdraw a notice concerning Request for Public Comments Regarding OMB 
Clearance Entitled Federal Acquisition and Community Right-to-Know. 
This notice was published in the Federal Register on January 24, 1996 
(61 FR 1898).
