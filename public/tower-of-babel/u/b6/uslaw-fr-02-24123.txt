Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards; Accelerator Control

AGENCY: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM); correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the proposed regulatory text to a 
proposed rule published in the Federal Register of July 23, 2002 (67 FR 
48117), regarding the Federal motor vehicle safety standard for 
accelerator control systems. This correction provides correct 
references in four places to provisions in the test procedures. The 
comments to the proposed rule were due by September 23, 2002. Because 
of the changes in this correction document, we extend the comment 
period to October 7, 2002.
