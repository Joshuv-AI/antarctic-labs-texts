DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Black Hills National Forest Advisory Board

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of meeting of the Black Hills National 
Forest Advisory Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U. S. Department of Agriculture, Forest Service, Black 
Hills National Forest cancelled the October 16, 2013 meeting of the 
Black Hills National Forest Advisory Board (Board), due to the Federal 
Government furlough which began on October 1, 2013. The original Notice 
of Meeting for the October 16, 2013 meeting was published in the 
Federal Register, Volume 78, Number 187, Thursday, September 26, 2013, 
pages 59337-59338.
