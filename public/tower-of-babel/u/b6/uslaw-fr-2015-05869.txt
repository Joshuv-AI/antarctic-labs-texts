DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. IN15-5-000]


City Power Marketing, LLC; K. Stephen Tsingas; Notice of 
Designation of Commission Staff As Non-Decisional

    With respect to an order issued by the Commission on March 6, 2015 
in the above-captioned docket,\1\ with the exceptions noted below, the 
staff of the Office of Enforcement are designated as non-decisional in 
deliberations by the Commission in this docket. Accordingly, pursuant 
to 18 CFR 385.2202 (2014), they will not serve as advisors to the 
Commission or take part in the Commission's review of any offer of 
settlement. Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.2201 (2014), they are prohibited from communicating with advisory 
staff concerning any deliberations in this docket.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ City Power Marketing, LLC and K. Stephen Tsingas, 150 FERC ] 
61,176 (2015).
