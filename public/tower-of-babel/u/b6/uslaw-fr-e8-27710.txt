Notice of Correction to Notice of Federal Competitive Coal Lease 
Sale Offer, Colorado

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published a Notice of Federal 
Competitive Coal Lease Sale Offer in the Federal Register on October 
31, 2008 [73 FR 64981]. The DATES section of this notice incorrectly 
listed January 14, 2008 instead of January 14, 2009. The lease sale 
will be held at 10 a.m., Wednesday, January 14, 2009. Sealed bids must 
be received on or before 10 a.m., Wednesday, January 14, 2009.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kurt Barton at BLM Colorado State 
Office, 2850 Youngfield Street, Lakewood, Colorado 80215, or by 
telephone 303-239-3714.

Kurt Barton,
Solid Minerals Staff, Division of Energy, Lands and Minerals.
