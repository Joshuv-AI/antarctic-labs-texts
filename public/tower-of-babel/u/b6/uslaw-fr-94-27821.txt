DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Home Federal Savings Bank, Norfolk, VA; Notice of Replacement of 
Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Subdivision (C) of Sec. 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator Home Federal Savings Bank, Norfolk, Virginia 
(``Association''), with the Resolution Trust Corporation as sole 
Receiver for the Association on September 30, 1994.

    Dated: November 4, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
