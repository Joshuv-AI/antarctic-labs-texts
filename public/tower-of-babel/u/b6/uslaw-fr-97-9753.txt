GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Interagency Committee for Medical Records (ICMR); Revision of SF 
93, Medical Record--Report of Medical History

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration/ICMR is revising the SF 
93, Medical Record--Report of Medical History to update the information 
collected on the patient. You can obtain the updated form in three 
ways:

    From the ``U.S. Government Management Policy CD-ROM'';
    On the internet. Address: http://www.gsa.gov/forms, or;
    Through the Federal Supply Service using National Stock Number 
7540-00-181-8368 (revision 6-96).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Ms. Barbara Williams, General Services Administration, (202) 501-0581.

DATES: Effective April 16, 1997.
