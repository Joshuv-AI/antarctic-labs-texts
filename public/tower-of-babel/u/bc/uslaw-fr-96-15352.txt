Regulatory Review; Gas Pipeline Safety Standards

AGENCY: Research and Special Programs Administration (RSPA), DOT.

ACTION: Correction of amendment number of final rule document.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects the amendment number of the Final Rule 
document published in the Federal Register on Thursday, June 6, 1996 
(61 FR 28770). In the document heading on page 28770, the amendment 
number ``Amdt. 192-76'' is changed to read ``Amdt. 192-78.'' The Final 
Rule makes miscellaneous changes to the gas pipeline safety standards 
to provide clarity, eliminate unnecessary or burdensome requirements, 
and foster economic growth.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 8, 1996.
