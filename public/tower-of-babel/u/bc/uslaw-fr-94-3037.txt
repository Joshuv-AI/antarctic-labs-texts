Western Systems Power Pool; Informational Filing

February 4, 1994.

Pool (WSPP) filed certain information as required by May 13, 1993, 
letter order in the above-referenced proceeding. Copies of WSPP's 
informational filing are on file with the Commission and are available 
for public inspection.
