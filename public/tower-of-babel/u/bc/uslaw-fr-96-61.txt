DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

The Airport Traffic Control Tower at South Lake Tahoe, CA; Notice 
of Decommissioning

    Notice is hereby given that on December 31, 1995, federal funding 
will be withdrawn for the Airport Traffic Control Tower at South Lake 
Tahoe, California. Decommissioning efforts will be initiated on January 
1, 1996. This information will be reflected in the FAA Organization 
Statement the next time it is reissued.

(Sec. 313(a), 72 Stat. 752; 49 U.S.C. 1354)

    Issued in Lawndale, California on December 20, 1995
Lynore C. Brekke,
Acting Regional Administrator Western-Pacific Region.
