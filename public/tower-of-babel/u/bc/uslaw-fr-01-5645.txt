DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Grain Inspection, Packers and Stockyards Administration


Posting of Stockyards

    Pursuant to the authority provided under section 302 of the Packers 
and Stockyards Act (7 U.S.C. 202), it was ascertained that the 
livestock market named below was a stockyard as defined by Section 
302(a). Notice was given to the stockyard owner and to the public as 
required by Section 302(b), by posting notice at the stockyard on the 
date specified below, that the stockyard was subject to the provisions 
of the Packers and Stockyards Act, 1921, as amended (7 U.S.C. 181 et 
seq.).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Facility No., name, and location of
                 stockyard                         Date of posting
------------------------------------------------------------------------
GA-224 Dixie Livestock Market, Inc., Oak    June 26, 2000.
 Park, Georgia.
