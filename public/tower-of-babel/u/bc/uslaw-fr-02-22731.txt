-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) announces the 
availability of a final document, Ecological Risk Assessment for the 
Middle Snake River, Idaho (EPA/600/R-01/017, February 2002), prepared 
as a collaborative effort between the National Center for Environmental 
Assessment-Washington (NCEA-W), within EPA's Office of Research and 
Development; the Office of Environmental Assessment, Region 10 of the 
EPA; the University of Idaho; and the University of Illinois. 
Mathematical models and field observations were used to estimate 
ecological risks to rainbow trout, mountain whitefish, and white 
sturgeon from stressors such as reduced water flows and sedimentation. 
The report serves as an example of using ecological risk assessment to 
improve the use of sound science in watershed-scale decision making.
