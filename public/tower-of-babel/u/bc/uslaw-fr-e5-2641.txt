Eaton Corporation, Fluid Power Group, Vinita, OK; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 28, 2005 in response to petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Eaton Corporation, Fluid Power 
Group, Vinita, Oklahoma.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 11th day of May, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
