Kirin Cutting Service, Inc., San Francisco, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 11, 2006 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Kirin Cutting Service, Inc., San 
Francisco, California.
    The petition has been deemed invalid. Two of the three petitioners 
were separated from employment more than one-year prior to the date of 
the petition (August 11, 2006). Consequently, the investigation has 
been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 31st day of August 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
