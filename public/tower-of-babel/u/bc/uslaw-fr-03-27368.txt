Radio Broadcasting Services; Fortuna Foothills and Wellton, AZ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division dismisses a Petition for Rule Making filed 
by Dana J. Puopolo, requesting the allotment of Channel 240A to Fortuna 
Foothills, Arizona, as that community's first local aural transmission 
service. In order to accommodate this allotment, the petition for rule 
making also proposed the substitution of Channel 248A for vacant 
Channel 240A at Wellton, Arizona. See 68 FR 43705, July 24, 2003.
