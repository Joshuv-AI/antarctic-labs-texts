DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 16-17 April 1998.
    Time of Meeting: 0830-1700 (both days).
    Place: Association of the United States Army, 2425 Wilson 
Boulevard, Conference Main Room, Arlington, VA.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) 1998 Summer Study Group 
on ``Concepts and Technology for the Army Beyond 2010''

[[Page 13038]]

will meet to hear panel progress reports. These meetings will be 
open to the public. Any interested person may attend, appear before, 
or file statements with the committee at the time and in the manner 
permitted by the committee. For further information, please contact 
our office at (703) 604-7490.
Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
