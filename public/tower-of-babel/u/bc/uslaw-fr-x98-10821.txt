DELAWARE RIVER BASIN COMMISSION

18 CFR Part 401


Rules of Practice and Procedure

CFR Correction

    In Title 18 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 400 to End, 
revised as of April 1, 1998, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 6, Sec. 410.0 is corrected to read ``Sec. 401.0'' in the 
heading;
    2. On page 12, Sec. 401.35, paragraph (b)(18), second line, 
``specify'' is corrected to read ``specially'' and in paragraph (d), 
eighth line, ``Executor'' is corrected to read ``Executive Director'';
    3. On page 13, Sec. 401.36, paragraph (c), third line, insert the 
word ``sources'' after the word ``water'';
    4. On page 15, Sec. 401.73, paragraph (b), first line, the word 
``When'' is corrected to read ``Whenever'';
    5. On page 17, Sec. 401.82, paragraph (a), column 2, first line, 
``ins'' is corrected to read ``is'', and in the fourth and fifth line 
``Commissioner'' is corrected to read ``Commission or''.
