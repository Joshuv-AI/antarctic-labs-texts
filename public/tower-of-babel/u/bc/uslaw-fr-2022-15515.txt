Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2022-0786.
    Petitioner: Pemco World Air Services, Inc.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  25.807(g)(4).
    Description of Relief Sought: Petitioner is seeking relief from 14 
CFR 25.807(g)(4), which requires that there be at least 2 exits, 1 of 
which must be a Type II or larger exit, in each side of the fuselage 
when the airplane has a passenger seating configuration of 20 to 40 
seats. Specifically, the petitioner is proposing to modify Boeing Model 
737-700 airplanes into a flexible cargo-passenger configuration, known 
as a ``combi,'' of which the 24-passenger configuration would have 1 
oversized Type I exit on each side of the fuselage instead of 2 exits 
on each side of the fuselage (1 of which must be Type II or larger) as 
required by Sec.  25.807(g)(4).
