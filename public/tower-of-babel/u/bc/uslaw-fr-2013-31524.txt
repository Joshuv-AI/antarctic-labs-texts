Advisory Committee for Mathematical and Physical Sciences; Notice

1235, Arlington, Virginia 22230.

844-9416 (operator password: mpsac) The web address is: https://nsf.webex.com/nsf/j.php?ED=33883333&UID=501830907&PW=NNTVkZWU1MjAx&RT=MiMxMQ%3D%3D.

    Contact Person: Kelsey Cook, National Science Foundation, 4201

AND Caleb Autrey, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard,

    Minutes: Meeting minutes and other information may be obtained from

advice and recommendations concerning research in mathematics and 
physical sciences.

    Planning for April joint session with ACCI, and other April topics.
