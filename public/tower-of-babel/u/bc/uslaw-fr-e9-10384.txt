Employment and Training Administration

Autosplice, Inc.; San Diego, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    In accordance with Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
amended, an investigation was initiated on March 12, 2009 in response 
to a petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of 
Autosplice, Inc., San Diego, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
