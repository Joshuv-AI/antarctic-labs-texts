NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 03-075]


NASA Earth Science Information Systems and Services Subcommittee; 
Meeting

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Aeronautics and Space Administration announces a 
meeting of the Earth Science Information Systems and Services 
Subcommittee.

DATES: Wednesday, July 9, 2003, 8:30 a.m. to 5:45 p.m.; and Thursday, 
July 10, 2003, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.

ADDRESSES: Loews L'Enfant Plaza Hotel, 480 L'Enfant Plaza, Washington, 
DC 20024.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Martha Maiden, Code YF, National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration, Washington, DC 20546, 202/358-
1078.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting will be open to the public up to 
the seating capacity of the room. The agenda for the meeting is as 
follows:
    [sbull] Introduction, Opening Comments
    [sbull] Welcome, Charge to Earth Science Information Systems and 
Services Subcommittee (ESISS)

[[Page 38396]]

    [sbull] The New Face of Earth Science Enterprise (ESE) Data and 
Information Systems
    [sbull] Overview: From Earth Observation System Data and 
Information System(EOSDIS) to the next Decade
    [sbull] Overview: EOSDIS Current Operations, EOSDIS Maintenance and 
Development (EMD) Contract Award Status
    [sbull] Demonstration: The EOSDIS Core System (ECS) Ordering system
    [sbull] Demonstration: EOSDIS update: the EOS ClearingHouse (ECHO) 
system
    [sbull] Earth Science Information Partners (ESIPs) Federation 
Overview
    [sbull] Demonstration: Example of Federation capability 
(``Distributed Oceanographic Data System (DODS) live''?)
    [sbull] Overview: Science Investigator Processing System (SIPS) 
processing: An assessment
    [sbull] Moderate-Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) Data 
Analysis Processing System (MODAPS) (MODIS SIPS) presentation: Lessons 
learned
    [sbull] What is Strategic Evolution of ESE Data Systems (SEEDS)?
    [sbull] Overview: SEEDS strategy
    [sbull] SEEDS prototype: Precipitation Processing System (PPS)
    [sbull] Demonstration: PPS Live demonstration
    [sbull] Presentation: National Polar-orbiting Operational 
Environmental Satellite System (NPOESS)
    [sbull] Presentation: Climate Large-Array Systems and Services 
(CLASS)
    [sbull] Overview: Earth Science Technology Office (ESTO)--Selected 
Advanced Information Systems Technology (AIST) Projects
    [sbull] Demonstration: A maturing AIST technology project
    [sbull] Discussion: Major questions and issues
    It is imperative that the meeting be held on these dates to 
accommodate the scheduling priorities of the key participants. Visitors 
will be requested to sign a visitor's register.

June W. Edwards,
