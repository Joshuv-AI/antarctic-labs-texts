POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--Global Expedited Package Services--
Non-Published Rates

AGENCY: Postal Service\TM\

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing a request with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add Global Expedited Package Services 
Contracts--Non-Published Rates to the Competitive Products List 
pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642.

DATES: August 6, 2010.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Margaret M. Falwell, 703-292-3576.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice that it has filed with the Postal Regulatory 
Commission a Request of the United States Postal Service to add Global 
Expedited Package Contracts--Non-Published Rates to the Competitive 
Products List, and Notice of Filing (Under Seal) of Contract and 
Enabling Governors' Decision. Documents are available at http://www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. MC2010-29 and CP2010-72.

Neva R. Watson,
Attorney, Legislative.
