Pacific Gas and Electric Company, Sacramento Municipal Utility 
District; Notice of Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting 
Comments, Motions To Intervene, and Protests

    On March 25, 2020, Pacific Gas and Electric Company (transferor) 
and Sacramento Municipal District (transferee) filed a joint 
application for the transfer of license of the Chili Bar Hydroelectric 
Project No. 2155. The project is located on the South Fork American 
River in El Dorado County, California.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Chili Bar Hydroelectric Project from the transferor to the 
transferee.
