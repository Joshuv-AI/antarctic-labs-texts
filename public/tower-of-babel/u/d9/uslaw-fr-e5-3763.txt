Raybestos Automotive Components; a Subsidiary of Raytech 
Corporation, Sterling Heights, MI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 24, 2005 in response to a worker 
petition filed by United Automobile, Aerospace, & Agricultural 
Implement Workers of America, Local Union 771 on behalf of workers at 
Raybestos Automotive Components, a subsidiary of Raytech Corporation, 
Sterling Heights, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 22nd day of June, 2005.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
