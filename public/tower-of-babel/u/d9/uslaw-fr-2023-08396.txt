Affiliation and Lending Criteria for the SBA Business Loan 
Programs; Correction

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA or Agency) is 
correcting a final rule that appeared in the Federal Register on April 
10, 2023. The document issued a final rule that amended various 
regulations governing SBA's 7(a) Loan Program and 504 Loan Program, 
including regulations on use of proceeds for partial changes of 
ownership, lending criteria, loan conditions, reconsiderations, and 
affiliation standards, to expand access to capital to small businesses 
and drive economic recovery.

DATES: Effective May 11, 2023.
