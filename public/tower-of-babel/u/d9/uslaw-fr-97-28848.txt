and Stroke Special Emphasis Panel (Telephone Conference Call).
    Date: November 10, 1997.
    Time: 12:00 p.m.
    Place: National Institutes of Health, 7550 Wisconsin Avenue, 
Room 9C10, Bethesda, MD 20892.
    Contact Person: Dr. Paul Sheehy/Mr. Phillip Wiethorn, Scientific

Houston, TX 77030.

    Phone: (713) 796-0080.
    Contact Person: Dr. Katherine Woodbury, Scientific Review 
Administrator, NINDS, National Institutes of Health, 7550 Wisconsin 
Avenue, Room 9C10, Bethesda, MD 20892, (301) 496-9223.
    Purpose/Agenda: To review and evaluate a grant application.
