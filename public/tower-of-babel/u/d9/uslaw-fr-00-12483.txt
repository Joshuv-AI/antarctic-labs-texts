TENNESSEE VALLEY AUTHORITY


Environmental Impact Statement--Land Between the Lakes Public Use 
Plan, Lyon and Trigg Countries, Kentucky and Stewart County, Tennessee

AGENCY: Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA).

ACTION: Cancellation Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 1, 1995, TVA published in the Federal Register (60 FR 
11161-11162) a Notice of Intent (NOI) to prepare an Environmental 
Impact Statement (EIS) on alternatives for public use at the Land 
Between The Lakes (LBL) National Recreation Area. On October 1, 1999, 
pursuant to the Land Between The Lakes Protection Act of 1998, LBL was 
established as a unit of the National Forest System and administrative 
responsibility was transferred from TVA to the U.S. Department of 
Agriculture. The 1995 NOI is hereby rescinded.
