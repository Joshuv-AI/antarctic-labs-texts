DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Pathogenic 
Eukaryotes Study Section, February 9, 2006, 8 a.m. to February 10, 
2006, 5 p.m., Holiday Inn Georgetown, 2101 Wisconsin Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20007 which was published in the Federal Register on 
January 24, 2006, 71 FR 3871-3873.
    The meeting is cancelled due to a lack of quorum.

    Dated: February 3, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
