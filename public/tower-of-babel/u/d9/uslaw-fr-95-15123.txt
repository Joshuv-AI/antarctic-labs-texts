DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP93-49-000]


Paiute Pipeline Company; Notice of Settlement Conference

June 15, 1995.
    Pursuant to the Commission order which issued on January 19, 1993, 
and a notice of extension of time which issued on May 13, 1993, a 
settlement conference will be held to resolve the issues raised in the 
above-captioned proceeding.
    The conference will be held on Friday, June 23, 1995 at 10 a.m., in 
a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 810 First Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
