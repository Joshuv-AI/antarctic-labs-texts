Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Notice of Lien.
    OMB No.: 0970-0153.
    Description: Section 324 of PRWORA '96 (Pub. L. 104-193), requires 
DHHS to promulgate a standard lien form for use by the State CSE 
programs to secure delinquent child support obligations in interstate 
cases.
