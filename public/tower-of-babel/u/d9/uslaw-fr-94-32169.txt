DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[NAFTA-00132]

 

F.C.I. Freeman, South Dakota; Notice of Revised Determination on 
Reconsideration; Correction

    This notice corrects the notice for petition NAFTA-00132 which was 
published in the Federal Register on December 7, 1994 (59 FR 63111) in 
FR Document 94-30098. This revises the impact date on the 3rd from the 
last line of the first column on page 63111. The impact date should 
read ``December 8, 1993'' instead of June 7, 1993.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 21st day of December 1994.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
