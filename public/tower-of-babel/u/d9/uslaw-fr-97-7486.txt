FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY
[FEMA-1168-DR]


West Virginia; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster 
Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
State of West Virginia, (FEMA-1168-DR), dated March 7, 1997, and 
related determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 14, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Magda Ruiz, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of West Virginia, is hereby amended to include Public Assistance and 
Hazard Mitigation in those areas determined to

[[Page 14144]]

have been adversely affected by the catastrophe declared a major 
disaster by the President in his declaration of March 7, 1997:

    The counties of Braxton, Cabell, Calhoun, Clay, Gilmer, Jackson, 
Kanawha, Lincoln, Mason, Putnam, Roane, Tyler, Wayne, Wetzel, Wirt, 
and Wood for Public Assistance and Hazard Mitigation (already 
designated for Individual Assistance).

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance.)
Lacy E. Suiter,
Executive Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
