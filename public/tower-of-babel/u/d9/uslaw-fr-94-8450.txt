NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION
 

Advisory Panel for Systematic and Population Biology; Correction

    The dates for the meeting of the Advisory Panel for Systematic and 
Population Biology were incorrect. The correct dates are April 26-29, 
1994.
    The notice for this meeting originally appeared in the March 10th 
issue of the Federal Register, Vol. 59, No. 47, p. 11329.

    Dated: April 5, 1994.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
