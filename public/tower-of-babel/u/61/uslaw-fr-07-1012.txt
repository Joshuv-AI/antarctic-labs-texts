GOVERNMENT PRINTING OFFICE


Depository Library Council to the Public Printer Meeting

    The Depository Library Council to the Public Printer (DLC) will 
meet on Sunday, April 15, 2007, through Wednesday, April 18, 2007 in 
Denver, Colorado. The sessions will take place from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. on 
Sunday through Tuesday, and 8 a.m. to 12 noon on Wednesday. The meeting 
will be held at the Adam's Mark Hotel, 1550 Court Place, Denver, 
Colorado. The purpose of this meeting is to discuss the Federal 
Depository Library Program. All sessions are open to the public. The 
sleeping rooms available at the Adam's Mark Hotel will be at the 
Government rate of $127.00 (plus applicable state and local taxes, 
currently 14.85%) a night for a single or double. The Adam's Mark Hotel 
is in compliance with the requirements of Title III of the Americans 
with Disabilities Act and meets all Fire Safety Act regulations.

William H. Turri,
Acting, Public Printer of the United States.
