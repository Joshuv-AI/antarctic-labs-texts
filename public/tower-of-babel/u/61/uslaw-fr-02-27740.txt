Agency Information Collection Activities; Announcement of 
OMBApproval; State Enforcement Notifications

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration is announcingthat a 
collection of information entitled ``State EnforcementNotifications'' 
has been approved by the Office of Management andBudget (OMB) under the 
Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Peggy Robbins, Officeof Information 
Resources Management (HFA-250), Food and DrugAdministration, 5600 
Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857,301-827-1223.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register of August 9, 2002 
(67 FR 51860),the agency announced that the proposed information 
collection had beensubmitted to OMB for review and clearance under 44 
U.S.C. 3507. An agencymay not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not 
required to respond to, acollection of information unless it displays a 
currently valid OMB controlnumber. OMB has now approved the information 
collection and has assignedOMB control number 0910-0275. The approval 
expires on October 31,2005. A copy of the supporting statement for this 
information collectionis available on the Internet at http://www.fda.gov/ohrms/dockets.

    Dated: October 25, 2002.
