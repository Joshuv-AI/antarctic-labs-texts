DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-60,032]


Ford Motor Company Product Development; Dearborn, MI; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 7, 2006 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Ford Motor Company, Product 
Development, Dearborn, Michigan.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of September, 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
