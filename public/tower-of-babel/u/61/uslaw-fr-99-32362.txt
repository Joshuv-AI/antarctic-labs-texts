NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


National Science Board Delegation of Authority

AGENCY: National Science Foundation.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: As authorized by Sec. 5(e)(2) of the National Science 
Foundation Act of 1950, as amended, the National Science Board remakes 
its delegation of authority to the National Science Foundation Director 
to review, approve and take final action on grants, contracts, or other 
arrangements.

DATES: Approved November 18, 1999.

ADDRESSES: National Science Board, National Science Foundation, 4201 
Wilson Boulevard, Room 1225, Arlington, Virginia, 22207.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Marta Cehelsky, Executive Officer, 
NSB, (703) 306-2000.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.

    Dated: December 2, 1999.
Marta Cehelsky,
Executive Officer.
