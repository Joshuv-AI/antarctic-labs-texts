DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Patent and Trademark Office

37 CFR Part 1

 

Duty of Disclosure

CFR Correction

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    In title 37 of the Code of Federal Regulations, revised as of July 
1, 1993, on page 63, in Sec. 1.175, paragraph (b) was inadvertently 
removed. As reinstated, the text of paragraph (b) reads as follows:


Sec. 1.175  Reissue oath or declaration.

* * * * *
    (b) Corroborating affidavits or declarations of others may be filed 
and the examiner may, in any case, require additional information or 
affidavits or declarations concerning the application for reissue and 
its object.
