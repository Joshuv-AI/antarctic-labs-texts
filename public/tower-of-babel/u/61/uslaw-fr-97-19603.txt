DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Minority Business Development Agency


Business Development Center Applications: Baltimore

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) program to operate the Baltimore 
MBDC. The Baltimore, Maryland solicitation was originally published in 
the Federal Register, Monday, December 23, 1996, Vol. 61, No. 247, Page 
67531.
