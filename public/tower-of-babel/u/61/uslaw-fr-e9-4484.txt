Television Broadcasting Services; Waco, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of petitioner Comcorp of Texas 
License Corp. (``Comcorp''), the permittee of post-transition DTV 
channel 44, Waco, Texas, dismisses Comcorp's pending petition for 
rulemaking to substitute DTV channel 25 for post-transition DTV channel 
44 at Waco.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Adrienne Y. Denysyk, Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
