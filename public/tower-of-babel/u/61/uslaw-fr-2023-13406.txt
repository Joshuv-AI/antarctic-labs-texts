Table of Frequency Allocations and Radio Regulations; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 7, 2023, the Federal Communications Commission's 
Office of Engineering and Technology issued a final rule making non-
substantive editorial revisions to conform certain of the Commission's 
rules to the formatting requirements of the Code of Federal 
Regulations. This document corrects two typographical errors in the 
rule.

DATES: Effective July 7, 2023.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Patrick Forster, Office of Engineering 
and Technology, at (202) 418-7061, [email protected].

37318 in the Federal Register of Wednesday, June 7, 2023, the following 
corrections are made:
