Radio Broadcasting Services; Fisher and Thief River Falls, MN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 70 FR 17049 
(April 4, 2005), this Report and Order reallots Channel 262C1, Station 
KSNR(FM) (``KSNR'') from Thief River Falls, Minnesota, to Fisher, 
Minnesota, and modifies Station KSNR's license accordingly. The 
coordinates for Channel 262C1 at Fisher, Minnesota, are 47-58-38 NL and 
96-36-42 WL, with a site restriction of 24.2 kilometers (15.1 miles) 
northeast of Fisher.

DATES: Effective November 17, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
