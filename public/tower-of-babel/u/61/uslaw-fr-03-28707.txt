SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Public Law 94-409, that the Securities 
and Exchange Commission will hold the following meeting during the week 
of November 17, 2003:
    An Open Meeting will be held on Wednesday, November 19, 2003 at 2 
p.m., in Room 1C30, the William O. Douglas Room.
    The subject matter of the Open Meeting scheduled for Wednesday, 
November 19, 2003 will be:
    The Commission will consider whether to adopt amendments to 
Schedule 14A and Forms 10-Q, 10-QSB, 10-K, and 10-KSB under the 
Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and Form N-CSR under the Securities 
Exchange Act of 1934 and the Investment Company Act of 1940. The 
amendments would require expanded disclosure related to the operation 
of board nominating committees and new disclosure concerning security 
holder communications with board members.
    For further information, please contact Lillian Brown at (202) 824-
5250.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact: The Office of the Secretary at (202) 942-7070.

    Dated: November 12, 2003.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
