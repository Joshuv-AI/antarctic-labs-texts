Alice Falls Corporation, Alice Falls Hydro, LLC; Notice of 
Application for Transfer of License, and Soliciting Comments and 
Motions To Intervene

    On February 23, 2012, Alice Falls Corporation (transferor) and 
Alice Falls Hydro, LLC (transferee) filed an

[[Page 21762]]

application for transfer of license \1\ for the Alice Falls 
Hydroelectric Project, No. 5867, located on the AuSable River in 
Clinton and Essex counties, New York.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 25 FERC ] 62,015, Order Issuing License (Major Under 5MW) 
and Denying Competing Preliminary Permit Applications, issued 
October 5, 1983.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Alice Falls Hydroelectric Project from transferor to transferee.
