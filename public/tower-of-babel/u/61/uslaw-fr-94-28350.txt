Electric Clearinghouse, Inc.; Notice of Informational Filing

November 10, 1994.

filed certain information as required by the Commission's April 7, 
1994, letter order in Docket No. ER94-968-000. Copies of the Electric 
Clearinghouse, Inc. informational filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
