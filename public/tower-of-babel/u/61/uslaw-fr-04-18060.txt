ACTION: Notice of issuance of policy.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of PS-ACE100-2004-10023. 
ThisPolicy Statement clarifies the applicability of AC 43.13-1B, Change 
1 for flammability of electrical wire used in part 23 aircraft. 
Electrical wire listed in section 7 of AC 43.13-1B,Change 1 complies 
with Sec. Sec.  23.853 and 23.1359 and is acceptable for use in part 23 
aircraft without further testing. The draft policy statement was issued 
for Public Comment on March 26, 2004. When possible, comments received 
were used to modify the draft policy.

DATES: PS-ACE100-2004-10023 was issued by the Manager, Small Airplane 
Directorate on July 9, 2004.
