DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, June 
06, 2017, 08:00 a.m. to June 06, 2017, 06:00 p.m., NIEHS/National 
Institute of Environmental Health, Keystone Building, 530 Davis Drive, 
Research Triangle Park, NC 27709 which was published in the Federal 
Register on May 26, 2017, 82 FR 24363.
    This meeting is being amended to change the date from Tuesday, June 
6, 2017 to Thursday, July 6, 2017, 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 13, 2017.
Natasha M. Copeland,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
