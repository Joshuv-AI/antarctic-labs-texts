FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 08-29; MB Docket No. 07-143; RM-11381]


Radio Broadcasting Service; Charlo, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division grants a petition for rulemaking filed by 
Spanish Peaks Broadcasting, Inc. for a new allotment at Charlo, 
Montana. Channel 251C3 can be allotted at Charlo, Montana, in 
compliance with the Commission's technical engineering requirements, at 
47-32-20 North Latitude and 114-08-52 West Longitude with a site 
restriction of 11.3 kilometers (7.0 miles) north of Charlo, Montana.

DATES: Effective February 18, 2008.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth 
Street, SW., Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2187.
