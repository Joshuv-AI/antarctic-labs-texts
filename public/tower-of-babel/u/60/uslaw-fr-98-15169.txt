SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3078]


State of Tennessee; (Amendment #3)

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated May 18, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning 
on April 16, 1998 and continuing through May 18, 1998.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is June 19, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is January 20, 1999.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: May 21, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
