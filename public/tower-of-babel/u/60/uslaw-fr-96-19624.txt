DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Renewal of the U.S. Automotive Parts Advisory Committee

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Commerce.

ACTION: Renewal of the U.S. Automotive Parts Advisory Committee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Having determined that the committee's work continues to be in 
the public interest in connection with the performance of duties 
imposed on the Department by law, the U.S. Automotive Parts Advisory 
Committee (APAC) was renewed. The renewal of the committee is in 
accordance with the Federal

[[Page 40408]]

Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. App. 2, and 41 CFR Subpart 101-6.10 
(1990), Federal Advisory Committee Management Rule.
    The APAC was established by the Secretary of Commerce on June 6, 
1989, to advise Department of Commerce officials on issues related to 
sales of U.S.-made auto parts to Japanese markets.
    The Committee functions as an advisory body in accordance with the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act. Authority for the committee is 
contained in 15 U.S.C. Sec. 4704, as amended by section 510 of Public 
Law 103-236 (April 30, 1994).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Reck, U.S. Department of 
Commerce, International Trade Administration, Trade Development, Office 
of Automotive Affairs, (202) 482-1418.
