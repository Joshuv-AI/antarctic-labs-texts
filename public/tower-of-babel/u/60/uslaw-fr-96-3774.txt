DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Equitrans, L.P.; Notice of Corrected Tariff Sheets Filing

February 14, 1996.
    Take notice that on February 9, 1996, Equitrans, L.P. (Equitrans), 
submitted for filing in its FERC Gas Tariff First Revised Volume NO. 1 
the following proposed tariff sheets: Third Revised Sheet No. 58; Third 
Revised Sheet No. 203A; and Second Revised Sheet No. 238.
    Equitrans states that these proposed tariff sheets are being 
submitted in order to correct the pagination or the superseding 
pagination contained on the corresponding proposed tariff sheets which 
were submitted for filing by Equitrans on January 23, 1996.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street NE., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Rule 211 of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR Section 385.211). All such 
protests must be filed as provided in Section 154.210 of the 
Commission's Regulations. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection 
in the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
