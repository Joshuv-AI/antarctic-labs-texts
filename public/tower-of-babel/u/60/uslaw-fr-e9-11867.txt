Numonyx--California Technology Center, Santa Clara, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 31, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Numonyx--California Technology 
Center, Santa Clara, California.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 28th day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
