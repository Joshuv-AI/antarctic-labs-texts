SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2878]


West Virginia; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area (Amendment #1)

    The above-numbered Declaration, approved on July 29, 1996, is 
hereby amended to correct the economic injury number assigned to this 
disaster which was inadvertently published as 97300 in the original 
declaration. The correct number is 897300.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for physical damage is September 27, 1996, and 
for economic injury the deadline is April 29, 1997.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)
