Vermont; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
state of Vermont, (FEMA-1201-DR), dated January 15, 1998, and related 
determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 26, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Madge Dale, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of Vermont, is hereby amended to include Individual Assistance for the 
following areas among those areas determined to have been adversely 
affected by the catastrophe declared a major disaster by the President 
in his declaration of January 15, 1998:

    Addison, Chittenden, Franklin, Grand Isle, Orange, and Windsor 
Counties for Individual Assistance (already designated for Public 
Assistance).

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance.)
Lacy E. Suiter,
Executive Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
