FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Request for Additional Information

    The Commission gives notice that it has requested that the parties 
to the below listed agreement provide additional information pursuant 
to section 6(d) of the Shipping Act of 1984, 46 U.S.C. app. 1705(d). 
The Commission has determined that further information is necessary to 
evaluate the impact of the proposed agreement on transportation costs 
and services. This action prevents the agreement from becoming 
effective as originally scheduled.
    Agreement No.: 201128.
    Title: Florida Ports Conference II.
    Parties: Canaveral Port Authority; Broward County, Port Everglades

[[Page 15809]]

Department; Jacksonville Port Authority; Port of Key West, City of Key 
West Transportation Department; Manatee County Port Authority; Miami-
Dade County, Port of Miami; Ocean Highway and Port Authority, Nassau 
County, Port of Fernandina; Port of Palm Beach District; Panama City 
Port Authority; City of Pensacola, Port of Pensacola; and Tampa Port 
Authority.

    Dated: March 29, 2002.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
