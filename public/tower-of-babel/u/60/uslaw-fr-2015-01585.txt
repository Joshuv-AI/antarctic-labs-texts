Extending Public Scoping Period for the Snow Mountain Solar 
Project on the Las Vegas Paiute Indian Reservation, Clark County, NV.

AGENCY: Bureau of Indian Affairs, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) is extending the public 
scoping period for the Snow Mountain Solar Project on the Las Vegas 
Paiute River Indian Reservation.

DATES: Scoping comments are due on January 30, 2015.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Chip Lewis at (602) 379-6782; 
email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The BIA published a Notice of Intent to 
Prepare an Environmental Impact Statement in the Federal Register on 
November 19, 2014 (79 FR 68909) and provided for a 30-day scoping 
comment period. The BIA is extending the comment period from December 
19, 2014, to January 30, 2015 and will accept any comments received 
prior to January 30, 2015. Please refer to the November 19, 2014 (79 FR 
68909) Notice of Intent for project details and commenting 
instructions.

    Dated: January 14, 2015.
