NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards Subcommittee Meeting on 
Future Plant Designs; Canceled

    The meeting of the ACRS Subcommittee on Future Plant Designs 
scheduled to be held on November 21, 2002, Room T-2B1, 11545 Rockville 
Pike, Rockville, Maryland, has been canceled due to the unavailability 
of documents. Notice of this meeting was published in the Federal 
Register on Monday, November 4, 2002 (67 FR 67218).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Medhat M. El-Zeftawy (telephone 
301-415-6889) between 7:30 a.m. and 5 p.m. (EST) or by e-mail
