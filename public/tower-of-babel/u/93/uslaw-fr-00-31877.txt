ACTION: Notice of Consideration; correction.

SUMMARY: This document corrects a notice relating to the consideration 
of issuance of amendments to Facility Operating License Nos. NPF-87 and 
NPF-89 issued to TXU Electric Company, et. al., appearing in the 
Federal Register on December 4, 2000 (65 FR 75737). This action is 
necessary to correct the websites listed in the notice for viewing the 
electronic copies of documents related to this notice.
