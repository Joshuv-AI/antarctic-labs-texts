    In accordance with Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 USC 
2273) the Department of Labor issued a Certification of Eligibility to 
Apply for Worker Adjustment Assistance on August 28, 1992, applicable 
to all workers of UNOCAL Corporation, North American Oil and Gas 
Division, Mid-Continent Region, headquartered in Oklahoma City, 
Oklahoma. The certification was subsequently amended November 3, 1992.
    New information received from the State Agency show that some of 
the workers at UNOCAL had their unemployment insurance (UI) taxes paid 
to Union Oil of California.
    Accordingly, the Department is amending the certification to 
properly reflect this matter.
    The intent of the Department's certification is to include all 
workers of UNOCAL Corporation who were adversely affected by increased 
imports.
    The amended notice applicable to TA-W-27, 491 is hereby issued as 
follows:

    All workers of UNOCAL Corporation,     a/k/a Union Oil of 
California, North American Oil and Gas Division, Mid-Continent 
Region, headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma (TA-W-27,488) and
