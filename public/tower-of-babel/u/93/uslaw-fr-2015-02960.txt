DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Clinical Trials and Translational Research Advisory 
Committee, March 04, 2015, 09:00 a.m. to March 04, 2015, 04:00 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, Building 31, C-Wing, 6th Floor, 31 
Center Drive, Room 10, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the 
Federal Register on January 09, 2015, 801428.
    The meeting notice is amended to reflect that the meeting will also 
be available via http://videocast.nih.gov The meeting is open to the 
public.

Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
