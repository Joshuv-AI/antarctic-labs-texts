Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

May 30, 2002.
    Petition of Reconsideration has been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th Street, SW, 
Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. Oppositions to this 
petition must be filed by June 21, 2002. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of the 
Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition must be 
filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.

00-138).
