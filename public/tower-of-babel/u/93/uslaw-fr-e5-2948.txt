ECSO Integrated Manufacturing, a Division of ESCO Corporation, 
Tempe, AZ; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 14, 2005 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at ESCO Integrated 
Manufacturing, a division of ESCO Corporation, Tempe, Arizona.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 25th day of May 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
