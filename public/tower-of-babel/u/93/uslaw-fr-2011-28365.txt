 Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XXXV; Riverbank Hydro No. 4, LLC; 
Notice Announcing Filing Priority for Preliminary Permit Applications

    On October 27, 2011, the Commission held a drawing to determine 
priority among competing preliminary permit applications with identical 
filing times. In the event that the Commission concludes that neither 
of the applicants' plans is better adapted than the other to develop, 
conserve, and utilize in the public interest the water resources of the 
region at issue, the priority established by this drawing will serve as 
the tiebreaker. Based on the drawing, the order of priority is as 
follows:
1. Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XXXV--Project No. 14136-000
2. Riverbank Hydro No. 4, LLC--Project No. 14139-000

    Dated: October 27, 2011.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
