FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-1483, MM Docket No. 01-134, RM-10137]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Elk City, OK and Borger, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition filed by TV 31, 
L.L.C. requesting the reallotment of NTSC Channel 31 from Elk City, 
Oklahoma, to Borger, Texas, and modification of the construction permit 
for Station KBCA to specify Borger, Texas, as the community of license. 
The coordinates for Channel 31 at Borger are 35-41-56 and 100-53-34. In 
accordance with Section 1.420(i) of the Commission's Rules, we shall 
not accept competing expressions of interest in the use of Channel 31 
at Borger.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before August 13, 2001, and reply 
comments on or before August 28, 2001.
