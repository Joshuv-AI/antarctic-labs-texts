Cove Point LNG Limited Partnership; Non-Disclosure Agreement

I hereby agree that I will not disclose the non-public material I have 
requested in this proceeding (specified below) to anyone other than, as 
appropriate, my client, my supervisor(s), or anyone else whom I 
represent or to whom I report. That person(s) in turn may not disclose 
the information to anyone. I understand that the contents of the non-
public material, any notes or other memoranda, or any other form of 
information that copies or discloses this material shall not be 
disclosed to anyone other than as noted. I further understand that I 
shall use this material only in connection with this proceeding. I 
acknowledge that a violation of this agreement constitutes a violation 
of the Commission's directive at 97 FERC para.61,181 (2001) that 
certain material in this proceeding be treated as privileged.
