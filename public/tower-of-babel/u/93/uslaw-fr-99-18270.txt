DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Human Genome Research Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Human Genome Research Institute Special Emphasis Panel, July 22, 1999, 
8:30 AM to July 23, 1999, 6 PM, Hyatt Regency, One Metro Center, 
Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on July 
1, 1999, 64 FR 35673.
    The meeting will be held in its entirety on July 23 instead of July 
22-23. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: July 9, 1999.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
