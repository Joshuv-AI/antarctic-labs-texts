Farah/Savane Intl., El Paso, TX; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 18, 2000 in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Farah/Savane Intl., El Paso, Texas.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
37,999). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 25th day of September, 2000.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
