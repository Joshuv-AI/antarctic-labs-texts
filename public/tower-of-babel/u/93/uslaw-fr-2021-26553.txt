SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On November 26, 2021, the Department of Commerce (Commerce) 
inadvertently published duplicate copies of the final results of the 
2019-2020 antidumping duty administrative review of large residential 
washers from Mexico.\1\ The first version of this notice (86 FR 67444) 
incorrectly listed the applicable date of this notice, which is listed 
correctly as the publication date (i.e., November 26, 2021) in the 
second version of this notice (86 FR 67446). The inadvertent duplicate 
publication of this notice does not constitute redetermination of this 
proceeding. This notice serves as a notification of, and correction to, 
this inadvertent duplicate publication.
