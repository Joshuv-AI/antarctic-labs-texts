SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Public Law 94-409, that the Securities 
and Exchange Commission will hold an Open Meeting on August 25, 2010 at 
10 a.m., in the Auditorium, Room L-002.
    The subject matter of the Open Meeting will be:

Item 1: The Commission will consider whether to adopt changes to the 
federal proxy and other rules to facilitate director nominations by 
shareholders.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items.
    For further information and to ascertain what, if any, matters have 
been added, deleted or postponed, please contact:
    The Office of the Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: August 18, 2010.
Florence E. Harmon,
Deputy Secretary.
