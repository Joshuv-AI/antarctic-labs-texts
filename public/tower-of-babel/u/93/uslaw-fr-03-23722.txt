Vaughan Furniture Company, Inc., Johnson City, Tennessee; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 18, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Vaughan 
Furniture Company, Inc., Johnson City, Tennessee.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 21st day of August, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
