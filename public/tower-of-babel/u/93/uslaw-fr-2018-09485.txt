Southern Power Company; Notice Shortening Comment Period

    On April 25, 2018, Southern Power Company (Southern) filed a 
petition for waiver and request for shortened comment period and 
expedited action (Petition) in the above-referenced proceeding. 
Included in Southern's Petition was a request to shorten the date for 
filing comments to the Petition.
    Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that the date for filing 
comments to Southern's request is shortened to and including May 1, 
2018.

    Dated: April 30, 2018.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
