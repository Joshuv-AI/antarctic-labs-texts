Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-34-2022]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 75--Phoenix, Arizona; Authorization of 
Production Activity; Lucid Motors USA, Inc. (Electric Automobiles and 
Subassemblies); Casa Grande and Tempe, Arizona

    On July 29, 2022, Lucid Motors USA, Inc., submitted a notification 
of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facilities 
within Subzone 75N, in Casa Grande and Tempe, Arizona.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (75 FR 50288, August 16, 2022). On 
November 28, 2022, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at this 
time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including Section 400.14.

    Dated: November 28, 2022.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
