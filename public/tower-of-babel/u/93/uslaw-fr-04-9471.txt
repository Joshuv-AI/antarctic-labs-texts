38 CFR Parts 3 and 4

RIN 2900-AE91


Schedule for Rating Disabilities; the Musculoskeletal System

AGENCY: Department of Veterans Affairs.

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is withdrawing its 
proposal to amend that portion of its Schedule for Rating Disabilities 
that addresses musculoskeletal conditions, and related regulations, 
published in the Federal Register on February 11, 2003 (68 FR 6998). 
Nine commenters responded, raising numerous issues. VA has considered 
the issues and believes a new proposal is appropriate. Accordingly, VA 
is withdrawing the proposal and is developing a new proposal, which it 
intends to publish at a later date.
