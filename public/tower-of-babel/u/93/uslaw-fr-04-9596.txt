Additional Information

    By a vote of 3-0 on April 20, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec.  9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that

the public.

originally scheduled for Thursday, May 6, 2004 was rescheduled for June 
2, 2004.
