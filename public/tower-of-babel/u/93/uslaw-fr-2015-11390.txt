DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Veterans' Advisory Committee on Rehabilitation, Notice of Charter 
Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 
U.S.C., dated October 6, 1972, that the Veterans' Advisory Committee on 
Rehabilitation has been renewed for a 2-year period beginning April 20, 
2015, through April 20, 2017.

    Dated: April 20, 2015.
Rebecca Schiller,
Committee Management Officer.
