Hillman Power Company, L.L.C.; Notice of Initiation of Proceeding 
And Refund Effective Date

    On September 30, 2013, the Commission issued an order that 
initiated a proceeding in Docket No. EL13-89-000, pursuant to section 
206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. 824e (2006), to determine 
the justness and reasonableness of the

[[Page 61947]]

proposed revenue requirement of the Hillman Power Company, L.L.C. 
Hillman Power Company, L.L.C., 144 FERC ] 61,245 (2013).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL13-89-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: September 30, 2013.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
