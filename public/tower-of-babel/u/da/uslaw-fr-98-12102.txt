DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Warren Grant Magnuson Clinical Center; Notice of Meeting of the 
Board of Governors of the Warren Grant Magnuson Clinical Center

    Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
meeting of the Board of Governors of the Warren Grant Magnuson Clinical 
Center, May 27, 1998. The Board of Governors will meet at the National 
Institutes of Health, Clinical Center (Building 10), Medical Board Room 
(2C116), 9000 Rockville Pike, Bethesda, Maryland, from 9:00 a.m. until 
approximately 12:30 p.m.
    The entire meeting will be open to the public and will include 
review of the minutes of the March 23, 1998 Executive Committee 
meeting, updates on the budget, strategic planning, and the Clinical 
Research Center.
    Attendance by the public will be limited to space available.
    For further information, contact Ms. Maggi Stakem, Office of the 
Director, Warren Grant Magnuson Clinical Center, Building 10, Room 
2C146, Bethesda, Maryland 20892, (301) 496-4114.
    Individuals who plan to attend and need special assistance, such as 
sign language interpretation or other reasonable accommodations, should 
contact Ms. Stakem in advance of the meeting.

    Dated: May 1, 1998.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
