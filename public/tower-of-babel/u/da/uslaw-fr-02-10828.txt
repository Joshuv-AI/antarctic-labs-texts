TENNESSEE VALLEY AUTHORITY


Notice of Publication of Draft Report Implementing Guidelines for 
Ensuring and Maximizing the Quality, Objectivity, Utility and Integrity 
of Information Disseminated by Federal Agencies

AGENCY: Tennessee Valley Authority.

ACTION: Notice of publication of draft report with request for 
comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On September 28, 2001 the Office of Management and Budget 
published Guidelines for Ensuring and Maximizing the Quality, 
Objectivity, Utility and Integrity of Information Disseminated by 
Federal Agencies. In response to those guidelines, the Tennessee Valley 
Authority (TVA) has posed a draft report setting forth its proposed 
information quality guidelines on its Web site at www.tva.gov/infoquality.
(Authority: Section 515, Pub. L. 106-554, 66 FR 49718 (Sept. 28, 
2001))

DATES: Effective Date: May 1, 2002.
    Comment Date: Comments on TVA's draft report must be submitted on 
or before June 3, 2002.

ADDRESSES: Please submit comments to Information Quality, 400 West 
Summit Hill Drive ET-6A, Knoxville, Tennessee 37902.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Henry J. Collins, 400 West Summit Hill 
Drive, WT-8A-K, Knoxville, Tennessee 37902. Telephone 865-632-6127.

    Dated: April 26, 2002.
Diane J. Bunch,
Chief Information Officer.
