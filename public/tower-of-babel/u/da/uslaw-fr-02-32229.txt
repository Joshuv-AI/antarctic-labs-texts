Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-42,194]


Jean Michael's Incorporated, Willingboro, NJ; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 30, 2002, on behalf of workers at Jean 
Michael's Inc., Willingboro, New Jersey.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-41, 699). Consequently, further investigation 
in this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 4th day of December, 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
