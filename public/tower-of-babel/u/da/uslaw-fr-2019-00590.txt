Self-Regulatory Organizations; Cboe EDGX Exchange, Inc.; Notice 
of Withdrawal of a Proposed Rule Change To Adopt Complex Reserve Order 
Functionality

 January 28, 2019.
    On November 8, 2018, Cboe EDGX Exchange, Inc. filed with the 
Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), pursuant to 
Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (``Act'') \1\ 
and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to adopt Complex 
Reserve Order functionality. The proposed rule change was published for 
comment in the Federal Register on November 27, 2018.\3\ On December 
19, 2018, the Commission extended the time period within which to 
approve the proposed rule change, disapprove the proposed rule change, 
or institute proceedings to determine whether to approve or disapprove 
the proposed rule change to February 25, 2019.\4\ The Commission 
received no comment letters regarding the proposed rule change.

    On January 9, 2019, the Exchange withdrew the proposed rule change 
(SR-CboeEDGX-2018-049).
