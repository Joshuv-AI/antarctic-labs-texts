Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2342-011]


PacifiCorp; Notice Clarifying Comment Deadline for Offer of 
Settlement and Application for Amendment of License

    The Commission's Notice of Offer of Settlement and Application for 
Amendment of License, issued February 2, 2000, in this proceeding, set 
a deadline for filing comments and or motions in Project No. P-2342-011 
of March 15, 2000, or 45 days after the filing of the PacifiCorp's 
Removal Plan with the Commission, whichever occurs later. On February 
11, 2000, PacifiCorp filed the Removal Plan. Accordingly, the deadline 
for filing comments and or motions in Project No. P-2342-011 is now 
March 27, 2000.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary
