DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Meeting of the Advisory Committee on Minority Health

AGENCY: Department of Health and Human Services, Office of the 
Secretary, Office of Public Health and Science, Office of Minority 
Health.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Advisory Committee on Minority Health will meet to discuss 
racial and ethnic disparities in health, as well as other related 
issues. The meeting is open to the public. There will be an opportunity 
for public comment which will be limited to five minutes per speaker. 
Individuals who would like to submit written statements should mail or 
fax their comments to the Office of Minority Health at least two 
business days prior to the meeting.

DATES: The Advisory Committee on Minority Health will meet on Thursday, 
March 21, 2002 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Friday, March 22, 2000 from 
8:30 a.m. to 12 noon.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Hamilton Crowne Plaza, Oasis 
Room, 1001 14th Street, NW., Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Sheila P. Merriweather, Office of 
Minority Health, Rockwall Building, 5515 Security Lane, Suite 1000, 
Rockville, MD 20852. Phone: 301-443-9923, Fax: 301-443-8280.
