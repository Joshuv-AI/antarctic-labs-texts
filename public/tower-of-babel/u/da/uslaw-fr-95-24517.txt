DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee will meet on October 
23 and October 24, 1995 at the Weaverville Victorian Inn Conference 
Room, 1709 Main Street, Weaverville, California. The meeting will begin 
at 10 a.m. on October 23 and adjourn at 5 p.m. The meeting will 
reconvene at 8 a.m. on October 24 and continue until 4 p.m. Agenda 
items to be covered include: (1) Research and monitoring activities in 
the Province; (2) salvage activities discussion; (4) watershed 
selection criteria for setting priorities and status of completed and 
planned analyses; and (5) a public comment period. All PAC meetings are 
open to the public. Interested citizens are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:

Yreka, California 96097; telephone 916-842-6131, (FTS) 700-467-1300.
