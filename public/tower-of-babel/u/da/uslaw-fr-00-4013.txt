SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Economic Injury Disaster #9G67]


State of New York (and a Contiguous County in the State of New 
Jersey)

    Bronx County and the contiguous counties of New York, Queens, and 
Westchester in the State of New York, and Bergen County, New Jersey 
constitute an economic injury disaster loan area as a result of a fire 
that occurred on October 20, 1999 in the Castle Hill section of the 
Bronx. Eligible small businesses and small agricultural cooperatives 
without credit available elsewhere may file applications for economic 
injury assistance as a result of this disaster until the close of 
business on November 9, 2000 at the address listed below or other 
locally announced locations: U.S. Small Business Administration, 
Disaster Area 1 Office, 360 Rainbow Blvd, South, 3rd Floor, Niagara 
Falls, NY 14303.
    The interest rate for eligible small businesses and small 
agricultural cooperatives is 4 percent.
    The economic injury number for the State of New Jersey is 9G68.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59002)
