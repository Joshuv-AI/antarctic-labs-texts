DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Cancer 
Institute Clinical Trials and Translational Research Advisory 
Committee, July 14, 2010, 8 a.m. to July 14, 2010, 4 p.m., National 
Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 
which was published in the Federal Register on May 19, 2010, 75FR28028.
    The meeting is cancelled due to scheduling conflicts.

    Dated: June 7, 2010.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
