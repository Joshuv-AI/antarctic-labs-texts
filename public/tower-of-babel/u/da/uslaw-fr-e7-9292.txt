Agency Information Collection Activities; Proposed Collection; 
Comment Request; The SunWise Program; EPA ICR Number 1904.03, OMB 
Control Number 2060-0439; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency published a notice in the 
Federal Register of February 12, 2007, concerning a request for 
comments on an Information Collection Request for the SunWise program, 
EPA ICR No. 1904.03, OMB Control No. 2060-0439. The notice contained an 
incorrect EPA ICR Number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kristinn Vazquez, (202) 343-9246.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of February 12, 2007, in FR Doc. E7-2308, 
on page 6564, at the top of the second column, correct the title to 
read: Agency Information Collection Activities: Proposed Collection; 
Comment Request; The SunWise Program; EPA ICR No. 1904.03, OMB Control 
No. 2060-0439.
    In the Federal Register of February 12, 2007, in FR Doc. E7-2308, 
on page 6565, at the top of the second column, correct the ICR numbers 
to read: EPA ICR No. 1904.03, OMB Control No. 2060-0439.

    Dated: May 9, 2007.
Elizabeth Craig,
Acting Assistant Administrator for Air and Radiation.
