DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE


Department of the Air Force

HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Pub. L. 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the 2003 S&T Review and the Chief of Staff and 
Secretary of the Air Force. The purpose of the meeting is to allow the 
SAB leadership to advise the Air Force leadership on the outcome of the 
2003 Review. Because classified and contractor-proprietary information 
will be discussed, this meeting will be closed to the public.

DATES: February 20, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Room 4E869, The Pentagon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Maj John Pernot, Air Force Scientific 
Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 5D982, 
Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
