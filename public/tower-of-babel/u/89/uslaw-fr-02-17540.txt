Availability of ``Allocation of Fiscal Year 2002 Youth and the 
Environment Training and Employment Program Funds''

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of document availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: EPA is announcing availability of a memorandum entitled 
``Allocation of Fiscal Year 2002 Youth and the Environment Training and 
Employment Program Funds'' issued on May 28, 2002. This memorandum 
provides National guidance for the allocation of funds used under 
section 104(b)(3) of the Clean Water Act.

ADDRESSES: Municipal Assistance Branch (4204-M), U.S. EPA, 1200 
Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20460.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Curt Baranowski, (202) 564-0636 or 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The subject memorandum may be viewed and 
downloaded from Program's web page at http://www.epa.gov/owm/youth.htm 
under the Grant Guidance link.

    Dated: June 7, 2002.
James A. Hanlon,
Director, Office of Wastewater Management.
