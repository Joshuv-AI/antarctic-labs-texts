FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2353]


Petitions For Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

August 19, 1999.
    Petitions For Reconsideration have been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of these documents 
are available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th Street, 
SW, Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to these petitions 
must be filed by September 8, 1999. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of the 
Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1). Replies to an opposition must be 
filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.
    Subject: Revision of the Commission's Rules to Ensure Capatibility 
with Enhanced 911 Emergency Calling System (CC Docket No. 94-102, RM-
8143).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 2.

    Federal Communications Commission.
Magalie Roman Salas,
Secretary.
