DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration


Division of Foreign Labor Certification; the Use of Sheepherders 
for Vegetation Management; General Administration Letter No. 3-01

    The Employment and Training Administration administers and 
interprets the requirements of the temporary, alien agricultural labor 
certification (H-2A) program. These interpretations are issued in 
General Administration Letters (GAL's) to its Regional Offices and the 
State Employment Security Agencies. The GAL below is published in the 
Federal Register in order to inform the public.

GAL No. 3-01

    GAL No. 3-01 provides policy clarification and procedural guidance 
for the processing of nonagricultural sheep and goat herder 
applications as H-2A.

    Dated: April 6, 2001.
Raymond J. Uhalde,
Deputy Assistant Secretary of Labor.
