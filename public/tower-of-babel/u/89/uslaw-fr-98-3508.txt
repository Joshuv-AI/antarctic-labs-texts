UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE BOARD OF GOVERNORS


Sunshine Act Meeting; Notification of Items Added to Meeting 
Agenda

DATE OF MEETING: February 2, 1998.

STATUS: Closed.

PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: 63 FR 3362, January 22, 1998.

CHANGE: At its meeting on February 2, 1998, the Board of Governors of 
the United States Postal Service voted unanimously to add an item to 
the agenda of its closed meeting held on that date: Uniform Program.
