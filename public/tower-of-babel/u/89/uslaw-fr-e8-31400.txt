Alaska Native Claims Selection

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of decision approving lands for conveyance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: As required by 43 CFR 2650.7(d), notice is hereby given that 
an appealable decision approving lands for conveyance pursuant to the 
Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act will be issued to Russian Mission 
Native Corporation. The lands are in the vicinity of Russian Mission, 
Alaska, and are located in:

Seward Meridian, Alaska

T. 18 N., R. 66 W.,
    Secs. 5 and 6.

    Containing approximately 1,052 acres.

T. 21 N., R. 66 W.,
    Secs. 5, 8, 17, and 20;
    Secs. 21, 28, 29, and 32;
    Secs. 33 and 34.

    Containing approximately 6,260 acres.

T. 18 N., R. 67 W.,
    Secs. 1 to 5, inclusive;
    Secs. 7 to 11, inclusive;
    Secs. 14 to 18, inclusive;
    Secs. 21, 22, 23, and 28.

    Containing approximately 7,776 acres.

T. 20 N., R. 67 W.,
    Secs. 1 and 2.

    Containing approximately 1,143 acres.

T. 18 N., R. 68 W.,
    Sec. 13.

    Containing approximately 593 acres.
    Aggregating approximately 16,824 acres.

    The subsurface estate in these lands will be conveyed to Calista 
Corporation when the surface estate is conveyed to Russian Mission 
Native Corporation. Notice of the decision will also be published four 
times in the Tundra Drums.

DATES: The time limits for filing an appeal are:
    1. Any party claiming a property interest which is adversely 
affected by the decision shall have until February 4, 2009 to file an 
appeal.
    2. Parties receiving service of the decision by certified mail 
shall have 30 days from the date of receipt to file an appeal.
    Parties who do not file an appeal in accordance with the 
requirements of 43 CFR Part 4, Subpart E, shall be deemed to have 
waived their rights.

ADDRESSES: A copy of the decision may be obtained from: Bureau of Land 
Management, Alaska State Office, 222 West Seventh Avenue, 13, 
Anchorage, Alaska 99513-7504.
