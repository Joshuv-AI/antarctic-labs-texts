DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-33,185]


Montana Power Co., Missoula, Montana, Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 10, 1997 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed January 21, 1997 on behalf of workers at Montana Power 
Company located in Missoula, Montana (TA-W-33,185).
    The petitioning group of workers are covered under an existing 
Trade Adjustment Assistance certification (TA-W-33,085A). Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC., this 14th day of March 1997.
Russell T. Kile,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
