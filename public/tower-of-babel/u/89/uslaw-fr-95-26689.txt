INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION


Notice of Intent To Engage in Compensated Intercorporate Hauling 
Operations

    This is to provide notice as required by 49 U.S.C. 10524(b)(1) that 
the named corporations intend to provide or use compensated 
intercorporate hauling operations as authorized in 49 U.S.C. 10524(b).

1. Parent Corporation and Address of Principal Office

Pine Bluff Sand and Gravel Company, Post Office Box 7008, 1501 Port 
Road, Pine Bluff, Arkansas 71611

2. Wholly Owned Subsidiaries Which Will Participate in the Operations, 
and State of Incorporation

(a) Mobley Construction Company, Inc., Arkansas Corporation
(b) Mobley Transport Company, Inc., Arkansas Corporation.
Vernon A. Williams,
Secretary.
