Self-Regulatory Organizations; Bats BZX Exchange, Inc.; Notice of 
Withdrawal of a Proposed Rule Change, as Modified by Amendment No. 1, 
To List and Trade Under BZX Rule 14.11(c)(4) the Shares of the VanEck 
Vectors AMT-Free National Municipal Index ETF of VanEck Vectors ETF 
Trust

September 25, 2017.
    On January 27, 2017, Bats BZX Exchange, Inc. (``Exchange'') filed 
with the Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), pursuant 
to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 \1\ and Rule 
19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to list and trade under BZX 
Rule 14.11(c)(4) shares of the VanEck Vectors AMT-Free National 
Municipal Index ETF of VanEck Vectors ETF Trust. The proposed rule 
change was published for comment in the Federal Register on February 
14, 2017.\3\ On March 10, 2017, the Exchange filed Amendment No. 1 to 
the proposed rule change.\4\ On March 30, 2017, the Commission 
designated a longer period within which to approve the proposed rule 
change, disapprove the proposed rule change, or institute proceedings 
to determine whether to disapprove the proposed rule change.\5\ On May 
11, 2017, the Commission instituted proceedings to determine whether to 
approve or disapprove the proposed rule change.\6\ On August 9, 2017, 
the Commission issued a notice of designation of a longer period for 
Commission action on proceedings to determine whether to approve or 
disapprove the proposed rule change.\7\ The Commission received no 
comments on the proposed rule change.
