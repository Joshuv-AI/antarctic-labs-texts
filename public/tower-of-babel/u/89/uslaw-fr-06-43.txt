FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR PART 73

[DA 05-3212, MB Docket No. 01-180, RM-10200, RM-11018]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Holdenville and Pauls Valley, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a counterproposal to allot Channel 265A 
at Holdenville, OK, as a second local service. The document also 
dismisses a mutually exclusive proposal to allot Channel 266A at Pauls 
Valley, OK, because it was not technically correct at the time it was 
filed. See 66 F.R. 44586, August 24, 2001. See also SUPPLEMENTARY 
INFORMATION.

DATES: Effective January 30, 2006.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Andrew J. Rhodes, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Report and Order, MB Docket 01-180, adopted December 14, 2005 and 
released December 16, 2005.
