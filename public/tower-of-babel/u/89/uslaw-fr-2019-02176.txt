SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Correction

    On February 1, 2019, the Department of Commerce (Commerce) 
published the rescission of 2016-2018 countervailing duty (CVD) 
administrative review of 1-Hydroxyethylidene-1,1-Diphoshonic Acid 
(HEDP) from the People's Republic of China (China).\1\ The Rescission 
inadvertently identified the incorrect period of review (POR) 
associated with the CVD administrative review on HEDP from China (i.e., 
incorrect POR November 14, 2016, through April 30, 2018). The correct 
POR associated with the CVD order on HEDP from China is September 8, 
2016, through December 31, 2017. This notice serves as a correction 
notice.
