DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2266-102]


Nevada Irrigation District; Notice of Designation of Certain 
Commission Personnel as Non-Decisional

    Commission staff members Rachael Warden (Office of the General 
Counsel 202-502-8717; [email protected]) and Elizabeth Molloy 
(Office of the General Counsel; 202-502-8771; 
[email protected]) are assigned to help resolve issues related 
to the Yuba-Bear Hydroelectric Project No. 2266.
    As ``non-decisional'' staff, Ms. Warden and Ms. Molloy will not 
participate in an advisory capacity in any matters related to the Yuba-
Bear Hydroelectric Project No. 2266.

    Dated: October 27, 2020.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
