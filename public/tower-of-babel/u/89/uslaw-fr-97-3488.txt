COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION

Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: HOLDING THE MEETING: Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., Thursday, February 27, 1997.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

PLACE: 1155 21st St., N.W., Washington, D.C. Lobby Level Hearing Room.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

Final Rulemaking concerning contract Market Rule Review Procedures
Revised procedures for Commission review and approval of 
applications for Contract Market Designation and of Exchange Rules 
relating to Contract terms and conditions-final rules

market designation in Basic Formula Price Milk futures and the 
options, including a report on issues involving the National Cheese 
Exchange
