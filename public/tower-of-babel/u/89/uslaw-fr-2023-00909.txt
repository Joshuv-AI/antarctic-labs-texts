Self-Regulatory Organizations; NYSE American LLC; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend Rule 7.19E Concerning Pre-
Trade Risk Controls

January 12, 2023.
    On November 17, 2022, NYSE American LLC (``NYSE American'') filed 
with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the ``Commission''), 
pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 
(``Act'' or ``Exchange Act'') \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder \2\ to add 
additional pre-trade risk controls to Rule 7.19E. The proposed rule 
change was published for comment on December 5, 2022.\3\ On January 10, 
2023, NYSE American withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-NYSEAMER-
2022-53).
