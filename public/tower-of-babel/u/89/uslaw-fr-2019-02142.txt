DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. CP15-521-000]


Gulf LNG Liquefaction Company, LLC, Gulf LNG Energy, LLC, Gulf 
LNG Pipeline, LLC; Notice of Reopening of Comment Period

    On November 15, 2018, the Commission issued a notice setting 
January 7, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments on 
the Gulf LNG Liquefaction Project draft environmental impact statement. 
Due to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies between December 
22, 2018 and January 25, 2019, the Commission is reopening the comment 
period until February 25, 2019.

    Dated: February 7, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
