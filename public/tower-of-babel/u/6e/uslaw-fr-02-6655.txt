Additional Information

    By a vote of 5-0 on March 7, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of a) Duke Cogema Stone & Webster (Savannah River Mixed 
Oxide Fuel Fabrication Facility); Georgians Against Nuclear Energy's 
Petition for Interlocutory Review and Request for Stay Pending Review 
and b) Private Fuel Storage (Independent Spent Fuel Storage 
Installation) Docket No. 72-22-ISFSI; Review of LBP-02-08 (February 22, 
2002)'' be held on March 7, and on less than one week's notice to the 
public.
