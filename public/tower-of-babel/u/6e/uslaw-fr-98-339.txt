DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER98-934-000]


Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation; Notice of Filing

December 31, 1997.
    Take notice that on December 4, 1997, Rochester Gas and Electric 
Corporation (RG&E), filed a Service Agreement between RG&E and the AIG 
Trading Corporation (Customer). This Service Agreement specifies that 
the Customer has agreed to the rates, term and conditions of RG&E's 
FERC Electric Tariff, Original Volume No. 3 (Market-Based Rate Tariff) 
accepted by the Commission in Docket No. ER97-3553-000.
    RG&E requests waiver of the Commission's sixty (60) day notice 
requirements and an effective date of November 12, 1997, for the AIG 
Trading Corporation Service Agreement. RG&E has served copies of the 
filing on the New York State Public Service Commission and on the 
Customer.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before January 12, 1998. Protests 
will be

[[Page 841]]

considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make the protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
