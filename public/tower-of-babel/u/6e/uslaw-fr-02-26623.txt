    Purpose: The Health Professions and Nurse Education Special 
Emphasis Panel shall advise the Associate Administrator for Health 
Professions on the technical merit of grants to improve the 
training, distribution, utilization, and quality of personnel 
required to staff the Nation's health care delivery system.
    Agenda: The open portion of each meeting from 8 a.m. to 
approximately 10 a.m. will cover introductions, opening remarks, 
housekeeping details, and an orientation to the review process. The 
meetings will be closed at approximately 10:00 a.m. on the first day 
of each meeting until adjournment for the review of grant 
applications, the disclosure of which would constitute a clearly 
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.
