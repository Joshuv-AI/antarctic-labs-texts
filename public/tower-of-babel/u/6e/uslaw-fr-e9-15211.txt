Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,898]


American and Efird, Inc., Mt. Holly, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 7, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers of American and Efird, Inc., Mt. Holly, 
North Carolina.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 18th day of May 2009.
