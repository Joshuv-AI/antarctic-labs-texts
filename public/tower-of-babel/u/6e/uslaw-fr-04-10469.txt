SUMMARY: The technical amendments to the document titled ``Oil and Gas 
and

[[Page 25500]]

Sulphur Operations in the Outer Continental Shelf--Relief or Reduction 
in Royalty Rates--Deep Gas Provisions'' published at 69 FR 24052 (April 
30, 2004) contained an incorrect effective date for the changes 
included in the document. This document corrects the effective date for 
all changes and amendments to May 3, 2004.

DATES: Effective Date: The effective date for all changes and 
amendments to 30 CFR Part 203 that were published at 69 FR 24052 (April 
30, 2004) is May 3, 2004.
