47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2784; MM Docket No. 98-162; RM-9263]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Sugar Hill and Taccoa, GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Southern Broadcasting of Pensacola, Inc. to the extent of setting aside 
a previous action substituting Channel 291C1 for Channel 291C at 
Taccoa, Georgia, reallotting Channel 291C1 to Sugar Hill, Georgia, and 
modifying the license of Station WNGC to specify operation on Channel 
291C1 at Sugar Hill. See 66 FR 39456, published July 20, 2001. As a 
result, Station WNGC will continue to be licensed on Channel 291C at 
Taccoa, Georgia. The reference coordinates for Channel 291C at Taccoa, 
Georgia, are 34-22-41 and 83-39-30.

DATES: Effective December 14, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2177.
