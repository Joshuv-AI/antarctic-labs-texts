GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION

[GSA Bulletin FPMR D-258]


Public Buildings Space

    This notice contains GSA Bulletin FPMR D-258 which announces the 
redesignation of a Federal Building. The text of the bulletin follows:
TO: Heads of Federal Agencies
SUBJECT: Redesignation of a Federal Building
    1. Purpose. This bulletin announces the redesignation of a Federal 
Building.
    2. Expiration date. This bulletin expires April 20, 2002. However, 
the building redesignation announced by this bulletin will remain in 
effect until canceled or superseded.
    3. Redesignation. The former and new names of the building being 
redesignated is as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
              Former name                            New name
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Main Justice Department Building...  Robert F. Kennedy Department of
                                          Justice Building.
950 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW............  950 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW.
Washington, DC 20530...................  Washington, DC 20530.
------------------------------------------------------------------------


    Dated: November 20, 2001.
Stephen A. Perry,
Administrator of General Services.
