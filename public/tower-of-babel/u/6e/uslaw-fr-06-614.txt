FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting Notice; Announcing an Open Meeting of the 
Board of Directors

Time and Date: An open meeting of the Board of Directors is scheduled 
to begin at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, January 25, 2006.

Place: Board Room, First Floor, Federal Housing Finance Board, 1625 Eye 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20006.

Status: The meeting will be open to the public.

Matters To Be Considered at the Meeting: Reappointment to the Board of 
Directors of the Office of Finance.
    Amendment to the Capital Structure Plan of the Federal Home Loan 
Bank of Atlanta.

Contact Person for More Information: Shelia Willis, Paralegal 
Specialist, Office of General Counsel, at 202-408-2876 or 
[email protected].

    By the Federal Housing Finance Board.

    Dated: January 18, 2006.

John P. Kennedy,
 General Counsel.
