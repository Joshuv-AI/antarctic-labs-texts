POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Notice of Facility Visit

March 10, 1995.
    Members of the Commission and its advisory staff will visit the 
offices of [[Page 14041]] WEFA in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania on 
Thursday, March 16, 1995. WEFA officials will explain the process of 
preparation of the economic model of direct response advertising it 
developed for the Direct Marketing Association. A report of the visit 
will be on file in the Docket Room of the Commission.
Margaret P. Crenshaw,
Secretary.
