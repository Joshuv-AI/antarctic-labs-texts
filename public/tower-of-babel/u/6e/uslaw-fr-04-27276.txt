DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the Air Force Scientific Advisory Board. The 
purpose of the meeting is to present and discuss the findings of the 
2004 Science and Technology Quality Review of Air Force Research 
Laboratory programs. Because classified and contractor-proprietary 
information will be discussed, this meeting will be closed to the 
public.

DATES: January 19, 2005.

ADDRESS: 1670 Air Force Pentagon, Room 4E916, Washington, DC 20330-
1670.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Major Kyle Gresham, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington, DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4808.

Albert F. Bodnar,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
