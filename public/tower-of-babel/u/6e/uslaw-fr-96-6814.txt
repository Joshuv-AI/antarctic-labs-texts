Termination of Classification and Opening of Lands to Entry in 
Mohave County, Arizona

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This Notice will open 60 acres to location and entry under the 
public land laws and general mining laws.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 22, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce Bailey, Realty Specialist, 
Kingman Resource Area, 2475 Beverly Avenue, Kingman, Arizona 86401, 
telephone (520) 757-3161.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The following described lands were 
classified on March 11, 1975, under the

classification is no longer needed:
