FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. E8-1202 published on page 
4573 of the issue for Friday, January 25, 2008.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for HOTC, Inc., Wray, Coloardo, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City (Todd Offenbacker, Assistant 
Vice President) 925 Grand Avenue, Kansas City, Missouri 64198-0001:
    1. HOTC, Investment Company, to become a bank holding company by 
acquiring 100 percent of the voting shares of Wray State Bank, both of 
Wray, Colorado.
    Comments on this application must be received by February 19, 2008.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, February 1, 
2008.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Secretary of the Board.
