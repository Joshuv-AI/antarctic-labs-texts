[Project No. 3511-024]


Lower Saranac Hydro, LLC; Notice of Denial of Water Quality 
Certification

    On May 29, 2020, Lower Saranac Hydro, LLC (Lower Saranac Hydro) 
filed an application for a license for the Groveville Hydroelectric 
Project (project) in the above captioned docket. Lower Saranac Hydro 
filed with the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation 
(New York DEC) a request for water quality certification for the 
project under section 401(a)(1) of the Clean Water Act on July 2, 2021. 
On June 6, 2022, the New York DEC denied certification for the project. 
Pursuant to 40 CFR 121.8, we are providing notice that New York DEC's 
denial satisfies the requirements of 40 CFR 121.7(e).

    Dated: June 28, 2022.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
