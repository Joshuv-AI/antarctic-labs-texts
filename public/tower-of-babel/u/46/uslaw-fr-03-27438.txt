Penn-Union Corp., Nesco Division, Edinboro, PA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 3, 2003, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Penn-Union Corporation, Nesco 
Division, Edinboro, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 29th day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
