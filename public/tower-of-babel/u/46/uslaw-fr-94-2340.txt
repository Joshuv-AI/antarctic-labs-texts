INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 337-TA-315 (Enforcement Proceeding)]

 

In the Matter of Certain Plastic Encapsulated Integrated 
Circuits: Designation of Commission Investigative Attorney

    Notice is hereby given that, as of this date, Thomas L. Jarvis, 
Esq. of the Office of Unfair Import Investigations is designated as the 
Commission investigative attorney in the above-cited investigation.
    The Secretary is requested to publish this Notice in the Federal 
Register.

    Dated: January 26, 1994.
Lynn I. Levine,
Director, Office of Unfair Import Investigations, 500 E Street, S.W., 
Washington, DC 20436
