SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[License No. 04/04-0213]

 

Quantum Capital Partners, Ltd.; Notice of Surrender of License

    Notice is hereby given that Quantum Capital Partners, Ltd. (QCP), 
4400 NE 25th Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33308 has surrendered its 
License to operate as a small business investment company under the 
Small Business Act of 1958, as amended (ACT). QCP was licensed by the 
Small Business Administration on July 7, 1982.
    Under the authority vested by the Act pursuant to the Regulations 
promulgated thereunder, the surrender of the License was accepted on 
November 4, 1994, and accordingly, all rights, privileges, and 
franchises derived therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Companies)

    Dated: November 22, 1994.
Robert D. Stillman,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
