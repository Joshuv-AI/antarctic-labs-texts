DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Pennsylvania Power & Light Company; Notice of Filing

August 7, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 18, 1997, Pennsylvania Power & Light 
Company (PP&L), filed a Refund Report July 18, 1997, in connection with 
a late-filed Service Agreement with Koch Power Services, Inc. (KPSI) 
for the sale of capacity and/or energy under PP&L's Short Term Capacity 
and/or energy Sales Tariff. The Service Agreement adds KPSI as an 
eligible customer under the Tariff.
    PP&L states that copies of this filing have been supplied to KPSI 
and to the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before August 20, 1997. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
