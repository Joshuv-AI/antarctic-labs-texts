MORRIS K. UDALL SCHOLARSHIP AND EXCELLENCE IN NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL 
POLICY FOUNDATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    The Board of Trustees of the Morris K. Udall Scholarship & 
Excellence in National Environmental Policy Foundation will hold a 
meeting beginning at 9:00 a.m. on Thursday, June 18, 1998, at the 
office of Bracy Williams & Co., 601 13th Street NW, Ste. 900 South, 
Washington, D.C.
    The matters to be considered will include (1) A report on the U. 
S., Institute of Environmental Conflict Resolution; and (2) A report 
from the Udall Center for Studies and Public Policy. The meeting is 
open to the public.
    Contact Person for More Information: Christopher L. Helms, 803 East 
First Street, Tucson, AZ 85719. Telephone: (520) 670-5523.

    Dated this 11th day of May, 1998.
Christopher L. Helms,
Director.
