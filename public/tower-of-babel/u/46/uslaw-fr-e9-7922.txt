OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


April 7, 2009 Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Annual Public Hearing meeting was 
published in the Federal Register (Volume 74, Number 56, Page 12913) on 
March 25, 2009. No requests were received to provide testimony or 
submit written statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's annual 
public hearing scheduled for 2 p.m. on April 7, 2009 has been 
cancelled.

via facsimile at (202) 218-0136, or via e-mail at 
[email protected].
