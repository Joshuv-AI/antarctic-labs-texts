FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Deletion of Items From Sunshine Act Meeting

February 21, 2018.

scheduled for consideration at the Thursday, February 22, 2018, Open 
Meeting and previously listed in the Commission's Notice of February 
15, 2018.
