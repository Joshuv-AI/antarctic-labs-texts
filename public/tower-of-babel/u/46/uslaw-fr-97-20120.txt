Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Union Electric Development Corporation; Notice of Filing

July 25, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 8, 1997, Union Electric Development 
Corporation (UEDC) filed with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
an application for authority to charge market based rates and for 
certain waivers and authorizations. UEDC requested waiver of notice to 
permit its proposed rate schedule to become effective on July 9, 1997, 
one day after the date of filing.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protect said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before August 6, 1997. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
