SUMMARY: This document sets forth 14 reservation proposals requesting 
to amend the FM Table of Allotments by reserving certain vacant FM 
allotments for noncommercial educational use in Canton, Illinois, 
Cedarville, Illinois, Council Grove, Kansas, Clifton, Illinois, 
Farmersburg, Indiana, Freeport, Illinois, Fowler, Indiana, Golden 
Meadow, Louisiana, Homer, Louisiana, Madison, Indiana, Pinckneyville, 
Illinois, Terre Haute, Indiana, Ringgold, Louisiana and Smith Mills, 
Kentucky. The Audio Division requests comment on petitions filed by 
Illinois State University and Starboard Media Foundation, Inc. 
proposing the reservation of vacant Channel 277A at Canton, Illinois 
for noncommercial use. The reference coordinates for Channel *277A at 
Canton are 40-28-27 North Latitude and 90-3-1 West Longitude. The Audio 
Division requests comment on a petition filed by The Catholic Diocese 
of Rockford proposing the reservation of vacant Channel 258A at 
Cedarville, Illinois. The reference coordinates for Channel *258A at 
Cedarville are 42-21-50 North Latitude and 89-40-59 West Longitude. See 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION, infra.

comments on or before June 11, 2004.
