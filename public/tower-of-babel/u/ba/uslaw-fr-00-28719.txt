DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Board of Visitors Meeting

AGENCY: Department of Defense Acquisition University.

ACTION: Board of visitors meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The next meeting of the Defense Acquisition University (DAU) 
Board of Visitors (BoV) will be held at the Packard Conference Center, 
Building 184, Ft. Belvoir, Virginia on Wednesday December 6, 2000 from 
0900 until 1500. The purpose of this meeting is to report back to the 
BoV on continuing items of interest.
    The meeting is open to the public; however, because of space 
limitations, allocation of seating will be made on a first-come, first 
served basis. Persons desiring to attend the meeting should call Mr. 
John Michel at 703.805.4575.

    Dated: November 3, 2000.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
