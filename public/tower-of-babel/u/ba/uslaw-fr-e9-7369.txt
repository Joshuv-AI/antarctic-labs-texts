DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


IndyMac Federal Bank, FSB; Pasadena, CA; Notice of Appointment of 
Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, on March 19, 2009, pursuant to the 
authority contained in section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, 
the Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS) replaced the Federal Deposit 
Insurance Corporation (FDIC) as conservator for IndyMac Federal Bank, 
FSB, Pasadena, California (Association) with the FDIC as Receiver for 
the Association and OTS duly appointed the FDIC as sole receiver for 
the Association (OTS Order No. 2009-17).

    Dated: March 27, 2009.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
