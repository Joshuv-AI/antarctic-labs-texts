ARCTIC RESEARCH COMMISSION


Meetings

November 4, 1996.
    Notice is hereby given that the U.S. Arctic Research Commission 
will hold its 45th Meeting from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Monday and 
Tuesday, 2 and 3 December at the American Geophysical Union, 2000 
Florida Avenue, NW, Washington, DC. Agenda items include:

(1) Call to order and approval of the Agenda
(2) Approval of the minutes of the 44th Meeting
(3) Reports of Congressional Liaisons
(4) Agency Reports
(5) State of Alaska Report
(6) University of Alaska Report
(7) ARCUS Report
(8) Polar Information Working Group
(9) NASA Arctic Programs Review
(10) Strategic Planning
(11) Goals and Priorities Report

    The Monday business meeting will be followed by an Executive 
Session.
    Any person planning to attend this meeting who requires special 
accessibility features and/or auxiliary aids, such as sign language 
interpreters must inform the Commission in advance of those needs.

Contact Person for More Information: Dr. Garrett W. Brass, Executive 
Director, Arctic Research Commission, 703-525-0111 or TDD 703-306-0090.
Garrett W. Brass,
Executive Director.
