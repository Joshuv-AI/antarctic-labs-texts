Independence Pipeline Company, ANR Pipeline Company, National 
Fuel Gas Supply Corporation, and Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line 
Corporation; Errata (December 22, 1999), Interim Order

Issued December 17, 1999.
    On December 17, 1999, the Commission issued an Interim Order in the 
above-docketed proceedings (89 FERC 61,283). The following changes 
should be noted.
    (1) On page 103, Appendix A, delete Certificate Condition number 
14.
    (2) On page 100, add Ordering Paragraph (G) to read as follows: 
Independence, Transco and ANR each shall immediately designate an 
ombudsman to address promptly any complaints from landowners regarding 
trespassing or objectionable land acquisition techniques, as set out in 
the body of this order.
    (3) On page 101, formally Ordering Paragraph (G) becomes Ordering 
Paragraph (H).
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
