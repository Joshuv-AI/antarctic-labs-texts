M & J Clothing Sample, El Paso, Texas; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 27, 1998 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on of former workers at M & J Clothing Sample, located in El 
Paso, Texas (TA-W-34, 791).
    All production and production-related employment at M & J Clothing 
Sample, located in El Paso, Texas ceased in June of 1997, more than one 
year prior to the date of the petition (July 7, 1998).
    Section 223 of the Act specifies that no certification may apply to 
any worker whose last separation occurred more than one year before the 
date of the petition.
    Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. on this 17th day of August 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
