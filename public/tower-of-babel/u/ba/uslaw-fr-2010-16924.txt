FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Farm Credit Administration Board; Amendment to Sunshine Act 
Meeting

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.
SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Government in the Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 
552b(e)(3)), the Farm Credit Administration gave notice on July 6, 2010 
(75 FR 38811) of the regular meeting of the Farm Credit Administration 
Board (Board) scheduled for July 8, 2010. This notice is to amend the 
agenda by adding an item to the open session of that meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Roland E. Smith, Secretary to the Farm 
Credit Administration Board, (703) 883-4025, TTY (703) 883-4056.

ADDRESSES: Farm Credit Administration, 1501 Farm Credit Drive, McLean, 
Virginia 22102-5090.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Parts of this meeting of the Board will be 
open to the public (limited space available), and parts of this meeting 
will be closed to the public. The agenda for July 8, 2010, is amended 
by adding an item to the open session to read as follows:

Open Session

B. New Business

     Proposed Bookletter--Farm Credit System Bank Merger 
Applications.

    Dated: July 7, 2010.
Roland E. Smith,
Secretary, Farm Credit Administration Board.
