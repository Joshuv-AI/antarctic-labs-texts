OFFICE OF THE UNITED STATES TRADE REPRESENTATIVE


Designation of Singapore Under 19 U.S.C. 2511(b)(3)

    Under the authority delegated to me by the President in section 1-
201 of Executive Order 12260 of December 31, 1980, I hereby direct that 
products of Singapore be treated as eligible products for purposes of 
section 1-101 of Executive Order 12260. Such treatment shall not apply 
to procurements by the Department of Energy, the Department of 
Transportation, the Army Corps of Engineers, the Tennessee Valley 
Authority and the Bureau of Reclamation. If Singapore has not completed 
negotiations on its accession to the WTO Government Procurement 
Agreement by July 31, 1996, this designation may be revoked.
Michael Kantor,
United States Trade Representative.
