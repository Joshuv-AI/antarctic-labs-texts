DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP95-449-000]


Trunkline Gas Company; Notice of Technical Conference

November 24, 1995.
    In the Commission's order issued on October 25, 1995 in the above-
captioned proceeding, the Commission ordered that a technical 
conference be convened to resolve issues raised by the filing. The 
conference to address the issues has been scheduled for December 11, 
1995, at 2:00 p.m. in a room to be designated at the offices of the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
