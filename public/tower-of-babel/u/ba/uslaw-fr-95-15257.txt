DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Office of Educational Research and Improvement (OERI)--Regional 
Educational Laboratory Program; Availability of Request

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Notice of availability of request for proposals.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On May 18, 1995, the Secretary published a notice in the Commerce 
Business Daily (page 2) announcing intent to issue a request for 
proposals (RFP) for the Regional Educational Laboratory Program. The 
Department of Education proposes to operate ten Regional Educational 
Laboratories to serve ten geographic regions. The closing date for 
responding to the RFP is August 8, 1995.
    The purpose of this notice is to inform interested parties that 
copies of the RFP may be obtained--
     By telephone at (202) 708-6498;
     By mail or telegram to Department of Education, 7th and D 
Streets, S.W., Room 3633, ROB 3, Washington, D.C. 20202-4725; or
     In person at the mail address.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
LaVerne Reddick (202) 708-8222 or Latonya Simpson (202) 708-6498.

    Dated: June 16, 1995.
Sharon P. Robinson,
Assistant Secretary for Educational Research and Improvement.
