DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, June 26, 2013, 06:00 p.m. to 
June 27, 2013, 03:00 p.m., Holiday Inn Express, 1775 Rockville Pike, 
Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal Register on 
March 29, 2013, 78 FR 19275.
    This notice is being amended to change the location from Holiday 
Inn Express, 1775 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852 to Hilton 
Washington, DC/Rockville, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852. The 
meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 7, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
