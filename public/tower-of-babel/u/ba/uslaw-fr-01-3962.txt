Radio Broadcasting Services; Rincon, PR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the request of Ocean Communications to 
allot Channel 300B to Rincon, PR, as its first local aural service. See 
65 FR 44507, July 18, 2000. The proposed transmitter site is located 
within the Desecheo National Wildlife Refuge and the Fish and Wildlife 
Service, U.S. Department of the Interior, has stated that it is 
unlikely that it would favorably consider a request to erect a tower 
there.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
