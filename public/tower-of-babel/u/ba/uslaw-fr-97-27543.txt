DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY


Departmental Office; Debt Management Advisory Committee; Meeting

    The notice of the public meeting of the Treasury Borrowing Advisory 
Committee of The Bond Market Association that was announced in the 
Federal Register of October 7, 1997 (62 FR 52378) is hereby amended. 
The time of the meeting has been changed from 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. 
Eastern Time on October 28, 1997. The notification that was published 
on October 1 remains unchanged in all other respects.
    October 10, 1997.
Gary Gensler,
Assistant Secretary (Financial Markets).
