DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
Employment and Traininig Administration

 

Labor Surplus Area Classification Under Executive Order 12073 and 
10582; Time Extension for the Annual List of Labor Surplus Areas; 
Notice of Time Extension for the Annual List of Labor Surplus Areas

AGENCY: Employment and Training Administration, Labor.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The annual list of labor surplus areas for the period October 
1, 1993 through September 30, 1994, is extended until further notice. 
The Employment and Training Administration will continue to accept 
exceptional circumstance petitions for labor surplus area designations 
on the basis of the criteria in effect for the current annual list 
which is being extended. Upon completion of a conversion on the labor 
surplus area program, a new labor surplus area list will be announced 
and published in the Federal Register.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: William J. McGarrity, Labor Economist, 
USES, Employment and Training Administration, 200 Constitution Avenue 
NW., Room N-4470, Attention: TEESS, Washington, D.C. 20210. Telephone: 
202-219-5185.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. on September 26, 1994.
Doug Ross,
Assistant Secretary.
