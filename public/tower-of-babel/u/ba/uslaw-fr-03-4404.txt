DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Notice of Availability of Novel Composite Material Technologies 
for Exclusive, Partially Exclusive or Non-Exclusive Licenses

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive or non-exclusive licenses relative to 
novel composite material based technologies as described in U.S. Patent 
application ``Method for Producing Nano-Textured Solid Surfaces'' (U.S. 
Patent Application No. 10/318667). Any license shall comply with 35 
U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR part 404.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael D. Rausa, U.S. Army Research 
Laboratory, Office of Research and

Ground, MD 21005-5425, telephone: (410) 278-5038.
