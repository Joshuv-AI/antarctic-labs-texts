NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[Docket No. 40-3453-MLA-3; ASLBP No. 99-761-04-MLA]


Moab Mill Reclamation Trust; Notice of Reconstitution

    Pursuant to the authority contained in 10 CFR 2.721 and 2.1207, the 
Special Assistant in the captioned 10 CFR part 2, subpart L proceeding 
is hereby replaced by appointing Administrative Judge Richard F. Cole 
in place of Administrative Judge Frederick J. Shon.
    All correspondence, documents, and other material shall be filed 
with the Presiding Officer in accordance with 10 CFR 2.1203. The 
address of the new Special Assistant is: Richard F. Cole, Atomic Safety 
and Licensing Board Panel, U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission, 
Washington, DC 20555-0001.


[[Page 54081]]


    Issued at Rockville, Maryland, this 30th day of August 2000.
G. Paul Bollwerk III,
Chief Administrative Judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel.
