DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Rural Electrification Administration

 

Municipal Interest Rates for the Second Quarter of 1994: 
Correction

AGENCY: Rural Electrification Administration, USDA.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: There is a typographical error in the notice document 
published on April 1, 1994, at 59 FR 15371, setting out the interest 
rates on advances from municipal rate loans for interest rate terms 
that begin during the second quarter of 1994. The correct interest rate 
for terms that end in 1997 is 4.000 percent.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sue Arnold, Management Analyst, U.S. 
Department of Agriculture, Rural Electrification Administration, room 
2230-s, 14th Street & Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, DC 20250-
1500. Telephone: 202-720-0736. FAX: 202-720-4120.

    Dated: March 31, 1994.

Administrator.
