FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 99-10133) published on page 
20003 of the issue for Friday, April 23, 1999.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for Amoret Bancshares, Butler, Missouri, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City (D. Michael Manies, 
Assistant Vice President) 925 Grand Avenue, Kansas City, Missouri 
64198-0001:
    1. Amoret Bancshares, Butler, Missouri; to acquire 100 percent of 
the voting shares of C.J. Bancshares, Inc., Harrisonville, Missouri, 
and thereby indirectly acquire Citizens Bank of Missouri, 
Harrisonville, Missouri.
    Comments on this application must be received by May 17, 1999.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, April 26, 
1999.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
