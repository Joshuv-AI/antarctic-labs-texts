U.S. CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION

TIME AND DATE: 2:00 p.m., Wednesday, June 29, 1994.

Location: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the Public.

Matter to be Considered:

1. Gas-Fired Water Heaters

    The Commission will consider options for Commission action to 
address the risk that gas-fired water heaters will ignite vapors 
from flammable liquids that are present in the home.

    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207 (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: June 23, 1994.
Sadye E. Dunn,
Secretary.
