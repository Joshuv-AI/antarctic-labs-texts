CTS Corporation; CTS Communications Components, Inc., Including 
On-Site Leased Workers of Excel and Spherion Albuquerque, New Mexico; 
Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at CTS Corporation, CTS 
Communications Components, Inc., including on-site leased workers of 
Excel and Spherion, Albuquerque, New Mexico. The application contained 
no new substantial information which would bear importantly on the 
Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was 
issued.

TA-W-56,674; CTS Corporation, CTS Communications Components, Inc., 
Including On-Site Leased Workers of Excel and Spherion, Albuquerque, 
New Mexico (June 7, 2005).

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 8th day of June 2005.
Timothy Sullivan,
