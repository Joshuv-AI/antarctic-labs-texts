DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-2-2024]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 134; Authorization of Production 
Activity; Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.; (Passenger Motor 
Vehicles); Chattanooga, Tennessee

    On January 4, 2024, Volkswagen Group of America, Inc., submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facility within FTZ 134, in Chattanooga, Tennessee.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (89 FR 1520, January 10, 2024). On May 
3, 2024, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that no 
further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
section 400.14.

    Dated: May 3, 2024.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
