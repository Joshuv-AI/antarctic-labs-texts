DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Economics and Statistics Administration


Performance Review Board Membership; Correction

SUMMARY: In FR Doc. 01-23281 published on page 48232 in the Federal 
Register on September 19, 2001, the list of executives eligible to 
serve on the Economics and Statistics Administration Performance Review 
Board requires a change to the spelling of one name and the addition of 
two board members. Change the name of ``Brent R. Moton'' to read 
``Brent R. Moulton'', and add the names: Alan C. Lorish and Gloria 
Gutierrez.

    Dated: October 3, 2001.
James K. White,
Associate Under Secretary for Management, Chair, Performance Review 
Board.
