FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-123; RM-9062]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Grand Isle, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Grand Isle Radio, allots 
Channel 283A to Grand Isle, Louisiana, as the community's first local 
aural transmission service. See 62 FR 23427, April 30, 1997. Channel 
283A can be allotted to Grand Isle in compliance with the Commission's 
minimum distance separation requirements without the imposition of a 
site restriction. The coordinates for Channel 283A at Grand Isle are 
29-13-54 NL and 89-59-54 WL. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

DATES: Effective October 6, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications will open on October 6, 1997, and close on November 6, 
1997.
