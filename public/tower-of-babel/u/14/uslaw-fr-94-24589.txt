Title 3--
The President
                Executive Order 12929 of September 29, 1994

 
Delegation of Authority Regarding the Naval 
                Petroleum and Oil Shale Reserves

                By the authority vested in me as President by the 
                Constitution and the laws of the United States of 
                America, including section 301 of title 3 and sections 
                7427 and 7428 of title 10, United States Code, and in 
                order to meet the goals and requirements of the Naval 
                Petroleum and Oil Shale Reserves, it is hereby ordered 
                as follows:

                The functions vested in the President by sections 7427 
                and 7428 of title 10 of the United States Code are 
                delegated to the Secretary of Energy.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    September 29, 1994.
