COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Agenda and Notice of Public Meeting of the New Hampshire Advisory 
Committee

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the rules and 
regulations of the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights, that a community 
forum of the New Hampshire Advisory Committee will begin at 9 a.m. and 
end at 3:30 p.m., Thursday, December 4, 2003, in the Aldermanic 
Chambers of Manchester City Hall, 108 Elm Street, Manchester, New 
Hampshire.. The purpose of the community forum is to address issues 
related to access to health care by limited-English-proficient and 
hearing-impaired persons.
    Persons desiring additional information should contact Aonghas St-
Hilaire of the Eastern Regional Office, (202) 376-7533, TDD: (202) 376-
8116. Hearing impaired persons who will attend the community forum and 
require the services of a sign language interpreter should contact the 
Eastern Regional Office at least 10 (ten) working days before the 
scheduled date of the community forum.
    The community forum will be conducted pursuant to the provisions of 
the rules and regulations of the Commission.

    Dated at Washington, DC November 19, 2003.
Ivy L. Davis,
Chief, Regional Programs Coordination Unit.
