Voluntary Poultry Grade Standards

Correction

    In proposed rule document 94-25760 beginning on page 52469, in the 
issue of Tuesday, October 18, 1994, make the following corrections:


Sec. 70.221  [Corrected]

    1.On page 52474, in the second column, in Sec. 70.221, in paragraph 
(g)(3), in the table, under the heading entitled ``Maximum aggregate 
area permitted'' insert a line to separate the heading from the 
subheading entitled ``Breast, legs, and parts''.
    2.On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 70.221, in 
paragraph (g)(3), in the same table, under the subheading entitled 
``Breast, legs, and parts'', in the first line, ``1\3/4\ in.'' should 
read ``\3/4\ in.''.
    3.On the same page, in the third column, in Sec. 70.221, in 
paragraph (g)(4), in the table, under the subheading entitled 
``Elsewhere'', in the first line, ``1\3/4\ in.'' should read ``1\1/4\ 
in.''.


Sec. 70.230  [Corrected]

    4.On the same page, in the second column, in Sec. 70.230, in 
paragraph (e)(2), in the table, the heading ``Maximum aggregate'' 
should read ``Maximum aggregate area permitted''.
    5.On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 70.230, in 
paragraph (e)(2), in the table, under the heading ``Maximum aggregate 
area permitted'', in the second line, ``1 incn'' should read ``1 
inch''.
