DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration


Drug Safety and Risk Management Advisory Committee; Notice of 
Postponement

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is postponing the 
meeting of the Drug Safety and Risk Management Advisory Committee 
scheduled for September 18, 2003, due to Hurricane Isabel. This meeting 
was announced in the Federal Register of June 30, 2003 (68 FR 38713). 
An amendment to the notice of meeting was announced in the Federal 
Register of July 23, 2003 (68 FR 43534). The future date for this 
meeting is to be determined.
