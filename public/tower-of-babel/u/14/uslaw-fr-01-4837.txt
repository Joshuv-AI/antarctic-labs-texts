NATIONAL ARCHIVES AND RECORDS ADMINISTRATION

Information Security Oversight Office


National Industrial Security Program Policy Advisory Committee; 
Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 U.S.C. 
app. 2) and implementing regulation 41 CFR 101.6, announcement is made 
for a meeting of the National Industrial Security Program Policy 
Advisory Committee (NISPPAC).

DATES: April 5, 2001, from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.

ADDRESSES: National Archives and Records Administration, 700 
Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Room 105, Washington, DC 20408.
    Purpose: To discuss National Industrial Security Program policy 
matters.
    This meeting will be open to the public. However, due to space 
limitations and access procedures, the name and telephone number of 
individuals planning to attend must be submitted to the Information 
Security Oversight Office (ISOO) no later than March 28, 2001. ISOO 
will provide additional instructions for gaining access to the location 
of the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Steven Garfinkel, Director, 
Information Security Oversight Office, National Archives Building, 700 
Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Room 100, Washington, DC 20408, telephone 
(202) 219-5250.

    Dated: February 14, 2001.
Mary Ann Hadyka,
Committee Management Officer.
