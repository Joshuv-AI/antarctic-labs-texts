Forest City Technologies, Inc., Wixom, MI; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 25, 2006 in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers of Forest City Technologies Inc., Wixom, Michigan.
    One of the three petitioning workers was separated more than one 
year prior to the October 18, 2006 date of the petition. The petition 
has therefore been deemed invalid. Consequently, the investigation has 
been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 27th day of October 2006.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
