Wright Express LLC, a Subsidiary of Cendant Corporation Quality 
Assurance Department, South Portland, ME; Dismissal of Application for 
Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Wright Express LLC., a subsidiary 
of Cendant Corporation, Quality Assurance Department, South Portland, 
Maine. The application contained no new substantial information which 
would bear importantly on the Department's determination. Therefore, 
dismissal of the application was issued.
