Citizens South Banking Corporation, Gastonia, NC; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on August 14, 2002, the Director, 
Examination Policy, Office of Thrift Supervision (``OTS''), or her 
designee, acting pursuant to delegated authority, approved the 
application of Citizens South Bank, Gastonia, North Carolina, to 
convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of the application 
are available for inspection by appointment (phone number: 202-906-5922 
or e-mail: [email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, 1700 
G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS Southeast Regional 
Office, 1475 Peachtree Street, NE., Atlanta, GA 30309.

    Dated: August 15, 2002.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
