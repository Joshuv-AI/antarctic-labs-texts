DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

National Institute of Corrections


Advisory Board Meeting

TIME AND DATA: 8:00 a.m., Tuesday, July 1, 1997.

PLACE: Sheraton City Centre, 1143 New Hampshire Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20037.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Update on the Corrections Program Office 
Violent Offender and Truth In Sentencing Grant Program, update on the 
NIC Executive Excellence Program, a history of NIC Work Plan/
Feasibility Study, victims issues discussion points, a status report on 
the Mental Health Survey in Jails, an update on NIC's Strategic 
Planning, the NIC Program Plan for FY 1998, election of officers and 
liaisons, and a quarterly report from the Office of Justice Programs.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Larry Solomon, Deputy Director, (202) 307-3106, ext. 155.
Morris L. Thigpen,
Director.
