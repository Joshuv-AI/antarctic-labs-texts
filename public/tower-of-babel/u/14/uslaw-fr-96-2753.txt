DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Strategic Environmental Research and Development Program (SERDP) 
Fiscal Year 1996 Annual Report and Five-Year (1996-2000) Strategic 
Investment Plan; Notice

    In accordance with Title 10, U.S.C. Section 2902, the Strategic 
Environmental Research and Development Program Fiscal Year 1996 

[[Page 4964]]
Annual Report and Five-Year (1996-2000) Strategic Investment Plan is 
available for review by the public for a period of 30 days from the 
date of this notice.
    Reviews shall be by appointment only, by contacting Ms. Jenny 
Dowden at (703) 506-1400, extension 563 and conducted at the SERDP 
Support Office in the space of LABAT-ANDERSON Incorporated, 8000 
Westpark Drive, McLean, Virginia 22102. Additional copies of the Fiscal 
Year 1996 Annual Report and Five-Year (1996-2000) Strategic Investment 
Plan are not available.

    Dated: February 2, 1996.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
