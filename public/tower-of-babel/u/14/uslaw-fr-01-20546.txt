Cooper Bussman, Black Mountain, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 23, 2001, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by the company on behalf of workers at Cooper Bussman, Black 
Mountain, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 31st day of July, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
