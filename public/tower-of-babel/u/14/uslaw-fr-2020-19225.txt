Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2020-0453.
    Petitioner: Soaring Society of America.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  61.56(c)(1).
    Description of Relief Sought: Soaring Society of America requests 
relief from Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) Sec.  
61.56(c)(1) for the purpose of training under Sec. Sec.  61.109(f)(1) 
or (f)(2) and 61.129(f)(1) or (f)(2). This exemption would permit an 
applicant who seeks to add a glider category rating to a pilot 
certificate to conduct solo flight operations as required by Sec.  
61.63(b) without meeting the flight review requirements of Sec.  
61.56(c)(1) while under the supervision of a certified flight 
instructor (CFI).
