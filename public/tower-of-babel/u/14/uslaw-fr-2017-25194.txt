ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[Region 4 Library; FRL-9970-91-Region 4]


Notice of Change to Operating Hours

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Informational notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) is issuing this 
notice to advise the public that the Region 4 Library will change its 
operating hours from 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. to 9:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m., Monday 
through Friday. The library will remain closed on Federal holidays.

DATES: The new operating hours for the Region 4 Library will be 
effective November 1, 2017.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The Region 4 Federal Library Managers 
Shayla Patillo at (404) 562-8385 or LouAnn Gross at (404) 562-9642.

    Dated: October 27, 2017.
Kristy H. Eubanks,
Assistant Regional Administrator, Region 4.
