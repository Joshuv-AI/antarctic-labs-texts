Armstrong Energy Resources; Notice of Extension of Time

September 27, 1996.
    Take notice that a 90-day extension of time for filing comments on 
Scoping Document I issued July 24, 1996, in the above referenced 
proceedings is granted. Comments which were due October 7, 1996, will 
now be due by January 6, 1997.
    A subsequent public scoping meeting concerning the impact of 
transmission line right-of-way will be conducted at a future date 
following public notice of such meeting.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
