National Institute on Drug Abuse; Amended Notice of Meeting

Institute on Drug Abuse Special Emphasis Panel, October 16, 2013, 08:00 
a.m. to October 16, 2013, 05:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Neuroscience Center, 6001 Executive Boulevard, Rockville, MD 20852

21, 2013, 9:00 a.m. to November 21, 2013, 6:00 p.m., Hyatt Regency 
Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro Center, 7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, 
MD 20814. The meeting is closed to the public.
