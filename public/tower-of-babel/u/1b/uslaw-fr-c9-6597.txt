FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION

16 CFR Part 241


Request for Comment Concerning Guides for the Dog and Cat Food 
Industry

Correction

    In proposed rule document 99-6597, beginning on page 13368, in the 
issue of Thursday, March 18, 1999, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 13369, in the first column, under the heading II. 
Regulatory Review Program, in the second line from the bottom, 
``technologies'' should read ``technological''.
    2. On the same page, in the same column, under the heading III. 
Request for Comment, in paragraph (2), in the fourth line, ``effect'' 
should read ``affect''.
    3. On the same page, in the same column, under the same heading, in 
paragraph (6), in the last line of the column, ``good'' should read 
``food''.
