Notice of Public Meeting: Concessions Management Advisory Board

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of public meeting of the Concessions 
Management Advisory Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On February 14, 2013, the National Park Service announced that 
a public meeting of the Concessions Management Advisory Board would be 
held March 20, 2013 in Washington, DC. This meeting has been cancelled. 
A future meeting date for this Board may be scheduled and would be 
announced in the Federal Register.

DATES: The public meeting previously scheduled for March 20 is 
cancelled.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For further information contact 
Deborah Harvey, Acting Chief, National Park Service, Commercial 
Services Program, 1201 Eye Street NW., Washington, DC 20005, Telephone: 
202-513-7156.

    Dated: March 11, 2013.
Lena McDowall,
Associate Director, Business Services.
