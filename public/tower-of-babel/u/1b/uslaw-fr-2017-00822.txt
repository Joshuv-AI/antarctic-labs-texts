Category 3 Source Security and Accountability; Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Source protection; public meetings and request for comment; 
correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register (FR) on January 9, 
2017, regarding Category 3 source security and accountability. This 
action is necessary to delete erroneous text in the paragraph under the 
heading ``IV. Public Comments Process.''

DATES: The correction is effective January 17, 2017.
