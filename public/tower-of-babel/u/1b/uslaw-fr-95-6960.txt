DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Natural Resources Conservation Service


East Wenatchee Watershed, Washington; Notice

AGENCY: Natural Resources Conservation Service.

ACTION: Notice of deauthorization of federal funding.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Summary: Pursuant to the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act, 
Public Law 83-566, and the Natural Resources Conservation Service 
Guidelines (7 CFR 622), the Natural [[Page 15125]] Resources 
Conservation Service gives notice of the deauthorization of Federal 
funding for the East Wenatchee Watershed project, Douglas County, 
Washington, effective on February 10, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lynn A. Brown, State Conservationist, 
Natural Resources Conservation Service, W. 316 Boone Avenue, Suite 
#450, Spokane, Washington 99201-2348, telephone: (509) 353-2337.

    Dated: March 13, 1995.
Lynn A. Brown,
State Conservationist.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 10.904, 
Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention. Office of Management and 
Budget Circular No. A-95 regarding State and local clearinghouse 
review of Federal and federally assisted programs and projects is 
applicable.)
