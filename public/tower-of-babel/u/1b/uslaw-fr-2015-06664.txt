List of Approved Spent Fuel Storage Casks: Holtec International 
HI-STORM Underground Maximum Capacity Canister Storage System, 
Certificate of Compliance No. 1040; Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
final rule that was published in the Federal Register (FR) on March 6, 
2015. The final rule amends the NRC's spent fuel storage regulations by 
adding the Holtec International HI-STORM Underground Maximum Capacity 
Canister Storage System, Certificate of Compliance No. 1040, to the 
``List of approved spent fuel storage casks.'' This action is necessary 
to correct the Certificate Expiration Date from March 6, 2035, to April 
6, 2035.

DATES: This correction is effective April 6, 2015.
