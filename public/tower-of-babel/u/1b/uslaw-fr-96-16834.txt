DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Defense Partnership Council Meeting

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Change in meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 20, 1996, the Department of Defense published a notice 
to announce a meeting of the Defense Partnership Council. (61 FR 31510-
31511) This notice is to announce that the meeting date has been 
changed to July 24, 1996. Comments should be received by July 19, 1996, 
in order to be considered at the July 24 meeting. All other information 
remains the same.

ext. 704.
