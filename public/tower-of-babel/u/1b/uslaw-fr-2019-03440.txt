DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2079-079]


Placer County Water Agency; Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 10, 2019, the Commission issued a notice through the 
FERC eLibrary system \1\ setting March 11, 2019, as the end of the 
formal period to file comments, motions to intervene, protests, 
recommendations, and terms and conditions on the amendment application 
for the Middle Fork American River Hydroelectric Project No. 2079. Due 
to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies between December 22, 
2018 and January 25, 2019, the Commission is extending the comment 
period until March 26, 2019.
