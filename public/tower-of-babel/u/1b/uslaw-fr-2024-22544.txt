[AC 187-1T]


Schedule of Charges Outside the United States

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) is announcing the 
availability of Advisory Circular (AC) 187-1T which transmits an 
updated schedule of charges for services of FAA Flight Standards 
Aviation Safety Inspectors outside the United States.

DATES: This AC is effective on October 1, 2024.

ADDRESSES: How to obtain copies: A copy of this publication may be 
downloaded from: http://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Tish Thompkins, Flight Standards 
Service, AFS-50, Federal Aviation Administration, 800 Independence 
Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20591, telephone (202) 267-0996, 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The advisory circular has been updated in 
accordance with the procedures listed in 14 CFR part 187, Appendix A.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on September 26, 2024.
Robert Ruiz,
Deputy Executive Director, Flight Standards Service.
