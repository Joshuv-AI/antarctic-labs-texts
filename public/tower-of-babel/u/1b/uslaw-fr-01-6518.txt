BROADCASTING BOARD OF GOVERNORS


Sunshine Act Meeting

DATE AND TIME: March 29, 2001; 11 a.m.

PLACE: The Doral Hotel, 4400 N.W. 87th Avenue, Miami, Florida 33166.

OPEN MEETING: The members of the Advisory Board for Cuba Broadcasting 
will meet in open session to review and discuss a number of issues 
pertaining to the Martis including programming contracts, UHF 
reception, and the status of the aerostat. The purpose of the meeting 
is to review the effectiveness of the operations of Radio and TV Marti 
and advise the President and the Broadcasting Board of Governors 
accordingly.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Persons interested in obtaining 
more information should contact Yvonne Soler McKinley at (305) 437-
7244.

    Dated: March 12, 2001.
Carol Booker,
Legal Counsel.
