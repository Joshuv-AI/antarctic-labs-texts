Allister Fabricating, Inc., Lannon, WI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 11, 2003, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Allister 
Fabricating, Inc., Lannon, Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 30th day of June, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
