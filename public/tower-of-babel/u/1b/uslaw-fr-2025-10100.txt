Rivers Electric, LLC; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal of 
Application for Amendment of License

    On July 14, 2023, Rivers Electric, LLC (licensee) filed an 
application for non-capacity amendment of the license for the 1.00-
megawatt Mill Pond Hydroelectric Project No. 9985. On March 31, 2025, 
the licensee filed a notice of withdrawal of the amendment application. 
The project is located on the Catskill Creek in Greene County, New 
York.
    No motion in opposition to the notice of withdrawal has been filed, 
and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the withdrawal. 
Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the application became effective on 
April 15, 2025, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
