DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. CP13-83-000]


Arlington Storage Company, LLC, LP; Notice of Reopening of 
Comment Period

    On September 13, 2013, the Commission issued a notice setting 
October 15, 2013, as the date to file comments regarding the Gallery 2 
Expansion Project Environmental Assessment. Because of the limited 
funding to federal programs and resources between October 1 and 16, 
2013, the Commission is reopening the comment period to allow affected 
agencies and others the opportunity to comment. This reopened comment 
period now expires on November 1, 2013.

    Dated: October 18, 2013.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
