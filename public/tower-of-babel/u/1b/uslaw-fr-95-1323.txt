Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-30,532]


Coordinated Apparel Group, Inc., Jackson, South Carolina; 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 17, 1994 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Coordinated Apparel Group, 
Incorporated, Jackson, South Carolina.
    All workers of the subject firm are covered under amended 
certification (TA-W-30,364C). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose; and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 10th day of January 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
