Power Authority of the State of New York; Notice Granting Late 
Intervention

October 10, 2003.
    On May 22, 2002, the Commission issued a notice of application 
accepted for filing and solicitation of motions to intervene and 
protests for the St. Lawrence-FDR Power Project 2000, located on the 
St. Lawrence River in St. Lawrence County, New York. The notice 
established July 22, 2002, as the deadline for filing motions to 
intervene.
    On March 28, 2003, Congressman Dennis J. Kucinich, representing 
Ohio's 10th Congressional District, filed an untimely motion to 
intervene and comments. Granting the motion to intervene will not 
unduly delay or disrupt the proceeding, or prejudice other parties to 
it. Therefore, pursuant to Rule 214,\1\ the motion to intervene filed 
by Congressman Dennis J. Kucinich is granted, subject to the 
Commission's rules and regulations.
