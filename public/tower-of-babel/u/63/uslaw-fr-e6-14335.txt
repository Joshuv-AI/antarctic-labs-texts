AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Department of Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Notice of Performance Review Board (PRB) appointments.

SUMMARY: DOT publication of the names of the persons selected to serve 
on the various Departmental PRBs as required by 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Patricia A. Prosperi, Departmental 
Director, Office of Human Resource Management, (202) 366-4088.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The persons named below have been selected 
to serve on one or more Departmental PRBs.
