Filing of Plat of Survey, Maine; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning the filing of a plat of survey. The notice 
contained an incorrect filing time period.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Bureau of Land Management, 7450 Boston 
Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 22153. Attn: Cadastral Survey. 703-
440-1688.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of August 3, 2006, in FR Doc. E6-12097 on 
page 44042, under DATES, correct 30 calendar days from the date of 
publication in the Federal Register to read: on the date of publication 
in the Federal Register.
