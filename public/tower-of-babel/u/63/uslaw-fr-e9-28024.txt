OmniAmerican Bank, Fort Worth, TX; Approval of Conversion 
Application

    Notice is hereby given that on November 12, 2009, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of OmniAmerican Bank, Fort 
Worth, Texas, to convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of 
the application are available for inspection by appointment (phone 
number: (202) 906-5922 or e-mail: public.info@ots.treas.gov">public.info@ots.treas.gov) at the 
Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the 
OTS Western Regional Office, 225 E. John Carpenter Freeway, Suite 500, 
Irving, Texas 75062-2326.

    Dated: November 16, 2009.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
