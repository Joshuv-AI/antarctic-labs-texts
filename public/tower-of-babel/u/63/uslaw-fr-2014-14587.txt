DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the 50th Anniversary Kennedy Half-Dollar Silver Coin 
Collection and 50th Anniversary Kennedy Half-Dollar Uncirculated Coin 
Set

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing a price of $99.95 for the 
50th Anniversary Kennedy Half-Dollar Silver Coin Collection and a price 
of $9.95 for the 50th Anniversary Kennedy Half-Dollar Uncirculated Coin 
Set.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Marc Landry, Acting Associate Director 
for Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.

    Dated: June 17, 2014.
Richard A. Peterson,
Deputy Director, United States Mint.
