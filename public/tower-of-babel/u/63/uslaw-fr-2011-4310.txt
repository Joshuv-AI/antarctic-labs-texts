40 CFR Part 60


Standards of Performance for New Stationary Sources

CFR Correction

    In Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 60 (Sec.  60.1 
to end of part 60 sections), revised as of July 1, 2010:
    1. On page 334, at the end of Sec.  60.101a, an effective date note 
is added to read as follows:


Sec.  60.101a  Definitions.

* * * * *

    Effective Date Note: At 73 FR 78552, Dec. 22, 2008, in Sec.  
60.101a the definition of ``flare'' was stayed from Feb. 24, 2009 
until further notice.

    2. On page 337, at the end of Sec.  60.102a, an effective date note 
is added to read as follows:


Sec.  60.102a  Emissions limitations.

* * * * *

    Effective Date Note: At 73 FR 78552, Dec. 22, 2008, in Sec.  
60.102a, paragraph (g) was stayed from Feb. 24, 2009 until further 
notice.

    3. On page 353, at the end of Sec.  60.107a, an effective date note 
is added to read as follows:


Sec.  60.107a  Monitoring of emissions and operations for fuel gas 
combustion devices.

* * * * *

    Effective Date Note: At 73 FR 78552, Dec. 22, 2008, in Sec.  
60.107a, paragraphs (d) and (e) were stayed from Feb. 24, 2009 until 
further notice.
