DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Cancer 
Institute Special Emphasis Panel, August 26, 2014, 8:00 a.m. to August 
26, 2014, 3:00 p.m., NCI Shady Grove 3W032/034, Rockville, MD, 20850 
which was published in the Federal Register on July 17, 2014, 79 FR 
41701.
    This meeting was canceled due to unforeseen circumstances. A new 
date will be set in the near future.

    Dated: August 12, 2014.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
