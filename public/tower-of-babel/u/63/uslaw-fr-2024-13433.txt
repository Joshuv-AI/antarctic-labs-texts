Notice of Meeting

Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, June

Davis Drive, Durham, NC 27709 which was published in the Federal 
Register on May 8, 2024, 89 FR 38909.

Officer and Contact Person from Alfonso Latoni, Ph.D., to Varsha 
Shukla, Ph.D., Scientific Review Branch, National Institute of 
Environmental Sciences, National Institutes of Health, 530 Davis Drive, 
Keystone Building, Durham, NC 27713, (984) 287-3288, 
[email protected]. The meeting location, date, and time will remain 
the same. The meeting is closed to the public.
