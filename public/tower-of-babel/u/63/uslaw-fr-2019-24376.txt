SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

FEDERAL REGISTER CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: 84 FR 58772, 1 
November 2019.

PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TIME AND DATE OF THE MEETING: Tuesday, November 5, 
2019 at 10:00 a.m.

CHANGES IN THE MEETING: The following item will not be considered 
during the Open Meeting on Tuesday, November 5, 2019:
     The Commission will consider whether to propose amendments 
under the Advisers Act of 1940 to the rules that prohibit certain 
investment adviser advertisements and payments to solicitors.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact the Office of the Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: November 4, 2019.
Vanessa A. Countryman,
Secretary.
