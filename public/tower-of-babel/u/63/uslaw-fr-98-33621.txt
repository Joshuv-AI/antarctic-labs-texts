Wyoming Regulatory Program

AGENCY: Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Interior.

ACTION: Proposed rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to the proposed Federal 
rule published on July 29, 1998 (63 FR 40384; administrative record No. 
WY-33-8), under the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 
(SMCRA). This notice is intended to correct two typographical errors 
and inserts two items omitted in the list of intended modifications to 
the Wyoming rules and regulations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 18, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Guy Padgett, 307-261-6550; Internet, 
[email protected]

Correction of Publication

    In the proposed rule FR Doc. 98-20262, on page 63 FR 40385 in the 
Federal Register issue of July 29, 1998, make the following 
corrections:
    1. In the center column, (12) should read, ``Chapter 8, Section 3-
4, revises the rules on special alternative standards for existing as 
well as new special bituminous coal mines;''
    2. In the center column, (13) should read, ``Chapter 12, Section 
1(a)(iv)(B), . . . .;''
    3. In the third column, add in numerical order, ``(22) Chapter 1, 
Section 2(v), revising the definition of critical habitat;'' and ``(23) 
Chapter 8, Section 5, General Performance Standards.''

    Dated: December 9, 1998.
James F. Fulton,
Acting Regional Director, Western Regional Coordinating Center.
