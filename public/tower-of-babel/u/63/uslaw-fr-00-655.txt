Radio Broadcasting Services; Warrenton, OR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposal rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the request of Mountain West 
Broadcasters to allot Channel 259A to Warrenton, OR, as its first local 
aural service, as the proposed reference coordinates for the allotment 
are in the Columbia River. See 64 FR 28133, May 25, 1999. There is no 
transmitter site on land which would allow compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation and city-grade coverage 
requirements.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

Report and Order, MM Docket No. 99-157, adopted November 24, 1999, and
