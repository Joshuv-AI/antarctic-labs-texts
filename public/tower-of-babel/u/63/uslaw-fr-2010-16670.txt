DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Charter Schools Program (CSP) Grants for Replication and 
Expansion of High-Quality Charter Schools

AGENCY: Office of Innovation and Improvement, Department of Education.

ACTION: Notice inviting applications for new awards for fiscal year 
(FY) 2010; extension of application deadline. CFDA Number 84.282M.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 24, 2010, we published in the Federal Register (75 FR 
28789) a notice inviting applications for new awards for FY 2010 for 
the Charter Schools Program Grants for Replication and Expansion of 
High-Quality Charter Schools. That notice specified that applications 
must be submitted by July 7, 2010. We are extending the deadline for 
the transmittal of applications to July 14, 2010 and the deadline for 
intergovernmental review to September 14, 2010.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This notice extends the deadline for 
transmittal of applications for the Charter Schools Program Grants for 
Replication and Expansion of High-Quality Charter Schools FY 2010 
competition to July 14, 2010 and the deadline for intergovernmental 
review to September 14, 2010. We are taking this action to ensure 
applicants have sufficient time to consider the responses to Frequently 
Asked Questions recently posted on the Department's Web site. The 
revised dates are as follows:
    Deadline for Transmittal of Applications: July 14, 2010.
    Deadline for Intergovernmental Review: September 14, 2010.
