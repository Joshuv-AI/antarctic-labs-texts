Radio Broadcasting Services; Logandale, NV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the request of Mountain West 
Broadcasting to allot Channel 291C1 to Logandale, NV, as its first 
local aural service, finding that the petitioner failed to provide a 
sufficient showing to determine that it is a ``community'' for 
allotment purposes.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 12th Street, S.W., 
Room TW-A325, Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
