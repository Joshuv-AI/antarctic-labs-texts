NATIONAL ENDOWMENT FOR THE ARTS

 

Expansion Arts Advisory Panel, Panel Meeting Cancellation; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

ACTION: Notice of correction to Federal Register Notice regarding the 
Overview Section of the Expansion Arts Advisory Panel to the National 
Council on the Arts.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice to announce the cancellation of the above panel 
meeting previously scheduled to meet on June 16, 1994 and published in 
Federal Register 59 FR 28567 on June 2, 1994.

EXPLANATION: Current new guidelines cover a two-year period, therefore 
there will be no opportunity for field input this session. 
Additionally, the Expansion Arts Program has no significant field data, 
new initiatives, or personnel action to report. Cancellation is deemed 
both prudent and cost-saving at this time.

    Dated: June 6, 1994.
Yvonne M. Sabine,
Director, Office of Panel Operations, National Endowment for Arts.
