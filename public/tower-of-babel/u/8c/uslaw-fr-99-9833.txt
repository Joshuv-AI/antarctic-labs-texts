FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


FCC Closes on April 23, 1999

Released: April 12, 1999.
    The U.S. Office of Personnel Management has issued guidance for the 
closing of certain federal agencies on Friday, April 23, 1999, as a 
result of the planned activities associated with the 50th Anniversary 
NATO Summit. In accordance with this guidance, the FCC will be closed 
on Friday, April 23, 1999. All filings, paper and electronic, due on 
April 23, 1999, will be accepted as timely on the next official work 
day.

Federal Communications Commission.
Magalie Roman Salas,
Secretary.
