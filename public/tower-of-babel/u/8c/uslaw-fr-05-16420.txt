SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Advisory Board, Small Business Development Centers; 
Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, National Advisory Board of 
the Office of Small Business Development Centers, will be hosting a 
public meeting via conference call to discuss such matters that may be 
presented by members, the staff of the U.S. Small Business 
Administration, or interested others. The conference will take place on 
Thursday, August 25, 2005 at 11 a.m. eastern standard time.
    Anyone wishing to participate or make an oral presentation to the 
Board must contact Erika Fischer, Senior Program Analyst, U.S. Small 
Business Administration, Office of Small Business Development Centers, 
409 3rd Street, SW., Washington, DC 20416, telephone (202) 205-7045 or 
fax (202) 481-0681.

Matthew K. Becker,
Committee Management Officer.
