Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff; 
Class II Special Controls; Guidance Document: Topical Oxygen Chamber 
for Extremities; Availability; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is correcting a notice 
that appeared in the Federal Register of April 25, 2011 (76 FR 22906). 
The document announced the availability of the guidance entitled 
``Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff; Class 
II Special Controls Guidance Documents: Topical Oxygen Chamber for 
Extremities.'' The document published inadvertently with outdated 
information in the ADDRESSES, FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT, and 
Electronic Access sections. This document corrects those errors.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Charles N. Durfor, Center for Devices 
and Radiological Health, 10903 New Hampshire Ave., Bldg. 66, Rm. G424, 
Silver Spring, MD 20993-0002, 301-796-6438.
