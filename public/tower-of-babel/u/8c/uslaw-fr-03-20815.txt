DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Pub. L. 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the Technology for Machine-to-Machine (MTM)

[[Page 48890]]

Integration. The purpose of the meeting is to allow the SAB leadership 
to brief the Chief of Staff of the Air Force on the outcome of the 
Technology for Machine-to-Machine Integration. Because classified and 
contractor-proprietary information will be discussed, this meeting will 
be closed to the public.

DATES: September 2, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Pentagon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The Air Force Scientific Advisory 
Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Room 5D982, Washington DC 
20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
