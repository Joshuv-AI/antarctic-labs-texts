NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Polar Programs; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:
    Name: Advisory Committee for Polar Programs (1130).
    Date/Time: October 7, 2002; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., October 8, 2002; 
8:30 a.m. to 2 p.m.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Room: 1235, 
Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Brenda Williams, Office of Polar Programs (OPP), 
National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 
22230. (703) 292-8030.
    Purpose of Meeting: To advise NSF on the impact of its policies, 
programs and activities on the polar research community; to provide 
advice to the Director of OPP on issues related to long range planning, 
and to form ad hoc subcommittees to carry out needed studies and tasks.
    Agenda: Discussion of NSF-wide initiatives, long-range planning and 
GPRA.

    Dated: September 12, 2002.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
