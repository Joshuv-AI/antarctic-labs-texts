FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-137; RM-8823]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Negaunee, MI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action allots Channel 270A to Negaunee, Michigan, as the 
community's second FM broadcast service in response to a petition filed 
by Todd Stuart Noordyk (``Noordyk''). See 61 FR 35705, July 8, 1996. We 
shall also modify the application filed by Noordyk for Channel 258A at 
Negaunee to specify Channel 270A with cut-off protection. The 
coordinates for Channel 270A are 46-28-18 and 87-36-55. Canadian 
concurrence has been obtained for this allotment. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 25, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
