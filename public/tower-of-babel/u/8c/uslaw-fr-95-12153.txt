Canadian Border Ports; Baudette, MN

AGENCY: Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, USDA.

ACTION: Direct final rule; confirmation of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 29, 1995, the Animal and Plant Health Inspection 
Service published a direct final rule. (See 60 FR 16043-16045). The 
direct final rule notified the public of our intention to amend the 
animal importation regulations by adding Baudette, MN, as a Canadian 
border port for pet birds, poultry, horses, ruminants, swine, and germ 
plasm. We did not receive any written adverse comments or written 
notice of intent to submit adverse comments in response to the direct 
final rule

EFFECTIVE DATE: The effective date of the direct final rule is 
confirmed as May 30, 1995.
