Department of State FY 2018 & FY 2017 Service Contract Inventory

AGENCY: Department of State.

ACTION: Notice of release of the Department of State's FY 2018 & FY 
2017 Service Contract Inventory.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Acting in compliance with Section 743 of Division C of the 
Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2010, the Department of State is 
publishing this notice to advise the public of the availability of the 
FY 2018 & FY 2017 Service Contract Inventory. The FY 2018 Service 
Contract Inventory includes the FY 2018 Planned Analysis, and the FY 
2017 Meaningful Analysis. The FY 2017 Service Contract Inventory 
includes the FY 2017 Planned Analysis, and the FY 2016 Meaningful 
Analysis. The inventory was developed in accordance with guidance 
issued by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB), Office of Federal 
Procurement Policy (OFPP).

DATES: The inventory is available on the Department's website as of 
July 14, 2020.

ADDRESSES: The Department of State has posted its FY 2018 & FY 2017 
Service Contract Inventory at the following link: https://csm.state.gov/index2.html.
