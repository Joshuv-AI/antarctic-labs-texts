DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP96-200-045]


Reliant Energy Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Proposed 
Changes in FERC Gas Tariff

September 3, 1999.
    Take notice that on September 1, 1999, Reliant Energy Gas 
Transmission Company (REGT) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas 
Tariff, Fifth Revised Volume No. 1, the following revised tariff sheet 
to be effective September 1, 1999:

Original Sheet No. 8H

    REGT states that the purpose of this filing is to reflect the 
implementation of a negotiated rate contract.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Sec. 385.211 of the 
Commission's rules and regulations. All such protests must be filed as 
provided in Sec. 154.210 of the Commission's regulations. Protests will 
be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action 
to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room. This 
filing may be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/
rims.htm (call 202-208-2222 for assistance).
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
