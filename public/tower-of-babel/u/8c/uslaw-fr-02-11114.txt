COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled for 16 
May 2002 at 10 a.m. in the Commission's offices at the National 
Building Museum, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street NW., 
Washington, DC, 20001-2728. Items of discussion affecting the 
appearance of Washington, DC may include buildings, parks and 
memorials.
    Draft agendas are available to the public one week prior to the 
meeting. Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written 
or oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, 
Secretary,

[[Page 30368]]

Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200. 
Individuals requiring sign language interpretation for the hearing 
impaired should contact the Secretary at least 10 days before the 
meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, DC, April 30, 2002.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
