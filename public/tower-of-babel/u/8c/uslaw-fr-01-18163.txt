Cancellation of Customs Broker Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Customs broker license cancellations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to section 641 of the Tariff Act of 1930 as amended 
(19 USC 1641) and the Customs Regulations (19 CFR 111), the following 
Customs broker licenses are cancelled. Some of these entities continue 
to provide broker services under another of their multiple license 
numbers. Because previous publication of some records cannot be readily 
verified, the records are now being published to ensure Customs 
compliance with administrative requirements.
