Bethlehem Steel Corporation, Bethlehem Lukens, Coatesville, PA; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 9, 2003, in response to a petition filed on behalf 
of workers at Bethlehem Steel Corporation, Bethlehem Lukens, 
Coatesville, Pennsylvania.
    This petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on May 16, 2003 and which remains in effect (TA-W-
51,241B). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 22nd day of May 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
