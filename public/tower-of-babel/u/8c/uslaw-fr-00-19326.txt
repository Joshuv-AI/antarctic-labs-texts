SUMMARY: This action corrects Public Land Order No. 7437, 65 FR 15917-
15918, published March 24, 2000.
    On page 15917, second column under T. 9 S., R. 16 E., which read 
``Sec. 16, lots 7 to 16 inclusive.'' is hereby corrected to read ``Sec. 
16, lots 7 to 10, and 12'' and under T. 10 S., R. 18 E., which reads 
``Sec. 3, lot 9, and SW\1/4\NW\1/4\.'' is hereby corrected to read 
``Sec. 3, lot 9, and SW\1/4\SW\1/4\.''

Branch Chief, Lands and Minerals.
