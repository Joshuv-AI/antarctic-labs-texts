DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER94-1174-000]

 

Florida Power Corp.; Filing

April 28, 1994.
    Take notice that on April 20, 1994, Florida Power Corporation (FPC) 
tendered for filing an agreement between itself and Oglethorpe Power 
Corporation pursuant to which FPC will sell to Oglethorpe 50 MW of 
peaking capacity and energy in the months of June through September 
1997 and 275 MW of peaking capacity and energy in the months of June 
through September 1998.
    FPC proposes to make the Agreement effective 60 days after filing. 
FPC states that copies of its filing have been served upon Oglethorpe 
and the Public Service Commissions of Georgia and Florida.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426, in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules 
of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before May 12, 1994. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion 
to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
