DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service

 

Allegheny Wild and Scenic River Southern and Northern Advisory 
Councils; Meetings

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Northern Advisory Council for the Allegheny National Wild 
and Scenic River will meet at 7 pm, Tuesday, March 22, 1994 at the 
Warren Public Library, Warren, PA.
    The Southern Advisory Council will meet at 7 pm, Wednesday, March 
23, 1994, at the Emlenton Civic Club, Emlenton, PA.
    Both Councils will discuss (1) work on a newsletter informing the 
public of their progress to date and (2) also resource opportunities 
along the river.
    Meetings are open to the public. A sign language interpreter will 
be provided if requested by March 14, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Donna McDonald, Allegheny National Forest, 222 Liberty Street, Warren, 
Pennsylvania 16365, 814/723-5150 or 814/726-2710 (TTY).

    Dated: February 23, 1994.
Lionel A. Lemery,
Wild and Scenic River Coordinator.
