DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-36,449]


International Mill Service, Beaumont, Texas; Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 21, 1999 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on June 21, 1999 on behalf of workers at International Mill 
Service, located in Beaumont, Texas.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-35,086). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C., this 25th day of June, 1999.
Linda G. Poole,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
