COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled for 
October 19, 2000, at 10 a.m., in the Commission's offices at the 
National Building Museum (Pension Building), Suite 312, Judiciary 
Square, 441 F Street, NW., Washington, DC, 20001-2728. Items of 
discussion will include designs for projects affecting the appearance 
of Washington, DC, including buildings and parks.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200. 
Individuals requiring sign language interpretation for the hearing 
impaired should contact the Secretary at least 10 days before the 
meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, DC, October 2, 2000.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
