Gustavus Electric Company; Alaska Power Company; Notice of 
Application For Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments and Motions 
To Intervene

    On January 17, 2014, Gustavus Electric Company (transferor) and 
Alaska Power Company (transferee) filed an application for transfer of 
license of the Falls Creek Hydroelectric Project located on Falls Creek 
(A.K.A. Kahtaheena River), Gustavus, Alaska.
    The transferor and transferee seek Commission approval to transfer 
the license for the Falls Creek Hydroelectric Project from the 
transferor to the transferee.
