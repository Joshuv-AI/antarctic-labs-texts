U.S. Nuclear Command and Control System Comprehensive Review

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice of meeting; Amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On August 11, 2008 (73 FR 46605), the Department of Defense 
announced a closed meeting of the U.S. Nuclear Command and Control 
System Comprehensive Review Advisory Committee. This notice is being 
published to announce changes in the time, location and agenda of the 
meeting.

DATES: September 3, 2008 (0830-1700) and September 4, 2008 (0800-1730).

ADDRESSES: U.S. Strategic Command Headquarters Conference Room, Offutt 
AFB, NB.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. William L. Jones, (703) 681-8681, 
U.S. Nuclear Command and Control System Support Staff (NSS), Skyline 3, 
5201 Leesburg Pike, Suite 500, Falls Church, VA 22041.
