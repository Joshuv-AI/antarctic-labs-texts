DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Health Care Financing Administration

42 CFR Part 422


Medicare + Choice Program

CFR Correction

    In Title 42 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 400 to 429, 
revised as of Oct. 1, 2000, in part 422, on page 777, Sec. 422.156 is 
corrected by correctly revising paragraph (e)(1) to read as follows:

[[Page 13855]]

Sec. 422.156  Compliance deemed on the basis of accreditation.

* * * * *
    (e) * * *
    (1) HCFA determines, on the basis of its own investigation, that 
the M+C organization does not meet the Medicare requirements for which 
deemed status was granted.
