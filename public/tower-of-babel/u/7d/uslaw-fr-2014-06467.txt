SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Public Law 94-409, that the Securities 
and Exchange Commission will hold a cybersecurity roundtable on March 
26, 2014 from 9:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
    Roundtable panelists will discuss the cybersecurity landscape and 
cybersecurity issues faced by exchanges and other key market systems, 
broker-dealers, investment advisers, transfer agents, and public 
companies. Panelists also will be invited to discuss industry and 
public-private sector coordination efforts relating to assessing and 
responding to cybersecurity issues.
    The roundtable discussion will be held at SEC headquarters at 100 F 
Street NE., in Washington, DC. The roundtable will be webcast on the 
Commission's Web site at www.sec.gov and will be archived for later 
viewing. Seating for the public will be available.
    For further information, please contact: The Office of the 
Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: March 19, 2014.
Kevin M. O'Neill,
Deputy Secretary.
