Gastrointestinal Drugs Advisory Committee; Cancellation

AGENCY:  Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION:  Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  The meeting of the Gastrointestinal Drugs Advisory Committee 
scheduled for May 20, 2009, is cancelled. This meeting was announced in 
the Federal Register of April 7, 2009 (74 FR 15739). The FDA's Center 
for Drug Evaluation and Research is continuing to review the 
application that was going to be discussed by the committee.

[email protected], or FDA Advisory Committee Information Line,

3014512538. Please call the Information Line for up-to-date information
