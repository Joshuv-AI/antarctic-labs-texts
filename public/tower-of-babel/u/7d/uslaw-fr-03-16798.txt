DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Forever/NPC Resorts; Correction

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to 36 CFR 51.23, notice is hereby given that the 
National Park Service intends to extend the following expiring 
concession contract for a period of up to one year, or until such time 
as a new contract is awarded, whichever occurs sooner.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The concession authorization expires by its 
terms on December 31, 2002. The National Park Service has determined 
that the proposed short-term extension is necessary in order to avoid 
interruption of visitor services and has taken all reasonable and 
appropriate steps to consider alternatives to avoid such interruption. 
This extension will allow the National Park Service to complete and 
issue a prospectus leading to the competitive selection of 
concessioners for a long-term concession contract covering this 
operation.
