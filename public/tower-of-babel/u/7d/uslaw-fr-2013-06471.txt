DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, March 22, 2013, 3:00 p.m. to 
March 22, 2013, 4:30 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 6701 
Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in the Federal 
Register on March 4, 2013, 78 FR 14099.
