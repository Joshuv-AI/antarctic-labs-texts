DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. OA96-16-001]


Idaho Power Company; Notice of Filing

October 3, 1997.
    Take notice that on August 15, 1997, Idaho Power Company (IPC) 
tendered for filing with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission an 
Index of Purchasers listing all customers who have executed Service 
Agreements under Idaho Power Company FERC Electric Tariff No. 5.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest this filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All motions 
or protests should be filed on or before October 15, 1997. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission determining the appropriate action 
to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
