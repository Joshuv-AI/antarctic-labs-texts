DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Foreign Claims Settlement Commission

[F.C.S.C. Meeting and Hearing Notice No. 2-13]


Sunshine Act Meeting

    The Foreign Claims Settlement Commission, pursuant to its 
regulations (45 CFR 503.25) and the Government in the Sunshine Act (5 
U.S.C. 552b), hereby gives notice in regard to the scheduling of an 
open meeting as follows:
    Friday, February 15, 2013: 9:00 a.m.--Oral hearing on Objection to 
Commission's Proposed Decision in Claim No. LIB-II-165.
    Status: Open.
    All meetings are held at the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission, 
600 E Street NW., Washington, DC. Requests for information, or advance 
notices of intention to observe an open meeting, may be directed to: 
Judith H. Lock, Executive Officer, Foreign Claims Settlement 
Commission, 600 E Street NW., Suite 6002, Washington, DC 20579. 
Telephone: (202) 616-6975.

Jeremy R. LaFrancois,
Chief Administrative Counsel.
