Grant Awards to Applicants for Funds to Provide Civil Legal

Legal Services Corporation (LSC or Corporation) announced its intention 
to award grants and contracts to provide economical and effective 
delivery of high quality civil legal services to eligible low-income 
clients, beginning January 1, 1997. The following organization should 
have also been included.
