DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Executive Committee Meeting of the Defense Advisory Committee on 
Women in the Services (DACOWITS)

AGENCY: Advisory Committee on Women in the Services, Office of the 
Secretary, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to section 10(a), Public Law 92-463, as amended, 
notice is hereby given of a forthcoming Quarterly Executive Committee 
Meeting of the Defense Advisory Committee on Women in the Services 
(DACOWITS). The purpose of the Executive Committee Meeting is to review 
the responses to the recommendations and request for information 
adopted by the Committee at the DACOWITS 2000 Fall Conference.

DATES: February 12, 2001, 9:15 a.m.-4:30 p.m.

ADDRESSES: OSD Conference Room 1E801, #7, The Pentagon, Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lieutenant Colonel Susan E. Kolb, 
ARNGUS, DACOWITS and Military Women Matters, OASD (Force Management 
Policy), 4000 Defense Pentagon, Room 3D769, Washington, DC 20301-4000; 
telephone (703) 697-2122.
