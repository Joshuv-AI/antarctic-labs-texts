DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 20, 2009 the U.S. Department of Education published a 
comment period notice in the Federal Register (Page 11291, Column 3) 
for the information collection, `` Feasibility and Conduct of an Impact 
Evaluation of Title I Supplemental Education Services''. This notice 
amends the responses to 22,260 and the burden hours to 2,139. The IC 
Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management Services, Office 
of Management, hereby issues a correction notice as required by the 
Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

    Dated: March 23, 2009.
Angela C. Arrington,
IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management Services, 
Office of Management.
