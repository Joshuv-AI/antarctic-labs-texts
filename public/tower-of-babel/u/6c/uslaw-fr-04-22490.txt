SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3620]


State of Florida (Amendment 4)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency--effective 
September 25, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
include Manatee, Sarasota, and Suwannee as disaster areas due to 
damages caused by Hurricane Frances occurring on September 3, 2004, and 
continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous county of Madison in the State of 
Florida may be filed until the specified date at the previously 
designated location. All other counties contiguous to the above named 
primary counties have previously been declared.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 3, 2004 and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 6, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)
    Dated: September 30, 2004.
Cheri L. Cannon,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
