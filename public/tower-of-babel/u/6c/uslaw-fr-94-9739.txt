FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM
 

Mabrey Bancorporation, Inc.; Change in Bank Control Notices; 
Acquisitions of Shares of Banks or Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 94-8428) published on page 
16815 of the issue for Friday, April 8, 1994.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for Mabrey Bancorporation, Inc., is revised to read as follows:
    1. Mabrey Bancorporation, Inc., Okmulgee, Oklahoma; to acquire a 
controlling interest in CSB, Inc., Bixby, Oklahoma, which is the 
proposed bank holding company owning 80 percent of Citizens Security 
Bancshares, Inc., Bixby, Oklahoma, and Citizens Security Bank and Trust 
Company, Bixby, Oklahoma. In addition, Applicant proposes to directly 
acquire up to 20 percent of Citizens Security Bancshares.
    Comments on this application must be received by March 23, 1994.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, April 18, 
1994.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
