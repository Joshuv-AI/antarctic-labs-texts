DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the 2012 America the Beautiful Quarters Five Ounce 
Silver Uncirculated CoinsTM

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the price of the 2012 
America the Beautiful Quarters Five Ounce Silver Uncirculated 
CoinsTM.
    The coins will be offered for sale at a price of $204.95.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B.B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing, United States Mint, 801 9th Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.
