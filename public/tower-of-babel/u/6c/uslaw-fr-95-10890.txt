NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

Use of NUMARC/EPRI Report TR-102348 for Analog-to-Digital 
Replacements; Issued

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of issuance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has issued Generic 
Letter 95-02 on informing licensees for reactors of the NRC staff's new 
position on the use of Nuclear Management and Resource Council/Electric 
Power Research Institute (NUMARC/EPRI) Report TR-102348, ``Guideline on 
Licensing Digital Upgrades,'' dated December 1993, as acceptable 
guidance for determining when an analog-to-digital replacement can be 
performed without prior NRC staff approval under the requirements of 
Sec. 50.59 of Title 10 of the Code of Federal Regulations. This generic 
letter is available in the Public Document Rooms under accession number 
9504140227. The resolution of public comments received on this generic 
letter is discussed in a memorandum which is also available in the 
Public Document Rooms under accession number 9504260141.

DATES: The generic letter was issued on April 26, 1995.

ADDRESSES: Not applicable.
