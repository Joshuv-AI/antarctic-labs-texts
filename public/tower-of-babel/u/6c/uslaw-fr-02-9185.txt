DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 7118-007]


State of Maine Department of Marine Resources; Notice of Site 
Visit

April 10, 2002.
    On April 23, 2002, the Office of Energy Projects staff will 
participate in a site visit to the Smelt Hill Hydroelectric Project on 
the Presumpscot River in the town of Falmouth, Maine. The site visit 
will begin at about 9 a.m. near the dam. All interested parties may 
attend the site visit. Those planning to attend must provide their own 
transportation. For further information, please contact the

[[Page 18602]]

Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 208-0004.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
