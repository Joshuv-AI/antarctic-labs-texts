DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

18 CFR Part 35


Filing of Rate Schedules and Tariffs

CFR Correction

    In Title 18 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 1 to 399, 
revised as of April 1, 2015, in Sec.  35.1, make the following changes:
    1. On page 270, in paragraphs (d)(2) and (d)(3), add the phrase ``, 
tariffs or service agreements'' after the phrase ``rate schedules'', 
and
    2. On page 270, in paragraph (g), in the first sentence, add the 
word ``service'' before the third occurrence of the word ``agreement''.
