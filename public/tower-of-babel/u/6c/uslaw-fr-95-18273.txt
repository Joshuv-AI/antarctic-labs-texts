DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Flight Service Station at Salinas, CA; Notice of Closure

    Notice is hereby given that on July 20, 1995, the Flight Service 
Station (FSS) at Salinas, California, closed. Services to the general 
public of Salinas, California, formerly provided by this facility, are 
provided by the Automated Flight Service Station (AFSS) in Oakland, 
California. This information will be reflected in the next issue of the 
FAA Organization Statement.


(Sec. 313(a), 72 Stat. 752, 49 U.S.C. 1354)

    Issued in Lawndale, California, on July 14, 1995.
Lynore C. Brekke,
Acting Regional Administrator, Western-Pacific Region.
