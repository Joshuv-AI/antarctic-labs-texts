CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Federal Register Citation of Previous Announcement: Vol. 67, No. 
207, Friday, October 25, 2002, page 65538.
    Previously Announced Time and Date of Meeting: 10:00 a.m., Friday, 
November 1, 2002.
