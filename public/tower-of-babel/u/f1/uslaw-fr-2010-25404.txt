DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Bureau of Labor Statistics


Proposed Collection, Comment Request

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Labor published a document in the Federal 
Register of October 4, 2010, concerning a request for comments on the 
proposed revision of the National Compensation Survey (1220-0164). The 
document contained an incorrect date for submission of comments.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nora Kincaid, BLS Clearance Officer, 
at 202-691-7628 (this is not a toll free number). (See ADDRESSES 
section.)

Correction

    In the Federal Register of October 4, 2010, 75 FR 61178, in the 
second column, correct the DATES caption to read:

DATES: Written comments must be submitted to the office listed in the 
ADDRESSES section of this notice on or before December 3, 2010.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of October 2010.
Kimberley Hill,
Chief, Division of Management Systems, Bureau of Labor Statistics.
