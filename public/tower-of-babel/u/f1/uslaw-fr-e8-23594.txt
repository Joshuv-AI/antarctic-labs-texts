Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 23, 2008, 8 a.m. to 
October 24, 2008, 5 p.m., Holiday Inn Express Hotel and Suites, San 
Francisco Fisherman's Wharf, 550 North Point Street, San Francisco, CA, 
91433 which was published in the Federal Register on September 19,

closed to the public.
