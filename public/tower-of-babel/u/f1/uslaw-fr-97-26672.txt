DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Surface Transportation Board
[STB Finance Docket No. 33408]


Gateway Western Railway Company--Lease Exemption--The Burlington 
Northern and Santa Fe Railway Company

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under 49 U.S.C. 10502, the Board exempts from the requirements 
of 49 U.S.C. 11323-25 the lease by Gateway Western Railway Company of 
The Burlington Northern and Santa Fe Railway Company's Coburg Line, 
extending a distance of 5.45 miles between milepost 0.0 at the 
Sheffield interlocking and milepost 5.45 near BV Junction, in Kansas 
City, Jackson County, MO, subject to standard labor protective 
conditions.

DATES: The exemption will be effective November 7, 1997. Petitions to 
stay must be filed by October 23, 1997. Petitions to reopen must be 
filed by November 3, 1997.

ADDRESSES: An original and 10 copies of all pleadings referring to STB 
Finance Docket No. 33408 must be filed with the Surface Transportation 
Board, Office of the Secretary, Case Control Unit, 1925 K Street, N.W., 
Washington, DC 20423-0001; in addition, a copy of all pleadings must be 
served on petitioner's representative: William C. Sippel, Oppenheimer 
Wolff & Donnelly, Two Prudential Plaza, 45th Floor, 180 North Stetson 
Avenue, Chicago, IL 60601.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Beryl Gordon (202) 565-1600. [TDD for
