SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The Marine Mammal Protection Act prohibits the taking of marine 
mammals except under permit or exemption. Section 118 of the Act 
authorizes an exemption for commercial fisheries provided that fishers 
in Category I and II fisheries (those with the most likely interactions 
with marine mammals) to register for a certificate of exemption that 
will protect the fisher from prosecution for violating the moratorium 
on the taking of marine mammals.
