DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Census Bureau


National Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Census Bureau, Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public virtual meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Census Bureau is giving notice of a virtual meeting of the 
National Advisory Committee (NAC). The Committee will address policy, 
research, and technical issues relating to a full range of Census 
Bureau programs and activities, including the decennial census, 
demographic and economic statistical programs, field operations, and 
information technology. Last minute changes to the schedule are 
possible, which could prevent giving advance public notice of schedule 
adjustments. Please visit the Census Advisory Committees website at 
http://www.census.gov/cac for the NAC meeting information, including 
the agenda, and how to join the meeting.

DATES: The virtual meeting will be held on:

 Thursday, May 5, 2022, from 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. EDT, and
 Friday, May 6, 2022, from 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. EDT.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The NAC provides technical expertise to 
address Census Bureau program needs and objectives. The members of the 
NAC are appointed by the Director of the Census Bureau. The NAC has 
been established in accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
(title 5, United States Code, appendix 2, section 10).
    All meetings are open to the public. A brief period will be set
