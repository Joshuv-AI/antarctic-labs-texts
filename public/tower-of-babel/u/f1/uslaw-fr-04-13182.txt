SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3576; Amdt. 1]


State of Missouri

    The above-numbered declaration is hereby amended to change the 
declaration number for the contiguous State of Kansas to 358406.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is July 26, 2004, and for 
economic injury the deadline is February 26, 2005.

59008)
