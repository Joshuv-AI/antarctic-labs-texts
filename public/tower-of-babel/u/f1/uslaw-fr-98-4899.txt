Algonquin Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Extension of Time

February 20, 1998.
    The Massachusetts Energy Facilities Siting Board (EFSB) filed a 
request asking for an extension of time in which to file comments on 
the Notice of Intent to Prepare an Environmental Assessment for the 
Proposed ANP Bellingham Lateral Project and Request for Comments on 
Environmental Issues (NOI), issued January 16, 1998 (63 FR 3560, 1/23/
98). Comments were due February 17, 1998.
    Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that an extension of 
time is granted to the EPSB until March 20, 1998, for the filing of 
comments on the NOI.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
