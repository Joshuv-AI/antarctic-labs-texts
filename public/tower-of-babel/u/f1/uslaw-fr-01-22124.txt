DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Head Start Program Information Report.
    OMB No.: 0980-0017.
    Description: The report is submitted annually by all Head Start and 
Early Head Start grantees and delegate agency. The report contains 
information which the grantees complete such as, number and ages of 
children enrolled, what educational and health services they receive 
and what family services are provided.
