DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, New Strategies 
for Growing 3D Tissues, which was published in the Federal Register on 
February 15, 2011, FR 2011-3373.
    The meeting was originally advertised for March 9-10, 2011. The 
meeting will take place on March 9, 2011 only. The meeting is closed to 
the public.

    Dated: February 23, 2011.
Jennifer S. Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
