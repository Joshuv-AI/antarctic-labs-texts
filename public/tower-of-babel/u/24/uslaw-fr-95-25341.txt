Defense Federal Acquisition Supplement Contracting by Negotiation

AGENCY: Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense has decided to withdraw a proposed 
rule published on March 24, 1995 (60 FR 15528). The rule proposed DFARS 
revisions to allow the head of the contracting activity to determine 
the appropriate level for approval of second and subsequent rounds of 
best and final offers for competitive negotiated acquisitions under 
other than formal source selection. Public comments 

[[Page 53575]]
received in response to the proposed rule indicated that industry did 
not support the proposed DFARS revisions.
