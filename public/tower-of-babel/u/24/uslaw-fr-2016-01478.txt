NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee On Reactor Safeguards (ACRS); Meeting of the 
ACRS Subcommittee on Fukushima; Revised Date of the February 19, 2016, 
ACRS Subcommittee Meeting

    The ACRS Subcommittee meeting on Fukushima scheduled for February 
19, 2016, 8:30 a.m. until 5:00 p.m., has been changed to February 18, 
2016.
    The notice of this meeting was previously published in the Federal 
Register on Thursday, January 14, 2016, (81 FR 1968).
    Information regarding this meeting can be obtained by contacting 
Kathy Weaver, Designated Federal Official (DFO) (Telephone 301-415-6236 
or Email: [email protected]) between 7:30 a.m. and 5:15 p.m. (EST)).

    Dated: January 19, 2016.
Mark L. Banks,
Chief, Technical Support Branch, Advisory Committee on Reactor 
Safeguards.
