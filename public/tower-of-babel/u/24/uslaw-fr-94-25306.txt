FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 92-248; RM-8105]

 

Radio Broadcasting Services; Southern Shores, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Joseph A. Booth, allots 
Channel 265C2 to Southern Shores, NC, as the community's first local 
aural transmission service. See 57 FR 55502, November 25, 1992. Channel 
265C2 can be allotted to Southern Shores without the imposition of a 
site restriction, at coordinates North Latitude 36-06-30 and West 
Longitude 75-43-20. Final action on applications for a construction 
permit may be withheld until Station WKJA at Belhaven, NC, is licensed 
at the coordinates specified in its outstanding construction permit 
(BPH-861103IG). With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 21, 1994. The window period for filing 
applications will open on November 21, 1994, and close on December 7, 
1994.
