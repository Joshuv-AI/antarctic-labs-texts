DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 14692-001]


Moriah Hydro Corporation; Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 8, 2019, the Commission issued a notice setting March 9, 
2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments on the 
preliminary permit application for the Lyon Mountain Energy Storage 
Project No. 14692. Due to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies 
between December 22, 2018 and January 25, 2019, the Commission is 
extending the comment period until March 26, 2019.

    Dated: February 8, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
