DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

International Trade Administration


Multi-Agency Business Development Infrastructure Mission to China 
and Hong Kong, and Business Development Mission to Korea; Correction

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Commerce.

ACTION: Correction to 64 FR Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    This notice is to provide updated and corrected information for 64 
FR 477-479 which appeared in the Federal Register on January 5, 1999. 
The notice announced the Multi-Agency Business Development Mission to 
China and Hong Kong and the Business Development Mission to Korea. The 
corrected information is as follows:

Change of Title:
    Multi-Agency Business Development Mission to China and the Business 
Development Mission to Korea
Change of Dates for the Trade Missions:
    Korea: March 25-27--Seoul
    China: March 28-April 1--Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou

    Application Deadline: The new deadline for applications is February 
5, 1999.

    Dated: January 22, 1999.
Lucie Naphin,
Director, Office of Business Liaison.
