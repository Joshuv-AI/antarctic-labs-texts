SUMMARY: The comment period for the Army Science Board Request for 
Information on Robotic and Autonomous Systems-of-Systems (RAS) 
Technology Initiatives notice published in the Federal Register on 
Monday, May 16, 2016 (81 FR 30264), required comment packages be 
submitted by Friday, May 27, 2016. The comment period has been extended 
to Tuesday, June 14, 2016.
