SUMMARY: Correction is made to the investigation number and caption for 
notice 79 FR 62465 which was published on Friday, October 17, 2014. The 
investigation number should be corrected from 337-TA-884 to 337-TA-925, 
and the caption should be corrected from Certain Consumer Electronics 
with Display and Processing Capabilities to Communications or Computing 
Devices and Components Thereof.
