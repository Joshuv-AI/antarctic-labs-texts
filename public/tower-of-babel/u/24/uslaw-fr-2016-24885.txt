NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards; Meeting of the ACRS 
Subcommittee on Metallurgy & Reactor Fuels; Cancellation of the October 
21, 2016, ACRS Subcommittee Meeting

    The ACRS Subcommittee meeting on Metallurgy & Reactor Fuels 
scheduled for October 21, 2016 (new date), 8:30 a.m. until 12:00 p.m., 
has been cancelled.
    The notice of this meeting was previously published in the Federal 
Register on Tuesday, October 4, 2016, (81 FR 68461).
    Information regarding this meeting can be obtained by contacting 
Christopher Brown, Designated Federal Official (DFO) (Telephone 301-
415-7111 or Email: [email protected]) between 7:30 a.m. and 
5:15 p.m. (EST)).

    Dated: October 5, 2016.
Mark L. Banks,
Chief, Technical Support Branch, Advisory Committee on Reactor 
Safeguards.
