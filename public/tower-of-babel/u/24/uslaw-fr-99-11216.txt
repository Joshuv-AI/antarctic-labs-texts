DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. CP98-538-000]


Midwestern Gas Transmission; Notice of Site Visit

April 29, 1999.
    On May 6, 1999, the staff of the Office of Pipeline Regulation 
(OPR) will be conducting an inspection of Midwestern Gas Transmission 
Company's (Midwestern) Grain Processing Corporation Sales Tap Project. 
This recently constructed 2.84-mile-long pipeline is in Knox and 
Daviess Counties, Indiana. Representatives of Midwestern will accompany 
the OPR staff.
    All interested parties may attend, although those planning to 
attend must provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee of the 
Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 208-1088.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
