Market Development Cooperator Program

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Commerce.

ACTION: Correction of notice of funding availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On page 31781 in the issue of Friday, May 10, 2002, in the second 
column, ``June 10, 2002'' should read ``July 1, 2002''. With this 
change, the affected sentence reads as follows: ``From May 10, 2002, 
until July 1, 2002, the Department does not counsel potential 
applicants regarding the merits of projects they may propose in their 
applications.''
