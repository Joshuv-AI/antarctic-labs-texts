Harco National Insurance Company; Surety Companies Acceptable on 
Federal Bonds; Redomestication

    Harco National Insurance Company has redomesticated from the state 
of New York to the State of Illinois effective December 31, 1994. The 
company was last listed as an acceptable surety on Federal bonds at 59 
FR 34158, July 1, 1994.
    Federal bond-approving officers should annotate their reference 
copies of the Treasury Circular 570, 1994 revision, on page 34158 to 
reflect this change.
    Questions concerning this notice may be directed to the Surety Bond 
Branch, Funds Management Division, Financial Management Service, 
Department of the Treasury, 3700 East West Highway, Rm. 6F04, 
Hyattsville, MD 20782, telephone (FTS/202) 874-6507.
