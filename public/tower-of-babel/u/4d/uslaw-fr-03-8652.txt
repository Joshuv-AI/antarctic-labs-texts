ACTION: Correction of notice of investigation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Due to a typographical error, the Commission's notice 
published in the Federal Register on April 4, 2003 (68 F.R.16551) 
incorrectly stated that the complaint was filed on March 3, 2002. The 
corrected date is March 3, 2003.

Secretary to the Commission.
