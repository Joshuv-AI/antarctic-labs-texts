GOVERNMENT PUBLISHING OFFICE


Depository Library Council to the Acting Deputy Director Meeting

    The Depository Library Council (DLC) to the Acting Deputy Director, 
Government Publishing Office (GPO) will meet on Monday, October 21, 
2019 through Wednesday, October 23, 2019 in Arlington, Virginia. The 
sessions will take place from 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday and 
Tuesday and 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., on Wednesday. The meeting will be 
held at the Doubletree Hotel, 300 Army Navy Drive, Arlington, Virginia. 
The purpose of this meeting is to discuss the Federal Depository 
Library Program. All sessions are open to the public. The United States 
Government Publishing Office is in compliance with the requirements of 
Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act and meets all Fire 
Safety Act regulations.

John W. Crawford,
Acting Deputy Director, U.S. Government Publishing Office.
