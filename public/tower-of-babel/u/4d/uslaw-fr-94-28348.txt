Direct Electric Inc.; Notice of Informational Filing

November 10, 1994.

filed certain information as required by the Commission's July 18, 
1994, letter order in Docket No. ER94-1161-000. Copies of DEI's 
informational filing are on file with the Commission and are available 
for public inspection.
