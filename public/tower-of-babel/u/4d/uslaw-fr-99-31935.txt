DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-36,869]


Grand Rapids Die Cast, Grand Rapids, Michigan; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 27, 1999 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on September 13, 1999 on behalf of workers at Grand 
Rapids Die Cast, Grand Rapids, Michigan.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-36,814). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 18th day of November, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
