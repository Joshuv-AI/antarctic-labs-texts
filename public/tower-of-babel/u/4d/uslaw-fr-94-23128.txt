SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

``FEDERAL REGISTER'' CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: [59 FR 46701, 
September 9, 1994].

STATUS: Open meeting.

PLACE: 450 Fifth Street, NW., Washington, DC.

DATE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED: September 9, 1994.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Cancellation.
    The open meeting scheduled for Wednesday, September 14, 1994, at 
2:30 p.m. has been cancelled.
    Commissioner Schapiro, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above change and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact:
    The Office of the Secretary (202) 942-7070.

    Dated: September 13, 1994.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
