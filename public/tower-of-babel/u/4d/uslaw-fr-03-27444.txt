Seahawk Seafoods, Inc., Valdez, AK; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on July 30, 2003, in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Seahawk Seafoods, 
Inc., Valdez, Alaska.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 11th day of September, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
