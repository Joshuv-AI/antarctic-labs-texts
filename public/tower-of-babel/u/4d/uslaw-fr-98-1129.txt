Aviation Rulemaking Advisory Committee Meeting on Aircraft

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public that the 
January 22 meeting of the Federal Aviation Administration Aviation 
Rulemaking Advisory Committee, scheduled to discuss Aircraft 
Certification Procedures Issues (63 FR 122), has been cancelled.
