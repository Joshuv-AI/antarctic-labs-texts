DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP01-503-000]


Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America; Notice Denying Emergency 
Request for Reconsideration

June 3, 2005.
    On May 24, 2005, Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America (Natural) 
filed, in the above-docketed proceeding, an emergency request for 
reconsideration of the Commission's order setting for hearing the issue 
of appropriate hydrocarbon dew point (HDP) safe harbor level on 
Natural's system. On May 31, the Commission issued a notice shortening 
the period for filing answers to Natural's request to June 2, 2005. On 
May 27, and June 2, 2005, Alliance Pipeline L.P., Indicated Shippers, 
and Aux Sable Liquid Products, L.P. filed respective answers in 
opposition to Natural's request. By this notice, Natural's May 24, 
2005, emergency request for reconsideration is hereby denied.

    By direction of the Commission.
Linda Mitry,
Deputy Secretary.
