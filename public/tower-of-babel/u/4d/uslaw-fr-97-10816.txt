DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. TM97-4-32-001]


Colorado Interstate Gas Company; Notice Compliance of Tariff 
Filing

April 22, 1997.
    Take notice that on April 16, 1997, Colorado Interstate Gas Company 
(CIG) filed Substitute First Revised Fourth Revised Sheet No. 230, 
First Revised Volume No. 1 and First Revised First Revised Sheet No. 
230A, First Revised Volume No. 1, pursuant to the Commission's Letter 
Order issued March 27, 1997 which requires CIG to submit this filing to 
revise Section 1.14 of the General Terms and Conditions of its FERC Gas 
Tariff, First Revised Volume No. 1
    CIG states that copies of this filing have been served on CIG's 
jurisdictional customers and public bodies.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such protests must be 
filed in accordance with Section 154.210 of the Commission's 
Regulations. Protests will be considered by the Commission in 
determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to 
make protestants parties to the proceeding. Copies of this filing are 
on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection in 
the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
