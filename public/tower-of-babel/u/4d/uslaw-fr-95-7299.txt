FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-15; RM-8411]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Ravenswood and Elizabeth, West 
Virginia

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of MediaCom, Inc., reallots 
Channel 291A from Ravenswood to Elizabeth, West Virginia, as that 
community's first local aural transmission service, and modifies 
Station WRZZ(FM)'s license accordingly. See 59 FR 10607, March 7, 1994. 
Channel 291A can be allotted to Elizabeth in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without the 
imposition of a site restriction. The coordinates for Channel 291A at 
Elizabeth are 39-03-48 and West Longitude 81-23-43. Since Elizabeth is 
located within 320 kilometers (200 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border, 
concurrence of the Canadian government has been obtained. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 4, 1995.
