Viscotec Automotive Products, LLC, Morganton, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    In accordance with Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
amended, an investigation was initiated on March 31, 2009 in response 
to a worker petition filed on behalf of workers of Viscotec Automotive 
Products, LLC, Morganton, North Carolina.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 29th day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
