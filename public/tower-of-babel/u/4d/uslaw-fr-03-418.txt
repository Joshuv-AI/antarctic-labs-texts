Pfaltzgraff Company, Thomasville, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 9, 2002 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Pfaltzgraff Company, 
Thomasville, Pennsylvania.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-41,917, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 18th day of December 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
