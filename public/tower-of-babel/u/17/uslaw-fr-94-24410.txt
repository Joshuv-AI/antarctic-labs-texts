DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
National Institutes of Health

 

National Institute on Aging; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the meeting of the 
Biological and Clinical Aging Review Subcommittee A, National Institute 
on Aging, October 3, 1994, from 8:30 a.m. to adjournment, at the 
Holiday Inn Crowne Plaza, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, Maryland, 
which was published in the Federal Register on September 21, 1994, Vol. 
59, page 48441.
    The meeting was cancelled because the majority of the members were 
not available to meet to conduct the committee's business.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 93.866, Aging 
Research, National Institutes of Health.)

    Dated: September 28, 1994.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
