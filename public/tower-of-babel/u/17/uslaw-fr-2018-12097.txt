DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. PR17-60-002]


Notice Establishing Comment Period; Atmos Pipeline--Texas

    On May 23, 2018, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission staff held an 
informal technical conference to discuss issues raised in the protests 
and comments regarding the January 25, 2018 filing made by Atmos 
Pipeline--Texas in the above-captioned docket. This notice establishes 
the comment periods for parties wishing to submit comments following 
the technical conference. All parties are invited to submit initial 
comments on or before Wednesday, June 13, 2018. Reply comments are due 
on or before Tuesday, July 3, 2018.
    For more information, please contact Deirdra Archie at 
[email protected] or (202) 502-6819.

    Dated: May 29, 2018.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
