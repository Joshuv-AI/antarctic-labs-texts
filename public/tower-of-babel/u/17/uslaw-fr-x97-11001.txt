DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

National Highway Traffic Safety Administration

49 CFR Part 571


Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards

CFR Correction

    In Title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 400 to 999, 
revised as of October 1, 1996, make the following correction:
    On page 482, in Sec. 571.208, in the first column, paragraph 
S4.5.3.5 should be added immediately following paragraph S4.5.3.4 to 
read as follows:
    S4.5.3.5 A replacement automatic belt shall be meet the 
requirements of S4.1(k) of Standard No. 209.
