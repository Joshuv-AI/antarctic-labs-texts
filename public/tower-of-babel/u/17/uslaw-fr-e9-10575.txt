Findlay Industries, Inc., Findlay, OH; Findlay Industries, Inc., 
Springfield, OH; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 26, 2009, in response to 
petitions filed by UNITE HERE!, Toledo, Ohio, on behalf of workers at 
Findlay Industries, Inc., Findlay and Springfield, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that his petitions be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of April 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
