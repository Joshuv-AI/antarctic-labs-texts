DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke; 
Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Infrastructure, 
Neuroinformatics, and Computational Neuroscience Subcommittee meeting 
scheduled on May 30, 2002, 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM, National Institutes of 
Health, Building 31, C Wing, Conference Room 9, 9000 Rockville Pike, 
Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on May 
2, 2002, 67 FR 02-10911.

    Dated: May 24, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
