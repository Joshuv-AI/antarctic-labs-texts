DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management
[ES-960-1420-00; ES-50265, Group 160, Wisconsin]


Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Wisconsin, Stayed

    On Tuesday, April 13, 1999 there was published in the Federal 
Register, Volume 64, Number 70, on pages 18047-18048 a notice entitled 
``Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Wisconsin''. In said notice was a 
plat depicting the dependent resurvey of a portion of the west 
boundary, a portion of the subdivisional lines, and the subdivision of 
sections 7 and 18, in Township 35 North, Range 15 West, of the 4th 
Principal Meridian, Wisconsin, accepted March 30, 1999.
    The official filing of the plat is hereby stayed, pending 
consideration of questions as to the technical aspects of the survey.

    Dated: June 20, 1999.
Joseph W. Beaudin,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
