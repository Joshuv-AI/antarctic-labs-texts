SELECTIVE SERVICE SYSTEM


Notice to Registrants Who Have Attained Age 26 Years

    Registrants who have attained age 26 years do not have the duty to 
notify the Selective Service System of a change in their address.
    If a registrant notifies the System of a change in his address the 
System may record it.
    This notice is issued pursuant to my authority in 32 CFR 1621.1.

    Dated: December 14, 1995.
Gil Coronado,
Director of Selective Service.
