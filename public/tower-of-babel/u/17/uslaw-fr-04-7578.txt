Summitville Tiles, Inc., Summitville, OH; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 26, 2004, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at 
Summitville Tiles, Inc., Summitville, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 24th day of March, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
