DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-34, 949]


Ademco Group, El Paso, TX; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 8, 1998 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Admeco Group, El Paso, Texas.
    All workers of the subject firm are included under an existing 
investigation in process (TA-W-34,856). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 13th day of October 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
