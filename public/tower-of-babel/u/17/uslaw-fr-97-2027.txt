[FEMA-1152-DR]


Washington; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
State of Washington (FEMA-1152-DR), dated January 7, 1997, and related 
determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 15, 1997,

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Magda Ruiz, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of Washington, is hereby amended to include an additional category of 
assistance under the Public Assistance program in those areas 
determined to have been adversely affected by the catastrophe declared 
a major disaster by the President in his declaration of January 7, 
1997:

    The counties of Klickitat, Pend Oreille, and Spokane for 
Category G under Public Assistance. Federal assistance to replace 
trees is not eligible. (These counties have already been designated 
for Categories A-F under Public Assistance and Hazard Mitigation.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance.)
Lacy E. Suiter,
Executive Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
