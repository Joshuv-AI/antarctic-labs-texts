Employment and Training Administration

Dana Corporation, Engine and Fluid Management Group; Crenshaw, 
Mississippi; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 15, 2003, in response to a 
worker petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at 
Dana Corporation, Engine and Fluid Management Group, Crenshaw, 
Mississippi.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 17th day of January, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
