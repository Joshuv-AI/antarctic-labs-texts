FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[FRS 16622]


Deletion of Items From March 31, 2020 Open Meeting

March 31, 2020.
    The following items have been adopted by the Commission and deleted 
from the list of items scheduled for consideration at the Tuesday, 
March 31, 2020, Open Meeting. The items were previously listed in the 
Commission's Notice of Tuesday, March 24, 2020.
