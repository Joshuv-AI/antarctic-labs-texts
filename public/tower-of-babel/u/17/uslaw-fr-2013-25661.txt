DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Host 
Interactions with Bacterial Pathogens Study Section, October 18, 2013, 
8:00 a.m. to October 18, 2013, 6:00 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One 
Bethesda Metro Center, 7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which 
was published in the Federal Register on September 24, 2013, 78 FR 185 
Pgs. 58547-58548.

end on November 15, 2013 at 6:30 p.m. The meeting location remains the 
same. The meeting is closed to the public.
