Radio Broadcasting Services; Gulf Hammock, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Withdrawal of proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies the proposal allotment of Channel 257A at 
Gulf Hammock, Florida, in response to a petition filed by Levy County 
Broadcasting. See 64 FR 12922, March 16, 1999. The Notice of proposed 
rulemakiing summarized at 64 FR 12922 questioned community status and 
requested additional information. Based on the information supplied by 
petitioner, it was determined that Gulf Hammock did not qualify as a 
community for allotment purposes.
