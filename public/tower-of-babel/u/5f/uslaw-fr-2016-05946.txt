DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


U.S. Air Force Exclusive Patent License

AGENCY: Air Force Research Laboratory Information Directorate, Rome, 
New York, Department of the Air Force, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of intent to issue an exclusive patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of part 404 of Title 37, Code of 
Federal Regulations, which implements Public Law 96-517, as amended, 
the Department of the Air Force announces its intention to grant Lilo 
Life LLC, a corporation of New York, having a place of business at 106 
Genesee St., Utica, New York 13413, an exclusive license in any right, 
title and interest the United States Air Force has in: In U.S. Patent 
No. 8,317,058 entitled ``Bicyclists' Water Bottle with Bottom Drinking 
Valve'', issued on November 27th, 2012, U.S. Design Patent No. D588,856 
issued on March 24th, 2009, and U.S. Design Patent D583,626 issued on 
December 20th, 2008.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: An exclusive license for this patent 
will be granted unless a written objection is received within fifteen 
(15) days from the date of publication of this Notice. Written 
objections should be sent to: Air Force Research Laboratory, Office of 
the Staff Judge Advocate, AFRL/RIJ, 26 Electronic Parkway, Rome, New 
York 13441-4514. Telephone: (315) 330-2087; Facsimile (315) 330-7583.

Henry Williams,
Acting Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
