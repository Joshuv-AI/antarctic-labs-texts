 INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 731-TA-298 (Third Review)]


Porcelain-on-Steel Cooking Ware From China

Determination

    On the basis of the record \1\ developed in the subject five-year 
review, the United States International Trade Commission (Commission) 
determines, pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 
U.S.C. 1675(c)), that revocation of the antidumping duty order on 
porcelain-on-steel cooking ware from China would be likely to lead to 
continuation or recurrence of material injury to an industry in the 
United States within a reasonably foreseeable time.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ The record is defined in sec. 207.2(f) of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure (19 CFR Sec.  207.2(f)).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Background

    The Commission instituted this review on October 1, 2010 (75 FR 
62144) and determined on January 4, 2011 that it would conduct an 
expedited review (76 FR 2920, January 18, 2011).
    The Commission transmitted its determination in this review to the 
Secretary of Commerce on February 28, 2011. The views of the Commission 
are contained in USITC Publication 4216 (February 2011), entitled 
Porcelain-on-Steel Cooking Ware from China: Investigation No. 731-TA-
298 (Third Review).
