Bynum Hydro Company, PK Ventures I Limited Partnership; Notice of 
Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions to 
Intervene, and Protests

    On January 16, 2015, Commission staff directed PK Ventures I 
Limited Partnership (PK Ventures) to file documentation of project 
ownership and an application to transfer the license for the Bynum 
Project No. 4093 from Bynum Hydro Company, the licensee of record, to 
PK Ventures, the owner of the project facilities. On February 18, 2015, 
PK Ventures filed a response, including among other things, 
documentation of its ownership of the project and a copy of an earlier 
filing with the Commission in which it seeks to transfer the license 
from Bynum Hydro to it. The project is located on the Haw River in 
Chatham County, North Carolina.
