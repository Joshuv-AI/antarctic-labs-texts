NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Notice of Changes in Subject of Meeting

    The National Credit Union Administration Board determined that its 
business requires the addition of the following two items to the 
previously announced closed meeting (Federal Register, Vol. 64. No. 69, 
page 17688, Monday, April 12, 1999) scheduled for Thursday, April 15, 
1999.
    4. Administrative Action under Section 206 of the Federal Credit 
Union Act. Closed pursuant to exemptions (8), (9)(A)(ii) and (9)(B).
    5. Y2K 1999 Examination Program. Closed pursuant to exemption (8).
    The Board voted unanimously that agency business requires that 
these items be considered with less than the usual seven days notice, 
that they be closed to the public, and that no earlier announcement of 
these changes was possible.
    The previously announced items were:
    1. Administrative Action under Section 107 of the Federal Credit 
Union Act and Part 701 of NCUA's Rules and Regulations. Closed pursuant 
to exemption (8).
    2. Administrative Action under Section 206 of the Federal Credit 
Union Act. Closed pursuant to exemptions (4), (7) and (8).
    3. Two (2) Personnel Actions. Closed pursuant to exemptions (2) and 
(6).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Becky Baker, Secretary of the Board,
