SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster #2938]


State of West Virginia; (AMENDMENT No. 1)

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency, dated March 15, 1997, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning 
on February 28, 1997 and continuing through March 15, 1997.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for physical damage is May 6, 1997, and for 
loans for economic injury the deadline is December 8, 1997.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: March 20, 1997.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
