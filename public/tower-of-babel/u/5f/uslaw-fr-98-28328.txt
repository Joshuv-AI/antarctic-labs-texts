DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. CP97-750-000]


Valero Natural Gas Pipeline Company; Notice of Corporate Name 
Change

October 19, 1998.
    On October 13, 1998, Mobil Natural Gas Pipeline Company (MNGPC) 
made a filing formally notifying the Commission of the change in its 
corporate name to Valero Natural Gas Company (VNGPC). By this notice, 
the certificate issued to MNGPC in the above-docketed proceeding is 
hereby redesignated to reflect the change in name to VNGPC.
    Given that MNGPC was not obligated to file a tariff, no new tariff 
sheets are necessary to reflect the change in corporate name.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
