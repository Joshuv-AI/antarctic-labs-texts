Employment and Training Administration

Lenz and Rieker, Totowa, New Jersey; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 20, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Lenz & Riecker, Totowa, New Jersey.
    The Department has been unable to locate principals of the firm or 
otherwise obtain information to reach a determination on worker 
eligibility. Consequently further investigation in this case would 
serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 5th day of September 2002.
Richard Church
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
