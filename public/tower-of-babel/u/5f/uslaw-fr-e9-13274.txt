DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Advisory Board, June 11-12, 2009, 8 a.m. to 12 p.m., National 
Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892, 
which was published in the Federal Register on May 14, 2009, 74 FR 
22753.
    This notice is amended to change the times of the open session on 
June 11, 2009 to 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. and the closed session to 3:30 
p.m. to 5 p.m. Additionally, the open session on June 12, 2009 has been 
cancelled.

    Dated: May 28, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
