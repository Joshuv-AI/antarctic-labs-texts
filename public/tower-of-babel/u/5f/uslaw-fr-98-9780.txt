DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


National Register of Historic Places; Notification of Pending 
Nominations

    Nominations for the following properties being considered for 
listing in the National Register were received by the National Park 
Service before April 4, 1998. Pursuant to Sec. 60.13 of 36 CFR Part 60 
written comments concerning the significance of these properties under 
the National Register criteria for evaluation may be forwarded to the 
National Register, National Park Service, P.O. Box 37127, Washington, 
D.C. 20013-7127. Written comments should be submitted by April 29, 
1998.
Beth Savage,
Acting Keeper of the National Register.
