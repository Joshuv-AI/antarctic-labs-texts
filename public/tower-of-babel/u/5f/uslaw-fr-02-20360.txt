SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3428]


State of Texas, (Amendment #8); Disaster Loan Areas

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated July 31, 2002, the above numbered declaration 
is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as 
beginning on June 29, 2002 and continuing through July 31, 2002.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is September 2, 2002, and for 
economic injury the deadline is April 4, 2003.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: August 6, 2002.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
