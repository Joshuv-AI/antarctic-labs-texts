SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2728; Amendment #4]

 

Georgia; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    The above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended, in accordance 
with notices from the Federal Emergency Management Agency dated August 
16 and August 23, 1994, to include Oglethorpe County in the State of 
Georgia as a disaster area as a result of damages caused by severe 
storms and flooding resulting from Tropical Storm Alberto beginning on 
July 3, 1994 and continuing through July 25, 1994, and to extend the 
deadline for filing applications for physical damages. The deadline is 
hereby extended thirty days to October 4, 1994.
    In addition, application for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous counties of Clarke, Elbert, 
Greene, Madison, Oconee, Taliaferro, and Wikes in the State of Georgia 
may be filed until the specified date at the previously designated 
location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for economic injury is April 7, 1995.
    The economic injury number for Georgia is 829300.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: August 30, 1994.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
