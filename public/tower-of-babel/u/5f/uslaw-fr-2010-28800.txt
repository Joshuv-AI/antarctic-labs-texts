Minden Bancorp, Inc., Minden, LA; Approval of Conversion 
Application

    Notice is hereby given that on November 9, 2010, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of Minden Mutual Holding 
Company and MBL Bank, Minden, Louisiana, to convert to the stock form 
of organization. Copies of the application are available for inspection 
by appointment (phone number: 202-906-5922 or e-mail 
[email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, 
NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS Western Regional Office, 122 W. 
John Carpenter Freeway, Suite 600, Irving, Texas 75261-9027.

    Dated: November 10, 2010.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
