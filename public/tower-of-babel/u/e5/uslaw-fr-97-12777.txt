National Register of Historic Places; Notification of Pending 
Nominations

    The following property is being considered for listing in the 
National Register and was received by the National Park Service on May 
9, 1997. Pursuant to section 60.13 of 36 CFR Part 60 written comments 
concerning the significance of these properties under the National 
Register criteria for evaluation may be forwarded to the National 
Register, National Park Service, P.O. Box 37127, Washington, D.C.

20013-7127. Written comments should be submitted by May 30, 1997.
