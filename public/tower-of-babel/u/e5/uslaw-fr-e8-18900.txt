SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published a document in the 
Federal Register on March 31, 2008, transferring public land to the 
Department of Energy. The document contained some errors in the land 
description.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mary von Koch, 435-259-2128.
