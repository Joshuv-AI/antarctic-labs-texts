Shephard Clothing Company, Inc., New Bedford, MA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 11, 2003, in response to a worker petition that 
was filed on behalf of workers at Shepard Clothing Company, Inc., New 
Bedford, Massachusetts. All workers at the subject firm were certified 
on June 11, 2001 (TA-W-38,955). That certification expires two years 
from date of certification. All employees were laid off prior to the 
expiration of that TAA certification.
    Consequently, further investigation would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 14th day of April, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
