NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Public Hearing on Trans World Airlines (TWA) Flight 800 Accident

    In connection with its investigation of the accident involving 
Trans World Airlines (TWA) flight 800, a Boeing 747-100, N93119, in the 
Atlantic Ocean near East Moriches, NY, July 17, 1996, the National 
Transportation Safety Board will convene a public hearing beginning at 
9:00 a.m., on Monday, December 8, 1997, at the Baltimore Convention 
Center, Halls A and B, One West Pratt Street, Baltimore, MD. For more 
information, contact Shelly Hazle, Office of Public Affairs, 
Washington, D.C. 20594, telephone (202) 314-6100.

    Dated: October 30, 1997.
Ray Smith,
Alternate Federal Register Liaison Officer.
