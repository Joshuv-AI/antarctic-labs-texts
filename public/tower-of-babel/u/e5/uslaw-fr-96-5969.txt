COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Amendment to Notice of Public Meeting of the Louisiana Advisory 
Committee

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the rules and 
regulations of the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights, that a meeting of 
the Louisiana Advisory Committee to the Commission announced in the 
Federal Register, FR Doc 96-2570, 61 FR 4624, published February 7, 
1996, will convene at 6:00 p.m. and adjourn at 8:30 p.m. on March 28, 
1996, at the Radisson Hotel, 4728 Constitution, Baton Rouge, Louisiana 
70808. (This amendment is for change of location and time only.)
    Persons desiring additional information, should contact Melvin L. 
Jenkins, director of the Central Regional Office, 913-551-1400 (TTY 
913-551-1414).

    Dated at Washington, DC, March 7, 1996.
Carol-Lee Hurley,
Chief, Regional Programs Coordination Unit.
