SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

 

Portland District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Portland District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Thursday, December 8, 1994 from 
1. p.m. to 4:30 p.m. and Friday, December 9, 1994 from 8 a.m. to 12 
noon at the Surf Sand Motel, Gower & Rock Court Street, Cannon Beach, 
Oregon to discuss such matter as may be presented by members, staff of 
the U.S. Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. John L. Gilman, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 222 SW., Columbia, Suite 
500, Portland, OR 97201-6695, (503) 326-5221.

    Dated: October 31, 1994.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Acting Assistant Administrator, Office of Advisory Councils.
