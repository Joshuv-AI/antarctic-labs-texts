DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Civil Tiltrotor Development Advisory Committee

    Pursuant to section 10(A)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
Public Law (72-362); 5 U.S.C. (App. I), notice is hereby given of the 
cancellation of a meeting of the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) 
sponsored Civil Tiltrotor Development Advisory Committee (CTRDAC) 
previously announced for June 29 in Washington DC. The meeting will be 
rescheduled on a later date. A Federal Register announcement will be 
published once a date has been chosen.
    For further information, contact Ms. Karen Braxton (202) 267-9451.

    Dated: June 14, 1995.
Richard A. Weiss,
Designated Federal Official, Civil Tiltrotor Development Advisory 
Committee.
