DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Intent To Grant Partially Exclusive Patent License; 
Pinon Technologies, Inc.

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy herby gives notice of its intent to 
grant to Pinon Technologies, Inc., a revocable, nonassignable, 
partially exclusive license in the United States to practice the 
Government-Owned invention(s) described in U.S. Patent Number 5,962,863 
entitled ``Laterally Disposed Nanostructures of Silicone on Insulating 
Substrate'', issue date October 4, 1999.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license must file 
written objections along with supporting evidence, if any, not later 
than September 4, 2008.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Office of 
Research and Technology Applications, Space and Naval Warfare Systems 
Center, San Diego, Code 73120, 53560 Hull St., San Diego, CA 92152-
5001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen H. Lieberman, Ph.D., Office of 
Research and Technology Applications, Space and Naval Warfare Systems 
Center, San Diego, Code 73120, 53560 Hull St., San Diego, CA 92152-
5001, telephone: 619-553-2778, E-Mail: [email protected].
