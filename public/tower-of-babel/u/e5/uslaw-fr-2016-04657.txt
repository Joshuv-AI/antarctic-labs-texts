OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sundhine Act Cancellation Notice--OPIC's March 9, 2016 Annual 
Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Annual Public Hearing was 
published in the Federal Register (Volume 81, Number 7, Pages 1449-
1450) on January 12, 2016. No requests were received to provide 
testimony or submit written statements for the record; therefore, 
OPIC's Annual Public Hearing scheduled for 1 p.m., March 9, 2015 has 
been cancelled.
    Contact Person for Information: Information on the hearing 
cancellation may be obtained from Catherine F. I. Andrade at (202) 336-
8768, or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: February 26, 2016.
Catherine F.I. Andrade,
OPIC Corporate Secretary.
