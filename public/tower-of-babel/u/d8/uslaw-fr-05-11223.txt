DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Biomaterials and 
Biointerfaces Study Section, June 27, 2005, 8 a.m. to June 28, 2005, 5 
p.m., Holiday Inn Select Bethesda, 8120 Wisconsin Ave, Bethesda, MD, 
20814 which was published in the Federal Register on May 26, 2005, 70 
FR 30475-30477.
    The meeting is cancelled due to a lack of quorum.

    Dated: May 27, 2005.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
