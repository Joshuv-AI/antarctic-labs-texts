Defense Contracting; Reporting Procedures on Defense-Related

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, DoD.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This rule is the fiscal year 1993 revision of the section 
listing DoD contractors receiving contract awards of $10 million or 
more. This part is published to comply with the provisions of section 
1, Public Law 97-295, October 12, 1982; 10 U.S.C. 2397.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 30, 1993.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. R.S. Drake, Acting Director, Directorate for Information Operations 
and Reports, Washington Headquarters Services, 1215 Jefferson Davis 
Highway, suite 1204, Arlington, VA 22202-4302; Telephone (703) 746-
0334.
