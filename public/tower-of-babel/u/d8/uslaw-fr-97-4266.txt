SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Washington, D.C. District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region III District Advisory 
Council located in the geographical area of Washington, D.C., will hold 
a public meeting from 9:00 a.m.-11:00 a.m., on Wednesday, February 19, 
1997, at 733 15th Street, N.W., Suite 300, Washington, D.C., to discuss 
such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Anita L. Irving, Public 
Information Officer, U.S. Small Business Administration, 1110 Vermont 
Avenue, N.W., Suite 900, (P.O. Box 34500), Washington, D.C. 20045; 
telephone 202/606-4000, ext. 275.

    Dated: February 3, 1997.
Michael P. Novelli,
Director, Office of Advisory Councils.
