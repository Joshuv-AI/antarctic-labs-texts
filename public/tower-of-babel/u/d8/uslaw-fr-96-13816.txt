DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Center for Research Resources; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Scientific 
and Technical Review Board on Biomedical and Behavioral Research 
Facilities, on June 18-19, 1996, Holiday Inn, Georgia Room, 8120 
Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814, which was published in the 
Federal Register on May 7, 1996, 61 FR 20533.
    The committee was to have convened June 18-19, 1996, but will now 
meet June 18-20, 1996.
    The meeting will be open to the public on June 18, from 8:00 a.m. 
to 8:30 a.m. The meeting will be closed on June 18, from 8:30 a.m. 
until adjournment on June 20.

    Dated: May 29, 1996.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
