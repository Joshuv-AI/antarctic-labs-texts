DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-31,345]


Adams-Millis, High Point, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 21, 1995 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on August 9, 1995 on behalf of workers at Adams-Millis, High 
Point, North Carolina (a division of the Sara Lee Corporation).
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-30,083, Adams-Millis, High Point, North 
Carolina, certified August 29, 1994, impact date of June 29, 1993 and 
an expiration date of August 29, 1996). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 29th day of August, 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
