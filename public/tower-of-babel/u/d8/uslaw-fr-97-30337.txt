DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration

21 CFR Parts 510, 520, 522, 524, and 558


Animal Drugs, Feeds, and Related Products; Change of Sponsor

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is amending the animal 
drug regulations to reflect the change of sponsor for 61 approved new 
animal drug applications (NADA's) from Mallinckrodt Veterinary, Inc., 
to Schering-Plough Animal Health Corp.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 19, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Thomas J. McKay, Center for Veterinary 
Medicine (HFV-102), Food and Drug Administration, 7500 Standish Pl., 
Rockville, MD 20855, 301-827-0213.



SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Mallinckrodt Veterinary, Inc., Mundelein, IL 
60060, has informed FDA that it has transferred the ownership of, and 
all rights and interests in the approved NADA's to Schering-Plough 
Animal Health Corp. The agency is amending 21 CFR 510, 520, 522, 524, 
and 558 to reflect the change of sponsor.
