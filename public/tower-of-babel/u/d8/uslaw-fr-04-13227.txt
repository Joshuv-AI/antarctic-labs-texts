THE COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled for 16 
June 2004 at 10 a.m. in the Commission's offices at the National 
Building Museum, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 401 F Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20001-2728. Items of discussion affecting the appearance 
of Washington, DC may include buildings, parks and memorials.
    Draft agendas and additional information regarding the Commission 
are available on our Web site: http://www.cfa. gov. Inquiries regarding 
the agenda and requests to submit written or oral statements should be 
addressed to Frederick J. Lindstrom, Acting Secretary, Commission of 
Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200. Individuals 
requiring sign language interpretation for the hearing impaired should 
contact the Secretary at least 10 days before the meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, DC, 31 May 2004.
Frederick J. Lindstrom,
Acting Secretary.
