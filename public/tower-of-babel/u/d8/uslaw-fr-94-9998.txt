SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
 

Montpelier District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Montpelier District Advisory 
Council will gold a public meeting at 10:00 a.m. on Wednesday, April 
27, 1994, at the New England Culinary Institute, 250 Main Street, 
Montpelier, Vermont to discuss such matters as may be presented by 
members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Kenneth A. Silvia, 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, Federal 
Building, 87 State Street, P.O. Box 605, Montpelier, Vermont 05601, 
(802) 828-4422.

Dorothy A. Overal,
Acting Assistant Administrator, Office of Advisory Councils.
