DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Sunshine Act Meeting

Public Announcement

Pursuant To The Government In the Sunshine Act (Public Law 94-409) [5 
U.S.C. Section 552b]

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Department of Justice.

TIME AND DATE: 1:30 p.m., Monday, September 23, 1996.

PLACE: 5550 Friendship Boulevard, Suite 400, Chevy Chase, Maryland 
20815.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The following matters have been placed on the 
agenda for the open Parole Commission meeting:
    1. Approval of minutes of previous Commission meeting.
    2. Reports from the Chairman, Commissioners, Legal, Chief of Staff, 
Case Operations, and Administrative Sections.
    3. Proposal for Modification of Transfer Treaty Regulation at 28 
C.F.R. Sec. 2.62(e) for short term offenders.
    4. Proposal for Continuation of Expedited Revocation Project.
    5. Proposal for Special Parole Condition Regarding Computer Use.

AGENCY CONTACT: Tom Kowalski, Case Operations, United States Parole 
Commission, (301) 492-5962.

    Dated: September 13, 1996.
Michael A. Stover,
General Counsel, U.S. Parole Commission.
