DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Board of 
Scientific Counselors, NIEHS, March 20, 2011, 7 p.m. to March 22, 2011, 
12:30 p.m., Doubletree Guest Suites, 2515 Meridian Parkway, Research 
Triangle Park, NC, 27713 which was published in the Federal Register on 
February 23, 2011, 76 FR 36.

date. The meeting will be held Sunday, May 22, 2011 at 7 p.m. through 
Tuesday, May 24, 2011 at 12:30 p.m. The meeting is partially Closed to 
the public.
