DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission and Commission Staff Attendance at 
Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc. Meetings

members of the Commission and its staff may attend the following MISO-
related meetings: MISO Board of Directors Meeting, December 7, 2017, 
8:30 a.m.-11:00 a.m.

MISO Headquarters, 720 City Center Drive, Carmel, IN 46032-7574.

https://www.misoenergy.org. All meetings are Eastern Time.
