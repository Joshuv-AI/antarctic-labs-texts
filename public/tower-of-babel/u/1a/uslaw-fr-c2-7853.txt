Proposed Establishment of Class D Airspace, Proposed Modification

Correction

    In proposed rule document 02-7853 beginning on page 15502 in the 
issue of Tuesday, April 2, 2002, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 15502, in the first column, under the SUMMARY: heading, 
in the 11th line, ``carriers'' should read ``carrier''.


Sec.  71.1  [Corrected]

    2. On page 15503, in the first column, Sec.  71.1, under the 
heading, AGL MI D Marquette, MI [New], in the fourth line, ``the'' 
should read ``and''.
    3. On page 15503, in the first column, Sec.  71.1, the heading, 
``Paragraph 6002 Class D airspace areas extending upward from the 
surface of the earth.'' should read `` Paragraph 6002 Class E airspace 
areas extending upward from the surface of the earth.''.
