Radio Broadcasting Services; Jackson, WY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 69 FR 35564 
(June 25, 2004), this Report and Order dismisses the Petition for Rule 
Making in MB Docket No. 04-214 proposing to allot Channel 249A at 
Jackson, Wyoming. No expression of interest was filed in this proposal.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria M. McCauley, Media Bureau 
(202) 418-2180.
