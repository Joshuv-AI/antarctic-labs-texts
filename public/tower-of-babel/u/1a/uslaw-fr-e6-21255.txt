AAR Manufacturing dba AAR Cargo Systems, Livonia, MI; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 20, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at AAR 
Manufacturing, dba Cargo Systems, Livonia, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Dated: December 4, 2006.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
