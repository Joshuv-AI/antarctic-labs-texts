DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Trinity County Resource Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Trinity County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC) will meet 
on August 19, 2002 in Weaverville, California. The purpose of the 
meeting is to discuss the selection of Title II projects under Public 
Law 106-393, H.R. 2389, the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-
Determination Act of 2000, also called the ``Payments to States'' Act.

DATES: The meeting will be held on August 19, 2002 from 6:30 to 8:30 
p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Trinity County Office of 
Education Conference Room, 201 Memorial Drive, Weaverville, California.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce Anderson, Designated Federal 
Official, USDA, Shasta Trinity National Forests, P.O. Box 1190, 
Weaverville, CA 96093. Phone: (530) 623-1709. Email: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting will focus on prioritizing and 
selecting Title II projects for fuels treatment and sediment reduction. 
The meeting is open to the public. Public input opportunity will be 
provided and individuals will have the opportunity to address the 
committee at that time.

    Dated: July 16, 2002.
S.E. ``Lou'' Woltering,
Forest Supervisor.
