DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 3424]


Office of the Coordinator for Counterterrorism; Designation of a 
Foreign Terrorist Organization

AGENCY: Department of State.

ACTION: Designation of a Foreign Terrorist Organization.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Pursuant to Section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act 
(``INA''), as added by the Antiterrorism and Effective Death Penalty 
Act of 1996, Public Law 104-132, section 302, 110 Stat. 1214, 1248 
(1996), and amended by the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant 
Responsibility Act of 1996, Public Law 104-208, 110 Stat. 3009 (1996), 
the Secretary of State hereby designates, effective September 25, 2000, 
the following organization as a foreign terrorist organization:

The Islamic Movement of Uzbekistan

    Dated: September 21, 2000.
Ambassador Michael A. Sheehan,
Coordinator for Counterterrorism, Department of State.
