DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board

ACTION: Meeting date change.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Defense Science Board Task Force on Intelligence Needs for 
Homeland Defense closed meeting scheduled for April 24-25, 2001, has 
been changed to April 23-24-25-26, 2001. The location of the meeting 
has not changed; the meeting will be held at Strategic Analysis, Inc., 
3601 Wilson Boulevard, Suite 600, Arlington, VA.

    Dated: March 28, 2001.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
