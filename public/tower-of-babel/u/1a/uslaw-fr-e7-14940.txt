    This Notice also lists the following information:
    Title of Proposal: Lender Qualifications for Multifamily 
Accelerated Processing (MAP).
    OMB Control Number, if applicable: 2502-0541.
    Description of the Need for the Information and Proposed Use: 
Multifamily Accelerated Processing (MAP) is a procedure that permits 
approved lenders to prepare, process, and submit loan applications for 
Federal Housing Administration (FHA) multifamily mortgage insurance. An 
FHA-approved multifamily Lender wishing to participate in MAP must 
submit a MAP application package so that HUD may determine whether or 
not it meets the additional qualifications required of a MAP Lender. A 
Quality Control Plan is a required exhibit in the Lender application 
package.
    Agency Form Numbers, if Applicable: None.
    Estimation of the Total numbers of Hours Needed To Prepare the 
Information Collection Including Number of Respondents, Frequency of 
Response, and Hours of Response: The annual number of respondents is 25 
preparing 25 responses annually. The hours per response are 20 hours 
for the MAP Lender application, and the frequency of response is on 
occasion. The total estimated annual burden hours is 500.
    Status of the Proposed Information Collection: Extension of a 
currently approved collection.
