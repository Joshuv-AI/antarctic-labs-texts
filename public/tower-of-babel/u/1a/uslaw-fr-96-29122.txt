FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Farm Credit Administration Board; Regular Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 552b(e)(3)), that the November 14, 1996 regular 
meeting of the Farm Credit Administration Board (Board) will not be 
held.
