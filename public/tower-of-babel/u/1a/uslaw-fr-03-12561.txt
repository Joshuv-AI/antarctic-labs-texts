DBM Technologies LLC, Blow Molding, Corunna, Michigan; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 14, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at DBM Technologies LLC, Blow 
Molding, Corunna, Michigan.
    The subject plant closed more than one year prior to the date of 
the petition. Section 223(b) of the Act specifies that no certification 
may apply to any worker whose last separation occurred more than one 
year before the date of the petition. Consequently, the investigation 
has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 9th day of May, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
