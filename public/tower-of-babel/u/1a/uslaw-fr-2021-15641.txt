Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Petition for Reconsideration (Petition) has been filed in the 
Commission's rulemaking proceeding by Catherine Wang, on behalf of 
Shure Incorporated.

DATES: Oppositions to the Petition must be filed on or before August 9, 
2021. Replies to an opposition must be filed on or before August 17, 
2021.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 45 L Street NE, 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Hugh VanTuyl, Office of Engineering 
and Technology Bureau, (202) 418-7506 or [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a summary of the Commission's 
document, Report No. 3178, released July 7, 2021. The full text of the 
Petition can be accessed online via the Commission's Electronic Comment 
Filing System at: http://apps.fcc.gov/ecfs/. The Commission will not 
send a Congressional Review Act (CRA) submission to Congress or the 
Government Accountability Office pursuant to the CRA, 5 U.S.C. 
801(a)(1)(A), because no rules are being adopted by the Commission.
    Subject: Unlicensed White Space Device Operations in the Television 
Bands, FCC 20-156, published at 86 FR 11490, February 25, 2021, in ET 
Docket No. 20-36. This document is being published pursuant to 47 CFR 
1.429(e). See also 47 CFR 1.4(b)(1) and 1.429(f), (g).
