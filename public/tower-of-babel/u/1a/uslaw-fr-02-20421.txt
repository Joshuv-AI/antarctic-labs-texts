DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, August 3, 2002, 12 p.m. to 
August 3, 2002, 1 p.m., NIH, Rockledge 2, Bethesda, MD 20892 which 
published in the Federal Register on July 26, 2002, 67 FR 48931-48932.
    The meeting will be held on August 20, 2002. The time and location 
remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.
