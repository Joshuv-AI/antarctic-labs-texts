DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,428]


Multi-Plex: Howe, IN; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 27, 2009 in response to a 
worker petition filed on behalf of workers of Multi-Plex, Howe, 
Indiana.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of March 2009.
Linda G. Poole
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
