POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--Global Plus 3 Contracts

AGENCY: Postal ServiceTM.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing a request with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add the Global Plus 3 product to the 
Competitive Products List.

DATES: Effective date: June 16, 2016.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher C. Meyerson, (202) 268-
7820.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice that, pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642, on June 10, 2016, 
it filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission a Request of the United 
States Postal Service to add Global Plus 3 to the Competitive Product 
List. Documents are available at www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. MC2016-152 
and CP2016-196.

Stanley F. Mires,
Attorney, Federal Compliance.
