Southern Company Services, Inc.; Notice of FERC Staff Attendance

February 12, 2009.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on February 13, 2009 at 10:30 a.m. (EDT), members of its 
staff will participate in a seminar and interactive webcast relating to 
the Southern Company Energy Auction. The agenda and other documents for 
the webcast are available through the Southern Company Services, Inc. 
(Southern Company) Web site, http://www.southerncompany.com/energyauction/. Sponsored by Southern Company, the webcast is open to 
all market participants. Commission staff's attendance is part of the 
Commission's ongoing monitoring of Southern Company's development of 
the Southern Company Energy Auction. The webcast may discuss matters at 
issue in the above-captioned dockets.
