COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Proposed Additions to Procurement List; Correction

    In the document appearing on page 45506, F.R. Doc. 99-21669, in the 
issue of August 20, 1999, in the second column, the service listed as 
Laundry Service, Naval Air Station, Brunswick, Maine and Portsmouth, 
New Hampshire should read Laundry Service, Naval Air Station, 
Brunswick, Maine and Portsmouth Naval Shipyard, Portsmouth, New 
Hampshire.
Beverly L. Milkman,
Executive Director.
