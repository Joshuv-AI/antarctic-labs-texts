47 CFR Part 73

[DA 04-87; MB Docket No. 03-26; RM-10638]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Shawnee and Topeka, KS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Cumulus Licensing Corp., 
licensee of FM Station KMAJ, Channel 299C, Topeka, Kansas, removes 
Channel 299C at Topeka, Kansas, from the FM Table of Allotments, allots 
Channel 299C1 at Shawnee, Kansas, as the community's first local FM 
service, and modifies the license of FM Station KMAJ to specify 
operation on Channel 299C1 at Shawnee. Channel 299C1 can be allotted to 
Shawnee, Kansas, in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements with a site restriction of 41.3 km (25.6 miles) 
west of Shawnee. The coordinates for Channel 299C1 at Shawnee, Kansas, 
are 3909[min]06[sec] North Latitude and 
9509[min]28[sec] West Longitude.

DATES: Effective March 8, 2004.
