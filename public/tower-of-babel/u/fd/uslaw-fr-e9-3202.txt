DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, March 12, 2009, 8:30 a.m. to 
March 13, 2009, 5 p.m., Bethesda Marriott--Pooks Hill, 5151 Pooks Hill 
Road, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register 
on January 12, 2009, 74 FR71219.
    This notice is amended to change the Scientific Review Officer from 
Rhonda J. Moore, PhD to Joyce C. Pegues, PhD. The meeting is closed to 
the public.

    Dated: February 9, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
