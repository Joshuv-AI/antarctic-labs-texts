Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards; Air Brake Systems

AGENCY: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), 
Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Request for comments; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On July 24, 1995, NHTSA published a document requesting 
comments about devices that remove water and other contaminants from 
air brake systems. The agency has since learned that the docket number 
in the heading of that document is incorrect. (60 FR 37864) Today's 
document corrects the docket number to read ``[Docket No. 95-65; Notice 
1]''. The July 24, 1995 document had read ``[Docket No. 95-57; Notice 
1]''.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The correction to the July 24, 1995 document is 
effective on August 17, 1995.
