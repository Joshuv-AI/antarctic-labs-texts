California Independent System Operator Corporation; Notice of 
Deadlines for Comments and Reply Comments

September 23, 2005.
    On September 13, 2005, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission staff 
convened a technical conference to explore tariff issues related to 
demand response options, including special case nodal pricing and the 
establishment of zones for wholesale customers, in accordance with the 
directive of the July 1, 2005 Order on Further Amendments to the 
California Independent System Operator's Comprehensive Market Redesign 
Proposal.\1\ As stated at the technical conference, comments concerning 
the issues discussed at the technical conference are due on September 
27, 2005, and reply comments are due on October 4, 2005.
