UNITED STATES INFORMATION AGENCY

Culturally Significant Objects Imported for Exhibition; 
Determinations

    This is an amendment to Notice Regarding Culturally Significant 
Objects Imported for Exhibition in the exhibit ``Sepphoris in Galilee: 
Crosscurrents of Culture''. This is to amend Federal Register Doc. 96-
28328, 61 FR 56742 (November 4, 1996) by adding the following language 
after the words ``on or about July 6, 1997,'' to read:

``and at the University of Michigan Museum of Art in Ann Arbor, 
Michigan from on or about September 7, 1997 to on or about December 14, 
1997 and at the Michael C. Carlos Museum at Emory University in 
Atlanta, Georgia from on or about January 24, 1998, to on or about 
April 12, 1998,''.

    Dated: March 19, 1997.
R. Wallace Stuart,
Acting General Counsel.
