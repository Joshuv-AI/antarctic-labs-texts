    Needs and Uses: CAPE is statutorily required by Title 10, United 
Stated Code in Section 2334(g), to ``develop policies, procedures, 
guidance and a collection method to ensure that quality acquisition 
cost data are collected to facilitate cost estimation and comparison 
across acquisition programs.'' Section 2334(g) also contains a 100-
million-dollar threshold statutory requirement for providing cost data 
from each acquisition program that exceeds this amount.
    Affected Public: Businesses or other for-profit; Not-for-profit
