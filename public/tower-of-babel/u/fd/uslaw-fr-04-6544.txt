S&S Distribution Center, a Subsidiary of Land N Sea Company, 
Inc., Roebuck, SC; Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at S&S Distribution Center, a 
subsidiary of Land N Sea Company, Inc., Roebuck, South Carolina. The 
application contained no new substantial information which would bear 
importantly on the Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of 
the application was issued.
