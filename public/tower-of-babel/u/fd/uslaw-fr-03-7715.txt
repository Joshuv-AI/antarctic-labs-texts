DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the Transformational Communications Advanced 
Technology Study. The purpose of the meeting is to conduct a mid term 
review of the study. This meeting will be closed to the public.

DATES: March 31-April 1, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Bldg 201, Kirtland AFB NM 87117.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Maj John J. Pernot, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.
