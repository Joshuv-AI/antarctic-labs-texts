DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 20, 2005, 5 p.m. to 
October 21, 2005, 2:30 p.m., Hilton Crystal City, 2399 Jefferson Davis 
Highway, Arlington, VA 22202 which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 28, 2005, 70 FR 56727-56729.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the reassignment of the 
applications.

    Dated: October 6, 2005.
Anthony M. Coelho, Jr.,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
