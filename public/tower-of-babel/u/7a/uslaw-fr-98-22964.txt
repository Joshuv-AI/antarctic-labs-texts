DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Office of Management; Performance Review Board Membership

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Notice of Membership of the Performance Review Board (PRB).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Secretary announces the names of members of the PRB for 
the Department of Education. Under 5 U.S.C. 4314 (c) (1) through (5), 
each agency is required to establish one or more Senior Executive 
Service (SES) PRBs. The PRB reviews and evaluates the initial appraisal 
of a senior executive's performance along with any comments by senior 
executives and any higher level executive and makes

[[Page 45802]]

recommendations to the appointing authority relative to the performance 
of the senior executive, including making recommendations on 
performance awards.
    The PRB is also responsible for providing recertification 
recommendations for career SES appointees in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 
3393 (a) and 5 CFR 317.504 (f). Recommendations on SES pay level 
adjustments are also made by the PRB.
