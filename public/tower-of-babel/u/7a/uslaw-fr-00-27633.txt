SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3290]


State of Montana; Amendment #2

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated September 26, 2000, the above-numbered 
Declaration is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this 
disaster as beginning on July 13, 2000 and continuing through September 
25, 2000.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is October 29, 2000 and for 
economic injury the deadline is May 30, 2001.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: October 6, 2000.
Allan I. Hoberman,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
