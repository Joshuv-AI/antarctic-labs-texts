Idaho: Filing of Protraction Diagrams in Idaho

    The protraction diagrams (7) of the following described land were 
officially filed in the Idaho State Office, Bureau of Land Management, 
Boise, Idaho, effective 9:00 a.m. April 1, 1998.
    The protraction diagrams (7) for partially surveyed T. 10 S., R. 41 
E.; T. 10 S., R. 42 E; T. 11 S., R. 42 E.; T. 12 S., R. 41 E.; T. 13 
S., R 41 E.; T. 14 N., R. 41 E.; and T. 15 S., R. 41 E., Boise 
Meridian, Idaho were accepted April 1, 1998. The preparation of these 
diagrams was requested by the USDA Forest Service, Geometronics Service 
Center, to support its mapping program.
    All inquiries concerning the survey of the above described land 
must be sent

[[Page 17442]]

to the Chief, Cadastral Survey, Idaho State Office, Bureau of Land 
Management, 1387 S. Vinnell Way, Boise, Idaho, 83709-1657.

    Dated: April 1, 1998.
Duane E. Olsen,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor for Idaho.
