Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2022-0114.
    Petitioner: Federal Express Corporation.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  121.1105(b)(1), 
121.1105(b)(2), and 121.1105(b)(3).
    Description of Relief Sought: Federal Express Corporation (FedEx) 
is requesting an exemption for the 757 fleet to 14 CFR 121.1105(b)(1), 
(2), and (3) to extend the interval for aging aircraft inspection and 
records review from the current requirement of seven years to an 
interval of eight years. FedEx operates the 757 fleet at low 
utilization rates, and the ability to extend the reviews to eight years 
(when the airplane undergoes a thorough and extensive maintenance 
visit) supports more comprehensive inspections of repairs and 
alterations of the aircraft structure.
