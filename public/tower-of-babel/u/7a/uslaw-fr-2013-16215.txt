Public Land Order No. 7818; Withdrawal of Public Lands for the 
Protection and Preservation of Solar Energy Zones for Future Energy 
Development; Arizona, California, Colorado, Nevada, New Mexico, and 
Utah

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Public land order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This order withdraws 303,900 acres of public lands from 
location and entry under the United States mining laws, subject to 
valid existing rights, for a period of 20 years to protect 17 Solar 
Energy Zones (SEZ) for future solar energy development. The lands have 
been and will remain open to mineral and geothermal leasing, and 
mineral material sales.

DATES: As of: June 27, 2013.

by Section 204 of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976, 
43 U.S.C. 1714, it is ordered as follows:
