[Docket No. 40-8968-ML and ASLBP No. 95-706-01-ML]


Hydro Resources, Inc.; Notice of Reconstitution

    Pursuant to the authority contained in 10 CFR 2.721 and 2.1207, the 
Presiding Officer in the captioned 10 CFR Part 2, Subpart L proceeding 
is hereby replaced by appointing Administrative Judge Peter B. Bloch as 
Presiding Officer in place of Administrative Judge Thomas S. Moore.
    All correspondence, documents and other material shall be filed 
with the Presiding Officer in accordance with 10 CFR 2.1203 (1997). The 
address of the new Presiding Officer is: Administrative Judge Peter B. 
Bloch, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, U.S. Nuclear Regulatory 
Commission, Washington, D.C. 20555.

    Issued at Rockville, Maryland, this 9th day of December 1999.
G. Paul Bollwerk III,
Chief Administrative Judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel.
