Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 168--Dallas/Fort Worth, Texas, 
Authorization of Production Activity, Gulfstream Aerospace Corporation 
(Disassembly of Aircraft), Dallas, Texas

    On December 7, 2018, the Metroplex International Trade Development 
Corporation, grantee of FTZ 168, submitted a notification of proposed 
production activity to the FTZ Board on behalf of Gulfstream Aerospace 
Corporation, within Subzone168E, in Dallas, Texas.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (83 FR 64517-64518, December 17, 
2018). On May 16, 2019, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at this 
time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including Section 400.14.

    Dated: May 16, 2019.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
