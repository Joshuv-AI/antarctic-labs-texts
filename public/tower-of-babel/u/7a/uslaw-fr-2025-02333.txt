Self-Regulatory Organizations; NYSE Arca, Inc.; Notice of 
Withdrawal of a Proposed Rule Change To Waive the Options Regulatory 
Fee (ORF) for December 2024

February 3, 2025.
    On November 25, 2024, NYSE Arca, Inc. (the ``Exchange'' or ``NYSE 
Arca'') filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the 
``Commission''), pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities 
Exchange Act of 1934 (the ``Act'') \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a 
proposed rule change (File No. SR-NYSEARCA-2024-90) to amend its 
Options Fee Schedule regarding the Options Regulatory Fee (``ORF'').\3\ 
The proposed rule change was immediately effective upon filing with the 
Commission pursuant to Section 19(b)(3)(A) of the Act.\4\ The proposed 
rule change was published for comment in the Federal Register on 
December 16, 2024.\5\ On January 23, 2025, pursuant to Section 
19(b)(3)(C) of the Act, the Commission temporarily suspended the 
proposed rule change and instituted proceedings under Section 
19(b)(2)(B) of the Act to determine whether to approve or disapprove 
the proposed rule change.\6\ On January 27, 2025, the Exchange withdrew 
the proposed rule change (SR-NYSEARCA-2024-90).
