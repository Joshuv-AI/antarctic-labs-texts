Padgett Furniture Manufacturing Company, Inc.; Calhoun, TN; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 2, 2003, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Padgett 
Furniture Manufacturing Company, Inc., Calhoun, Tennessee.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 11th day of September, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
