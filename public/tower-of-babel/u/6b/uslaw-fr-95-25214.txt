DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Oregon Coast Provincial Advisory Committee Meeting

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Oregon Coast Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC) will meet 
on October 20, 1995, in Corvallis, Oregon, at the Siuslaw National 
Forest Supervisor's Office, 4077 Research Way. The meeting will begin 
at 10 a.m. and continue until 3:30 p.m. Agenda items to be covered 
include: (1) PAC progress report/direction for the next year; (2) 
Federal lands assessment: summary and lessons learned; (3) 1996 
watershed restoration program, and (4) open public forum. All Oregon 
Coast Province Advisory Committee meetings are open to the public. The 
``open forum'' is scheduled near the conclusion of the meeting. 
Interested citizens are encouraged to attend. The Committee welcomes 
the public's written comments on committee business at any time.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Direct questions regarding this meeting to Rick Alexander, Public 
Affairs Officer, at (503) 750-7075, or write to Forest Supervisor, 
Siuslaw National Forest, P.O. Box 1148, Corvallis, Oregon 97339.

    Dated: October 3, 1995.
James R. Furnish,
Forest Supervisor.
