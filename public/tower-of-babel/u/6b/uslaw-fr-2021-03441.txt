DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Office of the Director, National Institutes of Health; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the virtual meeting of the 
Advisory Committee to the Director, February 26, 2021, 3:00 p.m. to 
5:30 p.m., which was published in the Federal Register on February 5, 
2021, 85 FR 8371.
    The meeting notice is amended to revise the agenda topic as 
follows: Update on NIH Workforce Plans to Promote Diversity, Equity, 
and Inclusion in Biomedical Research and Request for Concept Clearance 
for an FY21 Common Fund Initiative.

    Dated: February 16, 2021.
David W. Freeman,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
