PJM Interconnection, L.L.C., and the PJM Open access Transmission 
Tariff.
    The amendments (1) enable PJM to obtain energy from members in 
emergencies, (2) enable PJM to pay members their actual costs incurred 
when PJM directs the cancellation of start-up of a generating unit, (3) 
change the date and time by which certain requests for non-firm 
transmission service must be made, and (4) correct the congestion 
pricing provisions so that PJM credits non-firm transmission customers 
that buy-through congestion, when their transactions produce flows that 
alleviate congestion.
    PJM requests an effective date of April 1, 1998, for the amendments 
to the Operating Agreement and PJM Tariff.

Paragraph E at the end of this notice.
