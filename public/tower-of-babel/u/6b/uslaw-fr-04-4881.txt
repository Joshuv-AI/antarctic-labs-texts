Petitions for Reconsideration of Action in Docketed Proceedings

February 25, 2004.

    Petitions for Reconsideration have been filed in the Commission's 
license transfer proceeding listed in this Public Notice. The full text 
of this document is available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 
445 12th Street, SW., Washington, DC, or may be purchased from the 
Commission's copy contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. 
Oppositions to these petitions must be filed by March 19, 2004. See 
Section 1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies 
to an opposition must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing 
oppositions have expired.
    Subject: In the Matter of General Motors Corporation and Hughes 
Electronics Corporation, Transferors (MB Docket No. 03-124); and The 
News Corporation Limited, Transferee, For Authority to Transfer 
Control.
    Number of Petitions Filed: 2.
