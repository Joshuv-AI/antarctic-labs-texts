Alden Manufacturing, Co. Chicago, IL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 14, 2003 in response to a 
petition filed on by a company official on behalf of workers of Alden 
Manufacturing, Co., Chicago, Illinois.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 30th day of March 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
