DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Office of the Secretary

 

Defense Advisory Committee on Military Personnel Testing; Meeting

action: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given that a 
meeting of the Defense Advisory Committee on Military Personnel Testing 
is scheduled to be held from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on September 22, 
1994, and from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on September 23, 1994. The 
meeting will be held at The Colonial Inn, 910 Prospect Street, La 
Jolla, California. The purpose of the meeting is to review planned 
changes and progress in developing paper-and-pencil and computerized 
enlistment tests and Department of Defense's Student Testing Program. 
Persons desiring to make oral presentations or submit written 
statements for consideration at the Committee meeting must contact Dr. 
Jane M. Arabian, Assistant Director, Accession Policy, Office of the 
Under Secretary of Defense (Personnel and Readiness), Room 2B271, The 
Pentagon, Washington, DC 20301-4000, telephone (703) 697-9271, no later 
than August 29, 1994.

    Dated: July 19, 1994.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
