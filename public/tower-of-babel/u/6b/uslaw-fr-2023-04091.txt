COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Virginia Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of virtual business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a virtual business meeting of the Virginia 
Advisory Committee. The meeting scheduled for Tuesday, February 28, 
2023, at 12:00 p.m. (ET) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal 
Register of Monday, February 13, 2023, in FR Doc. 2023-02998 in the 
first and second columns of page 9226.
