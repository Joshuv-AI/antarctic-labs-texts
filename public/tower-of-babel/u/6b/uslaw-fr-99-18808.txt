DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Treasury Decisions; Cancellations of Customs Brokers' Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Brokers' licenses cancellations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    I, as Commissioner, hereby pursuant to section 641(f), Tariff Act 
of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1641(f)) and section 111.51(a) of the 
Customs Regulations (19 CFR 111.51(a)), cancel the following Customs 
brokers' licenses without prejudice.
