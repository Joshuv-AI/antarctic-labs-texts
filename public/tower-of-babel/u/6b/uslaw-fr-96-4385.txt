Publication of Report

AGENCY: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of updated survivability and vehicle miles traveled 
schedules.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NHTSA has updated the passenger car and light truck schedules 
for survivability and vehicle miles traveled (VMT). These schedules are 
presented in the Final Report Updated 

[[Page 7300]]
Vehicle Survivability and Travel Mileage Schedules (DOT HS808339, 
November 1995). These updated schedules will be used to assess the 
effects of proposed fuel economy and safety standards.

4795.
