DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Office of Postsecondary Education; Correction

ACTION: Correction; Fund for the Improvement of Postsecondary 
Education--Special Focus Competition: European Community-United States 
of America Cooperation Program in Higher Education and Vocational 
Education and Training.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: We correct the dates listed in the sections entitled DATES and 
IV. Application and Submission Information, 3. Submission Dates and 
Times, as published in the Federal Register on December 30, 2003 (68 FR 
75221).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On December 30, 2003, we published a notice 
in the Federal Register inviting applications for the Fund for the 
Improvement of Postsecondary Education--Special Focus Competition: 
European Community-United States of America Cooperation Program in 
Higher Education and Vocational Education and Training Program. The 
dates listed in the sections entitled DATES and IV. Application and 
Submission Information, 3. Submission Dates and Times are corrected to 
read as follows.
    Applications Available: January 23, 2004.
    Deadline for Transmittal of Applications: April 23, 2004.
    Deadline for Intergovernmental Review: June 16, 2004.

(202) 502-7503 or by e-mail: [email protected].
