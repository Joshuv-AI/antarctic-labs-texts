Raider Apparel Inc. Alma, Georgia; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
section 250(a), Subchapter D, Chapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 USC 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
December 14, 2000 in response to a petition filed on behalf of workers 
at Raider Apparel Inc., Alma, Georgia.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (NAFTA-3103). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 23rd day of April, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
