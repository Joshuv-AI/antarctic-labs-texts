DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-40,794]


Frederick Goldman, New York, New York; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 11, 2002, in response to a petition filed by 
workers on behalf of all workers at Frederick Goldman, New York, New 
York.
    The petition group of workers is under an existing investigation 
for which a determination has not been issued (TA-W-40,774). 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 4th day of March, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
