DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 9373]


In the Matter of the Designation of the Libyan Islamic Fighting 
Group, Also Known as LIFG, as a Foreign Terrorist Organization Pursuant 
to Section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as Amended

    Based upon a review of the Administrative Record assembled in this 
matter, and in consultation with the Attorney General and the Secretary 
of the Treasury, I conclude that the circumstances that were the basis 
for the designation of the Libyan Islamic Fighting Group also known as 
LIFG as foreign terrorist organization have changed in such a manner as 
to warrant revocation of the designation.
    Therefore, I hereby determine that the designation of the Libyan 
Islamic Fighting Group as a foreign terrorist organization, pursuant to 
Section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended (8 
U.S.C. 1189), shall be revoked.
    This determination shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: November 30, 2015.
 John F. Kerry,
Secretary of State.
