DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Notification of United States Mint 2009 Presidential $1 Coin 25-
Coin Numismatic Rolls Pricing

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the price of the 2009 
Presidential $1 Coin 25-Coin Numismatic Rolls.
    The 2009 Presidential $1 Coin 25-Coin Numismatic Rolls, honoring 
Presidents William Henry Harrison, John Tyler, James K. Polk and 
Zachary Taylor, will be priced at $35.95 each. Rolls of Presidential $1 
Coins struck at both the United States Mint at Philadelphia and the 
United States Mint at Denver will be available.
    The first 2009 Presidential $1 Coin 25-Coin Numismatic Roll, 
honoring President William Henry Harrison, will be offered for sale on 
February 19, 2009.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B.B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 Ninth Street, NW.; 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.
