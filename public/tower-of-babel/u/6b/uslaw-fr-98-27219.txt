SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[License No. 09/09-5382]


South Bay Capital Corporation; Notice of Surrender of License

    Notice is hereby given that South Bay Capital Corporation, 5325 E. 
Pacific Coasts Highway, Long Beach, CA 90804, has surrendered its 
license to operate as a small business investment company under the 
Business Investment Act of 1958, as amended (the Act). South Bay 
Capital Corporation was licensed by the Small Business Administration 
on October 25, 1989.
    Under the authority vested by the Act and pursuant to the 
regulations promulgated thereunder, the surrender was effective as of 
September 24, 1998, and accordingly, all rights, privileges, and 
franchises derived therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Companies)

    Dated: October 2, 1998.
Don A. Christensen,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
