COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Minnesota Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Minnesota Advisory 
Committee. The meeting scheduled for Thursday, July 28, 2022, at 11 
a.m. (CT) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of 
Monday, June 13, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-12597, in the first and second 
columns of page 35723.
