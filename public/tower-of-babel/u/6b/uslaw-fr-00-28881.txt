Radio Broadcasting Services; Butte Falls, OR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Butte Falls Radio, allots 
Channel 225A to Butte Falls, OR, as the community's first local aural 
service. See 65 FR 34997, June 1, 2000. Channel 225A is allotted 
instead of the originally proposed Channel 255A in order to avoid a 
short-spacing to the proposed allotment of Channel 255A at Hornbrook, 
CA, in MM Docket No. 00-73. Channel 225A can be allotted to Butte Falls 
in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements with a site restriction of 3.4 kilometers (2.1 miles) 
northwest, at coordinates 42-33-33 NL; 122-36-13 WL, to avoid a short-
spacing to Stations KLAD-FM, Channel 223C, Klamath Falls, OR, and KKNU, 
Channel 226C, Springfield, Oregon. A filing window for Channel 225A at 
Butte Falls will not be opened at this time. Instead, the issue of 
opening a filing window for this channel will be addressed by the 
Commission in a subsequent order.

DATES: Effective December 11, 2000.
