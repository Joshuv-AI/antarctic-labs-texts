DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Neurological Sciences and Disorders B, June 21, 1999, 7:30 a.m. to June 
22, 1999, 5 p.m., Holiday Inn Bethesda, 8120 Wisconsin Avenue, 
Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on May 
6, 1999, 64 FR 24411.
    The NSDB meeting will now be held June 21-23, 1999 from 8:30 a.m. 
to 5 p.m. each day at the Holiday Inn Bethesda, 8120 Wisconsin Avenue, 
Bethesda, MD, 20814. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 14, 1999.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, National Institutes of Health.
