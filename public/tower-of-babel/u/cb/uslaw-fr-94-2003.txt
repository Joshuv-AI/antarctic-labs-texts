South Carolina Electric & Gas Co.; Duke Power Co.; Correction to 
Notice of Intent To Prepare an Environmental Assessment and Conduct 
Public Scoping Meetings and Site Visit

    Issued January 25, 1994.
    The site visit and scoping meetings scheduled for February 1, and 
2, 1994, respectively, are canceled. [See Notice issued December 28, 
1993, 59 FR 302 (January 4, 1994)].
    The site visit and scoping meetings will be rescheduled and a 
notice will be issued.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
