SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Notice of Cancellation; Clarksburg District Advisory Council 
Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Clarksburg District 
Advisory Council will hold a public meeting on Thursday, August 15, 
1996 at 10:00 a.m. at Eat'N Park Restaurant, 100 Tolley Street, 
Bridgeport, West Virginia, to discuss matters as may be presented by 
members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present.
    A new date for the meeting will be announced and the public will be 
notified of date, time and place of meeting and notice will be 
published in the Federal Register at least 30 days prior to the meeting 
in accordance with the regulations.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Thomas Tolan, Acting 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 168 West Main 
Street, Clarksburg, West Virginia 26301, (304) 623-5631.

    Dated: August 1, 1996.
Michael P. Novelli,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
