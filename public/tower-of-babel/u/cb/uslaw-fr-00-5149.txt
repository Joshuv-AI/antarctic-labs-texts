Radio Broadcasting Services; Paxton, Overton, Hershey, Sutherland 
and Ravenna, NE

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Mountain West Broadcasting, 
allots Channel 228C1 to Paxton, NE, Channel 257C2 to Overton, NE, 
Channel 297C1 to Hershey, NE, Channel 264C1 to Sutherland, NE, and 
276C2 to Ravenna, NE, as each community's first local aural service. 
Each of the allotments can be made in compliance with the Commission's 
minimum distance separation requirements without the imposition of a 
site restriction. The allotment coordinates are: Paxton, NE, 41-06-48 
NL; 101-21-12 WL; Overton, NE, 40-44-24 NL; 99-42-06 WL; Hershey, NE, 
41-09-30 NL; 101-00-00 WL; Sutherland, NE, 41-09-30 NL; 101-07-36 WL; 
Ravenna, NE, 41-01-36 NL; 98-54-48 WL. See 64 FR 27132, May 25, 1999, 
64 FR 28424, May 26, 1999, 64 FR 28425, May 26, 1999, and 64 FR 29978, 
June 4, 1999. A filing window for these channels will not be opened at 
this time. Instead, the issue of opening a filing window for these 
channels will be addressed by the Commission in a subsequent order.

DATES: Effective April 3, 2000.
