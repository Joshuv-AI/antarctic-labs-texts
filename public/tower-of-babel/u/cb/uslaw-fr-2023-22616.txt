COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the West Virginia Advisory Committee 
to the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights

AGENCY: U.S. Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice of public meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
rules and regulations of the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights 
(Commission) and the Federal Advisory Committee Act, that the West 
Virginia Advisory Committee (Committee) to the U.S. Commission on Civil 
Rights will hold meetings via Zoom. The purpose of these meetings is to 
discuss, plan, and vote, as needed, on matters related to the 
Committee's project on the civil rights impact of WV public school 
disciplinary policies, practices and procedures on students of color, 
students with disabilities and LGBTA+ students.
