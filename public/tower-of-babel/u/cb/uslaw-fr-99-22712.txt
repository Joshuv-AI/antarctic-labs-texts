NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management; Renewal

    The NSF management official having responsibility for the NSB 
Public Service Award Committee (#5195) has determined that renewing 
this charter for another two years is necessary and in the public 
interest in connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the 
Director, National Science Foundaton (NSF), by 42 USC 1861 et seq. This 
determination follows consultation with the Committee Management 
Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Authority for this Committee will expire on September 4, 2001, 
unless it is renewed. For more information, please contact Karen York, 
NSF, at (703) 306-1182.

    Dated: August 26, 1999.
Karen J. York,
Committee Management Officer.
