ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

40 CFR Part 52

[IL117-2; FRL 5886-9]


Approval and Promulgation of Implementation Plans; Illinois

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Final rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On July 14, 1997 (62 FR 37494), the EPA approved Illinois' 
July 14, 1997, submittal of Rate-of-Progress plans to reduce Volatile 
Organic Compounds emissions in the Chicago

[[Page 46447]]

and Metro-East St. Louis areas by 15 percent (%) by November 15, 1996, 
contingency plans to reduce VOC emissions by an additional 3% beyond 
the ROP plans, and transportation control measures for the Metro-East 
St. Louis area as revisions to the Illinois State Implementation Plan 
(SIP). The EPA is withdrawing this final rule due to receipt of adverse 
comments. In a subsequent final rule EPA will summarize and respond to 
the comments received and announce final rulemaking action on these 
requested Illinois SIP revisions.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 3, 1997.

ADDRESSES: Copies of the documents relevant to this action are
