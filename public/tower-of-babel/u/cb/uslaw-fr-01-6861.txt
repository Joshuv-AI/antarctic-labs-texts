SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region I Hartford District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Region I Hartford, 
Connecticut District Advisory Council, will hold a public meeting on 
Monday, April 2, 2001, at 8:30 a.m. at the Connecticut District Office, 
330 Main Street, Hartford, Connecticut 06106, to discuss such matters 
as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business 
Administration or others present. For further information, write or 
call Ms. Marie Record, District Director, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, 330 Main Street, Hartford, Connecticut, telephone (860) 
240-4700.

Nancyellen Gentile,
Committee Management Officer.
