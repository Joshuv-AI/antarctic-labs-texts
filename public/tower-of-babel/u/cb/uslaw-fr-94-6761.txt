NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION
[Docket Nos. 50-413 and 50-414]

 

Duke Power Company; Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications 
and Amendments to Operating Licenses Involving no Significant Hazards 
Consideration

    In the Federal Register for Wednesday, March 2, 1994, beginning on 
page 10005, please make the following correction to the BiWeekly Notice 
for the Catawba Nuclear Station, Units 1 and 2:
    Under the ``Description of amendment request:,'' the sentence ``* * 
*  High Relative Humidity (70%) * * * '' should read ``* * * 
High Relative Humidity (>70%) * * *''

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 16th day of March 1994.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Robert E. Martin,
Project Manager, Project Directorate II-3, Division of Reactor 
Projects--I/II, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
