DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Home Unity Federal Savings and Loan Association, Lafayette Hill, 
PA; Replacement of Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Subdivision (C) of section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator for Home Unity Federal Savings and Loan 
Association, Lafayette Hill, Pennsylvania (``Association''), with the 
Resolution Trust Corporation as sole Receiver for the Association on 
August 27, 1993.

    Dated: February 3, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Deborah F. Silberman,
Assistant Chief Counsel.
