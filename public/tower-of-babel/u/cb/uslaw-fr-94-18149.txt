DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice lists the membership to the Departmental Offices' 
Performance Review Board (PRB) and supersedes the list published in 58 
FR 156 dated August 16, 1993, in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4). 
The purpose of the PRB is to review the performance of member of the 
Senior Executive Service and make recommendations regarding performance 
ratings, performance awards, and other personnel actions.

    The name and titles of the PRB members are as follows:
