General Electric Company Industrial Systems, Tell City, IN; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
Section 250(a), Subchapter D, Chapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 U.S.C. 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
September 7, 1999 in response to a petition filed on behalf of workers 
at General Electric Company, Industrial Systems, Tell City, IN.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (NAFTA-3377). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 22nd day of December, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
