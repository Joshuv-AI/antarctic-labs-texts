DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Siuslaw Resource Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Siuslaw Resource Advisory Committee will meet in Florence, 
OR. The purpose of the meeting is to Review RAC FY07 Business, 
Information Share, Public Forum and 2007 Project Review/
Recommendations.

DATES: The meeting will be held June 8, 2006, beginning at 9:30 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Community Baptist Church, 
4590 Highway 101, Florence Oregon 97439.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Linda Stanley, Community Development 
Specialist, Siuslaw National Forest, 541/928-7085 or write to Forest 
Supervisor, Siuslaw national Forest, P.O. Box 1148, Corvallis, OR 
97339.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: A public input period will begin before 2007 
project review.
