FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 93-319; RM-8404]

 

Radio Broadcasting Services; Omak, WA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Omak Community Broadcasters, 
allots Channel 282C2 at Omak, Washington, as that community's second 
local FM transmission service. See 59 FR 2344, January 14, 1994. 
Channel 282C2 can be allotted to Omak in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements with a site 
restriction of 16.5 kilometers (10.2 miles) south to avoid short-
spacings to vacant Channel 281B, Trail, British Columbia and Station 
KAFE, Channel 282C2, Bellingham, Washington. The coordinates for 
channel 282C2 at Omak are north Latitude 48-15-44 and West Longitude 
119-31-58. Since Omak is located within 320 kilometers (200 miles) of 
the U.S.-Canadian border, concurrence of the Canadian government has 
been obtained. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective Dec. 5, 1994. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 282C2 at Omak, Washington, will open on Dec. 
5, 1994, and close on Jan. 5, 1995.
