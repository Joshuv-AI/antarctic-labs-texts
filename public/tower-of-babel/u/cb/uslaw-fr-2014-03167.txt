BROADCASTING BOARD OF GOVERNORS


Sunshine Act Meeting; Canceled

DATE AND TIME:  Monday, February 10, 2014, 11:30 a.m. EST

PLACE:  Broadcasting Board of Governors, Cohen Building, Room 3321, 330 
Independence Ave. SW., Washington, DC 20237.

SUBJECT:  Cancellation of Special Meeting of the Broadcasting Board of 
Governors
SUMMARY: The Broadcasting Board of Governors (BBG) has canceled the 
telephonic meeting to discuss and approve the agency's Operating Plan 
for Fiscal Year 2014.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION:  Persons interested in obtaining 
more information should contact Paul Kollmer-Dorsey at (202) 203-4545.

Paul Kollmer-Dorsey,
General Counsel.
