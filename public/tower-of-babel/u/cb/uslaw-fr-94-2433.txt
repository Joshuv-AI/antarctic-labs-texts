DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
[T.D. 94-12]

 

Recordation of Trade Name: ``Superior Seedless Grape Co.''

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of recordation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 27, 1993, a notice of application for the 
recordation under section 42 of the Act of July 5, 1946, as amended (15 
U.S.C. 1124), of the trade name ``Superior Seedless Grape Co.,'' was 
published in the Federal Register (58 FR 57894). The notice advised 
that before final action was taken on the application, consideration 
would be given to any relevant data, views, or arguments submitted in 
writing by any person in opposition to the recordation and received not 
later than December 27, 1993. No responses were received in opposition 
to the notice. Accordingly, as provided in Sec. 133.14, Customs 
Regulations (19 CFR 133.14), the name ``Superior Seedless Grape Co.,'' 
is recorded as the trade name used by Sun World, Inc., a corporation 
organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, located at P. O. Box 
1028, 53-990 Enterprise Way, Coachella, California 92223.
    The trade name is used in connection with table grapes.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 3, 1994.
