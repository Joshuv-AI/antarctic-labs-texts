NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Notice of Change in Subject and Time of Meeting

    The National Credit Union Administration Board determined that its 
business required the deletion of the following item from the 
previously announced closed meeting (Federal Register, Vol. 67, No. 
199, pp. 63682-63683, October 15, 2002) scheduled for Thursday, October 
17, 2002.

1. Administrative Action under Part 702 of NCUA's Rules and 
Regulations. Closed pursuant to Exemptions (8), (9)(A)(ii) and (9)(B).

    The Board voted unanimously that this item be removed from the 
closed agenda.
    The previously announced item were:
    1. Administrative Action under Part 702 of NCUA's Rules and 
Regulations. Closed pursuant to Exemptions (8), (9)(A)(ii) and (9)(B).
    2. One (1) Personnel Matter. Closed pursuant to Exemptions (2) and 
(6).
    In addition, the time of the previously announced closed Board 
meeting was changed from 11:30 a.m. on October 17, 2002 to 9:15 a.m. on 
the same date.
    For Further Information Contact: Becky Baker, Secretary of the 
Board, Telephone (703) 518-6304.

Becky Baker,
Secretary of the Board.
