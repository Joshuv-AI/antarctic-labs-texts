National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Cardiovascular and Sleep Epidemiology Study Section, February 16, 2006, 
8 a.m. to February 17, 2006, 5 p.m., Holiday Inn Select Bethesda, 8120 
Wisconsin Ave, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal 
Register on January 27, 2006, 71 FR 4600-4603.

Road, Bethesda, MD 20814. The meeting dates and time remain the same. 
The meeting is closed to the public.
