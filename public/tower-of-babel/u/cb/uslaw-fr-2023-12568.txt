National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, NCI Program Project (P01) 
Review SEP-E, July 19, 2023, 12:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., National Cancer 
Institute Shady Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Room 7W126, 
Rockville, Maryland 20850 which was published in the Federal Register 
on June 06, 2023, FR Doc 2023-11973, 88 FR 37079.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting start time from 
12:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. The meeting end time, date, and location will 
stay the same. The meeting is closed to public.
