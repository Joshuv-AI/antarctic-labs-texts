RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given that the Railroad Retirement Board will hold 
a meeting on September 23, 1997, 1:30 p.m., at the Board's meeting room 
on the 8th floor of its headquarters building, 844 North Rush Street, 
Chicago, Illinois, 60611. The agenda for this meeting follows:

Portion Open to the Public:
    (1) Proposed Flexitime/Variable Workweek Changes
    (2) Federal Ban on Smoking on Federal Property
    (3) Employee Service--Environmental Contractors with CSX 
Transportation Company
    (4) Coverage Determination--Pioneer Railroad Equipment Company, 
Ltd.
    (5) Regulations--Part 230 (Reduction and Non-Payment of Annuities 
by Reason of Work)
    (6) Local Area Network (LAN) Proposal for the Board Offices
    (7) Year 2000 Issues
    (8) Labor Member Truth in Budgeting Status Report
Portion Closed to the Public:
    (A) Last Person Employment Deductions for Dual Annuitants (Marie A. 
Fese and Frank J. Fese)

    The person to contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, 
Secretary to the Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: September 15, 1997.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
