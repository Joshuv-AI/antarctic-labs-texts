COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled for 18 
September 1997 at 10:00 AM in the Commission's offices at the Pension 
Building, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, N.W., Washington, 
D.C. 20001. The meeting will focus on a variety of projects affecting 
the appearance of the city.

[[Page 47651]]

    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200. 
Individuals requiring sign language interpretation for the hearing 
impaired should contact the Secretary at least 10 days before the 
meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, D.C. 29 August 1997.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
