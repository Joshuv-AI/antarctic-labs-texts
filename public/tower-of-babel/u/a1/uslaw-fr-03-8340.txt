Motorola, Inc., Deer Park, IL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 19, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Motorola, Inc., Deer Park, Illinois.
    The Department has amended an active certification for workers of 
Motorola, Inc., Global Telecom Solutions Sector (GTSS) formerly Network 
Solutions Sector (NSS) (TA-W-40,501), to include the petitioning group 
of workers.
    Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 17th day of March 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
