FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

[FMC-2025-0011]


Executive Order (E.O.) 14294 (Fighting Overcriminalization in 
Federal Regulations)

AGENCY: Federal Maritime Commission.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On May 9, 2025, the President signed Executive Order (E.O.) 14294, 
Fighting Overcriminalization in Federal Regulations. Section 7 of the 
Executive Order requires each agency to publish guidance in the Federal 
Register by June 23, 2025, that describes the agency's plan to address 
criminally liable regulatory offenses. None of the statutes or 
regulations that the Federal Maritime Commission administers carry 
criminal penalties for violations.

    By the Commission.
David Eng,
Secretary.
