Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Refugee Unaccompanied Minor Placement Report & Minor 
Progress Reports; ORR-3 and ORR-4.
    OMB No.: 0970-0034.
    Description: The two reports collect information necessary to 
administer the Unaccompanied Refugee Minor (URM) program. The ORR-3 
(Placement Report) is submitted to the Office of Refugee Resettlement 
(ORR) by the State agency at initial placement within 30 days of the 
placement, and whenever there is a change in the child's status, 
including termination from the program, within 60 days of the change or 
closure of the case. The ORR-4 (Outcomes Report) is submitted along 
with the initial ORR-3 placement report and again within approximately 
12 months of the initial placement and each subsequent 12 months to 
record outcomes of the child's progress toward the goals listed in the 
child's case plan and particularly for youth 17 years of age and above 
related to independent living and/or educational plans.
    Respondents: State governments.
