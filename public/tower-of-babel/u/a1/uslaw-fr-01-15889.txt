Availability of Reregistration Eligibility Decision Document for 
Comment; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  In the Federal Register of May 23, 2001 (66 FR 28469) (FRL-
6775-9), EPA announced the availability and start of a 60-day public 
comment period on the Reregistration Eligibility Decision document for 
the pesticide active ingredient triallate. On page 28469, second 
column, under the DATES caption, the date provided for the receipt of 
comments was inadvertently listed as May 20, 2001. The correct date is 
July 23, 2001. This notice announces the correct comment period.

DATES: Comments, identified by docket control number OPP-34226A, must 
be received on or before July 23, 2001.

ADDRESSES: For detailed instructions concerning the submission of 
comments, refer to Unit I.C. of the Federal Register of May 23, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: By mail: Carol Stangel, Special Review
