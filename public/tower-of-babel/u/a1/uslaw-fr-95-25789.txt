NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD

Managing Human Fatigue in Transportation: A Multimodal Symposium
    A multimodal symposium on fatigue in transportation, its effects on 
safety, and fatigue countermeasures co-sponsored by the National 
Transportation Safety Board and NASA Ames Research Center will be held 
on November 1 and 2, 1995, at the Sheraton Premiere Hotel, Tysons 
Corner, Virginia (near Dulles Airport). For more information, contact 
the Office of Public Affairs, Washington, D.C. 20594, telephone (202) 
382-0660.

    Dated: October 12, 1995.
Bea Hardesty,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
