Prohibition on the Transportation of Devices Designed as Chemical 
Oxygen Generators as Cargo in Aircraft; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.


[[Page 62977]]


ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM); correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the NPRM published in 
the Federal Register (63 FR 45912) on August 27, 1998. The NPRM 
proposes to ban, in certain domestic operations, the transportation of 
devices designed to chemically generate oxygen, including devices that 
have been discharged and newly manufactured devices that have not yet 
been charged for the generation of oxygen, with limited exceptions.
