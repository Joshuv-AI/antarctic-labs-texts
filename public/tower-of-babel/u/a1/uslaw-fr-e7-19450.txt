DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration


Center for Substance Abuse Treatment; Notice of Meeting

    Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
meeting of the Center for Substance Abuse Treatment (CSAT) National 
Advisory Council on October 17, 2007.
    The meeting is open and will include discussion of the Center's 
policy issues, and current administrative, legislative, and program 
developments.
    Attendance by the public will be limited to space available. Public 
comments are welcome. Please communicate with the CSAT Council 
Executive Secretary, Ms. Cynthia Graham (see contact information 
below), to make arrangements to attend, comment or to request special 
accommodations for persons with disabilities.
    Substantive program information, a summary of the meeting, and a 
roster of Council members may be obtained as soon as possible after the 
meeting, either by accessing the SAMHSA Committee Web site, 
www.nac.samhsa.gov/CSAT/csatnac.aspx, or by contacting Ms. Graham. The 
transcript of the meeting will also be available on the SAMHSA 
Committee Web site within three weeks after the meeting.

    Committee Name: Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services 
Administration, CSAT National Advisory Council.
    Date/Time/Type: October 17, 2007, from 8:30 a.m.-5 p.m.: Open.
    Place: 1 Choke Cherry Road, Sugarloaf and Seneca Conference 
Rooms, Rockville, Maryland 20857.
    Contact: Cynthia Graham, M.S., Executive Secretary, SAMHSA/CSAT 
National Advisory Council, 1 Choke Cherry Road, Room 5-1036, 
Rockville, MD 20857, Telephone: (240) 276-1692, Fax: (240) 276-1690,
