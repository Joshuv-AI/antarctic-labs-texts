Caribbean Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and

ACTION: Notice of public hybrid meeting (in-person/virtual).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council (CFMC) will hold the 
184th public hybrid meeting to address the items contained in the 
tentative agenda included in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

DATES: The 184th CFMC public hybrid meeting will be held on April 23, 
2024, from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. A closed session will be held from 4:45 
p.m. to 5:30 p.m., to discuss personnel matters, and on April 24, 2024, 
from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. AST.
