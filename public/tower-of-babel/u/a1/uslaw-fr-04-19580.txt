Notice of Cancellation of Customs Broker National Permit

Homeland Security.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

(19 U.S.C. 1641) and the Customs Regulations (19 CFR 111.51), the 
following Customs broker national permits are canceled without 
prejudice.
