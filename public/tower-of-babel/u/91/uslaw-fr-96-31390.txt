DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP96-348-000]


Panhandle Eastern Pipe Line Corporation; Notice of Technical 
Conference

December 5, 1996.
    In the Commission's order issued on September 30, 1996, in the 
above-captioned proceeding, the Commission held that the filing raises 
certain issues for which a technical conference is to be convened.
    The conference to address the issues has been scheduled for 
Thursday, December 19, 1996, at 10:00 a.m. in a room to be designated 
at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
