Revocation of Customs Broker Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Customs broker license revocations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to section 641 of the Tariff Act of 1930 as amended 
(19 USC 1641) and the Customs Regulations (19 CFR 111), the following 
Customs broker licenses are revoked. Please be aware that some of these 
entities may continue to provide broker services under another valid 
brokerage license. Because previous publication of some records cannot 
be readily verified, the records are now being published to ensure 
Customs compliance with administrative requirements.
