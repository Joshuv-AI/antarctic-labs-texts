DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force

32 CFR Part 835


Support of Nongovernmental Test and Evaluation

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, Department of Defense.

ACTION: Final rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Air Force is amending Title 32, Chapter 
VII of the CFR by removing Part 835, Support of Nongovernmental Test 
and Evaluation. The rule is removed since the source document, AFR 80-
19, was rescinded.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 6, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Patsy Conner, Air Force Federal 
Register Liaison Officer, SAF/AAIQ, 1610 Air Force Pentagon, 
Washington, DC 20330-1610.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

List of Subjects in 32 CFR Part 835

    Federal buildings and facilities, Research.

    Authority: 10 U.S.C. 8013

PART 835--[REMOVED]

    Accordingly, 32 CFR, Chapter VII, is amended by removing Part 835.
Patsy J. Conner,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
