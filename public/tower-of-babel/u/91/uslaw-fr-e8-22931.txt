FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Deletion of Agenda Items from September 25, 2008 Open Meeting

September 25, 2008.
    The following items have been deleted from the list of Agenda items 
scheduled for consideration at the September 25, 2008, Open Meeting and 
previously listed in the Commission's Notice of September 18, 2008, 73 
FR 55082, September 24, 2008. Items 1 through 2, items 4 through 14, 
and item 16 have been adopted by the Commission.
