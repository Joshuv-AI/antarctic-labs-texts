DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Behavior 
and Social Science of Aging Review Committee, October 03, 2013, 04:30 
p.m. to October 04, 2013, 02:00 p.m., Doubletree Hotel Bethesda, 8120 
Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 11, 2013, 78 FR 55752.
    The meeting has been changed to December 10, 2013, 03:00 p.m. to 
07:00 p.m. and December 11, 2013, 09:00 a.m. to 07:00 p.m. This meeting 
is now a teleconference. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 25, 2013.
David Clary,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
