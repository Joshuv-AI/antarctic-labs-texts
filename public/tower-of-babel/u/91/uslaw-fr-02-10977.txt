DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the PBA Study Panel Chairs. The purpose of the 
meeting is to allow the SAB and study leadership to gather information 
from CNN related to prediction and news gathering methods. Because 
information to be discussed is sensitive and proprietary, this meeting 
will be closed to the public.

DATES: 22 May 2002, 0930-1600L.

ADDRESSES: CNN, One CNN Center, Atlanta GA 30303.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Colonel Marian Alexander, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
