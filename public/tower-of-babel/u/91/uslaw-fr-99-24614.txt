DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Government-Owned Invention for 
Licensing

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention U.S. Patent Number 5,752,713 entitled 
Discriminate Reduction Data Processing is assigned to the United States 
Government as represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available 
for licensing by the Department of the Navy.

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the patent cited should be directed 
to Naval Surface Warfare Center, Carderock Division, Code 0117, 9500 
MacArthur Blvd, West Bethesda, MD 20817-5700.
