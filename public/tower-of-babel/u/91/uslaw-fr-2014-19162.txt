Radio Broadcasting Services; Haynesville, Louisiana

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of SSR Communications, 
Inc., allots Channel 286A at Haynesville, Louisiana, as a ``backfill'' 
allotment to prevent the removal of the community's potential first 
local service that accommodates the ``hybrid'' application for Station 
KIMW, Channel 288A from Haynesville, Louisiana, to Heflin, Louisiana. A 
staff engineering analysis indicates that Channel 286A can be allotted 
to Haynesville consistent with the minimum distance separation 
requirements of the Commission's rules with a site restriction 4.6 
kilometers (2.9 miles) south of the community. The reference 
coordinates are 33-00-12 NL and 93-08-19 WL.

DATES: Effective September 8, 2014.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth 
Street SW., Washington, DC 20554.
