DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


State Energy Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of open teleconference correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On June 27, 2007, the Department of Energy published a notice of 
open teleconference announcing a teleconference of the State Energy 
Advisory Board, 72 FR 35227. In that notice, the meeting was scheduled 
for July 19, 2007. Today's notice is announcing that the meeting date 
will be July 18, 2007.

    Issued in Washington, DC on July 2, 2007.
Rachel M. Samuel,
Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
