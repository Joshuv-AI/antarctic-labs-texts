DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Navy

 

Board of Visitors to the United States Naval Academy; Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 
U.S.C. App. 2), notice is hereby given that the Board of Visitors to 
the United States Naval Academy will meet on September 26, 1994, Alumni 
Hall, at 8:30 a.m. The session will be open to the public.
    The purpose of the meeting is to make such inquiry as the Board 
shall deem necessary into the state of morale and discipline, the 
curriculum, instruction, physical equipment, fiscal affairs, and 
academic methods of the Naval Academy.
    For further information concerning this meeting contact: LCDR, 
Timothy A. Batzler, U.S. Navy, Executive Secretary to the Board of 
Visitors, Office of the Superintendent, United States Naval Academy, 
Annapolis, MD 21402-5000, Telephone: (410) 293-1503.

    Dated: September 9, 1994
L. R. McNees
LCDR, JAGC, USN, Federal Register Liaison Officer.
