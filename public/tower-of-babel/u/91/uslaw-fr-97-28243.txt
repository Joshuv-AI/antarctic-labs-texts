The Solid Surface Craftsman, Schenectady, New York; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 11, 1997 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on July 25, 1997 on behalf of workers at The Solid Surface 
Craftsman, Schenectady, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 10th day of October, 1997.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
