SUMMARY: In FR Doc. 95-11147 in the Federal Register of May 10, 1995, 
at page 24855, the following correction is made: in the second 
paragraph under ``Supplementary Information'' change ``The company has 
requested a 1-year renewal of the temporary tolerances'' to read `` The 
company has requested a 2-year renewal of the temporary tolerances.''
