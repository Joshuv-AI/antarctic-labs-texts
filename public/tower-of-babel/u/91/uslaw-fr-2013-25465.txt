DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, 
October 10, 2013, 12:30 p.m. to October 10, 2013, 5:00 p.m., NIEHS/
National Institutes of Health, Keystone Building, 530 Davis Drive, 
Research Triangle Park, NC 27709 which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 16, 2013, 78 FR 56902.
    This meeting, originally scheduled for October 10, 2013, will be 
held on November 14, 2013 from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. The meeting is closed 
to the public.

    Dated: October 22, 2013.
Carolyn Baum,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
