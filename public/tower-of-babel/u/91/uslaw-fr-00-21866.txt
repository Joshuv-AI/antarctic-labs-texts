Montana Off-Road Vehicle Designation

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Department of the Interior.

ACTION: Notice to limit off-road vehicle use on public lands.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that motorized vehicle use is limited 
to designated roads on public lands within the following block 
management areas. This will be in effect during the hunting season as 
established by the Montana Department of Fish, Wildlife, and Parks.

Designation and Location

    All motorized vehicles will be limited to designated open roads on 
public lands during hunting season.
