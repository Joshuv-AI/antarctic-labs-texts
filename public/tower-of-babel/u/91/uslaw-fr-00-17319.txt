Johnson Controls, Incorporated, Goshen, IN; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 15, 2000, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Johnson Controls, 
Incorporated, Goshen, Indiana.
    The company official and union official submitting the petition 
have requested that the petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 12th day of June, 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
