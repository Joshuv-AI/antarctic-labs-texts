DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Drug Enforcement Administration


Importer of Controlled Substances; Notice of Application; 
Correction

    By Notice dated November 21, 2006, and published in the Federal 
Register on December 1, 2006, (71 FR 69590), Notice of Application, 
Hospira, Inc., 1776 North Centennial Drive, McPherson, Kansas 67460-
1247, paragraph three should be corrected to read as follows: The 
company plans to import Remifentanil for use in dosage form 
manufacturing.

    Dated: January 5, 2007.
Joseph T. Rannazzisi,
Deputy Assistant Administrator, Office of Diversion Control, Drug 
Enforcement Administration.
