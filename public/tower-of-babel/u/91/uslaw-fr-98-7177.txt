DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the meeting of the 
National Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, March 23-24, 1998, 
Double Tree Hotel, Rockville, Maryland, which was published in the 
Federal Register on February 9, 1998 (63 FR 6574).
    The meeting was cancelled due to a scheduling conflict.

    Dated: March 12, 1998.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
