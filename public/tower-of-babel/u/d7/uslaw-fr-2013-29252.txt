Petition For Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2013-0795.
    Petitioner: Cirrus Design Corporation.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR 23.177(b).
    Description of Relief Sought: Petitioner requests relief from 
static directional and lateral stability requirements for its Cirrus 
model SR20 aircraft. The aircraft were manufactured with a wing design 
that required a rudder aileron interconnect (RAI) to comply with 
requirements of section 23.177(b); however, flight tests with use of a 
roll trim system in lieu of the RAI show a level of safety at least 
equal to the intent of the static lateral stability requirements of 
that section.
