DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

International Trade Administration


Legal Services Trade Mission to China

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Department of Commerce, International Trade 
Administration, U.S. and Foreign Commercial Service is amending the 
Notice published at 78 FR 20893, April 8, 2013, regarding the 
Executive-Led Legal Services Trade Mission to China scheduled for 
September 16-18, 2013, to postpone the mission until further notice.
