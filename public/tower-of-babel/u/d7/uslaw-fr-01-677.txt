FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-02, MM Docket No. 00-178, RM-9914]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Charlotte, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Charlotte-Mecklenburg Public 
Broadcasting Authority, licensee of noncommercial educational station 
WTVI-TV, NTSC channel * 42, substitutes DTV channel * 11 for station 
WTVI-TV's assigned DTV channel * 24 at Charlotte, North Carolina. See 
65 FR 59388, October 5, 2000. DTV channel * 11 can be allotted to 
Charlotte in compliance with the principle community coverage 
requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates (35-17-14 N. 
and 80-41-45 W.) with a power of 2.0, HAAT of 387 meters and with a DTV 
service population of 1747 thousand. With is action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

DATES: Effective February 20, 2001.
