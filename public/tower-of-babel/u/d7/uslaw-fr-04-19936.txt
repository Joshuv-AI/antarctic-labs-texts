Certification of Aircraft and Airmen for the Operation of Light-
Sport Aircraft; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is correcting an inadvertent error in a correction 
published in the Federal Register on Wednesday, August 18, 2004 (69 FR 
51162). The correction related to a final regulation published in the 
Federal Register of Tuesday, July 27, 2004 (69 FR 44772) on the 
certification of aircraft and airmen for the operation of light-sport 
aircraft.

DATES: The regulation is effective September 1, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Susan Gardner, Flight Standards 
Service, General Aviation and Commercial Division (AFS-800), Federal 
Aviation Administration, 800 Independence Ave., SW., Washington, DC 
20591; telephone 907-271-2034, or 202-267-8212.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. 04-18904 appearing on page 51162 
in the Federal Register of Wednesday, August 18, 2004, which corrected 
an amendment to Sec.  91.319, in the DATES caption, ``The regulation is 
effective September 4, 2004'' is corrected to read ``The regulation is 
effective September 1, 2004.''

    Issued in Washington, DC, on August 27, 2004.
