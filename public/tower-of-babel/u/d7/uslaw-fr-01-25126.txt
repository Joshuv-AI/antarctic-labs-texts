SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

Federal Register Citation of Previous Announcement: [66 FR 49727, 
September 28, 2001].

Status: Open Meeting.

Place: 450 Fifth Street, NW., Washington, DC.

Date and Time of Previously Announced Meeting: Wednesday, October 3, 
2001 at 10 a.m.

Change in the Meeting: Deletion of Item.
    The following item will not be considered at the open meeting 
scheduled for Wednesday, October 3, 2001: Final Rule Amendments to 
Broker-Dealer Books and Records Rules 17a-3 and 17a-4 Under the 
Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact:
    The Office of the Secretary at (202) 942-7070.

    Dated: October 2, 2001.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
