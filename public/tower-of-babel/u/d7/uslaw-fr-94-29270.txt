License Cancellation

AGENCY: Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: General notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to 19 CFR 111.51(a), the 
following Customs broker license has been cancelled without prejudice, 
effective May 2, 1994. This license was issued in the Los Angeles 
District.

Jack Matsukawa--license no. 4131
