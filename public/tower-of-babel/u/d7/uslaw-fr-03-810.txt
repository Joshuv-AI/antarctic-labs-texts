Radio Broadcasting Services; Johannesburg and Edwards, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Amaturo Group of Los Angeles, Ltd., this 
document dismisses a petition for reconsideration filed by Amaturo 
Group of Los Angeles, Ltd., seeking reconsideration of the Report and 
Order in this proceeding. See 65 FR 53639, September 5, 2000. This 
petition for reconsideration was opposed by Adelman Communications, 
Inc, and petitioner filed a response. Petitioner subsequently filed a 
request to withdraw the petition for reconsideration, contingent on the 
finality of the Report and Order in MM Docket No. 99-329. This docket 
is now final. All parties filed affidavits attesting to the fact that 
it no consideration was promised or paid.

(202) 418-2180.
