DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP96-199-000]


Mississippi River Transmission Corporation; Notice of Informal 
Settlement Conference

March 12, 1997.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference will be convened 
in this proceeding on March 20, 1997, at 10:00 a.m., at the offices of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, DC, for the purposes of exploring the possible settlement 
of the referenced docket.
    Any party, as defined by 18 CFR 385.102(c) or any participant, as 
defined by 18 CFR 385.102(b) is invited to attend. Persons wishing to 
become a party must move to intervene and receive intervenor status 
pursuant to the Commission's regulations (18 CFR 385.214).
    For additional information, contact Kathleen M. Dias at (202) 208-
0524 or Russell B. Mamone at (202) 208-0744.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
