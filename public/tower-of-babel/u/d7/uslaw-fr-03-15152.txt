DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 24-2003]


Foreign-Trade Zone 43--Battle Creek, MI; Application for Subzone, 
Perrigo Company (Pharmaceutical Products), Allegan and Muskegon 
Counties, MI; Correction

    The Federal Register notice (68 FR 27985-27986, 5/22/2003) 
describing the application by the City of Battle Creek, Michigan, 
grantee of FTZ 43, requesting special-purpose subzone status for the 
pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution facilities of Perrigo 
Company (Perrigo) at locations in Allegan and Muskegon Counties, 
Michigan, is corrected as follows:
    Paragraph 6 should read ``The closing period for their receipt is 
July 21, 2003.''

    Dated: June 6, 2003.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Executive Secretary.
