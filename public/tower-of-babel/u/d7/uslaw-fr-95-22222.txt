DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Immigration and Naturalization Service

Special Filing Instructions for ABC Class Members; Correction

AGENCY: Immigration and Naturalization Service, Justice.

ACTION: Notice; correction of form number.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Friday, July 7, 1995, the Immigration and Naturalization 
Service (the Service) published a notice at 60 FR 35424-35430, entitled 
``Special Filing Instructions for ABC Class Members.'' In that notice 
the Service provided special instructions for class members eligible 
for asylum benefits under the settlement agreement reached in American 
Baptist Churches v. Thornburgh, 760 F. Supp. 796 (N.D. Cal. 1991). That 
notice document 95-16771, erroneously referenced the Form I-855, ABC 
Change of Address Form.

    Accordingly, on page 35424, under ``Supplementary Information:'' 
the entry for ``Form I-855'' where it is listed in the second, third 
and fourth paragraphs, should have read ``Form M-426.''


    Dated: August 31, 1995.
Doris Meissner,
Commissioner, Immigration and Naturalization Service.
