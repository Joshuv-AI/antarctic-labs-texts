DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project Nos. 14179-000, 14194-000]


Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XLIV FFP Project 51, LLC; Notice 
Announcing Filing Priority for Preliminary Permit Applications

    On February 22, 2012, the Commission held a drawing to determine 
priority among competing preliminary permit applications with identical 
filing times. In the event that the Commission concludes that none of 
the applicants' plans are better adapted than the others to develop, 
conserve, and utilize in the public interest the water resources of the 
region at issue, the priority established by this drawing will serve as 
the tiebreaker. Based on the drawing, the order of priority is as 
follows:
    1. Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XLIV, Project No. 14179-000.
    2. FFP Project 51, LLC, Project No. 14194-000.

    Dated: February 22, 2012.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
