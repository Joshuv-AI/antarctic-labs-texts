DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Meeting of the Defense Policy Board; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On August 6, 2010, the Department of Defense (DoD) published a 
notice in the Federal Register (75 FR 47553) announcing a meeting of 
the Defense Policy Board. This document corrects the August 6 notice by 
changing the ``June 24, 2010'' meeting date to ``September 14, 2010.'' 
As discussed in the August 6 notice, the meeting is closed to the 
public.

DATES: The meeting will be held on September 13 (from 0730 hrs until 
1800 hrs) and on September 14, 2010 (from 0730 hrs until 1000 hrs).

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Pentagon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Ann Hansen, 703-571-9232.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of August 6, 2010, in FR Doc. 2010-19316, 
on page 47553, in the second column, in the first paragraph under the 
``SUMMARY'' caption, remove ``June 24, 2010'' and add in its place 
``September 14, 2010''.

    Dated: August 17, 2010.
Mitchell S. Bryman,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
