Atlantic Coast Financial Corporation, Waycross, GA; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on November 12, 2010, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of Atlantic Coast Federal, 
MHC and Atlantic Coast Bank, Waycross, Georgia, to convert to the stock 
form of organization. Copies of the application are available for 
inspection by appointment (phone number: 202-906-5922 or e-mail 
[email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, 
NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS Southeast Regional Office, 1475 
Peachtree Street, NE., Atlanta, Georgia 30309.

    Dated: November 16, 2010.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
