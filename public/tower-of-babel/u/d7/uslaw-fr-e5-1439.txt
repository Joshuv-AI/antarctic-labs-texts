DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-56,666]


Aim Nationalease Old Fort, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 2, 2005 in response to a petition 
filed on by a company official on behalf of

[[Page 16845]]

workers of Aim Nationalease, Old Fort, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of March, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
