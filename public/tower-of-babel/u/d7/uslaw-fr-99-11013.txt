Extension of Time Limits for Preliminary Results

    The Department of Commerce (the Department) received a request to 
conduct an administrative review of the antidumping duty order on Gray 
Portland Cement and Clinker from Mexico. On September 29, 1998, the 
Department initiated this administrative review covering the period 
August 1, 1997, through July 31, 1998.
    Because of the complexity of certain issues in this case, it is not 
practicable to complete this review within the time limits mandated by 
section 751(a)(3)(A) of the Act. Therefore, in accordance with that 
section, the Department is extending the time limits for the 
preliminary results to August 31, 1999 (see Memorandum from Richard 
Moreland to Robert LaRussa, Re: Extension of Preliminary Results). The 
Department intends to issue the final results of review 120 days after 
the publication of the preliminary results. This extension of the time 
limit is in accordance with section 751(a)(3)(A) of the Act.
