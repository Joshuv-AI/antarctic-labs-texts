EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY COMMISSION

29 CFR Part 1630


Regulations To Implement the Equal Employment Provisions of the 
Americans With Disabilities Act, as Amended

CFR Correction

    In Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 900 to 1899, 
revised as of July 1, 2011, on page 365, in Sec.  1630.2, in paragraph 
(o)(1)(ii), remove the words ``a qualified individual with a 
disability'' and add, in their place, ``an individual with a disability 
who is qualified''.
