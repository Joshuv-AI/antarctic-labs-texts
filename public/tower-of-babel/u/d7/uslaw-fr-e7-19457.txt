Agency Information Collection Activities; Announcement of Office 
of Management and Budget Approval; Human Tissue Intended for 
Transplantation

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing that a 
collection of information entitled ``Human Tissue Intended for 
Transplantation'' has been approved by the Office of Management and 
Budget (OMB) under the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jonna Capezzuto, Office of the Chief 
Information Officer (HFA-250), Food

[[Page 56362]]

and Drug Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, 301-
827-4659.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register of April 25, 2007 
(72 FR 20555), the agency announced that the proposed information 
collection had been submitted to OMB for review and clearance under 44 
U.S.C. 3507. An agency may not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not 
required to respond to, a collection of information unless it displays 
a currently valid OMB control number. OMB has now approved the 
information collection and has assigned OMB control number 0910-0302. 
The approval expires on August 31, 2010. A copy of the supporting 
statement for this information collection is available on the Internet 
at http://www.fda.gov/ohrms/dockets.

    Dated: September 26, 2007.
Jeffrey Shuren,
Assistant Commissioner for Policy.
