FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 01-28713) published on page 
57718 of the issue for Friday, November 16, 2001.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for First National Bancshares, Inc., Goodland, Kansas, is revised to 
read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City (Susan Zubradt, Assistant 
Vice President) 925 Grand Avenue, Kansas City, Missouri 64198-0001:
    1. First National Bancshares, Inc., ESOP and 401 (K) Trusts, and 
First National Bancshares, Inc., both of Goodland, Kansas; to acquire 
86.4 percent of the voting shares of Security State Bank, Bird City, 
Kansas.
    Comments on this application must be received by December 10, 2001.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, November 27, 
2001.
Margaret McCloskey Shanks,
Assistant Secretary of the Board.
