DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP95-145-000]


Northwest Pipeline Corp.; Technical Conference

April 13, 1995.
    In the order issued on March 1, 1995, in this docket, the 
Commission directed staff to convene a technical conference to address 
the concerns raised by the protests to the filing.
    Take notice that the conference has been scheduled for Tuesday, 
April 25, 1995, at 9 a.m., in a room to be designated at the offices of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 810 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
