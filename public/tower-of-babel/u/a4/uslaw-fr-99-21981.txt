DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY


Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice lists the membership to the Department Offices' 
Performance Review Board (PRB) and

[[Page 47230]]

supersedes the list published in Federal Register 54187, Vol. 63, No. 
195, dated October 8, 1998, in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4). The 
purpose of the PRB is to review the performance of members of the 
Senior Executive Service and make recommendations regarding performance 
ratings, performance awards, and other personnel actions.
