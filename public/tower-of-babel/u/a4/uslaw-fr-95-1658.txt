DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Air Force


USAF Scientific Advisory Board Meeting

    The Basic Research Panel of the USAF Scientific Advisory Board will 
meet on February 20-24, 1995 at Bolling AFB, Washington DC, from 8 a.m. 
to 5 p.m.
    The purpose of the meeting will be to provide science and 
technology assessments on basic research related issues.
    The meeting will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 
552b of Title 5, United States Code, specifically subparagraphs (1) and 
(4) thereof.
    For further information, contact the Scientific Advisory Board 
Secretariat at (703) 697-4648.
Patsy J. Conner,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
