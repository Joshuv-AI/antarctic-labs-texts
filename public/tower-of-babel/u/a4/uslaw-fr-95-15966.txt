Federal Family Education Loan Program

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Final regulations--Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On November 30, 1994, and June 15, 1995 the Secretary published in 
the Federal Register final regulations for the Federal Family Education 
Loan Program (59 FR 61424) and (60 FR 31410). These regulations correct 
those regulations to read as follows--
    1. In the November 30, 1994 regulations (page 61424) under 
``Effective Date'', ``682.603'' is corrected to read ``682.604''.


Sec. 682.603  [Corrected]

    2. In the June 15, 1995 regulations, page 31411, item 2, 
``682.603'' is corrected to read ``682.604''.
