DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Alpine County, CA, Resource Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Alpine County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC) will meet 
on June 3, 2002, in Markleeville, California. The purpose of the 
meeting is to discuss issues relating to implementing the Secure Rural 
Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (Payments to 
States) and the expenditure of Title II funds benefiting National 
Forest System lands on the Humboldt-Toiyabe, and Stanislaus National 
Forests in Alpine County.

DATES: The meeting will be held June 3, 2002 at 6 p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Turtle Rock County Park, 
Markleeville, CA.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Laura Williams, Committee Coordinator, 
USDA, Humboldt-Toiyabe National Forest, 1536 S Carson St., Carson City, 
NV 89701, (775) 884-8150, E-mail: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Agenda items to be covered include: (1) Old 
business; (2) Project Criteria Discussion; (3) Camping in Alpine 
County; (4) Project Proposals; (5) New business & Public comment.
    The meeting is open to the public. Public input opportunity will be 
provided and individuals will have the opportunity to address the 
Committee at that time.

    Dated: May 13, 2002.
Laura J. Williams,
Carson Acting District Ranger.
