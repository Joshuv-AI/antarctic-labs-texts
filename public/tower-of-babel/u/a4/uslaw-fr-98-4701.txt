CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: Consumer Product Safety Commission, Washington, DC 20207.

TIME AND DATE: Monday, March 2, 1998, 10:00 a.m.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the Public.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

1. Upholstered Furniture

    The Commission will consider options for Commission action to 
address the risk of fires caused by small open flame ignition of 
upholstered furniture.

2. Minoxidil

    The staff will brief the Commission on a proposed rule requiring 
child-resistant packaging under the Poison Prevention Packaging Act for 
preparations containing more than 14 mg of minoxidil.
    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207 (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: February 19, 1998.
Sadye E. Dunn,
Secretary.
