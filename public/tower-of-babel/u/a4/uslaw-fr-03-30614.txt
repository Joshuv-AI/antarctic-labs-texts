DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice No. (4553)]


Determination Under Presidential Proclamation

    I hereby make the determination provided for in Section 5 of the 
Presidential Proclamation No. 7060, of December 12, 1997, that the 
suspension of entry into the United States as immigrants or 
nonimmigrants of senior officials of the National Union for the Total 
Independence of Angola (UNITA) and adult dependents of their immediate 
families is no longer necessary. Restrictions imposed in said 
proclamation pursuant to Section 212(f) of the Immigration and 
Nationality Act of 1952, as amended (8 U.S.C. 1182(f)), shall therefore 
lapse, and said proclamation shall terminate, effective immediately.
    This determination shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: November 28, 2003.
Colin L. Powell,
Secretary of State, Department of State.
