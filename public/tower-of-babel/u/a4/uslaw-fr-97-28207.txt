DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Giant Multi-Resource Management Project

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Revised Notice of Intent to prepare an environmental impact 
statement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The USDA Forest Service, Tahoe National Forest, has changed 
the proposed action for this EIS. The new proposal includes only timber 
harvest, road construction, road decommissioning, and fuel treatment. 
The design of the new proposal incorporates and addresses all issues 
previously identified. The Forest Service now anticipates issuing a 
Draft EIS in the last quarter of Calendar Year 1997, and filing a Final 
EIS in the first quarter of Calendar Year 1998.

DATE: Comments concerning the scope of the analysis should be received 
by November 15, 1997.

ADDRESSES: Send written comments to John Bradford, EIS Team Leader, 
Foresthill Ranger District, 22830 Foresthill Road, Foresthill, CA 
95631.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: John Bradford, EIS Team Leader, 
Foresthill Ranger District, 916-478-6254.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On June 2, 1997 a Notice of Intent to 
prepare an Environmental Impact Statement was published in the Federal 
Register (Vol. 62, No. 105, Pages 29706-29707).

    Dated: October 16, 1997.
John H. Skinner,
Forest Supervisor.
