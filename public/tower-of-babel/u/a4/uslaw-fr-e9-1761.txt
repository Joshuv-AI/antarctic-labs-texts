Alaska Village Electric Cooperative; Notice of Preliminary Permit 
Application Accepted for Filing and Soliciting Comments, Motions To 
Intervene, and Competing Applications

January 21, 2009.
    On October 9, 2008, and supplemented on January 15, 2009, Alaska 
Village Electric Cooperative filed an application, pursuant to section 
4(f) of the Federal Power Act, proposing to study the feasibility of 
the Shungnak River Hydroelectric Project located on the Shungnak River 
in the Northwest Arctic Borough, Alaska.
    The proposed Shungnak River Hydroelectric Project would consist of: 
(1) A proposed 200-foot-high, 400-foot-long earth filled gravity dam; 
(2) a proposed 2.2 square mile reservoir with a storage capacity of 
42,000 acre-feet; (3) a proposed 2,000-foot-long, 10-foot diameter 
steel or concrete penstock; (4) a proposed powerhouse containing four 
1.25-megawatt generators; (5) a proposed 12.7-mile-long, 7,200/12,400-
volt transmission line; and (6) appurtenant facilities. The Shungnak 
River Hydroelectric Project is estimated to have an annual generation 
of 35.04-gigawatt-hours, which would be sold to the community of 
Shungnak or mining operations in the vicinity of the project.
