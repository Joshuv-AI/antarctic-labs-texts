DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice No. 2848]


United States International Telecommunications Advisory Committee 
(ITAC), Study Group B, Meeting Notice

    The Department of State announces that the United States 
International Telecommunications Advisory Committee, Telecommunications 
Standardization Sector (ITAC-T) Study Group B will meet at 9:00 am on

[[Page 44668]]

September 24, 1998, at U.S. Department of Commerce, 325 Broadway, 
Conference Room 3012, Boulder, CO. 80303.
    The agenda for the Study Group B meeting will include preparations 
for ITU-T Study Group 15 meeting in October 1998.
    Members of the General Public may attend this meeting and join in 
the discussions, subject to the instructions of the Chair. Admittance 
of public members will be limited to seating available.
    In this regard, entrance to the meeting please inform Marcie 
Geissinger, Carole Craig, or Amy Moulton by September 21 at 303-497-
5216, fax 3030-497-5993, or [email protected] if you intend 
to attend this U.S. Study Group B Meeting. Provide your name, 
organization, and telephone number.

    Dated: July 30, 1998.
Marian R. Gordon,
Chairman, U.S. ITAC for Telecommunications Standardization.
