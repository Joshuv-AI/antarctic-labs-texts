Request for Proposals: Correction

action: Correction Notice.

in the issue of Tuesday, February 22, 1994, the issuance date of RFP 
02-94-ProPAC was incorrect. The notice should read that RFP 02-94-
ProPAC wil be issued on or about March 14, 1994.
