DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration


Advisory Committees; Renewals

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announces the renewal 
of certain FDA advisory

[[Page 6762]]

committees by the Commissioner of Food and Drugs (the Commissioner). 
The Commissioner has determined that it is in the public interest to 
renew the charters of the committees listed below for an additional 2 
years beyond charter expiration date. The new charters will be in 
effect until the dates of expiration listed below. This notice is 
issued under the Federal Advisory Committee Act of October 6, 1972 
(Pub. L. 92-463 (5 U.S.C. app. 2)).

DATES:  Authority for these committees will expire on the dates 
indicated below unless the Commissioner formally determines that 
renewal is in the public interest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

National Mammography Quality Assurance      July 6, 1999
