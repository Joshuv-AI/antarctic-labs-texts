Agency Information Collection Activities; Announcement of OMB 
Approval; Request for Information From U.S. Processors that Export to 
the European Community

AGENCY:  Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION:  Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing that a 
collection of information entitled ``Request for Information From U.S. 
Processors that Export to the European Community'' has been approved by 
the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) under the Paperwork Reduction 
Act of 1995 (the PRA).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Margaret R. Schlosburg, Office of 
Information Resources Management (HFA-250), Food and Drug 
Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-1223.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:  In the Federal Register of June 1, 1998 (63 
FR 29738), the agency announced that the proposed information 
collection had been submitted to OMB for review and

[[Page 46800]]

clearance under section 3507 of the PRA (44 U.S.C. 3507). An agency may 
not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not required to respond to, a 
collection of information unless it displays a currently valid OMB 
control number. OMB has now approved the information collection and has 
assigned OMB control number 0910-0320. The approval expires on July 31, 
2001.

    Dated: August 25, 1998.
William K. Hubbard,
Associate Commissioner for Policy Coordination.
