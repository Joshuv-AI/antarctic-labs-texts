Radio Broadcasting Services; Bridgeton and Elmer, NJ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule, dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In this document, the Commission dismisses the petition for 
rulemaking filed by Cohanzick Broadcasting Corporation requesting the 
reallotment of Channel 299B from Bridgeton, New Jersey, to Elmer, New 
Jersey. Petitioner, by filing amended proposal requesting the 
substitution of Channel 300A for 299B at Bridgeton and reallotment of 
Channel 300A from Bridgeton to Pennsauken, New Jersey, has abandoned 
interest in original proposal.
