Withdrawal of Applications for Subzone Status for Flint Ink North 
America Corporation Plants

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the applications 
requesting special-purpose subzone status for the ten Flint Ink North 
America Corporation plants. The applications were filed on October 7, 
2002 (66 FR 64088-64096, 10/17/02).
    The withdrawal was requested because of changed circumstances, and 
the cases have been closed without prejudice.

    Dated: June 25, 2003.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Executive Secretary.
