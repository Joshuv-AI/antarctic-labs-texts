Office of Thrift Supervision

Indian Village Community Bank, Gnadenhutten, OH; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on May 12, 1999, the Director, Office 
of Examination & Supervision, Office of Thrift Supervision, or his 
designee, acting pursuant to delegated authority, approved the 
application of Indian Village Community Bank, Gnadenhutten, Ohio, to 
convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of the application 
are available for inspection at the Dissemination Branch, Office of

[[Page 27355]]

Thrift Supervision, 1700 G Street, NW, Washington, DC 20552, and the 
Central Regional Office, Office of Thrift Supervision, 200 West Madison 
Street, Suite 1300, Chicago, Illinois 60606.

    Dated: May 14, 1999.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
