SUMMARY: The Copyright Office is making non-substantive housekeeping 
amendments to its regulations to update them and to correct minor 
errors.

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 5, 1998.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Copyright Office periodically reviews 
its regulations as published in the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) 
to correct minor or typographical errors in the published text. The 
Office has identified minor errors in the currently published rules. 
The following sections are amended to correct these errors: 
Secs. 201.17(b)(2), 201.17(f)(4), 201.17(h)(3)(iii)(B)(1), 
201.17(h)(9), 201.18(a)(3), 201.19(a)(3), 201.33(e)(1), 251.6(a), 
251.30(a)(1), 251.32(d), 251.43(e), 251.46(c)(3), 251.46(d), 251.47(e), 
251.47(f), 251.47(i), 251.47(k), 251.48(b), 251.60, 253.7(e), 
256.2(b)(2), 258.1, and 260.3(e). Typographical errors are corrected in 
Secs. 201.11(e)(3), 201.17(e)(2)(i), 201.17(e)(12), 201.29(e)(3), 
251.3(a), 251.3(b), 252.4(a)(1), 257.4(a)(1), and 259.5(a)(1).

    Accordingly, 37 CFR Chapter II is corrected by making the following 
corrections and amendments:
