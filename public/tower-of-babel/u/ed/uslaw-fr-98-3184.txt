FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY

[FEMA-1199-DR]


New Hampshire; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster 
Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
State of New Hampshire, (FEMA-1199-DR), dated January 15, 1998, and 
related determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 24, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Madge Dale, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of New Hampshire, is hereby amended to include Individual Assistance 
for the following areas among those areas determined to have been 
adversely affected by the catastrophe declared a major disaster by the 
President in his declaration of January 15, 1998:

    Belknap, Carroll, Cheshire, Coos, Grafton, Hillsborough, 
Merrimack, Strafford, and Sullivan Counties for Individual 
Assistance (already designated for Public Assistance).

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance.)
Lacy E. Suiter,
Executive Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
