OFFICE OF SPECIAL COUNSEL


Change of Address for Dallas Office

AGENCY: Office of Special Counsel

ACTION: Notice

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Effective Monday, December 9, 2002, the Dallas Field Office 
(DFO) of the U.S. Office of Special Counsel (OSC) moved to the A. Maceo 
Smith Federal Building, 525 Griffin Street, Room 824, Box 103, Dallas, 
TX 75202. The DFO telephone number continues to be (214) 767-8871. 
Additional OSC contact information can be found on the agency Web site 
at http://www.osc.gov.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 9, 2002.

Planning and Advice Division, 1730 M Street, NW. (Suite 201), 
Washington, DC 20036; by telephone, at (202) 653-8971; or by fax, at 
(202) 653-5161.
