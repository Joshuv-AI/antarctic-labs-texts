El Paso Natural Gas Company; Notice Shortening Answer Period

September 14, 2004.
    On September 13, 2004, El Paso Natural Gas Company and the Settling 
Parties in the above-captioned proceedings, filed an Offer of 
Settlement comprised of an Explanatory Statement and Stipulation and 
Agreement in these proceedings. By this notice, the period for filing 
initial comments on the Offer of Settlement is hereby shortened to and 
including September 24, 2004. Reply shall be filed on or before October 
6, 2004.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
