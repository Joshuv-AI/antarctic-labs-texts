Fortis Plastics, LLC, Fort Smith, AR; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 4, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the Rapid Response Coordinator for the State of 
Arkansas on behalf of workers of Fortis Plastics, LLC, Fort Smith, 
Arkansas.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 13th day of March, 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
