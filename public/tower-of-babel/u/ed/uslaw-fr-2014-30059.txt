Notice of Realty Action: Modified Competitive Sealed Bid Sale of 
Public Land in Sweetwater County, Wyoming

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects the date that the Bureau of Land 
Management will open sealed bids for this public land sale. The 
original notice, which was published on Tuesday November 18, 2014, (79 
FR 68693), incorrectly stated the date. On page 68693, column 3, line 9 
under DATES, which reads ``January 20, 2015'', is hereby corrected to 
read ``January 21, 2015.''

Mary Jo Rugwell,
Acting Wyoming State Director.
