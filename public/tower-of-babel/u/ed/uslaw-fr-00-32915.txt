FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Notice of a Matter To Be Added to the Agenda for Consideration at 
an Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that the following matter 
will be added to the ``discussion agenda'' for consideration at the 
open meeting of the Board of Directors of the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation scheduled to be held at 10:00 a.m. on Thursday, December 
21, 2000, in the Board Room on the sixth floor of the FDIC Building 
located at 550--17th Street, NW., Washington, DC:
    Memorandum and resolution re: Disclosure and Reporting of 
Community; Reinvestment Act-Related Agreements: Joint Final Rule.
    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Robert E. Feldman, Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-6757.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

    Dated: December 20, 2000.
Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
