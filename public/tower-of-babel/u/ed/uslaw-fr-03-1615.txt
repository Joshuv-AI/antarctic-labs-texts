Willard Janke v. Public Service Company of Colorado; Notice 
Granting Extension of Time to File Answer to Complaint

January 17, 2003.
    On January 3, 2003, notice was issued of Willard Janke's complaint 
against the Public Service Company of Colorado (Public Service), the 
licensee for the Tacoma-Ames Project No. 400. The notice established 
January 21, 2003, as the deadline for filing the answer to the 
complaint.
    On January 15, 2003, Public Service filed a motion requesting an 
extension to January 24, 2003, of the deadline for filing its answer. 
Public Service has shown good cause for granting the extension. 
Accordingly, its request is granted and its answer must be filed on or 
before January 24, 2003.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
