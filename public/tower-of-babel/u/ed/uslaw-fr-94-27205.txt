DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army

 

Change of Address

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DOD.

ACTION: Public notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    This is a notice to the general public of a change of address for 
the U.S. Army Freedom of Information/Privacy Act Office.
    Old Address: U.S. Army Information Systems Command-Pentagon, (ASOK-
OP-F), Crystal Square No. 2, Suite 201, 1725 Jefferson Davis Highway, 
Arlington, VA 22202.
    New Address: U.S. Army Freedom of Information/Privacy Act Office, 
(SAIS-IDP-F/P), Suite 201, 1725 Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, VA 
22202-4102. This change is effective immediately.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rose Marie Christensen, (703) 607-
3452, telfax: (703) 607-3450.
Kenneth L. Denton,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
