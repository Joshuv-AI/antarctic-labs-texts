Government-Owned Inventions, Available for Licensing

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Availability of Inventions for Licensing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Patent applications on the inventions listed below assigned to 
the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, have been filed in 
the United States Patent and Trademark Office, and are available for 
licensing.

telephone (650) 604-5104; fax (650) 604-2767.

NASA Case No.: ARC-17241-1: Optimum Strategies for Selecting Descent 
Flight-Path Angles;
NASA Case No.: ARC-17299-1: Unmanned Aerial Systems Traffic Management;
NASA Case No.: ARC-17085-1: Electromagnetic Monitoring and Control of a 
Plurality of Nanosatellites;

Fabrication of Printable Electronics and Functional Coatings;
NASA Case No.: ARC-17335-1: A Simplified Production of Organic
