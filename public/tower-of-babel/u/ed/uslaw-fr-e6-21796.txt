Employment and Training Administration

Lee Middleton Original Dolls, Inc. Belpre, OH; Notice of 
Termination Of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on December 8, 2006, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Lee Middleton Original Dolls, 
Inc., Belpre, Ohio.
    The petition has been deemed invalid. The petition contained 
petitioner information for three workers but was not signed by all 
three workers.
    Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
