DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


U.S. Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces Code Committee Meeting

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the forthcoming public meeting of the 
Code Committee established by Article 146(a), Uniform Code of Military 
Justice, 10 U.S.C. 946(a), to be held at the Courthouse of the United 
States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces, 450 E Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20442-0001, at 10:00 a.m. on Tuesday, September 12, 
2000. The agenda for this meeting will include consideration of 
proposed changes to the Uniform Code of Military Justice and the Manual 
for Courts-Martial, United States, and other matters relating to the 
operation of the Uniform Code of Military Justice throughout the Armed 
Forces.

DATES: September 12, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Thomas F. Granahan, Clerk of the 
Court, United States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces, 450 E 
Street, Northwest, Washington, D.C. 20042-0001, telephone (202) 761-
1448.

    Dated: August 24, 2000.
