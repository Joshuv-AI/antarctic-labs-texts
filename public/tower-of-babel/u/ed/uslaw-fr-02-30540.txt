DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On November 19, 2002, the Department of Education published a 
30-day public comment period notice in the Federal Register (Page 
69729, Column 1) for the information collection, ``School Improvement: 
Foreign Language Assistance Program for Local Educational Agencies''. 
Under reporting and recordkeeping burden, responses were stated as 150 
and burden hours as 12,000. The correct responses are 250 and the 
burden hours are 20,000. The Leader, Regulatory Management Group, 
Office of the Chief Information Officer, hereby issues a correction 
notice as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.
