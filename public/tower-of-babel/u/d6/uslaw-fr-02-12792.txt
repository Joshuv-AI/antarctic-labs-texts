Brookline Bancorp, Inc., Brookline, Massachusetts; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on, May 15, 2002, the Director, 
Examination Policy, Office of Thrift Supervision (``OTS''), or her 
designee, acting pursuant to delegated authority, approved the 
application of Brookline Savings Bank, Brookline, Massachusetts, to 
convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of the application 
are available for inspection by appointment (phone number: 202-906-5922 
or e-mail: [email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, OTS, 
1700 G Street, NW, Washington DC 20552, and the OTS Northeast Regional 
Office, 10 Exchange Place, 18th Floor, Jersey City, New Jersey 07302.

    Dated: May 17, 2002.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
