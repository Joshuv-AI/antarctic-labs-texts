DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


Healthcare Infection Control Practices Advisory Committee 
(HICPAC); Correction

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Healthcare 
Infection Control Practices Advisory Committee (HICPAC); March 4, 2021, 
9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., EST in the original FRN.
    The teleconference was published in the Federal Register on January 
14, 2021, Volume 86, Number 9, pages 3155-3156.
    The teleconference is being corrected to update the meeting time 
and should read as follows:
    DATE: The meeting will be held on March 4, 2021, from 1:00 p.m. to 
3:00 p.m., EST.

3, Atlanta, Georgia 30329-4027, telephone (404) 498-0730; 
[email protected].
