SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region 1--Maine District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region 1 Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Augusta, Maine will hold a public 
meeting at 1:30 p.m. November 18th, 2003 at the University of Southern 
Maine, 68 High Street, Room 118, Portland, Maine to discuss such 
matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Mary McAleney, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 68 Sewall Street, Room 
512, Augusta, Maine 04330, (207)-622-8386 phone, (207)-622-8277 fax.

Scott R. Morris,
Deputy Chief of Staff.
