INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[USITC SE-04-024]


Government in the Sunshine Act Meeting; Cancellation of 
Commission Meeting

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

Original Date:  September 21, 2004.

Original Time:  2 p.m.

Place:  Room 101, 500 E Street SW., Washington, DC 20436, Telephone: 
(202) 205-2000.

Status:  Open to the public.
    In accordance with 19 CFR 201.35(d)(1), the Commission has 
determined to cancel the meeting which was scheduled for September 21, 
2004 at 2 p.m. The meeting will be rescheduled at a later date. Earlier 
notification of this action was not possible.

    Issued: September 16, 2004.
