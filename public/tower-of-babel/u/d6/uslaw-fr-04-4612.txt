NATIONAL ARCHIVES AND RECORDS ADMINISTRATION


Electronic Records Policy Working Group Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Archives and Records Administration (NARA).

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Electronic Records Policy Working Group is holding a 
public meeting to obtain views from the public and Federal agencies on 
implementing section 207(e)(1)(A) of the E-Government Act of 2002. That 
section calls for ``the adoption by agencies of policies and procedures 
to ensure that chapters 21, 25, 27, 29, and 31 of title 44, United 
States Code, are applied effectively and comprehensively to Government 
information on the Internet and to other electronic records.'' 
Additional information on the Electronic Records Policy Working Group 
and the meeting agenda is provided in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION 
section of this notice.

DATES: The meeting will be held on March 30, 2004, from 1 p.m. to 4 
p.m.
    Because seating is limited to 200 people and we must provide a list 
of attendees to the building security staff in advance, you must 
register no later than March 26, 2004. Registrations will be taken on a 
first-come, first-served basis.

ADDRESSES: The location of the meeting is: National Capital Planning 
Commission, 401 9th Street, NW., Suite 500 North, Washington DC 20576.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pamela Mason at 301-837-0975 or 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Electronic Records Working Group was 
established by the Interagency Committee on Government Information 
(ICGI), to fulfill the requirements of subsection 207(e) of the Act, 
``Public Access to Electronic Information.'' The Working Group's 
members are drawn from a number of Federal agencies, with NARA as the 
chair. The Working Group has held several focus groups with interested 
stakeholders from Federal agencies, public interest groups, and 
professional organizations to address the following three issues:
    [sbull] The definition to be used for ``Government information on 
the Internet and other electronic records''.
    The operating definitions currently used by the Working Group are 
as follows:
    Government information on the Internet--
    [sbull] Information posted on Government Web sites,
    [sbull] Information exchanged between Federal agencies,
    [sbull] Information exchanged between Federal agencies and the 
public,
    [sbull] Information exchanged between Federal agencies and other 
governments,
    [sbull] Government-enabled Web services,
    [sbull] Standard government forms,
    [sbull] E-government business transactions.
    Other electronic records--Electronic information meeting the 
definition of a Federal record per 44 U.S.C. 3301. Records include:
    [sbull] All books, papers, maps, photographs, machine readable 
materials, or other documentary materials,
    [sbull] Regardless of physical form or characteristics,
    [sbull] Made or received by an agency of the United States 
government:
    [sbull] Under Federal law, or
    [sbull] In connection with the transaction of public business;
    [sbull] And preserved or appropriate for preservation by that 
agency or its legitimate successor:
    [sbull] As evidence of the organization, functions, policies, 
decisions, procedures, operations or other activities of the 
government, or
    [sbull] Because of the informational value of the data in them (44 
U.S.C. 3301).
    [sbull] Perceived barriers to effective management of ``Government 
information on the Internet and other electronic records''.
    The operating definition of effective management currently used by 
the Working Group includes:
    [sbull] Managing through the life cycle,
    [sbull] Providing for accessibility and retrieval,

[[Page 9856]]

    [sbull] Providing sufficient security,
    [sbull] Ensuring consistency (ability to reproduce record),
    [sbull] Providing for the integrity of records over time,
    [sbull] Ensuring no loss of records,
    [sbull] Ensuring compatibility with standard formats,
    [sbull] Managing format changes over time,
    [sbull] Providing for long-term record storage and migration of 
formats,
    [sbull] Managing the location of records over time,
    [sbull] Cost effective,
    [sbull] Appropriate long-term custodianship.
    [sbull] What guidance tools would assist in overcoming the 
identified barriers.
    At this public meeting, the Working Group will highlight findings 
from the focus groups and seek additional comments from the attendees 
on the three issues.
