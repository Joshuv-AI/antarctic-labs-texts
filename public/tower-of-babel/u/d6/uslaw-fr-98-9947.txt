FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Farm Credit Administration Board; Amendment to Sunshine Act 
Meeting

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Government in the Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 
552b(e)(3)), the Farm Credit Administration gave notice on April 6, 
1998 (63 FR 16813) of the regular meeting of the Farm Credit 
Administration Board (Board) scheduled for April 9, 1998. This notice 
is to amend the agenda by adding an item for a closed session of that 
meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Floyd Fithian, Secretary to the Farm 
Credit Administration Board, (703) 883-4025, TDD (703) 883-4444.

ADDRESSES: Farm Credit Administration, 1501 Farm Credit Drive, McLean, 
Virginia 22102-5090.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Parts of this meeting of the Board were open 
to the public (limited space available), and parts of this meeting were 
closed to the public. The agenda for April 9, 1998, is amended by 
adding a closed session to read as follows:

Closed Session*

D. Report

--OSMO Report

    Session Closed--Exempt pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552b(c) (8) and (9).
    Dated: April 9, 1998.
Floyd Fithian,
Secretary, Farm Credit Administration Board.
