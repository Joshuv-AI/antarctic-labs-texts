DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Office of the Secretary


Members of Performance Review Boards

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, USDA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the appointment of members of the 
Performance Review Boards (PRB's) for the U.S. Department of 
Agriculture (USDA). The USDA PRB's ensure meaningful distinctions in 
performance as they review Senior Executive Service (SES) performance 
appraisals and make recommendations to the Secretary of Agriculture, 
regarding final performance ratings, performance awards, salary 
adjustments, and Presidential Rank Awards for SES members.

DATES: Effective upon publication in the Federal Register.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barbara Holland, Office of Human 
Resources Management, Executive Resources and Services Division, U.S. 
Department of Agriculture, 1400 Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, 
DC 20250, (202) 720-2101.
