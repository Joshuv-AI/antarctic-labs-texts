Notice of Meeting of John Day-Snake Resource Advisory Council

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Prineville District.

ACTION: Meeting of John Day-Snake Resource Advisory Council; Pendleton, 
Oregon; May 11 & 12, 1998.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A meeting of the John Day-Snake Resource Advisory Council will 
be held on May 11, 1998 from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the Department 
of Natural Resources Conference Room on the Umatilla Reservation, and 
on May 12 from 7:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the Doubletree Inn, 304 SE Nye 
Ave., Pendleton, Oregon. The meeting is open to the public. Public 
comments will be received at 1:00 p.m. on May 12. The May 11 session 
will include a tour of Tribal Headquarters and a field trip to view 
conservation work on the reservation. The May 12 session will include 
the future RAC program of work, a review of the BLM Strategic Plan, 
approval of a subgroup nomination and selection process, an update of 
the John Day River plan subgroup and an update on the recreation fee 
demonstration areas.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
James L. Hancock, Bureau of Land Management, Prineville District 
Office, 3050 NE Third Street, P.O. Box 550, Prineville, Oregon 97754, 
or call 541-416-6700.
