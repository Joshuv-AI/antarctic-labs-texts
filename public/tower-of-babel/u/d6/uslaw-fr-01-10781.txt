DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Membership of Performance Review Board

April 26, 2001.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby provides notice of 
the membership of its Performance Review Board (PRB). This action is 
undertaken in accordance with Title 5, U.S.C., Section 4314(c)(4). The 
Commission's PRB will be comprised of the following members:

Thomas R. Herlihy
Daniel L. Larcamp
Cynthia A. Marlette

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
