COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Connecticut Advisory Committee to 
the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights; Revision to Date

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice: revision to meeting date.

Federal Register of Friday, June 16, 2023, concerning a meeting of the

a.m. ET to Thursday, July 6, 2023, at 12:00 p.m. ET.
