ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Notices

Agency: Election Assistance Commission.

Date and Time: Thursday, June 3, 2004, at 9 a.m.

Place: Loyola University, Water Tower Campus, 25 East Pearson, Chicago, 
IL 60611, 15th Floor, Kasbeer Hall.

Status: This meeting will be open to the public.

Note: Early arrival: Those attending are advised to arrive early for 
registration and security check.

Purpose: To conduct a public hearing to identify best practices, 
problems and transition issues associated with optical scan, punch 
card, and lever machine voting systems and the success and problems 
identified with the use of provisional voting.
    The following witness panels will be presented: punchcard panel, 
lever machine panel, optical scan panel and provisional vote panel.

For Further Information Contact: Bryan Whitener, Telephone: (202) 566-
3100.

Paul S. DeGregorio,
Commissioner, Election Assistance Commission.
