DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases Special Emphasis Panel, 
December 14, 2016, 8:00 a.m. to December 15, 2016, 6:00 p.m., 
Doubletree Hotel Bethesda, (Formerly Holiday Inn Select), 8120 
Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published in the Federal 
Register on November 21, 2016, 81 FR 83253.
    This meeting notice is amended to change the start date of the 
meeting from December 14, 2016 to December 9, 2016. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: November 22, 2016.
Natasha M. Copeland,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
