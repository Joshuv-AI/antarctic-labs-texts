DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 5930]


Advisory Panel to the United States Section of the North Pacific 
Anadromous Fish Commission; Notice of Public Meeting

    The Advisory Panel to the United States Section of the North 
Pacific Anadromous Fish Commission will meet on September 27, 2007, via 
conference call. This session will involve discussion of the Fifteenth 
Annual Meeting of the North Pacific Anadromous Fish Commission, to be 
held on October 8-12, 2007 in Valdivostok, Russia. The discussion will 
begin at 3:30 p.m. EST and is open to the public.
    Requests for the conference call-in phone number or for further 
information on the meeting should be directed to Ms. Nicole M. Ricci, 
Office of Marine Conservation (OES/OMC), Room 2758, U.S. Department of 
State, Washington, DC 20520-7818. Ms. Ricci can be reached by telephone 
at (202) 647-1073 or by Fax (202) 736-7350.

    Dated: August 28, 2007.
David A. Balton,
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Oceans and Fisheries, Department of 
State.
