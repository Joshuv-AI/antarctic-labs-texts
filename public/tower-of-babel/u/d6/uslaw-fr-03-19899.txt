DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Office of the Secretary

[Docket OST-2003-14525]


Application of Reliant Airlines, Inc. and Kalitta Charters II, 
LLC, for Transfer of Certificate Authority

AGENCY: Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice of order to show cause (Order 2003-7-37).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is directing all interested 
persons to show cause why it should not issue an order finding Kalitta 
Charters II, LLC, fit, willing, and able, and transferring to it the 
all-cargo authority contained in the interstate and foreign cargo 
charter certificates currently issued to Reliant Airlines, Inc.

DATES: Persons wishing to file objections should do so no later than 
August 13, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Objections and answers to objections should be filed in 
Docket OST-2003-14525 and addressed to the Department of Transportation 
Dockets (M-30, Room PL-401), U.S. Department of Transportation, 400 
Seventh Street, SW., Washington, DC 20590, and should be served upon 
the parties listed in Attachment A to the order.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Delores King, Air Carrier Fitness 
Division (X-56, Room 6401), U.S. Department of Transportation, 400 
Seventh Street, SW., Washington, DC 20590, (202) 366-2343.

    Dated: July 30, 2003.
Michael W. Reynolds,
Acting Assistant Secretary for Aviation and International Affairs.
