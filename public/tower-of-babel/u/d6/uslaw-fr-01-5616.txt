DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board; Notice of Change of Advisory Committee 
Meetings

SUMMARY: The meeting of the Defense Science Board (DSB) Task Force on 
High Energy Laser Weapon Systems Applications, which was scheduled for 
April 17-18, 2001, has been rescheduled for April 16, 2001. In 
addition, the meeting scheduled for May 15-16, 2001, will now be held 
on May 15, 2001 only. The meeting will be held Strategic Analysis Inc., 
3601 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22201.

    Dated: March 1, 2001.
L. M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
