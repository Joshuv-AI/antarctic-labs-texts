POSTAL REGULATORY COMMISSION


Facility Tours

AGENCY: Postal Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of Commission tours.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Wednesday afternoon, June 27, 2007, Postal Regulatory 
Commissioners and advisory staff members will tour California Community 
News production facilities in Irwindale, California. The purpose of the 
tour is to observe company operations.

DATES: June 27, 2007 (2:45 p.m.).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ann C. Fisher, Chief of Staff, Postal 
Regulatory Commission, at 202-789-6803 or [email protected].
