DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Board of Visitors Meeting

AGENCY: Defense Acquisition University, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of Board of Visitors meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The September 18, 2002 meeting of the Defense Acquisition 
University (DAU) Board of Visitors (BoV) announced in the Federal 
Register on August 20, 2002 (67 FR 53916) has been canceled. The 
meeting has been rescheduled for October 22, 2002. The purpose of this 
meeting is to report back to the BoV on continuing items of interest.

DATES: October 22, 2002 from 0900-1500.

ADDRESSES: Packard Conference Center, Building 184, Fort Belvoir, 
Virginia.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Diane Reid at 703-805-5133.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting is open to the public; however, 
because of space limitations, allocation of seating will be made on a 
first-come, first served basis. Persons desiring to attend the meeting 
should call Ms. Diane Reid at 703-805-5133.

    Dated: September 12, 2002.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternative OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of 
Defense.
