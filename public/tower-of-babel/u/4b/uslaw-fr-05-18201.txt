SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Mississippi Disaster Number MS-00005

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Amendment 1.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is an amendment of the Presidential declaration of a 
major disaster for the State of Mississippi (FEMA-1604-DR), dated 8/29/
2005.
    Incident: Hurricane Katrina.
    Incident Period: 8/29/2005 and continuing.
    Effective Date: 9/6/2005.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: 10/28/2005.
    EIDL Loan Application Deadline Date: 5/29/2006.

ADDRESSES: Submit completed loan applications to: U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Disaster Area Office 3, 14925 Kingsport Road, Fort 
Worth, TX 76155.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: A. Escobar, Office of Disaster 
Assistance, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street, Suite 
6050, Washington, DC 20416.
