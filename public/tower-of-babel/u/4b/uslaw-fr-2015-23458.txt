CFR Correction

200 to 299, revised as of October 1, 2014, on page 296, in section 
237.102-70, paragraph (d)(2) is reinstated to read as follows:


Sec.  237.102-70  Prohibition on contracting for firefighting or 
security-guard functions.

    (d) * * *
* * * * *
    (2) Follow the procedures at PGI 237.102-70(d) to ensure that the 
personnel limitations specified in paragraph (d)(1)(iv) of this 
subsection are not exceeded.
