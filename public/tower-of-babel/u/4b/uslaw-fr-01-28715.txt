DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Alpine County, CA, Resource Advisory Committee (RAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Alpine County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC) will meet 
on December 3, 2001, in Markleeville, CA. The purpose of the meeting is 
to discuss issues relating to implementing the Secure Rural Schools and 
Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (Payments to States) and the 
expenditure of Title II funds benefiting National Forest System lands 
on the Humboldt-toiyabe, and Stanislaus National Forests in Alpine 
County.

DATES: The meeting will be held December 3, 2001, from 3 p.m. to 5:30 
p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Turtle Rock County Park, 
markleeville, CA.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Laura Williams, Committee Coordinator, 
USDA, Humboldt-Toiyabe National Forest, 1536 S. Carson St., Carson 
City, NV 89701, (775) 884-8150, email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Agenda items to be covered includel: (1) 
Welcome and introductions; (2) Overview of Payments to States Act, PL 
106-393; (3) Developing organizational guidelines for the Alpine County 
RAC; (4) Planning processes for projects in Alpine county; (5) 
Preliminary presentation of some project ideas; (6) Public comment. The 
meeting is open to the public. Public input opportunity will be 
provided and individuals will have the opportunity to address the 
Committee at that time.

    Dated: November 8, 2001.
Gary Schiff,
Carson District Ranger.
