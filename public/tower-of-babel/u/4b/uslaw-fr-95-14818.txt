DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
Minority Business Development Agency


Business Development Center Applications: Charleston, SC

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) Program to operate the Charleston, 
South Carolina MBDC. This solicitation was originally published in the 
Federal Register, Wednesday, May 10, 1995, Vol. 60, No. 90, 24838.
