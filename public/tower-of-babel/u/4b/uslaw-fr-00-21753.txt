DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Patent and Trademark Office


Performance Review Board

AGENCY: United States Patent and Trademark Office, Department of 
Commerce.

ACTION: Announcement of membership of the United States Patent and 
Trademark Office Performance Review Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In conformance with the Civil Service Reform Act of 1978, 5 
U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), the United States Patent and Trademark Office 
announces the appointment of persons to serve as members of its 
Performance Review Board.

ADDRESSES: Comments should be addressed to the Director, Office of 
Human Resources, United State Patent and Trademark Office, One Crystal 
Park, Suite 707, Washington, DC 20231.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sydney Rose at (703) 305-8062.
