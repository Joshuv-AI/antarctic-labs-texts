SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2852; Amendment #3]


Illinois; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated May 23, 1996, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to include Gallatin and Wabash Counties in the State of 
Illinois as a disaster area due to damages caused by severe storms and 
flooding beginning on April 28, 1996 and continuing through May 17, 
1996.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous counties of Hardin in Illinois and 
Union in Kentucky may be filed until the specified date at the 
previously designated location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for physical damage is July 5, 1996, and for 
loans for economic injury the deadline is February 6, 1997.
    The economic injury number for the State of Kentucky is 891500.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: May 30, 1996.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
