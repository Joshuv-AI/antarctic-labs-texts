DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice:11252]


In the Matter of the Designation of the Eastern Turkistan Islamic 
Movement Also Known as ETIM as a ``Terrorist Organization'' Pursuant to 
Section 212(a)(3)(B)(vi)(II) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as 
Amended

    Acting under the authority of Section 212(a)(3)(B)(vi)(II) if the 
INA, as amended, I hereby revoke the designation of the Eastern 
Turkistan Islamic Movement, also known as ETIM, as a ``terrorist 
organization'' under Section 212(a)(3)(B)(vi)(II) if the INA. This 
determination shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: October 20, 2020.
Michael R. Pompeo,
Secretary of State.
