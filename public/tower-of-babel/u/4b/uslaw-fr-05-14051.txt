COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Schedule of Meetings

    Listed below are the schedule of meetings of the Old Georgetown 
Board for 2006. The Commission's office is located at the National 
Building Museum, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 401 F Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20001-2728. The Old Georgetown Board meetings are held 
on the 1st Thursday of each month, excluding August. Items of 
discussion affecting the appearance of Georgetown in Washington, DC may 
include buildings, parks and memorials.
    Draft agendas and additional information regarding the Commission 
are available on our Web site: http://www.cfa.gov. Inquiries regarding 
the agenda and request to submit written or oral statements should be 
addressed to Thomas E. Lebke, Secretary, Commission of Fine Arts, at 
the above address or call 202-504-2200. Individuals requiring sign 
language interpretation for the hearing impaired should contact the 
Secretary at least 10 days before the meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, DC, July 12, 2005.
Thomas Luebke,
Secretary.
