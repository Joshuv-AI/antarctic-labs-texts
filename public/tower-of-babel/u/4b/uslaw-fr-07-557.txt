DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of General Medical Sciences, Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the MARC 
Review Subcommittee A, February 15, 2007, 8 a.m. to February 15, 2007, 
5 p.m. Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro Center, 7400 
Wisconsin Ave., Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal

p.m. and on February 16, 2007, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. The meeting is 
closed to the public.
