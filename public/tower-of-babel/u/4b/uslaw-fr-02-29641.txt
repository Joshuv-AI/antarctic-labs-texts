Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-42,291]


Playtex Apparel, Inc., Dover, DE; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 28, 2002 in response to a worker petition, 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Playtex Apparel, Inc, Dover, 
Delaware.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.
