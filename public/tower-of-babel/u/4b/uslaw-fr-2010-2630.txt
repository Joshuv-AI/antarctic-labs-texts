Commission.

Time and Date: February 12, 2010 at 11 a.m.

Place: Room 110, 500 E Street, SW., Washington, DC 20436, Telephone: 
(202) 205-2000.

Status: Open to the public.

    3. Ratification List.

agenda of the following meeting.
