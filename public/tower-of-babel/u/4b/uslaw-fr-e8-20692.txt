Employment and Training Administration

Veyance Technologies, Inc.; Fairlawn, OH; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 25, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Veyance 
Technologies, Inc., Fairlawn, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC. this 29th day of August 2008.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
