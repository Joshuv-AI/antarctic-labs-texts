DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Delegation of Authority No. 560]


Delegation of Authority Public Posting of Reports

    By virtue of the authority vested in the Secretary of State by the 
laws of the United States, including 22 U.S.C. 2651a and section 
7016(b)(2)(A) of the Department of State, Foreign Operations, and 
Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2024 (div. F, Pub. L. 118-47), I 
hereby delegate to the Under Secretary for Political Affairs and the 
Under Secretary for Management, to the extent authorized by law, the 
authority to determine that the public posting of a report to the 
Congress would compromise national security, including the conduct of 
diplomacy, or the report contains proprietary or other privileged 
information.
    The reference in this delegation of authority to a statute shall be 
deemed to be a reference to such statute as amended from time to time 
and shall be deemed to apply to any provision of law that is the same 
or substantially the same as such statute. This delegation of authority 
does not repeal or modify any other delegation of authority currently 
in effect.
    The Secretary, Deputy Secretary, or Deputy Secretary for Management 
and Resources may exercise any authority or function delegated herein.
    This memorandum shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: July 24, 2024.
Antony J. Blinken,
Secretary of State.
