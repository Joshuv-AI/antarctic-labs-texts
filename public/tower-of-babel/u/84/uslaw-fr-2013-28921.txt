Revisions to Procedural Regulations Governing Filing, Indexing 
and Service by Oil Pipelines, Electronic Tariff Filings; Notice of 
Changes to eTariff Part 341 Type of Filing Codes

    Order No. 780, effective June 28, 2013, revised Part 341 of the 
Commission's regulations to revise the filing, indexing and service 
procedures used by oil pipelines.\1\ Take notice that, effective 
December 23, 2013, the list of available eTariff Type of Filing Codes 
(TOFC) will be modified as follows: \2\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Filing, Indexing and Service Requirements for Oil Pipelines, 
143 FERC ] 61,137 (2013) (Order No. 780).
    \2\ The type of filing business process categories are described 
in the Implementation Guide for Electronic Filing of Parts 35, 154, 
284, 300, and 341 Tariff Filings (August 12, 2013), found on the 
Commission's Web site, http://www.ferc.gov/docs-filing/etariff/implementation-guide.pdf.
