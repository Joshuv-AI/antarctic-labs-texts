DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans

    The Department of Veterans Affairs' notice that a meeting of the 
Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans, authorized by P.L. 103-446, to 
be held from March 11, 1996 to March 13, 1996, is hereby canceled. The 
notice appeared in the Federal Register on February 14, 1996, Vol. 61, 
No. 31, page 5837.
    If you have any questions, please contact Mr. Anthony T. Hawkins, 
Associate Director, Center for Minority Veterans, (phone 202-273-6708).

    Dated: February 26, 1996.

    By direction of the Secretary:
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
