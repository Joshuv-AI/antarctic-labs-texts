DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. OA96-67-003]


Montaup Electric Company; Notice of Filing

September 24, 1997.
    Take notice that on August 29, 1997, Montaup Electric Company 
tendered for filing Revised sheets Nos. 45, 59 and 50 to its open 
access transmission filing in the above-referenced docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before October, 1997. Protests will 
be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action 
to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
