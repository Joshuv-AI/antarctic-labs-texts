Deletion of Items From April 20, 2023 Open Meeting

April 19, 2023.
    The following items were adopted by the Commission and deleted from 
the list of items scheduled for consideration at the Thursday, April 
20, 2023, Open Meeting. Item #4 was adopted on April 18, 2023. Item #7 
was adopted and released on April 17, 2023. Both items were previously 
listed in the Commission's Sunshine Notice on Thursday, April 13, 2023.
