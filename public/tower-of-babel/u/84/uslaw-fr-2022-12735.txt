FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

[Docket No. 22-14]


MSRF, Inc., Complainant v. HMM Company Limited, and Yang Ming 
Marine Transport Corporation, Respondents; Notice of Filing of 
Complaint and Assignment

Served: June 8, 2022.
    Notice is given that a complaint has been filed with the Federal 
Maritime Commission (Commission) by MSRF, Inc. (MSRF), hereinafter 
``Complainant'', against HMM Company Limited (HMM) and Yang Ming Marine 
Transport Corporation (Yang Ming), hereinafter ``Respondents''. 
Complainant alleges that Respondents are ocean common carriers 
organized under the laws of the Republic of Korea and Taiwan, 
respectively.
    Complainant alleges that Respondents violated 46 U.S.C. 41102(c), 
41104(a)(2), 41104(a)(5), 41104(a)(9), and 41104 (a)(10). The full text 
of the complaint can be found in the Commission's Electronic Reading 
Room at https://www2.fmc.gov/readingroom/proceeding/22-14/.
    This proceeding has been assigned to Office of Administrative Law 
Judges. The initial decision of the presiding officer in this 
proceeding shall be issued by June 8, 2023, and the final decision of 
the Commission shall be issued by December 22, 2023.

William Cody,
Secretary.
