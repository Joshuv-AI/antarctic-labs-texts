American Standard Brands, Crane Plastic, Mansfield, OH; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 3, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers of American Standard Brands, Crane Plastic, 
Mansfield, Ohio.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Conse quently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 31st day of March 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
