SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

A. OMB Control Number, Title, and Any Associated Form(s)

    9000-0129, Cost Accounting Standards Administration.

B. Need and Uses

    This clearance covers the information that contractors must submit 
to comply with the following Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 
requirements:
    FAR 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards. This 
clause requires contractors performing Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) 
covered contracts to submit notifications and descriptions of certain 
cost accounting practice changes, including revisions to their 
Disclosure Statements, if applicable. Often these descriptions are 
quite complex. This clause also requires contractors to submit 
rationale to support any contractor written statement that the cost 
impact of the change is immaterial.
    The information is used by contracting officers for ascertaining 
compliance with CAS.
