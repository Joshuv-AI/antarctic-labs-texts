Further Definition of ``Swap,'' ``Security-Based Swap,'' and 
``Security-Based Swap Agreement''; Mixed Swaps; Security-Based Swap 
Agreement Recordkeeping

AGENCY: Commodity Futures Trading Commission; Securities and Exchange 
Commission.

ACTION: Joint proposed rules; proposed interpretations; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commodity Futures Trading Commission and the Securities 
and Exchange Commission published a document in the Federal Register of 
May 23, 2011 that referenced an incorrect RIN and an incorrect cite in 
an authority citation. This correction is being published to correct 
both the RIN and the authority citation.
