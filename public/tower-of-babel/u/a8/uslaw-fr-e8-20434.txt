DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Macromolecular Structure and Function B Study Section, October 6, 2008, 
8 a.m. to October 7, 2008, 6 p.m., Melrose Hotel, 2430 Pennsylvania 
Avenue, NW., Washington, DC, 20037 which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 21, 2008, 73 FR 49465-49467.
    The meeting will be held one day only October 6, 2008. The meeting 
time and location remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.
