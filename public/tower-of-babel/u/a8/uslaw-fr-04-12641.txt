TI Group Automotive Systems LLC, Greenville, TN; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 29, 2004, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at TI Group 
Automotive Systems LLC, Greenville, Tennessee.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 26th day of April, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
