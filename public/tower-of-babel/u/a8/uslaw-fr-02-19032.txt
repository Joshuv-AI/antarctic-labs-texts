DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Defense 
(DoD).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the appointment of the members of the 
Senior Executive Services (SES) Performance Review Board (PRB) for the 
OIG DoD, as required by 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4). The PRB provides fair and 
impartial review of SES performance appraisals and makes 
recommendations regarding performance ratings, perforamnce awards and 
recertification to the Inspector General.

DATES: July 31, 2002.
