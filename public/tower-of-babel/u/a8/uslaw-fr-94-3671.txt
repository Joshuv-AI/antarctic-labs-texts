DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service

 

Westslope Timber Sale, Plumas National Forest; Environmental 
Impact Statement Cancellation

    The Plumas National Forest is no longer involved in the preparation 
of an Environmental Impact Statement for the Westslope Timber Sale.
    The notice of Intent, published in the Federal Register on March 5, 
1991, is hereby rescinded (56 FR 9194).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R.C. Bennett, Environmental 
Coordinator, Plumas National Forest, Box 11500, Quincy, CA 95971, 
telephone (916) 283-2050.

    Dated: January 22, 1994.
H. Wayne Thornton,
Forest Supervisor.
