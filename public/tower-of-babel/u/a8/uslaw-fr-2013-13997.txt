National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, July 
15, 2013, 8:00 a.m. to July 15, 2013, 5:00 p.m., National Institute of 
Environmental Health Sciences, 111 T.W. Alexander Drive, Research 
Triangle Park, NC, 27709 which was published in the Federal Register on 
May 29, 2013, 2013-12635.

from July 15, 2013 to July 16, 2013. The meeting is closed to the 
public.
