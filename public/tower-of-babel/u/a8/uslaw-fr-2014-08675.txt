National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, June 11, 2014, 11:00 a.m. to June 11, 2014, 01:00 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, Two Democracy Plaza, 6707 Democracy 
Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal 
Register on April 09, 2014, 79 FR 19637.

01:00 p.m. at the National Institutes of Health, Two Democracy Plaza, 
6707 Democracy Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892. The meeting is closed to 
the public.
