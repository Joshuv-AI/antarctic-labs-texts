PANAMA CANAL COMMISSION

48 CFR Chapter 35


Federal Acquisition Regulations

Removal of CFR Chapter

    Effective October 1, 2004, the Panama Canal Commission was 
terminated by Public Law 108-309. Therefore the Office of the Federal 
Register is removing the Panama Canal Commission's regulations pursuant 
to its authority to maintain an orderly system of codification under 44 
U.S.C. 1510 and 1 CFR 8.2
    Accordingly, 48 CFR is amended by removing Chapter 35 consisting of 
parts 3501 through 3599.
