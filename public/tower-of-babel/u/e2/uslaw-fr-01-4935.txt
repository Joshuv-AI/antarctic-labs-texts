OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: Overseas Private Investment Corporation.

PURPOSE: Public Hearing in conjunction with quarterly meeting of OPIC's 
Board of Directors, to afford an opportunity for any person to present 
views regarding the activities of the Corporation.

February 22, 2001.

March 8, 2001 has been postponed until further notice.
