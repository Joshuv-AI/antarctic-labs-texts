Background

    The intent of this policy is to provide guidance for conducting a 
propeller safety analysis. Although 14 CFR part 35 does not explicitly 
require a safety analysis, safety analyses are frequently conducted to 
support part 35 requirements, special conditions, and aircraft 
manufacture certification requirements. This policy does not establish 
new requirements.
