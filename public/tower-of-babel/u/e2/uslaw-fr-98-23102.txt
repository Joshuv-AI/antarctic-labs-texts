DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Defense Intelligence Agency


Science and Technology Advisory Board Closed Panel Meeting

AGENCY: Department of Defense, Defense Intelligence Agency.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of Subsection (d) of Section 10 of 
Public Law 92-463, as amended by Section 5 of Public Law 94-409, notice 
is hereby given that a closed meeting of the DIA Science and Technology 
Advisory Board has been scheduled as follows:

DATES: 10 September 1998 (800 am to 1600 pm).

ADDRESSES: The Defense Intelligence Agency, Bolling AFB, Washington, DC 
20340-5100.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Maj Donald R. Culp, USAF, Executive 
Secretary, DIA Science and Technology Advisory Board, Washington, D.C. 
20340-1328 (202) 231-4930.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The entire meeting is devoted to the 
discussion of classified information as defined in Section 
552(b)(c)(l), Title 5 of the U.S. Code and therefore will be closed to 
the public. The Board will receive briefings on and discuss several 
current critical intelligence issues and advise the Director, DIA, on 
related scientific and technical matters.

    Dated: August 24, 1998.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
