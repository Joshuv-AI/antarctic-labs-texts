SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster P032]


State of North Dakota (Amendment 2)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective July 
9, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to include 
McHenry and Pierce Counties and the Turtle Mountain Indian Reservation 
in the State of North Dakota as a disaster area due to damages caused 
by severe storms, flooding, and ground saturation occurring on March 
26, 2004, and continuing.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is July 6, 2004.
(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59008.)

    Dated: July 13, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
