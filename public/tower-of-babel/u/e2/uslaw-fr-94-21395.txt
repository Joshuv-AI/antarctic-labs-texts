DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

UNITED STATES PAROLE COMMISSION

 

Public Announcement

Pursuant To The Government In the Sunshine Act

(Public Law 94-409) [5 U.S.C. Section 552b]

DATE AND TIME: Wednesday, August 31, 1994, 2:00 p.m. (Eastern Standard 
Time).

PLACE: 5550 Friendship Boulevard, Chevy Chase, Maryland, 20815.

STATUS: Open--Meeting.

MATTER CONSIDERED: The following matter will be considered:

    (1) The selection of an effective date for the elimination of 
the Commission's South Central Region, and the reassignment of the 
states formerly in the South Central Region to other regions.

AGENCY CONTACT: TOM KOWALSKI, Case Operations, United States Parole 
Commission, (301) 492-5962.

    Dated: August 22, 1994.
Michael A. Stover,
General Counsel, U.S. Parole Commission.
