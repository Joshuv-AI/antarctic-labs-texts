FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-102; RM-8969]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Slidell and Kenner, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Phase II Broadcasting, 
reallots Channel 287C1 from Slidell to Kenner, Louisiana, and modifies 
Station WLTS-FM's license to specify Kenner as its community of 
license. See 62 FR 15869, April 3, 1997. Channel 287C1 can be allotted 
to Kenner in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements at the site specified in Station WLTS-FM's 
license. The coordinates for Channel 287C1 at Kenner are 29-58-57 NL 
and 89-57-09 WL. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 3, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
