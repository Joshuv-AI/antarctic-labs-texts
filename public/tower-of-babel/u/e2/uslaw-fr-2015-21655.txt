DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-29-2015]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 148--Knoxville, Tennessee; Authorization 
of Production Activity, CoLinx, LLC (Bearing Units), Crossville, 
Tennessee

    On April 29, 2015, the Industrial Development Board of Blount 
County and the Cities of Alcoa and Maryville, Tennessee, grantee of FTZ 
148, submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the 
FTZ Board on behalf of CoLinx, LLC, within Sites 2 and 6, in 
Crossville, Tennessee.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (80 FR 26539, 5-8-2015). The FTZ Board 
has determined that no further review of the activity is warranted at 
this time. The production activity described in the notification is 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the Board's regulations, 
including Section 400.14.

    Dated: August 27, 2015.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
