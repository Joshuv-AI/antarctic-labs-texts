DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Meeting

    Pursuant to section 10(a) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended, notice is hereby given of a meeting of the National Asthma 
Education Prevention Program Coordinating Committee.
    The meeting will be open to the public, with attendance limited to 
space available. Individuals who plan to attend and need special 
assistance, such as sign language interpretation or other reasonable 
accommodations, should notify the Contact Person listed below in 
advance of the meeting.

    Name of Committee: National Asthma Education Prevention Program 
Coordinating Committee.
    Date: July 9, 2021.
    Time: 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
    Agenda: To review and discuss asthma guidelines and 
implementation; updates on current and future activities. Discussion 
on programmatic updates.
