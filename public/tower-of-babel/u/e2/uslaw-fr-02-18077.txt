JTD, Incorporated, Tigard, OR; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called NAFTA-TAA and in accordance with section 
250(a), Subchapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended 
(19 USC 2331), an investigation was initiated on January 23, 2002 in 
response to a petition filed on the same date on behalf of workers at 
JTD, Incorporated, Tigard, Oregon.
    The company official submitting the petition has requested that the 
petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case 
would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 5th day of July 2002.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
