DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for National September 11 Memorial & Museum Commemorative 
Medal

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the price of the National 
September 11 Memorial & Museum Commemorative Medal. Introductory 
pricing will be $56.95, and regular pricing, which will go into effect 
approximately 60 days after the on-sale date, will be $66.95.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B. B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street, NW.; 
Washington, DC 20220, or call 202-354-7500.
