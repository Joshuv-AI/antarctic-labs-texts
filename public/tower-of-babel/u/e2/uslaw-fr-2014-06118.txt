DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby gives notice that

[[Page 15591]]

members of the Commission's staff will attend the following meeting 
related to the Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc. (MISO)--
PJM Interconnection, LLC (PJM) Joint and Common Market Initiative 
(Docket No. AD14-3-000): MISO/PJM Joint Stakeholder Meeting--March 21, 
2014

    The above-referenced meeting will be held at: MISO Headquarters, 
720 City Center Drive, Carmel, IN 46032-7574.
    The above-referenced meeting is open to the public.
    Further information may be found at www.misoenergy.org.
