POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Joint Briefing on Commission Functions and Greeting Card Industry 
Issues

AGENCY: Postal Rate Commission.

ACTION: Notice of briefing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On April 5, 2005, briefings on the Commission's role in rate 
setting and on greeting card industry issues will take place in the 
Commission's conference room. Participants will include Commissioners, 
greeting card industry executives, and staff.

DATES: April 5, 2005.

ADDRESSES: Postal Rate Commission, 1333 H Street, NW., Suite 300, 
Washington, DC 20268-0001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen L. Sharfman, General Counsel, 
202-789-6818.

    Dated: March 29, 2005.
Steven W. Williams,
Secretary.
