Bank of Ruston, Ruston, Louisiana; Approval of Conversion 
Application

Thrift Supervision approved the application of Bank of Ruston, Ruston, 
Louisiana, to convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of the 
application are available for inspection by appointment (phone number:

Reading Room, 1700 G Street, NW.,
