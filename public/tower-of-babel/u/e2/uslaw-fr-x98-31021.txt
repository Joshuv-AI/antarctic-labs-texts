Correction

    In rule document 98-27091 beginning on page 55040 in the issue of 
Wednesday, October 14, 1998, make the following correction:


217.7103-3  [Corrected]

    On page 55052, in the first column, after amendatory instruction 4. 
and the section heading name, paragraph (b) of section 217.7103-3 was 
omitted. Under the section heading insert paragraph (b) to read as 
follows:
``(b) Prepare the solicitation in the uniform contract format and in 
accordance with FAR Subpart 14.2 or 15.2, as applicable.
* * * * *''
