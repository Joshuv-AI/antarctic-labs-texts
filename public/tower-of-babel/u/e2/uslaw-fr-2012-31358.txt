Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council (Council) 
announces its intent to hold a workshop. The purpose of the workshop is 
to consider options for improving the management of the longfin and 
Illex squid fisheries, with a focus on responsive harvest strategies 
that account for changing stock conditions over the course of the year. 
The Council intends for managers, scientists, and fishermen to 
collaboratively consider if responsive harvest strategies are feasible 
and appropriate for optimizing yield in these fisheries. Discussions at 
the workshop will culminate in a workshop report and a series of 
follow-up port meetings that will inform consideration of future 
management actions.

DATES: The workshop will be held from 1 p.m. Tuesday, January 15, 2013 
until 1 p.m. Thursday, January 17, 2013.
