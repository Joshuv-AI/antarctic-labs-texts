Radiation Oncology Center at Marlton (ROCM) Marlton, NJ 
(Byproduct Materials License No. 29-28685-01); Notice of Prehearing 
Conference

    Notice is hereby given that a prehearing conference will be held in 
this enforcement proceeding, beginning at 9:30 a.m., on Wednesday, 
October 11, 1995, and continuing (to the extent necessary) at 9:00 
a.m., on Thursday, October 12, 1995, at Two White Flint North, 
Commission Hearing Room, Room 3 B 45, 11545 Rockville Pike, Rockville, 
Maryland. The purpose of the prehearing conference will be to define 
further the issues to be litigated during the proceeding, to establish 
discovery schedules, and to deal with other matters bearing upon the 
evidentiary hearing (such as the date, time and location of such 
hearing).
    Members of the public are invited to attend the conference but will 
not be permitted to participate in the proceeding.

    Rockville, Maryland, August 28, 1995.

    For the Atomic Safety and Licensing Board.
Charles Bechhoefer,
Chairman, Administrative Judge.
