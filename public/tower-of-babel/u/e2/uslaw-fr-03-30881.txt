FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-3854, MB Docket No. 02-222, RM-10491]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Spokane, WA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, by this document, dismisses a petition for 
rule making filed by KSKN Television, Inc., requesting the substitution 
of DTV channel 48 for station KSKN-TV's assigned DTV channel 36. See 67 
FR 52923, August 14, 2002. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

418-1600.
