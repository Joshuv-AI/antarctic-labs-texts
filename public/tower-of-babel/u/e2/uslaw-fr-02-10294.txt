NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Office of Polar Programs Advisory Committee

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Office of Polar Programs Advisory Committee (1130).
    Dates/Time: May 13, 2002; 8:30 am to 5 pm. May 14, 2002; 8:30 am 
to 2:00 pm.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Room 
1235, Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contract Person: Brenda Williams, Office of Polar Programs 
(OPP), National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, 
Arlington, VA 22230. (703) 292-8030.
    Minutes: May be obtained from the contact person list above.
    Purpose of Meeting: To advise NSF on the impact of its policies, 
programs and activities on the polar research community; to provide 
advice to the Director of OPP on issues related to long range 
planning, and to form ad hoc subcommittees to carry out needed 
studies and tasks.
    Agenda: Discussion of NSF-wide initiatives, long-range planning 
and GPRA.

    Dated: April 23, 2002
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
