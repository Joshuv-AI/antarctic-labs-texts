Meadwestvaco Corporation Consumer and Office Products Division, 
Enfield, CT; Notice of Termination of Investigation on Reconsideration

    By application dated April 1, 2009, the petitioners requested 
administrative reconsideration of the Department's negative 
determination regarding eligibility for workers and former workers of 
MeadWestvaco Corporation, Consumer and Office Products Division, 
Enfield, Connecticut (subject firm) to apply for Trade Adjustment 
Assistance (TAA) and Alternative Trade Adjustment Assistance (ATAA). 
The Department's Notice of Affirmative Determination Regarding 
Application for Reconsideration was signed on April 7, 2009, and 
published in the Federal Register on April 16, 2009 (74 FR 17691).
    The petitioners have requested that the request for reconsideration 
be withdrawn. Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of April 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
