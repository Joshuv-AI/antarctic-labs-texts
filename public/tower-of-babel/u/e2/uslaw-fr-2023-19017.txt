DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Dental and Craniofacial Research Council, September 12, 2023, 
4:45 p.m. to September 13, 2023, 3:30 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, Natcher Building, 45 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892, which 
was published in the Federal Register on August 17, 2023, FR Document 
2023-17712, 88 FR 56028.
    The National Advisory Dental and Craniofacial Research Council Open 
and Closed Sessions meeting room has changed from the Natcher Building, 
45 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892, and will now be held at the John 
Edward Porter Neuroscience Research Center Building, 35 Convent Drive 
Room 620/630, Bethesda, MD 20892. Meeting times remain the same. The 
meeting is partially closed to the public.

    Dated: August 29, 2023.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
