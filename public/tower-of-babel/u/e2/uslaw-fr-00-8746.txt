LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Meeting of the Board of Directors 
Operations and Regulations Committee

TIME AND DATE: The Operations and Regulations Committee of the Legal 
Services Corporation's Board of Directors will meet on April 14, 2000. 
The meeting will begin at 2:30 p.m. and continue until the Committee 
concludes its agenda.

LOCATION: Marriott Wardman Park Hotel, 2660 Woodley Road, NW, 
Washington, DC 20008.

STATUS OF MEETING:  Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:  
    1. Approval of agenda.
    2. Consider and act on proposed Operations and Regulations 
Committee procedures.
    3. Consider and act on other business.
    4. Public Comment.

CONTACT PERSON FOR INFORMATION: Victor M. Fortuno, Vice President for 
Legal Affairs, General Counsel and Secretary of the Corporation, at 
(202) 336-8800.

SPECIAL NEEDS: Upon request, meeting notices will be made available in 
alternate formats to accommodate visual and hearing impairments. 
Individuals who have a disability and need an accommodation to attend 
the meeting may notify Shannon Nicko Adaway, at (202) 336-8800.

    Dated: April 4, 2000.
Victor M. Fortuno,
Vice President for Legal Affairs, General Counsel and Corporate 
Secretary.
