Tuscadora Yarns, Inc., Mt. Pleasant, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 9, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Tuscarora 
Yarns, Inc., Mt. Pleasant, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 9th day of January, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
