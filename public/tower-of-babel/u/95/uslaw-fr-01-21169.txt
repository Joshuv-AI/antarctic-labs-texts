DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Advisory Circular 25.1435-1, Hydraulic System Certification Tests 
and Analysis

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance of advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Advisory Circular (AC) 
25.1435-1, Hydraulic System Certification Tests and Analysis. This AC 
provides guidance material for use as an acceptable means, but not the 
only means, of demonstrating compliance with the airworthiness 
standards for transport category airplanes that contain hydraulic 
system requirements. It is not mandatory and does not constitute a 
regulation.

DATES: Advisory Circular 25.1435-1 was issued by the Acting Manager, 
Transport Airplane Directorate, Aircraft Certification Service, ANM-
100, on May 21, 2001.
