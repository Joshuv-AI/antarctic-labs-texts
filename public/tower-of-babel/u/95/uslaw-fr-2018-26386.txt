DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases; 
Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National 
Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, December 6, 2018, 6:00 p.m. to December 7, 2018, 5:00 
p.m., Residence Inn Arlington Pentagon City, 550 Army Navy Drive, 
Arlington, VA, 22202 which was published in the Federal Register on 
October 26, 2018, FR Doc. 23393.
    This meeting has been cancelled and will be rescheduled.

    Dated: November 29, 2018.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
