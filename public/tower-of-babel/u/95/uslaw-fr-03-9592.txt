OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act; Meeting

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (Volume 68, Number 62, Page 15773) on April 1, 
2003. No requests were received to provide testimony or submit written 
statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in 
conjunction with OPIC's April 24, 2003 Board of Directors meeting 
scheduled for 2 pm on April 17, 2003 has been cancelled.

CONTACT PERSON FOR INFORMATION: Information on the hearing cancellation

at (202) 218-0136, or via e-mail at [email protected].
