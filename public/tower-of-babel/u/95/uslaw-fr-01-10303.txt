DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Second Supplemental Record of Decision (ROD) for Reuse and 
Disposal at Lowry Air Force Base (AFB), CO

    The Second Supplemental ROD for Lowry AFB changes four decisions 
made in the original Lowry AFB ROD signed on August 1, 1994. Three of 
the changes involve conveying Parcels F, FF, and S to the Lowry 
Redevelopment Authority to be included in their Economic Development 
Conveyance. The fourth change is to assign Parcel MM to the Department 
of Health and Human Services for their conveyance of the property to 
Third Way Center.

Janet A. Long,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
