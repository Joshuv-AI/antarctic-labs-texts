DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. ER95-1050-000]


Sonat Power Marketing, Inc; Notice of Filing

August 7, 1995.

    Take notice that on June 13, 1995, Sonat Power Marketing, Inc. 
tendered for filing an amendment in the above-referenced docket.

    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street NE., Washington, DC 
20426, in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules 
of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before August 11, 1995. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this application are on file with 
the Commission and are available for public inspection.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,

Acting Secretary.
