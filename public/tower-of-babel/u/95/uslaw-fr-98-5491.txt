SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3049]


State of Tennessee; Amendment #3

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated February 12, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is 
hereby amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as 
beginning on January 6, 1998 and continuing through February 12, 1998.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is March 13, 1998 and for 
economic injury the deadline is October 13, 1998.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: February 20, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
