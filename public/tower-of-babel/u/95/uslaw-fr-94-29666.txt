DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Office of the Secretary

 

Additions to the Senior Executive Service Performance Review 
Board Membership

    Title 5, U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), of the Civil Service Reform Act of 
1978, Public Law 95-484, requires that the appointment of Performance 
Review Board members be published in the Federal Register.
    On November 21, 1994, the Department of Health and Human Services' 
PRB membership was published in the Federal Register. The following 
members are hereby added to that membership:

Walter D. Broadnax
Kathleen A. Buto
Paul M. Schwab

    Dated: November 25, 1994.
Thomas S. McFee,
Assistant Secretary for Personnel Administration.
