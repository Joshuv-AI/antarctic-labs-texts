GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION

41 CFR Chapters 300 and 301

RIN 3090-AG25


Federal Travel Regulation; General Guides and Temporary Duty 
(TDY) Travel Allowances

AGENCY: Office of Government wide Policy, GSA.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to a final rule on Federal 
Travel Regulations appearing in the Federal Register of Wednesday, 
April 1, 1998 (63 FR 15950). The rule added a new Chapter 300, and 
revised Chapter 301, except Appendixes A and B.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 1, 1998.
