Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Staff Attendance at Southwest Power Pool Board of 
Directors Meetings

members of its staff may attend the meetings of the Southwest Power 
Pool, Inc. (SPP) Board of Directors, as noted below. Their attendance 
is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach efforts.

Las Colinas Boulevard, Irving, TX 75039.

p.m.).
