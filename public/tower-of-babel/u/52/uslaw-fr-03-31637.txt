DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Notice of Mineral County Resource Advisory Committee Meeting

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the authorities in the Federal Advisory Committee 
Act (Pub. L. 92-463) and under the Secure Rural Schools and Community 
Self-Determination Act of 2000 (Pub. L. 106-393) the Lolo National 
Forest's Mineral County Resource Advisory Committee will meet on 
January 6, 2004 at 6 p.m. until 7 p.m. in Superior, Montana for a 
business meeting. The meeting is open to the public.

DATES: January 6, 2004.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Mineral County Courthouse, 
300 River Street, Superior, MT 59872.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Harper, Designated Forest 
Official (DFO), District Ranger, Lolo National Forest, at (406) 822-
4233.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Agenda topics for this meeting include 
nominations and election of a new Committee Chairperson, review of 
Project Funding Criteria agreed to last year, and discussion or review 
about new project proposals, as authorized under title II of Public Law 
106-393. If the meeting location is changed, notice will be posted in 
local newspapers, including the Mineral Independent and the Missoulian.

    Dated: December 12, 2003.
Deborah L.R. Austin,
Designated Federal Official.
