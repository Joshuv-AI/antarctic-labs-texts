FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-1249; MM Docket No. 01-69; RM-10081]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Parker, AZ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 66 FR 17843 
(April 4, 2001), this document allots Channel 247C3 to Parker, Arizona, 
and provides Parker with its fourth local aural transmission service. 
The coordinates for Channel 247C3 at Parker are 34-03--11 North 
Latitude and 114-17-18 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective July 8, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
