DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Agency Information Collection Under OMB Review

    Title: National Evaluation of Runaway & Homeless Youth--Follow-Up 
Study.
    PMB No.: 0980-0217.
    Description: This evaluation of the services and long-term effects 
of Services provided by runaway and homeless youth centers, will be 
used by program managers and child welfare planning and administrative 
staff, to improve their overall effectiveness.
    Respondents: Individuals or households.
