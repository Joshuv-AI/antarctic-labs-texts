Correction of Publication

    In the final rule beginning on page 42922 in the issue of Friday, 
August 19, 1994, the following corrections are being made:


Appendix I to Part 121  [Corrected]

    1. On page 42928, first column, paragraph I, fourth line, the word 
``complies'' is corrected to read ``comply''.
    2. On page 42932, second column, paragraph No. 6, seventh line, the 
word ``problems'' is corrected to read ``programs''.
