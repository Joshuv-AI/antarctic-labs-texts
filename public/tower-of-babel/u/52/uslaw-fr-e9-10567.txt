Maine Woods Company, Portage Lake, ME; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 4, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Maine 
Woods Company, Portage Lake, Maine.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 8th day of April 2009.
 Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
