Tai Ping Carpets Americas, Formerly Known as Edward Fields, 
Incorporated, a Divison of Tai Ping Carpets Americas, Inc., College 
Point, NY; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on July 18, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Tai Ping Carpets Americas, 
formerly known as Edward Fields, Inc., a division of Tai Ping Carpets 
Americas, Inc., College Point, New York.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification (TA-W-57,805) which expires on September 23, 2007. 
Further investigation in this case would duplicate efforts and serve no 
purpose; therefore the investigation under this petition has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 18th day of August 2006.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
