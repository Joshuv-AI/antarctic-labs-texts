DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, February 22, 2018, 12:00 p.m. 
to February 22, 2018, 03:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on January 24, 2018, 83 FR 3351.

February 22, 2018 to March 21, 2018. The meeting is closed to the 
public.
