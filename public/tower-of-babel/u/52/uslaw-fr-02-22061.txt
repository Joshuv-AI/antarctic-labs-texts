National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

Institute Director's Consumer Liaison Group, September 5, 2002, 2 p.m. 
to September 5, 2002, 4 p.m., 6116 Executive Blvd, Rockville, MD, 20852 
which was published in the Federal Register on August 16, 2002, 
67FR535392.
