DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Closing and Filing Deadlines

    Take notice that on Friday, June 18, 2021, the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission will be closed in observance of Juneteenth 
National Independence Day.
    On June 17, 2021, the President of the United States signed into 
law the Juneteenth National Independence Day Act, establishing June 19 
as a federal holiday. Because June 19, 2021, falls on a Saturday, the 
holiday will be observed this year on June 18, 2021.
    In accordance with section 385.2007 of the Commission's rules, 18 
CFR 385.2007 (2020), all filings and documents due to be filed on 
Friday, June 18, 2021, will be accepted as timely on the next official 
business day.

    Dated: June 17, 2021.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
