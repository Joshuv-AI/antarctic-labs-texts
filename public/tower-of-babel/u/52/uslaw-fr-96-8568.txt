20892, (301) 435-1783.

    Place: Holiday Inn, Chevy Chase, MD.
    Contact Person: Dr. Ramesh Nayak, Scientific Review 
Administrator, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Room 5146, Bethesda, Maryland

set forth in sections 552b(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C. 
Applications and/or proposals and the discussions could reveal
