DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
Fish and Wildlife Service


Receipt of Applications for Permits Under Section 10(a)(1)(A) of 
the Endangered Species Act

AGENCY: Fish and Wildlife Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of receipt of applications for permits.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The following applicants have applied for a permit to conduct 
certain activities with endangered species. This notice is provided 
pursuant to section 10(c) of the Endangered Species Act of 1973, as 
amended (16 U.S.C., et seq.).

Permit No. PRT-795928

    Applicant: Wetlands Research Associates Inc., San Rafael, 
California.
    The applicant requests a permit to take (harass by surveying nests, 
band) the California clapper rail (Sterna antillarum [=albifrons] 
browni) in San Diego County, California, to determine the reproductive 
success of nests and monitor individuals of the species for the purpose 
of enhancement of the survival of the species.

Permit No. PRT-787037

    Applicant: Branchiopod Research Group, University of San Diego, San 
Diego, California.
    The applicant requests amendment of their permit to include take 
(harass by survey, collect and sacrifice) of eggs and hatched 
individuals of the conservancy fairy shrimp (Branchinecta conservatio), 
longhorn fairy shrimp (Branchinecta longiantenna), and vernal pool 
tadpole shrimp (Lepidurus packardi) to determine presence or absence of 
the species, and conduct genetic, life history, development, 
physiology, and behavior studies on the species from vernal pools 
throughout the species' range in California for the purpose of 
scientific research.
