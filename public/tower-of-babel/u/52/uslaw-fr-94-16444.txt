NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION
 

NUREG-1021, Revision 7, Supplement 1, ``Operator Licensing 
Examiner Standards''; Availability

    The Nuclear Regulatory Commission has issued Supplement 1 to 
Revision 7 of NUREG-1021, ``Operator Licensing Examiner Standards.'' 
The Commission uses this document to provide policy and guidelines for 
NRC examiners and to establish the procedures and practices for 
conducting initial and requalification written examinations and 
operating tests for licensed applicants and licensed operators at power 
reactor facilities pursuant to Part 55 of Title 10 of the Code of 
Federal Regulations. The policies and guidelines contained in the 
supplement will be used for all examinations commencing 30 days from 
the date of this notice.
    Copies of NUREG-1021, Revision 7, Supplement 1, may be purchased 
from the Superintendent of Documents, U.S. Government Printing Office, 
P.O. Box 37082, Washington, DC 20013-7082. Copies are also available 
from the National Technical Information Service, 5285 Port Royal Road, 
Springfield, VA 22161. A copy is also available for inspection and/or 
copying for a fee in the NRC Public Document Room, 2120 L Street, NW. 
(Lower Level), Washington, DC.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 21st day of June 1994.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Robert M. Gallo,
Chief, Operator Licensing Branch, Division of Reactor Controls and 
Human Factors, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
