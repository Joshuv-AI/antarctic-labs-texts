Avenue, Room 9C10, Bethesda, MD 20892, (301) 496-9223.

    Contact Person: Dr. Paul Sheehy, Scientific Review 
Administrator, Scientific Review Branch, NINDS, 7550 Wisconsin 
Avenue, Room 9C10, Bethesda, MD 20892, (301) 496-9223.
    Purpose/Agenda: To review and evaluate one grant application.
