DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP86-168-022]


Columbia Gas Transmission Corporation; Notice of Refund Report

October 26, 1995.
    Take notice that on August 21, 1995, Columbia Gas Transmission 
Corporation (Columbia) tendered for filing with the Commission a report 
summarizing refunds flowed through to customers in the form of credits 
to invoices issued on or around July 10, 1995, which were payable to 
Columbia on or before July 20, 1995, that portion of the refund 
received by Columbia from Wyoming Interstate Company in Docket No. 
RP85-39 applicable to the period 4/1/1987-3/31/1990. Columbia states 
that interest was included in the amount refunded to each customer 
calculated through July 19, 1995, in accordance with Section 
154.67(c)(2) of the Commission's Regulations. Columbia states that this 
refund is being flowed through in accordance with Article I Section C 
of Columbia's Global Settlement in Docket No. RP86-168-020.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Rule 211 of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211). All such protests 
should be filed on or before November 2, 1995. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
