Ketcham Diversified Tooling, Inc., Cambridge Springs, PA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 25, 2002, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by the company on behalf of workers at Ketcham Diversified 
Tooling, Inc., Cambridge Springs, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 11th day of April, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
