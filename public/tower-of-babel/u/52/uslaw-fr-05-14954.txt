FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 05-1999; MB Docket No. 05-135, RM-11215]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Jackson and Madison, MS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of New South Communications, Inc., licensee of 
Station WUSJ(FM), Jackson, Mississippi, the Audio Division reallots 
Channel 242C0 from Jackson to Madison, Mississippi as the community's 
first

[[Page 44515]]

local transmission service, and modifies the license for Station WUSJ 
(FM) to reflect the new community. See 70 FR 19396, April 13, 2005. 
Channel 242C0 is reallotted at Madison at petitioner's site 24.0 
kilometers (14.9 miles) southwest of the community at coordinates 32-
11-29 NL and 90-24-22 WL.

DATES: Effective August 29, 2005.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria M. McCauley, Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
