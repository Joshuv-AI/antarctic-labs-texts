SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Purpose of the Workshop

    The Department will present its methodology and results pertaining 
to the analysis completed to date (Supplemental ANOPR analysis), answer 
questions to help participants prepare their written comments on the 
Supplemental ANOPR, and entertain comments on that document. The 
Department will also explain its plans for the remaining analysis (NOPR 
analysis) and seek comments and suggestions for improving its 
methodologies, assumptions, and data sources.
