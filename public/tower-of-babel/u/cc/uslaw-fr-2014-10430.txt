Version 5 Critical Infrastructure Protection Reliability 
Standards; Supplemental Notice Concerning Post Technical Conference 
Comments

    As announced during the Commission staff convened technical 
conference held on April 29, 2014 to discuss issues related to Critical 
Infrastructure Protection Issues Identified in Order No. 791, parties 
may choose to file post-technical conference comments. Parties wishing 
to file comments on the matters discussed at the technical conference 
should do so on the following schedule:
    Comments: Due on or before May 21, 2014.

    Dated: April 30, 2014.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
