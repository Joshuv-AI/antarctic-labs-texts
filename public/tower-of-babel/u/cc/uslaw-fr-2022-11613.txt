CFR Correction

    This rule is being published by the Office of the Federal Register 
to correct an editorial or technical error that appeared in the most 
recent annual revision of the Code of Federal Regulations.

0
In Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 1900 to 1910.999, 
revised as of July 1, 2021, in Sec.  1910.95, in section II of appendix 
A, remove the term ``TWA 6.61'' in the formula and add the term 
``TWA=16.61'' in its place.
