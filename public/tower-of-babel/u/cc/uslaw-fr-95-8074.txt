DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[PRT-800458]


Receipt of Application(s) for Permit

    The following applicant has applied for a permit to conduct certain 
activities with endangered species. This notice is provided pursuant to 
Section 10(c) of the Endangered Species Act of 1973, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1531, et seq.)
    Applicant: Mark D. Hovorka, Department of Biology, York University, 
North York, Ontario, Canada.
    The applicant requests a permit to include take of the lesser long-
nosed bat (Leptonycteris curasoae yerbabuenae) for the purpose of 
scientific research and enhancement of propagation and survival of the 
species as prescribed by Service recovery documents.

ADDRESSES: Written data or comments should be submitted to the 
Assistant Regional Director, Ecological Services, U.S. Fish and 
Wildlife Service, P.O. Box 1306, Albuquerque, New Mexico 87103, and 
must be received by the Assistant Regional Director within 30 days from 
the date of this publication.
    Documents and other information submitted with this application are 
available for review, subject to the requirements of the Privacy Act 
and Freedom of Information Act, by any party who submits a written 
request for a copy of such documents to the above [[Page 16883]] office 
within 30 days of the date of publication of this notice. (See 
ADDRESSES above.)
James A. Young,
Acting Regional Director, Region 2, Albuquerque, New Mexico.
