GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Regulation and Program Development Division; Cancellation of a 
Standard Form

AGENCY: Federal Supply Service, General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the General Services Administration's 
intent to cancel the following Standard form because of low user 
demand:
    SF 1203, U.S. Government Billing of Lading-Privately Owned Personal 
Property (7-part snapout version) (identified by NSN 7540-01-082-0589). 
The 7-part continous feed version of this form is still available from 
FSS.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: General Services Administration, Form 
Management, (202) 501-0581.

DATES: Effective March 13, 2000.

    Dated: February 28, 2000.
Barbara M. Williams,
Deputy Standard and Optional Forms Management Officer.
