FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2386, MM Docket No. 01-160, RM-10159]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Albuquerque, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of ACME Television Licenses of 
New Mexico, LLC, substitutes DTV channel 45 for DTV channel 51c at 
Albuquerque, New Mexico. See 66 FR 40174, August 2, 2001. DTV channel 
45 can be allotted to Albuquerque, New Mexico, in compliance with the 
principle community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at 
reference coordinates (35-12-48 N. and 106-27-00 W.) with a power of 
245, HAAT of 1287 meters and with a DTV service population of 759 
thousand. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 3, 2001.
