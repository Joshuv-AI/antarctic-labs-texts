SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Purpose of the Committee

    The National Electric and Magnetic Fields Advisory Committee 
(NEMFAC) advises the Department of Energy and the National Institute of 
Environmental Health Sciences (NIEHS) on the design and implementation 
of a five-year, national electric and magnetic fields (EMF) research 
and public information dissemination (RAPID) program. The Secretary of 
Energy, pursuant to Section 2118 of the Energy Policy Act of 1992, Pub. 
L. 102-486, has overall responsibility for establishing the national 
program which includes health effects research, development of 
technologies to assess and manage exposures, and dissemination of 
information.
