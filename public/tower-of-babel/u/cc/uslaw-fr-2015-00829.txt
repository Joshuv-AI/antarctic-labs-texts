DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


U.S. Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces Code Committee; 
Meeting

ACTION: Notice of Public Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the forthcoming public meeting of the 
Code Committee.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: William A. DeCicco, Clerk of Court, 
United States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces, 450 E Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20442-0001, telephone (202) 761-1448.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Code Committee was established by 
Article 146(a), Uniform Code of Military Justice, 10 U.S.C. 946(a), to 
be held at the Courthouse of the United States Court of Appeals for the 
Armed Forces, 450 E Street NW., Washington, DC 20442-0001, at 10:00 
a.m. on Tuesday, March 3, 2015. The agenda for this meeting will 
include consideration of proposed changes to the Uniform Code of 
Military Justice and the Manual for Courts-Martial, United States, and 
other matters relating to the operation of the Uniform Code of Military 
Justice throughout the Armed Forces.

    Dated: January 14, 2015.
Aaron Siegel,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
