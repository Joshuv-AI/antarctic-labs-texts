DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Notice of Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 7 & 8 February 1996.
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1700, 7 February 1996; 0930-1700, 8 
February 1996.

[[Page 4259]]

    Place: Pentagon--Washington, DC.

Agenda

    The Army Science Board (ASB) Ad Hoc Study on ``Army Digitization 
Information Systems Vulnerabilities and Security'' will meet for two 
days to hear selected briefings on the study subject. These meetings 
will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 552b(c) of 
title 5, U.S.C., specifically subparagraph (4) thereof, and Title 5, 
U.S.C., Appendix 2, subsection 10(d). The proprietary matters to be 
discussed are so inextricably intertwined so as to preclude opening 
any portion of these meetings. For further information, please 
contact Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
