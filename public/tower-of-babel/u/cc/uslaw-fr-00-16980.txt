SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region IV Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The Southeastern States Regulatory Fairness Board will hold a 
public hearing on August 1, 2000, at 9 a.m. located at Tougaloo 
College, Health and Wellness Center, 500 W. County Line Road, Tougaloo, 
Mississippi to receive comments and testimony from small businesses and 
representatives of trade associations concerning federal regulatory 
enforcement or compliance actions taken by federal agencies. 
Transcripts of these proceedings will be posted on the Internet. These 
transcripts are subject only to limited review by the National 
Ombudsman. For further information, call Elestine Harvey (312) 353-
1744.

Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administrator/Public Liaison.
