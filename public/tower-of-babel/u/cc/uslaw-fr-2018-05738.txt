DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, April 26, 2018, 10:00 a.m. to 
April 26, 2018, 06:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 9609 
Medical Center Drive, Room 7W106, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on February 05, 2018, 83 FR 5113.
    The meeting has been amended to change the start time from 10:00 
a.m. to 11:00 a.m. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 16, 2018.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
