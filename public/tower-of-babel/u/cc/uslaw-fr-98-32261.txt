TransTexas Gas Corporation, Houston, Texas; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 28, 1998, in response to a petition filed on 
the same date on behalf of workers at TransTexas Gas Corporation, 
Houston, Texas.
    The company official submitting the petition has requested that the 
petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case 
would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 9th day of November, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
