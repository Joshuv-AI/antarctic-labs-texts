DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Public Outreach Meeting

November 17, 1998.
    Between the years 2000 and 2010, a number of hydropower project 
licenses in California will expire. We have scheduled a public outreach 
meeting in Sacramento, California for the purpose of discussing 
concerns and experiences stakeholders may have in using an alternative 
process in licensing hydropower projects. The agenda is as follows:
    Place: Sterling Hotel, 1300 H Street, Sacramento, California 95814.
    Date: Wednesday, December 9, 1998.
    Time: 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
    The Sterling Hotel is located in downtown Sacramento at the 
junction of 13th Street and ``H'' Street. From the Sacramento Airport, 
it is about a 15 minute drive. From the Airport take Interstate 5 South 
to the ``Old Sacramento J Street'' off-ramp. Go on J Street and make a 
left onto 13th. Take 13th to H Street. There is a parking garage 
between ``J'' and ``I'' Streets on 13th.
    For further information, please contact Theresa Gibson at (202) 
219-2793.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
