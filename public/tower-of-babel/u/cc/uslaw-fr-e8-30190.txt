COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Correction of Notice of Quarterly Update of A-List and Movement 
of Products Between the A-List, B-List and C-List

    In the notice appearing on page 72445, FR Doc E8-28252, 
Clarification of Scope of Procurement List Additions, on November 28, 
2008, the Committee published a list of products and NSNs that moved 
from B-List to A-List.
    This notice adds two additional products and NSNs (Marker, Tube 
Type, Blue-7520-01-511-4319) and (Marker, Tube Type, Red-7520-01-511-
4324) that moved from B-List to A-List.

Barry S. Lineback,
Sr. Program Analyst.
