    b. Place: District Courtroom, Swain County Administration Building, 
101 Mitchell Street, Bryson City, North Carolina 28713-2321.
    c. FERC Contact: Lee Emery at (202) 502-8379 or [email protected].
    d. Purpose of Meeting: Discuss the effects of continued operation 
of the Bryson Project on Tribal fishing and boating rights on the
