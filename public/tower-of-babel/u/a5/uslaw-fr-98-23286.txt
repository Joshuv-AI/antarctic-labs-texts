RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting

    The meeting of the Railroad Retirement Board which was to be held 
on August 26, 1998, 9:00 a.m., at the Board's meeting room on the 8th 
floor of its headquarters building, 844 North Rush Street, Chicago, 
Illinois 60611, has been rescheduled to September 22, 1998, at 9:00 
a.m. The agenda for this meeting was published at 63 FR 44479 on August 
19, 1998.
    The entire meeting will be open to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: August 25, 1998.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
