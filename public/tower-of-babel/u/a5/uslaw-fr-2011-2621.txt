Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XL; FFP Missouri 4, LLC; Allegheny 4 
Hydro, LLC; Three Rivers Hydro, LLC; Notice Announcing Filing Priority 
for Preliminary Permit Applications

January 28, 2011.
    On January 27, 2011, the Commission held a drawing to determine 
priority between four competing preliminary permit applications with 
identical filing times. In the event that the Commission concludes that 
none of the applicants' plans are better adapted than the others to 
develop, conserve, and utilize in the public interest the water 
resources of the region at issue, the priority established by this 
drawing will serve as the tiebreaker. Based on the drawing, the order 
of priority is as follows:

1. Three Rivers Hydro, LLC--Project No. 13782-000
2. FFP Missouri 4, LLC--Project No. 13750-000
3. Allegheny 4 Hydro, LLC--Project No. 13776-000
4. Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XL--Project No. 13746-000

Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
