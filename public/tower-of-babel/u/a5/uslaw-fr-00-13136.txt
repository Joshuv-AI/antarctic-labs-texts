Radio Broadcasting Services; Medina, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for rule making filed by 
Medina Radio Broadcasting Company requesting the allotment of Channel 
296A at Medina, Texas. See 64 FR 36324, July 6, 1999. This document in 
this proceeding questioned community status and requested commenting 
parties to present the Commission with information demonstrating 
community status. Based on the totality of evidence submitted, we do 
not believe that Medina qualifies as a community for allotment purposes 
and that it would not serve the public interest to make a channel 
allotment in response to Medina Radio's proposal.
