Revocation of Marine Control Surveyors, Inc. Customs Gauger 
Approval

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of Revocation of Customs Gauger Approval.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Marine Control Surveyors, Inc., of Groves, Texas, a Customs 
approved gauger, under Section 151.13 of the Customs Regulations (19 
CFR 151.13), was found not operating in compliance with Customs laws 
and regulations. Specifically, Marine Control Surveyors, Inc. does not 
have a valid bond filed with Customs as required under Section 
151.13(b)(8) of the Customs Regulations. Accordingly, pursuant to 
151.13(k) of the Customs Regulations, notice is hereby given that the 
Customs commercial gauger approval of Marine Control Surveyors, Inc. 
has been revoked with prejudice.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 30, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Michael Parker, Science Officer,
