FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 95-135; RM-8681]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Honor, MI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the petition filed by Roger L. Hoppe, II 
(``Hoppe'') for reconsideration of the Report and Order in MM Docket 
No. 95-135, 61 FR 24243, May 14, 1996. The Report and Order allotted 
Channel 264A to Honor, Michigan, as a first local service and denied 
Hoppe's one step application and counterproposal as untimely to 
substitute Channel 264C2 for Channel 261A at Bear Lake, Michigan. The 
Commission has affirmed the action taken in the Report and Order that a 
first local service at Honor, Michigan, will better serve the public 
interest than expanded service at Bear Lake, Michigan.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 2, 1997.
