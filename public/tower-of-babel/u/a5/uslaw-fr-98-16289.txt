SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region I Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U. S. Small Business Administration Region I Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Boston, will hold a public meeting 
at 10:00 a.m. on Tuesday, June 16, 1998, at the Boston District Office, 
Room 265, to discuss such matters as may be presented by members and 
staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For Further Information, please write or call Ms. Mary E. McAleney, 
District, U. S. Small Business Administration, 10 Causeway Street, Room 
265, Boston, Massachusetts 02222-1093, telephone (617) 566-5560.

    Dated: June 15, 1998.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
