Filtex Incorporated, Guntersville, AL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 21, 2003 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the company on behalf of workers at Filtex 
Incorporated, Guntersville, Alabama.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 4th day of April, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
