Golden Parachute Payments and Indemnification Payments

AGENCY: Federal Housing Finance Agency.

ACTION: Correcting Amendments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to Sec. Sec.  1231.3 and 
1231.4 of the interim final regulation concerning Golden Parachute 
Payments and Indemnification Payments published in the Federal Register 
on Tuesday, September 16, 2008. These sections should read 
``Reserved.''

DATES: Effective Date: September 19, 2008.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Alfred M. Pollard, General Counsel 
(OFHEO), telephone (202) 414-3788 or Christopher Curtis, General 
Counsel (FHFB), telephone (202) 408-2802 (not toll-free numbers), 
Federal Housing Finance Agency, Fourth Floor, 1700 G Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20552. The telephone number for the Telecommunications 
Device for the Deaf is (800) 877-8339.

Need for Correction

    As published on September 16, 2008, the interim final regulation 
contained clerical errors, which these amendments correct.
