Cooper Wiring Devices, Long Island City, NY; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 15, 2005 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the United Auto Workers Local 365 on behalf of 
workers at Cooper Wiring Devices, Long Island City, New York.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of May, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
