Bulk Manufacturer of Controlled Substances Application: Cambridge 
Isotope Lab; Correction

AGENCY: Drug Enforcement Administration, Justice.

ACTION: Notice of application; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) published a document 
in the Federal Register of September 14, 2020, concerning a notice of 
application. The document contained a misspelling (Isotype vs. 
Isotope).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Correction

    In the Federal Register of September 14, 2020, in FR Doc. 2020-
20160 (85 FR 56633), on page 56633-56634, correct all instances of the 
registrant name to read Cambridge Isotope Lab.

William T. McDermott,
Assistant Administrator.
