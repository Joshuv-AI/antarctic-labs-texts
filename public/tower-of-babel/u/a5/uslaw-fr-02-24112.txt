Employment and Training Administration

Johanna York, Inc., New York, NY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 20, 2002 in response to a worker petition, which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Johanna York, Inc., New York, New 
York.
    The Department has been unable to locate or communicate with 
principals of the firm or otherwise obtain information to reach a 
determination on worker eligibility. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 19th day of August 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
