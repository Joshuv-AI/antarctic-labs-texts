NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

Commonwealth Edison Company, Zion Nuclear Power Station, Units 1 
and 2; Correction to Director's Decision Under 10 CFR 2.206 (DD-95-09)

    In the Notice beginning on page 28808 in the issue of Friday, June 
2, 1995, make the following correction:
    On page 28811, Section E. Potential Threats, in the second 
paragraph, the fourth sentence should read:
    On a daily basis, the staff evaluates threat-related information to 
ensure the design basis threat statements in the regulations remain a 
valid basis for safeguards system design.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission,
Clyde Y. Shiraki,
Project Manager, Project Directorate III-2, Division of Reactor 
Projects III/IV, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
