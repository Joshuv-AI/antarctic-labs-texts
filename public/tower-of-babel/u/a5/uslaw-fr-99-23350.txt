POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Notice of Visit

AGENCY: Postal Rate Commission.

ACTION: Notice of visit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A member of the recent Postal Service/Industry Periodicals 
task force, Rita Cohen, of the Magazine Publishers of America, will 
visit the Commission and present a briefing describing the analysis and 
conclusions of the task force. The briefing will begin at 10:00 a.m.

DATES: The date of the visit is Wednesday, September 15, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Postal Rate Commission (Hearing Room), 1333 H Street, NW., 
Suite 300, Washington, DC 20268-0001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen L. Sharfman, General Counsel, 
Postal Rate Commission, Suite 300, 1333 H Street, NW., Washington, DC 
20268-0001 (202) 789-6820.

    Dated: September 2, 1999.
Margaret P. Crenshaw,
Secretary.
