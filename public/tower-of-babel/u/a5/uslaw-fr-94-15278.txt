DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
 

Bureau of Land Management Notice of Realty Action

[UT-050-4320-01; UTU-63484]
AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management.

ACTION: Notice of realty action UTU-63484; noncompetitive sale of 
public land in Sampete County, Utah.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The following, legally described public land has been examined 
and found suitable for direct sale under Section 203 of the Federal 
Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (90 Stat. 2750, 43 U.S.C. 1713) 
at not less than the appraised fair market value of $6,000.00. The land 
will not be offered by direct sale until 60 days after the date of 
publication of this notice. The land was previously offered by modified 
competitive sale in 1983 but was not sold. The land is now being 
reoffered for sale.

Salt Lake Meridian, Utah

T. 14 S., R. 2 E.,
    Sec. 25, SE\1/4\NW\1/4\.

    Containing approximately 40 acres.

    The land described is hereby segregated from appropriation under 
the public lands laws, including the mining laws, pending disposition 
of this action or 270 days from the date of publication of this notice, 
whichever occurs first. The public land described above is being 
offered by direct sale to Garth W. Christensen.
    The land has been identified for disposal in the Richfield 
District's land use plan. The public land has no legal or public 
access, is isolated and disconnected from other public lands and is 
difficult and uneconomic to manage as part of the public lands system 
and is not suitable for management by another Federal department or 
agency. Garth W. Christensen has ownership in private lands, which are 
adjacent to the subject parcel, on three sides.
    The patent, when issued, will contain certain reservations to the 
United States. Detailed information concerning these reservations as 
well as specific conditions of the sale are available for review at the 
Richfield District Office, Bureau of Land Management, 150 East 900 
North, Richfield, Utah 84701.
    For a period of 45 days from the date of publication of this notice 
in the Federal Register, interested parties may submit comments to the 
District Manager, Richfield District, at the above address. In the 
absence of timely objections, this proposal shall become the final 
determination of the Department of the Interior.
    Beginning September 15, 1994, if the parcel is not purchased by 
Garth W. Christensen, it will be reoffered for sale to the general 
public. Bids will be accepted on a continuing basis until the parcel is 
sold or the sale is cancelled.

    Dated: June 6, 1994.
Samuel R. Rowley,
Associate District Manager.
