FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 95-83; RM-8634]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Littlefield, Wolfforth and Tahoka, 
TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for rule making by 21st 
Century Radio Ventures, Inc., requesting the reallotment of Channel 
238C3 from Littlefield to Wolfforth, Texas, and the modification the 
construction permit of Station KAIQ (FM) to specify Wolfforth as 
Station KAIQ (FM)'s community of license. See 60 FR 32933, June 26, 
1995. The proposed reallotment was denied because retaining the 
allotment at Littlefield would provide a second aural reception service 
to 3,113 persons, which outweighs a first local transmission service to 
Wolfforth (population 1,941). With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.
