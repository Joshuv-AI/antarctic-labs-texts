FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM
 

Lynda C. Thompson; Change in Bank Control Notices; Acquisitions 
of Shares of Banks or Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 94-21210) published on page 
44426 of the issue for Monday, August 29, 1994.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta heading, the entry for 
Lynda C. Thompson is revised to read as follows:
    1. Lynda C. Thompson, Duluth, Georgia; to retain personally 9.8 
percent and as trustee for the American Bancshares, Inc., ESOP, 
Marietta, Georgia, 2.4 percent of the voting shares of American 
Bancshares, Inc., Marietta, Georgia, and thereby indirectly control 
Cobb American Bank & Trust Company, Marietta, Georgia. Notificant also 
proposes to acquire an additional 1.4 percent in her personal capacity 
and 3.0 as trustee, of American Bancshares, Inc., for a total of 16.6 
percent.
    Comments on this application must be received by September 12, 
1994.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, September 7, 
1994.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
