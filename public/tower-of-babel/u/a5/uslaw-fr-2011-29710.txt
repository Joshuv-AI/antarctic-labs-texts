Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records; Withdrawal

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary of Defense, Department of Defense 
(DoD).

ACTION: Notice to delete a System of Records; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On November 14, 2011 (76 FR 70427), Department of Defense 
published a Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records notice (FR Doc. 
2011-29200). This notice had already been published in the Federal 
Register on August 22, 2011 (76 FR 52322, FR Doc. 2011-21286). The 
notice of November 14, 2011 published in error. This document withdraws 
that notice.

DATES: The notice published on November 14, 2011 (76 FR 70427) is 
withdrawn, effective November 17, 2011.
