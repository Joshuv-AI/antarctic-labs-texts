Midwest Independent Transmission System Operator, Inc.; Notice of 
Technical Conference

May 28, 2003.
    Take notice that a technical conference will be held on Thursday, 
June 26, 2003, at 9 a.m. in a room to be determined, at the offices of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.
    The technical conference will address the Midwest Independent 
Transmission System Operator, Inc.'s Market Mitigation Measures, as 
proposed in its filings in the above dockets.
    Further details of the conference, including the agenda, will be 
specified in a subsequent notice. All interested persons and Staff are 
permitted to attend the conference, and registration is not required.
    There will be no transcript of the conference and there will be no 
telephone link communications. For more information about the 
conference, please contact Jeffrey Hitchings at (202) 502-6042 or 
[email protected].

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
