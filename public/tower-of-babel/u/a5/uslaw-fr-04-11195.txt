Childhood Asthma Prevalence and Risk Factors at the Border; 
Notice of Intent to Fund Single Eligibility Award

A. Purpose

    The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) announces the 
intent to fund fiscal year (FY) 2004 funds for a grant program for 
Childhood Asthma Prevalence and Risk Factors at the Border. The Catalog 
of Federal Domestic Assistance number for this program is 93.283.

B. Eligible Applicant

    Assistance will be provided only to the California Department of 
Health Services. Staff at the California Department of Health Services 
has previously conducted asthma studies at the U.S./Mexico border, and 
serve as an invaluable resource for this activity. No other 
organization has the depth of collaborative history in asthma research 
studies in this geographic area along the U.S/Mexican border.

C. Funding

    Approximately $210,000 is available in FY 2004 to fund this award. 
It is expected that the award will begin in August 2004, and will be 
made for a 12-month budget period within a project period of one year. 
Funding estimates may change.
