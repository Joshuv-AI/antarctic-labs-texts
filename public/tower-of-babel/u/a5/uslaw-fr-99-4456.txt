FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 99-57; RM-9460]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Upton, WY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a petition filed by Windy 
Valley Broadcasting proposing the allotment of Channel 290C1 at Upton, 
Wyoming, as the community's first local aural transmission service. 
Channel 290C1 can be allotted to Upton in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at city reference 
coordinates. The coordinates for Channel 290C1 at Upton are 44-05-54 
North Latitude 104-37-36 West Longitude.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before April 5, 1999, and reply 
comments on or before April 20, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner, his counsel, or consultant, as follows: Victor A. 
Michael, Jr. President, Windy Valley Broadcasting, 6807 Foxglove Drive, 
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82009 (Petitioner).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon P. McDonald, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
