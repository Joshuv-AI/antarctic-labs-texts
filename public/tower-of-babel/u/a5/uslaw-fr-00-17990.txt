INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 731-TA-697 (Review)]


Pure Magnesium From Russia

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Termination of five-year review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The subject five-year review was initiated in April 2000 to 
determine whether revocation of the existing antidumping duty order on 
pure magnesium from Russia would be likely to lead to continuation or 
recurrence of dumping and of material injury to a domestic industry. On 
July 7, 2000, the Department of Commerce published notice that it was 
revoking the order ``[b]ecause no domestic party responded to the 
sunset review notice of initiation by the applicable deadline'' (65 FR 
41944). Accordingly, pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff Act of 
1930

[[Page 44077]]

(19 U.S.C. 1675(c)), the subject review is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 7, 2000.
