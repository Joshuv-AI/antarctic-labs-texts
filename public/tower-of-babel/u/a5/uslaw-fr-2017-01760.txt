FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Market Access Agreement; Correction

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

ACTION: Notice of approval of the Draft Third Amended and Restated 
Market Access Agreement; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Farm Credit Administration (FCA) published a notice in the 
Federal Register on January 18, 2017, of its approval of the Draft 
Third Amended and Restated Market Access Agreement (Draft Third 
Restated MAA). This document corrects that notice by inserting a minor 
term and phrase that were inadvertently omitted in the Draft Third 
Restated MAA which was included in the notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: 

David Lewandrowski, Senior Policy Analyst, Office of Regulatory Policy, 
Farm Credit Administration, 1501 Farm Credit Drive, McLean, VA 22102-
5090, (703) 883-4212, TTY (703) 883-4056; or
Rebecca S. Orlich, Senior Counsel, Office of General Counsel, Farm 
Credit Administration, McLean, VA 22102-5090, (703) 883-4420, TTY (703) 
883-4056.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The FCA published a document in the Federal 
Register on January 18, 2017 (82 FR 5565). In FR Doc. 2017-01054, make 
the following corrections on two separate pages:
    1. Add the term ``Leverage Ratio'' on page 5567, in the first 
column, line 63 after ``Tier 1''.
    2. On page 5576, in the second column, line 44, add the phrase ``as 
amended from time to time or any successor thereto'' to the end of the 
sentence.
    3. On page 5576, in the second column, line 46, add the phrase ``as 
amended from time to time or any successor thereto'' to the end of the 
sentence.
