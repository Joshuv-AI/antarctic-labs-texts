Surface Transportation Board

Railroad Cost Recovery Procedures--Productivity Adjustment

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board, Transportation.

ACTION: Proposed adoption of a Railroad Cost Recovery Procedures 
productivity adjustment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Surface Transportation Board proposes to adopt 1.057 
(5.7%) as the measure of average growth in railroad productivity for 
the 1993-1997 (5-year) period. The current value of 9.7% was developed 
for the 1992 to 1996 period.

DATES: Comments are due by February 16, 1999.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The proposed productivity adjustment is effective 
February 28, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Send comments (an original and 10 copies) referring to STB 
Ex Parte No. 290 (Sub-No. 4) to: Office of the Secretary, Case Control 
Branch, 1925 K Street, NW, Washington, DC 20423-0001. Parties should 
submit all pleading and attachments on a 3.5-inch diskette in
