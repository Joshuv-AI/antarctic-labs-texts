National Institute on Drug Abuse; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of changes in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Drug Abuse Special Emphasis Panel, February 19, 2014, 
08:00 a.m. to February 20, 2014, 05:00 p.m., Hilton Garden Inn, 7301

Register on January 22, 2014, 79, 15 FR 2014-01189.

08:00 a.m. to February 19, 2014, 06:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to 
the public.
