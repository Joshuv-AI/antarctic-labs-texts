DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Delta Federal Savings Bank, Westminster, CA; Notice of 
Replacement of Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
subdivision (C) of section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator for Delta Federal Savings Bank, Westminster, 
California (``Association''), with the Resolution Trust Corporation as 
sole Receiver for the Association on March 25, 1994.

    Dated: April 5, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
