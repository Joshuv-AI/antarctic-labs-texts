National Marine Sanctuary Program Regulations

CFR Correction

    This rule is being published by the Office of the Federal Register 
to correct an editorial or technical error that appeared in the most 
recent annual revision of the Code of Federal Regulations.

0
In Title 15 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 800 to End, 
revised as of January 1, 2024, amend Sec.  922.132(f) by adding a 
second to last sentence to read as follows:


Sec.  922.132  Prohibited or otherwise regulated activities.

* * * * *
    (f) * * * For the purposes of this subpart, the disposal of dredged 
material does not include the beneficial use of dredged material as 
defined by Sec.  922.131. * * *
