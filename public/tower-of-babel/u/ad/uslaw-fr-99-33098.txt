National Register of Historic Places;Notification of Pending 
Nominations

listing in the National Register were received by the National Park 
Service before December 11, 1999.

concerning the significance of these properties under the National 
Register criteria for evaluation may be forwarded to the National 
Register, National Park Service, 1849 C St. NW, NC400, Washington, DC
