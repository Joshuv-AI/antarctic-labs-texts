DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the Unmanned Aerial Vehicles and Long-Range 
Strike Studies. The purpose of the meeting is to brief the Chief of 
Staff on the results of the study. This meeting will be closed to the 
public.

DATES: 31 July 2003.

ADDRESSES: Room 4E987, The Pentagon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Maj Dwight Pavek, Air Force Scientific 
Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 5D982, 
Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
