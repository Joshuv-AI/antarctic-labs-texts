DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby gives notice that 
members of the Commission's staff may attend the following meetings 
related to the transmission planning activities of the Southwest Power 
Pool, Inc. (SPP):
    Seams FERC Order 1000 Task Force meetings:

October 12, 2012
10:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m. CDT.

October 18, 2012,
10:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m. CDT.

    The above-referenced meetings will be held via teleconference.
    The above-referenced meetings are open to stakeholders.
    Further information may be found at www.spp.org.
    The discussions at the meetings described above may address matters
