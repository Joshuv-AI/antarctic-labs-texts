    Time: 1:00 p.m. to Adjournment.
    Place: Teleconference, 6003 Executive Boulevard, Solar Bldg., 
Room 4C01, Bethesda, MD 20892, (301) 496-2550.
    Contact Person: Dr. Kevin Callahan, Scientific Review Adm., 6003 
Executive Boulevard, Solar Bldg., Room 4C20, Bethesda, Md 20892, 
(301) 496-8424.
    Purpose/Agenda: To evaluate a grant application.

set forth in sections 552(b)(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C. 
Applications and/or proposals and the
