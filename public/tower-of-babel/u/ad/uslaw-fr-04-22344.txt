DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Notice of Resource advisory Committee Meeting

AGENCY: Modoc Resource Advisory Committee, Alturas, California, USDA 
Forest Service.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the authorities in the Federal Advisory Committees 
Act (Pub. L. 92-463) and under the secure Rural Schools and Community 
Self-Determination Act of 2000 (Pub. L. 106-393) the Modoc National 
Forest's Modoc Resource Advisory Committee will meet Monday, November 
1st, 2004, in

[[Page 60351]]

Alturas, California for business meetings. The meetings are open to the 
public.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The business meeting November 1st begins at 
6 p.m., at the Modoc National Forest Office, Conference Room, 800 West 
12th St., Alturas. Agenda topics will include the final roll-call vote 
for a majority of the projects submitted for funding in fiscal year 
2005. Time will also be set aside for public comments at the beginning 
of the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stan Sylva, Forest Supervisor and 
Designated Federal Officer, at (530) 233-8700; or Public Affairs 
Officer Nancy Gardner at (530) 233-8713.

Stanley G. Sylva,
Forest Supervisor.
