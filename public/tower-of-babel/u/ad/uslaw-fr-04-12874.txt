Crawford Knitting Company, Inc. Ramseur, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 26, 2004 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Crawford 
Knitting Company, Inc., Ramseur, North Carolina (TA-W-54,595).
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 11th day of May 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
