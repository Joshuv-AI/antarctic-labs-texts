Business Development Center Applications: Chicago II, North

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency (MBDA) is revising 
the announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) Program to operate the Chicago II 
MBDC. The revised award number for the Chicago II, North MBDC is 05-10-
97002-01. This solicitation was originally published in the Federal 
Register, Tuesday, May 27, 1997, Vol. 62, No. 101, page 28673.
