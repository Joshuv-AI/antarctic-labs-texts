DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Integrative, Functional and Cognitive Neuroscience 5, February 18, 
2003, 8:30 a.m. to February 19, 2003, 10 a.m., which was published in 
the Federal Register on January 31, 2003, 68 FR 5032-5035.
    The meeting will be one day only February 18, 2003, from 8 a.m. to 
6 p.m. The location remains the same. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: February 11, 2003.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
