Takata Petri, Inc., Plants 2 & 3, Port Huron, MI; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 17, 2003, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Takata Petri, 
Inc., Plants 2 & 3, Port Huron, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 4th day of August 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
