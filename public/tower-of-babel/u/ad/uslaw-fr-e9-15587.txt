DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, July 20, 2009, 8 a.m. to July 
21, 2009, 5 p.m., Hotel Palomar, 2121 P Street, NW., Washington, DC 
20037 which was published in the Federal Register on June 15, 2009, 74 
FR 28260-28262.
    The meeting will be held at the Ritz Carlton Hotel, 1150 22nd 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20037. The meeting dates and time remain 
the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 23, 2009.
Anna Snouffer,
Deputy Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
