Meadowlark Group, Inc. directed to the Memorandum Opinion and Order in 
this proceeding. With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

2177.

20554, telephone 1-800-378-3160 or http://www.BCPIWEB.com. This

Commission is, therefore, not required to submit a copy of this Report
