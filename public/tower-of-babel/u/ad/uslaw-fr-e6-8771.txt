Forney Corporation, a Division of United Technologies Corp., 
Carrollton, TX; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 10, 2006 in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Forney Corporation, A Division 
of United Technologies Corporation, Carrollton, Texas.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 18th day of May 2006.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
