DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Attorney General; Certification of the Attorney General, 
Jefferson County, Texas

    In accordance with Section 6 of the Voting Rights Act of 1965, as 
amended, 42 U.S.C. 1973d, I hereby certify that in my judgment the 
appointment of examiners is necessary to enforce the guarantees of the 
Fourteenth and

[[Page 65075]]

Fifteenth Amendments of the Constitution of the United States in 
Jefferson County, Texas. This county is included within the scope of 
the determinations of the Attorney General and the Director of the 
Census made on September 18, 1975, under Section 4(b) of the Voting 
Rights Act of 1965 and published in the Federal Register on September 
23, 1975 (40 FR 43746).

    Dated: December 5, 1996.
Janet Reno,
Attorney General of the United States.
