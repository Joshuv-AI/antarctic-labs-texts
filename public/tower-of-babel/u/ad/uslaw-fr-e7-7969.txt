Oberle & Associates, Inc. Employed At Delphi, New Castle, IN; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 12, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a State agency representative on behalf of workers of 
Oberle & Associates, Inc. employed at Delphi, New Castle, Indiana.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification (TA-W-60,819 as amended) which expires on February 13, 
2009. Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 20th day of April, 2007.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
