Proposed Data Collections Submitted for Public Comment and 
Recommendations; Correction Centers for Disease Control and Prevention; 
Notice; Correction

    The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention published a document 
in the Federal Register concerning a retraction of a previously 
published 60-day Federal Register Notice. The document contained the 
incorrect Federal Register Notice number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Maryam Daneshvar, 404-639-4604.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of November 7, 2007, Volume 72, Number 215, 
in FR Doc. E7-21864 page 62857, under the agency name correct the 
Federal Register notice number 60 Day-07-07BS to read: 60 Day-07-06BS.

    Dated: November 7, 2007.
Maryam I. Daneshvar,

Prevention.
