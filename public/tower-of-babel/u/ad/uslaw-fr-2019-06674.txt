Television Broadcasting Services Gadsden and Hoover, Alabama

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of ION Media License Company, LLC. (ION), 
licensee of television station WPXH-TV, channel 45, Gadsden, Alabama 
(WPXH), the Commission has before it an unopposed Notice of Proposed 
Rulemaking to amend the Post-Transition Table of DTV Allotments by 
changing WPXH's community of license from Gadsden to Hoover, Alabama. 
The Commission believes that the proposed reallotment is consistent 
with the Commission's second allotment priority by providing Hoover 
with its first local transmission service. The Commission also notes 
that the proposed reallotment will not deprive Gadsden of its sole 
broadcast station because it will continue to be served by station 
WTJP-TV, licensed to Trinity Christian Center of Santa Ana, Inc. on 
channel 26 at Gadsden. We also grant ION's requested waiver of the 
Commission's rules.

DATES: Effective April 8, 2019.
