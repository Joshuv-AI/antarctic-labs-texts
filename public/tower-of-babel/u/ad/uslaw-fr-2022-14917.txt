DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 3071-007]


Rapidan Hydroelectric, LLC, Blue Earth County; Notice of Transfer 
of Exemption

    1. On May 31, 2022, Rapidan Hydroelectric, LLC, exemptee for the 5 
Megawatts Rapid Dam Hydroelectric Project No. 3071, filed a letter 
notifying the Commission that the project was transferred from Rapidan 
Hydroelectric, LLC to Blue Earth County. The exemption from licensing 
was originally issued on July 1, 1982.\1\ The project is located on the 
Blue Earth River, Blue Earth County, Minnesota. The transfer of an 
exemption does not require Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Rapidan Redevelopment Ltd, 20 FERC ] 62,006 (1982). 
Subsequently, on June 4, 2004, the project was transferred to 
Rapidan Hydroelectric, LLC.
