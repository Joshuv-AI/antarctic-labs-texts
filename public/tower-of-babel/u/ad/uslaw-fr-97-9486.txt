SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #2940; Amendment #1]


State of Illinois

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency, dated April 1, 1997, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to establish the incident period as beginning March 1 and 
closing effective April 1, 1997.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for physical damage is May 20, 1997, and for 
loans for economic injury the deadline is December 22, 1997.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: April 8, 1997.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
