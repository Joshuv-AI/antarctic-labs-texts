DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the 2020 Basketball Hall of Fame Commemorative Coin 
Program

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing pricing for the 2020 
Basketball Hall of Fame Commemorative Coin Program as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                           Introductory
                  Coin                         price       Regular price
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Silver Proof............................          $69.00          $74.00
Silver Uncirculated.....................           64.00           69.00
Clad Proof..............................           39.00           44.00
Clad Uncirculated.......................           37.00           42.00
Kids Set................................             N/A           45.00
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Products containing gold coins will be priced according to the 
Pricing of Numismatic and Commemorative Gold and Platinum Products Grid 
posted at www.usmint.gov.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rosa Williams, Program Manager for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW; Washington, 
DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.
