Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2009-0598.
    Petitioner: Bombardier.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR 26.
    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner seeks relief from part 
26 for its Bombardier CL-600-1A11, CL-600-2A12 and CL-600-2B16 
airplanes. These airplanes' maximum payload capacities and passenger 
capacities are below those specified for transport category airplanes. 
However, since these models are on the same Type Certification Data 
Sheet (TCDS) as the original Bombardier Model CL-600, they are subject 
to the part 26 rule.
