Mountain Manufacturing & Distribution, Bend, OR; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 17, 2004, in response to a 
petition filed by a community representative on behalf of workers at 
Mountain Manufacturing & Distribution, Bend, Oregon.
    The petitioner has withdrawn the petition. Consequently, this 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 1st day of April, 2004.
Richard Church
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
