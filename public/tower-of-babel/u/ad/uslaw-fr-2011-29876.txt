 DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY

Bureau of Customs and Border Protection


Notice of Revocation of Customs Broker Licenses

AGENCY: Bureau of Customs and Border Protection, U.S. Department of 
Homeland Security.

ACTION: Customs broker license revocations for the failure to file the 
2006 triennial status report and applicable fee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to section 641 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended, 
(19 U.S.C. 1641) and Title 19 of the Code of Federal Regulations at 
section 111.30(d), the following Customs broker licenses are revoked 
without prejudice.

    For the Triennial Report filing period of 2006, Customs and Border 
Protection notified the above-identified brokers via certified mail, 
return receipt at their last known address that the Triennial Report

omitted. See Notice of Cancellation of Customs Broker License, dated 
July 25, 2006 (71 FR 42105). The current Notice of Revocation of 
Customs Broker Licenses will correct this administrative oversight.
