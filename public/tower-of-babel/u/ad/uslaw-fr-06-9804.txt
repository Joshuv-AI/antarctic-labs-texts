DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information 
Technology; American Health Information Community Biosurveillance 
Workgroup Meeting

ACTION: Announcement of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the 13th meeting of the American Health 
Information Community Biosurveillance Workgroup in accordance with the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., App.).

DATES: January 5, 2007 from 12 to 3 p.m.

ADDRESSES: Mary C. Switzer Building (330 C Street, SW., Washington, DC 
20201), Conference Room 4090 (please bring photo ID for entry to a 
Federal building).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: http://www.hhs.gov/healthit/ahic/bio_main.html.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Workgroup will continue discussing the 
Biosurveillance Priority Area matrix.
    The meeting will be available via internet access. For additional 
information, go to http://www.hhs.gov/healthit/ahic/bio_instruct.html.
