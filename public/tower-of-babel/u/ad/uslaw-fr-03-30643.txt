Injectable or Implantable Dosage Form New Animal Drugs; Meloxicam

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is amending the animal 
drug regulations to reflect approval of a new animal drug application 
(NADA) filed by Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica, Inc. The NADA provides 
for use of meloxicam injectable solution in dogs for the control of 
pain and inflammation associated with osteoarthritis.

DATES: This rule is effective December 10, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Melanie R. Berson, Center for 
Veterinary Medicine (HFV-110), Food and Drug Administration, 7500 
Standish Pl., Rockville, MD 20855, 301-827-7540, e-mail: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica, Inc., 2621 
North Belt Highway, St. Joseph, MO 64506-2002, filed NADA 141-219 that 
provides for use of METACAM (meloxicam) Injectable Solution in dogs for 
the control of pain and inflammation associated with osteoarthritis. 
The NADA is approved as of November 12, 2003, and the regulations are 
amended

2. Section 522.1367 is added to read as follows:


Sec.  522.1367  Meloxicam.

    (a) Specifications. Each milliliter of solution contains 5.0 
milligrams (mg) meloxicam.
    (b) Sponsor. See No. 000010 in Sec.  510.600(c) of this chapter.
    (c) Conditions of use in dogs--(1) Amount. Administer 0.2 mg/
kilogram (kg) body weight by intravenous or subcutaneous injection on 
the first day of treatment. For treatment after day 1, administer 
meloxicam suspension orally at 0.1 mg/kg body weight once daily as in 
Sec.  520.1350(c) of this chapter.
    (2) Indications for use. For the control of pain and inflammation 
associated with osteoarthritis.
    (3) Limitations. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on 
the order of a licensed veterinarian.
