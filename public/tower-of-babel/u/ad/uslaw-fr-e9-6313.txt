Tech Group, Van Buren, AR; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 4, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by a State agency representative on behalf of workers of Tech 
Group, Van Buren, Arkansas.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 12th day of March 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
