OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (Volume 68, Number 237, Page 68958 and 68959) on 
December 10, 2003. No requests were received to provide testimony or 
submit written statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public 
hearing in conjunction with OPIC's January 6, 2004 Board of Directors 
meeting scheduled for 2 p.m. on December 30, 2003 has been cancelled.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Information on the hearing 
cancellation may be obtained from Connie M. Downs at (202) 336-8438, 
via facsimile at (202) 218-0136, or via e-mail at [email protected].

    Dated: December 30, 2003.
Connie M. Downs,
OPIC Corporate Secretary.
