DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-2-34,400]


Apocalypse Inc.; Ellenville, NY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, and investigation 
was initiated on April 6, 1998, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Apocalypse Inc., Ellenville, New 
York.

[[Page 33953]]

    The subject firm closed in March of 1997. The Department has been 
unable to locate principals of the firm or otherwise obtain information 
to reach a determination on worker eligibility. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 12th day of June, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
