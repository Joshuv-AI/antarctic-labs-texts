SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3057; Amendment # 2]


State of California

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated February 26, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is 
hereby amended to include Los Angeles, Orange, Stanislaus, and Trinity 
Counties in the State of California as a disaster area due to damages 
caused by severe winter storms and flooding beginning on February 2, 
1998 and continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous Counties of Riverside, San 
Bernardino, and San Diego in the State of California may be filed until 
the specified date at the previously designated location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is April 10, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is November 9, 1998.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: March 5, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
