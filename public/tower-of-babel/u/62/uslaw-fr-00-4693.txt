Fisheries of the Exclusive Economic Zone Off Alaska; 
Recordkeeping and Reporting Requirements; American Fisheries Act 
Reporting Requirements

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of effectiveness of data collection.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NMFS is announcing that information collection requirements 
were approved.

DATES: Effective February 16, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kent Lind, 907-586-7228.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The information collection requirements 
contained in paragraphs 679.5(a)(4)(iv), 679.5(f)(3), 679.5(f)(4), 
679.5(i)(1)(iii), 679.5(o), and 679.60(d), which were contained in the 
emergency interim rule to to implement major provisions of the American 
Fisheries Act (65 FR 4520, January 28, 2000), were approved by the 
Office of Management and Budget. These requirements are in effect as of 
February 16, 2000.

    Dated: February 23, 2000.
Bruce C. Morehead,
Acting Director, Office of Sustainable Fisheries, National Marine 
Fisheries Service.
