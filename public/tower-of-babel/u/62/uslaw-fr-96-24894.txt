DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Eastern Shore Natural Gas Company; Notice of Technical Conference

September 24, 1996.
    Take notice that a technical conference will be convened in the 
above-docketed proceeding on Wednesday, October, 2, 1996, at 10:00 
a.m., in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, DC, 20426. 
Any party, as defined in 18 CFR 385.102(c), any person seeking 
intervenor status pursuant to 18 CFR 385.214, and any participant, as 
defined in 18 CFR 385.102(b), is invited to participate.
    For additional information, please contact Carolyn Van Der Jagt, 
202-208-2246, or Tom Gooding, 202-208-1123, at the Commission.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
