DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Department of the Air Force


Notice To Extend Public Comment Period for the Draft 
Environmental Impact Statement for the United States Air Force F-35A 
Operational Beddown Air National Guard

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force.

ACTION: Notification of extension of public comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Air Force is issuing this notice to advise the public 
of an extension to the public comment period. The initial Notice of 
Availability was published in the Federal Register on August 9, 2019 
(Vol. 84, No. 154/Notices/39296), and established a public comment 
period from August 9, 2019 through September 27, 2019. The Air Force 
has extended the deadline for submitting public comments to November 1, 
2019. All substantive comments received by November 1, 2019 will be 
addressed in the Public Comment Section of the Final EIS.
