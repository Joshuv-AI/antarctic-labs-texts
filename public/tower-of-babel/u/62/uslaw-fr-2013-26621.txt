DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, October 3, 
2013, 08:00 a.m. to October 4, 2013, 05:00 p.m., The William F. Bolger 
Center 9600 Newbridge Drive, Potomac, MD 20854 which was published in 
the Federal Register on September 10, 2013, 78 FR 55268.
    The notice is amended to change the date of the meeting from 
October 3-4, 2013 to November 21-22, 2013. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: November 1, 2013.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
