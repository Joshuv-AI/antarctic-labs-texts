DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 24728]]

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Pub. L. 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the 2003 S&T Review and the Director of Defense 
Research and Engineering. The purpose of the meeting is to allow the 
SAB leadership to advise the Director on the outcome of the 2003 
Review. Because classified and contractor-proprietary information will 
be discussed, this meeting will be closed to the public.

DATES: May 9, 2003.

ADDRESSES: Room 4E987, the Pentagon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lt. Col. John Pernot, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
