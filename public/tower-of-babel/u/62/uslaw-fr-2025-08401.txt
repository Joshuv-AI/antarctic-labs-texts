COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: United States Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice of Commission public business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

DATES: Friday, May 16, 2025, 10 a.m. EST.

ADDRESSES: Meeting to take place virtually and is open to the public 
via livestream on the Commission's YouTube page: https://www.youtube.com/user/USCCR/videos.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joe Kim: 202-499-0263. 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In accordance with the Government in 
Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 552b), the

[[Page 20147]]

Commission on Civil Rights is holding a meeting to discuss the 
Commission's business for the month of May. This business meeting is 
open to the public. Computer assisted real-time transcription (CART) 
will be provided. The web link to access CART (in English) on Friday, 
May 16, 2025, is https://www.streamtext.net/player?event=USCCR. Please 
note that CART is text-only translation that occurs in real time during 
the meeting and is not an exact transcript.

Meeting Agenda

I. Approval of Agenda
II. Business Meeting
    A. Discussion and Vote on the planning documents for the 2025 
Briefing Report Topic on Mental Health in Juvenile Justice.
    B. Discussion and Vote on State Advisory Committee Appointments.
    C. Management and Operations.
     Staff Director's Report
III. Adjourn Meeting

    Dated: May 8, 2025.
David Mussatt,
