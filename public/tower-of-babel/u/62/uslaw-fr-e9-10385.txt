Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,587]


Vitec Group Communications; Clear-Com Division; Alameda, CA; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 13, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of the workers at Vitec Group Communications, 
Clear-Com Division, Alameda, California.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
