DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 28 & 29 February 1996.
    Time of Meeting: 0745-1730, 28 February1996; 0745-1300, 29 
February 1996.
    Place: USASSDC--Huntsville, AL.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board (ASB) Independent Assessment 
Panel on ``Hit-To-Kill Interceptor Lethality'' will meet to 
consolidate lethality information from individual members and 
prepare a format for the outbrief. These meetings will be open to 
the public. Any interested person may attend, appear before, or file 
statements with the committee at the time and in the manner 
permitted by the committee. For further information, please call 
Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
