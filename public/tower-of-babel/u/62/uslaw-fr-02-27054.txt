DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP02-132-003]


Viking Gas Transmission Company; Notice To Place Tariff Sheets 
Into Effect

October 17, 2002.
    Take notice that on October 11, 2002 Viking Gas Transmission 
Company (Viking) tendered for filing a Motion To Place Settlement Rates 
into Effect on an Interim Basis Subject To Conditions (Motion) and 
tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, First Revised 
Volume No. 1, the tariff sheets listed on Appendix A to the filing to 
become effective July 1, 2002 subject to the conditions set forth in 
Viking's Motion.
    Viking states that the purpose of this filing is to place the 
Settlement Rates into effect on an interim basis subject to certain 
conditions in accordance with the Offer of Settlement filed in this 
proceeding on September 13, 2002.
    Viking states that copies of this filing have been served on all 
parties designated on the official service list in this proceeding, on 
all Viking's jurisdictional customers and to affected state regulatory 
commissions.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before October 24, 2002. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceedings. This filing 
is available for review at the Commission in the Public Reference Room 
or may be viewed on the Commission's Web site at http://www.ferc.gov 
using the ``FERRIS'' link. Enter the docket number excluding the last 
three digits in the docket number field to access the document. For 
Assistance, call (202) 502-8222 or for TTY, (202) 502-8659. Comments, 
protests and interventions may be filed electronically via the Internet 
in lieu of paper. The Commission strongly encourages electronic 
filings. See, 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the 
Commission's Web site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
