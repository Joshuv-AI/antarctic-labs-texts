DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Fogarty International Center; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Fogarty 
International Center Advisory Board, September 14, 2015, 01:00 p.m. to 
September 15, 2015, 03:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 16 
Center Drive, Lawton L. Chiles International House, Bethesda, MD, 20892 
which was published in the Federal Register on August 11, 2015, 80 FR 
48115.
    The meeting is being amended to change the time of the meeting. The 
closed session will be Sept. 14 from 1:00 p.m.-2:15 p.m., then the open 
session @2:30 p.m. and adjourn @ 5:15 p.m. Open session Sept.15 from 9 
a.m. and adjourn @ 12:00 noon. The meeting is partially closed to the 
public.

    Dated: August 26, 2015.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
