DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board, Paducah

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On October 6, 1999, the Department of Energy published a notice of 
open meeting announcing a meeting of the Environmental Management Site-
Specific Advisory Board, Paducah 64 FR 54281. In that notice, the 
meeting was scheduled for October 21, 1999 at 6:00 p.m.-8:30 p.m. 
Today's notice is announcing that the meeting will be starting at 5:30 
p.m.-8:30 p.m.

    Issued in Washington, DC on October 7, 1999.

Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
