    On January 5, 2016, Arkansas Electric Cooperative Corporation; 
Mississippi Delta Energy Agency and its members, Clarksdale Public 
Utilities Commission of the City of Clarksdale, Mississippi and Public 
Service Commission of Yazoo City, Mississippi; Hoosier Energy Rural 
Electric Cooperative, Inc.; and South Mississippi Electric Power 
Association filed a motion for clarification regarding the submission 
of briefs on exceptions, in the above-referenced proceeding (Motion). 
On January 8, 2016, MISO Transmission Owners filed an answer to the 
Motion. Notice is hereby given that the deadline for submitting briefs 
on exceptions is set to and including January 21, 2016. The deadline to 
file briefs opposing exceptions is set to and including February 10, 
2016.
