Avery Dennison, Office Products Group, Flowery Branch, GA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 13, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Avery 
Dennison, Office Products Group, Flowery Branch, Georgia.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 8th day of December, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
