Agency Information Collection Activities; Submission for OMB 
Review; Comment Request; Job Clubs Study

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Labor published a document in the Federal 
Register of February 26, 2013, concerning request for comments on site 
visits to job clubs. The document contained incorrect dates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Contact Ben Seigel by telephone at 
202-693-6032 (this is not a toll-free number) or by email at 
[email protected].

Correction

    In the Federal Register of February 26, 2013, in FR Doc. 2013-
04391, on page 13086, in the second column, correct the DATES caption 
to read:

DATES: Written comments must be submitted to the office listed in the 
addressee section on or before April 27, 2013.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of March, 2013.
