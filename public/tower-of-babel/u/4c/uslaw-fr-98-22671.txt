DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board, Nevada 
Test Site

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Meeting cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee 
Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 86 Stat. 770), notice is hereby given of the 
cancellation of the open Environmental Management Site-Specific 
Advisory Board (EM SSAB), September 2, 1998, from 5:30 p.m.-9:00 p.m., 
at the U.S. Department of Energy Nevada Support Facility, Great Basin 
Room, 232 Energy Way, North Las Vegas, Nevada. This meeting was 
announced in the Federal Register on Friday, August 14, 1998 (63 FR 
43692).

    Issued at Washington, DC on August 19, 1998.
Rachel Samuel,
Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
