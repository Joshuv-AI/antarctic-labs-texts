DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Bureau of Labor Statistics


Technical Advisory Committee; Notice of Meeting and Agenda; 
Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Labor Statistics.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Labor Statistics published a document in the 
Federal Register on October 18, 2024, announcing the November 7, 2024 
Technical Advisory Committee meeting. The document contained an 
incorrect registration link.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register Notice on page 83908 in the third column, 
correct https://blstac.beventbrite.com to read: Individuals planning to 
attend the meeting should register at https://blstac.eventbrite.com.

    Dated: October 23, 2024.
Leslie Bennett,
Chief, Division of Management Systems.
