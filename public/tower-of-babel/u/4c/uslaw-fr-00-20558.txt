DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 24-98]


Foreign-Trade Zone 169--Manatee County, FL, Application for 
Subzone Status, Aso Corporation (Bandages); Reopening of Comment Period

    Notice is hereby given that the comment period regarding the 
application of the Manatee County Port Authority, grantee of FTZ 169, 
requesting special-purpose subzone status for the adhesive bandages 
manufacturing facility of Aso Corporation, located in Sarasota County, 
Florida (63 FR 26776, 5/14/98), has been reopened until August 28, 
2000, to accept additional information from the applicant and 
interested parties.

    Dated: August 8, 2000.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Executive Secretary.
