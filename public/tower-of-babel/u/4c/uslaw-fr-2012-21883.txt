DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Rural Housing Service


Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Low Loan-to-Cost Ratio

AGENCY: Rural Housing Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Rural Housing Service announces the low loan-to-cost ratio 
required for loans guaranteed under Option Three (Continuous Guarantee) 
of the Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program (GRRHP). The GRRHP is 
authorized by section 538 of the Housing Act of 1949, as amended (42 
U.S.C. 1490p-2) and operates under 7 CFR part 3565. The low loan-to-
cost ratio is defined at 50 percent in order for a loan to be eligible 
for a single continuous guarantee for construction and permanent loans 
pursuant to 7 CFR part 3565.52. The Rural Housing Service is not 
modifying the lease-up reserves and percent of guarantee previously 
established for this program.
