Television Broadcast Service; New Iberia, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Iberia Communications, LLC, 
substitutes channel 50 for channel 53-. See 67 FR 47757, July 22, 2002. 
TV channel 50 can be allotted to New Iberia, Louisiana, in compliance 
with the principle community coverage requirements of Section 73.610 at 
coordinates 29-55-12 N. and 91-46-07 W. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 12, 2002.
