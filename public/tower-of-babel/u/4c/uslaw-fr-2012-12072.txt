DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of FERC Staff Attendance at the Louisiana Public Service 
Commission's Business and Executive Session Meeting

members of its staff may attend the meeting noted below. Their 
attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach efforts.
