OFFICE OF MANAGEMENT AND BUDGET


Budget Analysis Branch; Sequestration Final Report

AGENCY: Office of Management and Budget--Budget Analysis Branch.

ACTION: Notice of Transmittal of Final Sequestration Report to the 
President and Congress.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Section 254(b) of the Balanced Budget and 
Emergency Control Act of 1985, as amended, the Office of Management and 
Budget hereby reports that it has submitted its Final Sequestration 
Report to the President, the Speaker of the House of Representatives, 
and the President of the Senate.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jason Orlando, Budget Analysis 
Branch--202/395-7436.

    Dated: December 11, 1998.
Clarence C. Crawford,
Associate Director for Administration.
