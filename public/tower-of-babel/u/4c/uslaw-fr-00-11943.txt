DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Indian Affairs


Indian Gaming

AGENCY: Bureau of Indian Affairs, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of amendment to approved Tribal-State Compact.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Section 11 of the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act 
(IGRA),

[[Page 30606]]

Pub. L. 100-497, 25 U.S.C. 2710, the Secretary of the Interior shall 
publish, in the Federal Register, notice of approved Tribal-State 
Compacts for the purpose of engaging in Class III gaming on Indian 
lands. The Assistant Secretary--Indian Affairs, Department of the 
Interior, through his delegated authority, has approved the Amendment 
to the Interim Agreement with the State of Montana and the Confederated 
Salish and Kootenai Tribes, which was executed on March 21, 2000.

DATES: This action is effective May 12, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: George T. Skibine, Director, Office of 
Indian Gaming Management, Bureau of Indian Affairs, Washington, DC 
20240. (202) 219-4066.

    Dated: May 3, 2000.
Kevin Gover,
Assistant Secretary--Indian Affairs.
