DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Minerals Management Service


Notice on Outer Continental Shelf Oil and Gas Lease Sales

AGENCY: Minerals Management Service, Interior.

ACTION: Revision to List of Restricted Joint Bidders.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    In accordance with the joint bidding provisions of 30 CFR 256.41, 
the Minerals Management Service published in the Federal Register on 
March 26, 1999, at 64 FR 14751, the List of Restricted Joint Bidders 
covering Outer Continental Shelf oil and gas lease sales to be held 
during the period from May 1, 1999, through October 31, 1999.
    That List of Restricted Joint Bidders inadvertently omitted from 
Group IV two new entities resulting from the merger of the BP group of 
companies and the Amoco group of companies.
    The list of companies published under Group IV of the List of 
Restricted Joint Bidders is, by this Notice, revised to include these 
two additional companies:

    BP Amoco Corporation; and
    Amoco Production Company.

    Dated: April 13, 1999.
Ralph V. Ainger,
Chief, Leasing Division.
