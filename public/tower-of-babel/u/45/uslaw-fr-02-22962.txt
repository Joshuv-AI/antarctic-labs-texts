DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-41,879]


Scotty Fashions 6, Lehighton, PA; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 22, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Scotty Fashions 6, Lehighton, 
Pennsylvania.
    The investigation revealed that there is an existing petition (TA-
41,665A) for the subject firm that has been assigned to another 
investigator.
    Consequently further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of August, 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
