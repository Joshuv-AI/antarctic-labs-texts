Thompson-Hancok Technologies, Burlington, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 15, 2003 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Thompson-
Hancock Technologies, Burlington, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 16th day of June, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
