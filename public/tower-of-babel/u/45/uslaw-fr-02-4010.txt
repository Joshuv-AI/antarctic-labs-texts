SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region VI Regulatory Fairness Board; Public Federal Regulatory 
Enforcement Fairness Roundtable

    The Small Business Administration Region VI Regulatory Fairness 
Board and the SBA Office of the National Ombudsman, will hold a Public 
Roundtable on Wednesday, February 20, 2002 at 1 p.m. at the Arkansas 
State Chamber of Commerce /AIA, 410 South Cross Street, Little Rock, 
Arkansas 72201, to provide small business owners and representatives of 
trade associations with an opportunity to share information concerning 
the federal regulatory enforcement and compliance environment.
    Anyone wishing to attend or to make a presentation must contact Deb 
Mathis in writing or by fax, in order to be put on the agenda. Deb 
Mathis, Arkansas State Chamber of Commerce /AIA, 410 South Cross 
Street, Little Rock, Arkansas 72201, Phone (501) 374-9225, fax (501) 
372-2722, e-mail aia.org">Dmathis@ascc-aia.org.
    For more information see our website at http://www.sba.gov/ombudsman/events/dsp_roundtable.html.
