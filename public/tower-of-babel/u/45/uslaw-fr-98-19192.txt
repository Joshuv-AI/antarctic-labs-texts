DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-131-007, GT98-23-001 and RP98-184-001]


Overthrust Pipeline Company; Notice of Tariff Filing

July 14, 1998.
    Take notice that on July 10, 1998, Overthrust Pipeline Company 
tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, First Revised 
Volume No. 1-A the following revised tariff sheets, to be effective as 
shown below:

[[Page 38825]]



------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Substitute revised tariff sheets              Effective date        
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Substitute Third Revised Sheet No. 1....  November 1, 1997.             
Substitute Fourth Revised Sheet No. 1...  April 1, 1998.                
Substitute Fifth Revised Sheet No. 30...  November 1, 1997.             
Substitute Sixth Revised Sheet No. 30...  April 1, 1998.                
Substitute First Revised Sheet No. 34A..  November 1, 1997.             
Substitute Second Revised Sheet No. 34A.  May 8, 1998.                  
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Overthrust states that the filing is being made in response to the 
Commission's June 22, 1998 letter in Docket No. RP97-301-002.
    Overthrust states that this tariff filing (1) incorporates into 
certain tariff sheets filed by Overthrust in Docket Nos. RP97-131-005, 
GT98-23-000 and RP98-184-000 tariff revisions that were approved by the 
Commission's June 22 letter order, and (2) corrects the pagination of 
tariff sheets tendered with this filing that became effective between 
November 1, 1997, and May 8, 1998.
    Overthrust states further that a copy of this filing has been 
served upon its customers, the Public Service Commission of Utah and 
the Public Service Commission of Wyoming.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should be a protest with 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed as 
provided in Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceedings. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission 
and are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
