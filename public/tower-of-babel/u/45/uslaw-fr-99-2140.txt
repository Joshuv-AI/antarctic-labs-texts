Employment and Training Administration

Harsco Corporation, Heckett Multiserv Division, Warren, Ohio; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 14, 1998 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed November 30, 1998 on behalf of workers at Heckett 
Multiserv, a Division of Harsco Corporation, Warren, Ohio.
    The petitioning group of workers are included in an existing Trade 
Adjustment Assistance petition investigation (TA-W-35,348). 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would service no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C., this 20th day of January 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
