Employment and Training Administration

General Motors Corporation, Delphi Division, Albany, New York; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 8, 1997, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Delphi Division of General 
Motors, Albany, Georgia.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
34, 060). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 21st day of December, 1997,
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
