Demetron Kerr, Danbury, CT; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 4, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a single worker on behalf of workers at Demetron 
Kerr, Danbury, Connecticut.
    The petition regarding the investigation has been deemed invalid. 
Valid petitions must be filed by three or more workers, by a duly 
authorized representative of such workers, by employers of such 
workers, by one-stop operators, or by one-stop partners. Consequently, 
the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 17th day of April 2006.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
