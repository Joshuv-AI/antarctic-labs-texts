Technical Associates Employed at Prestolite Wire Corporation, 
Tifton, Georgia; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 6, 2004 in response to a petition 
filed by a state representative on behalf of workers of Technical 
Associates employed at Prestolite Wire Corporation, Tifton, Georgia.
    The petitioning worker is covered by an active certification for 
workers of Prestolite Wire Corporation, Tifton, Georgia, issued on June 
29, 2004 and which remains in effect (TA-W-55,061 as amended). 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 12th day of August, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
