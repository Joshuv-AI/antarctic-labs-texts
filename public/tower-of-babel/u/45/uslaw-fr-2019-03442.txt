Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2960-006]


City of Gonzales, Texas; Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 10, 2019, the Commission issued a notice through the 
FERC eLibrary system \1\ setting March 11, 2019, as the deadline for 
filing motions to intervene and protests, comments, recommendations, 
preliminary terms and conditions, and preliminary prescriptions in the 
relicensing of the Gonzales Project, and April 25, 2019, as the 
deadline for filing reply comments. Due to the funding lapse at certain 
federal agencies between December 22, 2018 and January 25, 2019, the 
Commission is extending the deadlines by 15 days.
