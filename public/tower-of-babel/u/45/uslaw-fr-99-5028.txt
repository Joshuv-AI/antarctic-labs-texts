GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


President's Commission on the Celebration of Women in American 
History; Meeting

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Meeting Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the President's Commission on the 
Celebration of Women in American History will hold an open meeting from 
9:00 a.m. to Noon on Tuesday, March 16, 1999, at the State Plaza Hotel, 
2116 F Street, NW, Washington, DC 20037. The State Plaza Hotel 
telephone number is (202) 861-8200.

PURPOSE: To review the recommendations for the President's final 
report.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Martha Davis (202) 501-2272, Assistant 
to the Associate Administrator for Communications, General Services 
Administration. Also, inquiries may be sent to [email protected].

    Dated: February 24, 1999.
Beth Newburger,
Associate Administrator for Communications.
