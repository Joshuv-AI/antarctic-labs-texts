FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

Date and Time: Wednesday, January 7, 2004 at 10 a.m.

Place: 999 E Street, NW., Washington, DC (Ninth Floor).

Status: This meeting will be open to the public.

Items to be Discussed:
    Correction and Approval of Minutes.
    Draft Advisory Opinion 2003-36: Republican Governors Association by 
Executive Director, Edward T. Tobin III.
    Notice of Availability: Petition for Rulemaking on Public Access to 
Materials Relating to Closed Enforcement Cases.
    Routine Administrative Matters.


Date and Time: Tuesday, January 13, 2004 at 10 a.m.

Place: 999 E Street, NW., Washington, DC.

Status: This meeting will be open to the public.

Items to be Discussed:
    Compliance matters pursuant to 2 U.S.C. 437g.
    Audits conducted pursuant to 2 U.S.C. 437g, Sec.  438(b), and title 
26, U.S.C.
    Matters concerning participation in civil actions or proceedings or 
arbitration.
    Internal personnel rules and procedures or matters affecting a 
particular employee.
