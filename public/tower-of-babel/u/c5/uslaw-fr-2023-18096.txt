Amendments to OMB Control Numbers and Certain Forms; Correction

AGENCY: Office of Natural Resources Revenue (``ONRR''), Interior.

ACTION: Correcting amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 8, 2011, ONRR published a direct final rule that, 
among other things, corrected a thermal energy displaced equation 
without updating an image of the equation set forth in the regulations 
for illustration purposes. This document provides a replacement

[[Page 57882]]

thermal energy displaced equation image.

DATES: This rule is effective on August 24, 2023.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For questions concerning this direct 
final rulemaking, contact Luis Aguilar, Regulatory Specialist, by phone 
at (303) 231-3418, or by email at [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: ONRR published a direct final rule in the 
Federal Register on December 8, 2011 (76 FR 76612). ONRR amended the 
thermal energy displaced equation in Sec.  1206.356(a)(2) from 
``0.113681'' to ``0.133681.'' Section 1206.356(a)(2) contained an image 
of the equation for illustration purposes. However, ONRR did not 
provide an updated equation image. This document provides the correct 
equation image.
