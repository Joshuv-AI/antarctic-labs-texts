-----------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Eugene A. Rosengarden, Director, 
Office of Tariff Affairs and Trade Agreements (O/TA&TA) (202-205-2592).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Commission instituted investigation No. 
332-388 on November 5, 1997. In the notice of institution, the

2000.

Initial public comment deadline May 29, 1998
Publish draft HTS proposals for comment April 1, 1999
Deadline for public comment June 30, 1999
Final Commission report February 28, 2000
