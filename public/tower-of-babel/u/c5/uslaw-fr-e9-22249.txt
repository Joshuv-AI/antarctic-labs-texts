DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Notice of Proposed Information Collection Request

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On August 18, 2009, the Department of Education published a 
comment period notice in the Federal Register (Page 41690, Column 3) 
for the information collection, ``Beginning Teacher Longitudinal Study 
(BTLS) 2009-2012''. This notice is being corrected to state that 
interested persons are invited to submit comments on or before 
September 18, 2009. The IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information 
Management Services, Office of Management, hereby issues a correction 
notice as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

    Dated: September 10, 2009.
Angela C. Arrington,
IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management Services, 
Office of Management.
