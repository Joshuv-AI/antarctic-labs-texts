Conso International Corp., Union, SC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 2, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Conso 
International Corporation, Union, South Carolina (TA-W-52,710).
    There is a duplicate petition in process for the same worker group 
(TA-W-52,722). Consequently, further investigation in this case would 
serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 15th day of September, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
