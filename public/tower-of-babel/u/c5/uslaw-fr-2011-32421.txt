DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Delegation of Authority 304]


Delegation of Duties, Functions, and Responsibilities Vested in 
the Director General of the Foreign Service and Director of Human 
Resources

    By virtue of the authority vested in me as Director General of the 
Foreign Service and Director of Human Resources, and to the extent 
authorized by law, I hereby delegate the duties, functions, and 
authorities vested in me as Director General of the Foreign Service and 
Human Resources, to my Principal Deputy Steven A. Browning.
    This delegation does not include the authorities vested in me by 
Delegation 151 (related to my consultative role in connection with 
certain disputes between a labor organization and United States 
Forces), nor does it include any other duties, functions, and 
responsibilities required by law to be exercised by other authority. 
Notwithstanding this delegation, the functions and authorities 
delegated herein may also be exercised by the Secretary, the Deputy 
Secretary, the Deputy Secretary for Management and Resources, the Under 
Secretary for Management, and me.
    This delegation will terminate upon the subsequent appointment of a 
Director General of the Foreign Service and Director of Human 
Resources, or sooner if revoked by competent authority.

    Dated: December 9, 2011.
Nancy J. Powell,
Director General of the Foreign Service and Director of Human 
Resources.
