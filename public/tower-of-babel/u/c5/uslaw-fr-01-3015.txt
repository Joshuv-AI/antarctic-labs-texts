FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-217, MM Docket No. 00-181, RM-9933]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Henderson, NV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of KVVU Broadcasting 
Corporation, licensee of station KVVU-TV, NTSC channel 5, substitutes 
DTV channel 9 for DTV 24 at Henderson, Nevada. See 65 FR 59797, October 
6, 2000. DTV channel 9 can be allotted to Henderson in compliance with 
the principle community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at 
reference coordinates (36-00-28 N. and 115-00-24 W.) with a power of 
85.6, HAAT of 407 meters and with a DTV service population of 734 
thousand. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 19, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
