DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Women Veterans, Notice of Meeting Amendment

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is announcing an amendment 
to the notice of meeting of the Advisory Committee on Women Veterans on 
August 20-24, 2012, in the Dennis Auditorium, 2B-137, at the VA 
Maryland Health Care System, 10 North Green Street, Baltimore, MD, from 
8:30 a.m. until 4:30 p.m. each day. This meeting was announced in the 
Federal Register on Monday, August 13, 2012, (77 FR 156). Any member of 
the public wishing to attend or seeking additional information should 
contact Ms. Middleton at (202) 461-6193 and not (202) 273-7092 as 
previously provided.

    Dated: August 13, 2012.

    By Direction of the Secretary.
Vivian Drake,
Committee Management Officer.
