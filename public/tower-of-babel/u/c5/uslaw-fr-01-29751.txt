Aquatech, Inc., Cookeville, TN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 9, 2001 in response to a worker petition, 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Aquatech, Inc., Cookeville, 
Tennessee.
    The group of workers at Aquatech, Inc., Cookeville, Tennessee was 
certified on October 2, 2001 (TA-W-39,813C). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 15th day of November, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
