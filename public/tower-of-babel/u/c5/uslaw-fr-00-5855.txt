March 3, 2000.

1. Madison Windpower, LLC

[Docket No. EG00-103-000]

    Take notice that on February 29, 2000, Madison Windpower, LLC 
(Madison), a limited liability company with its principal place of 
business at 7500 Old Georgetown Road, Bethesda, MD 20814, filed with 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission an application for 
determination of exempt wholesale generator status pursuant to Part 365 
of the Commission's regulations.
    Madison proposes to construct, own or lease and operate a nominally 
rated 11.55 MW wind-power generating facility in the Town of Madison, 
Madison County, New York. The proposed power plant is expected to 
commence commercial operation in the 3rd quarter of 2000. All output 
from the plant will be sold by Madison exclusively at wholesale.
    Comment date: March 24, 2000, in accordance with Standard Paragraph 
E at the end of this notice. The Commission will limit its 
consideration of comments to those that concern the adequacy or 
accuracy of the application.
