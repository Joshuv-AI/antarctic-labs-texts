HARRY S. TRUMAN SCHOLARSHIP FOUNDATION


Harry S. Truman Scholarship 1999 Competition

AGENCY: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation.

ACTION: Notice of Closing for Nominations from Eligible Institutions of 
Higher Education.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority 
contained in the Harry S. Truman Memorial Scholarship Act, Public Law 
93-642 (20 U.S.C. 2001), nominations are being accepted from eligible 
institutions of higher education for 1999 Truman Scholarships. 
Procedures are prescribed in 45 CFR 1801 (August 22, 1994; vol. 59, No. 
161 sec. 13).
    In order to be assured consideration, all documentation in support 
of nominations for the competition must be received by the Truman 
Scholarship Review Committee, 2201 North Dodge, P.O. Box 168, Iowa 
City, IA 52243 no later than January 26, 1999, from participating four 
year institutions.

    Dated: December 17, 1998.
Louis H. Blair,
Executive Secretary.
