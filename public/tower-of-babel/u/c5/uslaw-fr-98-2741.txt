SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    As a result of an agreement between the U.S. and ``free world'' 
countries, the import certificate/delivery verification procedures were 
established. This collection provides a means to authorize approved 
imports to the U.S. to be transhipped to another destination instead of 
being imported to the U.S. directly as approved on the Import 
Certificate. When this occurs, this is considered a ``triangular'' 
transaction.
