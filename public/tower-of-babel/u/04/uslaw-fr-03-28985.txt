Public Information Regulations; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is correcting the 
public information regulations to correct an error that was 
incorporated in the regulations. This action is being taken to improve 
the accuracy of the regulations.

DATES: This correction is effective July 28, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce A. Strong, Office of Policy and 
Planning (HF-27), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-7010.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register of May 12, 2003 (68 
FR 25283), FDA published a final rule that, among other things, amended 
its regulations, in part 20 (21 CFR part 20). In Sec.  20.120, the zip 
code for the Dockets Management Branch is incorrect. This document 
corrects that error.


Sec.  20.120  [Corrected]

0
1. On page 25287, in the second column, Sec.  20.120 Records available 
in Food and Drug Administration Public Reading Rooms is corrected in 
the third sentence of paragraph (a) by removing ``20857'' and adding in 
its place ``20852''.
