DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ES97-44-001]


Orange and Rockland Utilities, Inc.; Notice of Filing

September 19, 1997.
    Take notice that on September 18, 1997, Orange and Rockland 
Utilities, Inc. (O&R) filed an amendment to its application for 
authorization to issue securities in the above-captioned docket. The 
only change to the previously-approved application is a request by O&R 
that the authorization become effective October 1, 1997.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests must be filed on or before September 25, 1997. Protests will 
be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action 
to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
