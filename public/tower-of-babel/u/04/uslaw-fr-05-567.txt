SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    These recordkeeping and reporting requirements are clear statements 
of normal business records for high performance computers (HPC) that 
are expected to be maintained by end-users in destinations where there 
is a potential for diversion to unauthorized endusers. The records must 
be available for inspection by U.S. officials to maintain surveillance 
of HPC usage and implementation of appropriate safeguards.

II. Method of Collection
