DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice No. 3466]


Shipping Coordinating Committee; Notice of Meeting

    The Shipping Coordinating Committee will conduct an open meeting at 
9 a.m. on Monday, December 11, 2000, in Room 6319, at U.S. Coast Guard 
Headquarters, 2100 Second Street, SW., Washington, DC 20593-0001. This 
meeting will discuss the upcoming 44th Session of the Subcommittee on 
Stability and Load Lines and on Fishing Vessels Safety (SLF) and 
associated bodies of the International Maritime Organization (IMO) 
which will be held on September 17-21, 2001, at the IMO Headquarters in 
London, England.
    Items of discussion will include the following:
    a. Review of results from the previous Session (SLF 43),
    b. Harmonization of damage stability provisions in the IMO 
instruments,
    c. Revision of technical regulations of the 1966 International Load 
Line Convention,
    d. Revisions to the Fishing Vessel Safety Code and Voluntary 
Guidelines.
    Members of the public may attend this meeting up to the seating 
capacity of the room. Interested persons may seek information by 
writing: Mr. Paul Cojeen, U.S. Coast Guard Headquarters, Commandant (G-
MSE-2), Room 1308, 2100 Second Street, SW., Washington, DC 20593-0001 
or by calling (202) 267-2988.

    Dated: November 8, 2000.
Stephen Miller,
Executive Secretary, Shipping Coordinating Committee.
