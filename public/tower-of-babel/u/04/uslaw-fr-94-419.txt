DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Forest Service

 

Grand Island Advisory Commission; Meeting

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Grand Island Advisory Commission meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Grand Island Advisory Commission will meet on January 21, 
1994 at 8 a.m. at the Comfort Inn on M-28 East in Munising, Michigan. 
An agenda for the one day meeting will consist of a final planning team 
update, a review of the final changes to the alternatives and a 
discussion by the Advisory Commission on the recommendation to the 
Secretary
    Interested members of the public are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Direct questions about this meeting to Dennis Jones, Public Service 
Team Leader, Hiawatha National Forest, 2727 N. Lincoln Road, Escanaba, 
MI 49829, (906) 786-4062.

    Dated: January 3, 1994.
Deborah Rebholz,
Acting Forest Supervisor.
