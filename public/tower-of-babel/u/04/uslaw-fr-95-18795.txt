FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 95-112, RM-8663]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Lordsburg, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a petition filed by 
Hildalgo County Broadcasters to allot Channel 279C3 to Lordsburg, NM, 
as the community's second local FM service. Channel 279C3 can be 
allotted to Lordsburg in compliance with the Commission's minimum 
distance separation requirements without the imposition of a site 
restriction, at coordinates 32-20-48; 108-42-36. Mexican concurrence in 
the allotment is required since Lordsburg is located within 320 
kilometers (199 miles) of the U.S.-Mexican border.


[[Page 39143]]

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 18, 1995, and 
reply comments on or before October 3, 1995.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should
