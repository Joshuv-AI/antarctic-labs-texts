DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Grand Canyon National Park, Coconino County, AZ

AGENCY: National Park Service, DOI.

ACTION: Notice and request for comment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that a proposal for a cellular 
communication site at Grand Canyon National Park has been received. The 
company proposes installing and operating a wireless telecommunications 
facility on the existing tower of US WEST at Grand Canyon Village of 
the park.

DATES: Written comments must be submitted on or before July 5, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Direct all written comments to Superintendent, Attn.: 
Barbara Nelson, Telecommunications Specialist, Grand Canyon National 
Park, P.O. Box 129, Grand Canyon, AZ 86023.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sandi Perl, Management Assistant, at 
telephone number 520-638-7885.

    Dated: June 1, 1999.
Robert L. Arnberger,
Superintendent.
