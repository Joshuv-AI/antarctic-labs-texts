DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Lower Snake River District Resource Advising Council; Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management--Interior.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Lower Snake River District Resource Advisory Council will 
meet in Boise to discuss a variety of district and regional issues, 
including use of public land recreation trails in the Boise Foothills, 
status of the U.S. Air Force's Enhanced Training in Idaho Draft 
Environmental Impact Statement, and the Draft Owyhee Resource 
Management Plan.

DATES: March 20, 1997. The meeting will begin at 9 a.m. A public 
comment period will begin at 9:30 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The Lower Snake River District Office is located at 3948 
Development Avenue, Boise, Idaho.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barry Rose, Lower Snake River District 
Office (208-384-3393).

    Dated: January 22, 1997.
Barry Rose,
Public Affairs Specialist.
