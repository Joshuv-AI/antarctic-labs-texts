DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 3025-031]


Green Mountain Power Corporation; Notice of Effectiveness of 
Withdrawal of License Application

    On March 30, 2022, Green Mountain Power Corporation (GMP) filed a 
relicense application for the 450-kilowatt Kelley's Falls Hydroelectric 
Project No. 3025. On December 29, 2022, GMP filed a notice of 
withdrawal of its application.
    No motion in opposition to the notice of withdrawal has been filed, 
and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the withdrawal. 
Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the application became effective on 
January 13, 2023, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 18 CFR 385.216(b) (2022).
