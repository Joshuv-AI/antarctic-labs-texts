Radio Broadcasting Services; Buttonwillow and Shafter, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of American General Media of Texas, Inc., this 
document substitutes Channel 226A for Channel 282A at Shafter, 
California, and modifies the Station KCOO license to specify operation 
on Channel 226A. This document also allots Channel 265A to 
Buttonwillow, California, as a first local service. See 67 FR 52925, 
published August 14, 2002. The reference coordinates for the Channel 
226A allotment at Shafter, California are 35-30-06 and 119-16-18. The 
reference coordinates for the Channel 265A allotment at Buttonwillow, 
California, are 35-23-56 and 119-29-52. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 31, 2002.

2177.
