DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management

Extension of the Environmental Assessment Comment Period for the 
Proposed Amendment of the California Desert Conservation Area Plan, 
1980

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management announces an extension of the 
comment period until January 25, 1999. The extension is in response to 
public requests for additional time to submit comments on the proposed 
plan amendment.
