SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2811]


U.S. Territory of the Virgin Islands; Amendment #3

    The above numbered Declaration is hereby amended, effective 
December 20, 1995 to extend the termination date for filing 
applications for physical damage until January 15, 1996. The 
termination date for economic injury remains the same, June 17, 1996, 
at the previously designated location.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: December 21, 1995.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
