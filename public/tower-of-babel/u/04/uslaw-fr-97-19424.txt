DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Deschutes Provincial Interagency Executive Committee (PIEC), 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Deschutes PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on August 27, 
1997 at the Welcome Center on Highway 97 in Bend, OR. The meeting will 
start at 9:00 a.m. and finish at 5:00 p.m. Agenda items include: (1) 
Completion of comments on the DEIS documents for the Eastside Ecosystem 
project and (2) Open public forum. All Deschutes Province Advisory 
Committee meetings are open to the public.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Beyer, Province Liaison, USDA, 
Bend-Fort Rock Ranger District, 1230 N.E. 3rd, Bend, Oregon 97701, 541-
383-4705.

    Dated: July 15, 1997.
Sally Collins,
Deschutes National Forest Supervisor.
