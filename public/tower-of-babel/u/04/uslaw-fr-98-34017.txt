DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-35,272]


Mead Corporation; Rumford, Maine; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 250 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 30, 1998, in response to a petition filed on 
the same date on behalf of workers at Mead Corporation, Rumford, Maine.
    Currently, there is a petition investigation (TA-W-35,090) in 
progress for the workers at the subject firm. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 10th day of December 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office Trade Adjustment Assistance.
