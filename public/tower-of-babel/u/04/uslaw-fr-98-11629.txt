COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Proposed Additions to the Procurement List; Correction

issue of April 24, 1998, in the second column, the service listed as

Materials Store, Shaw Air Force Base, South Carolina.''
