DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


Carteret Federal Savings Bank of New Jersey; Notice of 
Appointment of Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Section 5 (d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision has duly appointed the Resolution Trust Corporation as sole 
Receiver for Carteret Federal Savings Bank of New Jersey, Newark, New 
Jersey, on January 20, 1995.

    Dated: February 8, 1995.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
