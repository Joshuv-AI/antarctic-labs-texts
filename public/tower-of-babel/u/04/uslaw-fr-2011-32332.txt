DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Nuclear Energy Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Department of Energy, Office of Nuclear Energy.

ACTION: Notice of Renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Section 14(a)(2)(A) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act, App. 2, and Section 102-3.65(a), Title 41, Code of 
Federal Regulations, and following consultation with the Committee 
Management Secretariat, General Services Administration, notice is 
hereby given that the Nuclear Energy Advisory Committee will be renewed 
for a two-year period.
    The Committee will provide advice to the Department of Energy on 
complex science and technical issues that arise in the planning, 
managing, and implementation of DOE's nuclear energy program.
    Additionally, the renewal of the NEAC has been determined to be 
essential to conduct business of the Department of Energy's and to be 
the in the public interest in connection with the performance of duties 
imposed upon the Department of Energy, by law and agreement. The 
Committee will continue to operate in accordance with the provisions of 
the Federal Advisory Committee Act, the rules and regulations in 
implementation of that Act.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kenneth Wade, Designated Federal 
Officer at (301) 903-6509.

    Issued at Washington, DC, on December 12, 2011.
Carol A. Matthews,
Committee Management Officer.
