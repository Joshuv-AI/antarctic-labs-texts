NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Special Emphasis Panel in Undergraduate Education; Notice of 
Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:


    Name: Special Emphasis Panel in Undergraduate Education (1214).
    Date/Time: June 12-13, 2001; 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
    Place: Rooms 320, 330, 340, 365, 370, 380, and 390 National 
Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Closed.
    Contact Person: Dr. Herbert Levitan, National Science 
Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230 Telephone: 
(703) 292-8670.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning the continuation of proposals submitted to NSF for 
financial support.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate Distinguished Teaching Scholars 
proposals as part of the selection process for awards.
    Reason for Closing: The project being reviewed includes 
information of a proprietary or confidential nature, including 
technical information; financial data, such as salaries; and 
personal information concerning individual associated with the 
proposals. These matters are exempt under 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(c), (4) 
and (6) of the Government in the Sunshine Act.


    Dated: March 6, 2001.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
