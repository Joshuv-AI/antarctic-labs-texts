Army Science Board; Notice of Closed Meeting In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following Committee Meeting: Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB) Dates of Meeting: 11-12 March 1996 Place: Los Angeles Air Force Base, California Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Summer Study on ``Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)'' will meet for briefings and discussions on the Army's Concept of Employment for UAVs and view UAV training operations. This meeting will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 522b(c) of Title 5, U.S.C., specifically paragraph (1) thereof, and Title 5, U.S.C., Appendix 2, subsection 10(d). The classified and unclassified matters to be discussed are so inextricably intertwined so as to preclude opening any portion of this meeting. For further information, please contact Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-0781. Michelle P. Diaz, Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
