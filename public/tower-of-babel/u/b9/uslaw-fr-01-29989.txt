WTO Dispute Settlement Proceeding Brought by Brazil Regarding 
Antidumping Duties Imposed by the United States on Silicon Metal From 
Brazil

AGENCY: Office of the United States Trade Representative.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR) is 
providing notice of a correction to the notice published in the Federal 
Register on November 21, 2001, 66FR 58546, titled ``WTO Dispute 
Settlement Proceeding Brought by Brazil Regarding Antidumping Duties 
Imposed by the United States on Silicon Metal from Brazil.'' In the 
section of that notice titled ``Major Issues Raised by Brazil,'' the 
second sentence of the first paragraph should read, ``However, section 
351.106(c) of the DOC's regulation, 19 CFR 351.106(c), applies a 0.5 
percent de minimis standard in the case of reviews.''

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Katherine J. Mueller, Assistant 
General Counsel, Office of the United States Trade Representative, 600 
17th Street, NW., Washington, DC, (202) 395-0317.

A. Jane Bradley,
Assistant United States Trade Representative for Monitoring and 
Enforcement.
