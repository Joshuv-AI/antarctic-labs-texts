OFFICE OF MANAGEMENT AND BUDGET


Discount Rates for Cost-Effectiveness Analysis of Federal 
Programs

AGENCY: Office of Management and Budget

ACTION: Revisions to Appendix C of OMB Circular A-94

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) revised Circular A-
94 in 1992. With this action, OMB has revised the Circular and 
specified certain discount rates to be updated annually when the 
interest rate and inflation assumptions used to prepare the Budget of 
the United States Government were changed. These updated discount rates 
are found in Appendix C of the revised Circular and are to be used for 
cost-effectiveness analysis, including lease-purchase analysis, as 
specified in the revised Circular. These rates do not apply to 
regulatory analysis.
    The revised Circular can be accessed at https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Appendix-C.pdf.

DATES: The revised discount rates will be in effect through December 
2021.
