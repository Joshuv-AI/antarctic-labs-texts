PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.; Notice of Staff Technical Conference

    On December 14, 2011, the Commission issued an order in this 
proceeding establishing a technical conference to explore the issues 
presented by PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.'s (PJM) filing.\1\ Take notice 
that the technical conference will be held on February 14, 2012 
beginning at approximately 10 a.m. (EDT) at the Commission's 
headquarters, located at 888 First Street NE., Washington, DC 20426. 
The conference will consist of a presentation by PJM addressing the 
questions listed in the December 14 Order interspersed with questions 
from Commission staff. To expedite the conference, PJM is requested to 
file written answers to the Commission's questions by February 10, 
2012.
