DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. CP02-45-000]


Texas Eastern Transmission, LP; Notice of Site Visit

January 31, 2002.
    On Wednesday, February 20, 2002, the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission (FERC) staff will conduct a site visit of Texas Eastern 
Transmission, L.P.'s (Texas Eastern) Hanging Rock Lateral Project in 
Scioto and Lawrence Counties, Ohio. We will visit sites along the 9.6-
mile-long pipeline project.
    We will meet at the following location at 9 AM on Wednesday 
February 20, 2002: Texas Eastern's Right-of-Way Office, 433 Center 
Street, Wheelersburg, Ohio 45694.
    For further information call the Office of External Affairs, at 
(202) 208-0004.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
