NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Notice of Meeting

Time and Date: 10 a.m., Thursday, September 19, 2002.

Place: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047, 1775 Duke Street, Alexandria, 
VA 22314-3428.

Status: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:
    1. Request from a Federal Credit Union to Convert to a Community 
Charter.
    2. Proposed Rule: Part 740 of NCUA's Rules and Regulations, 
Advertising.
    3. Proposed Rule: Section 701.19 of NCUA's Rules and Regulations, 
Retirement Benefits for Federal Credit Union Employees.
    4. Proposed Rule: Part 741 of NCUA's Rules and Regulations, 
Overseas Branching by Federally Insured Credit Unions.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Becky Baker, Secretary of the Board, 
Telephone: 703-518-6304.

Becky Baker,
Secretary of the Board.
