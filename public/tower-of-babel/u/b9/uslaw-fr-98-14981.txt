The Proctor and Gamble Manufacturing Company, Health Care 
Division, Greenville, SC; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 27, 1998 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on April 15, 1998 on behalf of workers at Proctor & Gamble, 
Health Care Division, Greenville, South Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 27th day of May, 1998
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
