DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 24, 2005, 9 a.m. to June 
24, 4 p.m., Marriott Bethesda North Conference Center, 5701 Marinelli 
Rd, Bethesda, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal Register on 
May 26, 2005, 70 FR 30475-30477.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the reassignment of the 
applications.

    Dated: May 31, 2005.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
