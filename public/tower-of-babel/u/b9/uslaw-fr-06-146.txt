DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Lower Tucannon Ecosystem Management Project, Umatilla National 
Forest, Columbia County, WA

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On July 9, 2003, a Notice of Intent (NOI) to prepare an 
environmental impact statement (EIS) for the Lower Tucannon Ecosystem 
Management Project on the Pomeroy Ranger District of the Umatilla 
National Forest, was published in the Federal Register (68 FR 40900). 
The Forest Service has decided to cancel the preparation of this EIS. 
The NOI is herby rescinded.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Questions maybe addressed to Dean 
Millett, Timber Management Assistant, Pomeroy Ranger District, 71 West 
Main Street, Pomeroy, WA 99374, telephone 509-843-1891.

    Dated: January 3, 2006.
Kevin Martin,
Forest Supervisor.
