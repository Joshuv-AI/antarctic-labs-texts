DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Geriatrics and Gerontology Advisory Group, Notice of Meeting; 
Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act that a meeting of the Geriatrics and 
Gerontology Advisory Group (the Committee) that was scheduled for April 
1, 2020 has been postponed. Details on a future meeting will be posted 
at a later date.

    Dated: March 30, 2020.
LaTonya L. Small,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
