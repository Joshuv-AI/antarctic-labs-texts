SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Wisconsin State Advisory Council; Public Hearing

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Wisconsin State Advisory 
Council, located in the geographical area of Milwaukee, Wisconsin, will 
hold a public meeting from 12:00 p.m. to 1:00 p.m. January 21, 1999 at 
Metro Milwaukee Area Chamber (MMAC) Association of Commerce Building; 
756 North Milwaukee Street, Fourth Floor, Milwaukee, Wisconsin to 
discuss such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. 
Small Business Administration, or others present.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Yolanda Lassiter, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, 310 West Wisconsin Avenue, Milwaukee, Wisconsin 53203; 
(414) 297-1092.

    Dated: January 8, 1999.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
