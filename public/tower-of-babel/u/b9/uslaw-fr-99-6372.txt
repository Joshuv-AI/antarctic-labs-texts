Employment and Training Administration

F.W.A.-J.S.M. Drilling Company, Midland, Texas; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 22, 1999 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on January 29, 1999 on behalf of workers at F.W.A.-
J.S.M. Drilling Company, Midland, Texas.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-34,550). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of February, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
