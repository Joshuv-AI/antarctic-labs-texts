Notice of Application for Withdrawal and Public Meeting; Oregon; 
Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) published a notice in the 
Federal Register on August 1, 2011 (76 FR 45848) that contained an 
incorrect deadline for making public comments and an incorrect location 
and time for a public meeting.

Correction:

    On page 45848, in the first column, line 45, correct the DATES 
caption to read:

DATES: Comments must be received by November 30, 2011.''

    On page 45848, in the second column, line 63, correct the date to 
read: ``November 30, 2011.''
    On page 45848, in the third column, line 19, correct the date and 
meeting location to read: ``will be held on October 26, 2011, from 1 
p.m. to 3 p.m. and from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. at the Best Western Beachfront 
Inn, 16008 Boat Basin Road, Brookings, Oregon 97415.''
