DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, February 11, 2009, 8 a.m. to 
February 13, 2009, 5 p.m., Hilton Washington/Rockville, 1750 Rockville 
Pike, Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal Register 
on October 15, 2008, 73 FR 61143.
    This notice is amended to change the date & time from February 11, 
2009, 8 a.m. to February 13, 2009, 5 p.m. to February 11, 7 a.m. to 
February 12, 2009, 5 p.m. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: November 20, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
