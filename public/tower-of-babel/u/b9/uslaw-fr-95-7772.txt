U.S. DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Minority Business Development Agency


Native American Business Development Center Applications: 
Minnesota

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications to organizations to 
operate its Minnesota Native American Business Development Center. The 
solicitation was originally published in the Federal 
[[Page 16451]] Register, Wednesday, January 25, 1995, Vol. 60, No.16; 
4890.
