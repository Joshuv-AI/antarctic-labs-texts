Fisher Scientific Company, A Division of Fisher Scientific 
International, Inc., Laboratory Equipment Division, Indiana, PA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 1, 2005, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Fisher 
Scientific Company, a division of Fisher Scientific International, 
Inc., Laboratory Equipment Division, Indiana, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 8th day of March, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
