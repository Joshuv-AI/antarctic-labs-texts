Radio Broadcasting Services; Sibley, IA, and Brandon, SD

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
21st Century Radio Ventures, Inc. directed to the Report and Order in 
this proceeding which allotted Channel 261A to Brandon, South Dakota. 
See 63 FR 64876, November 24, 1998. With this action, the proceeding is 
terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2177.
