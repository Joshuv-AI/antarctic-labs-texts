DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
Minority Business Development Agency


Nationwide Capital Development Center

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
competitive solicitation for operation of the Nationwide Capital 
Development Center. The solicitation was originally published in the 
Federal Register, Tuesday, July 16, 1996, Vol. 61, No. 137, Page 37047.

    Dated: November 20, 1996.
Donald L. Powers,
Federal Register Liaison Officer, Minority Business Development Agency.
