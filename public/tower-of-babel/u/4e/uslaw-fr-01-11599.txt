DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, April 30, 2001, 8 a.m. to May 
1, 2001, 5 p.m., Ramada Inn Rockville, 1775 Rockville Pike, Rockville, 
MD 20852 which was published in the Federal Register on April 19, 2001, 
66 FR 20151.

2001. The meeting is closed to the public.
