DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

[Treasury Order Number 102-21]


Designation of the Assistant Secretary for Management and Chief 
Financial Officer as the Chief Operating Officer

    Dated: February 23, 1998.

    1. By virtue of the authority vested in the Secretary of the 
Treasury, including the authority vested by 31 U.S.C. 321(b), I hereby 
designate the Assistant Secretary for Management and Chief Financial 
Officer as the Department's Chief Operating Officer for purposes of the 
Presidential Memorandum, ``Implementing Management Reform in the 
Executive Branch,'' dated October 1, 1993.
    2. This Order shall expire January 20, 2001.
Robert E. Rubin,
Secretary of the Treasury.
