SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2837]


Washington; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area (Amendment #1)

    In accordance with notices from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated February 26, March 4, and March 6, 1996, the above-
numbered Declaration is hereby amended to include Kitsap, Lincoln, and 
Skagit Counties in the State of Washington as a disaster area due to 
damages caused by high winds, severe storms, and flooding beginning on 
January 26, 1996 and continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous Washington counties of Ferry, 
Okanogan, San Juan, Stevens, and Whatcom may be filed until the 
specified date at the previously designated location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for physical damage is April 11, 1996, and for 
loans for economic injury the deadline is November 12, 1996.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: March 15, 1996.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator For Disaster Assistance.
