GOVERNMENT PRINTING OFFICE


Depository Library Council to the Public Printer; Meeting

    The Depository Library Council to the Public Printer will meet (due 
to the Government shutdown in October the meeting has been rescheduled) 
on Wednesday, April 30, 2014, through Friday, May 2, 2014, from 8:30 
a.m. to 5:30 p.m., in Washington, DC. The meeting will be held at the 
U.S. Government Printing Office, located at 732 North Capitol Street 
NW., Washington, DC. The purpose of this meeting is to discuss the 
Federal Depository Library Program. All sessions are open to the 
public. The U.S. Government Printing Office is in compliance with the 
requirements of Title III of the Americans With Disabilities Act and 
meets all Fire Safety Act regulations.

Davita Vance-Cooks,
Public Printer of the United States.
