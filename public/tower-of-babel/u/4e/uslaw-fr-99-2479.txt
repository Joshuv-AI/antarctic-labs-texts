DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management
[NV-020-1430-00]


Notice of Availability of Approved Plan Amendment and Decision 
Record; Nevada

January 25, 1999.
AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Availability, Decision Record for the Approved 
Paradise-Denio and Sonoma-Gerlach Management Framework Plans Lands 
Amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

DATES: The Approved Paradise-Denio and Sonoma-Gerlach Management 
Framework Plans Lands Amendment and Decision Record will be distributed 
and made available to the public on or around February 16, 1999.

ADDRESS: A copy of the Approved Plan Amendment and Decision Record can 
be obtained from: Bureau of Land Management, Winnemucca Field Office, 
5100 East Winnemucca Boulevard, Winnemucca, Nevada 89445.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mary Figarelle, Realty Specialist, 
Winnemucca Field Office, 5100 E. Winnemucca Boulevard, Winnemucca, 
Nevada 89445, (775) 623-1500.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Approved Lands Amendment and Decision 
Record complete the land use planning process, and document the changes 
to various decisions as they pertain to the retention, acquisition, and 
disposal of public lands managed by the Winnemucca Field Office of the 
Bureau of Land Management.
Terry A. Reed,
Field Manager.
