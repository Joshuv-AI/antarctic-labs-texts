ACTION: Final rule, dismissal of petition for reconsideration.

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a Petition for Reconsideration filed 
by Indiana University and denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed 
by Indiana Community Radio Corporation both directed to the Report and 
Order in this proceeding. With this action, the proceeding is 
terminated.
