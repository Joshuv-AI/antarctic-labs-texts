DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Secret Service


Appointment of Performance Review Board (PRB) Members

    This notice announces the appointment of members of the Senior 
Executive Service Performance Review Boards in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 
4314(c)(4) for the rating period beginning October 1, 2000, and ending 
September 30, 2001. Each PRB will be composed of at least three of the 
Senior Executive Service members listed below.

Name and Title
    Larry L. Cockell, Deputy Director, U.S. Secret Service
    James E. Bauer, Assistant Director, Investigations (USSS)
    Carlton D. Spriggs, Assistant Director, Protective Operations 
(USSS)
    Barbara S. Riggs, Assistant Director, Protective Research (USSS)
    Dana A. Brown, Assistant Director, Administration (USSS)
    George D. Rogers, Assistant Director, Inspection (USSS)
    Donald A. Flynn, Assistant Director, Human Resources and Training 
(USSS)
    H. Terrence Samway, Assistant Director, Government Liaison & Public 
Affairs (USSS)
    John J. Kelleher, Chief Counsel (USSS)

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sheila M. Lumsden, Chief, Personnel 
Division, 950 H St., NW., Suite 7400,
