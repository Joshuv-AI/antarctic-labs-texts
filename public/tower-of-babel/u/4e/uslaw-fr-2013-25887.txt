DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, Genetic Epidemiology, October 
29, 2013, 09:30 a.m. to October 29, 2013, 10:30 a.m., Wingate by 
Wyndham, 105 State Street, York, PA 17404 which was published in the 
Federal Register on October 03, 2013, 78 FR 192 Pgs. 61376-61377.

December 6, 2013 at 11:00 a.m. The meeting location remains the same. 
The meeting is closed to the public.
