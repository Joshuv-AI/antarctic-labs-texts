ACTION: Meeting withdrawal notice.

SUMMARY: The Board of Visitors of the U.S. Air Force Academy Notice of 
Meeting is withdrawing the notice, this meeting published on 23 March 
2017 [FR 2017-05625].

withdrawing the meeting notice of the Board of Visitors that published 
on the 23rd since this meeting has been amended.
