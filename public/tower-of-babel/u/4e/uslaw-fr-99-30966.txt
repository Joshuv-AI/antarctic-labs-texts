DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board
[Docket 54-99]


Foreign-Trade Zone 21--Charleston, South Carolina, Area; 
Application for Expansion; Correction

    The Federal Register notice (64 FR 61820, November 15, 1999) 
describing the application submitted to the Foreign-Trade Zones Board 
by the South Carolina State Ports Authority, grantee of FTZ 21, 
requesting an expansion of its general-purpose zone to include an 
additional site at the former Charleston Naval Base and Shipyard, 
Cosgrove Avenue, N. Charleston, SC, is corrected as follows: last 
paragraph on page 61821, giving the location for the public inspection 
facility in N. Charleston, SC, has been changed to ``Office of the Port 
Director, U.S. Customs Service, 200 East Bay Street, Charleston, SC 
29401.''

    Dated: November 19, 1999.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Acting Executive Secretary.
