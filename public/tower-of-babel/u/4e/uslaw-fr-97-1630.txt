DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. TM97-5-16-002]


National Fuel Gas Supply Corporation; Notice of Correction to 
Tariff Filing

January 16, 1997.
    Take notice that on January 10, 1997, National Fuel Gas Supply 
Corporation (National) tendered for filing, a correction to part of its 
FERC Gas Tariff, Third Revised Volume No. 1, with a proposed effective 
date of January 1, 1997.
    National states that on January 8, 1997, in Docket No. TM-97-5-16-
001, National filed Fourth Revised Sheet No. 29. However, the plain 
version of Fourth Revised Sheet No. 29 contained an

[[Page 3504]]

error. Accordingly, National submitted Substitute Fourth Revised Sheet 
No. 29 to correct that error.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Rule 211 of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211). All such protests 
must be filed in accordance with Section 154.210 of the Commission's 
Regulations. Protests will be considered by the Commission in 
determining the appropriate action to be taken but will not serve to 
make protestants parties to the proceeding. Copies of this filing are 
on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
