COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: 9:30 a.m. EST, Friday, January 10, 2025.

PLACE: Virtual meeting.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Matters relating to the CFTC's bargaining 
position and related issues concerning ongoing negotiations over CFTC 
employee compensation and benefits. In the event that the time, date, 
or location of this meeting changes, an announcement of the change, 
along with the new time, date, and/or place of the meeting will be 
posted on the Commission's website at https://www.cftc.gov/.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Christopher Kirkpatrick, 202-418-
5964.
