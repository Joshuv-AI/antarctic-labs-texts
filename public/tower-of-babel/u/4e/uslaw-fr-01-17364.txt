DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On May 7, 2001, a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 66, No. 88, Page 23044, that an application had been filed with 
the Fish and Wildlife Service by Dave Dillard for a permit (PRT-041031) 
to import one polar bear (Ursus maritimus) trophy taken from the 
Northern Beaufort Sea population, Canada for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on June 25, 2001, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
    Documents and other information submitted for these applications 
are available for review by any party who submits a written request to 
the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Division of Management Authority, 
4401 North Fairfax Drive, Room 700, Arlington, Virginia 22203. Phone 
(703) 358-2104 or Fax (703) 358-2281.

    Date: June 29, 2001.
Monica Farris,
Senior Permit Biologist, Branch of Permits, Division of Management 
Authority.
