Lee Sportswear, Inc., Plantersville (Tupelo), Mississippi; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 16, 1997 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on June 16, 1997 on behalf of workers at Lee Sportswear, 
Incorporated, located in Plantersville (Tupelo), Mississippi.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 30th day of September, 1997.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
