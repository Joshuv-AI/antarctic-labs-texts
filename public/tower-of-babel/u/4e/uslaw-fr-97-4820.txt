HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES


Designation of Agent To Receive Child Support and Alimony Orders 
and Process Pursuant to Sec. 362 of Pub. L. 104-193

AGENCY: U.S. House of Representatives.

ACTION: Notice: Designation of Agent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity 
Reconciliation Act of 1996 (Pub. L. 104-193, Sec. 362), the United 
States House of Representatives designates the Office of General 
Counsel for the House to receive orders and accept service of process 
in matters relating to child support or alimony.

ADDRESSES: Such orders and process shall be directed to: Office of 
General Counsel, U.S. House of Representatives, 219 Cannon Building, 
Washington, DC 20515, (202) 225-9700.

    Authority: 42 U.S.C. Sec. 659, as amended by Sec. 362, Pub. L. 
104-193, 110 Stat. 2105.

    Dated: February 21, 1997.
Robin H. Carle,
Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives.
