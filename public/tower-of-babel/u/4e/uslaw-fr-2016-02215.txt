FEDERAL COMMUNICATION COMMISSION


Schedule Change to Sunshine Act Meeting

January 28, 2016.
    Please note that the time for the Federal Communications Commission 
Open Meeting is rescheduled from 10:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
    The Federal Communications Commission will consider the Agenda 
items listed on the Commission's Notice of January 21 at the Open 
Meeting on Thursday, January 28, 2016, scheduled to commence at 1:00 
p.m. in room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street SW., Washington, DC.
    The prompt and orderly conduct of the Commission's business 
requires this change and no earlier announcement was practicable.

Federal Communications Commission.
Marlene H. Dortch,
Secretary.
