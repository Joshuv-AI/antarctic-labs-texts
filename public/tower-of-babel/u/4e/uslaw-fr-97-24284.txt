DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records Notice

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DOD.
ACTION: Notice to amend record systems.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: 
    The Department of the Army proposes to amend the preamble to the 
Army's compilation of Privacy Act systems of records notices. The 
amendment consists of deleting the For more information contact: 
paragraph, and adding two new paragraphs.
EFFECTIVE DATE: September 15, 1997.

Program Division, U.S.
