DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR


Draft Environmental Impact Statement/Environmental Impact Report 
on the Draft Truckee River Operating Agreement; Correction

AGENCY: Department of the Interior.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Interior published a document in the 
Federal Register issue of March 13, 1998, concerning the availability 
for a draft environmental impact statement/draft environmental impact 
report; INT-DES-98-8. The comment period has been extended to June 29, 
1998.

Correction

    In the Federal Register issue of March 13, 1998, in FR Doc. 98-6517 
on page 12502, in the first column, replace the date by which written 
comments must be received with the following:

DATES: Written comments on the draft EIS/EIR should be submitted to the 
Bureau of Reclamation no later than June 29, 1998.

ADDRESSES: Written comments on the draft EIS/EIR should be addressed 
to: TROA Draft EIS/EIR Comments, Bureau of Reclamation, Lahontan Basin 
Area Office, PO Box 640, Carson City, NV 89702-0640.
