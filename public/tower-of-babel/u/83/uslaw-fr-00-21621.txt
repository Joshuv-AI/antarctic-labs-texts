SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Economic Injury Disaster #9I06, Amdt. #1]


State of Alaska

    The above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended to include the 
Regional Educational Attendance Area #45, Southwest Region (previously 
referred to as REAA #6), as a contiguous area as a result of a fishery 
resource disaster, as determined by the Secretary of Commerce, due to 
extremely low salmon returns beginning in 1997 and continuing.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., applications for 
economic injury may be filed until May 9, 2001.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59002.)

    Dated: August 16, 2000.
Kris Swedin,
Acting Administrator.
