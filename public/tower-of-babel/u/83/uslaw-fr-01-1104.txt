GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Office of Communications; Cancellation of a Standard Form

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Agriculture and the Department of Interior 
has cancelled the following Optional Form because of low usage: OF 289, 
Property Loss or Damage Report--Fire Suppression.

DATES: Effective upon publication in the Federal Register.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Barbara Williams, General Services 
Administration, (202) 501-0581.

Barbara M. Williams,
