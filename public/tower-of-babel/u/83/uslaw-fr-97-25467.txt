DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


Delegation of Authority

    Notice is hereby given that I have delegated to the Director, 
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, with authority to 
redelegate, the following authorities vested in the Secretary of Health 
and Human Services under Title XXVI of the Public Health Service Act, 
``Health Care Services Program,'' and the Ryan White Care Act 
Amendments of 1996 (P.L. 104-146), as amended hereafter, insofar as 
these authorities pertain to the functions assigned to the Centers for 
Disease Control and Prevention:

Section 2625--CDC Guidelines for Pregnant Women
Section 2626--Perinatal Transmission of HIV Disease; Contingent 
Requirement Regarding State Grants Under This Part
Section 2628--Report by the Institute of Medicine
Sections 2641-2650--Formula Grants for States
Sections 2661-2667--General Provisions
Section 2675--Coordinaiton
Section 2680--Grants to States and political subdivisions of States 
to implement guidelines and model curriculum for health workers and 
public safety workers, including emergency response employees.
Sections 2681-2690--Notificaiton of Possible Exposure to Infectious 
Diseases

    The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the Health 
Resources and Services Administration shall cooperate in the 
implementation of Section 2626(e).
    This delegation shall be excised under the Department's existing 
delegation of authority and policy on regulations.
    This delegation became effective upon date of signature. In 
addition, I have affirmed and ratified any actions taken by the 
Director, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention or his 
subordinates which involved the exercise of the authorities delegated 
herein prior to the effective date of the delegation.

    Dated: September 15, 1997.
Donna E. Shalala,
Secretary.
