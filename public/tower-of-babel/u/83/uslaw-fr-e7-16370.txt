Agency Information Collection Activities: Submission for OMB

ACTION: Correction of total burden hours.

SUMMARY: The Health Resources and Services Administration published an 
Agency Information Collection document in the Federal Register of July 
31, 2007 (FR Doc. E7-14680), on page 41759, regarding Bureau of Primary 
Health Care (BPHC) Uniform Data System (OMB No. 0915-0193). In the 
burden table, the total burden hours published are incorrect.

Correction

    In the Federal Register issue of July 31, 2007, FR Doc. E7-14680), 
on page 41759, correct the Total Burden Hours as follows:
