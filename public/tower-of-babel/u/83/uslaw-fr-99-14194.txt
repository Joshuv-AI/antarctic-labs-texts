DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management
[ES-960-1910-4595] ES-50412, Group 200, Florida


Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Florida

    The plat of the dependent resurvey of fractional sections 20 and 
29, and the survey of lots 8 and 9 in fractional section 20, Township 9 
South, Range 11 West, Tallahassee Meridian, Florida, will be officially 
filed in Eastern States, Springfield, VA at 7:30 a.m., on July 9, 1999.
    The survey was requested by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers.
    All inquiries or protests concerning the technical aspects of the 
survey must be sent to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor, Eastern States, 
Bureau of Land

[[Page 30058]]

Management 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, VA 22153, prior to 7:30 
a.m., July 9, 1999.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the appropriate fee.

    Dated: May 25, 1999.
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
