Preparation for Global Harmonization Task Force Conference in 
Barcelona, Spain, Including a Discussion of Guidance Proposed for 
Comment and Currently Under Development and Possibilities for New 
Topics; Public Meeting; Cancellation

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is canceling the public 
meeting for the Global Harmonization Task Force Conference in 
Barcelona, Spain scheduled for October 1, 2001. The meeting was 
announced in the Federal Register of September 13, 2001 (66 FR 47676). 
It will be rescheduled at a later date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kimberly Topper, Center for Drug 
Evaluation and Research (HFD-21), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 
Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-7001.

    Dated: September 25, 2001.
Margaret M. Dotzel,
Associate Commissioner for Policy.
