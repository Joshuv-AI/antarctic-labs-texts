DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Draft Environmental Impact Statement/General Management Plan, 
Death Valley National Park, California; Notice of Extension of Public 
Comment Period

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Sec. 102(2)(C) of the National Environmental 
Policy Act of 1969 (P.L. 91-190 as amended), the National Park Service, 
Department of the Interior, has prepared a Draft Environmental Impact 
Statement (DEIS) assessing three alternatives for, and potential 
impacts of, a proposed General Management Plan for Death Valley 
National Park, California. In deference to public interest expressed by 
local governmental agencies, organizations, and other interested 
parties, the original 90-day public comment period has been extended an 
additional five weeks through January 15, 1999.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Copies of the DEIS can be reviewed at local 
libraries, and internet access is available at www.nps.gov/deva. A 
limited number of copies may still be available upon phone request at 
(760) 786-2331. All written comments must be postmarked not later than 
January 15, 1999, and should be sent to the Superintendent, Death 
Valley National Park, Furnace Creek, CA 92328.

    Dated: November 20, 1998.
Cynthia L. Ip,
Acting Regional Director, Pacific West.
