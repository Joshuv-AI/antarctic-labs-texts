SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Introduction

    The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has prepared an 
Interim Staff Guidance (ISG) No. 23 document, entitled ``Application of 
ASTM Standard Practice C1671-07 when performing technical reviews of 
spent fuel storage and transportation packaging licensing actions.'' 
This ISG document would provide guidance to the NRC staff when 
reviewing licensee integrated safety analyses, license or Certificate 
of Compliance applications or amendment requests, or other related 
activities for dry cask storage systems under Title 10 of the Code of 
Federal Regulations (10 CFR) part 71 and 10 CFR part 72.
