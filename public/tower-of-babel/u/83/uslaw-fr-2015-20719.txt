Lost Creek ISR, LLC; Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Temporary Exemption; issuance; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register on July 24, 2015, 
that gave notice to the public that it is considering issuance of a 
temporary exemption from certain NRC financial assurance requirements 
to Lost Creek ISR, LLC, for its Lost Creek In Situ Recovery (ISSR) 
Project in Crook County, Wyoming. This action is being taken to correct 
the date that the exemption expires.

DATES: This correction is effective on August 21, 2015.
