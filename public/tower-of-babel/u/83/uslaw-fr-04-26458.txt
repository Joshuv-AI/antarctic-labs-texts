FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Acquisition of Shares of Bank or 
Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 04-25773) published on page 
67913 of the issue for Monday, November 22, 2004.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for Embry W. Williams, Jr., Amarillo, Texas, is revised to read as 
follows:
    1. Embry W. Williams, Jr., Amarillo, Texas; Embry W. Williams, III, 
Richardson, Texas; and James David Williams, Kerrville, Texas; to 
acquire voting shares of Union Bancshares, Inc., Clayton, New Mexico, 
and thereby indirectly acquire voting shares of The First National Bank 
of New Mexico, Clayton, New Mexico.
    Comments on this application must be received by December 6, 2004.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, November 26, 
2004.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
