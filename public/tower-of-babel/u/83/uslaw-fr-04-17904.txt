Digital Television Broadcast Service; Apalachicola, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Liberty County Educational, 
allots DTV channel 3 to Apalachicola, Florida, as the community's first 
local commercial television service. See 69 FR 9790, March 2, 2004. DTV 
channel 3 can be allotted to Apalachicola in compliance with the 
Sections 73.623(d) and 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 29-45-05 N. 
and 84-52-19 W. with a power of 45 and HAAT of 305 meters. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective September 13, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
