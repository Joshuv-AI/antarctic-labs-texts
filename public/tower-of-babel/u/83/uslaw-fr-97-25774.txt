DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Indian Affairs


Mission Valley Power Utility, Montana Power Rate Adjustment

ACTION: Notice of rate adjustment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) is adjusting the electric 
power rates for customers of Mission Valley Power (MVP), the 
Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribal entity operating the power 
facility of the Flathead Irrigation and Power Project of the Flathead 
Reservation under a Public Law 93-638 contract. The Notice of Proposed 
Rate Adjustment for MVP was published in the Federal Register on August 
18, 1997, 62 FR 44013. The public and interested parties were provided 
an opportunity to submit written comments during the 30-day period 
subsequent to August 18, 1997. No comments were received. The following 
table illustrates the impact of the rate adjustment:
