Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line Corporation; Notice of Site Visit

March 19, 1997.
    On April 2-3, 1997, beginning at 12:00 p.m., the Office of Pipeline 
Regulation (OPR) staff will conduct a site visit with Transcontinental 
Gas Pipe Line Corporation of the proposed Maiden Lateral Looping 
Project in Lincoln and Catawba Counties, North Carolina.
    All parties may attend. Those planning to attend must provide their 
own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.
Robert J. Cupina,
Deputy Director, Office of Pipeline Regulation.
