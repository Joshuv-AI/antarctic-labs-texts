DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
[Dept. Circ. 570, 1994--Rev., Supp. No. 11; 4-00236]


Municipal Bond Investors Assurance Corporation; Surety Companies 
Acceptable on Federal Bonds; Termination of Authority

    Notice is hereby given that the Certificate of Authority issued by 
the Treasury to Municipal Bond Investors Assurance Corporation of 
Armonk, New York, under the United States Code, Title 31, Sections 
9304-9308 to qualify as an acceptable surety on Federal bonds is 
terminated effective today.
    The Company was last listed as an acceptable surety on Federal 
bonds at 59 FR 34166, July 1, 1994, and subsequently suspended 
effective December 20, 1994, at 60 FR 531, January 4, 1995.
    With respect to any surety bonds currently in force with Municipal 
Bond Investors Assurance Corporation, bond-approving officers may let 
such bonds run to expiration and need not secure new bonds. However, no 
new bonds should be accepted from the Company. In addition, bonds that 
are continuous in nature should not be renewed.
    Questions concerning this notice may be directed to the Department 
of the Treasury, Financial Management Service, Funds Management 
Division, Surety Bond Branch, Hyattsville, MD 20782, telephone (202/
FTS) 874-6779.

    Dated: February 28, 1995.
Charles F. Schwan III,
Director, Funds Management Division, Financial Management Service.
