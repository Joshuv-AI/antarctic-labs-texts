SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Economic Injury Disaster #9974]


State of New York and Contiguous Counties in the State of New 
Jersey

    New York County and the contiguous counties of Bronx, Kings, 
Queens, and Richmond in the State of New York, and Bergen and Hudson 
Counties in the State of New Jersey constitute an economic injury 
disaster loan area as a result of a construction accident that occurred 
on July 21, 1998 in Manhattan. Eligible small businesses and small 
agricultural cooperatives without credit available elsewhere may file 
applications for economic injury assistance as a result of this 
disaster until the close of business on May 14, 1999 at the address 
listed below or other locally announced locations: Small Business 
Administration, Disaster Area 1 Office, 360 Rainbow Blvd, South 3rd 
Floor, Niagara Falls, NY 14303.
    The interest rate for eligible small businesses and small 
agricultural cooperatives is 4 percent.
    The economic injury number for the State of New Jersey is 997500.
