NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Public Hearing

    The National Transportation Safety Board will convene a public 
hearing beginning at 9:00 a.m., local time on Wednesday, February 17, 
1999, at the Hyatt Regency Hotel, 400 SE 2nd Avenue, Miami, Florida 
33131-2197 concerning the Fire Aboard Cruise Ship Ecstasy on July 20, 
1998. For more information, contact Donald Tyrrell, NTSB Office of 
Marine Safety at (202) 314-6455 or Ted Lopatkiewicz, NTSB Office of 
Public Affairs at (202) 314-6100.

    Dated: January 14, 1999.
Rhonda Underwood,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
