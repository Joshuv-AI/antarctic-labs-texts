2007-2012 Strategic Plan Notice; Request for Comments

AGENCY: Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS), Treasury.

ACTION: Notice and request for comment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS) updates its Strategic 
Plan every three years. The mission and strategic goals contained in 
the Plan support statutory and regulatory requirements, current and 
long-range industry issues, and long-range strategic objectives. The 
goals and objectives are implemented through annual Performance Plans. 
OTS requests comments on its draft 2007-2012 Strategic Plan. The draft 
Plan is available on the OTS Internet Site at http://www.ots.treas.gov 
under ``About OTS: Plans and Reports''.

DATES: Comments must be submitted by July 20, 2007.
