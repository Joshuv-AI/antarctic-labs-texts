DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY


Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice lists the membership to the Departmental Offices' 
Performance Review Board (PRB) and supersedes the list published in 
Federal Register page 47230, Vol. 64, No. 167, dated August 30, 1999, 
in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4). The purpose of the PRB is to 
review the performance of members of the Senior Executive Service and 
make recommendations regarding performance ratings, performance awards, 
and other personnel actions.
