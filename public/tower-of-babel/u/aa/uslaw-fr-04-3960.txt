Radio Broadcasting Services; Montauk, NY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The document denies a Petition for Rule Making filed by Dana 
J. Puopolo, which requested the allotment of Channel 261A to Montauk, 
New York, as that community's third local aural transmission service. 
See 68 FR 42663, July 18, 2003.
