London International Group, LLC, Centre Plant, Dothan, AL; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on January 24, 2000, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on the same date on behalf of workers at London 
International Group, LLC, Centre Plant, Dothan, Alabama
    The company official submitting the petition has requested that the 
petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case 
would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 29th day of February, 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
