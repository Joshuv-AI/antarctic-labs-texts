STS Hydropower, LLC; Sugarloaf Hydro, LLC; Notice of Application 
for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To Intervene, 
and Protests

    On October 12, 2021, STS Hydropower, LLC (transferor) and Sugarloaf 
Hydro, LLC (transferee) filed jointly an application for the transfer 
of license of the Sugarloaf Hydroelectric Project No. 3819. The project 
is located at the Bureau of Reclamation's Mt. Elbert Conduit, Leadville 
City, in Lake County, Colorado.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Sugarloaf Hydroelectric Project from the transferor to the 
transferee. The transferee will be required by the Commission to comply 
with all the requirements of the license as though it were the original 
licensee.
