Victor Forstmann, Inc.; East Dublin, GA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 24, 2005 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the State of Georgia on behalf of workers at Victor 
Forstmann, Inc., East Dublin, Georgia.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 7th day of June, 2005.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
