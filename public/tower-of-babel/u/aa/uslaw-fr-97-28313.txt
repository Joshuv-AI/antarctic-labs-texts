DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board
[Order No. 928]


Expansion of Foreign-Trade Zone 182 Fort Wayne, Indiana, Area

    Pursuant to its authority under the Foreign-Trade Zones Act of June 
18, 1934, as amended (19 U.S.C. 81a-81u), the Foreign-Trade Zones Board 
(the Board) adopts the following Order:
    Whereas, an application from the City of Fort Wayne, Indiana, 
grantee of Foreign-Trade Zone 182, for authority to

[[Page 55574]]

expand FTZ 182-Site 3 in Fort Wayne and include an additional site in 
Huntington, Indiana, was filed by the Board on February 5, 1997 (FTZ 
Docket 6-97, 62 FR 7749, 2/20/97);
    Wheareas, notice inviting public comment was given in Federal 
Register and the application has been processed pursuant to the FTZ Act 
and the Board's regulations; and,
    Wheareas, the Board adopts the findings and recommendations of the 
examiner's report, and finds that the requirements of the FTZ Act and 
Board's regulations are satisfied, and that the proposal is in the 
public interest;
    Now, Therefore, the Board hereby orders:
    The application to expand FTZ 182 is approved, subject to the Act 
and the Board's regulations, including Section 400.28.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of October 1997.
Robert S. LaRussa,
Assistant Secretary of Commerce for Import Administration, Alternate 
Chairman Foreign-Trade Zones Board.

    Attest:
John J. Da Ponte, Jr.,
Executive Secretary.
