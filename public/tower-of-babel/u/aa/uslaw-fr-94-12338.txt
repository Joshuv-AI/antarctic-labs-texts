Bedford Federal Savings Bank, Bedford, Virginia; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on May 13, 1994, the Deputy Assistant 
Director, Corporate Activities Division, Office of Thrift Supervision, 
or her designee, acting pursuant to delegated authority, approved the 
application of Bedford Federal Savings Bank, Bedford, Virginia, convert 
to the stock form of organization. Copies of the application are 
available for inspection at the Information Services Division, Office 
of Thrift Supervision, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and 
the Southeast Regional Office, Office of Thrift Supervision, 1475 
Peachtree Street, NE, Atlanta, GA 30309.

    Dated: May 16, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
