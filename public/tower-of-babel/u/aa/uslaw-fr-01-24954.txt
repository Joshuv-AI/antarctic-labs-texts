Radio Broadcasting Services; Rose Hill, Trenton, Aurora, and 
Ocracoke, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, denial.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies an Application for Review filed by Connor 
Media Corporation directed to the Report and Order in this proceeding. 
See 61 FR 66618, published December 18, 1996. Specifically, that action 
allotted Channel 283A to Aurora, North Carolina. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2177.
