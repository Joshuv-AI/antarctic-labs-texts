PRESIDENT'S COUNCIL ON INTEGRITY AND EFFICIENCY


Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board Membership

AGENCY: President's Council on Integrity and Efficiency (PCIE).

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects a technical error that appeared in the 
October 10, 1996 Federal Register notice (61 FR 53242) setting forth 
the names and titles of the current membership of the PCIE Performance 
Review Board.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 5, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joel J. Schaer, (202) 619-0089.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On October 10, 1996, we published a notice 
in the Federal Register that updated the names and titles of those 
individuals who are current members of the PCIE Senior Executive 
Service Performance Review Board. As published, the portion of the 
notice listing the Department of Agriculture representatives contained 
an inadvertent technical error. As a result, the Department of 
Agriculture section is being corrected to read as follows:
