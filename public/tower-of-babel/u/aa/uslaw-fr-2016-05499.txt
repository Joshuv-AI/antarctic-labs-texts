Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Transwestern Pipeline Company LLC; Notice of Technical Conference

    Take notice that a technical conference will be held on Tuesday, 
April 5, 2016, at 9:00 a.m. (Eastern Standard Time), in Hearing Room 5 
at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    At the technical conference, the Commission Staff and the parties 
to the proceeding should be prepared to discuss all issues set for 
technical conference as established in the January 28, 2016 Order 
(Transwestern Pipeline Company LLC, 154 FERC ] 61,051 (2016)). All 
interested persons are permitted to attend.
    Commission conferences are accessible under section 508 of the 
Rehabilitation Act of 1973. For accessibility accommodations please 
send an email to [email protected] or call toll free 1-866-208-
3372 (voice) or 202-502-8659 (TTY); or send a fax to 202-208-2106 with 
the required accommodations.
    For more information about this technical conference, please 
contact David Faerberg, 202-502-8275, [email protected].
