POSTAL SERVICE


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: Monday, March 24, 2025, at 3:30 p.m. EST.

PLACE: Washington, DC, at U.S. Postal Service Headquarters, 475 
L'Enfant Plaza SW.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: On March 24, 2025, the members of the Board 
of Governors of the United States Postal Service voted unanimously to 
hold and to close to public observation a special meeting in 
Washington, DC. The Board determined that no earlier public notice was 
practicable. The Board considered the below matters.
    1. Administrative Matters.
    2. Executive Session.
    General Counsel Certification: The General Counsel of the United 
States Postal Service has certified that the meeting may be closed 
under the Government in the Sunshine Act.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Michael J. Elston, Secretary of 
the Board of Governors, U.S. Postal Service, 475 L'Enfant Plaza SW, 
Washington, DC 20260-1000. Telephone: (202) 268-4800.

Michael J. Elston,
Secretary.
