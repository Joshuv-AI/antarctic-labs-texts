DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, May 17, 1999, 8 a.m. to May 
18, 1999 5 p.m., Holiday Inn Bethesda, 8120 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, 
MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on April 29, 
1999, 64 FR 23088.
    The meeting will be held on June 23, 8 a.m.-June 24, 1999, 5 p.m., 
at the Holiday Inn-Gaitherburg, 2 Montgomery Village Avenue, 
Gaithersburg, MD, 20879. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 11, 1999.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, National Institutes of Health.
