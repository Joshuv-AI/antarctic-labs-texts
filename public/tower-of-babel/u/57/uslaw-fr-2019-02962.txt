Caribbean Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council's (Council) 
Scientific and Statistical Committee (SSC) and Districts Advisory 
Panels (DAPs) will hold a 3-day joint meeting, in March, to discuss the 
items contained in the agenda in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

DATES: The meetings will be held on March 26, 2019, from 9 a.m. to 5 
p.m., March 27, 2019, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and March 28, 2019, from 9 
a.m. to 5 p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meetings will be held at the Embassy Suites Hotel, 
Tartak St., Isla Verde, Carolina, Puerto Rico.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Caribbean Fishery Management Council, 
270 Mu[ntilde]oz Rivera Avenue, Suite 401, San Juan, Puerto Rico 00918-
1903, telephone: (787) 766-5926.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The agenda for the meeting is the following:

March 26, 2019, 9 a.m.-12 p.m.

--Call to Order and Welcome--Miguel A. Rol[oacute]n
--SSC and DAP Members as Resources for Outreach and Education for the 
Stakeholders--Alida Ort[iacute]z
--Using Conceptual Models in an Ecosystem-Based Fisheries Management 
Framework
--Review of Ecosystem Conceptual Model and Applications
--Application for Risk Assessment
--DAPs and SSC Breakout Sessions to Populate Island-Based Conceptual 
Models

12 p.m.-1:30 p.m.

--Lunch Break

1:30 p.m.-5 p.m.

--DAPs and SSC Continuation of Breakout Sessions

March 27, 2019, 9 a.m.-10:30 a.m.

DAPs

10:45 a.m.-12 p.m.

--DAPs Chairs Reports to the Plenary

--Lunch Break

--Review Conceptual Model Outcomes

--Populate Risk Assessment Table

--Populate Risk Assessment Tables

--Other Business

    The order of business may be adjusted as necessary to accommodate 
the completion of agenda items. The meeting will begin on March 26, 
2019 at 9 a.m. and will end on March 28, 2019 at 5 p.m. Other than the 
start time, interested parties should be aware that discussions may 
start earlier or later than indicated. In addition, the meeting may be 
extended from, or completed prior to the date established in this 
notice.
