DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Head Start Bureau; Advisory Committee on Head Start Research and 
Evaluation; Notice of Meeting; Correction

AGENCY: Administration on Children, Youth and Families, ACF, DHHS.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Monday, April 5, 1999, a Notice was published in the 
Federal Register, document 99-8316, page 16470 announcing the Advisory 
Committee on Head Start Research and Evaluation meeting to be held on 
April 26-27, 1999 at Georgetown University. The Head Start Web site was 
incorrectly cited as http://www/dhhs.gov/programs/hsb. The correct web 
site is http://www2.acf.dhhs.gov/programs/hsb. For further information 
contact Deborah Roderick Stark at 301/889-0430.

    Dated: April 12, 1999.
Patricia Montoya,
Commissioner, Administration on Children, Youth, and Families.
