DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. FA21-5-000]


Commonwealth Edison Company; Notice of Designation of Commission 
Staff as Non-Decisional

    With respect to an order issued by the Commission on December 8, 
2023, in the above-captioned docket, with the exceptions noted below, 
the staff of the Office of Enforcement (OE) are designated as non-
decisional in deliberations by the Commission in this docket.\1\ 
Accordingly, pursuant to 18 CFR 385.2202 (2022), they will not serve as 
advisors to the Commission or take part in the Commission's review of 
any offer of settlement. Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 
18 CFR 385.2201 (2022), they are prohibited from communicating with 
advisory staff concerning any deliberations in this docket.
