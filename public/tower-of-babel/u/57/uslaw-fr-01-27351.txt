DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: IRS Project 1099.
    OMB No.: 0970-0183.
    Description: A Voluntary program which provides States' Child 
Support Enforcement agencies, upon their request access to the earned 
and unearned income information reported to IRS by employers and 
financial institutions. The IRS 1099 information

[[Page 55000]]

is used to locate noncustodial parents and to verify income and 
employment.
    Respondents: State IV-D programs.
