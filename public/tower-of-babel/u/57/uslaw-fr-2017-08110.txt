COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Procurement List; Proposed Deletion

AGENCY: Committee for Purchase From People Who Are Blind or Severely 
Disabled.

ACTION: Proposed deletion from the Procurement List.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Committee is proposing to delete a product from the 
Procurement List that was previously furnished by a nonprofit agency 
employing persons who are blind or have other severe disabilities.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before May 21, 2017.

ADDRESSES: Committee for Purchase From People Who Are Blind or Severely 
Disabled, 1401 S. Clark Street, Suite 715, Arlington, Virginia 22202-
4149.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Amy B. Jensen, Telephone: (703) 603-
7740, Fax: (703) 603-0655, or email [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This notice is published pursuant to 41 
U.S.C. 8503 (a)(2) and 41 CFR 51-2.3. Its purpose is to provide 
interested persons

[[Page 18742]]

an opportunity to submit comments on the proposed action.

Deletion

    The following product is proposed for deletion from the Procurement 
List:

Product

NSN(s)--Product Name(s): 8540-00-266-9898--Paper, Doily
Mandatory Source(s) of Supply: LC Industries, Inc., Durham, NC
Contracting Activity: General Services Administration, New York, NY

Director, Business Operations.
