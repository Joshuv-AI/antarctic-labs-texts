Radio Broadcasting Services; Bethel and Windsor, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to petition for rule making filed by Eure 
Communications, Inc. and Lifeline Ministries, Inc. this document 
substitutes Channel 255C3 for Channel 255A at Windsor, North Carolina, 
reallots Channel 255C3 from Windsor to Bethel, North Carolina, and 
modifies the Station WIAM license to specify operation on Channel 255C3 
at Bethel. See 69 FR 17125, April 1, 2004. The reference coordinates 
for the Channel 255C3 allotment at Bethel, North Carolina, are 35-48-25 
and 77-22-44. With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 29, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Media Bureau, (202) 418-
2177.
