DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 17, 2013, 08:00 a.m. 
to October 18, 2013, 05:00 p.m., The Dupont Hotel, 1500 New Hampshire 
Avenue NW., Washington, DC 20036, which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 20, 2013, 78 FR 183 Pgs. 57866-57867.
    The meeting will start on December 9, 2013 at 8:00 a.m. and end 
December 9, 2013 at 7:00 p.m. The meeting location remains the same. 
The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 29, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
