STATE JUSTICE INSTITUTE


Notice of Public Meeting; Board of Directors Meeting

Dates: Friday, February 28, 2003, 9 a.m.-5 p.m.

Place: State Justice Institute, 1650 King St. (Suite 600), Alexandria, 
VA.
