Abstract of Proposed Collection

    The Subnational Diplomacy Unit under the Department runs the Reta 
Jo Lewis Local Diplomat Program, which details a Foreign Service 
Officer or Civil Service employee to a local office, including a city, 
state, or county, for a year. The selection of local offices for this 
program must be competitive to provide a fair opportunity to all local 
offices that are interested in participating in the program. Therefore, 
to select local offices to participate in the program, the Subnational 
Diplomacy Unit must collect applications from local offices interested 
in the program.

Methodology

    The form will be emailed to local governments. After completion by 
the local governments, the form will be submitted via Microsoft Forms 
to the Department.
