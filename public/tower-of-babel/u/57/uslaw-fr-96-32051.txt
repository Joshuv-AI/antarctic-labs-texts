SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2894]


North Carolina; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area (Amendment #5)

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency, dated December 2, 1996, the above-numbered Declaration is 
hereby amended to extend the deadline for filing applications for 
physical damage as a result of this disaster to January 4, 1997.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for loans for economic injury is June 6, 1997.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: December 6, 1996.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
