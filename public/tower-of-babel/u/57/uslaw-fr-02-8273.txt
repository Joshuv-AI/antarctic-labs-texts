DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-41, 010]


Dupont Beaumont Works, Beaumont, TX; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 11, 2002, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by the company on behalf of workers at Dupont Beaumont Works, 
Beaumont, Texas.
    An active investigation covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in process (TA-W-40,949). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 18th day of March 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
