ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the final rule to 
implement Amendment 11 to the Fishery Management Plan for the Spiny 
Lobster Fishery in the Gulf of Mexico and South Atlantic Regions that 
published on Friday, July 27, 2012.

DATES: This correction is effective August 27, 2012.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Scott Sandorf, 727-824-5305; email: 
[email protected].

in the issue of Friday July 27, 2012, make the following corrections:
