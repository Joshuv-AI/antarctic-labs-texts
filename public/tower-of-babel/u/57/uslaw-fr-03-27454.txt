North Pacific Processors, Inc., Cordova, AK; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 11, 2003 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at North 
Pacific Processors, Inc., Cordova, Alaska.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 23rd day of September 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
