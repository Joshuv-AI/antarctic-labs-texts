DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP99-29-000]


Florida Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Request for Waiver

October 7, 1998.
    Take notice that on October 1, 1998, Florida Gas Transmission 
Company (FGT) tendered for filing a request for waiver of the November 
2, 1998

[[Page 55106]]

implementation date for Interruptible Bumping and the GISB Intraday 
Standards for approximately three months, until February 1, 1999.
    FGT states that copies of the filing have been served upon all 
customers and affected state regulatory commission.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Section 385.214 or 385.211 of the Commission's Rules 
and Regulations. All such motions or protests must be filed on or 
before October 14, 1998. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Any person wishing to 
become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection 
in the Public Reference Room.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
