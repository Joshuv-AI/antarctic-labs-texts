Big Creek Lodge & Outfitters Inc.; Idaho Aviation Foundation; 
Notice of Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, 
Motions To Intervene, and Protests

    On April 28, 2020, J Curtis Earl Idaho Aviation Foundation Inc., 
dba Idaho Aviation Foundation (transferee) filed an application for an 
after-the-fact transfer of license of the Big Creek Hydroelectric 
Project No. 10721. The project is located on McCorkle Creek, in Valley 
County, Idaho, and within the Payette National Forest.
    The applicant seeks Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Big Creek Hydroelectric Project from Big Creek Lodge & Outfitters 
Inc. (transferor) to the transferee. The Big Creek Lodge tragically 
burned to the ground in October 2008 and Idaho Aviation Foundation 
purchased the assets of Big Creek Lodge & Outfitters Inc. including the 
hydropower project.
