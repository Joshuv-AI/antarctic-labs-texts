SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Economic Injury Disaster 9Z06]


Commonwealth of Massachusetts (and Contiguous Counties in New 
Hampshire and Rhode Island)

    Barnstable, Essex, Norfolk, Plymouth and Suffolk Counties and the 
contiguous counties of Bristol, Middlesex, and Worcester in the 
Commonwealth of Massachusetts; Hillsborough and Rockingham Counties in 
the State of New Hampshire; and Providence in the State of Rhode Island 
constitute an economic injury disaster loan area as a result of a 
nor'easter storm that occurred on December 6-7, 2003. Eligible small 
businesses and small agricultural cooperatives without credit available 
elsewhere may file applications for economic injury assistance as a 
result of this disaster until the close of business on October 18, 
2004, at the address listed below or other locally announced locations: 
Small Business Administration, Disaster Area 1 Office, 360 Rainbow 
Blvd, South 3rd Floor, Niagara Falls, NY 14303.
    The interest rate for eligible small businesses and small 
agricultural cooperatives is 3.061 percent.
    The numbers assigned for economic injury for this disaster are 
9Z0600 for Massachusetts; 9Z0700 for New Hampshire; and 9Z0800 for 
Rhode Island.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59002)
