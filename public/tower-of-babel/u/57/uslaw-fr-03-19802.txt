Guideline for the Clinical Evaluation of Analgesic Drugs; 
Withdrawal of Guidance

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing the 
withdrawal of a guidance entitled ``Guideline for the Clinical 
Evaluation of Analgesic Drugs,'' which was issued on December 1, 1992. 
The guidance is outdated and no longer reflects FDA's current thinking 
on development of analgesic drugs. FDA is revising the guidance and 
will issue a draft for public comment in the future.

DATES: Comments on agency guidances are welcome at any time.
