DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commissioner and Staff Attendance at the National 
Association of Regulatory Utility Commissioners 2013 Summer Committee 
Meetings

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC or Commission) 
hereby gives notice that members of the Commission and/or Commission 
staff may attend the following meeting:
    FERC/National Association of Regulatory Utility Commissioners 
Sunday Morning Collaborative, July 21, 2013 (9:00 a.m.-11:45 a.m.), 
Sheraton Denver Downtown Hotel, 1550 Court Place, Denver, CO 80202.
    Further information may be found at http://summer.narucmeetings.org/agenda.cfm

    Dated: July 15, 2013.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
