DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Frederick 
National Laboratory Advisory Committee to the National Cancer 
Institute, February 20, 2019, 1:00 p.m. to February 20, 2019, 5:00 
p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 
TE406, Rockville, MD, 20850 which was published in the Federal Register 
on February 5, 2019, 84 FR 1759.
    This meeting was cancelled due to inclement weather and will not be 
rescheduled.

    Dated: February 28, 2019.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
