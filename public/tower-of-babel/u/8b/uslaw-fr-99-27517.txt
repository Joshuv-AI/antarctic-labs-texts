FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

October 14, 1999.

Open Commission Meeting, Thursday, October 21, 1999

the subjects listed below on Thursday, October 21, 1999, which is 
scheduled to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, 
S.W., Washington, D.C.
