FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 99-260, RM-9686]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Bristol, VT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a petition filed by Blue 
Sky Broadcasting, LLC, seeking the allotment of Channel 248A to 
Bristol, VT, as the community's first local aural service. Channel 248A 
can be allotted to Bristol in compliance with the Commission's minimum 
distance separation requirements with respect to domestic allotments 
without the imposition of a site restriction, at coordinates 44-08-18 
NL; 73-05-00 WL. Canadian concurrence as a specially negotiated short-
spaced allotment is required since Bristol is located within 320 
kilometers (200 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border and the allotment 
would be short-spaced to Station CHOM-FM, Channel 249C1, Montreal, 
Quebec.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 7, 1999, and reply 
comments on or before September 22, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 12th Street, SW, Room 
TW-A325, Washington, DC 20554. In addition to filing comments with the 
FCC, interested parties should serve the petitioner, or its counsel or 
consultant, as follows: David D. Oxenford, Fisher Wayland Cooper Leader 
& Zaragoza, L.L.P., 2001 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW, Washington, DC 20006 
(Counsel to petitioner).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
