SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

[File No. 500-1]


Concentrax, Inc.; Order of Suspension of Trading

November 22, 2002.
    It appears to the Securities and Exchange Commission that there is 
a lack of current and accurate information concerning the securities of 
Concentrax, Inc. (``Concentrax''), because of questions regarding: the 
accuracy and adequacy of assertions in press releases by Concentrax, 
concerning, among other things, the existence, status, and description 
of agreements announced by Concentrax in its press releases of October 
9, 2002, October 23, 2002, and October 31, 2002.
    The Commission is of the opinion that the public interest and the 
protection of investors require a suspension of trading in the 
securities of the above-listed company.
    Therefore, it is ordered, pursuant to Section 12(k) of the 
Securities Exchange Act of 1934, that trading in the above-listed 
company is suspended for the period from 9:30 a.m. EST, on Friday, 
November 22, 2002 through 11:59 p.m. EST, on Friday, December 6, 2002.

    By the Commission.
J. Lynn Taylor,
Assistant Secretary.
