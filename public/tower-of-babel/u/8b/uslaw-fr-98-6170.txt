Retraction of Revocation Notice

SUMMARY: The following Customs broker license numbers were erroneously 
included in a published list of revoked Customs brokers licenses in the 
Federal Register.

Mark Rendell Dawson--07156
Pamela Louise Schnetter--13140
Renee E. Stein--07160

Angeles, are valid licenses.
