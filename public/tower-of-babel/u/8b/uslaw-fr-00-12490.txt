DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management

[ID-010-0777-XQ]


Notice of Meeting

AGENCY: Lower Snake River District, Bureau of Land Management, 
Interior.

ACTION: Meeting notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Lower Snake River District Resource Advisory Council will 
meet in Boise. Potential agenda topics are the Interior Columbia Basin 
Ecosystem Management Plan, the Integrated Natural Resources Management 
Plan for the U.S. Air Force

[[Page 31597]]

Enhanced Training in Idaho project, sage grouse habitat management, 
implementation of rangeland standards and guidelines, off-highway 
vehicle management and other land management issues.

DATES: June 22, 2000. The meeting will begin at 9 a.m. Public comment 
periods will be held at 9:30 a.m. and 4 p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Lower Snake River District 
Office, located at 3948 Development Avenue, Boise, Idaho.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barry Rose, Lower Snake River District 
Office (208-384-3393).

    Dated: May 12, 2000.
Howard Hendrick,
District Manager.
