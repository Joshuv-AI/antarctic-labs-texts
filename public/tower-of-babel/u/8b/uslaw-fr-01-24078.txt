UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE BOARD OF GOVERNORS


Sunshine Act Meeting

DATE OF MEETING: September 10, 2001.

STATUS: Closed.

ADDITIONS:
    1. Extension of Experimental Ride Along Case.
    2. Filing of Experimental Case for Delivery Confirmation.
