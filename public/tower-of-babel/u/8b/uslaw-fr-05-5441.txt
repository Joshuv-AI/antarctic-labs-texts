DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Advisory Circular 33.75-1, Guidance Material for 14 CFR 33.75, 
Safety Analysis.

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance of advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Advisory Circular (AC) 
33.75-1, Guidance Material for 14 CFR 33.75, Safety Analysis. This AC 
sets forth acceptable methods of compliance with the safety analysis 
requirements of 14 CFR 33.75.

DATES: Advisory Circular 33.75-1 was issued by the Engine and Propeller 
Directorate, ANE-100, on March 4, 2005.
