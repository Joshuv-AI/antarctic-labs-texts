Pacific Corp; Notice of Deadline for Answers and Reply Comments

March 30, 2000.
    The Commission's February 2, 2000, Notice of Offer of Settlement 
and Application for Amendment of License, as clarified by a notice 
issued on March 9, 2000, set a deadline for filing comments and or 
motions in Project No. P-2342-011 of March 27, 2000. The deadline for 
filing answers and reply comments to filings submitted in response to 
the February 2 notice is April 11, 2000.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
