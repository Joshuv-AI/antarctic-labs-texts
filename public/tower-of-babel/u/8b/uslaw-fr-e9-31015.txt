47 CFR Part 73

Television Broadcasting Services; Atlantic City, NJ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission proposes the allotment of channel 4 to Atlantic 
City, New Jersey. The Commission is waiving the freeze on the filing of 
new DTV allotments to initiate this proceeding and to advance the 
policy, as set forth in Section 331(a) of the Communications Act of 
1934, as amended, to allocate not less than one very high frequency 
(``VHF'') commercial television channel to each State, if technically 
feasible.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before January 29, 2010, and reply 
comments on or before February 16, 2010.

445 12th Street, SW., Washington, DC 20554.
