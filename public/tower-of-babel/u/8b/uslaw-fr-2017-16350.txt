Chasm Hydro, Inc.; ECOsponsible, LLC; Notice of Application for 
Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To Intervene, and 
Protests

    On July 10, 2017, Chasm Hydro, Inc. (transferor) and ECOsponsible, 
LLC (transferee) filed an application for the transfer of license for 
the Ballard Mill Project No. 3267, from the transferor to the 
transferee. The project is located on the Salmon River in Franklin 
County, New York. The project does not occupy Federal lands.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Ballard Mills Project from the transferor to the transferee.
    Applicant's Contacts: For Transferor: Mr. John Dowd, President, 
Chasm Hydro, Inc., P.O. Box 265, Chateaugay, NY 12920.
    For Transferee: Mr. Dennis Ryan, Manager, ECOsponsible, LLC, P.O. 
Box 114, West Falls, NY 14170.
    FERC Contact: Patricia W. Gillis, (202) 502-8735, 
[email protected].
