Bank Mutual Corporation, Brown Deer, Wisconsin; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on July 31, 2003, the Director, 
Supervision Policy,

[[Page 46681]]

Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS), or her designee, acting pursuant to 
delegated authority, approved the application of Bank Mutual, Brown 
Deer, Wisconsin, to convert to the stock form of organization. Copies 
of the application are available for inspection by appointment (phone 
number: 202-906-5922 or e-mail: [email protected]) at the 
Public Reading Room, OTS, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552; and 
the OTS Midwest Regional Office, 225 E. John Carpenter Freeway, Suite 
500, Irving, Texas 75062-2326.

    Dated: August 1, 2003.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
