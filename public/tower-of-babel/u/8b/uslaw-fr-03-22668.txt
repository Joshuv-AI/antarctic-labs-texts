POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Briefing on Data System Changes

AGENCY: Postal Rate Commission.

ACTION: Notice of public briefing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Postal Service will present a briefing on 
September 17, 2003 at 10 a.m. in the Postal Rate Commission's hearing 
room on the proposed merger of two major data reporting systems. The 
systems affected by the merger are the Revenue, Pieces and Weight (RPW) 
system and the Origin Destination Information System (ODIS). The 
briefing is open to the public.

DATES: September 17, 2003, at 10 a.m.

ADDRESSES: Postal Rate Commission (hearing room), 1333 H Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20268-0001, Suite 300.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen L. Sharfman, General Counsel, 
202-789-6818.

Steven W. Williams,
Secretary.
