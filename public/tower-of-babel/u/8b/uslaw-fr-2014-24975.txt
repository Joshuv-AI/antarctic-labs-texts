DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Staff Attendance at Southwest Power Pool Regional 
Entity Trustee, Regional State Committee, Members' and Board of 
Directors' Meetings

October 14, 2014.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of its staff may attend the meetings of the 
Southwest Power Pool, Inc. (SPP) Regional Entity Trustee (RE), Regional 
State Committee (RSC), SPP Members Committee and Board of Directors, as 
noted below. Their attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing 
outreach efforts.
    All meetings will be held at SPP's Corporate Center, 201 Worthen 
Drive, Little Rock, AR 72223.

SPP RE

    October 27, 2014 (8:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m.)

SPP RSC

    October 27, 2014 (1:00 p.m.-5:00 p.m.)

SPP Members/Board of Directors

    October 28, 2014 (8:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m.)
