SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    United States vessels that fish on the high seas are required to 
possess a permit issued under the High Seas Fishing Compliance Act (16 
U.S.C. 5501 et seq. and 50 CFR 300.13). Applicants must submit 
application information to identify their vessels and intended fishing 
areas. The application information is used to process applications and 
maintain a register of U.S. vessels authorized to fish on the high 
seas.

II. Method of Collection
