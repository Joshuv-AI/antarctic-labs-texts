DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Closed 
Meetings

amended (5 U.S.C. Appendix 2), notice is hereby given of the following 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Special Emphasis Panel (SEP) meetings:

    Name of SEP: Maintenance of a Biological Specimen Repository.
    Date: February 4, 1997.
    Place: Holiday Inn Bethesda, 8120 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, 
Maryland 20814.
    Contact Person: Diane M. Reid, M.D., Two Rockledge Center, Room 
7182, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892-7924, (301) 435-0277.

meeting due to the urgent need to meet timing limitations imposed by 
the review and funding cycle.

0270.
