DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date(s) of Meeting: April 1-2, 2004.
    Time(s) of Meeting: 0800-1700, April 1, 2004, 0800-1700, April 
2, 2004.
    Place: Arlington, VA.
    Agenda: The FCS Urban Operations Study and Force Balance Study 
of the Army Science Board FY04 Summer Studies are holding a joint 
plenary on April 1-2, 2004. The meetings will be held at the Crystal 
City Sheraton in Arlington, VA and will begin at 0800 hrs on the 1st 
and will end at approximately 1700 hrs on the 2nd. For further 
information on the FCS Urban Operations Study, please contact MAJ Al 
Visconti at (865) 574-8798 or e-mail at [email protected]. For the 
Force Balance Study, please contact MAJ Al Klee at (703) 604-2212 or 
e-mail at [email protected].

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
