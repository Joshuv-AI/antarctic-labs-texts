Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-71,349]


Kroeschell Operations Inc., Pendergrass, GA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated in response to a petition filed on June 23, 
2009, by three workers of Kroeschell Operations Inc., Pendergrass, 
Georgia.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active petition 
(TA-W-71,185) filed on June 12, 2009, that is subject of an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued. 
Further investigation in this case would duplicate efforts and serve no 
purpose; therefore, the investigation under this petition has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 7th of August 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
