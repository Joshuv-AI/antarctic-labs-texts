DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Nuclear 
Dynamics and Transport, June 10, 2008, 8 a.m. to June 11, 2008, 5 p.m., 
Georgetown Suites, 1000 29th Street, NW., Washington, DC 20007 which 
was published in the Federal Register on April 22, 2008, 73 FR 21636-
21639.

6 p.m. The meeting location remains the same. The meeting is closed to 
the public.
