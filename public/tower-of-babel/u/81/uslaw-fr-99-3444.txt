DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 2977]


Bureau of Administration; Classification Authority Acting Under 
the Direction of the Senior Agency Official

    By virtue of the authority vested in me as the Senior Agency 
Official designated under Section 5.6 of the Executive Order on 
Classified National Security Information (EO 12958), and as Under 
Secretary of State for Management, I hereby authorize and direct the 
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Records and Publishing Services (A/RPS) 
to be the official to classify information on a document-by-document 
basis consistent with the circumstances and procedures described in 
section 1.8(d) of EO 12958. This authority shall be limited to 
information that meets the standards of the Order for classification 
and has not previously been disclosed to the public under proper 
authority. The Deputy Assistant Secretary shall act under the direction 
of the Under Secretary for Management and shall keep me apprised of 
actions taken under this authority.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 12, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Margaret P. Grafeld, Bureau of 
Administration, Department of State (202-647-6620).

    Dated: February 2, 1999.
Patrick F. Kennedy,
Under Secretary for Management, Acting.
