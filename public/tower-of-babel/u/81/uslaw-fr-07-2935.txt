DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Lassen National Forest, CA, Creeks Forest Health Recovery Project

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Cancellation of Notice of Intent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice cancels the Notice of Intent to prepare a 
Supplement to the Environmental Impact Statement for the Creeks Forest 
Health Recovery Project on the Lassen National Forest, published in the 
Federal Register on December 7, 2006, (Volume 71, Number 235, page 
70946).

ADDRESSES: Almanor District Ranger, Lassen National Forest, P.O. Box 
767, Chester, CA 96020.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rick Atwell, Interdisciplinary Team 
Leader, may be contacted by phone at (530) 258-2141.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: A Notice of Intent to prepare a Supplement 
to the Environmental Impact Statement for the Creeks Forest Health 
Recovery Project is cancelled due to additional information related to 
the California spotted owl analysis, conducted in concert with Pacific 
Southwest Research Station, for the Creeks project area.

    Dated: June 8, 2007.
Elizabeth Norton,
Acting Forest Supervisor.
