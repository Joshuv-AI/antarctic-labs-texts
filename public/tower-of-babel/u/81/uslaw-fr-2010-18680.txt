DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 20-2009]


Foreign-Trade Zone 29; Application for Subzone Authority; Dow 
Corning Corporation; Extension of Rebuttal Period

    Based on a request from an interested party, the rebuttal period on 
the preliminary recommendation for the application for subzone status 
at the Dow Corning Corporation (Dow Corning) facilities in Carrollton, 
Elizabethtown and Shepherdsville, Kentucky (75 FR 31763, 6/3/2010) is 
being extended to August 26, 2010 to allow additional time in which to 
provide rebuttal comments. The original submission shall be sent to the 
Board's Executive Secretary at: Foreign-Trade Zones Board, U.S. 
Department of Commerce, Room 2111, 1401 Constitution Ave., NW., 
Washington, DC 20230. An electronic copy shall be submitted to 
[email protected].
    For further information, contact Elizabeth Whiteman at 
[email protected] or (202) 482-0473.

    Dated: July 23, 2010.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
