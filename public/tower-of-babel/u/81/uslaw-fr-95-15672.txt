FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 95-24; RM-8583]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Clarendon, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of ROHO Broadcasting, allots 
Channel 257C2 to Clarendon, Texas, as the community's first local aural 
transmission service. See 60 FR 10534, February 27, 1995. Channel 257C2 
can be allotted to Clarendon, Texas, in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without the 
imposition of a site restriction. The coordinates for Channel 257C2 at 
Clarendon are 34-56-16 and 100-53-16. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated.

DATES: Effective August 7, 1995. The window period for filing 
applications will open on August 7, 1995, and close on September 7, 
1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
