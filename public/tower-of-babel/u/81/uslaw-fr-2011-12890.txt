DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL11-41-000]


California Independent System Operator Corporation; Notice of 
Institution of Section 206 Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On May 19, 2011, the Commission issued an order that instituted an 
investigation in Docket No. EL11-41-000, pursuant to section 206 of the 
Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. 824e, to evaluate the justness and 
reasonableness of section 10.3.6.3 of the California Independent System 
Operator Corporation's tariff. Cal. Indep. Operator Corp., 135 FERC ] 
61,159 (2011).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL11-41-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: May 19, 2011.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
