Adjustment of Status to That of Person Admitted for Permanent 
Residence; Temporary Removal of Certain Restrictions on Eligibility

Correction

    In rule document 94-25025 beginning on page 51091, in the issue of 
Friday, October 7, 1994, make the following corrections:


Sec. 245.10  [Corrected]

    1.On page 51096, in the second column, in the fourth line, after 
the word ``Form I-817'' insert ``; or''.
    2.On the same page, in the same column, paragraph (b)(3) was 
omitted and should have appeared as follows: ``(3) The child of a 
legalized alien, is unmarried and less than 21 years of age, qualifies 
for and has properly filed Form I-817, and submits a copy of his or her 
receipt or approval notice for filing Form I-817.''
