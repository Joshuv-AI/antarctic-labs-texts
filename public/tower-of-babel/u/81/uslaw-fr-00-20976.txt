DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On June 15, 2000, a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 65, No. 116, Page 37568, that an application had been filed with 
the Fish and Wildlife Service by Felix G. Widlacki, Orland Park, IL, 
for a permit (PRT-028560) to import a sport-hunted polar bear (Ursus 
maritimus) trophy, taken from the Norwegian Bay polar bear population, 
Northwest Territories, Canada for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on July 26, 2000, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
