Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act; Establishment of the

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) is correcting a final 
rule that appeared in the Federal Register of February 24, 2015 (80 FR 
9649). The document implementing modifications to the Multi-State Plan 
(MSP) Program based on the experience of the Program to date.

DATES: Effective March 26, 2015.

606-2128, by FAX at (202) 606-4430, or by email at [email protected].
