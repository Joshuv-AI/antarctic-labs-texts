DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL13-28-000]


Nevada Power Company; Notice of Initiation of Proceeding and 
Refund Effective Date

    On December 31, 2012, the Commission issued an order that initiated 
a proceeding in Docket No. EL13-28-000, pursuant to section 206 of the 
Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. 824e (2006), to determine the 
justness and reasonableness of the proposed transmission and ancillary 
service rates by Nevada Power Company. Nevada Power Company, 141 FERC ] 
61,267 (2012).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL13-28-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: December 31, 2012.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
