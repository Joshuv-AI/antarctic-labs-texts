Tube Fabrication Industries, Inc., Logansport, IN; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 27, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Tube Fabrication 
Industries, Inc., Logansport, Indiana. The workers at the subject 
facility produce steel tube components.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 18th day of May 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
