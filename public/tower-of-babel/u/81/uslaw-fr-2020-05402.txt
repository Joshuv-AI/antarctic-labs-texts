DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, Small Business: Biomedical 
Sensing, Measurement and Instrumentation, March 19, 2020, 08:00 a.m. to 
March 20, 2020, 05:00 p.m., Bahia Resort Hotel, 998 West Mission Bay 
Drive, San Diego, CA 92109, which was published in the Federal Register 
on February 20, 2020, 85 FR 9791.

Health, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892. The meeting date and 
time remains the same. The meeting is closed to the public.
