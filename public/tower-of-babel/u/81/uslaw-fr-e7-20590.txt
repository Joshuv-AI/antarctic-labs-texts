Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-62,045]


Tweel Home Furnishings Newark, NJ

Notice of Termination of Investigation
    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 27, 2007, in response to a 
petition filed by a One-Stop Operator/Partner in North Carolina on 
behalf of workers of Tweel Home Fashions, Newark, New Jersey.
    The One-Stop or state agency may only file petitions on behalf of 
workers employed by a firm located within its own State. Consequently, 
further investigation would serve no purpose, and the petition 
investigation is terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of October 2007
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
