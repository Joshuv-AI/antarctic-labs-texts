DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Office of the Secretary

[GWCRC Meeting Notice No. 4-04]


Guam War Claims Review Commission

    The Guam War Claims Review Commission, pursuant to section 10 of 
the Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 U.S.C. App. 10), hereby gives 
notice in regard to the scheduling of meetings for the transaction of 
Commission business, as follows:
    Date and Time: Wednesday, June 2, 2004, 10 a.m.; Thursday, June 3, 
2004, 10 a.m.; Friday, June 4, 2004, 10 a.m.
    Place: 600 E St., NW., Room 6002, Washington, DC.
    Subject Matter: Discussion of the report which the Commission is 
required to submit to the Secretary of the Interior and Congressional 
committees under the Guam War Claims Review Commission Act, Public Law 
107-333.
    Status: Open.
    Requests for information concerning these meetings should be 
addressed to David Bradley, Executive Director, Guam War Claims Review 
Commission, c/o Foreign Claims Settlement Commission of the United 
States, 600 E St., NW., Washington DC 20579, telephone (202) 616-6975, 
FAX (202) 616-6993.

Mauricio J. Tamargo,
Chairman.
