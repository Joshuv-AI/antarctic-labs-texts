Springs Industries, Ellijay, GA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on January 21, 2005, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Springs Industries, Ellijay, 
Georgia.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an earlier petition 
(TA-W-56,295) filed on January 5, 2005, that is the subject of an 
ongoing investigation for which a determination has not yet been 
issued. Further investigation in this case would duplicate efforts and 
serve no purpose; therefore the investigation under this petition has 
been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 24th day of January, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
