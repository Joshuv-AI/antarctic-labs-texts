UNITED STATES INFORMATION AGENCY


Culturally Significant Objects Imported for Exhibition; Addendum

    On November 1, 1996, notice was published at page 56606 of the 
Federal Register (61 FR 56606) by the United States Information Agency 
pursuant to Public Law 89-259 relating to the exhibit ``Jewels of the 
Romanovs: Treasures of the Russian Imperial Court.'' In addition to the 
first four venues listed therein, this exhibit will also be on 
temporary display at the Brooklyn Museum of Art from on or about March 
16, 1998 to on or about July 5, 1998.

    Dated: February 27, 1998.
Les Jin,
General Counsel.
