ICCAT Advisory Committee; Public Meetings; correction

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting; Correction

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In a previous Federal Register notice, incorrect dates were 
given for the fall meeting of the Advisory Committee to the U.S. 
Section to the International Commission for the Conservation of 
Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT). This document provides corrected dates for the 
meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Kerstetter at 301-713-2337 or 
Kimberly Blankenbeker at 301-713-2276.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the June 7, 1999, issue of the Federal Register, on page 30321, 
in the second column, the first sentence of the seventh paragraph under 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION, the dates should be corrected to read 
``October 24-26, 1999.''

    Dated: August 10, 1999.
Bruce C. Morehead,
Acting Director, Office of Sustainable Fisheries, National Marine 
Fisheries Service.
