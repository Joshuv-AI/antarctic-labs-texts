California Independent System Operator Corporation; Notice of 
Technical Conference

December 3, 2004.
    Take notice that a technical conference will be held on Wednesday, 
January 12, 2005, and Thursday, January 13, 2005, beginning at 9 a.m. 
(e.s.t.), in Room 3M-2B at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426. Pursuant to 
the Commission's October 28, 2004, Order,\1\ the technical conference 
will afford the parties an opportunity to discuss the California 
Independent System Operator

[[Page 75060]]

Corporation's (CAISO) proposed ``self-certification'' process and any 
party's alternate proposal on how best to achieve the CAISO's objective 
based upon the presentation of proposals at the technical conference. 
All proposals must be submitted within 30 days of the date of this 
notice. Parties must be prepared to discuss their proposals at the 
technical conference.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
