DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. CP97-769-000]


Colorado Interstate Gas Company; Notice of Site Visits

November 13, 1997.
    The Office of Pipeline Regulation (OPR) will conduct a site visit 
with representatives of Colorado Interstate Gas Company, of its 
proposed Campo Lateral in Las Animas and Baca Counties, Colorado. The 
site visit will

[[Page 61814]]

take place on November 19 and 20, 1997.
    All interested parties may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
