Northern Natural Gas Company; Errata Notice

July 19, 2005.
    On July 14, 2005, the Commission issued a ``Notice of Filing of a 
Report on Field Area Segmentation''. The third paragraph of the notice 
is revised to include a specific date for the filing of protests should 
read as follows:
    Any person desiring to protest this filing must file in accordance 
with Rule 211 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 
CFR 385.211). Protests to this filing will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Such protests 
must be filed on or before August 1, 2005. Anyone filing a protest must 
serve a copy of that document on all the parties to the proceeding.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
