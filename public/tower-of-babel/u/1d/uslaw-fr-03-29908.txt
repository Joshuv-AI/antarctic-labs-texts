DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Government-Owned Invention; Available 
for Licensing

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The following invention is assigned to the U.S. Government as 
represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available for licensing 
by the Department of the Navy. U.S. Invention Disclosure Navy Case 
Number 95,919 entitled ``Integrated Maritime Portable Acoustic Scoring 
and Simulator Control and Improvements.''

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the invention cited should be 
directed to the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Code 05T, 101 Strauss 
Avenue, Indian Head, MD 20640-5035.
