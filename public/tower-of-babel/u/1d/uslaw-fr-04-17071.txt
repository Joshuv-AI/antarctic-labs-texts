SUMMARY: This document corrects the heading of a document that reopened 
the comment period for a proposed rule published in the Federal 
Register of Wednesday, July 21, 2004, at 69 FR 43547. This document 
corrects the title to read as set forth above.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Catherine Freels, Designated Federal 
Official, P.O. Box 1430, Albuquerque, NM 87103-1430; phone: (505) 248-
7240; e-mail: [email protected].

Correction

    The document published Wednesday July 21, 2004, was incorrectly 
titled, ``Home-living Programs and School Closure and Consolidation.'' 
The title is corrected to read ``Implementation of the No Child Left 
Behind Act of 2001''.
