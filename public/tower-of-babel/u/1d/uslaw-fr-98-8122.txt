DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Olympic Provincial Interagency Executive Committee (PIEC), 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Olympic PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on April 17, 
1998 at the Jamestown s'Klallam Tribal Center, 1033 Old Blyn Highway, 
Sequim, Washington. The meeting will begin at 9:30 a.m. and continue 
until 3:00 p.m. Agenda items to be covered include: (1) Review and 
update on 1998 watershed restoration program; (2) Update on listing of 
salmon stocks under the Endangered Species Act in the Puget Sound area; 
(3) Update from the Monitoring sub-committee; (4) Review of Chief 
Dombeck's Agenda for the Forest Service; (5) Update or current projects 
and activities on the Quilcene Ranger District. Olympic Province 
Committee meetings are open to the public. Interested citizens are 
encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Direct questions regarding this 
meeting to Kathy Snow, Province Liaison, USDA, Quilcene Ranger 
District, P.O. Box 280, Quilcene, WA 98376, (360) 765-2211.

    Dated: March 20, 1998.
Claire Lavendel,
Acting Forest Supervisor.
