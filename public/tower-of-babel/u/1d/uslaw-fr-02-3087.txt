DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


North American Energy Working Group

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of postponement of public workshop.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On January 17, 2002, the United States delegation to the North 
American Energy Working Group's Electricity Regulatory Experts Group 
published a notice of a public workshop to be held at the Washington, 
DC headquarters of the Department of Energy, in Room 1E-245, on 
February 13, 2002 from 9 a.m. until 4 p.m., and a request for comments, 
67 FR 2423. The workshop will be postponed until further notice because 
the draft discussion paper of the Electricity Regulatory Issues Group 
of Experts, which was to form the basis of the workshop discussions, is 
not yet ready for public dissemination. In addition, it is requested 
that no further comments be submitted pursuant to the January 17, 2002 
notice.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on February 4, 2002.
Vicky Bailey,
Assistant Secretary, Office of Policy and International Affairs.
