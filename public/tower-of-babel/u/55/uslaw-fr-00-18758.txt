Radio Broadcasting Services; Elberton, Lavonia and Pendergrass, 
GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Radio Elberton, Inc., 
reallots Channel 221A from Elberton, GA, to Lavonia, GA, as the 
community's first local aural service, and modifies Station WWRK-FM's 
license accordingly. Channel 221A can be allotted to Lavonia in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements with a site restriction of 12.5 kilometers (7.8 miles) 
west, at coordinates 34-27-26 NL; 83-14-27 WL, to accommodate 
petitioner's desired transmitter site. This action found that the 
public interest was better served by the provision of a first local 
aural service for the more populous community of Lavonia than by the 
mutually exclusive proposal of Waves of Mercy Productions, Inc., to 
provide a first local aural service on noncommercial educational FM 
Channel 220A at Pendergrass, GA (BPED-1990630MB). See 64 FR 70672, 
December 17, 1999.
