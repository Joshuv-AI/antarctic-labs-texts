SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

17 CFR Part 200


Organization; Conduct and Ethics; and Information and Requests

CFR Correction

    In Title 17 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 200 to 239, 
revised as of April 1, 2015, on page 19, in Sec.  200.24, remove the 
words ``Associate Executive Director of the Office of the Comptroller'' 
and add in their place the words ``Chief Financial Officer''.
