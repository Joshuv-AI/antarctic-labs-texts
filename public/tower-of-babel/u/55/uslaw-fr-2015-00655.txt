DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Frederick 
National Laboratory Advisory Committee to the National Cancer 
Institute, February 3, 2015, 09:00 a.m. to February 4, 2015, 02:00 
p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, 
Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on 
December 30, 2014, 79 FR 78457.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date and time of the 
meeting to February 3, 2015 from 08:30 a.m. to 05:00 p.m. The meeting 
is open to the public.

Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
