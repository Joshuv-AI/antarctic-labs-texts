National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, October 26, 2016, 11:00 a.m. 
to October 26, 2016, 5:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 
9609 Medical Center Drive, 4W034, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on August 25, 2016, 81 FR 58524.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date of the meeting to 
November 15, 2016 from 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to 
the public.
