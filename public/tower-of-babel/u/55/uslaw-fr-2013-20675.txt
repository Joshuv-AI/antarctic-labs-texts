    Synopsis: As required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (44

3060-0734, 3060-0149, and 3060-1046. The total annual reporting burden

$450,000 in annual costs.
For 3060-0741: 573,767 responses, for a total of 575,448 hours, and no 
annual costs.
For 3060-1005: 32 responses, for a total of 830 hours, and no annual 
costs.
For 3060-0742: 10,001,890 responses, for a total of 672,516 hours, and 
$13,423,321 in annual costs.

For 3060-0734: 1,515 responses, for a total of 72,495 hours, and
