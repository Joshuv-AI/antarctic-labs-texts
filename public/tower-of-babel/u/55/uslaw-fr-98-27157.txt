DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP99-32-000]


Transwestern Pipeline Company; Notice of Proposed Changes in FERC 
Gas Tariff

October 5, 1998.
    Take notice that on October 1, 1998, Transwestern Pipeline Company 
(Transwestern), tendered for filing to become part of Transwestern's 
FERC Gas Tariff, Second Revised Volume No. 1 the following tariff 
sheets to be effective November 1, 1998:

Seventh Revised Sheet No. 49
Thirteenth Revised Sheet No. 80
Eighth Revised Sheet No. 80A
First Revised Sheet No. 80A.01
Original Sheet No. 80A.02
Sixth Revised Sheet No. 80B
Third Revised Sheet No. 81E
Original Sheet No. 81F

    Transwestern states that the above-listed tariff sheets are filed 
in compliance with Order No. 587-H issued July 15, 1998 in Docket No. 
RM96-1-008 (Order No. 587-H).
    Transwestern states that copies of the filing were served upon 
Tranwestern's customers and interested State Commissions.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or a protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Section 385.214 or 385.211 of the Commission's Rules 
and Regulations. All such motions or protests must filed in accordance 
with Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
