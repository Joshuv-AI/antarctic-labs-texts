NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice: 24-023]


Earth Science Advisory Committee; Meeting

AGENCY:  National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) 
announces a meeting of the Earth Science Advisory Committee (ESAC). 
This Committee functions in an advisory capacity to the Director, Earth 
Science Division, in the NASA Science Mission Directorate. The meeting 
will be held for the purpose of soliciting, from the scientific 
community and other persons, scientific and technical information 
relevant to program planning.

DATES: Tuesday, April 16, 2024, 8:30 a.m.-5 p.m., and Wednesday, April 
17, 2024, 8:30 a.m.-3 p.m., eastern time.

ADDRESSES: 
    For April 16, 2024, Public attendance will be virtual only. See 
dial-in and Webex information below.
    For April 17, 2024, NASA Headquarters, Room 8R40, 300 E Street SW, 
Washington, DC 20546.
