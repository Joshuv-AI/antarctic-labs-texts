DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-29,929]

 

AFL-CIO-N.M.U.; Port Arthur, TX; Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 31, 1994 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on May 9, 1994 on behalf of workers at AFL-CIO-N.M.U., Port 
Arthur, Texas.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 25th day of July, 1994.
Violet L. Thompson,
Deputy Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
