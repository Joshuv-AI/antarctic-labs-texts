Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-38,065]


It's Personal, New York, New York; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 5, 2000, in response to a petition filed on 
August 22, 2000 on behalf of workers at It's Personal, New York, New 
York.
    The Department of Labor has been unable to locate principals of the 
firm or otherwise obtain information to reach a determination on worker 
eligibility. Consequently, further investigation in this case would 
serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 20th day of November, 2000.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
