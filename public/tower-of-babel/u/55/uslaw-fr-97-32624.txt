ACTION: Correction to RIN number.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the RIN number of the Notice of 
Proposed Rulemaking [Docket RSPA-97-2362; Notice 1], published in the 
Federal Register on October 29, 1997 (62 FR 56141). In the document 
heading on page 56141, the RIN number ``RIN 2137-AD05'' is changed to 
read ``RIN 2137-AD06.'' The notice proposes to adopt as a referenced 
document an industry publication for pipeline leak detection, API 1130, 
``Computational Pipeline Monitoring.''
