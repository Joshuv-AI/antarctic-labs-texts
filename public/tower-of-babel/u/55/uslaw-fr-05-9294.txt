Radio Broadcasting Services; Washington, KS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making (``Notice''), 
69 FR 54614 (September 9, 2004), this Report and Order dismisses the 
underlying Petition for Rule Making requesting the allotment of Channel 
271A at Washington, Kansas, because no comments or expressions of 
interest in response to the notice were received.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
