U.S. Virgin Islands; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the U.S. 
Virgin Islands (FEMA-1067-DR), dated September 16, 1995, and related 
determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 22, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pauline C. Campbell, Response and 

[[Page 51479]]
Recovery Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, 
DC 20472, (202) 646-3606.
