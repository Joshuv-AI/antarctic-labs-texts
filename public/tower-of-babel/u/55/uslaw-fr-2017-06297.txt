FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Deletion of Items From Sunshine Act Meeting

March 23, 2017.
    The following consent agenda items have been deleted from the list 
of items scheduled for consideration at the Thursday, March 23, 2017, 
Open Meeting and previously listed in the Commission's Notice of March 
16, 2017. The Consent Agenda has been adopted by the Commission.
* * * * *

Consent Agenda

    The Commission will consider the following subjects listed below as 
a consent agenda and these items will not be presented individually:
