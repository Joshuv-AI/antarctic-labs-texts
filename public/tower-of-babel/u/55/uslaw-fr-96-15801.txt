Certain Public Lands in Uintah and Grand Counties, UT; Temporary 
Closure to Camping

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, DOI.

ACTION: Temporary closure to camping on certain Public Lands in Uintah 
and Grand Counties, Utah, between August 5, 1996, and August 23, 1996, 
for dedication of the Book Cliff Wildlife Initiative. Action taken in 
accordance with 43 CFR Subpart 8364.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Certain Public Lands in the Vernal District, Uintah and Grand 
Counties, will be temporarily closed to overnight camping from 0600 
hours August 5, 1996, to 2400 hours August 23, 1996.
    Lands closed to camping are identified as the following: T. 15 S., 
R. 24 E., Sec. 36 SE\1/4\, SE\1/4\, and T. 15 S. R. 24 E., Sec. 32, 
S\1/2\ SE\1/4\ in Uintah County, Utah. T. 15\1/2\ S., R. 24 E., Sec. 
34, Lots 1 & 2; NW\1/4\ SE\1/4\; E\1/2\, SW\1/4\, SLB&M, in Grand 
County, Utah.
    The following persons operating within the scope of their official 
duties are exempt from the provision of this closure order: Bureau of 
Land Management employees; State of Utah employees, and other 
organizations or groups directly involved in the dedication of the Book 
Cliffs Wildlife Initiative.
