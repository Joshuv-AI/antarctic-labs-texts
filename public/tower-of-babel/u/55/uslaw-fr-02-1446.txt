DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Intent to Grant Exclusive Patent License; American 
BioHealth Group, LLC

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to American BioHealth Group, LLC, a revocable, nonassignable, 
exclusive license in the United States to practice, with right to 
sublicense, the Government-owned invention described in U.S. Patent No. 
6,177,434, entitled ``Prevention or Reversal of Sensorial Hearing Loss 
Through Biological Mechanisms,'' issued January 23, 2001, Navy Case No. 
78,564.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the granting of this license has 
(15) days from the date of this notice to file written objections along 
with supporting evidence, if any.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Office of Naval 
Research, ONR 00CC, Ballston Tower One, 800 North Quincy St., 
Arlington, VA 22217-5660.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. A. David Spevack, Supervisory 
Associate Counsel, Intellectual Property, Office of Naval Research, ONR 
00CC, Ballston Tower One, 800 North Quincy St., Arlington, VA 22217-
5660, telephone (703) 696-4007, E-Mail [email protected] or fax 
(703) 696-6909.
