Bridge Street Financial, Inc., and Oswego County Savings Bank, 
Oswego, New York; Approval of Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on November 14, 2002, the Director, 
Supervision Policy, Office of Thrift Supervision (``OTS''), or her 
designee, acting pursuant to delegated authority, approved the 
application of Oswego County Savings Bank, Oswego, New York, to convert 
to the stock form of organization. Copies of the application are 
available for inspection by appointment (phone number: 202-906-5922 or 
e-mail: [email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, OTS, 
1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS Northeast 
Regional Office, 10 Exchange Place, 18th Floor, Jersey City, New Jersey 
07302.

    Dated: November 15, 2002.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
