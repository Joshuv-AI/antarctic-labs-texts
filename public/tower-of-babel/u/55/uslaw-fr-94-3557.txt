POSTAL RATE COMMISSION

 

Briefing by NAA

February 10, 1994.
    Notice is hereby given that on February 28, 1994, members of the 
Commission and staff will be briefed by Newspaper Association of 
America on current trends in the newspaper industry. The briefing will 
be conducted at the Commission's Washington, DC office.
    Anyone seeking further information, or wishing to attend, should 
contact Charles L. Clapp, Secretary of the Commission, at 202-789-6840.
Charles L. Clapp,
Secretary.
