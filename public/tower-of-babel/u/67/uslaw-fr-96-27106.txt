Dow Intrastate Gas Company; Notice of Informal Settlement 
Conference

October 17, 1996.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Thursday, October 24, 1996, at 
9:00 A.M. in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 
20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and Staff. For additional 
information, please contact Frank Sparber at (202) 208-0335.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
