Sunshine Act Notice--December 8, 2011 Board of Directors Meeting

York Avenue NW., Washington, DC.

portion will commence at 10:15 a.m. (approx.).

4. Minutes of the Closed Session of the September 22, 2011 Board of 
Directors Meeting.

Directors Meeting.
6. Pending Major Projects.
