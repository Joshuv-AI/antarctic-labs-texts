DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Organization of PJM States, Inc., District of Columbia Public 
Service Commission, Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission, Kentucky 
Public Service Commission, Maryland Public Service Commission, New 
Jersey Board of Public Utilities, North Carolina Utilities Commission, 
Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission, and the Virginia State 
Corporation Commission, Petitioners; v. PJM Interconnection, L.L.C., 
Respondent: Notice of Designation of Commission Staff as Non-Decisional

April 25, 2007.
    Effective April 24, 2007, Alan Haymes, of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (Commission), was designated as non-decisional 
staff in the above-captioned docket. As non-decisional staff, Mr. 
Haymes will not participate in an advisory capacity in deliberations on 
the issues pending therein. Separated non-decisional and advisory 
staffs are prohibited from communicating with one another concerning 
the deliberations set forth above.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
