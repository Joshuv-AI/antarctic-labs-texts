Official Trail Marker for the Juan Bautista de Anza National 
Historic Trail

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Official Insignia, Designation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Authority: National Trails System Act, 16 U.S.C. 1241(a) and 
1246c and Protection of Official Badges, Insignia, etc. in 18 U.S.C. 
701.
SUMMARY: This notice issues the official trail marker insignia of the 
Juan Bautista de Anza National Historic Trail. The insignia for this 
trail was developed as part of the Trail's comprehensive management and 
use plan published in 1996. This publication accomplishes the official 
designation of the insignia now in use by the National Park Service.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The primary author of this document is 
Steven Elkinton, Program Leader for National Trails System Programming, 
National Center for Recreation and Conservation.
    The insignia depicted below is prescribed as the official trail 
marker logo for the Juan Bautista de Anza National Historic Trail, 
administered by the National Park Service, Pacific Great Basin Regional 
Ofice, San Francisco, CA. Authorization for use of this trail marker is 
controlled by the administrator of the Trail.
