Port Townsend Paper Corporation, Portland, OR; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on January 22, 2002, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by workers at Port Townsend Paper Corporation in 
Portland, Oregon.
    The petitioning workers have formally withdrawn the petition and 
consequentially, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purposes, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 15th day of February, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
