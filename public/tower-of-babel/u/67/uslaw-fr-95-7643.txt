COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' next meeting is scheduled for 20 April 
1995 at 10 am in the Commission's offices in the Pension Building, 
Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. 20001 
to discuss various projects affecting the appearance of Washington, 
D.C., including buildings, memorials, parks, etc.; also matters of 
design referred by other agencies of the government.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, DC, 21 March 1995.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
