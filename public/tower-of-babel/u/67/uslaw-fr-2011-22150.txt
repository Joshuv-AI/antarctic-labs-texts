SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    This rule establishes a Softwood Lumber Research, Promotion, 
Consumer Education and Industry Information Order (Order). The purpose 
of the Order is to strengthen the position of softwood lumber in the 
marketplace, maintain and expand markets for softwood lumber, and 
develop new uses for softwood lumber within the United States. The 
Order is issued pursuant to the Commodity Promotion, Research, and 
Information Act of 1996 (7 U.S.C. 7411-7425).
