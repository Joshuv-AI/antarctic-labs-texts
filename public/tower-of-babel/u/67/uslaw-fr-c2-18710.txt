DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality


Request for Measures of Patients' Hospital Care Experiences

Correction

    In notice document 02-18710 beginning on page 48477 in the issue of 
Wednesday, July 24, 2002 make the following corrections:
    1. On page 48478, in the first column, in the 9th line ``a standard 
that would be used nation'' should read ``a standard for measuring and 
publicly reporting patients' hospital care experiences''.
    2. On the same page, in the same column, under DATES, in the first 
line ``subject'' should read ``submit''.
    3. On page 48479, in the first column, in the third paragraph, in 
the first line ``effect'' should read ``effort''.
