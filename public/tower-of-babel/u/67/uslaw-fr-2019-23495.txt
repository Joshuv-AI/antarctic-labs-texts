DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. El19-101-000]


Southwest Power Pool, Inc.; Notice of Institution of Section 206 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On October 17, 2019, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL19-101-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 
16 U.S.C. 824e (2018), instituting a proceeding concerning the justness 
and reasonableness of Southwest Power Pool, Inc.'s resource adequacy 
minimum run-time requirement. Southwest Power Pool, Inc., 169 FERC 
61,048 (2019).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL19-101-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.
    Any interested person desiring to be heard in Docket No. EL19-101-
000 must file a notice of intervention or motion to intervene, as 
appropriate, with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street NE, Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Rule 214 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure, 18 CFR 385.214 (2019), 
within 21 days of the date of issuance of the order.

    Dated: October 18, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
