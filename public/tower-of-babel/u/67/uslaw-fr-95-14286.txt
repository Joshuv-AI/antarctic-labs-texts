DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute of Mental Health; Notice of Cancellation of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of one meeting of the 
National Institute of Mental Health which was published in the Federal 
Register on May 22, 1995 (60 FR 27115): the Board of Scientific 
Counselors, National Institute of Mental Health, June 13-14, 1995, 
Conference Room 1B07, Building 36, National Institutes of Health, 9000 
Rockville Pike, Bethesda, MD 20892.
    The meeting was cancelled due to prior commitments of several 
members.

    Dated: June 5, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
