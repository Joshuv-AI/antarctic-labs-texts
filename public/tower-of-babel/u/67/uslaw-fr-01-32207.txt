Lynchburg Foundry Company, Radford, VA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 5, 2001 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on October 30, 2001 on behalf of workers at Lynchburg 
Foundry Company, Radford, Virginia. The subject firm is a subsidiary of 
Intermet Corporation.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
40,060). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of December 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
