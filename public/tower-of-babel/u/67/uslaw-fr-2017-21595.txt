Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-44-2017]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 265--Conroe, Texas; Authorization of 
Production Activity; Bauer Manufacturing LLC dba NEORig; (Stationary 
Oil/Gas Drilling Rigs); Conroe, Texas

    On June 5, 2017, the City of Conroe, grantee of FTZ 265, submitted 
a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board on 
behalf of Bauer Manufacturing LLC dba NEORig, within Site 1 of FTZ 265, 
in Conroe, Texas.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (82 FR 30821-30822, July 3, 2017). On 
October 3, 2017, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision 
that no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
Section 400.14.

    Dated: October 3, 2017.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
