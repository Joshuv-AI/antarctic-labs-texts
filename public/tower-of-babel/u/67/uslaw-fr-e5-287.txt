Xcel Energy Services, Inc. and Southwest Power Pool, Inc.; Notice

proceeding in Docket No. EL05-41-000 under section 206 of the Federal 
Power Act.

pursuant to section 206(b) of the Federal Power Act will be May 20, 
2005.
