NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE: 10 a.m., Thursday, February 15, 2007.

PLACE: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047, 1775 Duke Street, Alexandria, 
VA 22314-3428.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: 
    1. Quarterly Insurance Fund Report.
    2. Report to Congress on the Study of Possible Changes to the 
Deposit Insurance System.
    3. Appeal from Delaware Federal Credit of the Regional Director's 
Denial of Conversion from a Multiple Common Bond to a Community 
Charter.
    4. Final Rule: Part 701 of NCUA's Rules and Regulations, General 
Lending Maturity Limit and Other Financial Services.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mary Rupp, Secretary of the Board,
