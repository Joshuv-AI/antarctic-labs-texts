FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 99-1425; MM Docket No. 99-88; RM-9515]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wells, NV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Mountain West Broadcasting, 
allots Channel 280C1 to Wells, NV, as the community's first local aural 
transmission service. See 64 FR 15713, April 1, 1999. Channel 280C1 can 
be allotted to Wells in compliance with the Commission's minimum 
distance separation requirements without the imposition of a site 
restriction, at coordinates 41-06-42 NL; 114-57-48 WL. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective September 7, 1999. A filing window for Channel 280C1 
at Wells, NV, will not be opened at this time. Instead, the issue of 
opening a filing window for this channel will be addressed by the 
Commission in a subsequent order.

(202) 418-2180.
