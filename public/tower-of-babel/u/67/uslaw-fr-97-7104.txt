FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have flied 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, D.C. 
20573.

International Service, Inc., 12000 Beacom Road, Sunbury, OH 43074, 
Officers: Daniel G. Chase, President, Chris Bartholomew, Stockholder
Trico American Air Freight and Forwarding Co. Inc., 13734 Shoreline 
Court East, Earth City, MO 63045, Officers: Richard L. Goode, 
President, Lester E. Maull, Secretary
Impel America packing and Appliances Corp., 5461 N.W. 72nd Avenue, 
Miami, FL 33166, Officers: Hector V. Marulanda, President, Maria L. 
Marulanda, Secretary

    Dated: March 17, 1997.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
