COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION

17 CFR Part 9


Performance of Certain Functions by the National Futures 
Association With Respect to Regulation 9.11

Correction

    In rule document 99-18806 beginning on page 39913, in the issue of 
Friday, July 23, 1999, make the following correction(s):
    1. On page 39914, in the second column, under the heading A. 
Processing Regulation 9.11 Filings and Notice of Filing Deficiencies, 
in the ninth line, ``regulation 9.11'' should read ``Regulation 9.11''.
    2. On the same page, at the bottom of the third column, the first 
footnote designated as footnote 12 should be designated as footnote 11.
    3. On the page 39915, in the third column, under the heading IV. 
Conclusion and Order, in the fourth full paragraph, in the eighth line, 
``relating'' should read ``retaining''.
