DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, March 25, 2013, 8:00 a.m. to 
March 25, 2013, 5:00 p.m., Hilton Washington DC North--Gaithersburg, 
620 Perry Parkway, Gaithersburg, MD, 20877 which was published in the 
Federal Register on January 14, 2013, 78 FR 5190.
    This notice is being amended to change the title to ``Novel Imaging 
Agents to Expand the Clinical Toolkit''. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: February 6, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
