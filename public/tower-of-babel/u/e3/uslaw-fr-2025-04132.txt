FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 90

[ET Docket No. 19-138; FCC 24-123; FR ID 284135]


Use of the 5.850-5.925 GHz Band; Corrections

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Correcting amendments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 13, 2024, the Federal Communications Commission 
(Commission) revised Commission rules. That document inadvertently 
added a rule section to its regulatory text that does not exist, 
resulting in an erroneous entry in the Code of Federal Regulations 
(CFR). This document corrects these errors in the regulatory text and 
the final regulations as published in the CFR.

DATES: Effective on March 21, 2025.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jamie Coleman of the Office of 
Engineering and Technology, at [email protected] or 202-418-2705.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a summary of the FCC's Final rule, 
FCC 24-123, published December 13, 2024 (89 FR 100838). This is the 
second set of corrections. The first set of corrections was published 
in the Federal Register on January 17, 2025 (90 FR 5724). This document 
augments the corrections that were published in the Federal Register on 
January 17, 2025 (90 FR 5724).

List of Subjects in 47 CFR Part 90

    Communications equipment, Radio, Reporting and recordkeeping

    Accordingly, 47 CFR part 90 is corrected by making the following
