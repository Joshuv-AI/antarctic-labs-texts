SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Advisory Council; Notice of Cancellation for Public 
Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA), National Advisory 
Council public meeting originally scheduled for Friday, June 30, 2006, 
will be cancelled until further notice. The Web site will be updated 
with information on the new date, time and location. The Web site is 
http://www.sba.gov/nac/index.hml.
    If you have any questions, please contact Balbina Caldwell, 
Director of the National Advisory Council, SBA Headquarters, 409 3rd 
Street, SW., Washington, DC 20416, phone (202) 205-6914, e-mail: 
[email protected].

Matthew K. Becker,
Committee Management Officer.
