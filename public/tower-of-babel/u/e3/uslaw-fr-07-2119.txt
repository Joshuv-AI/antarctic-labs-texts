FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting Schedule Change; Open Commission Meeting, 
April 25, 2007

April 25, 2007.
    The Federal Communications Commission previously announced its 
intention to hold an Open Meeting on Wednesday, April 25, 2007 at 9:30 
a.m. The meeting has been postponed until 6:45 p.m. The prompt and 
orderly conduct of the Commission's business requires this change and 
no earlier announcement was practicable.

Federal Communications Commission.
Marlene H. Dortch,
Secretary.

    Editorial Note: This document was received at the Office of the 
Federal Register on April 26, 2007.
