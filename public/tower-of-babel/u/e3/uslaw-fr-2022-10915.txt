FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[FR ID 87793]


Open Commission Meeting Thursday, May 19, 2022

May 12, 2022.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Thursday, May 19, 2022, which is scheduled 
to commence at 10:30 a.m.
    Due to the current COVID-19 pandemic and related agency telework 
and headquarters access policies, this meeting will be in an electronic 
format and will be open to the public only on the internet via live 
feed from the FCC's web page at www.fcc.gov/live and on the FCC's 
YouTube channel.
