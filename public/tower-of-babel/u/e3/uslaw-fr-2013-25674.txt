DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering Special Emphasis 
Panel, October 10-11, 2013, 03:00 p.m.-06:00 p.m., National Institutes 
of Health, Two Democracy Plaza, Suite 920, 6707 Democracy Boulevard, 
Bethesda, MD 20892, which was published in the Federal Register on July 
18, 2013, 78 FR 42970.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date from October 10-
11, 2013, to November 26, 2013 at 10:00 a.m. to 06:00 p.m. The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 25, 2013.
David Clary,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
