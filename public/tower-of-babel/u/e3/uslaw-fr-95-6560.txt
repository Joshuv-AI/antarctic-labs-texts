DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Government-Industry Advisory Committee on the Operation and 
Modernization of the National Defense Stockpile

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

----------------------------------------------------------------------- [[Page 14430]] 


SUMMARY: The first meeting of this committee will be held on March 30, 
1995, at the Doubletree Hotel, 300 Army Navy Drive, Arlington, VA. The 
meeting is open to the public. This committee was established under 
Public Law 102-484. The agenda for the meeting is as follows:

8:30 a.m.: Briefings to the committee members on National Defense 
Stockpile issues
11:30 a.m.: Luncheon
1:00 p.m.: Member's discussion of scope of work and committee structure
4:00 p.m.: Adjourn.

    For additional information contact Mr. Tom Meeker at 703-607-3203.

    Dated: March 13, 1995.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
