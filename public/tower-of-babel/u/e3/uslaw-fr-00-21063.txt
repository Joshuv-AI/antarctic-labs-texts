DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of National Oceanographic Partnership Program Ocean 
Research Advisory Panel Meeting

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Ocean Research Advisory Panel (ORAP) will meet to discuss 
National Oceanographic Partnership Program (NOPP) activities. This 
meeting will be open to the public.

DATES: The meeting will be held on Tuesday, October 3, 2000, from 8:30 
a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Carnegie Endowment, Choate 
Room, 1779 Massachusetts Avenue, NW, Washington DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Steven E. Ramberg, Office of Naval 
Research, 800 North Quincy Street, Arlington, VA 22217-5660, telephone 
number: (703) 696-4358.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This notice of meeting is provided in 
accordance with the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 
U.S.C. App. 2). Members of the public should submit their comments one 
week in advance of the meeting to the meeting Point of Contact. In 
order to maintain the meeting time schedule, members of the public will 
be limited in their time to speak to the Panel. The purpose of this 
meeting is to discuss NOPP activities. The meting will include 
discussions on ocean observations, current and future NOPP activities, 
and other current issues in the ocean sciences community.

    Dated: August 8, 2000.
J.L. Roth,
Lieutenant Commander, Judge Advocate General's Corps, U.S. Navy, 
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
