DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On November 5, 2007, the Department of Education published a 
comment period notice in the Federal Register (Page 62446, Column 2) 
for the information collection, ``Online and Distance Education Courses 
at Postsecondary Institutions.'' The title of the notice is hereby 
corrected to ``After School Programs at Public Elementary Schools.''
    The IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management 
Services, Office of Management, hereby issues a correction notice as 
required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

    Dated: November 5, 2007.
Angela C. Arrington,
IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management Services, 
Office of Management.
