Radio Broadcasting Services; Charlotte, Texas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a proposal filed by Charlotte Radio 
Broadcasting Company requesting the allotment of Channel 227A at 
Charlotte, Texas, as the community's first local broadcast service. See 
64 FR 57836, October 27, 1999. As stated in the Notice, a showing of 
continuing interest is required before a channel will be allotted. 
Since there has been no interest expressed for the allotment of a 
channel at Charlotte, Texas, in response to the Notice of Proposed Rule 
Making, the Report and Order dismisses the proposal.
