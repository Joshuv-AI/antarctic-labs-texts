FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the 
agreements to the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, 
Federal Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 
20573. Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received 
within 12 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
and the Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited review. Copies of agreements are 
available through the Commission's website (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of Agreements at (202)-523-5793 or 
[email protected].
    Agreement No.: 012486-001.
    Agreement Name: Crowley/King Ocean Vessel Sharing Agreement.
    Parties: Crowley Latin America Services, LLC; King Ocean Services 
Limited, Inc.
    Filing Party: Wayne Rohde; Cozen O'Connor.
    Synopsis: The Amendment revises Article 8 to extend the duration of 
the Agreement.
    Proposed Effective Date: 3/4/2024.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC.Agreements.Web/Public/AgreementHistory/2005.
    Agreement No.: 201416.
    Agreement Name: CMA CGM to ONE USEC--Caribbean--WCSA Space Charter 
Agreement.
    Parties: CMA CGM S.A.; Ocean Network Express Pte. Ltd.
    Filing Party: Draughn Arbona; CMA CGM S.A.
    Synopsis: The Agreement authorizes CMA CGM to charter space to ONE 
in the trade between Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Chile on the one hand, 
and the U.S. East Coast on the other hand.
    Proposed Effective Date: 1/18/2024.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC.Agreements.Web/Public/AgreementHistory/86542.

    Dated: January 19, 2024.

Federal Register Alternate Liaison Officer, Federal Maritime
