FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD

TIME AND DATE: 10 a.m., March 21, 1994.

PLACE: 4th Floor, Conference Room, 1250 H Street NW., Washington, DC.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

    1. Approval of the minutes of the February 22, 1994, Board 
meeting.
    2. Thrift Savings Plan activity report by the Executive 
Director.

and Welfare Benefits Administration Review of the Policies and

Administration Staff.''
