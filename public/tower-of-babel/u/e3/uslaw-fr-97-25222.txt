Industrial Non-Hazardous Waste Policy Dialogue Committee; Notice 
of Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Charter for the Environmental Protection Agency's (EPA) 
Industrial Non-Hazardous Waste Policy Dialogue Committee will be 
renewed for an additional two-year period, as a necessary committee 
which is in the public interest, in accordance with the provisions of 
the Federal Advisory Committee Act of (FACA), 5 U.S.C. appl.2 section 
9(c). The purpose of INHWPDC is to provide advice and counsel to the 
EPA on issues associated with management and disposal guidelines for 
industrial, non-hazardous waste under the Resource Conservation and 
Recovery Act. It is determined that INHWPDC is in the public interest 
in connection with the performance of duties imposed on the Agency by 
law.
    Inquiries may be directed to Deborah Dalton, Designated Federal 
Officer, NACEPT, U.S. EPA, Deputy Director, Consensus and Dispute 
Resolution Program (mail code 2136), 401 M Street, SW, Washington, DC 
20460.

    Dated: September 17, 1997.
Deborah Dalton,
Designated Federal Officer.
