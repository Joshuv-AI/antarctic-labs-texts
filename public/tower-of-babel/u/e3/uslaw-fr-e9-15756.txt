Best Textiles International Ltd, New York, NY; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 27, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the State of Georgia Employment and Training 
Consultant on behalf of workers of Best Textiles International LTD, New 
York, New York.
    The petition regarding the investigation has been deemed invalid. 
State agency representatives cannot file petitions for workers located 
in another state. Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of June 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
