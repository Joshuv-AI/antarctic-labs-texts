NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards (ACRS) Subcommittee 
Meeting on Thermal-Hydraulic Phenomena; Revised

    A portion of the ACRS Subcommittee meeting on Thermal-Hydraulic 
Phenomena scheduled to be held on May 15-16, 2007, Commissioners' 
Conference Room O-1G16, 11555 Rockville Pike, Rockville, Maryland, will 
be closed to discuss vendor proprietary information pursuant to 5 
U.S.C. 552b(c)(4). All other items pertaining to this meeting remain 
the same as published previously in the Federal Register on Wednesday, 
May 2, 2007 (72 FR 24339).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Zena Abdullahi, Designated Federal 
Official (Telephone: 301-415-2808) between 7:30 a.m. and 4:15 p.m. (ET) 
or by e-mail [email protected].

Branch Chief, ACRS.
