DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Meeting Cancellation for Advisory Council on Dependents' 
Education

AGENCY: Department of Defense Education Activity, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Federal Advisory Committee Act, Appendix 2 of 
Title 5, United States Code, Public Law 92-463, a notice published on 
August 24, 2010, (FR Doc. 2010-15985), announcing the meetings of the 
Advisory Council on Dependents' Education (ACDE) scheduled to be held 
on November 12, 2010, from 8 a.m. to 11 a.m. and on November 19, 2010, 
from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.; those meetings have been cancelled. A new 
meeting date will be announced.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Leesa Rompre at (703) 588-3128 or 
[email protected].

    Dated: October 26, 2010.
Morgan F. Park,
Alternate OSD Federal Register, Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
