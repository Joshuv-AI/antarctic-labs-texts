Green Mountain Power Corporation; Notice Granting Late 
Intervention

June 15, 2004.
    On February 18, 2000, the Commission issued a notice of application 
accepted for filing and soliciting motions to intervene and protests 
for the Waterbury Hydroelectric Project No. 2090, located on the Little 
River in Washington County, Vermont. The notice established April 17, 
2000, as the deadline for filing motions to intervene.
    On March 25, 2002, the Vermont Agency of Natural Resources filed a 
motion to intervene. Granting the motion to intervene will not unduly 
delay or disrupt the proceeding, or prejudice other parties to it. 
Therefore, pursuant to Rule 214,\1\ the motion to intervene filed by 
the Vermont Agency of Natural Resources is granted, subject to the 
Commission's rules and regulations.
