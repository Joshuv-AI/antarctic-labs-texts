DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Air Force


USAF Scientific Advisory Board Meeting

    The Spring General Board Meeting of the HQ USAF Scientific Advisory 
Board will meet 30 April-2 May 1997 at Air Education and Training 
Command, Randolph AFB, TX from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
    The purpose of the meeting is for the members to receive feedback 
on studies of the past year and hear special briefing on the upcoming 
year's focus.
    The meeting will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 
552b of Title 5, United States Code, specifically subparagraphs (1) and 
(4) thereof.
    For further information, contact the HQ USAF Scientific Advisory 
Board Secretariat at (703) 697-8404.
Carolyn A. Lunsford,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
