Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2023-1782.
    Petitioner: SkyWest Charter.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  135.324(a).
    Description of Relief Sought: SkyWest Charter (SWC), a part 135 air 
carrier certificate holder, requests relief from the requirements of 
Section 135.324(a) to the extent necessary to allow personnel from 
SkyWest Airlines, a part 121 air carrier certificate holder, to conduct 
training, testing and checking of SWC's pilots using SkyWest Airlines' 
training programs.
