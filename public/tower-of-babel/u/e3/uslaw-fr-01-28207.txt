Radio Broadcasting Services; Sabinal, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 296A to Sabinal, Texas, in 
response to a petition filed by Katherine Pyeatt. See 66 FR 44587, 
August 24, 2001. The coordinates for Channel 296A at Sabinal are 29-20-
17 NL and 99-29-00 WL. There is a site restriction 2.9 kilometers (1.8 
miles) northwest of the community. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated. A filing window for Channel 296A at Sabinal will not be 
opened at this time. Instead, the issue of opening this allotment for 
auction will be addressed by the Commission in a subsequent order.

DATES: Effective December 10, 2001.

(202) 418-2180.
