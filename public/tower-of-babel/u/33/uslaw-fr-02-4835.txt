SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration is seeking 
renewed Paperwork Reduction Act clearance for requirements currently 
cleared under OMB Numbers 0648-0420 (scallops) and 0648-0334 
(groundfish and crab), but proposes to merge these requirements under 
the latter number. These two collections of information originally were 
needed to make eligibility determinations to obtain a License 
Limitation Permit (LLP) to deploy a harvesting vessel in the king or 
Tanner crab fisheries in the Bering Sea/Aleutian Islands Management 
Area (BSAI), in the scallop fisheries, and in the directed groundfish 
fisheries (except for IFQ sablefish and for demersal shelf rockfish 
east of 140 degrees West longitude) in the GOA or the BSAI. The LLP has 
no expiration date; consequently, the application for eligibility was a 
one-time procedure. This collection now supports LLP transfer 
activities for crab, scallops, and groundfish, and any appeals 
resulting from denied actions.

II. Method of Collection

    The information is submitted to respond to requirements set forth 
in regulations at 50 CFR part 679.4. Paper applications are required 
from participants, and methods of submittal include facsimile 
transmission or mailing of paper forms.

III. Data
