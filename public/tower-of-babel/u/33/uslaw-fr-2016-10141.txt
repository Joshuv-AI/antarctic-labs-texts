Summit Hydropower, Inc.; Aspinook Hydro, LLC; Notice of 
Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To 
Intervene, and Protests

    On April 12, 2016, Summit Hydropower, Inc. (transferor) and 
Aspinook Hydro, LLC (transferee) filed an application for transfer of 
license of the Wyre Wynd Project No. 3472. The project is located on 
the Quinebaug River in New London County, Connecticut. The project does 
not occupy Federal lands.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Wyre Wynd Project from the transferor to the transferee.
