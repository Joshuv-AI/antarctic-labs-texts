FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-1726; MM Docket No. 01-197; RM-10170]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Baird, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 243C3 to Baird, Texas, in 
response to a petition filed by Katherine Pyeatt. See 66 FR 46426, 
September 5, 2001. The coordinates for Channel 243C3 at Baird are 32-
35-06 and 99-21-56. There is a site restriction 21.4 (13.3 miles) north 
of the community. With this action, this proceeding is terminated. A 
filing window for Channel 243C3 at Baird will not be opened at this 
time. Instead, the issue of opening this allotment for auction will be 
addressed by the Commission in a subsequent order.

DATES: Effective September 3, 2002
