Citizens Power & Light Corp.; Notice of Informational Filing

November 14, 1994.
    Take notice that on November 4, 1994, Citizens Power & Light 
Corporation (CP&L) filed certain information as required by the 
Commission's August 8, 1989 letter order in Docket No. ER89-401-000. 
Copies of CP&L's informational filing are on file with the Commission 
and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
