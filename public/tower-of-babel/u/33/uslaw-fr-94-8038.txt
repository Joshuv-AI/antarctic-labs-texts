DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Committee Meeting--Yakima Training Central Cultural and Natural 
Resources Committee--Policy Committee

AGENCY: Headquarters, I Corps and Fort Lewis, DOD.

ACTION: Advisory committee notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463) announcement is made of the following 
committee meeting:
    Name of the Committee: Yakima Training Center Cultural and Natural 
Resources Committee--Policy Committee.
    Date of the Meeting: May 19, 1994.
    Place: Yakima Training Center, Building 266, Yakima, Washington.
    Time: 1 p.m.
    Proposed Agenda:
    (1) Preliminary training calendar for YTC.
    (2) Update on YTC expansion acquisitions.
    (3) Cultural and Natural Resources Management Plan.
    All proceedings are open. For further information contact Stephen 
Hart, Chief, Civil Law, (206) 967-4540.
Kenneth L. Denton,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
