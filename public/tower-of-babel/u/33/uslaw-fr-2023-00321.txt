SUMMARY: On December 19, 2022, the Department of Education published in 
the Federal Register a request for information regarding HEA pooled 
evaluation. We are correcting the docket ID provided in that notice. 
All other information in the request for information, including the 
February 17, 2023, deadline for public comments, remains the same.

DATES: We must receive your comments on or before February 17, 2023.
