-----------------------------------------------------------------------

 

federal mine safety and health review commission

time and date: 2:00 p.m., Thursday, February 24, 1994.

place: 9th Floor, 1120 Twentieth Street, NW., Washington, DC.

status: Open.

matters to be considered: The Commission will hear oral argument on the 
following:

    1. W-P Coal Co., Docket No. WEVA 92-746 (Issues include whether 
the judge erred in concluding that the Secretary of Labor acted 
improperly in citing W-P for violations of the Mine Act committed 
its contractor.)

    Any person attending this oral argument who requires special 
accessibility features and/or auxiliary aids, such as sign language 
interpreters, must inform the Commission in advance of those needs. 
Subject to 29 CFR 2706.150(a)(3) and 2706.160(e).

time and date: Immediately following oral argument.

status: Closed [Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(10)].

matters to be considered: The Commission will consider and act upon the 
following:

    1. W-P Coal Co., Docket No. WEVA 92-746 (See Oral Argument 
Listing)

    It was determined by unanimous vote of Commissioners that this 
meeting be held in closed session.

contact person for more info: Jean Ellen (202) 653-5629/(202) 708-9300 
for TDD Relay/1-800-877-8339 for toll free.

    Dated: February 16, 1994.
Jean H. Ellen,
Chief Docket Clerk.
