Hewlett-Packard Company, Imaging & Printing Group--Technology 
Platforms Division; Corvallis, Oregon; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 3, 2005 in response to a worker petition which was 
filed on behalf of workers at Hewlett-Packard Company, Imaging & 
Printing Group--Technology Platforms Division, Corvallis, Oregon.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, (TA-W-56,696) which expires on May 7, 2007. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 2nd day of June, 2005.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
