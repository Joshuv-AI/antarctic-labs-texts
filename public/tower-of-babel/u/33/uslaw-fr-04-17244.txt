47 CFR Part 73

Digital Television Broadcast Service; Moscow, ID

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of the State Board of 
Education, State of Idaho, substitutes TV channel *35 for TV channel 
*12-- and DTV channel *12 for DTV channel *35 at Moscow, Idaho. See 67 
FR 63873, October 16, 2002. TV channel *35 can be substituted for TV 
channel *12--with a minus offset consistent with Sections 73.610 and 
73.611 of the Commission's Rules at coordinates 46-41-07 N. and 116-50-
34 W. DTV channel *12 can be substituted for DTV channel *35 at Moscow 
in compliance with the principal community coverage requirements of 
Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 46-40-54 N. and 116-58-13 W. 
with a power of 128.5, HAAT of 339.7 meters and with a DTV service 
population of 243 thousand. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

DATES: Effective September 13, 2004.
