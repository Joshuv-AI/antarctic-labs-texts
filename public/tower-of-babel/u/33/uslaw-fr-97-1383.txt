Old Augusta Railroad Company--Whole-Line Abandonment Exemption--
in Perry County, MS

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board.

ACTION: Notice of exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Board, under 49 U.S.C. 10502, exempts from the 
requirements of 49 U.S.C. 10903-04, the abandonment by Old Augusta 
Railroad Company of its entire 2.5-mile rail line located between 
milepost 0.0 at Augusta and milepost 2.5 at New Augusta, in Perry 
County, MS, subject to labor protective conditions.

DATES: This exemption will be effective on February 20, 1997. Petitions 
to stay must be filed by February 5, 1997 and petitions to reopen must 
be filed by February 17, 1997.
