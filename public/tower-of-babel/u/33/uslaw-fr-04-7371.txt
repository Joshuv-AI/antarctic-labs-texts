Radio Broadcasting Services; Durant, Oklahoma and Whitewright, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 68 FR 74202 
(December 23, 2003) this Report and Order dismisses the Petition for 
Rule Making in MB Docket No. 03-245 proposing to reallot Channel 248C2 
from Durant, Oklahoma, to Whitewright, Texas. The petitioner had 
requested this dismissal.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
