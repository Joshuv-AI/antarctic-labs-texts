DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, March 11, 2003, 10 a.m. to 
March 11, 2003, 2 p.m., which was published in the Federal Register on 
February 28, 2003, 68 FR 9700-9703.
    The meeting will be held at One Washington Circle Hotel, One 
Washington Circle, NW., Washington, DC 20037. The meeting date and time 
remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 3, 2003.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
