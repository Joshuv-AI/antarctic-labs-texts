ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Cancellation Notice of a Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: United States Election Assistance Commission (EAC).

ACTION: Notice to Cancel EAC Standards Board Virtual Public Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Election Assistance Commission has cancelled the EAC 
Standards Board Virtual Public Meeting scheduled for Monday, June 18, 
2007, 7 a.m. EDT through Wednesday, June 20, 5 p.m. EDT. The meeting 
was announced in a sunshine notice that was published in the Federal 
Register on Thursday, May 31, 2007. PERSON TO CONTACT FOR INFORMATION:
    Bryan Whitener, Telephone: (202) 566-3100.

Gracia M. Hillman,
Commissioner, U.S. Election Assistance Commission.
