and Delegations of Authority published on February 27, 2001 (66 FR 
12525).

following correction. On page 12526 in the third column, paragraph D, 
third line, insert ``Secretary, through the Deputy Assistant Secretary 
for Administration, on,'' after the word, ``Assistant.''
