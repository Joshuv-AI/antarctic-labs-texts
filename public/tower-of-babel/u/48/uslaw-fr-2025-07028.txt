DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-61-2024]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 29; Authorization of Production 
Activity; GoPro, Inc; (Camera Bundles); Louisville, Kentucky

    On December 19, 2024, Arvato USA, LLC, on behalf of GoPro, Inc, 
submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ 
Board for its facility within FTZ 29, in Louisville, Kentucky.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (90 FR 70, January 2, 2025). On April 
18, 2025, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act

[[Page 17241]]

and the FTZ Board's regulations, including section 400.14.

    Dated: April 18, 2025.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
