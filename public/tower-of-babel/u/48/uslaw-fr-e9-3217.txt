DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE


Supplemental Notice of Lodging of Consent Decree Under the Clean 
Water Act

    On January 14, 2009, the Department of Justice published notice of 
the December 31, 2008, lodging of a proposed consent in United States 
and State of Oregon v. Pacific Northern Environmental Corp., dba 
Dedicated Fuels, Inc., Civil Action No. 3:08-cv-01513-HU (D. Or.). See 
74 FR 2101 (Jan. 14, 2009).
    The United States hereby supplements its notice because Appendix A 
to the proposed consent decree was omitted from the document when it 
was made available for public inspection. The period for submitting any 
comment on the proposed consent decree, including Appendix A, is hereby 
extended for a period of ten (10) days after the date of publication of 
this Supplemental Notice.

Robert E. Maher, Jr.,
Assistant Section Chief, Environmental Enforcement Section.
