DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Health Resources and Services Administration


Grants to Improve Emergency Medical Services and Trauma Care in 
Rural Areas

AGENCY: Health Resources and Services Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice of extension of application due date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice extends the application due date for grants to 
improve emergency medical services (EMS) and trauma care in rural 
areas. The application due date for the EMS/trauma care grants in rural 
areas is extended to July 19, 1995. All other aspects of the April 20, 
1995, Federal Register notice (60 FR 19753) remain the same.

    Dated: June 1, 1995.
Ciro V. Sumaya
Administrator.
