OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Review of a 
Revised Information Collection: 
RI 94-7

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of a revised information collection. RI 
94-7, Death Benefit Payment Rollover Election for Federal Employees 
Retirement System (FERS), provides FERS surviving spouses and former 
spouses with the means to elect payment of the FERS rollover-eligible 
benefits directly or to an Individual Retirement Arrangement.
    Approximately 1,850 RI 94-7 forms will be completed annually. We 
estimate it takes approximately 60 minutes to complete the form. The 
annual estimated burden is 1,850 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact Mary Beth Smith-Toomey on 
(202) 606-8358, FAX (202) 418-3251 or E-mail to [email protected]. 
Please provide a mailing address with your request.
