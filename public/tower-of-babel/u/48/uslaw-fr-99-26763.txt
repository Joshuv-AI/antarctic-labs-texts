DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-36,825]


Chahta Enterprise, Dekalb, MS; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 13, 1999 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Chahta Enterprise, Dekalb, 
Mississippi.
    All workers of the subject film are included under an investigation 
currently in process (TA-W-36,641). Consequently, further investigation 
in this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 28th day of September 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
