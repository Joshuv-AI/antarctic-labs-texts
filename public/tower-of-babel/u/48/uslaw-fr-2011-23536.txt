DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 5, 2011, 3:30 p.m. to 
October 5, 2011, 6:30 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro 
Center, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published in the Federal Register 
on September 6, 2011, 76 FR 55076-55077.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the reassignment of applications.

    Dated: September 7, 2011.
Jennifer S. Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
