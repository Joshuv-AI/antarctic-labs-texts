ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-5013-7]

 

Chesapeake Bay Program; 1987 Chesapeake Bay Agreement; Proposals 
for Review

    The following draft documents, prepared pursuant to the 1987 
Chesapeake Bay Agreement by the Living Resources Subcommittee of the 
Chesapeake Bay Program, are now available for public review:
     Revised Chesapeake Bay Oyster Management Plan,
     Revised Chesapeake Bay Blue Crab Management Plan,
     Aquatic Reef Habitat Plan.
    Public comments will be accepted through July 31, 1994. Comments 
should be sent to Jennifer Gavin, Chesapeake Bay Program Office, Suite 
410, Annapolis, Maryland 21403. To obtain copies of the draft plans, 
call Jennifer Gavin at (800) 968-7229.
William Matuszeski,
Director, Chesapeake Bay Program Office.
