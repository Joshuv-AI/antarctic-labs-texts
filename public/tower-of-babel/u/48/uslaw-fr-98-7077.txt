DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE


Estimates of the Voting Age Population for 1997

    Under the requirements of the 1976 amendment to the Federal 
Election Campaign Act, Title 2, United States Code, Section 441a(e), I 
hereby give notice that the estimates of the voting age population for 
July 1, 1997, for each state and the District of Columbia are as shown 
in the following table.
    I have certified these counts to the Federal Election Commission.

    Dated: March 11, 1998.
William M. Daly,
Secretary of Commerce.
