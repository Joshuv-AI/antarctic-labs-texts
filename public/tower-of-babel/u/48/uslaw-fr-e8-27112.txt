47 CFR Part 73

Television Broadcasting Services; Kansas City, MO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission grants a petition for rulemaking filed by KMBC 
Hearst-Argyle Television, Inc., permittee of station KMBC-DT, to 
substitute DTV channel 29 for post-transition DTV channel 9 at Kansas 
City, Missouri.

DATES: Effective December 15, 2008.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shaun A. Maher, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
