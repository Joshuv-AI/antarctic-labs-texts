Agency Information Collection Activities; Announcement of OMB 
Approval; Guidance for Industry on Formal Dispute Resolution; Appeals 
Above the Division Level

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 8716]]

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing that a 
collection of information entitled ``Guidance for Industry on Formal 
Dispute Resolution; Appeals Above the Division Level'' has been 
approved by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) under the 
Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (the PRA).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Karen L. Nelson, Office of Information 
Resources Management (HFA-250), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 
Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-1482.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register of March 19, 1999 
(64 FR 13587), the agency announced that the proposed information 
collection had been submitted to OMB for review and clearance under 44 
U.S.C. 3507. An agency may not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not 
required to respond to, a collection of information unless it displays 
a currently valid OMB control number. OMB has now approved the 
information collection and has assigned OMB control number 0910-0430. 
The approval expires on December 31, 2002. A copy of the supporting 
statement for this information is available on the Internet at http://www.fda.gov/ohrms/dockets.

    Dated: February 14, 2000.
