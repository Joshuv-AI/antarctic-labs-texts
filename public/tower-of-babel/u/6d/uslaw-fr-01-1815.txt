INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Emergency Notice of Change of Time of 
Meeting

Agency Holding the Meeting: United States International Trade 
Commission.

Date and Time: January 18, 2001 at 11 a.m.

Place: Room 101, 500 E Street SW., Washington, DC 20436, Telephone: 
(202) 205-2000.

Status: Open to the public.
    In accordance with 19 CFR Sec. 201.35(d)(1), notice is hereby given 
that the Commission has determined to change the time of the meeting 
being held Thursday, January 18, 2001 from 2 p.m. to 11 a.m. Earlier 
notification of such change was not possible.

    Issued: January 17, 2001.

    By order of the Commission.
Donna R. Koehnke,
Secretary.
