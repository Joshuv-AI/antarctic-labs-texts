DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-17-2019]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 203--Moses Lake, Washington; 
Authorization of Production Activity; Framatome, Inc. (Fuel Rod 
Subassemblies); Richland, Washington

    On March 6, 2019, Framatome, Inc. submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility within 
FTZ 203, in Richland, Washington.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (84 FR 11503, March 27, 2019). On July 
5, 2019, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that no 
further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
Section 400.14.

    Dated: July 8, 2019.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
