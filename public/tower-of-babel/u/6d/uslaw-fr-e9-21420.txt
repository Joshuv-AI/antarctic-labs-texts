DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Plantation Fuel Reduction, Eldorado National Forest, Eldorado 
County, CA

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Withdrawal of notice of intent to prepare an Environmental 
Impact Statement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The USDA Forest Service, Eldorado National Forest will not 
prepare an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) for a proposal to treat 
approximately 4,637 acres of selected plantations on the Georgetown and 
Pacific Ranger Districts with a combination of mechanical precommercial 
thinning and control of competitive vegetation using mechanical and 
chemical treatments.
    The Notice of intent for this project was published in Federal 
Register Vol. 71. No. 195, October 10, 2006/Notices pages 59428-59429.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dana Walsh, Georgetown Ranger 
District, 7600 Wentworth Springs Rd., Georgetown, CA 95634, or by 
telephone at 530-333-4312.

Ramiro Villalvazo,
Forest Supervisor.
