NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Engineering: Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463. as amended) the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting.

    Name and Committee Code: Advisory Committee for Engineering 
(#1170).
    Date and Time: October 28, 1998/8:30 a.m.-6:00 p.m. October 29, 
1998/8:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m.
    Place: Room 1235 (National Science Board Meeting Room), National 
Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Dr. Elbert L. Marsh, Deputy Assistant Director 
for Engineering, National Science Foundation, Suite 505, 4201 Wilson 
Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230; Telephone: (703) 306-1301. For 
easier building access, individuals planning to attend should 
contact Maxine Byrd at 703-306-1300 or at [email protected] so that your 
name can be added to the building access list.
    Minutes: May be obtained from the contact person listed above.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice, recommendations and 
counsel on major goals and policies pertaining to Engineering 
programs and activities.
    Agenda: Discussion on issues, opportunities, and future 
directions for the Engineering Directorate; in particular, the

[[Page 53106]]

Advisory Committee will focus on the NSF Performance Plan in 
response to the Government Performance and Results Act requirements.

    Dated: September 29, 1998.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
