Visas: Documentation of Immigrants Under the Immigration and 
Nationality Act; Validity of Immigrant Visas

AGENCY: Bureau of Consular Affairs, State.

ACTION: Correction to final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the final rule 
published on May 21, 1997 [62 FR 27693-27694]. The regulation 
implemented sec. 631(a) of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant 
Responsibility Act of 1996, which extended the validity of an immigrant 
visa from four months to six months.

DATES: This rule is effective October 1, 1997.
