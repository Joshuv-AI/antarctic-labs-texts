FEDERAL COMMUNICATION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    March 10, 2000; FCC To Hold Open Commission Meeting Friday, March 
17, 2000
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Friday, March 17, 2000, which is scheduled 
to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, S.W., 
Washington, D.C.
