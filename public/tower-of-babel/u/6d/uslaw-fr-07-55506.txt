CFR Correction

    In Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 63 (Sec. Sec.  
63.600 to 63.1199), revised as of July 1, 2006, in Sec.  63.1103, 
paragraph (e)(2), on page 547, in alphabetical order, add the 
definition of ``Organic HAP'' to read as follows:


Sec.  63.1103  Source category-specific applicability, definitions, and 
requirements.

* * * * *
    (e)* * *
    (2)* * *
    Organic HAP means the compounds listed in Table 1 to subpart XX of 
this part.
