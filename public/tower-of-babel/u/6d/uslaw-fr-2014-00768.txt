Radio Broadcasting Services; Heber Springs, Arkansas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Sydney Allison Sugg, the Audio Division 
amends the FM Table of Allotments, by allotting Channel 270C3 at Heber 
Springs, Arkansas, as the community third local FM transmission 
service. A staff engineering analysis confirms that Channel 270C3 can 
be allotted to Heber Springs consistent with the minimum distance 
separation requirements of the Rules with a site restriction 12.8 
kilometers (7.9 miles) northeast of the community. The reference 
coordinates are 35-34-12 NL and 91-55-41 WL.

DATES: Effective February 3, 2014.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2700.
