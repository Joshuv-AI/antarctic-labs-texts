Labinal-Corinth, Inc., Formerly Known as the Boeing Company, 
Boeing Defense and Space Group, Commercial Airplane Group, Corinth, TX; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 18, 2003 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Labinal-Corinth, Inc., Corinth, 
Texas.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-40,525E, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 18th day of August 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
