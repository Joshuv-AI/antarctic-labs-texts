DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

48 CFR Part 225

 

Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement; North American 
Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act

AGENCY: Defense.

ACTION: Correction to interim regulations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to an interim rule on 
North American Free Trade Agreement, that was published in the Federal 
Register on January 10, 1994 (59 FR 1288). This action is necessary to 
correct the amendatory language.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lucile Martin at (703) 604-5929.
Claudia L. Naugle,
Deputy Director, Defense Acquisition Regulations Council.
