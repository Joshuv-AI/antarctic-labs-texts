Timely Mailing Treated as Timely Filing

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service, Treasury.

ACTION: Correcting amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to final regulations (TD 
9543) that were published in the Federal Register on Tuesday, August 
23, 2011 (76 FR 52561), the regulations provide guidance on the proper 
use of registered or certified mail, or a service of a private delivery 
service designated under criteria established by the Internal Revenue 
Service, will constitute prima facie evidence of delivery. The 
regulations affect taxpayers who mail Federal tax documents to the 
Internal Revenue Service or the United States Tax Court.

DATES: This correction is effective on October 6, 2011 and applies to 
any payment or document mailed and delivered in accordance with the 
requirements of Sec.  301.7502-1 in an envelope bearing a postmark 
dated after September 21, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Steven Karon, (202) 622-4570 (not a 
toll-free number).
