Mountain Skylines, Inc, Leavenworth, WA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 8, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Mountain Skylines, Inc, 
Leavenworth, Washington.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of May 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
