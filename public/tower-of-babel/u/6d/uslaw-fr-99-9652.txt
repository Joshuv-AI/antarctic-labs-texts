DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP99-227-000]


High Island Offshore System; Notice of Technical Conference

April 13, 1999.
    In the Commission's order issued on March 31, 1999, 86 FERC para. 
61,321 (1999), the Commission directed that a technical conference be 
held to address issues raised by the filing.

[[Page 19142]]

    Take notice that the technical conference will be held on 
Wednesday, May 12, 1999, at 10:00 a.m., in a room to be designated at 
the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested parties and staff are permitted to attend.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
