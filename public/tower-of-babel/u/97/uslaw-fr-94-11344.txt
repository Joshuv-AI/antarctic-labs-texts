NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION

TIME AND DATE: 2:00 p.m., Thursday, May 12, 1994.

PLACE: Festival Center, 1640 Columbia Road, NW., Washington, DC.

STATUS: Open.

BOARD BRIEFINGS:

    1. Insurance Fund Report.
    2. Legislative Update.

    2. Final Rule: Amendments to Section 701.32, NCUA's Rules and

PLACE: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047; 1775 Duke Street, Alexandria, 
Virginia 22314-3428.

STATUS: Closed.

and (9)(B).
    4. Personnel Policies. Closed pursuant to exemption (2).

RECESS: 10:30 a.m.
