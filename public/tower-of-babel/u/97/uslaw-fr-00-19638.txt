DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases; 
Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National 
Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, July 31, 2000, 11 a.m. to July 31, 2000, 12 p.m., 6707 
Democracy Blvd, 2 Democracy Plaza, Rm 653, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was 
published in the Federal Register on July 13, 2000, 65 FR 43379.
    The meeting is cancelled due to application being withdrawn.

    Dated: July 24, 2000.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
