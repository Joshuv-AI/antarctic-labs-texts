National Science Foundation


Advisory Committee for Education and Human Resources; Notice of 
Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L.92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Committee on the NSF Niche in K-16 Education, a 
subcommittee of the Advisory Committee for Education and Human 
Resources (#1119).
    Date and Time: October 17, 2000; 1:30 p.m.-5:30 p.m.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Room 
1235 Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Dr. Daryl Chubin, NSB Senior Policy Officer, 
National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Room 1220, 
Arlington, VA 22230, Telephone: (703)292-7000.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations to the 
Advisory Committee for Education and Human Resources.
    Agenda: To discuss NSF's niche in K-16 Education.
    Reason for Late Notice: Coordinating members of committee to 
accommodate meeting schedules. This meeting needs to be held before 
the Advisory Committee for Education and Human Resources meeting on 
November 8-9.

    Dated: October 3, 2000.
Karen J. York,
Committee Management Officer.
