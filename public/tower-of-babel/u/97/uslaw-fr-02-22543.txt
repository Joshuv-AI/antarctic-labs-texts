St. Lawrence & Atlantic Railroad Company--Lease and Operation 
Exemption--Rail Lines of Fraser N.H. LLC

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Board grants an exemption under 49 U.S.C. 10502, from the 
prior approval requirements of 49 U.S.C. 11323-25, for St. Lawrence & 
Atlantic Railroad Company, a Class III rail carrier, to lease and 
operate over lines of Fraser N.H. LLC (Fraser), a Class III rail 
carrier, consisting of: (1) 5.5 miles of rail line between milepost 
154.6 at Berlin, NH, and milepost 149.1 at Gorham, NH; and (2) 0.5 
miles of rail line in the vicinity of Berlin, a total distance of 6.0 
miles in Coos County, NH.

DATES: This exemption will be effective September 5, 2002. Petitions to 
reopen must be filed by September 25, 2002.
