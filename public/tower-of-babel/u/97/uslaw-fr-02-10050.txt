A.S. Haight & Company, Inc. Cartersville, GA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 21, 2001, in response to a worker petition 
which was field by the UNITE! Southern Regional Joint Board, Local 308, 
on behalf of workers at Haight & Company, Inc., Cartersville, Georgia.
    The petitioning group of workers are subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
40,392). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 12th day of April, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
