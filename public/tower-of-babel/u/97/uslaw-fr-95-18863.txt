DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Agency Information Collection Under OMB Review

Title: Child Care and Development Block Grant Reporting Requirements
OMB No.: 0980-0241
Description: The purpose of this collection is to obtain data from 
CCDBG grantees regarding their efforts to increase the availability, 
affordability and quality of child care using CCDBG funds. This data 
collection is mandated by statue
Respondents: State governments
