Revocation of Customs Broker License

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Broker license revocation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the Commissioner of Customs, 
pursuant to Section 641, Tariff Act of 1930, as amended, (19 U.S.C. 
1641), and parts 111.51 and 111.74 of the Customs Regulations, as 
amended (19 CFR 111.51 and 111.74), canceled the following Customs 
broker license with prejudice.
