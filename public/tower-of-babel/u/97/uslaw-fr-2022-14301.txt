DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force

32 CFR Part 842


Administrative Claims

CFR Correction

    This rule is being published by the Office of the Federal Register 
to correct an editorial or technical error that appeared in the most 
recent annual revision of the Code of Federal Regulations.

0
In Title 32 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 800 to End, 
revised as of July 1, 2021, in Sec.  842.55, reinstate paragraph (r) to 
read as follows:


Sec.  842.55  Claims not payable

* * * * *
    (r) Is one for which a foreign government is responsible under 
SOFA, treaty, or other agreement. However, AFLOA/JACC may authorize 
payment of a claim where the foreign government refuses to recognize 
its legal responsibilities and the claimant has no other means of 
compensation.
