Notice To Extend Public Comment Period for a Draft Environmental 
Impact Statement (DEIS)

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice to extend public comment period for the Powder River 
Basin Oil and Gas DEIS.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Public comment period is being extended on the Powder River 
EIS. The comment period is being extended due to extensive public 
comment, a high level of public interest, and the lack of internet 
access during a portion of the comment period.

DATES: The public comment period is being extended to May 15, 2002. 
Submissions should be in writing or by E-mail (see addresses below).

ADDRESSES: Comments should be submitted in writing to: Field Manager, 
Bureau of Land Management, Buffalo Field Office, 1425 Fort Street, 
Buffalo, Wyoming 82834 or by E-mail to: [email protected].

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paul Beels, Powder River Oil and Gas 
EIS Project Leader, Bureau of Land Management, Buffalo Field Office, at 
the above addresses or at telephone number (307) 684-1100.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Notice of Availability of the DEIS was 
originally published in the Federal Register on January 11, 2002 (67 FR 
1497).

    Dated: April 12, 2002.
Alan L. Kesterke,
Associate State Director.
