Air Force A-76 Initiatives Cost Comparisons and Direct 
Conversions (As of 30 September 1999)

    The Air Force is in the process of conducting the following A-76 
initiatives. Cost comparisons are public-private competitions. Direct 
conversions are functions that may result in a conversion to contract 
without public competition. These initiatives were announced and in-
progress as of 30 September 1999, include the installation and state 
where the cost comparison or direct conversion is being performed, the 
total authorizations under study, public announcement date and actual 
or anticipated solicitation date. The following initiatives are in 
various stages of completion.
