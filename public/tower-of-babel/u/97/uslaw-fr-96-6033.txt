COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION


Coffee, Sugar and Cocoa Exchange: Proposed Amendments Relating to 
the Quality Standards, Delivery Ports, Packaging, Demurrage, and 
Trading Month Specifications for the White Sugar Futures Contract

AGENCY: Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

ACTION: Correction of Closing Date for Public Comment Period for 
Proposed Contract Rule Changes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On March 7, 1996, the Division of Economic Analysis (``Division''), 
acting pursuant to Commission Regulation 140.96, published a notice in 
the Federal Register (61 FR 9147) on behalf of Commodity Futures 
Trading Commission requesting public comment on the referenced proposed 
amendments by the Coffee, Sugar and Cocoa Exchange (``CSCE''). In 
accordance with Section 5a(a)(12) of the Commodity Exchange Act, the 
public comment period for the CSCE's proposed amendments ends April 8, 
1996.
    Any person interested in submitting written data, views or 
arguments on the proposed amendments should send such comments to Jean 
A. Webb, Secretary, Commodity Futures Trading Commission, Three 
Lafayette Centre, 1155 21st Street NW, Washington, D.C. 20581 by the 
specified date.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on March 8, 1996.
Blake Imel,
Acting Director.
