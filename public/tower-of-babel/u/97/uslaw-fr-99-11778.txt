3(A) of The Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 
U.S.C. 552B:

DATE AND TIME: May 12, 1999, 10:00 a.m.

PLACE: Room 2C, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.

STATUS: Open.

notice.

CONTACT PERSON FOR: David P. Boergers, secretary, telephone (202) 208-
0400. For a recording listing items stricken from or added to the
