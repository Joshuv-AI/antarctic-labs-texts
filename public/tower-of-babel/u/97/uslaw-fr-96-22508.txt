NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

10 CFR Chapter I


Issuance of Report on the NRC Regulatory Agenda

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Issuance of NRC Regulatory Agenda.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 46538]]

SUMMARY: The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has issued the NRC 
Regulatory Agenda for the period covering January through June of 1996. 
This agenda provides the public with information about NRC's rulemaking 
activities. The NRC Regulatory Agenda is a compilation of all rules on 
which the NRC has recently completed action, or has proposed action, or 
is considering action, and of all petitions for rulemaking that the NRC 
has received that are pending disposition. Issuance of this publication 
is consistent with Section 610 of the Regulatory Flexibility Act.

ADDRESSES: A copy of this report, designated NRC Regulatory Agenda 
(NUREG-0936), Vol. 15, No. 1, is available for inspection, and copying 
for a fee, at the Nuclear Regulatory Commission's Public Document Room, 
2120 L Street, NW. (Lower Level), Washington, DC.
