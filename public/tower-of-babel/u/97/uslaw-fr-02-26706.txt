DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the PubMed Central 
National Advisory Committee, November 4, 2002, 9:30 am to November 4, 
2002, 4 pm, Library of Medicine, Board Room, Room 2E17, Bldg. 38, 8600 
Rockville Pike, Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 21,2002, 67 FR 54227.
    The meeting is cancelled due to concern regarding quorum and 
availability of Committee members.

    Dated: October 10, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
