FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 98-3; RM-9218]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Manson, WA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Manson Broadcasting, allots 
Channel 234C3 at Manson, Washington, as the community's first local 
aural transmission service. See 63 FR 4206, January 28, 1998. Channel 
234C3 can be allotted to Manson in compliance with the Commission's 
minimum distance separation requirements at city reference coordinates. 
The coordinates for Channel 234C3 at Manson are 47-53-18 North Latitude 
and 120-09-18 West Longitude. Since Manson is located within 320 
kilometers (200 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border, concurrence of the 
Canadian government has been obtained. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective: March 15, 1999. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 234C3 at Manson, Washington, will not be 
opened at this time. Instead, the issue of opening a filing window for 
this channel will be addressed by the Commission in a subsequent order.
