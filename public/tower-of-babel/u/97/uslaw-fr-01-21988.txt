DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Notice of Proposed Information Collection Requests

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On August 27, 2001, a 30-day notice inviting comment from the 
public was published for the School Renovation, Individuals with 
Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), and Technology Grant Application in 
the Federal Register (Volume 66, Number 166) dated August 27, 2001. The 
abstract for this notice was printed incorrectly. The abstract for the 
information collection should read as follows:
    ED will use the information collected through this application to 
award grants to approximately 52 State educational agencies that will 
conduct competitive grant processes to award subgrants to eligible 
local educational agencies. The information will also be used to 
describe to the Congress and the public how these grants are being 
used.
    The Leader, Regulatory Information Management, Office of the Chief 
Information Officer, hereby issues a correction notice on the 
submission for OMB review as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 
1995.
