Radio Broadcasting Services; Madison, MO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division denies a Petition for Rule Making filed by 
American Family Association, requesting the reservation of vacant 
Channel 247C3 at Madison, Missouri for noncommercial educational use.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
