47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-893, MM Docket No. 00-138, RM-9896]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Boca Raton, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of The School Board of Broward 
County, Florida, substitutes DTV channel *40 for DTV channel *44 at 
Boca Raton, Florida. See 65 FR 50951, August 22, 2000. DTV channel *40 
can be allotted to Boca Raton in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates (25-59-34 N. and 80-10-27 W.) with a power of 1000, HAAT of 
310 meters and with a DTV service population of 3989 thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective June 3, 2002.
