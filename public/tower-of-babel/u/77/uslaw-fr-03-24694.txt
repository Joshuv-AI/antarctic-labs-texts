AT&T Wireless Services, Inc., Call Center, Livermore, CA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 18, 2003 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a state agency representative on behalf of workers at 
AT&T Wireless Services, Inc., Call Center, Livermore, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
