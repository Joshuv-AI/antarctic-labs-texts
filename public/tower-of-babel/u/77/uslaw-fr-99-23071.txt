Radio Broadcasting Services; Canovanas, Culebra, Las Piedras, 
Mayaguez Quebradillas San Juan and Vieques, PR, and Christiansted and 
Frederiksted, VI

ACTION: Final rule; Application for review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies an Application for Review filed by WKJB 
AM-FM, Inc. directed to the Memorandum Opinion and Order in this 
proceeding. Based upon preferential FM allotment priorities, the 
Commission finds a proposed channel substitution, its reallotment, and 
the modification of a station's license to be within the public's 
interest. With this action, the proceeding published September 16, 1996 
(61 FR 48638) is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.

and Order in MM Docket No. 91-259, adopted June 17, 1999, and released
