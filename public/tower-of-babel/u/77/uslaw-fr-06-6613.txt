Additional Information

    By a vote of 5-0 on July 24 and 25, 2006, the Commission determined 
pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec.  9.107(a) of the Commission's rules 
that ``Discussion of Management Issues (closed--ex 2)'' be held July 
27, 2006 and by a vote of 4-1 that the meeting be held on less than one 
week's notice to the public. Commissioner Jaczko did not vote to hold 
the meeting on short notice.
