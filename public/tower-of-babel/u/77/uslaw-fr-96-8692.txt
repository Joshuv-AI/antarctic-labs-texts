FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Rescission of Order of 
Revocation

    Notice is hereby given that the Order of Revocation revoking the 
license of Aces, Ltd., has been rescinded by the Federal Maritime 
Commission pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 
U.S.C. app. 1718) and the regulations of the Commission pertaining to 
the licensing of ocean freight forwarders, 46 CFR Part 510.
