DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

Advisory Committee on Former Prisoners of War, Notice of Charter 
Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (Public Law 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App.), that the Department of 
Veterans Affairs' Advisory Committee on Former Prisoners of War has 
been renewed for a 2-year period beginning September 29, 1995, through 
September 29, 1997.

    Dated: September 28, 1995.

    By Direction of the Secretary.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
