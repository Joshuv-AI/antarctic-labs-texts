Mississippi L&D 25 Hydro, LLC; Notice of Preliminary 
Permit Application Accepted for Filing and Soliciting Comments, Motions 
To Intervene and Competing Applications

February 4, 2009.
    On November 3, 2008, Mississippi L&D 25 Hydro, LLC filed 
an application, pursuant to section 4(f) of the Federal Power Act 
(FPA), proposing to study the feasibility of the Mississippi River Lock 
& Dam 25 Project to be located in Lincoln County, Missouri and Calhoun 
County, Illinois. The proposed project would utilize the existing U.S. 
Army Corps of Engineers Lock and Dam 25 on the Mississippi River.
    The proposed project would consist of: (1) A powerhouse containing 
4 turbine/generator units with a total installed capacity of 80.4 MW; 
(2) a 6.8 mile long, 34.5 kV transmission line and; (3) appurtenant 
facilities. The proposed project would have an annual production of 403 
GWh which would be sold to a local utility.
