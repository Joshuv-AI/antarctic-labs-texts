DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board
[Docket 15-99]


Foreign-Trade Zone No. 122--Corpus Christi, TX, Application for 
Subzone, Equistar Chemicals, LP (Oil Refinery), Nueces County, TX; 
Correction

    The Federal Register notice (64 FR 25477, 5/12/99) describing the 
application submitted to the Foreign-Trade Zones Board (the Board) by 
the Port of Corpus Christi Authority, grantee of FTZ 122, requesting 
special purpose subzone status for the petrochemical complex of 
Equistar Chemicals, LP, located in Nueces County, Texas, is corrected 
as follows: the word ``leased'' should be deleted from that portion of 
paragraph 2, sentence 1, describing the tanks at Site 2.

    Dated: June 2, 1999.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Acting Executive Secretary.
