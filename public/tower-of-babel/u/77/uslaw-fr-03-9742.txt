Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Foreign-Trade Zone 202-Los Angeles, CA; Withdrawal of Application 
for Subzone Status for California Steel Industries, Inc.; Steel Mill 
Products Manufacturing Facilities in Fontana, CA

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the application 
submitted by the Board of Harbor Commissioners of the City of Los 
Angeles, California, grantee of FTZ 202, requesting special-purpose 
subzone status for the manufacturing facilities (steel mill products) 
of California Steel Industries, Inc., in Fontana, California. The 
application was filed on May 24, 1996 (61 FR 14157, 6/6/96). The 
application has now been withdrawn by the applicant, and the case has 
been closed without prejudice.

    Dated: April 10, 2003.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Executive Secretary.
