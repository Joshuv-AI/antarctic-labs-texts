DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 3740]


U.S. Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy; Notice of Meeting

    The U.S. Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy will meet on 
Wednesday, September 19, 2001, in Room 600, 301 4th St., SW., 
Washington, DC from 3:30 p.m. to 5 p.m.
    The Commission will discuss public diplomacy strategies as they 
relate to the Middle East peace process and U.S. policies toward Iraq.
    Members of the general public may attend the meeting, although 
attendance of public members will be limited to the seating available. 
Access to the building is controlled, and individual building passes 
are required for all attendees. Persons who plan to attend should 
contact Bruce Gregory at (202) 619-4457.

    Dated: August 27, 2001.
Bruce Gregory,
U.S. Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy, U.S. Department of State.
