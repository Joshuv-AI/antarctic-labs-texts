DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-206-005]


Atlanta Gas Light Company; Supplemental Notice of Technical 
Conference

October 13, 1999.
    Take notice that the time for the technical conference scheduled in 
this docket on October 20, 1999 is rescheduled to 1:00 to 5:00 p.m., 
and will held in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, D.C. 
20426.
    The following is the agenda for the conference:

1. Examination of the Effort of the Waivers on the Interstate Market
2. Identification of Alternatives to the Waivers
3. Other issues

    Discussion at the conference will be focused mainly on the 
identification of issues and the relevant information.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
