Notice of Realty Action: Direct Sale of Public Lands in Pima 
County, AZ; Correction.

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Realty Action; Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) published a notice in the 
Federal Register of July 5, 2013, concerning a ``Direct Sale of Public 
Lands in Pima County, AZ.'' That notice

[[Page 49536]]

cited an erroneous serial number in its heading. This notice corrects 
that error.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Linda Dunlavey, Realty Specialist, 
BLM, Tucson Field Office, 3201 East Universal Way, Tucson, Arizona 
85756; telephone 520-258-7260 or email [email protected].

Correction

    In the Federal Register of July 5, 2013, in Federal Register 
document 78-40503, on page 40503, at the bottom of the first column, 
within the fourth line of the notice, correct the BLM's serial number 
to read AZA-36547 from the incorrect serial number AZA-281317-01.

Viola Hillman,
Tucson Field Manager.
