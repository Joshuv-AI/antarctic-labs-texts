Notice

July 14, 2005.
    The following notice of meeting is published pursuant to section 
3(a) of the government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. 94-409), 5 U.S.C 
552b:

    Agency Holding Meeting: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.
    Date and Time: July 21, 2005; 10 a.m.
    Place: Room 2C, 888 First Street, NE., Washington DC 20426.
    Status: Open.
    Matters to be Considered: Agenda.
    *Note--Items listed on the agenda may be deleted without further 
notice.
    Contact person for more information: Magalie R. Salas, 
Secretary, Telephone (202) 502-8400.
    For a recorded listing item stricken from or added to the 
meeting, call (202) 502-8627.

does not include a listing of all papers relevant to the items on 
the agenda; however, all public documents may be examined in the 
Public Reference Room.
