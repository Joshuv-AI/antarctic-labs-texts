NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management; Renewals, Reestablishment, Amendment, and 
Terminations

    Effective June 30, 1996, the following actions will occur in NSF's 
advisory committee structure:
    Renewals: The Assistant Directors having responsibility for the 
Advisory Committees listed below have determined that renewal of these 
groups is necessary and in the public interest in connection with the 
performance of duties imposed upon the Director, National Science 
Foundation (NSF), by 42 USC 1861 et seq. This determination follows 
consultation with the Committee Management Secretariat, General 
Services Administration. Authority for these Advisory Committees will 
expire on June 30, 1998, unless they are renewed.
