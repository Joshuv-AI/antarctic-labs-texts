FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 90-550; RM-7345]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Lafayette, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission dismisses a Petition for Reconsideration filed 
by C.R. Crisler. Crisler sought reconsideration of the action taken by 
the Chief, Allocations Branch in MM Docket No. 90-550, in which 
Lafayette FM Joint Venture (``LFMJV''), the permittee of Station 
KRRQ(FM) in Lafayette, Louisiana, was granted an upgrade of its station 
from Channel 238A to 238C2. 57 FR 45002 (Sept. 30, 1992). The 
Commission denied Crisler's petition in that it failed to raise 
arguments that warranted denying an upgrade of LFMJV's station.

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 20, 1995.
