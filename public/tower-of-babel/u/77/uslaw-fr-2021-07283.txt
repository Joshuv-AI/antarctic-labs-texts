COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Oregon Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Oregon Advisory Committee.

cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of Monday, April 5, 
2021, in FR Doc. 2021-06894, on page 17589.
