Yukon Pacific Company L.P.; Meeting on Environmental Issues

March 10, 1994.
    On March 15, 1994, Office of Pipeline and Producer Regulation 
environmental staff will attend a meeting with other agencies sponsored 
by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Region 10, to 
discuss with representatives of Yukon Pacific Company L.P. its proposed 
activities under the Clean Air Act and the Clean Water Act. The meeting 
will be at the offices of EPA, Region 10 in Seattle, Washington. For 
further information contact Chris Zerby at (202) 208-0111.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
