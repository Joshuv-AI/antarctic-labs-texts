FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 14-1479]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Various Locations

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division amends the FM Table of Allotments to remove 
certain vacant FM allotments that were auctioned in FM Auction 93 that 
are currently considered authorized stations. FM assignments for 
authorized stations and reserved facilities will be reflected solely in 
Media Bureau's Consolidated Database System (CDBS).

DATES: Effective October 28, 2014.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2700.
