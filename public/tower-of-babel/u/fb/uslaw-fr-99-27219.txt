DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket Nos. RP98-117-000 and TM99-2-53-000 (Consolidated)]


KN Interstate Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Settlement 
Conference

October 13, 1999.
    Take notice that a Settlement conference will be convened to 
discuss an Offer of Settlement in Docket Nos. RP98-117-000 and TM99-2-
53-000. The Settlement Conference is scheduled for Wednesday, October 
27, 1999, at 10 a.m. The Settlement Conference will be held at the 
Offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, 
NE, Washington, D.C. 20426, for the purpose of exploring settlement of 
the captioned proceedings.
    Any party as defined by 18 CFR 385.102(c), or any participant as 
defined in 18 CFR 385.102(b), is invited to attend. Persons wishing to 
become a party must move to intervene and receive intervenor status 
pursuant to the Commission's regulations (18 CFR 385.214).
    For additional information contact Thomas J. Burgess at (202) 208-
2058, Gray Denkinger at 208-2215, or Marcia C. Hooks at (202) 208-0993.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
