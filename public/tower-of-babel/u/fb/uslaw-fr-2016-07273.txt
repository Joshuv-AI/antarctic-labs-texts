SUMMARY: This notice corrects two dates that appear in the 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION section of a notice that published in the 
Federal Register on Wednesday, March 23, 2016 (81 FR 15555).
    On page 15555, column 3, line 24 of the notice, which reads 
``Wednesday, April, 13, 2015,'' is corrected to read, ``Wednesday, 
April 13, 2016.''
    On page 15555, column 3, line 35 of the notice, which reads 
``Thursday, September 3, 2015'' is corrected to read, ``Thursday, April 
14, 2016.''

Kristin Bail,
Acting Assistant Director, Resources and Planning.
