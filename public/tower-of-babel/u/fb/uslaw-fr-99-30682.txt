Revocation of Customs Broker License

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1641(g)(2)(c)), Part 111.53(b)(3)(c) and 
111.53(b)(3)(f), hereby revoke the following Customs broker licenses 
with prejudice.
