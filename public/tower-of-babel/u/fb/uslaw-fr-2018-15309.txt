FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 18-699; MB Docket No. 18-43; RM-11797]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Connerville, Oklahoma

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of The Chickasaw Nation, the Audio Division 
amends the FM Table of Allotments by allotting FM Channel 247A at 
Connerville, Oklahoma, as a Tribal Allotment and a first local Tribal-
owned service to the community. A staff engineering analysis indicates 
that Channel 247A can be allotted at Connerville, Oklahoma, as 
proposed, consistent with the minimum distance separation requirements 
of the Commission's rules with a site restriction 9.40 km (5.84 miles) 
southwest of the community. The reference coordinates are 34-25-00 NL 
and 96-43-53 WL.

DATES: Effective August 20, 2018.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Adrienne Y. Denysyk, Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2700.
