Agere Systems, Irwindale, California; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 27, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Agere Systems, Irwindale, California.
    The petition regarding the investigation has been deemed invalid. 
Petitions must be signed by three workers, a duly authorized 
representative of such workers, the employer of such workers, or an 
appropriate state official.
    The petition submitted in this case does not contain valid 
signatures of any of the above. Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 10th day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
