DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


President's Information Technology Advisory Committee (PITAC)

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This meeting is focused on the deliberation of PITAC's draft 
report on computational science. A small fraction of the meeting time 
may be allocated for other PITAC updates at the discretion of the co-
chairs and the designated Federal officer.

DATES: Wednesday, May 11, 2005, 5:30-7 p.m. eastern time.

ADDRESSES: By teleconference.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Information about participation by the 
public will be posted at PITAC's Web site (http://www.nitrd.gov/pitac) 
by April 26, 2005. The agenda for this meeting will be posted at this 
Web site when it becomes available. Meeting information may also be 
obtained by calling 703-292-4873 from Monday through Friday, 8 a.m.-5 
p.m. eastern time.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Alan Inouye at the National 
Coordination Office for Information Technology Research and Development 
at 703-292-4873 or by e-mail at [email protected].
