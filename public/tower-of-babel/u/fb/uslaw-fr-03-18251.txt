FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-2105; MB Docket No. 03-86 RM-10685]


Radio Broadcasting Services; George West, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In this document, the Commission allots Channel 250A at George 
West, Texas, in response to a petition filed by Charles Crawford. See 
68 FR 18178 (April 15, 2003). Channel 250A can be allotted at George 
West, Texas, with a site restriction 12 kilometers (7.4 miles) 
southwest of the community at coordinates 28-14-07 and 98-09-43. 
Although Mexican concurrence has been requested for the allotment of 
Channel 250A at George West, notification has not been received. 
Therefore, operation with the facilities specified for George West 
herein is subject to modification, suspension or termination without 
right to hearing, if found by the Commission to be necessary in order 
to conform to the 1992 USA-Mexico FM Broadcast Agreement or if 
specifically objected to by Mexico. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated. A filing window for channel 250A at George West will not 
be opened at this time. Instead, the issue of opening this allotment 
for auction will be addressed by the Commission in a subsequent order.

DATES: Effective August 18, 2003.
