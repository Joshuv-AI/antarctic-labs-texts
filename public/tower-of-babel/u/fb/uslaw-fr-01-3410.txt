FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-185, MM Docket No. 97-188, RM-9137]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Walnut Grove, MS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

[[Page 9676]]


ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Colon Johnston directed to the Report and Order in this proceeding to 
the extent of allotting Channel 244C2 to Walnut Grove, Mississippi. The 
Report and Order had dismissed this proposal. See 63 FR 26993, May 15, 
1998. The reference coordinates for the Channel 244C2 allotment at 
Walnut Grove, Mississippi, are 32-42-50 and 89-23-48. With this action, 
the proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 13, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2177.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Memorandum Opinion and Order in MM Docket No. 97-188. Adopted January 
24, 2001, and released January 26, 2001. The full text of this decision
