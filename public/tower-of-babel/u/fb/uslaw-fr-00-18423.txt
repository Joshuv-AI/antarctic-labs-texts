NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Notice of Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications and 
Amendments to Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards 
Considerations

    On June 28, 2000, the Federal Register published the Biweekly 
Notice of Applications and Amendments to Operating Licenses Involving 
No Significant Hazards Consideration. On page 39961, under Southern 
Nuclear Operating Company, Inc., et al., Docket Nos. 50-424 and 50-425, 
the Date of amendment request should have been March 6, 2000.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 14th day of July, 2000.


[[Page 45116]]


    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
John A. Zwolinski,
Director, Division of Licensing Project Management, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
