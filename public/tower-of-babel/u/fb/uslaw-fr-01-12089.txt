Radio Broadcasting Services; Northome, MN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a petition for rulemaking filed by 
PharrNorth Radio requesting the allotment of Channel 291A at Northome, 
Minnesota. See 66 FR 10266, February 14, 2001. PharrNorth withdrew its 
interest in an allotment at Northome, Minnesota, in accordance with 
Section 1.420(j) of the Commission's Rules. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

(202) 418-2180.
