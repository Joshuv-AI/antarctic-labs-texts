NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Business and Operations Advisory Committee; Meeting

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Business and Operations Advisory Committee (9556).
    Date/Time: March 26, 2002; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. (EST), March 27, 
2002; 8:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. (EST).
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Room 
1235,Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Louise McIntire, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230 (703) 292-8200.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice concerning issues related 
to the oversight, integrity, development and enhancement of NSF's 
business operations.
    Agenda:

March 26, 2002

    AM: Introductions and Updates--Office of Budget, Finance, and 
Award Management and Office of Information and Resource Management 
activities; President's Management Agenda.
    PM: Presentation and Discussion--Management Controls and Audit 
Findings.

March 27, 2002

    AM: Discussion--Meet with NSF Deputy Director; presentation by 
NSF ``customer'' panel.
    PM: Discussion--Planning for next meeting; feedback; other 
business.

    Dated: March 5,2002.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
