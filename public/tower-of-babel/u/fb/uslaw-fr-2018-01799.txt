Arizona Public Service Company Palo Verde Nuclear Generating 
Station, Units 1, 2, and 3

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Exemption; issuance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is issuing an 
exemption in response to a July 1, 2016, request, as supplemented by 
letter dated June 2, 2017, from Arizona Public Service Company (the 
licensee) in order to use Optimized ZIRLOTM fuel rod 
cladding material at Palo Verde Nuclear Generating Station, Units 1, 2, 
and 3.

DATES: The exemption was issued on January 23, 2018.
