DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Olympic Cross Cascade Pipeline Project, Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie 
National Forest, Snohomish, King, Kittitas, Grant, Adams, and Franklin 
Counties, Washington

AGENCY:  Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION:  Cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  On February 28, 1996, a Notice of Intent (NOI) to prepare an 
environmental impact statement for the Olympic Cross Cascade Pipeline 
Project, a new 230-mile pipeline from western Washington to 
southeastern Washington, was published in the Federal Register (61 FR 
7467). The proponent has withdrawn the proposal. When this project is 
again considered for implementation a new NOI will be filed. The 1996 
NOI is hereby rescinded.

Forest, Cle Elum Ranger District, 803 West Second Street, Cle Elum,
