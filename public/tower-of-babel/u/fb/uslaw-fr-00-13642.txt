DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Pension and Welfare Benefits Administration

[Exemption Application No. D-10654]


Withdrawal of Notice of Proposed Exemption Involving Fish Lake 
Beach, Inc. Profit Sharing Plan (the Plan); Located in Round Lake, 
Illinois

    In the Federal Register dated February 29, 2000 (65 FR 10826), the 
Department of Labor (the Department) published a notice of proposed 
exemption from the prohibited transaction restrictions of the Employee 
Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 and from certain taxes imposed 
by the Internal Revenue Code of 1986. The notice of proposed exemption 
concerned the prospective cash sale of a certain parcel of real 
property by the Plan to the trust of Emilie Keil, a party in interest 
with respect to the Plan.
    On April 7, 2000, the applicant informed the Department that it 
wished to withdraw the notice of proposed exemption.
    Accordingly, the notice of proposed exemption is hereby withdrawn.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 25th day of May, 2000.
Ivan L. Strasfeld,
Director of Exemption Determinations, Pension and Welfare Benefits 
Administration, Department of Labor.
