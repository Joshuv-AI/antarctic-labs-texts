Digital Flight Data Recorder Requirements--Changes to Recording 
Specifications and Additional Exceptions; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects amendment numbers assigned in the final 
rule published in the Federal Register on July 18, 2003. That action 
amended the flight data recorder regulations by expanding the recording 
specifications of certain data parameters for specified airplanes, and 
by adding aircraft models to the lists of aircraft excepted from the 
1997 regulations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: This correction is effective on February 11, 2004.
