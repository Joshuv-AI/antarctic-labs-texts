DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry


Meeting; Postponement

    Citizens Advisory Committee on Public Health Service (PHS) 
Activities and Research at Department of Energy (DOE) Sites: Oak Ridge 
Reservation Health Effects Subcommittee (ORRHES): the meeting 
originally planned for May 6, 2002, has been postponed until June 18, 
2002.
    The items originally scheduled for discussion on May 6th will be 
presented and discussed when the subcommittee meets in Oak Ridge on 
June 18, 2002.

[[Page 20791]]

of Health Assessment and Consultation, ATSDR, 1600 Clifton Road, NE., 
M/S E-54, Atlanta, Georgia 30333, telephone 1-888-42-ATSDR(28737), fax 
404/498-1744.

delegated the authority to sign Federal Register notices pertaining to 
announcements of meetings and other committee management activities, 
for both the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the Agency 
for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry.
