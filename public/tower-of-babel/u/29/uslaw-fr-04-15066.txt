DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

[Program Announcement 05003]


Tuberculosis Elimination and Laboratory; Notice of Availability 
of Funds--Amendment

    A notice announcing the availability of fiscal year (FY) 2004 funds 
for Tuberculosis Elimination and Laboratory was published in the 
Federal Register on May 27, 2004, Volume 69, Number 103, pages 30300-
30312. The notice is amended as follows: On page 30300, Column 1, 
``Application Deadline'', change deadline date to July 29, 2004. On 
page 30308, Column 3, ``Application Deadline'', change deadline date to 
July 29, 2004.

    Dated: June 28, 2004.
Alan Kotch,
Acting Director, Procurement and Grants Office, Centers for Disease 
Control and Prevention.
