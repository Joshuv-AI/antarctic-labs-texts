DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Yakima Training Center Cultural and Natural Resources Committee; 
Technical Committee

AGENCY: Headquarters, I Corps and Fort Lewis, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of Committee Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463) and Army Regulation 15-1, Committee 
Management, announcement is made of the following committee meeting:

    Name of the Committee: Yakima Training Center Cultural and 
Natural Resources Committee--Technical Committee.
    Date of the Meeting: November 19, 1994.
    Place: Yakima Training Center, Building 266, Yakima, Washington.
    Time: 1:00 p.m.
    Proposed Agenda: Cultural and Natural Resources Management Plan 
Development. All proceedings are open to the public. For further 
information contact Stephen Hart, Chief, Civil Law, (206) 967-4540.
Kenneth L. Denton,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
