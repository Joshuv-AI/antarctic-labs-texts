Correction to Rule Entitled: Implementation of the Wassenaar 
Arrangement List of Dual-Use Items Revisions: Computers; and Revisions 
to License Exception CTP

AGENCY: Bureau of Export Administration, Commerce.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 8, 2002 the Bureau of Export Administration (BXA) 
published a final rule revising the Commerce Control List to implement 
certain agreed changes in Category 4 (Computers) of the Wassenaar List 
of Dual-Use Goods and Technologies, specifically in the area of 
computers. This rule corrects an error in the rulemaking requirements 
section to indicate that this rule was determined by the Office of 
Management and Budget to be significant for purposes of Executive Order 
12866.

DATES: This rule is effective March 18, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharron Cook in the Office of Exporter 
Services, Bureau of Export Administration, at (202) 482-2440.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In rule FR Doc. 02-5563 published on March 
8, 2002, (67 FR 10611), BXA makes the following correction. On page 
10613, in the first column, under Rulemaking Requirements #1, revise 
the phrase ``not significant'' to read ``significant.''
