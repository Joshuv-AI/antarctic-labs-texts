SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3209]


Commonwealth of Pennsylvania; Amendment #1

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated September 17, 1999, the above-numbered Declaration is 
hereby amended to include Juniata County, Pennsylvania as a disaster 
area due to damages caused by severe storms and flooding that occurred 
on August 20-21, 1999.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous counties of Dauphin, Franklin, 
Huntingdon, Mifflin, Northumberland, Perry, and Snyder in the 
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania may be filed until the specified date at 
the previously designated location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is October 30, 1999 and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 1, 2000.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: September 21, 1999.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
