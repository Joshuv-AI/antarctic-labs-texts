DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

Office of Safe and Drug-Free Schools; Grant Competition To 
Prevent High-Risk Drinking or Violent Behavior Among College Students

ACTION: Correction; Notice correcting the Deadline dates.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: We correct the Deadline dates in the notice published on 
December 22, 2006 (71 FR 77004).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On December 22, 2006 we published a notice 
in the Federal Register inviting applications for the Grant Competition 
to Prevent High-Risk Drinking or Violent Behavior among College 
Students (71 FR 77004-77007). The Deadline dates in the notice were 
incorrect. The Deadline for Transmittal of Applications (as published 
on pages 77004 and 77005) is corrected to February 20, 2007, and the 
Deadline for Intergovernmental Review (as published on pages 77004 and 
77005) is corrected to April 20, 2007.
