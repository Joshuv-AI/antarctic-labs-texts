COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION


Order

    The Commission hereby (1) amends the charter of the Energy Markets 
Advisory Committee by renaming the committee the ``Energy and 
Environmental Markets Advisory Committee'' and substituting the 
attached ``Charter of the Energy and Environmental Markets Advisory 
Committee'' for the existing charter; (2) adopts the attached Findings 
in support of the charter amendment; (3) authorizes the Secretary to 
sign four official copies of the committee charter for filing; and (4) 
authorizes the filing of the amended charter with the Office of the 
Secretary; the Library of Congress; the Senate Committee on 
Agriculture, Nutrition and Forestry; and the House Committee on 
Agriculture.

    Dated: April 10, 2009.
David A. Stawick,
Secretary of the Commission.
