DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Meeting

April 29, 1998.
    Take notice that on May 28-29, 1998, the Commercial Practices 
Working Group (CPWG), will conduct its monthly meeting at the 
Commission's offices at 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426. 
The CPWG is a voluntary industry group with a diverse membership that 
has made recommendations to the Commission on the Open Access Same-time 
Information System (OASIS) and related matters. It is expected that the 
CPWG will discuss OASIS and reliability-related issues at the meetings. 
The meetings will be open to interested participants and the public.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
