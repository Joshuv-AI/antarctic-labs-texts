ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Indian Health Service published a 30 day Federal Register 
notice in the Federal Register (FR) on November 17, 2015 (80 FR 71813) 
to solicit comments from the general public on the information 
collection titled, ``Indian Health Service (IHS) Sharing What Works--
Best Practice, Promising Practice, and Local Effort (BPPPLE) Form,'' 
Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Control Number 0917-0034. The 
notice was submitted before the 60 day FR notice comment period for the 
same information collection ends on December 8, 2015. Therefore, the 
correct date for the deadline to submit comments regarding the 30 day 
FR notice is January 9, 2016.

[[Page 77647]]
