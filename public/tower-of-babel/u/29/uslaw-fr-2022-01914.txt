COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Connecticut Advisory Committee; 
Correction to Date

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; correction of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights is holding a briefing of the 
Connecticut Advisory Committee on Tuesday, February 15, 2022, at 4:00 
p.m. ET. This notice corrects the previous date of Monday, February 14, 
2022, to Tuesday, February 15, 2022, at 4:00 p.m. ET. The notice is in 
the Federal Register of Friday, January 21, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-
01119, in the second and third columns of page 3279.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Evelyn Bohor, (202) 381-8915, 
[email protected].
    CORRECTION: Date of Monday, February 14, 2022, at 4:00 p.m. ET to 
be replaced with meeting date of Tuesday, February 15, 2022, at 4:00 
p.m. ET.
