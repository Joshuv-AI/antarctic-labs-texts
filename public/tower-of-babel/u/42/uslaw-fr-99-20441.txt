SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The Trade Fair Certification (TFC) program is a service of the U.S. 
Department of Commerce (DOC) that provides DOC endorsement and support 
for private sector firms that organize high quality international trade 
fairs. The TFC program seeks to broaden the base of U.S. firms, 
particularly new-to-market companies by introducing them to key 
international trade fairs where they can achieve their export 
objectives. Those objectives include one or more of the following: 
direct sales, identification of local agents or distributors, market 
research and exposure, and joint venture licensing opportunities for 
their products and services. The objective of the application is to 
make a determination that we qualify the trade fair organizer to 
organize and manage U.S. exhibitions at a foreign trade show.
