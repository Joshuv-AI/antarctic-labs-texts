Marathon Electric, a Subsidiary of Regal-Beloit Corporation; 
Lima, OH; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 6, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by an IUE-CWA Region 7 official on behalf of workers of 
Marathon Electric, a subsidiary of Regal-Beloit Corporation, Lima, 
Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of October 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
