SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Augusta District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Augusta District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting at 9:00 a.m. on Monday, February 27, 
1995, at Key Bank Plaza Building, 284 Water Street, Augusta, Maine 
04330, to discuss such matters as may be presented by members, staff of 
the U.S. Small Business Administration, or other present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Roy Perry, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 155 Federal Street, 
Boston, Massachusetts 02110, (207) 622-8378.

    Dated: January 19, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
