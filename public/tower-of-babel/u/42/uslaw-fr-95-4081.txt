SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Public Meeting

    The National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board will 
hold a public meeting on March 7, 1995, from 9 AM through 4 PM, at U.S. 
Small Business Administration, Office of Small Business Development 
Centers Conference Room, 4th Floor, 409 3rd Street, SW., Washington, DC 
20416.
    The purpose of the meeting is to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by Advisory Board members, staff of the SBA, or others 
present.
    For further information, write or call Mary Ann Holl, SBA, 4th 
Floor, 409 3rd Street, SW., Washington, DC 20416, telephone 202/205-
7302.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Councils.
