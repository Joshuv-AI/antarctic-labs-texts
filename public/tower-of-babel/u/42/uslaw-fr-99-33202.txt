Public Land Order No. 7413; Withdrawal of Public Lands for the

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In Public Land Order No. 7413, 64 FR 54637-38, published 
February 20, 1997, as FR Doc. 99-26186, make the following corrections:
    On page 54638, paragraph 1, ``T. 31 N., R., 7 W., Sec. 4, NE\1/
4\NE\1/4\,'', is hereby corrected to read, ``T. 31 S., R., 7 W., Sec. 
4, NE\1/4\SE\1/4\''. ``Revested Oregon and California Railroad Grant 
Lands Island Creek Recreation Site, T. 31 N., R. 7 W.,'', is hereby 
corrected to read, ``Island Creek Recreation Site, T 31 S., R. 7 W.''. 
``T. 32 N., R. 7 W.,'', is hereby corrected to read, `` T. 32 S., R. 7 
W.,''. ``T. 30 S., R. 7 W., Sec. 5, SW\1/4\NE\1/4\NE\1/4\SW\1/4\,'', is 
hereby corrected to read, ``T. 30 S., R. 7 W., Sec. 5, SW\1/4\NE\1/
4\SW\1/4\,''.
    On page 54638, paragraph 2, ``T. 30 N., R. 7 W.,'', is hereby 
corrected to read, ``T. 30 S., R. 7 W.,''. Also, ``T. 31 S., R. 7 W., 
Sec. 4, that portion of the NE\1/4\SW\1/4\'', is hereby corrected to 
read, ``Iron Mountain Gold Panning Area, T. 31 S., R. 7 W., Sec. 4, 
that portion of the NE\1/4\SE\1/4\''.
Robert D. DeViney, Jr.,
Chief, Branch of Realty and Records Services, Oregon/Washington.
