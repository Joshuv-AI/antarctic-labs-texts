DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Notice of Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 21 and 22 March 1996.
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1700, 21 March 1996. 1000-1700, 22 March 
1996.
    Place: Pentagon-Washington, DC.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board (ASB) Summer Study on ``Technical 
Architecture C41'' will meet for briefings and discussion on the 
study subject. The meetings will be closed to the public in 
accordance with Section 552b(c) of title 5, U.S.C., specifically 
subparagraph (4) thereof, and Title 5, U.S.C. Appendix 2, subsection 
10(d). The proprietary matters to be discussed are so inextricably 
intertwined so as to preclude opening any portion of these meetings. 
For further information, please contact Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-
0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
