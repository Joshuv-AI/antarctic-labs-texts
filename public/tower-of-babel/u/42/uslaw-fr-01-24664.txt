Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Michigan

    The plat of the dependent resurvey of a portion of the 
subdivisional lines and the subdivision of sections 11, 14, and 15, 
Township 45 North, Range 19 West, Michigan Meridian, Michigan, will be 
officially filed in Eastern States, Springfield, Virginia at 7:30 a.m., 
on November 9, 2001.
    The survey was made at the request of the U.S. Forest Service.
    All inquiries or protests concerning the technical aspects of the 
survey must be sent to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor, Eastern States, 
Bureau of Land Management, 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 
22153, prior to 7:30 a.m., November 9, 2001.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the appropriate fee.

    Dated: September 10, 2001.
Stephen D. Douglas,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
