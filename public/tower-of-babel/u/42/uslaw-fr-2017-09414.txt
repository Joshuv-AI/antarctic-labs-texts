DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine Amended; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Library of Medicine Biomedical Library and Informatics Review 
Committee, June 15, 2017, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and June 16, 2017, 
9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., National Library of Medicine, Building 38, 
Lindberg Room, 2nd Floor, 8600 Rockville Pike, Bethesda, MD, 20892 
which was published in the Federal Register on February 27, 2017, 82 FR 
37, Page 11934.

[[Page 21827]]

    The second day of the Biomedical Library and Informatics Review 
Committee meeting, June 16, 2017, will now be held from 8:00 a.m. to 
6:00 p.m. instead of 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to 
the public.

    Dated: May 4, 2017.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
