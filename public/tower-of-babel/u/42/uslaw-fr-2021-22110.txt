Deletion of Items From September 30, 2021 Open Meeting

September 30, 2021.
    The following items have been adopted by the Commission and deleted 
from the list of items scheduled for consideration at the Thursday, 
September 30, 2021, Open Meeting. These items were previously listed in 
the Commission's Notice of Thursday, September 23, 2021.
