SUMMARY: In Public Land Order No. 7184, 61 FR 5719-5720, published 
February 14, 1996, as FR Doc. 96-3259, make the following correction:
    On page 5719, third column, paragraph 5, under T. 33 S., R. 13 W., 
which reads ``Secs. 13 to 24, inclusive, secs. 29 and 30,'' is hereby 
corrected to read ``Secs. 13 to 20, inclusive, secs. 22, 23, 24, 29, 
and 30.''
