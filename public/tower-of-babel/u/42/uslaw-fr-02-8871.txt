DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


National Security Education Board Group of Advisors Meeting

AGENCY: National Defense University, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of a

[[Page 17974]]

forthcoming meeting of the National Security Education Board Group of 
Advisors. The purpose of the meeting is to review and make 
recommendations to the Board concerning requirements established by the 
David L. Boren National Security Education Act, Title VIII of Public 
Law 102-183, as amended.

DATES: May 6-7, 2002. The meeting will be held on May 6, from 9 am 
until 5 pm and continue on May 7, from 9 am until 12 pm.

ADDRESSES: Northwestern University, Norris University Center, 1999 
South Campus Drive, Evanston, Illinois 60208-2500.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Edmond J. Collier, Director for 
Programs, National Security Education Program, 1101 Wilson Boulevard, 
Suite 1210, Rossyln P.O. Box 20010, Arlington, Virginia 22209-2248; 
(703) 696-1991. Electronic mail address: [email protected]

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The National Security Education Board Group 
of Advisors meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: April 5, 2002.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
