Notice of Meeting of Southeastern Oregon Resource Advisory 
Council

AGENCY: Vale District, Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Meeting of Southeastern Oregon Resource Advisory Council, 
Rangeland Health Standards and Guides subgroup: teleconference, May 27, 
1997.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A meeting of the Southeastern Oregon Resource Advisory 
Council's Rangeland Health Standards and Guides subgroup will be held 
by teleconference on May 27, 1997 from 8:00 to 10:00 p.m. (PDT). The 
teleconference is open to the public; access may be obtained at the BLM 
Office, BLM, 100 Oregon Street, Vale, Oregon.
    The Subcommittee will discuss standards and guidelines for 
livestock grazing on public lands.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Jonne Hower, Bureau of Land Management, Vale District, 100 Oregon 
Street, Vale, Oregon 97918 (Telephone (541) 473-3144.
