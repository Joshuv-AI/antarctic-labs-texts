CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, Washington, DC 20207.

TIME AND DATE: Monday, October 15, 2001, 2 p.m.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the Public.

MATTER TO BE CONSIDERED:   

Bed Rails

    The staff will brief the Commission on options for Commission 
action to address hazards associated with portable bed rails.

call (301) 504-0709.

0800.
