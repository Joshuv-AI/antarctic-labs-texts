DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Human Genome Research Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Human Genome Research Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 22, 
2011, 12 p.m. to November 22, 2011, 1 p.m., NHGRI Fishers Lane Office, 
5635 Fishers Lane, 4076, Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in the 
Federal Register on October 27, 2011, 76FRN66731.
    The meeting notice was amended to change the meeting date and time 
from November 22, 2011 to December 1, 2011 from 12 p.m. to 3 p.m. The 
meeting is closed to the public.
