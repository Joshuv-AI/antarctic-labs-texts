DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board, Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:
    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date(s) of Meeting: 18 & 19 March 2002.
    Time(s) of Meeting: 0800-1700, 18 March 2002. 0800-1700, 19 March 
2002.
    Place: Logistics Management Institute.
    1. Agenda: The Logistics Panel of the Army Science Board FY02 
Summer Study on ``Affordability of the Objective Force'' is holding a 
meeting on 18-19 March 2002. The meeting will be held at the Logistics 
Management Institute. The meeting will begin at 0800hrs on the 18th and 
will end at approximately 1700hrs on the 19th. For further information, 
please contact Mike Hendricks--703-617-7048 or e-mail. 
[email protected].

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
