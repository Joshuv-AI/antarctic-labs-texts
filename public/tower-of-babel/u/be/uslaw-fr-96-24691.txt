FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
[Report No. 2155]


Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarifications of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

September 23, 1996.
    A petition for reconsideration and clarification has been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of 
this document is available for viewing and copying in Room 239, 1919 M 
Street, NW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's 
copy contractor, ITS, Inc., (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to this 
petition must be filed October 11, 1996. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of the 
Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition must be 
filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.

    Subject: Implementation of Section 302 of the Telecommunications 
Act of 1996--Open Video Systems. (CS Docket No. 96-46)
    Number of Petitions Filed: 1.
