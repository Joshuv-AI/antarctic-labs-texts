FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Notices of Proposals to Engage in Permissible Nonbanking 
Activities or to Acquire Companies that are Engaged in Permissible 
Nonbanking Activites; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 05-18466) published on pages 
54746-54747 of the issue for Friday, September 16, 2005.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of New York heading, the entry for 
Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Sydney, Australia, is revised to read 
as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of New York (Jay Bernstein, Bank 
Supervision Officer) 33 Liberty Street, New York, New York 10045-0001:
    1. Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Sydney, Australia; to engage de 
novo through its subsidiary, CommSec LLC, New York, New York, in 
securities brokerage, private placement services, and other 
transactional services, pursuant to sections 225.28(b)(7)(i), 
(b)(7)(iii), and (b)(7)(v) of Regulation Y.
    Comments on this application must be received by October 3, 2005.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System,September 19, 
2005.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
