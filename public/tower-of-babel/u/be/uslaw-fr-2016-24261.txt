POSTAL SERVICE


Temporary Emergency Committee of the Board of Governors; Sunshine 
Act Meeting

DATES AND TIMES:  Tuesday, October 11, 2016, at 4:00 p.m.

PLACE:  Teleconference.

STATUS:  Closed.

Tuesday, October 11, 2016, at 4:00 p.m.

2. Financial Matters.
3. Pricing.

20260-1000. Telephone: (202) 268-4800.
