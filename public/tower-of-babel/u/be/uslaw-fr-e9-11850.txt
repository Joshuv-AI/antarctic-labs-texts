General Motors Grand Rapids Stamping, Metal Fabricating Division, 
Wyoming, MI; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 9, 2009, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official and by United Auto Workers Local 
730 on behalf of workers at General Motors Grand Rapids Stamping, Metal 
Fabricating Division, Wyoming, Michigan.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 29th day of April 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
