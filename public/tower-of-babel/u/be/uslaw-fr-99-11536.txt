Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Quarterly Performance Report, ORR-6.
    OMB No.: 0970-0036.
    Description: Data gathered from the Quarterly Performance Report 
(Form ORR-6) are used by ORR to estimate the number of months of 
Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA) and Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA) that 
ORR can provide based on appropriations; to determine priorities; and 
standards, budget requests, and assistance policies; to analyze data on 
service caseloads and program outcomes in order to monitor performance; 
and to compute refuge medical assistance (RMA) utilization rates.
    Respondents: State, Local or Tribal Governments.
