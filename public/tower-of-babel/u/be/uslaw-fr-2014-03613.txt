SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management, Oregon/Washington, published a 
document in the Federal Register on February 6, 2014, regarding a 
public meeting of the Coos Bay Resource Advisory Council which was 
scheduled for March 13, 2014. The meeting has been cancelled.
