DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Presentation

April 28, 1998.
    Take notice that on Tuesday, May 5, 1998, a presentation will be 
made by representatives of Morgan Stanley Co., Inc. to the 
Commissioners and staff. The subject of the presentation is Locational 
Pricing and the Convergence of Physical and Financial Markets in the 
Electricity and Natural Gas Industries.
    The presentation will take place at 3 p.m. in Room 3M-3 at the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426. The meeting is open to the public. Questions 
concerning the presentation should be directed to Kay Morice, 202-208-
0507.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
