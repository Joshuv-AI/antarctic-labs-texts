DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-36,195]


Jahmpasa USA, Incorporated, Vass, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 10, 1999 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers and former workers at Jahmpasa USA, 
Incorporated, located in Vass, North Carolina (TA-W-36,195).
    The Department of Labor has determined that the petitioning group 
of workers are covered by an existing certification, as amended (TA-W-
34,840A). Consequently, further investigation in this matter would 
serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 23rd day of July, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
