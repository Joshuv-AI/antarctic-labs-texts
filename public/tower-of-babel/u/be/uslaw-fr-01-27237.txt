Hayward Pool Products, Inc. a/k/a Hayward Industries, Inc., Kings 
Mountain, NC; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 17, 2001, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by the company on behalf of its workers at Hayward Pool 
Products, Inc., a/k/a Hayward Industries, Inc., Kings Mountain, North 
Carolina. The workers produce products related to the swimming pool 
industry, i.e. filters, skimmers, spare parts, etc.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, 
and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 15th day of October, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
