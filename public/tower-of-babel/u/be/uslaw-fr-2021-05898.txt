FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD


Notice of Board Meeting; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FRTIB published a document in the Federal Register of 
March 16, 2021, concerning a notice of its March 2021 Board Meeting. 
The agenda for that meeting has since been updated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kimberly Weaver, Director, Office of 
External Affairs, (202) 942-1640.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of March 16, 2021, in FR Doc 2021-05465, on 
page 14629, remove the following language regarding the closed session 
of the Board Meeting:

Closed Session

    1. Information covered under 5 U.S.C. 552b (c)(4).''

    Dated: March 16, 2021.
Dharmesh Vashee,
Acting General Counsel.
