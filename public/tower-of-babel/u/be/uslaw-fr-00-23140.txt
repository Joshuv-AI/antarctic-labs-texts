DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Information Collection To Be Submitted to the Office of 
Management and Budget (OMB) for Approval Under the Paperwork Reduction 
Act (PRA)

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Service) published an 
Information Collection Notice on September 5, 2000 (65 FR 53737), for 
the Federal Aid Grants Application Booklet. It stated in ``Action'' 
that it was for an Information Collection Renewal, it is a New 
Information Collection, not a renewal.

    Dated: September 5, 2000.
Rebecca A. Mullin,
Fish and Wildlife Service Information Collection Officer.
