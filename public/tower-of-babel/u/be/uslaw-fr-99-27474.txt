SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region V Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Wisconsin State Advisory 
Council, located in the geographical area of Milwaukee, Wisconsin, will 
hold a public meeting from 12 p.m. to 1 p.m., October 21, 1999 at Metro 
Milwaukee Area Chamber (MMAC) Association of Commerce Building; 756 
North Milwaukee Street, Fourth Floor, Milwaukee, Wisconsin to discuss 
such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Yolanda Lassiter, U.S. Small 
Business

Administration, 310 W. Wisconsin Ave., Milwaukee, Wisconsin 53203; fax 
(414) 297-3928.
