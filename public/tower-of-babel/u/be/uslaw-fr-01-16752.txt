DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Notice of Meeting of the Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and 
Availability

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, HHS.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and Availability will meet 
on Friday August 24, 2001 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. The meeting will take 
place at the Hyatt Regency Hotel on Capitol Hill, 400 New Jersey Ave., 
NW., Washington, DC 20001. The meeting will be entirely open to the 
public.
    The purpose of this meeting will be to present the Department's 
current and future plans to monitor supply and demand for blood and 
plasma products, and to solicit public comment on these plans.
    Public comment will be limited to five minutes per speaker. Those 
who wish to have printed material distributed to Advisory Committee 
members should submit thirty (30) copies to the Executive Secretary 
prior to close of business August 9, 2001. In addition, anyone planning 
to comment on either item is encouraged to contact the Executive 
Secretary at her/his earliest convenience.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: CAPT Lawrence C. McMurtry, Deputy 
Executive Secretary, Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and 
Availability, Department of Health and Human Services, Office of Public 
Health and Science, 200 Independence Ave., SW., Room 736-E, Washington, 
DC 20201. Phone (202) 690-5558, FAX (202) 690-7560, e-mail 
[email protected].

Lawrence C. McMurtry,
Deputy Executive Secretary, Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and 
Availability.
