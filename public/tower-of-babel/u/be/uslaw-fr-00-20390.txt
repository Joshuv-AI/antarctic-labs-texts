SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region IV Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

     The Georgia District Advisory Council, Regulatory Fairness Board 
will hold a public hearing on September 15, 2000, at 9 am located at 
the Georgia District Office Conference Room, at 233 Peachtree Street, 
NE Suite 1900, Atlanta, Georgia to receive comments and testimony from 
small businesses and representatives of trade associations concerning 
federal regulatory enforcement or compliance activities. These 
transcripts are subject only to limited review by the National 
Ombudsman.
    For further information, call Charles E. Anderson, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration at (404) 331-0266.

Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administrator/Public Liaison.
