FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD

Sunshine Act Meeting


Announcing an Open Meeting of the Board

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 A.M., December 14, 1999.

PLACE: Board Room, Second Floor, Federal Housing Finance Board, 1777 F 
Street, NW, Washington, DC 20006.

STATUS: The entire meeting will be open to the public.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED DURING PORTIONS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC:
     Proposed Rule: Restructuring of the Office of Finance
     Proposed Rule: Powers and Responsibilities of Federal Home 
Loan Bank Boards of Directors and Senior Management
     Interim Final Rule: Devolution of Corporate Governance 
Responsibilities

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Elaine L. Baker, Secretary to the 
Board, (202) 408-2837.
William W. Ginsberg,
Managing Director.
