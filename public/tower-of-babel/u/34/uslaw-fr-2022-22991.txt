North Pacific Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The North Pacific Fishery Management Council (Council) Bering 
Sea Fishery Ecosystem Plan Local Knowledge, Traditional Knowledge, and 
Subsistence Taskforce (LKTKS) meeting was to be held November 1, 2022 
through November 2, 2022, from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., Alaska Time. See 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting notice published on (87 FR 
60125). This notice announces that the meeting is cancelled and will be 
rescheduled and published in the Federal Register at a later date.
    Authority: 16 U.S.C. 1801 et seq.

    Dated: October 18, 2022.
Rey Israel Marquez,
Acting Deputy Director, Office of Sustainable Fisheries, National 
Marine Fisheries Service.
