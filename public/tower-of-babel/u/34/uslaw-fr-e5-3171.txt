Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-57,078]


Allied Bias Products; Jersey City, NJ; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 29, 2005, in response to a 
petition filed by a state agency representative on behalf of workers at 
Allied Bias Products, Jersey City, New Jersey.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation would serve no purpose the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 2nd day of June, 2005.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
