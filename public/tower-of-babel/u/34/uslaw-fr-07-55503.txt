SOCIAL SECURITY ADMINISTRATION

20 CFR Part 416


Revised Medical Criteria for Determination of Disability, 
Musculoskeletal System and Related Criteria

CFR Correction

    In Title 20 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 400 to 499, 
revised as of April 1, 2006, on page 948, Sec.  416.933 is corrected by 
adding a sentence after the second sentence to read as follows:


Sec.  416.933  How we make a finding of presumptive disability or 
presumptive blindness.

    * * * For other impairments, a finding of disability or blindness 
must be based on medical evidence or other information that, though not 
sufficient for a formal determination of disability or blindness, is 
sufficient for us to find that there is a high degree of probability 
that you are disabled or blind. * * *
