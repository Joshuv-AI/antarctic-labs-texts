DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Armed Forces Epidemiological Board (AFEB); Meeting

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of Pub. L. 92-463, The 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, this announces the forthcoming AFEB 
subcommittee meeting. This Board will meet form 0730-1600 on Wednesday, 
August 18, 1999. The purpose of the meeting is to review the Rand 
Report on Pyridostigmine Bromide. The meeting location will be at the 
Uniformed Services University of Health Services (USUHS), Bethesda, 
Maryland.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: COL Benedict Diniega, AFEB Executive 
Secretary, Armed Forces Epidemiological Board, Skyline Six, 5109 
Leesburg Pike, Room 682, Falls Church, Virginia 22041-3258, (703) 681-
8012/4.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.
Gregory D. Showalter,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
