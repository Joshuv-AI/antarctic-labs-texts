DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR


Minerals Management Service

AGENCY: Minerals Management Service, Interior.

ACTION: Availability of Revised Outer Continental Shelf (OCS) Official 
Protraction Diagram.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that effective with this publication, 
the following NAD 27-based OCS Official Protraction Diagram last 
revised on the date indicated, is on file and available for information 
only, in the Gulf of Mexico OCS Regional Office, New Orleans, 
Louisiana. In accordance with Title 43, Code of Federal Regulations, 
this diagram is the basic record for the description of minerals and 
oil and gas lease sales in the geographic area it represents.
