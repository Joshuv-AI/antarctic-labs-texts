Application for Conveyance of Land, Mohave County, AZ; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This correction document will open 400 acres in Mohave County 
to application under the public land laws and location and entry under 
the mining and mineral leasing laws and terminate the classification. 
It will also classify and segregate 270.17 acres in Mohave County from 
all forms of appropriation under the public land laws, including the 
general mining and mineral leasing laws, except for conveyance under 
the R&PP Act, as amended.

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 15, 1995.
