CFR Correction

    In Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 60 to 139, 
revised as of January 1, 2004, on page 474, Sec.  121.385 is corrected 
by adding paragraph (d) to read as follows:


Sec.  121.385  Composition of flight crew.

* * * * *
    (d) On each flight requiring a flight engineer at least one flight 
crewmember, other than the flight engineer, must be qualified to 
provide emergency performance of the flight engineer's functions for 
the safe completion of the flight if the flight engineer becomes ill or 
is otherwise incapacitated. A pilot need not hold a flight engineer's 
certificate to perform the flight engineer's functions in such a 
situation.
