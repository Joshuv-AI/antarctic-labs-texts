FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 08-1736; MB Docket No. 08-156; RM-11480]


Television Broadcasting Services; La Crosse, WI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a channel substitution 
proposed by WXOW-WQOW (``WXOW-WQOW''), the permittee of WXOW-DT, DTV 
channel 14, La Crosse, Wisconsin. WXOW-WQOW requests the substitution 
of DTV channel 48 for channel 14 at La Crosse.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 8, 2008, and reply 
comments on or before September 22, 2008.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Office of the Secretary, 
445 12th Street, SW., TW-A325, Washington, DC 20554. In addition to 
filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should serve counsel 
for petitioner as follows: Timothy J. Cooney, Esq., Wilkinson Barker 
Knauer LLP, 2300 N Street, NW., Suite 700, Washington, DC 20037.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce Bernstein, 
[email protected], Media Bureau, (202) 418-1600.
