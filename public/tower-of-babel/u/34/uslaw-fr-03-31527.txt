Advanced Design and Knits, Inc., Copiague, NY; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 23, 2003 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official, and ex-worker on behalf of 
workers at Advanced Design and Knits, Inc. Copiague, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 16th day of October, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
