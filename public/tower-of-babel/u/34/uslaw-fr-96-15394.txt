COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Agenda and Notice of Public Meeting Cancellation of the Alabama 
Advisory Committee

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the rules and 
regulations of the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights, that a meeting of 
the Alabama Advisory Committee to the Commission which was to have 
convened at 6:00 p.m. and adjourned at 9:00 p.m. on Thursday, June 27, 
1996, at the Radisson Hotel, 808 South 20th, Birmingham, Alabama, has 
been canceled.
    The original notice for the meeting was announced in the Federal 
Register on June 6, 1996, FR Doc. 96-14211, 61 FR 28837.
    Persons desiring additional information should contact Melvin L. 
Jenkins, Director of the Central Regional Office, 913-551-1400 (TDD 
913-551-1414).

    Dated at Washington, DC, June 12, 1996.
Carol-Lee Hurley,
Chief, Regional Programs Coordination Unit.
