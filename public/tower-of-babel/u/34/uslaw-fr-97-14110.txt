ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

    By a vote of 5-0 on May 12, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and 10 CFR Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Meeting with Foreign Dignitaries'' (Closed--Ex. 1) be held on May 12, 
and on less than one week's notice to the public.
    By a vote of 5-0 on May 15, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and 10 CFR Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Classified Security Briefing'' (Closed--Ex. 1) be held on May 15, and 
on less than one week's notice to the public.
    By a vote of 4-0 on May 20, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and 10 CFR Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Ralph L. Tetrick, Presiding Officer's Initial Decision 
and Memorandum and Order Denying Reconsideration and Stay, LBP-97-2 and 
LBP-97-6'' be held on May 20, and on less than one week's notice to the 
public.
    By a vote of 4-0 on May 21, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and 10 CFR Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Final Rule on Radiological Criteria for License 
Termination'' be held on May 21, and on less than one week's notice to 
the public.
