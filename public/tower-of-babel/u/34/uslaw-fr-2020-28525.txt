DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Basic 
Neuroscience of Aging Review Committee, January 19, 2021, 11:00 a.m. to 
January 21, 2021, 04:00 p.m., National Institute on Aging, Gateway 
Building, 7201 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published 
in the Federal Register on November 25, 2020, 85 FR 75345.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date of the meeting 
from January 20-21 to January 19-21, 2021. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: December 18, 2020.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
