DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-70,532]


VWR International, LLC, Bridgeport, NJ; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated in response to a petition filed on May 26, 
2009 by a one stop operator on behalf of workers of VWR International, 
LLC., Bridgeport, New Jersey.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, (TA-W-70,200) which expires on July 2, 2011. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 25th day of August 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
