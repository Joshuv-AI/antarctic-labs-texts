DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP97-406-000]


CNG Transmission Corporation; Notice of Technical Conference

October 10, 1997.
    In the Commission's order issued July 31, 1997, the Commission held 
that, if necessary, the Staff may convene a technical conference to 
address issues raised by comments on CNG's supplemental filing 
concerning changes to its terms and conditions of service.
    Take notice that the technical conference will be held on Friday, 
October 31, 1997 at 9:00 a.m., in a room to be designated at the 
offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, 
N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426. All interested parties and Staff are 
permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
