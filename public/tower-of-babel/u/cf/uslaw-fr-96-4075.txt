DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Notice of Meeting Cancellation

AGENCY: Lower Snake River District, Bureau of Land Management, 
Interior.

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Lower Snake River District Resource Advisory Council has 
cancelled its meeting scheduled for February 24, 1996 at the Idaho 
State Office of the Bureau of Land Management, 3380 Americana Terrace, 
Boise, Idaho 83706. The meeting has not been rescheduled.
