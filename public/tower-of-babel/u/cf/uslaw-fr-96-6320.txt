SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[License No. 03/03-0179]


Enterprise Venture Capital Corporation of Pennsylvania; Notice of 
Surrender of License

    Notice is hereby given that Enterprise Venture Capital Corporation 
of Pennsylvania, 111 Market Street, Johnstown, Pennsylvania 15901 has 
surrendered its license to operate as a small business investment 
company under the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, as amended 
(Act). Enterprise Capital Corporation was licensed by the Small 
Business Administration on September 11, 1985.
    Under the authority vested by the Act and pursuant to the 
Regulations promulgated thereunder, the surrender of the license was 
accepted on March 1, 1996, and accordingly, all rights, privileges, and 
franchises derived therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Companies).

    Dated: March 11, 1996.
Don A. Christensen,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
