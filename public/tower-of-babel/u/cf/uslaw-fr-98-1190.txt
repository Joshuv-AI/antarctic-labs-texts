POSTAL SERVICE


Sunshine Act Meeting, Board of Governors; Notification of Items 
Added to Meeting Agenda

DATE OF MEETING: January 5, 1998.

STATUS: Closed.

PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: 62 FR 66884, December 22, 1997.

CHANGE: At its meeting on January 5, 1998, the Board of Governors of 
the United States Postal Service voted unanimously to add two items to 
the agenda of its closed meeting held on that date:
    1. Performance Measurement.
    2. Facilities Redevelopment Project.
