DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL16-51-000]


Beaver Dam Energy LLC; Notice of Institution of Section 206 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On April 22, 2016, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL16-51-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2012), instituting an investigation into the justness and 
reasonableness of Beaver Dam Energy LLC's reactive power rate schedule. 
Beaver Dam Energy LLC, 155 FERC ] 61,086 (2016).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL16-51-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: April 22, 2016.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
