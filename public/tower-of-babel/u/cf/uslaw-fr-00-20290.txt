Notice of Proposed Withdrawal Correction, Oregon

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects an error in the legal description in the 
notice of proposed withdrawal published in 65 FR page 38849, dated June 
22, 2000, FR Doc. 00-15781. The land described as the W\1/2\ of sec. 
36, T. 28 S., R. 32 E., Willamette Meridian, should read, W\1/2\ of 
sec. 16, T. 28 S., R. 32 E., Willamette Meridian.

    Dated: August 2, 2000.

Chief, Branch Realty and Records Services.
