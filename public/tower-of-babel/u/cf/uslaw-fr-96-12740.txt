FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Request for Additional Information

    Agreement No.: 202-010424-032.
    Title: United States Atlantic and Gulf Hispaniola Steamship Freight 
Association.
    Parties: Crowley American Transport, Inc., NPR, Inc. d/d/a 
Navieras, A.P. Moller-Maersk Line, Sea-Land Service, Inc.
    Synopsis: Notice is hereby given that the Federal Maritime 
Commission pursuant to section 6(d) of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 
U.S.C. app. 1701-1720) has requested additional information from the 
parties to the Agreement in order to complete the statutory review of 
Agreement No. 202-010424-032 as required by the Act. This action 
extends the review period as provided in section 6(c) the Act.

    Dated: May 16, 1996.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
