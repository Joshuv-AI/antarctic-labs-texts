Forsyth Sales Company Greensboro, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
Section 250(a), Subchapter D, Chapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 USC 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
September 5, 1997 in response to a petition filed on behalf of workers 
at the Forsyth Sales Company, Greensboro, North Carolina.
    The petitioner, who was also an official of Forsyth Sales Company, 
was not responsive to requests by the Department for information 
necessary for the completion of the investigation. Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C., this 9th day of March 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
