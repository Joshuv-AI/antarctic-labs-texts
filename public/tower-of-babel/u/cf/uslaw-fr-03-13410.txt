Tyson Food, Inc., Berlin, MD; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 6, 2003 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Tyson 
Food, Inc., Berlin, Maryland.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of May, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
