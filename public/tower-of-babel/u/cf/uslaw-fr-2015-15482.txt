Designation of Areas for Air Quality Planning Purposes

CFR Correction

    In Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 81 to 84, 
revised as of July 1, 2014, on page 150, in Sec.  81.305, in the table 
entitled ``California--NO2 (2010 1-Hour Standard)'', for the 
entry ``Sacramento County'', the date in the second column is removed 
and the entry in the third column is corrected to read 
``Unclassifiable/Attainment''.
