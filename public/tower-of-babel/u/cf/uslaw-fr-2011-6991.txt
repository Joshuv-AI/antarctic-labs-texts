DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children And Families


Announcement of an Award

AGENCY: Office of Policy, Research and Evaluation, ACF, HHS.

ACTION: The Administration for Children and Families (ACF) announces 
the award of a cooperative agreement to the Congressional Hunger Center 
in Washington, DC.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    C.F.D.A. Number: 93.647.

    Statutory Authority:  The award is authorized under Section 1110 
of the Social Security Act, 42 U.S.C. 613.

SUMMARY: The Administration for Children and Families (ACF), Office of 
the Deputy Assistant Secretary and Inter-Departmental Liaison for Early 
Childhood Development announces the award of a cooperative agreement 
with the Congressional Hunger Center (CHC) in Washington, DC, to work 
with ACF programs on hunger and obesity issues for young children. An 
award in the amount of $3,000 has been made to the CHC.

DATES: March 15, 2011-August 1, 2011.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: George Askew, MD, FAAP, Senior Policy
