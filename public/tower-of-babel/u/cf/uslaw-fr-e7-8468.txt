Precision Technologies Incorporated; Reno, PA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 3, 2007 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Precision 
Technologies Incorporated, Reno, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of April 2007.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
