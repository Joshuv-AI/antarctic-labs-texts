DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Notice Correction; A Multi-Center International Hospital-Based 
Case-Control Study of Lymphoma in Asia (AsiaLymph) (NCI)

    The Federal Register notice published on February 24, 2012 (77 FR 
11136) announcing the submission to OMB of the project titled, ``A 
multi-center international hospital-based case-control study of 
lymphoma in Asia (AsiaLymph) (NCI)'' was submitted with an error. The 
``Type of Information Collection Request'' was incorrectly listed as an 
Emergency. This submission should be considered a new submission.

    Dated: February 24, 2012.
Vivian Horovitch-Kelley,
NCI Project Clearance Liaison, National Institutes of Health.
