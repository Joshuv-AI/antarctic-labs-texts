Safety Standards for Cranes and Derricks

AGENCY: Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), U.S. 
Department of Labor.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of March 29, 30, and 31, 2004, 
Negotiated Rulemaking Committee meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) 
announces the cancellation of the ninth meeting of the Crane and 
Derrick Negotiated Rulemaking Advisory Committee (C-DAC) previously 
scheduled for March 29, 30, and 31, 2004. The next C-DAC meeting will 
be held May 2004. A Federal Register notice specifying the exact dates 
and times for this meeting will be published at a later time.

John L. Henshaw,
Assistant Secretary of Labor for Occupational Safety and Health.
