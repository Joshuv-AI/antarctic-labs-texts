DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. TM95-2-43-000]

 

Williams Natural Gas Company; Notice of Proposed Changes in FERC 
Gas Tariff

December 5, 1994.
    Take notice that on December 1, 1995, Williams Natural Gas Company 
(WNG), tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, the 
following tariff sheets. The proposed effective date of these tariff 
sheets is January 1, 1995.

Second Revised Volume No. 1
Sixth Revised Sheet Nos. 6 and 6A
Original Volume No. 2
First Revised Sheet No. 362

    WNG states that these tariff sheets are being filed pursuant to 
Article 13 of the General Terms and Conditions of its FERC Gas Tariff 
to reflect revised fuel and loss reimbursement percentages. The 
percentages are based on actuals for the twelve months ended September 
30, 1994.
    WNG states that a copy of its filing was served on all 
jurisdictional customers and interested state commissions.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426, in accordance with Secs. 385.214 and 385.211 of the Commission's 
Rules and Regulations. All such motions or protests should be filed on 
or before December 12, 1994. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person 
wishing to become a party to the proceeding must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
