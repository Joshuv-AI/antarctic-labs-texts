Radio Broadcasting Services; Jasper, AR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division dismisses a petition for rule making filed 
by JEM Broadcasting Company, Inc., requesting the allotment of Channel 
245A to Jasper, Arkansas, as that community's first local aural 
transmission service. See 67 FR 69703, November 19, 2002. JEM 
Broadcasting Company, Inc, or no other party, filed comments in support 
of the allotment of Channel 245A to Jasper, Arkansas. It is the 
Commission's policy to refrain from making a new allotment to a 
community absent an expression of interest.
