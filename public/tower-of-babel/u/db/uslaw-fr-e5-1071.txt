Equitrans, L.P.; Notice of Technical Conference

March 8, 2005.
    Take a notice that the Commission will convene a technical 
conference on Tuesday, March 22, 2005, at 10 a.m., in a room to be 
designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street, NE., Washington DC 20426.
    The purpose of the Commission is to discuss the issues raised by 
Equitrans' proposed Rate Schedule Appalachian Gathering Service (AGS), 
and the deletion of Rate Schedules Interruptible Gathering Service 
(IGS) and Appalachian Pooling Service (APS) and Equitrans' proposal's 
compliance with Order Nos. 637 and 587, as well as any other non-rate 
issues the parties raised with respect to Rate Schedule AGS. The 
Commission directed its staff to convene this technical conference in a 
February 28, 2005 Order establishing a technical conference.\1\
