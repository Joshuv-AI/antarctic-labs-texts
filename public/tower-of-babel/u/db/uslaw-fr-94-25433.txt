DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP94-421-001]

 

National Fuel Gas Supply Corporation; Tariff Filing

October 7, 1994.
    Take notice that on October 4, 1994, National Fuel Gas Supply 
Corporation (National) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas 
Tariff, Third Revised Volume No. 1, Fourth Revised Sheet No. 236, with 
a proposed effective date of November 1, 1994.
    National states that this sheet was inadvertently omitted from its 
General Rate Filing made on September 30, 1994.
    National further states that copies of this filing were served upon 
the company's jurisdictional customers and the Regulatory Commission's 
of the States of New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Massachusetts, 
and New Jersey.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol 
Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Rule 211 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211). All such 
protest should be filed on or before October 7, 1994. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
