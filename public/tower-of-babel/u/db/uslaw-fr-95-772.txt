DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Army
Corps of Engineers


Availability of Guidance on Design-Build for Military 
Construction

AGENCY: Army Corps of Engineers, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Interested individuals may obtain copies of the Design-Build 
Instructions (DBI) For Military Construction, dated 29 October 1994. 
The purpose of the DBI is to serve as a practical guide for U.S. Army 
Corps of Engineers offices to consistently and efficiently plan, 
develop, and execute design-build contracts.

ADDRESSES: Copies of the DBI may be obtained from two sources; printed 
copies (as quantities last) from the Huntsville Division Engineer 
Office (CEHND-ED-ES), P.O. Box 1600, Huntsville, AL 35807-4301; or 
automated copies on the compact disk (CD-ROM), January 1995 issue of 
the Construction Criteria Base (CCB), from the National Institute of 
Building Sciences (NIBS), 1201 L Street, N.W. Suite 400, Washington, 
D.C. 20005-4024, (202) 289-7800, FAX (202) 289-1092. Written 
suggestions for improving the DBI may be submitted before 30 June 1995 
to HQUSACE, ATTN: CEMP-EA, 20 Massachusetts Avenue NW., Washington, 
D.C. 20314-1000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. Daniel W. Duncan, Architectural and Planning Branch, Directorate of 
Military Programs, (202) 272-0437.
Kenneth L. Denton,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
