DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Office of the Director, National Institutes of Health; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Secretary's Advisory Committee on Genetics, Health, and Society, 
October 8, 2009, 8 a.m. to October 9, 2009, 3:30 p.m., Department of 
Health and Human Services, Washington, DC 20201 which was published in 
the Federal Register on August 17, 2009, 74FR41446.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting location to the 
Park Hyatt Hotel, 1201 24th Street, NW., Washington, DC 20037. The 
meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: September 23, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
