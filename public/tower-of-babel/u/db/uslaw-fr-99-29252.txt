November 3, 1999.
    The following notice of meeting is published pursuant to section 
3(A) of the Goverment in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 U.S.C 
552B:

PLACE: Room 2C, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426.

STATUS: Open.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMAITON: David P. Boergers, Secretary,
