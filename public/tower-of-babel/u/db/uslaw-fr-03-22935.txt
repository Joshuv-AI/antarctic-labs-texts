Availability of Decision on Petition for Rulemaking To Repeal 
Regulation Related to Ballast Water

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of availability of EPA decision document.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the availability of EPA's Decision 
Documenton on a petition for rulemaking seeking repeal of a regulation 
that excludes vessel ballast water discharges from Clean Water Act 
permit requirements. Today's notice makes that Decision Document 
publicly available and announces EPA's denial of the petition.

DATES: EPA's Acting Administrator signed the Decision Document on 
September 2, 2003. For judicial review purposes, this action is final 
as of 1 p.m. (eastern time) on Tuesday, September 9, 2003, as provided 
at 40 CFR 23.2.

ADDRESSES: The administrative record is available for inspection and 
copying at the Water Docket, located at the EPA Docket Center in the 
basement of the EPA West Building, Room B-102, at 1301 Constitution 
Avenue., NW., Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ruby Cooper, U.S. EPA, Office Of 
Water, by phone at 202-564-0757 or by e-mail at [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
