FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why and of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, D.C. 
20573.

Pantrac Transport Corp., Brooklyn Navy Yard Bldg. #5, Suite 307, 
Flushing & Cumberland Ave., Brooklyn, NY 11205, Officer: Benjamin 
Hamalian, President
Panamerican All Trading Services Corp., 5461 N.W. 72nd Avenue, 
Miami, FL 33166, Officers: Jorge Murillo, President, Adriana P. 
Orozco, Secretary
Fidelity Forwarding International, Inc., 429 Piaget Avenue, Route 
46, Suite 4, Clifton, NJ 07011, Officers: Charles P. Alles, 
President, Jayne I. Alles, Secretary.

    Dated: November 21, 1996.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
