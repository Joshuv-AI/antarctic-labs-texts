Employment and Training Administration
[NAFTA-002287]


Heritage Hills, Tustin, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 250 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 30, 1998 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on March 13, 1998 on behalf of workers at Heritage Hills, 
Tustin, California. The subject firm is a division of Kimball 
International.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 8th day of April 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
