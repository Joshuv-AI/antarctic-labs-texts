Pomperaug Hydro Project, Andrew Peklo III; Notice Establishing 
Deadline for Comments and Reply Comments

    On December 15, 2011, the Commission issued notice that Office of 
Energy Projects staff will hold a site visit and technical meeting on 
January 18, 2012. The meeting will be transcribed by a court reporter 
and all oral comments made at the meeting will be included in the 
public record. Any written comments should be filed by February 17, 
2012, and any reply comments must be filed by March 5, 2012. If you 
have questions about filing comments, please contact Steve Kartalia at 
(202) 502-6131, or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: January 12, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
 Secretary.
