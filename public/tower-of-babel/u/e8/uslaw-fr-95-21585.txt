Radio Broadcasting Services; Billngs, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 242C1 to Billings, Montana, in 
response to a petition filed by Conway Broadcasting. See 60 FR 26402, 
May 17, 1995. Channel 242C1 can be allotted to Billings without a site 
restriction at coordinates 45-46-58 and 108-30-13. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 10, 1995. The window period for filing 
applications will open on October 10, 1995, and close on November 13, 
1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

and Order, MM Docket No. 95-65, adopted August 16, 1995, and released
