DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Fact-Finding Investigation of Potential ManipulationDocket No. 
PA02-2-000 of Electric and Natural Gas Prices; Notice of Clarification 
of March 5, 2002 Information Request

March 11, 2002.
    On March 5, 2002 (67 FR 11111, March 12, 2002), the Associate 
Director, Office of Markets, Tariffs and Rates, issued an information 
request in this proceeding directed to all jurisdictional sellers and 
all non-jurisdictional sellers in the West. In response to telephone 
inquiries received by Staff, the Associate Director clarifies that the 
March 5, 2002, information request does not apply to sales by 
qualifying facilities. The Associate Director reserves the right to 
issue information requests to qualifying facilities in the future in 
this proceeding.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
