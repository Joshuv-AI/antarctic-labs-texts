DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


AFIT Subcommittee of the Air University Board of Visitors Meeting

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, Air University Headquarters, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Air Force Institute of Technology Subcommittee of the Air 
University Board of Visitors will hold an open meeting on 12-14 March 
2007, with the first business session beginning at 0830 in the 
Superintendent's Conference Room, Building 642, Wright-Patterson Air 
Force Base, Ohio (5 seats available). The purpose of the meeting is to 
give the board an opportunity to review Air Force Institute of 
Technology's educational programs and to present to the Commandant a 
report of their findings and recommendations concerning these programs.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Contact Ms. Beverly Houtz, Academic 
Affairs Office, Air Force Institute of Technology, (937) 255-6565 ext 
4424.

Bao-Anh Trinh,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
