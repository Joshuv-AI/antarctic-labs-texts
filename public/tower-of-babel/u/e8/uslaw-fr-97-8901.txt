FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Formations of, Acquisitions by, 
and Mergers of Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 97-8135) published on page 
15518 of the issue for Tuesday, April 1, 1997.
    On page 15519, under the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas heading, 
the entry for BonState Bancshares, Inc., Bonham, Texas, is revised to 
read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas (Genie D. Short, Vice President) 
2200 North Pearl Street, Dallas, Texas 75201-2272:
    1. BonState Bancshares, Inc., Bonham, Texas, and Bonham Financial 
Services, Inc., Dover, Delaware; to become bank holding companies by 
acquiring 100 percent of the voting shares of Bonham State Bank, 
Bonham, Texas.
    Comments on this application must be received by April 25, 1997.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, April 2, 1997.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
