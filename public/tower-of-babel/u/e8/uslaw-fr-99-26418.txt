FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 99-2035; MM Docket No. 99-167; RM-9391]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Mount Olive and Staunton, IL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Talley Broadcasting 
Corporation, reallots Channel 287A from Mount Olive to Staunton, 
Illinois as its first local aural transmission service, and modifies 
Station WAOX(FM)'s construction permit accordingly. See 64 FR 28133, 
May 25, 1999. Channel 287A can be reallotted to Staunton in compliance 
with the Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without 
the imposition of a site restriction at petitioner's authorized 
construction permit site. The coordinates for Channel 287A at Staunton 
are 39-02-37 North Latitude and 89-44-56 West Longitude. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 15, 1999.
