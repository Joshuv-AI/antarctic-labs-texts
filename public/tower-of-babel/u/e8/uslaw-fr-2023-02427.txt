Regulation (FAR) in order to make needed editorial changes.

DATES: Effective: February 14, 2023.

Secretariat Division (MVCB), at 202-501-4755 or [email protected]. 
Please cite FAC 2023-02, Technical Amendments.

48 CFR part 2.

    Authority: 40 U.S.C. 121(c); 10 U.S.C. chapter 4 and 10 U.S.C.

20113.

PART 2--DEFINITIONS OF WORDS AND TERMS


2.101  [Amended]

0
2. Amend section 2.101, in paragraph (b), in the definition of 
``Contingency operation'' by removing from paragraph (2) the phrase 
``Chapter 15 of title 10 of the United States Code,'' and adding the 
phrase ``Chapter 13 of title 10 of the United States Code, and section 
3713 of title 14 of the United States Code,'' in its place.
