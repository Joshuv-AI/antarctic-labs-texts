Regulation Systems Compliance and Integrity; Correction

AGENCY: Securities and Exchange Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission'') is 
making a technical correction to its rules concerning Regulation 
Systems Compliance and Integrity (``Regulation SCI'') under the 
Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (``Exchange Act'') and conforming 
amendments to Regulation ATS under the Exchange Act, which applies to 
certain self-regulatory organizations (including registered clearing 
agencies), alternative trading systems (``ATSs''), plan processors, and 
exempt clearing agencies (collectively, ``SCI entities'').

DATES: Effective December 30, 2015.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sara Hawkins, Special Counsel, Office 
of Market Supervision, at (202) 551-5523 and Alexander Zozos, Attorney-
Adviser, Office of Market Supervision, at (202) 551-6932, Division of 
Trading and Markets, Securities and Exchange Commission, 100 F Street 
NE., Washington, DC 20549-7010.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Commission is making a technical 
correction to final rules that were published in the Federal Register 
on December 5, 2014 (79 FR 72251) as part of Regulation SCI under the 
Exchange Act and conforming amendments to Regulation ATS under the 
Exchange Act.
