BG Labs, a Subsidiary of Standex, Binghamton, NY; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 5, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers of BG Labs, a 
subsidiary of Standex, Binghamton, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 11th day of May 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
