SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given of a change in the 
meeting of the Disease, Disability, and Injury Prevention and Control 
Special Emphasis Panel (SEP)--RFA-CK-22-006, Clinical and Applied 
Research Strategies for the Prevention and Control of Fungal Diseases; 
April 14, 2022, 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m., EDT. The teleconference was 
published in the Federal Register on February 14, 2022, Volume 87, 
Number 30, page 8251.
    This meeting is being canceled in its entirety.
