NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION
[Docket Nos. 50-498 and 50-499]


South Texas Project; Local Public Document Room

    Notice is hereby given that the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) 
will not be relocating the local public document room (LPDR) for 
records pertaining to Houston Lighting and Power Company's South Texas 
Project. The LPDR will remain at the J.M. Hodges Library, Wharton 
County Junior College, 911 Boling Highway, Wharton, Texas. The Library 
Director has informed the NRC staff that they will continue with the 
maintenance and operation of the South Texas LPDR.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 10th day of March 1997.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Russell A. Powell,
Chief, Freedom of Information/Local Public Document Room Branch, Office 
of Information Resources Management.
