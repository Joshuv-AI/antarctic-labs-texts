Federal Management Regulation; Real Property Policies Update; 
Corrections

AGENCY: Office of Governmentwide Policy, General Services 
Administration (GSA).

ACTION: Correcting amendments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration is issuing amendments to 
FMR Amendment C-1, Real Property Policies Update, published in the 
Federal Register at 67 FR 76820, December 13, 2002, to correct the 
dollar thresholds associated with negotiated sales and explanatory 
statements to be consistent with existing statutes.

DATES: Effective Date: January 9, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stanley C. Langfeld, Director, Real 
Property Policy Division, Office of Governmentwide Policy, General 
Services Administration, by phone at (202) 501-1737 or by e-mail at 
[email protected].

[[Page 1168]]

Corrections

    In the final rule document appearing in the issue of December 13, 
2002, make the following corrections:


Sec.  102-75.880  [Corrected]

    1. On page 76864, third column, under Sec.  102-75.880, correct 
paragraph (a) by removing ``$50,000'' and adding ``$15,000'' in its 
place.


Sec.  102-75.885  [Corrected]

    2. On page 76864, third column, under Sec.  102-75.885, correct 
paragraph (b) by removing ``$700,000'' and adding ``$100,000'' in its 
place.


Sec.  102-75.905  [Corrected]

    3. On page 76865, second column, under Sec.  102-75.905, correct 
paragraphs (a) and (c) by removing ``$700,000'' and adding ``$100,000'' 
in its place.
