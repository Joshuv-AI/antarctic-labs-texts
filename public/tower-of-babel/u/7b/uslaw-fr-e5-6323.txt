Commscope, Inc.; Scottsboro, AL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 12, 2005 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Commscope, 
Inc., Scottsboro, Alabama.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 31st day of October, 2005.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
