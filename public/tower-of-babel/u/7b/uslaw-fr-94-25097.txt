SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

 

Agency Meetings

``FEDERAL REGISTER'' CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: [59 FR 50643, 
October 4, 1994].

STATUS: Open meeting/Closed meeting.

PLACE: 450 Fifth Street, NW., Washington, DC

DATE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED: October 5, 1994.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Cancellation.
    The open meeting scheduled for Wednesday, October 5, 1994, at 11 
a.m. has been cancelled. The closed meeting scheduled for Wednesday, 
October 5, 1994, following the 11 a.m., open meeting has been 
cancelled.
    Commissioner Beese, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above change and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact: The Office of the Secretary (202) 942-7070.

    Dated: October 5, 1994.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
