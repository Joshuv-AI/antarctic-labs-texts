DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-27-2017]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 43--Battle Creek, Michigan; 
Authorization of Production Activity, Pfizer, Inc., (Pharmaceutical 
Products), Kalamazoo, Michigan

    On April 12, 2017, Pfizer, Inc., submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board within Subzone 43E, in 
Kalamazoo, Michigan.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting

[[Page 41208]]

public comment (82 FR 26433, June 7, 2017). On August 15, 2017, the 
applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that no further 
review of the activity is warranted at this time. The production 
activity described in the notification was authorized, subject to the 
FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including Section 400.14.

    Dated: August 24, 2017.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
