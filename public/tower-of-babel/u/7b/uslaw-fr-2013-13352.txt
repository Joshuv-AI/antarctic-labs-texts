Relationship Between General Design Criteria and Technical 
Specification Operability

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Regulatory issue summary; issuance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is issuing 
Regulatory Issue Summary (RIS) 2013-05, ``NRC Position on the 
Relationship Between General Design Criteria and Technical 
Specification Operability.'' This RIS clarifies the NRC staff's 
position on the relationship between the general design criteria (GDC) 
for nuclear power plants and technical specification operability. In 
addition, the RIS clarifies the process for addressing any structure, 
system, or component nonconforming condition with GDC as incorporated 
into a plant's current licensing basis.
