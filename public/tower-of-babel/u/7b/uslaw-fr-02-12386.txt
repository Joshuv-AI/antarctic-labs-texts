Liberty Manufacturing; Liberty, SC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 29, 2002, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Liberty Manufacturing, 
Liberty, South Carolina.
    An active certification covering the petitioning worker group of 
workers remains in effect (TA-W-40,663). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose and the investigation 
has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 2nd day of May, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
