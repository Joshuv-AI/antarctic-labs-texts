DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4050]


Request for Proposals

    The Office of Crime Programs, Bureau for International Narcotics 
and Law Enforcement Affairs (INL/C) is seeking proposals from qualified 
U.S. Organizations and Institutions with the requisite capability and 
experience to develop a methodology for assessing the effectiveness of 
anticorruption policies and actions being taken by national 
governments. The methodology should include a process to identify 
deficiencies in such policies and actions for the purpose of providing 
constructive input and targeting technical assistance to address 
deficiencies. Organizations will also be asked to test this methodology 
on four sample countries. Current plans are to award up to a total of 
$200,000 to be available to develop this International Anticorruption 
Assessment Process (IAAP). Applications are due 7/12/02 at 4 p.m. EST. 
Interested applicants may obtain detailed application instructions from 
the following website: www.statebuy.gov; click on grant opportunities.
    For questions, please contact Linda Gower, Grants Officer, at (202) 
776-8774 or [email protected].
