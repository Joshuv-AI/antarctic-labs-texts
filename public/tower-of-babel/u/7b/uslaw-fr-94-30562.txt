DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[MT-070-05-1430-01; MT82585]

 

Notice of Realty Action; Exchange

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, USDI.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In notice document 94-27335 on page 55281 in the issue of 
Friday, November 4, 1994, make the following corrections:

    The paragraph which follows the legal description of the public 
lands should read: ``In exchange for some of these lands, the Untied 
States will acquire the following described land from Mr. Jack 
Thomas:''
    The legal description of the private land to be acquired should 
have T11S, R11W included in the description.
    In the last paragraph the estimated completion date should be April 
1995 rather the December 1994.

    Dated: December 5, 1994.
Orval L. Hadley,
Associate District Manager.
