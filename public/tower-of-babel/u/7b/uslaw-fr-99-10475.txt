DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-35, 396]


Zenith Electronics Corp., Melrose Park, Illinois; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 9, 1998 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers and former workers at Zenith Electronics 
Corporation, located in Melrose Park, Illinois (TA-W-35, 396).
    The Department of Labor has determined that the petitioners are 
covered under an existing certification, as amended (TA-W-34, 579). 
Consequently, further investigation in this matter would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of April 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
