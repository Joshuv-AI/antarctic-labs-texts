DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Patents Available for Licensing

AGENCY: U.S. Army Aviation and Troop Command, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive, or non-exclusive licenses under the 
following patent. Any licenses granted shall comply with 35 U.S.C. 209 
and 37 CFR part 404.

Issued Patent: 5,315,718
Title: Protective Helmet and Retention System Therefor
Issue Date: 05/31/94

ADDRESSES: Patent Attorney, Intellectual Property Law Branch, U.S. Army 
Aviation and Troop Command, 4300 Goodfellow Blvd., St. Louis, Missouri 
63120-1798.
