May 27, 2009.
    Take notice that during the month of April 2009, the status of the 
above-captioned entities as Exempt Wholesale Generators Companies 
became effective by operation of the Commission's regulations 18 CFR 
366.7(a), except for Docket Nos. EG09-19-000 and EG09-20-000, which 
became effective in February 2009; and Docket Nos. EG09-23-000, EG09-
24-000, and EG09-25-000, which became effective in March 2009.
