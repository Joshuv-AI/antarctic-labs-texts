SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Introduction

    The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has prepared a draft 
Interim Staff Guidance (ISG) No. 23 document, entitled ``Application of 
ASTM Standard Practice C1671-07 when performing technical reviews of 
spent fuel storage and transportation packaging licensing actions.'' 
This draft ISG document would provide guidance to the NRC staff when 
reviewing licensee integrated safety analyses, license or Certificate 
of Compliance applications or amendment requests, or other related 
activities for dry cask storage systems under 10 CFR part 71 and 10 CFR 
part 72. The NRC is soliciting public comments on this draft of ISG-23, 
which will be considered before the NRC issues any final version.
