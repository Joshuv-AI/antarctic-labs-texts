DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Integrative Health; Amended; 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Council for Complementary and Integrative Health, October 2, 
2015, 8:30 a.m. to October 2, 2015, 03:35 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Conference Room 10, Bethesda, MD 
20892 which was published in the Federal Register on September 8, 2015, 
80 FR 53814.
    This meeting notice is amended to change the time of the OPEN 
SESSION from 10 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. (ET). The meeting is partially Closed 
to the public.
