FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Notice of Change in Subject Matter of Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of subsection (e)(2) of the ``Government 
in the Sunshine Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b(e)(2)), notice is hereby given 
that at its open meeting held at 10:01 a.m. on Thursday, January 21, 
2016, the Corporation's Board of Directors determined, on motion of 
Vice Chairman Thomas M. Hoenig, seconded by Director Richard Cordray 
(Director, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau), concurred in by 
Director Thomas J. Curry (Comptroller of the Currency), and Chairman 
Martin J. Gruenberg, that Corporation business required the addition to 
the agenda for consideration at the meeting, on less than seven days' 
notice to the public, of the following matters:

Memorandum and resolution re: Interim Final Rule with Request for 
Comments: Expanded Exam Cycle for Certain Small Insured Depository 
Institutions and U.S. Branches and Agencies of Foreign Banks.

    The Board further determined, by the same majority vote, that no 
notice earlier than January 20, 2016, of the

[[Page 4299]]

change in the subject matter of the meeting was practicable.

    Dated: January 21, 2016.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
