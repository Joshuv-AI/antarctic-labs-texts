DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Health Resources and Services Administration


Advisory Council; Notice of Meeting

Correction

    In Federal Register Document 95-20814 appearing at pages 43805-06 
in the issue for Wednesday, August 23, 1995, the September 27, 1995, 
meeting of the ``National Advisory Council on Nurse Education and 
Practice and the Council on Graduate Medical Education'' has been 
changed. The meeting will include a demonstration of the computer-based 
requirements model at 7:45 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on September 26.
    All other information is correct as it appears.

    Dated: September 15, 1995.
Jackie E. Baum,
Advisory Committee Management Officer, HRSA.
