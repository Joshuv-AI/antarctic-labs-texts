SUMMARY: This is to correct the Federal Travel Regulation's proposed 
rule published in the Federal Register at 68 FR 38662, June 30, 2003, 
by revising a Table of Content entry that was inadvertently revised.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Laurie Duarte at (202) 501-4755, 
General Services Administration, Regulatory Secretariat, Washington, DC 
20405.

Correction

    In the proposed rule document appearing in the issue of June 30, 
2003, make the following correction:
    1. On page 38662, third column, table of contents, fourth section 
entry, remove ``301-73.1'' and add in its place ``301-50.4''.

    Dated: July 1, 2003.
