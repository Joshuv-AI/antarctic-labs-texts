FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[FR ID 75169]


Open Commission Meeting Wednesday, March 16, 2022

    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Wednesday, March 16, 2022, which is 
scheduled to commence at 10:30 a.m. Due to the current COVID-19 
pandemic and related agency telework and headquarters access policies, 
this meeting will be in an electronic format and will be open to the 
public only on the internet via live feed from the FCC's web page at 
www.fcc.gov/live and on the FCC's YouTube channel.

                                                Discrimination (GN

                                               Summary: The Commission

                                                of Inquiry that would

                                                and ensure that all

                                                States benefit from

                                                Congress's direction in

                                               Summary: The Commission

                                                Further Notice of
                                                Proposed Rulemaking that
                                                would seek comment on
                                                questions concerning the
                                                allocation of pole
                                                replacement costs
                                                between utilities and
