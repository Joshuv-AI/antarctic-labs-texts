Fraternity Federal Savings & Loan Association, Baltimore, MD; 
Approval of Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on February 10, 2011, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of Fraternity FS&LA, 
Baltimore, Maryland, to convert to the stock form of organization. 
Copies of the application are available for inspection by appointment 
(phone number: (202) 906-5922 or e-mail: public.info@ots.treas.gov">public.info@ots.treas.gov) at 
the Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and 
the OTS Southeast Regional Office, 1475 Peachtree Street, NE., Atlanta, 
Georgia 30309.

    Dated: February 10, 2011.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
