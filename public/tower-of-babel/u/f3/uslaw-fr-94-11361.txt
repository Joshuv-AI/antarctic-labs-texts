FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION
 

Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Reissuance of License

license has been reissued by the Federal Maritime Commission pursuant 
to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718) and the 
regulations of the Commission pertaining to the licensing of ocean 
freight forwarders, 46 CFR part 510.
