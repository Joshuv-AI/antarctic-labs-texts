[[Page 58064]]

    A copy of the preceding described amended protraction diagrams will 
be immediately placed in the open files and will be available to the 
public as a matter of information.
    If a protest against these amended diagrams is received prior to 
the date of the official filings, the filings will be stayed pending 
consideration of the protest. These particular amended protraction 
diagrams will not be officially filed until the day after all protests 
have been accepted or dismissed and become final or appeals from the 
dismissal affirmed.
    3. All inquiries relating to these lands should be sent to the 
Arizona State Office, Bureau of Land Management, 222 N. Central Avenue, 
P.O. Box 1552, Phoenix, Arizona 85001-1552.
