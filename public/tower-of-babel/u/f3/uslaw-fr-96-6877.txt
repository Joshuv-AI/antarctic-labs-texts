SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Boston District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Boston District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Thursday, April 18, 1996 at 9:30 
am at the SBA, 10 Causeway Street, Room 265, Boston, Massachusetts 
02222; to discuss matters as may be presented by members, staff of the 
U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Mary E. McAleney, 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 10 Causeway 
Street, Room 265, Boston, Massachusetts 02222-1093, (617) 565-5560.

    Dated:
    March 18, 1996.
Bill Combs,
Associate Administrator for Office of Communication and Public Liaison.
