DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration


Correction of Application Deadline for the Grant Program, 
Community Collaborations to Prevent Youth Violence and Promote Youth 
Development (SM 03-005) (Short Title: Youth Violence Prevention Grants)

AGENCY: Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration 
(SAMHSA), DHHS.

ACTION: Correction of application deadline for the grant program, 
Community Collaborations to Prevent Youth Violence and Promote Youth 
Development (SM 03-005) (Short Title: Youth Violence Prevention 
Grants).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice is to inform the public that the application 
deadline published on November 26, 2002, for the grant program, 
Community Collaborations to Prevent Youth Violence and Promote Youth 
Development (SM 03-005) (Short Title: Youth Violence Prevention 
Grants), is incorrect. The correct application deadline is January 22, 
2003.
