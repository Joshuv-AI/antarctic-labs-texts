DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


Universal Federal Savings Bank, Chicago, IL; Notice of 
Appointment of Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Sec. 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision has duly appointed the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation as sole Receiver for Universal Federal Savings Bank, 
Chicago, Illinois (OTS No. 03327), on June 27, 2002.

    Dated: June 28, 2002.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
