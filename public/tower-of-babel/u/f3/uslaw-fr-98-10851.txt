Proposed Tolerance Revocations for Canceled Pesticide Active 
Ingredients; Reopening of Comment Period

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).
ACTION: Proposed rule; reopening of comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: EPA is reopening the comment period for the proposed rule on 
revocation of tolerances and exemptions from the requirements of a 
tolerance for canceled pesticide active ingredients. The proposed 
revocation was published in the Federal Register of January 21, 1998. 
The comment period expired on March 23, 1998. One commenter, the 
European Union, requested additional time to make an analysis. In 
response, the Agency is reopening the comment period until May 5, 1998.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background

    In the Federal Register of January 21, 1998 (63 FR 3057)(5743-8), 
EPA issued a proposed rule to revoke tolerances and exemptions from the 
requirement of a tolerance for canceled pesticide active ingredients. 
The original due date for comments on the Proposed rule was March 23, 
1998. EPA is reopening the comment period until May 5, 1998. EPA 
received a request for an extension due to the need to collect specific 
information that may be responsive to the proposal.
