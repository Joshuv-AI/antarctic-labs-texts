Self-Regulatory Organizations; Cboe BZX Exchange, Inc.; Notice of 
Withdrawal of a Proposed Rule Change To Amend Its Fee Schedule

June 21, 2023.
    On March 6, 2023, Cboe BZX Exchange, Inc. (``Exchange'') filed with 
the Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), pursuant to 
Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (``Act'') \1\ 
and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to amend its fee 
schedule. The proposed rule change was published for comment in the 
Federal Register on March 16, 2023.\3\ On May 4, 2023, the Commission 
temporarily suspended the proposed rule change and instituted 
proceedings to determine whether to approve or disapprove the proposed 
rule change.\4\ On June 14, 2023, the Exchange withdrew the proposed 
rule change (CboeBZX-2023-020).
