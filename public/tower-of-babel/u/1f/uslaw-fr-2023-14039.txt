NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management Renewals

    The National Science Foundation (NSF) management officials having 
responsibility for the advisory committees listed below have determined 
that renewing these groups for another two years is necessary and in 
the public interest in connection with the performance of duties 
imposed upon the Director, National Science Foundation (NSF), by 42 
U.S.C. 1861 et seq. This determination follows consultation with the 
Committee Management Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Committees:

Advisory Committee for Biological Sciences, #1110
Advisory Committee for Cyberinfrastructure, #25150
Advisory Committee for STEM Education, #1119 (formerly, the Advisory 
Committee for Education and Human Resources)
Advisory Committee for Engineering, #1170
Advisory Committee for Geosciences, #1755
Advisory Committee for Integrative Activities, #1373
Alan T. Waterman Award Committee, #1172
Proposal Review Panel for Atmospheric and Geospace Sciences, #10751
