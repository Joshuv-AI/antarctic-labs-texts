Baltimore Gas and Electric Company; Notice of Filing

October 17, 1997.
    Take notice that on August 15, 1997, Baltimore Gas and Electric 
Company (BGE) submitted a compliance filing in accordance with the 
requirements of FERC's July 31, 1997 Order in Allegheny Power System, 
Inc., et al., 80 FERC para.61,143 (1997). Included in the filing is a 
revised Schedule 9, Real Power Losses, of BGE's Open Access 
Transmission Tariff, designated as FERC Electric Tariff, Original 
Volume No. 4.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest such filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions and protests should be filed on or before October 27, 1997. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
