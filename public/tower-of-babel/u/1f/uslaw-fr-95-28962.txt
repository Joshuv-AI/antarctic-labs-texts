DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP95-408-000 and RP95-408-001]


Columbia Gas Transmission Corporation; Notice of Settlement 
Conference

November 21, 1995.
    Take notice that Commission Staff will convene an informal 
settlement conference in this proceeding on November 29, 1995, at 10:00 
a.m. The conference will be held in a hearing room at the offices of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. The conference will continue through November 30, 
1995, if necessary.
    Any party, as defined by 18 CFR 385.102(c), or any participant, as 
defined in 18 CFR 385.102(b), may attend. Persons wishing to become a 
party must move to intervene and receive intervenor status pursuant to 
the Commission's Regulations, 18 CFR 385.214.

    For additional information, contact Thomas J. Burgess at (202) 
208-2058 or David R. Cain at (202) 208-0917.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
