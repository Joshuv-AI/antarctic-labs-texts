DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Announcement of Availability of Funds for Family Planning Male 
Reproductive Health Research Grants

AGENCY: Department of Health and Human Services, Office of the 
Secretary, Office of Public Health and Science, Office of Population 
Affairs.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of Population Affairs published a notice in the 
Federal Register of April 14, 2003 announcing the availability of funds 
for family planning male reproductive health research grants. A 
correction Notice was published on May 23, 2003. There was an error in 
this Notice. This Notice corrects that error.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Susan B. Moskosky, 301-594-4008.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of May 23, 2003, in FR Doc. 03-12983, on 
page 28228, in the first column, second paragraph under the heading 
``Correction'' correct the second sentence which reads ``Awards will 
range from $100,000 to $250,000 per year, inclusive of direct costs'' 
to read:
    ``Awards will range from $100,000 to $250,000 per year, inclusive 
of indirect costs.''

    Dated: May 30, 2003.
Alma L. Golden,
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Population Affairs.
