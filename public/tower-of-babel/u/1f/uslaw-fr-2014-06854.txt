DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Rural Business-Cooperative Service


Maximum Loan Amount for Business and Industry Guaranteed Loans in 
Fiscal Year 2014

AGENCY: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, USDA.

ACTION: The Notice of Funds Availability published in the Federal 
Register, Volume 78, Number 243, on December 18, 2013, that limited 
Business and Industry Guaranteed Loans to $10 million is hereby 
rescinded for Fiscal Year 2014. The carryover of unspent Fiscal Year 
2013 together with Fiscal Year 2014 funds has resulted in a program 
level that merits the removal of the $10 million restriction.
