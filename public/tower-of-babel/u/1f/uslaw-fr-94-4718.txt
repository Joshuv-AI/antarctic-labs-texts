DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families

 

Meeting of the U.S. Advisory Board on Child Abuse and Neglect

AGENCY: Administration for Children and Families, HHS.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Advisory Board on Child Abuse and Neglect will hold a 
meeting at the Department of Health and Human Services, Room 800 
Humphrey Building, 200 Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, DC 20301, 
from 9 a.m., March 15, 1994, through 3 p.m., March 17, 1994.
    This meeting is open to the public. If a sign language interpreter 
is needed, you may contact David Siegel at (202) 401-9215.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Marilyn J. Gosdeck, Special Projects 
Specialist, U.S. Advisory Board on Child Abuse and Neglect, room 303-D, 
Humphrey Building Washington, DC 20201, (202) 690-8604.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: During this meeting, the Advisory Board 
will: discuss the process for developing the fatalities report and its 
content; and the future directions of the Board. Nine new members of 
the Board will be sworn-in by the Secretary of Health and Human 
Services at a ceremony on March 15 at 4 p.m.


    Dated: February 21, 1994.
Preston Bruce,
Acting Executive Director, U.S. Advisory Board on Child Abuse and 
Neglect.
