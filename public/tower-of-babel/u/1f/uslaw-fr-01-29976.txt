DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. PR02-5-000]


Transok, LLC; Notice of Petition for Rate Approval

November 28, 2001.
    Take notice that on November 15, 2001, Transok, LLC (Transok) 
submitted for filing a revised fuel factor for its Palo Duro System for 
Fuel Year 2002 as calculated under the terms of Transok's filed fuel 
tracker. Transok seeks an effective date of January 1, 2002.
    Transok states that it is serving notice of the filing and the 
revised fuel percentage on all current shippers and on the Oklahoma 
Corporation Commission.
    Pursuant to section 284.123(b)(2), if the Commission does not act 
within 150 days of the filing date, these rates will be deemed to be 
fair and equitable and not in excess of an amount which interstate 
pipelines would be permitted to charge for providing similar 
transportation service. The Commission may, prior to the expiration of 
the 150-day period, extend the time for action or institute a 
proceeding to afford interested parties an opportunity for written 
comments and for the oral presentations of views, data and arguments.
    Any person desiring to participate in this rate proceeding must 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's rules of practice 
and procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All motions must be filed 
with the Secretary of the Commission on or before December 14, 2001. 
This petition for rate approval is on file with the Commission and is 
available for public inspection. This filing may also be viewed on the 
web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select ``Docket#'' 
and follow the instructions (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). 
Comments, protests and interventions may be filed electronically via 
the Internet in lieu of paper. See, 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the 
instructions on the Commission's web site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
