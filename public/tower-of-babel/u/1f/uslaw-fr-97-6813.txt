DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-33,207]


Gruen Marketing Corporation Exeter, Pennsylvania; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 18, 1997 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers and former workers at Gruen 
Marketing Corporation, located in Exeter, Pennsylvania (TA-W-33,207).
    On February 26, 1997, the Department of Labor issued a 
determination on behalf of the petitioning group of workers at Gruen 
Marketing Corporation, located in Exeter, Pennsylvania (TA-W-33,216). 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 27th day of February 1997.
Russell T. Kile,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
