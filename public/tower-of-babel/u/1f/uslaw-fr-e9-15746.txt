LDS Test and Measurement LLC, Middleton, WI; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 20, 2009, in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers of LDS Test and 
Measurement, LLC, Middleton, Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 17th day of June 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
