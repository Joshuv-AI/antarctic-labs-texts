DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2004-075-Massachusetts]


City of Holyoke Gas & Electric Department; Notice

February 18, 2004.
    By notice of January 28, 2003, Commission staff was assigned to 
help facilitate resolution of environmental and related issues 
associated with development of a comprehensive settlement agreement for 
the Holyoke Project. The following staff will also be considered 
``separated staff'' and will take no part in the Commission's review of 
the offer of settlement and the comprehensive settlement agreement, or 
deliberations concerning the disposition of the rehearings:
    Robert Grieve, Office of Energy Projects.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
