DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review: Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Biological 
Rhythms and Sleep Study Section, February 9, 2005, 9:00 a.m. to 
February 9, 2005, 5:00 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro 
Center, 7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published 
in the Federal Register on January 18, 2005, 70 FR 2872-2875.
    The meeting is cancelled due to a lack of quorum.

    Dated: January 19, 2005.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
