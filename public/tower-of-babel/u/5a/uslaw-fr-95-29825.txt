ASSASSINATION RECORDS REVIEW BOARD


Formal Determinations on Records Release: Correction

AGENCY: Assassination Records Review Board.

ACTION: Notice of Formal Determinations: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Assassination Records Review Board (Review Board) met on 
November 13, and November 14, 1995, and made formal determinations on 
the release of records under the President John F. Kennedy 
Assassination Records Collection Act of 1992 (JFK Act). The Review 
Board published a notice document in the Monday, December 4, 1995, 
Federal Register, reflecting those determinations. In that notice 
document 95-29389 beginning on page 62066, make the following 
corrections:
    On page 62066, in the third column of the FBI documents table, make 
the following corrections:
