California Independent System Operator Corporation; Notice of 
FERC Staff Attendance

December 15, 2008.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on the following dates members of its staff will attend a 
meeting of the Board of Governors of the California Independent System 
Operator (CAISO). Unless otherwise noted, this meeting will be held at 
the CAISO, 151 Blue Ravine Road, Folsom, CA or by teleconference. The 
agenda and other documents for the meetings are available on the 
CAISO's Web site, http://www.caiso.com.
    December 16-17, 2008, Board of Governors Meeting.
    Sponsored by the CAISO, this meeting is open to all market 
participants, and staff's attendance is part of the Commission's 
ongoing outreach efforts. This meeting may discuss matters at issue in 
the above captioned dockets.
