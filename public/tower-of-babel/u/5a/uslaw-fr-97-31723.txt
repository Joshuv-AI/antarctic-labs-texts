Opening of Lands; Montana

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Parcels of land which were segregated for Phase II of exchange 
MTM 84895 are no longer under consideration for exchange. This order 
terminates the exchange segregation and opens the following described 
lands to the public land laws and mining laws, subject to other 
segregations of record:

Principal Meridian, Montana

T. 3 N., R. 26 E.,
    Sec. 32, S\1/2\SE\1/4\.
T. 5 N., R. 28 E.,
    Sec. 28, NE\1/4\ and NE\1/4\SE\1/4\;
    Sec. 34, NE\1/4\NE\1/4\.
T. 6 N., R. 28 E.,
    Sec. 34, NE\1/4\NE\1/4\.
T. 4 N., R. 31 E.,
    Sec. 24, NE\1/4\NE\1/4\.
T. 6 N., R. 31 E.,
    Sec. 34, N\1/2\NE\1/4\ and W\1/2\SE\1/4\.
T. 4 N., R. 32 E.,
    Sec. 18, lot 3 and NE\1/4\SW\1/4\.

    Containing 635.46 acres in Yellowstone County.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 3, 1997.
