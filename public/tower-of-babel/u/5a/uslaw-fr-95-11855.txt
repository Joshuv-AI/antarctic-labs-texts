FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-127; RM-8537]

Radio Broadcasting Services; Wright City, OK
AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Texarkana Broadcasting, 
Incorporated, dismisses its request to allot Channel 277A to Wright 
City, OK, as the community's first local aural service. See 59 FR 
59744, November 18, 1994. No interest in applying for the channel was 
received by the Commission. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
