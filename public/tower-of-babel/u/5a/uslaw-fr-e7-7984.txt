April 20, 2007.
    Effective April 19, 2007, Alan Haymes, of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (Commission), was designated as non-decisional 
staff in the above-captioned docket. As non-decisional staff, Mr. 
Haymes will not participate in an advisory capacity in deliberations on 
the issues pending therein. Separated non-decisional and advisory 
staffs are prohibited from communicating with one another concerning 
the deliberations set forth above.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
