FEDERAL MINE SAFETY AND HEALTH REVIEW COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

February 2, 2003.

Time and Date: 10 a.m., Thursday, February 12, 2004.

Place: Hearing Room, 9th Floor, 601 New Jersey Avenue, NW., Washington, 
DC.

Status: Closed [pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(10)].

Matters To Be Considered: The Commission will consider and act upon the 
following in closed session:
    In re Request for Relief Cases. (The Commission will discuss 
methods of disposing of the approximately 50 cases pending on the 
Commission's docket that involve requests for relief from final 
Commission orders.)
