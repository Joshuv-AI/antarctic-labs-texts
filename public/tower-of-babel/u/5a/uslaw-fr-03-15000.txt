Special Conditions: Raytheon Aircraft Company HS 125 Series 700A 
and 700B Airplanes; High Intensity Radiated Fields (HIRF)

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final special conditions; request for comments; Correction

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes corrections to a Final special conditions; 
request for comment document published in the Federal Register on May 
19, 2003 (68 FR 26991), which issued special conditions for the 
Raytheon Aircraft Company Model HS 125 Series 700A and 700B airplanes 
modified by Raytheon Aircraft Services, Inc., for protection from HIRF.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jan Thor, FAA, Standardization Branch, 
ANM-113, Transport Airplane Directorate, Aircraft Certification 
Service, 1601 Lind Avenue, SW., Renton, Washington, 98055-4056; 
telephone (425) 227-21271 facsimile (425) 227-1330; or e-mail: 
[email protected].

Correction

    In Final special conditions; request for comment document FR Doc. 
03-12376, published on May 19, 2003 (68 FR 26991), make the following 
correction:
    1. On page 26991, in the third column, under the DATES section of
