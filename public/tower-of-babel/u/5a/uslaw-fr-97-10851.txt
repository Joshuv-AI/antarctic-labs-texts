FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-236; RM-8907]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wake Village, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Phillip W. O'Bryan, allots 
Channel 223A to Wake Village, Texas, as the community's first local FM 
service. See 61 FR 63809, December 2, 1996. Channel 223A can be 
allotted to Wake Village in compliance with the Commission's minimum 
distance separation requirements with a site restriction of 3.4 
kilometers (2.1 miles) northeast in order to avoid a short-spacing 
conflict with an application for Channel 224C2 at Blossom, Texas. The 
coordinates for Channel 223A at Wake Village are 33-25-09 NL and 94-04-
18 WL. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective June 2, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications will open on June 2, 1997, and close on July 2, 1997.
