DEPARTMENT OF STATE

Office of the Secretary
[Public Notice No. 2932]


Nigeria; Determination Under Presidential Proclamation

    I hereby make the determination provided for in section 6 of 
Presidential Proclamation No. 6636, of December 10, 1993, that the 
suspension of entry into the United States as immigrants and 
nonimmigrants of persons who formulate, implement or benefit from 
policies that impede Nigeria's transition to democracy is no longer 
necessary. Restrictions imposed in said proclamation, pursuant to 
Section 212(f) of the Immigration and Nationality Act of 1952 as 
amended (8 U.S.C. 1182(f)), shall therefore lapse, and said 
proclamation shall terminate effective immediately.
    This determination will be reported to Congress and published in 
the Federal Register.

    Dated: October 26, 1998.
Madeleine K. Albright,
Secretary of State.
