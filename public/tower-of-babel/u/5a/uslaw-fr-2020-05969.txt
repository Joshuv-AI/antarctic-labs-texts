DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Cancer 
Institute Board of Scientific Advisors, March 30, 2020, 8:00 a.m. to 
March 30, 2020, 6:05 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on March 9, 2020, 85 FR 13666. This 
meeting is being postponed in the best interest of the health and 
safety of employees, contractors, and all board/committee members. The 
meeting will be rescheduled in either late April or early May.

    Dated: March 17, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
