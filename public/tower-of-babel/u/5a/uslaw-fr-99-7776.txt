ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-6316-8]


Notice of Public Meeting on Drinking Water Issues

    Notice is hereby given that the U.S. Environmental Protection 
Agency (EPA) is holding a meeting on April 28-29, 1999, at Resolve, 
1255 23rd St., NW, Suite 275, Washington, DC 20037, for the purpose of 
exchanging technical information on issues related to the use of 
ultraviolet (UV) radiation for disinfection of drinking water. The 
meeting will start at 8:30 a.m. on Wednesday, April 28 and will adjourn 
on Thursday, April 29 at 4:00 p.m. The meeting will provide an 
evaluation of the current status of knowledge and prioritize additional 
research needs related to selected aspects of UV disinfection of 
drinking water. The public is invited to attend the meeting as 
observers. Seating is very limited so advance registration is required.

    For additional information about the meeting and to register, 
please contact Dan Schmelling of EPA's Office of Ground Water and 
Drinking Water at (202) 260-1439 or by e-mail at 
[email protected].

    Dated: March 23, 1999.
Cynthia C. Dougherty,
Director, Office of Ground Water and Drinking Water.
