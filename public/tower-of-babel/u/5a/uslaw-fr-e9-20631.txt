DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 1, 2009, 8 a.m. to 
October 2, 2009, 2:00 p.m., Bethesda Marriott, 5151 Pooks Hill Road, 
Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 13, 2009, 74 FR 40823-40824.
    The meeting was cancelled due to administration problems.

    Dated: August 20, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
