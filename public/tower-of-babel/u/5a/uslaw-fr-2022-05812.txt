Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2021-1022.
    Petitioner: Indro Robotics.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  107.15 and 107.49.
    Description of Relief Sought: Indro Robotics seeks relief to the 
extent necessary to operate a small unmanned aircraft system, below 55 
pounds, for beyond visual line of sight operations. The proposed 
personnel will include an offsite remote pilot in command who is 
remotely located and assisted by a visual observer who is onsite. The 
petitioner requests this relief in order to conduct commercial 
operations by inspecting solar farms on privately owned property with 
owner permission, in rural settings, and in Class G Airspace.
