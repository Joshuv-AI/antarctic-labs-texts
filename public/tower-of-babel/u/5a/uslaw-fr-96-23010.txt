DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel on the Role of 
Infectious Agents in Atherosclerosis and Restenosis, September 19, 
1996, 8:00 a.m., which was published in the Federal Register on August 
15, 1996 (61 FR 24230).
    The meeting was to have been held at the National Institutes of 
Health, Building 31, Room 5A16, Bethesda, Maryland, but has been 
changed to the Ramada Inn Rockville, 1775 Rockville Pike, Rockville, 
Maryland. As previously advertised, the meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: September 4, 1996.
Margery G. Grubb,
Senior Committee Management Specialist, NIH.
