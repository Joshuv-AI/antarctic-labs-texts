DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism Special Emphasis Panel, 
August 8, 2001, 8:00 a.m. to August 10, 2001, 6:00 p.m., DoubleTree 
Hotel, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in 
the Federal Register on July 16, 2001, 66 FR 37043-37044.
    The meeting will be held 08/07-10/2001 beginning at 6:30 p.m. 
instead of 08/08/2001 at 8 a.m. The location is the same. The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: July 24, 2001.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
