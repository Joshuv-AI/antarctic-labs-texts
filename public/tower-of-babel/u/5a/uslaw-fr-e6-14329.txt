E.I. Dupont, Dupont Automotive Systems Division, Troy, MI; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on July 25, 2006 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at E.I. DuPont, DuPont 
Automotive Systems Division, Troy, Michigan. The workers at the subject 
facility produced automotive paints.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 18th day of August, 2006.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
