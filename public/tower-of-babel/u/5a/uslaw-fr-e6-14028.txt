DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Intent To Grant Partially Exclusive Patent License; 
Omega Sensors, Inc.

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to Omega Sensors, Inc., a revocable, non-assignable, partially 
exclusive license in the United States to practice the Government-owned 
invention described in U.S. Patent Pending, entitled ``Method of 
fabricating a dual-suspension system for MEMS-based devices'', Navy 
Case Number 96659.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license must file 
written objections along with supporting evidence, if any, not later 
than September 8, 2006.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Office of 
Research and Technology Applications, Space and Naval Warfare Systems 
Center, Code 2112, 83570 Silvergate Ave., Room 2306, San Diego, CA 
92152-5048.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Stephen H. Lieberman, Office of 
Research and Technology Applications, Space and Naval Warfare Systems 
Center, Code 2112, 83570 Silvergate Ave., Room 2306, San Diego, CA 
92152-5048, telephone 619-553-2778, or e-mail: 
[email protected].
