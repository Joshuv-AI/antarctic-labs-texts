DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board


Foreign-Trade Zone 238--Dublin, VA Site Renumbering Notice

    Foreign-Trade Zone 238 was approved by the Foreign-Trade Zones 
Board on August 5, 1999 (Board Order 1047).
    FTZ 238 currently consists of 1 ``site'' totaling 50 acres in the 
Dublin area. The current update does not alter the physical boundaries 
that have previously been approved, but instead involves an 
administrative renumbering that separates certain non-contiguous sites 
for recordkeeping purposes.
    Under this revision, the site list for FTZ 238 will be as follows: 
Site 1 (35 acres)--within the New River Valley Airport on VA Route 100, 
Dublin; and, Site 2 (15 acres)--located at 4100 Bob White Boulevard, 
Pulaski.
