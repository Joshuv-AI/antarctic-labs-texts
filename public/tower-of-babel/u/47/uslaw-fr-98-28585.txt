NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[Docket No. 55-32442-SP and ASLBP No. 99-753-01-SP]


Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel; Shaun P. O'Hern (Denial 
of Reactor Operator's License); Notice of Hearing

    Before Administrative Judges: Peter B. Bloch, Presiding Officer, 
Dr. Richard F. Cole, Special Assistant.

    The request for a hearing filed by Shaun P. O'Hern on September 22, 
1998 has been granted. The hearing will be conducted pursuant to 10 CFR 
Part 2, Subpart L and may be determined entirely based on written 
presentations. The subject of the hearing is the denial of Mr. O'Hern's 
application to operate a nuclear reactor. This notice is published 
pursuant to 10 CFR 2.1205(j).

Rockville, Maryland, October 20, 1998.
Peter B. Bloch,
Administrative Judge, Presiding Officer.
