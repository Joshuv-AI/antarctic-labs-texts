FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-2709, MB Docket No. 03-110, RM-10700]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Conway, SC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of the South Carolina 
Educational Television Commission, substitutes DTV channel *9 for DTV 
channel *58 at Conway, South Carolina. See 68 FR 28806, May 27, 2003. 
DTV channel *9 can be allotted to Conway, South Carolina, in compliance 
with the principal community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) 
at reference coordinates 33-56-58 N. and 79-06-31 W. with a power of 
20, HAAT of 250.2 meters and with a DTV service population of 619 
thousand. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 14, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
