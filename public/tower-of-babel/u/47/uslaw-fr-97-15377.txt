COMMITTEE FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF TEXTILE AGREEMENTS


Amendment of Quota and Visa Requirements To Include a New Exempt 
Certification Arrangement for Chinese Floor Coverings Produced or 
Manufactured in the People's Republic of China; Correction

June 6, 1997.
    A notice and letter to the Commissioner of Customs were published 
in the Federal Register on May 16, 1997 (62 FR 27017). In the letter to 
Customs, 3rd column, 3rd paragraph, the 3rd line reads as follows: ``in 
by the shipment, quantity, date of.'' This line should be corrected to 
read ``in the shipment, quantity, date of.''
D. Michael Hutchinson,
Acting Chairman, Committee for the Implementation of Textile 
Agreements.
