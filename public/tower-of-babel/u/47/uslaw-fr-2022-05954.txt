Requirement for NASA Recipients of Financial Assistance Awards To 
Obtain a Quotation From Small and/or Minority Businesses, Women's 
Business Enterprises and Labor Surplus Area Firms

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Request for public comment on new term and condition that 
requires recipients of NASA financial assistance to obtain a quotation 
from small and/or minority businesses, women's business enterprises or 
labor surplus area firms; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) 
published a document in the Federal Register of February 23, 2022, 
concerning a request for public comment on new term and condition that 
requires recipients of NASA financial assistance to obtain a quotation 
from small and/or minority businesses, women's business enterprises or 
labor surplus area firms. The document contained an incorrect date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christiane S. Diallo, 
[email protected], (202) 358-5179.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    In the Federal Register of February 23, 2022, in [FR Doc. 2022-
03602], on page 10257, in the third column, correct the ``Dates'' 
caption to read:
    DATES: Comments must be received by April 24, 2022.

Nanette Smith,
Team Lead, NASA Directives and Regulations.
