Longwear Hosiery Mill, Inc., Hildebran, NC; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 23, 2005 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Longwear 
Hosiery Mill, Inc., Hildebran, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 30th day of March, 2005.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
