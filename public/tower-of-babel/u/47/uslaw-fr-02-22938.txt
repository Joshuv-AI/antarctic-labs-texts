DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


RTCA Special Committee 189/EUROCAE Working Group 53: Air Traffic 
Services (ATS) Safety and Interoperability Requirements

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of RTCA Special Committee 189/EUROCAE Working Group 53 
meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public of a 
meeting of RTCA Special Committee 189/EUROCAE Working Group 53: Air 
Traffic Services (ATS) Safety and Interoperability Requirements.

DATES: The meeting will be held September 16-20, 2002 starting at 9 
a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at Aeronautical Radio, Inc. 
(ARINC), 2551 Riva Road, Annapolis, MD 21401-7435.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: (1) RTCA Secretariat, 1828 L Street, 
NW., Suite 805, Washington, DC 20036; telephone (202) 833-9339; fax 
(202) 833-9434; Web site http://www.rtca.org; (2) Mr. Doug Blyth; (E-
mail) [email protected]; (Phone) 410-266-2982; (Fax) 410-266-2047.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal 
Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., Appendix 2), notice 
is hereby given for a Special Committee 189/EUROCAE Working Group 53 
meeting. Note: The plenary meeting on Monday and Friday will be held in 
Building 6, Room 6A2-3. Rooms will also be available for working group 
meetings. (1) At Building 6 use visitor's parking; (2) On arrival 
inside Building 6 stop at the security desk to sign-in and receive a 
name badge.
    The agenda will include:

[sbull] September 16:
[sbull] Opening Plenary Session (Welcome and Introductory Remarks, 
Review/Approval of Meeting Agenda, Review/Approval of Meeting Minutes)
[sbull] Sub-group and related reports; Position papers planned for 
plenary agreement; SC-189/WG-53 co-chair progress report
[sbull] September 17-19:
[sbull] PUB, Publications Integration Sub-group and Chair meetings
[sbull] INTEROP, Interoperability Sub-group
[sbull] ICSPR, Initial Continental Safety and Performance Requirements 
Sub-group
[sbull] IOSPR, Initial Oceanic Safety and Performance Requirements Sub-
group
[sbull] September 20:
[sbull] Closing Plenary Session (Welcome and Introductory Remarks, 
Review/Approval of Meeting Agenda)
[sbull] Sub-group and related reports; Position papers planned for 
plenary agreement; SC-189/WG-53 co-chair progress report and wrap-up

    Attendance is open to the interested public but limited to space 
availability. With the approval of the chairmen, members of the public 
may present oral statements at the meeting. Persons wishing to present 
statements or obtain information should contact the person listed in 
the FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT section. Members of the public may 
present a written statement to the committee at any time.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on September 3, 2002.
Janice L. Peters,
