FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-2149, MB Docket No. 02-101, RM-10429]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Reliance, SD

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Young Broadcasting of Sioux 
Falls, Inc., licensee of station KPLO-TV, substitutes DTV channel 13 
for DTV channel 14 at Reliance, South Dakota. See 67 FR 34670, May 15, 
2002. DTV channel 13 can be allotted to Reliance, South Dakota, in 
compliance with the principle community coverage requirements of 
Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 43-57-57 N. and 99-36-11 W. 
with a power of 40, HAAT of 338 meters and with a DTV service 
population of 53 thousand. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

DATES: Effective October 25, 2002.

418-1600.
