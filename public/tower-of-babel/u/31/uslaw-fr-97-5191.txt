Radio Broadcasting Services; Cloverdale, Montgomery, and Warrior, 
AL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for reconsideration filed by 
William P. Rogers that appeals the Report and Order, 60 FR 65021 
(December 18, 1995), in this proceeding insofar as it did not accept 
Rogers' counterproposal to allot Channel 254A to Florence, Alabama. The 
Report and Order was affirmed because Rogers' counterproposal did not 
provide 100 percent city-grade coverage of Florence, as required by 
Section 73.315(a) of the Commission's Rules.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 3, 1997.
