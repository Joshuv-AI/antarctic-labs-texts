SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

FEDERAL REGISTER CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: [61 FR 32668, June 
16, 1997]
STATUS: Closed/Open Meetings.

PLACE: 450 Fifth Street, N.W., Washington, D.C.

DATE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED: June 16, 1997.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Deletion/Cancellation.

    The following items were not considered at the closed meeting held 
on Monday, June 16, 1997:

Formal order of investigation.
Settlement of administrative proceedings of an enforcement nature.

    The open meeting scheduled for Wednesday, June 18, 1997, at 10:00 
a.m., has been canceled.
    Commissioner Wallman, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above changes and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact: The Office of the Secretary (202) 942-7070.

    Dated: June 17, 1997.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
