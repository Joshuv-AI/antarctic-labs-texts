Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-51,710]


Rayovac Corporation, Fennimore, Wisconsin; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 7, 2003 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Rayovac 
Corporation, Fennimore, Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
