Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Arkansas

    The plat of the dependent resurvey of a portion of the east, west 
and north (base line) boundaries, and a portion of the subdivisional 
lines of Township 1 South, Range 25 West, 5th Principal Meridian, 
Arkansas, will be officially filed in Eastern States, Springfield, 
Virginia at 7:30 a.m., on October 20, 1998.
    The survey was requested by the U.S. Forest Service.
    All inquiries or protests concerning the technical aspects of the 
survey must

[[Page 48520]]

be sent to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor, Eastern States, Bureau of Land 
Management, 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 22153, prior 
to 7:30 a.m., October 20, 1998.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the appropriate fee.

    Dated: September 2, 1998.
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief Cadastal Surveyor.
