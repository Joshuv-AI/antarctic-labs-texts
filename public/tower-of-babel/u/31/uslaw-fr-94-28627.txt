DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
 

Business Development Center Applications: Indianapolis, IN

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

ACTION: (Cancellation).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is canceling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) Program, to operate an MBDC in the 
Indianapolis Metropolitan Area. The previous announcement (closing 
date: August 31, 1994) was published in the Friday, July 15, 1994 
issue, Page 36164 of the Federal Register.

11.800 Minority Business Development Center
    (Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance)

    Dated: November 10, 1994.
Donald L. Powers,
Federal Register Liaison Officer, Minority Business Development Agency.
