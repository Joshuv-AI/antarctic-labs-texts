DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 6-8 April 1998.
    Time of Meeting: 0800-1700, 6-7 Apr 98; 0800-1300, 8 Apr 98.
    Place: Space & Missile Defense Command, Army Space Command--
Colorado Springs, CO.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) 1998 Summer Study Panel 
on ``Prioritizing Army Space Needs'' will meet for briefings and 
discussions concerning DoD space capabilities and limitations as 
well as current operational Reconnaissance, Intelligence, 
Surveillance and Target Acquisition programs and procedure. These 
meetings will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 
552b(c) of Title 5, U.S.C., specifically subparagraph (1) thereof, 
and Title 5, U.S.C., Appendix 2, subsection 10(d). The classified 
and unclassified matters to be discussed are so inextricably 
intertwined so as to preclude opening any portion of these meetings. 
For further information, please contact our office at (703) 604-
7490.
Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
