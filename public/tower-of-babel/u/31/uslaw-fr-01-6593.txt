NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Special Emphasis Panel in Undergraduate Education; Notice of 
Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Special Emphasis Panel in Undergraduate Education (1214).
    Date/Time: April 1-4, 2001; 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: Rooms 110, 320, 330, 370, 375, 390, 1235, National 
Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Dr. Joan Prival, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230. Telephone: (703) 292-
4635.
    Purpose of Meeting: To convene Principal Investigators from 32 
CETP projects to disseminate best practices in science and 
mathematics teacher preparation.
    Agenda: Presentations on accomplishments of teacher preparation 
projects and undergraduate course reform in science, mathematics, 
engineering, and technology.

    Dated: March 13, 2001.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
