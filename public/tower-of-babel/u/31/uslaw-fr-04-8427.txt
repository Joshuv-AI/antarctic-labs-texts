SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Public Federal Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Roundtable, Region 
V Regulatory Fairness Board

    The Small Business Administration Region V Regulatory Fairness 
Board and the SBA Office of the National Ombudsman will hold a Public 
Roundtable on Thursday, April 29, 2004, at 1 p.m. at the Radisson Hotel 
Lansing, Michigan Room 2 & 3, 111 N. Grand Avenue, Lansing, MI 48933, 
to provide small business owners and representatives of trade 
associations with an opportunity to share information concerning the 
federal regulatory enforcement and compliance environment.
    Anyone wishing to attend or to make a presentation must contact 
Constance Logan in writing or by fax, in order to be put on the agenda. 
Constance Logan, Senior Manager Economic Development, SBA Michigan 
District Office, 477 Michigan Avenue, Suite 515, Detroit, MI 48226, 
phone (313) 226-6075 Ext. 279, fax (313) 226-4769, e-mail: 
[email protected].
    For more information, see our Web site at http://www.sba.gov/ombudsman.

    Dated: April 8, 2004.
Peter Sorum,
Senior Advisor, Office of the National Ombudsman.
