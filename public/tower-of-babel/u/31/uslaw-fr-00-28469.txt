DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2203-007]


Alabama Power Company; Notice of Availability of Final 
Environmental Assessment

November 1, 2000.
    In accordance with the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 
and the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's (Commission) 
regulations, 18 CFR Part 380 (Order No. 486, 52 F.R. 47897), the Office 
of Energy Projects has reviewed the application for license amendment 
for the Holt Project, located on the Black Warrior River in Tuscaloosa 
County, Alabama and has prepared a Final Environmental Assessment (FEA) 
for the project.
    Copies of the FEA are available for review at the Commission's 
Public Reference Room, located at 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426, or by calling (202) 208-1371. The FEA may be viewed on the web 
at http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/rims.htm (call (202) 208-2222 for 
assistance).
    For further information, contact Steve Kartalia at (202) 219-2942.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
