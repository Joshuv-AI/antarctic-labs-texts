DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-14-2018]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 158--Vicksburg/Jackson, Mississippi; 
Authorization of Production Activity; International Converter 
(Insulation Facer); Iuka, Mississippi

    On February 23, 2018, International Converter submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facility within FTZ 158, in Iuka, Mississippi.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (83 FR 8965-8966, March 2, 2018). On 
June 25, 2018, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision 
that no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
Section 400.14.

    Dated: June 25, 2018.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
