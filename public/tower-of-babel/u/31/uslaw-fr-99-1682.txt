NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Public Hearing

    The National Transportation Safety Board will convene a public 
hearing beginning at 9:00 a.m., local time on Wednesday, February 3, 
1999, at the Charleston Hilton Hotel, 4770 Goer Drive, North 
Charleston, South Carolina 29406-6543 concerning the Sinking of the 
pleasure Morning Dew on December 29, 1997. For more information, 
contact Donald Tyrrell, NTSB Office of Marine Safety at (202) 314-6455 
or Matt Furman, NTSB Office of Public Affairs at (202) 314-6100.

    Dated: January 20, 1999.
Rhonda Underwood,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
