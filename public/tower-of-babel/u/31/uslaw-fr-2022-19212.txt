DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Human Genome Research Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Council for Human Genome Research, September 19, 2022, 09:00 
a.m. to September 20, 2022, 04:00 p.m., NHGRI, 6700B Rockledge Drive, 
Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 12, 2022, 87 FR 49873.
    This notice is being amended to remove the visitor testing 
requirement for entering NIH facilities due to CDC updates published 
August 11, 2022, regarding screening testing. The meeting is partially 
Closed to the public.

    Dated: August 31, 2022.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
