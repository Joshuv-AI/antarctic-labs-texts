NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

Consideration of Valve Mispositioning in Pressurized-Water 
Reactors; Issued

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of issuance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has issued Generic 
Letter 89-10, Supplement 7 to notify licensees of nuclear power 
reactors that the NRC is removing the recommendation that motor 
operated valve (MOV) mispositioning be considered by pressurized-water 
reactor licensees in responding to Generic Letter 89-10, ``Safety-
Related Motor-Operated Valve Testing and Surveillance,'' as was done 
for boiling-water reactor licensees in Supplement 4. Although this 
generic letter supplement forwards a new NRC position, no specific 
action or written response is required. This generic letter is 
available in the Public Document Rooms under accession number 
9601190442.

DATES: The generic letter was issued on January 24, 1996.

ADDRESSEES: Not applicable.
