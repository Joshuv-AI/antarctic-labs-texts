FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT ROAD

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., August 15, 1994.

PLACE: 4th Floor, Conference Room, 1250 H Street, N.W., Washington, 
D.C.

STATUS: Open.

Matters to be Considered:

    1. Approval of the minutes of the July 18, 1994, Board meeting.
    2. Thrift Savings Plan activity report by the Executive 
Director.
    3. Review of investment policy.
    4. Review of Arthur Andersen semiannual review.
    5. Review of KPMG Peat Marwick audit report ``Pension and 
Welfare Benefits Administration Review of U.S. Department of 
Treasury Operations Relating to the Thrift Savings Plan Investments 
in the Government Securities Fund.''

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Tom Trabucco, Director, Office of 
External Affairs, (202) 942-1640.

    Dated: July 28, 1994.
Roger W. Mehle,
Executive Director, Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.
