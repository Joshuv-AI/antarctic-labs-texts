May 15, 2003.
    On May 1, 2003, American Electric Power Service Corporation, 
Commonwealth Edison Company, Dayton Power and Light Company, Virginia 
Electric and Power Company, and PJM Interconnection, LLC (collectively, 
New PJM Companies) filed a compliance filing in the above-docketed 
proceeding. This compliance filing was noticed by the Commission on May 
8, 2003 and established a May 22, 2003 comment deadline. On May 6, 2003 
and May 7, 2003, the New PJM Companies filed erratas to their original 
compliance filing. The Commission issued a notice on May 13, 2003, 
establishing a May 28, 2003, deadline for filing comments on the second 
errata filing. This notice amends the prior notices issued in these 
dockets and establishes a new date for filing comments.
    Notice is hereby given that comments, protests and motions in 
response to the original compliance filing and the two erratas filed in 
this subdocket on May 6 and May 7, 2003, should be filed on or before 
June 4, 2003.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
