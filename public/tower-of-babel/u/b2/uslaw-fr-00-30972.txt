FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 00-2681, MM Docket No. 00-97; RM-9865]


Digital Television Broadcast Services; Richmond, VA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Central Virginia Educational 
Telecommunications Corporation, licensee of noncommercial station WCVE-
TV, substitutes DTV Channel *42 for station WCVE-TV's assigned DTV 
Channel *24a at Richmond, Virginia. See 65 FR 36808, June 12, 2000. DTV 
Channel *42 can be allotted to Richmond at coordinates ( 37-30-46 N. 
and 77-36-06 W.) with a power of 100, HAAT of 327 meters and with a DTV 
service population of 1097 thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATE: Effective January 16, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
