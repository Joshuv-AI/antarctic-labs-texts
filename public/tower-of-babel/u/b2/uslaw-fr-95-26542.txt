DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Notice of Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: December 14 & 15, 1995.
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1700, December 14, 1995, 1000-1700, 
December 15, 1995.
    Place: Pentagon--Washington, DC.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Ad Hoc Study on ``The 
Impact of Information Warfare on Army Command, Control, 
Communications, Computers and Intelligence (C4I) Systems'' will meet 
to hear briefings relative to the subject under study. These 
meetings will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 
552b(c) of title 5, U.S.C., specifically subparagraph (4) thereof, 
and Title 5, U.S.C., Appendix 2, subsection 10(d). The proprietary 
matters to be discussed are so inextricably intertwined so as to 
preclude opening any portion of these meetings. For further 
information, please contact Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
