FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 99-2000; MM Docket No. 99-121; RM-9552]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Eagle Nest, New Mexico

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the request of Mountain West 
Broadcasting to allot Channel 284C2 to Eagle Nest, New Mexico, finding 
that it is not a community for allotment purposes. See 64 FR 18872, 
April 16, 1999. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
