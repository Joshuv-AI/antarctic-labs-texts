OFFICE OF MANAGEMENT AND BUDGET

 
Budget Rescissions and Deferrals

To the Congress of the United States:

    In accordance with the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control 
Act of 1974, I herewith report one revised deferral of budget 
authority, totaling $7.3 million.
    The deferral affects the Department of Health and Human Services. 
The details of the revised deferral is contained in the attached 
report.
William J. Clinton.
The White House,
    May 2, 1994.
