OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT

Submission for OMB Review: Comment Request for Review of a 
Revised Information Collection: RI 38-107

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management has submitted to the Office of Management and 
Budget a request for review of a revised information collection. RI 38-
107, Verification of Who is Getting Payments, is used to verify that 
the entitled person is indeed receiving the monies payable. Failure to 
collect this information would cause OPM to pay monies absent the 
assurance of a correct payee.
    We estimate 25,400 RI 38-107 forms are completed annually. Each 
form takes approximately 10 minutes to complete. The annual estimated 
burden is 4,234 hours.
