Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,908]


DJ Fashions, LLC, New York, NY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 11, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of the workers at DJ Fashions, 
LLC, New York, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.


[[Page 30643]]


    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of May 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
