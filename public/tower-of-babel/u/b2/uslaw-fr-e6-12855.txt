FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 06-1484; MB Docket No. 05-184]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Aspen and Leadville, CO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, on its on motion, deletes Channel 228A at 
Leadville, Colorado to resolve existing distance spacing conflicts. It 
is Commission policy to refrain from maintaining an allotment in 
instances were there are no bona fide expressions of interest.

DATES: Effective September 5, 2006.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
