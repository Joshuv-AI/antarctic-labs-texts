DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given to amend the notice of the National Cancer 
Advisory Board Subcommittee on Activities and Agenda meeting which was 
published in the Federal Register (60 FR 33425) on June 28, 1995 to add 
a closed session.
    The Subcommittee meeting is scheduled to be open on July 14, 1995 
from 9 am to approximately 2:30 pm. In accordance with provisions set 
forth in sec. 552b(c)(9)(B), Title 5, U.S.C., and sec. 10(d) of Pub. L. 
92-463, the meeting will be closed from approximately 2:30 pm to 
adjournment for discussion of pending confidential budget matters. 
These discussions could disclose information, the premature disclosure 
of which would be likely to significantly frustrate implementation of a 
proposed agency action.

    Dated: July 6, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
