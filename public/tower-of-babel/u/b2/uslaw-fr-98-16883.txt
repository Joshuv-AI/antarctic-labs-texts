DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER98-2600-000]


Lakeport Hydroelectric Associates, Lakeport Hydroelectric Corp.; 
Notice of Withdrawal

June 19, 1998.
    Take notice that on June 16, 1998, Lakeport Hydroelectric 
Associates & Lakeport Hydroelectric Corp., tendered for filing Notice 
of Withdrawal of its filing made on April 20, 1998, in Docket No. ER98-
2600-000.
    A copy of the notice is being served upon Public Service Company of 
New Hampshire and New Hampshire Public Utilities Commission.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions and protests should be filed on or before July 1, 1998. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission to determine the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
