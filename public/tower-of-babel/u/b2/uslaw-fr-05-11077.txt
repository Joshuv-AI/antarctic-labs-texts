DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 23, 2005, 12:00 p.m. to 
June 23, 2005, 1:00 p.m., The Watergate, 2650 Virginia Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on May 
18, 2005, 70 FR 28549-23552.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the reassignment of the 
applications.

    Dated: May 25, 2005.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
