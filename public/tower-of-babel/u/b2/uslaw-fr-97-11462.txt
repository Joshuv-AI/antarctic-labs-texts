Administrative Review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Commerce (``the Department'') has received a 
request to conduct a new shipper administrative review of the 
antidumping duty order on certain forged stainless steel flanges from 
India. In accordance with 19 CFR Sec. 353.22(h), we are initiating this 
administrative review.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 2, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Thomas Killiam or John Kugelman,

respectively.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On February 24, 1997, the Department received a request, pursuant 
to section 751(a)(2)(B) of the Act and in accordance with 19 CFR 
Sec. 353.22(h), for a new shipper review of the antidumping duty order 
on certain forged stainless steel flanges from India, which has a 
February anniversary date. The request for a new shipper review did not 
include the necessary certifications pursuant to 19 CFR 
Sec. 353.22(h)(2). Pursuant to our instructions, Viraj supplemented its 
request on March 18 and April 1, 1997, to include the appropriate 
certifications.

Initiation of Review

    In accordance with section 751(a)(2)(B) (ii) of the Act and 19 CFR 
Sec. 353.22(h)(6), we are initiating a new shipper review of the 
antidumping duty order on certain forged stainless steel flanges from 
India. We intend to issue the final results of this review not later 
than 270 days from the date of publication of this notice.
