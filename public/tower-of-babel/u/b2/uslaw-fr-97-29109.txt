DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


National Register of Historic Places; Notification of Pending 
Nominations

    Nominations for the following properties being considered for 
listing in the National Register were received by the National Park 
Service before October 25, 1997. Pursuant to section 60.13 of 36 CFR 
Part 60 written

[[Page 59729]]

comments concerning the significance of these properties under the 
National Register criteria for evaluation may be forwarded to the 
National Register, National Park Service, P.O. Box 37127, Washington, 
D.C. 20013-7127. Written comments should be submitted by November 19, 
1997.
Carol D. Shull,
Keeper of the National Register.
