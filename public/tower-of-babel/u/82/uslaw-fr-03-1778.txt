FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting Notice; Announcing an Open Meeting of the 
Board

Time and Date: 3 p.m. Wednesday, January 29, 2003.

Place: Board Room, Second Floor, Federal Housing Finance Board, 1777 F 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20006.

[[Page 3531]]


Status: The entire meeting will be open to the public.

Matters to be Considered:
    [sbull] Rescission of Finance Board Resolutions Governing the 
Federal Home Loan Bank of Atlanta's Affordable Multifamily 
Participation Program.
    [sbull] Community Investment Cash Advance--Approval of Claritas, 
Inc. as Data Source for Determining Area Median Incomes.
    [sbull] Appointment of Federal Home Loan Bank Directors.
    [sbull] Delegation of Authority.

Contact Person for More Information: Elaine L. Baker, Secretary to the 
Board, (202) 408-2837.

Arnold Intrater,
General Counsel
