STATE JUSTICE INSTITUTE


SJI Board of Directors Meeting, Notice

AGENCY: State Justice Institute

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The SJI Board of Directors will be meeting on Monday, August 
29, 2022 at 1:00 p.m. CT. The purpose of this meeting is to consider 
grant applications for the 4th quarter of FY 2022, and other business.

Street, Chicago, IL, 60601.
