 DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Docket 8-2006


Foreign-Trade Zone 202 - Los Angeles, CA, Application for 
Subzone, Sharp Electronics Corporation, Correction

    The Federal Register notice (71 FR 12676, 3/13/2006) describing the 
application by the Board of Harbor Commissioners of the City of Los 
Angeles, grantee of FTZ 202, requesting special-purpose subzone status 
for the Sharp Electronics Corporation (Sharp) distribution facility, in 
Huntington Beach, California, is corrected as follows:
    Paragraph 2 should read ``The Sharp facility (539,000 sq. ft. of 
enclosed space on 23.4 acres) is located at 5901 Bolsa Avenue, 
Huntington Beach, California.''

    Dated: March 20, 2006.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Executive Secretary.
