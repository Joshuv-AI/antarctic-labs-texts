SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Regulations at 50 CFR 648.8 and 697.8 require that vessels, over 25 
ft (7.6 m) in registered length with Federal permits to fish in the 
Northeast, display the vessel's name and official number. The name and 
number must be of a specific size at specified locations. The vessel's 
name must be affixed to the port and starboard sides of the bow and, if 
possible, on its stern. The official number must be displayed on the 
port and starboard sides of the deckhouse or hull, and on an 
appropriate weather deck so as to be clearly visible from enforcement 
vessels and aircraft that aids in fishery law enforcement.
