FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[IB Docket No. 16-185; DA 20-1162; FRS 17134]


Second Meeting of the World Radiocommunication Conference 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, this 
notice advises interested persons that the second meeting of the World 
Radiocommunication Conference Advisory Committee (WAC) will be held on 
October 20, 2020. Due to exceptional circumstances, the Advisory 
Committee meeting will be convened as a virtual meeting with remote 
participation only. This second meeting will consider status reports 
and recommendations from its Informal Working Groups (IWG) concerning 
preparation for the 2023 World Radiocommunication Conference (WRC-23).

DATES: October 20, 2020; 11:00 a.m. EDT.

ADDRESSES: www.fcc.gov/live.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dante Ibarra, Designated Federal 
Official, World Radiocommunication Conference Advisory Committee, FCC 
International Bureau, Global Strategy and Negotiation Division, at 
[email protected], (202) 418-0610 or [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The FCC established the Advisory Committee 
to provide advice, technical support and recommendations relating to 
the preparation of United States proposals and positions for the 2023 
World Radiocommunication Conference (WRC-23).
    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, Public Law 
92-463, as amended, this notice advises interested persons of the 
second meeting of the Advisory Committee. Additional information 
regarding the Advisory Committee is available on the Advisory 
Committee's website, www.fcc.gov/wrc-23. The virtual meeting is open to 
the public. The meeting will be broadcast live with open captioning 
over the internet from the FCC Live web page at www.fcc.gov/live. There 
will be audience participation available; send live questions to 
[email protected] only during this meeting.
    The proposed agenda for the second meeting is as follows:
