DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
48 CFR Part 13

[FAR Case 90-32]

 

Federal Acquisition Regulation; U.S. Government Credit Cards

AGENCIES: Department of Defense (DOD), General Services Administration 
(GSA), and National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense, General Services Administration, 
and National Aeronautics and Space Administration have decided to 
withdraw a proposed rule, FAR case 90-32, U.S. Government Credit Cards, 
in light of the pending legislation on streamlining of the procurement 
process. This rule was published in the Federal Register on June 27, 
1990 (55 FR 26342).
