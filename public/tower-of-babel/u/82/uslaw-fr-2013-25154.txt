DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Student Assistance General Provisions--Subpart K--Cash 
Management; Extension of Public Comment Period; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 3, 2013 the U.S. Department of Education published 
a 30-day comment period notice in the Federal Register (Page 61346, 
Column 3 and Page 61347, Column 1 and 2) seeking public comment for an 
information collection entitled, ``Student Assistance General 
Provisions--Subpart K--Cash Management''. ED is extending the comment 
period to November 18, 2013 due to the public's inability to access the 
collection at the beginning of the comment period.
    The Acting Director, Information Collection Clearance Division, 
Privacy, Information and Records Management Services, Office of 
Management, hereby issues a correction notice as required by the 
Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

    Dated: October 22, 2013.
Stephanie Valentine,
Acting Director, Information Collection Clearance Division, Privacy, 
Information and Records Management Services, Office of Management.
