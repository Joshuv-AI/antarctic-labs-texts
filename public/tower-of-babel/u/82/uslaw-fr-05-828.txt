FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 04-4057, MB Docket No. 04-182, RM-10963]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Great Falls, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Montana State University, 
allots DTV channel *21 for noncommercial use at Great Falls, Montana. 
See 69 FR 30853, June 1, 2004. DTV channel *21 can be allotted to Great 
Falls, Montana, in compliance with the minimum geographic spacing 
requirements of Section 73.623(d) at reference coordinates 47-32-08 N. 
and 111-17-02 W. Since the community of Great Falls is located within 
400 kilometers of the U.S.-Canadian border, concurrence from the 
Canadian government was obtained for this

[[Page 2572]]

allotment. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective February 18, 2005.
