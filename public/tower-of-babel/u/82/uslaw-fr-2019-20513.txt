DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 10898]


Determination Pursuant to Section 7041(a)(3)(B) of the Department 
of State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 
2018

    By virtue of the authority vested in me as Secretary of State 
pursuant to section 7041(a)(3)(B) of the Department of State, Foreign 
Operations, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2018 (Div. K, Pub. 
L. 115-141) (the ``Act''), I hereby determine that it is important to 
the National Security interest of the United States to waive the 
certification requirement under section 7041(a)(3)(A) of the Act. I 
hereby waive that requirement.
    This determination shall be published in the Federal Register and 
shall be reported to Congress, along with the accompanying Memorandum 
of Justification.

    Dated: August 8, 2019.
Michael R. Pompeo,
Secretary of State.
