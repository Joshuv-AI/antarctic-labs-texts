Kentucky Apparel LLP; Amended Certification Regarding Eligibility 
to Apply for Worker Adjustment Assistance

    In accordance with Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 USC 
2273) the Department of Labor issued a Certification of Eligibility to 
Apply for Worker Adjustment Assistance on April 29, 1996, applicable to 
all workers of Kentucky Apparel LLP, located in Burkesville, Kentucky. 
The notice was published in the Federal Register on May 17, 1996 (61 FR 
24960).
    At the request of the petitioners, the Department reviewed the 
certification for workers of the subject firm. New information received 
by the company shows that worker separations did occur

[[Page 38581]]

during January, 1997 at the Gamaliel, Kentucky facility of Kentucky 
Apparel LLP. The workers produce denim jeans.
    The intent of the Department's certification is to include all 
workers of Kentucky Apparel LLP who were adversely affected by 
increased imports of jeans. Accordingly, the Department is amending the 
certification to cover the workers of Kentucky Apparel LLP, Gamaliel, 
Kentucky.
    The amended notice applicable to TA-W-32,178 is hereby issued as 
follows:

    All workers of Kentucky Apparel LLP, Burkesville, Kentucky (TA-
W-32,178) and Gamaliel, Kentucky (TA-W-32,178F) who became totally 
or partially separated from employment on or after March 11, 1995 
are eligible to apply for adjustment assistance under Section 223 of 
the Trade Act of 1974.

    Signed at Washington D.C. this 27th day of June, 1997.
Russell T. Kile,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment.
