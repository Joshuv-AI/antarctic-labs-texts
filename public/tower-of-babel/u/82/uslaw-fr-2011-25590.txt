Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2011-0824.
    Petitioner: American Eurocopter. Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR 
60 Table C2A, paragraph 4.b.3.
    Description of Relief Sought: American Eurocopter is seeking relief 
from the requirement that any geometric error between the image 
generator eye point and the pilot eye point must be 8 degrees or less 
for the EC175 Level D FFS currently being designed. American Eurocopter 
requests that a geometric error of 10 to 12 degrees be allowed subject 
to justification and agreement with the National Simulator Program 
Manager (NSPM) in accordance with helicopter characteristics and 
training needs.
