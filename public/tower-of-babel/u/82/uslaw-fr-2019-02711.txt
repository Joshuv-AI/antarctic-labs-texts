DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2169-114]


Notice of Comment Period Extension; Brookfield Smoky Mountain 
Hydropower LLC, Brookfield Smoky Mountain Hydropower LP

    On January 8, 2019, the Commission issued a notice setting February 
7, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments, motions to 
intervene, and protests on the transfer of license application for the 
Tapoco Hydroelectric Project No. 2169. Due to the funding lapse at 
certain federal agencies between December 22, 2018 and January 25, 
2019, the Commission is extending the comment period until February 25, 
2019.

    Dated: February 11, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
