NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION

Special Emphasis Panel in Research, Evaluation and Dissemination; 
Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Special Emphasis Panel in Research, Evaluation and 
Dissemination.
    Date and Time: June 5, 1995; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; June 6, 1995; 
8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; June 7, 1995; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; June 8, 
1995; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; June 9, 1995; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: Rooms 320, 370, and 375, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Closed.
    Contact Person: Dr. Nora Sabelli, Program Director, 4201 Wilson 
Boulevard, Room 855, Arlington, VA 22230. Telephone (703) 306-1651.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning proposals submitted to NSF for financial support.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate proposals and provide advice and 
recommendations as part of the selection process for proposals 
submitted to the Networking Infrastructure for Education Program.
    Reason for Closing: Because the proposals being reviewed include 
information of a proprietary or confidential nature, including 
technical information; financial data, such as salaries; and 
personal information concerning individuals associated with 
proposals, the meetings are closed to the public. These matters are 
within exemptions (4) and (6) of 5 U.S.C. 552b(c), Government in the 
Sunshine Act.

    Dated: May 15, 1995.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
