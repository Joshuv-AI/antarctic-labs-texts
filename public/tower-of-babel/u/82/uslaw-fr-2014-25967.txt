POSTAL SERVICE


Product Change--Standard Mail Negotiated Service Agreement

AGENCY: Postal Service.TM

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing a request with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add a negotiated service agreement 
regarding First Class Mail and Standard Mail to the market-dominant 
product list within the Mail Classification Schedule.

DATES: Effective date: October 31, 2014.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Valerie J. Pelton, 202-268-3049.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice that on October 27, 2014, pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 
3642 and 3622(c)(10), it filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission a 
Notice of the United States Postal Service of Filing of Contract and 
Supporting Data and Request to Add, Discover Financial Services 
Negotiated Service Agreement to the Market-Dominant Product List. 
Documents are available at www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. MC2015-3, R2015-2.
