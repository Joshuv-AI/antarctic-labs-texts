DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Recombinant DNA Advisory Committee; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Recombinant DNA 
Advisory Committee, January 7, 1999, 5:50 p.m. to 9:30 p.m., Hyatt 
Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro Center, Bethesda, Maryland 20814, 
which was published in the Federal Register on December 22, 1998, 63 FR 
70789.
    The meeting was canceled due to lack of agenda items.

    Dated: January 6, 1999.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, National Institutes of Health.
