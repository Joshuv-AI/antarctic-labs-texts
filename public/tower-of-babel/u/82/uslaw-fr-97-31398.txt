December 9, 1997 Board of Directors Meeting; Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE: Tuesday, December 9, 1997, 1:00 p.m. (Open Portion); 
1:30 p.m. (Closed Portion).

PLACE: Offices of the Corporation, Twelfth Floor Board Room, 1100 New 
York Avenue, N.W., Washington, D.C.

2. Testimonial
3. Approval of September 16, 1997 Minutes (Open Portion)

1. Finance Project in Jamaica
2. Insurance Project India
3. Approval of September 16, 1997 Minutes (Closed Portion)

5. Report on OPIC's Small Business Initiative
