DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 1930]

 

United States Organization for the International Telegraph and 
Telephone Consultative Committee (CCITT); Meeting Cancellation

    The United States Organization for the International Telegraph and 
Telephone Consultative Committee (CCITT) published notices of meetings 
in the Federal Register, Vol. 58, No. 218, Monday, November 15, 1993 
which included a scheduled meeting on January 11, 1994.
    The CCITT meeting dealing with Preparations for the Kyoto 
Plenipotentiary scheduled for January 11, 1994 is hereby cancelled. 
Hereafter, these meetings will be held under the International 
Telecommunications Advisory Committee, which will incorporate the 
CCITT, upon completion of the steps necessary to establish and provide 
required notices in accordance with Federal Register rules and 
regulations. For further information, contact the Office of William 
Jahn (202) 647-2592.

    Dated: December 27, 1993.
Earl S. Barbely,
Director, Telecommunications and Information Standards, Chairman U.S. 
CCITT.
