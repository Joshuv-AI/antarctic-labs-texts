DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. CP96-152-000, RP95-212, et al. PR94-3-000]


Riverside Pipeline Company, KansOk Partnership, et al., KansOk 
Partnership; Notice of Public Conference

June 14, 1996.
    Take notice that on June 25, 1996, at 10:00 am, the Commission 
Staff will convene a public conference in the above captioned dockets 
for the parties to discuss the resolution of the issues arising from 
Riverside's certificate application in Docket No. CP96-152-000, as well 
as all other issues in the referenced dockets.
    The conference will be held at the office of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 1st Street NE, Washington, D.C., 20426, in 
Room 3M-2B. All interested parties are invited to attend. However, 
attendance at the conference will not confer party status.

    For further information, contact George Dornbusch (202) 208-
0881, Office of Pipeline Regulation, Room 81-31.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
