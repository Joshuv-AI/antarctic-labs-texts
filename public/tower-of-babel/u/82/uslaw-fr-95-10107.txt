DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Division of Research Grants; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting May 8-9, 1995, 
starting at 8:30 a.m., Building 31C, Conference Room 6, of the Division 
of Research Grants Advisory Committee, which was published in the 
Federal Register on April 3 (60 FR 16880).
    This committee was to have convened at 8:30 a.m. on May 8 to 
adjournment on May 9, but has been changed to May 10-11, 1995, Building 
31C, Conference Room 6, National Institutes of Health, 9000 Rockville 
Pike, Bethesda, Maryland 20892.
    The meeting will be open to the public from 8:30 a.m. on May 10 to 
adjournment on May 11.

    Dated: April 18, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
