Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

    Petitions for reconsideration and clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of these 
documents are available for viewing and copying in Room 239, 1919 M 
Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. or may be purchased from the 
Commission's copy contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to 
these petitions must be filed November 28, 1997. See Section 1.4(b)(1) 
of the Commission's rule (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition 
must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has 
expired.
