Public Utilities Providing Service in California Under Sellers' 
Choice Contracts; Notice of Initiation of Proceeding and Refund 
Effective Date

June 22, 2004.
    On June 17, 2004, the Commission issued an order in the above-
referenced dockets initiating a proceeding in Docket No. EL04-108-000 
under section 206 of the Federal Power Act concerning issues related to 
sellers' choice contracts.
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL04-108-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the Federal Power Act will be 60 days 
following publication of this notice in the Federal Register.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
