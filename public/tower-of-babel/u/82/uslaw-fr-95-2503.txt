membership for 1995, the alternate members were inadvertently omitted.

Document 95-1382 (filed 1-19-95) under Section 4190 of the Federal 

[[Page 6553]]
Register published January 20, 1995, to add the alternate members as 
follows:

Scott Smith, Alternate SFS Member
