DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR


National Register of Historic Places; Notification of Pending 
Nominations

    Nominations for the following properties being considered for 
listing in the National Register were received by the National Park 
Service before January 14, 1995. Pursuant to Sec. 60.13 of 36 CFR part 
60 written comments concerning the significance of these properties 
under the National Register criteria for evaluation may be forwarded to 
the National Register, National Park Service, P.O. Box 37127, 
Washington, DC 20013-7127. Written comments should be submitted by 
February 9, 1995.
Carol D. Shull,
Chief of Registration, National Register.
