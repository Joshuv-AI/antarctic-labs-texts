Minority Business Development Agency Business Development Center 
Applications: Miami, FL
AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) Program to operate the Miami MBDC. 
This solicitation was originally published in the Federal Register, 
Wednesday, February 15, 1995, Vol. 60. No. 31, 8639.
