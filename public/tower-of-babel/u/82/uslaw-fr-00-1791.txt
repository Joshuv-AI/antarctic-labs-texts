DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Sunshine Act Meeting

January 19, 2000.
    The following notice of meeting is published pursuant to section 
3(a) of the Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 
U.S.C 552B:

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

DATE AND TIME: January 26, 2000, 10:00 a.m.

PLACE: Room 2C, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Agenda (Note--Items listed on the agenda 
may be deleted without further notice).

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: David P. Boergers, Secretary, 
Telephone (202) 208-0400. For a recording listing items stricken 
from or added to the meeting, call (202) 208-1627.

    This is a list of matters to be considered by the commission. It 
does not include a listing of all papers relevant to the items on 
the agenda; however, all public documents may be examined in the 
reference and information center.
