SUMMARY: The Forest Service published a document in the Federal 
Register of April 13, 2011, concerning a notice of meeting for the 
Central Montana Resource Advisory Committee. The document contained an 
incorrect date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ron Wiseman, 406-566-2292.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of April 13, 2011, in FR Doc. 2011-9006, on 
page 20624, in the second column, correcrt the DATES caption to read: 
The meeting will be held April 28, 2011 and will begin at 7 p.m.
