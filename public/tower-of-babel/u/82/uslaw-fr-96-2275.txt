FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 95-136; RM-8682]


Television Broadcasting Services; Sioux Falls, SD

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.


[[Page 4233]]

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Red River Broadcast Corp., 
allots UHF Television Channel 46 at Sioux Falls, South Dakota, as 
potentially the community's sixth local television broadcast service. 
See 60 FR 45390, August 31, 1995. We will also allow petitioner to 
amend its application (BPCT-941227KI) to specify operation on Channel 
46 in lieu of Channel 36+ and retain its cut-off protection. Channel 
46, with zero offset, can be allotted at Sioux Falls in compliance with 
the Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at city 
reference coordinates. The coordinates for Channel 46 are North 
Latitude 43-32-30 and West Longitude 96-44-00. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 11, 1996.
