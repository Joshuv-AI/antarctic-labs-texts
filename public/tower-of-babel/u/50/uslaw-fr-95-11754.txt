FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM

Farmers & Merchants Bank Employee Stock Ownership Plan; Change in 
Bank Control Notices; Acquisitions of Shares of Banks or Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 95-11754) published on page 
25723 of the issue for Friday, May 12, 1995.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta heading, the entry for 
Farmers & Merchants Bank Employee Stock Ownership Plan, is revised to 
read as follows:
    1. Farmers & Merchants Bank Employee Stock Ownership Plan, Forest, 
Mississippi; to acquire Bankers Capital Corporation, Forest, 
Mississippi, and thereby engage in making, acquiring or servicing loans 
or other extensions of credit, pursuant to Sec. 225.25(b)(1) of the 
Board's Regulation Y. The proposed activity will be conducted 
throughout the United States.
    Comments on this application must be received by May 26, 1995.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, May 22, 1995.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
