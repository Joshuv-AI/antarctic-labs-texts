SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

``FEDERAL REGISTER'' CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: [59 FR 8682, 
February 23, 1994].

STATUS Closed meeting.

PLACE: 450 Fifth Street, N.W., Washington, D.C.

DATE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED: Thursday, February 17, 1994.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Time Change.
    The closed meeting scheduled for Wednesday, February 23, 1994 at 
4:00 p.m., was changed to Wednesday, February 23, 1994 at 11:30 a.m.
    Commissioner Beese, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above change and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact: John Ramsey at (202) 272-2100.

    Dated: February 23, 1994.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
