DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL14-72-000]


UNS Electric, Inc.; Notice of Institution of Section 206 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On July 17, 2014, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL14-72-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2012), instituting an investigation into the justness and 
reasonableness of UNS Electric, Inc.'s (UNS Electric) lack of formula 
rate protocols under UNS Electric's Open Access Transmission Tariff. 
UNS Electric, Inc., 148 FERC ] 61,032 (2014).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL14-72-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: July 17, 2014.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
