SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3548]


District of Columbia (Amendment 1)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective 
September 29, 2003, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning on 
September 18, 2003 and continuing through September 29, 2003.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 19, 2003, and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 21, 2004.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: October 1, 2003.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
