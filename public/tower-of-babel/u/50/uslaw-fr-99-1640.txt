47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 90-318, RM-7311, 7516]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Chillicothe, Forest, Lima, New 
Washington, Peebles and Reynoldsburg, Ohio

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final Rule; Petition for Reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Pearl Broadcasting, Inc., this document 
dismisses the Petition for Reconsideration filed by Pearl Broadcasting, 
Inc. of the Report and Order, 61 FR 44288 (Aug. 28, 1996) which denied 
Pearl's request to change the community of license of Station WKKJ(FM), 
Channel 227B from Chillicothe to Reynoldsburg, Ohio and denied proposed 
allotments at Peebles, Forest and Lima, Ohio. The Commission determined 
that the request for dismissal complied with the requirements of 
Sec. 1.420(j) of the Commission's Rules. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 25, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Arthur D. Scrutchins, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.
