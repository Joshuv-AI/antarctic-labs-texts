Notice of Availability of Government-Owned Inventions; Available

SUMMARY: The invention listed below is assigned to the United States 
Government as represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available 
for licensing by the Department of the Navy. Patent application 14/
006,530: HIGH EFFICIENCY COMBUSTER AND CLOSED-CYCLE HEAT ENGINE 
INTERFACE. Powering system wherein heat-transfer liquid decouples an 
engine and combustor.

47522-5001.

47522-5001, telephone 812-854-4100.
