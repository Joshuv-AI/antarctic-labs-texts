Safety Standard for Crib Mattresses

CFR Correction

    This rule is being published by the Office of the Federal Register 
to correct an editorial or technical error that appeared in the most 
recent annual revision of the Code of Federal Regulations.

0
In Title 16 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 1000 to End, 
revised as of January 1, 2025, in Sec.  1241.2, in paragraph (a), 
remove the term ``http://www.astm.org/READINGROOM/'' and add ``https://www.astm.org/READINGLIBRARY/'' in its place.
