Federal Mine Safety and Health Review Commission

TIME AND DATE: 11:00 a.m., Tuesday, April 12, 1994.

PLACE: 6th Floor, 1730 K Street, NW., Washington, DC.

STATUS: Closed [Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. Sec. 552b(c)(10)].

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The Commission will consider and act upon the 
following:

    1. In re: Southmountain Coal Co., No. 94-1388, 4th Cir. Petition 
for Writ of Mandamus (Issues include consideration of court order.)

    It was determined by a unanimous vote of the Commission that a 
meeting be held on this item in closed session and that no earlier 
announcement of the meeting was possible.
