DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2574-032 Maine]


Merimil Limited Partnership; Notice of Site Visit

September 6, 2001.
    Take notice that the Applicant and Commission staff will tour the 
Lockwood Hydroelectric Project beginning at 1:30 p.m. on September 19, 
2002. All interested individuals, organizations, and agencies are 
invited to attend.
    All participants should meet at the Lockwood Project powerhouse on 
Water Street, Waterville, Maine.
    For further information contact Nan Allen at (202) 502-6128.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
