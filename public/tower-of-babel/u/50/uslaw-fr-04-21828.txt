SUMMARY: This notice sets forth the agenda of a forthcoming meeting of 
the National Museum and Library Services Board. This notice also 
describes the function of the Board. Notice of this meeting is required 
under the Sunshine in Government Act.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The National Museum and Library Services 
Board is established under the Museum and Library Services Act, 20 
U.S.C. Section 9101 et seq. The Board advises the Director of the 
Institute on general policies with respect to the duties, powers, and 
authorities related to Museum and Library Services.
    The executive session from 9 a.m. to 10:30 a.m. on Thursday, 
October 7, 2004 will be closed pursuant to subsections (c)(4) and 
(c)(6) of section 552b of Title 5, United States Code because the Board 
will consider information that may disclose: Trade secrets and 
commercial or financial information obtained from a person and 
privileged or confidential; and information of a personal nature the 
disclosure of which would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of 
personal privacy. The meetings from 11 a.m. until 12:30 p.m. on 
Thursday, October 7, 2004 and the meeting from 9 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. on 
Friday, October 8, 2004 are open to the public. If you need special 
accommodations due to a disability, please contact: Institute of Museum 
and Library Services, 1100 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 
20506, (202) 606-8536, TDD (202) 606-8636 at least seven (7) days prior 
to the meeting date.
