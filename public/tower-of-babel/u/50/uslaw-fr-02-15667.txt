FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Radio Broadcasting Services; Jennings and Iowa, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a petition for rule making filed by 
Apex Broadcasting, Inc. requesting the reallotment of Channel 225C2 
from Jennings, Louisiana to Iowa, Louisiana and modification of the 
authorization for Station KJEF-FM accordingly. See 67 FR 17670, April 
11, 2002. Apex Broadcasting, Inc. withdrew its interest in the 
allotment of Channel 225C2 at Iowa, Louisiana. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.
