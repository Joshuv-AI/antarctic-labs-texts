DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Meeting Notice--Military Justice Review Panel

AGENCY: General Counsel of the Department of Defense, Department of 
Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The DoD is publishing this notice to announce that the 
Military Justice Review Panel will host an open meeting on January 16-
17, 2024.

DATES: Tuesday, January 16, 2024--Open to the public from 9:45 a.m. to 
11:45 a.m. and 12:45 p.m. to 2 p.m. eastern standard time (EST) and 
Wednesday, January 17, 2024--Open to the public from 10:15 a.m. to 12 
p.m. and 1 p.m. to 2:45 p.m. EST.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to Sec.  5521 of the National 
Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2017, as amended 
by Sec.  531(k) of the FY 2018 NDAA, the Secretary of Defense 
established this panel to conduct independent periodic reviews and 
assessments of the operation of the Uniform Code of Military Justice 
(UCMJ). (10 U.S.C. 946. Art. 146 (effective Jan 1, 2019)).
    Purpose of the Meeting: Pursuant to UCMJ, Article 146, the MJRP 
shall conduct independent periodic reviews and assessments of the 
operation of the UCMJ. This will be the ninth meeting held by the MJRP. 
On Day 1, the MJRP will hold two open sessions. The first session will 
be composed of former military judges. After a lunch break, the MJRP 
will hear from a panel of special victims' counsel. On Day 2, MJRP 
members will hold two open sessions. First, the MJRP will hear from a 
panel composed of military appellate government counsel followed by a 
second session composed of military appellate defense counsel.
