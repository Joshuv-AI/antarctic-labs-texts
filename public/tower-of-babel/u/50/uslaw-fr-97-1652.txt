DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
[I.D. 011797A]


Endangered Species; Permits

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.
ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NMFS announces the availability of an Environmental Assessment 
and the receipt of an application for a permit to allow an incidental 
take of threatened and endangered species by Weyerhaeuser Company on 
portions of its lands in Lane, Linn, Benton, and Douglas Counties, OR. 
This notice of availability supplements the notice of availability 
provided by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service published elsewhere in 
this Federal Register volume.

    Authority: 16 U.S.C. 1531-1544, and 4201-4245.

    Dated: January 17, 1997.
Robert C. Ziobro,
Acting Chief, Endangered Species Division, Office of Protected 
Resources, National Marine Fisheries Service.
