DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


U.S. Air Force Exclusive Patent License

AGENCY: Air Force Research Laboratory Information Directorate, 
Department of the Air Force, DOD.

[[Page 40572]]


ACTION: Notice of intent to issue an exclusive patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of agency regulations, the 
Department of the Air Force announces its intention to grant Sensible 
Spreader Technologies, LLC, a corporation of New Hampshire having a 
place of business at 844 Elm Street, Manchester, New Hampshire 03101, 
an exclusive license in any right, title and interest the United States 
Air Force has in: In U.S. Patent 8,428,993 entitled ``Method and 
Apparatus for Risk Identification and Mitigation in Shift Work 
Fatigue'', issued on April 23, 2013.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  An exclusive license for this patent 
will be granted unless a written objection is received within fifteen 
(15) days from the date of publication of this Notice. Written 
objections should be sent to: Air Force Research Laboratory, Office of 
the Staff Judge Advocate, AFRL/RIJ, 26 Electronic Parkway, Rome, New 
York 13441-4514. Telephone: (315) 330-2087; Facsimile (315) 330-7583.

Henry Williams,
Acting Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
