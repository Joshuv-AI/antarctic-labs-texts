North Pacific Fishery Management Council; Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The North Pacific Fishery Management Council's Pacific 
Northwest Crab Industry Advisory Committee (Committee), will meet on 
February 9, 1994, at the Alaska Fisheries Science Center, 7600 Sand 
Point Way NE., room 2079, Building 4, Seattle, WA.
    The meeting will begin at 9 a.m. in room 2039, Building 4.
    The agenda includes the following:

(1) Election of a Chairman and a Vice Chairman;
(2) Discussion of Bering Sea shellfish issues--
    (a) Pre-season time limits for tank inspections,
    (b) Post-season deadlines for crab vessels to be alongside 
processors,
    (c) King and Tanner crab pot definitions,
    (d) Definition of hair crab pots, and
    (e) Pot limits for the Adak longline brown crab fishery and the 
red king crab fishery;
(3) Formulation of recommendations on these issues for both the 
North Pacific Fishery Management Council and the Alaska Board of 
Fisheries.

    This meeting is physically accessible to people with disabilities. 
Requests for sign language interpretation or other auxiliary aids 
should be directed to Judy Willoughby, on (907) 271-2809, at least 5 
working days prior to the meeting date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Arni Thomson, Alaska Crab Coalition, 
3901 Leary Way, NW., suite 6, Seattle, WA 98107; telephone (206) 547-
7560.

    Dated: January 26, 1994
David S. Crestin,
Acting Director, Office of Fisheries Conservation and Management, 
National Marine Fisheries Service.
