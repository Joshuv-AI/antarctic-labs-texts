CORPORATION FOR NATIONAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICE


Final Notice of Selection of AmeriCorps*VISTA Sponsors and 
Projects Guidelines

SUMMARY: The Corporation for National and Community Service is issuing 
this final notice addressing the criteria for sponsorship of new and 
existing AmeriCorps*VISTA projects, criteria for project selection, and 
the approval process at the State level. Also, the process for 
selecting national competitive and national demonstration 
AmeriCorps*VISTA projects is addressed.

DATES: The effective date of these guidelines was February 7, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Diana B. London, Deputy Director, AmeriCorps*VISTA, (202) 606-5000, 
extension 228. For individuals with disabilities, information will be 
made available in alternative formats, upon request.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Corporation for National and Community 
Service published the Selection of AmerCorps*VISTA Sponsors and 
Projects Guidelines in the Federal Register, Volume 60, No. 25, on 
February 7, 1995. The Corporation received no comments on the issues 
discussed in these guidelines.

    Dated: May 26, 1995.
Tracy Gray,
Acting Executive Vice-President Corporation for National and Community 
Service.
