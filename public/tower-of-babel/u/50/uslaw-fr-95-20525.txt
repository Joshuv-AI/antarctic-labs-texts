Customer-Owned Service Lines; Correction

AGENCY: Research and Special Programs Administration (RSPA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction of amendment number.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the amendment number of final rule 
document 95-20021 published in the Federal Register on Monday, August 
14, 1995 (60 FR 41821). In the document heading on page 41821, the 
amendment number ``Amdt. 192-3'' is changed to read ``Amdt. 192-74.'' 
The final rule requires operators of gas service lines who do not 
maintain buried customer piping up to building walls or certain other 
locations to notify their customers of the need to maintain that 
piping.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 13, 1995.
