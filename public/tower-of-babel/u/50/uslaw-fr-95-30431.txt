U-T Offshore System, High Island Offshore System; Notice of 
Compliance Filing

December 8, 1995.
    Take notice that on November 17, 1995, in accordance with the 
Commission's September 18, 1995 letter orders approving settlement, U-T 
Offshore System (U-T) and High Island Offshore System (HIOS) tendered 
for filing certain revised tariff sheets that reflect the approved, 
prospective settlement rates and the conversion of their tariffs from a 
volumetric to an thermal based tariff. U-T and HIOS state that the 
tariff sheets are to become effective December 1, 1995.
    U-T and HIOS state that copies of the filings have been served on 
all parties.
    Any person desiring to protest said filings should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C., 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such protest must be 
filed no later than December 14, 1995. Protests will be considered by 
the Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but 
will not serve to make protestants parties to these proceedings. Copies 
of these filings are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
