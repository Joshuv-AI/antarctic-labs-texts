SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

 

Hartford District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Hartford District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Monday, November 14, 1994 at 8:30 
a.m. at 2 Science Park, New Haven, Connecticut 06511, to discuss such 
matter as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. 
Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Jo-Ann Van Vechten, 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 330 Main Street, 
Hartford, Connecticut 06106, (203) 240-4670.

    Dated: October 26, 1994.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Acting Assistant Administrator, Office of Advisory Councils.
