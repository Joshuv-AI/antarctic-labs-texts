DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, October 14, 2009, 8 a.m. to 
October 15, 2009, 6 p.m., Hilton Washington DC/Rockville Executive 
Meeting C, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD, 20852 which was 
published in the Federal Register on July 17, 2009, 74FR34765.
    The name of the committee on this Federal Register Notice has been 
amended to read `Epidemiology, Prevention, Control & Population 
Sciences' and not as otherwise stated. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

     Dated: August 17, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
