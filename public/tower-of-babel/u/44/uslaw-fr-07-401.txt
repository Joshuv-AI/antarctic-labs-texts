DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, February 27, 2007, 12 p.m. to 
February 27, 2007, 4 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 6130 
Executive Blvd., Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal 
Register on December 28, 2006, 71 FR 78214.
    The meeting notice is changed to reflect the change in the name of 
the committee from ``SBIR Topic 230 (Phases I & II)'' to ``SBIR, 
Synthesis Stable Isotope-Labeled Steroids as Internal Standards.'' The 
meeting is closed to the public.
