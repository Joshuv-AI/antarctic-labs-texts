DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Office of the Secretary


Meeting of the Semiconductor Technology Council; Notice

SUMMARY: Under the provisions of PL 92-463, the ``Federal Advisory 
Committee Act,'' notice is hereby given that the Semiconductor 
Technology Council will hold its third meeting. The Council's mission 
is to: Link industry and national security needs to opportunities for 
cooperative investments, foster pre-competitive cooperation among 
industry, government and academia, recommend opportunities for new R&D 
efforts and potential to rationalize and align on-going industry and 
government investments. Part of the meeting will be closed to the 
public in accordance with Section 10(d) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act, and pursuant to the appropriate provisions of Section 
552b(c) (3) and (4), Title 5, U.S.C. There will be an open section from 
12:30 to 1:30 p.m. for a discussion of MicroElectroMechanical Systems 
(MEMS) technology. Advanced registration is required for this session.

DATES: September 18, 1995.

ADDRESSES: 1300 N. 17th St., Suite 1450, Arlington, VA 22209.
