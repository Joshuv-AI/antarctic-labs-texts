Notice of Direct Sale of Public Lands in Klamath County, Oregon--
Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the Notice of Direct Sale of Public Lands in Klamath 
County, Oregon beginning on page 53019 in the issue of Friday, October 
10, 1997 Volume 62, Number 197 make the following correction:
    On page 53019 in the third column, the first sentence in paragraph 
one of the Summary lists the appraised fair market value as 
$645,000.00. The appraised fair market value should be changed to 
$625,400.00.

    Dated: November 25, 1997.
A. Barron Bail,
Area Manager, Klamath Falls Resource Area.
