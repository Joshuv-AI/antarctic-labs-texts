CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, Washington, DC 20207.

TIME AND DATE: Thursday, May 29, 1997, 10:00 a.m.

Location: Room 410, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Part Open to the Public; Part Closed.

Matters to be considered:

Open to the Public
