DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Federal Aviation Administration


Change 1, Advisory Circular (AC) 25-7, Flight Test Guide for 
Certification of Transport Category Airplanes

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance of change to advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Change 1 to Advisory 
Circular (AC) 25-7, Flight Test Guide for Certification of Transport 
Category Airplanes. This change to the basic advisory circular provides 
updated guidance to ensure consistent application of certain 
airworthiness requirements recently adopted by Amendment 25-84.

DATES: Change 1 to AC 25-7 was issued on June 6, 1995, by the Acting 
Manager of the Transport Airplane Directorate, Aircraft Certification 
Service, in Renton, Washington.
HOW TO OBTAIN COPIES: A copy of Change 1 may be obtained by writing to 
the U.S. Department of Transportation, Utilization and Storage Section, 
M-443.2, Washington, DC 20590.

    Issued in Renton, Washington, on July 26, 1995.
Darrell M. Pederson,
Acting Manager, Transport Airplane Directorate, Aircraft Certification 
Service, ANM-100.
