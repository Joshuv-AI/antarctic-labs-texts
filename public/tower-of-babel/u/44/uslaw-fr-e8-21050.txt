DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Blue Ribbon Panel on VA-Medical School Affiliations; Notice of 
Renewal

    In compliance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463), the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice that the 
Blue Ribbon Panel on VA-Medical School Affiliations has been renewed 
through September 30, 2009.
    The Panel advises the Secretary of Veterans Affairs and the Under 
Secretary for Health on the framework for guiding VA's affiliations 
with medical schools and academic medical centers.

    Dated: August 29, 2008.

    By direction of the Secretary.
E. Philip Riggin,
Committee Management Officer.
