National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 18, 2016, 03:00 p.m. 
to November 18, 2016, 05:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 3W030, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on October 17, 2016, 81 FR 71516.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date and time from 
November 18, 2016, 3:00 p.m.-5:00 p.m. to December 15, 2016, 12:30 
p.m.-2:30 p.m. The meeting is closed to the public.
