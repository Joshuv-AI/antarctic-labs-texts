PART 73--RADIO BROADCAST SERVICES

    1. The authority citation for Part 73 continues to read as follows:

    Authority: 47 U.S.C. 154, 303, 334 and 336.


Sec.  73.202  [Amended]

    2. Section 73.202(b), the Table of FM Allotments under California, 
is amended by adding Channel 265A at Coalinga; by adding Channel 274A 
at Mecca; by adding Randsburg, Channel 271A; by adding Channel 299A at 
San Joaquin; and by adding Wasco, Channel 224A.
    3. Section 73.202(b), the Table of FM Allotments under Louisiana, 
is amended by adding Harrisonburg, Channel 232A; and by adding 
Rosepine, Channel 281A.
    4. Section 73.202(b), the Table of FM Allotments under New Mexico, 
is amended by adding Channel 288A at Taos; and by adding Taos Pueblo, 
Channel 292C3.
    5. Section 73.202(b), the Table of FM Allotments under Oklahoma, is 
amended by adding Channel 254A at Mooreland; by adding Ringwood, 
Channel 285A; and by adding Waynoka, Channel 231C2.
    6. Section 73.202(b), the Table of FM Allotments under Texas, is 
amended by adding Channel 288A at Port Isabel; and by adding Channel 
299A at Richland Springs.
    7. Section 73.202(b), the Table of FM Allotments under Washington, 
is amended by adding Coupeville, Channel 266A.
