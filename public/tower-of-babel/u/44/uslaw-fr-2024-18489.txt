DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Library of Medicine Board of Scientific Counselors, November 7, 2024, 
11:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., which was published in the Federal Register on 
July 15, 2024, 89 FR 135, Page Number 57422.
    This notice is being amended to announce that the meeting date will 
be changed from November 7, 2024, to November 6, 2024. The meeting will 
be virtual.

    Dated: August 13, 2024.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
