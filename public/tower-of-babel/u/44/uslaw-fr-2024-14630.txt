Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records

AGENCY: Department of State.

ACTION: Rescindment of a system of records notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The ``Communications Personnel Training Records, State-57'' 
which is being rescinded, contains information which was used to 
determine current and future training requirements of those individuals 
who are professional communications personnel and who have been tasked 
to perform additional back-up communications duties at Foreign Service 
posts.

DATES: On May 9, 2023, the Department of State published a notice in 
the Federal Register (88 FR 29960) that records in State-57 were being 
consolidated with ``Foreign Service Institute Records, State-14'' into 
a single modified State-14 because the records and system purposes were 
substantially similar. The consolidation of these two systems of 
records into State-14 became effective on June 8, 2023.
