Proposed Modification of Class D Airspace and Class E Airspace; 
Wilmington, OH

Correction

    In proposed rule document 99-5253, beginning on page 10241, in the 
issue of Wednesday, March 3, 1999, make the following corrections:


Sec. 71.1  [Corrected]

    1. On page 10242, in the first column, in Sec. 71.1, under the 
heading AGL OH E4 Wilmington, OH [Revised], in the fourth line, ``Lat. 
39 deg. 26' 16'' N.'' should read ``Lat. 39 deg. 26' 15'' N.''.
    2. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 71.1, under the 
same heading, in the sixth line, ``Lat. 39 deg. 26' 47'' N.'' should 
read ``Lat. 39 deg. 25' 47'' N.'', and ``long. 083 deg. 48' 94'' W.'' 
should read ``long. 083 deg. 48' 04'' W.''.
    3. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 71.1, under the 
same heading, in the 10th line, ``4.1-mile'' should read ``4.2-mile''.
