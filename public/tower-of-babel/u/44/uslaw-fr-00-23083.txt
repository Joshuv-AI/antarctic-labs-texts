DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Geological Survey


MPI Drilling, Inc.; Proposed Cooperative Research and Development 
Agreement

AGENCY: United States Geological Survey, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of proposed Cooperative Research and Development 
Agreement (CRADA) negotiations under the Technology Transfer Act of 
1986.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Geological Survey (USGS) is contemplating 
entering into a CRADA with MPI Drilling, Inc. for the development of 
improved drilling technology.

INQUIRIES: Information on the proposed CRADA is available to the public 
upon request at the address below. If any other parties are interested 
in similar activities with the USGS, please contact: Wayne L. Newell, 
12201 Sunrise Valley Drive, Reston, Virginia 20192; Telephone: 703-648-
6991; Internet: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This notice is to meet the USGS requirement 
stipulated in the Survey Manual.

P. Patrick Leahy,
Associate Director for Geology.
