Land Acquisitions; Wilton Rancheria

AGENCY: Bureau of Indian Affairs, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary--Indian Affairs made 
a final agency determination to acquire 35.92 acres, more or less, in 
the City of Elk Grove, Sacramento County, California (Site) in trust 
for the Wilton Rancheria for gaming and other purposes on January 19, 
2017.

DATES: The final determination was made on January 19, 2017. The land 
was acquired in trust on February 10, 2017.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Paula L. Hart, Director, Office of 
Indian Gaming, Bureau of Indian Affairs, MS-3543 MIB, 1849 C Street NW, 
Washington, DC 20240, telephone (202) 219-4066, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On January 19, 2017, the Principal Deputy 
Assistant Secretary--Indian Affairs issued a decision to accept the 
Site, consisting of approximately 35.92 acres, more or less, of land in 
trust for the Wilton Rancheria (Tribe), under the authority of the 
Indian Reorganization Act, 25 U.S.C. 5108. The Principal Deputy 
Assistant Secretary--Indian Affairs determined that Tribe's request 
also meets the requirements of the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act's 
``Restored Lands'' exception, 25 U.S.C. 2719(b)(1)(B)(iii), to the 
general prohibition contained in 25 U.S.C. 2719(a) on gaming on lands 
acquired in trust after October 17, 1988.
