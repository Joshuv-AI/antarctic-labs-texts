DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-29,927; NAFTA-00120]


Walker Manufacturing Co.; Hebron, OH; Notice of Revocation of 
Negative Determination

    This notice revokes the Notice of Negative Determination on 
Reconsideration for petitions TA-W-29,927 and NAFTA-00120 which was 
published on page 8065 in the Federal Register on February 10, 1995 (60 
FR 8065) in Document Number FR 95-3404.
    This notice is revoked since it was published prematurely.

    Signed in Washington, D.C., this 14th day of February 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
