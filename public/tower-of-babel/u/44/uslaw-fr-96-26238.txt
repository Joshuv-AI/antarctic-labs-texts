    Name of SEP: Biological and Physiological Sciences.
    Date: October 17, 1996.
    Time: 10:00 a.m.
    Place: Embassy Suites Hotel, Washington, DC.
    Contact Person: Dr. Michael Land, Scientific Review 
Administrator, 6701
