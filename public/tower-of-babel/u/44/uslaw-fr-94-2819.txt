                     Sunshine Act Meetings





 

COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., Wednesday, February 23, 1994.

PLACE: 2033 K St., N.W., Washington, D.C., Lower Level Hearing Room.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

--Proposed rules on holding company risk assessment
--Final rules relating to reparation proceedings
--Proposal by the Chicago Mercantile Exchange to recommence trading 
in the dormant one-year U.S. Treasury Bill futures contract and 
application for designation as a contract market in options on that 
future contract

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Jean A. Webb, Secretary of the 
Commission.
Jean A. Webb,
Secretary of the Commission.
