Modification of Class E Airspace; Circleville, OH; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects two (2) errors contained in a final rule 
that was published in the Federal Register on Friday, January 17, 2003 
(68 FR 2422). The final rule modified Class E airspace at Circleville, 
OH.

EFFECTIVE DATE: 0901 UTC, March 20, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Denis C. Burke, Air Traffic Division, 
Airspace Branch, AGL-520, Federal Aviation Administration, 2300 East 
Devon Avenue, Des Plaines, IL 60018, telephone: (847) 294-7477.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

History

    Federal Register Document 03-1124 published on Friday, January 27, 
2003 (68 FR 2422), modified Class E airspace at Circleville, OH. The 
Docket contained an incorrect lat./long., and also contained a 
misspelled city name, both contained in the legal description. This 
action corrects these errors.
    Accordingly, pursuant to the authority delegated to me, the errors 
for the Class E airspace, Circleville, OH, as published in the Federal 
Register Friday, January 17, 2003 (68 FR 2422), (FR Doc. 03-1124), is 
corrected as follows:


Sec.  71.1  [Corrected]

0
On page 2422, Column 3, in the legal description:
0
1. On the second (2nd) line, correct: ``Cillicothe'' to read: 
``Chillicothe''.
0
2. On the third (3rd) line, correct: ``(Lat. 39[deg] 26' 29''N., long. 
83[deg] 01' 41''W.)'' to read: (Lat. 39[deg]26'29'' N., long. 
83[deg]01'21'' W.''.

    Issued in Des Plaines, Illinois, on March 5, 2003.
