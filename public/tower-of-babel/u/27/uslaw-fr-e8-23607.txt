Submission for OMB Review; Request for Comments on a Revised 
Information Collection

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget (OMB) a request for review of a revised information 
collection. This information collection, ``Evidence to Prove Dependency 
of a Child'' (OMB Control No. 3206-0206; form RI 25-37), is designed to 
collect sufficient information for OPM to determine whether the 
surviving child of a deceased federal employee is eligible to receive 
benefits as a dependent child.
    Approximately 250 forms are completed annually. We estimate it 
takes approximately 60 minutes to assemble the needed documentation.

[[Page 58685]]

The annual estimated burden is 250 hours.
