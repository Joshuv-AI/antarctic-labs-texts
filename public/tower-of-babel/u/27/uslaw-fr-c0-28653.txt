Self-Regulatory Organizations; Notice of Filing of Proposed Rule 
Change and Amendment Nos. 1, 2, and 3 by the National Association of 
Securities Dealers, Inc. Concerning Related Performance Information

Correction

    In notice document 00-28653 beginning on page 67025 in the issue of 
Wednesday, November 8, 2000, make the following correction:
    On page 67025, in the third column, in paragraph (1), beginning on 
the 13th line, remove the sentence that begins with ``However'' and add 
the following sentence in its place: ``[However, communications may not 
include the performance of an existing fund for the purposes of 
promoting investment in a similar, but new investment option (i.e., 
clone fund or model fund) available in a variable contract.]''
