DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, July 12, 2017, 11:00 a.m. to 
July 12, 2017, 3:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 7W114, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on May 31, 2017, 82 FR 24983.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the meeting title to 
``Biospecimen and Innovative Technology''. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: June 27, 2017.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
