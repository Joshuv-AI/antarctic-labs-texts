29 CFR Part 1404


Arbitration Policy; Roster of Arbitrators, and Procedures for 
Arbitration Services

agency: Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service.

action: Correction to proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: A proposed rule on arbitration policy contained an error in 
page costs. This document is intended to correct that error.

for further information contact: Peter L. Regner, (202) 606-8181.

supplementary information: In proposed rule document 97-6305 beginning 
on page 11797, in the Federal Register issue of Thursday, March 13, 
1997, make the following correction:
    In the appendix to 29 CFR Part 1404 on page 11805, under ``List and 
biographical sketches of arbitrators in specific areas'', the term 
``$10 per page'' should read ``$.10 per page''.

Director.
