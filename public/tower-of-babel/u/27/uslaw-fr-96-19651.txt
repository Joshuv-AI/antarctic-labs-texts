Shepard's/McGraw-Hill Companies Colorado Springs, Colorado; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 28, 1996 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on May 10, 1996 on behalf of workers at Shepard's/McGraw-Hill 
Companies, Colorado Springs, Colorado.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 18th day of July, 1996.
Russell T. Kile,
Acting Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of 
Trade Adjustment Assistance.
