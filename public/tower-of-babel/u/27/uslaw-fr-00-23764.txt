SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board; Public 
Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration National Small Business 
Development Center Advisory Board will hold a public meeting on 
Thursday, October 5, 2000, from 10 am to 4 pm at the Fontainebleau 
Hilton Hotel, Miami Beach, Florida to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, 
or others present.
    For further information, please write or call Ellen Thrasher, U.S. 
Small Business Administration, 409 Third Street, SW., Fourth Floor, 
Washington, DC 20416. Telephone number (202) 205-6817.

Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administrator.
