DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Navy

 

Intent To Grant Partially Exclusive Patent License; Serotech, 
Inc.

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to Serotech, Inc., a revocable, nonassignable, partially 
exclusive license in the United States to practice the Government-owned 
inventions described in U.S. Patent No. 5,276,695, ``Multifrequency, 
Rapidly Sequenced or Simultaneous Tunable Laser'', issued January 4, 
1994.
    Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license has 60 days 
from the date of this notice to file written objections along with 
supporting evidence, if any. Written objections are to be filed with 
the Office of Naval Research (ONR OOCC), Ballston Tower One, Arlington, 
Virginia 22217-5660.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. R.J. Erickson, Staff Patent Attorney, Office of Naval Research (ONR 
OOCC), Ballston Tower One, 800 North Quincy Street, Arlington, Virginia 
22217-5660, telephone (703) 696-4001.

    Dated: November 7, 1994.
L.R. McNees,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
