DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Louisiana Pacific; Moyie Springs, ID; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 5, 1995, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on June 5, 1995, on behalf of workers at Louisiana Pacific, 
Moyie Springs, Idaho.
    Workers at Louisiana Pacific, Moyie Springs, Idaho, have been 
certified eligible to apply for trade adjustment assistance (TA-W-
30,952E). This certification, which covers the petitioning group of 
workers, remains in effect. Consequently, further investigation in this 
case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 23rd day of June, 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
