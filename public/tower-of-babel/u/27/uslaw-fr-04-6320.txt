Television Broadcast Service; Osage Beach, MO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Timothy D. Lischwe, allots 
channel 49 to Osage Beach, Missouri. See 68 FR 61788, October 30, 2003. 
TV channel 49 can be allotted to Osage Beach with a plus offset in 
compliance with the Minimum distance separation requirements. The 
coordinates for channel 49+ are 38-17-33 N. and 92-34-24 W. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective April 19, 2004.

418-1600.
