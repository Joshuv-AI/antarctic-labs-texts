COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the American Samoa Advisory 
Committee; Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the American Samoa Advisory 
Committee. The meeting scheduled for Wednesday, December 15, 2022, at 
12 p.m. (SST) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of 
Friday, July 8, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-14527, in the first and second 
column of page 40783.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Brooke Peery, (202) 701-1376,
