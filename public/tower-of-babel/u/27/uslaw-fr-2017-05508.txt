SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Public Law 94-409, the Securities and 
Exchange Commission will hold an Open Meeting on Wednesday, March 22, 
2017, at 10:00 a.m., in the Auditorium, Room L-002.
    The subject matter of the Open Meeting will be:
     The Commission will consider whether to adopt an amendment 
to Rule 15c6-1 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to shorten the 
standard settlement cycle for most broker-dealer transactions from 
three business days after the trade date to two business days after the 
trade date.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items.
    For further information and to ascertain what, if any, matters have 
been added, deleted, or postponed, please contact Brent J. Fields in 
the Office of the Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: March 15, 2017.
Brent J. Fields,
Secretary.
