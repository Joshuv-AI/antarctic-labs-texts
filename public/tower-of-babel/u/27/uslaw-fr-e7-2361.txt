Office of Postsecondary Education; Overview Information; 
International Research and Studies Program

ACTION: Correction; Notice correcting the dates.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: We correct the Applications Available and Deadline for 
Transmittal of Applications dates in the notice published on January 
24, 2007 (72 FR 3123).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On January 24, 2007, we published a notice 
in the Federal Register (72 FR 3123-3127) inviting applications for new 
awards for fiscal year (FY) 2007 for the International Research and 
Studies program. The Applications Available date (as published on pages 
3123 and 3124) is corrected to February 12, 2007 and the Deadline for 
Transmittal of Applications date (as published on pages 3123 and 3124) 
is corrected to March 29, 2007.

e-mail: [email protected].
