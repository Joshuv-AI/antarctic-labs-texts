National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin 
Diseases; Notice of Closed Meetings

    Place: Doubletree Hotel, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, 
Maryland 20852.
    Contact Person: Aftab A. Ansari, Ph.D., Scientific Review 
Administrator, NIAMS, 45 Center Drive, Room 5AS-25S, Bethesda, 
Maryland 20892. Telephone: 301-594-4952.

    This notice is being published less than 15 days prior to the 
meeting due to the urgent need to meet timing limitations imposed by

    The meetings will be closed in accordance with the provisions 
set forth in secs. 552b(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C. The 
discussions of these applications could reveal confidential trade 
secrets or commercial property such as patentable and person 
information concerning individuals associated with applications, the 
disclosure of which would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion 
of personal privacy.
