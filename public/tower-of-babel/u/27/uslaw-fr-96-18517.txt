NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 96-078]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Prospective Patent License.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that SafetySCAN, LLC of Orchard Park, 
New York, has applied for an exclusive license to practice the 
invention described and claimed in a pending U.S. Patent, entitled 
``Flame Imaging System'' SSC-00040, which is assigned to the United 
States of America as represented by the Administrator of the National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections to the 
prospective grant of a license to SafetySCAN, LLC. should be sent to 
Beth Vrioni, John F. Kennedy Space Center, Mail Code: DE-TPO, Kennedy 
Space Center, FL 32899.

DATES: Responses to this Notice must be received on or before September 
20, 1996. For further information contact: Beth Vrioni at (407) 867-
2544.

    Dated: July 10, 1996.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
