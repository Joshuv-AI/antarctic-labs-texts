DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Emphasis Panel, February 19, 2003, 10:30 a.m. to 
February 19, 2003, 5:30 p.m., which was published in the Federal 
Register on February 5, 2003, 68 FR 5906-5908.
    The meeting times have been changed to 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. The meeting 
date and location remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: February 11, 2003.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
