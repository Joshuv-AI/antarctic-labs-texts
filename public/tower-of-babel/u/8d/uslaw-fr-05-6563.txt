FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Radio Broadcasting Services; Fisher and Thief River Falls, MN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition for rule making 
filed by Citicasters Licenses, L.P., licensee of Station KSNR(FM), 
Channel 262C1, Thief River Falls, Minnesota. Petitioner requests that 
the Commission reallot Channel 262C1 from Thief River Falls to Fisher, 
Minnesota. The coordinates for Channel 262C1 at Fisher are 47-58-38 NL 
and 96-36-42 WL, with a site restriction of 24.2 kilometers (15.1 
miles) northeast of Fisher.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before May 9, 2005, and reply 
comments on or before May 24, 2005.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, 445 12th 
Street, SW., Room TW-A325, Washington, DC 20554. In addition to filing 
comments with the FCC, interested parties should serve Petitioner's 
counsel, as follows: Marissa G. Repp, Esq., and Tarah S. Grant, Esq., 
Hogan & Hartson, L.L.P.; 555 Thirteenth Street, NW., Washington, DC 
20004-1109.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
