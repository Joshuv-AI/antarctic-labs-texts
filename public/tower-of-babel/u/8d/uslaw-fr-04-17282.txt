DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Notice of Public Hearings Addressing the Use of Helicopters and 
Motorized Vehicles During the Capture of Wild Horses

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Department of the Interior.

ACTION: Notice of public hearings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A public hearing addressing the use of motorized vehicles and 
helicopters during the capture of wild horses in the Little Book Cliffs 
Wild Horse Range, Grand Junction Field Office, Grand Junction, 
Colorado.

DATES: The public hearing has been scheduled for September 9, 2004 at 
the Grand Junction Field Office; 2815 H Road; Grand Junction, Colorado. 
Time of the meeting will be 7 p.m. Information for the meeting will be 
announced through public notices, local newspaper announcements and 
mailings.

ADDRESSES: Grand Junction Field Office: 2815 H Road, Grand Junction, 
Colorado 81506.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Little Book Cliffs wild horse gather is 
scheduled for completion during October 2004 if weather allows. 
Otherwise the gather will occur between August 1 and October 1, 2005: 
For additional information regarding the public hearing please contact 
Jim Dollerschell, Bureau of Land Management (BLM), 2815 H Road, Grand 
Junction, Colorado 81506; telephone (970) 244-3016 or e-mail [email protected].

    Dated: July 23, 2004.
Raul Morales,
Associate Field Office Manager.
