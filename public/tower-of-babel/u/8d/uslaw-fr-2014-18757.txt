NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[NRC-2014-0163]


Setpoints for Safety-Related Instrumentation

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Draft regulatory guide; extension of comment period and 
correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register (FR) on July 11, 
2014, announcing the release of draft regulatory guide (DG), DG-1141, 
``Setpoints for Safety-Related Instrumentation,'' for a 60 day public 
comment period. This action is being taken to correct the Agencywide 
Documents Access and Management System (ADAMS) Accession number for DG-
1141. The document at the corrected ADAMS number includes editorial 
changes that were not included in the previously referenced version of 
DG-1141, but there are no technical changes. Due to this error, the NRC 
is extending the comment period from September 9, 2014, to October 10, 
2014.

DATES: This correction is effective immediately. Submit comments by 
October 10, 2014. Comments received after this date will be considered 
if practical to do so, but the NRC is able to ensure consideration only 
for comments received on or before this date.
