NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


DOE/NSF Nuclear Science Advisory Committee, 1176; Notice of 
Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting.

    Name: DOE/NSF Nuclear Science Advisory Committee (1176).
    Date and Time: Thursday, March 6, 2003; 8:30 a.m.-6 p.m.; 
Friday, March 7, 2003; 8:30 a.m.-6 p.m.
    Place: Stafford I Building, Room 1235, National Science 
Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Dr. Bradley D. Keister, Program Director for 
Nuclear Physics, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., 
Arlington, VA 22230. Telephone: (703) 292-7377.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning the scientific programs of the NSF and DOE in the area of 
basic nuclear physics research.
