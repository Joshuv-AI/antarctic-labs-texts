DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Clinical 
Trials Review Committee, June 30, 2008, 8:30 a.m. to July 1, 2008, 5 
p.m., Pier 5 Hotel, 711 Eastern Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21202 which was 
published in the Federal Register on April 22, 2008, FRE8-8513.
    The meeting dates were changed from June 30-July 1, 2008 to June 
23-24, 2008. Also, meeting location was changed from Pier 5 Hotel to 
Sheraton Columbia Hotel. The rest of the information remains the same. 
The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: April 28, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
