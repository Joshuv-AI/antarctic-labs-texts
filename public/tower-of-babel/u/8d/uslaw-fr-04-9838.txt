COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Correction

    In the document appearing on page 22000, FR Doc. 04-9312, in the 
issue of April 23, 2004, in the second column, the Committee published 
an effective date of November 23, 2003 for addition of the Food Service 
Attendant, Elmendorf Air Force Base, Alaska to the Procurement List. 
The correct effective date should be May 23, 2004.

G. John Heyer,
General Counsel.
