Solvay Animal Health, Incorporated, Mendota Heights, Minnesota; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 6, 1997 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Solvay Animal Health, Incorporated, 
Mendota Heights, Minnesota.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose; and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 25th day of November 1997.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
