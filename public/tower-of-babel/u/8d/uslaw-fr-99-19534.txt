COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


``Additions to Procurement List'' Correction

    The Committee has combined and redesignated as JWOD Staffing 
Services the two services currently on the Committee's Procurement List 
as Administrative/General Support Services (GSA/FSS Region 7), (Up to 
50% of the Government's requirement) which was added to the Procurement 
List on August 30, 1996 (61 FR 45934) and Temporary Administrative/
General Support Services for GSA Regions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 
National Capitol Region (Up to 50% of the Government's requirement) 
which was added to the Procurement List on February 28, 1997 (62 FR 
9158).
Beverly L. Milkman,
Executive Director.
