FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 10-395; MB Docket No. 10-19; RM-11589]


Television Broadcasting Services; Oklahoma City, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission grants a petition for rulemaking filed by 
Griffin Licensing, L.L.C., the licensee of KWTV-DT, channel 9, Oklahoma 
City, requesting the substitution of channel 39 for channel 9 at 
Oklahoma City.

DATES: This rule is effective March 19, 2010.

(202) 418-1600.
