Tankers--Automatic Pilot Systems; Correction

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DHS.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard is correcting a final rule that appeared in 
the Fedreal Register on November 5, 2018. The document issued a final 
rule that permited tankers with automatic pilot systems that meet 
certain international standards to operate using those systems in 
shipping safety fairways and traffic separation schemes specified in 33 
CFR parts 166 and 167, respectively.

DATES: Effective December 5, 2018.
