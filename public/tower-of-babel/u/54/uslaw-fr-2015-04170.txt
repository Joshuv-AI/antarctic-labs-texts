DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Board of Scientific Advisors, March 11, 2015, 9:00 
a.m. to March 11, 2015, 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in 
the Federal Register on February 19, 2015, 80FR8889.
    This Notice is being amended to change the start time of the 
meeting from 9:00 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. The meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: February 24, 2015.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
