NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION

Sunshine Act Meeting


Notice of Changes in Subject of Meeting and Cancellation of 
Closed Meeting

    The National Credit Union Administration Board determined that its 
business required the deletion of the following two items from the 
previously announced closed meeting (Federal Register, Vol. 64, page 
54364, Wednesday, October 6, 1999) scheduled for Wednesday, October 6, 
1999.
    1. Field of Membership Appeal. Closed pursuant to exemption (8).
    2. Modification of NCUA's Indemnification Policy. Closed pursuant 
to exemptions (2) and (6).
    The Board voted unanimously that agency business required that 
these items be deleted from the agenda with less than the usual seven 
days notice and that no earlier announcement of these changes was 
possible.
    The previously announced items were the same two items deleted from 
the agenda. Since those were the only two items on the agenda, there 
being no agenda, the closed meeting was canceled.
