DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Staff Attendance at the Illinois Commerce Commission's 
``Planning For The Future'' Policy Session; Focus on 2015-2016 Winter 
Preparedness and Resource Adequacy in the Ameren Illinois Footprint

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of its staff may attend the above meeting of the 
Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC). Their attendance is part of the 
Commission's ongoing outreach efforts.
    The meeting will be held on November 19, 2015 from 10:00 a.m. to 
3:00 p.m. at in the Main Hearing Room at the ICC's Chicago office, 160 
North LaSalle, Suite C-800, Chicago, IL 60601.
    The discussions may address matters at issue in the following 
proceedings:

Docket No. ER11-4081, Midwest Independent System Operator, Inc.
Docket No. EL12-54, Viridity Energy, Inc. v. PJM Interconnection, 
L.L.C.
