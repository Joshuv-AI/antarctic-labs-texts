OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Review of an 
Information Collection: RI 78-11

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Public 
Law 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of an information collection. RI 78-11, 
Medicare Part B Certification, collects information from annuitants, 
their spouses, and survivor annuitants to determine their eligibility 
under the Retired Federal Employees Health Benefits Program for a 
Government contribution toward the cost of Part B Medicare.
    Approximately 100 RI 78-11 forms are completed annually. Each form 
requires approximately 10 minutes to complete for an annual estimated 
burden of 17 hours.
