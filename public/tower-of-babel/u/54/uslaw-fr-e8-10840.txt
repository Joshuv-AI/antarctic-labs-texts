DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Pathobiology of Kidney Disease Study Section, June 12, 2008, 8 a.m. to 
June 13, 2008, 5 p.m., Hotel Deca, 4507 Brooklyn Avenue, NE., Seattle, 
WA 98105 which was published in the Federal Register on May 2, 2008, 73 
FR 24296-24298.

time and location remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.
