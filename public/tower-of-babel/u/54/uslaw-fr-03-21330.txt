AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to the final rule published 
in the Federal Register on July 31, 2003. That rule upgraded 
flammability standards for thermal and acoustic insulation materials 
used in transport category airplanes.

[[Page 50055]]


EFFECTIVE DATE: This correction is effective on August 20, 2003.
