DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meetings

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meetings of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, The Strong 
Heart Study--Field Centers and the Strong Heart Study--Coordinating 
Center meetings, Hilton Garden Inn Bethesda, 7301 Waverly Street, 
Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on 
March 19, 2018, 83FR12013.
    This notice is amended to change the meeting times on April 11, 
2018. The Strong Heart Study--Field Centers is amended to occur at 
10:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. The Strong Heart Study--Coordinating Center is 
amended to occur at 8:30 a.m. to 10:30 a.m. The meetings are closed to 
the public.

    Dated: March 20, 2018.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
