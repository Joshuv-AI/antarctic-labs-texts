SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3147]


State of Florida (Amendment #1)

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated November 16, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is 
hereby amended to include Palm Beach County, Florida as a disaster area 
due to damages caused by Tropical Storm Mitch beginning November 4, 
1998 and continuing through November 5, 1998.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous counties of Broward, Glades, 
Hendry, and Martin in the State of Florida may be filed until the 
specified date at the previously designated location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is January 5, 1999 and for 
economic injury the termination date is August 6, 1999.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: November 19, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
