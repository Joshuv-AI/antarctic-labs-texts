DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL20-34-000]


Constellation Power Source Generation, LLC; Notice of Institution 
of Section 206 Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On July 16, 2020, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL20-34-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2018), instituting an investigation into the continued 
justness and reasonableness of Constellation Power Source Generation, 
LLC's rate schedule for Reactive Supply and Voltage Control from 
Generation or Other Sources Service. Constellation Power Source 
Generation, LLC, 172 FERC 61,051 (2020).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL20-34-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.
    Any interested person desiring to be heard in Docket No. EL20-34-
000 must file a notice of intervention or motion to intervene, as 
appropriate, with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street NE, Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Rule 214 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure, 18 CFR 385.214 (2019), 
within 21 days of the date of issuance of the order.

    Dated: July 16, 2020.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
