Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs; Notice: Extension of 
Deadline for South Pacific Scholarship Program RFP

SUMMARY: The deadline for the South Pacific Scholarship RFP has been 
extended from April 7, 2000 to April 24, 2000. The RFP was originally 
published as Public Notice 3242 in the Federal Register on March 9, 
2000 (65 FR 12609).

[email protected] 202/619-6409 (tel), or 202/205-2452 (fax).

    Dated: March 15, 2000.
Evelyn S. Lieberman,
