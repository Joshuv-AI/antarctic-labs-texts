Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 8, 2018, 8:00 a.m. to 
June 8, 2018, 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 6701 Rockledge

on May 15, 2018, 83 FR 22503.

2018, 5:00 p.m. The meeting location remains the same. The meeting is 
closed to the public.
