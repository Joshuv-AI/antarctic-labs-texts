National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Acquired 
Immunodeficiency Syndrome Research Review Committee, which was 
published in the Federal Register on February 19, 2019, 84 FR 4834, Pg 
4834.
    Amendment changes the meeting dates from August 1-2, 2019 to August 
14-15, 2019. The meeting is closed to the public.
