Sipex Corporation; Milpitas, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 20, 2005 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Sipex 
Corporation, Milpitas, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 5th day of October, 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
