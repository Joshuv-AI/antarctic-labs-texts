FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Agreement Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice that on March 21, 2002, the 
Westbound Transpacific Stabilization Agreement withdrew its proposed 
Reefer Trade Management Program, FMC Agreement No. 011325-027. The 
Commission, therefore, will not hold an oral hearing on the matter as 
noticed in the Federal Register on March 20, 2002, 67 FR 12993.

    Dated: March 22, 2002.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
