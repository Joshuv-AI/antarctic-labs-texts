NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION

Special Emphasis Panel in Design, Manufacture, and Industrial 
Innovation; Notice of Meeting

    In according with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Special Emphasis Panel in Design, Manufacture, and 
Industrial Innovation--#1194.
    Date and Time: December 8, 1995, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.
    Place: Rooms 310, 340, 365, 370, 380, and 530, National Science 
Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Closed.
    Contact Person: Dr. Warren DeVries and Dr. Kesh Narayanan, 
Manufacturing Processes and Equipment Program, Dr. Pius Egbelu, 
Operations Research and Production Systems Program, Dr. George 
Hazelrigg and Dr. Christina Gabriel, Design and Integration 
Engineering Program, and Mr. Warren Chernock, National Science 
Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning proposals submitted to the NSF for financial support.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate Faculty Early Career Development 
(CAREER) proposals as part of the selection process for awards.
    Reasons for Closing: The proposals being reviewed include 
information of a proprietary or confidential nature, including 
technical information, financial data such as salaries, and personal 
information concerning individuals association with the proposals. 
These matters are exempt under 5 USC 552b(c)(4) and (6) of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act.

    Date: November 13, 1995.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
