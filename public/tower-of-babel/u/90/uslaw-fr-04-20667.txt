SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3620]


State of Florida; Amendment 1

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency--effective 
September 7, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
include Alachua, Clay, Duval, Flagler, Hendry, Putnam, Seminole, St. 
Johns, and Volusia counties as disaster areas due to damages caused by 
Hurricane Frances occurring on September 3, 2004, and continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous counties of Baker, Bradford, 
Columbia, Gilchrist, Nassau, and Union in the State of Florida may be 
filed until the specified date at the previously designated location. 
All other counties contiguous to the above named primary counties have 
previously been declared.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 3, 2004 and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 6, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)


    Dated: September 8, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
