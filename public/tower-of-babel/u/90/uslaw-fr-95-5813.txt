POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Notice of Commission Visits

March 3, 1995.
    Notice is hereby given that members of the Postal Rate Commission 
and certain advisory staff members will visit the facilities of the 
following businesses to observe their operations:

Penton Press, Cleveland, Ohio and then meet with officials from Lands 
End, Inc. in Milwaukee, Wisconsin on March 7, 1995.
Quad/Graphics, Hartford, Wisconsin on March 8, 1995.
Hallmark Cards, Kansas City, Missouri, March 9, 1995.

    Reports of these visits will be placed on file in the Commission's 
Docket Room. For further information contact Margaret P. Crenshaw, 
Secretary of the Commission at 202-789-6840.
Margaret P. Crenshaw,
Secretary.
