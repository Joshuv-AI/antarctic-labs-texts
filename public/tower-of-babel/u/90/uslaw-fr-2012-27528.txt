SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Public Law 94-409, that the Securities 
and Exchange Commission will host the SEC Government-Business Forum on 
Small Business Capital Formation on Thursday, November 15, 2012, 
beginning at 9:00 a.m. in the auditorium of the Commission's 
headquarters at 100 F Street NE., Washington, DC. Doors will open at 
8:30 a.m. Visitors will be subject to security checks.
    This Sunshine Act notice is being issued because a majority of the 
Commission may attend the meeting.
    The forum will include remarks by SEC Commissioners and panel 
discussions that Commissioners may attend. Panel topics will include 
JOBS Act implementation and small business capital formation issues not 
addressed by the JOBS Act. Members of the public may attend the forum 
without charge. The Commissioner remarks and panel discussions will be 
webcast from the SEC's Web site.
    For further information, please contact the Office of the Secretary 
at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: November 7, 2012.
Elizabeth M. Murphy,
Secretary.
