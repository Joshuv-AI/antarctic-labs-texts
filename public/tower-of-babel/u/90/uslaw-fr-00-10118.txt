NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Polar Programs; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Advisory Committee for Polar Programs (1130).
    Date and Time: May 22-23, 2000, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Room 
1235, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Brenda Williams, Office of Polar Programs (OPP), 
National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Suite 755, 
Arlington, VA 22230. Telephone: (703) 306-1030.
    Minutes: May be obtained from the contact person listed above.
    Purpose of Meeting: To advise NSF on the impact of its policies, 
programs and activities on the polar research community; to provide 
advice to the Director of OPP on issues related to long range 
planning, and to form ad hoc subcommittees to carry out needed 
studies and tasks.
    Agenda: Discussion of NSF-wide initiatives, long-range planning, 
and GPRA.

    Dated: April 13, 2000.
Karen J. York,
Committee Management Officer.
