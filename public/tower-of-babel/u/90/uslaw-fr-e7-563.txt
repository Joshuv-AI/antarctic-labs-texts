DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Southern Company Services, Inc.; Notice of Non-Decisional Status

January 10, 2007.
    Take notice that, for purposes of the above-captioned docket (and 
all subdockets in those dockets), Scott P. Molony, Chief, Regulatory 
Accounting Branch, and Steven D. Hunt, Auditor, in the Division of 
Financial Regulation, Office of Enforcement are non-decisional 
authorities and non-decisional employees. Cf. 18 CFR 385.102(a) (2006) 
(definition of decisional authority); 18 CFR 385.2201(c)(3) (2006) 
(definition of decisional employee).

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
