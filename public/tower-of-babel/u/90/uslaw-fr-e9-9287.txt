POSTAL REGULATORY COMMISSION


Briefing on Postal Technology

AGENCY: Postal Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of briefing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: There will be a presentation by Dr. Leon Pinsov, Vice 
President, International Standards and Advanced Technology, Pitney 
Bowes Corporation, on ``Postal Product Innovation and New Opportunities 
for Postal Commerce'' on Thursday, April 30, 2009, beginning at 1 p.m., 
in the Postal Regulatory Commission's hearing room. The briefing is 
open to the public; however, seating is limited, and those wishing to 
reserve a seat should telephone Leona Anasiewiez at 202-789-6877. The 
program will be Webcast at http://www.prc.gov.

DATES: April 30, 2009.

ADDRESSES: Postal Regulatory Commission, 901 New York Avenue, NW., 
Suite 200, Washington, DC 20268-0001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ann Fisher, director, Office of Public 
Affairs and Government Relations, Postal Regulatory Commission, 202-
789-6803.

Steven W. Williams,
Secretary.
