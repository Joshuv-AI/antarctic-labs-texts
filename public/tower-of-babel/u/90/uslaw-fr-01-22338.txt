DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of New Docket Prefix ``AD''

August 30, 2001.
    Notice is hereby given that a new docket prefix ``AD'' has been 
established to identify Administrative items which may be listed on the 
Commission meeting schedule.

[[Page 46619]]

    The prefix will be ADFY-NNN-000, where ``FY'' stands for the fiscal 
year in which the item is being scheduled, and ``NNN'' is a sequential 
number.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
