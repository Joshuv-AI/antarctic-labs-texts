DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Proposed Collection; Comment Request; National Cancer Institute 
Science Enrichment Program Surveys

SUMMARY: In compliance with the requirement of section 44 U.S.C. 
3507(a)(1)(D) of the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, for opportunity 
for public comment on proposed data collection projects, the National 
Cancer Institute (NCI), the National Institutes of Health (NIH) will 
publish periodic summaries of proposed projects to be submitted to the 
Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for review and approval.
    Proposed Collection: Title: National Cancer Institute Science 
Enrichment Program Evaluation: Follow-up Survey. Type of Information 
collection Request: New. Need and Use of Information Collection: NCI 
SEP is a five-week summer residential program on university campuses 
that services under-represented minority and under-served students who 
have just completed ninth grade. The program goals are to (1) Encourage 
student participants to select careers in science, mathematics, and/or 
research, and (2) broaden and enrich students' science, research, and 
sociocultural backgrounds. The proposed data collection is a follow-up 
survey of SEP students who participated in the program during one of 
three different funding cycles between 1990 and 2003, and a control 
group of students who did not participate in the program. The 
information from the proposed data collection will supplement previous 
evaluation results, which have been and will continue to be used to 
judge program process and outcomes. Frequency of Response: One time. 
Affected Public: Individuals or households. Type of Respondents: High 
school and college students, and young adults. Cost to Respondents: 
$5,872. The annual reporting burden is as follows:
