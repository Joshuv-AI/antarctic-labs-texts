Procedures Related to Motions; Correction

AGENCY: Postal Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the DATES section to a proposed rule 
published in the Federal Register of February 1, 2016. The Commission 
did not intend to permit interested persons to file reply comments. The 
Commission is seeking initial comments only.

DATES: Comments are due: March 2, 2016. There will be no reply comment 
period.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David A. Trissell, General Counsel, at 
202-789-6820.

Correction

    In proposed rule FR Doc. 2016-01735, beginning on page 5085 in the 
issue of February 1, 2016, make the following correction to the Dates 
section. On page 5085 in the first column, revise the DATES to read as 
follows:

DATES: Comments are due: March 2, 2016. There will be no reply comment 
period.

    By the Commission.
Stacy L. Ruble,
Secretary.
