DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 1, 2009, 8 a.m. to June 
2, 2009, 5 p.m., The Melrose Hotel, 2430 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC, 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on 
May 14, 2009, 74 FR 22755-22757.
    The meeting will be held June 2, 2009 to June 3, 2009. The meeting 
time and location remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 21, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
