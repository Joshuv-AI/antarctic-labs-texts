NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
[Notice 96-084]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: NationalAeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that J & J Engineering, Inc., of Bain 
Bridge Island, WA 98110, has applied for a partially exclusive license 
to practice the invention described in U.S. Patent No. 5,377,100, 
entitled ``Method of Encouraging Attention by Correlating Video Game 
Difficulty with Attention Level,'' which was issued on December 27, 
1994, to the United States of America as represented by the 
Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. 
Written objections to the prospective grant of a license should be sent 
to Mr. George F. Helfrich, Patent Counsel, Langley Research Center.

DATES: Response to this notice must be received by September 23, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. George F. Helfrich, Patent Counsel, Langley Research Center, Mail 
Code 212, Hampton, VA 23681; telephone (757) 864-9260; fax (757) 864-
9190.

General Counsel.
