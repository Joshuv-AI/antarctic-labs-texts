DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Change Notice for RTCA Program Management Committee

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of RTCA Program Management Committee meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public of a 
meeting of the RTCA Program Management Committee.

DATES: The meeting will be held December 9, 2003 starting at 9 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at RTCA, Inc., 1828 L Street, NW., 
Suite 805, Washington, DC 20036.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: RTCA Secretariat, 1828 L. Street, NW., 
Suite 850, Washington, DC 20036; telephone (202) 833-9339; fax (202) 
833-9434; Web site http://www.rtca.org.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal 
Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., Appendix 2), notice 
is hereby given for a Program Management Committee meeting. The revised 
agenda will include:

[sbull] December 9:
[sbull] Opening Session (Welcome and Introductory Remarks, Review/
Approve Summary of Previous Meeting)
    [sbull] Publication Consideration/Approval:
    [sbull] Final Draft, Aircraft Surveillance Applications (ASA) 
MASPS, RTCA Paper No. 208-03/PMC-303, prepared by SC-186.
[sbull] Discussion:
    [sbull] Special Committee 147, TCAS
    [sbull] Discuss/Approve Revised Terms of Reference
    [sbull] Special Committee 181
    [sbull] Final Report
    [sbull] Special Committee Chairman's Report
    [sbull] Action Item Review:
    [sbull] Review/Status--All open action items
    [sbull] Closing Session (Other Business, Document Production, Date 
and Place of Next Meeting, Adjourn)

    Attendance is open to the interested public but limited to space 
availability.

[[Page 66526]]

With the approval of the chairmen, members of the public may present 
oral statements at the meeting. Persons wishing to present statements 
or obtain information should contact the person listed in the FOR 
FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT section. Members of the public may present 
a written statement to the committee at any time.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on November 6, 2003.
Robert Zoldos,
FAA System Engineer, RTCA Advisory Committee.
