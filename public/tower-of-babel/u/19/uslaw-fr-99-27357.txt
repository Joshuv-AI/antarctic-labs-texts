TriState Pipeline, L.L.C.; Notice to Postpone the Public Meetings 
for the Proposed Tristate Pipeline Project

October 14, 1999.
    By letter dated October 12, 1999, TriState Pipeline, L.C.C. 
(TriState) requested that the Commission hold in abeyance its 
application until TriState files a project status report no later than 
January 15, 2000. Therefore, the staff is postponing the TriState 
Pipeline Project Draft Environmental Impact statement (DEIS) public 
meetings scheduled for October 20 and 21, 1999. The written comment 
period on the DEIS is extended until January 15, 2000, and the staff 
may reschedule the public meetings following their review of TriState's 
project report.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
