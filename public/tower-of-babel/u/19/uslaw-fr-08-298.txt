DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, February 20, 
2008, 8 a.m. to February 21, 2008, 1 p.m., Courtyard Marriott, 2899 
Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, VA 22202 which was published in the 
Federal Register on January 9, 2008, FR08-41.
    The panel name was changed from Patient Oriented and Career 
Enhancement Awards for Stem Cell Research to NHLBI-Patient Oriented 
Research (K23, 24, and 25's) Career Enhancement Awards. The rest of the 
information remains the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: January 18, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
