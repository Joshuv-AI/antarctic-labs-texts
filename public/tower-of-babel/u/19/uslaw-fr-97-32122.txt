DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. CP96-610-000]


Granite State Gas Transmission, Inc.; Notice Rescheduling Oral 
Argument

December 3, 1997.
    On September 24, 1997, the Commission issued an Order Setting 
Proceeding for Oral Argument in the above-docketed proceeding. On 
October 3, 1997, the Commission issued an order suspending the 
scheduled oral argument. By this notice, the oral argument which was 
previously scheduled for October 9, 1997, is rescheduled for Thursday, 
December 18, 1997, at 9:00 a.m. in a hearing room to be designated at 
the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission at 888 1st 
Street, N.E., Washington, D.C.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
