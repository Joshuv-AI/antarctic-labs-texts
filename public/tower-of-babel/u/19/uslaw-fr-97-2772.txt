DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. CP96-16-000 and CP96-16-001]


Transcontinental Gas Pipeline Corporation; Notice of Meeting

January 30, 1997.
    On February 6, 1997, Office of Pipeline Regulation Staff will meet 
with representatives of Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line Corporation 
(Transco) to discuss pre-filing matters for a compliance filing on 
compressor station architectural design required by Commission Order 
issued December 2, 1996 in Docket No. CP96-16-000 and 001. Transco 
requested this pre-filing meeting by letter filed January 29, 1997 in 
the subject docket.
    The meeting will be at 10:00 AM at the Commission's headquarters, 
888 First Street NE, Washington, DC.
Kevin P. Madden,
Director, Office of Pipeline Regulation.
