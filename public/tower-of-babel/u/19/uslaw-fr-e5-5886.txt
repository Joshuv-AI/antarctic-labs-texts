Nu-Gro Technologies, Inc., Gloversville, NY; Dismissal of 
Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Nu-Gro Technologies, Inc., 
Gloversville, New York. The application contained no new substantial 
information which would bear importantly on the Department's 
determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-57,781; Nu-Gro Technologies, Inc., Gloversville, New York 
(October 11, 2005).

    Signed at Washington, DC this 13th day of October 2005.
Douglas F. Small,
Acting Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
