Radio Broadcasting Services; Auxvasse, MO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division dismisses a Petition for Rule Making filed 
by Charles Crawford, requesting the allotment of Channel 235A at 
Auxvasse, Missouri, as its first local service. Charles Crawford, or no 
other party, filed comments supporting the allotment of Channel 235A at 
Auxvasse, Missouri. It is the Commission's policy to refrain from 
making a new allotment to a community absent an expression of interest.
