FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Deletion of Agenda Items From November 13th 
Open Meeting

November 12, 2003.
    The following items have been deleted from the list of Agenda items 
scheduled for consideration at the November 13, 2003, Open Meeting and 
previously listed in the Commission's Notice of November 6, 2003.

3 International

    Title: Procedures to Govern the Use of Satellite Earth Stations on 
Board Vessels in the 5925-6425 MHz/3700-4200 MHz Bands and 14.0-14.5 
GHz/11.7-12.2 GHz Bands (IB Docket No. 02-10).
    Summary: The Commission will consider a Notice of Proposed 
Rulemaking concerning Earth stations on board vessels that are used to 
provide broadband telecommunications services on passenger, government, 
cargo, and recreational vessels.

4 Office of Engineering and Technology

    Title: Revision of Parts 2 and 15 of the Commission's Rules to 
Permit Unlicensed National Information Infrastructure (UNII) devices in 
the 5 GHz band (ET Docket No. 03-122; RM-10371).
    Summary: The Commission will consider a Report and Order to provide 
an additional 255 MHz of spectrum for unlicensed wireless devices 
operating in the 5 GHz region of the spectrum.

Federal Communications Commission.
Marlene H. Dortch,
Secretary.
