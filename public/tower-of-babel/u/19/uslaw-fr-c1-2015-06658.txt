Correction

    In rule document 2015-06658, appearing on pages 16128-16222, in the 
Issue of March 26, 2015, make the following corrections:


Sec.  3162.3-3  Subsequent well operations; Hydraulic fracturing. 
[Corrected]

    1. On page 16218, in Sec.  3162.3-3 (a)(5), in the table in the 
first column, in the fifth row, the entry ``(5) Authorized drilling 
operations were completed after September 22, 2015.'' should read ``(5) 
Authorized drilling operations were completed after December 26, 
2014.''

    2. On the same page, in Sec.  3162.3-3, in the same table, in the 
first column, in the sixth row, the entry ``(6) Authorized drilling 
activities were completed before September 22, 2015'' should read ``(6) 
Authorized drilling activities were completed before December 26, 
2014.''
