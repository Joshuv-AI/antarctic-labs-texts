Announcement of the Availability of Financial Assistance and 
Request for Applications To Support Adoption Opportunities 
Demonstration Projects, Child Abuse and Neglect Discretionary 
Activities, Child Welfare Training Projects, and Abandoned Infants 
Assistance Awards

AGENCY: Children's Bureau, Administration on Children, Youth and

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the Notice that was 
published in the Federal Register on Thursday, April 13, 2000 (65 FR 
19904). The information in the ``Eligible Applicants'' sections of the 
notice is more restrictive than intended. For the correct less 
restrictive requirements please see the complete announcement package 
posted on the Children's Bureau website: http://www.acf.dhhs.gov/programs/cb/policy/cb200001.htm.

351-2293 or send an email to [email protected]. You can also contact Sally 
Flanzer, Children's Bureau, at 202-215-8914.
