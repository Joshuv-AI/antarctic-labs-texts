DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Notice of Performance Review Board Members

AGENCY: Corporate Senior Executive Management Office, Department of 
Veterans Affairs.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Agencies are required to publish a notice in the Federal 
Register of the appointment of Performance Review Board (PRB) members. 
This notice announces the appointment of individuals to serve on the 
PRB of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

DATES: The appointments are effective as of October 26, 2018.

ADDRESSES: Corporate Senior Executive Management Office, Department of 
Veterans Affairs, 810 Vermont Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20420.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Contact Tracey Therit, Acting 
Executive Director, Corporate Senior Executive Management Office 
(006D), Department of Veterans Affairs, 810 Vermont

[[Page 54979]]

Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20420, (202) 461-7865.
