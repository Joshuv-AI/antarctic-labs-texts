National Institutes of Health


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request; Pilot Study 
Evaluating the Cross-Cultural Equivalency of the Tobacco Use Supplement 
to the Current Survey (TUS-CPS); Correction

    As published in the Federal Register, September 16, 2004 (69 FR 
55824), the notice contains an error in the first sentence of the 
SUMMARY section. In referencing provisions of the Paperwork Reduction 
Act, we inadvertently cited section 3506(c)(2)(A). Accordingly, the 
referenced section is corrected to read ``3507(a)(1)(D).''

    Dated: September 22, 2004.
Rachelle Ragland-Greene,
NCI Project Clearance Liaison, National Institutes of Health.
