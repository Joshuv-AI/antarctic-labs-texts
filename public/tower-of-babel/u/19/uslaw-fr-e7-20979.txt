Towing Safety Advisory Committee; Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DHS.

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. App. (Pub. 
L. 92-463), the Secretary of Homeland Security has renewed the charter 
for the Towing Safety Advisory Committee (TSAC) for a 2-year period, 
from August 30, 2007 until August 30, 2009. This Committee advises the 
Coast Guard on matters related to shallow-draft inland and coastal 
waterway navigation and towing safety.

ADDRESSES: A copy of this notice and the Committee charter is available 
in our online docket, USCG-2007-0027, at http://www.regulations.gov. 
You may request a copy of the charter by writing Mr. Gerald Miante; 
Ass't. Executive Director of TSAC; U.S. Coast Guard Headquarters; CG-
5521, Room 1210; 2100 Second St., SW., Washington, DC 20593.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Miante at 202-372-1401 or via e-
mail at [email protected].
