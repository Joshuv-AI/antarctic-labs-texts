Hynix Semiconductor America, Inc., San Jose, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 1, 2002 in response to petition filed by the 
company on behalf of workers at Hynix Semiconductor America, Inc., San 
Jose, California.
    The petition regarding the investigation has been deemed invalid. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 15th day of November, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
