Bosal Industries Georgia, a Subsidiary of Bosal International 
North America Lavonia, GA and Bosal Industries Tennessee, a Subsidiary 
of Bosal International North America, Columbia, TN; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 8, 2007 in response to a 
petition filed by a Company Official and on behalf of workers at Bosal 
Industries Georgia, a subsidiary of Bosal International North America, 
Lavonia, Georgia (TA-W-60,918) and Bosal Industries Tennessee, a 
subsidiary of Bosal International North America, Columbia, Tennessee 
(TA-W-60,918A).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 9th day of March, 2007.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
