Sunshine Act Meeting Notice; Change of Time of Sunshine Act 
Meeting

AGENCY HOLDING THE MEETING: United States International Trade 
Commission.

DATE:  December 13, 2013.

NEW TIME:  9:30 a.m.

PLACE:  Room 101, 500 E Street SW., Washington, DC 20436, Telephone: 
(202) 205-2000.

STATUS: Open to the public.
    In accordance with 19 CFR Sec.  201.35(d)(1), the Commission hereby 
gives notice that the meeting of December 13, 2013 will be held at 9:30 
a.m.

not disposed of at the scheduled meeting, may be carried over to the 
agenda of the following meeting. Earlier notification of this change 
was not possible.
