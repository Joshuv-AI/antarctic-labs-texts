Self-Regulatory Organizations; MIAX PEARL LLC; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend the MIAX PEARL Options Fee 
Schedule To Increase the Monthly Fees for MIAX Express Network Full 
Service Port

May 9, 2022.
    On April 1, 2022, MIAX PEARL LLC (``MIAX Pearl'' or ``Exchange'') 
filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), 
pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 
(``Act''),\1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to 
amend the MIAX Pearl Options Fee Schedule to increase the monthly fees 
for the MIAX Express Network Full Service (``MEO'') Ports.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 15 U.S.C. 78s(b)(1).
    \2\ 17 CFR 240.19b-4.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The proposed rule change was immediately effective upon filing with 
the Commission pursuant to Section 19(b)(3)(A) of the Act.\3\ On April 
20, 2022, the proposed rule change was published for comment in the 
Federal Register and, pursuant to Section 19(b)(3)(C) of the Act,\4\ 
the Commission: (1) Temporarily suspended the proposed rule change; and 
(2) instituted proceedings under Section 19(b)(2)(B) of the Act \5\ to 
determine whether to approve or disapprove the proposed rule change.\6\ 
On May 2, 2022, the Exchange withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-
PEARL-2022-12).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

or not the person is a member of the self-regulatory organization.'' 
15 U.S.C. 78s(b)(3)(A)(ii).
