DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Project No. 6759-016]


Aquenergy Systems, Inc.; Notice of Site Visit to Apalache 
Hydroelectric Project

June 2, 1999.
    Take notice that Commission staff will hold a site visit with 
Aquenergy Systems, Inc., licensee for the constructed Apalache 
Hydroelectric Project, FERC No. 6759-016. The project is located on the 
South Tyger River off Highway 357 in Greer, South Carolina 29651. The 
site visit will be held on Wednesday, June 16, 1999, from 11:30 a.m. to 
approximately 2:00 p.m.
    The purpose of this visit is to enable Commission staff responsible 
for preparing the environmental assessment (EA) of the proposed 
surrender of license to view the existing dam, reservoir, and nearby 
areas. All interested individuals, organizations, and agencies are 
invited to attend the site visit.
    Participants will meet at the parking lot adjacent to the project 
powerhouse, near the old Apalache Mill. Participants should provide 
their transportation to and from the site.
    If you have any questions concerning this matter, please contact 
Jim Haimes, EA Coordinator for the Commission, at (202) 219-2780 or 
Beth Harris, Project Engineer for the licensee, in Greenville, South 
Carolina, at (864) 281-9630.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
