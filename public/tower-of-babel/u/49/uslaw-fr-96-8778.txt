DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Endangered and Threatened Species Permits Issued

AGENCY: Fish and Wildlife Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Permits Issued for the Months of October 1995 through 
March 1996.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 15820]]


    Notice is hereby given that the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service has 
taken the following action with regard to permit applications duly 
received in accordance with section 10 of the Endangered Species Act of 
1973, as amended (16 U.S.C. 1539, et seq.). Each permit listed as 
issued was granted only after it was determined that it was applied for 
in good faith, that by granting the permit it will not be to the 
disadvantage of the endangered species, and that it will be consistent 
with the purposes and policy set forth in the Endangered Species Act of 
1973, as amended.
