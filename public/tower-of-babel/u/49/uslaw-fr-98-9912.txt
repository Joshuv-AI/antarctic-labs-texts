DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices; Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Advisory Committee on Immunization 
Practices (ACIP), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), 
Department of Health and Human Services, has been renewed for a 2-year 
period beginning April 1, 1998, through April 1, 2000.
    For further information, contact Dixie E. Snider, Jr., M.D., 
Executive Secretary, ACIP, CDC, 1600 Clifton Road, NE,
(M/S D-50), telephone 404/639-7240 or fax 404/639-7342.

    Dated: April 9, 1998.
Nancy C. Hirsch,
Acting Director, Management Analysis and Services Office, Centers for 
Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
