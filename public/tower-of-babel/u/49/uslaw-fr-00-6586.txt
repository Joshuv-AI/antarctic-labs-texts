Notice of Filing of Plat of an Island; Minnesota, Suspended

    On Tuesday, January 18, 2000, there was published in the Federal 
Register, Volume 5, Number 11, on page 2640, a notice entitled, 
``Notice of Filing of Plat of an Island; Minnesota.'' Said notice 
referenced the filing of the plat of the survey of an island in Cedar 
Lake, Township 117 North, Range 30 West, Fifth Principal Meridian, 
Minnesota, accepted on January 6, 2000.
    This plat officially filed on February 22, 2000, is hereby 
suspended pending the consideration of a protest against the survey.

    Dated: March 7, 2000.
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
