SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the provisions of the 
Government in the Sunshine Act, Pub. L. 94-409, that the Securities and 
Exchange Commission will hold an Open Meeting on Monday, April 21, 2008 
at 10 a.m. in the Auditorium, Room L-002.
    Chairman Cox, as duty officer, determined that no earlier notice 
thereof was possible.
    The subject matter of the Open Meeting scheduled for April 21, 2008 
will be:
    The Commission will consider whether to propose amendments to 
provide for corporate financial statement information to be filed with 
the Commission in interactive data format, and a near- and long-term 
schedule therefor.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items.
    For further information and to ascertain what, if any, matters have 
been added, deleted or postponed, please contact:
    The Office of the Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: April 15, 2008.
Nancy M. Morris,
Secretary.
