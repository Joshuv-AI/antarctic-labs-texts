Pricing for the 2014 50th Anniversary Kennedy Half-Dollar Gold

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the price of the 2014 
50th Anniversary Kennedy Half-Dollar Gold Proof Coin. This coin will be 
offered for sale based on the following pricing grid:
