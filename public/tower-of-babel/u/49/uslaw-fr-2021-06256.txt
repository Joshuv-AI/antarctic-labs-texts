DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration


Advisory Committee for Women's Services; Notice of Meeting 
Cancellation

published a notice in the Federal Register concerning a meeting of the 
Advisory Committee for Women's Services. The meeting scheduled for 
Wednesday, March 31, 2021 at 1 p.m. is cancelled. The notice is in the 
Federal Register of Wednesday, March 10, 2021, in FR Doc. 2021-04935, 
on page 13725.
