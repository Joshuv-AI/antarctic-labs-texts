Radio Broadcasting Services; Fremont and Sunnyvale, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 66 FR 63654 
(December 10, 2001) this document reallots Channel 285A from Fremont, 
California to Sunnyvale, California and provides Sunnyvale with its 
first local aural transmission service. The coordinates for Channel 
285A at Sunnyvale are 37-18-41 North Latitude and 121-48-58 West 
Longitude.

DATES: Effective July 29, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
