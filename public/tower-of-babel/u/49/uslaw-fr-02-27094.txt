Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

October 18, 2002.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of these 
documents are available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 
12th Street, SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the 
Commission's copy contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. 
Oppositions to these petitions must be filed by November 8, 2002. See 
Section 1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies 
to an opposition must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing 
oppositions has expired.
