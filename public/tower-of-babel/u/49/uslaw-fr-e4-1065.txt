Warner Electric, Inc., a Subsidiary of Colfax Corporation, 
Roscoe, IL; Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Warner Electric, Inc., a 
subsidiary of Colfax Corporation, Roscoe, Illinois. The application 
contained no new substantial information which would bear importantly 
on the Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the 
application was issued.

TA-W-53,973; Warner Electric, Inc., a subsidiary of Colfax 
Corporation, Roscoe, Illinois (April 28, 2004).


    Signed in Washington, DC this 3rd day of May, 2004.
Timothy Sullivan,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
