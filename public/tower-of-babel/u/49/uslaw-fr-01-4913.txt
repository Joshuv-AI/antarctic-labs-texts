47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-486, MM Docket No. 00-188, RM-9969]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; New Orleans, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of WWL-TV, Inc., licensee of 
station WWL-TV, substitutes DTV channel 36 for DTV channel 30 at New 
Orleans, Louisiana. See 65 FR 60163, October 10, 2000. DTV channel 36 
can be allotted to New Orleans in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates (29-54-23 N. and 90-02-23 W.) with a power of 1000, HAAT of 
305 meters and with a DTV service population of 1712 thousand.

DATES: Effective April 12, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
