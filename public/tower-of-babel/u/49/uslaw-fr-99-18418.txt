DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-36,321]


Blanch, Div. of Warnaco, New York, New York; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 1, 1999 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on June 1, 1999 on behalf of workers at Blanch, a division of 
Warnaco, located in New York, New York.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-35,700). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 11th day of June, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
