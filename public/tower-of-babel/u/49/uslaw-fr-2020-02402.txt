DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, April 16, 4:00 p.m. to April 
17, 2020, 5:00 p.m., Bethesda North Marriott Hotel & Conference Hotel, 
5701 Marinelli Road, Rockville, MD, 20850 which was published in the 
Federal Register on January 30, 2020, 85 FR 5456.
    This meeting notice is amended to change the meeting dates and 
start and end times. The meeting will now be held on April 15, 2020, 
3:00 p.m. to April 16, 2020, 6:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: February 3, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
