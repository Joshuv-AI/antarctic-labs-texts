Annova LNG Common Infrastructure, LLC, Annova LNG Brownsville A, 
LLC, Annova LNG Brownsville B, LLC, Annova LNG Brownsville C, LLC; 
Notice of Reopening of Comment Period

    On December 14, 2018, the Commission issued a notice setting 
February 4, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments on 
the Annova LNG Brownsville Project draft environmental impact 
statement. Due to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies between 
December 22, 2018 and January 25, 2019, the Commission is reopening the 
comment period until March 13, 2019.

    Dated: February 7, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
