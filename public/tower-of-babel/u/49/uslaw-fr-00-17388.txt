Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Foreign-Trade Zone 27--Boston, MA; Application for Subzone 
Status, J. Baker, Inc. (Distribution of Apparel, Footwear and 
Accessories), Canton, MA; Amendment of Application

    Notice is hereby given that the application of the Massachusetts 
Port Authority, grantee of FTZ 27, requesting authority for special-
purpose subzone status for the apparel, footwear and accessories 
distribution facility of J. Baker, Inc. (J. Baker), has been amended to 
include an additional site at 120 Shawmut Road (67,200 sq. ft.), 
Canton, Massachusetts.
    The application otherwise remains unchanged.
    The comment period is reopened to August 10, 2000.

    Dated: June 29, 2000.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Acting Executive Secretary.
