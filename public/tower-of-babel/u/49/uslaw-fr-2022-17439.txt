COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Florida Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Federal Register concerning a briefing of the Florida Advisory 
Committee. The briefing scheduled for Wednesday, August 24, 2022, at

Monday, August 1, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-16357, in the third column of 
page 46937 and the first column of page 46938.
