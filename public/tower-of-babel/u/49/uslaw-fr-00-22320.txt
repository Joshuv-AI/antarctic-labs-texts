ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 5-0 on August 21, the Commission 
determined pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the

for Partial Reconsideration of CLI-00-08'' be held on August 21, and on
