DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute of Mental Health; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Mental Health Initial Review Group, Psychobiology, 
Behavior, and Neuroscience Review Committee, which was published in the 
Federal Register on April 17, 1995 (60 CFR 19268).
    This committee was to have convened at 9 a.m. on June 19 at the 
Ramada Inn Rockville in Rockville, Maryland. The location has been 
changed to the Crowne Plaza Holiday Inn, 1750 Rockville Pike, 
Rockville, MD 20857.

    Dated: May 24, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
