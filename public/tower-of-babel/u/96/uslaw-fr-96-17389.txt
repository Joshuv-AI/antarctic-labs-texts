DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


The National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) 
Scientific and Technical Meetings on Occupational Exposure to Asphalt 
During Roofing and Paving Operations: Correction

Federal Register Citations of Previous Announcements: 61 FR 28590-
28591--dated June 5, 1996, and 61 FR 30242--dated June 14, 1996.

SUMMARY: Notice is given that the purpose of these meetings on July 8-
10, 1996, and July 22-24, 1996, has been restructured to address only 
those issues relevant to the control of asphalt exposures (e.g., 
engineering controls, work practices). The draft NIOSH ``Working Paper 
on Occupational Exposure to Asphalt'' will not be discussed at these 
meetings. The meeting times, dates, place, and status remain unchanged.

Office, 4676 Columbia Parkway, M/S C-34, Cincinnati, Ohio 45226.
