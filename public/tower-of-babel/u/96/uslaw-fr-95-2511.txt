FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR Part 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contract the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, D.C. 20573.

Synergy Logistics Services, 3912 Brayton Ave Long Beach, CA 90807, 
Thomas David Daniels, Sole Proprietor
Ford Freight Forwarders, Inc., 12861 S.W. 147th Terrace Rd., Miami, FL 
33186, Officer: Santiago Lostorto, President
Freight Service Network Inc., 914 Sivert Drive, Wood Dale, IL 60191, 
Officers: Sadru Rasool, President, Grace Kung, Treasurer

    Dated: January 27, 1995.

    By the Federal Maritime Commission.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
