CORPORATION FOR NATIONAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICE


Learn and Serve America National Clearinghouse

AGENCY: Corporation for National and Community Service.

ACTION: Notice of correction of dates and award amount.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects the date of availability for the 
application guidelines, the application deadline, and the first year's 
award amount published in the Federal Register on June 9, 1997 (62 FR 
31417, 31418). The new dates are revised in the notice as follows: 
``Application guidelines will be available August 15, 1997. 
Applications must be submitted to the Corporation no later than 3:00 
p.m. (EST) September 30, 1997.''. The new award amount is revised as 
follows: ``The first year's award will total approximately $750,000.''.

    Dated: August 19, 1997.
Stewart A. Davis,
Acting General Counsel, Corporation for National and Community Service.
