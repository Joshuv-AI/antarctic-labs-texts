AGENCY: Postal Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

SUMMARY: The Commission is updating the product lists. This action 
reflects a publication policy adopted by Commission order. The 
referenced policy assumes periodic updates. The updates are identified 
in the body of this document. The product lists, which is re-published 
in its entirety, includes these updates.
