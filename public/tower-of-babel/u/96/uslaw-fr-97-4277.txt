Notice of Realty Action; Classification of Public Lands for 
Recreation and Public Purposes, Kern County, California; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In notice document 97-1899 beginning on page 3911 in the issue 
of Monday, January 27, 1997, make the following correction: The legal 
land description was written as follows:

San Bernardino Meridian

T.9S., R.13W.,
    Section 14 Lots 6-7 (excluding MS 5254, MS 5210, and MS 5217)

    Containing 15.41 acres, more or less.

    The legal description should be changed to read as follows:

San Bernardino Meridian

T.9N., R.13W.,
    Section 14 Lots 6-7 (excluding MS 5254 and MS 5217)

    Containing 15.41 acres, more or less.
Lee Delaney,
Area Manager.
