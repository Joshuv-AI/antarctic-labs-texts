ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY
[FRL-4877-7]

 

Jones Tire & Battery Site; Notice of Proposed Settlement

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice of proposed De Minimis settlement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under section 122(g)(4) of the Comprehensive Environmental 
Response, Compensation and Liability Act (CERCLA), the Environmental 
Protection Agency (EPA) has offered to 217 potentially responsible 
parties an Administrative Order on Consent (AOC) to settle claims for 
past and future removal actions at the Jones Tire & Battery Site, 
Birmingham, Alabama. EPA will consider public comments on the proposed 
settlement for thirty (30) days. EPA may withdraw from or modify the 
proposed settlement should such comments disclose facts or 
considerations which indicate the proposed settlement is inappropriate, 
improper, or inadequate. Copies of the proposed settlement are 
available from: Ms. Ann M. Mayweather, Waste Management Division, U.S. 
EPA, Region IV, 345 Courtland Street NE., Atlanta, Georgia 30365, (404) 
347-5059.
    Written comments may be submitted to Ms. Mayweather within 30 days 
of the date of publication.

    Dated: April 8, 1994.
Richard D. Green,
Acting Director.
