DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering Special Emphasis 
Panel, October 3, 2013, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, Two Democracy Plaza, Suite 920, 6707 Democracy Boulevard, 
Bethesda, MD 20892, which was published in the Federal Register on June 
21, 2013, 78 FR 37557.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date from October 3, 
2013, to November 4-6, 2013. The time and location remains the same. 
The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 23, 2013.
David Clary,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
