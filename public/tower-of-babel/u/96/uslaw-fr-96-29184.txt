SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area # 2895]


Virginia; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area; Amendment # 5

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency, dated October 31, 1996, the above-numbered Declaration is 
hereby amended to include the Independent City of Hampton in the 
Commonwealth of Virginia as a disaster area due to damages caused by 
Hurricane Fran and associated severe storm conditions, including high 
winds, tornadoes, wind driven rain, and river and flash flooding from 
September 5 through September 23, 1996.
    Any counties contiguous to the above-named city and not listed 
herein have been previously declared.

[[Page 58436]]

    The deadline for filing applications for physical damage for the 
above-mentioned city is December 23, 1996, and for loans for economic 
injury the deadline is June 9, 1997. All other information remains the 
same.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: November 6, 1996.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
