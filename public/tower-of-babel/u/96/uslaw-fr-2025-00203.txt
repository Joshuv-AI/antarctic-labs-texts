United States Mint


Pricing for the 2025 United States Marine Corps 250th Anniversary 
Commemorative Coin Program and the 2025 Precious Metals Grid

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing pricing for the 2025 
United States Marine Corps 250th Anniversary Commemorative Coin Program 
as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                 Introductory    Regular
                     Coin                            price        price
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Silver Proof..................................          $91.00    $96.00
Silver Uncirculated...........................           89.50     94.50
Clad Proof....................................           56.00     61.00
Clad Uncirculated.............................           54.00     59.00
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Products containing gold coins will be priced according to the 
Pricing of Numismatic and Commemorative Gold and Platinum Products Grid 
posted at www.usmint.gov.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rosa Matos, Acting Sr. Program Manager 
for Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW, 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.
