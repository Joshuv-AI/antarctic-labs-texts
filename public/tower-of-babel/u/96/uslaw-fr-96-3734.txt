DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Advisory Committees; Renewals

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announces the renewal 
of certain FDA advisory committees by the Commissioner of Food and 
Drugs. The Commissioner has determined that it is in the public 
interest to renew the charters of the committees listed below for an 
additional 2 years beyond charter expiration date. The new charters 
will be in effect until the dates of expiration listed below. This 
notice is issued under the Federal Advisory Committee Act of October 6, 
1972 (Pub. L. 92-463 (5 U.S.C. app. 2)).

DATE: Authority for these committees will expire on the dates indicated 
below unless the Commissioner formally determines that renewal is in 
the public interest.
