AIM Pipeline Company; Notice of Informal Settlement Conference

August 20, 1997.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Thursday, September 4, 1997, at 
10:00 A.M. in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 
20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Fred Ni at (202) 208-2218.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
