COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Commonwealth of the Northern 
Mariana Islands Advisory Committee; Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of virtual business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a virtual business meeting of the 
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands Advisory Committee. The 
meeting scheduled for Friday, February 10, 2023, at 8:30 a.m. (ChST) is 
cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of Friday, November 4, 
2022, in FR Doc. 2022-24056 in the
