CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Meeting Notice

TIME AND DATE: June 16, 1999, Wednesday, 10:00 a.m.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the public.


[[Page 30966]]


MATTER TO BE CONSIDERED:

Children's Sleepwear Amendment Revocation

    The Commission will consider options related to the proposed 
revocation of the amendments to the children's sleepwear flammability 
standards issued in 1996 and 1999.
    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207, (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: June 7, 1999.
Sadye E. Dunn,
Secretary.
