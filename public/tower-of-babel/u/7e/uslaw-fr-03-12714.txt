SUMMARY: The Minerals Management Service published a document in the 
Federal Register of April 29, 2003, concerning major portion prices and 
due dates for additional royalty payments on Indian gas production in 
designated areas not associated with an index zone. Information was 
erroneously omitted from the table.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: John Barder, 303-231-3702.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of April 29, 2003, in FR Doc. 03-10534, on 
page 22736, the second entry of the table is corrected to read:

     MMS-designated areas          (MMBtu)    2001 (MMBtu)  2001 (MMBtu)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
