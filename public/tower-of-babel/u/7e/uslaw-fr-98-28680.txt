SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Rocky Mountain States Regional Fairness Board Strategy Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Rocky Mountain States 
Regional Fairness Board Strategy Meeting, to be held on October 21, 
1998, starting at 10:30 am at 721 19th Street (Room To Be Determined 
and Posted at Building Entrance), Denver, CO 80202, to collect Fairness 
Board members' comments on the 4/20/98 proceedings, as well as to 
obtain recommendations and other input for the annual Report to 
Congress.
    For further information contact Gary P. Peele, telephone (312) 353-
0880.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
