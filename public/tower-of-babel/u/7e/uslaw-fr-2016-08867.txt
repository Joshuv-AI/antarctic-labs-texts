NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Business and Operations Advisory Committee Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:
    Name: Business and Operations Advisory Committee (9556).
    Date/Time: May 11, 2016; 1:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. (EST) May 12, 2016; 
9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. (EST).
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, 
Arlington, Virginia 22230; Stafford I, Room 1235.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Patty Balanga, National Science Foundation, 4201 
Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230; (703) 292-8100.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice concerning issues related to 
the oversight, integrity, development and enhancement of NSF's business 
operations.

Agenda

Wednesday, May 11, 2016; 1:00 p.m.-5:30 p.m.

    Welcome/Introductions; BFA/OIRM Updates; Enterprise Risk 
Management; From Systems to Data and Beyond; Benchmarking; 
Recommendations of the National Academy of Public Administration (NAPA) 
Study of NSF's Use of Cooperative Agreements to Support Large Scale 
Investments in Science and Technology.

Thursday, May 12, 2016; 9:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m.

    Modernization of Business Processes and Workforce Structures: A 
Discussion of Lessons Learned; Discussion with Chief Operating Officer; 
Meeting Wrap-Up.

    Dated: April 13, 2016.
Crystal Robinson,
Committee Management Officer.
