NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards Subcommittee Meeting on 
Safeguards and Security; Notice of Meeting

    For the closed meeting of the ACRS Subcommittee on Safeguards and 
Security scheduled for February 21, 2003, starting time has been 
changed to 8:30 a.m. instead of 1 p.m. in the NRC Auditorium, 11545 
Rockville Pike, Rockville, Maryland.
    Notice of this meeting was published in the Federal Register on 
Tuesday, February 4, 2003 (68 FR 5667). All other items pertaining to 
this meeting remain the same as previously published.
    Further information contact: Dr. Richard P. Savio (telephone 301/
415-7363) between 7:30 a.m. and 4:15 p.m. (ET).

Sher Bahadur,
