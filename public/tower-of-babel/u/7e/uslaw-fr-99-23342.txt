DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY


United States Secret Service

Action: Appointment of performance review board (PRB) members.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    This notice announces the appointment of members of the Senior 
Executive Service Performance Review Boards in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 
4314(c)(4) for the rating period beginning October 1, 1998, and ending 
September 30, 1999. Each PRB will be composed of at least three of the 
Senior Executive Service members listed below:
Name and Title
Bruce J. Bowen: Deputy Director (USSS)
Jane E. Vezeris: Assistant Director, Administration (USSS)
H. Terrence Samway: Assistant Director, Government Liaison and Public 
Affairs (USSS)
Gordon S. Heddell: Assistant Director, Inspection (USSS)
Kevin T. Foley: Assistant Director, Investigations (USSS)
Carlton D. Spriggs: Assistant Director, Protective Operations (USSS)
Barbara S. Riggs: Assistant Director, Protective Research (USSS)
Charles N. DeVita: Assistant Director, Training (USSS)
John J. Kelleher: Chief Counsel (USSS)

FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION CONTACT: Sheila M. Lumsden, Chief, Personnel 
Division, 950 H Street, Suite 7300, Washington, DC 20001, Telephone No. 
(202) 406-5635.

Jane E. Vezeris,
Acting Director.
