DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice of Advisory Committee meeting date change.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Friday, December 13, 2002 (67 FR 76728), the Department of 
Defense announced closed meetings of the Defense Science Board (DSB) 
Task Force on Seabasing. The meetings originally scheduled for February 
25-26, 2003, have been changed to February 26-27, 2003. The meetings 
will be held at Strategic Analysis Inc., 3601 Wilson Boulevard, Suite 
600, Arlington, VA.

    Dated: February 12, 2003.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
