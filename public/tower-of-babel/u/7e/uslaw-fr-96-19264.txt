Pacific Gas and Electric Company; San Diego Gas and Electric 
Company; Southern California Edison Company; Notice of Possible 
Availability of Technical Conference Broadcast

July 24, 1996.
    On July 15, 1996, the Commission issued a notice of Technical 
Conference to be held on August 1, 1996, in these proceedings. If there 
is sufficient interest, the Capitol Connection may broadcast the 
technical conference on August 1, 1996, to interested persons. The 
conference would be broadcast nationally. However, due to prior 
commitments, there will be no local broadcast of the conference. 
Persons interested in receiving the broadcast for a fee should contact 
Julia Morelli at the Capitol Connection ((703) 993-3100) no later than 
Friday, July 26, 1996.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
