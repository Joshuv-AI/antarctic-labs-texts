PRESIDENTIAL COMMISSION ON THE ASSIGNMENT OF WOMEN IN THE ARMED FORCES

32 CFR Chapter XXIX


Removal of CFR Chapter

    Since the Presidential Commission on the Assignment of Women in the 
Armed Forces is legally terminated and its regulations are no longer in 
force and effect, the Office of the Federal Register is removing 32 CFR 
Chapter XXIX from

[[Page 30543]]

the Code of Federal Regulations, in compliance with the provisions in 1 
CFR 8.2.
    Accordingly, 32 CFR is amended by removing part 2900 and vacating 
chapter XXIX.
