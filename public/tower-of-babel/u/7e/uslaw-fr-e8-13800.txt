DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, July 8, 2008, 8 a.m. to July 
8, 2008, 4:30 p.m., One Washington Circle Hotel, One Washington Circle, 
NW., Washington, DC, 20037 which was published in the Federal Register 
on June 2, 2008, 73 FR 31493-31495.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the applications being withdrawn.

    Dated: June 12, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
