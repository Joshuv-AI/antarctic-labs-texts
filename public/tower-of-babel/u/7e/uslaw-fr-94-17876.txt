DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
 

Air Traffic Control Tower; Pine Bluff, AR; Decommissioning

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration [FAA], DOT.

ACTION: Notice of Decommissioning.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that on or about July 29, 1994, the 
Airport Traffic Control Tower at Pine Bluff, Arkansas, will be 
decommissioned. This information will be reflected in the FAA 
Organization Statement the next time it is issued.

49 U.S.C. 1348, 1354(a); 49 U.S.C. 106(g).

    Issued in Fort Worth, Texas, on July 6, 1994.
Wm. Jack Sasser,
Acting Regional Administrator, Southwest Region.
