Anthology, Inc., A Division of R. R. Donnelley & Sons Company, 
Pre-Media Technologies Division; Arlington Heights, IL; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on December 23, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of the workers at Anthology, Inc., a Division 
of R. R. Donnelley & Sons Company, Pre-Media Technologies Division, 
Arlington Heights, Illinois.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 17th day of March 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
