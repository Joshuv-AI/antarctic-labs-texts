DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 11, 2013, 08:00 a.m. 
to October 11, 2013, 06:00 p.m., Doubletree Hotel Washington, 1515 
Rhode Island Ave. NW., Washington, DC 20005 which was published in the 
Federal Register on September 12, 2013, 78 FR 56239.
    The meeting will be held on December 13, 2013 from 11:30 a.m. to 
12:30 p.m. The meeting location remains the same. The meeting is closed 
to the public.

    Dated: October 29, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
