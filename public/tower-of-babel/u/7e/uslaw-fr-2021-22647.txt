    Title of the Collection: Project Abstract Summary.
    Type of Collection: Renewal.
    OMB No.: 4040-0019.

Abstract

    The Project Abstract Summary form provides the Federal grant-making 
agencies an alternative to the Standard Form 424 data set and form. 
Agencies may use Project Abstract Summary form for grant programs not 
required to collect all the data that is required on the SF-424 core 
data set and form. Project Abstract Summary form is used by 
organizations to apply for Federal financial assistance in the form of 
grants. This form is evaluated by Federal agencies as part of the 
overall grant application. This IC expires on February 28, 2022. 
Grants.gov seeks a three-year clearance of these collections.
