DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2101-159]


Sacramento Municipal Utility District's Proposed Whitewater 
Recreation Management Plan for the Upper American River Hydroelectric 
Project No. 2101; Comment Period Extension

    On January 22, 2019, the Commission issued a notice through the 
FERC eLibrary system \1\ setting February 21, 2019, as the end of the 
formal period to file comments, motions to intervene, and protests on 
P-2101-159: Sacramento Municipal Utility District's proposed whitewater 
recreation management plan for the Upper American River Hydroelectric 
Project No. 2101, located on the Rubicon River, Silver Creek, and South 
Fork American River in El Dorado and Sacramento counties, California. 
Due to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies between December 
22, 2018, and January 25, 2019, the Commission is extending the comment 
period until February 28, 2019.
