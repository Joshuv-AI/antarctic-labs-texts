GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Interagency Committee for Medical Records (ICMR) Revision of SF 
533, Medical Record--Prenatal and Pregnancy

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration/ICMR revised the SF

[[Page 20996]]

533, Medical Record--Prenatal and Pregnancy to comply with the 
Antepartum College of Obstetrics and Gynecology standards. You can 
obtain the updated form through the Federal Supply Service using 
National Stock Number 7540-00-634-4276 (Revision 12/1999).
    You can also obtain a sample copy from the internet. Address: 
http://www.gsa.gov/forms/forms.htm

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Barbara Williams, General Services 
Administration, (202) 501-0581.

DATES: Effective April 26, 2001.

    Dated: April 11, 2001.
Barbara M. Williams,
Deputy Standard and Optional Forms Management Officer, General Services 
Administration.
