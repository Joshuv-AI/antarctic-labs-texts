DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Reclamation


Glen Canyon Dam Adaptive Management Work Group (AMWG), Notice of 
Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Reclamation, Interior.

ACTION: Cancellation of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Reclamation is canceling the Adaptive Management 
Work Group Meeting scheduled for January 7-8, 2004, in Phoenix, Arizona 
due to additional time required to prepare the Fiscal Year 2005 budget 
and other agenda items. The meeting will be rescheduled for March 2004 
and will be noticed in the Federal Register when arrangements have been 
made.
