Clifton Power Corporation; Commissioner of Public Works of the 
City of Spartanburg, SC; Notice of Application for Transfer of License 
and Soliciting Comments and Motions To Intervene

January 7, 2010.
    On November 4, 2009, Commissioner of Public Works of the City of 
Spartanburg, South Carolina (Applicant) filed an application for an 
involuntary transfer of license of the Clifton Mills 1 
Hydroelectric Project No. 4632 located on the Pacolet River, in 
Spartanburg County, South Carolina.
    The Applicant states it acquired the Clifton Mills 1 
Hydroelectric Project from Clifton Power Corporation through 
foreclosure and seeks Commission approval for the involuntary transfer 
of the license for the project from Clifton Power Corporation to the 
Applicant.
