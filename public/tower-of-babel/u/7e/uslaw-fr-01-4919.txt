Radio Broadcasting Services; Graham, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Graham Tollway Broadcasting Company proposed the allotment of 
Channel 253A at Graham, Texas. See 64 FR 36322, July 6, 1999. The 
proposal for Graham has been withdrawn with no other interest expressed 
in an allotment at Graham. A counterproposal was filed by North Texas 
Radio Group, L.P., proposing changes at Bridgeport, Bonham, Palestine, 
Price, Range and Stephenville, Texas and Ardmore, Lawton, Tecumseh and 
Fort Towson, Oklahoma (RM-9828). Although the counterproposal was 
placed on public notice, it was found to be technically unacceptable 
and has been dismissed. Therefore, the petition and counterproposal 
have been dismissed, with no action taken with respect to the above-
listed communities.
