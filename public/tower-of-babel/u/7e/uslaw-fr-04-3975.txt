FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Radio Broadcasting Services; Woodson, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 66 FR 39128 
(July 27, 2001), this Report and Order dismisses the Petition for Rule 
Making in MM Docket No. 01-157, proposing to allot Channel 298A at 
Woodson, Texas. The petitioner had requested this dismissal.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria McCauley, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
