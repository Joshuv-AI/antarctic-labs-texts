Employment and Training Administration
[NAFTA 3593]


Sensory Devices, Inc. Waukesha, WI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 22, 1999, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Sensory Devices, Inc., Waukesha, Wisconsin.
    The petitioning group of workers has requested that its petition 
for Transitional Trade Adjustment Assistance be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 14th day of December, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
