SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
 

National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board; Public 
Meeting

    The National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board will 
hold a public meeting on September 18, 1994, through noon September 20, 
1994, at the University of Hawaii Small Business Development Center, 
523 West Lanikaula Street, Hilo, Hawaii.
    The purpose of the meeting is to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by Advisory Board members, staff of the SBA, or others 
present.
    For further information, write or call Judith Dunn, SBA, 5th Flr., 
409 3rd Street, S.W., Washington, D.C. 20416, telephone 202/205-7301.
Johnnie L. Albertson,
Associate Administrator for Small Business Development Centers.
