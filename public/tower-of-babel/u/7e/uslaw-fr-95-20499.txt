DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management
[NV-930-1430-01; N-57841]


Notice of Realty Action: Non-Competitive Sale of Public Lands

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management.

ACTION: Non-competitive sale of public lands in Clark County, NV.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A Notice of Realty Action was previously published in the 
Federal Register, pages 59787 and 59788, volume 59, No. 222, on 
November 18, 1994, segregating certain described land from all other 
forms of appropriation under the public land laws, including the 
general mining laws, except for sales and disposals under the mineral 
disposal laws.
    This Notice of Realty Action extends the segregation period an 
additional 270 days until May 14, 1996.

    Dated: August 14, 1995.
Michael F. Dwyer,
District Manager, Las Vegas, NV.
