NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Notice of Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications and 
Amendments to Facility Operating Licenses Involving No Significant 
Hazards Considerations

    On September 20, 2000, the Federal Register published the Biweekly 
Notice of Applications and Amendments to Facility Operating Licenses 
Involving Notice of Issuance of Amendments. On page 56964, column 2, 
TXU Electric, Docket Nos. 50-445 and 50-446, Comanche Peak Steam 
Electric Station, Unit Nos. 1 and 2, the numbers of the amendments 
issued should be Amendment Nos. 79 and 79.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 21st day of September 2000.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
John A. Zwolinski,
Director, Division of Licensing Project Management, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
