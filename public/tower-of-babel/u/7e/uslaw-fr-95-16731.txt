Medalist, Apparel, Inc., Reading, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 12, 1995 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Medalist Apparel, Inc., Reading, 
Pennsylvania.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-30,583A). Consequently, further investigation 
in this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 23rd day of June, 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
