    Take notice that the Commission has received the following Natural 
Gas Pipeline Rate and Refund Report filings:

Filings Instituting Proceedings

    Docket Numbers: RP22-1244-000.
    Applicants: Carlsbad Gateway, LLC.
    Description: Petition for Approval of Settlement of Carlsbad 
Gateway, LLC.
    Filed Date: 9/26/22.
    Accession Number: 20220926-5118.
    Comment Date: 5 p.m. ET 10/11/22.

    Docket Numbers: RP22-1266-000.
    Applicants: Energ[iacute]a de Baja California, S. de R.L. de C.V., 
ENERGIA DE BAJA CALIFORNIA, S, DE, RL DE CV.

Pipeline, LLC.
    Filed Date: 9/29/22.
    Accession Number: 20220929-5072.
    Comment Date: 5 p.m. ET 10/04/22.

proceedings must file in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the 
Commission's Regulations (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214) on or before 5 
p.m. eastern time on the specified comment date. Protests may be 
considered, but intervention is necessary to become a party to the 
proceeding.
