DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Defense Partnership Council Meeting; Cancellation


Office of the Secretary

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 5, 2000 (65 FR 26190), the Department of Defense 
published a notice to announce a meeting of the Defense Partnership 
Council meeting to be held on May 23, 2000. This notice is to announce 
the cancellation of the meeting due to conflicts in members' schedules.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Ben James, Chief, Labor Relations 
Branch, Field Advisory Services Division, Defense Civilian Personnel 
Management Service, 1400 Key Boulevard, Suite B-200, Arlington, VA 
22209-5144, telephone 703-696-1450.

    Dated: May 10, 2000.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
