Radio Broadcasting Services; Peach Springs, Arizona

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document amends the FM Table of Allotments, of the 
Federal Communications Commission's (Commission) rules, by substituting 
Channel 287A for vacant Channel 280A at Peach Springs, Arizona to 
accommodate the hybrid modification application for Station KIDD(FM), 
Fort Mohave, Arizona to specify operation on Channel 280C2 in lieu of 
Channel 280A. A staff engineering analysis indicates that Channel 287A 
can be allotted to Peach Springs, Arizona, consistent with the minimum 
distance separation requirements of the Commission's rules, with a site 
restriction of 12.6 km (7.8 miles) northeast of the community. The 
reference coordinates are 35-33-18 NL and 113-18-02 WL.

DATES: Effective July 17, 2023.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2054.
