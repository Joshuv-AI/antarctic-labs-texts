SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3418]


State of Illinois (Amendment 2)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated May 23, 2002, the above numbered declaration 
is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as 
beginning April 21, 2002 and continuing through May 23, 2002.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is July 20, 2002 and for 
economic injury the deadline is February 21, 2003.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)
