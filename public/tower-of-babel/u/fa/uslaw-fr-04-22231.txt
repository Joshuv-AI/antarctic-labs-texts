POSTAL SERVICE

39 CFR Part 111


General Information on Postal Service

AGENCY: Postal Service.

ACTION: Final Rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This rule amends the Postal Service regulations to reflect 
current information regarding the publication and availability of the 
Domestic Mail Manual (DMM).

DATES: Effective Date: This final rule is effective on October 4, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sherry L. Freda, (202) 268-7259.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This rule amends 39 CFR part 111 to conform 
to the yearly publication schedule of the Domestic Mail Manual, and to 
reflect the publication and availability of the DMM to all users on the 
Internet at http://pe.usps.gov. The table of contents of the DMM 
previously set forth in Sec.  111.5 is removed as superfluous.
