DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. MG99-19-001]


Pine Needle LNG Company, L.L.C.; Notice of Filing

August 30, 1999.
    Take notice that on August 11, 1999, Pine Needle LNG Company, 
L.L.C. (Pine Needle) filed a standards of conduct report in response to 
the Commission's July 16, 1999 order.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 88 FERC para.61,085 (1999).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest the filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 or 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 or 358.214). All such motions to 
intervene or protest should be filed on or before September 14, 1999. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection. This filing may be 
viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/rims.htm (call 202-
208-2222 for assistance).
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
