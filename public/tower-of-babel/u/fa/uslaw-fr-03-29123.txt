Tingley Rubber Corporation, South Plainfield, NJ; Negative 
Determination Regarding Eligibility To Apply for Worker Adjustment 
Assistance

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 2, 2003, in response to a 
petition filed by the United Steelworkers of America on behalf of 
workers of Tingley Rubber Corporation, South Plainfield, New Jersey. 
The workers produced protective rubber and PVC footwear.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on August 15, 2003, and which remains in effect 
(TA-W-39,814, as amended). Consequently, further investigation in this 
case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.
