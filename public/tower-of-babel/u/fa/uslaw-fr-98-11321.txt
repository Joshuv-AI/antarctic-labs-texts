DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: May 5-6, 1998.
    Time of Meeting: 0800-1700, 5 May 98. 0800-1400, 6 May 98.
    Place: Virginia Modeling Analysis and Simulation Center, 
Suffolk, VA.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Issue Group on ``Human 
Behavior in Combat'' will meet for briefings and discussions on the 
study topic. These meetings will be open to the public. Any 
interested person may attend, appear before, or file statements with 
the committee at the time and in the manner permitted by the 
committee. For further information, please call our office at (703) 
604-7490.
Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
