FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement(s) Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement(s) under the Shipping Act of 1984.
    Interested parties can review or obtain copies of agreements at the 
Washington, DC, offices of the Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, 
N.W., Room 962. Interested parties may submit comments on an agreement 
to the Secretary, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, on 
or before March 13, 1998.
    Agreement No.: 218-011530-001.
    Title: Samson/Sea-Land Cooperative Working Agreement.
    Parties: Samson Tug and Barge Sea-Land Service, Inc.
    Synopsis: The proposed modification changes the Agreement's 
expiration date from April 8, 1998 to January 1, 1999.
    Agreement No.: 202-011604-001.
    Title: USA Conference.
    Parties: Sea-Land Service, Inc., A.P. Moller-Maersk Line, Farrell 
Lines Incorporated.
    Synopsis: The proposed modification deletes the provision under 
Article 7 of the Agreement which requires the parties to post a 
financial guarantee.

    Dated: February 25, 1998.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
