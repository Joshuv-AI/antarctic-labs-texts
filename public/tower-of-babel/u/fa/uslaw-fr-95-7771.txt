DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Native American Business Consultant Applications: Nationwide

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications to operate its Native 
American Business Consultant (NABC) Program. This solicitation was 
originally published in the Federal Register, Thursday, January 12, 
1995, Vol. 60, No. 8, 2947.

11.801  Native American Program

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance)

    Dated: March 24, 1995.
