DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board; Meeting Date Change of Advisory Committee 
Meeting

SUMMARY: The Defense Science Board (DSB) Task Force on Systems 
Technology for the Future U.S. Strategic Posture closed meeting 
scheduled for March 14-15, 2001, has been changed to March 29-30, 2001. 
The meeting will be held at Strategic Analysis Inc., 3601 Wilson 
Boulevard, Suite 600, Arlington, VA.

    Dated: March 1, 2001.
L. M. Bynum,
Alternate OSC Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
