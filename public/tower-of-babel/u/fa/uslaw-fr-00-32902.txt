DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP00-404-000]


Northern Natural Gas Company; Notice of Technical Conference

December 20, 2000.
    Take notice that a technical conference to further discuss the 
various issues raised by northern Natural Gas Company's Order No. 637 
compliance filing will be held on Tuesday, January 23, 2001, and if 
necessary, Wednesday January 24, 2001, at 10:00 am, in a room to be 
designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
