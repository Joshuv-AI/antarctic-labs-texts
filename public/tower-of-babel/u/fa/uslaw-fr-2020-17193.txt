Electronic Document Management System (eLibrary) Enhancements; 
Notice Announcing Release of Modernized eLIBRARY System

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission), hereby gives 
notice announcing upcoming enhancements to its Electronic Document 
Management System (eLibrary), that will be available the week of August 
3. The Commission plans to upgrade its existing system with newer, more 
robust and user-friendly technology. Ultimately, the new system will 
provide users with an improved user interface, more reliable search 
capabilities and greater system stability.
    This version includes, but is not limited to the following 
enhancements:
     A modern look and feel to the eLibrary site
     Improved navigation and consolidated search screens
     Removal of redundant features
     Improved search accuracy and relevance
     On-Demand PDF generation for files in an accession
     Multiple file zip and download from the search results
     Improved reliability
    Please see https://www.ferc.gov/ for additional details on FERC's 
modernized eLibrary system, including additional information on file 
formats, text searchable versus image formats, file names, security, et 
al.
