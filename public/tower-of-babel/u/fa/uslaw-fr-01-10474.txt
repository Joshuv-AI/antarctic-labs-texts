DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP01-249-001]


Trunkline Gas Company; Notice of Compliance Filing

April 23, 2001.
    Take notice that on April 17, 2001, Trunkline Gas Company 
(Trunkline) tendered for filing additional workpapers to support its 
Fuel Reimbursement Filing. Trunkline asserts that the purpose of this 
filing is to comply with the Commission's order issued March 28, 2001 
in Docket No. RP01-249-000, 94 FERC para. 61,348 (2001).
    Trunkline further states that copies of this filing are being 
served on all parties to this proceeding and applicable state 
regulatory agencies.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before April 30, 2001. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection 
in the Public Reference Room. This filing may be viewed on the web at 
http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/rims.htm (call 202-208-2222 for 
assistance). Comments, protests and interventions may be filed 
electronically via the internet in lieu of paper. See, 18 CFR 
385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the Commission's web site 
at http://www.ferc.fed.us/efi/doorbell.htm.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
