[NAFTA--05977]


Progress Lighting, Philadelphia, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
section 250(a), subchapter D, chapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 U.S.C. 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
March 12, 2002, in response to a petition filed on behalf of workers at 
Progress Lighting, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (NAFTA-04208A, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 25th day of July, 2002.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
