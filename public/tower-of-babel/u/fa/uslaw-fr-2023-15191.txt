COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Minnesota Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of virtual business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a virtual business meeting of the Minnesota 
Advisory Committee. The meeting scheduled for Thursday, July 27, 2023, 
at 12:30 p.m. (CT) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register 
of Wednesday, June 21, 2023, in FR Doc. 2023-13124 in the third column 
of page 40199 and first column of page 40200.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Liliana Schiller, Support Services 
Specialist, at [email protected] or (202) 770-1856.

Supervisory Chief, Regional Programs Unit.
