DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Protection and Advocacy System Annual Statement of 
Objectives and Priorities.
    OMB No.: New Request.
    Description: Section 142(a)(2) of the DD Act requires the State 
Protection and Advocacy System (P&As) to develop, by January 1st of 
each year, a Statement of Objectives and Priorities (SOP) and provide 
an opportunity for the public to comment on it. The final statement 
must be submitted (along with the prior year's PPR) to the regional 
office of DHHS. The Statement will provide the public and the 
Department a better understanding of the operation of the advocacy 
services and provide more comprehensive reporting to Congress.
    Respondents: State, Local or Tribal; Not-for-profit Institutions; 
and Individuals or Households.
