Gerald Logging; Eureka, MT; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 27, 2009 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Gerald 
Keller Logging, Eureka, Montana.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of March 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
