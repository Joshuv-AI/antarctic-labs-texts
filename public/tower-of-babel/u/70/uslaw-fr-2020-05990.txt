DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Special Medical Advisory Group, Notice of Meeting--Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act that a meeting of the Special Medical 
Advisory Group (the Committee) that was scheduled for March 20, 2020 
has been postponed. Details on a future meeting will be posted at a 
later date.

    Dated: March 17, 2020.
LaTonya L. Small,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
