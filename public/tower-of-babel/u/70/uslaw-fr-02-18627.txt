DEPARTMENT OF LABOR


Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs; Notice of 
Reinstatement of Chicago Messenger Service

AGENCY: Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Department of 
Labor.

ACTION: Notice of reinstatement, Chicago Messenger Service.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice advises that Chicago Messenger Service has been 
reinstated as an eligible bidder on Federal contracts and subcontracts. 
For further information, contact Charles E. James, Sr., Deputy 
Assistant Secretary for Federal Contract Compliance Programs, U.S. 
Department of Labor, 200 Constitution Avenue, N.W., Room C-3325, 
Washington, D.C. 20210, (202-693-0101).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Chicago Messenger Service, Chicago, 
Illinois, is, as of this date, reinstated as an eligible bidder on 
Federal contracts and subcontracts.

    Signed: July 15, 2002, Washington, D.C.
Charles E. James, Sr.
Deputy Assistant Secretary.
