Radio Broadcasting Services; Knightdale and Wilson, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a petition filed by Capstar TX Limited 
Partnership, licensee of Station WRDU(FM), Channel 291C0, Wilson, North 
Carolina, requesting the reallotment of Channel 291C0 from Wilson to 
Knightdale, as its first local service, and modification of the Station 
WRDU(FM) license to reflect the change. Channel 291C0 can be reallotted 
to Knightdale, using reference coordinates 35-47-50 NL and 78-22-15 WL, 
which requires a site restriction of 10 kilometers (6.2 miles) east of 
the community to avoid short-spacings to the license sites of Station 
WFJA(FM), Channel 288A, Sanford, North Carolina and Station WMNA-FM, 
Channel 292A, Gretna, Virginia.

DATES: Effective July 10, 2006.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
