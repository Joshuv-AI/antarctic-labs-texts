Watlow Controls, Winona, MN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 19, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Watlow Controls, Winona, 
Minnesota.
    The petitioner has withdrawn this petition; thus, further 
investigation would serve no purpose and the investigation is 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 26th day of August, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
