DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the 2011 American Eagle Silver Proof Coin

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the re-pricing of the 
2011 American Eagle Silver Proof Coin. The price of the coins will be 
raised from $59.95 to $68.45.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B.B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street, NW.; 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.

    Authority:  31 U.S.C. 5111, 5112 & 9701.

    Dated: August 23, 2011.
Richard A. Peterson,
Deputy Director, United States Mint .
