DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

34 CFR Part 682

RIN 1840-AB62

Federal Family Education Loan Program

Correction

    In rule document 94-14593 beginning on page 31084 in the issue of 
Thursday, June 16, 1994, make the following correction:
    On page 31085, in the first column, the seventh paragraph should 
read as follows:
Discussion: The Secretary agrees with the commenter that loans made 
under the Federal Direct Student Loan Program would be eligible for the 
forgiveness program, if this program receives funding. If funded, 
Direct Loan regulations would specify Direct Loan borrowers' 
eligibility for loan forgiveness under this program.
