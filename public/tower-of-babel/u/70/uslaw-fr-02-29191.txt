Sound Federal Bancorp, Inc., Mamaroneck, New York; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on November 12, 2002, the Director, 
Supervision, Office of Thrift Supervision (``OTS''), or her designee, 
acting pursuant to delegated authority, approved the application of 
Sound Federal Savings and Loan Association, Mamaroneck, New York, to 
convert to the stock form of organization. Copies of the application 
are available for inspection by appointment (phone number: 202-906-5922 
or e-mail: [email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, OTS, 
1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS Northeast 
Regional Office, 10 Exchange Place, 18th Floor, Jersey City, New Jersey 
07302.

    Dated: November 13, 2002.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Nadine Y. Washington,
Corporate Secretary.
