DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Antitrust Division


Notice Pursuant to the National Cooperative Research and 
Production Act of 1993--United Technologies Research Center (``UTRC''): 
Open Software Tools for Condition Based Maintenance

    Notice is hereby given that, on October 19, 1999, pursuant to 
section 6(a) of the National Cooperative Research and Production Act of 
1993, 15 U.S.C. 4301 et seq. (``the Act''), United Technologies 
Research Center (``UTRC'') has filed written notifications 
simultaneously with the Attorney

[[Page 30613]]

General and the Federal Trade Commission disclosing (1) the identities 
of the parties and (2) the nature and objectives of the venture. The 
notifications were filed for the purpose of invoking the Act's 
provisions limiting the recovery of antitrust plaintiffs to actual 
damages under specified circumstances. Pursuant to section 6(b) of the 
Act, the identities of the parties are United Technologies Corporation 
(``UTC''), Hartford, CT; and i2 Federal, Inc., Irving, TX. The nature 
and objectives of the venture are to engage in cooperative research and 
development in the area of Open Software Tools for Condition Based 
Maintenance pursuant to an Advanced Technology Program with the 
National Institute of Standards and Technology.

Constance K. Robinson,
Director of Operations, Antitrust Division.
