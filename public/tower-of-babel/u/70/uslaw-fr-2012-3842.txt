NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards Meeting of the ACRS 
Subcommittee on Thermal-Hydraulics Phenomena; Revision to February 22, 
2012, ACRS Meeting Federal Register Notice

    The Federal Register Notice for the ACRS Subcommittee meeting on 
Thermal-Hydraulics Phenomena scheduled to be held on February 22, 2012,

Register on February 1, 2012 [77 FR 5063].

contacting Kent Howard, Designated Federal Official (Telephone: 301-
415-2989, Email: [email protected]) between 7:30 a.m. and 5:15 p.m. 
(ET).
