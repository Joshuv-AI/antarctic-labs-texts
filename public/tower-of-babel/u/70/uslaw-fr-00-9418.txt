DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Office of the Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation; 
Notice of Grant Award with Southern Illinois University at Edwardsville

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Office of the Assistant Secretary, for 
Planning and Evaluation (ASPE).
    The Office of the Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation 
(ASPE) announces the award of a grant in the amount of $782,000 for the 
period of April 1, 2000 through March 31, 2001 to Southern Illinois 
University at Edwardsville in support of the SIUE Institute for Urban 
Research. This project was earmarked in the FY 2001 Policy Research 
Appropriation.
    For more information contact Mrs. Adrienne D.B. Little, Grant 
Management Officer, Office of the Assistant Secretary for Planning and 
Evaluation, Room 405F, 200 Independence Avenue, SW, Washington, DC 
20201.

    Dated: April 3, 2000.
Margaret A. Hamburg,
Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation.
