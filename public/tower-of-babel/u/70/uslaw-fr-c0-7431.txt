    In rule document 00-7431 beginning on page 16299 in the issue of 
March 28, 2000, make the following corrections:

``Amendment No. 0 is applicable for casks manufactured before [insert 
effective date of final rule].'' should read ``Amendment No. 0 is 
applicable for casks manufactured before April 27, 2000.''
    2. On the same page, in the same column, in the eighth line, 
``Amendment No. 1 is applicable for casks manufactured after [insert 
effective date of final rule].'' should read ``Amendment No. 1 is 
applicable for casks manufactured after April 27, 2000.''
