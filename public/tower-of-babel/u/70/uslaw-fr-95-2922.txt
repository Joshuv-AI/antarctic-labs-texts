DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. ER95-111-000]


The Electric Exchange; Notice of Filing

February 1, 1995.
    Take notice that on January 27, 1995, The Electric Exchange 
(Applicant), filed an amendment to its application filed October 31, 
1994 in the above-referenced docket requesting Commission acceptance of 
Applicant's Rate Schedule FERC No. 1; the granting of certain blanket 
approvals, including the authority to sell electricity at market-based 
rates; and the waiver of certain Commission regulations. Notice of the 
original application was published in the Federal Register on November 
29, 1994 (59 FR 60969).
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 
20426, in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules 
of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before February 13, 1995. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
