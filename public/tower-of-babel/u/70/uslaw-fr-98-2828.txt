JUDICIAL CONFERENCE OF THE UNITED STATES


Meeting of The Judicial Conference Advisory Committee on Rules of 
Bankruptcy Procedure

AGENCY: Judicial Conference of the United States Advisory Committee on 
Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Advisory Committee on Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure will 
hold a two-day meeting. The meeting will be open to public observation 
but not participation and will be held each day from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 
p.m.

DATES: March 26-27, 1998.

ADDRESSES: Winrock International Conference Center, Petit Jean 
Mountain, 38 Winrock Drive, Morrilton, Arkansas.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
John K. Rabiej, Chief, Rules Committees Support Office, Administrative 
Office of the United States Courts, Washington, DC 20544, telephone 
(202) 273-1820.

    Dated: January 30, 1998.
John K. Rabiej,
Chief, Rules Committees Support Office.
