DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. ER96-1742-000]


Southern California Edison Company; Notice of Filing

May 22, 1996.
    Take notice that on May 7, 1996, Southern California Edison Company 
tendered for filing a Notice of Cancellation of Rate Schedule FERC No. 
338 and all supplements thereto.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888

[[Page 26898]]

First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Rules 
211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 
385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or protests should be 
filed on or before June 4, 1996. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person 
wishing to become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
