SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region 1--Maine District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Maine District Advisory 
Council, located in the geographical area of Augusta, Maine will hold a 
public meeting on Wednesday, March 22, 2006, starting at 10 a.m. The 
meeting will be held at the Care & Comfort, 180 Main Street, 
Waterville, Maine. The purpose of the meeting is to discuss advice and 
opinions regarding the effectiveness of and the need for SBA programs, 
particularly within the local districts which members represent.
    Any member of the public wishing to attend must contact Mary 
McAleney, District Director, Maine District Office, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, 68 Sewall Street, Room 512, Augusta, Maine 04330, phone 
(207) 622-8386; fax (207)-622-8277; [email protected].

Matthew K. Becker,
Committee Management Officer.
