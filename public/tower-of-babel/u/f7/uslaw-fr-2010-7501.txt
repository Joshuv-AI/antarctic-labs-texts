Notice

    In accordance with Section 309(a) of the Clean Air Act, EPA is 
required to

[[Page 16787]]

make its comments on EISs issued by other Federal agencies public. 
Historically, EPA has met this mandate by publishing weekly notices of 
availability of EPA comments, which includes a brief summary of EPA's 
comment letters, in the Federal Register. Since February 2008, EPA has 
been including its comment letters on EISs on its Web site at: http://www.epa.gov/compliance/nepa/eisdata.html. Including the entire EIS 
comment letters on the Web site satisfies the Section 309(a) 
requirement to make EPA's comments on EISs available to the public. 
Accordingly, after March 31, 2010, EPA will discontinue the publication 
of this notice of availability of EPA comments in the Federal Register.
