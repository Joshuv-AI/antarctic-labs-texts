CFR Correction

    In Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 170 to 199, 
revised as of April 1, 1998, page 349, Sec. 178.2010 is corrected in 
the table in paragraph (b), in the entry for 2,2'-Ethylidenebis(4,6-di-
tert-butylphenol) (CAS Reg. No. 35958-30-6) by inserting the following 
between the words ``chapter'' and ``food'' in the first line in entry 
10:


Sec. 178.2010  Antioxidants and/or stabilizers for polymers.
