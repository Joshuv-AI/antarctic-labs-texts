SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2896; Amendment #3]


Puerto Rico; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency, dated November 6, 1996, the above-named Declaration is hereby 
amended to extend the deadline for filing applications for loans for 
physical damage until November 26, 1996.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for loans for economic injury is June 11, 1997.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: November 12, 1996.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
