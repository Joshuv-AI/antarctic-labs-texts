Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2022-0118.
    Petitioner: Air Transport Services, Inc.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  61.113(c).
    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner is requesting relief 
from Sec.  61.113(c) to be allowed reimbursement of fuel costs for 
volunteer pilots conducting charitable flights that provide disaster 
relief and humanitarian aid to individuals affected by natural 
disasters, pandemics, and medical emergencies primarily in the 
Caribbean Islands. Additionally, the petitioner requests to provide 
evacuation services from disaster areas, as well as, the transportation 
of doctors and medical equipment into disaster areas to provide on-site 
emergency treatment.
