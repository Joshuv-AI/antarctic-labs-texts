Cable Television Consumer Protection and Competition Act of 1992; 
Direct Broadcast Satellite Public Interest Obligations

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, denied.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies all Petitions for Reconsideration filed 
in this proceeding. This document has been superceded by a Sua Sponte 
Order on Reconsideration, FCC 04-44, adopted March 3, 2004 and released 
March 25, 2004. The new Order reflects changes in rules regarding 
children's advertising limits and clarification of rules regarding 
political broadcasting.
