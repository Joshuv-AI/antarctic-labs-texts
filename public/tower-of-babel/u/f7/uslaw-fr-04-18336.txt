DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-55,287]


Marconi, Toccoa, GA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on July 21, 2004, in response to a petition 
filed by the company on behalf of workers at Marconi, Toccoa, Georgia.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 29th day of July, 2004.
