ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-6331-3]


National Advisory Council for Environmental Policy and 
Technology: Full Council Meeting

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Cancellation of notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a cancellation notice for the April 28-29, 1999 
meeting of the National Advisory Council for Environmental Policy and 
Technology (NACEPT). NACEPT provides advice and recommendations to the 
Administrator of EPA on a broad range of environmental policy issues. 
The meeting was being held to formally present reports and 
recommendations to EPA and to discuss future activities and projects of 
NACEPT.

DATES: The public meeting was to be held on Wednesday, April 28, 1999 
from 1:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., and Thursday, April 29, 1999 from 8:30 
a.m. to 3:00 p.m. On both days, the meeting was to be held at the 
Ramada Plaza Hotel, 901 Fairfax Street, Alexandria, Virginia. This 
meeting was open to the public.

ADDRESSES: Written comments should be sent to Joseph A. Sierra, 
Designated Federal Officer, NACEPT, U.S. EPA, Office of Cooperative 
Environmental Management (1601-F), 401 M Street, SW, Washington, DC 
20460.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Joseph A. Sierra at the address shown above and 202-260-9741; Fax 202-
260-6882.

    Dated: April 20, 1999.
Gordon Schisler,
Deputy Director, Office of Cooperative, Environmental Management.
