DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Cancellation of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Heart, 
Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel Meeting on Ensuring 
Adequate Utilization of Epidemiologic Data, May 7, 1998, which was 
published in the Federal Register on March 4, 1998 (63 FR 10643).
    The meeting was canceled due to a shortage of funds in the current 
fiscal year.

    Dated: April 2, 1998.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
