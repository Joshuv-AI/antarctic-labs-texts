DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Minerals Management Service


Notice on Outer Continental Shelf (OCS) Oil and Gas Lease Sales

AGENCY: Minerals Management Service, Interior.

ACTION: List of restricted joint bidders.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the authority vested in the Director of the MMS by 
the joint bidding provisions of 30 CFR 256.41, each entity within one 
of the following groups shall be restricted from bidding with any 
entity in any other of the following groups at OCS oil and gas lease 
sales to be held during the bidding period May 1, 2005, through October 
31, 2005. The List of Restricted Joint Bidders published October 18, 
2004, in the Federal Register at 69 FR 61402 covered the period 
November 1, 2004, through April 31, 2005.
