DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On November 14,1997, a notice was published in the Federal 
Register, Vol. 62, No. 220, Page 61139, that an application had been 
filed with the Fish and Wildlife Service by Charles L. Chappell for a 
permit (PRT-836256) to import a sport-hunted polar bear (Ursus 
maritimus) trophy, taken from the Lancaster Sound population, Northwest 
Territories, Canada, prior to April 30, 1994 for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on January 9,1998, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
