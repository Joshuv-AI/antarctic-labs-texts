Mississippi River Transmission Corporation; Notice Rescheduling 
Technical Conference

November 4, 1998.
    Take notice that the Commission staff will convene a technical 
conference as provided by the Commission order in this proceeding 
issued October 14, 1998.\1\ The technical conference, previously 
scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, 1998, at 10:00 a.m., has been 
rescheduled. The technical conference will be held on Thursday, 
November 12, 1998, in a room to be designated at the offices of the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Mississippi River Transmission Corporation, 85 FERC 
para.61,049 (1998).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Attendance will be limited to parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Jerie O'Connor at (202) 208-0459, or Harris 
Wood at (202) 208-0224.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
