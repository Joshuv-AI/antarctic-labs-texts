    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on June 10, 2009, members of its staff will participate in 
the Wind Integration Workshop conducted by the Midwest Independent 
Transmission System Operator, Inc. (Midwest ISO). The agenda and other 
documents for the meeting are available on the Midwest ISO's Web site, 
http://www.midwestiso.org.
    The meeting is open to all market participants, and Commission 
staff's attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach 
efforts. The meeting may discuss matters at issue in the above 
captioned dockets.
