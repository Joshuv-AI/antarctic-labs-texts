LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Request for Letters of Intent To Apply for 2016 Technology 
Initiative Grant Funding; Correction

AGENCY: Legal Services Corporation.

ACTION: Correction Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On January 20, 2016, the Legal Services Corporation (LSC) 
published a notice in the Federal Register (81 FR 3162) titled 
``Request for Letters of Intent To Apply for 2015 Technology Initiative 
Grant Funding.'' The title to this notice contained a typographical 
error. This document corrects the notice by revising the title.

DATES: This correction is effective January 20, 2016

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mark Freedman, Senior Associate 
General Counsel, Legal Services Corporation, 3333 K Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20007; (202) 295-1500; [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The title should read ``Request for Letters 
of Intent to Apply for 2016 Technology Initiative Grant Funding.''

    Dated: January 27, 2016.
Katherine Ward,
Executive Assistant to the Vice President for Legal Affairs and General 
Counsel.
