Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-43-2019]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 176--Rockford, Illinois; Authorization 
of Production Activity; Staal & Plast USA, Inc. (Irrigation Trays); 
Sycamore, Illinois

    On June 24, 2019, Staal & Plast USA, Inc., submitted a notification 
of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility 
within FTZ 176, in Sycamore, Illinois.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (84 FR 31833, July 3, 2019). On 
October 22, 2019, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at this 
time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including Section 400.14.

    Dated: October 22, 2019.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
