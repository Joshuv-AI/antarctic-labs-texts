Cancellation of DoD Advisory Group on Electron Devices

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the cancellation of the DoD Advisory 
Group on Electron Devices meeting originally scheduled for June 15, 
1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Elise Rabin, AGED Secretariat, 1745 
Jefferson Davis Highway, Crystal Square Four, Suite 500, Arlington, 
Virginia 22202.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On April 26, 1999 (64 FR 20279), the 
Department of Defense published the notice announcing the meeting of 
the DoD Advisory Group on Electron Device.
