ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 3-0 on July 6, the Commission 
determined pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and 9.107(a) of the Commission's 
rules that ``Affirmation of (1) Pacific Gas & Electric Co. (Diablo 
Canyon Power Plant, Units 1 and 2); Applicant's motion to terminate 
proceeding and Intervenor's motion to vacate, (2) Duke Energy Corp. 
(Catawba Nuclear Station, Units 1 and 2), Order on the Board's 
certified questions on admissibility of a security contention, and (3) 
Appointment of a Pre-License Application Presiding Officer (PAPO) for 
High Level Waste Repository Pre-License Application Phase'' be held 
July 7, and on less than one week's notice to the public.
