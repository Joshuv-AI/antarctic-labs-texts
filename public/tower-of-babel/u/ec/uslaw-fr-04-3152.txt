SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Foreign governments sometimes require U.S. importers of strategic 
commodities to furnish their supplier with a U.S. Delivery Verification 
Certificate validating that the commodities shipped to the U.S. were in 
fact received. This procedure increases the effectiveness of controls 
over exports of strategic commodities.

II. Method of Collection

    Submitted, as required, on form BIS-647P.
