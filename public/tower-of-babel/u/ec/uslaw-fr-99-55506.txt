Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs

CFR Correction

    In Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 500 to 599, 
revised as of April 1, 1998, on page 176, second column, Sec. 520.2158b 
is corrected by adding paragraph (d) to read as follows:


Sec. 520.2158b  Dihydrostreptomycin tablets.

 * * * * *
    (d) Conditions of use. Calves--(1) Amount. 150 milligrams of 
dihydrostreptomycin and 1.5 grams of chlorhexidine dihydrochloride per 
100 pounds of body weight per day.
    (2) Indications for use. Treatment of bacterial scours in calves.
    (3) Limitations. Administer orally once a day for 5 days; withdraw 
3 days before slaughter.
