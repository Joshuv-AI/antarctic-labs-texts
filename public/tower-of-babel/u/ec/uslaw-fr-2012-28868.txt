Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 308-005--Oregon]


PacifiCorp Energy; Notice of Corrected Process Plan and Schedule

    On August 4, 2011, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
(Commission) issued the Scoping Document 2 for relicensing of 
PacifiCorp Energy's 1.1-megawatt Wallowa Falls Hydroelectric Project 
(FERC No. 308). Appendix B of the Scoping Document 2 provides a process 
plan and schedule for the Integrated Licensing Process. The process 
plan and schedule incorrectly identifies January 13, 2013, as the due 
date for filing the initial study report, and January 13, 2014, as the 
due date for filing the updated study report.
    The correct due date is January 3, 2013, for the initial study 
report, and January 3, 2014, for the updated study report. There are no 
other modifications to the previously issued schedule.

    Dated: November 23, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
