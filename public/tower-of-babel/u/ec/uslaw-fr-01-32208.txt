STMicroelectronics, Inc. (ST) San Diego, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 3, 2001, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at STMicroelectronics, Inc., San 
Diego, California.
    The company official submitting the petition has requested that the 
petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case 
would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 21st day of December, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
