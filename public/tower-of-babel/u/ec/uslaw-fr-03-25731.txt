Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-52,706]


Compaq Computer Corporation, Denver, CO; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 29, 2003 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by the State TAA Coordinator on behalf of workers at Compaq 
Computer Corporation, Denver, Colorado.

[[Page 58711]]

    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 8th day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
