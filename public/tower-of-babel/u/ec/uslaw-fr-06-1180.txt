Office of the National Coordinator; American Health Information 
Community Biosurveillance Workgroup Meeting

ACTION: Announcement of meeting.

SUMMARY: This notice announces the second meeting of the American 
Health Information Community Biosurveillance Workgroup in accordance 
with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., 
App.)

DATES: February 24, 2006 from 1 p.m. to 5 p.m.

ADDRESSES: Cohen Building (330 Independence Ave., SW., Washington, DC 
20201), Conference Room 5051.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: http://www.hhs.gov/healthit.
