UNITED STATES INFORMATION AGENCY

NIS Secondary School Initiative Inbound Academic Year Placement

AGENCY: United States Information Agency.

ACTION: Amendment--Request for proposals.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is an amendment to the Request for Proposals (RFP) 
published September 7, 1995, beginning on page 46683 and ending on page 
46685, concerning placement of students from the Newly Independent 
States (NIS) of the former Soviet Union who will be in the United 
States under the 1996/97 Academic Year Program of the NIS Secondary 
School Initiative (Announcement Number E/P-96-12). This amendment 
changes the day listed with the deadline date to Thursday, October 19. 
The RFP incorrectly says Friday, October 19.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:

619-6299.
