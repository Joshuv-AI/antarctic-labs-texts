47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 93-295; RM-8362]


Radio Broadcasting Services; San Clemente, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document deletes FM Channel 285A at San Clemente, 
California, in response to a Commission directive, based upon the 
unavailability of a transmitter site on non-military property for use 
by a fully spaced station at that community. See 58 FR 65155, December 
13, 1993. With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 18, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
