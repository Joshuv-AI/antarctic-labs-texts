FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 04-264, MB Docket No. 03-193, RM-10768]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Hobbs, NM

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Eastern New Mexico 
University, allots DTV channel *47 to Hobbs, New Mexico, as the 
community's first noncommercial television allotment. See 68 FR 54408,

[[Page 8333]]

September 17, 2003. DTV channel *47 can be allotted to Hobbs, New 
Mexico, in compliance with sections 73.623(d) and 73.625(a) of the 
Commission's Rules at coordinates 32-45-20 N. and 103-11-09 W. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 22, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
