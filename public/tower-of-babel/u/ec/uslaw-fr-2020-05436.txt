DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the NHLBI 
Mentored Clinical and Basic Science Review Committee, March 19, 2020, 
10:30 a.m. to March 20, 2020, 03:00 p.m., Holiday Inn National Airport 
Hotel, 2650 Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, VA 22202 which was 
published in the Federal Register on January 29, 2020, 85 FR 5219.
    The NHLBI IRG meeting is being amended due to a change in the 
meeting format. This two-day meeting to be held on March 19-20, 2020 
will be a teleconference meeting. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 11, 2020.
Ronald J. Livingston, Jr.,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
