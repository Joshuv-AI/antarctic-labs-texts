Petition for Exemption



    Docket No.: FAA-2011-0580.

    Petitioner: Aviation Partners, Inc..

    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 25.671(c)(2).

    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner requests relief from 

the requirement that an airplane be capable of continued safe flight 

and landing, following a dual hydraulic-system failure or any single 

failure in combination with a probable hydraulic failure. The relief 

sought would apply to the petitioner's Dassault Falcon 900 airplanes 

equipped with Aviation Partners, Inc. blended winglets.
