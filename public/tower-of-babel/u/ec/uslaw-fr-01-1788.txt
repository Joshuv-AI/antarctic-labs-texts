SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3316]


State of Oklahoma; Amendment #1

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated January 10, 2001, the above-numbered 
Declaration is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this 
disaster as beginning on December 25, 2000 and continuing through 
January 10, 2001.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is March 6, 2001 and for 
economic injury the deadline is October 5, 2001.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: January 11, 2001.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
