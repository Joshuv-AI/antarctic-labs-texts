Kaiser Federal Bank, Covina, California, and Kaiser Federal 
Financial Group, Inc., Covina, CA; Approval of Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on November 9, 2007, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of Kaiser Federal Bank, 
Covina, California, to convert to the stock form of organization. 
Copies of the application are available for inspection by appointment 
(phone number: 202-906-5922 or e-mail: [email protected]) at 
the Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and 
the OTS West Regional Office, Pacific Plaza, 2001 Junipero Serra 
Boulevard, Suite 650, Daly City, California 94014-1976.

    Dated: November 9, 2007.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
