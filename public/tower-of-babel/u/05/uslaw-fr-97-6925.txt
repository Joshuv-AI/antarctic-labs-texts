Indiana; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
State of Indiana (FEMA-1165-DR), dated March 6, 1997, and related 
determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 8, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Magda Ruiz, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of Indiana, is hereby amended to include Hazard Mitigation and 
Categories A and B under the Public Assistance program in those areas 
determined to have been adversely affected by the catastrophe declared 
a major disaster by the President in his declaration of March 6, 1997:

The counties of Clark, Crawford, Dearborn, Floyd, Harrison, 
Jefferson, Ohio, Perry, Posey, Spencer, Switzerland, Vanderbaugh, 
and Warrick for Hazard Mitigation and Categories A and B under the 
Public Assistance program (already designated for Individual 
Assistance).

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance.)
Lacy E. Suiter,
Executive Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
