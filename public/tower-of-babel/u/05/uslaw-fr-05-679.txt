DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

    Title: Head Start Program Information Report.
    OMB No.: 0980-0017.
    Description: Section 650 of the Head Start Act requires the 
Secretary of the Department of Health and Human Services to prepare and 
submit a report to the Congress at least once every two years. This 
report shall include information contained in the document entitled the 
``Head Start Program Information Report.'' The Head Start Bureau is 
proposing to renew approval of this information collection document.
    Respondents: Head Start and Early Head Start grantees and delegate 
agencies.
