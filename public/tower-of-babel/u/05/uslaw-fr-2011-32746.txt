Recordkeeping and Reporting Requirements Under Title VII, the ADA 
and GINA

CFR Correction

    In Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 900 to 1899, 
revised as of July 1, 2011, in Part 1602, remove the words ``section 
709(c) of title VII or section 107 of the ADA'' and add in their place 
the words ``section 709(c) of title VII, section 107 of the ADA, or 
section 207(a) of GINA'' wherever they appear in the following 
sections:
