Regulations for the Relicensing of Hydroelectric Projects

January 4, 1996.
AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, DOE.

ACTION: Petition for rulemaking; extension of time.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 31, 1995, the Commission issued a notice of 
National Hydropower Association's (NHA) petition for rulemaking in the 
above-captioned docket (60 FR 56278, November 8, 1995). Certain 
departments and agencies of the Federal Government are presently closed 
for all but emergency matters due to a lack of appropriated funds. The 
Commission anticipates that a number of these agencies may wish to file 
comments on the petition. In order to accommodate this unusual 
circumstance, notice is hereby given that an extension of time is 
granted.

DATES: Initial comments by all parties shall be filed on or before 
February 5, 1996. Reply comments shall be due on or before March 4, 
1996.

ADDRESSES: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.
