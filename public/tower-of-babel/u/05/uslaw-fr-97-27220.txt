BARRY GOLDWATER SCHOLARSHIP AND EXCELLENCE IN EDUCATION FOUNDATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE: 2:00 pm, Wednesday, October 29, 1997.

PLACE: SDG-59, Dirksen Senate Office Building, Washington, DC 20510.

STATUS: The meeting will be open to the public.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

    1. Review and approval of the minutes of the March 12, 1997 Board 
of Trustees meeting meeting.
    2. Report on end of fiscal year financial status of the Foundation 
endowment fund.
    A. Review of investment policy and current portfolio.
    3. Discussion of the proposed 1998/99 scholarship competition.
    4. Other Business brought before the Board of Trustees.
