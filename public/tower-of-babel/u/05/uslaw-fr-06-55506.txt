Importation of Tobacco Products and Cigarette Papers and Tubes

CFR Correction

revised as of April 1, 2005, on page 894, in Sec.  41.86, paragraph 
(d), in the last sentence remove ``ATF'' and add in its place ``TTB,'' 
and on page 902, in Sec.  41.126, last sentence, remove ``regional 
director (compliance)'' and add in its place ``appropriate TTB 
officer.''
