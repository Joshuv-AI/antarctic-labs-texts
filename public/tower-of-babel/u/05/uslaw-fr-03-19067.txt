DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Notice of Meeting of the Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and 
Availability

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and Availability will meet 
on Thursday August 21, 2003, and Friday, August 22, 2003, from 8 a.m. 
to 5 p.m. The meeting will take place at the Hyatt Regency Hotel on 
Capitol Hill, 400 New Jersey Ave., NW., Washington, DC 20001. The 
meeting will be entirely open to the public.
    The purpose of this meeting will be to examine the effect of mass 
smallpox vaccinations on the blood donor base and the effects of 
emerging infectious diseases and bioterrorism on the blood supply.
    Public comment will be solicited at the meeting. Public comment 
will be limited to five minutes per speaker. Those who wish to have 
printed material distributed to Advisory Committee members should 
submit thirty (30) copies to the Acting Executive Secretary prior to 
close of business August 15, 2003. Those who wish to utilize electronic 
data projection in their presentation to the Committee must submit 
their material to the Acting Executive Secretary prior to close of 
business August 15, 2003. In addition, anyone planning to comment is 
encouraged to contact the Acting Executive Secretary at her/his 
earliest convenience.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: CAPT Lawrence C. McMurtry, Acting 
Executive Secretary, Advisory Committee on Blood Safety and 
Availability, Department of Health and Human Services, Office of Public 
Health and Science, 1101 Wootton Parkway, Room 275, Rockville, MD 
20852; (301) 443-4788, FAX (301) 443-4361, e-mail 
[email protected].
