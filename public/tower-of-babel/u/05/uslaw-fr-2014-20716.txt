DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council Meeting, September 9, 2014, 
8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 
Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892, which was published in the Federal 
Register on July 18, 2014, 79 FR 42024.
    This notice is being amended to notify the public of a change in 
start time of the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council 
Meeting from 12:30 p.m. to 1:00 p.m. on September 9, 2014. This notice 
is also being amended to indicate a possible change to the start time 
(8:00 a.m.) of the September 10, 2014 meeting. The meeting start time 
will be determined at the end of the meeting day on September 9, 2014 
and will be announced at the meeting and published on the Council Web 
site (http://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/about/committees/nhlbac/#agenda).
    The meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: August 26, 2014.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
