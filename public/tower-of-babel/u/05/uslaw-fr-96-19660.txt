FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Sunshine Act Meeting; Additional Item To Be Considered at Open 
Meeting, Thursday, August 1st

    The Federal Communications Commission will consider an additional 
item on the subject listed below at the Open Meeting scheduled for 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, August 1, 1996, at 1919 M Street, NW., Washington, DC.

Item No., Bureau, Subject

    2--Common Carrier--Title: Implementation of the Local 
Competition Provisions in the Telecommunications Act of 1996 (CC 
Docket No. 96-98); Interconnection between Local Exchange Carriers 
and Commercial Mobile Radio Service Providers (CC Docket No. 95-
185). Summary: The Commission will consider actions to implement 
Sections 251 and 252 enacted in the Telecommunications Act of 1996; 
actions regarding interconnection between local exchange carriers 
and commercial mobile radio service providers.

    The prompt and orderly conduct of Commission business requires that 
less than 7-days notice be given consideration of this additional item.
    Action by the Commission July 29, 1996. Commissioners Hundt, 
Chairman; Quello, Ness and Chong voting to consider this item.
    Additional information concerning this meeting may be obtained from 
Audrey Spivack or David Fiske, Office of Public Affairs, telephone 
number (202) 418-0500.
    Copies of materials adopted at this meeting can be purchased from 
the FCC's duplicating contractor, International Transcription Services, 
Inc. at (202) 857-3800. Audio and Video Tapes of this meeting can be 
purchased from Telspan International at (301) 731-5355. This meeting 
can be viewed over George Mason University's ``Capitol Connection.'' 
For Information on this service call (703) 993-3100.
