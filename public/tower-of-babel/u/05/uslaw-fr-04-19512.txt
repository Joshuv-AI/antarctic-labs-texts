DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Willamette Province Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Willamette Province Advisory Committee (PAC) will meet in 
McKenzie Bridge, Oregon. The purpose of the meeting is to review 
projects planned and implemented under the Northwest Forest Plan (NFP) 
in the Central Cascades Adaptive Management Area. The specific topics 
to be covered at the meeting include research and NFP projects in the 
Central Cascades Adaptive Management Area.

DATES: The meeting will be held September 16, 2004.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the HJ Andrews Research Center 
on the Willamette National Forest. Send written comments to Neal 
Forrester, Willamette Province Advisory Committee, c/o Willamette 
National Forest, PO Box 10607, Eugene, Oregon 97440, (541) 225-6436 or 
electronically to [email protected].

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Neal Forrester, Willamette National 
Forest, (541) 225-6436.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting is open to the public. The 
Committee will meet at the Eugene District of the Bureau of Land 
Management at 8 a.m. on September 16 and travel to the HJ Andrews 
Research Center. The field trip is open to the public, but they must 
provide their own transportation. A public forum will be provided and 
individuals will have the opportunity to address the PAC at about 10 
a.m. at the Research Center. Oral comments will be limited to three 
minutes. However, persons who wish to bring matters to the attention of 
the Committee may file written statements with the PAC staff before or 
after the meeting.

    Dated: August 19, 2004.
Dallas J. Emch
Forest Supervisor, Willamette National Forest.
