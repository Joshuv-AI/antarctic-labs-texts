Radio Broadcasting Services; Pleasanton, Bandera, Hondo, 
Hollywood Park, and Dilley, Texas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission dismisses as moot the Application for Review 
filed by Reding Broadcasting Company, requesting reconsideration of a 
dismissal of its proposal to substitute Channel 252A for Channel 253C2 
at Pleasanton, TX, substitute Channel 253A for Channel 290A at Hondo, 
TX and Channel 252A for Channel 276A at Bandera, TX. Petitioner 
received requested relief in MM Docket No. 98-55.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria M. McCauley, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.
