DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 7104]


Notice of Proposal To Extend the Agreement Between the Government 
of the United States of America and the Government of the Republic of 
Colombia Concerning the Imposition of Import Restrictions on 
Archaeological Material From the Pre-Columbian Cultures and Certain 
Ecclesiastical Material from the Colonial Period of Colombia

    The Government of the Republic of Colombia has informed the 
Government of the United States of its interest in an extension of the 
Agreement Between the Government of the United States of America and 
the Government of the Republic of Colombia Concerning the Imposition of 
Import Restrictions on Archaeological Material from the Pre-Columbian 
Cultures and Certain Ecclesiastical Material from the Colonial Period 
of Colombia.
    Pursuant to the authority vested in the Assistant Secretary for 
Educational and Cultural Affairs, and pursuant to the requirement under 
19 U.S.C. 2602(f)(1), an extension of this Agreement is hereby 
proposed.
    Pursuant to 19 U.S.C. 2602(f)(2), the views and recommendations of 
the Cultural Property Advisory Committee regarding this proposal will 
be requested.
    A copy of the Agreement, the Designated List of restricted 
categories of material, and related information can be found at the 
following Web site: http://exchanges.state.gov/heritage/.

    Dated: August 1, 2010.
Ann Stock,
Assistant Secretary, Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs, 
Department of State.
