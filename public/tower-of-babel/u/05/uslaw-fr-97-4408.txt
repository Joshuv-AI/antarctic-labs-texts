DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP97-251-000]


Viking Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Penalty Revenue 
Crediting Report

February 18, 1997.
    Take notice that on February 12, 1997, Viking Gas Transmission 
Company (Viking) filed a report of penalty revenues and credit for the 
period of November 1, 1995 through October 31, 1996.
    Viking states that copies of the filing have been mailed to all of 
its jurisdictional customers and to affected state regulatory 
commissions.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure. All such motions or protests must be filed on 
or before February 25, 1997. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make the protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person 
wishing to become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
