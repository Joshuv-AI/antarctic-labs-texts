Radio Broadcasting Services; Roundup, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of William J. Edwards, 
allots Channel 248A at Roundup, Montana, as the community's first local 
FM service. Channel 248A can be allotted to Roundup, Montana, in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements with a site restriction of 1.08 km (0.6 miles) northeast 
of Roundup. The coordinates for Channel 248A at Roundup, Montana, are 
46-26-58 North Latitude and 108-31-44 West Longitude. The Canadian 
government has concurred in this allotment. A filing window for Channel 
248A at Roundup, MT, will not be opened at this time. Instead, the 
issue of opening this allotment for auction will be addressed by the 
Commission in a subsequent Order.

DATES: Effective April 18, 2003.
