NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Social, Behavioral and Economic Sciences; 
Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Advisory Committee for Social, Behavioral, and Economic 
Sciences (1171).
    Date/Time: June 7, 2007; 1 p.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Room 920, 
Virtual meeting, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Mr. Tyrone Jordan, Office of the Assistant 
Director, Directorate for Social, Behavioral, and Economic Sciences, 
National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Room 905, 
Arlington, VA 22230, 703-292-8741.
    Summary Minutes: May be obtained from contact person listed 
above.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations to the 
National Science Foundation on major goals and policies pertaining 
to Social, Behavioral and Economic Sciences Directorate programs and 
activities.
    Agenda: Topics:
     Science of Science and Innovation Policy.
     Human and Social Dynamics Priority Area.
     International Polar Year.
     Division of Social & Economic Sciences: Committee of 
Visitors (COV) Report.
     Initiatives for the FY 2009 OMB Budget.
     NSTC Subcommittee on the SBE Sciences--Prospectus of 
Grand Challenges.
     Briefing on IPAMM (Working Group on Impact of Proposal 
Award and Management Mechanisms).
     Agenda Items for fall 2007 Meeting.

    Dated: May 8, 2007.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
