South Atlantic Fishery Management Council; Public Hearings

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notification of public hearings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The South Atlantic Fishery Management Council (Council) will 
convene a series of public hearings regarding Amendment 18 to the 
Fishery Management Plan for Coastal Migratory Pelagic Resources of the 
Gulf of Mexico and South Atlantic. Amendment 18 will modify the total 
allowable catch (TAC) for the Atlantic migratory group king mackerel 
and Spanish mackerel fisheries, and change the commercial trip limits 
for Spanish mackerel to reflect recent changes in the fishing year.

DATES: The public hearings will be held in March 2007. See 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION for the specific dates and times of the 
public hearings.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Council initiated a regulatory amendment 
in June 2006 to adjust the TAC for both Atlantic migratory group king 
and Spanish mackerel following an assessment and report reflecting the 
need to reduce the current TACs. The Council is proposing to reduce the 
current annual TAC for king mackerel from 10.0 million lb (4.5 million 
kg) to 7.1 million lb (3.2 million kg), and for Spanish mackerel from 
7.04 million lb (3.19 million kg) to 6.7 million lb (3.0 million kg). 
Amendment 18 was changed from a regulatory amendment to a plan 
amendment in September 2006 to allow more flexibility for alternatives. 
While the title has changed, the alternatives and information contained 
in the plan amendment remain the same.
