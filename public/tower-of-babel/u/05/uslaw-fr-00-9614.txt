Radio Broadcasting Services; Coon Valley and Westby, WI and 
Lanesboro, MN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Petition for reconsideration, denial.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for reconsideration of a 
Report and Order Order, 63 FR 30145 (June 3, 1998), that allotted 
Channel 280A to both Westby, Wisconsin, and Lanesboro, Minnesota. These 
allotments provide first local aural transmission services to two 
communities that are more populous than Coon Valley, Wisconsin.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
