ACTION: Notice of typographical corrections.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a notice of typographical correction 
with respect to a notice of Grant of Individual Exemptions published on 
July 31, 1997, at 62 FR 41092 (the prior notice).

Correction: The prior notice identified Exemption Application No. D-
10398, Robert A. Benz & Co., P.A., as Prohibited Transaction Exemption 
97-39. The correct Prohibited Transaction number is 97-38.
