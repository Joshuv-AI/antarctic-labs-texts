Adelphia Gateway, LLC; Notice of Reopening of Comment Period

    On January 4, 2019, the Commission issued a notice setting February 
3, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments on the 
Adelphia Gateway Project environmental assessment. Due to the funding 
lapse at certain federal agencies between December 22, 2018 and January 
25, 2019, the Commission is reopening the comment period until March 1, 
2019.

    Dated: February 8, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
