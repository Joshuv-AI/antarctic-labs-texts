DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 6474-003]


KC Pittsfield LLC, Daley Energy, LLC; Notice of Transfer of 
Exemption

    1. By letter filed February 15, 2019, KC Pittsfield LLC informed 
the Commission that the exemption from licensing for the 100-MW Eastman 
Brook Project No. 6474, originally issued September 7, 1982 \1\ has 
been transferred from KC Pittsfield LLC to Daley Energy, LLC. The 
project is located on Eastman Brook in Grafton County, New Hampshire. 
The transfer of an exemption does not require Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Notice of Exemption From Licensing. Eastman Brook Hydro, 20 
FERC ] 62,426 (1982).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Daley Energy, LLC is now the exemptee of the Eastman Brook 
Project No. 6474. All correspondence should be forwarded to: Mr. Travis 
Daley, Daley Energy, LLC, 210 Route 10, Piermont, NH 03779, Phone: 802-
291-0839, Email: [email protected].
