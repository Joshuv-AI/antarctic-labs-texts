Petition For Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2022-0767.
    Petitioner: Flexjet, LLC.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 135.152(j).
    Description of Relief Sought: Flexjet, LLC petitions for an 
exemption from 14 CFR 135.152(j) that would allow the Gulfstream 450 to 
meet the flight data recording requirements of part 91, Appendix E and 
Sec.  91.609(c)(1), instead of the 88 required parameters detailed in 
Sec.  135.152(h). As these aircraft do not have an 88-parameter FDR 
installed, such relief is necessary until such time that Gulfstream 
makes the components available.
