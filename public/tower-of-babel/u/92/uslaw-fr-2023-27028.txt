Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE:  2:00 p.m., Monday, December 18, 2023.

PLACE:  1255 Union Street NE, Suite 500, Washington, DC 20002.

STATUS:  Parts of this meeting will be open to the public. The rest of
