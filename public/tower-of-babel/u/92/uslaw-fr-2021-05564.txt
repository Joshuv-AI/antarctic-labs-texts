CIVIL RIGHTS COMMISSION


Notice of Public Meeting of the Maine Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Maine Advisory Committee. 
The meeting scheduled for Thursday, March 18, 2021 at 12 p.m. (ET) is 
cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of Friday, February 
26, 2021, in FR Doc. 2021-03973, on page 11720.
