J.H.L. Fashion Inc., New York, NY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on December 20, 2007 in response to a 
petition filed by workers of J.H.L. Fashion Inc., New York, New York.
    The petition does not contain three valid worker signatures; 
therefore, the petition itself is invalid. Consequently, the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 31st day of December 2007.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
