Notice of Coal Action, New Mexico

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of availability, Record of Decision (ROD) for the 
Thermal Energy Preference Right Lease Applications (PRLA's) San Juan 
County, New Mexico.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The PRLA process requires that ROD be made available to the 
public. The ROD is the document announcing the BLM's decision regarding 
PRLA commercial quantities determinations. This action establishes the 
availability of the ROD for Thermal Energy's PRLA's.

ADDRESSES: Copies of the ROD can be obtained at the New Mexico State 
Office, Bureau of Land Management, P.O. Box 27115, Santa Fe, New Mexico 
87502-0115.

    Dated: July 25, 1997.
Richard A. Whitley,
Acting State Director.
