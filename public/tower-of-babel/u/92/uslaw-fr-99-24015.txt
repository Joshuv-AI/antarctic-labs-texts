FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

September 8, 1999.
FCC TO HOLD OPEN COMMISSION MEETING: Wednesday, September 15, 1999.

the subjects listed below on Wednesday, September 15, 1999, which is 
scheduled to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, 
S. W., Washington, D.C.
