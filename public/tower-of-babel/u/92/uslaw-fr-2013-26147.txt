DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Community 
Influences on Health Behavior Study Section, October 21, 2013, 08:00 
a.m. to October 22, 2013, 06:00 p.m., The St. Regis Washington DC, 923 
16th Street NW., Washington, DC 20006 which was published in the 
Federal Register on September 26, 2013, 78 FR 59361.
    The meeting will be held at the Embassy Suites DC Convention Center 
Hotel, 900 10th Street NW., Washington, DC 20001. The meeting will 
start on November 18, 2013 at 08:00 a.m. and end on November 19, 2013 
at 05:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to the public.
