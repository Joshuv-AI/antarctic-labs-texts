DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee will meet on 
September 11 and 12, 1997 at the Park Community Center, Crater Lake 
National Park, Steel Circle, Crater Lake, Oregon. On September 11, the 
meeting will begin at 10:00 a.m. and adjourn at 5:00 p.m. The meeting 
on September 12 will resume at 8:00 a.m. and adjourn at 3:00 p.m. 
Agenda items to be covered include: (1) Klamath PAC salvage 
subcommittee recommendation update; (2) Northwest Forest Plan 
Implementation; (3) Subcommittee Reports; and (4) public comment 
periods. All PAC meetings are open to the public. Interested citizens 
are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
