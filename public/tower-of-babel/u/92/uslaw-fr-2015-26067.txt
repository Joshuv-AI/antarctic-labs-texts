Proposed Data Collection Submitted for Public Comment and 
Recommendations

AGENCY: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), Department of 
Health and Human Services (HHS).

ACTION: Cancellation of notice with comment period

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The notice ``Proposed Data Collection Submitted for Public 
Comment and Recommendations'' on Personal Protective Equipment 
Information (PPE-Info) Database (80 FR 60906, October 8, 2015) is 
cancelled. This noticed invited comment on the Personal Protective 
Equipment Information (PPE-Info) Database which is a compendium of 
personal protective equipment (PPE) Federal regulations and consensus 
standards. This proposed data collection will be resubmitted at a later 
date for public comment once the review to include one additional 
standard is completed on the data collection instrument.
