SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3608; Amendment 2]


State of West Virginia

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency--effective 
September 1, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning July 22, 
2004, and continuing through September 1, 2004.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is October 5, 2004, and for 
economic injury the deadline is May 6, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: September 8, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
