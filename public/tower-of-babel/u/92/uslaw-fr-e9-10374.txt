Frontier Spinning Mills, Inc.; Cheraw, SC; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    In accordance with Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
amended, an investigation was initiated on February 9, 2009 in response 
to a petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of 
Frontier Spinning Mills, Inc., Cheraw, South Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 1st day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
