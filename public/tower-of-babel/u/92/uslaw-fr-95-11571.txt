DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Availability of U.S. Patents for Licensing

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Physical Sciences Directorate, 
and U.S. Army Communications-Electronics Command.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 37 CFR 404.6 announcement is made of the 
availability of the following U.S. patents for non-exclusive, exclusive 
or partially exclusive licensing. All of the listed patents have been 
assigned to the United States of America as represented by the 
Secretary of the Army, Washington, D.C.
    These patents cover a wide variety of technical arts including 
permanent magnet designs for various applications, power sources, 
phased array antennae, microstrip devices and applications, varying 
types resonators and oscillators for different applications, as well as 
many other different technical arts.
    Under the authority of section 11(a)(2) of the Federal Technology 
Transfer Act of 1986 (Public Law 99-502) and Section 207 of Title 35, 
United States Code, the Department of the Army as represented by the 
Army Research Laboratory, Physical Sciences Directorate, and the 
Communications-Electronics Command wish to license the U.S. patents 
listed below in a non-exclusive, exclusive or partially exclusive 
manner to any party interested in manufacturing, using, and/or selling 
devices or processes covered by these patents.
