National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Clinical Trials and Translational Research Advisory 
Committee, November 4, 2009, 8 a.m. to November 4, 2009, 5 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, 
MD 20892, which was published in the Federal Register on August 31, 
2009, 74FR44860.

meeting is open to the public.
