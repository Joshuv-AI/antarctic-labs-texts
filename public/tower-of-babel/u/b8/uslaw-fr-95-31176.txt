INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION
[Finance Docket No. 32820]


Norfolk Southern Railway Company and Atlantic and East Carolina 
Railway Company--Lease and Operation Exemption--North Carolina Railroad 
Company

AGENCY: Interstate Commerce Commission.

ACTION: Notice of Exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission exempts from the prior approval requirements of 
49 U.S.C. 11343-45 the renewal of a lease to allow Norfolk Southern 
Railway Company and Atlantic and East Carolina Railway Company to 
continue to lease and operate approximately 317 miles of North Carolina 
Railroad Company's rail line, between Charlotte and Morehead City, NC, 
subject to standard employee protective conditions.

DATES: This exemption is effective on December 22, 1995. Petitions to 
reopen must be filed by January 11, 1996.
