FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY
 

J.P. Morgan & Co. Incorporated; Permissible Nonbanking 
Activities; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 94-10526) published on page 
22853 of the issue for Tuesday, May 3, 1994.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of New York heading, the entry for 
JP Morgan & Co. Incorporated is revised to read as follows:
    1. J.P. Morgan & Co. Incorporated, New York, New York; to retain a 
partnership investment of 39.6 percent of equity and to increase its 
interest to 59.4 percent, and thereby engage in community development 
activities pursuant to Sec.  225.25(b)(6) of the Board's Regulation Y; 
to retain 6.06 percent equity interest in The New York Equity Fund 1989 
Limited Partnership and a 18.73 percent partnership interest in HUDCTC 
Limited Partnership, and thereby engage in community development 
activities pursuant to Sec.  225.25(b)(6) of the Board's Regulation Y.
    Comments on this application must be received by May 27, 1994.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, May 4, 1994.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
