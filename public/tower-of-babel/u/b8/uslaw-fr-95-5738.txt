DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP95-28-000]


Williams Natural Gas Co.; Notice of Technical Conference

March 3, 1995.
    In the Commission's order issued on November 30, 1994 in the above-
captioned proceeding, the Commission ordered that a technical 
conference is convened to resolve issues raised by the filing. The 
conference to address the issues has been scheduled for March 29, 1995, 
at 1:00 p.m. in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 810 First Street NE., Washington, DC, 
20426.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
