DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

License Cancellations

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: General Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to 19 CFR 111.51(a), the 
following Customs broker licenses have been cancelled due to the death 
of the broker. These licenses were issued in the Charleston Customs 
District.

Dolan Forte--license no. 4290,
John Leon Joye, Jr.--license no. 2888,
Albert L. Thompson--license no. 2192.

    Dated: April 14, 1994.
Robert S. Fernadez,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Operations.
