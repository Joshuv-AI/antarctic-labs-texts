SUMMARY: This notice amends the Notice of Realty Action for Direct Sale 
of Public Lands in Clark County, Nevada, published in 70 FR 77184-
77186. Under the section entitled SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION, the 
following correction is made. The Land Proposed for Sale is changed 
from:

T. 23 S., R. 61 E.,
    Sec. 9, S\1/2\SE\1/4\SWNW \1/4\ to
T. 23 S., R. 61 E.,
    Sec. 9, S\1/2\NE\1/4\SW\1/4\NW\1/4\.

    All other Terms and Conditions of the sale remain unchanged.

    Dated: January 23, 2006.
Juan Palma,
Field Manager.
