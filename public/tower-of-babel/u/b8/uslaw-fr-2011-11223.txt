NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards Meeting of the ACRS 
Subcommittee on EPR; Cancellation to May 11, 2011, ACRS Meeting--
Federal Register Notice

    The Federal Register Notice for the ACRS Subcommittee Meeting on 
the design certification application review of the U.S. Evolutionary 
Power Reactor scheduled to be held on May 11, 2011, is being canceled.
    The notice of this meeting was previously published in the Federal 
Register on Wednesday, Monday, April 25, 2011 [75 FR 22935].
    Further information regarding this meeting can be obtained by 
contacting Derek Widmayer, Designated Federal Official (Telephone: 301-
415-7366, E-mail: [email protected]) between 7:30 a.m. and 5:15 
p.m. (ET).

    Dated: May 3, 2011.
Yoira Diaz-Sanabria,
Acting Chief, Reactor Safety Branch B, Advisory Committee on Reactor 
Safeguards.
