Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line Corporation; Notice of Site Visit

June 27, 2002.
    On July 9, 2002, the Office of Energy Projects (OEP) staff will 
conduct a pre-certification site visit of Transcontinental Gas Pipe 
Line Corporation's (Transco) Trenton Woodbury Expansion Project in 
Mercer and Burlington Counties, New Jersey. We will examine the 
proposed project route and potential route variations. Representatives 
of Transco will be accompanying the OEP staff.
    All interested parties may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation. Those interested in attending should 
meet at 1:00 p.m. at the following location: Municipal Building, 
Township of Chesterfield, 300 Bordentown-Chesterfield Road, Trenton, 
New Jersey 08620.
    For additional information, please contact the Commission's Office 
of External Affairs at 1-866-208-FERC.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
