SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Advisory Council Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) National Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Monday, September 11, 1995 from 
10:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and Tuesday, September 12, 1995 from 9:00 a.m. 
to noon in Washington, DC at the Dupont Plaza Hotel, 1500 New Hampshire 
Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20036.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Dorothy Overal, 
Director, Office of Advisory Council, 409 3rd Street, SW., Washington, 
DC 20416, (202) 205-6434.


[[Page 43181]]

    Dated: August 14, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
