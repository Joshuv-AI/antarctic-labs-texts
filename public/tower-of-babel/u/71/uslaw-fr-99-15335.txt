47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 93-158, RM-8239, RM-8317]


FM Broadcasting Services; Hazlehurst, Utica, and Vicksburg, 
Mississippi

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final Rule; application for review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In MM Docket No. 93-158, the Chief, Policy and Rules Division, 
dismissed with prejudice, pursuant to a request for settlement filed 
jointly by Flinn Broadcasting Corporation and Donald B. Brady, an 
Application for Review filed by Brady. He had requested review of the 
Memorandum Opinion and Order, 61 FR 7999, published March 1, 1996, on 
reconsideration of the Report and Order, 59 FR 55,593, published 
November 8, 1994. The Report and Order had granted a counterproposal in 
this proceeding, substituting Channel 265C2 at Utica, Mississippi, and 
two accommodating channel substitutions. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective June 17, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: J. Bertron Withers, Jr., Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.
