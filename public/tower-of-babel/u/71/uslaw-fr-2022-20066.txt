City of Tacoma, Department of Public Utilities, Light Division, 
TPU Water Division; Notice of Transfer of Exemption

    1. On August 19, 2022, City of Tacoma, Department of Public 
Utilities, Light Division (City of Tacoma) exemptee for the 850-
Kilowatt Hood Street Hydroelectric Project No. 10256, filed a letter 
notifying the Commission that the project was transferred from City of 
Tacoma to TPU Water Division. The exemption from licensing was 
originally issued on August 17, 1987.\1\ The project is located on the 
Tacoma Water Transmission System in Pierce County, Washington. The 
transfer of an exemption does not require Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ City of Tacoma, Department of Public Utilities, Light 
Division, 40 FERC ] 62,181 (1987).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. TPU Water Division is now the exemptee of the Hood Street 
Hydroelectric Project No. 10256. All correspondence must be forwarded 
to Mr. Travis Nelson, TPU Water Division, (d.b.a.--Tacoma Water), 3628 
S 35th Street, Tacoma, WA 98409, Phone: (253) 579-4082, Email:
