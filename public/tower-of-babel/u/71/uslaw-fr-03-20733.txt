DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Occupational Safety and Health Administration


Maritime Advisory Committee on Occupational Safety and Health

AGENCY: Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), Labor.

ACTION: Reestablishment of the Maritime Advisory Committee for 
Occupational Safety and Health (MACOSH); appointment of Committee 
members; Amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the July 31, 2003, Federal Register, OSHA published a 
notice reestablishing the Maritime Advisory Committee for Occupational 
Safety and Health (MACOSH) and appointing the committee members (68 FR 
44970). Three names were inadvertently omitted. The notice is hereby 
amended to add the following names and organizations they represent: 
Peter Schmidt, State of Washington; Augustin Tellez, Seafarers 
International Union; and Charles I. Thompson III, Virginia 
International Terminals.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: OSHA, Office of Communications, Room 
N-3647, U.S. Department of Labor, 200 Constitution Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20210; Telephone: (202) 693-1999.
