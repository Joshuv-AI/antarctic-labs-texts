Self-Regulatory Organizations; Nasdaq PHLX LLC; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Changes To Amend Open Outcry Options 
Transaction Charges

March 29, 2022.
    On March 10, 2022, Nasdaq PHLX LLC (``Phlx'') filed with the 
Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission'') pursuant to Section 
19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (``Act'') \1\ and Rule 
19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to increase the Floor Lead 
Market Maker and Floor Market Maker options transaction charge and pay 
a Floor Broker rebate whenever a Floor Broker executes an order contra 
a Floor Lead Market Maker or Floor Market Maker in certain open outcry 
transactions in multiply-listed Penny and non-Penny symbols. The 
proposed rule change was immediately effective upon filing with the 
Commission pursuant to Section 19(b)(3)(A) of the Act.\3\ The proposed 
rule change was published for comment in the Federal Register on March 
23, 2022.\4\ On March 29, 2022, Phlx withdrew the proposed rule change 
(SR-Phlx-2022-10).
