LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION

 

Board of Directors; Audit and Appropriations Committee Meeting 
Notice

TIME AND DATE: The Legal Services Corporation Board of Directors Audit 
and Appropriations Committee will meet on December 12, 1994. The 
meeting will commence at 9:00 a.m.

PLACE: The Washington Court Hotel, 525 New Jersey Avenue, N.W., 
Washington Room, Washington, D.C. 20001, (202) 628-2100.

STATUS OF MEETING: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

OPEN SESSION:

1. Approval of Agenda.
2. Approval of Minutes of November 5, 1994 Meeting
3. Consider and Act on Proposed Committee Meeting Schedule for 
Calendar Year 1995
4. Consider and Act on Methodology for Determining the Level of 
Compensation Paid to the Inspector General
5. Consider and Act on Other Business

CONTACT PERSON FOR INFORMATION: Patricia Batie (202) 336-8800.
    Upon request, meeting notices will be made available in alternate 
formats to accommodate visual and hearing impairments.
    Individuals who have a disability and need an accommodation to 
attend the meeting may notify Patricia Batie (202) 336-8800.

    Date Issued: December 1, 1994.
Patricia D. Batie,
Corporate Secretary.
