SUMMARY: In the Federal Register issue of Thursday, August 18, 1999, 
make the following correction:

Correction

    In the Federal Register issue of Wednesday, August 18, 1999, in FR 
Doc. 99-21257, on page 45027, the grant category beginning in the first 
column under the heading ``Health and Welfare Technical Advisory Group 
(CFDA# 93.110AI)'' is withdrawn from competition due to consideration 
of alternative mechanisms to fund proposed activities.
