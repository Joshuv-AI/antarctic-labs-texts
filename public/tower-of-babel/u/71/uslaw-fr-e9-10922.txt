Bridgestone APM Plant 1, Carey, OH; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 1, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by three workers on behalf of the workers at Bridgestone APM 
Plant 1, Carey, Ohio.
    The petitioners requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
