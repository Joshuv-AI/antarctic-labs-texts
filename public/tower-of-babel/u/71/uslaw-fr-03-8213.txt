DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Notice of Advisory Committee Meeting date change.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On Friday, March 21, 2003 (68 FR 13906), the Department of 
Defense announced closed meetings of the Defense Science Board Task 
Force on Missile Defense, Phase III--Modeling and Simulation. The 
meeting originally planned for May 1-2, 2003, has been rescheduled to 
May 12, 2003, at the Institute for Defense Analyses, 1801 N. Beauregard 
Street, Alexandria, VA.
