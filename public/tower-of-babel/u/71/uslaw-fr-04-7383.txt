DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY

Bureau of Customs and Border Protection


Notice of Revocation of Customs Broker Licenses

AGENCY: Bureau of Customs and Border Protection, U.S. Department of 
Homeland Security.

ACTION: Customs broker license revocations for failure to file the 
triennial status report and applicable fee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to section 641 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended 
(19 U.S.C. 1641), and Title 19 of the Code of Federal Regulations at 
section 111.30, the following Customs broker licenses are revoked by 
operation of law without prejudice. Note that some of these entities 
may continue to provide broker services under another valid brokerage 
license.

    Dated: March 18, 2004.
Jayson P. Ahern,
Assistant Commissioner, Office of Field Operations.
