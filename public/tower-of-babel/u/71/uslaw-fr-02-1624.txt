DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Office of the Secretary


Presidential Task Force on Employment of Adults With 
Disabilities; Notice of Postponement of Meeting

    The Department of Labor published notice of an open public meeting 
of the Presidential Task Force on Employment of Adults with 
Disabilities in the Federal Register on December 17, 2001 (66 FR 
64987). That meeting has been postponed. The meeting will be 
rescheduled and its new date will be announced and published in this 
publication.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paul Bennett at 202/693-4939 (voice), 
202/693-4929 (fax), or 202/693-4920 (TTY).

    Dated: January 16, 2002.
Gary B. Reed,
Acting Executive Director, Presidential Task Force on Employment of 
Adults with Disabilities.
