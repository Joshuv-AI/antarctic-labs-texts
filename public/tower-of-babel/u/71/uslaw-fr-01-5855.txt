ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

Availability of ``Allocation of Fiscal Year 2001 Operator 
Training Grants''

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of document availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: EPA is announcing availability of a memorandum entitled 
``Allocation of Fiscal Year 2001 Operator Training Grants'' issued on 
March 1, 2001. This memorandum provides National guidance for the 
allocation of funds used under section 104(g)(l) of the Clean Water 
Act.

ADDRESSES: Municipal Assistance Branch, U.S. EPA, 1200 Pennsylvania 
Avenue, NW (4204-M), Washington, D.C. 20460.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Curt Baranowski, (202) 564-0636 or 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The subject memorandum may be viewed and 
downloaded from EPA's homepage, www.epa.gov/owm/mab/owm0317.pdf.

    Dated: March 1, 2001.
Michael B. Cook,
Director, Office of Wastewater Management.
