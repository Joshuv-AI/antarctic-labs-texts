Texaco Refining and Marketing Inc. v. SFPP, L.P.; ARCO Products 
Company, Mobil Oil Corporation v. SFPP, L.P.; Ultramar Inc. v. SFPP, 
L.P.; SFPP, L.P.; Notice of Suspension of Procedural Schedule

July 22, 2008.
    On July 17, 2008, all parties in the above-captioned proceeding 
filed pursuant to Rule 212 of the Commissions Rules of Practice and 
Procedure, a Joint Motion to Suspend Paper Hearing Procedural Schedule 
and Request for Expedited Action. That filing advised the Commission 
that a limited settlement had been reached on the return on equity 
issues in the above-captioned proceeding. The parties also stated that 
therefore there was no further need to proceed with the hearing 
schedule that had been established in the above-captioned proceeding 
and requested that the hearing schedule be suspended. Notice is hereby 
given that the hearing schedule established in Docket No. OR96-2-012, 
et al., is suspended until further action by the Commission.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
