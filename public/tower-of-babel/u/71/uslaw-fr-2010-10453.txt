Transferring Certain Enforcement Hotline Matters to the Dispute 
Resolution Service: Correction

AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Final Rule: correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission is correcting a final 
rule that appeared in the Federal Register of April 26, 2010, 75 FR 
21503.
    The document transferred certain enforcement hotline matters to the 
Commission's Dispute Resolution Service. This document corrects various 
Part references on the first page of the rule and in the amendatory 
language.

DATES: Effective May 1, 2010.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stuart Fischer, Office of Enforcement, 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, (202) 502-8517.
    Nils Nichols, Office of Administrative Litigation/Dispute 
Resolution Service, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, (202) 502-8638.

26, 2010 (75 FR 21403), make the following corrections:

``18 CFR Parts 1b and 157''.
    2. On page 21505, column 2, the words of issuance are corrected to
