DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Cancer 
Institute Clinical Trials and Translational Research Advisory 
Committee, November 6, 2019, 8:00 a.m. to November 6, 2019, 4:00 p.m., 
National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, East Wing, Conference Room 
TE406, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on February 15, 2019, 84 FR 4486.
    This meeting has been cancelled.

    Dated: October 9, 2019.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
