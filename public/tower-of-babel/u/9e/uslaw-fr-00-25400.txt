NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management; Renewal

    The NSF management official having responsibility for the U.S. 
National Assessment Synthesis Team (#5219) has determined that renewing 
through October 31, 2000, is necessary and in the public interest in 
connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the Director, 
National Science Foundation (NSF), by 42 USC 1861 et seq. This 
determination follows consultation with the Committee Management 
Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Authority for this Committee will expire on October 31, 2000. For 
more information, please contact Karen York, NSF, at (703) 292-4387.

    Dated: September 28, 2000.
Karen J. York,
Committee Management Officer.
