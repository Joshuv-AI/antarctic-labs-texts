Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP); Cancellation 
of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Advisory 
Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP); June 18, 2021, 11:00 a.m. 
to 5:00 p.m., EDT (dates and times subject to change), in the original 
FRN.
    The virtual meeting was published in the Federal Register on June 
15, 2021, Volume 86, Number 113, pages 31716-31717.
    Due to the Federal holiday on June 18, 2021, the ACIP meeting has 
been canceled in its entirety. In addition, the previously scheduled 
June 18, 2021 agenda items will be discussed at the ACIP meeting on 
June 23-25, 2021. Additionally, public comments submitted to docket 
number CDC-2021-0060 will be shared with ACIP members at or prior to 
the June 23-25, 2021 meeting.

8367; Email: [email protected].
