SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: By a vote of 3-0 on May 7 and 10, the

CFR 50.48 to Allow Performance-Based Approaches Using National Fire

Generating Plants,' 2001 Edition'' be held on May 11, and on less than 
one week's notice to the public.
