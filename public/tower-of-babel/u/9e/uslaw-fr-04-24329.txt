DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4818]


U.S. National Commission for UNESCO Notice of Commission 
Establishment

    The Department of State Announces the Establishment of the U.S. 
National Commission for the United Nations Educational, Scientific, and 
Cultural Organization (UNESCO).
    The U.S. National Commission for UNESCO, which will operate 
pursuant to the requirements of the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
(FACA), will provide recommendations to the Secretary of State and to 
the U.S. Mission to UNESCO in Paris. The primary focus of the 
recommendations will relate to the formulation and implementation of 
U.S. policy towards UNESCO on matters of education, science, 
communications, and culture. In its efforts to uphold and promote human 
rights, tolerance, and learning worldwide, the U.S. National Commission 
for UNESCO is necessary and in the public interest.
