DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. AD15-5-000]


Available Transfer Capability Standards for Wholesale Electric 
Transmission Services; Supplemental Notice Providing for Post-Workshop 
Comments

    As discussed in prior notices in this docket, Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (Commission) staff will convene a workshop to 
discuss the calculation of Available Transfer Capability (ATC) for 
wholesale electric

[[Page 23266]]

transmission services.\1\ Parties wishing to file comments following 
the April 21, 2015 workshop may do so on or before May 6, 2015.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\Prior Notices were issued on December 30, 2014 and January 
30, February 27 and March 31, 2015.
