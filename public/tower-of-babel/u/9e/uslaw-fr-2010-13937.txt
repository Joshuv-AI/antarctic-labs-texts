Office of New Reactors: Notice of Availability of the Final Staff 
Guidance; Section 14.3.12 on Physical Security Hardware Inspections, 
Tests, Analyses, and Acceptance Criteria

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The NRC is issuing its Final Revision 1 to NUREG-0800, 
``Standard Review Plan (SRP) for the Review of Safety Analysis Reports 
for Nuclear Power Plants,'' Section 14.3.12 on ``Physical Security 
Hardware--Inspections, Tests, Analyses, and Acceptance Criteria,'' 
(Agencywide Documents Access and Management System (ADAMS) Accession 
No. ML100970568).
    The NRC staff issues revisions to SRP sections to facilitate timely 
implementation of the current staff guidance and to facilitate reviews 
to amendments to licenses for operating reactors or for activities 
associated with review of applications for early site permits and 
combined licenses for the Office of New Reactors. The NRC staff will 
also incorporate Revision 1 of SRP Section 14.3.12 into the next 
revisions of the Regulatory Guide 1.206, ``Combined License 
Applications for Nuclear Power Plants,'' and related guidance 
documents.
    Disposition: On January 28, 2010, the NRC staff issued the proposed 
Revision 1 on Section 14.3.12 on ``Physical Security Hardware--
Inspections, Tests, Analyses, and Acceptance Criteria,'' ADAMS 
Accession No. ML100040148. There were no comments received on the 
proposed revision. Therefore, the guidance is issued as final without 
changes to the proposed notification as above.
