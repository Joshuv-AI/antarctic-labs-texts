Shell Chemical Company, Point Pleasant Polyester Plant, Apple 
Grove, West Virginia; Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Shell Chemical Company, Point 
Pleasant Polyester Plant, Apple Grove, West Virginia. The application 
contained no new substantial information which would bear importantly 
on the Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the 
application was issued.

    TA-W-37, 314; Shell Chemical Company, Point Pleasant Polyester 
Plant, Apple Grove, West Virginia (June 12, 2000).

    Signed at Washington, DC this 12th day of June, 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
