Agere Systems, Infrastructre Division, Breinigsville, PA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 20, 2002, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Agere Systems, Infrastructure Division, 
Breinigsville, Pennsylvania.

[[Page 59554]]

    The petition has been deemed invalid. The petitioning group of 
three workers did not have the necessary three signatures sufficient to 
constitute a valid petition. Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of August, 2002.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
