PEACE CORPS


Information Collection Request Under OMB Review

AGENCY: Peace Corps.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Peace Corps published a document in the Federal Register 
of July 11, 2011, [FR Doc. 2011-17273, pages 40755-40756], concerning 
request for comments on an information collection. This document 
corrects errors in that notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Denora Miller can be contacted by 
telephone at 202-692-1236 or e-mail at [email protected].

Correction

    On page 40756, in the first column, line four, should read:
    ``The Peace Corps invites the general public to comment on a 
proposed revision of a currently approved collection, Peace Corps 
Volunteer Application (OMB Control Number 0420-0005).''
    On page 40756, in the first column, under the heading SUPPLEMENTARY 
INFORMATION (3) should read:
    ``Type of Review: Revision of a currently approved collection.''

    Dated: July 19, 2011.
Earl W. Yates,
Associate Director, Management.
