Ocean Transportation Intermediary License; Rescission of Order of 
Revocation

licenses are being rescinded by the Federal Maritime Commission

409) and the regulations of the Commission pertaining to the licensing 
of Ocean Transportation Intermediaries, 46 CFR part 515.
