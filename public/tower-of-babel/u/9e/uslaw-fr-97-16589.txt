DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER96-2821-000]


Northern States Power Company; Notice of Filing

June 19, 1997.
    Take notice that on May 16, 1997, Northern States Power Company 
(NSP) tendered for filing its amendment to the Interconnection and 
Interchange Agreement between NSP and North Central Power Company (NCP) 
in the above referenced docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before June 30, 1997. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the

[[Page 34242]]

Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
