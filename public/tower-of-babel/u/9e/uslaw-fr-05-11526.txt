Poliovirus Antibody Seroprevalence Among Inner City Preschool 
Children, Post-OPV Era; Notice of Availability of Funds--Amendment

    A notice announcing the availability of fiscal year (FY) 2005 funds 
for a cooperative agreement for Poliovirus Antibody Seroprevalence 
Among Inner City Preschool Children, Post-OPV Era was published in the 
Federal Register, Tuesday, May 10, 2005, Volume 70, Number 89, pages 
24594-24598.
    This notice has been withdrawn and applications are not being 
accepted for funding.

    Dated: June 2, 2005.
William P. Nichols,
Director, Procurement and Grants Office, Centers for Disease Control 
and Prevention.
