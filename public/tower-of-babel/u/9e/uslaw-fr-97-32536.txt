COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' meeting scheduled for 18 December 1997 
has been cancelled. The next meeting is scheduled for 22 January 1998 
at 10:00 a.m. in the Commission's offices in the Pension Building, 
Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. 
20001, to discuss various projects affecting the appearance of 
Washington, D.C.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200.

    Dated In Washington, D.C., December 3, 1997.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
