OVERSEAS PRIVATE INTESTMENT CORPORATION


May 16, 2002 Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (Volume 67, Number 84, Page 21779) on May 1, 2002. 
No requests were received to provide testimony or submit written 
statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in 
conjunction with OPIC's May 22, 2002 Board of Directors meeting 
scheduled for 2 p.m. on May 16, 2002 has been cancelled.
