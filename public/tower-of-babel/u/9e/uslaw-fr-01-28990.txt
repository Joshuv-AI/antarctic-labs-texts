Art Unlimited, LLC, New Holstein, WI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 7, 2001, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Art Unlimited, LLC, located in 
New Holstein, Hurley, and Montreal, Wisconsin.
    This case is being terminated because the petitioner was requested 
that the petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 5th day of November, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
