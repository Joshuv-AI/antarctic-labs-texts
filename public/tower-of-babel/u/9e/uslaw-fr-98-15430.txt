Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement; Title to 
Government Property

AGENCY: Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Director of Defense Procurement is withdrawing a proposed 
rule published in the Federal Register on October 17, 1997 (62 FR 
54008), under DFARS Case 97-D027, Title to Government Property. The 
rule proposed amendments to the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation 
Supplement to reduce the amount of Government-owned special tooling and 
test equipment in the possession of DoD contractors. After considering 
public comments, the Director of Defense Procurement has decided to 
withdraw the proposed rule.
