Charles Navasky & Co., Inc. Philipsburg, PA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 23, 1998 in response to a petition field on 
behalf of workers at Charles Navasky & Co., Inc., Philipsburg, 
Pennsylvania.
    This case is being terminated because the petitioning group of 
workers are subject to an ongoing investigation for which a 
determination has not yet been issued. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose; and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 16th day of March, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
