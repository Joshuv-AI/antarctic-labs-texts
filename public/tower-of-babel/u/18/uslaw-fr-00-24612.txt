FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

September 20, 2000.
    Petition for Reconsideration has been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th Street, SW, 
Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to this petition must 
be filed by October 11, 2000. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's 
rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition must be filed within 
10 days after the time for filing oppositions have expired.
    Subject: Interconnection and Resale Obligations Pertaining to 
Commercial Mobile Radio Service (CC Docket No. 94-54).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 1.
