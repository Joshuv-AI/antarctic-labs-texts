Radio Broadcasting Services; Huntley, Montana

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document amends the Table of FM Allotments, of the 
Federal Communications Commission's (Commission) rules, by allotting 
Channel 284A at Huntley, Montana, as the community's first local 
service. To accommodate the allotment, we modify the license of KYSX, 
Billings, Montana to specify operation on Channel 286A in lieu of 
Channel 283C1. A staff engineering analysis reveals that Channel 284A 
can be allotted to Huntley, Montana, consistent with the minimum 
distance separation requirements of the Commission's rules with a site 
restriction of 13.9 km (8.6 miles) north of the community.

DATES: Effective December 2, 2024.
