DEPARTMENT OF STATE
 

United States International Telecommunications Advisory Committee 
Radiocommunication Sector Study Group 9; Meeting Notice

    The Department of State announces that the United States 
International Telecommunications Advisory Committee (ITAC), 
Radiocommunication Sector Study Group 9, will meet on April 28, 1994, 
at 9:30 a.m. in room 315 at the Federal Communication Commission, 1919 
M Street, NW. Washington, DC 20554.
    This is the initial meeting to prepare for the international 
meeting scheduled to be held November 1-11, 1994. Additionally, the 
meeting will consider other activities affecting the work of Study 
Group 9 such as the work of Task Group 2/2 (Sharing with the MSS and 
BSS under 3 GHz), Task group 4/5 (Feeder Links), sharing with the space 
science services and any other matters within the competence of this 
Study Group.
    Members of the General Public may attend the meetings and join in 
the discussions, subject to the instructions of the Chairman. Those 
planning to attend the meeting should contact Mr. Alex Latker by phone 
at (202) 632-3214 or by fax at (202) 634-1382.

    Dated: March 31, 1994.
Warren G. Richards,
Chairman, U.S. ITAC for ITU-Radiocommunication Sector.
