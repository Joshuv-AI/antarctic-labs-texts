SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

[Release No. 34-44304; File No. 4-444]


Roundtable on Portals

AGENCY: Securities and Exchange Commission

ACTION: Notice of roundtable meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 23, 2001, the Securities and Exchange Commission will 
host a roundtable to discuss issues related to relationships between 
Internet websites and financial service providers. Invitees include a 
cross section of individuals, including representatives from the 
financial industry, representatives of the Internet community, 
regulators, and academics.
    The roundtable will take place at the Commission's William O. 
Douglas Room, Room 1C30, 450 Fifth Street, NW., Washington, DC, from 1 
p.m. to

[[Page 27712]]

4:30 p.m. The public is invited to observe the roundtable discussions. 
Seating is available on a first-come first-serve basis. The schedule, 
agenda, and list of panelists for the roundtable will be posted on the 
Commission's Internet website (http://www.sec.gov).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Brian Bussey or Christine Richardson, 
Office of the Chairman, at (202) 942-0100.

    Dated: May 14, 2001.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
