DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Office of Energy Research


Fusion Energy Advisory Committee; Meeting Postponement

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of meeting postponement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: An open meeting of the Fusion Energy Advisory Committee that 
was scheduled to be held on January 18-19, 1996, at 9:00 a.m., at the 
Omni Shoreham Hotel in Washington, D.C., has been rescheduled due to 
the snow emergency. The meeting will be held on January 26-27, 1996, at 
the same location. This meeting was announced in the Federal Register 
on Wednesday, January 10, 1996. (61-FR-724).

    Issued at Washington, D.C., on January 17, 1996.
Rachel Murphy Samuel,
Acting Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
