DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 7141-002]


Mill Shoals Hydro Company, Inc.; Mill Shoals Hydro Company, LLC; 
Notice of Transfer of Exemption

    1. By letter filed April 24, 2015,\1\ William B. Conway, Jr., 
Counsel for Enel Green Power North America, Inc. (EGPNA),\2\ informed 
the Commission that the exemption from licensing for the Milstead Dam 
Project, FERC No. 7141, originally issued April 15, 1983,\3\ has been 
transferred to Mill Shoals Hydro Company, LLC, an affiliate of Enel 
Green Power. The project is located on the Yellow River in Rockdale 
County, Georgia. The transfer of an exemption does not require 
Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Seventeen other exempted projects which are to be 
transferred were included in the April 24, 2015 letter. These 
exemptions will be handled under separate proceedings.
    \2\ Enel Green Power North America, Inc. is a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Enel Green Power. Enel Green Power is a well-
capitalized publicly traded company.
    \3\ 23 FERC ] 62,045, Notice of Exemption From Licensing (1983).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Mill Shoals Hydro Company, LLC is now the exemptee of the 
Milstead Dam Project, FERC No. 7141. All correspondence should be 
forwarded to: Mill Shoals Hydro Company, LLC, c/o Enel Green Power 
North America, Inc., Attn: General Counsel, 1 Tech Drive, Suite 220, 
Andover, MA 01810.

    Dated: May 19, 2015.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
