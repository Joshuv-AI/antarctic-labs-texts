Temporary Closure of the Red Rock Canyon Visitors Center, Las 
Vegas, Nevada

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: The Acting Field Manager, Las Vegas Field Office, Las Vegas, 
Nevada, announces a temporary closure of the Red Rock National 
Conservation Area, Visitors Center, in Las Vegas, Nevada. The Red Rock 
Visitors Center needs urgent repair and replacement of a hot tar roof. 
The Scenic Drive at the Red Rock Canyon will remain open.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

EFFECTIVE DATES: The closure will go into effect March 27, 2000 through 
March 31, 2000. The Visitors Center will reopen on April 1, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sheree Fisher at (702) 647-5142 or Bob 
Dunn at (702) 647-5103.

James R. Dunn,
Acting Field Manager.
