FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Detailed information concerning this 
action is available for review at the Bureau of Land Management, Elko 
Resource Area, 3900 E. Idaho Street, Elko, Nevada.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Upon publication of this notice in the 
Federal Register, the mineral interests described above will be 
segregated to the extent that they will not be open to appropriation 
under the public land laws, including the mining laws. The segregative 
effect of the application shall terminate upon issuance of a patent or 
other document of conveyance of such mineral interests, upon final 
rejection of the application, or two years from the date of filing of 
the application, whichever occurs first.
