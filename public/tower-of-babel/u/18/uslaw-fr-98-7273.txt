FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement(s) Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement(s) under the Shipping Act of 1984.
    Interested parties can review or obtain copies of agreements at the 
Washington, DC offices of the Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, 
N.W., Room 962. Interested parties may submit comments on an agreement 
to the Secretary, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, 
within 10 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 203-011075-043.
    Title: Central America Discussion Agreement.
    Parties: Concorde Shipping, Inc., Dole Fresh Fruit, King Ocean 
Central America, S.A., Crowley American Transport, Inc., Seaboard 
Marine, Ltd., A.P. Moller-Maersk Line, Sea-Land Service, Inc.
    Synopsis: The proposed Amendment revises Article 7(k) of the 
Agreement to provide that the amount of the required security is 
$5,000, or one-half of the total estimated operating expenses of the 
Agreement in the year immediately preceding the calendar year in which 
the new member joins the Agreement.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.

    Dated: March 16, 1998.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
