DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

National Agricultural Statistics Service


Notice of Intent To Seek Approval To Reinstate an Information 
Collection; Correction

AGENCY: National Agricultural Statistics Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, the 
National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS) published a correction 
notice in the Federal Register of April 12, 2021, concerning the 
intention of NASS to seek reinstatement of an information collection, 
the 2022 Census of Agriculture. The document contained an incorrect 
date.
