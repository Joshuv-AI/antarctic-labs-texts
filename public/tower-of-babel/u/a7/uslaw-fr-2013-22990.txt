DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 06, 2013, 06:30 p.m. 
to November 07, 2013, 04:00 p.m., Hilton Washington/Rockville, 1750 
Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 16, 2013, 78 FR 50065.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the location from the 
Hilton Washington/Rockville, 1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852 
to Crowne Plaza Washington DC/Rockville, 3 Research Court, Rockville, 
MD 20850. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: September 17, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
