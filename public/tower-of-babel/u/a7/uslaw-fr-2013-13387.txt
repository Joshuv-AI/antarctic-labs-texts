OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act; Public Hearing, June 5, 2013; Cancellation

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Public Hearing in Conjunction 
with each Board meeting was published in the Federal Register (Volume 
78, Number 95, Pages 28897 and 28898) on May 16, 2013. No requests were 
received to provide testimony or submit written statements for the 
record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing scheduled for 2 p.m., June 5, 
2013 in conjunction with OPIC's June 13, 2013 Board of Directors 
meeting has been cancelled.
