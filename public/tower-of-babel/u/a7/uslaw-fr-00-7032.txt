Guardian Pipeline, L.L.C.; Notice of Site Visit

March 16, 2000
    On March 29 and 30, 2000 the Commission's Office of Energy Projects 
(OEP) staff will conduct an inspection of the pipeline route proposed 
by Guardian Pipeline, L.L.C. (Guardian) for the Guardian Pipeline 
Project. The proposed route, crossing portions of Wisconsin and 
Illinois, will be inspected by helicopter. The aerial inspection will 
begin at Milwaukee's General Mitchell International Airport on the 
morning of March 29. If weather conditions preclude an overflight, the 
inspection will be conducted by automobile from a location to be 
determined. The inspection will continue along the route southward from 
Ixonia, Wisconsin to Joliet, Illinois. Representatives of Guardian will 
accompany the OEP staff.
    All interested parties may attend, although those planning to 
attend must provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee of the 
Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 208-1088.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
