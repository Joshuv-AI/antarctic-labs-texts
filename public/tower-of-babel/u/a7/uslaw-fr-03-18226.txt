Radio Broadcasting Services; Clarendon, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division dismisses a Petition for Rule Making filed 
by Maurice Salsa, requesting the allotment of Channel 237A at 
Clarendon, Texas, as that community's second local aural transmission 
service. See 68 FR 18179, April 15, 2003. Maurice Salsa, or no other 
party, filed comments in support of the allotment of Channel 237A to 
Clarendon, Texas. It is the Commission's policy to refrain from making 
a new allotment to a community absent a bona fide expression of 
interest.
