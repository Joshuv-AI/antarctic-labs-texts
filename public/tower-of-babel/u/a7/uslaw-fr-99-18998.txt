New England Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The New England Fishery Management Council (Council) is 
cancelling the meeting of its Research Steering and Experimental 
Fisheries Committee scheduled for July 29, 1999 at 9:30 a.m. The 
meeting was announced in the Federal Register on July 13, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paul J. Howard, Executive Director, 
New England Fishery Management Council; (781) 231-0422.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The initial notice published on July 13, 
1999 (64 FR 37751). Because of scheduling conflicts, the meeting of the 
Research Steering and Experimental Fisheries Committee will be 
cancelled. An alternative date will be announced as soon as possible.

    Dated: July 20, 1999.
Richard W. Surdi,
Acting Director, Office of Sustainable Fisheries, National Marine 
Fisheries Service.
