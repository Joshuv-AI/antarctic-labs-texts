East Tennessee Natural Gas Company; Notice of Site Visit

June 13, 2000.
    On June 19, 20, and 21, 2000, the staff of the Office of Energy 
Projects will be conducting an environmental site visit of East 
Tennessee Natural Gas Company's Rocky Top Expansion Project in Wythe, 
Smyth, and Washington Counties, Virginia and Greene, Roane, McMinn, 
Morgan, Overton, Fentress, and Hamilton Counties, Tennessee. All 
parties may attend. Those planning to attend must provide their own 
transportation.
    For further information about where the site inspection will begin, 
please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-1088.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
