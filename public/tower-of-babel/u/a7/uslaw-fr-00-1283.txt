NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Public Hearing

    The National Transportation Safety Board will convene a public 
hearing beginning at 9:00 a.m., local time on Wednesday, January 26-29, 
2000, at the Arkansas Excelsior Hotel, Three Statehouse Plaza, Little 
Rock, Arkansas 72201, concerning American Airlines, Inc., Flight 1420, 
McDonnell Douglas MD-82 Accident in Little Rock, Arkansas on June 1, 
1999. For more information, contact Ben Berman, NTSB Office of Aviation 
Safety at (202) 314-6331 or Paul Schlamm NTSB Office of Public Affairs 
at (202) 314-6100.
    Individuals requesting specific accommodation should contact Mrs. 
Carolyn Dargan on 202-314-6305 by Friday, January 21, 2000.

    Dated: January 13, 2000.
Rhonda Underwood,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
