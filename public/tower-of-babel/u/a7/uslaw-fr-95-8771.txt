NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Two Public Forums for Alaskan Air Safety Study

    As part of a special study of aviation safety in Alaska, the 
National Transportation Safety Board will convene two public forums. 
The forums will be held in Juneau, Alaska, on May 22, 1995, at the 
Westmark Baranof Hotel, 127 North Franklin St., and in Anchorage, 
Alaska, on May 24 and 25, at the Sheraton Anchorage Hotel, 401 East 
Sixth Avenue. For more information, contact Mike Benson, Office of 
Public Affairs, Washington, D.C. 20594, telephone (202) 382-0660.

    Dated: April 5, 1995.
Bea Hardesty,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
