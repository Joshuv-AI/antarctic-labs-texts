SUMMARY: In notice document on page 22170 in the issue of Friday, April 
29, 1994, make the following corrections:

1. Delete entire paragraph entitled ADDRESSES.
2. Insert the following paragraph after the last paragraph of the 
original notice.

Area Manager, Yuma Resource Area, at the above address.
