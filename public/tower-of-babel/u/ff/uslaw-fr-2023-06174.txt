Submission for OMB Review; GSA Equity Study on Remote Identity 
Proofing; Correction

AGENCY: Technology Transformation Services (TTS), General Services 
Administration (GSA).

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration published a document in 
the Federal Register of February 10, 2023, concerning a request for 
comments regarding a new information collection. The document contained 
information in the discussion and analysis section that is no longer 
needed.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Tiffany Andrews or Gerardo E. Cruz-
Ortiz by phone 202-969-0772 or via email to 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of February 10, 2023, in FR Doc. 2023-
03131, on page 8864, in the second column, correct the sixth paragraph 
to read:
    GSA is consulting with the Center for Information Technology 
Research (CITeR) and researchers at Clarkson University to ensure that 
the statistical design of the study is sound. GSA representatives have 
met with staff from other government agencies that have conducted 
similar research. These groups have agreed that the collection is 
useful and necessary to improve the delivery of government services.

Beth Anne Killoran,
Deputy Chief Information Officer.
