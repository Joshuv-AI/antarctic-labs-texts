DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Attendance at Nepool Meetings

February 26, 2009.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby gives notice that 
members of the Commission and Commission staff may attend the following 
upcoming NEPOOL meetings:
     NEPOOL Participants Committee;
     March 6 (Manchester, VT);
     April 3 (Boston, MA);
     May 1 (Location TBD);
     June 5 (Location TBD);
     June 23 (Location TBD);
     NEPOOL Markets Committee Meeting;
     March 10-11 (Marlborough, MA);
     March 25-26 (Location TBD);
     April 6-7 (Westborough, MA);
     May 12-13 (Marlborough, MA);
     June 9-10 (Marlborough, MA).
