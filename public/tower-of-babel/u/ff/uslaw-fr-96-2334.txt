NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Special Emphasis Panel in Chemical and Transport Systems Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Special Emphasis Panel in Chemical & Transport Systems 
(#1190).
    Date: February 20, 1996; 8:30 am to 5:00 pm.
    Place: Room 580, National Science Foundation 4201 Wilson 
Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Type of Meeting: Closed.
    Contact Person: Dr. Farley Fisher, Program Director, 4201 Wilson 
Blvd., Arlington, VA 22230 Telephone: 703/306-1370.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning proposals submitted to NSF for financial support.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate Engineering Research Equipment 
proposals submitted to the Kinetics and Catalysis Program as part of 
the selection process for awards.
    Reason for Closing: The proposals being reviewed include 
information of a proprietary or confidential nature, including 
technical information; financial data, such as salaries; and 
personal information 

[[Page 4294]]
concerning individuals associated with the proposals. The matters are 
exempt under 5 U.S.C. 552b.(c)(4) and (6) of the Government Sunshine 
Act.

    Dated: January 31, 1996
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
