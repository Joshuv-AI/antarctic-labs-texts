DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Child 
Psychopathology and Developmental Disabilities Study Section, October 
17, 2013, 8:00 a.m. to October 18, 2013, 5:00 p.m., The William F. 
Bolger Center, 9600 Newbridge Drive, Potomac, MD 20854 which was 
published in the Federal Register on September 23, 2013, 78 FR Pgs. 
58324-58325.
    The meeting will be held at the National Institutes of Health, 6701 
Rockledge Dr., Bethesda, MD 20892. The meeting will start on November 
18, 2013 at 8:00 a.m. and will end on November 20, 2013 at 10:00 a.m. 
The meeting is closed to the public.
