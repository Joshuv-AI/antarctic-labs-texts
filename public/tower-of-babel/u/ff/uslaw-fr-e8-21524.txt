DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of General Medical Sciences; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory General Medical Sciences Council, September 18, 2008, 8:30 
a.m. to September 19, 2008, 5 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Natcher Building, Conference Rooms E1 & E2, 9000 Rockville Pike, 
Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 21, 2008, 73 FR 49468-49469.
    The meeting will be open to the public on September 19, 2008 from 
8:30 a.m. to adjournment. The meeting is partially closed to the 
public.

    Dated: September 9, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
