[Disaster Declaration 10066 and 10067]


Louisiana Disaster LA-00001

AGENCY: Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a notice of an Administrative declaration of a 
disaster for the State of Louisiana, dated 03/03/2005.
    Incident: Severe Storms and Tornadoes.
    Incident Period: 11/23/2004.
    Effective Date: 03/03/2005.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: 05/02/2005.
    EIDL Loan Application Deadline Date: 12/05/2005.

ADDRESSES: Submit completed loan applications to: U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Disaster Area Office 1, 360 Rainbow Blvd. South 3rd 
Floor, Niagara Falls, NY 14303.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: A. Escobar, Office of Disaster 
Assistance, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street, Suite 
6050, Washington, DC 20416.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given that as a result of 
the Administrator's disaster declaration on 03/03/2005, applications 
for disaster loans may be filed at the address listed above or other 
locally announced locations.
    The following areas have been determined to be adversely affected 
by the disaster:
    Primary Parish: La Salle.
    Contiguous Parishes: Louisiana, Avoyelles, Caldwell, Catahoula, 
Grant, Rapides, Winn.
