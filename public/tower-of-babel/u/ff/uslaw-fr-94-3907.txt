DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
 

National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the March 1, 1994, meeting of 
the National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders 
Special Emphasis Panel, which was published in the Federal Register on 
January 31, 1994, 59 FR 4289.
    This Special Emphasis Panel was to have convened at 8 a.m. on March 
1, 1994, at the Chevy Chase Holiday Inn, but has been changed to 8 
a.m., March 11, 1994, at the Bethesda Marriott, 5151 Pooks Hills Road, 
Bethesda MD.
    The meeting will be closed to the public.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 93.173 
Biological Research Related to Deafness and Other Communicative 
Disorders)

    Dated: February 14, 1994.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
