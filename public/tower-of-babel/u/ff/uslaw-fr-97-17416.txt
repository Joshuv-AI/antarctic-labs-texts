DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans; Notice of Availability 
of Annual Report

    Under section 10(d) of Public Law 92-462 (Federal Advisory 
Committee Act) notice is hereby given that the Annual Report of the 
Department of Veterans Affairs' Advisory Committee of Minority Veterans 
for Fiscal Year 1996 has been issued. The Report summarizes activities 
of the Committee on matters relative to the administration of benefits, 
medical care services, and outreach as it relates to minority group 
veterans by the Department. The Report discusses the Committee's 
mission, goals and objectives, and makes recommendations to the 
Secretary. It is available for public inspection at two locations:

Federal Document Section, Exchange and Gifts Division, LM 632, Library 
of Congress, Washington, DC 20540
    and
Department of Veterans Affairs, Center for Minority Veterans, VACO 
Suite 700, 810 Vermont Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20420.

    Dated: June 20, 1997.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
