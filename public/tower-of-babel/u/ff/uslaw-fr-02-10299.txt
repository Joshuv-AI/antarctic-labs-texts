Limited Ports of Entry for Pet Birds, Performing or Theatrical 
Birds, and Poultry and Poultry Products

AGENCY: Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, USDA.

ACTION: Direct final rule; confirmation of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On February 12, 2002, we published a direct final rule in the 
Federal Register (See 67 FR 6369-6370.) The direct final rule notified 
the public of our intention to amend the regulations regarding ports 
designated for the importation of pet birds, performing or theatrical 
birds, and poultry and poultry products by removing Boston, MA, from 
the lists of limited ports of entry. We did not receive any written 
adverse comments or written notice of intent to submit adverse comments 
in response to the direct final rule.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The effective date of the direct final rule is 
confirmed as April 15, 2002.
