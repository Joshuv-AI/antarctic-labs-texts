AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Treasury.

ACTION: Correction to temporary regulations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to temporary regulations 
that were published in the Federal Register on Tuesday, December 30, 
2003 (68 FR 75119) requiring information reporting by a corporation if 
control of the corporation is acquired or if the corporation has a 
recapitalization or other substantial change in capital structure.

DATES: This correction is effective December 30, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Rose, (202) 622-4910 (not a 
toll-free number).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    The temporary regulations (TD 9101) that are the subject of these 
corrections are under sections 6043(c) and 6045 of the Internal Revenue 
Code.

Need for Correction

    As published, the temporary regulations (TD 9101) contain errors 
that may prove to be misleading and are in need of clarification.
