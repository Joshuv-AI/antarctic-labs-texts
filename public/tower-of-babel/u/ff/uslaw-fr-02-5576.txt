Celanese AG, Celanese Acetate Division, Rock Hill, SC; Dismissal 
of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18 (C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Celanese AG, Celanese Acetate 
Division, Rock Hill, South Carolina. The application contained no new 
substantial information which would bear importantly on the 
Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was 
issued.

TA-W-39,172; Celanese AG, Celanese Acetate Division, Rock Hill, South 
Carolina (February 22, 2002)

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 1st day of March, 2002.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
