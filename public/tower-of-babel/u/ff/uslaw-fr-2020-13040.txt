SUMMARY: The Office of Fossil Energy (FE) of the Department of Energy 
gives notice that during April 2020, it issued orders granting 
authority to import and export natural gas, to import and export 
liquefied natural gas (LNG), and to vacate prior authorization. These 
orders are summarized in the attached appendix and may be found on the 
FE website at https://www.energy.gov/fe/listing-doefe-authorizationsorders-issued-2020. They are also available for 
inspection and copying in the U.S. Department of Energy (FE-34), 
Division of Natural Gas Regulation, Office of Regulation, Analysis, and 
Engagement, Office of Fossil Energy, Docket Room 3E-033, Forrestal 
Building, 1000 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20585, (202) 586-
9387. The Docket Room is open between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 
p.m., Monday through Friday, except Federal holidays.

    Signed in Washington, DC, on June 12, 2020.
Amy Sweeney,
Director, Office of Regulation, Analysis, and Engagement Office of Oil 
and Natural Gas.
