DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Internal Revenue Service


Open Meeting of the Taxpayer Advocacy Panel Earned Income Tax 
Credit Issue Committee

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: An open meeting of the Taxpayer Advocacy Panel Earned Income 
Tax Credit Issue Committee will be conducted (via teleconference) to 
discuss various issues. The public is invited to make oral comments.

DATES: The meeting will be held Wednesday, September 17, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Marisa Knispel at 1-888-912-1227 
(toll-free), or 718-488-3557 (non toll-free).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given pursuant to section 
10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory

[[Page 54058]]

Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. App. (1988) that an open meeting of the 
Taxpayer Advocacy Panel Earned Income Tax Credit Issue Committee will 
be held Wednesday, September 17, 2003 from 2 p.m. to 3 p.m. EDT via a 
telephone conference call. The public is invited to make oral comments. 
Individual comments will be limited to 5 minutes. For more information 
or to confirm attendance, notification of intent to attend the meeting 
must be made with Marisa Knispel. Mrs. Knispel may be reached at 1-888-
912-1227 or 718-488-3557, or write Marisa Knispel, TAP Office, 10 
MetroTech Center, 625 Fulton Street, Brooklyn, NY 11201, or post 
comments to the Web site: http://www.improveirs.org.
    The agenda will include the following: Various IRS issues.

    Dated: September 10, 2003.
Tersheia Carter,
Acting Director, Taxpayer Advocacy Panel.
