California Independent System Operator Corporation; Notice 
Establishing Post-Technical Conference Schedule

October 2, 2007.
    In accordance with the directive of the September 21, 2006, Order 
Conditionally Accepting the California Independent System Operator 
Corporation's (CAISO) Electric Tariff Filing to Reflect Market Redesign 
and Technology Upgrade (MRTU),\1\ Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
staff convened a technical conference on September 26-27, 2007, to help 
determine which practices or details in the Business Practice Manuals 
(BPMs) should be included in the MRTU Tariff.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Cal. Indep. Sys. Operator Corp., 116 FERC ] 61,274, at P 
1370 (2006).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    As stated at the technical conference, the CAISO will file a 
response concerning the issues discussed at the technical conference by 
November 15, 2007. Following the CAISO's filing, parties may file 
comments, due on November 30, 2007, and reply comments, due on December 
7, 2007.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
