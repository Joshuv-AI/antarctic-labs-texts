SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The American Fisheries Act (AFA), signed into law in October 1998, 
established a new allocation program for the pollock fishery of the 
Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands Management Area (BSAI). NOAA issued an 
emergency interim rule to give immediate effect to all AFA-mandated 
management measures including new recordkeeping and reporting 
requirements for the BSAI pollock fishery, for processors that receive 
groundfish from AFA catcher vessels, and BSAI pollock fishery 
cooperatives formed under the AFA. These measures received emergency 
approval under the Paperwork Reduction Act, and NOAA is now seeking 
extension of that approval under normal clearance procedures.

II. Method of Collection

    All documents are submitted or retained in paper form except for 
shoreside processor logbooks, which are submitted in electronic form.

collection of information on respondents, including through the use of
