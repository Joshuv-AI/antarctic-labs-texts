DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Shell Energy North America (US), LP; Notice of Institution of 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

October 12, 2010.
    On October 12, 2010, the Commission issued an order that instituted 
a proceeding in Docket No. EL10-83-000, pursuant to section 206 of the 
Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. 824e (2005), concerning the justness 
and reasonableness of Shell Energy North America (US), LP's market-
based rate authority in the Central and Southwest balancing authority 
area. Shell Energy North America (US), LP, 133 FERC ] 61,033 (2010).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL10-83-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
