SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Bycatch Reduction Devices (BRDs) are used in shrimp trawls in the 
Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) to reduce the bycatch of other species. 
Only BRDs certified by the NOAA Fisheries can be used. Persons seeking 
to get certification from NOAA Fisheries for BRDs must submit 
information showing that testing proves the effectiveness of the 
equipment.

II. Method of Collection

    The information is submitted in paper form.

III. Data

    OMB Number: 0648-0345.
    Form Number: None.
    Type of Review: Regular submission
    Affected Public: Business or other for-profit organizations, 
individuals or households.
    Estimated Number of Respondents: 45.
    Estimated Time Per Response: 140 minutes for an application for
