FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

Time and Date: 10 a.m.--March 31, 2004.

place: 800 North Capitol Street, NW., First Floor Hearing Room, 
Washington, DC.

Status: A portion of the meeting will be open to the public and the 
remainder of the meeting will be closed.

Matters to be Considered: The open portion of the meeting:
    1. Docket No. 04-02--Optional rider for proof of additional NVOCC 
financial responsibility.
    The closed portion of the meeting:
    1. Petition No. P3--99-Petition of China Ocean Shipping (Group) 
Company for a partial exemption from the Controlled Carrier Act.
    2. Petition No. P4-03--Petition of China Shipping Container Lines 
Co., Ltd. for permanent full exemption from the first sentence of 
section 9(C) of the Shipping Act of 1984.
    3. Petition No. P6-03--Petition of SINOTRANS Container Lines Co., 
Ltd. (SINOLINES) for a full exemption from the first sentence of 
section 9(c) of the Shipping Act of 1984, as amended.
    4. Docket No. 98-14--Shipping restrictions, requirements and 
practices of the People's Republic of China.

Contact Person for More Information: Bryant L. VanBrakle, Secretary, 
(202) 523-5725.

Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
