Lionbridge US, Inc, Waltham, MA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 3, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by an Oregon One Stop Operator on behalf of the workers of 
Lionbridge US, Inc, Waltham, Massachusetts.
    Since the state official in Oregon is not a duly authorized 
representative of the workers in Massachusetts, the petition is deemed 
invalid.
    Additionally, the petitioner has requested that the petition be 
withdrawn. Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 21st day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
