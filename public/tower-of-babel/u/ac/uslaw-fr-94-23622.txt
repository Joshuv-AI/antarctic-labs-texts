DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-30, 171]

 

Aileen's, Incorporated, New York, New York; Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 1, 1994 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Aileen's, Incorporated, New York, New 
York.
    All workers from the subject firm are covered under amended 
certification (TA-W-29,494A). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose; and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 31st day of August 1994.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Service Group, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
