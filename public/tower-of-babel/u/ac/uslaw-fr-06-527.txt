Criminal Jurisdiction Over Civilians Employed by or Accompanying 
the Armed Forces Outside the United States, Service Members, and Former 
Service Members

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 22, 2005, The Department of Defense published 
another proposed rule in error. The rule should have been published as 
a final rule. This document withdraws that rule.

DATES: Effective Date: The proposed rule published at 70 FR 75998 is 
withdrawn, effective December 22, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: L.M. Bynum, 703-696-4970.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The proposed rule for public comments was 
previously published on February 2, 2004 (69 FR 4890). Comments 
received were considered.
