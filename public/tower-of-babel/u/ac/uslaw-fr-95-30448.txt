SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2811; Amendment #2]


U.S. Territory of the Virgin Islands; Declaration of Disaster 
Loan Area

    The above numbered Declaration is hereby amended, effective 
November 14, 1995 to extend the termination date for filing 
applications for physical damage until December 15, 1995. The 
termination date for economic injury remains the same, June 17, 1995, 
at the previously designated location.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: December 7, 1995.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
