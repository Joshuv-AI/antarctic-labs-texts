FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION

12 CFR Part 617

RIN 3052-AC45


Borrower Rights; Effective Interest Rates; Effective Date

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

ACTION: Notice of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Farm Credit Administration (FCA or Agency), through the 
FCA Board (Board), issued a final rule under part 617 on December 22, 
2009 (74 FR 67970) amending FCA's regulations to ensure that borrowers 
with loans directly tied to a widely publicized external index receive 
appropriate disclosure of interest rate changes in accordance with 
statutory requirements while allowing Farm Credit System institutions 
to provide the notices in a more efficient manner. In accordance with 
12 U.S.C. 2252, the effective date of the final rule is 30 days from 
the date of publication in the Federal Register during which either or 
both Houses of Congress are in session. Based on the records of the 
sessions of Congress, the effective date of the regulations is March 2, 
2010.

DATES: Effective Date: Under the authority of 12 U.S.C. 2252, the 
regulation amending 12 CFR part 617 published on December 22, 2009 (74 
FR 67970) is effective March 2, 2010.
