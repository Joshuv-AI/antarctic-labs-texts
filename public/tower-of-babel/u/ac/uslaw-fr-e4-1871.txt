DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. ER04-699-000]


Entergy Services, Inc.; Notice of Meeting

August 13, 2004.
    Notice is hereby given that Entergy Services, Inc. (Entergy) will 
hold a meeting to discuss the types of products that Entergy is 
currently interested in procuring in the weekly market and under the 
revised Weekly Procurement Process (WPP) proposed in the above 
captioned docket. The meeting will be held on August 26, 2004, at 10 
a.m. (EDT) in a room to be designated in the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426. Staff of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission is expected to 
participate. The meeting is open to the public.
    For further information, please contact Anna Cochrane at (202) 502-
6357; [email protected].

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
