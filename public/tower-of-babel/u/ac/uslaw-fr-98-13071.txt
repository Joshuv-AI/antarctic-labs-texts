DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-99-001]


Tennessee Gas Pipeline Company; Notice of Pro Forma Compliance 
Filing

May 12, 1998.
    Take notice that on May 7, 1998, Tennessee Gas Pipeline Company 
(Tennessee) tendered for filing the following pro forma tariff sheets:

Pro Forma Sheet No. 231
Pro Forma Sheet No. 232
Pro Forma Sheet No. 232A
Pro Forma Sheet No. 234
 Pro Forma Sheet No. 235

    Tennessee states that the proposed pro forma tariff sheets are 
filed in compliance with the Commission's

[[Page 27276]]

April 30, 1998 Notice in the above-referenced docket. Tennessee states 
that the pro forma tariff sheets incorporate several changes being 
proposed by Tennessee to address the parties' concerns raised in this 
docket and addressed at a technical conference on April 8, 1998. In 
accordance with the April 30, 1998 Notice, Tennessee requests that the 
Commission accept the tendered pro forma tariff sheets for filing.
    Pursuant to the notice issues April 30, 1998, initial comments are 
due May 13, 1998, and reply comments are due May 20, 1998.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
