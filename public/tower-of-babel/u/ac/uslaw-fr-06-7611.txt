September 21, 2006 Board of Directors Meeting

Time and Date: Thursday, September 21, 2006, 10 a.m. (Open Portion),

    4. Finance Project--Global.
    5. Approval of July 13, 2006 Minutes (Closed Portion).

    7. Reports.
