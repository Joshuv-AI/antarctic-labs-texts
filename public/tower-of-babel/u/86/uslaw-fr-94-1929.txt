DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
14 CFR Part 73

[Docket No. 25767; Special Federal Aviation Regulation (SFAR) No. 53-3]


Establishment of Warning Areas in the Airspace Overlying the 
Waters Between 3 and 12 Nautical Miles From the United States Coast

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects an error to the Final Rule, on 
``Establishment of Warning Areas in the Airspace Overlying the Waters 
Between 3 and 12 Nautical Miles From the United States Coast'', which 
was published on Wednesday, December 29, 1993 (58 FR 69128). The 
Amendment No. was omitted.

20591, telephone (202) 267-8783.

December 29, 1993, (58 FR 69128), in the Heading next to the ``Docket 
No. 25767'', please insert ``Amendment No. 73-7''.
