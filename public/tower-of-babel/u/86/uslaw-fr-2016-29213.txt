Violence Against Women Reauthorization Act of 2013: 
Implementation in HUD Housing Programs; Correction

AGENCY: Office of General Counsel, HUD.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On November 16, 2016, HUD published a final rule implementing 
in HUD's regulations the requirements of the 2013 reauthorization of 
the Violence Against Women Act (VAWA). After publication, HUD 
discovered an incorrect compliance date in the preamble and an 
incorrect paragraph designation in the regulatory text. The compliance 
date, with respect to completing an emergency transfer plan and 
providing emergency transfers, and associated recordkeeping and 
reporting requirements, was incorrectly listed as May 15, 2017, in the 
preamble. The regulatory text provided the correct date of June 14, 
2017. This document makes the necessary correction to the preamble to 
reflect the compliance date in the regulatory text of June 14, 2017 and 
the paragraph designations in the regulatory text.

DATES: This correction is effective December 16, 2016.
