COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS

 

Notice of Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' meeting scheduled for 21 April 1994 
has been cancelled. The next meeting is scheduled for 19 May 1994 at 10 
am in the Commission's offices in the Pension Building, suite 312, 
Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, NW., Washington, DC 20001 to discuss 
various projects affecting the appearance of Washington, DC, including 
buildings, memorials, parks, etc.; also matters of design referred by 
other agencies of the government.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, DC 6 April 1994.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
