DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of New Docket Prefix ``MO''

November 5, 2003.
    Notice is hereby given that a new docket prefix ``MO'' has been 
established to identify Issuances related to Market Oversight 
activities.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
