FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

July 14, 2000.

FCC TO HOLD OPEN COMMISSION MEETING FRIDAY, JULY 21, 2000

    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Friday, July 21, 2000, which is scheduled 
to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, S.W., 
Washington, D.C.
