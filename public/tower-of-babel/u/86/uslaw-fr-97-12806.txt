POSTAL SERVICE


Sunshine Act Meeting; Notification of Item Added to Meeting 
Agenda

DATE OF MEETING: May 5, 1997.

STATUS: Closed.

PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: 62 FR 20227, April 25, 1997.

CHANGE: At its meeting on May 5, 1997, the Board of Governors of the 
United States Postal Service voted unanimously to add an item to the 
agenda of its closed meeting held on that date: Consideration of the 
Report of the Capital Projects Committee on the Tray Management System.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Thomas J. Koerber, Secretary of 
the Board, U.S. Postal Service, 475 L'Enfant Plaza, SW., Washington, DC 
20260-1000. Telephone (202) 268-4800.
Thomas J. Koerber,
Secretary.
