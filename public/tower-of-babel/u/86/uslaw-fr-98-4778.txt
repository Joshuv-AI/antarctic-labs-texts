NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting; Public Hearing on Safety Oversight of the 
Union Pacific Railroad

    The National Transportation Safety Board will convene a public 
hearing beginning at 9:00 a.m. local time on Wednesday, March 18, 1998, 
at the Springfield Hilton Hotel, 6550 Loisdale, Road, Springfield, 
Virginia. For more information, contact James P. Dunn, NTSB Office of 
Railroad Safety at (202) 314-6430 or Shelly Hazle, NTSB Office of 
Public Affairs at (202) 314-6100.
