DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. CP17-41-000]


Eagle LNG Partners Jacksonville, LLC; Notice of Reopening of 
Comment Period

    On November 16, 2018, the Commission issued a notice setting 
January 7, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments on 
the Jacksonville Project draft environmental impact statement. Due to 
the funding lapse at certain federal agencies between December 22, 2018 
and January 25, 2019, the Commission is reopening the comment period 
until February 25, 2019.

    Dated: February 7, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
