FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as Ocean 
Freight Forwarder--Ocean Transportation Intermediaries pursuant to 
section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 as amended (46 U.S.C. app. 1718 
and 46 CFR 515).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarder, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573.

Global Management Distribution Corp., 6308 Wood Lake Road, Jupiter, FL 
33458, Officers: George M. Donaldson, Jr., President (Qualifying 
Individual), Mary Beth Donaldson, Vice President
Cosmo Ocean Freight, Ltd., 146-22 Guy R. Brewer Blvd., Jamaica, NY 
11434, Officers: Michael NG, President (Qualifying Individual), Patrick 
Chu, Vice President
Frontier Freight Forwarders, Inc., 706 Mission Street, Suite 900, San 
Francisco, CA 94103, Officers: Lynn C. Fritz, President, Linda K. 
Ellis, Asst. Vice President (Qualifying Individual)
Interworld Services, Inc., 17047 Erin Way Ct., Houston, TX 77095, 
Officers: Irene Sadkowski Cosme, President (Qualifying Individual), 
Armando Miralles, Vice President
