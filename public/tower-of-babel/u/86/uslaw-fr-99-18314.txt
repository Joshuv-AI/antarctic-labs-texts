Change in Report Series from Print Publication to CD-ROM and 
Internet Access

AGENCY: Census Bureau, Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of Publication Program Change.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Census Bureau will cease printed publication of the 
``Census Catalog and Guide'' with the 1998 edition. This publication's 
information, and additional data, will be available as the ``Product 
Catalog'' on the Internet at www.census.gov. Also, the information will 
be distributed annually on CD-ROM.

EFFECTIVE DATE: August 18, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barbara Aldrich, Marketing Services 
Office/Customer Services Center, U.S. Census Bureau, Washington, DC 
20233, telephone: 301-457-1225.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The ``Census Catalog and Guide'' is a 
comprehensive description of all data products issued by the Census 
Bureau. The catalog provides abstracts of CD-ROMs, publications, maps, 
computer tapes, diskettes, and items available via the Internet. These 
abstracts include the data time, the geographic scope, and the subject 
content, along with ordering information. For additional information 
about the catalog, please contact the official named above.

    Dated: July 14, 1999.
Kenneth Prewitt,
Director, Bureau of the Census.
