L and L Products, Inc., Romeo, MI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 25, 2009 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at L and L Products, Inc., Romeo, 
Michigan. The workers at the subject facility produce automotive 
sealants.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 28th day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
