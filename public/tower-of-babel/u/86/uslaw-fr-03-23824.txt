DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Acker Fire Salvage, Umpqua National Forest, Douglas County, OR

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 24, 2003, a Notice of Intent (NOI) to prepare an 
environmental impact statement (EIS) for the Acker Fire Salvage on the 
Tiller Ranger District of the Umpqua National Forest, was published in 
the Federal Register (68 FR 37451). Forest Service has decided to 
cancel the preparation of this EIS. The NOI is hereby rescinded.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Questions maybe addressed to Alan 
Baumann, Timber Management Assistant, Tiller Ranger District, 27812 
Tiller Trail Highway, Tiller, OR 97484, telephone: 541-825-3201.

    Dated: September 9, 2003.
James A. Caplan,
Forest Supervisor.
