DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[NAFTA-03730]


Timbergon, Redmond, Oregon; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 250 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 17, 2000 on behalf of workers at Timbergon, 
Redmond, Oregon.
    The petitioner requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 6th day of April 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
