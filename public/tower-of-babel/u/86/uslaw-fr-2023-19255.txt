DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Biomedical 
Library, Informatics and Data Science Review Committee, November 2-3, 
2023, which was published in the Federal Register on August 2, 2023, 88 
FR 147, Page 50884.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting times to 9:30 
a.m. to 5:30 p.m. on November 2nd and 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. on 
November 3rd. The meeting is closed to the public.
