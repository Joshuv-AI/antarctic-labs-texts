Announcement of the U.S. Geological Survey Science Strategy 
Planning Feedback Process

AGENCY: U.S. Geological Survey, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Feedback Process.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 34063]]

SUMMARY: The U.S. Geological Survey is creating 10-year strategies for 
each of its Mission Areas: Climate and Land Use Change, Core Science 
Systems, Ecosystems, Energy and Minerals, Environmental Health, Natural 
Hazards, and Water. This process involves gathering input from the 
public on draft strategy documents. Feedback can be offered at http://www.usgs.gov/start_with_science.

DATES: The comment period on questions and drafts closes at midnight on 
August 1, 2012.
