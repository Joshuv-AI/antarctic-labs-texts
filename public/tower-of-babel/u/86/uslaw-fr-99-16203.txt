SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The GLOBE (Global Learning and Observations to Benefit the 
Environment) Program is an international science and environment 
education program involving elementary and secondary students. Feedback 
from participating teachers and students is necessary to guide 
necessary program changes, to help the program meet its goals, and to 
aid the continued growth of the program.

II. Method of Collection

    Annual surveys of teachers and students involved in the GLOBE 
Program will be conducted through the World Wide Web, with hard copies 
of the survey instruments available as needed.
