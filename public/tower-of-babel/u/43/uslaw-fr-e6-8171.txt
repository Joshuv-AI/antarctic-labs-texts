FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 06-986; MB Docket No. 04-375; RM-11038]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Hillsborough and Louisburg, NC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of New Century Media Group, 
LLC, reallots Channel 273A from Louisburg, North Carolina to 
Hillsborough, North Carolina, and modifies the license of Station 
WKXU(FM), accordingly. The coordinates for Channel 273 at Hillsborough 
are 36-06-49 North Latitude and 79-00-20 West Longitude, with a site 
restriction of 9.51 kilometers (5.91 miles) northeast of the community.

DATES: Effective June 26, 2006.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Helen McLean, Media Bureau, (202) 418-
2738.
