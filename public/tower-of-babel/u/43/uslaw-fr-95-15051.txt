Radio Broadcasting Services; Ketchikan, AK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 260A to Ketchikan, Alaska, as 
that community's third local FM service, in response to a petition for 
rule making filed on behalf of TLP Communications, Inc. See 59 FR 
62390, December 5, 1994. Coordinates used for Channel 260A at Ketchikan 
are 55-20-30 and 131-38-48. With this action, the proceeding is 
terminated.

DATES: Effective July 31, 1995. The window period for filing 
applications on Channel 260A at Ketchikan, Alaska, will open on July 
31, 1995, and close on August 31, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180. Questions related to the window application filing process 
for Channel 260A at Ketchikan, Alaska, should be addressed to the Audio 
Services Division, FM Branch, (202) 418-2700.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Report and Order, MM Docket No. 94-138, adopted June 7, 1995, and 
released June 14, 1995. The full text of this Commission decision is
