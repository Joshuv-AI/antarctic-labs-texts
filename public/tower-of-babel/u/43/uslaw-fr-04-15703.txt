DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Natural Resources Conservation Service


Task Force on Agricultural Air Quality

AGENCY: Natural Resources Conservation Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Agricultural Air Quality Task Force (AAQTF) will meet to 
continue discussions on critical air quality issues in relation to 
agriculture. Special emphasis will be placed on obtaining a greater 
understanding about the relationship between agricultural production 
and air quality. The meeting is open to the public and a draft agenda 
of the meeting is attached.

DATES: The meeting will convene on Wednesday, July 21, 2004, at 8 a.m., 
and continue until 5 p.m.; resume Thursday, July 22, 2004, from 8 a.m. 
to 4 p.m. Individuals with written materials, and those who have 
requests to make oral presentations, should contact the Natural 
Resources Conservation Service (NRCS), at the address below on or 
before July 12, 2004.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Best Western Coeur d'Alene

Procedural

    This meeting is open to the public. At the discretion of the Chair, 
members of the public may give oral presentations during the meeting. 
Persons wishing to make oral presentations should notify Mr. Graves no 
later than July 12, 2004. If a person submitting material would like a 
copy distributed to each member of the committee in advance of the 
meeting, that person should submit 30 copies to Mr. Graves no later 
than July 12, 2004.
