DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. ID-2589-001]


Brian R. Foster; Notice of Filing

March 21, 1996.
    Take notice that on January 3, 1996, Brian R. Foster (Applicant) 
tendered for filing an application under Section 305(b) of the Federal 
Power Act to hold the following positions: Director, Savannah Electric 
and Power Company; Executive Vice President, NationsBank of Georgia, 
National Association.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before April 1, 1996. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
