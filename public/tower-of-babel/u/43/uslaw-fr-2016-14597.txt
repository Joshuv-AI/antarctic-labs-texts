DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 4283-005]


Fred N. Sutter, Jr.; Shamrock Utilities, LLC; Notice of Transfer 
of Exemption

    1. By letter filed May 16, 2016, Fred N. Sutter, Jr. informed the 
Commission that the exemption from licensing for the Sutter's Mill 
Project No. 4283, originally issued March 1, 1982 \1\ has been 
transferred to Shamrock Utilities, LLC. The project is located on 
Millseat Creek in Shasta County, California. The transfer of an 
exemption does not require Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 18 FERC ] 62,359 (1982), Order Granting Exemption from 
Licensing of a Small Hydroelectric Project of 5 Megawatts or Less.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Shamrock Utilities, LLC is now the exemptee of the Sutter's Mill 
Project, No. 4283. All correspondence should be forwarded to: Mr. Rocky 
Ungaro, Shamrock Utilities, LLC, P.O. Box 859, Palo Cedro, CA 96073.

    Dated: June 15, 2016.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
