SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration National Advisory Council, 
will hold a public meeting October 1-3, 2000 located at the Wyndham 
Miami Biscayne Bay Hotel, 1601 Biscayne Boulevard Miami, Florida to 
discuss such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. 
Small Business Administration, or others present. For further 
information call Bettie Baca, Counselor to the Administrator/Public 
Liaison, (202) 205-2469.

Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administrator/Public Liaison.
