DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. RP95-326-000 and RP95-242-000]


Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America; Notice of Technical 
Conference

September 25, 1995.
    Take notice that the technical conference in these proceedings 
which was convened July 13 and August 3, 1995, will continue on Friday, 
October 6, 1995, at 10:00 a.m., in a room to be designated at the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 810 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426. As established at the August 3, 1995 conference, 
the discussion at the October 6 conference will be limited to the 
remaining fuel issues. All interested persons and staff are permitted 
to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
