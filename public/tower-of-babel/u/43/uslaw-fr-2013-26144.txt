DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, October 01, 2013, 08:00 a.m. 
to October 02, 2013, 05:00 p.m., Bethesda Marriott Hotel, 5151 Pooks 
Hill Road, Bethesda, MD 20812, which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 16, 2013, 78FR50065.
    Due to the absence of either an FY 2014 appropriation or Continuing 
Resolution for the Department of Health and Human Services, the meeting 
is rescheduled for November 12-13, 2013. The meeting times and location 
remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 29, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
