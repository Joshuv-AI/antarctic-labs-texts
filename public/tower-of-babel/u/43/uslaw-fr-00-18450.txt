DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Defense Partnership Council Meeting

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION:  Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 21, 2000, 65 FR 38518, the Department of Defense 
published a notice to announce a meeting of the Defense Partnership 
Council to be held July 26, 2000.
    This notice is to announce that the meeting is cancelled due to 
conflicts in members' schedules.
