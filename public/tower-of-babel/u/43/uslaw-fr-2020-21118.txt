DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Office of The Director, National Institutes of Health; 
Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Office of The 
Director, National Institutes of Health, October 9, 2020, 3:00 p.m. to 
October 9, 2020, 4:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 
One, One Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the 
Federal Register on August 26, 2020, 85 FR 52614.
    This meeting is being cancelled due to scheduling difficulties and 
the presentation will be rescheduled for the ACD in December 2020.

    Dated: September 21, 2020.
Natasha M. Copeland,
Deputy Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
