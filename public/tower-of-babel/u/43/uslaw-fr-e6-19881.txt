DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Notice of Proposed Information Collection Requests

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On November 20, 2006, the Department of Education published a 
notice in the Federal Register (Page 67115, Column 3) for the 
information collection, ``Mathematics and Science Partnerships Program: 
Annual Performance Report''. This notice hereby corrects the burden 
hours to 8,400. The IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information 
Management Services, Office of Management, hereby issues a correction 
notice as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

    Dated: November 20, 2006.
Angela C. Arrington,
IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management Services, 
Office of Management.
