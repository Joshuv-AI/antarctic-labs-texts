DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commissioners and Staff Attendance at FERC Leadership 
Development Program Graduation/Induction Ceremony

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC or Commission) 
hereby gives notice that members of the Commission and/or Commission 
staff may attend the following event:

FERC Leadership Development Program Graduation/Induction Ceremony: 888 
First Street NE., Washington, DC 20426.
May 8, 2012 (10:00 a.m.-11:00 a.m.).

    The event will introduce and welcome 17 employees selected for the 
2012 Leadership Development Program and graduate 15 employees from the 
2011 program.

    Dated: April 30, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
