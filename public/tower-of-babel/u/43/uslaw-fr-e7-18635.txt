DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Air University Board of Visitors; Notice of Meeting

ACTION: Notice of Meeting of Air University Board of Visitors.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The purpose of the meeting is to give the board an opportunity 
to review Air University educational programs and to present a report 
of their findings and recommendations concerning these programs.

DATES: The Air University Board of Visitors will hold an open meeting 
on November 5-6, 2007, 8-4:30 CDT, Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Dorothy Reed, Chief of Academic 
Affairs, Air University Headquarters, Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama 
36112-6335, at (334) 953-5159.

Bao-Anh Trinh,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
