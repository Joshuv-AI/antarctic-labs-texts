FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Open Commission Meeting, Thursday, April 
28, 2005

April 21, 2005.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Thursday, April 28, 2005, which is 
scheduled to commence at in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, SW., 
Washington, DC.

                                the Satellite Home Viewer Extension and

                                satellite.
