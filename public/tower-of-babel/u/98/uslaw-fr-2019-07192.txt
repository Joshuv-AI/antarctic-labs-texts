Extension of Comment Period for the Safety Zone; Gastineau 
Channel, Juneau, AK

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DHS.

ACTION: Extension of comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Coast Guard is extending the comment period 
for the notice of proposed rulemaking

[[Page 14664]]

for a safety zone which appeared in the Federal Register on April 2, 
2019 (33 CFR 165) for the proposed modification of an existing safety 
zone for certain waters of the Gastineau Channel in Juneau, AK. The 
notice of proposed rulemaking is to expand an existing safety zone for 
certain waters of the Gastineau Channel in order to improve safety of 
large passenger vessels anchoring within the safety zone. The comment 
period has been extended an additional 30 days to May 13, 2019.

DATES: Comments and related material must be submitted to the online 
docket at http://www.regulations.gov or reach the Docket Management 
Facility on or before May 13, 2019.

ADDRESSES: You may submit comments identified by docket number USCG-
2018-1057 using the Federal eRulemaking Portal at https://www.regulations.gov.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: If you have questions on this notice 
please contact LT Kristi Sloane, Sector Juneau, Waterways Management 
Division, U.S. Coast Guard, at telephone number 907-463-2846 or email 
to [email protected].
