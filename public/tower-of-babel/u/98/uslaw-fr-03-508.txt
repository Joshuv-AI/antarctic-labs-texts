SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3473]


State of Alaska (Amendment 2)

    In accordance with information received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency dated December 20, 2002, the above-numbered 
declaration is hereby amended to establish the incident period as 
beginning on October 23, 2002 and continuing through December 20, 2002.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is February 3, 2003, and for 
economic injury the deadline is September 4, 2003.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: December 31, 2002.
S. George Camp,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
