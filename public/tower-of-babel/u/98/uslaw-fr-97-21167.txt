NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice (97-108)]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that UE Systems, Inc., Elmsford, New 
York, has applied for an exclusive license for the patent application 
entitled ``Ultrasonic Imaging System,'' KSC-11909, which is assigned to 
the United States of America as represented by the Administrator of the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections to 
the prospective grant of a license to UE Systems, Inc. should be sent 
to Beth Vrioni, John F. Kennedy Space Center, Mail Code DE-TPO, Kennedy 
Space Center, FL 32899.

DATES: Responses to this notice must be received by October 10, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Beth Vrioni at (407) 867-2544.

    Dated: August 4, 1997.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
