FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-1261, MM Docket No. 01-50, RM-10059]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Little Rock, AR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Arkansas Educational 
Television Commission, licensee of noncommercial educational station 
KETS(TV), substitutes DTV channel *5 for DTV channel *47 at Little 
Rock, Arkansas. See 66 FR 12750, February 28, 2001. DTV channel *5 can 
be allotted to Little Rock in compliance with the principle community 
coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 
(34-28-23 N. and 92-12-11 W.) with a power of 2.1, HAAT of 540.1 meters 
and with a DTV service population of 848 thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective July 9, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
