OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission For OMB Review; Request For Review of a Revised 
Information Collection: (OMB Control No. 3206-0136; Standard Form 2823)

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget (OMB) a request for review of a revised information 
collection. This information collection, ``Designation of Beneficiary: 
Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance'' (OMB Control No. 3206-0136; 
Standard Form 2823), is used by any Federal employee or retiree covered 
by the Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Program to instruct the 
Office of Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance how to distribute the 
proceeds of his or her life insurance when the statutory order of 
precedence does not meet his or her needs.
    We estimate 47,000 SF 2823 forms are completed annually by 
annuitants and 1,000 forms are completed by assignees. Each form takes 
approximately 15 minutes to complete. The annual estimated burden is 
12,000 hours.
