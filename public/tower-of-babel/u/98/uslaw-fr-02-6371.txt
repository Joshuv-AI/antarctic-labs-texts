47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-486; MM Docket No. 01-137; RM-10156]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Altus, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Katherine Pyeatt, dismisses 
the petition proposing the allotment of Channel 295C2 at Altus, 
Oklahoma, as the community's third local FM transmission service. See 
66 FR 35768, July 9, 2001. A showing of continuing interest is required 
before a channel will be allotted. It is the Commission's policy to 
refrain from making an allotment to a community absent an expression of 
interest.
