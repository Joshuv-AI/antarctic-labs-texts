The Applicable Statute:

    Unless otherwise indicated, all citations to the statute are 
references to the provisions effective January 1, 1995, the effective 
date of the amendments made to the Tariff Act of 1930 (the Act) by the 
Uruguay Round Agreements Act (URAA).

Postponement of Preliminary Determinations

    On October 16, 1995, Rockwell International Corporation, the 
petitioner, requested that the Department postpone the preliminary 
determinations of these investigations by 50 days. Pursuant to section 
773 (c)(1)(A) of the Act, we are postponing the date of the preliminary 
determinations as to whether sales of large newspaper printing presses 
from Germany and Japan have been made at less than fair value until no 
later than January 26, 1996.
    This notice is published pursuant to section 733(c)(2) of the Act.
