U.S. Bronze Foundry & Machine Incorporated, Meadville, 
Pennsylvania; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 29, 2001, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at U.S. Bronze Foundry & Machine 
Inc., Meadville, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 12th day of March, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
