DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL05-2-000]


Public Service Company of New Mexico; Notice of Initiation of 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

December 21, 2004.
    On December 20, 2004, the Commission issued an order in the above-
docketed proceeding. The Commission's order institutes a proceeding in 
Docket No. EL05-2-000 under section 206 of the Federal Power Act with 
respect to the justness and reasonableness of Public Service Company of 
New Mexico's market-based rates.
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL05-2-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the Federal Power Act will be 60 days 
following publication of this notice in the Federal Register.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
