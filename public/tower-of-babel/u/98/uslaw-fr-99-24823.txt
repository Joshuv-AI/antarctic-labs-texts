SUMMARY: This notice modifies the existing Recreation and Public 
Purposes Classification published 62 FR 45267, August 26, 1997.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 24, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Peter Graves, Ridgecrest Field Office, 
BLM, 300 So. Richmond Road, Ridgecrest, CA 93555, (760) 384-5429.
