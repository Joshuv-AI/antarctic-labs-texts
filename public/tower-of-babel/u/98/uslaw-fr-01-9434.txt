DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 17-19 April 2001.
    Time of Meeting: 0830-1545, 17 April 2001, 0830-1545, 18 April 
2001.
    Place: Presidential Towers, Arlington, VA.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) panel will conduct an Ad 
Hoc Study on ``Adapting Future Wireless Communication'' to examine 
potential future commercial wireless capabilities and recommend 
which capabilities may have applicability for the Objective Force. 
The meetings will be open to the public. Any interested person may 
attend, appear before, or file statements with the committee at the 
time and in the manner permitted by the committee. For further 
information, please contact Mr. Jeff Ozimek, Army Science Board 
Staff Assistant, 732-532-5496 or Ms. Lisa Calabrese, Army Science 
Board Staff Assistant, 732-427-4646.

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
