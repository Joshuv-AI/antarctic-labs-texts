SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    This survey is part of a subsistence Pacific halibut program that 
is intended to allow qualified persons to practice the long-term 
customary and traditional harvest of Pacific halibut for food in a non-
commercial manner. The annual survey would be voluntarily submitted to 
NOAA to report Pacific halibut catch for the prior year. The catch 
reports will be used to help track the impacts of the program.

II. Method of Collection

    The annual survey may be submitted by mail, FAX, e-mail or by 
individual post-season interviews.

    Comments are invited on: (a) whether the proposed collection of

(c) ways to enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of the

automated collection techniques or other forms of information 
technology.
    Comments submitted in response to this notice will be summarized
