SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3469]


State of Alaska

    As a result of the President's major disaster declaration for 
Public Assistance on November 8, 2002, and subsequent amendment adding 
Individual Assistance on November 18, 2002, I find that the Alaska 
Gateway Regional Educational Attendance Area (REAA) and the Copper 
River Regional Educational Attendance Area (REAA) in the State of 
Alaska constitute a disaster area due to damages caused by an 
earthquake occurring on November 3, 2002 and continuing through 
November 10, 2002. Applications for loans for physical damage as a 
result of this disaster may be filed until the close of business on 
January 17, 2003 and for economic injury until the close of business on 
August 18, 2003 at the address listed below or other locally announced 
locations: Small Business Administration, Disaster Area 4 Office, P.O. 
Box 13795, Sacramento, CA 95853-4795.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the following areas and jurisdictions may be 
filed until the specified date at the above location: Chugach REAA, 
Delta/Greely REAA, Yukon Flats REAA, City & Borough of Yakutat, 
Fairbanks North Star Borough, and Matanuska--Susitna Borough in the 
State of Alaska. The interest rates are:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
