Buccaneer Gas Pipeline Company, L.L.C.; Notice of Site Visit

April 18, 1999.
    On April 24 through April 27, 2000, the Office of Energy Projects 
staff will conduct a site visit of proposed facilities and alternatives 
with representatives of Buccaneer Gas Pipeline Company, of the 
Buccaneer Gas Pipeline Project In Pasco, Osceola, Polk, Hardee, Lake, 
and Brevard, Counties, Florida.
    All interested parties may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.

David P. Boerger,
Secretary.
