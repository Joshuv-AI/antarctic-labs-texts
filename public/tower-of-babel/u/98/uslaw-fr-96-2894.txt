DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-31,748]


Union Supply Company Midland, TX; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 18, 1995 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Union Supply Company, Midland, 
Texas.
    The Petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose; and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 19th day of January, 1996.
Russell T. Kile,
