U.S. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

 

Notification of Extension of Filing Deadline

    This is to give notice that the deadline for filing applications 
for physical disaster loans as a result of the Midwest floods has been 
extended to February 15, 1994 for three of the nine states that had 
counties Presidentially declared as disaster areas. Those states and 
their respective declaration numbers are:

Illinois--2662
Iowa--2661
Missouri--2663

    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for economic injury loans will remain as 
previously published for each state.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: January 19, 1994.
Alfred E. Judd,
Acting Assistant Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
