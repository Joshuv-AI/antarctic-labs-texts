Texas Gas Transmission, LLC; Notice of Technical Conference

December 6, 2006.
    Take notice that the Commission will convene a technical conference 
on Tuesday, January 9, 2007, at 10 a.m., in a room to be designated at 
the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, NE., Washington DC 20426.
    The technical conference will discuss the issues raised by Texas 
Gas Transmission, LLC's September 11, 2006 annual filing, as amended 
September 12, 2006, to adjust its Effective Fuel Retention Percentages 
pursuant to section 16 of the General Terms and Conditions of its 
tariff. The order establishing a technical conference was issued on 
October 31, 2006.\1\
