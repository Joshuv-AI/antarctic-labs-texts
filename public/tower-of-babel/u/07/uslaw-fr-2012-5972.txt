PostRock KPC Pipeline, LLC; Notice of Technical Conference

    Take notice that the Commission will convene a technical conference 
on Tuesday, March 27, 2012, at 10:30 a.m. (EDT), in a room to be 
designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street NE., Washington DC 20426.
    The technical conference will address all aspects of PostRock KPC 
Pipeline, LLC's (KPC) proposed tariff revisions to be part of its FERC 
Gas Tariff, Third Revised Volume No. 1. The proposed revisions would 
allow KPC to consider blending and pairing arrangements when deciding 
whether to exercise discretion under the tariff to accept receipts that 
fail to meet applicable gas quality specifications, as discussed in the 
Commission's Order issued on February 29, 2012.\1\
