HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the Air Force Scientific Advisory Board. The 
purpose of the meeting is to brief the Secretary of the Air Force. This 
meeting will be closed to the public.

ADDRESSES: Pentagon, Washington DC.

Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 5D982,
