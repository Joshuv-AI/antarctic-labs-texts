FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Transporation Intermediary License; Correction

    In the Federal Register Notice listing applicants for licenses as 
Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier and Ocean Freight Forwarder--Ocean 
Transportation Intermediary published October 24, 2001 (66 FR 53795) 
the reference to Direct Shipping Line is corrected to read: ``Direct 
Shipping Corp.''

    Dated: December 14, 2001.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
