Radio Broadcasting Services; Fruit Cove and St. Augustine, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 70 FR 48361 
(August 17, 2005), this document reallots Channel 231C3 from St. 
Augustine, Florida to Fruit Cove, Florida, and modifies the license of 
Station WSOS-FM, accordingly. The coordinates for Channel 231C3 at 
Fruit Cove are 30-01-27 North Latitude and 81-36-19 West Longitude, 
with a site restriction of 10.2 kilometers (6.4 miles) south of the 
community.

DATES: Effective January 30, 2006.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Helen McLean, Media Bureau, (202) 418-
2738.
