DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-59,601]


Hospira; Ashland, OH; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 21, 2006 in response to a petition filed by 
United Steelworkers of America, Local 196L, on behalf of workers at 
Hospira, Ashland, Ohio (TA-W-59,601).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 27th day of June, 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
