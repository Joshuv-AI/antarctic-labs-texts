Airworthiness Directives; Rolls-Royce plc RB211-22B, RB211-524,

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 58258]]

SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to Airworthiness Directive 
(AD) 2004-19-04. That AD applies to Rolls-Royce plc (RR) RB211-22B, 
RB211-524, and RB211-535 series turbofan engines. That AD was published 
in the Federal Register on September 22, 2004 (69 FR 56683). In the 
amendatory language, under Sec.  39.13 [Amended], the amendment number 
for the AD was inadvertently omitted. This document corrects that 
omission. In all other respects, the original document remains the 
same.

EFFECTIVE DATE: Effective September 30, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher Spinney, Aerospace 
Engineer, Engine Certification Office, FAA, Engine and Propeller

engines, was published in the Federal Register on September 22, 2004 
(69 FR 56683). The following correction is needed:
