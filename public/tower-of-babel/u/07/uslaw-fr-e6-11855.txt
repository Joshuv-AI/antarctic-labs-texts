Rose Art Industries Incorporated; Livingston, NJ; Rose Art 
Industries, LLC; Wood-Ridge, NJ; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 12, 2006 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Rose Art Industries 
Incorporated in Livingston and Wood-Ridge, New Jersey.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 21st day of June, 2006.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
