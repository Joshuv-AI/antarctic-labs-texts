SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The Cooperative Tagging Center attempts to determine the migratory 
patterns and other biological information about billfish, tunas, and 
other game fish. Volunteer anglers are asked to tag and release their 
catch. Anglers provide information about the fish tagged and the 
location of tagging.

II. Method of Collection

    Respondents fill out and mail in a Fish Tagging Report card when 
they tag a fish.
