Lewistown District; Resource Advisory Council Meeting

AGENCY: Lewistown District Office, Bureau of Land Management, DOI.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Lewistown District Resource Advisory Council will meet 
February 3 and 4, 1998, at the Montana Fish, Wildlife and Parks Region 
4 Headquarters Office at 4600 Giant Spring Road in Great Falls, 
Montana.
    The February 3, portion of the session will begin at 7:45 a.m. with 
opening comments. The council will consider revising their mission 
statement, then discuss/consider the status of the Devil's Kitchen Work 
Group; the Eye of the Needle; Oil and Gas development along the Rocky 
Mountain Front; conservation easements; and the Two Crow land exchange 
through the rest of the day.
    There will also be a public comment period at 11:30 am on February 
3.
    The February 4, portion of the session will begin at 8:00 am and 
the council will discuss/consider off-road vehicle regulation 
enforcement; the BLM's re-organization; fire management proposals; and 
the status of the Sweet Grass Hills/Little Rockies land exchange. After 
lunch, the council will tour the River's Edge Trail along the Missouri 
River, then close this meeting.

DATES: February 3 and 4, 1998.

LOCATION: Montana Fish, Wildlife and Parks Region 4 Headquarters 
Office, 4600 Giant Spring Road in Great Falls, Montana.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: District Manager, Lewistown District 
Office, Bureau of Land Management, Box 1160, Airport Road, Lewistown, 
MT 59457.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting is open to the public and there 
will be a public comment period as detailed above.

    Dated: December 29, 1997.
B. Gene Miller,
Associate District Manager.
