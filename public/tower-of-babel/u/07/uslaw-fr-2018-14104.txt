Agency information Collection Activity: Application by Insured

AGENCY: Veterans Benefits Administration, Department of Veterans 
Affairs.

ACTION: Notice to withdraw.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 22, 2018, the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) 
erroneously posted a consecutive 30-day Federal Register Notice 
(Application by Insured Terminally Ill Person for Accelerated Benefits) 
Document Number: 2018-13397; OMB control number: 2900-0618.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Cynthia Harvey-Pryor, Enterprise 
Records Service (005R1B), Department of Veterans Affairs, 810 Vermont 
Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20420, at 202-461-5870.

Correction

    VA wishes to inform the public that it is withdrawing FR Document 
Number: 2018-13397, 83 FR 29155. This was a duplicate 30-day Public 
Notice published in error. The correct and initial 30-day Notice posted 
May 24, 2018, Volume 83, No. 101, page 24163, FR Document Number: 2018-
11131, which has since concluded.

    Dated: June 26, 2018.

    By direction of the Secretary.
Cynthia D. Harvey-Pryor,
