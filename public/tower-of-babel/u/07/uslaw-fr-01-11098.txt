Raider Apparel Inc., Alma, GA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 27, 2000 in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Raider Apparel Inc., Alma, Georgia.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-36,121). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of April, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division Trade Adjustment Assistance.
