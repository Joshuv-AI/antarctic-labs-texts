DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Notice of Intent To Rule on Request To Dispose 5.3 and .8 Acres 
of Land at Sanford Seacoast Regional Airport, Sanford, ME

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Request for public comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is being given that the FAA is considering a request 
from the Town of Sanford ME, to dispose of 5.3 and .8 acres of land at 
Sanford Seacoast Regional Airport, Sanford, ME. The land is no longer 
needed for aviation purposes and may be disposed of by the airport. 
These parcels are located in non-aeronautical use area and will have no 
effect on any existing or future aviation development needs. The 
proceeds from the sale of the two properties will be placed in the 
airports operating and maintenance account.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before June 16, 2021.
