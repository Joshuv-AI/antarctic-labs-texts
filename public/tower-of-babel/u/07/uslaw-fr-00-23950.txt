CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE: 10 a.m., Thursday, September 21, 2000.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the Public.

Matter to be Considered: Bed Rails: The Commission will consider 
options concerning whether the Commission should issue an advance 
notice of proposed rulemaking (ANPR) addressing a risk of injury/death 
associated with certain portable bed rails.
    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207, (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: September 13, 2000.
Sadye E. Dunn,
Secretary.
