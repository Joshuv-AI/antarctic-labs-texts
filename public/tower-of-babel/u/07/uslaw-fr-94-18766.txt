INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION

[Finance Docket No. 29653 (Sub-No. 6)]

 

The Baltimore and Ohio Railroad Company, et al.; Pooling of Car 
Service Regarding Multi-Level Cars

AGENCY: Interstate Commerce Commission.

ACTION: Notice of application.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The signatory railroads1 to an agreement for the pooling 
of car service with respect to multi-level cars used to transport motor 
vehicles (multi-level cars) and boxcars used to transport automobile 
parts (auto-parts boxcars) filed an application seeking approval of an 
amendment to their existing agreement. The amendment involves the 
authority of the Steering Committee or Executive Committee, established 
pursuant to the agreement, to issue recommendations on the optimum pool 
size and the number of cars each carrier should contribute to the pool. 
A proceeding is instituted to consider the subject application.

    \1\ The Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway Company; 
Burlington Northern Railroad Company; Canadian Pacific Limited; 
Chicago and North Western Transportation Company; CN North America 
Systems; Consolidated Rail Corporation; CSX Transportation 
Incorporated; Florida East Coast Railway Company; Illinois Central 
Railroad Company; Missouri Pacific Railroad Company; New York, 
Susquehanna and Western Railway Corporation; Norfolk Southern 
Corporation; Soo Line Railroad Company; Southern Pacific 
Transportation Company; and Union Pacific Railroad Company.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
