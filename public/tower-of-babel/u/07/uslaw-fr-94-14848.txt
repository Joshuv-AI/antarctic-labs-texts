DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
 

Notice of Disposition of Application for Duty-Free Entry of 
Scientific Instrument

    The applicant requested withdrawal of its application since no duty 
was levied on the entry covered by Docket Number 94-030 (See notice at 
59 FR 18994, April 21, 1994). Pursuant to Sec. 301.5(g) of the 
regulations, we hereby discontinue processing this application.
Pamela Woods,
Acting Director, Statutory Import Programs Staff
