DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 11397]


Determination Pursuant to Section 451 of the Foreign Assistance 
Act for the Use of Funds To Support South Sudan

    Pursuant to section 451 of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (the 
``Act'') (22 U.S.C. 2261), section 1-100(a)(1) of Executive Order 12163 
and Delegation of Authority 245-2, I hereby authorize, notwithstanding 
any other provision of law, the use of up to $3,500,000 made available 
to carry out provisions of the Act (other than the provisions of 
chapter 1 of part I of the Act) to provide assistance authorized by 
part I of the Act to support countries that participate in the 
reconstituted Joint Monitoring and Evaluation Commission (RJMEC) and 
the Ceasefire and Transitional Security Arrangements Monitoring and 
Verification Mechanism (CTSAMVM) in South Sudan, as well as support to 
improve oversight of private security contractors working with the 
United Nations Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS).
    This Determination and the accompanying Memorandum of Justification 
shall be promptly reported to the Congress. This Determination shall be 
published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: March 15, 2021.
Daniel B. Smith,
Acting Deputy Secretary of State.
