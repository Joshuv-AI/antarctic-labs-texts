SUMMARY: This document contains a Notice of Typographical Correction 
with respect to a Notice of Grant of Individual Exemptions published on 
April 7, 1998 at 63 FR 17034 and 17035 (the Prior Notice).

Correction

    The Prior Notice identified Exemption Application No. D-10523, 
Overland, Ordal, Thorson & Fennell Pulmonary Consultants, P.C., as 
Prohibited Transaction Exemption 98-15. The correct Prohibited 
Transaction number is 98-16.
