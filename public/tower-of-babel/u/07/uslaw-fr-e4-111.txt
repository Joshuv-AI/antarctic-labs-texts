DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

High Island Offshore System, LLC; Notice of Technical Conference

January 16, 2004.
    In its Order issued December 24, 2003,\1\ the Commission directed 
that a technical conference be held to investigate High Island Offshore 
System's proposal to implement a Natural Gas Liquids Bank as part of 
its tariff.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ High Island Offshore System, L.L.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Take notice that a technical conference will be held on Tuesday, 
February 3, 2004, at 10 a.m., in a room to be designated at the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426.
    All interested parties and staff are permitted to attend.

Linda Mitry,
Acting Secretary.
