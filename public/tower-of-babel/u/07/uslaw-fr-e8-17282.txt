DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of FERC Staff Attendance at Southwest Power Pool 
Independent Coordinator of Transmission (ICT) Transmission Summit 
Meeting

July 22, 2008.

members of its staff may attend the meeting noted below. Their 
attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach efforts.

ICT Transmission Summit

Canal Street,New Orleans, LA 70130.
