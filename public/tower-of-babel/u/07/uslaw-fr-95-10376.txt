DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Cancer Institute; Notice of Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the meeting of the 
Acrylonitrile Study Advisory Panel, National Cancer Institute, May 3, 
1995, 6130 Executive Boulevard, Room 418, Rockville, Maryland, which 
was published in the Federal Register on March 17 (60 FR 14439).
    The meeting is being canceled because current pending issues were 
resolved at the March meeting of this Panel and it was decided an 
additional meeting at this time is not necessary.

    Dated: April 20, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
