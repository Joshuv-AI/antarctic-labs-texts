Copiah County Storage Company; Notice of Route and Site Review

January 3, 2002.
    On January 16, 2002, the staff of the Office of Energy Projects 
(OEP) will conduct a site review of the proposed Copiah Storage 
Project. The Copiah Storage Project facilities are proposed for 
construction by Copiah County Storage Company (Copiah). The proposed 
compressor site and natural gas storage cavern site, located in Copiah 
County, Mississippi, will be reviewed on January 16, 2002. 
Representatives of Copiah will accompany the OEP staff.
    Anyone interested in attending the route and site review or 
obtaining further information may contact the Commission's Office of 
External Affairs at (202) 208-1088. Attendees must provide their own 
transportation.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
