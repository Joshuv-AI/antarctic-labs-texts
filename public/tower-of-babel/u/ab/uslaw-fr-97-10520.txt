Minnesota; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
State of Minnesota, (FEMA-1175-DR), dated April 8, 1997, and related 
determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 15, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Magda Ruiz, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of Minnesota, is hereby amended to include the following areas among 
those areas determined to have been adversely affected by the 
catastrophe declared a major disaster by the President in his 
declaration of April 8, 1997:

    The counties of Aitkin, Anoka, Becker, Blue Earth, Carver, 
Dakota, Goodhue, Grant, Hennepin, Houston, Kandiyohi, Lake of the 
Woods, Le Sueur, Lincoln, Mahnomen, Morrison, Nicollet, Ramsey, 
Redwood, Renville, Scott, Sibley, Stevens, Wabasha, and Winona for 
Individual Assistance, Categories A and B under the Public 
Assistance program and Hazard Mitigation.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance)
Dennis H. Kwiatkowski,
Deputy Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
