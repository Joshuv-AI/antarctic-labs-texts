47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 93-66; RM-8192]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Huntsville and Willis, Texas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of New Wavo Communications 
Group, Inc., reallocates Channel 279C3 from Huntsville to Willis, 
Texas, and modifies Station KVST(FM)'s license to specify Willis as its 
community of license. See 58 FR 17818, April 4, 1993. Channel 279C3 can 
be allocated in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements with a site restriction of 5.3 kilometers (3.3 
miles) northwest to accommodate New Wavo's desired site. The 
coordinates for Channel 279C3 at Willis are 30-26-55 and 95-31-48.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 12, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau,
