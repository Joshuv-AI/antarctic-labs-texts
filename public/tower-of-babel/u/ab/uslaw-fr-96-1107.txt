Light Truck Average Fuel Economy Standard, Model Year 1998

AGENCY: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On January 3, 1996, NHTSA published a notice of proposed 
rulemaking (61 FR 145) to establish a corporate average fuel economy 
standard for light trucks for model year 1998. The comment closing date 
was given as February 20, 1996, under the ``Dates'' heading on page 145 
but as March 4, 1996, under the ``Comments'' section on page 155. The 
comment closing date on page 155 should be February 20, 1996.

    Issued on: January 18, 1996.
Barry Felrice,
Associate Administrator for Safety Performance Standards.
