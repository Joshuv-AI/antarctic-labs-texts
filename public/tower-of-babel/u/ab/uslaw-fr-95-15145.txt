FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-125, RM-8534; RM-8575]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Fredericksburg, Helotes, 
Castroville, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial of.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the petition filed by October 
Communications Group, Inc., requesting the reallotment of Channel 266C 
from Fredericksburg, TX, to either Helotes or Castroville, TX, as their 
first local aural transmission service, and the modification of Station 
KONO-FM's license accordingly.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

Report and Order, MM Docket No. 94-125, adopted June 8, 1995, and 
released June 16, 1995. The full text of this Commission decision is
