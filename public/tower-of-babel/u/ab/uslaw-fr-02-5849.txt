FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-500, MM Docket No. 01-222, RM-10240]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Charleston, SC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of WCIV, LLC, licensee of 
station WCIV(TV), NTSC channel 4, Charleston, South Carolina, 
substitutes DTV channel 34 for DTV channel 53. See 66 FR 47903, August 
14, 2001. DTV channel 34 can be allotted to Charleston in compliance 
with the principle community coverage requirements of section 73.625(a) 
at reference coordinates 32-55-28 N. and 79-41-58 W. with a power of 
340, HAAT of 597 meters and with a DTV service population of 774 
thousand. With is action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective April 22, 2002.
