DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Joint Advisory Committee on Nuclear Weapons Surety

ACTION: Notice of advisory committee meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Joint Advisory Committee (JAC) on Nuclear Weapons Surety 
will meet in closed session on 19-20 December, 1994, at Institute for 
Defense Analyses, Alexandria, Virginia.
    The Joint Advisory Committee is charged with advising the Secretary 
of Defense, Secretary of Energy, and the Joint Nuclear Weapons Council 
on nuclear weapons systems surety matters. At this meeting, the Joint 
Advisory Committee will receive classified briefings on DoD nuclear 
readiness capabilities.
    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Public Law 
92-463, as amended, Title 5, U.S.C. App. II, (1988)), this meeting 
concerns matters, sensitive to the interests of national security, 
listed in 5 U.S.C. Section 552b (c)(1) and accordingly this meeting 
will be closed to the public.

    Dated: November 18, 1994.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
