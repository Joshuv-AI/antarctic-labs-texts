SUMMARY: The Siuslaw Resource Advisory Committee will meet in 
Corvallis, OR. The purpose of the meeting is to Review RAC FY08 
Business, Elect Chairperson, Set FY08 Overhead Rate, Information Share, 
Public Forum and 2008 Project Review/Recommendations.

DATES: The meeting will be held September 21, 2007 beginning at 9:30 
a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Siuslaw National Forest 
Supervisor's Office, 4077 SW Research Way, Corvallis, Oregon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Linda Stanley, Community Development 
Specialist, Siuslaw National Forest, 541/928-7085 or write to Forest 
Supervisor, Siuslaw National Forest, 4077 SW Research Way, Corvallis, 
OR 97339.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: A public input period will begin before 2008 
project review.
