SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region I--Boston District Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region I Advisory Counsel, 
located in the geographical area of Boston, will hold a public meeting 
at 10:00 a.m., on Thursday, April 2, 1998, at the Boston District 
Office, Room 265, to discuss such matters as may be presented by 
members and staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present.
    For further information, please write or call Ms. Mary E. McAleney, 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 10 Causeway 
Street, Room 265, Boston, Massachusetts 02222-1093, telephone (617) 
565-5560.
Eugene Carlson,
Associate Administrator, Office of Communications & Public Liaison.
