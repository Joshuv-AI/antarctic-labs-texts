DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
 

Cooper River Federal Savings Association, North Charleston, South 
Carolina; Replacement of Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Subdivision (C) of Sec. 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator Cooper River Federal Savings Association, 
North Charleston, South Carolina (``Association''), with the Resolution 
Trust Corporation as sole Receiver for the Association on June 3, 1994.

    Dated: July 6, 1994.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
