East Tennessee Natural Gas Company; Notice of Site Visit

March 4, 2002.
    Between March 18-20, 2002 the staff will be conducting site visits 
of the project route for the proposed Patriot Expansion in Wythe and 
Smyth Counties in Virginia and in Sullivan, Knox, Hamilton, Franklin, 
and Morgan Counties in Tennessee. Representatives of East Tennessee 
Natural Gas Company will accompany Commission staff. Anyone interested 
in participating in the site visits may contact the Commission's Office 
of External Affairs at (201) 208-1088 for more details and must provide 
their own transportation.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
