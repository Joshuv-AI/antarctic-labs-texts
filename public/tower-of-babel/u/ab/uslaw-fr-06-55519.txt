Truth in Lending (Regulation Z)

CFR Correction

    In Title 12 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 220 to 299, 
revised as of January 1, 2006, on page 284, in Sec.  226.7, the last 
sentence of paragraph (f) is corrected to read as follows:


Sec.  226.7  Periodic statement.

* * * * *
    (f) * * * If there is more than one periodic rate, the amount of 
the finance charge attributable to each rate need not be separately 
itemized and identified.
