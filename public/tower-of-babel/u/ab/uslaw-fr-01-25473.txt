Brother, Industries, USA, Bartlett, TN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 17, 2001 in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Brother Industries, USA, Bartlett, Tennessee.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn, 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 21st day of September, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division Trade Adjustment Assistance.
