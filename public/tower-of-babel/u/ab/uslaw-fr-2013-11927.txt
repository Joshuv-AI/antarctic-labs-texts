Contractor Legal Management Requirements; Acquisition 
Regulations; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy (DOE) is correcting a final rule that 
appeared in the Federal Register of May 3, 2013 (78 FR 25795). In this 
document, DOE revised existing regulations covering contractor legal 
management requirements. Conforming amendments were also made to the 
Department of Energy Acquisition Regulation (DEAR).

DATES: This correction is effective July 2, 2013.

Email: [email protected].
