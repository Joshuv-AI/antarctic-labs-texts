DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket Nos. RP94-6-000 and RP94-64-000]

 

Northern Natural Gas Co; Technical Conference

March 1, 1994.
    Take notice that at 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, March 22, 1994, the 
Commission staff will convene a technical conference in the above-
captioned proceedings. Any discussion and/or review of confidential 
data will be restricted to those parties who have signed a 
confidentiality agreement.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\The Commission staff is not required to sign a 
confidentiality agreement.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The technical conference will be held at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 810 First Street, NE., Washington, DC.
Linwood A Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
