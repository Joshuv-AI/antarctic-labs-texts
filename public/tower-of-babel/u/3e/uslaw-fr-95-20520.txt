SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Portland District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Portland District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Thursday, September 14, 1995 from 
1:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. and Friday, September 15, 1995 from 8:00 a.m. to 
12 noon at the Riverhouse, 3075 N Highway, Bend, Oregon, to discuss 
matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. John L. Gilman, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 222 SW. Columbia, Suite 
500, Portland, OR 97201-6695, (503) 326-5221.

    Dated: August 14, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
