FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-2150, MB Docket No. 02-102, RM-10430]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Florence, SD

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Young Broadcasting of Sioux 
Falls, Inc., substitutes DTV channel 2 for DTV channel 25 at Florence, 
South Dakota. See 67 FR 34670, May 15, 2002. DTV channel 2 can be 
allotted to Florence in compliance with the principle community 
coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 44-
57-56 N. and 97-35-22 W. with a power of 3.7, HAAT of 243 meters and 
with a DTV

[[Page 62650]]

service population of 119 thousand. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 25, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
