Formal Determinations on Records Release: Correction

AGENCY: Assassination Records Review Board.

ACTION: Notice of formal determinations: correction.

SUMMARY: The Assassination Records Review Board (Review Board) met on 
August 2 and August 3, 1995, and made formal determinations on the 
release of records under the President John F. Kennedy Assassination 
Records Collection Act of 1992 (JFK Act). The Review Board published a 
notice document in the Tuesday, August 22, 1995, Federal Register, 
reflecting those determinations. In that notice document 95-20720 
beginning on page 43581, make the following corrections:
