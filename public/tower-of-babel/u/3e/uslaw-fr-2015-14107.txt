DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 3230-010]


Chasm Hydro Partnership; Ampersand Chasm Falls Hydro LLC; Notice 
of Transfer of Exemption

    1. By letter filed March 3, 2015, Ampersand Chasm Falls Hydro LLC 
informed the Commission that the exemption from licensing for the 
Chateaugay Chasm Project, FERC No. 3230, originally issued June 15, 
1981,\1\ has been transferred to Ampersand Chasm Falls Hydro LLC. The 
project is located on the Chateaugay River in Franklin County, New 
York. The transfer of an exemption does not require Commission 
approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ 15 FERC ] 62,339, Order Granting Exemption from Licensing of 
a Small Hydroelectric Project of 5 Megawatts or Less (1981).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Ampersand Chasm Falls Hydro LLC is now the exemptee of the 
Chateaugay Chasm Project, FERC No. 3230. All correspondence should be 
forwarded to: Ian Chow, Project Manager, Ampersand Chasm Falls Hydro 
LLC, 717 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 1A, Boston, MA 02111.

    Dated: June 3, 2015.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
