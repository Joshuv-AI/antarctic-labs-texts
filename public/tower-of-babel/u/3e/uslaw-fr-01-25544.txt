Additional Information
    By a vote of 4-0 on September 26 and 27, the Commission determined 
pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules 
that ``Affirmation of Final Rulemaking to Amend 10 CFR Part 55, 
`Operators' Licenses,' Regarding Operator License Eligibility and the 
Use of Simulator Facilities in Operator Licensing; and, Revision 3 of 
Regulatory Guide 1.149, ``Nuclear Power Plant Simulation Facilities for 
Use in Operator Training and License Examinations'' be held on 
September 28, and on less than one week's notice to the public
    By a vote of 4-0 on October 3, the Commission determined pursuant 
to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Duke Energy Corporation License Renewal Application 
for Catawba, Units 1 and 2, and McGuire, Units 1 and 2; Licensing Board 
Referral and Scheduling Order'' be held on October 4, and on less than 
one week's notice to the public.
