DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

20850.

0288.

    Date: September 26, 1996.

20850.

(301) 435-0288.
    Purpose/Agenda: To review and evaluate contract proposals.

    Contact Person: S. Charles Selden, Ph.D., Two Rockledge Center, 
Room 7196, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892-7924, (301) 435-
0288.
