OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Review of a 
Revised Information Collection: Form RI 38-115

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of a revised information collection. RI 
38-115, Representative Payee Survey, is designed to collect information 
about how the benefits paid to a representative payee have been used or 
conserved for the benefit of the incompetent annuitant.
    Approximately 4,067 RI 38-115 forms will be completed annually. 
This form takes approximately 20 minutes to complete. The annual burden 
is 1,356 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact Jim Farron on (202) 418-3208, 
or E-mail to [email protected]
