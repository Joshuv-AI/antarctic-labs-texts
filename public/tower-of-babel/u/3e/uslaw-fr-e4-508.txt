FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that at 9 a.m. on 
Wednesday, March 10, 2004, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation's 
Board of Directors will meet in closed session, pursuant to sections 
552b(c)(2), (c)(6), (c)(8), and (c)(9)(A)(ii) of title 5, United States 
Code, to consider matters relating to the Corporation's corporate, 
supervisory, and personnel activities.
    The meeting will be held in the Board Room on the sixth floor of 
the FDIC Building located at 550 17th Street, NW., Washington, DC.
    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Robert E. Feldman, Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-7043.

    Dated: March 5, 2004.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
 Executive Secretary.
