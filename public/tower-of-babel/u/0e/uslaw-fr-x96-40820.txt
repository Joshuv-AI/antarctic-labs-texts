SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

13 CFR Part 121


Small Business Size Standards

Correction

    In rule document 96-1348 beginning on page 3280 in the issue of 
January 31, 1996 make the following correction:


Sec. 121.201  [Corrected]

    On page 3289, in Sec. 121.201, in the table ``Size Standards by SIC 
Industry'', under the heading ``Size standards in number of employees 
or millions of dollars'', the entries corresponding to ``0211 Beef 
Cattle Feedlots (Custom)'' and ``0252 Chicken Eggs'' should read 
respectively ``$1.5'' and ``$9.0''
