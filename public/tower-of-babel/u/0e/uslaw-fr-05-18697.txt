FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Network Reliability and Interoperability Council Meeting 
Postponed

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice of postponement of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 
U.S.C. App. 2, Public Law 92-463, as amended, this notice advises 
interested persons that the meeting of the Network Reliability and 
Interoperability Council scheduled for September 21, 2005 has been 
cancelled and will be rescheduled for a date to be subsequently 
announced.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jeffery Goldthorp at (202) 418-1096, 
TTY (202) 418-2989, or e-mail [email protected].
