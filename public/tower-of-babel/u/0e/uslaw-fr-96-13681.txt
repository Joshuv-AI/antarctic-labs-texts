OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT

Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Review of an 
Expiring Information Collection: SF 2823

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management will submit to the Office of Management and Budget 
a request for a clearance of an expiring information collection. SF 
2823, Designation of Beneficiary for Federal Employees Group Life 
Insurance, is used by any Federal employee or retiree covered by the 
Federal Employees Group Life Insurance Program to instruct the Office 
of Federal Employees Group Life Insurance how to distribute the 
proceeds of his or her life insurance when the statutory order of 
precedence does not meet his or her needs.
    We estimate 1,000 SF 2823 forms are completed annually by 
annuitants. Each form takes approximately 15 minutes to complete for an 
annual estimated burden of 250 hours.
