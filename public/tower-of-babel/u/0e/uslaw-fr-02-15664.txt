Notice of Solicitation for Training and Technical Assistance for

AGENCY: Office for Victims of Crime, Office of Justice Programs, 
Justice.

ACTION: Correction to notice of solicitation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document provides the corrected information regarding 
when submissions are due for the Training and Technical Assistance for 
Services for Trafficking Victims solicitation, which was first 
published in the Federal Register on June 17, 2002 at 67 FR 41272.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michelle Avery Weston, Program 
Specialist (telephone 202-514-5084 or e-mail [email protected].

DATES: Applications for competitive programs must be received (not 
postmarked) at the OVC Training and Technical Assistance Center located 
at 10530 Rosehaven Street, Suite 400, Fairfax, Virginia 22030 on 
Monday, July 29, 2002, no later than 5:30 eastern standard time. OVC 
will not grant extensions of the due date.

    Dated: June 17, 2002.
John W. Gillis,
Director, Office for Victims of Crime.
