DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management
[ES-960-1420-00] ES-50265, Group 160, Wisconsin


Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Wisconsin, Stay Lifted

    On Thursday, May 27, 1999 there was published in the Federal 
Register, Volume 64, Number 102, on page 28833 a notice entitled 
``Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Wisconsin, Stayed''. Said notice 
referenced the stay of the plat of the dependent resurvey of a portion 
of the west boundary, a portion of the subdivisional lines, and the 
subdivision of sections 7 and 18, in Township 35 North, Range 15 West, 
of the 4th Principal Meridian, Wisconsin, accepted March 30, 1999.
    Consideration of all questions regarding technical aspects of the 
survey have been made, and the plat of survey accepted March 30, 1999, 
was officially filed in Eastern States Office, Springfield, Virginia, 
at 7:30 a.m. on June 29, 1999.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the appropriate fee.

    Dated: June 28, 1999.
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief, Cadastral Surveyor.
