SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Notice of Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board 
Members

AGENCY: Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Members of the FY 1999 Performance Review Board 
Members.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Section 4314 (c)(4) of Title 5, U.S.C., requires that Federal 
agencies publish notification of the appointment of individuals who may 
serve as members of that Agency's Performance Review Boards (PRB).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The following is the FY 1999 PRB roster:
