    Note: Beginning July 2, 1995, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission 
will be operating under a delegation of authority to chairman 
Shirley A. Jackson, because with three vacancies on the Commission, 
it will be temporarily without a quorum. As a legal matter, 
therefore, the Sunshine Act does not apply; but in the interests of 
openness and public accountability, the Commission will continue to 
conduct business as though the Sunshine Act were applicable.
