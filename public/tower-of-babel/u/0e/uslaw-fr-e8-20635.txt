DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Macromolecular Structure and Function D Study Section, October 7, 2008, 
8 a.m. to October 8, 2008, 5:30 p.m., Hotel Rouge, 1315 16th Street, 
Washington, DC, 20036 which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 25, 2008, 73 FR 50046-50048.
    The meeting will be held one day only October 7, 2008, from 8 a.m. 
to 6:30 p.m. The meeting location remains the same. The meeting is 
closed to the public.
