DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Administration for Children and Families


Agency Information Collection Under OMB Review

    Title: Low income home energy assistance program (LIHEAP) carryover 
and reallotment report.
    OMB No.: 0970-0106.
    Description: The data collected will be used to determine the 
amount of LIHEAP funds to be held available for the following fiscal 
year and the amount, if any, available for reallotment to other 
grantees in order to carry out the requirements of section 2610 of the 
LIHEAP statute.
    Respondents: State governments.
