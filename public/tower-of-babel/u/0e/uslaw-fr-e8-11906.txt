Stark Candy Company, a Division of New England Confectionary 
Company, Pewaukee, WI; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 14, 2008 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Stark 
Candy Company, a division of New England Confectionary Company, 
Pewaukee, Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of May 2008.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
