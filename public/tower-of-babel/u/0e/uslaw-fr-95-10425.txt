DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Defense Science Board Task Force on Combat Identification; Change 
in Date of Advisory Committee Meeting

Summary: The meeting of the Defense Science Board Task Force on Combat 
Identification scheduled for June 13-14, 1995 as published in the 
Federal Register (Vol. 60, No. 54, Page 14932, Tuesday, March 21, 1995, 
FR Doc. 95-6956) will be held on June 14-15, 1995. In all other 
respects the original notice remains unchanged.

    Dated: April 24, 1995.
