U.S.-Haiti Business Development Council

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Commerce.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice supplements the Federal Register Notice of May 1, 
1998 (63 FR 24162-24163) announcing membership opportunities for the 
U.S.-Haiti Business Development Council. All information in the 
previous announcement remains current, except for the change to the 
closing date, as explained herein.

DATES: This notice extends the closing date of the referenced Federal 
Register Notice for two months to August 1, 1998.
