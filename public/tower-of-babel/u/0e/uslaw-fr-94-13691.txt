Title 3--
The President
                Presidential Determination No. 94-24 of May 16, 1994

 

Theater Missile Defense Cooperation With the 
                Allies

                Memorandum for the Secretary of Defense

                1. Pursuant to the authority vested in me by the 
                National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 
                1994, Public Law 103-160, I hereby certify that the 
                Director, Ballistic Missile Defense Organization, has 
                formally submitted to representatives of the member 
                nations of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and 
                Japan, Israel, and South Korea a proposal concerning 
                coordination of the development and implementation of 
                U.S. Theater Missile Defense (TMD) programs with TMD 
                programs of our friends and allies.

                2. You are authorized and directed to notify the 
                Congress of this determination and to publish it in the 
                Federal Register.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    Washington, May 16, 1994.
