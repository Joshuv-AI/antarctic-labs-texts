DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Grand Canyon National Park, Coconino County, Arizona

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice and Request For Comment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that a proposal for a cellular 
communication site at Grand Canyon National Park has been received. The 
project will be to construct and operate a telecommunications facility 
at Hopi Point on the South Rim of the park.

DATES: Written comments must be submitted on or before January 11, 
1999.

ADDRESSES: Direct all written comments to Superintendent, Attn.: 
Barbara Nelson, Telecommunications Specialist, Grand Canyon National 
Park, P.O. Box 129, Grand Canyon, AZ 86023.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paul Glazer, Chief, Branch 
Information/Telecommunications Management at telephone number 520-638-
7738.

    Dated: December 3, 1998.
Steven D. Bone,
Acting Deputy Superintendent.
