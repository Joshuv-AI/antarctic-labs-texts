NewPage Wisconsin System Inc., Kaukauna Utilities; Notice of 
Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments and Motions 
To Intervene

January 29, 2010.
    On January 25, 2010, NewPage Wisconsin System Inc. (transferor) and 
Kaukauna Utilities (transferee) filed an application for transfer of 
license of the Kimberly Project No. 10674 located on the Fox River in 
Outagamie County, Wisconsin.
    The transferor and transferee seek Commission approval to transfer 
the license for the Kimberly Project from the transferor to the 
transferee.

Washington, DC 20037, (202) 457-5618. For transferee: Mike Pedersen, 
Kaukauna Utilities, 777 Island Street, Kaukauna, WI 54130, (920) 766-
5721, and Nancy J. Skancke, Law Offices of GKRSE, 1500 K St., NW. Suite 
330, Washington, DC 20005, (202) 408-5400.
