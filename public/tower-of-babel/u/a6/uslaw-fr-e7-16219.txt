COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Clarification of Notice of Procurement List Additions

    On page 45008, FR Doc E7-15668, Additions to the Procurement List, 
in the issue of August 10, 2007, the Committee published Procurement 
List Additions.
    This notice provides clarification of coverage for all of the NSNs 
under the following product headers: ``File, Folder, Classification'' 
and ``Inkjet Printer Cartridge.''

Coverage

    A-List for the total Government requirement as specified by the 
General Services Administration.

Kimberly M. Zeich,
Director, Program Operations.
