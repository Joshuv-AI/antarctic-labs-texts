DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of the Commission's staff may attend the following 
meetings related to the wholesale markets of ISO New England Inc. (ISO-
NE):

Markets and Public Policy: Solution Ideas Day, August 11, 2016, 10:00 
a.m.-5:00 p.m. (EST)

    The above-referenced meetings will be held at: Colonnade Hotel, 120 
Huntington Ave., Boston, MA 02116.
    Further information may be found at www.nepool.com.
    The discussion at the meeting described above may address matters 
at issue in the following proceedings:

Docket Nos. EL13-33 and EL 14-86, Environment Northeast et al. v. 
Bangor Hydro-Electric Company et al.
Docket No. EL16-19, ISO New England Inc. Participating Transmission 
Owners Administrative Committee
Docket No. EL16-38, Dominion Energy Marketing, Inc. et al. v. ISO New 
England Inc.
Docket No. EL16-93, NextEra Energy Resources, LLC and PSEG Companies v. 
ISO New England Inc.
