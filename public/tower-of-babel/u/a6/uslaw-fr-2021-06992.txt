DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine Notice of Meetings; Amended Notice 
of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Board of 
Regents, May 11-12, 2021, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., held virtually, 
which was published in the Federal Register on March 2, 2021, 86 FR 39, 
Page 12197.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting to a one-day 
meeting on May 11, 2021. The URL link to this meeting is: https://videocast.nih.gov. Any member of the public may submit written comments 
no later than 15 days after the meeting.

    Dated: March 31, 2021.
Ronald J. Livingston, Jr.,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
