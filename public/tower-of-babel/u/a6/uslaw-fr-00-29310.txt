RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Notification of Meeting

    The Railroad Retirement Board hereby gives notice that the Board 
will meet at 11:00 a.m., November 15, 2000, in the Board Room on the 
8th floor of the agency's headquarters building located at 844 N. Rush 
Street, Chicago, Illinois. The Board, by recorded vote, has determined 
that agency business requires the scheduling of this meeting with less 
than one week notice. The subjects to be addressed at this meeting are 
the Hearings Officer Vacancy and Director of Administration/Chief 
Enterprise Architect position.
    The entire meeting will be closed to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: November 9, 2000.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
