NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION

 

Special Emphasis in Biological Sciences; Notice of Meetings

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following three meetings of the Special Emphasis Panel in Biological 
Sciences (1754).

    Date and Time: March 14-16, 1994; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: Room 320, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson 
Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Contact Person: Dr. Eve Barak (703) 306-1442 and Dr. Jerry 
Johnson (703) 306-1439, Program Managers, National Science 
Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate Research Planning and Career 
Advancement proposals for Women Scientists and Engineers as part of 
the selection process for awards.

    Date and Time: April 13-15, 1994; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: Room 330, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson 
Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Contact Person: Dr. DeLill Nasser (703) 306-1439 and Dr. Ann 
Sakai (703) 306-1481, Program Managers, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate Postdoctoral Research Fellowships 
in Molecular Evolution proposals as part of the selection process 
for awards.

    Date and Time: April 18-20, 1994; 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
    Place: Rooms 360 and 360-2, National Science Foundation, 4201 
Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA.
    Contact Person: Dr. DeLill Nasser (703) 306-1439 and Dr. Gary 
Cecchini (703) 306-1443, Program Managers, National Science 
Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Agenda: To review and evaluate NSF Young Investigator proposals 
as part of the selection process for awards.

    Type of Meetings: Closed.
    Purpose of Meetings: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning support for research proposals submitted to the NSF for 
financial support.
    Reason for Closing: The proposals being reviewed include 
information of a proprietary or confidential nature, including 
technical information; financial data, such as salaries; and 
personal information concerning individuals associated with the 
proposals. These matters are exempt under 5 U.S.C. 552b(c) (4) and 
(6) of the Government in the Sunshine Act.

    Dated: February 9, 1994.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
