Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Withdrawal of Production Notification; Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 
30--Salt Lake City, Utah; Albion Laboratories, Inc. (Mineral Amino Acid 
Chelates); Ogden, Utah

    On August 15, 2022, Albion Laboratories, Inc. (Albion) submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board (the 
Board) for its facilities within Subzone 30E in Ogden, Utah. The 
notification was processed in accordance with the regulations of the 
FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal Register 
inviting public comment (87 FR 51054, August 19, 2022). On October 31, 
2022, Albion requested to withdraw the notification and the case has 
been closed without prejudice.

    Dated: October 31, 2022.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
