Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Prokaryotic Cell and Molecular Biology Study Section, November 3, 2021, 
10:00 a.m. to November 4, 2021, 7:00 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, Rockledge II, 6701 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD, 20892 which

#2021-21459, V-86--Page 54703.

November 3-4, 2021 to November 3, 2021. The meeting time and place 
remains the same. The meeting is closed to the public.
