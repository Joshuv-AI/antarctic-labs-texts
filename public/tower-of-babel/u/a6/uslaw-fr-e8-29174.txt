Scott Curtis Construction Company, Inc., Granite Falls, NC; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on November 17, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Scott 
Curtis Construction Company, Inc., Granite Falls, North Carolina. The 
workers at the subject facility build houses.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 19th day of November 2008.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
