DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Corrected Notice of Change of Name of Approved Trustee

    This corrects Notice appearing at 61 FR 29445 (June 10, 1996).
    Notice is hereby given that effective December 1, 1995, approved 
Trustee, Shawmut Bank Connecticut N.A., with offices at 777 Main 
Street, Hartford, Connecticut 06115, changed its name to Fleet National 
Bank of Connecticut. Further, effective April 1, 1996, Fleet National 
Bank of Connecticut changed its name to Fleet National Bank.

    Dated: July 24, 1996.

    By Order of the Maritime Administrator.
Edmund T. Sommer, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
