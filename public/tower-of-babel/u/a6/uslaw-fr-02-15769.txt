National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National

to June 25, 2002, 6 p.m., National Cancer Institute, 9000 Rockville 
Pike, Building 31, C Wing, 6th Floor, Conference Room 10, Bethesda, MD
