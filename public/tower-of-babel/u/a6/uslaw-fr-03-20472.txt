2003-2008 Strategic Plan Notice; Request for Comments

AGENCY: Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS), Treasury.

ACTION: Notice and request for comment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS) updates its Strategic 
Plan every three years. The mission and strategic goals contained in 
the plan support statutory and regulatory requirements, current and 
long-range industry issues, feedback from stakeholders, and long-range 
strategic objectives. The goals and objectives are implemented through 
annual performance plans.
    OTS requests comments on its draft 2003-2008 Strategic Plan. The 
draft Plan is available on the OTS Internet site at www.ots.treas.gov 
under About OTS: Plans and Reports.

DATES: Comments must be submitted by August 22, 2003.
