SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Minneapolis/St. Paul Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Minneapolis/St. Paul 
District Advisory Council will hold a public meeting on Thursday, March 
23, 1995 at 12:00 noon, at the Decathlon Athletic Club, 1700 East 79th 
Street, Bloomington, Minnesota, to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, 
or others present.

(The meeting scheduled for March 31, 1995 has been canceled).

    For further information, write or call Mr. Edward A. Daum, 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 610-Butler Square, 100 
North Sixth street, Minneapolis, Minnesota 55403, (612) 370-2306.

    Dated: February 21, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
