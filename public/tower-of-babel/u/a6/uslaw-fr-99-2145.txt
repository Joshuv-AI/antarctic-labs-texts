Employment and training Administration

Rayonier, Incorporated Inland Wood Products Division Plummer, 
Idaho; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 14, 1998 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed November 30, 1998 on behalf of workers at Rayonier, 
Incorporated, Inland Wood Products Division, Plummer, Idaho.
    The petitioning group of workers are covered under an existing 
Trade Adjustment Assistance investigation (TA-W-35, 305). Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would service no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of January 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
