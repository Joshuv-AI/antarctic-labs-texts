Johnson Screens Inc., New Brighton, MN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 7, 2004 in response to a petition filed by 
the TAA Coordinator of the Department of Employment Economic 
Development, Saint Paul, Minnesota on behalf of workers at Johnson 
Screens Inc., New Brighton, Minnesota.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 10th day of September 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
