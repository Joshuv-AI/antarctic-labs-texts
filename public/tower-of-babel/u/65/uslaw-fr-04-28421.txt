The Federal Communications Commission's Form 501 Approved by the 
Office of Management and Budget (OMB)

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In this document, the Office of Management and Budget issued a 
Notice of Action approving the revised Federal Communications 
Commission Form 501, Slamming Complaint Form. The form was revised to 
ensure that consumers have to file a slamming complaint only once, 
rather than having to seek multiple avenues of redress.

DATES: Effective November 29, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Marks or Kelli Farmer of the 
Consumer & Governmental Affairs Bureau at (202) 418-2512 (voice).
