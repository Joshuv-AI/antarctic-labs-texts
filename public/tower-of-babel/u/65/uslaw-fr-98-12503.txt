DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Intent to Grant Exclusive Patent License; MedAcoustics, 
Inc.

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to MedAcoustics, Inc., a revocable, nonassignable, exclusive 
license in the

[[Page 26163]]

United States, to practice the Government-owned inventions described in 
U.S. Patent No. 5,617,869 entitled ``A Device and Method for Locating 
Flow Blockage in a Three-Dimensional Object,'' and U. S. Patent No. 
5,727,561 entitled ``Method and Apparatus for Non-Invasive Detection 
and Analysis of Turbulent Flow in a Patient's Blood Vessels'' in the 
field of medical devices.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license must file 
written objections along with supporting evidence, if any, not later 
than July 13, 1998.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Office of Naval 
Research, ONR 00CC, Ballston Tower One, 800 North Quincy Street, 
Arlington, Virginia 22217-5660.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. R. J. Erickson, Staff Patent 
Attorney, Office of Naval Research, ONR 00CC, Ballston Tower One, 800 
North Quincy Street, Arlington, Virginia 22217-5660, telephone (703) 
696-4001.
