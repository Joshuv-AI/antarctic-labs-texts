National Library of Medicine Notice of Meeting Amended Notice of 
Meeting

Library of Medicine Special Emphasis Panel, June 16, 2023, 12:00 p.m. 
to 6:00 p.m. This notice was published in the Federal Register on March 
23, 2023, 88 FR 56, Page 17587.

11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The meeting is closed to the public.
