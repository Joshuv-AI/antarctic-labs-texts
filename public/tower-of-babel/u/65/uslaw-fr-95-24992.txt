DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

Advisory Committee on the Readjustment of Vietnam and Other War 
Veterans; Notice of Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (Public Law 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App.), that the Department of 
Veterans Affairs' Advisory Committee on the Readjustment of Vietnam and 
Other War Veterans has been renewed for a 2-year period beginning 
September 1, 1995, through September 1, 1997.

    Dated: September 28, 1995.

    By Direction of the Secretary.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
