Alaska Regulatory Program; Correction

AGENCY: Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Interior.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement (OSM) 
is correcting the effective date of a final rule that appeared in the 
Federal Register on March 31, 1997. The document approved an amendment 
to the Alaska regulatory program (62 FR 15115), effective on the date 
of publication, March 31, 1997. OSM had prepared a separate rulemaking 
on March 5, 1997, (62 FR 9932), which became effective April 4, 1997. 
Due to the difference in the effective date, the March 5, 1997 rule 
would result in a nullification of the Alaska state program amendment 
previously listed. Therefore, this document corrects the effective date 
of the Alaska state program amendment to April 7, 1997.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The amendment to 30 CFR Part 902 (62 FR 15115), is 
effective April 7, 1997.
