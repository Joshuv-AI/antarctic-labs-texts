FDA Modernization Act of 1997; Modifications to the List of 
Recognized Standards, Recognition List Number: 008; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is correcting a notice 
that appeared in the Federal Register of April 28, 2003 (68 FR 22391). 
The document announced a publication entitled ``FDA Modernization Act 
of 1997; Modifications to the List of Recognized Standards, Recognition 
List Number; 008.'' The publication contains modifications the agency 
is making to the list of standards FDA recognizes for use in the 
premarket reviews. The document published with an inadvertent error. 
This document corrects that error.

DATES: May 9, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce Strong, Office of Policy and 
Planning (HF-27), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-7010.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. 03-10417, appearing on page 22397 
in the Federal Register of Monday, April 28, 2003, the following 
correction is made:
    On page 22397, the section heading ``IV. Listing of New Entries'' 
is corrected to read ``III. Listing of New Entries.''
