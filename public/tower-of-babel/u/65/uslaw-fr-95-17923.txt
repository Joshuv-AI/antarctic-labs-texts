Owen/Galderma, et al.; Withdrawal of Approval of 1 New Drug 
Application, 23 Abbreviated New Drug Applications, and 5 Abbreviated 
Antibiotic Applications

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is withdrawing approval 
of 1 new drug application (NDA), 23 abbreviated new drug applications 
(ANDA's), and 5 abbreviated antibiotic applications (AADA's). The 
holders of the applications notified the agency in writing that the 
drug products were no longer marketed and requested that the approval 
of the applications be withdrawn.

EFFECTIVE DATE: August 21, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lola Batson, Center for Drug 
Evaluation and Research (HFD-360), Food and Drug Administration, 7500 
Standish Pl., Rockville, MD 20855, 301-594-1038.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The holders of the applications listed in 
the table in this document have informed FDA that these drug products 
are no longer marketed and have requested that FDA withdraw approval of 
the applications. The applicants have also, by their request, waived 
their opportunity for a hearing.
