Greenbrier Pipeline Company, LLC; Notice of Site Visit

November 7, 2002.
    On November 20, 2002, the staff of the Office of Energy Projects 
will conduct a site visit of Greenbrier Pipeline Company, LLC's 
Greenbrier Pipeline Project in North Carolina and Virginia. The project 
area will be inspected by automobile and on foot, as appropriate.
    All interested parties may attend the site visit. Those planning to 
attend must provide their own transportation. For additional 
information about the site visit, contact the Commission's Office of 
External Affairs at 1 866 208-FERC (3372).

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
