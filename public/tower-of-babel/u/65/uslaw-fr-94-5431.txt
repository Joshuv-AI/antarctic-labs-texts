SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster Loan Area #2690; Amdt. 2]

 

California; Declaration of Disaster Loan Area

    The above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended, effective 
February 22, 1994, to extend the filing deadline of February 25, 1994 
for applications for physical damage as a result of this disaster to 
April 26, 1994.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the termination date 
for filing applications for economic injury is July 28, 1994.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: February 28, 1994.
Bernard Kulik,
Assistant Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
