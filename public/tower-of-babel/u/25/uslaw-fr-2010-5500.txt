DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, March 25, 2010, 12:30 p.m. to 
March 25, 2010, 5:30 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 6116 
Executive Boulevard, 210, Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in 
the Federal Register on February 19, 2010, 75 FR 7489.
    This FRN is amending the name of the committee from SBIR Topic 258 
to ``Innovative Devices to Protect Radiosensitive Organs.'' The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 5, 2010.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
