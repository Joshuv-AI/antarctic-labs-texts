Radio Broadcasting Services; Williston, SC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Henry B. Shaffer, allots 
Channel 260A at Williston, South Carolina, as the community's second 
local FM service. Channel 260A can be allotted to Williston, South 
Carolina, in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements with a site restriction of 18.2 kilometers 
(11.3 miles) east of Williston. The coordinates for Channel 260A at 
Williston, South Carolina, are 41-31-30 North Latitude and 120-19-45 
West Longitude.

DATES: Effective June 29, 2009.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Deborah Dupont, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
