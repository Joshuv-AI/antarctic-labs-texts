Ansonia Copper and Brass, Anosonia, CT, Ansonia Copper and Brass, 
Waterbury, CT; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 14, 2005 in response to a petition 
filed by a state agency representative on behalf of workers at Ansonia 
Copper and Brass, Ansonia, Connecticut, and Ansonia Copper and Brass, 
Waterbury, Connecticut.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 6th day of April 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
