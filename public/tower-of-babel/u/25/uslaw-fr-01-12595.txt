NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee on Equal Opportunity in Science and Engineering; Notice 
of Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended, the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:

    Name: Committee on Equal Opportunity in Science and Engineering 
(1173).
    Date/Time: June 14, 2001, 8 a.m.-5:45 p.m. and June 15, 2001, 8 
a.m.-3 p.m.
    Place: Room 1235, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson 
Blvd., Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Michelle McMurry, Executive Secretary, CEOSE, 
National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd., Arlington, VA 22230, 
Phone (703) 292-8094.
    Minutes: May be obtained from the Executive Secretary at the 
above address.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice and recommendations 
concerning proposals submitted to NSF for financial support.
