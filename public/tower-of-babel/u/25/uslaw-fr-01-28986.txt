Prime Tanning Company Rochester, NH; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 24, 2001, in response to a worker petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Prime Tanning Company, Rochester, New 
Hampshire.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation (TA-W-40,051). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 5th day of November 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of, Trade Adjustment Assistance.
