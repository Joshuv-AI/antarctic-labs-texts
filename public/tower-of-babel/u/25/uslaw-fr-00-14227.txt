Pacific Gas & Electric Company; Notice of Meetings

June 1, 2000.
    Take notice there will be meetings of the Ecological Resources 
subgroup of the Mokelumne Relicensing Collaborative on June 7-8, and 
13-15, 2000. There will be meetings of the Recreation subgroup on June 
8 and 14-15, 2000. The Ecological Resources and Recreation subgroups 
will meeting jointly on June 9, 2000. The Full Collaborative will meet 
on June 21-22, and 28-29, 2000. These meetings will be held from 9 a.m. 
to 4 p.m. at 2740 Gateway Oaks Drive, in Sacramento, California. 
Expected participants need to give their names to David Moller (PG&E) 
at (415) 973-4696.
    For further information, please contact Diana Shannon at (202) 208-
7774.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
