DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 3758]


Determination: Assistance to Peru: Determination Pursuant to 
Section 530 of the Foreign Operations, Export Financing, and Related 
Programs Appropriations Act, 2001

    Pursuant to section 530 of the Foreign Operations, Export 
Financing, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2001, I hereby 
determine that the Government of Peru has made substantial progress in 
creating the conditions for free and fair elections, respecting human 
rights, rule of law, independence and constitutional role of judiciary 
and national congress, freedom of expression and independent media. 
This determination shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: August 7, 2001.
Colin L. Powell,
Secretary of State.
