DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. OA97-12-000]


Central Vermont Public Service Corporation; Notice of Filing

November 26, 1996.
    Take notice that on October 16, 1996, Central Vermont Public 
Service Corporation tendered for filing an amendment to its October 11, 
1996 filing in the above-reference docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion

[[Page 64074]]

to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Rules 
211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 
385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or protests should be 
filed on or before December 6, 1996. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person 
wishing to become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
