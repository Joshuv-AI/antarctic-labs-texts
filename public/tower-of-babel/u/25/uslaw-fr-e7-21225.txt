DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. ER06-615-000]


California Independent System; Operator Corporation; Notice of 
FERC Staff Attendance

October 22, 2007.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on October 25-26, 2007, members of its staff will attend 
the meeting of Western Electricity Coordinating Council (WECC) Market 
Interface Committee in Vancouver, BC. The agenda and other documents 
for the meeting are available on the WECC Web site, http://www.wecc.biz.
    Sponsored by the WECC, the meeting is open to all market 
participants, and staff's attendance is part of the Commission's 
ongoing outreach efforts. The meetings may discuss matters at issue in 
the above captioned docket.
