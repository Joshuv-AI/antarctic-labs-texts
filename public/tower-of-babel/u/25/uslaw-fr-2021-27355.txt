Notice of Inventory Completion: Diablo Valley College, Pleasant 
Hill, CA; Withdrawal

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Diablo Valley College, a campus of Contra Costa Community 
College District, is rescinding a Notice of Inventory Completion 
published in the Federal Register on April 1, 2021.

DATES: This withdrawal is effective December 17, 2021.

969-2001, email [email protected].
