DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Pathobiology of Kidney Disease Study Section, October 11, 2007, 8 a.m. 
to October 12, 2007, 5 p.m., Hotel Rouge, 1315 16th Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20036 which was published in the Federal Register on 
September 21, 2007, 72 FR 54051-54054.
    The meeting will be held one day only--October 11, 2007, 8 a.m. to 
6 p.m. The meeting location remains the same. The meeting is closed to 
the public.

    Dated: October 2, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
