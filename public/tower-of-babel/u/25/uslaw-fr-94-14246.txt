DEPARTEMENT OF LABOR
 

Glass Ceiling Commission; Postponement of Open Site Hearing

SUMMARY: This notice postpones the June 24, 1994 Open Site Hearing of 
the Glass Ceiling Commission. The notice for this Open Site Hearing was 
announced in the Federal Register on May 17, 1994 (59 FR 25670). A new 
date for the Open Site Hearing will be announced in the future.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Glass Ceiling Commission, U.S. 
Department of Labor, 200 Constitution Avenue, NW., room C-2313, 
Washington, DC 20210, (202) 219-7342.

    Dated: Signed at Washington, DC this 7th day of June, 1994.
Robert B. Reich,
Secretary of Labor.
