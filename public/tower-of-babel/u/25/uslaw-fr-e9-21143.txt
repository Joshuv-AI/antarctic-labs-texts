OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act; September 3, 2009; Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Public Hearing in Conjunction 
with each Board meeting was published in the Federal Register (Volume 
74, Number 149, Page 39118) on August 5, 2009. No requests were 
received to provide testimony or submit written statements for the 
record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing scheduled for 2 p.m., 
September 3, 2009 in conjunction with OPIC's September 17, 2009 Board 
of Directors meeting has been cancelled.
    Contact Person for Information: Information on the hearing 
cancellation may be obtained from Connie M. Downs at (202) 336-8438, 
via facsimile at (202) 218-0136, or via e-mail at 
[email protected].
