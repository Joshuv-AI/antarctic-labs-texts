DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders; 
Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the October 27, 1995 meeting 
of the Deafness and Other Communication Disorders Programs Advisory 
Committee, which was published in the Federal Register on October 5, 
1995, 60 FR 52199.
    The committee was to have convened at 8:00 a.m. on October 27 in 
Building 31C, Conference Room 6, 900 Rockville Pike, Bethesda, MD. The 
meeting has been changed to a telephone conference call, and will be 
held from 10:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m. EST, in Building 31C, Room 3C05.
    The entire meeting will be open to the public, with attendance 
limited to space available.

    Dated: October 17, 1995.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
