Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for OMB Control Number 0704-
0250. This correction revises numbers delineated concerning the public 
burden data.

OUSD(AT&L)DPAP(DARS), 3060 Defense Pentagon, Room 3B855, Washington, DC 
20301-3060.

78 FR 15711, in the second column, correct the Number of Respondents 
and to Responses per Respondent to read:
