OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


September 5, 2002 Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (Volume 67, Number 163, Page 54504) on August 22, 
2002. No requests were received to provide testimony or submit written 
statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in 
conjunction with OPIC's September 12, 2002 Board of Directors meeting 
scheduled for 2 p.m. on September 5, 2002 has been cancelled.
