DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2576-185]


FirstLight Hydro Generating Company; FirstLight CT Housatonic 
LLC; Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 3, 2019, the Commission issued a notice setting February 
2, 2019, as the end of the formal period to file comments, motions to 
intervene, and protests on the transfer of license application for the 
Housatonic River Hydroelectric Project No. 2576. Due to the funding 
lapse at certain federal agencies between December 22, 2018 and January 
25, 2019, the Commission is extending the comment period until February 
25, 2019.

    Dated: February 11, 2019.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
