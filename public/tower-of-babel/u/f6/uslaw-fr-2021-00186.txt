DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Board of 
Regents, February 9-10, 2021, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the National 
Institutes of Health, Building 38, Lindberg Room, 8600 Rockville Pike, 
Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on 
November 20, 2020, 85 FR 225, Page 74361.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting to a one-day 
meeting on February 9, 2021 and the time change to 10 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. 
The URL link to this meeting is: https://videocast.nih.gov. Any member 
of the public may submit written comments no later than 15 days after 
the meeting.

    Dated: January 5, 2021.
Ronald J. Livingston, Jr.,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
