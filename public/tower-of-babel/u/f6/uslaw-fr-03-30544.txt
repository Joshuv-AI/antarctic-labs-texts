Radio Broadcasting Services; Rising Star, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In this document, the Commission dismisses a petition for rule 
making filed by Charles Crawford (``Petitioner''), requesting the 
allotment of Channel 290C3 at Rising Star, Texas. See 68 FR 47283, 
August 8, 2003. Petitioner's comments were late-filed with no request 
to accept on a late-filed basis. Although timely filed, a 
counterproposal filed by Katherine Pyeatt was dismissed as unacceptable 
due to a short spacing to a licensed station. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.
