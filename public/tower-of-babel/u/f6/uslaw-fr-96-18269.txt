DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE
United States Parole Commission


Public Announcement: Pursuant to the Government In the Sunshine 
Act (Public Law 94-409) [5 U.S.C. Section 552b]

TIME AND DATE: 2:30 p.m., Thursday, July 11, 1996.

PLACE: 5550 Friendship Boulevard, Suite 400, Chevy Chase, Maryland 
20815.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The following matters have been placed on the 
agenda for the open Parole Commission meeting:

    1. Approval of minutes of previous Commission meeting.
    2. Reports from the Chairman, Commissioners, Legal, Chief of 
Staff, Case Operations, and Administrative Sections.
    3. Proposal to Amend Regulations to Provide that Transfer Treaty 
Hearings be Conducted by One Hearing Examiner.
    4. Proposal Regarding Computer Restrictions on Parolees.
