Agency Information Collection Activities; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency published a document in 
the Federal Register of August 7, 1998, concerning request for comments 
on specific aspects of the information collection activities for the 
EPA Worker Protection Standard for Hazardous Waste Operations and 
Emergency Response. The document contained an incorrect date.

DATES: Comments must be submitted on or before October 6, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sella M. Burchette, (732) 321-6726.

Correction

    In the Federal Register issue of August 7, 1998, in FR Doc. 07au98-
100, on page 42396, correct the ``Dates'' caption to read:

    Dated: August 17, 1998.
Stephen D. Luftig,
Director, Office of Emergency and Remedial Response.
