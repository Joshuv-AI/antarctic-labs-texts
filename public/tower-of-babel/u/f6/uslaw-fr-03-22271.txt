Edison Fashion, Inc., Bronx, NY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on July 30, 2003, in response to a petition 
filed by UNITE!, New York Apparel and Allied Workers Joint Board, Local 
10, Local 89-22-1 and Local 189, on behalf of workers at Edison 
Fashion, Inc., Bronx, New York.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 12th day of August, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
