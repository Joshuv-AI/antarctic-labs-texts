DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

National Urban and Community Forestry Advisory Council

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting; cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 12, 1995, at 60 FR 25688, the Forest Service published 
notice of a National Urban and Community Forestry Advisory Council 
[[Page 28570]] meeting scheduled to be held in Carlisle, Illinois, June 
8-10, 1995. That meeting has been cancelled. Notices of future meetings 
of the Advisory Council will be published in the Federal Register.
