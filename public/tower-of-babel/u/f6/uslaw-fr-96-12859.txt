Notice of Program Plans for Fiscal Year 1996; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Justice, Office of Justice Programs.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the notice document 61 FR 21238 beginning on page 21238 in 
the issue of Thursday, May 9, 1996, make the following correction:
    On page 21288 in the second column, the address for receipt of a 
Program Announcement and Application Kit for the Office for Victims of 
Crime was listed as 1301 Pennsylvania Avenue, Suite 200, NW, 
Washington, D.C. 20531. This should be changed to 633 Indiana Avenue, 
NW, Washington D.C. 20531.

    Dated: May 16, 1996.
Reginald L. Robinson,
Deputy Assistant Attorney General, Office of Justice Programs.
