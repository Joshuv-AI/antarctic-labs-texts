DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP 92-154-001]


Colorado Interstate Gas Company; Notice of Compliance Filing

September 11, 1997.
    Take notice that on September 8, 1997, Colorado Interstate Gas 
Company (CIG), tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, 
First Revised Volume No. 1, Third Revised Sheet No. 369 to be effective 
October 15, 1997.
    CIG states that Sheet No. 369 was a tariff sheet authorized by 
Commission Order issued May 14, 1992 in Docket No. RP92-154-000. The 
tariff sheet identified customers' buyout-buydown obligation pursuant 
to Order No. 528.
    CIG states the filing is being made to ``clean up'' Sheet No. 369. 
One Buyer had elected to amortize its payment of its obligation over a 
60-month period. The 60-month payment period has terminated and the 
Buyer has paid its obligation; therefore, the filing reflects all 
Buyers have now paid their obligation pursuant to the authorization in 
Docket No. RP92-154-000.
    CIG states that copies of the filing have been sent to all parties 
in Docket No. RP92-154-000.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, DC 20426 in accordance with the Sec. 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such protests must be 
filed in accordance with Sec. 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. 
All protests filed with the Commission will be considered by it in 
determining the

[[Page 48828]]

appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make the 
protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of the filing are on 
file with the Commission and are available for public inspection in the 
Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
