DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Cancellation of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National Heart, 
Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, May 17, 2000, 2:30 
p.m. to May 17, 2000, 3:30 p.m., NIH, Rockledge II, Bethesda, MD, 20892 
which was published in the Federal Register on April 26, 2000, 65 FR 
24491-24492.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the fact that a review meeting is 
no longer required.

    Dated: May 12, 2000.
LeVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
