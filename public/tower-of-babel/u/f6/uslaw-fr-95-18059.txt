DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
DEPARTMENT OF THE ARMY


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 8 August 1995.
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1700.
    Place: Ft. Belvoir, VA.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board (ASB) Research and Advanced 
Concepts Issue Group will meet to address the Objective Individual 
Combat Weapon program and its relation to the Decisive Infantry 
Weapons study. This meeting will be open to the public. Any 
interested person may attend, appear before, or file statements with 
the committee at the time and in the manner permitted by the 
committee. For further information, please contact Michelle Diaz at 
(703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
