DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Aviation Rulemaking Advisory Committee; Meeting Cancellation

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public that the 
January 22, 1998, meeting of the Aviation Rulemaking Advisory Committee 
(ARAC) scheduled to discuss general aviation operations issues (63 FR 
122, January 2, 1998) has been cancelled.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Noreen Hannigan, Office of Rulemaking (ARM-106), Federal Aviation 
Administration, 800 Independence Ave., SW, Washington, DC 20591; 
telephone (202) 267-7476; fax (202) 267-5075.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on January 13, 1998.
Louis C. Cusimano,
Assistant Executive Director for General Aviation Operations, Aviation 
Rulemaking Advisory Committee.
