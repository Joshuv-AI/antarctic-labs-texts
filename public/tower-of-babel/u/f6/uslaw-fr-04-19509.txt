DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Environmental Review of Proposed Incidental Take Permit and 
Habitat Conservation Plan for the Kauai Island Utility Cooperative, 
Hawaii

AGENCY: Fish and Wildlife Service, Interior.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service is publishing this notice 
to correct a previous notice (document 04-16095) published on Thursday, 
July 15, 2004. On page 42447, under the heading DATES, the August 16, 
2004, deadline for receipt of written comments is incorrect. The 
correct deadline is October 16, 2004. In addition, on page 42447 under 
the heading SUMMARY and on page 42448 under the heading SUPPLEMENTARY 
INFORMATION, Environmental Review, the previous notice incorrectly 
referred to preparation of a ``joint Federal/State environmental 
document.'' This reference is corrected to delete the words ``joint'' 
and ``/State''. The environmental document is a Federal document 
prepared by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.

    Dated: August 4, 2004.
Don Weathers,
Acting Deputy Regional Director, Region 1, U.S. Fish and Wildlife 
Service.
