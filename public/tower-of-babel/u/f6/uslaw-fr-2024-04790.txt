NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Engineering; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation (NSF) announces the 
following meeting:
    Name and Committee Code: Advisory Committee for Engineering 
(#1170)--Hybrid Meeting.
    Date and Time: April 4, 2024; 10:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. (Eastern); April 
5, 2024; 8:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m. (Eastern).
    Place: NSF, 2415 Eisenhower Avenue, Alexandria, VA 22314/Hybrid.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Persons: Don Millard, National Science Foundation, 2415 
Eisenhower Avenue, Alexandria, VA 22314; Telephone: (703) 292-8300.
    Additional meeting information, an updated agenda, and registration 
information will be posted at https://www.nsf.gov/eng/advisory.jsp.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice, recommendations and counsel 
on major goals and policies pertaining to engineering programs and 
activities.
