COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Briefing of the Guam Advisory Committee to the 
U.S. Commission on Civil Rights; Correction

a.m.-10:00 a.m. ChST (6:00 p.m.-8:00 p.m. ET).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Liliana Schiller, Support Services 
Specialist, at [email protected] or (202) 770-1856.

Correction:

Number 2025-08391, on page 20271, first column, correct the meeting 
time to: 8:00 a.m.-10:00 a.m. ChST (6:00 p.m.-8:00 p.m. ET).
