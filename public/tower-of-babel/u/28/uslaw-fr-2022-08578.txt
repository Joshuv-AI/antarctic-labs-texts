PART 15--CONTRACTING BY NEGOTIATION


15.601   [Amended]

0
6. Amend section 15.601 by removing the definition of ``Commercial item 
offer'' and adding the definition ``Commercial product or commercial 
service offer'' in its place to read as follows:


15.601   Definitions.

* * * * *
    Commercial product or commercial service offer means an offer of a 
commercial product or commercial service that the vendor wishes to see 
introduced in the Government's supply system as an alternate or a 
replacement for an existing supply item. This term does not include 
innovative or unique configurations or uses of commercial products or 
commercial services that are being offered for further development and 
that may be submitted as an unsolicited proposal.
* * * * *
