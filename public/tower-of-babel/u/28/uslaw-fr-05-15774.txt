NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Notice of Meeting; Advisory Committee For Polar Programs

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Advisory Committee for Polar Programs (1130).
    Date/Time: August 11, 2005, 4 p.m. to 5 p.m. e.d.t.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, 
Stafford I, Room 390.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Altie Metcalf, Office of Polar Programs (OPP), 
National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 
22230, (703) 292-8030.
    Minuets: May be obtained from the contact person listed above.
    Purpose of Meeting: Receive the report prepared by the 
Subcommittee on USAP Resupply. This meeting will be by 
teleconference for the Committee members.
    Agenda: Introductions; Advisory Committee comments on the draft 
report; comments from other interested parties; Committee discussion 
and consideration of the report.

    Dated: August 4, 2005.
Suzanne Plimpton,
Management Analyst.
