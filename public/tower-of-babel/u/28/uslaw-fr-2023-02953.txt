Self-Regulatory Organizations; NYSE Arca, Inc.; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend Rule 6.40P-O Concerning 
Pre-Trade Risk Controls

February 7, 2023.
    On December 14, 2022, NYSE Arca, Inc. (``NYSE Arca'') filed with 
the Securities and Exchange Commission (the ``Commission''), pursuant 
to section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (``Act'' or 
``Exchange Act'') \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder \2\ a proposed rule 
change to add additional pre-trade risk controls to Rule 6.40P-O. The 
proposed rule change was published for comment on December 21, 2022.\3\ 
On February 6, 2023, NYSE Arca withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-
NYSEARCA-2022-82).
