SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) issued M-21-07, 
``Completing the Transition to Internet Protocol Version 6 (IPv6)'' 
located at: https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/M-21-07.pdf.
    In November 2020 to update guidance on the Federal government's 
operational deployment and use of IPv6. The memo communicates five 
categories of agency-level requirements for completing the deployment 
of IPv6 across all Federal information systems and services:

 Preparing for an IP6-only infrastructure
 Adhering to Federal IPv6 Acquisition Requirements
 Evolving the USGv6 Program
 Ensuring Adequate Security
 Government-wide Responsibilities
