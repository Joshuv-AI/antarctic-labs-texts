ACTION: Final rule; delay of effective date; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects an error to a Final Rule, on ``Special 
Visual Flight Rules (SVFR); Denver, CO'', which was published on 
Friday, May 13, 1994 (59 FR 24915). The Amendment number is incorrect.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 13, 1994.

27583'', please change the Amendment number to read ``91-241''.
