FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement(s) Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement(s) under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties can 
review or obtain copies of agreements at the Washington, DC offices of 
the Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, NW., Room 940. Interested 
parties may submit comments on an agreement to the Secretary, Federal 
Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within 10 days of the date 
this notice appears in the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 011733-005, -006.
    Title: Common Ocean Carrier Platform Agreement.
    Parties: Alianca Navegacao e Logistica Ltda.; A.P. Moller-Maersk 
Sealand; CMA CGM, S.A.; CP Ships Limited; Hamburg Sud; Hapag-Lloyd 
Container Linie GmbH; Mediterranean Shipping Company, S.A.; Nippon 
Yusen Kaisha; P&O Nedlloyd Limited; Safmarine Container Lines N.V.; 
United Arab Shipping Company (S.A.G.).
    Synopsis: Proposed Amendment No. 005 would permit non-party ocean 
common carriers to use the Portal authorized by the agreement on a 
trial basis without becoming a party to the agreement. Amendment No. 
006 adds CP Ships Limited, for and on behalf of its ocean common 
carrier subsidiaries, as a non-shareholder party to the agreement.

    Dated: July 19, 2002.

    By order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Theodore A. Zook,
