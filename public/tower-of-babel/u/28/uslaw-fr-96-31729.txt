DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Air Traffic Control Tower; West Memphis, Arkansas; Notice of 
Decommissioning

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration [FAA], DOT.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that on or about December 27, 1996, the 
Airport Traffic Control Tower at West Memphis, Arkansas will be 
decommissioned. This information will be reflected in the FAA 
Organization Statement the next time it is issued.

    49 U.S.C. 1348, 1354(a); 439 U.S.C. 106(g)

    Issued in Fort Worth, Texas, on December 6, 1996.
Clyde M. DeHart, Jr.,
Regional Administrator, Southwest Region.
