FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: Federal Election Commission

DATE AND TIME: Thursday, November 13, 2014 at 2:00 p.m.

PLACE: 999 E Street NW., Washington, DC.

STATUS: This meeting will be closed to the public.

ITEMS TO BE DISCUSSED: Internal personnel rules and internal rules and 
practices. Information the premature disclosure of which would be 
likely to have a considerable adverse effect on the implementation of a 
proposed Commission action.

PERSON TO CONTACT FOR INFORMATION: Judith Ingram, Press Officer,
