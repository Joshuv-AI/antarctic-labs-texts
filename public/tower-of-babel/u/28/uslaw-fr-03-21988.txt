EXPORT-IMPORT BANK

Sunshine Act Meeting




ACTION: Notice of a partially open meeting of the Board of Directors of 
the Export-Import Bank of the United States.

Time and Place: Thursday, August 28, 2003 at 9:30 a.m. The meeting will 
be held at Ex-Im Bank in Room 1143, 811 Vermont Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20571.

Open Agenda Item: Short Term Insurance Program for Iraq.

Public Participation: The meeting will be open to public participation 
for Item No. 1 only. Attendees that are not employees of the Executive 
Branch will be required to sign in prior to the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Office of the Secretary, 811 Vermont 
Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20571 (Telephone No. 202-565-3957).

James K. Hess,
Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer.
