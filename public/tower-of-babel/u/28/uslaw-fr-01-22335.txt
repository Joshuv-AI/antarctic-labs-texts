DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL01-111-000]


California Independent System Operator; Notice of Change in 
Docketing

August 30, 2001.
    Take notice that on June 25, 2001, July 11, 2001, August 10, 2001, 
the California Independent System Operator (CAISO) filed market 
monitoring reports in Docket Nos. EL00-95-000, EL00-98-000 and RT01-85-
000, as required by the Commission's April 26, 2001 Order.\1\ A new 
docket number has been established for these filings, and the CAISO 
shall hereafter file all market monitoring reports under Docket Number 
EL01-111-000. Previous filings, identified above, will be re-designated 
in the new docket.
