Digital Television Broadcast Service; Hammond, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of KB Prime Media LLC, 
substitutes DTV channel 42 for NTSC channel 62+ at Hammond, Louisiana. 
See 67 FR 40632, June 13, 2002. DTV channel 42 can be allotted to 
Hammond, Louisiana, in compliance with the principle community coverage 
requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 29-58-57 N. 
and 89-57-09 W. with a power of 1000, HAAT of 308 meters and with a DTV 
service population of 1667 thousand. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 4, 2002.
