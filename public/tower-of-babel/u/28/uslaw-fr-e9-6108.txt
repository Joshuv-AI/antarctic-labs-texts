DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Veterans' Advisory Committee on Environmental Hazards; Notice of 
Meeting Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under Public 
Law 92-463 (Federal Advisory Committee Act) that a meeting of the 
Veterans' Advisory Committee on Environmental Hazards scheduled for 
March 23-24, 2009, in room 630 at the Department of Veterans Affairs, 
810 Vermont Avenue, NW., Washington, DC has been cancelled.

    Dated: March 13, 2009.
    By Direction of the Secretary.
E. Philip Riggin,
Committee Management Officer.
