SUMMARY: The Indian Health Service published a document in the Federal 
Register (FR) on April 29, 2008. The document contained one error.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christina Rouleau, Office of 
Management Services, Indian Health Service, 801 Thompson Avenue, Suite 
450, Rockville, MD 20852, Telephone (301) 443-5938. (This is not a 
toll-free number.)

Correction

    In the Federal Register of April 29, 2008, in FR Doc. E8-9258, on 
page 23254, in the second column, second paragraph, under SUMMARY, 
correct Domestic/Intimate Partner Violence screening in women ages 14-
15 to read: Domestic/Intimate Partner Violence screening in women ages 
15-40.
