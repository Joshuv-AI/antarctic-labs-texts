FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Open Commission Meeting, Wednesday, July 
14, 1999

    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Wednesday, July 14, 1999, which is 
scheduled to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room C305, at 445 12th Street, 
S.W., Washington, D.C.
