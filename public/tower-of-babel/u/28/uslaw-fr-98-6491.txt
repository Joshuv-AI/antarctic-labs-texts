Millennium Pipeline Company, L.P. Columbia Gas Transmission 
Corporation; Notice of Change of Location for Scoping Meeting

March 9, 1998.
    The location for the environmental scoping meeting in Binghamton, 
New York, on March 18, 1998, has been changed from the Binghamton High 
School to the following facility: East Middle School, 167 Frederick 
Street, Auditorium, Binghamton, New York.
    For further information, call Paul McKee, Office of External 
Affairs, at (202) 208-1088.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
