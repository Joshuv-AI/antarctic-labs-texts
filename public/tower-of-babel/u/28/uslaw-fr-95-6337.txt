FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
[Report No. 2062]


Petition for Reconsideration and Clarification of Actions in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

March 10, 1995.
    Petition for reconsideration have been filed in the Commission 
rulemaking proceedings listed in this public notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of these documents are 
available for viewing and coping in Room 239, 1919 M Street, NW., 
Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc., (202) 875-3800. Opposition to these petitions 
must be filed March 30, 1995. See Sec. 1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's 
rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(2)). Replies to an opposition must be filed within 
10 days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.

Subject: Amendment of the Amateur Service Rules to Implement a Vanity 
Call Sign System. (PR Docket No. 93-305)
