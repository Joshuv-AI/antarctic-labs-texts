DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Notice of Cancellation of Meeting 
Panel

    Notice is hereby given that the meeting of the President's Cancer 
Panel, National Cancer Institute, National Institutes of Health, 
scheduled for May 22, 1997 and published in the Federal Register (62 FR 
19124) on April 18, 1997 is hereby canceled due to scheduling 
conflicts.

    Dated: May 1, 1997.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
