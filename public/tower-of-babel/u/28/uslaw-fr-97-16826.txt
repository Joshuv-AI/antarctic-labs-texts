Alliance Pipeline L.P.; Notice of Environmental Site Visit for 
the Proposed Alliance Pipeline Project

June 23, 1997.
    On July 7 through July 11, the staff of the Office of Pipeline 
Regulation will conduct a helicopter overflight of the proposed 
Alliance Pipeline Project facilities and alternatives. In addition, 
there will be limited site visits, as time permits, to the locations of 
proposed facilities in Will and Grundy Counties, Illinois.
    Anyone interested in participating in the site visits may contact 
Mr. Paul McKee in the Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 
208-1088 for more details and must provide their own transportation.
Kevin P. Madden,
Director, Office of Pipeline Regulation.
