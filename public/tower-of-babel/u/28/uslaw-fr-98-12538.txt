SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region I--New England States Regional Fairness Board; Public 
Hearing

    The New England States Regional Fairness Board Hearing to be held 
on June 22, 1998, starting at 9:30 a.m., at the University of Maine at 
Augusta, 46 University Drive, Jewett Hall Auditorium, Augusta, Maine 
04330, in space is being donated by the University of Maine, to discuss 
such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U. S. Small 
Business, and others present.
    For further information contact Gary P. Peele, telephone (312) 353-
0880.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
