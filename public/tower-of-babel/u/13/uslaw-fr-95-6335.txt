FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[MM Docket No. 88-257, RM-6299]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Kingsville and Ingleside, Texas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; waiver of automatic stay.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a motion for waiver of automatic stay 
filed by Kingsville Radio Company on the effect of the substitution of 
Channel 224C2 for Channel 224A at Kingsville, TX and the modification 
of its license for Station KNGV(FM) accordingly. See Report and Order 
57 FR 3952 (February 3, 1992). This action is granted by the Commission 
without prejudice to any further action the Commission may take 
regarding the application for review in MM Docket No. 88-257.

Effective Date: March 15, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Arthur D. Scrutchins, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 776-1660.
