DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, July 
30, 2009, 2 p.m. to July 30, 2009, 4:30 p.m., NIEHS/National Institutes 
of Health, Keystone Building, 530 Davis Drive, Research Triangle Park, 
NC 27709 which was published in the Federal Register on July 2, 2009, 
FR E9-15689.
    The meeting date was changed from July 30, 2009 to August 12, 2009 
and the meeting time was changed from 2 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: July 9, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
