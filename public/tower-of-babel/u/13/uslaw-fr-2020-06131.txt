DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, 
March 25, 2020, 08:00 a.m. to March 26, 2020, 03:00 p.m., National 
Institute of Environmental Health Science, Keystone Building, 530 Davis 
Drive, Research Triangle Park, NC 27709 which was published in the 
Federal Register on February 10, 2020, 85 FRN 5460.
    The March 25th-26th 2020, NIEHS Special Emphasis Panel Meeting is 
being amended due to a change in the meeting format. This two-day 
meeting is being changed from an in-person meeting to a teleconference 
meeting. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 18, 2020.
Tyeshia M. Roberson,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
