COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION


Meeting Notice

AGENCY: Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

ACTION: Notice of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Time and Date: 1 p.m.-4:30 p.m., December 7, 1999.
    Status: Open.

SUMMARY: This is to give notice that Chairman William J. Rainer will 
hold a public roundtable meeting to discuss emerging technology related 
issues as they pertain to the financial services and commodities 
markets.

PLACE: 1155 21st St., NW, Washington, DC, Lobby Level Hearing Room.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: De'Ana H. Dow, Legal Counsel to 
Chairman Rainer, at 202-418-5030, or Elizabeth Fox, Legal Counsel to 
Commissioner Newsome, at 202-418-5052. Written comments should be 
submitted to Jean A. Webb, Secretary, Commodity Futures Trading 
Commission, Three Lafayette Centre, 1155 21st Street, N.W., Washington, 
D.C. 20581.

    Issued in Washington, D.C. on November 24, 1999.

Jean A. Webb,
Secretary of the Commission.
