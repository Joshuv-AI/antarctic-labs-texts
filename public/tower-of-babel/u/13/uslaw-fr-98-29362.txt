DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. CP98-247-000]


Midcoast Interstate Transmission, Inc.; Notice of Site Visit

October 28, 1998.
    On November 5 and 6, 1998, the Office of Pipeline Regulation (OPR) 
staff will conduct a site visit, with representatives of Midcoast 
Interstate Transmission, Inc., of construction activities in Colbert 
County, Alabama.
    All interested parties may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
