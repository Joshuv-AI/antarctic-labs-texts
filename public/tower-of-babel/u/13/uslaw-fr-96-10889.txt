POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Notice of Commission Visit

April 29, 1996.
    Notice is hereby given that members of the Postal Rate Commission 
and certain advisory staff members will visit the following business to 
observe its operation.
    Mailboxes, Etc., Washington, D.C., May 2, 1996.
    A report of this visit will be placed on file in the Commission's 
Docket Room.
    For further information, contact Margaret P. Crenshaw, Secretary of 
the Commission at 202/789-6840.
Margaret P. Crenshaw,
Secretary.
