DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Government-Industry Advisory Committee on the Operation and 
Modernization of the National Defense Stockpile

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The fifth meeting of this committee will be held on April 10, 
1996, at the Defense Logistics Agency Headquarters Complex, Fort 
Belvoir, VA. The meeting is open to the public. This committee was 
established under Public Law 102-484. The meeting time and agenda are 
as follows:

TIME: 9:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.

AGENDA: The Committee will hear reports from the working group on Sales 
Methodology and from the working group on Stockpile Modernization. For 
additional information contact Tom Meeker at 703-767-6476.

    Dated: March 22, 1996.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
