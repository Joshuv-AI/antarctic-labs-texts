SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[License No. 04/04-0310]


Claritas Capital Specialty Debt Fund, L.P.; Surrender of License 
of Small Business Investment Company

    Pursuant to the authority granted to the United States Small 
Business Administration under the Small

[[Page 55734]]

Business Investment Act of 1958, as amended, under Section 309 of the 
Act and Section 107.1900 of the Small Business Administration Rules and 
Regulations (13 CFR 107.1900) to function as a small business 
investment company under the Small Business Investment Company License 
No. 04/04-0310 issued to Claritas Capital Specialty Debt Fund, L.P. 
said license is hereby declared null and void.

    U.S. Small Business Administration.
Christopher L. Weaver,
Acting Associate Administrator, Office of Investment and Innovation.
