-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Notice is hereby given of a change in the 
meeting of the Disease, Disability, and Injury Prevention and Control 
Special Emphasis Panel (SEP)--RFA-CE-22-001, Grants to Support New 
Investigators in Conducting Research Related to Understanding Polydrug 
Use Risk and Protective Factors; March 15-16, 2022, 8:30 a.m.-5:30 
p.m., EDT. The web conference was published in the Federal Register on 
January 5, 2022, Volume 87, Number 3, page 460.
    This meeting is being canceled in its entirety.

Telephone: (404) 639-6473, Email: [email protected].

the Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry.
