DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

48 CFR Parts 915, 916 and 970

RIN 1991-AB19


Acquisition Regulation: Certified Cost or Pricing Data Threshold 
and Requirements for a Determination and Findings for Use of Cost-
Reimbursement Contracts

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy amends its Acquisition Regulation to 
effect changes to the threshold for certified cost or pricing data; and 
deleting the requirement for a determination and findings for use of 
cost reimbursement contracts. These changes are required by the Federal 
Acquisition Streamlining Act of 1994 and subsequent changes to the 
Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR).

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 10, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Terrence D. Sheppard, (202) 586-8174, 
Office of Policy (HR-51), Office of Procurement and Assistance 
Management, Department of Energy, 1000 Independence Avenue, SW., 
Washington, D.C. 20585.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    DOE published an interim rule in the Federal Register on April 10, 
1995 (60 FR 18030). The public comment period closed June 9, 1995. No 
public comments were received. Accordingly, the interim rule is adopted 
as a final rule.
