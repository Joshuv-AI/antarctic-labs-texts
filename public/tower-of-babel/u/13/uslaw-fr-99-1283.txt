DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB)
    Date of Meeting: 19 & 20 January 1999
    Time of Meeting: 0830-1600
    Place: 2425 Wilson Blvd., Arlington, VA 22201
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Summer Study Panel on 
``Enabling Rapid and Decisive Strategic Maneuver for the Army After 
2010'' will meet for discussions. These meetings will be open to the 
public. Any interested person may attend, appear before, or file 
statements with the committee at the time and in the manner 
permitted by the committee. For further information, please call 
Jacqueline Ladd at (703) 604-7479.
Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
