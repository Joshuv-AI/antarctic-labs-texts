Title 3--
The President
                Presidential Determination No. 94-33 of July 14, 1994

 

Determination Pursuant to Section 2(b)(2) of the 
                Migration and Refugee Assistance Act of 1962, as 
                Amended

                Memorandum for the Secretary of State

                Pursuant to section 2(b)(2) of the Migration and 
                Refugee Assistance Act of 1962, as amended, 22 U.S.C. 
                2601(b)(2), I hereby designate Palestinian refugees in 
                the Middle East as qualifying for assistance, and 
                determine that such assistance will contribute to the 
                foreign policy interests of the United States.

                You are authorized and directed to inform the 
                appropriate committees of the Congress of this 
                determination and the obligation of funds under this 
                authority, and to publish this determination in the 
                Federal Register.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    Washington, July 14, 1994.
