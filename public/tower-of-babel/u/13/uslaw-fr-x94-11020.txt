DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

 

Immigration and Naturalization Service

8 CFR Part 274a

Powers and Duties of Service Officers; Availability of Service 
Records, Control of Employment of Aliens

CFR Correction

    In Title 8 of the Code of Federal Regulations, revised as of 
January 1, 1994, the text of the first sentence of Sec. 274a.12 (c)(10) 
is incorrect. It should appear as follows:


Sec. 274a.12  Classes of aliens authorized to accept employment.

* * * * *
    (c) * * *
    (10) An alien who has filed an application for suspension of 
deportation pursuant to part 244 of this chapter.
