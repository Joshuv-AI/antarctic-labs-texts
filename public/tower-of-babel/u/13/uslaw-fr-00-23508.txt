UFE Incorporated El Paso, TX; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 12, 2000, in response to a petition which was 
filed by UNITE on behalf of workers at UFE, Incorporated, El Paso, 
Texas.
    The signatories of the petition do not represent the workforce at 
the subject firm, either as a union or as a duly authorized 
representative. Thus, this investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 23rd day of August 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
