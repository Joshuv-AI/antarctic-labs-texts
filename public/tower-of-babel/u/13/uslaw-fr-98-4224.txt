FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-116, RM-8507, RM-8567]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Jefferson City, Cumberland Gap, 
Elizabethton, TN, and Jonesville, VA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Eaton P. Govan, III and Berton B. Cagle directed to the Report and 
Order in this proceeding. The Report and Order denied their proposals 
for a reallotment of a channel from Jefferson City to Cumberland Gap, 
Tennessee, and an upgrade at Elizabethton. Instead, the Report and 
Order allotted Channel 256A to Jonesville, Virginia. See 60 FR 57368, 
published November 15, 1995. With this action the proceeding is 
terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 20, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2177.
