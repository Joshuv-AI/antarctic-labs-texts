FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Acquisitions of Shares of a Bank 
or Bank Holding Company; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 2022-17925) published on 
page 51099 in the first column of the issue for Friday, August 19, 
2022.
    Under A. Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, entry 1 is corrected to 
read as follows:
    1. The Revocable Trust Agreement No. 060134, James O. Beavers, 
trustee, both of Taylorville, Illinois; to retain voting shares of 
First Bancorp of Taylorville, Inc., and thereby indirectly retain 
voting shares of First National Bank in Taylorville, Taylorville, 
Illinois, and First Security Bank, Mackinaw, Illinois.
    Comments on this application must be received by September 8, 2022.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
Ann E. Misback,
Secretary of the Board.
