SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    This collection is required by The Defense Production Act. This law 
requires United States firms to furnish information to the Department 
of Commerce regarding offset agreements exceeding $5,000,000 in value 
associated with sales of weapon system or defense-related items to 
foreign countries or foreign firms. Offsets are industrial or 
commercial compensation practices required as a condition of purchase 
in either government-to-government or commercial sales of defense 
articles and/or defense services as defense as defined by the Arms 
Export Control Act and the International Traffic in Arms Regulations. 
Such offsets are required by most major trading partners when 
purchasing U.S. military equipment or defense related items.
