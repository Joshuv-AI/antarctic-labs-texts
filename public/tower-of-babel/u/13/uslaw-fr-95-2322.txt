SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
[License #08-0002]


First Midwest Capital Corp.; Notice of License Surrender

    Notice is hereby given that First Midwest Capital Corporation 
(``FMCC''), has surrendered its license to operate as a small business 
investment company under the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, as 
amended (``the Act''). FMCC was licensed by the Small Business 
Administration on March 19, 1959.
    Under the authority vested by the Act and pursuant to the 
regulations promulgated thereunder, the surrender of the license was 
accepted on January 24, 1995, and accordingly, all rights, privileges, 
and franchises derived therefrom have been terminated.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59.011, Small 
Business Investment Companies)

Robert D. Stillman,
Associate Administrator for Investment.
