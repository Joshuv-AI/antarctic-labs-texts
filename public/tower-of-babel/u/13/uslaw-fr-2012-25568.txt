FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments on the agreements to the Secretary, Federal Maritime 
Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within ten days of the date this 
notice appears in the Federal Register. Copies of the agreements are 
available through the Commission's Web site (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of Agreements at (202)-523-5793 or 
[email protected].
    Agreement No.: 011502-005.
    Title: NYK/HUAL Space Charter and Cooperative Working Agreement.
    Parties: Hoegh Autoliners AS and Nippon Yusen Kaisha (``NYK'').
    Filing Party: Wayne R. Rohde, Esq.; Cozen O'Connor; 1627 I Street 
NW.; Suite 1100; Washington, DC 20006.
    Synopsis: The amendment clarifies that the agreement covers U.S. 
export trades only, adds Libya and Lebanon to the geographic scope of 
the agreement, and corrects the U.S. address of Hoegh Autoliners.

    Agreement No.: 011741-018.
    Title: U.S. Pacific Coast-Oceania Agreement.
    Parties: A.P. Moller-Maersk A/S; ANL Singapore PTE Ltd./CMA CGM 
S.A.; Hamburg-Sud; and Hapag-Lloyd AG.
    Filing Party: Wayne R. Rohde, Esq.; Cozen O'Connor; 1627 I Street 
NW., Suite 1100; Washington, DC 20006.
    Synopsis: The amendment would adjust the minimum and maximum size 
of vessels which can be deployed by the parties.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.

    Dated: October 12, 2012.
Rachel E. Dickon,
Assistant Secretary.
