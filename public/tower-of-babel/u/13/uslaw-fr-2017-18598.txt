DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board, Hanford

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of Cancellation of Open Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On August 21, 2017, the Department of Energy (DOE) published a 
notice of open meeting announcing a meeting on September 6-7, 2017, of 
the Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board, Hanford. 
This

[[Page 41617]]

notice announces the cancellation of this meeting.

DATES: The meeting scheduled for September 6-7, 2017, announced in the 
August 21, 2017, issue of the Federal Register (FR Doc. 2017-17600, 82 
FR 39572), is cancelled.

Richland, WA, 99352; Phone: (509) 376-5803; or Email: 
[email protected].
