Antidumping Investigation on Uranium from the Republic of 
Kazakhstan: Notice of Extension of Time for Briefs and Hearing

AGENCY: Import Administration, International Trade Administration, U.S.

date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 29, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: James C. Doyle or Juanita H. Chen, 
Enforcement Group III, Office VII, Import Administration, International 
Trade Administration, U.S. Department of Commerce, 1401 Constitution 
Avenue, N.W., Washington, DC 20230, telephone: (202) 482-0159 or (202) 
482-0409, respectively.

Extension of Time

    Pursuant to 19 CFR 353.36, the Department has determined that it is 
necessary to conduct verification of certain information in Kazakhstan 
during the period May 3, 1999 through May 9, 1999. As a result of this 
verification, the Department is granting an extension of time for the 
briefs. Case briefs are now due May 17, 1999, and rebuttal briefs are 
now due May 21,

[[Page 24138]]

1999. As a result of the extension of time for submission of briefs, 
the hearing date has also been extended and is now set for May 25, 
1999.

    Dated: April 29, 1999.
Roland L. MacDonald,
Acting Deputy Assistant Secretary, Enforcement Group III.
