DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 11727]


Determination Pursuant to Section 451 of the Foreign Assistance 
Act for the Use of Funds To Support South Sudan Peace Agreement 
Monitoring Mechanisms

    Pursuant to section 451 of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (the 
``Act'') (22 U.S.C. 2261), section 1-100(a)(1) of E.O. 12163 and 
Delegation of Authority 513, I hereby authorize, notwithstanding any 
other provision of law, the use of up to $3,000,000 made available to 
carry out provisions of the Act (other than the provisions of chapter 1 
of part I of the Act) to provide assistance authorized by part I of the 
Act to support countries that participate in the Reconstituted Joint 
Monitoring and Evaluation Commission and the Ceasefire and Transitional 
Security Arrangements Monitoring and Verification Mechanism in South 
Sudan.
    This Determination and the accompanying Memorandum of Justification 
shall be promptly reported to the Congress. This Determination shall be 
published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: February 24, 2022.
Brian P. McKeon,
Deputy Secretary of State for Management and Resources.
