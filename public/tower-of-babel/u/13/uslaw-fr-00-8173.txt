Radio Broadcasting Services; Polson, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a Petition for Rule Making filed by 
Mountain West Broadcasting requesting the allotment of Channel 259C3 at 
Polson, Montana. See 64 FR 8788, February 23, 1999. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
