Pacific Gas & Electric Company; Notice of Meetings

May 10, 2000.
    Take notice there will be a joint meeting of the Recreation and 
Ecological Resources subgroups of the Mokelumne Relicensing 
Collaborative on May 16, 2000. There will be meetings of the Ecological 
Resources subgroup on May 17-18, 2000, and a meeting of the Recreation 
subgroup on May 23, 2000. The Full Collaborative will meet on May 24-
25, 2000. These meetings will be held from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at 
2740 Gateway Oaks Drive, in Sacramento, California. Expected 
participants need to give their names to David Moller (PG&E) at (415) 
973-4696.
    For further information, please contact Diana Shannon at (202) 208-
7774.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
