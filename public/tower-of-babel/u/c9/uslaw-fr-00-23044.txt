SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3269; Amendment #5]


State of North Dakota

    In accordance with information received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended to 
expand the incident period for this disaster and to extend the deadline 
for filing applications for physical damage. The incident period is now 
established as beginning on April 5, 2000 and continuing through August 
12, 2000. The deadline for filing applications for physical damage as a 
result of this disaster has been extended from August 26, 2000 to 
September 22, 2000.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for economic injury is March 27, 2001.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: August 25, 2000.
James E. Rivera,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
