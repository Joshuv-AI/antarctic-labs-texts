FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
 

FCC Reestablishes Charter for Small Business Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice of Advisory Committee Renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the GSA Final Rule on Federal Advisory 
Committee Management, 41 CFR Secs. 101-106, the Federal Communications 
Commission is giving official notice of the reestablishment of the 
charter for the Small Business Advisory Committee. The term of the 
current charter is for a two-year period and expires on May 31, 1996.

DATES: May 31, 1994.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 1919 M Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION CONTACT: John Winston, Director, Office of 
Small Business Activities, Federal Communications Commission, at (202) 
632-1571.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Small Business Advisory Committee 
advises the FCC on small business issues, including minority and female 
participation issues, with regard to reviewing existing policies, 
recommending changes to policies as appropriate, and promoting new 
telecommunications services.

Federal Communications Commission.
William F. Caton,
Secretary.
