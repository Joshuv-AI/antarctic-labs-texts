Revisions to Electric Quarterly Report Filing Process; Notice of 
Deactivation of Sandbox Electronic Test Site

    On September 13, 2013, the Commission issued a notice in this 
proceeding extending the availability of the Sandbox Electronic Test 
Site (ETS) until further notice. Take notice that the ETS will be 
deactivated on April 13, 2017.
    Order No. 770 \1\ revised the process for making EQR filings. The 
ETS was made available to filers prior to the implementation of the 
revised process. Although the ETS will no longer be available after 
April 13, 2017, the Test-Only functionality in the filing system will 
continue to be available for filers to test their Electric Quarterly 
Reports (EQR) prior to submitting them to the Commission.
