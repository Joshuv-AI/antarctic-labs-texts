OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Review of a 
Revised Information Collection: RI 25-15

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of a revised information collection. RI 
25-15, Notice of Change in Student's Status, collects information from 
adult children of deceased Federal employees or annuitants to assure 
that the child continues to be eligible for payments from OPM.

[[Page 44248]]

    Approximately 2,500 certifications are processed annually. Each 
form takes approximately 30 minutes to complete. The annual estimated 
burden is 1,250 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact Mary Beth Smith-Toomey on 
(202) 606-8358, or E-mail to [email protected].
