DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
Geological Survey

 

LAC Mineral, Inc.; Contribution Acceptance

AGENCY: U.S. Geological Survey, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the U.S. Geological Survey has 
accepted from LAC Minerals (USA), Inc. a contribution of $7,500 to 
support geologic studies of mineral deposits, particularly the studies 
of samples from the El Indio district in Chile.

DATES: This notice is effective September 22, 1994.

ADDRESSES: Information on the work is available to the public upon 
request at the following location: U.S. Geological Survey, Branch of 
Central Mineral Resources, Box 25046, MS-935, Denver Federal Center, 
Denver, Colorado 80225.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Dr. Charles Thorman of the U.S. Geological Survey, Branch of Central 
Mineral Resources, at the address given above; telephone 303/236-5601.

Benjamin A. Morgan,
Chief Geologist.
