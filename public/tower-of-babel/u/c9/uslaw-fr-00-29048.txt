DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco and Firearms

[Notice No. 907]


Appointment of Individuals To Serve as Members of the Performance 
Review Board (PRB); Senior Executive Service

SUMMARY: Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), this notice announces the 
appointment of members of the Performance Review Board for the Bureau 
of Alcohol, Tobacco and Firearms (ATF) for the rating period beginning 
October 1, 1999, and ending September 30, 2000. This notice effects 
changes in the membership of the ATF PRB previously appointed November 
12, 1999 (64 FR 61688).
    The names and titles of the ATF PRB members are as follows:

John J. Manfreda, Chief Counsel, Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco and 
Firearms, Department of the Treasury
John Dooher, Director, Washington Office, Federal Law Enforcement 
Training Center, Department of the Treasury
H. Douglas Cunningham, Deputy Assistant Director, Office of

[[Page 68171]]

Administration, U.S. Secret Service, Department of the Treasury

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dennis Snyder, Personnel Division, 
Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco and Firearms, 650 Massachusetts Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20226; telephone (202) 927-8610.

    Signed: November 6, 2000.
Bradley A. Buckles,
Director.
