DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

National Institute of Corrections


Meeting Announcement

    A meeting of the Task Force on Prison Construction Standardization 
and Techniques will be held on August 17, 1996 at the Opryland Hotel, 
2800 Opryland Drive, Nashville, TN 37214. The meeting will convene in 
the Johnson Room, parts A & B, at 9:00 am and adjourn at 4:00 pm. The 
purpose of the meeting will be to update members on the progress to 
date on the detention construction monogram development. No part of the 
meeting will be closed.
    For more information contact Dee Halley, National Institute of 
Corrections, Academy Division, 1960 Industrial Circle, Suite A, 
Longmont, CO 80501; 1-800-995-6429 or fax 1-303-682-0469.
Larry Solomon,
Acting Director.
