National Institute on Drug Abuse; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Drug Abuse Special Emphasis Panel, February 1, 2008, 1 
p.m. to February 1, 2008, 5 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 6101

Federal Register on January 9, 2008, Volume 73, Number 6.

meeting is closed to the public.
