FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION


Notice of Meeting

May 8, 2002.
    The following notice of meeting is published pursuant to Section 
3(a) of the Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 
U.S.C. 552b:

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

DATE AND TIME: May 15, 2002 (30 Minutes Following Regular Commission 
Meeting).

PLACE: Room 2C, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Docket No. EL02-75-000, Duke Energy Trading 
and Marketing, L.L.C. v. Entergy Arkansas, Inc., Entergy Gulf States, 
Inc., Entergy Louisiana, Inc., EntergyMississippi, Inc., Entergy New 
Orleans, Inc. and Entergy Services, Inc.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Magalie R. Salas, Secretary, 
Telephone (202) 208-0400.
