FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the 
agreements to the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, 
Federal Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 
20573. Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received 
within 12 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
and the Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited

[[Page 51981]]

review. Copies of agreements are available through the Commission's 
website (www.fmc.gov) or by contacting the Office of Agreements at 
(202)-523-5793 or [email protected].
    Agreement No.: 201392.
    Agreement Name: Yang Ming Joint Service Agreement.
    Parties: Yang Ming Marine Transport Corporation; Yang Ming 
(Singapore) Pte. Ltd.; and Yang Ming (UK) Ltd.
    Filing Party: Josh Stein, Cozen O'Connor.
    Synopsis: The Agreement permits the parties to establish a joint 
service in the trades worldwide to and from the United States.
    Proposed Effective Date: 9/30/2022.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/FMC.Agreements.Web/Public/AgreementHistory/68502.

    Dated: August 19, 2022.
William Cody,
Secretary.
