DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. OA97-699-000]


Florida Power & Light Company; Notice of Filing

August 6, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 15, 1997, Florida Power & Light Company 
(FPL) tendered for filing its revised Open Access Transmission Tariff 
(Tariff) in compliance with the Commission's directive in Order No. 
888-A, issued on March 4, 1997 in Docket Nos. RM95-8-001 and RM94-7-
002. The Tariff supersedes FPL's currently effective open access 
transmission tariff filed on July 9, 1996, Docket No. OA96-39-000, in 
compliance with Order No. 888. FPL requests that the Tariff be made 
effective July 14, 1997.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest this filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before August 19, 1997. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceedings. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this application are on file with 
the Commission and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
