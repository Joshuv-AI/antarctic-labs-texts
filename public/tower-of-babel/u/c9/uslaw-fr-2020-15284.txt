Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-19-2020]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 77--Memphis, Tennessee, Authorization of 
Production Activity, ISK Biosciences Corporation (Agricultural 
Chemicals), Memphis, Tennessee

    On March 12, 2020, the City of Memphis, grantee of FTZ 77, 
submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ 
Board on behalf of ISK Biosciences Corporation, within Subzone 77I, in 
Memphis, Tennessee.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (85 FR 17310, March 27, 2020). On July 
10, 2020, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
Section 400.14.

    Dated: July 10, 2020.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
