Fiscal Service

31 CFR 210


Federal Government Participation in the Automated Clearing 
House--Extension of Public Comment Period

AGENCY: Financial Management Service, Fiscal Service, Department of the 
Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of extension of public comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On April 12, 2001, the Financial Management Service (FMS) 
published a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) proposing to revise 
the regulation, 31 CFR part 210, governing the use of the Automated 
Clearing House (ACH) system by Federal agencies (66 FR 18888). The 
proposed revisions address the conversion of checks to ACH debit 
entries at Federal agency points-of-purchase and at lockbox locations 
where payments to Federal agencies are sent. The proposed revisions 
also address the origination by agencies of ACH debit entries 
authorized over the Internet. FMS requested that public comments on the 
proposed revisions be submitted by July 11, 2001. In response to 
requests by several parties for an extension of the comment deadline, 
FMS is extending the comment period to July 31, 2001.

DATES: Comments will be accepted through July 31, 2001.
