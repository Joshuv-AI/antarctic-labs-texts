OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Review of a 
Revised Information Collection; RI 25-7

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and budget (OMB) a request for review of a revised information 
collection. RI 25-7, Martial Status Certification Survey, is used to 
determine whether windows, widowers, and former spouses receiving 
survivor annuities from OPM have remarried before reaching age 55 and, 
thus, are no longer eligible for benefits.
    Approximately 2,500 RI 25-7 forms are completed annually. The form 
takes approximately 15 minutes to complete. The annual estimated burden 
in 625 hours.
