Radio Broadcasting Services; East Harwich, Nantucket, and South 
Chatham, MA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Brewster Broadcasting 
Co. in its counterproposal to a petition for rulemaking by John 
Garabedian, allots Channel 254A at East Harwich, Massachusetts, as the 
community's first local FM service. Channel 254A can be allotted to 
East Harwich, Massachusetts, in compliance with the Commission's 
minimum distance separation requirements with a site restriction of 5.7 
km (3.5 miles) southeast of East Harwich. The coordinates for Channel 
254A at East Harwich, Massachusetts, are 41-40-33 North Latitude and 
69-58-03 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective April 4, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Deborah Dupont, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
