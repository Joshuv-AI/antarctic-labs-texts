National Library of Medicine; Cancellation of Meeting

of Medicine Special Emphasis Panel, July 11, 2007, 8:30 a.m. to July 
11, 2007, 5 p.m., Embassy Suites at the Chevy Chase Pavilion, 4300 
Military Road, NW., Washington, DC 20015 which was published in the
