DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration Drug Educational Forum; Public 
Workshop; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice of public workshop; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is correcting a notice 
that appeared in the Federal Register of February 3, 2005 (70 FR 5686). 
The document announced a public workshop. The document was published 
with a typographical error in the Supplementary Information section. 
This document corrects that error.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joyce Strong, Office of Policy (HF-
27), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 
20857, 301-827-7010.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. 05-2098, appearing on page 5686, 
in the Federal Register of Thursday, February 3, 2005, the following 
correction is made:
    1. On page 5687, in the second column, the fifth line from the 
bottom should read ``abbreviated new drug applications (ANDAs)''.

    Dated: February 8, 2005.
Jeffrey Shuren,
Assistant Commissioner for Policy.
