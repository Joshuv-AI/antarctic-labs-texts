DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Oregon Coast Provincial Advisory Committee Meeting

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Oregon Coast Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC) will meet 
on January 17, 2002. The meeting will begin at 9 a.m., in the Siuslaw 
River Room, at Siuslaw National Forest Headquarters, 4077 SW Research 
Way, Corvallis, OR. Agenda items will include: Grand Ronde Stewardship; 
2001 in Review; Focus on the Coming Year (theme, projects, topics); and 
Round Robin Information Sharing. A fifteen-minute public comment period 
is scheduled at 1:45 p.m. The committee welcomes the publics' written 
comments on committee business at any time. The meeting should end 
around 3:00 p.m. Interested citizens are encouraged to attend. Lunch 
will be on your own in Corvallis.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joni Quarnstrom, Public Affairs 
Specialist, Siuslaw National Forest, 541/750-7075 or write to Forest 
Supervisor, Siuslaw National Forest, P.O. Box 1148, Corvallis, OR 
97339.

    Dated: December 19, 2001.
Gloria D. Brown,
Forest Supervisor.
