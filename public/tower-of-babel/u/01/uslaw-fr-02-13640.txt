DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 4, 5 & 6 June 2002.
    Time of Meeting: 0730-1700, 4 June 2002; 0800-1700, 5 June 2002; 
0730-1630, 6 June 2002.
    Place: SAIC.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Study ``Ensuring the 
Financial Viability of the Objective Force'' will have a Plenary 
Meeting on 4, 5 and 6 June. The meeting will be held at SAIC, 4001 
Fairfax Drive, Arlington, VA. The Meeting will begin at 0730 hours 
on the 4th and end at approximately 1630 hours on June 6th.


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Major Robert Grier, 703-604-7478 or e-
mail: [email protected].

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
