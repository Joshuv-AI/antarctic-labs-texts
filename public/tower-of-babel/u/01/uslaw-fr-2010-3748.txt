DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Board of Scientific Advisors, March 8, 2010, 8 a.m. to 
March 9, 2010, 12 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 
Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal 
Register on January 20, 2010, 75FR3243.
    This Federal Register Notice is amended to change the meeting to a 
one day meeting to be held on March 8, 2010, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. The 
meeting is open to the public.

    Dated: February 18, 2010.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
