NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 05-060]


NASA Universe Exploration Strategic Roadmap Committee; Meeting by 
Telephone Conference

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, Public 
Law 92-463, as amended, the National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration announces a meeting by teleconference of the NASA 
Universe Exploration Strategic Roadmap Committee.

DATES: Friday, April 8, 2005, from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m., eastern standard 
time.
    Phone Number: Public Access Listen Only--1-800-358-0735, passcode 
8920561.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Michael Salamon, 202-358-0441.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting will be open to the public up to 
the line capacity of the conference telephone system.
    The agenda for the meeting is as follows:

--Discussion of draft interim strategic roadmap.

    It is imperative that the meeting be held on these dates to 
accommodate the scheduling priorities of the key participants.

    Dated: March 18, 2005.
P. Diane Rausch,
Advisory Committee Management Officer, National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration.
