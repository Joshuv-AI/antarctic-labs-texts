SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Salt Lake City Advisory Council Meeting Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Salt Lake City, Utah 
District Advisory Council will hold a public meeting on Tuesday, April 
23, 1996 at 9:00 a.m. at the Executive Board Room of First Interstate 
Bank, at 180 South Main Street, Salt Lake City, to discuss matters as 
may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business 
Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Stan Nakano, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 125 South State Street, 
Salt Lake City, Utah 84138, (801) 524-5804.

    Dated: March 22, 1996.
Bill Combs,
Associate Administrator for Office of Communication and Public Liaison.
