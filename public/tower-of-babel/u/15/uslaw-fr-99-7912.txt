Transport One Acid Spill Superfund Site; Mt. Vernon, Rockcastle 
County, KY; Notice of Proposed Settlement

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice of proposed settlement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under Section 122(h)(1) of the Comprehensive Environmental 
Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA), the Environmental 
Protection Agency (EPA) has proposed to settle claims for response 
costs at the Transport One Acid Spill Superfund Site (the ``Site'') 
located in Mt. Vernon, Rockcastle County, Kentucky with Transport One. 
EPA will consider public comments on the proposed settlement for thirty 
days. EPA may withdraw from or modify the proposed settlement should 
such comments disclose facts or considerations which indicate the 
proposed settlement is inappropriate, improper, or inadequate. Copies 
of the proposed settlement are available from: Ms. Paula V. Batchelor, 
U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, Region 4, Program Services 
Branch, Waste Management Division, 61 Forsyth Street, S.W., Atlanta, 
Georgia 30303, (404) 526-8887.
    Written comments may be submitted to Ms. Batchelor at the above 
address within 30 days of the date of publication.

    Dated: March 16, 1999.
James L. Miller,
Acting Chief, Program Services Branch, Waste Management Division.
