Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY HOLDING THE MEETING: United States International Trade 
Commission.

TIME AND DATE: November 19, 2001 at 2 p.m.

PLACE: Room 101, 500 E Street SW., Washington, DC 20436, Telephone: 
(202) 205-2000.

STATUS: Open to the public.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:
    1. Agenda for future meeting: none.
    2. Minutes.
    3. Ratification List.
    4. Inv. No. 731-TA-859 (Final)(Remand)(Certain Circular Seamless 
Stainless Steel Hollow Products from Japan)--briefing and vote. (The 
Commission is currently scheduled to transmit its views on remand to 
the Court of International Trade on December 3, 2001.)

not disposed of at the scheduled meeting, may be carried over to the 
agenda of the following meeting. Earlier announcement of this meeting 
was not possible.
