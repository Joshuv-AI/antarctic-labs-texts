Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 219; Authorization of Production 
Activity; Barco Stamping Co. Inc.; (Stamped Metal Products); Yuma, 
Arizona; Correction

18115), for the authorization of production activity for Barco Stamping 
Co. Inc., located in Yuma, Arizona, is corrected as follows:
    In the first paragraph, the location identified as ``Subzone 219B'' 
should read ``FTZ 219''.
