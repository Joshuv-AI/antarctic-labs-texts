DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On May 22, 2000, a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 65, No. 99, Page 32121, that an application had been filed with 
the Fish and Wildlife Service by Darrell W. Hindman, St. Louis, MO, for 
a permit (PRT-027204) to import a sport-hunted polar bear (Ursus 
maritimus) trophy, taken from the Northern Beaufort Sea polar bear 
population, Canada for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on August 9, 2000, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
    On July 7, 2000, a notice was published in the Federal Register,
