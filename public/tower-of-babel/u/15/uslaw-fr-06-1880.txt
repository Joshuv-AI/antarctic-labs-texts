National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, March

[[Page 10541]]

9, 2006, 8 a.m. to March 10, 2006, 5 p.m., Bethesda Marriott, 5151 
Pooks Hill Road, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal 
Register on January 24, 2006, 71FR3861.
    The meeting is amended to change the contact person from Timothy C. 
Meeker, M.D., Scientific Review Administrator (SRA) to Joyce C. Pegues, 
PhD, SRA, NCI, 6116 Executive Blvd., Rm 7149, Bethesda, MD 20892, (301) 
594-1286. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: Feb. 21, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
