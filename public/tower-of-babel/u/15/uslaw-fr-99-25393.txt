DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. CP99-94-000]


Florida Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Intent To Participate 
in Meeting on FGT Phase IV Project

September 24, 1999.
    The staff of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission will attend a 
meeting in the City of Maitland, Florida, at 10 a.m., on Thursday, 
September 30, 1999, at 601 South Lake Destiny Drive, Suite 450. The 
purpose of attending the meeting is to coordinate the review of 
information required for evaluating the effects of the above-referenced 
docket and project, with regard to federally listed and protected 
species. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and Florida Gas 
Transmission Company (FGT) will discuss the project status and species 
involved, and potential impacts and mitigation. The staff will use this 
information in preparing its biological assessment for the above-
referenced docket and project.
    For additional information, contact Mr. Paul McKee of the 
Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 208-1088.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary
