DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of OASIS How Working Group Meeting

April 28, 1999.
    Take notice that on May 24, 1999, from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and on 
May 25, 1999, from 8:00 AM to 12:00 noon, the OASIS How Working Group 
(How Group) will conduct its monthly meeting at the Commission's 
offices at 888 First Street, N.E., Room 3M-2A, Washington, D.C. 20426. 
The How Group is a voluntary industry group with a diverse membership 
that has made recommendations to the Commission on the Open Access 
Same-time Information System (OASIS) and related matters. It is 
expected that the How Group will discuss OASIS-related issues at the 
meetings. The meetings will be open to interested participants and the 
public.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
