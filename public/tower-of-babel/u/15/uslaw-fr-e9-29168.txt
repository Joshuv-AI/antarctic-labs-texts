Mitsubishi Electric Automotive America, Inc., Mason, OH; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated in response to a petition filed on June 8, 
2009 by a company official on behalf of workers of Mitsubishi Electric 
Automotive America, Inc., Mason, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 17th day of August 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
