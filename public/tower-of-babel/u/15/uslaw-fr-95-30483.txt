DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
Date of Meeting: 19 December 1995.
Time of Meeting: 0900-1700.
Place: Pentagon--Washington, DC.
Agenda: The Army Science Board (ASB) C4I Issue Group will meet to hear 
selected briefings relative to the study on ``A Strategy for Leveraging 
Commercial Technologies for Future Army Radios.'' This meeting will be 
open to the public. Any interested person may attend, appear before, or 
file statements with the committee at the time and in the manner 
permitted by the committee. For further information, please call 
Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
