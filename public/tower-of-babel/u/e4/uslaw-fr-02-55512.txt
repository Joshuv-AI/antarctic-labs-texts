DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Mines

30 CFR Chapter VI


Removal of CFR Chapter

    Effective April 26, 1996, the Bureau of Mines was terminated by 
Public Law 104-99, 110 Stat. 32. Therefore, the Office of the Federal 
Register is removing the Bureau of Mines regulations pursuant to its 
authority to maintain an orderly system of codification under 44 U.S.C. 
1510 and 1 CFR part 8.
    Accordingly, 30 CFR is amended by removing parts 601-652 and 
vacating Chapter VI.
