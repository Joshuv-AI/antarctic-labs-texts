DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Literature 
Selection Technical Review Committee, October 22-23, 2020, 8:30 a.m. to 
5:00 p.m., video assisted meeting, which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 5, 2020, 85 FR 151, Page 47391.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting dates from 
October 22-23, 2020 to October 21-22, 2020. This meeting will be a 
video assisted meeting. The videocast link is https://videocast.nih.gov/.

    Dated: August 13, 2020.
Ronald J. Livingston, Jr.,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
