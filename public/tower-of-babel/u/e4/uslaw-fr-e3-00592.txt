DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Public Utility District No. 2 of Grant Project No. P-2114-116; 
County Washington; Errata Notice

December 12, 2003.
    On November 3, 2003, the Commission issued a ``Notice of 
Application Tendered for Filing with the Commission, Soliciting 
Additional Study Requests, and Establishing Procedural Schedule for 
Relicensing and a Deadline for Submission of Final Amendments'' in the 
above-referenced proceeding.
    Item ``1.'' of the referenced Notice read ``Deadline for filing 
additional study requests and requests for agency cooperating status: 
December 22, 2003.'' The date should have been: December 29, 2003.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
