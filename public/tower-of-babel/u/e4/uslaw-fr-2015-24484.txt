DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 23, 2015, 8:30 a.m. 
to October 23, 2015, 5:00 p.m., Courtyard by Marriott, 5520 Wisconsin 
Avenue, Chevy Chase, MD, 20815 which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 17, 2015, 80 FR 55866.
    The meeting has been cancelled due to the reassignment of 
applications.
