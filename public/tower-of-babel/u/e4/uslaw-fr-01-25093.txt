NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Committee Management; Renewal

    The NSF Management Official having responsibility for the DOE/NSF 
Nuclear Science Advisory Committee (#1176) has determined that renewing 
this

[[Page 51074]]

committee for another two years is necessary and in the public interest 
in connection with the performance of duties imposed upon the Director, 
National Science Foundation (NSF), by 42 U.S.C. 1 et seq. This 
determination follows consultation with the Committee Management 
Secretariat, General Services Administration.
    Authority for this committee will expire on October 1, 2003, unless 
it is renewed. For more information, please contact Susanne Bolton, 
NSF, at 703/292-7488.

    Dated: October 2, 2001.
Susanne Bolton,
Committee Management Officer.
