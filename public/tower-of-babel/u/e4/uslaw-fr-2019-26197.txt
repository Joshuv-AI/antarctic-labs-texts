DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL20-9-000]


Oxbow Creek Energy LLC; Notice of Institution of Section 206 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On November 26, 2019, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL20-9-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2018), instituting an investigation into whether Oxbow 
Creek Energy LLC's rate are just and reasonable. Oxbow Creek Energy 
LLC, 169 FERC ] 61,162 (2019).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL20-9-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.
    Any interested person desiring to be heard in Docket No. EL20-9-000 
must file a notice of intervention or motion to intervene, as 
appropriate, with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street NE, Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Rule 214 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure, 18 CFR 385.214 (2019), 
within 21 days of the date of issuance of the order.

    Dated: November 27, 2019.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
