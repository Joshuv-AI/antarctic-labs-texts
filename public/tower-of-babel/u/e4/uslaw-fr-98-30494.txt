FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 98-115; RM-9292]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Stevensville, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 283A to Stevensville, Montana, in 
response to a petition filed by L. Topaz Enterprises, Inc. See 63 FR 
38786, July 20, 1998. The coordinates for Channel 283A at Stevensville 
are 46-30-24 and 114-05-18. Canadian concurrence has been obtained for 
this allotment. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 21, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
