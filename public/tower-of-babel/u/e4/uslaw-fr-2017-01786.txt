DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of the Commission's staff may attend the following 
meetings related to the wholesale markets of ISO New England Inc.:
    Integrating Markets and Public Policy:

January 25, 2017, 9:30 a.m.-4:00 p.m. (EST), Doubletree Hotel, 5400 
Computer Drive, Westborough, MA 01581
February 16, 2017, 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. (EST), Doubletree Hotel, 5400 
Computer Drive, Westborough, MA 01581

    Further information may be found at www.nepool.com/IMAPP.php.
