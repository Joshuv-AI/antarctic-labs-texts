SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Providence Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Providence District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Friday, March 24, 1995 at 8:00 
a.m. at the Providence Marriott, Charles at Orms Streets, Providence, 
Rhode Island 02904 to discuss matters as may be presented by members, 
staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Mr. Joseph P. Loddo, 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 380 Westminster 
Street, Providence, Rhode Island 02903, (401) 528-4580.

    Dated: March 10, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
