FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Technological Advisory Council Federal Advisory Committee 
Websites

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This announcement advises interested persons that publicly 
accessible websites have been established for members of the 
Technological Advisory Council (``Council'') of the Federal 
Communications Commission. Members of the Council will provide their 
views on a continuing basis at the Internet websites listed below.

Focus Groups

Spectrum Management--http://www.jacksons.net/tac
Network Access--http://www.fcc.ini.cmu.edu/FCC/index.html
Accessibility for Disabled Persons--http://trace.wisc.edu/docs/fccadv/
disability.htm

Washington, D.C. 20554; telephone (202) 418-2478.
