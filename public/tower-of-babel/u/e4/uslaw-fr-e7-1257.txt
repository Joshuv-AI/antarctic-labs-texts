SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board Public 
Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA), National Small 
Business Development Center (SBDC) Advisory Board will hold a public 
annual spring meeting on Monday, March 5, 2007, starting at 11 a.m. 
until 4 p.m. (EST). The meeting will take

[[Page 3902]]

place at the SBA Office of Entrepreneurial Development Conference Room, 
409 Third Street, SW., 6th Floor, Washington, DC 20416.
    The purpose of the meeting is to meet and welcome new board 
members; and to facilitate board briefings with the SBA Administrator, 
Senior staff, and SBA program offices. Anyone wishing to attend must 
contact Erika Fischer, Senior Program Analyst, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Office of Small Business Development Centers, 409 3rd 
Street, SW., Washington, DC 20416, telephone (202) 205-7045 or fax 
(202) 481-0681.

Matthew Teague,
Committee Management Officer.
