Preparing and Auditing Direct Loan and Loan Guarantee Subsidies 
Under the Federal Credit Reform Act

AGENCY: Office of Management and Budget.

ACTION: Notice of document availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This Notice indicates the availability of the third Financial 
Accounting and Auditing Technical Release, ``Preparing and Auditing 
Direct Loan and Loan Guarantee Subsidies under the Federal Credit 
Reform Act.'' The Federal Accounting Standards Advisory Board cleared 
the technical release on July 31, 1999. Technical Release No. 3 is 
available on Financenet at http://www.financenet.gov/financenet/fed/aapc/technicl.htm.

ADDRESSES: Copies of Technical Release No. 3 may be obtained free of 
charge from the Federal Accounting Standards Advisory Board, 441 G 
Street NW, Mailstop 6K17V, Washington, DC 20548 (telephone 202-512-
7350).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Zavada (telephone: 202-395-
3993), Office of Federal Financial Management, Office of Management and 
Budget, 725 17th Street, N.W., Room 6025, Washington, DC 20503.
