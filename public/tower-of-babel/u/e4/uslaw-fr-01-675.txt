DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board; Notice of Change of Advisory Committee 
Meeting

SUMMARY: The Defense Science Board (DSB) Task Force on High Energy 
Laser Weapon Systems Applications originally planned to meet January 
23-24, 2001; however, the meeting has been rescheduled for January 25-
26, 2001. The meeting will be held at Strategic Analysis Inc., 3601 
Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22201.

    Dated: January 4, 2001.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
