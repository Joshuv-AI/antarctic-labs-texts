Notice of Issuance of TR 20, Implementation Guidance for Leases

AGENCY: Federal Accounting Standards Advisory Board.

Advisory Board (FASAB) has issued Technical Release (TR) 20, 
Implementation Guidance for Leases.

FASAB at (202) 512-7350.

Director, 441 G Street NW, Suite 1155, Washington, DC 20548, or call
