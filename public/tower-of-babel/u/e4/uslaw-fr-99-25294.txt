DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-35,592]


North American Refractories Company, Womelsdorf, Pennsylvania; 
Dismissal of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at the North American Refractories 
Company, Womelsdorf, Pennsylvania. The application contained no new 
substantial information which would bear importantly on the 
Department's

[[Page 52544]]

determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-35,592; North American Refractories, Womelsdorf, Pennsylvania 
(September 20, 1999).

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 22nd day of September, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
