OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Public Hearing in Conjunction 
with each Board meeting was published in the Federal Register (Volume 
69, Number 194, Page 60196) on October 7, 2004. No requests were 
received to provide testimony or submit written statements for the 
record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in conjunction with OPIC's 
November 10, 2004 Board of Directors meeting scheduled for 2 p.m. on 
November 4, 2004 has been cancelled.

CONTACT FOR INFORMATION: Information on the hearing cancellation may be 
obtained from Connie M. Downs at (202) 336-8438, via facsimile at (202) 
218-0136, or via e-mail at [email protected].
