DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Clinical 
Aging Review Committee, September 26, 2019, 12:00 p.m. to September 27, 
2019, 12:00 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro Center, 
7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published in the 
Federal Register on July 31, 2019, 84 FR 37328.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the time of the meeting 
from 12:00 p.m.-12:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m.-1:00 p.m. The meeting is closed 
to the public.

    Dated: September 27, 2019.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
