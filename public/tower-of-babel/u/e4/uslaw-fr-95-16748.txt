DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


AC No. 20-AIR-DU; Proposed Advisory Circular (AC) on Voluntary 
Industry Distributor/Dealer Accreditation Program

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of availability of proposed AC.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The guidance material in this AC describes voluntary programs 
in which distributors and dealers of civil aircraft parts can obtain 
accreditation of quality control systems, which would assure that the 
approval status of their parts is properly documented.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before August 4, 1995.

ADDRESSES: Send all comments and requests for copies of the proposed AC 
to: Federal Aviation Administration, Aircraft Maintenance Division, 
Attention: AFS-350, 800 Independence Avenue SW., Washington, DC 20591.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Richard E. Nowak, AFS-350, at the above address; telephone: (202) 267-
7228 (8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. EDT).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This proposed AC describes voluntary 
programs in which distributors and dealers of civil aircraft parts can 
obtain accreditation of quality control systems, which would assure 
that the approval 

[[Page 35447]]
status of their parts is properly documented. The guidance material 
contained in this AC would assist civil aviation parts distributor/
dealers in the conduct of parts distribution.

    Issued in Washington, D.C., on June 30, 1995.
