Utah Stamping Company, Clearfield, UT; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 4, 2009, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Utah Stamping Company, 
Clearfield, Utah.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 26th day of March 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
