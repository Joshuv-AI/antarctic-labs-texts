FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 95-99; RM-8612]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Buffalo Gap, VA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of D.J. Broadcasting, Inc., 
licensee of Station WKDE(FM), Channel 288A, Altavista, Virginia, and 
Ridle Radio, Inc., licensee of Station WZXI(FM), Channel 288A, Buffalo 
Gap, Virginia, substitutes Channel 238A for Channel 288A at Buffalo Gap 
and modifies Station WZXI(FM)'s license accordingly. See 60 FR 33388, 
June 28, 1995. Channel 238A can be allotted to Buffalo Gap in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements and can be used at the transmitter site specified in 
Station WZXI(FM)'s license. The coordinates for Channel 238A at Buffalo 
Gap are 38-10-55 and 79-13-34. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 13, 1995.
