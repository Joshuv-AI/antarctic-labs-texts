Drawbridge Operation Regulations; Manitowoc River, WI

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Direct final rule; confirmation of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 27868]]

SUMMARY: On March 6, 2001, we published a direct final rule (66 FR 
13433). This direct final rule notified the public of our intent to 
revise the operating regulations governing the Eighth Street bridge 
(mile 0.29), Tenth Street bridge (mile 0.43), and the Wisconsin Central 
(formerly Soo Line) bridge (mile 0.91), on the Manitowoc River. The 
direct final rule re-established the schedule published in 1983 that 
was erroneously removed by another rule in 1984. We have not received 
an adverse comment or notice of intent to submit adverse comment on 
this rule. Therefore, this rule will go into effect as scheduled.

DATES: The effective date of the direct final rule is confirmed as June 
4, 2001.
