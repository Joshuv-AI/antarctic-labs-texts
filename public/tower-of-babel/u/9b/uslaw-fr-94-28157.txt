United States Enrichment Corporation

Board of Directors
TIME AND DATE: 8:00 a.m., Tuesday, November 15, 1994.

Maryland 20817.

STATUS: Part of this meeting will be open to the public. The balance of

     Consideration of personnel issues of the Corporation.

Portions Closed to the Public

     Review of commercial and/or financial issues of the
