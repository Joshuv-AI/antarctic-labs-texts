National Boating Safety Advisory Council; Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Secretary of Transportation has renewed the charter for 
the National Boating Safety Advisory Council (NBSAC) to remain in 
effect for a period of 2 years from December 20, 1998 until December 
20, 2000. NBSAC is a federal advisory committee constituted under 5 
U.S.C. App. 2. Its purpose is to provide advice and make 
recommendations to the Coast Guard on regulations and other major 
recreational boating safety matters.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For questions on this notice, contact 
Mr. Albert J. Marmo, Executive Director of NBSAC, telephone 202-267-
0950, fax 202-267-4285. For questions on viewing the docket, contact 
Dorothy Walker, Chief, Dockets, Department of Transportation, 202-366-
9329.
