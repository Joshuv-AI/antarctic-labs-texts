OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


July 10, 2003 Public Hearing; Sunshine Act

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (Volume 68, Number 121, Page 37593) on June 24, 
2003. No request were received to provide testimony or submit written 
statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in 
conjunction with OPIC's July 17, 2003 Board of Directors meeting 
scheduled for 2 PM on July 10, 2003 has been cancelled.
