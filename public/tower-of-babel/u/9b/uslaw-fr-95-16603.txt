DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
U.S. Geological Survey


Calista Corp.

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the U.S. Geological Survey is 
planning to enter into a Cooperative Research and Development Agreement 
(CRADA) with Calista Corporation, an Alaska Native regional 
corporation. The purpose of the CRADA is to conduct geologic mapping 
and geochemical sampling in the Holy Cross A-4 and A-5 quadrangles on 
both Calista land and adjoining Federal land. Any other organizations 
interested in pursuing the possibility of a CRADA for similar kinds of 
activities should contact the U.S. Geological Survey.

DATE: This notice is effective July 6, 1995.

ADDRESS: Information on the proposed CRADA is available to the public 
upon request at the following location: U.S. Geological Survey, Branch 
of Alaskan Geology, 4200 University Drive, Anchorage, Alaska 99508-
4667.
