FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 00-1675; MM Docket No. 99-241; RM-9480]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Stamps and Fouke, AR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a request on behalf of In Phase Broadcasting, 
Inc., permittee of Station KLMZ, Channel 282A, Stamps, Arkansas, this 
document reallots Channel 282A to Fouke, Arkansas, and modifies the 
authorization for Station KLMZ accordingly, pursuant to the provisions 
of Section 1.420(i) of the Commission's Rules. See 64 FR 37924, July 
14, 1999. Coordinates used for Channel 282A at Fouke are 33-15-42 NL 
and 93-53-06 WL.

DATES: Effective September 11, 2000.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
