DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Office of the Secretary
[Order 94-2-30]

 

Fitness Determination of Island Air Charters, Inc.

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice of Commuter Air Carrier Fitness Determination--Order to 
Show Cause.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is proposing to find Island 
Air Charters, Inc., fit, willing, and able to provide commuter air 
service under section 419(e) of the Federal Aviation Act.

RESPONSES: All interested persons wishing to respond to the Department 
of Transportation's tentative fitness determination should file their 
responses with the Air Carrier Fitness Division, P-56, Department of 
Transportation, 400 Seventh Street SW., room 6401, Washington, DC 
20590, and serve them on all persons listed in Attachment A to the 
order. Responses shall be filed no later than March 7, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Carol Woods, Air Carrier Fitness 
Division (P-56, room 6401), U.S. Department of Transportation, 400 
Seventh Street SW., Washington, DC 20590, (202) 366-2340.

DATED: February 18, 1994.
Patrick V. Murphy,
Acting Assistant Secretary for Policy and International Affairs.
