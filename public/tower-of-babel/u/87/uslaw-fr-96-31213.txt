DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Final Public Meeting on Electricity Restructuring

AGENCY: Office of Policy, U.S. Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of public meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On November 8, 1996, the U.S. Department of Energy announced 
two additional public meetings to solicit input from affected 
constituencies before formulating the Department's recommendation 
respecting electric industry restructuring (61 FR 57858). This 
announcement details the location of the southeast regional public 
meeting in Atlanta, GA. The last of four public meetings, the Atlanta 
meeting will provide an opportunity to revisit issues already covered 
as well as new ones such as research and development, the federal role 
in power marketing, and tax issues. Participants will be allowed to 
address other topics pertaining to electric industry restructuring.

DATE: December 12, 1996.

ADDRESS: Atlanta Hilton and Towers, 255 Courtland Street, NE, Atlanta, 
Georgia, 404.659.2000.
