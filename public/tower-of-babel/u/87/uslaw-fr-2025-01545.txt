Self-Regulatory Organizations; New York Stock Exchange LLC; NYSE 
American LLC; NYSE Arca, Inc.; NYSE Chicago, Inc.; NYSE National, Inc.; 
Notice of Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend the Connectivity 
Fee Schedule

January 16, 2025.
    On December 17, 2024, New York Stock Exchange LLC, NYSE American 
LLC, NYSE Arca, Inc., NYSE Chicago, Inc., and NYSE National, Inc. (the 
``Exchanges'') each filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission 
(``Commission''), pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities 
Exchange Act of 1934 \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule 
change to amend the connectivity fee schedule to add fees for 
connectivity from the Mahwah Data Center to one or more trading floors. 
The proposed rule changes were published for comment in the Federal 
Register on January 3, 2025.\3\

    On December 31, 2024, the Exchanges withdrew the proposed rule 
changes (SR-NYSE-2024-81, SR-NYSEAMER-2024-80, SR-NYSEARCA-2024-113, 
SR-NYSECHX-2024-37, SR-NYSENAT-2024-33).
