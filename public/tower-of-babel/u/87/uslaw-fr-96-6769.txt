[Project No. 3083-072 and 3083-078]


Oklahoma Municipal Power Authority; Notice Shortening Comment 
Period

March 15, 1996.
    On March 14, 1996, the Oklahoma Municipal Power Authority (OMPA) 
field an offer of settlement in the above-captioned together with an 
unopposed request for shortening of the comment period under Rule 
602(f)(2) of the Commission's Rule of Practice and Procedure, 18 CFR 
385.602(f)(2). OMPA requests shortened comment periods in order to 
facilitate Commission action on the offer by May 1, 1996, when OMPA is 
required to begin monitoring and managing its hydroelectric electric 
operations to maintain water quality standards for dissolved oxygen 
under its previously approved water quality plan. OMPA requests that 
the comment period be shortened to March 29, 1996 (15 days) for 
original comments, and to April 5, 1996 (7 days) for reply comments. 
For good cause shown, the comment periods are shortened as requested.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
