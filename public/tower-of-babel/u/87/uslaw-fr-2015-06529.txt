California Independent System Operator Corporation; Notice of 
FERC Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on March 18, 2015 members of its staff will attend the 
California Independent System Operator's (CAISO) Market Performance and 
Planning Forum. The agenda and other documents for the meeting are 
available on CAISO's Web site, www.caiso.com.
    Sponsored by CAISO, the meeting is open to all market participants 
and staff's attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach 
efforts. The meeting may discuss matters at issue in the above 
captioned dockets.

[email protected] (916) 294-0322.
