NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 00-037]


Notice of Prospective Copyright License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that Knowledge Technologies, Inc., of 
Miami, FL, has applied for an exclusive copyright license in North, 
Central and South America, to ARC-15008, ``Postdoc,'' which is assigned 
to the United States of America as represented by the Administrator of 
the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections 
to the prospective grant of a license should be sent to Ames Research 
Center.

DATE: Responses to this notice must be received by June 26, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Padilla, Patent Counsel, NASA 
Ames Research Center, M/S 202A-3, Moffett Field, CA 94035-1000, (650) 
604-5104.

    Dated: April 21, 2000.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
