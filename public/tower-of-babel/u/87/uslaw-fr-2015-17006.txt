Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2015-0945.
    Petitioner: Matthew Gerlitzki.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  121.436 (a)(3) and (c).
    Description of Relief Sought: Mr. Matthew Gerlitzki seeks relief 
from Sec.  121.436 (a)(3) and (c) to allow his military pilot in 
command flight time in rotary turbine-powered aircraft to be credited 
toward the 1000 hours of experience required by Sec.  121.436 (a)(3). 
Section Sec.  121.436 (c) states that a pilot may credit 500 hours of 
military flight time obtained as pilot in command of a multiengine 
turbine-powered, fixed wing airplane in an operation requiring more 
than one pilot.
