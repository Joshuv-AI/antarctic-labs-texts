Sunshine Act Meeting

the Export-Import Bank of the United States.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

be held at Ex-Im Bank in Room 1143, 811 Vermont Avenue NW., Washington, 
DC 20571.

for Item No. 1 only.

FURTHER INFORMATION: For further information, contact: Office of the 
Secretary, 811 Vermont Avenue NW., Washington, DC 20571 (202) 565-3336.
