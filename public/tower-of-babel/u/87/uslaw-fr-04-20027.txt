OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Public Hearing

September 2, 2004.
    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (Volume 69, Number 152, Page 48257) on August 9, 
2004. No requests were received to provide testimony or submit written 
statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in 
conjunction with OPIC's September 9, 2004 Board of Directors meeting 
scheduled for 10 a.m. on September 9, 2004 has been cancelled.
