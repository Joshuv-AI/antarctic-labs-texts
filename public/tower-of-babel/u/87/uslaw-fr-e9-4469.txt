DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

(Docket 65-2008)


Foreign-Trade Zone 207 Richmond, Virginia, Withdrawal of Request 
for Subzone Status, Qimonda North America Corporation, Sandston, 
Virginia

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the application of the 
Capital Region Airport Commission, grantee of FTZ 207, requesting 
special-purpose subzone status on behalf of Qimonda North America 
Corporation in Sandston, Virginia. The application was filed on 
December 2, 2008 (73 FR 76613, 12/17/2008).
    The case has been closed without prejudice.

    Dated: February 24, 2009.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
