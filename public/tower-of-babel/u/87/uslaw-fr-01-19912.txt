DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 45-99]


Withdrawal of Application for Subzone Status for J. Baker, Inc.; 
(Distribution of Apparel, Footwear and Accessories) Foreign-Trade Zone 
27--Boston, MA (Canton, MA)

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the application 
submitted by the Massachusetts Port Authority, grantee of FTZ 27, 
Boston, MA, requesting special-purpose subzone status for the apparel, 
footwear and accessories warehousing/distribution facilities of J. 
Baker, Inc., located in Canton, MA. The application was filed on 
September 13, 1999 (64 FR 49440, 9/13/99).
    The withdrawal was requested because of changed circumstances, and 
the case has been closed without prejudice.

    Dated: July 31, 2001.
Dennis Puccinelli,
Executive Secretary.
