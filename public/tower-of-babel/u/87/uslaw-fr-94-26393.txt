FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-75; RM-8483]

 

Radio Broadcasting Services; Casper, WY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Bruce L. Erickson, allots 
Channel 247A at Casper, Wyoming, as that community's fifth local 
commercial FM transmission service. See 59 FR 37456, July 22, 1994. 
Channel 247A can be allotted to Casper in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without the 
imposition of a site restriction. The coordinates for Channel 247A at 
Casper are North Latitude 42-50-48 and West Longitude 106-18-48. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 5, 1994. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 247A at Casper, Wyoming, will open on December 
5, 1994, and close on January 5, 1995.
