Heritage Sportswear Marion, South Carolina; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 12, 2001, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Heritage Sports-wear, Marion, South 
Carolina.
    The petitioners were separated from the subject firm more than a 
year prior to the postmark date of the petition February 24, 2001. 
Section 223(b)(1) of the Trade Act of 1974 specifies that no 
certification may apply to any worker whose last separation occurred 
more than a year before the date of the petition. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 12th day of April 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
