SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Introduction

    Pursuant to 10 CFR 2.106, the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission 
(NRC) is providing notice of the issuance of Amendment 51 to Special 
Nuclear Materials License SNM-124 to Nuclear Fuel Services, Inc. (NFS) 
authorizing the possession and use of special nuclear material in the 
Blended Low-Enriched Uranium Oxide Conversion Building and Effluent 
Processing Building at the licensee's site in Erwin, Tennessee. NFS' 
request for the proposed action was previously noticed in the Federal 
Register on December 24, 2003 (68 FR 74653), along with a notice of 
opportunity to request a hearing.
    This amendment complies with the standards and requirements of the 
Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended, and NRC's rules and regulations 
as set forth in 10 CFR chapter 1. Accordingly, this amendment was 
issued on July 30, 2004, and was effective immediately.
