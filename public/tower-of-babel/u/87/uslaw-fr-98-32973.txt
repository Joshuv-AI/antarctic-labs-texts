FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY

Puerto Rico; Amendment No. 5 to Notice of a Major Disaster 
Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, (FEMA-1247-DR), dated September 24, 1998, 
and related determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 20, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Madge Dale, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In a letter dated November 20, 1998, the 
President amended his initial declaration letter to reflect the 
incident period for this disaster as September 20, 1998, through and 
including October 27, 1998.
