DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Forest Service


Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee will meet on 
September 19 and September 20, 1996 at the Tree House Inn Conference 
Room, I-5 and Lake Street, Mt. Shasta, California. The meeting on 
Thursday, September 19, will begin at 9:30 a.m. and continue until 5:00 
p.m. The meeting on Friday, September 20 is a field trip and will 
convene at 8:00 a.m., leaving from the Tree House Inn. The field trip 
will adjourn at approximately 3:30 p.m. Agenda items to be covered 
include: (1) information on a proposed social assessment contract for 
the 9 northern counties of California; (2) a report on the findings 
from the implementation monitoring task group; (3) a salvage 
subcommittee report; (4) a report on the 3 PIEC meeting; (5) standing 
committee reports; and (6) public comment periods. All PAC meetings are 
open to the public. Interested citizens are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:

Yreka, California 96097; telephone 916-842-6131, (FTS) 700-467-1309.
