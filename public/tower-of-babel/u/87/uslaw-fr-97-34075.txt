NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Membership on the Executive Resources Board

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Appointment to the Executive Resources Board for the Senior 
Executive Service.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has announced the 
following appointments to the NRC Executive Resources Board. The 
Executive Resources Board is responsible for providing institutional 
continuity in executive personnel management by overseeing NRC's Senior 
Executive Service (SES) and Senior Level System (SLS) succession 
planning, merit staffing, and position management activities.
