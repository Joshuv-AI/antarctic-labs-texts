DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-13-2021]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 72--Indianapolis, Indiana; Authorization 
of Production Activity; XPO Logistics (Wearable Electronic 
Communication/Data Device Kitting); Clayton, Indiana

    On February 18, 2021, XPO Logistics submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility within 
FTZ 72, in Clayton, Indiana.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (86 FR 11921, March 1, 2021). On June 
21, 2021, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
Section 400.14.

    Dated: June 21, 2021.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
