FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement(s) Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement(s) under the Shipping Act of 1984.
    Interested parties can review or obtain copies of agreements at the 
Washington, DC offices of the Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, 
N.W., Room 962. Interested parties may submit comments on an agreement 
to the Secretary, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, 
within 10 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 217-011317-003.
    Title: PONL/BHP-IMTL Space Charter Agreement.
    Parties: P&O Nedlloyd Limited (``PONL'') BHP-IMTL.
    Synopsis: The proposed Agreement modification (1) substitutes P&O 
Nedlloyd Limited for its commonly-owned affiliate, P&O Nedlloyd B.V. 
(formerly named Nedlloyd Lijnen BV) as party to the Agreement; (2) 
changes the name of the Agreement to reflect the foregoing 
substitution; (3) deletes U.S. Atlantic and Gulf ports, as well as the 
ports in New Zealand, Chile, Peru, and Panama from the scope of the 
Agreement; and (4) makes other non-substantial changes to the 
Agreement.

    Dated: May 12, 1998.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
