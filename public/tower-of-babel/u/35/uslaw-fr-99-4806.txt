DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP98-404-002]


Mississippi River Transmission Corporation; Notice of Motion for 
Reconsideration

February 22, 1999.
    Take notice that on January 22, 1999, the Missouri Public Service 
Commission (MoPSC), tendered for filing a motion for reconsideration of 
the Director's December 22, 1998, letter order in this proceeding. 
Although MoPSC styled its filing as a request for rehearing, the filing 
was not made within the required 30 days of the date of order issuance. 
Accordingly, the filing will be treated as a motion for reconsideration 
rather than a request for rehearing.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
