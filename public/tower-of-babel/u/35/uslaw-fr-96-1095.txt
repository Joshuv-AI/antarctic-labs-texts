THE COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


National Capital Arts and Cultural Affairs 1996 Grant Program

    Though the 1996 budget is not complete, it seems prudent to begin a 
process which would allow the NCACA Program to be prepared if and when 
an appropriation is made. There is no guarantee that the program will 
be able to proceed, nor that funds will be available for grants. If 
your organization is interested in receiving an application package, 
however, please request one in writing.
    Questions can be referred to Don Myer on the Commission staff at 
202-504-2200.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
