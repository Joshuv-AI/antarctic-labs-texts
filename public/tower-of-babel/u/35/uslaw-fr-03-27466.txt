POSTAL SERVICE

39 CFR Part 111


Sender-Identified Mail: Enhanced Requirement for Discount Rate 
Mailings

AGENCY: Postal Service.

ACTION: Proposed rule; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The proposal to require enhanced sender identification for all 
discount rate mailings published in the Federal Register on October 21, 
2003 (Vol. 68, No. 203, pages 60052-60054), is withdrawn.

DATES: This notice is effective upon publication.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joel Walker (703) 292-3652.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Postal Service will issue a further 
notice regarding this proposal at a later date.
