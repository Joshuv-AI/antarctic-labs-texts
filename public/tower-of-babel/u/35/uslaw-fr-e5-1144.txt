G & H Machine Company, Inc., Kinzee, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 9, 2005, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at G & H Machine Company, Inc., 
Kinzee, Pennsylvania.
    The petition regarding the investigation contained no company 
contact information and has therefore been deemed invalid. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of February 2005.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
