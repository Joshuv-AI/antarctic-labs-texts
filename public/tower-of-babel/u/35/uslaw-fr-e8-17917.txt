FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 08-1630; MB Docket No. 08-134; RM-11466]


Television Broadcasting Services; Bismarck, ND

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a channel substitution 
proposed by KBMY-KBCY, LLC (``KBMY''), the licensee of KBMY(TV), analog 
channel 17, and KBMY-DT, DTV channel 16, Bismarck, North Dakota. KBMY 
requests the substitution of DTV channel 17, for DTV channel 16 at 
Bismarck.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 4, 2008, and reply 
comments on or before September 19, 2008.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Office of the Secretary, 
445 12th Street, SW., TW-A325, Washington, DC 20554. In addition to 
filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should serve counsel 
for petitioner as follows: John M. Burgett, Esq., Wiley Rein LLP, 117 K 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20006.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Brown, [email protected], 
Media Bureau, (202) 418-1600.
