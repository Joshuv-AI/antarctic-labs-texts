DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment Standards Administration


Correction

AGENCY: Employment Standards Administration, Labor.

SUMMARY: In notice document 95-29334 beginning on page 61712 in the 
issue of Friday, December 1, 1995, make the following correction.
    On page 61713, right hand column, the OMB clearance number for 
forms WH-2, WH-205, WH-226-MIS, and WH-226A-MIS is listed as 1215-0158. 
This should be changed to 1215-0005.

    Dated: December 14, 1995.
Margaret J. Sherrill,
Chief, Branch of Management, Review and Analysis, Division of Financial 
Management, Office of Management, Administration and Planning 
Employment Standards Administration.
