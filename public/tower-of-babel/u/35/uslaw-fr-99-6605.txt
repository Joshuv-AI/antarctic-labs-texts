Patents Available for Licensing

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of the general 
availability of exclusive or partially exclusive licenses under the 
following patents. Any license granted shall comply with 35 U.S.C. 209 
and 37 CFR part 404. Applications will be evaluated utilizing the 
following criteria: (1) Ability to manufacture and market the 
technology; (2) manufacturing and marketing capability; (3) time 
required to bring technology to market and production rate; (4) 
royalties; (5) technical capabilities; (6) small business status.
