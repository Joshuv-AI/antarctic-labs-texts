DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Agricultural Research Service


Government Owned Invention Available for Licensing

AGENCY: Agricultural Research Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of government owned invention available for licensing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention disclosed in U.S. Patent Application Serial No. 
08/471,349, ``Fiber and Fiber Products Produced from Feathers'' is 
owned by the U.S. Government as represented by the Department of 
Agriculture, and is available for licensing in accordance with 35 
U.S.C. 207 and 37 CFR 404 to achieve expeditious commercialization of 
results of federally funded research and development. International 
patent applications have been filed in selected countries to extend 
market coverage for U.S. companies and may also be available for 
licensing.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Technical and licensing information on this invention may be obtained 
by writing to: June Blalock, Technology Licensing Coordinator, USDA, 
ARS, Room 415, Bldg. 005, BARC-West, Beltsville, Maryland 20705; Phone 
301-504-5989 or Fax 301-504-5060.
June Blalock,
Technology Licensing Coordinator.
