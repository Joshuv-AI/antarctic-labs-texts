FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting Notice; Announcing a Partially Open Meeting 
of the Board of Directors

Time and Date: The open portion of the meeting of the Board of 
Directors is scheduled to begin at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, April 14, 
2004. The closed portion of the meeting will follow immediately the 
open portion of the meeting.

Place: Board Room, Second Floor, Federal Housing Finance Board, 1777 F 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20006.

Status: The first portion of the meeting will be open to the public. 
The final portion of the meeting will be closed to the public.

Matters To Be Considered at the Open Portion of the Meeting: Federal 
Home Loan Bank of Topeka Capital Plan Amendment.

Matters To Be Considered at the Closed Portion of the Meeting: Further 
Consideration of Various Disclosure Initiatives and Supervisory Issues 
Regarding Enhanced Securities Disclosure.

Contact Person for More Information: Mary Gottlieb, Paralegal 
Specialist, Office of General Counsel, by telephone at 202/408-2826 or 
by electronic mail at [email protected].

    Dated: April 7, 2004.
    By the Federal Housing Finance Board.
John Harry Jorgenson,
General Counsel.
