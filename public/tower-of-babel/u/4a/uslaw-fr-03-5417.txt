Crowe Logging, Inc., Encampment, WY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 22, 2003, in response to a 
worker

[[Page 11150]]

petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Crowe 
Logging, Inc., Encampment, Wyoming.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 24th day of February 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
