Self-Regulatory Organizations; BOX Options Exchange LLC; Notice 
of Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend Rules 7150 and 7245

June 22, 2018.
    On May 14, 2018, BOX Options Exchange LLC (the ``Exchange'') filed 
with the Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), pursuant 
to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 \1\ and Rule 
19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to amend Rules 7150 and 
7245 to provide certain account type information in its Price 
Improvement Period and Complex Price Improvement Period auction 
notifications. The proposed rule change was published for comment in 
the Federal Register on May 31, 2018.\3\ The Commission received no 
comment letters on the proposal. On June 20, 2018, the Exchange 
withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-BOX-2018-17).
