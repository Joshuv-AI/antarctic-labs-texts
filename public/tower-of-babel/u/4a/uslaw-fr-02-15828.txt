DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

[Program Announcement 02128]


Targeted Injury Intervention Programs; Notice of Availability of 
Funds; Amendment

    A notice announcing the availability of Fiscal Year 2002 funds for 
a cooperative agreement for Targeted Injury Intervention Programs was 
published in the Federal Register dated May 9, 2002, Vol. 67, No. 90, 
pages 31331-31334. Page 31332, Section F. Submission and Deadline, 
Application, Paragraph 2, line 1, should be changed to read: ``On or 
before 5 p.m. Eastern Standard Time on July 8, 2002, submit the 
application * * *''

    Dated: June 18, 2002.
Edward Schultz,
Acting Director, Procurement and Grants Office, Centers for Disease 
Control and Prevention.
