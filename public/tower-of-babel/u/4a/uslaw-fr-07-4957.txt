DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


NetBank, Alpharetta, GA; Notice of Appointment of Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owner's Loan Act, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision has duly appointed the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation as sole Receiver for the NetBank, Alpharetta, Georgia (OTS 
No. 8475), on September 28, 2007.

    Dated: October 2, 2007.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Legal Information Assistant.
