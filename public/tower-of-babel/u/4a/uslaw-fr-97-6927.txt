Tennessee; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
State of Tennessee (FEMA-1167-DR), dated March 7, 1997, and related 
determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 9, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Magda Ruiz, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the State 
of Tennessee, is hereby amended to include Categories A and B under the 
Public Assistance program in those areas determined to have been 
adversely affected by the catastrophe declared a major disaster by the 
President in his declaration of March 7, 1997:

The counties of Carroll, Cheatham, Dyer, Madison, McNairy, 
Montgomery, and Obion for Categories A and B under the Public 
Assistance program (already designated for Individual Assistance and 
Hazard Mitigation).

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance.)
Lacy E. Suiter,
Executive Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
