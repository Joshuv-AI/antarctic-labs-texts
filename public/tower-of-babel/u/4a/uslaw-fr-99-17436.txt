Original Bradford Soap Works Incorporated, West Warwick, Rhode 
Island; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 19, 1999, in response to a worker petition filed 
on behalf of workers at Original Bradford Soap Works, Incorporated, 
West Warwick, Rhode Island.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of June, 1999.
Linda G. Poole,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
