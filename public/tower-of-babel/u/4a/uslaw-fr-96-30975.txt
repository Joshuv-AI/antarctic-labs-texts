COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' meeting scheduled for 19 December 1996 
has been canceled. The next meeting is scheduled for 16 January 1997 at 
10:00 AM in the Commission's offices in the Pension Building, Suite 
312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. 20001 to 
discuss various projects affecting the appearance of Washington, D.C., 
including buildings, memorials, parks, etc.; also matters of design 
referred by other agencies of the government.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, D.C. November 26, 1996.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
