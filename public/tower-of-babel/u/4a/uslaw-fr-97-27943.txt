FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-148; RM-9088]


Radio Broadcasting Services; New London, IA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Sound In Spirit 
Broadcasting, Inc., allots Channel 247A at New London, Iowa, as the 
community's first local aural transmission service. Channel 247A can be 
allotted to New London in compliance with the Commission's minimum 
distance separation requirements with a site restriction of 2.7 
kilometers (1.7 miles) west in order to avoid a short-spacing conflict 
with the licensed operation of Station WFYR-FM, Channel 247B1, Elmwood, 
Illinois. The coordinates for Channel 247A at New London are 40-55-30 
NL and 91-25-40 WL. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective: November 24, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 247A at New London, Iowa, will open on 
November 24, 1997, and close on December 26, 1997.
