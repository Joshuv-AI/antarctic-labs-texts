Radio Broadcasting Services; Antlers, OK; Hico, TX, and Hugo, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Liberman Broadcasting of 
Dallas License LLC, allots FM Channel 293A in lieu of vacant FM Channel 
285A at Hico, Texas. Channel 293A can be allotted at Hico, Texas, in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements with a site restriction of 5.5 km (3.4 miles) south of 
Hico at the following reference coordinates: 31-56-00 North Latitude 
and 98-02-00 West Longitude. The Audio Division further amends the 
reference coordinates of vacant FM Channel 285A at Broken Bow, 
Oklahoma, to reflect a site restrict of 12.8 km (7.9 miles) northeast 
of Broken Bow at the following reference coordinates: 34-06-21 North 
Latitude and 94-38-09 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective November 24, 2008.

ADDRESSES: Secretary, Federal Communications Commission, 445 12th 
Street, SW., Washington, DC 20554.

418-2180.
