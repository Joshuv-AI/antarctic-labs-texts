INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION
[Finance Docket No. 32677]


Penske Dedicated Logistics Corporation--Control Exemption-- 
Leaseway Transportation Corporation and Its Carrier Subsidiaries

AGENCY: Interstate Commerce Commission.

ACTION: Notice of exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under 49 U.S.C. 10505, the Commission exempts from the 
regulatory requirements of 49 U.S.C. 11343 et seq., the acquisition and 
control of Leaseway Transportation Corporation (LTC) and its 26 motor 
carrier subsidiaries by Penske Dedicated Logistics Corporation (PDLC). 
PDLC is under common control with a rail carrier.

DATES: This exemption is effective on April 14, 1995. Petitions to 
reopen must be filed by May 4, 1995.
