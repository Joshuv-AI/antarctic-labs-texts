DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board, Nevada 
Test Site

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Meeting cancellation notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee 
Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 86 Stat. 770), notice is hereby given of the 
cancellation of the open Environmental Management Site-Specific 
Advisory Board (EM SSAB), Nevada Test Site Advisory Committee meeting, 
which was scheduled to be held on Wednesday, March 4, 1998, from 5:30 
p.m.-9:00 p.m., at the U.S. Department of Energy Nevada Support 
Facility, Great Basin Room, 232 Energy Way, North Las Vegas, Nevada. 
This meeting was announced in the Federal Register on Tuesday, February 
10, 1998 (63 FR 6736).

    Issued at Washington, DC on February 18, 1998.
Althea T. Vanzego,
Acting Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
