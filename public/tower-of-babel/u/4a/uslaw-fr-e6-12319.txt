Radio Broadcasting Services; Madisonville and Rosebud, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document, at the request of Petitioner Charles Crawford, 
dismisses his pending petition for rulemaking to allot Channel 267A at 
Rosebud, Texas. The dismissed proposal would have required a change in 
reference coordinates for Channel 267A at Madisonville, Texas, and the 
reclassification of Station KNUE(FM), Tyler, Texas to a Class C0 
facility. The document therefore terminates this proceeding.
