DENALI COMMISSION


Withdrawal of Denali Commission Fiscal Year 2025 Draft Work Plan

AGENCY: Denali Commission.

ACTION: Notice; withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 11, 2024, the Denali Commission published a Federal 
Register notice to allow public comment on the Denali Commission Fiscal 
Year 2025 Draft Work Plan. The notice is hereby withdrawn.

DATES: The document published at 89 FR 49162 on June 11, 2024, is 
withdrawn as of June 17, 2024.
