ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-8189-8]


Notice of Charter Renewal

    Agency: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).
    Notice is hereby given that the Charter for the Environmental 
Protection Agency's National Advisory Council for Environmental Policy 
and Technology (NACEPT) will be renewed for an additional two-year 
period. This committee has been determined to be in the public 
interest, in accordance with the provisions of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (FACA), 5 U.S.C. App. 2 Sec.  9(c). The purpose of NACEPT 
is to provide advice and recommendations to the Administrator of EPA on 
a broad range of environmental policy, technology and management 
issues. Inquiries may be directed to Sonia Altieri, U.S. EPA, (Mail 
Code 1601-E), 1200 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20460, 
telephone (202) 233-0061, or [email protected].

    Dated: June 20, 2006.
Rafael DeLeon,
Director, Office of Cooperative Environmental Management.
