Parat Automotive USA, Duncan, SC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 14, 2008 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Parat Automotive 
USA, Duncan, South Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 6th day of May 2008.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
