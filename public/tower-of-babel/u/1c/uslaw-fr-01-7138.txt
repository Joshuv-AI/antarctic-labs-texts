Requirements for Permits and Permit Processing; Correction

AGENCY: Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Interior.

ACTION: Final rule, correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: We, the Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, 
are publishing corrections to a final rule which was published on 
Tuesday, December 19, 2000 (65 FR 79582). The final rule related to 
requirements for permits and permit processing and ownership and 
control under the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977, 
as amended.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 23, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stephen McEntegart, Office of Surface 
Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, 1951 Constitution Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20240. Telephone: 202-208-2968. Electronic Mail: 
[email protected]. Additional information concerning OSM and related 
documents may be found on OSM's Internet home page (Internet address: 
http://www.osmre.gov) and on our AVS Office's Internet home page 
(Internet address: http://www.avs.osmre.gov).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: We are making corrections to the final rule 
published on Tuesday, December 19, 2000 (65 FR 79582). The final rule 
redesignated former Sec. 773.13 as Sec. 773.6. In amendatory language 
revising a cross-reference contained in the newly designated paragraph 
Sec. 773.6(a)(3)(ii), we made a typographical error by citing the 
paragraph as ``Sec. 773.5(a)(3)(ii).'' The instruction should have read 
``newly designated Sec. 773.6(a)(3)(ii).''
    The final rule also redesignated former paragraph Sec. 773.15(d) as 
section Sec. 773.16. Former paragraph Sec. 773.15(d) began with the 
paragraph heading ``Performance bond submittal.'' Inadvertently, we 
failed to instruct the Federal Register to delete the paragraph heading 
for Sec. 773.15(d) and to use it as the section heading for 
Sec. 773.16.

    Accordingly, the publication on December 19, 2000, of the final 
rule which was the subject of FR Doc. 00-32002, is corrected as 
follows:


Sec. 773.6  [Corrected]

    1. On page 79663, in the third column, in amendatory instruction 
number 12, the citation to ``Sec. 773.5(a)(3)(ii)'' is corrected to 
read ``Sec. 773.6(a)(3)(ii).''


Sec. 773.16  [Corrected]

    2. On page 79663, in the second column, amendatory instruction 
number 10 is corrected by adding the following redesignation in 
sequential order to the table to read as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                  Section                     Is redesignated  as * * *
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
                   *        *        *        *      *
773.15(d), paragraph heading..............  773.16, section heading.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
