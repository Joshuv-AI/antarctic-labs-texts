DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Notice of Intent To Grant Exclusive Patent License; Banix 
Corporation

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to Banix Corporation, a revocable, nonassignable, exclusive 
license in the United States to practice the Government owned invention 
described in U.S. Patent No. 5,551,349 entitled ``Internal Conduit 
Vehicle,'' issued, September 3, 1996.
    Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license has 60 days 
from the date of this notice to file written objections along with 
supporting evidence, if any. Written objections are to be filed with 
the Office of Naval Research, ONR 00CC, Ballston Tower One, 800 North 
Quincy Street, Arlington, Virginia 22217-5600.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. R.J. Erickson, Staff Patent 
Attorney, Office of Naval Research, ONR OOCC, Ballston Tower One, 800 
North Quincy Street, Arlington, Virginia 22217-5660, telephone (703) 
696-4001.

    Dated: December 10, 1996.
D. E. Koenig, Jr.,
LCDR, JAGC, USN, Federal Register Liaison Officer.
