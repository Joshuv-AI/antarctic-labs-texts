INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION
[Investigation No. 337-TA-349]

 

Certain Diltiazem Hydrochloride and Diltiazem Preparations; 
Notice of Designation of Additional Commission Investigative Attorney

    Notice is hereby given that, as of this date, John M. Whealan, Esq. 
and Juan S. Cockburn, Esq. of the Office of Unfair Import 
Investigations are designated as the Commission investigative attorneys 
in the above-cited investigation instead of John M. Whealan, Esq.
    The Secretary is requested to publish this Notice in the Federal 
Register.

    Dated: September 9, 1994.
Lynn I. Levine,
Director, Office of Unfair Import Investigations.
