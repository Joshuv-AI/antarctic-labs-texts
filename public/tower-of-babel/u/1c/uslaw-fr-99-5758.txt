Office of Arms Control and Nonproliferation Policy; Proposed

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In notice FR document 99-5101 beginning on page 10132 in the 
issue of Tuesday, March 2, 1999, make the following correction:
    On page 10132, in the second column, SUMMARY section, second 
paragraph, the sentence should read, ``This subsequent arrangement 
concerns the transfer of 5,000,000 grams of natural uranium from Canada 
to EURATOM for toll enrichment. The enrichment will not exceed 20%.''

    For the Department of Energy.
Edward T. Fei,
