FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Radio Broadcasting Services; Broken Bow, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 67 FR 64598 
(October 21, 2002), this Report and Order dismisses the Petition for 
Rule Making in MM Docket No. 02-301 proposing to allot Channel 232A to 
Broken Bow, Oklahoma. The petitioner had requested this dismissal.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
