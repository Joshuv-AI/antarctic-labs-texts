DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP97-392-001]


National Fuel Gas Supply Corporation; Notice of Technical 
Conference

November 6, 1997.
    Pursuant to the Commission's order, issued on October 29, 1997, a 
technical conference will be held to resolve the issues raised in the 
above-captioned proceeding.
    The conference will be held on Tuesday, November 18, 1997 from 
10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., in a room to be designated at the offices of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426.
    All interested parties and Staff are permitted to attend.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
