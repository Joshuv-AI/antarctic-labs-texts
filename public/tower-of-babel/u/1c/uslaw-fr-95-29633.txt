DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. IS94-32-000]


Chevron Pipe Line Co.; Notice of Informal Settlement Conference

November 30, 1995.
    Take notice that Commission Staff will convene an informal 
settlement conference in this proceeding on December 6, 1995, at 11:00 
a.m., at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 
First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C.
    Any party, as defined by 18 CFR 385.102(c), or any participant, as 
defined in 18 CFR 385.102(b), may attend. Persons wishing to become a 
party must move to intervene and receive intervenor status pursuant to 
the Commission's regulations, 18 CFR 385.214.

[[Page 62421]]

    For additional information, contact Donald Heydt at (202) 208-0740 
or Russell Mamone at (202) 208-0744.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
