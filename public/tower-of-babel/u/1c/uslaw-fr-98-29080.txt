Mississippi River Transmission Corporation; Notice Establishing 
Technical Conference

October 26, 1998.
    Take notice that the Commission staff will convene a technical 
conference as provided by the Commission order in this proceeding 
issued October 14, 1998.\1\ The technical conference will be held on 
Wednesday, November 4, 1998, at 10:00 a.m. in a room to be designated 
at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Mississippi River Transmission Corporation, 85 FERC para. 
61,049 (1998).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Attendance will be limited to parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Jerie O'Connor at (202) 208-0459.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
