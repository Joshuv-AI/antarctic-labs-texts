McMurray Fabrics, Inc., Jamesville, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 29, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at McMurray Fabrics 
Jamesville, Inc., Jamesville, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 30th day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
