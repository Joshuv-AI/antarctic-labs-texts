DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permits for Marine Mammals

    On June 26, 1997, a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 62, No. 123, Page 34482, that an application had been filed with 
the Fish and Wildlife Service by Donald Williams for a permit (PRT-
830806) to import a personal sport-hunted polar bear from the Northwest 
Territories, Canada.
    Notice is hereby given that on August 8, 1997, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
