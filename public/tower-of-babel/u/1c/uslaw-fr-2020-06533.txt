POSTAL SERVICE


Board of Governors; Sunshine Act Meeting

TIME AND DATE:  Wednesday, April 1, 2020, at 9:00 a.m.; Wednesday, 
April 1, 2020, at 12:00 p.m.

PLACE:  Washington, DC, at U.S. Postal Service Headquarters, 475 
L'Enfant Plaza SW, in the Benjamin Franklin Room.

STATUS:  Wednesday, April 1, 2020, at 9:00 a.m.--Closed. Wednesday, 
April 1, 2020, at 12:00 p.m.--Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: 

Wednesday, April 1, 2020, at 9:00 a.m. (Closed)

1. Strategic Issues.
2. Financial and Operational Matters.
3. Administrative Issues.

Wednesday, April 1, 2020, at 12:00 p.m. (Open)

1. Remarks of the Chairman of the Board of Governors.
2. Remarks of the Postmaster General and CEO.
3. Borrowing Resolution.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION:  Michael J. Elston, Secretary of 
the Board of Governors, U.S. Postal Service, 475 L'Enfant Plaza SW, 
Washington, DC 20260-1000. Telephone: (202) 268-4800.

Michael J. Elston,
Secretary.
