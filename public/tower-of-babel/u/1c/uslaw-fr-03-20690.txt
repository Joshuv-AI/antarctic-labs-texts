Hops Produced in Washington, Oregon, Idaho and California; 
Postponement of Hearing on Proposed Marketing Agreement and Order No. 
991

AGENCY: Agricultural Marketing Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of postponement of public hearing on proposed marketing 
agreement and order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The public hearing scheduled to consider a proposed marketing 
agreement and order under the Agricultural Marketing Agreement Act of 
1937 to cover hops grown in Washington, Oregon, Idaho and California 
has been postponed until after October 1, 2003. The notice of public 
hearing was announced in the Federal Register on Monday, July 28, 2003, 
at 68 FR 44244. Another notice will be published announcing the new 
hearing dates.
