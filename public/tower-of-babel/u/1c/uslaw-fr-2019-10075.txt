FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Open Commission Meeting, Thursday, May 9, 2019

    The Federal Communications Commission held an Open Meeting on the 
subjects listed below on Thursday, May 9, 2019 which scheduled to 
commence at 10:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street SW, 
Washington, DC.
