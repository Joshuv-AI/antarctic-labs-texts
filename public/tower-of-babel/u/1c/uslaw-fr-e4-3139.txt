DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-55,647]


Freudenberg Nonwovens, Madison, TN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 21, 2004 in response to a petition filed by 
a company official on behalf of workers at Freudenberg Nonwovens, 
Madison, Tennessee (TA-W-55,647).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 25th day of October 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
