Intermetro Industries Corp., Cucamonga, CA; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 31, 2003, in response to a petition filed by 
a company official on behalf of workers at InterMetro Industries 
Corporation, Cucamonga, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 12th day of January, 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
