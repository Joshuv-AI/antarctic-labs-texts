DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Delegation of Authority No. 394]


Designation of the Department of State Representative to the 
Administrative Conference of the United States

    By virtue of the authority vested in the Secretary of State, 
including Section 1 of the State Department Basic Authorities Act, as 
amended (22 U.S.C. 2651a), and 5 U.S.C. 593, and delegated pursuant to 
Delegation of Authority 198, dated September 16, 1992, and to the 
extent authorized by law, I hereby designate the Department of State 
Legal Adviser as the Department of State government representative to 
the Administrative Conference of the United States.
    This delegation of authority may be re-delegated, to the extent 
authorized by law.
    Notwithstanding this delegation of authority, the Secretary, the 
Deputy Secretary, the Deputy Secretary for Management and Resources, 
and the Under Secretary for Management may exercise any function or 
authority delegated by this delegation of authority.
    This Delegation of Authority will be published in the Federal 
Register.

    Dated: April 29, 2016.
Patrick F. Kennedy,
Under Secretary of State for Management, Department of State.
