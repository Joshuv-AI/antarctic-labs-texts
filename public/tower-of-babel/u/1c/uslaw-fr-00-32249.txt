FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2457]


Petitions for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceedings

December 11, 2000.
    Petitions for Reconsideration have been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th Street, 
SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to these petitions 
must be filed by January 3, 2001. See section 1.4(b)(1) of the 
Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition must be 
filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions have 
expired.
    Subject: Compatibility Between Cable and Consumer Electronics 
Equipment (PP Docket No. 00-67).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 2.
    Subject: 1998 Biennial Regulatory Review 47 CFR part 90--Private 
Land Mobile Radio Services (WT Docket No. 98-182, RM-9222).
    Replacement of Part 90 by Part 88 to Revise the Private Land Mobile 
Radio Services and Modify the Policies Governing Them and Examination 
of the Exclusivity and Frequency Assignment Policies of the Private 
Land Mobile Services (PR Docket No. 92-235).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 4.
