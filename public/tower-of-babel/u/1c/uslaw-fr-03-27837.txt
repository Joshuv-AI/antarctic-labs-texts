DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-52,830]


Surgical Specialties Corp., Ada, Oklahoma; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 11, 2003 in response to a 
worker petition filed on behalf of workers at Surgical Specialties 
Corp., Ada, Oklahoma
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
