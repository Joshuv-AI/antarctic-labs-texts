FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that the previously 
announced meeting of the Board of Directors scheduled to be held on 
Wednesday, March 10, 2004, at 10 a.m. has been rescheduled for 8:30 
a.m. that same day.
    No earlier notice of the change in time of this meeting was 
practicable.

    Dated: March 5, 2004.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
