Chock Full o' Nuts, Linden, NJ; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 17, 1997, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Chock Full o' Nuts, Linden, 
New Jersey.
    All workers were separated from the subject firm more than one year 
prior to the date of the petition. Section 223 of the Act specifies 
that no certification may apply to any workers whose last separation 
occurred more than one year before the date of the petition. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 1st day of April, 1997.
Russell T. Kile,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
