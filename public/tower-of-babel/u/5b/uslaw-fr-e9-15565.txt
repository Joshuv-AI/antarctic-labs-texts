OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request For Review Of A 
Revised Information Collection

[OMB Control No. 3206-0033; Form RI 25-7]

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) intends to submit to the Office of 
Management and Budget (OMB) a request for comments on a revised 
information collection. This information collection Marital Status 
Certification Survey, ``(OMB Control No. 3206-0033; Form RI 25-7), is 
used to determine whether widows, widowers, and former spouses 
receiving survivor annuities from OPM have remarried before reaching 
age 55 and, thus, are no longer eligible for benefits.
    There are approximately 24,000 respondents. Each form takes 
approximately 15 minutes to complete. The annual estimated burden is 
625 hours.
