New Hampshire: Final Authorization of State Hazardous Waste 
Management Program Revisions; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Immediate final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency published in the Federal 
Register of November 14, 1994 (59 FR 56397) the authorization of New 
Hampshire's Hazardous Waste Management Program Revision under the 
Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA). The document stated that 
the effective date was January 13, 1994. This was a typographical 
error. The correct effective date is January 13, 1995. This document 
corrects that error.
    This document is also correcting typographical errors that were in 
the crosswalk listings of the federal requirements in the same 
immediate final rule.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The effective date for the immediate final rule 
published at 59 FR 56397 is January 13, 1995.
