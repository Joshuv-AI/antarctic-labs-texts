DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL00-95-000, et al.]


San Diego Gas & Electric Company v. Sellers of Energy and 
Ancillary Services Into Markets Operated by the California Independent 
System Operator Corporation and the California Power Exchange; Notice 
of Designation of Certain Commission Personnel as Non-Decisional

    Commission staff member Aaron Siskind, of the Office of Energy 
Market Regulation, previously was assigned to assist the Office of 
Administrative Litigation in settlement and litigation proceedings in 
the above-referenced dockets. In addition, Mr. Siskind

[[Page 20380]]

remains assigned to the designated matters to assist in litigation 
proceedings.
    As non-decisional staff, Mr. Siskind will not participate in an 
advisory capacity in the Commission's review of any future filings in 
the above-referenced dockets, including offers of settlement or 
settlement agreements.
    Mr. Siskind will not be assigned as advisory staff to review and 
process subsequent filings that are made in the above-referenced 
dockets, including any offer of settlement or settlement agreement. 
Non-decisional staff and advisory staff are prohibited from subsequent 
communications with one another concerning matters in the above-
referenced dockets.

    Dated: March 28, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
