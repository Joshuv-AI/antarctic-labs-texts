INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[USITC SE-99-017]


Emergency Notice of Rescheduling of Meeting and Additional Agenda 
Item; Sunshine Act Meeting Notice

AGENCY HOLDING THE MEETING: United States International Trade 
Commission.

ORIGINAL TIME AND DATE: April 26, 1999 at 2:00 p.m.

NEW DATE AND TIME: April 29, 1999 at 11:00 a.m.

PLACE: Room 101, 500 E Street SW, Washington, DC 20436, Telephone: 
(202) 205-2000.

STATUS: Open to the public.

ADDITIONAL AGENDA ITEM: 6. Inv. No. 731-TA-787 (Final)(Extruded Rubber 
Thread from Indonesia)--briefing and vote.

    Note: Outstanding action jackets becomes agenda item 7.

    Under 19 C.F.R. 201.35 (c)(1) of the Commission's rules, the 
Commission has determined to reschedule the meeting of Monday, April 
26, 1999 to Thursday, April 29, 1999. Thus, the meeting of April 26, 
1999 is rescheduled and the additional agenda item schedule for April 
29, 1999 is as listed above. The deadline for transmission of the 
Commission's determination and views to Commerce is hereby changed from 
the administrative deadline of Monday, May 3, 1999 to the statutory 
deadline of Friday, May 7, 1999. In accordance with Commission policy, 
subject matter listed above, not disposed of at the scheduled meeting, 
may be carried over to the agenda of the following meeting.

    Issued: April 26, 1999.
    By order of the Commission.

Donna R. Koehnke,
Secretary.
