FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MB Docket No. 12-235; RM-11670, DA 12-1398]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Knox City, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document proposes to amend the FM Table of Allotments. 
The Commission requests comment on a petition filed by Big Cat 
Broadcasting, LLC, proposing to amend the Table of Allotments by 
substituting Channel 277A for vacant Channel 291A, at Knox City, Texas. 
The proposal is part of a contingently filed ``hybrid'' application and 
rule making petition. Channel 277A can be allotted at Knox City, Texas, 
in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements at a site with reference coordinates of 33-33-08 North 
Latitude and 99-49-38 West Longitude. See SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION 
infra.

DATES: The deadline for filing comments is October 15, 2012. Reply 
comments must be filed on or before October 30, 2012.
