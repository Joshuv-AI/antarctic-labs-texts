DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Office of The Secretary


Application Of Kitty Hawk International d/b/a American 
International Airways and Kalitta Air, L.L.C. For Transfer Of 
Certificate Authority

AGENCY: Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice of order to show cause (Order 2000-10-29) Docket OST-
2000-7588.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 65894]]

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is directing all interested 
persons to show cause why it should not issue an order finding Kalitta 
Air, L.L.C., fit, willing, and able, and (1) transferring to it the 
all-cargo authority contained in the interstate scheduled certificate 
currently issued to Kitty Hawk International, Inc. d/b/a American 
International Airways, and (2) canceling the passenger authority 
contained in that certificate.

DATES: Persons wishing to file objections should do so no later than 
November 6, 2000.

ADDRESSES: Objections and answers to objections should be filed in 
Docket OST-2000-7588 and addressed to the Department of Transportation 
Dockets (SVC-124.1, Room PL-401), U.S. Department of Transportation, 
400 Seventh Street, SW., Washington, DC 20590, and should be served 
upon the parties listed in Attachment A to the order.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Delores King, Air Carrier Fitness 
Division (X-56, Room 6401), U.S. Department of Transportation, 400 
Seventh Street, SW., Washington, DC 20590, (202) 366-2343.

    Dated: October 27, 2000.
Susan McDermott,
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Aviation and International Affairs.
