DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Availability of Non-Exclusive, Exclusive, or Partially-Exclusive 
Licensing Composite Materials Manufacturing Technology

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Maryland.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive or non-exclusive Licenses under 
patent 5,210,499, issued 11 May 1993, entitled ``In-Situ Sensor Method 
and Device''. Licenses shall comply with 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR 404.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. Michael D. Rausa, Technology Transfer Office (APG Site), AMSRL-TT-
TA, U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Aberdeen Proving Ground, MD 21005-
5425, telephone number (410) 278-5028.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Written objections must be filed within 3 
months from the date of publication of this notice in the Federal 
Register.

Gregory D. Showalter,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
