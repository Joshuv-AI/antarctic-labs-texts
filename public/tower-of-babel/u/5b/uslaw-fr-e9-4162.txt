BPUS Generation Development LLC; Notice of Preliminary Permit 
Application Accepted for Filing and Soliciting Comments, Motions To 
Intervene, and Competing Applications

February 20, 2009.
    On October 31, 2008, BPUS Generation Development LLC (BPUS 
Generation) filed an application, pursuant to section 4(f) of the 
Federal Power Act, proposing to study the feasibility of the Lake 
Roosevelt Pumped Storage Project. The project would be located on 
federal lands in Ferry, Grant and Okanogan Counties in Washington 
State, approximately 40 miles northwest of the Town of Nespelem, 
Washington, and 38 miles southwest of the Town of Northport, 
Washington, and would utilize the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation's Lake 
Roosevelt Dam.
    The proposed project would use the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation's 
Lake Roosevelt Dam as its lower reservoir and would consist of: (1) An 
upper reservoir with a surface area of approximately 123 acres at a 
normal water surface elevation ranging from 2,780 to 2,880 feet m.s.l.; 
(2) a proposed powerhouse containing 4 generating units having a total 
installed capacity of 1,310 megawatts, (3) a proposed intake structure, 
(4) a proposed 50-mile-long, 500 kV transmission line, and (5) 
appurtenant facilities. The project would have an annual generation of 
approximately 3,750 gigawatt-hours that would be sold to a local 
utility.
