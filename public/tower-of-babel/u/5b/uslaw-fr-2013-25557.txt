DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, ZRG1 F07-K (20), October 24, 
2013, 08:00 a.m. to October 24, 2013, 04:30 p.m., Renaissance 
Washington DC, Dupont Circle, 1143 New Hampshire Avenue NW, Washington, 
DC, 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on October 1, 
2013, 78 FR Pgs. 60297-60299.

Rockledge Dr., Bethesda, MD 20892. The meeting will start on November 
13, 2013 at 8:00 a.m. and will end on November 14, 2013 at 8:00 p.m. 
The meeting is closed to the public.
