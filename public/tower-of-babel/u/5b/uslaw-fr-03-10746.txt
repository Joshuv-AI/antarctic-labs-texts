Dana/Torque Traction Tech., Inc., Whitsett, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 28, 2003, in response to a worker petition filed 
on behalf of workers at Dana/Torque-Traction Tech., Inc., Whitsett, 
North Carolina.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
51,406). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 17th day of April 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
