Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy; Notice of Meeting

    The U.S. Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy, reauthorized 
pursuant to Pub.L. 106-113 (H.R. 3194, Consolidated Appropriations Act, 
2000), will meet on Tuesday, September 12, 2000 in Room 600, 301 4th 
St., SW., Washington, DC from 2 pm to 4 pm.
    The Commission will discuss its plans for assessing the 
consolidation of USIA into the State Department and the effectiveness 
of U.S. public diplomacy in the former Soviet Union.
    Members of the general public may attend the meeting, though 
attendance of public members will be limited to the seating available. 
Access to the building is controlled, and individual building passes 
are required for all attendees. Persons who plan to attend should 
contact David J. Kramer, Executive Director, at (202) 619-4463.

    Dated: August 2, 2000.
David J. Kramer,
Executive Director, Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy, Department 
of State.
