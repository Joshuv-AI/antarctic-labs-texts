DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 1986]

 

Certification Pursuant to Section 576 of the Foreign Operations, 
Export Financing, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 1994 (Titles 
I-V of Public Law 103-87)

    Pursuant to the authority vested in me by the laws of the United 
States, including section 576 of the Foreign Operations, Export 
Financing, and Related Programs Appropriations Act (Titles I-V of Pub. 
L. 103-87) and the related Presidential delegation of authority dated 
March 29, 1994, I hereby certify that the Government of Russia has not 
provided assistance to Cuba during the eighteen month period preceding 
April 1, 1994.
    This certification shall be reported to Congress and published in 
the Federal Register.

    Dated: April 1, 1994.
Warren Christopher,
Secretary of State.
