Establishment of Class E2 Airspace; and Modification of Class E5 
Airspace; Storm Lake, IA

Correction

    In rule document 05-15311 beginning on page 44465 in the issue of 
Wednesday, August 3, 2005, make the following corrections:


Sec.  71.1  [Corrected]

    1. On page 44466, in Sec.  71.1, in the first column, under 
Paragraph 6002 Class E airspace designated as surface areas., in the 
second line, ``ACT IA E2 Storm Lake, IA'', should read ``ACE IA E2 
Storm Lake, IA''.
    2. On the same page, in Sec.  71.1, in the first column, under  
Paragraph 6005 Class E airspace areas extending upward from 700 feet or 
more above the surface of the earth., in the second line, ``ACT IA E5 
Storm Lake, IA'' should read ``ACE IA E5 Storm Lake, IA''.
