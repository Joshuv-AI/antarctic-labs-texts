STATE JUSTICE INSTITUTE


SJI Board of Directors Meeting, Notice

AGENCY: State Justice Institute.

ACTION: Notice of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The SJI Board of Directors will be meeting on Monday, April 8, 
2013 at 1:00 p.m. The meeting will be held at SJI Headquarters in 
Reston, Virginia. The purpose of this meeting is to consider grant 
applications for the 2nd quarter of FY 2013, and other business. All 
portions of this meeting are open to the public.

ADDRESSES: SJI Headquarters, 11951 Freedom Drive, Reston, VA 20190, 
571-313-8843.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jonathan Mattiello, Executive 
Director, State Justice Institute, 11951 Freedom Drive, Suite 1020, 
Reston, VA 20190, 571-313-8843, [email protected].

Jonathan D. Mattiello,
Executive Director.
