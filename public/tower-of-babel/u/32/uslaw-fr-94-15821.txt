DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Federal Railroad Administration

 

Northeast Corridor Safety Committee; Public Meeting

    Pursuant to Section 11 of the Rail Safety Improvement Act of 1988 
(Pub. L. 100-342), notice is hereby given that a public meeting of the 
Northeast Corridor Safety Committee will be held on July 7, 1994, at 10 
a.m. in room 6244 of the Nassif Building, 400 Seventh Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20590.
    The meeting is called for the purpose of providing counsel and 
advice to the Department of Transportation on safety improvements on 
the main line of the Northeast Corridor (NEC). The major topic on the 
agenda is a briefing by the Authur D. Little Company on their risk 
assessment for Amtrak associated with higher speeds and more frequent 
service between New York and Boston.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on June 24, 1994.
Bruce M. Fine,
Acting Associate Administrator for Safety.
