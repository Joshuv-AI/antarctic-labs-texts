Foreign-Trade Zone 214; Application for Subzone Expansion; 
Consolidated Diesel Company; Whitakers, North Carolina; Correction

regarding the subzone expansion application for Consolidated Diesel 
Company, located in Whitakers, North Carolina, is corrected as follows:

``Consolidated Diesel Company''.
