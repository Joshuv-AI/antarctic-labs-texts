BIPARTISAN COMMISSION ON ENTITLEMENT AND TAX REFORM

 

Meeting

    Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the Public Law 92-463, that the 
Bipartisan Commission on Entitlement and Tax Reform will hold a meeting 
on Friday, July 15, 1994 from 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m in the Cannon House 
Office Building, Room 210, Washington, DC.
    The meeting of the Commission shall be open to public. The proposed 
agenda includes discussion on entitlement expenditure growth and its 
relation to long-term fiscal and budget policy.
    Records shall be kept of all Commission proceedings and shall be 
available for public inspection in room 825 of the Hart Senate Office 
Building, 120 Constitution Avenue, NE., Washington, DC 20510.
J. Robert Kerrey,
Chairman.
John C. Danforth,
Vice-Chairman.
