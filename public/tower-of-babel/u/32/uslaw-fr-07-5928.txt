DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Alternative Medicine; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine Special Emphasis 
Panel, December 7, 2007, 8 a.m. to December 7, 2007, 5 p.m., Bethesda 
Marriott, 5151 Pooks Hill Road, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published 
in the Federal Register on November 13, 2007, 72FR63915.
    This meeting is being amended to change the format to a telephone 
conference and to change the date from December 7, 2007 to December 18, 
2007. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: November 27, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
