Kincaid Furniture Company, Inc., Hudson, North Carolina; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on December 31, 2003, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Kincaid Furniture Company, Inc., 
Hudson, North Carolina.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification for Kincaid Furniture Company, Inc., Plant 8 currently 
known as Plant 18, Lenoir, North Carolina (TA-W-50,735, as amended).
    Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 17th day of February, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
