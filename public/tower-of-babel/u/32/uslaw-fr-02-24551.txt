DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Drug Abuse; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Drug Abuse Special Emphasis Panel, September 24, 2002, 10 
a.m. to September 24, 2002, 12 p.m., Neuroscience Center, National 
Institutes of Health, 6001 Executive Blvd., Rockville, MD 20852 which 
was published in the Federal Register on August 27, 2002, Vol. 67, 
Number 166.
    The date, time and location of the meeting has been changed to 
October 4, 2002, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the DoubleTree Hotel, 1750 
Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: September 20, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
