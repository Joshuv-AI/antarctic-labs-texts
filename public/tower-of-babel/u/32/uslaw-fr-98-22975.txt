FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Request for Additional Information

Agreement No.: 203-011198-009
Title: Puerto Rico/Caribbean Discussion Agreement.
Parties:

    Crowley American Transport
    NPR, Inc.
    Dole Ocean Liner Express
    Sea-Land Service, Inc.

Synopsis: The Federal Maritime Commission hereby gives notice, pursuant 
to section 6(d) of the Shipping Act of 1984, 46 U.S.C. app. Secs. 1701 
et. seq., that it has requested the agreement parties to submit 
additional information regarding their agreement. Further information 
is necessary so the Commission can determine the impact of the proposed 
agreement modification. This action prevents the agreement from 
becoming effective as originally scheduled.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.

    Dated: August 24, 1998.
Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
