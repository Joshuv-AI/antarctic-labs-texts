SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

13 CFR Part 121


Small Business Size Regulations

CFR Correction

    In Title 13 of the Code of Federal Regulations, revised as of 
January 1, 2009, on page 357, in Sec.  121.201, in the table ``Small 
Business Size Standards by NAICS Industry'', under Sector 54, Subsector 
541, remove the three subentries under NAICS code 541712, beginning 
with the word ``EXCEPT,''.
