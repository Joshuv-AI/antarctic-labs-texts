Special Federal Aviation Regulation No. 36, Development of Major 
Repair Data

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; corrections.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY Federal Aviation Administration published a final rule in the 
Federal Register (64 FR 958) on January 6, 1999, that amends and 
extends Special Federal Aviation Regulation (SFAR) No. 36, which 
provides that holders of authorized repair station or aircraft 
operating certificates may approve aircraft products or articles for 
return to service after accomplishing major repairs using self-
developed repair data that have not been directly approved by the FAA. 
This document corrects an error in the heading and adds the date of 
issuance to the final rule.
