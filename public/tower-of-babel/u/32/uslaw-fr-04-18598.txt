NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 04-100]


Notice of Prospective Patent Application License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Prospective Patent Application License.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that NanoConduction Inc., of Los 
Gatos, CA has applied for an exclusive license to practice the 
inventions disclosed in a filed U.S. Patent Application No. 10/825,795, 
NASA Case No. ARC-15173-1, entitled ``Nanoengineered Thermal Materials 
Using Carbon Nanotube Array Composites,'' which is assigned to the 
United States of America as represented by the Administrator of the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections to 
the prospective grant of a license should be sent to Ames Research 
Center.

DATES: Responses to this notice must be received by August 31, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Padilla, Chief Patent Counsel, 
NASA Ames Research Center, M/S 202A-4, Moffett Field, CA 94035-1000, 
(650) 604-5104.

    Dated: July 27, 2004.
Keith T. Sefton,
Deputy General Counsel, Administration and Management.
