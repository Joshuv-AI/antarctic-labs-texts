SUMMARY: The Federal Aviation Administration published a document in 
the Federal Register of December 8, 2023, concerning its Policy 
Regarding Processing Land Use Changes on Federally Acquired or 
Federally Conveyed Airport Land. The document contained an incorrect 
FAA Docket Number.

DATES: This correction is effective January 8, 2024.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael Helvey, Airport Compliance and 
Management Analysis, ACO-1, Federal Aviation Administration, 800 
Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20591, telephone (202) 267-3085; 
facsimile: (202) 267-4629.

Correction

read:
