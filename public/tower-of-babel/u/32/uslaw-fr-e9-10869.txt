Gunderson Rail Services, LLC, dba Greenbrier Rail Services, a 
Subsidiary of the Greenbrier Companies, Chicago Heights, IL; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 4, 2009 in response to a worker 
petition filed by workers of Gunderson Rail Services, LLC, dba 
Greenbrier Rail Services, a subsidiary of The Greenbrier Companies, 
Chicago Heights, Illinois.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of April 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
