RTCA, Inc.; ``Technical Management Committee''; Notice of RTCA 
Technical Management Committee Meeting To Be Held December 1, 1995; 
Meeting Cancellation

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The RTCA Technical Management Committee meeting scheduled to 
be held on December 1, 1995, as announced in a notice published in the 
Federal Register for November 13, 1995 (60 FR 57053), has been 
canceled. A new date has not been determined at this time.

    Issued in Washington, D.C., on November 22, 1995.
