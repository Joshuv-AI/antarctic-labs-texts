ACTION: Extension of time notice; Correction.

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published an extension of time 
to complete a public land competitive sale in the Federal Register on 
October 15, 1999. The legal description inadvertently omitted parcels 
available for sale, and two case file numbers were omitted from the 
above description.

(520) 317-3208.
