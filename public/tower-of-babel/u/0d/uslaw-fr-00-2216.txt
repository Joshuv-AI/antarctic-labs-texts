Sunshine Act Meeting

VA 22314-3428.

STATUS: Closed.

exemptions (8), (9)(A)(ii) and (9)(B).

Regulations. Closed pursuant to exemptions (8), (9)(A)(ii) and (9)(B).
    3. Two (2) Personnel Actions. Closed pursuant to exemptions (2), 
(5), (6), (7), and (9)(B).
