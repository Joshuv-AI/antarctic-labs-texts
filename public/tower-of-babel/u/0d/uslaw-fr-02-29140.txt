DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 11843-002]


Gene Arlin Shanks; Notice of Surrender of Preliminary Permit

November 8, 2002.
    Take notice that Gene Arlin Shanks, permittee for the proposed 
Elfin Inian Project, has requested that its preliminary permit be 
terminated. The permit was issued on December 6, 2000, and would have 
expired on November 30, 2003. The project would have been located on 
Elfin Cove near the town of Elfin Cove, Alaska.
    The permittee filed the request on August 20, 2002, and the 
preliminary permit for Project No. 11843 shall remain in effect through 
the thirtieth day after issuance of this notice unless that day is a 
Saturday, Sunday, or holiday as described in 18 CFR 385.2007, in which 
case the permit shall remain in effect through the first business day 
following that day. New applications involving this project site, to 
the extent provided for under18 CFR part 4, may be filed on the next 
business day.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
