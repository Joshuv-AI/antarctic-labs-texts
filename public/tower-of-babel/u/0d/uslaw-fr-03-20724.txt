Stanek Tool Corporation, New Berlin, WI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on July 16, 2003, in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Stanek Tool 
Corporation, New Berlin, Wisconsin.
    The company official has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation would serve no purpose and the 
investigation under this petition has been terminated.
