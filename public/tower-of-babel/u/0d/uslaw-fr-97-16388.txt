FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Formations of, Acquisitions by, 
and Mergers of Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 97-15834) published on page 
32810 of the issue for Tuesday, June 17, 1997.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis heading, the entry for 
Cabot Bankshares, Inc., Cabotr, Arkansas, is revised to read as 
follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (Randall C. Sumner, Vice 
President) 411 Locust Street, St. Louis, Missouri 63102-2034:
    1. Cabot Bankshares, Inc., Cabot, Arkansas; to acquire 10 percent 
of the voting shares of The Capital Bank, Little Rock, Arkansas, a de 
novo bank.
    Comments on this application must be received by July 11, 1997.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, June 18, 1997.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
