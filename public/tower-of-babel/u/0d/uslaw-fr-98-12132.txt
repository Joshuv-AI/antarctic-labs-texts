Designation of an Urbanized Area for Flagstaff, AZ

AGENCY: Bureau of the Census, Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of designation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Based on the results of a special census conducted April 1, 
1995, the Bureau of the Census designated Flagstaff, Arizona, as an 
urbanized area under criteria published October 22, 1990 in the Federal 
Register (55 FR 42592-42596, Oct. 22, 1990). The Flagstaff, Arizona, 
urbanized area has a population of 53,355.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Joel L. Morrison, Chief, Geography 
Division, Bureau of the Census, Washington, DC 20233-7400, telephone 
(301) 457-1132.


[[Page 25199]]

    Since 1986, the Census Bureau has allowed the delineation of new 
urbanized areas based on a special census taken in the intercensal 
period. The Census Bureau delineates urbanized areas every 10 years as 
part of the decennial census of population and housing or following a 
special census.
