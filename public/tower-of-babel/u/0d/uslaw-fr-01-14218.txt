SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the final rule, which 
was published on Wednesday, May 30, 2001 (66 FR 29224). This rule 
relates to electronic recordkeeping by investment companies and 
investment advisers. In FR Document No. 01-13526 beginning on page 
29224 for Wednesday, May 30, 2001, the docket line contains an error. 
The docket line is correct as set forth above.
