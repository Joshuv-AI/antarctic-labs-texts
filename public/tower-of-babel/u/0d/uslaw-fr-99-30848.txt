Pennsylvania Electric Co./GPU Genco; Notice of Meeting

November 22, 1999.
    The applicant has scheduled a meeting to present and discuss issues 
relevant to the Water Quality Work Group as part of the Alternative 
Licensing Process. The meeting will be held at the Piney Station 
beginning at 10 AM on December 8, 1999.
    If you want to attend the meeting, require directions, or have 
other quesetions, please contact Glenn Neiport at (814) 226-8630.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
