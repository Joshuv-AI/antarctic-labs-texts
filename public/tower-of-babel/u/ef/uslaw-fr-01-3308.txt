DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Aging Transport Systems Rulemaking Advisory Committee; Meeting 
Cancellations

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public that the 
February 8 and 9, 2001, meeting of the Aging Transport Systems 
Rulemaking Advisory Committee (66 FR 8842, February 2, 2001) has been 
cancelled. The meeting will be rescheduled and announced in a later 
Federal Register notice.
