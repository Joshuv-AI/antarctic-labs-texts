FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 4-4325) published on pages 
13037 and 13038 of the issue for March 19, 2004.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Boston heading, the entry for 
Salisbury Bancorp, Inc., Lakeville, Connecticut, is revised to read as 
follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Boston (Richard Walker, Community 
Affairs Officer) 600 Atlantic Avenue, Boston, Massachusetts 02106-2204:
    1. Salisbury Bancorp, Inc., Lakeville, Connecticut; to merge with 
Canaan National Bancorp, Inc., Canaan, Connecticut, and thereby 
indirectly acquire the Canaan National Bank, Canaan, Connecticut.
    Comments on this application must be received by April 5, 2004.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, March 22, 
2004.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
