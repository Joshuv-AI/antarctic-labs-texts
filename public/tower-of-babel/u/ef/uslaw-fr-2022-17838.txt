DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of General Medical Sciences; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory General Medical Sciences Council, September 15, 2022, 09:30 
a.m. to September 15, 2022, 04:30 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
Natcher Building, 45 Center Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was 
published in the Federal Register on December 29, 2021, 304881.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the end time of the Open 
Session. The Open Session will be held on September 15, 2022, at 9:30 
a.m. until 12:30 p.m. The Closed Session will still be held from 1:30 
p.m. to 4:30 p.m. The meeting is partially Closed to the public.
    Information is also available on the Institute's/Center's home 
page: www.nigms.nih.gov/, where an agenda and any additional 
information for the meeting will be posted when available.

    Dated: August 15, 2022.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
