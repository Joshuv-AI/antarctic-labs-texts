SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region I Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region I Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Augusta, Maine will hold a public 
meeting at 4 p.m. on Tuesday, September 12th at the Caribou Hotel & 
Conference Center to discuss such matters as may be presented by 
members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present. For further information, write or call Mary McAleney, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 40 Western Avenue, 
Augusta, Maine 04330, (207) 622-8378.

Bettie Baca,
Counselor to the Administrator/Public Liaison.
