DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality


Study Section Meetings--Change of Location

    With this notice, the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality 
informs the public of the change of location for two meetings. The 
original notice of these meetings was published in the Federal Register 
on January 7, 2003, Volume 68, Number 4, Page 785.
    Below are the change of location of two meetings highlighted in 
bold.
[sbull] Name of Subcommittee: Health Systems Research.
    Date: February 24-25, 2003, (Open from 6 p.m. to 6:15 p.m. on 
February 24 and closed for remainder of the meeting).
    Place: AT-Doubletree Hotel (for both days), 1750 Rockville Pike, 
Conference Room TBD, Rockville, Maryland 20852.
[sbull] Name of Subcommittee: Health Care Quality and Effectiveness 
Research.
    Date: February 26-27, 2003, (Open from 7 p.m. to 7:15 p.m. on 
February 26 and closed for remainder of the meeting).
    Place: AT-Doubletree Hotel (for both days), 1750 Rockville Pike, 
Conference Room TBD, Rockville, Maryland 20852.
    Agenda items for these meetings are subject to change as priorities 
dictate.

    Dated: January 17, 2003.
Carolyn M. Clancy,
Acting Director.
