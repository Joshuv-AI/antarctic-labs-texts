DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, Mechanisms of Fusion-Driven 
Oncogenesis in Childhood Cancers and Next Generation Chemistry Centers 
for Fusion Oncoproteins, March 29, 2024, 09:00 a.m. to March 29, 2024, 
05:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 9609 Medical Center 
Drive, Room 7W260, Rockville, Maryland, 20850 which was published in 
the Federal Register on December 20, 2023, FR Doc 2023-27963, 88 FR 
88100.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting date from March 
29, 2024 to March 27, 2024. The meeting time and location will remain 
the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: January 16, 2024.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
