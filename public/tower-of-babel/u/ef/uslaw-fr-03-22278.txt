DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-52,584]


Siebel Systems, Emeryville, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 18, 2003 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the One Stop Career Center, on behalf of workers at 
Siebel Systems, Emeryville, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of August, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
