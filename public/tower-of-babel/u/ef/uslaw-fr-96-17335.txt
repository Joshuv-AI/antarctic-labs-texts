U.S. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Atlanta District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Atlanta District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Friday, July 12, 1996 at 7:30 
a.m. at the Wyndham Garden Hotels, 125 10th Street, NE., Atlanta, GA 
30309 to discuss matters as may be presented by members, staff of the 
U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Laura A. Brown, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 1720 Peachtree Road, NE., 
Suite 600, Atlanta, GA 30309, (404) 347-4147 extension 46.

    Dated: July 1, 1996.
Michael P. Novelli,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
