DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Subcommittee A--Cancer Centers, April 5, 2006, 1 p.m., to April 5, 
2006, 5 p.m., Wyndham City Center Hotel, 1143 New Hampshire Ave., NW., 
Washington, DC, 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on 
January 24, 2006, 71 FR 3860-3861.
    The meeting is amended to change the meeting location from Wyndham 
City Center Hotel, 1143 New Hampshire Ave, NW., Washington, DC 20037 to 
Wyndham Hotel, 1400 M St., NW., Washington, DC 20005. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: February 22, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
