February 2, 1000.
    The following Notice of Meeting is published pursuant to Section 
3(A) of the government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 
U.S.C. 552B:

DATE AND TIME: February 9, 2000,
10: A.M.

PLACE: Room 2C 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, DC 20426.

STATUS: Open.

notice.
