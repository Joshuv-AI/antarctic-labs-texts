DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Transit Administration


Safety Advisory 23-1 Bus-to-Person Collisions

AGENCY: Federal Transit Administration (FTA), Department of 
Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Notice of Safety Advisory.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Transit Administration (FTA) is issuing Safety 
Advisory 23-1 to encourage agencies that provide bus service to 
consider mitigation strategies to reduce bus-to-person collisions. This 
safety advisory provides guidance on identifying specific hazards that 
may cause or contribute to bus-to-person collisions, completing a 
safety risk assessment, and developing mitigations to address the 
assessed safety risks for transit agencies. FTA's Safety Advisory 23-1 
``Bus-to-Person Collisions'' is available on the agency's public 
website (https://www.transit.dot.gov/regulations-and-guidance/safety/fta-safety-advisories).
