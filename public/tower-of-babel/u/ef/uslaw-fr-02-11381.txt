DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary

32 CFR Part 286


DoD Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Program

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Final rule; amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The search and review rates for processing Freedom of 
Information Act (FOIA) requests within the Department of Defense are 
being increased at the recommendation of the General Accounting Office 
(GAO). FOIA

[[Page 31128]]

requesters will incur more direct costs for search and review, if 
applicable.

DATES: This rule is effective July 1, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: D. Maier, (703) 697-1160.
