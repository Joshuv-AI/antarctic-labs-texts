Green Island Power Authority; Albany Engineering Corporation; 
Notice of Application for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments 
and Motions To Intervene

    On August 14, 2014, Green Island Power Authority (transferor) and 
Albany Engineering Corporation (transferee) filed an application for a 
partial transfer of license of the Green Island Project located on the 
Hudson River in Saratoga and Rensselaer counties, New York.
    The transferor and transferee seek Commission approval to partially 
transfer the license for the Green Island Project to add the transferee 
as a co-licensee.
