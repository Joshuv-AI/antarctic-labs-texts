DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Clinical Center; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of change in the meeting of the NIH Advisory 
Board for Clinical Research, January 31, 2005, 2 p.m. to January 31, 
2005, 5 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 10, 10 Center 
Drive, Medical Board Room 2C116, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published 
in the Federal Register on January 12, 2005, 70 FR 2177.
    The open session will start from 10 a.m.-2 p.m. The closed session 
will be held from 2 p.m. until adjournment. The meeting will be held in 
Room 4-2551, CRC Medical Board Room. The meeting is partially Closed to 
the public.

    Dated: January 27, 2005.
Anna Snouffer,
Deputy Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
