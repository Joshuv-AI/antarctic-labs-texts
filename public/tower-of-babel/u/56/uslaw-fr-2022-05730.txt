FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD


Notice of Board Meeting; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FRTIB published a document in the Federal Register of 
March 14, 2022, concerning a notice of its March 2022 Board Meeting. 
The date of that meeting has since changed.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kimberly Weaver, Director, Office of 
External Affairs, (202) 942-1640.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of March 14, 2022, in FR Doc 2022-05319, on 
page 14265, change the date of the Board Meeting: ``March 24, 2022 at 
10:00 a.m.''

    Dated: March 15, 2022.
Dharmesh Vashee,
General Counsel.
