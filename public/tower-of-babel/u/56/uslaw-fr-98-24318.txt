Penn-Tex Corp, Inc., West Hazeleton, PA; Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was intitiated on June 22, 1998, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Penn-Tex Corp., Inc., West Hazelton, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TAW-
34,480). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 28th day of August, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
