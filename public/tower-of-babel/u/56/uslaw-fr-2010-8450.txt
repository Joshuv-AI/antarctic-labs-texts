FM TABLE OF ALLOTMENTS, Jewett, Texas

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division seeks comments on a petition filed by 
Charles Crawford, proposing the allotment of FM Channel 232A at Jewett, 
Texas, as a first local service. The reference coordinates for Channel 
232A at Jewett are 31-18-56 NL and 96-03-32 WL.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before May 17, 2010, and reply 
comments on or before June 1, 2010.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 12th Street, SW, 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Andrew J. Rhodes, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
