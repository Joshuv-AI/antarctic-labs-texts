DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of FERC Staff Attendance at Southwest Power Pool's 
Synergistic Planning Project Team and Cost Allocation Working Group 
Meetings

DATES: March 25, 2009.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby gives notice that 
members of its staff may attend the meetings of the Southwest Power 
Pool (SPP) Synergistic Planning Project Team and the SPP Cost 
Allocation Working Group, as noted below. Their attendance is part of 
the Commission's ongoing outreach efforts.
