Food Additives Permitted in Feed and Drinking Water of Animals; 
Ammonium Formate

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule; correcting amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is amending the 
regulations for food additives permitted in feed and drinking water of 
animals to correct the description of ammonium formate used as an 
acidifying agent in swine feed. This action is being taken to improve 
the accuracy of the regulations.

DATES: This rule is effective July 17, 2013.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: George K. Haibel, Center for 
Veterinary Medicine (HFV-6), Food and Drug Administration, 7519 
Standish Pl., Rockville, MD 20855, 240-276-9019, email: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: FDA has noticed the regulations for food 
additives permitted in feed and drinking water of animals do not 
correctly describe ammonium formate used as an acidifying agent in 
swine feed. At this time, FDA is making a correcting amendment. This 
action is being taken to improve the accuracy of the regulations.
