DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

National Institute of Corrections


Advisory Board Meeting

    Time and Date: 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Monday, March 4, 2002 & 8:30 
a.m. to 12 noon on Tuesday, March 5, 2002.
    Place: Portland Marriott Downtown, 1401 S.W. Naito Parkway, 
Portland, Oregon 97201.
    Status: Open.
    Matters to be Considered: Presentations on an initiative addressing 
transition from prison to community, including the Oregon Model and the 
Multnomah County Data Warehouse Project; election of new officers; 
division reports on FY 2003 Service Plan and FY 2004 budget 
recommendations; and update on Interstate Compact activities.
