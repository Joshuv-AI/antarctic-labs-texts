DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Genetic Variation 
and Evolution Study Section, October 14, 2004, 8 a.m., to October 16, 
2004, 4 p.m., The River Inn, 924 25th Street, NW., Washington, DC 
20037, which was published in the Federal Register on September 16, 
2004, 69 FR 55830-55832.
    The meeting is cancelled due to lack of quorum.

    Dated: October 17, 2004.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
