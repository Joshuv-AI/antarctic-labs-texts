DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Bureau of Transportation Statistics


Advisory Council on Transportation Statistics

AGENCY: Bureau of Transportation Statistics, DOT.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation published a document in the 
Federal Register of February 11, 1998, concerning the meeting date and

[[Page 8254]]

closing date for building admittance to the Advisory Council on 
Transportation Statistics. The document contained incorrect dates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Carolee Bush, (202) 366-6946.

Correction

    In the Federal Register issue of February 11, 1998, in FR Doc. 98-
3427, on page 7049, second column, first paragraph under the DOT 
notice, correct the meeting date to read: Friday, March 6, 1998 (rather 
than Wednesday, November 12, 1997).
    Also in the same issue and same document, on page 7049, third 
column, second paragraph, persons who planned to attend the meeting 
were told to contact Carolee Bush prior to November 10. That date 
should be corrected to read March 4.

    Dated: February 12, 1998.
Robert A. Knisely,
Executive Director, Advisory Council on Transportation Statistics.
