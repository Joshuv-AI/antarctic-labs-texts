INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION
[Finance Docket No. 32679]


Union Pacific Corporation--Securities Exemption

AGENCY: Interstate Commerce Commission.

ACTION: Notice of exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, under 49 U.S.C. 10505, exempts from the 
requirements of 49 U.S.C. 11301 the issuance, by Union Pacific 
Corporation, of certain securities in a principal amount not to exceed 
$2.3 billion.

DATES: This exemption will be effective on April 10, 1995. Petitions to 
reopen must be filed by April 27, 1995.

ADDRESSES: Send pleadings referring to Finance Docket No. 32679 to: (1) 
Office of the Secretary, Case Control Branch, Interstate Commerce 
Commission, 1201 Constitution Avenue, N.W., Washington, DC 20423; and 
(2) Carl W. von Bernuth, Esq., Senior Vice President and General 
Counsel, Union Pacific Corporation, Eighth and Eaton Avenues, 
Bethlehem, PA 18018.
