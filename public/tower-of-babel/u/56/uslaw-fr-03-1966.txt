FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-159, MB Docket No. 02-91, RM-10411]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Cheboygan, MI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of WPBN/WTOM License 
Subsidiary, Inc., substitutes DTV channel 35 for DTV channel 14 at 
Cheboygan, Michigan. See 67 FR 31170, May 9, 2002. DTV channel 35 can 
be allotted to Cheboygan, Michigan, in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates 45-39-01 N. and 84-20-37 W. with a power of 80, HAAT of 168 
meters and with a DTV service population of 68 thousand. Since the 
community of Cheboygan is located within 400 kilometers of the U.S.-
Canadian border, concurrence from the Canadian government has been 
obtained for this allotment. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.
