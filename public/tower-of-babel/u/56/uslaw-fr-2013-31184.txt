New Animal Drugs for Use in Animal Feeds; Bambermycins; 
Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration is correcting a document that 
appeared in the Federal Register of December 16, 2013 (78 FR 76059). 
The document amended the animal drug regulations to remove dairy 
replacement heifers from the pasture cattle class for which free-
choice, loose-mineral medicated feeds containing bambermycins are 
approved. The document was published with an incorrect docket number. 
This document corrects that error.

DATES: Effective on December 16, 2013.
