FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 99-1839 ; MM Docket No. 97-216; RM-9153]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Berlin and North Conway, NH

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Fuller-Jeffrey Radio of New 
England, Inc., reallots Channel 279C from Berlin to North Conway, NH, 
as the community's first local aural service, and modifies the license 
of Station WPKQ's license to specify North Conway as its community of 
license. See 62 FR 54819, October 22, 1997. The Commission found that a 
waiver of Section 73.207 of the Commission's Rules was warranted in 
this case since Station WPKQ is a grandfathered short-spaced station 
with respect to Station WKNE, Channel 279B, Keene, NH, and the 
allotment will not alter any of the existing short-spacings. Channel 
279C can be allotted to North Conway at Station WPKQ's presently 
licensed transmitter site, 44-16-14 NL; 71-18-15 WL. With this action, 
this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 25, 1999.
