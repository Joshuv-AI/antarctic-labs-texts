DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Notice of Meeting of the Advisory Committee on Minority Health

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Office of Public Health and Science, 
Office of Minority Health.

ACTION: Notice is given of the third meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Advisory Committee on Minority Health will meet on Thursday, 
September 20, 2001 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Friday, September 21, 
2001, from 8:30 a.m.-3 p.m. The meeting will be held at the Holiday Inn 
Georgetown, Mirage I Room, 2101 Wisconsin Avenue, NW., Washington, DC.
    The Advisory Committee will discuss racial and ethnic disparities 
in health, as well as, other related issues.
    The meeting is open to the public. There will be an opportunity for 
public comment which will be limited to five minutes per speaker. 
Individuals who would like to submit written statements should mail or 
fax their comments to the Office of Minority Health at least two 
business days prior to the meeting.
    For further information, please contact Ms. Patricia Norris, Office 
of Minority Health, Rockwall II Building, 5515 Security Lane, Suite 
1000, Rockville, Maryland 20852. Phone: 301-443-5084 Fax: 301-594-0767.

    Dated: August 23, 2001.
Nathan Stinson, Jr.,
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Minority Health.
