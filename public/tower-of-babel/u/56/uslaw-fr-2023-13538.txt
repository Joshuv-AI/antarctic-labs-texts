Rural and Tribal Assistance Pilot Program; Correction

ACTION: Notice of funding opportunity, correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is correcting a notice 
published on June 15, 2023 issue of the Federal Register entitled 
``Notice of Funding Opportunity to Establish Cooperative Agreements 
with Technical Assistance Providers for the Fiscal Year 2022 Thriving 
Communities Program''. This notice corrects several web links and 
updates the close date of the application period.
