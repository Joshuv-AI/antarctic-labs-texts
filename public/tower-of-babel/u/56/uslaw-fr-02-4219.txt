FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-299; MM Docket No. 98-159; RM-9290]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wallace, ID, and Bigfork, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Bee Broadcasting, Inc. directed to the Report and Order in this 
proceeding. See 66 FR 29726, published May 17, 2001. Specifically, that 
action reallotted Channel 264C to Bigfork, Montana, and modified the 
Station KSIL construction permit accordingly.

418-2177.
