POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Notice of Commission Visits

April 12, 1996.
    Notice is hereby given that members of the Postal Rate Commission 
and certain advisory staff members will visit the following businesses 
and U.S. Postal Service mail processing facility to observe their 
operations:

Columbia House, Terre Haute, Indiana, April 15-16, 1996
U.S. Postal Service Air Hub Operation, Indianapolis, Indiana, April 16, 
1996
BMG Direct Marketing, Inc., Indianapolis, Indiana, April 17, 1996
Federal Express Hub, Memphis, Tennessee, May 14-15, 1996

    Reports of these visits will be placed on file in the Commission's 
Docket Room.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Margaret P. Crenshaw, Secretary of the 
Commission at 202/789-6840.
Margaret P. Crenshaw,
Secretary.
