SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3101]


State of Vermont; Amendment #3

    In accordance with information received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning on June 
17, 1998 and continuing through July 13, 1998.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is August 29, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is March 30, 1999.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: July 27, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
