GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Federal Supply Service, Engineering Division; Creation of OF 89, 
Maintenance Record For Security Containers/Vault Doors

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration/Federal Supply Service, 
Engineering Division is creating the OF 89, Maintenance Record For 
Security Containers/Vault Doors to record all maintenance performed on 
a container or vault by locksmiths or other technical person. You can 
obtain a camera copy in two ways:

    On the internet. Address: http://www.gsa.gov/forms/forms.htm, 
or;
    From Form-X, Attn.: Barbara Williams, (202) 501-0581.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Jeffery Schatz (703) 305-6338. 
This contact is for information about completing the form only.

DATES: Effective January 19, 1999.

    Dated: December 21, 1998.
Barbara M. Williams,
Deputy Standard Optional Forms Management Officer.
