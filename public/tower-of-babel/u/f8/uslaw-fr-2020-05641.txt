DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, March 26, 2020, 8:00 a.m. to 
March 27, 2020, 7:00 p.m., Marriott Bethesda North Hotel & Conference 
Hotel, 5701 Marinelli Rd., Rockville, MD 20850 which was published in 
the Federal Register on January 30, 2020, 85 FR 5459.
    This meeting notice is amended to change the meeting location, 
times, and format. The meeting will now be held on March 26, 2020, 8:00 
a.m. to March 27, 2020, 4:00 p.m. as a teleconference at National 
Cancer Institute (NCI) Shady Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 
Rockville, MD 20850. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 13, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
