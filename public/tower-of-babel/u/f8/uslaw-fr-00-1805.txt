SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Declaration of Economic Injury Disaster #9G20


State of Washington

    King, Pierce, Snohomish, Thurston, and Whatcom Counties and the 
contigous counties of Chelan, Grays Harbor, Island, Kitsap, Kittitas, 
Lewis, Mason, Okanogan, Skagit, and Yakima in the State of Washington 
constitute an economic injury disaster area due to the effects of the 
warm water current known as El Nino beginning in 1997. Eligible small 
businesses and small agricultural cooperatives without credit available 
elsewhere may file applications for economic injury assistance for this 
disaster until the close of business on September 22, 2000 at the 
address listed below or other locally announced locations: U.S. Small 
Business Administration, Disaster Area 4 Office, P.O. Box 13795, 
Sacramento, CA 95853-4795.
    The interest rate for eligible small businesses and small 
agricultural cooperatives is 4 percent.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program No. 59002)
