Revocation of Customs Broker License

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Broker license revocation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

parts 111.52 and 111.74 of the Customs Regulations, as amended (19 CFR 
111.52 and 111.74), the following Customs broker licenses are canceled 
without prejudice.
