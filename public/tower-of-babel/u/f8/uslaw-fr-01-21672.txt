DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Invention for Licensing; Government-
Owned Invention

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention listed below is assigned to the United States 
Government as represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available 
for licensing by the Department of the Navy. U.S. Patent No. 6,264,543 
entitled ``Meat Tenderization and Sterilization Using Axial Planer 
Shockwaves.''

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the patent cited should be directed 
to Coastal Systems Station, Dahlgren Division, NSWC, 6703 W. Hwy 98, 
Code CP20L, Panama City, FL 32407-7001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Harvey A. Gilbert, Counsel, 
Coastal Systems Station, 6703 W. Hwy 98, Code CP20L, Panama City, FL 
32407-7001, telephone (850) 234-4646.
