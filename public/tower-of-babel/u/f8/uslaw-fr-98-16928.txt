DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Surface Transportation Board
[STB Ex Parte No. 290 (Sub No. 5) (98-3)]


Quarterly Rail Cost Adjustment Factor

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board.

ACTION: Approval of rail cost adjustment factor.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Board has approved the third quarter 1998 rail cost 
adjustment factor (RCAF) and cost index filed by the Association of 
American Railroads. The third quarter 1998 RCAF (Unadjusted) is 0.998. 
The third quarter 1998 RCAF (Adjusted) is 0.629. The third quarter 1998 
RCAF-5 is 0.626.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 1, 1998.
