CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION

16 CFR Part 1700


Requirements for the Special Packaging of Household Substances; 
Correction

AGENCY: Consumer Product Safety Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The CPSC corrects the amendments to its requirements under the 
Poison Prevention Packaging Act of 1970 (``PPPA'') for child-resistant 
packaging which appeared in the Federal Register on July 21, 1995 (60 
FR 37710). The correction specifies the effective date for the 
amendment to 16 CFR 1700.14 (see 60 FR at 37739, col. 2).

DATES: The amendment to 16 CFR 1700.14 will become effective July 22, 
1996.
