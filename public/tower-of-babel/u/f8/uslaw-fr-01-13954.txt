SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3320, Amdt. #6]


State of Washington

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated May 21, 2001, the above-numbered Declaration 
is hereby amended to extend the deadline for filing applications for 
physical damages as a result of this disaster to June 30, 2001.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for economic injury the deadline is November 30, 
2001.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008.)

    Dated: May 29, 2001.
James E. Rivera,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
