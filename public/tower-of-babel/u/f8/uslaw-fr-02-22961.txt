Standard Container Company, Badger Basket Division, Edgar, WI; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 1, 2002, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by the company on behalf of workers at Standard Container 
Company, Badger Basket Division, Edgar, Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 22nd day of August, 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
