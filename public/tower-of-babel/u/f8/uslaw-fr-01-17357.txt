Jacmel Jewelry; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 13, 2001, in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Jacmel Jewelry, New York, New York.
    The petition was signed by one individual who was later found not 
to be an official in either the union or company. Therefore the 
petition is invalid and no further investigation is warranted. 
Therefore, the petition investigation is terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 29th day of June, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
