DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
Office of Thrift Supervision


Standard Federal Savings Association, Frederick, MD; Replacement 
of Conservator With a Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
Subdivision (C) of Sec. 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the 
Office of Thrift Supervision has duly replaced the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as Conservator Standard Federal Savings Association, 
Frederick, Maryland (``Association''), with the Resolution Trust 
Corporation as sole Receiver for the Association on June 30, 1995.

    Dated: July 25, 1995.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Kimberly M. White,
Corporate Technician.
