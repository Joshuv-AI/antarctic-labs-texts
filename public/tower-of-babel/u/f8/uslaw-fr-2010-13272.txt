DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of FERC Attendance at the Entergy Regional State Committee 
Meeting

May 26, 2010.

Commissioners and members of its staff may attend the meeting noted 
below. Their attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach 
efforts.

Blvd., Point Clear, AL 36564, 251-928-9201.
