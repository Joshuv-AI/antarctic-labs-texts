DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration


U.S. Integrated Ocean Observing System (IOOS[supreg]) Advisory 
Committee Public Meeting Cancelled

AGENCY: U.S. Integrated Ocean Observing System (IOOS[supreg]), National 
Ocean Service (NOS), National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration 
(NOAA), Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On February 21, 2025, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
Administration published a notice in the Federal Register announcing a 
U.S. Integrated Ocean Observing System (IOOS[supreg]) Advisory 
Committee public meeting to be held from March 12th to March 14th, 
2025. This notice announces the cancellation of that public meeting.

533-9455; Email [email protected].
