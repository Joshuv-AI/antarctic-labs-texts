DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, April 6, 2004, 8 a.m. to April 
7, 2004, 5 p.m., Gaithersburg Hilton, 620 Perry Parkway, Gaithersburg, 
MD 20877 which was published in the Federal Register on January 30, 
2004, 69 FR 4524.
    The meeting is being amended due to change in meeting type from a 
regular meeting to a teleconference. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: March 5, 2004.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
