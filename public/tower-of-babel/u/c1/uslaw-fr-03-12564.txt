Potash Corporation of Saskachewan, Inc., Information Systems 
Department, North Brook, Illinois; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 25, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Potash Corporation of 
Saskatchewan, Inc., Information Systems Department, North Brook, 
Illinois, and Aurora, North Carolina.
    The petition regarding the investigation was not signed by three 
workers employed at each of the locations indicated in the petition and 
has therefore been deemed invalid. Consequently, the investigation has 
been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 9th day of May, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
