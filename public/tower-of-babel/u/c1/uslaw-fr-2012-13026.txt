PPL Montana, LLC; Notice of Meeting

    a. Date and Time of Meeting: Thursday, June 7, 2012 at 1:00 p.m. 
EDT.
    b. Place: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First St. NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, Room 3M-1.
    c. FERC Contact: Gary Cohen, (202) 502-8321 or [email protected].
    d. Purpose of Meeting: Commission staff will meet with PPL Montana, 
LLC (PPL Montana) and the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes to 
discuss issues related to PPL Montana's petition for a declaratory 
order concerning provisions of the license for Kerr Hydroelectric 
Project No. 5.
    e. All interested parties are hereby invited to attend.

    Dated: May 23, 2012.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
