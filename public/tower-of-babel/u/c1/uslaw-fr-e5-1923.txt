COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Reversal of Suspension

    In the document appearing on page 15287, FR Doc. 05-5961, in the 
issue of March 25, 2005, in the first column, the Committee published 
an effective date of April 24, 2005, for addition of the Base Supply 
Center & Individual Equipment Element, Hill Air Force Base, Utah to the 
Procurement List. In the document appearing on page 17970, FR Doc. E5-
1623, in the issue of April 8, 2005, in the first column, the Committee 
published the suspension of the effective date of April 24, 2005, for 
addition of the Base Supply Center & Individual Equipment Element, Hill 
Air Force Base, Utah to the Procurement List. This effective date was 
suspended until further notice. The Committee has decided to reinstate 
the original effective date. Accordingly, this Procurement List 
addition will be effective on April 24, 2005.

Sheryl D. Kennerly,
Director, Information Management.
