DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Pub. L. 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the Secretary's Advisory Group. The purpose of 
this meeting is to provide advice to the Secretary of the Air Force on 
short and long-term space-related strategy issues for the Air Force. 
This meeting will be closed to the public.

DATE: May 8, 2003.

[[Page 24445]]


ADDRESS: Room 4E869, The Pentagon, Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lt. Col. John J. Pernot, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm 
5D982, Washington DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4811.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
