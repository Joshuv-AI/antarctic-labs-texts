DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Notice of issuance of Record of Decision Regarding an Air-to-
Ground Training Range in Blaine County, MT

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of record of decision.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Air Force issued a Record of Decision (ROD) on May 13, 
2002. The ROD reflected the Air Force decision to develop an air-to-
ground training range in Blaine County, Montana (Alternative 1). The 
range is designed to enhance the training for the Montana Air National 
Guard's 120th Fighter Wing stationed at Great Falls International 
Airport.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: John J. Cabala, Maj, USAF (703) 697-
1731.
