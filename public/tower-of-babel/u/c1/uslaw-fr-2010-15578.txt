DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Interim Change to the Military Freight Traffic Unified Rules 
Publication (MFTURP) No. 1

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Military Surface Deployment and Distribution Command 
(SDDC) is providing notice that it will release an interim change to 
the MFTURP No. 1 on Monday, June 28, 2010. The interim change updates 
Section A, Part VI, Paragraph A, Advancing Charges (045). The update 
provides clearer guidance on when Transportation Service Providers 
(TSP) may charge for Advancing Charges.

DATES:  Effective date: June, 28, 2010.
