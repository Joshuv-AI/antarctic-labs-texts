Food and Drug Administration

National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer

[[Page 36735]]

Advisory Board which was published in the Federal Register on June 26, 
1996 (61 FR 33129).
    The National Cancer Advisory Board was scheduled to hold an open 
telephone conference on July 18, 1996 from 1 pm to approximately 2 pm. 
The date has been changed to July 29, 1996; the time remains the same.

    Dated: July 3, 1996.
Susan K. Feldman,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
