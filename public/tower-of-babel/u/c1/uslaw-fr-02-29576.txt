Digital Television Broadcast Service; Miami, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of NBC Stations Management, 
Inc., licensee of station WTVJ(TV), substitutes DTV channel 31 for DTV 
channel 30 at Miami. See 65 FR 4407, July 17, 2000. DTV channel 31 can 
be allotted to Miami, Florida, in compliance with the principle 
community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference 
coordinates 25-58-07 N. and 80-13-20 W. with a power of 1000, HAAT of 
318 meters and with a DTV service population of 3995 thousand. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 30, 2002.
