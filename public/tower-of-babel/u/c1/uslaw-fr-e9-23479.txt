FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 0


Commission Organization

CFR Correction

    In Title 47 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 0 to 19, 
revised as of October 1, 2008, on page 93, in Sec.  0.607, reinstate 
paragraph (d) to read as follows:


Sec.  0.607  Transcript, recording or minutes; availability to the 
public.

* * * * *
    (d) The Commissioner presiding at the meeting will prepare a 
statement setting out the time and place of the meeting, the names of 
persons other than Commission personnel who were present at the 
meeting, and the names of Commission personnel who participated in the 
discussion. These statements will be retained in a public file in the 
Minute and Rules Branch, Office of the Secretary.
