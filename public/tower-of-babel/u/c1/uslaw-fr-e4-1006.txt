Eureka Security Printing, Jessup, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 18, 2004, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Eureka Security Printing, Jessup, 
Pennsylvania (TA-W-54,551).
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 2nd day of April, 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
