DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Government-Industry Advisory Committee on the Operation and 
Modernization of the National Defense Stockpile

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The sixth meeting of this committee will be held on May 17, 
1996, at the Cascades Meeting Center, Williamsburg Woodlands, in 
Colonial Williamsburg, VA. The meeting is open to the public. This 
committee was established under Public Law 102-484. The meeting times 
and agenda are as follows:

TIME: 9:00 am to 2:30 pm.

AGENDA: The Committee will hear reports from the working group on Sales 
Methodology and from the working group on Stockpile Modernization.
    For additional information contact Tom Meeker at 703-767-6476.
