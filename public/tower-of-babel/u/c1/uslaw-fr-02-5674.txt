FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Technological Advisory Council Meeting Postponed

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 
U.S.C. App. 2, Public Law 92-463, as amended, this notice advises 
interested persons that the meeting of the Technological Advisory 
Council scheduled for March 20, 2002 has been cancelled and will be 
rescheduled at a later date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jeffery Goldthorp, [email protected], 
or 202-418-1096.
