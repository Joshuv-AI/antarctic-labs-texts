DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration


Center for Substance Abuse Treatment; Notice of Meeting

    Pursuant to Pub.L. 92-463, notice is hereby given of a meeting of 
the Center for Substance Abuse Treatment (CSAT) National Advisory 
Council to be held in September 1996.
    A portion of the meeting will be open and include discussion of the 
Center's policy issues and current administrative, legislative, and 
program developments.
    The meeting will include the review, discussion, and evaluation of 
individual grant applications, contract proposals, and discussion of 
information about the Center's procurement plans. Therefore a portion 
of the meeting will be closed to the public as determined by the 
Administrator, SAMHSA, in accordance withTitle 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(3), 
(4), and (6) and 5 U.S.C. App. 2, Sec. 10(d).
    A summary of the meeting and roster of council members may be 
obtained from: Ms. Joann M. Exline, National Advisory Council, CSAT, 
Rockwall II Building, Suite 619, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, Maryland 
20857, Telephone: (301) 443-8923.
    Substantive program information may be obtained from the contact 
whose name and telephone number is listed below.

    Committee Name: Center for Substance Abuse Treatment National 
Advisory Council
    Meeting Dates: September 18, 1996, 9:00 a.m.--5:00 p.m. 
September 19, 1996, 9:00 a.m.--2:15 p.m.
    Place: Holiday Inn, 5520 Wisconsin Avenue, Chase Room, Chevy 
Chase, Maryland 20856
