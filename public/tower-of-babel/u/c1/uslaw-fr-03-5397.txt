FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2596]


Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

March 3, 2003.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this public notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of this 
document is available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th 
Street, SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's 
copy contractor, Qualex International, (202) 863-2893. Oppositions to 
these petitions must be filed by March 24, 2003. See section 1.4(b)(1) 
of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition 
must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has 
expired.
    Subject: Revision of the Commission's rules to ensure compatibility 
with Enhanced 911 Emergency Calling Systems (CC Docket No. 94-102).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 3.
    Subject: Review of the Commission's Broadcast and Cable Equal 
Employment Opportunity rules and policies (MM Docket No. 98-204).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 3.

Marlene H. Dortch,
Secretary.
