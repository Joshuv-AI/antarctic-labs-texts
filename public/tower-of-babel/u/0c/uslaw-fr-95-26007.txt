DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB)
    Date of Meeting: 27 October 1995
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1500
    Place: Austin, TX
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Ad Hoc Study on ``Tank 
Modernization'' will meet for briefings and discussions on 
electromagnetic and electrothermal chemical gun propulsion and 
hypervelocity physics. This meeting will be closed to the public in 
accordance with Section 552b(c) of Title 5, U.S.C., specifically 
paragraph (1) thereof, and Title 5, U.S.C., Appendix 2, subsection 
10(d). The classified and unclassified matters to be discussed are 
so inextricably intertwined so as to preclude opening any portion of 
this meeting. For further information, please contact Michelle Diaz 
at (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
