ACTION: Notice of change in meeting location.

SUMMARY: The meeting scheduled for September 30--October 1, 1999 in

damage. The new location for the meeting is in Virginia at the Hyatt 
Arlington, 1325 Wilson Blvd., Arlington, Va., (703) 525-1234. This 
meeting was announced in the Federal Register on August 18, 1999, 64 FR 
44912.
