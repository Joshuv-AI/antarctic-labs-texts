FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Revocations

    The Federal Maritime Commission hereby gives notice that the 
following freight forwarder licenses have been revoked pursuant to 
section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718) and the 
regulations of the

[[Page 13386]]

Commission pertaining to the licensing of ocean freight forwarders, 
effective on the corresponding revocation dates shown below:
    License Number: 4096.
    Name: American Cargo Forwarding, Inc.
    Address: 11020 King Street, Suite 350, Overland Park, KS 66210.
    Date Revoked: February 21, 1997.
    Reason: Failed to maintain a valid surety bond.

    License Number: 3109.
    Name: Denise Zappola d/b/a Corporate Relocation Services.
    Address: 284 McClean Avenue, Staten Island, NY 10305.
    Date Revoked: August 29, 1996.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Director, Bureau of Tariffs, Certification and Licensing.
