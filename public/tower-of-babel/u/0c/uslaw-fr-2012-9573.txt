DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Intent To Grant Exclusive Patent License; Aurora 
Technologies, LLC

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to Aurora Technologies, LLC a revocable, nonassignable, 
exclusive license to practice in the United States, the Government-
owned inventions described in Navy Case 101713 entitled Laser Treated 
Heat Transfer Surface.//Navy Case 101445 entitled Surface Enhancement 
Via Applied Laser Energy.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license must file 
written objections along with supporting evidence, if any, not later 
than May 7, 2012.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Naval Surface 
Warfare Center, Crane Div, Code OOL, Bldg. 2, 300 Highway 361, Crane, 
IN 47522-5001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Christopher Monsey, Naval Surface 
Warfare Center, Crane Div, Code OOL, Bldg 2, 300 Highway 361, Crane, IN 
47522-5001, telephone 812-854-4100.
