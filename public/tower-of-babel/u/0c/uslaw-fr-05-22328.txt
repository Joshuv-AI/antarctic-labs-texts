Agilent Technologies, Inc. Wireless Business Unit a Division of 
the Electronic Measurements Group Loveland, CO; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 7, 2005, in response to a 
petition filed by a State agency representative on behalf of workers of 
Agilent Technologies, Inc., Wireless Business Unit, a division of the 
Electronics Measurements Group, Loveland, Colorado.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by a current 
certification (TA-W-57,742J) issued on September 30, 2005, applicable 
to all workers of Agilent Technologies, Inc., Electronics Measurement 
Group, Loveland, Colorado. Consequently, further investigation in this 
case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 20th day of October, 2005.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
