DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Highway Administration


Environmental Impact Statement: Allegheny, Beaver and Butler 
Counties, PA

AGENCY: Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), Department of 
Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Cancellation of the notice of intent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice rescinds the previous Notice of Intent (issued 
February 10, 1997--Vol. 62, No. 27) to prepare an Environmental Impact 
Statement for a proposed transportation improvement in the vicinity of 
northern Allegheny County and southern Beaver and Butler Counties.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David W. Cough, P.E., Director of 
Operations, Federal Highway Administration, Pennsylvania Division 
Office, 228 Walnut Street, Room 508, Harrisburg, PA 17101-1720, 
Telephone (717) 221-3411--OR--Tom Fox, Assistant District Engineer for 
Design, Pennsylvania Department of Transportation, District 11-0, 45 
Thoms Run Road, Bridgeville, PA 15017, Telephone (412) 429-5005.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Additional traffic analyses have indicated 
that no single reasonable and cost effective alternative exists for the 
large-scale study corridor. Environmental Assessments and/or 
Categorical Exclusion Evaluations may be pursued, as appropriate, based 
on a revised project scoping.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Number 20.205, 
Highway Planning and Construction. The regulations implementing 
Executive Order 12372 regarding intergovernmental consultation on 
Federal programs and activities apply to this program.)
