New England Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of a meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The New England Fishery Management Council (Council) has 
cancelled a public meeting of its Monkfish Committee that was scheduled 
for Wednesday, September 1, 2021.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Thomas A. Nies, Executive Director, 
New England Fishery Management Council; telephone: (978) 465-0492.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The original meeting notice published in the 
Federal Register on Monday, August 16, 2021 (86 FR 45711).
    The meeting will be rescheduled at a later dated and announced in 
the Federal Register.
    Authority: 16 U.S.C. 1801 et seq.
