GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Office of Communications; Cancellation of an Optional Form by the 
Department of State

AGENCY: Office of Communications, GSA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of State cancelled the following Optional Form 
because of low usage: OF 126, Foreign Service Residence and Dependency 
Report.

DATES: Effective October 12, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Charles Cunningham, Department of 
State, (202) 312-9605.

    Dated: October 2, 2001.
