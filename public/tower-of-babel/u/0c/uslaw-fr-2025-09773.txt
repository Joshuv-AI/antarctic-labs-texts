Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2024-2497.
    Petitioner: Pyka Inc.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  61.3(a)(1)(i), 
61.3(c)(1), 61.23(a)(2), 91.119(c), 91.121, 91.151(a),291.403(b), 
91.405(a), 91.407(a)(1), 91.417(a), and 91.417(b).
    Description of Relief Sought: Pyka Inc. (Pyka) seeks to amend their 
exemption, Exemption No. 23028, which allows Pyka to operate the Pyka 
Pelican and Pyka Pelican Cargo unmanned aircraft system (UAS), weighing 
55 pounds (lbs.) or greater, for the purposes described in the 
associated Special Airworthiness Certificate--Experimental Category 
(SAC-EC). Pyka's amendment, if granted, would allow for night 
operations, beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) operations in scenarios 
where the unmanned aircraft system (UAS) is operated within the visual 
line of sight (VLOS) of a visual observer (VO), and remove the 
requirement that the pilot in command (PIC) pass a 14 CFR part 61 
airman knowledge test.
