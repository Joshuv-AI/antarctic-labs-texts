DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Advisory Circular 25.24, Sustained Engine Imbalance

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance of advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Advisory Circular (AC) 
25,24, Sustained Engine Imbalance. This AC sets forth an acceptable 
means, but not the only means, of demonstrating compliance with the 
provisions of title 14, Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR), part 25 
related to the aircraft design for sustained engine rotor imbalance for 
transport category airplanes. Like all ACs, it is not regulatory but is 
to provide guidance for applicants in demonstrating compliance with the 
objective safety standards set forth in the rule.

DATES: Advisory Circular 25.24 was issued by the Manager, Transport 
Airplane Directorate, Aircraft Certification Service, ANM-100, on 
August 2, 2000.
