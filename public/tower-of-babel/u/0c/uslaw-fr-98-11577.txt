Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Atlantic City Electric Company; Notice of Filing

April 27, 1998.
    Take notice that on March 2, 1998, Atlantic City Electric Company 
(Atlantic City Electric) in compliance with the Commission's January 
29, 1998 order, 82 FERC para. 61,068, filed revised network tariff 
service rates based on the annual peak which the Commission directed 
the PJM Companies to file consistent with the use of such peaks for the 
allocation of fixed transmission rights. Atlantic City Electric 
requests that this compliance fling be allowed to become effective on 
April 1, 1998. Atlantic City Electric served copies of the filing on 
persons on the service list.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE, Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure, 18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214. All such motions or protests 
should be filed on or before May 8, 1998. Protests filed with the 
Commission will be considered by it in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken but will not serve to make the protestants parties 
to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a 
motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
