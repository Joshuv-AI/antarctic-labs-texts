DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Rural Housing Service


Notice of Funds Availability (NOFA) Inviting Applications for the 
Rural Community Development Initiative (RCDI); Correction

AGENCY: Rural Housing Service, USDA.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Rural Housing Service (RHS) corrects a notice published 
March 17, 2000 (65 FR 14525). This action is taken to correct the 
definition of ``low-income community''.
    Accordingly, the notice published March 17, 2000 (65 FR 14525), is 
corrected as follows:
    On page 14525 in the third column under ``Definitions for RCDI 
Purposes'', the definition for ``Low-income community'' should read 
``Low-income community--a city, town, village, county, parish, borough, 
or federally recognized Indian tribe with a median household income at, 
or below, 80 percent of the statewide median household income.''

    Dated: April 26, 2000.
Inga Smulkstys,
Acting Under Secretary, Rural Development.
