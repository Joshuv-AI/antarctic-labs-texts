OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION

    12. Finance Project--Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Angola, South Africa, 
Ivory Coast, Tanzania and Uganda.

    14. Approval of November 19, 2009 Minutes (Closed Session).
    15. Pending Major Projects.
