SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board; Public 
Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration National Small Business 
Development Center Advisory Board will hold a public meeting on Monday, 
March 5, 2001, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the Crystal City Gateway Hotel, 
Crystal City, Virginia to discuss such matters as may be presented by 
members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present.
    For further information, please write or call Ellen Thrasher, U.S. 
Small Business Administration, 409 Third Street, SW., Fourth Floor, 
Washington, DC 20416. Telephone number (202) 205-6817.

Nancyellen Gentile,
Committee Management Officer.
