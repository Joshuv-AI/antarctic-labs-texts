DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


Board of Scientific Counselors, National Center for Infectious 
Diseases (BSC, NCID): Notice of Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Board of Scientific Counselors, 
National Center for Infectious Diseases (BSC, NCID), Centers for 
Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), Department of Health and Human 
Services, has been renewed for a 2-year period beginning October 31, 
1997, through October 31, 1999.
    For further information, contact Joseph E. McDade, Ph.D., Acting 
Deputy Director, National Center for Infectious Diseases, CDC, 1600 
Clifton Road, m/s C20, Atlanta, Georgia 30333.

    Dated: November 19, 1997.
Carolyn J. Russell,
Director, Management Analysis and Services Office, Centers for Disease 
Control and Prevention (CDC).
