CEMCO, Willits, CA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 16, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at CEMCO, 
Willits, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 14th day of July 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
