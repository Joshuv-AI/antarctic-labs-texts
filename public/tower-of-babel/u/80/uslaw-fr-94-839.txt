NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

 

Advisory Committee on Nuclear Waste; Postponement of Meeting

    A meeting of the 61st Advisory Committee on Nuclear Waste (ACNW) 
scheduled to be held on Wednesday and Thursday, January 19 and 20, 
1994, in room P-110, 7920 Norfolk Avenue, Bethesda, Maryland has been 
postponed until February 23 and 24, 1994. Notice of the meeting was 
published in the Federal Register on Thursday, December 23, 1993 (58 FR 
68180).
    For further information contact: Mr. Richard Major, Chief, Nuclear 
Waste Branch, (telephone: 301/492-8109) between 7:30 a.m. and 4:15 p.m. 
(EST).

    Dated: January 7, 1994.
John C. Hoyle,
Advisory Committee Management Officer.
