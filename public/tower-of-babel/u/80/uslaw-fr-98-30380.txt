SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region 1 Advisory Council Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Region 1 Advisory Council, 
located in the geographical area of Augusta, will hold a public meeting 
at 10 a.m. on Monday, December 8th, 1998 at the Eastern Maine 
Development Corporation, 1 Cumberland Place, Suite 300, Bangor, Maine, 
to discuss such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the 
U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mary McAleney, District Director, U.S. 
Small Business Administration, 40 Western Avenue, Augusta, Maine 04330, 
207-622-8242.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, External Affairs.
