NATIONAL COUNCIL ON DISABILITY


Youth Advisory Committee Meeting (Teleconference)

    Time and Date: 12:30 p.m., EST, February 4, 2005.
    Place: National Council on Disability, 1331 F Street, NW., Suite 
850, Washington, DC.
    Agency: National Council on Disability (NCD).

    Contact Person for More Information: Geraldine Drake Hawkins, 
Ph.D., Program Analyst, National Council on Disability, 1331 F Street, 
NW., Suite 850, Washington, DC 20004; (202) 272-2004 (voice), (202) 
272-2074 (TTY), (202) 272-2022 (fax), [email protected] (e-mail).
    Youth Advisory Committee Mission: The purpose of NCD's Youth 
Advisory Committee is to provide input into NCD activities consistent 
with the values and goals of the Americans with Disabilities Act.
