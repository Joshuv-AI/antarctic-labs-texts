SUMMARY: The proposed information collection requirement described has

as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act. The Department is 
soliciting comments on the public comments on the subject proposal.

DATES: Comments Due Date: November 1, 1999.

Control Number and should be sent to: Joseph F. Lackey, Jr., HUD Desk

Office Building, Washington, DC 20503.

    Title: Personal Financial and Credit statement.
    OMB Control Number: 2502-0001.
    Type of submission: Reinstate without Change.
    Need and use of the information: The information to be collected
