COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Guam Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning an in-person meeting of the Guam Advisory 
Committee. This meeting, scheduled for Friday, June 2, 2023, at 9:00 
a.m. ChST, has been cancelled due to Typhoon Mawar. The notice is in 
the Federal Register of Thursday, May 11, 2023, in FR Document Number 
2023-10088, in the first and second columns of page 30276.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Liliana Schiller, Support Services 
Specialist, at [email protected] or (312) 353-8311.

    Dated: May 30, 2023.
David Mussatt,
Supervisory Chief, Regional Programs Unit.
