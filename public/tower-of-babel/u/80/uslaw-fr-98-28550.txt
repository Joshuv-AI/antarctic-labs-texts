OFFICE OF THE UNITED STATES TRADE REPRESENTATIVE


Notice of Cancellation of Meeting of the Trade and Environment 
Policy Advisory Committee (TEPAC)

AGENCY: Office of the United States Trade Representative.

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A notice was published in the Federal Register dated October 
16, 1998, Volume number 63, FR DOC. 98-27861, page 55673, announcing a 
meeting of the Trade and Environment Policy Advisory Committee (TEPAC) 
scheduled for October 30, 1998 from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The meeting 
was to be open to the public from 4:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. and closed to 
the public from 1:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. However, due to scheduling 
conflicts the meeting had to be canceled.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Bill Daley, Office of the United States Trade Representative, (202) 
395-6120.
Pate Felts,
Assistant U.S. Trade Representative.
