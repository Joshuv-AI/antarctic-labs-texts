47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2055; MM Docket No. 01-89; RM-10094]


Television Broadcasting Services; Decatur, Plano, TX.

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission dismisses a petition for rule making filed by 
Word of God Fellowship, Inc. (``petitioner''), requesting the 
reallotment of Television Channel 29 from Decatur to Plano, Texas as 
the community's first local transmission service. Petitioner filed no 
comments in response to the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Victoria M. McCauley, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.

Report and Order, MM Docket No. 01-89 adopted August 22, 2001 and 
released August 31, 2001. The full text of this Commission decision is
