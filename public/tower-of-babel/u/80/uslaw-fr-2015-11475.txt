DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Determination Concerning a Petition To Add a Class of Employees 
To the Special Exposure Cohort

AGENCY: National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH), 
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Department of Health and 
Human Services (HHS).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: HHS gives notice of a determination concerning a petition to 
add a class of employees from the St. Louis Airport Storage Site 
(SLAPS) in St. Louis, Missouri, to the Special Exposure Cohort (SEC) 
under the Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program 
Act of 2000 (EEOICPA).

[[Page 27330]]


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Stuart L. Hinnefeld, Director, 
Division of Compensation Analysis and Support, National Institute for 
Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH), 1090 Tusculum Avenue, MS C-47, 
Cincinnati, OH 45226-1938, Telephone 1-877-222-7570. Information 
requests can also be submitted by email to [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

    Authority: [42U.S.C.7384q].

    On April 9, 2015, the Secretary of HHS determined that the 
following class of employees does not meet the statutory criteria for 
addition to the SEC as authorized under EEOICPA:

    All employees of the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), its 
predecessor agencies, and their contractors and subcontractors who 
worked at the St. Louis Airport Storage Site in St. Louis, Missouri, 
from November 3, 1971, through December 31, 1973, and from January 
1, 1984,through December 31, 1998.
