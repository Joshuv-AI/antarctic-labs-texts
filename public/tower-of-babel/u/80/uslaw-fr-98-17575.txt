DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Project No. 1962-000, CA]


Pacific Gas & Electric Company; Notice of Public Meeting to 
Discuss Processing Alternatives for the Application for Relicensing of 
the Rock Creek-Cresta Hydroelectric Project

June 26, 1998.
    Take notice that the Commission staff will hold a meeting with 
Pacific Gas & Electric Company (PG&E), the applicant for the Rock 
Creek-Cresta Hydroelectric Project No. 1962, the U.S. Forest Service, 
and representatives of the intervenors in the relicensing proceeding 
for the project. The project is located on the North Fork Feather River 
in Plumas County, California. The meeting will be held on Tuesday, July 
14, 1998, from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at the U.S. Fish and Wildlife 
Service's offices at 3310 El Camino Street, Suite 130, in Sacramento, 
California.
    The purpose of the meeting is to discuss alternatives for 
processing the application for relicensing of the Rock Creek-Cresta 
Project. All interested persons are invited to attend the meeting.
    For further information, please contact Dianne Rodman at (202) 219-
2830.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
