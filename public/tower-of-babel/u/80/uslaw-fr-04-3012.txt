Tree Source Industries, Inc., Portland, Oregon; Dismissal of 
Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Tree Source Industries, Inc., 
Portland, Oregon. The application contained no new substantial 
information which would bear importantly on the Department's 
determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-53,106; Tree Source Industries, Inc. Portland, Oregon (February 2, 
2003)

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of February 2004.
Timothy Sullivan,
