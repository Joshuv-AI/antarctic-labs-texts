Regulation (FAR) in order to make editorial changes.

PART 14--SEALED BIDDING

0
2. In section 14.403, revise paragraph (c) to read as follows:


14.403  Recording of Bids.

* * * * *
    (c) The forms identified in paragraph (a) of this section need not

or petroleum products or by the Defense Supply Center Philadelphia for 
perishable subsistence items.
