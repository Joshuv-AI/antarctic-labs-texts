NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice: (02-149)]


Availability of Annual Report on Alternative Fuel Vehicle (AFV) 
Acquisitions for Fiscal Years 1999, 2000, and 2001

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice of availability of NASA's annual report on its 
alternative fuel vehicle (AFV) acquisitions for fiscal years 1999, 
2000, and 2001.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Under the Energy Policy Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 13211-13219) as 
amended by the Energy Conservation Reauthorization Act of 1998 (Pub. L. 
105-388), and Executive Order 13149 (April 2000), ``Greening the 
Government Through Federal Fleet and Transportation Efficiency,'' 
NASA's annual AFV reports are available on the following NASA Web site: 
http://www.hq.nasa.gov/office/codej/codejlg/afv.htm.

ADDRESSES: Logistics Management Office, NASA Headquarters, Code JG, 300 
E Street SW., Washington, DC 20546-0001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: William Gookin, (202) 358-2306, or 
[email protected].

Jeffrey E. Sutton,
Assistant Administrator for Management Systems.
