DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Colorado Interstate Gas Company; Notice of Technical Conference

October 12, 2000.
    On June 15, 2000, Colorado Interstate Gas Company (CIG) filed in 
compliance with Order No. 637. A technical conference to discuss the 
various issues raised by CIG's filing was held on October 3, 2000.
    Take notice that a second technical conference to discuss the issue 
of segmentation on CIG's system, and remaining issues raised by CIG's 
filing, will be held Thursday, October 26, 2000, at 9 am in a room to 
be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
