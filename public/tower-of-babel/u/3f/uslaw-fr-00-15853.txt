SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Section 306(c) of the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery Conservation and 
Management Act (16 U.S.C. 1801 et seq.), as amended by section 112(c) 
of the Sustainable Fisheries Act (Public Law 104-297), requires that 
foreign fishing vessels operating in internal U.S. waters report the 
tonnage and location of fish received from vessels of the United 
States. NOAA uses the information collected to properly allocate the 
reported receipts of fish to the area(s) from which the fish were 
caught, and to ensure that all transfers of fish from U.S. vessels to 
foreign vessels are accounted for in monitoring quotas and for resource 
assessment and statistical reporting purposes.
