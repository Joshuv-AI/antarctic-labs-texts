FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

* * * * *

Date and Time: Tuesday, July 20, 2004 at 10 a.m.

Place: 999 E Street, NW., Washington, DC

Status: This meeting will be closed to the public.

Items To Be Discussed:
    Compliance matters pursuant to 2 U.S.C. 437.

    Advisory Opinion 2004-21: On Time Systems, Inc. by Matthew L. 
Ginsberg, Chief Executive Officer.

of Representatives.
    Routine Administrative Matters.
