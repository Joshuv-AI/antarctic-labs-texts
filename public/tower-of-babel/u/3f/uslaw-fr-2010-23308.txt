DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 44-2010]


Termination of Review of Sourcing Change, Foreign-Trade Subzone 
61H, Baxter Healthcare of Puerto Rico, (Inhalation Anesthetics 
Manufacturing), Guayama, PR

    Notice is hereby given of termination of a sourcing change review 
related to certain chemical ingredients at the manufacturing facility 
of Baxter Healthcare of Puerto Rico located in Guayama, Puerto Rico (75 
FR 40795-40796, 7/14/2010). The termination is based on an analysis of 
the record and resulting determination that no further action is 
warranted.

    Dated: September 3, 2010.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
