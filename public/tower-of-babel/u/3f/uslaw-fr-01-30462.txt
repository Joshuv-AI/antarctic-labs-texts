DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Armed Forces Epidemiological Board (AFEB); Closed Meeting

AGENCY: Office of The Surgeon General, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of closed meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 63685]]

SUMMARY: In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of Public Law 92-463, The 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, this announces the forthcoming select 
AFEB subcommittee meeting. This Board will meet via teleconference on 
20 December 2001. The purpose of the meeting is to review a draft 
manuscript and make recommendations on potential policy implications. 
The manuscript has not formally been published and is protected by 
intellectual property rights.
    This meeting will be closed to the public.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lt Col James R. Riddle, Executive 
Secretary, Armed Forces Epidemiological Board, Skyline Six, 5109 
Leesburg Pike, Room 682, Falls Church, Virginia 22041-3258, (703) 681-
8012/3.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.

Luz D. Ortiz,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
