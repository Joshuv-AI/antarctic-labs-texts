Elimination of Certain Documentation Requirements for Articles

AGENCY: Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects a final rule document which amended the 
Customs Regulations by removing certain documentation requirements 
relating to the entry of articles claimed to be entitled to a partial 
duty exemption or duty-free treatment under various special tariff 
provisions or programs. The correction involves an amendatory 
instruction regarding Sec. 178.2 of the Customs Regulations which lists 
the control numbers for approvals of information collection 
requirements.

EFFECTIVE DATE: This correction is effective June 16, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Craig Walker, Office of Regulations 
and Rulings, 202-482-6980.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    On May 17, 1994, Customs published in the Federal Register (59 FR 
25563) a final rule document amending the Customs Regulations to remove 
certain documentation requirements relating to the entry of articles 
claimed to be entitled to a partial duty exemption or duty-free 
treatment under various special tariff provisions or programs. Among 
the amendments were consequential amendments to Sec. 178.2 of the 
Customs Regulations (19 CFR 178.2) concerning the revision, removal or 
addition of listings in the table setting forth the listing of Office 
of Management and Budget control numbers for approvals of information 
collection requirements pursuant to the Paperwork Reduction Act of 
1980. One of the removed listings referred to in the amendatory 
instruction was the listing for Sec. 10.8(e). However, this reference 
was incorrect because, in an interim rule document published in the 
Federal Register on December 30, 1993 (58 FR 69460), Sec. 178.2 had 
been amended by removing from the table the reference to 
``Sec. 10.8(e)'' and adding, in its place, the reference 
``Sec. 10.8(f)''. Accordingly, this document corrects the amendatory 
instruction for Sec. 178.2 in the May 17, 1994, final rule document to 
properly refer to the removal of Sec. 10.8(f).

Correction of Publication

    In the document published in the Federal Register as T.D. 94-47 on 
May 17, 1994 (59 FR 25563), on page 25571, second and third columns, 
the amendatory instruction for Sec. 178.2 is corrected to read as 
follows:

listings for Secs. 10.8, 10.9, and 10.198 to read as follows:''
