Agenda

    The public is invited to participate in workshops to hear and 
discuss the potential for the New England Fishery Management Council to 
regulate fisheries on Georges Bank using what is known as Ecosystem-
Based Fishery Management or EBFM. The Council developed an example 
Fishery Ecosystem Plan (eFEP) that describes a general framework to 
account for trophic interactions and managed groups of stocks as a 
stock complex to reduce fishing costs and bycatch technical 
interactions. It also discusses management options that need to be 
considered to implement EBFM policies. EBFM Public Outreach Materials 
are available on the Council's website and include a short introductory 
video, infographics, stakeholder brochures, presentations, and 
interactive tools.
