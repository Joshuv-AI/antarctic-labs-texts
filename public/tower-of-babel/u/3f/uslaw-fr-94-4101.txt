NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

 

Advisory Committee on the Medical Uses of Isotopes: Meeting 
Notice

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The meeting of the Advisory Committee on the Medical Uses of 
Isotopes (ACMUI) scheduled for February 24, 1994, has been canceled. 
The notice for this meeting appeared in the Federal Register on 
February 9, 1994 (59 FR 6061).
    The next meeting of the ACMUI will be held on May 19 and 20, 1994. 
Information regarding this meeting will be noticed no less than 15 days 
before the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION, CONTACT: Sally L. Merchant, Office of Nuclear 
Material Safety and Safeguards, MS 6-H-3, U.S. Nuclear Regulatory 
Commission, Washington, DC 20555, Telephone (301) 504-2637.

    Dated: February 17, 1994.
John C. Hoyle,
Advisory Committee Management Officer.
