POSTAL RATE COMMISSION


Notice of Visit

AGENCY: Postal Rate Commission.

ACTION: Notice of visit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pitney Bowes employees will visit the Commission to present a 
briefing at 10:00 a.m. on Pitney Bowes' ``fast forward'' system and 
joint Pitney Bowes/Postal Service activities. This visit has been 
scheduled as a follow-up to address questions raised by an earlier 
Commission visit to Pitney Bowes facilities.

DATES: The visit is scheduled for Monday, October 25, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Postal Rate Commission (Conference Room), 1333 H Street, 
NW., Suite 300, Washington, DC 20268-0001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Margaret P. Crenshaw, Secretary, 
Postal Rate Commission, Suite 300, 1333 H Street, NW., Washington, DC 
20268-0001 (202) 789-6840.

    Dated: October 15, 1999.
Margaret P. Crenshaw,
Secretary.
