SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    NOAA operates two space-based data collection systems: the 
Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite (GOES) Data 
Collection System and the Argos Data Collection System. Both systems 
are operated to support environmental applications. Since the entire 
capacity of the systems is not used by NOAA, this extra capacity is 
made available to other users who meet

[[Page 63236]]

certain criteria set forth in 15 CFR part 911.

II. Method of Collection

    Applications are submitted on forms that respond to requirements 
detailed in 15 CFR part 911.

IV. Request for Comments

    Comments are invited on: (a) Whether the proposed collection of 
information is necessary for the proper performance of the functions of

(c) ways to enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of the 
information to be collected; and (d) ways to minimize the burden of the 
collection of information on respondents, including through the use of 
automated collection techniques or other forms of information 
technology.
