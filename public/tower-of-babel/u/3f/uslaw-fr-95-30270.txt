Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the public.

Children's Sleepwear

    The Commission will consider whether to issue final amendments 
of the children's sleepwear flammability standards to exempt tight 
fitting sleepwear garments and sleepwear garments intended for 
children younger than six months of age.
    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.
