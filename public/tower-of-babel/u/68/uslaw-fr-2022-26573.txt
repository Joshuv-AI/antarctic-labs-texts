COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Texas Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Texas Advisory Committee. 
The meeting scheduled for Wednesday, December 14, 2022, at 1 p.m. (CT) 
is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of Wednesday, 
November 16, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-24970, in the first and second 
column of page 68673.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Brooke Peery, (202) 701-1376, 
[email protected].
