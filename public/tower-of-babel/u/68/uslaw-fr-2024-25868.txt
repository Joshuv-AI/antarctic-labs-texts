DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-49-2024]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 250; Withdrawal of Notification of 
Proposed Production Activity; Boss Laser LLC; (Laser Machines); 
Sanford, Florida

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the notification of 
proposed production activity submitted by Boss Laser LLC for its 
facility in Sanford, Florida, within FTZ 250. The notification was 
docketed on September 17, 2024 (89 FR 77472, September 23, 2024). The 
withdrawal was requested by Boss Laser LLC on October 31, 2024.

    Dated: November 1, 2024.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
