DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Defense Science Board Task Force on Human Resources Strategy

ACTION: Notice of Advisory Committee Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Defense Science Board Task Force on Human Resources 
Strategy will meet in open session on December 21-22, 1998 at the 
Pentagon, Arlington, Virginia.
    The mission of the Defense Science Board is to advise the Secretary 
of Defense and the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and 
Technology

[[Page 68757]]

on scientific and technical matters as they affect the perceived needs 
of the Department of Defense.
    Persons interested in further information should call Ms. Julie 
Evans at (703) 527-5410.

    Dated: December 8, 1998.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
