DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 11087]


Advisory Committee on Historical Diplomatic Documentation; Notice 
of Cancellation of Open Meeting

    Due to concerns surrounding the spread of COVID-19, the Advisory 
Committee on Historical Diplomatic Documentation is cancelling its open 
meeting previously scheduled on Monday, June 1. If the meeting is 
rescheduled, the Department of State will issue a Federal Register 
Notice with details.
    For additional information, contact Adam Howard, Office of the 
Historian, at [email protected].

Zachary A. Parker,
Director, Office of Directives Management, U.S. Department of State.
