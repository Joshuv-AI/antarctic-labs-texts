Lower Snake River District Resource Advisory Council; Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Lower Snake River District Resource Advisory Council will 
meet May 14, 1996 at 9:00 a.m. The primary agenda item for the meeting 
will be to review consolidated statewide standards for rangeland health 
and guidelines for managing livestock grazing on federal public lands 
in Idaho. A public comment period will be held at 9:30 a.m.

DATES: May 14, 1996 beginning at 9:00 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meetings will be held in the main conference room at the 
National Interagency Fire Center administration building, 3833 S. 
Development Ave., Boise, Idaho.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barry Rose, Lower Snake River District 
Office, 3948 Development Avenue, Boise, Idaho 83705, (208) 384-3393.

    Dated: April 5, 1996.
Barry Rose,
Acting District Manager.
