    Name of SEP: Cancer Therapy with Activated Natural Killer Cells.
    Date: February 10, 1997.
    Time: 1 p.m.
    Place: Teleconference, Executive Plaza North, Room 635E, 6130 
Executive Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892.

North, Room 635E, 6130 Executive Boulevard, MSC 7405, Bethesda, MD 
20892-7405, telephone: 301/402-0371.

forth in secs. 552(c)(4) and 552(c)(6), Title 5, U.S.C. Applications
