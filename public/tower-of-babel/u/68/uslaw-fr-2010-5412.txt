    Date Filed: February 26, 2010.

    Subject: PTC COMP Mail Vote 626, Resolution 011a, Mileage Manual 
Non TC Member/Non IATA Carrier Sectors. Intended effective date: 15 
March for implementation 1 April 2010.

    Date Filed: February 26, 2010.

    Subject: Mail Vote 625--Resolution 010p, TC3 Japan, Korea-South 
East Asia, Special Passenger Amending Resolution from Korea (Rep. of) 
to Guam, Northern Mariana Islands (Memo 1357). Intended effective date: 
22 February 2010.
