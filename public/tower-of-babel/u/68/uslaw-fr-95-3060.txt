Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc., et al.; Electric 
Rate and Corporate Regulation Filings

January 31, 1995.
    Take notice that the following filings have been made with the 
Commission:

1. Consolidated Edison Co. of New York, Inc.

[Docket No. ER94-175-000]

    Take notice that on January 20, 1995, Consolidated Edison Company 
of New York, Inc. (``Con Edison''), tendered for filing an amendment to 
its agreement with Long Island Lighting Company (``LILCO'') to provide 
for the purchase and sale of energy and capacity subject to cost based 
ceiling rates. The ceiling rate for energy is 100 percent of the 
Seller's Incremental Cost (``SIC'') plus up to 10 percent of the SIC 
(where such 10 percent is limited to 1 mill per Kwhr when the SIC in 
the hour reflects a purchased power resource). The ceiling rate for 
capacity sold by Con Edison is $7.70 per megawatt hour. The ceiling 
rate for capacity sold by LILCO is $7.44 per megawatt hour.
    Con Edison states that a copy of this filing has been served by 
mail upon LILCO.
    Comment date: February 14, 1995, in accordance with Standard 
Paragraph E at the end of this notice.
