NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Notice of Change in Time of Meeting

    It has been determined by the National Credit Union Administration 
Board that it will be necessary to change the time of the previously 
announced open Board meeting (Federal Register, Vol. 68, No. 120, page 
37177, June 23, 2003) scheduled for Thursday, June 26, 2003 at 10 a.m. 
The meeting will now be held at 2 p.m. Earlier announcement of this 
change was not possible.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Becky Baker, Secretary of the Board, 
Telephone: (703) 518-6304.

Becky Baker,
Secretary of the Board.
