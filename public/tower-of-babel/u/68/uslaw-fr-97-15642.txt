DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Drug Enforcement Administration


Importation of Controlled Substances Application for Radian 
International LLC; Notice of Correction

in the issue of Monday, May 19, 1997, the third paragraph should read: 
``The firm plans to import small quantities of the listed controlled 
substances for the manufacture of analytical reference standards.''

Enforcement Administration.
