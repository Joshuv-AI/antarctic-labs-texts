Workshop on Characterizing and Presenting Summary Chemical

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice of Postponement of a Workshop.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice is intended to advise interested persons that the 
Workshop on Characterizing and Presenting Summary Chemical Exposure 
Assessment Information scheduled for September 25 and 26, 2001 has been 
postponed. The Workshop Notice appeared in the Federal Register on 
September 17, 2001, (66 FR 48056) (FRL-6800-1). A new date has not been 
set for the workshop.

20460; telephone number: (202) 260-6146; e-mail: [email protected].
