GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE


National Health Care Workforce Commission

AGENCY: Government Accountability Office (GAO).

ACTION: Notice on letters of nomination.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act gave the 
Comptroller General of the United States responsibility for appointing 
15 members to the National Health Care Workforce Commission, with 
appointments to be made not later than September 30th, 2010. 
Qualifications of the commissioners are identified in Section 5101 of 
the Act. For these appointments, I am announcing the following: Letters 
of nomination and resumes should be submitted by June 30th, 2010 to 
ensure adequate opportunity for review and consideration of nominees 
prior to appointment of members. Letters of nomination and resumes can 
be sent to either the e-mail or mailing address listed below.

ADDRESSES: Nominations can be submitted by either of the following: E-
mail: [email protected]. Mail: GAO Health Care, Attention: National 
Health Care Workforce Commission Nominations, 441 G Street, NW., 
Washington, DC 20548.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: GAO: Office of Public Affairs, (202) 
512-4800.

Gene L. Dodaro,
Acting Comptroller General of the United States.
