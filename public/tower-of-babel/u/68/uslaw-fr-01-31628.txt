Kentucky, Electric Steel, Ashland, KY; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of Trade Act of 1974, an investigation was 
initiated on June 18, 2001 in response to a worker petition which was 
filed on the same date on behalf of workers at Kentucky Electric Steel, 
Ashland, Kentucky.
    The petitioning group of workers is subject to an ongoing 
investigation for which a determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-
39,419). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 13th day of December, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
