Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Vascular 
Cell and Molecular Biology Study Section, June 19, 2006, 8 a.m. to June 
20, 2006, 5 p.m., Hilton Silver Spring, 8727 Colesville Road, Silver 
Spring, MD 20910 which was published in the Federal Register on May 11, 
2006, 71 FR 27505-27507.

Washington, DC 20007. The meeting dates and time remain the same. The
