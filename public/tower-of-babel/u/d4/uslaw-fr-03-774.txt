The invention available for licensing is: [Docket No.: 99-027US]
    Title: Parallel X-ray Nanotomography.
    Abstract: An apparatus for nanotomography uses an x-ray source 
comprising a laser generated plasma. X-rays generated in the x-ray 
source are collected and focused using a collector optic onto a sample. 
The collector optic is preferably comprised of Wolter optics combining 
reflection off an ellipsoid with a reflection off a hyperboloid. X-rays 
emitted from the sample are focused with an objective lens assembly. 
The objective lens assembly includes an array of fresnel zone plates. 
An image formation and acquisition apparatus form an image based on the 
received X-rays. The array of fresnel zone places is an important 
feature of the invention, as the array dramatically improves the 
intensity of the x-rays reaching the detector over a conventional 
objective lens. A laser-based x-ray source is also key to the 
invention, generating an x-ray beam of sufficient intensity to provide 
sufficient counting statistics for a tomographic reconstruction to be 
obtained.
