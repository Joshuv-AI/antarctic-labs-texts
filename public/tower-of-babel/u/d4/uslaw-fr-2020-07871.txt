NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act: Notice of Agency Meeting

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., Thursday, April 16, 2020.

PLACE: Due to the COVID-19 Pandemic, the meeting will be open to the 
public via live webcast only. Visit the agency's homepage 
(www.ncua.gov.) and access the provided webcast link.

STATUS: This meeting will be open to the public.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:
    1. NCUA Rules and Regulations, Central Liquidity Facility.
    2. NCUA Rules and Regulations, Regulatory Relief in Response to 
COVID-19 Pandemic.
    3. NCUA Rules and Regulations, Real Estate Appraisal Relief.
    4. NCUA Rules and Regulations, Real Estate Appraisal Threshold 
Levels.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Gerard Poliquin, Secretary of the 
Board, Telephone: 703-518-6304.

Gerard Poliquin,
Secretary of the Board.
