DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of Defense Education Benefits Board of Actuaries; 
Notice of Meeting

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A meeting of the board has been scheduled to execute the 
provisions of Chapter 101, Title 10, United States Code (10 U.S.C. 2006 
et. seq.). The Board shall review DoD actuarial methods and assumptions 
to be used in the valuation of the G.I. Bill. Persons desiring to 1) 
attend the DoD Education Benefits Board of Actuaries meeting or 2) make 
an oral presentation or submit a written statement for consideration at 
the meeting must notify Patricia Robertson at (703) 696-6336 by July 
28, 1995.

DATES: August 4, 1995, 10 am to 1 pm.

ADDRESSES: Room 1E801 #4.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:

Benjamin I. Gottlieb, Executive Secretary, DoD Office of the Actuary, 
4th floor, 1600 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22209-2593, (703) 696-
5869.

    Dated: May 18, 1995.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
