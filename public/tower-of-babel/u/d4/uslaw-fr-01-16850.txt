Compaq Computers, Houston, TX and Flextronics International, San 
Jose, CA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 7, 2001 in response to a petition filed on April 
23, 2001 on behalf of workers at Compaq Computers, Offline System 
Support, Houston, Texas; and, Flextronics International, Offline System 
Support, San Jose, California.
    The petitioning workers were not employees of either of the subject 
firms on the date of their separation. Therefore it is determined that 
the petition is invalid, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 25th day of June, 2001.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
