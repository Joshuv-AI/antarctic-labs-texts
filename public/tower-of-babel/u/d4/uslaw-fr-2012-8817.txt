DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of the Commission's staff may attend the following 
meeting. Their attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach 
efforts.

Order No. 1000 Interregional Coordination Meeting

April 12, 2012

    8:30 a.m.-3 p.m. (local time).
    The above-referenced meeting will be held at: Renaissance Oklahoma 
City, 10 North Broadway Avenue, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma 73102.
    The above-referenced meeting is open to the public.

    Further information may be found at www.misoenergy.org or 
www.spp.org.
