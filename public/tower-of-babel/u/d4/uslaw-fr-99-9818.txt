DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Armed Forces Epidemiological Board (AFEB)

AGENCY: Office of the Surgeon General, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of Pub. L. 92-463, The 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, this announces the forthcoming AFEB 
meeting. This Board will meet from 0730-1630 on Monday, 24 May 1999. 
The purpose of the meeting is to address classified issues pertaining 
to the current DoD 1999 Biological Warfare Threat List. The meeting 
location will be at the Institute for Defense Analysis, Alexandria, 
Virginia. The meeting will be closed to the public in accordance with 
section 552b(c) of Title 5, U.S.C., specifically subparagraph (1) 
thereof and Title 5, U.S.C., appendix 1, subsection 10(d).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: COL Benedict Diniega, AFEB Executive 
Secretary, Armed Forces Epidemiological Board, Skyline Six, 5109 
Leesburg Pike, Room 682, Falls Church, Virginia 22041-3258, (703) 681-
8012/4.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.
Gregory D. Showalter,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
