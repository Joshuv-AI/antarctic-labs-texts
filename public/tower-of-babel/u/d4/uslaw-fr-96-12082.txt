SUMMARY: In notice document 96-10340 beginning on page 18632 in the 
Federal Register issue of Friday, April 26, 1996, make the following 
correction:

Commission's 120-day decision schedule was previously listed as August 
20, 1996. This should be changed to read August 13, 1996.

Margaret P. Crenshaw,
