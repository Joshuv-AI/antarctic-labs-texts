SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Rocky Mountain States Regional Fairness Board Public Hearing

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Rocky Mountain States 
Regional Fairness Board Strategy Meeting, to be held on August 4, 1999, 
starting at 12:30 pm at 123 West E Street Casper, WY 82601 to receive 
comments and testimony from small businesses and representatives of 
trade associations concerning regulatory enforcement or compliance 
taken by federal agencies. Transcripts of these proceedings will be 
posted on the Internet. These transcripts are subject only to limited 
review by the National Ombudsman.
    For further information contact Gary P. Peele, telephone (312) 353-
0880.
Andrew A. Rivera,
Deputy Director of External Affairs.
