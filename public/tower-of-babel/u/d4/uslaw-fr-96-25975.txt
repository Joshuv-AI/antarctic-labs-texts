DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Cancer Institute; Notice of Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the meeting of the 
Cancer Centers Working Group of NCI Board of Scientific Advisors 
scheduled for October 14, 1996, which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 26 (61 FR 50495).
    The meeting was canceled due to administrative complications.

    Dated: October 3, 1996.
Paula N. Hayes,
Acting Committee Management Officer, NIH.
