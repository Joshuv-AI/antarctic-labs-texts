DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Change in Meeting

    Upon the affirmative votes of Chairman Chatterjee and Commissioners 
Glick and McNamee, this Notice hereby cancels the Commission meeting 
scheduled for March 19, 2020. All orders listed on the Sunshine Act 
Notice that was published on March 12, 2020, will be processed by 
notational voting.

    Issued: March 13, 2020.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
