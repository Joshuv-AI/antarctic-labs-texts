FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Freight Forwarder License Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR Part 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573.

International Globtrade, Inc. d/b/a JAB Forwarding, 332 S. Michigan 
Ave., Suite 1522, Chicago, IL 60604, Officers: Spiro Jankovich, 
President Frederick W. Amft, Vice President
Perfect West Inc., 17813 S. Main St., Suite #116, Gardena, CA 90248, 
Officer: Sung Woo Won, President
Castine Forwarding, Inc., 1235 Chestnut Street, Athol, MA 01331, 
Officers: Donald R. Castine, President, Cheney M. Castine, Vice 
President.

Joseph C. Polking,
Secretary.
