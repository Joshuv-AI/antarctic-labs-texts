Cancellation of Proposed Withdrawals; Montana

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice cancels three withdrawal applications affecting 
2,051.95 acres of National Forest System lands for charcoal kilns and 
research natural areas. The segregative effect of the applications was 
previously terminated and the lands were opened to surface entry and 
mining, subject to other segregations of record. The lands have been 
and remain open to mineral leasing.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 17, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sandra Ward, Bureau of Land 
Management, Montana State Office, P.O. Box 36800, Billings, Montana 
59107-6800, 406-896-5052.

published in the Federal Register December 1, 1966 (31 FR 15098), April
