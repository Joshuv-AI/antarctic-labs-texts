DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, July 30, 2015, 11 a.m. to July 
31, 2015, 6 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 9609 Medical 
Center Drive, 2W032/034, Rockville, MD 20850 which was published in the 
Federal Register on June 9, 2015, 80 FR 32577.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the end date from July 31, 
2015 to July 30, 2015, this meeting conclude in one day. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: August 10, 2015.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
