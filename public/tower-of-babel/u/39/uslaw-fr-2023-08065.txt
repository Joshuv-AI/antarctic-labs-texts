DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-64-2022]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 75; Authorization of Production 
Activity; TSMC Arizona Corporation; (Semiconductor Wafers); Phoenix, 
Arizona

    On December 13, 2022, TSMC Arizona Corporation submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facility within Subzone 75O, in Phoenix, Arizona.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (87 FR 79856, December 28, 2022). On 
April 12, 2023, the applicant was notified of the FTZ

[[Page 23396]]

Board's decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at 
this time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including section 400.14.

    Dated: April 12, 2023.
Camille R. Evans,
Acting Executive Secretary.
