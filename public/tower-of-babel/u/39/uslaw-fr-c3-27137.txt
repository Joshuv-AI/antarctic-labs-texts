Correction

    In notice document 03-27137 beginning on page 61532 in the issue of 
Tuesday, October 28, 2003, make the following corrections:
    On page 61532, in the second column, in paragraph (iv), the 
sentence beginning eight lines from the bottom should read: ``Promptly 
following an issuance of any employment inducement grant in reliance on 
this exception, a company must disclose in a press release the material 
terms of the grant, including the recipient(s) of the grant and the 
number of shares involved.''
     On page 61532, in the second column, in the document file line, 
``C3'' should read ``03''.
