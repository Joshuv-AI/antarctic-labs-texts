DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Minority Business Development Agency


Business Development Center Applications: Las Vegas and Anaheim

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) program to operate the Las Vegas and 
Anaheim MBDCs. The solicitations were originally published in the 
Federal Register on Wednesday, June 12, 1996, Vol. 61, No. 114, Page 
29733 and Wednesday, August 14, 1996, Vol. 61, No. 158, Page 42232.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance: 11.800 Minority Business 
Development Center)

    Dated: May 14, 1997.
Frances B. Douglas,
Alternate Federal Register Liaison Officer, Minority Business 
Development Agency.
