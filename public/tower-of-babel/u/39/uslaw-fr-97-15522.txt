FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-248; RM-8950]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Dickson, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Redwood Broadcasting, Inc., 
allots Channel 278C3 to Dickson, OK, as the community's first local 
aural transmission service. See 61 FR 66250, December 17, 1996. Channel 
278C3 can be allotted to Dickson in compliance with the Commission's 
minimum distance separation requirements without the imposition of a 
site restriction, at coordinates 34-11-14 North Latitude and 96-59-03 
West Longitude. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective July 21, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications will open on July 21, 1997, and close on August 21, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
