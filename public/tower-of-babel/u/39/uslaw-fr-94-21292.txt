DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

 

General Counsel; Appointment of Members of the Legal Division to 
the Performance Review Board, Internal Revenue Service

    Under the authority granted to me as Chief Counsel of the Internal 
Revenue Service by the General Counsel of the Department of the 
Treasury by General Counsel Order No. 21 (Rev. 4), and pursuant to the 
Civil Service Act, I hereby appoint the following persons to the Legal 
Division Performance Review Board, Internal Revenue Service Panel:

1. Chairperson, David L. Jordan, Acting Chief Counsel;
2. Dennis I. Foreman, Deputy General Counsel;
3. Sarah Hall Ingram, Associate Chief Counsel (Employee Benefits and 
Exempt Organizations);
4. Martha Sullivan, Central Regional Counsel;
5. Mark S. Kaizen, Assistant Chief Counsel (General Legal Services);
6. William F. Hammack, Dallas District Counsel.
This publication is required by 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4).
David L. Jordan,
Acting Chief Counsel.
