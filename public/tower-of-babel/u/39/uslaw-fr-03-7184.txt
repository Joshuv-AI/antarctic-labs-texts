DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Reclamation


Glen Canyon Dam Adaptive Management Work Group (AMWG), Notice of 
Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Reclamation, Interior.

ACTION: Cancellation of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Reclamation is canceling the Adaptive Management 
Work Group Meeting scheduled for March 28, 2003, in Flagstaff, Arizona. 
At the AMWG meeting held on January 28, 2003, the Humpback Chub Ad Hoc 
Group (HBC AHG) was formed to consider actions to implement a 
comprehensive research and management program for the humpback chub in 
the Colorado River. As such, the HBC AHG requires additional time to 
complete various assignments in preparation for presentation to the 
AMWG.
