DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Notice of Partially Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB)
Date of Meeting: 24 & 25 January 1996
Time of Meeting: 0900-1100, 24 January 1996 (Closed)
    1200-1600, 24 January 1996 (Open)
    0930-1600, 25 January 1996 (Open)
Place: Pentagon--Washington, DC

Agenda: The Army Science Board's Ad Hoc Study on ``Army Digitization 
Information Systems Vulnerabilities and Security'' will meet for 
briefings and discussions relative to the study subject. The open 
portions of these meetings are open to the public. Any person may 
attend, appear before or file statements with the committee. The 
closed portion of these meetings will be closed to the public in 
accordance with Section 552b(c) of title 5, U.S.C., specifically 
subparagraph (4) thereof, and Title 5, U.S.C., Appendix 2, 
subsection 10(d).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michelle Diaz (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
