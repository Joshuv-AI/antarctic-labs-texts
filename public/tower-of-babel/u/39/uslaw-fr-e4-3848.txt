DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL05-4-000]


Duke Power, a Division of Duke Power Corporation; Notice of 
Initiation of Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

December 21, 2004.
    On December 15, 2004, the Commission issued an order in the above-
docketed proceeding. The Commission's order institutes a proceeding in 
Docket No. EL05-4-000 under section 206 of the Federal Power Act with 
respect to the justness and reasonableness of Duke Power's market-based 
rates.
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL05-4-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the Federal Power Act will be 60 days 
following

[[Page 78005]]

publication of this notice in the Federal Register.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
