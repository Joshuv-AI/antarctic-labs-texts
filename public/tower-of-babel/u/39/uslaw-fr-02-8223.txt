COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is scheduled for 18 
April 2002 at 10:00 am in the Commission's office at the National 
Building Museum, Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street, NW, 
Washington, DC 2001-2728. Items of discussion affecting the appearance 
of Washington, DC, may include buildings, parks and memorials.
    Draft agendas are available to the public one week prior to the 
meeting. Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written 
or oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, 
Secretary, Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-
504-2200. Individuals requiring sign language interpretation for the 
hearing impaired should contact the Secretary at least 10 days beforet 
the meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, DC., 1 April 2002.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
