SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board; Public 
Meeting

    The National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board will 
hold a public meeting on Monday, March 4, 1996, from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 
p.m. at the U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 Third Street, S.W., 
4th Floor, Washington, DC 20416.
    The purpose of the meeting is to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by Advisory Board members, staff of the SBA, or others 
present.
    For further information, write or call Mary Ann Holl, SBA, 4th 
Floor, 409 3rd Street, S.W., Washington, DC 20416, (202) 205-7302.
    Dated: February 1, 1996

Bill Combs,
Associate Administrator for Office of Communication and Public Liaison
