Spherion Leased Workers Working On-Site at JDS Uniphase 
Corporation, Rochester, MN; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 13, 2006 in response to a petition 
filed by a state workforce agent on behalf of leased Spherion workers 
working on-site at JDS Uniphase Corporation; Rochester, Minnesota.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, TA-W-55,262 (amended March 21, 2006), which expires on 
August 16, 2006. Consequently, further investigation in this case would 
serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 21st day of March, 2006.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
