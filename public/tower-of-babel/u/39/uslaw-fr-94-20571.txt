DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
 

Merger of Approved Trustee

    Notice is hereby given that Irving Trust Company, New York, New 
York, merged with and into The Bank of New York, 101 Barclay Street, 
New York, New York 10286, under the name of The Bank of New York as the 
surviving corporation in the merger.

    Dated: August 15, 1994.

    By Order of the Maritime Administrator.

James E. Saari,
Acting Secretary.
