DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Intent To Grant an Exclusive or Partially Exclusive License to 
Allen Telecom Group, Inc., Decibel Products Division

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of intent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In compliance with 37 CFR part 404 et seq., the Department of 
the Army hereby gives notice of its intent to grant to ALLEN TELECOM 
GROUP, INC., DECIBEL PRODUCTS DIVISION, a corporation having its 
principle place of business at 8635 Stemmons Freeway, Dallas, Texas 
75247-3701, an exclusive or partially exclusive licenses under U.S. 
Patents 5,486,491, issued 23 Jan 1996, entitled ``Ceramic Ferroelectric 
Composite Material--BSTO-ZRO2; 5,312,790, issued 17 May 1994, 
entitled ``Ceramic Ferroelectric Material''; and 5,427,988, issued 27 
June 1995, entitled ``Ceramic Ferroelectric Composite Material--BSTO-
MGO''. Anyone wishing to object to the granting of these licenses has 
60 days from the date of this notice to file written objections along 
with supporting evidence, if any.

Ground, Maryland 21005-5425, Telephone (410) 278-5028.
