FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2417]


Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

June 15, 2000.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of 
this document is available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 
12th Street, S.W., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the 
Commission's copy contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to 
these petitions must be filed by July 7, 2000. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of 
the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition 
must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has 
expired.
    Subject: Amendment of the Commission's Rules Regarding Multiple 
Address Systems (WT Docket No. 97-81).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 3.

Requirements for Incumbent Local Exchange Carriers (CC Docket No. 98-
137).
    Number of Petitions File: 1.
    Subject: Local Compensation and Broadband Reporting (CC Docket No. 
99-301).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 1.
