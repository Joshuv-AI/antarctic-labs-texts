FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 05-36; MB Docket No. 03-181, RM-10758, and RM-11123]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Blanchard, Elmore City, Weatherford 
and Wynnewood, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Wright Broadcasting 
Systems, Inc., licensee of FM Station KWEY, Channel 247C1, Weatherford, 
Oklahoma, deletes Channel 247C1 at Weatherford, Oklahoma, from the FM 
Table of Allotments, allots Channel 247A at Blanchard, Oklahoma, as the 
community's first local FM service, and modifies the license of FM 
Station KWEY to specify operation on Channel 247A at Blanchard. Channel 
247A can be allotted to Blanchard, Oklahoma, in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements with a site 
restriction of 2.1 km (1.3 miles) southwest of Blanchard. The 
coordinates for Channel 247A at Blanchard, Oklahoma, are 35-07-21 North 
Latitude and 97-40-18 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective February 25, 2005.
