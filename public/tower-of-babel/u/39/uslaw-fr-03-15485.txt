Intel Corporation, Systems Manufacturing Technology Development, 
Hillsboro, OR, and Intel Corporation, Systems Manufacturing Technology 
Development, Dupont, WA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 14, 2003 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Intel 
Corporation, Systems Manufacturing Technology Development, Hillsboro, 
Oregon (TA-W-51,749) and Intel Corporation, Systems Manufacturing 
Technology Development, DuPont, Washington (TA-W-51,749A).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 6th day of June, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
