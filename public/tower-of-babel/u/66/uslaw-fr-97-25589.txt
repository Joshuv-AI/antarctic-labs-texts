FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-8; RM-8957]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Amelia, LA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial of petition for rulemaking.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission denies the petition for rule making filed by 
Amelia Broadcasting of Louisiana, proposing the allotment of Channel 
249C3 to Amelia, Louisiana. See 62 FR 3851, January 27, 1997. The 
proposal is denied because of the lack of a suitable transmitter site. 
With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
