DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Sleep 
Disorders Research Advisory Board, April 6, 2023, 12 p.m. to 5 p.m., 
and April 7, 2023, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Two 
Rockledge Centre, Rockledge I, 6705 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 
which was published in the Federal Register on March 16, 2023, 88 FRN 
16275.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting start time for 
April 6, 2023 from 12:00 p.m. to 1:00 p.m. Also, the in person location 
is amended for both days to Two Rockledge Centre, 6701 Rockledge Drive, 
Suite 260, Bethesda, MD 20817.

    Dated: March 20, 2023.
David W. Freeman,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
