DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Board of Scientific Counselors Basic Sciences 
Subcommittee which was published in the Federal Register on July 3, 
1996 (61 FR 34856).
    The Board of Scientific Counselors Basic Sciences Subcommittee was 
scheduled to meet on July 22-23, but will now meet on July 22 only. The 
open sessions will be from 9 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. and 12 noon to 1 p.m. 
The meeting will be closed to the public from 9:30 a.m. to 12 noon and 
from 1 p.m. to 6 p.m.

    Dated: July 17, 1996.
Margery G. Grubb,
Senior Committee Management Specialist, NIH.
