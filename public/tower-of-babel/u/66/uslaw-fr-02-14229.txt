DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Notice of Prospective Grant of Two Partially Exclusive Patent 
Licenses

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the provisions of 35 U.S.C. 209(c)(1) and 
37 CFR Part 404.7(a)(1)(i), Army Research Laboratory (ARL) hereby gives 
notice that it is contemplating the grant of two partially exclusive 
licenses in the United States to practice the invention embodied in 
U.S. Patent 5,876,960, ``Bacterial Spore Detection and Quantification 
Methods, to the following small businesses: Nomadics, Inc., 1024S 
Innovation Way, Stillwater, OK 74074 and Ocean Optics, Inc., 380 Main 
Street, Dunedin, FL 34698.
    This technology relates to methods for the detection and 
quantification of bacterial spores in a sample medium.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Norma Cammarata, Technology 
Transfer Office, U.S. Army Research Laboratory, ATTN: AMSRL-DP-T, 
Powder Mill Road, Adelphi, MD 20783-1197, Phone: (301) 394-2952 or e-
mail: [email protected]

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The prospective partially exclusive licenses 
will be royalty bearing and will comply with the terms and conditions 
of 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR 404.7. The prospective partially exclusive 
licenses may be granted, unless ARL recieves written evidence and 
argument to establish that the grant of the lincese would not be 
consistent with the requirements of 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR 404.7 by 
21 June 2002.
