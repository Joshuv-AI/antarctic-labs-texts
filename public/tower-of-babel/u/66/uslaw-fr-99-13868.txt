DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. EG99-127-000]


Foote Creek III, LLC; Amended Application for Commission 
Determination of Exempt Wholesale Generator Status

May 26, 1999.
    Take notice that on May 20, 1999, Foote Creek III, LLC tendered for 
filing with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission an amendment to 
its original request for Commission Determination of Exempt Wholesale 
Generator Status. In its amendment, Foote Creek III, LLC states, among 
other things, that it will be both the owner and the operator of the 
Foote Creek III, LLC facility described in its application.
    Any person desiring to be heard concerning the amended application 
for exempt wholesale generator status should file a motion to intervene 
or comments with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Rules 211 and 
214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 
and 385.214). The Commission will limit its consideration of comments 
to those that concern the adequacy or accuracy of the amended 
application. All such motions and comments should be filed on or before 
June 16, 1999, and must be served on the applicant. Any person wishing 
to become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this 
filing are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection or on the Internet at http://www.ferc.fed.us./online/
rims.htm (please call (202) 208-2222 for assistance).
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
