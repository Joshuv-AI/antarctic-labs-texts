COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Nevada Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Nevada Advisory Committee. 
The meeting scheduled for Monday, December 12, 2022, at 3 p.m. (PT) is 
cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of Friday, October 14, 
2022, in FR Doc. 2022-22374, in the second column of page 62360 and in 
the first column of page 62361.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ana Victoria Fortes, (202) 681-0857, 
[email protected].
