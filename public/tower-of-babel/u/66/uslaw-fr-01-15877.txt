Chesapeake Bay Program FY2002 Request; Correction of Website

    On May 30, 2001, at (66 FR 29308) of the Federal Register, the U.S. 
Environmental Protection Agency, Chesapeake Bay Program issued a 
request for proposals (RFP) that would further the goals of the 
Chesapeake 2000 Agreement. The RFP was available May 30, 2001. The 
website that was listed was incorrect. The correst website is: http://www.epa.gov/r3chespk/ You may receive a paper copy by calling Kim 
Scalia at 215-814-5421 or by a e-mail at [email protected] or by 
calling Lori Mackey at 410-267-5715 or by e-mail at 
[email protected]. All proposals must be postmarked by Monday, July 
16, 2001.
