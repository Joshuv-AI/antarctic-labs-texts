FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Request for Additional Information

    The Commission gives notice that it has requested that the parties 
to the below listed agreements provide additional information pursuant 
to section 6(d) of the Shipping Act of 1984, 46 U.S.C. app. 1701 et 
seq. The Commission has determined that further information is 
necessary to evaluate the agreements. This action prevents the 
agreements from becoming effective as originally scheduled.
    Agreement No.: 011804.
    Title: Eastern Car Liner/Fesco Ocean Management Ltd. Space Charter 
Agreement.
    Parties: Eastern Car Liner, Ltd., Fesco Ocean Management Limited.
    Agreement No.: 011807.
    Title: SNL/HASCO Cross Space Charter and Sailing Agreement.
    Parties: Sinotrans Container Line Co., Ltd., Shanghai Hai Hua 
Shipping Co., Ltd.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.

    Dated: June 28, 2002.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
