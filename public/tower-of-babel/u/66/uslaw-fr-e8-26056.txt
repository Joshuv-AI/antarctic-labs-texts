Employment and Training Administration

JDS Uniphase; Louisville, CO; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 14, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of JDS 
Uniphase, Louisville, Colorado.
    The Department has determined that this petition is a duplicate of 
petition number TA-W-64,132, instituted on September 30, 2008. The 
investigation of that petition is ongoing and determination has not yet 
been issued. Therefore, further investigation in the case would serve 
no purpose, and this investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 23rd day of October 2008.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
