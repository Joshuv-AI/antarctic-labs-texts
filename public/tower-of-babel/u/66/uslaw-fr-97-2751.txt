DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Meeting of the Military Health Care Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Department of Defense, Military Health Care Advisory Committee.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On January 28, 1997 (62 FR 4036), the Department of Defense 
published a notice announcing a meeting of the Military Health Care 
Advisory Committee. This meeting has been POSTPONED until mid-March, 
due to scheduling conflicts which have not permitted full attendance by 
Committee members. All other information remains unchanged.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Gary A. Christopherson, Senior 
Advisor, or Commander Sidney Rodgers, Special Assistant to PDASD, 
Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs), 1200 
Defense Pentagon, Room 3E346, Washington, DC 20301-1200; telephone 
(703) 697-2111.

    Dated: January 29, 1997.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
