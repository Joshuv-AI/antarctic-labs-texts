DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Parole Commission

Sunshine Act Meeting


PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

Pursuant To The Government In the Sunshine Act (Public Law 94-409 
[5 U.S.C. Section 552b]

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Department of Justice, United States Parole 
Commission.

TIME AND DATE: 2:00 p.m., Monday, August 17, 1998.

PLACE: 5550 Friendship Boulevard, Suite 400, Chevy Chase, Maryland 
20815.

STATUS: Open.

MATTER TO BE CONSIDERED: The meeting is being held to discuss the 
budget proposal for the fiscal year 2000.
    Earlier notice of this meeting could not be made because the 
Commission was only advised on this date of the deadline set by the 
Department of Justice for the draft budget proposal, and a later 
meeting would conflict with Commissioners' schedules.

AGENCY CONTACT: Pamela Posch, Office of the General Counsel, United 
States Parole Commission, (301) 492-5959.

    Dated: August 11, 1998.
Michael A. Stover,
General Counsel, U.S. Parole Commission.
