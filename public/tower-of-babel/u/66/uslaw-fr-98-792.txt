SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the final rule 
published in the Federal Register (62 FR 66248) on December 17, 1997. 
The final rule codified the provisions of Special Federal Aviation 
Regulation (SFAR) No. 50-2, Special Flight Rules in the Vicinity of 
Grand Canyon National Park (GCNP); modified the dimensions of the GCNP 
Special Flight Rules Area (SFRA); established new and modified existing 
flight-free zones; established new and modified existing flight 
corridors; established reporting requirements for commercial 
sightseeing companies operating in the SFRA; prohibited commercial 
sightseeing operations in certain areas during certain time periods; 
and limited the number of aircraft that can be used for commercial 
sightseeing operations in the SFRA.

EFFECTIVE DATES: The effective date of January 31, 1998, for 14 CFR 
Sections 93.301, 93.305, and 93.307, is delayed until 0901 UTC January 
31, 1999. Section 9 of SFAR No. 50-2 is amended effective January 16, 
1998.
