DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration


Pulmonary-Allergy Drugs Advisory Committee; Cancellation

AGENCY:  Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION:  Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is canceling the 
meeting of the Pulmonary-Allergy Drugs Advisory Committee scheduled for 
December 20, 2002. This meeting was announced in the Federal Register 
of November 13, 2002 (67 FR 68878).
