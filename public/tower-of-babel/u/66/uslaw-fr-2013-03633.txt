DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Revised Pricing Grid for Gold and Platinum Products

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing a revised pricing grid 
for 2013 gold and platinum products. Please see the grid following 
signature.

DATES: This grid will be effective Wednesday, February 27, 2013, at 12 
noon (ET).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Marc Landry, Acting Associate Director 
for Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.

    Authority:  31 U.S.C. 5111, 5112 & 9701; Pub. L. 111-262, sec. 
6.

    Dated: February 11, 2013.
Richard A. Peterson,
Acting Director, United States Mint.
