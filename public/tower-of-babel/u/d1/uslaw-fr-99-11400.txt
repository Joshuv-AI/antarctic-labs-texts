FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Freight Forwarder License Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission

[[Page 24395]]

applications for licenses as ocean freight forwarders pursuant to 
section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR 
510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573. 
S.A.T. Sea & Air Transport Inc., 1200 South 192nd Street, Suite 200, 
Seattle, WA 98148, Officers: Peter J. Beckett, President, George M. 
Goodwin, Secretary/Treasurer.

    Dated: May 3, 1999.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
