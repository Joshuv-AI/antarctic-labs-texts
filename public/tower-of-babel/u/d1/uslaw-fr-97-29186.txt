DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Southwest Oregon Provincial Interagency Executive Committee 
(PIEC), Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Southwest Oregon PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on 
November 20 at the Douglas County Library, at 1409 NE Diamond Lake 
Blvd. in Roseburg, Oregon. The meeting will begin at 9:00 a.m. and 
continue until 5:00 p.m. Agenda items to be covered include: (1) Survey 
and Manage requirements of the Northwest Forest Plan; (2) Province 
monitoring priorities; (3) Forest health issues; (4) Report from 
Aquatic Conservation Subcommittee; (5) Report from local BLM and Forest 
Service on local issues; and (6) Public comment. All Province Advisory 
Committee meetings are open to the public.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Direct questions regarding this 
meeting to Chuck Anderson, Province Advisory Committee staff, USDA, 
Forest Service, Rogue River National Forest, 333 W. 8th Street, 
Medford, Oregon 97501, phone 541-858-2322.
