FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Schedule Change; FCC To Hold Open 
Commission Meeting, Wednesday, December 11, 2002

December 10, 2002.
    Please note that the time for the Federal Communications Commission 
Open Meeting is rescheduled from 9:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Wednesday, December 11, 2002, which is 
scheduled to commence at 1:30 p.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, 
SW., Washington, DC.
