FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Early Closing on December 24, 1998

Released: December 21, 1998.
    In accordance with the guidance issued by the U.S. Office of 
Personnel Management to implement the half-day closing of government 
department and agencies on Thursday, December 24th, 1998, the Federal 
Communications Commission will close its offices at 1:30 p.m. on 
December 24, 1998. All filings, paper and electronic, due on December 
24, 1998, will be accepted as timely on the next official work day.

Federal Communications Commission.
William F. Caton,
Deputy Secretary.
