Pacific Gas and Electric Company; Notice Granting Late 
Intervention

July 8, 2004.
    On December 16, 2003, Pacific Gas and Electric Company filed an 
application for a new license for the continued operation and 
maintenance of the 143-megawatt Poe Project No. 2107, located on the 
North Fork Feather River in Butte County, near Pulga, California. The 
Commission issued public notice of the application on March 24, 2004, 
setting May 24, 2004, as the deadline for filing motions to intervene.
    On June 14, 2004, Butte County, California, filed a late motion to 
intervene. Granting the late motion to intervene will not unduly delay 
or disrupt the proceeding or prejudice other parties to it. Therefore, 
pursuant to Rule 214,\1\ the late motion to intervene filed by Butte 
County, California, is granted, subject to the Commission's rules and 
regulations.
