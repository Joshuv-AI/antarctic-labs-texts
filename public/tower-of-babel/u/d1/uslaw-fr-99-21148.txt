DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-36,595]


AMP Incorporated, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 26, 1999 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers and former workers at AMP Incorporated, 
located in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania (TA-W-35,598).
    The Department of Labor has determined that the petition is 
invalid. Under the Trade Act of 1974, a petition may be filed by a 
group of three or more workers in an appropriate subdivision of a firm, 
by a company official, or by their union or other duly authorized 
representative. the petitioners do not share a common work location and 
the petitioners are not authorized to file on behalf of all workers of 
the company. Consequently, further investigation in this matter would 
serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 27th day of July 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
