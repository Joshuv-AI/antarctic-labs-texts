AGENCY: National Endowment for the Humanities; National Foundation on

ACTION: Notice of republication of systems of records and new routine

SUMMARY: The National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) published a 
notice of systems of records in the Federal Register of April 26, 2017. 
The references in the document to specific sections of the Code of 
Federal Regulations are out of date.

Counsel, Office of the General Counsel, National Endowment for the 
Humanities, 400 7th Street SW, Room 4060, Washington, DC 20506; (202) 
606-8322; [email protected].
