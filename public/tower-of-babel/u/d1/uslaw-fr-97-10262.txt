FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY

[FEMA-1166-DR]


Federated States of Micronesia; Amendment to Notice of a Major 
Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
Federated States of Micronesia, (FEMA-1166-DR), dated March 11, 1997, 
and related determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: April 8, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Magda Ruiz, Response and Recovery 
Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, DC 20472, 
(202) 646-3260.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the 
Federated States of Micronesia, is hereby amended to include Hazard 
Mitigation in those areas determined to have been adversely affected by 
the catastrophe declared a major disaster by the President in his 
declaration of March 11, 1997:

    Yap State for Hazard Mitigation.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance No. 83.516, Disaster 
Assistance)
Dennis H. Kwiatkowski,
Deputy Associate Director, Response and Recovery Directorate.
