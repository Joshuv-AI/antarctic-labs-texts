DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


VA Fleet Alternative Fuel Vehicle (AFV) Program Report

AGENCY: Department of Veterans Affairs.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Energy Policy Act of 1992 (EPAct) (42 
U.S.C. 13211-13219) as amended by the Energy Conservation 
Reauthorization Act of 1998 (Pub. L. 105-388), the Department of 
Veterans Affairs' annual alternative fuel reports are available on the 
following Department of Veterans Affairs Web site: http://www.va.gov/afv.
