Jersey Central Power & Light Company, PSEG Fossil, LLC, Yards 
Creek Energy, LLC; Notice of Application for Partial Transfer of 
License and Soliciting Comments, Motions to Intervene, and Protests

    On May 6, 2020, Jersey Central Power & Light Company (JCP&L or 
transferor) and PSEG Fossil, LLC (PSEG) current co-licensees, and Yards 
Creek Energy, LLC (YCE or transferee) filed a joint application for a 
partial transfer of the license for the Yards Creek Pumped Storage 
Hydroelectric Project No. 2309. The project is located on Yards Creek 
in Warren County, New Jersey.
    The applicants seek Commission approval for a partial transfer of 
the license for the project to remove JCP&L as a co-licensee and to add 
YCE as co-licensee.
