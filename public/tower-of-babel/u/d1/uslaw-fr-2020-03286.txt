DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, Cellular, Molecular and 
Integrative Reproduction Study Section, February 20, 2020, 10:00 a.m. 
to February 20, 2020, 12:00 p.m., Embassy Suites Alexandria Old Town, 
1900 Diagonal Road, Alexandria, VA 22314 which was published in the 
Federal Register on January 27, 2020, 85 FR 4672.
    Meeting is being cancelled.

    Dated: February 13, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
