DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
 

RTCA, Inc., Special Committee 182; Minimum Operational 
Performance Standard for an Avionics Computer Resource; Meeting

    Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
(Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., appendix I), notice is hereby given for 
Special Committee 182 meeting to be held March 8-9, starting at 9 a.m. 
The meeting will be held at the RTC Conference Room, 1140 Connecticut 
Avenue, NW., Suite 1020, Washington, DC 20036.
    The agenda for this meeting is as follows: (1) Chairman's 
introductory remarks; (2) Review and approval of meeting agenda; (3) 
Review committee terms of reference, RTCA Paper No. 12-94/TMC-117 
(enclosed); (4) Identify goals, develop work program and examine 
milestones; (5) Assign tasks; (6) Other business; (7) Date and place of 
next meeting.
    Attendance is open to the interested public but limited to space 
available. With the approval of the Chairman, members of the public may 
present oral statements at the meeting. Persons wishing to present 
statements or obtain information should contact the RTCA Secretariat, 
1140 Connecticut Avenue, NW., suite 1020, Washington, DC 20036; (202) 
833-9339. Any member of the public may present a written statement to 
the committee at any time.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on February 2, 1994.
Joyce J. Gillen,
Designated Officer.
