DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Health Resources and Services Administration


Advisory Commission on Childhood Vaccines Meeting Cancellation

AGENCY: Health Resources and Services Administration; Department of 
Health and Human Services.

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is to notify the public that the March 3, 2022, meeting 
of the Advisory Commission on Childhood Vaccines (ACCV) is canceled and 
will be rescheduled. This meeting was announced in the Federal 
Register, Vol. 87, No. 20 on Monday, January 31, 2022 (FR Doc. 2022-
01848 Filed 1-28-22). Future meetings will occur in June, September, 
and December of calendar year 2022 and were announced through the same 
Federal Register notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: CDR Reed Grimes, Designated Federal 
Official, ACCV, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, Maryland 20857, 
telephone: (301) 443-6634 or email: [email protected].
