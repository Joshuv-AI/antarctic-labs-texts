AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and

ACTION: Notice of a public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council will hold its 165th 
meeting in April to discuss the items contained in the agenda in the 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

DATES: The meetings will be held on April 23-24, 2019, from 9 a.m. to 5 
p.m.

ADDRESSES: The meetings will be held at the Courtyard Marriott Isla 
Verde Beach Resort, 7012 Boca de Cangrejos Avenue, Isla Verde, 
Carolina, Puerto Rico 00907.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Caribbean Fishery Management Council, 
270 Mu[ntilde]oz Rivera Avenue, Suite 401, San Juan, Puerto Rico 00918-
1903; telephone: (787) 766-5926.
