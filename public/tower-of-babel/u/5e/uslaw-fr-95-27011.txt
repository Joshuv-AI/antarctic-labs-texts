DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. GT96-6-000]


Kern River Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Refund Report

October 26, 1995.
    Take notice that on October 17, 1995, Kern River Gas Transmission 
Company (Kern River) tendered for filing a refund report pursuant to 
the Commission's May 3, 1995, ``Order Granting Clarification'' issued 
in Docket No. RP95-124-001.
    Kern River states that it has refunded the Gas Research Institute 
(GRI) demand surcharge refund received from GRI based on the non-
discounted GRI dollars paid by each firm shipper during the 1994 
calendar year as a percentage of the total non-discounted GRI demand 
dollars paid by all firm shippers. Kern River further states that it 
made these refunds in the form of credits to invoices issued on October 
6, 1995. The total amount credited was $646,159.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before November 2, 1995. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion 
to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
