DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee will meet on May 6-
7, 1999, at the Six Rivers National Forest Conference Room, 1330 
Bayshore Way, Eureka, California. On Thursday, May 6, the PAC will meet 
from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. On Friday, May 7, the meeting will start at 
8:00 a.m. and adjourn at 12:00 p.m. Agenda items for the meeting 
include: (1) Water Rights Adjudication Process in the Upper Klamath 
Basin; (2) Five County Coho Salmon Planning Effort; (3) Economic Impact 
of the Northwest Forest Plan on Two Small Communities in Oregon; (4) 
Subcommittee Reports; and (5) Public Comment Periods. All PAC meetings 
are open to the public. Interested citizens are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Connie Hendryx, USDA, Klamath National 
Forest, 1312 Fairlane Road, Yreka, California 96097; telephone 530-841-
4468 (voice), TDD 530-841-4573.

    Dated: April 19, 1999.
