DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Airport Surface Movement Conference

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of conference.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is issuing this notice to advise the public of a 
conference on Airport Surface Movement. FAA will solicit the public for 
input on technologies and procedural changes needed to implement and 
improve the airport safety and surface movement effort.

DATES: The conference will be held on Wednesday, March 8, 1995, through 
March 10, 1995.

[[Page 10141]] ADDRESSES: The conference will be held at the Arlington 
Renaissance Hotel, 950 North Stafford Street, Arlington, Virginia.

FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION OR TO RECEIVE A REGISTRATION FORM: Call 
(301) 949-7477, or Fax inquiries to (301) 949-5154, to the attention of 
Deana Lou.

    Issued in Washington, D.C. on February 1, 1995.
Michael J. Harrison,
Airport Surface Manager.
