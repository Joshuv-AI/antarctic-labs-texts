Robotex, Incorporated, Lumberton, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 25, 1999, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Robotex, Incorporated, 
Lumberton, NC.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 17th day of December 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
