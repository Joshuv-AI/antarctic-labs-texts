SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to the summary of petitions 
received published in the Federal Register on November 12, 2003 (68 FR 
64186). That notice contained a summary of certain petitions seeking 
relief from specified requirements of 14 CFR.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Tim Adams (202) 267-8033, Sandy 
Buchanan-Sumter (202) 267-7271, Office of Rulemaking (ARM-1), Federal 
Aviation Administration, 800 Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, DC 
20591.

    In notice of petitions for exemption FR Doc. 03-28256, published on

``Docket No.: FAA-2003-16195'' to read ``Docket No.: FAA-2003-16138''.
