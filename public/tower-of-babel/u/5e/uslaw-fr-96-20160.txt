CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission.

TIME AND DATE: 11:00 a.m., Tuesday, August 6, 1996.

LOCATION: Room 714, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Closed to the Public.

Matter to be Considered:

Children's Sleepwear Enforcement

    The Commission will consider issues related to the enforcement 
of the children's sleepwear standard.

    The Commission decided on August 1, 1996, that agency business 
required scheduling this meeting without the usual seven days advance 
public notice.
    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, MD 20207 (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: August 2, 1996.
Sadye E. Dunn,
Secretary.
