DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


HQ USAF Scientific Advisory Board

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, notice is hereby given of the 
forthcoming meeting of the 2004 Science and Technology Quality Review 
Panel. The purpose of the meeting is to allow the Air Force Scientific 
Advisory Board to assess the quality and long-term relevance of Air 
Force Research Laboratory Special Program research. Because classified 
and contractor-proprietary information will be discussed, this meeting 
will be closed to the public.

DATES: 15-18 November 2004.

ADDRESSES: Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, OH.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Major Kyle Gresham, Air Force 
Scientific Advisory Board Secretariat, 1180 Air Force Pentagon, Rm. 
5D982, Washington, DC 20330-1180, (703) 697-4808.

Pamela D. Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
