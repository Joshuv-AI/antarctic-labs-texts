Radio Broadcasting Services; Mt. Pleasant, IA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies the petition for reconsideration filed by 
Susan I. Coloff of our Report and Order, 55 FR 47764 (Nov. 15, 1990) in 
this proceeding. The Report and Order substituted FM channel 288C3 for 
Channel 288A at Mount Pleasant and modified the license for Station 
KILJ to specify operation on the higher powered channel. The Commission 
determined that the public interest would be served by the substitution 
of Channel 288C3 for Channel A since it would provide the community 
with a wide coverage area FM service. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated.

Effective Date: November 9, 1995.
