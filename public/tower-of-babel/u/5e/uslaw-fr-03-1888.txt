OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


January 23, 2003, Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its public hearing was published in 
the Federal Register (volume 68, number 4, page 816) on January 7, 
2003. No requests were received to provide testimony or submit written 
statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public hearing in 
conjunction with OPIC's January 30, 2003, Board of Directors meeting 
scheduled for 1 p.m. on January 23, 2003, has been cancelled.
    Contact Person for Information: Information on the hearing 
cancellation may be obtained from Connie M. Downs at (202) 336-8438, 
via facsimile at (202) 218-0136, or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: January 22, 2003.
