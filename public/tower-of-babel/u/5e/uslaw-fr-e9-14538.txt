SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

Federal Register Citation of Previous Announcement: [74 FR 28293, June 
15, 2009]

Status: Closed Meeting.

Place: 100 F Street, NE., Washington, DC.

Date and Time of Previously Announced Meeting: Friday, June 19, 2009 at 
11 a.m.

Change in the Meeting: Additional Item.
    The following item has been added to the Friday, June 19, 2009 
Closed Meeting agenda: Formal Order of Investigation.
    The General Counsel of the Commission, or his designee, has 
certified that, in his opinion, one or more of the exemptions set forth 
in 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(c)(3), (5), (7) and (10) and 17 CFR 200.402(a)(3), 
(5), (7) and (10) permit consideration of the scheduled matter at the 
Closed Meeting.
    Commissioner Walter, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above change and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact the Office of the Secretary at (202) 551-5400.

    Dated: June 16, 2009.
Elizabeth M. Murphy,
Secretary.
