FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


FCC To Hold Open Commission Meeting Tuesday, June 9, 1998

June 2, 1998.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Tuesday, June 9, 1998, which is scheduled 
to commence at 3:00 p.m. in Room 856, at 1919 M Street, NW., 
Washington, D.C.
