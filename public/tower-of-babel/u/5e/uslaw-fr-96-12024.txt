DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
CA-058-1020-00


Notice of Resource Advisory Council Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the next meeting of the of the 
Ukiah Resource Advisory Council will be held on Thursday, May 30, and 
Friday, May 31, 1996 in Ukiah, California.

DATES: The meeting is scheduled for Thursday, May 30 and Friday, May 
31, 1996.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting on Thursday will begin at 10:00 
a.m. at the Clear Lake Resource Area Office conference room, 2550 North 
State Street, Ukiah, CA. It will begin with a four hour training 
session on the basics of rangeland health. The remainder of Thursday 
will be spent in a tour of the Scotts Creek Allotment in Lake County. 
Friday, May 31 will be spent on finalizing standards and guidelines for 
rangeland health for public lands administered by the Bureau of Land 
Management in northwestern California.
    The meeting is open to the public with a public comment period 
scheduled for 1:00-2:00 p.m., Friday, May 31. Depending on the number 
of persons wishing to speak, a time limit may be imposed. Summary 
minutes of the meeting will be maintained at the Arcata, Clear Lake and 
Redding Resource Area Offices.

FOR MORE INFORMATION CONTACT: Renee Snyder, Bureau of Land Management, 
Clear Lake Resource Area, 2550 N. State St., Ukiah, CA 95482, 707-468-
4000.
Valerie Parker,
Acting Clear Lake Resource Area Manager.
