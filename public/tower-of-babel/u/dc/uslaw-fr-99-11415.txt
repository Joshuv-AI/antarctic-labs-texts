Cancellations of Customs Brokers' Licenses

AGENCY: Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Brokers' licenses cancellations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1641(f)) and Sec. 111.51(a) of the 
Customs Regulations (19 CFR 111.51(a)), hereby cancel the following 
Customs brokers' licenses without prejudice.
