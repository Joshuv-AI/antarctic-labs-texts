47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-2734; MM Docket No. 01-178; RM-10195]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wadley, GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 66 FR 42622 
(August 14, 2001), this document allots Channel 227A to Wadley, 
Georgia, and provides Wadley with its first local aural transmission 
service. The coordinates for Channel 227A at Wadley are 32-52-00 North 
Latitude and 82-24-15 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective January 7, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
