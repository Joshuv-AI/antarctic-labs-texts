DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Availabilty of a Pop-up Target System for Non-Exclusive, 
Exclusive, or Partially Exclusive Licensing

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Adelphi, Maryland.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive or non-exclusive licenses relative to 
a novel pop-up target system as described in the U.S. Army Research 
Laboratory patent docket #ARL 98-16 and a subsequent patent application 
to the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. A licensing meeting is 
scheduled for Wednesday, 15 July 1998 at Aberdeen Proving Ground, MD. 
Visit http://www.fedlabs.org/flc/ma/pl for technical and registration 
information. A non-disclosure agreement must be signed prior to 
attending the licensing meeting. Licenses shall comply with 35 U.S.C. 
209 and 37 CFR 404.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Michael D. Rausa, U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Office of Research and 
Technology Applications, ATTN: AMSRL-CS-TT/Bldg. 434, Aberdeen Proving 
Ground, Maryland 21005-5425, Telephone: (410) 278-5028.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.
