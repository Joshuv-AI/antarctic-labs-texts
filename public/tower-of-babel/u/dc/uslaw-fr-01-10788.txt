SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Persons holding Individual Transferable Quotas (ITQ) in the surf 
clam/ocean quahog fishery are annually issued an allowable quota for 
harvest. Individual allocations may be transferred to other fishermen, 
but the ITQ holder must register such a transfer with NOAA in advance. 
The information is used by NOAA for enforcing the quotas.

II. Method of Collection

    A paper form is used for each transfer.
