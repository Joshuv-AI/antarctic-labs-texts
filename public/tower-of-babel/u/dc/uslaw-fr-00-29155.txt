DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-37,914 and NAFTA-4042]


Joseph Timber Company, Joseph, OR; Dismissal of Application for 
Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Joseph Timber Company, Joseph, 
Oregon. The application contained no new substantial information which 
would bear importantly on the Department's determination. Therefore, 
dismissal of the application was issued.

TA-W-37,914 & NAFTA-4042; Joseph Timber Company, Joseph, Oregon 
(November 2, 2000)


    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 7th day of November, 2000.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
