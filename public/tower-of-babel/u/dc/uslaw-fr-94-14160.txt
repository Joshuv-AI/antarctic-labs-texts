FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM
 

Norwest Corporation; Acquisition of Company Engaged in 
Permissible Nonbanking Activities; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 94-13153) published on page 
28105 of the issue for Tuesday, May 31, 1994.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis heading, the entry 
for Norwest Corporation is corrected to read as follows:
    Norwest Corporation, Minneapolis, Minnesota; to acquire through 
Norwest Mortgage, Inc., Minneapolis, Minnesota, the mortgage business 
of American National Bank of Silver City, Silver City, New Mexico, and 
thereby engage in originating and servicing mortgages pursuant to Sec.  
225.25(b)(1) of the Board's Regulation Y. These activities will be 
conducted in Silver City, New Mexico.
    Comments on this application must be received by June 24, 1994.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, June 6, 1994.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
