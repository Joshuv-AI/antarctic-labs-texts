DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Olympic Provincial Interagency Executive Committee (PIEC), 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Olympic PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on January 29, 
1997 at the Skokomish Tribal Center located at N. 80 Tribal Center Road 
in Shelton, Washington. The meeting will begin at 9:30 a.m. and 
continue until 3:00 p.m. Agenda items to be covered include: (1) Survey 
of Provincial Advisory Committees; (2) Update on Hood Canal District 
Programs; (3) Recreation Fee Demonstration; (4) Water Quality Planning; 
(5) Update on Olympic AMA Guide; and (6) Open Public Forum. Olympic 
Province Committee meetings are open to the public. Interested citizens 
are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Direct questions regarding this meeting to Kathy Snow, Province 
Liaison, USDA, Quilcene Ranger District, P.O. Box 280, Quilcene, WA 
98376, (360) 765-2211.

    Dated: December 19, 1997.
Ronald R. Humphrey,
Forest Supervisor.
