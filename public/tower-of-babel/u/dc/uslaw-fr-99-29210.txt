INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 701-TA-224 (Review)]


Live Swine From Canada

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Termination of five-year review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 29, 1999, the Department of Commerce released its 
negative final determination of the likelihood of continuation or 
recurrence of a countervailable subsidy in connection with the subject 
five-year review. Accordingly, pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff 
Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. Sec. 1675(c)), the five-year review of the 
countervailing duty order concerning live swine from Canada 
(investigation No. 701-TA-224 (Review)) is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 29, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Karen Taylor (202-708-4101), Office of

General information concerning the Commission may also be obtained by 
accessing its internet server (http://www.usitc.gov).

    Authority: This five-year review is being terminated under 
authority of title VII of the Tariff Act of 1930; this notice is 
published pursuant to section 207.69 of the Commission's rules (19 
CFR Sec. 207.69).
