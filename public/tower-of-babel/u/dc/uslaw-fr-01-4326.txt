FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-381, MM Docket No. 99-268, RM-9691]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Chattanooga, TN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Sarkes Tarzian, Inc., 
licensee of station WRCB-TV, NTSC channel 3, substitutes DTV channel 13 
for DTV channel 55 at Chattanooga, Tennessee.

[[Page 11118]]

See 64 FR 45500, August 20, 1999. DTV channel 13 can be allotted to 
Chattanooga in compliance with the principal community coverage 
requirements of Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates (35-09-40 N. 
and 85-18-52 W.) with a power of 37.0, HAAT of 325 meters and with a 
DTV service population of 915 thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective April 2, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
