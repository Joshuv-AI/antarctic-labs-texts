Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Minnesota

    The plat of the dependent resurvey of portions of the south, east 
and north boundaries, portions of the subdivisional lines, the survey 
of the subdivision of sections 1 through 4, 6, 7, 9 through 21, 23 
through 28, and 30 through 36, the reestablishment of the record 
meander line in sections 2, 3, 4, 11, 18, 23 and 34, and the metes-and-
bounds survey in section 34, Township 143 North, Range 39 West, 5th 
Principal Meridian, Minnesota, will be officially filed in Eastern 
States, Springfield, Virginia at 7:30 a.m., on June 19, 2000.
    The survey was requested by the Bureau of Indian Affairs.
    All inquiries or protests concerning the technical aspects of the 
survey must be sent to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor, Eastern States, 
Bureau of Land Management, 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 
22153, prior to 7:30 a.m., June 19, 2000.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the appropriate fee.

    Dated: May 4, 2000.
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
