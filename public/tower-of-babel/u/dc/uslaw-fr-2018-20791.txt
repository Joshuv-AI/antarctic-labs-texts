FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

FEDERAL REGISTER CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: 83 FR 47616.

PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TIME AND DATE OF THE MEETING: Tuesday, September 
25, 2018 at 10:00 a.m.

CHANGES IN THE MEETING: This meeting will also discuss:
    Investigatory records compiled for law enforcement purposes and
