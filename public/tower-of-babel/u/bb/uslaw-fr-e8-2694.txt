DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

National Highway Traffic Safety Administration


Reports, Forms and Record Keeping Requirements Agency Information 
Collection Activity Under OMB Review; Correction

AGENCY: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, DOT published 
a document in the Federal Register of January 30, 2008, concerning 
request for comments on proposed collection of information in 
compliance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 [44 U.S.C. 3501 et 
seq.]. The document contained incorrect dates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paul Simmons, 202-366-2315.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of January 30, 2008, in FR Doc. 08-377, on 
page 5627, in the second column, correct the DATES caption to read:

DATES: Comments must be received on or before February 29, 2008.
