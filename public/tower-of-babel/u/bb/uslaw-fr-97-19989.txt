DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary

32 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter A


Acquisition

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: Final rule; correcting amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This rule is published to give the heading ``Acquisition'' for 
32 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter A. On April 10, 1997 (62 FR 17549), the 
Department of Defense added to subchapter A a new regulation on 
criteria for nominating an acquisition program as a participant in the 
Defense Acquisition Pilot Program. This rule correctly designates a 
heading for subchapter A which was inadvertently omitted in the April 
10 regulation.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 30, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
L. Bynum or P. Toppings, 703-697-4111.

SUBCHAPTER A--ACQUISITION

    By the authority of 10 U.S.C. 301, the heading for 32 CFR Chapter 
I, Subchapter A is added as set forth above.
