1, 2004.

    A copy of the plats may be obtained from the Public Room at the 
Oregon State Office, Bureau of Land Management, 333 SW. 1st Avenue, 
Portland, Oregon 97204, upon required payment. A person or party who 
wishes to protest against a survey must file a notice that they wish to 
protest. (at the above address) with the State Director, Bureau of Land 
Management, Portland, Oregon.
    For further information contact: Chief, Branch of Geographic 
Sciences, Bureau of Land Management, (333 SW. 1st Avenue) P.O. Box 
2965, Portland, Oregon 97208.
