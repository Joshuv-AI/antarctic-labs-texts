Additional Information

    By a vote of 5-0 on June 14, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Order Referring Petitions to Intervene in MOX 
Proceeding to Licensing Board'' be held on June 14, and on less than 
one week's notice to the public.

U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Power Authority of the State of New York Entery 
Companies; Applications to Transfer Licenses for Indian Point 3 and 
Fitzpatrick Nuclear Plants; Merits Decision'' to be held on June 21, 
and on less than one week's notice to the public.
