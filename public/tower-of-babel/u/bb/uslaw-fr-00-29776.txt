DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

48 CFR Part 215


Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement; Profit Policy

AGENCY: Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Director of Defense Procurement is sponsoring a public 
meeting to discuss the proposed Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation 
Supplement (DFARS) rule on changes to profit policy published in the 
Federal Register at 65 FR 45574 on July 24, 2000. The Director of 
Defense Procurement would like to hear the views of interested parties 
on what they believe to be the key issues pertaining to the proposed 
rule and potential alternatives. A listing of some of the possible 
issues is included on the Internet Home Page of the Office of Cost, 
Pricing, and Finance at http://www.acq.osd.mil/dp/cpf.
    Subsequent to the discussions at the public meeting, the Director 
of Defense Procurement intends to publish a revised proposed rule for 
additional public comment.

DATES: The public meeting will be conducted at the address shown below 
on December 12, 2000, from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m., local time.

ADDRESSES: The public meeting will be conducted at Crystal Square 4,
