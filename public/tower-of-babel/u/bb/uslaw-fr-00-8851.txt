Radio Broadcasting Services; Texas and Oklahoma

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to counterproposals in this proceeding filed by 
First Broadcasting Management, LLC, KCYT-FM License Corporation, Gain-
Air, Inc., WBAP/KSCS Operating, Ltd., Blue Bonnet Radio, Inc., Heftel 
Broadcasting Corporation, Metro Broadcasters-Texas, Inc., Jerry Snyder 
and Associates, Inc., and Hunt Broadcasting, this document granted 
multiple channel substitutions and changes of community of license in 
Cross Plains, Allen, Benbrook, Brownwood, Burkburnnett, Campbell, 
Clifton, Coleman, Commerce, Detroit, Graham, Granbury, Haskell, Kerens, 
Mason, Jacksboro, McKinney, Muenster, San Saba, Snyder, Terrell, 
Vernon, Waco, and Wichita Falls, TX; Alva, Anadarko, Antlers, Ardmore, 
Atoka, Comanche, Dickson, Duncan, Durant, Eldorado, Hugo, and Lone 
Grove, OK. See Supplementary Information. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective May 4, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.
