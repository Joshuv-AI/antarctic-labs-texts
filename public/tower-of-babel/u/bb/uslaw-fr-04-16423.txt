N.E.W. Plastics Corp., Coleman, WI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 15, 2004, in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at N.E.W. Plastics 
Corporation, Coleman, Wisconsin.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 6th day of July, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
