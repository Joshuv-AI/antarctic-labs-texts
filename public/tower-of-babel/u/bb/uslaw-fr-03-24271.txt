DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration


Membership of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration 
Performance Review Board

AGENCY: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), 
Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of membership of NOAA Performance Review Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), NOAA announces the 
appointment of twenty-three members to serve on the NOAA Performance 
Review Board (PRB). The NOAA PRB is responsible for reviewing 
performance appraisals and ratings of Senior Executive Service (SES) 
members and making written recommendations to the appointing authority 
on SES retention and compensation matters, including performance-based 
pay adjustments, awarding of bonuses and reviewing recommendations for 
potential Presidential Rank Award nominees, and SES recertification. 
The appointment of members to the NOAA PRB will be for a period of 24 
months.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The effective date of service of the twenty-three 
appointees to the NOAA Performance Review Board is September 25, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Colette Magwood, Acting Executive 
Resources Program Manager, Human Resources Management Office, Office of 
Finance and Administration, NOAA, 1305 East-West Highway, Silver 
Spring, Maryland 20910, (301) 713-0530 (ext. 204).
