FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Notice of Agency Meeting; Sunshine Act Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that at 2:30 p.m. on 
Wednesday, January 31, 2007, the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation's Board of Directors will meet in closed session, pursuant 
to section 552b(c)(2), (c)(4), (c)(6), (c)(8), (9)(A)(ii), (c)(9)(B), 
and (c)(10), Title 5, United States Code, to consider matters relating 
to the Corporation's supervisory and corporate activities.
    The meeting will be held in the Board Room on the sixth floor of 
the FDIC Building located at 550 17th Street, NW., Washington, DC.
    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Robert E. Feldman, Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-7122.

    Dated: January 24, 2007.

Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
