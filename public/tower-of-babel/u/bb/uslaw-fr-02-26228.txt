FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-2311; MB Docket No. 02-287; RM-10569]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Stuart, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition filed by Robert 
Fabian proposing the allotment of Channel 228A at Stuart, Oklahoma, as 
that community's first commercial broadcast transmission service. 
Channel 228A can be allotted to Stuart in compliance with the minimum 
distance separation requirements of the Commission's Rules at the city 
reference coordinates without a site restriction. The coordinates for 
Channel 228A at Stuart are 34-54-18 and 96-06-00.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before November 18, 2002, and reply 
comments on or before December 3, 2002.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 445 Twelfth Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554. In addition to filing comments with the FCC, 
interested parties should serve the petitioner, as follows: Robert 
Fabian, 4 Hickory Crossing Lane, Argyle, Texas, 76226.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rolanda F. Smith, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
