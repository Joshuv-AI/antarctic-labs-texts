DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Cemeteries and Memorials, Notice of 
Rechartering

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended Pub. L. 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App.), that the Department of Veterans 
Affairs' Advisory Committee on Cemeteries and Memorials has been 
rechartered for 2-year period beginning September 9, 1997, through 
September 9, 1999.

    Dated: September 12, 1997.

    By direction of the Secretary-Designate.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
