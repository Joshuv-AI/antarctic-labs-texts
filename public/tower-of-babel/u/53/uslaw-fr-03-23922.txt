FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-2636; MM Docket No. 03-36; RM-10431]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Woodbine, IA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Harrison County Radio, this document 
dismisses its Petition for Rule Making proposing to allot Channel 293A 
to Woodbine, Iowa. See 68 FR 8728, published February 25, 2003. With 
this action, the proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.
