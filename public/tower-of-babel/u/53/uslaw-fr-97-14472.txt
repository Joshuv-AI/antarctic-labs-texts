    Petitions for reconsideration have been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room 239, 1919 M Street, NW., 
Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to this petition must

rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1). Replies to an opposition must be filed within 
10 days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.
