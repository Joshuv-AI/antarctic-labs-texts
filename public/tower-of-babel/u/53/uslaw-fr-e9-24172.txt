DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 4, 2009, 8 a.m. to 
November 5, 2009, 5 p.m., Bethesda North Marriott Hotel & Conference 
Center, Montgomery County Conference Center Facility, 5701 Marinelli 
Road, North Bethesda, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 21, 2009, 74FR42315.
    This Federal Register Notice is being amended to reflect a change 
in the name of the committee to ``Application of Emerging Technologies 
and Biospecimen Science for Cancer Research'' and not as otherwise 
stated. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 1, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
