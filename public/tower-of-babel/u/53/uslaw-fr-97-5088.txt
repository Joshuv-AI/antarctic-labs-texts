NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting; Corporate Culture and Transportation: A 
Symposium

    A symposium on the effect that corporate management philosophies 
and practices have on transportation safety will be conducted by the 
National Transportation Safety Board. The symposium will be held on 
April 24 and 25, 1997, at the Hyatt Regency Hotel in Crystal City, 
Virginia. For more information, contact Julie Beal at (202) 314-6000 or 
fax (202) 314-6293.

February 25, 1997.
Bea Hardesty,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
