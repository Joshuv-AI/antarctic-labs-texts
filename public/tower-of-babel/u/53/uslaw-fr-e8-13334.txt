FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. E8-12885 published on page 
32709 of the issue for Tuesday, June 10, 2008.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for Pinnacle Bancorp, Inc., Central City, Nebraska, is revised to read 
as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City (Todd Offenbacker, Assistant 
Vice President) 1 Memorial Drive, Kansas City, Missouri 64198-0001:
    1. Pinnacle Bancorp, Inc., Central City, Nebraska, to acquire 100 
percent of the voting shares of First Azle Bancshares, Inc., Azle, 
Texas, and thereby indirectly acquire voting shares of Wood Financial 
Group, Inc., Dover, Delaware, and First Bank, Azle, Texas.
    Comments on this application must be received by July 3, 2008.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, June 10, 2008.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
