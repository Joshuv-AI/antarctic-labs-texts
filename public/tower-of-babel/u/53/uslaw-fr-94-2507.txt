OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT

 

Proposed Information Collection Submitted to OMB for Review

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (title 
44, U.S.C., chapter 35), this notice announces a request to revise the 
use of OPM Form 805 that collects information from the public. OPM Form 
805, Application to be Listed Under the Voting Rights Act of 1965, is 
used to elicit information from persons applying for voter registration 
under the authority of the Voting Rights Act of 1965. The requirements 
for voter eligibility vary from State to State; therefore, OPM Form 805 
is a blanket number covering 10 forms which conform to the individual 
State's requirements. The form requires 20 minutes to complete. 
Approximately 10 individuals complete the form annually for a total 
public burden of 4 hours.
    For copies of this proposal call C. Ronald Trueworthy on (703) 908-
8550.
