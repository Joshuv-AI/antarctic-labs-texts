Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-35-2023]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 207; Authorization of Production 
Activity; LEGO Manufacturing Richmond, Inc. (LEGO[supreg] Bricks and 
Toy Sets), Chester and Colonial Heights, Virginia

    On May 24, 2023, the LEGO Group submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board on behalf of LEGO 
Manufacturing Richmond, Inc., within FTZ 207, for its facilities in 
Chester and Colonial Heights, Virginia.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (88 FR 35832, June 1, 2023). On 
September 21, 2023, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at this 
time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including section 400.14.

    Dated: September 21, 2023.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
