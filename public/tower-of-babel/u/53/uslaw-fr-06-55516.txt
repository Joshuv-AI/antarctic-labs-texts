DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service

7 CFR Part 319


Foreign Quarantine Notices

CFR Correction

    In Title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 300 to 399, 
revised as of January 1, 2006, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 378, in Sec.  319.56-2d, paragraph (c), and on page 384, 
in Sec.  319.56-2l, paragraph (b)(2)(ii), remove the title ``Deputy 
Administrator of the Plant Protection and Quarantine Programs'' and add 
in its place ``Administrator''; and
    2. On page 385, in Sec.  319.56-2m, remove the table in paragraph 
(b).
