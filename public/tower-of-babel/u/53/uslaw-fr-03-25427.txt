Establishment of Class E Airspace at Afton Municipal Airport, 
Afton, WY

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action deletes reference to the magnetic headings in the 
airspace description of the Class E airspace at Afton Municipal 
Airport, Afton, WY, that was published on July 31, 2003 (68 FR 44874), 
Airspace Docket 02-ANM-07.

EFFECTIVE DATE: 0901 UTC, October 30, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ed Haeseker, ANM-520.7; telephone 
(425) 227-2527; Federal Aviation Administration, Docket No. 02-ANM-07, 
1601 Lind Avenue SW., Renton, Washington 98055-4056.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
    History: Airspace Docket 02-ANM-07 published on July 31, 2003 (68 
FR 44874), established Class E Airspace at Afton Municipal Airport, 
Afton, WY, effective date of October 30, 2003. Magnetic as well as true 
heading were used to describe parameters of the Class E Airspace for 
Afton Municipal Airport, Afton, WY. This action only deletes references 
to the magnetic headings.

E Airspace; Airways; Routes; and Reporting Points [Amended]

The references to magnetic headings in the description of the Class E

ANM UT E5 Afton, WY (Corrected)
Afton Municipal Airport, WY
    (Lat 42[deg]42'41'' N, long. 110[deg]56'32'' W)

    That airspace extending upward from 700 feet above the surface 
of the earth within 6.5 mile radius of the Afton Municipal Airport, 
and within 2 miles either side of the 355[deg] bearing from the 
airport extending from the 6.5 miles radius to 7.5 miles north of 
the airport, and within 2 miles either side of the 185[deg] bearing 
from the airport extending from the 6.5 mile radius to 19.3 miles 
south of the airport.
