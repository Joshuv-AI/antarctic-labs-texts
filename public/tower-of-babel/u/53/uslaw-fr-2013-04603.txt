Federal Acquisition Regulation; Technical Amendments

AGENCY: Department of Defense (DoD), General Services Administration 
(GSA), and National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes amendments to the Federal Acquisition 
Regulation (FAR) in order to make editorial changes.

DATES: Effective February 28, 2013.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The Regulatory Secretariat, 1275 First 
Street NE., 7th Floor, Washington, DC 20417, 202-501-4755, for 
information pertaining to status or publication schedules. Please cite 
FAC 2005-66, Technical Amendments.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In order to update certain elements in 48 
CFR parts 5, 7, 10, and 52, this document makes editorial changes to 
the FAR.

    Therefore, DoD, GSA, and NASA amend 48 CFR parts 5, 7, 10, and 52 
as set forth below:

0
1. The authority citation for 48 CFR parts 5, 7, 10, and 52 is revised 
to read as follows:

    Authority:  40 U.S.C. 121(c); 10 U.S.C. chapter 137; and 51 
U.S.C. 20113.

PART 5--PUBLICIZING CONTRACT ACTIONS


5.601  [Amended]

0
2. Amend section 5.601 by removing from paragraphs (a), (b)(1), and 
(b)(2) ``http://www.contractdirectory.gov/contractdirectory/'' and 
adding ``https://www.contractdirectory.gov/contractdirectory/'' in its 
place.
