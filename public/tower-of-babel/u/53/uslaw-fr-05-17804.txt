DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4709]


Renewal of the Charter of the Advisory Committee on Cultural 
Diplomacy

    Summary: The Department of State announces that the charter of the 
Advisory Committee on Cultural Diplomacy has been renewed for an 
additional two-year period, to expire on August 31, 2007.
    The Department of State announces that the charter of the Advisory 
Committee on Cultural Diplomacy, established under Public Law 107-228, 
section 224, has been renewed for an additional two-year period. The 
charter will now expire on August 31, 2007.
    The Advisory Committee on Cultural Diplomacy was established to 
``advise the Secretary on programs and policies to advance the use of 
cultural diplomacy in United States foreign policy.''

    Dated: August 31, 2005.
Daniel Schuman,
Chief, Cultural Programs Division, Department of State.
