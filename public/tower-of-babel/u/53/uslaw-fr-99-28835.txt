SUMMARY: A notice concerning a proposed Bureau of Land Management 
withdrawal was published on August 6, 1999. This notice corrects the 
legal description in two places.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Cliff Yardley, BLM Arizona State 
Office, 602-417-9437.

42959 (third column), Sec. 17 under T. 11 N., R. 2 E., is corrected to 
read:

Sec. 17, W\1/2\W\1/2\E\1/2\;

    2. In the Federal Register publication of August 6, 1999, page 
42960 (first column), Sec. 19 under T. 10 N., R. 3 E., is corrected to 
read:

Sec. 19, lots 1 to 7, inclusive, NE\1/4\, E\1/2\NW\1/4\, NE\1/
4\SW\1/4\, N\1/2\SE\1/4\, and SE\1/4\SE\1/4\.
