FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[DA 99-385]


Unified Policy For Dismissing Applications and Pleadings

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 13582]]

SUMMARY: This document released on February 24, 1999, under delegated 
authority, announced a unified policy regarding dismissing and 
returning applications and dismissing pleadings associated with 
applications. The policy is intended to promote: the correct filing of 
applications and pleadings associated with such applications, expedited 
processing of all wireless applications, and consistency in the 
treatment of all applications received by the Wireless 
Telecommunications Bureau.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 1, 1999.
