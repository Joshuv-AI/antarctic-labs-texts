DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
Minerals Management Service


Outer Continental Shelf, Alaska Region, Proposed Cook Inlet Lease 
Sale 149

AGENCY: Minerals Management Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Availability of the Final Environmental Impact 
Statement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Minerals Management Service (MMS) has prepared a final 
Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) relating to the proposed 1996 
Outer Continental Shelf oil and gas lease sale of available unleased 
blocks in Cook Inlet. The proposed Cook Inlet Sale 149 will offer for 
lease approximately 2.0 million acres. Single copies of the EIS can be 
obtained from the Regional Director, Minerals Management Service, 
Alaska Region, 949 East 36th Avenue, Anchorage, Alaska 99503-4302, 
Attention: Public Information. Copies can be requested by telephone, 
(907) 271-6070.
