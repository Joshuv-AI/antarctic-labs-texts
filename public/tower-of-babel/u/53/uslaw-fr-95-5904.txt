Dee Exploration, Inc.; Whitesboro, TX; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on January 17, 1995 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Dee Exploration, Inc., 
Whitesboro, Texas.
    All workers were separated from the subject firm more than one year 
prior to the date of the petition. Section 223 of the Act specifies 
that no certification may apply to any worker whose last separation 
occurred more than one year before the date of the petition. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 3rd day of March, 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
