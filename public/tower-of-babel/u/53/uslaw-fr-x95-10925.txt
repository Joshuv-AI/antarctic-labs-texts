Radio Broadcasting Services; Ash Grove, MO

CFR Correction

of October 1, 1994, on page 94, in Sec. 73.202, in the table for FM 
allocations for the state of Missouri, the entry for Ash Grove was 
inadvertently ommited. The entry should read as follows:
