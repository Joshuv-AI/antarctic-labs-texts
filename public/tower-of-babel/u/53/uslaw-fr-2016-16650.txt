DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 1494-433; Oklahoma]


Grand River Dam Authority; Notice of Tribal Consultation Meeting

    On May 6, 2016, the Grand River Dam Authority (GRDA) filed an 
application to amend the rule curve specified in Article 401 of the 
license for the Pensacola Project. The rule curve sets forth target 
water elevations for Grand Lake O' the Cherokees.
    The Commission will hold a meeting with representatives of the 
tribes comprising the Inter-Tribal Council, Inc. potentially affected 
by GRDA's application to amend the rule curve. The meeting will be held 
from 9:00 a.m. to about 4:00 p.m. on Wednesday August 3, 2016, at the 
Miami Tribe of Oklahoma Council House, 2319 Newman Road, Miami, 
Oklahoma 74354.
    Interested parties may attend the meeting as observers. The meeting 
will be transcribed by a court reporter and the transcript will be 
placed in the record of this proceeding.
    For further information on this meeting please contact either B. 
Peter Yarrington at (202) 502-6129 or Linda Stewart at (202) 502-6680.

    Dated: July 8, 2016
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
