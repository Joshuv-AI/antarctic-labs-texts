DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Meeting of the DoD Healthcare Quality Initiative Review Panel

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION:  An executive/administration meeting for DoD Healthcare Quality 
Initiatives Review Panel has been scheduled for September 14 & 15, 
2000.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice set forth the meeting of the DoD Healthcare 
Quality Initiatives Review Panel. Notice of meeting is required under 
The Federal Advisory Committee Act.

ADDRESSES: Hilton Crystal City, 2399 Jefferson Davis Hwy, Arlington, VA 
22202.
    Time: September 14th, 5 pm to 8 pm; September 15th, 8 am to 5:30 
pm.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION: Contact Gia Edmonds at (703) 933-8325.
