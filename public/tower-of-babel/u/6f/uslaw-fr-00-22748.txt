FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 00-1959, MM Docket No. 00-152, RM-9943]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Elkhart, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition filed by Elkhart 
Broadcasting Company proposing the allotment of Channel 265A at 
Elkhart, Texas. The channel can be allotted to Elkhart in compliance 
with the Commission's spacing requirements at coordinates 31-34-07 NL 
and 95-41-52 WL. There is a site restriction 12.8 kilometers (8.0 
miles) southwest of the community.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before October 16, 2000, and reply 
comments on or before October 31, 2000.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner's counsel, as follows: Mark N. Lipp, Scott C. 
Cinnamon, Shook Hardy & Bacon, L.L.P., 600 14th Street, NW., Suite 800, 
Washington, DC 20005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
