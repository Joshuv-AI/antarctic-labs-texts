Title 3--
The President
                Presidential Determination No. 95-6 of November 30, 
                1994

 

Assistance Program for Independent States of the
                Former Soviet Union

                Memorandum for the Secretary of State

                Pursuant to subsection (d) under the heading 
                ``Assistance for the New Independent States of the 
                Former Soviet Union'' in title II of the Foreign 
                Operations, Export Financing, and Related Programs 
                Appropriations Act, 1995 (titles I-V of Public Law 103-
                306), I hereby determine that it is in the national 
                interest of the United States to make available funds 
                appropriated under that heading without regard to the 
                restriction in that subsection.

                You are authorized and directed to notify the Congress 
                of this determination and to publish it in the Federal 
                Register.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    Washington, November 30, 1994.
