DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[Application No. D-9627]


Hassan Zekavat, M.D., P.A. Money Purchase Pension Plan (the Plan) 
Located in Moorestown, NJ

AGENCY: Pension and Welfare Benefits Administration.

ACTION: Withdrawal of proposed exemption.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    In the Federal Register dated January 31, 1996 (60 FR 3483), the 
Department of Labor (the Department) published a notice of pendency of 
a proposed exemption from the prohibited transaction restrictions of 
the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 and from certain 
taxes imposed by the Internal Revenue Code of 1986. The notice of 
pendency concerned an application filed on behalf the Plan.
    By a letter dated September 10, 1996, the applicant's 
representatives informed the Department that they wanted to withdraw 
the application from consideration.
    Notice is hereby given that the Department has made a final 
decision to withdraw the notice of pendency for the proposed exemption 
from the Federal Register.
    Accordingly, the notice of pendency is hereby withdrawn.

    Signed at Washington DC, this 26th day of September 1996.
Ivan Strasfeld,
Director of Exemption Determinations, Pension and Welfare Benefits 
Administration, U.S. Department of Labor.
