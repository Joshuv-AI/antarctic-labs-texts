FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-744, MM Docket No. 01-44, RM-10022]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Derby, KS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Pappas Telecasting of 
America, allots DTV channel 46 to Derby, Kansas. See 66 FR 12753, 
February 28, 2001. DTV channel 46 can be allotted to Derby, Kansas, in 
compliance with the principle community coverage requirements of 
Section 73.625(a) at reference coordinates 37-54-12 N. and 97-37-06 W. 
with a power of 1000, HAAT of 246 meters. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective May 5, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-1600.
