Mississippi River Transmission Corporation; Revised Notice 
Rescheduling Technical Conference

November 5, 1998.
    Take notice that the Commission staff will convene a technical 
conference as provided by the Commission order in this proceeding 
issued October 14,

[[Page 63310]]

1998.\1\ The technical conference, previously scheduled for Wednesday, 
November 4, 1998, at 10:00 a.m., has been rescheduled.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Mississippi River Transmission Corporation, 85 FERC 
para.61,049 (1998).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Take notice that the technical conference will be held on Thursday, 
November 12, 1998, at 2:00 p.m. in a room to be designated at the 
offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, 
N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
    Attendance will be limited to parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Jerie O'Connor at (202) 208-0459, or Harris 
Wood at (202) 208-0224.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
