Notice of Right-of-Way Application; New Mexico

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: An application, serialized as NMNM91469, was received from El 
Paso Natural Gas Company (EPNG) for a natural gas pipeline right-of-way 
in San Juan County, New Mexico.
    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to Section 28 of the Mineral 
Leasing Act of 1920 (30 USC 185), as amended by the Act of November 16, 
1973, (37 Stat. 576), EPNG has applied for a right-of-way for 36 inch 
diameter pipeline that is 5.20 miles in length. An additional 11.00 
miles would be located on land administered by the Bureau of Indian 
Affairs. The project would loop existing lines and will help relieve 
line pressure and also help in accommodating projected volumes. The 
proposed line crosses the following public lands.
