Agency Information Collection Activities: Proposed Collection; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: National Archives and Records Administration (NARA).

ACTION: Cancellation of notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 4, 2014, NARA published a notice (79 FR 72033) 
that the agency was proposing to reinstate the information collection 
National Historical Publications and Records Commission (NHPRC) Grant 
Program, Budget Form and Instructions (OMB number 3095-0013, agency 
form number NA Form 17001), which NHPRC uses in its grant program. NARA 
invited the public to comment on the proposed information collection 
pursuant to the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995. However, NARA is now 
cancelling that notice and request for comment, due to errors. NARA 
will publish a new, corrected notice and request for comments within 
the week.

DATES: This cancellation is effective immediately upon publication in 
the Federal Register.

ADDRESSES: Paperwork Reduction Act Comments (ISSD), Room 4400, National

[email protected].
