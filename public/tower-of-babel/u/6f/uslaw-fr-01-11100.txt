DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-39,031]


IER, Inc. Temple, TX; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 16, 2001 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at IER, Inc., Temple, Texas.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already

[[Page 22264]]

in effect (TA-W-37,848, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 18th day of April, 2001.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
