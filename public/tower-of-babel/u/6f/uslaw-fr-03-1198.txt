FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-86, MM Docket No. 00-139, RM-9915]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Little Rock, AR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of River City Broadcasting, 
Inc. (formerly Channel 42 of Little Rock, Inc.), substitutes DTV 
channel 44 for DTV channel 43c. See 65 FR 51277, August 23, 2000. DTV 
channel 44 can be allotted to Little Rock, Arkansas, in compliance with 
the principle community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at 
reference coordinates 34-47-56 N. and 92-29-44 W. with a power of 1000, 
HAAT of 334 meters and with a DTV service population of 866 thousand. 
With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 3, 2003.

418-1600.
