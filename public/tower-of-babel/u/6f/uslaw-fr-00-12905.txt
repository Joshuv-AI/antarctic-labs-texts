DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special emphasis Panel, June 7, 2000, 
7 PM to June 8, 2000, 3 PM, Columbia Sheraton, 10207 Wincopin Circle, 
Columbia, MD, 21044 which was published in the Federal Register on 
April 2, 2000, 65 FR 24491-24492.
    The name of the meeting will be changed to Hematopoietic Stem Cell 
Biology RFA. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 12, 2000.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
