DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,611]


Kreber; High Point, NC; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 17, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Kreber, High Point, 
North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 20th day of April 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
