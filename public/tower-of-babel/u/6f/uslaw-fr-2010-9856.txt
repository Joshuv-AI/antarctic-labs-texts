FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments on the agreements to the Secretary, Federal Maritime 
Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within ten days of the date this 
notice appears in the Federal Register. Copies of the agreements are 
available through the Commission's Web site (http://www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of Agreements at (202)-523-5793 or 
[email protected].
    Agreement No.: 011545-002.
    Title: Agreement Between CSAV and Mitsui.
    Parties: Compania Sud Americana de Vapores, S.A. (CSAV) and Mitsui 
O.S.K. Lines, Ltd.
    Filing Party: Walter H. Lion, Esq.; McLaughlin & Stern, LLP; 260 
Madison Avenue; New York, NY 10016.
    Synopsis: The amendment deletes the pricing and pooling authorities 
and makes conforming technical changes.

    Agreement No.: 012092-001.
    Title: MOL/''K'' Line Space Charter and Sailing Agreement.
    Parties: Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha, Ltd. and Mitsui O.S.K. Lines, Ltd.
    Filing Parties: John P. Meade, Esq.; Vice President-Law; ``K'' Line 
America, Inc.; 6009 Bethlehem Road; Preston, MD, 21655.
    Synopsis: The amendment expands the geographic scope to include 
South East Asia ports, West Coast Canadian ports and U.S. Atlantic 
Coast ports. It also modifies the number of vessels and TEU capacities 
that may be deployed under the agreement.

    Agreement No.: 012095.
    Title: Hybur Ltd./Seafreight Space Charter Agreement.
    Parties: Hybur Ltd. and Seafreight Line, Ltd.
    Filing Parties: Alfred McNab; General Manager and Secretary; Hyde 
Shipping Corporation.; 10025 N.W. 116th Way, Suite 2; Medley, FL 33178.
    Synopsis: The agreement authorizes Seafreight to charter space from 
Hybur Ltd. between the ports of Port Everglades, FL and George Town, 
Grand Cayman.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
    Dated: April 23, 2010.
Karen V. Gregory,
Secretary.
