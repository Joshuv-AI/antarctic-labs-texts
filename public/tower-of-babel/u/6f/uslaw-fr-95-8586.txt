DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Notification of Proposed Terminations or Substantial Reductions 
of Major Defense Programs

    Section 4471 of the FY93 Defense Authorization Act, as amended by 
section 1372 of the FY94 Defense Authorization Act and section 1142 of 
the FY95 Defense Authorization Act, requires that each prime contractor 
under a major defense program be notified if the program is proposed 
for substantial reductions or terminations as forwarded to Congress in 
the Presidents Budget.
    The following Air Force prime contractor is hereby notified the 
program listed below has been proposed to be terminated by the Fiscal 
Year 96 President's budget:
