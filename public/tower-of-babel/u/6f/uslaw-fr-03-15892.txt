SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster P010]


State of Arkansas (Amendment 1)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective June 
10, 2003, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to establish 
the incidence period for this disaster as beginning on May 2, 2003, and 
continuing through June 10, 2003.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is August 5, 2003.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59008.)

    Dated: June 18, 2003.
Cheri C. Wolff,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
