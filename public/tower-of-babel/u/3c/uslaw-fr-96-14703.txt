FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 89-590; RM-7071, RM-7855]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Sumter, Orangeburg and Columbia, SC

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Gamecock City Broadcasting, 
Inc. (now Threshold Broadcasting, Inc.), dismisses the request to 
reallot Channel 267C from Sumter to Columbia, South Carolina, and 
modify Station WWDM's license accordingly. See 55 Fed. Reg. 883, 
January 10, 1990. The Commission also denies the request of Radio South 
Carolina, Inc., to reallot Channel 294C1 from Orangeburg to Columbia, 
and the modification of Station WTCB(FM)'s license accordingly. See 57 
Fed. Reg. 7902, March 5, 1992. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 11, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
