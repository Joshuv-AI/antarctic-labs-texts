Drexel Heritage Furnishings, Inc., Drexel, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 3, 2003 in response to a petition 
filed by workers at Drexel Heritage Furnishings, Drexel, North 
Carolina.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on May 9, 2002, and remains in effect (TA-W-
41,003). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 10th day of April, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
