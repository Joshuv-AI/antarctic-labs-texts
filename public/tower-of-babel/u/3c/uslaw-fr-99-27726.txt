FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Notice of Proposals To Engage in Permissible Nonbanking 
Activities or To Acquire Companies That Are Engaged in Permissible 
Nonbanking Activities; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 99-27116) published on page 
56210 of the issue for Monday, October 18, 1999.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia heading, the entry 
for Patriot Bank Corp., Inc., Pottstown, Pennsylvania, is revised to 
read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia (Michael E. Collins, Senior 
Vice President) 100 North 6th Street, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19105-
1521:
    1. Patriot Bank Corp., Inc., Pottstown, Pennsylvania; to acquire 
ZipFinancial.com, Inc., and thereby engage de novo in providing data 
processing and data transmission services via the Internet, pursuant to 
section 225.28(b)(14) of Regulation Y, and in providing management 
consulting advice to unaffiliated depository institutions, pursuant to 
section 225.18(b)(9) of Regulation Y.
    Comments on this application must be received by November 2, 1999.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, October 19, 
1999.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
