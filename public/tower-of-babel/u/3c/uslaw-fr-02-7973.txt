Radio Broadcasting Services; Boscobel, WI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 244C3 to Boscobel, Wisconsin, in 
response to a petition filed by Starboard Broadcasting, Inc. See 67 FR 
2704, January 14, 2002. The coordinates for Channel 244C3 at Boscobel, 
Wisconsin, are 43-08-04 NL and 90-42-19 WL. A filing window for Channel 
244C3 at Boscobel, Wisconsin, will not be opened at this time. Instead, 
the issue of opening this allotment for auction will be addressed by 
the Commission in a subsequent order.

DATES: Effective April 29, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
