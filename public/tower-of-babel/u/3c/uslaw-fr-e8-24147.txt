COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Correction of Notice of Additions & Deletion

    In the notice appearing on page 57590, FR Doc E8-23317, Procurement 
List Additions & Deletions, on October 3, 2008, the Committee published 
the following product and NSN (Bag, Sand, Polypropylene, 26'' x 14'', 
8105-01-467-0402).
    This notice corrects the size for the above mentioned product and 
NSN to Bag, Sand, Polypropylene, 20'' x 14'', 8105-01-467-0402.
