Maryland.

* * * * *

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 4-0 on March 6, the Commission 
determined pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec.  9.107(a) of the 
Commission's rules that ``Discussion of Legislative Issues (Closed--Ex. 
9)'' be held on March 6, and on less than one week's notice to the 
public.
    By a vote of 5-0 on March 6, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec.  9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Final Rule to Standardize the Process for Allowing a 
Licensee to Release Part of Its Reactor Facility or site for 
Unrestricted Use Before NRC Has Approved Its License Termination Plan'' 
be held on March 7, and on less than one week's notice to the public.
    By a vote of 5-0 on march 7, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(E) and Sec.  9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Discussion of legislative Issues (Closed--Ed. 9)'' be held on March 
7, and on less than one week's notice to the public.
