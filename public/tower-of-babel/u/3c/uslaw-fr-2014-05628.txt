Reliability Standards for Physical Security Measures; Notice of 
Designation of Commission Staff as Non-Decisional

    With respect to an order issued by the Commission today in the 
above-captioned docket, pursuant to 18 CFR 385.2201(c)(3) (2013), the 
staff identified below from the Office of Electric Reliability are 
designated as non-decisional in deliberations by the Commission in this 
docket. Accordingly, these persons will not serve as advisors to the 
Commission or otherwise be involved in the decisional process in this 
proceeding. Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.2201 (2013), they are prohibited from communicating with advisory 
staff concerning any deliberations in this docket.
    The staff designated as non-decisional are:

Edward Franks
Regis Binder
David O'Connor
Mary Agnes Nimis
Justin Kelly
Andres Lopez

     Dated: March 7, 2014.
 Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
