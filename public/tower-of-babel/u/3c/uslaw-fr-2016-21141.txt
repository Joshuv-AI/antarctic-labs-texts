FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Request for Additional Information

    The Commission gives notice that it has formally requested that the 
parties to the below listed agreement provide additional information 
pursuant to 46 U.S.C. 40304(d). This action prevents the agreement from 
becoming effective as originally scheduled. Interested parties may file 
comments within fifteen (15) days after publication of this notice in 
the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 012426.
    Title: OCEAN Alliance Agreement.
    Parties: COSCO Container Lines Co., Ltd.; CMA CGM S.A., APL Co. Pte 
Ltd, and American President Lines, Ltd. (acting as one party); 
Evergreen Marine Corporation (Taiwan) Ltd. acting on its own behalf 
and/or on behalf of other members of the Evergreen Line Joint Service 
Agreement (ELJSA); and Orient Overseas Container Line Limited and OOCL 
(Europe) Limited (acting as one party).

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.

    Dated: August 30, 2016.
Rachel E. Dickon,
Assistant Secretary.
