FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 90-647; RM-7180]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Ladysmith and Hallie, WI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document reallots Channel 279C1 from Ladysmith, 
Wisconsin, to Hallie, Wisconsin, and modifies the license for Station 
WWBI to specify Hallie as its community of license in response to a 
petition filed by Stewards of Sound, Inc. See 56 FR 1509, January 15, 
1991. The coordinates for Channel 279C1 at Hallie are 45-06-35 and 91-
09-43. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 5, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
