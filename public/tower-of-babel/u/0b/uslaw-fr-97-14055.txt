SUMMARY: In notice document 97-10699, published at 62 FR 20205 (April 
25, 1997), in the table on page 20209, ``Table 4--Seventy Percent of

omitted. The corrected table (lines 14 and 30) is published as the 
attachment to this document
    We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
