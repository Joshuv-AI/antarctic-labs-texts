DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. QF95-220-000]


Central Wayne Energy Recovery, Limited Partnership; Notice of 
Supplement to Filing

February 21, 1995.
    On February 14, 1995, Central Wayne Energy Recovery, Limited 
Partnership (Applicant) tendered for filing a supplement to its filing 
in this docket. No determination has been made that the submittal 
constitutes a complete filing.
    The supplement provides additional information pertaining primarily 
to the technical data and the ownership structure of the small power 
production facility.
    Any person desiring to be heard or objecting to the granting of 
qualifying status should file a motion to intervene or protest with the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with rules 211 and 214 of the 
Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such motions or 
protests must be filed by March 14, 1995, and must be served on the 
applicant. Protests will be considered by the Commission in 
[[Page 10582]] determining the appropriate action to be taken but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person 
wishing to become a party must file a petition to intervene. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
