NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[Docket No. 40-8027-MLA-4; ASLBP No. 99-770-09-MLA]


Sequoyah Fuels Corporation, Gore, Oklahoma; Notice of 
Reconstitution

    Pursuant to the authority contained in 10 CFR 2.722 and 2.1209, the 
Special

[[Page 55415]]

Assistant in the above-captioned 10 CFR part 2, subpart L proceeding is 
hereby replaced by appointing Administrative Judge Anthony J. Baratta 
in place of Administrative Judge Thomas D. Murphy.
    All correspondence, documents, and other material shall be filed 
with the Special Assistant in accordance with 10 CFR 2.1203. The 
address of the new Special Assistant is: Administrative Judge Anthony 
J. Baratta, Special Assistant, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, 
U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Washington, DC 20555-0001.

    Issued at Rockville, Maryland, this 16th day of September 2003.
G. Paul Bollwerk, III,
Chief Administrative Judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel.
