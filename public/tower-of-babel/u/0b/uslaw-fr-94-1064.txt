SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

``FEDERAL REGISTER'' CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: [59 FR 1415, 
January 10, 1994].

STATUS: Open meeting.

PLACE: 450 Fifth Street, NW., Washington, DC.

DATE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED: Thursday, January 6, 1994.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Time Change.
    The open meeting scheduled for Wednesday, January 12, 1994 at 10 
a.m., has been rescheduled for Wednesday, January 12, 1994 at 11 a.m.
    Commissioner Beese, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above change and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact: Blair Thomas at (202) 272-2300.

    Dated: January 11, 1994.
Margaret H. McFarland,
Deputy Secretary.
