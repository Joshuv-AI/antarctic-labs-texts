DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Announcement of Membership of the Patent and Trademark Office 
Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Patent and Trademark Office, Commerce.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In conformance with the Civil Service Reform Act of 1978, 5 
U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), the Patent and Trademark Office announces the 
appointment of persons to serve as members of its Performance Review 
Board.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Alethea Long-Green, Director, Office of Human Resources, Patent and

[[Page 54627]]

Trademark Office, One Crystal Park, Suite 700, Washington, DC 20231 or 
telephone (703) 305-8062.

Trademark Office Performance Review Board is as follows:

Financial Officer, Patent and Trademark Office, Washington, DC 20231,

1998
