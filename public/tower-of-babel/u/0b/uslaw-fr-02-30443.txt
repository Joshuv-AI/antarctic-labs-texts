INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigations Nos. 731-TA-757 and 759 (Review)]


Collated Roofing Nails From China and Taiwan

AGENCY: International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Termination of five-year reviews.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The subject five-year reviews were initiated in October 2002 
to determine whether revocation of the antidumping duty orders on 
collated roofing nails from China and Taiwan would be likely to lead to 
continuation or recurrence of dumping and of material injury to a 
domestic industry. On November 25, 2002, the Department of Commerce 
published notice that it was revoking the orders effective November 25, 
2002 because ``no domestic party responded to the sunset review notice 
of initiation by the applicable deadline'' (67 FR 70578). Accordingly, 
pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 
1675(c)), the subject reviews are terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 25, 2002.
