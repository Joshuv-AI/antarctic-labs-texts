PPL Montana, LLC and Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes of 
the Flathead Nation; Notice of Effective Date of Withdrawal of Petition 
for Declaratory Order

June 2, 2003.
    On June 19, 2001, PPL Montana, LLC (PPL Montana) filed a petition 
for declaratory order to clarify its obligations as co-licensee of the 
Kerr Project No. 5, located on the Flathead River in Lake and Flathead 
Counties, Montana, and partially on lands within the Flathead Indian 
Reservation. On April 17, 2003, PPL Montana filed a notice of 
withdrawal of its petition.
    No motion to the notice of withdrawal was filed, and the Commission 
took no action to disallow the withdrawal. Accordingly, pursuant to 
rule 216 of the Commission's rules of practice and procedure,\1\ the 
withdrawal became effective on May 2, 2003.
