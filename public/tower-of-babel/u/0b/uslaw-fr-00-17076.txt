GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Federal Travel Regulation; National Travel Conference 2000

AGENCY: Office of Governmentwide Policy, GSA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration (GSA) is announcing that 
significant changes to the Federal Travel Regulation will be discussed 
at a national travel conference to be held in Orlando, Florida, October 
30 through November 1, 2000. Additionally, Travel Managers of the Year 
Awards will be presented by Government Executive magazine. Federal 
agencies may hold agency-wide meetings at this conference.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jane Groat, Office of Governmentwide 
Policy, Travel Management Policy Division, at (202) 501-4318, or by e-
mail to [email protected] or at www.nationaltravel2000.com.

    Dated: June 30, 2000.
William T. Rivers,
Acting Director, Travel Management Policy Division.
