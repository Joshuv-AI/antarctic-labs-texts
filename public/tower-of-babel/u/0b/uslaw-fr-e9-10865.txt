DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,794]


Leggett and Platt, Inc., Whittier, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 14, 2009, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Leggett and Platt, Whittier, 
California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.


[[Page 21826]]


    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of April 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
