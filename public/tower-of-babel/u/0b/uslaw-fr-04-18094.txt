SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster P043; Amdt. 1]


State of South Dakota

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective July 
28, 2004, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to include 
Yankton County in the State of South Dakota as a disaster area due to 
damages caused by severe storms and flooding occurring on May 28, 2004, 
and continuing.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is September 20, 2004.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59008).

    Dated: August 3, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
