DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP02-114-001]


Tennessee Gas Pipeline Company; Notice of Technical Conference

May 17, 2002.
    Take notice that a technical conference will be convened to address 
issues raised by the filing in the above reference proceeding. The 
technical conference will be held on Monday June 24, 2002 at 1 p.m. in 
a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    All interested parties and staff are permitted to attend.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
