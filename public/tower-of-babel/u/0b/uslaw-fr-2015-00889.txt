Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records

AGENCY: Defense Finance and Accounting Office, DoD.

ACTION: Notice to amend a System of Records.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Defense Finance and Accounting Service proposes to amend a 
system of records, T1025, entitled ``Mentoring Program'' in its 
inventory of record systems subject to the Privacy Act of 1974, as 
amended. This system provides DFAS civilian employees with an automated 
mentoring system which will match mentees with potential mentors based 
on mentee need and mentor capabilities and experience. The system will 
facilitate the tracking and management of the DFAS mentoring 
relationship.

DATES: Comments will be accepted on or before February 20, 2015. This 
proposed action will be effective on the date following the end of the 
comment period unless comments are received which result in a contrary 
determination.
