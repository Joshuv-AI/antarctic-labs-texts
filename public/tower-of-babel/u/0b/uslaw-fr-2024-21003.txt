FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD


Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the appointment of the members of the 
Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board for the Federal 
Retirement Thrift Investment Board. The purpose of the Performance 
Review Board is to make written recommendations on each executive's 
annual summary ratings, performance-based pay adjustment, and 
performance awards to the appointing authority.

DATES: This notice is applicable on September 16, 2024.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kelly Powell, HR Specialist, at 202-
942-1681.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Title 5, U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), requires that 
the appointment of Performance Review Board members be published in the 
Federal Register before Board service commences. The following persons 
will serve on the Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board's 
Performance Review Board which will review initial

[[Page 75542]]

summary ratings to ensure the ratings are consistent with established 
performance requirements, reflect meaningful distinctions among senior 
executives based on their relative performance and organizational 
results and provide recommendations for ratings, awards, and pay 
adjustments in a fair and equitable manner: Thomas Brandt, Jim 
Courtney, Kim Weaver, and Trevor Williams.

Dharmesh Vashee,
General Counsel, Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.
