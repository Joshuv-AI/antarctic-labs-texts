Petitions for Exemption; Summary of Petitions Received;

ACTION: Notice of petitions for exemption received and of dispositions

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to a summary of certain 
petitions seeking relief from specified requirements of the Federal 
Aviation Regulations (14 CFR Chapter I) published in the Federal 
Register on January 14, 1999 (64 FR 2533).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Cheri Jack, (202) 267-7271.

Correction of Publication

    In petitions for exemption FR Doc. 99-857 on page 2533 in the 
Federal Register issue of January 14, 1999, make the following 
correction:
    1. On page 2533, in column 1, in the heading, on line 3 from the 
top, correct ``Summary Notice No. PE-99-28'' to read ``Summary Notice 
No. PE-99-01''.
