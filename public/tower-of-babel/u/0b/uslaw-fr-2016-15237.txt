AGENCY:  Department of Defense (DoD), General Services Administration

ACTION: Proposed rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: DoD, GSA, and NASA are issuing a correction to FAR Case 2016-
004; Acquisition Threshold for Special Emergency Procurement Authority, 
which was published in the Federal Register at 81 FR 39882, June 20, 
2016.

DATES: Effective: June 28, 2016.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Camara Francis, Procurement 
Analyst, at 202-550-0935 for clarification of content. For information 
pertaining to status or publication schedules, contact the Regulatory 
Secretariat Division at 202-501-4755. Please cite FAR Case 2016-004; 
Correction.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In rule FR Doc. 2016-14413, published in the Federal Register at 81 
FR 39882, June 20, 2016, make the following correction:
    On page 39883, in the third column, section 13.003, third line, 
remove ``$750,00'' and add ``$750,000'' in its place.

    Authority: 40 U.S.C. 121(c); 10 U.S.C. chapter 137; and 51 
U.S.C. 20113.
