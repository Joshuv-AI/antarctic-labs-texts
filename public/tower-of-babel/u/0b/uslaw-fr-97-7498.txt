OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request For Review of a 
Revised Information Collection: Forms RI 20-7 and RI 30-3

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Public 
Law 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management has submitted to the Office of Management and 
Budget a request for clearance of a revised information collection. RI 
20-7, Representative Payee Application, is used by CSRS and FERS to 
collect information from persons applying to be fiduciaries for 
annuitants or survivor annuitants who appear to be incapable of 
handling their own funds or for minor children. RI 30-3, Information 
Necessary for a Competency Determination, collects medical

[[Page 14172]]

information regarding the annuitant's competency for OPM's use in 
evaluating the annuitant's condition.
    Approximately 12,480 RI 20-7 forms will be completed annually. Each 
form requires approximately 30 minutes to complete. The annual burden 
is 6,240 hours. Approximately 250 RI 30-3 forms will be completed 
annually. Each form requires approximately 1 hour to complete. The 
total annual burden is 6,490 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact Jim Farron on (202) 418-3208, 
or E-mail to [email protected].
