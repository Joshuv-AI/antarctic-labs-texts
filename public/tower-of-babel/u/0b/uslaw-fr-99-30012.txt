DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Office of the Secretary


Members of Performance Review Boards

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, USDA.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the appointment of members of the 
Performance Review Boards (PRBs) for the U.S. Department of 
Agriculture, (USDA). The USDA PRBs provide fair and impartial review of 
Senior Executive Service (SES) performance appraisals and make 
recommendations to the Secretary of Agriculture, regarding final 
performance ratings, performance awards, pay adjustments, 
recertification and Presidential Rank Awards for SES members.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 17, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Barbara Holland, Office of Human 
Resources Management, Executive Resources and Services Division, U.S. 
Department of Agriculture, 1400 Independence Avenue, SW, Washington, DC 
20250, (202) 720-6047.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The publication of PRB membership is 
required by section 4314(c)(4) of Title 5, U.S.C. The following 
membership lists represents a standing register, from which specific 
PRBs will be constituted.
