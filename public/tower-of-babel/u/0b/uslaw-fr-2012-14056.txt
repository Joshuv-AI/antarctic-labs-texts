Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) DNA 
Samples

AGENCY: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), Department of 
Health and Human Services (DHHS).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 
will not be receiving DNA proposals in the near future. NHANES is 
changing its plan for making DNA available for genetic research and its 
proposal guidelines. NHANES will announce when it will reopen its 
repository for use of DNA specimens for research protocols once it has 
developed its new plan of operation.
