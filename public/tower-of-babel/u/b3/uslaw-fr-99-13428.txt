FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

May 20, 1999.

FCC To Hold Open Commission Meeting Thursday, May 27, 1999

    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below on Thursday, May 27, 1999, which is scheduled 
to commence at 9:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street, S.W., 
Washington, D.C.

[[Page 28187]]

                                                                 Further Notice of Proposed Rulemaking to

                                                                 Rural LECs (CC Docket No. 97-160).
