SUMMARY: Under the provisions of the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995

Witness.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

A. Purpose

    SF 94 is used by all Federal agencies to report accident 
information involving U.S. Government motor vehicles. The SF 94 is an 
essential part of the investigation of motor vehicle accidents, 
especially those involving the public with a potential for claims 
against the United States. It is a vital piece of information in 
lawsuits and provides the Assistant United States Attorneys with a 
written statement to refresh recollection of accidents, as necessary. 
The SF 94 is usually completed at the time of an accident involving a 
motor vehicle owned or leased by the Government. Individuals, other 
than the vehicle operator, who witness the accident, complete the 
form.Use of the SF 94 is prescribed in FMR 102-34.300(b) and Federal 
Property Management Regulations 101-39.40(b).
