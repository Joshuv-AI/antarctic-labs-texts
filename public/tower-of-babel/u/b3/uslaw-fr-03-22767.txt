SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the Environmental Protection 
Agency (EPA) has posted State and Tribal 8-hour Ozone Air Quality 
Designation Recommendations on the web as they have been received.

ADDRESSES: State and tribal recommendations are available for public 
inspection at EPA's Web site at: http://www.epa.gov/oar/oaqps/glo/designations/ and at the Office of Air and Radiation (OAR) Docket 
Center, Docket Number OAR 2003-0083, respectively.
