DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Alternative Medicine; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine Special Emphasis 
Panel, July 8, 2002, 2 pm to July 8, 2002, 5 pm, 6707 Democracy Blvd., 
Bethesda, MD, 20892 which was published in the Federal Register on June 
18, 2002, 67 FR 41435.
    The meeting will be held July 9, 2002 from 2 pm to 5 pm, 6707 
Democracy Blvd., Bethesda, Maryland (telephone conference call). The 
meeting is closed to the public.
