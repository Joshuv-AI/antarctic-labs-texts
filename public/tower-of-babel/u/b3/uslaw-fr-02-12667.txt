FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2553]


Petitions for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceedings

May 15, 2002.
    Petitions for Reconsideration have been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th 
Street, SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's 
copy contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. Oppositions 
to these petitions must be filed by June 5, 2002. See Section 1.4(b)(1) 
of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition 
must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has 
expired.
    Subject: Implementation of the pay telephone reclassification and 
compensation provision's of the Telecommunications Act of 1996 (CC 
Docket No. 96-128), Amendment of FM Table of Allotments (MM 
Docket No. 01-120, RM-10126).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 3.

Marlene H. Dortch,
Secretary.
