DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT

Notice of Proposed Information Collection for Public Comment; 
Consolidated Planning; Comment Due Date

AGENCY: Office of the Assistant Secretary for Community Planning and 
Development, HUD.

ACTION: Notice of proposed information collection for public comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 31, 1997 (62 FR 68296), HUD published a notice of 
proposed information collection for Consolidated Planning for Community 
and Development (CPD) programs, and solicited comments on the proposed 
information collection. The December 31, 1997 notice inadvertently 
omitted the comment due date. The purpose of this notice is to advise 
that the comment due date for the December 31, 1997 notice is March 2, 
1998.

ADDRESSES: Interested persons are invited to submit comments regarding 
this proposal. Comments should refer to the proposal by name and/or OMB 
Control Number and should be sent to: Reports Liaison Officer, Sheila 
E. Jones, Department of Housing and Urban Development, 451 7th Street, 
SW, Room 7230, Washington, DC 20410.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Sal Sclafani, Acting Director, Policy Division, 202-708-0614, ex. 4364.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On December 31, 1997 (62 FR 68296), HUD 
published a notice of proposed information collection for Consolidated 
Planning for Community and Development (CPD) programs, and solicited 
comments on the proposed information collection. The December 31, 1997 
notice inadvertently omitted the comment due date. The purpose of this 
notice is to advise that the comment due date for the December 31, 1997 
notice is March 2, 1998.
