Magnuson-Stevens Act Provisions; Fisheries Off West Coast States; 
Pacific Coast Groundfish Fishery; Pacific Fishery Management Plan; 
Amendment 28; Correction

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Final rule; correction

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NMFS published a final rule that implemented Amendment 28 to 
the Pacific Coast Groundfish Fishery Management Plan in the Federal 
Register on November 19, 2019 (84 FR 63966). The final rule changed 
boundaries of closed areas that affect commercial vessels fishing under 
the Pacific Coast Groundfish Fishery

[[Page 70905]]

Management Plan with bottom contacting gear in Federal waters off of 
Washington, Oregon, and California.

DATES: Effective January 1, 2020.

NMFS, 7600 Sand Point Way NE, Seattle, WA 98115, phone: 206-526-6147,
