Modine Aftermarket Holdings, Inc. Merced, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 5, 2001, in response to a worker petition filed 
on behalf of workers at Modine Aftermarket Holdings, Inc., Merced, 
California.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect until August 27, 2001 (NAFTA-3324). Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
