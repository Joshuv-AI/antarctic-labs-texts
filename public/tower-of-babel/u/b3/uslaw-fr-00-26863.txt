National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Advisory 
Committee to the Director, National Cancer Institute, November 6, 2000, 
9:30 a.m. to November 6, 2000, 11 p.m., National Institutes of Health, 
9000 Rockville Pike, Building 31, Room 11A10, Bethesda, MD 20892 which 
was published in the Federal Register on October 5, 2000, 65FR59453.

open to the public.

LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
