FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-1895 MM Docket No. 99-318, RM-9745]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Panama City, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Waitt License Company of 
Florida, Inc., licensee of station WPGX(TV), substitutes DTV channel 9 
for DTV 29c at Panama City, Florida. See 64 FR 60149, November 4, 1999. 
DTV channel 9 can be allotted to Panama City in compliance with the 
principle community coverage requirements of Section 73.625(a) at 
reference coordinates (30-13-45 N. and 85-23-20 W.) with a power of 
130, HAAT of 264 meters and with a DTV service population of 312 
thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective September 24, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
