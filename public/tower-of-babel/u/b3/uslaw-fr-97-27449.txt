National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases

    Name of SEP: ZDK1-GRB5-J1 P.
    Date: December 2-4, 1997.
    Time: 7:30 PM.
    Place: Empire Radisson Hotel, 44 West 63rd Street, New York, New 
York 10023.
    Contact Person: Francisco O. Calvo, Ph.D., Chief Special 
Emphasis Panel, Review Branch, DEA, NIDDK, Natcher Building, Room 
6as-37E, National Institutes of Health, Bethesda, Maryland 20892-
6600, Phone: (301) 594-8897.

    This meeting will be closed in accordance with the provisions 
set forth in secs. 552b(c)(4) and 552b(c)(6), Title 5 U.S.C. 
Applications and/or proposals and the discussions could reveal 
confidential trade secrets or commercial property such as patentable
