FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 98-90; RM-9070]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Dayton, WA and Weston, OR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Dayton Broadcasting Company, 
substitutes Channel 270C2 for Channel 272A at Dayton, Washington, 
reallots Channel 270C2 from Dayton to Weston, Oregon, and modifies 
Station KZZM(FM)'s license accordingly. See 63 FR 34620, June 25, 1998. 
Channel 270C2 can be reallotted to Weston in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without the 
imposition of a site restriction at petitioner's requested transmitter 
site. The coordinates for Channel 270C2 at Weston are 45-47-12 North 
Latitude and 118-15-46 West Longitude. With this action, this 
proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 22, 1999.
