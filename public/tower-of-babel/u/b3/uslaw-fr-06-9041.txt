DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY


 Senior Executive Service; Departmental Offices; FY 2006 
Performance/Bonus Year Review Board

AGENCY: Treasury Department.

ACTION: Notice of membership of the Departmental Offices Performances 
Review Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Effective Date: Membership is effective on the date of this notice.
SUMMARY: Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), this notice announces the 
appointment of members of the Departmental Offices Performance Review 
Board. The purpose of this Board is to review and make recommendations 
concerning proposed Performance ratings, bonuses and other appropriate 
personnel actions for incumbents of SES positions. The Board shall 
consist of at least three members. In the case of an appraisal of a 
career appointee, more than half the members shall consist of career 
appointees. The names and titles of the Board members are attached.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Melissa Talavera, Supervisory Human 
Resources Specialist, Department of the Treasury, Office of Human 
Resources, 1500 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20220, 
Telephone: 202-622-1044.

Melissa Talavera,
Director, Office of Human Resources.
