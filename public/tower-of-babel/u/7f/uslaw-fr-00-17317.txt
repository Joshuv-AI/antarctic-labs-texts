Furniture Crafters, Grants Pass, Oregon; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 24, 2000, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on the same date on behalf of workers at Furniture Crafters 
Grants Pass, Oregon.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 9th day of June, 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
