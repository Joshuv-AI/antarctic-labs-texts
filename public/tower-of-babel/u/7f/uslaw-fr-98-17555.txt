FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 98-97; RM-9287]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Hudson, WY

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a petition filed by Windy 
Valley Broadcasting proposing the allotment of Channel 286C at Hudson, 
Wyoming, as the community's first local aural transmission service. 
Channel 286C can be allotted to Hudson in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements at city reference 
coordinates. The coordinates for Channel 286C at Hudson are North 
Latitude 42-54-24 and West Longitude 108-35-00.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before August 17, 1998, and reply 
comments on or before September 1, 1998.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner, his counsel, or consultant, as follows: A. Wray 
Fitch, III, Esq., Gammon & Grange, P.C., 8280 Greensboro Drive, McLean, 
Virginia 22102-3807 (Counsel for Petitioner).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon P. McDonald, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
