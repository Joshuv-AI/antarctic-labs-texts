    Note: The Nuclear Regulatory Commission is operating under a 
delegation of authority to Chairman Shirley Ann Jackson, because 
with three vacancies on the Commission, it is temporarily without a 
quorum. As a legal matter, therefore, the Sunshine Act does not 
apply; but in the interests of openness and public accountability, 
the Commission will conduct business as though the Sunshine Act were 
applicable.
