October 31, 2001.

3(A) of the Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 
U.S.C 552B:

DATE AND TIME: November 7, 2001, 10 a.m.

PLACE: Room 2C, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.

STATUS: Open.

notice.
