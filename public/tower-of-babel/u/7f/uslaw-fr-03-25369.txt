SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3545]


State of North Carolina (Amendment 1)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective 
September 26, 2003, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning on 
September 18, 2003 and continuing through September 26, 2003.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 17, 2003, and for 
economic injury the deadline is June 18, 2004.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)
