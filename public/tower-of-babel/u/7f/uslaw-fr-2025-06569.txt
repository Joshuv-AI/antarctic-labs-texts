COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the U.S. Virgin Islands Advisory 
Committee; Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; Cancellation of Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the U.S. Virgin Islands 
Advisory Committee. The meeting, scheduled for Wednesday, April 23, 
2025, at 12:00 p.m. ET, has been cancelled. The notice is in the 
Federal Register on Monday, April 7, 2025, in FR Document Number 2025-
05897 on pages 14956-14957.
