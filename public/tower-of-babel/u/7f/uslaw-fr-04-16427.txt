Charleston Hosiery, Inc., Fort Payne, AL; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 28, 2004, in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Charleston Hosiery, Inc., Ft. Payne, 
Alabama.
    The petition is invalid because two of the three workers have not 
been separated nor is there a threat of separation. Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 6th day of July, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
