FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Sunshine Act Meeting

Agency Holding the Meeting: Committee on Employee Benefits of the 
Federal Reserve System.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ The Committee on Employee Benefits considers matters 
relating to the Retirement, Thrift, Long-Term Disability Income, and 
Insurance Plans for employees of the Federal Reserve System.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Time and Date: 2:30 p.m., Wednesday, July 1, 1998.

Place: Marriner S. Eccles Federal Reserve Board Building, 20th and C 
Streets, NW., Washington, D.C. 20551.

Status: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:
    1. Proposals relating to Federal Reserve System benefits.
    2. Issues relating to potential litigation.
    3. Any items carried forward from a previously announced meeting.

Contact Person for More Information: Lynn S. Fox, Assistant to the 
Board; 202-452-3204.

Supplementary Information: You may contact the Board's Web site at 
http://www.bog.frb.fed.us for an electronic announcement of this 
meeting. (The Web site also includes procedural and other information 
about the meeting.)

    Dated: June 24, 1998.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
