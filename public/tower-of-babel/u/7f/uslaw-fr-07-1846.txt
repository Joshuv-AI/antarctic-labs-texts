DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Drug Abuse; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Drug Abuse Special Emphasis Panel, April 18, 2007, 9 a.m. 
to April 19, 2007, 5 p.m., Courtyard by Marriott Rockville, 2500 
Research Boulevard, Rockville, MD, 20850 which was published in the 
Federal Register on March 28, 2007, Volume 72, Number 59.
    The date of the meeting was changed to May 9-10, 2007. The meeting 
is closed to the public.

    Dated: April 5, 2007.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
