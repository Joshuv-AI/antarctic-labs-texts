FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License Revocations

    The Federal Maritime Commission hereby gives notice that the 
following freight forwarder licenses are revoked pursuant to section 19 
of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718) and the regulations 
of the Commission pertaining to the licensing of ocean freight 
forwarders, effective on the corresponding revocation dates shown 
below:
    License Number: 3176.
    Name: Immediate Transportation Company of New York, Inc.
    Address: 606 Merrick Road, Lynbrook, NY 11563.
    Date Revoked: August 23, 1995.
    Reason: Failed to maintain a valid surety bond.

    License Number: 3721.
    Name: Jotadece International Freight Forwarders, Inc.
    Address: 2706-2708 NW., 112th Ave., Miami, FL 33172.
    Date Revoked: September 30, 1995.
    Reason: Failed to maintain a valid surety bond.

    License Number: 3949.
    Name: Sterling Cargo International, Inc. dba Sterling 
International.
    Address: P.O. Box 1896, Grapevine, TX 76099.
    Date Revoked: October 2, 1995.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.

    License Number: 3606.
    Name: Professional Shipping Company, Inc.
    Address: 9105 NW., 27th Ave., Miami, FL 33147.
    Date Revoked: October 2, 1995.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Director, Bureau of Tariffs, Certification and Licensing.
