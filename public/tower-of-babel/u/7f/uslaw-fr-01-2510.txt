SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3317]


State of Texas; Declaration of Disaster (Amendment #1)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, dated January 15, 2001, the above-numbered 
Declaration is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this 
disaster as beginning on December 12, 2000 and continuing through 
January 15, 2001.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is March 9, 2001 and for 
economic injury the deadline is October 9, 2001.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: January 19, 2001.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
