CORPORATION FOR NATIONAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICE


Learn and Serve America--School and Community-Based Programs; 
Correction

    The application deadline published in the Federal Register on 
January 23, 1997, page 3500, second column, under DATES, ``All 
applications must be received by 3:30 p.m., Eastern Standard Time, 
March 12, 1997,'' is changed to, ``All applications must be received by 
3:30 p.m., Eastern Standard Time, March 14, 1997.''

    Dated: January 29, 1997.
Barry W. Stevens,
Acting General Counsel, Corporation for National and Community Service.
