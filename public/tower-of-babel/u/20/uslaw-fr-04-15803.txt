DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Notice of Proposed Information Collection Requests

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On July 7, 2004, the Department of Education published a 
notice in the Federal Register (Page 40881, Column 3) for the 
information collection, ``Paul Douglas Teacher Scholarship Program 
Performance Report.'' The title of this information collection has been 
corrected. The corrected title is ``Targeted Teacher Deferments 
(Teacher Shortage Areas)''. The Leader, Regulatory Information 
Management Group, Office of the Chief Information Officer, hereby 
issues a correction notice as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act 
of 1995.

    Dated: July 8, 2004.
Angela C. Arrington,
Regulatory Information Management Group, Office of the Chief 
Information Officer.
