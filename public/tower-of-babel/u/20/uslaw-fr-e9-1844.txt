DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Drug Abuse; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Drug Abuse Special Emphasis Panel, January 27, 2009, 9 
a.m. to January 27, 2009, 1 p.m., Courtyard by Marriott Rockville, 2500 
Research Boulevard, Rockville, MD 20850 which was published in the 
Federal Register on December 12, 2008, Vol. 73, No. 240.
    The location of the meeting was changed to Rockville Hilton Hotel, 
1750 Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: January 22, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
