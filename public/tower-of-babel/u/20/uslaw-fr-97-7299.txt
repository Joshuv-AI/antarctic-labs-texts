USG Pipeline Company; Notice of Site Visit

March 18, 1997.
    On March 26-27, 1997, beginning at 12:00 p.m., the Office of 
Pipeline Regulation (OPR) staff will conduct a site visit with USG 
Pipeline Company of the proposed USG Pipeline Project in Marion County, 
Tennessee, and Jackson County, Alabama.
    All parties may attend. Those planning to attend must provide their 
own transportation.
    For further information, please contact Paul McKee at (202) 208-
1088.
Warren C. Edmunds,
Acting Director, Office of Pipeline Regulation.
