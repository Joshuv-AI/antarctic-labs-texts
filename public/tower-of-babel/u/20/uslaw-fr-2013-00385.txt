DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Mental Health; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Mental Health Council, January 24, 2013, 08:30 a.m. to January 
24, 2013, 05:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Neuroscience 
Center, 6001 Executive Boulevard, Conference Room C/D/E, Rockville, MD 
20852, which was published in the Federal Register on December 13, 
2012, 77 FR 74198.
    The time for each session has changed. The open session will still 
start at 08:30 a.m. but will now recess at 01:00 p.m. The closed 
session will now start at 02:00 p.m. and will still end at 05:00 p.m. 
The meeting is partially Closed to the public.

    Dated: January 7, 2013.
Carolyn A. Baum,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
