DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Ocean Energy Management


Notice on Outer Continental Shelf Oil and Gas Lease Sales

AGENCY: Bureau of Ocean Energy Management, Interior.

ACTION: List of Restricted Joint Bidders.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM) 
regulatory restrictions on joint bidding, the Director of BOEM is 
publishing a List of Restricted Joint Bidders. Each entity within one 
of the following groups is restricted from bidding with any entity in 
any of the other following groups at Outer Continental Shelf oil and 
gas lease sales held during the bidding period of May 1, 2021, through 
October 31, 2021.

DATES: This List of Restricted Joint Bidders covers the bidding period 
of May 1, 2021, through October 31, 2021, and succeeds the prior list 
published on September 24, 2020 (85 FR 60266), which covered the period 
of November 1, 2020, through April 30, 2021.
