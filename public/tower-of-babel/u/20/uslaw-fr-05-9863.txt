Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice if hereby given of a change in the meeting of the 
Gastrointestinal Cell and Molecular Biology Study Section, June 6, 
2005, 8 a.m. to June 7, 2005, 5 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One

public.
