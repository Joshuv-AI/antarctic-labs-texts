47 CFR Part 73

[DA 03-629; MB Docket No. 02-120; RM-10442]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Owen, Wisconsin

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Starboard Broadcasting, 
Inc., allots Channel 242C3 at Owen, Wisconsin, as the community's first 
local FM service. Channel 242C3 can be allotted to Owen, Wisconsin, in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements with a site restriction of 12.9 km (8.0 miles) northeast 
of Owen. The coordinates for Channel 242C3 at Owen, Wisconsin, are 45-
03-08 North Latitude and 90-29-21 West Longitude. A filing window for 
Channel 242C3 at Owen, WI, will not be opened at this time. Instead, 
the issue of opening this allotment for auction will be addressed by 
the Commission in a subsequent Order.

DATES: Effective April 28, 2003.

418-2180.
