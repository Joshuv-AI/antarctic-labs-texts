Subtitle C, Regulations Relating to Education

CFR Correction

    In Title 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 680 to End, 
revised as of July 1, 2011, on page 395, the heading ``Subtitle C--
Regulations Relating to Education'' is added above chapter XI--National 
Institute for Literacy. In title 34, chapters XI and XII are designated 
under subtitle C.
