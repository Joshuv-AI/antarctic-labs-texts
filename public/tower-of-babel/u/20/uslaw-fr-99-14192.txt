DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

[NM-050-1430-00]


Notice of Reopening of Public Land, Socorro County, NM

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management (BLM), Interior.

ACTION: Reopening order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the temporary emergency closure of 
public land in Socorro County, New Mexico, which was announced in the 
Federal Register on Thursday, May 20, 1999, Vol. 64, No. 97, page 
27600, is no longer in effect. The lands described in the temporary 
closure notice are reopened to public use and access.

DATES: The reopening of the subject public land is effective as of May 
26, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kate Padilla, Socorro Field Manager, 
or Jon Hertz, Assistant Field Manager, 198 Neel Avenue, NW, Socorro, NM 
87801, telephone (505) 835-0412.

    Dated: May 26, 1999.
Kate Padilla,
Field Manager.
