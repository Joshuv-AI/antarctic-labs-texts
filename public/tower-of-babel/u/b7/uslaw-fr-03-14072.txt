Federal Aviation Administration


Weight and Balance Control Program Committee; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of request for participation; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes corrections to the notice of request of 
participation published in the Federal Register on May 28, 2003 (68 FR 
31740), which announces the formation of the Weight and Balance Control 
Program Aviation Rulemaking Committee to conduct a review of AC 120-27C 
and other related guidance, and provide advice and recommendations.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Darcy Reed, 202-267-9948, or e-
mail: [email protected].

Correction

    In the notice FR Doc. 03-13243, published on May 28, 2003 (68 FR 
31740), make the following correction:
    On page 31741, in the first column, fourth full paragraph, line 
two, correct ``docket number FAA-2003-XXXX'' to read ``docket number 
FAA-2003-15281.''

    Issued in Washington, DC on May 30, 2003.
Carol E. Giles,
