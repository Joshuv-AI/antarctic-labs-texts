DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Nuclear Energy Advisory Committee; Meeting

AGENCY: Office of Nuclear Energy, Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of cancellation of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 26, 2017, the Department of Energy (DOE) published a 
notice of open meeting, scheduled for June 20, 2017, and request for 
comment, of the Nuclear Energy Advisory Committee (NEAC). This notice 
announces the cancellation of this meeting. The rescheduled meeting 
will be held at a date to be determined (August or September).

DATES: The meeting scheduled for June 20, 2017, announced in the May 
26, 2017, issue of the Federal Register (FR Doc. 2017-10877, 82 FR 
101), is cancelled.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rob Rova, Designated Federal Officer, 
U.S. Department of Energy, 1000 Independence Avenue SW., Washington, DC 
20585; email: [email protected] or phone: 301-903-9096.

    Issued at Washington, DC, on June 14, 2017.
