Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America; Notice of Rescheduling 
of Conference

January 31, 2000.
    Take notice that due to closure of the Federal Government because 
of inclement weather, the conference in the above-captioned proceeding 
originally scheduled for Tuesday, January 25, 2000, has been 
rescheduled for Thursday, February 10, 2000, beginning at 10:00 a.m., 
in Hearing Room No. 1, at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
