SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Providence Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Providence District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Friday, January 5, 1996 at 8:00 
a.m. at the Providence Marriott, Charles at Orms Streets, Providence, 
Rhode Island to discuss matters as may be presented by members, staff 
of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call the office of the District 
Director, Providence District Office, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, 380 Westminster Street, Providence, Rhode Island 02903, 
(401) 528-4580.

    Dated: December 8, 1995.

Art DeCoursey,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
