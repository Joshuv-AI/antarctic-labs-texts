NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Business and Operations Advisory Committee; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 
as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the following 
meeting:

    Name: Business and Operations Advisory Committee (9556).
    Date/Time: October 29, 2001 8:30 am-5:15 pm (EDT) and October 
30, 2001 8:30 am-2 pm (EDT).
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Room 
110, Arlington, VA.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Mary Ann Birchett, National Science Foundation, 
4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA 22230 (703) 292-8200.
    Purpose of Meeting: To provide advice concerning issues related 
to the oversight, development, and enhancement of NSF's business 
operations.

     Update of Recent Activities
     Follow-up of Discussion Items from Spring Meeting

     Other Business
