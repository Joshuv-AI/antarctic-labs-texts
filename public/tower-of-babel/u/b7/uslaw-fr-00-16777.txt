FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA-1412, MM Docket No. 99-291; RM-9665]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Reno, NV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Sarkes Tarzian, Inc., 
licensee of Station KTVN(TV), Reno, Nevada, substitutes DTV Channel 13 
for DTV Channel 32 at Reno, Nevada. See 64FR 52486, September 29, 1999. 
DTV Channel 13 can be allotted to Reno at coordinates (39-18-45 N. and 
119-53-00 W.) with a power of 12, HAAT of 906 meters and with a DTV 
service population of 481 thousand. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated.

DATES: Effective August 14, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
