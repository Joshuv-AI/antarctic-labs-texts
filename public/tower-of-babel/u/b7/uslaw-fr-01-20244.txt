DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 6032-041]


Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation and Fourth Branch Associates; 
Notice of Site Visit

August 7, 2001.
    On Wednesday, August 22, 2001 the Office of Energy Projects staff 
will conduct a site visit of the Mechanicville Project licensed to the 
Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation and Fourth Branch Associates. The 
purpose of the site visit is to review the project features identified 
by commentors and intervenors in the implied surrender proceeding 
currently before the Commission. The inspection will begin at 2 PM at 
the Mechanicville Project located on New York State Route 32 in the 
Town of Half Moon, New York.
    All interested parties may attend the inspection. For further 
information, please contact the Commission's Office of External Affairs 
at (202) 208-0004.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
