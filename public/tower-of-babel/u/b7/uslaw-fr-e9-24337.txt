DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 3-2007]


Foreign-Trade Zone 43 Battle Creek, Michigan, Termination of 
Review of Sourcing Change, Subzone 43D, Perrigo Company (Ibuprofen 
Products), Allegan, Michigan

    Notice is hereby given of termination of a sourcing change review 
relating to the over-the-counter (OTC) ibuprofen operations at Subzone 
43D at the Perrigo Company (Perrigo) OTC pharmaceutical manufacturing 
facilities in Allegan, Michigan (72 FR 10642, 3/9/07). The termination 
is based on an analysis of the record and resulting determination that 
no Board action is warranted.

    Dated: October 1, 2009.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
