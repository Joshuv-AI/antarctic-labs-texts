DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention


Clinical Laboratory Improvement Advisory Committee: Notice of 
Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Clinical Laboratory Improvement 
Advisory Committee, of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention 
(CDC), of the Department of Health and Human Services, has been renewed 
for a two-year period beginning February 19, 1998, through February 19, 
2000.
    For further information, contact Edward L. Baker, M.D., Executive 
Secretary, Clinical Laboratory Improvement Advisory Committee, CDC, 
1600 Clifton Road, NE, M/S G-25, Atlanta, GA 30333, telephone 770/488-
2402 or fax 770/488-2420.

    Dated: February 19, 1998.
Carolyn J. Russell,
Director, Management Analysis and Services Office, Centers for Disease 
Control and Prevention (CDC).
