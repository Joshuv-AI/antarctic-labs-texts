DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee (PAC)

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Klamath Provincial Advisory Committee will meet on 
February 5 and February 6, 1995 at the Hoopa Valley Tribe Neighborhood 
Facility Gymnasium, Highway 96, Hoopa, California. The meeting will 
begin at 10 a.m. on February 5 and adjourn at 5 p.m. The meeting will 
reconvene at 8 a.m. on February 6 and continue until 4 p.m. Agenda 
items to be covered include: (1) Forest health/stewardship contract 
update; (2) third party monitoring; (3) Province-wide forest health 
strategy; (4) short-term salvage priorities; (5) standing committee 
reports; and (6) public comment periods. All PAC meetings are open to 
the public. Interested citizens are encouraged to attend.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
