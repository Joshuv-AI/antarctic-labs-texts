                Presidential Determination No. 94-32 of July 12, 1994

 

Presidential Determination on RFE/RL Relocation

                Memorandum for the Secretary of State

                Pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 
                308(k) of the United States International Broadcasting 
                Act of 1994 (Public Law 103-236) (the ``Act''), I 
                hereby certify that significant national interest 
                requires a relocation of the operations of RFE/RL, 
                Incorporated from Munich, Germany, to Prague, Czech 
                Republic, before the confirmation of a new Board of 
                Broadcasting Governors can be completed.

                You are authorized and directed to transmit this 
                determination to the appropriate committees of the 
                Congress (as defined in section 314 of the Act) and to 
                arrange for its publication in the Federal Register.

                    (Presidential Sig.)>


                THE WHITE HOUSE,

                    Washington, July 12, 1994.
