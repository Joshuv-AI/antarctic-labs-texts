FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 94-80, RM-8445]

 

Television Broadcasting Services, Menominee and Iron Mountain, 
MI; Wittenberg, WI; and Ely, MN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The document requests comments on a petition filed by Douglas 
A. Maszka, d/b/a Tri-Cities Television Company, requesting the 
substitution of Channel *25+ for vacant Channel *17+ at Iron Mountain, 
Michigan, allotment of channel 17- at Wittenberg, Wisconsin, and 
allotment of channel 31+ at Menominee, Michigan. Since alternate 
channels are available for Wittenberg and Menominee that do not require 
a substitution at Iron Mountain, we shall propose the allotment of 
Channel 25+ at Menominee, Michigan, with a site restriction 17.8 
kilometers north at coordinates 45-16-00 and 87-36-48 and the allotment 
of channel 46 at Wittenberg, Wisconsin, with a site restriction 30.7 
kilometers north at coordinates 45-05-53 and 89-07-31. In the 
alternative, we shall also propose, as requested by petitioner, the 
allotment of channel 31+ at Menominee, Michigan, at coordinates 45-16-
00 and 87-36-48, the substitution of Channel *25+ for vacant Channel 
*17+ at Iron Mountain, Michigan, at coordinates 45-49-12 and 88-04-31, 
and the allotment of Channel 17- to Wittenberg, Wisconsin, at 
coordinates 45-05-00 and 89-10-06. We shall also change the offset for 
vacant Channel *17 at Ely, Minnesota, from minus to plus at coordinates 
47-54-06 and 91-51-48 to accommodate Channel 17- at Wittenberg. Since 
Menominee, Wittenberg, Iron Mountain and Ely are located within 400 
kilometers (250 miles) of the U.S.-Canadian border, concurrence of the 
Canadian government will be requested for these allotments.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before September 6, 1994, and reply 
comments on or before September 21, 1994.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner, as follows: Douglas A. Maszka, d/b/a Tri-Cities 
Television Company, 600 Vroman Street, Green Bay, Wisconsin 54303.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 634-6530.
