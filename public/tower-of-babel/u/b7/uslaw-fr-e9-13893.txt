DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,345]


Quala-Die, Inc., St. Marys, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 23, 2009, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Quala-Die, Inc., St. Marys, 
Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 15th day of May 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
