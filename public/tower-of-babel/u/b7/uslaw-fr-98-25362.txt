Agency Information Collection Activities; Announcement of OMB 
Approval; Current Good Manufacturing Practice for Type A Medicated 
Articles

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing that a 
collection of information entitled ``Current Good Manufacturing 
Practice for Type A Medicated Articles'' has been approved by the 
Office of Management and Budget (OMB) under the Paperwork Reduction Act 
of 1995 (the PRA).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Denver Presley, Office of Information 
Resources Management (HFA-250), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 
Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-1472.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register of July 21, 1998 (63 
FR 39092), the agency announced that the proposed information 
collection had been submitted to OMB for review and clearance under 
section 3507 of the PRA (44 U.S.C. 3507). An agency may not conduct or 
sponsor, and a person is not required to respond to, a collection of 
information unless it displays a currently valid OMB control number. 
OMB has now approved the information collection and has assigned OMB 
control number 0910-0154. The approval expires on August 31, 2001.

    Dated: September 16, 1998.
 William K. Hubbard,
 Associate Commissioner for Policy Coordination.
