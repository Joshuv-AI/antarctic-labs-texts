DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases; 
Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the National 
Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, July 20, 2004, 8 a.m. to July 20, 2004, 5 p.m., 
Bethesda, Marriott Suites, 6711 Democracy Boulevard, Bethesda, MD, 
10817 which was published in the Federal Register on May 28, 2004, 69 
FR 30687.
    This meeting was cancelled due to the time and new location.

    Dated: June 9, 2004.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
