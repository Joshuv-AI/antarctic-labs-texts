DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

Advisory Committee on Structural Safety of Department of Veterans 
Affairs Facilities, Notice of Meeting

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), in accordance with Public 
Law 92-463, gives notice that meetings of the Advisory committee on 
Structural Safety of Department of Veterans Affairs Facilities will be 
held on:

Monday, December 9, 1996, at 8:30 a.m.-4:30 p.m.
Tuesday, December 10, 1996, at 10:00 a.m.-12:30 p.m.

    The location of the meetings will be 811 Vermont Avenue, NW; 
Washington, DC; in Room 438 on December 9, 1996, and Room 442 on 
December 10, 1996.
    The all day meeting of Monday, December 9, 1996, is primarily 
designed as a work session to go over the developments in the field of 
structural design, as a result of lessons learned from the damages 
caused by recent earthquakes around the world, and fire safety issues. 
The Tuesday, December 10, 1996, meeting is of a formal nature, where 
structural safety issues from natural disasters and fire will be voted 
upon.
    Both meetings will be open to the public. It will be necessary for 
those wishing to attend to contact Krisna K. Banga, Senior Structural 
Engineer, Standards Service, Facilities Quality Office, Office of 
Facilities Management, Department of Veterans Affairs Central Office 
(phone 202-565-9370) prior to December 2, 1996.

    Dated: November 18, 1996.

    By Direction of the Secretary:
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
