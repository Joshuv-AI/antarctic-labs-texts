Animal Drug User Fee Rates and Payment Procedures for Fiscal Year 
2010; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration is correcting a notice 
entitled that appeared in the Federal Register of August 3, 2009 (74 FR 
38429). The document announced the Fiscal Year 2010 fee rates for the 
Animal Drug User Fee Act. The document was published with a 
typographical error. This document corrects that error.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Miller, Office of Financial 
Management (HFA-100), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 Fishers Lane, 
Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-3917.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. E9-18459, appearing on page 38429 
in the Federal Register of Monday, August 3, 2009, the following 
correction is made:
    1. On page 38429, in the third column, in the first sentence of the 
last paragraph under Background, ``$209,400'' is corrected to read 
``$290,400''.
