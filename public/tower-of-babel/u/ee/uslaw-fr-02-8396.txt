Radio Broadcasting Services; West Hurley, Rosendale and 
Rhinebeck, NY, and North Canaan and Sharon, CT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, petition for reconsideration, denied.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Sacred Heart University, Inc. directed to the Report and Order in this 
proceeding. See 66 FR 39454, published July 31, 2001. Specifically, 
that action allotted Channel 273A* to Rhinebeck, New York, and reserved 
this channel for noncommercial educational use. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.

418-2177.
