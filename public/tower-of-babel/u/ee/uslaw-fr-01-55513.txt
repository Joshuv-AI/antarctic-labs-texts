FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73


Radio Broadcasting Services

CFR Correction

    In Title 47 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 70 to 79, 
revised as of October 1, 2000, in part 73, Sec. 73.202(b) is corrected 
in the Table of FM Allotments on page 111 under Texas by removing 
channel 233A and adding channel 223A at Wake Village; and on page 112, 
under Vermont by removing Middlebury, Channel 265A, and by adding 
Berlin, Channel 265C2 and Hardwick, Channel 290A.
