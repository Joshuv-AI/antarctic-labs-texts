Allen Edmonds Shoe Corporation, Lake Church Division, Beigton, 
WI; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 2, 2004 in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Allen Edmonds Shoe Corporation, Lake 
Church Division, Beigton, Wisconsin.
    The petition has been deemed invalid. One of the three petitioning 
workers was separated from the subject firm more than one year before 
the date of the petition. Section 223(b) of the Act specifies that no 
certification may apply to any worker whose last separation occurred 
more than one year before the date of the petition. Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 15th day of March 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
