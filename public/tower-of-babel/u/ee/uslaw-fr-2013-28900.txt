DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Application of Ultimate JETCHARTERS, LLC for Commuter Air Carrier 
Authority

AGENCY: Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice of Order to Show Cause (Order 2013-11-20).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is directing all interested 
persons to show cause why it should not issue an order finding Ultimate 
JETCHARTERS, LLC, fit, willing, and able, and awarding it commuter air 
carrier authority to conduct scheduled commuter service.

DATES: Persons wishing to file objections should do so no later than 
December 18, 2013.

ADDRESSES: Objections and answers to objections should be filed in 
Docket DOT-OST-2012-0108 and addressed to Docket Operations, (M-30, 
Room W12-140), U.S. Department of Transportation, 1200 New Jersey 
Avenue SE., Washington, DC 20590, and should be served upon the parties 
listed in Attachment A to the order.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lauralyn Remo, Air Carrier Fitness 
Division (X-56), U.S. Department of Transportation, 1200 New Jersey 
Avenue SE., Washington, DC 20590, (202) 366-9721.

Susan L. Kurland,
Assistant Secretary for Aviation and International Affairs.
