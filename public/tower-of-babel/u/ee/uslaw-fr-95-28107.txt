POSTAL SERVICE

39 CFR Parts 224, 261, 262, 263, 264, 265, 266, 267 and 268


Release of Information and Records Management

AGENCY: Postal Service.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This rule revises organizational names and titles contained in 
Postal Service regulations relating to the policies for the release of 
information and records management. These names and titles changed as a 
result of agency restructuring. The revisions reflect to whom the 
public should address issues relating to the release of information and 
records management. This action will bring organizational mentions in 
agency regulations up to date with the current structure of the Postal 
Service.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 15, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Betty Sheriff, Records, (202) 268-
2608.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: As a result of an agency restructuring, 
Postal Service regulations governing the release of information and 
records management contain outdated organizational names and titles. 
This 

[[Page 57344]]
rule will revise those names and titles to show the current 
organization and official responsible for release of information and 
records management functions.
