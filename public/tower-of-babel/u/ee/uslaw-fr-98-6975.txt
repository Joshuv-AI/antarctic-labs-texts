DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Minerals Management Service


Outer Continental Shelf, Alaska Region, Beaufort Sea Lease Sale 
170

AGENCY: Minerals Management Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Availability of the final environmental impact 
statement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Minerals Management Service (MMS) has prepared a final 
Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) relating to the proposed 1998 
Outer Continental Shelf oil and gas lease sale in the Beaufort Sea. The 
proposed Beaufort Sea Sale 170 will offer for lease approximately 1.08 
million acres. You

[[Page 13269]]

may obtain single copies of the final EIS from the Regional Director, 
Minerals Management Service, Alaska Region, 949 East 36th Avenue, 
Anchorage, Alaska 99503-4302, Attention: Public Information. You may 
request copies by telephone at (907) 271-6070; 1-800-764-2627; or via 
e-mail at [email protected].
    Copies of the final EIS are also available for inspection in the 
following public libraries:

Alaska
