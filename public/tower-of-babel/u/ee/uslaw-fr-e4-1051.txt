OWT Industries, Inc., Power Tool Operations, Pickens, SC; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on April 8, 2004 in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at OWT Industries, Inc., Power 
Tool Operations, Pickens, South Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of April 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
