Petition For Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2010-0134.
    Petitioner: Al's Aerial Spraying, LLC.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR 137.51(b)(4)(i).
    Description of Relief Sought: Petitioner seeks modifications to the 
existing conditions and limitations in Exemption No. 10142, which 
allows petitioner to use a single-engine turbopropeller-powered Air 
Tractor aircraft to make turnarounds over congested areas in a loaded 
configuration. The petitioner is requesting conditions and limitations 
be changed as follows: (1) The altitude over congested areas where a 
turnaround maneuver begins be reduced from 200 feet to 100 feet above 
ground level, and (2) the emergency landing area glide distance be 
increased from no more than 1 nautical mile to no more than 1.4 
nautical miles.
