DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. EG94-15-000]

 

2322117 Nova Scotia Limited; Errata to Determination of Exempt 
Wholesale Generator Status

February 8, 1994.

    On January 25, 1994, a notice entitled ``Determination of Exempt 
Wholesale Generator Status'' was issued in the above-referenced docket. 
That notice should have been entitled ``Notice of Application For 
Determination of Exempt Wholesale Generator Status.'' (59 FR 4912, 
February 2, 1994).

    In addition, the date for comments is being extended from February 
14, 1994 to February 21, 1994.

Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
