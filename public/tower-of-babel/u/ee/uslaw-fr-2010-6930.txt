Gallery Leather Company, Inc., Trenton, ME; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated in response to a petition filed on November 
20, 2009, by a company official on behalf of workers of Gallery Leather 
Company, Inc., Trenton, Maine.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Accordingly, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 10th day of March, 2010.
Del Min Amy Chen,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
