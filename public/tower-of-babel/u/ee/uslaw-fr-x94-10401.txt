Tuesday, March 8, 1994, make the following corrections:

    1. On page 10870, in the second column, inSec. 254.5(a), in the 
second line, remove the word ``is''.


Sec. 254.7  [Corrected]

    2. On page 10870, in the third column, in Sec. 254.7(a)(2), in the 
ninth line from the bottom, ``parties'' should read ``parcels''.


Sec. 254.14  [Corrected]

    3. On page 10873, in the second column, in Sec. 254.14(b), in the 
fifth line, the period at the end should be a colon; and in (b)(1), the 
colon at the end should be a semi-colon.


Sec. 254.17  [Corrected]

    4. On page 10874, in the third column, in the fourth line, 
``Sec. 254.4'' should read ``Sec. 254.14''.
