FIFRA Scientific Advisory Panel; Open Meeting; Change of Agenda

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the Federal Register of January 25, 1995, EPA announced a 
1-day meeting of the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide 
Act (FIFRA) Scientific Advisory Panel (SAP) Subpanel on Plant 
Pesticides to review a set of scientific issues being considered by the 
Agency in connection with Monsanto's application for registration of a 
transgenic plant pesticide. This notice announces an agenda 
modification to the meeting. The Agency's original agenda focussed on 
the plant pesticide containing the active ingredient Bacillus 
thuringiensis subsp. tenebrionis delta endotoxin protein as produced by 
the CryIIIA gene and its controlling sequences in potatoes. The 
discussion will now include risk issues associated with the production 
of Bacillus thuringiensis tenebrionis delta endotoxin in other plants.

DATES: The meeting will be held on Wednesday, March 1, 1995, from 8:30 
a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Conference Room (Fish Bowl), 1921 Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, 
VA 22202.
