Iroquois Gas Transmission System, L.P.; Notice of Annual Charge 
Adjustment Filing

September 11, 1996.
    Take notice that on September 6, 1996 Iroquois Gas Transmission 
System, L.P. (Iroquois) tendered for filing to become part of its FERC 
Gas Tariff, First Revised Volume No. 1, Thirteenth Revised Sheet No. 4. 
The proposed effective date of the tariff sheet is October 1, 1996.
    Iroquois states that, pursuant to Section 154.402 of the 
Commission's regulations and Section 12.2 of the General Terms and 
Conditions of its tariff, Iroquois is making its Annual Charge 
Adjustment (ACA) filing to reflect a decrease of $0.0003 per Dth (from 
$0.0023 to $0.0020 per Dth) in its ACA surcharge.
    Iroquois states that copies of the filing were served upon all 
jurisdictional customers and interested state regulatory commissions.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with 18 CFR 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed as 
provided in Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the

[[Page 48931]]

Commission and are available for public inspection in the Public 
Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
