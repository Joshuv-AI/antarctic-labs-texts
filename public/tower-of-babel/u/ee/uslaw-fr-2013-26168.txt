DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Clinical 
Neuroplasticity and Neurotransmitters Study Section, October 10, 2013, 
08:00 a.m. to October 11, 2013, 05:00 p.m., Hilton Long Beach and 
Executive Center, 701 West Ocean Boulevard, Long Beach, CA 90831, which 
was published in the Federal Register on September 11, 2013, 78 FR 
55753.
    The meeting will start on December 2, 2013, 8:00 a.m. and end on 
December 3, 2013, 5:00 p.m. The meeting will be held at the Melrose 
Hotel, 2430 Pennsylvania Avenue, Washington, DC 20037. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: October 29, 2013.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
