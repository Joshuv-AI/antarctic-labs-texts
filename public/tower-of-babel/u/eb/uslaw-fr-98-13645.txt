Air Force A-76 Initiatives Cost Comparisons and Direct 
Conversions (As of April 1998)

    Air Force is in the process of conducting the following A-76 
initiatives. Cost comparisons are public-private competitions. Direct 
conversions are functions that may result in a conversion to contract 
without public competition. These initiatives were announced and in-
progress as of April 1998, include the installation and state where the 
cost comparison is being performed, the total authorizations under 
study, public announcement date and anticipated solicitation date. The 
following initiatives are in various stages of completion.
