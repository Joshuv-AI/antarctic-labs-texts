DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice: 7452]


Determination Pursuant to Section 451 of the Foreign Assistance 
Act Relating to Assistance for Individuals To Support Near East 
Regional Democracy

    Pursuant to section 451 of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, as 
amended (the ``Act'') (22 U.S.C. section 2261) and section 1-100 of 
Executive Order 12163, as amended, I hereby authorize, notwithstanding 
any other provision of law, the use of $5,000,000 in FY 2010 Economic 
Support Funds in order to provide assistance for individuals in support 
of Near East Regional Democracy.
    This Determination shall be reported to the Congress promptly and 
published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: April 19, 2011.
Hillary Rodham Clinton,
Secretary of State.
