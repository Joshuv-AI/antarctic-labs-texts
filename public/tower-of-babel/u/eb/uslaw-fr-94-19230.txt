DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[WY-930-4210-06; WYW 130940]

 

Notice of Proposed Withdrawal and Opportunity for Public Meeting; 
Correction of Legal Description; Wyoming

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of correction of legal description.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects the legal description previously 
published in the Federal Register Notice of February 1, 1994 (59 FR 
4721) for a proposed withdrawal of land and minerals to protect 
important scenic, recreation, and cultural resource values in the Devil 
Canyon Area near Lovell, Wyoming. The description is corrected as 
follows: On page 4721, column 3, the last line, which reads ``Sec. 14, 
NE\1/4\SE\1/4\, N\1/2\NE\1/4\, NW\1/4\;'' is hereby corrected to read 
``Sec. 14, N\1/2\, SE\1/4\;''. On page 4722, column 1, line 1, which 
reads ``Sec. 15, N\1/2\, SE\1/4\.'', is hereby corrected to read ``Sec. 
15, N\1/2\NE\1/4\, SE\1/4\NE\1/4\.''

    Dated: July 28, 1994.
Avis D. Rostron,
Acting Associate State Director.
