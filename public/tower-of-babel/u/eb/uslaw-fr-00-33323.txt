    On December 15, 2000, the Commission issued an order \1\ in Docket 
Nos. EL00-95-000, EL00-95-002, and EL00-95-003, et al., requiring, 
among other things, a technical conference on the development of market 
monitoring procedures for the markets involving the California 
Independent System Operator. The technical conference required by the 
December 15, 2000 order will convene at 9:30 a.m. on January 23, 2001, 
at 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC, in the Commission meeting 
room, Room 2C. If necessary, the conference will continue through 5:30 
p.m. of the same day. All parties of record and other interested 
parties are welcome to attend.
