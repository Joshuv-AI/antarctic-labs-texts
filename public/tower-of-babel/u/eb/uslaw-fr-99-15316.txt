Pegasus Gold Corporation d.b.a. Florida Canyon Mining, 
Incorporated, Nevada Operations, Spokane, WA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 250 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 18, 1999, in response to a petition filed on the 
same date on behalf of workers at Pegasus Gold Corporation, Spokane, 
Washington. The workers produce gold dore.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 24th day of May 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
