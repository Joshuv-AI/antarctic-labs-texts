FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Notice of a Matter to Be Added To the Agenda for Consideration at 
an Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that the following matter 
will be added to the ``discussion agenda'' for consideration at the 
open meeting of the Board of Directors of the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation scheduled to be held at 10:00 a.m. on Tuesday, March 11, 
1997, in the Board Room on the sixth floor of the FDIC Building located 
at 550--17th Street, N.W., Washington, D.C.:

Memorandum and resolution re: Proposed Rule Regarding Part 369--
Prohibition Against Using Interstate Branches Primarily for Deposit 
Production

    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Robert E. Feldman, Deputy Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-6757.

    Dated: March 10, 1997.

    Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
Deputy Executive Secretary.
