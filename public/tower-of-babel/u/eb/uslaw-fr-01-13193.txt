DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Biomedical 
Library Review Committee, June 14, 2001, 8:30 am to June 15, 2001, 5 
pm, National Library of Medicine, 8600 Rockville Pike, Board Room, 
Bethesda, MD, 20894 which was published in the Federal Register on 
April 12, 2001, 66 FR 18962.
    On June 15, 2001 the meeting will be open to the public from 8:30 
to 9 a.m. and closed from 9 a.m. to adjournment. The meeting is 
partially closed to the public.

    Dated: May 18, 2001.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
