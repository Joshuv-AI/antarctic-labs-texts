Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2013-0680.
    Petitioner: Jamco America Inc.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected:
    25.785(d), 25.807(c)(1), 25.807(c)(5), 25.809(f)(1), 25.813(b), 
25.857(e), and 25.1447(c)(1).
    Description of Relief Sought:
    The petitioner requests relief to allow interior modifications to a 
Dubai Air Wing Boeing Model 747-412F (Freighter), serial number 28032, 
to increase the number of persons carried on the upper deck from 8 to a 
maximum of 19. Fifteen will be qualified as supernumeraries occupying 
the main deck with access to the main deck cargo compartment in flight 
and during taxi, takeoff, and landing. The petitioner proposes to use 
the exit on the main deck of the airplane for emergency evacuation.
