SUMMARY: This is an amendment of the Administrative disaster 
declaration for the State of COLORADO dated 09/16/2015.

[[Page 74189]]

    Incident: Landslides.
    Incident Period: 04/24/2015 and continuing through 11/16/2015.
    Effective Date: 11/19/2015.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: 11/16/2015.
    Economic Injury (EIDL) Loan Application Deadline Date: 06/16/2016.

ADDRESSES: Submit completed loan applications to: U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Processing and Disbursement Center, 14925 Kingsport 
Road, Fort Worth, TX 76155.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: A. Escobar, Office of Disaster 
Assistance, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street SW., 
Suite 6050, Washington, DC 20416.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of the Administrative disaster 
declaration for the State of Colorado, dated 09/16/2015 is hereby 
amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning 
04/24/2015 and continuing through 11/16/2015.
    All other information in the original declaration remains 
unchanged.
