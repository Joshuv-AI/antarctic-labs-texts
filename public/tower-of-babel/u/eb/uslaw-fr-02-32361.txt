DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, 
December 10, 2002, 3 p.m. to December 10, 2002, 4 p.m., which was 
published in the Federal Register on November 1, 2002, 67 FR 66650.
    The telephone conference call meeting will be held in January 14, 
2003 at 1 p.m., instead of December 10, 2002, as previously advertised. 
The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: December 17, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
