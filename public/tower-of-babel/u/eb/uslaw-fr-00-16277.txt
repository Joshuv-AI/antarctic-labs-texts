GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Women's Progress Commemoration Commission

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Meeting Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the Women's Progress Commemoration 
Commission will hold an open meeting from 8 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. on 
Wednesday, July 12, 2000, at the Holiday Inn Waterloo/Seneca Falls, 
2468 NY State Route 414, Waterloo, NY.
    Purpose: The Commission will meet to hear testimony from interested 
parties and discuss methods to commemorate sites of historic 
significance relating to women in American history.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Martha Davis (202) 501-0705, Assistant 
to the Associate Administrator for Communications, General Services 
Administration. Also, inquiries may be sent to [email protected].

    Dated: June 20, 2000.
Beth Newburger,
Associate Administrator for Communications.
