DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Availability of Government-Owned Inventions; Available 
for Licensing

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The invention listed below is assigned to the U.S. Government 
as represented by the Secretary of the Navy and is available for 
licensing by the Department of the Navy. U.S. Patent No. 6,157,875: 
Image Guided Weapon System and Method.

DATES: Applications for an exclusive, partially exclusive, or 
nonexclusive license may be submitted at any time from the date of this 
notice.

ADDRESSES: Requests for copies of the patent cited should be directed 
to: Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division, Code 498400D, 1900 N. 
Knox Road Stop 6312, China Lake, CA 93555-6106, and must include the 
patent number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael D. Seltzer, Ph.D., Head, 
Technology Transfer Office, Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division, 
Code 498400D, 1900 N. Knox Road Stop 6312, China Lake, CA 93555-6106, 
telephone 760-939-1074, or E-Mail at [email protected].
