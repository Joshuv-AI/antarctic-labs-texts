SUMMARY: This document corrects errors that appeared in MSHA's preamble 
and proposed rule for Verification of Underground Coal Mine Operators' 
Dust Control Plans and Compliance Sampling for Respirable Dust.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Marvin W. Nichols, Jr., Director, 
Office of Standards, Regulations, and Variances, MSHA, 202-693-9440.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On March 6, 2003, (68 FR 10784), MSHA 
published a proposed rule in the Federal Register that would require 
mine operators to verify through sampling the effectiveness of the dust 
control parameters for each mechanized mining unit (MMU) specified in 
the mine ventilation plan. For samples to be valid, the operator would 
be required to sample on a production shift during which the amount of 
material produced by a MMU is at or above the verification production 
level using only the dust control parameters listed in the ventilation 
plan.
