Radio Broadcasting Services; Copeland, KS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Greater Plains Christian 
Radio, Inc., allots Channel *280C1 to Copeland, Kansas, as a reserved 
channel for noncommercial use, to provide the community with an 
additional FM service. See 60 FR 32935, June 26, 1995. Channel *280C1 
can be allotted to Copeland, Kansas, in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements without the 
imposition of a site restriction. The coordinates for Channel *280C1 at 
Copeland are 37-32-31 and 100-37-45. With this action, this proceeding 
is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 4, 1996. The window period for filing 
applications will open on March 4, 1996, and close on March 19, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
