FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Agency Forms Under Review: Correction

AGENCY: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In notice document 95-18314 beginning on page 38339 in the 
issue of Wednesday, July 26, 1995, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 38340 in the first column, the comment period previously 
was stated to expire on August 21, 1995. DATES: should be changed to 
read ``Comments must be submitted on or before August 25, 1995.''
    2. On page 38341 in the third column, the annual reporting hours 
for the third report of those proposed to be extended without revision, 
OMB Docket Number 7100-0042, Applications for the Issuance and 
Cancellation of Federal Reserve Stock-National Bank, Nonmember Bank, 
Member Bank, were incorrectly stated. Annual reporting hours: should be 
changed to read ``940 (FR 2030: 43; FR 2030a: 28; FR 2056: 797; FR 
2086a: 26; FR 2086b: 24; FR 2087: 22).''

Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, July 31, 1995.
William W. Wiles,
Secretary of the Board.
