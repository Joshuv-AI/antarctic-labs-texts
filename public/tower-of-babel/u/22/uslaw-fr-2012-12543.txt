FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Open Commission Meeting; Thursday, May 24, 
2012

Date: May 17, 2012.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
the subjects listed below Thursday, May 24, 2012, which is scheduled to 
commence at 10:30 a.m. in Room TW-C305, at 445 12th Street SW., 
Washington, DC. Then Commissions is waving the sunshine period 
prohibition contained in section 1.1203 of the Commission's rule, 47 
CFR 1.1203, until 5:00 p.m. on Friday, May 18, 2012. Thus presentation 
with respect to the items listed below will be permitted until that 
time.
