FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2647]


Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

February 23, 2004.
    Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's Rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and 
published pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of this 
document is available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th 
Street, SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's 
copy contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. Oppositions to 
these petitions must be filed by March 16, 2004. See Section 1.4(b)(1) 
of the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition 
must be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions have 
expired.
    Subject: In the Matter of the Review of Part 15 and other Parts of 
Commission's Rules (ET Docket No. 01-278, RM-9375, RM-10051)
    Amendment of Parts 2 and 15 of the Commission's Rules to Deregulate 
the Equipment Authorization Requirements for Digital Devices (ET Docket 
No. 95-19).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 2.

Marlene H. Dortch,
Secretary.
