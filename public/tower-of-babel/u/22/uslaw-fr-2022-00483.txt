DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Aging Special Emphasis Panel, February 14, 2022, 1:00 p.m. 
to 3:30 p.m., National Institute on Aging, Gateway Building, 7201 
Wisconsin Avenue, Suite 2W200, Bethesda, MD 20892, which was published 
in the Federal Register on December 16, 2021, FR Doc 2021-27280, 86 FR 
71512.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date of the meeting 
from February 14, 2022 to February 24, 2022. The meeting is closed to 
the public.

    Dated: January 7, 2022.
Miguelina Perez,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
