INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION
[Investigation No. 337-TA-315]

 

Certain Plastic Encapsulated Integrated Circuits; Designation of 
Additional Commission Investigative Attorney; Enforcement Proceeding

    Notice is hereby given that, as of this date, Thomas L. Jarvis, 
Esq. and Mary Jane Boswell, Esq. of the Office of Unfair Import 
Investigations are designated as the Commission investigative attorneys 
in the above-cited investigation instead of Thomas L. Jarvis, Esq.
    The Secretary is requested to publish this Notice in the Federal 
Register.

    Dated: April 29, 1994.
Lynn I. Levine,
Director, Office of Unfair Import Investigations.
