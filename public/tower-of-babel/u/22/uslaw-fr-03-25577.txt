Airworthiness Directives; Pratt & Whitney PW4000 Series Turbofan 
Engines; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration, DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document makes a correction to Airworthiness Directive 
(AD) 2003-19-15, applicable to Pratt & Whitney PW4000 series turbofan 
engines. AD 2003-19-15 was published in the Federal Register on 
September 30, 2003 (68 FR 56143). In the amendatory language, under 
Sec.  39.13 [Amended], the amendment number of the new action was 
inadvertently omitted. This document corrects that omission. In all 
other respects, the original document remains the same.

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 9, 2003.

7133; fax (781) 238-7199.
