ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

40 CFR Part 761


Polychlorinated Biphenyls (PCBs), Manufacturing, Processing, 
Distribution in Commerce, and Use Prohibitions

CFR Correction

    In Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 700 to 789, 
revised as of July 1, 1999, page 537, part 761, Sec. 761.30 is 
corrected by reinstating paragraph (j)(4) to read as follows:


Sec. 761.30  Authorizations.

* * * * *
    (j) * * *
    (4) No person may manufacture, process, or distribute in commerce 
PCBs for research and development unless they have been granted an 
exemption to do so under TSCA section 6(e)(3)(B).
