Millennium Pipeline Co., L.P. and Columbia Gas Transmission 
Corp.; Notice of Meeting Attendance

September 11, 1998.
    The environmental staff will attend a U.S. Fish and Wildlife 
Service (FWS) meeting about the Millennium Project with other federal 
and state resource agencies on September 23, 1998. The meeting will be 
in Cortland, New York, at the FWS Office. A memorandum summarizing 
issues identified during the meeting will be filed in the public record 
for these dockets.
    Information about the meeting may be obtained from: Diane Mann-
Klager (FWS) at (607) 753-9334.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
