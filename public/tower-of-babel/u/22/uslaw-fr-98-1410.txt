SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region VIII Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Regional VIII Advisory 
Council located in the geographical area of Salt Lake City, Utah, will 
hold a public meeting at 9:00 a.m. on Wednesday, February 18, 1998, at 
the Salt Lake Area Chamber of Commerce, Media Room, at 175 East 400 
South, Suite 600, Salt Lake City, to discuss such matters as may be 
presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, 
or others present.

    For further information, write or call Stan Nakano, District 
Director, U.S. Small Business, 125 South State Street, Salt Lake 
City, Utah 84138, (801) 524-5804.

Eugene Carlson,
Associate Administrator, Office of Communication & Public Liaision.
