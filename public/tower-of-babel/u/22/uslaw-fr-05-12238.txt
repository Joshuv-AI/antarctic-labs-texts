Coast Guard

Special Local Regulations for Marine Events; Piankatank River, 
Gloucester County, Virginia

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DHS.

ACTION: Notice of meeting; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard published a document in the Federal Register 
on May 27, 2005, concerning a public meeting to provide a forum for 
citizens to provide oral comments relating to the ``2005 Piankatank 
River Race'', a marine event proposed to be held over the waters of the 
Piankatank River in Gloucester County, Virginia on July 23, 2005. The 
document contained the incorrect zip code in the ADDRESSES paragraph.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dennis Sens, Recreational Boating 
Safety Specialist, Fifth Coast Guard District, telephone 757-398-6204, 
Fax 757-398-6203.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of May 27, 2005, in FR Doc. Vol 70, Number 
102, on page 30656, in the second column, correct the zip code within 
the ADDRESSES paragraph from ``23321'' to read ``23704''.

    Dated: June 14, 2005.
Kevin B. Smith,
Captain, U.S. Coast Guard, Commander, Fifth Coast Guard District, Chief 
of Operations.
