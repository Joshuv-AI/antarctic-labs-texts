SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In accordance with the Government in

assisted real-time transcription (CART) will be provided. The web link 
to access CART (in English) on Friday, February 21, 2025, is https://www.streamtext.net/player?event=USCCR. Please note that CART is text-
only translation that occurs in real time during the meeting and is not 
an exact transcript.
