Radio Broadcasting Services; Brandon, SD, Emmetsburg, Sanborn and 
Sibley, IA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; denial of petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Saga Communications of Iowa, LLC directed to the Report and Order in 
this proceeding which substituted Channel 261C3 for Channel 261A at 
Emmetsburg, Iowa, and modified the license of Station KDWD to specify 
operation on Channel 261C3. In doing so, the Report and Order also 
denied a competing proposal by Saga Communications of Iowa, LLC to 
upgrade a vacant Channel 261A allotment at Brandon, South Dakota. See 
67 FR 64048, October 17, 2002. With this action, the proceeding is 
terminated.
