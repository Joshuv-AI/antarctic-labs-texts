NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on Reactor Safeguards; Revised

    The agenda for the 498th meeting of the Advisory Committee on 
Reactor Safeguards scheduled to be held on December 5-7, 2002, in 
Conference Room T-2B3, 11545 Rockville Pike, Rockville, Maryland, has 
been revised to Close the following session on Thursday, December 5, 
2002.
    1:30 P.M.--2:15 P.M.: Meeting with Mr. Lawrence Williams, Her 
Majesty's Chief Inspector, Nuclear Installations Inspectorate (NII), 
United Kingdom (U.K.) (Closed)--The Committee will hold discussions 
with Mr. Williams, NII, U.K., regarding several items of mutual 
interest, including pre-decisional plans to expand the nuclear program 
in U.K. [Note: This session will be closed to protect information 
provided in confidence by a foreign source pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 
552b(c)(4).]
    The agenda for December 6 and 7, 2002, remains the same as 
previously published in the Federal Register on Wednesday, November 20, 
2002 (67 FR 70094).
    For further information, contact: Dr. Sher Bahadur, Associate 
Director for Technical Support, ACRS, (Telephone: 301-415-0138), 
between 7:30 a.m. and 4:15 p.m., EST.

    Dated: November 21, 2002.
Andrew L. Bates,
Advisory Committee Management Officer.
