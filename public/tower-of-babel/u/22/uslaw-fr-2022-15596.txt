Gas Pipeline Rate and Refund Report filings:

Filings Instituting Proceedings

[[Page 43511]]

    Description: Sec.  4(d) Rate Filing: Negotiated Rate--Amended Duke 
En FL 9000105 to be effective 9/1/2022.

proceedings must file in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the 
Commission's Regulations (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214) on or before 5:00 
p.m. Eastern time on the specified comment date. Protests may be

proceeding.

(https://elibrary.ferc.gov/idmws/search/fercgensearch.asp) by querying 
the docket number.

facilities filings can be found at: http://www.ferc.gov/docs-filing/efiling/filing-req.pdf. For other information, call (866) 208-3676 
(toll free). For TTY, call (202) 502-8659.
