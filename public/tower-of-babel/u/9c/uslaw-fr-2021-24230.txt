Adelphia Gateway, LLC; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal of 
Application

    On December 7, 2020, Adelphia Gateway, LLC (Adelphia) filed a prior 
notice application to construct and operate a 3,000-horsepower 
compressor unit at its Marcus Hook Compressor Station in Delaware 
County, Pennsylvania. On October 12, 2021, Adelphia filed a notice of 
withdrawal of its application. No motion in opposition to the notice of 
withdrawal has been filed, and the Commission has taken no action to 
disallow the withdrawal. Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the application 
became effective on October 28, 2021, and this proceeding is hereby 
terminated.
