Program Integrity: Gainful Employment; Correction

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Final regulations; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 31, 2014, we published in the Federal Register 
final regulations for Program Integrity: Gainful Employment (Gainful 
Employment rule). This document corrects regulatory text, footnotes, 
and a chart in the Gainful Employment rule.

DATES: Effective July 1, 2015.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This document corrects: (1) Footnote text 
that was omitted from the Gainful Employment rule; (2) Sec.  668.412 of 
the regulations to include the implementation date for the disclosure 
requirements; (3) the equations for calculating completion rates for 
full-time students in Sec.  668.413(b)(1)(i) of the regulations; (4) 
Sec.  668.413 to add mean earnings in addition to median earnings; and 
(5) the notification provisions in Sec.  668.413(c)(2) of the 
regulations.
    In the Gainful Employment rule:
     The text of certain footnotes was omitted;
     We discussed that institutions must begin complying with 
the requirements in Sec.  668.412 of the regulations beginning January 
1, 2017. However, that language was inadvertently omitted from the 
regulatory text;
     Dividing lines were omitted from the chart on page 64954 
that would enhance the data presentation;
     We revised Sec.  668.412(a)(11) of the proposed 
regulations to add mean earnings, in addition to median earnings, as a 
possible disclosure item to be included on the disclosure template, but 
we did not revise Sec.  668.413 of the regulations to reflect this 
addition; and
     Section 668.413(c)(2) referred incorrectly to the cohort 
period with respect to the calculation of median loan debt.
