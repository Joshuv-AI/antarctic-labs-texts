Purcell Services, Ltd., Prudhoe Bay, AK; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, and investigation 
was initiated on October 12, 1999 in response to a worker petition on 
behalf of workers at Purcell Services, Ltd., Prudhoe Bay, Alaska.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 10th day of November, 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
