NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION
 

Draft NUREG; Issuance, Availability

    The Nuclear Regulatory Commission has issued a draft report 
entitled ``Revised Analyses of Decommissioning for the Reference 
Boiling Water Reactor Power Station'' (NUREG/CR-6174). This draft 
report, prepared for the NRC by Battelle Pacific Northwest Laboratories 
(PNL), is available for review and comment.
    The draft report presents the results of a review and reevaluation 
of the original boiling water reactor (BWR) decommissioning study 
(NUREG/CR-0672), and subsequent addenda which addressed technology, 
safety and cost issues associated with decommissioning a large nuclear 
power plant. This reevaluation was performed to update the current cost 
estimates to decommission the reference BWR from NUREG/CR-0672, which 
was Washington Public Power Supply System's Washington Nuclear Plant 
Two (WNP-2).
    This report and the recently released draft NUREG/CR-5884, entitled 
``Revised Analyses of Decommissioning for the Reference Pressurized 
Water Reactor Power Station'' should be viewed as a first step in 
developing a more parametric approach to estimating decommissioning 
costs. The results of these studies, including input from the public, 
will be utilized by the NRC staff as part of its effort to determine if 
revisions of the decommissioning regulations are warranted.
    A separate draft report, NUREG/CR-6270, entitled ``Estimating 
Boiling Water Reactor Decommissioning Costs'' will be issued shortly 
for public comment and it will be noticed in the Federal Register when 
it becomes available. It is a user's manual for a computer program 
developed by PNL that was used to calculate the decommissioning cost 
estimates presented in NUREG/CR-6174.
