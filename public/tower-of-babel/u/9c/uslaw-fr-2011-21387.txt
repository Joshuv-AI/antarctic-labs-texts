SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Postponement of Preliminary Determination

    On April 27, 2011, the Department of Commerce (the Department) 
initiated the antidumping duty investigation on nails from the United 
Arab Emirates. See Certain Steel Nails From the United Arab Emirates: 
Initiation of Antidumping Duty Investigation, 76 FR 23559 (April 27, 
2011). The notice of initiation stated that the Department would issue 
its preliminary determination for this investigation no later than 140 
days after the issuance of the initiation in accordance with section 
733(b)(1)(A) of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (the Act), and 19 
CFR 351.205(b)(1) unless postponed.
    On August 8, 2011, Mid Continent Nail Corporation (the petitioner) 
made a timely request pursuant to section 733(c)(1) of the Act and 19 
CFR 351.205(b)(2) and (e) for postponement of the preliminary 
determination in this investigation. The petitioner requested a 50-day 
postponement of the preliminary determination in order to allow the 
Department additional time to resolve a number of complex issues in 
this investigation.
    The petitioner submitted a request for postponement of the 
preliminary determination more than 25 days before the scheduled date 
of the preliminary determination. See 19 CFR 351.205(e). Therefore, 
because the petitioner provided reasons for its request and the 
Department finds no compelling reasons to deny the request, the 
Department is postponing the deadline for the preliminary determination 
in accordance with section 733(c)(1)(A) of the Act and 19 CFR 
351.205(b)(2) and (e) by 50 days to October 27, 2011. The deadline for 
the final determination will continue to be 75 days after the date of 
the preliminary determination unless extended.
    This notice is issued and published pursuant to section 733(c)(2) 
of the Act and 19 CFR 351.205(f)(1).
