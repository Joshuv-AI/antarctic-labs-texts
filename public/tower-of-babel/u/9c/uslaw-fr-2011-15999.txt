DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, July 7, 2011, 8 a.m. to July 
8, 2011, 5 p.m., Bethesda North Marriott Hotel & Conference Center, 
5701 Marinelli Road, Bethesda, MD 20852 which was published in the 
Federal Register on May 16, 2011, 76FR28236.
    This notice is amending the dates and times of the meeting from 
July 7-8, 2011, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. to the following dates and times: July 
6th--7:30 to 10 p.m., July 7th--8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and July 8th--8 a.m. 
to 4 p.m. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 21, 2011.
Jennifer S. Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
