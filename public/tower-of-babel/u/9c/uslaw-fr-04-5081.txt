Harriet and Henderson Yarns, Inc., Fort Payne Distribution 
Center, Fort Payne, AL; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on February 4, 2004, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Harriet and Henderson Yarns, 
Inc., Fort Payne Distribution Center, Fort Payne, Alabama.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-53,293B, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 11th day of February, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
