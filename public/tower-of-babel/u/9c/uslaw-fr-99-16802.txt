Office of Science; High Energy Physics Advisory Panel

ACTION: Notice of meeting cancellation.

SUMMARY: An open meeting of the High Energy Physics Advisory Panel was 
scheduled to be held on July 12-13, 1999, at the Holiday Inn, 2 
Montgomery Village Avenue, in Gaithersburg, Maryland 20879, has been 
cancelled due to scheduling difficulties. This meeting was announced in 
the Federal Register on May 17, 1999, 64 FR 26740.
