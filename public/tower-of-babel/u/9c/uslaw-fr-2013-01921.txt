    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on January 30, 2013, members of its staff will attend a 
meeting hosted by California Independent System Operator regarding the 
interregional coordination requirements established by Order No. 1000. 
The agenda and other documents for the meeting will be available at 
https://www.columbiagrid.org/O1000Inter-documents.cfm.
    The meeting is open to all stakeholders and Commission staff's 
attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing outreach efforts. The 
meeting may discuss matters at issue in the above captioned dockets.
