DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project Nos. 14188-00o, 14200-000]


 Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XXVIII; FFP Project 59, LLC; Notice 
Announcing Filing Priority for Preliminary Permit Applications

    On February 22, 2012, the Commission held a drawing to

[[Page 12281]]

determine priority among competing preliminary permit applications with 
identical filing times. In the event that the Commission concludes that 
none of the applicants' plans are better adapted than the others to 
develop, conserve, and utilize in the public interest the water 
resources of the region at issue, the priority established by this 
drawing will serve as the tiebreaker. Based on the drawing, the order 
of priority is as follows:
    1. FFP Project 59, LLC, Project No. 14200-000.
    2. Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XXXVIII, Project No. 14188-000.

    Dated: February 22, 2012.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
