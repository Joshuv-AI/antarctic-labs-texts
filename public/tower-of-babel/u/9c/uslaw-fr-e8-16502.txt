DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


IndyMac Bank, F.S.B., Pasadena, CA; Notice of Appointment of 
Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision has duly appointed the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation as sole Receiver for IndyMac Bank, F.S.B., Pasadena, 
California (OTS No. 03970) and as Conservator for IndyMac Federal Bank, 
FSB, Pasadena, California (OTS No. 18115) on July 11, 2008.

    Dated: July 15, 2008.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
