Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and 
Children (WIC): 2020/2021 Income Eligibility Guidelines; Correction

AGENCY: Food and Nutrition Service (FNS), USDA.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Agriculture Food and Nutrition Service 
published a document in the Federal Register of May 26, 2020 concerning 
income eligibility guidelines for the Special Supplemental Nutrition 
Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC). The document contains a 
mislabeled column in one of the tables.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sara Olson, Chief, Policy Branch, 
Supplemental Food Programs Division, FNS, USDA, 1320 Braddock Place, 
Alexandria, Virginia 22314, (703) 605-4013.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of May 26, 2020, in FR Doc. 2020-11251, on 
page 31435, in the table titled ``INCOME ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES 
(effective from July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2021),'' in the first column 
``Household Size,'' of the middle row ``Alaska,'' correct the numbering 
in the column to read: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, each add'l fam mem add.

Pamilyn Miller,
Administrator, Food and Nutrition Service.
