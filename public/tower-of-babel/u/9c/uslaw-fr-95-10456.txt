FEDERAL MINE SAFETY AND HEALTH REVIEW COMMISSION


TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., Wednesday, April 26, 1995.

PLACE: Room 600, 1730 K Street, NW., Washington, DC.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The Commission will consider and act upon the 
following:

    1. Joy Technologies, Docket No. WEST 93-129. (Continuation of 
discussion of issues that include whether a vendor of mining 
equipment may be cited as an independent contractor-operator based 
on the actions of its service representative.)
    No earlier announcement of the meeting was possible. Any person 
attending the open portion of this meeting who requires special 
accessibility features and/or auxiliary aids, such as sign language 
interpreters, must inform the Commission in advance of those needs. 
Subject to 29 CFR 2706.150(a)(3) and 2706.160(e).
