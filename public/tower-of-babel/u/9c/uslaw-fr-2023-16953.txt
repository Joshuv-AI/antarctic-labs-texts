Draft NUREG: Revision to Subsequent License Renewal Guidance 
Documents, and Supplement to Associated Technical Bases Document; 
Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Draft report; request for comment; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register (FR) on July 11, 
2023, requesting public comment on three draft NUREGs that provide 
revised guidance for subsequent license renewal and the associated 
technical bases for the revised guidance documents. This action is 
necessary to correct tables within the Draft NUREG-2192, ``Standard 
Review Plan for Review of Subsequent License Renewal Applications for 
Nuclear Power Plants, Draft Report for Comment,'' Revision 1.

DATES: The correction takes effect on August 8, 2023.
