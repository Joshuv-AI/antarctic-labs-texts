SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

[File No. 500-1]


Ewan 1, INC. n/k/a AccessKey IP, Inc.; Order of Suspension of 
Trading

April 19, 2013.
    It appears to the Securities and Exchange Commission that there is 
a lack of current and accurate information concerning the securities of 
Ewan 1, Inc. n/k/a AccessKey IP, Inc. (``AccessKey'') because it has 
not filed a periodic report since it filed its registration pursuant to 
the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (``Exchange Act'') on August 21, 
2002.
    The Commission is of the opinion that the public interest and the 
protection of investors require a suspension of trading in the 
securities of AccessKey. Therefore, it is ordered, pursuant to Section 
12(k) of the Exchange Act, that trading in the securities of AccessKey 
is suspended for the period from 9:30 a.m. EDT on April 19, 2013, 
through 11:59 p.m. EDT on May 2, 2013.

    By the Commission.
Elizabeth M. Murphy,
Secretary.
