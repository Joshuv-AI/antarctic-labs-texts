DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Domestic Advisory Panel (DAP) on Early Intervention and Education 
for Infants, Toddlers, Preschool Children, and Children With 
Disabilities

AGENCY: Department of Defense Domestic Dependent Elementary and 
Secondary Schools (DDESS).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Public Law 92-463, as amended (5 U.S.C. app. II), 
notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Domestic Advisory Panel 
(DAP) on Early Intervention and Education for Infants, Toddlers, 
Preschool Children, and Children with Disabilities is scheduled to be 
held from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. on December 7-8, 1999. The meeting is open 
to the public and will be held in the Director's Conference Room on the 
9th floor, DoDEA Headquarters, 4040 North Fairfax Drive, Arlington, 
Virginia 22203. The purpose of the meeting is to: (1) Review the 
response to the panel's recommendations from the April 1999 meeting; 
(2) review and comment on data and information provided by the 
Department of Defense Domestic Dependent Elementary and Secondary 
Schools; and (3) establish subcommittee as necessary. Persons desiring 
to attend the meeting or desiring to make oral presentations or submit 
written

[[Page 57071]]

statements for consideration by the panel must contact Dr. David V. 
Burket at (703) 696-4354, extension 1455.

    Dated: October 18, 1999.
L.M. Bynum,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
