Integrated Licensing Process Outreach; Notice of Public Outreach 
Meetings on the Intergrated Licensing Process

September 30, 2003.
    Commission staff from the Office of Energy Projects will hold three 
public Outreach Meetings on the new Integrated Licensing Process at the 
locations and times listed below. The purpose of the Outreach program 
is to familiarize federal, state, and other government agencies, Indian 
tribes, nongovernmental organizations, licensees, and other interested 
parties with the new Integrated Licensing Process set forth by Order 
Number 2002, issued on July 23, 2003.

Location, Date and Time

Doubletree Seattle Airport, 187450 Pacific Highway South Seattle, WA 
98188, 206-246-8600.

8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.
Red Lion Hotel, 1401 Arden Way, Sacramento, CA 95815, 916-922-8041,
October 29, 2003
8:30 to 12:30 p.m.

Crown Plaza Hotel, State & Lodge Albany, NY 12207, 518-462-6611
November 5, 2003
8:30 to 12:30 p.m.
