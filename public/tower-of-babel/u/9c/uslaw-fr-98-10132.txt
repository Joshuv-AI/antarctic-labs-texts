DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Notice of Intent To Grant Exclusive License; MSE Technology 
Applications, Incorporated

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of Intent To Grant Exclusive License.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy hereby gives notice of its intent 
to grant to MSE Technology Applications, Incorporated a revocable, 
nonassignable, exclusive license in the United States to practice the 
Government owned inventions described in:
    U.S. Patent Number 5,025,849 entitled Centrifugal Casting of 
Composites.

DATES: Anyone wishing to object to the grant of this license must file 
written objections along with supporting evidence, if any, not later 
than June 15, 1998.

ADDRESSES: Written objections are to be filed with the Carderock 
Division, Naval Surface Warfare Center, Code 004, 9500 MacArthur 
Boulevard, West Bethesda MD 20817-5700.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Dick Bloomquist, Director 
Technology Transfer, Carderock Division, Naval Surface Warfare Center, 
Code 0117, 9500 MacArthur Boulevard, West Bethesda MD 20817-5700, 
telephone (301) 227-4299.

    Dated: April 6, 1998.
Lou Rae Langevin,
LT, JAGC, USN, Alternate Federal Register Liaison Officer.
