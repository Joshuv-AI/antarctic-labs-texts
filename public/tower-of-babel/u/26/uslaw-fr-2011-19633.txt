PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.; PJM Power Providers Group v. PJM 
Interconnection, L.L.C.; Notice Establishing Post-Technical Comment 
Period

    As indicated in the June 29, 2011 Notice in these dockets, comments 
on the technical conference that was held on July 28, 2011, to discuss 
issues related to PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (PJM)'s Minimum Offer 
Price Rule (MOPR) and resources designed as ``self supply,'' \1\ are 
due 21 days from the date of this conference, or Thursday, August 18, 
2011.
