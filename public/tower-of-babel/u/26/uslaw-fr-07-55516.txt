44 CFR Part 64


Communities Eligible for the Sale of Insurance

CFR Correction

    In Title 44 of the Code of Federal Regulations, revised as of 
October 1, 2006, on page 339, in Sec.  64.4, paragraph (b), in the 
fourth sentence, remove the words ``within the newly-month period,'' 
and add the words ``within the newly-acquired area the requirements of 
Sec.  60.3(b) of this subchapter. During the six month period,'' in 
their place.
