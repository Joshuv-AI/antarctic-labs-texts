Notice of Proposed Withdrawal, Transfer of Jurisdiction, and 
Notice of Public Meeting; California

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) published a notice in the 
Federal Register on December 31, 2003, (68 FR 75628) that contained an 
erroneous legal description. This correction also supersedes and 
replaces the correction made on November 17, 2010 (75 FR 70288).

Correction

    On page 75628 in the second column, line 12, the acreage is 
corrected to read 3,385 acres of public lands for use as a mountain 
warfare training facility.
    On page 75628 in the second column, lines 56 and 57, correct the 
legal description to read: sec. 24, lots 4, 5, 20, 22, 24, 26, and 
SW\1/4\SW\1/4\; and sec. 25, all;
    On page 75628 in the third column, line 2, the acreage is corrected 
to read 3,385 acres in San Diego County.
