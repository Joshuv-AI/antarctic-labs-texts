FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 2011-15233) published on 
pages 35893 and 35894 of the issue for Monday June 20, 2011.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of New York heading, the entry for 
Banco do Brasil S.A., Brasilia, Brazil, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of New York (Ivan Hurwitz, Vice President) 
33 Liberty Street, New York, New York 10045-0001:
    1. Banco do Brasil S.A., Brasilia, Brazil, and Caixa de Previdencia 
dos Funcionarios do Banco do Brasil, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil; to become 
bank holding companies by acquiring 51 percent of the voting shares of 
Eurobank, Boca Raton, Florida.
    Comments on this application must be received by July 15, 2011.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, June 20, 2011.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
