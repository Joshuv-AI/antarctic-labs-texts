Research, Development and Demonstration Funding; Notice of 
Extension of Comment Period

Issued June 19, 1997.
AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking; extension of comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On April 30, 1997, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
issued a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (62 FR 24853, May 7, 1997) 
proposing to amend its research, development and demonstration 
regulations to propose a new funding mechanism for the Gas Research 
Institute. The date for filing further comments in this docket is being 
extended at the request of various interested entities.

DATES: Comments shall be filed on or before August 29, 1997.

ADDRESSES: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:

Secretary.
