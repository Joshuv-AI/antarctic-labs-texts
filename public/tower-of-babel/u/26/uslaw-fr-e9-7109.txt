Air Products and Chemicals Inc., Electronics Division, Easton, 
PA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 23, 2009 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Air 
Products and Chemicals Inc., Electronics division, Easton, 
Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of March 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
