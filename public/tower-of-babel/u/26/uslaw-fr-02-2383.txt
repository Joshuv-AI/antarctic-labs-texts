DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Research Resources; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the General 
Clinical Research Centers Review Committee, February 12, 2002, 8 a.m. 
to February 14, 2002, 6 p.m., Holiday Inn--Chevy Chase, 5520 Wisconsin 
Avenue, Chevy Chase, MD 20815, which was published in the Federal 
Register on January 3, 2002, 67 FR 336-337.
    The meeting has been changed to Feb. 12-13, 2002; the location 
remains the same. The meeting is partially closed to the public.

    Dated: January 25, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
