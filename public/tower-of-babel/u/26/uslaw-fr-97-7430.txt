Intent To Prepare a Draft Environmental Impact Statement (DEIS)

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the previous Federal Register notice (Vol. 62, No. 26, 
pages 5803-5804) Friday, February 7, 1997, make the following 
corrections:

words ``Natural Resources'' pertaining to the Maryland Department, are 
changed to read ``the Environment.'' The site was erroneously listed as 
``Natural Resources.''
    On page 5803, column 3, paragraph 6, second and third line, the 
words ``Natural Resources'' pertaining to the Maryland Department, are 
changed to read ``the Environment.'' The site was erroneously listed as 
``Natural Resources.''
