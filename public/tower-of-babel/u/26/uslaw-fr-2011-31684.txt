Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[Docket 60, 61 and 62-2011]


Foreign-Trade Zones 140 and 78; Applications for Subzone 
Authority; Dow Corning Corporation, Hemlock Semiconductor Corporation 
and Hemlock Semiconductor, L.L.C.; Extension of Comment Periods

    The comment periods for the applications for subzone authority at 
the Dow Corning Corporation facility in Midland, Michigan (76 FR 63282-
63283, October 12, 2011), at the Hemlock Semiconductor Corporation 
facility in Hemlock, Michigan (76 FR 63282, October 12, 2011) and at 
the Hemlock Semiconductor, L.L.C. facility in Clarksville, Tennessee 
(76 FR 63281-63282, October 12, 2011) are being extended. A public 
hearing will be held on the applications, and the comment periods are 
being extended through the date of the public hearing. When the public 
hearing has been scheduled, a notice will be published with the date 
and time of the hearing as well as the specific date on which the 
comment periods will close following the hearing.
    For further information, contact Elizabeth Whiteman at 
[email protected] or (202) 482-0473.
