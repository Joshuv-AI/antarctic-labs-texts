DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Exclusive License Announcement

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 37 CFR 404.7(a)(1)(I), announcement is made 
of prospective exclusive license of U.S. Patent 5,609,290, ``Fluxless 
Soldering Method'', for the purpose of manufacturing, using, and 
selling the processes involved in this invention.
    This invention is described as a Fluxless Soldering Method. One of 
the seven inventors of this invention has assigned his rights to the 
United States of America as represented by the Secretary of the Army, 
Washington, DC. The other six inventors have assigned their rights to 
the University of North Carolina at Charlotte which has exclusively 
licensed all of its interest to Integrated Electronics Innovations, 
Inc.
    Under the authority of Section 11(a)(2) of the Federal Technology 
Transfer Act of 1986 (Public Law 99-502) and Section 207 of Title 35, 
United States Code, the Department of the Army, as represented by the 
Army Research Laboratory, intends to grant a limited term exclusive or 
partially exclusive license of the above named patent to Integrated 
Electronics Innovations, Inc., a small business which is interested in 
manufacturing, using, and/or selling the processes involved in this 
invention.
