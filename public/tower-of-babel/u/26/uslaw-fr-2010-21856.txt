DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


Imperial Savings and Loan Association, Martinsville, VA; Notice 
of Appointment of Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act,

[[Page 54004]]

the Office of Thrift Supervision has duly appointed the Federal Deposit 
Insurance Corporation as sole Receiver for Imperial Savings and Loan 
Association, Martinsville, Virginia (OTS No. 7270) on August 20, 2010.

    Dated: August 26, 2010.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
