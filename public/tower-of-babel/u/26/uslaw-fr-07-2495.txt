DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Advisory Board, June 14, 2007, 2 p.m. to June 15, 2007, 12 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, 
MD 20892, which was published in the Federal Register on May 2, 2007, 
72 FR 24319.
    The notice is being amended to change the open session start and 
end times on June 14, 2007 to 1:15 p.m.--4:20 p.m. and the end time on 
June 15, 2007 to 11:45 a.m. The meeting is partially Closed to the 
public.

    Dated: May 14, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
