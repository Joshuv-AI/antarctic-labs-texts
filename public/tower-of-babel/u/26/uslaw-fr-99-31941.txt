Kimberly-Clark Corporation, Munising Mill, Munising, Michigan; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (Pub. L. 193-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
section 250(a), Subchapter D, Chapter 2, Title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 U.S.C. 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
September 24, 1999, in response to a petition filed on behalf of 
workers at Kimberly-Clark Corporation, Munising Mill, Munising, 
Michigan.
    In a statement dated October 26, 1999, the company official 
submitting the petition requested that the petition for NAFTA-TAA be 
withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 22nd day of November 1999.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
