    Status: Closed.
    Matters To Be Considered: Pursuant to Sec.  102.139(a) of the 
Board's Rules and Regulations, the Board or a panel thereof will 
consider ``the issuance of a subpoena, the Board's participation in a 
civil action or proceeding or an arbitration, or the initiation, 
conduct, or disposition * * * of particular representation or unfair 
labor practice proceedings under section 8, 9, or 10 of the [National 
Labor Relations] Act, or any court proceedings collateral or ancillary 
thereto.'' See also 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(10).
