SUMMARY: The Federal Aviation Administration published a document in 
the Federal Register of January 9, 2017, concerning the Tenth RTCA SC-
229 406 MHz ELT Plenary Joint with WG-98. The document contained the 
incorrect location.

or 202-330-0654, or The RTCA Secretariat, 1150 18th Street NW., Suite 
910, Washington, DC 20036, or by telephone at (202) 833-9339, fax at 
(202) 833-9434, or Web site at http://www.rtca.org.

Correction

    In Federal Register, of January 9, 2017, in FR doc Vol. 82, No. 5, 
on page 2435 in Column 3 Correct the caption ADDRESS to read:

ADDRESSES:  The meeting will be held at: E.A.S.A., Konrad-Adenauer-Ufer 
3, D-50668 Cologne, Germany.
