OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request for Reclearance of a 
Revised Information Collection: RI 25-37

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of an information collection. RI 25-37, 
Evidence to Prove Dependency of a Child, is designed to collect 
sufficient information for OPM to determine whether the surviving child 
of a deceased federal employee is eligible to receive benefits as a 
dependent child.
    Approximately 250 forms are completed annually. We estimate it 
takes approximately 60 minutes to assemble the needed documentation. 
The annual estimated burden is 250 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, please contact Mary Beth Smith-Toomey 
at (202) 606-8358, FAX (202) 418-3251 or via E-mail at 
[email protected]. Please include a mailing address with your request.
