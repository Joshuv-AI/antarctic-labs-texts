SUMMARY: On Monday, October 4, 2004 (69 FR 59214) the Department of 
Defense announced closed meetings of the Defense Science Board (DSB) 
Task Force on December 9-10, 2004, at the Institute for Defense 
Analyses, 4850 Park Center Drive, Alexandria, VA. These meetings will 
now be held at Strategic Analysis Inc., 3601 Wilson Boulevard, Suite 
500, Arlington, VA.

    Dated: November 29, 2004.
