Agency Information Collection Activities; Announcement of OMB 
Approval; Protection of Human Subjects, Recordkeeping and Reporting 
Requirements for Institutional Review Boards

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing that a 
collection of information entitled ``Protection of Human Subjects, 
Recordkeeping and Reporting Requirements for Institutional Review 
Boards,'' has been approved by the Office of Management and Budget 
(OMB) under the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (the PRA).

[[Page 52418]]


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Karen L. Nelson, Office of Information 
Resources Management (HFA-250), Food and Drug Administration, 5600 
Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, 301-827-1482.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In the Federal Register of March 30, 2001 
(66 FR 17427), the agency announced that the proposed information 
collection had been submitted to OMB for review and clearance under 44 
U.S.C. 3507. An agency may not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not 
required to respond to, a collection of information unless it displays 
a currently valid OMB control number. OMB has now approved the 
information collection and has assigned OMB control number 0910-0130. 
The approval expires on September 30, 2004. A copy of the supporting 
statement for this information collection is available on the Internet 
at http://www.fda.gov/ohrms/dockets.

    Dated: October 5, 2001.
