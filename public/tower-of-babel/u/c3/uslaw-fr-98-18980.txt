Employment and Training Administration
[TA-W-34,596]


Koehler Manufacturing Company (Marlborough, MA); Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 1, 1998 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Koehler Manufacturing Company, 
Marlborough, Massachusetts.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently,

[[Page 38429]]

further investigation in this case would serve no purpose; and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 2nd day of July 1998.
Linda G. Poole,
Acting Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of 
Trade Adjustment Assistance.
