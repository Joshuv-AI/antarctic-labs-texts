ACTION: Recreation and Public Purpose Lease/conveyance correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 27, 2003, a Notice of Realty Action was published 
in Federal Register Volume 68, No. 207, page 61231 for BLM serial 
number N-75747. The legal description for this notice inadvertently 
listed the Range as R.60E. It should have been listed as R.59E. 
Therefore, the correct legal description is as follows:

Mount Diablo Meridian

T. 20S., R. 59E.,
    Sec. 12
    W\1/2\NW\1/4\NW\1/4\NW\1/4\, SW\1/4\NW\1/4\NW\1/4\, W\1/2\SE\1/
4\NW\1/4\NW\1/4\, W\1/2\NW\1/4\SW\1/4\NW\1/4\.
    Containing 25 acres, more or less.
    All of the other information in the NORA was correct.

    Dated: November 19, 2003.
