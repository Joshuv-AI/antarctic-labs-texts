SUMMARY: This document corrects an error that appeared in the notice 
published in the July 31, 2015, Federal Register entitled ``Findings of 
Research Misconduct.''

DATES: Effective Date: August 11, 2015. Applicability Date: The 
correction notice is applicable for the Findings of Research Misconduct 
notice published on July 31, 2015.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Karen Gorirossi or Dr. Kristen 
Grace at 240-453-8800.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

I. Background

    In FR Doc. 2015-18794 of July 31, 2015 (80 FR 45661-45662), there 
is an error in the grant information. The error is identified and 
corrected in the Correction of Errors section below.

II. Correction of Errors

    In FR Doc. 2015-18794 of July 31, 2015 (80 FR 45661-45662), make 
the following correction:
    1. On page 45661, third column, in FR Doc. 2015-18794, second 
paragraph, last line, delete ``and TA MH020002'' so that the last two 
lines of the paragraph read ``grants R01 MH087214 and R01 MH077105.''
