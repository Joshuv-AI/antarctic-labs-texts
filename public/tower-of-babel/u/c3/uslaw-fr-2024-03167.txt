NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice: (24-011)]


NASA Planetary Science Advisory Committee; Meeting

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act, the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) announces a 
meeting of the Planetary Science Advisory Committee. The meeting will 
be held for the purpose of soliciting, from the scientific community 
and other

[[Page 11879]]

persons, scientific and technical information relevant to program 
planning.

DATES: Monday, March 4, 2024, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; and Tuesday, 
March 5, 2024, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. All times are Eastern Time.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. KarShelia Kinard, Science Mission 
Directorate, NASA Headquarters, Washington, DC 20546, (202) 358-2355 or 
[email protected].
