North Pacific Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The North Pacific Fishery Management Council (Council) 
Observer Advisory Committee (OAC) subgroup on low sampling rates in 
partial coverage.

DATES: The meeting will be held Thursday, August 3, 2017, from 9 a.m. 
to 3 p.m. Alaska time.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held by teleconference only: (907) 271-
2896.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Diana Evans, Council staff; telephone: 
(907) 271-2809.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Agenda

    The agenda will be to discuss progress on the items on the OAC 
subgroup's workplan, posted here: https://www.npfmc.org/wp-content/PDFdocuments/conservation_issues/Observer/OACSubgroupWorkplan617.pdf.
    Meeting will be listening-only for those that are not on the OAC 
subgroup.

Special Accommodations

    The meeting is via teleconference. Request for auxiliary aids 
should be directed to Maria Shawback at (907) 271-2809 at least 7 
working days prior to the meeting date.

    Dated: July 17, 2017.
Tracey L. Thompson,
Acting Deputy Director, Office of Sustainable Fisheries, National 
Marine Fisheries Service.
