FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-104, RM-9048]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wellington, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a Petition for Reconsideration filed 
by Hunt Broadcasting, Inc. directed to the Report and Order in this 
proceeding. The Report and Order had determined that a counterproposal 
filed by Hunt Broadcasting proposing the modification of its Station 
KIKM license to specify operation on Channel 269C at Azle, Texas, was 
unacceptable. See 63 FR 11379, published March 9, 1995. With this 
action the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: February 5, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177.
