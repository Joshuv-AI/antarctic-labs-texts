FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-173; RM-9134]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Lexington, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.


[[Page 165]]


ACTION: Final rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action dismisses a petition for rule making filed by Lee 
County Broadcasters requesting the allotment of Channel 286A to 
Lexington, TX. See 62 FR 43302, August 13, 1997. Petitioner requested 
withdrawal of its petition for rule making. It is Commission policy to 
refrain from allotting a channel absent an expression of interest.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
