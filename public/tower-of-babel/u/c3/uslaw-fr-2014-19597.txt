DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, September 30, 2014, 08:00 a.m. 
to October 01, 2014, 05:00 p.m., Hilton Rockville Hotel, Rockville, MD, 
20852 which was published in the Federal Register on July 17, 2014, 79 
FR 41701.
    Meeting location has been changed to the Doubletree Bethesda, 8120 
Wisconsin Ave, Bethesda, MD 20814. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: August 13, 2014.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
