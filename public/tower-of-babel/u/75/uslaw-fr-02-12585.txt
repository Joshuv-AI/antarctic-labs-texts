Notice of Meetings

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Medford District Resource Advisory Committee will meet in 
Medford to tour project sites and to discuss proposed 2003 projects. 
Agenda topics include on-site inspections of 2002 projects and proposed 
2003 projects, review of last meeting minutes, presentations on 
proposed fiscal year 2003 Title II projects, and discussion regarding 
proposed projects.

DATES: July 11, 2002, August 8, 2002, August 22, 2002, and August 25, 
2002. The field trips on July 11, 2002 and August 25, 2002 will begin 
at 7 a.m. The meetings on August 8, 2002 and August 22, 2002 will begin 
at 10 a.m. A public comment period will be held from 2 p.m. to 2:30 
p.m. The field trips and meetings are expected to adjourn at 4 p.m.

ADDRESSES: The field trips will start from, and the meetings will be 
held at, the Medford District Office, located at 3040 Biddle Road, 
Medford, Oregon.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Karen Gillespie, Medford District 
Office (541-618-2424).

    Dated: April 15, 2002.
Ron Wenker,
District Manager.
