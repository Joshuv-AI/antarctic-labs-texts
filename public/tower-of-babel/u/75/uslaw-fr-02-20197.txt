Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-41,173]


Progress Lighting, Philadelphia, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 25, 2002, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Progress Lighting, Philadelphia, 
Pennsylvania.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-38,307A, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 25th day of July, 2002.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Director, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
