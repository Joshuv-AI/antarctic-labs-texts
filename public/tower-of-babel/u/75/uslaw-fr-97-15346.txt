DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP97-237-004]


TransColorado Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Compliance 
Filing

June 6, 1997.
    Take notice that on May 30, 1997, TransColorado Gas Transmission 
Company (TransColorado) tendered for filing and acceptance, pursuant to 
Subpart C of 154 of the Commission's Regulations Under the Natural Gas 
Act and in compliance with the Commission's letter order issued May 15, 
1997 at Docket No. RP97-237-002, the following tariff sheets to its 
FERC Gas Tariff.

Original Volume No. 1

First Revised Sheet No. 206
Original Sheet Nos. 409-422

    TransColorado states that the tariff sheets are being tendered to 
implement a pro forma Trading Partner Agreement for the electronic 
exchange of information pursuant to the Commission's directive. The 
tendered tariff sheets are proposed to become effective June 29, 1997.
    TransColorado states that copies of the filing were served upon all 
parties of record in this proceeding, all interstate pipeline system 
customers and affected state regulatory commissions, in accordance with 
the requirements of Section 385.2010 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed in 
accordance with Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection in the Public 
Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
