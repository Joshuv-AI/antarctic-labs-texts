Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-32-2019]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 84--Harris County, Texas; Authorization 
of Production Activity; Coreworks, LLC; (Brazed Aluminum Heat 
Exchangers and Cryogenic Equipment); Katy, Texas

    On April 30, 2019, Coreworks, LLC submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility within 
FTZ 84, in Katy, Texas.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (84 FR 20090, May 8, 2019). On August 
28, 2019, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including Sec.  
400.14.

    Dated: August 28, 2019.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
