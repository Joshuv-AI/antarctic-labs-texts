NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications and Amendments to 
Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Consideration

    On January 7, 2003, the Federal Register published the ``Biweekly 
Notice of Applications and Amendments to Operating Licenses Involving 
No Significant Hazards Considerations.'' On page 808, for GPU Nuclear 
Corporation and Saxton Nuclear Experimental Corporation, Table 1, last 
column total ``2.70e+05'' should read ``3.23e-04.''

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 15th day of January, 2003.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
John A. Zwolinski,
Director, Division of Licensing Project Management, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
