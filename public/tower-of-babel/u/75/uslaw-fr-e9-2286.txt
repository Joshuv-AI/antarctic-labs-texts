California Independent System, Operator Corporation; Notice of 
FERC Staff Attendance

January 28, 2009.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that on the following dates members of its staff will 
participate in teleconferences to be conducted by the California 
Independent System Operator (CAISO). The agenda and other documents for 
the teleconferences are available on the CAISO's Web site, http://www.caiso.com.

January 30, 2009--Teleconference on MRTU Pricing Review
February 2, 2009--Teleconference on MRTU Systems Testing
February 3, 2009--Teleconference on MRTU Parallel Operations
February 10, 2009--Teleconference on MRTU Parallel Operations

    Sponsored by the CAISO, the teleconferences are open to all market 
participants, and Commission staff's attendance is part of the 
Commission's ongoing outreach efforts. The teleconferences may discuss 
matters at issue in the above captioned dockets.
