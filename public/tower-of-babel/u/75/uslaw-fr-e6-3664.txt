Kern River Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Technical 
Conference

March 8, 2006.
    Take notice that the Commission will convene a technical conference 
on Tuesday, March 21, 2006, at 9:30 a.m. (EST), in a room to be 
designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street, NE., Washington DC 20426.
    The technical conference will deal with issues related to Kern 
River Gas Transmission Company's prior-period adjustments to its gas 
compressor fuel and lost and unaccounted for balances, as discussed in 
the February 15, 2006 order.\1\
