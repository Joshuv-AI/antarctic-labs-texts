Additional Information

    By a vote of 4-0 on February 12, 2015, the Commission determined 
pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and '9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
the above referenced Discussion of Internal Personnel Rules and 
Practices be held with less than one week notice to the public. The 
meeting is scheduled on February 18, 2015.
