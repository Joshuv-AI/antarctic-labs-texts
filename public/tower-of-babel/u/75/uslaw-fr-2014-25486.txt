Carbon Pollution Emission Guidelines for Existing Stationary 
Sources: Electric Utility Generating Units

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Proposed rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) published in the 
Federal Register on September 25, 2014, an extending the comment period 
for the proposed rule titled ``Carbon Pollution Emission Guidelines for 
Existing Stationary Sources: Electric Utility Generating Units'' by 45 
days. Inadvertently, that proposed rule included an incorrect digit in 
the docket number for the proposed rule. This proposed rule corrects 
that error.

DATES: This correction is effective on October 27, 2014.

docket number published on September 25, 2014.
    In FR Doc. 2014-22832, published on September 25, 2014 (79 FR
