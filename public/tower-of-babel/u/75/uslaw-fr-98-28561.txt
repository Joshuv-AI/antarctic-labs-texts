SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3135; Amendment #2]


State of Florida

    In accordance with information received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning on 
September 25, 1998 and continuing through October 7, 1998.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 27, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is June 28, 1999.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: October 16, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
