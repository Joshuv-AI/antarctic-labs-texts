Radio Broadcasting Services; Americus and Oglethorpe, GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 69 FR 54614 
(September 9, 2004), this Report and Order grants the proposal to allot 
Channel 295A to Americus, Georgia, and grants a request to dismiss a 
counterproposal to allot Channel 295 To Oglethorpe, Georgia. The 
coordinates for Channel 295A at Americus, Georgia are 32-04-51 North 
Latitude and 84-15-20 West Longitude, with a site restriction of 2.4 
kilometers (1.5 miles) northwest of Americus, Georgia.

DATES: Effective August 8, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
