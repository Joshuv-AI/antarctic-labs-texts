DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice #2579]


Bureau of Political-Military Affairs; Determination Under the 
Foreign Assistance Act of 1961

    Pursuant to Section 654(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, 
as amended (``the Act''), notice is hereby given that the Acting 
Secretary of State has made a determination pursuant to the Foreign 
Operations, Export Financing and Related Programs

[[Page 43767]]

Appropriations Act, 1994, the Foreign Operations, Export Financing and 
Relating Programs Appropriations Act, 1995, and the Foreign Operations, 
Export Financing and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 1996, and has 
concluded that publication of the determination would be harmful to the 
national security of the United States.

    Dated: July 31, 1997.
Thomas E. McNamara,
Assistant Secretary of State for Political-Military Affairs.
