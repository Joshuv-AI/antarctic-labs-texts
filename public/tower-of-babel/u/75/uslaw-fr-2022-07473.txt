COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Illinois Advisory Committee; 
Correction

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register on Tuesday, March 29, 2022, concerning a meeting of 
the Illinois Advisory Committee. The document contained the incorrect 
meeting time.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Liliana Schiller, [email protected], 
202-376-7756.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Correction

    In the Federal Register on Tuesday, March 29, 2022, in FR Document 
Number 2022-06530, on page 17981, second column, correct the meeting 
time in the SUMMARY to 1:00 p.m. CT.

    Dated: Tuesday, April 4, 2022.
David Mussatt,
Supervisory Chief, Regional Programs Unit.
