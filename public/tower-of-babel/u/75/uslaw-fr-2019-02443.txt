SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Technology description follows.

Adeno-Associated Viruses In Inner-Ear Therapeutics

    Available for licensing and commercial develop are intellectual 
property rights associated with adeno-associated viral vector (AAV) 
mediated inner-ear gene therapy can prevent and reverse hair cell 
damage to improve auditory function. Hearing loss is associated with 
age or trauma induced inner ear hair cell damage or hereditary genetic 
defects in the inner ear development. The delivery of functional copies 
of mutated or functionally impaired genes can restore hearing. An 
effective gene therapy requires a powerful delivery vehicle such as a 
viral vector with high infection efficiency to the inner ear cells. The 
inventors identified the recombinant AAV2.7m8 virus with modified 
capsid protein with a high viral vector efficiency for delivering 
genetic therapeutic payloads to multiple cell types of mammalian inner 
ear.

[[Page 4502]]

Potential Commercial Applications

    --Promising gene therapy vector for the treatment of hearing loss 
and dizziness.
    -- AAV2.7m8 can be used for human inner ear gene therapy for 
various diseases of the ear.
