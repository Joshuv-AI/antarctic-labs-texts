DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. RP94-78-002]

 

Algonquin Gas Transmission Co.; Proposed Changes in FERC Gas 
Tariff

February 18, 1994.
    Take notice that on February 15, 1994, Algonquin Gas Transmission 
Company (Algonquin) submitted a revised tariff sheet in compliance with 
ordering paragraph (D) of the Commission's January 6, 1994, order in 
Docket No. RP94-78-000.
    Algonquin tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, 
Fourth Revised Volume No. 1, Sub Alt Second Revised Sheet No. 97.
    Algonquin requests that the Commission accept the revised tariff 
sheet effective January 7, 1994.
    Algonquin states that the revised tariff sheet conforms to the 
changes proposed in Texas Eastern's January 14 filing in Docket No. 
RP94-66-000 to recover GSR costs (1) through a demand surcharge, 
instead of a direct bill, and (2) over a reasonable period.
    Algonquin states that copies of this filing were served upon each 
affected party and interested state commission and parties on the 
service list in Docket No. RP94-78-000.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol 
Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 
of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure. All such protests 
should be filed on or before February 28, 1994. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection in the public reference room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
