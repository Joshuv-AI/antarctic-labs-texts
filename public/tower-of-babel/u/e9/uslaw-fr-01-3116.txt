FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2461]


Applications for Review of Action In Rulemaking Proceeding

January 31, 2000.
    Applications for Review have been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR 1.115(a). The full text of these documents are 
available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th Street, 
SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to these applications 
must be filed by February 22, 2001. See section 97.15(b) of the 
Commission's rules (47 CFR 97.15(b)). Replies to an opposition must be 
filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions have 
expired.
    Subject: Modification and Clarification of Policies and Procedures 
Governing Siting and Maintenance of Amateur Radio Antennas and Support 
Structures, and Amendment of Section 97.15 of the Rules Governing the 
Amateur Radio Service (RM-8763).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 2.

Federal Communications Commission.

Secretary.
