FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 05-2942; MB Docket No. 05-188; RM-11240]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Bass River Township and Ocean City, 
NJ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 70 FR 31409 
(June 1, 2005), this Report and Order substitutes Channel 293A for 
Channel 292A, Station WKOE(FM), Ocean City, New Jersey; reallots 
Channel 293A, from Ocean City to Bass River Township; and modifies 
Station WKOE's license accordingly. The coordinates for Channel 293A at 
Bass River Township, New Jersey, are 39-39-00 NL and 74-21-20 WL, with 
a site restriction of 10.4 kilometers (6.4 miles) northeast of Bass 
River Township.

DATES: Effective December 27, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
