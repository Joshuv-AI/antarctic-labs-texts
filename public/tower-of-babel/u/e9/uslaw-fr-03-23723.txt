Joy Mining Machinery, Abingdon, Virginia; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 13, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
filed on behalf of a worker at Joy Mining Machinery, Abingdon, 
Virginia.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 22nd day of August, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
