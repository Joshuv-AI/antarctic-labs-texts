Miles Chemical Laboratories, Haledon, NJ; Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 28, 1994 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Miles Chemical Laboratories, 
Haledon, New Jersey.
    All workers were separated from the subject firm more than one year 
prior to the date of the petition. Section 223 of the Act specifies 
that no certification may apply to any worker whose last separation 
occurred more than one year before the date of the petition. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 18th day of January, 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
