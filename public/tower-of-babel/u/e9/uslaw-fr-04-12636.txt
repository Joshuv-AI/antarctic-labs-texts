Bacon Felt Company, Inc., Taunton, MA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an

[[Page 31642]]

investigation was initiated on April 19, 2004, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Bacon Felt 
Company, Inc., Taunton, Massachusetts.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 19th day of May, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
