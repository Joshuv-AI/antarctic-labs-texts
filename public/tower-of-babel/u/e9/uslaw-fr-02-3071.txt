FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Request for Additional Information

    The Commission gives notice that it has requested that the parties 
to the below listed agreement provide additional information pursuant 
to section 6(d) of the Shipping Act of 1984, 46 U.S.C. app. Secs. 1701 
et seq. The Commission has determined that further information is 
necessary to evaluate the proposed agreement. This action prevents the 
agreement from becoming effective as originally scheduled.
    Agreement No.: 011784.
    Title: Indamex/TSA Bridging Agreement.
    Parties: The Indamex Agreement, Transpacific Stabilization 
Agreement.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.

    Dated: February 4, 2002.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
