DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Special Medical Advisory Group; Notice of Charter Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (Public Law 92-463; 5 U.S.C. App.), that the Department of 
Veterans Affairs' Special Medical Advisory Group has been renewed for a 
2-year period beginning March 9, 2000, through March 9, 2002.

    Dated: March 9, 2000.

    By direction of the Secretary.
Marvin R. Eason,
Committee Management Officer.
