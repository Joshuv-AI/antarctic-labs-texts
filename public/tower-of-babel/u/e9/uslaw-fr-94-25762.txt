Stone Crab Fishery of the Gulf of Mexico; Correction

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of availability; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This announcement corrects the comment date for a notice of 
availability of Amendment 5 to the Fishery Management Plan for the 
Stone Crab Fishery of the Gulf of Mexico, which was published on 
September 30, 1994.

ADDRESSES: Comments must be mailed to the Southeast Regional Office, 
NMFS, 9721 Executive Center Drive N., St. Petersburg, FL 33702.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Peter Eldridge, 813-570-5306.

Correction of Publication

    Accordingly, the publication on September 30, 1994 (59 FR 49908) of 
the announcement of the availability of Amendment 5 [I.D. 092794B], 
which was the subject of FR Doc. 94-24243 is corrected in the DATES 
portion of the preamble to read as follows:

DATES: Comments must be submitted by November 25, 1994.

    Dated: October 12, 1994.
Joe P. Clem,
Acting Director, Office of Fisheries Conservation and Management, 
National Marine Fisheries Service.
