OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT
 

Request for Clearance of a Revised Information Collection Form RI 
30-10

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (title 
44, U.S. Code, chapter 35), this notice announces a request for 
clearance of a revised information collection. Form RI 30-10, Disabled 
Dependent Questionnaire, is used to collect sufficient information 
about the medical condition and earning capacity for OPM to be able to 
determine whether a disabled adult child is eligible for health 
benefits coverage and/or survivor annuity payments under the Civil 
Service Retirement System/Federal Employees Retirement System.
    Approximately 2,500 RI 30-10 forms are completed annually. The form 
requires approximately 30 minutes to complete. The annual burden is 
1,250 hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact C. Ronald Trueworthy on (703) 
908-8550.

DATES: Comments on this proposal should be received on or before July 
15, 1994.
