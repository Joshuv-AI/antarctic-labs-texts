DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Closed Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act 

[[Page 36110]]
(Pub.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following Committee 
Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 28 July 1995.
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1530.
    Place: Pentagon--Washington, DC.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) 1994 Summer Study on 
``Technical Architecture Command, Control, Communications and 
Computers (C4'') Systems.'' Assorted briefings to the Future Army 
Radio Study Group will be conducted. This meeting will be closed to 
the public in accordance with Section 552b(c) of Title 5, U.S.C., 
specifically paragraph (4) thereof, and Title 5, U.S.C., Appendix 2, 
subsection 10(d). The proprietary information to be discussed is so 
inextricably intertwined so as to preclude opening any portion of 
this meeting. The ASB Administrative Officer may be contacted for 
further information at (703) 695-0781.
Karen L. Blystone,
Sgt., U.S. Army.
