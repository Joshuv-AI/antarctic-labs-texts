Change of Mailing Address; Notice

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Change of mailing address.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice sets forth the new mailing address of the Bureau 
of Land Management, Lea County Inspection Station, Hobbs, New Mexico.

DATE: August 19, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Howard Parman, Public Affairs Officer, Bureau of Land Management 1717 
West 2nd Street, Roswell NM 88201 (505) 627-0212.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Department of Interior's Bureau of Land 
Management, New Mexico State Office, Roswell District Office, Carlsbad 
Resource Area Office, Lea County Inspection Station, Hobbs, New Mexico 
is changing their mailing address effective August 19, 1996. The new 
mailing address will be: Bureau of Land Management, Lea County 
Inspection Station, 414 W. Taylor, Hobbs, New Mexico 88240.
