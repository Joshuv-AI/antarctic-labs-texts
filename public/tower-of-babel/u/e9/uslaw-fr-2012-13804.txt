OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act; Public Hearing Cancellation Notice; June 6, 2012

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Public Hearing in Conjunction 
with each Board meeting was published in the Federal Register (77 FR 
13158) on March 5, 2012. No requests were received to provide testimony 
or submit written statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's public 
hearing scheduled for 2 p.m., March 21, 2012 in conjunction with OPIC's 
March 29, 2012 Board of Directors meeting has been cancelled.

CONTACT PERSON FOR INFORMATION: Information on the hearing cancellation 
may be obtained from Connie M. Downs at (202) 336-8438, or via email at 
[email protected].

    Dated: March 19, 2011.
Connie M. Downs,
OPIC Corporate Secretary.
