Airport Improvement Program (AIP) Grant Assurances; Errata Notice 
Extending Comment Date

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), Department of 
Transportation.

    On April 4, 2022, the FAA issued a notice in the above-captioned 
matter. The notice indicated that the FAA would accept comments 
concerning the proposed modified grant assurances until ``April 12, 
2022.'' This notice extends the comment due date to April 20, 2022.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on April 5, 2022.
Robert A. Hawks,
Deputy Director, Office of Airport Planning and Programming.
