Television Broadcast Service; Longview, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Estes Broadcasting, Inc., 
substitutes channel 38- for channel 54+ at Longview, Texas. See 68 FR 
33431, June 4, 2003. TV channel 38- can be allotted to Longview, Texas, 
in compliance with the minimum distance separation requirements of 
Sections 73.610 and 73.698 of the Commission's Rules. The coordinates 
for channel 38- at Longview are North Latitude 32-35-23 and West 
Longitude 95-23-27. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective December 1, 2003.

418-1600.
