SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region X Regulatory Fairness Board; Public Federal Regulatory 
Enforcement Fairness Roundtable

    The Small Business Administration Region X Regulatory Fairness 
Board and the SBA Office of the National Ombudsman will hold a Public 
Roundtable on Thursday, July 10, 2003 at 10 a.m. at the Boise Metro 
Chamber of Commerce, 250 S. 5th Street, Boise, ID 83701, to provide 
small business owners and representatives of trade associations with an 
opportunity to share information concerning the federal regulatory 
enforcement and compliance environment.
    Anyone wishing to attend or to make a presentation must contact 
Irene Gonzalez in writing or by fax, in order to be put on the agenda. 
Irene Gonzalez, U.S. Small Business Administration, Boise District 
Office, 1020 Main Street, Suite 290, Boise, ID 83702, phone (208) 334-
1696 ext. 249, fax (208) 334-9353, e-mail: [email protected].
    For more information, see our Web site at http://www.sba.gov/ombudsman.

    Dated: June 23, 2003.
Michael L. Barrera,
National Ombudsman.
