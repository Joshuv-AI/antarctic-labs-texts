Neat Feet Hosiery, Inc., Stoneville, NC; Dismissal of Application 
for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Neat Feet Hosiery, Inc., 
Stoneville, North Carolina. The application contained no new 
substantial information which would bear importantly on the 
Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was 
issued.

TA-W-56,401; Neat Feet Hosiery, Inc. Stoneville, North Carolina (May 
9, 2005)

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 13th day of May 2005.
Timothy Sullivan,
