DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. IN18-7-000]


Footprint Power LLC; Footprint Power Salem Harbor Operations LLC; 
Notice of Designation of Commission Staff as Non-Decisional

    With respect to an order issued by the Commission in the above-
captioned docket,\1\ with the exceptions noted below, the staff of the 
Office of Enforcement are designated as non-decisional in deliberations 
by the Commission in this docket. Accordingly, pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.2202 (2017), they will not serve as advisors to the Commission or 
take part in the Commission's review of any offer of settlement. 
Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 18 CFR 385.2201 (2017), 
they are prohibited from communicating with advisory staff concerning 
any deliberations in this docket.
