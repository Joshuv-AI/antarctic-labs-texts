DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
Customs Service
[T.D. 94-10]

 

Revocation of Customs Broker License

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: General notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that on December 23, 1993, the 
Secretary of the Treasury, pursuant to Section 641, Tariff Act of 1930, 
as amended (19 U.S.C. 1641), and Sec. 111.45(a) of the Customs 
Regulations, as amended (19 CFR 111.45(a)), ordered the revocation of 
the license (No. 6911) issued to Bernard Flores in the San Juan Customs 
District to conduct Customs business.

    Dated: January 24, 1994.
Janet L. Labuda,
Director, Office of Trade Operations.
