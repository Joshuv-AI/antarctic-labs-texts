RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Public Meeting; Sunshine Act

    Notice is hereby given that the Railroad Retirement Board will hold 
a meeting on October 21, 1998, 9:00 a.m., at the Board's meeting room 
on the 8th floor of its headquarters building, 844 North Rush Street, 
Chicago, Illinois, 60611. The agenda for this meeting follows:

(1) Senator Daschle's Amendment Relating to Surviving Divorced Spouses
(2) Investment Practices: Barra Rogers Casey Update
(3) Posting of the General Counsel Vacancy
(4) 14th Annual Railroad Retirement Board Award for Excellence Program
(5) Special Act/Special Service Awards
(6) Year 2000 Issues

    The entire meeting will be open to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: October 9, 1998.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
