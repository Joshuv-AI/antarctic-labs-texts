Alaska Energy Authority; Errata Notice

    A typographical error was made in our September 17, 2012 Notice of 
``Extension of Time To File Comments on the Proposed Study Plan and the 
Revised Study Plan for the Susitna-Watana'' Project No. 14241. Comments 
on the proposed study plan are due November 14, 2012. All other dates 
in the notice are correct.

    Dated: September 21, 2012.
