Federal Acquisition Regulation; OMB Control Numbers

AGENCIES: Department of Defense (DOD), General Services Administration 
(GSA), and National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Technical amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The list of Office of Management and Budget approvals under 
the Paperwork Reduction Act has been updated to incorporate numerous 
additions and deletions which resulted from recent changes to the 
Federal Acquisition Regulation.

DATES: Effective Date: December 28, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The FAR Secretariat, room 4037, GS 
Building, Washington, DC 20405, (202) 501-4755. Please cite FAC 90-23, 
Technical Amendment.

    Dated: December 7, 1994.
Albert A. Vicchiolla,
Director, Office of Federal Acquisition Policy.

    48 CFR Part 1 is amended as set forth below:

PART 1--FEDERAL ACQUISITION REGULATIONS SYSTEM

    1. The authority citation for 48 CFR part 1 continues to read as 
follows:

    Authority: 40 U.S.C. 486(c); 10 U.S.C. Chapter 137; and 42 
U.S.C. 2473(c).

    2. Section 1.105 is revised to read as follows:


1.105  OMB Approval under the Paperwork Reduction Act.

    The Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (Pub. L. 96-511) imposes a 
requirement on Federal agencies to obtain approval from the Office of 
Management and Budget (OMB) before collecting information from ten or 
more members of the public. The information collection and 
recordkeeping requirements contained in this regulation have been 
approved by the OMB. The following OMB control numbers apply:
