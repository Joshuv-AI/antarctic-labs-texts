DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging, Amended Notice of Closed Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Aging Special Emphasis Panel, National Institute on Aging, 
November 17, 1997, Gateway Building, 7201 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, 
Maryland which was published in the Federal Register on October 16, 
(Vol. 62, No. 200).
    This committee was to have convened at 12:00 noon on November 17, 
but has been changed to 12:00 noon on December 2. The location remains 
the same.
    As previously announced, this meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: November 4, 1997.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Office, NIH.
