ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 4-0 on August 4, the Commission

Commission's rules that ``Affirmation of Atlas Corporation: Docket No.

``Affirmation of 7/30/97 Letter from Native American Petitioners--In 
the Matter of Energy Fuels Nuclear, Inc.'' be held on August 7, and on 
less than one week's notice to the public.
