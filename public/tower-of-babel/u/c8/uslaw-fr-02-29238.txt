Radio Broadcasting Services; Coosada, Alabama

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule, petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document grants a petition for reconsideration filed by 
Media Equities Corporation, seeking reconsideration of the Report and 
Order in this proceeding, and requesting deletion of Channel 226A at 
Coosada, Alabama. See 67 FR 20459, April 25, 2002. That Report and 
Order allotted, at Media Equities' request, Channel 226A at Coosada, 
Alabama. Media Equities requests reconsideration of this allotment and 
withdraws its expression of interest. With no other expression of 
interest in the allotment, the allotment is deleted.

DATES: Effective December 9, 2002.

(202) 418-2180.
