Steelcase, Inc., Kentwood, MI; Steelcase University, Grand 
Rapids, MI; Steelcase, Inc., Caledonia, MI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 28, 2009, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Steelcase, 
Inc., Kellwood, Michigan (TA-W-65,857), Steelcase University, Grand 
Rapids, Michigan (TA-W-65,857A), and Steelcase, Inc., Caledonia, 
Michigan (TA-W-65,857B).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Therefore, the investigation under this petition has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of May, 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
