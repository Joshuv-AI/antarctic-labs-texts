DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


American Sterling Bank, Sugar Creek, MO; Notice of Appointment of 
Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision has duly

[[Page 18805]]

appointed the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation as sole Receiver 
for American Sterling Bank, Sugar Creek, Missouri (OTS No. 15909) on 
April 17, 2009.

    Dated: April 20, 2009.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
