DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. GP94-2-011]


Columbia Gas Transmission Corporation; Notice of Refund Report

March 27, 2002.
    Take notice that on March 22, 2002, Columbia Gas Transmission 
Corporation (Columbia) tendered for filing with the Commission its 
Refund Report made to comply with the April 17, 1995 Settlement 
(Settlement) in Docket No. GP94-02, et al. as approved by the 
Commission on June 15, 1995 (Columbia Gas Transmission Corp., 71 FERC 
para. 61,337 (1995)).
    On February 20, 2002 Columbia states that it made refunds, as 
billing credits and with checks, in the amount of $308,553.40. The 
refunds represent deferred tax refunds received from Trailblazer 
Pipeline Company and Overthrust Pipeline Company. These refunds were 
made pursuant to Article VIII, Section E of the Settlement using the 
allocation percentages shown on Appendix G, Schedule 5 of the 
Settlement. The refunds include interest at the FERC rate, in 
accordance with the Code of Federal Regulations, Subpart F, Section 
154.501(d).
    Columbia states that copies of its filing have been mailed to all 
affected customers and state commissions.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be

[[Page 15558]]

filed on or before April 3, 2002. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not serve to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of 
this filing are on file with the Commission and are available for 
public inspection. This filing may also be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select ``Docket#'' and follow the 
instructions (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). Comments, protests and 
interventions may be filed electronically via the Internet in lieu of 
paper. See, 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the 
Commission's web site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
