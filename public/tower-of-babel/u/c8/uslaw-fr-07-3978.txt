DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Parole Commission


Public Announcement; Pursuant to the Government in the Sunshine 
Act (Public Law 94-409) [5 U.S.C. Section 552b]

Agency Holding Meeting:  Department of Justice, United States Parole 
Commission.

Time and Date:  10 a.m., Thursday, July 26, 2007.

Place:  5550 Friendship Blvd., Fourth Floor, Chevy Chase, MD 20815.

Status:  Open.

Matters Considered:  At the July 26, 2007 meeting the Commission 
considered the following item that was placed on the agenda the day 
before the meeting, in addition to the items previously noticed:
    Consideration of the Commission's funding request for FY 2009. The 
Commission voted to approve the Chairman's proposed funding request by 
a vote of 3-1.

Agency Contact:  Thomas W. Hutchison, Chief of Staff, United States 
Parole Commission, (301) 492-5990.

    Dated: August 9, 2007.
Rockne Chickinell,
