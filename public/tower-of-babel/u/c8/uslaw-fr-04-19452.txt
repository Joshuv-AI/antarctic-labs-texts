Approval of Ballast Water Treatment Systems; Correction

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DHS.

ACTION: Notice with request for comments; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Coast Guard is correcting a notice with request for 
comments that appeared in the Federal Register of August 5, 2004 (69 FR 
47453). The notice with request for comments seeks consultation with 
all interested and affected parties in establishing a program to 
approve ballast water treatment systems. This correction clarifies the 
notice.

DATES: This correction is effective on July 28, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: If you have questions on this rule, 
call Mr. Bivan Patnaik, Project Manager, Environmental Standards 
Division, Coast Guard, telephone 202-267-1744, e-mail: 
[email protected]. If you have questions on viewing the docket, 
call Ms. Andrea M. Jenkins, Program Manager, Docket Operations, 
telephone 202-366-0271.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Coast Guard's Approval of Ballast Water 
Treatment Systems notice (FR Doc. 04-17827) appearing on page 47454 of
