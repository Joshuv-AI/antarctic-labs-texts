Re-Source America, Inc.; Mebane, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 20, 2006 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Re-Source 
America, Inc., Mebane, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn at this 
time. Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 28th day of June 2006.
 Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
