NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION
 

Notice of Workshop

    The National Science Foundation (NSF) will hold a two-day workshop 
on November 6-8, 1994. The workshop will take place at the Washington, 
DC Renaissance Hotel, 999 9th Street, NW, Washington, DC (202) 898-
9000. Sessions will be held from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. on Sunday, 
8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Monday, and 8:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on 
Tuesday.
    The purpose of the workshop is to provide NSF information gathered 
from a wide variety of institutions of higher education and 
professional organizations about the trends, research results, and 
current issues in the undergraduate preparation of future K-12 teachers 
of science, mathematics and technology.
    The workshop will not operate as an advisory committee. It will be 
open to the public. Participants will include approximately 100 leaders 
in science, engineering, mathematics, technology, and education.
    For additional information, contact Dr. Tina Straley, Division of 
Undergraduate Education, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Arlington, VA, 
(703)306-1669.

    Dated: September 27, 1994.
Robert F. Watson,
Director, Division of Undergraduate Education.
