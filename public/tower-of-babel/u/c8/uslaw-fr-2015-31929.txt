Self-Regulatory Organizations; NASDAQ OMX BX, Inc.; Notice of 
Withdrawal of Proposed Rule Change To Amend the Fees Schedule

December 15, 2015.
    On November 12, 2015, the NASDAQ OMX BX, Inc. (``Exchange'') filed 
with the Securities and Exchange Commission (``Commission''), pursuant 
to section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934\1\ and Rule 
19b-4 thereunder,\2\ a proposed rule change to amend the Exchange's 
fees schedule. The proposed rule change was published for comment in 
the Federal Register on December 1, 2015.\3\ The Commission received no 
comment letters on the proposal. On December 11, 2015, the Exchange 
withdrew the proposed rule change (SR-BX-2015-071).
