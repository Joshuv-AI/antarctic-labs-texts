Red Lake Gas Storage, L.P.; Notice of Public Comment Meeting and 
Site Visit for the Proposed Red Lake Gas Storage Project and Request 
for Comments on Environmental Issues

October 16, 2002.
    The staff of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) 
is issuing this notice to announce the date and location of a public 
scoping meeting on the proposed Red Lake Gas Storage Project. The 
Commission staff will be preparing an environmental assessment (EA) for 
Red Lake Gas Storage, L.P.''s project in Mohave County, Arizona. The 
planned facilities would consist of two solution-mined underground salt 
caverns, about 52 miles of various diameter pipeline, a 25,000-
horsepower (hp) compressor station, a 9,000-hp compressor station, four 
water withdrawal wells, four brine disposal wells, and appurtenant 
facilities. The EA will be used by the Commission in its decision-
making process to determine whether the project is in the public 
convenience and necessity. You are invited to attend the public meeting 
to enter into the Commission's record any comments you might have. 
Comments received at the scoping meeting will help us to determine the 
issues to be evaluated in the EA. The date, time, and location are 
shown below:
    Date and Time: November 14, 2002, 7 to 10 pm.
    Location Phone: Mohave Community College, Kingman Campus, Room 
Number 111, 1971 Jagerson Avenue, Kingman, AZ 86401.
    Phone: (928) 757-4331.
    For additional information, please contact Mr. Thomas Russo of the 
Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 502-8004.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
