DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER97-3665-000]


Union Electric Company; Notice of Filing

July 25, 1997.
    Take notice that on July 8, 1997, Union Electric Company (UE) filed 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission an application for 
authority to charge market based rates and for certain waivers and 
authorizations. UE requested waiver of notice to permit its proposed 
rate schedule to become effective on July 9, 1997, one day after the 
date of filing.
