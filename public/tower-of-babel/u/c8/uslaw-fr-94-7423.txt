NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION
 

DOE/NSF Nuclear Science Advisory Committee; Meeting

    Name: DOE/NSF Nuclear Science Advisory Committee.
    Date and Time: April 19, 1994 from 8:30 a.m. to 1 p.m.
    Place: Department of Energy, Forrestall Building, room 1E-245, 
1000 Independence Ave., SW., Washington, DC 20585.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: John W. Lightbody, Program Director for Nuclear 
Physics, National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Blvd, Arlington, 
VA 22230. Telephone: (703) 306-1890.
    Minutes: May be obtained from the contact person listed above.
    Purpose of Meeting: To advise the National Science Foundation 
and the Department of Energy on scientific priorities within the 
field of basic nuclear science research.
    Agenda: Presentation of NSAC Subcommittee Report on Assessment 
and Planning for the DOE Nuclear Physics Program (W. Henning); (*) 
Discussion and recommendations regarding the Charge to NSAC (*) 
Public Comments. (*) Persons wishing to speak should make 
arrangements through the Contact Person identified above.

    Dated: March 24, 1994.
M. Rebecca Winkler,
Committee Management Officer.
