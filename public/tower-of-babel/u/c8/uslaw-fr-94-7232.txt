OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT

 

Request for Clearance of a New Information Collection, Form RI 
98-7

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (title 
44, U.S. Code, chapter 35), this notice announces a request for 
clearance of a new information collection. Form RI 98-7, We Need 
Important Information About Your Eligibility for SSA Disability 
Benefits (SSA Decision not received), is used to verify receipt of SSA 
disability benefits, make necessary adjustments to the FERS disability 
benefit, and notify the annuitant of any overpayment amount payable to 
OPM. It also specifically notifies the annuitant of his or her 
responsibility to notify OPM of his or her Social Security status and 
the consequences of non-notification.
    There are estimated to be 2,000 respondents for RI 98-7. It takes 
approximately 5 minutes to complete RI 98-7 for an annual burden of 166 
hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact C. Ronald Trueworthy on (703) 
908-8550.
