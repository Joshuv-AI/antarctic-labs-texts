GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


SES Performance Review Board

AGENCY: General Services Administration

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given of the appointment of members of the 
GSA Performance Review Board.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Karla J. Hester, Director of Executive 
Resources, Office of the Chief Human Capital Officer, General Services 
Administration, 1800 F Street, N.W., Washington, DC 20405, (202) 501-
1207.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Section 4313(c)(1) through (5) of title 5 
U.S.C. requires each agency to establish, in accordance with 
regulations prescribed by the Office of Personnel Management, one or 
more SES performance review board(s). The board reviews and evaluates 
the initial appraisal of a senior executive's performance by the 
supervisor, and considers recommendations to the appointing authority 
regarding the performance of the senior executive.
    The following have been designated as members of the Performance 
Review Board of the General Services Administration:

    James A. Williams, Federal Acquisition Service Commissioner
    Jon A. Jordan, Federal Acquisition Service Controller
