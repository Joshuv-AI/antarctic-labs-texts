McCain Foods, Burley, ID; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 7, 2000, in response to a petition filed on the 
same date on behalf of workers at McCain Foods, Burley, Idaho.
    The company official submitting the petition has requested that the 
petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case 
would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C., this 4th day of April, 2000.
Grant D. Beale,
Program Manager, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
