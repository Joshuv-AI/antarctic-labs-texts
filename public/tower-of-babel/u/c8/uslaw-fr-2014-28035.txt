DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Membership of the Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Office of Management, Department of Education.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Secretary publishes a list of persons who may be named to 
serve on the Performance Review Board that oversees the evaluation of 
performance appraisals for Senior Executive Service members of the 
Department.

DATES: Effective Date: November 26, 2014.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Membership

    Title 5, U.S.C. 4314(c)(4) of the Civil Service Reform Act of 1978, 
Public Law 95-454, requires that the appointment of Performance Review 
Board members be published in the Federal Register. The following 
persons may be named to serve on the Performance Review Board:
