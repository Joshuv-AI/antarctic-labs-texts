DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service

Notice of Extension of Concession Contracts

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Public notice is hereby given that the National Park Service 
proposes to extend the following expiring concession contracts for a 
period of up to 1 (one) year, or until such time as a new contract is 
executed, whichever occurs sooner.

DATES: Effective Date: January 1, 2013.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jo A. Pendry, Chief, Commercial 
Services Program, National Park Service, 1201 Eye Street NW., 11th 
Floor, Washington, DC 20005; telephone (202) 513-7156.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: All of the listed concession authorizations 
will expire by their terms on or before December 31, 2012. Pursuant to 
36 CFR 51.23, the National Park Service has determined that the 
proposed short-term extensions are necessary in order to avoid 
interruption of visitor services and has taken all reasonable and 
appropriate steps to consider alternatives to avoid such interruption.
