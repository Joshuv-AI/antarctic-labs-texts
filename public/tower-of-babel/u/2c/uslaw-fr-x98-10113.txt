Scaffolds

CFR Correction

    In Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, part 1926, revised 
as of July 1, 1997, on page 311, second column, in the last line of the 
effective date note, the bold text reading, ``Training requirements'' 
should be removed. The following section number and heading should 
precede the text following the effective date note.


Sec. 1926.454  Training requirements.
