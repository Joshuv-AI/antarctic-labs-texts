DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Price for the 2012 Annual Uncirculated Dollar Coin Set

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing a price of $54.95 for the 
2012 Annual Uncirculated Dollar Coin Set. This set contains the 
following

[[Page 54660]]

uncirculated coins: four Presidential $1 Coins, one Native American $1 
Coin, and one American Eagle Silver Coin.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B. B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street, NW; 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.

    Authority: 31 U.S.C. 5111, 5112 & 9701.

    Dated: August 28, 2012.
Richard A. Peterson,
Deputy Director, United States Mint.
