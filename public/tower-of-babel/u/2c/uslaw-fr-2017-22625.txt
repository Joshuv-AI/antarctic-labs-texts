POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--Global Plus 1E

AGENCY: Postal ServiceTM.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing a request with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add the Global Plus 1E product to the 
Competitive Products List.

DATES: Date of notice: October 18, 2017.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kyle Coppin, 202-268-2368.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice that, pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642, on October 13, 
2017, it filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission a Request of The 
United States Postal Service to add Global Plus 1E to the Competitive 
Products List. Documents are available at www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. 
MC2018-7 and CP2018-12.

Stanley F. Mires,
Attorney, Federal Compliance.
