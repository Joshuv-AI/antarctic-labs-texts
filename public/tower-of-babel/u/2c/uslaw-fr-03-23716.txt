DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-52,553]


Berwick Weaving, Inc., Berwick, Pennsylvania; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August

[[Page 54491]]

13, 2003, in response to a worker petition filed on behalf of workers 
at Berwick Weaving, Inc., Berwick, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 27th day of August 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
