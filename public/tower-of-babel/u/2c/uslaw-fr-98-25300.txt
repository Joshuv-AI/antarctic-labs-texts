DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Mojave National Reserve; Advisory Commission; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given in accordance with the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act that a meeting of the Mojave National Preserve Advisory 
Commission will be held September 28 and 29, 1998; assemble at 9:00 AM 
at the Hole-in-the-Wall Visitor Center, Mojave National Preserve, 
California.
    The agenda: Staffing and Funding, Presentation of the General 
Management Plan, and Other Planning.
    The Advisory Commission was established by Pub. L. 103-433 to 
provide for the advice on development and implementation of the General 
Management Plan.
