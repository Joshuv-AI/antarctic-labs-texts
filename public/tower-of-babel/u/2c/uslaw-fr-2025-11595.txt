DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Integrative Health; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Council for Complementary and Integrative Health, July 01, 
2025, 10:00 a.m. to July 01, 2025, 05:00 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, DEM 2, 6707 Democracy Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was 
published in the Federal Register on April 28, 2025, 90 FR 17605.
    This notice is being updated to reflect the new URL for the Open 
Session of the NCCIH Advisory Council meeting. The open session can be 
accessed through the following Microsoft Teams Meeting URL address: 
https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/19%3ameeting_OTRmYmQ1MTMtMjQ1YS00OWRjLWIxOGYtNWE3NmRlMTdmNzdm%40thread.v2/0?context=%7b%22Tid%22%3a%2214b77578-9773-42d5-8507-251ca2dc2b06%22%2c%22Oid%22%3a%221efc1acb-c84a-498f-a01d-25e39a717c5e%22%7d.
    The event will be recorded and will be made available for viewers 
to watch using the NIH Videocast URL link: https://videocast.nih.gov/PastEvents.
    The meeting is partially closed to the public.

    Dated: June 20, 2025.
