NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act Meetings

Time and Date: 10:00 a.m., Tuesday, July 24, 2012.

Place: Board Room, 7th Floor, Room 7047, 1775 Duke Street (All visitors 
must use Diagonal Road Entrance), Alexandria, VA 22314-3428.

Status: Open.

    3. NCUA's Rules and Regulations, ``Troubled Condition'' Definition.
    4. NCUA's Rules and Regulations, Access to Emergency Liquidity.
    5. 2012 Temporary Corporate Credit Union Stabilization Fund 
Assessment.
