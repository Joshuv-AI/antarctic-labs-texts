SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3100]


State of Ohio; Amendment #1

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated July 20, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to include Morrow County, Ohio as a disaster area due to 
damages caused by severe storms, flooding, and tornadoes beginning on 
June, 24, 1998 and continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in Marion County, Ohio which is contiguous may be 
filed until the specified date at the previously designated location.
    Any counties contiguous to the above-name primary county and not 
listed herein have been previously declared.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is August 29, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is March 30, 1999.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: July 27, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
