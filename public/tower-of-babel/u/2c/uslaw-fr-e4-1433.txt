Weaver's Cove Energy L.L.C. and Mill River Pipeline L.L.C.; 
Notice of Public Meeting Attendance

June 22, 2004.
    On Wednesday, June 30, 2004, staff of the Office of Energy Projects 
(OEP) will participate in a public meeting held by State Representative 
Joseph N. Amaral in Tiverton, Rhode Island regarding Weaver's Cove 
Energy, L.L.C.'s proposed liquefied natural gas (LNG) import terminal 
and storage facility in Fall River, Massachusetts. The public meeting 
will start at 7 p.m. (e.s.t.) at the Patriot's Club (formerly the Ponta 
Delgada Club) on 70 Shove Street in Tiverton, Rhode Island.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
