DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Warm Spring Habitat Enhancement EIS--Helena-Lewis and Clark 
National Forest, Jefferson County, Montana

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Withdrawal of Notice of Intent to prepare an Environmental 
Impact Statement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The USDA Forest Service is withdrawing the Notice of Intent to 
prepare an Environmental Impact Statement for the Warm Spring Habitat 
Enhancement project on the Helena-Lewis and Clark National Forest. A 
Notice of Intent to prepare an Environmental Impact Statement was 
published in the Federal Register on October 9, 2009 (pages 52174-
52175)

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jennifer Woods, Helena-Lewis and Clark 
National Forest, 1220 38th Street North, Great Falls, Montana 59405, 
(406) 791-7765.

    Dated: September 14, 2016.
William Avey,
Forest Supervisor, Helena-Lewis and Clark National Forest.
