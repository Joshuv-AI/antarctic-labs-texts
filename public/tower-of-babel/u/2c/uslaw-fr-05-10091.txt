SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

[File No. 500-1]


In Sino Silver Corp.; Order of Suspension of Trading

May 17, 2005.
    It appears to the Securities and Exchange Commission that the 
public interest and the protection of investors require a suspension of 
trading in the securities of Sino Silver Corp. (``Sino''), trading 
under the stock symbol SSLV on the Over-the-Counter Bulletin Board 
(``OTCBB''). The Commission has concerns about the accuracy and 
completeness of information about Sino contained in press releases and 
public filings with the Commission relating to a change in control over 
Sino. In addition, the Commission is concerned that Sino, its 
affiliates, and others may be engaged in the unlawful distribution of 
restricted Sino securities through the OTCBB.
    The Commission is of the opinion that the public interest and the 
protection of investors require a suspension of trading in the 
securities of the above listed company.
    Therefore, it is ordered, pursuant to Section 12(k) of the 
Securities Exchange Act of 1934, that trading in the above listed 
company is suspended for the period from 9:30 a.m. EST, May 17, 2005 
through 11:59 p.m. EST, on May 31, 2005.

    By the Commission.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
