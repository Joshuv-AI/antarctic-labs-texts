DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases Special Emphasis Panel, 
April 23, 2015, 01:00 p.m. to April 23, 2015, 04:00 p.m., National 
Institutes of Health, 5601 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD, 20852 which was 
published in the Federal Register on March 25, 2015, 80 FR 15798-15799.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the date of the meeting 
from 4/23/2015 to 5/22/2015. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: April 22, 2015.
David Clary,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
