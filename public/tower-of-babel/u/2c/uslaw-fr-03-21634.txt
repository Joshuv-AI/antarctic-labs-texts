Notice of Submission of Proposed Information Collection to OMB: 
Applications for Housing Assistance Payments

AGENCY: Office of the Chief Information Officer, HUD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The proposed information collection requirement described 
below has been submitted to the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) 
for review, as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act.
    Housing owners/agents submit vouchers to HUD or their Contract 
Administrators (CA)/Performance Based Contract Administrators (PBCA) 
monthly to receive assistance payments for the difference between the 
gross rent and the total tenant payment for all assisted tenants. 
Special claims vouchers are also submitted by owners/agents to HUD or 
their CA/PBCA to receive an amount to offset unpaid rent, tenant 
damages, vacancies, and/or debt service losses. This is a proposed 
revision to the current information collection.
    The Department is soliciting public comments on the subject 
proposal.
