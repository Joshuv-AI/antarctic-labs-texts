Quarterly Rail Cost Adjustment Factor

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board.

ACTION: Approval of rail cost adjustment factor.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Board has approved the first quarter 2000 rail cost 
adjustment factor (RCAF) and cost index filed by the Association of 
American Railroads. The first quarter 2000 RCAF (Unadjusted) is 1.043. 
The first quarter 2000 RCAF (Adjusted) is 0.594. The first quarter 2000 
RCAF-5 is 0.581.
