DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-62,889]


Robert Bosch Tool Corporation, Lincolnton, NC; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 25, 2008, in response to a 
worker petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at 
Robert Bosch Tool Corporation, Lincolnton, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 13th day of March 2008.
 Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
