DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project Nos. 2897-048, 2932-047, 2941-043, 2931-042, 2942-051]


Sappi North America, Inc.; Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 23, 2019, the Commission issued a notice through the 
FERC eLibrary system \1\ setting February 22, 2019, as the end of the 
formal period to file comments on the Notice of Availability of Draft 
Environmental Assessment for the surrender of the Saccarappa Project 
(P-2897-048), and amendments to the licenses for the Mallison Falls, 
Little Falls, Gambo, and Dundee projects (P-2932-047, P-2941-043, P-
2931-042, and P-2942-051), respectively. Due to the funding lapse at 
certain federal agencies between December 22, 2018 and January 25, 
2019, the Commission is extending the comment period until March 1, 
2019.
