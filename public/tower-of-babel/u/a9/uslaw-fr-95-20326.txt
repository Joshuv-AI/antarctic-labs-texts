CONGRESSIONAL BUDGET OFFICE


Notice of Transmittal of Sequestration Update Report for Fiscal 
Year 1996 to Congress and the Office of Management and Budget

    Pursuant to Section 254(b) of the Balanced Budget and Emergency 
Deficit Control Act of 1985 (2 U.S.C. 904(b)), the Congressional Budget 
Office hereby reports that it has submitted its Sequestration Update 
Report for Fiscal Year 1996 to the House of Representatives, the 
Senate, and the Office of Management and Budget.
Stanley L. Greigg,
Director, Office of Intergovernmental Relations, Congressional Budget 
Office.
