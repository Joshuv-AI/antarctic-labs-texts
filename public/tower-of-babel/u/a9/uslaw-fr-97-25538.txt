DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. EC97-50-000]


Heartland Energy Services, Inc.; Notice of Filing

    Take notice that on September 17, 1997, Heartland Energy Services, 
Inc. (HES), filed additional information regarding the nature of the 
jurisdictional contracts being transferred from HES to the joint 
venture, Cargill-IEC, L.L.C.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filings should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 285.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before September 29, 1997. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any persons wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.
Acting Secretary.
