Amerex Corp.; Scotch Plains, NJ; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 7, 2004 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a state agency representative on behalf of workers at 
Amerex Corp., Scotch Plains, New Jersey.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 28th day of April 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
