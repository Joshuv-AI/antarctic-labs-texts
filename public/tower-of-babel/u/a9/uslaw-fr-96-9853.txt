NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

Correction to Notice of Partial Denial of Amendment to Facility 
Operating Licenses and Opportunity for a Hearing

    On April 10, 1996, the Federal Register published a Notice of 
Partial Denial of Amendment to Facility Operating Licenses and 
Opportunity for a Hearing. On page 15983, under Commonwealth Edison 
Company, Docket Nos. STN 50-454, STN 50-455, STN 50-456 and STN 50-457, 
first paragraph, the Facility Operating License Nos. should have read 
NPF-37, NPF-66, NPF-72 and NPF-77.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 16th day of April 1996.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
George F. Dick, Jr.,
Sr. Project Manager, Project Directorate III-2, Division of Reactor 
Projects--III/IV, Office of Nuclear Reactor Regulation.
