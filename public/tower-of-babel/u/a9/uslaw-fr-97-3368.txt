Mississippi: Proposed Reinstatement of Terminated Oil and Gas 
Lease

    Under the provisions of Public Law 97-451, a petition for 
reinstatement of oil and gas lease LAES 46792, Wayne County, 
Mississippi, was timely filed and accompanied by all required rentals 
and royalties accruing from June 1, 1996, the date of termination.
    No valid lease has been issued affecting the lands. The lessee has 
agreed to new lease terms for rentals and royalties at rates of $10 per 
acre and 16\2/3\ percent. Payment of $500 in administrative fees and a 
$125 publication fee has been made.
    The Bureau of Land Management is proposing to reinstate the lease 
effective June 1, 1996, subject to the original terms and conditions of 
the lease and the increased rental and royalty rates cited above. This 
is in accordance with section 31(d) and (e) of the Mineral Leasing Act 
of 1920, as amended (30 U.S.C. 188 (d) and (e)).
