FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Radio Broadcasting Services; Sun Valley, NV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of James D. Sleeman, allots 
Channel 229A to Sun Valley, NV, as the community's first local FM 
service. See 60 FR 5158, January 26, 1995. Channel 229A can be allotted 
to Sun Valley in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements without the imposition of a site restriction, 
at coordinates 39-35-47 North Latitude and 119-46-30 West Longitude. 
With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective July 28, 1995. The window period for filing 
applications will open on July 28, 1995, and close on August 28, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
