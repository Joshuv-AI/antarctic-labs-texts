Radio Broadcasting Services; Martin, Tiptonville, and Trenton, TN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for reconsideration of a 
Report and Order Order, 63 FR 49684 (September 17, 1998), that denied a 
petition for rule making and the alternative allotment plan filed by 
Thunderbolt Broadcasting Company (``Thunderbolt'') proposing the 
substitution of Channel 267C3 for Channel 269A at Martin, Tennessee. To 
accommodate its proposal to upgrade Station WCMT(FM)'s Channel 269A to 
Channel 267C3 at Martin, Thunderbolt also proposed to substitute 
Channel 247A for Channel 267C3 at Tiptonville, Tennessee and to 
substitute Channel 249C3 for Channel 248C3 at Trenton, Tennessee. These 
alternate proposals were rejected because engineering studies 
determined that retaining Channel 267C3 at Tiptonville would result in 
service to many more people than allotting Channel 267C3 to Martin.
