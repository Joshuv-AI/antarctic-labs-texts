DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration


Proposed Information Collection; Comment Request; Inventory of 
U.S. Marine Protected Areas: Site Characteristics and Human Uses; 
Correction

AGENCY: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, Commerce.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Commerce published a document in the Federal 
Register of February 2, 2015, concerning request for comments on a 
proposed information collection under the Paperwork Reduction Act, OMB 
Control No. 0648-0449. The document contained incorrect dates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Charles M. Wahle, Senior 
Scientist, NOAA's National Marine Protected Areas Center, via email at 
[email protected], or by telephone: (831) 647-6460.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

I. Correction

    In the Federal Register of February 2, 2015, Vol. 80, No. 21, on 
page 5515, in the third column, correct the ``Dates'' caption to read: 
``DATES: Written comments must be submitted on or before April 3, 
2015''.

    Dated: February 5, 2015.
