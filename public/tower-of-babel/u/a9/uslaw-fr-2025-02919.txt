DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-56-2024]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 49; Authorization of Production 
Activity; Merck, Sharp & Dohme LLC; (Pharmaceutical Products for 
Research and Development); Rahway, New Jersey

    On October 18, 2024, Merck, Sharp & Dohme LLC submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facility within Subzone 49Y, in Rahway, New Jersey.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (89 FR 87542-87543, November 4, 2024). 
On February 18, 2025, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at this 
time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including section 400.14.

    Dated: February 18, 2025.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
