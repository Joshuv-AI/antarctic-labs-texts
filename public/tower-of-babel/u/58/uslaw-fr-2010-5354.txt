ABM Janitorial Services, Subsidiary of ABM Industries, Inc., 
Janitorial Division, Formerly Working at Chrysler Group, LLC, Formerly 
Known as Chrysler, LLC, Plymouth Road Office Complex, Detroit, MI; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated in response to a petition filed on 
September 8, 2009 on behalf of workers of ABM Janitorial Services, 
subsidiary of ABM Industries, Inc., Janitorial Division, working at 
Chrysler Group, LLC, formerly known as

[[Page 11938]]

Chrysler, LLC, Plymouth Road Complex, Detroit, Michigan.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, (TA-W-70,948) which expires on January 20, 2012. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of March 2009.
 Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
