DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary

[Transmittal Nos. 09-18]


36(b)(1) Arms Sales Notification

AGENCY: Department of Defense, Defense Security Cooperation Agency.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense is publishing the unclassified text 
of a section 36(b)(1) arms sales notification. This is published to 
fulfill the requirements of section 155 of Public Law 104-164 dated 21 
July 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. B. English, DSCA/DBO/CFM, (703) 
601-3740.
    The following is a copy of a letter to the Speaker of the House of 
Representatives, Transmittals 09-18 with attached transmittal, and 
policy justification.

    Dated: March 20, 2009.
Patricia L. Toppings,
OSD Federal Register, Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
Defense Security Cooperation Agency
201 12th Street South, Ste 203
Arlington, VA 22202-5408
Mar 13 2009.

The Honorable Nancy Pelosi
Speaker of the House of Representatives,
Washington, DC 20515-6501

    Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the reporting requirements of 
Section 36(b)(1) of the Arms Export Control Act, as amended, we are 
forwarding herewith Transmittal No. 09-18, concerning the Department 
of the Army's proposed Letter(s) of Offer and Acceptance to Mexico 
for defense articles and services estimated to cost $93 million. 
After this letter is delivered to your office, we plan to issue a 
press statement to notify the public of this proposed sale.

Sincerely,

Jeanne L. Farmer,

Acting Director.
