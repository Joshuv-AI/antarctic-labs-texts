SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3553]


State of Texas; Amendment

    The above-numbered declaration is hereby amended to extend the 
incident period for this disaster to October 16, 2003.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is December 8, 2003, and for 
economic injury the deadline is July 7, 2004.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: October 21, 2003.
Hector V. Barreto,
Administrator.
