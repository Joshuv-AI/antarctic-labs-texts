GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION

[Wildlife Order 184; 7-D-KS-486]


Public Buildings Service; Cheney Dam and Reservoir, Sedgwick and 
Reno Counties, Kansas; Transfer of Property

    Pursuant to section 2 of Public Law 537, 80th Congress, approved 
May 19, 1948 (16 U.S.C. 667c), notice is hereby given that:
    1. By deed from the General Services Administration, dated July 13, 
1995, and 150.80 acres of land, known as Cheney Dam and Reservoir 
situated in the counties of Sedgwick and Reno, Kansas, has been 
transferred to the State of Kansas.
    2. The above described property was conveyed for wildlife 
conservation in accordance with the provisions of section 1 of Public 
Law 80-537 (16 U.S.C. 667b), as amended by Public Law 92-432.

    Dated: December 4, 1996.
Gordon S. Creed,
Assistant Deputy Commissioner, Office of Property Disposal.
