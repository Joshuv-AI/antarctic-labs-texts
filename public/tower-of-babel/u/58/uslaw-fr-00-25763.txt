DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management

[ES-960-1910-BJ-4489] ES-50828, Group 30, Illinois


Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Illinois

    The plat of the survey of the Lock and Dam No. 26 and the Locks and 
Dam No. 26 Replacement acquisition boundaries, in Township 5 North, 
Ranges 9 and 10 West of the 3rd Principal Meridian, Illinois, will be 
officially filed in Eastern States, Springfield, Virginia at 7:30 a.m., 
on November 9, 2000.
    The survey was requested by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers.

[[Page 59867]]

    All inquiries or protests concerning the technical aspects of the 
survey must be sent to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor, Eastern States, 
Bureau of Land Management, 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 
22153, prior to 7:30 a.m., November 9, 2000.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the appropriate fees.

    Dated: September 25, 2000
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
