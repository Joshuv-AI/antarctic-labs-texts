SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

``FEDERAL REGISTER'' CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT: [60 FR 26481, 
May 17, 1995].

STATUS: Open Meeting.

PLACE: 450 Fifth Street, N.W., Washington, D.C.

DATE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED: May 17, 1995.

CHANGE IN THE MEETING: Deletion.
    The following item will not be considered at an open meeting 
scheduled for Friday, May 19, 1995, at 11:00 a.m.

    Whether to propose amendments to the all-holder's/best price 
provisions of: (i) Rule 13e-4; and (ii) 14d-10 to prohibit a tender 
offeror from paying a soliciting dealer fee to a security holder 
with respect to any tender by that holder for its own account.

    Commissioner Wallman, as duty officer, determined that Commission 
business required the above change and that no earlier notice thereof 
was possible.
    At times, changes in Commission priorities require alterations in 
the scheduling of meeting items. For further information and to 
ascertain what, if any, matters have been added, deleted or postponed, 
please contact: The Office of the Secretary (202) 942-7070.

    Dated: May 18, 1995.
Jonathan G. Katz,
Secretary.
