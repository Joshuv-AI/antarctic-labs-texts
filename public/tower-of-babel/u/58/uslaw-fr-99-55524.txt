CFR Correction

    In Title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 100 to 185, 
revised as of Oct. 1, 1998, on pages 88, 89, and 91, Sec. Sec. 171.11 
(d)(9)(iii), 171.12 (b)(8)(iii), and 171.12a (b)(5)(iii) are corrected 
by removing the word ``POISON'' the first time it appears and adding in 
its place the words ``POISON INHALATION HAZARD''.
