ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking; correction.

SUMMARY: This document corrects a missing e-mail address in a proposed 
rule published in the Federal Register of September 9, 2010, regarding 
relief from certain disclosure, reporting and recordkeeping 
requirements that Commission staff previously has issued on a case-by-
case basis to commodity pool operators (CPOs).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David A. Stawick, 202-418-5071.

Correction

    In proposed rule FR Doc. 2010-22395, beginning on page 54794 in the 
issue of September 9, 2010, make the following correction. In the 
ADDRESSES section, add the e-mail address [email protected] 
in the place of ``[email address TBD]''.
