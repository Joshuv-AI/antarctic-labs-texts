DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
National Park Service


National Register of Historic Places; Notification of Pending 
Nominations

    Nominations for the following properties being considered for 
listing in the National Register were received by the National Park 
Service before November 9, 1996. Pursuant to section 60.13 of 36 CFR 
Part 60 written comments concerning the significance of these 
properties under the National Register criteria for evaluation may be 
forwarded to the National Register, National Park Service, P.O. Box 
37127, Washington, D.C. 20013-7127. Written comments should be 
submitted by December 4, 1996.
Beth Boland,
Acting Keeper of the National Register.
