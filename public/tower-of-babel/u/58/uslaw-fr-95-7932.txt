DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-30,799]


Huls America, Incorporated, Elizabeth, NJ; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 13, 1995 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers and former workers at Huls America, 
Incorporated, Elizabeth, New Jersey (TA-W-30,799).
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 22nd day of March 1995.
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
