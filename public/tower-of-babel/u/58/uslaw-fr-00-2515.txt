DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

National Institute of Corrections


Advisory Board Meeting

TIME AND DATE: 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Monday, March 6, 2000 and 8:30 
a.m. to 12 noon to Tuesday, March 7, 2000.

PLACE: Westin Hotel--Seattle, 1900 Fifth Avenue, Seattle, Washington 
98101.

STATUS:  Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Tours/Presentations Concerning King County 
Crisis Triage Unit/Pre-Booking Diversion, Seattle Police Department 
Crisis Intervention Team, King County Mental Health Court Proceedings 
and Post-Booking Diversion Proceedings; Updates on Mentally Ill in 
Prisons and Jails, the NIC Strategic Plan, Interstate Compact 
Activities, Advisory Board Hearings; Reports by Program Divisions; FY 
2001 Service Plan Recommendations; and FY 2002 Budget Recommendations.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION:  Larry Solomon, Deputy Director, 
(202) 307-3106, ext. 155.
