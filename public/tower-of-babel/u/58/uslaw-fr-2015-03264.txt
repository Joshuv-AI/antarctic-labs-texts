MoGas Pipeline LLC; Notice of Technical Conference

    Take notice that a technical conference will be held on Tuesday, 
February 24, 2015, at 10:00 a.m. (Eastern Standard Time), in a room to 
be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission (Commission), 888 First Street NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    At the technical conference, the Commission Staff and the parties 
to the proceeding should be prepared to discuss all issues set for the 
technical conference as established in the January 30, 2015 Order.\1\
