SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Chairs-Regional Fairness Boards 1-10 Meeting

    The Small Business Administration Chairs-Regional Fairness Board 1-
10 Meeting Located in the geographical area of Chicago, IL, will hold a 
strategy meeting at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday, February 9, 1999 at SBA 
National Ombudsman's Office, 500 W Madison St., Suite 1240, Chicago, IL 
60661, to collect Fairness Board Chairs' comments on the 1999 draft 
Report To Congress, as well as to obtain recommendations and other 
input for the annual Report to Congress.

    For further information, contact, Gary P. Peele (312) 353-0880.
Shirl Thomas,
Director, Office of External Affairs.
