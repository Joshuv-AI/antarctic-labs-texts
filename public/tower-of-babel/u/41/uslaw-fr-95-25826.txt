DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Glass Ceiling Commission Closed Meeting

    Summary: Pursuant to section 10(a) of FACA, this is to announce a 
meeting of the Glass Ceiling Commission which is to take place on 
Wednesday, October 25, 1995.
    Time and Place: The closed meeting will be held on Wednesday, 
October 25, 1995 from 1 p.m. to 2 p.m. and held at the U.S. Department 
of Labor, Room C2313, 200 Constitution Avenue, NW., Washington, DC.
    The Commission will meet in closed session in order to discuss 
commercial characteristics of applicants for the Frances Perkins-
Elizabeth Hanford Dole National Award For Diversity and Excellence in 
American Executive Management. The closing of this meeting is 
authorized by section 10(d) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act and 
Section (c)(4) of the Government in the Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 
552b(c)(4)). This closing allows the Commission to discuss matters 
which if disclosed in an open meeting would reveal information that 
would not customarily be released to the public by the applicants.
    For Further Information Contact: Ms. Rene Redwood, Executive 
Director, Glass Ceiling Commission, U.S. Department of Labor, 200 
Constitution Avenue, NW., Room C-2313, Washington, DC 20210, (202) 219-
7342.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 12th day of October, 1995.
Rene A. Redwood,
Executive Director.
