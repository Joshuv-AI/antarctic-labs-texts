Smead Manufacturing Company, Springfield, OH; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    In accordance with Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
amended, an investigation was initiated on February 25, 2009 in 
response to a worker petition filed on behalf of workers of Smead 
Manufacturing Company, Logan, Ohio.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
