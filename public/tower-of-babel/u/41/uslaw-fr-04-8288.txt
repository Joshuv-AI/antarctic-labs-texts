Parole Commission


Sunshine Act Meeting; Public Announcement Pursuant to the 
Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. 94-409) (5 U.S.C. 552b)

Commission.

    4. Approval of Rules and Procedures Memorandum 2003-01.
    5. Discussion of Proposal to Amend 28 CFR 2.12(a).

agency contact: Thomas W. Hutchison, Chief of Staff, United States 
Parole Commission, (301) 492-5990.
