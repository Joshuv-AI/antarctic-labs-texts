FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices, Acquisition of Shares of Bank or 
Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. E7-19094) published on page 
54914 of the issue for Thursday, September 27, 2007.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City heading, the entry 
for Joseph M. Sullivan, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City (Todd Offenbacker, Assistant 
Vice President) 925 Grand Avenue, Kansas City, Missouri 64198-0001:
    1. Joseph M. Sullivan, as co-trustee, and individually; to retain 
voting shares of Grant County Bank Employee Stock Ownership Plan, and 
thereby indirectly retain voting shares of Resource One, Inc., and 
Grant County Bank, all of Ulysses, Kansas.
    Comments on this application must be received by October 12, 2007.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, September 28, 
2007.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Secretary of the Board.
