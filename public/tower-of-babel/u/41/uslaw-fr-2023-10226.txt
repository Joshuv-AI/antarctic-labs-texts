Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

and Development of the Eye Study Section, June 8-9, 2023, 9:00 a.m. to 
6:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Rockledge II, 6701 Rockledge

Federal Register on May 1, 2023, 88 FR 26581, page 26581-26582.

from Kevin Czaplinski to Zubaida Saifudeen. The meeting is closed to 
the public.
