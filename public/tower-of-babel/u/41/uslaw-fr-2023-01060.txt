BP America Inc., BP Corporation North America Inc., BP America 
Production Company, BP Energy Company; Updated Notice of Designation of 
Commission Staff as Non-Decisional

    With respect to orders issued by the Commission in the above-
captioned docket, with the exceptions noted below, the staff of the 
Office of Enforcement are designated as non-decisional in deliberations 
by the Commission in this docket. Accordingly, pursuant to 18 CFR 
385.2202 (2022), they will not serve as advisors to the Commission or 
take part in the Commission's review of any offer of settlement. 
Likewise, as non-decisional staff, pursuant to 18 CFR 385.2201 (2022), 
they are prohibited from communicating with advisory staff concerning 
any deliberations in this docket.
    Exceptions to this designation as non-decisional are:

Grace Kwon
Laura Vallance
Jennifer Gordon
Joseph Cleaver

    Dated: January 13, 2023.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
