Lord Corporation, Aerospace Products Division, Erie, PA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on June 18, 2003, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Lord 
Corporation, Aerospace Products Division, Erie, Pennsylvania (TA-W-
52,084).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 17th day of July, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
