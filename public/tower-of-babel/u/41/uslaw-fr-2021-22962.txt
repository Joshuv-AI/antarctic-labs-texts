BOST5 Hydroelectric LLC; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal of 
Application for Amendment of License

    On February 9, 2021, BOST5 Hydroelectric LLC (BOST5) filed an 
application to amend the license for the Red River Lock and Dam No. 5 
Hydroelectric Project. On September 20, 2021, BOST5 filed a notice of 
withdrawal of its application for amendment of license.
    No motion in opposition to the notice of withdrawal has been filed, 
and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the withdrawal. 
Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the amendment application became 
effective on October 6, 2021, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
