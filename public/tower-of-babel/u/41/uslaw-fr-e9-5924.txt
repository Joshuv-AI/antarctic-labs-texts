Employment and Training Administration

Delphi Steering; Saginaw, MI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 4, 2009 in response to a petition 
filed by

[[Page 11764]]

a company official on behalf of workers of Delphi Steering, Saginaw, 
Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Therefore, the investigation under this petition has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 11th day of March, 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
