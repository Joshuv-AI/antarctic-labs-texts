DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Importer of Controlled Substances; Notice of Registration

    By Notice dated June 19, 1995, and published in the Federal 
Register on June 30, 1995, (60 FR 34297), Roberts Laboratories, Inc., 
Meridian Center III, 4 Industrial Way West, Eatontown, New Jersey 
07724, made application to the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) to 
be registered as an importer of Propiram (9649), a basic class of 
controlled substance listed in Schedule I.
    No comments or objections have been received. Therefore, pursuant 
to Section 1008(a) of the Controlled Substances Import and Export Act 
and in accordance with Title 21, Code of Federal Regulations, Section 
1311.42, the above firm is granted registration as an importer of the 
basic class of controlled substance listed above.


    Dated: September 29, 1995.
Gene R. Haislip,
Deputy Assistant Administrator, Office of Diversion Control, Drug 
Enforcement Administration.
