Moyno, Inc., a Division of Robbins & Myers, Inc., Springfield, 
OH; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    In accordance with Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as 
amended, an investigation was initiated on March 31, 2009 in response 
to a petition filed by a United Auto Workers Local 902 Union 
representative on behalf of workers of Moyno, Inc., a Division of 
Robbins & Myers, Inc., Springfield, Ohio.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of April 2009.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
