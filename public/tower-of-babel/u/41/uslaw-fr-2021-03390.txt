NEIGHBORHOOD REINVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: 4:00 p.m., Wednesday, February 24, 2021.

PLACE: Via Conference Call.

STATUS: Parts of this meeting will be open to the public. The rest of

opinion, one or more of the exemptions set forth in 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(2)

 Executive Session

I. CALL TO ORDER
II. Discussion Item Welcome/Introduction
III. Discussion Item Market Landscape Presentation
IV. Discussion Item Strategic Planning Overview
V. Discussion Item Strategic Questions
VI. Discussion Item Timeline and Next Steps
