Sun Microsystems, Inc.; Anta Clara, CA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 10, 2006 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers at SUN Microsystems, 
Inc., Santa Clara, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 12th day of May, 2006.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
