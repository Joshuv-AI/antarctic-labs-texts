OVERSEAS PRIVATE INVESTMENT CORPORATION


Sunshine Act; Cancellation Notice of Annual Public Hearing

    OPIC's Sunshine Act notice of its Annual Public Hearing was 
published in the Federal Register (Volume 78, Number 17, Page 5516) on 
January 25, 2013. No requests were received to provide testimony or 
submit written statements for the record; therefore, OPIC's Annual 
Public Hearing scheduled for 2 p.m., March 13, 2013 has been cancelled.

CONTACT PERSON FOR INFORMATION: Information on the hearing cancellation 
may be obtained from Connie M. Downs at (202) 336-8438, via facsimile 
at (202) 218-0136, or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: February 26, 2013.
Connie M. Downs,
OPIC Corporate Secretary.
