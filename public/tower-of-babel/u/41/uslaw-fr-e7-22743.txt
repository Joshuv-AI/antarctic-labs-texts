Superior Studs, LLC, a Wholly Owned Subsidiary of Swanson Group 
Manufacturing, LLC, Glide, OR; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on August 16, 2007 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Superior 
Studs, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Swanson Group Manufacturing, 
LLC, Glide, Oregon. The workers at the subject facility produced stud 
lumber.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 8th day of November 2007.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
