FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[FRS 16825]


Sunshine Act Meeting

June 2, 2020.

TIME AND DATE:  Open Commission Meeting, Tuesday, June 9, 2020.
    The Federal Communications Commission will hold an Open Meeting on 
Tuesday, June 9, 2020, which is scheduled to commence at 10:30 a.m.

PLACE:  Due to the current COVID-19 pandemic and related agency 
telework and headquarters access policies, this meeting will be in a 
wholly electronic format and will be open to the public on the internet 
via live feed from the FCC's web page at www.fcc.gov/live and on the 
FCC's YouTube channel.

STATUS:  Open to the public.
