Rockwell Automation, Dublin, GA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 3, 2003, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Rockwell 
Automation, Dublin, Georgia (TA-W-52,744).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 11th day of September, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
