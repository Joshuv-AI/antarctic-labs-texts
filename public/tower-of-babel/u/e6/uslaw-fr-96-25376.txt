Prospective Payment Assessment Commission


Meetings

    Notice is hereby given of the meetings of the Prospective Payment 
Assessment Commission on Tuesday and Wednesday, October 8 and 9, 1996, 
at the Madison Hotel, 15th & M Streets, NW, Washington, DC, 202/862-
1600.
    The Full Commission will convene at 9:00 a.m. on October 8, 1996, 
and adjourn at approximately 5:00 p.m. On Wednesday, October 9, 1996, 
the meeting will convene at 9:00 a.m. and adjourn at approximately 3:30 
p.m. The meetings will be held in Executive Chambers 1, 2, and 3 each 
day.
    All meetings are open the public.
Donald A. Young,
Executive Director.
