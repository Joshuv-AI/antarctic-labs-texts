DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

TPC Corporation; Notice of Filing

December 5, 1996.
    Take notice that on October 15, 1996, TPC Corporation tendered for 
filing its Code of Conduct in compliance with the Commission's 
September 30, 1996, order issued in this docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in 
accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice 
and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before December 13, 1996. Protests will 
be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action 
to be taken, but will not serve to make

[[Page 65203]]

protestants parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a 
party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on 
file with the Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
