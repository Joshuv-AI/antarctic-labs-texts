DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 27, 2002, 9 a.m. to June 
27, 2002, 10 a.m., Melrose Hotel, 2430 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW., 
Washington, DC 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on 
June 12, 2002, 67 FR 40326-40329.
    The meeting will be held on June 28, 2002. The time and location 
remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 18, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
