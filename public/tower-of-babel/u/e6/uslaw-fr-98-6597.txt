DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Intent To Grant an Exclusive Patent License

    Pursuant to the provisions of Part 404 of Title 37, Code of Federal 
Regulations, which implements Pub. L. 96-517, the Department of the Air 
Force announces its intention to grant Air Fletcher, corporation 
application pending in California, an exclusive license under: United 
States Provisional Application Serial No. TBD filed in the name of 
Fletcher A Burns for a ``Structural Mount For Head Up Display.''
    The license described above will be granted unless an objection 
thereto, together with a request for an opportunity to be heard, if 
desired, is received in writing by the addressee set forth below within 
sixty (60) days from the date of publication of this Notice. 
Information concerning the application may be obtained, on request, 
from the same addressee.
    All communications concerning this Notice should be sent to: Mr. 
Randy Heald, Patent Attorney, Secretary of the Air Force, Office of the 
General Counsel, SAF/GCQ, 1501 Wilson Blvd., Suite 805, Arlington, VA 
22209-2403, (703) 696-9037.
Barbara Carmichael,
Alternate Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
