Correction

    FR document 94-1557 was published in the issue of Monday, January 
31, 1994, beginning on page 4500. This document was a request for 
information on proposed exemptions from the list of regulated 
substances and on the impact of proposed accident prevention 
requirements. It was published in the Notices section of the Federal 
Register. It should have appeared in the Proposed Rules section.
    In addition, the CFR citation should be added to the heading of the 
document as set forth above.
