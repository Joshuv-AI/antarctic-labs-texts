DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. PR93-3-000]

 

Montana Power Company; Informal Settlement Conference

March 8, 1994.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Monday, March 21, 1994, at 11 A.M. 
in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Mark Hegerle at (202) 208-0927.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
