Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council (MAFMC); Public Meeting; 
Correction

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of scoping meetings; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council is holding public 
scoping meetings to gather input on the development of the Excessive 
Shares Amendment to the Surfclam and Ocean Quahog FMP. Input obtained 
from these meetings will be considered by the Council when developing 
this Amendment.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Christopher Moore, Executive 
Director, Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council, telephone: (302) 
526-5255.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register on June 23, 2017, in FR Doc. 2017-13152, on 
page 28643, in the first column, the SUMMARY is corrected as set forth 
above.
