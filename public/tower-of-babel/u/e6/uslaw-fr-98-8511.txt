NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY HOLDING THE MEETING: National Labor Relations Board.

TIME AND DATE: 3:00 p.m., Tuesday, March 17, 1998.

PLACE: Board Conference Room, Eleventh Floor, 1099 Fourteenth St., 
N.W., Washington, D.C. 20570.

STATUS: Closed to public observation pursuant to 5 U.S.C. Section 
552b(c)(2) (internal personnel rules and practices); and (c)(6)

unwarranted invasion of personal privacy).
