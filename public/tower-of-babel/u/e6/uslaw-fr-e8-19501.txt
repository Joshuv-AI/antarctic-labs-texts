-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The General Services Administration (GSA) is issuing a 
correction to GSAR Amendment 2008-01; GSAR Case 2006-G505; (Change 22), 
which was published in the Federal Register at 73 FR 46202, August 8, 
2008.

DATES: Effective Date: August 22, 2008.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The Regulatory Secretariat (VPR), Room 
4041, GS Building, Washington, DC, 20405, (202) 501-4755, for 
information pertaining to status or publication schedules. For 
clarification of content, contact Mr. Ernest Woodson, Procurement 
Analyst, at (202) 501-3775. Please cite GSAR Amendment 2008-01; GSAR 
Case 2006-G505; (Change 22); Correction.

 Correction
    In the final rule document appearing in the issue of August 8, 
2008:
    1. On page 46202, second column, last paragraph under 
``Background,'' remove ``deleted in its entirety because of its 
potential for conflict'' and add ``revised to ensure consistency'' in 
its place.
