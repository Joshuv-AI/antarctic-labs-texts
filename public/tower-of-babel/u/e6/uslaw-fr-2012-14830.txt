Petition for Exemption

Docket No.: FAA-2012-0587
Petitioner: Fern Case
Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR Sec.  121.317(f) and (k)

Description of Relief Sought:

    The relief sought would allow Ms. Fern Case to use the aircraft 
lavatory while the Fasten Seat Belt sign is illuminated. Without this 
exemption, Ms. Case would not be able to fly due to a functional 
disorder requiring access to the lavatory without reasonable notice.
