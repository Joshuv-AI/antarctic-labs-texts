Notice of Proposed Withdrawal and Opportunity for Public Meeting; 
Montana; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published a Notice of Proposed 
Legislative Withdrawal in the Federal Register on August 7, 2007. The 
document contained an incorrect legal description.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sandra Ward, 406-896-5052.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of August 7, 2007, in FR Doc. E7-15366, on 
page 44174, in the third column, the legal description under Sec. 3, 
which reads ``S\1/2\N\1/4\'', is corrected to read ``S\1/2\N\1/2\'', 
and under Sec. 4, which reads ``S\1/2\N\1/4\'', is corrected to read 
``S\1/2\N\1/2\''.

    Dated: August 14, 2007.
Cindy Staszak,
Chief, Branch of Land Resources.
