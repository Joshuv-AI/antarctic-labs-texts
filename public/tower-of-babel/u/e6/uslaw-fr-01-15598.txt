DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Parole Commission


Sunshine Act Meeting

ACTION: Public Announcement of meeting pursuant to the Government in 
the Sunshine Act (Public Law 94-409; 5 U.S.C. Section 552b).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Agency Holding Meeting: Department of Justice, United States Parole 
Commission.

Time and Date: 9:30 a.m., Friday, June 22, 2001.

Place: 5550 Friendship Blvd., Fourth Floor, Chevy Chase, MD 20815.

Status: Open.

Matters to be Considered: The following matters have been placed on the 
agenda for the open Parole Commission meeting:
    1. Approval of minutes of previous Commission meeting.
    2. Reports from the Chairman, Commissioners, Legal, Chief of Staff, 
Case Operations, and Administrative Sections.
    3. Discussion on recommended revision of 28 CFR Sec. 2.81(d) 
concerning the procedures for reparole hearings in District of Columbia 
Code cases.

Agency Contact: Sam Robertson, Case Operations, United States Parole 
Commission, (301) 492-5962.

    Dated: June 15, 2001.
Michael A. Stover,
General Counsel, U.S. Parole Commission.
