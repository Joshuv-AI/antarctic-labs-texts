DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 1, 2009, 11 a.m. to 
October 1, 2009, 12 p.m., One Washington Circle Hotel, One Washington 
Circle, NW., Washington, DC 20037 which was published in the Federal 
Register on September 15, 2009, 74 FR 47262-47264.
    The meeting was cancelled due to administration problems.

    Dated: September 21, 2009.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
