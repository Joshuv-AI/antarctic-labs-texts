DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, Translational and Basic 
Research Early Lesions (U54 and U24), March 02, 2022, 09:00 a.m. to 
March 03, 2022, 06:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute at Shady Grove, 
Room 7W108, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, Maryland 20850 which 
was published in the Federal Register on December 20, 2021, FR Doc 
2021-27482, 86 FR 71902.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting date from March 
2-3, 2022 to March 15-16, 2022. The meeting times and location will 
stay the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: January 5, 2022.
David W. Freeman,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
