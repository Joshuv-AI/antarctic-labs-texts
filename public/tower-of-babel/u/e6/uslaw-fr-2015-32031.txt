DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the 2016 Mark Twain Commemorative Coin Program

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing pricing for the 2016 Mark 
Twain Commemorative Coin Program as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                           Introductory
                  Coin                         price       Regular price
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Silver Proof............................          $45.95          $50.95
Silver Uncirculated.....................           44.95           49.95
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Products containing gold coins will be priced according to the 
Pricing of Numismatic and Commemorative Gold and Platinum Products Grid 
posted at www.usmint.gov.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ann Bailey, Products Manager for 
Numismatic and Bullion; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW., 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.

    Dated: December 15, 2015.
