Revisions to the California State Implementation Plan, Feather 
River Air Quality Management District; Correction

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Direct final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) is correcting a 
direct final rule that appeared in the Federal Register on July 8, 
2015. The document approved revisions to various sections of the 
California State Implementation Plan (SIP). This document adds the 
appropriate amendatory language to Sec.  52.220, Subpart F.

DATES: Effective on September 8, 2015.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kevin Gong, EPA Region IX, (415) 972-
3073, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The EPA published a document in the Federal 
Register on July 8, 2015, (80 FR 38959) approving revisions to various 
sections of the California State Implementation Plan (SIP) in Sec.  
52.220, Subpart F. This correction adds the appropriate amendatory 
language.

Correction

    In FR Doc. 2015-16627 appearing on page 38964 in the Federal
