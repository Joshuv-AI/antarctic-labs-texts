High Density Airports; Allocation of Slots; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the notice of proposed 
rulemaking published in the Federal Register on January 12, 1999 (64 FR 
2086). That document proposed rulemaking regarding takeoff and landing 
slots and slot allocation procedures at certain High Density Traffic 
Airports.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lorelei D. Peter, (202) 267-3073.

    In proposed rule FR Doc. 99-621 beginning on page 2086 in the
