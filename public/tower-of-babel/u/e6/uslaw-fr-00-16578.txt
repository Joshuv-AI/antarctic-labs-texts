DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE


Office of the Secretary; Meeting of the DoD Healthcare Quality 
Initiative Review Panel

AGENCY: Department of Defense.

ACTION: An executive/administration meeting for DoD Healthcare Quality 
Initiatives Review Panel has been scheduled for July 6 & 7, 2000.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice set forth the meeting of the DoD Healthcare 
Quality Initiatives Review Panel. Notice of meeting is required under 
The Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463).

DATES: July 6 & 7, 2000.

ADDRESSES: Sheraton Crystal City, 1800 Jefferson Davis Hwy., Arlington, 
VA 22202.

TIME: July 6th, 5:30 p.m. to 8:30 p.m.; July 7th, 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 
p.m.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For information please contact Gia 
Edmonds at (703) 933-8325.
