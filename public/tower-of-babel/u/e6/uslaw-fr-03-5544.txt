Southern Georgia Cap Company dba Georgia Headwear and Apparel, 
Waycross, GA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 6, 2003 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Southern 
Georgia Cap Company, doing business as Georgia Headwear and Apparel, 
Waycross, Georgia.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation would serve no useful purpose and 
the investigation has been terminated.
