DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the 2016 American Liberty Silver Medals

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the price of the 2016 
American Liberty Silver Medals. Each medal will be priced at $34.95. 
Two silver medals will be offered--one from the United States Mint at 
West Point and one from the United States Mint at San Francisco.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Cathy Olson (Laperle), Marketing 
Specialist; Numismatic and Bullion Directorate; United States Mint; 801 
9th Street NW.; Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7519.
