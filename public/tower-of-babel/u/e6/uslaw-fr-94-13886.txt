DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[NAFTA-00083]

 

Miami Trim, Inc; Miami, FL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to title V of the North American Free Trade Agreement 
Implementation Act (P.L. 103-182) concerning transitional adjustment 
assistance, hereinafter called (NAFTA-TAA), and in accordance with 
section 250(a), subchapter D, chapter 2, title II, of the Trade Act of 
1974, as amended (19 U.S.C. 2273), an investigation was initiated on 
April 18, 1994, in response to a petition filed by the company on 
behalf of workers at Miami Trim, Inc. in Miami, Florida. The firm 
produces buttons and trim for apparel.
    In a letter dated May 20, 1994, the petitioner requested that the 
petition for NAFTA-TAA be withdrawn. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 25th day of May, 1994.
Marvin M. Fooks,
Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
