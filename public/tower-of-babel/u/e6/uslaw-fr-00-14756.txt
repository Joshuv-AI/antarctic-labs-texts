COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts is schedule for 
June 15, 2000, at 10 a.m. in the Commission's offices at the National 
Building Museum (Pension Building), Suite 312, Judiciary Square, 441 F 
Street, N.W., Washington, D.C., 20001-2728. Items of discussion will 
include designs for projects affecting the appearance of Washington, 
D.C., including buildings and parks.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and request to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call 202-504-2200. 
Individuals requiring sign language interpretation for the hearing 
impaired should contact the Secretary at least 10 days before the 
meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, D.C., May 31, 2000.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
