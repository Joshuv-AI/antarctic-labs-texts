DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Aging Transport Systems Rulemaking Advisory Committee Meeting

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces a public meeting of the FAA's Aging 
Transport Systems Rulemaking Advisory Committee (ATSRAC).

DATES: The FAA will hold the meeting on January 22 and 23, 2003, in 
Savannah, Georgia from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on the 22nd and from 8:30 
a.m. to noon on the 23rd.

ADDRESSES: Hyatt Regency Savannah, 2 West Bay Street, Savannah, 
Georgia, 31401.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shirley Stroman, Office of Rulemaking, 
ARM-208, FAA, 800 Independence Avenue, SW, Washington, DC 20591; 
telephone (202)267-7470; fax (202)267-5075; or e-mail 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The ATSRAC will meet at the Hyatt Regency 
Savannah at the address shown under the ADDRESSES heading in this 
notice. The meeting agenda will include the following:
    [sbull] Presentation of Working Group 10's (Small Transport 
Airplane

[[Page 155]]

Harmonization Working Group) final report
    [sbull] Discussion of comments on the FAA's Intrusive Inspection 
Recommendations Final Report
    [sbull] Discussion of ATSRAC's recommendation to the FAA on an 
Enhanced Zonal Analysis Procedure implementation schedule
    [sbull] Update on FAA's Research and Development Program
    [sbull] Presentation by Robins Air Force Base: Lubricant 
Effectiveness Study for Corrosion Protection and Improved Reliability 
of Avionics
    The meeting is open to the public, but attendance will be limited 
to the availability of meeting room space. The FAA will make the 
following services available if you request them by January 14, 2003:
    [sbull] Teleconferencing
    [sbull] Sign and oral interpretation
    [sbull] A listening device
    Individuals using the teleconferencing service and calling from 
outside the Washington, DC metropolitan area will be responsible for 
paying long distance charges. To arrange for any of the above services, 
contact the person listed under the FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT 
heading of this notice.
    The public may present written statements to the Committee at any 
time by providing 20 copies to the Committee's Executive Director or by 
bringing the copies to the meeting. Public statements will be 
considered if time permits.

    Issued in Washington, DC on December 23, 2002.
Anthony F. Fazio,
Director, Office of Rulemaking.
