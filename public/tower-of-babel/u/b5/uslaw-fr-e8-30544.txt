FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 08-2677; MB Docket No. 08-126; RM-11458]


Television Broadcasting Services; Canton, Ohio

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission requests comments on a channel substitution 
proposed by Trinity Christian Center of Santa Ana, Inc., d/b/a Trinity 
Broadcast Network (``Trinity''), the licensee of WDLI-DT, post-
transition DTV channel 39, Canton, Ohio. Trinity requests the 
substitution of DTV channel 49 for post-transition DTV channel 39 at 
Canton.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before January 22, 2009, and reply 
comments on or before February 6, 2009.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Office of the Secretary, 
445 12th Street, SW., Washington, DC 20554. In addition to filing 
comments with the FCC, interested parties should serve counsel for 
petitioner as follows: Colby M. May, Esq., P.C., 205 3rd Street, SE., 
Washington, DC 20003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Brown, [email protected], 
Media Bureau, (202) 418-1600.
