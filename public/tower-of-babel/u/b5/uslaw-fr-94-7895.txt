DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. CP88-195-016]

 

CNG Transmission Corp.; Proposed Changes in FERC Gas Tariff

March 29, 1994.
    Take notice that on March 24, 1994, CNG Transmission Corporation 
(CNG) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Original 
Volume No. 2A, the following tariff sheets, with an effective date of 
October 1, 1993:

Sub. First Revised Sheet No. 407,
Sub. First Revised Sheet No. 426,
Sub. First Revised Sheet No. 444,
Sheet Nos. 420-422,
Sheet No. 440,
Sheet No. 441,
Sheet No. 448.

    CNG states that the purpose of this filing is to correct the 
pagination of certain tariff sheets filed in this proceeding on March 
14, 1994.
    CNG states that copies of this filing were served on the affected 
customers.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol 
Street, NE., Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with rule 211 of the 
Commission's Rule of Practice and Procedure, 18 CFR 385.211. All such 
protests should be filed on or before April 5, 1994. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
