DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Library of Medicine; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Library of Medicine Special Emphasis Panel, November 18, 2002, 9 a.m. 
to November 19, 2002, 5 p.m., which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 29, 2002, 67 FR 55414.
    The meeting will be rescheduled to January 13-14, 2003, to allow 
for sufficient time for an optimal review process. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: November 7, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
