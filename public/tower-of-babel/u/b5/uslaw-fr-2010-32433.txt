JUDICIAL CONFERENCE OF THE UNITED STATES


Meeting of the Judicial Conference Advisory Committee on Rules of 
Evidence

AGENCY: Judicial Conference of the United States Advisory Committee on 
Rules of Evidence.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Advisory Committee on Rules of Evidence will hold a one-

participation.

ADDRESS:  University of Pennsylvania, Law School, 3400 Chestnut Street, 
Philadelphia, PA 19104.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Peter G. McCabe, Secretary, Rules 
Committee Support Office, Administrative Office of the United States 
Courts, Washington, DC 20544, telephone (202) 502-1820.
