NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Biological Sciences; Notice of Meeting

    In accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-
463, as amended), the National Science Foundation announces the 
following meeting:
    Name: Advisory Committee for Biological Sciences (#1110).
    Date and Time: April 25, 2016; 8:30AM--5:00PM. April 26, 2016; 
8:30AM--4:00PM.
    Place: National Science Foundation, 4201 Wilson Boulevard, Room 
1235, Arlington, VA 22230.
    Please contact Rachel Evans at [email protected] to obtain a visitor 
badge. All visitors to the NSF will be required to show photo ID to 
obtain a badge.
    Type of Meeting: Open.
    Contact Person: Charles Liarakos, National Science Foundation, 4201 
Wilson Boulevard, Room 605, Arlington, VA 22230; Tel No.: (703) 292-
8400
    Purpose of Meeting: The Advisory Committee for the Directorate for 
Biological Sciences (BIO) provides advice, recommendations, and 
oversight concerning major program emphases, directions, and goals for 
the research-related activities of the divisions that make up BIO.
    Agenda: Agenda items will include Leading Edge, NEON science, 
Portfolio analysis, the Strategic Vision for the Biological Sciences, 
and other matters relevant to the Directorate for Biological Sciences.

    Dated: March 28, 2016.
Crystal Robinson,
Committee Management Officer.
