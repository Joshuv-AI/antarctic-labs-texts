FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 00-21591) published on pages 
51618 and 51619 of the issue for Thursday, August 24, 2000.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco heading, the entry 
for BOU Bancorp, Inc., Ogden, Utah, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco (Maria Villanueva, 
Consumer Regulation Group) 101 Market Street, San Francisco, California 
94105-1579:
    1. BOU Bancorp, Inc., Ogden, Utah; to become a bank holding company 
by acquiring 100 percent of the voting shares of Bank of Utah, Ogden, 
Utah.
    Comments on this application must be received by September 15, 
2000.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, August 29, 
2000.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
