DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

18 CFR Part 35


Filing of Rate Schedules and Tariffs

CFR Correction

    In Title 18 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 1 to 399, 
revised as of April 1, 2015, on page 275, in Sec.  35.11, in the first 
sentence, the phrase ``a rate schedule, tariff, or service agreement,'' 
is revised to read, ``a rate schedule or tariff, tariff or service 
agreement,''; and the phrase ``the rate schedule or tariff would become 
effective'' is revised to read ``the rate schedule or tariff, tariff or 
service agreement would become effective''. And, in the second 
sentence, the phrase ``under such rate schedule or tariff,'' is revised 
to read ``under such rate schedule or tariff, tariff or service 
agreement,''.
