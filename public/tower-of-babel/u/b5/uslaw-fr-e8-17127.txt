Best Textiles International Ltd., Formerly Known as Best: Artex 
LLC, Highland, IL; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 30, 2008, in response to a worker 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Best Textiles International 
Ltd., Highland, Illinois.
    Workers of Best: Artex LLC, Highland, Illinois, were certified 
eligible to apply for trade adjustment assistance under petition number 
TA-W-61,393, expiring on May 24, 2009. This certification was amended 
on July 9, 2008, to include workers whose wages were reported under the 
corporate name of Best Textiles International Ltd., Highland, Illinois, 
the subject of this investigation.
    Since the petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, (TA-W-61,393, amended), further investigation in this 
case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 16th day of July 2008.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
