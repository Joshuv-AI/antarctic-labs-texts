INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 731-TA-856 (Final)]


Certain Ammonium Nitrate From Russia

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Suspension of investigation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 16, 2000, the Department of Commerce published notice 
in the Federal Register of the suspension of its antidumping 
investigation on certain ammonium nitrate from Russia (65 FR 37759). 
The basis for the suspension is an agreement between the Department of 
Commerce and the Ministry of Trade of the Russian Federation accounting 
for substantially all imports of ammonium nitrate from Russia, wherein 
the Ministry of Trade has agreed to restrict exports of ammonium 
nitrate from all Russian producers/exporters to the United States and 
to ensure that such exports are sold at or above the agreed reference 
price. Accordingly, the U.S. International Trade Commission gives 
notice of the suspension of its antidumping investigation involving 
imports from Russia of certain ammonium nitrate, provided for in 
subheading 3102.30.00 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United 
States.

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 16, 2000.
