Equity Options With Flexible Terms; Qualified Covered Call 
Treatment; Suspension of Rule

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Treasury.

ACTION: Suspension of final rule and announcement of new effective 
date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document suspends a final rule that was published in the 
Federal Register on Monday, April 29, 2002 (67 FR 20896) providing 
guidance on the application of the rules governing qualified covered 
calls.

DATES: The final rule published April 29, 2002 (67 FR 20896) is 
suspended effective April 29, 2002. That rule will be effective July 
29, 2002.
