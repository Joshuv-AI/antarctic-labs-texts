DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Research Resources; Notice of Cancellation of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the meeting of the 
Board of Scientific Counselors, National Center for Research Resources, 
July 9-10, 1997, Building 31, Conference Room 3B13, National Institutes 
of Health, Bethesda, Maryland, which was published in the Federal 
Register on May 14, 1997, (62 FR 26550).
    The meeting is canceled due to scheduling conflicts of several 
members of the Board and will be rescheduled at a later date.

    Dated: June 18, 1997.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Committee Management Officer, NIH.
