OFFICE OF THE UNITED STATES TRADE REPRESENTATIVE


Notice of Change in Meeting Time of the Industry Sector Advisory 
Committee on Small and Minority Business (ISAC-14)

AGENCY: Office of the United States Trade Representative.

ACTION: Notice of change in meeting time.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A notice was published in the Federal Register dated March 28, 
2000, Volume 65, Number 60, page 16450, announcing a meeting of the 
Industry Sector Advisory Committee on Small and Minority Business 
(ISAC-14) scheduled for April 10, 2000, from 9:30 a.m. to 2:45 p.m. The 
meeting was to be opened to the public from 9:30 a.m. to 10:30 a.m. and 
again from 11 a.m. to 2:45 p.m. and closed to the public from 10:30 
a.m. to 11 a.m. However, due to scheduling conflicts the meeting has 
been rescheduled from 9:15 a.m. to 3 p.m. The meeting will be closed to 
the public from 9:15 a.m. until 10 a.m. and opened to the public from 
10 a.m. to 3 p.m.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ladan Manteghi, Office of the United 
States Trade Representative, (202) 395-6120.

Pate Felts,
Assistant U.S. Trade Representative.
