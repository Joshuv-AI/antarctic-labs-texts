DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. CP94-161-000]

 

Avoca Natural Gas Storage; Site Visit

April 29, 1994.
    On May 4 and 5, 1994, the Office of Pipeline Regulation staff will 
conduct a site visit with representatives of Avoca Natural Gas Storage 
for the facilities proposed in the Avoca Gas Storage Field Project. The 
proposed facilities are near the towns of Avoca and Wallace in Steuben 
County, New York.
    Parties to the proceeding may attend. Those planning to attend must 
provide their own transportation. For further information, call Steven 
G. Grape, (202) 208-0812.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
