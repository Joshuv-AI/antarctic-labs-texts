Radio Broadcasting Services; Lahaina and Waianae, HI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, 68 FR 2733 
(January 21, 2003), this Report and Order reallots Channel 266C, FM 
Station KLHI, Lahaina, Hawaii, to Waianae, Hawaii, and modifies Station 
KLHI's license accordingly. The coordinates for Channel 266C at 
Waianae, Hawaii, are 21-23-51 NL and 158-06-01 WL, with a site 
restriction of 10.7 kilometers (6.6 miles) southeast of Waianae.

DATES: Effective May 23, 2005.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
