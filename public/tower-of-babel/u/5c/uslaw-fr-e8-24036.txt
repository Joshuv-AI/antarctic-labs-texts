NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice (08-076)]


NASA Advisory Committee; Notice of Renewal.

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice of Renewal of the Charter for the NASA Advisory Council.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to sections 14(b)(1) and 9(c) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), and after consultation with the 
Committee Management Secretariat, General Services Administration, the 
Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration 
(NASA) has determined that a renewal of the Charter for the Agency-
established NASA Advisory Council is necessary and in the public 
interest in connection with the performance of duties imposed upon NASA 
by law. The purpose of the NASA Advisory Council is to provide advice 
and make recommendations to the NASA Administrator on Agency programs, 
policies, plans, financial controls and other matters pertinent to the 
Agency's responsibilities. The structure and duties of this Council is 
unchanged.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. P. Diane Rausch, Advisory 
Committee Management Officer, Office of External Relations, National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration, Washington, DC 20546, 202-358-
4510.

    Dated: October 2, 2008.
P. Diane Rausch,
Advisory Committee Management Officer, National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration.
