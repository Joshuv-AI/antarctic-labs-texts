NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Advisory Committee for Integrative Activities; Cancellation of 
Meeting

AGENCY: National Science Foundation.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

    The National Science Foundation published a notice in the Federal 
Register September 6, 2024, in FR Doc. 2024-20063 at 89 FR 72901, 
concerning a meeting of the Advisory Committee for Integrative 
Activities. The meeting scheduled for Monday, September 30, 2024, at 1 
p.m. (ET) is cancelled.
