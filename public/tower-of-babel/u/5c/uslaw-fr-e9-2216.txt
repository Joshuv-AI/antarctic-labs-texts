City of Quincy, IL; Notice of Competing Preliminary Permit 
Application Accepted for Filing and Soliciting Comments and Motions To 
Intervene

January 27, 2009.
    On November 10, 2008, the City of Quincy, Illinois filed an 
application, pursuant to section 4(f) of the Federal Power Act, 
proposing to study the feasibility of the Quincy Area Hydropower 
Project, which would be located near the town of Clarksville on the 
Mississippi River at the existing U.S. Army Corps of Engineers' Lock 
and Dam No. 24 and Reservoir in Calhoun County, Illinois and Pike 
County, Missouri. The proposed project would utilize federal lands.
    The proposed Quincy Area Hydropower Project would utilize the U.S. 
Army Corps of Engineers' Lock and Dam No. 24 and would consist of the 
following new facilities: (1) An intake structure, (2) a powerhouse 
containing 30 generating units with a total installed capacity of 15 
MW, (3) a 2.7-mile-long, 34.5 kV underground transmission line, 
connecting to an existing power line, and (4) appurtenant facilities. 
The project would have an annual generation of 88 gigawatt-hours, which 
would be sold to a local utility.
