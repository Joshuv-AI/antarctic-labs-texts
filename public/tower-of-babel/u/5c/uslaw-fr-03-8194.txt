COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS


Notice of Meeting

    The next meeting of the Commission of Fine Arts, that was scheduled 
for 17 April 2003 has been rescheduled for 22 April 2003 at 10 a.m. in 
the Commission's offices at the National Building Museum, Suite 312, 
Judiciary Square, 401 F Street, NW., Washington, DC 20001-2728. Items 
of discussion affecting the appearance of Washington, DC, may include 
buildings, parks and memorials.
    Draft agendas and additional information regarding the Commission 
are available on our Web site: www.cfa.gov. Inquiries regarding the 
agenda and requests to submit written or oral statements should be 
addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, Commission of Fine Arts, 
at the above address or call (202) 504-2200. Individuals requiring sign 
language interpretation for the hearing impaired should contact the 
Secretary at least 10 days before the meeting date.

    Dated in Washington, DC, on the 31st of March, 2003.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
