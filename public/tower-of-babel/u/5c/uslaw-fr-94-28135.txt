DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Project No. 11367-001]

 

Peak Power Corp., Kvaerner Venture, Inc. and Las Vegas Energy 
Storage Limited Partnership; Postponement of Scoping Meetings

November 8, 1994.
    The public and agency meetings, and the site visit, for the Sheep 
Mountain Hydroelectric Project (FERC No. 11367-001), which were 
scheduled for November 15 and 16, 1994, are postponed. (59 FR 55089), 
November 3, 1994). Another notice will be issued as soon as the 
meetings are rescheduled.
    If you have any question on this matter, please call Mike 
Strzelecki at 202-219-2827.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
