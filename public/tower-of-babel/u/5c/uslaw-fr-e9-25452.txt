DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Performance Review Board Members

    Title 5, U.S.C. Section 4314(c)(4) of the Civil Service Reform Act 
of 1978, Public Law 95-454, requires notice of appointment of 
individuals to serve as a member of the Performance Review Board shall 
be published in the Federal Register.
    The following individuals are hereby appointed to serve on 
Performance Review Boards within the Department of Health and Human 
Services. These individuals supplement membership on existing 
Performance Review Boards.

Office of the Secretary

    Moulds, Donald, Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary.
    Monahan, John, Director, Office of Global Health Affairs.

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

    Branche, Christine, Associate Director for NIOSH.

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA)

    Morford, Thomas G., Associate Administrator, Office of Operations.

Indian Health Service (IHS)

    Karol M.D., Susan, Chief Medical Officer.

    Dated: October 16, 2009.
Antonia T. Harris,
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Human Resources, Department of Health 
and Human Services.
