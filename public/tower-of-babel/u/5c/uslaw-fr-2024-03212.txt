National Library of Medicine; Notice of Meeting Amended Notice of 
Meeting

Biomedical Library, Informatics and Data Science Review Committee, May 
17-18, 2024, which was published in the Federal Register on December 
21, 2023, 2023, 88 FR 88407.
    The meeting will be amended to change the meeting dates from March 
7-8, 2024, to March 7, 2024. The meeting will be closed to the public.
