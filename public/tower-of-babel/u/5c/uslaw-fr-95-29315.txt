FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Revocations

following freight forwarder licenses are revoked pursuant to section 19

of the Commission pertaining to the licensing of ocean freight 
forwarders, effective on the corresponding revocation dates shown 
below:

Address: 400 South 4th Street, Ste. 206, Minneapolis Grain Exchange, 
Minneapolis, MN 55415
