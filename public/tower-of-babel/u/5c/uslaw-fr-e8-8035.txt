    1. One (1) Insurance Appeal. Closed pursuant to Exemption (6).
    2. Request for Review under Sections 747.306 and 747.308 of NCUA's 
Rules and Regulations. Closed pursuant to Exemptions (6) and (8).
    3. One (1) Merger Application under Section 205 of the Federal 
Credit Union Act. Closed pursuant to Exemption (8).
    4. One (1) Administrative Action under Section 208 of the Federal 
Credit Union Act. Closed pursuant to Exemptions (8), (9)(A)(ii), and 
(B).
