DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. ER95-1049-000]


Gateway Energy, Inc.; Notice of Filing

June 19, 1995.
    Take notice that on June 12, 1995, Gateway Energy, Inc. tendered 
for filing an amendment to its May 15, 1995, filing in the above-
referenced docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protects said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, N.E., Washington, DC 
20426, in accordance with Rule 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before June 30, 1995. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
