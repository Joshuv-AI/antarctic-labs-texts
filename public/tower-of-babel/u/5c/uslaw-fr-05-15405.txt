Parole Commission


Pursuant To The Government In The Sunshine Act (Public Law 94-
409) (5 U.S.C. 552b)

Commission.

Time and Date: 2:30 p.m., Tuesday, August 2, 2005.

Place: 5550 Friendship Blvd., Fourth Floor, Chevy Chase, MD 20815.

Status: Open.
