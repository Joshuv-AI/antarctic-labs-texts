DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Mental Health; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Mental 
Health Services Research Committee, November 7, 2019, 8:00 a.m. to 
November 7, 2019 p.m., 05:00 p.m., The Dupont Hotel, 1500 New Hampshire 
Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20036 which was published in the Federal 
Register on August 201, 2019, 84 FR 43150.
    This notice is to amend the date of the Mental Health Service 
Research Committee (SERV) meeting from November 7, 2019 to November 8, 
2019. The meeting times and location remain the same. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: August 27, 2019.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
