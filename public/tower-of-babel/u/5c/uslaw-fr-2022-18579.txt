COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the West Virginia Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the West Virginia Advisory 
Committee. The meeting scheduled for Tuesday, September 6, 2022, at 
11:30 a.m. ET is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of 
Monday, April 11, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-07663, in the third column of 
page 21091 and the first column of page 21092.
