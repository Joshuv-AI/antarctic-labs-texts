HARRY S. TRUMAN SCHOLARSHIP FOUNDATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Meeting of the Trustees and Officers of the 
Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation

May 7, 2004, 9:30-11 a.m., U.S. Capitol, Room HC-8
I. Call to Order.
II. Welcome: President Albright.
III. Introduction of new Trustees and presentation of Certificates 
of Appointment.
IV. Approval of minutes of Meeting of September 3, 2003.
V. Report from the Executive Secretary: Ratification of the 2004 
Truman Scholars.
VI. Financial Report of the Foundation.
VII. Discussion of the Report by the Task Force on Scholar 
Accountability.
VIII. Progress Report on the Task Force on Reinventing the Truman 
Scholarship Foundation.
IX. Recommendations from President Albright.
X. Old Business/New Business.
XI. Adjournment.

Louis H. Blair,
Executive Secretary.
