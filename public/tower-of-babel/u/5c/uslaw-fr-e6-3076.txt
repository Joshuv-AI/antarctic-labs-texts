ABCO Rents of Clinton, Clinton, NC; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 7, 2006, in response to a 
worker petition filed by a company spokesman on behalf of workers at 
ABCO Rents of Clinton, Clinton, North Carolina.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 9th day of February 2006.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
