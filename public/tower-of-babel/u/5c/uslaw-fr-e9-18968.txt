DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Thrift Supervision


Peoples Community Bank, West Chester, OH; Notice of Appointment 
of Receiver

    Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to the authority contained in 
section 5(d)(2) of the Home Owners' Loan Act, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision (OTS) has duly appointed the Federal Deposit Insurance 
Corporation as sole Receiver for Peoples Community Bank, West Chester, 
Ohio (OTS No. 08097), on July 31, 2009.

    Dated: August 4, 2009.

By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
