NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

[Docket No. 40-8943-MLA; ASLBP No. 07-859-03-MLA-BD01]


Notice of Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Reconstitution: Crow 
Butte Resources, Inc. (North Trend Expansion Project)

    Pursuant to 10 CFR 2.313(c) and 2.321(b), the Atomic Safety and 
Licensing Board in the above-captioned Crow Butte Resources, Inc. 
license amendment proceeding is hereby reconstituted by appointing 
Administrative Judge Craig M. White to serve as a Licensing Board 
member.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Judge White's appointment in this proceeding is necessitated 
by the recent retirement of Administrative Judge Richard E. 
Wardwell, who previously served on this Licensing Board.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    All correspondence, documents, and other materials shall continue 
to be filed in accordance with the NRC E-Filing rule. See 10 CFR 2.302 
et seq.

    Dated: October 10, 2019.
Edward R. Hawkens,
Chief Administrative Judge, Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel, 
Rockville, Maryland.
