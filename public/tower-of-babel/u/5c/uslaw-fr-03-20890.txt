SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster 3526]


State of Indiana; Amendment 2

    In accordance with the notice received from the Department of

[[Page 48981]]

Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective 
August 6, 2003, the above numbered declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning on July 4, 
2003 and continuing through August 6, 2003.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is September 9, 2003, and for 
economic injury the deadline is April 12, 2004.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: August 8, 2003.
Cheri L. Cannon,
Acting Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
