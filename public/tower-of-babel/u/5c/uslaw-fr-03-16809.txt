SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


National Small Business Development Center Advisory Board; Public 
Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration National Small Business 
Development Center Advisory Board will hold a public meeting on Sunday, 
July 20, 2003, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the Westin Riverwalk Hotel, San 
Antonio, Texas to discuss such matters as may be presented by members, 
staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others present. For 
further information, please write or call Evelyn Y. Prentice, U.S. 
Small Business Administration, 409 Third Street, SW., Sixth Floor, 
Washington, DC 20416, telephone number (202) 205-6185.

Candace Stoltz,
Director, Advisory Councils.
