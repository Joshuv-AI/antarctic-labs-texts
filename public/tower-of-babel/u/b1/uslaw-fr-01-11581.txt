FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY HOLDING THE MEETING: Federal Maritime Commission.

TIME AND DATE: 10 a.m.--May 10, 2001.

PLACE: 800 North Capitol Street, N.W., First Floor Hearing Room, 
Washington, D.C.

STATUS: A portion of the meeting will be open to the public. The 
remainder of the meeting will be closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The open portion of the meeting:
    1. Shipper Export Declaration (SED) Fees
    2. Commission Alternative Dispute Resolution Program
    The closed portion of the meeting:
    1. Executive Tug Franchises--Marine Terminal Operators Serving the 
Lower Mississippi River
    2. Exclusive Tug Arrangements in Florida Ports

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Bryant L. VanBrakle, Secretary, 
(202) 523-5725.

Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
