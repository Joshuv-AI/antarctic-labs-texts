SUMMARY: The Coast Guard published a document in the Federal Register 
on May 4, 2004 (69 FR 24513), providing required notice of substantive 
rules issued by the Coast Guard and temporarily effective between 
January 1, 2004 and March 31, 2004. The document incorrectly used 
docket number USCG-2004-17636. This document revises the docket number.

DATES: This correction is effective May 17, 2004.
