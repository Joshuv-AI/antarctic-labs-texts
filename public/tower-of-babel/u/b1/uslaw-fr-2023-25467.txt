Foreign-Trade Zone 26; Application for Subzone; Helena 
Industries, LLC; Cordele, Georgia; Correction

    The Federal Register notice published on November 14, 2023 (88 FR 
77952) regarding the subzone application for Helena Industries, LLC, 
located in Cordele, Georgia, is corrected as follows:
    In the first paragraph, first sentence, the grantee organization 
should read ``Georgia Foreign-Trade Zone, Inc., grantee of FTZ 26''.
    For further information contact Christopher Kemp at 
[email protected].
