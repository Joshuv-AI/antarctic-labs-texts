DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Navy


Meeting of the Chief of Naval Operations (CNO) Executive Panel; 
Correction

AGENCY: Department of the Navy, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of closed meeting; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Navy published a document in the Federal 
Register of April 26, 2005, announcing a closed meeting of the CNO 
Executive Panel. The document contained incorrect date and time.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Lieutenant Commander Christopher 
Corgnati, CNO Executive Panel, 4825 Mark Center Drive, Alexandria, VA 
22311, 703-681-4909.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of April 26, 2005, in FR Doc. 05-8268, on 
page 21403, in the second column, correct the DATES caption to read:

DATES: The meeting will be held on Friday, June 24, 2005, from 9 a.m. 
to 10 a.m.

    Dated: May 3, 2005.
I.C. Le Moyne Jr.,
Lieutenant, Judge Advocate General's Corps, U.S. Navy, Alternate 
Federal Register Liaison Officer.
