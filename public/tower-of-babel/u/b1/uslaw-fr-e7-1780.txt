FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices, Acquisition of Shares of Bank or 
Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 07-1536) published on pages 
4507-4508 of the issue for Wednesday, January 31, 2007.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta heading, the entry for 
Brenda Morris Griner, is revised to read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta (Andre Anderson, Vice President) 
1000 Peachtree Street, N.E., Atlanta, Georgia 30309:
    1. Brenda Morris Griner, Columbia, Mississippi; to acquire 
additional voting shares of First Federal Bancorp, and thereby 
indirectly acquire additional voting shares of First Southern Bank, 
both of Columbia, Mississippi.
    Comments on this application must be received by February 15, 2007.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, January 31, 
2007.
Robert deV. Frierson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
