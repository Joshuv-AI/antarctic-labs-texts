DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Office of the Special Trustee for American Indians


Notice and Request for Comments; Correction

AGENCY: Office of the Special Trustee for American Indians.

ACTION: Notice and request for comments; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In compliance with section 3506(c)(2)(A) of the Paperwork 
Reduction Act of 1995, the Office of Special Trustee for American 
Indians announced the proposed extension of a public information 
collection, ``Trust Funds for Tribes and Individual Indians, 25 CFR 
115,'' OMB Control No. 1035-0004, and that it is seeking comments on 
its provisions. The notice seeking public comments was published on 
October 16, 2009 at 74 FR 53292, and stated that comments must be 
received by November 16, 2009.
    Notice of Correction: The comment period is actually open until 
December 16, 2009. Please use this new date as the time during which 
comments will be received in accordance with the other provisions of 
the October 16, 2009 notice.

    Dated: October 21, 2009.
Linda S. Thomas,
Information Collection Clearance Officer.
