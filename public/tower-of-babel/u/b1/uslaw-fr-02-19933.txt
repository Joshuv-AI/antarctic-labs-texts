Alliance Pipeline L.P.; Notice of Compliance Filing

August 1, 2002.
    Take notice that on July 3, 2002, Alliance Pipeline L.P. (Alliance) 
tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Original Volume No. 
1, the revised tariff sheets attached to Appendix A to the filing, with 
an effective date of August 2, 2002.
    Alliance states that the purpose of this tariff filing is to comply 
with the Commission's Order issued June 5, 2002, on the compliance by 
Alliance with Commission Order Nos. 637, 587-G and 587-L.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before August 8, 2002. Protests will be considered by the Commission 
in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection. This filing may also be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.gov using the ``RIMS'' link, select ``Docket#'' and follow the 
instructions (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). Comments, protests and 
interventions may be filed electronically via the Internet in lieu of 
paper. The Commission strongly encourages electronic filings. See, 18 
CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the instructions on the Commission's web 
site under the ``e-Filing'' link.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
