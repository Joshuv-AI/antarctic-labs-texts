Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 64--Jacksonville, Florida; Authorization 
of Production Activity; Saft America Inc. (Lithium-Ion Batteries); 
Jacksonville, Florida

    On June 1, 2015, the Jacksonville Port Authority, grantee of FTZ 
64, submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ 
Board on behalf of Saft America Inc., within Site 10, in Jacksonville, 
Florida.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (80 FR 33479, June 12, 2015). The FTZ 
Board has determined that no further review of the activity is 
warranted at this time. The production activity described in the 
notification is authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the Board's 
regulations, including Section 400.14.

    Dated: September 28, 2015.
Pierre V. Duy,
Acting Executive Secretary.
