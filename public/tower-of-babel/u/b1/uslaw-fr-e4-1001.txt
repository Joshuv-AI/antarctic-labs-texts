Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-54,416]


Plastic Components, Inc., Germantown, WI; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 3, 2004, in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at Plastic Components, Inc., Germantown, 
Wisconsin.
    The petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 2nd day of April, 2004.
