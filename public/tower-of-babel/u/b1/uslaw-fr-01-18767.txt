SUMMARY: This action corrects an error in the legal description in 
Public Land Order No. 7413, 64 FR 54637-54638, published October 7, 
1999, as FR Doc. 99-26186. On page 54638, paragraph 1, ``T. 32 N., R., 
2 W.'', is hereby corrected to read, ``T. 30 S., R., 2 W.''.
