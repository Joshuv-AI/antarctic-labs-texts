LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting of the Corporation's Board of Directors: 
Correction

    In the meeting notice published on December 27, 1996 (61 FR 68304), 
please make the following correction to the agenda:
    In item 13, change ``Consider and act on proposed policies and 
procedures for annual performance reviews of the Corporation's 
President and Inspector General'' to ``Consider and act on proposed 
policies and procedures relating to communications between the 
Corporation and the Congress.''

    Dated: December 30, 1996.
Victor M. Fortuno,
General Counsel.
