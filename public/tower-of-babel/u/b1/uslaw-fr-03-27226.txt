October 23, 2003.

Correction

    In FR Document No. 03-25263, beginning on page 57716 for Monday 
October 6, 2003, the last full sentence in the text of column 2 on page 
57720, which states that the provisions of the proposed rule change are 
in effect on a pilot basis until December 3, 2003, was incorrectly 
stated. The sentence should read as follows:
    Furthermore, pursuant to Amendment No. 3 to the proposed rule 
change, these provisions of the proposed rule change are in effect on a 
pilot basis until December 1, 2003.

    For the Commission, by the Division of Market Regulation, 
pursuant to delegated authority.\1\
