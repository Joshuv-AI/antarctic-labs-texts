INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation No. 731-TA-25 (Second Review)]


Anhydrous Sodium Metasilicate From France

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Termination of five-year review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The subject five-year review was initiated in September 2004 
to determine whether revocation of the antidumping duty order on 
anhydrous sodium metasilicate from France would be likely to lead to 
continuation or recurrence of dumping and of material injury to a 
domestic industry. On October 21, 2004, the Department of Commerce 
published notice that it was revoking the order effective October 21, 
2004 because ``no domestic party responded to the sunset review notice 
of initiation by the applicable deadline'' (69 FR 61789). Accordingly, 
pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 
1675(c)), the subject review is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 21, 2004.
