DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Customs Service
[T.D. 95-97]


Revocation of Customs Broker License

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: General Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that on October 25, 1995, the Secretary 
of the Treasury, pursuant to Section 641, Tariff Act of 1930, as 
amended (19 U.S.C. 1641), and Part 111.45(a) of the Customs 
Regulations, as amended (19 CFR 111.45(a)), ordered the revocation of 
license (No. 6884) issued to John V. Urbano to conduct Customs 
business.

    Dated: November 20, 1995.
Anne K. Lombardi,
Deputy Director, Trade Compliance.
