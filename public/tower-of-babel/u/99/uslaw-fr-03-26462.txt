SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Public Federal Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Roundtable; Region 
III Regulatory Fairness Board

    The Small Business Administration Region III Regulatory Fairness 
Board and the SBA Office of the National Ombudsman will hold a Public 
Roundtable on Thursday, October 30, 2003 at 8:30 a.m. at Annapolis City 
Council Chamber, City Hall, 2nd Floor, Duke of Gloucester Street, 
Annapolis, MD 21401, to provide small business owners and 
representatives of trade associations with an opportunity to share 
information concerning the federal regulatory enforcement and 
compliance environment.
    Anyone wishing to attend or to make a presentation must contact 
Oliver J. Phillips in writing or by fax, in order to be put on the 
agenda. Oliver J. Phillips, Chief of Business Development, SBA 
Baltimore District Office, 10 S. Howard

[[Page 60134]]

Street, Suite 6220, Baltimore, MD 21201, phone (410) 962-6195 x337, fax 
(410) 962-1805, e-mail: [email protected].
    For more information, see our Web site at http://www.sba.gov/ombudsman.

    Dated: October 10, 2003.
Peter Sorum,
National Ombudsman (Acting).
