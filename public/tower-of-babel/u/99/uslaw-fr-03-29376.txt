Belden Wire & Cable. Richmond, IN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 25, 2003 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Belden Wire & 
Cable, Richmond, Indiana (TA-W-52,673).
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 3rd day of October 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
