DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP99-385-001]


Steuben Gas Storage Company; Notice of Compliance Filing

August 5, 1999.
    Take notice that on July 30, 1999, Steuben Gas Storage Company 
(Steuben) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas Tariff, Original 
Volume No. 1, the tariff sheet listed below, to be effective August 1, 
1999:

Substitute Third Revised Sheet No. 132B

    Steuben states the attached tariff sheet is being filed in 
compliance with the Commission's Order issued on July 23, 1999, at 
Docket No. RP99-385-000.
    Steuben states that copies of the filing were served upon the 
company's Jurisdictional customers.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426, in accordance with section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed as 
provided in Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission

[[Page 43686]]

in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve 
to make protestants parties to the proceedings. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public inspection 
in the Public Reference Room. This filing may be viewed on the web at 
http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/rims.htm (call 202-208-2222 for 
assistance).
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
