FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION

12 CFR Part 630

Disclosure to Investors in System-wide and Consolidated Bank Debt 
Obligations of the Farm Credit System; System Audit Committee; 
Effective Date

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.

ACTION: Notice of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Farm Credit Administration (FCA or Agency), through the 
FCA Board (Board), issued a final rule under part 630 on September 26, 
2012 (77 FR 59050) amending our regulations relating to the Federal 
Farm Credit Banks Funding Corporation System Audit Committee and the 
Farm Credit System annual report to investors. In accordance with 12 
U.S.C. 2252, the effective date of the final rule is 30 days from the 
date of publication in the Federal Register during which either or both 
Houses of Congress are in session. Based on the records of the sessions 
of Congress, the effective date of the regulations is December 12, 
2012.

DATES: Effective Date: Under the authority of 12 U.S.C. 2252, the 
regulation amending 12 CFR part 630 published on September 26, 2012 (77 
FR 59050) is effective December 12, 2012.
