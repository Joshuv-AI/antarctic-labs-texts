SUMMARY: This rule is the fiscal year 1995 revision of the section 
listing DoD contractors receiving contract awards of $10 million or 
more. This part is published to comply with the provisions of section 
1, Pub. L. 97-295, October 12, 1982; 10 U.S.C. 2397.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 30, 1995.

Reports, Washington Headquarters Services, 1215 Jefferson Davis 
Highway, Suite 1204, Arlington, VA 22202-4302. Telephone (703) 604-
4569.
