DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY


Public Meeting of the President's Advisory Panel on Federal Tax 
Reform

AGENCY: Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice advises all interested persons of the location of 
the May 17, 2005, public meeting of the President's Advisory Panel on 
Federal Tax Reform. This meeting was previously announced in 70 FR 
22758 (May 2, 2005).

DATES: The meeting will be held on Tuesday, May 17, 2005, and will 
begin at 9:30 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Georgetown University Law 
Center, 600 New Jersey Ave., NW., Washington, DC 20001. Seating will be 
available to the public on a first-come, first-served basis.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: The Panel staff at (202) 927-2TAX 
(927-2829) (not a toll-free call) or e-mail [email protected] 
(please do not send comments to this box). Additional information is 
available at http://www.taxreformpanel.gov.

    Dated: May 12, 2005.
Mark S. Kaizen,
Designated Federal Officer.
