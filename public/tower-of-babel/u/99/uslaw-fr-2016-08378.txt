DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


US Air Force Exclusive Patent License

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force, Air Force Research Laboratory 
Information Directorate, Rome, New York, DOD.

ACTION: Notice of intent to issue an exclusive patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of part 404 of Title 37, Code of 
Federal Regulations, which implements Public Law 96-517, as amended, 
the Department of the Air Force announces its intention to grant The 
Curators of the University of Missouri, a public corporation of 
Missouri having a place of business at the Office of Technology 
Management and Industry Relations, 1601 S. Providence Road, #124, 
Columbia, Missouri 65211, an exclusive license in any right, title and 
interest the United States Air Force has in: In U.S. Patent No. 14/
795,953 entitled ``SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR STATIC AND MOVING OBJECT 
DETECTION'', filed July 10, 2015.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: An exclusive license for this patent 
will be granted unless a written objection is received within fifteen 
(15) days from the date of publication of this Notice.

[[Page 21541]]

Written objections should be sent to: Air Force Research Laboratory, 
Office of the Staff Judge Advocate, AFRL/RIJ, 26 Electronic Parkway, 
Rome, New York 13441-4514. Telephone: (315) 330-2087; Facsimile (315) 
330-7583.

Henry Williams Jr.,
Acting Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
