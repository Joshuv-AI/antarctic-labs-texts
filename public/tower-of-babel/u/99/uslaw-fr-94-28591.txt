DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
National Highway Traffic Safety Administration

 

Amendment to Notice of NHTSA Industry Meetings

    This notice refers back to the Federal Register Notice, Vol. 59, 
No. 217/Thursday, November 10, 1994, Page 56106. Please take note, the 
Industry Meetings referred to in this notice have been given two 
different room numbers. The Industry Meetings on December 20 and 
December 21, 1994 will be held at the Department of Transportation, 400 
7th Street SW., Washington, DC in Room 2230 not 4436-40.

    Issued: November 15, 1994.
Barry Felrice,
Associate Administrator for Rulemaking.
