DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Marine Mammals; Photography Permit Application No. 997-1704

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that this corrects the document 
published on June 26, 2003, announcing that Bob McLaughlin, P.O. Box 
496, 339 Glenwood, Eastsound, Washington 98245, had applied in due form 
for a permit to take several species of non-listed marine mammals for 
purposes of commercial/educational photography.

DATES: This action is effective July 9, 2003.

ADDRESSES: The application and related documents are available for 
review upon written request or by appointment in the offices listed in 
the original document published on June 26, 2003, as well as:
    Southwest Region, NMFS, 501 West Ocean Blvd., Suite 4200, Long 
Beach, CA 90802-4213; phone (562)980-4001; fax (562)980-4018.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jennifer Jefferies or Ruth Johnson, 
(301)713-2289.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The last sentence of the first paragraph 
under SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION in document 68 FR 38011 is revised to 
read as follows, ``However, in the meantime, NMFS has received and is 
processing this request as a ``pilot'' application for Level B 
Harrassment of non-listed marine mammals for photographic purposes.''
