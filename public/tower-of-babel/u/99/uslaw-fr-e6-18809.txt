    On October 25, 2006, the Commission issued an order that instituted 
a proceeding in the above-referenced dockets, pursuant to section 206 
of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. 824e, concerning the justness 
and reasonableness of the tariffs of the independent system operators 
(ISOs) and regional transmission organizations (RTOs).
    The refund effective date in the above-docketed proceedings, 
established pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of 
publication of this notice in the Federal Register.

 Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
