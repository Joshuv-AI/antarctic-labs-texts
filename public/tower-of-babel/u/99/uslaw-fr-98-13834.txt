DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Project No. 77-110]


Pacific Gas and Electric Company; Notice of Extension of Comment 
Due Date

May 19, 1998.
    On April 13, 1998, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission 
(Commission) issued notice of an application for amendment of the 
license for the Potter Valley Project (FERC No. 77-110) and of our 
intent to prepare an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) (Published 4/
17/98, 63 FR 19247), in support of the Commission's decision in this 
matter. The proposed amendment involves changes in the minimum flow 
requirements at the project, located on the Eel and East Fork Russian 
Rivers, in Lake and Mendocino Counties, California.
    The notice established June 8, 1998 as the deadline for submitting 
any comments, protests, or motions to intervene in the proceeding.
    Take notice that the deadline for submitting any comments, 
protests, or motions to intervene in the proceeding is hereby extended 
to June 15, 1998.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
