DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council Meeting, September 9, 2014, 
08:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 
Center Drive, Bethesda, MD, 20892, which was published in the Federal 
Register on July 18, 2014, 79 FR 42024.
    This notice is being amended to change the time of the September 9, 
2014 National Heart, Lung, and Blood Advisory Council Meeting start 
time to 12:30 p.m. and the change the location of the meeting to the 
National Institutes of Health, Building 35A, Porter Building, Room 640, 
35A Convent Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892. The meeting is open to the 
public.

    Dated: August 15, 2014.
Michelle Trout,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
