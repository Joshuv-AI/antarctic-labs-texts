PAROLE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Department of Justice, United States Parole 
Commission.

TIME AND DATE: 10:30 a.m., Wednesday, January 6, 1999.

PLACE: 5550 Friendship Boulevard, Suite 400, Chevy Chase, Maryland 
20815.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The following matters have been placed on the 
agenda for the open Parole Commission meeting:

    1. Approval of minutes of previous Commission meeting.
    2. Reports from the Chairman, Commissioners, Legal, Chief of 
Staff, Case Operations, and Administrative Sections.
    3. Proposed Amendments to the Interim Regulations for D.C. Code 
Prisoners. Amendments to the following regulations will be 
discussed:
    (a) Sec. 2.76: Reduction in minimum sentence.
    (b) D.C. Youth Rehabilitation Act:

Sec. 2.71: Application for parole;
Sec. 2.75: Reconsideration proceedings;
Sec. 2.87: Reparole.

    (c) ``Attempted murder'' in Category III of the D.C. Guidelines.
    (d) Sec. 2.80: ``Current offense'' for probation violators.
    (e) Medical and geriatric parole:

Sec. 2.77: Medical parole;
Sec. 2.78: Geriatric parole;

    (f) Sec. 2.63: Rewarding Assistance to Law Enforcement.

AGENCY CONTACT: Tom Kowalski, Case Operations, United States Parole 
Commission, (301) 492-5962.

    Dated: December 30, 1998.
Michael A. Stover,
General Counsel, U.S. Parole Commission.
