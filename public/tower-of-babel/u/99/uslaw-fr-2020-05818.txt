DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, April 7, 2020, 
08:00 a.m. to April 7, 2020, 12:30 p.m., Embassy Suites--Chevy Chase 
Pavilion, 4300 Military Road NW, Washington, DC 20015 which was 
published in the Federal Register on March 04, 2020, 85 FR 12799.
    The NHLBI Special Emphasis Panel meeting is being amended due to a 
change in the meeting format. This one day meeting to be held on April 
7, 2020 will be a teleconference meeting. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: March 16, 2020.
Ronald J. Livingston, Jr.,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
