FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
obtain copies of agreements by contacting the Commission's Office of 
Agreements at 202-523-5793 or via email at [email protected]. 
Interested parties may submit comments on an agreement to the 
Secretary, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within 10 
days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register.

    Agreement No.: 011889.
    Title: HMM/SINOLINES Space Charter Agreement.
    Parties: Hyundai Merchant Marine Co., Ltd. (``HMM'') and SINOLINES 
Container Lines Co., Ltd. (``SINOLINES'').
    Filing Party: Eliot J. Halperin, Esq.; Manelli Denison & Selter 
PLLC; 2000 M Street, NW., 7th floor; Washington, DC 20036-3307.
    Synopsis: The proposed agreement would authorize SINOLINES to take 
space on HMM's vessels operating between China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and 
Korea, on the one hand, and the U.S. West Coast, on the other hand. The 
parties request expedited review.
