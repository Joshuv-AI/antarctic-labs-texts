Louisiana Intrastate Gas Company, L.L.C.; Notice of Informal 
Settlement Conference

October 6, 1997.
    Taken notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Wednesday, October 15, 1997, at 
1:00 p.m. in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal 
Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC 
20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and staff. For additional 
information, please contact Fred Ni at (202) 208-2218.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
