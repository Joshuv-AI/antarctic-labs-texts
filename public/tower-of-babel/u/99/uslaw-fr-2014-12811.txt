DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 8753]


Determination by the Secretary of State Relating to Iran 
Sanctions

AGENCY: Department of State.

    This notice is to inform the public that the Secretary of State 
determined on May 27, 2014, pursuant to Section 1245(d)(4)(D) of the 
National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2012 (NDAA) (Pub. L. 
112-81), as amended by the Iran Threat Reduction and Syria Human Rights 
Act (Pub. L. 112-158), that as of May 27, 2014, each of the following 
purchasers of oil from Iran has qualified for the 180-day exception 
outlined in section 1245(d)(4)(D): Malaysia, Singapore, South Africa. 
The Secretary of State last made exception determinations under Section 
1245(d)(4)(D) of the NDAA regarding these purchasers on November 29, 
2013.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Amos J. Hochstein, Deputy Assistant 
Secretary of State, Bureau of Energy Resources, (202) 736-7873.
