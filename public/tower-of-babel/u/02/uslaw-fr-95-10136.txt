U.S. Railroad Retirement Board


Notice of Public Meeting

    Notice is hereby given that the Railroad Retirement Board will hold 
a meeting on April 27, 1995, 9:00 a.m., at the Board's meeting room on 
the 8th floor of its headquarters building, 844 North Rush Street, 
Chicago, Illinois, 60611. The agenda for this meeting follows:

    (1) Request for Transfer of Funds--Replacement of Xerox 9790 
Non-Impact Printer--Bureau of Data Processing
    (2) Legislation--104th Congress
    (3) Personal Papers of Executive Branch Officials
    (4) Description of the Board for the Administrative Circular on 
Agency Organization
    (5) Regulations:

    B. Part 255, Recovery of Overpayments
    C. Part 366 and 367, Collection of Debts

    The entire meeting will be open to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: April 18, 1995.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
