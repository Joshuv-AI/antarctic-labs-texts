SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council's Scientific and 
Statistical Committee (SSC) will hold a three-day meeting to discuss 
the items contained in the following agenda:

DATES: The meetings will be held on April 19 through April 21, 2016.

ADDRESSES: The meetings will be held at the Council' Office, 270 
Mu[ntilde]oz Rivera Avenue, Suite 401, San Juan, Puerto Rico.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Caribbean Fishery Management Council, 
270 Mu[ntilde]oz Rivera Avenue, Suite 401, San Juan, Puerto Rico 00918-
1903; telephone: (787) 766-5926.

    --Species Complexes--SERO Update

    --ABC Control Rule

    --Finalize 5-year Research Plan
--Other Business
