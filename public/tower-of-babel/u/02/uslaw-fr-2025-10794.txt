Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Seattle City Light; Notice of Extension of Time

    On June 3, 2025, Bonneville Power Administration (BPA) submitted a 
motion requesting from the Commission an extension of time until June 
17, 2025, to answer the application filed by Seattle City Light in this 
proceeding on May 20, 2025. BPA represents that the requested extension 
of time to June 17, 2025, will permit BPA to answer the complex 
factual, policy and legal issues raised by Seattle City Light's 
application.
    Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that BPA's requested 
extension of time is granted, and the deadline for filing the response 
to the Seattle City Light's application is hereby extended to and 
including June 17, 2025.

    Dated: June 9, 2025.
Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Secretary.
