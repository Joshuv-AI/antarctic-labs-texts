POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--International Priority Airmail, 
International Surface Air Lift, Commercial ePacket, Priority Mail 
Express International, Priority Mail International & First-Class 
Package International Service With Reseller Agreement: Postal 
ServiceTM

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing a request with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add an International Priority Airmail, 
International Surface Air Lift, Commercial ePacket, Priority Mail 
Express International, Priority Mail International & First-Class 
Package International Service with Reseller contract to the list of 
Negotiated Service Agreements in the Competitive Product List in the 
Mail Classification Schedule.

DATES: Date of notice: July 1, 2020.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher C. Meyerson, (202) 268-
7820.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service[supreg] 
hereby gives notice that, pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642 and 3632(b)(3), on 
June 15, 2020, it filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission a USPS 
Request to Add International Priority Airmail, International Surface 
Air Lift, Commercial ePacket, Priority Mail Express International, 
Priority Mail International & First-Class Package International Service 
with Reseller Contract 1 to Competitive Product List. Documents are 
available at www.prc.gov, Docket Nos. MC2020-172 and CP2020-195.

Attorney, Federal Compliance.
