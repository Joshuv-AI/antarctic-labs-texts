SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Corrections to Initiation and Preliminary Results Dates of Review

    On March 5, 2003, the Department of Commerce published its Notice 
of Initiation of Countervailing Duty New Shipper Review: Certain Pasta 
from Italy, 68 FR 10446. In that notice, the intiation of the review 
was dated February 21, 2003. Further, we indicated that pursuant to 19 
CFR 351.214(h)(i), we intend to issue the preliminary results of the 
review not later than 180 days from the date of publication of the 
notice.
    We issue this notice to correct and amend the initiation and 
preliminary result dates of the review because of a typographical 
error. The intiation of the review should be dated February 27, 2003, 
and pursuant to 19 CFR 351.214(h)(i), we intend to issue the 
preliminary results of the review not later than 180 days from this 
corrected date of initiation.
    This notice is published pursuant to section 751(a) of the Act and 
19 CFR 351.214.
