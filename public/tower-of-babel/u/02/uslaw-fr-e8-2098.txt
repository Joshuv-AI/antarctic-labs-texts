Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2007-0105.
    Petitioner: Cessna Aircraft Company.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  23.855(c)(2).
    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner requests relief from 
the requirements of Sec.  23.855(c)(2) for a smoke or fire detector in 
the baggage compartments of the Cessna Model 525C aircraft. If granted, 
the petitioner would be allowed to obtain a type certificate for the 
Cessna Model 525C without a smoke or fire detector in the forward or 
aft baggage compartments.
