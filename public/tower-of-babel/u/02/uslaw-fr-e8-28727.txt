DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Advisory Board, December 9, 2008, 8:30 a.m. to 3:55 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center Drive, Bethesda, 
MD 20892, which was published in the Federal Register on November 24, 
2008 73 FR 71015.
    This notice is amended to change the start time of the open session 
on December 9, 2008 to approximately 11:15 a.m. and the end time to 
3:30 p.m. The closed session will be held from 3:30 p.m. to 3:55 p.m.

    Dated: November 25, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
