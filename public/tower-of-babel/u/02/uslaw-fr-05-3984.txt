SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Region 1--Maine District Advisory Council Public Meeting; 
Correction

    The U.S. Small Business Administration, Maine District Advisory 
Council, located in the geographical area of Augusta, Maine, will be 
hosting a public meeting to discuss such matters as may be presented by 
members, staff of the U.S. Small Business Administration, or others 
present. Previously, the meeting was scheduled for Tuesday, March 22, 
2005, and has been canceled for a later date. The meeting has been 
rescheduled for Wednesday, March 30, 2005, at 10 a.m. The meeting will 
take place at the U.S. Small Business Administration, Maine District 
Office, 68 Sewall Street, Room 510, Augusta, Maine.
    Anyone wishing to attend must contact Mary McAleney in writing or 
by fax. Mary McAleney, District Director, U.S. Small Business 
Administration, 68 Sewall Street, Room 512, Augusta, Maine 04330, (207) 
622-8386 telephone, (207) 622-8277 fax.

Matthew K. Becker,
Committee Management Officer.
