NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Biweekly Notice; Applications and Amendments to Facility 
Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards Considerations; 
Correction

    This document corrects a notice appearing in the Federal Register 
on May 21, 1997 (62 FR 27807). The action is necessary to add a Federal 
Register publication date, citation number, and a sentence.
    On page 27808, in the first column, in the second complete 
paragraph, following ``Date of initial notice in Federal Register,'' 
insert ``August 14, 1996 (61 FR 42285). The February 7, 1997 supplement 
contained clarifying information which did not affect the no 
significant hazards consideration.''

    Dated: at Rockville, Maryland, this 28th day of May 1997.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
David L. Meyer,
Rules and Directives Branch, Division of Administrative Services, 
Office of Administration.
