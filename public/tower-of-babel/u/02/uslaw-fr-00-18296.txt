Radio Broadcasting Services; Leeds, UT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule, denial.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a petition for rule making filed by 
Mountain West Broadcasting requesting the allotment of Channel 287C2 at 
Leeds, Utah. See 64 FR 34751, June 29, 1999. Based on the information 
submitted by Mountain West Broadcasting, we believe it has failed to 
establish that Leeds qualifies as a community for allotment purposes 
and therefore it would not serve the public interest to allot a channel 
to Leeds.
