Radio Broadcasting Services; Galesburg, IL, and Ottumwa, IA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Galesburg Broadcasting Company directed to the Report and Order in this 
proceeding which upgraded Station KTWA, Ottumwa, Iowa, to specify 
operation on Channel 224C2. See 63 FR 57608, October 28, 1998. With 
this action, the proceeding is terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau (202) 
418-2177
