Emerson Power Transmission, a Subsidiary of Emerson Electric; 
Aurora, IL; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 4, 2008, in response to a petition filed by 
workers at Emerson Power Transmission, a subsidiary of Emerson 
Electric, Aurora, Illinois.
    This petitioner group of workers is covered by an active 
certification, (TA-W-62,689) which expires on February 27, 2010. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 5th day of September 2008.
 Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
