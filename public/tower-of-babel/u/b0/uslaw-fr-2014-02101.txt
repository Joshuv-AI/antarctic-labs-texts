DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Advisory Board, February 26, 2014, 06:00 p.m. to February 28, 
2014, 12:00 p.m., National Institutes of Health, Building 31, 31 Center 
Drive, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the Federal Register 
on January 22, 2014, 79 FR 3600.

[[Page 6206]]

    The meeting notice is amended to cancel the third day (February 28, 
2014) of the meeting. The meeting is partially closed to the public.

    Dated: January 28, 2014.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
