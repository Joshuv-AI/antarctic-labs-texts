Digital Television Broadcast Services; Klamath Falls, OR

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of California Oregon 
Broadcasting, Inc., licensee of Station KOTI-TV, Klamath Falls, Oregon, 
substitutes DTV Channel 13 for Station KOTI-TV's assigned DTV Channel 
40 at Klamath Falls. See 64 FR 54269, October 6, 1999. DTV Channel 13 
can be allotted to Klamath Falls at coordinates (42-05-48 N. and 121-
37-57 W.) with a power of 45.3, HAAT of 671 meters and with a DTV 
service population of thousand.
    With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective October 23, 2000.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
