Tri Star Precision, Inc., Gilberts, IL; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 15, 2004 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Tri Star 
Precision, Inc., Gilberts, Illinois.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 23rd day of January, 2004.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
