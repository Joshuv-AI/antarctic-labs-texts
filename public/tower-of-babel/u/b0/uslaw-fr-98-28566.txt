DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


RTCA, Inc.; Certification Task Force

Cancellation

    The October 29-30, RTCA Certification Task Force meeting announced 
in the Federal Register, 63 FR 55423 (Thursday, October 15, 1998), 
second column, has been canceled. It will be rescheduled to early 
December. The revised data and location will be announced later.
    Persons wishing to obtain further information should contact RTCA 
at (202) 833-9339 (phone), (202) 833-9434 (fax), or [email protected] 
(e-mail).

    Issued in Washington, DC, on October 20, 1998.
Janice L. Peters,
Designated Official.
