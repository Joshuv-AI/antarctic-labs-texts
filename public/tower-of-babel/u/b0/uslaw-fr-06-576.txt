FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

Radio Broadcasting Services; Burnet, Calvert, Cameron, Elgin, 
Grapeland, Junction, and Mason, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

[[Page 4052]]


ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses the underlying proposal filed by 
Robert Fabian for a Channel 280A allotment at Grapeland, Texas, and 
denies a Counterproposal filed by Elgin FM Limited Partnership in 
response to the Notice of Proposed Rule Making in this proceeding. See 
68 FR 42664, July 18, 2003. With this action, the proceeding is 
terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Media Bureau, (202) 418-
2177.
