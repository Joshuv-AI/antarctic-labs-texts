SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3133; Amendment #2]


State of Louisiana

    In accordance with information received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended to 
establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning on 
September 9, 1998 and continuing through October 4, 1998.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 22, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is June 23, 1999.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: October 7, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
