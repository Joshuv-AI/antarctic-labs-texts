EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY COMMISSION

29 CFR Part 1602


Records and Reports

AGENCY: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission.

ACTION: Notice of extension of deadline for filing report.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that the deadline for filing the 1995 
State and Local Government Information Report (EEO-4) required by 29 
CFR 1602.32 is extended from September 30, 1995 to October 31, 1995. 
There is no change in the reference period used for information 
reported (i.e., payroll period which includes June 30, 1995).

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 2, 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Joachim Neckere, Director, Program Research and Surveys Division, at 
(202) 663-4958 (voice)  or (202) 663-7063 (TDD).

    For the Commission.

    Dated: September 26, 1995.
Gilbert F. Casellas,
Chairman.
