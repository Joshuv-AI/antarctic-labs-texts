NATIONAL COUNCIL ON DISABILITY


International Watch Advisory Committee Meetings (Conference 
Calls); Correction

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Council on Disability published a document in the 
Federal Register on December 2, 2002, concerning meeting dates for its 
International Watch Advisory Committee. The document contained one 
incorrect date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joan M. Durocher, Attorney Advisor and 
Designated Federal Official, National Council on Disability, 1331 F 
Street NW., Suite 850, Washington, DC 20004; 202-272-2004 (voice), 202-
272-2074 (TTY), 202-272-2022 (fax), [email protected] (e-mail).

Correction

    In the Federal Register of December 2, 2002, in FR Doc. 02-30401, 
on page 71595, correct the ``Time and Dates for 2003'' caption to read:
    Time and Dates for 2003: 12 noon, Eastern Time, January 9, March 
13, May 1, July 3, September 4, November 6.

    Dated: January 27, 2003.
Ethel D. Briggs,
Executive Director.
