Alaska Native Claims Selection; Notice for Publication

    In accordance with Departmental regulation 43 CFR 2650.7(d), notice 
is hereby given that a decision to issue conveyance under the 
provisions of Sec. 14(a) of the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act of 
December 18, 1971, 43 U.S.C. 1613(a), will be issued to Kuukpik, 
Corporation for approximately 15,987 acres. The corresponding 
subsurface estate will be conveyed to Arctic Slope Regional Corporation 
pursuant to Sec. 1431(o) of the Alaska National Interest Lands 
Conservation Act of December 2, 1980, Pub. L. 96-487, 94 Stat. 2533. 
The lands involved are in the vicinity of Nuiqsut, Alaska, within the 
following townships:

Umiat Meridian, Alaska

T. 11 N., R. 3 E.
T. 10 N., R. 4 E.
T. 11 N., R. 4 E.

    A notice of this decision will be published once a week, for four 
(4) consecutive weeks, in the Arctic Sounder. Copies of the decision 
may be obtained by contacting the Alaska State Office of the Bureau of 
Land Management, 222 West Seventh Avenue, #13, Anchorage, Alaska 99513-
7599 ((907) 271-5960).
    Any party claiming a property interest which is adversely affected 
by the decision, an agency of the Federal government, or regional 
corporation, shall have until November 29, 2000 to file an appeal. 
However, parties receiving service by certified mail shall have 30 days 
from the date of receipt to file an appeal. Appeals must be filed in 
the Bureau of Land Management at the address identified above, where 
the requirements for filing an appeal may be obtained. Parties who do 
not file an appeal in accordance with the requirements of 43 CFR part 
4, Subpart E, shall be deemed to have waived their rights.

Nancy Larsen,
Land Law Examiner, Branch of ANCSA Adjudication.
