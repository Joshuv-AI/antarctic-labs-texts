The Eureka Company, El Paso, TX; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 3, 2002 in response to a worker petition 
signed on August 2, 2002 on behalf of workers at The Eureka Company, El 
Paso, Texas.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-39,101) through May 8, 2002. Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 23rd day of October, 2002.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
