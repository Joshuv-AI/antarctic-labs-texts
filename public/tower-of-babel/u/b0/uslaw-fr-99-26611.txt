NATIONAL ARCHIVES AND RECORDS ADMINISTRATION


Advisory Committee on Presidential Libraries Meeting; Partial 
Closed Meeting

    This notice amends the Federal Register notice issued on August 31, 
1999 announcing a meeting of the Advisory Committee on Presidential 
Libraries on October 14, 1999, from 10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m., in room 105 
of the National Archives Building, 700 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW, 
Washington, DC. Pursuant to Section 10(d) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act and 5 U.S.C. 552b(c)(9)(B), it has been determined that a 
portion of the meeting will be closed. The open portion of the meeting 
will be held from 10:30 to 11:30 and will include agenda items covering 
digitization issues and an update on new libraries. The closed portion 
of the meeting will be held from 11:30 to 2:00 and will call for a 
discussion of proposed legislation and 2001 budget items that will not 
be available to the public until the President's budget and message 
goes to Congress in February,

[[Page 55496]]

2000. For further information, call David F. Peterson at 301-713-6050.
Mary Ann Hadyka,
Committee Management Officer.
