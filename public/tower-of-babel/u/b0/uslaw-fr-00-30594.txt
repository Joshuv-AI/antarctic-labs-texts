Cross Bay Pipeline Company, L.L.C. and Transcontinental Gas Pipe 
Line Corporation; Notice of Site Visit

November 22, 2000.
    On November 30, 2000, staff from the Office of Energy Projects 
(OEP) will conduct a pre-certification site visit of the proposed Cross 
Bay Project at Cross Bay Pipeline Company, L.L.C.'s (Cross Bay) 
proposed and alternative sites for the Cross Bay Compressor Station in 
Middlesex County, New Jersey, Representatives of Cross Bay will 
accompany the OEP staff.
    All interested parties may attend the site visit. Those planning to 
attend must provide their own transportation. For further information 
on attending the site visit, please contact the Commission's Office of 
External Affairs at (202) 208-0004.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
