DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Human Genome Research Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Inherited Disease Research Access Committee, April 27, 2006, 8:30 a.m. 
to April 27, 2006, 5 p.m., George Washington University Inn, 824 New 
Hampshire Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20037 which was published in the 
Federal Register on February 15, 2006, 71 FRN 7981.
    The meeting of the Center for Inherited Disease Research Access 
Committee will also include a session on April 26, 2006, from 7 p.m. to 
10 p.m. at The George Washington University Inn. The meeting is closed 
to the public.

    Dated: March 8, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
