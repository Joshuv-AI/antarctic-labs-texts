DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke Special Emphasis Panel, 
November 27, 2023, 09:00 a.m. to November 29, 2023, 06:00 p.m., Crowne 
Plaza National Airport, 1480 Crystal Drive, Arlington, VA 22202 which 
was published in the Federal Register on September 25, 2023, 88 FR 
67334.
    This notice is being amended to change the location to Canopy by 
Hilton, 940 Rose Avenue, North Bethesda, MD 20852, and change the 
meeting times to 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The meeting dates remains the 
same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: November 13, 2023.
David W. Freeman,
Supervisory Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee 
Policy.
