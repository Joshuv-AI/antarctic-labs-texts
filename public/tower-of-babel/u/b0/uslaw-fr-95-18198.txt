FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

Radio Broadcasting Services; Milledgeville, GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies the petition for reconsideration filed by 
Radio Perry, Inc. (``Perry''). Perry seeks review of an action taken by 
the Chief, Allocations Branch on August 17, 1993, returning its 
petition for rule making by letter as unacceptable for consideration. 
Perry's proposal sought to downgrade Station WLRR(FM) from Channel 
264C3 to Channel 264A at Milledgeville, Georgia. The Commission has 
determined that Perry has not presented any new arguments or facts in 
this proceeding. Therefore, we will deny the petition for 
reconsideration. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 25, 1995.
