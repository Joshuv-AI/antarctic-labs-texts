DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Office of the Secretary


Approved Information Collection Activities; Comment Request; 
Generic Clearance for the Collection of Qualitative Feedback on Agency 
Service Delivery; Correction

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Commerce.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On June 9, 2020, the Department of Commerce, published a 30-
day public comment period notice in the Federal Register with FR 
Document Number 2020-12413 (Pages 35257-35258), and on March 24, 2020, 
published a 60-day public comment period notice with FR Document Number 
2020-06174 (Page 16612) seeking public comments for an information 
collection entitled, ``Generic Clearance for the Collection of 
Qualitative Feedback on Agency Service Delivery.'' This document 
referenced incorrect information in the DATA section, and Commerce 
hereby issues a correction notice as required by the Paperwork 
Reduction Act of 1995.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For additional information contact the 
DOC PRA Clearance Officer, Office of Policy and Governance at 
[email protected].
