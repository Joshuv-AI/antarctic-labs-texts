SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
[Rel. No. IC-20048A; No. 811-1671]

 

The Travelers Fund B for Variable Contracts

February 28, 1994.
    On February 1, 1994, the Securities and Exchange Commission issued 
a notice of an Application for an Order under Section 8(f) of the 
Investment Company Act of 1940 (``1940 Act'') (Rel. No. IC-20048). The 
notice stated that interested persons had until January 28, 1994 to 
request a hearing on this application. This corrects the prior notice, 
which should have stated that interested persons had until 5:30 p.m. on 
February 28, 1994 to request a hearing on this application.

    For the Commission, by the Division of Investment Management, 
pursuant to delegated authority.
Margaret H. McFarland,
Deputy Secretary.
