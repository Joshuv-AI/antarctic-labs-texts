Employment and Training Administration

Phoenix Lace Cutting, Inc., North Bergen, NJ; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 21, 2004 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by the New Jersey Trade Coordinator on behalf of workers at 
Phoenix Lace Cutting, Inc., North Bergen, New Jersey.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 29th day of June, 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
