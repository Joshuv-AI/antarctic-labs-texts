COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Redesignation of Services; Correction

    In the document appearing on page 2565, FR Doc 04-1029, in the 
issue of January 16, 2004, in the first and second columns, the 
Committee published a redesignation of services currently on the 
Procurement List for Janitorial/Custodial, Johnstown, Pennsylvania. The 
name of the Nonprofit Agency associated with this redesignation was 
misspelled. It should read Goodwill Industries of the Conemaugh Valley, 
Inc., Johnstown, Pennsylvania.

Sheryl D. Kennerly,
Director, Information Management.
