License Cancellation

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury

ACTION: General Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that, pursuant to 19 CFR 111.51(a), the 
following Customs broker license has been cancelled due to the death of 
the broker. This license was issued in the New York region.
    Louis Jean Noens--license No. 12662.

    Dated: November 18, 1994.
Philip Metzger,
Director, Office of Trade Operations.
