DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Availability of U.S. Patents for Non-Exclusive, Exclusive, or 
Partially-Exclusive Licensing

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory, Adelphi, Maryland.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 37 CFR 404.6, announcement is made of the 
availability of the following U.S. patents for non-exclusive, partially 
exclusive or exclusive licensing. All of the listed patents have been 
assigned to the United States of America as represented by the 
Secretary of the Army, Washington, D.C.
    These patents cover a wide variety of technical arts including: A 
long-range nonlethal bullet, a device to detect muzzle flash from a 
gun.
    Under the authority of Section 11(a)(2) of the Federal Technology 
Transfer Act of 1986 (Pub. L. 99-502) and Section 207 of Title 35, 
United States Code, the Department of the Army as represented by the 
U.S. Army Research Laboratory wish to license the U.S. patents listed 
below in a non-exclusive, exclusive or partially exclusive manner to 
any party interested in manufacturing, using, and/or selling devices or 
processes covered by these patents.
    Title: Long-Range Nonlethal Bullet.
    Inventor: Raine M. Gilbert.
    Patent Number: 5,691,501.
    Issued Date: November 25, 1997.

    Title: Infrared Sniper Detection Enhancement.
    Inventor: David B. Hillis.
    Patent Number: 5,686,889.
    Issue Date: November 11, 1997.
