INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION

 

Intent To Engage in Compensated Intercorporate Hauling Operations

    This is to provide notice as required by 49 U.S.C. 10524(b)(1) that 
the named corporations intend to provide or use compensated 
intercorporate hauling operations as authorized in 49 U.S.C. 10524(b).
    1. Parent corporation and address of principal office: JP 
Foodservice, Inc., 9830 Patuxent Woods Drive, Columbia, MD 21046.
    2. Wholly-owned subsidiaries which will participate in the 
operations and states of incorporation:
    (i) Sky Bros., Inc.--Pennsylvania.
    (ii) Illinois Fruit & Produce--Illinois.
Sidney L. Strikland, Jr.,
Secretary.
