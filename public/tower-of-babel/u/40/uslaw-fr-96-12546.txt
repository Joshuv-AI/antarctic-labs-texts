COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION


Advisory Committee on CFTC-State Cooperation; May 21, 1996 Public 
Meeting

AGENCY: Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

ACTION: Correction of time of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On May 9, 1996, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission published 
a notion in the Federal Register (61 FR 21163) announcing that the 
Commission's Advisory Committee on CFTC-State Cooperation will conduct 
a public meeting on May 21, 1996 that will begin at 9:00 a.m. and last 
until 12:00 noon. The time that this May 21, 1996 meeting will be held 
is from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. in the first floor hearing room (Room 
1000) of the Commission's Washington, D.C. headquarters, Three 
Lafayette Centre, 1155 21st Street NW., Washington, D.C.
Jean A. Webb,
Secretary of the Commission.
