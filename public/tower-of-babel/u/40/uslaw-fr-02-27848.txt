Food Safety and Inspection Service

Codex Alimentarius: Meeting of the Codex Committee on Food Import 
and Export Inspection and Certification Systems

AGENCY: Food Safety and Inspection Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food Safety and Inspection Service published a document in 
the Federal Register on October 2, 2002, in FR Doc. Number 02-24978 on 
page 61847, concerning announcement of public meetings. The document 
contained an incorrect date.
    The date for the second public meeting was incorrect. The DATES 
caption should be corrected to read:

``DATES: The public meetings are scheduled for Tuesday, October 22, 
2002 from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. and Tuesday, November 19, 2002 from 1 p.m. 
to 3 p.m.''
