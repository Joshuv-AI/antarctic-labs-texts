Micro Component Technology; St. Paul, MN; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 6, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a community representative on behalf of workers at 
Micro Component Technology, St. Paul, Minnesota.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on January 10, 2003, and which remains in effect 
(TA-W-50,394). Consequently, further investigation in this case would 
serve no purpose and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 14th day of January, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
