    On March 24, 2011, the Commission held a drawing to determine 
priority among competing preliminary permit applications with identical 
filing times. In the event that the Commission concludes that none of 
the applicants' plans are better adapted than the others to develop, 
conserve, and utilize in the public interest the water resources of the 
region at issue, the priority established by this drawing will serve as 
the tiebreaker. Based on the drawing, the order of priority is as 
follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                Project
                                                                  No.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FFP Missouri 5, LLC......................................   13757-000
2. Solia 2 Hydroelectric, LLC...............................   13764-000
3. Lock+ Hydro Friends Fund XXXIV...........................   13742-000
