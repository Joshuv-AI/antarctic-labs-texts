DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL02-123-000]


Boston Edison Company; Notice of Initiation of Proceeding and 
Refund Effective Date

September 10, 2002.
    Take notice that on August 22, 2002, the Commission issued an order 
in the above-referenced docket initiating an investigation under 
section 206 of the Federal Power Act.
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL02-123-000, established 
pursuant to section 206 (b) of the Federal Power Act, will be 60 days 
following publication of this notice in the Federal Register.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
