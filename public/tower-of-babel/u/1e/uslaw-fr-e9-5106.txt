Canisteo Savings and Loan Association, Canisteo, NY; Approval of 
Conversion Application

    Notice is hereby given that on January 29, 2009, the Office of 
Thrift Supervision approved the application of Canisteo Savings and 
Loan Association, Canisteo, New York, to convert to the stock form of 
organization. Copies of the application are available for inspection by 
appointment (phone number: (202) 906-5922 or e-mail: 
[email protected]) at the Public Reading Room, 1700 U Street, 
NW., Washington, DC 20552, and the OTS Northeast Regional Office, 
Harborside Financial Center Plaza Five, Suite 1600, Jersey City, New 
Jersey 07311.

    Dated: March 4, 2009.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
