DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

President's Export Council: Meeting of the Subcommittee on 
Foreign Market Development, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East

AGENCY: International Trade Administration, Department of Commerce.

ACTION: Amendment to citation 60 FR, page 25200, May 11, 1995, change 
in location of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The meeting of the President's Export Council Subcommittee on 
Foreign Market Development, Asia, Africa and the Middle East, scheduled 
for May 17, 1995, from 2 to 5 p.m., will be held at the following 
address: U.S. Department of Commerce, Room 3407, 14th Street & 
Constitution Avenue, NW., Washington DC 20230.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Linda Breslau, President's Export 
Council, Room 2015B, Washington, DC 20230, telephone 202-482-1124.

    Dated: May 11, 1995.
Jane Siegel,
Staff Director and Executive Secretary, President's Export Council.
