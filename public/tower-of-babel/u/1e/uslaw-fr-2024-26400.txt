Gas Pipeline Rate and Refund Report filings:

Filings Instituting Proceedings

    Docket Numbers: PR25-9-000.
    Applicants: Columbia Gas of Ohio, Inc.
    Description: Supplement to October 25, 2024 FERC Gas Tariff Filing 
of Columbia Gas of Ohio, Inc.
    Filed Date: 11/4/24.
    Accession Number: 20241104-5216.
    Comment Date: 5 p.m. ET 11/18/24.
