DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Shasta County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC)

AGENCY: USDA Forest Service.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Shasta County Resource Advisory Committee (RAC) will meet 
on May 15, 2002, in Redding, Calif. The purpose of the meeting will be 
to review and discuss remaining project proposals.

DATES: The meeting will be held on May 15, 2002, from 8 a.m. to noon.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held in the Shasta County Office of 
Education conference room, 1644 Magnolia Ave., Redding, CA

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon Heywood, Designated Federal 
Official, USDA Shasta-Trinity National Forest, 2400 Washington Ave., 
Redding, CA. Phone: (530) 242-2200. Email:[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting is open to the public. Time will 
be provided for public input, giving individuals the opportunity to 
address the committee.

    Dated: April 19, 2002.
J. Sharon Heywood,
Forest Supervisor.
