Employment and Training Administration

Gerdau Ameristeel, Perth Amboy, NJ; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 19, 2007 in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Gerdau 
Ameristeel, Perth Amboy, New Jersey.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 5th day of December, 2007.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
