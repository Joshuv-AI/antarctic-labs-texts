East Tennessee Natural Gas Company; Notice of Meeting

August 14, 2002.
    The staff of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) will 
conduct a meeting with the Blue Ridge Coalition and any other 
interested persons pertaining to East Tennessee Natural Gas Company's 
proposed natural gas pipeline Patriot Project in Tennessee, Virginia, 
and North Carolina. The meeting will be held in Room 3M-3 of the FERC 
headquarters in Washington DC, 888 First Street, NE., on August 22 
starting at 3 p.m. Any interested persons may attend.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
