DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 2655]


Bureau of Political-Military Affairs; Determination Under the 
Arms Export Control Act

    Pursuant to Section 654(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, 
as amended, notice is hereby given that the Acting Secretary of State 
has made a determination pursuant to Section 81 of the Arms Export 
Control Act and has concluded that publication of the determination 
would be harmful to the national security of the United States.

    Dated: November 19, 1997.
Thomas E. McNamara,
Assistant Secretary of State for Political-Military Affairs.
