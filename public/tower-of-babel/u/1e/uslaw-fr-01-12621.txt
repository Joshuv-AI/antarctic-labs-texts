OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Proposed Collection; Comment Request for Review of a Revised 
Information Collection: Form RI 95-4

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Public 
Law 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of a revised information collection. RI 
95-4, Marital Information Required of Refund Applicants, is used by OPM 
to pay refunds of retirement contributions when the information is not 
included on the SF 3106, Application for Refund of Retirement 
Deductions (FERS). To pay these benefits, all applicants for refund 
must provide information to OPM about their marital status and whether 
any spouse(s) or former spouse(s) have been informed of the proposed 
refund.
    Approximately 2,600 RI 95-4 forms will be completed annually. We 
estimate it takes approximately 30 minutes to complete the form. The 
annual burden is 1,300 hours.
