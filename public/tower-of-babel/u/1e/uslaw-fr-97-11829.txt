FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-130; RM-8818]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Grenada, MS

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Darby Radio, allots Channel 
267A to Grenada, Mississippi, as an additional FM service. See 61 FR 
31085, June 19, 1996. Channel 267A can be allotted to Grenada in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements with a site restriction of 9.2 kilometers (5.7 miles) west 
to avoid a short-spacing conflict with the licensed site of Station 
WJDQ(FM), Channel 267C1, Meridian, Mississippi. The coordinates for 
Channel 267A at Grenada are 33-47-48 NL and 89-54-29 WL. With this 
action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective June 16, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications will open on June 16, 1997, and close on July 17, 1997.
