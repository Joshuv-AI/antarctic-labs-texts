NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice (96-034)]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of Prospective Patent License.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    NASA hereby gives notice that the SR Technology, Inc., of Houston, 
Texas 77064, has applied for an exclusive license to practice the 
invention protected by U.S. Patent No. 5,449,211 entitled ``Grapple 
Fixture for Use With Electromagnetic Attachment Mechanism,'' which was 
issued on September 12, 1995, and is assigned to the National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections to the 
prospective grant of a license should be sent to Mr. Hardie R. Barr, 
Patent Attorney, Johnson Space Center.

DATE: Responses to this notice must be received by May 28, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTRACT: Mr. Hardie R. Barr, Patent Attorney, 
Johnson Space Center, Mail Code HA, Houston, TX 77058-3696; telephone 
(713) 483-1003.

    Dated: March 21, 1996.
Edward A. Frankle,
General Counsel.
