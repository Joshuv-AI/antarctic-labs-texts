FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-700; MM Docket No. 99-330, RM-9677]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Kankakee and Park Forest, IL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Gene Milner Broadcasting Co,

[[Page 17815]]

Inc., reallots Channel 260B from Kankakee to Park Forest, Illinois, and 
modifies Station WRZA(FM)'s license accordingly. See 65 FR 3406, 
January 25, 2000. Channel 260B can be reallotted to Park Forest in 
compliance with the Commission's minimum distance separation 
requirements without the imposition of a site restriction at 
petitioner's presently licensed site. The coordinates for Channel 260B 
at Park Forest are 41-18-04 North Latitude and 87-49-35 West Longitude.

DATES: Effective May 7, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon P. McDonald, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
