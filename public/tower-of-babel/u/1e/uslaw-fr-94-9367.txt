Patents Available for Licensing

AGENCY: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, DOD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, 
announces the general availability of exclusive, partially exclusive or 
nonexclusive licenses under the following patents. Any licenses granted 
shall comply with 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR part 404.
