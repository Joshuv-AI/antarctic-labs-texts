DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 6412-001]


Empire Hydro Partners, Lyonsdale Hydroelectric Co., Inc.; Notice 
of Transfer of Exemption

    1. By letter filed March 1, 2024, Empire Hydro Partners and 
Lyonsdale Hydroelectric Co., Inc. informed the Commission that the 
exemption from licensing for the Port Leyden Project No. 6412, 
originally issued April 4, 1983,\1\ has been transferred to Lyonsdale 
Hydroelectric Co., Inc. The project is located on the Black River in 
the Village of Port Leyden, Towns of Leyden and Lyonsdale, Lewis 
County, New York. The transfer of an exemption does not require 
Commission approval.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Lyonsdale Hydroelectric Co., Inc., 23 FERC ] 62,005 (1983) 
(Order Granting Exemption from Licensing of a Small Hydroelectric 
Project of 5 Megawatts or Less).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2. Lyonsdale Hydroelectric Co., Inc., located at 35 Harbor Cove 
Drive, The Woodlands, Texas 77381 is now the exemptee of the Port 
Leyden Project No. 6412.

    Dated: March 18, 2024.
Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Acting Secretary.
