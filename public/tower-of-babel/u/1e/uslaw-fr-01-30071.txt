Chiquola Fabrics, LLC, Kingsport, Tennessee; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 29, 2001 in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on the same date on behalf of workers at Chiquola 
Fabrics, LLC, Kingsport, Tennessee. Chiquola Fabrics, LLC purchased JPS 
Converter and Industrial Corporation, Borden Plant, Kingsport, 
Tennessee, whose workers were certified eligible to apply for Trade 
Adjustment Assistance (TA-W36,891). That certification has been amended 
to encompass workers at the same facility employed by Chiquola Fabrics, 
LLC, Kingsport, Tennessee.
    The petitioner in this case has requested that the petition be 
withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 26th day of November, 2001.
Edward A. Tomchick,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
