DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary

 

Strategic Environmental Research and Development Program, 
Scientific Advisory Board; Meeting

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee meeting:

DATES OF MEETING: Wednesday, February 23, 1994 thru Friday, February 
25, 1994. 0800 to approximately 1700.

PLACE: In the auditorium at the Environmental Protection Agency, Air & 
Energy Engineering Research Lab, Research Triangle Park, NC.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Research and Development proposals requesting 
SERDP funds in excess of $1M will be reviewed.
    This meeting is open to the public. Any interested person may 
attend, appear before, or file statements with the Scientific Advisory 
Board at the time and in the manner permitted by the Board.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Ann Maxwell or Mr. John Rupnik, 
2200 Clarendon, suite 900, Arlington, VA 22201, 703-525-9400.

    Dated: February 7, 1994.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
