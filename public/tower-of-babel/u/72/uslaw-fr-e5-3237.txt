Meyersdale Manufacturing Co., Division of Elbeco, Inc., 
Meyersdale, PA; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 1, 2005, in response to a worker 
petition filed by UNITE (Mid-Atlantic Regional Joint Board) on behalf 
of workers at Meyersdale Manufacturing Co., division of Elbeco, Inc., 
Meyersdale, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 8th day of June, 2005.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
