Final Revision to Branch Technical Position 7-19 Guidance for 
Evaluation of Defense in Depth and Diversity to Address Common-Cause

Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Standard review plan-final section revision; issuance; 
correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register on January 29, 2021, 
regarding the final revision to Branch Technical Position (BTP) 7-19, 
``Guidance for Evaluation of Defense in Depth and Diversity to Address 
Common-Cause Failure Due to Latent Design Defects in Digital Safety 
Systems.'' This action is necessary to correct the Agency Document 
Access and Management System (ADAMS) Accession number for the staff 
responses to public comments on the draft version of BTP 7-19.

DATES: The correction takes effect on February 22, 2021.
