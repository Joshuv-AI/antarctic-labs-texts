DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Notice Pursuant to the National Cooperative Research and 
Production Act of 1993; Osinet Corporation

    Notice is hereby given that, on July 11, 1995, pursuant to Section 
6(a) of the National Cooperative Research and Production Act of 1993, 
15 U.S.C. 4301 et seq. (``the Act''), OSINET Corporation (``OSINET'') 
has filed written notifications simultaneously with the Attorney 
General and the Federal Trade Commission disclosing certain 
information. The notifications were filed for the purpose of extending 
the Act's provisions limiting the recovery of antitrust plaintiffs to 
actual damages under specified circumstances. Specifically, the change 
is as follows: Hewlett-Packard Company has ceased membership in OSINET 
effective June 2, 1995.
    No other changes have been made in either the membership or planned 
activity of the group research project. Membership in this group 
research project remains open, and OSINET intends to file additional 
written notifications disclosing all changes in membership.
    On April 15, 1991, OSINET filed its original notification pursuant 
to Section 6(a) of the Act. The Department of Justice published a 
notice in the Federal Register pursuant to Section 6(b) of the Act on 
November 19, 1991 (56 FR 

[[Page 3464]]
58400). The last notification was filed with the Department on May 22, 
1995. A notice was published in the Federal Register pursuant to 
Section 6(b) of the Act on July 25, 1995 (60 Fed. Reg. 38058).
Constance K. Robinson,
Director of Operations, Antitrust Division.
