DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4568]


Bureau of Administration; Notice of Availability of Alternative 
Fueled Vehicle (AFV) Report for Fiscal Year 2003

AGENCY: Department of State.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Department of State, Bureau of Administration, is 
issuing this notice in order to comply with the Energy Policy Act of 
1992 and 42 U.S.C. 13218(b). The purpose of this notice is to announce 
the public availability of the Department of State's interim Fiscal 
Year 2003 report at the following Web site: http://www.state.gov/m/a/26931.htm. A final report will be made available upon completion of 
data collection.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Questions regarding reports on the AFV 
report Web site should be addressed to the Domestic Fleet Management 
and Operations Division (A/OPR/GSM/FMO) [Attn: Barry Shpil], 2201 C 
Street NW (Room B258), Washington, DC 20520, phone 202-647-3628.

    Dated: December 9, 2003.
Vincent J. Chaverini,
Deputy Assistant Secretary Office of Operations, Department of State.
