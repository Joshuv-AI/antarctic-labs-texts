FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Notice of a Matter To Be Withdrawn From 
Consideration at an Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that the following matter 
will be withdrawn from the ``Discussion Agenda'' at the Federal Deposit 
Insurance Corporation's Board of Directors open meeting scheduled to be 
held at 10:00 a.m. on Tuesday, April 28, 1998:

    Memorandum re: General Counsel Opinion Regarding Interest 
Charges by Interstate State Banks.

    Dated: April 22, 1998.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
