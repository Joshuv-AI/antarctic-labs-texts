DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary

32 CFR Part 90

[DoD Instruction 7045.18]

 

Collection of Indebtedness Due the United States

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary of Defense, DoD.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Defense hereby removes 32 CFR part 90 
concerning collection of indebtedness. This part has served the purpose 
for which it was intended and is no longer valid.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 9, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:L. M. Bynum, Correspondence and 
Directives Directorate, 1155 Defense Pentagon, Washington, DC 20301-
1155, (703) 697-4111.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Department of Defense information on this 
subject may be found in DoD 7000.14-R, Volume 5. This document may be 
obtained, at cost, from the National Technical Information Service 
(NTIS), 5285 Port Royal Road, Springfield, VA 22161.

List of Subjects in 32 CFR Part 90

    Claims, Government employees, Military personnel, Wages.

PART 90--[REMOVED]

    Accordingly, by the authority of 10 U.S.C. 301, 32 CFR part 90 is 
removed.
    Dated: March 3, 1994.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
