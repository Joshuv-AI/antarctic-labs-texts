SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3246 (Amendment #1)]


Commonwealth of Kentucky

    In accordance with a notice received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency dated March 2, 2000, the above-numbered Declaration 
is hereby amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as 
beginning on February 18, 2000 and continuing through March 2, 2000.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is

[[Page 14007]]

April 28, 2000 and for economic injury the deadline is November 28, 
2000.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008).

    Dated: March 7, 2000.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
