NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


Notice of Correction to Biweekly Notice Applications and 
Amendments to Operating Licenses Involving No Significant Hazards 
Considerations

    On November 3, 1999, the Federal Register published the Biweekly 
Notice of Applications and Amendments to Operating Licenses Involving 
No Significant Hazards Consideration. On page 59797 the 30-day date for 
hearing request should be corrected from ``December 10, 1999,'' to read 
``By December 3, 1999, the licensee may file a request for a hearing. * 
* *''

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 3rd day of November 1999.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
John A. Zwolinski,
Director, Division of Licensing Project Management, Office of Nuclear 
Reactor Regulation.
