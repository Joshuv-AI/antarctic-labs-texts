NATIONAL COMMISSION ON LIBRARIES AND INFORMATION SCIENCE

MEETING STATUS: Open.

DATE AND TIME:

Tuesday, March 22, 1994, 1:30-5:00 p.m.
Wednesday, March 23, 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.
Thursday, March 24, 8:30 a.m.-3:00 p.m.

PLACE: Westin Peachtree Plaza, 210 Peachtree St., Atlanta, GA.

9200) no later than one week in advance of the meeting.
