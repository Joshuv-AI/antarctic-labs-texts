DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, TEP-1B:SBIR Contract Review, 
February 2, 2022, 10:00 a.m. to February 2, 2022, 4:30 p.m., National 
Cancer Institute at Shady Grove, Room 7W030, 9609 Medical Center Drive, 
Rockville, Maryland 20850, which was published in the Federal Register 
on December 08, 2021, FR Doc. 2021-26593, 86 FR 69660.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting date from 
February 2, 2022 to February 8, 2022. The meeting times and location 
will stay the same. The meeting is closed to public.

    Dated: January 18, 2022.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
