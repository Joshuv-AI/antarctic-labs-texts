DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Cancer Genetics 
Study Section, February 12, 2004, 8 a.m. to February 13, 2004, 5 p.m., 
Churchill Hotel, 1914 Connecticut Avenue, NW., Washington, DC 20009 
which was published in the Federal Register on January 14, 2004, 69 FR 
2151-2153.
    The meeting is cancelled due to a lack of quorum.

    Dated: January 20, 2004
Laverne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
