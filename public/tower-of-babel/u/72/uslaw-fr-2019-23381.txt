DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE


Membership of the Senior Executive Service and Senior Level 
Standing Performance Review Boards

AGENCY: Department of Justice.

ACTION: Notice of Department of Justice's standing members of the 
Senior Executive Service and Senior Level Performance Review Boards.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Pursuant to the requirements of 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(4), the Department 
of Justice announces the membership of its 2019 Senior Executive 
Service (SES) and Senior Level (SL) Standing Performance Review Boards 
(PRBs). The purpose of a PRB is to provide fair and impartial review of 
SES/SL performance appraisals, executive development plans, and award 
recommendations/pay adjustments.
    The PRBs will make recommendations regarding the final performance 
ratings to be assigned, SES/SL awards and/or pay adjustments to be 
awarded.
