FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-145; RM-8831]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Battle Mountain, NV

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Battle Mountain 
Communications, allots Channel 253A

[[Page 14005]]

to Battle Mountain, NV, as the community's first local aural service. 
See 61 FR 37715, July 19, 1996. Channel 253A can be allotted to Battle 
Mountain in compliance with the Commission's minimum distance 
separation requirements without the imposition of a site restriction, 
at coordinates 40-38-18 North Latitude; 116-56-06 West Longitude. With 
this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective May 5, 1997. The window period for filing applications 
will open on May 5, 1997, and close on June 5, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Leslie K. Shapiro, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
