BLM Nevada State Office Moves From 850 Harvard Way, Reno, NV to 
1340 Financial Blvd., Reno, NV 89502, on March 10, 1998

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Bureau of land management Nevada State Office Move Location and 
date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management's Nevada State Office will move 
March 10, 1998, to 1340 Financial Blvd. Near McCarran and Mill Streets. 
The public room at 850 Harvard Way will close for business at noon, 
March 10, 1998, and will reopen at 1340 Financial Blvd. on March 16, 
1998.

Reno, Nevada, 89502-2055, telephone for 850 Harvard Way, 702-785-6632, 
for 1340 Financial Blvd., 702-861-6400.
