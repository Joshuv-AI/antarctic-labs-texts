ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Notice; Withdrawal

AGENCY: U.S. Election Assistance Commission.

ACTION: Sunshine Act notice; notice of public agenda, withdrawal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action withdraws the Sunshine Act Notice published in the 
Federal Register on August 21, 2023, Public Meeting: U.S. Election 
Assistance Commission.

DATES: As of August 22, 2023, the Sunshine Act Notice published in the 
Federal Register on August 21, 2023 (88 FR 56811) is withdrawn.
