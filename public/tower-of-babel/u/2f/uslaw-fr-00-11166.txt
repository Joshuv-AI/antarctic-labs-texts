Airport Privatization Pilot Program; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION:  Notice of acceptance for review: Preliminary application for 
Rafael Hernandex Airport, Aguadilla, Puerto Rico; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to a notice of acceptance 
for review. The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) completed its 
review of the Rafael Hernandez Airport (BQN) preliminary application 
for participation in the airport privatization pilot program. The 
preliminary application was accepted for review, with a filing date of 
December 20, 1999.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kevin C. Willis (202) 267-8741.
