Tribal Consultation on Indian Education Topics; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Indian Affairs, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of tribal consultation meetings; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the notice that was published in the 
Federal Register on May 7, 2002 (67 FR 30722), by changing the comment 
deadline.

Correction

    On page 30722, in the second column under the DATES section, the 
first sentence ``Comments are due on or before June 28, 2002.'' is 
corrected to read: ``Comments are due on or before July 26, 2002.''
    All other information published in the May 7, 2002 notice remains 
unchanged.
