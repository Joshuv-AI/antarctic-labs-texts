DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 Department of the Air Force

 

USAF Scientific Advisory Board Meeting

    The Intelligence Mission Panel of the USAF Scientific Advisory 
Board will meet on 7-8 December 1994 at The Air Intelligence Agency, 
Kelly AFB, San Antonio, TX from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
    The purpose of the meeting is to provide technical assistance to HQ 
AIA in the development of the ``Airborne Needs in 2015''.
    The meeting will be closed to the public in accordance with Section 
552b of Title 5, United States Code, specifically subparagraphs (1) and 
(4) thereof.
    For further information, contact the Scientific Advisory Board 
Secretariat at (703) 697-8845.
Patsy J. Conner,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
