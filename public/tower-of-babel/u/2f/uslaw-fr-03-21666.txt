DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Office of Science; Advanced Scientific Computing Advisory 
Committee

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    On July 30, 2003, the Department of Energy published a notice of 
open meeting announcing a meeting of the Advanced Scientific Computing 
Advisory Committee 68 FR 44756. Today's notice is announcing the 
cancellation of the meeting scheduled for August 21-22, 2003, because 
the committee will not have a quorum of members for the meeting.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on August 20, 2003.
Rachel M. Samuel,
Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
