OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Proposed Collection; Comment Request for Reclearance of 
Information Collection, OPM Form 805 Series

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that OPM will submit a 
request to the Office of Management and Budget for reclearance of the 
OPM Form 805 Series that collects information from the public. OPM Form 
805, Application to be Listed Under the Voting Rights Act of 1965, is 
used to elicit information from persons applying for voter registration 
under the authority of the Voting Rights Act of 1965. The requirements 
for voter eligibility vary from State to State; therefore, OPM Form 805 
is a blanket number covering a number of forms which conform to the 
individual State's requirements. For a number of years, there have been 
forms for 10 States: Alabama, Arizona, Georgia, Louisiana, Mississippi, 
New Mexico, North Carolina, South Carolina, Texas (English and Spanish 
language versions), and Utah. Because OPM has never been asked to list 
voters in Arizona, New Mexico, North Carolina, and Utah, the approval 
of these four forms is being permitted to lapse at the request of the 
Voting Rights Section in the Civil Rights Division of the Department of 
Justice. The form requires 20 minutes to complete. Approximately 10 
individuals complete the form annually for a total public burden of 4 
hours. For copies of this proposal call James M. Farron on (202) 418-
3208 or e-mail to [email protected].

DATES: Comments on this proposal should be received on or before March 
17, 1997.
