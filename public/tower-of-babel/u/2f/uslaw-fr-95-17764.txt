ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

Public Notice; Extension of Public Comment Period for the Lake 
Michigan Lakewide Management Plan

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Extension of public comment period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

ACTION: This notice extends the public comment period for the revised 
draft Lakewide Management Plan (LaMP) for Lake Michigan by 30 days, 
through August 5, 1995. A Notice of Availability for the draft Lake 
Michigan LaMP was published in the Federal Register on May 5, 1995 (60 
FR 22381-22388), soliciting public review and comment.
    All comments should be addressed to Jeanette Morris-Collins, 
Environmental Protection Assistant, U.S. EPA, Region 5 (WQ-16J), 77 
West Jackson Boulevard, Chicago, Illinois 60604 (telephone: 312/886-
0152).
