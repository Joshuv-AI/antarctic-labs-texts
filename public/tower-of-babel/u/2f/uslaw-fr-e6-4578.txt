FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Transportation Intermediary License Rescission of Order of 
Revocation

    Notice is hereby given that the Order revoking the following 
license is being rescinded by the Federal Maritime Commission pursuant 
to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718) and the 
regulations of the Commission pertaining to the licensing of Ocean 
Transportation Intermediaries, 46 CFR part 515.
    License Number: 015795N.
    Name: Eurocargo Express, LLC dba Eurocargo Express.
    Address: 5250 West Century Blvd., Suite 620, Los Angeles, CA 90045.
    Order Published: FR: 02/23/06 (Volume 71, No. 36, Pg. 9342).

Peter J. King,
Deputy Director, Bureau of Certification and Licensing.
