DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Notice

October 15, 1996.
    The members of the Commission will be attending the DOE/NARUC 
National Electricity Forum to be held October 20-22, 1996 in Santa Fe, 
New Mexico at the Sweeney Convention Center. The Forum is open to the 
public. Further information about registering may be obtained from Ann 
Thompson at NARUC, (202) 898-2210.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
