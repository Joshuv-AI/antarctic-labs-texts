DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Fish and Wildlife Service


Issuance of Permit for Marine Mammals

    On April 21, 2000, a notice was published in the Federal Register, 
Vol. 65, No. 92, Page 30426, that an application had been filed with 
the Fish and Wildlife Service by James Adams, Fruitport, MI, for a 
permit (PRT-026025) to import a sport-hunted polar bear (Ursus 
maritimus) trophy, taken from the Southern Beaufort Sea polar bear 
population, Northwest Territories, Canada for personal use.
    Notice is hereby given that on July 12, 2000, as authorized by the 
provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended (16 
U.S.C. 1361 et seq.) the Fish and Wildlife Service authorized the 
requested permit subject to certain conditions set forth therein.
