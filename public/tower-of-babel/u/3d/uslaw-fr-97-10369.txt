STATUS: Open.

ADDRESSES: The Old Post Office Building, Room M-09, 1100 Pennsylvania 
Avenue, NW, Washington, D.C. 20506.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Isa Bauerlein, Special Assistant to the Director, Institute of Museum 
and Library Services, 1100 Pennsylvania Avenue, N.W., Room 510, 
Washington, D.C. 20506--(202) 606-8536.

8636 at least seven (7) days prior to the meeting date.
