ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

40 CFR Part 170


Worker Protection Standard

CFR Correction

    In Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 150 to 189, 
revised as of July 1, 2006, in Sec.  170.112, on page 212, paragraph 
(a)(1) is corrected to read as follows:


Sec.  170.112  Entry restrictions.

    (a) * * * (1) After the application of any pesticide on an 
agricultural establishment, the agricultural employer shall not allow 
or direct any worker to enter or to remain in the treated area before 
the restricted-entry interval specified on the pesticide labeling has 
expired, except as provided in this section.
