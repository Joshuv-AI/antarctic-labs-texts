Koppers Charleston Superfund Site; Notice To Rescind Federal 
Register Notice Dated October 1, 2002

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency.

ACTION: Notice to rescind previous Federal Register notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On October 1, 2002 at 67 FR 61624, the Environmental 
Protection Agency (EPA) published a Notice of Proposed Settlement for 
response costs incurred by EPA at the Koppers Charleston Superfund Site 
located in Charleston, Charleston County, South Carolina. That notice 
was published prematurely. The purpose of this notice is to rescind 
EPA's October 1, 2002 Federal Register Notice regarding the settlement 
of response costs at the Site. The Notice of Proposed Settlement for 
the Site may be republished in the future following final approval of 
the settlement.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Paula Batchelor at 404-562-8887.
