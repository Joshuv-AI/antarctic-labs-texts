NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

10 CFR Parts 30, 32, and 35

RIN: 3150-AD69

 

Preparation, Transfer for Commercial Distribution, and Use of 
Byproduct Material for Medical Use; Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Final rule: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects a final rule, published in the Federal 
Register on December 2, 1994 (59 FR 61767), that amends the regulations 
for the medical use of byproduct material. This action is necessary to 
correct language in the preamble to reflect regulatory text, remove an 
obsolete cross-reference, and specify a date certain in document text.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 1995.

ADDRESSES: Copies of the public record, including the final regulatory 
analysis and any public comments received on the proposed rule, may be 
examined and copied for a fee in the Commission's Public Document Room 
at 2120 L Street, NW. (Lower Level), Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. Anthony N. Tse, Office of Nuclear 
Regulatory Research, U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Washington, DC 
20555, telephone (301) 415-6233.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
    1. On page 61777, the third column, in the third paragraph of the 
discussion under the heading Section 35.981 Training for Experienced 
Nuclear Pharmacists, the words ``on or before (the date of publication 
in the Federal Register)'' should read ``before December 2, 1994,''.
    2. On page 61781, in Sec. 32.72(b)(4), the words ``(the date of 
publication in the Federal Register)'' should read ``December 2, 
1994,''.
    3. On page 61785, Sec. 35.415(a)(1) is corrected to read as 
follows:


Sec. 35.415  Safety precautions.

    (a) * * *
    (1) Not quarter the patient or the human research subject in the 
same room with an individual who is not receiving radiation therapy 
unless the licensee can demonstrate compliance with the requirements of 
Sec. 20.1301(a) of this chapter at a distance of 1 meter from the 
implant;
* * * * *
    4. On page 61787, in Sec. 35.981, the words ``(the date of 
publication in the Federal Register)'' should read ``December 2, 
1994,''.
