California: Elimination of Dunes Vista Long Term Visitor Area

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management, El Centro Field Office will 
eliminate Dunes Vista as one of the designated Long-Term Visitor Areas 
available in the California Desert District.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 15, 1998.
