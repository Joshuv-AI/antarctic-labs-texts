DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

National Park Service


Concession Contracts and Permits; Expiring Contracts; Extension

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Public notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to 36 CFR 51.23, notice is hereby given that the 
National Park Service intends to extend the following expiring 
concession contracts for a period of up to 2 years, or until such time 
as a new contract is awarded, whichever occurs sooner.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: All of the listed concession authorizations 
will expire on December 31, 2002. The National Park Service has 
determined that the proposed short-term extensions are necessary in 
order to avoid interruption of visitor services and has taken all 
reasonable and appropriate steps to consider alternatives to avoid such 
interruption. These extensions will allOW the National Park Service to 
develop prospectuses leading to the competitive selection of 
concessioners for new long-term concession contracts covering these 
operations.
