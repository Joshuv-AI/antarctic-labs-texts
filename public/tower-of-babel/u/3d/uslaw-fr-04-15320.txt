J&L Specialty Steel, LLC, Louisville Plant, Louisville, OH; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 10, 2004 in response to a petition 
filed by a company official on behalf of workers of J&L Specialty 
Steel, LLC, Louisville Plant, Louisville, Ohio.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on September 9, 2002 which remains in effect until 
September 9, 2004 (TA-W-39,575). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve

[[Page 40982]]

no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of June, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
