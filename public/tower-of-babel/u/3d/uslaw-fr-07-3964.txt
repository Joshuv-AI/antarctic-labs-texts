Federal Aviation Administration


Advisory Circular 33.63-1, Turbine Engine Vibration

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of issuance of advisory circular.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the issuance of Advisory Circular (AC) 
33.63-1, Turbine Engine Vibration. This advisory circular (AC) provides 
guidance and acceptable methods, but not the only methods, that may be 
used to demonstrate compliance with the vibration requirements of Sec.  
33.63 of Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR part 33).

DATES: The Engine and Propeller Directorate issued AC 33.63-1 on July 
25, 2007.
