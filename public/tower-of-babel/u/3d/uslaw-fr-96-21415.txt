FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM

Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding 
Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 96-17095) published on page 
35215 - 35216 of the issue for Friday, July 5, 1996.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco heading, the entry 
for Eggemeyer Corp., Castle Creek Capital Partners Fund--I, L.P., 
Castle Creek Capital, L.L.C., is revised to read as follows:
    1. Eggemeyer Advisory Corp., Castle Creek Capital Partners Fund--I, 
L.P., and Castle Creek Capital, L.L.C., all of Chicago, Illinois; to 
become bank holding companies by acquiring more than 25 percent of the 
voting shares of Monarch Bancorp, Laguna Niguel, California, and 
thereby indirectly acquire control of Monarch Bank, Laguna Niguel, 
California, and Western Bank, Los Angeles, California.
    Comments on this application must be received by September 3, 1996.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, August 16, 
1996.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
