DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration

 

Advisory Committees; Renewals

AGENCY:  Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION:  Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announces the renewal 
of FDA advisory committees by the Commissioner of Food and Drugs. This 
notice is issued under the Federal Advisory Committee Act of October 6, 
1972 (Pub. L. 92-463 (5 U.S.C. app. 2)).

DATES:  Authority for these committees will expire on the dates 
indicated below unless the Commissioner formally determines that 
renewal is in the public interest.
