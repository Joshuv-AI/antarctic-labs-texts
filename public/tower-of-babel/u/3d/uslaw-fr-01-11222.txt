PRESIDIO TRUST


Notice of Public Meeting

AGENCY: The Presidio Trust.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with Sec. 103(c)(6) of the Presidio Trust Act, 
16 U.S.C. 460bb note, Title I of Public Law 104-333, 110 Stat. 4097, 
and in accordance with the Presidio Trust's bylaws, notice is hereby 
given that a public meeting of the Presidio Trust Board of Directors 
will be held from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. on Wednesday, May 23, 2001, 
at the Officers' Club, 50 Moraga Avenue, Presidio of San Francisco, 
California. The Presidio Trust was created by Congress in 1996 to 
manage approximately eighty percent of the former U.S. Army base known 
as the Presidio, in San Francisco, California.
    The purposes of this meeting are to: (1) Receive staff reports 
regarding environmental remediation, wildlife at the Presidio, and 
historic building rehabilitation; (2) receive a staff report and take 
action regarding the Vegetation Management Program; and (3) receive 
public comment in accordance with the Trust's Public Outreach Policy.
    Time: The meeting will be held from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. on 
Wednesday, May 23, 2001.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Officers' Club, 50 Moraga 
Avenue, Presidio of San Francisco.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Craig Middleton, Deputy Director for 
Operations and Governmental Affairs, the Presidio Trust, 34 Graham 
Street, P.O. Box 29052, San Francisco, California 94129-0052, 
Telephone: (415) 561-5300.

    Dated: April 30, 2001.
Karen A. Cook,
General Counsel.
