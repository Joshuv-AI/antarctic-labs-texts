Pacific Gas and Electric Company; Notice of Filing

November 18, 1996.
    Take notice that on November 1, 1996, Pacific Gas and Electric 
Company (PG&E) tendered for filing an amendment (Second Amendment) to 
the Control Area and Transmission Service Agreement (Agreement) between 
PG&E and Destec Power Services, Inc. (DPS) which was filed previously 
with the Commission on December 6, 1994, in FERC Docket No. ER95-262-
000.
    The purpose of the Second Amendment is to adopt new contract 
language which reflects settlement of various terms which were previous 
issues between the Parties.
    Copies of the filing were served upon DPS and California Public 
Utilities Commission.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 18 CFR 385.214). All such 
motions or protests should be filed on or before November 29, 1996. 
Protests will be considered by the Commission in determining the 
appropriate action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants 
parties to the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must 
file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the 
Commission and are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
