MK Gold Company, Searchlight, Nevada; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 25, 2002 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed on behalf of workers at MK Gold Company, Searchlight, Nevada.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
is already in effect (TA-W-39,592, as amended). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 23rd day of April, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
