DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:
    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date(s) of Meeting: 26 & 27 March 2002.
    Time(s) of Meeting: 0900-1700, 26 March 2002, 0900-1300, 27 March 
2002.
    Place: SAIC.
    1. The Acquisition and Technology Panel, Army Science Board FY02 
Summer Study on ``Affordability of the Objective Force'' is holding a 
meeting on 26-27 March 2002. The meeting will be held at SAIC, 1710 
SAIC Drive, McLean, VA. The meeting will begin at 0900hrs on the 26th 
and will end at approximately 1300hrs on th 27th. For further 
information, please contact Tom Conway--703-617-9438 or e-mail Conway, 
Tom Civ AMCRDA01.

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
