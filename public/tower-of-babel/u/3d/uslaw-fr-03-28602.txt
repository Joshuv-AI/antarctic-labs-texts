[30Day-69-03]


Proposed Data Collections Submitted for Public Comment and 
Recommendations Withdrawal

    A notice announcing a 30-Day comment period for the proposed 
project: Health and Safety Outcomes Related to Work Schedules in 
Nurses--NEW--The National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health 
(NIOSH), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) was 
inadvertently republished in the Federal Register November 5, 2003, [68 
FR 62607 through 62608]. This notice is hereby withdrawn.
    The official publication of this notice was September 18, 2003, [68 
FR 54732] and stands as published.

    Dated: November 10, 2003.
James D. Seligman,
Chief Information Officer, Office of the Chief Operations Officer, 
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
