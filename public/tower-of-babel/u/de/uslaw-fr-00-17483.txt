Notice of Realty Action; Recreation and Public Purposes (R&PP) 
Act Classification; Arizona; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management published a document in the 
Federal Register of May 27, 1999, concerning a R&PP Classification in 
Mohave County, Arizona. The document contained an incorrect section 
number in the legal description.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Laurie Ford, Realty Specialist, (435) 
688-3271.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of May 27, 1999, in FR Doc. 64-102, on page 
28832, the second section of the legal description should read section 
10 instead of section 9 as follows:

    Sec. 10, NE\1/4\NE\1/4\NW\1/4\NE\1/4\ (2.5 acres for a fire 
station).
