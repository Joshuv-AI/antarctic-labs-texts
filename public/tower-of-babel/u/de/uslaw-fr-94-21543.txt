DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
EERE-Denver Regional Support Office

 

Financial Assistance Award; Intent to Award Grant to the State of 
Utah

AGENCY: Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of noncompetitive financial assistance award.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Energy announces, pursuant to the DOE 
Financial Assistance Rules 10 CFR 600.7, that it intends to award a 
grant based on a noncompetitive determination to the State of Utah to 
support the activities of the Region VIII Weatherization Managers 
Association in the amount of $325,000. The State of Utah is the sponsor 
of the Weatherization Managers Association which is an organization 
comprised of representatives from each of the six states in the region: 
Colorado, Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota, Utah and Wyoming.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The proposed financial assistance to the 
State of Utah for the Weatherization Managers Association will assist 
the six states' Weatherization Assistance Programs by supporting and 
stimulating technical development activities which improve the depth of 
knowledge and the caliber of technical skills of the regional 
Weatherization grantees and subgrantees. The project's objectives are 
to facilitate technical assistance activities identified by the 
Weatherization Managers Association which benefit all of the state 
programs in the region.
    The proposed project meets the criteria of 10 CFR Part 600.7 
(b)(2)(i) (B) and (D) in that the activity would be conducted by the 
Weatherization Managers Association using their own resources. However, 
DOE support of the activity will enhance the public benefits to be 
derived by increasing the amount and quality of training to be 
presented. The Denver Support Office knows of no other entity which is 
conducting or planning to conduct any of these activities.
    The Weatherization Managers Association sponsored by the State of 
Utah is the only region-specific organization of which every 
Weatherization program is a member and, therefore, possesses exclusive 
capability to perform the activities successfully.

Project Period

    The initial term of the award will be for twelve (12) months, for a 
possible project period of three years from the initial date of award. 
Possible additional funding in future years will be based on 
satisfactory completion of the initial term and availability of 
funding.
