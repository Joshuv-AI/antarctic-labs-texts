Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 16, 2008, 8 a.m. to June 
17, 2008, 5 p.m., Four Points Sheraton, 1201 K Street, NW., Washington,

FR 24296-24298. The meeting will be held June 23, 2008. The meeting
