Notice Providing for Opening of Public Land; Wyoming; Correction

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice will correct an error in the land description 
published in the Federal Register, Vol. 63, No. 219, page 63491, on 
November 13, 1998.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 1, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Tamara Gertsch, BLM Wyoming State 
Office, P.O. Box 1828, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82003-1828, 307-775-6115.
Correction
    The land description published in the Federal Register, Vol. 63, 
No. 219, page 63491, on November 13, 1998, is hereby corrected as 
follows:
    In the opening order notice, on page 63491, column 1, line 34, the 
legal description which reads T. 36 N., R. 108 W., is changed to read 
T. 34 N., R. 108 W.

    Dated: November 24, 1998.
Tamara Gertsch,
Realty Specialist.
