National Boating Safety Advisory Council; Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Secretary of Transportation has renewed the charter for 
the National Boating Safety Advisory Council (NBSAC) for 2 years from 
December 20, 2000 until December 20, 2002. NBSAC is a Federal advisory 
committee under 5 U.S.C. App. 2. It advises the Coast Guard on the need 
for Federal regulations and other major boating safety matters.

ADDRESSES: You may request a copy of the charter by writing to 
Commandant (G-OPB-1), U.S. Coast Guard, 2100 Second Street SW., 
Washington, DC 20593-0001; by calling 202-267-0950; or by faxing 202-
267-4850. This notice and the charter are available on the Internet at 
http://dms.dot.gov.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Albert J. Marmo, Executive 
Director of NBSAC, telephone 202-267-0950, fax 202-267-4285.

    Dated: January 17, 2001.
