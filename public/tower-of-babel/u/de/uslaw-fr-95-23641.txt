UNITED STATES INSTITUTE OF PEACE


Announcement of 1996 Solicited Grant Topics

AGENCY: United States Institute of Peace.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The agency is Soliciting Applications for Projects on the 
following topics:

Solicitation A: New Approaches to Conflict Management, Peacemaking, 
and Peacekeeping
Solicitation B: Economic and/or Environmental Factors and 
International Conflict
Solicitation C: Professional Conflict Resolution Training Programs 
and Materials
Solicitation D: Cross-Cultural Negotiation Research and Training

DATES: Application Material Available in September, 1995. Receipt Date 
for Return of Applications: January 2, 1996. Notification of Awards: 
April, 1996.
