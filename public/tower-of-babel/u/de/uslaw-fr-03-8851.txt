Nortel Networks, Department 2446, Research Triangle 
Park, NC and Including an Employee of Nortel Networks, Department 
2446, Located in New York; Amended Certification Regarding 
Eligibility To Apply for Worker Adjustment Assistance

    In accordance with Section 223 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 
2273) the Department of Labor issued a Certification of Eligibility to 
Apply for Worker Adjustment Assistance on January 17, 2003, applicable 
to workers of Nortel Networks, Department 2446, Research 
Triangle Park, North Carolina. The notice was published in the Federal 
Register on February 6, 2003 (68 FR 6212).
    At the request of the petitioner, the Department reviewed the 
certification for workers of the subject firm. New information shows 
that worker separations occurred involving an employee of Department 
2446, Research Triangle Park, North Carolina facility of 
Nortel Networks located in New York. This employee provided 
verification testing and turnup for the production of fiber optic 
backbone telecommunications network at Department 2446, 
Research Triangle Park, North Carolina location of the subject firm.
    Based on these findings, the Department is amending this 
certification to include an employee of the Research Triangle Park, 
North Carolina facility of Nortel Networks, Department 2446 
located in New York.
    The intent of the Department's certification is to include all 
workers of Nortel Networks, Department 2446 who were adversely 
affected by increased imports.
    The amended notice applicable to TA-W-50,036 is hereby issued as 
follows:

    All workers of Nortel Networks, Department 2446, 
Research Triangle Park, North Carolina (TA-W-50,036), including an 
employee of Nortel Networks, Department 2446, Research 
Triangle Park, North Carolina, located in New York (TA-W-50,036A), 
who became totally or partially separated from employment on or 
after November 5, 2001, through January 17, 2005, are eligible to 
apply for adjustment assistance under Section 223 of the Trade Act 
of 1974.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 21st day of March 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
