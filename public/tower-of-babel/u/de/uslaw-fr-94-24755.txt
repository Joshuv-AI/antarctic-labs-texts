DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[CA-942-5700-10]

 

Filing of Plats of Survey; California

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The purpose of this notice is to inform the public and 
interested state and local government officials of the latest filing of 
Plats of Survey in California.

EFFECTIVE DATES: Filing was effective at 10:00 a.m. on the date of 
submission to the Bureau of Land Management (BLM), California State 
Office, Public Room.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Clifford A. Robinson, Chief, Branch of 
Cadastral Survey, Bureau of Land Management (BLM), California State 
Office, 2800 Cottage Way, Room E-2845, Sacramento, CA 95825, 916-978-
4775.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Plats of Survey of lands described below 
have been officially filed at the California State Office, Sacramento, 
CA.

Humboldt Meridian, California

T. 10 N., R. 3 E.--Dependent resurvey and subdivision of section, 
(Group 1117) accepted August 10, 1994, to meet certain 
administrative needs of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, Northern 
California Agency.
T. 11 N., R. 3 E.--Dependent resurvey and subdivision of sections, 
(Group 1117) accepted August 10, 1994, to meet certain 
administrative needs of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, Northern 
California Agency.

Tract 37, (Group 1149) accepted August 30, 1994, to meet certain
