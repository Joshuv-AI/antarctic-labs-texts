DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. Law 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB) Analysis Panel.
    Date of Meeting: May 1-2, 2001.
    Time of Meeting: 0800-1700.
    Places: May 1--IDA; May 2--Ft. Belvoir.
    Agenda: The Analysis Panel of the Army Science Board's (ASB) 
Summer Study, ``Objective Force Soldier/Soldier Teams'' will visit 
IDA and Ft. Belvoir. These meetings will be open to the public. Any 
interested person may attend, appear before, or file statements with 
the committee at the time and in the manner permitted by the 
committee.
    For further information: Please contact Karen Williams at (407) 
384-3937.

Wayne Joyner,
Executive Assistant, Army Science Board.
