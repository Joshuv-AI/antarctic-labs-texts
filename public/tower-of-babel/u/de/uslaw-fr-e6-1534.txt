DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration


Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request; Correction

AGENCY: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).
SUMMARY: This corrects the title of the information collection (OMB 
Control No. 0648-471) submitted to the Office of Management and Budget 
for review.
    The notice was published on January 17, 2006 (Vol. 71, No. 10, page 
2514).

Correction

    The title of the information collection was listed as ``Deep Seabed 
Mining Exploration Licenses.'' The correct title is ``Highly Migratory 
Species Scientific Research Permits, Exempted Fishing Permits, and 
Letters of Authorization''.

    Dated: January 31, 2006.
Gwellnar Banks,
Management Analyst, Office of the Chief Information Officer.
