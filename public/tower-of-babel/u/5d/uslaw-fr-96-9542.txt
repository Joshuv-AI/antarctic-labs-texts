DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Health Resources and Services Administration


The Ryan White Comprehensive Aids Resources Emergency Act of 
1990; Availability of Funds for Early Intervention Services

AGENCY: Health Resources and Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice of extension of application due date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice extends the due date previously published in the 
Federal Register on March 20, 1996 (61 FR 11424) for applications for 
discretionary grants to provide outpatient early intervention services 
including primary care services with respect to human immunodeficiency 
virus (HIV) disease. The new due date is June 12, 1996. All other 
information remains unchanged.

    Dated: April 12, 1996.
Ciro V. Sumaya,
Administrator.
