DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE


Notice of Extension of Comment Period on Consent Decree Under the 
Clean Air Act

    Under 28 CFR 50.7, notice is hereby given that the comment period 
for the proposed Consent Decree lodged on October 22, 1998, with the 
United States District Court for the District of Columbia in United 
States v. Detroit Diesel Corporation, Civil Action No. 98-2548 (HHK), 
is being extended through January 12, 1999. The original notice of this 
proposed settlement, which summarizes the settlement and identifies 
where copies of the Consent Decree may be obtained, was published in 
the Federal Register on November 3, 1998, Vol. 63, No. 212, Pg. 59331-
59332. Comments should be addressed to the Assistant Attorney General 
of the Environment and Natural Resources Division, Department of 
Justice, Washington, DC 20530, should refer to United States v. Detroit 
Diesel Corporation, Civil Action No. 98-2548 (HHK), D.J. Ref. 90-5-2-1-
2253, and should be received by January 12, 1999.
Joel M. Gross,
Chief, Environmental Enforcement Section, Environment and Natural 
Resources Division.
