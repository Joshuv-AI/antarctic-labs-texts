Consolidated Metco, Inc., Rivergate Plant, Portland, OR; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 21, 2000, in response to a worker petition 
which was filed by the International Association of Machinists and 
Aerospace Workers, Lodge 1432, on behalf of workers at Consolidated 
Metco, Inc., Rivergate Plant, Portland, Oregon.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, 
and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 12th day of October 2000.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
