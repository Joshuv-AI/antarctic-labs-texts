DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
[Dept. Circ. 570, 1995--Rev., Supp. No. 10]


Surety Companies Acceptable on Federal Bonds; Termination of 
Authority: Delta Casualty Company

    Notice is hereby given that the Certificate of Authority issued by 
the Treasury to DELTA CASUALTY COMPANY, of Chicago, Illinois, under the 
United States Code, Title 31, Sections 9304-9308, to qualify as an 
acceptable surety on Federal bonds is terminated effective today.
    The Company was last listed as an acceptable surety on Federal 
bonds at 60 FR 34440, June 30, 1995.
    With respect to any bonds currently in force with Delta Casualty 
Company, bond-approving officers may let such bonds run to expiration 
and need not secure new bonds. However, no new bonds should be accepted 
from the Company. In addition, bonds that are continuous in nature 
should not be renewed.
