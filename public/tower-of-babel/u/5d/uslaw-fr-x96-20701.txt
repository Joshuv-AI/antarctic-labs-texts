Correction

    In rule document 96-11493 beginning on page 21904 in the issue of 
Friday, May 10, 1996, make the following corrections:


Sec. 29.610  [Corrected]

    1. On page 21907, in the third column, Sec. 29.610(d)(3) should 
read: ``Provide and electrical return path, under both normal and fault 
conditions, on rotorcraft having grounded electrical systems; and''.
    2. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 29.610(d)(4), in 
the third line, insert ``electrical and'' after ``essential''.


Sec. 29.631  [Correctd]

    3. On the same page, in the same column, in Sec. 29.631, in the 
first line, ``designated'' should read ``designed''.
