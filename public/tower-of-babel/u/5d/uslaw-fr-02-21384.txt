Columbia Gas Transmission Corporation; Notice of Public Meeting

August 16, 2002.
    Take notice that a meeting is scheduled in the above-captioned 
proceedings for September 4, 2002, at 2 p.m., in Room No. 62-26, at the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426. The meeting was requested by Columbia Gas 
Transmission Corporation for the purpose of discussing potential 
modifications to the projects in the referenced dockets. Any interested 
persons may attend.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
