DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP97-3-009]


Texas Eastern Transmission Corporation; Notice of Compliance 
Filing

November 14, 1997.
    Take notice that on November 12, 1997, Texas Eastern Transmission 
Corporation (Texas Eastern) tendered for filing as part of its FERC Gas 
Tariff, Sixth Revised Volume No. 1, the following tariff sheet, to 
become effective November 1, 1997:

Sub Third Revised Sheet No. 681.

    Texas Eastern asserts that the purpose of this filing is to comply 
with the Commission's Letter Order issued October 27, 1997, in Docket 
No. RP97-3-008, which required Texas Eastern to file a substitute 
tariff sheet reflecting the correct version numbers for GISB standards 
incorporated by reference on Sheet No. 681
    Texas Eastern states that the above listed tariff sheet contains 
the modifications required by the October 27, 1997 letter order.
    Texas Eastern states that copies of the filing were served on all 
affected parties.
    Any person desiring to protest this filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed as 
provided in Section 154.210 of the Commission's Regulations. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission 
and are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
