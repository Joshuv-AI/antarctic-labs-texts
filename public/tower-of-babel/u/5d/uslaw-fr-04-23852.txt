FARM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION


Farm Credit Administration Board; Regular Meeting

AGENCY: Farm Credit Administration.
SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given, pursuant to the Government in the 
Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 552b(e)(3)), that the November 10, 2004 regular 
meeting of the Farm Credit Administration Board (Board) has been 
rescheduled. The regular meeting of the Board will be held November 18, 
2004 starting at 9 a.m. An agenda for this meeting will be published at 
a later date.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jeanette C. Brinkley, Secretary to the 
Farm Credit Administration Board, (703) 883-4009, TTY (703) 883-4056.

ADDRESSES: Farm Credit Administration, 1501 Farm Credit Drive, McLean, 
Virginia 22102-5090.

    Dated: October 20, 2004.
Jeanette C. Brinkley,
Secretary, Farm Credit Administration Board.
