FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD


Sunshine Act Meeting Notice: Addition of Agenda Item

Federal Register Citation of Previous Announcement: 70 FR 53796, 
September 12, 2005.

Addition of Agenda Item: The Board of Directors determined that agency 
business required the addition on less than seven days notice and that 
no earlier notice was practicable of the following item to the open 
portion of the meeting held on September 14, 2005:
    Expedited Regulatory Relief to Support Victims of Hurricane 
Katrina. The Board of Directors will consider a resolution that would 
extend any regulatory relief granted to an individual Federal Home Loan 
Bank for purposes of assisting victims of Hurricane Katrina to any or 
all of the other Banks, so that the members of other Banks also may 
provide housing and financial assistance to victims of Hurricane 
Katrina throughout the country.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shelia Willis, Paralegal Specialist, 
Office of General Counsel, at (202) 408-2876 or [email protected].

    Dated: September 14, 2005.

    By the Federal Housing Finance Board.
John P. Kennedy,
General Counsel.
