DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

 

Veterans' Advisory Committee on Education, Notice of Meeting 
Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs notice that a meeting of the 
Veterans' Advisory Committee on Education, authorized by 38 U.S.C. 
3692, to be held on November 14, 1994 and November 15, 1994, is hereby 
cancelled. The notice appeared in the Federal Register on November 1, 
1994, on page 54674.
    If you have any questions, please contact Mrs. Celia P. Dollarhide, 
Director, Education Service (phone 202-273-7132).

    Dated: November 7, 1994.

    By direction of the Secretary.
Heyward Bannister,
Committee Management Officer.
