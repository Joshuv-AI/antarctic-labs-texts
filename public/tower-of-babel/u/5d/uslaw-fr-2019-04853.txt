DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin 
Diseases; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases Special 
Emphasis Panel, March 29, 2019, 08:00 a.m. to March 29, 2019, 05:00 
p.m., National Institutes of Health, One Democracy Plaza, 6701 
Democracy Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was published in the 
Federal Register on February 07, 2019, 84 FR 2551.
    This meeting is being amended to change the date of the meeting 
from March 29, 2019 to April 1, 2019. This meeting is closed to the 
public. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: March 12, 2019.
Sylvia L. Neal,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
