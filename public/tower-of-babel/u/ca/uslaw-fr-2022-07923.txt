COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Maine Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; cancellation of meeting date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a meeting of the Maine Advisory Committee. 
The meeting scheduled for Wednesday, May 4, 2022, at 12:00 p.m. (ET) is 
cancelled. The notice is in the Federal Register of Thursday, March 3, 
2022, in FR Doc. 2022-04438, in the first of page 12078 and the first 
and second columns of page 12079.
