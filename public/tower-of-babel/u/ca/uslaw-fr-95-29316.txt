FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

Ocean Freight Forwarder License; Rescission of Order of 
Revocation

the licenses of the following ocean freight forwarders have been 
rescinded by the Federal Maritime Commission pursuant to section 19 of 
the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 1718) and the regulations of 
the Commission pertaining to the licensing of ocean freight forwarders, 
46 CFR 510.
