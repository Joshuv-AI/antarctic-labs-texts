Petition For Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2024-2630.
    Petitioner: Merlin Labs, Inc.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  91.9(a) and 91.319(c).
    Description of Relief Sought: Petitioner is requesting an exemption 
from 14 CFR 91.9(a) and 91.319(c) to allow for the operation of two 
modified Cessna 208B Caravans, which have been issued Special 
Airworthiness Certificates in the Experimental Category, over densely 
populated areas outside of congested airways for the purposes of 
research and development. The petitioner is developing an advanced 
autopilot system which requires flight testing while conducting 
published instrument approaches in-and-out of Laurence G. Hanscom Field 
Airport (KBED). Flights over densely populated areas would be 
restricted to operations which are necessary to conduct published 
instrument approaches in-and-out of KBED. At no point will the aircraft 
be operated as an unmanned system, or an optionally piloted system.
