DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Sunshine Act Meeting

February 4, 1998.
    The following notice of meeting is published pursuant to section 
3(A) of the Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. No. 94-409), 5 
U.S.C. 552B:

AGENCY HOLDING MEETING: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

DATE AND TIME: February 11, 1998, 10:00 a.m.

PLACE: Room 2C, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Agenda.

    *Note--Items listed on the agenda may be deleted without further 
notice.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: David P. Boergers, Acting 
Secretary, telephone (202) 208-0400. For a recording listing items 
stricken from or added to the meeting, call (202) 208-1627.
    This is a list of matters to be considered by the Commission. It 
does not include a listing of all papers relevant to the items on the 
agenda; however, all public documents may be examined in the Reference 
and Information Center.
