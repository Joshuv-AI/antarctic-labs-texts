INTER-AMERICAN FOUNDATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Draft Agenda for Board of Directors 
Meeting, January 21, 2005, 9 a.m.-12:30 p.m.

    The meeting will be held at the Office of Entergy Corporation, 101 
Constitution Avenue, NW., Suite 200 East, Washington, DC 20001.
    The meeting will be closed as provided in 22 CFR 1004.4(f) to 
discuss matters related to the search for candidates for the position 
of President of the Inter-American Foundation.

9 a.m. Call to order. Begin executive session.
12 p.m. Break for lunch.
1:30 p.m. Continue executive session.
4 p.m. Close.

Jocelyn Nieva,
Acting General Counsel.
