DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Commission Staff Attendance

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission hereby gives notice that 
members of the Commission's staff may attend the following meetings 
related to the transmission planning activities of the Midwest 
Independent Transmission System Operator, Inc. and Southwest Power 
Pool:

Order No. 1000-MISO-SPP Workshop--July 9-10

    The above-referenced meeting will be held at:

Hilton St. Louis at the Ballpark, One South Broadway, St. Louis, MO 
63102

    The above-referenced meeting is open to the public.
    Further information may be found at http://spp.org/.
