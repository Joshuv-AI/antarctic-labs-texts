Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 176--Rockford, Illinois, Authorization 
of Production Activity, UniCarriers Americas Corporation (Forklift 
Engines and Assemblies), Marengo, Illinois

    On February 18, 2020, UniCarriers Americas Corporation submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facilities within Subzone 176E, in Marengo, Illinois.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (85 FR 12497, March 3, 2020). On June 
17, 2020, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision that 
no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
Section 400.14.

    Dated: June 17, 2020.
Andrew McGilvray,
Executive Secretary.
