Colorado Interstate Gas Company; Notice of Technical Conference

February 13, 1996.
    A technical conference well be held to discuss issues raised in the 
above-captioned proceeding on Tuesday, March 5, 1996, at 9:30 a.m., in 
room 3M2B, at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 
888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend. However, 
attendance does not confer party status.
    For additional information, contact Timothy W. Gordon at (202) 208-
2265.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
