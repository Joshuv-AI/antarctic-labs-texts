FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-66, RM-8729, RM-8821]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Sibley, IA, and Brandon, SD

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document allots Channel 261A to Brandon, South Dakota. 
See 61 FR 15442, April 8, 1996; The reference coordinates for Channel 
261A at Brandon, South Dakota, are 43-36-02 and 96-31-15. With this 
action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 23, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2177.
