DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Technology Demonstration

May 10, 2000.
    Take notice that, on May 24, 2000, Edward Cazalet, of Automated 
Power Exchange, Inc., will make a presentation before the Commission, 
interested members of the staff, and interested members of the public 
demonstrating the possible use of e-commerce technologies and the 
Internet to develop markets for various power products, including 
transmission rights.
    The presentation will be held on May 24, 2000, at 10:00 a.m., in 
the Commission Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 888 First Street, N.E., 
Washington, D.C. 20426.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
