DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Panhandle Eastern Pipe Line Company and Southwest Gas Storage 
Company; Notice of Technical Conference

January 16, 1998.
    A technical conference will be held to discuss issues raised in the 
above-captioned proceeding on Thursday, February 19, 1998, at 10:00 
a.m. in Room 3M3, at the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
    All interested persons and Staff are permitted to attend. However, 
attendance does not confer party status.
    For additional information, contact Timothy W. Gordon at (202) 208-
2265.
David P. Boergers,
Acting Secretary.
