Dekko Technology, Inc., Claypool, IN; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on May 11, 2004 in response to a petition filed on behalf 
of workers at Dekko Technology, Inc., Claypool, Indiana. The 
petitioners have requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 18th day of May 2004.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
