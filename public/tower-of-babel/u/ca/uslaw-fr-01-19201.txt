SUMMARY: NHTSA published a document in the Federal Register of July 24, 
2001, 66 FR 38449, concerning request for public comment on proposed 
collection of information. The document contained an incorrect docket 
number.
    The purpose of this notice is to notify the public of this error so 
comments are submitted to the correct docket number.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Walter Culbreath, 202-366-1566.

01-18402, on page 38449, in the third column at the bottom of the page, 
correct the ``Docket Number'' to read:

[U.S. DOT Docket Number NHTSA-2001-10113]
