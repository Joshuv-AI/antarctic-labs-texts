ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: By a vote of 5-0 on June 19, the Commission 
determined pursuant to U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the 
Commission's rules that ``Discussion of Intragovernmental Issues'' 
(Closed--Ex. 4 and 9) be held on June 19, and on less than one week's 
notice to the public.
    By a vote of 5-0 on June 20, the Commission determined pursuant to 
U.S.C. 552b(e) and Sec. 9.107(a) of the Commission's rules that 
``Affirmation of Carolina Power & Light Company (Shearon Harris Nuclear 
Power Plant), Docket No. 50-400-LA, LBP-00-12 (Memorandum and Order 
Ruling on Designation of Issues for an Evidentiary Hearing) (May 5, 
2000)'' (Public Meeting) be held on June 20, and on less than one 
week's notice to the public.
