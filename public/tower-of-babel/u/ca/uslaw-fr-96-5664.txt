NATIONAL FOUNDATION ON THE ARTS AND THE HUMANITIES


Commission of Fine Arts; Meeting

    The Commission of Fine Arts' meeting scheduled for 14 March 1996 
has been cancelled. The next meeting is scheduled for 18 April 1996 at 
10:00 a.m. in the Commission's offices in the Pension Building, Suite 
312, Judiciary Square, 441 F Street NW., Washington, DC 20001 to 
discuss various projects affecting the appearance of Washington, DC, 
including buildings, memorials, parks, etc.; also matters of design 
referred by other agencies of the government.
    Inquiries regarding the agenda and requests to submit written or 
oral statements should be addressed to Charles H. Atherton, Secretary, 
Commission of Fine Arts, at the above address or call the above number.

    Dated in Washington, DC on March 4, 1996.
Charles H. Atherton,
Secretary.
