NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION


State of Ohio: NRC Staff Assessment of a Proposed Agreement 
Between the Nuclear Regulatory Commission and the Sate of Ohio; 
Correction

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice of a proposed agreement with the State of Ohio; 
Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects two notices appearing in the Federal 
Register on March 18, 1999 (64 FR 13453), and March 25, 1999 (64 FR 
14473). This action is necessary to correct the comment period 
expiration date in each notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David L. Meyer, Chief, Rules and 
Directives Branch, Division of Administrative Services, Office of 
Administration, Washington, D.C. 20555-0001, telephone 301-415-7162, e-
mail [email protected].
    1. In the Federal Register dated March 18, 1999, on page 13453, in 
the second column, under the DATES heading, April 19, 1999, is 
corrected to read April 12, 1999.
    2. In the Federal Register dated March 25, 1999, on page 14473, in 
the second column, under the DATES heading, April 26, 1999, is 
corrected to read April 12, 1999.

    Dated at Rockville, Maryland, this 1st day of April 1999.

    For the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
