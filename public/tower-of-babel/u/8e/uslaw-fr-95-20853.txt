Revocation of Customs Broker Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Customs Service, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Broker license revocations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that on July 5, 1995, the Secretary of 
the Treasury, pursuant to Section 641, Tariff Act of 1930, as amended, 
(19 U.S.C. 1641), and Part 111.74 of the Customs Regulations, as 
amended (19 CFR 111.74), ordered the revocation of the following 
Customs broker licenses due to the failure of the broker to file the 
status report as required by 19 CFR 111.30(d). The list of affected
