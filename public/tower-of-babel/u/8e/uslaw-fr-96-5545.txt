DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-31,949]


P & K Dress Corporation, Little Falls, New York; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on December 11, 1995 in response to a worker at P & K 
Dress Corporation, Little Falls, New York.
    An active certification covering the petitioning group of workers 
remains in effect (TA-W-31,710). Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 28th day of February, 1996.
Russell T. Kile,
Acting Program Manager, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
