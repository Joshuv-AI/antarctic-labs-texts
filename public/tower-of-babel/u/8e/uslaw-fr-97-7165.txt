DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. EG93-49-000]


Clarke Generating Company, L.P; Notice of Surrender of Exempt 
Wholesale Generator Status

March 17, 1997.
    Take notice that on March 11, 1997, pursuant to section 365.7 of 
the Commission's regulations, 18 CFR 365.7, Clarke Generating Company, 
L.P. filed notification that it surrenders its status as an exempt 
wholesale generator under section 32(a)(1) of the Public Utility 
Holding Company Act of 1935, as amended.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
