FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

FEDERAL REGISTER CITATION NOTICE OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT:  84 FR 716.

PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TIME AND DATE OF THE MEETING:  Tuesday, February 
5, 2019 at 10:00 a.m. and its continuation at the conclusion of the 
open meeting on February 7, 2019.

CHANGES IN THE MEETING: 
    This meeting will also discuss:
    Matters relating to internal personnel decisions, or internal rules 
and practices.
    Information the premature disclosure of which would be likely to 
have a considerable adverse effect on the implementation of a proposed 
Commission action.
