INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Investigation Nos. 701-TA-582 and 731-TA-1377 (Review)]


Ripe Olives From Spain; Withdrawal of Determinations

SUMMARY: On July 31, 2024, the Commission published a Federal Register 
notice of determinations for ripe olives from Spain. The notice is 
hereby withdrawn.

DATES: The document published at 89 FR 61497 on July 31, 2024 is 
withdrawn as of August 5, 2024.

    By order of the Commission.

    Issued: August 6, 2024.

Supervisory Hearings and Information Officer.
