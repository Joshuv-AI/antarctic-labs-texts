DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

34 CFR Part 361


State Vocational Rehabilitation Services Program; Delay of 
Effective Date

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Final regulations; delay of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the memorandum of January 20, 2001 (66 FR 
7702, January 24, 2001), from the Assistant to the President and Chief 
of

[[Page 8771]]

Staff, entitled ``Regulatory Review Plan,'' this regulation temporarily 
delays the effective date of the regulations entitled State Vocational 
Rehabilitation Services Program published in the Federal Register on 
January 17, 2001 (66 FR 4380).

EFFECTIVE DATE: The effective date of the regulations amending 34 CFR 
Part 361 published at 66 FR 4380, January 17, 2001 is delayed for 60 
days until April 17, 2001.
