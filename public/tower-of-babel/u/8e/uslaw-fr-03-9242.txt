    b. FERC Contact: Patricia Leppert at (202) 502-6034; 
[email protected] or John Costello at (202) 502-6119; 
[email protected].

draft Programmatic Agreement proposed by the Commission for the Keuka 
Project.

to the participants prior to the meeting)
(3) Summary of Meeting
(4) Follow-up Actions
