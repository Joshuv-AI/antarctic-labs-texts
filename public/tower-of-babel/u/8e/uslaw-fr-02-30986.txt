Public Land Order No. 7546; Withdrawal of Public Lands for 
Protection of Sweetwater River Recreational, Scenic, Riparian, 
Historic, and Wildlife Resources; Wyoming

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Public Land Order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This order withdraws 4,943.13 acres of public lands from 
surface entry and mining for a period of 20 years to protect and 
preserve significant recreational, scenic, riparian, historic, and 
wildlife resources along segments of the Sweetwater River. The lands 
are not available for mineral leasing in accordance with the Bureau of 
Land Management Green River Resource Management Plan.

EFFECTIVE DATE: December 9, 2002.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Janet Booth, BLM Wyoming State Office, 
5353 N. Yellowstone Road, P.O. Box 1828, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82003, 307-
775-6124.

    2. The withdrawal made by this order does not alter the 
applicability of those public land laws governing the use of the land 
under lease, license, or permit, or governing the disposal of the 
mineral or vegetative resources other than under the mining laws.
    3. This withdrawal will expire 20 years from the effective date of 
this order, unless, as a result of a review conducted before the 
expiration date pursuant to Section 204(f) of the Federal Land Policy
