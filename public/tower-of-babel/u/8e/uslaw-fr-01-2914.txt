FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-183, MM Docket No. 99-346; RM-9763]


Digital Television Broadcast Services; Evansville, IN

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Tri-State Public Teleplex, 
Inc., licensee of noncommercial educational station WNIN(TV), 
substitutes DTV channel *12 for DTV channel *54 at Evansville, Indiana. 
See 64 FR 70670, December 17, 1999. DTV channel *12 can be allotted to 
Evansville at coordinates (38-01-27 N. and 87-21-43 W.) with a power of 
15.0, HAAT of 177 meters and with a DTV service population of 599 
thousand. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective March 19, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-1600.
