FEDERAL MINE SAFETY AND HEALTH REVIEW COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Notice

February 6, 2015.

TIME AND DATE: 10:00 a.m., Thursday, February 19, 2015.

PLACE: The Richard V. Backley Hearing Room, Room 511N, 1331 
Pennsylvania Avenue NW., Washington, DC 20004 (enter from F Street 
entrance).

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: The Commission will hear oral argument in the 
matter Big Ridge, Inc. v. Secretary of Labor, Docket Nos. LAKE 2011-
699-R, et al. (Issues include whether the Administrative Law Judge 
erred in upholding certain orders issued pursuant to sections 103(j) 
and 103(k) of the Federal Mine Safety and Health Act of 1977.)
    Any person attending this oral argument who requires special 
accessibility features and/or auxiliary aids, such as sign language 
interpreters, must inform the Commission in advance of those needs. 
Subject to 29 CFR 2706.150(a)(3) and Sec.  2706.160(d).
