ADDRESSES: Meeting to take place by telephone and open to the public by 
telephone: 1-800-635-7637, Conference ID code #386-6160. Computer 
assisted real-time transcription (CART) will be provided. The web link 
to access CART (in English) on Friday October 30, 2020, is https://www.streamtext.net/player?event=USCCR. Please note that CART is text-

     Staff Director's Report
