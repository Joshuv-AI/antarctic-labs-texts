DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army, Corps of Engineers

33 CFR Part 334


Danzer Zones and Restricted Areas

AGENCY: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, DoD.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document contains corrections to the final regulation 
which was published on April 10, 1997, (62 FR 17550-17559). The 
original document contained several errors which are corrected and 
Sec. 334.1110 was inadvertently amended. This document removes that 
amendment.

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 12, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Ralph Eppard, Regulatory Branch, 
CECW-OR at (202) 761-1783.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

    1. The Corps published a final rule in the August 27, 1996 Federal 
Register (61 FR 43969) which amended Sec. 334.1110, and in the final 
rule (62 FR 17550-17559) published in the Federal Register on April 10, 
1997, we inadvertently made similar changes to Sec. 334.1110. This 
correction removes the amendment made on April 10, 1997.


Sec. 334.1110  [Corrected]

    On page 17558, in the first column, remove amendatory instruction 
#78 and the amendments to Sec. 334.1110.
    2. In addition, we are making the following corrections:


Sec. 334.310  [Corrected]

    On page 17553, in the first column, in paragraph (b)(2) of 
Sec. 334.310 in the second line, the reference to ``within 300 years of 
any naval vessel'' is corrected to read ``within 300 yards of any naval 
vessel''.


Sec. 334.670  [Corrected]

    On page 17555, in the first column, in paragraph (b)(2) of 
Sec. 334.670, in the seventh line, correct ``warming'' to read 
``warning''.


Sec. 334.730  [Corrected]

    On page 17555, in the center column, in paragraph (b)(2) of 
Sec. 334.730, in the fourth line, correct ``Intracostal'' to read 
``Intracoastal''.


Sec. 334.750  [Corrected]

    On page 17555, in the third column, in paragraph (b)(1) of 
Sec. 334.750, in the first line, capitalize the ``N'' in the word 
``No''.


Sec. 334.960  [Corrected]

    On page 17557, in the first column, in paragraph (b)(4) of 
Sec. 334.960, correct the sentence by inserting the word ``area'', 
between the words ``the'' and ``immediately''.


Sec. 334.1410  [Corrected]

    On page 17559, in the center column, in paragraph (b)(1) of 
Sec. 334.1410, correct the sentence by inserting the word ``of'' 
between ``display'' and ``signals''.


Sec. 334.1450  [Corrected]

    On page 17559, in the center column, in paragraph (b)(1) of 
Sec. 334.1450, in the eighth line, correct the word ``with'' to read 
``within''.

    Dated: April 23, 1997.
