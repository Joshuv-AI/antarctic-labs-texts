Radio Broadcasting Services; Andalusia, AL and Holt, FL

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a proposal filed on behalf of Capstar TX 
Limited Partnership, the Commission reallots Channel 251C1 from 
Andalusia, Alabama, to Holt, Florida, as that community's first local 
aural transmission service, and modifies the license for Station 
WTKE(FM) to specify Holt as its community of license. See 65 FR 7817, 
February 16, 2000. Coordinates used for Channel 251C1 at Holt, Florida, 
are 30-59-57 NL and 86-41-20 WL.

DATES: Effective October 16, 2000.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Nancy Joyner, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
