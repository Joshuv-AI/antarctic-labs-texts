DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Behavior 
and Social Science of Aging Review Committee, October 11, 2007, 4 p.m. 
to October 12, 2007, 5 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro 
Center, 7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published 
in the Federal Register on August 28, 2007, 72 FR 49287.
    The meeting will be held at the Embassy Suites Hotel at the Chevy 
Chase Pavilion, 4300 Military Road, NW., Washington, DC 20015. The 
meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: August 30, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
