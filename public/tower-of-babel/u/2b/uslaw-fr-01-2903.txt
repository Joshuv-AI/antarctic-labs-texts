DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 06-07 February 2001.
    Time of Meeting: 0830-1630, 06 February 2001, 0830-1630, 07 
February 2001.
    Place: Aerojet Sacramento Facility, Highway 50 & Aerojet Road, 
Rancho Cordova, CA 95760.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) study on Venture Capital 
will have their kickoff meeting to be briefed by Study Sponsors and 
to break into individual panels. There will be a briefing by RAND, 
discussion on the CIA Initiative, and Army Procurement, to name a 
few. For further information, please contact LTC John Anzalone, 
Operations Research Analyst, (703) 604-7436.


Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
