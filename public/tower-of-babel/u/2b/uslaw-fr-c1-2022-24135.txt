(a)(3) through (5) are set forth below.''


Sec.  537.1  Applications for approval [Corrected].

    (a) * * *
    (1) Each person with management responsibility for a management 
contract;
    (2) Each person who is a director of a corporation that is a party 
to a management contract;
    (3) All persons who have 10 percent or more or indirect financial 
interest in a management contract;
    (4) All entities with 10 percent or more financial interest in a 
management contract; and
    (5) Any other person or entity with a direct or indirect financial 
interest in a management contract otherwise designated by the 
Commission.
