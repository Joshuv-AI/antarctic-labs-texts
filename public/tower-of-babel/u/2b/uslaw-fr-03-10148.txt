DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-51,296]


Federal Mogul Ignition Group, Lighting Division, Hampton, VA; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 25, 2003, in response to a worker petition dated 
March 19, 2003 filed by a company official on behalf of workers at 
Federal Mogul Ignition Group, Lighting Division, Hampton, Virginia.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.
