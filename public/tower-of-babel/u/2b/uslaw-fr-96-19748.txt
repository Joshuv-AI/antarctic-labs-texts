Drawbridge Operation Regulations; Saginaw River, MI

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Notice; Direct final rule confirmation of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On May 14, 1996, the Coast Guard published, in the Federal 
Register, a direct final rule, 61 FR 24235, CGD09-96-003. This direct 
final rule notified the public of the Coast Guard's intent to place the 
CSX Railroad bridge, mile 18.0 over the Saginaw River in Saginaw, MI, 
in a fixed status. The bridge will be locked in the closed position. 
The Coast Guard has not received any adverse comments or any notice of 
an intent to submit adverse comments objecting to this rule as written. 
Therefore, this rule will go into effect as scheduled.

EFFECTIVE DATE: The effective date of the direct final rule is 
confirmed as August 12, 1996.
