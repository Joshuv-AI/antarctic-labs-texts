Perm-O-Penn Exploration, Midland, Texas; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Purusant to Section 250 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on September 15, 1998 on behalf of workers at Perm-O-Penn 
Exploration, Midland, Texas.
    The petitioner requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 19th day of October, 1998.
Grant D. Beale,
Acting Director, Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
