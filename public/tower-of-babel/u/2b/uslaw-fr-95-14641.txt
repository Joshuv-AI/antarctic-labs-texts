Wyoming; Bridger Coal Co.; Exploration License

June 9, 1995.
AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Correction--Bridger Coal Co. Exploration License.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects an error in the legal description for a 
Notice of Invitation for Coal Exploration License WYW136502 previously 
published in the Federal Register June 2, 1995 (60 FR 28792). The legal 
land description is changed to read T. 20 N., R. 100 W., 6th P.M., 
Wyoming, instead of T. 40 N., R. 100 W., 6th P.M. The remainder of the 
Notice of Invitation remains unchanged.
Pamela J. Lewis,
Supervisory Land Law Examiner.
