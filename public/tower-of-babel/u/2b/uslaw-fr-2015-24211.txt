Deletion of Consent Agenda Items From September 17, 2015; Open 
Meeting

Agenda items scheduled for consideration at the Thursday, September 17, 
2015, Open Meeting and previously listed in the Commission's Notice of 
September 10, 2015. Items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, and 11 have been 
adopted by the Commission.
