DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 9, 2006, 8 a.m. to June 
9, 2006, 6 p.m., Latham Hotel, 3000 M Street, NW., Washington, DC 20007 
which was published in the Federal Register on May 5, 2006, 71 FR 
26550-26552.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the reassignment of the 
applications.

    Dated: May 23, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
