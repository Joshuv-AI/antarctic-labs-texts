DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)


Public Health Service (PHS) Act; Delegation of Authority

    Notice is hereby given that I have delegated to the Associate 
Director for Science, CDC, without authority to redelegate, the 
authority vested in the Director, CDC, under section 301(d), of the PHS 
Act (42 U.S.C. 241 et seq.).
    This delegation became effective upon date of signature.

    Dated: July 29, 2003.
Julie Louise Gerberding,
Director.
