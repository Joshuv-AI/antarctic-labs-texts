DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Cancellation of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of the cancellation of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, October 29, 2001, 11 am to 
October 29, 2001, 12 pm, Latham Hotel, 3000 M Street, NW., Washington, 
DC 20007-3701 which was published in the Federal Register on October 
15, 2001, 66 FR 52441-52444.
    The meeting is cancelled due to the applications being transferred 
to another meeting.

    Dated: October 23, 2001.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
