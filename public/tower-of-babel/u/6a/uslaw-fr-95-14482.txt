CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION

TIME AND DATE: Thursday, June 15, 1995, 10:00 a.m.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the Public.

Matter to be Considered:

Protocol Revisions

    The Commission will consider issuance of a final rule revising 
the child-resistant packaging test protocols under the Poison 
Prevention Packaging Act.

call (301) 504-0709.
