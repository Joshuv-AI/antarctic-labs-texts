Alaska Resource Advisory Council Public Meeting

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of Alaska Resource Advisory Council Public Meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Alaska Resource Advisory Council will hold a public 
meeting Thursday, November 30, 1995 and Friday, December 1, 1995 in 
Fairbanks, Alaska. The public meeting will be from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 
p.m. on Thursday, November 30, and from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on 
Friday, December 1. The meeting will be held in the training room of 
the Bureau of Land Management Alaska Fire Service office, located on 
post at Fort Wainwright, Alaska. Public comments will be taken from 
10:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. on Thursday, November 30. Written comments may 
be submitted at the meeting. The council will elect officers and will 
discuss:
    1. State of Alaska land selection and priority;
    2. Squirrel River study and river management;
    3. Wild and Scenic River Act, with emphasis on the Gulkana and 
Fortymile Rivers;
    4. Dalton Highway Recreation Activity Plan;
    5. Old and new business.

ADDRESSES: Inquiries about this meeting should be sent to the External 
Affairs Office, Bureau of Land Management, 222 W. 7th Ave., No. 13, 
Anchorage, Alaska 99513-7599.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Vickery, at (907) 271-5555.

    Dated: October 5, 1995.
Sally Wisely,
Associate State Director.
