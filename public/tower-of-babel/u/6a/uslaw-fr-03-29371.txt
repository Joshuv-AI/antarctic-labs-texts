Fishing Vessel (F/V) K2, Cordova, AK; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 4, 2003, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Fishing 
Vessel (F/V) K2, Cordova, Alaska.
    The investigation revealed that the subject firm did not separate 
or threaten to separate a significant number or proportion of workers 
as required by section 222 of the Trade Act of 1974. Significant number 
or proportion of the workers means that at least three workers in a 
firm with a workforce of

[[Page 66127]]

fewer than 50 workers would have to be affected. Separations by the 
subject firm did not meet this threshold level; consequently the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 25th day of September, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
