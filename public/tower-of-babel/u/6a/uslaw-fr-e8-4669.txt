Lear Corporation, Fenton, MI; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 31, 2008 in response to a worker 
petition filed by the United Auto Workers on behalf of workers at the 
Lear Corporation, Fenton, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of March, 2008.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
