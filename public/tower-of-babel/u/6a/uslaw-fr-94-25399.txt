                     Sunshine Act Meetings






 

U.S. CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION

TIME AND DATE: Thursday, October 13, 1994, 10:00 a.m.

LOCATION: Room 420, East West Towers, 4330 East West Highway, Bethesda, 
Maryland.

STATUS: Open to the Public.

MATTER TO BE CONSIDERED:

Naproxen

    The staff will brief the Commission on a staff recommendation 
that the Commission propose a rule to require child-resistant 
packaging under the Poison Prevention Packaging Act for over-the-
counter drugs containing 250 mg or more of naproxen per package.

    For a recorded message containing the latest agenda information, 
call (301) 504-0709.

CONTACT PERSON FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Sadye E. Dunn, Office of the 
Secretary, 4330 East West Highway., Bethesda, MD 20207 (301) 504-0800.

    Dated: October 6, 1994.
Sadye E. Dunn,

Secretary.
