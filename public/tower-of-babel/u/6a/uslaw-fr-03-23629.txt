NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 03-111]


Notice of Prospective Patent License

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

ACTION: Notice of prospective patent license.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NASA hereby gives notice that NanoConduction Inc., of Los 
Gatos, CA has applied for an exclusive license to practice the 
invention contained in the pending patent application corresponding to 
NASA Case No. ARC-15042-1, entitled ``Carbon Nanotube Interconnect 
Technology for Microprocessors and DRAM Capacitors,'' which is assigned 
to the United States of America as represented by the Administrator of 
the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. Written objections 
to the prospective grant of a license should be sent to Ames Research 
Center.

DATES: Responses to this notice must be received by October 17, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Padilla, Chief Patent Counsel, 
NASA Ames Research Center, M/S 202A-4, Moffett Field, CA 94035-1000, 
650-604-5104.

    Dated: September 9, 2003.
Robert M. Stephens,
Deputy General Counsel.
