DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Environmental Management Advisory Board; Notice of Open Meeting 
Correction

    On March 26, 1999, the Department of Energy published a notice of 
open meeting announcing a meeting of the Environmental Management 
Advisory Board (64 FR 14714). In that notice, the meeting was scheduled 
for April 22-23, 1999. Today's notice is announcing that the meeting 
will only take place on April 22, 1999, at 8:30am to 3:00pm.

    Issued in Washington, D.C. on April 8, 1999.
Rachel M. Samuel,
Deputy Advisory Committee Management Officer.
