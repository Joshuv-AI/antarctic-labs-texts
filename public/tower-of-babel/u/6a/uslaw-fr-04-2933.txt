SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

I. Introduction

    Pursuant to 10 CFR 2.106, the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission 
(NRC) is providing notice of the issuance of Amendment 47 to Special 
Nuclear Material License SNM-124 to Nuclear Fuel Services, Inc. (NFS) 
authorizing the possession and use of special nuclear material in the 
Blended Low-Enriched Uranium (BLEU) Preparation Facility (BPF) at the 
licensee's site in Erwin, Tennessee. The NFS' request for the proposed 
action was previously noticed in the Federal Register on January 7, 
2003 (68 FR 796) along with a notice of opportunity to request a 
hearing.
    This amendment complies with the standards and requirements of the 
Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended, and NRC's rules and regulations 
as set forth in 10 CFR chapter 1. Accordingly, this amendment was 
issued on January 13, 2004, and was effective immediately.
