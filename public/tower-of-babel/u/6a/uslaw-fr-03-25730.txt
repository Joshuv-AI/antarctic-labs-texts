Fishing Vessel (F/V) Rimrock, Kodiak, AK; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on September 2, 2003 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of the 
Fishing Vessel (F/V) Rimrock, Kodiak, Alaska.
    All workers were separated from the subject firm more than one year 
before the date of the petition. Section 223(b) of the Act specifies 
that no certification may apply to any worker whose last separation 
occurred more than one year before the date of the petition. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 10th day of September, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
