DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Price for the 2012 American Eagle Silver Proof Coin

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is lowering the price of the 2012 
American Eagle Silver Proof Coin. The product will now be offered for 
sale at a price of $54.95.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: B. B. Craig, Associate Director for 
Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW; Washington, 
DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.
