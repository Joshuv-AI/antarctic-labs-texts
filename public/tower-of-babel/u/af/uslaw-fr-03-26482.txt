Zawick Manufacturing Co., Hellertown, PA; Notice of Termination 
of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 27, 2003 in response to a worker petition which 
was filed by UNITE! on behalf of workers at Zawick Manufacturing 
Company, Hellertown, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 12th day of September, 2003.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
