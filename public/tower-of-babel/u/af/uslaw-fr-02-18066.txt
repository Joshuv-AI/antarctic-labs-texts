Delphi Harrison Thermal Systems, Lockport, NY; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on August 27, 2001, in response to a worker petition that 
was filed on behalf of workers at Delphi Harrison Thermal Systems, 
Lockport, New York.
    A NAFTA-TAA petition filed on behalf of the workers at the subject 
firm was terminated (NAFTA-6089, signed July 5, 2002).
    This case is being terminated because the separated workers have 
been rehired since the filing of the petition. Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 5th day of July 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
