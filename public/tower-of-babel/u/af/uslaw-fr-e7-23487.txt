DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


 Notification of United States Mint Coin Product Price Adjustment

ACTION: Notification of United States Mint Coin Product Price 
Adjustment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The United States Mint is announcing the price of the 2008 
American Eagle Silver Proof Coin.
    Because of increases in the cost of silver, the United States Mint 
will begin accepting orders for the 2008 American Eagle Silver Proof 
Coin at the price indicated below, effective December 3, 2007:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                               New 2008
                    Product                      2007 price   coin price
------------------------------------------------------------------------
American Eagle Silver Proof Coin..............      $ 29.95      $ 31.95
------------------------------------------------------------------------


FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Gloria C. Eskridge, Associate Director 
for Sales and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 Ninth Street, NW.; 
Washington, DC 20220; or call 202-354-7500.

    Dated: November 28, 2007.
