FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 06-954; MB Docket No. 05-316; RM-11294]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Arnold and City of Angels, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a Notice of Proposed Rule Making, this Report 
and Order allots Channel 240A, FM Station KBYN, Arnold, California from 
Arnold to City of Angels, California, and modifies Station KBYN's 
license accordingly. The coordinates for Channel 240A at City of 
Angels, California are 38-05-32 NL and 120-27-22 WL, with a site 
restriction of 8.6 kilometers (5.3 miles) east of City of Angels.

DATES: Effective June 19, 2006.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission; 445 12th Street, SW., 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: R. Barthen Gorman, Media Bureau, (202) 
418-2180.
