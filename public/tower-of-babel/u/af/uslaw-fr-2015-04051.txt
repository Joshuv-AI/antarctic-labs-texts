AGENCY: Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department of Defense

ACTION: Notice correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the date in a notice published in the 
Federal Register on February 23, 2015, (80 FR 9445) concerning request 
for comments on the proposed extension of OMB control number 0704-0479, 
DFARS 234.2, Earned Value Management System. The document contained an 
incorrect date for submission of public comments. The new date is April 
24, 2015.

Correction

DATES: DoD will consider all comments received by April 24, 2015.
