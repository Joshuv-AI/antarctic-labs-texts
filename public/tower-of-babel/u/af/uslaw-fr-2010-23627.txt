DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Notice of Meeting

September 15, 2010.
    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) hereby gives 
notice that members of its staff may attend the meeting noted below. 
The meeting will be hosted by Entergy Arkansas, Inc. (EAI) and will 
examine, among other things, steps by EAI in exploring future successor 
arrangements. Their attendance is part of the Commission's ongoing 
outreach efforts.
    September 23, 2010 (9:30 a.m.-4 p.m.), Embassy Suites Hotel, 11301 
Financial Center Parkway, Little Rock, AR 72211, 501-312-9000.
    The discussions may address matters at issue in the following
