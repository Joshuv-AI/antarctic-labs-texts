-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects final regulations (TD 9144) that were 
published in the Federal Register on Monday, August 3, 2004 (69 FR 
46401) and corrected on Monday, October 18, 2004 (69 FR 61309). The 
final regulations relate to statutory options.

DATES: This document is effective on August 3, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Erinn Madden, (202) 622-6030 (not a 
toll-free number).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    The final regulations (TD 9144) that are the subject of these 
corrections are under sections 421, 422, and 424 of the Internal 
Revenue Code.

Need for Correction

    As published, the final regulations (TD 9144) contain errors that 
may prove to be misleading and are in need of clarification.
