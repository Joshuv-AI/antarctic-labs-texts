DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Office of the Secretary


Applications of Capital Cargo International Airlines, Inc. for 
Issuance of New Certificate Authority

AGENCY: Department of Transportation.

ACTION: Notice of Order to Show Cause (Order 95-12-29) Dockets OST-95-
589 and OST-95-590.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Transportation is directing all interested 
persons to show cause why it should not issue orders (1) finding 
Capital Cargo International Airlines, Inc., fit, willing, and able, and 
(2) awarding it certificates to engage in foreign charter air 
transportation of property and mail and interstate all-cargo air 
transportation.

DATES: Persons wishing to file objections should do so no later than 
January 2, 1996.

ADDRESSES: Objections and answers to objections should be filed in 
Dockets OST-95-589 and OST-95-590 and addressed to the Documentary 
Services Division (C-55, Room PL-401), U.S. Department of 
Transportation, 400 Seventh Street, SW, Washington, D.C. 20590 and 
should be served upon the parties listed in Attachment A to the order.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Janet A. Davis, Air Carrier 
Fitness Division (X-56, Room 6401), U.S. Department of Transportation, 
400 Seventh Street, SW, Washington, D.C. 20590, (202) 366-9721.

    Dated: December 20, 1995
Mark L. Gerchick,
Acting Assistant Secretary for Aviation and International Affairs.
