Olympic National Forest, Jefferson County, Washington;

SUMMARY: On August 12, 2005, the USDA, Forest Service, Olympic National 
Forest, published a Notice of Intent in Federal Register (70 FR 47171) 
to prepare an environmental impact statement (EIS) for Dosewallips Road 
Washout Project. The Notice of Intent is being revised to correct the 
project's location from Oregon State to Washington State, and to 
correct the Olympic National Park e-mail contact address. The correct 
address is [email protected].
