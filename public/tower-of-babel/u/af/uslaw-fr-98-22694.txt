DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Office of the Secretary


Executive Committee Meeting of the Defense Advisory Committee on 
Women in the Services (DACOWITS)

AGENCY: Department of Defense, Advisory Committee on Women in the 
Services.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to Section 10(a), Pub. L. 92-463, as amended, notice 
is hereby given of a forthcoming Quarterly Executive Committee Meeting 
of the Defense Advisory Committee on Women in the Services (DACOWITS). 
The purpose of the Executive Committee Meeting is to review the 
responses to the recommendations and request for information adopted by 
the committee at the DACOWITS 1998 Spring Conference.

DATES: September 14, 1998, 8:30 a.m.-4 p.m.

ADDRESSES: SECDEF Conference Room 3E869, The Pentagon, Washington, DC.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Lieutenant Colonel Sandy Lewis, ARNGUS, DACOWITS and Military Women 
Matters, OASD (Force Management Policy), 4000 Defense Pentagon, Room 
3D769, Washington, DC 20301-4000; telephone (703) 697-2122.
