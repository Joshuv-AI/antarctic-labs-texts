FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2528]


Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

February 1, 2002.
    Petition for Reconsideration has been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceeding listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room CY-A257, 445 12th Street, 
SW., Washington, DC or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, Qualex International (202) 863-2893. Oppositions to this 
petition must be filed by February 21, 2002. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of 
the Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(1)). Replies to an opposition must 
be filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has 
expired.
    Subject: Telecommunications Industry's Environmental Civil 
Violattions in U.S. Territorial Waters (South Florida and the Virgin 
Islands and along the Coastal Wetlands of Maine--FCC Accountability and 
Responsibility for Rulemaking regarding the NEPA, NHPA (RM-9913).
    Number of Petitions Filed: 1.

William F. Caton,
Acting Secretary.
