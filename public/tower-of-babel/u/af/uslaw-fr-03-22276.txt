Chilkoot Fish Company, Haines, AK; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on May 14, 2003, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Chilkoot 
Fish Company, Haines, Alaska.
    The petitioner no longer wants to pursue the petition 
investigation. Consequently, further investigation would serve no 
purpose and the investigation is terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 13th day of August, 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
