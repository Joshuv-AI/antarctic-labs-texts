FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[MM Docket No. 96-39; RM-8757]


Television Broadcasting Services; Irma, WI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action dismisses a petition for rule making filed by 
David A. White requesting the allotment of UHF Television Channel 30+ 
to Irma, Wisconsin. See 61 FR 10978, March 18, 1996. No comments were 
received at the Commission stating an intention to file an application 
for Channel 30+ at Irma, Wisconsin. It is Commission policy to refrain 
from allotting a channel absent an expression of interest.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
