Tuesday, December 21, 1999, make the following corrections:

fifth line, ``), 42 CFR part 121,'' should be added after ``1999''.

the fifth sentence ``The Institute's report, issued in July 1999, the 
public comments received on the April 2, 1998, rule, and extensive 
consultation with representatives of the OPTN.'' should be removed.
    3. On the same page, in the same column, in second line from the 
bottom ``amendment sot'' should read ``amendments to''.
    4. On the same page, in the third column, in the eighth line, 
``413(b)W'' should read ``413(b)1''.
