COMMITTEE FOR PURCHASE FROM PEOPLE WHO ARE BLIND OR SEVERELY DISABLED


Procurement List Proposed Addition; Corrections

    In the document appearing on page 56634, FR Doc. 01-28211, in the 
issue of November 9, 2001, in the third column the Committee published 
a notice of deletions to the Procurement List of, among other things, 
Enamel, Lacquer, National Stock Number (NSN) 8010-00-942-8712. This 
notice is amended to correct the NSN to 8010-00-941-8712.
