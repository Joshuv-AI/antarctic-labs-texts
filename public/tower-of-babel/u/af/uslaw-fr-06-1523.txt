Radio Broadcasting Services; Coalinga, CA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule, dismissal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document dismisses a pending petition for rulemaking 
filed by 105 Mountain Air, Inc. to allot Channel 265A at Coalinga, 
California for failure to state a continuing interest in the requested 
allotment. The document therefore terminates the proceeding. See 69 FR 
46474, published August 3, 2004.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Helen McLean, Media Bureau (202) 418-
2738.
