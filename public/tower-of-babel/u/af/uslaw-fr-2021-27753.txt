SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD

[Docket No. EP 290 (Sub-No. 5) (2022-1)]


Quarterly Rail Cost Adjustment Factor

AGENCY: Surface Transportation Board.

ACTION: Approval of rail cost adjustment factor.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Board has approved the first quarter 2022 Rail Cost 
Adjustment Factor (RCAF) and cost index filed by the Association of 
American Railroads. The first quarter 2022 RCAF (Unadjusted) is 1.154. 
The first quarter 2022 RCAF (Adjusted) is 0.478. The first quarter 2021 
RCAF-5 is 0.451.

DATES: Applicability Date: January 1, 2022.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pedro Ramirez at (202) 245-0333. 
Assistance for the hearing impaired is available through the Federal 
Relay Service at (800) 877-8339.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Additional information is contained in the 
Board's decision, which is available at www.stb.gov.
