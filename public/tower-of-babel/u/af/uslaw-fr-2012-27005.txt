High Point Gas Transmission, LLC; Notice Establishing Deadline 
for Comments

    On October 26, 2012, High Point Gas Transmission, LLC (High Point) 
filed a response to the Commission's October 16, 2012 Data Request in 
the captioned proceedings.
    Notice is hereby given that participants in the captioned 
proceedings may file comments to High Point's Data Response on or 
before 5:00 p.m. Eastern time on Tuesday, November 6, 2012.

    Dated: October 31, 2012.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
