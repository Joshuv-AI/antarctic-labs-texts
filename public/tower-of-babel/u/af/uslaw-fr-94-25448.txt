DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Community College of the Air Force Meeting

    The Community College of the Air Force (CCAF) Board of Visitors 
will hold a meeting on Thursday, 18 November 1994 at 8:30 a.m. in the 
Sheppard Air Force Base Officer's Club, Sheppard Air Force Base, Texas. 
The meeting will be open to the public.
    The purpose of the meeting is to review and discuss academic 
policies and issues relative to the operation of the CCAF. Agenda items 
include a CCAF mission briefing, faculty credentials, and reaffirmation 
of the CCAF.
    For further information contact First Lieutenant Kyle Monson, (205) 
953-2703, Community College of the Air Force, Maxwell Air Force Base, 
Alabama 36112-6653.
Patsy J. Conner,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
