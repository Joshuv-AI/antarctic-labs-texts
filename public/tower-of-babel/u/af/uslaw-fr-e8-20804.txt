Middlebury Electric, LLC; Notice of Application for Preliminary 
Permit Accepted for Filing and Soliciting Comments, Motions to 
Intervene, and Protests

September 2, 2008.
    On June 5, 2008, Middlebury Electric, LLC filed an application, 
pursuant to section 4(f) of the Federal Power Act (FPA), to study the 
feasibility of Middlebury Upper Hydroelectric Project. The proposed 
project would be located on the Otter Creek in Addison County, Vermont. 
The properties that would be associated with the proposed Middlebury 
Upper Hydroelectric Project are owned by the applicant.
    The proposed project using the existing properties, including the 
sluiceway, intake infrastructure, and flume, would consist of: (1) A 
proposed powerhouse, containing a single 400 kW generating unit; (2) a 
proposed penstock approximately 120 feet long, 7.5 feet wide; (3) a 
proposed new transmission line around 500 feet long; and (4) 
appurtenant facilities. The proposed

[[Page 52341]]

project would have an average annual generation of approximately 2.8 
gigawatt-hours, to be utilized by Middlebury College.
