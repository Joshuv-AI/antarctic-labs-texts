DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Notice of Proposed Information Collection Requests

AGENCY: Department of Education.

ACTION: Correction Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On December 21, 2006, the Department of Education published a 
notice in the Federal Register (Page 76641, Column 2) for the 
information collection, ``Social and Character Development Research 
Program National Evaluation.'' This notice hereby corrects the burden 
hours to 8,081 and the annual responses to 15,859.
    The IC Clearance Official, Regulatory Information Management 
Services, Office of Management, hereby issues a correction notice as 
required by the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995.

    Dated: December 27, 2006.
James Hyler,
Acting Leader, Information Policy and Standard Team, Regulatory 
Information Management Services, Office of Management.
