Radio Broadcasting Services; Douglas, Tifton and Unionville, GA

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document denies the petition for reconsideration filed by 
Tifton Broadcasting Corporation and affirms our action in the Report 
and Order 60 FR 37597 (July 21, 1995) which substituted Channel 223C3 
for Channel 223A at Douglas, Georgia, reallotted Channel 223C3 from 
Douglas to Tifton, Georgia, and modified the construction permit for 
Station WKZZ(FM) accordingly. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.
EFFECTIVE DATE: February 7, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Authur D. Scrutchins, Mass Media 
Bureau, (202) 418-2180.
