DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Geriatrics and Gerontology Advisory Committee, Notice of Charter 
Renewal

    This gives notice under the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 
92-463) of October 6, 1972, that the Department of Veterans' Affairs 
Geriatrics and gerontology Advisory Committee has been renewed for a 2-
year period beginning September 5, 2000, through September 5, 2002.

    Dated: September 5, 2000.

    By direction of the Acting Secretary.
Marvin R. Eason,
Committee Management Officer.
