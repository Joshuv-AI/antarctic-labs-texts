DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Southern California Edison Company; Notice of Change of Informal 
Settlement Conference

August 28, 2002.
    The Notice of Informal Settlement Conference issued on August 23, 
2002 in the above-captioned proceeding stated that the conference would 
be held on September 4th and 5th 2002 . The dates have been changed to 
September 5th and 6th, 2002. The time is still 10:00 a.m.
    Any party, as defined by 18 CFR 385.102(c), or any participant as 
defined by 18 CFR 385.102(b), is invited to attend. Persons wishing to 
become a party must move to intervene and receive intervenor status 
pursuant to the Commission's regulations (18 CFR 385.214).
    For additional information, please contact Thomas J. Burgess at 
(202)5026058 or Dawn K. Martin at (202)5028661.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Deputy Secretary.
