Cranberry Pipeline Corporation; Notice of Informal Settlement

captioned proceeding will be held on Tuesday, November 10, 1998, at 
10:00 a.m. in Room No. 91-62 at the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission, 888 First Street, NE., Washington, DC.

additional information, please contact Penny Murrell at (202) 208-0531.
