DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
 

Defense Science Board Task Force on Depot Maintenance Management

ACTION: Change in Date of Advisory Committee Meeting Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The meeting of the Defense Science Board Task Force on Depot 
Maintenance Management scheduled for January 20-21, 1993 as published 
in the Federal Register (Vol. 58, No. 247, Page 68635, Tuesday, 
December 28, 1993, FR Doc. 93-31555) will be held on February 3-4, 
1994. In all other respects the original notice remains unchanged.

    Dated: February 7, 1994.
Patricia L. Toppings,
Alternate OSD Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Defense.
