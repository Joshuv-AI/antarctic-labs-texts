Notice of Filing of Plat of Survey; Illinois

    The plat, in five sheets, of the dependent resurvey of the west 
boundary and subdivisional lines, and the survey of the Lock and Dam 
No. 26 Acquisition Boundary, Township 12 South, Range 1 West, and the 
dependent resurvey of portions of the north boundary, portions of the 
subdivisional lines, and the survey of the Lock and Dam Nos. 25 and 26 
Acquisition Boundaries, Township 12 South, Range 2 West, and the 
dependent resurvey of the north and east boundaries and subdivisional 
lines, Township 12 South, Range 3 West, all of the Fourth Principal 
Meridian, Illinois, will be officially filed in Eastern States, 
Springfield, Virginia at 7:30 a.m., on January 20, 1998.
    The survey was requested by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers.
    All inquiries or protests concerning the technical aspects of the 
survey must be sent to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor, Eastern States, 
Bureau of Land Management, 7450 Boston Boulevard, Springfield, Virginia 
22153, prior to 7:30 a.m., January 20, 1998.
    Copies of the plat will be made available upon request and 
prepayment of the reproduction fee of $2.75 per copy.

    Dated: December 8, 1997.
Stephen G. Kopach,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor.
