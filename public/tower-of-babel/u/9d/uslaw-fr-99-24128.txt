National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, September 13, 1999, 1:00 p.m. 
to September 13, 1999, 2:00 p.m., 6130 Executive Boulevard, EPN/F, 
Rockville, MD 20852 which was published in the Federal Register on 
August 24, 1999, 64FR46208.
