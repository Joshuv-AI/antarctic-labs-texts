Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

Against Microbial Diseases Study Section, October 24, 2013, 08:30 a.m. 
to October 25, 2013, 05:00 p.m., Renaissance Washington, DC, Dupont 
Circle, 1143 New Hampshire Avenue NW., Washington, DC 20037, which was 
published in the Federal Register on October 01, 2013, 78 FR 190 Pg. 
60297.

Hotel, 999 Ninth Street NW., Washington, DC 20001. The meeting will 
start on December 12, 2013 at 8:30 a.m. and end on December 13, 2013 at
