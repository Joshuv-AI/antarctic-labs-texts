DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Food and Drug Administration

21 CFR Part 201


Labeling

CFR Correction

    In Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 200 to 299, 
revised as of April 1, 2003, in the first sentence of the introductory 
text of Sec.  201.122, on page 54, the phrase `` `Rx only' '' is 
removed and the phrase `` `Caution: For manufacturing, processing, or 
repacking' '' is added in its place, and the phrase `` `Caution: 
Federal law prohibits dispensing without prescription' '' is removed 
and the phrase `` `Rx only' '' is added in its place.
