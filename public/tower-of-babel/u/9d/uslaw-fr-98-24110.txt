ACTION: Correction to the July 30, 1998, Notice of Disclaimer of 
Interest as published in the Federal Register (63 FR 40729).

SUMMARY: The purpose of this notice is to correct the list of lands 
described in the July 30, 1998, Notice of Disclaimer of Interest as 
published in the Federal Register (63 FR 40729) for certain islands 
under the Michigan Public Lands Improvement Act of October 28, 1998.
    The following parcels were inadvertently included in the Federal 
Register Notice of July 30, 1998, and are hereby deleted.
