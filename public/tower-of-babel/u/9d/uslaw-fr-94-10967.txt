Sunshine Act Notice

 

FEDERAL RETIREMENT THRIFT INVESTMENT BOARD

TIME AND DATE: 9:00 a.m.--May 16, 1994.

PLACE: 4th Floor, Conference Room, 1250 H Street, N.W., Washington, 
D.C.

STATUS: Open.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:

    1. National Finance Center recordkeeping and agency liaison.
    2. Benefits administration.
    3. Investments.
    4. Participant communications.
    5. Approval of the minutes of the last meeting.
    6. Thrift Savings Plan activities report by the Executive 
Director.
    7. Annual financial audit presentation.
    8. Approval of the update of the FY 1994--FY 1995 budgets.
    9. Investment policy review.
    10. Review of additional Thrift Savings Plan funds.
    11. Status of audit recommendations.

CONTACT PERSONS FOR MORE INFORMATION: Tom Trabucco, Director, Office of 
External Affairs, (202) 942-1640.

    Dated: May 2, 1994.
Roger W. Mehle,
Executive Director, Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board.
