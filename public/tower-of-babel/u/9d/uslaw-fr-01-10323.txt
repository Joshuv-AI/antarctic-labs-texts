Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America; Notice of Technical

issues raised by filing.

May 22, 2001, at 10 a.m., in a room to be designated, at the offices of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426.
