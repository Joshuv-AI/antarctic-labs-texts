Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2016-9427.
    Petitioner: AgrowSoft, LLC.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 107.36; 137.19(c), (d), (e)(2)(ii), 
(iii), and (v); 137.31(b); 137.42.
    Description of Relief Sought: AgrowSoft, LLC dba AgrowDrone 
(``AgrowDrone'') an operator of Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) 
seeks an exemption to operate its commercial remotely-piloted 
helicopter, the UAS-H, to provide agricultural related services in the 
United States. The UAS-H is capable of providing a wide array of 
essential agricultural spraying services, including: Watering, 
fertilizers, pesticides, and herbicides. For agricultural purposes, the 
UAS-H is only flown over uninhabited areas (e.g., fields, groves, and 
orchards) and away from airports (i.e., three nautical miles or more) 
or populated areas. All flights will occur over private or controlled 
access property with the property owner's prior consent and knowledge.
