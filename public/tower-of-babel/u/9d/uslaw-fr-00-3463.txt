Algonquin Gas Transmission Company; Notice of Site Visit

February 9, 2000.
    On February 16, 2000 the Office of Pipeline Regulation (OPR) staff 
will inspect Algonquin Gas Transmission Company's (Algonquin) proposed 
route and potential alternative routes for the Fore River Project in 
Norfolk County, Massachusetts. The areas will be inspected by 
automobile. Representatives of Algonquin will accompany the OPR staff. 
Anyone interested in participating in the site visits must provide 
their own transportation.
    For additional information, contact Mr. Paul McKee of the 
Commission's Office of External Affairs at (202) 208-1088.

Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
