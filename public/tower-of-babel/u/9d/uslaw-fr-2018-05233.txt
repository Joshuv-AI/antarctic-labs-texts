Associated Electric Cooperative, Inc.; Notice of Petition for 
Partial Waiver

    Take notice that on March 9, 2018, pursuant to section 292.402 of 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's (Commission) Rules and 
Regulations,\1\ 18 CFR 292.402 (2018), Associated Electric Cooperative, 
Inc. (Associated Electric), on behalf of 56 rural electric cooperative 
member-owners (collectively, the Participating Members),\2\ request a 
partial waiver of certain obligations imposed on the Participating 
Members under sections 292.303(a) and 292.303(b) of the Commission's 
Regulations \3\ implementing section 210 of the Public Utility 
Regulatory Policies Act of 1978, all as more fully explained in the 
petition.
