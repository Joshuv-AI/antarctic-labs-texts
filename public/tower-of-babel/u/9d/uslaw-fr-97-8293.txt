DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institute of Mental Health; Closed Meetings

    Pursuant to Section 10(d) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (5 U.S.C. Appendix 2), notice is hereby given of the following 
meetings of the National Institute of Mental Health Special Emphasis 
Panel:

    Committee Name: National Institute of Mental Health Special 
Emphasis Panel.

    Time: 3 p.m.
    Place: Parklawn, Room 9C-26, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 
20857.
    Contact Person: Phyllis D. Artis, Parklawn, Room 9C-26, 5600 
Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857, Telephone: 301, 443-6470.

Applications and/or proposals and the discussions could reveal
