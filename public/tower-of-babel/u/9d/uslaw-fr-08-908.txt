DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, April 7, 2008, 8 a.m. to April 
7, 5 p.m., Gaithersburg Hilton, 620 Perry Parkway, Gaithersburg, MD, 
20877 which was published in the Federal Register on February 15, 2008, 
73FR8886-8887.
    This notice is amended to change the name from ``Biosensors for 
Early Cancer Detection & Risk Assessment/Novel & Improved Methods to 
Measure Cancer Epigenetic Biomarkers'' to ``Biosensors/Cancer 
Epigenetic Biomarkers''. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: February 25, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
