DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Special Emphasis Panel, February 20, 
2008, 8 a.m. to February 21, 2008, 1 p.m., Courtyard Marriott, 2899 
Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, VA, 22202 which was published in 
the Federal Register on January 28, 2008, FR08-298.
    The meeting dates were changed from February 20-21, 2008 to 
February 21-22, 2008. The rest of the information remains the same. The 
meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: January 30, 2008.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
