AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This Notice corrects a Notice of Application for a Recordable 
Disclaimer of Interest from Randy Lynne Jackson, personal 
representative of the estate of Donald F. Jackson, deceased. The BLM 
published the original notice on Monday, December 22, 2008 [73 FR 
78388]. The original notice included a metes and bounds legal 
description which contained several errors.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Laura Summers, (208) 373-3866.
