Approval and Promulgation of Implementation Plans; Indiana

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The EPA is proposing to approve revisions to Indiana's opacity 
rules. The Indiana Department of Environmental Management (IDEM) 
submitted revised opacity rules on October 21, 1999, as a requested 
revision to its State Implementation Plan (SIP). The revisions address 
the provisions of Indiana's opacity rule concerning the startup and 
shutdown of operations, the terminology used in discussing averaging 
periods, time periods for temporary exemptions, alternative opacity 
limits, and conflicts between visible opacity readings and continuous 
opacity monitor data.
    A major new component of this rule is a provision that allows the 
State to incorporate source-specific startup and shutdown provisions 
into federal operating permits for certain utility boilers, as long as 
those provisions fall within a range established in the rule. All of 
the revisions satisfy EPA guidelines.
