DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute on Aging; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute on Aging Special Emphasis Panel, June 16, 2004, 6 p.m. to 
June 17, 2004, 5 p.m., Holiday Inn Chevy Chase, 5520 Wisconsin Avenue, 
Chevy Chase, MD 20815 which was published in the Federal Register on 
May 17, 2004, 69 FR 27934.
    The meeting will now be held July 6-7, 2004 at the same time and 
location. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: May 21, 2004.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
