[FEMA-1138-DR]


Pennsylvania; Amendment to Notice of a Major Disaster Declaration

AGENCY: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice amends the notice of a major disaster for the 
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (FEMA-1138-DR dated September 13, 1996, 
and related determinations.

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 15, 1996

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pauline C. Campbell, Response and 
Recovery Directorate, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Washington, 
DC 20472, (202) 646-3606.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of a major disaster for the 
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, is hereby amended to include Public 
Assistance in the following areas determined to have been adversely 
affected by the catastrophe declared a major disaster by the President 
in his declaration of September 13, 1996:
    The counties of Huntingdon, Juniata, Mifflin, Perry, Cumberland, 
and Montgomery for Public Assistance (already designated for Individual 
Assistance and Hazard Mitigation Assistance).
