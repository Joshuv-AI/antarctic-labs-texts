DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Mental Health; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Mental Health Special Emphasis Panel, December 4, 2000, 
8:30 am to December 4, 2000, 5:00 pm, Bethesda Holiday Inn, 8120 
Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the 
Federal Register on October 25, 2000, 65 FR 63878.
    The meeting time has been changed to 10:30 am to 6:00 pm on the 
same day at the same hotel. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: November 6, 2000.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
