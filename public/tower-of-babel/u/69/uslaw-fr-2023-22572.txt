Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-38-2023]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 163; Authorization of Production 
Activity; Puerto Rico Steel Products Corporation; (Construction and 
Fencing Products); Coto Laurel, Puerto Rico

    On June 8, 2023, Puerto Rico Steel Products Corporation submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its 
facility within Subzone 163L, in Coto Laurel, Puerto Rico.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (88 FR 38811, June 14, 2023). On 
October 6, 2023, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision 
that no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
section 400.14.

    Dated: October 6, 2023.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
