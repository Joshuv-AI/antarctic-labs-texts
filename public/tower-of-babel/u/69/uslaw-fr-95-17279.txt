OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Notice of Request for Reclearance of RI 20-001

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980 (title 
44, U.S. Code, chapter 35), this notice announces a request for a 
reclearance of an information collection. RI 20-1, Application for 
Minimum Annuity, is completed by annuitants to determine if they 
quality for minimum annuity under certain provisions of 5 U.S.C. 
8345(f).
    Approximately 50 RI 20-1s are completed annually. We estimate that 
it takes 15 minutes to fill out the form. The annual burden is 13 
hours.
    For copies of this proposal, contact Doris R. Benz on (703) 908-
8564.

DATES: Comments on this proposal should be received on or before August 
13, 1995.
