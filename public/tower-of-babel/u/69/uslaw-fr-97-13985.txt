Upper Peninsula Power Company; Notice of Meeting To Discuss 
Settlement Issues

May 22, 1997.
    On June 5, 1997, at 9:00 a.m. Central Daylight Time, Commission 
staff from the Office of Hydropower Licensing will attend a settlement 
meeting for the proposed relicensing of the Bond Falls Hydroelectric 
Project. The meeting will be held in the Sylvania Visitor Center in 
Watersmeet, MI.
    This hydropower project is located on major branches of the 
Ontonagon River, a tributary of western Lake Superior, in Ontonagon and 
Gogebic counties of the upper peninsula of Michigan, and Vilas County 
in northern Wisconsin.
    The purpose of this meeting is to discuss the water budget modeling 
of various options before the settlement team. This meeting will 
provide an opportunity for the settlement team and others that may 
attend this meeting to fully understand the water budget modeling, but 
will not serve as a forum to debate the merits of the settlement 
process.
    For further information, please contact Frankie Green at (202) 501-
7704.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
