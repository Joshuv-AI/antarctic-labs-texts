RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD


Notification of Meeting

    The Railroad Retirement Board hereby gives notice that the Board 
will meet at 9:30 a.m. (CST), February 21, 1995, in the Board Room on 
the 8th floor of the agency's headquarters building located at 844 N. 
Rush Street, Chicago, Illinois. The Board, by recorded vote, has 
determined that agency business requires the scheduling of this meeting 
with less than one week notice. The subject to be addressed at this 
meeting is the posting for purposes of filling two positions: Chief 
Information Officer and Director of Programs.
    The entire meeting will be open to the public. The person to 
contact for more information is Beatrice Ezerski, Secretary to the 
Board, Phone No. 312-751-4920.

    Dated: February 17, 1995.
Beatrice Ezerski,
Secretary to the Board.
