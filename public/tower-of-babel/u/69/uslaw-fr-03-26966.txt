Small Business Government Contracting Programs; Correction

AGENCY: Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects the DATES section of the final rule 
amending 13 CFR part 125, published on October 20, 2003, in 68 FR 
60006, which amended regulations governing small business prime 
contracting assistance.

DATES: The effective date of the rule FR Doc. 03-26514 published on 
October 20, 2003 (68 FR 60006) is corrected to October 20, 2003.
