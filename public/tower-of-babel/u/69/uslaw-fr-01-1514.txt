FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreement(s) Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of the filing of the following 
agreement(s) under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties can 
review or obtain copies of agreements at the Washington, DC offices of 
the Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, NW., Room 940. Interested 
parties may submit comments on an agreement to the Secretary, Federal 
Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573, within 10 days of the date 
this notice appears in the Federal Register.
    Agreement No.: 011642-004.
    Title: East Coast United States/East Coast of South America Vessel 
Sharing Agreement.
    Parties: A.P. Moller-Maersk Sealand, P&O Nedlloyd Limited, P&O 
Nedlloyd B.V., Oceanica AGW Com. E Rep. Ltda., Compania Sud Americana 
de Vapores, S.A., Companhia Libra de Navegacao S.A., Alianca Navegacao 
e Logistica Ltda., Hamburg-Sud.
    Synopsis: The proposed amendment: deletes Euroatlantic Container 
Line and Braztrans Transportes Maritimos Limitada as parties to the 
agreement; adds Companhia Libra de Navegacao S.A. as a party to the 
agreement; revises slot allocations; and reflects the various trade 
names used by certain parties.

    Dated: January 12, 2001.

    By Order of the Federal Maritime Commission.
Bryant L. VanBrakle,
Secretary.
