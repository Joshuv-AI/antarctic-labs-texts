OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Comment Request for Review of a Revised Information Collection: 
Forms RI 20-7 and RI 30-3

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (Pub. 
L. 104-13, May 22, 1995), this notice announces that the Office of 
Personnel Management (OPM) has submitted to the Office of Management 
and Budget a request for review of a revised information collection. RI 
20-7, Representative Payee Application, is used by the Civil Service 
Retirement System (CSRS) and the Federal Employees Retirement System 
(FERS) to collect information from persons applying to be fiduciaries 
for annuitants or survivor annuitants who appear to be incapable of 
handling their own funds or for minor children. RI 30-3, Information 
Necessary for a Competency Determination, collects medical information 
regarding the annuitant's competency for OPM's use in evaluating the 
annuitant's condition.
    Approximately 12,480 RI 20-7 forms will be completed annually. Each 
form requires approximately 30 minutes to complete. The annual burden 
is 6,240 hours. Approximately 250 RI 30-3 forms will be completed 
annually. Each form requires approximately 1 hour to complete. The 
total annual burden is 6,490 hours. For copies of this proposal, 
contact Mary Beth Smith-Toomey on (202) 606-8358, or E-mail to 
[email protected].
