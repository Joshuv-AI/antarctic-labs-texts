DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[OR-050-1020-00: GP7-0067]


Notice of Meeting of John Day-Snake Resource Advisory Council

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Prineville District.

ACTION: Meeting of John Day-Snake Resource Advisory Council: Pendleton, 
Oregon; February 27, 1997.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A meeting of the John Day-Snake Resource Advisory Council will 
be held on February 27, 1997 from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, at the Red Lion 
Inn, 304 SE Nye Ave, Pendleton, Oregon. Public comments will be 
received from 1:00 pm to 2:00 pm on February 27, 1997. Topics to be 
discussed include the Interior Columbia Basin Ecosystem Management 
Project, Standards for Rangeland Health and Guidelines for Livestock 
Grazing on public lands, and Council Work Plan for 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
James L. Hancock, Bureau of Land Management, Prineville District 
Office, 3050 NE Third Street, Prineville, Oregon 97754, or call 541-
416-6700.

James L. Hancock,
District Manager.
