Industrial Metal Products, Lansing, Michigan; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on March 4, 2005 in response to a petition 
filed by a state agent representative on behalf of workers at 
Industrial Metal Products, Lansing, Michigan.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 7th day of April, 2005.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
