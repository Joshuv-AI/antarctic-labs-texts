NATIONAL FOUNDATION ON THE ARTS AND THE HUMANITIES

Amended Notice of Meeting; Museum Advisory Panel, Agenda of 
Overview Panel Meeting: Correction

May 30, 1995.
ACTION: Notice of correction of agenda to include a closed session.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects the scheduling of open sessions for the 
Museum Advisory Panel (Overview Section) previously published in the 
Federal Register on May 25, 1995 (FR page 27796).
    This panel, scheduled to meet in room M-07 at the Nancy Hanks 
Center, 1100 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW, Washington DC, will now include a 
closed session from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. on June 14 for a brief 
application review. The remainder of the meeting from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. 
on June 13 and from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. will remain open as originally 
scheduled.

    Dated: May 30, 1995.
Yvonne M. Sabine,
Director, Office of Council & Panel Operations, National Endowment for 
the Arts.
