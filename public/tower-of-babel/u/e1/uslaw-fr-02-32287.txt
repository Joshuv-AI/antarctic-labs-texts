FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 02-3382, MB Docket No. 02-220, RM-10518]


Digital Television Broadcast Service; Christiansted, VI

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Virgin Blue, Inc., 
substitutes DTV channel 23 for DTV channel 5 at

[[Page 78193]]

Christiansted, Virgin Islands. See 67 FR 5292, August 14, 2002. DTV 
channel 23 can be allotted to Christiansted in compliance with the 
principle community coverage requirements of section 73.625(a) at 
reference coordinates 17-44-40 N. and 64-43-40 W. with a power of 0.85, 
HAAT of 130 meters and with a DTV service population of 48 thousand. 
With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective January 27, 2003.

418-1600.
