DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, June 30, 2005, 8:30 a.m. to 
June 30, 2005, 3 p.m., Hyatt Regency Bethesda, One Bethesda Metro 
Center, 7400 Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published 
in the Federal Register on May 31, 2005, 70 FR 30958-30961.
    The meeting title has been changed to ``Small Business: Digestive 
Sciences''. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 1, 2005.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
