SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster #3138]


State of Alabama; Amendment #2

    In accordance with information received from the Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby amended to 
include Choctaw and

[[Page 57727]]

Lowndes Counties, Alabama as a disaster area due to damages caused by 
Hurricane Georges.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the following contiguous counties may be filed 
until the specified date at the previously designated location: 
Autauga, Dallas, and Sumter in the State of Alabama, and Lauderdale in 
the State of Mississippi. All other counties contiguous to the above-
named primary counties have been previously declared.
    This declaration is further amended to establish the incident 
period as beginning on September 25, 1998 and continuing through 
October 6, 1998.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is November 29, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is June 30, 1999.
    The number for economic injury in the State of Mississippi is 
9A4200.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Dated: October 16, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
