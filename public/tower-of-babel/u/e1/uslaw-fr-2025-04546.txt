Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2024-1862.
    Petitioner: Dynamic Ventures Inc.
    Sections of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  61.51(f)(2) and 
61.51(j)(4).
    Description of Relief Sought: Petitioner has requested an exemption 
to allow its pilots who the National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration (NASA) requires to be assigned to act as second-in-
command (SIC) of flights in public aircraft operations to be allowed to 
log SIC time for these flights in small turboprop aircraft that do not 
require a SIC by type certificate. Petitioner has also requested the 
exemption to allow their SIC pilots to retroactively include SIC flight 
time from such PAO flights that were not required by type certificate 
or the regulations under which the flights were conducted to have a SIC 
on the aircraft.
