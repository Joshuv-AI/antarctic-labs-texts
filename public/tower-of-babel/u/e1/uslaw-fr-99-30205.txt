OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


The Combined Federal Campaign

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice of limitation on the recognition of national 
federations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Combined Federal Campaign (CFC) regulations at 
5 CFR 950.301(a) which states that ``the Director may from time to time 
place a moratorium on the recognition of national federations'', I 
hereby establish such action for a one year period, beginning with the 
national application process for the year 2000 campaign.
    This moratorium will provide an opportunity for the Office of 
Personnel Management to strengthen the monitoring and auditing process, 
as well as capability to ensure that national federations meet and 
operate in accordance with the public accountability standards of 5 CFR 
950.203 and conform to the requirements of 5 CFR 950.301. This action 
does not prohibit any charity from applying to the CFC national listing 
as an unaffiliated organization, or from applying to the 20 existing 
federations, which currently represent 964 of the 1317 national 
charitable organizations. A list of the existing federations is 
attached.

EFFECTIVE DATE: Year 2000 Campaign.
