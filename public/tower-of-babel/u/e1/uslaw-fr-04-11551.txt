SUMMARY: This document denies a Petition for Partial Reconsideration 
and Request for Expedited Action filed by Rawhide Radio, L.L.C., 
Capstar TX Limited Partnership and Clear Channel Broadcast Licenses, 
Inc. See 68 FR 26557, published May 16, 2003. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.
