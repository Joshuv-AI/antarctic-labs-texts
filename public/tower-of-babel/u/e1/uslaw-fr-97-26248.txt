FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-9; RM-8929, RM-9067]


Radio Broadcasting Services; New Boston, TX and Idabel, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission, at the request of Dixie Broadcasting Company, 
allots Channel 286A to New Boston, Texas. See 62 FR 3851, January 27, 
1997. In response to a counterproposal filed by Idabel Community 
Broadcasters (RM-9067), the Commission also allots Channel 275A to 
Idabel, Oklahoma. Channel 286A and Channel 275A can be allotted to New 
Boston and Idabel, respectively, in compliance with the Commission's 
minimum distance separation requirements. The coordinates for Channel 
286A at New Boston, Texas, are 33-27-41 NL and 94-31-00 WL. The 
coordinates for Channel 275A at Idabel, Oklahoma, are 33-53-48 NL and 
94-49-42 WL. With this action, this proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective November 10, 1997. The window period for filing 
applications for Channel 286A at New Boston, Texas, and Channel 275A at 
Idabel, Oklahoma, will open on November 10, 1997, and close on December 
11, 1997.
