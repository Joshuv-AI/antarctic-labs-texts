DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Office of Public Health and Science


Federal Policies Affecting the Future of Academic Health Centers

AGENCY: Office of Public Health and Science.

ACTION: Correction to notice of two public hearings and comment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice corrects an announcement published in the Federal 
Register: July 29, 1997 (Volume 62, Number 145) page 40532-40533.

CORRECTION: On page 40532, column 2, in DATES section, the deadline for 
receiving requests to give oral testimony is changed from August 7, 
1997 to August 12, 1997. The hearing scheduled on August 25, 1997 in 
Houston, TX is changed to September 2, 1997 in Houston, TX, with the 
starting time to be announced. Written comments accompanying oral 
testimony for the September 2, 1997 hearing is changed from August 11, 
1997 to August 13, 1997. Submission deadline for final written 
comments, without oral testimony, and for final written comments 
accompanying oral testimony is changed from August 23, 1997 to August 
25, 1997.

    Dated: August 5, 1997.
Ciro V. Sumaya,
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Health
