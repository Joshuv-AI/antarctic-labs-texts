DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION


Flight Service Station at Sheridan, Wyoming; Closing

    Notice is hereby given that on or about June 7, 1995, the flight 
service station at Sheridan, Wyoming, will be closed. Services to the 
aviation public formerly provided by this facility will be provided by 
the automated flight service station in Casper, Wyoming. This 
information will be reflected in the FAA Organization Statement the 
next time it is issued. Sec. 313(a) of Federal Aviation Act of 1958, as 
amended, 72 Stat. 752; 49 U.S.C. App. 1354(a).

    Issued in Seattle, Washington, on May 5, 1994.
Frederick M. Isaac,
Regional Administrator, Northwest Mountain Region.
