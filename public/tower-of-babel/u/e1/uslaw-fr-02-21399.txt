GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Office of Management Services; Cancellation of an Optional Form 
by the Department of State

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of State is canceling the following Optional 
Form because of low demand in the Federal Supply Service: OF 157, 
Medical Examination of Applicants for United States Visas.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Charles Cunningham, Department of 
State, 202-312-9605.

DATES: Effective August 22, 2002.

    Dated: August 14, 2002.
Barbara M. Williams,
Deputy Standard and Optional Forms Management Officer, General Services 
Administration.
