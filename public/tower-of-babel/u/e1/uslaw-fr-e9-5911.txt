Indalex, Inc.; Lincolnshire Corporate Office; Lincolnshire, IL; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 13, 2009 in response to a 
worker petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of 
Indalex, Inc., Lincolnshire Corporate Office, Lincolnshire, Illinois.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 9th day of March 2009.
Richard Church
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
