Sunshine Act Meeting

Sunshine Act Meeting of the Board of Directors

CORRECTION: As published on Oct. 28, 1997 (62 FR 55833), the agenda for 
the meeting scheduled for Nov. 15, 1997, is incorrect. The publication 
is corrected as follows:
    9. Consider and act on the report of the Ad Hoc Committee on 
Performance Reviews of the President and Inspector General.
    a. Consider and act on procedural matters, including personal 
performance plans for the President and the Inspector General, written 
submissions prior to interviews, and interview protocols.
