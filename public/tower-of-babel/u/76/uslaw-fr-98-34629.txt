FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 2310]


Petitions for Reconsideration and Clarification of Action in 
Rulemaking Proceedings

December 23, 1998.
    Petitions for reconsideration and clarification have been filed in 
the Commission's rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice 
and published pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of 
these documents are available for viewing and copying in Room 239, 1919 
M Street,

[[Page 72314]]

N.W., Washington, D.C. or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc. (202) 857-3800. Oppositions to these petitions 
must be filed by January 15, 1999. See Section 1.4(b)(1) of the 
Commission's rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1). Replies to an opposition must be 
filed within 10 days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.

SUBJECT: Federal-State Joint Board on University Service Forward-
Looking Mechanism for High Cost Support for Non-Rural LECs (CC Docket 
No. 96-45).

NUMBER OF PETITIONS FILED: 4.
