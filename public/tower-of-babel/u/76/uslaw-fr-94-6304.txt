FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION

 

Ocean Freight Forwarder License Applicants

    Notice is hereby given that the following applicants have filed 
with the Federal Maritime Commission applications for licenses as ocean 
freight forwarders pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 
(46 U.S.C. app. 1718 and 46 CFR 510).
    Persons knowing of any reason why any of the following applicants 
should not receive a license are requested to contact the Office of 
Freight Forwarders, Federal Maritime Commission, Washington, DC 20573.

Global Shipping and Trade Services, Inc., 2050 So. Oneida Street, suite 
116, Denver, CO 80274,
    Officers: Mohamed A. Bernaz, President, Ramadan L. Larbah, 
Stockholder, Hassen A. Hassen, Stockholder.
Quality Forwarding Corp., 2445 S.W. 23rd Avenue, Miami, FL 33145,
    Magda J. Gonzalez, President/Director.
Transport Partners International, Inc., 2211 Lauder Road, Houston, TX 
77039,
    Officers: Albert J. Larose, President, Gregory P. Back, Secretary, 
Michael K. Freeman, Stockholder.

    Sole Proprietor.

    Officers: Daniel Gamas, President/Director, John H. Shaw, 
Secretary/Director.
