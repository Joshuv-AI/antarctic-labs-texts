FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Formations of, Acquisitions by, 
and Mergers of Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 97-20271) published on page 
41387 of the issue for Friday, August 1, 1997.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta heading, the entry for 
Rockdale National Bankshares, Inc., Conyers, Georgia, is revised to 
read as follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta (Lois Berthaume, Vice President) 
104 Marietta Street, N.W., Atlanta, Georgia 30303-2713:
    1. Rockdale National Bankshares, Conyers, Georgia; to become a bank 
holding company by acquiring 100 percent of the voting shares of 
Rockdale National Bank, Conyers, Georgia (in organization).
    Comments on this application must be received by August 22, 1997.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, August 4, 
1997.
Jennifer J. Johnson,
Deputy Secretary of the Board.
