DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
[Docket No. PR95-8-000]


Arkansas Western Gas Company; Notice of Informal Settlement 
Conference

May 1, 1995.
    Take notice that an informal settlement conference in the above-
captioned proceeding will be held on Tuesday, May 16, 1995 at 2:00 p.m. 
in a room to be designated at the offices of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 825 North Capitol Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 
20426.
    Attendance will be limited to the parties and participants, as 
defined by 18 CFR 385.102(b) and (c). Persons wishing to become a party 
must move to intervene and receive intervenor status pursuant to 
Sec. 385.214 of the Commission's regulations.
    For additional information, please contact Mark E. Hegerle at (202) 
208-0287.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
