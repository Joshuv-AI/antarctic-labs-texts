FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: We list the contact for each 
individual RAC in the table above. If you have general questions about 
the RACs or the video teleconference you may contact Karen Slater at 
(202) 452-0358.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The RACs advise the Secretary of the 
Interior, through the Bureau of Land Management, on a variety of 
planning and management issues associated with public land management. 
Every RAC will participate in a video teleconference on November 20 at 
10 a.m., Mountain Standard Time.
    All meetings are open to the public. The public may present written 
comments to the Councils. Each formal Council meeting will also have 
time allocated for hearing public comments. Depending on the number of 
persons wishing to comment and time available, the time for individual 
oral comments may be limited. Individuals who plan to attend and need 
special assistance, such as sign language interpretation, tour 
transportation or other reasonable accommodations, should contact the 
BLM as provided above.
