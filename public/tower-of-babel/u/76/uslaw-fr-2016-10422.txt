M.S.P.H., Director, Extramural Research Program Operations and 
Services, CDC, 4770 Buford Highway NE., Mailstop F-80, Atlanta, Georgia 
30341, Telephone: (770) 488-6295, [email protected].

for both the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the Agency 
for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry.
