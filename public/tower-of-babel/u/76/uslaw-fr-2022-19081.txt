FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Acquisitions of Shares of a Bank 
or Bank Holding Company; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 2022-18061) published on 
page 51418 in the first column of the issue for Monday, August 22, 
2022.
    Under A. Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, entry 1 is corrected to 
read as follows:
    1. Bernard Bennett Banks, Evanston, Illinois, as trustee of the 
Voting Trust Agreement, Miami, Florida; to acquire voting shares of 
National Bancorp Holdings, Inc., and thereby indirectly acquire voting 
shares of The Federal Savings Bank, both of Chicago, Illinois.
    Comments on this application must be received by September 19, 
2022.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
Michele Taylor Fennell,
Deputy Associate Secretary of the Board.
