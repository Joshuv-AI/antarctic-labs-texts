Integrated Silicon Solution, Inc. (ISSI); San Jose, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 17, 2009, in response to a petition 
filed on behalf of workers at Integrated Silicon Solution, Inc., San 
Jose, California.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 3rd day of February 2010.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
