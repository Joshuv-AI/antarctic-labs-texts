Correction

    In proposed rule document 94-24476 beginning on page 51030 in the 
issue of Thursday, October 6, 1994, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 51033, in footnote 6 at the end of the table, ``No'' 
should read ``Not''.
    2. On page 51043, in Table 9, in the first column, in the first 
entry, remove ``1'' preceding ``Overdose''.


Sec. 310.518  [Corrected]

    3. On page 51058, in the second column, in Sec. 310.518, in the 
introductory text, lines two and three should read ``in solid oral 
dosage form (e.g., tablets or capsules) shall meet the following''.
    4. On the same page, in the third column, in the same section, in 
paragraph (a), lines six and seven should read ``unit of the product, 
e.g., tablet or capsule.''.
