DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
[OR-110-1220; G5-205]


Notice Rescinding Emergency Closure and Restriction on Public 
Lands Near West Fork of Williams Creek

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Medford District, Grants Pass 
Resource Area, Interior.

ACTION: Public notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The notice published on page 45489 in the issue of Thursday, 
August 31, 1995, closing entry to and prohibiting physical presence 
upon all lands, roads and trails near West Fork 

[[Page 63540]]
of Williams Creek in Josephine County, Oregon is hereby rescinded.

    Dated: December 1, 1995.
Wayne M. Kuhn,
Associate District Manager.
