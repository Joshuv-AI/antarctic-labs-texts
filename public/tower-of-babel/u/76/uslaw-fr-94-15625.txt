DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

RTCA, Inc.; RTCA Special Committee 169 Twelfth Meeting; Notice

    Pursuant to section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act 
(Pub. L. 92-463, 5 U.S.C., Appendix I), notice is hereby given for 
Special Committee 169 meeting to be held July 21, starting at 9:30 a.m. 
The meeting will be held at the RTCA Conference Room, 1140 Connecticut 
Avenue, NW., Suite 1020, Washington, DC 20036. Agenda is as follows: 
(1) Chairman's introductory remarks; (2) Review of meeting agenda; (3) 
Approval of the Summary of the Eleventh Meeting held April 12, 1994. 
(RTCA Paper No. 224-94/ SC169-223); (4) Report on Working Group 3, 
Flight Information Services Communications, activities; (5) Status ATN; 
(6) Ground to ground data link systems; (7) Other business; (8) Date 
and place of next meeting.
    Attendance is open to the interested public but limited to space 
availability. With the approval of the Chairman, members of the public 
may present oral statements at the meeting. Persons wishing to present 
statements or obtain information should contact the RTCA Secretariat, 
1140 Connecticut Avenue NW., Suite 1020, Washington, D.C. 20036; (202) 
833-9339. Any member of the public may present a written statement to 
the committee at any time.

    Issued in Washington, DC, on June 21, 1994.
David W. Ford,
Designated Officer.
