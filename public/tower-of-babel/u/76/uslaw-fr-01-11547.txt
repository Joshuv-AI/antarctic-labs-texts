SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    Regulations at 50 CFR 660.24 and 660.47 require that certain 
fishing gear must be marked. In the Western Pacific Pelagic Fishery, 
the vessel operator must ensure that the official number of the vessel 
is affixed to every longline buoy and float. In the Western Pacific 
Crustacean Fishery, each trap and float must be marked with the 
vessel's identification number. The marking of gear aids law 
enforcement and is valuable in actions concerning the damage and loss 
of gear.
