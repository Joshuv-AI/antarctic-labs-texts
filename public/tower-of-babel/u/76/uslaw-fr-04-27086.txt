SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Declaration of Disaster P077]


State of Alaska (Amendment 1)

    In accordance with a notice received from the Department of 
Homeland Security--Federal Emergency Management Agency, effective 
November 30, 2004, the above numbered Public Assistance declaration is 
hereby amended to include the Kashunamiut (Chevak) Regional Educational 
Attendance Area (REAA), the Lower Kuskokwim REAA, the Lower Yukon REAA, 
and the Pribilof Island REAA in the State of Alaska as disaster areas 
due to damages caused by a severe winter storm, tidal surges and 
flooding occurring on October 18 through 20, 2004. All other 
information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for filing 
applications for physical damage is January 14, 2005.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59008).

    Dated: December 2, 2004.
Herbert L. Mitchell,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
