SAPA Fabricated Products, Magnolia, AR; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on June 9, 2008, in response to a petition 
filed by the Arkansas State Workforce Office on behalf of the workers 
at SAPA Fabricated Products, Magnolia, Arkansas.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 18th day of July 2008.
Richard Church,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
