DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Availability of A Novel Multi-Layer Composite Material 
Manufacturing Process (Co-Injection Resin Transfer Molding) for 
Exclusive, Partially Exclusive or Non-exclusive Licenses

AGENCY: U.S. Army Research Laboratory.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive or non-exclusive licenses relative to 
a novel multi-layer composite material manufacturing process (co-
injection resin transfer molding) as described in the U.S. Army 
Research Laboratory patent docket# ARL 97-17 and a subsequent patent 
application to the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. A licensing 
meeting is scheduled for Thursday, 29 January 1998, at Aberdeen Proving 
Ground, MD. Visit http://www.fedlabs.org/ma/pl for technical and 
registration information. A non-disclosure agreement MUST be signed 
prior to attending the licensing meeting. Licenses shall comply with 35 
U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR 404.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Michael D. Rausa, U.S. Army Research 
Laboratory, Office of Research and Technology Applications, ATTN: 
AMSRL-CS-TT/Bldg. 434, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland 21005-5425, 
Telephone (410) 278-5028.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: None.
