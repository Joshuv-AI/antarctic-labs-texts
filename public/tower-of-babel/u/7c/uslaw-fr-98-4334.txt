FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 97-117; RM-9009]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wray and Otis, CO

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In response to a request by New Directions Media, Inc., 
licensee of Station KATR-FM, Wray, Colorado, this document substitutes 
Channel 252C1 for Channel 252C2 at Wray, reallots Channel 252C1 to 
Otis, Colorado, and modifies the license for Station KATR-FM 
accordingly, pursuant to the provisions of Sections 1.420(g) and (i) of 
the Commission's Rules. See 62 FR 22900, April 28, 1997. The allotment 
of Channel 252C1 to Otis will provide that community with its first 
local aural transmission facility without depriving Wray of local aural 
service, and enable Station KATR-FM to expand its service area. 
Coordinates used for Channel 252C1 at Otis are 40-08-54 and 102-57-48. 
With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 23, 1998.
