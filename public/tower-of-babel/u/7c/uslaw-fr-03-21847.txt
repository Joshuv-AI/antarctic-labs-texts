DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Intent To Grant an Exclusive Patent License

    Pursuant to the provisions of part 404 of title 37, code of Federal 
Regulations, which implements Public Law 96-517, as amended, the 
Department of the Air Force announces its intention to grant MJS 
International, Inc., a corporation of Missouri, having a place of 
business at Chesterfield, Missouri, an exclusive license in any right, 
title and interest the Air Force has in:
    U.S. Patent No. 6,267,039, issued 31 July 2001, entitled ``Aircraft 
Missile Hit Survivability Using Infrared Lamp and Sacrificial Support 
Structure,'' by Gregory J. Czarnecki.
    A license for this patent will be granted unless a written 
objection is received within fifteen (15) days from the date of 
publication of this Notice. Written objection should be sent to: Air 
Force Materiel Command Law Office, AFMCLO/JAZ, 2240 B. Street, Rm 100, 
Wright-Patterson AFB OH 45433-7109. Telephone: (937) 255-2838; 
Facsimile (937) 255-7333.

Pamela Fitzgerald,
Air Force Federal Register Liaison Officer.
