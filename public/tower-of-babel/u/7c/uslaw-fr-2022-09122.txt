Joint Federal-State Task Force on Electric Transmission; Notice 
of Meeting and Agenda

    As first announced in the Commission's March 22, 2022 Notice in the 
above-captioned docket,\1\ the third public meeting of the Joint 
Federal-State Task Force on Electric Transmission (Task Force) will be 
held virtually on Friday, May 6, 2022, from approximately 10:00 a.m. to 
4:00 p.m. Eastern time. Commissioners may attend and participate in 
this meeting. Attached to this Notice is an agenda for the meeting.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Joint Fed.-State Task Force on Elec. Transmission, Notice, 
Docket No. AD21-15-000 (issued Mar. 22, 2022).
