Caribbean Fishery Management Council; Public Meetings

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meetings of the Annual Catch Limit Plan 
Development Group (ACLG) and Scientific and Statistical Committee 
(SSC).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council will hold meetings of 
its ACLG and SSC.

DATES: The ACLG will meet on February 23-25, 2009, and the SSC will 
meet on February 26-27, 2009. Both meetings will be held at the Embassy 
Suites Hotel, located at 8080 Tartak St., Isla Verde, Carolina, Puerto 
Rico 00979.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Caribbean Fishery Management Council, 
268 Munoz Rivera Avenue, Suite 1108, San Juan, Puerto Rico 00918; 
telephone: (787) 766-5926.
