DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 1962]

 

Delegation of Authority 211; Authorization of Testimony and 
Production of Documents

    By virtue of the authority vested in me by 22 CFR 172.4(b) and 
section 4 of the Act of May 26, 1949 (63 Stat. 111, 22 U.S.C. 2658), I 
hereby delegate to Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary for Consular 
Affairs the authority conferred on the Assistant Secretary for Consular 
Affairs pursuant to 22 CFR 172.4 and 172.5 concerning approval of 
testimony and production of documents by Department of State employees. 
This authority may be redelegated to another Deputy Assistant 
Secretary. Notwithstanding this delegation of authority, the Assistant 
Secretary for Consular Affairs may at any time exercise any authority 
conferred upon that officer by 22 CFR part 172.

    Dated: January 31, 1994.
Mary A. Ryan,
Assistant Secretary of Consular Affairs.
