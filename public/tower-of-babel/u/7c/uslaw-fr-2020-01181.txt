Wiscons8, LLC; IOWAter Power Corporation; Notice of Application 
for Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To Intervene, 
and Protests

    On December 30, 2019, Wiscons8, LLC (transferor) and IOWAter Power 
Corporation (transferee) filed an application for the transfer of 
license of the Big Falls Milldam Project No. 12449. The project is 
located on the Little Wolf River in Waupaca County, Wisconsin.
    The applicants seek Commission approval to transfer the license for 
the Big Falls Milldam Project from the transferor to the transferee.
