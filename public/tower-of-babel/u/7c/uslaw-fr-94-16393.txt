DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
Minority Business Development Agency

 

Business Development Center Applications: Indianapolis, IN

agency: Minority Business Development Agency, Commerce.

action: Cancellation of notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: The Minority Business Development Agency (MBDA) is canceling 
the announcement to solicit competitive applications under its Minority 
Business Development Center program to operate a Indianapolis, Indiana 
MBDA for a three (3) year period, starting September 1, 1994, to August 
31, 1995, in the Indianapolis, Indiana MSA (Closing date May 2, 1994). 
Refer to the Federal Register dated March 23, 1994.

    Dated: June 30, 1994.
David Vega,
Regional Director, Chicago Regional Office.
