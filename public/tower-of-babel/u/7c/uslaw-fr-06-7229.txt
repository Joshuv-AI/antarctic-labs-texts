DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, September 11, 2006, 5 p.m. to 
September 13, 2006, 6 p.m., Doubletree Hotel, 8120 Wisconsin Ave., 
Bethesda, MD, 20814 which was published in the Federal Register on July 
25, 2006. 71 FR 42098.
    The meeting notice is amended to reflect the change in hotel from 
the Doubletree Hotel, 8120 Wisconsin Ave., Bethesda, MD 20814 to the 
Clarion Hotel, 8400 Wisconsin Ave., Bethesda, MD 20814. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: August 22, 2006.
Anna Snouffer,
Acting Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
