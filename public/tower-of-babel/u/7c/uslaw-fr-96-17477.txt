FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
[Report No. 2141]


Petition for Reconsideration of Action in a Rulemaking Proceeding

July 8, 1996.
    A Petition for reconsideration has been filed in the Commission's 
rulemaking proceedings listed in this Public Notice and published 
pursuant to 47 CFR Section 1.429(e). The full text of this document is 
available for viewing and copying in Room 239, 1919 M Street, N.W., 
Washington, D.C. or may be purchased from the Commission's copy 
contractor, ITS, Inc., (202) 857-3800. Opposition to this petition must 
be filed July 25, 1996. See section 1.4(b)(1) of the Commission's rules 
(47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)). Replies to an opposition must be filed within 10 
days after the time for filing oppositions has expired.

Subject: Amendment of Section 73.202(b), FM Broadcast Stations, 
Table of Allotments. (Denison-Sherman, Paris, Jacksboro, Texas and 
Madill, Oklahoma) (MM Docket No. 95-126, RM-8671)

    Number of Petitions Filed: 1.

Federal Communications Commission.
