DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. IS17-522-000]


Supplemental Notice of Technical Conference; Colonial Pipeline 
Company

    On September 29, 2017, a notice was issued stating that a technical 
conference will be held to address the effect of the tariff changes 
proposed by Colonial Pipeline Company in its June 23, 2017 filing in 
this docket. The technical conference will be held on Wednesday, 
October 25, 2017 at 9:00 a.m., in a room to be designated at the 
offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street 
NE., Washington, DC 20426.
    For interested persons who cannot attend the technical conference, 
a call-in number has been established. Dial in from your phone:

From within local Wash, DC area 202-502-6888
From outside Local Wash, DC area 1-877-857-1347
Meeting ID: 0255

    Dated: October 12, 2017.
Nathaniel J. Davis, Sr.,
Deputy Secretary.
