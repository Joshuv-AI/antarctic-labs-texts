SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On October 15, 2015, the Department of 
Commerce (the Department) published the preliminary affirmative less 
than fair value determination on certain polyethylene terephthalate 
resin from the People's Republic of China.\1\ The Preliminary 
Determination contained inadvertent errors in the chart containing the 
weighted-average margins. Specifically, certain exporter names were 
matched with the incorrect producer names. The chart below contains the 
correct combinations of names:

People's Republic of China: Preliminary Determination of Sales at 
Less Than Fair Value and Postponement of Final Determination, 80 FR
