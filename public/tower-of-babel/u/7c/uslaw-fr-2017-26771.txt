DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Notice of Opportunity for Public Comment on a Land Swap Between 
the Northern Maine Regional Airport and the Presque Isle Industrial 
Council, Presque Isle, ME

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Request for public comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is being given that the FAA is considering a request 
from the Northern Maine Regional Airport, for a land swap with the 
Presque Isle Industrial Council. The on-airport land, currently in use 
as non-aeronautical development, is to be swapped with four parcels of 
land along the northern ramp area of airport and land within the 
northern approach. The land swap will further enhance the protection of 
the northern approach area while also providing developable land for 
aeronautical uses.

DATES: Comments must be received on or before January 11, 2018.
