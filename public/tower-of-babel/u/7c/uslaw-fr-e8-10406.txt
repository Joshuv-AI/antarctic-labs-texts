Public Announcement; Sunshine Act Meeting

    Pursuant to the Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. 94-409) [5 
U.S.C. Section 552b].

Agency Holding Meeting: Department of Justice, United States Parole 
Commission.

Time and Date: 10 a.m., Tuesday, May 13, 2008.

Place: 5550 Friendship Boulevard, Fourth Floor, Chevy Chase, Maryland 
20815.

Status: Open.

Quarterly Business Meeting.
    2. Reports from the Chairman, Commissioners, Chief of Staff, and 
Section Administrators.
    3. YRA Misdemeanor Offenders--Use of Misconduct Reports to Issue 
Set Aside Certificates.

Agency Contact: Thomas W. Hutchison, Chief of Staff, United States 
Parole Commission, (301) 492-5990.
