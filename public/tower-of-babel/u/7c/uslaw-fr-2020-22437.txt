DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 12, 2020, 10:00 a.m. 
to November 12, 2020, 5:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, 
Rockville, MD 20850 which was published in the Federal Register on 
September 04, 2020, 85 FR 55308.
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting start time from 
10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. The meeting will now be held from 1:00 p.m. to 
5:00 p.m. on November 12, 2020. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 5, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
