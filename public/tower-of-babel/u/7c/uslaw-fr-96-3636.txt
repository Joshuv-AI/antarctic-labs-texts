Panhandle Eastern Pipe Line Company; Notice of Technical

Thursday, February 29, 1996 at 10:00 a.m. in a room to be designated at 
the offices of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First 
Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426.
