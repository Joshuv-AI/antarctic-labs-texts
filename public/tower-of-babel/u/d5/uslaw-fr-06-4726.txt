DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Antitrust Division


Notice Pursuant to the National Cooperative Research and 
Production Act of 1993--United Technologies Research Center

    Notice is hereby given that, on March 21, 2006, pursuant to section 
6(a) of the National Cooperative Research and Production Act of 1993, 
15 U.S.C. 4301 et seq. (``the Act''), United Technologies Research 
Center (``UTRC''), has filed written notifications simultaneously with 
the Attorney General and Federal Trade Commission disclosing (1) the 
identities of the parties to the venture and (2) the nature and 
objectives of the venture. The notifications were filed for the purpose 
of invoking the Act's provisions limiting the recovery of antitrust 
plaintiffs to actual damages under specified circumstances.
    Pursuant to section 6(b) of the Act, the identities of the parties 
to the venture are: United Technologies Corporation, East Hartford, CT; 
and Rotary Power LLC, Fort Salonga, NY. The general area of UTRC's 
planned activity is to engage in cooperative research and development 
in the area of compound gas turbine/rotary shaft power producing 
engines.

Dorothy B. Fountain,
Deputy Director of Operations, Antitrust Division.
