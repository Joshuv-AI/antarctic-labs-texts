COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS


Notice of Public Meeting of the Ohio Advisory Committee; 
Cancellation

AGENCY: Commission on Civil Rights.

ACTION: Notice; Cancellation of virtual business meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Commission on Civil Rights published a notice in the 
Federal Register concerning a virtual business meeting of the Ohio 
Advisory Committee. The meeting scheduled for Wednesday, October 5, 
2022, at 12:00 p.m. (ET) is cancelled. The notice is in the Federal 
Register of Tuesday, September 13, 2022, in FR Doc. 2022-19698 in the 
first column of page 55990.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Melissa Wojnaroski, 
[email protected].
