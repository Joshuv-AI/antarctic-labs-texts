DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Human Genome Research Institute; Amended Notice of 
Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Human Genome Research Institute Special Emphasis Panel, November 29, 
2011, 8 a.m. to November 29, 2011, 5 p.m., Residence Inn Bethesda, 7335 
Wisconsin Avenue, Bethesda, MD 20814 which was published in the Federal

[[Page 65739]]

Register on October 14, 2011, 76FR63932.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the meeting end date from 
November 29, 2011 to November 30, 2011. The meeting is closed to the 
public.

    Dated: October 18, 2011.
Jennifer S. Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
