DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,865]


Seel Tool and Die, St. Marys, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 29, 2009, in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers of Seel Tool and Die, St. Marys, 
Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.


[[Page 27352]]


    Signed at Washington, DC, this 8th day of May 2009.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
