Birch Power Company; Notice of Technical Meeting

    a. Date and Time of Meeting: January 23, 2017 at 2:00 p.m. Eastern 
Standard Time (1:00 p.m. Central Standard Time).

    c. FERC Contact: Adam Peer at [email protected], or (202) 502-
8449.

Adam Peer at (202) 502-8449 by January 17, 2017, to RSVP and to receive 
specific instructions on how to participate.
