DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, September 18, 2019, 8:00 a.m. 
to September 19, 2019, 3:00 p.m., Gaithersburg Washingtonian Marriott, 
9751 Washingtonian Blvd., Gaithersburg, MD 20878 which was published in 
the Federal Register on August 12, 2019, 84 FR 39858.
    The meeting notice is amended to change the meeting start time on 
September 18, 2019 from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and the meeting end time 
on September 19, 2019 from 3:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. The meeting is closed 
to the public.

    Dated: August 27, 2019.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
