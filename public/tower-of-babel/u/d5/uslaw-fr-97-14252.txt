DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. ER97-2770-000]


Toledo Edison Company; Notice of Filing

May 27, 1997.
    Take notice that on April 30, 1997, Toledo Edison Company (Toledo) 
tendered for filing its quarterly report of transactions for the period 
January 1, 1997 to March 31, 1997.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before June 4, 1997. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to

[[Page 29724]]

become a party must file a motion to intervene. Copies of this filing 
are on file with the Commission and are available for public 
inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
