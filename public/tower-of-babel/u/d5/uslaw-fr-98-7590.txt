SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Declaration of Disaster #3057: State of California (Amendment #3)

    In accordance with a notice from the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency dated March 6, 1998, the above-numbered Declaration is hereby 
amended to include Kern, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego, and 
Tulare Counties in the State of California as a disaster area due to 
damages caused by severe winter storms and flooding beginning on 
February 2, 1998 and continuing.
    In addition, applications for economic injury loans from small 
businesses located in the contiguous Counties of Imperial in 
California; Clark in Nevada, and LaPaz and Mohave in Arizona may

[[Page 14164]]

be filed until the specified date at the previously designated 
location.
    All other information remains the same, i.e., the deadline for 
filing applications for physical damage is April 10, 1998 and for 
economic injury the termination date is November 9, 1998.
    The economic injury number for Nevada is 976300 and for Arizona the 
number is 976400.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Program Nos. 59002 and 
59008)

    Date: March 10, 1998.
Bernard Kulik,
Associate Administrator for Disaster Assistance.
