47 CFR Part 73

[DA 01-347; MM Docket No. 00-171; RM-9926]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wells and Woodville, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document reallots Channel 234C2 from Woodville, Texas, to 
Wells, Texas, and modifies the license for Station KVLL to specify 
operation on Channel 234C2 at Wells in response to a petition filed by 
Radio Woodville, Inc. See 65 FR 59162, October 4, 2000. The coordinates 
for Channel 234C2 at Wells are 31-12-37 and 94-57-15.

DATES: Effective March 26, 2001.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a summary of the Commission's Report 
and Order, MM Docket No. 00-171, adopted January 31, 2001, and released 
February 9, 2001. The full text of this Commission decision is 
available for inspection and copying during normal business hours in
