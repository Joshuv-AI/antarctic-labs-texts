DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
[TA-W-30,491]

 

Vought Aircraft Company Dallas, Texas; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on November 14, 1994 in response to a worker petition 
which was filed on behalf of workers at Vought Aircraft Company, 
Dallas, Texas.
    Active certification covering the petitioning group of workers at 
the subject firm remains in effect (TA-W-29,206). Consequently, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, D.C. this 22nd day of November, 1994
Victor J. Trunzo,
Program Manager, Policy and Reemployment Services, Office of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance.
