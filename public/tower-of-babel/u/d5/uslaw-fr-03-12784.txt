DuCoa L.P. ; Filing of Food Additive Petition (Animal Use); 
Natamycin; Change of Petitioner's Name

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has been notified by 
the petitioner, DuCoa L.P., that it has changed its name to Arkion Life 
Sciences, doing business at the same address. On September 20, 1995 (60 
FR 48715), a Federal Register notice announced that a food additive 
petition (FAP 2234) had been filed by DuCoa L.P. proposing the use of 
natamycin as a mold retardent in broiler chicken feed.
