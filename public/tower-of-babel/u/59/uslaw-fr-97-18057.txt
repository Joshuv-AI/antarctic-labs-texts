SUMMARY: This action corrects an error in the land description in 
Public Land Order No. 7263, 62 FR 31450-31451, published June 9, 1997, 
FR Doc. 97-14927.

EFFECTIVE DATE: July 10, 1997.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Shirley J. Macke, BLM Alaska State 
Office, 222 W. 7th Avenue, No. 13, Anchorage, Alaska 99513-7599, 907-
271-3266.
    On page 31451, first column, 15th line, which reads ``21, T. 55 S., 
R. 71 E., is more'' is hereby corrected to read ``21, T. 55 S., R. 77 
E., is more''.

    Dated: June 27, 1997.
Donald W. Baggs,
