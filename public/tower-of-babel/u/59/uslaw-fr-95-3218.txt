Annex I--Modifications to General Note 4(d) of 
                the HTS

                Effective with respect to articles both: (i) imported 
                on or after January 1, 1976, and (ii) entered, or 
                withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after 
                July 1, 1995.

                (a) deleting the following HTS subheadings and the 
                country set out opposite such subheadings:
