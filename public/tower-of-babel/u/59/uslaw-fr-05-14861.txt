FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Ocean Transportation Intermediary License Revocations

following Ocean Transportation Intermediary licenses have been revoked 
pursuant to section 19 of the Shipping Act of 1984 (46 U.S.C. app. 
1718) and the regulations of the Commission pertaining to the licensing 
of Ocean Transportation Intermediaries, effective on the corresponding 
date shown below:

    Name: Delmas.
    Address: 1 quai Colbert, 76600 Le Havre, France.
    Date Revoked: July 1, 2005.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.

    License Number: 000429F.
    Name: Reedy Forwarding Company, Inc.
    Address: 631 Southwest 21 Road, Miami, FL 33129.
    Date Revoked: June 10, 2005.
    Reason: Surrendered license voluntarily.

    License Number: 017976N.
    Name: Sanwoo (America) Inc. dba Amos Cargo Service.
    Address: 2100 91st Street, North Bergen, NJ 07047.
    Date Revoked: June 30, 2005.
