Strengthening Institutions, American Indian Tribally Controlled 
Colleges and Universities and Alaska Native and Native Hawaiian-Serving 
Institutions Programs

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: On May 19, 2006, we published a notice in 
the Federal Register (71 FR 29130) inviting applications for new awards 
for fiscal year (FY) 2006 for the Strengthening Institutions, American 
Indian Tribally Controlled Colleges and Universities and Alaska Native 
and Native Hawaiian-Serving Institutions programs. On page 29131 of 
that notice, we included charts listing the maximum award amounts, the 
estimated number of awards and the estimated average size of awards for 
the FY 2006 competitions for these programs. The charts incorrectly 
identified the Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance (CFDA) number for 
the Strengthening Institutions program as ``83.031A.'' This notice 
corrects that error.
    The CFDA number listed for the Strengthening Institutions program 
in the charts on page 29131 is corrected to read ``84.031A.''
