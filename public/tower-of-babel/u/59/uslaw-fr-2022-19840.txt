SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Disaster Declaration #17567 and #17568; CALIFORNIA Disaster Number CA-

Administrative Declaration Amendment of a Disaster for the State 
of California

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Amendment 1.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is an amendment of the Administrative declaration of 
disaster for the State of CALIFORNIA dated 08/09/2022.
    Incident: Oak Fire.
    Incident Period: 07/22/2022 through 08/16/2022.

DATES: Issued on 09/07/2022.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: 10/10/2022.
    Economic Injury (EIDL) Loan Application Deadline Date: 05/09/2023.

ADDRESSES: Submit completed loan applications to: U.S. Small Business 
Administration, Processing and Disbursement Center, 14925 Kingsport 
Road, Fort Worth, TX 76155.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: A. Escobar, Office of Disaster 
Assistance, U.S. Small Business Administration,

[[Page 56481]]

409 3rd Street SW, Suite 6050, Washington, DC 20416, (202) 205-6734.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of the Administrator's disaster 
declaration for the State of California, dated 08/09/2022 is hereby 
amended to establish the incident period for this disaster as beginning 
07/22/2022 and continuing through 08/16/2022.
    All other information in the original declaration remains 
unchanged.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Number 59008)

Isabella Guzman,
Administrator.
