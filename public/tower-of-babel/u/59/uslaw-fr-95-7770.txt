DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Business Development Center Applications: Cleveland, OH

AGENCY: Minority Business Development Agency.

ACTION: Cancellation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Minority Business Development Agency is cancelling the 
announcement to solicit competitive applications under for its Minority 
Business Development Center (MBDC) Program to operate the Cleveland, 
Ohio MBDC. This solicitation was originally published in the Federal 
Register, Monday, October 17, 1994, Vol. 59, No. 199, 52288.
