Commission Organization

CFR Correction

    In Title 47 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 0 to 19, 
revised as of October 1, 2006, on page 45, in Sec.  0.406, in paragraph 
(b)(2), the eighth sentence, beginning with ``Additional procedures 
applicable . . .'', is removed and a sentence is added following the 
sixth sentence to read as follows:


Sec.  0.406  The rules and regulations.

* * * * *
    (b) * * *
    (2) * * * Part 1, subpart E, of this chapter contains general rules 
and procedures applicable to common carriers. * * *
