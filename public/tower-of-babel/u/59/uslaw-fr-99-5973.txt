UNITED STATES INFORMATION AGENCY


Culturally Significant Objects Imported for Exhibition; 
Determinations

AGENCY: United States Information Agency.
    This is an amendment to Notice Regarding Culturally Significant 
Objects Imported for Exhibition in the exhibit entitled 
``Impressionists in Winter: Effets de Neige.'' This is to amend Federal 
Register Doc. 98-19127, 63 FR 38696 (July 17, 1998) by adding the 
following language after the words ``to May 2, 1999'': ``and at The 
Brooklyn Museum of Art from on or about May 27, 1999, to on or about 
August 29, 1999.''

    Dated: March 4, 1999.
Les Jin,
General Counsel.
