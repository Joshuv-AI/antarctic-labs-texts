Dearborn Brass, 21st Century Companies, Inc., Media, PA; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on October 4, 2000 in response to a petition filed on 
behalf of workers at Dearborn Brass, 21st Century Companies, Inc., 
Media, Pennsylvania.
    The petition verification stage of the investigation revealed the 
petitioning group of workers are actually located in Tyler, Texas. The 
worker group is subject to an ongoing investigation for which a 
determination has not yet been issued (TA-W-38,349). Consequently, 
further investigation in this case would serve no purpose, and the 
investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC this 12th day of December, 2000.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division Trade Adjustment Assistance.
