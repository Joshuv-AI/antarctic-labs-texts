FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[DA 04-915; MM Docket No. 01-182; RM-10202]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Clarksville, TX, and Haworth, OK

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Audio Division, at the request of Haworth Broadcasting 
Company, allots Channel 294A at Haworth, Oklahoma, as the community's 
first local FM service. This allotment at Haworth, Oklahoma, was 
adopted in lieu of the original proposal of Katherine Pyeatt, 
requesting the allotment of Channel 294A at Clarksville, Texas. Channel 
294A can be allotted to Haworth, Oklahoma, in compliance with the 
Commission's minimum distance separation requirements with a site 
restriction of 10.1 km (6.3 miles) south of Haworth. The coordinates 
for Channel 294A at Haworth, Oklahoma, are 33-45-33 North Latitude and 
94-41-06 West Longitude. A filing window for Channel

[[Page 20555]]

294A at Haworth, Oklahoma, will not be opened at this time. Instead, 
the issue of opening this allotment for auction will be addressed by 
the Commission in a subsequent Order.

DATES: Effective May 20, 2004.
