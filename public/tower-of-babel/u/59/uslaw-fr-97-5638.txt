DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Office of Hearings and Appeals


Notice of Cases Filed During the Week of January 6 Through 
January 10, 1997

    During the Week of January 6 through January 10, 1997, the appeals, 
applications, petitions or other requests listed in this Notice were 
filed with the Office of Hearings and Appeals of the Department of 
Energy.
    Any person who will be aggrieved by the DOE action sought in any of 
these cases may file written comments on the application within ten 
days of publication of this Notice or the date of

[[Page 10557]]

receipt of actual notice, whichever occurs first. All such comments 
shall be filed with the Office of Hearings and Appeals, Department of 
Energy, Washington, D.C. 20585-0107.

    Dated: February 27, 1997.
George B. Breznay,
Director, Office of Hearings and Appeals.
