Parker International Products, Inc.; Worcester, MA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on January 29, 2008 in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of Parker 
International Products, Inc., Worcester, Massachusetts.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated. Therefore, further 
investigation in this case would serve no purpose.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 7th day of February, 2008.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
