DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Southwest Oregon Provincial Interagency Executive Committee 
(PIEC), Advisory Committee

agency: Forest Service, USDA.

action: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

summary: The Southwest Oregon PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on 
August 25, 1998 in Gold Beach, Oregon.
    The meeting will begin at 9:00 a.m. and continue until 5:00 p.m. 
Agenda items to be covered include: (1) review of committee operating 
guides; (2) final version of issues and work plan for Advisory 
Committee; (3) demonstration of compact disk containing geographic 
information across all ownerships within the province; (4) local issue 
presentation by Siskiyou National Forest; (5) Timber, monitoring, and 
aquatic conservation strategy subcommittees will develop action plans 
for their assigned tasks; (7) public comment. All Province Advisory 
Committee meetings are open to the public.

for further information contact: Direct questions regarding this 
meeting to Chuck Anderson, Province Advisory Committee staff, USDA, 
Forest Service, Rogue River National Forest, 333 W. 8th Street, 
Medford, Oregon 97501, phone 541-858-2322.

    Date: August 3, 1998.
James T. Gladen,
Forest Supervisork, Designated Federal Official.
