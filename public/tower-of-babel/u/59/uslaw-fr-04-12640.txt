Iomega Corp., Roy, UT; Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on April 5, 2004, in response to a worker 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers at Iomega 
Corp., Roy, Utah.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 28th day of April, 2004.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
