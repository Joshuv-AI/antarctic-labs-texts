DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Special Emphasis Panel, June 
27, 2001, 1 p.m. to June 29, 2001, 5 p.m. Nat. Institute of 
Environmental Health Sciences, South Campus, Building 101, Conference 
Room B, Research Triangle Park, NC, 27709 which was published in the 
Federal Register on June 7, 2001, FR 66: 30742.
    The meeting will be held on July 15, 2001 from 7 p.m. to July 18, 
2001 at 5 p.m. at the Hawthorne Suites, Meredith Drive, Durham, NC. The 
meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: June 20, 2001.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
