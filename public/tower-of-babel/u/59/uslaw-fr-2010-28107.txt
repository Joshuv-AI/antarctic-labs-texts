Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2008-0433.
    Petitioner: Aviation Fabricators.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: 14 CFR 23.815.
    Description of Relief Sought: Aviation Fabricators requests an 
exemption to permit them to install Beechcraft Aft Jump Seat Kits in 
Beechcraft model B300 aircraft with a wide back executive seat 
configuration. The revised configuration would increase seating in the 
aircraft to 10 or 11 passengers, without replacing the existing cabin 
seats with a narrower seat.
