FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Notice of Proposals To Engage in Permissible Activities or To 
Acquire Companies That are Engaged in Permissible Nonbanking 
Activities; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 97-27898) published on pages 
54850 and 54851 of the issue for Wednesday, October 22, 1997.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis heading, the entry for 
Area Bancshares Corporation, Ownesboro, Kentucky, is revised to read as 
follows:
    A. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (Randall C. Sumner, Vice 
President) 411 Locust Street, St. Louis, Missouri 63102-2034:
    1. Area Bancshares Corporation, Owensboro, Kentucky; to acquire a 
company and thereby engage in developing and providing data processing 
and data transmission services to financial institutions for use in 
providing products and services over the Internet, pursuant to Sec.  
225.28(b)(14) of the Board's Regulation Y.
    Comments on this application must be received by November 5, 1997.
