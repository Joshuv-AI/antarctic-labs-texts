1. CMS Generation Pinamucan Operating Limited Duration Company

[Docket No. EG97-29-000]

    On January 27, 1997, CMS Generation Pinamucan Operating Limited 
Duration Company, Fairlane Plaza South, 330 Town Center Drive, Suite 
1100, Dearborn, Michigan 48126, filed with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission an application for determination of exempt 
wholesale generator status pursuant to Part 365 of the Commission's 
Regulations.
    CMS Generation Pinamucan Operating Limited Duration Company is a 
wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of CMS Generation Co., a Michigan 
corporation, which is a wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of CMS Energy 
Corporation, also a Michigan corporation. CMS Generation Pinamucan 
Operating Limited Duration Company will operate, as an agent of the 
owner, an approximately 63 megawatt diesel fuel-fired electric 
cogeneration facility (the ``Facility'') located in the Cavite Export 
Processing Zone in Rosario, Cavite, Philippines.
    Comment date: February 14, 1997, in accordance with Standard 
Paragraph E at the end of this notice. The Commission will limit its 
consideration of comments to those that concern the adequacy or 
accuracy of the application.
