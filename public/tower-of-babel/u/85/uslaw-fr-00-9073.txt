DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation


Advisory Board; Notice of Change of Meeting Location

    The location of the meeting of the Advisory Board of the Saint 
Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, to be held at 9 a.m. on 
Wednesday, April 12, 2000, notice of which was published in the Federal 
Register on March 30 (65 FR 17000), has been changed. The new location 
is the Sheraton West Palm

[[Page 19433]]

Beach Hotel, 630 Clearwater Park Road, West Palm Beach, Florida.

    Issued at Washington, D.C. on April 7, 2000.
Marc C. Owen,
Advisory Board Liaison.
