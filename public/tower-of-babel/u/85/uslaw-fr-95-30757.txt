FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 90-468; RM-7380]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Wickenburg and Lake Havasu City, AZ

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule; petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This documents grants a Petition for Reconsideration filed by 
Interstate Broadcasting System of Arizona, Inc., licensee of Station 
KRDS-FM, Channel 287C2, Wickenburg, Arizona, directed to the Report and 
Order in this proceeding which had upgraded the Station KRDS-FM license 
to specify operation on Channel 287C1. See 56 FR 43884, September 5, 
1991. With this action, the proceeding is terminated.

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 26, 1996.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Mass Media Bureau, (202) 
776-1654.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a synopsis of the Commission's 
Memorandum Opinion and Order in MM Docket No. 90- 468, adopted December 
6, 1995, and released December 12, 1995. The full text of this decision
