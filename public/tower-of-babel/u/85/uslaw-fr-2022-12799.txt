Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, Small Business: Health 
Informatics, June 29, 2022, 10:00 a.m. to June 29, 2022, 7:00 p.m.,

20892 which was published in the Federal Register on June 03, 2022, FR

29, 2022, to June 29, 2022--June 30, 2022. The meeting is closed to the 
public.
