DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Notice of Modoc County RAC Meetings

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the authorities in the Federal Advisory Committees 
Act (Pub. L. 92-463) and under the Secure Rural Schools and Community 
Self-Determination Act of 2000 (Pub. L. 106-393), the Modoc National 
Forest's Modoc County Resource Advisory Committee will meet Monday May 
3, 2004, from 6 to 8 p.m. in Alturas, California. The meeting is open 
to the public.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Agenda topics for the meeting include 
approval of the April 5, 2003, minutes, review of two short form 
proposals for possible funding in 2005. The meeting will be held at 
Modoc National Forest Office, Conference Room, 800 West 12th St., 
Alturas, California on Monday, May 3, 2004, from 6 to 8 p.m. Time will 
be set aside for public comments at the beginning of the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Forest Supervisor Stan Sylva, at (530) 
233-8700; or Public Affairs Officer Nancy Gardner at (530) 233-8713.

Nancy Gardner,
Acting Forest Supervisor.
