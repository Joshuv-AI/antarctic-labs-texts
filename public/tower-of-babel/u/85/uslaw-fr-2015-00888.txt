SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

I. Abstract

    StormReady and TsunamiReady are voluntary programs offered as a 
means of providing guidance and incentive to officials interested in 
improving their respective hazardous weather operations. The StormReady 
Application Form, Tsunami-Ready Application Form and TsunamiReady/
StormReady Application Form are used by localities to apply for initial 
StormReady or TsunamiReady and StormReady recognition and renewal of 
that recognition every six years. The government will use the 
information collected to determine whether a community has met all of 
the criteria to receive StormReady and/or TsunamiReady recognition. In 
addition, businesses, schools, non-profit organizations and other non-
governmental entities often establish severe weather safety plans and 
actively promote severe weather safety awareness activities but may not 
have the resources necessary to fulfill all the eligibility 
requirements to achieve the full StormReady recognition. These entities 
may apply through the StormReady Supporter program for recognition.
