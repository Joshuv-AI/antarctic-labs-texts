Towing Safety Advisory Committee; Charter Renewal

AGENCY: Coast Guard, DOT.

ACTION: Notice of charter renewal.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Secretary of Transportation has renewed the charter for 
the Towing Safety Advisory Committee (TSAC) for a period of two years 
from May 19, 2001, until May 19, 2003. TSAC is a federal advisory 
committee constituted under 5 U.S.C. App. 2. It advises the Secretary 
of Transportation on matters relating to shallow-draft inland and 
coastal waterway navigation and towing safety.
    This notice and the charter are available on the Internet at http://dms.dot.gov. The charter is also available on TSAC's Internet web page 
at http://www.uscg.mil/hq/g-m/advisory/index.htm.
