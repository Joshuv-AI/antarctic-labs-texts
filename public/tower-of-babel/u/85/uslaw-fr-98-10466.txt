    Periodically, the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services 
Administration (SAMHSA) will publish a list of information collection

the SAMHSA Reports Clearance Officer on (301) 443-8005.

Proposed Project

    State Prevention Needs Assessments: Alcohol and Other Drugs--New--
SAMHSA's Center for Substance Abuse Prevention (CSAP) has awarded 
contracts to several States (Cohort III) to collect data to assess the 
nature and extent of substance abuse prevention services needs. In 
addition, the data collection from these States will be used to pilot 
test several instruments and methodologies to determine standard 
measures and methodologies to be used by the next cohort of States 
(Cohort IV) funded to conduct needs assessment studies.
    Data will be collected in school surveys, community-based 
prevention resource assessments (CRA), and special population studies. 
The information collected in this project will be combined with 
existing sources and may use multiple approaches to assess risk and 
protective factors for substance use, prevention service needs, and 
substance abuse prevalence. These needs assessment studies will permit 
cross-State comparisons of predictor variables to assist Federal 
program planning, allocating resources, and responding to the 
Government Performance and Results Act (GPRA).
