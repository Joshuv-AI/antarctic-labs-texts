FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[Report No. 1]


Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Commission Proceeding

May 31, 1996.
    A petition for partial reconsideration has been filed with respect 
to the Commission's Memorandum Opinion and Order listed below. The full 
text of this document is available for viewing and copying in Room 610, 
1919 M Street, NW., Washington, DC, by contacting Donna Viert ((202) 
418-1725). In addition, copies may be purchased from the Commission's 
copy contractor, ITS, Inc. ((202) 857-3800). In accordance with section 
1.45(b) of the Commission's Rules (47 CFR 1.4(b)(1)) oppositions to 
this petition for partial reconsideration must be filed June 24, 1996. 
Replies to an opposition must be filed within 10 days after the time 
for filing oppositions has expired.

Subject: Settlements in Comparative Broadcast Proceedings-Emergency 
Request for Immediate Declaratory Ruling. FCC 96-179 released April 
26, 1996.
Filed By: Gene A. Bechtel, Bechtel & Cole, Chartered on May 28, 
1996.
