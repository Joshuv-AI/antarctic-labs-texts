SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Atlanta District Advisory Council Meeting; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Administration Atlanta District Advisory 
Council will hold a public meeting on Thursday, November 9, 1995 from 
8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and Friday, November 10, 1995 from 8:30 a.m. to 
12:00 noon at the Best Western Motel, Tifton, GA, to discuss matters as 
may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small Business 
Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ms. Laura A. Brown, Acting 
District Director, U.S. Small Business Administration, 1720 Peachtree 
Road, NE, Suite 600, Atlanta, GA 30309 (404) 347-4147, Ext. 46.

    Dated: September 28, 1995.
Art DeCoursey,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
