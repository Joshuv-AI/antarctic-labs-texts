DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 4256]


In the Matter of the Designation of Lashkar i Jhangvi as a 
Foreign Terrorist Organization Pursuant to Section 219 of the 
Immigration and Nationality Act

    Based upon a review of the Administrative Record assembled in this 
matter and in consultation with the Attorney General and the Secretary 
of the Treasury, I conclude that there is a sufficient factual basis to 
find that the relevant circumstances described in section 219 of the 
Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended (hereinafter ``INA''), 
exist with respect to Lashkar i Jhangvi. Therefore, I intend to 
designate that organization as a foreign terrorist organization 
pursuant to section 219(a) of the INA.
    This designation shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: January 21, 2003.
Colin L. Powell,
Secretary of State, Department of State.
