Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Sinclair Oil Corporation v. Platte Pipe Line Company; Notice of 
Complaint

March 4, 1999.
    Take notice that on February 26, 1999, Sinclair Oil Corporation 
(Sinclair) tendered for filing a complaint against Platte Pipe Line 
Company (Platte).
    Sinclair states that it has tendered to Platte for shipment 
unadulterated crude oil and has received from Platte contaminated crude 
oil of considerably lesser value.
    Sinclair also alleges that Platte has failed to specify in its 
tariff the terms and conditions of shipment and has instead reserved 
these matters for the exercise of its ``sole discretion''.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said complaint should 
file a motion to intervene or a protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 214 and 211 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure 18 CFR 385.214, 385.211. All such motions or 
protests should be filed on or before March 29, 1999. Protests will be 
considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate action to 
be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to the 
proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion to 
intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection. This filing may be viewed on the 
web at http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/rims.htm (call 202-208-2222 for 
assistance). Answers to this complaint shall be due on or before March 
29, 1999.
David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
