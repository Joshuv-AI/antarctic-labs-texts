SUMMARY: This document contains a correction to the final rule to 
implement Amendment 31 to the Fishery Management Plan for the Reef Fish 
Resources of the Gulf of Mexico that was published in the Federal 
Register Monday, April 26, 2010.

DATES: This correction is effective May 26, 2010.
