Gateway Country Store, Whitehall Mall, Whitehall, PA; Dismissal 
of Application for Reconsideration

    Pursuant to 29 CFR 90.18(C) an application for administrative 
reconsideration was filed with the Director of the Division of Trade 
Adjustment Assistance for workers at Gateway Country Store, Whitehall 
Mall, Whitehall, Pennsylvania. The application contained no new 
substantial information which would bear importantly on the 
Department's determination. Therefore, dismissal of the application was 
issued.

TA-W-55,333; Gateway Country Store, Whitehall Mall Whitehall Mall, 
Pennsylvania (September 16, 2004)

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 17th day of September 2004.
Timothy Sullivan,
