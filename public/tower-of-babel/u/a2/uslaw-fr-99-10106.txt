Notice of Meeting of John Day--Snake Resource Advisory Council

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Prineville District Office.

ACTION: Meeting of John Day--Snake Resource Advisory Council: John Day, 
Oregon; June 3 & 4, 1999.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: A meeting of the John Day--Snake Resource Advisory Council 
will be held on June 3rd from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and on June 4th 
from 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the U.S. Forest Service Offices, 431 
Paterson Bridge Road, John Day, Oregon 97845. The meeting is open to 
the public. Public comments will be received at 10:00 a.m. on June 4th. 
Topics to be discussed by the Council will include: A field trip on 
June 3rd to the Summit Fire to look at forest health problems in the 
region; John Day River Plan update; program of work for 1999; 
presentation of weed management; report on Resource Emphasis and Action 
Priorities (REAP); and ICBEMP update. Transportation for the field trip 
will not be provided to the public.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
James L. Hancock, Bureau of Land Management, Prineville District 
Office, 3050 NE Third Street, P.O. Box 550, Prineville, Oregon 97754, 
or call (541) 416-6700.

    Dated: April 13, 1999.
James L. Hancock,
District Manager.
