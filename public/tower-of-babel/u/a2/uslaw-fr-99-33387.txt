NATIONAL CREDIT UNION ADMINISTRATION


Sunshine Act Meeting; Notice of Change in Subject of Meeting

    The National Credit Union Administration Board determined that its 
business required the addition of the following item to the previously 
announced closed meeting (Federal Register, Vol. 64, No. 239, page 
69802, Tuesday, December 14, 1999) scheduled for Thursday, December 16, 
1999.

    5. Delegation of Authority. Closed pursuant to exemptions (2), 
(6) and (7).

    The Board voted unanimously that agency business required that this 
item be considered with less than the usual seven days notice, that it 
be closed to the public, and that no earlier announcement of this 
change was possible.
    The previously announced items were:

    1. Field of Membership Appeal. Closed pursuant to exemption (8).
    2. Insurance Appeal. Closed pursuant to exemptions (6) and (8).
    3. Proposed Modification to NCUA's Indemnification Policy. 
Closed pursuant to exemptions (2), (6) and (7).
    4. Two (2) Personnel Matters. Closed pursuant to exemptions (2), 
(5), (6) and (7).
