DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 6244]


Determination Under Section 699C of the Department of State, 
Foreign Operations, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2008, 
Relating to Assistance to the Democratic Republic of Congo

    Pursuant to the authority vested in me by Section 699C(c) of the 
Department of State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs 
Appropriations Act, 2008 (Div. J, P.I. 110-161), I hereby determine 
that waiver of the application of the restriction in Section 699C(a) to 
the Democratic Republic of Congo is important to the national interest 
of the United States and thereby waive this restriction with respect to 
that country.
    This determination shall be reported to Congress and published in 
the Federal Register.

    Dated: May 9, 2008.
Condoleezza Rice,
Secretary of State, Department of State.
