NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION


Biological Science Advisory Committee; Notice of Meeting: 
Correction

Federal Register on April 2, 2012, a notice of an open meeting for the 
Biological Sciences Advisory Committee, 1110. This notice is 
to correct the year of the meeting date.

Correction

    On page 19740, column 2, under Date and Time, please replace 
``April 26, 2011; 1 p.m. to 3 p.m.'' with ``April 26, 2012; 1 p.m. to 3 
p.m.''
