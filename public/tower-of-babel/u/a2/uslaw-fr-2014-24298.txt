DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL14-98-000]


Arizona Public Service Company; Notice of Institution of Section 
206 Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On October 3, 2014, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL14-98-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e (2012), instituting an investigation to determine whether 
market-based rate authority for Arizona Public Service Company (APS) in 
the APS balancing authority area and the Phoenix Valley Load Pocket is 
just and reasonable. Arizona Public Service Company, 149 FERC ] 61,013 
(2014).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL14-98-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.

    Dated: October 3, 2014.
 Kimberly D. Bose,
 Secretary.
