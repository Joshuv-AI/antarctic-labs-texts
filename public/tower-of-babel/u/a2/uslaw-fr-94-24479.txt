DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

[Treasury Order Number 102-16]

 

Delegation of Authority to the Assistant Secretary (Management) 
to Accept Gifts and Bequests to the Department of the Treasury; 
Delegation

    By virtue of the authority vested in the Secretary of the Treasury 
by 31 U.S.C. 321(b), I hereby delegate all authority granted to the 
Secretary under 31 U.S.C. 321(d) to the Assistant Secretary 
(Management)/Chief Financial Officer to accept, hold, administer and 
use gifts and bequests of property, both real and personal, for the 
purpose of aiding or facilitating the work of the Department of the 
Treasury. Gifts and bequests of money and proceeds from sales of other 
property received as gifts and bequests shall be deposited in the 
Treasury in a separate fund and shall be disbursed on order of the 
Assistant Secretary (Management). The Assistant Secretary (Management)/
Chief Financial Officer may redelegate or assign any authority 
delegated by this Order.

    Dated: September 28, 1994
Lloyd Bentsen,
Secretary of the Treasury.
