DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. RP00-372-001]


ANR Pipeline Company; Notice of Compliance Filing

October 25, 2000.
    Take notice that on October 19, 2000, ANR Pipeline Company (ANR) 
tendered for filing in compliance with the Commission's order dated 
September 29, 2000 at Docket No. RP00-372-000, a revised allocation of 
interest costs.
    On June 30, 2000 ANR Pipeline Company (ANR) filed an Interest 
Recovery Plan to direct bill certain shippers for interest charges paid 
by ANR to Great Lakes Gas Transmission L.P. (Great Lakes). The order 
required ANR to allocate, but not direct bill, costs to all discount 
shippers, unless such discount shippers are subject to additional 
charges.
    Any person desiring to protest said filing should file a protest 
with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, NE., 
Washington, DC 20426, in accordance with Section 385.211 of the 
Commission's Rules and Regulations. All such protests must be filed on 
or before November 1, 2000. Protests will be considered by the 
Commission in determining the appropriate action to be taken, but will 
not be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceedings. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission 
and are available for public inspection in the Public Reference Room. 
This filing may be viewed on the web at http://www.ferc.fed.us/online/rims.htm (call 202-208-2222 for assistance). Beginning November 1, 
2000, comments and protests may be filed electronically via the 
internet in lieu of paper. See 18 CFR 385.2001(a)(1)(iii) and the 
instructions on the Commission's web site at
http://www.ferc.fed.us/efi/doorbell.htm.

David P. Boergers,
Secretary.
