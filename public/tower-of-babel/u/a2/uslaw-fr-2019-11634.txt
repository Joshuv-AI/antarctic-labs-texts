Federal Railroad Administration

Petition for Waiver of Compliance

    Under part 211 of Title 49 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR), this 
document provides the public notice that on February 13, 2019, the City 
of Fargo, North Dakota (City), petitioned the Federal Railroad 
Administration (FRA) for a waiver of compliance from certain provisions 
of the Federal railroad safety regulations contained at 49 CFR part 
222. FRA assigned the petition Docket Number FRA-2019-0040.
    Specifically, the City seeks a waiver from the provisions of 49 CFR 
222.35(b)(1) so that a new quiet zone consisting of two public highway-
rail grade crossings (7th Avenue North & 16th Street North) may be 
established, without constant warning time (CWT) devices on one of the 
yard tracks. The City asserts that the 16th Street North crossing 
consists of two main line tracks and two yard tracks. The 16th Street 
crossing is equipped with flashing lights and gates, and CWT devices on 
the two main line tracks and the north yard track; however, the south 
yard track does not have CWT devices. The south yard track uses a 
battery relay, which will trigger the crossing active lights/gates when 
equipment is within limits of the crossing.
