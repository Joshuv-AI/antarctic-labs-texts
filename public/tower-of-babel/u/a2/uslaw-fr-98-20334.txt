GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Fleet Management Division; Cancellation of Standard Forms

AGENCY: Federal Supply Service, General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces the General Services Administration's 
intent to cancel the following Standard forms:
    SF 149, U.S. Government National Credit Card, and SF 149A, U.S. 
Government Fleet Credit Card.
    Both of these forms were replaced with a bank credit card.

DATES: Effective July 30, 1998.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Mr. William Webster, Environmental and Legislation Branch (703) 305-
6276. This contact is for information on the new fleet services credit 
card only.

    Dated: July 20, 1998.
Barbara M. Williams,
Deputy Standard and Optional Forms Management Officer.
