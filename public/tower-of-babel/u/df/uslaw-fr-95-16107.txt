FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM

Fredric R. LeVarge, et al.; Change in Bank Control Notices; 
Acquisitions of Shares of Banks or Bank Holding Companies; Correction

    This notice corrects a notice (FR Doc. 95-15412) published on page 
32680 of the issue for Friday, June 23, 1995.
    Under the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta heading, the entry for 
Fredric R. LeVarge, is revised to read as follows:
    1. Fredric R. and Patricia M. LeVarge, both of Tampa, Florida; to 
acquire an additional 10.2 percent, for a total of 19.5 percent of the 
voting shares of City Financial Corporation of Tampa, Tampa, Florida, 
and thereby indirectly acquire City First Bank, Tampa, Florida.
    Comments on this application must be received by July 7, 1995.
