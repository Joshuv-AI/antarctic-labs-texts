CORPORATION FOR NATIONAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICE

TIME AND DATE: February 24, 1995, 10:00 am-4:00 pm.

Place: The Corporation For National Service, 8th Floor Conference Room, 
1201 New York Ave., N.W., Washington, D.C. 20525.

status: The meeting will be open to the public up to the seating 
capacity of the room.

matters to be considered: The Board of Directors of the Corporation for 
National and Community Service will meet on February 24, 1995, to 
review reports from Committees of the Board of Directors on Corporation 
activities, review the report from the Chief Executive Officer, and to 
review the status of various Corporation initiatives. There will be a 
period devoted to public comment at the end of the meeting.

contact person for further information: Rhonda Taylor, Associate 
Director of Special Projects and Initiatives, The Corporation For 
National Service, 8th Floor, Room 8619, 1201 New York Avenue, N.W., 
Washington, D.C. 20525. Phone (202) 606-5000 ext. 282. Fax (202) 565-
2794.

    Dated: February 13, 1995.
Terry Russell,
General Counsel.
