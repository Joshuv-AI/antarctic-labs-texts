DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Docket No. EL05-87-000]


Tucson Electric Power Company; Notice of Institution of 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

April 19, 2005.
    On April 14, 2005, the Commission issued an order initiating a 
proceeding in Docket No. EL05-87-000 under section 206 of the Federal 
Power Act concerning the justness and reasonableness of Tucson Electric 
Power Company's market-based rates in the Tucson control area. Tucson 
Electric Power Company 111 FERC ]61,037 (2005).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL05-87-000, established 
pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act, will be 60 days 
following publication of this notice in the Federal Register.

Magalie R. Salas,
Secretary.
