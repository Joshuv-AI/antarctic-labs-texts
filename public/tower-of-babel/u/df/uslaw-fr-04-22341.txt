DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Oregon Coast Provincial Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Oregon Coast Province Advisory Committee will meet in 
Corvallis, OR, October 14, 2004. The theme of the meeting is 
Introduction/Overview/Business Planning. The agenda includes: PAC 
Subcommittee Updates, RiverPrize Update, Special Forest Products, 2005 
Meeting Theme/Agenda/Dates, Public Comment and Round Robin.

DATES: The meeting will be held October 14, 2004, beginning at 9 a.m.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be held at the Siuslaw National Forest 
Headquarters, 4077 SW. Research Way, Corvallis, Oregon, 97333.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Joni Quarnstrom, Public Affairs 
Specialist, Siuslaw National Forest, 541-750-7075, or write to Siuslaw 
National Forest Supervisor, P.O. Box 1148, Corvallis, OR 97339.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting is open to the public. Council 
Discussion is limited to Forest Service/BLM staff and Council Members. 
Lunch will be on your own. A public input session will be at 2 p.m. for 
fifteen minutes. The meeting is expected to adjourn around 3 p.m.

    Dated: September 29, 2004.
Michael A. Harvey,
Assistant Recreational Staff.
