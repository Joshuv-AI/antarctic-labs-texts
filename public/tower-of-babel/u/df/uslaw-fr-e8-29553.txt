FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION


Notice of Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that at 10:30 a.m. on 
Tuesday, December 16, 2008, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation's 
Board of Directors will meet in closed session, pursuant to section 
552b(c)(2), (c)(4), (c)(6), (c)(8), (9)(A)(ii), (9)(B), and (10) of 
Title 5, United States Code, to consider matters relating to the 
Corporation's supervisory and corporate activities.
    The meeting will be held in the Board Room on the sixth floor of 
the FDIC

[[Page 75720]]

Building located at 550 17th Street, NW., Washington, DC.
    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Robert E. Feldman, Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-7043.

    Dated: December 9, 2008.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,
Executive Secretary.
