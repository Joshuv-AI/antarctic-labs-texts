SUMMARY: The Department of the Army announces the general availability 
of exclusive, partially exclusive, or nonexclusive licenses under the 
following patents that are listed in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION 
paragraph. Any licenses granted shall comply with 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 
CFR part 404.

Soldier and Biological Chemical Command, Kansas Street, Natick, MA 
01760, Phone; (508) 233-4928 or E-mail:
