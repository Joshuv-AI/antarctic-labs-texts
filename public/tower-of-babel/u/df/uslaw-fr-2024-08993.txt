    May 9, 2023 [verbar] 8:30 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Eastern
North American Electric Reliability Corporation: Project 2023-02 
Analysis and Mitigation of BES Inverter-Based Resource Performance 
Issues Standard Drafting Team Meeting, Hybrid
    May 8, 2023[verbar] 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Eastern
    May 9, 2023 [verbar] 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Eastern
