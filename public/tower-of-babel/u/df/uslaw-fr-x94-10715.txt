    In rule document 94-15510 beginning on page 33388 in the issue of

    1. On page 33397, in the second column, in Sec. 195.246, paragraph 
(b), in the third line, ``3.7 m 12-ft-deep'' should read ``3.7 m (12 
ft) deep''.


Sec. 195.248  [Corrected]

    2. On the same page, in the same column, in amendatory instruction 
18. for Sec. 195.248, in the eighth line, ``12-ft-deep'' should read 
``3.7 m (12 ft) deep''.
