Institute of Education Sciences; English Language Acquisition 
Evaluation Program; Notice of Applicable Program Regulations

    The following Education Department General Administrative 
Regulations apply to this program: 34 CFR parts 74, 77, 80, 81, 82, 85, 
86 (part 86 applies only to Institutions of Higher Education), 97, 98, 
and 99. In addition 34 CFR part 75 is applicable, except for the 
provisions in 34 CFR 75.100, 75.101(b), 75.102, 75.103, 75.105, 
75.109(a), 75.200, 75.201, 75.209, 75.210, 75.217, 75.219 (except that 
the Department may select an application for funding without following 
the applicable procedures if the application was evaluated under the 
preceding competition of the program; the application rated high enough 
to deserve selection; and the application was not selected for funding 
because the application was mishandled by the Department), 75.220 and 
75.230.
