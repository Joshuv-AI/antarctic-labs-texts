FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

[MM Docket No. 99-219, RM-9638]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Choteau, MT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document requests comments on a petition filed by the 
Battani Corporation proposing the allotment of Channel 271C1 at 
Choteau, Montana, as the community's first local service. The channel 
can be allotted to Choteau with a site restriction 29.4 kilometers 
(18.3 miles) south of the community at coordinates 47-33-40 NL and 112-
18-43 WL. Canadian concurrence will be requested for the allotment of 
Channel 271C1 at choteau.

DATES: Comments must be filed on or before August 9, 1999, and reply 
comments on or before August 24, 1999.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, Washington, DC. 20554. In 
addition to filing comments with the FCC, interested parties should 
serve the petitioner's counsel, as follows: Robert Lewis Thompson, 
Taylor Thiemann & Aitken, L.C., 908 King Street, Suite 300, Alexandria, 
VA 22314.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kathleen Scheuerle, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.

of Proposed Rule Making, MM Docket No. 99-219, adopted June 9, 1999,
