DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Intent To Grant an Exclusive License to Foster Miller, Inc.

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with 35 U.S.C. 209(e) and 37 CFR 404.7(a)(l)(i), 
announcement is made of the intent to grant an exclusive, royalty-
bearing, revocable license to U.S. patent application number 09,715,496 
filed November 17, 2000 entitled ``Wearable Transmission Device'' to 
Foster Miller, Inc. with its principal place of business at 350 Second 
Avenue, Waltham, MA 02451.

DATES: File written objections by September 10, 2003.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Robert Rosenkrans at U.S. Army 
Soldier and Biological Chemical Command, Kansas Street, Natick, MA 
01760, Phone; (508) 233-4928 or e-mail: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The prospective partially exclusive licenses 
will be royalty bearing and will comply with the terms and conditions 
of 35 U.S.C. 209 and 37 CFR 404.7. The prospective exclusive licenses 
may be granted, unless within fifteen (15) days from the date of this 
published notice, Soldier and Biological Chemical Command receives 
written evidence and argument to establish that the grant of the 
license would not be consistent with the requirements of 35 U.S.C. 209 
and 37 CFR 404.7.

Luz D. Ortiz,
