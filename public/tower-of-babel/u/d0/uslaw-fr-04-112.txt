Radio Broadcasting Services; Salina, UT

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: At the request of Sierra Grande Broadcasting this document 
allots Channel 233C to Salina, Utah. See 67 FR 47502, published July 
19, 2002. The reference coordinates for the Channel 233C allotment at 
Salina, Utah, are 38-52-24 and 111-51-33. With this action, the 
proceeding is terminated.

DATES: Effective January 29, 2004.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Robert Hayne, Media Bureau (202) 418-
2177.
