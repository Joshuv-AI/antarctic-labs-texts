DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 3309-021]


Marlow Hydro, LLC; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal of 
License Application

    On December 1, 2020, Marlow Hydro, LLC (Marlow Hydro) filed an 
application for a subsequent license for the 225-kilowatt Nash Mill Dam 
Hydroelectric Project No. 3309. On May 5, 2020, Marlow Hydro filed a 
notice of withdrawal of its application.
    No motion in opposition to the notice of withdrawal has been filed, 
and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the withdrawal. 
Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the application became effective on May 
21, 2021, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
