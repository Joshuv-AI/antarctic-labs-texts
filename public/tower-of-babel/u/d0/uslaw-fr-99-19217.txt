DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. RP97-420-000]


Southern Natural Gas Company; Notice of Report Following 
Technical Conference

July 22, 1999.
    Take notice that on June 30, 1999, in compliance with the schedule 
established at the April 15, 1999 technical conference held in the 
above-
referenced proceeding, Southern Natural Gas Company (Southern) tendered 
for filing the results of its working sessions with shippers regarding 
the use of certain operational flow orders (OFOs) on Southern's system.
    Reply comments on Southern's June 30, 1999 filing are due on or 
before August 2, 1999.
Linwood A. Watson, Jr.,
Acting Secretary.
