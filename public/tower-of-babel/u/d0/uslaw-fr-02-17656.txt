DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Center for 
Scientific Review Special Emphasis Panel, July 10, 2002, 8:30 a.m. to 
July 10, 2002, 5 p.m., Monarch Hotel, 2400 M Street, NW., Washington, 
DC 20037 which was published in the Federal Register on June 18, 2002, 
67 FR 41437-41439.
    The meeting has been changed to August 2, 2002. The time and 
location remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: July 3, 2002.
LaVerne Y. Stringfield,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
