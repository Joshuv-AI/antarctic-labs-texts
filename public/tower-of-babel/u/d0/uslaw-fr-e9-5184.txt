DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

Employment and Training Administration

[TA-W-65,152]


CCL Container, Hermitage, PA; Notice of Termination of 
Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on February 6, 2009, in response to a 
petition filed by a company official on behalf of workers of CCL 
Container, Hermitage, Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, the investigation has been terminated.


[[Page 10620]]


    Signed at Washington, DC, this 27th day of February 2009.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
