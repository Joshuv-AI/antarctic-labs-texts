FEDERAL ELECTION COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meeting

AGENCY: Federal Election Commission

DATE AND TIME:  Wednesday, April 19, 2017 at 10:00 a.m. and its 
continuation at the conclusion of the open meeting on April 20, 2017.

PLACE: 999 E Street NW., Washington, DC.

STATUS: This meeting will be closed to the public.

ITEMS TO BE DISCUSSED: 
Compliance matters pursuant to 52 U.S.C. 30109.
Information the premature disclosure of which would be likely to have a 
considerable adverse effect on the implementation of a proposed 
Commission action.
Matters concerning participation in civil actions or proceedings or 
arbitration.
