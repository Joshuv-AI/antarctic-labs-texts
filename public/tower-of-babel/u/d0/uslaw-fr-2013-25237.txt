DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, October 17, 2013, 05:00 p.m. 
to October 18, 2013, 06:00 p.m., Bethesda North Marriott Hotel & 
Conference Center, 5701 Marinelli Road, Bethesda, MD, 20852 which was 
published in the Federal Register on September 23, 2013, 78FR58323.
    Due to the absence of either an FY 2014 appropriation or Continuing 
Resolution for the Department of Health and Human Services, the SEP 
meeting is rescheduled for November 12-13, 2013. The meeting times and 
location remain the same. The meeting is closed to the public.

    Dated: October 22, 2013.
Melanie J. Gray,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
