Revision to Transmission Vegetation Management Reliability 
Standard; Notice of Compliance Filing

    Take notice that on July 12, 2013, the North American Electric 
Reliability Corporation (NERC), pursuant to Order No. 777 \1\ submitted 
a compliance filing: (1) Providing a description of NERC's plan to 
conduct testing to develop empirical data regarding the flashover 
distances between conductors and vegetation; (2) modifying the 
Violation Risk Factor for Requirement R2; and (3) confirming NERC has 
posted guidance materials for NERC Reliability Standard FAC-003-2 to 
its Web site.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Revisions to Reliability Standard for Transmission 
Vegetation Management, Order No. 777, 142 FERC ] 61,208 (2013).
