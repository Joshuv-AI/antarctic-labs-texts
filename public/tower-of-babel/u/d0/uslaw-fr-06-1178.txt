DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES


Office of the National Coordinator; American Health Information 
Community Electronic Health Record Workgroup Meeting

ACTION:  Announcement of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  This notice announces the second meeting of the American 
Health Information Community Electronic Health Record Workgroup in 
accordance with the Federal Advisory Committee Act (Pub. L. 92-463, 5 
U.S.C., App.).

DATES:  February 22, 2006 from 1 p.m. to 5 p.m.

ADDRESSES:  Hubert H. Humphrey Building (200 Independence Ave., SW., 
Washington, DC 20201), conference room 425A.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  http://www.hhs.gov/healthit.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:  A Web address for the meeting will be 
available at: http://www.hhs.gov/healthit.
