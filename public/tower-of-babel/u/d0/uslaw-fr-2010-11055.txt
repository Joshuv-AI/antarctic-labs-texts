DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Center for Nutrition Policy and Promotion


Agency Information Collection Activities; Current Collection: 
Comment Request--Innovations for Healthy Kids Challenge To Promote the 
Open Government Initiative; Correction

AGENCY: Center for Nutrition Policy and Promotion, USDA.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of Agriculture, Center for Nutrition Policy and 
Promotion published a document in the Federal Register on April 28, 
2010, concerning requests for comments on the Innovations for Healthy 
Kids Challenge to Promote the Open Government Initiative. The document 
contained an incorrect Web address.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Donna Johnson-Bailey at 703-305-3300.

Correction

    In the Federal Register of April 28, 2010, in FR/Vol. 75, No. 81 on 
page 22357, the third column, correct the Web site to read: http://www.appsforhealthykids.com/.

    Dated: May 4, 2010.
Robert Post,
Acting Executive Director, Center for Nutrition Policy and Promotion.
