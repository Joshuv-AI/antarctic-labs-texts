DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with Section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 12 & 13 December 1995.
    Time of Meeting: 0900-1700, 12 & 13 December 1995.
    Place: Pentagon--Washington, DC.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board (ASB) C4I Ad Hoc Study Group will 
meet to hear selected briefings relative to the study on ``Army 
Digitization Information Systems Vulnerabilities and Security.'' 
These meetings will be open to the public. Any interested person may 
attend, appear before, or file statements with the committee at the 
time and in the manner permitted by the committee. For further 
information, please contact Michelle Diaz at (703) 695-0781.
Michelle P. Diaz,
Acting Administrative Officer, Army Science Board.
