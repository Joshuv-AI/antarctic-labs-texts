SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Investment Advisory Council; Public Meeting

    The U.S. Small Business Investment Advisory Council will hold a 
public meeting from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Thursday, May 11, 1995, at the 
ANA Hotel, located at 2900 M Street, NW, Washington, DC, to discuss 
such matters as may be presented by members, staff of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration, or others present.
    For further information, write or call Ed Cleveland, U.S. Small 
Business Administration, 409 Third Street, SW, Washington, DC 20416, 
(202) 205-6510.

    Dated: April 27, 1995.
Dorothy A. Overal,
Director, Office of Advisory Council.
