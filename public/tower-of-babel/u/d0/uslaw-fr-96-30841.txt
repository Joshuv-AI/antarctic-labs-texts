DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Comprehensive Management Plans for the Wild and Scenic Rivers on 
the Ozark National Forest

AGENCY: Forest service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of the availability of comprehensive management plans 
for Wild and Scenic Rivers.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Forest Service, USDA has prepared comprehensive management 
plans for the following designated Wild and Scenic Rivers located on 
the Ozark National Forest in Arkansas: Big Piney Creek, Buffalo, 
Hurricane Creek, Mulberry, North Sylamore Creek, and Richland Creek. 
These plans may be reviewed at the USDA Forest Service, Southern 
Regional Office, 1720 Peachtree Road, NW, Atlanta, Georgia and the 
Ozark-St. Francis National Forests, 605 West Main, Russellville, 
Arkansas. Information may be obtained by contacting Richard Bowie, 
Ozark-St. Francis National Forests, 605 West Main, Box 1008, 
Russellville, AR 72811-1008, (501) 968-2354.

    Dated: November 26, 1996.
R. Gary Pierson,
Acting Deputy Regional Forester for Natural Resources.
