DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
[Docket No. EC98-6-000]


American Ref-fuel Company of Essex County, Air Products Ref-fuel 
of Essex County, Inc., Duke/UAE Ref-Fuel LLC, and Duke/UAE Essex LLC; 
Notice of Filing

November 10, 1997.
    Take notice that American Ref-fuel Company of Essex County, Air 
Products Ref-fuel of Essex County, Inc., Duke/UAE Ref-Fuel LLC, and 
Duke/UAE Essex LLC, on November 5, 1997, tendered for filing, at the 
request of Commission staff, a Purchase Agreement reflecting the 
disposition for which Commission approval is sought in this docket.
    Any person desiring to be heard or to protest said filing should 
file a motion to intervene or protest with the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20426, 
in accordance with Rules 211 and 214 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure (18 CFR 385.211 and 385.214). All such motions 
or protests should be filed on or before November 17, 1997. Protests 
will be considered by the Commission in determining the appropriate 
action to be taken, but will not serve to make protestants parties to 
the proceeding. Any person wishing to become a party must file a motion 
to intervene. Copies of this filing are on file with the Commission and 
are available for public inspection.
Lois D. Cashell,
Secretary.
