Employment and Training Administration

Teleflex Automotive Group, Warren, Michigan; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, an 
investigation was initiated on October 28, 2003 in response to a 
petition filed on behalf of workers at Teleflex Automotive Group, 
Warren, Michigan. The workers produced automotive cable assembly 
components.
    All workers were separated from the subject firm more than one year 
before the date of the petition. Section 223 (b) of the Act specifies 
that no certification may apply to any worker whose last separation 
occurred more than one year before the date of the petition. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC, this 13th day of November 2003.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
