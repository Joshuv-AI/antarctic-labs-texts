DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2341-033]


Georgia Power Company; Notice of Comment Period Extension

    On January 24, 2019, the Commission issued a notice through the 
FERC eLibrary system \1\ setting February 25, 2019, as the end of the 
formal period to file comments, motions to intervene, or protests on 
the application for surrender of license for Georgia Power Company's 
Langdale Hydroelectric Project No. 2341, located on the Chattahoochee 
River in the City of Valley, Chambers County, Alabama and Harris 
County, Georgia. Due to the funding lapse at certain federal agencies 
between December 22, 2018 and January 25, 2019, the Commission is 
extending the comment period until March 4, 2019.
