Ideal Federal Savings Bank, Baltimore, MD; Approval of Conversion 
Application

    Notice is hereby given that on May 24, 2010, the Office of Thrift 
Supervision approved the application of Ideal Federal Savings Bank, 
Baltimore, Maryland, to convert to the stock form of organization. 
Copies of the application are available for inspection by appointment 
(phone number: (202) 906-5922 or e-mail: pub1ic.info@ots.treas.gov">pub1ic.info@ots.treas.gov) at 
the Public Reading Room, 1700 G Street, NW., Washington, DC 20552, and 
the OTS Southeast Regional Office, 1475 Peachtree Street, NE., Atlanta, 
Georgia 30309.

    Dated: May 26, 2010.

    By the Office of Thrift Supervision.
Sandra E. Evans,
Federal Register Liaison.
