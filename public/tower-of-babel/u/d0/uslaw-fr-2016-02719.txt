    Abstract of proposed collection:
    The DS-2029, Application for Consular Report of Birth Abroad of a 
Citizen of the United States of America, is used by citizens of the 
United States to report the birth of their children born abroad. The 
information collected on this form will be used to determine whether 
the child born abroad to a U.S. citizen parent or parents acquired U.S. 
citizenship at birth. 22 CFR 50.5-50.7 are important legal authorities 
that permit the Department to use this form.
    Methodology:
    An application for a Consular Report of Birth is normally made in 
the consular district in which the birth occurred. The parent 
respondents will complete the form and present it to a U.S. Embassy or 
Consulate, where a consular officer will examine the documentation and 
enter the information provided into the Department of State American 
Citizen Services (ACS) electronic database.
