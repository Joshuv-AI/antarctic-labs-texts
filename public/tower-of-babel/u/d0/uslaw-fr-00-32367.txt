DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION


Web-Based Education Commission: Postponement of Press Conference

AGENCY: Office of Postsecondary Education, Education.

SUMMARY: The Commission published a notice in the Federal Register on 
December 11, 2000 (65 FR 77350), announcing a press conference to be 
held on December 14, 2000 at 9:30 a.m. Due to a scheduling conflict, 
the press conference has been rescheduled.

DATES: The press conference will be held on December 19, 2000 at 1 p.m. 
It will be held at the National Press Club, 529 14th St., NW., in 
Washington, DC, in the Hollerman Lounge.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: David Byer, Executive Director, Web-
based Education Commission, U.S. Department of Education, 1990 K 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20006-8533. Telephone: (202) 219-7045. Fax: 
(202) 502-7675. Email: [email protected].
