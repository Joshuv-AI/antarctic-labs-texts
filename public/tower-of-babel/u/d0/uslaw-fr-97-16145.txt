DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

Forest Service


Deschutes Provincial Interagency Executive Committee (PIEC), 
Advisory Committee

AGENCY: Forest Service, USDA.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Deschutes PIEC Advisory Committee will meet on July 21 & 
22, 1997 at The Best Western Inn in Prineville, OR on Highway 26. The 
meeting will start at 9:00 am. and finish at 5:00 pm. both days. Agenda 
items include: (1) Update on rangelands, (2) Review of DEIS documents 
for the Eastside Ecosystem project, (3) Reports from subcommittees, and 
(4) Open public forum. All Deschutes Province Advisory Committee 
meetings are open to the public.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Pam Beyer, Province Liaison, USDA, Fort Rock Ranger District, 1230 N.E. 
34d, Bend, Oregon 97701, 541-383-4705.

    Dated: June 10, 1997.
Sally Collins,
Deschutes National Forest Supervisor.
