DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Army Science Board; Notice of Open Meeting

    In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (P.L. 92-463), announcement is made of the following 
Committee Meeting:

    Name of Committee: Army Science Board (ASB).
    Date of Meeting: 2-3 April 2001.
    Time of Meeting: 0830-1500.
    Place of Meeting: Fort Belvoir, Virginia.
    Agenda: The Army Science Board's (ASB) Special Summer Study on 
``Manpower and Personnel for the Soldier Systems in the Objective 
Force'' will have their 2nd meeting at Fort Belvoir. Will have 
backbriefs concerning the March 13 Plenary Meeting, Manpower 
Requirements, and Joint Warfighter, to name a few: These meetings 
will be open to the public. Any interested person may attend, appear 
before, or file statements with the committee at the time and in the 
manner permitted by the committee. For further information, please 
contact, MAJ Joseph Jones on (703) 614-6020.

Wayne Joyner,
Program Support Specialist, Army Science Board.
