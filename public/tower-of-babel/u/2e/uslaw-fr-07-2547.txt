DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Center for Scientific Review; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Enabling 
Bioanalytical and Biophysical Technologies Study Section, June 11, 
2007, 8:30 a.m. to June 12, 2007, 5 p.m. The River Inn, 924 25th 
Street, NW., Washington, DC 20037 which was published in the Federal 
Register on April 20, 2007, 72 FR 19941-19942.
    The meeting will be held one day only June 11, 2007, 8:30 a.m. to 6 
p.m. The meeting location remains the same. The meeting is closed to 
the public.

    Dated: May 16, 2007.
Jennifer Spaeth,
Director, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
