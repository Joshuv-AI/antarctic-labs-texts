FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION

Notice of Matters To Be Moved From the Discussion Agenda to the Summary 
Agenda for Consideration at an Agency Meeting

    Pursuant to the provisions of the ``Government in the Sunshine 
Act'' (5 U.S.C. 552b), notice is hereby given that the following 
matters will be moved from the ``discussion agenda'' to the ``summary 
agenda'' for consideration at the open meeting of the Board of 
Directors of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation scheduled to be 
held at 10:00 a.m. on Monday, October 30, 1995, in the Board Room on 
the sixth floor of the FDIC Building located at 550 17th Street, N.W., 
Washington, D.C.:

Memorandum re: Corporation's September 30, 1995 Financial Statements
Memorandum re: Quarterly Budget Variance Summary Report

    Requests for further information concerning the meeting may be 
directed to Mr. Jerry L. Langley, Executive Secretary of the 
Corporation, at (202) 898-6757.

    Dated: October 26, 1995.

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
Robert E. Feldman,

Deputy Executive Secretary.
