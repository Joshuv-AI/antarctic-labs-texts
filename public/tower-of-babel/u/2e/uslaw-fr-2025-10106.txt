Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.; Notice of Motion 
for Extension of Effective Date, Motion for Shortened Response Period, 
and Request for Expedited Action

    On May 23, 2025, pursuant to Rules 212 and 2008 of the Commission's 
Rules of Practice and Procedure,\1\ the Midcontinent Independent System 
Operator, Inc. (MISO) filed a motion for extension of the requested 
effective date of MISO's proposed revisions of its Open Access 
Transmission, Energy and Operating Reserve Markets Tariff and pro forma 
agreements submitted in compliance of Order Nos. 2023 and 2023-A,\2\ a 
motion for a shorter answer period, and a request for expedited action 
on its extension motion. MISO requests that the Commission extend 
MISO's originally requested effective date from June 1, 2025 to 30 days 
after the date on which the Commission accepts MISO's compliance filing 
in the above referenced docket.
