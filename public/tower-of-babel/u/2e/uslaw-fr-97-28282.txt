DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Armed Forces Epidemiological Board (AFEB)

AGENCY: Office of The Surgeon General.

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with section 10(a)(2) of Public Law 92-463, The 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, this announces the forthcoming AFEB 
Meeting. The meeting will be held from 0800-1630, Thursday and Friday, 
December 11-12, 1997. The purpose of the meeting is to address pending 
Board issues, provide briefings for Board members on topics related to 
ongoing and new Board issues, and to conduct an executive working 
session. The meeting location will be at the Walter Reed Army Institute 
Research (WRAIR), Washington, D.C. The meeting will be open to the 
public, but limited by space accommodations. Any interested person may 
attend, appear before or file statements with the committee at the time 
and in the manner permitted by the committee.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
COL Vicky Fogelman, AFEB Executive Secretary, Army Forces 
Epidemiological Board, Skyline Six, 5109 Leesburg Pike, Room 693, Falls 
Church, Virginia 22041-3258, (703) 681-8012/4.
