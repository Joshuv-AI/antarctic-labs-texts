GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION


Federal Acquisition Policy Division, FAR Secretariat; 
Cancellation of an Optional Form

AGENCY: General Services Administration.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Acquisition Regulations eliminated the need for 
Optional Form 333, Procurement Integrity Certification For Procurement 
Officials removing the regulations that required its use. Therefore, OF 
333 is cancelled. This deleted requirement was effective January 1, 
1997.
