SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    This is a request to extend the currently approved information 
collection. This information is collected from all testing or 
calibration laboratories that apply for NVLAP accreditation. Applicants 
provide information, such as name, address, phone and fax numbers, 
contact person(s), and select the test methods or parameters for which 
the laboratory is seeking accreditation. The application must be signed 
by the authorized representative of the laboratory, who commits the 
laboratory to comply with NVLAP's accreditation requirements. The 
information is necessary to evaluate the competency of laboratories to 
carry out specific tests or calibrations or types of tests or 
calibrations. The information collection is mandated by 15 CFR part 
285.

II. Method of Collection

    An application for accreditation is provided to each new or renewal 
applicant laboratory and can be submitted to NVLAP either 
electronically or by mail.
