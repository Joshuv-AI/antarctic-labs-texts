Notice of Filing of Plats

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of filing of plats; Colorado.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) Colorado State Office is 
publishing this notice to inform the public of the filing of the land 
survey plats listed below.

DATES: The plats described in this notice were filed on April 13 and 
18, 2012.

ADDRESSES: BLM Colorado State Office, Cadastral Survey, 2850 Youngfield 
Street, Lakewood, Colorado 80215-7093.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Randy Bloom, Chief Cadastral Surveyor 
for Colorado, (303) 239-3856.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The supplemental plat of Section 1, in 
Township 13 South, Range 92 West, Sixth Principal Meridian, Colorado, 
was accepted and filed on April 13, 2012.
    The supplemental plat of Section 31, in Township 5 North, Range 86 
West, Sixth Principal Meridian, Colorado, was accepted and filed on 
April 18, 2012.
    The supplemental plat in Township 5 North, Range 87 West, Sixth 
Principal Meridian, Colorado, was accepted and filed on April 18, 2012.

Randy Bloom,
Chief Cadastral Surveyor for Colorado.
