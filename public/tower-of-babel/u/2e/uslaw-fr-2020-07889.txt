DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Institute Special Emphasis Panel, May 20, 2020, 8:00 a.m. to May 
21, 2020, 2:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute Shady Grove, Shady 
Grove, 9609 Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD, 20850 which was 
published in the Federal Register on March 31, 2020, 85 FR 17896 .
    This notice is being amended to change the meeting start time from 
8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. The teleconference meeting will now be held from 
May 20, 2020, 9:00 a.m. to May 21, 2020, 2:00 p.m. The meeting is 
closed to the public.

    Dated: April 9, 2020.
Melanie J. Pantoja,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
