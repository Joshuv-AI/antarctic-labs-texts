Contract Reporting Requirements of Intrastate Natural Gas 
Companies; Notice of Availability of Form 549D Quarterly Reports for Q1 
and Q2 2011

    The Commission is making available to the public the Form No. 
549D--Quarterly Transportation & Storage Reports for Intrastate Natural 
Gas and Hinshaw Pipelines for the first and second quarters of 2011. 
The data are located on the Commission's Web site at http://eformspublic.ferc.gov/.
    The public may search and download the entire database for all 
quarters or specific quarters or for specific companies. As future 
quarterly reports are filed with the Commission, they will also be 
available on the Commission's Web site after the deadline for filing.
    Any questions or comments regarding the data should be sent to the 
[email protected] mail box.

    Dated: November 8, 2011.
Kimberly D. Bose,
Secretary.
