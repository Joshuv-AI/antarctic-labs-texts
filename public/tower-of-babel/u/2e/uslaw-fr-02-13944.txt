U.S. Steel Corp., Clairton Works, Clairton, Pennsylvania; Notice 
of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 25, 2002 in response to a petition, which was 
filed by United Steelworkers of America, Local 1557, on behalf of 
workers at Clairton Works, U.S. Steel Corporation, Clairton, 
Pennsylvania.
    The petitioner has requested that the petition be withdrawn. 
Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve no 
purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed in Washington, DC, this 15th day of May, 2002.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
