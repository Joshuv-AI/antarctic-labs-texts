General Operating and Flight Rules

CFR Correction

    In Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations, parts 60 to 139, 
revised as of Jan. 1, 1998, make the following corrections:
    1. On page 173, left column, the date in Sec. 9 of the effective 
date note for SFAR No. 50-2 is corrected to read ``January 31, 1999''.
    2. On page 261, in Appendix G to part 91, right column, under 
Section 2. Aircraft Approval, in paragraphs (c) (2)(i), (ii), (3)(i) 
and (ii) the symbol ``#'' is corrected to read ``''.
    3. On page 452, Sec. 121.402, paragraph (a), add the word 
``flight'' between the words ``provide'' and ``training'' in the sixth 
line.
    4. On page 520, Sec. 121.713, paragraph (b)(2), ``Sec. 119.35'' is 
corrected to read ``Sec. 119.36''.
