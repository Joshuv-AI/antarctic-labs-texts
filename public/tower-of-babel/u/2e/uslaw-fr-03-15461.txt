Delco Remy America, Inc. d/b/a Remy Logistic, Anderson, IN; 
Notice of Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on March 17, 2003 in response to a worker petition filed 
on behalf of workers at Remy Logistic, a division of Delco Remy 
America, Inc., Anderson, Indiana.
    The petitioning group of workers is covered by an active 
certification issued on March 24, 2003 and which remains in effect (TA-
W-50,728). Consequently, further investigation in this case would serve 
no purpose, and the investigation has been terminated.

    Signed at Washington, DC this 23rd day of May, 2003.
Elliott S. Kushner,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
