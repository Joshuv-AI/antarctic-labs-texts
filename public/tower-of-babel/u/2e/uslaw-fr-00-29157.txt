Automation Technology Corp., Santa Cruz, CA; Notice of 
Termination of Investigation

    Pursuant to Section 221 of the Trade Act of 1974, an investigation 
was initiated on July 24, 2000, in response to a petition filed by a 
company official on behalf of workers at Automation Technology Corp., 
Santa Cruz, California.
    The company official who filed the original petition has requested 
that the petition be withdrawn. Consequently, further investigation in 
this case would serve no purpose, and the investigation has been 
terminated.

    Signed at Washington, D.C. this 31th day of October 2000.
Linda G. Poole,
Certifying Officer, Division of Trade Adjustment Assistance.
