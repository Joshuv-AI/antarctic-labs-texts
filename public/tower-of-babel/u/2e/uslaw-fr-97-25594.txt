FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION

47 CFR Part 73

[MM Docket No. 96-264; RM-8977]


Radio Broadcasting Services; Roxton, TX

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Proposed rule; denial of petition for rulemaking.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action dismisses a petition for rule making filed by 
Northeast Texas Broadcasters requesting the allotment of Channel 257A 
to Roxton, Texas, because of a short-spacing conflict with a one step 
application filed by OARA for Channel 257C2 at Linden, Texas. See 62 FR 
2639, January 17, 1997. With this action, this proceeding is 
terminated.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Pam Blumenthal, Mass Media Bureau, 
(202) 418-2180.
