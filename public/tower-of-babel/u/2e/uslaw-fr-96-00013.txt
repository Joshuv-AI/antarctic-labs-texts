DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Foreign Agricultural Service


Briefing on Status of Preparations for the World Food Summit

ACTION: Notice of meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that there will be a meeting to inform 
interested parties on the outcome of the January meeting of the Food 
and Agriculture Organization's Committee on World Food Security (CFS). 
The primary item on the agenda for the CFS is review of the draft 
policy statement and plan of action for the World Food Summit. The 
Summit is scheduled to be convened in Rome, Italy, in November, 1996.

DATES: The meeting will be held Wednesday, February 7, 1996 from 2-4 
p.m. at the U.S. Department of Agriculture, Room 3107S South Building, 
14th and Independence Aves., SW., in Washington, DC.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The minutes of the meeting announced in this 
notice shall be available for review. The meeting is open to the public 
and members of the public may provide comments in writing to Buzz 
Guroff, National Secretary for the World Food Summit, Foreign 
Agricultural Service, Room 3008 South Building, U.S. Department of 
Agriculture, 14th and Independence Aves., SW., Washington, DC 20250.

    Signed at Washington, DC, December 26, 1995.
August Schumacher, Jr.,
Administrator, Foreign Agricultural Service.
