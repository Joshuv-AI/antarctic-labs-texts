8 C.F.R. § 241.20 — Proceedings commenced prior to April 1, 1997.
Citation: us:cfr:8:241.20
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-241.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 241 (Apprehension And Detention Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart B of 8 CFR part 241 applies to exclusion proceedings commenced prior to April 1, 1997. All references to the Act contained in this subpart are references to the Act in effect prior to April 1, 1997.
