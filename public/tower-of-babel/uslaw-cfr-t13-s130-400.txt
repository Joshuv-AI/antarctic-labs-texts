13 C.F.R. § 130.400 — Application procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:13:130.400
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-130.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 130 (Small Business Development Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All SBDC applicants must comply with the annual notice of funding opportunity, including format, conditions, submission requirements, and due dates, for their new or renewal application to receive consideration.
