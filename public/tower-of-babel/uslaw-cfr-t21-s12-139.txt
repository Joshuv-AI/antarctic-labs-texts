21 C.F.R. § 12.139 — Reconsideration and stay of action.
Citation: us:cfr:21:12.139
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-12.139
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 12 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following notice or publication of the final decisions, a participant may petition the Commissioner for reconsideration of any part or all of the decision under § 10.33 or may petition for a stay of the decision under § 10.35.
