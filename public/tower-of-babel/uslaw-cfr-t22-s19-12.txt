22 C.F.R. § 19.12 — Employment in a Government agency.
Citation: us:cfr:22:19.12
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-19.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 19 (Benefits For Spouses And Former Spouses Of Participants In The Foreign Service Retirement And Disability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An annuitant who is reemployed by a Federal Government agency may not receive a combination of salary and annuity which exceeds his/her Foreign Service salary at the time of retirement. Refer to § 19.9-4.
