32 C.F.R. § 283.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:283.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-283.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 283 (Waiver Of Debts Resulting From Erroneous Payments Of Pay And Allowances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes policy and assigns responsibilities for considering applications for the waiver of debts resulting from erroneous payments of pay and allowances (including travel and transportation allowances) to or on behalf of members of the Uniformed Services and civilian DoD employees under 10 U.S.C. 2774, 32 U.S.C. 716, 5 U.S.C. 5584.
