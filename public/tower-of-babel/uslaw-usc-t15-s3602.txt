15 U.S.C. § 3602 — Conversion lending
Citation: us:usc:15:3602
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3602
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 62 (Condominium And Cooperative Conversion Protection And Abuse Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that lending by federally insured lending institutions for the conversion of rental housing to condominiums and cooperative housing should be discouraged where there are adverse impacts on housing opportunities of the low- and moderate-income and elderly and handicapped tenants involved.
