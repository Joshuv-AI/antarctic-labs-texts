39 C.F.R. § 211.1 — Disposition of former title 39, U.S.C.
Citation: us:cfr:39:211.1
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-211.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 211 (Application Of Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise continued in effect as postal regulations, all provisions of former title 39, U.S.C., which were continued in effect as regulations of the Postal Service by section 5(f) of the Postal Reorganization Act, are revoked. This revocation does not apply to postal regulations which embody or are derived from provisions of former title 39.
