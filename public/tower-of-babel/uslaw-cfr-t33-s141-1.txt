33 C.F.R. § 141.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 141 (Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes rules governing restrictions on the employment of personnel on units engaged in OCS activities.
