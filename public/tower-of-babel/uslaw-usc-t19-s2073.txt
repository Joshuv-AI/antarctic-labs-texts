19 U.S.C. § 2073 — Transfer of personnel, etc., to Service
Citation: us:usc:19:2073
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

(a)
Repealed. Sept. 3, 1954, ch. 1263, § 10, 68 Stat. 1229.
(b)
The records, property (including office equipment), and personnel of the Division of Customs are transferred to the United States Customs Service.
