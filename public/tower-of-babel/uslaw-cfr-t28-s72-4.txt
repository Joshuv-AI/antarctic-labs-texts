28 C.F.R. § 72.4 — Where sex offenders must register.
Citation: us:cfr:28:72.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-72.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 72 (Sex Offender Registration And Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A sex offender must register, and keep the registration current, in each jurisdiction in which the offender resides, is an employee, or is a student. For initial registration purposes only, a sex offender must also register in the jurisdiction in which convicted if that jurisdiction is different from the jurisdiction of residence.
