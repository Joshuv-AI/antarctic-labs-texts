47 C.F.R. § 76.940 — Prospective rate reduction.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.940
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.940
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A franchising authority may order a cable operator to implement a reduction in basic service tier or associated equipment rates where necessary to bring rates into compliance with the standards set forth in §§ 76.922 and 76.923.
