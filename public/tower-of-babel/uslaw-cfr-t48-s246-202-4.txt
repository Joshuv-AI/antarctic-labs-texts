48 C.F.R. § 246.202-4 — 246.202-4 Higher-level contract quality requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:246.202-4
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-246.202-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 246 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(1) Higher-level contract quality requirements are used in addition to a standard inspection requirement.
(2) Higher-level contract quality requirements, including nongovernment quality system standards adopted to meet DoD needs, are listed in the DoD Index of Specifications and Standards.
