7 C.F.R. § 51.1430 — U.S. Extra Fancy.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 51 (Fresh Fruits, Vegetables, And Other Products (Inspection, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“U.S. Extra Fancy” consists of pecan kernels which meet the following requirements:
(a) For quality:
(1) Well dried;
(2) Moisture content shall not be more than 4.5 percent, unless otherwise specified;
(3) Well developed;
(4) Uniform in color;
(5) Not darker than “light;”
(6) Free from damage by any cause; and
(7) Comply with tolerances for defects (see § 51.1437).
(b) For size:
(1) Uniform in size; and
(2) Conform to size classification or count specified.
