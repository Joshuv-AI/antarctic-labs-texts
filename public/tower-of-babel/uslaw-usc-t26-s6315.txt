26 U.S.C. § 6315 — Payments of estimated income tax
Citation: us:usc:26:6315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6315
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter B (Receipt Of Payment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payment of the estimated income tax, or any installment thereof, shall be considered payment on account of the income taxes imposed by subtitle A for the taxable year.
