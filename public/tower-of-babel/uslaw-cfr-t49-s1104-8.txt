49 C.F.R. § 1104.8 — Objectionable matter.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1104.8
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1104.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1104 (Filing With The Board-Copies-Verification-Service-Pleadings, Generally). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may order that any redundant, irrelevant, immaterial, impertinent, or scandalous matter be stricken from any document.
