14 C.F.R. § 67.3 — Issue.
Citation: us:cfr:14:67.3
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-67.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 67 (Medical Standards And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who meets the medical standards prescribed in this part, based on medical examination and evaluation of the person's history and condition, is entitled to an appropriate medical certificate.
