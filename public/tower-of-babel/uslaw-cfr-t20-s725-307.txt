20 C.F.R. § 725.307 — Cancellation of a request for withdrawal.
Citation: us:cfr:20:725.307
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-725.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 725 (Claims For Benefits Under Part C Of Title Iv Of The Federal Mine Safety And Health Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time prior to approval, a request for withdrawal may be canceled by a written request of the claimant or a person authorized to act on the claimant's behalf or on behalf of the claimant's estate.
