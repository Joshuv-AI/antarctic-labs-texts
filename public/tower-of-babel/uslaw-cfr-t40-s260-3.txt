40 C.F.R. § 260.3 — Use of number and gender.
Citation: us:cfr:40:260.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-260.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 260 (Hazardous Waste Management System: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in parts 260 through 273 of this chapter:
(a) Words in the masculine gender also include the feminine and neuter genders; and
(b) Words in the singular include the plural; and
(c) Words in the plural include the singular.
