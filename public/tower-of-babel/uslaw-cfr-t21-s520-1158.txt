21 C.F.R. § 520.1158 — Iodochlorhydroxyquin.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 520 (Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each bolus contains 10 grams of iodochlorhydroxyquin.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 054771 in § 510.600 (c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use—(1) Amount. 1 bolus (10 grams) daily for a 1,000-pound horse.
(2) Indications for use. For treatment of equine diarrhea.
(3) Limitations. Do not use in horses intended for human consumption. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
