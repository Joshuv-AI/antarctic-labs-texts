25 C.F.R. § 171.200 — How do I request irrigation service from the BIA?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.200
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must request service from the irrigation facility servicing your farm unit.
(b) Your request must contain at least the following information:
(1) Your full legal name;
(2) Where you want service;
(3) The time and date you want service to start;
(4) How long you want service;
(5) The rate of water flow you want, if available;
(6) How many acres you want to irrigate; and
(7) Any additional information required by the project office responsible for providing your irrigation service.
(c) You must request supplemental water in accordance with the project guidelines established by the specific project providing your irrigation service.
