16 U.S.C. § 7808 — Territorial participation
Citation: us:usc:16:7808
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7808
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 97 (Convention On The Conservation And Management Of High Seas Fishery Resources In The South Pacific). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall ensure participation in the Commission and its subsidiary bodies by American Samoa, Guam, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands to the extent allowed under United States law.
