5 C.F.R. § 352.403 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this subpart:
(a) Agency means the International Atomic Energy Agency;
(b) Officer means any Presidential appointee or elected officer who leaves his position after August 27, 1957, and within 90 days enters employment with the agency; and
(c) Term of employment means not more than 3 consecutive years of employment beginning with entrance on duty in the agency.
