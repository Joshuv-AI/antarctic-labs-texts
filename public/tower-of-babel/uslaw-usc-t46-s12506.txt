46 U.S.C. § 12506 — Delegation of authority
Citation: us:usc:46:12506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12506
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 125 (Vessel Identification System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may delegate to an agency, a State, or a qualified person the authority to—
(1)
establish and maintain the vessel identification system; and
(2)
charge fees under section 12505 of this title to a person making information available to or requesting information from the vessel identification system.
