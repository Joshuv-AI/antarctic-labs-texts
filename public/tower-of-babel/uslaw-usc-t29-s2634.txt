29 U.S.C. § 2634 — Compensation
Citation: us:usc:29:2634
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2634
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 28 (Family And Medical Leave); Subchapter II (Commission On Leave). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Pay
Members of the Commission shall serve without compensation.
(b) Travel expenses
Members of the Commission shall be allowed reasonable travel expenses, including a per diem allowance, in accordance with section 5703 of title 5 when performing duties of the Commission.
