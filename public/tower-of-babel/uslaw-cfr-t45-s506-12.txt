45 C.F.R. § 506.12 — Membership in the Armed Forces of the United States; establishment of.
Citation: us:cfr:45:506.12
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-506.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 506 (Eligibility Requirements For Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regular appointment, enrollment, enlistment or induction in the Armed Forces of the United States must be established by certification obtained from the Department of Defense.
