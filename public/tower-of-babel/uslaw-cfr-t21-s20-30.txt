21 C.F.R. § 20.30 — Food and Drug Administration Division of Freedom of Information.
Citation: us:cfr:21:20.30
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-20.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 20 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The office responsible for Agency compliance with the Freedom of Information Act and this part is the Division of Freedom of Informationat the address located on the agency's web site at http://www.fda.gov.
(b) All requests for Agency records shall be sent in writing to this office.
