29 C.F.R. § 401.12 — Trust in which a labor organization is interested.
Citation: us:cfr:29:401.12
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-401.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 401 (Meaning Of Terms Used In This Subchapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Trust in which a labor organization is interested means a trust or other fund or organization (a) which was created or established by a labor organization, or one or more of the trustees or one or more members of the governing body of which is selected or appointed by a labor organization, and (b) a primary purpose of which is to provide benefits for the members of such labor organization or their beneficiaries.
