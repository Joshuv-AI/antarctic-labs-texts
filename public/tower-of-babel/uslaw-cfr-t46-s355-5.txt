46 C.F.R. § 355.5 — Additional material.
Citation: us:cfr:46:355.5
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-355.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 355 (Requirements For Establishing United States Citizenship). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If additional material is determined to be essential to clarify or support the evidence of U.S. citizenship, such material must be furnished by the primary corporation upon request by MARAD.
