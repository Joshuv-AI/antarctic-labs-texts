22 C.F.R. § 504.7 — Service of requests or demands.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 504 (Testimony By Bbg Employees, Production Of Official Records, And Disclosure Of Official Information In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests or demands for official records or information or testimony under this Subpart must be served on the General Counsel, BBG, 330 Independence Ave., SW., Washington, DC 20237 by mail or fax at (202) 203-4585 and clearly marked “Part 504—Request for Testimony or Official Records in Legal Proceedings.”
