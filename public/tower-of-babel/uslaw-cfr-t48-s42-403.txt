48 C.F.R. § 42.403 — 42.403 Evaluation of contract administration offices.
Citation: us:cfr:48:42.403
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-42.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 42 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Onsite inspections or evaluations of the performance of the assigned functions of a contract administration office shall be accomplished only by or under the direction of the agency of which that office is a part.
