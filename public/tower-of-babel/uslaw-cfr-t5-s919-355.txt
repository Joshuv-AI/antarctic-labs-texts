5 C.F.R. § 919.355 — What information must I provide to a higher tier participant before entering into a covered transaction with that participant?
Citation: us:cfr:5:919.355
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-919.355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 919 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before you enter into a covered transaction with a person at the next higher tier, you as a lower tier participant must notify that person if you know that you or any of the principals are presently excluded or disqualified.
