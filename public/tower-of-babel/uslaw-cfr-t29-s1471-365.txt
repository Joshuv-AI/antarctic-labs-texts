29 C.F.R. § 1471.365 — What must I do if I learn of information required under § 1471.355 after entering into a covered transaction with a higher tier participant?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.365
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.365
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time after you enter into a lower tier covered transaction with a person at a higher tier, you must provide immediate written notice to that person if you learn either that—
(a) You failed to disclose information earlier, as required by § 1471.355; or
(b) Due to changed circumstances, you or any of the principals for the transaction now meet any of the criteria in § 1471.355.
