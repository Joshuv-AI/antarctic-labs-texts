10 U.S.C. § 7532 — Factories and arsenals: manufacture at
Citation: us:usc:10:7532
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7532
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 763 (Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army shall have supplies needed for the Department of the Army made in factories or arsenals owned by the United States, so far as those factories or arsenals can make those supplies on an economical basis.
