41 C.F.R. § 101-30.400 — -30.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-30.400
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-30.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-30 (30—Federal Catalog System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the policies and procedures governing the dissemination of Federal catalog data, the conversion to and use of the Federal catalog system by Federal agencies, and the requesting of supply support from Government supply managers.
