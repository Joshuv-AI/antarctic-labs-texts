13 C.F.R. § 106.503 — Are there types of Gifts which SBA may not solicit and/or accept?
Citation: us:cfr:13:106.503
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-106.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 106 (Cosponsorships, Fee And Non-Fee Based Sba-Sponsored Activities And Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. SBA shall not solicit and/or accept Gifts of or for (or use cash Gifts to purchase or engage in) the following:
(a) Alcohol products;
(b) Tobacco products;
(c) Pornographic or sexually explicit objects or services;
(d) Gambling (including raffles and lotteries);
(e) Parties primarily for the benefit of Government employees; and
(f) Any other product or service prohibited by law or policy.
