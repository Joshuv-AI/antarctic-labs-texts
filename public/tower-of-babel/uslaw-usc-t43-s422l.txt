43 U.S.C. § 422l — Application of this subchapter to Hawaii
Citation: us:usc:43:422l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section422l
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter IV (Construction Of Small Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter as heretofore and hereafter amended, shall apply to the State of Hawaii.
