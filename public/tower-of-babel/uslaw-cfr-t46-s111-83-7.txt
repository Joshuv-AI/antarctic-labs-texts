46 C.F.R. § 111.83-7 — -7 High voltage shore connection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.83-7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.83-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ships connecting to shore power and receiving high voltage shore power (over 1000 volts) should meet the requirements of IEC/IEEE 80005-1:2019 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
