7 C.F.R. § 1000.6 — Supply plant.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1000.6
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1000.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1000 (General Provisions Of Federal Milk Marketing Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Supply plant means a plant approved by a duly constituted regulatory agency for the handling of Grade A milk that receives milk directly from dairy farmers and transfers or diverts fluid milk products to other plants or manufactures dairy products on its premises.
