Normal Spaces via Urysohn’s Lemma as a Lifting Property
Robert Maxton

Abstract
We present a translation of Urysohn’s description of normal spaces (as those where disjoint closed subsets are separated by a continuous function) into the language of lifting properties in 𝐓𝐨𝐩 \mathbf{Top} , correcting a frequently-cited erroneous translation. We also present a translation of the definition of hereditarily normal spaces as those in which every open subspace is normal, which we consider simpler and more directly derived from the classical definition.

4
Conclusion

We have here provided a pair of translations of standard topological
separation axioms into categorical language. A formalization in Lean,
making use of Mathlib, can be found at
https://github.com/robertmaxton42/mathlib4/tree/separation
.
