Uniform finiteness of measures of maximal entropy for C r C^{r} surface diffeomorphisms with large entropy
Chiyi Luo, Dawei Yang

Abstract
We prove that for a C r C^{r} surface diffeomorphism f f satisfying h top ​ ( f ) > λ min ​ ( f ) r h_{\rm top}(f)>\frac{\lambda_{\min}(f)}{r} , the number of ergodic measures of maximal entropy is upper semi-continuous at f f . This result connects to the discussion in [ 6 , Remark 1.9] .

Introduction

Entropy, a fundamental concept in ergodic theory, plays a central role in understanding chaotic systems.
Assume that
X
is a compact metric space and
T:X\to X
is a continuous map.
The topological entropy of
T
, denoted by
h_{\rm top}(T)
, quantifies the complexity of a dynamical system by counting the growth of distinct orbits over time.
From the classical theorem of Bogoliubov-Krylov
[
3
]
, every continuous map on a compact metric space admits at least one invariant probability measure.
For such measures, Kolmogorov and Sinai introduced measure-theoretic entropy, or metric entropy, which quantifies the complexity of a dynamical system from the perspective of an invariant measure.
For a
T
-invariant probability measure
\mu
, we denote by
h_{\mu}(T)
the metric entropy of
\mu
with respect to
T
.
The topological entropy and the metric entropy are related by variational principle in dynamical systems (see
[
9
]
):

h_{\rm top}(T)=\sup\{h_{\mu}(T):\mu~\text{is}~T\text{-invariant}\}=\sup\{h_{\mu}(T):\mu~\text{is}~T\text{-ergodic}\}.

For systems with positive topological entropy, a special class of invariant measures is given by those for which the metric entropy equals the topological entropy of the system. Such measures are called
measures of maximal entropy
.
Specifically, a
T
-invariant measure
\mu
is said to be a measure of maximal entropy of
T
if
h_{\mu}(T)=h_{\rm top}(T)
.

For systems with expansivity, measures of maximal entropy exist, such as for uniformly expanding maps, uniformly hyperbolic attractors, and sub-shifts of finite type.
Newhouse
[
15
]
established an important result, showing that for
C^{\infty}
diffeomorphisms, measures of maximal entropy always exist, as the entropy map
\mu\mapsto h_{\mu}(f)
is upper semi-continuous.
Note that almost every ergodic component of a measure of maximal entropy is an ergodic measure of maximal entropy.
An important quantity is the number of ergodic measures of maximal entropy, which is related to the number of invariant elementary pieces of the system.
For example, the Smale spectral decomposition theorem
[
22
]
states that the non-wandering set of an Axiom A diﬀeomorphism can be decomposed into finitely many hyperbolic basic set (hyperboic isolated transitive set).
It is important to note that each hyperbolic basic set has a unique measure of maximal entropy.
Based on several examples, Newhouse
[
16
]
conjectured that
C^{\infty}
surface diffeomorphisms with positive topological entropy admit only finitely many ergodic measures of maximal entropy.

This conjecture was recently proved in the remarkable work by Buzzi-Crovisier-Sarig
[
6
]
.
Beyond uniform hyperbolicity, they applied the homoclinic classes of hyperbolic ergodic measures to establish a spectral decomposition theorem for
C^{\infty}
surface diffeomorphisms with positive topological entropy.
They proved that, on the homoclinic class of any hyperbolic ergodic measure with positive entropy, the measure of maximal entropy is uniquely existent.
Moreover, they showed that the number of homoclinic classes of hyperbolic ergodic measures with metric entropy uniformly greater than zero is finite.
Consequently, the proved that
C^{\infty}
surface diffeomorphisms with positive topological entropy admit only finitely many ergodic measures of maximal entropy.

Beyond
C^{\infty}
diffeomorphisms, measures of maximal entropy do not always exist, even for diffeomorphisms.
Examples can be found in
[
7
]
and
[
14
]
.
However, for
C^{r}
diffeomorphisms with large entropy, it remains meaningful to investigate analogous results.
Initially, Sarig
[
23
]
proved that any
C^{1+\alpha}
surface diffeomorphisms with positive entropy admits at most countably many measures of maximal entropy.
Later, Buzzi-Croviser-Sarig
[
6
]
showed that for
C^{r},r>1
surface diffeomorphism
f
with
h_{\rm top}(f)>\lambda_{\min}(f)/r
, the number of ergodic measures of maximal entropy is finite.
The definition of
\lambda_{\min}(f)
will be provided later, and we refer to the condition
h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)>\lambda_{\min}(f)/r
as the condition under which
f
has large entropy.
It is noteworthy that the existence of measures of maximal entropy under the same condition was recently established by Burguet
[
5
]
, see also in Section
3
.

Another interesting question is how the number of measures of maximal entropy changes under perturbations of the system.
For diffeomorphisms,
[
6
, Theorem 4]
showed that the number of ergodic measures of maximal entropy varies upper semi-continuously in the
C^{\infty}
topology for surface diffeomorphisms with positive entropy.
However, for a
C^{r}
diffeomorphism
f
,
[
6
, Remark 1.9]
observed that it is unknown whether the number of ergodic measures of maximal entropy varies upper semi-continuously in the
C^{r}
topology under the hypothesis
h_{\rm top}(f)>\lambda_{\min}(f)/r
.

In this work, we prove the upper semi-continuity of the number of ergodic measures of maximal entropy for
C^{r}
surface diffeomorphisms with large entropy, i.e., the unknown statement in
[
6

.

Appendix A
Uniform hyperbolicity

A compact invariant set
\Lambda
is said to be
hyperbolic
if there are constants
C>0
and
\lambda\in(0,1)
, and a
Df
-invariant continuous splitting
T_{\Lambda}M=E^{s}\oplus E^{u}
, such that for any
x\in\Lambda
and any
n\in\mathbb{N}
, one has

\|Df^{n}|_{E^{s}(x)}\|\leq C\lambda^{n},~~~\|Df^{-n}|_{E^{u}(x)}\|\leq C\lambda^{n}.

A hyperbolic isolated transitive set is called a
basic set
.

We summarize the following theorem for hyperbolic sets.

Theorem A.1
.

Hyperbolic sets always admit measures of maximal entropy, i.e., if
\Lambda
is a hyperbolic set, then there is an ergodic measure
\mu
supported on
\Lambda
such that
h_{\mu}(f)=h_{\rm top}(f|_{\Lambda})
.

Any basic set
\Lambda
of a
C^{1}
diffeomorphism
f
admits a continuation, i.e., there is a
C^{1}
neighborhood
\mathcal{U}
of
f
such that for any
g\in\mathcal{U}
,
\Lambda
has a continuation
\Lambda_{g}
which is topological conjugate to
\Lambda
. Moreover, one has that
h_{\rm top}(g|_{\Lambda_{g}})=h_{\rm top}(f|_{\Lambda})
.

For a compact invariant set
\Lambda
, for any point
x\in\Lambda
and for any periodic orbits
O\subset\Lambda
, one has that
W^{u}(x)
is accumulated by
W^{u}(O)
.

We will use the dynamical Sard’s theorem from Buzzi-Crovisier-Sarig
[
6
, Theorem 4.2]
. Based on Section 4 of
[
6
]
, we will state its version for a basic set of a
C^{r}
surface diffeomorphism. Note that
[
6
]
considered some continuous lamination. However, in their applications, this continuous lamination is taken to be
W^{u}(\Lambda)
.

Theorem A.2
.

Let
f
be a
C^{r}
surface diffeomorphism,
\Lambda
be a hyperbolic basic satisfying

\frac{h_{\rm top}(f|_{\Lambda})}{\lambda^{s}(\Lambda,f)}>1/r,

and
\gamma

If for any point
