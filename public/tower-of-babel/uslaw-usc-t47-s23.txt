47 U.S.C. § 23 — Injury to submarine cables in efforts to save life excepted
Citation: us:usc:47:23
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section23
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 2 (Submarine Cables). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 21 and 22 of this title shall not apply to a person who breaks or injures a cable in an effort to save the life or limb of himself or of any other person, or to save his own or any other vessel: Provided, That he takes reasonable precautions to avoid such breaking or injury.
