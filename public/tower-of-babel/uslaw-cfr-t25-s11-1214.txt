25 C.F.R. § 11.1214 — Relationship of this subpart to other remedies.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.1214
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.1214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The remedies provided in this subpart are in addition to the other civil or criminal remedies available to the petitioner.
