46 U.S.C. § 56504 — Changes in adjustments and arrangements
Citation: us:usc:46:56504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section56504
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Control Of Merchant Marine Capabilities); Chapter 565 (Essential Vessels Affected By Neutrality Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may change an adjustment or arrangement made under this chapter as the Secretary considers necessary to carry out this chapter.
