10 C.F.R. § 824.5 — Investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:824.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-824.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 824 (Procedural Rules For The Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Classified Information Security Violations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may conduct investigations and inspections relating to the scope, nature and extent of compliance by a person with DOE security requirements specified in § 824.4(a) and (b) and take such action as the Director deems necessary and appropriate to the conduct of the investigation or inspection, including signing, issuing and serving subpoenas.
