32 U.S.C. § 329 — Prohibition on private funding for interstate deployment
Citation: us:usc:32:329
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section329
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 3 (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of the National Guard may not be ordered to cross a border of a State to perform duty (under this title or title 10) if such duty is paid for with private funds, unless such duty is in response to a major disaster or emergency under section 401 of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5170).
