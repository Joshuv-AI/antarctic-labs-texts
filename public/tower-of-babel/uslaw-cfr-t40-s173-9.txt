40 C.F.R. § 173.9 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:40:173.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-173.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 173 (Procedures Governing The Rescission Of State Primary Enforcement Responsibility For Pesticide Use Violations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State may appeal an order rescinding, in whole or in part, its primary enforcement responsibility for pesticide use violations to the appropriate federal court pursuant to section 16 of FIFRA.
