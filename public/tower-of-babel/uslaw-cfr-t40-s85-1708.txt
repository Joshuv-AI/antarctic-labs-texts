40 C.F.R. § 85.1708 — National security exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:40:85.1708
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-85.1708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 85 (Control Of Air Pollution From Mobile Sources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A manufacturer requesting a national security exemption must state the purpose for which the exemption is required and the request must be endorsed by an agency of the Federal Government charged with responsibility for national defense.
