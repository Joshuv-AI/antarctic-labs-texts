31 U.S.C. § 1555 — Closing of appropriation accounts available for indefinite periods
Citation: us:usc:31:1555
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1555
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Accounting); Subchapter IV (Closing Accounts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An appropriation account available for obligation for an indefinite period shall be closed, and any remaining balance (whether obligated or unobligated) in that account shall be canceled and thereafter shall not be available for obligation or expenditure for any purpose, if—
(1)
the head of the agency concerned or the President determines that the purposes for which the appropriation was made have been carried out; and
(2)
no disbursement has been made against the appropriation for two consecutive fiscal years.
