2 C.F.R. § 2701.1 — Adoption of 2 CFR Part 200.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2701.1
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2701.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2701 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under the authority listed above, the U.S. Small Business Administration adopts the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Guidance in 2 CFR part 200, except for 2 CFR 200.74, 200.92, and 200.93. Thus, this part gives regulatory effect to the OMB guidance and supplements the guidance as needed for the Administration.
