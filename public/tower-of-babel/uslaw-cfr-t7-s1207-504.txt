7 C.F.R. § 1207.504 — Term of office.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1207.504
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1207.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1207 (Potato Research And Promotion Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term of office of Board members shall be for three years and shall begin March 1 and end on the last day of February.
(b) Board members shall serve during the term of office for which they are selected and have qualified and until their successors are selected and have qualified.
