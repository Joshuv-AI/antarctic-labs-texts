18 C.F.R. § 1300.101 — Cross references to employee ethical conduct standards and other applicable regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1300.101
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1300.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1300 (Standards Of Conduct For Employees Of Tennessee Valley Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) are subject to the executive branch-wide standards of ethical conduct at 5 CFR part 2635 and to the TVA regulations at 5 CFR part 7901 which supplement the executive branch-wide standards. In addition, certain TVA employees are subject to executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
