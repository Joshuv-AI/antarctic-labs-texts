25 C.F.R. § 38.11 — Length of the regular school term.
Citation: us:cfr:25:38.11
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-38.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 38 (Education Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The length of the regular school term shall be at least 180 student instructional days, unless a waiver has been granted under the provisions of 25 CFR 36.61.
