32 C.F.R. § 228.15 — Restriction regarding animals.
Citation: us:cfr:32:228.15
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-228.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 228 (Security Protective Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No animals except guide dogs for the blind or hearing impaired, or guard or search dogs used by authorized state or federal officials, shall be brought upon protected property, except as authorized by the NSA Director of Security or his designee at each Agency facility.
