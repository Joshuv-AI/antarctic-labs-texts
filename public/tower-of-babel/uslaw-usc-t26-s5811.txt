26 U.S.C. § 5811 — Transfer tax
Citation: us:usc:26:5811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5811
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter A (Taxes); Part II (Tax On Transferring Firearms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Rate
There shall be levied, collected, and paid on firearms transferred a tax at the rate of—
(1)
$200 for each firearm transferred in the case of a machinegun or a destructive device, and
(2)
$0 for any firearm transferred which is not described in paragraph (1).
(b) By whom paid
The tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be paid by the transferor.
(c) Payment
The tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be payable by the appropriate stamps prescribed for payment by the Secretary.
