26 C.F.R. § 1.241-1 — -1 Allowance of special deductions.
Citation: us:cfr:26:1.241-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.241-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 1 (Income Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A corporation, in computing its taxable income, is allowed as deductions the items specified in Part VIII (section 242 and following), Subchapter B, Chapter 1 of the Code, in addition to the deductions provided in part VI (section 161 and following) Subchapter B, Chapter 1 of the Code.
