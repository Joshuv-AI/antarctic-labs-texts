40 C.F.R. § 190.01 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:190.01
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-190.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 190 (Environmental Radiation Protection Standards For Nuclear Power Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part apply to radiation doses received by members of the public in the general environment and to radioactive materials introduced into the general environment as the result of operations which are part of a nuclear fuel cycle.
