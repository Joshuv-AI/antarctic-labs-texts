32 C.F.R. § 766.1 — Purpose.

Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-766.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 766 (Use Of Department Of The Navy Aviation Facilities By Civil Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the policy and procedures for the use of Navy and Marine Corps aviation facilities by aircraft other than U.S. Department of Defense aircraft.
