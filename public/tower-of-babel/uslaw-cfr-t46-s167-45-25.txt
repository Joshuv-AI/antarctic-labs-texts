46 C.F.R. § 167.45-25 — -25 Fire mains and hose connections.
Citation: us:cfr:46:167.45-25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-167.45-25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All pipes used as mains for conducting water from fire pumps on nautical school ships shall be of steel, wrought iron, brass, or copper with wrought iron brass, or composition hose connections.
