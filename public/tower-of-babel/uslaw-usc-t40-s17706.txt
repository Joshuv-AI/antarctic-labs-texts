40 U.S.C. § 17706 — Gross proceeds as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury
Citation: us:usc:40:17706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section17706
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 177 (Alaska Communications Disposal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The gross proceeds of each transfer shall be deposited in the Treasury as miscellaneous receipts.
