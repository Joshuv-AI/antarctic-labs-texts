14 C.F.R. § 22.155 — Fire protection.
Citation: us:cfr:14:22.155
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-22.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 22 (Design, Production, And Airworthiness Requirements For Non-Type Certificated Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hazards of fuel or electrical fires following a survivable emergency landing must be minimized by incorporating design features to sustain static and dynamic deceleration loads without structural damage to fuel or electrical system components or their attachments that would leak fuel to an ignition source or allow electrical power to become an ignition source.
