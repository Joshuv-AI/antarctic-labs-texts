7 C.F.R. § 1414.108 — Payment calculation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1414.108
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1414.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1414 (Bridge Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payments will be determined by multiplying the eligible acres of an eligible specialty crop by the payment rate for such specialty crop. Payment rates are specified in table 1 to § 1414.105(a).
