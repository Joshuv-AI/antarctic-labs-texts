7 U.S.C. § 6211 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:6211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6211
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 91 (Lime Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such funds as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
(b) Administrative expenses
The funds so appropriated shall not be available for payment of the expenses or expenditures of the Board in administering any provisions of an order issued under this chapter.
