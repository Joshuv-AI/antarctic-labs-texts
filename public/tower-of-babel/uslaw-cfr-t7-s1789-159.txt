7 C.F.R. § 1789.159 — Contract administration.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1789.159
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1789.159
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1789 (Use Of Consultants Funded By Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The government shall be solely responsible for the administration of a Consulting Contract and shall have complete control over the scope of the Consultant's work, the timetable for performance, the standards to be applied in determining the acceptability of deliverables and the approval of payment of Invoices.
