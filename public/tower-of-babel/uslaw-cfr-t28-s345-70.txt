28 C.F.R. § 345.70 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:28:345.70
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-345.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 345 (Federal Prison Industries (Fpi) Inmate Work Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FPI provides incentive awards of various types to inmate workers for special achievements in their work, scholarship, suggestions, for inventions which improve industry processes or safety or which conserve energy or materials consumed in FPI operations, and for outstanding levels of self-development.
