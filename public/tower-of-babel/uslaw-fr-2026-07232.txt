Bolstering Tourism Recovery and Air Service for the Commonwealth 
of Northern Mariana Islands; Public Meetings

AGENCY: National Travel and Tourism Office (NTTO), International Trade 
Administration, U.S. Department of Commerce (DOC); Office of the 
Assistant Secretary for Aviation and International Affairs (OST), U.S. 
Department of Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Notice of public meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Travel and Tourism Office (NTTO) and Office of 
the Secretary of Transportation (OST) announce two public meetings to 
be held virtually and the dates, times, and location of the meetings. 
The purpose of these meetings is to convene stakeholders to provide a 
forum for individual ideas and approaches for improving the tourism 
sector in and air service for the Commonwealth of Northern Mariana 
Islands (CNMI). At the first public meeting, the agenda will focus on 
tourism recovery. At the second public meeting, the agenda will focus 
on air service matters.

DATES: The first public meeting regarding recovery in the tourism 
sector will convene on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, beginning at 6:00 
p.m. Eastern Time (ET). The second meeting regarding air services will 
convene on Wednesday, April 29, 2026, beginning at 6:00 p.m. Eastern 
Time (ET).

ADDRESSES: The virtual meetings will be open to the public via 
registration. Registration in advance of the meeting date for the 
virtual meetings is available at:
    April 22 Tourism Sector: https://usdot.zoomgov.com/webinar/register/WN_5fYI9Kr1QI29Ftf4NexmHw#/registration.
    April 29 Air Services: https://usdot.zoomgov.com/webinar/register/WN_GnznpjYGQEyktWmiFhwm4A#/registration.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Office of the Secretary of 
Transportation, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background

    The CNMI experienced a significant decline in tourism after natural 
disasters and the COVID-19 public health emergency. These economic 
conditions and the transportation costs associated with its geographic 
isolation have hindered a recovery in the tourism sector, which is a 
primary factor in the CNMI economy. The scope and purpose of the 
meetings entail obtaining thoughtful feedback from participants about 
approaches to a recovery in the tourism sector and provision of 
affordable air services.

II. Agenda

    First Meeting, Wednesday, April 22, 2026, beginning at 6:00 p.m. 
Eastern Time (ET). The meeting is not expected to exceed 90 minutes.
    At the first meeting on recovery in the tourism sector, the agenda 
will cover the following topics:

1. Call to Order, Meeting Logistics
2. Opening Remarks
3. Public Comment
4. Closing and Adjournment

    Second Meeting, Wednesday, April 29, 2026, beginning at 6:00 p.m. 
Eastern Time (ET). The meeting is not expected to exceed 90 minutes.
    At the second meeting on air services, the agenda will cover the 
following topics:

1. Call to Order, Meeting Logistics
2. Opening Remarks
3. Public Comment
4. Closing and Adjournment

III. Public Participation

    The meetings will be open to the public via virtual meeting 
platform. Members of the public who wish to participate in the virtual 
meetings can attend by registering via the relevant website listed 
above.
