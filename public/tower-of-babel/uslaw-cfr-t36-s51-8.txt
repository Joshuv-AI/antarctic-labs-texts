36 C.F.R. § 51.8 — Where will the Director publish the notice of availability of the prospectus?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.8
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director will publish notice of the availability of the prospectus at least once in the System for Award Management (SAM) where Federal business opportunities are electronically posted, or in a similar publication if this site ceases to be used. The Director, if determined appropriate, may also publish notices electronically on websites including social media and in local or national newspapers or trade magazines.
