2 C.F.R. § 1128.320 — Period of availability of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1128.320
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1128.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1128 (Recipient Financial And Program Management: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requirement. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must specify the period during which Federal funds are available for obligation by recipients for project or program purposes.
(b) Award terms and conditions. A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must include the wording appendix C to this part provides for Section C of FMS Article III to specify the period of availability of funds.
