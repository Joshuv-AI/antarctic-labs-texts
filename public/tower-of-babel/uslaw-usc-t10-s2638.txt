10 U.S.C. § 2638 — Transportation of civilian clothing of enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:2638
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2638
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 157 (Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the military department concerned may provide for the transportation of the civilian clothing of any person entering the armed forces as an enlisted member to the member’s home of record.
