2 C.F.R. § 200.428 — Collections of improper payments.
Citation: us:cfr:2:200.428
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Guidance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.428
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 200 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The costs incurred by a recipient or subrecipient to recover improper payments, including improper overpayments, are allowable as either direct or indirect costs, as appropriate. The recipient or subrecipient may use the amounts collected in accordance with cash management standards described in § 200.305.
