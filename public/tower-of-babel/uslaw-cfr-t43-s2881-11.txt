43 C.F.R. § 2881.11 — When do I need a grant from BLM for an oil and gas pipeline?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2881.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2881.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2880 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Mineral Leasing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must have a BLM grant under 30 U.S.C. 185 for an oil or gas pipeline or related facility to cross Federal lands under:
(a) BLM's jurisdiction; or
(b) The jurisdiction of two or more Federal agencies.
