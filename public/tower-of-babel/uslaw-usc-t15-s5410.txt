15 U.S.C. § 5410 — Relationship to State laws
Citation: us:usc:15:5410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5410
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 80 (Fasteners). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to preempt any rights or causes of action that any buyer may have with respect to any seller of fasteners under the law of any State, except to the extent that the provisions of this chapter are in conflict with such State law.
