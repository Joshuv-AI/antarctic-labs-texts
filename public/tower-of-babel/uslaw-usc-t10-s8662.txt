10 U.S.C. § 8662 — Naming
Citation: us:usc:10:8662
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8662
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 863 (Naval Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Not more than one vessel of the Navy may have the same name.
(b)
The Secretary of the Navy may change the name of any vessel bought for the Navy.
