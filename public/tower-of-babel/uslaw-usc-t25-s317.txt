25 U.S.C. § 317 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:25:317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section317
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 8 (Rights-Of-Way Through Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall make all needful rules and regulations, not inconsistent with sections 312 to 318 of this title, for the proper execution and carrying into effect of all the provisions of said sections.
