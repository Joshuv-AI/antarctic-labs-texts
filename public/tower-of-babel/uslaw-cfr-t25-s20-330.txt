25 C.F.R. § 20.330 — What is the payment standard for Emergency Assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.330
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The approved payment will not exceed the Bureau's maximum Emergency Assistance payment standard established by the Assistant Secretary.
