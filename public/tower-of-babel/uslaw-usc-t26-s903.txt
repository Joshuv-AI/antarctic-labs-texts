26 U.S.C. § 903 — Credit for taxes in lieu of income, etc., taxes
Citation: us:usc:26:903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section903
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter N (Tax Based On Income From Sources Within Or Without The United States); Part III (Income From Sources Without The United States); Subpart A (Foreign Tax Credit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this part and of sections 164(a) and 275(a), the term “income, war profits, and excess profits taxes” shall include a tax paid in lieu of a tax on income, war profits, or excess profits otherwise generally imposed by any foreign country or by any possession of the United States.
