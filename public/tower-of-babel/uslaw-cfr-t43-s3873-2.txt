43 C.F.R. § 3873.2 — Effect of decision that land is mineral.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3873.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3873.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3870 (Adverse Claims, Protests And Conflicts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fact that a certain tract of land is decided upon testimony to the mineral in character is by no means equivalent to an award of the land to a miner. In order to secure a patent for such land, he must proceed as in other cases, in accordance with this part.
