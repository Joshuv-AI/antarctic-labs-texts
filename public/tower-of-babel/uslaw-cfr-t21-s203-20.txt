21 C.F.R. § 203.20 — Sales restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:203.20
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-203.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 203 (Prescription Drug Marketing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 203.22 or § 203.23, no person may sell, purchase, or trade, or offer to sell, purchase, or trade any prescription drug that was:
(a) Purchased by a public or private hospital or other health care entity; or
(b) Donated or supplied at a reduced price to a charitable organization.
