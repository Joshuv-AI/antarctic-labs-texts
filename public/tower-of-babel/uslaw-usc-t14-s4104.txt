14 U.S.C. § 4104 — Availability of facilities and appropriations
Citation: us:usc:14:4104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section4104
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 41 (General Provisions For Coast Guard Reserve And Auxiliary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The services and facilities of and appropriations for the Coast Guard shall be available to effectuate the purposes of the Reserve and the Auxiliary.
