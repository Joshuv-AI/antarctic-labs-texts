Cardinal invariants associated with the combinatorics of the uniformity number of the ideal of meager-additive sets
Miguel A. Cardona

Abstract
In [ CMRM24 ] , it was proved that it is relatively consistent that bounding number 𝔟 \mathfrak{b} is smaller than the uniformity of ℳ ​ 𝒜 \mathcal{MA} , where ℳ ​ 𝒜 \mathcal{MA} denotes the ideal of the meager-additive sets of 2 ω 2^{\omega} . To establish this result, a specific cardinal invariant, which we refer to as 𝔟 b 𝖾𝗊 \mathfrak{b}_{b}^{\mathsf{eq}} , was introduced in close relation to Bartoszyński’s and Judah’s characterization of the uniformity of ℳ ​ 𝒜 \mathcal{MA} . This survey aims to explore this cardinal invariant along with its dual, which we call as 𝔡 b 𝖾𝗊 \mathfrak{d}_{b}^{\mathsf{eq}} . In particular, we will illustrate its connections with the cardinals represented in Cichoń’s diagram. Furthermore, we will present several open problems pertaining to these cardinals.

Definition 1.13
.

We say that
\mathsf{R}=\langle X,Y,\sqsubset\rangle
is a
relational system
if it consists of two non-empty sets

Y

\sqsubset
.

(1)

A set

is
\mathsf{R}
-bounded
if
\exists\,y\in Y\ \forall\,x\in F\colon x\sqsubset y
.

(2)

A set
E\subseteq Y
is
\mathsf{R}
-dominating
if
\forall\,x\in X\ \exists\,y\in E\colon x\sqsubset y
.

We associate two cardinal invariants with this relational system
\mathsf{R}

\mathfrak{b}(\mathsf{R}):=\min\{|F|\colon F\subseteq X\text{ is }\mathsf{R}\text{-unbounded}\}
the
unbounding number of
\mathsf{R}
, and

\mathfrak{d}(\mathsf{R}):=\min\{|D|\colon D\subseteq Y\text{ is }\mathsf{R}\text{-dominating}\}
the
dominating number of
\mathsf{R}
.

Note that
\mathfrak{d}(\mathsf{R})=1
iff
\mathfrak{b}(\mathsf{R})
is undefined (i.e. there are no
\mathsf{R}
-unbounded sets, which is the same as saying that
X
is
\mathsf{R}
-bounded). Dually,
\mathfrak{b}(\mathsf{R})=1
iff
\mathfrak{d}(\mathsf{R})
is undefined (i.e. there are no
\mathsf{R}
-dominating families).

Directed preorders provide a very representative broad example of relational systems.

Definition 3.24
