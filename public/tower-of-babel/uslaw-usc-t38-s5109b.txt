38 U.S.C. § 5109B — Expedited treatment of returned and remanded claims
Citation: us:usc:38:5109B
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5109B
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 51 (Claims, Effective Dates, And Payments); Subchapter I (Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall take such actions as may be necessary to provide for the expeditious treatment by the Veterans Benefits Administration of any claim that is returned by a higher-level adjudicator under section 5104B of this title or remanded by the Board of Veterans’ Appeals.
