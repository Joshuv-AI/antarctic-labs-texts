25 C.F.R. § 40.3 — Applications.
Citation: us:cfr:25:40.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-40.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 40 (Administration Of Educational Loans, Grants And Other Assistance For Higher Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for educational loans, grants, and working scholarships shall be submitted through the superintendent or officer in charge of the agency at which the applicant is enrolled in the manner prescribed by the Commissioner.
