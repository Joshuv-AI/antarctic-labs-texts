39 C.F.R. § 966.5 — Effect of petition filing.
Citation: us:cfr:39:966.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-966.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 966 (Administrative Offsets Initiated Against Former Postal Service Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt and docketing of the former employee's petition, the Recorder will notify the General Counsel that the petition has been filed and that a timely filed petition stays further collection action.
