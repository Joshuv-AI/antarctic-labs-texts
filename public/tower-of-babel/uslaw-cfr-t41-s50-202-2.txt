41 C.F.R. § 50-202.2 — -202.2 Minimum wage in all industries.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-202.2
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-202.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-202 (202—Minimum Wage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In all industries, the minimum wage applicable to employees described in § 50-201.102 of this chapter shall be not less than $3.35 per hour commencing January 1, 1981, $3.80 per hour commencing April 1, 1990, and $4.25 per hour commencing April 1, 1991.
