25 C.F.R. § 153.6 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:25:153.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-153.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 153 (Determination Of Competency: Crow Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appeal to the Secretary of the Interior may be made within 30 days from the date of notice to the applicant of the decision of the Commissioner of Indian Affairs.
