31 U.S.C. § 6207 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:6207
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6207
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 62 (Consolidated Federal Funds Report). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1986, 1987, 1988, 1989, and 1990.
