Pacific Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Pacific Fishery Management Council's (Pacific Council) 
Scientific and Statistical Committee (SSC) will hold a virtual meeting 
to discuss matters relevant to the November 2026 Pacific Council 
meeting. This meeting is open to the public.

DATES: The SSC meeting will be held Tuesday, November 10, 2026, from 8 
a.m. until 12 p.m. (Pacific Time) or until business for the day has 
been completed.

ADDRESSES: The SSC meeting will be conducted online. Specific meeting 
information, materials, and instructions for how to connect to the 
meeting remotely will be provided in the meeting announcement on the 
Pacific Council's website (see https://www.pcouncil.org). Please 
contact Hayden York ([email protected]) or (503) 820-2424 for 
technical assistance.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Diana Perry, Staff Officer, Pacific 
Council; telephone: (503) 820-2414, email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The SSC meeting will focus on the Groundfish 
Stock Assessment Process for 2027, Groundfish Stock Assessment Terms of 
Reference, and SSC workload planning. Additionally, the Committee may 
also discuss any other topics on the Pacific Council's November agenda. 
The SSC will prepare their recommendations for Pacific Council 
consideration at their November 2026 meeting. No management actions 
will be decided by the SSC.
    Although non-emergency issues not contained in the meeting agenda 
may be discussed, those issues may not be the subject of formal action 
during these meetings. Action will be restricted to those issues 
specifically listed in this document and any issues arising after 
publication of this document that require emergency action under 
section 305(c) of the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery Conservation and 
Management Act, provided the public has been notified of the intent to 
take final action to address the emergency.

Special Accommodations

    Requests for sign language interpretation or other auxiliary aids 
should be directed to Hayden York ([email protected]; (503) 820-
2424) at least 10 days prior to the meeting date.
