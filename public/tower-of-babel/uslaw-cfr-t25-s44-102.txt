25 C.F.R. § 44.102 — Does this part affect existing tribal rights?
Citation: us:cfr:25:44.102
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-44.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 44 (Grants Under The Tribally Controlled Schools Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not:
(a) Affect in any way the sovereign immunity from suit enjoyed by Indian tribes;
(b) Terminate or change the trust responsibility of the United States to any Indian tribe or individual Indian;
(c) Require an Indian tribe to apply for a grant; or
(d) Impede awards by any other Federal agency to any Indian tribe or tribal organization to administer any Indian program under any other law.
