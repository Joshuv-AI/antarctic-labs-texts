41 C.F.R. § 301-10.1 — -10.1 Eligibility for transportation expenses payment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.1
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are eligible for payment of authorized transportation expenses when performing official travel, including fares, rental fees, mileage payments, and other expenses related to transportation.
