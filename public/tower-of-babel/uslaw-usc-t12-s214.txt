12 U.S.C. § 214 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:12:214
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section214
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XV (Conversion Of National Banks Into State Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
As used in this subchapter and section 321 of this title the term “State bank” means any bank, banking association, trust company, savings bank (other than a mutual savings bank), or other banking institution which is engaged in the business of receiving deposits and which is incorporated under the laws of any State, any Territory of the United States, Puerto Rico, or the Virgin Islands, or which is operating under the Code of Law for the District of Columbia.
(b)
For purposes of merger or consolidation under this subchapter and section 321 of this title the term “national banking association” means one or more national banking associations, and the term “State bank” means one or more State banks.
