32 C.F.R. § 935.2 — Purpose.

Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide—
(a) For the civil administration of Wake Island;
(b) Civil laws for Wake Island not otherwise provided for;
(c) Criminal laws for Wake Island not otherwise provided for; and
(d) A judicial system for Wake Island not otherwise provided for.
