32 C.F.R. § 842.111 — Recovery rates in government facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:32:842.111
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-842.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 842 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Register contains the rates set by the Office of Management and Budget, of which judges take judicial notice. Apply the rates in effect at the time of care to claims.
