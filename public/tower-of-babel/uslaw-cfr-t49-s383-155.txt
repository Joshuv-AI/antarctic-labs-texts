49 C.F.R. § 383.155 — Tamperproofing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:383.155
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-383.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 383 (Commercial Driver'S License Standards; Requirements And Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States must make the CLP and CDL tamperproof to the maximum extent practicable. At a minimum, a State must use the same tamperproof method used for noncommercial drivers' licenses.
