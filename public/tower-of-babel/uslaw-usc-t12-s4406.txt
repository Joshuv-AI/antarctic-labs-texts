12 U.S.C. § 4406 — Relationship to other payments systems
Citation: us:usc:12:4406
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4406
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 45 (Payment System Risk Reduction); Subchapter I (Bilateral And Clearing Organization Netting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall have no effect by implication or otherwise on the validity or legal enforceability of a netting arrangement of any payment system which is not subject to this chapter.
