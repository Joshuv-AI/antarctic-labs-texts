27 C.F.R. § 479.161 — National Firearms Act stamps.
Citation: us:cfr:27:479.161
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-479.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 479 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“National Firearms Act” stamps evidencing payment of the transfer tax or tax on the making of a firearm are maintained by the Director. The remittance for purchase of the appropriate tax stamp shall be submitted with the application. Upon approval of the application, the Director will cause the appropriate tax to be paid by affixing the appropriate stamp to the application.
