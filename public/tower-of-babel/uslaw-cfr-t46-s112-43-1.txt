46 C.F.R. § 112.43-1 — -1 Switches.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.43-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.43-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An emergency lighting system must not have a switch, except:
(a) In a distribution panel;
(b) As required in § 112.43-7; or
(c) In a circuit that serves a hazardous space such as a paint room or cargo handling room if the switch is located outside of the hazardous location.
