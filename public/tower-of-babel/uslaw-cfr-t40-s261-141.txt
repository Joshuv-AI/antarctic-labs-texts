40 C.F.R. § 261.141 — Definitions of terms as used in this subpart.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-261.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 261 (Identification And Listing Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms defined in § 265.141(d), (f), (g), and (h) of this chapter have the same meaning in this subpart as they do in § 265.141 of this chapter.
