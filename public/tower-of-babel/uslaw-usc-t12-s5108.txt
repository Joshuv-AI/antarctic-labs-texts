12 U.S.C. § 5108 — Backup authority to establish a nationwide mortgage licensing and registry system
Citation: us:usc:12:5108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5108
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 51 (Secure And Fair Enforcement For Mortgage Licensing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If at any time the Director determines that the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry is failing to meet the requirements and purposes of this chapter for a comprehensive licensing, supervisory, and tracking system for loan originators, the Director shall establish and maintain such a system to carry out the purposes of this chapter and the effective registration and regulation of loan originators.
