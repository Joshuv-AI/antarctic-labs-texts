21 U.S.C. § 147 — Repeal of inconsistent laws
Citation: us:usc:21:147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section147
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 4 (Animals, Meats, And Meat And Dairy Products); Subchapter IV (Importation Of Milk And Cream). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any laws or parts of laws inconsistent with this subchapter are repealed.
