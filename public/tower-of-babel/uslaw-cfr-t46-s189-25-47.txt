46 C.F.R. § 189.25-47 — -47 Chemical and explosive hazards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:189.25-47
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-189.25-47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 189 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The marine inspector shall inspect every chemistry laboratory, scientific laboratory, and chemical storeroom during each inspection for certification and periodic inspection.
(b) Magazines, vans, and chests shall be inspected during each inspection for certification and periodic inspection.
