15 U.S.C. § 7902 — Prohibition on bringing of qualified civil liability actions in Federal or State court
Citation: us:usc:15:7902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7902
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 105 (Protection Of Lawful Commerce In Arms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
A qualified civil liability action may not be brought in any Federal or State court.
(b) Dismissal of pending actions
A qualified civil liability action that is pending on October 26, 2005, shall be immediately dismissed by the court in which the action was brought or is currently pending.
