47 C.F.R. § 90.549 — Transmitter certification.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.549
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.549
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transmitters operated in the 758-775 MHz and 788-805 MHz frequency bands must be of a type that have been authorized by the Commission under its certification procedure as required by § 90.203.
