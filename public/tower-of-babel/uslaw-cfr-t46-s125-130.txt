46 C.F.R. § 125.130 — Carriage of packaged hazardous materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:125.130
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-125.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 125 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An OSV may carry packaged hazardous materials, or hazardous materials in portable tanks, if the materials are prepared, loaded, and stowed in compliance with 49 CFR parts 171 through 179, as applicable.
