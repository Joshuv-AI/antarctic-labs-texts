17 C.F.R. § 242.800 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:17:242.800
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-242.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 242 (Regulations M, Sho, Ats, Ac, Nms, Se, And Sbsr, And Customer Margin Requirements For Security Futures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of §§ 242.800 through 242.835 shall apply to every security-based swap execution facility that is registered or is applying to become registered as a security-based swap execution facility under section 3D of the Securities Exchange Act (“Act”).
