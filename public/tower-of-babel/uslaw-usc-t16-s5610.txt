16 U.S.C. § 5610 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:5610
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5610
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 76 (Northwest Atlantic Fisheries Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter, including to pay the United States contribution to the Organization as provided in Article IX of the Convention, $500,000 for each fiscal year through fiscal year 2021.
