10 C.F.R. § 780.42 — Notice of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:780.42
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-780.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 780 (Patent Compensation Board Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within thirty (30) days after the filing of the application, the Board will serve on all parties a notice of hearing to be held not later than sixty (60) days after the filing of the application.
