21 C.F.R. § 56.105 — Waiver of IRB requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:21:56.105
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-56.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 56 (Institutional Review Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On the application of a sponsor or sponsor-investigator, the Food and Drug Administration may waive any of the requirements contained in these regulations, including the requirements for IRB review, for specific research activities or for classes of research activities, otherwise covered by these regulations.
