46 C.F.R. § 107.405 — Safety Equipment Certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:46:107.405
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-107.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 107 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A self-propelled unit of at least 500 gross tons that engages in international voyages is issued a safety equipment certificate if the inspector issues it a certificate of inspection under § 107.211 or § 107.215 and it meets chapter 3 of the International Convention for Safety of Life at Sea, 1974.
(b) A Safety Equipment Certificate expires 60 months after the date of issue.
