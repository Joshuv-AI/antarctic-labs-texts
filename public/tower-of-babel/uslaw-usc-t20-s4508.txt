20 U.S.C. § 4508 — Amount of fellowships
Citation: us:usc:20:4508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4508
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 57 (James Madison Memorial Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each student awarded a fellowship shall receive a stipend which shall not exceed the cost to the student for tuition, fees, books, room and board, or $12,000, whichever is less, for each academic year of study.
