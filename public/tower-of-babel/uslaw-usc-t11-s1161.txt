11 U.S.C. § 1161 — Inapplicability of other sections
Citation: us:usc:11:1161
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1161
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter IV (Railroad Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 341, 343, 1102(a)(1), 1104, 1105, 1107, 1129(a)(7), and 1129(c) of this title do not apply in a case concerning a railroad.
