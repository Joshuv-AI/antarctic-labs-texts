46 C.F.R. § 35.35-35 — -35 Duties of Person in Charge of transfer—TB/ALL.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-35.35-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 35 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Person in Charge of the transfer of liquid cargo in bulk, fuel oil in bulk, or bunkers in bulk must control the transfer as follows:
(a) Supervise the operations of cargo-system valves.
(b) Commence transfer of cargo at slow rate of cargo flow.
(c) Observe cargo connections for leakage.
(d) Observe pressure on cargo system.
(e) If transfer is loading (rather than discharging), observe rate of loading to avoid overflow of tanks.
(f) Comply with 33 CFR 156.120 and 156.150.
