12 C.F.R. § 333.1 — Classification of general character of business.
Citation: us:cfr:12:333.1
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-333.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 333 (Extension Of Corporate Powers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State nonmember insured banks are divided into five categories for the purpose of classifying their general character or type of business, 2 viz: commercial banks, banks and trust companies, savings banks (including mutual and stock), industrial banks, and cash depositories.
