33 C.F.R. § 333.71 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:333.71
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-333.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 333 (Processing Of Department Of The Army Permits And 33 U.S.C. 408 Permissions, National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sections of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any section or portion therein is stayed or determined to be invalid, or the applicability of any section to any person or entity is held invalid, it is the Corps' intention that the validity of the remainder of those parts will not be affected. The remaining sections or portions, and all applications thereof, shall continue to be in effect.
