40 C.F.R. § 1516.5 — How can I get information about how CEQ has used its records about me?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1516.5
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1516.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1516 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You can request information about how CEQ has used its records about you—called an “accounting of disclosures”—using the same procedures you would use to make a request for access to your records under § 1516.4.
