14 U.S.C. § 2109 — Selection boards; oath of members
Citation: us:usc:14:2109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2109
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter I (Appointment And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each member of a selection board shall swear—
(1)
that the member will, without prejudice or partiality, and having in view both the special fitness of officers and the efficiency of the Coast Guard, perform the duties imposed upon the member; and
(2)
an oath in accordance with section 931.
