41 C.F.R. § 302-3.301 — -3.301 Transportation of household goods to an alternate location.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.301
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who has successfully completed their service agreement, may transport their household goods to a location other than their place of actual residence when they separate from the Government. However, the cost cannot exceed what it would cost to the place of actual residence.
