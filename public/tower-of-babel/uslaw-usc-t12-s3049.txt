12 U.S.C. § 3049 — Authorization of appropriations for administration; availability of amounts
Citation: us:usc:12:3049
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3049
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 31 (National Consumer Cooperative Bank); Subchapter II (Office Of Self-Help Development And Technical Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Office $2,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1979, and for each of the two succeeding fiscal years, such sums as may be necessary for the administration of this subchapter. Any sums so appropriated shall remain available until expended.
