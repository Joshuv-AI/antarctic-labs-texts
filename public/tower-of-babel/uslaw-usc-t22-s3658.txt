22 U.S.C. § 3658 — Benefits based on basic pay
Citation: us:usc:22:3658
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3658
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 2 (Employees); Subpart ii (Wage And Employment Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of determining—
(1)
amounts of compensation for disability or death under chapter 81 of title 5, relating to compensation for work injuries;
(2)
benefits under subchapter III of chapter 83 or chapter 84 of title 5, relating to retirement;
(3)
amounts of insurance under chapter 87 of title 5, relating to life insurance;
(4)
amounts of overtime pay or other premium pay;
(5)
annual leave benefits; and
(6)
any other benefits related to basic pay;
the basic pay of each employee shall include the rate of basic pay established for his position under section 3642 of this title plus the amount of any additional compensation provided under section 3657(a) 1 of this title.
