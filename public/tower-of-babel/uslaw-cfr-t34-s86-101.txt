34 C.F.R. § 86.101 — What review of IHE drug prevention programs does the Secretary conduct?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.101
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary annually reviews a representative sample of IHE drug prevention programs.
