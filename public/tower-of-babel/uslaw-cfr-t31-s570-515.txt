31 C.F.R. § 570.515 — Funds of the General National Maritime Transport Company unblocked.
Citation: us:cfr:31:570.515
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-570.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 570 (Libyan Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As of November 18, 2011, all funds, including cash, securities, bank accounts, and investment accounts, and precious metals of the General National Maritime Transport Company blocked pursuant to § 570.201(a)(1) or (3) are unblocked.
(b) Within 10 business days of the release of any blocked funds or precious metals pursuant to paragraph (a) of this section, a report must be filed with the Compliance Division of the Office of Foreign Assets Control via email to OFACreport@treasury.gov. For each amount released, the report must include a copy of the related initial blocking report and indicate the date that the funds were released and the amount released.
