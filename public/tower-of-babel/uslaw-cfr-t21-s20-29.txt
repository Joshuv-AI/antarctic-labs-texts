21 C.F.R. § 20.29 — Prohibition on withdrawal of records from Food and Drug Administration files.
Citation: us:cfr:21:20.29
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-20.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 20 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may withdraw records submitted to the Food and Drug Administration. All Food and Drug Administration records shall be retained by the agency until disposed of pursuant to routine record disposal procedures.
