21 C.F.R. § 573.740 — Odorless light petroleum hydrocarbons.
Citation: us:cfr:21:573.740
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-573.740
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 573 (Food Additives Permitted In Feed And Drinking Water Of Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Odorless light petroleum hydrocarbons complying with § 172.884(a) and (b) of this chapter may be safely used in an amount not in excess of that required as a component of insecticide formulations used in compliance with regulations issued in this part.
