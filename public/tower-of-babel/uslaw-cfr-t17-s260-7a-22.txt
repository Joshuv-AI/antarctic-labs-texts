17 C.F.R. § 260.7a-22 — a-22 Information unknown or not reasonably available.
Citation: us:cfr:17:260.7a-22
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-260.7a-22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 260 (General Rules And Regulations, Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information required shall be given insofar as it is known or can be obtained by reasonable investigation. Responsibility for the accuracy or completeness of information obtained from persons other than affiliates may be disclaimed. As to information which is unknown and is unavailable after reasonable investigation, there shall be included a statement as to the nature of the investigation.
