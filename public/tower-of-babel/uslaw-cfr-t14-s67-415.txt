14 C.F.R. § 67.415 — Return of medical certificate after suspension or revocation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:67.415
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-67.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 67 (Medical Standards And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The holder of any medical certificate issued under this part that is suspended or revoked shall, upon the Administrator's request, return it to the Administrator.
