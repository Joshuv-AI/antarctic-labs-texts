42 U.S.C. § 2136 — Classes of facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:2136
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2136
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter IX (Atomic Energy Licenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may—
(a)
group the facilities licensed either under section 2133 or 2134 of this title into classes which may include either production or utilization facilities or both, upon the basis of the similarity of operating and technical characteristics of the facilities;
(b)
define the various activities to be carried on at each such class of facility; and
(c)
designate the amounts of special nuclear material available for use by each such facility.
