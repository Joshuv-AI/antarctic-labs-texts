5 C.F.R. § 1202.1 — Designating Chairman of Statutory Review Board.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1202.1
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1202.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1202 (Statutory Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the written request of the Department of Transportation, the Chairman of the Board will designate a presiding official of the Board to serve as the Chairman of any Board of Review established by the Secretary of Transportation under 5 U.S.C. 3383(b) to review certain actions to remove air traffic controllers.
