42 U.S.C. § 300mm–52 — World Trade Center Health Registry
Citation: us:usc:42:300mm–52
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300mm–52
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXXI (World Trade Center Health Program); Part C (Research Into Conditions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of ensuring ongoing data collection relating to victims of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks, the WTC Program Administrator shall ensure that a registry of such victims is maintained that is at least as comprehensive as the World Trade Center Health Registry maintained under the arrangements in effect as of January 1, 2015, with the New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene.
