Estimates for the Gelfand widths of intersections of finite-dimensional balls
A.A. Vasil’eva

Abstract
In this paper, we obtain order estimates for the Gelfand widths of intersections of finite-dimensional balls under some conditions on parameters.



1
Introduction

In this paper, we consider the problem of estimating the Gelfand
n
-widths of an intersection of finite-dimensional balls for
n\leqslant aN
, where
a\in(0,\,1/2]
is an absolute constant. The similar problem for the Kolmogorov widths was considered in
[
1
,
16
]
. For some conditions on the parameters, we obtain the order estimates for the Gelfand widths; as a consequence, under some conditions on the parameters, we get the order estimates for linear widths.

Let us give the definitions of the Gelfand, Kolmogorov and linear widths.

Let
X
be a linear normed space. By
X^{*}
we denote the dual space for
X
, by
{\cal L}_{n}(X)
, the family of all linear subspaces in
X
of dimension at most
n
, by
L(X,\,X)
, the space of linear continuous operators on
X
. For
A\in L(X,\,X)
, by
{\rm rk}\,A
we denote the dimension of the range of
A
. Let
M\subset X
be a nonempty set,
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
. The Gelfand, Kolmogorov and linear
n
-widths of the set
M
in
X
are defined, correspondingly, as follows:

d^{n}(M,\,X)=\inf_{x_{1}^{*},\,\dots,\,x_{n}^{*}\in X^{*}}\sup\Bigl\{\|x\|:\;x\in M\cap\Bigl(\cap_{j=1}^{n}\ker x_{j}^{*}\Bigr)\Bigr\};

d_{n}(M,\,X)=\inf_{L\in{\cal L}_{n}(X)}\sup_{x\in M}\inf_{y\in L}\|x-y\|;

\lambda_{n}(M,\,X)=\inf_{A\in L(X,\,X),\,{\rm rk}\,A\leqslant n}\sup_{x\in M}\|x-Ax\|.

From the definitions it follows that
d_{n}(M,\,X)\leqslant\lambda_{n}(M,\,X)
,
d^{n}(M,\,X)\leqslant\lambda_{n}(M,\,X)
.

Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
1\leqslant p\leqslant\infty
,
(x_{i})_{i=1}^{N}\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
. We set
\|(x_{i})_{i=1}^{N}\|_{l_{p}^{N}}=\left(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}|x_{i}|^{p}\right)^{1/p}
for
p<\infty
,
\|(x_{i})_{i=1}^{N}\|_{l_{p}^{N}}=\max_{1\leqslant i\leqslant N}|x_{i}|
by
p=\infty
. The space
\mathbb{R}^{N}
with this norm is denoted by
l_{p}^{N}
. By
B_{p}^{N}
we denote the unit ball in
l_{p}^{N}
.

Given
1\leqslant p\leqslant\infty
, we denote by
p^{\prime}
the dual exponent of
p
defined by the equation
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
.

The problem of estimating
d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
,
d^{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
and
\lambda_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
was studied in
[
10
,
14
,
13
,
15
,
7
,
8
,
9
,
4
,
5
,
3
]
. For
p\geqslant q
, the sharp values of these widths are known
[
13
,
15
]
, as well as the sharp values of
d_{n}(B_{1}^{N},\,l_{2}^{N})=\lambda_{n}(B_{1}^{N},\,l_{2}^{N})

[
10
,
14
]
. Hence from the duality formulas
d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})=d^{n}(B_{q^{\prime}}^{N},\,l_{p^{\prime}}^{N})
,
\lambda_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})=\lambda_{n}(B_{q^{\prime}}^{N},\,l_{p^{\prime}}^{N})
(see
[
6
]
) we get the sharp values of
d^{n}(B_{2}^{N},\,l_{\infty}^{N})=\lambda_{n}(B_{2}^{N},\,l_{\infty}^{N})
. Order estimates for
d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
,
d^{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
and
\lambda_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
are obtained, correspondingly, for
\{1\leqslant p\leqslant q\leqslant\infty:\;q<\infty\;\text{or }(q=\infty,\;p\geqslant 2)\}
,
\{1\leqslant p\leqslant q\leqslant\infty:\;p>1\,\text{or }(p=1,\;q\leqslant 2)\}
,
\{1\leqslant p\leqslant q\leqslant\infty:\;(p,\,q)\neq(1,\,\infty)\}
(see
[

4

In
[
1
]
order estimates for

were obtained for
N=2n
(here
\nu_{\alpha}>0
,
\alpha\in A
). In
[

; after that, the similar problem for mixed norms was studied

The order estimates of

(where

) were obtained for
1\leqslant q<\infty
and arbitrary

, as well as for

and
p_{\alpha}\geqslant 2
,
\alpha\in A
. Also some simple cases were mentioned, when
\lambda_{n}(\cap_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}B^{N}_{p_{\alpha}},\,l_{q}^{N})
and

can be similarly estimated
[

. In particular, for the Gelfand widths the order estimates can be easily written if 1)
p_{\alpha}\geqslant q
,

, 2)

\inf_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}N^{1/q-1/p_{\alpha}}
and

In this paper we consider two more areas of parameters, where we could obtain order estimates of
d^{n}(\cap_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}B^{N}_{p_{\alpha}},\,l_{q}^{N})
.

First we consider the case of an intersection of a finite family of balls
\cap_{j=1}^{r}\nu_{j}B^{N}_{p_{j}}
, when

\displaystyle p_{1}<p_{2}<\dots<p_{r},

(1)

\displaystyle\nu_{1}\geqslant\nu_{2}\geqslant\dots\geqslant\nu_{r},\quad\nu_{1}N^{-1/p_{1}}\leqslant\nu_{2}N^{-1/p_{2}}\leqslant\dots\leqslant\nu_{r}N^{-1/p_{r}}.

(2)

The condition (
1
) can be satisfied by choosing the numeration; if
p_{i}=p_{j}
for some
i\neq j
, the problem can be reduced to the case of less number of balls. If (
2
) fails, we have
\nu_{i}B^{N}_{p_{i}}\subset\nu_{j}B^{N}_{p_{j}}
or
\nu_{i}B^{N}_{p_{i}}\supset\nu_{j}B^{N}_{p_{j}}
for some
i\neq j
, and the problem can be reduced to the case of less number of balls. Hence in the case of an intersection of a finite number of balls it suffices to obtain the estimates under the conditions (
1
), (

).

Let
X
,
Y
be sets,
f_{1}
,
f_{2}:\ X\times Y\rightarrow\mathbb{R}_{+}
.
We write
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{2}(x,\,y)
(or

f_{2}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\gtrsim}f_{1}(x,\,y)
) if for each

there is

such that
f_{1}(x,\,y)\leqslant c(y)f_{2}(x,\,y)
for all
x\in X
;
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\asymp}f_{2}(x,\,y)
if
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{2}(x,\,y)
and
f_{2}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{1}(x,\,y)
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
2\leqslant q\leqslant\infty
,
1<p_{1}<p_{2}<\dots<p_{r}\leqslant q
. Suppose that (
2
) holds and
p_{1}<2
. Then for
n\leqslant N/2

d^{n}(\cap_{j=1}^{r}\nu_{j}B_{p_{j}}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{p_{1}}{\asymp}\min\{\nu_{1}n^{-1/2}N^{1/p_{1}^{\prime}},\,\nu_{r}\}.

Theorem 2
.

Let
2\leqslant q\leqslant\infty
,
2<p_{1}<p_{2}<\dots<p_{r}
. Suppose that
(
2
) holds and
p_{1}<q<p_{r}
. Given
p_{i}\leqslant q
,
p_{j}\geqslant q
we define the numbers
\lambda_{ij}\in[0,\,1]
by the equation

\displaystyle\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1-\lambda_{ij}}{p_{i}}+\frac{\lambda_{ij}}{p_{j}}.

(3)

Then for
n\leqslant N/4

d^{n}(\cap_{j=1}^{r}\nu_{j}B_{p_{j}}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})\asymp\min_{p_{i}\leqslant q,\,p_{j}\geqslant q}\nu_{i}^{1-\lambda_{ij}}\nu_{j}^{\lambda_{ij}}.

1

.

Let

5
Application: estimates for the widths of intersections of Sobolev classes

We consider the example from
[
18
]
of the intersection of Sobolev classes on a John domain; the example of the intersection of one-dimensional periodic Sobolev classes (where the smoothness may be noninteger) can be considered similarly.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}
be a bounded domain,
1\leqslant p\leqslant\infty
,
r\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
. Given
f\in L_{1}^{{\rm loc}}(\Omega)
, we denote by
\nabla^{r}f
the vector of all partial derivatives of order
r
(in the sense of distributions). If all its components are elements of
L_{p}(\Omega)
, we say that
f
is an element of the Sobolev space
{\cal W}^{r}_{p}(\Omega)
. The set

W^{r}_{p}(\Omega)=\{f\in{\cal W}^{r}_{p}(\Omega):\;\|\nabla^{r}f\|_{L_{p}(\Omega)}\leqslant 1\}

is the Sobolev class; here
\|\nabla^{r}f\|_{L_{p}(\Omega)}
is the
L_{p}
-norm of the function
|\nabla^{r}f(\cdot)|
.
