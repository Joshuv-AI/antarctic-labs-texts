5 C.F.R. § 532.411 — Details.
Citation: us:cfr:5:532.411
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-532.411
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 532 (Prevailing Rate Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appropriated fund employee detailed to a position other than the position to which appointed shall be paid at the rate of the position to which appointed.
