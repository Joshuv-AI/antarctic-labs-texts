30 C.F.R. § 254.50 — Spill response plans for facilities located in State waters seaward of the coast line.
Citation: us:cfr:30:254.50
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-254.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 254 (Oil-Spill Response Requirements For Facilities Located Seaward Of The Coast Line). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owners or operators of facilities located in State waters seaward of the coast line must submit a spill-response plan to BSEE for approval. You may choose one of three methods to comply with this requirement. The three methods are described in §§ 254.51, 254.52, and 254.53.
