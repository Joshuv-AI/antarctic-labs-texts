5 C.F.R. § 2418.18 — Will the FLRA issue a refund if money is erroneously collected on a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:5:2418.18
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2418.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2418 (Flra Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FLRA shall promptly refund to a debtor any amount collected on an FLRA debt when the debt is waived or otherwise found not to be owed to the United States, or as otherwise required by law. Refunds under this part shall not bear interest unless required by law.
