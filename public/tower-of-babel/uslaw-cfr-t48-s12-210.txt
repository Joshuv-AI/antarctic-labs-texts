48 C.F.R. § 12.210 — 12.210 Contract financing.
Citation: us:cfr:48:12.210
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-12.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 12 (Acquisition Of Commercial Products And Commercial Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Customary market practice for some commercial products and commercial services may include buyer contract financing. The contracting officer may offer Government financing in accordance with the policies and procedures in part 32.
