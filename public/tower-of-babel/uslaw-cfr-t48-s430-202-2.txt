48 C.F.R. § 430.202-2 — 430.202-2 Impracticality of submission.
Citation: us:cfr:48:430.202-2
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-430.202-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 430 (Cost Accounting Standards Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, without the power to redelegate, is authorized to determine, in accordance with 48 CFR 9903.202-2, that the Disclosure Statement is impractical to secure and to authorize award without obtaining the Disclosure Statement.
