43 C.F.R. § 429.27 — What general information appears in use authorizations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:429.27
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-429.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 429 (Use Of Bureau Of Reclamation Land, Facilities, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each use authorization will contain:
(a) An adequate description of the land, facilities, or waterbodies where the use will occur;
(b) A description of the specific use being authorized together with applicable restrictions or conditions that must be adhered to;
(c) The conditions under which the use authorization may be renewed, terminated, amended, assigned or transferred, and/or have the use fee adjusted; and
(d) Primary points of contact and other terms and conditions.
