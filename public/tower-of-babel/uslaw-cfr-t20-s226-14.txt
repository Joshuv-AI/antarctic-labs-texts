20 C.F.R. § 226.14 — Employee regular annuity rate.
Citation: us:cfr:20:226.14
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-226.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 226 (Computing Employee, Spouse, And Divorced Spouse Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regular annuity rate payable to the employee is the total of the employee tier I, tier II, and vested dual benefit amounts, from §§ 226.10-226.12.
