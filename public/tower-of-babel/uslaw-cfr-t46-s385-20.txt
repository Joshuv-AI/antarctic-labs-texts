46 C.F.R. § 385.20 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:385.20
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-385.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 385 (Research And Development Grant And Cooperative Agreements Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only those definitions needed to understand this part will be defined. Generally, terms defined elsewhere in statutes, OMB circulars, and other Federal requirements will not be restated. Special attention is directed to the definitions in the Standard General Provisions of MarAd's grant and cooperative agreements (See § 385.62).
