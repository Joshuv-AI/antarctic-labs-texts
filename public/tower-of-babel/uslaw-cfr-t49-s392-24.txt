49 C.F.R. § 392.24 — Emergency signals; flame-producing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:392.24
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 392 (Driving Of Commercial Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No driver shall attach or permit any person to attach a lighted fusee or other flame-producing emergency signal to any part of a commercial motor vehicle.
