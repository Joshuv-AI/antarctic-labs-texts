25 C.F.R. § 293.6 — Who can submit a compact or amendment?
Citation: us:cfr:25:293.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-293.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 293 (Class Iii Tribal-State Gaming Compacts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either party (Tribe or State) to a compact or amendment can submit the compact or amendment to the Secretary for review and approval.
