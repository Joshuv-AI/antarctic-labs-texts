19 U.S.C. § 2024 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:19:2024
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2024
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 8 (Automotive Products); Subchapter III (Tariff Adjustment And Other Adjustment Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary from time to time to carry out the provisions of this subchapter, which sums are authorized to be appropriated to remain available until expended.
