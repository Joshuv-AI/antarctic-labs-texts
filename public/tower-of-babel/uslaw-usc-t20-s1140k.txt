20 U.S.C. § 1140k — Definition of student with a print disability
Citation: us:usc:20:1140k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1140k
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part D (Programs To Provide Students With Disabilities With A Quality Higher Education); Subpart 3 (Commission On Accessible Materials; Programs To Support Improved Access To Materials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subpart, the term “student with a print disability” means a student with a disability who experiences barriers to accessing instructional material in nonspecialized formats, including an individual described in section 121(d)(2) of title 17.
