41 C.F.R. § 304-6.5 — -6.5 Handling statutorily protected information.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-6.5
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-6.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-6 (6—Payment Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information that is protected by statute from disclosure to the public should not be reported. However, if agencies omit otherwise reportable information from the OGE Form 1353 or SF 326 because the information may not be disclosed, agencies must notify OGE unless otherwise prohibited by law and, if requested by the Director of OGE, make the information available for inspection by an OGE employee with the requisite clearance.
