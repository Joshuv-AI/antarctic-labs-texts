47 C.F.R. § 2.1207 — Examination of imported equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:47:2.1207
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-2.1207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 2 (Frequency Allocations And Radio Treaty Matters; General Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to determine compliance with its regulations, Commission representatives may examine or test any radio frequency device that is imported. If such radio frequency device has already entered the U.S., the ultimate consignee or subsequent owners of that device must, upon request, made within one year of the date of entry, make that device available for examination or testing by the Commission.
