22 C.F.R. § 224.41 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:22:224.41
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-224.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 224 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the A.I.D. Administrator.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the A.I.D. Administrator.
