25 C.F.R. § 171.220 — What must I do to my farm unit to receive irrigation service?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.220
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must meet the following requirements for us to provide service:
(a) Put water we deliver to authorized uses;
(b) Make sure your farm ditch has sufficient capacity to carry the water we deliver; and
(c) Properly operate, maintain, and rehabilitate your farm ditch.
