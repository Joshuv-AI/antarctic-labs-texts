26 U.S.C. § 5202 — Supervision of operations
Citation: us:usc:26:5202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5202
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter C (Operation Of Distilled Spirits Plants); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All operations on the premises of a distilled spirits plant shall be conducted under such supervision and controls (including the use of Government locks and seals) as the Secretary shall by regulations prescribe.
