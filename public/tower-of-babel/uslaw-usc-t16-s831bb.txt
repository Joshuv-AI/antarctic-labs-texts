16 U.S.C. § 831bb — Reservation of right to amend or repeal
Citation: us:usc:16:831bb
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section831bb
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12A (Tennessee Valley Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this chapter is expressly declared and reserved, but no such amendment or repeal shall operate to impair the obligation of any contract made by said Corporation under any power conferred by this chapter.
