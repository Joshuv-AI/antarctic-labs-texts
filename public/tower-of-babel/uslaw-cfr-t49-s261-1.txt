49 C.F.R. § 261.1 — Cross-reference to credit assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:261.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-261.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 261 (Credit Assistance For Surface Transportation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in 49 CFR part 80 shall be followed in complying with the requirements of this part. Title 49 CFR part 80 implements the Transportation Infrastructure Finance and Innovation Act of 1998, secs. 1501 et seq., Pub. L. 105-178, 112 Stat. 107, 241.
