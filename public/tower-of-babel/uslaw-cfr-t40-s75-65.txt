40 C.F.R. § 75.65 — Opacity reports.
Citation: us:cfr:40:75.65
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-75.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 75 (Continuous Emission Monitoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator or designated representative shall report excess emissions of opacity recorded under § 75.57(f) to the applicable State or local air pollution control agency.
