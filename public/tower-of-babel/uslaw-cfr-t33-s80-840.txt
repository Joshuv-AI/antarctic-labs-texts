33 C.F.R. § 80.840 — Sabine Pass, TX to Galveston, TX.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.840
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.840
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A line drawn from the Sabine Pass East Jetty Light to the seaward end of the Sabine Pass West Jetty.
(b) Lines drawn across the small boat passes through the Sabine Pass East and West Jetties.
(c) A line formed by the centerline of the highway bridge over Rollover Pass at Gilchrist.
