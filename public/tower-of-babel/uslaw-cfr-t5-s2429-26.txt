5 C.F.R. § 2429.26 — Other documents.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2429.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2429 (Miscellaneous And General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Authority or the General Counsel, or their designated representatives, as appropriate, may in their discretion grant leave to file other documents as they deem appropriate.
(b) A copy of such other documents shall be served on the other parties.
