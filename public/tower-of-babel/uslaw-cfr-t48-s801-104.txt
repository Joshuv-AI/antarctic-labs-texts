48 C.F.R. § 801.104 — 801.104 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:801.104
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-801.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 801 (Department Of Veterans Affairs Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAR and the VAAR apply to all FAR-based VA actions using appropriated funds unless otherwise specified in this regulation. Supply Fund monies (38 U.S.C. 8121) and General Post Funds (38 U.S.C. 8302) are appropriated funds.
