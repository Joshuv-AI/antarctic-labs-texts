46 C.F.R. § 13.117 — Re-issuance of expired tank vessel endorsements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:13.117
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-13.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 13 (Certification Of Tank Vessel Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever an applicant applies for re-issuance of any tank vessel endorsement more than 12 months after expiration of the previous endorsement, the applicant must meet the requirements for an original endorsement.
