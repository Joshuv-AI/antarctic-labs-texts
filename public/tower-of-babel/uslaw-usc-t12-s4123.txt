12 U.S.C. § 4123 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:12:4123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4123
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 42 (Low-Income Housing Preservation And Resident Homeownership); Subchapter I (Prepayment Of Mortgages Insured Under National Housing Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter, or the application of such provision with respect to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of this Act, and the application of such provision to any other person or circumstance, shall not be affected by such holding.
