18 U.S.C. § 3150 — Applicability to a case removed from a State court
Citation: us:usc:18:3150
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3150
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 207 (Release And Detention Pending Judicial Proceedings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter apply to a criminal case removed to a Federal court from a State court.
