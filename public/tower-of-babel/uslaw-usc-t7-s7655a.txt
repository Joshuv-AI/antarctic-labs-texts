7 U.S.C. § 7655a — Food safety education initiatives

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 103 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education Reform); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions); Part B (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Initiative authorized
The Secretary may carry out a food safety education program to educate the public and persons in the fresh produce industry about—
(1)
scientifically proven practices for reducing microbial pathogens on fresh produce; and
(2)
methods of reducing the threat of cross-contamination of fresh produce through sanitary handling practices.
(b) Cooperation
The Secretary may carry out the education program in cooperation with public and private partners.
(c) Authorization of appropriations
There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this section $1,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2008 through 2023, to remain available until expended.
