42 C.F.R. § 413.355 — Additional payment: QIO reimbursement for cost of sending records electronically or by photocopy and mailing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:413.355
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-413.355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 413 (Principles Of Reasonable Cost Reimbursement; Payment For End-Stage Renal Disease Services; Prospectively Determined Payment Rates For Skilled Nursing Facilities; Payment For Acute Kidney Injury Dialysis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An additional payment is made to a skilled nursing facility in accordance with § 476.78 of this chapter for the costs of sending requested patient records to the QIO in electronic format, by facsimile, or by photocopying and mailing.
