48 C.F.R. § 25.901 — 25.901 Policy.

Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-25.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 25 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

United States laws impose duties on foreign supplies imported into the customs territory of the United States. Certain exemptions from these duties are available to Government agencies. Agencies must use these exemptions when the anticipated savings to appropriated funds will outweigh the administrative costs associated with processing required documentation.
