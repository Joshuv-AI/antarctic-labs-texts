43 U.S.C. § 899 — Limitation of quantity to be conveyed
Citation: us:usc:43:899
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section899
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 21 (Grants In Aid Of Railroads And Wagon Roads). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No more lands shall be certified or conveyed to any State or to any corporation or individual, for the benefit of either of the companies herein mentioned, where it shall appear to the Secretary of the Interior that such transfers may create an excess over the quantity of lands to which such State, corporation, or individual would be rightfully entitled.
