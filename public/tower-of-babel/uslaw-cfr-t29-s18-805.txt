29 C.F.R. § 18.805 — Hearsay within hearsay.
Citation: us:cfr:29:18.805
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-18.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 18 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Administrative Hearings Before The Office Of Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hearsay included within hearsay is not excluded under the hearsay rule if each part of the combined statements conforms with an exception to the hearsay rule provided in these rules.
