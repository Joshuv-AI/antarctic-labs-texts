49 C.F.R. § 663.27 — Pre-award purchaser's requirements certification.
Citation: us:cfr:49:663.27
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-663.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 663 (Pre-Award And Post-Delivery Audits Of Rolling Stock Purchases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, a pre-award purchaser's requirements certification is a certification a recipient keeps on file that—
(a) The rolling stock the recipient is contracting for is the same product described in the purchaser's solicitation specification; and
(b) The proposed manufacturer is a responsible manufacturer with the capability to produce a vehicle that meets the recipient's specification set forth in the recipient's solicitation.
