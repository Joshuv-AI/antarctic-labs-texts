19 U.S.C. § 4086 — Termination of relief authority
Citation: us:usc:19:4086
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4086
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 26 (Dominican Republic-Central America Free Trade); Subchapter III (Relief From Imports); Part B (Textile And Apparel Safeguard Measures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No import relief may be provided under this part with respect to any article after the date that is 5 years after the date on which the Agreement enters into force.
