43 C.F.R. § 37.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:37.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-37.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 37 (Cave Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide the basis for identifying and managing significant caves on Federal lands administered by the Secretary of the Interior.
