41 C.F.R. § 101-29.208 — -29.208 Commercial item description (CID).
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-29.208
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-29.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-29 (29—Federal Product Descriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A commercial item description is an indexed, simplified product description that describes by function or performance characteristics of available, acceptable commercial products that will satisfy the Government's needs. These documents are issued or controlled by the General Services Administration and are listed in the GSA “Index of Federal Specifications, Standards and Commercial Item Descriptions.”
