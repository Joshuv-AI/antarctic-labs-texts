24 C.F.R. § 985.109 — Default under the Annual Contributions Contract (ACC).
Citation: us:cfr:24:985.109
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-985.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 985 (Section 8 Management Assessment Program (Semap) And Small Rural Pha Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD may determine that an PHA's failure to correct identified SEMAP deficiencies or to prepare and implement a corrective action plan required by HUD constitutes a default under the ACC.
