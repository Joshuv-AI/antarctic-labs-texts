42 U.S.C. § 7923 — Limitation of contractual authority
Citation: us:usc:42:7923
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7923
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 88 (Uranium Mill Tailings Radiation Control); Subchapter I (Remedial Action Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority under this subchapter to enter into contracts or other obligations requiring the United States to make outlays may be exercised only to the extent provided in advance in annual authorization and appropriation Acts.
