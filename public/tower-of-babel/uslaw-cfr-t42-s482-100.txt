42 C.F.R. § 482.100 — Condition of participation: Organ procurement.
Citation: us:cfr:42:482.100
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-482.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 482 (Conditions Of Participation For Hospitals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transplant program must ensure that the hospital in which it operates has a written agreement for the receipt of organs with an OPO designated by the Secretary that identifies specific responsibilities for the hospital and for the OPO with respect to organ recovery and organ allocation.
