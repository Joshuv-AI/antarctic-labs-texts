21 C.F.R. § 82.304 — FD&C Red No. 4.
Citation: us:cfr:21:82.304
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-82.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 82 (Listing Of Certified Provisionally Listed Colors And Specifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The color additive FD&C Red No. 4 shall conform in identity and specifications to the requirements of § 74.1304(a)(1) and (b) of this chapter. FD&C Red No. 4 is restricted to use in externally applied drugs and cosmetics.
