50 U.S.C. § 4013 — Interlocutory orders
Citation: us:usc:50:4013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4013
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter VI (Administrative Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An interlocutory order issued by a court under this chapter may be revoked, modified, or extended by that court upon its own motion or otherwise, upon notification to affected parties as required by the court.
