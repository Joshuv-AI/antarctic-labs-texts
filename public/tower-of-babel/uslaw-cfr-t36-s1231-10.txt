36 C.F.R. § 1231.10 — Who has the authority to approve the transfer of records from the custody of one executive agency to another?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1231.10
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1231.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1231 (Transfer Of Records From The Custody Of One Executive Agency To Another). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NARA must approve in writing the transfer of records from the custody of one executive agency to another, except as provided in § 1231.18(a).
