28 C.F.R. § 33.10 — State government.
Citation: us:cfr:28:33.10
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-33.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 33 (Bureau Of Justice Assistance Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All states are eligible to apply for and receive block grants. Section 404 of the Act. State, as defined in the statute, means any state of the United States and includes the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, and the Virgin Islands. Section 901(a)(2) of the Act.
