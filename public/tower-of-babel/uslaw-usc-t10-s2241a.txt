10 U.S.C. § 2241a — Prohibition on use of funds for publicity or propaganda purposes within the United States
Citation: us:usc:10:2241a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2241a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 134 (Miscellaneous Administrative Provisions); Subchapter I (Miscellaneous Authorities, Prohibitions, And Limitations On The Use Of Appropriated Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available to the Department of Defense may not be obligated or expended for publicity or propaganda purposes within the United States not otherwise specifically authorized by law.
