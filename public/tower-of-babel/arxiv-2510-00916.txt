New variants of N = 3 , 4 N=3,4 superconformal mechanics


Abstract
We construct superconformal mechanics with N = 3 N=3 and N = 4 N=4 supersymmetries that were inspired by analogies with the supersymmetric Schwarzian mechanics. The Schwarzian, being another system with superconformal symmetry, provides insight into the field content of supersymmetric mechanics, most notably, on the number and properties of the fermionic fields involved. Adding more fermionic fields (four in the N = 3 N=3 case and eight in the N = 4 N=4 case) made it possible to construct systems possessing maximal superconformal symmetries in N = 3 N=3 and N = 4 N=4 , namely O ​ S ​ p ​ ( 3 | 2 ) OSp(3|2) and D ⁡ ( 1 , 2 , α ) D(1,2;\alpha) . In the case of N = 4 N=4 supersymmetry, we explicitly construct a new variant of N = 4 N=4 superconformal mechanics in which all bosonic subalgebras of D ⁡ ( 1 , 2 , α ) D(1,2;\alpha) superalgebra have bosonic realization. In addition, the constructed systems involve s ​ o ​ ( 3 ) so(3) currents whose parametrization is not fixed, which allows to consider different underlying geometries.

For example, even the simplest case of

Here the angular part of the Hamiltonian

To construct three supercharges

\quad\big\{\psi_{i},\psi_{j}\big\}={\rm i}\;\delta_{ij}\,,\quad\big\{\chi,\chi\big\}={\rm i}\,.

The superconformal charges of the

SO(3)
symmetry, one can expect the metric to describe a
3
-sphere. This becomes clear after field redefinition:

\displaystyle y_{i}=\frac{z_{i}}{1-\frac{1}{16}z^{2}},\;\;u=2\log r,\;\;\lambda_{i}=\frac{1}{r}\psi_{i},\;\;{\widetilde{\Sigma}}=-\frac{{\rm i}}{r}\chi,\;\;{\tilde{N}}_{ij}=\big(1-\frac{1}{16}z^{2}\big)\delta_{ij}+\frac{1}{8}z_{i}z_{j}+\frac{1}{2}\epsilon_{ijm}z_{m}\;\;\Rightarrow

\displaystyle S_{N=3}=\frac{1}{2}\int dt\left(\dot{r}^{2}+r^{2}\frac{{\dot{z}}_{i}{\dot{z}}_{i}}{\big(1+\frac{1}{16}z^{2}\big)^{2}}-{\rm i}\dot{\psi}_{i}\psi_{i}-{\rm i}{\dot{\chi}}\chi-{\rm i}\big(\epsilon_{ijk}\psi_{j}\psi_{k}+2\chi\psi_{i}\big)\big(\tilde{N}^{-1}\big)_{im}\dot{z}_{m}-r^{-2}\chi\epsilon_{ijk}\psi_{i}\psi_{j}\psi_{k}\right).

(3.21)

The respective Hamiltonian, momenta and Dirac brackets read

\displaystyle H=\frac{p_{r}^{2}}{2}+\frac{1}{2r^{2}}\frac{p_{i}p_{i}}{\left(1+\frac{1}{16}z^{2}\right)^{2}}+\frac{{\rm i}}{2r^{2}}p_{i}{\tilde{N}}_{ij}\big(2\chi\psi_{j}+\epsilon_{jmn}\psi_{m}\psi_{n}\big),

\displaystyle p_{r}=\dot{r},p_{i}=r^{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{16}z^{2}\right)^{-2}{\dot{z}}_{i}-{\rm i}\chi\psi_{k}\big({\tilde{N}}^{-1}\big)_{ki}-\frac{{\rm i}}{2}\epsilon_{mnp}\psi_{n}\psi_{p}\big({\tilde{N}}^{-1}\big)_{mi},

\displaystyle\big\{p_{r},r\big\}=1,\;\;\big\{p_{i},z_{j}\big\}=\delta_{ij},\;\;\big\{\psi_{i},\psi_{j}\big\}={\rm i}\delta_{ij},\;\;\big\{\chi,\chi\big\}={\rm i}.

(3.22)

The transformation laws of the components are remarkably simple:

\displaystyle\delta f|_{\theta\rightarrow 0}=(\epsilon_{i}D_{i}f)|_{\theta\rightarrow 0}\Rightarrow\;\;\delta r={\rm i}\epsilon_{i}\psi_{i},\;\;\delta\psi_{i}=-\epsilon_{i}p_{r}-\frac{\epsilon_{j}\epsilon_{jik}{\tilde{N}}_{nk}p_{n}}{r},

\displaystyle\delta z_{i}=\frac{{\rm i}\epsilon_{j}}{r}\big(-\chi{\tilde{N}}_{ij}+{\tilde{N}}_{ik}\epsilon_{kjm}\psi_{m}\big),\;\;\delta\chi=\frac{1}{r}\epsilon_{i}{\tilde{N}}_{ji}p_{j}.

(3.23)

The transformation laws of the fermions (
3
) contain
p_{i}
and
z_{i}
only as a part of combination
J_{i}=-{\tilde{N}}_{ji}p_{j}
. As can be checked, these currents form the
so(3)
algebra:

\big\{J_{i},J_{j}\big\}=\epsilon_{ijk}J_{k}.

(3.24)

The transformations (
3
) can be reproduced via the Dirac bracket:

\delta f={\rm i}\big\{\epsilon_{i}Q_{i},f\big\},\;\;Q_{i}=p_{r}\psi_{i}+\frac{1}{r}J_{i}\chi+\frac{1}{r}\epsilon_{ijk}J_{j}\psi_{k}.

(3.25)

These supercharges coincide with ones constructed in Section 2 for a particular choice of
J_{i}
.

{{\cal N}}=4

Among the constructed superconformal mechanics, the different variants with
{{\cal N}}=4

The most general
{{\cal N}}=4,d=1

D(1,2;\alpha)

[
23
,
24
]

[
9
]
and references therein).
However, most of the realizations were based on one or another fixed
type of the irreducible
{{\cal N}}=4,d=1
supermultiplet. Only recently, the study of superconformal systems including some pairs of such multiplets was initiated in
[
14
]
and then continued in

[
17
]
. However, even in these generalizations only one
su(2)
subgroup of
R

su(2)

In this section we construct supercharges and Hamiltonian of

{{\cal N}}=4
superconformal mechanics with both
su(2)
subgroups having the bosonic core.

4.1
Basic ingredients

To construct
{{\cal N}}=4
superconformal mechanics with
D(1,2;\alpha)
dynamical supersymmetry, let us introduce the following set of bosonic and fermionic fields:

•

the bosonic field
r

,
to realize the conformal

symmetry as in (

•

two triplets of the bosonic currents

and

\left\{J^{ab},\,J^{cd}\right\}=-\left(\epsilon^{ac}J^{bd}+\epsilon^{bd}J^{ac}\right),\quad\left\{T^{ij},\,T^{kl}\right\}=-\left(\epsilon^{ik}T^{jl}+\epsilon^{jl}T^{ik}\right).

(4.1)

We do not fix the parameterization of these
su(2)
subalgebras.

•

eight fermionic fields
\psi^{ia}
and
\sigma^{ia}

\left\{\psi^{ia},\psi^{jb}\right\}=2{\rm i}\,\epsilon^{ij}\epsilon^{ab},\quad\left\{\sigma^{ia},\sigma^{jb}\right\}=2{\rm i}\,\epsilon^{ij}\epsilon^{ab}.

(4.2)

From these fermions one can construct four
su(2)

\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{ab}=\frac{{\rm i}}{4}\psi^{ia}\psi_{i}^{b},\quad\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ab}=\frac{{\rm i}}{4}\sigma^{ia}\sigma_{i}^{b},\quad\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{ij}=\frac{{\rm i}}{4}\psi^{ia}\psi^{j}_{a},\quad\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ij}=\frac{{\rm i}}{4}\sigma^{ia}\sigma^{j}_{a}.

(4.3)

All these currents obey the same brackets as in (
4.1
).

•

From the fermionic fields
\psi^{ia}
and
\sigma^{ia}
one may construct additional currents

\widehat{V}^{ab}=\frac{{\rm i}}{8}\left(\psi^{ia}\sigma_{i}^{b}+\psi^{ib}\sigma_{i}^{a}\right),\;\widehat{W}^{ij}=\frac{{\rm i}}{8}\left(\psi^{ia}\sigma^{j}_{a}+\psi^{ja}\sigma^{i}_{a}\right),

(4.4)

which span two

algebras together with the generators
\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{ab}+\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ab}
and
\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{ij}+\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ij}

\displaystyle\left\{\widehat{V}^{ab},\,\widehat{V}^{cd}\right\}=-\frac{1}{4}\left(\epsilon^{ac}(\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{bd}+\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{bd})+\epsilon^{bd}(\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{ac}+\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ac})\right),

\displaystyle\left\{\widehat{W}^{ij},\,\widehat{W}^{kl}\right\}=-\frac{1}{4}\left(\epsilon^{ik}(\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{jl}+\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{jl})+\epsilon^{jl}(\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{ik}+\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ik})\right).

(4.5)

•

Finally, we have the
U(1)
current

\widehat{Z}=\frac{i}{4}\psi^{ia}\sigma_{ia}.

(4.6)

Coming back to a possible superfield description of our set of fields note
that the fields

{{\cal N}}=4
superfield

(1,4,3)

(3,4,1)

\{T^{ij},{\dot{\sigma}}{}^{ia},{\cal A}\}

All together, these components fit well into the unrestricted
N=4
superfield
r

4.2
Supercharges and Hamiltonian

Using the Anzatz (
2.7

{{\cal N}}=4
superconformal mechanics can be found to be

\displaystyle Q^{ia}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle p_{r}\,\psi^{ia}-\frac{1}{r}\,2\alpha\,\left(T^{ij}+\frac{1}{3}\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{ij}+\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ij}\right)\psi_{j}{}^{a}+\frac{1}{r}2\alpha\left(T^{ij}+\frac{2}{3}\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ij}\right)\sigma_{j}{}^{a}+

(4.7)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r}2(1+\alpha)\left(J^{ab}+\frac{1}{3}\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{ab}+\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ab}\right)\psi^{i}{}_{b}+\frac{1}{r}2(1+\alpha)\left(J^{ab}+\frac{2}{3}\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ab}\right)\sigma^{i}{}_{b}+\frac{M}{r}\sigma^{ia}.

They obey the standard brackets

\left\{Q^{ia},Q^{jb}\right\}=4\,{\rm i}\,\epsilon^{ij}\epsilon^{ab}H

(4.8)

where the Hamiltonian

\displaystyle H

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}p_{r}^{2}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\left[2(1+\alpha)^{2}J^{ab}J_{ab}+2\alpha^{2}T^{ij}T_{ij}-2(1+\alpha)J^{ab}\left((\widehat{J}_{\psi})_{ab}-(\widehat{J}_{\sigma}){}_{ab}+2\widehat{V}_{ab}\right)\right]+

(4.9)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{2}}\left[2\alpha T^{ij}\left((\widehat{T}_{\psi})_{ij}-(\widehat{T}_{\sigma})_{ij}-2\widehat{W}_{ij}\right)-\frac{1+\alpha}{3}\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{ab}\left((\widehat{J}_{\psi})_{ab}+6(\widehat{J}_{\sigma})_{ab}\right)+\frac{\alpha}{3}\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{ij}\left((\widehat{T}_{\psi})_{ij}+6(\widehat{T}_{\sigma})_{ij}\right)\right]+

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{2}}\left[(1+\alpha)\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ab}\left((\widehat{J}_{\sigma})_{ab}-\frac{8}{3}\widehat{V}_{ab}\right)-\alpha\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ij}\left((\widehat{T}_{\sigma})_{ij}+\frac{8}{3}\widehat{W}_{ij}\right)+2M\widehat{Z}+\frac{M^{2}}{2}\right].

To visualize the dynamical symmetry of the system, one has to calculate the Poisson brackets between

S^{ia}=r\psi^{ia}.

(4.10)

\left\{S^{ia},Q^{jb}\right\}=4{\rm i}\,\epsilon^{ij}\epsilon^{ab}D+4\,{\rm i}\alpha\epsilon^{ab}\left(T^{ij}+\widehat{T}_{\psi}^{ij}+\widehat{T}_{\sigma}^{ij}\right)-4\,{\rm i}\,(1+\alpha)\epsilon^{ij}\left(J^{ab}+\widehat{J}_{\psi}^{ab}+\widehat{J}_{\sigma}^{ij}\right).

5
Conclusion

In this article we have considered the construction of superconformal mechanical systems with
N=3
and
N=4
supersymmetries, that were inspired by analogies with the supersymmetric Schwarzian mechanics
[
6
,
13
]
. The Schwarzian, being another system with superconformal symmetry, provides insight into the field content of supersymmetric mechanics, most notably, into the number and properties of the fermionic fields involved. Adding more fermionic fields (four in the
N=3
case and eight in the
N=4
case) made it possible to construct systems possessing maximal superconformal symmetries in
N=3
and
N=4
, namely
OSp(3|2)
and
D(1,2;\alpha)
. In addition, the constructed systems involve
so(3)
currents whose parametrization is not fixed, which allows one to consider different underlying geometries. The construction of the superfield
N=3
action is also very similar to the Schwarzian one, including the group element, superfield constraints and the superfield Lagrangian.

Our study opens up possibilities of constructing new
superconformal mechanical systems, especially ones with extended

{{\cal N}}=5,6
supersymmetries, which could be analogues of the generalized Schwarzians
[
28
]
.
