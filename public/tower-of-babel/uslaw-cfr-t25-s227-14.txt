25 C.F.R. § 227.14 — Government reserves right to purchase oil and gas.
Citation: us:cfr:25:227.14
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-227.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 227 (Leasing Of Certain Lands In Wind River Indian Reservation, Wyoming, For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In time of war or other public emergency any of the executive departments of the United States Government shall have the option to purchase at the posted market price on the date of sale all or any part of the minerals produced under any lease.
