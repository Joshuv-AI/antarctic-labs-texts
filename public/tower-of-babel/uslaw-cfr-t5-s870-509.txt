5 C.F.R. § 870.509 — Transfers to international organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:870.509
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-870.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 870 (Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee transferred to an international organization may continue life insurance coverage as provided in 5 U.S.C. 3582. Regulations governing these transfers are in part 352 of this title.
