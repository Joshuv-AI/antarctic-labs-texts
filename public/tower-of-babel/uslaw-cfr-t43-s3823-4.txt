43 C.F.R. § 3823.4 — Withdrawal from operation of the mining laws.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3823.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3823.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3820 (Areas Subject To Special Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective at midnight, December 31, 1983, subject to valid rights then existing, the minerals in lands within National Forest Wilderness are withdrawn from the operation of the mining laws by virtue of the provisions of section 4(d)(3) of the Wilderness Act.
