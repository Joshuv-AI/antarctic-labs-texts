10 C.F.R. § 170.31 — Schedule of fees for materials licenses and other regulatory services, including inspections, and import and export licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:170.31
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-170.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 170 (Fees For Facilities, Materials, Import And Export Licenses, And Other Regulatory Services Under The Atomic Energy Act Of 1954, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants for materials licenses, import and export licenses, and other regulatory services, and holders of materials licenses or import and export licenses shall pay fees for the following categories of services. For those fee categories identified to be subject to full cost fees, full cost fees will be assessed for all licensing and inspection activities, unless otherwise indicated.
