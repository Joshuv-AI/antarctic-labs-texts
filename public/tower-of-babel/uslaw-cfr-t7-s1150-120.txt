7 C.F.R. § 1150.120 — Imported dairy product.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1150.120
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1150.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1150 (Dairy Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Imported dairy product means any product that is imported into the United States under any of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) classification numbers listed in § 1150.152(b)(1).
