33 C.F.R. § 88.05 — Law enforcement vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:88.05
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-88.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 88 (Annex V: Pilot Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Law enforcement vessels may display a flashing blue light when engaged in direct law enforcement or public safety activities. This light must be located so that it does not interfere with the visibility of the vessel's navigation lights.
(b) The blue light described in this section may be displayed by law enforcement vessels of the United States and the States and their political subdivisions.
