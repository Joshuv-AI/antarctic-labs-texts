41 C.F.R. § 105-55.024 — -55.024 Consideration of tax consequences to the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-55.024
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-55.024
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-55 (55—Collection Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In negotiating a compromise, the General Services Administration (GSA) may consider the tax consequences to the Government. In particular, GSA may consider requiring a waiver of tax-loss-carry-forward and tax-loss-carry-back rights of the debtor. For information on discharge of indebtedness reporting requirements see § 105-55.030.
