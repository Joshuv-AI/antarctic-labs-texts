49 C.F.R. § 511.61 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:511.61
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-511.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 511 (Adjudicative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the procedures and requirements necessary to obtain a settlement of a case of violation of section 507 (1) or (2) of the Motor Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act, as amended, Pub. L. 94-163, 89 Stat. 911 (15 U.S.C. section 2007(1)(2)). No settlement of such cases may be had except as in accordance with this subpart.
