13 C.F.R. § 107.1220 — Requirement for Licensee to file quarterly financial statements.
Citation: us:cfr:13:107.1220
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-107.1220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 107 (Small Business Investment Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leveraged Licensees must submit to SBA a Financial Statement on SBA Form 468 (Short Form) as of the close of each quarter of your fiscal year (other than the fourth quarter, which is covered by your annual filing of Form 468 under § 107.630(a)). You must file this form within 45 days after the close of the quarter. You will not be eligible for a draw if you are not in compliance with this section.
