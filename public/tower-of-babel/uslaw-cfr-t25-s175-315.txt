25 C.F.R. § 175.315 — What will happen if I do not pay my bill?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.315
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you do not pay your bill prior to the close of business on the due date, your bill will be past due.
(b) If your bill is past due we may:
(1) Disconnect your service; and
(2) Not reconnect your service until your bill, including any applicable fees, is paid in full.
(c) Specific regulations regarding non-payment can be found in 25 CFR 143.5(c).
