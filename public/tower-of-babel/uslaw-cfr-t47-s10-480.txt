47 C.F.R. § 10.480 — Language support.
Citation: us:cfr:47:10.480
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-10.480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 10 (Wireless Emergency Alerts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating CMS Providers are required to transmit WEA Alert Messages that are issued in the Spanish language or that contain Spanish-language characters.
