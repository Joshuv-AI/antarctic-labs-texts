15 U.S.C. § 6711 — Functional regulation of insurance
Citation: us:usc:15:6711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6711
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 93 (Insurance); Subchapter I (State Regulation Of Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The insurance activities of any person (including a national bank exercising its power to act as agent under section 92 of title 12) shall be functionally regulated by the States, subject to section 6701 of this title.
