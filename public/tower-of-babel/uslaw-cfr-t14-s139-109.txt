14 C.F.R. § 139.109 — Duration of certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:14:139.109
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-139.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 139 (Certification Of Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Airport Operating Certificate issued under this part is effective until the certificate holder surrenders it or the certificate is suspended or revoked by the Administrator.
