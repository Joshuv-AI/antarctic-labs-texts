16 U.S.C. § 609 — Permits to cut and remove timber; citizens of Modoc County, California
Citation: us:usc:16:609
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section609
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be lawful for the Secretary of the Interior to grant permits under the provisions of section 607 of this title, to citizens of Modoc County, California, to cut timber in the State of Nevada for agricultural, mining, or other domestic purposes, and to remove the timber so cut to Modoc County, State of California.
