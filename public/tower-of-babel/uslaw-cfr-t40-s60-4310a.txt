40 C.F.R. § 60.4310a — a What stationary combustion turbines are not subject to this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.4310a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.4310a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An integrated gasification combined cycle electric utility steam generating unit subject to subpart Da of this part is not subject to this subpart.
(b) A stationary combustion turbine used in a combustion turbine test cell/stand, as defined in § 60.4420a, is not subject to this subpart.
(c) If any solid fuel is combusted in the HRSG, the HRSG is not subject to this subpart.
(d) Combustion turbines subject to title II of the Clean Air Act are not subject to this subpart.
