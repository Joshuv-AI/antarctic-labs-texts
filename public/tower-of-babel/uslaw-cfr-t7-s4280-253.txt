7 C.F.R. § 4280.253 — Award approval.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.253
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.253
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency will approve a Federal Award once the Applicant has met all the conditions specified in the LOC. The approval will be conveyed through the execution of a Form RD 4280-2, which is the FAA, and provides all the terms of the Federal Award. Once the Federal Award has been approved, the Recipient must begin work on the Project and incur costs as described in the approved work plan and budget, which are attached to Form RD 4280-2. Costs must be allowable and be incurred during the approved Period of Performance.
