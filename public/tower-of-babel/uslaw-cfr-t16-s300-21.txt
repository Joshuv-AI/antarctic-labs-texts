16 C.F.R. § 300.21 — Marking of samples, swatches or specimens.
Citation: us:cfr:16:300.21
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-300.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 300 (Rules And Regulations Under The Wool Products Labeling Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where samples, swatches or specimens of wool products subject to the act were used to promote or effect sales of such wool products in commerce, said samples, swatches and specimens, as well as the products themselves, shall be labeled or marked to show their respective fiber contents and other information required by law.
