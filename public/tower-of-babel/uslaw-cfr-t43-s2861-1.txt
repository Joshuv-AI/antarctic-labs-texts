43 C.F.R. § 2861.1 — What requirements of part 2800 apply to my grant?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2861.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2861.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2860 (Communications Uses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grants issued under this part must comply with the requirements of part 2800, except as otherwise described in this part.
