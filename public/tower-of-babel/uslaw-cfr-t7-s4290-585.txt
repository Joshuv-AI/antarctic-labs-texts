7 C.F.R. § 4290.585 — Voluntary decrease in RBIC's Regulatory Capital.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.585
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.585
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must obtain the Agency's prior written approval to reduce your Regulatory Capital by more than two percent in any fiscal year. At all times, you must retain sufficient Regulatory Capital to meet the minimum capital requirements in the Act and § 4290.210, and sufficient Leverageable Capital to avoid having excess Leverage in violation of section 384E(d) of the Act.
