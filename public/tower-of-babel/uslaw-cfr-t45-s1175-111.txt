45 C.F.R. § 1175.111 — Notice.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1175.111
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1175.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1175 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The National Endowment For The Humanities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency shall make available to employees, applicants, participants, beneficiaries, and other interested persons such information regarding the provisions of this part and its applicability to the programs or activities conducted by the agency, and make such information available to them in such manner as the head of the agency finds necessary to apprise such persons of the protections against discrimination assured them by section 504 and this regulation.
