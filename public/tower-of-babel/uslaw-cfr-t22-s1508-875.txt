22 C.F.R. § 1508.875 — May I ask the debarring official to reconsider a decision to debar me?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1508.875
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1508.875
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1508 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, as a debarred person you may ask the debarring official to reconsider the debarment decision or to reduce the time period or scope of the debarment. However, you must put your request in writing and support it with documentation.
