14 C.F.R. § 203.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:203.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-203.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 203 (Waiver Of Warsaw Convention Liability Limits And Defenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all direct U.S. and foreign direct air carriers, except for air taxi operators as defined in part 298 of this chapter that (a) are not commuter air carriers, (b) do not participate in interline agreements, and (c) do not engage in foreign air transportation.
