6 C.F.R. § 7.30 — Documents of permanent historical value.
Citation: us:cfr:6:7.30
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-7.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 7 (Classified National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The original classification authority, to the greatest extent possible, shall declassify classified information contained in records determined to have permanent historical value under 44 U.S.C. 2107 before they are accessioned into the National Archives.
