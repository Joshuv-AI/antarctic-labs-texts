44 C.F.R. § 207.3 — Applicability and eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:44:207.3
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-207.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 207 (Management Costs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only PA and HMGP grantees with PA and HMGP grants awarded pursuant to major disasters and emergencies declared by the President on or after November 13, 2007 are eligible to apply to FEMA for management cost funding under this part.
