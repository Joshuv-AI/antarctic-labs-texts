42 U.S.C. § 19061 — Unmanned aircraft systems technologies
Citation: us:usc:42:19061
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19061
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 163 (Research And Development, Competition, And Innovation); Subchapter III (National Science Foundation For The Future); Part E (Fundamental Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In coordination with the Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration and the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the Director shall carry out a program of research and related activities related to unmanned aircraft system technologies, which may include a prize competition pursuant to section 3719 of title 15 and support for undergraduate and graduate curriculum development.
