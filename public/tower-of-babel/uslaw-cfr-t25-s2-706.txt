25 C.F.R. § 2.706 — When must I file my statement of reasons?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.706
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.706
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit your statement of reasons to the reviewing official and interested parties no later than 10 days after filing your Notice of Appeal.
