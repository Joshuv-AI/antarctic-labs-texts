10 C.F.R. § 71.65 — Additional requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:71.65
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-71.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 71 (Packaging And Transportation Of Radioactive Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, by rule, regulation, or order, impose requirements on any licensee, in addition to those established in this part, as it deems necessary or appropriate to protect public health or to minimize danger to life or property.
