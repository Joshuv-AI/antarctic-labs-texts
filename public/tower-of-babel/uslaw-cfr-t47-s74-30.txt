47 C.F.R. § 74.30 — Antenna structure, marking and lighting.
Citation: us:cfr:47:74.30
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-74.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 74 (Experimental Radio, Auxiliary, Special Broadcast And Other Program Distributional Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of part 17 of the FCC rules (Construction, Marking, and Lighting of Antenna Structures) require certain antenna structures to be painted and/or lighted in accordance with the provisions of §§ 17.47 through 17.56 of the FCC rules.
