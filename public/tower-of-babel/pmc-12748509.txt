Correction to: Long‐Term Outcomes of Peripheral Artery Disease in Veterans: Analysis of the Peripheral Artery Disease Long‐Term Survival Study (PEARLS)
Published: 2025

In the article by Girotra et al, “Long‐Term Outcomes of Peripheral Artery Disease in Veterans: Analysis of the Peripheral Artery Disease Long‐Term Survival Study (PEARLS)” which published online March 21, 2025 (J Am Heart Assoc. 2025;14:e038403. DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.124.038403), a correction was needed.

There was an error in the affiliation information for Drs Mohammad Al‐Garadi, Michael Matheny, and Glenn T. Gobbel. Their affiliations previously read “Vanderbilt University Medical Center, Kansas City, MO (M.A., M.E.M., G.T.G.); Tennessee Valley Health System Veterans Affairs Medical Center, Kansas City, MO (M.A., M.E.M., G.T.G.)”

The corrected affiliation now reads, “Vanderbilt University Medical Center, Nashville, TN (M.A., M.E.M., G.T.G.); Tennessee Valley Health System Veterans Affairs Medical Center, Nashville, TN (M.A., M.E.M., G.T.G.)”

The authors and publisher regret the error.

The online version of the article has been updated and is available here: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.124.038403.
