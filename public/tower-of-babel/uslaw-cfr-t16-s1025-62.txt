16 C.F.R. § 1025.62 — Authority for representation.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1025.62
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1025.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1025 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudicative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual acting in a representative capacity in any adjudicative proceedings may be required by the Presiding Officer or the Commission to show his/her authority to act in such capacity. A regular employee of a party who appears on behalf of the party may be required by the Presiding Officer or the Commission to show his/her authority to so appear.
