7 C.F.R. § 614.13 — Appealability review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:614.13
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-614.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 614 (Nrcs Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If NRCS states that a decision is not adverse to the individual participant, and thus, no right to appeal exists, NRCS will notify the participant that he may seek review of that determination from the NAD Director.
