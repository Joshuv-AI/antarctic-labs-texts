46 C.F.R. § 108.113 — Structural standards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.113
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 108.109, each unit must meet the structural standards of the American Bureau of Shipping's Rules for Building and Classing Offshore Mobile Drilling Units, 1978.
