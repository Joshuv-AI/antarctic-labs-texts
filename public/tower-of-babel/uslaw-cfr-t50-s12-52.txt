50 C.F.R. § 12.52 — Can I file a petition for remission for my abandoned property?
Citation: us:cfr:50:12.52
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-12.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 12 (Seizure And Forfeiture Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may file a petition for remission of abandoned property with the Service and seek the return of property you had voluntarily abandoned, within the time period described in § 12.33. If you have agreed to abandon property, your right to seek relief is limited to whatever process expressly was reserved in the abandonment document you signed.
