5 C.F.R. § 838.133 — Minimum awards.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.133
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payments under this part will not be less than one dollar per month. Any court order that awards a former spouse a portion of an employee annuity or a former spouse survivor annuity in an amount of less than one dollar per month will be treated as an award of an annuity equal to one dollar per month.
