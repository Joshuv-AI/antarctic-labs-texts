48 C.F.R. § 32.600 — 32.600 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.600
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for identifying, collecting, and deferring collection of contract debts (including interest, if applicable). Sections 32.607, 32.608, and 32.610 of this subpart do not apply to claims against common carriers for transportation overcharges and freight and cargo losses (31 U.S.C. 3726).
