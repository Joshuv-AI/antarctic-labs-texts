2 U.S.C. § 4106 — Stationery rooms of House and Senate; specification of classes of articles purchasable
Citation: us:usc:2:4106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4106
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 41 (Congressional Officers And Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Committee on House Oversight of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate, respectively, shall make and issue regulations specifying the classes of articles which may be purchased by or through the stationery rooms of the House and Senate.
