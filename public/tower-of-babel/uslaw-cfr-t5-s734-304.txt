5 C.F.R. § 734.304 — Candidacy for public office.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.304
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee may not run for the nomination or as a candidate for election to partisan political office, except as specified in § 734.207.
