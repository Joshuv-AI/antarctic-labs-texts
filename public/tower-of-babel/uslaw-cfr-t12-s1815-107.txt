12 C.F.R. § 1815.107 — Determination of review requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1815.107
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1815.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1815 (Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In deciding whether to prepare an EIS, the designated Fund official shall determine whether the proposal is one that normally:
(a) Requires an EIS;
(b) Requires an environmental assessment, but not necessarily an EIS; or
(c) Does not require either an EIS or an environmental assessment (categorical exclusion).
