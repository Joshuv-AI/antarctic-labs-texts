49 C.F.R. § 571.4 — Explanation of usage.
Citation: us:cfr:49:571.4
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-571.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 571 (Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The word any, used in connection with a range of values or set of items in the requirements, conditions, and procedures of the standards or regulations in this chapter, means generally the totality of the items or values, any one of which may be selected by the Administration for testing, except where clearly specified otherwise.
