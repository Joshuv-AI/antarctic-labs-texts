15 U.S.C. § 78o–2 — Liabilities arising prior to amendment unaffected
Citation: us:usc:15:78o–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section78o–2
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2B (Securities Exchanges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act shall be deemed to extinguish any liability which may have arisen prior to the effective date of this Act by reason of any violation of section 78o of this title or of any rule or regulation thereunder.
