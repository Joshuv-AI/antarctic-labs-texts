18 U.S.C. § 3615 — Criminal default
Citation: us:usc:18:3615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3615
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 229 (Postsentence Administration); Subchapter B (Fines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, having been sentenced to pay a fine, willfully fails to pay the fine, shall be fined not more than twice the amount of the unpaid balance of the fine or $10,000, whichever is greater, imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
