30 C.F.R. § 735.4 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:30:735.4
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-735.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 735 (Grants For Program Development And Administration And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director shall administer the State grant program for the development, administration, and enforcement of State programs under this part.
(b) The Director or his authorized designee shall receive, review and approve grant applications under this part.
