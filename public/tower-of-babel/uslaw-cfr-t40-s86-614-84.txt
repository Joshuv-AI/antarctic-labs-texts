40 C.F.R. § 86.614-84 — -84 Hearings on suspension, revocation, and voiding of certificates of conformity.
Citation: us:cfr:40:86.614-84
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-86.614-84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 86 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Highway Vehicles And Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 40 CFR part 1068, subpart G, apply if a manufacturer requests a hearing regarding suspension, revocation or voiding of certificates of conformity.
