41 C.F.R. § 304-9.4 — -9.4 Partial expense reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-9.4
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-9.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-9 (9—Contributions And Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may reimburse an employee for training expenses that are not fully paid by a donor, an amount considered sufficient to cover the balance of expenses to the extent authorized by law and regulation, including 5 U.S.C. 4109 and 5 U.S.C. 4110.
