36 U.S.C. § 200105 — Principal office
Citation: us:usc:36:200105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section200105
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2001 (Society Of American Florists And Ornamental Horticulturists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the corporation shall be located in the District of Columbia. However, annual meetings may be held wherever the corporation decides.
