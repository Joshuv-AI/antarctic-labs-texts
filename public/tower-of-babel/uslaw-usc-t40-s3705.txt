40 U.S.C. § 3705 — Safety programs
Citation: us:usc:40:3705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3705
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 37 (Contract Work Hours And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor shall—
(1)
provide for the establishment and supervision of programs for the education and training of employers and employees in the recognition, avoidance, and prevention of unsafe working conditions in employment covered by this chapter; and
(2)
collect reports and data and consult with and advise employers as to the best means of preventing injuries.
