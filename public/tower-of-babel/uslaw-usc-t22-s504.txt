22 U.S.C. § 504 — Transfer of hemisphere territory from one non-American power to another; recognition; consultation with American Republics
Citation: us:usc:22:504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section504
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 10 (Hemispheral Relations); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(1)
The United States would not recognize any transfer, and would not acquiesce in any attempt to transfer, any geographic region of this hemisphere from one non-American power to another non-American power; and
(2)
If such transfer or attempt to transfer should appear likely, the United States shall, in addition to other measures, immediately consult with the other American republics to determine upon the steps which should be taken to safeguard their common interests.
