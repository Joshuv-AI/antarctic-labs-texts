45 C.F.R. § 95.19 — Exceptions to time limits.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 95 (General Administration—Grant Programs (Public Assistance, Medical Assistance And State Children'S Health Insurance Programs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The time limits in §§ 95.7 and 95.10 do not apply to any of the following—
(a) Any claim for an adjustment to prior year costs.
(b) Any claim resulting from an audit exception.
(c) Any claim resulting from a court-ordered retroactive payment.
(d) Any claim for which the Secretary decides there was good cause for the State's not filing it within the time limit.
