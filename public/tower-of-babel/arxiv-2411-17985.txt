Abstract
Let V V be an n n -dimensional vector space over the finite field 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} and let [ V k ] q \left[V\atop k\right]_{q} denote the family of all k k -dimensional subspaces of V V . Let δ d ​ ( ℱ ) \delta_{d}(\mathcal{F}) denote the minimum degree in ℱ \mathcal{F} of all d d -dimensional subspaces. In this paper we show that δ d ​ ( ℱ ) ≤ [ n − d − 1 k − d − 1 ] \delta_{d}(\mathcal{F})\leq\left[n-d-1\atop k-d-1\right] in any intersecting family ℱ ⊆ [ V k ] q \mathcal{F}\subseteq\left[V\atop k\right]_{q} , where k > d ≥ 2 k>d\geq 2 and n ≥ 2 ​ k + 1 n\geq 2k+1 .

\begin{array}[]{rl}&~~~~\frac{\left[k-i\atop d-i\right]^{2}\left[k-r\atop k-i\right]\left[n-i-r\atop k-i\right]}{\left[k-r\atop d-r\right]\left[n-d-r\atop k-d\right]}\\[8.5359pt]

&=\frac{\prod\limits_{j=r}^{r+k-i-1}\left(q^{k-j}-1\right)\cdot\prod\limits_{j=i+r}^{r+d-1}\left(q^{n-j}-1\right)\cdot\prod\limits_{j=r}^{i-1}\left(q^{d-j}-1\right)}{\prod\limits_{j=i}^{d-1}\left(q^{d-j}-1\right)\cdot\prod\limits_{j=r}^{k-1}\left(q^{k-j}-1\right)}\\[8.5359pt]

&=\left[n-i-r\atop d-i\right]\left[d-r\atop i-r\right],\end{array}

the identity we would like to prove is equivalent to

\begin{array}[]{rl}\sum\limits_{i=r}^{d}(-1)^{i-r}q^{\binom{i-r}{2}}\left[d-r\atop i-r\right]\left[n-i-r\atop d-i\right]=q^{(d-r)^{2}}\left[n-d-r\atop d-r\right].\end{array}

(11)

Letting
z
be an indeterminate, for
r\leq d<k
, obviously,

\begin{array}[]{rl}\frac{\prod\limits_{s=0}^{d-r-1}(1-q^{s}z)}{\prod\limits_{s=0}^{n-d-r}(1-q^{s}z)}=\frac{1}{\prod\limits_{s=d-r}^{n-d-r}(1-q^{s}z)},\end{array}

which is equivalent to

\begin{array}[]{rl}\left(\sum\limits_{s=0}^{d-r}(-1)^{s}q^{\binom{s}{2}}\left[d-r\atop s\right]z^{s}\right)\left(\sum\limits_{t=0}^{\infty}\left[n-d-r+t\atop t\right]z^{t}\right)=\sum\limits_{m=0}^{\infty}\left[n-2d+m\atop m\right](q^{d-r}z)^{m}\end{array}

by Lemma
2.3
. Then (
11
) follows from comparing the coefficients of
z^{i-r}\cdot z^{d-i}=z^{d-r}~(r\leq i\leq d)
in the above equality. This completes the proof.
∎

4
Concluding remarks

In the present paper, we obtained a
d
-degree version of the Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem for finite vector spaces. We proved that for
n\geq 2k+1
, the upper bound of the minimum
d
-degree of any intersecting family is
\left[n-d-1\atop k-d-1\right]
. It is very interesting to continue to study the structure of the intersecting family that reaches the upper bound.

In
[
10
]
, Huang and Zhao combined a result from discrete geometry with eigenvalues of the Kneser graph to prove a degree version of Pyber’s result
[
12
]
for cross-intersecting families of sets. It deserves to explore a degree version for cross-intersecting families in vector spaces using spectral graph theory.

\begin{array}[]{rl}a_{0}=\frac{q^{n}-q^{d}}{q^{n}-q^{k}}\left[n-k\atop k\right],~a_{1}=\frac{1-q^{d}}{q^{n}-q^{k}}\left[n-k\atop k\right],\end{array}

\begin{array}[]{rl}b_{0}=q^{d^{2}}\left[k\atop d\right]\left[n-d\atop k-d\right]\left[n-d\atop d\right],~b_{1}=-q^{k+(d-1)^{2}-1}\left[k-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop k-d\right].\end{array}

Then,

\begin{array}[]{rl}&~~~~b_{0}-\frac{a_{0}b_{1}}{a_{1}}\\[8.5359pt]
&=b_{0}\left(1-\frac{a_{0}}{a_{1}}\cdot\frac{b_{1}}{b_{0}}\right)\\[8.5359pt]
&=b_{0}\left(1-\frac{q^{n}-q^{d}}{1-q^{d}}\cdot\frac{-q^{k+(d-1)^{2}-1}\left[k-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop k-d\right]}{q^{d^{2}}\left[k\atop d\right]\left[n-d\atop d\right]\left[n-d\atop k-d\right]}\right)\\[8.5359pt]
&=b_{0}\left(1-\frac{q^{n}-q^{d}}{q^{d}-1}\cdot q^{k-2d}\cdot\frac{q^{d}-1}{q^{k}-1}\cdot\frac{q^{d}-1}{q^{n-d}-1}\cdot\frac{q^{n-k}-1}{q^{n-d}-1}\right)\\[8.5359pt]
&=b_{0}\left(1-q^{k-d}\cdot\frac{q^{d}-1}{q^{k}-1}\cdot\frac{q^{n-k}-1}{q^{n-d}-1}\right)\\[8.5359pt]
&=b_{0}\cdot\frac{(q^{k-d}-1)(q^{n}-1)}{(q^{k}-1)(q^{n-d}-1)},\end{array}

the desired result follows.
∎

Lemma A.3
.

,

Let

[x]_{i}:=\prod\limits_{j=0}^{i-1}(q^{x-j}-1),

S_{i}(n)=\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki+k}[k-1]_{i-1}}{[n-k-1]_{i-1}},

T_{i}(n)=\frac{q^{(k-2d)(i-1)+\binom{i}{2}}[d-1]_{i-1}^{2}[n-k-1]_{i-1}}{[k-1]_{i-1}[n-d-1]_{i-1}^{2}}.

We establish the following Lemmas.

Lemma A.4
.

Let
3\leq i\leq d<k
and
2k\leq n
. Then

(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)-(q^{d}-1)T_{i}(n)<q^{k}-q^{d}

Proof.

For fixed
k
,
d
and
n
, let

\alpha_{i}=\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki+k}[k-1]_{i-1}}{[n-k-1]_{i-1}},

\beta_{i}=\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-di+d}[d-1]_{i-1}}{[n-d-1]_{i-1}}.

Then
S_{i}(n)=\alpha_{i}
,
T_{i}(n)=\frac{\beta_{i}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}}
.
Note that for
3\leq i\leq d<k
,

\frac{\alpha_{i+1}}{\alpha_{i}}=\frac{1-q^{i-k}}{q^{n-k-i}-1}<1.

(21)

Since
\frac{\beta_{i+1}}{\beta_{i}}\cdot\frac{\alpha_{i}}{\alpha_{i+1}}=\frac{(1-q^{i-d})(q^{n-k-i}-1)}{(q^{n-d-i}-1)(1-q^{i-k})}<\frac{q^{n-k-i}-1}{q^{n-d-i}-1}<1
,

\frac{\beta_{i+1}}{\alpha_{i+1}}<\frac{\beta_{i}}{\alpha_{i}}.

(22)

Since
[k-1]_{i-1}>q^{(k-d)(i-1)}[d-1]_{i-1}
,
[n-d-1]_{i}>q^{(k-d)i}[n-k-1]_{i}
and
3\leq i\leq d<k
,

\begin{array}[]{rl}\alpha_{i}-\alpha_{i+1}&=\left(1-\frac{\alpha_{i+1}}{\alpha_{i}}\right)\alpha_{i}\\[8.5359pt]
&\!\!\overset{\eqref{e16}}{=}\left(1-\frac{1-q^{i-k}}{q^{n-k-i}-1}\right)\alpha_{i}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\frac{q^{n-k-i}+q^{i-k}-2}{q^{n-k-i}-1}\alpha_{i}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\frac{q^{n-k-i}+q^{i-k}-2}{q^{n-k-i}-1}\cdot\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki+k}[k-1]_{i-1}}{[n-k-1]_{i-1}}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki+k}[k-1]_{i-1}(q^{n-k-i}+q^{i-k}-2)}{[n-k-1]_{i}}\\[8.5359pt]
&>\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-di+d}[d-1]_{i-1}(q^{n-k-i}+q^{i-k}-2)}{[n-k-1]_{i}}\\[8.5359pt]
&>\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-di+d}[d-1]_{i-1}(q^{n-k-i}+q^{i-k}-2)\cdot q^{(k-d)i}}{[n-d-1]_{i}}\\[8.5359pt]
&>\frac{q^{\binom{i}{2}-di+d}[d-1]_{i-1}(q^{n-d-i}+q^{i-d}-2)}{[n-d-1]_{i}}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\left(1-\frac{1-q^{i-d}}{q^{n-d-i}-1}\right)\beta_{i}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\left(1-\frac{\beta_{i+1}}{\beta_{i}}\right)\beta_{i}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\beta_{i}-\beta_{i+1}.\end{array}

Then

\alpha_{i}-\beta_{i}>\alpha_{i+1}-\beta_{i+1}.

(23)

Since

\begin{array}[]{rl}(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)-(q^{d}-1)T_{i}(n)&=(q^{k}-q^{d})S_{i}(n)+(q^{d}-1)\left(S_{i}(n)-T_{i}(n)\right)\\[8.5359pt]
&=(q^{k}-q^{d})\alpha_{i}+(q^{d}-1)\left(\alpha_{i}-\frac{\beta_{i}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}}\right)\\[8.5359pt]
&=(q^{k}-q^{d})\alpha_{i}+(q^{d}-1)(\alpha_{i}-\beta_{i})(1+\frac{\beta_{i}}{\alpha_{i}})\\[8.5359pt]
\end{array}

and note (
21
), (
22
) and (
23
), it suffices to check the case
i=3
. Since

\begin{array}[]{rl}(q^{k}-1)S_{3}(n)-(q^{d}-1)T_{3}(n)&<(q^{k}-1)S_{3}(n)\\[8.5359pt]
&=(q^{k}-1)\cdot\frac{q^{3-2k}[k-1]_{2}}{[n-k-1]_{2}}\\[8.5359pt]
&=\frac{q^{3-2k}(q^{k}-1)(q^{k-1}-1)(q^{k-2}-1)}{(q^{n-k-1}-1)(q^{n-k-2}-1)}\\[8.5359pt]
&\leq q^{3-2k}(q^{k}-1)\\[8.5359pt]
&<q^{k}-q^{d},\end{array}

the result holds.
∎

Lemma A.5
.

Let
2\leq i\leq d<k
,
2k\leq n
and
a_{i},b_{i}
be defined in (
15
) and (
16
) respectively. Then

b_{i}<\frac{a_{i}b_{1}}{a_{1}}.

Proof.

Since
b_{1}=-q^{k+(d-1)^{2}-1}\left[k-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop k-d\right]<0
and
a_{1}=\frac{1-q^{d}}{q^{n}-q^{k}}\left[n-k\atop k\right]
,

\begin{array}[]{rl}&~~~~b_{i}<\frac{a_{i}b_{1}}{a_{1}}\\[8.5359pt]
&\Leftrightarrow\frac{b_{i}}{-b_{1}}<\frac{a_{i}}{-a_{1}}\\[8.5359pt]
&\Leftrightarrow\frac{(-1)^{i}q^{ki+(d-i)^{2}-\binom{i+1}{2}}\left[k-i\atop d-i\right]\left[n-d-i\atop d-i\right]\left[n-d-i\atop k-d\right]}{q^{k+(d-1)^{2}-1}\left[k-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop k-d\right]}<\frac{\frac{q^{k}-q^{d}}{q^{n}-q^{k}}\left[n-k\atop k\right]+(-1)^{i}q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki}\left[n-k-i\atop k-i\right]}{\frac{q^{d}-1}{q^{n}-q^{k}}\left[n-k\atop k\right]}\\[8.5359pt]
&\Leftrightarrow\frac{(-1)^{i}q^{(k-2d)(i-1)+\binom{i}{2}}\left[k-i\atop d-i\right]\left[n-d-i\atop d-i\right]\left[n-d-i\atop k-d\right]}{\left[k-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop d-1\right]\left[n-d-1\atop k-d\right]}<\frac{(q^{k}-q^{d})+(-1)^{i}q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki}(q^{n}-q^{k})\frac{\left[n-k-i\atop k-i\right]}{\left[n-k\atop k\right]}}{q^{d}-1}\\[8.5359pt]
&\Leftrightarrow\frac{(-1)^{i}q^{(k-2d)(i-1)+\binom{i}{2}}[d-1]_{i-1}^{2}[n-k-1]_{i-1}}{[k-1]_{i-1}[n-d-1]_{i-1}^{2}}<\frac{(q^{k}-q^{d})+(-1)^{i}q^{\binom{i}{2}-ki+k}(q^{k}-1)\frac{[k-1]_{i-1}}{[n-k-1]_{i-1}}}{q^{d}-1}\\[8.5359pt]
&\Leftrightarrow(-1)^{i}T_{i}(n)<\frac{(q^{k}-q^{d})+(-1)^{i}(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)}{q^{d}-1}\\[8.5359pt]
&\Leftrightarrow(-1)^{i}\left((q^{d}-1)T_{i}(n)-(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)\right)<q^{k}-q^{d}.\end{array}

We can distinguish the cases when
i
is odd and even. Let us consider the first case when
i
is even.
Since
d<k
,
2k\leq n
and
\frac{q^{s}-1}{q^{t}-1}>q^{s-t}
for
s>t
.

\begin{array}[]{rl}\frac{S_{i}(n)}{T_{i}(n)}&=\frac{[k-1]_{i-1}^{2}[n-d-1]_{i-1}^{2}}{q^{(2k-2d)(i-1)}[d-1]_{i-1}^{2}[n-k-1]_{i-1}^{2}}\\[8.5359pt]
&>\frac{[n-d-1]_{i-1}^{2}}{[n-k-1]_{i-1}^{2}}\\[8.5359pt]
&>1,\end{array}

that is,
S_{i}(n)>T_{i}(n)
. Hence,

(q^{d}-1)T_{i}(n)-(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)<(q^{d}-1)S_{i}(n)-(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)=(q^{d}-q^{k})S_{i}(n)<0<q^{k}-q^{d},

which implies the desired result.

Now suppose
i
is odd and thus
i\geq 3
. Then

b_{i}<\frac{a_{i}b_{1}}{a_{1}}\Leftrightarrow(q^{k}-1)S_{i}(n)-(q^{d}-1)T_{i}(n)<q^{k}-q^{d}.

Hence the result holds by Lemma
A.4
.
∎
