34 C.F.R. § 381.20 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:381.20
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-381.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 381 (Protection And Advocacy Of Individual Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any fiscal year in which the amount appropriated for the PAIR program is less than $5,500,000, the Secretary evaluates applications under the procedures in 34 CFR part 75.
