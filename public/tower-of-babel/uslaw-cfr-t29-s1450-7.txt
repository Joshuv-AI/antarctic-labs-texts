29 C.F.R. § 1450.7 — Return of property.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1450.7
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1450.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1450 (Collections Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in this regulation is intended to deter FMCS from demanding the return of specific property or from demanding, the return of the property or the payment of its value.
