14 C.F.R. § 221.165 — Cessation of operations without successor.
Citation: us:cfr:14:221.165
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-221.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 221 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a carrier ceases operations without having a successor, it shall:
(a) File a notice in each tariff of its own issue and cancel such tariff in its entirety.
(b) Revoke all powers of attorney and concurrences which it has issued.
