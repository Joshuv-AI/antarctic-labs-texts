43 C.F.R. § 2650.5-6 — -6 Adjustment to plat of survey.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2650.5-6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2650.5-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2650 (Alaska Native Selections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All conveyances issued for lands not covered by officially approved surveys of the Bureau of Land Management shall note that upon the filing of an official plat of survey, the boundary of the selected area, described in terms of protraction diagrams or by metes and bounds, shall be redescribed in accordance with the plats of survey. However, no change will be made in the land selected.
