20 C.F.R. § 686.460 — What restrictions are there on the assignment of eligible applicants for nonresidential enrollment in Job Corps?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.460
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.460
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No more than 20 percent of students enrolled in Job Corps nationwide may be nonresidential students.
