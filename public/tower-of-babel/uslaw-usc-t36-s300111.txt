36 U.S.C. § 300111 — Authority of the Comptroller General of the United States
Citation: us:usc:36:300111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section300111
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Chapter 3001 (The American National Red Cross). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General of the United States is authorized to review the corporation’s involvement in any Federal program or activity the Government carries out under law.
