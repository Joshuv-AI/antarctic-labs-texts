19 U.S.C. § 4673 — Funding
Citation: us:usc:19:4673
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4673
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter VI (Labor Monitoring And Enforcement); Part C (Independent Mexico Labor Expert Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall provide necessary funding to support the work of the Board, including with respect to translation services and personnel support.
