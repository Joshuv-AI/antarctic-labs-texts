14 C.F.R. § 135.67 — Reporting potentially hazardous meteorological conditions and irregularities of ground facilities or navigation aids.
Citation: us:cfr:14:135.67
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-135.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 135 (Operating Requirements: Commuter And On Demand Operations And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a pilot encounters a potentially hazardous meteorological condition or an irregularity in a ground facility or navigation aid in flight, the knowledge of which the pilot considers essential to the safety of other flights, the pilot shall notify an appropriate ground radio station as soon as practicable.
