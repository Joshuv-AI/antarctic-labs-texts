(a) Each enclosure of an overcurrent protective device must meet Sections 240-30 and 240-33 of NFPA 70 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
(b) No enclosure may be exposed to the weather unless accepted by the Commandant.
