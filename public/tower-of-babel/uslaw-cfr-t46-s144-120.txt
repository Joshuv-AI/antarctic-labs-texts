46 C.F.R. § 144.120 — A classed vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:144.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-144.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 144 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel currently classed by a recognized classification society is deemed to be in compliance with the requirements of subparts B and C of this part.
