33 C.F.R. § 96.300 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:96.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-96.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 96 (Rules For The Safe Operation Of Vessels And Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the standards for the responsible person of a company and its vessel(s) to obtain the required and voluntary, national and international certification for the company's and vessel's safety management system.
