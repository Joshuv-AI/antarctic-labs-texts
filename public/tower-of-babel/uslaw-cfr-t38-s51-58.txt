38 C.F.R. § 51.58 — Requirements and Standards applicable for payment of per diem.
Citation: us:cfr:38:51.58
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-51.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 51 (Per Diem For Nursing Home, Domiciliary, Or Adult Day Health Care Of Veterans In State Homes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State home must meet the requirements in subpart C and the standards in the applicable subpart to be recognized, certified, and receive per diem for that program of care:
(a) For nursing home care, subpart D.
(b) For domiciliary care, subpart E.
(c) For adult day health care, subpart F.
