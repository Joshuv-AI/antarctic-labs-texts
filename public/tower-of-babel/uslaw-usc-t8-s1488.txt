8 U.S.C. § 1488 — Nationality lost solely from performance of acts or fulfillment of conditions
Citation: us:usc:8:1488
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1488
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter III (Nationality And Naturalization); Part III (Loss Of Nationality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The loss of nationality under this part shall result solely from the performance by a national of the acts or fulfillment of the conditions specified in this part.
