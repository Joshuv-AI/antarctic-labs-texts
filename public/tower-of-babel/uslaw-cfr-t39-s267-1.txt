39 C.F.R. § 267.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:39:267.1
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-267.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 267 (Protection Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part addresses the protection of information and records in the custody of the Postal Service throughout all phases of information flow and within all organization components, and includes micromated, manual and data processing information.
