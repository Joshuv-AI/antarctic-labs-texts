45 C.F.R. § 1232.16 — Procedures.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1232 (Nondiscrimination On Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedural provisions applicable to title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 apply to this part. These procedures are found in §§ 1203.6 through 1203.11 of this title.
