32 C.F.R. § 1633.5 — Securing information.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1633.5
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1633.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1633 (Administration Of Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The classifying authority is authorized to request and receive information whenever such information will assist in determining the proper classification of a registrant.
