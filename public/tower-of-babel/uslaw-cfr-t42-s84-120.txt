42 C.F.R. § 84.120 — Inhalation and exhalation valves; minimum requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.120
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Inhalation and exhalation valves shall be provided where necessary and protected against damage and distortion.
(b) Inhalation valves shall be designed and constructed to prevent excessive exhaled air from adversely affecting cartridges, canisters, and filters.
(c) Exhalation valves shall be protected against external influence, and designed and constructed to prevent inward leakage of contaminated air.
