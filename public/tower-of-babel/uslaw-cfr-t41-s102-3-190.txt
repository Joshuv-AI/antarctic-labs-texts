41 C.F.R. § 102-3.190 — -3.190 Severability of provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-3.190
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-3.190
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-3 (3—Federal Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, it is GSA's intention that the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
