43 U.S.C. § 390oo — Temporary supplies of water
Citation: us:usc:43:390oo
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section390oo
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter I–A (Reclamation Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Limitations inapplicable
Neither the ownership limitations of this subchapter nor the ownership limitations of any other provision of Federal reclamation law shall apply to lands which receive only a temporary, not to exceed one year, supply of water made possible as a result of—
(1)
an unusually large water supply not otherwise storable for project purposes; or
(2)
infrequent and otherwise unmanaged flood flows of short duration.
(b) Waiver of payment for temporary water supplies
The Secretary shall have the authority to waive payments for a supply of water described in subsection (a).
