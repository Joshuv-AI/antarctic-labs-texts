16 C.F.R. § 1605.12 — Nonexclusive delegation of power.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1605.12
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1605.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1605 (Investigations, Inspections And Inquiries Pursuant To The Flammable Fabrics Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision contained herein delegating any of the Commission's powers shall be construed as limiting the actual authority of the Commission to exercise the same powers.
