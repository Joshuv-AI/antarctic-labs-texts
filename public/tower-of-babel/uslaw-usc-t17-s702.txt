17 U.S.C. § 702 — Copyright Office regulations
Citation: us:usc:17:702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section702
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 7 (Copyright Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Register of Copyrights is authorized to establish regulations not inconsistent with law for the administration of the functions and duties made the responsibility of the Register under this title. All regulations established by the Register under this title are subject to the approval of the Librarian of Congress.
