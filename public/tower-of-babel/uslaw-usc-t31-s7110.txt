31 U.S.C. § 7110 — Use of joint financing provisions for Federal-State assisted projects
Citation: us:usc:31:7110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section7110
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 71 (Joint Funding Simplification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the President, the head of an executive agency may make an agreement with a State to extend the benefits of this chapter to a project involving assistance from at least one executive agency and at least one State agency. The agreement may include arrangements to process requests or administer assistance on a joint basis.
