30 C.F.R. § 75.601-2 — -2 Short circuit protection; use of fuses; approval by the Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.601-2
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.601-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fuses shall not be employed to provide short circuit protection for trailing cables unless specifically approved by the Secretary.
