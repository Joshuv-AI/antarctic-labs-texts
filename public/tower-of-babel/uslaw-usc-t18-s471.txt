18 U.S.C. § 471 — Obligations or securities of United States
Citation: us:usc:18:471
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section471
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 25 (Counterfeiting And Forgery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, with intent to defraud, falsely makes, forges, counterfeits, or alters any obligation or other security of the United States, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 20 years, or both.
