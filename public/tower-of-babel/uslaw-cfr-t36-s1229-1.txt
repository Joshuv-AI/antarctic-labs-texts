36 C.F.R. § 1229.1 — What is the scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1229.1
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1229.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1229 (Emergency Authorization To Destroy Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part describes certain conditions under which records may be destroyed without regard to the provisions of part 1226 of this subchapter.
