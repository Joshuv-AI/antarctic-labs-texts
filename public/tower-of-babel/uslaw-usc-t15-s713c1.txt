15 U.S.C. § 713c–1 — Annual report to Congress by Federal Surplus Commodities Corporation
Citation: us:usc:15:713c–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section713c–1
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Surplus Commodities Corporation shall submit to Congress on the first day of each regular session an annual report setting forth a statement of the activities, receipts, and expenditures of the Corporation during the previous year.
