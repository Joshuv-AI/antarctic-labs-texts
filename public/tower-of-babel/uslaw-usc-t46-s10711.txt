46 U.S.C. § 10711 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:46:10711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10711
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 107 (Effects Of Deceased Seamen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An owner or master violating this chapter are each liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of 3 times the value of the seaman’s money, property, and wages involved or, if the value is not determined, of $200.
