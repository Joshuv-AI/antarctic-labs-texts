44 C.F.R. § 401.6 — Violations.
Citation: us:cfr:44:401.6
Creator: Department Of Commerce And Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-401.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 401 (Shipping Restrictions (T-1)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who wilfully violates any provisions of this order or wilfully conceals a material fact or furnishes false information in the course of operation under this order is guilty of a crime and upon conviction may be punished by fine or imprisonment or both. In addition, administrative action may be taken against any such person, denying him the privileges generally accorded under this order.
