29 C.F.R. § 417.7 — Transcript.
Citation: us:cfr:29:417.7
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-417.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 417 (Procedure For Removal Of Local Labor Organization Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An official reporter shall make the only official transcript of the proceedings. Copies of the official transcript shall be made available upon request addressed to the Director in accordance with the provisions of part 70 of this title.
