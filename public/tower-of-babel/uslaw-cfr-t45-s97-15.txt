45 C.F.R. § 97.15 — For what purposes can grant funds be used?
Citation: us:cfr:45:97.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-97.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 97 (Consolidation Of Grants To The Insular Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds awarded under a consolidated grant must be used for purposes authorized by the statutes and regulations of the programs included in the consolidated grant. In its application for a consolidated grant the insular area is to indicate the amount of funds that will be allocated to the eligible programs.
