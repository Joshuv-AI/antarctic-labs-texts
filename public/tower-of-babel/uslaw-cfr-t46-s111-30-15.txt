46 C.F.R. § 111.30-15 — -15 Nameplates.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.30-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.30-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each device must have a nameplate showing the device's function.
(b) Each nameplate for a circuit breaker must show the electrical load served and the setting of the circuit breaker.
