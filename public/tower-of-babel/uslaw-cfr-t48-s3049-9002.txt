48 C.F.R. § 3049.9002 — 3049.9002 Contract clause (USCG).

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3049 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USCG contracting officers shall insert the clause at 3052.249-90, Contract Termination (USCG), in all solicitations and contracts, including contracts for commercial items, with a total value of more than $1,000,000.
