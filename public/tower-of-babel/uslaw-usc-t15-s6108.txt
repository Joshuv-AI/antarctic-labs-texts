15 U.S.C. § 6108 — Review
Citation: us:usc:15:6108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6108
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 87 (Telemarketing And Consumer Fraud And Abuse Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the expiration of 5 years following the date of the first promulgation of rules under section 6102 of this title, the Commission shall review the implementation of this chapter and its effect on deceptive telemarketing acts or practices and report the results of the review to the Congress.
