42 U.S.C. § 5197c — Annual report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:42:5197c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5197c
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV–B (Emergency Preparedness); Part B (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall annually submit a written report to the President and Congress covering expenditures, contributions, work, and accomplishments of the Federal Emergency Management Agency pursuant to this subchapter, accompanied by such recommendations as the Administrator considers appropriate.
