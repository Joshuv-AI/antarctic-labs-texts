32 C.F.R. § 74.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 74 (Appointment Of Doctors Of Osteopathy As Medical Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement the provisions of Pub. L. 763, 84th Congress (70 Stat. 608), relating to the appointment of doctors of osteopathy as medical officers.
