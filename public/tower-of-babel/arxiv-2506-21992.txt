Explicit construction of states in orbifolds of products of 𝑵 = 𝟐 N=2 Superconformal ADE Minimal models
Boris Eremin, Sergej Parkhomenko

Abstract
We generalize the explicit construction of fields in orbifolds of products of N = ( 2 , 2 ) N=(2,2) minimal models, developed by A.Belavin, V.Belavin and S.Parkhomenko to include minimal models with D and E-type modular invariants. It is shown that spectral flow twisting by the elements of admissible group G adm G_{\text{adm}} , which is used in the construction of the orbifold, is consistent with the nondiagonal pairing of D and E-type minimal models. We obtain the complete set of fields of the orbifold from the mutual locality and other requirements of the conformal bootstrap. The collection of mutually local primary fields is labeled by the elements of mirror group G adm ∗ G^{*}_{\text{adm}} . The permutation of G adm G_{\text{adm}} and G adm ∗ G^{*}_{\text{adm}} is given by the mirror spectral flow construction of the fields and transforms the original orbifold into a mirror one. This transformation is by construction an isomorphism of N = ( 2 , 2 ) N=(2,2) models. We illustrate our construction for the orbifolds of A 2 ​ E 7 3 \textbf{A}_{2}\textbf{E}_{7}^{3} model.

Conformal Field Theory models with

;

The approach of Gepner is based on the axioms of modular bootstrap, which implies the construction of a modular invariant partition function as a main ingredient of the construction.

In the papers

;

However, it is well known

;

superconformal minimal models includes also the models with pairing of type
D
and
E

D
and
E

We begin with Section
2

A
,
D

In Section

G_{\text{adm}}
of orbifold. Then we represent our explicit construction of fields which is based on the spectral flow twisting and mutual (qusi)-locality requirements of the fields of orbifold.

In Section
4

3

In Section

(a,c)

(3)

Recall that chiral-primary and anti-chiral-primary states

(4)

In the models of
N=(2,2)

In the modular bootstrap approach the particular way of pairing can be read off from the partition function. As it was found by D.Gepner

Among them, the most important matrix

They found the list

(6)

\displaystyle\left(\textbf{D}_{2j+2}\right),k=4j:\sum_{l=0}^{j-1}\left|\chi_{2l}+\chi_{k-2l}\right|^{2}+2\chi_{k/2}\bar{\chi}_{k/2},

\displaystyle\left(\textbf{D}_{2j+1}\right),k=4j-2:\sum_{l=0}^{k/2}\left|\chi_{2l}\right|^{2}+\sum_{l=0}^{k/2-1}\chi_{2l+1}\bar{\chi}_{k-2l-1},

\displaystyle\left(\textbf{E}_{6}\right),k=10:\left|\chi_{0}+\chi_{6}\right|^{2}+\left|\chi_{3}+\chi_{7}\right|^{2}+\left|\chi_{4}+\chi_{10}\right|^{2},

\displaystyle\left(\textbf{E}_{7}\right),k=16:\left|\chi_{0}+\chi_{16}\right|^{2}+\left|\chi_{4}+\chi_{12}\right|^{2}+\left|\chi_{6}+\chi_{10}\right|^{2}+\left|\chi_{8}\right|^{2}+\left(\chi_{2}+\chi_{14}\right)\bar{\chi}_{8}+\chi_{8}\left(\bar{\chi}_{2}+\bar{\chi}_{14}\right),

3.2
Spectral Flow twisting and mutual locality of fields

Conclusion

We presented the explicit construction of fields in orbifolds of products of
N=(2,2)
minimal models of ADE types, using spectral flow twisting and conformal bootstrap axioms. The initial orbifold model
M_{\vec{k}}/G_{\text{adm}}
and their mirror
M_{\vec{k}}/G_{\text{adm}}^{*}
are shown to be isomorphic, with the groups
G_{\text{adm}}
and
G_{\text{adm}}^{*}
related by Berglund-Hubsch-Krawitz-like condition. Thus, our results can be considered as a conformal bootstrap version of the modular bootstrap construction of mirror orbifolds from
GrPl
. The geometric interpretation of the orbifolds of ADE models that we consider was studied in
Fuchs:1989pt
;
Lynker
, where it was shown that such orbifolds are related to Calabi-Yau manifolds defined as a complete intersection in projective spaces. In this paper we thereby have given an explicit construction of fields in
\sigma
-models on the Calabi-Yau manifolds studied there.

Our approach has been illustrated through the example of mirror pairs of orbifolds of the
(1_{A})(16_{E})^{3}
model.

Our explicit construction of fields of the orbifolds can certainly be extended to build the physical states in the models of Type
II
and Heterotic superstring compactifications. While the isomorphism mapping between the mirror orbifold models enables a proof of mirror symmetry between
IIA
and
IIB
models compactified on the orbifolds of ADE type composite models.
