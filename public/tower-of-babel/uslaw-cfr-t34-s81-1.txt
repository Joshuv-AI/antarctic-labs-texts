34 C.F.R. § 81.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:34:81.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 81 (General Education Provisions Act—Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part govern the enforcement of legal requirements under applicable programs administered by the Department of Education and implement Part E of the General Education Provisions Act (GEPA).
