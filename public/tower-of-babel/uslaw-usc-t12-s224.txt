12 U.S.C. § 224 — Status of reserve cities under former statutes
Citation: us:usc:12:224
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section224
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter I (Definitions, Organization, And General Provisions Affecting System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The organization of reserve districts and Federal reserve cities shall not be construed as changing the present status of reserve cities except in so far as this chapter changes the amount of reserves that may be carried with approved reserve agents located therein.
