36 U.S.C. § 30702 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:36:30702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section30702
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 307 (Board For Fundamental Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of the corporation is to foster the development of fundamental education through programs and projects such as—
(1)
giving citizens (children, youth, and adults) an opportunity to acquire the understandings and skills necessary to relate the resources of the community to the needs and interests of the community;
(2)
demonstrating programs of fundamental education and measuring results; and
(3)
training men and women as leaders in fundamental education by providing internships and other experiences.
