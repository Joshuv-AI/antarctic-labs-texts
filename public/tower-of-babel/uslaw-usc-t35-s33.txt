35 U.S.C. § 33 — Unauthorized representation as practitioner
Citation: us:usc:35:33
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section33
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part I (United States Patent And Trademark Office); Chapter 3 (Practice Before Patent And Trademark Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, not being recognized to practice before the Patent and Trademark Office, holds himself out or permits himself to be held out as so recognized, or as being qualified to prepare or prosecute applications for patent, shall be fined not more than $1,000 for each offense.
