12 C.F.R. § 150.240 — May I hold investments of a fiduciary account off-premises?
Citation: us:cfr:12:150.240
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-150.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 150 (Fiduciary Powers Of Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may hold the investments of a fiduciary account off-premises, if this practice is consistent with applicable law, and you maintain adequate safeguards and controls.
