24 C.F.R. § 266.606 — Mortgage insurance premium: Duration and method of paying.
Citation: us:cfr:24:266.606
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-266.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 266 (Housing Finance Agency Risk-Sharing Program For Insured Affordable Multifamily Project Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Duration of payments. Mortgage insurance premium payments must continue annually until one of the following occurs:
(1) The mortgage is paid in full;
(2) A deed to the HFA is filed for record;
(3) An application for initial claim payment is received by the Commissioner; or
(4) The contract of insurance is otherwise terminated.
(b) Method of payment. The HFA shall pay any mortgage insurance premium required by this part in cash.
