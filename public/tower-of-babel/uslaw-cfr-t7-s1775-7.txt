7 C.F.R. § 1775.7 — Environmental requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1775.7
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1775.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1775 (Technical Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grants made for the purposes in §§ 1775.36 and 1775.66 must comply with the environmental review requirements in accordance with 7 CFR part 1970.
