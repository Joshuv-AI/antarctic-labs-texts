22 C.F.R. § 213.26 — Payment of compromised claims.
Citation: us:cfr:22:213.26
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-213.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 213 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CFO normally will not approve a debtor's request to pay a compromised claim in installments. However, where the CFO determines that payment of a compromise by installments is necessary to effect collection, a debtor's request to pay in installments may be approved.
