12 C.F.R. § 343.60 — Qualification and licensing requirements for insurance sales personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:12:343.60
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-343.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 343 (Consumer Protection In Sales Of Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An institution may not permit any person to sell or offer for sale any insurance product or annuity in any part of its office or on its behalf, unless the person is at all times appropriately qualified and licensed under applicable State insurance licensing standards with regard to the specific products being sold or recommended.
