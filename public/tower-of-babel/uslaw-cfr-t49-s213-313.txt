49 C.F.R. § 213.313 — Application of requirements to curved track.
Citation: us:cfr:49:213.313
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-213.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 213 (Track Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise provided in this part, requirements specified for curved track apply only to track having a curvature greater than 0.25 degree.
