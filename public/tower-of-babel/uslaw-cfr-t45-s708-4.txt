45 C.F.R. § 708.4 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:708.4
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-708.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 708 (Collection By Salary Offset From Indebted Current And Former Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are to be followed when:
(a) The Commission is owed a debt by an individual who is a current employee of the Commission; or
(b) The Commission is owed a debt by an individual currently employed by another Federal agency; or
(c) The Commission employs an individual who owes a debt to another Federal agency.
