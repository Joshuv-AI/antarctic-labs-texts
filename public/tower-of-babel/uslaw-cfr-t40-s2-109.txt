40 C.F.R. § 2.109 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:40:2.109
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-2.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 2 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
