5 C.F.R. § 839.903 — What happens to the Social Security taxes I erroneously paid when my employer corrects my retirement coverage to CSRS?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.903
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except for the last 3 years, the money you erroneously paid into Social Security will remain to your credit in the Social Security fund. The Social Security Administration will include all but those last 3 years in determining your eligibility for, and the amount of, future benefits.
(b) The amount you paid into Social Security for the last 3 years will be used to help pay your CSRS retirement deductions.
