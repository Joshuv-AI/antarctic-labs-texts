21 C.F.R. § 58.41 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:21:58.41
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-58.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 58 (Good Laboratory Practice For Nonclinical Laboratory Studies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each testing facility shall be of suitable size and construction to facilitate the proper conduct of nonclinical laboratory studies. It shall be designed so that there is a degree of separation that will prevent any function or activity from having an adverse effect on the study.
