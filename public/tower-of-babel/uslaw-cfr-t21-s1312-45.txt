21 C.F.R. § 1312.45 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1312.45
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1312.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1312 (Importation And Exportation Of Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any hearing on the denial of an application for an import, export, or transshipment permit, the Administrator shall have the burden of proving that the requirements for such permit pursuant to sections 1002, 1003, and 1004 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 952, 953, and 954) are not satisfied.
