43 C.F.R. § 2866.40 — How will the BLM calculate rent for an authorization for ancillary Communications Uses associated with Communications Uses on the rent schedule?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2866.40
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2866.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2860 (Communications Uses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the ancillary communication equipment is used solely in direct support of the primary use (see the definition of communications uses rent schedule in § 2861.5 and the definition of ancillary in § 2801.5 of this chapter), the BLM will calculate and charge rent only for the primary use.
