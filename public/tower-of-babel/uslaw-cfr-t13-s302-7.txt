13 C.F.R. § 302.7 — Amendments and changes.
Citation: us:cfr:13:302.7
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-302.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 302 (General Terms And Conditions For Investment Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Recipients shall submit requests for amendments to Investment awards in writing to EDA for approval and shall provide such information and documentation as EDA deems necessary to justify the request.
(b) Any changes to Projects made without EDA's approval are made at the Recipient's risk of non-payment of costs, suspension, termination or other applicable EDA action with respect to the Investment.
