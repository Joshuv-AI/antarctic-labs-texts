33 C.F.R. § 149.670 — What are the requirements for marking a general alarm system?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.670
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.670
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each of the following must be marked with the words “General Alarm” in yellow letters at least 1 inch high on a red background:
(a) Each general alarm box; and
(b) Each audio or visual device described under § 149.665 of this part for signaling the general alarm.
