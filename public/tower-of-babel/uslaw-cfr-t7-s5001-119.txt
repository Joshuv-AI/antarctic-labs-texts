7 C.F.R. § 5001.119 — Ineligible REAP projects.
Citation: us:cfr:7:5001.119
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Rural Housing Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-5001.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 5001 (Guaranteed Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owner occupied bed and breakfasts are ineligible projects in the REAP program.
