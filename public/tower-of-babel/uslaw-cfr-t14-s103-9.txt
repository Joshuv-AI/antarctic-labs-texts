14 C.F.R. § 103.9 — Hazardous operations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:103.9
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-103.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 103 (Ultralight Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may operate any ultralight vehicle in a manner that creates a hazard to other persons or property.
(b) No person may allow an object to be dropped from an ultralight vehicle if such action creates a hazard to other persons or property.
