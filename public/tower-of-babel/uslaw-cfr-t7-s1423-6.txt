7 C.F.R. § 1423.6 — Financial information documentation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1423.6
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1423.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1423 (Commodity Credit Corporation Approved Warehouses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be approved under this part, a warehouse operator shall submit a current financial statement at the time of application, and annually thereafter, as provided for in the applicable storage agreement.
