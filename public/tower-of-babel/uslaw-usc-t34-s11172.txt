34 U.S.C. § 11172 — Grants for technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:34:11172
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11172
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter II (Programs And Offices); Part E (Developing, Testing, And Demonstrating Promising New Initiatives And Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator may make grants to and contracts with public and private agencies, organizations, and individuals to provide technical assistance to States, units of general local government, Indian tribal governments, local private entities or agencies, or any combination thereof, to carry out the projects for which grants are made under section 11171 of this title.
