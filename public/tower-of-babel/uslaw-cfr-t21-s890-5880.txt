21 C.F.R. § 890.5880 — Multi-function physical therapy table.
Citation: us:cfr:21:890.5880
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-890.5880
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 890 (Physical Medicine Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A multi-function physical therapy table is a device intended for medical purposes that consists of a motorized table equipped to provide patients with heat, traction, and muscle relaxation therapy.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
