11 C.F.R. § 100.12 — Identification (52 U.S.C. 30101(13)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:100.12
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-100.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 100 (Scope And Definitions (52 U.S.C. 30101)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Identification means, in the case of an individual, his or her full name, including: First name, middle name or initial, if available, and last name; mailing address; occupation; and the name of his or her employer; and, in the case of any other person, the person's full name and address.
