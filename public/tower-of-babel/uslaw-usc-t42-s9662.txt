42 U.S.C. § 9662 — Limitation on contract and borrowing authority
Citation: us:usc:42:9662
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9662
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 103 (Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, And Liability); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any authority provided by this Act, including any amendment made by this Act, to enter into contracts to obligate the United States or to incur indebtedness for the repayment of which the United States is liable shall be effective only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in appropriation Acts.
