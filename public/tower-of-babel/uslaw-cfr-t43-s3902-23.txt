43 C.F.R. § 3902.23 — Individuals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3902.23
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3902.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3900 (Oil Shale Management—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individuals who are applicants must provide to the BLM a signed statement showing:
(a) U.S. citizenship; and
(b) That acreage holdings do not exceed the limits in § 3901.20 of this chapter. This includes holdings through a corporation, association, or partnership in which the individual is the beneficial owner of more than 10 percent of the stock or other instruments of control.
