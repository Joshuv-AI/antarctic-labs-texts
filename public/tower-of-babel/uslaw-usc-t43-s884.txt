43 U.S.C. § 884 — Right of forfeiture of railroad grants not affected
Citation: us:usc:43:884
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section884
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 21 (Grants In Aid Of Railroads And Wagon Roads). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 882 to 885 of this title shall not affect the right of the Government to declare or enforce a forfeiture of any lands so granted; but all the rights of the United States to said lands or to any interest therein shall be and remain as if said sections had not passed, except as to the lien mentioned in section 882 of this title.
