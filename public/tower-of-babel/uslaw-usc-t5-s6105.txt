5 U.S.C. § 6105 — Closing of Executive departments
Citation: us:usc:5:6105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section6105
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart E (Attendance And Leave); Chapter 61 (Hours Of Work); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An Executive department may not be closed as a mark to the memory of a deceased former official of the United States.
