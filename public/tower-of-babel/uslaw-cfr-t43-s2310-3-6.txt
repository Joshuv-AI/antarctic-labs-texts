43 C.F.R. § 2310.3-6 — -6 Transfer of jurisdiction.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2310.3-6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2310.3-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2300 (Land Withdrawals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A public land order that reserves lands for a department, agency or office, shall specify the extent to which jurisdiction over the lands and their related resource uses will be exercised by that department, agency or office. (See § 2310.3-2(c) of this title).
