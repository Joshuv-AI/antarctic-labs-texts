Tessellations of rational complex functions and the Riemann’s existence theorem
Alvaro Alvarez–Parrilla, Roberto Gutiérrez–Soto, Jesús Muciño–Raymundo

Abstract
A complex rational function R R , of degree n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 , on a compact Riemann surface M M provided with a cyclic order on its q q distinct critical values, determines an homogeneous tessellation of the Riemann surface M M , whose 2 ​ n 2n tiles are topological q q –gons with alternating colors. The tessellation provides a simple and straightforward visual description of the rational function R R . Conversely, assume a possibly non homogeneous tessellation 𝒯 \mathscr{T} of a compact oriented C 1 C^{1} surface ℳ \mathcal{M} , with tiles of alternating colors and a suitable labelling in the vertices of its tiles. Non homogeneous means that the tiles of 𝒯 \mathscr{T} are ρ \rho –gons, for different values of ρ \rho . Then there exists a Riemann surface structure M M on ℳ \mathcal{M} , a complex rational function R R and a cyclic order of its critical values, such that the tessellation of R R on M M topologically coincides with the original 𝒯 \mathscr{T} .

Tessellations and graphs

Let
