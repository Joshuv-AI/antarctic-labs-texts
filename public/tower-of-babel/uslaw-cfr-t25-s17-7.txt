25 C.F.R. § 17.7 — Limiting number of witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:25:17.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-17.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 17 (Action On Wills Of Osage Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the evidence seems clear and conclusive, the special attorney may, in his discretion, limit the number of witnesses to be examined formally upon any matter.
