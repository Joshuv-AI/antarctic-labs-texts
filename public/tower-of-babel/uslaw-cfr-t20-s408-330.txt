20 C.F.R. § 408.330 — How long will your application remain in effect?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.330
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your application for SVB will remain in effect from the date it is filed until we make a final determination on it, unless there is a hearing decision on your application. If there is a hearing decision, your application will remain in effect until the hearing decision is issued.
