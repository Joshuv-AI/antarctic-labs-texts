M. C. Araújo, J. Furtado, J. G. Lima, T. Mariz

Abstract
In this work, we investigate vacuum polarization in the Rarita-Schwinger model within the framework of Very Special Relativity. We examine both massive and massless spin- 3 / 2 3/2 fields coupled to the Maxwell field. The Mandelstam-Leibbrandt prescription is applied in order to evaluate the one-loop integrals, and we work within the S ​ I ​ M ​ ( 2 ) SIM(2) limit.

The theory of VSR offers a particularly intriguing proposal by questioning whether the exact symmetry group of nature is in fact the Poincaré group, rather than one of its proper subgroups. II
RS model in the VSR framework

In this section, we are interested in studying the RS Lagrangian of spin-
3/2
field in the framework of VSR. The Lagrangian density is given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}=\bar{\psi}^{\mu}\Lambda_{\mu\nu}\psi^{\nu},

(1)

where the operator
\Lambda_{\mu\nu}
can be written as

\Lambda^{\mu\nu}=\frac{i}{2}\left\{\sigma^{\mu\nu},\left(i\not{\mathcal{D}}-m+\frac{m_{\nu}^{2}}{2}\frac{i\not{n}}{n\cdot\mathcal{D}}\right)\right\}.

(2)

Here,

denotes the Dirac sigma matrix,
\mathcal{D}_{\mu}=\partial_{\mu}-ieA_{\mu}
is the covariant derivative,
m
represents the usual fermion mass,
m_{\nu}
is a mass parameter assigned by the VSR, and
n^{\mu}
is a fixed null vector satisfying
n\cdot n=0
. The above Lagrangian can be equivalently rewritten in an expanded form as

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bar{\psi}_{\mu}((i\tilde{\not{\mathcal{D}}}-m)g^{\mu\nu}-i(\gamma^{\mu}\tilde{\mathcal{D}}^{\nu}+\gamma^{\nu}\tilde{\mathcal{D}}^{\mu})+i\gamma^{\mu}\tilde{\not{\mathcal{D}}}\gamma^{\nu}+m\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{\nu})\psi_{\nu},

(3)

where

\tilde{\mathcal{D}}_{\mu}=\mathcal{D}_{\mu}+\frac{m_{\nu}^{2}}{2}\frac{n_{\mu}}{n\cdot\mathcal{D}}.

(4)

Now, within the VSR framework, the Feynman rules yield the propagator of the RS model in
D
dimensions as

iG^{\mu\nu}(p)=i\frac{\not{\tilde{p}}+m}{\tilde{p}^{2}-m^{2}}\left(g^{\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{D-1}\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{\nu}-\frac{D-2}{D-1}\frac{\tilde{p}^{\mu}\tilde{p}^{\nu}}{m^{2}}-\frac{1}{D-1}\frac{\gamma^{\mu}\tilde{p}^{\nu}-\gamma^{\nu}\tilde{p}^{\mu}}{m}\right),

(5)

while the interaction vertices of interest are given by

\displaystyle-ieV^{\lambda\mu\nu}(p,p^{\prime})=-ie\left({g_{\alpha}}^{\lambda}+\frac{m_{\nu}^{2}}{2}\frac{n_{\alpha}n^{\lambda}}{(n\cdot p)(n\cdot p^{\prime})}\right)\gamma^{\mu\alpha\nu},

(6)

and

\displaystyle-ieV^{\kappa\lambda\mu\nu}(p,p^{\prime},k,k^{\prime})=-ie\frac{m_{\nu}^{2}}{2}\frac{n_{\alpha}n^{\kappa}n^{\lambda}}{(n\cdot p)(n\cdot p^{\prime})}\left(\frac{1}{n\cdot(p+k)}+\frac{1}{n\cdot(p+k^{\prime})}\right)\gamma^{\mu\alpha\nu},

(7)

with
\tilde{p}_{\mu}=p_{\mu}-\frac{m_{\nu}^{2}}{2}\frac{n_{\mu}}{n\cdot p}
and
\gamma^{\mu\alpha\nu}=g^{\mu\nu}\gamma^{\alpha}-g^{\mu\alpha}\gamma^{\nu}-g^{\nu\alpha}\gamma^{\mu}+\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{\alpha}\gamma^{\nu}
. It is worth mentioning that the propagator in Eq. (
5
) is not applicable when the fermion mass
m
vanishes. In this limit, the RS theory possesses an intrinsic gauge symmetry that is not broken by the extra mass term induced by VSR. Therefore, for
m=0
, the appropriate propagator is

iG^{\mu\nu}(p)=\frac{i}{\tilde{p}^{2}}\left(\not{\tilde{p}}g^{\mu\nu}-\frac{2}{D-2}(\gamma^{\mu}\tilde{p}^{\nu}+\gamma^{\nu}\tilde{p}^{\mu})+\frac{1}{D-2}\gamma^{\mu}\not{\tilde{p}}\gamma^{\nu}\right)+\frac{i}{\tilde{p}^{4}}\left(\frac{4}{D-2}-\frac{1}{\xi}\right)\tilde{p}^{\mu}\not{\tilde{p}}\tilde{p}^{\nu},

(8)

which is obtained by supplementing the Lagrangian with a gauge-fixing term of the form
{\cal L}_{GF}=-\xi\bar{\psi}_{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}i\not{\partial}\gamma^{\nu}\psi_{\nu}
. It should also be noted that, with this choice, the vertices remain unchanged.

IV
Conclusions

We have calculated the photon self-energy arising from one-loop vacuum polarization in the RS theory within the VSR framework. We have considered both massive and massless spin-
3/2
fields coupled to the Maxwell field. For the massive case, as expected, we obtain the gauge-invariant expression (
34
), which has a smooth limit as
m_{\nu}\to 0
and allows us to recover the standard result
Barua:1978ck
. Moreover, we find that the
m_{\nu}
-dependent divergent contribution is present only in the transverse projector (
k^{2}g^{\mu\nu}-k^{\mu}k^{\nu}
), as in the usual QED. For the massless case, we again obtain a gauge-invariant result (
III.2
), but this time it does not exhibit a smooth limit when
m_{\nu}\to 0
. Using the usual gauge choice
\xi=\frac{1}{2}
, which removes the last term in the propagator (
8
), we arrive at the compact expression (
54
), whose divergent term closely resembles that in QED. This similarity suggests that the massless theory may be renormalizable, a point that will be investigated in a forthcoming work.
