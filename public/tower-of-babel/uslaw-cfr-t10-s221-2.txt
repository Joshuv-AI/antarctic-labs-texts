10 C.F.R. § 221.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:221.2
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-221.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 221 (Priority Supply Of Crude Oil And Petroleum Products To The Department Of Defense Under The Defense Production Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the mandatory supply of crude oil, refined petroleum products (including liquefied petroleum gases) and lubricants to the Department of Defense for its own use or for purchases made by the Department of Defense on behalf of other Federal Government agencies.
