8 C.F.R. § 274a.4 — a.4 Good faith defense.
Citation: us:cfr:8:274a.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-274a.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 274a (PART 274a—CONTROL OF EMPLOYMENT OF ALIENS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer or a recruiter or referrer for a fee for employment who shows good faith compliance with the employment verification requirements of § 274a.2(b) of this part shall have established a rebuttable affirmative defense that the person or entity has not violated section 274A(a)(1)(A) of the Act with respect to such hiring, recruiting, or referral.
