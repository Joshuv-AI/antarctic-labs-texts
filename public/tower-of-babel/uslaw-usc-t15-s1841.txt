15 U.S.C. § 1841 — Emergency Loan Guarantee Board; establishment; membership; voting
Citation: us:usc:15:1841
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1841
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 45 (Emergency Loan Guarantees To Business Enterprises). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is created an Emergency Loan Guarantee Board (referred to in this chapter as the “Board”) composed of the Secretary of the Treasury, as Chairman, the Chairman of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, and the Chairman of the Securities and Exchange Commission. Decisions of the Board shall be made by majority vote.
