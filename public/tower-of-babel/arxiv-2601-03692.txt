Role of Σ ⁡ ( 1660 ) \Sigma(1660) in the K − ​ p → π 0 ​ π 0 ​ Σ 0 K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0} reaction
Xing-Yi Ji, Si-Wei Liu, Wen-Tao Lyu, De-Min Li, En Wang, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
The processes of K − ​ p → π 0 ​ π 0 ​ Σ 0 K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0} and K − ​ p → π 0 ​ Λ ​ ( 1405 ) K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\Lambda(1405) are studied within the effective Lagrangian approach. In addition to the “background” contribution from the u u -channel nucleon pole term, contribution from the Σ ⁡ ( 1660 ) \Sigma(1660) resonance with spin-parity J P = 1 / 2 + J^{P}=1/2^{+} is also considered. For the K − ​ p → π 0 ​ π 0 ​ Σ 0 K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0} reaction, we perform a calculation for the total and differential cross sections by considering the contribution from the Σ ⁡ ( 1660 ) \Sigma(1660) intermediate resonance decaying into π 0 ​ Λ ​ ( 1405 ) \pi^{0}\Lambda(1405) with Λ ⁡ ( 1405 ) \Lambda(1405) decaying into π 0 ​ Σ 0 \pi^{0}\Sigma^{0} . With our model parameters, the available experimental data on both the K − ​ p → π 0 ​ π 0 ​ Σ 0 K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0} and K − ​ p → π 0 ​ Λ ​ ( 1405 ) K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\Lambda(1405) reactions can be fairly well reproduced. It is shown that we really need the contribution from the Σ ⁡ ( 1660 ) \Sigma(1660) resonance, and that these experimental measurements could be used to determine some properties of the Σ ⁡ ( 1660 ) \Sigma(1660) resonance.

Numerical results and discussions

With the formalism and ingredients given above, the total and differential cross sections versus the

\Lambda_{\Sigma(1660)}

are all fixed to
1000

1

The affection of varying the cutoff parameters have also been tested, but no significantly better fits were obtained.
The masses and widths of these involved particles are taken as the nominal values quoted in the PDG
38

\Sigma(1660)
is a broad state and its width has not yet been established

\Gamma_{\Sigma(1660)}=200

\Sigma(1660)\bar{K}N

IV
Summary

In this work, the process
K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0}
has been analyzed within the effective Lagrangian approach. The contributions from the
s
-channel involving the
\Sigma(1660)
and the non-resonant background from the
u
-channel nucleon pole have been considered. The total cross section and angular distributions for the
K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0}
process have been presented. Meanwhile, the total cross section for the
K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\Lambda(1405)
reaction has also been studied within the same model parameters. Our results are in good agreement with the experimental data and support the conclusion that the
\Sigma(1660)
resonance plays a key role in the
K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\Sigma^{0}
process with its decaying into the
\pi\Lambda(1405)
channel. Furthermore, a significant peak has been predicted in the total cross section of
K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\Lambda(1405)
around
p_{K^{-}}=0.75
GeV, arising from the intermediate
\Sigma(1660)
.

However, it is noted that the experimental uncertainties remain large and that data in the low-energy region of the total cross section for
K^{-}p\to\pi^{0}\Lambda(1405)
are not yet available. These key pieces of information are crucial for confirming the properties of the
\Sigma(1660)
resonance. Therefore, precise future measurements of these processes, such
as by the KLF Collaboration at JLab using the GlueX spectrometer
1
and possible Huizhou Hadron Spectrometer at HIAF
4
, are strongly encouraged, as they would be crucial for elucidating the properties of the
\Sigma(1660)
and determining the coupling strength for
\Sigma(1660)\to\pi\Lambda(1405)
.
