45 C.F.R. § 1170.11 — General prohibition against discrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1170.11
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1170.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1170 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified handicapped person shall, on the basis of handicap, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity that receives Federal financial assistance.
