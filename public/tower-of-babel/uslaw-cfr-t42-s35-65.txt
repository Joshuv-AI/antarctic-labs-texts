42 C.F.R. § 35.65 — Acceptable personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:42:35.65
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-35.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 35 (Hospital And Station Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contributions of personal property which may be accepted pursuant to § 35.62 include, but are not limited to, recreational equipment, furniture, radios and television sets. After its useful life, any cash proceeds realized upon disposition of such property shall be deposited to the credit of the patient fund and shall be available for expenditure pursuant to § 35.66(c).
