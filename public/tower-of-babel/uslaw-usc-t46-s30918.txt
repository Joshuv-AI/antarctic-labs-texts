46 U.S.C. § 30918 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:46:30918
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30918
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of each department of the United States Government, and the board of trustees of each federally-owned corporation, shall report to Congress at each session thereof all arbitration awards and settlements agreed to under this chapter since the previous session, for which the time to appeal has expired or been waived.
