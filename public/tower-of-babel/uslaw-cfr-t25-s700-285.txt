25 C.F.R. § 700.285 — Amendment of records.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.285
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.285
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Privacy Act permits an individual to request amendment of a record pertaining to him if be believes the record is not accurate, relevant, timely or complete, 5 U.S.C. 552a(d)(2). A request for amendment of a record shall be submitted in accordance with the procedures in this subpart.
