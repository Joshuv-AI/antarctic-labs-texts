41 C.F.R. § 301-10.112 — -10.112 Liability for unauthorized non-contract carrier use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.112
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are responsible for any additional costs or penalties incurred by using a non-contract carrier when contract service is available and no authorized exception applies.
