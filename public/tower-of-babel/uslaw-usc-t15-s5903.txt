15 U.S.C. § 5903 — Relation to other laws
Citation: us:usc:15:5903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5903
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 85 (Armored Car Industry Reciprocity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall supersede any provision of State law (or the law of any political subdivision of a State) that is inconsistent with this chapter.
