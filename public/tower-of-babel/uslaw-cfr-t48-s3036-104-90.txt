48 C.F.R. § 3036.104-90 — 3036.104-90 Authority for one-step turn-key design-build contracting for the United States Coast Guard (USCG).
Citation: us:cfr:48:3036.104-90
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3036.104-90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3036 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Head of the Contracting Activity (HCA) of the U.S. Coast Guard may use one-step turn-key selection procedures to enter into fixed-price design-build contracts in accordance with 14 U.S.C. 677.
