45 C.F.R. § 264.81 — What expenditures qualify for Territories to meet the Matching Grant MOE requirement?
Citation: us:cfr:45:264.81
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-264.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 264 (Other Accountability Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To meet the Matching Grant MOE requirements, Territories may count:
(a) Territorial expenditures made in accordance with §§ 263.2, 263.3, 263.4, and 263.6 of this chapter that are commingled with Federal TANF funds or made under a segregated TANF program; and
(b) Territorial expenditures made pursuant to the regulations at 45 CFR parts 1355 and 1356 for the Foster Care and Adoption Assistance programs and section 477 of the Act for the Independent Living program.
