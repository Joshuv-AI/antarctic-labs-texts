42 U.S.C. § 11381 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:42:11381
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter IV (Housing Assistance); Part C (Continuum Of Care Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this part are—
(1)
to promote community-wide commitment to the goal of ending homelessness;
(2)
to provide funding for efforts by nonprofit providers and State and local governments to quickly rehouse homeless individuals and families while minimizing the trauma and dislocation caused to individuals, families, and communities by homelessness;
(3)
to promote access to, and effective utilization of, mainstream programs described in section 11313(a)(7) of this title and programs funded with State or local resources; and
(4)
to optimize self-sufficiency among individuals and families experiencing homelessness.
