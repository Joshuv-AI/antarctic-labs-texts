14 U.S.C. § 2148 — Separation for failure of selection for promotion or continuation; time of
Citation: us:usc:14:2148
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2148
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If, under section 2142, 2143, 2144, 2145, 2150, or 2151 of this title, the discharge or retirement of any officer would be required less than six months following approval of the report of the board which considered but did not select him for promotion or continuation, the discharge or retirement of such officer shall be deferred until the last day of the sixth calendar month after such approval.
