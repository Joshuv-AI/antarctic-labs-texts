48 C.F.R. § 6101.34 — 6101.34 Ex parte communications[Rule 34].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6101.34
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6101.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6101 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member of the Board or of the Board's staff will communicate with a party about any material issue in a case outside of the presence of the other party, and no one shall attempt such communications on behalf of a party. This rule does not bar such communications about the Board's administrative functions or procedures.
