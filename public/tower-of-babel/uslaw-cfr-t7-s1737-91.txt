7 C.F.R. § 1737.91 — Approval.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1737.91
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1737.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1737 (Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Telecommunications Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A loan is approved when the Administrator, or whoever is delegated authority, signs the administrative findings and the letter to the borrower announcing the loan.
(b) If the loan is not approved, RUS shall notify the borrower, in writing, of the reasons.
