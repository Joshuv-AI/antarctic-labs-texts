42 U.S.C. § 297g — Modification of agreements; compromise, waiver or release
Citation: us:usc:42:297g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section297g
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter VI (Nursing Workforce Development); Part E (Student Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may agree to modifications of agreements made under this part, and may compromise, waive, or release any right, title, claim, or demand of the United States arising or acquired under this part.
