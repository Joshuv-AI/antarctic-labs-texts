38 C.F.R. § 21.5062 — Date of disenrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.5062
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.5062
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual will be disenrolled effective:
(a) The date the Department of Veterans Affairs or the Service Department determines he or she has ceased to be legally entitled to participate; or
(b) The date the individual negotiates the check which represents a refund of his or her remaining contributions to the fund, whichever is earlier.
