41 U.S.C. § 104 — Commercially available off-the-shelf item
Citation: us:usc:41:104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section104
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 1 (Definitions); Subchapter I (Subtitle Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subtitle, the term “commercially available off-the-shelf item”—
(1)
means an item that—
(A)
is a commercial product (as described in section 103(1) of this title);
(B)
is sold in substantial quantities in the commercial marketplace; and
(C)
is offered to the Federal Government, without modification, in the same form in which it is sold in the commercial marketplace; but
(2)
does not include bulk cargo, as defined in section 40102(4) of title 46, such as agricultural products and petroleum products.
