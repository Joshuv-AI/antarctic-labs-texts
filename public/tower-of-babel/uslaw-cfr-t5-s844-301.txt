5 C.F.R. § 844.301 — Commencing date of disability annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:844.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-844.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 844 (Federal Employees' Retirement System—Disability Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A disability annuity under this part commences on the day after the employee separates or the day after pay ceases and the employee meets the requirements for title to an annuity.
