52 U.S.C. § 20905 — Administration of programs
Citation: us:usc:52:20905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20905
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter I (Payments To States For Election Administration Improvements And Replacement Of Punch Card And Lever Voting Machines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In administering the programs under this subchapter, the Administrator shall take such actions as the Administrator considers appropriate to expedite the payment of funds to States.
