34 U.S.C. § 11277 — Evaluation and information

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter III (Runaway And Homeless Youth); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
If a grantee receives grants for 3 consecutive fiscal years under part A, B, C, D, or E (in the alternative), then the Secretary shall evaluate such grantee on-site, not less frequently than once in the period of such 3 consecutive fiscal years, for purposes of—
(1)
determining whether such grants are being used for the purposes for which such grants are made by the Secretary;
(2)
collecting additional information for the report required by section 11275 of this title; and
(3)
providing such information and assistance to such grantee as will enable such grantee to improve the operation of the centers, projects, and activities for which such grants are made.
(b) Cooperation
Recipients of grants under this subchapter shall cooperate with the Secretary’s efforts to carry out evaluations, and to collect information, under this subchapter.
