46 C.F.R. § 117.64 — Emergency Position Indicating Radiobeacons (EPIRB).
Citation: us:cfr:46:117.64
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-117.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 117 (Lifesaving Equipment And Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel that operates on the high seas, or that operates beyond three miles from the coastline of the Great Lakes, must have on board an FCC Type Accepted Category 1, 406 MHz EPIRB, installed to automatically float free and activate.
