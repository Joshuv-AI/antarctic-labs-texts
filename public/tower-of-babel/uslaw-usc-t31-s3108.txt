31 U.S.C. § 3108 — Prohibition against circulation privilege
Citation: us:usc:31:3108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3108
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 31 (Public Debt); Subchapter I (Borrowing Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An obligation issued under sections 3102–3104(a)(1) and 3105–3107 of this title may not bear the circulation privilege.
