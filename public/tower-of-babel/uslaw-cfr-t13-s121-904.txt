13 C.F.R. § 121.904 — When does SBA determine the size status of a business concern?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.904
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.904
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For compliance with programs of other agencies, SBA will base its size determination on the size of the concern as of the date set forth in the request of the other agency.
