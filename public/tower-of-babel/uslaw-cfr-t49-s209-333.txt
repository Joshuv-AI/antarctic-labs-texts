49 C.F.R. § 209.333 — Prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:209.333
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-209.333
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 209 (Railroad Safety Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An individual subject to a disqualification order shall not work for any railroad in any manner inconsistent with the order.
(b) A railroad shall not employ any individual subject to a disqualification order in any manner inconsistent with the order.
