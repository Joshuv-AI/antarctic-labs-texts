27 C.F.R. § 21.106-T — -T Distilled lime oil (Citrus aurantifolia).
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.106-T
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.106-T
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specific gravity at 25 °C. 0.850 to 0.870.
(b) Refractive index at 20 °C. 1.4740 to 1.4780.
(c) Optical rotation at 20 °C. +30° to +50°.
(d) Aldehyde content (as citral). 0.5 to 3.0 percent.
(e) Terpene content (as limonene). 45 percent minimum.
