20 C.F.R. § 71.4 — Limitation upon benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:20:71.4
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-71.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 71 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person, except a widow or a child, shall be entitled to benefits under this subchapter for disability with respect to himself and to death benefits on account of the death of another.
