34 U.S.C. § 20322 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:34:20322
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section20322
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 203 (Victims Of Child Abuse); Subchapter II (Court-Appointed Special Advocate Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to ensure that by January 1, 2015, a court-appointed special advocate shall be available to every victim of child abuse or neglect in the United States that needs such an advocate.
