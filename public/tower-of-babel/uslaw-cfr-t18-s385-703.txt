18 C.F.R. § 385.703 — Contents of decisions (Rule 703).
Citation: us:cfr:18:385.703
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-385.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 385 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any decision in a proceeding is part of the record of that proceeding and will contain:
(a) A ruling on each exception presented and any finding or conclusion, with supporting reasons, on any material issue of fact, law, or discretion presented on the record; and
(b) The appropriate rule, order, sanction, relief, or a denial of any rule, order, motion, or relief.
