5 C.F.R. § 752.606 — Agency records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:752.606
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-752.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 752 (Adverse Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency must maintain copies of, and will furnish to the Merit Systems Protection Board and to the appointee upon his or her request, the following documents:
(a) Notice of the proposed action;
(b) Appointee's written reply, if any;
(c) Summary of the appointee's oral reply, if any;
(d) Notice of decision; and
(e) Any order effecting the action, together with any supporting material.
