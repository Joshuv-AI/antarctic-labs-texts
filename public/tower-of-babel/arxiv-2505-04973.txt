Prime scattering geodesic theorem
Sudhir Pujahari, Punya Plaban Satpathy

Abstract
. The modular surface, given by the quotient ℳ = ℍ / PSL ​ ( 2 , ℤ ) \mathcal{M}=\mathbb{H}/\text{PSL}(2,\mathbb{Z}) , can be partitioned into a compact subset ℳ c \mathcal{M}_{c} and an open neighborhood of the unique cusp in ℳ \mathcal{M} . We consider scattering geodesics in ℳ \mathcal{M} , first introduced by Victor Guillemin in [ 6 ] for hyperbolic surfaces with cusps. These are geodesics in ℳ \mathcal{M} that lie in ℳ ∖ ℳ c \mathcal{M}\setminus\mathcal{M}_{c} for both large positive and negative times. Associated with such a scattering geodesic in ℳ \mathcal{M} , a finite sojourn time is defined in [ 6 ] . In this article, we study the distribution of these scattering geodesics in ℳ \mathcal{M} and their associated sojourn times . In this process, we establish a connection between the counting of scattering geodesics on the modular surface and the study of positive integers whose prime divisors lie in arithmetic progression. This article is the first such result for scattering geodesics.

Introduction and main results

The study of geodesics has been a central theme in mathematics due to its connection with different branches of Mathematics and Physics.

Inspired by Riemann’s explicit formula, Selberg

One of the key characteristics of Selberg’s trace formula is its unique insight into the relationship between the discrete spectrum of the hyperbolic Laplacian and the lengths of closed geodesics on a compact surface. Selberg later extended this formula to the non-compact case, incorporating both the discrete spectrum and the determinant of the scattering matrix, which encodes information about the continuous spectrum (cf.

In a series of papers written in the 1970s, Lax and Phillips applied their scattering theory framework to the spectral decomposition of the Laplace operator on finite-area hyperbolic surfaces with cusps.
