15 U.S.C. § 719j — Export limitations
Citation: us:usc:15:719j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section719j
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15C (Alaska Natural Gas Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any exports of Alaska natural gas shall be subject to the requirements of the Natural Gas Act [15 U.S.C. 717 et seq.] and section 103 1 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act, except that in addition to the requirements of such Acts, before any Alaska natural gas in excess of 1,000 Mcf per day may be exported to any nation other than Canada or Mexico, the President must make and publish an express finding that such exports will not diminish the total quantity or quality nor increase the total price of energy available to the United States.
