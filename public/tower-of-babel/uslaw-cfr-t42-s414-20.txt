42 C.F.R. § 414.20 — Formula for computing fee schedule amounts.

Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-414.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 414 (Payment For Part B Medical And Other Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Participating supplier. The fee schedule amount for a participating supplier for a physician service as defined in § 414.2 is computed as the product of the following amounts:
(1) The RVUs for the service.
(2) The GAF for the fee schedule area.
(3) The CF.
(b) Nonparticipating supplier. The fee schedule amount for a nonparticipating supplier for a physician service as defined in § 414.2 is 95 percent of the fee schedule amount as calculated in paragraph (a) of this section.
