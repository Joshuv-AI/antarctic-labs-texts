33 C.F.R. § 148.213 — How do I withdraw my application?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.213
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant may withdraw an application at any time before the proceeding is terminated by delivering or mailing notice of withdrawal to the Commandant (CG-5P) for docketing.
