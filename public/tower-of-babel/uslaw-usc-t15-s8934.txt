15 U.S.C. § 8934 — Data usage
Citation: us:usc:15:8934
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8934
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 115 (Perfluoroalkyl And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances And Emerging Contaminants); Subchapter III (Usgs Performance Standard). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Director shall provide the sampling data collected under section 8933 of this title to—
(1)
the Administrator; and
(2)
other Federal and State regulatory agencies on request.
(b) Usage
The sampling data provided under subsection (a) shall be used to inform and enhance assessments of exposure, likely health and environmental impacts, and remediation priorities.
