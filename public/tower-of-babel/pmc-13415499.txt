Retraction Note: Gamma-H2AX upregulation caused by Wip1 deficiency increases depression-related cellular senescence in hippocampus
Authors: Zhi-Yong He, Wen-Yue Wang, Wei-Yan Hu, Lu Yang, Yan Li, Wei-Yuan Zhang, Ya-Shu Yang, Si-Cheng Liu, Feng-Lan Zhang, Rong Mei, Da Xing, Zhi-Cheng Xiao, Ming Zhang
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/srep34558, published online 30 September 2016

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding the figures in this Article. When flipped vertically, the CA1 WT + Flx panel in Figure 3a appears to overlap with the CA1 CUMS + Flx panel in Figure 5d. The Editors requested the original data underlying the paper, which the Authors were unable to provide in full. When reviewing the material the Authors did provide, the Editors noticed that the raw image file provided for the DG WT panel in Figure 1a appears to be inconsistent with the Figure in the published Article. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the research presented in this Article.

Wei-Yan Hu, Zhi-Cheng Xiao, Ming Zhang agree with this retraction. Zhi-Yong He, Wen-Yue Wang, Lu Yang, Yan Li, Wei-Yuan Zhang, Ya-Shu Yang, Si-Cheng Liu, Feng-Lan Zhang, Rong Mei, and Da Xing did not respond to the Editors’ correspondence about this retraction.
