43 C.F.R. § 2812.9 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2812.9
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2812.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2810 (Tramroads And Logging Roads). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appeal pursuant to part 4 of 43 CFR Subtitle A, may be taken from any final decision of the authorized officer, to the Board of Land Appeals, Office of the Secretary.
