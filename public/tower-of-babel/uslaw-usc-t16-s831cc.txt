16 U.S.C. § 831cc — Separability
Citation: us:usc:16:831cc
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section831cc
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12A (Tennessee Valley Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The sections of this chapter are declared to be separable, and in the event any one or more sections of this chapter be held to be unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity of other sections of this chapter.
