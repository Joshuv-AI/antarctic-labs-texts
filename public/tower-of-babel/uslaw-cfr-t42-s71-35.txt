42 C.F.R. § 71.35 — Report of death or illness on carrier during stay in port.
Citation: us:cfr:42:71.35
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-71.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 71 (Foreign Quarantine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master of any carrier at a U.S. port shall report immediately to the quarantine station at or nearest the port the occurrence, on board, of any death or any ill person among passengers or crew.
