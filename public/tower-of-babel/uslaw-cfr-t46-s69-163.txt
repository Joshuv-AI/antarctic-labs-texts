46 C.F.R. § 69.163 — Under-deck tonnage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:69.163
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-69.163
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 69 (Measurement Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The under-deck tonnage provisions in § 69.109 apply; except that, under this subpart, spaces between the line of the tonnage deck and the tonnage deck itself due to a stepped tonnage deck are included in under-deck tonnage.
