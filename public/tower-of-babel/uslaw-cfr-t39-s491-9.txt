39 C.F.R. § 491.9 — Restrictions on garnishment.
Citation: us:cfr:39:491.9
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-491.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 491 (Garnishment Of Salaries Of Employees Of The Postal Service And The Postal Rate Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Garnishments under this section shall be subject to the restrictions in 15 U.S.C. 1671-1677, including limits on the amounts which can be withheld from an employee's pay and the priority of garnishments.
