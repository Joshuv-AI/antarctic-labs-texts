28 C.F.R. § 6.1 — Consent of warden or superintendent required.
Citation: us:cfr:28:6.1
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-6.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 6 (Traffic In Contraband Articles In Federal Penal And Correctional Institutions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The introduction or attempt to introduce into or upon the grounds of any Federal penal or correctional institution or the taking or attempt to take or send therefrom anything whatsoever without the knowledge and consent of the warden or superintendent of such Federal penal or correctional institution is prohibited.
