38 C.F.R. § 80.14 — Compliance review requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:38:80.14
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-80.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 80 (Veteran And Spouse Transitional Assistance Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Site visits. VA will conduct, as needed, site visits to grantee locations to review grantee accomplishments and management control systems.
(b) Inspections. VA will conduct, as needed, inspections of grantee records to determine compliance with the provisions of this part. All visits and evaluations will be performed with minimal disruption to the grantee to the extent practicable.
