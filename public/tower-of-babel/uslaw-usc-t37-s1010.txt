37 U.S.C. § 1010 — Commissioned officers: promotions; effective date for pay and allowances
Citation: us:usc:37:1010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section1010
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 19 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer of a uniformed service who is promoted to a grade above second lieutenant or ensign is entitled to the pay and allowances of the grade to which promoted on the effective date of the promotion.
