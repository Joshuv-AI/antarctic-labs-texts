7 C.F.R. § 1412.4 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1412.4
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1412.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1412 (Agriculture Risk Coverage And Price Loss Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant may seek reconsideration and review of any individual program eligibility adverse determination made under this part in accordance with the appeal regulations found at parts 11 and 780 of this title.
