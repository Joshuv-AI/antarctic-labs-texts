17 U.S.C. § 1318 — Copies of records
Citation: us:usc:17:1318
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1318
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 13 (Protection Of Original Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon payment of the prescribed fee, any person may obtain a certified copy of any official record of the Office of the Administrator that relates to this chapter. That copy shall be admissible in evidence with the same effect as the original.
