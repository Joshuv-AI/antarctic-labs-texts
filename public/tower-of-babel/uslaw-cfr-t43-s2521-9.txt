43 C.F.R. § 2521.9 — Relinquishments.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2521.9
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2521.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2520 (Desert-Land Entries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A desert-land entry may be relinquished at any time by the party owning the same. Conditional relinquishments will not be accepted.
