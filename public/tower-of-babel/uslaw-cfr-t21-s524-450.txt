21 C.F.R. § 524.450 — Clotrimazole.
Citation: us:cfr:21:524.450
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-524.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 524 (Ophthalmic And Topical Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each gram of cream contains 10 milligrams of clotrimazole.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 058198 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use—(1) Amount. Apply 1/4-inch ribbon of cream per square inch of lesion once daily for 2 to 4 weeks.
(2) Indications of use. For the treatment of fungal infections of dogs and cats caused by Microsporum canis and Trichophyton mentagrophytes.
(3) Limitations. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
