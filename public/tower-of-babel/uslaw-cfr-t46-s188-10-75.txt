46 C.F.R. § 188.10-75 — -75 Undocumented vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:188.10-75
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-188.10-75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 188 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This term means any vessel which is not required to have, and does not have, a valid marine document issued by the U.S. Coast Guard.
