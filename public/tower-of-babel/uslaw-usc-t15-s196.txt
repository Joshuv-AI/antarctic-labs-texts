15 U.S.C. § 196 — Payments for rent of offices in foreign countries
Citation: us:usc:15:196
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section196
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 5 (Statistical And Commercial Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 3324(a) and (b) of title 31 shall not apply to advance payments for rent of offices in foreign countries by the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce.
