10 C.F.R. § 605.13 — Cost sharing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:605.13
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-605.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 605 (The Office Of Science Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cost sharing is not required nor will it be considered as a criterion in the evaluation and selection process unless otherwise provided under § 605.10(d)(5).
