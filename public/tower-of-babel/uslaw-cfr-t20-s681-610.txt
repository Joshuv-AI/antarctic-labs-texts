20 C.F.R. § 681.610 — Does the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act require Local Workforce Development Boards to offer summer employment opportunities in the local youth program?
Citation: us:cfr:20:681.610
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-681.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 681 (Youth Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, WIOA does not require Local WDBs to offer summer youth employment opportunities as summer employment is no longer its own program element under WIOA. However, WIOA does require Local WDBs to offer work experience opportunities using at least 20 percent of their funding, which may include summer employment.
