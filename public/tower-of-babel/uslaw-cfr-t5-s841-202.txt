5 C.F.R. § 841.202 — Applications required.
Citation: us:cfr:5:841.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-841.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 841 (Federal Employees Retirement System—General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No benefit is payable under FERS, until after the claimant has applied for the benefit in the form prescribed by OPM.
(b) An employee, Member, or survivor may exercise any option or make any election authorized by FERS only in the form prescribed by OPM.
