20 C.F.R. § 205.4 — Claiming status as an employee representative.
Citation: us:cfr:20:205.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-205.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 205 (Employee Representative). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual who claims status as an employee representative shall file a report in accordance with § 209.10 of this chapter.
