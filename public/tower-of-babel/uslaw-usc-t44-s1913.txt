44 U.S.C. § 1913 — Appropriations for supplying depository libraries; restriction
Citation: us:usc:44:1913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1913
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 19 (Depository Library Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations available for the Office of Superintendent of Documents may not be used to supply depository libraries documents, books, or other printed matter not requested by them, and their requests shall be subject to approval by the Superintendent of Documents.
