46 C.F.R. § 137.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:137.100
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-137.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 137 (Vessel Compliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part describes the procedures owners or managing operators of towing vessels must use to demonstrate compliance with the requirements of this subchapter.
