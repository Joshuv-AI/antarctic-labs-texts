21 C.F.R. § 862.1550 — Urinary pH (nonquantitative) test system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:862.1550
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-862.1550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 862 (Clinical Chemistry And Clinical Toxicology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A urinary pH (nonquantitative) test system is a device intended to estimate the pH of urine. Estimations of pH are used to evaluate the acidity or alkalinity of urine as it relates to numerous renal and metabolic disorders and in the monitoring of patients with certain diets.
(b) Classification. Class I (general controls). The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to § 862.9.
