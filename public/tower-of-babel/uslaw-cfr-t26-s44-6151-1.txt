26 C.F.R. § 44.6151-1 — -1 Time and place for paying taxes.
Citation: us:cfr:26:44.6151-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-44.6151-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 44 (Taxes On Wagering; Effective January 1, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The taxes imposed by sections 4401 and 4411 shall, without assessment or notice and demand, be paid to the internal revenue officer with whom the returns are required to be filed at the time fixed for filing returns. For provisions relating to the time for filing returns, see section 6071 and § 44.6071-1. For provisions relating to the place for filing returns, see section 6091 and § 44.6091-1.
