7 C.F.R. § 767.102 — Leasing non-real estate inventory property.
Citation: us:cfr:7:767.102
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-767.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 767 (Inventory Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency does not lease non-real estate property unless it is attached as a fixture to real estate inventory property that is being leased and it is essential to the farming operation.
