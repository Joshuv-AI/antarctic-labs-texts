45 C.F.R. § 2551.81 — What type of clients are eligible to be served?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2551.81
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2551.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2551 (Senior Companion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Senior Companions serve only adults, primarily older adults, who have one or more physical, emotional, or mental health limitations and are in need of assistance to achieve and maintain their highest level of independent living.
