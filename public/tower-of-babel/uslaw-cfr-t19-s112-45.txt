19 C.F.R. § 112.45 — Surrender of identification cards.
Citation: us:cfr:19:112.45
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-112.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 112 (Carriers, Cartmen, And Lightermen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The identification card shall be surrendered by the holder or licensee to the port director when:
(a) The employee holder leaves the employment of the licensed cartman or lighterman;
(b) The cartman or lighterman bond or license is terminated; or
(c) The card is revoked or suspended pursuant to § 112.48.
