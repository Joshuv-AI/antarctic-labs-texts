19 C.F.R. § 176.22 — Deletion of protest or entry number.
Citation: us:cfr:19:176.22
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-176.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 176 (Proceedings In The Court Of International Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any protest number or entry number is to be deleted from a schedule of protest numbers or entry numbers attached to or embodied in a statement of agreed facts, a line shall be drawn through the number and the change shall be initialed by the authorized official making and approving the deletion.
