49 C.F.R. § 195.577 — What must I do to alleviate interference currents?
Citation: us:cfr:49:195.577
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-195.577
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 195 (Transportation Of Hazardous Liquids By Pipeline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For pipelines exposed to stray currents, you must have a program to identify, test for, and minimize the detrimental effects of such currents.
(b) You must design and install each impressed current or galvanic anode system to minimize any adverse effects on existing adjacent metallic structures.
