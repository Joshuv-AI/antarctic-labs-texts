14 C.F.R. § 325.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:325.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-325.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 325 (Essential Air Service Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish procedures to be followed in designating eligible places and in determining essential air transportation levels for eligible places, and in the appeals and periodic reviews of these determinations, under 49 U.S.C. 41732.
