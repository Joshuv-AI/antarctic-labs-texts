20 C.F.R. § 355.47 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:20:355.47
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-355.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 355 (Regulations Under The Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The complaint with respect to a claim or statement must be served in the manner specified in § 355.8 not later than the later of—
(a) 6 years after the date on which such claim or statement is made; or
(b) 3 years after the date on which facts material to the action are known or reasonably should have been known by the authority head, but in no event more than 10 years after the date on which the claim or statement was made.
