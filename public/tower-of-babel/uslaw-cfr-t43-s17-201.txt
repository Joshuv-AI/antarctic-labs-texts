43 C.F.R. § 17.201 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:43:17.201
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-17.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 17 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each recipient of Federal financial assistance from the Department of the Interior and to each program or activity that receives such assistance.
