14 C.F.R. § 27.995 — Fuel valves.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.995
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.995
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) There must be a positive, quick-acting valve to shut off fuel to each engine individually.
(b) The control for this valve must be within easy reach of appropriate crewmembers.
(c) Where there is more than one source of fuel supply there must be means for independent feeding from each source.
(d) No shutoff valve may be on the engine side of any firewall.
