33 C.F.R. § 147.1108 — Platform EDITH safety zone.
Citation: us:cfr:33:147.1108
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-147.1108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 147 (Safety Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Description: The area within a line 500 meters from each point on the structure's outer edge. The position of the center of the structure is 33°-35′-45″ N. 118°-08′-27″ W.
(b) Regulations: No vessel may enter or remain in this safety zone except for the following: (1) An attending vessel, (2) a vessel under 100 feet in length overall not engaged in towing, or (3) a vessel authorized by the Commander, USCG Southwest District.
