42 U.S.C. § 5319 — Community participation in programs
Citation: us:usc:42:5319
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5319
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 69 (Community Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No community shall be barred from participating in any program authorized under this chapter solely on the basis of population, except as expressly authorized by statute.
