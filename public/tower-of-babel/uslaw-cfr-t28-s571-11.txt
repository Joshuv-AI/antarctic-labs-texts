28 C.F.R. § 571.11 — Program responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:28:571.11
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-571.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 571 (Release From Custody). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Warden shall designate to a staff member the responsibility to:
(a) Determine the general release needs of the inmate population;
(b) Coordinate the institution release preparation program;
(c) Chair the Release Preparation Program Committee;
(d) Contact and schedule volunteers from the local community to participate in the release preparation program.
