25 C.F.R. § 580.12 — Does an appeal of a Chair's decision stay the effect of that decision?
Citation: us:cfr:25:580.12
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-580.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 580 (Rules Of General Application In Appeal Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided by Commission regulations at 25 CFR 522.5 and 522.7, the filing of an appeal does not stay the effect of the Chair's decision. The appellant must comply with the Chair's decision pending the outcome of the appeal.
