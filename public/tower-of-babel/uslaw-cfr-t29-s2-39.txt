29 C.F.R. § 2.39 — Political or religious affiliation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2.39
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2 (General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions about awards of Federal financial assistance must be free from political interference or even the appearance of such interference and must be made on the basis of merit, not on the basis of the religious affiliation of a recipient organization or lack thereof.
