14 C.F.R. § 120.3 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:120.3
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-120.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 120 (Drug And Alcohol Testing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish a program designed to help prevent accidents and injuries resulting from the use of prohibited drugs or the misuse of alcohol by employees who perform safety-sensitive functions in aviation.
