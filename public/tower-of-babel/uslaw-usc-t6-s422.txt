6 U.S.C. § 422 — Procurements for defense against or recovery from terrorism or nuclear, biological, chemical, or radiological attack
Citation: us:usc:6:422
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section422
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter VIII (Coordination With Non-Federal Entities; Inspector General; United States Secret Service; Coast Guard; General Provisions); Part F (Federal Emergency Procurement Flexibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authorities provided in this part apply to any procurement of property or services by or for an executive agency that, as determined by the head of the executive agency, are to be used to facilitate defense against or recovery from terrorism or nuclear, biological, chemical, or radiological attack, but only if a solicitation of offers for the procurement is issued during the 1-year period beginning on November 25, 2002.
