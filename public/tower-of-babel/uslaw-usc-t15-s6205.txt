15 U.S.C. § 6205 — Exception to certain disclosure restrictions
Citation: us:usc:15:6205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6205
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 88 (International Antitrust Enforcement Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 1313 of this title, and sections 46(f) and 57b–2 of this title, shall not apply to prevent the Attorney General or the Commission from providing to a foreign antitrust authority antitrust evidence in accordance with an antitrust mutual assistance agreement in effect under this chapter and in accordance with the other requirements of this chapter.
