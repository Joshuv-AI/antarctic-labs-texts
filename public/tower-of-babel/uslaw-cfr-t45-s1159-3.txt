45 C.F.R. § 1159.3 — Where should individuals send inquiries about the Endowment's systems of records or implementation of the Privacy Act?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1159.3
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1159.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1159 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inquiries about the Endowment's systems of records or implementation of the Privacy Act should be sent to the following address: National Endowment for the Arts; Office of the General Counsel; 1100 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW; Room 518; Washington, DC 20506.
