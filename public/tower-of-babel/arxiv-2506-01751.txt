Introduction

In this paper, we provide novel mean value estimates related to the extended main conjecture of Vinogradov’s mean value theorem
[
Woo23a
, Conjecture 8.2]
.

Conjecture 1.1

(
[
Woo23a
]
)
.

Suppose that
d\in\mathbb{N}
,
\boldsymbol{\alpha}=(\alpha_{d},\ldots,\alpha_{1})\in\mathbb{R}^{d}

]

with

. In
[

]

Theorem 1.2
.

For

\mathcal{I}_{p,2}(u;N)\ll N^{\epsilon}(N^{p-3}+N^{p/2-u}).

.

We have the following:

As for comparison with results available hitherto, it follows by Vinogradov’s mean value theorem that whenever

\displaystyle\ll N^{p/2-u+\epsilon}.

4.
Major arcs estimates

The purpose of this section is to prove Lemma

. The main ingredient in this section is a variant of
[

, Lemma 2]
. We record here this variant of
[
Bru88
, Lemma 2]
as a lemma. Combining this with refined shifting variables argument introduced in section

and interpolation theory may be useful in obtaining upper bounds for

-norm of exponential sums over the major arcs, for
p
positive numbers. The following lemma is stated in greater generality than is strictly required here, to facilitate future applications. More precisely, we shall use the following lemma only for the case

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
Z
and
Y
be positive numbers with
Y\leq Z.
For
1\leq a\leq q\leq Y
with
(q,a)=1
. Let
\mathfrak{M}(q,a)
denote the interval
\left[\frac{a}{q}-\frac{Y}{qZ},\frac{a}{q}+\frac{Y}{qZ}\right]
and assume that the
\mathfrak{M}(q,a)
are pairwise disjoint. Write
\mathfrak{M}
for the union of
\mathfrak{M}(q,a)
for all
1\leq a\leq q\leq Y
with
(q,a)=1
. Let
G:\mathfrak{M}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
be a function satisfying

G(\alpha)\ll q^{-1}\left(1+Z\left|\alpha-\frac{a}{q}\right|\right)^{-1}\ \ \textrm{for}\ \ \alpha\in\mathfrak{M}(q,a).

Furthermore, let
H
be a positive number with
\log H\asymp\log Z.

Let
F:\mathbb{R}\rightarrow[0,\infty)
be a function with a Fourier expansion

F(\alpha):=\sum_{h\in\mathbb{Z}}\psi_{h}e(\alpha h),

where
|\psi_{h}|\ll_{K}|h|^{-K}
for all
K>0
whenever
|h|>H
. Then, for
0\leq\nu\leq 2,
one has

\displaystyle\int_{\mathfrak{M}}F(\alpha)|G(\alpha)|^{\nu}d\alpha

\displaystyle\ll_{K}\left\{\begin{aligned} &Z^{\epsilon-1}Y^{2-\nu}|\psi_{0}|+Z^{\epsilon-1}Y^{1-\nu}\sum_{|h|\leq H}|\psi_{h}|+H^{-K},\ \ \text{when}\ 0\leq\nu\leq 1\\
&Z^{\epsilon-1}Y^{2-\nu}|\psi_{0}|+Z^{\epsilon-1}\sum_{|h|\leq H}|\psi_{h}|+H^{-K},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \text{when}\ 1\leq\nu\leq 2,\end{aligned}\right.

for all
K>0.

Proof.

On writing
\beta=\alpha-a/q
, we have

(4.1)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathfrak{M}}F(\alpha)|G(\alpha)|^{\nu}d\alpha

\displaystyle\ll\sum_{1\leq q\leq Y}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq a\leq q\\
(q,a)=1\end{subarray}}\int_{|\beta|\leq Y/(qZ)}q^{-\nu}(1+Z|\beta|)^{-\nu}F(\beta+a/q)d\beta

\displaystyle\ll\sum_{1\leq q\leq Y}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq a\leq q\\
(q,a)=1\end{subarray}}\sum_{h\in\mathbb{Z}}q^{-\nu}\psi_{h}\rho_{h}e\left(\frac{ah}{q}\right),

where

\rho_{h}=\int_{|\beta|\leq Y/(qZ)}(1+Z|\beta|)^{-\nu}e(\beta h)d\beta.

Notice that one has

(4.2)

\rho_{h}\ll\left\{\begin{aligned} &Z^{-1}Y^{1-\nu}q^{\nu-1}(\log Z),\ \ \text{when}\ 0\leq\nu\leq 1.\\
&Z^{-1}(\log Z),\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \text{when}\ 1\leq\nu\leq 2.\end{aligned}\right.

Furthermore, it follows by
[
HW79
, Theorem 271]
that for

, one has

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq a\leq q\\
(q,a)=1\end{subarray}}e\left(\frac{ah}{q}\right)\leq\sum_{d|(q,h)}d.

Therefore, when

(\ref{4.1})
,

and
(\ref{4.2})
that
