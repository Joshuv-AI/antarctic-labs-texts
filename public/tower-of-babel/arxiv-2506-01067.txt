Typical T T -free graphs
Bruce Reed, Yelena Yuditsky

Abstract
We prove that for every tree T T which is not an edge, for almost every graph G G which does not contain T T as an induced subgraph, V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) has a partition into α ⁡ ( T ) − 1 \alpha(T)-1 parts certifying this fact. Each part induces a graph which is P 4 P_{4} -free and has further properties which depend on T T . As a consequence we obtain good bounds (often tight up to a constant factor) on the number of T T -free graphs and show in a follow-up paper [ RY ] that almost every T T -free graph G G has chromatic number equal to the size of its largest clique.

2.3

T
-free graphs

In this section we determine the growth rates of the
T
-free graphs for each tree

and the specific partition that a typical
T
-free graph has. We set out lower bounds on the growth rate we can prove, and upper bounds which we will know hold once we have proven Theorem
1.2
.

We have already noted that we can prove that the number of
T
-free graphs is at least
2^{(1-\frac{1}{\alpha(T)-1}){n\choose 2}}

just by considering one partition into cliques and that if Theorem
1.2
holds then it is at most

2^{(1-\frac{1}{\alpha(T)-1}){n\choose 2}}2^{O(n\log n)}
.

We want to improve these bounds by summing over all
T
-freeness certifying partitions, the number of
T
-free graphs certified by the given partition. We can determine if a partition is certifying by examining the edges within the parts.
If it is, every choice of edges between the parts yields a
T
-free graph.
So, for any partition
\pi=\pi_{1},...,\pi_{\alpha(T)-1}
of
V_{n}

be the number of pairs of vertices which lie in different partition elements and

the number of

C(\pi)2^{m_{\pi}}

Now,
m_{\pi}={n\choose 2}-\sum_{i=1}^{\alpha(T)-1}{|\pi_{i}|\choose 2}
. Thus, letting
d_{i}=|\pi_{i}|-\frac{n}{w}
,

m_{\pi}=(1-\frac{1}{w})\frac{n^{2}}{2}-\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{\alpha(T)-1}d^{2}_{i}}{2}
. Given Theorem
1.2
,
there are only
2^{O(n\log n)}
choices for the edges within the parts. Thus, if the theorem is true
almost all
T
will be certified by partitions where
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{\alpha(T)-1}d^{2}_{i}}{2}=O(n\log n)

and hence for all
i
,
|\pi_{i}-\frac{n}{\alpha(T)-1}|=O(n^{2/3})
.

Some
