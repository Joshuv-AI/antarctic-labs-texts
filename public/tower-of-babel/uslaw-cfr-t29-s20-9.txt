29 C.F.R. § 20.9 — Waiver of credit reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:29:20.9
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-20.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 20 (Federal Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency head (or designee) may waive reporting a commercial debt or delinquent consumer debt to a credit reporting agency, if otherwise appropriate and if reporting the debt would not be in the best interests of the United States.
