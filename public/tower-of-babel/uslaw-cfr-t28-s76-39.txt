28 C.F.R. § 76.39 — Compromise or settlement after Decision and Order of a Judge.
Citation: us:cfr:28:76.39
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-76.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 76 (Rules Of Procedure For Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Possession Of Certain Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The United States Attorney having jurisdiction over the case may, at any time before the Attorney General issues an order, compromise, modify, or remit, with or without conditions, any civil penalty imposed under this section.
(b) Any compromise or settlement must be in writing.
