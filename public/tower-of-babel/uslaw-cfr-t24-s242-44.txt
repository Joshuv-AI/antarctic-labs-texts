24 C.F.R. § 242.44 — Construction standards.
Citation: us:cfr:24:242.44
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-242.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 242 (Mortgage Insurance For Hospitals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Work designed and performed under this section shall conform to the standards adopted by HUD, which, at a minimum, shall include the “Guidelines for Construction and Equipment of Hospital and Medical Facilities,” which is regularly updated and published by the American Institute of Architects.
