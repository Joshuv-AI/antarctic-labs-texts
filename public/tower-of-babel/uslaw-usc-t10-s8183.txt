10 U.S.C. § 8183 — State Department: assignment of enlisted members as custodians of buildings in foreign countries
Citation: us:usc:10:8183
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8183
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 823 (Special Assignments And Details). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the request of the Secretary of State, the Secretary of the Navy may assign enlisted members of the naval service to serve as custodians under the supervision of the principal officer at any embassy, legation, or consulate.
