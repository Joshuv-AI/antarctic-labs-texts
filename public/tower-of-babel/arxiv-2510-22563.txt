Diffusion operators on p p -adic analytic manifolds
Patrick Erik Bradley

Abstract
Kernel functions for Laplacian integral operators are constructed on p p -adic analytic manifolds using charts and transition maps like in Riemannian geometry. In the compact case, an operator of Vladimirov-Taibleson type parametrised by a real parameter s s is defined. Its L 2 L^{2} -spectrum is established, and it is shown that it gives rise to a Feller group. In this way, the Cauchy problem for the corresponding heat equation is solved in the positive by a transition function of a Markov process. Finally the existence of a heat kernel function and a Green function in the case s > 1 s>1 is proven.

Lemma 4.9

The operator

The proof follows the lines of the proof of

which shows that the positive maximum principle is satisfied.

3. The range

is dense in

for some
\eta>0
. If

, then

is a bounded operator, and one can argue as in

Since all three criteria of the Hille-Ray-Yosida Theorem are satisfied, the assertion now follows.

Theorem 4.11

The heat kernel function

H(t,x,y)=1+\sum\limits_{\psi}e^{-t\lambda_{\psi}}\psi(x)\overline{\psi(y)}

Proof.

The convergence of the series

\displaystyle H(t,x,x)
