Author Correction: A multicellular self-organized probiotic platform for oral delivery enhances intestinal colonization
Authors: Hua Liu, Zhijie Chen, Qiaowen Lin, Yi Chen, Liwen Hong, Jie Zhong, Zhengwei Cai, Zhengting Wang, Wenguo Cui
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62349-x, published online 01 August 2025

In the originally published version of this article, the qPCR data shown for csgD in Fig. 3g were inadvertently duplicated from the iraM results during figure assembly. The authors have rechecked the original raw qPCR data and replaced the duplicated csgD data with the correct values. The corrected csgD data show the same overall direction of change as shown in the original figure. This correction does not affect the interpretation of the result or the conclusions of the article. The figure and Source Data are now replaced in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. For comparison, the original Fig. 3 is available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment.
