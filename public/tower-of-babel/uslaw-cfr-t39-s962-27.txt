39 C.F.R. § 962.27 — (Rule 27) Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:39:962.27
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-962.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 962 (Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A hearing under this part concerning a claim or statement allegedly made, presented, or submitted in violation of 31 U.S.C. 3802 must be commenced within six years after the date on which such claim or statement is made, presented, or submitted.
