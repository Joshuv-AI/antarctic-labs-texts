30 C.F.R. § 762.1 — Scope.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-762.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 762 (Criteria For Designating Areas As Unsuitable For Surface Coal Mining Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the minimum criteria to be used in determining whether lands should be designated as unsuitable for all or certain types of surface coal mining operations.
