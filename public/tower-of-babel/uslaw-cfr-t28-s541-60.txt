28 C.F.R. § 541.60 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:28:541.60
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-541.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 541 (Inmate Discipline And Special Housing Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an effort to maintain a safe and orderly environment within its institutions, the Bureau of Prisons may place in controlled housing status an inmate who tests HIV positive when there is reliable evidence that the inmate may engage in conduct posing a health risk to another person.
