46 C.F.R. § 52.25-3 — -3 Feedwater heaters (modifies PFH-1).
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.25-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.25-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Feedwater heaters must meet the requirements in PFH-1 of Section I of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 52.01-1).
