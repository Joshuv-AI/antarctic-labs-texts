Correction: Association between EAT-Lancet diet adherence and incident type 2 diabetes in Shanghai adults: a prospective cohort study
Authors: Xiaoli Xu, Genming Zhao, Xing Liu, Xiaohua Liu, Huilin Xu, Yonggen Jiang, Qian Peng, Jianhua Shi, Dandan He, Yibin Zhou, Mengru He
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Public Health 25, 4242 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-24961-w

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified errors in the 'Study design and population' and 'Assessment for dietary intake and the EAT-Lancet diet index (ELDI)' sections, as well as in Figure 1.

The updated 'Study design and population' and 'Assessment for dietary intake and the EAT-Lancet diet index (ELDI)' sections is given below and the changes have been highlighted in bold.

Study design and population

The sentence currently reads:

“…having chronic diseases or conditions (with a history or current presence of malignant neoplasms, chronic kidney disease, chronic hepatitis, cirrhosis, coronary heart disease (CHD), gout, fatty liver, or identified during the baseline medical examination) (n = 15748); and abnormal energy intake [34] (ntake falling outside the acceptable ranges defined — males < 800 or > 4200 kcal/day and females < 600 or > 3500 kcal/day) (n = 7532) …

The sentence should read:

“...having chronic diseases or conditions [with a history or current presence of malignant neoplasms, chronic kidney disease, chronic hepatitis, cirrhosis, coronary heart disease (CHD), gout, fatty liver, or identified during the baseline medical examination] or abnormal energy intake [34] (intake falling outside the acceptable ranges defined — males < 800 or >4200 kcal/day and females < 600 or >3500 kcal/day) (n = 23,280)...”

Assessment for dietary intake and the EAT-Lancet diet index (ELDI)

The sentence currently reads:

The frequency of diet was divided into eight grades: “never”, “less than once a month”, “1–3 times a month”, “1–3 times per month or 4–6 times per week”, and “once, twice or more than three times a day”.

The sentence should read:

The frequency of diet was divided into eight grades: “never”, “less than once a month”, “1–3 times a month”, “1–3 times per week or 4–6 times per week”, and “once, twice or more than three times a day”.
