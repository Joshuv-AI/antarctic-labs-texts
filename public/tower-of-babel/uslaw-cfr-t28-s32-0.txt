28 C.F.R. § 32.0 — Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:28:32.0
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-32.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 32 (Public Safety Officers' Death, Disability, And Educational Assistance Benefit Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements the Act, which, as a general matter, authorizes the payment of three different legal gratuities:
(a) Death benefits;
(b) Disability benefits; and
(c) Educational assistance benefits.
