46 C.F.R. § 201.18 — Practice before the Administration defined.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.18
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Practice before the Administration shall be deemed to comprehend all matters connected with any presentation to the Administration or its staff.
