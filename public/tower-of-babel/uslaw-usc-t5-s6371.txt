5 U.S.C. § 6371 — Accrual of leave
Citation: us:usc:5:6371
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section6371
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart E (Attendance And Leave); Chapter 63 (Leave); Subchapter IV (Voluntary Leave Bank Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

While using leave made available to an employee from a leave bank, annual and sick leave shall accrue to the credit of such employee and shall become available for use by such employee in the same manner as provided for under section 6337.
