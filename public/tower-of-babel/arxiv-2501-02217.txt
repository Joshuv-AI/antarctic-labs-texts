Electric dipole moments from the perspective of a scalar triplet and singlet extension of the MSSM: A study of neutrons, electrons, mercury, and b and c quarks
Qing-hua Li 1,2 ,Jin-Lei Yang 1,2,3 ,Xiang Yang 1,2,3 , Tai-Fu Feng 1,2,3,4

Abstract
In the framework of the minimal supersymmetric model extension with new scalar triplets and singlet (TNMSSM), we analyze the electric dipole moment (EDM) of neutron ( d n d_{n} ), electron EDM( d e d_{e} ), the mercury EDM( d H ​ g d_{Hg} ), b b quark ( d b d_{b} ) and c c quark ( d c d_{c} ) by considering the contributions from the one-loop diagrams, some two-loop diagrams and the Weinberg operators. The effects of TNMSSM specific CPV sources χ d , χ t \chi_{d},\;\chi_{t} on d n d_{n} , d e d_{e} , d H ​ g d_{Hg} , d b d_{b} , d c d_{c} are specialized, it is found that they have significant contributions to these EDMs, and the current upper bounds on d n d_{n} impose strict constraints on χ d , χ t \chi_{d},\;\chi_{t} . The theoretical predictions on d b d_{b} , d c d_{c} can reach about 10 − 22 10^{-22} e ⋅ \cdot cm and 10 − 23 10^{-23} e ⋅ \cdot cm respectively by taking the upper bounds on d n d_{n} , d e d_{e} , d H ​ g d_{Hg} into account, which have great potential to be observed in future.
