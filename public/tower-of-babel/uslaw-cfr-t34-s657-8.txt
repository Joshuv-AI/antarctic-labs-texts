34 C.F.R. § 657.8 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:34:657.8
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-657.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 657 (Foreign Language And Area Studies Fellowships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this part or its application to any person, act, or practice is held invalid, the remainder of the part or the application of its provisions to any other person, act, or practice will not be affected thereby.
