49 U.S.C. § 28504 — Applicability of other laws
Citation: us:usc:49:28504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section28504
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part E (Miscellaneous); Chapter 285 (Commuter Rail Mediation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit a rail transportation provider’s right under section 28103(b) to enter into contracts that allocate financial responsibility for claims.
