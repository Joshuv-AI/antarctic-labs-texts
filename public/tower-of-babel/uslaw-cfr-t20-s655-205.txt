20 C.F.R. § 655.205 — Herding and range livestock job orders.
Citation: us:cfr:20:655.205
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-655.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 655 (Temporary Employment Of Foreign Workers In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer whose job opportunity has been determined to qualify for the procedures in §§ 655.200 through 655.235 is not required to comply with the job order filing timeframe requirements in § 655.121(a) and (b) or the job order review process in § 655.121(e) and (f). Rather, the employer must submit the job order along with a completed Application for Temporary Employment Certification, as required in § 655.215, to the designated NPC for the NPC's review.
