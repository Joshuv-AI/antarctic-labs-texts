33 C.F.R. § 157.228 — Isolating Valves: Closed during a voyage.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.228
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.228
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The master of each U.S. tank vessel under § 157.10a(b), § 157.10a(c)(2), § 157.10b(a)(2), or § 157.10c(c)(2) shall ensure that the valves under § 157.222(d) remain closed during each voyage.
(b) The master of each foreign tank vessel meeting § 157.10a(b), § 157.10a(c)(2), § 157.10b(a)(2), or § 157.10c(c)(2) shall ensure that the valves under § 157.222(d) remain closed when the vessel is on a voyage in the navigable waters of the United States.
