41 C.F.R. § 105-56.011 — -56.011 Non-waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-56.011
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-56.011
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-56 (56—Salary Offset For Indebtedness Of Federal Employees To The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any portion of a debt being collected under 5 U.S.C. 5514 will not be construed as a waiver of any rights which the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law unless there are statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary.
