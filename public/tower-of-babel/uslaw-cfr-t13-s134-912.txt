13 C.F.R. § 134.912 — Discovery and oral hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:13:134.912
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-134.912
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 134 (Rules Of Procedure Governing Cases Before The Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Discovery will not be permitted. Oral hearings will not be held unless the Judge determines that the dispute cannot be resolved except by the taking of live testimony and the confrontation of witnesses.
