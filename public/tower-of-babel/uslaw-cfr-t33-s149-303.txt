33 C.F.R. § 149.303 — What survival craft and rescue boats may be used on a manned deepwater port?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.303
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each survival craft on a manned deepwater port must be one of the following:
(1) A lifeboat meeting the requirements of § 149.306 of this part; or
(2) A liferaft meeting the requirements of § 149.308 of this part.
(b) Each rescue boat on a manned deepwater port must be a rescue boat meeting the requirements of § 149.314 of this part.
