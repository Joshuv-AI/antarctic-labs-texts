16 C.F.R. § 609.1 — Scope of regulations in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:16:609.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-609.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 609 (Free Electronic Credit Monitoring For Active Duty Military). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements Section 605A(k)(2) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. 1681c-1(k)(2), which requires consumer reporting agencies that compile and maintain files on consumers on a nationwide basis to provide a free electronic credit monitoring service to active duty military consumers that, at a minimum, notifies them of any material additions or modifications to their files.
