14 C.F.R. § 297.21 — Objections to registration application.
Citation: us:cfr:14:297.21
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-297.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 297 (Foreign Air Freight Forwarders And Foreign Cooperative Shippers Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons objecting to registration by a foreign air freight forwarder or foreign cooperative shippers association shall file their objections with the Office of International Aviation, U.S. Air Carrier Licensing/Special Authorities Division, within 28 days of the filing date of the registration forms. The Department will list the names and nationality of all persons applying for registration in its Weekly Summary of Filings.
