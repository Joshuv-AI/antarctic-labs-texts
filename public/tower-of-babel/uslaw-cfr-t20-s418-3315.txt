20 C.F.R. § 418.3315 — What is earned income?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3315
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Earned income is defined in § 416.1110 of this chapter and may be in cash or in kind. We may count more of your earned income than you actually receive. We count gross income, which is more than you actually receive, if amounts are withheld from earned income because of a garnishment, or to pay a debt or other legal obligation such as taxes, or to make any other similar payments.
