36 C.F.R. § 1150.81 — Consolidated or joint hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1150.81
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1150.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1150 (Practice And Procedures For Compliance Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In cases in which the same or related facts are asserted to constitute noncompliance with standards or guidelines and requirements, the judge may order all related cases consolidated and may make other orders concerning the proceedings as will be consistent with the objective of securing a just and inexpensive determination of the case without unnecessary delay.
