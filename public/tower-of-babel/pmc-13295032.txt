The authors also wish to clarify that, in the original article, Figures 1A,B, 2A,B, 3A,B, 4A,B, 5A,B, and 6A,B were reproduced from ref 38 (10.1016/j.catcom.2022.106516), published by Fouzia Perveen, Muhammad Farooq et al., under a CC-BY-NC-ND license (License number: 6239211474126). Corresponding authors Fouzia Perveen and Muhammad Farooq hold copyright for 10.1016/j.catcom.2022.106516 and, therefore, permit reuse and derivation of the Figures/Scheme. Parts C of Figures 1–6 in the original article correspond to the reported WO3-modified material.
