28 C.F.R. § 18.8 — Recommended decision.
Citation: us:cfr:28:18.8
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-18.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 18 (Office Of Justice Programs Hearing And Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within a reasonable time after the close of the record of the hearings conducted under § 18.6, the hearing officer shall submit findings of fact, conclusions of law, and a recommended order to the responsible agency official, in writing. The hearing officer shall promptly make copies of these documents available to the parties.
