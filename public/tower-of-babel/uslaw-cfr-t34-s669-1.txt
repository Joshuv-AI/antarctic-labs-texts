34 C.F.R. § 669.1 — What is the Language Resource Centers Program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:669.1
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-669.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 669 (Language Resource Centers Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Language Resource Centers Program makes awards, through grants or contracts, for the purpose of establishing, strengthening, and operating centers that serve as resources for improving the nation's capacity for teaching and learning foreign languages effectively.
