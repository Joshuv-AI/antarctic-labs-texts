12 C.F.R. § 1011.30 — Persons to whom subpart B is inapplicable.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1011.30
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1011.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1011 (Purchasers' Revocation Rights, Sales Practices And Standards (Regulation K)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Newspaper or periodical publishers, job printers, broadcasters, or telecasters, or any of the employees thereof, are not subject to this subpart unless the publishers, printers, broadcasters, or telecasters:
(a) Have actual knowledge of the falsity of the advertisement or
(b) Have any interest in the subdivision advertised or
(c) Also serve directly or indirectly as the advertising agent or agency for the developer.
