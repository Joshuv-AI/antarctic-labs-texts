10 U.S.C. § 8761b — Authority to make grants for purposes of Naval Sea Cadet Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:8761b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8761b
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 875 (Disposal Of Obsolete Or Surplus Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the availability of funds for this purpose, the Secretary of the Navy may make grants to support the purposes of Naval Sea Cadet Corps, a federally chartered corporation under chapter 1541 of title 36.
