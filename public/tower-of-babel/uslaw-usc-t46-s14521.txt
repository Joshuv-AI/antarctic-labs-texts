46 U.S.C. § 14521 — Application

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 145 (Regulatory Measurement); Subchapter III (Simplified System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies to a vessel described in section 14501 of this title that is not measured under subchapter II of this chapter.
