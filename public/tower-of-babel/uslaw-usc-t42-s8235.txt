42 U.S.C. § 8235 — “Residential building” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:8235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8235
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter II (Residential Energy Conservation); Part C (Residential Energy Efficiency Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this part, the term “residential building” means any building used as a residence which is not a new building to which final standards under sections 6833(a) and 6834 1 of this title apply and which has a system for heating, cooling, or both.
