45 C.F.R. § 2516.800 — What are the purposes of an evaluation?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.800
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every evaluation effort should serve to improve program quality, examine benefits of service, or fulfill legislative requirements.
