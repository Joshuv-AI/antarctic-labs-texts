INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION

[Inv. No. 701-TA-768 (Final)]


Steel Concrete Reinforcing Bar From Algeria; Closure of 
Investigation

AGENCY: United States International Trade Commission.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: On March 27, 2026, the Department of Commerce published notice 
in the Federal Register of an affirmative final countervailing duty 
determination in connection with steel concrete reinforcing bar from 
Algeria (91 FR 14808). However, the Office of the United States Trade 
Representative has advised the Commission of its determination that 
Algeria is not a Subsidies Agreement country (90 FR 34334, July 21, 
2025). The Commission did not make a preliminary determination for the 
countervailing duty investigation concerning steel concrete reinforcing 
bar from Algeria and does not make a final determination for the 
countervailing duty investigation concerning steel concrete reinforcing 
bar from Algeria (See 19 U.S.C. 1671(c)). Accordingly, the Commission's 
countervailing duty investigation concerning steel concrete reinforcing 
bar from Algeria (Investigation No. 701-TA-768 (Final)) is closed.

DATES: March 27, 2026.
