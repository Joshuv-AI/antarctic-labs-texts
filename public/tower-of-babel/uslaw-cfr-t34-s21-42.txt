34 C.F.R. § 21.42 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:34:21.42
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-21.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 21 (Equal Access To Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Within 15 days after receiving an answer, an applicant may file a reply.
(b) If the applicant's reply is based on any alleged facts not in the record of the adversary adjudication, the applicant shall include with the reply either—
(1) Supporting affidavits; or
(2) A request for further proceedings under § 21.44.
