17 C.F.R. § 260.11b-4 — b-4 Definition of “cash transaction” in section 311(b)(4).
Citation: us:cfr:17:260.11b-4
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-260.11b-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 260 (General Rules And Regulations, Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “cash transaction”, as used in section 311(b)(4), means any transaction in which full payment for goods or securities sold is made within 7 days after delivery of the goods or securities in currency or in checks or other orders drawn upon banks or bankers and payable upon demand.
