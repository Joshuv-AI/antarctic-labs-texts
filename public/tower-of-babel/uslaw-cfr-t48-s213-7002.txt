48 C.F.R. § 213.7002 — 213.7002 Purchase orders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:213.7002
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-213.7002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 213 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer need not obtain a contractor's written acceptance of a purchase order or modification of a purchase order for an acquisition under the 8(a) Program pursuant to 219.804-2(2).
