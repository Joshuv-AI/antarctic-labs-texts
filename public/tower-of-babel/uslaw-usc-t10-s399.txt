10 U.S.C. § 399 — Notifications relating to military operations in the information environment: requirement to notify Chief of Mission
Citation: us:usc:10:399
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section399
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 19 (Cyber And Information Operations Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not authorize a military operation in the information environment under this title intended to cause an effect in a country unless the Secretary fully informs the chief of mission for that country under section 207 of the Foreign Service Act of 1980 (22 U.S.C. 3927) of the planned operation.
