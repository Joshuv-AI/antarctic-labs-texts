48 C.F.R. § 9903.101 — 9903.101 Cost Accounting Standards.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9903.101
Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9903.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9903 (Contract Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public Law 100-679 (41 U.S.C. 422) requires certain contractors and subcontractors to comply with Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) and to disclose in writing and follow consistently their cost accounting practices.
