Products of random Hermitian matrices and brickwork Hurwitz numbers


Abstract
We consider products of n n random Hermitian matrices which generalize the one-matrix model and show its relation to Hurwitz numbers which count ramified coverings of certain type. Namely, these Hurwitz numbers count 2 ​ k 2k -fold ramified coverings of the Riemann sphere with arbitrary ramification type over 0 0 and ∞ \infty and ramifications related to the partition ( 2 k ) (2^{k}) (“brickworks” - involutions without fixed points) elsewhere.
