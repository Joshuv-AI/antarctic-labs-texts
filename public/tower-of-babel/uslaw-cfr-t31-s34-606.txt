31 C.F.R. § 34.606 — Recordkeeping—NOAA RESTORE Act Science Program.
Citation: us:cfr:31:34.606
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-34.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 34 (Resources And Ecosystems Sustainability, Tourist Opportunities, And Revived Economies Of The Gulf Coast States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients and other entities receiving funds under the NOAA RESTORE Act Science Program must maintain records as prescribed by NOAA and make the records available to NOAA.
