26 C.F.R. § 57.9 — Refund claims.
Citation: us:cfr:26:57.9
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-57.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 57 (Health Insurance Providers Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any claim for a refund of the fee must be made by the entity that paid the fee to the government and must be made on Form 843, “Claim for Refund and Request for Abatement,” in accordance with the instructions for that form.
