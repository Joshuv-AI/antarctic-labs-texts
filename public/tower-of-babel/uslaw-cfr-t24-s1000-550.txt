24 C.F.R. § 1000.550 — If the TDHE is the recipient, does it have to submit a copy of its audit to the Indian tribe?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.550
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Indian tribe as the grant beneficiary must receive a copy of the audit report so that it can fully carry out its oversight responsibilities with NAHASDA.
