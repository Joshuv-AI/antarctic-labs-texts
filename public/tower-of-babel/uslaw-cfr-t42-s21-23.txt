42 C.F.R. § 21.23 — False statements as disqualification.
Citation: us:cfr:42:21.23
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-21.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 21 (Commissioned Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Willfully false statements shall be cause for rejection of the application or, as provided in subpart N of this part, for dismissal.
