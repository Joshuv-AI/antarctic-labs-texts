7 C.F.R. § 1217.85 — Personal liability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1217.85
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1217.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1217 (Softwood Lumber Research, Promotion, Consumer Education And Industry Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member or employee of the Board shall be held personally responsible, either individually or jointly with others, in any way whatsoever, to any person for errors in judgment, mistakes, or other acts, either of commission or omission, as such member or employee, except for acts of dishonesty or willful misconduct.
