21 C.F.R. § 1316.01 — Scope of subpart A.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1316.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1316.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1316 (Administrative Functions, Practices, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures regarding administrative inspections and warrants pursuant to sections 302(f), 510, 1008(d), and 1015 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 822(f), 880, 958(d), and 965) are governed generally by those sections and specifically by the sections of this subpart.
