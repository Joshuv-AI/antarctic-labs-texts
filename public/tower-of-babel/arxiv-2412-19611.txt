Newman-Janis Algorithm from Taub-NUT Instantons
Joon-Hwi Kim

Abstract
It is shown that the Kerr metric represents the nonlinear superposition of self-dual and anti-self-dual Taub-NUT instantons. This promotes the Newman-Janis algorithm to a rigorous derivation of the Kerr metric with a definite physical origin. In the same way, the Kerr-Newman and charged Kerr-Taub-NUT solutions are systems of Taub-NUT instantons and chiral dyons.

By analogy,

It is known

The metric of the Taub-NUT solution develops a semi-infinite line defect

A modern framework

For example,
the Coulomb solution
is the counterpart of

It has been recently shown

The chiral limits of the Taub-NUT solution

Historically, they were introduced

Remarkably,

Next, we regard the Taub-NUT instantons as

\displaystyle{\includegraphics[valign={c}]{zagblob.pdf}}\>\>+\>\>{\includegraphics[valign={c}]{zigblob.pdf}}\,\,\,\,=\,\,\,\,\text{Kerr}\,.

A crucial feature of Einstein’s gravity theory,

First of all,
