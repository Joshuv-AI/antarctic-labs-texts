48 C.F.R. § 9.108-4 — 9.108-4 Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9.108-4
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9.108-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any agency head may waive the prohibition in subsection 9.108-2 and the requirement of subsection 9.108-3 for a specific contract if the agency head determines in writing that the waiver is required in the interest of national security, documents the determination, and reports it to the Congress.
