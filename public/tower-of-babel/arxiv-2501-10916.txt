Inequalities and asymptotics for hook lengths in ℓ \ell -regular partitions and ℓ \ell -distinct partitions
Eunmi Kim

Abstract
. In this article, we study hook lengths in ℓ \ell -regular partitions and ℓ \ell -distinct partitions. More precisely, we establish hook length inequalities between ℓ \ell -regular partitions and ℓ \ell -distinct partitions for hook lengths 2 2 and 3 3 , by deriving asymptotic formulas for the total number of hooks of length t t in both partition classes, for t = 1 , 2 , 3 t=1,2,3 . From these asymptotics, we show that the ratio of the total number of hooks of length t t in ℓ \ell -regular partitions to those in ℓ \ell -distinct partitions tends to a constant that depends on ℓ \ell and t t . We also provide hook length inequalities within ℓ \ell -regular partitions and within ℓ \ell -distinct partitions.

Lemma 4.2

Let

Proof.

Using the partial fraction

\frac{1}{(1-q^{\ell(m+1)})(1-q^{\ell(m+2)})}=\frac{1}{1-q^{\ell}}\left(\frac{1}{1-q^{\ell(m+1)}}-\frac{q^{\ell}}{1-q^{\ell(m+2)}}\right),

\displaystyle D_{\ell,2}(q)=

\displaystyle\frac{q-q^{\ell}}{1-q^{\ell}}\sum_{m\geq 0}\frac{q^{m+1}}{1-q^{\ell(m+1)}}+\left(1-\frac{q-q^{\ell-1}}{1-q^{\ell}}\right)\sum_{m\geq 0}\frac{q^{2(m+1)}}{1-q^{\ell(m+1)}}-\sum_{m\geq 0}\frac{q^{\ell(m+1)}}{1-q^{\ell(m+1)}}

\displaystyle\hskip 275.0pt-\frac{q^{\ell-1}-q^{\ell}}{1-q^{\ell}}\sum_{m\geq 0}\frac{q^{(\ell+1)(m+1)}}{1-q^{\ell(m+1)}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\big(f_{h,k,1}(z)-f_{h,k,\ell}(z)\big)F_{h,k,1}(z)+\big(1-f_{h,k,1}(z)+f_{h,k,\ell-1}(z)\big)F_{h,k,2}(z)-F_{h,k,\ell}(z)

where

f_{h,k,a}(z):=\frac{\left(\zeta_{k}^{h}e^{-\frac{2\pi z}{k}}\right)^{a}}{1-\left(\zeta_{k}^{h}e^{-\frac{2\pi z}{k}}\right)^{\ell}}\quad\text{ and }\quad F_{h,k,a}(z):=\sum_{m\geq 0}f_{h,k,a}\left((m+1)z\right).

First, assume that
k|\ell

Applying Lemma

We evaluate

for

2.3

\displaystyle I^{*}_{h,k,a}

\displaystyle=\zeta_{k}^{ah}\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{e^{-\frac{2\pi au}{k}}}{1-e^{-\frac{2\pi\ell u}{k}}}-\frac{ke^{-u}}{2\pi\ell u}\right)du

\displaystyle=\frac{k\zeta_{k}^{ah}}{2\pi\ell}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{e^{-\frac{a}{\ell}u}}{1-e^{-u}}-\frac{e^{-u}}{u}\right)du+\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{e^{-u}}{u}-\frac{e^{-\frac{ku}{2\pi\ell}}}{u}\right)du\right)

\displaystyle=-\frac{k\zeta_{k}^{ah}}{2\pi\ell}\left(\psi\left(\frac{a}{\ell}\right)+\log\left(\frac{2\pi\ell}{k}\right)\right),

which gives that

F_{h,k,a}(z)=-\zeta_{k}^{ah}\left[\frac{k}{2\pi\ell z}\left(\operatorname{Log}\left(\frac{2\pi\ell z}{k}\right)+\psi\left(\frac{a}{\ell}\right)\right)+\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{a}{\ell}\right)\right]+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{z}{k}\right).

(4.2)

Therefore, from (
4.1
) and (

for

\displaystyle\left[1-\gamma-\left(1-\frac{2}{\ell}\right)\psi\left(\frac{1}{\ell}\right)-\frac{2}{\ell}\psi\left(\frac{2}{\ell}\right)\right]\frac{1}{2\pi\ell z}+\mathcal{O}\left(1\right),

(4.5)

Lemma 5.1

Proof.

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{1}{(n+x+1)^{2}}+2\sum_{n\geq 0}\left(\frac{n}{(n+x)^{2}}-\frac{n}{(n+2x)^{2}}\right)\geq\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{1}{(n+2)^{2}}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{6}-1>0.

Similarly, we observe for

\displaystyle-g^{\prime}_{3}(x)

\displaystyle=\psi^{\prime}(x+1)+\left[-\frac{5}{2}\left(\psi(x)+x\psi^{\prime}(x)\right)+\left(\psi(2x)+2x\psi^{\prime}(2x)\right)+\frac{3}{2}\left(\psi(3x)+3x\psi^{\prime}(3x)\right)\right]
