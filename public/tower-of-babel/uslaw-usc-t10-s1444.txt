10 U.S.C. § 1444 — Regulations; determinations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter I (Retired Serviceman’S Family Protection Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The President shall prescribe regulations to carry out this subchapter. Those regulations shall, so far as practicable, be uniform for the armed forces, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, and the Public Health Service.
(b)
Determinations and certifications of eligibility for, and payments of, annuities and other payments or refunds under this subchapter shall be made by the department concerned. However, in the case of a department other than a military department, payments shall be made through the disbursing facilities of the Department of the Treasury.
