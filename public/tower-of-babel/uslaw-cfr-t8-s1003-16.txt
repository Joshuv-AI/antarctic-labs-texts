8 C.F.R. § 1003.16 — Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1003.16
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1003.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1003 (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The government may be represented in proceedings before an Immigration Judge.
(b) The alien may be represented in proceedings before an Immigration Judge by an attorney or other representative of his or her choice in accordance with 8 CFR part 1292, at no expense to the government.
