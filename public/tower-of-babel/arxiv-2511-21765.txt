Convex Hölder bound and its applications


Abstract
Given l , m , s ∈ [ 1 , ∞ ] l,m,s\in[1,\infty] such that l < s < m l<s<m an upper bound on the s s norm is given using l l norm and m m norm. The result is applied in bounding odd values of zeta function, binomial sums and gamma and beta functions.

2.2
Convex Hölder bound

Theorem 2.1
.

Given
l<s<m
and given
l
norm and
m
norm of a function
f
in a measure space, we have

(\lVert f\rVert_{s})^{s}\leq(\lVert f\rVert_{l})^{l\frac{(m-s)}{m-l}}(\lVert f\rVert_{m})^{m\frac{s-l}{m-l}}
.

Proof.

we split
|f|^{s}=|f|^{\alpha+\beta}
and apply the Hölders inequality with a suitable choice of
p
. In order to use
l
and
m
norms, we have the following conditions,

\displaystyle\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\
p&0\\
0&\frac{p}{p-1}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}\alpha\\
\beta\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}s\\
l\\
m\end{bmatrix}.

(15)

From
\alpha+\beta=s
, we get the following convex combination,
l\frac{1}{p}+m\frac{p-1}{p}=s
.
Thus solving for
p
,

\displaystyle p

\displaystyle=\frac{m-l}{m-s},

(16)

\displaystyle q

\displaystyle=\frac{p}{p-1},

(17)

\displaystyle=\frac{m-l}{s-l}.

(18)

Now by applying the Hölders inequality we get,

\displaystyle(\lVert f\rVert_{s})^{s}\leq(\lVert f\rVert_{l})^{l\frac{(m-s)}{m-l}}(\lVert f\rVert_{m})^{m\frac{s-l}{m-l}}.

(19)

When
m=\infty
, we can chose
p=1
. This gives
\alpha=l
and
\beta=s-l
. This gives,

\displaystyle(\lVert f\rVert_{s})^{s}\leq(\lVert f\rVert_{l})^{l}(\lVert f\rVert_{\infty})^{s-l}.

(20)

∎

From the above theorem, the bound obtained in the previous section is given when
l=1,m=\infty
and
s=\frac{5}{4}
.

3.2
Bounding binomial sums

Binomial sums of the form
\sum_{k=0}^{N}\binom{N}{k}k^{s}
is possible to evaluate recursively when
s
is a non negative integer. However when
s>1
is not an integer, the expression does not have the closed form. as an example, we have
\sum_{k=0}^{N}\binom{N}{k}k=N2^{N-1}
and

\sum_{k=0}^{N}\binom{N}{k}k^{2}=2^{N-2}(N+N^{2})
.
Thus for any
1\leq s\leq 2
we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\binom{N}{k}k^{s}

\displaystyle\leq(N^{2-s}(N+N^{2})^{s-1})2^{(N-1)(2-s)+(N-2)(s-1)},

(23)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\binom{N}{k}k^{s}

\displaystyle\leq(N^{2-s}(N+N^{2})^{s-1})2^{N-s}.

(24)
