28 U.S.C. § 544 — Oath of office
Citation: us:usc:28:544
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section544
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 35 (United States Attorneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each United States attorney, assistant United States attorney, and attorney appointed under section 543 of this title, before taking office, shall take an oath to execute faithfully his duties.
