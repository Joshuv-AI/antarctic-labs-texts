14 C.F.R. § 23.2215 — Flight load conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:23.2215
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-23.2215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 23 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must determine the structural design loads resulting from the following flight conditions:
(a) Atmospheric gusts where the magnitude and gradient of these gusts are based on measured gust statistics.
(b) Symmetric and asymmetric maneuvers.
(c) Asymmetric thrust resulting from the failure of a powerplant unit.
