25 C.F.R. § 179.201 — How does the Secretary distribute principal and income to the holder of a life estate without regard to waste?
Citation: us:cfr:25:179.201
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-179.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 179 (Life Estates And Future Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary must distribute all income, including bonuses and royalties, to the life estate holder to the exclusion of any holders of remainder interests.
