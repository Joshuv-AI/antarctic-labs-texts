49 U.S.C. § 110 — Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation
Citation: us:usc:49:110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section110
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation established under section 1 of the Act of May 13, 1954 (33 U.S.C. 981), is subject to the direction and supervision of the Secretary of Transportation.
(b)
The Administrator of the Corporation appointed under section 2 of the Act of May 13, 1954 (33 U.S.C. 982), reports directly to the Secretary.
