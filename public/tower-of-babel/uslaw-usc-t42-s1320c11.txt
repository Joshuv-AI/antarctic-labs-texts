42 U.S.C. § 1320c–11 — Exemptions for religious nonmedical health care institutions
Citation: us:usc:42:1320c–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1320c–11
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XI (General Provisions, Peer Review, And Administrative Simplification); Part B (Peer Review Of Utilization And Quality Of Health Care Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this part shall not apply with respect to a religious nonmedical health care institution (as defined in section 1395x(ss)(1) of this title).
