50 U.S.C. § 3502 — Seal of office
Citation: us:usc:50:3502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3502
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 46 (Central Intelligence Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall cause a seal of office to be made for the Central Intelligence Agency, of such design as the President shall approve, and judicial notice shall be taken thereof.
