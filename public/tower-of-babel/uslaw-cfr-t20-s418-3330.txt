20 C.F.R. § 418.3330 — What is unearned income?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3330
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unearned income is all income that is not earned income. We describe some of the types of unearned income we count in § 418.3335.
