26 C.F.R. § 31.3101-3 — -3 When employee tax attaches.
Citation: us:cfr:26:31.3101-3
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-31.3101-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 31 (Employment Taxes And Collection Of Income Tax At Source). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee tax attaches at the time that the wages are received by the employee. For provisions relating to the time of such receipt, see § 31.3121(a)-2.
