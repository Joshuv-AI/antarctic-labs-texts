46 C.F.R. § 161.006-3 — -3 Materials and workmanship.
Citation: us:cfr:46:161.006-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-161.006-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 161 (Electrical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Materials. The materials shall be of best quality and suitable in every respect for the purpose intended. All materials shall be corrosion resistant. The use of acid flux in making joints shall not be permitted.
(b) Workmanship. The workmanship shall be first class in every respect.
