46 C.F.R. § 148.435 — Electrical circuits in cargo holds.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.435
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.435
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During transport of a material that Table 148.10 of this part associates with a reference to this section, each electrical circuit terminating in a cargo hold containing the material must be electrically disconnected from the power source at a point outside of the cargo hold. The point of disconnection must be marked to prevent the circuit from being reenergized while the material is on board.
