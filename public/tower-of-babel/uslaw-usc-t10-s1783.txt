10 U.S.C. § 1783 — Family members serving on advisory committees
Citation: us:usc:10:1783
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1783
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 88 (Military Family Programs And Military Child Care); Subchapter I (Military Family Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A committee within the Department of Defense which advises or assists the Department in the performance of any function which affects members of military families and which includes members of military families in its membership shall not be considered an advisory committee under section 1001(2) of title 5 solely because of such membership.
