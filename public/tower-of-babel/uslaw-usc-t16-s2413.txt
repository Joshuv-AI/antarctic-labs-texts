16 U.S.C. § 2413 — Saving provisions
Citation: us:usc:16:2413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2413
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 44 (Antarctic Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Regulations
All regulations promulgated under this chapter prior to October 2, 1996, shall remain in effect until superseding regulations are promulgated under section 2405 of this title.
(b) Permits
All permits issued under this chapter shall remain in effect until they expire in accordance with the terms of those permits.
