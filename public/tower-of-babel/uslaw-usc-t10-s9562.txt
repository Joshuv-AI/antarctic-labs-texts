10 U.S.C. § 9562 — Clothing
Citation: us:usc:10:9562
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9562
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 965 (Issue Of Serviceable Material To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may prescribe the quantity and kind of clothing to be issued annually to members of the Air Force and members of the Space Force.
