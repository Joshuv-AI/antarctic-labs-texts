25 C.F.R. § 226.44 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:25:226.44
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-226.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 226 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person, firm or corporation aggrieved by any decision or order issued by or under the authority of the Superintendent, by virtue of the regulations in this part, may appeal pursuant to 25 CFR part 2.
