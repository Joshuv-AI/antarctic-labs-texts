30 C.F.R. § 903.843 — Federal enforcement.

Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-903.843
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Part 843 of this chapter, Federal Enforcement, applies regarding enforcement action on coal exploration and surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
(b) In addition to the requirements of part 843 of this chapter, OSM will furnish copies of enforcement actions and orders to show cause, upon request, to a designated Arizona State agency with jurisdiction over mining.
