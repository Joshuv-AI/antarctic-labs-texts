18 C.F.R. § 11.5 — Exemption of minor projects.
Citation: us:cfr:18:11.5
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-11.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 11 (Annual Charges Under Part I Of The Federal Power Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No exemption will be made from payment of annual charges for the use of Government dams or tribal lands within Indian reservations but licenses may be issued without charges other than for such use for the development, transmission, or distribution of power for domestic, mining, or other beneficial use in minor projects.
