26 U.S.C. § 1464 — Refunds and credits with respect to withheld tax
Citation: us:usc:26:1464
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1464
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 3 (Withholding Of Tax On Nonresident Aliens And Foreign Corporations); Subchapter B (Application Of Withholding Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where there has been an overpayment of tax under this chapter, any refund or credit made under chapter 65 shall be made to the withholding agent unless the amount of such tax was actually withheld by the withholding agent.
