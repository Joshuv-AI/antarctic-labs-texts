18 C.F.R. § 1306.3 — Surrender of possession.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1306.3
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1306.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1306 (Relocation Assistance And Real Property Acquisition Policies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Possession of real property will not be taken until the owner has been paid the agreed purchase price or TVA's estimate of just compensation has been deposited in court in a condemnation proceeding. To the greatest extent practicable, no person will be required to move from property acquired by TVA without at least 90 days' written notice thereof.
