42 U.S.C. § 1654 — Persons excluded from benefits
Citation: us:usc:42:1654
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1654
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Disability Or Death To Persons Employed At Military, Air, And Naval Bases Outside United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not apply in respect to the injury or death of (1) an employee subject to the provisions of subchapter I of chapter 81 of title 5; (2) an employee engaged in agriculture, domestic service, or any employment that is casual and not in the usual course of the trade, business, or profession of the employer; and (3) a master or member of a crew of any vessel.
