Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 155, Notification of Proposed Production 
Activity; Kerrville Public Utility Board Public Facility Corporation; 
(Natural Gas Reciprocating Internal Combustion Engine Generation Power 
Plant); Garwood, Texas

    Kerrville Public Utility Board Public Facility Corporation 
submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ 
Board (the Board) for its facility in Garwood, Texas within Subzone 
155F. The notification conforming to the requirements of the Board's 
regulations (15 CFR 400.22) was received on October 19, 2026.
    Pursuant to 15 CFR 400.14(b), FTZ production activity would be 
limited to the specific foreign-status material(s)/component(s) and 
specific finished product(s) described in the submitted notification 
(summarized below) and subsequently authorized by the Board. The 
benefits that may stem from conducting production activity under FTZ 
procedures are explained in the background section of the Board's 
website--accessible via www.trade.gov/ftz.
    The proposed finished product is a natural gas reciprocating 
internal combustion engine generation power plant facility.
    The proposed foreign-status materials/components include: internal 
combustion engines for stationary power generation (cast iron and 
steel); engine flywheels (steel), engine base plates (steel), and three 
phase synchronous alternators (steel and copper) (duty rate ranges from 
duty-free to 2.4%).
    The request indicates that certain materials/components are subject 
to duties under section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962 and 
section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, depending on the country of 
origin. The applicable section 232 and section 301 decisions require 
subject merchandise to be admitted to FTZs in privileged foreign status 
(19 CFR 146.41).
    Public comment is invited from interested parties. Submissions 
shall be addressed to the Board's Executive Secretary and sent to: 
[email protected]. The closing period for their receipt is December 7, 
2026.
    A copy of the notification will be available for public inspection 
in the ``Online FTZ Information System'' section of the Board's 
website.
