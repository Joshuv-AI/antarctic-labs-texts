Trace inequalities for piecewise W 1 , p \W^{1,p} functions over general polytopic meshes
Michele Botti, Lorenzo Mascotto

Abstract
Trace inequalities are crucial tools to derive the stability of partial differential equations with inhomogeneous, natural boundary conditions. In the analysis of corresponding Galerkin methods, they are also essential to show convergence of sequences of discrete solutions to the exact one for data with minimal regularity under mesh refinements and/or degree of accuracy increase. In nonconforming discretizations, such as Crouzeix-Raviart and discontinuous Galerkin, the trial and test spaces consists of functions that are only piecewise continuous: standard trace inequalities cannot be used in this case. In this work, we prove several trace inequalities for piecewise W 1 , p \W^{1,p} functions. Compared to analogous results already available in the literature, our inequalities are established: ( i ) on fairly general polytopic meshes (with arbitrary number of facets and arbitrarily small facets); ( ii ) without the need of finite dimensional arguments (e.g., inverse estimates, approximation properties of averaging operators); ( iii ) for different ranges of maximal and nonmaximal Lebesgue indices. AMS subject classification : 46E35, 65N30. Keywords : Trace inequalities; piecewise W 1 , p \W^{1,p} functions; nonconforming finite elements; polytopic meshes; spaces of bounded variation.

State-of-the-art.

For simplicial meshes,
Girault and Wheeler
[
12

proved a trace inequality for lowest order

Buffa and Ortner

Analogous results are given in
[

where the main technical tool in the proof

Trace inequalities for broken Sobolev spaces

[

novel discrete trace inequalities
for hybrid methods were proved,

-seminorm on the boundary.
All the references above are based on

2.4
Functions of bounded variation

The space
\BV(\Omega)
consists of functions
whose distributional derivative is a Radon measure
with finite total variation, i.e.,

\BV(\Omega):=\{v\in\L^{1}(\Omega)\mid|Dv|(\Omega)<\infty\}\qquad\text{where}\qquad|Dv|(\Omega):=\sup_{\boldsymbol{\xi}\in\boldsymbol{C}^{1}_{0}(\Omega)\,,\,{\left\|{\boldsymbol{\xi}}\right\|}_{\L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq 1}\int_{\Omega}v\ \nabla\cdot\boldsymbol{\xi},

which we endow with the norm

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\BV,\Omega}:={\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{1}(\Omega)}+|Dv|(\Omega).

In what follows, we review some properties of functions of bounded variation;
cf.
[
1
]
for a detailed presentation of these spaces.
The following result provides
the existence of a bounded, linear trace operator
from
\BV(\Omega)
into
\L^{1}(\partial\Omega)
;
details can be found
in
[
13
, Theorem 5.5]
,

[
5
, Theorem 4.2]
,
and
[
1
, Chapter 3]
.

Lemma 2.4

(Trace inequality in
\BV(\Omega)
)
.

There exists a positive constant
C_{\rm BV}
only depending on
\Omega

such that

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{1}(\partial\Omega)}\leq C_{\rm BV}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\BV,\Omega}\qquad\qquad\qquad\forall v\in\BV(\Omega).

(15)

Another important result states that

\BV(\Omega)
embeds continuously in broken Sobolev spaces
for any mesh as in Section
2.2
;
the proof is rather standard but we report it for the
sake of completeness.

Lemma 2.5

(Bound of the
\BV
-norm
by broken Sobolev norms)
.

Given
C_{\rm SP}
as in (
12
)
and a polytopic mesh
\mathcal{T}_{h}

as in Section
2.2
,
we have

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\BV,\Omega}\leq(1+|\Omega|^{\frac{1}{d}}C_{\rm SP}){\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,1}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}\qquad\qquad\qquad\forall v\in\W^{1,1}(\mathcal{T}_{h}).

Proof.

Let
v
be in
\W^{1,1}(\mathcal{T}_{h})
.
We first bound the total variation
|Dv|(\Omega)

using an integration by parts
and Hölder’s inequality (
1
and
\infty
):

\displaystyle|Dv|(\Omega)

\displaystyle=\sup_{\boldsymbol{\xi}\in\boldsymbol{C}^{1}_{0}(\Omega)\,,\,{\left\|{\boldsymbol{\xi}}\right\|}_{\L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq 1}\sum_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\int_{K}v\ \nabla\cdot\boldsymbol{\xi}

\displaystyle=\sup_{\boldsymbol{\xi}\in\boldsymbol{C}^{1}_{0}(\Omega)\,,\,{\left\|{\boldsymbol{\xi}}\right\|}_{\L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq 1}\Big(-\int_{\Omega}\nabla_{h}v\cdot\boldsymbol{\xi}+\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{I}}\int_{F}\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]\,\boldsymbol{\xi}\cdot\boldsymbol{n}_{F}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\boldsymbol{\xi}\in\boldsymbol{C}^{1}_{0}(\Omega)\,,\,{\left\|{\boldsymbol{\xi}}\right\|}_{\L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq 1}\Big({\left\|{\nabla_{h}v}\right\|}_{\mathbf{L}^{1}(\Omega)}+\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{I}}{\left\|{\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]}\right\|}_{\L^{1}(F)}\Big){\left\|{\boldsymbol{\xi}}\right\|}_{\L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq{\left|{v}\right|}_{\W^{1,1}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}.

Using Hölder’s inequality (
1^{*}
and
(1^{*})^{\prime}=d
)
and (
12
), we deduce

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{1}(\Omega)}\leq|\Omega|^{\frac{1}{d}}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{1^{*}}(\Omega)}\leq|\Omega|^{\frac{1}{d}}C_{\rm SP}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,1}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}.

The assertion follows noting

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\BV,\Omega}\leq{\left|{v}\right|}_{\W^{1,1}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}+|\Omega|^{\frac{1}{d}}C_{\rm SP}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,1}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}.

∎

3.1
Preliminary results

We begin with an algebraic bound.

Lemma 3.1

(Algebraic bound)
.

Given

and

Otherwise, we have

a^{r}-b^{r}=(a-b)(a^{r-1}+a^{r-2}b+\dots+ab^{r-2}+b^{r-1}).

A consequence of the previous bound is

\begin{split}\sum_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{K}^{d-\frac{(d-1)p^{\prime}}{q^{\prime}}}{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}_{\L^{q}(\partial K)}&\leq\gamma^{-\frac{p^{\prime}}{q^{\prime}}}\sum_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\Big(C_{4}^{p^{\prime}}{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}_{\L^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}(K)}+h_{K}^{(q-1)(p^{\prime}+dq^{\prime}-dp^{\prime})}{\left\|{\nabla v}\right\|}_{\mathbf{L}^{p}(K)}^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}\Big)\\
&\leq\gamma^{-\frac{p^{\prime}}{q^{\prime}}}\Big(C_{4}^{p^{\prime}}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}(\Omega)}^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}+\max_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}(h_{K}^{(q-1)(p^{\prime}+dq^{\prime}-dp^{\prime})}){\left\|{\nabla_{h}v}\right\|}_{\mathbf{L}^{p}(\Omega)}^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}\Big).\end{split}

Plugging the previous result into (
24
) implies

\begin{split}&C_{1}^{-1}{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{q}_{\L^{q}(\partial\Omega)}\\
&\leq\left[{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{q-1}_{\L^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}(\Omega)}+\gamma^{-\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}\Big(C_{4}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}(\Omega)}^{(q-1)}+\max_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\Big(h_{K}^{(q-1)(1-d+\frac{dq^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\Big){\left\|{\nabla_{h}v}\right\|}_{\mathbf{L}^{p}(\Omega)}^{(q-1)}\Big)\right]{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,p;q}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}\\
&\leq\frac{\gamma^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}+C_{4}}{\gamma^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}}{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{q-1}_{\L^{p^{\prime}(q-1)}(\Omega)}{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,p;q}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}+\frac{1}{\gamma^{\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}}}\max_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\Big(h_{K}^{(q-1)(1-d+\frac{dq^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}})}\Big){\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,p;q}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}^{q}.\end{split}

Taking
q=p^{{\ddagger}}
,
and using the broken Sobolev-Poincaré
inequality (
12
) together with
bound (
11
)
and the identity

(p^{{\ddagger}}-1)\Big(1-d+d\frac{(p^{{\ddagger}})^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}\Big)=\frac{d(p-1)^{2}}{p(d-1)},

we get

\begin{split}{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{p^{{\ddagger}}}_{\L^{p^{{\ddagger}}}(\partial\Omega)}&\leq\frac{C_{1}}{\gamma^{\frac{d(p-1)}{dp-1}}}\Big(\left(\gamma^{\frac{d(p-1)}{dp-1}}+C_{4}\right)(C_{\rm SP})^{p^{{\ddagger}}-1}+\max_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\Big(h_{K}^{\frac{d(p-1)^{2}}{p(d-1)}}\Big)\Big){\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,p;p^{{\ddagger}}}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}^{p^{{\ddagger}}}.\end{split}

If instead we take
q=p^{\sharp}
,
use the broken Sobolev-Poincaré
inequality (
13
),
and observe

(p^{\sharp}-1)\Big(1-d+d\frac{(p^{\sharp})^{\prime}}{p^{\prime}}\Big)=0,

then we obtain

\begin{split}{\left\|{v}\right\|}^{p^{\sharp}}_{\L^{p^{\sharp}}(\partial\Omega)}&\leq\frac{C_{1}}{\gamma^{\frac{d(p-1)}{p(d-1)}}}\Big(\left(\gamma^{\frac{d(p-1)}{p(d-1)}}+C_{4}\right)(C_{\rm SP}^{\sharp})^{p^{\sharp}-1}+1\Big){\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,p;p^{\sharp}}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}^{p^{\sharp}}.\end{split}

Bounds (
22
)
and (
23
)
follow using Hölder’s inequality (
r/q
and
(r/q)^{\prime}
)
with
r=p^{{\ddagger}}
and
r=p^{\sharp}
.
∎

Theorem 3.5

(Sobolev-trace inequalities for piecewise
\W^{1,p}
functions:
case 3
)
.

Given
p
in
[d,\infty)
and
q
in
[p,\infty)
,
we have

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{q}(\partial\Omega)}\leq C_{\rm S}^{\flat}(p,q,\Omega){\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,p;q}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}\qquad\qquad\qquad\forall v\in\W^{1,p}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h}),

(26)

where, given
C_{\rm S}^{\sharp}

as in (
23
),

\displaystyle C_{\rm S}^{\flat}(p,q,\Omega)

\displaystyle:=C_{\rm S}^{\sharp}(q^{\flat},q,\Omega)\max_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\big(|K|^{-1}h_{K}^{d}\card{(\mathcal{F}^{K})}\big)^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}|\Omega|^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}.

Proof.

Recall that
q^{\flat}
in
(1,d)
is such that,
cf. (
5
)–(
6
),

q^{\flat}:=\frac{qd}{q+d-1}<d\leq p.

(27)

Using (
23
), we write

{\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\L^{q}(\partial\Omega)}\leq C_{\rm S}^{\sharp}(q^{\flat},q,\Omega){\left\|{v}\right\|}_{\W^{1,q^{\flat};q}_{\Gamma_{D}}(\mathcal{T}_{h})}.

To conclude, we need to estimate from above
the two terms in the DG norm
appearing on the right-hand side;
cf. (
10b
).
We begin with the gradient term:

{\left\|{\nabla_{h}v}\right\|}_{\mathbf{L}^{q^{\flat}}(\Omega)}\overset{\eqref{relation:r-d-p}}{\leq}|\Omega|^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}{\left\|{\nabla_{h}v}\right\|}_{\mathbf{L}^{p}(\Omega)}.

We now focus on the jump terms: applying Hölder’s inequality for sequences (
p/(p-q^{\flat})
and
p/q^{\flat}
), we obtain

\begin{split}\big(\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{ID}}&{\left\|{\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]}\right\|}_{\L^{q}(F)}^{q^{\flat}}\big)^{\frac{1}{q^{\flat}}}=\big(\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{ID}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{\frac{d(p-q^{\flat})}{p}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{-\frac{d(p-q^{\flat})}{p}}{\left\|{\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]}\right\|}_{\L^{q^{\flat}}(F)}^{q^{\flat}}\big)^{\frac{1}{r}}\\
&\leq\big(\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{ID}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{d}\big)^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}\big(\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{ID}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{-\frac{d(p-q^{\flat})}{q^{\flat}}}{\left\|{\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]}\right\|}_{\L^{q}(F)}^{p}\big)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq\big(\sum_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}^{K}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{d}\big)^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}\big(\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{ID}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{-\frac{d(p-q^{\flat})}{q^{\flat}}}{\left\|{\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]}\right\|}_{\L^{q}(F)}^{p}\big)^{\frac{1}{p}}\\
&\leq\max_{K\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\big(|K|^{-1}h_{K}^{d}\card{(\mathcal{F}^{K})}\big)^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}|\Omega|^{\frac{p-q^{\flat}}{pq^{\flat}}}\big(\sum_{F\in\mathcal{F}_{h}^{ID}}\widetilde{h}_{F}^{-\frac{d(p-q^{\flat})}{q^{\flat}}}{\left\|{\left[\!\left[v\right]\!\right]}\right\|}_{\L^{q}(F)}^{p}\big)^{\frac{1}{p}}.\end{split}

The assertion follows noting that

-\frac{d(p-q^{\flat})}{q^{\flat}}=-\frac{p}{q^{\prime}}-\frac{dp}{p^{\prime}}+\frac{dp}{q^{\prime}}.

∎

Remark 3

(Applicability of
trace inequalities for piecewise
\W^{1,p}
functions
in the analysis of nonconforming Galerkin methods)
.

Inequality (
20
)
is suited for the analysis of interior penalty
discontinuous Galerkin methods
for nonlinear problems; such methods in fact
involve the
\mathbf{L}^{p}
norm of the broken gradient
and the
\L^{p}
norm of the jumps.
For
p<d
, inequality (
23
)
exhibits the maximal Lebesgue regularity
indices on the left-hand side
and on the jump terms,
while inequality (
22
)
is its counterpart with weaker norms;
a variant of both inequalities (namely that with a projection
onto constants over facets of the jumps)
can be employed in the analysis of Crouzeix-Raviart schemes,
as it allows for improved Lebesgue norms
on the left-hand side compared to (
20
).
Inequality (
26
)
is instead concerned with the case
p\geq d

and involves the same Lebesgue norms on
\partial\Omega

and on the facet jumps.
