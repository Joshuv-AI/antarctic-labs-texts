25 C.F.R. § 273.206 — May a contract be appealed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.206
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A contractor may appeal:
(1) An adverse decision or action of the Bureau regarding a contract; or
(2) A decision to cancel a contract for cause.
(b) The Secretary encourages contractors to seek all means of dispute resolution before a formal appeal.
