7 C.F.R. § 2620.3 — Requests.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2620.3
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2620.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2620 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for OIG records shall be submitted to OIG's Office of Counsel and will be processed in accordance with subpart A of part 1 of this title. Specific guidance on how to submit requests (including current contact methods) is available through OIG's Web site, https://www.usda.gov/oig/foiareq.htm, and USDA's public FOIA Web site.
