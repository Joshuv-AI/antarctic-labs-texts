50 U.S.C. § 3124 — Extraterritorial jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:50:3124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3124
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter IV (Protection Of Certain National Security Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is jurisdiction over an offense under section 3121 of this title committed outside the United States if the individual committing the offense is a citizen of the United States or an alien lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence (as defined in section 1101(a)(20) of title 8).
