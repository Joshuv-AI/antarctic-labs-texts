40 C.F.R. § 7.140 — General prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:40:7.140
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-7.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Assistance From The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person in the United States may, on the basis of age, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity receiving EPA assistance.
