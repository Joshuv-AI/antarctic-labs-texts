13 C.F.R. § 107.1640 — SBA access to records of the CRA, Brokers, Dealers and Pool or Trust assemblers.
Citation: us:cfr:13:107.1640
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-107.1640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 107 (Small Business Investment Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CRA and any broker, dealer and Pool or Trust assembler operating under the regulations in this part shall make all books, records and related materials associated with Debentures, Participating Securities and TCs available to SBA for review and copying purposes. Such access shall be at such party's primary place of business during normal business hours.
