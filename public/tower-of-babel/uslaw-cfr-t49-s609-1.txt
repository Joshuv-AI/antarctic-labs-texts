49 C.F.R. § 609.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:609.1
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-609.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 609 (Transportation For Elderly And Handicapped Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish formally the requirements of the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) on transportation for elderly and handicapped persons.
