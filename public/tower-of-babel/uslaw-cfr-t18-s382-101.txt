18 C.F.R. § 382.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:18:382.101
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-382.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 382 (Annual Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish procedures for calculating and assessing annual charges to reimburse the United States for all of the costs incurred by the Commission, other than costs incurred in administering Part I of the Federal Power Act and costs recovered through the Commission's filing fees.
