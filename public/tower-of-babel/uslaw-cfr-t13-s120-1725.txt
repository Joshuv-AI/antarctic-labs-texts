13 C.F.R. § 120.1725 — No Program Preference by Seller or Pool Originator.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.1725
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.1725
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Seller and the Pool Originator must not establish a Program Preference, which is defined in 13 CFR 120.10.
