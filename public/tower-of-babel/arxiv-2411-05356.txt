Xi Wang, Hailou Yao, Lei Shen

Abstract
We study the λ \lambda -pure global dimension of a Grothendieck category 𝒜 \cal A , and provide two different applications about this dimension. We obtain that if the λ \lambda -pure global dimension P λ . gldim ​ 𝒜 < ∞ \mathrm{P}_{\lambda}.\mathrm{gldim}{\cal A}<\infty , then (1) The ordinary bounded derived category (where 𝒜 \cal A has enough projective objects) and the bounded λ \lambda -pure one differ only by a homotopy category; (2) The λ \lambda -pure singularity category 𝐃 λ ​ - ​ s ​ g b ​ ( 𝒜 ) = 0 \mathbf{D}_{\lambda\textrm{-}sg}^{b}({\cal A})=0 . At last, we explore the reason why the general construction of classic Buchweitz-Happel Theorem is not feasible for λ \lambda -pure one.

is a Grothendieck category with enough

-pure injective objects, then
\mathbf{D}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{A})
also can be represented by

-pure injective objects.
This is dual to
\lambda
-pure projective case in

]

In fact, the previous claim about “with enough
\lambda
-pure injective objects" is very complicated.
Rosický
[
24
, Question 1]
set a question: Does there exist a regular cardinal
\lambda
such that a locally presentable additive category has enough
\lambda
-pure injectives?
There seems to be limited research about it at present, and we only find Izurdiaga-S̆aroch
[
17

proved that a ring
R
is right pure-semisimple if and only if there exists a regular uncountable
\lambda
such that the category Mod-
R
has enough
\lambda
-pure injective objects.

If we roughly assume that the answer to Rosický
[
24
, Question 1]
is affirmative, i.e., the Grothendieck category, which is locally presentable by
[
24
, page 2]
, has enough
\lambda
-pure injective objects.
Whether the
\lambda
-pure version of Buchweitz-Happel Theorem can be verified by
\lambda
-pure injective objects?
This is still uncertain, it depends entirely on a conjecture: A chain complex
\bm{F}
is
\lambda
-pure acyclic if and only if any chain map
\bm{F}\rightarrow\bm{E}
is null-homotopic, where
\bm{E}
is a complex with
\lambda
-pure injective objects.
Note this conjecture is the
\lambda
-pure version of
[
25

.
If the conjecture is negative, then we may construct the
\lambda
-pure version of Buchweitz-Happel Theorem as is in the classic case, which is also similar to Chen and Li-Huang.
Else if the conjecture is positive, then it is dual to the
\lambda
-pure projective case. Therefore the general method is not feasible too.
