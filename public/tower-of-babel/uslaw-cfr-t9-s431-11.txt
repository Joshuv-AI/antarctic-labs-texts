9 C.F.R. § 431.11 — Personnel and training.
Citation: us:cfr:9:431.11
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-431.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 431 (Thermally Processed, Commercially Sterile Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All operators of thermal processing systems specified in § 431.6 and container closure technicians must be under the direct supervision of a person who has successfully completed a school of instruction that is generally recognized as adequate for properly training supervisors of canning operations.
