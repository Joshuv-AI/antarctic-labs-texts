14 C.F.R. § 253.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:253.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-253.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 253 (Notice Of Terms Of Contract Of Carriage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set uniform disclosure requirements, which preempt any State requirements on the same subject, for terms incorporated by reference into contracts of carriage for scheduled service in interstate and overseas passenger air transportation.
