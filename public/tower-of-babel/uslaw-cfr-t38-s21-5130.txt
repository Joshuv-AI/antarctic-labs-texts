38 C.F.R. § 21.5130 — Payments; educational assistance allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.5130
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.5130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will apply the following sections in administering benefits payable under 38 U.S.C. Chapter 32:
(a) Section 21.4131—Commencing dates (except paragraph (d)).
(b) Section 21.4135—Discontinuance dates.
(c) Section 21.4138 (except paragraph (b)—Certifications and release of payments.
(d) Section 21.4146—Assignments of benefits prohibited.
(e) Section 21.4136(k) (except paragraph (k)(3))—Mitigating circumstances.
