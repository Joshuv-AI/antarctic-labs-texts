39 C.F.R. § 3021.43 — Due date for replies to the Postal Service.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3021.43
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3021.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3021 (Rules For Appeals Of Postal Service Determinations To Close Or Consolidate Post Offices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Petitioner, and any other participant seeking to have the Commission return the matter for further consideration, may file a reply to the Postal Service response not more than 10 days after the date of the Postal Service response. Replies are limited to issues discussed in the responses of the Postal Service and other participants seeking affirmation of the Postal Service determination.
