25 C.F.R. § 1000.2150 — How may interest or investment income that accrues on funds awarded under a funding agreement be used?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.2150
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.2150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Interest or income earned on investments or deposits of awards made under a funding agreement may be:
(1) Used for any governmental purpose approved by the Tribe/Consortium; or
(2) Used to provide expanded services under the funding agreement and to support some or all of the costs of investment services.
(b) The retention of interest or investment income under paragraph (a) of this section shall not diminish the amount of funds a Tribe/Consortium is entitled to receive under a funding agreement in the year the interest or income is earned or in a subsequent fiscal year.
