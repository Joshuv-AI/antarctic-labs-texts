36 U.S.C. § 140505 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:140505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section140505
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1405 (Military Order Of The Purple Heart Of The United States Of America, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend a constitution and bylaws for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
make contracts;
(4)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(5)
borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property;
(6)
charge and collect membership dues; and
(7)
sue and be sued.
