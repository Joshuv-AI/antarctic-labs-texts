7 C.F.R. § 2903.16 — Organizational management information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2903.16
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2903.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2903 (Biodiesel Fuel Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Specific management information relating to an applicant shall be submitted on a one-time basis as part of the responsibility determination prior to the award of a grant identified under this program, if such information has not been provided previously. Copies of forms recommended for use in fulfilling these requirements will be provided as part of the preaward process.
