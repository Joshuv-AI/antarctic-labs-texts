48 C.F.R. § 1480.601 — 1480.601 Subcontracting limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1480.601
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1480.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1480 (Acquisitions Under The Buy Indian Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In contracts awarded under the Buy Indian Act and this part, the CO must insert the clause FAR 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting.
(b) The CO must also insert the clause at 1452.280-3, Indian Economic Enterprise subcontracting limitations, in all awards to ISBEEs and IEEs pursuant this part.
