Lemma 1

For

\mathscr{d}_{2N}(x)=\mathscr{d}_{2N-3}(xq^{2})

Proof.

This establishes the lemma.

\mathscr{d}_{1}(x)=1+xq

\mathscr{d}_{2}(x)=1

\mathscr{d}_{3}(x)=1+x(q+q^{3})+x^{2}q^{2}
