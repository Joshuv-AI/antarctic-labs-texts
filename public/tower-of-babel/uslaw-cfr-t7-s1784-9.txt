7 C.F.R. § 1784.9 — Grant amount.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1784.9
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1784.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1784 (Rural Alaskan Village Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grants will be made for up to 75 percent of the project development and/or construction costs, which does not include project administrative costs. Pursuant to 7 U.S.C. 1926d, the State of Alaska shall provide 25 percent in matching funds from non-Federal sources.
