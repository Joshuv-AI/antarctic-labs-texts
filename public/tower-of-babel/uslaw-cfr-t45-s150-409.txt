45 C.F.R. § 150.409 — Amendment of notice of assessment or request for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.409
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ALJ may permit CMS to amend its notice of assessment, or permit the respondent to amend a request for hearing that complies with § 150.407(a), if the ALJ finds that no undue prejudice to either party will result.
