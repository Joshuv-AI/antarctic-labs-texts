11 U.S.C. § 1165 — Protection of the public interest
Citation: us:usc:11:1165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1165
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter IV (Railroad Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In applying sections 1166, 1167, 1169, 1170, 1171, 1172, 1173, and 1174 of this title, the court and the trustee shall consider the public interest in addition to the interests of the debtor, creditors, and equity security holders.
