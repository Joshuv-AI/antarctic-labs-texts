42 C.F.R. § 422.686 — Record of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.686
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.686
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A complete record of the proceedings at the hearing is made and transcribed and made available to all parties upon request.
(b) The record may not be closed until a hearing decision has been issued.
