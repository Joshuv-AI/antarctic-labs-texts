22 U.S.C. § 527 — Purchases of implements of war, etc., from American Republics
Citation: us:usc:22:527
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section527
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 10 (Hemispheral Relations); Subchapter II (War Materials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretaries of the Army and of the Navy are authorized to purchase arms, ammunition, and implements of war produced within the jurisdiction of any American republic if such arms, ammunition, or implements of war cannot be produced in the United States.
