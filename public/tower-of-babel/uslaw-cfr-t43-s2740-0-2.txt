43 C.F.R. § 2740.0-2 — -2 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2740.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2740.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2740 (Recreation And Public Purposes Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective is to meet the needs of certain State and local governmental agencies and other qualified organizations for public lands required for recreational and public purposes.
