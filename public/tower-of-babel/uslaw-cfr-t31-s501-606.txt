31 C.F.R. § 501.606 — Reporting and recordkeeping requirements applicable to economic sanctions programs.
Citation: us:cfr:31:501.606
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-501.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 501 (Reporting, Procedures And Penalties Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reporting and recordkeeping requirements set forth in this subpart are applicable to economic sanctions programs for which implementation and administration have been delegated to the Office of Foreign Assets Control.
