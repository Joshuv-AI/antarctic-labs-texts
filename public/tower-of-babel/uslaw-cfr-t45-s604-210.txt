45 C.F.R. § 604.210 — Reporting.

Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 604 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
