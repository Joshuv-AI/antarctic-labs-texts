16 U.S.C. § 1111 — Exemption from taxation
Citation: us:usc:16:1111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1111
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 22 (International Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall not be subject to Federal, State, or municipal taxation in the United States on any real or personal property held by it or on any gift, bequest, or devise to it of any personal or real property, or on its income, whether from governmental appropriations, admission fees, concessions, or donations.
