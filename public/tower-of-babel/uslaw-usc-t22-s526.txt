22 U.S.C. § 526 — Protection of patent rights
Citation: us:usc:22:526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section526
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 10 (Hemispheral Relations); Subchapter II (War Materials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army and the Secretary of the Navy shall in all contracts or agreements for the sale of such matériel fully protect the rights of all citizens of the United States who have patent rights in and to any such matériel which is authorized to be sold and the funds collected for royalties on such patents shall be paid to the owners and holders of such patents.
