12 C.F.R. § 625.23 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:12:625.23
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 625 (Application For Award Of Fees And Other Expenses Under The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the adversary adjudication, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 625.25 of this part.
