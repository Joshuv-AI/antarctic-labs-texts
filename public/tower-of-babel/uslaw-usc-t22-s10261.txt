22 U.S.C. § 10261 — Sanctions exception relating to importation of goods
Citation: us:usc:22:10261
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section10261
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 109 (Burma Unified Through Rigorous Military Accountability); Subchapter V (Sanctions Exception Relating To Importation Of Goods). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The authorities and requirements to impose sanctions under this chapter shall not include the authority or requirement to impose sanctions on the importation of goods.
(b) Good defined
In this section, the term “good” means any article, natural or man-made substance, material, supply, or manufactured product, including inspection and test equipment, and excluding technical data.
