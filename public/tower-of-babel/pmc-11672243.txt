Identification of ANXA1 as a Novel Upstream Negative Regulator of Notch1 Function in AML (Adv. Sci. 48/2024)
Authors: Gang Shao, Xi Wang, Yiting Zheng, Junjie Ma, Lei Wang, Zhibin Yan, Zeyu Sun, Shuyuan Zhang, Hongzhang Wu, Yudie Lv, Hemiao Huang, Jianhu Li, Tianyi Zhu, Bing Yang, Nanxi Wang, Tao Chen, Xuancheng Guo, Yuanting Jin, Jian Kang, Huafeng Wang, Yihai Cao, Caiyun Fu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Anti‐Acute Myeloid Leukemia by NICD Inhibitory Peptides The cover depicts a microscopic view of anbacute myeloid leukemia (AML) cell. Inside the cell membrane, ANXA1 (green) holds onto Notch1 (blue) so tightly resulted in the degradation of Notch1. However, NICD inhibitory peptides competitive bind with ANXA1 in order to release Notch1, and then intracellular domain of Notch1 (NICD) is cut by γ‐secretase (red) to restore its transcriptional regulatory activity and tumor suppressor function in AML. More details can be found in article number 2409726 by Caiyun Fu and co‐workers.
