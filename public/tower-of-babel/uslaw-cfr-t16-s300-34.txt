16 C.F.R. § 300.34 — Reference to existing guaranty on labels not permitted.
Citation: us:cfr:16:300.34
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-300.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 300 (Rules And Regulations Under The Wool Products Labeling Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No representation or suggestion that a wool product is guaranteed under the act by the Government, or any branch thereof shall be made on or in the stamp, tag, label, or other mark of identification, applied or affixed to wool products.
