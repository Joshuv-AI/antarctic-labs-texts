39 U.S.C. § 605 — Searching vessels for letters
Citation: us:usc:39:605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section605
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 6 (Private Carriage Of Letters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer or employee of the Postal Service performing duties related to the inspection of postal matters, when instructed by the Postal Service to make examinations and seizures, and any customs officer without special instructions shall search vessels for letters which may be on board, or which may have been conveyed contrary to law.
