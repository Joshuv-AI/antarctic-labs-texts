29 C.F.R. § 4010.14 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4010.14
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4010.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4010 (Annual Financial And Actuarial Information Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If all of the information required under this part is not provided within the specified time limit, PBGC may assess a separate penalty under ERISA section 4071 against the filer and each member of the filer's controlled group (other than an exempt entity). PBGC may also pursue other equitable or legal remedies available to it under the law.
