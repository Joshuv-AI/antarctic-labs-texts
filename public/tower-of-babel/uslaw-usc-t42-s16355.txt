42 U.S.C. § 16355 — National Laboratory designation
Citation: us:usc:42:16355
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16355
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IX (Research And Development); Part I (Research Administration And Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After August 8, 2005, the Secretary shall not designate a facility that is not listed in section 15801(3) of this title as a National Laboratory.
