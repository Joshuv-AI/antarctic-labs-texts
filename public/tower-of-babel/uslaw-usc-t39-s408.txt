39 U.S.C. § 408 — International money-order exchanges
Citation: us:usc:39:408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section408
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 4 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may make arrangements with other governments, with which postal conventions are or may be concluded, for the exchange of sums of money by means of postal orders. It shall fix limitations on the amount which may be so exchanged and the rates of exchange.
