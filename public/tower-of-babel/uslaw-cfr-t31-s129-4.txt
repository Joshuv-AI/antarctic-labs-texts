31 C.F.R. § 129.4 — Recordkeeping requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:31:129.4
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-129.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 129 (Portfolio Investment Survey Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reporters shall maintain all information used in preparing a report under this part for the period specified in the notice published by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to section 129.3, and shall make this information available for review and inspection at the request of the Department of the Treasury.
