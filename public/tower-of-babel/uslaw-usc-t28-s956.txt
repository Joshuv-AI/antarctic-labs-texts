28 U.S.C. § 956 — Powers and duties of clerks and deputies
Citation: us:usc:28:956
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section956
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 57 (General Provisions Applicable To Court Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The clerk of each court and his deputies and assistants shall exercise the powers and perform the duties assigned to them by the court.
