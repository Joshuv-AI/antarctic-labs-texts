22 U.S.C. § 287e–3 — Limitation on the United States share of assessments for United Nations regular budget
Citation: us:usc:22:287e–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section287e–3
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVI (United Nations Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds available to the Department of State shall be used to pay the United States share of assessed contributions for the regular budget of the United Nations in an amount greater than 22 percent of the total of all assessed contributions for that budget.
