45 U.S.C. § 917 — Applicability of National Environmental Policy Act
Citation: us:usc:45:917
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section917
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 18 (Milwaukee Railroad Restructuring). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of the National Environmental Policy Act [42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.] shall not apply to transactions carried out pursuant to this chapter.
