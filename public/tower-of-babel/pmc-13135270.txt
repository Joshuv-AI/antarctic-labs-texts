Correction: Circular RNA F-circSR derived from SLC34A2-ROS1 fusion gene promotes cell migration in non-small cell lung cancer
Authors: Ke Wu, Xun Liao, Youling Gong, Juan He, Jian-Kang Zhou, Shuangyan Tan, Wenchen Pu, Canhua Huang, Yu-Quan Wei, Yong Peng
Published: 2026

Correction: Mol Cancer 18, 98 (2019)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-019-1028-9

Following the publication of the original article [1], the authors identified the figure-preparation errors. They have re-checked their raw data and experimental records for these experiments, and prepared the corrected figure panels.

The affected panels and the corrected figures are as follows:

1. Fig. 2D / Fig. 2H: Transwell migration images intended for Fig. 2G were inadvertently used.

2. Fig. 2E: The image for H1299-SR1 (0 h) was inadvertently duplicated from H1299-SR2 (0 h).

3. Fig. 3C: The image for F-circSR2-M1 was inadvertently duplicated from F-circSR1-M1.

4. Fig. S2F: The transwell migration image for A549-Ctrl-siSR1-2 was inadvertently duplicated from A549-Ctrl-siCtrl.
