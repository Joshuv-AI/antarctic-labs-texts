12 C.F.R. § 404.38 — No private right of action.
Citation: us:cfr:12:404.38
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-404.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 404 (Information Disclosure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed as creating any right, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or equity by a party against Ex-Im Bank or the United States.
