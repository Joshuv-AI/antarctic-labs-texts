45 C.F.R. § 2552.111 — Under what conditions may an agency or organization sponsor a Foster Grandparent project without AmeriCorps funding?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2552.111
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2552.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2552 (Foster Grandparent Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible agency or organization who wishes to sponsor a Foster Grandparent project without AmeriCorps funding must make an application through the designated grants management system which is approved by AmeriCorps and documented through the Notice of Grant Agreement (NGA).
