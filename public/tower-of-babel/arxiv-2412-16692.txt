2 + ⁣ + 2^{++} Di-gluonium from LSR at higher order Note: Talk given at the 14th International Conference in High-Energy Physics - HEPMAD24 (21-26th October 2024, Antananarivo-Madagascar). Journal: Journal of Subatomic Particles and Cosmology
Siyuan Li, Stephan Narison, Davidson Rabetiarivony, Tom Steele

Abstract
We improve the determination of the mass and coupling of the 2 + ⁣ + 2^{++} tensor di-gluonium by using relativistic QCD Laplace sum rules (LSR). In so doing, we evaluate the next-to-leading order (NLO) corrections to the perturbative (PT) and ⟨ α s ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle\alpha_{s}G^{2}\rangle condensate and the lowest order (LO) ⟨ G 3 ⟩ \langle G^{3}\rangle contributions to the 2 + ⁣ + 2^{++} di-gluonium two-point correlator. Within a vacuum saturation estimate ( k G = 1 k_{G}=1 ) of the dimension-eight gluon condensates, we obtain: M T = 3028 ​ ( 287 ) ​ MeV M_{T}=3028(287)\penalty\,\mbox{MeV} and the renormalization group invariant (RGI) coupling f ^ T = 224 ​ ( 33 ) ​ MeV \hat{f}_{T}=224(33)\penalty\,\mbox{MeV} . Assuming that the factorization hypothesis can be violated, we study the effect of the violation factor k G k_{G} on the results and obtain: M T = 3188 ​ ( 337 ) ​ MeV M_{T}=3188(337)\penalty\,\mbox{MeV} and f ^ T = 245 ​ ( 32 ) ​ MeV \hat{f}_{T}=245(32)\penalty\,\mbox{MeV} for k G = ( 3 ± 2 ) k_{G}=(3\pm 2) . Our estimation does not favour the interpretation of the observed f 2 ​ ( 2010 ) f_{2}(2010) , f 2 ​ ( 2300 ) f_{2}(2300) and f 2 ​ ( 2340 ) f_{2}(2340) as pure glueball state.

The two-point correlation function

We shall work with the two-point function:

\displaystyle\psi^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}_{T}(q^{2})

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle i\int d^{4}x\,e^{iqx}\langle 0|\mathcal{T}\theta^{\mu\nu}_{G}(x)(\theta^{\rho\sigma}_{G}(0))^{{\dagger}}|0\rangle

(1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bigg(P^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\equiv\eta^{\mu\rho}\eta^{\nu\sigma}+\eta^{\mu\sigma}\eta^{\nu\rho}-\frac{2}{n-1}\eta^{\mu\nu}\eta^{\rho\sigma}\bigg)\psi_{T}(q^{2}),

built from the gluon component of the energy momentum tensor:

\theta^{\mu\nu}_{G}=\alpha_{s}\left[-G^{\mu,a}_{\alpha}G^{\nu\alpha}_{a}+\frac{1}{4}G^{a}_{\alpha\beta}G^{\alpha\beta}_{a}\right],

(2)

with:

\eta^{\mu\nu}\equiv g^{\mu\nu}-q^{\mu}q^{\nu}/q^{2}~~\mbox{and}~~P_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}P^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=2(n^{2}-n-2),

(3)

where:
n=4+2\epsilon
is the space time dimension used for dimensional regularization and renormalization.

Comments and conclusion

We have presented improved predictions of QSSR of the mass and coupling of
2^{++}
tensor di-gluonium state using LSR method. We have evaluated the PT and
D=4
gluon condensate NLO corrections and tested the effect of the estimate of the
D=8
gluon condensates on the results.

– The PT (resp.
\langle\alpha_{s}G^{2}\rangle
) NLO corrections increase the mass by about
561
(resp.
376
)
MeV
from its LO value:
2091\penalty\,\mbox{MeV}

LNSR
.

– Moving the mass from its LO value to the NLO one:
3028\,\penalty\,\mbox{MeV}
, the
\alpha_{s}
corrections increase the value of the coupling by about
84\penalty\,\mbox{MeV}
. However, if one fixes the mass at its LO value, the coupling is slightly affected by the NLO corrections.

– Our results for NLO
\langle\alpha_{s}G^{2}\rangle
and LO
\langle G^{3}\rangle
condensates term do not agree with the ones in Ref.
ZHUG
. The discrepancy may come from the different current used by the authors in
ZHUG
.

– Compared to other approaches, the predicted mass of lattice QCD which is expected to be in the range
(2.27\sim 2.67)\penalty\,\mbox{GeV}

LATTG06
,
LATTG12
,
TEPER
is slightly lower than ours and the one of instanton liquid model (
1525\penalty\,\mbox{MeV}
) is too low. However, our estimated mass goes in line within the errors with the ones of ADS/QCD

– Confronted to experimental data, our prediction does not favour the interpretation of

,
f_{2}(2300)
and
