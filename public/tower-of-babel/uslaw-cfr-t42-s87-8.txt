42 C.F.R. § 87.8 — How may a grantee use grant funds?
Citation: us:cfr:42:87.8
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-87.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 87 (National Institute For Occupational Safety And Health Research And Demonstration Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee shall only spend funds it receives under this part according to the approved application and budget, the authorizing legislation, the terms and conditions of the grant award, the applicable cost principles specified in subpart Q of 45 CFR part 74, and the regulations of this part.
