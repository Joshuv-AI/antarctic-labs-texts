30 C.F.R. § 816.42 — Hydrologic balance: Water quality standards and effluent limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:816.42
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-816.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 816 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—Surface Mining Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Discharges of water from areas disturbed by surface mining activities shall be made in compliance with all applicable State and Federal water quality laws and regulations and with the effluent limitations for coal mining promulgated by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency set forth in 40 CFR part 434.
