46 U.S.C. § 55110 — Transportation of valueless material or dredged material
Citation: us:usc:46:55110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55110
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 551 (Coastwise Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 55102 of this title applies to the transportation of valueless material or dredged material, regardless of whether it has commercial value, from a point in the United States or on the high seas within the exclusive economic zone, to another point in the United States or on the high seas within the exclusive economic zone.
