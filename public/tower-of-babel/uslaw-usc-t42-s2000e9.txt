42 U.S.C. § 2000e–9 — Conduct of hearings and investigations pursuant to section 161 of title 29
Citation: us:usc:42:2000e–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000e–9
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter VI (Equal Employment Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of all hearings and investigations conducted by the Commission or its duly authorized agents or agencies, section 161 of title 29 shall apply.
