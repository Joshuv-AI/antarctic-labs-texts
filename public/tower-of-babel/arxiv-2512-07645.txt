\displaystyle W^{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(\Omega):=\left\{u\in L^{\widehat{G}_{x}}(\Omega):J_{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(\lambda u)<+\infty\quad\textnormal{ for some }\lambda>0\right\},

where

J_{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(u):=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega}G_{x,y}\left(D_{s(.,.)}u\right)d\mu,

with
D_{s(.,.)}u:=D_{s(.,.)}u(x,y):=\displaystyle\frac{u(x)-u(y)}{|x-y|^{s(x,y)}}
and
d\mu:=\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{~d}y}{|x-y|^{N}}.

It is well known that
d\mu
is a regular Borel measure on the set
\Omega\times\Omega
.

The space
W^{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(\Omega)
is endowed with the norm

\|u\|_{W^{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(\Omega)}:=\|u\|_{L^{\widehat{G}_{x}}(\Omega)}+[u]_{s(.,.),G_{x,y}},

(2.1)

where
[.]_{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}
is the so called Gagliardo seminorm defined by

[u]_{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}=\inf\left\{\lambda>0:J_{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}\left(\frac{u}{\lambda}\right)\leqslant 1\right\}.

Remark 2.3

(see
[
29
]
)
.

Since the assumption
(g_{4})
implies that the function
G_{x,y}
and
\widetilde{G}_{x,y}
satisfy the

-condition, then the space
W^{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(\Omega)
is a reflexive and separable Banach space. Moreover, if

is convex on

, then the space
W^{s(.,.),G_{x,y}}(\Omega)
is a uniformly convex space.

\displaystyle+\epsilon\left(\int_{\Omega}\widehat{a}_{x}(|u_{0}|)u_{0}\varphi dx-\int_{\Omega}h(x)u_{0}^{-m(x)}\varphi dx\right)

and

\displaystyle\ I_{2}

\displaystyle=\epsilon\int_{\Omega_{\varepsilon}}\int_{\Omega_{\varepsilon}}a_{x,y}\!\left(\left|D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}\right|\right)D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}D_{s(.,.)}\varphi d\mu

\displaystyle-\epsilon\int_{\Omega_{\varepsilon}}\widehat{a}_{x}(|u_{0}|)u_{0}\varphi dx.

Considering that
u_{0}>0
and the Lebesgue measure of the integration domain

\Omega_{\varepsilon}
tends to zero as
\epsilon\to 0
, we have

\int_{\Omega_{\varepsilon}}\int_{\Omega_{\epsilon}}a_{x,y}\!\left(\left|D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}\right|\right)D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}D_{s(.,.)}\varphi d\mu\to 0,\quad\text{as }\epsilon\to 0,

and

\int_{\Omega_{\epsilon}}\widehat{a}_{x}(|u_{0}|)u_{0}\varphi dx\to 0,\quad\text{as }\varepsilon\to 0.

Moreover, considering that
a_{x,y}(\cdot)\in(0,\infty)
, we can drop the term

-\int_{\Omega_{\epsilon}}\int_{\Omega_{\epsilon}}a_{x,y}\!\left(\left|D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}\right|\right)D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}d\mu-\int_{\Omega^{\epsilon}}\widehat{a}_{x}(|u_{0}|)u_{0}^{2}dx,

in (
3.8
) as it is negative. Therefore, by dividing (
3.8
)
by
\epsilon
and letting
\epsilon\to 0
, we find

\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega}a_{x,y}\!\left(\left|D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}\right|\right)D_{s(.,.)}u_{0}D_{s(.,.)}\varphi d\mu+\int_{\Omega}\widehat{a}_{x}(|u_{0}|)u_{0}\varphi dx-\int_{\Omega}h(x)u_{0}^{-m(x)}\varphi dx\geq 0.

(3.9)

Since
\varphi
