15 U.S.C. § 1720 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:1720
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1720
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 42 (Interstate Land Sales). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter.
