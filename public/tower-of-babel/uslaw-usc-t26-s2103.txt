26 U.S.C. § 2103 — Definition of gross estate
Citation: us:usc:26:2103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2103
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 11 (Estate Tax); Subchapter B (Estates Of Nonresidents Not Citizens). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of the tax imposed by section 2101, the value of the gross estate of every decedent nonresident not a citizen of the United States shall be that part of his gross estate (determined as provided in section 2031) which at the time of his death is situated in the United States.
