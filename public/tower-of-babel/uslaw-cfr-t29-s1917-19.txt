29 C.F.R. § 1917.19 — Movement of barges and railcars.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1917.19
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1917.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1917 (Marine Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Barges and railcars shall not be moved by cargo runners (running rigging) from vessel cargo booms, cranes or other equipment not suitable for the purpose.
