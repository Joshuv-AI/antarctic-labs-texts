Q*-normal spaces
Hamant Kumar, Neeraj Kumar Tomar

Abstract
. In this paper, using Q ∗ \displaystyle Q^{*} -closed sets, we introduce a new version of normality called, Q ∗ \displaystyle Q^{*} -normality which is a weak form of normality. Further utilizing Q ∗ ​ g \displaystyle Q^{*}g -closed sets, we obtain some characterizations of Q ∗ \displaystyle Q^{*} -normal and normal spaces and also obtain some preservation theorems for Q ∗ \displaystyle Q^{*} -normal spaces.
