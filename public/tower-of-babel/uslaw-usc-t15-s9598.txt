15 U.S.C. § 9598 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:9598
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9598
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter VII (Administrative Powers Of The Agency; Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Under Secretary $110,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2021 through 2025 to carry out this chapter, of which—
(1)
a majority shall be used in each such fiscal year to carry out the MBDA Business Center Program under part B of subchapter I, including the component of that program relating to specialty centers; and
(2)
$20,000,000 shall be used in each such fiscal year to carry out subchapter III.
