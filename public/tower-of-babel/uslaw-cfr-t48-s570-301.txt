48 C.F.R. § 570.301 — 570.301 Market survey.
Citation: us:cfr:48:570.301
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-570.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 570 (Acquiring Leasehold Interests In Real Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conduct a market survey to identify potential sources. Use information available in GSA or from other sources to identify locations capable of meeting the Government's requirements.
