12 C.F.R. § 229.40 — Effect of merger transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:12:229.40
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-229.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 229 (Availability Of Funds And Collection Of Checks (Regulation Cc)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, two or more banks that have engaged in a merger transaction may be considered to be separate banks for a period of one year following the consummation of the merger transaction.
