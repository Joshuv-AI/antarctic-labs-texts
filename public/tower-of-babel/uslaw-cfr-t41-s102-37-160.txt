41 C.F.R. § 102-37.160 — -37.160 Distribution documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.160
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All SASPs must document the distribution of Federal surplus property on forms that are prenumbered, provide for donees to indicate the primary purposes for which they are acquiring property, and include the:
(a) Certifications and agreements in §§ 102-37.340 and 102-37.345; and
(b) Period of restriction during which the donee must use the property for the purpose for which it was acquired.
