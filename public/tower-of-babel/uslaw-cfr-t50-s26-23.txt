50 C.F.R. § 26.23 — Exception for entry to the headquarters office.
Citation: us:cfr:50:26.23
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-26.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 26 (Public Entry And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The headquarters office of any national wildlife refuge is open to public access and admission during regularly established business hours.
