41 C.F.R. § 101-1.108 — -1.108 Agency implementation and supplementation of FPMR.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-1.108
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-1.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Chapters 102 through 150 of this title are available for agency implementation and supplementation of FPMR contained in chapter 101 of this title. Supplementation pertains to agency regulations in the subject matter of FPMR but not yet issued in chapter 101.
