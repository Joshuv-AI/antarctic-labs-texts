22 C.F.R. § 1004.3 — Requirement of open meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1004.3
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1004.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1004 (Rules For Implementing Open Meetings Within The Inter-American Foundation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members shall not jointly conduct or dispose of agency business other than in accordance with this section. Except as provided in § 1004.4 every portion of every meeting of the agency shall be open to public observation.
