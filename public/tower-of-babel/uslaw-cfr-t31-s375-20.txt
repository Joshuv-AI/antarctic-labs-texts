31 C.F.R. § 375.20 — When will the Treasury Department decide which offers to accept?
Citation: us:cfr:31:375.20
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-375.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 375 (Marketable Treasury Securities Redemption Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will determine which offers or portions of offers to accept after the closing time for receipt of tenders. All such determinations will be final.
