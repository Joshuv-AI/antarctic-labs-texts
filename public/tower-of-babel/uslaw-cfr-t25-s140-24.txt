25 C.F.R. § 140.24 — Cash payments only to Indians.
Citation: us:cfr:25:140.24
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-140.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 140 (Licensed Indian Traders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Traders must not pay Indians in tokens, tickets, store orders, or anything else of that character. Payment must be made in money, or in credit if the Indian is indebted to the trader.
