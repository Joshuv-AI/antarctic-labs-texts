22 U.S.C. § 2082 — Participation in Interama by States and foreign countries
Citation: us:usc:22:2082
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2082
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 29A (Inter-American Cultural And Trade Center). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized, by proclamation or in such other manner as he may deem proper, to invite the several States of the United States and foreign countries to take part in Interama, except that no Communist de facto government holding any people in subjugation shall be invited to participate.
