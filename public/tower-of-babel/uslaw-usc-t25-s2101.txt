25 U.S.C. § 2101 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:25:2101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2101
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 23 (Development Of Tribal Mineral Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter, the term—
(1)
“Indian” means any individual Indian or Alaska Native who owns land or interests in land the title to which is held in trust by the United States or is subject to a restriction against alienation imposed by the United States;
(2)
“Indian tribe” means any Indian tribe, band, nation, pueblo, community, rancheria, colony, or other group which owns land or interests in land title to which is held in trust by the United States or is subject to a restriction against alienation imposed by the United States; and
(3)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
