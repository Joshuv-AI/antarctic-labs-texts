10 U.S.C. § 1173 — Enlisted members: discharge for hardship
Citation: us:usc:10:1173
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1173
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 59 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary concerned, a regular enlisted member of an armed force who has dependents may be discharged for hardship.
