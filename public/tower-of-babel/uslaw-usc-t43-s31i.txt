43 U.S.C. § 31i — Report on resource research activities
Citation: us:usc:43:31i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section31i
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 2 (United States Geological Survey). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Once every five years the National Academy of Sciences shall review and report on the resource research activities of the Survey.
