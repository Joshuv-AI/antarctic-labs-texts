Coinductive well-foundedness
Iosif Petrakis

Abstract
We introduce a coinductive version ∃ WF ℕ \exists\mathrm{WF}_{{\mathbb{N}}} of the well-foundedness of ℕ {\mathbb{N}} that is used in our proof within minimal logic of the constructive counterpart CLNP \mathrm{CLNP} to the standard least number principle LNP \mathrm{LNP} . According to CLNP \mathrm{CLNP} , an inhabited complemented subset of ℕ {\mathbb{N}} has a least element if and only if it is downset located. The use of complemented subsets of ℕ {\mathbb{N}} in the formulation of CLNP \mathrm{CLNP} , instead of subsets of ℕ {\mathbb{N}} , allows a positive approach to the subject that avoids negation. Generealising ∃ WF ℕ \exists\mathrm{WF}_{{\mathbb{N}}} , we define ∃ \exists -well-founded sets and we prove their fundamental properties.



Keywords
: constructive mathematics, number theory, least number principle, complemented subsets, well-founded sets
