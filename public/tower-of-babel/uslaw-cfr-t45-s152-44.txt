45 C.F.R. § 152.44 — End of PCIP program coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:45:152.44
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-152.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 152 (Pre-Existing Condition Insurance Plan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective January 1, 2014, coverage under the PCIP program (45 CFR part 152) will end.
