41 C.F.R. § 60-30.14 — -30.14 Designation of Administrative Law Judges.
Citation: us:cfr:41:60-30.14
Creator: Chapter 60—Office Of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Equal Employment Opportunity, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-60-30.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 60-30 (30—Rules Of Practice For Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hearings shall be held before an Administrative Law Judge of the Department of Labor who shall be designated by the Chief Administrative Law Judge of the Department of Labor. After commencement of the proceeding but prior to the designation of an Administrative Law Judge, pleadings and papers shall be filed with the Chief Administrative Law Judge.
