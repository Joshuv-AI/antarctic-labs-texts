35 U.S.C. § 134 — Appeal to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board
Citation: us:usc:35:134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section134
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 12 (Examination Of Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Patent Applicant.—
An applicant for a patent, any of whose claims has been twice rejected, may appeal from the decision of the primary examiner to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board, having once paid the fee for such appeal.
(b) Patent Owner.—
A patent owner in a reexamination may appeal from the final rejection of any claim by the primary examiner to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board, having once paid the fee for such appeal.
