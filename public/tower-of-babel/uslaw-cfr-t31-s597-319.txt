31 C.F.R. § 597.319 — U.S. financial institution.
Citation: us:cfr:31:597.319
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-597.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 597 (Foreign Terrorist Organizations Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term U.S. financial institution means:
(a) Any financial institution organized under the laws of the United States, including such financial institution's foreign branches;
(b) Any financial institution operating or doing business in the United States; or
(c) Those branches, offices and agencies of foreign financial institutions which are located in the United States, but not such foreign financial institutions' other foreign branches, offices, or agencies.
