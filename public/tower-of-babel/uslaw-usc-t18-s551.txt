18 U.S.C. § 551 — Concealing or destroying invoices or other papers
Citation: us:usc:18:551
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section551
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 27 (Customs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever willfully conceals or destroys any invoice, book, or paper relating to any merchandise imported into the United States, after an inspection thereof has been demanded by the collector of any collection district; or
Whoever conceals or destroys at any time any such invoice, book, or paper for the purpose of suppressing any evidence of fraud therein contained—
Shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.
