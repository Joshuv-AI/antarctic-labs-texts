TIGER-FLOWER, known botanically as Tigridia, a genus of
bulbous plants (natural order Iridaceae), natives of Mexico,
Central America, Peru and Chile. They have long narrow
plicately-veined leaves springing from the bulb and a stem bearing
two or three scattered smaller leaves and above a few flowers
emerging from a spathe. The flowers are spotted (whence the
name tiger-flower or tiger-iris) and have free segments springing
from a tube; the three large broad outer segments are concavely
spreading, the three inner are much smaller and more erect.
T. pawnia (Flower of Tigris) has large flowers with a golden
orange, white or yellow ground colour.
