49 C.F.R. § 213.141 — Self-guarded frogs.
Citation: us:cfr:49:213.141
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-213.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 213 (Track Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The raised guard on a self-guarded frog shall not be worn more than three-eighths of an inch.
(b) If repairs are made to a self-guarded frog without removing it from service, the guarding face shall be restored before rebuilding the point.
