25 U.S.C. § 5631 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:25:5631
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5631
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 48 (Indian Trust Asset Reform); Subchapter III (Improving Efficiency And Streamlining Processes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to ensure a more efficient and streamlined administration of duties of the Secretary of the Interior with respect to providing services and programs to Indians and Indian tribes, including the management of Indian trust resources.
