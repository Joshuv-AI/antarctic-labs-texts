12 C.F.R. § 1261.15 — Minimum number of member directorships.

Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1261.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1261 (Federal Home Loan Bank Directors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except with respect to member directorships of a Bank resulting from the merger of any two or more Banks, the number of member directorships allocated to each state shall not be less than the number of directorships allocated to that state on December 31, 1960.
