25 U.S.C. § 125 — Expenditure of moneys of tribes of Quapaw Agency
Citation: us:usc:25:125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section125
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter II (Disbursement Of Moneys And Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No moneys shall be expended from tribal or individual funds belonging to the Quapaw or other tribes of Indians of the Quapaw Agency in the State of Oklahoma without specific authority of law.
