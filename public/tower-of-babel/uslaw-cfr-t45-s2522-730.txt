45 C.F.R. § 2522.730 — How and when do I submit my evaluation to the AmeriCorps?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.730
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you are an existing grantee recompeting for AmeriCorps funds for the first time, you must submit a summary of your evaluation efforts or plan to date, and a copy of any evaluation that has been completed, as part of your application for funding.
(b) If you again compete for AmeriCorps funding after a second three-year grant cycle, you must submit the completed evaluation with your application for funding.
