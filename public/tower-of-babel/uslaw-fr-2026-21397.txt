AGENCY: Federal Railroad Administration (FRA), Federal Transit 
Administration (FTA), U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Notice, request for comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces that FRA and FTA (Agencies) have 
received and reviewed an application package from the State of Utah 
(State), Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT), requesting 
participation in the Surface Transportation Project Delivery Program 
(Program). Under the Program, the Agencies may assign, and a State may 
assume, responsibilities under the National Environmental Policy Act 
(NEPA), and all or part of the Agencies' responsibilities for 
environmental review, consultation, or other actions required under any 
Federal environmental laws except certain statutory exclusions with 
respect to one or more railroad projects and one or more public 
transportation projects within the State. The Agencies have determined 
the application package to be complete and developed a draft Memorandum 
of Understanding (MOU) with the State outlining how UDOT will implement 
the Program with the Agencies' oversight. The public is invited to 
comment on UDOT's request, including its application package and the 
proposed MOU, which includes the proposed assignments and assumptions 
of environmental review, consultation, and other activities.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Background

    Section 327 of Title 23, United States Code (23 U.S.C. 327) 
establishes the Surface Transportation Project Delivery Program, also 
referred to as the NEPA Assignment Program (Program). It allows the 
Secretary of the U.S. Department of Transportation (Secretary) to 
assign, and a State to assume, responsibility for all or part of the 
Secretary's responsibilities for environmental review, consultation, or 
other actions required under the National Environmental Policy Act 
(NEPA) (42 U.S.C. 4321-4370h) and any Federal environmental law with 
respect to one or more highway projects within the State, as well as 
one or more railroad, public transportation, and/or multimodal 
projects. FRA is authorized to act on behalf of the Secretary with 
respect to these matters for railroad projects and FTA is authorized to 
act on behalf of the Secretary with respect to these matters for public 
transportation projects.
    The State of Utah initially participated in the Federal Highway 
Administration's (FHWA's) Program. In accordance with 23 U.S.C. 327, 
the NEPA Assignment Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) was executed 
between the State, acting through UDOT, and FHWA and became effective 
on January 17, 2017. After the initial MOU term, the agreement was 
renewed on May 26, 2022. Since being accepted into the FHWA NEPA 
Assignment Program, the State has successfully completed four audits, 
seven self-assessments, and one monitoring event. FHWA has consistently 
publicly stated that the State continues to meet all requirements of 
the NEPA Assignment Program. The State will have participated in the 
Program for 10 years on December 17, 2027.
    Pursuant to 23 CFR 773.107(b)(1) and (2), UDOT provided public 
notice of its draft joint application requesting FRA and FTA NEPA 
assignment responsibilities on July 29, 2026. It was available for a 
30-day comment period, with comments due by the close of business on 
August 28, 2026. The notice was published in a statewide newspaper and 
is available at: https://connect.udot.utah.gov/events/notice-of-availability-of-the-utah-department-of-transportations-application-to-assume-the-federal-railroad-administrations-and-federal-transit-administrations-nepa-responsi/. UDOT also sent notice of the joint 
application by email and letter with request for comment to all transit 
agencies in Utah, metropolitan planning organizations, regional 
planning organizations, associations of governments, regulatory 
agencies, and Native American Tribes. UDOT notified State agencies 
through the State of Utah's Resource Development Coordination Council, 
which is responsible for notifying all State agencies and compiling any 
comments received related to actions that may affect the physical 
resources of the State. On September 3, 2026, UDOT formally submitted 
its joint application package to the Agencies. The submission includes 
a verification of Statewide notice and solicitation of public comment, 
Utah's determination that the State of Utah's Public Records Act is 
comparable to the Federal Freedom of Information Act, the draft MOU, 
and the UDOT Commissioner's signature approving the application.
    Under the proposed MOU, the Agencies would assign to the State the 
responsibility for making decisions on railroad and public 
transportation projects as described in UDOT's application and in Part 
2 of the draft MOU. As provided in subpart 2.3, UDOT is requesting to 
assume the Agencies' responsibilities under NEPA for the following 
covered classes of railroad and public transportation projects upon 
execution of the Program MOU with the Agencies: all railroad projects 
in Utah whose source of Federal funding comes from FRA or require FRA 
approvals; all public transportation projects in Utah whose source of 
Federal funding comes from FTA or requires FTA approvals; and all 
projects funded by other Federal agencies, or projects without any 
Federal funding, that also require FRA or FTA approvals. As provided in 
subparts 2.1.1 and 2.1.2, UDOT is requesting to assume the Agencies' 
responsibilities under NEPA for these covered railroad and public 
transportation projects where UDOT is the Direct Recipient and 
responsible for the design (for FTA) or final design (for FRA) and 
construction of the project, or where UDOT is designated in a project-
specific agreement as the project sponsor or co-sponsor. For these 
projects, UDOT requests to assume only the Agencies' NEPA 
responsibilities; the request does not include assuming the NEPA 
responsibilities of other Federal agencies. UDOT may assume the 
Agencies' responsibilities under NEPA for locally administered railroad 
or public transportation projects by mutual agreement between the State 
and FRA or FTA, as applicable, pursuant to the requirements of 23 
U.S.C. 327(l). In addition, UDOT would establish appropriate 
relationships with other Operating Administration(s) involved in a 
multimodal project, including cooperating agency, participating agency, 
and lead or co-lead agency relationships under NEPA. Lastly, UDOT may 
rely on other Federal agencies' NEPA analyses consistent with NEPA, 
USDOT, FRA and FTA regulations, policies, and guidance, as applicable. 
UDOT's assumption of these NEPA Assignment responsibilities will 
provide for the highest degree of program-wide consistency and 
efficiency in document review and agency coordination. It will also 
provide the greatest opportunity for streamlining benefits.
    Excluded from assignment are the following:
    (1) Railroad or public transportation projects that cross State 
boundaries or that cross or are adjacent to international boundaries. 
For purposes of the State's application and the proposed MOU, a project 
is considered ``adjacent to international boundaries'' if it requires 
the issuance of a new, or modification of an existing, Presidential 
Permit.
    (2) As provided at 23 U.S.C. 327(a)(2)(D), any railroad or public 
transportation project that is not assumed by the State as identified 
in the State's application and under subparts 2.3 through 2.6 of the 
proposed MOU, remains the responsibility of FRA or FTA.

    The proposed MOU would allow UDOT to act in the place of FRA and 
FTA in carrying out the environmental review-related functions 
described above, except with respect to Government-to-Government 
consultations with federally recognized Indian Tribes. UDOT would 
continue to handle routine consultations with the Tribes and 
understands that a Tribe has the right to direct consultation with FRA 
or FTA upon request. UDOT may assist FRA or FTA with Government-to-
Government consultations, with consent of a Tribe, but FRA and FTA 
remain responsible for the consultation.
    In addition, UDOT would not assume FRA's or FTA's responsibilities 
for conformity determinations required under Section 176 of the CAA (42 
U.S.C. 7506), or any responsibility under 23 U.S.C. 134 or 135, or 
under 49 U.S.C. 5303 or 5304.
    FRA and FTA will consider the comments submitted on UDOT's joint 
application and the proposed MOU. A copy of the joint application 
package and proposed MOU may be viewed on the docket (FRA-2026-2740) at 
https://www.regulations.gov. A copy also may be viewed on UDOT's 
website at: https://connect.udot.utah.gov/about-us/program-development-group/environmental-division/. Any final MOU approved by FRA and FTA 
may include changes based on comments and consultations relating to the 
proposed MOU and will be made publicly available.
