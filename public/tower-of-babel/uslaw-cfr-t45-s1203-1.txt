45 C.F.R. § 1203.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1203.1
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1203.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1203 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs—Effectuation Of Title Vi Of The Civil Rights Act Of 1964). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate the provisions of title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (hereafter referred to as title VI), to the end that a person in the United States shall not, on the ground of race, color, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to discrimination under a program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance from ACTION.
