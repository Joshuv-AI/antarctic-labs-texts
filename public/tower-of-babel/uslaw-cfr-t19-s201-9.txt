19 C.F.R. § 201.9 — Methods employed in obtaining information.
Citation: us:cfr:19:201.9
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-201.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 201 (Rules Of General Application). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In obtaining information necessary to carry out its functions and duties, the Commission may employ any means authorized by law. In general, the Commission obtains pertinent information from its own files, from other agencies of the Government, through questionnaires and correspondence, through field work by members of the Commission's staff, and from testimony and other information presented at the hearings.
