21 C.F.R. § 369.7 — Warnings required by official compendia.
Citation: us:cfr:21:369.7
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-369.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 369 (Interpretative Statements Re Warnings On Drugs And Devices For Over-The-Counter Sale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any drug included in the official compendia defined by the act shall bear such warning or caution statement as may be required by such compendia, and no statement in subpart B or subpart C of this part is intended to alter, modify, or permit the omission of any such statement required by such compendia.
