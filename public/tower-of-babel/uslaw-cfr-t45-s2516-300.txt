45 C.F.R. § 2516.300 — Who may participate in a school-based service-learning program?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.300
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Students who are enrolled in elementary or secondary schools on a full-time or part-time basis may participate in school-based programs.
