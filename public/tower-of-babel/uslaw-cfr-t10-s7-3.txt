10 C.F.R. § 7.3 — Interpretations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:7.3
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-7.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 7 (Advisory Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as specifically authorized by the Commission in writing, no interpretation of the meaning of the regulations in this part by an NRC officer or employee, other than a written interpretation by the General Counsel, shall be binding upon the Commission.
