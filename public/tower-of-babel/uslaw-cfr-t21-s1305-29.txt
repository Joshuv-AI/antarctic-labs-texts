21 C.F.R. § 1305.29 — Reporting to DEA.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1305.29
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1305.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1305 (Orders For Schedule I And Ii Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A supplier must, for each electronic order filled, forward either a copy of the electronic order or an electronic report of the order in a format that DEA specifies to DEA within two business days.
