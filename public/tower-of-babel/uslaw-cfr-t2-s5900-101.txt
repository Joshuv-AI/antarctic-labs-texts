2 C.F.R. § 5900.101 — Adoption of 2 CFR part 200.
Citation: us:cfr:2:5900.101
Creator: Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5900.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5900 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council adopts the Office of Management and Budget guidance in 2 CFR part 200, as revised effective October 1, 2024, except for 2 CFR 200.322(c) (collectively, OMB guidance). This part gives regulatory effect to the OMB guidance.
