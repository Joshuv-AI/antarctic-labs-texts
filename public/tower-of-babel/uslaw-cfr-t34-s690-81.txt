34 C.F.R. § 690.81 — Fiscal control and fund accounting procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:34:690.81
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-690.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 690 (Federal Pell Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An institution shall follow provisions for maintaining general fiscal records in this part and in 34 CFR 668.24(b).
(b) An institution shall maintain funds received under this part in accordance with the requirements in § 668.164.
