7 C.F.R. § 3200.11 — Liabilities and losses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3200.11
Creator: Office Of Procurement And Property Management, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3200.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3200 (Department Of Agriculture Guidelines For The Acquisition And Transfer Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USDA assumes no liability with respect to accidents, bodily injury, illness, or any other damages or loss related to excess personal property transferred under this part.
