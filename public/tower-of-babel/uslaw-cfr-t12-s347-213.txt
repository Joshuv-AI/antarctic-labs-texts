12 C.F.R. § 347.213 — Establishment or operation of noninsured foreign branch.
Citation: us:cfr:12:347.213
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-347.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 347 (International Banking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A foreign bank may establish or operate a state branch, as provided by state law, without federal deposit insurance whenever:
(1) The branch only accepts initial deposits in an amount equal to the SMDIA or greater; or
(2) The branch meets the criteria set forth in § 347.214 or § 347.215.
