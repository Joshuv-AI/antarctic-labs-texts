16 U.S.C. § 667c — Publication of designating order
Citation: us:usc:16:667c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section667c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter I (Game, Fur-Bearing Animals, And Fish). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever any real property is transferred pursuant to sections 667b to 667d of this title, the Administrator of General Services shall make and have published in the Federal Register an appropriate order, which may be revised from time to time in like manner, designating for which of the purposes specified in section 667b of this title the property so transferred shall be used.
