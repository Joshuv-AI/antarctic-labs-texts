14 C.F.R. § 294.80 — Waiver of sovereign immunity.
Citation: us:cfr:14:294.80
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-294.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 294 (Canadian Charter Air Taxi Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By accepting an approved registration under this part, a registrant waives any right it may possess to assert any defense of sovereign immunity in any action or proceeding instituted against it in any court or other tribunal in the United States based upon any claim arising out of its operations under this part.
