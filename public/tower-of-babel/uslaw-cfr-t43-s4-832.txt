43 C.F.R. § 4.832 — Consultation and advice.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.832
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.832
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The administrative law judge shall not consult any person, or party, on any fact in issue or on the merits of the matter before the disobedient party unless upon notice and opportunity for all parties to participate.
(b) No employee or agent of the Federal Government engaged in the investigation and prosecution of a proceeding governed by these rules shall participate or advise in the rendering of any recommended or final decision, except as witness or counsel in the proceeding.
