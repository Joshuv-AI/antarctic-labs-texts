25 C.F.R. § 26.26 — What Job Placement services may I receive?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.26
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As determined by the service provider, you may receive transportation to work for a limited period, funds to finalize your job resume, and job placement assistance.
