46 C.F.R. § 58.60-3 — -3 Pressure vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.60-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.60-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pressure vessel that is a component in an industrial system under this subpart must meet the applicable requirements in § 54.01-5 of this subchapter.
