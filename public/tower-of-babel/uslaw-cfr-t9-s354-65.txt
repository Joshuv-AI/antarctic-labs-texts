9 C.F.R. § 354.65 — Form of inspection mark.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.65
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The inspection mark approved for use on inspected and certified edible products shall be contained within a circle and include the following wording: “Inspected for Wholesomeness by U.S. Department of Agriculture.” The form and arrangement of such wording shall be as indicated in the example below. The plant number of the official plant shall be set forth if it does not appear on the packaging material.
