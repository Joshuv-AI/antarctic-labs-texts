41 C.F.R. § 102-71.110 — -71.110 Monitoring and controlling areas designated for tobacco products by an agency head.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.110
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agency heads are responsible for monitoring and controlling areas designated by them under § 102-74.95(d) of this subchapter and identifying these areas with proper signage. Suitable, uniform signs must be furnished and installed by the occupant agency.
