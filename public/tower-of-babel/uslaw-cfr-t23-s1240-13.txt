23 C.F.R. § 1240.13 — Determination of national average seat belt use rate.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1240.13
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1240.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1240 (Safety Incentive Grants For Use Of Seat Belts—Allocations Based On Seat Belt Use Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The national average seat belt use rate for a calendar year shall be the sum of the individual State seat belt use rates for all the States, after weighting each individual State seat belt use rate in accordance with the procedures of appendix D of this part.
