20 U.S.C. § 4331 — Authority
Citation: us:usc:20:4331
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4331
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 55 (Education Of The Deaf); Subchapter I (Gallaudet University; National Technical Institute For The Deaf; Other Programs); Part B (National Technical Institute For The Deaf). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of providing a residential facility for postsecondary technical training and education for individuals who are deaf in order to prepare them for successful employment, the institution of higher education with which the Secretary has an agreement under this part is authorized to operate and maintain a National Technical Institute for the Deaf.
