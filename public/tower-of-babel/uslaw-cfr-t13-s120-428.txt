13 C.F.R. § 120.428 — What happens to a securitizer's other PLP responsibilities if SBA suspends its PLP approval privilege?
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.428
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.428
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The securitizer must continue to service and liquidate loans according to its PLP Supplemental Agreement.
