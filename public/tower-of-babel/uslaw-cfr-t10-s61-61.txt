10 C.F.R. § 61.61 — Applicant qualifications and assurances.
Citation: us:cfr:10:61.61
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-61.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 61 (Licensing Requirements For Land Disposal Of Radioactive Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant shall show that it either possesses the necessary funds or has reasonable assurance of obtaining the necessary funds, or by a combination of the two, to cover the estimated costs of conducting all licensed activities over the planned operating life of the project, including costs of construction and disposal.
