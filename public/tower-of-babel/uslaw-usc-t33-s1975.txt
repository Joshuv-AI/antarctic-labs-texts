33 U.S.C. § 1975 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:33:1975
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 33A (Marine Debris Research, Prevention, And Reduction); Subchapter III (Genius Prize For Save Our Seas Innovations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 60 days after the date on which a cash prize is awarded under this title,1 the Secretary shall post on a publicly available website a report on the prize competition that includes—
(1)
if the Secretary has entered into an agreement under section 1973 of this title, a statement by the Marine Debris Foundation that describes the activities carried out by the Marine Debris Foundation relating to the duties described in section 1973 of this title; and
(2)
a statement by 1 or more of the judges appointed under section 1974 of this title that explains the basis on which the winner of the cash prize was selected.
