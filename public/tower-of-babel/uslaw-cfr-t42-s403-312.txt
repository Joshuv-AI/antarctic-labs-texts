42 C.F.R. § 403.312 — Submittal of application.
Citation: us:cfr:42:403.312
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-403.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 403 (Special Programs And Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief Executive Officer of the State is responsible for—
(a) Submittal of the application to CMS for approval; and
(b) Supplying the assurances and necessary documentation as required under §§ 403.304 through 403.308.
