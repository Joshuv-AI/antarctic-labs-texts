46 C.F.R. § 172.215 — Specific applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:172.215
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-172.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 172 (Special Rules Pertaining To Bulk Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each new Great Lakes vessel of 1600 gross tons or more carrying dry cargo in bulk.
