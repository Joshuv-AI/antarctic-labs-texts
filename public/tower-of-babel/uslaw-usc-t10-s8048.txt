10 U.S.C. § 8048 — Medical Officer of the Marine Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:8048
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8048
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 806 (Headquarters, Marine Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is a Medical Officer of the Marine Corps who shall be appointed from among flag officers of the Navy.
(b)
The Medical Officer of the Marine Corps, while so serving, shall hold the grade of rear admiral (lower half).
