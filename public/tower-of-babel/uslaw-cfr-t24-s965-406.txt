24 C.F.R. § 965.406 — Benefit/cost analysis for similar projects.
Citation: us:cfr:24:965.406
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-965.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 965 (Pha-Owned Or Leased Projects—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PHAs with more than one project of similar design and utilities service may prepare a benefit/cost analysis for a representative project. A finding that a change in metering is not cost effective for the representative project is sufficient reason for the PHA not to perform a benefit/cost analysis on the remaining similar projects.
