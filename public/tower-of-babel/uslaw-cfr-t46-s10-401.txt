46 C.F.R. § 10.401 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:10.401
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-10.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 10 (Merchant Mariner Credential). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the general requirements applicable to offerors of all approved courses and training programs which may be accepted instead of sea service, examination required by the Coast Guard, or STCW assessments, or which satisfy course completion requirements.
