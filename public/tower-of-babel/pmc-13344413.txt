Correction to “Society for Maternal‐Fetal Medicine Special Statement: Incorporating quality improvement roles into your maternal‐fetal medicine career”
Published: 2026

Society for Maternal‐Fetal Medicine (SMFM), Hamm, R.F., Bauer, S.T., Atallah, F., Chavan, N.R., Combs, C.A., Davidson, C.M., Goffman, D., Martinez‐King, C., Srinivas, S.K. and SMFM Patient Safety and Quality Committee (2026), Society for Maternal‐Fetal Medicine Special Statement: Incorporating quality improvement roles into your maternal‐fetal medicine career. Pregnancy, 2(2): e70268. https://doi.org/10.1002/pmf2.70268.

In the originally published version of this article, the names of Niraj R. Chavan and Carolina Martinez‐King were omitted in the author byline. The correct byline should have read:

Society for Maternal‐Fetal Medicine (SMFM), Rebecca F. Hamm, Samuel T. Bauer, Fouad Atallah, Niraj R. Chavan, C. Andrew Combs, Christina M. Davidson, Dena Goffman, Carolina Martinez‐King, Sindhu K. Srinivas, SMFM Patient Safety and Quality Committee

We apologize for this error.
