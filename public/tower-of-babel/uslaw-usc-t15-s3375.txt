15 U.S.C. § 3375 — Filing of contracts and agreements
Citation: us:usc:15:3375
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3375
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 60 (Natural Gas Policy); Subchapter III (Additional Authorities And Requirements); Part B (Other Authorities And Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may, by rule or order, require any first sale purchaser of natural gas under a new contract, a successor to an existing contract, or a rollover contract to file with the Commission a copy of such contract, together with all ancillary agreements and any existing contract applicable to such natural gas.
