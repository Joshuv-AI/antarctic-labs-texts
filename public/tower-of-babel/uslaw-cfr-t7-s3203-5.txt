7 C.F.R. § 3203.5 — Dollar limitation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3203.5
Creator: Office Of Procurement And Property Management, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3203.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3203 (Guidelines For The Transfer Of Excess Computers Or Other Technical Equipment Pursuant To Section 14220 Of The 2008 Farm Bill). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no dollar limitation on excess computers or other technical equipment obtained under this part.
