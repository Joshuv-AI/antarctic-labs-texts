P P -adic dynamical systems for p p -adic λ \lambda -models on the Cayley trees
Farrukh Mukhamedov \orcidID 0000-0001-5728-6394 and Otabek Khakimov \orcidID 0000-0002-8918-9094

Abstract
We review and propose to use of associated dynamical system to explore the phase transition phenomena in p p -adic statistical mechanics setting, by means of the renormalization techniques. Main focus of the paper is the p p -adic λ \lambda -model on the Cayley tree. We study generalized p p -adic quasi Gibbs measures for the λ \lambda -model. These measures associate recurrence equations that determine dynamical systems for the model. A main point is to verify and confirm that the indicated predictions from a dynamical system point of view are indeed true.
