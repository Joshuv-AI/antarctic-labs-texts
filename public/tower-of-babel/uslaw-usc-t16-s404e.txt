16 U.S.C. § 404e — Donations of money; acquisition of title to lands
Citation: us:usc:16:404e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section404e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XLVII (Mammoth Cave National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the establishment of the said Mammoth Cave National Park the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to accept donations of money for the acquisition of lands and rights therein and to acquire the same by purchase, condemnation, or otherwise.
