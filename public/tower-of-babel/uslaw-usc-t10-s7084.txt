10 U.S.C. § 7084 — Chief of Veterinary Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:7084
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7084
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 707 (The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chief of the Veterinary Corps of the Army shall be appointed from among officers of the Veterinary Corps.
