24 C.F.R. § 700.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:700.100
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-700.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 700 (Congregate Housing Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this part augment the requirements of section 802 of the National Affordable Housing Act of 1990 (approved November 28, 1990, Public Law 101-625) (42 U.S.C. 8011), (hereinafter, section 802), as amended by the Housing and Community Development Act of 1992 (Public Law 102-550, approved October 28, 1992), which authorizes the Congregate Housing Services Program (hereinafter, CHSP or Program).
