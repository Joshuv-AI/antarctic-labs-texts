54 U.S.C. § 304103 — Cooperation between Council and instrumentalities of executive branch of Federal Government
Citation: us:usc:54:304103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section304103
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3041 (Advisory Council On Historic Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Council may secure directly from any Federal agency information, suggestions, estimates, and statistics for the purpose of this chapter. Each Federal agency may furnish information, suggestions, estimates, and statistics to the extent permitted by law and within available funds.
