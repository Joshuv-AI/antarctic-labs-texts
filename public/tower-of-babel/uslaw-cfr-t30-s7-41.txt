30 C.F.R. § 7.41 — Purpose and effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:30:7.41
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-7.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 7 (Testing By Applicant Or Third Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the specific requirements for MSHA approval of battery assemblies intended for incorporation in approved equipment in underground mines. It is effective August 22, 1988. Applications for approval or extensions of approval submitted after August 22, 1989, shall meet the requirements of this part.
