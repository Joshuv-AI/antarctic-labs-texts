10 C.F.R. § 54.23 — Contents of application—environmental information.
Citation: us:cfr:10:54.23
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-54.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 54 (Requirements For Renewal Of Operating Licenses For Nuclear Power Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each application must include a supplement to the environmental report that complies with the requirements of subpart A of 10 CFR part 51.
