19 C.F.R. § 190.25 — Destruction under CBP supervision.
Citation: us:cfr:19:190.25
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-190.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 190 (Modernized Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claimant may destroy merchandise and obtain drawback by complying with the procedures set forth in § 190.71 relating to destruction.
