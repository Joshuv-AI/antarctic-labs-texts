28 U.S.C. § 1508 — Jurisdiction for certain partnership proceedings
Citation: us:usc:28:1508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1508
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 91 (United States Court Of Federal Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Court of Federal Claims shall have jurisdiction to hear and to render judgment upon any petition under section 6226 or 6228(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
