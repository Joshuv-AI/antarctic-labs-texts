45 C.F.R. § 304.10 — General administrative requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:304.10
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-304.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 304 (Federal Financial Participation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a condition for Federal financial participation, the provisions of2 CFR parts 200 and 300 (with the exception of 2 CFR 200.306 and 200.328) establishing uniform administrative requirements and cost principles shall apply to all grants made to States under this part.
