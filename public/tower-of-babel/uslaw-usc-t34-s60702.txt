34 U.S.C. § 60702 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:34:60702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section60702
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 607 (Project Safe Neighborhoods Block Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General of the United States is authorized to establish and carry out a program, to be known as the “Project Safe Neighborhoods Block Grant Program” within the Office of Justice Programs at the Department of Justice.
