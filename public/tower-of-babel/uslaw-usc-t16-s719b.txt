16 U.S.C. § 719b — Acceptance of gifts, devises, and bequests
Citation: us:usc:16:719b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section719b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter V (Junior Duck Stamp Conservation And Design Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may accept and use any gift, devise, or bequest of personal property, or proceeds thereof, for the purpose of funding the activities described in section 719a(c)(1)(A) and (B) of this title.
