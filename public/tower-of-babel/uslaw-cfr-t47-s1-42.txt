47 C.F.R. § 1.42 — Applications, reports, complaints; cross-reference.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.42
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Rules governing applications and reports are contained in subparts D, E, and F of this part.
(b) Special rules governing complaints against common carriers arising under the Communications Act are set forth in subpart E of this part.
(c) Rules governing the FCC Registration Number (FRN) are contained in subpart W of this part.
