24 C.F.R. § 110.20 — Availability of posters.
Citation: us:cfr:24:110.20
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-110.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 110 (Fair Housing Poster). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All persons subject to this part may obtain fair housing posters from the Department's regional and area offices. A facsimile may be used if the poster and the lettering are equivalent in size and legibility to the poster available from the Department.
