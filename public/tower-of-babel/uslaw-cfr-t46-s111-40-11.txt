46 C.F.R. § 111.40-11 — -11 Numbered switching unit and panelboard directory.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.40-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.40-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each panelboard switching unit must be numbered.
(b) Each panelboard must have:
(1) A circuit directory cardholder; and
(2) A circuit directory that has:
(i) The circuit designation of each circuit;
(ii) A description of the load of each circuit; and
(iii) The rating or setting of the overcurrent protective device for each circuit.
