39 U.S.C. § 1009 — Personnel not to receive fees
Citation: us:usc:39:1009
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section1009
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 10 (Employment Within The Postal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer or employee of the Postal Service may not receive any fee or perquisite from a patron of the Postal Service on account of the duties performed by virtue of his appointment, except as authorized by law.
