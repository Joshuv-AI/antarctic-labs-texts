Abstract
. In this article, we introduce the notion of u u - S S -projective relative to a module. Let S S be a multiplicative subset of a ring R R and M M an R R -module. An R R -module P P is said to be u u - S S -projective relative to M M if for any u u - S S -epimorphism f : M → N f:M\to N , the induced map Hom R ​ ( P , f ) : Hom R ​ ( P , M ) → Hom R ​ ( P , N ) \text{Hom}_{R}(P,f):\text{Hom}_{R}(P,M)\to\text{Hom}_{R}(P,N) is u u - S S -epimorphism. Dually, we also introduce u u - S S -injective relative to a module. Some properties of these notions are discussed. Several characterizations of u u - S S -semisimple modules in terms of these notions are given. The notions of u u - S S -quasi-projective and u u - S S -quasi-injective modules are also introduced and some of their properties are discussed.

. Thus

. Therefore,

∎
