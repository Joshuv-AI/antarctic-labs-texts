43 C.F.R. § 418.7 — Who may receive irrigation deliveries.
Citation: us:cfr:43:418.7
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-418.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 418 (Operating Criteria And Procedures For The Newlands Reclamation Project, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Project irrigation water deliveries may be made only to eligible land to be irrigated. The District must maintain records for each individual water right holder indicating the number of eligible acres irrigated and the amount of water ordered and delivered.
