30 C.F.R. § 556.707 — What if I want to transfer different types of lease interests (not only record title interests) in the same lease to different parties?
Citation: us:cfr:30:556.707
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-556.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 556 (Leasing Of Sulfur Or Oil And Gas And Financial Assurance Requirements In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not transfer different types of lease interests in a lease to different parties using the same instrument. You must submit duplicate, originally executed forms for each transfer, to a different party, of a different type of lease interest. The form used to transfer each type of lease interest must be accompanied by a cover letter executed by one of the parties to the transfer (or an authorized agent thereof) and evidence of payment via pay.gov.
