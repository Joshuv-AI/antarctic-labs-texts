25 C.F.R. § 166.305 — When is grazing capacity determined?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.305
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before we grant, modify, or approve a permit, in consultation with the Indian landowners, we will establish the total grazing capacity for each range unit based on the summation of each parcel's productivity. We will also establish the season(s) of use on Indian lands.
