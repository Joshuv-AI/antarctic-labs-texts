9 C.F.R. § 314.7 — Carcasses of livestock condemned on ante-mortem inspection not to pass through edible product areas.
Citation: us:cfr:9:314.7
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-314.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 314 (Handling And Disposal Of Condemned Or Other Inedible Products At Official Establishments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carcasses of livestock which have been condemned on ante-mortem inspection shall not be taken through rooms or compartments in which an edible product is prepared, handled, or stored.
