Recent developments in chemical reactive flow of CNTs-hybrid nanomaterial with Soret-Dufour effects using Levenberg-Marquardt technique
Authors: Munawar Abbas, Mustafa Bayram, Noor Haval Jamal, Mustafa Inc, Gafur Abdulakimov, Muhammad Shafique, Mohamed Abbas El-Naggar
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
In this study, heat and mass transmission in chemical reactive flow of hybrid nanomaterial flowing across a Riga plate with Soret-Dufour effects are studied using integrated numerical computing through artificial neural networks that are back propagated with the Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm (ANN-BPLMA). The suggested model for energy-efficient process cooling in porous media with chemical reaction and Soret-Dufour properties employs a Riga-actuated CNTs-water-based hybrid nanofluid. It has promising applications in current thermal systems. It can be used to improve cooling performance in nuclear reactors, heat exchangers, and electronic device cooling applications requiring high thermal conductivity and accurate heat management. In order to explore the hybrid nanofluid the governing equations provide partial differential equations (PDEs) that mathematically explain temperature, velocity and concentration. The ODEs are generated from these PDEs by using the suitable similarity transformations. The Bvp4c method is used to generate the graphical results. Graphs are employed to describe how various parameters affect temperature, velocity and thermal profiles. The temperature and concentration profiles become more pronounced as the Soret and Dufour numbers increase.

Methodology

The BVP4C method is very helpful since it solves nonlinear BVPs in fluid mechanics and heat transmission in an accurate and efficient manner. It uses a comparison approach with adaptive mesh refinement to increase correctness around steep gradients or singularities. Furthermore, BVP4C is resilient, simple to implement in MATLAB, and capable of handling complex boundary conditions, making it an effective tool for studying real-world engineering and scientific challenges.

Artificial neural networks

In order to solve difficult real-world problems, MLAs have been established in a variety of academic disciplines, such as engineering, science, and mathematics. By completing tasks or examples, the Supervised Learning Algorithm (SLA) simulates how humans learn. To aid in this process, a training dataset—also known as a teaching signal—is provided to the neural network (NN). In order to improve the NN's effectiveness in learning the desired mapping, the model can modify its internal weights by back-propagating the error between the expected and actual outputs during training. This section provides a quick summary of the findings for the suggested Artificial Neural Network with Levenberg-Marquardt technique.

Conclusions

In this research analysis the impact of thermal radiation on flow of HNF across a Riga plate with Soret and Dufour effects. Water is mixed with two dissimilar kinds of carbon nanotubes SWCNTs and MWCNTs. The proposed model for energy-efficient process cooling in permeable medium with Soret-Dufour significance makes use of a Riga-activated CNTs-water-based hybrid nanofluid. It has potential applications in modern thermal systems.
