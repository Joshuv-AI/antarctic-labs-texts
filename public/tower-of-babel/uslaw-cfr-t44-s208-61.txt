44 C.F.R. § 208.61 — Payment of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:44:208.61
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-208.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 208 (National Urban Search And Rescue Response System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS will reimburse all allowable costs for which a Sponsoring Agency requests reimbursement within 30 days after DHS determines that reimbursement is allowable, in whole or in part, at any stage of the reimbursement and appeal processes identified in this subpart.
