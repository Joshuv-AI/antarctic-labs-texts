7 U.S.C. § 2008k — Making and servicing of loans by personnel of State, county, or area committees
Citation: us:usc:7:2008k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2008k
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall use personnel of a State, county or area committee established under section 590h(b)(5) of title 16 to make and service loans under this chapter to the extent the personnel have been trained to do so.
