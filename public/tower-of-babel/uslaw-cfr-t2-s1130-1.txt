2 C.F.R. § 1130.1 — Purpose of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1130.1
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1130.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1130 (Property Administration: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part specifies standard wording of general terms and conditions concerning equipment, supplies, and real, intangible, and federally owned property.
(b) It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.310 through 200.316, as that guidance applies to general terms and conditions of grants and cooperative agreements.
