43 C.F.R. § 3440.1-6 — -6 Cancellation or forfeiture.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3440.1-6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3440.1-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3440 (Licenses To Mine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any license to mine may be canceled or forfeited for violation of the Act under which the license to mine was issued, applicable Federal laws and regulations, or the terms and conditions of the license to mine.
