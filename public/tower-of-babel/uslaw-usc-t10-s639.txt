10 U.S.C. § 639 — Continuation on active duty to complete disciplinary action
Citation: us:usc:10:639
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section639
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 36 (Promotion, Separation, And Involuntary Retirement Of Officers On The Active-Duty List); Subchapter IV (Continuation On Active Duty And Selective Early Retirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When any action has been commenced against an officer with a view to trying such officer by court-martial and such officer is to be separated or retired in accordance with this chapter, the Secretary of the military department concerned may delay the separation or retirement of the officer, without prejudice to such action, until the completion of the action.
