16 U.S.C. § 715k–4 — Accounting and use of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:715k–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section715k–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter III (Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated each fiscal year pursuant to sections 715k–3 to 715k–5 of this title shall be accounted for, added to, and used for purposes of the migratory bird conservation fund 1 established pursuant to section 718d of this title.
