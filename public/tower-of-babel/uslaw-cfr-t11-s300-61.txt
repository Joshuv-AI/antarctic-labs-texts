11 C.F.R. § 300.61 — Federal elections (52 U.S.C. 30125(e)(1)(A)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:300.61
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-300.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 300 (Non-Federal Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person described in 11 CFR 300.60 shall solicit, receive, direct, transfer, spend, or disburse funds in connection with an election for Federal office, including funds for any Federal election activity as defined in 11 CFR 100.24, unless the amounts consist of Federal funds that are subject to the limitations, prohibitions, and reporting requirements of the Act.
