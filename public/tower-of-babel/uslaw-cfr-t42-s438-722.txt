42 C.F.R. § 438.722 — Disenrollment during termination hearing process.
Citation: us:cfr:42:438.722
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-438.722
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 438 (Managed Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After a State notifies an MCO, PCCM or PCCM entity that it intends to terminate the contract, the State may do the following:
(a) Give the entity's enrollees written notice of the State's intent to terminate the contract.
(b) Allow enrollees to disenroll immediately without cause.
