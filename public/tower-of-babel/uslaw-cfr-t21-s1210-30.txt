21 C.F.R. § 1210.30 — Hearing procedure for permit denial, suspension, and revocation.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1210.30
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1210.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1210 (Regulations Under The Federal Import Milk Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who contests denial, suspension, or revocation of a permit shall have an opportunity for a regulatory hearing before the Food and Drug Administration pursuant to subpart F of part 16 of this chapter.
