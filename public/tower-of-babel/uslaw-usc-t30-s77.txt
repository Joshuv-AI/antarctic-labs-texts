30 U.S.C. § 77 — Alabama coal lands; agricultural entry
Citation: us:usc:30:77
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section77
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3 (Lands Containing Coal, Oil, Gas, Salts, Asphaltic Materials, Sodium, Sulphur, And Building Stone); Subchapter I (Coal Land Entries In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unreserved public lands containing coal deposits in the State of Alabama which on April 23, 1912, were being withheld from homestead entry under the provisions of section 171 of this title, may be entered under the homestead laws of the United States subject to the provisions, terms, conditions, and limitations prescribed in sections 83 to 85 of this title.
