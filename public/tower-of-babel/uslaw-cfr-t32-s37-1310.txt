32 C.F.R. § 37.1310 — Intellectual property.
Citation: us:cfr:32:37.1310
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-37.1310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 37 (Technology Investment Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inventions, data, works of authorship, and other intangible products of intellectual effort that can be owned by a person, whether or not they are patentable or may be copyrighted. The term also includes mask works, such as those used in microfabrication, whether or not they are tangible.
