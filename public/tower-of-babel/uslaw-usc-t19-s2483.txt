19 U.S.C. § 2483 — Consequential changes in Tariff Schedules of the United States
Citation: us:usc:19:2483
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2483
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter VI (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall from time to time, as appropriate, embody in the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States the substance of the relevant provisions of this chapter, and of other Acts affecting import treatment, and actions thereunder, including removal, modification, continuance, or imposition of any rate of duty or other import restriction.
