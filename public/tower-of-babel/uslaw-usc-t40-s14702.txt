40 U.S.C. § 14702 — Nondiscrimination
Citation: us:usc:40:14702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section14702
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 147 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual in the United States shall not, because of sex, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under, a program or activity receiving federal financial assistance under this subtitle.
