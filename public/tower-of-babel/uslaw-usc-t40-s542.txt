40 U.S.C. § 542 — Care and handling
Citation: us:usc:40:542
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section542
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter III (Disposing Of Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The disposal of surplus property, and the care and handling of the property pending disposition, may be performed by the General Services Administration or, when the Administrator of General Services decides, by the executive agency in possession of the property or by any other executive agency that agrees.
