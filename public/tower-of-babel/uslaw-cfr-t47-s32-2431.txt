47 C.F.R. § 32.2431 — Aerial wire.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.2431
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.2431
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This account shall include the original cost of bare line wire and other material used in the construction of such plant.
(b) The cost of permits and privileges for the construction of cable and wire facilities shall be included in the account chargeable with such construction.
(c) The cost of drop and block wires served by aerial wire shall be included in Account 2421, Aerial Cable.
