20 U.S.C. § 4501 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:4501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4501
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 57 (James Madison Memorial Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to establish the James Madison Fellowship Program which is designed to encourage graduate study of the American Constitution, its roots, its formation, its principles, and its development.
