7 C.F.R. § 955.27 — Failure to nominate.
Citation: us:cfr:7:955.27
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-955.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 955 (Vidalia Onions Grown In Georgia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If nominations are not made within the time and manner prescribed in § 955.22, the Secretary may, without regard to nominations, select members and alternates on the basis of the representation provided for in § 955.20.
