41 C.F.R. § 101-5.103 — -5.103 Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-5.103
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-5.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-5 (5—Centralized Services In Federal Buildings And Complexes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent practicable, GSA will provide or arrange for the provision of centralized services whenever such services insure increased efficiency and economy to the Government without hampering program activities or essential internal administration of the agencies to be served.
