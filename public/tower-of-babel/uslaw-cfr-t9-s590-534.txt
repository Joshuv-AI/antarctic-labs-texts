9 C.F.R. § 590.534 — Freezing facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:9:590.534
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-590.534
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 590 (Inspection Of Eggs And Egg Products (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Freezing rooms, either on or off the premises, must be capable of solidly freezing, or reducing to a temperature of 10 °F or lower, all liquid egg products.
