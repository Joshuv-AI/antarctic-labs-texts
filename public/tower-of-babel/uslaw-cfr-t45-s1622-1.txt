45 C.F.R. § 1622.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1622.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1622.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1622 (Public Access To Meetings Under The Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is designed to provide the public with full access to the deliberations and decisions of the Board of Directors of the Legal Services Corporation, committees of the Board, and state Advisory Councils, while maintaining the ability of those bodies to carry out their responsibilities and protecting the rights of individuals.
