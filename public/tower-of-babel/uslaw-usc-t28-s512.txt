28 U.S.C. § 512 — Attorney General to advise heads of executive departments
Citation: us:usc:28:512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section512
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 31 (The Attorney General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of an executive department may require the opinion of the Attorney General on questions of law arising in the administration of his department.
