6 C.F.R. § 13.11 — Referral of Complaint and answer to the Presiding Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:6:13.11
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-13.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 13 (Administrative Remedies For False Claims And Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of an answer, the Reviewing Official will refer the matter to an Presiding Officer by filing the Complaint and answer in accordance with § 13.26.
