42 C.F.R. § 486.500 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:486.500
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-486.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 486 (Conditions For Coverage Of Specialized Services Furnished By Suppliers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 1861(s)(2)(iii) of the Act requires the Secretary to establish the conditions that home infusion therapy suppliers must meet in order to participate in the Medicare program and which are considered necessary to ensure the health and safety of patients.
