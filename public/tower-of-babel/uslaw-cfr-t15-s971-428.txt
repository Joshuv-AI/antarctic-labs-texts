15 C.F.R. § 971.428 — Other necessary permits.
Citation: us:cfr:15:971.428
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-971.428
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 971 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Commercial Recovery Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each permit will provide that securing the deep seabed mining permit for activities described in the recovery plan and accompanying application does not eliminate the need to secure all other necessary Federal, State, and local permits.
