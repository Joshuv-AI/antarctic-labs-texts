14 C.F.R. § 49.19 — Effective date of filing for recordation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:49.19
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-49.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 49 (Recording Of Aircraft Titles And Security Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A conveyance is filed for recordation upon the date and at the time it is received by the FAA Aircraft Registry.
