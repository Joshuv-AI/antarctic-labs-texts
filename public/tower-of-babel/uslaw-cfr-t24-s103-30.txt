24 C.F.R. § 103.30 — How should I bring a claim that I am the victim of discrimination?
Citation: us:cfr:24:103.30
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-103.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 103 (Fair Housing—Complaint Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You can file a claim by mail or telephone with any of HUD's Offices of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity or with any State or local agency that HUD has certified to receive complaints.
(b) You can call or go to any other HUD office for help in filing a claim. These offices will send your claim to HUD's Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity, which will contact you about the filing of your complaint.
