50 C.F.R. § 28.43 — Destruction of dogs and cats.
Citation: us:cfr:50:28.43
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-28.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 28 (Enforcement, Penalty, And Procedural Requirements For Violations Of Subchapter C). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dogs and cats running at large on a national wildlife refuge and observed by an authorized official in the act of killing, injuring, harassing or molesting humans or wildlife may be disposed of in the interest of public safety and protection of the wildlife.
