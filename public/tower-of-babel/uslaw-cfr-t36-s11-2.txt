36 C.F.R. § 11.2 — Uses.
Citation: us:cfr:36:11.2
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-11.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 11 (Arrowhead And Parkscape Symbols). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may permit the reproduction, manufacture, sale, and use of the “Arrowhead Symbol” or the “Parkscape Symbol”, with or without charge, for uses that will contribute to purposes of education and conservation as they relate to the program of the National Park Service. All other uses are prohibited.
