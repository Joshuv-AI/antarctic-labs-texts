49 C.F.R. § 238.501 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:238.501
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-238.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 238 (Passenger Equipment Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains inspection, testing, and maintenance requirements for railroad passenger equipment that operates at speeds exceeding 125 mph but not exceeding 160 mph.
