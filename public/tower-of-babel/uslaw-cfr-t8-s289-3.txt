8 C.F.R. § 289.3 — Recording the entry of certain American Indians born in Canada.
Citation: us:cfr:8:289.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-289.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 289 (American Indians Born In Canada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lawful admission for permanent residence of an American Indian born in Canada shall be recorded on Form I-181.
