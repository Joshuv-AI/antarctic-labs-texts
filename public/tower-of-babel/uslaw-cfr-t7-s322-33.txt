7 C.F.R. § 322.33 — Restricted articles in a commercial bonded vehicle arriving at a land border port in the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:7:322.33
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-322.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 322 (Bees, Beekeeping Byproducts, And Beekeeping Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you import a restricted article through a land border port in the United States by commercial vehicle (i.e., automobile or truck), then the person carrying the package containing the restricted article or the driver of the vehicle must present the export certificate required by § 322.30 (if applicable) and an invoice or packing slip accurately indicating the complete contents of the shipment to the inspector at the land border port.
