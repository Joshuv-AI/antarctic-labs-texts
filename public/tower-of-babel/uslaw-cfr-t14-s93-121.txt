14 C.F.R. § 93.121 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:93.121
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-93.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 93 (Special Air Traffic Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart designates high density traffic airports and prescribes air traffic rules for operating aircraft, other than helicopters, to or from those airports.
