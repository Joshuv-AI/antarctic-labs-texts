46 C.F.R. § 167.15-50 — -50 Tailshaft examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:167.15-50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-167.15-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tailshaft examinations on nautical school ships must conform with the examination requirements in part 61 of this chapter.
