10 C.F.R. § 862.6 — Voluntary minimum altitude.
Citation: us:cfr:10:862.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-862.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 862 (Restrictions On Aircraft Landing And Air Delivery At Department Of Energy Nuclear Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to complying with all applicable FAA prohibitions or restrictions, aircraft are requested to maintain a minimum altitude of 2,000 feet above the terrain of a designated site. Applicable FAA prohibitions or restrictions take precedence over this voluntary minimum altitude.
