34 C.F.R. § 373.11 — What other factors does the Secretary consider when making a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:373.11
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-373.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 373 (Rehabilitation National Activities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary funds only those applications submitted in response to competitions announced in the Federal Register.
(b) The Secretary may consider the past performance of the applicant in carrying out activities under previously awarded grants.
(c) The Secretary awards bonus points if identified and published in the Federal Register for specific competitions.
