37 C.F.R. § 11.38 — Contested case.
Citation: us:cfr:37:11.38
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-11.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 11 (Representation Of Others Before The United States Patent And Trademark Office). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the filing of an answer by the respondent, a disciplinary proceeding shall be regarded as a contested case within the meaning of 35 U.S.C. 24. Evidence obtained by a subpoena issued under 35 U.S.C. 24 shall not be admitted into the record or considered unless leave to proceed under 35 U.S.C. 24 was previously authorized by the hearing officer.
