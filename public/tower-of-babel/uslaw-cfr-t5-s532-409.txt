5 C.F.R. § 532.409 — Grading or regrading of positions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:532.409
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-532.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 532 (Prevailing Rate Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 532.703(b)(10), a change in an employee's rate of basic pay as a result of the grading or regrading of the employee's position shall be effective on the date the grading or regrading action is finally approved by the agency or on a subsequent specifically stated date.
