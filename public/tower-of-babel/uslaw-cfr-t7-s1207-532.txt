7 C.F.R. § 1207.532 — Retention period for records.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1207.532
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1207.532
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1207 (Potato Research And Promotion Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each handler and importer required to make reports pursuant to this subpart shall maintain and retain such records for at least 2 years beyond the end of the marketing year of their applicability:
(a) One copy of each report made to the Board; and
(b) Such records as are necessary to verify such reports.
