40 C.F.R. § 265.300 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:265.300
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-265.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 265 (Interim Status Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage, And Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to owners and operators of facilities that dispose of hazardous waste in landfills, except as § 265.1 provides otherwise. A waste pile used as a disposal facility is a landfill and is governed by this subpart.
