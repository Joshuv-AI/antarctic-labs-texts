25 C.F.R. § 2.812 — May I appeal the denial of my request for an extension of time?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.812
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.812
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The denial of an extension of time to submit the Objection to the Statement of Performance is not appealable.
