14 U.S.C. § 2164 — Officers considered for removal; retirement or discharge; separation benefits
Citation: us:usc:14:2164
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2164
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
At any time during proceedings under section 2159 of this title, and before the removal of an officer, the Secretary may grant a request—
(1)
for voluntary retirement, if the officer is otherwise qualified therefor; or
(2)
for discharge with separation benefits under section 2146(c) of this title.
(b)
Each officer removed from active duty under section 2163 of this title shall—
(1)
if on the date of removal the officer is eligible for voluntary retirement under any law, be retired in the grade for which he would be eligible if retired at his request; or
(2)
if on that date the officer is ineligible for voluntary retirement under any law, be honorably discharged with separation benefits under section 2146(c) of this title, unless under regulations promulgated by the Secretary the condition under which the officer is discharged does not warrant an honorable discharge.
