20 C.F.R. § 416.426 — Change in status involving an individual; ineligibility occurs.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.426
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.426
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever benefits are suspended or terminated for an individual because of ineligibility, no benefit is payable for that month.
