28 C.F.R. § 544.50 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:28:544.50
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-544.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 544 (Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of Prisons offers eligible inmates the opportunity under its occupational education programs to participate in occupational education courses for the purpose of obtaining marketable skills designed to enhance post-release employment opportunities.
