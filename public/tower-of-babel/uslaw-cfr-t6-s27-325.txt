6 C.F.R. § 27.325 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:6:27.325
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-27.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 27 (Chemical Facility Anti-Terrorism Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Assistant Director bears the initial burden of proving the facts necessary to support the challenged administrative action at every proceeding instituted under this subpart.
