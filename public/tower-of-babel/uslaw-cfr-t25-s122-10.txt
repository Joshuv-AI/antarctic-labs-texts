25 C.F.R. § 122.10 — Appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:25:122.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-122.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 122 (Management Of Osage Judgment Funds For Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedure for appealing any decision regarding the awarding of funds under this part shall be made in accordance with 25 CFR part 2, Appeals from Administrative Action.
