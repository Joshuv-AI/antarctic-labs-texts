23 C.F.R. § 680.102 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:680.102
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-680.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 680 (National Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Standards And Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except where noted, these regulations apply to all NEVI Formula Program projects as well as projects for the construction of publicly accessible EV chargers that are funded with funds made available under Title 23, United States Code, including any EV charging infrastructure project funded with Federal funds that is treated as a project on a Federal-aid highway.
