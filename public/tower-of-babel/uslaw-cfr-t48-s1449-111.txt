48 C.F.R. § 1449.111 — 1449.111 Review of proposed settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1449.111
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1449.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1449 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All proposed settlement agreements shall be reviewed by the SOL and approved at one level above the CO. Settlement agreements of $250,000 or more shall be approved by the BPC.
