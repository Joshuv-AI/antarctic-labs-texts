43 C.F.R. § 3809.573 — What happens if the State makes a demand against my financial guarantee?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3809.573
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3809.573
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the State makes a demand against your financial guarantee, thereby reducing the available balance, you must do both of the following:
(a) Notify BLM within 15 calendar days; and
(b) Replace or augment the financial guarantee within 30 calendar days if the available balance is insufficient to cover the remaining reclamation cost.
