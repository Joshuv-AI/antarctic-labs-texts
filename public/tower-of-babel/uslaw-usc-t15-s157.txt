15 U.S.C. § 157 — Regulations and fees; disposition of fees and penalties
Citation: us:usc:15:157
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section157
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 4 (China Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary is authorized to make such regulations as may be necessary to carry into effect the functions vested in him or in the registrar by this chapter.
(b)
The Secretary is authorized to prescribe and fix the amount of such fees (other than the incorporation fee) to be paid him or the registrar for services rendered by the Secretary or the registrar to any person in the administration of the provisions of this chapter. All fees and penalties paid under this chapter shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States as miscellaneous receipts.
