14 C.F.R. § 1214.1107 — Notification.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1214.1107
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1214.1107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1214 (Space Flight). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Selectees and the appropriate military services will be notified and the public informed. All unsuccessful qualified applicants will be notified of nonselection and given the opportunity to update their applications and indicate their desire to receive consideration for future selections.
