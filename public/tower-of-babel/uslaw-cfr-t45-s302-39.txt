45 C.F.R. § 302.39 — Standards for program operation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:302.39
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-302.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 302 (State Plan Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State plan shall provide that the IV-D agency will comply with the standards for program operation and the organizational and staffing requirements prescribed by part 303 of this chapter.
