54 U.S.C. § 312101 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:54:312101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section312101
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3121 (National Trust For Historic Preservation In The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Board.—
The term “Board” means the board of trustees of the National Trust.
(2) National trust.—
The term “National Trust” means the National Trust for Historic Preservation in the United States established under section 312102 of this title.
