46 C.F.R. § 503.13 — Incorporation by reference.
Citation: us:cfr:46:503.13
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-503.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 503 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, matter which is reasonably available to the class of persons affected hereby shall be deemed published in the Federal Register when incorporated by reference therein with the approval of the Director of the Office of the Federal Register.
