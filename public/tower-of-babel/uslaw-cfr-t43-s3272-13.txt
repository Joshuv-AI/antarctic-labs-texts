43 C.F.R. § 3272.13 — How will BLM review my utilization plan and notify me of its decision?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3272.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3272.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When BLM receives your utilization plan, we will make sure it is complete and review it for compliance with § 3200.4.
(b) If another Federal agency manages the surface of your lease, we will consult with that agency as part of the plan review.
(c) If we need any further information to complete our review, we will contact you in writing and suspend our review until we receive the information.
(d) We will notify you in writing of our decision on your plan.
