32 C.F.R. § 1280.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1280.2
Creator: Department Of Defense, Defense Logistics Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1280.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1280 (Investigating And Processing Certain Noncontractual Claims And Reporting Related Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Claims Investigating Officer. A military officer or civilian employee of DLA, appointed in accordance with this part 1280, to investigate and process claims within the purview of this part 1280.
(b) Member of the Army, member of the Navy, member of the Marine Corps, member of the Air Force. Officers and enlisted personnel of these Military Services.
