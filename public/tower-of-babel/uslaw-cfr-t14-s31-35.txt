14 C.F.R. § 31.35 — Fabrication methods.
Citation: us:cfr:14:31.35
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-31.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 31 (Airworthiness Standards: Manned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The methods of fabrication used must produce a consistently sound structure. If a fabrication process requires close control to reach this objective, the process must be performed in accordance with an approved process specification.
