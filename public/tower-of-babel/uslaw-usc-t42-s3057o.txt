42 U.S.C. § 3057o — Funding set aside
Citation: us:usc:42:3057o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3057o
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter X (Grants For Native Americans); Part E (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Of the funds appropriated under section 3057n(1) for a fiscal year, not more than 5 percent shall be made available to carry out part D for such fiscal year, provided that for such fiscal year—
(1)
the funds appropriated for parts A and B are greater than the funds appropriated for fiscal year 2019; and
(2)
the Assistant Secretary makes available for parts A and B no less than the amount of resources made available for fiscal year 2019.
