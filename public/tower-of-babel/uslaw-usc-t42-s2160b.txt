42 U.S.C. § 2160b — Authority to suspend nuclear cooperation with nations which have not ratified the Convention on the Physical Security of Nuclear Material
Citation: us:usc:42:2160b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2160b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter X (International Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may suspend nuclear cooperation under this chapter with any nation or group of nations which has not ratified the Convention on the Physical Security of Nuclear Material.
