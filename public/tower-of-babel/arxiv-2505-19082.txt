A classification of rational 3-tangles
Bo-hyun Kwon

Abstract
. In this paper, we define the normal form and normal coordinate of a rational 3-tangle T T with respect to ∂ E 1 \partial E_{1} , where E 1 E_{1} is the fixed two punctured disk in Σ 0 , 6 \Sigma_{0,6} . Among all normal coordinates of T T with respect to ∂ E 1 \partial E_{1} , we investigate the collection of minimal normal coordinates of T T . We show that the simplicial complex constructed with normal forms of the rational 3-tangle is contractible. As an effectiveness of the contractibility of the simplicial complex by normal forms of T T , we would choose a minimal normal coordinate of T T with a certain rule for the representative for the rational 3 3 -tangle T T . This classifies rational 3 3 -tangles up to isotopy.

Introduction

2

The definition of the

n
-tangles and rational numbers if

so far. Some topologists call the rational tangles as

tangles although trivial in knot diagrams means that there is no crossing up to isotopy.
Over 50 years, we were not able to extend the classification of rational

-tangles to a classification of rational
n
-tangles(

) properly, especially rational
3
-tangles in advance. Cabrera
[
1
]
found a pair of invariants which is defined for all rational
3
-tangles and it classifies a special set of rational
3
-tangles containing the
3
-braid group. The author
[
3
]
gave an algorithm to distinguish arbitrary two rational
3
-tangles. Currently, we defined an
arc systems
of a rational
3
-tangle
T
and made a nice operator called

which preserve the rational tangle
T
but change the arc system of
T
. This operator is very useful tool to detect the trivial rational
3
-tangle called
\infty
tangle. In the argument for the algorithm we developed in
[
3
]
, the main difficulty to make the algorithm was how to check whether or not the given rational
3
-tangle is the
\infty
tangle. The result by the bridge arc replacement gives much easier criterion to detect
\infty

[

defined
