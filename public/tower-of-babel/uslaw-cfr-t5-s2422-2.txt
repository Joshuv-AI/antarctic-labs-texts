5 C.F.R. § 2422.2 — Who may file a petition?
Citation: us:cfr:5:2422.2
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2422.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2422 (Representation Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual; a labor organization; two or more labor organizations acting as a joint-petitioner; an individual acting on behalf of any employee(s); an agency or activity; or a combination of the above may file a representation petition. But,
(a) Only a labor organization may file a petition under § 2422.1(a)(1);
(b) Only an individual may file a petition under § 2422.1(a)(2); and
(c) Only an agency or a labor organization may file a petition under § 2422.1(b) or (c).
