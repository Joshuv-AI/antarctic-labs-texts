7 U.S.C. § 2239 — Funds for printing, binding, and scientific and technical article reprint purchases
Citation: us:usc:7:2239
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2239
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available to the Department of Agriculture may be used for printing and binding, including the purchase of reprints of scientific and technical articles.
