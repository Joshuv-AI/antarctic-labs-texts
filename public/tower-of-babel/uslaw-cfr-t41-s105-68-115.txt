41 C.F.R. § 105-68.115 — -68.115 How does an exclusion restrict a person's involvement in covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.115
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With the exceptions stated in §§ 105-68.120, 105-68.315, and 105-68.420, a person who is excluded by the General Services Administration or any other Federal agency may not:
(a) Be a participant in a(n) GSA transaction that is a covered transaction under subpart B of this part;
(b) Be a participant in a transaction of any other Federal agency that is a covered transaction under that agency's regulation for debarment and suspension; or
(c) Act as a principal of a person participating in one of those covered transactions.
