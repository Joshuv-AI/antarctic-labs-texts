46 C.F.R. § 13.119 — Expiration of endorsements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:13.119
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-13.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 13 (Certification Of Tank Vessel Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tank vessel endorsement is valid for the duration of the Merchant Mariner Credential (MMC) on which the endorsement appears.
