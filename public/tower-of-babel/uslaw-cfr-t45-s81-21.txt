45 C.F.R. § 81.21 — Parties; General Counsel deemed a party.
Citation: us:cfr:45:81.21
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-81.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 81 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 80 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term party shall include an applicant or recipient or other person to whom a notice of hearing or opportunity for hearing has been mailed naming him a respondent.
(b) The General Counsel of the Department of Health and Human Services shall be deemed a party to all proceedings.
