22 C.F.R. § 91.3 — Assistance to Customs and Tariff Commission representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:22:91.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-91.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 91 (Import Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consular officers shall render all proper assistance to Customs and Tariff Commission representatives abroad to aid them in the performance of their official duties.
