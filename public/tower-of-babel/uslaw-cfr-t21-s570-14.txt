21 C.F.R. § 570.14 — Indirect food additives resulting from packaging materials for animal feed and pet food.
Citation: us:cfr:21:570.14
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-570.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 570 (Food Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regulations providing for the use of food packaging materials in parts 174 through 179 of this chapter are incorporated in Subchapter E as applicable to packaging materials used for animal feed and pet food.
