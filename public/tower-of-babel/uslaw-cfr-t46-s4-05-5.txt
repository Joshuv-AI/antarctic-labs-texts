46 C.F.R. § 4.05-5 — -5 Substance of marine casualty notice.
Citation: us:cfr:46:4.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-4.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 4 (Marine Casualties And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice required in § 4.05-1 must include the name and official number of the vessel, floating OCS facility, or MODU involved, the name of the owner or agent, the nature and circumstances of the casualty, the locality in which it occurred, the nature and extent of injury to persons, and the damage to property.
