25 C.F.R. § 247.17 — What are the restrictions on fires?
Citation: us:cfr:25:247.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-247.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 247 (Use Of Columbia River Treaty Fishing Access Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You cannot burn timber, trees, slash, brush or grass unless you have a permit issued by the Area Director or his designee.
(b) You cannot build a fire in an unsafe location or leave a fire without completely extinguishing it.
(c) You must control all fire and not allow it to escape.
