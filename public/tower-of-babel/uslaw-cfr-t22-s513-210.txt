22 C.F.R. § 513.210 — Voluntary exclusion.
Citation: us:cfr:22:513.210
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-513.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 513 (Government Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement) And Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Grants)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons who accept voluntary exclusions under § 513.315 are excluded in accordance with the terms of their settlements. Broadcasting Board of Governors shall, and participants may, contact the original action agency to ascertain the extent of the exclusion.
