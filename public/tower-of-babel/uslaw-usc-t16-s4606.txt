16 U.S.C. § 4606 — Distribution of appropriate items
Citation: us:usc:16:4606
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4606
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 66 (Take Pride In America Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to distribute pamphlets and other such appropriate items in order to promote the purposes of the TPIA Program.
