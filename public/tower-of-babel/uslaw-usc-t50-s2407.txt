50 U.S.C. § 2407 — General Counsel
Citation: us:usc:50:2407
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2407
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 41 (National Nuclear Security Administration); Subchapter I (Establishment And Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is a General Counsel of the Administration. The General Counsel is the chief legal officer of the Administration.
