22 U.S.C. § 4859 — Protection of public entrances of United States diplomatic missions abroad
Citation: us:usc:22:4859
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4859
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 58 (Diplomatic Security); Subchapter IV (Diplomatic Security Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall install and maintain a walk-through metal detector or other advanced screening system at public entrances of each United States diplomatic mission abroad.
