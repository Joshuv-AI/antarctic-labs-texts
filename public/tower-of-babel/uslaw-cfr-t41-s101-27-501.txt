41 C.F.R. § 101-27.501 — -27.501 Eligibility for return.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-27.501
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-27.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-27 (27—Inventory Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

GSA stock items for which no current or future agency requirements are anticipated are eligible for return to GSA for credit. Despite eligibility for return to GSA, consideration should be given to the transportation costs involved as related to the value of the items, and, where excessive, such items shall not be reported to GSA.
