22 C.F.R. § 512.15 — Referrals to the Department of Justice or the General Accounting Office.
Citation: us:cfr:22:512.15
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-512.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 512 (Collection Of Debts Under The Debt Collection Act Of 1982). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Referrals to the Department of Justice or the General Accounting Office shall be made in accordance with the standards set forth in part 105 of the Federal Claims Collection Standards (4 CFR part 105).
