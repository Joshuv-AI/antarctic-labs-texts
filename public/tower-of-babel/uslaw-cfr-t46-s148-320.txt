46 C.F.R. § 148.320 — Tankage; garbage tankage; rough ammonia tankage; or tankage fertilizer.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part applies to rough ammonia tankage in bulk that contains 7 percent or more moisture by weight, and garbage tankage and tankage fertilizer that contains 8 percent or more moisture by weight.
(b) Tankage to which this part applies may not be loaded in bulk if its temperature exceeds 38 °C (100 °F).
(c) During the voyage, the temperature of the tankage must be monitored often enough to detect spontaneous heating.
