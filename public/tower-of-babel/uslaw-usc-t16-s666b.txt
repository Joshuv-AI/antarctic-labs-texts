16 U.S.C. § 666b — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:666b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section666b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter I (Game, Fur-Bearing Animals, And Fish). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The terms “wildlife” and “wildlife resources” as used herein include birds, fishes, mammals, and all other classes of wild animals and all types of aquatic and land vegetation upon which wildlife is dependent.
