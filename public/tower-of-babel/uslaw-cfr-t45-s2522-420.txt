45 C.F.R. § 2522.420 — What basic criteria does AmeriCorps use in making funding decisions?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.420
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In evaluating your application for funding, AmeriCorps will assess:
(a) Your program design;
(b) Your organizational capability; and
(c) Your program's cost-effectiveness and budget adequacy.
