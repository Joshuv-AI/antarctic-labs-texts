31 C.F.R. § 596.305 — General license.
Citation: us:cfr:31:596.305
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-596.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 596 (Terrorism List Governments Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term general license means any license or authorization the terms of which are set forth in this part.
