46 C.F.R. § 201.8 — Use of gender and number.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.8
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Words importing the singular number may extend and be applied to several persons or things; words importing the plural number may include the singular; and words importing the masculine gender may be applied to females.
