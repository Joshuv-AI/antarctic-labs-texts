25 C.F.R. § 1200.4 — May tribes exercise increased direction over their trust funds and retain the protections of Federal trust status?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1200.4
Creator: Office Of The Special Trustee For American Indians, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1200.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1200 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Tribal Self-Governance Act (25 U.S.C. 458) and the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 450 et seq.) provide other options for trust funds management. A tribe may choose to manage its trust funds under the provisions of these Acts if it wishes. These options are covered by 25 CFR part 900 (the “Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act Program”) and 25 CFR part 1000 (the “Self-Governance Program”).
