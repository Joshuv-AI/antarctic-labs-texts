Preparation optimization, structural characterization, stability and promoting calcium absorption mechanism of sturgeon cartilage peptide-calcium chelates
Authors: Xing Xie, Xiao-xue Wang, Qin-zhu Deng, Qing-hui Wen, Chun-ming Tan, Jin-qiu Xu, Zong-cai Tu, Lu Zhang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
The preparation process of sturgeon cartilage peptide (SCP)‑calcium chelates (SCP-Ca) was optimized in this work, its structure, stability and promoting calcium absorption mechanism were explored. The optimum conditions for preparing SCP-Ca with Ca amount of 44.47 ± 0.87 mg/g were 50 min, 40 °C, pH of 8 and a peptide-to‑calcium mass ratio of 11:1. The C—O and —COO− groups of Asp and Glu mainly attended SCP-Ca formation, and resulted to the change of random coil and β-sheet contents in SCP. SCP-Ca showed good processing and digestive stability. SCP-Ca strengthened calcium transport volume with peak value of 1.74 ± 0.15 μg, and promoted MC3T3-E1 osteoblast mineralization by modulating RUNX2, OSX, OCN and ALP expressions. Peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY were potential calcium-chelating peptides of SCP, and exhibited strong binding ability with calcium absorption proteins Cav1.3 and TRPV6. This research can provide scientific basis for the utilization of SCP-Ca as novel calcium supplements.

Introduction

Calcium is a vital constant element in body, and accounting for approximately 2% of adult body weight. Calcium plays critical roles in maintaining normal nerve and muscle excitability, reducing capillary permeability and regulating enzyme and glandular secretion (Verbekeet et al., 2024). According to the guidelines of FDA, the recommended daily calcium intake is 1300 mg, while approximately 5 billion people exist calcium deficient in the worldwide, especially for the elderly and children (Passarelli et al., 2024). Prolonged calcium deficiency will generate bone injury, and result to bone related diseases like osteoporosis. Operative and drug interventions are applied to bone diseases therapy. Operative intervention includes autografts, bone substitutes and tissue engineering technology, while is only performed for few serious bone diseases patients (Savadkouhi et al., 2025). Drug treatment is important method with excellent effect, and contains health biologics, antiresorptive and anabolic agents, selective estrogen receptor modulators and so on, especially for calcium supplements, which can enhance bone formation and quality with low cost (Perrone et al., 2025). Currently, commercial calcium supplements include inorganic and organic calcium. Although the cost of inorganic calcium is relatively inexpensive, but it shows low bio-availability and various side effects such as flatulence and bloating (Xie et al., 2026). Long time consumption of organic acid calcium like calcium citrate will lead to gastrointestinal diseases and the decrease of iron absorption (Li et al., 2018). Thus, screening of calcium supplements with natural, highly efficient and good absorption has been a current research hotspot.

Natural bio-active peptides were demonstrated to be excellent carriers for metal ion chelation and transport. Peptide‑calcium chelates have attracted much attention due to good bio-availability, safety and significant calcium supplementation effect. For example, bovine bone collagen peptide‑calcium chelates showed significant calcium transport ability and bio-availability (Qi et al., 2023). Carp and tilapia skin collagen peptide‑calcium chelates showed superior effect on calcium absorption in comparison with inorganic salt calcium, and could respectively alleviate osteoporosis in cell and animal models (Chen et al., 2017; Wang, Chen, et al., 2025). Meanwhile, the structure and stability of peptide‑calcium chelates are highly related with its function. Numerous studies reported that the Ca2+ majorly combined with α-amino and carboxyl groups of peptides, and the binding model determined the processing and digestive stability, and finally leading to the difference of anti-osteoporosis effect (Xie et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2022). In addition, ALP is the indicator of osteoblast differentiation, while RUNX2, OSX and OCN were the key targets for preventing and treating osteoporosis, which can promote osteoblast mineralization by regulating calcium metabolism and mineral deposition (Yun et al., 2023). Wang et al. (2022) reported that calcium-binding peptides from soy yogurt could modulate osteoblast differentiation and bone mineralization by upregulating the mRNA expression of Alp, Ocn and Runx2. Peptides from oyster protein hydrolysates reduced bone loss of zebrafish by affecting the gene expression of Runx2 and Osx (Zhong et al., 2025). Therefore, the structure, stability and these key gene targets are vital for evaluating the application potential of peptide‑calcium chelates as a novel calcium supplement.

Sturgeons, as one of the most economically value species in the aquaculture industry, are recognized for delicate texture and absence of fishy odor, and China is accounted for approximately 86% of global production (Tang et al., 2019). Sturgeons are often processed to fresh fish fillets and caviar, while cartilage is the main by-products with a 10% proportion of fish weight (Lin & Chen, 2025). Sturgeon cartilage is commonly used to extract chondroitin sulfate and peptides as well as produce foods due to its high content in protein and glycosaminoglycan. Many studies proved that sturgeon cartilage peptides exhibited various physiological functions like anti-inflammatory, antioxidant and immune regulation (Li et al., 2024). For example, Li et al. (2022) found that sturgeon cartilage peptides could alleviate rheumatoid arthritis by reducing the thickness of ankle joint in rats. Yuan et al. (2023) reported that peptide LLEL from sturgeon cartilage significantly decreased the NO and IL-6 levels of RAW264.7 macrophages through MAPK and IL-17 signaling pathways. Thus, sturgeon cartilage peptides are the ideal carriers for chelating calcium ions. While, the preparation process of sturgeon cartilage peptide‑calcium chelates (SCP-Ca) is lacking, and its stability and promoting calcium absorption mechanism are also necessary to investigate.

In this research, the preparation method of SCP-Ca was optimized, and then its structural characteristics and calcium transport ability was explored with spectral technology and Caco-2 cells monolayers model. The processing and digestive stability of SCP-Ca was evaluated, and the potential calcium chelating peptides from SCP were also screened by LC-MS/MS and molecular docking technologies. The mechanism by which SCP-Ca promotes calcium absorption was investigated by analyzing key gene and protein expression in an MC3T3-E1 cell model, as well as the binding ability to the calcium transport proteins TRPV6 and Cav1.3.

Materials and methods

Materials and chemicals

Sturgeon cartilage peptide was provided by Yantai Jiahui Marine Biotechnology Co., Ltd. (Shandong, China). The commercial kits were purchased from Nanjing Jiancheng Bioengineering Institute (Nanjing, China). All of reagents were analysis grade.

Preparation optimization of SCP-Ca

SCP-Ca was prepared according to the method provided by Yuan et al. (2024). The sturgeon cartilage peptide (SCP) was dissolved with PBS solution (0.1 M, pH = 7.4) in a tube, and mixed with different mass ratio of calcium chloride, then the pH was adjusted to different values. The mixtures were incubated in a constant temperature water bath (SHJ-6A, Jiangsu Changzhou Guoyu Instrument Manufacturing Co., Ltd., China) at different temperatures. After chelating for certain time, the samples were cooled to 25 °C, then an 8-fold volume of ethanol was added to alcohol precipitation for 4 h at 4 °C. After filtering (TGL-16 M, Bioridge, China) at 8000 r/min for 10 min, the supernatants were obtained for calcium content analysis, while the residues were collected and freeze-dried to get SCP-Ca. The range of chelating time, temperature, pH and peptide‑calcium ratio were respectively 30–70 min, 25–80 °C, 5–9 and 1:3–1:17. The calcium ions levels were evaluated with corresponding kit, and calcium chelation rate were calculated by using following formula:(1)Calcium chelation rate%=Et−ESEt×100%where, Et and Es were the additive amount and supernatants content of calcium ion, respectively.

Structural characterization of SCP-Ca

Determination of ultraviolet-visible (UV) and fluorescence spectrum

The SCP solutions (5.0 mg/mL) were reacted with various concentrations of CaCl₂ solution in tubes, and were used to prepare SCP-Ca according to the optimal method obtained in 2.2. The UV spectra of SCP-Ca were recorded at the range of 190–400 nm with a UV spectrophotometer (U-2910, Hitachi, Japan). The fluorescence spectra were scanned at an excitation and emission wavelength of 280 nm and 200–400 nm by using a fluorescence spectrometer (F7000, Hitachi, Japan).

Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR)

Lyophilized SCP or SCP-Ca (1.0 mg) was reacted with 200 mg of dry KBr in a mortar, and then were scanned with a FTIR spectrometer (Nicolet 6700, Thermo Nicolet Corporation, USA) at a wavelength range of 4000 to 400 cm−1 after pressing into pellets.

Scanning electron microscope (SEM) and elemental composition analysis (EDS)

The SCP and SCP-Ca powders were fixed, and gilded for 20 s before analyzing by SEM (S-3400 N, Hitachi, Japan). Then, the mixtures were detected by EDS (EA3000, EuroVector, Italy). Finally, all images were obtained.

Determination of circular dichroism (CD) spectroscopy

The secondary structures of SCP and SCP-Ca were characterized with a CD spectrometer (J-1500, Splitting, Japan). Samples were detected at a wavelength range of 190–240 nm, the bandwidth and scanning speed were 1 nm and 120 nm/min. The secondary structure contents were quantified by using CDNN software.

Determination of amino acid composition

The amino acid composition of SCP and SCP-Ca was determined according to Yuan et al. (2024). SCP or SCP-Ca (100 mg), 10 mL of 6 mol/L HCl and 1.0 g of phenol were mixed in ampoule bottles, and then was sealed and subjected to hydrolysis at 110 °C for 22 h. The hydrolysates were concentrated to dryness, and then were dissolved with 0.02 mol/L HCl solution, finally were measured by a fully automated amino acid analyzer (S—433D, Sykam GmbH, Germany) after filtering through a 0.22 μm aqueous filter membrane.

Stability of SCP-Ca

The thermal, acid-base, sugar/salt and digestive stability of SCP-Ca were analyzed by the method described by Zhao et al. (2024). For thermal stability, SCP-Ca solutions were respectively kept at 25, 40, 55, 70 and 85 °C for 2 h. For acid-base stability, the pH of SCP-Ca solutions was adjusted to 2, 4, 6, 8 and 9, and placed at 25 °C for 2 h. For sugar/salt stability, 0.2%, 0.4%, 0.8%, 1.6%, and 3.2% lactose or NaCl were respectively added to SCP-Ca solutions, and kept at 25 °C for 2 h. For digestive stability, the simulated gastric fluid (SGF) was prepared with NaCl (2.0 mg/mL) and pepsin (3.2 mg/mL), and simulated intestinal fluid (SIF) contained pancreatin (4.5 mg/mL), NaHCO3 (62.5 mg/mL) and bile salts (30 mg/mL). SCP-Ca samples (40 mg/mL, 7 mL) was mixed with equal volume of SGF, and the pH was adjusted to 3.0. After incubation at 37 °C for 2 h, the pH was adjusted to 7.5, and equal volume of SIF (pH = 7.5) was mixed at 37 °C for another 2 h reaction. During gastric and intestinal digestion phases, the samples were collected every 30 min, and then inactivated digestive enzymes. Finally, the supernatants of all samples in stability experiment were obtained after ethanol precipitation, and were used to detect the calcium ion content with commercial kit. The calcium retention rate was measured according to the formula in 2.2 (1).

Calcium transport experiment in Caco-2 cell membrane

Cytotoxicity assay

Caco-2 cells were cultured with DMEM high-glucose medium containing 10% (v/v) fetal bovine serum and 1% (v/v) penicillin-streptomycin at 37 °C with a 5% CO₂ atmosphere. When the cell fusion reached to 80–90.0%, it was digested with EDTA solution containing 0.25% trypsin for 3–4 min.Then, cell suspension at a density of 1.0 × 105 cells/mL was seeded into plates, and kept for 24 h. After discarding medium, the Caco-2 cells were washed twice, and then the new culture medium containing CaCl₂ (10–100 μg/mL) or SCP-Ca (10–100 μg/mL) were added and reacted for another 24 h. At last, CCK-8 reagent was given in each well, and the absorbance values were detected at 450 nm by using a Synergy H1 microplate reader (BioTek Instruments, Inc., USA) after 2 h of reaction. The Caco-2 cell viability was calculated with the following formula.(2)Cell viability (%) = ((ODs - ODb)/(ODc -ODb))*100where, ODs, ODb and ODc represents the absorbance value of CaCl₂ or SCP-Ca, blank and control groups, respectively.

Determination of alkaline phosphatase (ALP) and calcium transportation contents

The Caco-2 cells (0.5 mL) with a density of 1 × 105 cells/mL was placed at the upper chamber of a 12-well plate apical (AP) side, and let it grow on the polycarbonate membrane, while the lower chamber of plate basolateral (BL) side was full of complete medium. The transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER) values were evaluated every 2 d. The supernatants in AP and BL sides of Caco-2 cell membrane were obtained at 13–21 d, and its ALP activity was determined using an ALP assay kit. When the TEER values achieved to 400 Ω·cm2, the Caco-2 cell monolayers was washed twice by using Hank's balanced salt solution (HBSS) buffer, then HBSS buffer (1.5 mL) was added to BL side, while CaCl₂ (160 μg/mL, 0.5 mL) or SCP-Ca (60 μg/mL, 0.5 mL) solutions were put in AP side. The samples in the edge of the BL side were collected at the time point of 30, 60, 90 and 120 min. The calcium transportation contents were calculated according to the following formula:(3)Tn=1.5Cn+0.2×∑k=1n−1Ckwhere, Tₙ was the calcium content at the time points of 30, 60, 120 and 180 min; 1.5, 1.2 and Cn (Ck) represented the volume of HBSS buffer, supernatants, and calcium ion concentration of each tome point, respectively.

Anti-osteoporosis activity of SCP-ca in MC3T3-E1 cells

Cytotoxicity assay

The influence of SCP-Ca on MC3T3-E1 cells viability was measured based on the report described by Wang et al. (Wang, Jiang, et al., 2025). MC3T3-E1 cells were placed into 12-well plates, and then added the medium containing 100 nM dexamethasone, 10 nM l-ascorbic acid and 10 mM β-glycerophosphate disodium. Logarithmic growing MC3T3-E1 cells (0.6 × 104 cells/well) were put into plates. After reaction for 24 h, culture medium was replaced with mediums containing various concentration of CaCl2 or SCP-Ca solution. Following another 24 h reaction, the medium was replaced with fresh medium including 10% CCK-8 solution for 2 h incubation. The absorbance values of samples were measured at 450 nm with a microplate reader (SMR60047, Uscn Life Science, Inc., China). The MC3T3-E1 cell viability was calculated with the formula in 2.6.1(2).

Determination of alkaline phosphatase (ALP) activity and alizarin red staining

MC3T3-E1 cells were cultured in plates (0.6 × 104 cells/well). The old medium was removed after reaction for 24 h, and the new mediums with various concentration of CaCl2 or SCP-Ca were added for another 24 h incubation. Then, the medium was discarded, and the CaCl2 or SCP-Ca samples were obtained for ALP and alizarin red staining analysis. In ALP experiment, the MC3T3-E1 cells supernatants were got by centrifuging at 3000 r/min for 3 min, and were subjected to evaluate ALP activity with accorded kit. In alizarin red staining experiment, the MC3T3-E1 cells were washed with phosphate-buffered saline (PBS) for three times, and Alizarin Red staining solution was used to stain for 10–15 min after fixation. Then, the MC3T3-E1 cells were washed with deionized water, followed by counterstain and xylene dehydration. Finally, the MC3T3-E1 cells were observed with a microscope (XZ-10, Guangzhou Mingmei Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd., China).

RT-qPCR and western blot analysis

Total RNA was obtained from MC3T3-E1 cells by using a commercial RNA extraction kit, and its purity were analyzed with a spectrophotometer (NanoDrop 2000, Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA). Subsequently, RNA was reverse-transcribed into cDNA with corresponding kit. Finally, the cDNA was performed to PCR analysis. The primer sequences of runt-related transcription factor 2 (Rux2), osterix (Osx), osteocalcin (Ocn) and alkaline phosphatase (Alp) are listed in Table S1.The transcript content of β-actin was chosen as control, and data were deal with by using 2^(-ΔΔCt) method.

Total protein of MC3T3-E1 cells was extracted and detected by a BCA assay kit, and were separated by gel electrophoresis, then was transferred into polyvinylidene fluoride membranes, and finally were blocked for 1 h. The mixtures were reacted at 4 °C overnight after adding RUNX2, OSX, OCN and ALP antibodies (1:1000), then the membranes were washed. Subsequently, the mixtures were mixed with secondary antibody for 30 min reaction. The immunoreactive bands were observed with a chemiluminescence detection system (JS-1070P, Shanghai Peiqing Science & Technology Co., Ltd., China). The collected images were further deal with IPWIN60 software.

LC-MS/MS analysis

The peptide sequences of SCP-Ca were evaluated with a Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (ThermoFisher scientific, USA) according to the method reported by Wang et al. (Wang, Tian, et al., 2020). The samples were separated by an RP-C18 column (0.15 mm × 150 mm, Column Technology Inc.) with temperature of 25 °C. The injection volume and flow rate was set at 2 μL and 0.25 mL/min, respectively. The mobile phases were consisted of 0.1% formic acid aqueous (A) and acetonitrile (B) solutions, and the elution program was as follows: 0–50 min, 4%–50% B; 50–54 min, 50%–100% B; 54–60 min,100% B. The mass spectrometry condition was set as follows: positive ion mode; m/z, 0–2000 Da; bombardment voltage, 20 eV. The result analysis was performed by MaxQuant software, and peptide sequences were identified by comparing with the corresponding database.

Molecular docking

The molecular docking analysis was carried out to explore the interaction mechanism of selected peptides with Ca2+ and calcium channel proteins (An et al., 2025). The structure of calcium ion was achieved from the PubChem database. The three-dimensional structures of the peptides were got by using PyMOL software according to the amino acid composition, and then were processed with Chem3D software. The top two peptides with lowest binding energies in all samples were further used to dock with proteins TRPV6 and Cav1.3. The structures of TRPV6 (PDB ID: 6bO8) and Cav1.3 (PDB ID: 7uhf) were got from the RCSB PDB database, then were processed by removing H2O molecules and adding polar hydrogen. Molecular docking was analyzed with AutoDock 4.2 software, and model with lowest binding energies was selected after 100 times running. The docking results were visualized through PyMOL and LigPlot softwares.

Statistics analysis

The results were presented as mean ± standard deviation. The statistical analysis was carried out by SPSS software 22.0. The results were evaluated by one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) with Scheffé test, and p < 0.05 presented significant difference.

Results and discussion

Preparation optimization of SCP-Ca

The most suitable chelation time, temperature, pH, and peptide-to‑calcium ratio for preparing of SCP-Ca was optimized. As displayed in Fig. 1(A), with the rising of chelating time, the chelation rate of SCP-Ca was firstly enhanced and then reduced, and exhibited the highest value (56.69 ± 0.90%) at 50 min. Sheep bone peptide also showed the strong calcium chelation rate at 50 min, and was accorded with our study (Hu et al., 2022). From Fig. 1(B), it can be seen that the chelation rate reached the peak value of 57.48 ± 2.07% at 40 °C. High temperature led to slight drop of calcium chelation amount. Similar phenomenon was also observed for the preparation of chicken blood peptides‑calcium chelates (Yang et al., 2024). In Fig. 1(C), the chelation rate was positively related with pH, and exhibited the maximum value of 62.83 ± 0.89% when the pH was 9. There is insignificant difference between pH 8 and 9. In short, H+ can compete with Ca2+ for binding sites with SCP in acidic condition, high temperature and long reaction time will reduce the stability of SCP-Ca (Ke et al., 2022), resulting to the drop of chelation rate. As presented in Fig. 1(D), the peptide‑calcium ratio displayed significant effect on calcium chelation rate of SCP, and exhibited a dose-effect. When peptide‑calcium ratio was ranged from 1:1 to 17:1, the calcium chelation rate was increased from 10.05 ± 0.90% to 83.15 ± 0.52%. The cost is an important factor to evaluate peptide-to‑calcium mass ratio, and is decided on its application potential. With a comprehensive consideration of cost and calcium chelation rate, the optimal peptide-to‑calcium mass ratio for SCP-Ca is selected as 11:1. A similar results was also found on phosvitin hydrolysate‑calcium chelates (Liu et al., 2025), which may be due to the rising of collision opportunities between calcium and peptides. Therefore, the best preparation process for SCP-Ca was time of 50 min, temperature of 40 °C, pH of 8 and a peptide-to‑calcium mass ratio of 11:1, its calcium chelating amount was 44.47 ± 0.87 mg/g.Fig. 1Effect of time (A), temperature (B), pH (C), and peptide-calcium ratio (D) on the Ca2+ chelation rate of SCP (n = 3). Structural characterization of SCP-Ca

UV and fluorescence spectroscopy analysis

The UV and fluorescence spectrum can reflect the effect of Ca2+ on the structure of SCP. From Fig. 2(A), CaCl2 had no UV absorption. SCP and SCP-Ca exhibited a maximum absorption peak at 282 nm, the UV absorption intensity of SCP-Ca was obviously lower than SCP, and showed a dose effect. The phenomenon proved that Ca2+ was bond with SCP. Ding et al. (2025) also found that Ca2+ chelation caused the drop of UV absorption value in nannochloropsis peptides. As shown in Fig. 2(B), CaCl2 showed no fluorescence intensity. With the rising of CaCl2 concentration, the fluorescence intensity of SCP was significantly declined, suggesting that the formation of SCP-Ca resulted to the fluorescence quenching of SCP. A similar trend was also observed by black bean protein hydrolysate after chelating with Ca2+ (Wang et al., 2020).Fig. 2The structural characterization of SCP and SCP-Ca. FTIR analysis

FTIR spectroscopy was applied to analyze the calcium ion chelation sites of SCP. As presented in Fig. 2 (C), calcium ion chelation caused significant change in FTIR spectrum of SCP. In comparison with SCP, the absorption peak of SCP-Ca exhibited a red shift from 3473.76 and 1400.07 cm−1 to 3353.19 and 1409.71 cm−1, indicating N—H and -COO− groups entered the formation of SCP-Ca (Xie et al., 2026). The characteristic absorption peaks at 3087.48 in SCP-Ca and 1454.06 cm−1 in SCP were produced by C—H stretching and in-plane bending vibration (Xun et al., 2023), suggesting that the C—H group was the coordination site of calcium ion. The absorption peaks at 1157.08, 1074.16 and 1079.94 cm−1 represented that C—O group was involved in the binding of SCP and calcium ion (Wang et al., 2025). Thus, the results indicating that these groups were the Ca2+ chelation sites of SCP. Wu et al. (2020) also confirmed that phosphorylated pork bone collagen peptides chelated with Ca2+ through carboxyl and amino groups.

CD spectra analysis

In Fig. 2 (D), the CD spectra of SCP-Ca and SCP was obviously different, suggesting that Ca2+ chelation changed the secondary structure of SCP. Two absorption peaks at near 200 and 220 nm represented the existence of α-helix structure. According to Fig. 2 (E), by compared with SCP, the α-helix, β-turn and random coil content of SCP-Ca were respectively increased by 2.57%, 3.23% and 8.07%, but β-sheet content was reduced by 12.47%. The result indicated that Ca2+ could promote the structural unfolding of SCP, and then exposed more chelating site.

SEM and EDS analysis

As shown in Fig. 3 (A-F), SCP exhibited a spherical structure with surface cracks and pits, while SCP-Ca displayed small cluster-like structures and arranged tightly, and the phenomenon was more obvious in high magnification images, implying that Ca2+ chelation enhanced the aggregation of SCP. From Fig. 3 (G-J), it can be seen that only SCP-Ca exhibited high-intensity calcium signal with weight of 2.36 ± 0.19%, while C, N and O were the major elements in both SCP and SCP-Ca, and its weight reached to 93.74–98.19%. The above results confirmed that Ca2+ was bond with SCP to form SCP-Ca. C, N and O are the basic elements with high content and weight, Luo et al. (2022) also observed the same conclusion in the study of Tilapia bone collagen peptide‑calcium chelates with value of 81.58–99.77%.Fig. 3SEM and EDS analysis of SCP and SCP-Ca. Amino acid composition analysis

As presented in Table S2, Gly and Glu exhibited high level in SCP and SCP-Ca, and Met showed the lowest content. Compared with SCP, the Asp, Thr, Glu, Gly, Ile, and Tyr contents in SCP-Ca were remarkably enhanced, especially for acidic amino acids. The Asp and Glu levels in SCP-Ca was rose from 9.48 ± 0.07% and 17.90 ± 0.12% to 13.52 ± 1.34% and 21.00 ± 0.87%, respectively. While, the basic amino acids like His in SCP-Ca were obviously declined due to the electrostatic repulsion of Ca2+. FTIR results also found that Ca2+ was bond with the carboxyl groups of acidic amino acids, and were aligned with this observation. The data demonstrating that Asp and Glu played important roles in the formation of SCP-Ca, and were same as tuna bone peptides‑calcium chelates (Zhong et al., 2023). Oxygen-containing amino acids can give electrons to metal ions and form two coordination covalent bonds (Liu et al., 2019). The C-terminus of acidic amino acids like Asp and Glu is rich in oxygen-rich amino acids and show negative charges (Yu et al., 2024), thereby displaying stronger calcium-chelating ability than other amino acids.

Stability analysis of SCP-Ca

The anti-osteoporosis activity of peptide‑calcium chelates was depended on its stability during processing and digestion, so the temperature, pH, sugar/salt and digestion stability of SCP-Ca were evaluated. In Fig. 4 (A), when the temperature at 25–85 °C, the calcium retention rate of SCP-Ca was stable at lower than 55 °C, and displayed a slight decrease at 85 °C, while all of values were over 80%. As presented in Fig. 4 (B), the calcium retention rate was gradually reduced with the decline of pH, and was over 70% when the pH was higher than 2. At pH of 2, the calcium retention rate was only 60.25 ± 2.25%, and maybe cause by the competition of H+ and Ca2+on active binding sites of SCP. While, the pH of most common food was over 2.0. The results indicating that SCP-Ca possessed good thermal and pH stability, and was comparable with glycated walnut peptide‑calcium chelates (Wang, Jiang, et al., 2025).Fig. 4Effect of temperature (A), pH (B), lactose (C), sodium chloride (D), and simulated gastrointestinal digestion (E-F) on the stability of SCP-Ca (n = 3). Effect of temperature (A), pH (B), lactose (C), sodium chloride (D), and simulated gastrointestinal digestion (E-F) on the stability of SCP-Ca (n = 3). Different letters show significant difference in different groups (p < 0.05).

NaCl is the common seasoning in food, while lactose can promote calcium absorption and be used as nutritional sweeteners in calcium supplements. As given in Fig. 4 (C), the calcium retention rate of SCP-Ca gradually decreased with the rising of NaCl concentration, and was over 80% in all samples. In Fig. 4 (D), when the concentration of lactose was increased from 0.2 to 3.2%, the calcium retention rate of SCP-Ca was first dropped and then kept stable, and remained over 85%. The decline of stability could be explained by the structural change of SCP. A similar trend was also reported by Wang et al. (Wang, Chen, et al., 2025) in the study of silver carp skin peptide‑calcium chelates, and exhibited comparable stability with SCP-Ca. In addition, the digestive enzyme and pH in the gastrointestinal tract have a great effect on bio-availability of calcium supplements. According to Fig. 4(E), the calcium retention rate of SCP-Ca was only 56.24 ± 1.07% at the point of 30 min, and had no significant change in whole gastric digestion phase. This may be due to the low pH environment and pepsin hydrolysis (Wu et al., 2019), and the data was consistent with pH stability result. In small intestine digestion phase (Fig. 4 (F)), the calcium retention rate of SCP-Ca was obviously enhanced, and reached to 94.26 ± 2.98% at the point of 120 min, which was slightly lower than bovine bone collagen peptide‑calcium chelates with value of 98.50% (Zhang et al., 2021). These findings suggested that SCP-Ca displayed excellent NaCl, lactose stability and calcium bio-availability, and maybe explain the coordinate bonds and tight-knit structure of SCP with calcium ions (Zhang et al., 2023). Meanwhile, the newly produced peptides during gastrointestinal digestion can chelate with calcium ions, resulting to the rising of calcium retention rate (Cui et al., 2019). Walnut and silver carp skin collagen peptides‑calcium chelate also showed the similar results with our research (Wang and Chen et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025).

Effect of SCP-Ca on calcium transport in Caco-2 cell monolayers

Caco-2 cells viability and TEER value evaluation

The impact of CaCl₂ and SCP-Ca on Caco-2 cells viability were described in Fig. 5 (A). The Caco-2 cells proliferation was significant after incubation with SCP-Ca, and displayed obvious dose effect. When the concentration of SCP-Ca was 10–100 μg/mL, the cell viability was over 100%, which was higher than that of CaCl₂. Calcium transport efficiency analysis

The medium concentration (40 μg/mL) of CaCl₂ and SCP-Ca was chosen to measure the calcium transport ability. It can be seen from Fig. 5 (C) that the calcium transport ability of SCP-Ca was obviously increased with the extend of time, and exhibited the maximum value with 1.74 ± 0.15 μg at 120 min, which was significant stronger than that of CaCl₂ with value of 1.50 ± 0.09 μg. The results proved that SCP-Ca could promote calcium transport and absorption in Caco-2 cell monolayers, and the effect was better than CaCl₂. Silver carp skin calcium-chelating peptides also displayed the same phenomenon with our study (Wang et al., 2025).

ALP activity

ALP is a key early-stage indicator of osteogenic differentiation, and was decided to bone growth (Wang, Jiang, et al., 2025). As given in Fig. 6 (B), the ALP activity of MC3T3-E1 cells was significant enhanced after treating with SCP and SCP-Ca, and displayed dose effect. By compared with control group, the ALP activity in SCP-Ca group was increased by 3.02–7.22 times. While deer tendon peptide‑calcium chelates treatment possessed lower ALP level than SCP-Ca (Sun et al., 2024). In addition, although SCP showed modulation effect on ALP activity, but the enhance capability was remarkably weaker than SCP-Ca, suggesting that SCP was mainly through promoting calcium absorption to influence ALP activity, and was accorded to Caco-2 cell monolayers transport result.

Effect of SCP-Ca on MC3T3-E1 cells mineralization

Alizarin red staining can react with calcium nodules to form deep red or purplish-red complexes, and the chroma is positively with MC3T3-E1 cells mineralization. In the Fig. 6 (C), the number of mineralized nodules in SCP and SCP-Ca groups were elevated than control group, and SCP-Ca exhibited the largest amount points with deep red. These findings was same as ovine bone collagen peptide‑calcium chelates (Zhang et al., 2023).The data confirmed that SCP-Ca could promote MC3T3-E1 cells mineralization, and may majorly due to its high calcium ion bio-availability.

Regulation effect of SCP-Ca on key genes and proteins expression related with MC3T3-E1 cells differentiation

RUNX2 can control mesenchymal cells to transform into osteoblasts, and OSX can regulate osteocyte maturation and new bone growth (Chen et al., 2023). OCN is the important components in extracellular matrix of bone. ALP and OCN were respectively early and late osteogenic biomarkers, both of them and RUNX2 built the transcriptional network for improving bone formation (Ouyang et al., 2022). As shown in Fig. 7 (A-H), SCP-Ca treatment obviously increased the mRNA and protein expression levels of RUNX2, OSX, OCN and ALP, and showed a remarkably dose effect. Compared with control group, the mRNA expressions of RUNX2, OSX, OCN and ALP were respectively elevated by 1.23–4.19, 1.45–7.78, 1.73–8.63 and 1.67–7.35 times. Especially, when the concentration of calcium ion in SCP-Ca was 36 μg/mL, the RUNX2, OSX, OCN and ALP protein expression contents were rose by 4.26, 6.23, 6.05 and 13.80 folds than untreated group. Screening highly active calcium-chelating peptides from SCP-Ca

The top 20 peptides identified in SCP-Ca were selected for molecular docking with Ca2+ based on response intensity and scores. As listed in Table S3, the mass and length of 20 peptides were ranged from 9 to 17 and 965.4454–1559.7580. Lin et al. (2024) also found that calcium-chelating peptides derived from marine proteins showed the mass of 200–2000 Da, and was similar with our study. The secondary mass spectra of peptides EKSIDDLEDELY and GFAGDDAPRA were presented in Fig. 8 (A-B), and it given lowest binding energies with values of −7.22 and − 6.18 kcal/mol, respectively, while that of other peptides larger than −6.1 kcal/mol. The oxygen atom, hydroxyl group, lone pair electrons, imidazole group and cysteine of amino acids have been reported to show strong metal chelating abilities, and the involved amino acids included Asp (D), Glu(E), Phe(F), Arg (R), Pro (P), Ser (S), Tyr (Y), Lys (K) and so on (Yu et al., 2024). For example, Glu and Asp had been reported to the important sites of peptide‑calcium chelation, and could influence local charge density and promote calcium binding (Wang et al., 2023). The strong calcium-chelating ability of peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY is due to the existence of these characteristic amino acids. In the Fig. S1 (A-B), the Ca2+ was mainly bond in Glu or Asp of peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY, and was accorded with the data of amino acid composition, indicating that acidic amino acids were the key binding sites of SCP. The molecular interaction mechanism of key protein in transcellular pathway and potential calcium-chelating peptides from SCP-ca

Calcium ion absorption is mainly occurred in small intestine through transcellular pathways. TRPV6 is the key rate limiting calcium absorption channel with high selectivity, while Cav1.3 shows high expression in intestine for enhancing calcium absorption, and is independent of the regulation of Calbindin-D9k and Vitamin D, which are the most important transport proteins in transcellular pathways (Tian et al., 2023). To explore the potential promoting calcium ion absorption mechanism, the molecular docking technology was applied to investigate the molecular interactions of peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY with TRPV6 and Cav1.3. As shown in Fig. 8 (C and E) and Fig. S1 (C—D), the binding energies of peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY with TRPV6 were respectively −8.3 and − 6.1, indicating that these two peptides could spontaneously react with TRPV6. Peptide GFAGDDAPRA formed 2 hydrogen bonds with Asp580 and Ile575, and 5 hydrogen bonds were existed between peptide EKSIDDLEDELY with Gln369, Gln370, Tyr349, Tyr509 and Arg363. The two peptides also interacted with TRPV6 through hydrophobic interactions, and the involved amino acid residues included Asn572, Trp583, Asp525, Glu515, Glu518, Asp364 and so on. Acidic amino acids like Asp and Glu were important for the transporter capability of TRPV6 (Liu et al., 2025). According to Fig. 8 (D and F) and Fig. S1 (E-F), peptides GFAGDDAPRA (−8.3 kcal/mol) and EKSIDD- LEDELY (−6.5 kcal/mol) were also spontaneously bond with Cav1.3, and were respectively formed 13 and 9 hydrogen bonds, the combined amino acid residues were Glu1047, Asp395, Ser1050, Arg1043, Glu759 and etc. While, the hydrophobic interaction of peptide GFAGDDAPRA (contained 10 amino acid residues, such as Glu412) with Cav1.3 was significantly lower than that of peptide EKSIDDLEDELY (contained 15 amino acid residues, like Glu1157). Wang et al. (Wang, Jiang, et al., 2025) also observed that AERGVLYR was interacted with the amino acid residues of Cav1.3 (Glu, Ser and Arg) and TRPV6 (Asp and Glu), and then strengthened the stability of peptide‑calcium chelate, which was similar with our findings. The results demonstrating that peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY may spontaneously bind with calcium transport proteins TRPV6 and Cav1.3, and then enter intestinal epithelial cells from intestinal lumen, finally circulate in the blood through transcellular pathway, resulting to promote calcium absorption (Tian et al., 2023).

Conclusions

The optimal preparation conditions, structure, stability and enhancing calcium absorption mechanism of SCP-Ca were investigated. The results displayed that the best parameters for preparing SCP-Ca was time of 50 min, temperature of 40 °C, pH of 8 and a peptide-to‑calcium mass ratio of 11:1, with calcium chelating amount of 44.47 ± 0.87 mg/g. Spectrum analysis confirmed that SCP was successfully bond with calcium ion, and the involved groups included N—H, C—H, C—O and -COO−, which also caused the unfolding of its secondary structure. Amino acid composition analysis found that Asp and Glu were mainly participated in the formation of SCP-Ca. SCP-Ca exhibited excellent thermal, pH, NaCl, lactose and digestive stabilities. SCP-Ca significantly improved the calcium transport and absorption of Caco-2 cell monolayers, and that was better than CaCl₂. SCP-Ca treatment obviously enhanced the ALP activity and expression of important targets like RUNX2, OSX, OCN and ALP, thereby showed anti-osteoporosis activity by promoting the MC3T3-E1 osteoblast differentiation and mineralization. Peptides GFAGDDAPRA and EKSIDDLEDELY from SCP were the most stable calcium-binding peptides with low binding energy, and could interact with active amino acid sites of calcium channel proteins Cav1.3 and TRPV6, then may strengthen the calcium absorption and utilization. In the future, SCP-Ca can be developed as a novel calcium supplement for osteoporosis relief. The challenges for next work are to explore the in vivo specific therapeutic targets of SCP-Ca and further enhance its calcium chelation amount.
