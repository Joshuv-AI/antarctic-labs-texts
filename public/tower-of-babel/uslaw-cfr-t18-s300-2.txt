18 C.F.R. § 300.2 — Informal conference.
Citation: us:cfr:18:300.2
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 300 (Confirmation And Approval Of The Rates Of Federal Power Marketing Administrations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator or a designee may confer with Commission staff prior to submitting an application under subpart B, with respect to the appropriate form and content of such application.
