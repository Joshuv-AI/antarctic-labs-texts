43 C.F.R. § 3120.60 — Parcels not bid on at auction.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3120.60
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3120.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3120 (Competitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lands offered at the competitive auction that received no bids may be offered in a future competitive auction.
