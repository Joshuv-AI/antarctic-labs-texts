13 C.F.R. § 134.915 — Remand.
Citation: us:cfr:13:134.915
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-134.915
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 134 (Rules Of Procedure Governing Cases Before The Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If OHA grants a Size Standard Petition, OHA will remand the matter to the Office of Size Standards for further analysis. Once remanded, OHA no longer has jurisdiction over the matter unless a new Size Standard Petition is filed as a result of a new final rule published in the Federal Register.
