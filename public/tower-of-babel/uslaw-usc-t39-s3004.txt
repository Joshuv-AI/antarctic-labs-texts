39 U.S.C. § 3004 — Delivery of mail to persons not residents of the place of address
Citation: us:usc:39:3004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3004
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 30 (Nonmailable Matter). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Postal Service determines that letters or parcels sent in the mail are addressed to places not the residence or regular business address of the person for whom they are intended, to enable the person to escape identification, the Postal Service may deliver the mail only upon identification of the person so addressed.
