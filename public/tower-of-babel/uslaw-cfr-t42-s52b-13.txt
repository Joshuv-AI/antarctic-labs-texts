42 C.F.R. § 52b.13 — b.13 Additional conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:52b.13
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-52b.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 52b (PART 52b—NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH CONSTRUCTION GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may with respect to any grant award impose additional conditions consistent with the regulations of this part prior to or at the time of any award when in the Director's judgment the conditions are necessary to assure or protect advancement of the approved project, the purposes of the applicable provisions of the Act, or the conservation of grant funds.
