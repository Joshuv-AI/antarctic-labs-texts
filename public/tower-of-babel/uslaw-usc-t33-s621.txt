33 U.S.C. § 621 — By what methods river and harbor work may be authorized to be prosecuted
Citation: us:usc:33:621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section621
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter V (Prosecution Of Work Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any public work on canals, rivers, and harbors adopted by Congress may be prosecuted by direct appropriations, by continuing contracts, or by both direct appropriations and continuing contracts.
