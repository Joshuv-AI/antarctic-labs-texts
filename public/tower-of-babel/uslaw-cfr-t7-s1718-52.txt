7 C.F.R. § 1718.52 — Existing mortgages.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1718.52
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1718.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1718 (Loan Security Documents For Electric Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in this subpart amends, invalidates, terminates or rescinds any existing mortgage entered into between the borrower and RUS and any other mortgagees.
