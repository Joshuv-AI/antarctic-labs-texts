7 C.F.R. § 982.66 — Shipment reports.
Citation: us:cfr:7:982.66
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-982.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 982 (Hazelnuts Grown In Oregon And Washington). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each handler shall report to the Board the respective quantities of inshell and shelled hazelnuts handled by him during such periods and in such manner as are prescribed by the Board with the approval of the Secretary.
