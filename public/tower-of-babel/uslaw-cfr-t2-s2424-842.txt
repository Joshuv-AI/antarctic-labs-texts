2 C.F.R. § 2424.842 — Who conducts fact finding for HUD debarments?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2424.842
Creator: Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2424.842
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2424 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In all HUD debarments, the official who shall conduct additional proceedings where disputed material facts are challenged shall be a hearing officer.
