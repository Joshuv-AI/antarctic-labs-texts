40 U.S.C. § 17705 — Approval of Federal Communications Commission
Citation: us:usc:40:17705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section17705
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 177 (Alaska Communications Disposal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A transfer under this chapter does not require the approval of the Federal Communications Commission except to the extent that the approval of the Commission is necessary under section 17704(3) of this title.
