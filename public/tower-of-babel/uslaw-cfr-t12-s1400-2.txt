12 C.F.R. § 1400.2 — Board of Directors of the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1400.2
Creator: Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1400.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1400 (Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board of Directors of the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation is entrusted with the responsibility to manage the Corporation. The Board of Directors consists of the members of the Farm Credit Administration Board. The Chairman of the Corporation is elected by the members of the Board.
