2 C.F.R. § 180.940 — Excluded or exclusion.
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.940
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.940
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Excluded or exclusion means:
(a) That a person or commodity is prohibited from being a participant in covered transactions, whether the person has been suspended; debarred; proposed for debarment under 48 CFR part 9, subpart 9.4; voluntarily excluded; or
(b) The act of excluding a person.
