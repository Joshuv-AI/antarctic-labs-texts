42 C.F.R. § 413.373 — Other adjustments to the AKI dialysis payment rate.
Citation: us:cfr:42:413.373
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-413.373
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 413 (Principles Of Reasonable Cost Reimbursement; Payment For End-Stage Renal Disease Services; Prospectively Determined Payment Rates For Skilled Nursing Facilities; Payment For Acute Kidney Injury Dialysis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) CMS applies the wage-adjusted add-on per treatment adjustment for home and self-dialysis training as set forth at § 413.235(c) to payments for AKI dialysis claims that include such training.
(b) The payment rate for AKI dialysis may be adjusted by the Secretary (on a budget neutral basis for payments under section 1834(r) of the Act) by any other adjustment factor under subparagraph (D) of section 1881(b)(14) of the Act.
