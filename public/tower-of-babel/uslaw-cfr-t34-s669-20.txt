34 C.F.R. § 669.20 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:669.20
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-669.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 669 (Language Resource Centers Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary evaluates an application for an award on the basis of the criteria contained in §§ 669.21 and 669.22. The Secretary informs applicants of the maximum possible score for each criterion in the application package or in a notice published in the Federal Register.
