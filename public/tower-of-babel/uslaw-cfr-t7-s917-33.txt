7 C.F.R. § 917.33 — Powers of Control Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:917.33
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-917.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 917 (Fresh Pears And Peaches Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Control Committee shall have the following powers:
(a) To administer, as specifically provided in this part, the terms and provisions of this part.
(b) To make administrative rules and regulations in accordance with and to effectuate the terms and provisions of this part.
(c) To receive, investigate, and report to the Secretary complaints of violations of the provisions of this part.
(d) To recommend to the Secretary amendments to this part.
