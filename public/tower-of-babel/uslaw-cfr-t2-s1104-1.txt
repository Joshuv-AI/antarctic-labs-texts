2 C.F.R. § 1104.1 — Purpose of this part.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1104 (Implementation Of Governmentwide Guidance For Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides an update to the DoD interim implementation of Office of Management and Budget (OMB) guidance in 2 CFR part 200. It supersedes the initial interim implementation of that guidance that DoD adopted in 2 CFR part 1103 on December 19, 2014.
