5 C.F.R. § 316.303 — Tenure of term employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:316.303
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-316.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 316 (Temporary And Term Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A term employee does not acquire a competitive status on the basis of his term appointment.
(b) The employment of a term employee ends automatically on the expiration of his term appointment unless he has been separated earlier in accordance with this chapter.
