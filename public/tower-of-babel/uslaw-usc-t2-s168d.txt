2 U.S.C. § 168d — Authorization of appropriations for Constitution Annotated
Citation: us:usc:2:168d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section168d
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums, to remain available until expended, as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of sections 168 to 168d of this title.
