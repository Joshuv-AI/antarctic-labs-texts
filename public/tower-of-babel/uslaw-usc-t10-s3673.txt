10 U.S.C. § 3673 — Limitation on indemnification
Citation: us:usc:10:3673
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3673
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart C (Contracting Methods And Contract Types); Chapter 257 (Contracts For Long-Term Lease Or Charter Of Vessels, Aircraft, And Combat Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense may not be used to indemnify any person under the terms of a contract entered into under this chapter—
(1)
for any amount paid or due by any person to the United States for any liability arising under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986; or
(2)
to pay any attorneys’ fees in connection with such contract.
