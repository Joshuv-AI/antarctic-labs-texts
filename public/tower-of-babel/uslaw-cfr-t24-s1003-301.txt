24 C.F.R. § 1003.301 — Selection process.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1003.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1003 (Community Development Block Grants For Indian Tribes And Alaska Native Villages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Threshold requirement. An applicant that has an outstanding ICDBG obligation to HUD that is in arrears, or one that has not agreed to a repayment schedule, will be disqualified from the competition.
(b) Application rating. NOFAs will define and establish weights for the selection criteria, will specify the maximum points available, and will describe how point awards will be made.
