42 C.F.R. § 121.14 — Reimbursement for living organ donors: incidental non-medical expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:42:121.14
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-121.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 121 (Organ Procurement And Transplantation Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following incidental non-medical expenses incurred by donating individuals toward making living donations of their organs may be reimbursed:
(1) Lost wages;
(2) Child-care expenses; and
(3) Elder-care expenses.
