20 C.F.R. § 418.2225 — Which more recent tax year will we use?

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will follow the rules in § 418.1225, except that any references in that section to regulations in subpart B of this part shall be treated as references to the corresponding regulation in this subpart.
