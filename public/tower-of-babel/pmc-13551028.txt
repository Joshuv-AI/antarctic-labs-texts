Expression of Concern on ‘TAp73β and DNp73β activate the expression of the pro-survival caspase-2S’
Authors: Julian E Sale, Barry L Stoddard
Published: 2026

This is an Expression of Concern regarding: Wen Hong Toh, Emmanuelle Logette, Laurent Corcos, Kanaga Sabapathy, TAp73β and DNp73β activate the expression of the pro-survival caspase-2S, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 36, Issue 13, 1 August 2008, Pages 4498–4509, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn414

In March 2026, the Editors were made aware of concerns regarding potential image irregularities in Figures 4B and 4D of the above article. These concerns relate to the apparent duplication of immunoblot bands that appear to have been previously published in a different journal article, under different experimental conditions.

In addition, the Editors noted that the error bars in Figures 2B and 5E exhibit features that are not characteristic of error bars generated automatically from source data by standard graphing software.

The Editors have contacted the authors and are escalating to the institution for investigation in line with COPE guidance. In the interim, the Editors are publishing this Expression of Concern to alert readers while the outcome of the investigation is pending and advise readers to examine the details of this study with particular care.
