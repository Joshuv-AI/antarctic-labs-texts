25 C.F.R. § 170.608 — Can a Tribe pay contract support costs from DOI or BIA appropriations?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.608
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Contract support costs for TTP construction projects cannot be paid out of DOI or BIA appropriations.
