34 C.F.R. § 650.37 — What records and reports are required from fellows?
Citation: us:cfr:34:650.37
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-650.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 650 (Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual who is awarded a fellowship shall keep such records and submit such reports as are required by the Secretary.
