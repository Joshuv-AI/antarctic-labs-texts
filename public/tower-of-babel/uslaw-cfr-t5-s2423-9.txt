5 C.F.R. § 2423.9 — How are charges amended?
Citation: us:cfr:5:2423.9
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2423.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2423 (Unfair Labor Practice Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before the issuance of a complaint, the Charging Party may amend the charge under the requirements set forth in § 2423.6.
