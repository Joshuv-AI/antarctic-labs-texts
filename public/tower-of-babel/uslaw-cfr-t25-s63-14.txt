25 C.F.R. § 63.14 — What positions require a background investigation and determination of suitability for employment or retention?
Citation: us:cfr:25:63.14
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-63.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 63 (Indian Child Protection And Family Violence Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All positions that allow an applicant, employee, or volunteer regular contact with or control over Indian children are subject to a background investigation and determination of suitability for employment.
