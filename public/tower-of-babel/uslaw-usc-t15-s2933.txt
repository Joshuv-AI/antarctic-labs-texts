15 U.S.C. § 2933 — United States Global Change Research Program
Citation: us:usc:15:2933
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2933
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 56A (Global Change Research); Subchapter I (United States Global Change Research Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall establish an interagency United States Global Change Research Program to improve understanding of global change. The Program shall be implemented by the Plan developed under section 2934 of this title.
