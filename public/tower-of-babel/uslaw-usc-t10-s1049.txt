10 U.S.C. § 1049 — Subsistence: miscellaneous persons
Citation: us:usc:10:1049
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1049
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 53 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following persons may be provided subsistence at the expense of the United States:
(1)
Enlisted members while sick in hospitals.
(2)
Applicants for enlistment and selective service registrants called for induction.
(3)
Prisoners.
(4)
Civilian employees, as authorized by law.
(5)
Supernumeraries, when necessitated by emergent military circumstances.
