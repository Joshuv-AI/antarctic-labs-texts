34 C.F.R. § 642.3 — Who are eligible participants?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.3
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are eligible for training under this program:
(a) Leadership personnel and full and part-time staff members of projects funded under the Federal TRIO Programs.
(b) Individuals preparing for employment as leadership personnel or staff in projects funded under the Federal TRIO Programs.
