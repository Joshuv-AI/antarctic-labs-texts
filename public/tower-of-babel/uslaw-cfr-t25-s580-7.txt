25 C.F.R. § 580.7 — What is the burden of proof and standard of review?
Citation: us:cfr:25:580.7
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-580.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 580 (Rules Of General Application In Appeal Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Chair bears the burden of proof to support his or her action or decision by a preponderance of the evidence.
(b) The Commission shall review the Chair's actions or decisions de novo.
