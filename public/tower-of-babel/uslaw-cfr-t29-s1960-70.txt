29 C.F.R. § 1960.70 — Reporting of serious accidents.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1960.70
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1960.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1960 (Basic Program Elements For Federal Employee Occupational Safety And Health Programs And Related Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must provide the Office of Federal Agency Programs with a summary report of each fatal and catastrophic accident investigation. The summaries shall address the date/time of accident, agency/establishment named and location, and consequences, description of operation and the accident, causal factors, applicable standards and their effectiveness, and agency corrective/preventive actions.
