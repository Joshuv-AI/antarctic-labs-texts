40 C.F.R. § 98.342 — GHGs to report.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.342
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.342
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must report CH4 generation and CH4 emissions from landfills.
(b) You must report CH4 destruction resulting from landfill gas collection and combustion systems.
(c) You must report under subpart C of this part (General Stationary Fuel Combustion Sources) the emissions of CO2, CH4, and N2O from each stationary combustion unit following the requirements of subpart C.
