7 C.F.R. § 771.6 — Environmental requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:771.6
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-771.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 771 (Boll Weevil Eradication Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No loan will be made until all Federal and state statutory and regulatory environmental requirements have been complied with.
