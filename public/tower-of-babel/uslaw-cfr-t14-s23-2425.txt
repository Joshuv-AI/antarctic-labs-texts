14 C.F.R. § 23.2425 — Powerplant operational characteristics.
Citation: us:cfr:14:23.2425
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-23.2425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 23 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The installed powerplant must operate without any hazardous characteristics during normal and emergency operation within the range of operating limitations for the airplane and the engine.
(b) The pilot must have the capability to stop the powerplant in flight and restart the powerplant within an established operational envelope.
