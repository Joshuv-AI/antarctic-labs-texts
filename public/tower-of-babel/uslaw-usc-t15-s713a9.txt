15 U.S.C. § 713a–9 — Reimbursement of corporation from funds of Government agencies for services, losses, operating costs, or commodities purchased
Citation: us:usc:15:713a–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section713a–9
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Full reimbursement shall be made to the Commodity Credit Corporation for services performed, losses sustained, operating costs incurred, or commodities purchased or delivered to or on behalf of the Lend-Lease Administration, the Army or Navy, the Board of Economic Warfare, the Reconstruction Finance Corporation, or any other Government agency, from the appropriate funds of these agencies.
