21 C.F.R. § 640.51 — Eligibility of donors.
Citation: us:cfr:21:640.51
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-640.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 640 (Additional Standards For Human Blood And Blood Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Whole blood donors must meet the criteria for eligibility prescribed in §§ 630.10 and 630.15 of this chapter.
(b) Collection establishments must determine the eligibility of plasmapheresis donors in accordance with §§ 630.10 and 630.15 of this chapter.
