42 U.S.C. § 16523 — Alaska natural gas pipeline
Citation: us:usc:42:16523
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16523
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter XVI (Studies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after August 8, 2005, and every 180 days thereafter until the Alaska natural gas pipeline commences operation, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission shall submit to Congress a report describing—
(1)
the progress made in licensing and constructing the pipeline; and
(2)
any issue impeding that progress.
