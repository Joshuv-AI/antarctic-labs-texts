39 C.F.R. § 953.6 — Compromise and informal dispositions.
Citation: us:cfr:39:953.6
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-953.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 953 (Mailability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either party may request the other to consider informal disposition of any question of mailability, and the scheduled hearing date may be postponed by the presiding officer for such period of time as may be necessary to accommodate settlement discussions between the parties.
