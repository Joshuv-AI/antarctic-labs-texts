26 U.S.C. § 5357 — Premises
Citation: us:usc:26:5357
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5357
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter F (Bonded And Taxpaid Wine Premises); Part I (Establishment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Bonded wine cellar premises, including noncontiguous portions thereof, shall be so located, constructed, and equipped, as to afford adequate protection to the revenue, as regulations prescribed by the Secretary may provide.
