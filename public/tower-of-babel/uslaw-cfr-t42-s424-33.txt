42 C.F.R. § 424.33 — Additional requirements: Claims for services of providers and claims by suppliers and nonparticipating hospitals.
Citation: us:cfr:42:424.33
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-424.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 424 (Conditions For Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All claims for services of providers and all claims by suppliers and nonparticipating hospitals must be—
(a) Filed by the provider, supplier, or hospital; and
(b) Signed by the provider, supplier, or hospital unless CMS instructions waive this requirement.
