49 C.F.R. § 1253.30 — Retention of records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1253.30
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1253.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1253 (Rate-Making Organization; Records And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each organization subject to sections 5a or 5b shall retain records or documents relating to its transactions or activities in accordance with part 1220, Preservation of Records, of this chapter.
