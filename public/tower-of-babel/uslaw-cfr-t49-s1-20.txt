49 C.F.R. § 1.20 — Secretary of Transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1 (Organization And Delegation Of Powers And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary is the head of the Department. The Secretary exercises oversight of all of the OST components, as well as each of the Operating Administrations, and overall planning, direction, and control of the Department's agenda.
