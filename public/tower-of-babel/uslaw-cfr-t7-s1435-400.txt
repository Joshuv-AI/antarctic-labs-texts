7 C.F.R. § 1435.400 — General statement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1435.400
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1435.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1435 (Sugar Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart will be applicable in the event that raw, refined, or in-process sugar is owned and held in CCC inventory (accumulated under the program authorized by section 156 of the Federal Agriculture Improvement and Reform Act, as amended) as specified in subpart B of this part.
