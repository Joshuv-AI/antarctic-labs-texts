30 C.F.R. § 1241.73 — May ONRR reduce my penalty once it is assessed?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1241.73
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1241.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1241 (Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ONRR's Director or his or her delegate may compromise or reduce a civil penalty assessed under this part.
