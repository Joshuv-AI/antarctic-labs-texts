43 C.F.R. § 4300.20 — How do I apply for a permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must execute a completed application for a grazing permit (Form 4201-1) and file it in the BLM office with jurisdiction over the lands for which you are applying.
