9 C.F.R. § 85.2 — Notice relating to the existence of the contagion of pseudorabies.
Citation: us:cfr:9:85.2
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-85.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 85 (Pseudorabies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notice is hereby given that there is reason to believe that the contagion of pseudorabies may exist in each State and that to prevent the spread and dissemination of the contagion thereof, and to protect the livestock of the United States, the regulations in this part are promulgated.
