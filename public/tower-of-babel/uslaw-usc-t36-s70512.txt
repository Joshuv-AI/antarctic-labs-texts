36 U.S.C. § 70512 — Deposit of assets on dissolution or final liquidation
Citation: us:usc:36:70512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70512
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 705 (The Foundation Of The Federal Bar Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On dissolution or final liquidation of the corporation, any assets of the corporation remaining after the discharge of all liabilities shall be distributed—
(a)
as provided by the board of directors; and
(b)
in compliance with the charter and bylaws.
