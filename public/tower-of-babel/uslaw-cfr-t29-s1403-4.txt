29 C.F.R. § 1403.4 — Assignment of mediators.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1403.4
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1403.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1403 (Functions And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Service will assign one or more mediators to each labor-management dispute in which it has been determined that its services should proffered.
