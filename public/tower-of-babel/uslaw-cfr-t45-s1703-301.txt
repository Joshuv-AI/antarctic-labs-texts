45 C.F.R. § 1703.301 — Meeting place.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1703.301
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1703.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1703 (Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Meetings will be held in meeting rooms designated in the public announcement. Whenever the number of observers is greater than can be accommodated in the meeting room designated, every reasonable effort will be made to provide alternative facilities.
