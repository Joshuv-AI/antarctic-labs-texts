5 C.F.R. § 880.301 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:880.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-880.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 880 (Retirement And Insurance Benefits During Periods Of Unexplained Absence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes OPM's policy concerning the availability and amount of CSRS and FERS annuity payments and the continuation of FEHBP and FEGLI coverage and premiums while an annuitant is classified as a missing annuitant.
