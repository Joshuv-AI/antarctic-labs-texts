14 C.F.R. § 5.51 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:5.51
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-5.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 5 (Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person required to have an SMS under this part must apply safety risk management to the following:
(a) Implementation of new systems.
(b) Revision of existing systems.
(c) Development of operational procedures.
(d) Identification of hazards or ineffective risk controls through the safety assurance processes in subpart D of this part.
