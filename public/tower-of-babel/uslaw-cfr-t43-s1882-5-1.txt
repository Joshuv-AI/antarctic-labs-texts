43 C.F.R. § 1882.5-1 — -1 Tenure of loan.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1882.5-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1882.5-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1880 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loans shall be for a period not to exceed 10 years. Loan documents shall include a schedule of repayment showing the amount of the principal and interest due on each installment.
