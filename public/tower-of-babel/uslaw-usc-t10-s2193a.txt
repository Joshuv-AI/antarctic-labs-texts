10 U.S.C. § 2193a — Improvement of education in technical fields: general authority for support of elementary and secondary education in science and mathematics
Citation: us:usc:10:2193a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2193a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 111 (Support Of Science, Mathematics, And Engineering Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense, in coordination with the Secretary of Education, may establish programs for the purpose of improving the mathematics and scientific knowledge and skills of elementary and secondary school students and faculty members.
