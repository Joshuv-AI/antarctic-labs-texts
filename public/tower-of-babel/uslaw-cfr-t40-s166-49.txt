40 C.F.R. § 166.49 — Public notice of crisis exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:166.49
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-166.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 166 (Exemption Of Federal And State Agencies For Use Of Pesticides Under Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Periodic notices. At least quarterly, the Administrator shall issue a notice in the Federal Register announcing issuance of crisis exemptions. The notice shall contain all of the following:
(1) The name of the applicant;
(2) The pesticide authorized for use;
(3) The crop or site to be treated; and
(4) The name, address, and telephone number of a person in the Agency who can provide further information.
(b) Annual reports. Annually, the Agency shall issue a notice in the Federal Register that shall summarize:
(1) The number of crisis exemptions declared; and
(2) The number of crisis exemptions revoked.
