52 U.S.C. § 21022 — Amount of payment
Citation: us:usc:52:21022
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21022
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part D (Election Assistance); Subpart 2 (Payments To States And Units Of Local Government To Assure Access For Individuals With Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The amount of a payment made to an eligible State or an eligible unit of local government for a year under this subpart shall be determined by the Secretary.
(b) Continuing availability of funds after appropriation
A payment made to an eligible State or eligible unit of local government under this subpart shall be available without fiscal year limitation.
