29 C.F.R. § 1601.34 — Rules to be liberally construed.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1601.34
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1601.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1601 (Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules and regulations shall be liberally construed to effectuate the purpose and provisions of title VII, the ADA, GINA, and the PWFA.
