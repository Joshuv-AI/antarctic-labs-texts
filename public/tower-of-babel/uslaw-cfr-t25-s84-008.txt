25 C.F.R. § 84.008 — What is the effect of the Secretary's disapproval of a contract or agreement that requires Secretarial approval under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:84.008
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-84.008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 84 (Encumbrances Of Tribal Land—Contract Approvals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary disapproves a contract or agreement that requires Secretarial approval under this part, the contract or agreement is invalid as a matter of law.
