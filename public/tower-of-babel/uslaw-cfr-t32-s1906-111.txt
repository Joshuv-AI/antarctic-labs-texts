32 C.F.R. § 1906.111 — Notice.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1906.111
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1906.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1906 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Central Intelligence Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency shall make available, to employees, applicants, participants, beneficiaries, and other interested persons, such information regarding the provisions of this part and its applicability to the programs or activities conducted by the Agency, and make that information available to them in such manner as the Director finds necessary to apprise those persons of the protections against discrimination assured them by section 504 and the regulations in this part.
