Regarding the concerns with similarities between the unpublished material for this article and the material published in [3, retracted in 4], the corresponding author stated that errors were made during the figure preparation of the images presented in the original version of this manuscript, which were corrected in subsequent revisions. Regarding the concerns with similarities between the unpublished material in this article and the material published in [2], the corresponding author stated that the material in [2] was used without the data owner’s authorization.

The corresponding author provided the individual-level data underlying the graphs and tables presented in this article. Editorial assessment of these data raised additional concerns about the integrity and reliability of these data.

In light of the concerns observed with the individual-level data underlying this study, the PLOS One Editors retract this article.
