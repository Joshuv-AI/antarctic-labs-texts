46 C.F.R. § 44.310 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:44.310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-44.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 44 (Special Service Limited Domestic Voyages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hopper dredge means a self-propelled dredge with an open hold or hopper in the hull of the dredge that receives dredged material.
Working freeboard means one-half the distance between the mark of the load line assigned under this subchapter and the freeboard deck.
