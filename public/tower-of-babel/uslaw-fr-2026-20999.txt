DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

[Project No. 2275-052]


Public Service Company of Colorado; Notice of Availability of 
Environmental Assessment

    In accordance with the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 
and the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's (Commission or FERC) 
regulations, 18 CFR part 380, Commission staff reviewed Public Service 
Company of Colorado's application to decommission and surrender the 
license for the Salida Hydro Nos. 1 & 2 Project No. 2275 and have 
prepared an Environmental Assessment (EA) for the project.\1\ The 
licensee is proposing to surrender the project license and decommission 
and remove certain Salida U2 facilities and the remaining 
decommissioned Salida U1 powerhouse and adjacent substation. After 
decommissioning the site would be restored. The project is located on 
the South Arkansas River and Fooses Creek in Chaffee County, Colorado. 
The project is partially located on federal lands administered by U.S. 
Forest Service's Pike-San Isabel National Forests & Cimarron and 
Comanche National Grasslands--Salida Ranger District.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ The unique identification number for documents relating to 
this environmental review is EAXX-019-20-000-1780990050.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The EA contains Commission staff's analysis of the potential 
environmental effects of the proposed surrender, alternatives to the 
proposed action, and concludes that the proposed decommissioning and 
surrender of license for the Salida Hydro Nos. 1 & 2 Project would not 
constitute a major federal action that would significantly affect the 
quality of the human environment.
