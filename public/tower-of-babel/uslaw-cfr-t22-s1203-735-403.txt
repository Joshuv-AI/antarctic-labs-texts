22 C.F.R. § 1203.735-403 — -403 Employee's complaint on filing requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1203.735-403
Creator: United States International Development Cooperation Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1203.735-403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1203 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employee shall have the opportunity for review through agency grievance procedure of the employee's complaint that the employee's position has been improperly included within § 1203.735-401 as one requiring the submission of a statement of employment and financial interests. Employees are reminded that they may obtain counseling pursuant to § 1203.735-103 prior to filing a complaint.
