40 C.F.R. § 437.33 — Effluent limitations attainable by the application of the best available technology economically achievable (BAT).
Citation: us:cfr:40:437.33
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-437.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 437 (The Centralized Waste Treatment Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 125.30 through 125.32 or § 437.30(b), any existing point source subject to this subpart must achieve limitations representing the application of BAT: Limitations for copper, zinc, acetone, acetophenone, 2-butanone, o-cresol, p-cresol, phenol, pyridine, and 2,4,6-trichlorophenol are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 437.31.
