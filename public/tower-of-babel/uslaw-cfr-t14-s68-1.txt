14 C.F.R. § 68.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:68.1
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-68.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 68 (Requirements For Operating Certain Small Aircraft Without A Medical Certificate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the medical education and examination requirements for operating an aircraft under § 61.113(i) of this chapter without holding a medical certificate issued under part 67 of this chapter.
