28 C.F.R. § 549.91 — Definition of “sexually dangerous person.”
Citation: us:cfr:28:549.91
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-549.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 549 (Medical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, a “sexually dangerous person” is a person:
(a) Who has engaged or attempted to engage in:
(1) Sexually violent conduct; or
(2) Child molestation; and
(b) Has been assessed as sexually dangerous to others by a Bureau mental health professional.
