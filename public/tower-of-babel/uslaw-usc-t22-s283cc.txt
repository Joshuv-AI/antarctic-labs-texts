22 U.S.C. § 283cc — Applicability of Bretton Woods Agreements Act
Citation: us:usc:22:283cc
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section283cc
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XII–A (Inter-American Investment Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 286b of this title shall apply with respect to the Corporation to the same extent as with respect to the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development and the International Monetary Fund.
