20 U.S.C. § 9404 — Authorization and appropriation of funds
Citation: us:usc:20:9404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9404
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 75 (Early Learning Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Department of Health and Human Services to carry out this chapter—
(1)
$750,000,000 for fiscal year 2001;
(2)
$1,000,000,000 for fiscal year 2002;
(3)
$1,500,000,000 for fiscal year 2003; and
(4)
such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2004 and 2005.
