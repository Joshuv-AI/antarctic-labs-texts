43 C.F.R. § 3252.10 — What operational standards apply to my exploration operations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3252.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3252.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must keep exploration operations under control at all times by:
(a) Conducting training during your operation to ensure that your personnel are capable of performing emergency procedures quickly and effectively;
(b) Using properly maintained equipment; and
(c) Using operational practices that allow for quick and effective emergency response.
