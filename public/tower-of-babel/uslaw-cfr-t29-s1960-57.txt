29 C.F.R. § 1960.57 — Training of safety and health inspectors.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1960.57
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1960.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1960 (Basic Program Elements For Federal Employee Occupational Safety And Health Programs And Related Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall provide training for safety and health inspectors with respect to appropriate standards, and the use of appropriate equipment and testing procedures necessary to identify and evaluate hazards and suggest general abatement procedures during or following their assigned inspections, as well as preparation of reports and other documentation to support the inspection findings.
