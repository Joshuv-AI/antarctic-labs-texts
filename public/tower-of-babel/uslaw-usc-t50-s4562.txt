50 U.S.C. § 4562 — Territorial application of chapter
Citation: us:usc:50:4562
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4562
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 55 (Defense Production); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall be applicable to the United States, its Territories and possessions, and the District of Columbia.
