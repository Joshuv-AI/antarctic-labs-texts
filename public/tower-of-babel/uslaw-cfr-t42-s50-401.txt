42 C.F.R. § 50.401 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:42:50.401
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-50.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 50 (Policies Of General Applicability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes an informal procedure for the resolution of certain postaward grant and cooperative agreement disputes within the agencies and offices identified in § 50.402.
