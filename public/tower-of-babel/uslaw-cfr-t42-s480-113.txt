42 C.F.R. § 480.113 — QIO access to information collected for QIO purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:42:480.113
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-480.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 480 (Acquisition, Protection, And Disclosure Of Quality Improvement Organization Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Institutions and other entities must disclose to the QIO information collected by them for QIO purposes.
(b) Information collected or generated by institutions or practitioners to carry out quality review studies must be disclosed to the QIO.
