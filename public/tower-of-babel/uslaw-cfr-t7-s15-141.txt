7 C.F.R. § 15.141 — Contents of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:7:15.141
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-15.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 15 (Nondiscrimination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each decision of a hearing officer shall set forth his ruling on each finding, conclusion, or exception presented, and shall identify the requirement or requirements imposed by or pursuant to the regulations in this part with which it is found that the applicant or recipient has failed to comply.
