40 C.F.R. § 85.2101 — General applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:85.2101
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-85.2101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 85 (Control Of Air Pollution From Mobile Sources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Sections 85.2101 through 85.2111 are applicable to all 1981 and later model year vehicles subject to standards under 40 CFR part 86, subpart S.
(b) References in this subpart to engine families and emission control systems shall be deemed to apply to durability groups and test groups as applicable.
