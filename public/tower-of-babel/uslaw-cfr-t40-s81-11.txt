40 C.F.R. § 81.11 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:40:81.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-81.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 81 (Designation Of Areas For Air Quality Planning Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Air quality control regions designated by the Administrator pursuant to section 107 of the Act are listed in this subpart. Regions so designated are subject to revision, and additional regions may be designated, as the Administrator determines necessary to protect the public health and welfare.
