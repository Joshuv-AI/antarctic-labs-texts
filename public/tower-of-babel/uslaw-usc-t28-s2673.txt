28 U.S.C. § 2673 — Reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:28:2673
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2673
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 171 (Tort Claims Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of each federal agency shall report annually to Congress all claims paid by it under section 2672 of this title, stating the name of each claimant, the amount claimed, the amount awarded, and a brief description of the claim.
