Following publication of the original article [1], we were notified that the 4th author’s name was incorrectly listed as “Shengyu Xu” instead of “Shengyuan Xu.” The error was due to an incorrect romanized pinyin spelling of the author’s Chinese name.

The original article has been corrected.
