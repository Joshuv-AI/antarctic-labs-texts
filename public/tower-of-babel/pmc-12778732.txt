Reflex Hammer in the Angio‐suite
Authors: Rijah Chhapra, Saurav Das
Published: 2023

Artist: This piece is a collaborative effort conceptualized by Dr Das, vascular neurology attending at University of Kentucky and executed by Dr Chhapra, psychiatry resident at University of Kentucky. Dr Das has an eye for art and thinks passionately about promoting neurology trainees in a neurointervention career pathway, and Dr Chhapra is a skilled illustrator.

About the Technique: Sepia ink on paper

About the Picture: Following the development of diagnostic cerebral angiogram by a Portuguese neurologist, Egas Moniz, the practice of neurointervention has been enriched by contributions from multiple subspecialties including neurosurgery, radiology, and neurology. In the wake of clinical trials demonstrating strong benefit of endovascular treatment for acute stroke, there is a surge in interest among neurology trainees to pursue neurointervention as a career. 1 , 2

Historically, neurologists have carried a reflex hammer as a part of their physical examination toolkit, and it has become an integral symbol of neurologists’ identity in popular culture. The knee hammer is depicted as sticking out of a neurologist's white coat pocket, hanging next to the lead aprons and shields in the angio‐suite, which is often a collaborative space between the aforementioned subspecialties. This piece of art is intended to be emblematic of the important role of neurologists and neurology trainees in the angio‐suite and in the historical as well as contemporary practice of neurointervention.
