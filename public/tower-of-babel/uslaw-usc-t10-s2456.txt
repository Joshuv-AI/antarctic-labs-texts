10 U.S.C. § 2456 — Coordination with General Services Administration
Citation: us:usc:10:2456
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2456
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 145 (Cataloging And Standardization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To avoid unnecessary duplication, the Administrator of General Services and the Secretary of Defense shall coordinate the cataloging and standardization activities of the General Services Administration and the Department of Defense.
