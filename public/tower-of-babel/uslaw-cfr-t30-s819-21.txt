30 C.F.R. § 819.21 — Auger mining: Protection of underground mining.
Citation: us:cfr:30:819.21
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-819.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 819 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards-Auger Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Auger holes shall not extend closer than 500 feet (measured horizontally) to any abandoned or active underground mine workings, except as approved in accordance with § 816.79 of this chapter.
