21 C.F.R. § 131.25 — Whipped cream products containing flavoring or sweetening.
Citation: us:cfr:21:131.25
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-131.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 131 (Milk And Cream). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The unqualified name “whipped cream” should not be applied to any product other than one made by whipping the cream that complies with the standards of identity for whipping cream (§§ 131.150 and 131.157 of this chapter). If flavoring and/or sweetening is added, the resulting product is a flavored and/or sweetened whipped cream, and should be so identified.
