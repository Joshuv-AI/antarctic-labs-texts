42 C.F.R. § 59a.1 — a.1 Programs to which these regulations apply.
Citation: us:cfr:42:59a.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-59a.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 59a (PART 59a—NATIONAL LIBRARY OF MEDICINE GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations of this subpart apply to grants of funds, materials, or both, for establishing, expanding, and improving basic medical library resources as authorized by section 474 of the Act (42 U.S.C. 286b-5).
(b) This subpart also applies to cooperative agreements awarded for this purpose. In these circumstances, references to “grant(s)” shall include “cooperative agreements(s).”
