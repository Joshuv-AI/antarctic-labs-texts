43 U.S.C. § 11 — Restriction on officers, clerks, and employees
Citation: us:usc:43:11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section11
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Land Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The officers, clerks, and employees in the Bureau of Land Management are prohibited from directly or indirectly purchasing or becoming interested in the purchase of any of the public land; and any person who violates this section shall forthwith be removed from his office.
