41 U.S.C. § 2306 — Openness of meetings
Citation: us:usc:41:2306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section2306
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 23 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator by regulation shall require that—
(1)
formal meetings of the Office of Federal Procurement Policy, as designated by the Administrator, for developing procurement policies and regulations be open to the public; and
(2)
public notice of each meeting be given not less than 10 days prior to the meeting.
