14 C.F.R. § 375.36 — Lease of foreign civil aircraft without crew.
Citation: us:cfr:14:375.36
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-375.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 375 (Navigation Of Foreign Civil Aircraft Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign civil aircraft that are leased without crew to an air carrier or citizen or permanent resident of the United States, and used by the lessee in otherwise authorized air transportation or commercial air operations, may be operated into, out of, and within the United States in accordance with any applicable regulations prescribed by the Federal Aviation Administration.
