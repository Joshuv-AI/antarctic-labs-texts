25 C.F.R. § 36.110 — Must programs provide space for storing personal effects?
Citation: us:cfr:25:36.110
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-36.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 36 (Minimum Academic Standards For The Basic Education Of Indian Children And National Criteria For Dormitory Situations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, students are entitled to private personal spaces for storing their own personal effects, including at least one lockable closet, dresser drawer, or storage space. However, all drawers, dressers, storage space, or lockable space are the property of the homeliving program and are subject to random search.
