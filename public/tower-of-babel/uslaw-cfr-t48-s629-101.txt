48 C.F.R. § 629.101 — 629.101 Resolving tax problems.
Citation: us:cfr:48:629.101
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-629.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 629 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In certain instances, acquisitions by posts are exempt from various taxes in foreign countries. Contracting officers shall ascertain such exemptions and take maximum advantage of them.
