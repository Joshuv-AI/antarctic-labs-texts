40 C.F.R. § 725.20 — Scope and purpose.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-725.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 725 (Reporting Requirements And Review Processes For Microorganisms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes general administrative procedures applicable to all persons who submit MCANs and exemption requests to EPA under section 5 of the Act for microorganisms.
