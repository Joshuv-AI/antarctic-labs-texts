11 U.S.C. § 762 — Notice to the Commission and right to be heard
Citation: us:usc:11:762
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section762
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 7 (Liquidation); Subchapter IV (Commodity Broker Liquidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The clerk shall give the notice required by section 342 of this title to the Commission.
(b)
The Commission may raise and may appear and be heard on any issue in a case under this chapter.
