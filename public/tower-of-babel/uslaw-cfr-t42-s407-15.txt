42 C.F.R. § 407.15 — General enrollment period.
Citation: us:cfr:42:407.15
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-407.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 407 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Enrollment And Entitlement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as specified in paragraph (b) of this section, the general enrollment period is January through March of each calendar year.
(b) An unlimited general enrollment period existed between April 1 and September 30, 1981. Any eligible individual whose initial enrollment period had ended, or whose previous period of entitlement had terminated, could have enrolled or reenrolled during any month of that 6-month period.
