50 C.F.R. § 82.4 — Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:50:82.4
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-82.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 82 (Administrative Procedures For Grants-In-Aid (Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Interior has delegated to the Director, Fish and Wildlife Service, his authority under the Marine Mammal Protection Act to enter into grants or other forms of financial assistance for research relevant to the protection and conservation of marine mammals covered by the Act excluding the order Cetacea and members, other than walruses, of the order Pinnipedia.
