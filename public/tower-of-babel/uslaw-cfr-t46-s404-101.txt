46 C.F.R. § 404.101 — Ratemaking step 1: Recognize previous operating expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:46:404.101
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-404.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 404 (Great Lakes Pilotage Ratemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director uses an independent third party to review each pilotage association's expenses, as reported and audited for the last full year for which figures are available, and determines which expense items to recognize for base ratemaking purposes in accordance with § 404.2 of this part.
