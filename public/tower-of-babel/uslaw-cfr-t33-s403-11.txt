33 C.F.R. § 403.11 — Findings and recommendations. [Rule 11]
Citation: us:cfr:33:403.11
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-403.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 403 (Rules Of Procedure Of The Joint Tolls Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall report its findings and recommendations in writing to The St. Lawrence Seaway Authority and the Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation and shall indicate whether the recommendations represent the unanimous agreement of the members of the Board and, if not, shall indicate those items on which unanimity was not achieved. [Rule 11]
