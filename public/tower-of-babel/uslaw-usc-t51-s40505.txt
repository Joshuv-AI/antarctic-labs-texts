51 U.S.C. § 40505 — Establishment of emergency medical service telemedicine capability
Citation: us:usc:51:40505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40505
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 405 (Biomedical Research In Space). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator, the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management Agency, the Director of the Office of Foreign Disaster Assistance, and the Surgeon General of the United States shall jointly create and maintain an international telemedicine satellite consultation capability to support emergency medical services in disaster-stricken areas.
