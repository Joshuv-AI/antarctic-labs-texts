42 C.F.R. § 136a.31 — a.31 Transition period.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136a (PART 136a—INDIAN HEALTH). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The transition period for full implementation of the new eligibility regulations consists of three parts;
(1) A six month delayed implementation;
(2) A six month grace period; and
(3) A health care continuity period determined by medical factors.
