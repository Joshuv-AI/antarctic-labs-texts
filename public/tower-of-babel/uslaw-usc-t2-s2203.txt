2 U.S.C. § 2203 — Use of the Emancipation Hall of the Capitol Visitor Center
Citation: us:usc:2:2203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2203
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 31 (Capitol Visitor Center); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Emancipation Hall of the Capitol Visitor Center may not be used for any event, except upon the passage of a resolution agreed to by both houses of Congress authorizing the use of the Emancipation Hall for that event.
