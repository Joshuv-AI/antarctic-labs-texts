45 C.F.R. § 681.25 — Where is the hearing held?
Citation: us:cfr:45:681.25
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-681.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 681 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ALJ will hold the hearing in any judicial district of the United States:
(a) In which the defendant resides or transacts business; or
(b) In which the claim or statement on which liability is based was made to NSF; or
(c) As agreed upon by the defendant and the ALJ.
