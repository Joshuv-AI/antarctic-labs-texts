15 U.S.C. § 4911 — Collection of data
Citation: us:usc:15:4911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4911
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 75 (National Trade Data Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 4908 of this title, nothing in this chapter shall be considered to grant independent authority to the Federal Government to collect any data or information from individuals or entities outside of the Federal Government.
