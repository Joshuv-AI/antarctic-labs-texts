14 U.S.C. § 2944 — Retired service members and dependents serving on advisory committees
Citation: us:usc:14:2944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2944
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 29 (Coast Guard Family Support, Child Care, And Housing); Subchapter III (Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A committee that—
(1)
advises or assists the Coast Guard with respect to a function that affects a member of the Coast Guard or a dependent of such a member; and
(2)
includes in its membership a retired Coast Guard member or a dependent of such a retired member;
shall not be considered an advisory committee under chapter 10 of title 5 solely because of such membership.
