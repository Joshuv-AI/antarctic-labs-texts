31 C.F.R. § 370.45 — What is the status of a security if the remittance cannot be collected?
Citation: us:cfr:31:370.45
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-370.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 370 (Electronic Transactions And Funds Transfers Relating To United States Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If we cannot promptly collect all of the remittance for a security, we may in our discretion cancel the security unless it has been legally transferred for value to a third person who had no knowledge of the improper debit entry at the time of the transfer.
