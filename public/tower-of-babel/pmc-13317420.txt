Evaluation and comparison of the clinical significance of miR-21-5p levels from different specimen sources in patients with fungal keratitis
Authors: Qian Liu, Ping Chen, Zhenlan Chen, Hui Liu, Shasha Han
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
BackgroundFungal keratitis (FK) is a type of infectious keratitis with a high risk of causing blindness. Therefore, it is crucial to identify effective diagnostic indicators and understand its pathological mechanism.MethodsQuantitative Reverse Transcription polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR) was employed to detect miR-21-5p expression in tears, serum, and corneal tissues from patients with FK and non-infectious keratitis (NIK) controls. Pearson correlation analysis was used to assess the expression correlation of miR-21-5p across different sample types. Additionally, the correlations between miR-21-5p and the pro-inflammatory cytokines IL-6, TNF-α, and IL-1β were analyzed.ResultsThe expression of miR-21-5p in each sample of the FK group was significantly higher than that of the NIK group, and the expression in corneal tissue was higher than that in tears and serum. In the FK group, the expression levels of IL-6, TNF-α and IL-1β varied significantly among the three sample types (all P < 0.001). The expression of miR-21-5p was significantly positively correlated with the levels of IL-6, TNF-α and IL-1β in serum, tear fluid and corneal tissue (all P < 0.001).ConclusionmiR-21-5p is highly expressed in FK serum, tears, and corneal tissues, and the expression of miR-21-5p is positively correlated with the levels of inflammatory factors.

Introduction

Keratitis is classified into infectious keratitis and non-infectious keratitis (NIK) [1, 2]. Infectious keratitis is an inflammatory disease caused by the invasion of pathogens such as bacteria, fungi, and viruses into the cornea [3]. Fungal keratitis (FK) is one of the most common types of infectious keratitis in clinical practice, mainly caused by Fusarium spp. [4]. NIK is predominantly caused by immune dysregulation, aseptic post-traumatic inflammation, chemical irritation, and other non-infectious etiologies [5, 6]. Although their etiological mechanisms differ significantly, both FK and NIK manifest with common ocular symptoms including eye pain, photophobia, lacrimation, and blurred vision [7, 8]. If FK is not accurately distinguished from NIK in the early stage, it can easily progress to severe complications such as corneal scarring, corneal perforation, or even ocular enucleation [9]. This not only seriously affects the visual function and quality of life of patients, but also imposes a heavy medical burden on families and society. Therefore, the identification of early diagnostic markers with high sensitivity and specificity to effectively distinguish FK from NIK is of critical practical value for refining clinical diagnostic workflows and guiding targeted treatment decisions.

MicroRNA (miRNA) is an endogenous non-coding RNA with a length of 18–25 nucleotides [10, 11]. It regulates post-transcriptional gene expression by targeting and binding to the mRNA of target genes [12]. Numerous studies have implicated multiple miRNAs in the regulatory network of infectious keratitis pathogenesis, including miR-155 [13], miR-132 [14], miR-329 [15], and miR-129-5p [16]. Boomiraj et al. conducted microRNA expression profiling analysis on human corneas in fungal keratitis and identified miR-21-5p was upregulated [17]. Kim et al. studied the miRNA expression in the tears of patients with herpes-induced epithelial keratitis and found that miR-21-5p was upregulated [18]. Despite preliminary confirmation of an association between miR-21-5p and FK onset in these studies, its utility in distinguishing FK from NIK remains controversial. Most existing studies have focused on the detection of miR-21-5p levels in a single specimen type. However, there has been no systematic research on whether the expression of miR-21-5p varies among different specimen sources. The corneal tissue, as the direct site of lesion occurrence, may directly reflect the degree of local inflammation [19]. Tears, as an important component of the intraocular microenvironment, have a close relationship with corneal lesions. Peripheral blood specimens have the advantages of convenient acquisition and non-invasiveness, but whether their miR-21-5p levels can accurately reflect the local infection situation of the eye still needs further verification.

This study aimed to detect the expression levels of miR-21-5p in serum, tears and corneal tissue from FK patients and NIK controls, and to evaluate the value of miR-21-5p as a molecular indicator for FK, and provide new ideas and experimental basis for the clinical diagnosis and treatment of FK.

Materials and methods

Clinical sample collection

This study included 89 patients with FK and 42 patients with NIK who visited our hospital from January 2020 to June 2023. FK was diagnosed using typical clinical manifestations (e.g., feathery corneal infiltration, dry-looking ulcers, and satellite lesions) as well as positive detection results of Fusarium-specific pathogens, such as direct smear microscopy, fungal culture, and nucleic acid amplification testing for Fusarium-specific DNA. Inclusion criteria for FK patients: [1] Meet the diagnostic criteria for keratitis, with a disease course of ≤ 3 weeks; [2] Age 20–65 years old; [3] Patients gave informed consent and signed the research informed consent form. Exclusion criteria for FK patients: [1] History of local or systemic use of anti-inflammatory/antibacterial agents within the past two weeks; [2] Comorbidity with other organic ocular disorders; [3] Sample contamination, insufficient sample volume, or improper sample preservation during collection. Inclusion criteria for NIK patients: [1] After corneal scraping, pathogen culture or molecular biology testing, the results were all negative; [2] After standard anti-infection treatment, the clinical symptoms did not improve, and the infection factors were further excluded. The final diagnosis of the NIK group in this study included: neurotrophic keratitis, exposure keratitis, persistent epithelial defect, and immune-related keratitis. This study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Dongying Hekou District People’s Hospital.

Clinical information collection

During the period of including the research subjects, the collection of baseline clinical data was also completed simultaneously. The various basic information of the patient, including age, gender, body mass index (BMI), smoking and drinking history were all recorded through standardized questionnaire surveys and clinical examinations. The intraocular pressure (IOP) of the affected eyes of the patients was measured using a non-contact tonometer (NCT), and the average value of three consecutive measurements was taken as the final IOP data. The measurement and assessment of the best corrected visual acuity (BCVA) are carried out according to international standard visual charts. The severity of keratitis was graded into mild (< 2.5 mm, superficial, no hypopyon), moderate (< 3.5 mm, < 1/2 corneal thickness, hypopyon < 4 mm), and severe (> 5 mm, > 1/2 corneal thickness, hypopyon > 4 mm) based on ulcer size, depth, and anterior chamber involvement [20]. All operations were performed by the same trained ophthalmic medical staff to ensure the consistency of the data.

Specimen collection

Serum sample: All patients had their elbow venous blood collected at the early morning of the day of their visit, with 4 mL of blood drawn in an empty stomach state. The blood was left at room temperature to stand for 25 min, then centrifuged at 3500 r/min for 10 min to separate the upper serum. The serum was frozen at -80℃ for storage and future testing.

Tear sample: Before collecting the sample, please ask the patient to close their eyes and relax for three minutes. Gently remove the tear fluid (about 30 µL) from the tear ridge of the eyelid using a sterile micropipette (with sterile tips), being careful not to come into contact with the ocular surface tissues. Transfer it right away to an EP tube and store it at -80 °C.

Corneal tissue samples: During the corneal lesion treatment, corneal tissue samples were taken from each patient under sterile conditions. All patients received topical anesthesia with 0.4% oxybuprocaine hydrochloride eye drops. Tissue samples were gently scraped from the active infiltrative borders of corneal ulcers using a sterile corneal curette under a slit-lamp microscope, avoiding the necrotic center, and immediately placed into sterile centrifuge tubes.

qRT-PCR analysis

TRIzol (Invitrogen, Thermo Scientific, USA) was used to extract total RNA from tear, serum, and corneal tissue. A NanoDrop 2000 (Thermo Scientific, USA) was used to quantify the RNA. 1.0 ug of total RNA was converted into cDNA using a PrimeScript RT Reagent Kit (Takara, Japan). SYBR-Green Master Mix (Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc., Hercules, CA, USA) was used to amplify the cDNA. RT-PCR was performed using the ABI7500 RT-PCR System (Thermo Scientific, USA). After standardizing the internal reference gene (U6), the relative miR-21-5p expression levels were calculated.

Expression of inflammatory factors

The concentrations of IL-6, TNF-α and IL-1β in serum, tear and corneal tissue homogenate were detected by ELISA method. The operation closely adhered to the instructions using commercial kits (R&D Systems, USA). Sequentially add the samples, standards and biotin-labeled horseradish peroxidase conjugates to the microplate. Incubate at 37℃ for 30 min. After the incubation and washing steps, add the chromogenic substrate for the color reaction, and incubate in the dark for 15 min. To stop the reaction, add the stop solution once the color has stabilized. All samples were divided into 3 replicate wells. The standard substances were serially diluted and a standard curve was plotted. The absorbance values were measured at a wavelength of 450 nm using an enzyme reader (BioTek, USA), and the sample concentrations were calculated.

Bioinformatics analysis

We used starBase (http://starbase.sysu.edu.cn/), TargetScan (http://www.targetscan.org/) and miRWalk (http://mirwalk.umm.uni-heidelberg.de/) to predict the target genes of miR-21-5p. The online Venn tool (http://bioinformatics.psb.ugent.be/webtools/Venn/) was used to generate a Venn diagram to visualize the number of unique and overlapping target genes among these three databases. The overlapping target genes were uploaded to the STRING database (https://string-db.org/) to construct a PPI network. The PPI network was imported into Cytoscape software for visualization.

Data statistics and analysis

All data were analyzed using GraphPad 9.0 (GraphPad Software, USA) and SPSS 26.0 (IBM, USA). Inter-group comparisons were conducted using the independent sample t-test. Categorical data were analyzed using the chi-square test. Correlation analysis used Pearson. Receiver operating characteristic curve (ROC) analysis was used to evaluate the diagnostic efficacy of miR-21-5p. A P < 0.05 was considered statistically significant.

Results

Comparison of baseline data of the research subjects

A total of 131 patients with keratitis were included in this study, including 89 cases in the FK group and 42 cases in the NIK group (Table 1). There were no significant differences between the two groups of patients in terms of age, gender, BMI, smoking history, drinking history, history of ocular trauma, IOP and BCVA (all P > 0.05). Additionally, there were no statistically significant differences in the severity of keratitis (Mild/Moderate/Severe) or the baseline levels of proinflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α, IL-1β) in serum, tears and corneal tissue between the two groups (all P > 0.05).

Comparison of miR-21-5p expression differences from different specimen sources

In order to investigate the expression pattern of miR-21-5p in FK, we detected its expression levels in different samples using qRT-PCR. The results showed that the expression levels of miR-21-5p in the tear, serum, and corneal tissue of patients with FK were significantly higher than those in the NIK group (Fig. 1A-C, P < 0.001). In the FK group, the expression of miR-21-5p in serum, tear fluid, and corneal tissue showed an increasing trend (Fig. 1D, P < 0.001).

Correlation analysis of miR-21-5p expression in different specimen sources

Pearson correlation analysis was used to evaluate the correlation between miR-21-5p expression in tears, serum and corneal tissue in the FK group. The results showed that there was a positive correlation between miR-21-5p in corneal tissue and tears (Fig. 2A, r = 0.6234, P < 0.001); there was a positive correlation between miR-21-5p in corneal tissue and serum (Fig. 2B, r = 0.6095, P < 0.001); and there was also a positive correlation between miR-21-5p in tears and serum (Fig. 2C, r = 0.6869, P < 0.001).

Analysis of the efficacy of miR-21-5p in different specimens for the diagnosis of keratitis

The ROC curve for the diagnosis of FK using each kind of specimen’s miR-21-5p was plotted. The results (Figs. 3) showed that the area under the curve (AUC) of miR-21-5p in serum was 0.804, the cut-off value was 0.620, the sensitivity was 64.04%, and the specificity was 83.33%; the AUC of miR-21-5p in tears was 0.838, the cut-off value was 0.816, the sensitivity was 61.80%, and the specificity was 97.62%; the AUC of miR-21-5p in corneal tissue was 0.879, the cut-off value was 1.091, the sensitivity was 82.02%, and the specificity was 88.10%.

Bioinformatics analysis of potential target genes of miR-21-5p

We constructed a protein-protein interaction (PPI) network for these 95 core candidate target genes (Fig. 6B). Discussion

In ophthalmology, FK is a frequent cause of blindness, and its pathogenesis is complicated [21, 22]. Clinical diagnosis relies on pathogen detection, but this approach has notable limitations, including prolonged testing turnaround times and suboptimal sensitivity [23, 24]. There is an urgent need to discover diagnostic markers with high specificity and convenience, and to clarify their mechanism of action in inflammatory regulation [25]. This study systematically investigated the expression levels of miR-21-5p and inflammatory factors in different specimens (tears, serum, and corneal tissue) from FK patients and NIK controls, thereby providing novel experimental evidence to support the clinical diagnosis of FK and further elucidate its underlying pathogenic mechanisms.

miR-21-5p is a type of important endogenous miRNA, plays a crucial role in the occurrence and development of various ocular diseases as well as in the regulation of corneal physiological functions [26, 27]. According to earlier research, miR-21-5p can control the expression of genes linked to the aqueous humor outflow pathway, which helps to maintain IOP homeostasis [28]. The expression of miR-21-5p dramatically increased following sodium iodate (NaIO₃) treatment in the dry age-related macular degeneration rat model, indicating that it may be involved in the pathological process of retinal degenerative diseases [29]. Furthermore, the small RNA sequencing results based on the Illumina NextSeq 500 platform indicated that miR-21-5p was significantly highly expressed in human corneal epithelial stem cells, suggesting that it may regulate physiological processes such as the proliferation, differentiation, and maintenance of the barrier function of corneal epithelial cells [30]. Although much attention has been paid to the regulatory role of miR-21-5p in ocular diseases, there is still a lack of research on its expression characteristics in various biological samples of FK patients at present. In this study, the expression levels of miR-21-5p in the tears, serum and corneal tissues of FK patients and the NIK control group were detected. The findings demonstrated that the FK patients’ expression levels of miR-21-5p were significantly higher than those of the NIK group in all three specimens (tears, serum, and corneal tissues) (all P < 0.001). Additionally, the expression of miR-21-5p in the FK group exhibited a progressively rising trend from serum to tears to corneal tissues. This finding is consistent with the results of previous studies: microRNA expression profiling of corneal tissues from patients with fungal keratitis showed that the level of miR-21-5p was significantly elevated [17]. It is worth noting that the expression level of miR-21-5p in tears has a positive correlation with the corneal tissue (r = 0.6234). Tear specimens can be obtained non-invasively and can well reflect the local pathological state of the cornea, providing a feasible approach for clinical dynamic monitoring of corneal inflammatory activity.

We further evaluated the diagnostic efficacy of miR-21-5p in different specimens for FK through ROC curve analysis. The results showed that the AUC of miR-21-5p in corneal tissue for diagnosing FK was the largest (AUC = 0.879), with a sensitivity of 82.02% and a specificity of 88.10%. The AUC of miR-21-5p in tear fluid was 0.838, and its specificity was as high as 97.62%. The AUC of miR-21-5p in serum was 0.804, with a sensitivity of 64.04% and a specificity of 83.33%. This indicates that miR-21-5p may serve as a potential biological indicator for assisting in the differentiation between FK and NIK. We compared the expression levels of IL-1β, TNF-α and IL-6 in the FK group and the NIK group. The results showed that there were no significant differences in these inflammatory cytokines between the two groups. However, in the FK group, the expression of miR-21-5p was significantly positively correlated with the levels of IL-1β, TNF-α and IL-6. Further bioinformatics analysis predicted and screened out four core target genes of miR-21-5p, including STAT3, PIK3R1, VCL and CDK6. Among them, STAT3 has been confirmed to be involved in the inflammatory response and progression of infectious keratitis [31, 32]. Therefore, we speculate that miR-21-5p may regulate the inflammatory process of FK through targeting STAT3 and other core target genes in a synergistic manner.

This study still has certain limitations. Firstly, the sample size is relatively small. Only fungal keratitis caused by Fusarium spp. was included in the study, while several common non-infectious types of keratitis were included as controls. However, other types of infectious keratitis as well as subtypes of NIK were not analyzed separately in detail. Subsequently, we will expand the sample size and conduct stratified analysis for each subtype, which will help to further clarify the expression and role of miR-21-5p in different keratitis subtypes. Secondly, in this study, we only predicted the potential target genes of miR-21-5p through bioinformatics analysis. In the future, we will further verify through cell and animal experiments that miR-21-5p specifically regulates the inflammatory response of FK through interacting with the target genes.

Conclusion

This study confirmed that miR-21-5p was highly expressed in the tear, serum, and corneal tissues of patients with FK. Among them, miR-21-5p in tear fluid has both non-invasive characteristics and good diagnostic efficacy. miR-21-5p may participate in the progression of FK by regulating inflammatory reaction process.
