21 C.F.R. § 200.51 — Aqueous-based drug products for oral inhalation.
Citation: us:cfr:21:200.51
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-200.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 200 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All aqueous-based drug products for oral inhalation must be manufactured to be sterile.
(b) Manufacturers must also comply with the requirements in § 211.113(b) of this chapter.
