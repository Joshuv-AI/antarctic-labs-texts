45 C.F.R. § 83.5 — Effect of title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972.
Citation: us:cfr:45:83.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-83.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 83 (Regulation For The Administration And Enforcement Of Sections 799A And 845 Of The Public Health Service Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The obligations imposed by this part are independent of obligations imposed by or pursuant to title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972.
