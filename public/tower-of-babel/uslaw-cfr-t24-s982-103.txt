24 C.F.R. § 982.103 — PHA application for funding.
Citation: us:cfr:24:982.103
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-982.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 982 (Section 8 Tenant-Based Assistance: Housing Choice Voucher Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A PHA must submit an application for program funding to HUD at the time and place and in the form required by HUD.
(b) For competitive funding under a NOFA, the application must be submitted by a PHA in accordance with the requirements of the NOFA.
(c) The application must include all information required by HUD. HUD requirements may be stated in the HUD-required form of application, the NOFA, or other HUD instructions.
