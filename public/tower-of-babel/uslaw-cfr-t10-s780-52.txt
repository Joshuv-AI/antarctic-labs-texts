10 C.F.R. § 780.52 — Notice and hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:780.52
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-780.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 780 (Patent Compensation Board Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall, in its discretion, afford the applicable party an opportunity for a hearing for the presentation of relevant evidence. Thirty (30) days notice shall be given of the time and place of such hearing. After expiration of the notice period, the Board shall proceed with a hearing and render its decision.
