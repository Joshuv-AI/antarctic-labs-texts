34 C.F.R. § 692.72 — May a State use the funds it receives under the SLEAP Program to pay administrative costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:692.72
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-692.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 692 (Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State may not use any of the funds it receives under the SLEAP Program to pay any administrative costs.
