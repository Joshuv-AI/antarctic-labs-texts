42 U.S.C. § 2000c–5 — Payments; adjustments; advances or reimbursement; installments
Citation: us:usc:42:2000c–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000c–5
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter IV (Public Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payments pursuant to a grant or contract under this subchapter may be made (after necessary adjustments on account of previously made overpayments or underpayments) in advance or by way of reimbursement, and in such installments, as the Secretary may determine.
