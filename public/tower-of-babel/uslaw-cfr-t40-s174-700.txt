40 C.F.R. § 174.700 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:174.700
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-174.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 174 (Procedures And Requirements For Plant-Incorporated Protectants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart lists the inert ingredients that have been exempted from FIFRA and FFDCA section 408 requirements and may be used in a plant-incorporated protectant listed in subpart B of this part.
