20 C.F.R. § 725.225 — Determination of dependency; parent, brother, or sister.
Citation: us:cfr:20:725.225
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-725.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 725 (Claims For Benefits Under Part C Of Title Iv Of The Federal Mine Safety And Health Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual who is the miner's parent, brother, or sister will be determined to have been dependent on the miner if, during the 1-year period immediately prior to the miner's death:
(a) The individual and the miner were living in the same household (see § 725.232); and
(b) The individual was totally dependent on the miner for support (see § 725.233(h)).
