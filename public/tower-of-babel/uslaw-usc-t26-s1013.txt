26 U.S.C. § 1013 — Basis of property included in inventory
Citation: us:usc:26:1013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1013
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter O (Gain Or Loss On Disposition Of Property); Part II (Basis Rules Of General Application). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the property should have been included in the last inventory, the basis shall be the last inventory value thereof.
