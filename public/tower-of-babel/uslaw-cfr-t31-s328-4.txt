31 C.F.R. § 328.4 — Effect of restrictive endorsements.
Citation: us:cfr:31:328.4
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-328.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 328 (Restrictive Endorsements Of U.S. Bearer Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bearer securities bearing restrictive endorsements as herein provided will thereafter be nonnegotiable and payment, redemption, or exchange will be made only as provided in such endorsements.
