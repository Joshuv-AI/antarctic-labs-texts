46 U.S.C. § 7104 — Certificates for medical doctors and nurses
Citation: us:usc:46:7104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7104
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 71 (Licenses And Certificates Of Registry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A certificate of registry as a medical doctor or professional nurse may be issued only to an applicant who has a license as a medical doctor or registered nurse, respectively, issued by a State.
