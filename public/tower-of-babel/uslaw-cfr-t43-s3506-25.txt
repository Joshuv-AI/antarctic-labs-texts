43 C.F.R. § 3506.25 — Once I have a license, what are my responsibilities?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3506.25
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3506.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must share with BLM all data you obtain during exploration. We will consider the data confidential and will not make the data public until either:
(a) The areas involved are leased; or
(b) BLM determines that it must release the data in response to a FOIA request.
