46 C.F.R. § 168.15-25 — -25 Washrooms.
Citation: us:cfr:46:168.15-25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-168.15-25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 168 (Civilian Nautical School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) There must be provided 1 shower for each 10 persons or fraction thereof and 1 wash basin for each 6 persons or fraction thereof for all persons who do not occupy rooms to which private or semi-private facilities are attached.
(b) All wash basins and showers must be equipped with adequate plumbing, including hot and cold running fresh water.
