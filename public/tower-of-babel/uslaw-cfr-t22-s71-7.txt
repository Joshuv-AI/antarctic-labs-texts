22 C.F.R. § 71.7 — Reports on catastrophes abroad.
Citation: us:cfr:22:71.7
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-71.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 71 (Protection And Welfare Of Citizens And Their Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a great catastrophe occurs abroad, either on land or on sea, the officer within whose district the catastrophe takes place or into whose district the survivors are brought shall report immediately by telegraph the names of any American citizens who have been killed or injured and the names of American citizens known to be safe.
