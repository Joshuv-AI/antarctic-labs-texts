16 U.S.C. § 460ffff–4 — Transfer of administrative jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:16:460ffff–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460ffff–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXLIII (Alabama Hills National Scenic Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Administrative jurisdiction over the approximately 56 acres of Federal land depicted on the Map as “USFS Transfer to BLM” is transferred from the Forest Service to the Bureau of Land Management.
