34 C.F.R. § 644.31 — What are unallowable costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:644.31
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-644.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 644 (Educational Opportunity Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs that are unallowable under the Educational Opportunity Centers program include, but are not limited to, the following:
(a) Tuition, fees, stipends, and other forms of direct financial support for participants.
(b) Research not directly related to the evaluation or improvement of the project.
(c) Construction, renovation, and remodeling of any facilities.
