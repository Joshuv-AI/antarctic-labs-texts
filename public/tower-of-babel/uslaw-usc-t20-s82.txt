20 U.S.C. § 82 — Aid in acquisition of collections
Citation: us:usc:20:82
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section82
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 4 (National Zoological Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The heads of executive departments of the Government are authorized and directed to cause to be rendered all necessary and practicable aid to the said Regents in the acquisition of collections for the Zoological Park.
