40 U.S.C. § 9303 — Access to Theodore Roosevelt Island
Citation: us:usc:40:9303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section9303
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 93 (Theodore Roosevelt Island). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the approval of the National Capital Planning Commission and the availability of appropriations, the Director of the National Park Service may provide suitable means of access to and on Theodore Roosevelt Island.
