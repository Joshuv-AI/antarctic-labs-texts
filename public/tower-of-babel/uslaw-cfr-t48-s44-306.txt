48 C.F.R. § 44.306 — 44.306 Disclosure of approval status.
Citation: us:cfr:48:44.306
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-44.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 44 (Subcontracting Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request, the ACO may inform a contractor that the purchasing system of a proposed subcontractor has been approved or disapproved, but shall caution that the Government will not keep the contractor advised of any changes in the approval status. If the proposed subcontractor's purchasing system has not been reviewed, the contractor shall be so advised.
