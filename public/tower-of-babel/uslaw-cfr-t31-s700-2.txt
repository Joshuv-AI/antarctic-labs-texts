31 C.F.R. § 700.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:31:700.2

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-700.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 700 (Regulations Governing Conduct In Or On The Federal Law Enforcement Training Center (Fletc) Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to the buildings and surrounding property of the FLETC, Glynco, Georgia; Artesia, New Mexico; FLECT's Washington Office; any other temporary site FLETC may occupy; and to all persons entering such buildings or property.
