47 C.F.R. § 69.604 — Billing and collection of access charges.
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.604
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Telephone companies shall bill and collect all access charges except those charges specified in §§ 69.116 and 69.117.
(b) All access charges shall be billed monthly.
