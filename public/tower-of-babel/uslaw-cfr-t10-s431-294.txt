10 C.F.R. § 431.294 — Uniform test method for the measurement of energy consumption of refrigerated bottled or canned beverage vending machines.

Creator: Department Of Energy

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 431 (Energy Efficiency Program For Certain Commercial And Industrial Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Scope. This section provides test procedures for measuring, pursuant to EPCA, the energy consumption of refrigerated bottled or canned beverage vending machines.
(b) Testing and Calculations. Determine the daily energy consumption of each covered refrigerated bottled or canned beverage vending machine by conducting the appropriate test procedure set forth in appendix A or B to this subpart.
