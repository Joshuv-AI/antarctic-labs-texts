15 U.S.C. § 1072 — Registration as constructive notice of claim of ownership
Citation: us:usc:15:1072
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1072
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter I (The Principal Register). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Registration of a mark on the principal register provided by this chapter or under the Act of March 3, 1881, or the Act of February 20, 1905, shall be constructive notice of the registrant’s claim of ownership thereof.
