13 C.F.R. § 134.1212 — Effects of the decision.
Citation: us:cfr:13:134.1212
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-134.1212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 134 (Rules Of Procedure Governing Cases Before The Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OHA may affirm, reverse, or remand a final SBA loan review decision. If remanded, OHA no longer has jurisdiction over the matter unless a new appeal is filed as a result of a new final SBA loan review decision.
