40 C.F.R. § 1066.970 — Refueling test for liquid fuels.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1066.970
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1066.970
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1066 (Vehicle-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as described in § 1066.975, test vehicles for refueling emissions as described in 40 CFR 86.150-98, 86.151-98, 86.152-98, and 86.154-98. Keep records as described in 40 CFR 86.155-98.
