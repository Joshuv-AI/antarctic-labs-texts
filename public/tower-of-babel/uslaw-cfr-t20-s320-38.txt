20 C.F.R. § 320.38 — Appeal to Board from decision of hearings officer.
Citation: us:cfr:20:320.38
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-320.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 320 (Initial Determinations Under The Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act And Reviews Of And Appeals From Such Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any claimant aggrieved by a decision of the hearings officer and any base-year employer(s) whose employee was awarded benefits, who participated in the appeal before the hearings officer, may appeal to the Board for review of the decision.
