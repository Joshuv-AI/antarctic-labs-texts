40 C.F.R. § 455.66 — Pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES).
Citation: us:cfr:40:455.66
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-455.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 455 (Pesticide Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7 and 403.13, no later than November 6, 1999 subpart which introduces pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403 and achieve the pretreatment standards for existing sources as follows: There shall be no discharge of process wastewater pollutants.
