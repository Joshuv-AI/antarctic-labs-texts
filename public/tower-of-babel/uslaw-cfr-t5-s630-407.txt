5 C.F.R. § 630.407 — Sick leave used in the computation of an annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.407
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sick leave used in the computation of an annuity is charged against an employee's sick leave account and may not thereafter be used, transferred, or recredited. All sick leave to the credit of an employee as of the date of his or her retirement (or death) and reported to OPM for credit towards the calculation of an annuity is considered used.
