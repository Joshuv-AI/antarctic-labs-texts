22 U.S.C. § 1643e — Offsets
Citation: us:usc:22:1643e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1643e
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter V (Claims Against Cuba And China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In determining the amount of any claim, the Commission shall deduct all amounts the claimant has received from any source on account of the same loss or losses.
