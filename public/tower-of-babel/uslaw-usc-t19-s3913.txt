19 U.S.C. § 3913 — Delegation of authorities
Citation: us:usc:19:3913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3913
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 25 (Clean Diamond Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may delegate the duties and authorities under this chapter to such officers, officials, departments, or agencies of the United States Government as the President deems appropriate.
