44 C.F.R. § 402.1 — Shipments from the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:44:402.1
Creator: Department Of Commerce And Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-402.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 402 (Shipments On American Flag Ships And Aircraft (T-1, Int. 1)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transportation Order T-1 applies to shipments from the United States, as well as to shipments from foreign ports, on American flag ships and aircraft.
