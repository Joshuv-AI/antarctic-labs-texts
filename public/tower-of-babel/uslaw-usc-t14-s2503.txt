14 U.S.C. § 2503 — Status of recalled personnel
Citation: us:usc:14:2503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2503
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 25 (Personnel; General Provisions); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All retired personnel when recalled to active duty shall serve in the grade or rate in which they were serving at the time of retirement.
