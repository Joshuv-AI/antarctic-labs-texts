16 C.F.R. § 456.6 — Declaration of Commission Intent.
Citation: us:cfr:16:456.6
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-456.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 456 (Ophthalmic Practice Rules (Eyeglass Rule)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In prohibiting the use of waivers and disclaimers of liability in § 456.2(d), it is not the Commission's intent to impose liability on an ophthalmologist or optometrist for the ophthalmic goods and services dispensed by another seller pursuant to the ophthalmologist's or optometrist's prescription.
