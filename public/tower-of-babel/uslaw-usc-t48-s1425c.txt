48 U.S.C. § 1425c — Authorization of loans, conveyances, etc.
Citation: us:usc:48:1425c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1425c
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter V (Public Housing And Urban Renewal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Legislature of Guam may by law assist such authority by furnishing, or authorizing the furnishing of, cash donations, loans, conveyances of real and personal property, facilities, and services, and otherwise, and may by law take other action in aid of urban renewal or housing or related activities.
