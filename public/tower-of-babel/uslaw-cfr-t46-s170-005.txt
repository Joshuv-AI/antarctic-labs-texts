46 C.F.R. § 170.005 — Vessel alteration or repair.
Citation: us:cfr:46:170.005
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-170.005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 170 (Stability Requirements For All Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Alterations and repairs to inspected vessels must be done—
(1) Under the direction of the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection; and
(2) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, in accordance with the regulations in this subchapter, to the extent practicable.
(b) Minor alterations and repairs may be done in accordance with regulations in effect at the time the vessel was contracted for.
