22 C.F.R. § 1425.4 — Board decision.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1425.4
Creator: Foreign Service Labor Relations Board; Federal Labor Relations Authority; General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority; And The Foreign Service Impasse Disputes Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1425.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1425 (Review Of Implementation Dispute Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall issue its decision taking such action and making such recommendations concerning the Foreign Service Grievance Board action as it considers necessary, consistent with applicable laws, rules, and regulations.
