25 U.S.C. § 199 — Access to records of Five Civilized Tribes
Citation: us:usc:25:199
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section199
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior, or his accredited representative, shall at all times have access to any books and records of the Choctaw, Chickasaw, Cherokee, Creek, and Seminole Tribes, whether in possession of any of the officers of either of said tribes or any officer or custodian thereof, of the State of Oklahoma.
