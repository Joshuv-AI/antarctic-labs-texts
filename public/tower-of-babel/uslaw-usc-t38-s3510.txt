38 U.S.C. § 3510 — Eligibility and entitlement generally
Citation: us:usc:38:3510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3510
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 35 (Survivors’ And Dependents’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter II (Eligibility And Entitlement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each eligible person shall, subject to the provisions of this chapter, be entitled to receive educational assistance.
