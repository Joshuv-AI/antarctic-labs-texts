47 C.F.R. § 18.121 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:47:18.121
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-18.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 18 (Industrial, Scientific, And Medical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Non-consumer ultrasonic equipment, and non-consumer magnetic resonance equipment, that is used for medical diagnostic and monitoring applications is subject only to the provisions of §§ 18.105, 18.109 through 18.119, 18.301 and 18.303 of this part.
