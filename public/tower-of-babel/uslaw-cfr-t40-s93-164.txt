40 C.F.R. § 93.164 — Inter-precursor mitigation measures and offsets.
Citation: us:cfr:40:93.164
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-93.164
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 93 (Determining Conformity Of Federal Actions To State Or Federal Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies must reduce the same type of pollutant as being increased by the Federal action except the State or Tribe may approve offsets or mitigation measures of different precursors of the same criteria pollutant, if such trades are allowed by a State or Tribe in a SIP or TIP approved NSR regulation, is technically justified, and has a demonstrated environmental benefit.
