46 C.F.R. § 160.055-19 — -19 Pamphlet or Placard.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.055-19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.055-19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each life preserver sold or offered for sale for use on recreational boats must be provided with a pamphlet or placard that a prospective purchaser can read prior to purchase, as specified in 33 CFR part 181 subpart G.
