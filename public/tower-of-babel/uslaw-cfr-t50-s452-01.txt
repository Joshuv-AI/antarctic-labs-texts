50 C.F.R. § 452.01 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:50:452.01

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 452 (Consideration Of Application By The Secretary). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the procedures to be used by the Secretary when examining applications for exemption from section 7(a)(2) of the Endangered Species Act.
