45 C.F.R. § 96.73 — Sterilization.
Citation: us:cfr:45:96.73
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-96.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 96 (Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a State authorizes sterilization as a family planning service, it must comply with the provisions of 42 CFR part 441, subpart F, except that the State plan requirement under 42 CFR 441.252 does not apply.
