10 U.S.C. § 8879 — Powers of district court over prize property notwithstanding appeal
Citation: us:usc:10:8879
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8879
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 883 (Prize). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding an appeal, the district court may make and execute all necessary orders for the custody and disposal of prize property.
