Synchronous efforts for burgeoning bioeconomy: The 4th international biodesign research conference and international symposium on development and application of modern biotechnology
Authors: Qiaoning He, Huimin Yu, Shihui Yang
Published: 2023

Background

As catalyst for sustainable systematic changes and transition, bioeconomy plays vital role in dealing with key economic, societal, and environmental challenges. Synthetic biology is poised to offer radical solutions to confront some of our most significant global challenges including climate changes, food shortages, ageing populations, as well as cancers and pandemic diseases, which can accelerate the development of bioeconomy. With the rapid development of synthetic biology and burgeoning investment in the new bioeconomy era, researchers have been desperately developing efficient and effective tools and strategies to replace fossil resources with renewable carbon resources, to upgrade traditional chemical processing with green bioprocessing, and to promote industrial efficiency and productivity through modern biotechnology (see Fig. 1, Fig. 2, Fig. 3).

Accordingly, the 4th International Biodesign Research Conference (IBDRC) and International Symposium on Development and Application of Modern Biotechnology (ISDAMB) aimed to showcase the latest achievements and research progresses in the field of Synthetic Biology and Biosystems Design, to foster communications and interdisciplinary interactions among government and investment agencies, universities and academic organizations, companies, as well as scientific journal offices and media sectors, to promote conversations between academia and industry, to foster the career development of young scholars and graduate students, and to promote exchanges and new cooperation between domestic and foreign experts in the fields of modern biotechnology, biosystems design, and synthetic biology. Furthermore, the 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB conference provided a wide platform to make synthetic biology and biosystems design become a more industrialized and information-rich technology for utilization. The scope of synthetic biology applications covered by the conference attracts a broad attendance, which will facilitate widespread adoption and integration of synthetic biology approaches and tools across the life sciences. More chances were provided to interact with researchers on the cutting edge of synthetic biology and biodesign.

The 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB was held from October 27 to 30, 2023 in Wuhan, China, the first offline conference after three successful virtual IBDRC, which were held online due to the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic from 2020 to 2022. The conference was guided by the Ministry of Education of China and supported by the Department of Education of Hubei Province and Kuan-cheng WANG Education Foundation. The conference was jointly hosted by Hubei University, Nanjing Agricultural University, and BioDesign Research (BDR), a Science Partner Journal of the American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS). It was also co-organized by several academic and corporate partners including State Key Laboratory of Biocatalysis and Enzyme Engineering, School of Life Sciences, Hubei University, Hubei Society for Synthetic Biology, Chinese Society for Microbiology, Tsingke Biotechnology Co., Ltd. etc. It attracted the congregation of many eminent national and international scientists, academicians, policy makers, managers, and journal reporters from various disciplines.

The conference

General information

The 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB was comprised of plenary and keynote lectures, eight parallel sessions, five forums, and poster presentations in the areas of biodesign and synthetic biology. A ‘BioDesign Research Journal Editorial Discussion’ session was also organized to recruit Junior Editorial Board members and increase the visibility of this new journal. The conference chairmen were Prof. Lixin MA (State Key Laboratory of Biocatalysis and Enzyme Engineering, China), Dr. Xiaohan YANG (Oak Ridge National Laboratory, USA), Dr. Xianen ZHANG (Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, China), and Henghua GUO (Anhui Huaheng Biotechnology Co., Ltd, China). Conference secretaries-general consisted of Drs. Shihui Yang and Aitao Li (Hubei University, China), Drs. Zong-Ming (Max) Cheng (Nanjing Agricultural University, China) and Jun Du (Tsingke Biotechnology Co., Ltd, China).

Inauguration

The 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB attracted more than 1000 experts, government officials, scholars, students, and entrepreneur representatives from more than 200 units in USA, France, Austria, Singapore, Thailand, and China. The Opening Ceremony featured welcome speeches from Prof. Jianping LIU (President of Hubei University), Qihong ZHOU (Deputy Director of the Education Department of Hubei province), Jiangang SUN (Vice Chairman of the Science and Technology Association of Hubei Province), Nianping HUANG (Director of the Foreign Cooperation and Exchange Office of the Education Department of Hubei Province), and Fengying KE (Director of Science and Technology Education Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs of Hubei Province).

Prof. LIU welcomed the dignitaries and participants and introduced the development of synthetic biology in Hubei University. He hoped that the participants could take the opportunity to deeply discuss biotechnology innovations and industrial development, and jointly plan for the innovative breakthroughs and accelerate the development of bioeconomy. Qihong ZHOU congratulated the convening of the conference in his speech, mentioning that the 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB will not only connect domestic and foreign colleges, research institutes, and enterprises to help drive the discussion on industrial demands, economic developments, and technological innovations, but also promote international education, research, and applications. After the welcome speeches, the opening ceremony of Global Artificial intelligence (AI) + SynBioChallenge was also launched.

Plenary and Keynote lectures

The plenary lectures on synthetic biology topics were given by Prof. Zixin DENG (Academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shanghai Jiao Tong University), Prof. Yuguo ZHENG (Academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, Zhejiang University of Technology), and Prof. Yingjin YUAN (Academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Tianjin University). They shared their excellent progresses and put forward particular opinions on the technical tips, innovation modes, and various applications of synthetic biology in sustainable biomanufacturing. They also emphasized the need for developing synthetic biology protocols to design, create, control, and program chassis cells.

Then, seven experts delivered their Keynotes Speeches focusing on the advances in synthetic biology for biomanufacturing and quantitative biology, including Prof. Qipeng YUAN from Beijing University of Chemical Technology (Construction and Industrial Application of Efficient Microbial Cell Factories), Prof. Dongzhi WEI from East China University of Science and Technology (Green Biomanufacturing Driven by Synthetic Biology), Prof. He HUANG from Nanjing Normal University (Industrialization Trend of Functional Fatty Acids Under the Common Health Society), Prof. Jean Marie FRANCOIS from Toulouse Biotechnology Institute, France (Progress Advances in the Production of Bio-sourced Methionine and its Hydroxyl Analogues), Dr. Xianwu QIN, the Chief Engineer of Angel Yeast Co., Ltd (Overcome the obstacles to the industrialization of synthetic biology), Prof. Chenli LIU from Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology Chinese Academy of Sciences (Bacterial cell cycle coordination in Escherichia coli), and Prof. Xianen ZHANG from Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (Synthetic biology and biomanufacturing).

A total of 47 presentations were also conducted in five forums as part of 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB to discuss the development and publication of scientific journals, the innovation and entrepreneurship, the policy and investment of synthetic biology, and research progresses of young scholars and graduate students.

These sessions and forums created more opportunities for communication and cooperation between the participants and the experts in various areas. The experts shared their experiences along with different case studies. More than 1000 participants joined the conference, exchanged ideas, and harvested valuable information from the speakers, the sponsorships, and also other participants.

Global AI + SynBioChallenge

A training course for Global AI and Synthetic Bio technology was conducted on October 27 during the 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB. The training course, lasting 4 hours with 4 presentations, attracted more than 100 offline and 3000 online participants. The presentations mainly involved AI, data mining, and machine learning. Machine learning and privacy computing in biotechnology applications, AI design for biosynthetic pathways, and recent advances in protein engineering with ChatGPT were carried out in the training.

A special opening ceremony of Global AI + SynBioChallenge (GAS) was organized to provide an open competition platform for synthetic biology technology. This special session chaired and briefly introduced by Prof. Qiannan HU (Shanghai Institute of Nutrition and Health, Institute of Chinese Academy). The GAS aims to understand the current technical status of synthetic biology industrialization and the progress that has been made to help promote the integration of artificial intelligence and biosynthesis industry in an all-round way.

BioDesign Research journal editorial discussion

The discussion was focused on the development of the BioDesign Research journal. The journal publishes high quality breakthrough research, reviews, editorials, and perspectives focusing on in silico biosystems design, genetic or epigenetic modification, and genome writing or rewriting in any organism. Executive editor Zong-Ming (Max) Cheng briefly introduced the features, current situations, developing trend, and newly achievements of BioDesign Research. The journal is dedicated to information exchange in the interdisciplinary field of biosystems design. Its unique mission is to pave the way towards the predictable de novo design and assessment of engineered living organisms using rational or automated methods to address global challenges in health, agriculture, and the environment. The participating experts and scholars provided invaluable insights to further strengthen and promote the BioDesign Research, and young talents with passion and vision on synthetic biology research and the development of academic journals were recruited.

Award ceremony

The excellent reports from young scholars and graduate students as well as outstanding posters were evaluated by experts during the 4th IBDRC and ISDAMB. A total of 30 people (16 females and 14 males) won prizes, including 6 first prizes, 9 second prizes, and 15 third prizes, evenly distributed in three categories, including Young Scholar Report Award, Gradstudent Report Award, and Poster Award. Executive Editors Zong-Ming Cheng and Shihui Yang of BioDesign Research presented awards to all the winners.
