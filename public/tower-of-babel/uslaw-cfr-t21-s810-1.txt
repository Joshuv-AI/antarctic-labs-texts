21 C.F.R. § 810.1 — Scope.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 810 (Medical Device Recall Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 810 describes the procedures that the Food and Drug Administration will follow in exercising its medical device recall authority under section 518(e) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.
