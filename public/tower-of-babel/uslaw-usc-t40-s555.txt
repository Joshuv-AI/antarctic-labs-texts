40 U.S.C. § 555 — Donation of law enforcement canines to handlers
Citation: us:usc:40:555
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section555
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter III (Disposing Of Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of a federal agency having control of a canine that has been used by a federal agency in the performance of law enforcement duties and that has been determined by the agency to be no longer needed for official purposes may donate the canine to an individual who has experience handling canines in the performance of those duties.
