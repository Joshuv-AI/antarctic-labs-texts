39 C.F.R. § 913.4 — Disclosure.

Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-913.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 913 (Procedures For The Issuance Of Administrative Subpoenas Under 39 U.S.C. 3016). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any documentary material provided pursuant to any subpoena issued under this section shall be exempt from disclosure under section 552 of title 5, United States Code.
