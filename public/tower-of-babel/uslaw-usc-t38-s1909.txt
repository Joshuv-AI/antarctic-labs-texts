38 U.S.C. § 1909 — Effective date of insurance
Citation: us:usc:38:1909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1909
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter I (National Service Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Insurance may be made effective, as specified in the application, not later than the first day of the calendar month following the date of application therefor, but the United States shall not be liable thereunder for death occurring before such effective date.
