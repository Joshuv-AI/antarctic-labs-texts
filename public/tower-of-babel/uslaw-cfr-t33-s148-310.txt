33 C.F.R. § 148.310 — How long does a license last?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each license remains in effect indefinitely subject to the following:
(a) If it is suspended or revoked by MARAD;
(b) If it is surrendered by the owner; or
(c) As otherwise provided by condition of the license.
