36 C.F.R. § 1231.14 — May the records of terminated agencies be transferred to another agency?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1231.14
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1231.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1231 (Transfer Of Records From The Custody Of One Executive Agency To Another). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The records of executive agencies whose functions are terminated or are in process of liquidation may be transferred to another executive agency that inherits the function. All such transfers must be made in accordance with the provisions of this part.
