12 C.F.R. § 141.25 — Single-family dwelling.
Citation: us:cfr:12:141.25
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-141.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 141 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term single-family dwelling means a structure designed for residential use by one family, or a unit so designed, whose owner owns, directly or through a non-profit cooperative housing organization, an undivided interest in the underling real estate, including property owned in common with others which contributes to the use and enjoyment of the structure or unit.
