43 C.F.R. § 3273.11 — When is a site license unnecessary?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3273.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3273.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You do not need a site license if your facility will be located:
(a) On private land or on split estate land where the United States does not own the surface; or
(b) On Federal land not leased for geothermal resources. In this situation, the Federal surface management agency will issue you the permit you need.
