22 U.S.C. § 288j — International Development Law Institute

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVIII (Privileges And Immunities Of International Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of the International Organizations Immunities Act (22 U.S.C. 288 and following), the International Development Law Institute shall be considered to be a public international organization in which the United States participates under the authority of an Act of Congress authorizing such participation.
