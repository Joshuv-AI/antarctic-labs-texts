7 U.S.C. § 506 — Returns under oath; administration
Citation: us:usc:7:506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section506
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21 (Tobacco Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The returns provided for in this chapter shall be made under oath before a collector or deputy collector of internal revenue, a postmaster, assistant postmaster, or anyone authorized to administer oaths by State or Federal law.
