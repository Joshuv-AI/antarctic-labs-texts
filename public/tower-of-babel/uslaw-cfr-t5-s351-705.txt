5 C.F.R. § 351.705 — Administrative assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:351.705
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-351.705
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 351 (Reduction In Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An agency may, at its discretion, adopt provisions that provide competing employees in the excepted service with assignment rights to other positions under the same appointing authority on the same basis as assignment rights provided to competitive service employees under § 351.701.
(b) Provisions adopted by an agency under paragraph (a) of this section:
(1) Shall be consistent with this part;
(2) Shall be uniformly and consistently applied in any one reduction in force;
(3) May not provide for the assignment of an other-than-full-time employee to a full-time position;
(4) May not provide for the assignment of a full-time employee to an other-than-full-time position;
(5) May not provide for the assignment of an employee in a competitive service position to a position in the excepted service; and
(6) May not provide for the assignment of an employee in an excepted position to a position in the competitive service.
