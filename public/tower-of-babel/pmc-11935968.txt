Targeting PD‐L1 with DNA Aptamers and Conjugated with Gemcitabine as a Novel Therapeutic Strategy for Bladder Cancer Chemotherapy Combined with Immunotherapy
Authors: Xing Hu, Hongliang Zeng, Yongbo Peng, Minhua Deng, Wei Xiang, Biao Liu, Jiahao Liu, Yunlong Fu, Zhiqiang Hu, Weibin Hou, Xuewen Liu, Jin Tang, Zhi Long, Long Wang, Jianye Liu
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
Cancer Therapy Aptamers have great functions in carrier drug delivery and have broad application prospects in the field of biomedicine. In article number 2300104, Jianye Liu and co‐workers report the synthesis of a novel material, PD‐L1‐GEMs wherein the PD‐L1 aptamer and gemcitabine, which has targeting ability, are coupled. The PD‐L1‐GEMs facilitate targeted chemotherapy and immunotherapy for bladder cancer simultaneously, greatly enhancing the efficacy of gemcitabine and reducing its side effects. © 2023 WILEY‐VCH GmbH
