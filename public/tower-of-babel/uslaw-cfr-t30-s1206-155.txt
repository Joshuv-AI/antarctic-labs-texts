30 C.F.R. § 1206.155 — What are my reporting requirements under an arm's-length transportation contract?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1206.155
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1206.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1206 (Product Valuation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must use a separate entry on form ONRR-2014 to notify ONRR of an allowance based on transportation costs that you or your affiliate incur(s).
(b) ONRR may require you or your affiliate to submit arm's-length transportation contracts, production agreements, operating agreements, and related documents.
(c) You can find recordkeeping requirements in parts 1207 and 1212 of this chapter.
