24 C.F.R. § 3288.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3288.10
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3288.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3288 (Manufactured Home Dispute Resolution Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of the HUD Manufactured Home Dispute Resolution Program established in this subpart B apply in each state that does not establish a state dispute resolution program that complies with the Act and has been accepted by HUD as provided in subpart D of this part.
