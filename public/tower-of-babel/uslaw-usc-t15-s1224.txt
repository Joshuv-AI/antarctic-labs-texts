15 U.S.C. § 1224 — Antitrust laws as affected
Citation: us:usc:15:1224
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1224
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 27 (Automobile Dealer Suits Against Manufacturers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No provision of this chapter shall repeal, modify, or supersede, directly or indirectly, any provision of the antitrust laws of the United States.
