14 C.F.R. § 1214.600 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1214.600
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1214.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1214 (Space Flight). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes policy and procedures for carrying mementos on the NASA missions, with the exception of mementos and personal effects carried onboard the International Space Station (ISS).
