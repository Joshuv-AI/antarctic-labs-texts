40 C.F.R. § 1800.202 — Proportionate number of miles of shoreline that experienced oiling as a result of the Deepwater Horizon oil spill.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1800.202
Creator: Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1800.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1800 (Spill Impact Component). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proportionate number of miles for each Gulf Coast State is determined by dividing each Gulf Coast State's number of miles of oiled shoreline determined in § 1800.201 by the total number of affected miles. This calculation yields the following:
(a) Alabama—8.04%.
(b) Florida—15.63%.
(c) Louisiana—58.92%.
(d) Mississippi—14.19%.
(e) Texas—3.22%.
