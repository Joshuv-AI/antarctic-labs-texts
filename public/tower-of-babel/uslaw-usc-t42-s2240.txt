42 U.S.C. § 2240 — Licensee incident reports as evidence
Citation: us:usc:42:2240
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2240
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XV (Judicial Review And Administrative Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No report by any licensee of any incident arising out of or in connection with a licensed activity made pursuant to any requirement of the Commission shall be admitted as evidence in any suit or action for damages growing out of any matter mentioned in such report.
