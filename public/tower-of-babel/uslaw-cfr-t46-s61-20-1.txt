46 C.F.R. § 61.20-1 — -1 Steering gear.
Citation: us:cfr:46:61.20-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-61.20-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 61 (Periodic Tests And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The marine inspector must inspect the steering gear at each inspection for certification for vessels whose Certificate of Inspections are renewed each year. For other vessels, the marine inspector must inspect the steering gear twice within a 5-year period, and no more than 3 years may elapse between any inspection and its immediate predecessor. The marine inspector may inspect the steering gear more often, if necessary.
(b) All devices employed in the change-over from automatic to manual operation must be examined and tested.
