32 C.F.R. § 339.13 — Reports to Congress and GAO.
Citation: us:cfr:32:339.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-339.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 339 (Dod Guidance Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the issuance of a final guidance document, the appropriate Federal Register Liaison Officer will submit a report to Congress and GAO in accordance with the procedures described in 5 U.S.C. 801 (the “Congressional Review Act”). If the CRA procedures are not followed, the guidance document can be nullified.
