10 C.F.R. § 1017.12 — Prohibitions on identifying Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1017.12
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1017.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1017 (Identification And Protection Of Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information, documents, and material must not be identified as being or containing UCNI in order to:
(a) Conceal violations of law, inefficiency, or administrative error;
(b) Prevent embarrassment to a person or organization;
(c) Restrain competition; or,
(d) Prevent or delay the release of any information that does not properly qualify as UCNI.
