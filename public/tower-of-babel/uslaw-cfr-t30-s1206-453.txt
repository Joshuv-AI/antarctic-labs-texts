30 C.F.R. § 1206.453 — Quality and quantity measurement standards for reporting and paying royalties.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1206.453
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1206.453
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1206 (Product Valuation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For all leases subject to this subpart, the quantity of coal on which royalty is due shall be measured in short tons (of 2,000 pounds each) by methods prescribed by the BLM. Coal quantity information will be reported on appropriate forms required under part 1210 of this subchapter.
