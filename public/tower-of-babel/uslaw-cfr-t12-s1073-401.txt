12 C.F.R. § 1073.401 — Administrative wage garnishment.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1073.401
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1073.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1073 (Procedures For Bureau Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may collect debts from a debtor's wages by means of administrative wage garnishment in accordance with the requirements of 31 U.S.C. 3720D under the procedures established in 31 CFR 285.11.
