Resonant Type-II + Type-I Hybrid Leptogenesis in SO(10) with Triplet–Neutrino Mass Degeneracy M T ≃ M N 𝟑 M_{T}\simeq M_{N_{3}}
Gayatri Ghosh

Abstract
The origin of the baryon asymmetry of the Universe remains one of the central open problems in particle physics and cosmology. We identify a new regime of hybrid resonant leptogenesis in renormalizable SO(10) grand unified theories, where a quasi-degeneracy between the scalar triplet and the heaviest right-handed neutrino, M T ≃ M N 3 , M_{T}\simeq M_{N_{3}}, induces an unavoidable interference between type-I and type-II decay amplitudes. This interference gives rise to a physical CP-violating phase, ϕ HR = arg ⁡ ( μ ​ f ​ Y ν † ) , \phi_{\rm HR}=\arg(\mu fY_{\nu}^{\dagger}), which cannot be removed by field redefinitions and leads to a resonant enhancement of the baryon asymmetry without extreme Yukawa hierarchies or tuning. We show that the observed asymmetry, Y B ≃ 8.7 × 10 − 11 , Y_{B}\simeq 8.7\times 10^{-11}, is naturally reproduced for masses around 10 11 ​ GeV 10^{11}\,\mathrm{GeV} , moderate washout, and 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 ) \mathcal{O}(1) phases. The mechanism is predictive and testable, with correlated implications for lepton-flavour violation and electric dipole moments.

II
SO(10) Framework and Hybrid Seesaw Structure

We outline the minimal renormalizable SO(10) framework required to realize the hybrid resonant leptogenesis regime identified in the previous section. Renormalizable SO(10) models provide a well-motivated and economical setting for neutrino mass generation, unification of fermion representations, and baryogenesis, while maintaining predictivity at high scales

Bertolini2013
;
Dueck2013
;
AltarelliReview2018
.

Matter fields are embedded in three copies of the spinorial representation
16_{F}
, while the scalar sector contains a
10_{H}
and a
\overline{126}_{H}
. This choice represents the minimal Higgs content capable of simultaneously generating realistic charged-fermion masses, Majorana neutrino masses, and scalar triplet interactions relevant for leptogenesis

BajcMinimalSO10
;
FukuyamaReview2021
. After symmetry breaking, the decomposition under

SU(3)_{C}\times SU(2)_{L}\times U(1)_{Y}

contains two Higgs doublets and an electroweak triplet

T\sim(1,3,1)
,
which plays a central role in the hybrid mechanism.

II.1
Yukawa Sector

The renormalizable SO(10) Yukawa Lagrangian is given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{Y}=16_{F}^{T}\,C\left(Y_{10}\,10_{H}+Y_{126}\,\overline{126}_{H}\right)16_{F}+\text{h.c.},

(1)

where
Y_{10}
and
Y_{126}
are complex symmetric matrices, as required by SO(10) invariance

AulakhGarg2008
;
GrimusKuhbock
. After symmetry breaking, the resulting fermion Yukawa matrices take the form

\displaystyle Y_{u}

\displaystyle=H+r_{u}F,

(2)

\displaystyle Y_{d}

\displaystyle=H+r_{d}F,

(3)

\displaystyle Y_{e}

\displaystyle=H+r_{e}F,

(4)

\displaystyle Y_{\nu}

\displaystyle=H+r_{\nu}F,

(5)

with
H\propto Y_{10}
and
F\propto Y_{126}
. This structure is known to successfully reproduce charged-fermion masses and mixings while allowing a dominant type-II contribution to neutrino masses

GohMohapatraNg
;
Malinsky2006
.

II.2
Right-Handed Neutrino Masses

The
\overline{126}_{H}
representation contains a Standard-Model singlet whose vacuum expectation value generates Majorana masses for right-handed neutrinos,

\displaystyle M_{R}=v_{R}F.

(6)

We assume a hierarchical spectrum
M_{1}\ll M_{2}\ll M_{3}
, consistent with generic SO(10) constructions

DevMohapatra2010
;
SenjanovicUnification2018
. Crucially, we focus on the regime

\displaystyle M_{T}\simeq M_{3}\equiv M_{0},

(7)

where the scalar triplet mass becomes quasi-degenerate with the heaviest right-handed neutrino. In renormalizable SO(10), this relation is technically natural, as both masses originate from the same
\overline{126}_{H}
multiplet and remain stable under radiative corrections

BabuMohapatra2012
;
DiLuzio2021
.

II.3
Scalar Triplet Sector

The electroweak triplet
T
couples to lepton doublets through

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{T}\supset\frac{1}{2}f_{\alpha\beta}\,\ell_{\alpha}^{T}i\sigma_{2}T\ell_{\beta}+\mu\,HHT^{\dagger}+\text{h.c.},

(8)

where
f_{\alpha\beta}
is a complex symmetric matrix.
After electroweak symmetry breaking, the induced triplet vacuum expectation value is

\displaystyle\langle T^{0}\rangle=\frac{\mu v^{2}}{M_{T}^{2}},

(9)

leading to a type-II seesaw contribution to neutrino masses

Schmidt2020
;
ChunReview2019
.

II.4
Hybrid Neutrino Masses

The light neutrino mass matrix receives both type-I and type-II contributions,

\displaystyle m_{\nu}=-v^{2}Y_{\nu}M_{R}^{-1}Y_{\nu}^{T}+2f\langle T^{0}\rangle.

(10)

In the parameter region of interest, the type-II term dominates the neutrino mass spectrum, while the type-I contribution remains nonzero and plays a crucial dynamical role in leptogenesis. This hybrid structure is essential for generating CP violation through interference effects

Hambye2019
;
AntuschHybrid2020
.

II.5
Hybrid Resonant CP Phase

When
M_{T}\simeq M_{3}
, loop corrections induce a physical CP-violating invariant,

\displaystyle\phi_{\rm HR}=\arg\!\left(\mu fY_{\nu}^{\dagger}\right),

(11)

which governs the interference between type-I and type-II decay amplitudes of
N_{3}
and
T
. This phase cannot be removed by field redefinitions and is absent in hierarchical limits, making it a genuine observable of the resonant hybrid regime

PilaftsisUnderwood
;
DevPilaftsisReview
.

III
Resonant Hybrid CP Asymmetries

The central feature of the present framework is that the CP-violating phase

\phi_{\rm HR}
arises only through the interference of type-I and type-II
contributions and is absent in either sector taken separately

Blanchet2015
;
Dev2018Review
. As a result, the generated baryon asymmetry
becomes sensitive to a new invariant combination of parameters, leading to a
qualitatively different scaling behaviour from conventional leptogenesis
scenarios
Pilaftsis1999
;
Fong2013
.

In the near-degenerate regime
M_{T}\simeq M_{N_{3}}
, loop diagrams involving
the scalar triplet contribute to the decay of the heaviest right-handed
neutrino, while neutrino loops induce CP violation in triplet decays

Hambye2002
;
AristizabalSierra2014
. The corresponding CP asymmetries
receive a resonant enhancement governed by a common Breit–Wigner denominator,
analogous to resonant leptogenesis but with intrinsically hybrid dynamics

PilaftsisUnderwood2004
;
DevPilaftsis2015
.

For the decay of
N_{3}
, the hybrid contribution to the CP asymmetry takes the
form

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{I,T}_{3\alpha}\big|_{\rm res}=\frac{3}{16\pi}\frac{M_{3}}{(Y_{\nu}Y_{\nu}^{\dagger})_{33}}\imaginary\!\left[\mu(fY_{\nu}^{\dagger})_{\alpha 3}(Y_{\nu})_{3\alpha}\right]

\displaystyle\times\frac{M_{3}\Gamma_{T}}{(M_{T}^{2}-M_{3}^{2})^{2}+M_{3}^{2}\Gamma_{T}^{2}}.

(12)

where
\Gamma_{T}
denotes the total triplet decay width. This contribution
dominates over the standard non-resonant type-I terms in the degenerate limit,
even for moderate Yukawa couplings
Biondini2016
;
Garbrecht2017
.

Similarly, the CP asymmetry in triplet decays receives a resonant contribution
from loops involving
N_{3}
,

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{II}_{\alpha\beta}\big|_{\rm res}=\frac{1}{4\pi}\frac{\imaginary\!\left[\mu f_{\alpha\beta}(Y_{\nu}^{\ast})_{3\alpha}(Y_{\nu}^{\ast})_{3\beta}\right]}{\Tr(ff^\dagger)+|\mu|^{2}/M_{T}^{2}}\frac{M_{T}M_{3}\Gamma_{3}}{(M_{T}^{2}-M_{3}^{2})^{2}+M_{3}^{2}\Gamma_{3}^{2}},

(13)

which mirrors the resonant enhancement found in scalar-mediated leptogenesis
scenarios
Hambye2012
;
Chun2017
.

Summing over flavours, the total CP asymmetry in the resonant regime is well
approximated by

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{\rm tot}_{3}\big|_{\rm res}\simeq\sum_{\alpha}\varepsilon^{I,T}_{3\alpha}\big|_{\rm res}+\sum_{\alpha\beta}\varepsilon^{II}_{\alpha\beta}\big|_{\rm res},

(14)

reflecting the common resonant structure shared by both contributions. The
appearance of the physical phase

\phi_{\rm HR}=\arg(\mu fY_{\nu}^{\dagger})
in both terms highlights the
intrinsically hybrid nature of CP violation in this regime and its departure
from conventional resonant leptogenesis frameworks

Dev2017
;
Antusch2021
.

The flavour-covariant treatment is essential in the temperature regime relevant
for the present analysis, where charged-lepton Yukawa interactions are partially
equilibrated and flavour coherence effects cannot be neglected

Abada2006Flavour
;
Nardi2006Flavour
. In this regime, the density-matrix
formalism provides a unified description interpolating between the fully
unflavoured and fully flavoured limits, while consistently accounting for
decoherence effects induced by Standard Model interactions

Beneke2010
;
Garbrecht2011Density
.

The interplay between right-handed neutrino decays and scalar triplet dynamics
leads to a non-trivial competition between source and washout terms, especially
in the resonant regime where both contributions are active simultaneously

AristizabalSierra2012
;
Biondini2018
. Gauge-mediated scatterings and triplet
annihilations play an important role in regulating the triplet abundance and
preventing its overproduction, thereby stabilizing the final baryon asymmetry
against variations of the initial conditions

Hambye2006Gauge
;
Blanchet2017Review
.

Spectator processes redistribute the generated lepton asymmetry among the
various particle species in the plasma and are consistently included through
equilibrium conditions imposed by fast Standard Model interactions

Nardi2009Spectator
;
Barbieri2000
. After sphaleron freeze-out, the final
baryon asymmetry is obtained via the standard conversion relation,

Y_{B}=c_{\rm sph}\,\Tr(Y_{\Delta_\ell}),

which remains valid in the presence of flavour correlations

HarveyTurner
;
Laine2013Sphaleron
.

IV
Numerical Strategy and Benchmark Choice

To demonstrate the viability of hybrid resonant leptogenesis in the regime

M_{T}\simeq M_{N_{3}}
, we adopt a representative parameter choice consistent
with renormalizable SO(10) unification and a dominantly type-II neutrino mass
spectrum
AltarelliReview2018
;
FukuyamaReview2021
;
SenjanovicUnification2018
.
The Dirac Yukawa couplings are taken to exhibit moderate hierarchies, ensuring
perturbativity and stability under renormalization group evolution

GrimusKuhbock
;
DiLuzio2021
, while remaining compatible with low-energy
neutrino data
BabuMohapatra2012
.

The heavy mass spectrum is hierarchical,

M_{1}\ll M_{2}\ll M_{3}
, with the scalar triplet quasi-degenerate with the
heaviest right-handed neutrino,

\displaystyle M_{T}=M_{3}(1+\delta),\qquad|\delta|\sim 10^{-3},

(15)

corresponding to a percent-level mass degeneracy. This naturally induces a
strong resonant enhancement of the hybrid CP asymmetry derived in Sec. III,
analogous in structure to resonant leptogenesis but arising here from an
intrinsically hybrid mechanism
PilaftsisUnderwood
;
DevPilaftsisReview
.
The triplet trilinear coupling is chosen to carry an
{\cal O}(1)
complex
phase, maximizing the physical hybrid invariant

\phi_{\rm HR}=\arg(\mu fY_{\nu}^{\dagger})
, which controls the interference
between type-I and type-II decay amplitudes
Hambye2019
;
AntuschHybrid2020
.

We assume thermal initial abundances for both the scalar triplet and the
heaviest right-handed neutrino, as ensured by gauge interactions in the early
Universe
Hambye2006Gauge
, and a vanishing initial lepton asymmetry.
Solving the flavour-covariant Boltzmann equations described in Sec. IV

Abada2006Flavour
;
Nardi2006Flavour
;
Garbrecht2011Density
, we find that the
observed baryon asymmetry of the Universe is reproduced for triplet and
right-handed neutrino masses around
10^{11}\,\mathrm{GeV}
, moderate washout,
and
{\cal O}(1)
CP-violating phases, in agreement with the measured value

Planck2018
.

Importantly, the required quasi-degeneracy between
M_{T}
and
M_{N_{3}}
is
not imposed ad hoc, but emerges naturally from the SO(10) structure of the
model
Bertolini2013
;
Malinsky2006
. This distinguishes the present
framework from conventional resonant leptogenesis scenarios, where mass
degeneracies must typically be tuned by hand
Pilaftsis1999
;
Dev2017
.

VI
Conclusions

We have developed the first complete formulation of
resonant hybrid
type-I plus type-II leptogenesis
within a renormalizable SO(10) grand unified
theory featuring a controlled quasi-degeneracy between the scalar triplet and
the heaviest right-handed neutrino,
M_{T}\simeq M_{N_{3}}
. In this regime, the
interference between type-I and type-II decay amplitudes induces a genuinely
new CP-violating invariant,

\phi_{\rm HR}=\arg(\mu fY_{\nu}^{\dagger})
, which cannot be removed by field
redefinitions and governs the dynamics of the baryon asymmetry.

We have shown that this hybrid CP phase leads to a resonant enhancement of the
lepton asymmetry through the constructive interference of triplet and
right-handed neutrino contributions. As a result, the observed baryon
asymmetry of the Universe can be reproduced for mass scales around

The resonant hybrid regime identified here is theoretically well motivated by
the structure of SO(10) unification, phenomenologically rich, and highly
predictive. It therefore provides a compelling and falsifiable pathway to
baryogenesis, linking the origin of the baryon asymmetry to neutrino masses
and low-energy CP violation within a unified framework.
Finally, we have shown that although the heavy states responsible for hybrid
leptogenesis reside far above the kinematic reach of the LHC, their effects do
not completely decouple from collider observables.
The hybrid CP-violating invariant induces a characteristic pattern of
dimension-six operators, leading to correlated signatures in Higgs CP
observables, Higgs–lepton couplings, lepton-flavour-violating dilepton
production, and electroweak precision measurements.
