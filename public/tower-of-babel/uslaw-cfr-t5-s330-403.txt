5 C.F.R. § 330.403 — Positions brought into the competitive service.
Citation: us:cfr:5:330.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-330.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 330 (Recruitment, Selection, And Placement (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may convert the appointment of a nonpreference eligible whose restricted position was brought into the competitive service under part 316 of this chapter, and who meets the requirements for conversion under part 315 of this chapter, to career or career conditional appointment.
