12 U.S.C. § 2012 — Board of directors
Citation: us:usc:12:2012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2012
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter I (Farm Credit Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Farm Credit Bank shall elect a board of directors of such number, for such term, in such manner, and with such qualifications, as may be required in its bylaws, except that, at least one member shall be elected by the other directors, which member shall not be a director, officer, employee, or stockholder of a System institution.
