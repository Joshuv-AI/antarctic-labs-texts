43 C.F.R. § 3836.25 — What if BLM denies my petition for deferment of assessment work?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3836.25
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3836.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3836 (Annual Assessment Work Requirements For Mining Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If BLM denies your petition for deferment of assessment work, and the assessment year has ended, BLM will give you 60 days from the date you receive the BLM decision denying the petition in which to pay the maintenance fee to maintain your claim.
