27 U.S.C. § 219a — Severability
Citation: us:usc:27:219a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title27-section219a
Context: United States Code, Title 27 (Intoxicating Liquors); Chapter 8 (Federal Alcohol Administration Act); Subchapter II (Alcoholic Beverage Labeling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this subchapter and this chapter and of the application of such provision to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
