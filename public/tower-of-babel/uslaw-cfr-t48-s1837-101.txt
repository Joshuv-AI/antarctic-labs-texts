48 C.F.R. § 1837.101 — 1837.101 Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1837 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pension portability means the recognition and continuation in a successor service contract of the predecessor service contract employees' pension rights and benefits.
