46 C.F.R. § 107.283 — Certificate of Inspection: Conditions of validity.
Citation: us:cfr:46:107.283
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-107.283
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 107 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To maintain a valid Certificate of Inspection, you must complete your annual and periodic inspections within the periods specified in §§ 107.269 and 107.270 and your Certificate of Inspection must be endorsed.
