46 C.F.R. § 113.35-13 — -13 Mechanical engine order telegraph systems; operation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.35-13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.35-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If more than one transmitter operates a common indicator in the engineroom, all the transmitters must be mechanically interlocked and operate in synchronism. A failure of the transmission wire or chain at any transmitter must not interrupt or disable any other transmitter.
