25 C.F.R. § 215.1 — No operations until lease approved.
Citation: us:cfr:25:215.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-215.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 215 (Lead And Zinc Mining Operations And Leases, Quapaw Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No operations under any lease executed under the regulations in this part shall be permitted upon any restricted or trust lands allotted to or inherited by an Indian until such lease covering such tract shall be approved by the Secretary of the Interior.
