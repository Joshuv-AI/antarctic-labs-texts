29 C.F.R. § 801.6 — Notice of protection.
Citation: us:cfr:29:801.6
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-801.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 801 (Application Of The Employee Polygraph Protection Act Of 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every employer subject to EPPA shall post and keep posted on its premises a notice explaining the Act, as prescribed by the Secretary. Such notice must be posted in a prominent and conspicuous place in every establishment of the employer where it can readily be observed by employees and applicants for employment. Copies of such notice may be obtained from local offices of the Wage and Hour Division.
