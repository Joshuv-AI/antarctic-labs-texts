27 C.F.R. § 46.105 — Receipt for taxes.
Citation: us:cfr:27:46.105
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-46.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 46 (Miscellaneous Regulations Relating To Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to § 46.106, the appropriate TTB officer will issue a receipt to a taxpayer if cash is received as a remittance in payment of special tax (including penalties and interest, if any), or for any type of remittance received if the taxpayer requests a receipt.
