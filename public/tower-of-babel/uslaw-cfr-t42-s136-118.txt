42 C.F.R. § 136.118 — Effect on existing rights.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.118
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are not meant to and do not:
(a) Affect, modify, diminish, or otherwise impair the sovereign immunity from suit enjoyed by an Indian tribe;
(b) Authorize, require or permit the termination of any existing trust responsibility of the United States with respect to the Indian people;
(c) Permit significant reduction in services to Indian people as a result of this subpart.
