36 C.F.R. § 1121.5 — Access to requested information to the individual.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1121.5
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1121.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1121 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon verification of identity the Board shall disclose to the individual or authorized representative the information contained in the record which pertains to that individual. Nothing in this section shall allow an individual access to any information compiled in reasonable anticipation of a civil action or proceeding.
