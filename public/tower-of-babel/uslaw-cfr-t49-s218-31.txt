49 C.F.R. § 218.31 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:218.31
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-218.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 218 (Railroad Operating Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes minimum operating rule requirements for the protection of railroad employees engaged in the operation of trains, locomotives and other rolling equipment.
