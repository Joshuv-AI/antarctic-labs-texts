14 C.F.R. § 1251.108 — Administrative requirements for small recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1251.108
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1251.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1251 (Nondiscrimination On Basis Of Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Associate Administrator may require any recipient with fewer than 15 employees, or any class of such recipients, to comply with §§ 1251.106 and 1251.107, in whole or in part, when the Associate Administrator finds a violation of this part or finds that such compliance will not significantly impair the ability of the recipient or class of recipients to provide benefits or services.
