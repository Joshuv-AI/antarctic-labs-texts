46 C.F.R. § 28.305 — Lifesaving and signaling equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:28.305
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-28.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 28 (Requirements For Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel to which this subpart applies must meet the requirements for life preservers, immersion suits, ring life buoys, distress signals, and survival craft in §§ 28.110, 28.115, 28.145 and table 28.120 (a), (b), or (c), as appropriate for the vessel type, on the date that its construction or major conversion is completed.
