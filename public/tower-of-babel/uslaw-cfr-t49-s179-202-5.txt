49 C.F.R. § 179.202-5 — -5 Tank head puncture resistance system.
Citation: us:cfr:49:179.202-5
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-179.202-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DOT-117 specification tank car must have a tank head puncture resistance system in conformance with § 179.16(c). The full height head shields must have a minimum thickness of 1/2 inch.
