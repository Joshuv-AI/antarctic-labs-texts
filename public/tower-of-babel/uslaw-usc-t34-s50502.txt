34 U.S.C. § 50502 — Blue Alert communications network
Citation: us:usc:34:50502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section50502
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 505 (National Blue Alert). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall establish a national Blue Alert communications network within the Department of Justice to issue Blue Alerts through the initiation, facilitation, and promotion of Blue Alert plans, in coordination with States, units of local government, law enforcement agencies, and other appropriate entities.
