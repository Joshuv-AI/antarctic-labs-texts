20 C.F.R. § 411.300 — What is an EN?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.300
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employment network (EN) is any qualified entity that has entered into an agreement with us to function as an EN under the Ticket to Work program and assume responsibility for the coordination and delivery of employment services, vocational rehabilitation services, or other support services to beneficiaries who have assigned their tickets to that EN.
