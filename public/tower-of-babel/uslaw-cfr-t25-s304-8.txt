25 C.F.R. § 304.8 — Use of label by dealer.
Citation: us:cfr:25:304.8
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-304.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 304 (Navajo, Pueblo, And Hopi Silver, Use Of Government Mark). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any dealer offering for sale silver bearing the Government mark may, if he wishes, attach to silver so marked a label or ticket calling attention to the Government mark.
