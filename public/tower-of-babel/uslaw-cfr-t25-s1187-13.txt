25 C.F.R. § 1187.13 — May applicants submit a joint application?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1187.13
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1187.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1187 (Indian Business Incubators Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Two or more eligible entities may submit a joint application for a project that combines the resources and expertise of those entities at a physical location dedicated to assisting Native businesses and Native entrepreneurs under the IBIP.
