25 C.F.R. § 700.211 — Basic rights and rules.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.211
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart do not deprive any displaced person of any rights described elsewhere in these regulations. The Commission may meet its obligation to provide persons with reasonable opportunities to relocate to a replacement dwelling by offering such opportunities developed or to be developed under this subpart.
