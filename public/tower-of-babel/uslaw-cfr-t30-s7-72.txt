30 C.F.R. § 7.72 — New technology.
Citation: us:cfr:30:7.72
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-7.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 7 (Testing By Applicant Or Third Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MSHA may approve a blasting unit that incorporates technology for which the requirements of this subpart are not applicable if the Agency determines that the blasting unit is as safe as those which meet the requirements of this subpart.
