48 C.F.R. § 2509.400 — 2509.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2509.400
Creator: Chapter 25—National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2509.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2509 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart supplements subpart 9.4 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation by prescribing NSF policies and procedures and assigning responsibility for making debarment and suspension decisions. Nothing in this subpart is intended to alter the effect of subpart 9.4.
