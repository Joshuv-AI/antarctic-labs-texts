12 C.F.R. § 390.306 — Public unit.
Citation: us:cfr:12:390.306
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-390.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 390 (Regulations Transferred From The Office Of Thrift Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term public unit means the United States, any state of the United States, the District of Columbia, any territory of the United States, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, any county, any municipality or any political subdivision thereof.
