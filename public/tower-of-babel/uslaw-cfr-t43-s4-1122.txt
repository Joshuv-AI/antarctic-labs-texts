43 C.F.R. § 4.1122 — Termination of jurisdiction.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1122
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in these regulations, the jurisdiction of an ALJ will terminate upon:
(a) The filing of a notice of appeal from an initial decision or other order dispositive of the proceeding;
(b) The issuance of an order by the Board granting a petition for review; or
(c) The expiration of the time period within which a petition for review or an appeal to the Board may be filed.
