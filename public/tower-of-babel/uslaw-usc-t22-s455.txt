22 U.S.C. § 455 — General penalty provision
Citation: us:usc:22:455
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section455
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 9 (Foreign Wars, War Materials, And Neutrality); Subchapter II (Neutrality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In every case of the violation of any of the provisions of this subchapter or of any rule or regulation issued pursuant thereto where a specific penalty is not provided in this subchapter, such violator or violators, upon conviction, shall be fined not more than $10,000, or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.
