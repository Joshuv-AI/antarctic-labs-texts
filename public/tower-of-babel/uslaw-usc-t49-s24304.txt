49 U.S.C. § 24304 — Employee stock ownership plans
Citation: us:usc:49:24304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section24304
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Passenger Transportation); Chapter 243 (Amtrak). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In issuing stock pursuant to applicable corporate law, Amtrak is encouraged to include employee stock ownership plans.
