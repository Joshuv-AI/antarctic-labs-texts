7 U.S.C. § 2351 — Day for taking action falling on Saturday, Sunday, or holiday
Citation: us:usc:7:2351
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2351
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter I (Plant Variety Protection Office); Part B (Legal Provisions As To The Plant Variety Protection Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the day, or the last day, for taking any action or paying any fee in the United States Plant Variety Protection Office falls on Saturday, Sunday, a holiday within the District of Columbia, or on any other day the Plant Variety Protection Office is closed for the receipt of papers, the action may be taken or the fee paid, on the next succeeding business day.
