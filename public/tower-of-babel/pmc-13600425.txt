Correction to: The Long-Term Effect of 2 and 3 Doses of Bivalent HPV Vaccination Against HPV Infections: A Comparison Between 2 Observational Cohort Studies
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: M Middeldorp, J W Duijster, B J A Hoeve-Bakker, J Berkhof, H E de Melker, The Long-Term Effect of 2 and 3 Doses of Bivalent HPV Vaccination Against HPV Infections: A Comparison Between 2 Observational Cohort Studies, The Journal of Infectious Diseases, 2026;, jiag167, https://doi.org/10.1093/infdis/jiag167

In the originally published version of this manuscript, the sentence “Among those who answered this question, pregnancy rates were similar between vaccinated and unvaccinated participants (average pregnancy rate of 3% in both groups in the HAVANA cohort and 0% in both groups in HAVANA2), suggesting that exclusion of vaccinated women is unlikely to have impacted our VE estimates” should instead read “Among those who answered this question, pregnancy rates were similar between vaccinated and unvaccinated participants (average pregnancy rate of 3% in both groups in the HAVANA cohort and 0% in both groups in HAVANA2), suggesting that exclusion of pregnant women is unlikely to have impacted our VE estimates”.

This error has been corrected.
