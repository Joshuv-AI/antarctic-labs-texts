36 C.F.R. § 909.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:36:909.101
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 909 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part effectuates section 119 of the Rehabilitation, Comprehensive Services, and Developmental Disabilities Amendments of 1978, which amended section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 to prohibit discrimination on the basis of handicap in programs or activities conducted by Executive agencies or the United States Postal Service.
