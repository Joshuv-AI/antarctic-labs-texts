22 U.S.C. § 6553 — Continuation of United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy
Citation: us:usc:22:6553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6553
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 74 (Foreign Affairs Agencies Consolidation); Subchapter III (United States Information Agency); Part C (Conforming Amendments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy, established under section 1469 of this title and section 8 of Reorganization Plan Numbered 2 of 1977, shall continue to exist and operate under such provisions of law.
