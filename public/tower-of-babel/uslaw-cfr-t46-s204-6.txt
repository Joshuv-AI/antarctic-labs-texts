46 C.F.R. § 204.6 — Payment of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:46:204.6
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-204.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 204 (Claims Against The Maritime Administration Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Once the amount to be paid has been agreed upon, the agency shall attempt to forward a check for such amount to the claimant within thirty days.
(b) If a claimant is represented by an attorney, both the claimant and the claimant's attorney shall be designated as payees on any check delivered to the claimant's attorney.
