49 C.F.R. § 601.23 — Initiation of rulemaking.
Citation: us:cfr:49:601.23
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-601.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 601 (Organization, Functions, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator initiates rulemaking on his/her own motion. However, in so doing, he/she may, in his/her discretion, consider the recommendations of his/her staff or other agencies of the United States or of other interested persons.
