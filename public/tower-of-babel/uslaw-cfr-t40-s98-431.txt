40 C.F.R. § 98.431 — Reporting threshold.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.431
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.431
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any importer or exporter of fluorinated GHGs contained in pre-charged equipment or closed-cell foams who meets the requirements of § 98.2(a)(4) must report each fluorinated GHG contained in the imported or exported pre-charged equipment or closed-cell foams.
