34 U.S.C. § 11271 — Assistance to potential grantees
Citation: us:usc:34:11271
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11271
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter III (Runaway And Homeless Youth); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide informational assistance to potential grantees interested in establishing runaway and homeless youth centers and transitional living youth projects.
