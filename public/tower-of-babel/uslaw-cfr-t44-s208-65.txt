44 C.F.R. § 208.65 — Mode of transmission.
Citation: us:cfr:44:208.65
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-208.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 208 (National Urban Search And Rescue Response System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When sending all submissions, determinations, and requests for supplemental information under this subpart, all parties must use a means of delivery that permits both the sender and addressee to verify the dates of delivery.
