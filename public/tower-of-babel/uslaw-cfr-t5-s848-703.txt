5 C.F.R. § 848.703 — Lump-sum credit.
Citation: us:cfr:5:848.703
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-848.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 848 (Phased Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual performs phased employment, the lump-sum credit as defined in 5 U.S.C. 8401(19) will be reduced by any annuity that is paid or accrued during phased employment.
