Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records

AGENCY: Department of State.

ACTION: Rescindment of a system of records notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The ``Board of Appellate Review Records,'' State-02, system of 
records notice, which is being rescinded, applies to the records of the 
Office of the Legal Adviser Board of Appellate Review (``L-BAR''). L-
BAR records included appeals of U.S. Department of State (the 
``Department'') determinations related to loss of U.S. nationality the 
denial of an application for a U.S. passport, or the revocation of a 
U.S. passport. State-02 contained correspondence between the appellant 
and their attorney at the Department, the appellant's passport file, 
and copies of decisions rendered by the Board of Appellate Review. The 
latter two categories of records were only maintained in State-02 for 
the duration of the review after which they were returned to the 
Department's Passport Records system of records, State-26.

DATES: The Department of State stopped maintaining the ``Board of 
Appellate Review Records, State-02'' in May 2024 following the 
disposition of remaining hard copy files pertaining to loss of 
nationality cases. All other records previously maintained in the 
system have been disposed of in accordance with the Department's 
domestic Legacy Records Disposition Schedule for hard copy records 
created before 2012; specifically, Chapter 23 Legal Affairs Records, 
N1-059-95-11, item 10b.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Board of Appellate Review, previously 
within the Department's Office of the Legal Adviser, was responsible 
for reviewing appeals related to Department determinations concerning 
cases of loss of nationality or the revocation of a passport. This 
board was disbanded in 2008. The administrative review functions 
related to passport revocations and loss of nationality have since been 
transferred to the Bureau of Consular Affairs. Relevant System of 
Records Notices pertaining to these records include State-05, Overseas 
Citizens Services Records and Other Overseas Records, State-26, 
Passport Records, and State-21, Legal Case Management Records.
