Generation of WTD, a control human iPSC line for genetic research
Authors: Bria L. Macklin, Wendy V. Runyon, Carissa M. Feliciano, Philip H. Dierks, Kaitlin R. Kelly, Hannah L. Watry, Luke M. Judge, Bruce R. Conklin
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
The establishment of well characterized control iPSC lines is essential for robust, reproducible research across laboratories. We used CRISPR/Cas9 to derive an isogeneic control line from a patient-derived iPSC line carrying a mutation in the NEFL gene (E396K). After correction of the E396K mutation, UCSFi003-A (WTD) exhibits multi-lineage differentiation potential, a normal karyotype, no large genomic abnormalities, and has consents for public distribution of cells and genomic data.

Resource Utility

Here, we engineered an isogenic control cell line where the disease-causing mutation has been corrected to the wild type sequence, to serve as a gene-edited control in in vitro disease modeling studies. This line particularly useful for studies that require engineering of one allele, since there are many heterozygous SNPs throughout the genome (data available upon request). This iPSC line has also been consented for wide use by the research community.

Resource details

CMT2E is a genetic progressive neuropathy caused by mutations in the gene NEFL, which currently has no cure. NEFL encodes the neurofilament light chain protein (NF-L) and mutations in NEFL disrupt the proper assembly and function of neurofilaments (Jordanova et al., 2003; Yuan et al., 2017; Zhao et al., 2017). Over 50 CMT-causing mutations have been reported in NEFL, which vary in penetrance and severity. E396K is a dominant, missense mutation with a mild phenotype in iPSC-derived neurons (Dua et al., 2024).

The parental cell line was generated using a non-viral, self-replicating RNA reprogramming vector expressing OCT4, KLF-4, SOX2, GLIS1, and c-MYC in fibroblasts isolated from a patient skin biopsy. The UCSFi003-A cell line was engineered by nucleofecting a synthetic gRNA targeting the mutation sequence, a recombinant HiFi SpCas9, along with a 60 bp template oligo containing the WT sequence including a silent mutation at the 397 residue to aid in genotyping (Fig. 1A).

After nucleofection, corrected iPSCs were enriched and genotyped using the sib-selection (Yuan et al., 2017) method and digital droplet PCR (ddPCR) to isolate a heterozygous monoclonal population (Miyaoka et al., 2014). The desired HDR event was further validated by Sanger sequencing (Fig. 1B). After clonal expansion, iPSCs demonstrated normal morphology (Fig. 1C) and expressed pluripotency markers assed via flow cytometry and immunofluorescence (Figs. 1E and 1F). Cells also exhibited a normal, unmodified karyotype and an identical STR analysis result to the parental cell line (Supplementary Figure).

To assess germline differentiation potential, the Trilineage Differentiation Kit (Stem Cell Technologies) was used to differentiate cells into the endoderm and ectoderm lineages. Mesoderm differentiation potential was confirmed using a small-molecule differentiation protocol (Fig. 1F).

Materials and Methods

Reprogramming

Fibroblasts from the E396K patient were reprogrammed into iPSCs using Stem Cell Technology’s ReproRNA-OKSGM kit (Cat. #05930). Colonies with characteristic iPSC morphology were manually picked and expanded as independent clones (Table 1).

hiPSC culture

iPSCs were cultured on Matrigel (0.337 mg/mL) (Corning)-coated cell culture plates at 37 °C and 5 % CO2, in mTeSR Plus (Stem Cell Technologies) medium, which was changed every other day. Cells were passaged when 60–80 % confluent using Accutase and seeded in media containing 10 uM Y-27632.

CRISPR/Cas9 mutation correction and genotyping

To prepare the ribonucleoprotein (RNP) complex, 240 pmol of gRNA of gRNA (IDT) and 120 pmol HiFi SpCas9 were mixed and incubated at room temperature for 20 min. 1×106 iPSCs were transfected with RNP and the HDR template oligo (IDT) using the P3 Primary Cell 4D-Nucleofector X Kit S (Lonza) with pulse code CA137 (Table 2). After nucleofection, cells were immediately collected in mTeSR Plus media containing 10 uM Y-27632 and seeded at a density of 20 cells per 96 well for enrichment via sib-selection.

To measure the frequency of the desired HDR modification, ddPCR was used. TaqMan SNP discrimination assays were designed using the PrimerExpress3 software and ordered from Thermo Fisher Scientific (Table 2). Once the HDR event was measured at a frequency higher than 10 % by ddPCR, cells were sorted as single cells by FACS. Clones were expanded and correct, heterozygous insertion was verified via Sanger sequencing and ddPCR.

Karyotyping and STR analysis

Cells were seeded at 20–30 % confluence and shipped to WiCell for STR analysis and karyotyping.

Flow cytometry

iPSCs were lifted and singularized using Accutase. For analysis of extracellular markers (Table 2), cells were incubated with conjugated antibodies for 30 min on ice, protected from light. For analysis of intracellular markers (Table 2), cells were fixed, permeabilized, and stained using the Fix and Perm Kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Cells were analyzed using the BD FACS ARIA. After flow cytometry was performed, analysis was performed using the FlowJo software. Cells were analyzed between passage 29 and 33.

Immunofluorescence

Cells were fixed with 4 % PFA, washed twice with PBS, then incubated with 5 % Normal Goat Serum (Thermo Fisher Scientific) at RT for 1 h. After removing blocking buffer and washing twice with PBS, primary antibody (Table 2) was added in 3 % NGS and incubated overnight at 4C. After removing this solution and washing 3 times with PBS, secondary antibodies (Table 2) and DAPI were added in 3 % NGS and incubated for 1 h at RT in the dark.

Trilineage Differentiation

To differentiate the iPSCs into the ectoderm and endoderm germ layers, the Stem Cell Technologies Trilineage Differentiation Kit was used according to the manufacturer’s instructions. To differentiate cells into the mesoderm lineage, iPSCs were seeded in mTeSR +with Y-27632 at 55 k/well in a 24-well plate. When confluent, mesoderm was induced using RPMI with B27 minus insulin and 6uM CHIR. The next day (d1), more RPMI/B27 minus insulin was added on top of the existing media. On day 2, media was replaced with RPMI/B27 minus insulin with 5uM IWP2. Cells we differentiated between passage 29 and 33.

DNA Isolation and genomic analysis

DNA was isolated using the Qiagen DNEasy Blood and Tissues Kit. For sequencing analysis, PCR was performed using DNA oligos from IDT and NEB’s Q5 High-Fidelity 2X Master Mix with the following thermocycling parameters: 1 cycle:98 °C/30 s, 33 cycles: 98 °C/10 s, 50 °C-72 °C (based on primer Tm)/30 s, 72 °C/30 s, 1 cycle: 72 °C.
