32 C.F.R. § 1602.5 — Area office staff.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1602.5
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1602.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1602 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The compensated employees, civilian and military, of the Selective Service System employed in an area office will be referred to as the area office staff.
