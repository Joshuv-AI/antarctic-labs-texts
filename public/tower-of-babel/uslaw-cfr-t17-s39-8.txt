17 C.F.R. § 39.8 — Fraud in connection with the clearing of transactions on a derivatives clearing organization.
Citation: us:cfr:17:39.8
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-39.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 39 (Derivatives Clearing Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be unlawful for any person, directly or indirectly, in or in connection with the clearing of transactions by a derivatives clearing organization:
(a) To cheat or defraud or attempt to cheat or defraud any person;
(b) Willfully to make or cause to be made to any person any false report or statement or cause to be entered for any person any false record; or
(c) Willfully to deceive or attempt to deceive any person by any means whatsoever.
