48 C.F.R. § 733.103-71 — 733.103-71 Filing of protest.

Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-733.103-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 733 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Protests must be in writing and addressed to the contracting officer for consideration by the M/OAA Director.
(b) A protest shall include, in addition to the information required in (48 CFR) FAR 33.103(d)(2), the name of the issuing Mission or office.
(c) Material submitted by a protester will not be withheld from any interested party outside the Government or from any Government agency if the M/OAA Director decides to release such material, except to the extent that the withholding of such information is permitted or required by law or regulation.
