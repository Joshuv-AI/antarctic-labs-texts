14 C.F.R. § 201.3 — Incorporation by reference.
Citation: us:cfr:14:201.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-201.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 201 (Air Carrier Authority Under Subtitle Vii Of Title 49 Of The United States Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Incorporation by reference shall be avoided. However, where two or more applications are filed by a single carrier, lengthy exhibits or other documents attached to one may be incorporated in the others by reference if that procedure will substantially reduce the cost to the applicant.
