48 C.F.R. § 49.105-1 — 49.105-1 Termination status reports.
Citation: us:cfr:48:49.105-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-49.105-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 49 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the TCO and contracting officer are in different activities, the TCO will furnish periodic status reports on termination actions to the contracting office upon request. The contracting office shall specify the information required.
