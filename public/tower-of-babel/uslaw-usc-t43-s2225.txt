43 U.S.C. § 2225 — Reclamation Drought Response Fund
Citation: us:usc:43:2225
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2225
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 40 (Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief); Subchapter II (Drought Contingency Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall undertake a study of the need, if any, to establish a Reclamation Drought Response Fund to be available for defraying those expenses which the Secretary determines necessary to implement plans prepared under section 2222 of this title and to make loans for nonstructural and minor structural activities for the prevention or mitigation of the adverse effects of drought.
