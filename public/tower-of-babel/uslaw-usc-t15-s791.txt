15 U.S.C. § 791 — Congressional declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:15:791
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section791
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 16C (Energy Supply And Environmental Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are (1) to provide for a means to assist in meeting the essential needs of the United States for fuels, in a manner which is consistent, to the fullest extent practicable, with existing national commitments to protect and improve the environment, and (2) to provide requirements for reports respecting energy resources.
