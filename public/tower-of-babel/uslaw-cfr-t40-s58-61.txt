40 C.F.R. § 58.61 — Monitoring other pollutants.
Citation: us:cfr:40:58.61
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-58.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 58 (Ambient Air Quality Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may promulgate criteria similar to that referenced in subpart B of this part for monitoring a pollutant for which an NAAQS does not exist. Such an action would be taken whenever the Administrator determines that a nationwide monitoring program is necessary to monitor such a pollutant.
