41 C.F.R. § 109-38.301-1.53 — -38.301-1.53 Responsibilities of motor vehicle operators.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-38.301-1.53
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-38.301-1.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-38 (38—Motor Equipment Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Designated contractors shall assure that their employees are aware of their responsibilities, identical to those listed in § 109-6.400-50 of this chapter for DOE employees, concerning the use and operation of Government motor vehicles.
