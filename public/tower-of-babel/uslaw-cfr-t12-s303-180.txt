12 C.F.R. § 303.180 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:303.180
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-303.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 303 (Filing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth procedures for complying with application requirements relating to the foreign activities of insured state nonmember banks, U.S. activities of insured branches of foreign banks, and certain foreign mergers of insured depository institutions.
