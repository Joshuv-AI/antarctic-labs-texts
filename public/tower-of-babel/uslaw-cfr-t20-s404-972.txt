20 C.F.R. § 404.972 — Effect of dismissal of request for Appeals Council review.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.972
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.972
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The dismissal of a request for Appeals Council review is binding and not subject to further review.
