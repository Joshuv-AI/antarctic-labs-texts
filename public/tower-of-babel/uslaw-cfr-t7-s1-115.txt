7 C.F.R. § 1.115 — Special procedures: Medical records.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.115
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event an agency receives a request pursuant to § 1.112 for access to medical records (including psychological records) whose disclosure it determines would be harmful to the individual to whom they relate, it may refuse to disclose the records directly to the requester but shall transmit them to a doctor designated by that individual.
