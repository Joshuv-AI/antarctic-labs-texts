49 C.F.R. § 399.211 — Maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:399.211
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-399.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 399 (Employee Safety And Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All steps, handholds, and/or deck plates required by this subpart shall be adequately maintained to serve their intended function.
