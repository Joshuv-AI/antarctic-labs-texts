12 C.F.R. § 361.1 — Why do minority- and women-owned businesses need this outreach regulation?
Citation: us:cfr:12:361.1
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-361.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 361 (Minority And Women Outreach Program Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the FDIC Minority and Women Outreach Program (MWOP) is to ensure that minority- and women-owned businesses (MWOBs) are given the opportunity to participate fully in all contracts entered into by the FDIC.
