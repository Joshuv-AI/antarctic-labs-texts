10 U.S.C. § 8169 — Policy as to leave and liberty
Citation: us:usc:10:8169
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8169
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The commanding officer of a vessel shall favor the faithful and obedient in granting leave and liberty.
