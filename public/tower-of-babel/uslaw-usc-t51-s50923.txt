51 U.S.C. § 50923 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:51:50923
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50923
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 509 (Commercial Space Launch Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall submit to Congress an annual report to accompany the President’s budget request that—
(1)
describes all activities undertaken under this chapter, including a description of the process for the application for and approval of licenses under this chapter and recommendations for legislation that may further commercial launches and reentries; and
(2)
reviews the performance of the regulatory activities and the effectiveness of the Office of Commercial Space Transportation.
