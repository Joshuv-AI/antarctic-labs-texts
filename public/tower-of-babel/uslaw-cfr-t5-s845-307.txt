5 C.F.R. § 845.307 — Burdens of proof.

Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-845.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 845 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Burden of OPM. The Associate Director must establish by the preponderance of the evidence that an overpayment occurred.
(b) Burden of annuitant. The recipient of an overpayment must establish by substantial evidence that he or she is eligible for waiver or an adjustment.
