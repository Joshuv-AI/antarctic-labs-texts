12 C.F.R. § 619.9025 — Agricultural land.
Citation: us:cfr:12:619.9025
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-619.9025
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 619 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Land improved or unimproved which is devoted to or available for the production of crops and other products such as but not limited to fruits and timber or for the raising of livestock.
