20 C.F.R. § 423.7 — Acknowledgment of mailed process.
Citation: us:cfr:20:423.7
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-423.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 423 (Service Of Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Social Security Administration will not provide a receipt or other acknowledgment of process received, except for a return receipt associated with certified mail and where otherwise required by law.
