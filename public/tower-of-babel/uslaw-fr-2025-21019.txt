Idaho Power Company; Notice of Institution of Section 206 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On October 16, 2025, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL26-2-000, pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e, instituting an investigation to determine whether Idaho 
Power Company's market-based rates are considered unjust, unreasonable, 
unduly discriminatory or preferential, or otherwise unlawful. Idaho 
Power Company, 193 FERC ] 61,024 (2025) (Idaho Power).
    As established in Idaho Power, the refund effective date, pursuant 
to section 206(b) of the FPA, is October 16, 2025.
    Any interested person desiring to be heard in Docket No. EL26-2-000 
must file a notice of intervention or motion to intervene, as 
appropriate, with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, in 
accordance with Rule 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure, 18 CFR 385.214 (2025), within 30 days of the date of 
issuance of the order.
