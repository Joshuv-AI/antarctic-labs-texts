34 C.F.R. § 694.10 — What are the requirements that a Partnership must meet in designating a fiscal agent for its project under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:694.10
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-694.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 694 (Gaining Early Awareness And Readiness For Undergraduate Programs (Gear Up)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Although any member of a Partnership may organize the project, a Partnership must designate as the fiscal agent for its project under GEAR UP—
(a) A local educational agency; or
(b) An institution of higher education.
