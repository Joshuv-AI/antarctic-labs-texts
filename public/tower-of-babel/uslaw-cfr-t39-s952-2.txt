39 C.F.R. § 952.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:39:952.2
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-952.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 952 (False Representation And Lottery Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules of practice shall be applicable in all formal proceedings before the Postal Service under 39 U.S.C. 3005, including such cases instituted under prior rules of practice pertaining to these or predecessor statutes, unless timely shown to be prejudicial to Respondent.
