45 C.F.R. § 172.301 — Submission of QHIN application.
Citation: us:cfr:45:172.301
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-172.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 172 (Trusted Exchange Framework And Common Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An entity seeking to be Designated as a QHIN must submit all of the following information in a manner specified by ASTP/ONC:
(a) Completed QHIN application, with supporting documentation, in a form specified by ASTP/ONC; and
(b) A signed copy of the Common Agreement.
