7 C.F.R. § 1.311 — Referral of complaint and answer to the ALJ.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.311
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of an answer, the reviewing official shall send to the ALJ copies of the complaint, proof of service, and the answer.
