36 C.F.R. § 1256.90 — What does this subpart cover?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1256.90
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1256.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1256 (Access To Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains procedures governing the public availability of audiovisual records and other materials subject to 22 U.S.C. 1461(b) that have been transferred to the National Archives of the United States by the United States Information Agency (USIA).
