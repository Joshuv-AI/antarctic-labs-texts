22 U.S.C. § 9810 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:22:9810
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9810
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 105 (Global Fragility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as a declaration of war or an authorization for the use of military force.
