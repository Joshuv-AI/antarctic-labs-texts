30 U.S.C. § 1012 — Waiver, suspension, or reduction of rental or royalty
Citation: us:usc:30:1012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1012
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 23 (Geothermal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may waive, suspend, or reduce the rental or royalty for any lease or portion thereof in the interests of conservation and to encourage the greatest ultimate recovery of geothermal resources, if he determines that this is necessary to promote development or that the lease cannot be successfully operated under the lease terms.
