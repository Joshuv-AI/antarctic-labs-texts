49 C.F.R. § 531.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:531.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-531.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 531 (Passenger Automobile Average Fuel Economy Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to increase the fuel economy of passenger automobiles by establishing minimum levels of average fuel economy for those vehicles.
