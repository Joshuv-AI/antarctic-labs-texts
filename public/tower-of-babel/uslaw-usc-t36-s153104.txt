36 U.S.C. § 153104 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:153104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153104
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1531 (The National Society Of The Daughters Of The American Revolution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its subordinate divisions have the exclusive right to use the name “National Society of the Daughters of the American Revolution”. The corporation has the exclusive right to use and to allow others to use seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
