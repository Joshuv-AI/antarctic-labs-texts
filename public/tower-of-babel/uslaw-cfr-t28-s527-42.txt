28 C.F.R. § 527.42 — Limitations on transfer of offenders to foreign countries.
Citation: us:cfr:28:527.42
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-527.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 527 (Transfers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transfer treaties and conventions, as well as 18 U.S.C. 4100-4115, impose specific requirements that an inmate must satisfy in order to be returned to his or her country of citizenship or nationality.
