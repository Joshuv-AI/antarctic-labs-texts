39 C.F.R. § 3060.14 — Competitive products enterprise statement of allocated assets and liabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3060.14
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3060.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3060 (Accounting Practices And Tax Rules For The Theoretical Competitive Products Enterprise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service will report the assets and liabilities of the theoretical competitive products enterprise as computed under §§ 3060.12 and 3060.13 in the format as prescribed under § 3060.30 for each fiscal year starting with FY 2010.
