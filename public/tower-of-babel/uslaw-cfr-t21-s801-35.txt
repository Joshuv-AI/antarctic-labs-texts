21 C.F.R. § 801.35 — Voluntary labeling of a device with a unique device identifier.
Citation: us:cfr:21:801.35
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-801.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 801 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The labeler of a device that is not required to bear a unique device identifier (UDI) may voluntarily comply with § 801.20. If a labeler voluntarily includes a UDI for a device, the labeler may voluntarily provide information concerning the device under subpart E of part 830 of this chapter.
(b) A device may bear both a Universal Product Code (UPC) and a UDI on its label and packages.
