14 C.F.R. § 91.805 — Final compliance: Subsonic airplanes.
Citation: us:cfr:14:91.805
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-91.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 91 (General Operating And Flight Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in §§ 91.809 and 91.811, on and after January 1, 1985, no person may operate to or from an airport in the United States any subsonic airplane covered by this subpart unless that airplane has been shown to comply with Stage 2 or Stage 3 noise levels under part 36 of this chapter.
