30 C.F.R. § 1202.51 — Scope and definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1202.51
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1202.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1202 (Royalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart is applicable to Federal and Indian (Tribal and allotted) oil and gas leases (except leases on the Osage Indian Reservation, Osage County, Oklahoma) and OCS sulfur leases.
(b) The definitions in § 1206.20 of this chapter are applicable to subparts B, C, D, and J of this part.
