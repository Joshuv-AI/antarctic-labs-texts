36 C.F.R. § 9.112 — What hydrologic standards must I meet?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.112
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must maintain hydrologic connectivity between surface water and groundwater during all operations.
(b) You must not cause measurable degradation of surface water or groundwater.
(c) You must conduct operations in a manner that maintains natural channel and floodplain processes and functions.
