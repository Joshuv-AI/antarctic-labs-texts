40 C.F.R. § 426.20 — Applicability; description of the sheet glass manufacturing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:426.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-426.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 426 (Glass Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges of pollutants resulting from the process in which several mineral ingredients (sand, soda ash, limestone, dolomite, cullen and other ingredients) are mixed, melted in a furnace, and drawn vertically from a melting tank to form sheet glass.
