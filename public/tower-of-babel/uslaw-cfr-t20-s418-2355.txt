20 C.F.R. § 418.2355 — What are the rules for reopening a decision by an ALJ of the Office of Medicare Hearings and Appeals (OMHA) or by the Medicare Appeals Council (MAC)?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.2355
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.2355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in 42 CFR 423.1980 through 423.1986 govern reopenings of decisions by an administrative law judge of the OMHA and decisions by the MAC. A decision by an administrative law judge of the OMHA may be reopened by the administrative law judge or the MAC. A decision by the MAC may be reopened only by the MAC.
