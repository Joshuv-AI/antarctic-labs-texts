41 C.F.R. § 302-9.172 — -9.172 “Authorized point of origin” when a POV, including a replacement POV, is transported to a post of duty subsequent to the time of assignment to that post of duty.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-9.172
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies determine the authorized point of origin within the United States when transporting a POV, including a replacement POV, to a post of duty subsequent to the time of an employee's assignment to that post of duty.
