47 C.F.R. § 18.311 — Methods of measurement.
Citation: us:cfr:47:18.311
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-18.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 18 (Industrial, Scientific, And Medical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The measurement techniques used to determine compliance with the technical requirements of this part are set out in FCC MP-5, “FCC Methods of Measurements of Radio Noise Emissions from Industrial, Scientific, and Medical equipment,” or compliance measurements made in accordance with the specific procedures otherwise authorized by the Commission.
