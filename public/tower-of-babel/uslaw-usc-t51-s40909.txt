51 U.S.C. § 40909 — Compliance with title IX of Education Amendments of 1972
Citation: us:usc:51:40909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40909
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 409 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To comply with title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (20 U.S.C. 1681 et seq.), the Administrator shall conduct compliance reviews of at least 2 grantees annually.
