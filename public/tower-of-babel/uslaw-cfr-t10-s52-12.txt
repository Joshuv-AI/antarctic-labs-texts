10 C.F.R. § 52.12 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:10:52.12
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-52.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 52 (Licenses, Certifications, And Approvals For Nuclear Power Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets out the requirements and procedures applicable to Commission issuance of an early site permit for approval of a site for one or more nuclear power facilities separate from the filing of an application for a construction permit or combined license for the facility.
