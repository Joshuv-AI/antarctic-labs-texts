14 C.F.R. § 161.111 — Availability of data and comments on a restriction implemented pursuant to an agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:14:161.111
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-161.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 161 (Notice And Approval Of Airport Noise And Access Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The airport operator shall retain all relevant supporting data and all comments relating to a restriction implemented pursuant to an agreement for as long as the restriction is in effect. The airport operator shall make these materials available for inspection upon request by the FAA. The information shall be made available for inspection by any person during the pendency of any petition for reevaluation found justified by the FAA.
