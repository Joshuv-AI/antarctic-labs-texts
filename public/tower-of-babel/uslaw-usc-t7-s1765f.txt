7 U.S.C. § 1765f — Availability of agency services, personnel, and facilities
Citation: us:usc:7:1765f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1765f
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter II (United States Agricultural Trade Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the request of the Secretary of Agriculture, each Federal agency may make its services, personnel, and facilities available to a United States Agricultural Trade Office in the performance of its functions. The Secretary of Agriculture may reimburse or advance funds to any such agency for services, personnel, and facilities so made available.
