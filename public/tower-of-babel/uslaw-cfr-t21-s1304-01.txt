21 C.F.R. § 1304.01 — Scope of part 1304.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1304.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1304.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1304 (Records And Reports Of Registrants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inventory and other records and reports required under section 307, section 311, or section 1008(e) of the Act (21 U.S.C. 827, 831, and 958(e)) shall be in accordance with, and contain the information required by, those sections and by the sections of this part.
