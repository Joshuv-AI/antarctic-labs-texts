24 C.F.R. § 576.406 — Equal participation of faith-based organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:576.406
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Community Planning And Development, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-576.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 576 (Emergency Solutions Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HUD program requirements in § 5.109 of this title apply to the ESG program, including the requirements regarding disposition and change in use of real property by a faith-based organization.
