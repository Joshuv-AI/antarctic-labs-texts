Correction: Stability and suitability of housekeeping genes in phlebotomine sand flies
Authors: Fabiana Sassù, Barbora Vomáčková Kykalová, Cecilia Stahl Vieira, Petr Volf, Erich Loza Telleria
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-74776-9, published online 07 October 2024

The original version of this Article contained errors in Table 8, where the second primer sequence listed for the L. longipalpis RiboL32 gene was duplicated from the first primer. As the result, the second primer sequence,

“GACCGATATGCCAAGCTAAAGCA”

now reads:

“GGGGAGCATGTGGCGTGTCTT”.

Additionally, the associated reference now reads: “adapted from Meireles-Filho et al. 200670”.

The original Table 8 and accompanying legend appear below.
