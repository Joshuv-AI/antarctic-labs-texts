46 C.F.R. § 131.110 — Notice and records.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.110
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must meet the requirements of part 4 of this chapter for reporting marine casualties and retaining voyage records.
