20 C.F.R. § 901.48 — Proposed findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:20:901.48
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-901.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 901 (Regulations Governing The Performance Of Actuarial Services Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in cases where the respondent has failed to answer the complaint or where a party has failed to appear at the hearing, the Administrative Law Judge, before making his/her decision, shall give the parties a reasonable opportunity to submit proposed findings and conclusions and supporting reasons therefor.
