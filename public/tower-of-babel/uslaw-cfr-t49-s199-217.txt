49 C.F.R. § 199.217 — On-duty use.
Citation: us:cfr:49:199.217
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-199.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 199 (Drug And Alcohol Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each operator shall prohibit a covered employee from using alcohol while performing covered functions. No operator having actual knowledge that a covered employee is using alcohol while performing covered functions shall permit the employee to perform or continue to perform covered functions.
