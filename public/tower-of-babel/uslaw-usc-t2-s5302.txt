2 U.S.C. § 5302 — Salaries payable monthly after taking oath
Citation: us:usc:2:5302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5302
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter I (Member Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Member and Delegate, after he has taken and subscribed the required oath, is entitled to receive his salary at the end of each month.
