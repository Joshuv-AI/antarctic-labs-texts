18 U.S.C. § 3235 — Venue in capital cases
Citation: us:usc:18:3235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3235
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 211 (Jurisdiction And Venue). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The trial of offenses punishable with death shall be had in the county where the offense was committed, where that can be done without great inconvenience.
