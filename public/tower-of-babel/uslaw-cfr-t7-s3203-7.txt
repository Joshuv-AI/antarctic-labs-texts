7 C.F.R. § 3203.7 — Title.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3203 (Guidelines For The Transfer Of Excess Computers Or Other Technical Equipment Pursuant To Section 14220 Of The 2008 Farm Bill). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Title of ownership to excess computers or other technical equipment transferred under this part shall automatically pass to the recipient once the transferring agency and recipient or designated organization sign the transfer form indicating that the designated organization has received the property.
