Correction: The impact of perceived adolescent academic burnout on parental acceptance and empathic behavior: a chain mediation model
Authors: Jinrui Tian, Ronghua Zhang
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Psychology 13, 972 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03166-1

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified errors in several references. The incorrect and correct references are listed below:

Reference 1 should be deleted, and the corresponding in-text citation should be changed to Reference 12.

Incorrect reference 4 was: Bilige S, Gan YQ. Academic burnout and psychological maladjustment among Chinese adolescents: A moderated mediation model. Curr Psychol. 2020;39:1525–34.

Correct reference 4 is: Walburg V. Burnout among high school students: a literature review. Child Youth Serv Rev. 2014;42:28–33. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.childyouth.2014.03.020.

Incorrect reference 8 was: Chen XL, Li J, Wang HY. (2020). A national survey on academic burnout among Chinese adolescents. Chin J Clin Psychol. 28(2):253–258.

Correct reference 8 is: Chen G, Chen JQ, editors. 中国关心下一代研究报告(2020) [Annual report on the care of the next generation in China (2020)]. Beijing: Social Sciences Academic Press; 2020. (in Chinese).

Incorrect reference 55 was: Wu Q, Xu W, Wang Y. Parental education anxiety and adolescent academic burnout: the mediating role of parental burnout and the moderating role of family function. J Adolesc. 2022;96:63–75.

Correct reference 55 is: Wu K, Wang F, Wang W, Li Y. Parents’ education anxiety and children’s academic burnout: the role of parental burnout and family function. Front Psychol. 2022;12:764824. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.764824.

Incorrect reference 57 was: Zhang J, Gan Y, Cham H. Perfectionism, academic burnout, and engagement among Chinese college students: a structural equation modeling analysis. Pers Individ Differ. 2013;53(4):486–91.

Correct reference 57 is: Zhang X, Klassen RM, Wang Y. Academic burnout and motivation of Chinese secondary students. Int J Soc Sci Humanit. 2013;3(2):134–138. https://doi.org/10.7763/IJSSH.2013.V3.212.

Incorrect reference 58 was: Zimmer-Gembeck et al.Parent emotion regulation, parenting practices, and child adjustment: an integrative review and meta-analysis. Dev Rev. 2022;64:101072.

Correct reference 58 is: Zimmer-Gembeck MJ, Rudolph J, Kerin J, Bohadana-Brown G. Parent emotional regulation: a meta-analytic review of its association with parenting and child adjustment. Int J Behav Dev. 2022;46(1):63–82. https://doi.org/10.1177/01650254211051086.
