25 C.F.R. § 213.21 — Rate of rents on leases other than oil and gas.
Citation: us:cfr:25:213.21
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-213.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 213 (Leasing Of Restricted Lands Of Members Of Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma, For Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On all mineral leases of allotted lands other than oil and gas leases, rental shall be paid annually in advance from the date of approval of the lease, as follows: Fifty cents per acre for the first year, 75 cents per acre for the second year, and $1 per acre for the third and each succeeding year of the term of the lease.
