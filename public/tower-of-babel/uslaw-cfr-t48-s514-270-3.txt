48 C.F.R. § 514.270-3 — 514.270-3 Evaluation factors for award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:514.270-3
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-514.270-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 514 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The solicitation should clearly state the basis for evaluating bids for aggregate award, require bidders to submit a price on each item within the group or a percentage to be added or subtracted from a list price, and advise bidders that failure to submit prices as required within a group makes a bid ineligible for award for that group.
