42 U.S.C. § 7381e — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:7381e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7381e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XIII (Department Of Energy Science Education Programs); Part A (Science Education Enhancement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Out of funds authorized to be appropriated for the Office of Science of the Department of Energy in a fiscal year, there are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for carrying out university research support and other science, mathematics, and engineering education programs authorized by this part and administered by the Office of Science of the Department of Energy, $40,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2023 through 2027.
