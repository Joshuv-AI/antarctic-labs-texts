14 C.F.R. § 73.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:73.1
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-73.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 73 (Special Use Airspace). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The airspace that is described in subpart B and subpart C of this part is designated as special use airspace. These parts prescribe the requirements for the use of that airspace.
