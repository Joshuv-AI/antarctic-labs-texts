30 U.S.C. § 940 — Applicability of amendments to part B of this subchapter to this part
Citation: us:usc:30:940
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section940
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter IV (Black Lung Benefits); Part C (Claims For Benefits After December 31, 1973). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The amendments made by the Black Lung Benefits Act of 1972, the Black Lung Benefits Reform Act of 1977 and the Black Lung Benefits Amendments of 1981 to part B of this subchapter shall, to the extent appropriate, also apply to this part.
