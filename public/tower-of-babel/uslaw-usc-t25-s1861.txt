25 U.S.C. § 1861 — Definition of tribally controlled postsecondary career and technical institution
Citation: us:usc:25:1861
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1861
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 20 (Tribally Controlled Colleges And Universities Assistance); Subchapter IV (Tribally Controlled Postsecondary Career And Technical Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “tribally controlled postsecondary career and technical institution” has the meaning given the term in section 2302 of title 20.
