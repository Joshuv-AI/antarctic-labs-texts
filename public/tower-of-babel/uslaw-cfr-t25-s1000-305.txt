25 C.F.R. § 1000.305 — Are there grants available to assist Tribes/Consortia to meet the requirements to participate in self-governance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.305
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, any Tribe/Consortium may apply, as provided in § 1000.315, for a grant to assist it to:
(a) Plan to participate in self-governance; and
(b) Negotiate the terms of the compact and funding agreement between the Tribe/Consortium and the Secretary.
