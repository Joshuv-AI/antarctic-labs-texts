50 C.F.R. § 550.151 — Program accessibility: New construction and alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:50:550.151
Creator: Marine Mammal Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-550.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 550 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By Marine Mammal Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each building or part of a building that is constructed or altered by, on behalf of, or for the use of the agency shall be designed, constructed, or altered so as to be readily accessible to and usable by handicapped persons. The definitions, requirements, and standards of the Architectural Barriers Act (42 U.S.C. 4151-4157), as established in 41 CFR 101-19.600 to 101-19.607, apply to buildings covered by this section.
