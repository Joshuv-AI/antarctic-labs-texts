7 C.F.R. § 160.26 — Withdrawal of request.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.26
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for service under the provisions in this part may be withdrawn at any time before the service has been completed, on notice to the official inspector: Provided, however, That the interested person shall reimburse the United States for the time spent and any expenses incurred prior to receipt of such withdrawal notice.
