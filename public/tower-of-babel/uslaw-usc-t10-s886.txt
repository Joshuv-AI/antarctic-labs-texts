10 U.S.C. § 886 — Art. 86. Absence without leave
Citation: us:usc:10:886
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section886
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any member of the armed forces who, without authority—
(1)
fails to go to his appointed place of duty at the time prescribed;
(2)
goes from that place; or
(3)
absents himself or remains absent from his unit, organization, or place of duty at which he is required to be at the time prescribed;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
