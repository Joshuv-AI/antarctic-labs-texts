34 C.F.R. § 655.32 — What additional factors does the Secretary consider in making grant awards?
Citation: us:cfr:34:655.32
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-655.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 655 (International Education Programs—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for 34 CFR parts 656, 657, and 661, to the extent practicable and consistent with the criterion of excellence, the Secretary seeks to achieve an equitable distribution of funds throughout the Nation.
