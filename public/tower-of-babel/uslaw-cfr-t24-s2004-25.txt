24 C.F.R. § 2004.25 — Final determination.
Citation: us:cfr:24:2004.25
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-2004.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 2004 (Subpoenas And Production In Response To Subpoenas Or Demands Of Courts Or Other Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Counsel makes the final determination on demands and requests to employees for production of official records and information or testimony. All final determinations are within the sole discretion of the Counsel. The Counsel will notify the requester of the final determination, the reasons for the grant or denial of the demand or request, and any conditions that the Counsel may impose on the release of records or information, or on the testimony of an OIG employee.
