9 C.F.R. § 77.16 — Interstate movement from nonaccredited States and zones.
Citation: us:cfr:9:77.16
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-77.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 77 (Tuberculosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cattle or bison that originate in a nonaccredited State or zone, and that are not known to be infected with or exposed to tuberculosis, may be moved interstate only if the cattle or bison are accompanied by VS Form 1-27 and are moved interstate for slaughter in an officially sealed means of conveyance directly to a recognized slaughtering establishment.
