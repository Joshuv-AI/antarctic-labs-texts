7 U.S.C. § 7450 — Suspension or termination
Citation: us:usc:7:7450
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7450
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 101 (Agricultural Promotion); Subchapter III (Canola And Rapeseed). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, whenever the Secretary finds that an order or a provision of an order obstructs or does not tend to effectuate the declared policy of this subchapter, suspend or terminate the operation of the order or provision. The suspension or termination of an order shall not be considered an order within the meaning of this subchapter.
