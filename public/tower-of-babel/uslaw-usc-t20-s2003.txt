20 U.S.C. § 2003 — Other Federal memorials prohibited
Citation: us:usc:20:2003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2003
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 42 (Harry S Truman Memorial Scholarships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Harry S Truman Scholarship Program as authorized by this chapter shall be the sole Federal memorial to President Harry S Truman.
