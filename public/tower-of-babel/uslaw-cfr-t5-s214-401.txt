5 C.F.R. § 214.401 — Types of positions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:214.401
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-214.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 214 (Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are two types of positions in the Senior Executive Service:
(a) General positions, which may be filled by a career, noncareer, limited emergency, or limited term appointee.
(b) Career reserved positions, which may be filled only by a career appointee.
