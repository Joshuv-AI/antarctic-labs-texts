2 C.F.R. § 180.135 — May a Federal agency grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.135
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A Federal agency head or designee may grant an exception permitting an excluded person to participate in a particular covered transaction. If the Federal agency head or designee grants an exception, the exception must be in writing and state the reason(s) for deviating from the government-wide policy in Executive Order 12549.
(b) An exception granted by one Federal agency for an excluded person does not extend to the covered transactions of another Federal agency.
