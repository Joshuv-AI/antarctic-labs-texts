45 C.F.R. § 81.103 — Exceptions to initial or recommended decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:81.103
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-81.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 81 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 80 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 20 days after the mailing of an initial or recommended decision, any party may file exceptions to the decision, stating reasons therefor, with the reviewing authority. Any other party may file a response thereto within 30 days after the mailing of the decision. Upon the filing of such exceptions, the reviewing authority shall review the decision and issue its own decision thereon.
