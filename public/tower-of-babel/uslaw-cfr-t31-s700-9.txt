31 C.F.R. § 700.9 — Photographs for news, advertising, or commercial purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:31:700.9
Creator: Federal Law Enforcement Training Center, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-700.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 700 (Regulations Governing Conduct In Or On The Federal Law Enforcement Training Center (Fletc) Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Photographs for news, advertising, or commercial purposes may be taken on the property only with the prior permission of the Director. Taking photographs of a student is prohibited without the consent of the student.
