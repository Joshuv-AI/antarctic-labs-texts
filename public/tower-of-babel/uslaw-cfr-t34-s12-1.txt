34 C.F.R. § 12.1 — What is the scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:34:12.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-12.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Educational Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is applicable to surplus Federal real property located within any State that is appropriate for assignment to, or that has been assigned to, the Secretary by the Administrator for transfer for educational purposes, as provided for in section 203(k) of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, as amended, 63 Stat. 377 (40 U.S.C. 471 et seq.).
