16 C.F.R. § 313.16 — Protection of Fair Credit Reporting Act.
Citation: us:cfr:16:313.16
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-313.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 313 (Privacy Of Consumer Financial Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to modify, limit, or supersede the operation of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. 1681 et seq.), and no inference shall be drawn on the basis of the provisions of this part regarding whether information is transaction or experience information under section 603 of that Act.
