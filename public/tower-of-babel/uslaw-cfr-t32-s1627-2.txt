32 C.F.R. § 1627.2 — Registration of volunteers.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1627.2
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1627.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1627 (Volunteers For Induction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a person who is required to be registered but who has failed to register volunteers for induction, he shall be registered.
(b) In registering a volunteer, the area office shall follow the procedure set forth in § 1615.3 of this chapter.
