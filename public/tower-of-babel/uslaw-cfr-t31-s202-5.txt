31 C.F.R. § 202.5 — Previously designated depositaries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:202.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-202.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 202 (Depositaries And Financial Agents Of The Federal Government 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A depositary previously designated will, by the acceptance or retention of deposits, be presumed to have assented to all the terms and provisions of this part and to the retention of collateral security theretofore pledged.
