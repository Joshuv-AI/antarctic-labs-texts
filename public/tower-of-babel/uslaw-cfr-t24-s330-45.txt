24 C.F.R. § 330.45 — Limitation on GNMA liability.
Citation: us:cfr:24:330.45
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-330.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 330 (Guaranty Of Multiclass Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for its guaranty, the Association undertakes no obligation and assumes no liability to any person with regard to or on account of the existence or operation of this part or the conduct of any participants in the Multiclass Securities program.
