49 C.F.R. § 581.3 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:49:581.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-581.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 581 (Bumper Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This standard applies to passenger motor vehicles other than multipurpose passenger vehicles and low-speed vehicles as defined in 49 CFR part 571.3(b).
