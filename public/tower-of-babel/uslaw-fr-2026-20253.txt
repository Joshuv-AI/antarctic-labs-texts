DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-49-2026]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 75; Authorization of Production 
Activity; Essai, Inc.; (Semiconductor Test Equipment); Chandler, 
Arizona

    On May 15, 2026, Essai, Inc. submitted a notification of proposed 
production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility within FTZ 75 in 
Chandler, Arizona.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (91 FR 30275, May 22, 2026). On 
September 28, 2026, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that no further review of the activity is warranted at this 
time. The production activity described in the notification was 
authorized, subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, 
including Sec.  400.14.

    Dated: September 28, 2026.
Colin Agostisi,
Executive Secretary.
