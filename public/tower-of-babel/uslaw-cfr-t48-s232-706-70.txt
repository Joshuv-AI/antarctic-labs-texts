48 C.F.R. § 232.706-70 — 232.706-70 Clause for limitation of Government's obligation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:232.706-70
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-232.706-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 232 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.232-7007, Limitation of Government's Obligation, in solicitations and resultant incrementally funded fixed-price contracts. The contracting officer may revise the contractor's notification period, in paragraph (c) of the clause, from “ninety” to “thirty” or “sixty” days, as appropriate.
