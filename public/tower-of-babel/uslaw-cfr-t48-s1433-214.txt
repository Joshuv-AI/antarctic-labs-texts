48 C.F.R. § 1433.214 — 1433.214 Alternative dispute resolution (ADR).
Citation: us:cfr:48:1433.214
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1433.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1433 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOI strongly encourages the use of ADR in the resolution of disputes in lieu of litigation or adjudication. Efforts shall be made to resolve disputes in an expeditious and financially responsible manner.
