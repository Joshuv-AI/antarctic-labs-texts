46 C.F.R. § 142.105 — Applicability and delayed implementation for existing vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:142.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-142.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 142 (Fire Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all towing vessels subject to this subchapter.
(a) An existing towing vessel must comply with the requirements in this part no later than either July 20, 2018 or the date the vessel obtains a Certificate of Inspection (COI), whichever date is earlier.
(b) The delayed implementation provisions in paragraph (a) of this section do not apply to a new towing vessel.
