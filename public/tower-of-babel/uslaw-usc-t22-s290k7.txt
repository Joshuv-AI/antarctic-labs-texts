22 U.S.C. § 290k–7 — Federal Reserve banks as depositories
Citation: us:usc:22:290k–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290k–7
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXVI (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any Federal Reserve bank that is requested to do so by the Agency shall act as its depository or as its fiscal agent, and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System shall supervise and direct the carrying out of these functions by the Federal Reserve banks.
