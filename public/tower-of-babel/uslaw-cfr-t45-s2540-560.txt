45 C.F.R. § 2540.560 — How do I renew authority to use a national service insignia?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2540.560
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2540.560
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2540 (General Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for renewed authority to use an insignia must follow the procedures for initial approval as set out in § 2540.540.
