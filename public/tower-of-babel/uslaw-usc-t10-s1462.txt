10 U.S.C. § 1462 — Assets of Fund
Citation: us:usc:10:1462
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1462
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 74 (Department Of Defense Military Retirement Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be deposited into the Fund the following, which shall constitute the assets of the Fund:
(1)
Amounts paid into the Fund under section 1466 of this title.
(2)
Any amount appropriated to the Fund.
(3)
Any return on investment of the assets of the Fund.
