48 C.F.R. § 16.206-1 — 16.206-1 Description.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 16 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fixed-ceiling-price contract with retroactive price redetermination provides for (a) a fixed ceiling price and (b) retroactive price redetermination within the ceiling after completion of the contract.
