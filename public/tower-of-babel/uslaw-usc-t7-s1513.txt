7 U.S.C. § 1513 — Books of account and annual reports of Corporation
Citation: us:usc:7:1513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1513
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 36 (Crop Insurance); Subchapter I (Federal Crop Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Corporation shall at all times maintain complete and accurate books of accounts and shall file annually with the Secretary a complete report as to the business of the Corporation.
