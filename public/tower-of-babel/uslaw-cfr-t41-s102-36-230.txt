41 C.F.R. § 102-36.230 — -36.230 Gift disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.230
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your agency has gift retention authority, you may retain gifts from the public. Otherwise, you must report gifts you receive on an SF 120 to GSA.
