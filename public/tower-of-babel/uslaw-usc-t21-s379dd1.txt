21 U.S.C. § 379dd–1 — Location of Foundation
Citation: us:usc:21:379dd–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section379dd–1
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter VII (General Authority); Part I (Reagan-Udall Foundation For The Food And Drug Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Foundation shall, if practicable, be located not more than 20 miles from the District of Columbia.
