45 C.F.R. § 1302.94 — Volunteers.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1302.94
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1302.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1302 (Program Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A program must ensure volunteers have been screened for appropriate communicable diseases in accordance with State, Tribal, or local laws. In the absence of State, Tribal, or local law, the Health and Mental Health Services Advisory Committee must be consulted regarding the need for such screenings.
(b) A program must ensure children are never left alone with volunteers.
