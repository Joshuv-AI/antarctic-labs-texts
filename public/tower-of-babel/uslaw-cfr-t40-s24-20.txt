40 C.F.R. § 24.20 — Final agency action.
Citation: us:cfr:40:24.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-24.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 24 (Rules Governing Issuance Of And Administrative Hearings On Interim Status Corrective Action Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The final decision and the final administrative order are final agency actions that are effective on filing and service. These actions are not appealable to the Administrator.
