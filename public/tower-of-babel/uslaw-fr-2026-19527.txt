Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 84, Notification of Proposed Production 
Activity; Enerflex Energy Systems Inc.; (Natural Gas Equipment); 
Houston, Texas

    Enerflex Energy Systems Inc. submitted a notification of proposed 
production activity to the FTZ Board (the Board) for its facility in 
Houston, Texas within FTZ 84. The notification conforming to the 
requirements of the Board's regulations (15 CFR 400.22) was received on 
August 27, 2026.
    Pursuant to 15 CFR 400.14(b), FTZ production activity would be 
limited to the specific foreign-status material(s)/component(s) and 
specific finished product(s) described in the submitted notification 
(summarized below) and subsequently authorized by the Board. The 
benefits that may stem from conducting production activity under FTZ 
procedures are explained in the background section of the Board's 
website--accessible via www.trade.gov/ftz.
    The proposed finished products include: steel base frames; various 
tanks (lube oil; water); cargo lifting slings; various assemblies 
(cargo lifting shackle; blower fan; natural gas compression; skid 
stabilizing steel outrigger); various skids (screw compression; amine 
pump; refrigeration screw compression; demethenizer valve; CO2 
liquefaction; dew point control; TEG dehydration; amine exchanger; 
amine filter; demethenizer; dehydration switch valve; dehydration 
regeneration); various compressors (centrifugal; skid-mounted gas); 
skid-mounted gas processors; carbon steel exhaust pipes; skid gratings; 
skid platforms; steel angle brace sets; expansion joints; natural gas 
distillers; natural gas rectifiers; natural gas condensate stabilizers; 
brazed aluminum heat exchangers; air coolers; steel U-bend tubes; 
natural gas cryogenic treatment systems; amine gas sweeteners; 
refrigerant reclaimers; spreader beams; and, power generators (duty 
rate ranges from duty-free to 4.2%).
    The proposed foreign-status materials/components include: plastic 
gasket cover plates; rubber O-rings; various pumps (lubrication; 
centrifugal liquid); exfoliated vermiculite insulation materials; 
various tubing (stainless steel; steel); carbon steel pipe sections; 
steel stud bolts with nuts; structural steel beams; galvanized steel 
bolts with nuts and washers; steel bolts; fall arrestors for high-
elevation safety attachments; guidelocks rail clamps; mechanical safety 
stop mechanisms; screw compressors; amine still reboilers; various 
exchangers (heat; treated gas); various coolers (air; butane product 
water; ethane); gas chillers; stabilizer reboilers; electric cabling 
trays; inlet gas filter coalescers; various filters (industrial dust; 
air); pressure vessels; pressure regulators; various valves (swing 
check; piston check; control; floating ball; trunnion ball; globe; 
gate; hand-operated; ball; butterfly); various gauges (level; 
temperature; pressure); mesh strainers; gaskets; various motors 
(electric; induction); gas-driven engines; industrial battery packs; 
natural gas heaters; various transmitters (moisture; temperature; 
pressure; level; differential pressure); thermowells; control panels; 
sample gas probes; and, level switches (duty rate ranges from duty-free 
to 5.6%).
    The request indicates that certain materials/components are subject 
to duties under section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, or 
section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, depending on the country of 
origin. The applicable section 232, and section 301 decisions require 
subject merchandise to be admitted to FTZs in privileged foreign status 
as described in 19 CFR 146.41.

2026.
