49 C.F.R. § 40.283 — How does a certification organization obtain recognition for its members as SAPs?
Citation: us:cfr:49:40.283
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-40.283
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 40 (Procedures For Transportation Workplace Drug And Alcohol Testing Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you represent a certification organization that wants DOT to authorize its certified drug and alcohol counselors to be added to § 40.281(a)(6), you may submit a written petition to DOT requesting a review of your petition for inclusion.
(b) You must obtain the National Commission for Certifying Agencies (NCCA) accreditation before DOT will act on your petition.
(c) You must also meet the minimum requirements of appendix G to this part before DOT will act on your petition.
