41 C.F.R. § 304-6.6 — -6.6 Reports for public inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-6.6
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-6.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-6 (6—Payment Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OGE must make any report filed by an agency under this part (that is not protected from disclosure by statute) available for public inspection and copying on the later of the following two dates:
(a) Within 30 days after the applicable due date.
(b) Within 30 days after the date OGE actually receives the report.
