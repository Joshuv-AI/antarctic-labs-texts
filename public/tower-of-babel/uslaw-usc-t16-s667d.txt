16 U.S.C. § 667d — Reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:16:667d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section667d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5A (Protection And Conservation Of Wildlife); Subchapter I (Game, Fur-Bearing Animals, And Fish). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A statement of the acreage and value of such property as may have been transferred pursuant to sections 667b to 667d of this title during the preceding fiscal year shall be annually prepared by the Administrator of General Services.
