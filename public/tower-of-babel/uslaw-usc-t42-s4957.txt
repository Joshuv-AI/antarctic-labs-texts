42 U.S.C. § 4957 — Participation of younger and older persons
Citation: us:usc:42:4957
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4957
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter I (National Volunteer Antipoverty Programs); Part A (Volunteers In Service To America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this part and part C, the Director shall take necessary steps, including the development of special projects, where appropriate, to encourage the fullest participation of individuals 18 through 27 years of age, and individuals 55 years of age and older, in the various programs and activities authorized under such parts.
