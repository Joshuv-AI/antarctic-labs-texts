42 U.S.C. § 285g–7 — Child health research centers
Citation: us:usc:42:285g–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285g–7
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 7 (Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute Of Child Health And Human Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Institute shall develop and support centers for conducting research with respect to child health. Such centers shall give priority to the expeditious transfer of advances from basic science to clinical applications and improving the care of infants and children.
