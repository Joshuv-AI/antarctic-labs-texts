Enumeration Algorithm for Genus-4 Superspecial Hyperelliptic Curves with Automorphism Group Q 8 Q_{8}


Abstract
In this study, we enumerate the class containing the quaternion group Q 8 Q_{8} , which was left out in previous research. It is known that hyperelliptic curves in this class are isomorphic to H a : y 2 = x ⁡ ( x 4 − 1 ) ​ ( x 4 − a ​ x 2 + 1 ) H_{a}:y^{2}=x(x^{4}-1)(x^{4}-ax^{2}+1) . Traditional methods required fixing one of the two hyperelliptic curves H a 1 H_{a_{1}} and H a 2 H_{a_{2}} and performing exhaustive isomorphism checks. One of the contributions of this study is the development of a method that enumerates isomorphism classes of H a H_{a} using only the parameter a a , without fixing one curve. As a result, we propose an algorithm to enumerate the number of isomorphism classes and representatives of hyperelliptic curves H a H_{a} with automorphism group containing the quaternion group Q 8 Q_{8} . Furthermore, we implemented the proposed method for each characteristic 5 < p < 10000 5<p<10000 , finding that there are no isomorphism classes for p ≡ 3 , 5 ( mod 8 ) p\equiv 3,5\pmod{8} , and the number of isomorphism classes for p ≡ 1 , 7 ( mod 8 ) p\equiv 1,7\pmod{8} is [ p / 48 ] [p/48] .
