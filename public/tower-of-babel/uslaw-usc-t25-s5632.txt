25 U.S.C. § 5632 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:25:5632
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 48 (Indian Trust Asset Reform); Subchapter III (Improving Efficiency And Streamlining Processes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) BIA
The term “BIA” means the Bureau of Indian Affairs.
(2) Department
The term “Department” means the Department of the Interior.
(3) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
(4) Under Secretary
The term “Under Secretary” means the Under Secretary for Indian Affairs established under section 5633(a) of this title.
