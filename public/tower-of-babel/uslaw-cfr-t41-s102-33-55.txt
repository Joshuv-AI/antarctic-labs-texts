41 C.F.R. § 102-33.55 — -33.55 Minimum requirements for CAS contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.55
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At a minimum, your CAS contracts and agreements must require that any provider of CAS comply with—
(a) Civil standards in 14 CFR that are applicable to the type of operations you are asking the contractor to conduct;
(b) Applicable military standards; or
(c) Your agency's Flight Program Standards.
