MOUNT SORREL, a market town in the Loughborough (Mid)
parliamentary division of Leicestershire, on the river Soar, 7 m.
N. of Leicester. Pop. (1901), 2417. The Sileby station on the
Midland main line lies 12 m. E. The position is beautiful,
a steep hill, once crowned by a castle, rising above the well-wooded
valley. At Barrow-upon-Soar, 22 m. N. (pop. 2409;
Barrow and Quorn railway station), lime is worked extensively.
The village of Quorndon or Quorn, 12 m. N.W., is the headquarters
of the well-known Quorn hunt. Quorndon is an urban
district (pop. 2173).
