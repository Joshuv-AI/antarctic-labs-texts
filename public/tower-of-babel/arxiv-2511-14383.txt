2.

In this section we discuss some preliminary facts that we need.

2.1
.

Let
\psi\colon R\rightarrow S
be a homomorphism of Noetherian rings. The André-Quillen homology
D_{n}(S|R,N)
of the
R
-algebra
S
with coefficients in an
S
-module
N
is the
n^{th}
homology module of
L(S|R)\otimes_{S}N
, where
L(S|R)
is the cotangent complex of
\psi
, uniquely defined in the derived category of
S
-modules
D(S)
, see
[
1
]
and
[
18
]
.
We follow the exposition in
[
9
]
. For
n=0,1,2
a nice treatment has been given in
[
10
]
. If
R
and
S
are (
\mathbb{Z}
)-graded and
\psi
is degree preserving then
L(S|R)
is a complex of graded
S
-modules and if
N
is a graded
S
-module then
D_{n}(S|R,N)
is a graded
S
-module for all
n\geq 0
.

2.2
.

If
\psi\colon R\rightarrow S
is essentially of finite type then
L(S|R)
is homotopic to a complex

\cdots\rightarrow L_{n}\rightarrow L_{n-1}\rightarrow\cdots\rightarrow L_{1}\rightarrow L_{0}\rightarrow 0,

where each
L_{i}
is a finitely generated free
S
-module, see
[
9
, 6.11]
.
So if
N
is a finitely generated
S
-module then so is
D_{n}(S|R,N)
for all
n\geq 0
.

2.3
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a Noetherian local ring. Let
T=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}T_{n}
be a
\mathbb{Z}
-graded ring with
T_{0}=A
. Assume
\mathfrak{n}=(\bigoplus_{n\leq-1}T_{n})\oplus\mathfrak{m}\oplus(\bigoplus_{n\geq 1}T_{n})
is a maximal ideal of
T
. Let
I
be a graded ideal of
T
. If

is Cohen-Macaulay then all non-zero Koszul homology modules of

have Krull dimension equal to
\dim T/I
. This is proved for local rings in
[
19
, 4.2.2]
. The same proof works for *-local rings.

2.4
.

(with hypotheses as in
2.3
). Assume
K=(u_{1},\ldots,u_{s})
where

are homogeneous. Let
M
be a graded
T/K
-module. (Here

need not be Cohen-Macaulay). Then there exists an exact sequence

\displaystyle 0\rightarrow D_{2}(T/K|T,M)

\displaystyle\rightarrow H_{1}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{s})\otimes_{T/K}M

\displaystyle\rightarrow(T/K)^{s}\otimes_{T/K}M\rightarrow K/K^{2}\otimes_{T/K}M\rightarrow 0.

3.

In this section we first prove:

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a Noetherian local ring and let
I\subseteq\mathfrak{m}
be an ideal of
A
. Let
M
be a finitely generated
A
-module with an
I
-stable filtration
\mathcal{F}=\{\mathcal{F}_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
. Let
\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M)=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{F}_{n}
be the extended Rees module of
M
with respect to
\mathcal{F}
. Then for
j\geq 1
there exists positive integers
s_{j}
(depending on
j
) such that

t^{-s_{j}}D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)|A,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M))=0.

In particular
D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)|A,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M))_{n}=0
for
n\ll 0
.

Proof.

Fix
j\geq 1
. We note that
D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)|A,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M))
is a finitely generated graded
\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)
-module. So it suffices to show
D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)|A,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M))_{t^{-1}}=0
.
We have an exact sequence of
\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)
-modules

0\rightarrow\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M)\rightarrow M[t,t^{-1}]\rightarrow L_{\mathcal{F}}(M)=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}M/\mathcal{F}_{n}\rightarrow 0.

We note that
L_{\mathcal{F}}(M)_{n}=0
for
n\ll 0
. In particular
L_{\mathcal{F}}(M)_{t^{-1}}=0
. So

\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M)_{t^{-1}}\cong M[t,t^{-1}]
.
We also have
\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)_{t^{-1}}=A[t,t^{-1}]
a smooth
A
-algebra.
We have for
j\geq 1

\displaystyle D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)|A,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M))_{t^{-1}}

\displaystyle\cong D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)_{t^{-1}}|A,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{F},M)_{t^{-1}})

\displaystyle=D_{j}(A[t,t^{-1}]|A,M[t,t^{-1}])=0.

The vanishing in the last line occurs since
A[t,t^{-1}]
is a smooth
A
-algebra.
∎

3.2
.

Let
M
be a finitely generated
A
-module. If
\mathcal{F}=\{\mathcal{F}_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
is an

-stable filtration then set

(with hypotheses as above). If for some

D_{j}(\widehat{\mathcal{R}}(I)|A,G_{\mathcal{F}}(M))=0

We have an exact sequence of graded
