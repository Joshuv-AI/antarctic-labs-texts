10 C.F.R. § 436.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:436.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-436.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 436 (Federal Energy Management And Planning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the rules for Federal energy management and planning programs to reduce Federal energy consumption and to promote life cycle cost effective investments in building energy systems, building water systems and energy and water conservation measures for Federal buildings.
