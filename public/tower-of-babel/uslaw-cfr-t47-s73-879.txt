47 C.F.R. § 73.879 — Signal retransmission.
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.879
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.879
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An LPFM licensee may not retransmit, either terrestrially or via satellite, the signal of a full-power radio broadcast station.
