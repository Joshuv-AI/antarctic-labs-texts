40 C.F.R. § 265.442 — Design and installation of new drip pads.
Citation: us:cfr:40:265.442
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-265.442
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 265 (Interim Status Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage, And Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owners and operators of new drip pads must ensure that the pads are designed, installed, and operated in accordance with one of the following:
(a) All of the applicable requirements of §§ 265.443 (except § 265.443(a)(4)), 265.444 and 265.445 of this subpart, or
(b) All of the applicable requirements of §§ 265.443 (except § 265.443(b)), 265.444 and 265.445 of this subpart.
