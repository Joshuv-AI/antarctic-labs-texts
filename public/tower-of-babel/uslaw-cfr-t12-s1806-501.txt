12 C.F.R. § 1806.501 — Compliance with government requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1806.501
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1806.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1806 (Bank Enterprise Award Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In carrying out its responsibilities pursuant to an Award Agreement, the Recipient must comply with all applicable Federal, State, and local laws, regulations (including but not limited to the Uniform Administrative Requirements, ordinances, and Executive Orders).
