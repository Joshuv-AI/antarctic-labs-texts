45 C.F.R. § 146.119 — HMO affiliation period as an alternative to a preexisting condition exclusion.
Citation: us:cfr:45:146.119
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-146.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 146 (Requirements For The Group Health Insurance Market). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules for HMO affiliation periods have been superseded by the prohibition on preexisting condition exclusions. See § 147.108 of this subchapter for rules prohibiting the imposition of a preexisting condition exclusion.
