42 C.F.R. § 410.163 — Payment for services furnished to kidney donors.
Citation: us:cfr:42:410.163
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-410.163
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 410 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this chapter, there are no deductible or coinsurance requirements with respect to services furnished to an individual who donates a kidney for transplant surgery.
