12 C.F.R. § 1240.45 — Securitization exposures to which the SSFA and the CRTA do not apply.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1240.45
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1240.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1240 (Capital Adequacy Of Enterprises). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Enterprise must assign a 1,250 percent risk weight to any acquired CRT exposure and all securitization exposures to which the Enterprise does not apply the SSFA under § 1240.43 or the CRTA under § 1240.44.
