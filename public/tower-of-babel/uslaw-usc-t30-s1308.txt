30 U.S.C. § 1308 — Advance appropriations
Citation: us:usc:30:1308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1308
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 25 (Surface Mining Control And Reclamation); Subchapter VII (Administrative And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, no authority to make payments under this chapter shall be effective except to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
