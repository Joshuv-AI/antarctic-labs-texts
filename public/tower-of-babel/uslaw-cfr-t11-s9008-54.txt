11 C.F.R. § 9008.54 — Examinations and audits.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9008.54
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9008.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9008 (Federal Financing Of Presidential Nominating Conventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall conduct an examination and audit of each host committee registered under 11 CFR 9008.51. The Commission will follow the same procedures during the audit, and will afford the committee the same right to respond, as are provided for audits of publicly funded candidates under 11 CFR 9007.1 and 9038.1, except that the Commission will not make any repayment calculations under this section.
