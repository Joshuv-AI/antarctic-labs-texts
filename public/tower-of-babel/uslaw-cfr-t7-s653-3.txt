7 C.F.R. § 653.3 — Adaptation of technical standards.
Citation: us:cfr:7:653.3
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-653.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 653 (Technical Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Technical standards and criteria developed on a national basis may require special adaptation to meet local needs. These adaptations must be approved by the Administrator of the Natural Resources Conservation Service or his designee.
