47 C.F.R. § 80.72 — Antenna requirements for coast stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.72
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All emissions of a coast station a marine-utility station operated on shore using telephony within the frequency band 30-200 MHz must be vertically polarized.
