46 C.F.R. § 2.75-60 — -60 Hazardous ships' stores.
Citation: us:cfr:46:2.75-60
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-2.75-60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 2 (Vessel Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hazardous ships' stores, as defined in § 147.3 of this chapter, must not be brought on board or used on any vessel unless they meet the requirements of part 147 of this chapter.
