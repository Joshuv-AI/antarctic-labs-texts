50 U.S.C. § 3302 — Identification of constituent components of base intelligence budget
Citation: us:usc:50:3302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3302
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 45 (Miscellaneous Intelligence Community Authorities); Subchapter I (Budget And Oversight). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of Central Intelligence shall include the same level of budgetary detail for the Base Budget that is provided for Ongoing Initiatives and New Initiatives to the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence of the House of Representatives and the Select Committee on Intelligence of the Senate in the congressional justification materials for the annual submission of the National Foreign Intelligence Program of each fiscal year.
