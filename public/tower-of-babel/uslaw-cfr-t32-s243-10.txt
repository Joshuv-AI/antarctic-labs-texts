32 C.F.R. § 243.10 — Disputes.
Citation: us:cfr:32:243.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-243.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 243 (Department Of Defense Ratemaking Procedures For Civil Reserve Air Fleet Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carriers should first address concerns to the ratemaking team for resolution. Ratemaking issues that are not resolved to the carrier's satisfaction through discussions with the ratemaking team may be directed to the USTRANSCOM contracting officer.
