48 C.F.R. § 925.7000 — 925.7000 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:925.7000
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-925.7000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 925 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies for selection for contract award of nuclear hot cell services when one of the competitors is a foreign company. This subpart does not apply to the acquisition and use of nuclear hot cell facilities on-site at a DOE-owned or -leased facility.
