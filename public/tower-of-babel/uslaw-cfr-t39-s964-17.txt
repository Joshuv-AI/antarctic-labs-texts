39 C.F.R. § 964.17 — Final agency decision.
Citation: us:cfr:39:964.17
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-964.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 964 (Withheld Mail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Judicial Officer renders the final agency decision and order which will be served upon the parties and upon the postmaster at the office where the mail at issue is being held.
