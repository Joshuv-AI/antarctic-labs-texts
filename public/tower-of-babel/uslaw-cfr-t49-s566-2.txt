49 C.F.R. § 566.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:566.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-566.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 566 (Manufacturer Identification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to facilitate the regulation of manufacturers under the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act, and to aid in establishing a code numbering system for all regulated manufacturers.
