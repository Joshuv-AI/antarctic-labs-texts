27 C.F.R. § 6.67 — Sales to retailer whose account is in arrears.
Citation: us:cfr:27:6.67
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-6.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 6 (“Tied-House”). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An extension of credit (for product purchases) by an industry member to a retailer whose account is in arrears does not constitute a means to induce within the meaning of the Act so long as such retailer pays in advance or on delivery an amount equal to or greater than the value of each order, regardless of the manner in which the industry member applies the payment in its records.
