21 C.F.R. § 117.8 — Applicability of subpart B of this part to the off-farm packing and holding of raw agricultural commodities.
Citation: us:cfr:21:117.8
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-117.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 117 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice, Hazard Analysis, And Risk-Based Preventive Controls For Human Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided by § 117.5(k)(1), subpart B of this part applies to the off-farm packaging, packing, and holding of raw agricultural commodities. Compliance with this requirement for raw agricultural commodities that are produce as defined in part 112 of this chapter may be achieved by complying with subpart B of this part or with the applicable requirements for packing and holding in part 112 of this chapter.
