10 U.S.C. § 7776 — Emergency construction: fortifications
Citation: us:usc:10:7776
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7776
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 779 (Real Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If in an emergency the President considers it urgent, a temporary fort or fortification may be built on private land if the owner consents in writing.
