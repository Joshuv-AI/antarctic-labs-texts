5 C.F.R. § 317.202 — Suitability inquiries regarding criminal history.
Citation: us:cfr:5:317.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-317.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 317 (Employment In The Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agency inquiries regarding criminal history must be done in accordance with the requirements under chapter 92 of title 5, U.S. Code and part 920 of this chapter.
