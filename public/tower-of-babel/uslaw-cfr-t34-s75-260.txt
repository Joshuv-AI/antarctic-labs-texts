34 C.F.R. § 75.260 — Allotments and reallotments.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.260
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under some of the programs covered by this part, the Secretary allots funds under a statutory or regulatory formula.
(b) Any reallotment to other grantees will be made by the Secretary in accordance with applicable statutes and regulations.
