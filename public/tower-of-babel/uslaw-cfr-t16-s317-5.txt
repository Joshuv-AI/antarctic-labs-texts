16 C.F.R. § 317.5 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:317.5
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-317.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 317 (Prohibition Of Energy Market Manipulation Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this Rule are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, it is the Commission's intention that the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
