40 C.F.R. § 59.695 — What provisions apply to confidential information?
Citation: us:cfr:40:59.695
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-59.695
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 59 (National Volatile Organic Compound Emission Standards For Consumer And Commercial Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 40 CFR 1068.10 and 1068.11 apply for information you submit under this part.
