10 U.S.C. § 10210 — Dissemination of information
Citation: us:usc:10:10210
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10210
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1007 (Administration Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall require the complete and current dissemination, to all Reserves and to the public, of information of interest to the reserve components.
