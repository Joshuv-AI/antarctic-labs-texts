34 U.S.C. § 20932 — Immunity for good faith conduct
Citation: us:usc:34:20932
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section20932
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 209 (Child Protection And Safety); Subchapter I (Sex Offender Registration And Notification); Part A (Sex Offender Registration And Notification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Government, jurisdictions, political subdivisions of jurisdictions, and their agencies, officers, employees, and agents shall be immune from liability for good faith conduct under this subchapter.
