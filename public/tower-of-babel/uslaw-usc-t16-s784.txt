16 U.S.C. § 784 — Jurisdiction of prosecutions
Citation: us:usc:16:784
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section784
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 11 (Regulation Of Landing, Curing, And Sale Of Sponges Taken From Gulf Of Mexico And Straits Of Florida). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any violation of the provisions of this chapter shall be prosecuted in the district court of the United States of the district wherein the offender is found or into which he is first brought.
