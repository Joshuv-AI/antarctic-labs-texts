25 C.F.R. § 162.323 — When are rental payments due under a residential lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.323
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.323
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A residential lease must specify the dates on which payments are due.
(b) Unless the lease provides otherwise, payments may not be made or accepted more than one year in advance of the due date.
(c) Payments are due at the time specified in the lease, regardless of whether the lessee receives an advance billing or other notice that a payment is due.
