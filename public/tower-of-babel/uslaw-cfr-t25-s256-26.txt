25 C.F.R. § 256.26 — Will I need flood insurance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.26
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You will need flood insurance if your house is located in an area identified as having special flood hazards under the Flood Disaster Protection Act of 1973 (Pub. L. 93-234, 87 Stat. 975). Your servicing housing office will advise you.
