36 U.S.C. § 152410 — Immunity of United States Government
Citation: us:usc:36:152410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section152410
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1524 (National Recording Preservation Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Government is not liable for any debts, defaults, acts, or omissions of the corporation. The full faith and credit of the Government does not extend to any obligation of the corporation.
