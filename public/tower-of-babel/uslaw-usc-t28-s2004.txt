28 U.S.C. § 2004 — Sale of personalty generally
Citation: us:usc:28:2004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2004
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 127 (Executions And Judicial Sales). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any personalty sold under any order or decree of any court of the United States shall be sold in accordance with section 2001 of this title, unless the court orders otherwise.
This section shall not apply to sales and proceedings under Title 11 or by receivers or conservators of banks appointed by the Comptroller of the Currency.
