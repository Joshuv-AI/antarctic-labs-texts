41 C.F.R. § 301-52.12 — -52.12 Tax reporting of late payment fees.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-52.12
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-52.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-52 (52—Claiming Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Late payment fees will not be reported as wages on a Form W-2. The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) has determined that the late payment fee is in the nature of interest (compensation for money use). The agency will report payments in accordance with IRS guidelines.
