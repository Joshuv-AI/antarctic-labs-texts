29 C.F.R. § 1690.302 — Issuances proposed by EEOC.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1690.302
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1690.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1690 (Procedures On Interagency Coordination Of Equal Employment Opportunity Issuances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the EEOC proposes to develop a significant issuance or any issuance requiring consultation, the procedure outlined in these regulations, shall also apply, as set forth in section 1-303 of the Order. The EEOC shall advise and consult with other affected agencies whenever it develops an issuance, in the same manner and to the same extent as other agencies are required to do in § 1690.301 of this part, and in other sections below.
