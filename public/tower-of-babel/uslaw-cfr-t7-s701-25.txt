7 C.F.R. § 701.25 — Practices carried out with aid from ineligible persons or ineligible legal entities.
Citation: us:cfr:7:701.25
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-701.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 701 (Emergency Conservation Program, Emergency Forest Restoration Program, And Certain Related Programs Previously Administered Under This Part). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any assistance provided by someone other than the eligible participant, including assistance from a State or Federal agency, shall be deducted from the participant's total costs incurred for the practice for the purpose of computing ECP or EFRP cost shares. If unusual conditions exist, the Deputy Administrator may waive deduction of such contributions upon a request from the State committee and demonstration of the need for such a waiver.
