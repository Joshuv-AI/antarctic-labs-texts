19 U.S.C. § 2466 — Agricultural exports of beneficiary developing countries
Citation: us:usc:19:2466
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2466
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter V (Generalized System Of Preferences). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The appropriate agencies of the United States shall assist beneficiary developing countries to develop and implement measures designed to assure that the agricultural sectors of their economies are not directed to export markets to the detriment of the production of foodstuffs for their citizenry.
