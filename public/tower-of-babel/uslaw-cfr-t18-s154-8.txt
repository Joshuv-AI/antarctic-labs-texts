18 C.F.R. § 154.8 — Informal submission for staff suggestions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:154.8
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-154.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 154 (Rate Schedules And Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any natural gas company may informally submit a proposed tariff or any part thereof or material relating thereto for the suggestions of the Commission staff prior to filing. Opinions of the Commission staff are not binding upon the Commission.
