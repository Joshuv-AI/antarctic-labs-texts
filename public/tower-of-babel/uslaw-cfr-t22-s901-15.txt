22 C.F.R. § 901.15 — Exclusive representative.
Citation: us:cfr:22:901.15
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-901.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 901 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exclusive representative means any labor organization which is certified as the exclusive representative of the bargaining unit of which the grievant or charged employee is a member.
