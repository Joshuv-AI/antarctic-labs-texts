39 C.F.R. § 955.8 — (Rule 8) Hearing request.
Citation: us:cfr:39:955.8
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-955.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As directed by the Board, each party must state whether it wants to have the appeal decided after a hearing or on the written record without a hearing. See Rules 11 and 17. A party's hearing request should state where and when it wants the hearing to be conducted. After considering the parties' requests, the Board will decide whether to hold a hearing. If the Board decides to hold a hearing, it will also determine the time and place of the hearing.
