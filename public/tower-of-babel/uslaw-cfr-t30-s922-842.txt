30 C.F.R. § 922.842 — Federal inspections.

Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-922.842
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 922 (Michigan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Part 842 of this chapter, Federal Inspections, shall apply to all exploration and surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
(b) In addition to the requirements of part 842, the Secretary will furnish a copy of each inspection report regarding inspections conducted pursuant to this subpart to the Michigan Department of Natural Resources upon request.
