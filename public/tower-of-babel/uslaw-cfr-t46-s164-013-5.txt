46 C.F.R. § 164.013-5 — -5 Acceptance tests.
Citation: us:cfr:46:164.013-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-164.013-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 164 (Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manufacturers shall ensure that the performance and identification tests described in UL 1191, as appropriate, are performed on a minimum of five samples in each of the lightest and darkest colors submitted for acceptance by a recognized laboratory accepted under § 164.019.
