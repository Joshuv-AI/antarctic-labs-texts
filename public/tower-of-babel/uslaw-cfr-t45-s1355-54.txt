45 C.F.R. § 1355.54 — CCWIS options.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1355.54
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1355.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1355 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a project meets, or when completed will meet, the requirements of § 1355.52, then ACF may approve CCWIS funding described at § 1355.57 for other ACF-approved data exchanges or automated functions that are necessary to achieve title IV-E or IV-B programs goals.
