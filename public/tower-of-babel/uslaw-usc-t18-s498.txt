18 U.S.C. § 498 — Military or naval discharge certificates
Citation: us:usc:18:498
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section498
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 25 (Counterfeiting And Forgery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever forges, counterfeits, or falsely alters any certificate of discharge from the military or naval service of the United States, or uses, unlawfully possesses or exhibits any such certificate, knowing the same to be forged, counterfeited, or falsely altered, shall be fined under this title 1 or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
