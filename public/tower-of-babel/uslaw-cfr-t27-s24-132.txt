27 C.F.R. § 24.132 — Change in bond status.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.132
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor must file an amended application if the proprietor's bond status changes in either of the following ways:
(a) A proprietor who has not furnished any bond becomes required to furnish a bond as provided under § 24.154(b); or
(b) A proprietor who has furnished a bond becomes exempt from bond requirements under § 24.146(d) and chooses to terminate all bond coverage as provided under § 24.160.
