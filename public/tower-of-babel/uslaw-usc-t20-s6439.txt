20 U.S.C. § 6439 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:20:6439
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6439
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter I (Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged); Part D (Prevention And Intervention Programs For Children And Youth Who Are Neglected, Delinquent, Or At-Risk); Subpart 1 (State Agency Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may reserve not more than 2.5 percent of the amount made available to carry out this subpart for a fiscal year to provide technical assistance to and support the capacity building of State agency programs assisted under this subpart.
