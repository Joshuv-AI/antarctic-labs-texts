12 C.F.R. § 8.1 — Scope and application.
Citation: us:cfr:12:8.1
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-8.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 8 (Assessment Of Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The assessments contained in this part are made pursuant to the authority contained in 12 U.S.C. 16, 93a, 481, 482, 1467, 1831c, 1867, 3102, and 3108; and 15 U.S.C. 78c and 78l.
