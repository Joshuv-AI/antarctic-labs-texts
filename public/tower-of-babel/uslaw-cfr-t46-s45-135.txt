46 C.F.R. § 45.135 — Hull openings at or below freeboard deck.
Citation: us:cfr:46:45.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-45.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 45 (Great Lakes Load Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Closures for hull openings at or below the freeboard deck must be as strong as the structure to which they are attached and must be watertight.
