19 C.F.R. § 144.13 — Bond requirements.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 144 (Warehouse And Rewarehouse Entries And Withdrawals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bond on Customs Form 301, containing the bond conditions set forth in § 113.62 of this chapter shall be filed in the amount required by the Center director to support the entry documentation.
