10 U.S.C. § 2257 — Use of recruiting materials for public relations
Citation: us:usc:10:2257
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2257
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 134 (Miscellaneous Administrative Provisions); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous Administrative Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may use for public relations purposes of the Department of Defense any advertising materials developed for use for recruitment and retention of personnel for the armed forces. Any such use shall be under such conditions and subject to such restrictions as the Secretary of Defense shall prescribe.
