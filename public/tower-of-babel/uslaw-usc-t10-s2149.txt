10 U.S.C. § 2149 — Applications for educational assistance
Citation: us:usc:10:2149
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2149
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 106A (Educational Assistance For Persons Enlisting For Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To receive educational assistance benefits under this chapter, a person entitled to such assistance under section 2142 or 2147 of this title shall submit an application for such assistance to the Secretary concerned in such form and manner as the Secretary concerned may prescribe.
