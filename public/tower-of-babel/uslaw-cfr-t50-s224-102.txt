50 C.F.R. § 224.102 — Permits for endangered marine and anadromous species.
Citation: us:cfr:50:224.102
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-224.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 224 (Endangered Marine And Anadromous Species). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall take, import, export, or engage in any activity prohibited by section 9 of the Act involving any marine species that has been determined to be endangered under the Endangered Species Conservation Act of 1969 or the Act, and that is under the jurisdiction of the Secretary, without a valid permit issued pursuant to part 222, subpart C of this chapter.
