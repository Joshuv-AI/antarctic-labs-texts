7 C.F.R. § 1951.859 — Term of loans.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1951.859
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1951.859
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1951 (Servicing And Collections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No loans shall be extended for a period exceeding 30 years. Principal payments on loans will be made at least annually. The initial principal payment may be deferred not more than 3 years.
(b) The terms of loan repayment will be those stipulated in the loan agreement and/or promissory note.
