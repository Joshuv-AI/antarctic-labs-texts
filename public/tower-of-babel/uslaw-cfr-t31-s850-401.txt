31 C.F.R. § 850.401 — Undertaking a notifiable transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:31:850.401
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-850.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 850 (Provisions Pertaining To U.S. Investments In Certain National Security Technologies And Products In Countries Of Concern). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A U.S. person that undertakes a notifiable transaction shall file a notification of that transaction with the Department of the Treasury pursuant to § 850.404.
