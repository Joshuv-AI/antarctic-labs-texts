40 C.F.R. § 202.11 — Effective date.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 202 (Motor Carriers Engaged In Interstate Commerce). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of Subpart B shall become effective October 15, 1975, except that the provisions of § 202.20(b) and § 202.21(b) of Subpart B shall apply to motor vehicles manufactured during or after the 1986 model year.
