40 C.F.R. § 79.3 — Availability of information.
Citation: us:cfr:40:79.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-79.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 79 (Registration Of Fuels And Fuel Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The availability to the public of information provided to, or otherwise obtained by, the Administrator under this part shall be governed by part 2 of this chapter except as expressly noted in subpart F of this part.
