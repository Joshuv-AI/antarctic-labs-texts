49 C.F.R. § 299.605 — Functional and performance qualification tests.
Citation: us:cfr:49:299.605
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-299.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 299 (Texas Central Railroad High-Speed Rail Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The railroad shall conduct functional and performance qualification tests, prior to commencing revenue operations, to verify that all safety-critical components meet all functional and all performance specifications.
