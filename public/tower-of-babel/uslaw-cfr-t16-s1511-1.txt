16 C.F.R. § 1511.1 — Scope of part 1511.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1511.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1511.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1511 (Requirements For Pacifiers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part 1511 sets forth the requirements whereby pacifiers (as defined in § 1511.2(a)) are not banned articles under § 1500.18(a)(8) of this chapter.
