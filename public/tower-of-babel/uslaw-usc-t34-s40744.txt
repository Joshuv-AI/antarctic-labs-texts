34 U.S.C. § 40744 — Offset of funds appropriated
Citation: us:usc:34:40744
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40744
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 407 (Dna Identification); Subchapter III (Dna Arrestee Collection Processes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any funds appropriated to carry out this subchapter, not to exceed $10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2013 through 2015, shall be derived from amounts appropriated pursuant to subsection (j) of section 40701 of this title in each such fiscal year for grants under such section.
