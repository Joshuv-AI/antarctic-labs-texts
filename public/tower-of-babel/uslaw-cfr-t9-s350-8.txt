9 C.F.R. § 350.8 — Scope and applicability of rules of practice.
Citation: us:cfr:9:350.8
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-350.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 350 (Special Services Relating To Meat And Other Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of practice of the Department of Agriculture in subpart H of part I, subtitle A, title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations, are the rules of practice applicable to adjudicatory, administrative proceedings under the regulations in this part (9 CFR part 350).
