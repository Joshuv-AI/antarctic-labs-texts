7 C.F.R. § 63.12 — Under Secretary for Research, Education, and Economics.
Citation: us:cfr:7:63.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-63.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 63 (National Sheep Industry Improvement Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under Secretary for Research, Education, and Economics means the Under Secretary for Research, Education, and Economics of the U.S. Department of Agriculture, or any other officer or employee of the Department designated by the Under Secretary to act in the Under Secretary's stead.
