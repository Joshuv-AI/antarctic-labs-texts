15 U.S.C. § 1093 — Registration certificates for marks on principal and supplemental registers to be different
Citation: us:usc:15:1093
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1093
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter II (The Supplemental Register). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The certificates of registration for marks registered on the supplemental register shall be conspicuously different from certificates issued for marks registered on the principal register.
