45 C.F.R. § 2553.105 — How are performance measures approved and documented?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.105
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) AmeriCorps reviews and approves performance measures for all applicants that apply for funding.
(b) An applicant must follow AmeriCorps provided guidance and formats when submitting performance measures.
(c) Final performance measures, as negotiated between the applicant and AmeriCorps, will be documented in the approved grant application.
