11 U.S.C. § 748 — Reduction of securities to money
Citation: us:usc:11:748
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section748
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 7 (Liquidation); Subchapter III (Stockbroker Liquidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after the date of the order for relief, the trustee shall reduce to money, consistent with good market practice, all securities held as property of the estate, except for customer name securities delivered or reclaimed under section 751 of this title.
