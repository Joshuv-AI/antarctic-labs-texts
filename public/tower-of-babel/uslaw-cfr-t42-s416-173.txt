42 C.F.R. § 416.173 — Publication of revised payment methodologies and payment rates.
Citation: us:cfr:42:416.173
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-416.173
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 416 (Ambulatory Surgical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS publishes annually, through notice and comment rulemaking in the Federal Register and/or via the Internet on the CMS Web site, the payment methodologies and payment rates for ASC services and designates the covered surgical procedures and covered ancillary services for which CMS will make an ASC payment and other revisions as appropriate.
