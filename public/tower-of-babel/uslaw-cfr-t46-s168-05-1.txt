46 C.F.R. § 168.05-1 — -1 Application of passenger vessel inspection laws.
Citation: us:cfr:46:168.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-168.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 168 (Civilian Nautical School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All laws covering the inspection of passenger vessels are hereby made applicable to all vessels or other floating equipment used by or in connection with any civilian nautical school, whether such vessels or other floating equipment are being navigated or not, except vessels of the Navy or Coast Guard.
