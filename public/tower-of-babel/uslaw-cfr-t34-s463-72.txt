34 C.F.R. § 463.72 — How does the eligible agency award funds to eligible providers for the Integrated English Literacy and Civics Education program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:463.72
Creator: Office Of Career, Technical, And Adult Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-463.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 463 (Adult Education And Family Literacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States must award funds for the Integrated English Literacy and Civics Education program to eligible providers in accordance with subpart C.
