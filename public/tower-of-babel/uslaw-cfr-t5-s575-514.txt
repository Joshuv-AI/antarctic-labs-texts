5 C.F.R. § 575.514 — What are an agency's monitoring responsibilities?
Citation: us:cfr:5:575.514
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-575.514
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 575 (Recruitment, Relocation, And Retention Incentives; Supervisory Differentials; And Extended Assignment Incentives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency must monitor the use of extended assignment incentives to ensure that the agency's extended assignment incentive plan and the payment of extended assignment incentives are consistent with the requirements and criteria established under 5 U.S.C. 5757 and this subpart.
