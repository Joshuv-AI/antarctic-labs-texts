43 C.F.R. § 2920.3 — Bureau of Land Management initiated land use proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2920.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2920.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2920 (Leases, Permits And Easements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where, as a result of the land use planning process, the desirability of allowing use of the public lands or providing increased service to the public from such use of the public lands is demonstrated, the authorized officer may identify a use for the public land and notify the public that proposals for utilizing the land through a lease, permit or easement will be considered.
