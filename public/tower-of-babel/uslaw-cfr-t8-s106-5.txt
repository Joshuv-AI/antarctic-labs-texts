8 C.F.R. § 106.5 — Authority to certify records.
Citation: us:cfr:8:106.5
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-106.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 106 (Uscis Fee Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of USCIS, or such officials as he or she may designate, may certify records when authorized under 5 U.S.C. 552 or any other law to provide such records.
