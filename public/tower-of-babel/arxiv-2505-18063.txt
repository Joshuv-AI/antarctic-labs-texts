A uniqueness result in the inverse problem for the anisotropic Schrödinger type equation from local measurements
Niall Donlon, Romina Gaburro

Abstract
We consider the inverse boundary value problem of the simultaneous determination of the coefficients σ \sigma and q q of the equation − div ( σ ∇ u ) + q u = 0 -\mbox{div}(\sigma\nabla u)+qu=0 from knowledge of the so-called Neumann-to-Dirichlet map, given locally on a non-empty curved portion Σ \Sigma of the boundary ∂ Ω \partial\Omega of a domain Ω ⊂ ℝ n \Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} , with n ≥ 3 n\geq 3 . We assume that σ \sigma and q q are a-priori known to be a piecewise constant matrix-valued and scalar function, respectively, on a given partition of Ω \Omega with curved interfaces. We prove that σ \sigma and q q can be uniquely determined in Ω \Omega from the knowledge of the local map.



Keywords
: Anisotropic Schrödinger equation; uniqueness; local Neumann-to-Dirichlet map.
