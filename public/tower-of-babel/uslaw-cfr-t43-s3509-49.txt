43 C.F.R. § 3509.49 — What terms and conditions apply to my fractional interest prospecting permit or lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3509.49
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3509.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will apply the commodity-specific terms and conditions found in this part to fractional interest prospecting permits and leases.
