26 U.S.C. § 5067 — Reduced rates not allowed for smuggled or illegally produced beer, wine, or spirits
Citation: us:usc:26:5067
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5067
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter A (Gallonage And Occupational Taxes); Part I (Gallonage Taxes); Subpart E (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of beer, wine, or distilled spirits that are smuggled into the United States or produced other than as authorized by this chapter—
(1)
the rates of tax under paragraphs (1)(A)(i) and (2) of section 5051(a) shall not apply in the case of any such beer,
(2)
the credit under section 5041(c) shall not apply in the case of any such wine, and
(3)
the rates of tax under section 5001(c) shall not apply in the case of any such distilled spirits.
