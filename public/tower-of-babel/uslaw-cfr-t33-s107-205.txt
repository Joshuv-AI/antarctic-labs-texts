33 C.F.R. § 107.205 — Purpose and delegation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:107.205
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-107.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 107 (National Vessel And Facility Control Measures And Limited Access Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to implement Presidential Proclamation 7757, and Secretary of Homeland Security Order 2004-001. All powers and authorities granted to officers of the Coast Guard by this subpart may be delegated to other officers and agents of the Coast Guard unless otherwise prohibited by law.
