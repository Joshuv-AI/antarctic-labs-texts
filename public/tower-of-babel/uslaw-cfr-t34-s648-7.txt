34 C.F.R. § 648.7 — What is the institutional matching contribution?
Citation: us:cfr:34:648.7
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-648.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 648 (Graduate Assistance In Areas Of National Need). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An institution shall provide, from non-Federal funds, an institutional matching contribution equal to at least 25 percent of the amount of the grant received under this part, for the uses indicated in § 648.63.
