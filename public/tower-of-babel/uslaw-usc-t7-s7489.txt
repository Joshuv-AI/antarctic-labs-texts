7 U.S.C. § 7489 — Relation to other programs
Citation: us:usc:7:7489
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7489
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 101 (Agricultural Promotion); Subchapter V (Popcorn). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter preempts or supersedes any other program relating to popcorn promotion organized and operated under the laws of the United States or any State.
