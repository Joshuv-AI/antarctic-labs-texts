17 C.F.R. § 9.6 — Sanctions for noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:17:9.6
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-9.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 9 (Rules Relating To Review Of Exchange Disciplinary, Access Denial Or Other Adverse Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that any party fails to file any document or make any appearance which is required under this part, the Commission may, in its discretion, and upon its own motion or upon the motion of any party to the proceeding, dismiss the proceeding before it, or, based on the record before it, affirm, modify, set aside, or remand for further proceedings, in whole or in part, the decision of the exchange.
