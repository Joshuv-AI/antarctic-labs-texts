40 C.F.R. § 1090.160 — Distributors, carriers, and resellers.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.160
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A distributor, carrier, or reseller must comply with the requirements of this section.
(a) Gasoline and diesel fuel standards. A distributor, carrier, or reseller must comply with the applicable requirements of subparts C and D of this part.
(b) Registration. A distributor or carrier must register with EPA under subpart I of this part if they are part of the 500 ppm LM diesel fuel distribution chain in a compliance plan submitted under § 1090.515(g).
(c) PTDs. On each occasion when a distributor, carrier, or reseller transfers custody or title to any fuel, fuel additive, or regulated blendstock, the transferor must provide to the transferee PTDs under subpart L of this part.
