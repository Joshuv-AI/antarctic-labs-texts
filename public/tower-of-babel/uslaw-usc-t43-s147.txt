43 U.S.C. § 147 — Disposition of proceeds of sale of withdrawn lands
Citation: us:usc:43:147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section147
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 6 (Withdrawal From Settlement, Location, Sale, Or Entry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The moneys derived from the sale of such lands and improvements shall be disposed of as are other receipts from the sale and disposal of public lands.
