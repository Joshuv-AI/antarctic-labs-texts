42 C.F.R. § 440.375 — Comparability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:440.375
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-440.375
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 440 (Services: General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States have the option to amend their State plan to provide benchmark or benchmark-equivalent coverage to individuals without regard to comparability.
