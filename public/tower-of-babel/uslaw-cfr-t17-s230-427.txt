17 C.F.R. § 230.427 — Contents of prospectus used after nine months.
Citation: us:cfr:17:230.427
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-230.427
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 230 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There may be omitted from any prospectus used more than 9 months after the effective date of the registration statement any information previously required to be contained in the prospectus insofar as later information covering the same subjects, including the latest available certified financial statement, as of a date not more than 16 months prior to the use of the prospectus is contained therein.
