45 U.S.C. § 1009 — Expedited proceedings
Citation: us:usc:45:1009
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section1009
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 19 (Rock Island Railroad Employee Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Commission shall give all proceedings involving the Rock Island Railroad preference over all other pending proceedings related to rail carriers and make all of its decisions at the earliest practicable time.
(b)
The Commission shall, within 100 days of the filing of an application (or such shorter period as the court may set) pursuant to section 915 of this title, reach a decision on all proceedings filed after January 1, 1980, which involve a sale, transfer or lease of any line of the Rock Island Railroad to a solvent carrier.
