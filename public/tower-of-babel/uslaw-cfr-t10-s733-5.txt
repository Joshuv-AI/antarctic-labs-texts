10 C.F.R. § 733.5 — Allegations received by DOE.
Citation: us:cfr:10:733.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-733.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 733 (Allegations Of Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If DOE receives directly a written allegation of research misconduct with regard to research under a DOE contract or financial assistance agreement, DOE will refer the allegation for processing to the DOE Element responsible for the contract or financial assistance agreement.
