15 C.F.R. § 998.40 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 998 (Marine And Aviation Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart—
(a) Establishes policy and implements 33 U.S.C. 3071(a)(8), (a)(13), (b), and (c) to provide protection against reprisal to NOAA Corps officers for making or preparing or being perceived as making or preparing a protected communication.
(b) Assigns responsibilities and delegates authority for such protection against reprisal and prescribes procedures.
