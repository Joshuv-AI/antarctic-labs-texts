20 C.F.R. § 234.34 — When an entitled relative of the employee dies before receiving payment of a due but unpaid annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:20:234.34
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-234.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 234 (Lump-Sum Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a person, who is entitled to unpaid annuities based upon his or her relationship to the employee, dies before negotiating the check in payment of the unpaid annuities, the amount to which he or she was entitled becomes payable to other relatives of the employee in the same degree of relationship. If no relatives in that degree of relationship survive, the amount becomes payable to relatives in the next degree of relationship.
