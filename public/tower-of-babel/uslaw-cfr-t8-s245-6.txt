8 C.F.R. § 245.6 — Interview.
Citation: us:cfr:8:245.6
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-245.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 245 (Adjustment Of Status To That Of Person Admitted For Permanent Residence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant for adjustment of status under this part shall be interviewed by an immigration officer. This interview may be waived in the case of a child under the age of 14; when the applicant is clearly ineligible under section 245(c) of the Act or § 245.1 of this chapter; or when it is determined by the Service that an interview is unnecessary.
