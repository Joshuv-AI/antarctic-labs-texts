39 U.S.C. § 203 — Postmaster General; Deputy Postmaster General
Citation: us:usc:39:203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section203
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 2 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The chief executive officer of the Postal Service is the Postmaster General appointed under section 202(c) of this title. The alternate chief executive officer of the Postal Service is the Deputy Postmaster General appointed under section 202(d) of this title.
