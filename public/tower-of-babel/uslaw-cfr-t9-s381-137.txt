9 C.F.R. § 381.137 — Evidence of labeling and devices approval.
Citation: us:cfr:9:381.137
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-381.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 381 (Poultry Products Inspection Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No inspector shall authorize the use of any device bearing any official inspection legend unless he or she has on file evidence that such device has been approved in accordance with the provisions of this subpart.
