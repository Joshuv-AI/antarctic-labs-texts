46 C.F.R. § 154.1809 — Loading and stability manual.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1809
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1809
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may operate a vessel unless that vessel has on board a loading and stability manual.
(b) The loading and stability manual must contain:
(1) Information that enables the master to load and ballast the vessel while keeping structural stresses within design limits; and
(2) The information required by § 170.110 of this chapter.
