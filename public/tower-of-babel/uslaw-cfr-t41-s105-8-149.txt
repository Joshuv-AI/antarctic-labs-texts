41 C.F.R. § 105-8.149 — -8.149 Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-8.149
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-8.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-8 (8—Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By General Services Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in §§ 105-8.150 and 105-8.154, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
