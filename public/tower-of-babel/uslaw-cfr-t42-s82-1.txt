42 C.F.R. § 82.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:42:82.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-82.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 82 (Methods For Conducting Dose Reconstruction Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide methods for determining a reasonable estimate of the radiation dose received by a covered employee with cancer under EEOICPA, through the completion of a dose reconstruction. These methods will be applied by the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) in a dose reconstruction program serving claimants under EEOICPA, as identified under § 82.0.
