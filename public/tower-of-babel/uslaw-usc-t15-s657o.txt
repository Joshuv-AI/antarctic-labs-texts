15 U.S.C. § 657o — Annual reports on disaster assistance
Citation: us:usc:15:657o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section657o
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14A (Aid To Small Business). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 45 days after the end of a fiscal year, the Administrator shall submit to the Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship of the Senate and the Committee on Small Business of the House of Representatives a report on the disaster assistance operations of the Administration for that fiscal year. The report shall—
(1)
specify the number of Administration personnel involved in such operations;
(2)
describe any material changes to those operations, such as changes to technologies used or to personnel responsibilities;
(3)
describe and assess the effectiveness of the Administration in responding to disasters during that fiscal year, including a description of the number and amounts of loans made for damage and for economic injury; and
(4)
describe the plans of the Administration for preparing to respond to disasters during the next fiscal year.
