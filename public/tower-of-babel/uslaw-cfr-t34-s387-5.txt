34 C.F.R. § 387.5 — What types of projects are authorized under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:387.5
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-387.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 387 (Innovative Rehabilitation Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Innovative Rehabilitation Training Program supports time-limited pilot projects through which new types of rehabilitation workers may be trained or through which innovative methods of training rehabilitation personnel may be demonstrated.
