2 U.S.C. § 1932 — Application of premium pay limits on annualized basis
Citation: us:usc:2:1932
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1932
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part B (Compensation And Other Personnel Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Any limits on the amount of premium pay which may be earned by officers and members of the Capitol Police during emergencies (as determined by the Capitol Police Board) shall be applied by the Chief of the Capitol Police on an annual basis and not on a pay period basis. Any determination under this subsection shall not be reviewable or appealable in any manner.
(b) Effective date
Subsection (a) shall apply with respect to hours of duty occurring on or after September 11, 2001.
