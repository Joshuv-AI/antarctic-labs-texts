49 U.S.C. § 22906 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:49:22906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Assistance); Chapter 229 (Rail Improvement Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation for capital grants under this chapter the following amounts:
(1)
For fiscal year 2009, $100,000,000.
(2)
For fiscal year 2010, $300,000,000.
(3)
For fiscal year 2011, $400,000,000.
(4)
For fiscal year 2012, $500,000,000.
(5)
For fiscal year 2013, $600,000,000.
