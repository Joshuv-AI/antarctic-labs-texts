25 C.F.R. § 900.44 — What minimum general standards apply to all Indian tribe or tribal organization financial management systems when carrying out a self-determination contract?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.44
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fiscal control and accounting procedures of an Indian tribe or tribal organization shall be sufficient to:
(a) Permit preparation of reports required by a self-determination contract and the Act; and
(b) Permit the tracing of contract funds to a level of expenditure adequate to establish that they have not been used in violation of any restrictions or prohibitions contained in any statute that applies to the self-determination contract.
