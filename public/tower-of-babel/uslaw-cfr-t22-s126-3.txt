22 C.F.R. § 126.3 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:126.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-126.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 126 (General Policies And Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In a case of exceptional or undue hardship, or when it is otherwise in the interest of the United States Government, the Deputy Assistant Secretary of State for Defense Trade Controls may make an exception to the provisions of this subchapter.
