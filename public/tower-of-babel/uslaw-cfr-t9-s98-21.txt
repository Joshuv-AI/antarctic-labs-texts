9 C.F.R. § 98.21 — Embryos from sheep in regions other than Australia, Canada, and New Zealand.
Citation: us:cfr:9:98.21
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-98.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 98 (Importation Of Certain Animal Embryos And Animal Semen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for embryos from sheep in Australia, Canada, or New Zealand, embryos from sheep may only be imported into the United States if they comply with all applicable provisions of this subpart and with § 98.10a.
