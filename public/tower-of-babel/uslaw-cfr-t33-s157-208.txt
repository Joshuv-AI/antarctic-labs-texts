33 C.F.R. § 157.208 — Dedicated Clean Ballast Tanks Operations Manual for foreign tank vessels: Submission.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.208
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the owner or operator of a foreign tank vessel meeting § 157.10a(b), § 157.10a(c)(2), § 157.10b(a)(2), or § 157.10c(c)(2) desires a Coast Guard approved Dedicated Clean Ballast Tanks Operations Manual under § 157.210, the owner or operator must submit two copies of a manual that meets § 157.224 to the Commandant (CG-CVC), Attn: Office of Commercial Vessel Compliance, U.S. Coast Guard Stop 7501, 2703 Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue SE., Washington, DC 20593-7501.
