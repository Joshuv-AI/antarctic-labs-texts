2 C.F.R. § 1326.970 — Nonprocurement transaction (Department of Commerce supplement to government-wide definition at 2 CFR 180.970).
Citation: us:cfr:2:1326.970
Creator: Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1326.970
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1326 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of the Department of Commerce, nonprocurement transaction includes the following:
(a) Joint project Agreements under 15 U.S.C. 1525.
(b) Cooperative research and development agreements.
(c) Joint statistical agreements.
(d) Patent licenses under 35 U.S.C. 207.
(e) NTIS joint ventures, 15 U.S.C. 3704b.
