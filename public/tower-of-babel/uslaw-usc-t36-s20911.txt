36 U.S.C. § 20911 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:36:20911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section20911
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 209 (American Ex-Prisoners Of War). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall comply with the law on service of process of each State in which it is incorporated and each State in which it carries on activities.
