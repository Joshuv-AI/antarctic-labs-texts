Blowup relations and q q -Painlevé VI
Artem Stoyan

Abstract
We propose and study blowup relations obeyed by the partition functions of 5 ​ d 5d 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 (quiver) SYM theories with S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) SU(2) gauge group and four flavours. By analyzing the Weyl group action on the sets of blowup relations, we identify the integer parameters of a blowup relation with the weights of a corresponding Lie algebra. We also explain how this action of the Weyl group follows from the Weyl group symmetry of the partition function. Finally, we use these relations to derive bilinear relations on the q q -Painlevé VI tau functions.
