31 C.F.R. § 584.406 — Offshore transactions involving blocked property.
Citation: us:cfr:31:584.406
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-584.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 584 (Magnitsky Act Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions in § 584.201 on transactions or dealings involving blocked property apply to transactions by any U.S. person in a location outside the United States with respect to property held in the name of a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 584.201(a).
