42 U.S.C. § 5415 — Certification by manufacturer of conformity of manufactured home with standards; form and placement of certification
Citation: us:usc:42:5415
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5415
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 70 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every manufacturer of manufactured homes shall furnish to the distributor or retailer at the time of delivery of each such manufactured home produced by such manufacturer certification that such manufactured home conforms to all applicable Federal construction and safety standards. Such certification shall be in the form of a label or tag permanently affixed to each such manufactured home.
