50 C.F.R. § 84.45 — How do I amend a proposal?
Citation: us:cfr:50:84.45
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-84.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 84 (National Coastal Wetlands Conservation Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following procedures in 43 CFR 12.70, you must submit a signed original and two copies of the revised SF 424, the revised portion of the project statement if appropriate, and an explanation of the reason for the revision to the Regional Director (Federal Aid).
