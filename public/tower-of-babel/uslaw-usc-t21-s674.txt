21 U.S.C. § 674 — Federal court jurisdiction of enforcement and injunction proceedings and other kinds of cases; limitations of section 607(e) of this title
Citation: us:usc:21:674
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section674
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter IV (Auxiliary Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States district courts, the District Court of Guam, the District Court of the Virgin Islands, the highest court of American Samoa, and the United States courts of the other Territories, are vested with jurisdiction specifically to enforce, and to prevent and restrain violations of, this chapter, and shall have jurisdiction in all other kinds of cases arising under this chapter, except as provided in section 607(e) of this title.
