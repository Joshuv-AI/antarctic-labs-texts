43 C.F.R. § 4.1109 — Service.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any party initiating a proceeding under the act must concurrently serve copies of the initiating documents on the appropriate office of the Office of the Solicitor representing OSMRE in the State or on the Indian lands in which the mining operation at issue is located and on any other statutory parties as specified under § 4.1105.
(b) The jurisdiction and contact information for the appropriate office of the Office of the Solicitor to be served under paragraph (a) of this section are set forth in the OHA Standing Orders on Contact Information.
(c) All other documents filed with DCHD must be served as specified in § 4.102.
(d) All other documents filed with the Board must be served as specified in § 4.407.
