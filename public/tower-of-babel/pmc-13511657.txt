Error in Figure

In the original publication [1], it was discovered that some of the representative images provided in Figure 4 overlap with those published in another paper [2] and are incorrect. This resulted from an unintended mix-up and inadvertent reuse of image panels during the parallel preparation of two closely related studies. This occurred because the figures were prepared using a similar layout and organizational structure. The authors would like to replace Figure 4A of the following published paper [1]. The new, corrected version of Figure 4 appears below. The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
