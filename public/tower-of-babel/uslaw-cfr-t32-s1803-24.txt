32 C.F.R. § 1803.24 — Notification of decision and right of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1803.24
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1803.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1803 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Section 3.6 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coordinator shall communicate the decision of NACIC to the requester within ten (10) days of completion of all review action. That correspondence shall include a notice of a right of administrative appeal to the Director, NACIC pursuant to § 3.6(d) of this Order.
