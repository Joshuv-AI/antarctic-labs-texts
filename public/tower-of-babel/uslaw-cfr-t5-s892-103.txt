5 C.F.R. § 892.103 — What can I do if I disagree with my agency's decision about my pre-or post-tax election?
Citation: us:cfr:5:892.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-892.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 892 (Federal Flexible Benefits Plan: Pre-Tax Payment Of Health Benefits Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may use the reconsideration procedure set out at §§ 890.104 of this chapter to request an agency to reconsider its initial decision affecting your participation in the premium conversion plan.
