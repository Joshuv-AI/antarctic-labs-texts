49 C.F.R. § 192.747 — Valve maintenance: Distribution systems.
Citation: us:cfr:49:192.747
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-192.747
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 192 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline: Minimum Federal Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each valve, the use of which may be necessary for the safe operation of a distribution system, must be checked and serviced at intervals not exceeding 15 months, but at least once each calendar year.
(b) Each operator must take prompt remedial action to correct any valve found inoperable, unless the operator designates an alternative valve.
