5 U.S.C. § 9007 — Jurisdiction of courts
Citation: us:usc:5:9007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section9007
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 90 (Long-Term Care Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States have original jurisdiction of a civil action or claim described in paragraph (1) or (2) of section 9003(c), after such administrative remedies as required under such paragraph (1) or (2) (as applicable) have been exhausted, but only to the extent judicial review is not precluded by any dispute resolution or other remedy under this chapter.
