Positive Weighted Partitions Generated by Double Series
George E. ANDREWS a and Mohamed EL BACHRAOUI b

Abstract
We investigate some weighted integer partitions whose generating functions are double-series. We will establish closed formulas for these q q -double series and deduce that their coefficients are non-negative. This leads to inequalities among integer partitions.

1
Introduction

Throughout, let
q
denote a complex number satisfying
|q|<1

and let
m
and
n
denote nonnegative integers.
We adopt the following standard notation from the theory of
q
-series
[
4
,
15
]

\displaystyle(a;q)_{0}=1,\qquad v(a;q)_{n}=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}\bigl(1-aq^{j}\bigr),\qquad(a;q)_{\infty}=\prod_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(1-aq^{j}\bigr),

\displaystyle(a_{1},\ldots,a_{k};q)_{n}=\prod_{j=1}^{k}(a_{j};q)_{n},\qquad\text{and}\qquad(a_{1},\ldots,a_{k};q)_{\infty}=\prod_{j=1}^{k}(a_{j};q)_{\infty}.

We shall need the following basic facts

(a;q)_{n+m}=(a;q)_{m}(aq^{m};q)_{n}\qquad\text{and}\qquad(a;q)_{\infty}=(a;q)_{n}(aq^{n};q)_{\infty}.

(1.1)

In this paper, we consider certain
q
-double series in one single variable
which turn out to be natural generating functions for weighted integer partitions.

Weighted integer partitions have been extensively studied in the past. A first systematic investigation of identities for weighted partitions is due to
Alladi
[
1
,
2
]
. For other references on weighted partitions and their applications,
see, for instance,
[
14
,
20
]
.

A power series
\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}q^{n}
is called positive, written
\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}q^{n}\succeq 0
,
if
a_{n}\geq 0
for any nonnegative integer
n
. Accordingly, we will write

\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}q^{n}\succeq\sum_{n\geq 0}b_{n}q^{n}
to mean that

\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}q^{n}-\sum_{n\geq 0}b_{n}q^{n}\succeq 0
.
Positivity results for
q
-series have been intensively studied in the past to some extent in connection with
Borwein’s famous positivity conjecture
[
3
]
.
For more on this, see, for instance,
[
11
,
12
,
19
,
21
]
.
Positivity results for alternating sums have also received much attention in recent years, see
for example
[
6
,
7
,
13
,
16
]
.

An important application of positivity results is the fact that positive series which are generating
functions for weighted partitions give rise to inequalities of integer partitions. About the
interplay between positive
q
-series and inequalities of integer partitions, we refer the reader
to
[
5
,
8
,
9
,
10
,
18
]
.

Our main goal in this work is to prove that certain
q
-double series are positive. As these series
turn out to be generating functions for weighted partitions, our results
yield inequalities of integer partitions.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section
2
, we introduce our series through the partitions they generate and we state our main
results. In Section
3
, we collect the lemmas needed to prove the main theorems.
Sections
4
–
6
are devoted to the proofs of the main results and their corollaries.

2
Main results

Definition 2.1
.

For any positive integer
N
,
let
F_{1}(N)
be the number of partitions of
N
,
where if the partition has
n
ones then the largest part is
2n+2k+1
for some
k
and all parts
>1

are in the interval
[2n+2,2n+2k+1]
, no even parts are repeated, and the partition is counted
with weight
(-1)^{j}
, where
j
is the number of even parts.
Then we have

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{1}(n)q^{n}=\sum_{k,n\geq 0}\frac{\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{k}}{\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{k}}q^{2k+3n+1}.

We now state our first main result.

Theorem 2.2
.

We have

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{1}(n)q^{n}=\frac{1}{\bigl(1-q^{2}\bigr)}\frac{\bigl(q^{2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}^{2}}{\bigl(q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}^{2}}-\frac{\bigl(1+q^{2}\bigr)}{(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}.

With the help of Theorem
2.2
, we will derive the following positivity result.

Corollary 2.3
.

There holds

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{1}(n)q^{n}\succeq 0
.

We now introduce our second integer partitions.

Definition 2.4
.

For any positive integer
N
,
let
F_{2}(N)
be the number of partitions of
N
such that for each
j=0,1,2
satisfying

3\mid 2k+j
for some
k
, there are
n+j-2
ones and
(2k+j)/3
threes, the remaining
parts lie in the set
\{2n+2\}\cup(2n+3,2n+2k+3]
, no even parts are repeated, and the partition is counted
with weight
(-1)^{j}
, where
j
is the number of even parts.
Then we have

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{2}(n)q^{n}=\sum_{k,n\geq 0}\frac{\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{k}}{\bigl(q^{2n+5};q^{2}\bigr)_{k}}q^{2k+n+2}.

Theorem 2.5
.

We have

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{2}(n)q^{n}=\frac{q\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}{(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{2}\bigr)}\frac{\bigl(q^{2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}^{2}}{\bigl(q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}^{2}}-\frac{q\bigl(1+q^{2}\bigr)}{(1-q)^{2}}.

Corollary 2.6
.

There holds

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{2}(n)q^{n}\succeq 0
.

We now deal with our third example of integer partitions.

Definition 2.7
.

For any positive integer
N
,
let
G(N)
be the number of partitions of
N
,
where if the partition has
3n
ones then the largest part is
2n+2k+2
for some
k
and all parts
are in the interval
[2n+1,2n+2k+2]
, no even parts are repeated, and the partition is counted
with weight
(-1)^{j}
, where
j
is the number of even parts.
Then we have

\sum_{n\geq 0}G(n)q^{n}=\sum_{k,n\geq 0}\frac{\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{k}}{\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{k}}q^{2k+5n+2}.

Theorem 2.8
.

We have

\sum_{n\geq 0}G(n)q^{n}=\frac{q^{3}}{(1+q)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}\frac{\bigl(q^{2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}^{2}}{\bigl(q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}^{2}}-\frac{q^{2}(1-q)\bigl(-1+q^{3}+q^{4}+q^{5}\bigr)}{\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)^{2}\bigl(1-q^{5}\bigr)}.

Corollary 2.9
.

There holds

\sum_{n\geq 0}G(n)q^{n}\succeq 0
.

Our proofs for Corollaries
2.3
,
2.6
and
2.9
on positive weighted partitions are all analytic.
Obviously, each of these three corollaries is equivalent to an inequality of integer partitions. So, it is natural to
ask for injective proofs for these inequalities.

3
Preliminary lemmas

In this section, we collect several lemmas which we will need to prove our main results.
To simplify the presentation, we introduce the following sequences.

Definition 3.1
.

For any positive integers
m
and
n
, let

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}A(m,n)q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{mn}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}},

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}A^{\prime}(m,n)q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{m(n+1)}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}},

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}B(m,n)q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{mn}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n-3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}},

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}B^{\prime}(m,n)q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{m(n+1)}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+5};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}.

Lemma 3.2
.

We have

\sum_{n\geq 0}A(2,n)q^{n}=\frac{-q\bigl(1+q^{2}\bigr)}{(1-q)^{2}\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}

(3.1)

and for any positive integer
m\geq 2
,

\sum_{n\geq 0}A(2m,n)q^{n}=\frac{-1}{(1-q)^{2}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{n+1}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-2}}.

(3.2)

Proof.

Throughout we will use Heine’s transformations
[
15
, Appendix III, equations (III.1)–(III.2)]

\displaystyle{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{a,b}{c};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muz\biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{(b,az;q)_{\infty}}{(c,z;q)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{c/b,z}{az};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0mub\biggr],

(3.3)

\displaystyle=\frac{(c/b,bz;q)_{\infty}}{(c,z;q)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{abz/c,b}{bz};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muc/b\biggr],

(3.4)

where

{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{a,b}{c};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muz\biggr]=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{(a;q)_{n}(b;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}(c;q)_{n}}z^{n}.

We will also use the
q
-binomial theorem
[
4
, equation (2.2.1)]
,

\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}z^{n}=\frac{(az;q)_{\infty}}{(z;q)_{\infty}}

(3.5)

and Ramanujan’s
{}_{1}\psi_{1}
summation formula
[
15
, equation (5.2.1)]

\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(b;q)_{n}}z^{n}=\frac{(q,b/a,az,q/(az);q)_{\infty}}{(b,q/a,z,b/(az);q)_{\infty}}.

(3.6)

We start with (
3.1
). By (
1.1
) and (
3.4
), we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}A(2,n)q^{2n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n}\bigl(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n-1},q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{n}q^{2n}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-1},q}{q^{4}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{2}\biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\frac{\bigl(q^{3},q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-2},q}{q^{3}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{3}\biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{-q}{(1-q)^{3}}\left(1-\frac{q(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{2}\bigr)}{\bigl(1-q^{2}\bigr)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}\right)=\frac{-q\bigl(1+q^{2}\bigr)}{(1-q)^{2}\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}.

Now assume that
m
is a positive integer such that
m\geq 2
.
We get by using (
1.1
) and (
3.3
)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}A(2m,n)q^{n}={}

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2mn}\bigl(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n-1},q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{n}q^{2mn}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-1},q}{q^{4}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{2m}\biggr]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\frac{\bigl(q,q^{2m-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{4},q^{2m};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{3},q^{2m}}{q^{2m-1}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq\biggr]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{\bigl(q^{2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\frac{\bigl(q^{2m-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2m};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\frac{\bigl(q^{3},q^{2m};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{2m-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{n}\bigl(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+2m-1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n+3},q^{2n+2m};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{-q}{(1-q)^{2}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{n}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-2}}=\frac{-1}{(1-q)^{2}}\sum_{n\geq 1}\frac{q^{n}\bigl(q^{2n};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-2}},

which confirms (
3.2
).
∎

Lemma 3.3
.

For any positive integer, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}A^{\prime}(2m,n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{1-q^{3}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{n+2m}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+5};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}

(3.7)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{1-q}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{3n+2m}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}.

(3.8)

Proof.

We start with (
3.7
). We have by (
1.1
)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}A^{\prime}(2m,n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2m(n+1)}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=q^{2m}\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q,q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2m}\bigl(q,q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{6};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}

\displaystyle=q^{2m}\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q,q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q,q^3}{q^6};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{2m}\biggr]

\displaystyle=q^{2m}\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q,q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\frac{\bigl(q,q^{2m+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{6},q^{2m};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^5,q^{2m}}{q^{2m+3}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq\biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{2m}}{1-q^{3}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{n}\bigl(q^{2n+2},q^{2m+2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n+5},q^{2m+2n};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{1-q^{3}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2m+n}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+5};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}},

where in the fourth step we applied (
3.3
) with
(a,b,c,z)=\bigl(q^{3},q,q^{6},q^{2m}\bigr)
. This proves the desired result.

As for (
3.8
), we omit the details as the proof follows exactly the same steps as in the proof of (
3.7
) with the exception that (
3.3
)
is employed with
(a,b,c,z)=\bigl(q,q^{3},q^{6},q^{2m}\bigr)
rather than
{(a,b,c,z)=\bigl(q^{3},q,q^{6},q^{2m}\bigr)}
.
∎

Lemma 3.4
.

There holds

\sum_{n\geq 0}A^{\prime}(m,n)q^{n}=\sum_{n\geq 0}(A(m,n)-A(m+2,n))q^{n}
.

Proof.

We need the following contiguous relation which can be found in
[
17
, equation (2.1)]
:

{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{a,b}{c};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muz\biggr]-{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{a,b}{c};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muqz\biggr]=z\frac{(1-a)(1-b)}{1-c}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{qa,qb}{qc};q\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muz\biggr].

(3.9)

Then by (
3.9
) applied with
q\to q^{2}
and
(a,b,c,z)=\bigl(q^{-1},q,q^{4},q^{m}\bigr)
, we get

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}(A(m,n)-A(m+2,n))q^{n}

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\left({}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-1},q}{q^4};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{m}\biggr]-{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-1},q}{q^4};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{m+2}\biggr]\right)

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}q^{m}\frac{\bigl(1-q^{-1}\bigr)(1-q)}{1-q^{4}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q,q^3}{q^6};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{m}\biggr]

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q,q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{m(n+1)}\bigl(q,q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{6};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{m(n+1)}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+6};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}\bigl(q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}.

This proves the lemma.
∎

Lemma 3.5
.

We have

\sum_{n\geq 0}B(2,n)q^{n}=\frac{-q^{3}\bigl(1-q^{3}-q^{4}-q^{5}\bigr)}{\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)^{2}\bigl(1-q^{5}\bigr)}

(3.10)

and for any positive integer
m\geq 2
,

\sum_{n\geq 0}B(2m,n)q^{n}=\frac{1}{(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{3n+4}\bigl(q^{2n+2};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-1}}{\bigl(q^{2n+1};q^{2}\bigr)_{m-2}}.

(3.11)

By (
1.1
) and (
3.4
), we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}B(2,n)q^{2n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n}\bigl(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2n-3},q^{2n+3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-3},q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{\bigl(q^{-3},q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}q^{2n}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-3},q^{3};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-3},q^3}{q^{4}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq^{2}\biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{-1},q;q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}\frac{\bigl(q,q^{5};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{\bigl(q^{2},q^{4};q^{2}\bigr)_{\infty}}{}_{2}\phi_{1}\biggl[\genfrac{.}{.}{0.0pt}{}{q^{-2},q^3}{q^{5}};q^{2}\mathchar 59\relax\mkern 6.0muq\biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{4}}{(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)^{2}}\left(1+\frac{q\bigl(1-q^{-2}\bigr)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)}{\bigl(1-q^{2}\bigr)\bigl(1-q^{5}\bigr)}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{q^{4}}{(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)^{2}}-\frac{q^{3}}{(1-q)\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)\bigl(1-q^{5}\bigr)}=\frac{-q^{3}\bigl(1-q^{3}-q^{4}-q^{5}\bigr)}{\bigl(1-q^{3}\bigr)^{2}\bigl(1-q^{5}\bigr)}.

This proves (
3.10
).

Now assume that
m
is a positive integer such that
m\geq 2
. Then
by using (
1.1
) and (
3.3
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}B(2m,n)q^{n}
