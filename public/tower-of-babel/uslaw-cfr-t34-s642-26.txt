34 C.F.R. § 642.26 — How does the Secretary set the amount of a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.26
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary sets the amount of a grant on the basis of—
(1) 34 CFR 75.232 and 75.233, for a new grant; and
(2) 34 CFR 75.253, for the second year of a project period.
(b) The Secretary uses the available funds to set the amount of the grant at the lesser of—
(1) 170,000; or
(2) The amount requested by the applicant.
