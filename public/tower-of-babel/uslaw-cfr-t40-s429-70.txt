40 C.F.R. § 429.70 — Applicability; description of the wood preserving-water borne or nonpressure subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:429.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-429.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 429 (Timber Products Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges and to the introduction of process wastewater pollutants into publicly owned treatment works from all nonpressure wood preserving treatment processes and all pressure wood preserving treatment processes employing water borne inorganic salts.
