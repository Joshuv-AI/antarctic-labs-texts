27 C.F.R. § 10.24 — Sales promotion contests.
Citation: us:cfr:27:10.24
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-10.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 10 (Commercial Bribery). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sales contests sponsored by an industry member which offer prizes directly or indirectly to trade buyer officers, employees or representatives are inducements within the meaning of the Act.
