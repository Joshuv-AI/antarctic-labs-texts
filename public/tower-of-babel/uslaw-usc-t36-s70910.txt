36 U.S.C. § 70910 — Headquarters and principal office
Citation: us:usc:36:70910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70910
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 709 (Future Farmers Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The headquarters and principal office of the FFA shall be as provided in the constitution or bylaws of the FFA. The activities of the FFA may be conducted throughout the States, territories, and possessions of the United States.
