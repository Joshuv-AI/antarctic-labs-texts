7 C.F.R. § 915.31 — Expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:915.31
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-915.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 915 (Avocados Grown In South Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The members of the committee and their respective alternates when performing duties at the direction of the committee, shall be reimbursed for expenses necessarily incurred by them in the performance of their duties under this part.
