31 C.F.R. § 7.5 — Responsibilities of employees.
Citation: us:cfr:31:7.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-7.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 7 (Employee Inventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All employees are required to report to the heads of their bureaus or offices any result of research, development, or other activity on their part which may constitute an invention and the circumstances under which this possible invention came into being.
