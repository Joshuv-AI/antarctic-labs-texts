31 U.S.C. § 3304 — Transfers of public money from depositaries
Citation: us:usc:31:3304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3304
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 33 (Depositing, Keeping, And Paying Money); Subchapter I (Deposits And Depositaries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury may transfer public money in the possession of a depositary—
(1)
to the Treasury; and
(2)
if the Secretary believes the safety of the public money and convenience require it, to another depositary.
