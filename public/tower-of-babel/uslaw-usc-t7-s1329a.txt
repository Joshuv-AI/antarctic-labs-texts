7 U.S.C. § 1329a — Discontinuance of acreage allotments on corn
Citation: us:usc:7:1329a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1329a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part B (Marketing Quotas); Subpart ii (Acreage Allotments—Corn). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, acreage allotments and a commercial corn-producing area shall not be established for the 1959 and subsequent crops of corn.
