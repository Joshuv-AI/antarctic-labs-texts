31 C.F.R. § 8.61 — Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:8.61
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-8.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 8 (Practice Before The Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco And Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A respondent or proposed respondent may appear in person or be represented by counsel or other representative who need not be enrolled to practice before the Bureau. The Director of Practice may be represented by an Attorney or other employee of the Treasury Department.
