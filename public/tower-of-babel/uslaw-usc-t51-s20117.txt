51 U.S.C. § 20117 — Disposal of excess land
Citation: us:usc:51:20117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section20117
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 201 (National Aeronautics And Space Program); Subchapter II (Coordination Of Aeronautical And Space Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the provisions of this or any other law, the Administration may not report to a disposal agency as excess to the needs of the Administration any land having an estimated value in excess of $50,000 that is owned by the United States and under the jurisdiction and control of the Administration, unless—
(1)
a period of 30 days has passed after the receipt by the Speaker and the Committee on Science and Technology of the House of Representatives and the President and the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate of a report by the Administrator or the Administrator’s designee containing a full and complete statement of the action proposed to be taken and the facts and circumstances relied upon in support of such action; or
(2)
each such committee before the expiration of that period has transmitted to the Administrator written notice to the effect that the committee has no objection to the proposed action.
