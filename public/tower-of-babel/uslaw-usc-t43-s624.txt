43 U.S.C. § 624 — Entry of approval on land records
Citation: us:usc:43:624
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section624
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 13 (Federal Lands Included In State Irrigation Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the approval of the district map or plat as hereinbefore provided by the Secretary of the Interior the officer designated by the Secretary of the Interior will note said approval upon his records where any unentered or entered and unpatented lands are affected.
