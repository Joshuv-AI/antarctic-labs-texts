43 C.F.R. § 5511.3-5 — -5 Removal of improvements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5511.3-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5511.3-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5510 (Free Use Of Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon expiration of the permit period the permittee will be given 90 days to remove equipment, personal property and any improvements he has placed on the land, except roads, culverts and bridges are to be left in place, in good condition and will become the property of the United States upon expiration of the 90-day removal period.
