14 C.F.R. § 194.251 — Alternate means to satisfy minimum curriculum content in training courses under part 142 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:14:194.251
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-194.251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 194 (Special Federal Aviation Regulation No. 120—Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification And Training; Operations Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A training course for which approval is requested is not required to consist of training on a task specified in an area of operation if the powered-lift is not capable of performing the task, provided the FAA has issued waiver authority for that task in accordance with § 194.207(b).
