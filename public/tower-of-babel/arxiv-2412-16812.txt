Equivalence of D and D’ properties in Banach spaces
Paulo Akira F. Enabe

Abstract
This work explores the equivalence of two sequential properties, 𝒟 \mathcal{D} and 𝒟 ′ \mathcal{D}^{\prime} , for dual Banach spaces under the weak* topology. Property 𝒟 \mathcal{D} ensures that any totally scalarly measurable function is also scalarly measurable, while property 𝒟 ′ \mathcal{D}^{\prime} states that every weakly* sequentially closed subspace of X ∗ X^{*} is weakly* closed. These properties, which are central to the study of the interplay between topology and measurability in Banach spaces, were originally investigated in [ 1 ] , where their equivalence was left as an open question. By examining the topological and measurable structures induced by the Baire σ \sigma -algebra, we prove that properties 𝒟 \mathcal{D} and 𝒟 ′ \mathcal{D}^{\prime} are indeed equivalent. The proof utilizes the relationship between total sets, weak* closures, and scalar measurability, extending previous results on sequential properties of dual Banach spaces. Additionally, we revisit the failure of property 𝒟 \mathcal{D} in nonseparable Banach spaces with M M -basic ℓ 1 + \ell_{1}^{+} -systems, providing a topological reinterpretation of this phenomenon. These findings contribute to a deeper understanding of the weak* topology and measurable mappings in Banach space theory.
