15 U.S.C. § 5522 — National Aeronautics and Space Administration activities
Citation: us:usc:15:5522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5522
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 81 (High-Performance Computing); Subchapter II (Agency Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As part of the Program described in subchapter I, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration shall conduct basic and applied research in networking and information technology, particularly in the field of computational science, with emphasis on aerospace sciences, earth and space sciences, and remote exploration and experimentation.
