39 U.S.C. § 405 — Printing of illustrations of United States postage stamps
Citation: us:usc:39:405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section405
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 4 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
When requested by the Postal Service, the Director of the Government Publishing Office shall print, as a public document for sale by the Superintendent of Documents, illustrations in black and white or in color of postage stamps of the United States, together with such descriptive, historical, and philatelic information with regard to the stamps as the Postal Service deems suitable.
(b)
Notwithstanding the provisions of section 505 of title 44, stereotype or electrotype plates, or duplicates thereof, used in the publications authorized to be printed by this section may not be sold or otherwise disposed of.
