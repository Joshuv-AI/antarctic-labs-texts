7 C.F.R. § 930.256 — Free and restricted percentages for the 2024-2025 crop year.
Citation: us:cfr:7:930.256
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-930.256
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 930 (Tart Cherries Grown In The States Of Michigan, New York, Pennsylvania, Oregon, Utah, Washington, And Wisconsin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The percentages for tart cherries handled by handlers during the crop year beginning on July 1, 2024, which shall be free and restricted, respectively, are designated as follows: Free percentage, 81 percent and restricted percentage, 19 percent.
