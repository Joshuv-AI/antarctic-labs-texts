5 U.S.C. § 5946 — Membership fees; expenses of attendance at meetings; limitations
Citation: us:usc:5:5946
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5946
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 59 (Allowances); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as authorized by a specific appropriation, by express terms in a general appropriation, or by sections 4109 and 4110 of this title, appropriated funds may not be used for payment of—
(1)
membership fees or dues of an employee as defined by section 2105 of this title or an individual employed by the government of the District of Columbia in a society or association; or
(2)
expenses of attendance of an individual at meetings or conventions of members of a society or association.
This section does not prevent the use of appropriations for the Department of Agriculture for expenses incident to the delivery of lectures, the giving of instructions, or the acquiring of information at meetings by its employees on subjects relating to the authorized work of the Department.
