46 C.F.R. § 188.10-43 — -43 Liquefied flammable gas.
Citation: us:cfr:46:188.10-43
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-188.10-43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 188 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This term means any flammable gas having a Reid vapor pressure exceeding 40 p.s.i. which has been liquefied.
