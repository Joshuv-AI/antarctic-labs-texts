4 C.F.R. § 11.1 — Right to representation before the Government Accountability Office.
Citation: us:cfr:4:11.1
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-11.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 11 (Recognition Of Attorneys And Other Representatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person having a claim or other rights assertable in the Government Accountability Office may pursue such claim or right individually or through an attorney or other representative.
