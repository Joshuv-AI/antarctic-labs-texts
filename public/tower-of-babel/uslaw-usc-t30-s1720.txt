30 U.S.C. § 1720 — Criminal penalties
Citation: us:usc:30:1720
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1720
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 29 (Oil And Gas Royalty Management); Subchapter I (Federal Royalty Management And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who commits an act for which a civil penalty is provided in section 1719(d) of this title shall, upon conviction, be punished by a fine of not more than $50,000, or by imprisonment for not more than 2 years, or both.
