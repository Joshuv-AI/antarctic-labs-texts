44 C.F.R. § 321.6 — Participation of small business.
Citation: us:cfr:44:321.6
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-321.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 321 (Maintenance Of The Mobilization Base (Department Of Defense, Department Of Energy, Maritime Administration)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agencies concerned with the order shall, in all of their programs for maintaining the mobilization base, be mindful of the national policy to protect the interests of small business, and to assure the maximum participation of small business in the mobilization base, including current procurement.
