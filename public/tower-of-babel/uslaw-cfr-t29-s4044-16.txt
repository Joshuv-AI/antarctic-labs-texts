29 C.F.R. § 4044.16 — Priority category 6 benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4044.16
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4044.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4044 (Allocation Of Assets In Single-Employer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The benefits assigned to priority category 6 with respect to each participant are all of the participant's benefits under the plan, whether forfeitable or nonforfeitable.
