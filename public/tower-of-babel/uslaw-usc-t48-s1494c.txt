48 U.S.C. § 1494c — Drug Enforcement Agency personnel assignments
Citation: us:usc:48:1494c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1494c
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 10 (Territorial Provisions Of A General Nature). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To assist in the enforcement of the controlled substances laws of the United States in coordination with law enforcement officers in insular areas in the eastern Caribbean and in the central and western Pacific, the Administrator of the Drug Enforcement Administration shall assign appropriate personnel and other resources to the Virgin Islands and Guam.
