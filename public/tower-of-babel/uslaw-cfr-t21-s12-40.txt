21 C.F.R. § 12.40 — Appearance.
Citation: us:cfr:21:12.40
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-12.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 12 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A person who has filed a notice of participation under § 12.45 may appear in person or by counsel or other representative in any hearing and, subject to § 12.89, may be heard concerning all relevant issues.
(b) The presiding officer may strike a person's appearance for violation of the rules of conduct in § 12.90.
