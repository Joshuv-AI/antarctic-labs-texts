36 C.F.R. § 228.50 — Production records.
Citation: us:cfr:36:228.50
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-228.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 228 (Minerals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At least annually, the purchaser or permittee must furnish a record of the volume extracted, in cubic yards or weight equivalent, to the authorized officer. The units of measurement must correspond to the units used in the appraisal or estimate.
