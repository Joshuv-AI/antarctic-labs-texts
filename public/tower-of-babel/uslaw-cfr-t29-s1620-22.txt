29 C.F.R. § 1620.22 — Employment cost not a “factor other than sex.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:1620.22
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1620.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1620 (The Equal Pay Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A wage differential based on claimed differences between the average cost of employing workers of one sex as a group and the average cost of employing workers of the opposite sex as a group is discriminatory and does not qualify as a differential based on any “factor other than sex,” and will result in a violation of the equal pay provisions, if the equal pay standard otherwise applies.
