29 C.F.R. § 500.5 — Filing of applications, notices and documents.
Citation: us:cfr:29:500.5
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-500.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 500 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise prescribed herein, all applications, notices and other documents required or permitted to be filed by these regulations shall be filed in accordance with the provisions of subpart F of the regulations.
