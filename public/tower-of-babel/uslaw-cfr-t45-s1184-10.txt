45 C.F.R. § 1184.10 — Disclaimer.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1184.10
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1184.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1184 (Implementation Of The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in the regulations in this part will be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under FOIA.
