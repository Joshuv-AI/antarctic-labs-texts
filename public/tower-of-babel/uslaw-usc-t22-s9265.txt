22 U.S.C. § 9265 — Notification of termination or suspension of sanctions
Citation: us:usc:22:9265
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9265
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 99 (North Korea Sanctions And Policy Enhancement); Subchapter V (Authorities And Requirements Related To Expanded Sanctions); Part I (Congressional Review And Oversight). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Before taking any action to terminate or suspend the application of sanctions under this subtitle 1 or an amendment made by this subtitle, the President shall notify the appropriate congressional committees of the President’s intent to take the action and the reasons for the action.
