19 U.S.C. § 391 — Ascertainment of duties on grain
Citation: us:usc:19:391
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section391
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions); Part 3 (Ascertainment, Collection, And Recovery Of Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of estimating the duties on importations of grain, the number of bushels shall be ascertained by weight, instead of by measuring; and sixty pounds of wheat, fifty-six pounds of corn, fifty-six pounds of rye, forty-eight pounds of barley, thirty-two pounds of oats, sixty pounds of peas, and forty-two pounds of buckwheat, avoirdupois weight, shall respectively be estimated as a bushel.
