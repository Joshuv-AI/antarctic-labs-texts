Shift-generated α \alpha -homogeneous classes of jointly measurable Random Fields
Enkelejd Hashorva

Abstract




Abstract:

We consider a class of shift-generated
\alpha
-homogeneous random fields (RFs)
\mathcal{C}[Z]
, defined through a functional identity involving a fixed
\alpha>0
and a given jointly measurable
\mathbb{R}^{d}
-valued RF
Z(t)
,
t\in\mathbb{R}^{\ell}
. The significance of such classes lies in the fact that their elements generate max-stable and stationary RFs.

We extend the original functional identity to a broad class of functionals, including the integral operator
\mathcal{S}(\cdot)
, and prove that
\mathcal{C}[Z]
contains at least one
L^{\alpha}
-continuous element. This enables us to show that
\mathcal{S}(Z)
is strictly positive — a key step in explicitly describing several elements of
\mathcal{C}[Z]
and in constructing RFs
Z
for which
\mathcal{C}[Z]
is shift-generated.

Finally, we investigate properties of local RFs and their connections with spectral tail and tail RFs.

Key words
:
Shift-generated random fields, functional identity,
tail random fields, spectral tail random fields, local tail random fields,
max-stable random fields, Brown-Resnick shift-generated class.

AMS 2020 subject classification
:
60G60, 60G70, 60G15.
