47 C.F.R. § 73.606 — Table of allotments.
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.606
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The table of allotments set forth in § 73.622(j) contains the channels designated for the listed communities in the United States, its Territories, and possessions. Channels designated with an asterisk are assigned for use by noncommercial educational broadcast stations only.
