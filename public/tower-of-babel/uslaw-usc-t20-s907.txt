20 U.S.C. § 907 — Applicability of other laws
Citation: us:usc:20:907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section907
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 25 (Pay And Personnel Program For Overseas Teachers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of any teacher who—
(1)
is performing services as a teacher at the close of a school year,
(2)
agrees in writing to serve as a teacher for the next school year, and
(3)
is employed in another position in the recess period immediately preceding such next school year, or, during such recess period, receives quarters, allowances, or additional compensation referred to in sections 905 and 906 of this title, or both, as the case may be,
section 5533 of title 5 shall not apply to such teacher by reason of any such employment during a recess period or any such receipt of quarters, allowances, or additional compensation, or both, as the case may be.
