21 C.F.R. § 801.116 — Medical devices having commonly known directions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:801.116
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-801.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 801 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A device shall be exempt from section 502(f)(1) of the act insofar as adequate directions for common uses thereof are known to the ordinary individual.
