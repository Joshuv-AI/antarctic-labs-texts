12 U.S.C. § 2274 — Power to remove directors and officers
Citation: us:usc:12:2274
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2274
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter V (Farm Credit Administration Organization); Part C (Enforcement Powers Of Farm Credit Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a Farm Credit Bank board, officer, or employee shall not remove any director or officer of any association.
