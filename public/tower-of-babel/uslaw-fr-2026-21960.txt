Administrative Declaration Amendment of a Disaster for the State 
of Colorado

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a correction to the Administrative declaration of 
disaster for the State of Colorado dated October 14, 2026.
    Incident: Wildfires, Flooding, and Mudslides.

DATES: Issued on October 23, 2026.
    Incident Period: June 27, 2026 through July 29, 2026.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: December 14, 2026.
    Economic Injury (EIDL) Loan Application Deadline Date: July 14, 
2027.

ADDRESSES: Visit the MySBA Loan Portal at https://lending.sba.gov to 
apply for a disaster assistance loan.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Sharon Henderson, Office of Disaster 
Recovery, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street SW, Suite 
6050, Washington, DC 20416, (202) 205-6734.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of the Administrative declaration 
for the State of Colorado, dated October 14, 2026, published in the 
Federal Register on October 19, 2026 at 91 FR 66191, is hereby 
corrected to update the incident period for this disaster as beginning 
June 27, 2026 and continuing through July 29, 2026.
    All other information in the original declaration remains 
unchanged.
