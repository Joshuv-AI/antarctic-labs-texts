32 C.F.R. § 1615.7 — Evidence of registration.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1615.7
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1615.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1615 (Administration Of Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of Selective Service Shall issue to each registrant written evidence of his registration. The Director of Selective Service will replace that evidence upon written request of the registrant, but such request will not be granted more often than once in any period of six months.
