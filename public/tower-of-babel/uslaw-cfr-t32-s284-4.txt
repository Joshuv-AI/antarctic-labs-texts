32 C.F.R. § 284.4 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:284.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-284.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 284 (Waiver Procedures For Debts Resulting From Erroneous Payments Of Pay And Allowances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DoD policy under 32 CFR part 283 that waiver applications for debts resulting from erroneous payments of pay and allowances (hereafter referred to as “waiver applications”) be processed according to all pertinent statutes, regulations, and other relevant authorities.
