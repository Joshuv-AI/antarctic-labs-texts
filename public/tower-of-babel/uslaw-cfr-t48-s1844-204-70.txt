48 C.F.R. § 1844.204-70 — 1844.204-70 NASA contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1844.204-70
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1844.204-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1844 (Subcontracting Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 1852.244-70, Geographic Participation in the Aerospace Program, in all research and development solicitations and contracts of $500,000 or over that will be performed within the United States.
