21 C.F.R. § 806.30 — FDA access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:21:806.30
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-806.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 806 (Medical Devices; Reports Of Corrections And Removals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each device manufacturer or importer required under this part to maintain records and every person who is in charge or custody of such records shall, upon request of an officer or employee designated by FDA and under section 704(e) of the act, permit such officer or employee at all reasonable times to have access to, and to copy and verify, such records and reports.
