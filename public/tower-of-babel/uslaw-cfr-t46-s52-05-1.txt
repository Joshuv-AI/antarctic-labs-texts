46 C.F.R. § 52.05-1 — -1 General (modifies PW-1 through PW-54).
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Boilers and component parts, including piping, that are fabricated by welding must be as indicated in PW-1 through PW-54 of Section I of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 52.01-1) except as noted otherwise in this subpart.
