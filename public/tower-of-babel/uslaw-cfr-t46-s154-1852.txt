46 C.F.R. § 154.1852 — Air breathing equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1852
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1852
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The master shall ensure that a licensed officer inspects the compressed air breathing equipment at least once each month.
(b) The master shall enter in the vessel's log a record of the inspection required under paragraph (a) of this section that includes:
(1) The date of the inspection; and
(2) The condition of the equipment at the time of the inspection.
