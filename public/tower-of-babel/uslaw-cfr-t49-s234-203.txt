49 C.F.R. § 234.203 — Control circuits.
Citation: us:cfr:49:234.203
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-234.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 234 (Grade Crossing Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All control circuits that affect the safe operation of a highway-rail grade crossing warning system shall operate on the fail-safe principle.
