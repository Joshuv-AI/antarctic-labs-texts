37 C.F.R. § 353.2 — Form and content of rehearing motions.
Citation: us:cfr:37:353.2
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-353.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 353 (Rehearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A motion for rehearing shall not exceed 10 pages in length and must set forth, in the beginning of its text, a brief summary statement of the aspects of the determination believed by the moving participant to be without evidentiary support in the record or contrary to legal requirements.
