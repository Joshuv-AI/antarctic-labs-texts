7 C.F.R. § 920.213 — Assessment rate.
Citation: us:cfr:7:920.213
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-920.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 920 (Kiwifruit Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On and after August 1, 2022, an assessment rate of $0.035 per 9-kilo volume-fill container or equivalent of kiwifruit is established for kiwifruit grown in California.
