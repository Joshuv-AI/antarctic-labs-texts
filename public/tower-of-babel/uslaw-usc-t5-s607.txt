5 U.S.C. § 607 — Preparation of analyses
Citation: us:usc:5:607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section607
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 6 (The Analysis Of Regulatory Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In complying with the provisions of sections 603 and 604 of this title, an agency may provide either a quantifiable or numerical description of the effects of a proposed rule or alternatives to the proposed rule, or more general descriptive statements if quantification is not practicable or reliable.
