7 C.F.R. § 4290.480 — Prior approval of changes to RBIC's business plan.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.480
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Without the Agency's prior written approval, no change in your business plan, upon which you were selected and licensed as a RBIC, may take effect.
