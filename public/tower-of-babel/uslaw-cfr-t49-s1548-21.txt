49 C.F.R. § 1548.21 — Screening of cargo.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1548.21
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1548.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1548 (Indirect Air Carrier Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An IAC may only screen cargo for transport on a passenger aircraft under §§ 1544.205 and 1546.205 if the IAC is a certified cargo screening facility as provided in part 1549.
