15 U.S.C. § 6407 — Lobbying restrictions
Citation: us:usc:15:6407
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6407
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 90 (Propane Education And Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds collected by the Council shall be used in any manner for influencing legislation or elections, except that the Council may recommend to the Secretary changes in this chapter or other statutes that would further the purposes of this chapter.
