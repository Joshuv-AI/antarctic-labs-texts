43 C.F.R. § 3602.25 — What rights and responsibilities does my assignee assume?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3602.25
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3602.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When BLM approves your assignment, your assignee is entitled to all the rights and is subject to all the obligations under the contract.
