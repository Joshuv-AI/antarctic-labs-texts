46 C.F.R. § 535.902 — Falsification of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:46:535.902
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-535.902
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 535 (Ocean Common Carrier And Marine Terminal Operator Agreements Subject To The Shipping Act Of 1984). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Knowing falsification of any report required by the Act or this part, including knowing falsification of any item in any applicable agreement information and/or reporting requirements pursuant to subparts E and G of this part, is a violation of the rules of this part and is subject to the civil penalties set forth in section 13(a) of the Act (46 U.S.C. 41107) and may be subject to the criminal penalties provided for in 18 U.S.C. 1001.
