5 C.F.R. § 2608.201 — General prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2608.201
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2608.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2608 (Testimony By Oge Employees Relating To Official Information And Production Of Official Records In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee may produce official records and information or provide any testimony relating to official information in response to a demand or request without the prior, written approval of the General Counsel.
