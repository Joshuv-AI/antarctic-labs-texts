36 C.F.R. § 1206.12 — What are my responsibilities once I have received a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.12
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Comply with all Federal regulations about grants administration that are contained in § 1206.72 and 2 CFR part 200.
(b) Comply with NHPRC grant announcements and other Commission guidance.
(c) Meet performance requirements defined in your grant application.
(d) Report on performance requirements defined in your grant application and other performance measures specified in the grant award.
(e) Comply with conditions set by the Commission according to § 1206.52.
