45 C.F.R. § 63.37 — Leasing facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:45:63.37
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-63.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 63 (Grant Programs Administered By The Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Planning And Evaluation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of a project involving the leasing of a facility, the grantee shall demonstrate that it will have the right to occupy, to operate, and, if necessary, to maintain and improve the leased facility during the proposed period of the project.
