33 C.F.R. § 150.710 — What are the requirements for supplying power to aids to navigation?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.710
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The power of all navigation aids must be maintained, at all times, at or above the level recommended by the equipment's manufacturer.
