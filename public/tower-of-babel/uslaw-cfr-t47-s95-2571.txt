47 C.F.R. § 95.2571 — MedRadio emission types.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.2571
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.2571
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A MedRadio station may transmit any emission type appropriate for communications in this service. Voice communications, however, are prohibited.
