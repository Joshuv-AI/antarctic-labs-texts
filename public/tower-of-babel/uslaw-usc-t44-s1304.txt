44 U.S.C. § 1304 — Army and Navy registers
Citation: us:usc:44:1304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1304
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 13 (Particular Reports And Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the usual number of the registers of the Army and Navy, fifteen hundred copies of each shall be printed: five hundred for the Senate, and one thousand for the House of Representatives.
