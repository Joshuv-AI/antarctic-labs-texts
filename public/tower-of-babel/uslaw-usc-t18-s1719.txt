18 U.S.C. § 1719 — Franking privilege
Citation: us:usc:18:1719
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1719
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 83 (Postal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever makes use of any official envelope, label, or indorsement authorized by law, to avoid the payment of postage or registry fee on his private letter, packet, package, or other matter in the mail, shall be fined under this title.
