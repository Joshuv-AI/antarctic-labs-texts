46 U.S.C. § 12301 — Numbering vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:12301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12301
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 123 (Numbering Undocumented Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An undocumented vessel equipped with propulsion machinery of any kind shall have a number issued by the proper issuing authority in the State in which the vessel principally is operated.
(b)
The Secretary may require an undocumented barge of more than 100 gross tons operating on the navigable waters of the United States to be numbered.
