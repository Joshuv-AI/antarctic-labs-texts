46 C.F.R. § 139.155 — Appeals of suspension or revocation of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:46:139.155
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-139.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 139 (Third-Party Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anyone directly affected by a decision to suspend or revoke an approval granted under this part may appeal the decision to the Coast Guard in accordance with the provisions of 46 CFR subpart 1.03.
