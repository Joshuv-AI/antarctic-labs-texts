25 C.F.R. § 571.9 — Subpoena of documents and other items.
Citation: us:cfr:25:571.9
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-571.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 571 (Monitoring And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By majority vote the Commission may authorize the Chairman to require by subpoena the production of certain documents and other items that are material and relevant to facts in issue in any matter under consideration or investigation by the Commission.
