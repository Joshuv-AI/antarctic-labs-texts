41 C.F.R. § 102-37.430 — -37.430 Authority for donations to public bodies.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.430
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 40 U.S.C. 527, the abandonment, destruction, or donation to public bodies of property which has no commercial value or for which the estimated cost of continued care and handling would exceed the estimated proceeds from its sale.
