10 U.S.C. § 1786 — Dependent student travel within the United States
Citation: us:usc:10:1786
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1786
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 88 (Military Family Programs And Military Child Care); Subchapter I (Military Family Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available to the Department of Defense for the travel and transportation of dependent students of members of the armed forces stationed overseas may be obligated for transportation allowances for travel within or between the contiguous States.
