40 C.F.R. § 62.14715a — a What must I include in the deviation report?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14715a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14715a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In each report required under § 62.14710a, for any pollutant or parameter that deviated from the emission limitations or operating limits specified in this subpart, include the four items described in paragraphs (a) through (d) of this section.
(a) The calendar dates and times your unit deviated from the emission limitations or operating limit requirements;
(b) The averaged and recorded data for the dates in paragraph (a) of this section;
(c) Duration and causes of the following:
(1) Each deviation from the emission limitations or operating limits and your corrective actions; and
(2) Bypass events and your corrective actions. Include the calendar date and start time of the bypass event; and
(d) A copy of the operating limit monitoring data during each deviation and, for any test report that documents the emission levels, the process unit(s) tested, the pollutant(s) tested and the date that the performance test was conducted. Submit, following the procedure specified in § 62.14730a(b)(1), the performance test report no later than the date that you submit the deviation report.
