10 C.F.R. § 61.58 — Alternative requirements for waste classification and characteristics.
Citation: us:cfr:10:61.58
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-61.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 61 (Licensing Requirements For Land Disposal Of Radioactive Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, upon request or on its own initiative, authorize other provisions for the classification and characteristics of waste on a specific basis, if, after evaluation, of the specific characteristics of the waste, disposal site, and method of disposal, it finds reasonable assurance of compliance with the performance objectives in subpart C of this part.
