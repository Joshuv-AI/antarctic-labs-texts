42 U.S.C. § 6618 — Major science and technology proposals
Citation: us:usc:42:6618
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6618
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 79 (Science And Technology Policy, Organization And Priorities); Subchapter II (Office Of Science And Technology Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall identify and provide an annual report to Congress on each major multinational science and technology project, in which the United States is not a participant, which has a total estimated cost greater than $1,000,000,000.
