45 C.F.R. § 1149.54 — Are there sanctions for misconduct?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.54
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The ALJ may sanction a person, including any party or representative, as outlined in § 1149.55, for the following:
(1) Failing to comply with an order, rule, or procedure governing the proceeding;
(2) Failing to prosecute or defend an action; or
(3) Engaging in other misconduct that interferes with the speedy, orderly, and fair conduct of a hearing.
(b) Any sanction issued under this section must reasonably relate to the severity and nature of the misconduct.
