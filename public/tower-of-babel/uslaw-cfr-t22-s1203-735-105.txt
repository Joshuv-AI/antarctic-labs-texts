22 C.F.R. § 1203.735-105 — -105 Disciplinary action.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1203.735-105
Creator: United States International Development Cooperation Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1203.735-105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1203 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A violation of the regulations in this part by an employee or special Government employee may be cause for appropriate disciplinary action, including separation for cause, which may be in addition to any penalty prescribed by law.
