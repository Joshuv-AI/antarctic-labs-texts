25 U.S.C. § 14b — Disposition of funds received from public for goods and services provided by Bureau of Indian Affairs
Citation: us:usc:25:14b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section14b
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to retain collections from the public in payment for goods and services provided by the Bureau of Indian Affairs. Such collections shall be credited to the appropriation account against which obligations were incurred in providing such goods and services.
