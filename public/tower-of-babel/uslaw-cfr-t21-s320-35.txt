21 C.F.R. § 320.35 — Requirements for in vitro testing of each batch.
Citation: us:cfr:21:320.35
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-320.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 320 (Bioavailability And Bioequivalence Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a bioequivalence requirement specifies a currently available in vitro test or an in vitro bioequivalence standard comparing the drug product to a reference standard, the manufacturer shall conduct the test on a sample of each batch of the drug product to assure batch-to-batch uniformity.
