42 U.S.C. § 300b–2 — Voluntary participation by individuals
Citation: us:usc:42:300b–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300b–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IX (Genetic Diseases, Hemophilia Programs, And Sudden Infant Death Syndrome); Part A (Genetic Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The participation by any individual in any program or portion thereof under this part shall be wholly voluntary and shall not be a prerequisite to eligibility for or receipt of any other service or assistance from, or to participation in, any other program.
