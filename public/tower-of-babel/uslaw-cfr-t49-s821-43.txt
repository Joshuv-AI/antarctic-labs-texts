49 C.F.R. § 821.43 — Effect of law judge's initial decision or appealable order and appeal therefrom.
Citation: us:cfr:49:821.43
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-821.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 821 (Rules Of Practice In Air Safety Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If no appeal from the law judge's initial decision or appealable order is timely filed, the initial decision or order shall become final with respect to the parties, but shall not be binding precedent for the Board. The filing of a timely notice of appeal with the Board shall stay the effectiveness of the law judge's initial decision or order, unless the basis for the decision or order is that the Board lacks jurisdiction.
