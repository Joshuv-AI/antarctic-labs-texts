34 U.S.C. § 10641 — Grant authority
Citation: us:usc:34:10641
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10641
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXXII (Crime Free Rural State Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall award grants to rural State criminal justice agencies, Byrne agencies, or other agencies as designated by the Governor of that State and approved by the Attorney General, to develop rural States’ capacity to assist local communities in the prevention and reduction of crime, violence, and substance abuse.
