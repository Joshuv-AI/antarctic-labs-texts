49 C.F.R. § 213.319 — Drainage.
Citation: us:cfr:49:213.319
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-213.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 213 (Track Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each drainage or other water carrying facility under or immediately adjacent to the roadbed shall be maintained and kept free of obstruction, to accommodate expected water flow for the area concerned.
