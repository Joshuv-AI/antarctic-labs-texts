39 C.F.R. § 965.2 — Scope of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:39:965.2
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-965.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 965 (Mail Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this part shall be applicable to mail dispute cases forwarded to the Judicial Officer pursuant to Postal Operations Manual section 616.21.
