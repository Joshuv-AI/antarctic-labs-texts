7 C.F.R. § 1230.611 — Porcine animal.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1230.611
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1230.611
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1230 (Pork Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Porcine animal means a swine, that is raised:
(a) As a feeder pig, that is, a young pig sold to another person to be finished over a period of more than 1 month for slaughtering;
(b) For breeding purposes as seedstock and included in the breeding herd; and
(c) As a market hog, slaughtered by the producer or sold to be slaughtered, usually within 1 month of such transfer.
