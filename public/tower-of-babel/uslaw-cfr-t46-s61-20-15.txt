46 C.F.R. § 61.20-15 — -15 Tailshaft examination.
Citation: us:cfr:46:61.20-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-61.20-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 61 (Periodic Tests And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in §§ 61.20-15 through 61.20-23 apply only to vessels in ocean and coastwise service. Each examination, inspection and test prescribed by these sections must be conducted in the presence of a marine inspector.
