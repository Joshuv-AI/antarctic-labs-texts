11 C.F.R. § 110.10 — Expenditures by candidates.
Citation: us:cfr:11:110.10
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-110.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 110 (Contribution And Expenditure Limitations And Prohibitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 11 CFR parts 9001, et seq. and 9031, et seq., candidates for Federal office may make unlimited expenditures from personal funds as defined in 11 CFR 100.33.
