7 C.F.R. § 906.29 — Expenses and compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:906.29
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-906.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 906 (Oranges And Grapefruit Grown In Lower Rio Grande Valley In Texas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The members of the committee, and alternates, shall serve without compensation; but they may be reimbursed for expenses necessarily incurred by them in the performance of their duties and in the exercise of their powers under this subpart.
