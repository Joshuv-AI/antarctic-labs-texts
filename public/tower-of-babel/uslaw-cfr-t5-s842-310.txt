5 C.F.R. § 842.310 — Service not creditable because of an election under part 847 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:5:842.310
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-842.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 842 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Basic Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any FERS service which becomes creditable under a retirement system established for nonappropriated fund employees due to an election made under part 847 of this chapter is not creditable for any purpose under FERS.
