Characterization of the W 1 + ∞ W_{1+\infty} -n-algebra and applications
Fridolin Melong and Raimar Wulkenhaar

Abstract
. In this paper, we construct the W 1 + ∞ W_{1+\infty} -n-algebras in the framework of the generalized quantum algebra. We characterize the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -multi-variable W 1 + ∞ W_{1+\infty} -algebra and derive its n n -algebra which is the generalized Lie algebra for n n even. Furthermore, we investigate the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -elliptic hermitian matrix model and determine a toy model for the generalized quantum W ∞ W_{\infty} constraints. Also, we deduce particular cases of our results. Keywords. Quantum algebra, ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -calculus, Conformal and W symmetry, n-algebra, matrix models.
