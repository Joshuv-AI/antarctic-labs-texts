42 C.F.R. § 50.201 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:50.201
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-50.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 50 (Policies Of General Applicability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to programs or projects for health services which are supported in whole or in part by Federal financial assistance, whether by grant or contract, administered by the Public Health Service.
