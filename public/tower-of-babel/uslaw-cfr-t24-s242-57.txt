24 C.F.R. § 242.57 — Maintenance of hospital facility.
Citation: us:cfr:24:242.57
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-242.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 242 (Mortgage Insurance For Hospitals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mortgagor shall maintain the hospital's grounds, buildings, and the equipment financed with mortgage proceeds in good repair, and shall promptly complete such repairs and maintenance as HUD considers necessary.
