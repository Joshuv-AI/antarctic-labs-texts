36 C.F.R. § 2.37 — Noncommercial soliciting.
Citation: us:cfr:36:2.37
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-2.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 2 (Resource Protection, Public Use And Recreation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Soliciting or demanding gifts, money, goods or services is prohibited, except pursuant to the terms and conditions of a permit that has been issued under § 2.50, § 2.51 or § 2.52.
