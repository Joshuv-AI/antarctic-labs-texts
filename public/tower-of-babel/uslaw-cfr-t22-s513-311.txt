22 C.F.R. § 513.311 — Investigation and referral.
Citation: us:cfr:22:513.311
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-513.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 513 (Government Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement) And Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Grants)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information concerning the existence of a cause for debarment from any source shall be promptly reported, investigated, and referred, when appropriate, to the debarring official for consideration. After consideration, the debarring official may issue a notice of proposed debarment.
