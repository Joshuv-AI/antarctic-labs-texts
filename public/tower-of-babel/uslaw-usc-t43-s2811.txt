43 U.S.C. § 2811 — Private sector
Citation: us:usc:43:2811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2811
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 46 (Geospatial Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Committee and each covered agency may, to the maximum extent practical, rely upon and use the private sector in the United States for the provision of geospatial data and services.
