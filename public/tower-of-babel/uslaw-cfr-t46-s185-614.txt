46 C.F.R. § 185.614 — Portable watertight containers for distress flares and smoke signals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.614
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.614
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Portable watertight containers for distress flares and smoke signals shall be of a bright color, and containers shall be clearly marked in legible contrasting letters at least 12.7 millimeters (0.5 inches) high: “DISTRESS SIGNALS”.
