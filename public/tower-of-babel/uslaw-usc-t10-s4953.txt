10 U.S.C. § 4953 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:10:4953
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4953
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart I (Defense Industrial Base); Chapter 388 (Procurement Technical Assistance Cooperative Agreement Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall prescribe regulations to carry out this chapter, and shall consult with an association recognized under section 4954(f) 1 regarding any revisions to such regulations.
