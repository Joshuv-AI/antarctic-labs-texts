39 C.F.R. § 953.17 — Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:39:953.17
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-953.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 953 (Mailability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 5 U.S.C. 551(14), 556(d), and 557(d) prohibiting ex parte communications are made applicable to proceedings under these rules of practice.
