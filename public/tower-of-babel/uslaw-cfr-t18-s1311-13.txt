18 C.F.R. § 1311.13 — May TVA waive any provision of these regulations?

Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1311 (Intergovernmental Review Of Tennessee Valley Authority Federal Financial Assistance And Direct Federal Development Programs And Activities). In an emergency, TVA may waive any provision of these regulations.
