14 C.F.R. § 101.11 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:101.11
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-101.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 101 (Moored Balloons, Kites, Amateur Rockets, And Unmanned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the operation of moored balloons and kites. However, a person operating a moored balloon or kite within a restricted area must comply only with § 101.19 and with additional limitations imposed by the using or controlling agency, as appropriate.
