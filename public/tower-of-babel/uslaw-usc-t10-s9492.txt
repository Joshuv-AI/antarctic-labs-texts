10 U.S.C. § 9492 — Status as volunteer civilian auxiliary of the Air Force

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 959 (Civil Air Patrol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Volunteer Civilian Auxiliary.—
The Civil Air Patrol is a volunteer civilian auxiliary of the Air Force when the services of the Civil Air Patrol are used by any department or agency in any branch of the Federal Government.
(b) Use by Air Force.—
(1)
The Secretary of the Air Force may use the services of the Civil Air Patrol to fulfill the noncombat programs and missions of the Department of the Air Force.
(2)
The Civil Air Patrol shall be deemed to be an instrumentality of the United States with respect to any act or omission of the Civil Air Patrol, including any member of the Civil Air Patrol, in carrying out a mission assigned by the Secretary of the Air Force.
