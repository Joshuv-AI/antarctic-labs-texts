45 C.F.R. § 506.13 — “Armed Forces of the United States” defined.
Citation: us:cfr:45:506.13
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-506.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 506 (Eligibility Requirements For Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Armed Forces of the United States means the United States Air Force, Army, Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard, and commissioned officers of the U.S. Public Health Service who were detailed for active duty with the Armed Forces of the United States.
