31 U.S.C. § 1302 — Determining amounts appropriated
Citation: us:usc:31:1302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1302
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 13 (Appropriations); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as specifically provided by law, the total amount appropriated in an appropriation law is determined by adding up the specific amounts or rates appropriated in each paragraph of the law.
