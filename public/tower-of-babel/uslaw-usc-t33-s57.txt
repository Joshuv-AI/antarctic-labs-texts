33 U.S.C. § 57 — Pike Creek, Wisconsin
Citation: us:usc:33:57
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section57
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter II (Waters Declared Nonnavigable: Change Of Name). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Pike Creek, in the State of Wisconsin, above the easterly side of the highway bridge at Sixth Avenue in the city of Kenosha is declared to be a nonnavigable stream within the meaning of the Constitution and laws of the United States.
The right to alter, amend, or repeal this section is expressly reserved.
