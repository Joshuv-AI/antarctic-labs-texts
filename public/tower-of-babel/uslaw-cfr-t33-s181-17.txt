33 C.F.R. § 181.17 — Label numbers and letters.
Citation: us:cfr:33:181.17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-181.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 181 (Manufacturer Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Letters and numbers on each label must:
(a) Be no less than one-eighth of an inch in height; and
(b) Contrast with the basic color of the label, except that the date of certification may be permanently stamped, engraved, or embossed on the label.
