16 U.S.C. § 410y–5 — Administration
Citation: us:usc:16:410y–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410y–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LVI (Chesapeake And Ohio Canal National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chesapeake and Ohio Canal National Historical Park shall be administered by the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with the Act of August 25, 1916 (30 Stat. 535; 16 U.S.C. 1, 2–4),1 as amended and supplemented.
