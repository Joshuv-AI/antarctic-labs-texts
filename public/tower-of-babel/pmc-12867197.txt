LNC01089-LINC00963/miR-1244-5p/IGF1 ceRNA Axis Regulate FOXO Signaling Pathway in Breast Cancer Patients: A Biomarker Discovery Investigation
Authors: Mohammad Rezaei, Ramin Masoudi Marghmaleki, Farzaneh Sanati Boroujeni, Abbas Shahriari, Shadi Omidghaemi, Mansoureh Azadeh
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
Background:Breast carcinoma (BC) ranks as one of the most prevalent illnesses among women, and a variety of factors, including inherited and environmental factors, can impact its start and progression. In this study, we tried to analyze the expression patterns of mRNAs and long noncoding RNAs and find novel biomarkers for the diagnosis and prognosis of BC during a systems biology approach.Materials and Methods:Microarray analysis was performed to find novel potential BC biomarkers. Using miRWalk, lncRRIsearch, STRING, and Cytoscape, noncoding and protein interaction analysis was utilized and visualized. Pathway enrichment and gene ontology analyses were performed to find accurate biological mechanisms of selected RNAs. qRT-PCR was established on 50 tumor samples compared to 50 control samples for validation of bioinformatics analyses and understanding of the diagnosis capability of selected RNAs.Results:IGF1 expression level had a significant reduction in BC based on microarray and qRT-PCR experiments. LINC00963 and LNC01089 also have significant decreases in expression levels based on GEPIA2 and qRT-PCR. LNC01089 and LINC00963 could represent suitable BC diagnostics (depending on receiver operating characteristic analysis) and prognosis (clinicopathological analysis) biomarkers. The two mentioned lncRNAs have direct interaction with IGF1 mRNA. miR-1244-5p as a potential upregulated oncogene of BC suppresses the expression level of LNC01089, LINC00963, and IGF1.Conclusion:LINC00963 and LNC01089 could regulate the FOXO signaling pathway through direct interaction with IGF1 mRNA. miR-1244-5p might also have a critical role in FOXO regulation through suppression of IGF1 and two mentioned lncRNAs.

INTRODUCTION

Breast cancer (BC) is one of the most common health concerns among women, and a range of variables, such as hereditary and environmental factors, can influence its onset and progression.[1] Breast cancer is the most frequent women’s cancer type, accounting for 1.8 million new cases in 2013 or 12% of all cases.[2] According to the histological and molecular information from previous studies, BC might be classified into three types: BC expressing a hormone receptor [estrogen receptor (ER+) or progesterone receptor (PR+)], BC expressing human epidermal receptor 2 (HER2+), and triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC).[3] Treatment plans should be based on the molecular characteristics of the BC. Furthermore, TNBC is classified into six types: basal-like 1 (BL-1) TNBC, basal-like 2 (BL-2) TNBC, immunomodulatory (IM) TNBC, mesenchymal (M), mesenchymal stem cell-like (MSL) TNBC, and luminal androgen receptor (LAR) TNBC.[4]

A number of biochemical biomarkers have been demonstrated. MicroRNAs (miRNAs) and exosomes are now recognized as viable diagnostic and therapeutic markers for breast cancer patients.[1] These compounds play a crucial part in the pathogenesis of breast cancer by affecting a variety of cell and molecular processes that contribute to the disease’s development.[5] miRNAs are short noncoding RNAs that inhibit the expression of genes by attaching to 3’UTR areas of transcripts.[6] miRNAs have the potential to influence a wide range of biological processes, including proliferation, stress responses, cell adhesion, motility, inflammation, cell survival, senescence, and apoptosis, all of which are important in carcinogenesis.[7] More than 3000 miRNAs have been discovered as being linked with the formation and progression of malignancies. Oncogenic miRNAs and suppressor miRNAs are the two types of miRNAs.[8]

Long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs) can affect the epigenetic landscape of chromatin by working with chromatin-modifying complexes to control the expression of their genes or other genes in a positive or negative way.[9] LncRNA is a kind of RNA molecule that is longer than 200 nucleotides (nt). They are noncoding RNAs but instead use RNA to regulate the expression level of different genes in different transcriptional levels.[10] For example, the expression level of BPX1 mRNA is regulated through the interaction with lncRNA LINC01521 in BC samples.[11] BAIAP2-AS1 also modulates THBS2 expression in gastric cancer (GC) patients.[12] LINC00520 could also regulate the ESR2 expression level in BC samples.[13] LncRNAs that regulate the immunological microenvironment of human breast cancer have been a focus of research. Some studies discovered an enormous number of various kinds of immune cells invading breast cancer tumors, not just in the cancer nest but additionally within the malignant matrix, and the type and number of immunological cells infiltrating surrounding the neoplasm were found to be directly associated with breast cancer prognosis.[141516]

“Insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor” (IGF1R) is a receptor with a domain for binding to ligand “insulin-like growth factor 1” (IGF1) and a tyrosine kinase domain.[17] When IGF1R interacts with IGF1, PI3K/AKT and “Ras/Raf/extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK)” pathways activated.[18] Previous studies discussed the possible roles of IGF1 in many types of tumors. For example, Qiao et al.[19] in 2021 revealed that IGF1 could regulate the expression level of HOXA13 in colorectal cancer (CRC) cells. The IR/IGF-1R pathway is closely controlled to maintain appropriate organismal development and metabolism. Pathophysiological diseases such as diabetes and cancer are caused by dysregulation of this system. In terms of the latter, abnormal IR/IGF-1R signaling has been linked to the onset and advancement of numerous cancers, including breast cancer.[20] In this study, we designed an integrated systems biology method to evaluate the possible noncoding regulatory factors of IGF1 in BC samples and the possible effect of those dysregulations in BC-related signaling pathways. Furthermore, we evaluate our selected lncRNAs and IGF1 biomarker capability in BC samples.

MATERIALS AND METHODS

Analysis of microarray datasets

Microarray analysis of GSE42568[21] and GSE45827[22] was performed using R Studio (4.2.3). Raw microarray datasets were obtained from gene expression omnibus (GEO) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/) utilizing GEOquery Bioconductor package.[23] The two mentioned datasets were combined and analyzed using R Studio. Using sva package, raw microarray data were normalized and the batch effect of the dataset was removed (https://bioconductor.org/packages/sva). For the quality control of the microarray dataset, principal component analysis (PCA) was performed. Using limma package, statistical analysis of microarray datasets was performed[24] with the package from Bioconductor. Drawing-related plots of microarray datasets were performed by ggplot2[25] and pheatmap2 packages. Genes with logFC > 2 and logFC < -2 and Adj. P. Val < 0.05 were identified as substantial dysregulated RNAs.

Bioinformatics analyses

For finding potential suppressor miRNAs for selected mRNA, the miRWalk[2627] online database was utilized (http://mirwalk.umm.uni-heidelberg.de/). miRNAs with a score of 1, binding to the 3’UTR area of mRNA located in the seed region, and lower binding energy were selected for further investigations. lncRNA interaction analysis was performed using lncRRIsearch[28] online software (http://rtools.cbrc.jp/LncRRIsearch/). Using miRNet, miRNA-lncRNA interaction was utilized (https://www.mirnet.ca/).[2930] To understand the accurate biological mechanisms of selected RNAs, pathway analysis was performed using the KEGG[3132] pathway (https://www.genome.jp/kegg/pathway.html). Interaction of selected mRNA with other related proteins was utilized using STRING[33] database (https://string-db.org/). Validation of microarray analysis and survival analysis were utilized using GEPIA2 software[34] (http://gepia2.cancer-pku.cn/). Expression analysis of miRNA was performed using ENCORI[35] (https://rnasysu.com/encori/panCancer.php). Visualization of selected interactions was performed by Cytoscape[3637] software. To understand the possible roles of selected RNAs in related biological processes and demonstrate of molecular functions of selected RNAs in different cellular components, the Enrichr[3839] online database was performed (https://maayanlab.cloud/Enrichr/).

Real-time PCR experiment

The overall RNA content of breast cancer tissue samples and standard breast tissue equivalents from the same patients was collected and extracted according to the manufacturer’s procedure using Trizol reagent (Promega Co., Madison, WI, USA). The first-strand cDNA synthesis package (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA) was used to make cDNA according to the manufacturer’s protocol. For the expression study of IGF1, LINC00963, and LNC01089 genes and GAPDH as a reference gene, the cDNA products were processed at 20°C. Using oligo software, specific primers were designed for each gene (version 7). Table 2 lists the primer sequences. The Magnetic Induction Cycler (MIC) was used to perform the qRT-PCR process (Bio Molecular Systems, Australia).

Statistical analyses

The GraphPad Prism (version 8) and GenEx (version 6) tools were used to analyze real-time PCR data employing the ddCt approach.[40] GraphPad software was used to create the expression charts. The Kolmogorov–Smirnov test was used to determine the normality of the expression data. On the expression data, the Wilcoxon test and paired t-test were used to assess the significance of our findings and compare the means of two normal and tumor samples. A receiver operating characteristic (ROC) study was used to estimate the diagnostic capability of tumor samples. The ROC results look at the area under the curve (AUC). An AUC of 0.7 to 0.8 denotes an acceptable biomarker, 0.8 to 0.9 denotes an excellent biomarker, and 0.9 to 1 denotes an extraordinary diagnostic biomarker.

Differential expression gene analysis showed that IGF1 has a significant downregulation in 248 tumor tissue samples as compared to 28 normal tissue samples (FC = 0.096, adj.P.Value = 6.55 × 1012, Figure 1b). This result revealed that IGF1 could have a remarkable tumor suppressor effect on breast cancer [Figure 1c].

miR-1224-5p modulates the expression level of IGF1, LNC01089, and LINC00963

The miRWalk program was used to analyze miRNA interactions to find potential regulatory miRNAs in breast cancer samples. The top 20 miRNAs which have RNA interaction with IGF1 mRNA have been selected. lncRNA–mRNA interaction analysis revealed that LNC01089 (energy: -57.80) and LINC00963 (energy: -628.58) have significant interaction with IGF1 mRNA. Furthermore, miRNA interaction analysis of LNC01089 and LINC00963 has been performed using miRNA, and the top 20 common regulatory miRNAs have been selected using venny online software [Figure 2a]. Based on the mentioned integrated interaction analysis, miR-1224-5p is the main regulatory miRNA in this network, which has interaction with IGF1 (score: 1, position: 3’UTR, energy: -22.8), LINC00963, and LNC01089 [Figure 2b].

DISCUSSION

We indicated in this research project that LNC01089 and LINC00963 could regulate IGF1 expression in BC patients. There was no previous study about the possible roles of this interaction with the risk of BC. Based on our study, LNC01089 might be evaluated as a possible BC diagnostic biomarker. There has been no previously conducted study on the potential relevance of LNC01089 as a BC diagnosis indicator for distinguishing normal samples from tumors. However, previous studies revealed that LNC01089 has a significant dysregulation in BC and other cancer types, and it plays a crucial role in BC development as a potential prognostic biomarker. For example, Sas-Chen et al.[41] 2016 revealed that LNC01089 had a significant downregulation in BC patients and could have a significant role in predicting poor BC prognosis. Based on this paper, knocking down LNC01089 could increase the migration of BC cells and positively affect cell invasion through the extracellular matrix. Another study in 2022 revealed that low LNC01089 expression is associated with a worse rate of survival in BC patients.[42] Our results validate the mentioned investigations about the possible roles of LNC01089’s downregulation in BC development. Yuan et al.[43] 2019 reported that the expression of LNC01089 was significantly reduced in BC patients. Furthermore, they indicated the correlation of LINC01089 low expression with metastasis to lymph nodes, age, and poor prognosis of BC. Based on our results, the expression level of LNC01089 has a significant correlation with age, tumor size, lymph node metastasis, ER receptor, and Her2 receptor. So, our results validated previous investigations about the possible correlation of LNC01089 expression level with the clinicopathological characteristics of BC patients. In addition to breast cancer, LNC01089 contributed to the development of other cancer types. For example, based on the study of Qian et al. in 2021,[44] in lung cancer cells, LNC01089 regulates the miR-301b-3p/STARD13 interaction axis, and through this mechanism, this lncRNA could decrease the migration rate of BC cells. LNC01089 also regulates the miR-27b-3p/FBLN5 axis in thyroid cancer patients, and this mechanism could suppress the malignant development.[45] There has been no previous research on the diagnostic utility of LNC01089 across any cancer form, and for the first time, we showed that this lncRNA has a significant diagnostic capability for distinguishing cancer samples from normal samples based on ROC analysis.

Previous studies revealed possible diagnostic and therapeutic roles of LINC00963 in different cancer types, including BC. Xie et al. 2022[46] demonstrated that LINC00963 could play a crucial role in the diagnosis and development of different cancer types. LINC00963 is frequently shown to be oncogenic by influencing different cellular processes such as proliferation, migration, invasion, EMT, and apoptosis. Overexpression of LINC00963 is linked to cancer clinicopathological characteristics and a poor prognosis, and it can be utilized to diagnose hepatocellular carcinoma. By binding competitively to 22 miRNAs in 14 cancers, LINC00963 can form a complex, competitive endogenous RNA (ceRNA) network. LINC00963 can also control four downstream protein-coding genes directly. By participating in EGFR transactivation, LINC00963 particularly promotes the transition of prostate cancer through an androgen-dependent to an androgen-independent phase. Based on the study of Wu et al.[47] in 2020, LINC00963 was discovered to be upregulated in BC patients, and its presence was correlated to metastasis to “lymph node, TNM stage, and differentiation grade”. Patients with breast cancer who had higher LINC00963 expression had shorter overall survival than those who had lower LINC00963 expression. Depletion of LINC00963 decreased breast cancer cell proliferation, migration, and invasion in vitro; accelerated apoptosis; and hampered tumor development in vivo. An examination into the mechanism of action found that LINC00963 can interact with microRNA-625 (miR-625). LINC00963 inhibited the inhibitory impact of miR-625 on high mobility group AT-hook 1 (HMGA1) in breast cancer cells by acting as a ceRNA for miR-625. Based on another study in 2019, suppression of LINC00963 could decrease the proliferation and tumorigenesis of BC cells. LINC00963 counteracts the restrictive action of miR-324-3p on ACK1 expression. In breast cancer tissues, there is a negative connection between miR-324-3p and LINC00963 expression. Overexpression of LINC00963 or ACK1 reverses miR-324-3p’s inhibitory effects on breast cancer cell growth and radiosensitivity. Furthermore, ACK1 knockdown inhibits LINC00963-dependent breast cancer development and carcinogenesis.[48] In the study of Arabpour et al. in 2021,[49] upregulation of LINC00963 is observed in luminal A and luminal B tumor samples compared to normal samples. Furthermore, based on their investigations, LINC00963 regulates BC development through potential interaction with mir-130a-3p/HSPA8 axis. Based on the study of Wang et al. in 2023,[50] LINC00963 stimulates nuclear translocation of FOSB and subsequent transcription activation of UBE3C, which improves BRCA cell radioresistance via increasing TP73 ubiquitination-dependent protein degradation. Our results about the gene expression pattern of LINC00963 validated our bioinformatics analyses, but it was different from previous investigations. Our results indicated a low expression of LINC00963 in BC samples, which was statistically significant, and based on this expression pattern and ROC analysis, we understood that LINC00963 might also have the potential to contribute to the diagnosis of Bc as an acceptable diagnostic biomarker of BC. This difference might be due to the difference in the samples or different clinicopathological situations of different samples in previous articles. However, we understood that the dysregulation of LINC00963 in BC is validated in different investigations, including our research.

There has yet to be any prior study on the potential involvement of miR-1224-5p with BC development. For the primary time, we found that miR-1224-5p is significantly upregulated in BC samples in our study. Furthermore, we revealed that miR-1224-5p interacted with the 3’UTR region of IGF1, LNC01089, and LINC00963 mRNA and suppressed this mRNA through the interaction. So, the upregulation of miR-1224-5p could be one of the main reasons for expression in IGF1, LINC00963, and LNC01089. Based on this analysis, IGF1 regulates the FOXO signaling pathway. By stimulating the PI3K/AKT kinase pathway, IGF-1 can induce the phosphorylation of FOXO3a (FKHRL1) in PC12 and uveal melanoma cells.[51] So, any disturbance in the expression level of LINC00963 and LNC01089 could disturb the FOXO signaling pathway through direct and indirect interaction with IGF1. Due to the limitations in our department, we could not establish experiments for validation of interaction between LNC01089 and LINC00963 and miR-1244-5p in BC samples. So, it is highly recommended that the mentioned interactions be evaluated using relevant interaction experiments like luciferase assay and RIP methods. CONCLUSION

LINC00963 and LNC01089 as the two potential BC diagnostic biomarkers could regulate the expression level of IGF1 through direct physical interaction and ceRNA interaction (via changes in the binding affinity of miR-1244-5p). miR-1244-5p could directly suppress the expression level of IGF1, LINC0109, and LINC00963, and through this mechanism, this miRNA could have a significant regulatory role in the FOXO signaling pathway.
