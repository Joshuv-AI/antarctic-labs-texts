46 C.F.R. § 64.17 — Minimum tank thickness.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as allowed in paragraph (b) of this section, a tank with a diameter of—
(1) 6 feet or less must have a shell and head of 3/16 inch thickness or more; or
(2) More than 6 feet must have a shell and head of 1/4 inch thickness or more.
(b) If the tank has additional framework to guard against accidental puncturing of the tank, the shell and head thickness must be 1/8 inch or more.
