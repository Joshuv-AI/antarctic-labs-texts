49 C.F.R. § 190.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:190.1
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-190.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 190 (Pipeline Safety Enforcement And Regulatory Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part prescribes procedures used by the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration in carrying out duties regarding pipeline safety under 49 U.S.C. 60101 et seq. (the pipeline safety laws) and 33 U.S.C. 1321 (the water pollution control laws).
(b) This subpart defines certain terms and prescribes procedures that are applicable to each proceeding described in this part.
