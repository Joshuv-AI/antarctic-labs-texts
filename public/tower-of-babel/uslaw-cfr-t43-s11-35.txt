43 C.F.R. § 11.35 — How does the trustee decide whether to use Type A or Type B procedures?
Citation: us:cfr:43:11.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-11.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 11 (Natural Resource Damage Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the natural resource trustee determines under § 11.34 that a Type A procedure is available, the trustee must then decide whether to use that procedure or use a Type B procedure. The trustee must make this decision by weighing the difficulty of collecting site-specific data against the suitability of the averaged data and simplifying assumptions in the Type A procedure for the release being assessed. The trustee may use a Type B procedure if they can be performed at a reasonable cost and if the increase in accuracy provided by those procedures outweighs the increase in assessment costs.
(b) If there is no appropriate Type A procedure, the trustee must use a Type B procedure to calculate all damages.
