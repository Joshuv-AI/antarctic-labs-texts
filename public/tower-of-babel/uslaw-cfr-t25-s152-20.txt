25 C.F.R. § 152.20 — Sale by Secretary of certain land in multiple ownership.
Citation: us:cfr:25:152.20
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-152.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 152 (Issuance Of Patents In Fee, Certificates Of Competency, Removal Of Restrictions, And Sale Of Certain Indian Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to the Act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 855), as amended (25 U.S.C. 372), if the Secretary decides that one or more of the heirs who have inherited trust land are incapable of managing their own affairs, he may sell any or all interests in that land. This authority is not applicable to lands authorized to be sold by the Act of May 14, 1948 (62 Stat. 236; 25 U.S.C. 483).
