25 C.F.R. § 36.101 — How often must students who have been separated for emergency health or behavioral reasons be supervised?
Citation: us:cfr:25:36.101
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-36.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 36 (Minimum Academic Standards For The Basic Education Of Indian Children And National Criteria For Dormitory Situations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Students who have been separated for emergency behavioral or health reasons must be supervised as frequently and as closely as the circumstances and protocols require. No student will be left unsupervised for any period until such factors as the student's health based on a medical assessment, the safety of the student, and any other applicable guidance for dealing with behavior or health emergencies are considered.
