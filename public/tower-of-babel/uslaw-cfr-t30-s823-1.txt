30 C.F.R. § 823.1 — Scope and purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 823 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Operations On Prime Farmland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth special environmental protection performance, reclamation, and design standards for surface coal mining and reclamation operations on prime farmland.
