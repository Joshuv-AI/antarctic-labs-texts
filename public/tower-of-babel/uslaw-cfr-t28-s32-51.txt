28 C.F.R. § 32.51 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:28:32.51
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-32.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 32 (Public Safety Officers' Death, Disability, And Educational Assistance Benefit Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with § 32.1, this subpart contains provisions applicable to Director appeals and reviews of claim approvals and denials made under subpart E of this part, and reviews of claim approvals under the Act, at 34 U.S.C. 10286 or Public Law 107-37.
