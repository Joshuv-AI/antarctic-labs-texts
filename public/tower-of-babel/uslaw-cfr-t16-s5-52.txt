16 C.F.R. § 5.52 — Investigation or proceeding.

Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-5.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 5 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Office of the General Counsel will ensure that the agency cooperates fully in any investigation of possible violations of subparts A-B or D of this part or 18 U.S.C. 207 by the Office of Inspector General, the Department of Justice, or the U.S. Office of Government Ethics.
(b) Upon the conclusion of an investigation by the Inspector General or a prosecution by the Department of Justice, the Commission will request that the Office of Inspector General provide the Commission with a report.
