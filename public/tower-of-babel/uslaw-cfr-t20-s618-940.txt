20 C.F.R. § 618.940 — Insufficient funds.
Citation: us:cfr:20:618.940
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-618.940
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 618 (Trade Adjustment Assistance Under The Trade Act Of 1974, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, during a fiscal year, the Department estimates that the amount of funds necessary to provide TaOA will exceed the annual cap under § 618.900, the Department will decide how the available funds that have not been distributed at the time of the estimate will be allocated among the States for the remainder of the fiscal year, and will communicate this decision to States through administrative guidance.
