Abstract
. We present a complete system of inequalities for the inradius, circumradius, and diameter in the 3 3 -dimensional Euclidean space. To do so, we prove quasiconcavity of the inradius evaluated over n n -simplices with a common facet independently of the norm/gauge under consideration.

C\in\mathcal{K}^{n}
be full-dimensional and
\left\{p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\subset\bd(C)
be affinely independent. Furthermore, let
\left\{q^{1},\ldots,q^{k}\right\}\subset\bd(C)
be contained in the same open half-space bounded by the hyperplane
\aff\left(\left\{p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\right)
. Then, for
S:=\conv\left(\left\{p^{1},p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\right)
with
p^{1}\in\conv_{C,\left\{p^{2},...p^{n+1}\right\}}
and
S_{i}:=\conv\left(\left\{q^{i},p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\right)

we have

r(S,C)\geq\min_{i\in[k]}r(S_{i},C).

Moreover, if
C
is smooth and
p^{1}
is not contained in
\conv\left(\left\{q^{1},\ldots,q^{k}\right\}\right)
, we have
r(S,C)>\min_{i\in[k]}r(S_{i},C)
.

Proof.

Essentially, it suffices to show that there exists some

q\in\conv\left(\left\{q^{1},\ldots,q^{k}\right\}\right)\cap S
.
Given such a
q
, it is obvious
that
r(S,C)\geq r(\conv\left(\left\{q,p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\right),C)
and

q\in\conv\left(\left\{q^{1},\ldots,q^{k}\right\}\right)
enables us to apply
Theorem

1.2
to obtain
r(\conv\left(\left\{q,p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\right),C)\geq\min_{i\in[k]}r(S_{i},C)
. Note that we have
r(S,C)>r(\conv\left(\left\{q,p^{2},\ldots,p^{n+1}\right\}\right),C)
if
C
is smooth and
q\in\inte(C)
by
Lemma

3.4
.

According to our choice of
p^{1}
there exist
\lambda_{j},\alpha_{i,j}\geq 0
,
j\in\left\{2,\ldots,,n+1\right\}
and
i\in[k]
, with
\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{j}=1
and
\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i,j}\geq 1
such that

p^{1}=\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{j}\left(p^{j}+\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i,j}(q^{i}-p^{j})\right)\in\bd(C).

We define

\displaystyle\beta_{i}

\displaystyle:=\frac{\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{j}\alpha_{i,j}}{\sum_{m=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{m}\sum_{l=1}^{k}\alpha_{l,j}},\quad i\in[k]

\displaystyle\mu_{1}

\displaystyle:=\frac{1}{\sum_{m=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{m}\sum_{l=1}^{k}\alpha_{l,j}},

\displaystyle\mu_{j}

\displaystyle:=\frac{\lambda_{j}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i,j}-1\right)}{\sum_{m=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{m}\sum_{l=1}^{k}\alpha_{l,j}},\quad j\in\left\{2,\ldots,n+1\right\}.

Then, we have
\beta_{i},\mu_{j}\in[0,1]
for
i\in[k]
and
j\in\left\{2,\ldots,n+1\right\}
and
\sum_{i=1}^{k}\beta_{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}\mu_{j}=1
.

Moreover,

\displaystyle q:

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}\mu_{j}p^{j}=\mu_{1}\left(\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{j}\left(p^{j}+\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i,j}(q^{i}-p^{j})\right)\right)+\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\mu_{j}p^{j}

\displaystyle=\mu_{1}\left(\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\lambda_{j}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i,j}q^{i}\right)+\sum_{j=2}^{n+1}\left(\mu_{1}\lambda_{j}\left(1-\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i,j}\right)+\mu_{j}\right)p^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{k}\beta_{i}q^{i}\in\conv\left(\left\{q^{1},\ldots,q^{k}\right\}\right),

concluding the proof.
∎

\displaystyle=\frac{2\sqrt{2}(d-2)^{3}}{(4-d)\sqrt{(4-d)(3d-4)(d-2)}}.

For
h
, using
d-x=\frac{3d-4}{2}
,
1-x=\frac{d-2}{2}
,
\frac{d}{2}-x=d-2
, and
4(d-x)-d^{2}=(d-2)(4-d)
, we obtain

\displaystyle h\left(\frac{4-d}{2}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{(d-2)^{2}(3d-4)(4-d)-d^{2}(d-2)^{2}}{2\sqrt{\frac{3d-4}{2}}\sqrt{(d-2)(4-d)}(d-2)(4-d)}

\displaystyle=\frac{-4(d-2)^{4}}{\sqrt{2}(d-2)(4-d)\sqrt{(4-d)(3d-4)(d-2)}}

\displaystyle=\frac{-2\sqrt{2}(d-2)^{3}}{(4-d)\sqrt{(4-d)(3d-4)(d-2)}}

Together, we obtain
h(x)+g(x)<g(\frac{4-d}{2})+h(\frac{4-d}{2})=0
. Therefore,
f^{\prime}(x)<0
for
x\in\left(\frac{4-d}{2},\frac{d}{4}\right]
and
f
is strictly decreasing.

The inradius of a simplex
S
optimally contained in
\mathds{B}
with at least five edges of length
D

\displaystyle r(S)

\displaystyle=f\left(\frac{D^{2}}{4}\right)=\frac{\frac{D^{2}}{2}-\frac{D^{2}}{4}}{\frac{2}{D}\sqrt{\frac{D^{2}}{4}(1-D^{2})+\frac{D^{4}}{4}}+\left(1-\frac{D^{2}}{4}\right)\sqrt{\frac{3D^{2}}{3D^{2}-D^{4}}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{D^{2}}{4+(4-D^{2})\sqrt{\frac{3}{3-D^{2}}}}=\frac{D^{2}\sqrt{3-D^{2}}}{4\sqrt{3-D^{2}}-\sqrt{3}(D^{2}-4)}

and they are the unique minimizers.
