29 C.F.R. § 785.28 — Involuntary attendance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:785.28
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-785.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 785 (Hours Worked). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Attendance is not voluntary, of course, if it is required by the employer. It is not voluntary in fact if the employee is given to understand or led to believe that his present working conditions or the continuance of his employment would be adversely affected by nonattendance.
