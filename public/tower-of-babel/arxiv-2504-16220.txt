Abstract
. We study a particular family of elements in the cohomology of the ℂ \mathbb{C} -motivic Steenrod algebra, also known as the ℂ \mathbb{C} -motivic Adams E 2 E_{2} -page. This family exhibits unusual periodicity properties, and it is related both to h 1 h_{1} -localization and to the algebraic Hurewicz image of the motivic modular forms spectrum mmf \mathrm{mmf} .

-localization

Recall that

\mathbb{C}
-motivic Steenrod algebra. We already have a thorough understanding of the

-periodic computations that have not been settled. The

-periodicization map takes the form

-motivic Steenrod algebra and

-periodicization map.

Our work shows that

and

and

of

with smallest possible stem such that

localizes to

w_{1}
-periodicity

The family of elements of the form
e_{0}g^{2^{n}-1}
displays a curious property with respect to
w_{1}
-periodicity. See
[
And18
]
and
[
IKL+25
]
for the precise meaning of
w_{1}
-periodicity.

Each member of our family is obtained from the previous member by application of a
w_{1}
-periodicity operator. More specifically,

e_{0}g^{2^{n+1}-1}=w_{1}^{2^{n+2}}\cdot e_{0}g^{2^{n}-1}

where
w_{1}^{2^{n+2}}\cdot(-)
is a Massey product operator
\left\langle h_{1},h_{1}^{2^{n+2}-1}h_{n+4},-\right\rangle
. However, none of these Massey product operators can be iterated, i.e.,

w_{1}^{2^{n+2}}\cdot\left(w_{1}^{2^{n+2}}\cdot e_{0}g^{2^{n}-1}\right)

is not well-defined. The elements
e_{0}g^{2^{n}-1}
are related by
w_{1}
-periodicity, but they are not a “
w_{1}
-periodic family” in the usual sense in which the degrees grow arithmetically. Rather, these elements form some kind of “sparse” family in which the degrees grow geometrically.

The sparseness of the family of elements
e_{0}g^{2^{n}-1}
is an exotic phenomenon, in the sense that it does not occur with
v_{1}
-periodicity in the cohomologies of the classical or
\mathbb{C}
-motivic Steenrod algebras.

Algebraic
\mathbb{C}
-motivic Hurewicz image

The
\mathrm{tmf}
-Hurewicz map is the unit map
S^{0}\rightarrow\mathrm{tmf}
of the topological modular forms spectrum
\mathrm{tmf}
. The image of this map on stable homotopy is completely known
[
BMQ23
]
. The
\mathbb{C}
-motivic analogue of this question is to compute the image in stable homotopy of the
\mathbb{C}
-motivic unit map
S^{0,0}\rightarrow\mathrm{mmf}
, where
\mathrm{mmf}
is the
\mathbb{C}
-motivic modular forms spectrum
[
GIKR22
]
. The image of the
\mathrm{mmf}
-Hurewicz map has not been completely determined.

An approximation to the
\mathrm{mmf}
-Hurewicz image is the algebraic
\mathrm{mmf}
-Hurewicz image, which is the image of the map of
\mathbb{C}
-motivic Adams
E_{2}
-pages induced by the unit map. Our work demonstrates that the algebraic
\mathrm{mmf}
-Hurewicz image is not so easy to describe. For example, the product
e_{0}g^{k}
belongs to the
\mathrm{mmf}
-Hurewicz image if and only if

is of the form

. On the other hand, the element

-Hurewicz image for all

We expect that other similarly complicated patterns appear in the algebraic
\mathrm{mmf}
-Hurewicz image, although we have not conducted a thorough analysis.
