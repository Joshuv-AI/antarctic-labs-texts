5 U.S.C. § 7321 — Political participation
Citation: us:usc:5:7321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section7321
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart F (Labor-Management And Employee Relations); Chapter 73 (Suitability, Security, And Conduct); Subchapter III (Political Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the policy of the Congress that employees should be encouraged to exercise fully, freely, and without fear of penalty or reprisal, and to the extent not expressly prohibited by law, their right to participate or to refrain from participating in the political processes of the Nation.
