21 C.F.R. § 510.203 — Initiation of a proceeding to establish or amend an import tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:21:510.203
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-510.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 510 (New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any interested person may request that the Commissioner establish or amend an import tolerance. Such a request must be in the form specified in § 510.205 of this chapter.
(b) The Commissioner may initiate a proceeding to establish or amend an import tolerance on his or her own initiative pursuant to § 10.25(b) of this chapter.
