25 C.F.R. § 301.1 — Eligibility for use of Government stamp.
Citation: us:cfr:25:301.1
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-301.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 301 (Navajo, Pueblo, And Hopi Silver And Turquoise Products; Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the detailed requirements that follow, the Government stamp shall be affixed only to work individually produced and to work entirely hand-made. No object produced under conditions resembling a bench work system, and no object in whose manufacture any power-driven machinery has been used, shall be eligible for the use of the Government stamp.
