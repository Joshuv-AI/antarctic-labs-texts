21 C.F.R. § 862.3110 — Antimony test system.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 862 (Clinical Chemistry And Clinical Toxicology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An antimony test system is a device intended to measure antimony, a heavy metal, in urine, blood, vomitus, and stomach contents. Measurements obtained by this device are used in the diagnosis and treatment of antimony poisoning.
(b) Classification. Class I.
