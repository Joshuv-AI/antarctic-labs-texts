7 C.F.R. § 621.33 — Conditions for approval.
Citation: us:cfr:7:621.33
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-621.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 621 (River Basin Investigations And Surveys). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A joint investigation and report is authorized by a resolution of the Committee on Environment and Public Works of the U.S. Senate or the Committee on Public Works and Transportation of the U.S. House of Representatives. Studies are initiated when funds for them are appropriated by the Congress.
