10 C.F.R. § 706.32 — Security indoctrination of non-employee representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:10:706.32
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-706.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 706 (Security Policies And Practices Relating To Labor-Management Relations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All collective bargaining representatives, company and union, who are to have access to Restricted Data, will be given appropriate security indoctrination.
