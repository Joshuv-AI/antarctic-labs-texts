30 C.F.R. § 921.764 — Process for designating areas unsuitable for surface coal mining operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:921.764
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-921.764
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 921 (Massachusetts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 764 of this chapter, State Processes for Designating Areas Unsuitable for Surface Coal Mining Operations, pertaining to petitioning, initial processing, hearing requirements, decisions, data base and inventory systems, public information, and regulatory responsibilities, are applicable in Massachusetts beginning on May 28 1983.
