Accessing nucleon transversity with one-point energy correlators
Mei-Sen Gao, Zhong-Bo Kang, Wanchen Li, Ding Yu Shao

Abstract
We propose a novel probe of the nucleon’s transversity distribution, h 1 q h_{1}^{q} , using the one-point energy correlator (OPEC), an infrared-and-collinear safe jet substructure observable. We demonstrate that in transversely polarized p ↑ ​ p p^{\uparrow}p collisions, the OPEC exhibits a single-spin asymmetry (SSA) with a clean sin ⁡ ( ϕ s − ϕ n ) \sin(\phi_{s}-\phi_{n}) angular dependence. This method probes SSA over a much wider kinematic range in the angular scale θ n \theta_{n} compared to traditional measurements of hadron transverse momentum j ⟂ j_{\perp} , establishing a complementary and systematically distinct channel to study the nucleon’s three-dimensional structure at RHIC and the future Electron-Ion Collider.

OPEC. –

In the collinear limit, the OPEC is encoded within general light cone correlators that define a new class of fragmenting jet functions (FJFs)

(2)

Factorization formula. –

We consider the OPEC in inclusive jet production for the polarized proton and unpolarized proton scattering process,
p^{\uparrow}+p\to J+X

\displaystyle\!\frac{\mathrm{d}\Sigma}{\mathrm{d}\theta_{n}\mathrm{d}\phi_{n}\mathrm{d}\eta\,\mathrm{d}p_{T}}=

\displaystyle\sum_{h\in J}\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}z_{h}\int\mathrm{d}^{2}\Omega_{\boldsymbol{h}}\,\delta\left(\phi_{n}-\phi_{h}\right)

(4)

\displaystyle\times\delta\left(\theta_{n}-\theta_{h}\right)\,z_{h}\,\frac{\mathrm{d}\sigma}{\mathrm{d}z_{h}\,\mathrm{d}^{2}\Omega_{h}\mathrm{d}\eta\,\mathrm{d}p_{T}}\,,

Relations to the Collins function. –

A powerful application of our formalism is to perform direct tests of the universality of polarized fragmentation. The Collins function
Collins 1993
;
Collins et al. 1994
, which consists the information of the azimuthal distribution of hadrons fragmenting from a transversely polarized quark, has been a cornerstone for accessing transversity, with its universality across different scattering processes being a crucial, yet challenging, assumption to verify
Boer et al. 2003
;
Metz 2002
;
Collins and Metz 2004
;
Yuan and Zhou 2009
;
Boer et al. 2010
.

Our polarized FJF,
\mathcal{J}_{1,\perp}^{q}

As an IRC safe observable, the OPEC FJF is theoretically cleaner and less susceptible to the complexities of hadronization models. Universality can therefore be tested by comparing measurements of
\mathcal{J}_{1,\perp}^{q}
in distinctly different environments: (1)
e^{+}e^{-}
annihilation, the cleanest environment where jets are produced from a quark-antiquark pair; (2) SIDIS, probing the fragmentation of a quark struck from within a nucleon; and (3) polarized proton-proton collisions, the most complex hadronic environment. A comparison of the extracted
J_{1,\perp}^{q}
from these channels, after accounting for their respective QCD evolution, would constitute a rigorous and independent test of the factorization and universality of the polarized fragmentation mechanism.

In the limit
\theta_{n}\gg\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q

Single spin asymmetry. –

The transverse single-spin asymmetry (SSA) of the OPEC is given by

A_{UT}^{\sin(\phi_{s}-\phi_{n})}=\frac{Z_{UT}}{Z_{UU}}.

(11)

In our numerical calculations, we have included contributions from the gluon-initiated jet channel, which enter the unpolarized structure function
Z_{UU}
and thus influence the normalization of the SSA. Our calculation uses the scale choice
\mu=Q=p_{T}
. A more complete treatment including the resummation of jet radius, which is necessary for achieving higher theoretical precision, will be presented in a future work. Besides, the full Sudakov exponential is modeled as
e^{-S_{\text{pert}}(Q,b_{*})/2-S_{\rm NP}(Q,b)}
. In the
S_{\text{pert}}
we separate perturbative and non-perturbative domains using the standard
b_{*}
-prescription, where
b_{*}=b/\sqrt{1+b^{2}/b_{\rm max}^{2}}
freezes the impact parameter at
b_{\rm max}=1.5~\text{GeV}^{-1}

Collins et al. 1985
;
Qiu and Zhang 2001
;
Landry et al. 2003
. This prevents the evolution from entering a region where perturbation theory is unreliable. For the unpolarized FJF, the non-perturbative component
S_{\rm NP}^{D_{1}}(Q,b)
is adapted from the well-established form used for Drell-Yan and SIDIS processes
Sun et al. 2018
. For the transversely polarized FJF, the corresponding non-perturbative factor
S_{\rm NP}^{\rm collins}(Q,b)
is taken from global analyses of the Collins effect
Kang et al. 2016a
.

Conclusions and outlook. –

We introduced the OPEC in jets for transversely polarized
p^{\uparrow}p
collisions, sensitive to the transverse polarization effect through a
\sin(\phi_{s}-\phi_{n})
modulation. Defined via an energy-weighted angular distribution of energy flux detectors inside jets, this observable provides a clean probe of the transversity PDF and Collins FF at high momentum scales. Compared with prior studies, we estimate that OPEC can access a much broader kinematic range of the jet substructure.

We also present a phenomenology study of SSA from OPEC via two frameworks: a) a TMD evolution framework using global fits from
Kang et al. 2016a
, b) the JAM3D-22 global analysis
Gamberg et al. 2022
, which omits TMD evolution. Both approaches reveal similar qualitative behavior, with partially overlapping uncertainty bands between the two approaches. The OPEC SSA offers a complementary channel to constrain the transversity PDF and Collins function at RHIC. Looking forward, implementing this observable at the future EIC would enable a pristine test of the fundamental principle of universality for the Collins FF by allowing a direct comparison between hadronic (
p^{\uparrow}p
) and deep-inelastic scattering (
ep^{\uparrow}
) processes. This establishes a vital new tool for precision studies of spin-dependent fragmentation and the rich internal landscape of the nucleon.
