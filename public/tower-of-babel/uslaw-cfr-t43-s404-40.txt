43 C.F.R. § 404.40 — What is the non-Federal share of operation, maintenance, and replacement costs?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.40
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are required to pay 100 percent of the operation, maintenance, and replacement costs of any rural water supply project planned under this program.
