24 C.F.R. § 203.408 — Form and amounts of debentures.
Citation: us:cfr:24:203.408
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-203.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 203 (Single Family Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Debentures issued under this part shall be in such form and amounts; and shall be subject to such term and conditions; and shall include such provisions for redemption, if any, as may be prescribed by the Secretary, with the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury; and may be in book entry or certificated registered form, or such other form as the Secretary by regulation may prescribe.
