18 C.F.R. § 281.305 — General rule.
Citation: us:cfr:18:281.305
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-281.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 281 (Natural Gas Curtailment Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any essential agricultural user subject to this subpart that has requested from any direct supplier priority 2 classification for volumes for any essential agricultural use establishment shall reduce its essential agricultural requirements calculated under § 281.208 to reflect the exclusion of volumes of natural gas for which its essential agricultural establishment has alternative fuel volumes under § 281.304.
