28 C.F.R. § 93.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:28:93.1
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-93.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 93 (Provisions Implementing The Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement Act Of 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth requirements and procedures to ensure that grants to States, State courts, local courts, units of local government, and Indian tribal governments, acting directly or through agreements with other public or private entities, exclude violent offenders from participation in programs authorized and funded under this part.
