10 U.S.C. § 7151 — Commissioned officer grades
Citation: us:usc:10:7151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7151
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 715 (Appointments In The Regular Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The commissioned grades in the Regular Army are:
(1)
Major general.
(2)
Brigadier general.
(3)
Colonel.
(4)
Lieutenant colonel.
(5)
Major.
(6)
Captain.
(7)
First lieutenant.
(8)
Second lieutenant.
