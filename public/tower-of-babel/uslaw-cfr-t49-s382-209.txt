49 C.F.R. § 382.209 — Use following an accident.
Citation: us:cfr:49:382.209
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-382.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 382 (Controlled Substances And Alcohol Use And Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No driver required to take a post-accident alcohol test under § 382.303 shall use alcohol for eight hours following the accident, or until he/she undergoes a post-accident alcohol test, whichever occurs first.
