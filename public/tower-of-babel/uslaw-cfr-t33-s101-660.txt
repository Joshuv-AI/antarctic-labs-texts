33 C.F.R. § 101.660 — Cybersecurity compliance documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:101.660
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-101.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 101 (Maritime Security: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each owner or operator must ensure that the cybersecurity portion of their Plan and penetration test results are available to the Coast Guard upon request. The Alternative Security Program provisions apply to cybersecurity compliance documentation and are addressed in 33 CFR 104.140 for vessels, 33 CFR 105.140 for facilities, and 33 CFR 106.135 for OCS facilities.
