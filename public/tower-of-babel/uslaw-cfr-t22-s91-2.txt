22 C.F.R. § 91.2 — Furnishing samples to collectors of customs or appraising officers.
Citation: us:cfr:22:91.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-91.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 91 (Import Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the receipt of a request therefor from a collector of customs or appraising officer of the Government of the United States, a consular officer shall procure and forward samples of merchandise being imported or offered for importation into the United States from his particular district.
