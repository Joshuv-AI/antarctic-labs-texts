7 U.S.C. § 1765 — Foreign service appropriations; applicability
Citation: us:usc:7:1765
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1765
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 43 (Foreign Market Development); Subchapter I (General Provisions; Agricultural Counselors And Agricultural AttachéS). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions in annual appropriation Acts of the Department of State facilitating the work of the Foreign Service of the United States shall be applicable under rules and regulations prescribed by the President or his designee to activities pursuant to this subchapter.
