26 U.S.C. § 5684 — Penalties relating to the payment and collection of liquor taxes
Citation: us:usc:26:5684
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5684
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part IV (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Common To Liquors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Failure to pay tax
Whoever fails to pay any tax imposed by part I of subchapter A at the time prescribed shall, in addition to any other penalty provided in this title, be liable to a penalty of 5 percent of the tax due but unpaid.
(b) Applicability of section 6665
The penalties imposed by subsection (a) shall be assessed, collected, and paid in the same manner as taxes, as provided in section 6665(a).
