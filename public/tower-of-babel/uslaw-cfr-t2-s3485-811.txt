2 C.F.R. § 3485.811 — When does a debarment affect title IV, HEA transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3485.811
Creator: Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3485.811
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3485 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A debarment under § 3485.611(b) takes effect 30 days after those procedures are complete.
(b) If the respondent appeals the debarment to the Secretary before the expiration of the 30 days under paragraph (a) of this section, the debarment takes effect when the respondent receives the Secretary's decision.
