48 C.F.R. § 32.401 — 32.401 Statutory authority.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may authorize advance payments in negotiated and sealed bid contracts if the action is appropriate under
(a) 41 U.S.C. chapter 45;
(b) 10 U.S.C. chapter 277; or
(c) Pub. L. 85-804 (50 U.S.C. 1431-1435) and Executive Order 10789, November 14, 1958 (3 CFR 1958 Supp. pp. 72-74) (see Subpart 50.1 for other applications of this statute).
