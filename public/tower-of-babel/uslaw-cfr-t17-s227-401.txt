17 C.F.R. § 227.401 — Exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:17:227.401
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-227.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 227 (Regulation Crowdfunding, General Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A funding portal that is registered with the Commission pursuant to § 227.400 is exempt from the broker registration requirements of section 15(a)(1) of the Exchange Act (15 U.S.C. 78o(a)(1)) in connection with its activities as a funding portal.
