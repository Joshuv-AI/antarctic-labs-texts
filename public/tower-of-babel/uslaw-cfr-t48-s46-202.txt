48 C.F.R. § 46.202 — 46.202 Types of contract quality requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:46.202
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-46.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 46 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contract quality requirements fall into four general categories, depending on the extent of quality assurance needed by the Government for the acquisition involved.
