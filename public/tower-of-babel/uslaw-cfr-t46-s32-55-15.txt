46 C.F.R. § 32.55-15 — -15 Ventilation for hold spaces—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.55-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.55-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hold spaces containing independent cargo tanks shall be considered to be equivalent to cargo pumprooms and shall be ventilated and safeguarded as such.
