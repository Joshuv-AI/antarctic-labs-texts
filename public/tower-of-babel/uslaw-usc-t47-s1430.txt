47 U.S.C. § 1430 — Annual report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:47:1430
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1430
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 13 (Public Safety Communications And Electromagnetic Spectrum Auctions); Subchapter II (Governance Of Public Safety Spectrum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Not later than 1 year after February 22, 2012, and each year thereafter, the First Responder Network Authority shall submit an annual report covering the preceding fiscal year to the appropriate committees of Congress.
(b) Required content
The report required under subsection (a) shall include—
(1)
a comprehensive and detailed report of the operations, activities, financial condition, and accomplishments of the First Responder Network Authority under this section; and
(2)
such recommendations or proposals for legislative or administrative action as the First Responder Network Authority deems appropriate.
(c) Availability to testify
The members of the Board and employees of the First Responder Network Authority shall be available to testify before the appropriate committees of the Congress with respect to—
(1)
the report required under subsection (a);
(2)
the report of any audit conducted under section 1429 1 of this title; or
(3)
any other matter which such committees may determine appropriate.
