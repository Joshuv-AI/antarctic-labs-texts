29 C.F.R. § 4281.41 — Benefit suspensions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4281.41
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4281.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4281 (Duties Of Plan Sponsor Following Mass Withdrawal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the plan sponsor determines that the plan is or is expected to be insolvent for a plan year, the plan sponsor shall suspend benefits to the extent necessary to reduce the benefits to the greater of the resource benefit level or the level of guaranteed benefits.
