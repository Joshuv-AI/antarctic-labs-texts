14 U.S.C. § 2309 — Recall to active duty with consent of member
Citation: us:usc:14:2309
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2309
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any enlisted member on the retired list may, with his consent, be assigned to such duties as he may be able to perform, except that no enlisted member on the retired list who has reached the age of sixty-two years shall be recalled in time of peace.
