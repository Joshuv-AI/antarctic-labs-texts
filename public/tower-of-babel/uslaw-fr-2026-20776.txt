Foreign-Trade Zones Board

Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 50, Notification of Proposed Production 
Activity; Sanyo Denki America, Inc.; (Cooling, Servo, and Power Systems 
and Assemblies); Torrance, California

    Sanyo Denki America, Inc. submitted a notification of proposed 
production activity to the FTZ Board (the Board) for its facilities in 
Torrance, California, within FTZ 50. The notification conforming to the 
requirements of the Board's regulations (15 CFR 400.22) was received on 
September 24, 2026.
    Pursuant to 15 CFR 400.14(b), FTZ production activity would be 
limited to the specific foreign-status material(s)/component(s) and 
specific finished product(s) described in the submitted notification 
(summarized below) and subsequently authorized by the Board. The 
benefits that may stem from conducting production activity under FTZ 
procedures are explained in the background section of the Board's 
website--accessible via www.trade.gov/ftz.
    The proposed finished products include: various rails (mounting 
support; magnetic); various fans (axial; centrifugal); various 
assemblies (fan; printed circuit; electric motor); filter kits for 
fans; stepper motors with output not exceeding 37.5 watts; alternating 
current motors with output exceeding 18.65 watts but not exceeding 37.5 
watts; motors with output not exceeding 750 watts; amplifiers; static 
converters; lithium-ion batteries; battery packs; battery cases; 
distribution boards for voltage not exceeding 1,000 volts; circuit 
breakers; and, insulated electric conductors (duty rate ranges from 
duty-free to 5.7%).
    The proposed foreign-status materials/components include: plastic 
components (inverter cabinets; power stands; housings); printed 
catalogs; stainless steel components (screws; machine frames; cooling 
manifolds; nozzles; motor shafts; motor bearings); steel components 
(wire; mounting brackets; clips; finger guards; heat sinks; housings; 
bases; guide rails; carriages; spindle housings; protective guards; 
bearings; shafts; motor frames); iron mounting brackets; copper 
components (anchor brackets; electrical connections; motor coils; 
connectors); various fans (axial; blower and centrifugal; cooling); 
aluminum fan blades; liquid cooling fan systems; filters (fan; noise); 
brass nozzles; cables (connection; electrical); servo motors with power 
output from 18.65 watts to 37.5 watts; DC stepper motors with power 
output to 37.5 watts; servo motors with power output to 1k watts and 
up; single linear motors; generators; magnetic linear rails motor 
stators; motor rotors; AC/DC drivers; wiring harnesses; USBs; static 
amplifiers; power converters; static converter battery packs; permanent 
magnets; lithium-ion batteries; dry contacts; smart card storage 
devices; resistors for power handling capacity; wire wound variable 
resistors; sensors (temperature; humidity); receptacle boxes; 
controllers (programmable logic; electrical); power conditioners; 
electrical plastic boards; contact interface cards; fan motor 
integrated circuits; insulated plastic connector fittings; training and 
demonstration equipment for industrial motor control and automation 
systems; tachometers; airflow testers; and, carbon brushes for motors 
(duty rate ranges from duty-free to 8.5%).
    The request indicates that certain materials/components are subject 
to duties under section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, or 
section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, depending on the country of 
origin. The applicable section 232, and section 301 decisions require 
subject merchandise to be admitted to FTZs in privileged foreign status 
(19 CFR 146.41).
    Public comment is invited from interested parties. Submissions 
shall be addressed to the Board's Executive Secretary and sent to: 
[email protected]. The closing period for their receipt is November 18, 
2026.
    A copy of the notification will be available for public inspection 
in the ``Online FTZ Information System'' section of the Board's 
website.
