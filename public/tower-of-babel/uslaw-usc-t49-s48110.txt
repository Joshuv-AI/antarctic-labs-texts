49 U.S.C. § 48110 — Facilities for advanced training of maintenance technicians for air carrier aircraft
Citation: us:usc:49:48110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section48110
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Financing); Chapter 481 (Airport And Airway Trust Fund Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the fiscal years ending September 30, 1993–1995, amounts necessary to carry out section 44515 1 of this title may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation out of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund established under section 9502 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 9502). The amounts remain available until expended.
