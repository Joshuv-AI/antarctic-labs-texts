f\in S_{k}(\Gamma_{1}(N))
be a CM newform of even weight and
p
a prime.
Let
F
be the Hecke field and
\lambda
a prime above
p
.
Let
V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)
be the associated
Galois representation
and

H^{1}_{\rm{f}}({\mathbb{Q}},V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)(k/2))
the Bloch–Kato Selmer group.
Then

H^{1}_{\rm{f}}({\mathbb{Q}},V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)(k/2))=0\implies{\mathrm{ord}}_{s=k/2}L(s,f)=0.

Proof.

The following is based on Theorem
2.6
.

Let
T\subset V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)
be a
G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}
-stable
O_{\lambda}
-lattice.
Since
H^{1}_{\rm{f}}({\mathbb{Q}},V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)(k/2))=0
, by the exact sequence
[
12
, (14.9.3)]
,
note that

(3.1)

H^{2}({\mathbb{Z}}[1/p],T(k/2))\otimes{\mathbb{Q}}=0.

Let
\pm=(-1)^{k/2-1}
and pick
\gamma\in V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)
such that
\gamma^{\pm}\neq 0
. Let
z
be the image of
the Beilinson–Kato element
{\bf{z}}_{\gamma}(f)
(cf. Theorem
2.4
) under the map

(3.2)

{\mathbb{H}}^{1}(V_{F_{\lambda}}(f))\simeq{\mathbb{H}}^{1}(V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)(k/2))\rightarrow H^{1}({\mathbb{Z}}[1/p],T(k/2))\otimes{\mathbb{Q}}.

By (
3.1
) and the Euler–Poincaré formula of Tate
[
12
, (14.9.5)]
,

\dim_{F_{\lambda}}H^{1}({\mathbb{Z}}[1/p],T(k/2))\otimes{\mathbb{Q}}=1.

Let
{\mathfrak{q}}
be the kernel of the
O_{\lambda}
-homomorphism
\Lambda\rightarrow O_{\lambda}
mapping

\sigma_{c}
to
c^{-k/2}
for any
c\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}^{\times}
(cf. §
2.2.2
).
Note that the map (
3.2
) factors through

{\mathbb{H}}^{1}(V_{F_{\lambda}}(f))_{{\mathfrak{q}}}/{\mathfrak{q}}{\mathbb{H}}^{1}(V_{F_{\lambda}}(f))_{{\mathfrak{q}}},

and
{\mathbb{H}}^{2}(V_{F_{\lambda}}(f))_{{\mathfrak{q}}}
vanishes by (

).
Hence Theorem
2.6
implies that

z
is an
F_{\lambda}
-basis of

Since

{\mathrm{loc}}_{p}(z)\notin H^{1}_{\rm{f}}({\mathbb{Q}}_{p},V_{F_{\lambda}}(f)(k/2))

Remark 3.2
.

This deduction of the rank zero
p
-converse
from Kato’s main conjecture holds for any elliptic newform.

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.
Let
\psi:{\mathbb{A}}_{K}^{\times}/K^{\times}\rightarrow K^{\times}
be the Hecke character associated to
E_{/K}
. Let
f
and

\overline{f}
be the CM newforms associated to
\psi
and
\overline{\psi}
, the corresponding Hecke field being a subfield of
K
. Note that
L(s,E_{/K})=L(s,\psi)\cdot L(s,\overline{\psi})=L(s,f)\cdot L(s,\overline{f})
.
In the same vein one has

{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(E_{/K})^{\vee}\otimes K\simeq H^{1}_{\rm{f}}({\mathbb{Q}},V_{K_{\lambda}}(f))\oplus H^{1}_{\rm{f}}({\mathbb{Q}},V_{K_{{\lambda}}}(\overline{f})),

where
the superscript ‘
\vee
’ denotes the Pontryagin dual, and

V_{K_{\lambda}}(\cdot)=V_{F_{\lambda}}(\cdot)\otimes_{F}K
.
Hence, by Theorem
3.1
,

{\mathrm{corank}}_{{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}}{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(E_{/K})=0\implies{\mathrm{ord}}_{s=1}L(s,E_{/K})=0.

If
E
descends to
{\mathbb{Q}}
, then
{\mathrm{corank}}_{{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}}{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(E_{/K})=2\cdot{\mathrm{corank}}_{{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}}{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(E_{/{\mathbb{Q}}})
and
{\mathrm{ord}}_{s=1}L(s,E_{/K})=2\cdot{\mathrm{ord}}_{s=1}L(s,E_{/{\mathbb{Q}}})
, which concludes the proof of Theorem
1.1
.
