14 C.F.R. § 198.13 — Premium insurance—payment of premiums.
Citation: us:cfr:14:198.13
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-198.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 198 (Aviation Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The insured must pay the premium for insurance issued under this part within the stated period after receipt of notice that premium payment is due and in accordance with the provisions of the applicable FAA insurance policy. Premiums must be sent to the FAA, and made payable to the FAA.
