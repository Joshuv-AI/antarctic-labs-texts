18 U.S.C. § 2708 — Exclusivity of remedies
Citation: us:usc:18:2708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2708
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 121 (Stored Wire And Electronic Communications And Transactional Records Access). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The remedies and sanctions described in this chapter are the only judicial remedies and sanctions for nonconstitutional violations of this chapter.
