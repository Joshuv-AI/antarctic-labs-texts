21 C.F.R. § 251.20 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:21:251.20
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-251.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 251 (Section 804 Importation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are not separate and are not severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall not continue in effect.
