20 C.F.R. § 366.4 — Notification to debtor.
Citation: us:cfr:20:366.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-366.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 366 (Collection Of Debts By Federal Tax Refund Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notification provided by the Board to the debtor will inform the debtor how he or she may present evidence to the Board that all or part of the debt is not past due or legally enforceable.
