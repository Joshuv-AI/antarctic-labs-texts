24 C.F.R. § 108.21 — Civil rights/compliance reviewing office compliance responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:24:108.21
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-108.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 108 (Compliance Procedures For Affirmative Fair Housing Marketing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The civil rights/compliance reviewing office shall be responsible for determining whether an applicant's actions are in apparent compliance with its approved AFHM plan, the AFHM regulations, and this part and for determining changes or modifications necessary in the plan after initiation of marketing.
