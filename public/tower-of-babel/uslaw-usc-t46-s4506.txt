46 U.S.C. § 4506 — Exemptions
Citation: us:usc:46:4506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4506
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 45 (Uninspected Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may exempt a vessel from any part of this chapter if, under regulations prescribed by the Secretary (including regulations on special operating conditions), the Secretary finds that—
(1)
good cause exists for granting an exemption; and
(2)
the safety of the vessel and those on board will not be adversely affected.
