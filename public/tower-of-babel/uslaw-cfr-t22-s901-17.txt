22 C.F.R. § 901.17 — Charged employee.
Citation: us:cfr:22:901.17
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-901.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 901 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charged employee means a member of the Senior Foreign Service or a member of the Service assigned to a salary class who has been proposed for separation for cause under section 610(a)(2) of the Act.
