17 C.F.R. § 37.1200 — Core Principle 12—Conflicts of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.1200
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.1200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The swap execution facility shall:
(a) Establish and enforce rules to minimize conflicts of interest in its decision-making process; and
(b) Establish a process for resolving the conflicts of interest.
