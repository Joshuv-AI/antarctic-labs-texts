48 U.S.C. § 1644 — Import duties on articles entering United States or possessions from Virgin Islands
Citation: us:usc:48:1644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1644
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 12 (Virgin Islands [1954]); Subchapter VII (Fiscal Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All articles coming into the United States from the Virgin Islands shall be subject to or exempt from duty as provided for in section 1301a 1 of title 19 and subject to internal-revenue taxes as provided for in section 7652(b) of title 26.
