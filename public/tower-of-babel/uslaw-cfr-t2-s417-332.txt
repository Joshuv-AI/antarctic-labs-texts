2 C.F.R. § 417.332 — What methods must I use to pass down requirements to participants in lower-tier covered transactions with whom I intend to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:2:417.332
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-417.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 417 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You as a participant must include a term or condition in lower tier covered transactions requiring lower tier participants to comply with subpart C of the OMB guidance in 2 CFR part 180, as supplemented by subpart C of this part.
