46 U.S.C. § 4703 — Penalty for unlawful abandonment of barge
Citation: us:usc:46:4703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4703
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 47 (Abandonment Of Vessels); Subchapter I (Barges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Thirty days after the notification procedures under section 4704(a)(1) are completed, the Secretary may assess a civil penalty of not more than $1,000 for each day of the violation against an owner or operator that violates section 4702. A vessel with respect to which a penalty is assessed under this subchapter is liable in rem for the penalty.
