10 U.S.C. § 2470 — Depot-level activities of the Department of Defense: authority to compete for maintenance and repair workloads of other Federal agencies
Citation: us:usc:10:2470
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2470
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 146 (Contracting For Performance Of Civilian Commercial Or Industrial Type Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A depot-level activity of the Department of Defense shall be eligible to compete for the performance of any depot-level maintenance and repair workload of a Federal agency for which competitive procedures are used to select the entity to perform the workload.
