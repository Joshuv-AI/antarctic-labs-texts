Correction: Impact of Aspirin on Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Events in Patients with Elevated Lipoprotein(a): A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis
Authors: Daniel Caldeira, Mariana Alves, Rita Avó-Baião, Andreia Lopes, Luisa Prada, Fausto J. Pinto
Published: 2026

Correction: American Journal of Cardiovascular Drugs 10.1007/s40256-026-00795-8

The sentence in Section 3, paragraph 3, lines 3-7 read as: This article also had data from another cohort, the Cardiovascular Health Study (CHS), which was not included because the population considered to have high Lp(a) levels had a threshold of 8.8 mg/dL (under the 50 mg/dL considered in the eligibility criteria).

should read:

This article also reported data from a third cohort, the Cardiovascular Health Study (CHS), in which lipoprotein(a) was quantified using an older monoclonal-antibody ELISA that measured only the protein moiety of the Lp(a) particle, rather than whole lipoprotein(a) mass concentration. Colantonio et al. defined high Lp(a) in CHS using a percentile-based calibration (≥8.8 mg/dL of Lp(a) protein, corresponding to the 85th percentile in White participants without CVD, analogous to the 50 mg/dL whole-mass threshold in ARIC and MESA). Because our eligibility criterion required Lp(a) mass concentration ≥50 mg/dL (or its closest equivalent on a comparable assay), and a direct conversion between Lp(a) protein concentration and whole Lp(a) mass concentration is not available, CHS was not included in the present meta-analysis.

The original article has been corrected.
