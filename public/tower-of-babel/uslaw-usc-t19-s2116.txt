19 U.S.C. § 2116 — Agreements with developing countries
Citation: us:usc:19:2116
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2116
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter I (Negotiating And Other Authority); Part 1 (Rates Of Duty And Other Trade Barriers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A United States negotiating objective under sections 2111 and 2112 of this title shall be to enter into trade agreements which promote the economic growth of both developing countries and the United States and the mutual expansion of market opportunities.
