A. Overview of Information Collection

    Title of Information Collection: Congregate Housing Services 
Program (CHSP).
    OMB Approval Number: 2502-0485.
    Type of Request: Extension of a currently approved collection.
    Form Number: HUD-90006, HUD-90198, HUD-91180-A, 91178-A, SF-425.
    Description of the need for the information and proposed use: 
Grantees' completion of the Annual Report gives HUD the information 
needed to know who each grant is serving and what services participants 
receive with grant funds. The Summary Budget and Annual Program Budget 
together form the grantee's annual extension request, showing itemized 
anticipated costs and a matrix of budgeted yearly costs; including how 
matching funds, participant fees, and grant funds are combined to run 
the program. Field staff use this budget to approve annual extension 
funding requests and to confirm grantees are meeting statutory and 
regulatory requirements. The Payment Voucher lets HUD monitor grant 
fund use for eligible activities over the grant term, and grantees can 
similarly use it to track and record payment reimbursement requests.
    Respondents: State, Indian tribe, unit of general local government, 
or local nonprofit housing sponsor, IHA, PHA, or for-profit or not-for-
profit owner,
    Estimated Number of Respondents: 30.
    Estimated Number of Responses: 330.
    Frequency of Response: Semi-annually to annually.
    Average Hours per Response: .82.
    Total Estimated Burdens: 1,050.

B. Solicitation of Public Comment

    This notice is soliciting comments from members of the public and 
affected parties concerning the collection of information described in 
Section A on the following:
    (1) Whether the proposed collection of information is necessary for 
the proper performance of the functions of the agency, including 
whether the information will have practical utility;
    (2) The accuracy of the agency's estimate of the burden of the 
proposed collection of information;
    (3) Ways to enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of the 
information to be collected; and
    (4) Ways to minimize the burden of the collection of information on 
those who are to respond; including through the use of appropriate 
automated collection techniques or other forms of information 
technology, e.g., permitting electronic submission of responses.
    HUD encourages interested parties to submit comment in response to 
these questions.
