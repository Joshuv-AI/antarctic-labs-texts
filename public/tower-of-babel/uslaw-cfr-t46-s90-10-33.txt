46 C.F.R. § 90.10-33 — -33 Rivers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:90.10-33
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-90.10-33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 90 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under this designation shall be included all vessels whose navigation is restricted to rivers and/or canals exclusively, and to such other waters as may be so designated by the Coast Guard District Commander.
