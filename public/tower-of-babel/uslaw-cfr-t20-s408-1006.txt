20 C.F.R. § 408.1006 — What is the effect of an initial determination?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.1006
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.1006
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An initial determination is binding unless you request a reconsideration within the stated time period, or we revise the initial determination.
