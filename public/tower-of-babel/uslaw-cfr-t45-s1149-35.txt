45 C.F.R. § 1149.35 — When may an ALJ grant a motion for discovery?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.35
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The ALJ may grant a motion for discovery only if he/she finds that the discovery sought—
(1) Is necessary for the expeditious, fair, and reasonable consideration of the issues;
(2) Is not unduly costly or burdensome;
(3) Will not unduly delay the proceeding; and
(4) Does not seek privileged information.
(b) The burden of showing that discovery should be allowed is on the party seeking discovery.
(c) The ALJ may grant discovery subject to a protective order.
