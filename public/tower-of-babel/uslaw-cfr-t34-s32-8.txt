34 C.F.R. § 32.8 — Pre-offset hearing on the written submissions.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 32 (Salary Offset To Recover Overpayments Of Pay Or Allowances From Department Of Education Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
