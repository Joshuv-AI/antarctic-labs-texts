20 U.S.C. § 1140n — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:20:1140n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1140n
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part D (Programs To Provide Students With Disabilities With A Quality Higher Education); Subpart 3 (Commission On Accessible Materials; Programs To Support Improved Access To Materials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to limit or preempt any State law requiring the production or distribution of postsecondary instructional materials in accessible formats to students with disabilities.
