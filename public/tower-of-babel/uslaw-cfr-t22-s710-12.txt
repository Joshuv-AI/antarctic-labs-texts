22 C.F.R. § 710.12 — Finding of violation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:710.12
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-710.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 710 (Administrative Enforcement Procedures Of Post-Employment Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The President of DFC shall take appropriate action referred to in § 710.13 in the case of an individual who is found in violation of the statutory or regulatory post-employment restrictions, after a final administrative decision.
