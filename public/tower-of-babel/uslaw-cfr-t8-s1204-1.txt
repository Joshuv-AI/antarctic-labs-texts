8 C.F.R. § 1204.1 — Single level of appellate review.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1204.1
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1204.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1204 (Immigrant Petitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The decision of the Board of Immigration Appeals concerning the denial of a relative visa petition under 8 CFR chapter I, part 204 because the petitioner failed to establish eligibility for the bona fide marriage exemption contained in that part will constitute the single level of appellate review established by statute.
