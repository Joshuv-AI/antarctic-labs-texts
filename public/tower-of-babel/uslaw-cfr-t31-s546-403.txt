31 C.F.R. § 546.403 — Termination and acquisition of an interest in blocked property.
Citation: us:cfr:31:546.403
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-546.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 546 (Sudan Stabilization Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Whenever a transaction licensed or authorized by or pursuant to this part results in the transfer of property (including any property interest) away from a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 546.201, such property shall no longer be deemed to be property blocked pursuant to § 546.201, unless there exists in the property another interest that is blocked pursuant to § 546.201, the transfer of which has not been effected pursuant to license or other authorization.
(b) Unless otherwise specifically provided in a license or authorization issued pursuant to this part, if property (including any property interest) is transferred or attempted to be transferred to a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 546.201, such property shall be deemed to be property in which that person has an interest and therefore blocked.
