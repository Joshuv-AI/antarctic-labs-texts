7 C.F.R. § 1744.208 — Rural development investments before November 28, 1990.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1744.208
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1744.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1744 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Guaranteed And Insured Telephone Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All investments made by a borrower shall be subject to the provisions of this subpart, regardless of when the investment was made or whether it has been approved by RUS. Any restrictions required by RUS as a condition to approving a rural development investment before November 28, 1990, shall continue to be in effect to the extent that such investment exceeds the maximum investment ratio or causes the borrower to fall below the minimum total assets ratio.
