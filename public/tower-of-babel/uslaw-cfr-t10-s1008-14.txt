10 C.F.R. § 1008.14 — Requests under false pretenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1008.14
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1008.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1008 (Records Maintained On Individuals (Privacy Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subsection (i)(3) of the Act provides that any person who knowingly and willingly requests or obtains any record concerning an individual from an agency under false pretenses shall be quilty of a misdeamenaor and fined not more than $5,000.
