34 C.F.R. § 668.31 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:34:668.31
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-668.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 668 (Student Assistance General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains rules by which a student establishes eligibility for assistance under the title IV, HEA programs. In order to qualify as an eligible student, a student must meet all applicable requirements in this subpart.
