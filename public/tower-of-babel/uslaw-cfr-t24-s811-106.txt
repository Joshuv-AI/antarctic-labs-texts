24 C.F.R. § 811.106 — Default under the contract.
Citation: us:cfr:24:811.106
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-811.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 811 (Tax Exemption Of Obligations Of Public Housing Agencies And Related Amendments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If HUD finds there is a default under the Contract, the field office shall so notify the trustee and give the trustee a specified reasonable time to take action to require the owner to correct such default prior to any suspension or termination of payments under the contract. In the event of a default under the contract, HUD may terminate or suspend payments under the contract, may seek specific performance of the contract and may pursue other remedies.
