40 C.F.R. § 435.50 — Applicability; description of the beneficial use subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:435.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-435.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 435 (Oil And Gas Extraction Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to those onshore facilities located in the continental United States and west of the 98th meridian for which the produced water has a use in agriculture or wildlife propagation when discharged into navigable waters. These facilities are engaged in the production, drilling, well completion, and well treatment in the oil and gas extraction industry.
