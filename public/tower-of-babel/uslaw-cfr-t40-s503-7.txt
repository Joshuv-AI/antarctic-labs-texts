40 C.F.R. § 503.7 — Requirement for a person who prepares sewage sludge.
Citation: us:cfr:40:503.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-503.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 503 (Standards For The Use Or Disposal Of Sewage Sludge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who prepares sewage sludge shall ensure that the applicable requirements in this part are met when the sewage sludge is applied to the land, placed on a surface disposal site, or fired in a sewage sludge incinerator.
