45 C.F.R. § 641.20 — Notification of the availability of environmental documents and other information.
Citation: us:cfr:45:641.20
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-641.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 641 (Environmental Assessment Procedures For Proposed National Science Foundation Actions In Antarctica). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Environmental Officer, Office of Polar Programs, shall make Environmental Action Memoranda, environmental documents and final data obtained under § 641.21, available to the public upon request. However, notice of such availability need not be given, except as specifically provided in this part.
