43 C.F.R. § 30.183 — Who may receive a renounced interest in trust or restricted land if the land will descend pursuant to a valid will?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.183
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.183
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A devisee may renounce an interest in trust or restricted land in favor of any one or more of the following:
(a) A lineal descendant of the testator;
(b) A co-owner;
(c) The Tribe with jurisdiction over the interest; or
(d) Any Indian.
