50 U.S.C. § 2421 — Protection of national security information
Citation: us:usc:50:2421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2421
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 41 (National Nuclear Security Administration); Subchapter II (Matters Relating To Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Policies and procedures required
The Administrator shall establish procedures to ensure the maximum protection of classified information in the possession of the Administration.
(b) Prompt reporting
The Administrator shall establish procedures to ensure prompt reporting to the Administrator of any significant problem, abuse, violation of law or Executive order, or deficiency relating to the management of classified information by personnel of the Administration.
