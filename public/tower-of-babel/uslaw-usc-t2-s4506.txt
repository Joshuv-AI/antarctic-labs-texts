2 U.S.C. § 4506 — Death gratuity payments as gifts
Citation: us:usc:2:4506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4506
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter I (General); Part A (Amount And Type). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any death gratuity payment at any time specifically appropriated by any Act of Congress or at any time made out of the applicable accounts of the House of Representatives or the contingent fund of the Senate shall be held to have been a gift.
