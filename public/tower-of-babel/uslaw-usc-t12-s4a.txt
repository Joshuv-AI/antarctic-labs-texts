12 U.S.C. § 4a — Delegation of authority by Comptroller
Citation: us:usc:12:4a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 1 (The Comptroller Of The Currency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller of the Currency may delegate to any duly authorized employee, representative, or agent any power vested in the office by law.
