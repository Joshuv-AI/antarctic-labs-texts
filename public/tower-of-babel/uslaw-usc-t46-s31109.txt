46 U.S.C. § 31109 — Payment of judgment or settlement
Citation: us:usc:46:31109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31109
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 311 (Suits Involving Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The proper accounting officer of the United States shall pay a final judgment, arbitration award, or settlement under this chapter on presentation of an authenticated copy. Payment shall be made from any money in the Treasury appropriated for the purpose.
