46 U.S.C. § 5108 — Special exemptions
Citation: us:usc:46:5108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section5108
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Load Lines Of Vessels); Chapter 51 (Load Lines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may exempt a vessel from any part of this chapter when—
(1)
the vessel is entitled to an exemption under an international agreement to which the United States Government is a party; or
(2)
under regulations (including regulations on special operations conditions) prescribed by the Secretary, the Secretary finds that good cause exists for granting an exemption.
(b)
When the Secretary grants an exemption under this section, the Secretary may issue a certificate of exemption stating the extent of the exemption.
(c)
A certificate of exemption issued under subsection (b) of this section shall be maintained as required by the Secretary.
