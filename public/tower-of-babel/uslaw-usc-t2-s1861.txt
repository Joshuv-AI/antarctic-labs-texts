2 U.S.C. § 1861 — Appropriations under control of Architect of the Capitol
Citation: us:usc:2:1861
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1861
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter IV (Appropriations And Expenditures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations under the control of the Architect of the Capitol shall be available for expenses of advertising and personal and other services.
