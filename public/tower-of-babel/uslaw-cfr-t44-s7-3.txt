44 C.F.R. § 7.3 — Application of this regulation.
Citation: us:cfr:44:7.3
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-7.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs (Fema Reg. 5)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person in the United States shall, on the ground of race, color, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to discrimination under any program to which this regulation applies.
