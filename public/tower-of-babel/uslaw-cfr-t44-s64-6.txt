44 C.F.R. § 64.6 — List of eligible communities.
Citation: us:cfr:44:64.6
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-64.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 64 (Communities Eligible For The Sale Of Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FEMA will maintain a list of communities eligible for the sale of flood insurance pursuant to the National Flood Insurance Program (42 U.S.C. 4001-4128). This list will be published and maintained on the internet or through another comparable method.
