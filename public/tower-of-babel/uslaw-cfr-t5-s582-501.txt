5 C.F.R. § 582.501 — Rules, regulations, and directives by agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:5:582.501
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-582.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 582 (Commercial Garnishment Of Federal Employees' Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appropriate officials of all agencies shall, to the extent necessary, issue implementing rules, regulations, or directives that are consistent with this part or as are otherwise in accordance with statutory law.
