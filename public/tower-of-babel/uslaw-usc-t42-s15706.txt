(a) Federal Emergency Management Agency
There are authorized to be appropriated to the Federal Emergency Management Agency for carrying out this chapter—
(1)
$5,332,000 for fiscal year 2015;
(2)
$5,332,000 for fiscal year 2016; and
(3)
$5,332,000 for fiscal year 2017.

$9,682,000 for fiscal year 2015;
(2)
$9,682,000 for fiscal year 2016; and
(3)
$9,682,000 for fiscal year 2017.

$4,120,000 for fiscal year 2015;
(2)
$4,120,000 for fiscal year 2016; and

$4,120,000 for fiscal year 2017.
