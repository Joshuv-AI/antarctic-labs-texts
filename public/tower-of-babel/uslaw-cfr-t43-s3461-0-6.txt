43 C.F.R. § 3461.0-6 — -6 Policy.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3460 (Environment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department shall carry out the review of Federal lands under section 522(b) of the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 (30 U.S.C. 1272(b)) principally through land use planning assessments by the surface management agency regarding the unsuitability of Federal lands for all or certain stipulated methods of coal mining.
