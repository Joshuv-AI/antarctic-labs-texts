16 U.S.C. § 669i — Rules and regulations

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5B (Wildlife Restoration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to make rules and regulations for carrying out the provisions of this chapter.
