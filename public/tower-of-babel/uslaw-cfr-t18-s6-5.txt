18 C.F.R. § 6.5 — Annual charges.
Citation: us:cfr:18:6.5
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-6.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 6 (Surrender Or Termination Of License). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Annual charges arising under a license surrendered or terminated shall continue until the effective date set forth in the Commission's order with respect to such surrender or termination.
