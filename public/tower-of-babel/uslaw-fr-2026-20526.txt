DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Office of Foreign Assets Control


Notice of OFAC Sanctions Actions

AGENCY: Office of Foreign Assets Control, Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets 
Control (OFAC) is publishing the names of one or more persons whose 
property and interests in property have been

[[Page 64225]]

unblocked and who have been removed from the Specially Designated 
Nationals and Blocked Persons List (SDN List). OFAC is also publishing 
the names of blocked property and persons whose property and interests 
in property remain blocked and on the SDN List or subject to the 
prohibitions of the Non-SDN Palestinian Legislative Council (NS-PLC) 
List, or the Sectoral Sanctions Identifications (SSI) List, but whose 
duplicate entries have been removed from OFAC sanctions lists. OFAC is 
also publishing updates to the identifying information of one or more 
persons or property currently included on the SDN List, NS-PLC List, or 
SSI List whose property and interests in property remain blocked or 
subject to the prohibitions of those lists, as applicable.

DATES: See SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION for relevant dates.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: OFAC: Associate Director for Global 
Targeting, 202-622-2420; Assistant Director for Licensing, 202-622-
2480; Assistant Director for Sanctions Compliance, 202-622-2490 or 
https://ofac.treasury.gov/contact-ofac.
