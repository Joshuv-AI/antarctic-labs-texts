41 C.F.R. § 102-3.110 — -3.110 Responsibilities of a chairperson of an independent Presidential advisory committee.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-3.110
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-3.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-3 (3—Federal Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The chairperson of an independent Presidential advisory committee must:
(a) Consult with the Secretariat concerning the designation of a CMO and DFO; and
(b) Consult with the Secretariat in advance regarding any proposal to close any meeting in whole or in part.
