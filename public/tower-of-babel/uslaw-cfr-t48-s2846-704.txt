48 C.F.R. § 2846.704 — 2846.704 Authority for use of warranties.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2846.704
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2846.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2846 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of a warranty in an acquisition shall be approved at a level above the contracting officer.
