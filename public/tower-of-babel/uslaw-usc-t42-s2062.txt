42 U.S.C. § 2062 — Irradiation of materials
Citation: us:usc:42:2062
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2062
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter IV (Production Of Special Nuclear Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission and persons lawfully producing or utilizing special nuclear material are authorized to expose materials of any kind to the radiation incident to the processes of producing or utilizing special nuclear material.
