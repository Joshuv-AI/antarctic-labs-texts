49 C.F.R. § 397.77 — Judicial review of dispute decision.
Citation: us:cfr:49:397.77
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-397.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 397 (Transportation Of Hazardous Materials; Driving And Parking Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any State or Indian tribe adversely affected by the Administrator's decision under § 397.75 of this subpart may seek review by the appropriate district court of the United States under such proceeding only by filing a petition with such court within 90 days after such decision becomes final.
