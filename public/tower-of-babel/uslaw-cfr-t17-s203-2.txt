17 C.F.R. § 203.2 — Information obtained in investigations and examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:203.2
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-203.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 203 (Rules Relating To Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information or documents obtained by the Commission in the course of any investigation or examination, unless made a matter of public record, shall be deemed non-public, but the Commission approves the practice whereby officials of the Divisions of Enforcement, Examinations, Corporation Finance, Trading and Markets, Investment Management, and the Office of International Affairs at the level of Assistant Director or higher, may engage in and may authorize members of the Commission's staff to engage in discussions with persons identified in § 240.24c-1(b) of this chapter concerning information obtained in individual investigations or examinations, including formal investigations conducted pursuant to Commission order.
