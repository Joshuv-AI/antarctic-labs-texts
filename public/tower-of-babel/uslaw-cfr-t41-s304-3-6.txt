41 C.F.R. § 304-3.6 — -3.6 Fly America Act compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.6
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are not required to use U.S. flag air carrier service when receiving air transportation paid in full directly by the non-Federal source or fully reimbursed to the agency by the non-Federal source.
