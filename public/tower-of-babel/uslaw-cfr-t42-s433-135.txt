42 C.F.R. § 433.135 — Basis and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:433.135
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-433.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 433 (State Fiscal Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements sections 1902(a)(25), 1902(a)(45), 1903(d)(2), 1903(o), 1903(p), and 1912 of the Act by setting forth State plan requirements concerning—
(a) The legal liability of third parties to pay for services provided under the plan;
(b) Assignment to the State of an individual's rights to third party payments; and
(c) Cooperative agreements between the Medicaid agency and other entities for obtaining third party payments.
