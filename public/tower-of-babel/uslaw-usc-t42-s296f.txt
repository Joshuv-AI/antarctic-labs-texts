42 U.S.C. § 296f — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:296f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section296f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter VI (Nursing Workforce Development); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated under this subchapter may be used by the Secretary to provide technical assistance in relation to any of the authorities under this subchapter.
