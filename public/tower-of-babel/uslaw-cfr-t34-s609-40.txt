34 C.F.R. § 609.40 — What are the matching requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:34:609.40
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-609.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 609 (Strengthening Historically Black Graduate Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an institution receives a grant in excess of $500,000, it must spend non-Federal funds to meet the cost of at least 50 percent of the activities approved in its application.
