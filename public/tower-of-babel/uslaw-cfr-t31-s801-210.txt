31 C.F.R. § 801.210 — Pilot program covered transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:31:801.210
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-801.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 801 (Pilot Program To Review Certain Transactions Involving Foreign Persons And Critical Technologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term pilot program covered transaction means:
(a) Any pilot program covered investment; or
(b) Any transaction by or with any foreign person that could result in foreign control of any pilot program U.S. business, including such a transaction carried out through a joint venture.
