31 C.F.R. § 526.316 — United States national.
Citation: us:cfr:31:526.316
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-526.316
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 526 (Hostages And Wrongful Detention Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term United States national means a “national of the United States” as defined in 8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(22) or 8 U.S.C. 1408, or a lawful permanent resident with significant ties to the United States.
