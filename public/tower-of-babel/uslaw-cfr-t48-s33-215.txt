48 C.F.R. § 33.215 — 33.215 Contract clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:33.215
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-33.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 33 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Insert the clause at 52.233-1, Disputes, in solicitations and contracts, unless the conditions in 33.203(b) apply. If it is determined under agency procedures that continued performance is necessary pending resolution of any claim arising under or relating to the contract, the contracting officer shall use the clause with its Alternate I.
(b) Insert the clause at 52.233-4 in all solicitations and contracts.
