45 C.F.R. § 1634.12 — Emergency procedures and waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1634.12
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1634.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1634 (Competitive Bidding For Grants And Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The President of the Corporation may waive the requirements of §§ 1634.6 and 1634.8(a)(3) and (5) when necessary to comply with requirements imposed by law on the awards of grants and contracts for a particular fiscal year.
