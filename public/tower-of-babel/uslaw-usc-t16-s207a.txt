16 U.S.C. § 207a — Application of Federal Power Act to lands acquired under section 207
Citation: us:usc:16:207a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section207a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXII (Lassen Volcanic National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of the Federal Power Act [16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.] shall not apply to or extend over the land acquired for inclusion in the Lassen Volcanic National Park in accordance with the provisions of this section and section 207 of this title.
