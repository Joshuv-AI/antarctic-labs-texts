2 C.F.R. § 1400.1012 — The OIG Administrative Remedies Division (ARD).
Citation: us:cfr:2:1400.1012
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1400.1012
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1400 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OIG ARD prepares and forwards suspension and/or debarment action referral memoranda to the Suspending and Debarring Official and may provide additional assistance, in the course of action proceedings.
