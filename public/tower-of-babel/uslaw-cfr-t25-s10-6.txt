25 C.F.R. § 10.6 — How is the BIA assured that the policies and standards are being applied uniformly and facilities are properly accredited?
Citation: us:cfr:25:10.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-10.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 10 (Indian Country Detention Facilities And Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tribes and BIA programs will use a phased approach to meeting all non-mandatory detention standards and will document progress on uniform reporting. The BIA Office of Law Enforcement Services will conduct periodic operational evaluations for oversight.
