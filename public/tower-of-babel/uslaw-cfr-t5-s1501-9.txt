5 C.F.R. § 1501.9 — Cases reviewable by the Board.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1501.9
Creator: The International Organizations Employees Loyalty Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1501.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1501 (Operations Of The International Organizations Employees Loyalty Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All cases in which an investigation has been made under Executive Order 10422, as amended, shall be referred to and reviewed by the Board in accordance with the Executive Order and the rules and regulations of the Board.
