48 C.F.R. § 236.604 — 236.604 Performance evaluation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:236.604
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-236.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 236 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prepare a separate performance evaluation after actual construction of the project. Ordinarily, the evaluating official should be the person most familiar with the architect-engineer contractor's performance.
