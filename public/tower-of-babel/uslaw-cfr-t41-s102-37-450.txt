41 C.F.R. § 102-37.450 — -37.450 Vehicle title transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.450
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Title to the vehicle rests with the holding agency until the SF 97-1 is signed by the donee upon receipt of the surplus motor vehicle. If applicable under the terms of the donation, the title will be conditional until the end of the period of restriction.
