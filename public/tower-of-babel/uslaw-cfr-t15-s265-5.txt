15 C.F.R. § 265.5 — Laws of Maryland and Colorado applicable.
Citation: us:cfr:15:265.5
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-265.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 265 (Regulations Governing Traffic And Conduct On The Grounds Of The National Institute Of Standards & Technology, Gaithersburg, Maryland, And Boulder And Fort Collins, Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specifically provided herein, the laws of the State of Maryland and of the State of Colorado shall be applicable to the site located within those respective States. The applicability of State laws shall not, however, affect or abrogate any other Federal law or regulation applicable under the circumstances.
