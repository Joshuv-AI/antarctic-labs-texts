15 U.S.C. § 2707 — Patents and inventions; statutory provisions applicable; contracts or grants covered

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 54 (Automotive Propulsion Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 5908 of title 42 shall apply to any contract (including any assignment, substitution of parties, or subcontract thereunder) or grant, entered into, made, or issued by the Secretary of Energy under this chapter.
