25 U.S.C. § 1303 — Habeas corpus
Citation: us:usc:25:1303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1303
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 15 (Constitutional Rights Of Indians); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The privilege of the writ of habeas corpus shall be available to any person, in a court of the United States, to test the legality of his detention by order of an Indian tribe.
