30 C.F.R. § 810.3 — Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:30:810.3
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-810.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 810 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall approve and promulgate minimum coal exploration and surface mining and reclamation operations performance standards and design requirements applicable under regulatory programs which are at least as stringent as subchapter K in accordance with subchapter C of this chapter.
