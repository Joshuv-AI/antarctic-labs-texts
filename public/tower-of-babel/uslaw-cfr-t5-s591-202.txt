5 C.F.R. § 591.202 — Why does the Government pay COLAs?
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Government pays COLAs as additional compensation to certain civilian Federal employees in specified nonforeign areas in consideration of higher living costs in the local area compared with living costs in the Washington, DC, area.
