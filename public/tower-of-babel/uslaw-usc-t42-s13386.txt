42 U.S.C. § 13386 — Export of domestic energy resource technologies to developing countries
Citation: us:usc:42:13386
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13386
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter VII (Global Climate Change). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, through the Trade Promotion Coordinating Council, shall develop policies and programs to encourage the export and promotion of domestic energy resource technologies, including renewable energy, energy efficiency, and clean coal technologies, to developing countries.
