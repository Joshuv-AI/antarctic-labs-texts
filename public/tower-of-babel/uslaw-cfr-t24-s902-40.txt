24 C.F.R. § 902.40 — Management operations assessment.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 902 (Public Housing Assessment System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Objective. The objective of the management operations indicator is to measure the PHA's performance of management operations through the management performance of each project.
(b) Exclusions. Mixed-finance projects are excluded from the management operations indicator.
