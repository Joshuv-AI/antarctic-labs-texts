45 C.F.R. § 156.606 — HHS audit authority.
Citation: us:cfr:45:156.606
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-156.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 156 (Health Insurance Issuer Standards Under The Affordable Care Act, Including Standards Related To Exchanges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may audit a plan or program recognized as minimum essential coverage under § 156.604 at any time to ensure compliance with the requirements of § 156.604(a).
