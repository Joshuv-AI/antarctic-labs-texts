25 C.F.R. § 12.13 — What happens if I do not follow the rules in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:12.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-12.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 12 (Indian Country Law Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your BIA law enforcement commission may be revoked, your law enforcement contract may be canceled, and you may no longer be eligible for tribal shares allocated from the law enforcement budget.
