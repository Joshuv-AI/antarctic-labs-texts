22 U.S.C. § 9688 — Incidental transfers
Citation: us:usc:22:9688
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9688
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter VI (Transitional Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget, in consultation with the Corporation, is authorized and directed to make such additional incidental dispositions of personnel, assets, and liabilities held, used, arising from, available, or to be made available, in connection with the functions transferred by this subchapter, as the Director may determine necessary to accomplish the purposes of this chapter.
