2 U.S.C. § 6504 — Committee on Rules and Administration; designation of employees to approve vouchers for payments from Senate contingent fund
Citation: us:usc:2:6504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6504
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Committee on Rules and Administration may authorize its chairman to designate any employee or employees of such Committee to approve in his behalf, all vouchers making payments from the contingent fund of the Senate, such approval to be deemed and held to be approval by the Committee on Rules and Administration for all intents and purposes.
