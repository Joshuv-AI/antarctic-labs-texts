14 U.S.C. § 2703 — Contingent expenses
Citation: us:usc:14:2703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2703
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter I (Personnel Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commandant may expend for contingencies of the Coast Guard a sum not to exceed $50,000 in any one fiscal year.
