41 C.F.R. § 102-194.10 — -194.10 What is a Standard form?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-194.10
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-194.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-194 (194—Standard And Optional Forms Management Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Standard form is a fixed or sequential order of data elements, prescribed by a Federal agency through regulation, approved by GSA for mandatory use, and assigned a Standard form number. This criterion is the same whether the form resides on paper or purely electronic.
