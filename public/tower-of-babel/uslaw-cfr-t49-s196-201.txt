49 C.F.R. § 196.201 — What is the purpose and scope of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:49:196.201
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-196.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 196 (Protection Of Underground Pipelines From Excavation Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes the enforcement authority and sanctions exercised by the Associate Administrator for Pipeline Safety for achieving and maintaining pipeline safety under this part. It also prescribes the procedures governing the exercise of that authority and the imposition of those sanctions.
