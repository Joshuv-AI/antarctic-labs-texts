40 C.F.R. § 420.74 — New source performance standards (NSPS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.74
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The discharge of wastewater pollutants from any new source subject to this subpart shall not exceed the standards set forth below.
(a) Primary mills, carbon and specialty—(1) Without scarfing.
(2) With scarfing.
(b) Section mills—(1) Carbon.
(2) Specialty.
(c) Flat mills—(1) Hot strip and sheet mills, carbon and specialty.
(2) Carbon plate mills.
(3) Specialty plate mills.
(d) Pipe and tube mills, carbon and specialty.
