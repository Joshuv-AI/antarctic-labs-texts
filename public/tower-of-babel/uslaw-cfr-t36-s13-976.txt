36 C.F.R. § 13.976 — Fire.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.976
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.976
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lighting or maintaining a fire is prohibited in the FDA except—
(a) In established receptacles within designated campgrounds;
(b) From October 1 through April 14 in that portion of the FDA where the park road is closed to motor vehicle use; and
(c) Under conditions that may be established by the Superintendent.
