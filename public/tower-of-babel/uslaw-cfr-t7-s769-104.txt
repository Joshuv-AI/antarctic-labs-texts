7 C.F.R. § 769.104 — Requirements of the ultimate recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:7:769.104
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-769.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 769 (Farm Loan Programs Relending Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Ultimate recipients must be individual Tribal members, Tribes or eligible Tribal entities, with authority to incur the debt and carry out the purpose of the loan.
(b) The intermediary lender will make this determination in accordance with the Intermediary Relending Agreement.
