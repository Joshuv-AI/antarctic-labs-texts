13 U.S.C. § 81 — Statistics on apparel and textile industries
Citation: us:usc:13:81
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section81
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 3 (Collection And Publication Of Statistics); Subchapter III (Apparel And Textiles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall collect and publish quarterly statistics relating to domestic apparel and textile industries.
