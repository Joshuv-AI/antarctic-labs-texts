20 C.F.R. § 361.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:20:361.1
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-361.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 361 (Recovery Of Debts Owed To The United States Government By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations, which implement 5 U.S.C. 5514, provide the standards and procedures which the Board will utilize to collect debts owed to the United States from the current pay accounts of its employees, including the current pay accounts of employees who owe debts to agencies other than the Board.
