33 U.S.C. § 1486 — Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund
Citation: us:usc:33:1486
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1486
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 28 (Pollution Casualties On The High Seas: United States Intervention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund shall be available to the Secretary for actions taken under sections 1474 and 1476 of this title.
