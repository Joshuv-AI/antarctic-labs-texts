41 C.F.R. § 50-204.69 — -204.69 Nitrous oxide.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-204.69
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-204.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-204 (204—Safety And Health Standards For Federal Supply Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The piped systems for the in-plant transfer and distribution of nitrous oxide shall be designed, installed, maintained, and operated in accordance with Compressed Gas Association Pamphlet G-8.1-1964.
