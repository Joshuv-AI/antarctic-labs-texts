46 C.F.R. § 161.002-10 — -10 Fire detection system control unit.
Citation: us:cfr:46:161.002-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-161.002-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 161 (Electrical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The fire detection system control unit must meet the requirements of § 111.01-9 of this chapter.
(b) Electrical supervision—Circuits. The circuits must comply with Chapter 23 of NFPA 72 (incorporated by reference, see § 161.002-1), and must be Class A or Class X pathway.
