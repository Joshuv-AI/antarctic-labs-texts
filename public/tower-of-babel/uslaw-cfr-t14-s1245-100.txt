14 C.F.R. § 1245.100 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1245.100
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1245.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1245 (Patents And Other Intellectual Property Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes regulations for the waiver of rights of the Government of the United States to inventions made under NASA contract in conformity with section 20135 of the National Aeronautics and Space Act (51 U.S.C. Chapter 201).
