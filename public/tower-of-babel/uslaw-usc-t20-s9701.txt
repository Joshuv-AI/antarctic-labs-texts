20 U.S.C. § 9701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:20:9701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9701
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 77 (Financial Literacy And Education Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
(1)
the term “Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Financial Literacy and Education Commission; and
(2)
the term “Commission” means the Financial Literacy and Education Commission established under section 9702 of this title.
