41 C.F.R. § 128-1.8010 — -1.8010 Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-1.8010
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-1.8010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart is intended to create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law by a party against the Department of Justice, its Seismic Safety Coordinators, its officers, or any employee of the Department.
