34 C.F.R. § 270.2 — Who is eligible to receive a grant under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:270.2
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-270.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 270 (Equity Assistance Center Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A public agency (other than a State educational agency or a school board) or private, nonprofit organization is eligible to receive a grant under this program.
