45 C.F.R. § 2553.51 — What are the terms of service of an RSVP volunteer?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.51
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An RSVP volunteer shall serve on a regular basis, or intensively on short-term assignments, consistent with the assignment description.
