14 C.F.R. § 221.52 — Airport to airport application, accessorial services.
Citation: us:cfr:14:221.52
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-221.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 221 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tariffs shall specify whether or not the fares therein include services in addition to airport-to-airport transportation.
