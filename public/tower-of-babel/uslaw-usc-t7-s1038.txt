7 U.S.C. § 1038 — Regulations; delegations of authority
Citation: us:usc:7:1038
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1038
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 33 (Farm Tenancy); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may make such rules and regulations and such delegations of authority as he may deem necessary to carry out the provisions of sections 1033 to 1039 of this title.
