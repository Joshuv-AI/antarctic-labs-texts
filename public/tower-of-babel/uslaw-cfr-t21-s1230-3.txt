21 C.F.R. § 1230.3 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1230.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1230.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1230 (Regulations Under The Federal Caustic Poison Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The word container as used in the regulations in this part means a retail parcel, package, or container suitable for household use and employed exclusively to hold any dangerous caustic or corrosive substance defined in the act.
(b) The words suitable for household use mean and imply adaptability for ready or convenient handling in places where people dwell.
