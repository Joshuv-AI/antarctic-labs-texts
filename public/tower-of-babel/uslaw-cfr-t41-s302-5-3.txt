41 C.F.R. § 302-5.3 — -5.3 Persons authorized to travel on a househunting trip at Government expense.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-5.3
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-5.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-5 (5—Allowance For Househunting Trip Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only the employee and their spouse or domestic partner may travel on a househunting trip at Government expense. If traveling separately, the reimbursement will be limited to the cost that would have been incurred if the employee and their spouse or domestic partner had travelled together on one round trip.
