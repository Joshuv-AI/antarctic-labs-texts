7 U.S.C. § 6512 — Other production and handling practices
Citation: us:usc:7:6512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6512
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 94 (Organic Certification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a production or handling practice is not prohibited or otherwise restricted under this chapter, such practice shall be permitted unless it is determined that such practice would be inconsistent with the applicable organic certification program.
