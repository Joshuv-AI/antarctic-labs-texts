Abstract
After decades of experimental efforts, the DAMA/LIBRA( DL \rm DL ) annual modulation (AM) analysis on the χ ​ N \chi{\rm N} (WIMP Dark Matter interactions on nucleus) channel remains the only one which can be interpreted as positive signatures. This has been refuted by numerous time-integrated (TI) and AM analysis. It has been shown that χ ​ e \rm\chi e (WIMP interactions with electrons) alone is not compatible with the DL AM data. We expand the investigations by performing an AM analysis with the addition of χ ​ e \rm\chi e long-range and short-range interactions to χ ​ N \chi{\rm N} , derived using the Frozen Core Approximation method. Two scenarios are considered, where the χ ​ N \chi{\rm N} and χ ​ e \rm\chi e processes are due to a single χ \chi ( Γ t ​ o ​ t 1 ​ χ {\Gamma}_{tot}^{1\chi} ) or two different χ \chi ’s ( Γ t ​ o ​ t 2 ​ χ {\Gamma}_{tot}^{2\chi} ). The combined fits with χ ​ N \chi{\rm N} and χ ​ e \rm\chi e provide stronger significance to the DL \rm DL AM data which are compatible with the presence of additional physical effects beyond χ ​ N \chi{\rm N} alone. This is the first analysis which explores how χ ​ e \rm\chi e AM can play a role in DL AM. The revised allowed regions as well as the exclusion contours from the other null AM experiments are presented. All DL AM allowed parameter spaces in χ ​ N \chi{\rm N} and χ ​ e \rm\chi e channels under both Γ t ​ o ​ t 1 ​ χ {\Gamma}_{tot}^{1\chi} and Γ t ​ o ​ t 2 ​ χ {\Gamma}_{tot}^{2\chi} are excluded at the 90% confidence level by the combined null AM results. It can be projected that DL-allowed parameter spaces from generic models with interactions induced by two-WIMPs are ruled out.



I
Introduction

There are compelling experimental evidence that about
one-quarter of the energy density of the Universe
is composed of dark matter (DM),
whose exact nature and properties remain unknown.
Searches of DM in numerous
directions with diverse techniques
are intense areas of fundamental research
Zyla et al. 2020
.
A favored DM candidate is the
weakly interacting massive particle
(WIMP, denoted as
\chi
)
Nilles 1984
;
Peskin 2015
.

Direct experimental searches assume finite interactions between
WIMPs with electrons (
\rm\chi e
) and nuclei (
\chi{\rm N}
).
Positive signatures manifest as
excess events over known background in the measured “time-integrated” (TI) energy spectra
and in their annual modulation (AM) due to
changes of the relative velocity between the Earth and the WIMPs in
the galactic halo
Drukier et al. 1986
.
TI analysis is sensitive to uncertainties of background modeling
while AM analysis only requires the background is stable with time
but independent of other details.

After decades of experimental efforts,
the only result consistent with positive WIMP signatures
is from the AM analysis on
\chi{\rm N}

from the DAMA/LIBRA(
\rm DL
) experiment with NaI(Tl)
scintillating crystals
Bernabei et al. 2008a
.
This interpretation, however, is challenged and rejected by
numerous
\chi{\rm N}
experiments with TI analysis

and several with
AM analysis
Abe et al. 2018
;
Yang et al. 2019
;
Amaré et al. 2021
;
Carlin et al. 2024
;
Coarasa et al. 2023

using a variety of targets.
In particular, the COSINE
Carlin et al. 2024
and
ANAIS
Coarasa et al. 2023
experiments
adopt the same NaI(Tl) as target nuclei as DL.
There are attempts to explain the
\rm DL
AM data
with scenarios other than
\chi{\rm N}
detection,
such as complications in the
analysis procedures
Adhikari et al. 2018
;
Buttazzo et al. 2020
.

II
Formulation

We performed an analysis
including
both

\rm\chi e
and
\chi{\rm N}
interactions.
The combined analysis would provide improved
statistical fit to the
\rm DL
data.
Three types of WIMPs interactions are included:
spin-independent WIMP-nucleon interaction (
\rm\chi N^{SI}
),
long and short-range WIMP-electron interaction
(
\rm\chi e^{LR}
and
\rm\chi e^{SR}
, respectively)
Pandey et al. 2020
.
Their cross-sections (respectively
\sigma^{\rm SI}_{\chi{\rm N}}
,
\sigma^{\rm LR}_{\rm\chi e}
and
\sigma^{\rm SR}_{\rm\chi e}
)
are all functions of
\rm m_{\chi}
.

IV
Conclusion

Our
{\Gamma}_{tot}^{2\chi}
analysis and results indicate that
the DL AM allowed regions associated with
\rm\chi e
are probed
and rejected by the various null AM experiments.
The CDEX
Yang et al. 2019
data placed more severe constraints on

\sigma^{\rm LR}_{\rm\chi e}
where the
(1/q^{2})
dependence favors
low threshold experiments.
The XMASS
Abe et al. 2018
experiment is more sensitive
to
\sigma^{\rm SR}_{\rm\chi e}
due to its large exposure.

It can be projected that the DL allowed regions
in generic two-WIMP models

\rm\chi e

Very little room is left to account for the DL AM data with
