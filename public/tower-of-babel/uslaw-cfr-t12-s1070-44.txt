12 C.F.R. § 1070.44 — Disclosure of confidential consumer complaint information.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1070.44
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1070.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1070 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CFPB may, to the extent permitted by law, disclose confidential consumer complaint information as it deems necessary to investigate, resolve, or otherwise respond to consumer complaints or inquiries concerning consumer financial products and services or a violation of Federal consumer financial law.
