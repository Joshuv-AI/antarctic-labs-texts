21 C.F.R. § 1314.50 — Employment measures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1314.50
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1314.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1314 (Retail Sale Of Scheduled Listed Chemical Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A regulated seller may take reasonable measures to guard against employing individuals who may present a risk with respect to the theft and diversion of scheduled listed chemical products, which may include, notwithstanding State law, asking applicants for employment whether they have been convicted of any crime involving or related to such products or controlled substances.
