12 C.F.R. § 304.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:304.1
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-304.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 304 (Forms, Instructions, And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart informs the public where it may obtain forms and instructions for reports, applications, and other submittals used by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), and describes certain forms that are not described elsewhere in FDIC regulations in this chapter.
