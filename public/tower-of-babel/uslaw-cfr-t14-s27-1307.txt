14 C.F.R. § 27.1307 — Miscellaneous equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.1307
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.1307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following is the required miscellaneous equipment:
(a) An approved seat for each occupant.
(b) An approved safety belt for each occupant.
(c) A master switch arrangement.
(d) An adequate source of electrical energy, where electrical energy is necessary for operation of the rotorcraft.
(e) Electrical protective devices.
