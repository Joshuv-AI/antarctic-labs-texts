15 C.F.R. § 1400.6 — Construction.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1400.6
Creator: Minority Business Development Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1400.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1400 (Determination Of Group Eligibility For Mbda Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this regulation shall be construed as subjecting any functions vested in, or assigned pursuant to law to any Federal department or agency or head thereof to the authority of any other agency or office exclusively, or as abrogating or restricting such functions in any manner.
