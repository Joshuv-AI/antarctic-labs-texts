13 C.F.R. § 136.102 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:13:136.102
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-136.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 136 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Small Business Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all programs or activities conducted by the Small Business Administration except for programs or activities conducted outside the United States that do not involve individuals with handicaps in the United States.
