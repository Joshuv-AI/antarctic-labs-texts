22 U.S.C. § 283 — Acceptance of membership by United States in Inter-American Development Bank
Citation: us:usc:22:283
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section283
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XII (Inter-American Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is hereby authorized to accept membership for the United States in the Inter-American Development Bank (hereinafter referred to as the “Bank”), provided for by the agreement establishing the bank (hereinafter referred to as the “agreement”) deposited in the archives of the Organization of American States.
