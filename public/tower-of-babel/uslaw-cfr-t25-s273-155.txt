25 C.F.R. § 273.155 — How will the existing contracting party know when reports are due?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.155
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BIE Director will provide existing contracting parties with timely information relating to:
(a) Initial and final reporting deadlines; and
(b) The consequences of failure to comply.
