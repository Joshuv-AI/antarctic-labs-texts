7 C.F.R. § 1427.174 — Maturity of seed cotton loans.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1427.174
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1427.174
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1427 (Cotton). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Seed cotton loans mature on demand by CCC but no later than May 31 following the calendar year in which such crop is normally harvested.
