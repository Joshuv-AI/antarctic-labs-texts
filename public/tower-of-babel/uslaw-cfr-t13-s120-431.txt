13 C.F.R. § 120.431 — Which Lenders may sell, sell participations in, or pledge 7(a) loans?
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.431
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.431
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Lenders may sell, sell participations in, or pledge 7(a) loans in accordance with this subpart.
