32 C.F.R. § 239.12 — Tax documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:239.12
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-239.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 239 (Homeowners Assistance Program—Application Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For disbursed funds, tax documents (if necessary) will be certified by HQUSACE Finance Center and distributed to applicants and the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) annually.
