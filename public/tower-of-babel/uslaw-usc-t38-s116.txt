38 U.S.C. § 116 — Reports to Congress: cost information
Citation: us:usc:38:116
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section116
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 1 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary submits to Congress, or any committee of Congress, a report that is required by law or by a joint explanatory statement of a committee of conference of the Congress, the Secretary shall include with the report—
(1)
a statement of the cost of preparing the report; and
(2)
a brief explanation of the methodology used in preparing that cost statement.
