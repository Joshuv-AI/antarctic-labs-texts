49 C.F.R. § 39.41 — May a passenger with a disability be required to travel with another person?
Citation: us:cfr:49:39.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-39.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 39 (Transportation For Individuals With Disabilities: Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must not require that a passenger with a disability travel with another person as a condition of being provided transportation on or use of a passenger vessel.
(b) Your personnel are not required to perform personal tasks (e.g., assisting with eating, dressing, toileting) for a passenger.
