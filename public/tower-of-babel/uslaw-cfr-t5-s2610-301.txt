5 C.F.R. § 2610.301 — Jurisdiction of adjudicative officer.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2610.301
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2610.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2610 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any provision in the Office's rules and regulations other than this part which limits or terminates the jurisdiction of an adjudicative officer upon the effective date of his or her decision in the underlying proceeding shall not in any way affect his or her jurisdiction to render a decision under this part.
