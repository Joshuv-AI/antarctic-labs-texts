7 C.F.R. § 3800.4 — Authority to act for the Chairperson.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3800.4
Creator: World Agricultural Outlook Board, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3800.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3800 (Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Chairperson is absent or temporarily unavailable, the Deputy Chairperson is authorized to act for the Chairperson.
