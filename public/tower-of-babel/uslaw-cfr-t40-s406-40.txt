40 C.F.R. § 406.40 — Applicability; description of the bulgur wheat flour milling subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:406.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-406.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 406 (Grain Mills Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the process in which wheat is parboiled, dried, and partially debranned in the production of bulgur.
