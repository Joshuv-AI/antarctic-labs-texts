2 C.F.R. § 1130.300 — Purpose of PROP Article III.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1130.300
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1130.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1130 (Property Administration: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PROP Article III specifies requirements for recipients' use and disposition of real property acquired or improved under an award. It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.311(b) and (c).
