43 C.F.R. § 3207.13 — Must I comply with the requirements of §§ 3207.11 and 3207.12 when my lease overlies a mining claim?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3207.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3207.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BLM will exempt you from complying with the requirements of §§ 3207.11 and 3207.12 when you demonstrate to BLM that:
(1) The mining claim has a plan of operations approved by the appropriate Federal land management agency; and
(2) Your development of the geothermal resource on the lease would interfere with the mining operations.
(b) The exemption provided under paragraph (a) of this section expires upon termination of the mining operations.
