5 C.F.R. § 351.405 — Demoted employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:351.405
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-351.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 351 (Reduction In Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who has received a written decision under part 432 or 752 of this chapter to demote him or her competes under this part from the position to which he or she will be or has been demoted.
