47 C.F.R. § 7.9 — Information pass through.
Citation: us:cfr:47:7.9
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-7.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 7 (Access To Voicemail And Interactive Menu Services And Equipment By People With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Telecommunications equipment and customer premises equipment shall pass through cross-manufacturer, non-proprietary, industry-standard codes, translation protocols, formats or other information necessary to provide telecommunications in an accessible format, if readily achievable. In particular, signal compression technologies shall not remove information needed for access or shall restore it upon decompression.
