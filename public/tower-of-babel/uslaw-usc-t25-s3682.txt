25 U.S.C. § 3682 — Assistant probation officers
Citation: us:usc:25:3682
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3682
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 38A (Indian Tribal Justice Technical And Legal Assistance); Subchapter II (Indian Tribal Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the maximum extent practicable, the chief judge or chief probation or pretrial services officer of each judicial district, in coordination with the Office of Tribal Justice and the Office of Justice Services, shall—
(1)
appoint individuals residing in Indian country to serve as probation or pretrial services officers or assistants for purposes of monitoring and providing services to Federal prisoners residing in Indian country; and
(2)
provide substance abuse, mental health, and other related treatment services to offenders residing on Indian land.
