39 C.F.R. § 957.7 — Failure to appear at the hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:39:957.7
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-957.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 957 (Debarment And Suspension From Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a party fails to appear at the hearing, the Hearing Officer may proceed with the hearing, receive evidence and issue findings of fact without requirement of further notice to the absent party.
