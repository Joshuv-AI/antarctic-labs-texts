29 C.F.R. § 457.20 — Intervenor.
Citation: us:cfr:29:457.20
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-457.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 457 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Intervenor means a party in a proceeding whose intervention has been permitted or directed by the Director, Chief Administrative Law Judge, or Administrative Law Judge, as the case may be.
