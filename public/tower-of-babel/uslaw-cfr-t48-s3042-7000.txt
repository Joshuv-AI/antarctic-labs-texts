48 C.F.R. § 3042.7000 — 3042.7000 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3042.7000
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3042.7000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3042 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at (HSAR) 48 CFR 3052.242-72, Contracting Officer's Representative, in solicitations and contracts when it is intended that a representative will be assigned to the contract to perform functions of a technical nature.
