Abstract

A submodule K K of an R R -module M M is said to be u u - S S -essential if whenever L L is a submodule of M M such that s 1 ​ ( K ∩ L ) = 0 s_{1}(K\cap L)=0 for some s 1 ∈ S s_{1}\in S , then s 2 ​ L = 0 s_{2}L=0 for some s 2 ∈ S s_{2}\in S . For example, we show that a u u - S S -injective u u - S S -envelope is characterized by a u u - S S -essential submodule.
