43 C.F.R. § 2920.5-3 — -3 Application review.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2920.5-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2920.5-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2920 (Leases, Permits And Easements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every application shall be reviewed to determine if it conforms to the notice of realty action. If the application does not meet the requirements of this subpart, the application may be denied, and the applicant shall be so advised in writing, with an explanation.
