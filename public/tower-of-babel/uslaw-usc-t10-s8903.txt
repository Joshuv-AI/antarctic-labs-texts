10 U.S.C. § 8903 — Release from Militia duty upon order to active duty in reserve components
Citation: us:usc:10:8903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8903
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 887 (Naval Militia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When ordered to active duty, a member of the Navy Reserve or the Marine Corps Reserve who is a member of the Naval Militia is relieved from all service and duty in the Naval Militia from the date of active duty specified in his orders until he is released from active duty.
