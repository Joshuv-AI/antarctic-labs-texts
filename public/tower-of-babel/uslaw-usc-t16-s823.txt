16 U.S.C. § 823 — Repeal of inconsistent laws
Citation: us:usc:16:823
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section823
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter I (Regulation Of The Development Of Water Power And Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this chapter are repealed: Provided, That nothing contained herein shall be held or construed to modify or repeal any of the provisions of the Act of Congress approved December 19, 1913, granting certain rights-of-way to the city and county of San Francisco, in the State of California.
