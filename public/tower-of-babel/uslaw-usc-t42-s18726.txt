42 U.S.C. § 18726 — Savings provision
Citation: us:usc:42:18726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18726
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 162 (Energy Infrastructure); Subchapter I (Grid Infrastructure And Resiliency); Part B (Cybersecurity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part affects the authority, existing on the day before November 15, 2021, of any other Federal department or agency, including the authority provided to the Secretary of Homeland Security and the Director of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency in title XXII of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 651 et seq.).
