10 C.F.R. § 474.5 — Review and Update.
Citation: us:cfr:10:474.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-474.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 474 (Electric And Hybrid Vehicle Research, Development, And Demonstration Program; Petroleum-Equivalent Fuel Economy Calculation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will review part 474 five years after the date of publication as a final rule to determine whether any updates and/or revisions are necessary. DOE will publish a notice in the Federal Register soliciting stakeholder input in this review. The Department will publish the findings of the review and any resulting adjustments to part 474 in the Federal Register.
