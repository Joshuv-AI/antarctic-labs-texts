45 C.F.R. § 287.145 — What measures will be used to determine NEW Program outcomes?
Citation: us:cfr:45:287.145
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-287.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 287 (The Native Employment Works (New) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each grantee must develop its own performance standards and measures to ensure accountability for its program results. A Tribe's program plan must identify planned program outcomes and the measures the Tribe will use to determine them. ACF will compare planned outcomes against outcomes reported in the Tribe's annual reports.
