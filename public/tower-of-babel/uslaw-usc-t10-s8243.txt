10 U.S.C. § 8243 — Fixing cost on certain vessels and stations
Citation: us:usc:10:8243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8243
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 827 (Rations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the Secretary of the Navy considers that it is undesirable to administer the mess on any ship or at any station under the quantity allowance prescribed in section 8242 of this title, he may fix the cost of each ration for that mess.
