45 C.F.R. § 1630.16 — Applicability to non-LSC funds.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1630.16
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1630.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1630 (Cost Standards And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No cost may be charged to non-LSC funds in violation of 45 CFR 1610.3 or 1610.4.
(b) LSC may recover from a recipient's LSC funds an amount not to exceed the amount improperly charged to non-LSC funds. The review and appeal procedures of §§ 1630.11 and 1630.12 govern any decision by LSC to recover funds under this paragraph.
