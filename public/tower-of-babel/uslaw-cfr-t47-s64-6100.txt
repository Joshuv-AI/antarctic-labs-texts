47 C.F.R. § 64.6100 — Minimum and maximum Prepaid Calling and Debit Calling account balances.
Citation: us:cfr:47:64.6100
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-64.6100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 64 (Miscellaneous Rules Relating To Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No Provider shall institute a minimum balance requirement for a Consumer to use Debit or Prepaid Calling for Incarcerated People's Communications Services.
(b) No Provider shall prohibit a Consumer from depositing at least $50 per transaction to fund a Debit or Prepaid Calling account that can be used for Incarcerated People's Communications Services.
