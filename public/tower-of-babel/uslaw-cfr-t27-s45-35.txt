27 C.F.R. § 45.35 — Liability for tax.
Citation: us:cfr:27:45.35
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-45.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 45 (Removal Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, For Use Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The manufacturer who removes tobacco products, or cigarette papers or tubes under this part shall be liable for the taxes imposed thereon by 26 U.S.C. 5701, until such tobacco products, or cigarette papers or tubes are received by the Federal agency. Any person who possesses tobacco products, or cigarette papers or tubes in violation of 26 U.S.C. 5751(a)(1) or (2), shall be liable for a tax equal to the tax on such articles.
