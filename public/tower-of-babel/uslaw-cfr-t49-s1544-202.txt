49 C.F.R. § 1544.202 — Persons and property onboard an all-cargo aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1544.202
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1544.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1544 (Aircraft Operator Security: Air Carriers And Commercial Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each aircraft operator operating under a full all-cargo program, or a twelve-five program in an all-cargo operation, must apply the security measures in its security program for persons who board the aircraft for transportation, and for their property, to prevent or deter the carriage of any unauthorized persons, and any unauthorized weapons, explosives, incendiaries, and other destructive devices, items, or substances.
