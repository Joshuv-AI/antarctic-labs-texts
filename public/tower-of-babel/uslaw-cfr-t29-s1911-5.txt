29 C.F.R. § 1911.5 — Minor changes in standards.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1911.5
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1911.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1911 (Rules Of Procedure For Promulgating, Modifying, Or Revoking Occupational Safety Or Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 6(b), when construed in light of the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act (5 U.S.C. 553), is read as permitting the making of minor rules or amendments in which the public is not particularly interested without the notice and public procedure which is otherwise required. Whenever such a minor rule or amendment is adopted, it shall incorporate a finding of good cause to this effect for not providing notice and public procedure.
