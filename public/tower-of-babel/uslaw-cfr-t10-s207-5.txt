10 C.F.R. § 207.5 — Violations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:207.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-207.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 207 (Collection Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any practice that circumvents or contravenes or results in a circumvention or contravention of the requirements outlined in the ESECA or any order issued pursuant thereto is a violation of the DOE regulations stated in this subpart.
