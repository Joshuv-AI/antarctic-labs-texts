15 U.S.C. § 6409 — Pricing
Citation: us:usc:15:6409
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6409
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 90 (Propane Education And Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all cases, the price of propane shall be determined by market forces. Consistent with the antitrust laws, the Council may take no action, nor may any provision of this chapter be interpreted as establishing an agreement to pass along to consumers the cost of the assessment provided for in section 6405 of this title.
