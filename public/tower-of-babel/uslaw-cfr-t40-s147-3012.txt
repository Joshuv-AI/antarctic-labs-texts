40 C.F.R. § 147.3012 — Construction requirements for Class I wells.
Citation: us:cfr:40:147.3012
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-147.3012
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 147 (State, Tribal, And Epa-Administered Underground Injection Control Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the cementing requirement of § 146.12(b) of this chapter, owners and operators of Class I wells shall, through circulation, cement all casing to the surface.
