46 U.S.C. § 56104 — Penalty for false statements
Citation: us:usc:46:56104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section56104
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Control Of Merchant Marine Capabilities); Chapter 561 (Restrictions On Transfers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person that knowingly makes a false statement of a material fact to the Secretary of Transportation or another officer, employee, or agent of the Department of Transportation, to obtain the Secretary’s approval under section 56101 or 56102 of this title, shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned for not more than 5 years, or both.
