42 C.F.R. § 421.114 — Assignment and reassignment of providers by CMS.
Citation: us:cfr:42:421.114
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-421.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 421 (Medicare Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS may assign or reassign any provider to any intermediary if it determines that the assignment or reassignment will be in the best interests of the Medicare program.
