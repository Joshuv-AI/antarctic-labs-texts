12 U.S.C. § 1443 — Forms of bank stock and obligations
Citation: us:usc:12:1443
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1443
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 11 (Federal Home Loan Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any stock, debentures, bonds, notes, or other obligations issued under the authority of this chapter may be issued in uncertificated form, utilizing a book entry method, or in certificated form under such rules, regulations, or guidelines as the Director 1 may provide.
