42 C.F.R. § 494.130 — Condition: Laboratory services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:494.130
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-494.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 494 (Conditions For Coverage For End-Stage Renal Disease Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The dialysis facility must provide, or make available, laboratory services (other than tissue pathology and histocompatibility) to meet the needs of the patient. Any laboratory services, including tissue pathology and histocompatibility must be furnished by or obtained from, a facility that meets the requirements for laboratory services specified in part 493 of this chapter.
