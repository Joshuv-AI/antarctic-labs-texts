43 C.F.R. § 3582.1-2 — -2 Hardrock minerals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3582.1-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3582.1-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3580 (Special Leasing Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise specifically provided in this subpart, leasing of deposits of hardrock minerals shall be governed by regulations in parts 3500 and 3560 of this title.
