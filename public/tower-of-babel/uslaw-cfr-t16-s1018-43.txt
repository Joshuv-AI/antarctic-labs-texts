16 C.F.R. § 1018.43 — Comprehensive review.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1018.43
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1018.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1018 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A comprehensive review of all Commission established or utilized advisory committees shall be made annually in accordance with section 10 of the GSA Circular No. A-63, as amended, and shall be submitted to the GSA Secretariat by November 30 of each year.
