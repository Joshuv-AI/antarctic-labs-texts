15 U.S.C. § 78kk — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:78kk
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section78kk
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2B (Securities Exchanges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to any other funds authorized to be appropriated to the Commission, there are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the functions, powers, and duties of the Commission—
(1)
for fiscal year 2011, $1,300,000,000;
(2)
for fiscal year 2012, $1,500,000,000;
(3)
for fiscal year 2013, $1,750,000,000;
(4)
for fiscal year 2014, $2,000,000,000; and
(5)
for fiscal year 2015, $2,250,000,000.
