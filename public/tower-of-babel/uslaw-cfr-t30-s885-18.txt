30 C.F.R. § 885.18 — What audit, accounting, and administrative requirements must I meet?
Citation: us:cfr:30:885.18
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-885.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 885 (Grants For Certified States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must comply with the audit requirements of the OMB Circular A-133.
(b) You must follow procedures governing grant accounting, payment, records, property, and management contained in 43 CFR part 12.
