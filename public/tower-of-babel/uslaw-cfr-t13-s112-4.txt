13 C.F.R. § 112.4 — Discrimination in employment.
Citation: us:cfr:13:112.4
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-112.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 112 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Title Vi Of The Civil Rights Act Of 1964). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Small business concerns and development companies which apply for or receive any financial assistance of the kind described in § 112.2(b)(1) and (2), including concerns which are identifiable beneficiaries of loans made under § 112.2(b)(2), may not discriminate on the grounds of race, color, or national origin in their employment practices. Such assistance is deemed to have as a primary objective the providing of employment.
