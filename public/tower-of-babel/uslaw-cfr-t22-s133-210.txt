22 C.F.R. § 133.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:22:133.210
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-133.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 133 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 133.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
