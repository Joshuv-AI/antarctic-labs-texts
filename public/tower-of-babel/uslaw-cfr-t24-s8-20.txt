24 C.F.R. § 8.20 — General requirement concerning program accessibility.
Citation: us:cfr:24:8.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-8.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 8 (Nondiscrimination Based On Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs And Activities Of The Department Of Housing And Urban Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in §§ 8.21(c)(1), 8.24(a), 8.25, and 8.31, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because a recipient's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity that receives Federal financial assistance.
