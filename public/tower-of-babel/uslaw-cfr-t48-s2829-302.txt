48 C.F.R. § 2829.302 — 2829.302 Application of State and local taxes to the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2829.302
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2829.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2829 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, purchases and leases made by the Federal Government are immune from State and local taxation.
