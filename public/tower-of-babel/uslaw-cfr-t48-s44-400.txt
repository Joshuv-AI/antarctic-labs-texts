48 C.F.R. § 44.400 — 44.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:44.400
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-44.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 44 (Subcontracting Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the policies limiting the contract clauses a contractor may be required to apply to any subcontractors that are furnishing commercial products, including commercial components, or commercial services in accordance with 41 U.S.C. 3307.
