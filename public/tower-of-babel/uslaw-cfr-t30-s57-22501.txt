30 C.F.R. § 57.22501 — Personal electric lamps (I-A, I-B, I-C, II-A, II-B, III, IV, V-A, and V-B mines).
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.22501
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
