22 U.S.C. § 1645j — Settlement period
Citation: us:usc:22:1645j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1645j
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter VII (Claims Against Vietnam). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall complete its affairs in connection with the settlement of claims pursuant to this subchapter not later than three years after the final date for the filing of claims as provided in section 1645b of this title.
