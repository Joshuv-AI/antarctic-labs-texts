2 C.F.R. § 180.875 — May I ask the debarring official to reconsider a decision to debar me?
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.875
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.875
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. As a debarred person, you may ask the debarring official to reconsider the debarment decision or to reduce the time period or scope of the debarment. However, you must submit your request in writing and support it with documentation.
