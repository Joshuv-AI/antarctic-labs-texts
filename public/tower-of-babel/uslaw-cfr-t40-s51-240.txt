40 C.F.R. § 51.240 — General plan requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.240
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State implementation plan must identify organizations, by official title, that will participate in developing, implementing, and enforcing the plan and the responsibilities of such organizations. The plan shall include any related agreements or memoranda of understanding among the organizations.
