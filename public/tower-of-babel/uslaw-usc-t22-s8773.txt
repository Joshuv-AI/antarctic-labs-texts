22 U.S.C. § 8773 — Report on membership of Iran in international organizations
Citation: us:usc:22:8773
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8773
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after August 10, 2012, and not later than September 1 of each year thereafter, the Secretary of State shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees a report listing the international organizations of which Iran is a member and detailing the amount that the United States contributes to each such organization on an annual basis.
