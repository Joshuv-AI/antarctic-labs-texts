45 C.F.R. § 1636.4 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1636 (Client Identity And Statement Of Facts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to cases for which private attorneys are compensated by the recipient as well as to those cases initiated by the recipient's staff.
