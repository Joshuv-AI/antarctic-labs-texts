33 C.F.R. § 110.74b — b Apollo Beach, Fla.
Citation: us:cfr:33:110.74b
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-110.74b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 110 (Anchorage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Beginning at a point approximately 300 feet south of the Tampa Sailing Squadron at latitude 27°46′50.2″ N., longitude 82°25′27.8″ W.; thence southeasterly to latitude 27°46′45.6″ N., longitude 82°25′23.2″ W.; thence southwesterly to latitude 27°46′35.8″ N., longitude 82°25′34.8″ W., thence northwesterly to latitude 27°46′39.9″ N., longitude 82°25′39.6″ W., thence to the point of beginning.
