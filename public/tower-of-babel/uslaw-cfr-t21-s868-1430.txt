21 C.F.R. § 868.1430 — Carbon monoxide gas analyzer.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-868.1430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 868 (Anesthesiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A carbon monoxide gas analyzer is a device intended to measure the concentration of carbon monoxide in a gas mixture to aid in determining the patient's ventilatory status. The device may use techniques such as infrared absorption or gas chromatography.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
