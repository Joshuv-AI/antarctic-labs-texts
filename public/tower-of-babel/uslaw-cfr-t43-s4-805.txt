43 C.F.R. § 4.805 — Reduction of time to file documents.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.805
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For good cause, the responsible Departmental official or the administrative law judge, with respect to matters pending before them, may reduce any time limit prescribed by the rules in this part, except as provided by law or in part 17 of this title.
