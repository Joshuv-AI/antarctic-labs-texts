36 C.F.R. § 12.1 — Applicability and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:36:12.1
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-12.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 12 (National Cemeteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to the national cemeteries administered by the National Park Service. These regulations supplement regulations found in parts 1-5 and 7 of this chapter and provide procedural guidance for the administration, operation and maintenance of these cemeteries.
