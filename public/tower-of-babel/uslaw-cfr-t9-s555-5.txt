9 C.F.R. § 555.5 — Transportation of inedible fish product in commerce.
Citation: us:cfr:9:555.5
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-555.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 555 (Transportation Of Fish Products In Commerce). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in § 325.11(e) of this chapter regarding the transportation of inedible livestock products apply to the transportation of inedible fish parts or products.
