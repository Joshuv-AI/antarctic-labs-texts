41 C.F.R. § 102-71.115 — -71.115 When a State or local government has a tobacco product-free ordinance that is stricter than the tobacco policy for Federal facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.115
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the subject facility is federally owned, then Federal preemption principles apply and the Federal policy controls. If the subject facility is privately owned, then Federal tenants are subject to the provisions of the State or local ordinance, even in the federally leased space, if the State or local restrictions are more stringent than the Federal policy.
