29 U.S.C. § 1397 — Application of part in case of certain pre-1980 withdrawals; adjustment of covered plan
Citation: us:usc:29:1397
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1397
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance); Part 1 (Employer Withdrawals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For the purpose of determining the amount of unfunded vested benefits allocable to an employer for a partial or complete withdrawal from a plan which occurs after September 25, 1980, and for the purpose of determining whether there has been a partial withdrawal after such date, the amount of contributions, and the number of contribution base units, of such employer properly allocable—
(1)
to work performed under a collective bargaining agreement for which there was a permanent cessation of the obligation to contribute before September 26, 1980, or
(2)
to work performed at a facility at which all covered operations permanently ceased before September 26, 1980, or for which there was a permanent cessation of the obligation to contribute before that date,
shall not be taken into account.
(b)
A plan may, in a manner not inconsistent with regulations, which shall be prescribed by the corporation, adjust the amount of unfunded vested benefits allocable to other employers under a plan maintained by an employer described in subsection (a).
