42 C.F.R. § 87.3 — Who is eligible to apply for a grant under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:42:87.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-87.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 87 (National Institute For Occupational Safety And Health Research And Demonstration Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any public or private agency or institution is eligible to apply for a grant under this part, except Federal agencies or institutions not specifically authorized by law to receive such a grant.
