34 C.F.R. § 34.27 — Actions by employer prohibited by law.
Citation: us:cfr:34:34.27
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-34.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 34 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer may not discharge, refuse to employ, or take disciplinary action against a debtor due to the issuance of a garnishment order under this part.
