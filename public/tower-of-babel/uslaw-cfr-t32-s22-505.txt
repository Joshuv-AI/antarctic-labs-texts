32 C.F.R. § 22.505 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:22.505
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-22.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 22 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—AWARD AND ADMINISTRATION). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to supplement other regulations that implement national policy requirements, to the extent that it is necessary to provide additional guidance to DoD grants officers.
