47 C.F.R. § 22.815 — Construction period for general aviation ground stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:22.815
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-22.815
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 22 (Public Mobile Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The construction period (see § 1.946 of this chapter) for general aviation ground stations is 12 months.
