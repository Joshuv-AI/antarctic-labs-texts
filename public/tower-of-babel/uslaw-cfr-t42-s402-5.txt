42 C.F.R. § 402.5 — Right to a hearing before the final determination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:402.5
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-402.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 402 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments, And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS or OIG does not make a determination adverse to any person under this part until the person has been given a written notice and opportunity for the determination to be made on the record after a hearing at which the person is entitled to be represented by counsel, to present witnesses, and to cross-examine witnesses against the person.
