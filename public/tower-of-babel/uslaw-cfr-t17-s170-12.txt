17 C.F.R. § 170.12 — Delegation of Authority to Director of the Market Participants Division.
Citation: us:cfr:17:170.12
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-170.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 170 (Registered Futures Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission hereby delegates, until the Commission orders otherwise, to the Director of the Market Participants Division the authority to take any of the actions enumerated in §§ 170.11 (b) and (c). Notwithstanding the provisions of this section, if the Director believes it appropriate, he may submit the matter to the Commission for its consideration.
