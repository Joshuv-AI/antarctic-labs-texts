25 U.S.C. § 1680s — Disease and injury prevention report
Citation: us:usc:25:1680s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1680s
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 18 months after March 23, 2010, the Secretary shall submit to the Committee on Indian Affairs of the Senate and the Committees on Natural Resources and Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives 1 describing—
(1)
all disease and injury prevention activities conducted by the Service, independently or in conjunction with other Federal departments and agencies and Indian tribes; and
(2)
the effectiveness of those activities, including the reductions of injury or disease conditions achieved by the activities.
