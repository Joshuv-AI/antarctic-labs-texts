49 C.F.R. § 234.219 — Gate arm lights and light cable.
Citation: us:cfr:49:234.219
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-234.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 234 (Grade Crossing Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each gate arm light shall be maintained in such condition to be properly visible to approaching highway users. Lights and light wire shall be secured to the gate arm.
