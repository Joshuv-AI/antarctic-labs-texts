43 C.F.R. § 30.269 — What notice of the hearing will the judge provide?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.269
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.269
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On receiving a demand for hearing, the judge must:
(a) Set a time and place for the hearing after expiration of the 30-day period fixed for the filing of the demand for hearing as provided in § 30.268; and
(b) Mail a notice of the hearing to all interested parties not less than 20 days in advance of the hearing.
