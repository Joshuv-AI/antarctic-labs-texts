46 C.F.R. § 95.16-35 — -35 Pressure relief.
Citation: us:cfr:46:95.16-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-95.16-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 95 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tight compartments, like refrigeration spaces and paint lockers, must have a way to relieve the accumulation of excessive pressure within the compartment when the extinguishing agent is injected.
