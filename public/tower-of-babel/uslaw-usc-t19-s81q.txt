19 U.S.C. § 81q — Transfer of grant
Citation: us:usc:19:81q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section81q
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1A (Foreign Trade Zones). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The grant shall not be sold, conveyed, transferred, set over, or assigned.
