26 C.F.R. § 1.857-10 — -10 Information returns.
Citation: us:cfr:26:1.857-10
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.857-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 1 (Income Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in §§ 1.857-8 and 1.857-9 shall be construed to relieve a real estate investment trust or its shareholders from the duty of filing information returns required by regulations prescribed under the provisions of subchapter A, chapter 61 of the Code.
