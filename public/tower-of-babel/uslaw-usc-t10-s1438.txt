10 U.S.C. § 1438 — Deposits for amounts not deducted
Citation: us:usc:10:1438
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1438
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter I (Retired Serviceman’S Family Protection Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If, for any period, a person who has been retired or has become entitled to retired or retainer pay, and who has elected an annuity under this subchapter, is not entitled to retired or retainer pay, he must deposit in the Treasury the amount that would otherwise have been deducted from his pay for that period to provide the annuity.
