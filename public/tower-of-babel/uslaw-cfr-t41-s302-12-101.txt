41 C.F.R. § 302-12.101 — -12.101 Rules to follow when contracting for relocation services.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-12.101
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-12.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-12 (12—Use Of A Relocation Services Company). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When contracting for relocation services, agencies must follow the rules contained in the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) (48 CFR) and/or all other acquisition regulations applicable to their agency.
