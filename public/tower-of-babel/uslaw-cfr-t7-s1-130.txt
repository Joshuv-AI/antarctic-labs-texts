7 C.F.R. § 1.130 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.130
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, words in the singular form shall be deemed to import the plural, and vice versa, as the case may require.
