36 C.F.R. § 1239.4 — What standards are used as guidance for this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1239.4
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1239.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1239 (Program Assistance And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations conform with guidance provided in ISO 15489-1:2001. Paragraphs 7.1, Principles of records management programmes, and 10, Monitoring and auditing, apply to this part.
