Planar Abelian Mirror Duals of 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 SQCD 3
Sergio Benvenuti, Riccardo Comi, Sara Pasquetti, Gabriel Pedde Ungureanu, Simone Rota, Anant Shri

Abstract
We propose an Abelian mirror dual for the 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 SQCD 3 that we obtain as real mass deformation of known 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 mirror pairs. We match the superconformal index and the 𝐒 b 3 \mathbf{S}^{3}_{b} partition function, discuss the agreement of the moduli spaces, and provide a map of the gauge invariant operators and the global symmetries as evidence of this duality.

II.1
Checks of the Duality

As a preliminary check of the proposed duality, we count the rank of the global symmetries of the planar theory. All
U(1)
symmetries rotating the chiral fields are either broken by
\mathcal{W}_{planar}
or removed by gauge transformations. The monopole superpotential breaks the topological

U(1)
topological symmetry for each column and an extra one associated with the node indicated in red, resulting in a

The duality predicts that the UV global symmetry enhances in the IR to
U(F)
, which is manifest in the UV in the electric SQCD
3

\mathcal{N}=4
quiver of Figure
1

In addition to the already mentioned check of the matching of the

In particular, we can detect

current
