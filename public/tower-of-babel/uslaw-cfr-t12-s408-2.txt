12 C.F.R. § 408.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:408.2
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-408.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 408 (Procedures For Compliance With The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish procedures which supplement the NEPA Regulations and provide for the implementation of those provisions identified in § 1507.3(b) of the NEPA Regulations.
