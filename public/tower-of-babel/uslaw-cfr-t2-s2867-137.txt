2 C.F.R. § 2867.137 — Who in the Department of Justice may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2867.137
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2867.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2867 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within the Department of Justice, the Attorney General or designee has the authority to grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction, as provided in the OMB guidance at 2 CFR 180.135.
