5 C.F.R. § 847.608 — Reduction in annuity due to deficiency.
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.608
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.608
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any annuity payable in the case of an employee who has made an election under subpart D of this part will include credit for the NAFI service. The monthly annuity rate on the date determined under § 847.603 will be permanently reduced by an amount equal to the amount of any deficiency divided by the present value factor for the annuitant's age on that date.
