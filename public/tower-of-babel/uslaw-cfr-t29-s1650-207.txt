29 C.F.R. § 1650.207 — Administrative charges.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1650.207
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1650.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1650 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All administrative charges incurred in connection with the referral of a debt to the Treasury and all costs of collection of the debt will be assessed on the debt and thus increase the amount of the offset.
