32 C.F.R. § 150.18 — Orders and decisions of the Court.
Citation: us:cfr:32:150.18
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-150.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 150 (Courts Of Criminal Appeals Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Court shall give notice of its orders and decisions by immediately serving them, when rendered, on appellate defense counsel, including civilian counsel, if any, government counsel and the Judge Advocate General, or designee, as appropriate.
