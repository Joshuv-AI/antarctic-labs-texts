19 C.F.R. § 145.41 — Other conditionally and unconditionally free merchandise.
Citation: us:cfr:19:145.41
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-145.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 145 (Mail Importations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Shipments of conditionally or unconditionally free merchandise not specifically treated elsewhere in this part may be passed free of duty and tax without issuing an entry, if the value is not over $2,500 and the port director is satisfied that the merchandise is entitled to free entry.
