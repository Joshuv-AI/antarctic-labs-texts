27 C.F.R. § 25.53 — Submissions of samples of fermented products.
Citation: us:cfr:27:25.53
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-25.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 25 (Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appropriate TTB officer may, at any time, require you to submit samples of:
(a) Cereal beverage, saké, or any fermented product produced at the brewery,
(b) Materials used in the production of cereal beverage, saké, or any fermented product; and
(c) Cereal beverage, saké, or any fermented product, in conjunction with the filing of a formula.
