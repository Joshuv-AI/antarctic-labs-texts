14 U.S.C. § 2158 — Review of records of officers
Citation: us:usc:14:2158
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2158
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe, by regulation, procedures to review the record of any officer of the Regular Coast Guard to determine whether he shall be required to show cause for his retention on active duty—
(1)
because his performance of duty has fallen below the standards prescribed by the Secretary, or
(2)
because of moral dereliction, professional dereliction, or because his retention is not clearly consistent with the interests of national security.
