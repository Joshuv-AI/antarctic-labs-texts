42 C.F.R. § 430.100 — Posthearing briefs.
Citation: us:cfr:42:430.100
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-430.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 430 (Grants To States For Medical Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presiding officer fixes the time for filing posthearing briefs, which may contain proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law. The presiding officer may also permit reply briefs.
