16 C.F.R. § 1700.2 — Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1700.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1700.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1700 (Poison Prevention Packaging). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authority under the Poison Prevention Packaging Act of 1970 is vested in the Consumer Product Safety Commission by section 30(a) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2079(a)).
