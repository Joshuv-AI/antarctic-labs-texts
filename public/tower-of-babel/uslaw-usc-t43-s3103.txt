43 U.S.C. § 3103 — Ground subsidence
Citation: us:usc:43:3103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section3103
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 49 (National Landslide Preparedness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As the Secretary determines to be appropriate and subject to appropriations, the Secretary, through existing programs, shall advance the identification, mapping, research, and monitoring of subsidence and groundwater resource accounting, particularly in areas affected by drought.
