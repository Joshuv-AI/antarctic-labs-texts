27 C.F.R. § 555.212 — Smoking and open flames.
Citation: us:cfr:27:555.212
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-555.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 555 (Commerce In Explosives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Smoking, matches, open flames, and spark producing devices are not permitted:
(a) In any magazine;
(b) Within 50 feet of any outdoor magazine; or
(c) Within any room containing an indoor magazine.
