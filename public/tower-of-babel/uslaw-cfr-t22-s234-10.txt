22 C.F.R. § 234.10 — Payment to USAID of excess amounts received by a Noteholder.
Citation: us:cfr:22:234.10
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-234.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 234 (Ukraine Loan Guarantees Issued Under The Support For The Sovereignty, Integrity, Democracy, And Economic Stability Of Ukraine Act Of 2014, Pub. L. 113-95—Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Noteholder shall, as a result of USAID paying compensation under this Guarantee, receive an excess payment, it shall refund the excess to USAID.
