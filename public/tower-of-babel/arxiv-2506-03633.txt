Abstract
. Assuming Gödel’s axiom of constructibility 𝕍 = 𝕃 , \mathbb{V}=\mathbb{L}, we construct an almost-free abelian group G G of singular cardinality, such that for any nontrivial subgroup G ′ ⊆ G G^{\prime}\subseteq G of smaller size, we have Hom ⁡ ( G ′ , ℤ ) ≠ 0 {\rm Hom}(G^{\prime},{\mathbb{Z}})\neq 0 , while Hom ⁡ ( G , ℤ ) = 0 {\rm Hom}(G,{\mathbb{Z}})=0 . This provides a consistent counterexample to the singular compactness of Hom {\rm Hom} .

Compactness (and its counterpart, incompactness) is a central theme in contemporary research. This concept broadly asserts that if every smaller subobject of a given object possesses a particular property denoted by
\Pr

\Pr
. In this paper, we are interested in the compactness property for the nontrivial duality with respect to the hom-functor
{\rm Hom}(-,\mathbb{Z})
at singular cardinals. Namely, we study the following property:

\Pr_{\lambda}

If
G

\lambda

any nontrivial subgroup
G^{\prime}\subseteq G
of size less than
\lambda,

{\rm Hom}(G^{\prime},{\mathbb{Z}})\neq 0
, then
{\rm Hom}(G,\mathbb{Z})\neq 0
.

For a given
\mu\leq\lambda

S^{\lambda}_{\mu}=\{\alpha<\lambda\mid{\rm cf}(\alpha)=\mu\}

\lambda

\diamondsuit_{S}
denote Jensen’s diamond (see Definition

Now, assuming

\lambda
-free abelian group
G
of size
\lambda
such that

{\rm Hom}(G,\mathbb{Z})=0

(see
[
2
]

of

{\rm Hom}(G^{\prime},\mathbb{Z})\neq 0.

In this paper, we investigate the consistency of the failure of

for some singular cardinal
