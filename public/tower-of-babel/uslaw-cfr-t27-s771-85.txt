27 C.F.R. § 771.85 — Stenographic record.
Citation: us:cfr:27:771.85
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-771.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 771 (Rules Of Practice In Explosive License And Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A stenographic record shall be made of the testimony and proceedings, including stipulations, admissions of fact, and arguments of counsel in all proceedings. A transcript of the evidence and proceedings at the hearing shall be made in all cases.
