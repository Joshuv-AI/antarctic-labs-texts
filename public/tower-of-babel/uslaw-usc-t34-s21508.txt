34 U.S.C. § 21508 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:34:21508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21508
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 215 (Advanced Notification Of Traveling Sex Offenders). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, in consultation with the Attorney General and the Secretary of Homeland Security, may provide technical assistance to foreign authorities in order to enable such authorities to participate more effectively in the notification program system established under this chapter.
