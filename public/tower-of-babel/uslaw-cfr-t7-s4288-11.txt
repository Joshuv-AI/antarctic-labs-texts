7 C.F.R. § 4288.11 — Eligible project costs.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4288.11
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4288.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4288 (Payment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible project costs will be only for project related construction costs for repowering improvements associated with the equipment, installation, engineering, design, site plans, associated professional fees, permits and financing fees.
