10 U.S.C. § 9532 — Factories, arsenals, and depots: manufacture at
Citation: us:usc:10:9532
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9532
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 963 (Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may have supplies needed for the Department of the Air Force made in factories, arsenals, or depots owned by the United States, so far as those factories, arsenals, or depots can make those supplies on an economical basis.
