5 C.F.R. § 10002.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:10002.1
Creator: National Council On Disability
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-10002.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 10002 (Implementation Of The Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this part implement the provisions of the Sunshine Act.
(b) Requests for all records other than those described in § 10002.9, shall be governed by the Council's Freedom of Information Act procedures at 5 CFR part 10001.
