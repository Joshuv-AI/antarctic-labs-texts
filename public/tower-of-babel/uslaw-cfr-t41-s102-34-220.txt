41 C.F.R. § 102-34.220 — -34.220 Government motor vehicle misuse.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-34.220
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-34.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-34 (34—Motor Vehicle Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Willful misuse of a Government motor vehicle will be reported to the agency head to investigate and, if appropriate, take disciplinary action under 31 U.S.C. 1349 or the violation will be reported to the Attorney General for prosecution under 18 U.S.C. 641.
