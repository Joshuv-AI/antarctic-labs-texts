12 C.F.R. § 264b.3 — b.3 Restrictions on acceptance of gifts and decorations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:264b.3
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-264b.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 264b (PART 264b—RULES REGARDING FOREIGN GIFTS AND DECORATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Board employees are prohibited from requesting or otherwise encouraging the tender of a gift or decoration from a foreign government.
(b) Board employees are prohibited from accepting a gift or decoration from a foreign government, except in accordance with this part.
