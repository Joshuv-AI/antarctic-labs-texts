12 U.S.C. § 1956 — Criminal penalty
Citation: us:usc:12:1956
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1956
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 21 (Financial Recordkeeping). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever willfully violates any regulation under this chapter shall be fined not more than $1,000 or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
