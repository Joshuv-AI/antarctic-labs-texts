3-particle contribution to the amplitude of the generic weak form factor topology

In this Section, we obtain factorization formula for the simplest amplitude of the generic weak form factor topology of
Fig.

\displaystyle A(q,q^{\prime})=\int dxdx^{\prime}dkdk^{\prime}\,D(k)\,D(k^{\prime})\,e^{iqx+ikx+iq^{\prime}x^{\prime}-ik^{\prime}x^{\prime}}\Psi(x,0,x^{\prime}|p),\quad p=q+q^{\prime},

(4)

We neglect possible differences between the masses

2.1
The
x
-vertex

We start with the
x
-vertex and introduce
l=k+q
, the momentum carried by the light constituent field
\phi(x)
:

\displaystyle\int\frac{dxdl}{m^{2}-(l-q)^{2}}e^{ilx}\langle 0|\phi(x)...|B(p)\rangle.

Almost full momentum of the heavy
B
-meson is carried by the heavy
b
-quark;
all light constituents carry only
O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/M_{B})
fraction of the
B
-meson momentum,
and thus the momentum

is soft,
l_{\mu}\sim O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD})
. This feature is provided by
the properties of the
B
-meson wave functions.

The component
q_{+}\sim M_{B}
is large, and the propagator is highly virtual,

\displaystyle m^{2}-2l_{-}(l_{+}-q_{+})+l_{\perp}^{2}\sim\Lambda_{\rm QCD}M_{B}.

(6)

Let us expand the field operator
\phi(x)
near
x=0

x_{-}
and
x_{\perp}
leads
to a well behaved Taylor series for the amplitude

\displaystyle x_{-}e^{il_{+}x_{-}}\frac{1}{m^{2}-2l_{-}(l_{+}-q_{+})+l_{\perp}^{2}}\to e^{il_{+}x_{-}}\frac{l_{-}}{(m^{2}-2l_{-}(l_{+}-q_{+})+l_{\perp}^{2})^{2}}.

(7)

Since
l_{-}=O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD})
and the virtuality of the propagator is
O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD}M_{B})

A(q,q^{\prime})
coming from any term

(1/M_{B})^{n}

compared to the contribution of the term
(x_{-})^{0}
. The same property holds for
(x_{\perp})^{n}
.

However, for powers of the variable
x_{+}
the situation is different:

\displaystyle x_{+}e^{il_{-}x_{+}}\frac{1}{m^{2}-2l_{-}(l_{+}-q_{+})+l_{\perp}^{2}}\to e^{il_{-}x_{+}}\frac{q_{+}}{(m^{2}-2l_{-}(l_{+}-q_{+})+l_{\perp}^{2})^{2}}.

Since
q_{+}\sim M_{B}
, the contribution to
A(q,q^{\prime})
coming from any power of
x_{+}^{n}
, including
x_{+}^{0}
,
has the same order of magnitude: the Taylor expansion of
\phi(x_{+})
near
x_{+}=0

. We therefore need to keep the full
x_{+}
-dependence of

\phi(x_{+})
on the light cone (
x^{2}=0

-vertex corresponds to expanding

near
x_{-}=0
,

\displaystyle\int\frac{dx_{+}dx_{-}dx_{\perp}dl_{+}dl_{-}dl_{\perp}}{(2\pi)^{4}}\frac{1}{m^{2}-2(l_{+}-q_{+})l_{-}+l_{\perp}^{2}}e^{il_{+}x_{-}+il_{-}x_{+}-il_{\perp}x_{\perp}}\langle 0|...\phi(x_{+})...|B(p)\rangle.

The

\displaystyle\int\frac{dx_{+}dl_{-}}{2\pi}\frac{1}{2q_{+}l_{-}}e^{il_{-}x_{+}}\langle 0|...\phi(x_{+})...|B(p)\rangle.

\displaystyle m^{2}-2l^{\prime}_{-}(l^{\prime}_{-}-q^{\prime}_{-})+{l^{\prime}_{\perp}}^{2}\sim\Lambda_{\rm QCD}M_{B}.

\displaystyle\Psi(x,x_{1},...,x_{n},0|p)=\langle 0|\phi(x)\phi(x_{1})...\phi(x_{n})\phi_{b}(0)|B(p)\rangle.

3.1
The propagators

The amplitude contains
n

x_{1},\dots,x_{n}
and
(n+1)

The standard procedure to handle these propagators is to introduce
(n+1)
Feynman parameters
v_{1},...,v_{n+1}

satisfying
v_{1}+...+v_{n+1}=1
,
0<v_{i}<1
to gather
(n+1)

the
(n+1)
-th power of the inverse single quadratic polynomial

\displaystyle P_{2}(k,l_{1},...,l_{n})=k^{2}v_{1}+(k-l_{1})^{2}v_{2}+...+(k-l_{1}-...-l_{n})^{2}v_{n+1},\quad k=q-l,

(14)

where mass terms have been omitted. We can write

\displaystyle P_{2}(k,l_{1},...,l_{n})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle k^{2}\underbrace{(v_{1}+v_{2}+...+v_{n+1})}_{=1}-2\,k\,l_{1}\underbrace{(v_{2}+...+v_{n+1})}_{1-v_{1}\equiv u_{1}}-...-2\,k\,l_{n}\underbrace{(v_{n+1})}_{v_{n+1}\equiv u_{n}}+O(l_{1},l_{2},...,l_{n})^{2}

(15)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k-u_{1}l_{1}-u_{2}l_{2}-\dots-u_{n}l_{n})^{2}+O(l_{1},l_{2},...,l_{n})^{2}=(q-\tilde{l})^{2}+O(l_{1},l_{2},...,l_{n})^{2},

where
O(l_{1},l_{2},...,l_{n})^{2}
denotes a quadratic polynomial composed of vectors
l_{1},...,l_{n}
and

\displaystyle\tilde{l}\equiv l+u_{1}l_{1}+u_{2}l_{2}+\dots+u_{n}l_{n}),\qquad 0<u_{n}<u_{n-1}...<u_{2}<u_{1}.

(16)

Since all components of the 4-vectors
l_{1},...,l_{n}
are soft,
and
(q-\tilde{l})^{2}\sim O(m_{b}\Lambda_{\rm QCD})
,
we can expand the denominator in powers of small parameters

l_{i}l_{j}/(q-\tilde{l})^{2}\simeq\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/m_{b}
.
The leading contribution to the amplitude (
12

corresponds to setting
l_{1},...,l_{n}\to 0
in the denominator, or, equivalently,
to replacing
P_{2}(k,l_{1},...,l_{n})\to(q-\tilde{l})^{2}
.

\displaystyle A^{(n,n^{\prime})}(q,q^{\prime})
