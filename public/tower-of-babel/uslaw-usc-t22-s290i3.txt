22 U.S.C. § 290i–3 — Applicability of Bretton Woods Agreements Act
Citation: us:usc:22:290i–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290i–3
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXIV (African Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 4 of the Bretton Woods Agreements Act (22 U.S.C. 286b) shall apply with respect to the Bank to the same extent as with respect to the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development and the International Monetary Fund.
