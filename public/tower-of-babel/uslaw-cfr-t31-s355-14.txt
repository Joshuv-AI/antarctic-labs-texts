31 C.F.R. § 355.14 — Can these regulations be waived?
Citation: us:cfr:31:355.14
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-355.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 355 (Regulations Governing Fiscal Agency Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We reserve the right, in our discretion, to waive any provision of the regulations in this part in any case or class of cases for the convenience of the United States, or to relieve any person of unnecessary hardship, if the waiver is not inconsistent with law and will not subject the United States to substantial expense or liability.
