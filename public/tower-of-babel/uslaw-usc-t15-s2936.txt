15 U.S.C. § 2936 — Scientific assessment
Citation: us:usc:15:2936
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2936
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 56A (Global Change Research); Subchapter I (United States Global Change Research Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On a periodic basis (not less frequently than every 4 years), the Council, through the Committee, shall prepare and submit to the President and the Congress an assessment which—
(1)
integrates, evaluates, and interprets the findings of the Program and discusses the scientific uncertainties associated with such findings;
(2)
analyzes the effects of global change on the natural environment, agriculture, energy production and use, land and water resources, transportation, human health and welfare, human social systems, and biological diversity; and
(3)
analyzes current trends in global change, both human-inducted 1 and natural, and projects major trends for the subsequent 25 to 100 years.
