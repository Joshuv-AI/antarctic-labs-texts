25 U.S.C. § 4355 — Effect
Citation: us:usc:25:4355
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4355
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 44A (Native American Tourism And Improving Visitor Experience). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter alters, or demonstrates congressional support for the alteration of, the legal relationship between the United States and any American Indian, Alaska Native, or Native Hawaiian individual, group, organization, or entity.
