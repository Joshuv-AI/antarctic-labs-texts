48 C.F.R. § 1843.205-70 — 1843.205-70 NASA contract clauses.

Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1843.205-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1843 (Contract Modifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially as stated at 1852.243-72, Equitable Adjustments, in solicitations and contracts for—
(a) Dismantling, demolishing, or removing improvements; or
(b) Construction, when the contract amount is expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold and a fixed-price contract is contemplated.
