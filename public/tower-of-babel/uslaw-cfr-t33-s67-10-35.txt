33 C.F.R. § 67.10-35 — -35 Notice of approval and withdrawal of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.10-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.10-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Coast Guard publishes a notice of the approval or withdrawal of approval of a sound signal in the Local Notice to Mariners.
(b) A listing of approved sound signals may be obtained from any District Commander.
