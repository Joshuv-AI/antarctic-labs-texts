18 C.F.R. § 344.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:344.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-344.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 344 (Filing Quotations For U.S. Government Shipments At Reduced Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part will apply to quotations or tenders made by all pipeline common carriers to the United States Government, or any agency or department thereof, for the transportation, storage, or handling of petroleum and petroleum products at reduced rates as permitted by section 22 of the Interstate Commerce Act. Excepted are filings which involve information, the disclosure of which would endanger the national security.
