46 C.F.R. § 111.05-15 — -15 Neutral grounding.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each propulsion, power, lighting, or distribution system having a neutral bus or conductor must have the neutral grounded.
(b) The neutral of a dual-voltage system must be solidly grounded at the generator switchboard.
