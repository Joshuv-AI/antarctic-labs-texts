7 C.F.R. § 1778.13 — Set-aside.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1778.13
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1778.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1778 (Emergency And Imminent Community Water Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At least 70 percent of all grants made under this grant program shall be for projects funded in accordance with § 1778.11(a).
(b) At least 50 percent of the funds appropriated for this grant program shall be allocated to rural areas with populations not in excess of 3,000 inhabitants according to the most recent decennical Census.
