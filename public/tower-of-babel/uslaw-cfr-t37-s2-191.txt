37 C.F.R. § 2.191 — Action of the Office based on the written record.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.191
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All business with the Office must be transacted in writing. The action of the Office will be based exclusively on the written record. No consideration will be given to any alleged oral promise, stipulation, or understanding when there is disagreement or doubt.
