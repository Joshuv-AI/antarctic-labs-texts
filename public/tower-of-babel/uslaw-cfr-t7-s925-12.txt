7 C.F.R. § 925.12 — Fiscal period.
Citation: us:cfr:7:925.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-925.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 925 (Grapes Grown In A Designated Area Of Southeastern California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fiscal period is synonymous with fiscal year and means the 12 month period beginning on December 1 of one year and ending the last day of November of the following year or such other period as the committee, with the approval of the Secretary, may prescribe.
