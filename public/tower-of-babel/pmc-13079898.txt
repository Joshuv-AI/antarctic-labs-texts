Author Correction: Disease exacerbation in human DMD MYOrganoids enables gene therapy evaluation and unveils persistence of fibrotic activity
Authors: Laura Palmieri, Giorgia Bimbi, Maxime Ferrand, Matteo Marcello, Louna Pili, Ai Vu Hong, Abbass Jaber, Riyad El-Khoury, Guy Brochier, Anne Bigot, David Israeli, Isabelle Richard, Sonia Albini
Published: 2026

Correction to: npj Regenerative Medicine 10.1038/s41536-025-00445-8, published online 02 January 2026

In the original version of this article, Figures 1 and 5 appeared incorrectly. In Figure 1, the drawings of organoids were missing in panel 1A, and the day labels indicating condensation kinetics were omitted in panel 1B. In Figure 5, the dystrophin staining image in panel 5G was incorrect and appeared to be duplicated. The original article has been corrected.
