42 U.S.C. § 12877 — Exemption
Citation: us:usc:42:12877
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12877
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter IV (Hope For Homeownership Of Multifamily And Single Family Homes); Part A (Hope For Homeownership Of Multifamily Units). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Eligible property covered by a homeownership program approved under this part shall not be subject to—
(1)
the Low-Income Housing Preservation and Resident Homeownership Act of 1990 [12 U.S.C. 4101 et seq.], or
(2)
the requirements of section 1701z–11 of title 12 applicable to the sale of projects either at foreclosure or after acquisition by the Secretary.
