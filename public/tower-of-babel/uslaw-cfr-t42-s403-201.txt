42 C.F.R. § 403.201 — State regulation of insurance policies.
Citation: us:cfr:42:403.201
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-403.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 403 (Special Programs And Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart do not affect the right of a State to regulate policies marketed in that State.
(b) Approval of a policy under the voluntary certification program, as provided for in § 403.235(b), does not authorize the insuring organization to market a policy that does not conform to applicable State laws and regulations.
