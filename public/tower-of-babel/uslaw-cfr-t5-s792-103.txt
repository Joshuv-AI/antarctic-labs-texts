5 C.F.R. § 792.103 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:792.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-792.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 792 (Federal Employees' Health, Counseling, And Work/Life Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all positions in Executive agencies as defined in section 105 of title 5 of the United States Code, and to those positions in the legislative and judicial branch of the Federal Government which are in the competitive service.
