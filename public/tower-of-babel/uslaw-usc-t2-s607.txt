2 U.S.C. § 607 — Lump-sum payments for annual leave to separated employees
Citation: us:usc:2:607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section607
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17 (Congressional Budget Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Director of the Congressional Budget Office shall have the authority to make lump-sum payments to separated employees of the Congressional Budget Office for unused annual leave.
(b)
Subsection (a) shall apply with respect to fiscal years beginning after September 30, 1996.
