14 C.F.R. § 22.190 — Finding of compliance by trained compliance staff.
Citation: us:cfr:14:22.190
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-22.190
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 22 (Design, Production, And Airworthiness Requirements For Non-Type Certificated Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The aircraft must have been found compliant with the provisions of the applicable FAA-accepted or approved consensus standards by individuals who have been trained on determining compliance with those consensus standards.
