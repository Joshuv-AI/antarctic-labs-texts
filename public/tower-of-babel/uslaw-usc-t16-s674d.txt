16 U.S.C. § 674d — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:674d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section674d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 6 (Game And Bird Preserves; Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated such sums as Congress shall from time to time deem necessary to carry out the purposes of sections 674a to 674c of this title.
