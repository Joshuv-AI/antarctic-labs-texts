NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION

Regulatory Guide: Acceptability of ASME OM-2 Code, Component 
Testing Requirements at Nuclear Facilities

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission

ACTION: Direct final guide; confirmation of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is confirming the 
effective date of August 18, 2026, for Revision 0 of Regulatory Guide 
(RG) 1.220, ``Acceptability of ASME OM-2 Code, Component Testing 
Requirements at Nuclear Facilities.'' The RG was noticed in the Federal 
Register on June 4, 2026, with a follow up Correction to the Notice, 
published on June 22, 2026, stating the effective date of August 18, 
2026. This new RG endorses, with a regulatory position, the American 
Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) Operation and Maintenance OM-2 
Code, Component Testing Requirements at Nuclear Facilities, 2024 
Edition, and describes an approach that is acceptable to the NRC staff 
for the development and implementation of an Inservice Testing (IST) 
Program for all types of nuclear facilities.

DATES: The effective date of August 18, 2026, for the direct final RG 
1.220, Revision 0, published on June 4, 2026 (91 FR 33770), as 
corrected on June 22, 2026 (91 FR 37152), is confirmed.
