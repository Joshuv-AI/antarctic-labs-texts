15 U.S.C. § 1847 — Maximum obligation
Citation: us:usc:15:1847
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1847
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 45 (Emergency Loan Guarantees To Business Enterprises). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The maximum obligation of the Board under all outstanding loans guaranteed by it shall not exceed at any time $250,000,000.
