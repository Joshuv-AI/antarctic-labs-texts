22 U.S.C. § 4220 — Medium for payment of fees
Citation: us:usc:22:4220
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4220
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter XIV (Powers, Duties And Liabilities Of Consular Officers Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All fees collected by diplomatic and consular officers for and in behalf of the United States shall be collected in the coin of the United States, or at its representative value in exchange.
