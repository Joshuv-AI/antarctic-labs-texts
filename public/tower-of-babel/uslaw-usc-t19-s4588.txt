19 U.S.C. § 4588 — Treatment of amendments to antidumping and countervailing duty law
Citation: us:usc:19:4588
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4588
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter IV (Antidumping And Countervailing Duties); Part A (Dispute Settlement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any amendment enacted after the USMCA that is made to—
(1)
section 303 1 or title VII of the Tariff Act of 1930 [19 U.S.C. 1671 et seq.], or any successor statute, or
(2)
any other statute which—
(A)
provides for judicial review of final determinations under such section, title, or successor statute, or
(B)
indicates the standard of review to be applied,
shall apply to goods from a USMCA country only to the extent specified in the amendment.
