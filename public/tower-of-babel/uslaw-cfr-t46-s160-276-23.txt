46 C.F.R. § 160.276-23 — -23 Procedure for approval of design or material change.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.276-23
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.276-23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The manufacturer must submit any proposed changes in design, material, or construction to the recognized laboratory for approval before changing PFD production methods.
(b) Determinations of equivalence of design, construction, and materials must be made only by the Commandant or a designated representative.
