25 U.S.C. § 166 — Applicability of chapter 10 of title 5
Citation: us:usc:25:166
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section166
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter III (Deposit, Care, And Investment Of Indian Moneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The activities of the Department of the Interior associated with the Department’s consultation with Indian tribes and organizations related to the management of funds held in trust by the United States for Indian tribes shall be exempt from chapter 10 of title 5.
