43 U.S.C. § 323 — Application to certain States
Citation: us:usc:43:323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section323
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 9 (Desert-Land Entries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 321 to 323, 325, and 327 to 329 of this title shall only apply to and take effect in the States of California, Colorado, Oregon, Nevada, Washington, Idaho, Montana, Utah, Wyoming, Arizona, New Mexico, and North and South Dakota.
