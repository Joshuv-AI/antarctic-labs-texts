44 C.F.R. § 71.1 — Purpose of part.
Citation: us:cfr:44:71.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-71.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 71 (Implementation Of Coastal Barrier Legislation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements section 11 of the Coastal Barrier Resources Act (Pub. L. 97-348) and section 9 of the Coastal Barrier Improvement Act of 1990 (Pub. L. 101-591), as those Acts amend the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4001 et seq.).
