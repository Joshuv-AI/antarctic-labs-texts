30 U.S.C. § 2006 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:30:2006
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 32 (Methane Hydrate Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this chapter, to remain available until expended—
(1)
$15,000,000 for fiscal year 2006;
(2)
$20,000,000 for fiscal year 2007;
(3)
$30,000,000 for fiscal year 2008;
(4)
$40,000,000 for fiscal year 2009; and
(5)
$50,000,000 for fiscal year 2010.
