2 C.F.R. § 3254.332 — What methods must I use to pass requirements down to participants at lower tiers with whom I intend to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3254.332
Creator: National Endowment For The Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3254.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3254 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You as a participant must include a term or condition in lower-tier transactions requiring lower-tier participants to comply with subpart C of the OMB guidance in 2 CFR part 180, as supplemented by this subpart.
