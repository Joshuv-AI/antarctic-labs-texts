49 C.F.R. § 191.21 — OMB control number assigned to information collection.
Citation: us:cfr:49:191.21
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-191.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 191 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline; Annual, Incident, And Other Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section displays the control number assigned by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) to the information collection requirements in this part. The Paperwork Reduction Act requires agencies to display a current control number assigned by the Director of OMB for each agency information collection requirement.
