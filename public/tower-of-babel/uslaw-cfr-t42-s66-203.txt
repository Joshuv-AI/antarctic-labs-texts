42 C.F.R. § 66.203 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:42:66.203
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-66.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 66 (National Research Service Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for a grant under this subpart, an applicant must be:
(a) A public or nonprofit private institution; and
(b) Located in a State, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, the Canal Zone, Guam, American Samoa, or the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands.
