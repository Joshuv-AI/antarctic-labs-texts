42 U.S.C. § 5027a — Acceptance of donations
Citation: us:usc:42:5027a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5027a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter II (National Senior Service Corps); Part D (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Except as provided in subsection (b), an entity receiving assistance under this subchapter may accept donations, including donations in cash or in kind fairly evaluated, including plant, equipment, or services.
(b) Exception
An entity receiving assistance under this subchapter to carry out an activity shall not accept donations from the beneficiaries of the activity.
