RNA polymerase II CTD Ser5 phosphorylation induces competing effects of expansion and compaction

In Table 1, the values for “No. of simulations” and “Total length analyzed (μs)” for “pS5S7” were incorrect in the originally published version of this paper. The correct values are “5” and “2.4,” respectively. The table has been corrected online. Although 10 simulations were run for the pS5S7 construct, the 5 simulations starting from the trans conformation of the middle heptad Pro6 did not use the proper GaMD potential boost and were therefore given extremely low weights in the re-weighting process, resulting in effectively only 5 simulations that were included in analysis for this construct. The authors apologize for this error, which resulted from improper location of the file containing the boost potential parameters.
