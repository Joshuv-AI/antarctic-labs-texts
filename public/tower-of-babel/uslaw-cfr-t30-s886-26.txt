30 C.F.R. § 886.26 — When and how can my grant be terminated for convenience?
Citation: us:cfr:30:886.26
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-886.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 886 (Reclamation Grants For Uncertified States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either you or we may terminate or reduce a grant if both parties agree that continuing the program would not produce benefits worth the additional costs. We will handle a termination for convenience as an amendment to the grant to be approved by the OSM official authorized to approve your grant.
