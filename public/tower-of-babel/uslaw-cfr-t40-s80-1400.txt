40 C.F.R. § 80.1400 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:80.1400
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-80.1400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 80 (Regulation Of Fuels And Fuel Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this Subpart M shall apply for all renewable fuel produced on or after July 1, 2010, for all RINs generated on or after July 1, 2010, and for all renewable volume obligations and compliance periods starting with January 1, 2010.
