48 C.F.R. § 2131.205-71 — 2131.205-71 Reinsurer administrative expense costs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2131.205-71
Creator: Chapter 21—Office Of Personnel Management, Federal Employees Group Life Insurance Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2131.205-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2131 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A charge of $500 per policy year per reinsurer of the FEGLI Program as set forth in the contract is an allowable cost when documented through an internal accounting entry of the contractor and actually paid. This amount is deemed to be sufficient to reimburse reinsurers for the minor administrative expenses incurred in reinsuring the FEGLI Program.
