32 C.F.R. § 158.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:158.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-158.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 158 (Operational Contract Support (Ocs) Outside The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes policy, assigns responsibilities, and provides procedures for operational contract support (OCS), including contract support integration, contracting support, management, and deployment of defense contractor personnel in applicable operations outside the United States.
