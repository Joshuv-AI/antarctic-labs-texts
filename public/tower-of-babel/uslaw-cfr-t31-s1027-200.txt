31 C.F.R. § 1027.200 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1027.200
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1027.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1027 (Rules For Dealers In Precious Metals, Precious Stones, Or Jewels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dealers in precious metals, precious stones, or jewels are subject to the program requirements set forth and cross referenced in this subpart. Dealers in precious metals, precious stones, or jewels should also refer to subpart B of part 1010 of this chapter for program requirements contained in that subpart which apply to dealers in precious metals, precious stones, or jewels.
