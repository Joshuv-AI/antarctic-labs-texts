17 C.F.R. § 274.222 — Form N-CR, Current report of money market fund material events.
Citation: us:cfr:17:274.222
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-274.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 274 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used by registered investment companies that are regulated as money market funds under § 270.2a-7 of this chapter to file current reports pursuant to § 270.30b1-8 of this chapter within the time periods specified in the form.
