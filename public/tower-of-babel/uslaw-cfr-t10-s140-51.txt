10 C.F.R. § 140.51 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:140.51
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-140.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 140 (Financial Protection Requirements And Indemnity Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies only to persons found by the Commission to be Federal agencies, which have applied for or are holders of licenses issued pursuant to part 50 of this chapter authorizing operation of nuclear reactors.
