29 U.S.C. § 162 — Offenses and penalties
Citation: us:usc:29:162
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section162
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 7 (Labor-Management Relations); Subchapter II (National Labor Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who shall willfully resist, prevent, impede, or interfere with any member of the Board or any of its agents or agencies in the performance of duties pursuant to this subchapter shall be punished by a fine of not more than $5,000 or by imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.
