2 U.S.C. § 1971 — Contributions of meals and refreshments during emergency duty
Citation: us:usc:2:1971
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1971
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter II (Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At any time on or after November 12, 2001, the United States Capitol Police may accept contributions of meals and refreshments in support of activities of the United States Capitol Police during a period of emergency (as determined by the Capitol Police Board).
