4 U.S.C. § 108 — Same; jurisdiction of United States over Federal areas unaffected
Citation: us:usc:4:108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section108
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 4 (The States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 105–110 of this title shall not for the purposes of any other provision of law be deemed to deprive the United States of exclusive jurisdiction over any Federal area over which it would otherwise have exclusive jurisdiction or to limit the jurisdiction of the United States over any Federal area.
