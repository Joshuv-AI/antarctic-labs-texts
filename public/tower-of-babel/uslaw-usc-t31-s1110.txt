31 U.S.C. § 1110 — Year-ahead requests for authorizing legislation
Citation: us:usc:31:1110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1110
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 11 (The Budget And Fiscal, Budget, And Program Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A request to enact legislation authorizing new budget authority to continue a program or activity for a fiscal year shall be submitted to Congress before May 16 of the year before the year in which the fiscal year begins. If a new program or activity will continue for more than one year, the request must be submitted for at least the first and 2d fiscal years.
