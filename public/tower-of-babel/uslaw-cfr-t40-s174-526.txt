40 C.F.R. § 174.526 — Hygromycin B phosphotransferase (APH4) marker protein in all plants; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:174.526
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-174.526
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 174 (Procedures And Requirements For Plant-Incorporated Protectants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Residues of the Hygromycin B phosphotransferase (APH4) enzyme in all plants are exempt from the requirement of a tolerance when used as a plant-incorporated protectant inert ingredient in cotton.
