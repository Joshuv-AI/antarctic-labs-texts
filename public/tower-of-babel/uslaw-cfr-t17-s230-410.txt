17 C.F.R. § 230.410 — Disclaimer of control.
Citation: us:cfr:17:230.410
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-230.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 230 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the existence of control is open to reasonable doubt in any instance, the registrant may disclaim the existence of control and any admission thereof; in such case, however, the registrant shall state the material facts pertinent to the possible existence of control.
