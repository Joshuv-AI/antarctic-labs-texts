46 U.S.C. § 101 — Agency
Citation: us:usc:46:101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section101
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “agency” means a department, agency, or instrumentality of the United States Government.
