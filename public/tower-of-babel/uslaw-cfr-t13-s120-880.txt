13 C.F.R. § 120.880 — Basic eligibility requirements.

Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.880
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the eligibility requirements specified in subpart A, to be an eligible Borrower for a 504 loan, a small business must:
(a) Use the Project Property (except that an Eligible Passive Company may lease to an Operating Company); and
(b) Together with its Affiliates, meet one of the size standards set forth in § 121.301(b) of this chapter.
