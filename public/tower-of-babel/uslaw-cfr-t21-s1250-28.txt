21 C.F.R. § 1250.28 — Source and handling of ice.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1250.28
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1250.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1250 (Interstate Conveyance Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ice coming in contact with food or drink and not manufactured on the conveyance shall be obtained from sources approved by competent health authorities. All ice coming in contact with food or drink shall be stored and handled in such manner as to avoid contamination.
