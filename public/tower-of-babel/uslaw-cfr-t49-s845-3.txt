49 C.F.R. § 845.3 — Sessions open to the public.
Citation: us:cfr:49:845.3
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-845.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 845 (Rules Of Practice In Transportation: Investigative Hearings; Meetings, Reports, And Petitions For Reconsideration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All investigative hearings shall normally be open to the public. However, no person shall be allowed at any time to interfere with the proper and orderly functioning of the hearing.
(b) Sessions shall not be open to the public when evidence of a classified nature or which affects national security is to be received.
