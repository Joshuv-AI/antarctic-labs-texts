46 C.F.R. § 70.20-1 — -1 Marine engineering details.
Citation: us:cfr:46:70.20-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-70.20-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 70 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All marine engineering details such as piping, valves, fittings, boilers, pressure vessels, etc., and their appurtenances installed on the vessel, shall be designed, constructed, and installed in accordance with the provisions of subchapter F (Marine Engineering) of this chapter.
