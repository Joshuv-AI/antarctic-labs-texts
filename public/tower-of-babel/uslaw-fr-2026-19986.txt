DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration


Industry Day, Aviation Spectrum Interference Detection Solution

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), Department of 
Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Notice of public event; Industry Day.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA is hosting an Industry Engagement Event for a Spectrum 
Interference Detection Solution October 13-15, 2026. The FAA will use 
the information from vendor responses during event registration to 
assist in determining any future phases of market investigation and 
engagement. The FAA does not commit to an acquisition with this notice.

DATES: Send comments on or before 28 SEP2026. The unclassified portion 
of the registration is being extended until 11:59 p.m. EST on Friday, 
October 2, 2026.

ADDRESSES: The full announcement and market survey questions for this 
event can also be found at: https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/d62d0987495f4afba55a071142cc6a1f/view.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Gilbert Park, FAA Contracting Officer, 
Training & Aviation Acquisition Branch (AAQ-740), 
[email protected], (405) 954-7898, FAA Headquarters, 800 
Independence Ave. SW, Washington, DC.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The FAA is hosting an Industry Engagement 
Event for a Spectrum Interference Detection Solution October 13-15, 
2026 for the following purposes:
    1. Communicate emerging need to industry for a new mission 
capability resulting from unprecedented spectrum utilization, requests, 
auctions, and technology proliferation.
    2. Provide background, historical events, and use case vision to 
inform industry about the FAA's interference detection capability gaps 
and inquire about possible solutions and technologies necessary to 
detect and locate spectrum interference in any one of the bands 
utilized for aviation.
    3. Gather information to assess the availability of solution(s). 
Registration will include a response to a short list of survey 
questions that one designated participant per vendor company will need 
to upload. Conduct follow-up with vendors, as needed, to understand RFI 
responses.
    4. Assess industry interest and availability of solutions among 
vendors

[[Page 61914]]

and determine where challenges exist in producing the needed 
capabilities for such a system.
    The FAA seeks to understand industry interest and capabilities to 
modernize and accelerate the real-time detection, characterization, and 
reporting of interference affecting spectrum-dependent aviation 
systems. Potential solutions should proactively monitor the radio 
frequency (RF) environment and, to the greatest extent practicable, 
detect, identify, characterize, geolocate, and alert on RF 
interference, jamming, spoofing, and other anomalous conditions that 
could degrade aviation communications, navigation, surveillance, or 
other spectrum-dependent aviation capabilities. The ideal solution will 
continuously monitor RF activity relevant to aviation operations, 
collect and analyze spectrum metrics and system performance data to 
identify abnormal or unauthorized emissions, correlate information 
across multiple sensors, platforms, and geographic locations, and 
provide timely reporting to support rapid assessment and mitigation. 
Desired capabilities include signal detection and classification, 
emitter characterization, geolocation where feasible, waveform and 
protocol analysis, trend analysis, and identification of potential 
interference sources and contributing factors.
    The solution should collect, retain, and report data to enable 
timely operational response by the FAA, as well as short-term 
operational assessments and long-term trend analysis. The solution 
should identify and visualize interference, congestion, and other RF 
activity patterns over time and across geographic locations. Historical 
data should be retained in a searchable repository and support flexible 
querying, export, visualization, and reporting. The solution should 
support post-event analysis by correlating RF conditions with aircraft 
operations and other relevant operational data, where available.
    The FAA is interested in solutions applicable across the National 
Airspace System (NAS), including airport surface, terminal, and enroute 
environments; continental United States (CONUS) and outside the 
continental United States (OCONUS); domestic and oceanic operations; 
and both ground-based and airborne systems. The system should be able 
to monitor all aviation frequencies in use. Solutions should be capable 
of supporting scalable deployment across distributed sensor networks 
while providing centralized data aggregation, analysis, and 
visualization.
    The FAA seeks to understand the stage of evolution of proposed 
solutions, including whether they are in the design, development, 
implementation, integration, operational, or commercially deployable 
phase. Respondents should also identify the role(s) they envision, such 
as technology developer, systems integrator, service provider, or 
system operator.
    Events: The Industry Day event is October 13-15, 2026.
    Industry Engagement Day 1: This day will include presentations by 
the FAA to describe the FAA interest and objectives for an aviation 
spectrum interference detection solution. Presentation topics include 
current aviation spectrum environment and interference challenges, FAA 
vision and exploration of potential solutions, and desired technical 
capabilities (monitoring, detection, analytics, data, architecture, and 
integration). The FAA is interested in feedback on potential 
acquisition solutions and the possibility of purchasing as a service. 
The first portion of the day will be open to all registrants and 
available in-person or online, while the second portion will be 
classified at the Secret level and restricted to registrants with a 
Secret Clearance and in-person only. The event will be held at The 
MITRE Corporation in McLean, VA.
    Vendor One-on-One Sessions (Days 2 and 3): The FAA may invite 
vendors to attend a One-On-One Session, either unclassified (in-person 
or virtual) or classified at the Secret level (in-person only). 
Sessions with individual vendors will be scheduled at the discretion of 
the FAA, based on vendor survey responses included in the event 
registration. If requested to provide additional information, brief 
vendor presentations are encouraged and vendors should expect 
discussion and questions from the FAA regarding their solution(s). The 
sessions will be held at The MITRE Corporation in McLean, VA.

Registration

    All attendees (virtual or in-person) wishing to participate in any 
portion of the event must complete registration at https://na.eventscloud.com/888200 by September 25, 2026. One registrant per 
vendor will be asked to act as the organization's designated point of 
contact and will be required to answer the vendor survey questions.
    Additionally, if you wish to participate in a Vendor One-on-One 
Session, include this request in the registration form. Note that 
participation in a Vendor One-on-One Session is at the discretion of 
the FAA and will be based on FAA personnel availability and need. The 
FAA will notify you of their availability and time for this session by 
October 2, 2026.
    The FAA may hold conversations, as needed, with respondents to 
clarify any technical or interpretive questions regarding the request 
for information.
