42 C.F.R. § 411.160 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:411.160
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-411.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 411 (Exclusions From Medicare And Limitations On Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth special rules that apply to individuals who are eligible for, or entitled to, Medicare on the basis of ESRD. (Section 406.13 of this chapter contains the rules for eligibility and entitlement based on ESRD.)
