40 C.F.R. § 273.20 — Exports.
Citation: us:cfr:40:273.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-273.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 273 (Standards For Universal Waste Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A small quantity handler of universal waste who sends universal waste to a foreign destination is subject to the requirements of 40 CFR part 262, subpart H.
