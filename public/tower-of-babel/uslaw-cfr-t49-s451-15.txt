49 C.F.R. § 451.15 — Application for individual approval.
Citation: us:cfr:49:451.15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-451.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 451 (Testing And Approval Of Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For approval of new containers by individual approval, each application must include the following:
(1) The manufacturer's identification number.
(2) The identification code of the owner, lessee, or bailee responsible for maintenance of the container.
