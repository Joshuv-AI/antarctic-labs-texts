9 C.F.R. § 311.22 — Hogs affected with urticaria, tinea tonsurans, demodex follicurlorum, or erythema.
Citation: us:cfr:9:311.22
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-311.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 311 (Disposal Of Diseased Or Otherwise Adulterated Carcasses And Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carcasses of hogs affected with urticaria (nettle rash), tinea tonsurans, demodex folliculorum, or erythema may be passed for human food after detaching and condemning the affected skin, if the carcass is otherwise not adulterated.
