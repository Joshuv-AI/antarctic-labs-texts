31 U.S.C. § 3341 — Sale of Government warrants, checks, drafts, and obligations
Citation: us:usc:31:3341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3341
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 33 (Depositing, Keeping, And Paying Money); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A disbursing official of the United States Government may sell a Government warrant, check, draft, or obligation not the property of the official at a premium, or dispose of the proceeds of the warrant, check, draft, or obligation, only if the official deposits the premium and the proceeds in the Treasury or with a depositary for the credit of the Government.
(b)
A disbursing official violating subsection (a) of this section shall be dismissed immediately.
