32 C.F.R. § 61.7 — Purpose.

Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-61.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 61 (Family Advocacy Program (Fap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part is composed of several subparts, each containing its own purpose. The purpose of the overall part is to implement policy, assign responsibilities, and provide procedures for addressing child abuse and domestic abuse in military communities.
(b) This subpart prescribes uniform program standards (PSs) for all installation FAPs.
