14 C.F.R. § 297.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:297.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-297.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 297 (Foreign Air Freight Forwarders And Foreign Cooperative Shippers Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes registration procedures and operating rules for foreign air carriers that engage indirectly in interstate or foreign air transportation of property. It relieves these carriers from certain provisions of Subtitle VII of Title 49 of the United States Code (Transportation), and establishes simplified reports for them.
