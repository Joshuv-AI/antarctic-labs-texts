10 U.S.C. § 237 — Embedded mental health providers of the reserve components: display of budget information
Citation: us:usc:10:237
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section237
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 9 (Defense Budget Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall submit to Congress, as a part of the documentation that supports the President’s annual budget for the Department of Defense, a budget justification display with respect to embedded mental health providers within each reserve component, including the amount requested for each such component.
