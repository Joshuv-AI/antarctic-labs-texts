47 C.F.R. § 24.11 — Initial authorization.
Citation: us:cfr:47:24.11
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-24.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 24 (Personal Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An applicant must file a single application for an initial authorization for all markets won and frequency blocks desired.
(b) Blanket licenses are granted for each market and frequency block. Applications for individual sites are not required and will not be accepted.
