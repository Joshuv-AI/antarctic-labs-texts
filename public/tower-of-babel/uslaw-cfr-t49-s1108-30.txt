49 C.F.R. § 1108.30 — No precedent.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1108.30
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1108.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1108 (Arbitration Of Certain Disputes Subject To The Statutory Jurisdiction Of The Surface Transportation Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Arbitration decisions under this subpart shall have no precedential value, and their outcomes and reasoning may not be submitted into evidence or argued in subsequent arbitration proceedings conducted under this subpart or in any Board proceeding, except an appeal of the arbitration decision under § 1108.31.
