25 C.F.R. § 256.25 — How will I find out that the work is done?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.25
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your servicing housing office will advise you, in writing, that the work has been completed in compliance with the project contract. Also, you will have a final walk-through of the house with a representative of your servicing housing office. You will be requested to verify that you received the notice of completion of the work by signing a copy of the notice and returning it to your servicing housing office.
