22 U.S.C. § 8175 — Notice of IAEA notifications
Citation: us:usc:22:8175
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8175
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 88 (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty—United States Additional Protocol Implementation); Subchapter VII (Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall notify Congress of any notifications issued by the IAEA to the United States under Article 10 of the Additional Protocol.
