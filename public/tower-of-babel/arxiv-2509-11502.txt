Abstract
By extending our analysis and simulations of our 37 K β \beta -decay data set to allow the β \beta asymmetry with respect to nuclear spin to vary with β \beta energy E β E_{\beta} , we have gained sensitivity to new physics that depends on a helicity factor for the β \beta , m β / E β m_{\beta}/E_{\beta} . In particular, we constrain Lorentz scalar and tensor quark-lepton interaction strengths at a sensitivity complementary to the similar Fierz interference term in neutron β \beta decay. Our result for that new physics is b F b_{F} = -0.0002 ± \pm 0.024 (stat) ± \pm 0.039 (syst), consistent with the standard model electroweak interaction value b F = 0 b_{F}=0 . We consider presently achieved complementarity to β \beta -decay and particle physics experiments, along with projectable technical improvements to our method.

The standard model electroweak interaction always makes left-handed helicity leptons paired with right-handed helicity antileptons.
In a previous publication

Our observable,

Here we extend simulations and analysis techniques to allow for different new physics that couples a

Theory and Methods

New physics that couples outgoing leptons and antileptons with same helicity– rather than opposite helicity as in the standard model weak interaction– produces a decay contribution dependent on a helicity factor of the massive
\beta

m_{\beta}/E_{\beta}

The decay rate expression then adds the Fierz term

\frac{dW}{dE}=F(E,Z)pEp_{\nu}E_{\nu}(1+bm/E+\hat{I}\cdot(A_{\beta}\frac{\vec{p_{\beta}}}{E})),

The normalized coefficient of

A_{\beta}[E]=A_{\beta}/(1+mb/E)\approx A_{\beta}(1-mb/E)

We write the approximate expression to emphasize that sensitivity to

\displaystyle A_{\beta}=\frac{\frac{\pm\rho^{2}}{J+1}(C_{T}C_{T}^{\prime}-C_{A}C_{A}^{\prime})}{1+\rho^{2}+((C_{S}^{2}+C_{S}^{\prime 2})+(C_{T}^{2}+C_{T}^{\prime 2})/2)}

There is new physics less universal than the Lee-Yang Lagrangian.
When quarks are combined into nucleons by QCD, there are two induced lepton-nucleon currents that flip sign when n

Conclusions

We have used our
\beta
asymmetry data from the decay of
37
K to constrain new physics that depends on the
\beta
energy, measuring the Fierz interference term from quark-lepton Lorentz scalar and vector couplings

b_{F}
= -0.0002
\pm
0.024 (stat)
\pm
0.039 (syst),
We provide constraints on a combination of Lorentz scalar and tensor lepton-quark couplings. Our present results using our laser-cooled atoms are dominated by a combination of statistics and solvable
\beta
scattering, and we hope to improve sensitivity by a factor of about five to enable complementarity with experiments indicating possible existence of non-standard model physics.
