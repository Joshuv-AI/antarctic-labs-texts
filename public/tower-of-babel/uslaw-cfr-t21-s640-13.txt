21 C.F.R. § 640.13 — Collection of the blood.
Citation: us:cfr:21:640.13
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-640.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 640 (Additional Standards For Human Blood And Blood Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The source blood shall be collected as prescribed in § 640.4.
(b) Source blood may also be derived from Whole Blood manufactured in accordance with applicable provisions of this subchapter.
