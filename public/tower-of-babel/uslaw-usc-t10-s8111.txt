10 U.S.C. § 8111 — Navy: grades above chief warrant officer, W–5
Citation: us:usc:10:8111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8111
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 812 (Grade And Rank Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The commissioned grades in the Navy above the grade of chief warrant officer, W–5, are the following:
(1)
Admiral.
(2)
Vice admiral.
(3)
Rear admiral.
(4)
Rear admiral (lower half).
(5)
Captain.
(6)
Commander.
(7)
Lieutenant commander.
(8)
Lieutenant.
(9)
Lieutenant (junior grade).
(10)
Ensign.
