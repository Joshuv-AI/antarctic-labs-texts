24 C.F.R. § 93.354 — Funding Accountability and Transparency Act.
Citation: us:cfr:24:93.354
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-93.354
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 93 (Housing Trust Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HTF grant to the grantee and all assistance provided to subgrantees and recipients shall be considered a Federal award for purposes of the Federal Funding Accountability and Transparency Act of 2006 (31 U.S.C. 6101 note).
