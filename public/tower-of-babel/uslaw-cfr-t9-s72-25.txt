9 C.F.R. § 72.25 — Dipping methods.
Citation: us:cfr:9:72.25
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-72.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 72 (Bovine Babesiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dipping is accomplished by thoroughly wetting the entire skin by either immersion in a chemical solution in a dip vat, or by spraying with a chemical solution using a spray-dip machine or a hand-held sprayer.
