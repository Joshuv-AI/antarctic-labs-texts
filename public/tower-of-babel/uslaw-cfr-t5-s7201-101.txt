5 C.F.R. § 7201.101 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:5:7201.101

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 7201 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Equal Employment Opportunity Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 CFR 2635.105, the regulations in this part apply to all employees of the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC), including members of the Commission and the General Counsel, and supplement the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch contained in 5 CFR part 2635.
