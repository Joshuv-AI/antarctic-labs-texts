16 C.F.R. § 1.23 — Quantity limit rules.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1.23
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1 (General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Quantity limit rules are authorized by section 2(a) of the Clayton Act, as amended by the Robinson-Patman Act. These rules have the force and effect of law.
