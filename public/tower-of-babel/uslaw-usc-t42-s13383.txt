42 U.S.C. § 13383 — Director of Climate Protection
Citation: us:usc:42:13383
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13383
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter VII (Global Climate Change). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within 6 months after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall establish, within the Department of Energy, a Director of Climate Protection (in this section referred to as the “Director”). The Director shall—
(1)
in the absence of the Secretary, serve as the Secretary’s representative for interagency and multilateral policy discussions of global climate change, including the activities of the Committee on Earth and Environmental Sciences as established by the Global Change Research Act of 1990 (Public Law 101–606) [15 U.S.C. 2921 et seq.] and the Policy Coordinating Committee Working Group on Climate Change;
(2)
monitor, in cooperation with other Federal agencies, domestic and international policies for their effects on the generation of greenhouse gases; and
(3)
have the authority to participate in the planning activities of relevant Department of Energy programs.
