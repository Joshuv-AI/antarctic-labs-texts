2 C.F.R. § 1104.115 — Regulations governing DoD Components' internal procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1104.115
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1104.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1104 (Implementation Of Governmentwide Guidance For Grants And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On an interim basis pending completion of the update of the DoDGARs to implement OMB guidance published in 2 CFR part 200, DoD Components' internal pre-award, time-of-award, and post-award procedures will continue to comply with requirements in parts 21 and 22 of the DoDGARs (32 CFR parts 21 and 22) and other applicable Defense Grant and Agreement Regulatory System (DGARS) policies.
