21 C.F.R. § 830.60 — Relabeling of a device that is required to bear a unique device identifier.
Citation: us:cfr:21:830.60
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-830.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 830 (Unique Device Identification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you relabel a device that is required to bear a unique device identifier (UDI), you must:
(a) Assign a new device identifier to the device, and
(b) Keep a record showing the relationship of the prior device identifier to your new device identifier.
