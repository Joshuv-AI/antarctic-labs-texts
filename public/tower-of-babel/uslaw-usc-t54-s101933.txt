54 U.S.C. § 101933 — Term of commercial services contracts
Citation: us:usc:54:101933
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101933
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1019 (Concessions And Commercial Use Authorizations); Subchapter III (Commercial Services Authorization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A commercial services contract entered into pursuant to this title shall be awarded for a term not to exceed 10 years.
