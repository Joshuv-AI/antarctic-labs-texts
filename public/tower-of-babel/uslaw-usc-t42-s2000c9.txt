42 U.S.C. § 2000c–9 — Classification and assignment
Citation: us:usc:42:2000c–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000c–9
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter IV (Public Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall prohibit classification and assignment for reasons other than race, color, religion, sex or national origin.
