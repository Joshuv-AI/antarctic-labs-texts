Introduction

Given a graph
F
, a graph is called
F
-free if it does not contain
F
as a subgraph.
The classic Turán-type problem asks what is the maximum number of edges in an
n
-vertex
F
-free graph, where the maximum number of edges is called the
Turán number
of
F
, denoted by
ex(n,F).
The
n
-vertex
F
-free graph with
ex(n,F)
edges is called the
extremal graph
for
F
and the set of all extremal graphs on
n
vertices for
F
is denoted by
Ex(n,F).

In 1941, Turán
[
35
]
proved Turán theorem, which states that the Turán graph
T_{r}(n),
the balanced complete
r
-partite graph on
n
vertices, is the unique extremal graph for
K_{r+1}
.
Denote by
t_{r}(n)
the number of edges in
T_{r}(n).

The celebrated Erdős-Stone-Simonovits theorem
[
13
,
14
]

states

ex(n,F)=\bigg(1-\frac{1}{r}\bigg)\frac{n^{2}}{2}+o(n^{2}),

where
\chi(F)=r+1
is the chromatic number of
F
.
A graph is color-critical if it contains an edge whose deletion reduces its chromatic number. For any color-critical graph
F
with chromatic number
\chi(F)=r+1
, Erdős-Stone-Simonovits theorem shows that
ex(n,F)=(1-\frac{1}{r})\frac{n^{2}}{2}+o(n^{2}).
Later, Simonovits
[
33
]
gave the exact Turán number of
F
and proved that
T_{r}(n)
is the unique extremal graph for large
n
.

Theorem 1.1
(Simonovits,
[
33
]
)

Let
F
be a color-critical graph with
\chi(F)=r+1
. Then
there exists a number
n_{0}(F)
such that
T_{r}(n)
is the only extremal graph with respect to

ex(n,F)
provided
n\geqslant n_{0}(F)
.

Let
A(G)
be the adjacency matrix and
Q(G)=D(G)+A(G)
be the signless Laplacian matrix of a graph
G
, respectively,
where
D(G)
is the degree diagonal matrix of
G
. The largest eigenvalue of
A(G)
(resp.
Q(G)
) is called the
adjacency
(resp.
signless Laplacian
)
spectral radius
of
G
, denoted by
\lambda(G)
(resp.
q(G)
).
The
signless Laplacian

spectral Turán number
of a graph
F
, denoted by
ex_{ssp}(n,F)
, is the maximum signless Laplacian spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
F
-free graphs.
Denote by
Ex_{ssp}(n,F)
the set of all
n
-vertex
F
-free graphs with the signless Laplacian spectral radius
ex_{ssp}(n,F)
.

In 2010, Nikiforov
[
29
]
proposed the adjacency spectral Turán-type problem which asks the maximum adjacency spectral radius of an
n
-vertex
F
-free graph.
Guiduli
[
19
]
and Nikiforov
[
28
]
independently proved adjacency spectral Turán theorem, showing that if
G
attains the maximum adjacency spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
K_{r+1}
-free graphs, then
G=T_{r}(n).
Nikiforov
[
30
]
proved the adjacency spectral Erdős-Stone-Simonovits theorem.
A graph on
(t-1)k+1
vertices consisting of
k
cliques each with

vertices which
intersect in exactly one common vertex is called a
(k,t)
-fan
and denoted by

.
When

is also called the
k
-fan
and denoted by

Cioabǎ, et al.
[

showed that if

is a

-fan

and

has the maximum adjacency spectral radius among all
n
-vertex
F
-free graphs, then

for sufficiently large

Desai, et al.
[
11
]
showed that for
k\geqslant 2
and

if
F
is a
(k,t)
-fan
F_{k,t}
and
G
attains the maximum adjacency spectral radius among all

-vertex
F
-free graphs, then

for sufficiently large

Cioabǎ, Desai and Tait
[
8
]
investigated the maximum adjacency spectral radius of an

-vertex graph containing no odd wheel and posed the following conjecture: For any graph

satisfying that the graphs in

are Turán graphs plus

G

-vertex

In 2022, Wang, Kang and Xue

(
[

Motivated by the above results,

Question 1.3

Let

be an integer and

Let

Then

.
Notice that

.

Theorem 3.1

Let
1\gg\epsilon>0,

and
F
be a graph with
ex(n,F)=t_{r}(n)+O(1).
If

Moreover, we have
\left(\frac{1}{r}-3\sqrt{\epsilon}\right)n<|V_{i}|<\left(\frac{1}{r}+3\sqrt{\epsilon}\right)n
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant r;

(ii)

where
G_{in},

are two graphs with the same vertex set

and edge sets
\cup_{i=1}^{r}E(V_{i}),

Proof.

Apply the graph regularity lemma to obtain an
\eta

Remove all edges between

and

Then the number of edges in (a) is no more than
\eta n^{2}\leqslant\epsilon n^{2}/16
, the number of edges in (b) is less than
\epsilon n^{2}/16
, and the number of edges in (c) is less than
