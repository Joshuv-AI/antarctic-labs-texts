25 C.F.R. § 166.423 — How do Indian landowners receive grazing rental payments that the BIA has received from permittees?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.423
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.423
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds will be paid to the Indian landowners by the Office of Trust Funds Management in accordance with 25 CFR part 115.
