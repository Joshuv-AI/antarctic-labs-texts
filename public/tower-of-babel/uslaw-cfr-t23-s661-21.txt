23 C.F.R. § 661.21 — When is a bridge eligible for rehabilitation?
Citation: us:cfr:23:661.21
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-661.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 661 (Tribal Transportation Facility Bridge Program (Ttfbp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for rehabilitation, a TTF bridge must be in poor or fair condition, have low load capacity, or need highway geometric improvements.
