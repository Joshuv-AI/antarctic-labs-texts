45 C.F.R. § 1608.7 — Attorney-client relationship.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1608.7
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1608.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1608 (Prohibited Political Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part is intended to prohibit an attorney or staff attorney from providing any form of legal assistance to an eligible client, or to interfere with the fulfillment of any attorney's professional responsibilities to a client.
