21 C.F.R. § 20.28 — Food and Drug Administration determinations of confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:21:20.28
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-20.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 20 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A determination that data or information submitted to the Food and Drug Administration will be held in confidence and will not be available for public disclosure shall be made only in the form of a regulation published or cross-referenced in this part.
