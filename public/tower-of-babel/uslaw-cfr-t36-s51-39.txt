36 C.F.R. § 51.39 — What are some examples of outfitter and guide concession contracts?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.39
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Outfitter and guide concession contracts may include, but are not limited to, concession contracts which solely authorize or require the guided conduct of river running, hunting (where otherwise lawful in a park area), fishing, horseback, camping, and mountaineering activities in the backcountry of a park area.
