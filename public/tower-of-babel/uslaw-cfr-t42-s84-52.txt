42 C.F.R. § 84.52 — Respiratory hazards; classification.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.52
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Respirators described in subparts H through L of this part shall be classified as approved for use against any or all of the following respiratory hazards:
(a) Oxygen deficiency;
(b) Gases and vapors; and
(c) Particles, including dusts, fumes and mists.
