40 C.F.R. § 60.1030 — Can the Administrator delegate authority to enforce these Federal new source performance standards to a State agency?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.1030
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.1030
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the Administrator can delegate all authorities in all sections of this subpart to the State for direct State enforcement.
