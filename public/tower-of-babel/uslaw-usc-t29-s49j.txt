29 U.S.C. § 49j — Notice of strikes and lockouts to applicants
Citation: us:usc:29:49j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section49j
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 4B (Federal Employment Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the provisions of this chapter the Secretary is authorized and directed to provide for the giving of notice of strikes or lockouts to applicants before they are referred to employment.
