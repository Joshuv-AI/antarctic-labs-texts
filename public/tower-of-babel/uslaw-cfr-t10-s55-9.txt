10 C.F.R. § 55.9 — Completeness and accuracy of information.
Citation: us:cfr:10:55.9
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-55.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 55 (Operators' Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information provided to the Commission by an applicant for a license or by a licensee or information required by statute or by the Commission's regulations, orders, or license conditions to be maintained by the applicant or the licensee shall be complete and accurate in all material respects.
