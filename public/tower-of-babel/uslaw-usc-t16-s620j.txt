16 U.S.C. § 620j — Authority of Export Administration Act of 1979
Citation: us:usc:16:620j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section620j
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in sections 620 to 620j of this title shall be construed to—
(1)
prejudice the outcome of pending or prospective petitions filed under, or
(2)
warrant the exercise of the authority contained in,
section 7 1 of the Export Administration Act of 1979 with respect to the export of unprocessed timber.
