47 C.F.R. § 18.109 — General technical requirements.

Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 18 (Industrial, Scientific, And Medical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ISM equipment shall be designed and constructed in accordance with good engineering practice with sufficient shielding and filtering to provide adequate suppression of emissions on frequencies outside the frequency bands specified in § 18.301.
