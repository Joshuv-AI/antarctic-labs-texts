42 C.F.R. § 409.89 — Exemption of kidney donors from deductible and coinsurance requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:409.89
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-409.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 409 (Hospital Insurance Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The deductible and coinsurance requirements set forth in this subpart do not apply to any services furnished to an individual in connection with the donation of a kidney for transplant surgery.
