45 C.F.R. § 2584.112 — Review by institution.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2584.112
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2584.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2584 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research covered by this part that has been approved by an IRB may be subject to further appropriate review and approval or disapproval by officials of the institution. However, those officials may not approve the research if it has not been approved by an IRB.
