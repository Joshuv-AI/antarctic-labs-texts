Correction to: Nature Cancer https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01163-w, published online 12 May 2026.

In the version of the article initially published, in the top-left graph of Fig. 3b, the y-axis label was “CD4+CD69+ (%, delta)” but should have been “CD8+CD69+ (%, delta)”. In the top-right box of Fig. 5d, the label “Post-GNOS-PV01” should have read “Pre-GNOS-PV01”. These corrections have been made to the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
