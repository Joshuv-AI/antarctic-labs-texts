45 C.F.R. § 78.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:78.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-78.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 78 (Conditions For Waiver Of Denial Of Federal Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is applicable to any decision to deny Federal benefits, under authority of 21 U.S.C. 853a, to an individual convicted of a Federal or State offense involving distribution or possession of a controlled substance as defined by the Controlled Substances Act, 21 U.S.C. 802.
