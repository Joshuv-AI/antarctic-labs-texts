40 C.F.R. § 24.06 — Designation of Presiding Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:40:24.06
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-24.06
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 24 (Rules Governing Issuance Of And Administrative Hearings On Interim Status Corrective Action Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a request for a hearing, the Regional Administrator shall designate a Presiding Officer to conduct the hearing and preside over the proceedings.
