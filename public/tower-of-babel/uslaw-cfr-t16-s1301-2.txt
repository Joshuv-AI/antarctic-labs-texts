16 C.F.R. § 1301.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1301.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1301.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1301 (Ban Of Unstable Refuse Bins). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to ban those refuse bins which come under the scope of this ban because they present an unreasonable risk of injury due to tip-over that can result in serious injury or death from crushing.
