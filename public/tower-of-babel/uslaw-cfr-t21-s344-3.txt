21 C.F.R. § 344.3 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:344.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-344.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 344 (Topical Otic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
(a) Anhydrous glycerin. An ingredient that may be prepared by heating glycerin U.S.P. at 150 °C for 2 hours to drive off the moisture content.
(b) Earwax removal aid. A drug used in the external ear canal that aids in the removal of excessive earwax.
(c) Water-clogged ears. The retention of water in the external ear canal, thereby causing discomfort and a sensation of fullness or hearing impairment.
(d) Ear drying aid. A drug used in the external ear canal to help dry water-clogged ears.
