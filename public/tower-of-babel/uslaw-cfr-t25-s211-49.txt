25 C.F.R. § 211.49 — Restrictions on operations.
Citation: us:cfr:25:211.49
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-211.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 211 (Leasing Of Tribal Lands For Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases issued under the provisions of the regulations in this part shall be subject to such restrictions as to time or times for well operations and production from any leased premises as the Secretary judges may be necessary or proper for the protection of the natural resources of the leased land and in the interest of the lessor.
