2 U.S.C. § 5124 — Lump sum allowance for Speaker
Citation: us:usc:2:5124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5124
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 51 (House Of Representatives Leadership); Subchapter II (The Speaker). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The aggregate amount otherwise authorized to be appropriated for a fiscal year for the lump-sum allowance for the Office of the Speaker of the House of Representatives shall be increased by $40,000.
(b)
This section shall apply with respect to fiscal year 2002 and each succeeding fiscal year.
