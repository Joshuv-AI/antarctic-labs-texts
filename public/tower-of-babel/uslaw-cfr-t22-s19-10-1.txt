22 C.F.R. § 19.10-1 — -1 Full annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:22:19.10-1
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-19.10-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 19 (Benefits For Spouses And Former Spouses Of Participants In The Foreign Service Retirement And Disability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a participant retires and does not provide a survivor annuity to a spouse, former spouse or designated beneficiary, the participant receives a “full” annuity. A full annuity means an annuity computed without any survivorship reduction. Example: Average salary $20,000 and maximum of 35 years of service.
