42 U.S.C. § 7254 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:42:7254
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7254
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VI (Administrative Provisions); Part C (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to prescribe such procedural and administrative rules and regulations as he may deem necessary or appropriate to administer and manage the functions now or hereafter vested in him.
