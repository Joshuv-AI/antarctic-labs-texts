42 U.S.C. § 19101 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:42:19101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19101
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 163 (Research And Development, Competition, And Innovation); Subchapter III (National Science Foundation For The Future); Part G (Directorate For Technology, Innovation, And Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established within the Foundation the Directorate for Technology, Innovation, and Partnerships to advance research and development, technology development, and related solutions to address United States societal, national, and geostrategic challenges, for the benefit of all Americans.
