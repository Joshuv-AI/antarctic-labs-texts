51 U.S.C. § 31503 — Laboratory productivity
Citation: us:usc:51:31503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section31503
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 315 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administration’s laboratories are a critical component of the Administration’s research capabilities, and the Administrator shall ensure that those laboratories remain productive.
