19 U.S.C. § 197 — Duties, how payable
Citation: us:usc:19:197
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section197
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 198 of this title all duties upon imports shall be collected in ready money, and shall be paid in coin, coin certificates, and such other certificates or Treasury notes as may by law be declared receivable in payment thereof.
