2 U.S.C. § 1555 — “Federal mandate” defined
Citation: us:usc:2:1555
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1555
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 25 (Unfunded Mandates Reform); Subchapter III (Review Of Federal Mandates). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 1502 of this title, for purposes of this subchapter the term “Federal mandate” means any provision in statute or regulation or any Federal court ruling that imposes an enforceable duty upon State, local, or tribal governments including a condition of Federal assistance or a duty arising from participation in a voluntary Federal program.
