42 U.S.C. § 6371i — Records
Citation: us:usc:42:6371i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6371i
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter III (Improving Energy Efficiency); Part E (Energy Conservation Program For Schools And Hospitals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each recipient of assistance under this part shall keep such records, provide such reports, and furnish such access to books and records as the Secretary may by rule prescribe.
