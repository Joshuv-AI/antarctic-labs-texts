48 C.F.R. § 801.000 — 801.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:801.000
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-801.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 801 (Department Of Veterans Affairs Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part includes general Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Acquisition Regulation (VAAR) policies, including information regarding the maintenance and administration of the VAAR, acquisition policies and practices, and procedures for deviation from the VAAR and the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR).
