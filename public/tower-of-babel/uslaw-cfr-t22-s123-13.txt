22 C.F.R. § 123.13 — Domestic aircraft shipments via a foreign country.
Citation: us:cfr:22:123.13
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-123.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 123 (Licenses For The Export And Temporary Import Of Defense Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A license is not required for the shipment by air of a defense article from one location in the United States to another location in the United States via a foreign country.
