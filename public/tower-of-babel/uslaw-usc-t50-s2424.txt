50 U.S.C. § 2424 — Procedures relating to access by individuals to classified areas and information of Administration
Citation: us:usc:50:2424
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2424
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 41 (National Nuclear Security Administration); Subchapter II (Matters Relating To Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall establish appropriate procedures to ensure that any individual is not permitted unescorted access to any classified area, or access to classified information, of the Administration until that individual has been verified to hold the appropriate security clearances.
