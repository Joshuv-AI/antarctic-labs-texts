50 C.F.R. § 31.13 — Do we allow commercial harvest of fishery resources?
Citation: us:cfr:50:31.13
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-31.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 31 (Wildlife Species Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Refuge managers may allow commercial harvest of fishery resources by issuance of a permit or by refuge-specific regulation in compliance with applicable State and Federal laws when compatible and in compliance with § 29.1 of this subchapter C.
