33 U.S.C. § 6 — Free passage to harbor of Michigan City, Indiana
Citation: us:usc:33:6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section6
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The passage of vessels to and from the harbor of Michigan City, in Indiana, shall be free and not subject to toll or charge.
