7 U.S.C. § 1471f — Use of Commodity Credit Corporation
Citation: us:usc:7:1471f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1471f
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter V (Emergency Livestock Feed Assistance Act Of 1988). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall carry out this subchapter through the use of the funds, facilities, and authorities of the Commodity Credit Corporation.
