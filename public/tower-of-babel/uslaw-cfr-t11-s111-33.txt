11 C.F.R. § 111.33 — What are the respondent's choices upon receiving the reason to believe finding and the proposed civil money penalty?
Citation: us:cfr:11:111.33
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-111.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 111 (COMPLIANCE PROCEDURE (52 U.S.C. 30109, 30107(a))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The respondent must either send payment in the amount of the proposed civil money penalty pursuant to 11 CFR 111.34 or submit a written response pursuant to 11 CFR 111.35.
