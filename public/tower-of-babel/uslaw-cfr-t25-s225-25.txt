25 C.F.R. § 225.25 — Resolution of disputes.
Citation: us:cfr:25:225.25
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-225.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 225 (Oil And Gas, Geothermal, And Solid Minerals Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A minerals agreement shall contain provisions for resolving disputes that may arise between the parties. However, no such provision shall limit the Secretary's authority or ability to ensure that the rights of an Indian mineral owner are protected in the event of a violation of the provisions of the minerals agreement by any other party to the minerals agreement.
