20 C.F.R. § 408.660 — What happens to your accumulated funds when your representative payee changes?

Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a description of what happens to your accumulated funds (including the interest earned on the funds) when we change your representative payee or when you begin receiving benefits directly, see § 404.2060 of this chapter.
