13 C.F.R. § 500.101 — Composition of the Board.
Citation: us:cfr:13:500.101
Creator: Emergency Oil And Gas Guaranteed Loan Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-500.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 500 (Emergency Oil And Gas Guaranteed Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board consists of the Chairman of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, who acts as Chairman of the Board, the Chairman of the Securities and Exchange Commission, and the Secretary of Commerce.
