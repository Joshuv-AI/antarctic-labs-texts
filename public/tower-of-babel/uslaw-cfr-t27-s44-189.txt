27 C.F.R. § 44.189 — Transfers between factories and export warehouses.
Citation: us:cfr:27:44.189
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-44.189
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 44 (Exportation Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, Or With Drawback Of Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where tobacco products, and cigarette papers and tubes are transferred, without payment of tax, from a factory to an export warehouse or between export warehouses, such articles shall be consigned to the export warehouse proprietor to whom such articles are to be delivered.
