5 C.F.R. § 838.625 — Types of annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.625
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.625
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Terms that are synonymous with net annuity are—
(1) Disposable annuity; and
(2) Retirement check.
(b) Terms that are synonymous with self-only annuity are—
(1) Life rate annuity;
(2) Unreduced annuity; and
(3) Annuity without survivor benefit.
(c) All court orders that do not specify net annuity or self-only annuity apply to gross annuity.
