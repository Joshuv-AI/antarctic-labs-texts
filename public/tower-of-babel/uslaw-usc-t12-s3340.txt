12 U.S.C. § 3340 — Time for proposal and adoption of standards
Citation: us:usc:12:3340
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3340
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 34A (Appraisal Subcommittee Of Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appraisal standards established under this chapter shall be proposed not later than 6 months and shall be adopted in final form and become effective not later than 12 months after August 9, 1989.
