14 C.F.R. § 414.29 — Safety element approval records.
Citation: us:cfr:14:414.29
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-414.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 414 (Safety Element Approvals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The holder of a safety element approval must maintain all records necessary to verify that the holder's activities are consistent with the representations contained in the application for which the approval was issued for the duration of the safety element approval plus one year.
