30 C.F.R. § 1217.301 — Lease account reconciliations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1217.301
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1217.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1217 (Audits And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Specific lease account reconciliations will be performed with priority being given to reconciling those lease accounts specifically identified by a State as having significant potential for underpayment.
