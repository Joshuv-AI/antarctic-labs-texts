11 C.F.R. § 9002.4 — Eligible candidates.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9002.4
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9002.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9002 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible candidates means those Presidential and Vice Presidential candidates who have met all applicable conditions for eligibility to receive payments from the Fund under 11 CFR part 9003.
