31 C.F.R. § 535.566 — Unblocking of foreign currency deposits held by U.S.-owned or controlled foreign firms.
Citation: us:cfr:31:535.566
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-535.566
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 535 (Iranian Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Deposits held abroad in currencies other than U.S. dollars by branches and subsidiaries of persons subject to the jurisdiction of the United States are unblocked, provided however that conversions of blocked dollar deposits into foreign currencies are not authorized.
