22 U.S.C. § 2606 — Audits of U.S. funds received by the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees
Citation: us:usc:22:2606
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2606
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 36 (Migration And Refugee Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Program audits
Funds may not be available to the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) under this chapter or any other Act unless provision is made for—
(1)
annual program audits to determine the use of UNHCR funds, including the use of such funds by implementing partners; and
(2)
such audits are made available through the Department of State for inspection by the Comptroller General of the United States.
(b) First program audit
The first program audit pursuant to subsection (a)(1) shall begin not later than June 1, 1986.
