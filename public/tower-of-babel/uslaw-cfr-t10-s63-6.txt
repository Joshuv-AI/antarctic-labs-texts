10 C.F.R. § 63.6 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:63.6
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-63.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 63 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In A Geologic Repository At Yucca Mountain, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, upon application by DOE, any interested person, or upon its own initiative, grant an exemption from the requirements of this part if it determines that the exemption is authorized by law, does not endanger life nor property nor the common defense and security, and is otherwise in the public interest.
