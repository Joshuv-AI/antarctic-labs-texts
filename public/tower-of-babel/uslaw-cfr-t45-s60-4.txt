45 C.F.R. § 60.4 — How information must be reported.
Citation: us:cfr:45:60.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-60.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 60 (National Practitioner Data Bank). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information must be reported to the NPDB as required under §§ 60.7, 60.8, 60.9, 60.10, 60.11, 60.12, 60.13, 60.14, 60.15 and 60.16 in such form and manner as the Secretary may prescribe.
