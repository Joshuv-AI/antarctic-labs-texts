20 U.S.C. § 84 — Plans for buildings and bridges
Citation: us:usc:20:84
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section84
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 4 (National Zoological Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All plans and specifications for the construction of buildings and bridges in the National Zoological Park shall be prepared under the supervision of the Smithsonian Institution.
