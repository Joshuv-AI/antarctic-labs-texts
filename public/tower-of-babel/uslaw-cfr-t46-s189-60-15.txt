46 C.F.R. § 189.60-15 — -15 Cargo Ship Safety Radio Certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:46:189.60-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-189.60-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 189 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every vessel equipped with a radio installation on an international voyage must have a Cargo Ship Safety Radio Certificate. Each radio installation must meet the requirements of the Federal Communication Commission and the International Convention for Safety of Life at Sea.
