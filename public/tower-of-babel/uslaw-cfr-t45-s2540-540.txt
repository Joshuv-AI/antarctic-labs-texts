45 C.F.R. § 2540.540 — Who has authority to approve use of national service insignia?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2540.540
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2540.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2540 (General Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approval for limited uses may be provided through the terms of a written grant or other agreement. All other uses must be approved in writing by the director of the Corporation's Office of Public Affairs, or his or her designee.
