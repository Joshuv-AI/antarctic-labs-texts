46 C.F.R. § 154.707 — Cargo boil-off as fuel: Ventilation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.707
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A ventilation hood or casing must be installed in areas occupied by flanges, valves, and piping at the fuel burner to cause air to sweep across them and be exhausted at the top of the hood or casing.
(b) The hood or casing must be mechanically exhaust-ventilated and meet § 154.1205.
(c) The ventilated hood or casing must have an airflow rate specially approved by the Commandant.
