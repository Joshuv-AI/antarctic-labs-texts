21 C.F.R. § 1271.26 — When must I amend my establishment registration?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1271.26
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1271.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1271 (Human Cells, Tissues, And Cellular And Tissue-Based Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the ownership or location of your establishment changes, or if there is a change in the United States agent's name, address, telephone number, or email address, you must submit an amendment to registration within 30 calendar days of the change.
