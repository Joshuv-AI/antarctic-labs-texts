30 U.S.C. § 306 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:30:306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section306
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 5 (Lease Of Oil And Gas Deposits In Or Under Railroads And Other Rights-Of-Way). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to adopt rules and regulations governing the exercise of the discretion and authority conferred by this chapter, which rules and regulations shall constitute a part of any application or lease hereunder.
