31 C.F.R. § 801.301 — Control.
Citation: us:cfr:31:801.301
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-801.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 801 (Pilot Program To Review Certain Transactions Involving Foreign Persons And Critical Technologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the sole purpose of determining whether a transaction could result in control of a pilot program U.S. business by a foreign person, the provisions set forth in subpart C of this part (excluding § 800.302(b) of this chapter and the examples thereunder) regarding covered transactions shall apply to any pilot program covered transaction declared to the Committee pursuant to § 801.401 or notified to the Committee pursuant to § 801.501.
