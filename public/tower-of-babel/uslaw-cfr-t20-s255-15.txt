20 C.F.R. § 255.15 — Waiver to an estate.
Citation: us:cfr:20:255.15
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-255.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 255 (Recovery Of Overpayments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall never be considered contrary to the purpose of the Railroad Retirement Act to recover an overpayment from the estate of an overpaid individual.
