12 U.S.C. § 1706b — Taxation of real property held by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:12:1706b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1706b
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter I (Housing Renovation And Modernization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to exempt any real property acquired and held by the Secretary in connection with the payment of insurance heretofore or hereafter granted under this subchapter from taxation by any State or political subdivision thereof, to the same extent, according to its value, as other real property is taxed.
