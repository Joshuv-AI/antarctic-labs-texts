36 U.S.C. § 21106 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:21106
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 211 (American Gold Star Mothers, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has only the powers provided in its bylaws and articles of incorporation filed in each State in which it is incorporated.
