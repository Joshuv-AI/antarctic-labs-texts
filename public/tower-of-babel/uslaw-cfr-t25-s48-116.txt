25 C.F.R. § 48.116 — Who may investigate compliance with a lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:48.116
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-48.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 48 (Leases Of Land Or Facilities Of Bureau-Operated Schools And Fundraising Activities At Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Head of the School or his or her designee or any Bureau employee may enter the leased facility at any reasonable time, upon reasonable notice, and consistent with any notice requirements under the lease to determine if the lessee is in compliance with the requirements of the lease.
