40 C.F.R. § 355.33 — What release quantities of EHSs and CERCLA hazardous substances trigger the emergency release notification requirements of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:355.33
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-355.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 355 (Emergency Planning And Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The release of a reportable quantity (RQ) of an EHS or CERCLA hazardous substance within any 24-hour period triggers the emergency release notification requirements. RQs for EHSs are listed in Appendices A and B of this part in the column labeled “reportable quantity.” RQs for CERCLA hazardous substances are listed in Table 302.4 of 40 CFR 302.4 in the column labeled “final RQ.”
