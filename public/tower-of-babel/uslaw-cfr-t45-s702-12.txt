45 C.F.R. § 702.12 — Contempt of the Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:45:702.12
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-702.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 702 (Rules On Hearings, Reports, And Meetings Of The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proceedings and process of the Commission are governed by 42 U.S.C. 1975a(e)(2), which provides that in case of contumacy or refusal to obey a subpoena, the Attorney General may in a Federal court of appropriate jurisdiction obtain an appropriate order to enforce the subpoena.
