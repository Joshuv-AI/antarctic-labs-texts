31 U.S.C. § 6715 — Enforcement by the Attorney General of prohibitions on discrimination
Citation: us:usc:31:6715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6715
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 67 (Federal Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may bring a civil action in an appropriate district court of the United States against a unit of general local government that the Attorney General has reason to believe has engaged or is engaging in a pattern or practice in violation of section 6711(a) or (b). The court may grant—
(1)
a temporary restraining order;
(2)
an injunction; or
(3)
an appropriate order to ensure enjoyment of rights under section 6711(a) or (b), including an order suspending, terminating, or requiring repayment of, payments under this chapter or placing additional payments under this chapter in escrow pending the outcome of the action.
