28 U.S.C. § 1691 — Seal and teste of process
Citation: us:usc:28:1691
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1691
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 113 (Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All writs and process issuing from a court of the United States shall be under the seal of the court and signed by the clerk thereof.
