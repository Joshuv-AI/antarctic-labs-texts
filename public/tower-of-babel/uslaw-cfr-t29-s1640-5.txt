29 C.F.R. § 1640.5 — Date of receipt.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1640.5
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1640.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1640 (Procedures For Coordinating The Investigation Of Complaints Or Charges Of Employment Discrimination Based On Disability Subject To The Americans With Disabilities Act And Section 504 Of The Rehabilitation Act Of 1973). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A complaint or charge of employment discrimination is deemed to be filed, for purposes of determining timeliness, on the date the complaint or charge is first received by a Federal agency with section 504 or ADA jurisdiction, regardless of whether it is subsequently transferred to another agency for processing.
