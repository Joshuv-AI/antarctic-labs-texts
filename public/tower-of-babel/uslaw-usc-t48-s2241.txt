48 U.S.C. § 2241 — Sense of Congress regarding permanent, pro-growth fiscal reforms
Citation: us:usc:48:2241
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section2241
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 20 (Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, And Economic Stability); Subchapter VII (Sense Of Congress Regarding Permanent, Pro-Growth Fiscal Reforms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that any durable solution for Puerto Rico’s fiscal and economic crisis should include permanent, pro-growth fiscal reforms that feature, among other elements, a free flow of capital between possessions of the United States and the rest of the United States.
