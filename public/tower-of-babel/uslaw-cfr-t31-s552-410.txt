31 C.F.R. § 552.410 — Setoffs prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:31:552.410
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-552.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 552 (Yemen Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A setoff against blocked property (including a blocked account), whether by a U.S. bank or other U.S. person, is a prohibited transfer under § 552.201 if effected after the effective date.
