Retraction Note: Marangoni convection in dissipative flow of nanofluid through porous space
Authors: Ikram Ullah, Mohammad Mahtab Alam, Muhammad Irfan Shah, Wajaree Weera
Published: 2023

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-023-30795-6, published online 18 April 2023

The Editors have retracted this Article. After being informed of concerns regarding the content of the Article, the Authors were requested to provide the original raw data underlying this study, but despite repeated requests were unable to provide this. In addition, the Editors have developed concerns regarding the veracity of the authorship of this Article. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the reliability and the integrity of the work presented in this Article.

All Authors disagree with the retraction.
