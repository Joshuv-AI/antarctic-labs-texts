42 U.S.C. § 15112 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:15112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15112
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 144 (Developmental Disabilities Assistance And Bill Of Rights); Subchapter III (Program For Direct Support Workers Who Assist Individuals With Developmental Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Developmental disability
The term “developmental disability” has the meaning given the term in section 15002 of this title.
(2) Institution of higher education
The term “institution of higher education” has the meaning given the term in section 1141 1 of title 20.
(3) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
