43 U.S.C. § 392 — Payments into reclamation fund of moneys received from entrymen and water right applicants
Citation: us:usc:43:392
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section392
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter II (Reclamation Fund Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All moneys received from entrymen or applicants for water rights shall be paid into the reclamation fund.
