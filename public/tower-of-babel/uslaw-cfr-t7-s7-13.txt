7 C.F.R. § 7.13 — Tie votes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:7.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-7.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 7 (Selection And Functions Of Farm Service Agency State And County Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tie votes in county committee elections will be settled by lot in a manner that is open to the public.
