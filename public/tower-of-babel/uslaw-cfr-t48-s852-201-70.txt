48 C.F.R. § 852.201-70 — 852.201-70 Contracting Officer's Representative.
Citation: us:cfr:48:852.201-70
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-852.201-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 852 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 801.604, insert the following provision:
The Contracting Officer reserves the right to designate representatives to act for him/her in furnishing technical guidance and advice or generally monitor the work to be performed under this contract. Such designation will be in writing and will define the scope and limitation of the designee's authority. A copy of the designation letter shall be furnished to the Contractor.
