45 C.F.R. § 46.203 — Duties of IRBs in connection with research involving pregnant women, fetuses, and neonates.
Citation: us:cfr:45:46.203
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-46.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 46 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to other responsibilities assigned to IRBs under this part, each IRB shall review research covered by this subpart and approve only research which satisfies the conditions of all applicable sections of this subpart and the other subparts of this part.
