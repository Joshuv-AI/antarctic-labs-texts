34 C.F.R. § 75.618 — Safety and health standards.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.618
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.618
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In planning for and designing a construction project,
(a) A grantee must comply with the following:
(1) The standards under the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 (See 29 CFR part 1910).
(2) State and local codes, to the extent that they are more stringent.
(b) A grantee may use additional standards and best practices to support health and wellbeing of students and staff.
