29 C.F.R. § 801.60 — Commencement of proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:29:801.60
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-801.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 801 (Application Of The Employee Polygraph Protection Act Of 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each administrative proceeding permitted under the Act and these regulations shall be commenced upon receipt of a timely request for hearing filed in accordance with § 801.53 of this part.
