16 U.S.C. § 1057 — Preparation of annual report by Director
Citation: us:usc:16:1057
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1057
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 20 (National Fisheries Center And Aquarium). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the National Fisheries Center and Aquarium shall prepare for the Advisory Board an annual report for presentation to the Secretary of the Interior and to the Congress.
