42 C.F.R. § 137.49 — What if a Self-Governance Tribe requests such incorporation at the negotiation stage of a compact or funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.49
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

In that event, such incorporation shall be deemed effective immediately and shall control the negotiation and resulting compact and funding agreement.
