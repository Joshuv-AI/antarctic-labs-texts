14 C.F.R. § 375.25 — Unauthorized operations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:375.25
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-375.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 375 (Navigation Of Foreign Civil Aircraft Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No foreign civil aircraft shall be navigated in the United States unless authorized by this part. Commercial air operations (other than those authorized by § 375.36) shall not be undertaken without a permit issued by the Department.
