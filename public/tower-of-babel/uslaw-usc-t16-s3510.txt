16 U.S.C. § 3510 — Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this chapter—
(1)
$2,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2006 through 2010; and
(2)
$1,962,000 for each of fiscal years 2025 through 2031.
