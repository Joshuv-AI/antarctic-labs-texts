34 C.F.R. § 30.31 — How does the Secretary apply funds recovered by offset if multiple debts are involved?
Citation: us:cfr:34:30.31
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-30.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 30 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary collects more than one debt of a debtor by administrative offset, the Secretary applies the recovered funds to satisfy those debts based on the Secretary's determination of the best interests of the United States, determined by the facts and circumstances of the particular case.
