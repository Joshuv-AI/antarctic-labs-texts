15 U.S.C. § 2692 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:2692
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter IV (Lead Exposure Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the purposes of this subchapter such sums as may be necessary.
