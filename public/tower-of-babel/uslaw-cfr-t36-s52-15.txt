36 C.F.R. § 52.15 — When will the Director reject a response?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.15
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director will reject any response if the Director makes any of the following determinations:
(a) The respondent is not a qualified entity.
(b) The response is not responsive to the requirements in the request for proposals. A response is not responsive if the Director determines that it is not timely, does not meet the minimum requirements of the proposed contract, or does not provide the information required by the request for proposals.
