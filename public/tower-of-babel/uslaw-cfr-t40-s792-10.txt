40 C.F.R. § 792.10 — Applicability to studies performed under grants and contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:40:792.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-792.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 792 (Good Laboratory Practice Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a sponsor or other person utilizes the services of a consulting laboratory, contractor, or grantee to perform all or a part of a study to which this part applies, it shall notify the consulting laboratory, contractor, or grantee that the service is, or is part of, a study that must be conducted in compliance with the provisions of this part.
