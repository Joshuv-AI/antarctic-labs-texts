48 C.F.R. § 616.000 — 616.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:616.000
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-616.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 616 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may use any of the contract types described in FAR part 16 for acquisitions made under simplified acquisition procedures. The contracting officer shall document his/her decision to use a contract type in accordance with the requirements of FAR part 16.
