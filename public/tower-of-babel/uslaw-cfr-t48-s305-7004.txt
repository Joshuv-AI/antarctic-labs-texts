48 C.F.R. § 305.7004 — 305.7004 Publicizing postaward.
Citation: us:cfr:48:305.7004
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-305.7004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 305 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notices of contract actions exceeding $25,000, funded in whole or in part by the PPHF, shall be identified on HHS' PPHF Web site at http://www.hhs.gov/open/prevention/index.html no later than 5 days after the contract action occurs.
