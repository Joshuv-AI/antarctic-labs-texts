24 C.F.R. § 35.610 — Exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:24:35.610
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-35.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 35 (Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention In Certain Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for insurance in connection with a refinancing transaction where an appraisal is not required under the applicable procedures established by HUD is excluded from the coverage of this subpart.
