41 C.F.R. § 304-3.13 — -3.13 Reporting requirements for non-Federal source payments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.13
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must provide their agency with information about payments received on the agency's behalf. The agency must submit to the U.S. Office of Government Ethics (OGE) a semiannual report (OGE Form 1353 or SF 326) of all payments it accepts under this part.
