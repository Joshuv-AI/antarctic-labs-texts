43 C.F.R. § 44.51 — Are there general procedures applicable to all PILT payments?
Citation: us:cfr:43:44.51
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-44.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 44 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The minimum payment that the Department will disburse to any local government is $100.00 (one hundred dollars).
(b) If Congress appropriates insufficient monies to provide full payment to each local government during any fiscal year, the Department will reduce proportionally all payments in that fiscal year.
