38 U.S.C. § 1304 — Special provisions relating to surviving spouses
Citation: us:usc:38:1304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1304
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 13 (Dependency And Indemnity Compensation For Service-Connected Deaths); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No dependency and indemnity compensation shall be paid to the surviving spouse of a veteran dying after December 31, 1956, unless such surviving spouse was married to such veteran—
(1)
before the expiration of fifteen years after the termination of the period of service in which the injury or disease causing the death of the veteran was incurred or aggravated; or
(2)
for one year or more; or
(3)
for any period of time if a child was born of the marriage, or was born to them before the marriage.
