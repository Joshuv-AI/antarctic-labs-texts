42 C.F.R. § 424.102 — Situations that do not constitute an emergency.
Citation: us:cfr:42:424.102
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-424.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 424 (Conditions For Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Without additional evidence of a threat to life or health, the following situations do not in themselves indicate a need for emergency services:
(a) Lack of care at home.
(b) Lack of transportation to a participating hospital.
(c) Death of the patient in the hospital.
