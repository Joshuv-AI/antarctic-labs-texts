24 C.F.R. § 30.25 — Violations by applicants for assistance.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 30 (Civil Money Penalties: Certain Prohibited Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The General Counsel, or his or her designee, may initiate a civil money penalty action against applicants for assistance, as defined in 24 CFR part 4, subpart A, who knowingly and materially violate the provisions of subsections (b) or (c) of section 102 of the HUD Reform Act of 1989 (42 U.S.C. 3545).
(b) Maximum penalty. The maximum penalty is $25,132 for each violation.
