41 C.F.R. § 301-73.100 — -73.100 Travel payment system and obtaining services.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-73.100
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-73.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-73 (73—Travel Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A travel payment system facilitates the payment of official travel and transportation expenses. Agencies must participate in GSA's travel payment system services program unless the agency is not a mandatory user of GSA's charge card program.
