22 U.S.C. § 282 — Acceptance of membership by United States in International Finance Corporation
Citation: us:usc:22:282
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section282
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XI (International Finance Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is hereby authorized to accept membership for the United States in the International Finance Corporation (hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”), provided for by the Articles of Agreement of the Corporation deposited in the archives of the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
