42 U.S.C. § 18120 — Application
Citation: us:usc:42:18120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18120
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 157 (Quality, Affordable Health Care For All Americans); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act, nothing in such Act (or an amendment made by such Act) shall be construed to—
(1)
prohibit (or authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to promulgate regulations that prohibit) a group health plan or health insurance issuer from carrying out utilization management techniques that are commonly used as of March 23, 2010; or
(2)
restrict the application of the amendments made by this subtitle.
