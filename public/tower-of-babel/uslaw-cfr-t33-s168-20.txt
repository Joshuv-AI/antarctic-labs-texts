33 C.F.R. § 168.20 — Applicable vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:168.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-168.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 168 (Escort Requirements For Certain Tankers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this part apply to the following laden tankers of 5,000 gross tons or more:
(a) All single hull tankers on the waters listed in § 168.40(a) and (b); and
(b) All double hull tankers on the waters listed in § 168.40(a).
