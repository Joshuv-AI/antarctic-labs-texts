20 U.S.C. § 1704 — Balance not required
Citation: us:usc:20:1704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1704
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter I (Equal Educational Opportunities); Part 2 (Unlawful Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The failure of an educational agency to attain a balance, on the basis of race, color, sex, or national origin, of students among its schools shall not constitute a denial of equal educational opportunity, or equal protection of the laws.
