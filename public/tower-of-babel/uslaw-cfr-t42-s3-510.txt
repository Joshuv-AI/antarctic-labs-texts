42 C.F.R. § 3.510 — Ex parte contacts.
Citation: us:cfr:42:3.510
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-3.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 3 (Patient Safety Organizations And Patient Safety Work Product). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No party or person (except employees of the ALJ's office) may communicate in any way with the ALJ on any matter at issue in a case, unless on notice and opportunity for both parties to participate. This provision does not prohibit a party or person from inquiring about the status of a case or asking routine questions concerning administrative functions or procedures.
