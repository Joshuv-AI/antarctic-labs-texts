42 U.S.C. § 6501 — “Petroleum” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:6501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6501
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 78 (National Petroleum Reserve In Alaska). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “petroleum” includes crude oil, gases (including natural gas), natural gasoline, and other related hydrocarbons, oil shale, and the products of any of such resources.
