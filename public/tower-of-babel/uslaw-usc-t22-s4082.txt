22 U.S.C. § 4082 — Loan of household effects
Citation: us:usc:22:4082
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4082
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter IX (Travel, Leave, And Other Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may, as a means of eliminating transportation costs, provide members of the Service with basic household furnishing and equipment for use on a loan basis in personally owned or leased residences.
