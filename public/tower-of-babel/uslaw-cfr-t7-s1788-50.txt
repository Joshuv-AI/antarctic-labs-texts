7 C.F.R. § 1788.50 — Acceptable sureties.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1788.50
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1788.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1788 (Rus Fidelity And Insurance Requirements For Electric And Telecommunications Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Surety companies providing contractors' bonds shall be listed as acceptable sureties in the U.S. Department of Treasury Circular No. 570. The circular is maintained through periodic publication in the Federal Register and is available on the Internet under ftp://ftp.fedworld.gov/pub/tel/sureties.txt, and on the Department of the Treasury's computer bulletin board at 202-874-6817.
