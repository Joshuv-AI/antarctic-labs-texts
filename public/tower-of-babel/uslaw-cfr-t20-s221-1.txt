20 C.F.R. § 221.1 — Introduction.
Citation: us:cfr:20:221.1
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-221.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 221 (Jurisdiction Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part explains the factors involved in deciding whether the Social Security Administration or the Railroad Retirement Board will pay benefits to a railroad employee, and his or her eligible family members, both before and after the employee's death. The agency that has jurisdiction over the payment of benefits also has jurisdiction of the applicant's Medicare coverage as described in section 7(d) of the Railroad Retirement Act. The Board is responsible for making this decision.
