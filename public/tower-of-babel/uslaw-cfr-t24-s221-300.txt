24 C.F.R. § 221.300 — Changes in the plan of apartment ownership.
Citation: us:cfr:24:221.300
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-221.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 221 (Low Cost And Moderate Income Mortgage Insurance—Savings Clause). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mortgagee shall notify the Commissioner of any changes in the plan of apartment ownership and in the administration of the property. Such notification shall be given either at the time of the conveyance of the property or at the time of the assignment of the mortgage. Any changes in such plan shall require approval by the Commissioner.
