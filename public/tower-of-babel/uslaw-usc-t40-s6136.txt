40 U.S.C. § 6136 — Suspension of prohibitions against use of Supreme Court grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:6136
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6136
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 61 (United States Supreme Court Building And Grounds); Subchapter IV (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To allow the observance of authorized ceremonies in the Supreme Court Building and grounds, the Marshal of the Supreme Court may suspend for those occasions any of the prohibitions contained in this subchapter as may be necessary for the occasion if—
(1)
responsible officers have been appointed; and
(2)
the Marshal determines that adequate arrangements have been made—
(A)
to maintain suitable order and decorum in the proceedings; and
(B)
to protect the Supreme Court Building and grounds and individuals and property in the Building and grounds.
