42 U.S.C. § 11803 — Approval of applications
Citation: us:usc:42:11803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11803
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 123 (Drug Abuse Education And Prevention); Subchapter I (Drug Education And Prevention Relating To Youth Gangs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In selecting among applications submitted under section 11802(a) of this title, the Secretary shall give priority to applicants who propose to carry out projects and activities—
(1)
for the purposes specified in section 11801 of this title in geographical areas in which frequent and severe drug-related crimes are committed by gangs whose membership is composed primarily of youth, and
(2)
that the applicant demonstrates have the broad support of community based organizations in such geographical areas.
