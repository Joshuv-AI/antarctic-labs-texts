12 U.S.C. § 2807 — Report
Citation: us:usc:12:2807
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2807
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 29 (Home Mortgage Disclosure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bureau, in consultation with the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development, shall report annually to the Congress on the utility of the requirements of section 2803(b)(4) of this title.
