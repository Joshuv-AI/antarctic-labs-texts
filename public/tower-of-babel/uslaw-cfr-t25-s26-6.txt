25 C.F.R. § 26.6 — Who is eligible to receive financial assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Financial assistance is only available to persons:
(a) Approved for training that will lead to permanent, gainful and meaningful employment; or
(b) Who have obtained a job and need financial assistance to retain the job, as determined by the servicing office.
