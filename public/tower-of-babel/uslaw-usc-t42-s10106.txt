42 U.S.C. § 10106 — Protection of classified national security information
Citation: us:usc:42:10106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10106
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall require the release or disclosure to any person or to the Commission of any classified national security information.
