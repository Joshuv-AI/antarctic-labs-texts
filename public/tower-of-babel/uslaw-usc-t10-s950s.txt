10 U.S.C. § 950s — Conviction of lesser offenses
Citation: us:usc:10:950s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section950s
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter VIII (Punitive Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An accused may be found guilty of an offense necessarily included in the offense charged or of an attempt to commit either the offense charged or an attempt to commit either the offense charged or an offense necessarily included therein.
