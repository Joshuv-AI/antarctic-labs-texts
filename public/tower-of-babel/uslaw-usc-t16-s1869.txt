16 U.S.C. § 1869 — Restoration study

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1869
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 38 (Fishery Conservation And Management); Subchapter IV (National Fishery Management Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary may conduct a study to update scientific information and protocols needed to improve restoration techniques for a variety of coast habitat types and synthesize the results in a format easily understandable by restoration practitioners and local communities.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There are authorized to be appropriated $500,000 for fiscal year 2007 to conduct this study.
