36 U.S.C. § 40507 — Principal office
Citation: us:usc:36:40507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section40507
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 405 (Congressional Medal Of Honor Society Of The United States Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the corporation shall be in the District of Columbia or another place decided by the board of directors. However, the activities of the corporation are not confined to the place where the principal office is located but may be conducted throughout the States, territories, and possessions of the United States.
