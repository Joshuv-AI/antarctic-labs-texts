26 U.S.C. § 176 — Payments with respect to employees of certain foreign corporations
Citation: us:usc:26:176
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section176
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part VI (Itemized Deductions For Individuals And Corporations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a domestic corporation, there shall be allowed as a deduction amounts (to the extent not compensated for) paid or incurred pursuant to an agreement entered into under section 3121(l) with respect to services performed by United States citizens employed by foreign subsidiary corporations. Any reimbursement of any amount previously allowed as a deduction under this section shall be included in gross income for the taxable year in which received.
