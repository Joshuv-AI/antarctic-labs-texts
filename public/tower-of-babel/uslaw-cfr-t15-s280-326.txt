15 C.F.R. § 280.326 — Records and files of the United States Patent and Trademark Office.
Citation: us:cfr:15:280.326
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-280.326
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 280 (Fastener Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The records relating to fastener insignia shall be open to public inspection. Copies of any such records may be obtained upon request and payment of the fee set by the Director, USPTO.
