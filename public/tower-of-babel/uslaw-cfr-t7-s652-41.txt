7 C.F.R. § 652.41 — Effect of filing deadlines.
Citation: us:cfr:7:652.41
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-652.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 652 (Technical Service Provider Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A technical service provider's failure to meet the filing deadlines under this subpart will result in the forfeiture of appeal rights. All filings must be received by NRCS no later than the close of business (5 p.m.) the last day of the filing period.
