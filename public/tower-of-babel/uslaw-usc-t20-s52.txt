20 U.S.C. § 52 — Evidence of title to site and buildings
Citation: us:usc:20:52
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section52
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter I (Charter Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The site and lands selected for buildings for the Smithsonian Institution shall be deemed appropriated to the institution, and the record of the description of such site and lands, or a copy thereof, certified by the chancellor and Secretary of the Board of Regents, shall be received as evidence in all courts of the extent and boundaries of the lands appropriated to the institution.
