24 C.F.R. § 291.10 — General policy regarding rental of acquired property.
Citation: us:cfr:24:291.10
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-291.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 291 (Disposition Of Hud-Acquired And -Owned Single Family Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD will lease acquired property to comply with other designated HUD programs, or when the Secretary determines that it is in the interest of HUD. Leases may include an option to purchase in appropriate circumstances.
