45 C.F.R. § 2556.700 — Who is covered by this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.700
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All VISTAs, including leaders and summer associates, are subject to this subpart.
(b) All employees of VISTA sponsors and subrecipients whose salaries or other compensation are paid, in whole or in part, with VISTA grant assistance are subject to this subpart.
(c) All VISTA sponsors and subrecipients are subject to this subpart.
