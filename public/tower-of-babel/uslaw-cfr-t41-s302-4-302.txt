41 C.F.R. § 302-4.302 — -4.302 Method for mileage reimbursement when POV use is authorized.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-4.302
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-4.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-4 (4—Allowances For Subsistence And Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For relocations within CONUS involving POV usage, the agency will reimburse mileage at the standard mileage rate specified in § 302-4.300. For an OCONUS relocation involving POV usage, the agency may allow reimbursement of certain actual expenses of using the POV (i.e., fuel plus the additional expenses listed in § 301-10.302 of this subtitle).
