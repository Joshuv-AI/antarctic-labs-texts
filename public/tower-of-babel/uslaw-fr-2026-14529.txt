DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army, Corps of Engineers


Notice of Mississippi River Commission Public Meetings for Fall 
2026

AGENCY: Corps of Engineers, Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Mississippi River Commission will hold its fall of 2026 
meetings at the below locations, dates, and times.

DATES: August 17, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., Caruthersville, 
Missouri; August 18, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., Memphis, Tennessee; 
August 19, 2026, 2:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., Vicksburg, MS; August 21, 
2026, 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., Morgan City, LA. Locations for the 
public meetings will take place on the Motor Vessel Mississippi. 
Additional details for the public meetings are

[[Page 45268]]

included in the Supplementary Information section of this notice.

ADDRESSES: The physical address for the Mississippi River Commission is 
3909 Halls Ferry Road, B1006, Vicksburg, Mississippi 39180.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Jacob Brister, Executive Director, 
Mississippi River Commission at 601-634-7023, or Ms. Edie Whittington, 
Mississippi River Commission, Administrative Officer at 601-634-5768 or 
email `[email protected]'. Also see: https://www.mvd.usace.army.mil/About/Mississippi-River-Commission-MRC/Public-Meeting-Schedule/.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The public hearings are held in accordance 
with Section 8 of the 1928 Flood Control Act.
    Status of all meetings below: Open to the public.
    Matters To Be Considered: (1) Summary report by the Commission on 
national and regional issues affecting the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers 
and Commission programs and projects on the Mississippi River and its 
tributaries; (2) District Commander's overview for the Commission on 
current project issues in the respective area; and (3) Presentations to 
the Commission by local organizations and members of the public giving 
views or comments on any issue affecting the programs or projects of 
the Commission and the Corps of Engineers.
    Time and Date: 9:00 a.m., August 17, 2026.
    Place: On board the Motor Vessel MISSISSIPPI at Caruthersville, 
Missouri--City Front.
    Time and Date: 9:00 a.m., August 18, 2026.
    Place: On board the Motor Vessel MISSISSIPPI at Memphis, 
Tennessee--Mud Island River Park Landing.
    Time and Date: 2:00 p.m., August 19, 2026.
    Place: On board the Motor Vessel MISSISSIPPI at Vicksburg, 
Mississippi--City Front.
    Time and Date: 9:00 a.m., August 21, 2026.
    Place: On board the Motor Vessel MISSISSIPPI at Morgan City, 
Louisiana--Port of Morgan City.

Garrett Dean,
Lieutenant Colonel, EN, Commanding.
