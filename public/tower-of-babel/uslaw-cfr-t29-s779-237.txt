29 C.F.R. § 779.237 — Enterprise engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce.
Citation: us:cfr:29:779.237
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-779.237
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 779 (The Fair Labor Standards Act As Applied To Retailers Of Goods Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under section 3(s) the “enterprise” to be covered must be an “enterprise engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce.” This is defined in section 3(s) as follows:
(a) Engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce, including
(b) Employees handling, selling or otherwise working on goods that have been moved in or produced for commerce by any person.
