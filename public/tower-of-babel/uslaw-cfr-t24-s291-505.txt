24 C.F.R. § 291.505 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:291.505
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-291.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 291 (Disposition Of Hud-Acquired And -Owned Single Family Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart:
Locality means the community, neighborhood, or jurisdiction of the unit of general local government, or Indian tribal government;
Unit of general local government means a county or parish, city, town, township, or other political subdivision of a State.
