5 C.F.R. § 930.110 — Identification of authorized operators and incidental operators.
Citation: us:cfr:5:930.110
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-930.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 930 (Programs For Specific Positions And Examinations (Miscellaneous)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must have procedures to identify employees who are authorized to operate Government-owned or -leased motor vehicles. Such procedures must provide for adequate control of access to vehicles and assure that the other requirements of this subpart are met.
