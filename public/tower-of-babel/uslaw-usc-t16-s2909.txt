16 U.S.C. § 2909 — Disclaimers

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 49 (Fish And Wildlife Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as affecting—
(1)
the authority, jurisdiction, or responsibility of the States to manage, control, or regulate fish and resident wildlife under State law;
(2)
any requirement under State law that lands, waters, and interests therein may only be acquired for conservation purposes if the owner thereof is a willing seller; and
(3)
the authority of the Secretary of Agriculture under the Act of March 2, 1931 (46 Stat. 1468–1469, 7 U.S.C. 426–426b).1
