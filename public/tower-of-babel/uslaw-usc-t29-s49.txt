29 U.S.C. § 49 — United States Employment Service established
Citation: us:usc:29:49
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section49
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 4B (Federal Employment Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to promote the establishment and maintenance of a national system of public employment service offices, the United States Employment Service shall be established and maintained within the Department of Labor.
