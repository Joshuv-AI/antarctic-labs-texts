33 U.S.C. § 1420 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:33:1420
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1420
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 27 (Ocean Dumping); Subchapter I (Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated, for purposes of carrying out this subchapter, not to exceed $12,000,000 for fiscal year 1993 and not to exceed $14,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1994, 1995, 1996, and 1997, to remain available until expended.
