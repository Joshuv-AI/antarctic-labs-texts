20 C.F.R. § 209.3 — Social security number required.
Citation: us:cfr:20:209.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-209.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 209 (Railroad Employers' Reports And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employer shall furnish to the Board a social security number for each employee for whom any report is submitted to the Board. Employers are encouraged to validate any social security number provided under this section.
