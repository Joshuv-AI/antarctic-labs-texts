48 C.F.R. § 1207.305 — 1207.305 Solicitation provisions and contract clause.

Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1207 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may insert clause 1252.237-73, Key Personnel, in solicitations and contracts when the acquisition is conducted pursuant to OMB Circular A-76 and meets the clause prescription requirements at 1237.110-70(b).
