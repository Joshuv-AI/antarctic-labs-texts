49 C.F.R. § 611.201 — New Starts eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:49:611.201
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-611.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 611 (Major Capital Investment Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To be eligible for an engineering grant under this part for a new fixed guideway or an extension to a fixed guideway, a project must:
(1) Be a New Starts project as defined in § 611.105; and
(2) Be approved into engineering by FTA.
(b) To be eligible for a construction grant under 49 U.S.C. 5309 for a new fixed guideway or extension to a fixed guideway, a project must:
(1) Be a New Starts project as defined in § 611.105;
(2) Have completed engineering;
(3) Receive a “medium” or better rating on project justification pursuant to § 611.203;
(4) Receive a “medium” or better rating on local financial commitment pursuant to § 611.205; and
(5) Meet the other requirements of 49 U.S.C. 5309.
