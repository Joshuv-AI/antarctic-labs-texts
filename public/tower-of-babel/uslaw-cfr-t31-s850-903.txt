31 C.F.R. § 850.903 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:31:850.903
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-850.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 850 (Provisions Pertaining To U.S. Investments In Certain National Security Technologies And Products In Countries Of Concern). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any of the provisions of this part, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held to be invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or application of such provisions to other persons or circumstances that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.
