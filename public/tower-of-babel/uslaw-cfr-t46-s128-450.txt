46 C.F.R. § 128.450 — Liquid-mud systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:128.450
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-128.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 128 (Marine Engineering: Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Liquid-mud piping systems may use resiliently seated valves of Category A to comply with §§ 56.20-15 and 56.50-60 of this chapter.
(b) Tanks for oil-based liquid mud must be fitted with tank vents equipped with flame screens. Vents must not discharge to the interior of the vessel.
