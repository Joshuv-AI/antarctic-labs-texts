45 C.F.R. § 162.100 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:162.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 162 (Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Covered entities (as defined in § 160.103 of this subchapter) must comply with the applicable requirements of this part.
