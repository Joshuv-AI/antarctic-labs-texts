20 C.F.R. § 217.25 — Who may cancel an application.
Citation: us:cfr:20:217.25
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-217.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 217 (Application For Annuity Or Lump Sum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application may be cancelled by the claimant or a person described in § 217.17. If the claimant is deceased, the person who is or could be eligible for any annuity accrual under part 234 of this chapter may cancel the application for the annuity.
