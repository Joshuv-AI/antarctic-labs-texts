7 C.F.R. § 782.19 — Penalty for noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:782.19
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-782.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 782 (End-Use Certificate Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be a violation of 18 U.S.C. 1001 for any entity to engage in fraud with respect to, or to knowingly violate, the provisions set forth in this part.
