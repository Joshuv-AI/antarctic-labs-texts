Abstract

Here we add a U(1) gauge theory to the simple EP exotic
invariant model in the paper E4. This paper E5 is the fifth in a
series of papers En.

1
Introduction

This paper is a preparation for the paper E6, which introduces
the Exotic Model, which is a combination of the Supersymmetric Standard Model with the
Exotic Invariant that couples to it. This paper describe the
same simple model as E4, but we add a U(1) gauge theory here,
and note the necessary changes.

The Exotic Invariant for the U(1) Case

Now we are ready to add the exotic invariant here. It is
essentially the same as we found in E4, except that the terms
have new pieces in them:

{\cal A}_{\rm X,U(1)}={\cal A}_{\rm X,E,U(1)}-{\cal A}_{\rm X,P,U(1)}

(29)

The mechanism that solves the constraint equation here is the
same as it was in E4.

The first term in (
29
)

{\cal A}_{\rm X,E,U(1)}=\int d^{4}x\left\{\phi^{\dot{\alpha}}\left[\left(b_{1}E\Gamma_{E}+b_{2}\psi_{E}^{\beta}Y_{E\beta}+b_{3}\Lambda_{E}F_{E}\right){\overline{C}}_{\dot{\alpha}}\right.\right.

(30)

\left.\left.+b_{4}\psi_{E}^{\alpha}\overline{D}_{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}{\overline{E}}+b_{4}g_{1}\overline{\lambda}_{\dot{\alpha}}E{\overline{E}}+b_{5}F_{E}{\overline{\psi}}_{E\dot{\alpha}}\right]\right.

\left.+W^{\dot{\alpha}\beta}\left[\left(b_{6}EY_{E\beta}+b_{7}\psi_{E\beta}\Lambda_{E}\right){\overline{C}}_{\dot{\alpha}}+b_{8}\psi_{E\beta}{\overline{\psi}}_{E\dot{\alpha}}+\right.\right.

(32)

\left.\left.b_{9}{E}\overline{D}_{\beta\dot{\alpha}}{\overline{E}}\right]+\chi^{\dot{\alpha}}\left[b_{10}E\Lambda_{E}{\overline{C}}_{\dot{\alpha}}+b_{11}E{\overline{\psi}}_{E\dot{\alpha}}\right]\right\}

important new terms in the exotic invariants.

6
Conclusion

The three papers E3, E4, E5 briefly summarize the basic facts
about the Exotic Invariants, which were introduced informally in E1, and
then deduced using the spectral sequence in E2 . Let us review
the results once more here:

1.

The full Exotic Invariant in (
29
) contains more than one Exotic Invariant type expression, of the form (
30
)–(
33
),
and they are subtracted, so that their variations cancel each
other as shown in (
36
).

2.

There are a few new terms when we add a gauge interaction with a
gauge theory, but that does not change the basic features of the
Exotic Invariant very much.

3.

It is easy to see that the results here generalize to
non-Abelian gauge groups in the usual way familiar in SUSY
theories.
We need to use covariant derivatives
\overline{D}_{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}{\overline{E}}

and there are new terms like
b_{4}g_{1}\overline{\lambda}_{\dot{\alpha}}E{\overline{E}}
, as we saw in (
30
). These new terms are very
important, for mass splitting, as we will see in E6, when we
look at the H and K fields and their Exotic Invariants.

4.

We need to add Completion Terms as in section
(
3
) to get the action including the Exotic Invariants to
satisfy the original Master Equation. This is not changed by the presence of
gauge couplings, because these terms have no derivatives.

These exotic invariants are now going to be used in E6 to modify the Supersymmetric Standard Model.
The sum of the special Exotic Invariant and the SSM we will call the XM. It
has a natural gauge symmetry breaking from
\rm SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)
to
\rm SU(3)\times U(1)
. But, as
usual, there is no spontaneous breaking of supersymmetry
included in that gauge symmetry breaking, so the supersymmetry
remains after gauge symmetry breaking. The supermultiplets are
still there.

However the XM contains a number of new and peculiar terms in
the Exotic Invariants that contain the H and K fields. In E7 we will deduce
the form of the quadratic ZX action which arises in the XM when
the gauge symmetry breaks from
\rm SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)
to
\rm SU(3)\times U(1)
by getting VEVs for
the H and K multiplets. That quadratic ZX action gets mixed with
the Higgs and Photon multiplets by some counterterms that are
needed at one loop as will be shown in E9. Then in E10 we will
show that a computer program confirms the results shown here and
in E6. The remaining unknown, which is very important, and which
has not yet been completed, is to find out whether the tachyons
in these theories can be eliminated by a choice of the
coefficients like the
a_{i}
that we used in front of the terms
for the action for the paper E4. This possiblity is suggested by
the results of E7 and E8. That question should also be resolved
in E10.
