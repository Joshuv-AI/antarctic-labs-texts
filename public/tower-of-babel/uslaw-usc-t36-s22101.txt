Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 221 (The American Society Of International Law). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Federal Charter.—
The American Society of International Law (in this chapter, the “corporation”) is a federally chartered corporation.
(b) Perpetual Existence.—
Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.
