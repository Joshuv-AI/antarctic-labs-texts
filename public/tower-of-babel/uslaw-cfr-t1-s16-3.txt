1 C.F.R. § 16.3 — Certifying duties.
Citation: us:cfr:1:16.3
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-16.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 16 (Agency Representatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency certifying officer is responsible for attaching the required number of true copies of each original document submitted by the agency to the Office of the Federal Register and for making the certification required by §§ 18.5 and 18.6 of this chapter.
