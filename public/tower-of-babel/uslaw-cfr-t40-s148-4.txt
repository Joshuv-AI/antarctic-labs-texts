40 C.F.R. § 148.4 — Procedures for case-by-case extensions to an effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:40:148.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-148.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 148 (Hazardous Waste Injection Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator of a Class I hazardous waste injection well may submit an application to the Administrator for an extension of the effective date of any applicable prohibition established under subpart B of this part according to the procedures of § 268.5.
