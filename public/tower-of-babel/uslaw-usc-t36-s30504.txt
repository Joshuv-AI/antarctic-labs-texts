36 U.S.C. § 30504 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:30504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section30504
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 305 (Blue Star Mothers Of America, Inc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual is eligible for membership in the corporation if—
(1)
she is a mother (meaning a woman who filled the role of birthmother, adoptive mother, step-mother, foster-mother, grandmother, or legal guardian) of a person who—
(A)
is serving in the Armed Forces; or
(B)
has served in, or has been honorably discharged from, the Armed Forces; and
(2)
she is living in the United States or is a citizen of the United States living outside the United States.
