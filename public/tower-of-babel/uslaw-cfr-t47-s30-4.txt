(f) 37-38.6 GHz band: This band is divided into the Lower 37 GHz band and the Upper 37 GHz band. Lower 37 GHz band channels: 37,000-37,100 MHz, 37,100-37,200 MHz, 37,200-37,300 MHz, 37, 300-37,400 MHz, 37,400-37,500 MHz, 37,500-37,600 MHz.
