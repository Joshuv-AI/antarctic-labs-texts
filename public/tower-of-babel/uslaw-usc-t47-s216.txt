47 U.S.C. § 216 — Receivers and trustees; application of chapter
Citation: us:usc:47:216
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section216
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter II (Common Carriers); Part I (Common Carrier Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to all receivers and operating trustees of carriers subject to this chapter to the same extent that it applies to carriers.
