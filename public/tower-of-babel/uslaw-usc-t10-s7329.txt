10 U.S.C. § 7329 — Computation of retired pay: law applicable
Citation: us:usc:10:7329
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7329
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 741 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of the Army retired under this chapter is entitled to retired pay computed under chapter 745 of this title.
