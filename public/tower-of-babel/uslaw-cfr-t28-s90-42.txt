28 C.F.R. § 90.42 — Monitoring and audit.
Citation: us:cfr:28:90.42
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-90.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 90 (Violence Against Women). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribes receiving reimbursement of expenses under the Tribal Reimbursement Program will be subject to regular monitoring and audits to ensure that expenses are properly documented and are allocable to the exercise of STCJ.
