16 U.S.C. § 610 — Permits to cut and remove timber; citizens of Washington County and Kane County, Utah
Citation: us:usc:16:610
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section610
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be lawful for the Secretary of the Interior to grant permits under the provisions of section 607 of this title, to citizens of Washington County and of Kane County, Utah, to cut timber on the public lands of the counties of Mohave and Coconino, Arizona, for agricultural, mining, or other domestic purposes, and remove the timber so cut to said Washington County and Kane County, Utah.
