33 C.F.R. § 142.36 — Protective clothing.
Citation: us:cfr:33:142.36
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-142.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 142 (Workplace Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Personnel in areas where there are flying particles, molten metal, radiant energy, heavy dust, or hazardous materials shall wear clothing and gloves providing protection against the hazard involved.
