26 C.F.R. § 56.6011-1 — -1 General requirement of return, statement, or list.
Citation: us:cfr:26:56.6011-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-56.6011-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 56 (Public Charity Excise Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every organization liable for the tax imposed by section 4911(a) shall file an annual return with respect to the tax on the form prescribed by the Internal Revenue Service for that purpose and shall include the information required by the form and its instructions.
