25 C.F.R. § 20.101 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.101
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part govern the provision to eligible Indians of the following kinds of financial assistance and social services:
(a) Adult Care Assistance;
(b) Burial Assistance;
(c) Child Assistance;
(d) Disaster Assistance;
(e) Emergency Assistance;
(f) General Assistance;
(g) Services to Children, Elderly and Families; and
(h) Tribal Work Experience Program.
