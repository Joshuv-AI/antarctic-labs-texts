2 C.F.R. § 1401.310 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1401.310
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1401.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1401 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 1401.305 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
