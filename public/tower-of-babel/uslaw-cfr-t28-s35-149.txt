28 C.F.R. § 35.149 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:28:35.149
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-35.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In State And Local Government Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 35.150, no qualified individual with a disability shall, because a public entity's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with disabilities, be excluded from participation in, or be denied the benefits of the services, programs, or activities of a public entity, or be subjected to discrimination by any public entity.
