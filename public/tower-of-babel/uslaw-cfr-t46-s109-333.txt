46 C.F.R. § 109.333 — Fire main cutoff valves.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.333
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.333
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master or person in charge shall insure that each fire main cutoff valve is open and sealed to prevent closing, except that a cutoff valve may be closed to protect the portion of the fire main system on an exposed deck from freezing.
