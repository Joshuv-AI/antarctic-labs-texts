33 C.F.R. § 169.125 — What classes of ships are required to make reports?
Citation: us:cfr:33:169.125
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-169.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 169 (Ship Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each self-propelled ship of 300 gross tons or greater must participate in the reporting systems, except government ships exempted from reporting by regulation V/8-1(c) of SOLAS. However, exempt ships are encouraged to participate in the reporting systems.
