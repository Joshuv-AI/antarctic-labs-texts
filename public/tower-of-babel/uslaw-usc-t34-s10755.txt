34 U.S.C. § 10755 — Authorization of funding
Citation: us:usc:34:10755
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10755
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XLI (Crisis Stabilization And Community Reentry Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the availability of appropriations, for purposes of carrying out this subchapter, the Attorney General is authorized to award not more than $10,000,000 of funds appropriated to the Department of Justice for these purposes for each of fiscal years 2021 through 2025.
