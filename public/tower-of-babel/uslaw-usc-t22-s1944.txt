22 U.S.C. § 1944 — Reconstruction assistance in Chile; authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:1944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1944
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24 (Mutual Security Program); Subchapter III (Economic Assistance); Part D (Special Assistance And Other Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to the President not to exceed $100,000,000, which shall remain available until expended, for use, in addition to other funds available for such purposes, in the reconstruction and rehabilitation of Chile on such terms and conditions as the President may specify.
