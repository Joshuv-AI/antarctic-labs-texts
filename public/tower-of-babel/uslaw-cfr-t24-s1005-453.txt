24 C.F.R. § 1005.453 — Amortization provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1005.453
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1005.453
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1005 (Loan Guarantees For Indian Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The loan must contain complete Amortization provisions satisfactory to HUD, requiring payments due on the first day of each month by the Borrower. The sum of the principal and interest payments in each month shall be substantially the same.
