47 C.F.R. § 301.130 — Technical assistance to Dispute Resolution Boards.
Citation: us:cfr:47:301.130
Creator: National Telecommunications And Information Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-301.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 301 (Relocation Of And Spectrum Sharing By Federal Government Stations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of a Dispute Resolution Board convened pursuant to subpart C of this part, the Technical Panel shall provide the board with such technical assistance as requested.
