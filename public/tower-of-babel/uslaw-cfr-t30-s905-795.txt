30 C.F.R. § 905.795 — Small operator assistance program.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 905 (California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 795 of this chapter, Small Operator Assistance Program, shall apply to any person making application for assistance under the small operator assistance program.
