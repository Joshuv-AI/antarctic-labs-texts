12 C.F.R. § 404.36 — Procedure for demands for testimony or production of documents regarding confidential information.
Citation: us:cfr:12:404.36
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-404.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 404 (Information Disclosure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to compliance with the requirements of this subpart, demands to provide testimony and/or produce records that concern information protected by the Privacy Act, 5 U.S.C. 552a, or any other authority mandating confidentiality of certain classes of records or information, must also satisfy the requirements for disclosure imposed by such authority before records may be produced or testimony given.
