28 C.F.R. § 81.3 — Designation of Federal Bureau of Investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:28:81.3
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-81.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 81 (Child Abuse And Child Pornography Reporting Designations And Procedures, And Child Pornography Victims Reserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For federal lands, federally operated facilities, or federally contracted facilities where no agency qualifies for designation under § 81.2, the Federal Bureau of Investigation is hereby designated as the agency to receive and investigate reports of child abuse made pursuant to 42 U.S.C. 13031 until such time as another agency qualifies as a designated agency under § 81.2.
