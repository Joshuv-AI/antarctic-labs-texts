40 C.F.R. § 62.15935 — How do I maintain my operator qualification?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15935
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15935
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To maintain qualification, you must complete an annual review or refresher course covering, at a minimum, the five topics described in paragraphs (a) through (e) of this section:
(a) Update of regulations;
(b) Incinerator operation, including startup and shutdown procedures, sewage sludge feeding and ash handling;
(c) Inspection and maintenance;
(d) Prevention of malfunctions or conditions that may lead to malfunction; and
(e) Discussion of operating problems encountered by attendees.
