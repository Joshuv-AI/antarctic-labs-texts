16 U.S.C. § 5201 — Obstruction of a lawful hunt
Citation: us:usc:16:5201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5201
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 72 (Recreational Hunting Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is a violation of this section intentionally to engage in any physical conduct that significantly hinders a lawful hunt.
