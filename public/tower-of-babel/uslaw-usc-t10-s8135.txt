10 U.S.C. § 8135 — Regular Marine Corps: order of filling vacancies in grade of second lieutenant
Citation: us:usc:10:8135
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8135
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 815 (Original Appointments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Vacancies on the active-duty list of the Marine Corps in the grade of second lieutenant shall be filled, so far as practicable, first, from members of the graduating class of the Naval Academy; second, from meritorious noncommissioned officers of the Regular Marine Corps; and third, from other persons.
