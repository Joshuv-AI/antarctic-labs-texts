16 U.S.C. § 460ccc — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:460ccc
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460ccc
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXIV (Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter, the term—
(a)
“conservation area” means the Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area established pursuant to section 460ccc–1 of this title;
(b)
“public lands” has the meaning stated in section 1702(e) of title 43; and
(c)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
