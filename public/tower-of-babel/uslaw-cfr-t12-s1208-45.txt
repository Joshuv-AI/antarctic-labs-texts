12 C.F.R. § 1208.45 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1208.45
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1208.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1208 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FHFA shall refund promptly those amounts recovered by administrative offset but later found not to be owed to the Federal Government. Unless required by law or contract, such refunds shall not bear interest.
