49 C.F.R. § 1150.13 — Relevant dates.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1150.13
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1150.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1150 (Certificate To Construct, Acquire, Or Operate Railroad Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exact dates of the period of operation which have been agreed upon by the D-OP, the offeror of the rail service continuation payment, and the owner of the line to be operated, in their lease and operating agreements.
