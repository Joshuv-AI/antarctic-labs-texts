24 C.F.R. § 17.111 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.111
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will refund promptly to the appropriate individual amounts offset under these regulations when:
(a) A debt is waived or otherwise found not owing the United States (unless expressly prohibited by statute or regulation); or
(b) The Department is directed by an administrative or judicial order to refund amounts deducted from the employee's current pay.
