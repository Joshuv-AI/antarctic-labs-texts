43 C.F.R. § 4150.4 — Impoundment and disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4150.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4150.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4100 (Grazing Administration—Exclusive Of Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unauthorized livestock remaining on the public lands or other lands under Bureau of Land Management control, or both, after the date set forth in the notice and order to remove sent under § 4150.2 may be impounded and disposed of by the authorized officer as provided herein.
