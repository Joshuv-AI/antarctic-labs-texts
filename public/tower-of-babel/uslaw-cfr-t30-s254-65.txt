30 C.F.R. § 254.65 — Purpose.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 254 (Oil-Spill Response Requirements For Facilities Located Seaward Of The Coast Line). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart describes the additional requirements for preparing OSRPs and maintaining oil spill preparedness for facilities conducting exploratory drilling operations from a mobile offshore drilling unit (MODU) on the Arctic OCS.
