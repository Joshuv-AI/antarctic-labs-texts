14 C.F.R. § 271.6 — Profit element.
Citation: us:cfr:14:271.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-271.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 271 (Guidelines For Subsidizing Air Carriers Providing Essential Air Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reasonable return for a carrier for providing essential air service at an eligible place generally will be set at a flat percentage, typically 5 percent of that carrier's projected operating costs as established under § 271.4, plus any applicable interest expenses on flight equipment.
