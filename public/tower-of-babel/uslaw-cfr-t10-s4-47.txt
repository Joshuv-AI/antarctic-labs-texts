10 C.F.R. § 4.47 — Noncompliance with § 4.21.
Citation: us:cfr:10:4.47
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-4.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 4 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant fails or refuses to furnish an assurance required under § 4.21 or otherwise fails or refuses to comply with a requirement imposed by or pursuant to that section, Federal financial assistance may be refused in accordance with the procedures of § 4.48.
