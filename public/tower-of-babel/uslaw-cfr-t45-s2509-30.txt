45 C.F.R. § 2509.30 — No judicial review or enforceable rights.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2509.30
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2509.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2509 (Administrative Practice And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart is intended to improve the internal management of CNCS. As such, it is for the use of CNCS employees only and is not intended to, and does not create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable by law or in equity by any party against the United States, its agencies or other entities, its officers or employees, or any other person.
