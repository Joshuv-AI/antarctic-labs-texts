5 U.S.C. § 576 — Enforcement of arbitration agreements
Citation: us:usc:5:576
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section576
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 5 (Administrative Procedure); Subchapter IV (Alternative Means Of Dispute Resolution In The Administrative Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An agreement to arbitrate a matter to which this subchapter applies is enforceable pursuant to section 4 of title 9, and no action brought to enforce such an agreement shall be dismissed nor shall relief therein be denied on the grounds that it is against the United States or that the United States is an indispensable party.
