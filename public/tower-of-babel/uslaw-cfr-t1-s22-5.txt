1 C.F.R. § 22.5 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:1:22.5
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-22.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 22 (Preparation Of Notices And Proposed Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each proposed rule required by section 553 of title 5, United States Code, or any other statute, and any similar document voluntarily issued by an agency shall include a statement of—
(a) The time, place, and nature of public rulemaking proceedings; and
(b) Reference to the authority under which the regulatory action is proposed.
