2 C.F.R. § 2424.1160 — May a limited denial of participation be terminated before the term of the limited denial of participation expires?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2424.1160
Creator: Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2424.1160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2424 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the cause for the limited denial of participation is resolved before the expiration of the 12-month period, the official who imposed the sanction may terminate it.
