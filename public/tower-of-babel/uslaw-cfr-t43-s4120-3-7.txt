43 C.F.R. § 4120.3-7 — -7 Contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4120.3-7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4120.3-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4100 (Grazing Administration—Exclusive Of Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer may accept contributions of labor, material, equipment, or money for administration, protection, and improvement of the public lands necessary to achieve the objectives of this part.
