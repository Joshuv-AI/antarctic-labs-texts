16 C.F.R. § 1402.3 — Definitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1402 (Cb Base Station Antennas, Tv Antennas, And Supporting Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The definitions in section 3 of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2052) apply to this part 1402.
(b) Antenna supporting structures, CB base station antennas, and TV antennas are defined in § 1402.1(b)(1) through (3).
