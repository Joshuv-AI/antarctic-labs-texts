42 U.S.C. § 2991a — Congressional statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:2991a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2991a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 34 (Economic Opportunity Program); Subchapter VIII (Native American Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to promote the goal of economic and social self-sufficiency for American Indians, Native Hawaiians, other Native American Pacific Islanders (including American Samoan Natives), and Alaska Natives.
