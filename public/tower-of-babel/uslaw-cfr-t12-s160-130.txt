12 C.F.R. § 160.130 — Prohibition on loan procurement fees.
Citation: us:cfr:12:160.130
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-160.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 160 (Lending And Investment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are a director, officer, or other natural person having the power to direct the management or policies of a Federal savings association, you must not receive, directly or indirectly, any commission, fee, or other compensation in connection with the procurement of any loan made by the savings association or a subsidiary of the savings association.
