43 C.F.R. § 3933.20 — Filing fees.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3933.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3933.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3930 (Management Of Oil Shale Exploration And Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each application for assignment or sublease of record title or overriding royalty must include the filing fee found in the fee schedule in § 3000.12 of this chapter. The BLM will not accept any assignment that does not include the filing fee.
