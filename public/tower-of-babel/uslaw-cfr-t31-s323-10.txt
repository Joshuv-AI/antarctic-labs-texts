31 C.F.R. § 323.10 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:31:323.10
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-323.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 323 (Disclosure Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart are issued to implement section 122 of the SECURE 2.0 Act of 2022, 31 U.S.C. 3105(f). The requirements of 31 U.S.C. 3105(f) are additionally met through the publication of a new Routine Use in the applicable Fiscal Service System of Record Notice.
