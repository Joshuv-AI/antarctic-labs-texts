42 C.F.R. § 421.103 — Payment to providers.
Citation: us:cfr:42:421.103
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-421.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 421 (Medicare Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Providers are assigned to intermediaries in accordance with § 421.104. As the Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs) are implemented, providers are reassigned from intermediaries to MACs in accordance with § 412.404 of this chapter.
