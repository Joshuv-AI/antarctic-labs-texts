16 U.S.C. § 620g — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:620g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section620g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out sections 620 to 620j of this title.
