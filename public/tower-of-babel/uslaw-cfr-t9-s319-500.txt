9 C.F.R. § 319.500 — Meat pies.
Citation: us:cfr:9:319.500
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-319.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 319 (Definitions And Standards Of Identity Or Composition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Meat pies such as “Beef Pie,” “Veal Pie,” and “Pork Pie” shall contain meat of the species specified on the label, in an amount not less than 25 percent of all ingredients including crust and shall be computed on the basis of the fresh uncooked meat.
