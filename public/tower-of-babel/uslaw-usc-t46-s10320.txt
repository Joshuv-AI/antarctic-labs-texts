46 U.S.C. § 10320 — Records of seamen
Citation: us:usc:46:10320
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10320
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 103 (Foreign And Intercoastal Voyages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe regulations requiring vessel owners to maintain records of seamen on matters of engagement, discharge, and service. A vessel owner shall make these records available to the seaman and the Coast Guard on request.
