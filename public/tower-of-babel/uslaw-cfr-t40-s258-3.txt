40 C.F.R. § 258.3 — Consideration of other Federal laws.
Citation: us:cfr:40:258.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-258.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 258 (Criteria For Municipal Solid Waste Landfills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator of a municipal solid waste landfill unit must comply with any other applicable Federal rules, laws, regulations, or other requirements.
