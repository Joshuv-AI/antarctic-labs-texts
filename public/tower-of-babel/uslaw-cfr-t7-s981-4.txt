7 C.F.R. § 981.4 — Almonds and almond biomass.
Citation: us:cfr:7:981.4
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-981.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 981 (Almonds Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Almonds means (unless otherwise specified) all varieties of almonds (except bitter almonds), either shelled or unshelled, grown in the State of California, and, for the purposes of research includes almond biomass.
(b) Almond biomass means the hulls, shells, and skins of harvested almonds and woody biomass derived from almond trees (e.g., tree limbs, bark, prunings).
