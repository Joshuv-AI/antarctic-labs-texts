45 C.F.R. § 1355.50 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1355.50
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1355.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1355 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 1355.50 through 1355.59 contain the requirements a title IV-E agency must meet to receive Federal financial participation authorized under sections 474(a)(3)(C) and (D), and 474(c) of the Act for the planning, design, development, installation, operation, and maintenance of a comprehensive child welfare information system.
