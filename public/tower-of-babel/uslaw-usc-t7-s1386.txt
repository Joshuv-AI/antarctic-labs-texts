7 U.S.C. § 1386 — Exemption from laws prohibiting interest of Members of Congress in contracts
Citation: us:usc:7:1386
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1386
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part F (Miscellaneous Provisions And Appropriations); Subpart i (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 6306 of title 41 and sections 431 and 432 of title 18 shall not be applicable to loans or payments made under this chapter (except under section 1383(a) of this title).
