46 C.F.R. § 151.50-81 — -81 Special operating requirements for heat sensitive cargoes.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.50-81
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.50-81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When table 151.05 refers to this section, the following apply to the cargo:
(a) Must not be carried in a tank equipped with heating coils unless the heating supply to the coils is disconnected.
(b) Must not be carried in a tank adjacent to another tank containing an elevated temperature cargo.
(c) Must not be carried in a deck tank.
