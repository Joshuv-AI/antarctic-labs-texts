46 U.S.C. § 11504 — Enforcement of forfeitures
Citation: us:usc:46:11504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section11504
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 115 (Offenses And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When an offense by a seaman also is a criminal violation, it is not necessary that a criminal proceeding be brought to enforce a forfeiture.
