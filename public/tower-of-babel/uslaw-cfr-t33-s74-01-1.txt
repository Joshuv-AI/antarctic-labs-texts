33 C.F.R. § 74.01-1 — -1 Claim for damage, destruction, or displacement.
Citation: us:cfr:33:74.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-74.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 74 (Charges For Coast Guard Aids To Navigation Work). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever an aid to navigation is damaged, destroyed, or displaced from its station, a claim shall be made on behalf of the United States.
