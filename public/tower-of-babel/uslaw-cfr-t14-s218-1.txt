14 C.F.R. § 218.1 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:218.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-218.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 218 (Lease By Foreign Air Carrier Or Other Foreign Person Of Aircraft With Crew). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this part the term lease shall mean an agreement under which an aircraft is furnished by one party to the agreement to the other party, irrespective of whether the agreement constitutes a true lease, charter arrangement, or some other arrangement.
