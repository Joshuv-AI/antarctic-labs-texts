16 C.F.R. § 1102.14 — Recall notices.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1102.14
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1102.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1102 (Publicly Available Consumer Product Safety Information Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All information presented in a voluntary or mandatory recall notice that has been made available to the public shall be accessible and searchable in the Database.
