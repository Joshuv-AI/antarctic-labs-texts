48 U.S.C. § 1396 — Duties and taxes covered into Virgin Islands treasury
Citation: us:usc:48:1396
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1396
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 7 (Virgin Islands); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The duties and taxes collected in pursuance of sections 1394 and 1395 of this title shall not be covered into the general fund of the Treasury of the United States, but shall be used and expended for the government and benefit of the Virgin Islands, under such rules and regulations as the President may prescribe.
