The impact of reform of medical insurance payment method on medical service pricing—based on empirical analysis of matched medical finance comprehensive data
Authors: Lele Li, Qiao Yu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundThe medical insurance payment method (MIPM) has an important impact on medical service pricing (MSP) in China. The objective of this study is to examine the impact of medical insurance payment method on medical service pricing with the hope of providing insights into appropriate MSP and resource allocation.MethodFrom the perspective of information asymmetry, using the matched medical finance comprehensive data of Chengdu City, our study empirically analyzed the effect of fixed payment by disease (FPD) on MSP by the difference in difference (DID) model.ResultsThe study found that FPD not only optimizes the allocation of medical resources, saves a large amount of medical insurance funds, but also reduces the personal burden of patients. FPD is adjusted by optimizing the structure of medical expenses and medical insurance compensation, which has certain effects on saving medical insurance funds, improving the quality of medical services, and reducing the burden on patients. It has achieved the goal of medical insurance payment method reform, and make the mechanism of MSP relatively more reasonable.ConclusionThis study provided policy implications for the government to implement multiple and compound medical insurance payment method and establish a more scientific and reasonable pricing mechanism for medical services.Keywords: Information asymmetry, Medical insurance payment method, Fixed payment by disease, Medical service pricing, Medical resources allocations

Introduction

Due to the information asymmetry in the field of medical services, the pricing of medical services has always been a difficult problem perplexing the whole world. On the one hand, under the condition of information asymmetry, the government price control fails, which distorts the market mechanism of MSP, resulting in unreasonable MSP which cannot accurately reflect the labor value of medical staff. On the other hand, doctors will use information advantages to seek rent in order to obtain more benefits, resulting in problems such as supply induced demand, excessive medical treatment and moral hazard. Neither of the government, patients nor other organizations are unable to obtain all effective medical service information, so it is difficult to reasonably price medical services. MSP has become the most critical factor affecting residents’ medical service demand [1–3]. Many countries regard the reform of medical insurance payment method as the key to solve the problem of difficult pricing of medical services. However, the existing studies mainly focus on the impact of medical insurance payment methods on medical expenses, and there are rare studies on the impact of medical insurance payment methods on MSP under the condition of information asymmetry. Therefore, this study focuses on the impact of medical insurance payment on MSP, and it has significant practical meaning for improving people’s health condition. Fixed payment by disease (FPD) refers to the independent accounting and total cost control of the entire diagnosis and treatment process for relatively independent diseases with standardized clinical pathways and no complications, achieving the highest limit price management of fixed payment, mainly including acute appendicitis, ureteral stones, acute mastitis, uterine fibroids, benign prostatic hyperplasia, ovarian benign tumors, internal medicine treatment of spontaneous pneumothorax, surgical treatment of spontaneous pneumothorax, idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura, and thrombotic external hemorrhoids.

Medical service market distinguishes itself from normal commodity market by its significant feature of information asymmetry [4, 5]. Feldstein believes that medical institutions (hospitals) will take advantage of information advantages to generate moral hazard to induce patients to consume more medical services, that is, supply induced demand [6]. The main causes lead to supply induced demand are that the medical insurance payment method cannot effectively restrict the behavior of doctors’ providing medical services or give reasonable pricing to medical services. Therefore, doctors, as the agent of patients, will use the information asymmetry between doctors and patients to seek rent and induce patients to consume medical services excessively [7]. Li & Yu make empirical analyze from the perspective of medical expense structure and medical insurance, and found that after payment by DRG, factors such as moral hazard, information asymmetry and medical insurance have a significant impact on the growth of medical expenses [8]. Li evaluated the reform of medical insurance payment method in Jinhua and Yuxi City by building a pilot evaluation framework of DRG. The study found that the reform of medical insurance payment method in Jinhua and Yuxi City has achieved remarkable results on the whole, while the clinical path management and the risk management of medical insurance fund should be strengthened [9]. Ding et al. made an empirical analysis on the data of DRG-PPS payment reform implemented by public hospitals in Beijing from 2011 to 2018, and found that there are exists problems in the reform of medical insurance payment, such as insignificant incentive effect, inaccurate division of disease groups, mismatch between payment standard and cost, decline in medical service quality and so on [10].

Zhu believes that medical institutions are not only medical service providers, but also agents of patients [11]. When medical institutions and doctors face the conflict between self-interest and the economic interests of patients, doctors will use the information advantage to induce patients to consume excessive medical services, and doctors play the role of incomplete agents at this time. The famous hypothesis of supply induced demand economics in health economics holds that because information asymmetry generally exists in the medical service market, information asymmetry will lead doctors to use the knowledge of disease diagnosis to deceive patients for they own benefits [12]. The incentive mechanism in China’s system design leads to the correlation between doctors’ income and the volume of medical services. Driven by the maximization of interests, doctors will use information asymmetry to induce patients to consume more medical services, increase patients’ medical expenses to increase economic income [13, 14]. Through the medical signaling game model under price control, Liu found that China’s price control led to the failure of the spontaneous signal transmission of the market and lead to the supply induced consumption demand of medical services [15].

Arrow believes that the problem of information asymmetry and uncertainty between the supplier and the demander is the most obvious one in the medical service market [16]. The key to understand the individual and organizational behavior of medical institutions is the limitation of information processing ability. Rice found that the quantity of medical services will increase significantly after the cost of doctor service is reduced [17]. Emons analyzed the data of seven important surgical patients in Switzerland and found that the medical expenses of ordinary patients were 33% higher than those of doctors or their families [18]. Yip based on the supply induced demand hypothesis, found that when doctors’ income decreases, they will make up for the income by increasing the quantity of medical services of patients [19]. In the 1960s, Arrow found that due to information asymmetry, medical insurance would lead to moral hazard of doctors and patients, resulting in excessive medical treatment [20]. The essence of this problem is the principal-agent and mechanism design under information asymmetry. To solve this problem, the breakthrough lies in the incentive regulation of the medical industry [21]. Different cities in China have carried out different reform of medical insurance payment method to control the unreasonable growth of medical expenses and avoid excessive medical treatment.

To sum up, most of the existing studies on the pricing of medical services under the condition of information asymmetry are based on theoretical analysis and mathematical models, and there are very few empirical studies. Although MIPM is the key to solve MSP, it is difficult to obtain effective data due to the serious information asymmetry in the medical service market and the high cost of obtaining medical information and knowledge. The data of medical institutions and medical insurance departments are open to the public, so it is difficult to obtain real medical service expenses information and medical insurance payment information. Therefore, there lacks empirical research on the impact of MIPM on MSP and its mechanism of MSP.

Method

Data source

In order to better understand the impact and mechanism of FPD on MSP, we match the two data by using computer, big data and other technologies to form a matched medical finance comprehensive data meeting our research needs. This is also an important exploration and innovation of data in this study, which is the first time in relevant research.

In order to further improve the settlement method of medical insurance premiums, perfect the pricing mechanism of medical services, control the unreasonable growth of medical expenses, and improve the quality and efficiency of medical services, Chengdu has carried out the reform of payment based on disease types. Following the principles of trial and error, dynamic adjustment, orderly promotion, and timely summary, Chengdu has determined the diseases, basic service items, fixed payment standards, cost settlement methods, and specific work implementation of fixed payment based on disease types, gradually promoting the reform of fixed payment based on disease types.

Starting from May 1, 2011, Chengdu first carried out the reform of fixed payment for basic medical insurance for urban employees based on disease types. For the first time, 10 diseases including acute appendicitis, ureteral stones, acute mastitis, uterine fibroids, benign prostatic hyperplasia, ovarian benign tumors, spontaneous pneumothorax internal treatment, spontaneous pneumothorax surgical treatment, idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura, and thrombotic external hemorrhoids were selected as pilot diseases. The pilot projects were carried out in designated medical institutions at the second level and above throughout the city, and detailed fixed payment standards based on disease types were formulated. At the same time, based on the implementation effect of fixed payment according to disease types, Chengdu has gradually increased the scope of diseases paid according to disease types and implemented the reform of fixed payment according to disease types in the basic medical insurance for urban and rural residents, on the basis of summarizing the experience of fixed payment according to disease types in the early stage of urban employee medical insurance.

The data used in our study comes from the huge sample data of medical insurance reimbursement and settlement of urban employees from 2010 to 2015 provided by the medical insurance administration of Chengdu City. There are 1,035,556 comprehensive medical financial data after grouping and matching which mainly include the information on the first page of medical records (disease diagnosis, ICD-10 code, outcome, hospital grade, etc.) Medical expense settlement information (actual compensation expenses, nominal compensation expenses, total medical expenses, drug expenses, examination expenses, material expenses, nursing expenses, bed expenses, blood expenses, treatment expenses, operation expenses, other expenses, etc.) and the basic personal characteristics of the patient (age, gender, disease, etc.). In order to better identify the net effect of FPD, we take May 1, 2011 as the central point for the reform of FPD in Grade II hospital. The time range of the selected data starts from January 1, 2010 to December 31, 2012, covering the changes before and after the reform of FPD in Grade II hospital, which better reflects the changes brought by the reform. At the same time, it can better control the individual characteristics of patients, make the analysis more rigorous and the conclusion more reliable. In our study, ureteral calculi were selected as the experimental group. In order to better ensure that the changing trend of patients who pay by disease quota and not pay by disease quota before the policy is the same, diseases similar to the diagnosis of ureteral calculi were selected as the control group, mainly including prostatic hypertrophy (hyperplasia), urethral infection, renal calculi, urinary tract infection, chronic renal insufficiency, chronic renal failure, uremic phase and acute pyelonephritis (Table 1).

This study aims to analyze whether a relatively reasonable mechanism of MSP has been established after the reform of FPD, which is mainly reflected in the impact on medical expenses, medical quality and medical insurance compensation. Specifically speaking, on the one hand, medical institutions will be directly affected by the reform, with the mechanism of MSP changing, the medical expenses, structure and medical insurance compensation will be inevitably influenced, and it is the same with the diagnosis and treatment behavior of doctors and the medical treatment behavior of patients. On the other hand, the diagnosis behavior of doctors and medical behavior and expenses of patients will be affected by many factors, which may have a certain fluctuation trend. DID method can identify the net effects of the policy and eliminate other effects not affected by the reform so as reflect the policy impact of the reform of FPD, and identify the rationality of the mechanism of medical services pricing. Therefore, we use the DID model to eliminate the effects of other factors affecting medical expenses, medical quality and medical insurance compensation, and estimate the impact of the reform of FPD on medical expenses, medical quality and medical insurance compensation.

Therefore, the dependent variables mainly include patients’ medical expenses, medical quality and medical burden. Among them, medical expenses mainly include total medical expenses, examination expenses, drug expenses, nursing expenses, bed expenses, material expenses, etc., which can not only reflect the changes in the details of medical expenses, but also reflect the changes in the structure of medical expenses. Medical quality mainly includes the outcome. The results of patients leaving the hospital are taken as the measurement indicators of medical quality, mainly including death, transfer, rehabilitation and others. This is also a relatively intuitive indicator in the measurement of medical quality and can be applied to empirical analysis [8]. The burden of medical treatment is mainly measured by the patients’ compensation expenses, reimbursement proportion and self-payment, mainly including nominal compensation expenses, actual compensation expenses, nominal reimbursement proportion, actual reimbursement proportion and self-payment. The control variables mainly reflect the personal characteristics of patients, including age, gender, disease type and hospital levels. On the whole, the matched medical finance comprehensive data records the real medical treatment information and medical insurance settlement information of each insured patient, so we can observe the real situation of each patients’ medical treatment, which is conducive to ensuring the authenticity and accuracy of the results.

Model setting

The DID method is used to estimate the impact of FPD on medical expenses, medical burden and medical quality of patients. Among the existing studies, DID is a widely used method in policy effect evaluation [22–25]. The model is set as follows:

Among them,Yit is the dependent variable, mainly including the structure of medical expenses (total medical expenses, drug expenses, inspection expenses, material expenses, etc.), medical quality (outcome), and medical burden (actual compensation amount and reimbursement proportion).Ti is a time dummy variable.Ti=0 means before the reform of FPD, andTi=1 indicates after the reform of FPD.Gi is a dummy variable for policy change.Gi=1 indicates the experimental group implemented the FPD (ureteral calculi).Gi=0 indicates that the control group did not implement the FPD (diseases similar to the diagnosis of ureteral calculi). μit refers to the covariates controlling the individual level, which is mainly composed of three groups of variables. The first is the individual characteristics, mainly including the patients’ gender, age and the square term of age. The second is the virtual variable of disease type. The third is the virtual variable of hospital level.

Parallel trend test

Using DID for policy evaluation, we must ensure that the change of outcome variables in the experimental group and the control group before and after the policy has nothing to do with the control group. That is to ensure the effectiveness of DID. This requires the outcome variables of the experimental group and the control group have no systematic or statistically significant difference in the absence of policy reform. Since we cannot observe the counterfactual situation without policy impact, we cannot directly test the identification hypothesis of DID. At present, the common practice of existing studies is to indirectly test the DID identification hypothesis by testing whether the outcome variables of the experimental group and the control group have the same time trend before the policy occurs, that is, the pre policy parallel trend test. At present, this is a common practice of using DID method in existing studies [22–25].

Before the empirical analysis, we first test the hypothesis. This paper tests the parallel trend hypothesis by changing the product of the experimental group dummy variable and the time dummy variable. The test results of total medical expenses and actual compensation expenses in this paper support the parallel trend hypothesis (Figs. 1 and 2).

Regression results

Impact on medical expenses

The regression results of model (1) are shown in Table 4. The cross terms of T and G represent the impact of fixed payment according to disease on medical expenses. On the whole, after the implementation of the reform of FPD, the total medical expenses decreased by 10.4%, indicating that the reduction of total medical expenses after the reform, and it has certain effect on controlling the growth of total medical expenses. From the perspective of medical expenses structure, after the reform of FPD, the inspection expenses is reduced by 30.6%, the material expenses is reduced by 49.5%, and the nursing cost is reduced by 45.6%. It indicates that after the reform, the inspection expenses, material expenses and nursing expenses are greatly reduced, and it has a better expenses control effect. However, the drug expenses increased by 22.4% and the bed expenses increased by 16.6%, indicating the reform has a poor effect on controlling the growth of drug expenses and bed expenses. There are two possible reasons. Firstly, before 2012, Chengdu City has not implemented the policy reform of canceling drug price addition, and the income of medical institutions mainly comes from drug. When the reform of FPD is implemented, the total medical expenses, inspection expenses, material expenses, nursing expenses and other expenses are reduced, medical institutions will increase drug expenses in order to ensure that the total income does not decrease. Income from bed expenses and other expenses may induce drug consumption and inpatient services to keep the total income of the hospital from declining. Secondly, although the reform of FPD has poor effect on controlling the growth of drug costs, it is found that the growth rate of drug expenses in 2011–2012 is lower than that in 2010–2012, indicating that although drug expenses are increasing, the growth rate of drug expenses is slowing down, so the reform plays a role in controlling the growth rate of drug expenses. In the short term, the reform has reduced the total medical expenses, changed the medical expenses structure, and achieved positive effect of controlling the growth of medical expenses. In the long run, the reform should be combined with other expenses control policies, such as the policy of separating medicine (abolishing the drug price addition). Especially, the government should formulate corresponding policies and measures for the increase of drug expenses and bed expense after the reform of FPD. Among the control variables, the effect of age on the total medical expense is significantly positive, and the square of age also has a positive influence on the medical expense, indicating that age has a positive correlation with the growth of medical expense. With the growth of age, the total medical expense continues to increase, but the extent of influence is less obvious.

Impact on medical insurance compensation

The regression results of model (1) are shown in Table 5. The cross terms of T and G represent the impact of FPD on medical insurance compensation. From the perspective of compensation expenses, after the reform of FPD, the nominal compensation expenses is reduced by 11.5% and the actual compensation expenses is reduced by 13.9%, indicating that the reform reduces the medical insurance compensation expenses of patients. From the perspective of reimbursement proportion, after the reform of FPD, the nominal reimbursement proportion decreased by 0.4% and the actual reimbursement proportion decreased by 2%, indicating that the reform reduced the proportion of medical insurance reimbursement of patients, but the reduction was less. From the perspective of individual self- payment, after the reform of FPD, the self-payment decreased by 5.1%, indicating that the self-payment of patients decreased after the reform. The compensation expense and reimbursement proportion of medical insurance are reduced, and the self-payment of patients is reduced. The possible reason is that the reform of fixed payment according to disease has significantly reduced the total medical expenses, which not only saves the medical insurance fund, but also reduces the medical burden of patients. Therefore, although the compensation expense and reimbursement proportion of medical insurance are reduced, the self-payment expense of patients has not increased, and it is also decreasing on the contrary. It shows that medical insurance and patients share the dividend of reducing medical expenses brought by the reform, and they realize the win-win result of saving medical insurance fund and reducing patients’ medical burden. In terms of medical quality, the outcome increased significantly by 149%, indicating that the reform of FPD has improved the quality of medical services. The reform of FPD quota not only optimizes the allocation of medical resources and saves a lot of medical insurance funds, but also reduces the personal burden of patients and improves the quality of medical services. It has played an important role in controlling the growth of medical expenses, reducing the medical burden of patients and ensuring people’s health.

Robustness test

This study further introduces the method of virtual policy time to test the robustness of the results of DID. For example, the parallel trend test conducted earlier, by introducing virtual policy impact time, it can support that the relevant impact results are caused by the reform of FPD. At the same time, in terms of medical expenses structure, the dependent variables are replaced by blood expenses, operation expenses and treatment expenses for robustness test.

Specifically, we refer to the practices of Zhang et al. [22] and Chen et al. [23] to set our test model similar to the empirical model (1):

Among them,Yit is the dependent variable, mainly including the structure of medical expenses (total medical expenses, drug expenses, inspection expenses, material expenses, etc.), medical quality (outcome), and medical burden (actual compensation amount and reimbursement proportion).Ti is a time dummy variable.Ti=0 means before the reform of FPD, andTi=1 indicates after the reform of FPD.Gi is a dummy variable for policy change.Gi=1 indicates the experimental group implemented the FPD (ureteral calculi).Gi=0 indicates that the control group did not implement the FPD (diseases similar to the diagnosis of ureteral calculi). μit refers to the covariates controlling the individual level, which is mainly composed of three groups of variables. The first is the individual characteristics, mainly including the patients’ gender, age and the square term of age. The second is the virtual variable of disease type. The third is the virtual variable of hospital level.

The difference between the test model (2) and the empirical model (1) is that if the FPD occurs on September 1, 2010 or after March 1, 2012, otherwise, it is assumed that the reform has been implemented on September 1, 2010 or March 1, 2012.Obviously, this is a virtual impact of the reform. If there is no statistically significant difference in parameters, it indicates that there is no statistically significant difference in the changes of medical expenses, medical burden and medical quality before and after September 1, 2010 or March 1, 2012, that is, it has passed the robustness test. As shown in Tables 6 and 7, it can be found that whether the impact of virtual policy is moved forward or backward, the blood expenses, operation expenses, treatment expenses, actual compensation expenses, individual self-pay and outcome results are not significant, indicating that there is no statistically significant difference between medical expenses and medical insurance compensation expenses, which proves that the result of the model is stable.

Discussion

This study had a number of important findings. Firstly, from the Table 4, we find that FPD reduces the medical expenses, which plays a role in controlling the unreasonable growth of medical expenses, and is also conducive to promoting the information disclosure of medical service items to some extent. The FPD belongs to the prospective payment system, and MIPM is changed from the retrospective payment system to the prospective payment system. The number of insured persons, the annual total number of visits, the average level of visit expenses and the calculated total annual overall compensation control in a certain region will be publicized, and the medical institutions will also disclose the medical service items, prices and other information, promoting the openness and transparency of medical service information, so patients can timely understand the medical insurance compensation and medical service information. Therefore, it reduces the information asymmetry between medical insurance, medical institutions and patients, and make the medical services pricing more reasonable.

Secondly, from the Table 5, we found that paying by disease quota reduces the personal burden of patients. FPD belongs to the prospective payment system, which is a market governance mechanism. Medical service providers and payers can establish a contract through negotiation, so as to control medical expenses into the internal incentive of medical service providers. By continuously strengthening and improving the reform of medical insurance payment mode, the government has made the prospective payment system the core of the market mechanism and established a public contract model between medical insurance institutions and medical institutions. Therefore, FPD can avoid information rent-seeking behavior, establish a public contract, realize the win-win of stakeholders and improve the mechanism of medical services pricing.

In general, after the reform of FPD, the total medical expenses, material expenses, examination expenses and nursing expenses have decreased significantly, and the growth rate of drug expenses has decreased significantly, indicating that FPD has effect on controlling the growth of medical expenses. The payment method of medical insurance promotes the disclosure of medical service information by adjusting the structure of medical expenses, avoiding the information rent-seeking of medical institutions and establishing the public contract between medical insurance authorities, medical institutions and patients. It is also of benefits to reducing information asymmetry, realizing the goal of medical insurance payment reform and controlling the growth of medical expenses, making the MSP mechanism relatively more reasonable and alleviating the dilemma of MSP.

Thirdly, from the Table 5, we find that MIPM has changed from the retrospective payment system to the prospective payment system. FPD has strong budget constraints on medical service providers, making up for the low efficiency of medical resource allocation caused by government failure. At the same time, FPD makes the income of medical service providers depend on their own medical expenses, which can better form an internal incentive mechanism for medical service providers, change medical institutions from external incentives to induce medical needs to internal incentives to reduce their own medical expenses, improve the incentive mechanism for medical service behavior, and standardize medical service behavior, so as to make the medical services pricing more reasonable.

Finally, after the reform of FPD, the nominal compensation expense, actual compensation expense, nominal reimbursement proportion and actual reimbursement proportion have decreased significantly, indicating that after the reform of FPD, the expenditure of medical insurance fund has been reduced and a large number of medical insurance funds have been saved. At the same time, the personal out of pocket expenses are also significantly reduced, indicating that the medical burden of patients is reduced. The outcome is significantly increased, indicating that the quality of medical services is significantly improved. It shows that the reform of FPD has certain effect on saving medical insurance fund, improving medical service quality and reducing patients’ medical burden. FPD can avoid information rent-seeking of medical institutions and establish public contracts between medical insurance authorities, medical institutions and patients, standardize medical service behavior and improve incentive mechanism by adjusting medical insurance compensation policy, reduce information asymmetry, achieve the goal of MIPM, improve the quality of medical services and reduce the burden of patients, make the mechanism of MSP more reasonable, and alleviate the dilemma of MSP.

Our study has several important contributions. Firstly, we matched the medical insurance reimbursement and settlement data with the medical record data of the hospital, forming a matched medical finance comprehensive data that satisfies the needs of the research. This is an important exploration and innovation in this study, which is also the first attempt in relevant research. Secondly, this study makes up for the deficiency of relevant empirical research by using the real medical service cost information and medical insurance payment information, and adopting the DID model to estimate the impact of medical insurance payment on MSP. Thirdly, this study studies the influence and mechanism of medical insurance payment on MSP under the condition of information asymmetry, so as to provide policy inspiration for the government to carry out the reform of multiple and compound medical insurance payment methods based on payment of specific disease, and improve the mechanism of MSP. It is of great significance to controlling the growth of medical expenses, improving the quality and efficiency of medical services.

Conclusion

In summary, our findings may contribute to the body of the knowledge on the impact of FPD on MSP in China. With the all-round development of information technology, MIPM has become an innovative means and tool for the comprehensive management of medical services in China. Transforming the pricing mechanism of medical services from the traditional decision-making or bargaining to digital, scientific, accurate technological governance, is crucial to achieving the modernization of governance capacity and governance system in the medical service field of China.
