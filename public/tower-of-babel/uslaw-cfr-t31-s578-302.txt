31 C.F.R. § 578.302 — Critical infrastructure sector.
Citation: us:cfr:31:578.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-578.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 578 (Cyber-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term critical infrastructure sector means any of the designated critical infrastructure sectors identified in Presidential Policy Directive 21 of February 12, 2013.
