Let

. Now, since

is steady, we get that for every

. By

6

, there are unique
\mathcal{R}(R)
-semimodule homomorphisms

such that

and
\lambda^{\prime}(\gamma)=N^{\prime}_{\gamma}
for every

. Thus by considering

and
\beta^{\prime}:=\lambda^{\prime}\psi
, we have

\gamma\in\Gamma

whence
\alpha\phi+\lambda=\alpha^{\prime}\phi+\lambda^{\prime}
. Hence we conclude that

\displaystyle\alpha+\beta

\displaystyle=(\alpha\phi+\lambda)\psi=(\alpha^{\prime}\phi+\lambda^{\prime})\psi=\alpha^{\prime}(\phi\psi)+\lambda^{\prime}\psi

\displaystyle=\alpha^{\prime}I_{\mathcal{R}(P)}+\lambda^{\prime}\psi=\alpha^{\prime}+\lambda^{\prime}\psi=\alpha^{\prime}+\beta^{\prime}.

∎
