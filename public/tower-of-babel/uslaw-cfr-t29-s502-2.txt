29 C.F.R. § 502.2 — Coordination of intake between DOL agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:29:502.2
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-502.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 502 (Enforcement Of Contractual Obligations For Temporary Alien Agricultural Workers Admitted Under Section 218 Of The Immigration And Nationality Act (Suspended 6-29-2009)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Complaints received by ETA or any State Workforce Agency (SWA) regarding contractual H-2A labor standards between the employer and the employee will be immediately forwarded to the appropriate WHD office for appropriate action under these regulations.
