Abstract
. The Katz-Sarnak density conjecture states that, as the analytic conductor R → ∞ R\to\infty , the distribution of the normalized low-lying zeros (those near the central point s = 1 / 2 s=1/2 ) converges to the scaling limits of eigenvalues clustered near 1 of subgroups of U ⁡ ( N ) U(N) . There is extensive evidence supporting this conjecture for many families, including the family of holomorphic cusp newforms. Interestingly, there are very few choices for the main term of the limiting behavior. In 2009, S. J. Miller computed lower-order terms for the 1-level density of families of elliptic curve L L -functions and compared to cuspidal newforms of prime level; while the main terms agreed, the lower order terms depended on the arithmetic of the family. We extend his work by identifying family-dependent lower-order correction terms in the weighted 1-level and 2-level densities of holomorphic cusp newforms up to O ⁡ ( 1 / log 4 ⁡ R ) O\left(1/\log^{4}R\right) error, sharpening Miller’s O ⁡ ( 1 / log 3 ⁡ R ) O\left(1/\log^{3}R\right) error. We consider cases where the level is prime or when the level is a product of two, not necessarily distinct, primes. We show that the rates at which the prime factors of the level tend to infinity lead to different lower-order terms, breaking the universality of the main behavior.

One helpful analogy to our results due to Miller
[

]

The universality suggested by the CLT is that, modulo normalization, the main terms are independent of the initial distribution.

For the first sum, since
q_{1}^{m}/kq_{2}\leq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{m\leq\frac{\log(kq_{2})}{\log(q_{1})}}\frac{m}{(\frac{q_{1}^{m}}{kq_{2}}+1)^{1/2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim_{k}\sum_{m\leq\frac{\log(kq_{2})}{\log(q_{1})}}m\lesssim_{k}\frac{\log^{2}(kq_{2})}{\log^{2}(q_{1})}.

The second sum requires more care. Define
\alpha=\lceil\log(kq_{2})/\log(q_{1})\rceil
. We can rewrite the sum as

(6.4)

\displaystyle\sum_{m\geq\alpha}\frac{m}{(\frac{q_{1}^{m}}{kq_{2}}+1)^{1/2}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(m+\alpha)\frac{1}{(\frac{q_{1}^{m+\alpha}}{kq_{2}}+1)^{1/2}}\lesssim\ \sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(m+\alpha)\frac{1}{(q_{1}^{m})^{1/2}}\lesssim\frac{\log(kq_{2})}{\log(q_{1})}.

Combining these together yields

\displaystyle\frac{\log(q_{1})}{q_{2}^{1/2}}\left[\sum_{m\leq\frac{\log(kq_{2})}{\log(q_{1})}}\frac{m}{(\frac{q_{1}^{m}}{kq_{2}}+1)^{1/2}}+\sum_{m>\frac{\log(kq_{2})}{\log(q_{1})}}\frac{m}{(\frac{q_{1}^{m}}{kq_{2}}+1)^{1/2}}\right]

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{\log(q_{1})}{q_{2}^{1/2}}\left[\frac{\log^{2}(kq_{2})}{\log^{2}(q_{1})}+\frac{\log(kq_{2})}{\log(q_{1})}\right]

(6.5)

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{\log^{2}(C_{2}N^{1-\delta})}{(C_{2}N^{1-\delta})^{1/2}\log^{2}(C_{1}N^{\delta})}+\frac{\log(C_{2}N^{1-\delta})}{(C_{2}N^{1-\delta})^{1/2}}.

Here we note that if
\delta=0
, left term dominates, otherwise the right term dominates, thus

\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\log(2q_{1}^{2m})}{(q_{1}^{m}+kq_{2})^{1/2}}\ =\ O(E(N,\delta))

E(N,\delta)\ =\ \begin{cases}\frac{\log(N)}{N^{\frac{1-\delta}{2}}}&\text{if }0<\delta\leq\frac{1}{2}\\[6.45831pt]
\frac{\log^{2}(N)}{N^{1/2}}&\text{if }\delta=0.\end{cases}

Combining this with the other error terms and observing that
\varphi(N)=\sum_{d|N}\mu(d)N/d
yields the lemma. ∎

The second term in (

Lemma D.2
.

(D.12)

Lemma D.4

(D.69)

\displaystyle\sum_{p_{1},p_{2}}\frac{\log(p_{1})\log(p_{2})}{p_{2}\log^{2}(R)}\widehat{\phi}\left(\frac{2\log(p_{2})}{\log(R)}\right)\frac{(p_{1}^{2}+3p_{1}+1)}{p_{1}(p_{1}+1)^{3}}

\displaystyle=\ \bigg[\frac{\widehat{\phi}(0)}{4\log(R)}+\frac{F_{1}\widehat{\phi}(0)}{\log(R)}+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right)\bigg]\cdot\left[\log{2}+\frac{3}{4}+F_{3}\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{\widehat{\phi}(0)(\log 2+\frac{3}{4}+F_{3})}{\log(R)}\left(\frac{1}{4}+F_{1}\right)+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right),

where
F_{1}=1+\int_{0}^{\infty}E(t)/t^{2}dt.

∎

Lemma D.9
.

Let
\widehat{\phi}
be even Schwartz function. Then

(D.70)

\displaystyle\sum_{p_{1},p_{2}}\frac{\log(p_{1})\log(p_{2})}{p_{2}\log(R)}\widehat{\phi}\left(\frac{2\log(p_{2})}{\log(R)}\right)\sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}C_{\ell}\frac{p_{1}^{\ell}(p_{1}-1)}{(p_{1}+1)^{2\ell+1}}

\displaystyle=\ \frac{\widehat{\phi}(0)(\log(2)+\frac{1}{4}+F_{4})}{\log(R)}\left(\frac{1}{4}+F_{1}\right)+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right).

Applying the same argument as in the previous computations and the fact that

(D.71)

\sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}C_{\ell}\frac{p_{1}^{\ell}(p_{1}-1)}{(p_{1}+1)^{2\ell+1}}=\frac{2p_{1}^{2}-p_{1}+1}{p_{1}(p+1)^{3}},

it follows that the sum equals

(D.72)

\frac{S(R)}{2\log(R)}\left[\sum_{p_{1}}\log(p_{1})\frac{2p_{1}^{2}-p_{1}+1}{p_{1}(p+1)^{3}}\right].

By the Prime Nnumber Theorem, letting
f(x)\ =\ -\frac{2x^{2}-x+1}{x(x+1)^{3}}
gives

(D.73)

\sum_{p_{1}}\log(p_{1})\frac{2p_{1}^{2}-p_{1}+1}{p_{1}(p+1)^{3}}\ =\ -\int_{1}^{\infty}tf^{\prime}(t)dt-\int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)f^{\prime}(t)dt.

The first term is

(D.74)

-\int_{1}^{\infty}tf^{\prime}(t)dt\ =\ -[tf(t)]_{1}^{\infty}+\int_{1}^{\infty}f(t)dt-1=\log(2)+\frac{1}{4}.

For the second term, note that

(D.75)

f^{\prime}(x)\ =\ -\frac{4x^{3}-5x^{2}+4x+1}{x^{2}\left(x+1\right)^{4}}

so that

(D.76)

-\int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)f^{\prime}(t)dt\ =\ \int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)\frac{4x^{3}-5x^{2}+4x+1}{x^{2}\left(x+1\right)^{4}}dt=:F_{4}.

Hence, (
D.73
) is equal to

(D.77)

\log(2)+\frac{1}{4}+F_{4}.

Substituting into (
D.72
), the sum is

(D.78)

\displaystyle\sum_{p_{1},p_{2}}\frac{\log(p_{1})\log(p_{2})}{p_{2}\log(R)}\widehat{\phi}_{2}\left(\frac{2\log(p_{2})}{\log(R)}\right)\sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}C_{\ell}\frac{p_{1}^{\ell}(p_{1}-1)}{(p_{1}+1)^{2\ell+1}}

\displaystyle=\ \bigg[\frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{4\log(R)}+\frac{F_{1}\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{\log(R)}+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right)\bigg]\cdot\left[\log 2+\frac{1}{4}+F_{4}\right]

\displaystyle=\ \frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)(\log 2+\frac{1}{4}+F_{4})}{\log(R)}\left(\frac{1}{4}+F_{1}\right)+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right).

∎

Lemma D.10
.

Let
\widehat{\phi}
be even Schwartz function. Then

(D.79)

\displaystyle\sum_{p}\frac{\log^{2}(p)}{\log^{2}(R)}\widehat{\phi}\left(\frac{2\log(p)}{\log(R)}\right)\sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}(C_{\ell+1}-C_{\ell})\frac{p^{\ell}(p-1)}{(p+1)^{2\ell+1}}

\displaystyle=\ \frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{4\log^{2}(R)}\ -\ \frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)\frac{(-9t^{4}+10t^{2}+10t+3)\log(t)+3t^{4}+3t^{3}-2t^{2}-3t-1}{t^{4}(t+1)^{4}}dt

\displaystyle+\ O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right).

We first simplify the sum over
\ell
. Let

(D.80)

G(x)\ :=\ \sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}C_{\ell}x^{\ell}\ =\ \frac{1-\sqrt{1-4x}}{2x}

and

(D.81)

S\ :=\ \sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}(C_{\ell+1}-C_{\ell})z^{\ell}

where
z=p/(p+1)^{2}.
Since
C_{0}=C_{1}=1
and
C_{2}=2
, it follows that

(D.82)

S\ =\ \frac{G(z)-1-z-2z^{2}}{z}-G+1+z=\frac{(1-z)G-1-z^{2}}{z}.

Plug in
z=p/(p+1)^{2}
, noting that
\sqrt{1-4z}=(p-1)/(p+1)
to get that

(D.83)

G(z)\ =\ \frac{p+1}{p}

and thus

(D.84)

S=\frac{3p^{2}+3p+1}{p^{2}(p+1)^{2}}.

Multiplying through by what we factored out, we get

(D.85)

\sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}(C_{\ell+1}-C_{\ell})\frac{p^{\ell}(p-1)}{p(p+1)^{2\ell+1}}=\frac{(p-1)(3p^{2}+3p+1)}{p^{3}(p+1)^{3}}.

The rest of the argument is analogous to (
D.6
). We define

(D.86)

g(x)\ :=\ \widehat{\phi}\left(\frac{2\log(x)}{\log(R)}\right)G(x)\log(x),

where

(D.87)

G(x)\ :=\ \frac{(x-1)(3x^{2}+3x+1)}{x^{3}(x+1)^{3}}.

Hence, we can rewrite the sum we want as

(D.88)

\frac{1}{\log^{2}(R)}\sum_{p}\log(p)\,g(p)\ =\ -\frac{1}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}tg^{\prime}(t)dt-\frac{1}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)g^{\prime}(t)dt.

Letting
u=2\log(p)/\log(R)
,

(D.89)

-\frac{1}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}tg^{\prime}(t)dt\ =\ \frac{1}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}g(t)dt\ =\ \frac{1}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}\log(t)\,\widehat{\phi}_{2}\left(\frac{2\log(t)}{\log(R)}\right)G(t)dt.

This is equal to

(D.90)

\frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{\log(t)(t-1)(3t^{2}+3t+1)}{t^{3}(t+1)^{3}}+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right)\ =\ \frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{4\log^{2}(R)}+O\left(\frac{1}{\log^{4}(R)}\right).

We now consider the error term. We have that

(D.91)

g^{\prime}(x)\ =\ \frac{A(x)\log(x)\widehat{\phi}_{2}^{\prime}\left(\frac{2\log(x)}{\log(R)}\right)+(B(x)\log(x)+C(x))\log(R)\widehat{\phi}_{2}\left(\frac{2\log(x)}{\log(R)}\right)}{\log(R)x^{4}(x+1)^{4}}

where
A(x)
,
B(x)
, and
C(x)
are polynomials. Expanding
\widehat{\phi}
and
\widehat{\phi}^{\prime}
, the main term doesn’t contribute, so the error is

(D.92)

-\frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)\frac{B(t)\log(t)+C(t)}{t^{4}(t+1)}dt

which is explicitly

(D.93)

-\frac{\widehat{\phi}_{2}(0)}{\log^{2}(R)}\int_{1}^{\infty}E(t)\frac{(-9t^{4}+10t^{2}+10t+3)\log(t)+3t^{4}+3t^{3}-2t^{2}-3t-1}{t^{4}(t+1)^{4}}dt.

Combining (
D.90
) and (
D.93
) yields the lemma.
∎
