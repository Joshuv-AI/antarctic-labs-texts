43 C.F.R. § 50.2 — How will reestablishment of this formal government-to-government relationship occur?
Citation: us:cfr:43:50.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-50.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 50 (Procedures For Reestablishing A Formal Government-To-Government Relationship With The Native Hawaiian Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Native Hawaiian government seeking to reestablish a formal government-to-government relationship with the United States under this part must submit to the Secretary a request as described in § 50.10. Reestablishment of a formal government-to-government relationship will occur if the Secretary grants the request as described in §§ 50.40 through 50.43.
