41 C.F.R. § 101-5.302 — -5.302 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-5.302
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-5.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-5 (5—Centralized Services In Federal Buildings And Complexes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the objective of GSA to provide or arrange for appropriate health service programs in all Government-owned and leased buildings, or groups of adjoining buildings, which it manages where the building population warrants, where other Federal medical facilities are not available, and, where the number of the occupying agencies indicating a willingness to participate in such a program on a reimbursable basis makes it financially feasible.
