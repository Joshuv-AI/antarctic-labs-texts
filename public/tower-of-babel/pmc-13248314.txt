Following the publication of original article [1], it is reported that there are typographical errors in Table 1: the qPCR log2FC value for gene OXCT1 needs to be corrected from −9.6 to −5.3; the % Promoter methylation change for gene OXCT1 needs to be corrected from +12.8% to +12.9%; the % Promoter methylation change for gene DNAJB2 needs to be corrected from +11% to +10.3%.

This correction does not affect findings/interpretation of the original article.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
