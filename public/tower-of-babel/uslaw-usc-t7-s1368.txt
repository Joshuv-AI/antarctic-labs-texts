7 U.S.C. § 1368 — Effect of increase on other quotas
Citation: us:usc:7:1368
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1368
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part C (Administrative Provisions); Subpart i (Publication And Review Of Quotas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any increase of any farm marketing quota for any farm as a result of review of the determination thereof under this subpart, the marketing quotas for other farms shall not be affected.
