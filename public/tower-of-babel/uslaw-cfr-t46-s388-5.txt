46 C.F.R. § 388.5 — Criteria for revocation of a waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:46:388.5
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-388.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 388 (Administrative Waivers Of The Coastwise Trade Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We shall revoke a waiver previously granted under this part if we determine, after notice and opportunity for a hearing, that fraud was involved in any part of the waiver application.
