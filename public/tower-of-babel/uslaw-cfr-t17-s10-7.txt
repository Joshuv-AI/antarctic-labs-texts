17 C.F.R. § 10.7 — Date of entry of orders.
Citation: us:cfr:17:10.7
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-10.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 10 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing any period of time involving the date of the entry of an order the date of entry shall be the date the order is served by the Proceedings Clerk.
