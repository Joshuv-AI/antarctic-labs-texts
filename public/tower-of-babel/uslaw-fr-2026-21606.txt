DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission


Sunshine Act Meetings

    The following notice of meeting is published pursuant to Section 
3(a) of the Government in the Sunshine Act (Pub. L. 94-409), 5 U.S.C. 
552b:

Agency Holding Meeting: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Nuclear 
Regulatory Commission.

DATE AND TIME: October 22, 2026.
    * Note--The Closed meeting will follow the Joint meeting of the 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission and the Nuclear Regulatory 
Commission.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ See Notice of Joint Meeting of the Federal Energy Regulatory 
Commission and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, October 14, 2026.

PLACE: Nuclear Regulatory Commission, One White Flint North, 11555 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rockville Pike, Rockville, MD 20852.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: Non-Public Cyber Security, Energy and 
Security Issues.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Debbie-Anne A. Reese, Secretary, 
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission. Telephone (202) 502-8400.
    Chairman Swett and Commissioners Rosner, See, Chang and LaCerte, 
voted to hold a closed meeting. Attached is the certification of the 
General Counsel explaining the action of closing the meeting.
    The Chairman and the Commissioners, their assistants, the General 
Counsel, and members of his staff, are expected to attend the meeting. 
Other staff members from the Commission's program offices who will 
advise the Commissioners in the matters discussed will also be present.

    Dated: October 15, 2026.
Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Secretary.
