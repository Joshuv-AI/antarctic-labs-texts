20 C.F.R. § 801.101 — Establishment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:801.101
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-801.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 801 (Establishment And Operation Of The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By Pub. L. 92-576, 82 Stat. 1251, in an amendment made to section 21 of the Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act (33 U.S.C. 921), there was established effective November 26, 1972, a Benefits Review Board, which is composed of members appointed by the Secretary of Labor.
