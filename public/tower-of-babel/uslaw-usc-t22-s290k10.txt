22 U.S.C. § 290k–10 — Effectiveness of Convention
Citation: us:usc:22:290k–10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290k–10
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXVI (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Articles 43 through 48, inclusive, of the Convention shall have full force and effect in the United States, its territories and possessions, and the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, upon the entry into force of the Convention for the United States.
