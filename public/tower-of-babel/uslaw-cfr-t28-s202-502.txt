28 C.F.R. § 202.502 — Information or informational materials.
Citation: us:cfr:28:202.502
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-202.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 202 (Access To U.S. Sensitive Personal Data And Government-Related Data By Countries Of Concern Or Covered Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not apply to data transactions to the extent that they involve the importation from any country, or the exportation to any country, whether commercial or otherwise, regardless of format or medium of transmission, of any information or informational materials.
