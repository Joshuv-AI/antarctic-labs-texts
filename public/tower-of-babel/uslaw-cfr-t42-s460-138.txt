42 C.F.R. § 460.138 — Committees with community input.
Citation: us:cfr:42:460.138
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-460.138
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 460 (Programs Of All-Inclusive Care For The Elderly (Pace)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A PACE organization must establish one or more committees, with community input, to do the following:
(a) Evaluate data collected pertaining to quality outcome measures.
(b) Address the implementation of, and results from, the quality improvement plan.
(c) Provide input related to ethical decisionmaking, including end-of-life issues and implementation of the Patient Self-Determination Act.
