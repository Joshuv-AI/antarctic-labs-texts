18 U.S.C. § 702 — Uniform of armed forces and Public Health Service
Citation: us:usc:18:702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section702
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 33 (Emblems, Insignia, And Names). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, in any place within the jurisdiction of the United States or in the Canal Zone, without authority, wears the uniform or a distinctive part thereof or anything similar to a distinctive part of the uniform of any of the armed forces of the United States, Public Health Service or any auxiliary of such, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
