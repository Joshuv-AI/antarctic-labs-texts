10 U.S.C. § 10001 — Definition of State
Citation: us:usc:10:10001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10001
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1001 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subtitle, the term “State” includes the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, and Guam.
