7 C.F.R. § 3560.261 — Improperly advanced rental assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3560.261
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3560.261
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3560 (Direct Multi-Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Improperly advanced RHS rental assistance resulting from tenant or borrower error or fraud constitutes unauthorized assistance and the provisions of subpart O of this part apply.
