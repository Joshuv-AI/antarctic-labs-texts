30 U.S.C. § 9 — Acceptance of lands from States
Citation: us:usc:30:9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section9
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 1 (United States Bureau Of Mines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to accept lands, buildings, or other contributions from the several States offering to cooperate in carrying out the purposes of section 8 of this title.
