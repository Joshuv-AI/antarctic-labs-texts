27 C.F.R. § 72.81 — Authority for disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:27:72.81
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-72.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 72 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Forfeited firearms, aummunition, explosive materials, or contraband cigarettes, not the subject of an allowed petition, may only be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of 26 U.S.C. 5872(b).
