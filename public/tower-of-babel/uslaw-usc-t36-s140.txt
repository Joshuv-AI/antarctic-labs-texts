36 U.S.C. § 140 — Stephen Foster Memorial Day
Citation: us:usc:36:140
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section140
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 1 (Patriotic And National Observances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may issue each year a proclamation—
(1)
designating January 13 as Stephen Foster Memorial Day; and
(2)
calling on the people of the United States to observe Stephen Foster Memorial Day with appropriate ceremonies, pilgrimages to his shrines, and musical programs featuring his compositions.
