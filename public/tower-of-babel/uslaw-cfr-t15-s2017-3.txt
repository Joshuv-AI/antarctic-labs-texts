15 C.F.R. § 2017.3 — Publication regarding petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:15:2017.3
Creator: Office Of The United States Trade Representative
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-2017.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 2017 (Petition Process To Review Eligibility Of Countries Under The African Growth And Opportunity Act (Agoa)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USTR will publish in the Federal Register:
(a) A list of actions taken in response to a petition, such as the publication of a Presidential proclamation modifying the designation of a country or the application of duty-free treatment with respect to articles from a country pursuant to the AGOA; and
(b) A list of petitions upon which no decision was made, and thus which are pending further review.
