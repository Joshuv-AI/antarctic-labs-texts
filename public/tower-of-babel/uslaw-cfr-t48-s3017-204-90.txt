48 C.F.R. § 3017.204-90 — 3017.204-90 Detention Facilities and Services (ICE).
Citation: us:cfr:48:3017.204-90
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3017.204-90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3017 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ICE Head of the Contracting Activity (HCA), without delegation, may enter into contracts of up to fifteen years' duration for detention or incarceration space or facilities, including related services.
