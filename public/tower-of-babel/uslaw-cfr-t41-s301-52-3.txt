41 C.F.R. § 301-52.3 — -52.3 Disallowed payment of a claimed item.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-52.3
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-52.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-52 (52—Claiming Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may disallow payment of a claimed item if the employee:
(a) Does not provide proper itemization of an expense;
(b) Does not provide required receipt(s) or other documentation required to support their claim; or
(c) Claims an expense which is not authorized.
