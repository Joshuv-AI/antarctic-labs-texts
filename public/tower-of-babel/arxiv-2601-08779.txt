Abstract
. Let A A be a separable, unital and exact C ∗ C^{*} -algebra satisfying the universal coefficient theorem. We prove uniqueness theorems up to unitary conjugacy for unital, full and nuclear maps from A A into ultraproducts of finite von Neumann factors: any two such maps agreeing on traces and total K K -theory are unitarily equivalent. There are two consequences. Firstly if one takes the factors to be a sequence ( M k n ) n (M_{k_{n}})_{n} of matrix algebras, we obtain a uniqueness result for quasidiagonal approximations of A A . Secondly, when ( ℳ , τ ℳ ) (\mathcal{M},\tau_{\mathcal{M}}) is a II 1 factor, a pair ϕ , ψ : A → ℳ \phi,\psi:A\to\mathcal{M} of unital, injective and nuclear maps are norm approximately unitarily equivalent if and only if τ ℳ ∘ ϕ = τ ℳ ∘ ψ \tau_{\mathcal{M}}\circ\phi=\tau_{\mathcal{M}}\circ\psi . The main strategy is to use Schafhauser’s classification of lifts along the trace–kernel extension from Schafhauser.Annals . Since our codomains may lack the tensorial absorption properties needed in Schafhauser.Annals , the main new ingredient is a suitable K ​ K KK -uniqueness theorem tailored to our situation. This is inspired by K ​ K KK -uniqueness theorems of Loreaux, Ng and Sutradhar ( ess-codim ).

The Gelfand–Niamark–Segal (GNS) construction (

(

(

Theorem

(Hadwin’s formulation of Voiculescu’s theorem)

The differences between this result and our Theorem

Indeed, a

The other difference is that Theorem

Classification via lifting along the trace–kernel extension and
KK
-uniqueness theorems

Our approach to proving Theorem
A
follows Schafhauser’s pioneering uniqueness of embeddings into
\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}
from
Schafhauser.Annals
. Schafhauser divided this problem into two pieces by means of the trace–kernel extension:

(0.3)

0\longrightarrow J_{\mathcal{Q}}\longrightarrow\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle q}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mathcal{R}^{\omega}\longrightarrow 0.

Here, the canonical embedding of
\mathcal{Q}
into the hyperfinite II
1
-factor
\mathcal{R}
induces a quotient map from
\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}
onto the von Neumann ultraproduct
\mathcal{R}^{\omega}
, and the unique trace
\tau_{\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}}
descends to
\tau_{\mathcal{R}_{\omega}}
, i.e.
\tau_{\mathcal{R}_{\omega}}\circ q=\tau_{\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}}
. Given a nuclear
C^{*}
-algebra
A
and two unital full
∗
-homomorphisms
\phi,\psi:A\to\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}
with
\tau_{\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}}\circ\phi=\tau_{\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}}\circ\psi

are unitarily equivalent. Moreover, this unitary lifts to
\mathcal{Q}_{\omega}
and so we can assume that
q\circ\phi=q\circ\psi
. Then

forms a
(A,J_{\mathcal{Q}})
-

and hence gives a class
[\phi,\psi]
in

Assuming now that

, the UCT can be used to show that
[\phi,\psi]
vanishes in

in the minimal unitisation of

. Once this happens

and
\psi
will be approximately unitarily equivalent in

, and hence (using reindexing) unitarily equivalent. However,
[\phi,\psi]=0
does not directly give a proper asymptotic unitary equivalence. Instead, Dadarlat–Eilers’

(

and
\psi\oplus\psi
.

Using the fact that
