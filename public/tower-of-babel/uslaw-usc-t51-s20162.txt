51 U.S.C. § 20162 — Definition of upper atmosphere
Citation: us:usc:51:20162
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section20162
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 201 (National Aeronautics And Space Program); Subchapter IV (Upper Atmosphere Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “upper atmosphere” means that portion of the Earth’s sensible atmosphere above the troposphere.
