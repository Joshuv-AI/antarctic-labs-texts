49 C.F.R. § 1548.1 — Applicability of this part.

Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1548 (Indirect Air Carrier Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes aviation security rules governing each indirect air carrier engaged indirectly in the air transportation of property on aircraft.
