45 C.F.R. § 2553.42 — Is an RSVP volunteer a federal employee, an employee of the sponsor or of the volunteer station?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.42
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RSVP volunteers are not employees of the sponsor, the volunteer station, AmeriCorps or the Federal Government.
