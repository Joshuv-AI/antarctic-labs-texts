31 C.F.R. § 34.507 — Recordkeeping—Spill Impact Component.
Citation: us:cfr:31:34.507
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-34.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 34 (Resources And Ecosystems Sustainability, Tourist Opportunities, And Revived Economies Of The Gulf Coast States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients must maintain records as prescribed by the Council and make the records available to the Council, and Treasury, including the Treasury Inspector General. In addition, the Council must make its records concerning the activities of recipients available to Treasury, including the Treasury Inspector General.
