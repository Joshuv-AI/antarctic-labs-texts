40 C.F.R. § 62.15070 — How do I comply with the increment of progress for awarding contracts?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15070
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15070
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit a signed copy of the contracts awarded to initiate onsite construction, initiate onsite installation of emission control equipment, and incorporate process changes. Submit the copy of the contracts with the notification that this increment of progress has been achieved. You do not need to include documents incorporated by reference or the attachments to the contracts.
