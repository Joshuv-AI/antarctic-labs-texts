18 C.F.R. § 415.20 — Class I projects.
Citation: us:cfr:18:415.20
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-415.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 415 (Basin Regulations—Flood Plain Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Projects described in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section shall be subject to review by the Commission under standards provided by this section and in accordance with the provisions of §§ 415.30 through 415.33 of this part, as follows:
(a) All projects subject to review by the Commission under section 3.8 of the Compact and the regulations thereunder.
(b) State and local standards of flood plain regulation.
