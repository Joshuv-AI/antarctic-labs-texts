34 C.F.R. § 390.1 — What is the Rehabilitation Short-Term Training program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:390.1
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-390.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 390 (Rehabilitation Short-Term Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This program is designed for the support of special seminars, institutes, workshops, and other short-term courses in technical matters relating to the vocational, medical, social, and psychological rehabilitation programs, independent living services programs, and client assistance programs.
