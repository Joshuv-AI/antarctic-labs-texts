40 C.F.R. § 463.16 — Pretreatment standards for new sources.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 463 (Plastics Molding And Forming Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) PSNS for bis(2-ethylhexyl)phthalate are reserved.
(b) Any new source subject to this subpart that introduces pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403—General Pretreatment Regulations.
