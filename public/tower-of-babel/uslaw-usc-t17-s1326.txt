17 U.S.C. § 1326 — Penalty for false marking
Citation: us:usc:17:1326
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1326
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 13 (Protection Of Original Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Whoever, for the purpose of deceiving the public, marks upon, applies to, or uses in advertising in connection with an article made, used, distributed, or sold, a design which is not protected under this chapter, a design notice specified in section 1306, or any other words or symbols importing that the design is protected under this chapter, knowing that the design is not so protected, shall pay a civil fine of not more than $500 for each such offense.
(b) Suit by Private Persons.—
Any person may sue for the penalty established by subsection (a), in which event one-half of the penalty shall be awarded to the person suing and the remainder shall be awarded to the United States.
