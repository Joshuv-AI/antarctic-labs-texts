40 C.F.R. § 246.200-7 — -7 Recommended procedures: Transportation.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 246 (Source Separation For Materials Recovery Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transportation to market may be supplied by the facility, by a private hauler, or by the purchaser. Collection of the recyclable paper should be on a regular, established schedule.
