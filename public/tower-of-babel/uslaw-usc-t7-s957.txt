7 U.S.C. § 957 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:7:957
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section957
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 32 (Peanut Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When used in this chapter—
(1)
The term “person” includes individuals, partnerships, corporations, and associations;
(2)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.
