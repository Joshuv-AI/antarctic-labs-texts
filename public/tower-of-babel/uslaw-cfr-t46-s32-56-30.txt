46 C.F.R. § 32.56-30 — -30 Category A machinery spaces: Bulkheads and decks—T/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.56-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.56-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Bulkheads and decks that separate category A machinery spaces from cargo pumprooms must be “A” Class construction.
(b) Bulkheads and decks that separate category A machinery spaces or cargo pumprooms, including the pumproom entrance, from accommodation, service, or control spaces must be “A-60” Class construction.
