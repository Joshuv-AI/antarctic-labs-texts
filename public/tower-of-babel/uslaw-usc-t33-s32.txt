33 U.S.C. § 32 — Lake George, Mississippi
Citation: us:usc:33:32
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section32
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter II (Waters Declared Nonnavigable: Change Of Name). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Lake George, in Yazoo County, in the State of Mississippi, is declared to be not a navigable water of the United States within the meaning of the laws enacted by the Congress for the preservation and protection of such waters.
The right of Congress to alter, amend, or repeal this section is expressly reserved.
