49 C.F.R. § 29.914 — How does a Tribe file a Contract Disputes Act claim?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.914
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.914
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Tribe must submit its claim in writing to the Self-Governance Official, who serves as the Department's awarding official for the purposes of Contract Disputes Act claims. The Self-Governance Official will document the receipt of the claim.
