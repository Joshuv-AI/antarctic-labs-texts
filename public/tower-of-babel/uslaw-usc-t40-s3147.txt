40 U.S.C. § 3147 — Suspension of this subchapter during a national emergency
Citation: us:usc:40:3147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3147
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter IV (Wage Rate Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may suspend the provisions of this subchapter during a national emergency.
