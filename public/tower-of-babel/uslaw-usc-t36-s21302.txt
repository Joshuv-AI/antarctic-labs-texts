36 U.S.C. § 21302 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:36:21302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21302
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 213 (American Historical Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to promote historical studies;
(2)
to collect and preserve historical manuscripts; and
(3)
other kindred purposes in the interest of American history and of history in America.
