28 C.F.R. § 301.205 — Appeal of determination.
Citation: us:cfr:28:301.205
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-301.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 301 (Inmate Accident Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inmate who disagrees with the decision regarding payment of lost-time wages may appeal that decision exclusively through the Administrative Remedy Procedure. (See 28 CFR part 542.)
