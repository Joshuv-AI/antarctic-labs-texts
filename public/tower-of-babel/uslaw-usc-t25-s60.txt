25 U.S.C. § 60 — Compensation prescribed to be in full
Citation: us:usc:25:60
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section60
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The several compensations prescribed by title 28 of the Revised Statutes shall be in full of all emoluments or allowances whatsoever. But where necessary, a reasonable allowance or provision may be made for offices and office contingencies.
