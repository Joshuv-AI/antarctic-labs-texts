10 U.S.C. § 4425 — Definition of weapon system component
Citation: us:usc:10:4425
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4425
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart F (Major Systems, Major Defense Acquisition Programs, And Weapon Systems Development); Chapter 327 (Weapon Systems Development And Related Matters); Subchapter II (Development, Prototyping, And Deployment Of Weapon System Components Or Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “weapon system component” has the meaning given the term “major system component” in section 4401 of this title.
