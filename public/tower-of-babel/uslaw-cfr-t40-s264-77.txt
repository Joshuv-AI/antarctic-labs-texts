40 C.F.R. § 264.77 — Additional reports.
Citation: us:cfr:40:264.77
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-264.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 264 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage, And Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to submitting the biennial reports and unmanifested waste reports described in §§ 264.75 and 264.76, the owner or operator must also report to the Regional Administrator:
(a) Releases, fires, and explosions as specified in § 264.56(j);
(b) Facility closures specified in § 264.115; and
(c) As otherwise required by subparts F, K through N, AA, BB, and CC of this part.
