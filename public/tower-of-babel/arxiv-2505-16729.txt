Equilibrium States, Zero Temperature Limits and Entropy Continuity for Almost-Additive Potentials
Jie CAO

Abstract
. This paper is devoted to study the equilibrium states for almost-additive potentials defined over topologically mixing countable Markov shifts (that is a non-compact space) without the big images and preimages (BIP) property. Let ℱ {\mathcal{F}} be an almost-additive and summable potential with bounded variation potential. We prove that there exists an unique equilibrium state μ t ​ ℱ \mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}} for each t > 1 t>1 and there exists an accumulation point μ ∞ \mu_{\infty} for the family ( μ t ​ ℱ ) t > 1 (\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}})_{t>1} as t → ∞ t\to\infty . We also obtain that the Gurevich pressure P G ​ ( t ​ ℱ ) P_{G}(t{\mathcal{F}}) is C 1 C^{1} on ( 1 , ∞ ) (1,\infty) and the Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy h ⁡ ( μ t ​ ℱ ) h(\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}}) is continuous at ( 1 , ∞ ) (1,\infty) . As two applications, we extend completely the results for the zero temperature limit [J. Stat. Phys. ,155 (2014),pp. 23-46] and entropy continuity at infinity [J. Stat. Phys., 126 (2007),pp. 315-324] beyond the finitely primitive case. We also extend the result [Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., 370 (2018), pp. 8451-8465] for almost-additive potentials.



1.
Introduction

The thermodynamic formalism is a branch of the ergodic theory that studies
existence, uniqueness and properties of equilibrium states. In this paper we study the equilibrium state associated with almost-additive potential
{\mathcal{F}}=\{f_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}
(see Definition
2.1
) defined over countable Markov shifts (CMS), that is, measures that maximize the value

h(\mu)+{\mathcal{F}}_{*}(\mu),

where
h(\mu)
is the measure entropy (see
[
35
, Chapter 4]
), and
{\mathcal{F}}_{*}(\mu):=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\int f_{n}\,d\mu
is the
Lyapunov exponent
for
\mu
, provided that the limit exists. Assuming uniqueness of equilibrium states

\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}}
for each scaled almost-additive potential
t{\mathcal{F}}
with
t>1
, we address three fundamental problems: the existence of accumulation points for the family
(\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}})_{t>1}
, the zero temperature limit and entropy continuity at infinity.

If the potential
{\mathcal{F}}
is additive (that is
f_{n}=S_{n}f
is the Birkhoff sum of
f
). A fundamental result in thermodynamic formalism
[
9
]
establishes that equilibrium states exhibit at most one equilibrium state, with existence typically requiring stringent dynamical conditions. Traditional approaches often assume the incidence matrix satisfies strong regularity properties–notably finite primitiveness or, in topologically mixing shifts with BIP condition
[
31
]
, which are equivalent to the existence of Gibbs measures. These classical criteria effectively constrain the system’s combinatorial complexity to ensure analytical tractability. In a significant advancement, Freire and Vargas
[
13
]
introduced a novel criterion that relaxes these prerequisites. By circumventing the finite primitivity assumption, their work extends equilibrium state existence theory to broader classes of symbolic dynamics, building upon earlier developments in
[
24
,
32
]
.

The thermodynamic formalism for almost-additive potentials over
CMS was developed in
[
16
]
. In particular, the existence of Gibbs state was established in
[
16
, Theorem 4.1]
. Later in
[
17
, Proposition 3.1]
, Iommi and Yayama introduced suitable conditions that ensure that certain Gibbs state is actually equilibrium state (this is not always the case in non-compact setting, see
[
31
, p. 1757]
).

Our main result for the equilibrium state is as follows:.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
(\Sigma,\sigma)
be a topologically mixing CMS and the potential
{\mathcal{F}}
be almost-additive and summable with bounded variation. Then, for any
t>1
there is an unique equilibrium state
\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}}
associated to
t{\mathcal{F}}
. Also, there exists an accumulation point
\mu_{\infty}
for the family
(\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}})_{t>1}
as
t\to\infty
.

As previously established, our methodological advancement resides in circumventing the necessity of Gibbs state across the entire space (the space without BIP property). Instead, we employ the condition that the almost-additive potential
{\mathcal{F}}
exhibits summability with bounded variation, thereby ensuring the existence of equilibrium states
\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}}
for each parameter
t>1
. It is essential to emphasize that while we demonstrate the existence of accumulation points for the sequence
(\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}})_{t>1}
, this does not inherently guarantee the existence of the limit
\lim_{t\to\infty}\mu_{t{\mathcal{F}}}
. To illustrate this subtlety, consider the simpler setting of compact subshifts of finite type with Hölder continuous potentials: Hochman and Chazottes
[
11
]
constructed an example where such convergence fails. This counterexample underscores the inherent complexity of equilibrium state behavior even in well-structured dynamical systems. However, under certain finite range assumptions convergence has been proved in
[
3
,
8
,
10
,
21
]
.

The other major result establishes that when the almost-additive potential

The regularity properties of the pressure are related to phase transitions. A cornerstone result by Ruelle

Within the context of ferromagnetism, this analytical regularity has been interpreted as indicative of “absence of phase transitions” (see

In statistical mechanics an important problem is that of describing how do equilibrium states varies as the temperature changes. Of particular importance is the case when the temperature decreases to zero (zero temperature limit). Indeed, this case is related to ground states, that is, measures supported on configurations of minimal energy

We also study a similar problem for the almost-additive potentials and obtain that the continuity of Lyapunov exponent

Definition 2.1
.

Let
(\Sigma,\sigma)
be a CMS. For each

,

be a continuous function. The potential
\mathcal{F}=\{f_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}

Definition 2.2

Let

be a CMS. The potential
