7 U.S.C. § 96 — Punishment for violation of prohibition
Citation: us:usc:7:96
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section96
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 4 (Naval Stores). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person willfully violating any provision of section 95 of this title shall, on conviction, be punished for each offense by a fine not exceeding $5,000 or by imprisonment for not exceeding one year, or both.
