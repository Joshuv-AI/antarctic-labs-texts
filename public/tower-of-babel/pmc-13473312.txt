Retraction Note: A randomized prospective comparison of the needleless mini‑sling “hammock” and “U‑shape” configurations for management of stress urinary incontinence: 18 month follow‑up results
Authors: Ozan Dogan, Alper Basbug, Aski Ellibes Kaya, Cigdem Pulatoglu, Murat Yassa
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics (2018) 297:1483–1493 10.1007/s00404-018-4755-z

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the data, specifically:

The values reported for BMI and duration of SUI in Table 1 are highly statistically significant, which raises concerns regarding the study’s randomisation.

The authors provided the raw data for the study, however post publication statistical review identified further apparent inconsistencies in the data. The Editor has therefore lost confidence in the results and conclusions of this article. Murat Yassa has stated on behalf of all authors that they do not agree to this retraction.
