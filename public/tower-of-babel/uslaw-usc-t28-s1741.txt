28 U.S.C. § 1741 — Foreign official documents
Citation: us:usc:28:1741
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1741
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 115 (Evidence; Documentary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An official record or document of a foreign country may be evidenced by a copy, summary, or excerpt authenticated as provided in the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
