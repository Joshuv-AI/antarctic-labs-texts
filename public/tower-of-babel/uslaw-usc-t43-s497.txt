43 U.S.C. § 497 — Action to recover operation charge and penalty
Citation: us:usc:43:497
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section497
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XI (Maintenance And Operation Of Works Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior suit or action may be brought for the amounts of operation or maintenance charges in default and penalties in like manner as provided in section 481 of this title.
