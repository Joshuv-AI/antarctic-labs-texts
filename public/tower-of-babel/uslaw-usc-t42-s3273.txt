42 U.S.C. § 3273 — Contributions of State or other public agencies toward cost of soil surveys
Citation: us:usc:42:3273
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3273
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 40 (Soil Information Assistance For Community Planning And Resource Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is further the sense of the Congress that the Secretary shall make a reasonable effort to assure that the contributions of any State or other public agency under any cooperative agreement which may be entered into between the Secretary and such State or other public agency with respect to a soil survey shall be a substantial portion of the cost of such soil survey.
