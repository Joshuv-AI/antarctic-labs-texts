42 U.S.C. § 272 — Administration of oaths by quarantine officers
Citation: us:usc:42:272
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section272
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part G (Quarantine And Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Medical officers of the United States, when performing duties as quarantine officers at any port or place within the United States, are authorized to take declarations and administer oaths in matters pertaining to the administration of the quarantine laws and regulations of the United States.
