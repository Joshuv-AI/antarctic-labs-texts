46 C.F.R. § 113.25-14 — -14 Electric cable and distribution fittings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.25-14
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.25-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each cable entrance to an emergency alarm signal or distribution fitting must be made watertight by a terminal or stuffing tube.
