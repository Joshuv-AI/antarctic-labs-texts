42 U.S.C. § 254c–11 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:254c–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section254c–11
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part D (Primary Health Care); Subpart i (Health Centers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide technical assistance to assist entities in complying with the requirements of sections 254c–9 to 254c–13 of this title in order to make such entities eligible to receive grants under section 254c–9 of this title.
