In recent years, there have been several results investigating the gain of regularity of solutions of Maxwell–Schrödinger systems. As it turned out, even in rough regimes where the datum is not regular, the coupling in these systems allows the existence of solutions which somehow are more regular than expected, the so–called Regularizing Effect. By now, it is known that this is caused by the very interesting nature of the coupling between the equations of systems of Maxwell–Schrödinger type. \displaystyle\Bigg(\int_{\Omega}\Big(|\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}-|\nabla\tilde{u}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{u}\Big)\cdot\big(\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}-\nabla\tilde{u}\big)

\displaystyle+\int_{\Omega}\Big(|\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}-|\nabla\tilde{v}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{v}\Big)\cdot\big(\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}-\nabla\tilde{v}\big)\Bigg)

\displaystyle\leqslant\limsup_{k\to\infty}\Bigg(\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\tilde{u}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{u}\cdot\big(\nabla\tilde{u}-\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}\big)+\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\tilde{v}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{v}\cdot\big(\nabla\tilde{v}-\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}\big)\Bigg)

\displaystyle=0.

Then, recalling that
\{\tilde{u}_{n}\}_{\mathbb{N}}
and
\{\tilde{v}_{n}\}_{\mathbb{N}}
are both bounded in
W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega)
, by Lemma
5.1
, we end up with

\displaystyle\limsup\limits_{n\to\infty}\bigg(\|\tilde{u}_{n}-\tilde{u}\|^{p}_{W^{1,p}_{0}}+\|\tilde{v}_{n}-\tilde{v}\|^{p}_{W^{1,p}_{0}}\bigg)

\displaystyle\leqslant C\limsup_{k\to\infty}\Bigg(\int_{\Omega}\Big(|\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}-|\nabla\tilde{u}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{u}\Big)\cdot\nabla(\tilde{u}_{n}-\tilde{u})

\displaystyle+\int_{\Omega}\Big(|\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}-|\nabla\tilde{v}|^{p-2}\nabla\tilde{v}\Big)\cdot\nabla(\tilde{v}_{n}-\tilde{v})\Bigg)=0,

where
C\geqslant 2+2\sup_{n}\{\|\nabla\tilde{u}_{n}\|^{p}_{L^{p}}+\|\nabla\tilde{v}_{n}\|^{p}_{L^{p}}\}
, and the result follows.
∎

In order to simplify some proofs, and also to let this manuscript self-contained, below we state and prove two well-known regularity results.

Lemma 5.3
.

Let

H:\Omega\times\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
be a Carathéodory functional and
F\in L^{t}(\Omega)
,
t>\frac{N}{p}
.

(a)

Suppose that
H(.,s)s\geqslant 0
a.e. in
\Omega
for all
s\in\mathbb{R}
.
Given
w\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega)
a weak subsolution of

(5.3)

\begin{cases}-\Delta_{p}w+H(x,w)=F\mbox{ in }\Omega\\
w=0\mbox{ on }\partial\Omega,\end{cases}

then
w\in L^{\infty}(\Omega)
and
\|w\|_{L^{\infty}}\leqslant C\|F\|_{L^{t}}^{\frac{1}{p-1}}
, where
C=C(p,t,N,\Omega)>0
.

(b)

Now, suppose that
|H(.,s)|\leqslant C_{0}(\beta|s|^{\theta}+\alpha)
, where
\alpha\geqslant 0

\beta>0
, and
0<\theta<\frac{p^{2}}{N-p}
, for all
s\in\mathbb{R}
a.e. in
\Omega
. Given
w\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega)
a weak subsolution of

(5.4)

\begin{cases}-\Delta_{p}w=H(x,w)\mbox{ in }\Omega\\
w=0\mbox{ on }\partial\Omega,\end{cases}

then
v\in L^{\infty}(\Omega)
and
\|w\|_{L^{\infty}}\leqslant C\big(\beta^{\frac{1}{p-1}}\|w\|^{\frac{\theta}{(p-1)}}_{L^{p^{*}}}+\alpha^{\frac{1}{p-1}}\big)
, where
C=C(p,C_{0},N,\theta,\Omega)>0
.

Proof.

Since
w\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega)
is a weak subsolution of (
5.3
), in order to prove (a) it is enough to take
G_{k}(w)
as a test function in the corresponding inequality (
5.3
) and then, remarking that

\int_{\Omega}H(x,w)G_{k}(w)\geqslant 0,

and that
t>\frac{N}{p}
, by straightforward applications of Hölder’s inequality in
F
and
G_{k}(w)
, the result follows directly by Lemma 6.2 p. 49 in
[
5
]
.

We turn our attention to the proof of (b). For this, let us consider
G_{k}(w)
as a test function in the corresponding inequality of (
5.4
) so that by the Sobolev and Hölder inequalities,
p^{*}/\theta

\displaystyle\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{p}{p^{*}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant C\int_{\Omega}|\nabla G_{k}(w)|^{p}\leqslant C\int_{\Omega}H(x,w)G_{k}(w)

\displaystyle\leqslant C\int_{\Omega}(\beta|w|^{\theta}+\alpha)|G_{k}(w)|

\displaystyle\leqslant C\Bigg(\beta\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|w|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{\theta}{p^{*}}}\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{\frac{p^{*}}{p^{*}-\theta}}\bigg)^{\frac{p^{*}-\theta}{p^{*}}}+\alpha\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|\Bigg)

\displaystyle\leqslant C\Bigg(\bigg(\beta\int_{\Omega}|w|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{\theta}{p^{*}}}+\alpha\Bigg)\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{\frac{p^{*}}{p^{*}-\theta}}\bigg)^{\frac{p^{*}-\theta}{p^{*}}},

where we have used that
p^{*}>\theta
and
p^{*}>\frac{p^{*}}{p^{*}-\theta}
, since
p<N
,
\theta<\frac{p^{2}}{N-p}
and clearly
p^{*}-\theta>1
. Thus, once again by the Hölder inequality but now for
p^{*}-\theta
and
\frac{p^{*}-\theta}{p^{*}-\theta-1}
we get

\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{\frac{p^{*}}{p^{*}-\theta}}\leqslant\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{p^{*}-\theta}}\mbox{meas}(\Omega_{k})^{\frac{p^{*}-\theta-1}{p^{*}-\theta}},

where
\Omega_{k}=\{x\in\Omega:|w(x)|>k\}.
Thence, by plugging the latter inequalities, we end up with

\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{p-1}{p^{*}}}\leqslant C\bigg(\beta\|w\|^{\theta}_{L^{p^{*}}}+\alpha\bigg)\mbox{meas}(\Omega_{k})^{\frac{p^{*}-\theta-1}{p^{*}}}

At this point, remark that given
0<k<\tau

\displaystyle(h-k)^{p-1}\mbox{meas}(\Omega_{h})^{\frac{p-1}{p^{*}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\bigg(\int_{\Omega_{h}}(h-k)^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{p-1}{p^{*}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\bigg(\int_{\Omega_{h}}|G_{k}(w)|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{p-1}{p^{*}}}\leqslant\bigg(\int_{\Omega}|G_{k}(w)|^{p^{*}}\bigg)^{\frac{p-1}{p^{*}}},

since
\Omega_{\tau}=\{x\in\Omega:w(x)>\tau\}\subset\Omega_{k}.

In this fashion, by combining the last two inequalities it is straightforward to check that

\mbox{meas}(\Omega_{h})\leqslant C\dfrac{\beta^{\frac{p^{*}}{p-1}}\|w\|_{L^{p^{*}}}^{\frac{p^{*}\theta}{(p-1)}}+\alpha^{\frac{p^{*}}{(p-1)}}}{(h-k)^{p^{*}}}\mbox{meas}(\Omega_{k})^{\frac{p^{*}-\theta-1}{p-1}},\forall\ 0<k<\tau.

Now, remark that since
0<\theta<\frac{p^{2}}{N-p}
we have
\frac{p^{*}-\theta-1}{p-1}>1
and the result follows by applying Lemme 4.1 (ii) p.93-94 in
[
14
]
for
\varphi(h)=\mbox{meas}(\Omega_{h})
,
\alpha=p^{*}
and
\beta=\frac{p^{*}-\theta-1}{p-1}
.
∎

Nonlinear Convergence for Two Variables

The next lemma is used to pass to the limit in delicate terms with low regularity. Despite that its based in the classic Vitali Theorem, we decided to formulate it in a more general form that we use in the present manuscript and to allocate it in the appendix, since it may be useful in other contexts.

Lemma 5.4
.

Consider
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
, bounded,
H:\Omega\times\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
, Carathéodory, and
\{\tilde{u}_{k}\}_{\mathbb{N}},\{\tilde{v}_{k}\}_{\mathbb{N}}
. Suppose that, for
i=1,2
given
0\leqslant s_{i}<+\infty
,
\max\{s_{i},1\}<q_{i}<+\infty
,
\frac{q_{i}}{q_{i}-s_{i}}<t_{i}\leqslant+\infty
, there exist
w_{i}\in L^{t_{i}}(\Omega),w_{i}\geqslant 0
a.e. in
\Omega
,
\tilde{u}
and
\tilde{u}\in L^{q_{1}}(\Omega)
and
\tilde{v}\in L^{q_{2}}(\Omega)
, for which

(i)

\tilde{u}_{k}\to\tilde{u}
and
\tilde{v}_{k}\to\tilde{v}
, a.e. in
\Omega
, if
k\to+\infty
,

(ii)

\{\tilde{u}_{k}\}_{\mathbb{N}}
is bounded in
L^{q_{1}}(\Omega)
and
\{\tilde{v}_{k}\}_{\mathbb{N}}
is bounded in
L^{q_{2}}(\Omega)
,

(iii)

\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|\leqslant\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\}}w_{1}|\tilde{u}_{k}|^{s_{1}}
for all
k,n\in\mathbb{N}
,

(iv)

Given
n_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
there exists
C=C(n_{0})>0
, such that

\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|\leqslant C(n_{0})\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}w_{2}|\tilde{v}_{k}|^{s_{2}}

for all
k\in\mathbb{N}
.

Then
H(.,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})\to H(.,\tilde{u},\tilde{v})
in
L^{1}(\Omega)
if
k\to+\infty.

Proof.

Since
H
is Carathéodory, from (i) we already have that

H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})\to H(x,\tilde{u},\tilde{v})
a.e. in
\Omega
. Thus, it is enough to prove that
\{H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})\}_{\mathbb{N}}
is uniformly integrable.

Indeed, consider
E\subset\Omega
, measurable, and
n\in\mathbb{N}
. By (iii), it is clear that

(5.5)

\int_{E}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|\leqslant\int_{E\cap|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|+\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\}}w_{1}|\tilde{u}_{k}|^{s_{1}}.

Next, recall that by the Chebyshev inequality,

\mbox{meas}(\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\})\leqslant\dfrac{\|u_{k}\|^{q_{1}}}{n^{q_{1}}},\ \forall k,n\in\mathbb{N}.

In this manner, if
t_{1}=+\infty
. by the Hölder inequality for
\frac{q_{1}}{s_{1}}
and
\frac{q_{1}}{q_{1}-s_{1}}
there follows

\displaystyle\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\}}w_{1}|\tilde{u}_{k}|^{s_{1}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\|w_{1}\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\tilde{u}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{1}}}^{s_{1}}\big(\mbox{meas}(\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\})\big)^{\frac{q_{1}-s_{1}}{q_{1}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\dfrac{1}{n^{q_{1}-s_{1}}}\|\tilde{u}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{1}}}^{s_{1}}\|w_{1}\|_{L^{\infty}},.

Now, when
t_{i}
is finite, by the the Hölder inequality for
t_{1}
,
\frac{t_{1}q_{1}}{t_{1}(q_{1}-s_{1})-q_{1}}
,
\frac{q_{1}}{s_{1}}
, if
s_{1}>0
, or
t_{1}
,
\frac{t_{1}}{t_{1}-1}
, if
s_{1}=0
, we arrive at

\displaystyle\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\}}w_{1}|\tilde{u}_{k}|^{s_{1}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\|w_{1}\|_{L^{t_{1}}}\|\tilde{u}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{1}}}^{s_{1}}\big(\mbox{meas}(\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\})\big)^{\frac{t_{1}(q_{1}-s_{1})-q_{1}}{t_{1}q_{1}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant\dfrac{C}{n^{\frac{q_{1}(t_{1}-1)}{t_{1}}-s_{1}}}\|w_{1}\|_{L^{t_{1}}},

where
C=\sup_{k}\{\|\tilde{u}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{1}}}^{\frac{q_{1}}{t_{1}^{\prime}}}\}
is finite by (ii).

Hence, by the last two inequalities since
q_{1}>s_{1}
and
\frac{q_{1}(t_{1}-1)}{t_{1}}>s_{1}
, given
\varepsilon>0
, consider
n_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
satisfying

(5.6)

\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|>n\}}w_{1}|\tilde{u}_{k}|^{s_{1}}<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

Further, considering this
n_{0}
fixed, by (iv) we have that

\int_{E\cap\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|\leqslant C(n_{0})\int_{\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}w_{2}|\tilde{v}_{k}|^{s_{2}}.

In particular, if
t_{2}
is finite, by the Hölder inequality, for
t_{2}
,
\frac{t_{2}}{t_{2}-1}
, if
s_{2}=0
, or
t_{2}
,
\frac{t_{2}q_{2}}{t_{2}(q_{2}-s_{2})-q_{2}}
,
\frac{q_{2}}{s_{2}}
, if
s_{2}>0
, we get

\displaystyle\int_{E\cap\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|

\displaystyle\leqslant C(n_{0})\|w_{2}\|_{L^{t_{2}}}\|\tilde{v}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{2}}}^{s_{2}}\big(\mbox{meas}(E)\big)^{\frac{t_{2}(q_{2}-s_{2})-q_{2}}{t_{2}q_{2}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant C_{2}\|w_{2}\|_{L^{t_{2}}}\big(\mbox{meas}(E)\big)^{\frac{t_{2}(q_{2}-s_{2})-q_{2}}{t_{2}q_{2}}},

for all
k\in\mathbb{N}
, where
C_{2}=C(n_{0})\sup_{k}\|\tilde{v}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{2}}}^{s_{2}}<+\infty
by (ii). Moreover, if
t_{2}=+\infty
, by the Hölder inequality for
\frac{q_{2}}{s_{2}}
and
\frac{q_{2}}{q_{2}-s_{2}}
there follows

\displaystyle\int_{E\cap\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|

\displaystyle\leqslant C(n_{0})\|w_{2}\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\tilde{v}_{k}\|_{L^{q_{2}}}^{s_{2}}\big(\mbox{meas}(E)\big)^{\frac{q_{2}-s_{2}}{q_{2}}}

\displaystyle\leqslant C_{2}\|w_{2}\|_{L^{t_{2}}}\big(\mbox{meas}(E)\big)^{\frac{q_{2}-s_{2}}{q_{2}}}.

In this way, by using that
q_{2}>s_{2}
and
t_{2}(q_{2}-s_{2})>q_{2}
, consider
\delta>0
such that if
\mbox{meas}(E)<\delta
, then

(5.7)

\int_{E\cap\{|\tilde{u}_{k}|\leqslant n_{0}\}}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

Thence, by combining (
5.5
),(
5.6
) and (
5.7
), for every
E\subset\Omega
, measurable, for which
\mbox{meas}(E)<\delta
, there follows that

\int_{E}|H(x,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})|<\varepsilon,\forall k\in\mathbb{N},

so that
\{H(.,\tilde{u}_{k},\tilde{v}_{k})\}_{\mathbb{N}}
is uniformly integrable and the result follows by the Vitali Theorem.
∎
