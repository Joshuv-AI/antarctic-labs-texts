42 U.S.C. § 5028a — Prohibition
Citation: us:usc:42:5028a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5028a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter II (National Senior Service Corps); Part E (Demonstration Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may not reduce the activities, projects, or volunteers funded under the other parts of this subchapter in order to support projects under this part.
