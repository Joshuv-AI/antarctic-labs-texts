46 U.S.C. § 57516 — Operating-differential subsidies
Citation: us:usc:46:57516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57516
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter II (Charters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If the Secretary of Transportation considers it necessary, the Secretary may make a contract with a charterer of a vessel owned by the Secretary for payment of an operating-differential subsidy, on the same terms and conditions, and subject to the same limitations and restrictions, as otherwise provided with respect to payment of operating-differential subsidies to operators of privately-owned vessels.
