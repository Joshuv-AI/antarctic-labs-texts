31 C.F.R. § 515.553 — Bank accounts of official representatives in Cuba of foreign governments.
Citation: us:cfr:31:515.553
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-515.553
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 515 (Cuban Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Specific licenses are issued authorizing payments from accounts of official representatives in Cuba of foreign governments for transactions which are not inconsistent with the purposes of any of the regulations in this chapter.
