42 U.S.C. § 14333 — Limitations on sale of zinc-carbon batteries containing mercury
Citation: us:usc:42:14333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14333
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 137 (Management Of Rechargeable Batteries And Batteries Containing Mercury); Subchapter III (Management Of Batteries Containing Mercury). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person shall sell, offer for sale, or offer for promotional purposes any zinc-carbon battery manufactured on or after May 13, 1996, that contains mercury that was intentionally introduced as described in section 14332 of this title.
