20 C.F.R. § 219.56 — When evidence of a parent's support is required.
Citation: us:cfr:20:219.56
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-219.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 219 (Evidence Required For Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a person applies for a parent's annuity, the Board will require evidence to show that the parent received at least one-half of his or her support from the employee in the one-year period before—
(a) The employee died; or
(b) The beginning of a period of disability if the employee had a period of disability which did not end before his or her death.
