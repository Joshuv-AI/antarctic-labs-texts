40 C.F.R. § 35.4106 — What information should an LOI include?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4106
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The LOI should clearly state that your group intends to apply for a TAG, and should identify:
(a) The name of your group;
(b) The Superfund site(s) for which your group intends to submit an application; and
(c) Provide the name of a contact person in the group and his or her mailing address and telephone number.
