16 U.S.C. § 1543 — Construction with Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972
Citation: us:usc:16:1543
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1543
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 35 (Endangered Species). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, no provision of this chapter shall take precedence over any more restrictive conflicting provision of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 [16 U.S.C. 1361 et seq.].
