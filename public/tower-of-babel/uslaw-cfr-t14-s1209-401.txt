14 C.F.R. § 1209.401 — Establishment.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1209.401
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1209.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1209 (Boards And Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to the authority of the National Aeronautics and Space Act of 1958 as amended (42 U.S.C. 2457(f) and 2458) and the Government Employees Incentive Awards Act of 1954 (5 U.S.C. 4501-6), the Board was established on December 4, 1958, and is further continued in effect by this subpart 4.
