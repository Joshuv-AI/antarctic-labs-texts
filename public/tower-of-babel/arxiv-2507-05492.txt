A note on type II superstring vertex operators
in the B-RNS-GSS formalism

Abstract




We construct integrated and unintegrated vertex operators for the type II superstring using the B-RNS-GSS formalism. The construction is done in flat spacetime background for both type II superstrings and type IIB superstring in a
AdS_{5}\times S^{5}
background.

Introduction

The Ramond-Neveu-Schwarz (RNS) superstring exhibits supersymmetry on the world-sheet, but its spacetime supersymmetric properties are difficult to verify
[
1
]
. On the other hand, the Green-Scwharz-Siegel (GSS) supertstring is manifestly supersymmetric but it has not been possible to quantize it while preserving the symmetries of the spacetime background
[
2
]
. About twenty-five years ago, Berkovits proposed a new superstring which has manisfest spacetime supersymmetry and can be quantized
[
3
]
and it is known as the pure spinor superstring (B). This formalism can be defined in any on-shell background spacetime even those with Ramond-Ramond background fields such as the classical example of type IIB superstring in a
AdS_{5}\times S^{5}
background
[
4
]
. This new formalism is conformal invariant in flat and curved backgrounds
[
5
,
6
]
but does not possess world-sheet supersymmetry. A new formalism was propesed that mixes all the good properties of the RNS, GSS and the B superstrings in
[
7
]
and is called B-RNS-GSS formalism.

This new formalism has a BRST charge that allows quantization and has the structure of a
N=2
world-sheet supersymmetric BRST charge. Imposing this fact on a curved background it is possible to define heterotic
[
8
]
and type II supertstrings
[
9
]
.

The purpose of this paper is to construct massless vertex operators using the B-RNS-GSS formalism. Vertex operators describe physical states and are used in the computation of scattering amplitudes that we are not going to explore here. The structure is in the bosonic string that we review now.

Consider the open bosonic string case. Physical states are defined in the cohomology of the BRST charge which, in the conformal gauge, is given by

\displaystyle Q=\oint dz\left(cT+bc\partial c\right),

(1.1)

where
T=-\frac{1}{2}\partial X_{m}\partial X^{m}
is the stress-energy tensor for the matter
X
world-sheet variables and
(b,c)
are the Faddeev-Popov ghosts.

The unintegrated vertex operator is defined as a zero conformal dimension and ghost number
1
operator in the cohomology of
Q
, that is,
QU=0
and
U\sim U+Q\Lambda
. For the massless state, it is given by

\displaystyle U=c\partial X^{m}A_{m}(X),

(1.2)

which is physical if the field
A_{m}(X)
satisfies the equations of a Maxwell field. The integrated vertex operator is a conformal weight operator
+1
with vanishing ghost number. For the Maxwell field, the integrated vertex is given by

\displaystyle V=\partial X^{m}A_{m}(x).

(1.3)

Note that
U
and
V
are related by

\displaystyle QV=\partial U.

(1.4)

Consider now the closed string. The BRST charge given by

\displaystyle Q=\oint dz\left(cT+bc\partial c\right)+\oint d\overline{z}\left(\widetilde{c}\overline{T}+\widetilde{b}\widetilde{c}\overline{\partial}\widetilde{c}\right),

(1.5)

where
T=-\frac{1}{2}\partial X_{m}\partial X^{m}
is the left-moving stress-energy tensor for the matter
X
world-sheet variables, similarly
\overline{T}=-\frac{1}{2}\overline{\partial}X_{m}\overline{\partial}X^{m}
is the right-moving stress-energy tensor for the matter
X
world-sheet variables. There are left-moving ghosts
(b,c)
and right-moving ghosts
(\widetilde{b},\widetilde{c})
.

The unintegrated vertex operator for the graviton is a conformal dimension
(0,0)
and ghost number
(1,1)
state in the cohomology of the BRST and it is given by

\displaystyle U=c\widetilde{c}\partial X^{m}\overline{\partial}X^{n}h_{mn}(X),

(1.6)

which turns out to describe a physical state when the field
h_{mn}
satisfies the linearized Einstein equations. The corresponding integrated vertex operator has conformal weight
(1,1)
and vanishing ghost number. It is given by

\displaystyle V=\partial X^{m}\overline{\partial}X^{n}h_{mn}(X).

(1.7)

The relations between
U
and
V
involves the operators
W
and
\overline{W}
which satisfy

\displaystyle QV=\overline{\partial}W-\partial\overline{W},\quad QW=\partial U,\quad Q\overline{W}=\overline{\partial}U.

(1.8)

The operators
W
and
\overline{W}
are given by

\displaystyle W=\widetilde{c}\partial X^{m}\overline{\partial}X^{n}h_{mn}(X),\quad\overline{W}=-c\partial X^{m}\overline{\partial}X^{n}h_{mn}(X).

(1.9)

The idea is to establish a relation like (
1.8
) for the massless states in the type II superstring using the B-RNS-GSS formalism in flat spacetime and in
AdS_{5}\times S^{5}
background. In section 2 we consider a superstring in flat spacetime and review the construction of massless vertex operators in the open superstring case and then we construct integrated and unintegrated vertex operators for the closed superstring. In section 3 we study the special case of the type IIB superstring in
AdS_{5}\times S^{5}
background and construct integrated and unintegrated vertex operators.

2
The flat spacetime case

The B-RNS-GSS formalism was introduced in
[
7
]
and we review the open string case (or the left-moving sector of the heterotic string) and below we will consider the type II case. The world-sheet action and the BRST charge are obtained by performing a similarity transformation on the RNS system together with the addition of the free field conjugate pairs
(\theta^{\alpha},p_{\alpha})
and
(\Lambda^{\alpha},w_{\alpha})
. Here
\alpha=1,\dots 16
,
\theta^{\alpha}
are the
N=1
superspace coordinates in ten dimensions. Recall that the RNS world-sheet fields are the spacetime coordinates
X^{m}
and their supersymmetry partners
\psi^{m}
together with super-reparametrization ghosts
(b,c,\beta,\gamma)
. The action is given by

\displaystyle S=\int d^{2}z~\left(\frac{1}{2}\partial X_{m}\overline{\partial}X^{m}+\frac{1}{2}\psi_{m}\overline{\partial}\psi^{m}+b\overline{\partial}c+\beta\overline{\partial}\gamma+p_{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\theta^{\alpha}+w_{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\Lambda^{\alpha}\right),

(2.1)

where the unconstrained bosonic variables
(w,\Lambda)
are in the untwisted case so they have conformal weights
(\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2})
, respectively. The BRST charge is given by

\displaystyle Q=\oint dz\left(cT-bc\partial c+b\gamma^{2}+\gamma G\right),

(2.2)

\displaystyle G=\Lambda^{\alpha}d_{\alpha}+\psi^{m}\Pi_{m}+w_{\alpha}\partial\theta^{\alpha}+\frac{1}{2}(\Lambda\gamma_{m}\Lambda)\psi^{m}

(2.3)

where
T
is the stress-energy tensor given by

\displaystyle T=-\frac{1}{2}\Pi_{m}\Pi^{m}-d_{\alpha}\partial\theta^{\alpha}-\frac{1}{2}\psi_{m}\partial\psi^{m}-w_{\alpha}\partial\Lambda^{\alpha}-\frac{3}{2}\beta\partial\gamma-\frac{1}{2}\gamma\partial\beta-2b\partial c+c\partial b,

(2.4)

and

\displaystyle\Pi^{m}=\partial X^{m}+\frac{1}{2}(\theta\gamma^{m}\partial\theta),

(2.5)

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}=p_{\alpha}-\frac{1}{2}(\gamma^{m}\theta)_{\alpha}\partial X_{m}-\frac{1}{8}(\gamma^{m}\theta)_{\alpha}(\theta\gamma_{m}\partial\theta).

(2.6)

Although to achieve nilpotency of the BRST charge one needs to add non-minimal bosonic variables as is discussed in
[
8
]
, our construction of massless vertex operators does not require these non-minimal variables so they are ognored in this paper.

The integrated vertex operator
V
for the massless states is given by

\displaystyle V=\partial\theta^{\alpha}A_{\alpha}+\Pi^{m}A_{m}+d_{\alpha}W^{\alpha}+\frac{1}{2}N^{mn}F_{mn}-\psi^{m}w_{\alpha}\partial_{m}W^{\alpha},

(2.7)

where
N^{mn}=-\psi^{m}\psi^{n}+\frac{1}{2}(\Lambda\gamma^{mn}w)
. The superfields
(A_{\alpha},A_{m},W^{\alpha},F_{mn}=\partial_{[m}A_{n]})
are constrained as

\displaystyle D_{\alpha}A_{\beta}+D_{\beta}A_{\alpha}=\gamma^{m}_{\alpha\beta}A_{m},

(2.8)

\displaystyle D_{\alpha}A_{m}-\partial_{m}A_{\alpha}=(\gamma_{m})_{\alpha\beta}W^{\beta},

(2.9)

\displaystyle\partial_{m}A_{n}-\partial_{n}A_{m}=F_{mn}.

(2.10)

It can be shown that

\displaystyle\partial^{m}F_{mn}=0,\quad\gamma^{m}_{\alpha\beta}\partial_{m}W^{\beta}=0,

(2.11)

then the theta independent components of
A_{m}
and
W^{\alpha}
describe the photon and the photino. We would like to have a relation like (
1.4
) so we obtain the unintegrated vertex operator. In the Lorentz gauge

\displaystyle\partial^{m}A_{m}=0,

(2.12)

the integrated vertex oprerator
V
is primary, that is

\displaystyle T(y)V(z)\to\frac{V(z)}{(y-z)^{2}}+\frac{\partial V(z)}{(y-z)}.

(2.13)

We now prove that there exists an unintegrated vertex operator
U
that satisfies
QV=\partial U
, just like in the bosonic case of (
1.4
). First we note that

\displaystyle G(y)V(z)\to\frac{u(z)}{(y-z)^{2}}+\frac{\partial u(z)}{(y-z)},

(2.14)

where
u
is a conformal weight
\frac{1}{2}
world-sheet field an it is given by

\displaystyle u=\Lambda^{\alpha}A_{\alpha}-\psi^{m}A_{m}-w_{\alpha}W^{\alpha}.

(2.15)

Finally we obtain that the unintegrated vertex operator satisfying
QV=\partial U
is

\displaystyle U=cV+\gamma u.

(2.16)

Consider the type II case. The world-sheet action and the BRST charge are obtained by performing a similarity transformation on the RNS system together with the free field conjugate pairs
(\theta^{\alpha},p_{\alpha}),(\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}},\overline{p}_{\overline{\alpha}})
and
(\Lambda^{\alpha},w_{\alpha}),(\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}},\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}})
. Here
\alpha=1,\dots 16
,
(\theta^{\alpha},\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}})
are the
N=2
superspace coordinates in ten dimensions. Recall that the RNS world-sheet fields are the space-time coordinates
X^{m}
and their supersymmetry partners
(\psi^{m},\overline{\psi}^{m})
together with superreparametrization ghosts
(b,c,\beta,\gamma),(\overline{b},\overline{c},\overline{\beta},\overline{\gamma})
. The action is given by

\displaystyle S=\int d^{2}z~

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{2}\partial X_{m}\overline{\partial}X^{m}+\frac{1}{2}\psi_{m}\overline{\partial}\psi^{m}+\frac{1}{2}\overline{\psi}_{m}\partial\overline{\psi}^{m}+b\overline{\partial}c+\overline{b}\partial\overline{c}+\beta\overline{\partial}\gamma+\overline{\beta}\partial\overline{\gamma}\right.

(2.17)

\displaystyle+\left.p_{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\theta^{\alpha}+\overline{p}_{\overline{\alpha}}\partial\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}+w_{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\Lambda^{\alpha}+\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\partial\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}\right),

(2.18)

where the unconstrained bosonic variables
(w,\Lambda),(\overline{w},\overline{\Lambda})
are in untwisted case so that
(\lambda,w)
have conformal weights
(\frac{1}{2},0)
and
(\overline{\lambda},\overline{w})
have conformal weights
(0,\frac{1}{2})
. The BRST charges are given by

\displaystyle Q_{L}=\oint dz\left(cT-bc\partial c+b\gamma^{2}+\gamma G_{L}\right),

(2.19)

\displaystyle G_{L}=\Lambda^{\alpha}d_{\alpha}+\psi^{m}\Pi_{m}+w_{\alpha}\partial\theta^{\alpha}+\frac{1}{2}(\Lambda\gamma_{m}\Lambda)\psi^{m},

(2.20)

\displaystyle Q_{R}=\oint d\overline{z}\left(\overline{c}\overline{T}-\overline{b}\overline{c}\overline{\partial}\overline{c}+\overline{b}\overline{\gamma}^{2}+\overline{\gamma}G_{R}\right),

(2.21)

\displaystyle G_{R}=\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}+\overline{\psi}^{m}\overline{\Pi}_{m}+\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\frac{1}{2}(\overline{\Lambda}\gamma_{m}\overline{\Lambda})\overline{\psi}^{m},

(2.22)

where
(T,\overline{T})
are the stress-energy tensor components and are given by

\displaystyle T=-\frac{1}{2}\Pi_{m}\Pi^{m}-d_{\alpha}\partial\theta^{\alpha}-\frac{1}{2}\psi_{m}\partial\psi^{m}-\frac{1}{2}w_{\alpha}\partial\Lambda^{\alpha}-\frac{1}{2}\Lambda^{\alpha}\partial w_{\alpha}-\frac{3}{2}\beta\partial\gamma-\frac{1}{2}\gamma\partial\beta-2b\partial c+c\partial b,

(2.23)

\displaystyle\overline{T}=-\frac{1}{2}\overline{\Pi}_{m}\overline{\Pi}^{m}-\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}-\frac{1}{2}\overline{\psi}_{m}\overline{\partial}\overline{\psi}^{m}-\frac{1}{2}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\overline{\partial}\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}-\frac{1}{2}\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}\overline{\partial}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}-\frac{3}{2}\overline{\beta}\overline{\partial}\overline{\gamma}-\frac{1}{2}\overline{\gamma}\overline{\partial}\overline{\beta}-2\overline{b}\overline{\partial}\overline{c}+\overline{c}\overline{\partial}\overline{b},

(2.24)

and

\displaystyle\Pi^{m}=\partial X^{m}+\frac{1}{2}(\theta\gamma^{m}\partial\theta),\quad\overline{\Pi}^{m}=\overline{\partial}X^{m}+\frac{1}{2}(\overline{\theta}\gamma^{m}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}),

(2.25)

\displaystyle d_{\alpha}=p_{\alpha}-\frac{1}{2}(\gamma^{m}\theta)_{\alpha}\partial X_{m}-\frac{1}{8}(\gamma^{m}\theta)_{\alpha}(\theta\gamma_{m}\partial\theta),

(2.26)

\displaystyle\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}=\overline{p}_{\overline{\alpha}}-\frac{1}{2}(\gamma^{m}\overline{\theta})_{\overline{\alpha}}\overline{\partial}X_{m}-\frac{1}{8}(\gamma^{m}\overline{\theta})_{\overline{\alpha}}(\overline{\theta}\gamma_{m}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}).

(2.27)

The integrated vertex operator
V
for the massless states can be obtained from the left-right product of
V_{L}\otimes V_{R}
where
V_{L}
and
V_{R}
have the form of (
2.7
). The integrated vertex becomes

\displaystyle V=

\displaystyle\left[\partial\theta^{\alpha}A_{\alpha}+\Pi^{m}A_{m}+d_{\alpha}W^{\alpha}+\frac{1}{2}N^{mn}F_{mn}-\psi^{m}w_{\alpha}\partial_{m}W^{\alpha}\right]\otimes

(2.28)

\displaystyle\left[\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}\widetilde{A}_{\overline{\alpha}}+\overline{\Pi}^{m}\widetilde{A}_{m}+\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}\widetilde{W}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\frac{1}{2}\overline{N}^{mn}\widetilde{F}_{mn}-\overline{\psi}^{m}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\partial_{m}\widetilde{W}^{\overline{\alpha}}\right]

(2.29)

\displaystyle\equiv\partial\theta^{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}A_{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}+\Pi^{m}\overline{\Pi}^{n}A_{mn}+d_{\alpha}\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}P^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}+N^{mn}\overline{N}^{pq}S_{mnpq}+\psi^{m}w_{\alpha}\overline{\psi}^{n}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}U_{mn}{}^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}

(2.30)

\displaystyle+\partial\theta^{\alpha}\overline{\Pi}^{m}A_{\alpha m}+\Pi^{m}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}A_{m\overline{\alpha}}+\partial\theta^{\alpha}\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}E_{\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+d_{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}E_{\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}

(2.31)

\displaystyle+\partial\theta^{\alpha}\overline{N}^{mn}\widehat{\Omega}_{\alpha mn}+N^{mn}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}\Omega_{\overline{\alpha}mn}+\partial\theta^{\alpha}\overline{\psi}^{m}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\widehat{C}_{m\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{m}w_{\alpha}\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}C_{m\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}

(2.32)

\displaystyle+\Pi^{m}\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+d_{\alpha}\overline{\Pi}^{m}E_{m}{}^{\alpha}+\Pi^{m}\overline{N}^{np}\widehat{\Omega}_{mnp}+N^{np}\overline{\Pi}^{m}\Omega_{mnp}

(2.33)

\displaystyle+\Pi^{m}\overline{\psi}^{n}\bar{\omega}_{\overline{\alpha}}C_{mn}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{n}w_{\alpha}\overline{\Pi}^{m}C_{mn}{}^{\alpha}+d_{\alpha}\overline{N}^{mn}\widehat{D}_{mn}{}^{\alpha}+N^{mn}\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}D_{mn}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}

(2.34)

\displaystyle+d_{\alpha}\overline{\psi}^{m}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}V_{m}{}^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{m}w_{\alpha}\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}\widehat{V}_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}\alpha}+N^{mn}\overline{\psi}^{p}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}Y_{mnp}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{p}w_{\alpha}\overline{N}^{mn}\widehat{Y}_{mnp}{}^{\alpha}.

(2.35)

The superfields in
V
satisfy the constraint equations

\displaystyle D_{(\alpha}A_{\beta)\overline{\alpha}}=-\gamma^{m}_{\alpha\beta}A_{m\overline{\alpha}},\quad D_{\alpha}A_{m\overline{\alpha}}+\partial_{m}A_{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}=-(\gamma_{m})_{\alpha\beta}E_{\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\beta},\quad\Omega_{\overline{\alpha}mn}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[m}A_{n]\overline{\alpha}},

(2.36)

\displaystyle D_{(\alpha}A_{\beta)m}=\gamma^{n}_{\alpha\beta}A_{nm},\quad D_{\alpha}A_{nm}-\partial_{n}A_{\alpha m}=(\gamma_{n})_{\alpha\beta}{}E_{m}{}^{\beta},\quad\Omega_{mnp}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[n}A_{p]m},

(2.37)

\displaystyle D_{(\alpha}E_{\beta)}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=-\gamma^{m}_{\alpha\beta}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}},\quad D_{\alpha}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\partial_{m}E_{\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=-(\gamma_{m})_{\alpha\beta}P^{\beta\overline{\alpha}},\quad D_{mn}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[m}E_{n]}{}^{\overline{\alpha}},

(2.38)

\displaystyle D_{(\alpha}\widehat{\Omega}_{\beta)mn}=\gamma^{p}_{\alpha\beta}\widehat{\Omega}_{pmn},\quad D_{\alpha}\widehat{\Omega}_{pmn}-\partial_{p}\widehat{\Omega}_{\alpha mn}=(\gamma_{p})_{\alpha\beta}\widehat{D}_{mn}{}^{\beta},\quad S_{pqmn}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[p}\widehat{\Omega}_{q]mn},

(2.39)

and

\displaystyle D_{(\overline{\alpha}}A_{\alpha\overline{\beta})}=\gamma^{m}_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}A_{\alpha m},\quad D_{\overline{\alpha}}A_{\alpha m}-\partial_{m}A_{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}=(\gamma_{m})_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}E_{\alpha}{}^{\overline{\beta}},\quad\widehat{\Omega}_{\alpha mn}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[m}A_{\alpha n]},

(2.40)

\displaystyle D_{(\overline{\alpha}}A_{m\overline{\beta})}=\gamma^{n}_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}A_{mn},\quad D_{\overline{\alpha}}A_{mn}-\partial_{n}A_{m\overline{\alpha}}=(\gamma_{n})_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\beta}},\quad\widehat{\Omega}_{mnp}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[n}A_{mp]},

(2.41)

\displaystyle D_{(\overline{\alpha}}E_{\overline{\beta})}{}^{\alpha}=\gamma^{m}_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}E_{m}{}^{\alpha},\quad D_{\overline{\alpha}}E_{m}{}^{\alpha}-\partial_{m}E_{\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}=(\gamma_{m})_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}P^{\alpha\overline{\beta}},\quad\widehat{D}_{mn}{}^{\alpha}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[m}E_{n]}{}^{\alpha},

(2.42)

\displaystyle D_{(\overline{\alpha}}\Omega_{\overline{\beta})mn}=\gamma^{p}_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}\Omega_{pmn},\quad D_{\overline{\alpha}}\Omega_{pmn}-\partial_{p}\Omega_{\overline{\alpha}mn}=(\gamma_{p})_{\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}}D_{mn}{}^{\overline{\beta}},\quad S_{mnpq}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{[p}\Omega_{q]mn},

(2.43)

and finally

\displaystyle C_{m\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}+\partial_{m}E_{\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}=C_{nm}{}^{\alpha}+\partial_{m}E_{n}{}^{\alpha}=\widehat{V}_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}\alpha}+\partial_{m}P^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}=\widehat{Y}_{npm}{}^{\alpha}+\partial_{m}\widehat{D}_{np}{}^{\alpha}=0,

(2.44)

\displaystyle\widehat{C}_{m\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\partial_{m}E_{\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=C_{nm}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\partial_{m}E_{n}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=V_{m}{}^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}+\partial_{m}P^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}=Y_{npm}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\partial_{m}D_{np}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=0,

(2.45)

\displaystyle U_{mn}{}^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}-\partial_{m}\partial_{n}P^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}=0.

(2.46)

As in the open string case, the gauge-fixing conditions

\displaystyle\partial^{m}A_{m\overline{\alpha}}=\partial^{m}A_{mn}=\partial^{m}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=\partial^{m}\widehat{\Omega}_{mnp}=\partial^{m}C_{mn}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}=0,

(2.47)

\displaystyle\partial^{m}A_{\alpha m}=\partial^{m}A_{nm}=\partial^{m}E_{m}{}^{\alpha}=\partial^{m}\Omega_{mnp}=\partial^{m}C_{mn}{}^{\alpha}=0,

(2.48)

help to set the integrated vertex operator
V
to be a primary world-sheet field. That is,

\displaystyle T(y)V(z,\overline{z})\to\frac{V(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)^{2}}+\frac{\partial V(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)},

(2.49)

\displaystyle\overline{T}(\overline{y})V(z,\overline{z})\to\frac{V(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})^{2}}+\frac{\overline{\partial}V(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})}.

(2.50)

Our purpose is to prove that we have vertex operators satisfying the equations like (
1.8
), that is

\displaystyle QV=\overline{\partial}W-\partial\overline{W},\quad QW=\partial U,\quad Q\overline{W}=\overline{\partial}U.

(2.51)

Given the integrated vertex operator
V
we first note that

\displaystyle G_{L}(y)V(z,\overline{z})\to\frac{w_{L}(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)^{2}}+\frac{\partial w_{L}(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)},

(2.52)

\displaystyle G_{R}(\overline{y})V(z,\overline{z})\to\frac{w_{R}(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})^{2}}+\frac{\overline{\partial}w_{R}(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})},

(2.53)

where

\displaystyle w_{L}=\overline{\partial}\overline{\theta}^{\overline{\alpha}}\left(\Lambda^{\alpha}A_{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{m}A_{m\overline{\alpha}}-w_{\alpha}E_{\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}\right)+\overline{\Pi}^{m}\left(\Lambda^{\alpha}A_{\alpha m}-\psi^{n}A_{nm}-w_{\alpha}E_{m}{}^{\alpha}\right)

(2.54)

\displaystyle+\overline{d}_{\overline{\alpha}}\left(\Lambda^{\alpha}E_{\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{m}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}-w_{\alpha}P^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}\right)+\overline{N}^{mn}\left(\Lambda^{\alpha}\widehat{\Omega}_{\alpha mn}-\psi^{p}\widehat{\Omega}_{pmn}-w_{\alpha}\widehat{D}_{mn}{}^{\alpha}\right)

(2.55)

\displaystyle+\overline{\psi}^{m}\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\left(\Lambda^{\alpha}\widehat{C}_{m\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}+\psi^{n}C_{nm}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}-w_{\alpha}V_{m}{}^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}\right),

(2.56)

\displaystyle w_{R}=\partial\theta^{\alpha}\left(-\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}A_{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}+\overline{\psi}^{m}A_{\alpha m}+\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}E_{\alpha}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}\right)+\Pi^{m}\left(\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}A_{m\overline{\alpha}}-\overline{\psi}^{n}A_{mn}-\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}E_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}\right)

(2.57)

\displaystyle+d_{\alpha}\left(-\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}E_{\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}+\overline{\psi}^{m}E_{m}{}^{\alpha}+\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}P^{\alpha\overline{\alpha}}\right)+N^{mn}\left(\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}\Omega_{\overline{\alpha}mn}-\overline{\psi}^{p}\Omega_{pmn}-\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}D_{mn}{}^{\overline{\alpha}}\right)

(2.58)

\displaystyle+\psi^{m}w_{\alpha}\left(-\overline{\Lambda}^{\overline{\alpha}}C_{m\overline{\alpha}}{}^{\alpha}+\overline{\psi}^{n}C_{nm}{}^{\alpha}+\overline{w}_{\overline{\alpha}}\widehat{V}_{m}{}^{\overline{\alpha}\alpha}\right).

(2.59)

Using these OPE’s, the BRST transformation of the integrated vertex operator
V
becomes

\displaystyle QV=\overline{\partial}W-\partial\overline{W},

(2.60)

with

\displaystyle W=\overline{c}V+\overline{\gamma}w_{R},\quad\overline{W}=-cV-\gamma w_{L}.

(2.61)

In order to obtain the relations in (
2.51
) we prove now that

\displaystyle QW=\partial U,\quad Q\overline{W}=\overline{\partial}U.

(2.62)

Since
G_{L}(y)G_{L}(z)\to-T(z)/(y-z)
and
G_{R}(\overline{y})G_{R}(\overline{z})\to-\overline{T}/(\overline{y}-\overline{z})
we obtain

\displaystyle G_{L}(y)w_{L}(z,\overline{z})\to-\frac{V(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)},\quad G_{R}(\overline{y})w_{R}(z,\overline{z})\to-\frac{V(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})}.

(2.63)

To obtain
QW
and
Q\overline{W}
we also need
G_{L}(y)w_{R}(z,\overline{z})
and
G_{R}(\overline{y})w_{L}(z,\overline{z})
. They are

\displaystyle G_{L}(y)w_{R}(z,\overline{z})\to\frac{u(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)^{2}}+\frac{\partial u(z,\overline{z})}{(y-z)},

(2.64)

\displaystyle G_{R}(\overline{y})w_{L}(z,\overline{z})\to-\frac{u(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})^{2}}-\frac{\overline{\partial}u(z,\overline{z})}{(\overline{y}-\overline{z})},
