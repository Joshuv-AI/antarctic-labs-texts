A New Class of Relations for Homogeneous Symmetric Polynomials
Boris Y. Rubinstein

Abstract
Recently we introduced a new class of relations for Bernoulli symmetric polynomials. This manuscript shows that these relations are valid for arbitrary homogeneous symmetric polynomial. Analysis of these relations leads to the discovery of a new type of nonlinear relations for the Bernoulli numbers.



Keywords
: integer partitions, homogeneous symmetric polynomials.

2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
: 11P82.

1
Introduction

Recently
[
9
]
we established existence of a new class of relations for the scalar partitions.
Their derivation is built on the
approach of J.J. Sylvester
[
10
, Lecture 5]

to the reduction of vector partition into the scalar ones and the
corresponding theorem of A. Cayley for double partitions
[
3
]
.
Based on these findings we conjectured the following relation

\frac{B_{k}({\bf x}^{m})}{\pi({\bf x}^{m})}-\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{y_{i}^{m-k-1}B_{k}({\bf s}^{m}_{i})}{\pi({\bf s}^{m}_{i})}=0,\quad 0\leq k\leq m-1,

(1)

where
B_{k}({\bf x}^{m})
denote the Bernoulli polynomials of higher order
[
2
,
5
]
,

{\bf x}^{m},~{\bf y}^{m}
are two sets of independent variables
x_{i},~y_{i}
respectively,
and
{\bf s}^{m}_{i}
are the rows of the

m\times m
matrix
{\bf S}({\bf x}^{m},{\bf y}^{m})
with the elements

s^{m}_{i,j}=s^{m}_{i,j}({\bf x}^{m},{\bf y}^{m})
defined by

s^{m}_{i,j}=y_{i}x_{j}-y_{j}x_{i}+x_{i}\delta_{ij},\ (1\leq j\leq m).

(2)

The direct computation shows that the relation (
1
)
holds for
m\leq 6
.

In this manuscript we show that this relation is valid for the whole class of
homogeneous symmetric polynomials that can be expressed by the complete Bell polynomials.
We then generalize it to
arbitrary
homogeneous symmetric polynomials.
In addition to the relation (
1
) we establish a weaker result valid
for
k\geq m
when all
y_{i}=1
. We also report a discovery of a new type of
nonlinear relations for the Bernoulli numbers.

Bernoulli polynomials as complete Bell polynomials

Recall the generating functions
G_{B}(s,t)

Use

(6)

The expression for
