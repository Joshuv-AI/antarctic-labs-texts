28 C.F.R. § 802.21 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:28:802.21
Creator: Court Services And Offender Supervision Agency For The District Of Columbia
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-802.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 802 (Disclosure Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may appeal a denial of a request for an accounting to the Office of the General Counsel in the same manner as a denial of a request for access to records (See § 802.16) and the same procedures will be followed.
