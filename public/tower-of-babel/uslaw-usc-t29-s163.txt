29 U.S.C. § 163 — Right to strike preserved
Citation: us:usc:29:163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section163
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 7 (Labor-Management Relations); Subchapter II (National Labor Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter, except as specifically provided for herein, shall be construed so as either to interfere with or impede or diminish in any way the right to strike, or to affect the limitations or qualifications on that right.
