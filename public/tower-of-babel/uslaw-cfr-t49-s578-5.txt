49 C.F.R. § 578.5 — Inflationary adjustment of civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:49:578.5
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-578.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 578 (Civil And Criminal Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The civil penalties set forth in this part continue in effect until adjusted by the Administrator. The Administrator shall review the amount of these civil penalties annually and will, if appropriate, adjust them by rule.
