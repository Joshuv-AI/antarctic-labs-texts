42 C.F.R. § 431.713 — Continuing study and investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:42:431.713
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-431.713
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 431 (State Organization And General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency or board must conduct a continuing study of nursing homes and administrators within the State to improve—
(a) Licensing standards; and
(b) The procedures and methods for enforcing the standards.
