A Simple Deterministic Reduction From Gomory-Hu Tree to Maxflow and Expander Decomposition
Maximilian Probst Gutenberg, Weixuan Yuan

Abstract
Given an undirected graph G G , a Gomory-Hu tree is a tree over the vertex set of G G that preserves all pairwise mincuts in G G . After various breakthroughs, the recent algorithm from [ KPYY25 ] gives a simple reduction to the maxflow problem. For unweighted m m -edge graphs, the reduction requires O ~ ​ ( m ) \tilde{O}(m) randomized time and reduces to maxflow instances of total size O ~ ​ ( m ) \tilde{O}(m) . This is the first reduction that is tight up to polylogarithmic factors. We de-randomize the above state-of-the-art result by presenting a simple deterministic reduction from Gomory-Hu tree to maxflow and expander decomposition. Our reduction again requires only O ~ ​ ( m ) \tilde{O}(m) time and reduces to maxflow and expander decomposition on instances of size O ~ ​ ( m ) \tilde{O}(m) . Our result implies that deterministic maxflow and expander decomposition with O ~ ​ ( m ) \tilde{O}(m) time directly yield an O ~ ​ ( m ) \tilde{O}(m) time deterministic algorithm for Gomory-Hu trees in unweighted graphs. Using state-of-the-art randomized expander decomposition algorithms, our reduction retains the guarantees of the reduction in [ KPYY25 ] up to polylogarithmic factors.

Gomory-Hu tree

In this section, we give a simple and deterministic reduction of (approximate) Gomory-Hu tree to expander decomposition and maxflow.
For unweighted graphs, we match the rates of state-of-the-art Gomory-Hu tree algorithms for both deterministic and randomized cases, i.e.,
\tilde{O}(1)

\tilde{O}(m)

For weighted graphs, one variant of our algorithm gives a deterministic reduction of

-approximate Gomory-Hu tree to expander decomposition and maxflow, leading to a near-linear time reduction to polylog maxflows if we adopt the near-linear time expander decomposition algorithm.

Following the standard approach, we aim to find a decomposition of

1.

Let

Our major modification of

]

When
