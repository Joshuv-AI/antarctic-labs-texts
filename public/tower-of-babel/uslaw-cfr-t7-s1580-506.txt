7 C.F.R. § 1580.506 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1580.506
Creator: Foreign Agricultural Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1580.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1580 (Trade Adjustment Assistance For Farmers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any producer aggrieved by a final agency determination under this part may appeal to the U.S. Court of International Trade for a review of such determination in accordance with its rules and procedures.
