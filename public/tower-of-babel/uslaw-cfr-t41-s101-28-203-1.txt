41 C.F.R. § 101-28.203-1 — -28.203-1 Government storage activity.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-28.203-1
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-28.203-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-28 (28—Storage And Distribution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Government activity or facility utilized for the receipt, storage, and issue of supplies, materials, and equipment, including storage of reserve or excess stocks or intransit storage. The activity may be either Government owned or leased, and it may be either Government operated or contract operated.
