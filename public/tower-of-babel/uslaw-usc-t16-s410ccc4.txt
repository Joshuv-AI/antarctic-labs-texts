16 U.S.C. § 410ccc–4 — General management plan
Citation: us:usc:16:410ccc–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410ccc–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–AA (Cane River Creole National Historical Park And National Heritage Area); Part A (Cane River Creole National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within 3 years after the date funds are made available therefor and in consultation with the Cane River Heritage Area Commission,1 the National Park Service shall prepare a general management plan for the historical park. The plan shall include, but need not be limited to—
(1)
a visitor use plan indicating programs and facilities that will be provided for public use, including the location and cost of an interpretive center;
(2)
programs and management actions that the National Park Service will undertake cooperatively with the heritage area commission,2 including preservation treatments for important sites, structures, objects, and research materials. Planning shall address educational media, roadway signing, and brochures that could be coordinated with the Commission pursuant to section 410ccc–23 of this title; and
(3)
preservation and use plans for any sites and structures that are identified for National Park Service involvement through cooperative agreements.
