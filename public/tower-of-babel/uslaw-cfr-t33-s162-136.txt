33 C.F.R. § 162.136 — Connecting waters from Lake Huron to Lake Erie; anchorage grounds.
Citation: us:cfr:33:162.136
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-162.136
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 162 (Inland Waterways Navigation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In the Detroit River, vessels shall be anchored so as not to swing into the channel or across steering courses.
(b) In the St. Clair River, vessels shall be anchored so as not to swing into the channel or across steering courses.
