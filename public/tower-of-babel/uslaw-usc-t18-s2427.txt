18 U.S.C. § 2427 — Inclusion of offenses relating to child pornography in definition of sexual activity for which any person can be charged with a criminal offense
Citation: us:usc:18:2427
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2427
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 117 (Transportation For Illegal Sexual Activity And Related Crimes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “sexual activity for which any person can be charged with a criminal offense” does not require interpersonal physical contact, and includes the production of child pornography, as defined in section 2256(8).
