14 C.F.R. § 63.19 — Operations during physical deficiency.
Citation: us:cfr:14:63.19
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-63.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 63 (Certification: Flight Crewmembers Other Than Pilots). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may serve as a flight engineer or flight navigator during a period of known physical deficiency, or increase in physical deficiency, that would make him unable to meet the physical requirements for his current medical certificate.
