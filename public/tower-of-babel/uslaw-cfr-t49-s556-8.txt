49 C.F.R. § 556.8 — Rescission of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:49:556.8
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-556.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 556 (Exemption For Inconsequential Defect Or Noncompliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may rescind a grant or denial of an exemption issued under this part any time after the receipt of new data and notice and opportunity for comment thereon, in accordance with §§ 556.5 and 556.7.
