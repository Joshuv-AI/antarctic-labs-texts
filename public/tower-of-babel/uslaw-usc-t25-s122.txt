25 U.S.C. § 122 — Limitation on application of tribal funds
Citation: us:usc:25:122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section122
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter II (Disbursement Of Moneys And Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds belonging to any Indian tribe with which treaty relations exist shall be applied in any manner not authorized by such treaty, or by express provisions of law; nor shall money appropriated to execute a treaty be transferred or applied to any other purpose, unless expressly authorized by law.
