45 C.F.R. § 91.17 — Special benefits for children and the elderly.
Citation: us:cfr:45:91.17
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-91.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 91 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Hhs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient operating a program or activity provides special benefits to the elderly or to children, such use of age distinctions shall be presumed to be necessary to the normal operation of the program or activity, notwithstanding the provisions of § 91.13.
