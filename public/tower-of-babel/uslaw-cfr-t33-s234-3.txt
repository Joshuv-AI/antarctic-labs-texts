33 C.F.R. § 234.3 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:33:234.3
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-234.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 234 (Corps Of Engineers Agency Specific Procedures To Implement The Principles, Requirements And Guidelines For Federal Investments In Water Resources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exceptions to any requirements or policy contained in this part may be requested by the Corps or the non-Federal interest or responsible Tribal, State, or local government. Exceptions must be requested in writing and will be reviewed for a decision by the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Civil Works.
