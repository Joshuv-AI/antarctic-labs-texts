15 C.F.R. § 270.204 — Provision of additional resources and services needed by a Team.
Citation: us:cfr:15:270.204
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-270.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 270 (National Construction Safety Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director will determine the appropriate resources that a Team will require to carry out its investigation and will ensure that those resources are available to the Team.
