5 C.F.R. § 844.303 — Minimum disability annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:5:844.303
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-844.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 844 (Federal Employees' Retirement System—Disability Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, an annuity payable under this part cannot be less than the amount of an annuity computed under 5 U.S.C. 8415 (excluding subsection (f) of that section) based on the annuitant's service.
