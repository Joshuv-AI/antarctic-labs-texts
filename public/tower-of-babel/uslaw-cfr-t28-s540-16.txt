28 C.F.R. § 540.16 — Inmate correspondence while in segregation and holdover status.
Citation: us:cfr:28:540.16
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-540.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 540 (Contact With Persons In The Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Warden shall permit an inmate in holdover status (i.e., enroute to a designated institution) to have correspondence privileges similar to those of other inmates insofar as practical.
(b) The Warden shall permit an inmate in segregation to have full correspondence privileges unless placed on restricted general correspondence under § 540.15.
