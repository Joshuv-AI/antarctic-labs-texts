42 U.S.C. § 9873 — Payments under allotments to States
Citation: us:usc:42:9873
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9873
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Planning And Development Of Dependent Care Programs And For Other Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall make payments, as provided by section 6503(a) of title 31, to each State from its allotments under section 9872 of this title from amounts appropriated under section 9871 of this title.
