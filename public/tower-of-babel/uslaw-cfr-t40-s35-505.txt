40 C.F.R. § 35.505 — Components of a complete application.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.505
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A complete application for an environmental program grant must:
(a) Meet the requirements in 2 CFR part 200, subpart C.
(b) Include a proposed work plan (§ 35.507 of this subpart); and
(c) Specify the environmental program and the amount of funds requested.
