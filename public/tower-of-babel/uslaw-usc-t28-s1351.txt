28 U.S.C. § 1351 — Consuls, vice consuls, and members of a diplomatic mission as defendant
Citation: us:usc:28:1351
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1351
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 85 (District Courts; Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts shall have original jurisdiction, exclusive of the courts of the States, of all civil actions and proceedings against—
(1)
consuls or vice consuls of foreign states; or
(2)
members of a mission or members of their families (as such terms are defined in section 2 of the Diplomatic Relations Act).
