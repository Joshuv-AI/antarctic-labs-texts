14 C.F.R. § 258.4 — Unfair and deceptive practice.
Citation: us:cfr:14:258.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-258.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 258 (Disclosure Of Change-Of-Gauge Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The holding out or sale of scheduled passenger air transportation that involves change-of-gauge service is prohibited as an unfair or deceptive practice or an unfair method of competition within the meaning of 49 U.S.C. 41712 unless, in conjunction with such holding out or sale, carriers and ticket agents follow the requirements of this part.
