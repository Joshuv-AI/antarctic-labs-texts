Kolmogorov widths of an intersection of anisotropic finite-dimensional balls: case 2 ≤ q j < ∞ 2\leq q_{j}<\infty
A.A. Vasil’eva

Abstract
Order estimates for the Kolmogorov n n -widths of ∩ α ∈ A ν α B k ¯ p ¯ \cap_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}B^{\overline{k}}_{\overline{p}} in l q ¯ k ¯ l^{\overline{k}}_{\overline{q}} are obtained; here q ¯ = ( q 1 , … , q d ) \overline{q}=(q_{1},\,\dots,\,q_{d}) , 2 ⩽ q j < ∞ 2\leqslant q_{j}<\infty , j = 1 , … , d j=1,\,\dots,\,d .

Definition 2
.

Let
Z\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}
,
1\leqslant m\leqslant d+1

if there is a set

such that one of the following conditions holds:

Z=\{(x_{1},\,\dots,\,x_{d})\in\mathbb{R}^{d}:\;x_{i}=1/q_{i},\;i\in I\};

\#I=m-1

3.

,
\#I=m

Z=\{(x_{1},\,\dots,\,x_{d})\in\mathbb{R}^{d}:\;\omega^{\prime}_{1/x_{i},q_{i}}=\omega^{\prime}_{1/x_{l},q_{l}}\in(0,\,1)\;\forall i,\,l\in I:\;q_{i}>2,\,q_{l}>2;

The plane

has codimension
