12 C.F.R. § 266.4 — Suspension of appearance privilege.
Citation: us:cfr:12:266.4
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-266.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 266 (Limitations On Activities Of Former Members And Employees Of The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any person knowingly and willfully fails to comply with the provisions of this part, the Board may decline to permit such person to appear personally before it or a Federal Reserve Bank for such periods of time as it may determine and may impose such other sanctions as the Board may deem just and proper.
