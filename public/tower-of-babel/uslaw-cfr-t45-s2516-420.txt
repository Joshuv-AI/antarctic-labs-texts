45 C.F.R. § 2516.420 — What must an LEA, local partnership, qualified organization or other eligible entity include in an application for a subgrant?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.420
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to apply for a subgrant from a State, Indian Tribe, or community-based entity under this part, an applicant must include the information required by the Corporation grantee.
