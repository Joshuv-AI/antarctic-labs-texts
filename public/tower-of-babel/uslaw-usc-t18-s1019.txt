18 U.S.C. § 1019 — Certificates by consular officers
Citation: us:usc:18:1019
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1019
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 47 (Fraud And False Statements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a consul, or vice consul, or other person employed in the consular service of the United States, knowingly certifies falsely to any invoice, or other paper, to which his certificate is authorized or required by law, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.
