46 U.S.C. § 30106 — Time limit on bringing maritime action for personal injury or death
Citation: us:usc:46:30106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 301 (General Liability Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by law, a civil action for damages for personal injury or death arising out of a maritime tort must be brought within 3 years after the cause of action arose.
