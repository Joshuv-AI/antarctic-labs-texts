10 U.S.C. § 8018 — Administrative Assistant
Citation: us:usc:10:8018
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8018
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 803 (Department Of The Navy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may appoint an Administrative Assistant in the Office of the Secretary of the Navy. The Administrative Assistant shall perform such duties as the Secretary may prescribe.
