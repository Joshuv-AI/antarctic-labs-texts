14 C.F.R. § 222.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:222.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-222.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 222 (Intermodal Cargo Services By Foreign Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all air transportation of property that includes both air movement by a direct foreign air carrier and surface transportation to or from any point within the United States (hereafter referred to as “intermodal cargo services”).
