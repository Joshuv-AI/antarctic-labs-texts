15 U.S.C. § 1665e — Consideration of ability to repay
Citation: us:usc:15:1665e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1665e
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part C (Credit Advertising And Limits On Credit Card Fees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A card issuer may not open any credit card account for any consumer under an open end consumer credit plan, or increase any credit limit applicable to such account, unless the card issuer considers the ability of the consumer to make the required payments under the terms of such account.
