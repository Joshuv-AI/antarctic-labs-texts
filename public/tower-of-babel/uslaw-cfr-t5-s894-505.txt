5 C.F.R. § 894.505 — Are retroactive premiums paid with pre-tax dollars (premium conversion)?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.505
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Retroactive premiums are not paid under premium conversion, except when you are changing your enrollment retroactively as a result of birth or adoption of a child. Any additional withholdings for retroactive premiums that are due must be made with after-tax dollars. The Administrator will bill you directly for any retroactive premiums that must be paid with after-tax dollars.
