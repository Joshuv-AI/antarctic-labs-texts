15 C.F.R. § 705.1 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:15:705.1
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-705.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 705 (Effect Of Imported Articles On The National Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
Department means the United States Department of Commerce and includes the Secretary of Commerce and the Secretary's designees.
Secretary means the Secretary of Commerce or the Secretary's designees.
Applicant means the person or entity submitting a request or application for an investigation pursuant to this part.
