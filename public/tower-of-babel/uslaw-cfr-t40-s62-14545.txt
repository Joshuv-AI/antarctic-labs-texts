40 C.F.R. § 62.14545 — What must I include in each notification of achievement of an increment of progress?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14545
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14545
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your notification of achievement of an increment of progress must include the four items specified in paragraphs (a) through (d) of this section.
(a) Notification of the date that the increment of progress has been achieved.
(b) Any items required to be submitted with each increment of progress.
(c) Signature of the owner or operator of the CISWI unit.
(d) The date you were required to complete the increment of progress.
