10 C.F.R. § 61.40 — General requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:10:61.40
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-61.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 61 (Licensing Requirements For Land Disposal Of Radioactive Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Land disposal facilities must be sited, designed, operated, closed, and controlled after closure so that reasonable assurance exists that exposures to humans are within the limits established in the performance objectives in §§ 61.41 through 61.44.
