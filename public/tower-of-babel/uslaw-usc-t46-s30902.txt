46 U.S.C. § 30902 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:46:30902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30902
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “federally-owned corporation” means a corporation in which the United States owns all the outstanding capital stock.
