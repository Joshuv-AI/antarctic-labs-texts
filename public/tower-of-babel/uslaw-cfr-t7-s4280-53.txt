7 C.F.R. § 4280.53 — Loan payments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.53
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Intermediary must make all REDL payments to Rural Development by electronic funds transfer or other means as specified in the loan documents.
