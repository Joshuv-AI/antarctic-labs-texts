46 C.F.R. § 35.35-40 — -40 Conditions under which transfer operations shall not be commenced or if started shall be discontinued—TB/ALL.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-35.35-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 35 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cargo transfer operations shall not be started or, if started, shall be discontinued under the following conditions:
(a) During severe electrical storms.
(b) If a fire occurs on the wharf or on the tanker or in the vicinity.
