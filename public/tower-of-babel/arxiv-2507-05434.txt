Motives of Certain Hyperplane Sections of Milnor Hypersurfaces
Evan Marth

Abstract
We construct a hyperplane section Y Y of a Milnor hypersurface associated to a regular semisimple endomorphism φ \varphi . Exploiting its structure as a hyperplane section of a projective bundle and its natural torus action, we give a motivic decomposition of Y Y , which encodes both the cellular structure of Y Y and the arithmetic of the eigenvalues of φ \varphi . This decomposition is proven without using the “nilpotence principle”, that is to say there are no “phantoms”.
