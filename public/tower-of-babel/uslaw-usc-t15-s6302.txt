15 U.S.C. § 6302 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:15:6302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6302
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 89 (Professional Boxing Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are—
(1)
to improve and expand the system of safety precautions that protects the welfare of professional boxers; and
(2)
to assist State boxing commissions to provide proper oversight for the professional boxing industry in the United States.
