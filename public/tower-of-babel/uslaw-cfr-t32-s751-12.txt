32 C.F.R. § 751.12 — Payments.
Citation: us:cfr:32:751.12
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-751.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 751 (Personnel Claims Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment of approved personnel claims will be made by the Navy or Marine Corps disbursing officer serving the adjudicating authority. Payments will be charged to funds made available to the adjudicating authority for this purpose.
