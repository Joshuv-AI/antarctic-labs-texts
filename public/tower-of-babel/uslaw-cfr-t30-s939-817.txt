30 C.F.R. § 939.817 — Performance standards—underground mining activities.

Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-939.817
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 939 (Rhode Island). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 817 of this chapter, Permanent Program Performance Standards—Underground Mining Activities, shall apply to any person who conducts underground coal mining operations.
