42 U.S.C. § 300b–4 — Public Health Service facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:300b–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300b–4
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IX (Genetic Diseases, Hemophilia Programs, And Sudden Infant Death Syndrome); Part A (Genetic Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a program within the Service to provide voluntary testing, diagnosis, counseling, and treatment of individuals respecting genetic diseases. Services under such program shall be made available through facilities of the Service to persons requesting such services, and the program shall provide appropriate publicity of the availability and voluntary nature of such services.
