The dynamics of the focusing NLH with a potential beyond the mass-energy threshold
Shuang Ji, Jing Lu, Fanfei Meng

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the dynamics of the focusing nonlinear Hartree equation with a Kato potential i ∂ t u + Δ u − V u = − ( | ⋅ | − γ ∗ | u | 2 ) u , x ∈ ℝ d i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-Vu=-(|\cdot|^{-\gamma}\ast|u|^{2})u,\quad x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d} under some assumptions on the potential V V . We prove the blow up versus global existence dichotomy for solutions beyond the threshold, based on the method from Duyckaerts-Roudenko [ 6 ] . Furthermore, our result compensates for the one of in [ 13 ] below that threshold.

Lemma 2.2

For

\displaystyle\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}V|u|^{2}dx\leqslant C_{d}\|V\|_{\mathcal{K}}\|\nabla u\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}.

In particular, if

, we have

\displaystyle(1-C_{d}\|V_{-}\|_{\mathcal{K}})\|\nabla u\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}\leqslant\|\mathcal{H}^{\frac{1}{2}}u\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}\mathcal{H}u\bar{u}dx\leqslant(1+C_{d}\|V\|_{\mathcal{K}})\|\nabla u\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}.

Proof.

We first consider the fundamental solution
K(x)
of the Laplace equation
-\Delta u=0
, that is
\Delta K(x)=\delta(x)
.
Then for the equation
-\Delta u=f
, we have

\displaystyle-\Delta u=-\Delta(-\Delta)^{-1}f=-\Delta(-K\ast f)=-\Delta(-K)\ast f=f.

For
d\geqslant 3
, the fundamental solution of the Laplace equation is

K(x)=C_{d}|x|^{2-d}
,
where

\displaystyle C_{d}=\frac{\Gamma(\frac{d}{2})}{(d-2)2\pi^{\frac{d}{2}}}.

Thus we find the relation
(-\Delta)^{-1}f=-K\ast f=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}C_{d}\frac{f(y)}{|x-y|^{d-2}}dy
.
Know that

\displaystyle\bigl\||V|^{\frac{1}{2}}(-\Delta)^{-1}|V|^{\frac{1}{2}}u\bigr\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}|V(x)|\biggl|C_{d}\frac{|V(y)|^{\frac{1}{2}}}{|x-y|^{d-2}}u(y)dy\biggr|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\leqslant\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}|V(x)|\biggl(C_{d}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}\frac{|V(y)|}{|x-y|^{d-2}}dy\biggr)\biggl(C_{d}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}\frac{|u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{d-2}}dy\biggr)dx

\displaystyle\leqslant C_{d}\|V\|_{\mathcal{K}}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}\frac{C_{d}|V(x)|}{|x-y|^{d-2}}|u(y)|^{2}dydx

\displaystyle\leqslant\bigl(C_{d}\|V\|_{\mathcal{K}}\bigr)^{2}\|u\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}.

From the
TT^{*}
argument
[
8
]
, we set
T=|V|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\nabla|^{-1}
.
Then we find

\displaystyle\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}V|u|^{2}dx=\bigl\||V|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\nabla|^{-1}\nabla u\bigr\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}\leqslant C_{d}\|V\|_{\mathcal{K}}\|\nabla u\|_{L_{x}^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{d})}^{2}.

(2.1)

In particular, if
\|V_{-}\|_{\mathcal{K}}<\frac{1}{C_{d}}
, combining with (
