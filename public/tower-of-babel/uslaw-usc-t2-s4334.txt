2 U.S.C. § 4334 — Availability of funds for franked mail expenses
Citation: us:usc:2:4334
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4334
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 43 (Congressional Committees); Subchapter III (Senate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds in the account, within the contingent fund of the Senate, available for the expenses of inquiries and investigations shall be available for franked mail expenses incurred by committees of the Senate the other expenses of which are paid from that account.
