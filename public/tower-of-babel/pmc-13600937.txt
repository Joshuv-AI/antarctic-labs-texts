Author Correction: AhR inhibition promotes axon regeneration via a stress–growth switch
Authors: Dalia Halawani, Yiqun Wang, Jiaxi Li, Daniel Halperin, Haofei Ni, Molly Estill, Aarthi Ramakrishnan, Li Shen, Arthur Sefiani, Cédric G. Geoffroy, Roland H. Friedel, Hongyan Zou
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10295-z Published online 1 April 2026

In the version of this article initially published, due to data transfer errors to GraphPad Prism, the plots in Fig. 5i, Extended Data Fig. 4b and Extended Data Fig. 7c were assembled from incorrect files containing partially duplicated, shifted or incomplete data cells. The plots, figure legends and Source Data files have been replaced with the correct data.

In addition, we corrected the n values in the legends for Fig. 4d and Fig. 5b, data formatting error in Source Data Fig. 5b, typographical errors in Source Data Extended Data Fig. 1h and missing data cells in Source Data Extended Data Fig. 5h, j and k.

These corrections do not alter the conclusions of the study. For comparison, the original figures are available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment. The figures and text are now updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.

Supplementary information is available in the online version of this amendment.
