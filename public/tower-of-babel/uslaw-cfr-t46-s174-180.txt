46 C.F.R. § 174.180 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:174.180
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-174.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 174 (Special Rules Pertaining To Specific Vessel Types). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to OSVs except liftboats inspected under subchapter L of this chapter and OSVs of at least 6,000 GT ITC (500 GRT if GT ITC is not assigned) as defined in § 125.160 of this chapter.
