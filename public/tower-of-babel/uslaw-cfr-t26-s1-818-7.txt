26 C.F.R. § 1.818-7 — -7 Denial of double deductions.
Citation: us:cfr:26:1.818-7
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.818-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 1 (Income Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 818(f) provides that the same item may not be deducted more than once under subpart B, part I, subchapter L, chapter 1 of the Code (relating to the determination of taxable investment income), and more than once under subpart C, part I, subchapter L, chapter 1 of the Code (relating to the determination of gain or loss from operations).
