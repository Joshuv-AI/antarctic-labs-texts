42 U.S.C. § 1320c — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:1320c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1320c
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XI (General Provisions, Peer Review, And Administrative Simplification); Part B (Peer Review Of Utilization And Quality Of Health Care Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this part is to establish the contracting process which the Secretary must follow pursuant to the requirements of section 1395y(g) of this title, including the definition of the quality improvement organizations with which the Secretary shall contract, the functions such quality improvement organizations are to perform, the confidentiality of medical records, and related administrative matters to facilitate the carrying out of the purposes of this part.
