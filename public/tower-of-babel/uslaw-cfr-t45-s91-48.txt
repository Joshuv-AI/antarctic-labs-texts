45 C.F.R. § 91.48 — Remedial action by recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:45:91.48
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-91.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 91 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Hhs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where HHS finds a recipient has discriminated on the basis of age, the recipient shall take any remedial action that HHS may require to overcome the effects of the discrimination. If another recipient exercises control over the recipient that has discriminated, HHS may require both recipients to take remedial action.
