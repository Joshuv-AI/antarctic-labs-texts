48 C.F.R. § 1733.203 — 1733.203 Applicability.

Creator: Chapter 17—Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1733.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1733 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Office of Personnel Management's (OPM) procurement executive shall make the determination prescribed under FAR 33.203(b).
(b) Requests for determinations under paragraph (a) of this section shall be submitted by OPM's contracting officer through OPM's head of the contracting activity to the procurement executive for further action.
