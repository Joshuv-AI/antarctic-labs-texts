10 U.S.C. § 273 — Training and advising civilian law enforcement officials
Citation: us:usc:10:273
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section273
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 15 (Military Support For Civilian Law Enforcement Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may, in accordance with other applicable law, make Department of Defense personnel available—
(1)
to train Federal, State, and local civilian law enforcement officials in the operation and maintenance of equipment, including equipment made available under section 372 1 of this title; and
(2)
to provide such law enforcement officials with expert advice relevant to the purposes of this chapter.
