48 C.F.R. § 1845.302 — 1845.302 Use of Government property on contracts with foreign governments or international organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1845.302
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1845.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1845 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NASA contracting officers will recover a fair share of the cost of Government property if such property is used in performing services or manufacturing articles for foreign countries or for international organizations.
