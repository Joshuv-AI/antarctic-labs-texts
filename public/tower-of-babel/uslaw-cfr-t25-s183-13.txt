25 C.F.R. § 183.13 — What would cause the Secretary to disapprove a request?
Citation: us:cfr:25:183.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-183.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 183 (Use And Distribution Of The San Carlos Apache Tribe Development Trust Fund And San Carlos Apache Tribe Lease Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will only disapprove a request for distribution of income from the Lease Fund if the request does any of the following:
(a) Fails to provide the documents identified in § 183.5;
(b) Fails to provide reports required under §§ 183.15 and 183.16; or
(c) Includes a use requested or written budget that does not comply with a specific provision of the Settlement Act or this part.
