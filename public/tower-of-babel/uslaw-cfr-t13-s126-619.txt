13 C.F.R. § 126.619 — When must a certified HUBZone small business concern recertify its status for a HUBZone contract?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.619
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.619
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A prime contractor that receives an award as a certified HUBZone small business concern must comply with the recertification requirements set forth in § 125.12 of this chapter regarding its status as a certified HUBZone small business.
