12 C.F.R. § 265.12 — Functions delegated to the Secretary of the Federal Open Market Committee.

Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026

The Secretary of the Federal Open Market Committee (or the Deputy Secretary in the Secretary's absence) is authorized:
(a) Records of policy actions. To approve for inclusion in the Board's Annual Report to Congress, records of policy actions of the Federal Open Market Committee.
(b) [Reserved]
