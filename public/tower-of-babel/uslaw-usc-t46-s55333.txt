46 U.S.C. § 55333 — Exemption from restriction on transporting certain cargo
Citation: us:usc:46:55333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55333
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 553 (Passenger And Cargo Preferences); Subchapter III (American Great Lakes Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The 3-year documentation requirement of section 55305(a) of this title does not apply to a vessel designated as an American Great Lakes vessel during the period of its designation.
