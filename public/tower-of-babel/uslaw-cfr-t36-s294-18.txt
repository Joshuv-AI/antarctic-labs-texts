36 C.F.R. § 294.18 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:36:294.18
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-294.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 294 (Special Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that any provision, section, subsection, or phrase of this subpart is determined by a court or body of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, unconstitutional, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions, sections, subsections, or phrases shall remain in full force and effect.
