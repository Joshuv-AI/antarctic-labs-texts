47 C.F.R. § 26.104 — Regulatory status.
Citation: us:cfr:47:26.104
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-26.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 26 (Space Launch Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licensees are permitted to provide services on a non-common carrier basis. A licensee may render communications services consistent with the regulatory status in its license and with the Commission's rules in this chapter applicable to the Space Launch Services.
