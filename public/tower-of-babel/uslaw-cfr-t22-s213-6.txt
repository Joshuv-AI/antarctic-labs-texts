22 C.F.R. § 213.6 — Omission not a defense.
Citation: us:cfr:22:213.6
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-213.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 213 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure by USAID to comply with any provision of this part is not available to a debtor as a defense against payment of a debt.
