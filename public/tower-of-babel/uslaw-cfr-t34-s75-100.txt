34 C.F.R. § 75.100 — Publication of an application notice; content of the notice.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each fiscal year the Secretary publishes application notices in the Federal Register that explain what kind of assistance is available for new grants under the programs that the Secretary administers.
(b) The application notice for a program explains one or more of the following:
(1) How to apply for a new grant.
(2) If preapplications are used under the program, how to preapply for a new grant.
