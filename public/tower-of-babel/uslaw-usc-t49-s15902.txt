49 U.S.C. § 15902 — Enforcement by the Board
Citation: us:usc:49:15902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section15902
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Pipeline Carriers); Chapter 159 (Enforcement: Investigations, Rights, And Remedies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board may bring a civil action to enforce an order of the Board, except a civil action to enforce an order for the payment of money, when it is violated by a pipeline carrier providing transportation subject to this part.
