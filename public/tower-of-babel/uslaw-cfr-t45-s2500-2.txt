45 C.F.R. § 2500.2 — Agency operating name.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2500.2
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2500.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2500 (Americorps Statement Of Organization, Agency Operating Name, And Logos). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Corporation for National and Community Service has adopted AmeriCorps as its official agency operating name.
(b) Use of AmeriCorps as the agency operating name incorporates the Corporation for National and Community Service by reference.
