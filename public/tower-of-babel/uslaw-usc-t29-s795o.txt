Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services); Subchapter VI (Employment Opportunities For Individuals With Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.
