34 U.S.C. § 60503 — Submission of reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:34:60503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section60503
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 605 (Recidivism Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than January 31 of each year, the Attorney General shall submit to the Committee on the Judiciary of the Senate and the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives each report required by the Attorney General under this Act or an amendment made by this Act during the preceding year.
