32 C.F.R. § 1657.3 — District appeal boards.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1657.3
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1657.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1657 (Overseas Registrant Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director shall establish district appeal boards with jurisdiction to determine appeals of claims of overseas registrants. Such boards shall consist of three or more members appointed by the President. The Director shall prescribe the geographic jurisdiction of each board.
