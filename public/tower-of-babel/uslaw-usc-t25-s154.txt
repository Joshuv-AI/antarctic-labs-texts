25 U.S.C. § 154 — Proceeds of sales of lands not subject to certain deductions
Citation: us:usc:25:154
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section154
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter III (Deposit, Care, And Investment Of Indian Moneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No part of the expenses of the public lands service shall be deducted from the proceeds of Indian lands sold through the Bureau of Land Management, except as authorized by the treaty or agreement providing for the disposition of the lands.
