22 C.F.R. § 221.23 — Payment to A.I.D. of excess amounts received by a Noteholder.
Citation: us:cfr:22:221.23
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-221.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 221 (Israel Loan Guarantee Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Noteholder shall, as a result of A.I.D. paying compensation under this Guarantee, receive an excess payment, it shall refund the excess to A.I.D.
