Finite coproducts, coregularity and coexactness for profinite interior algebras
Matteo De Berardinis

Abstract
In previous articles, we showed that the category of profinite L -algebras (where L is a normal modal logic with the finite model property) is monadic over Set . Then, we developed sequent calculi for extensions of the language of L with infinitary conjunctions and disjunctions, proving completeness with respect to profinite L -algebras and relating syntactic properties of the calculi with regularity/exactness properties of the category opposite to profinite L -algebras. In this paper, we focus on the algebraic perspective: we characterize those L extending S 4 whose profinite algebras enjoy such categorical properties.



keywords
profiniteness, interior algebras, regularity, Barr exactness

Introduction

In
[
4
]
we proved that the category
\Pro L\textbf{{MA}}_{\textit{fin}}
of profinite
L
-algebras, for any normal modal logic
L
with the finite model property, is monadic over
Set
. This result ensures that it is possible to describe profinite
L
-algebras in terms of (possibly infinitary) operations and equations, while guaranteeing the existence of the free objects. We then developed infinitary sequent calculi (see
[
5
]
) for propositional modal languages with infinitary conjunctions and disjunctions, enriching Maehara-Takeuti’s infinitary extension of the sequent calculus
LK
(introduced in
[
21
, Chapter 4]
) with peculiar modal rules, while keeping well-foundedness of the proofs. A Lindenbaum algebra construction proves that profinite
L
-algebras provide a complete semantics for such calculi.

The two aforementioned descriptions of profinite
L
-algebras are then compared in
[
5
]
: bridge theorems relating categorical properties of
\Pro L\textbf{{MA}}_{\textit{fin}}
, or of its dual category
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
of
locally finite
Kripke frames for
L
, and syntactic properties of the corresponding calculi are established. Namely, as every monadic category over
Set
,
\Pro L\textbf{{MA}}_{\textit{fin}}
(hence its dual

) is both complete and cocomplete and it has both a regular factorization system and a coregular one.

On the one hand, it turns out that the coincidence of the two factorization systems (which implies that both all epis and all monos are regular — actually, it is equivalent to the fact that all epis are regular in
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
) corresponds to a strong version of Beth’s Definability Property. On the other hand, the fact that all monos are regular in
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
is equivalent to an infinitary version of the standard Beth’s Property. Stability of the coregular factorization system in
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
(in
[
10
]
called — together with the existence of finite limits —

, as opposed to the standard regularity, which takes into account the regular factorization system) is equivalent to amalgamability for profinite
L
-algebras and it corresponds to Craig’s Interpolation Theorem for the global consequence relation. If, in addition to r-regularity, we require that equivalence relations are in one-to-one correspondence with quotients, we obtain
r-Barr exactness
. r-Barr exactness is also considered in
[
5
]
. In many cases, the aforementioned results are similar to those existing for the analogous analysis in the customary finitary fragment of our language (see
[
10
]
), with the difference that profinite algebras have to be replaced with finitely presented ones.

This paper can be seen as a compendium of
[
5
]
. When all monos are regular in
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
, for example whenever
L
extends
K4
(meaning that the Kripke models of
L
are transitive), then the notions of r-regularity and r-Barr exactness coincide with their standard (non r-) versions. We are interested in the extensions of the logic
S4
(the Kripke models are reflexive, as well as transitive); its algebraic models are called
interior
algebras, due to the fact that their axioms encode the properties defining the topological interior. In particular, we want to characterize the extensions
L\supseteq S4
for which
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
, the dual of
\Pro L\textbf{{MA}}_{\textit{fin}}
, is regular/Barr exact (we leave the full characterization of the extensions of
K4
for a future work).

The paper is structured as follows. In Section
2

[

In Section

In Section

with the finite model property for which

is a regular category, i.e. for which

\widehat{\mathsf{C}}\longrightarrow\textbf{{KFr}}
.

As before, we define a functor
\mathcal{F}^{\prime}\colon\textbf{{KFr}}\longrightarrow\widehat{\mathsf{C}}
, by setting
\mathcal{F}^{\prime}(W)(c)\coloneqq\textbf{{KFr}}[\mathcal{K}^{\prime}(c),W]
for each
W\in\textbf{{KFr}}
and
c\in\mathsf{C}
. All the results that we proved for
\mathcal{K}
and
\mathcal{F}
can be easily adapted to
\mathcal{K}^{\prime}
and
\mathcal{F}^{\prime}
, except for Theorem
43
, which seems not to hold in some cases. However, if we assume furthermore that the category
\mathsf{C}
itself is a poset, then Theorem
43
can be adapted as well. For a natural number
m
, denote by
[m]^{\prime}
the
m
-elements linearly ordered irreflexive chain (
[m]^{\prime}=\{1,\dots,m\}
, with
i\prec j
if and only if
i<j
).

Theorem 46
.

If
\mathsf{C}=(n,\leq)
, for some
n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\omega\}
, then
\mathcal{K}^{\prime}
restricts to an equivalence of categories between
\widehat{\mathsf{C}}
and
L_{\mathsf{C}}\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
, where
L_{\mathsf{C}}=\textit{GL}.3+\textit{bd}_{n}
(
L_{\mathsf{C}}
is the inconsistent logic if
n=0=\emptyset
).

Proof.

For each
m\in n
, we have that
\mathcal{K}^{\prime}(m)\cong[m+1]^{\prime}
and, for each Kripke frame
W
, the function
\varepsilon^{\prime}_{W}
(counit of the adjunction
\mathcal{K}^{\prime}\dashv\mathcal{F}^{\prime}
) can be rewritten as follows

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\coprod_{m\in n}\textbf{{KFr}}[[m+1]^{\prime},W]}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{W}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{g\colon[m+1]^{\prime}\longrightarrow W}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{g(1)}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\varepsilon^{\prime}_{W}}

It is straightforward to show that
\varepsilon^{\prime}_{W}
is injective for all
W\in\textbf{{KFr}}
(p-morphisms having an irreflexive chain
[m]^{\prime}
as domain are isomorphisms and the unique p-morphism
[m]^{\prime}\longrightarrow[m]^{\prime}
is the identity). Moreover, the logic of
\{[m+1]^{\prime}\ |\ m\in n\}
is exactly
\textit{GL}.3+\textit{bd}_{n}
.
∎

7
Conclusions

In this paper, motivated by the results of
[
4
,
5
]
, we studied regularity and Barr exactness of
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
, the dual of the category
\Pro L\textbf{{MA}}_{\textit{fin}}
of profinite
L
-algebras, among the normal modal logics
L\supseteq S4
with the finite model property. In doing so, we described the finite limits of
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
. Moreover, we introduced an adjunction between
KFr
and the category of presheaves over any small category
\mathsf{C}
that allowed us to present some
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
as presheaf toposes.

As we observed, the classification of the regularity for
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
, which is equivalent to the amalgamation property for
\Pro L\textbf{{MA}}_{\textit{fin}}
, follows Maksimova’s parallel results for the finitary fragment
[
16
,
17
]
(with the exception of
L=\textit{Grz}.3
, as observed in Remark
27
). In addition, regularity can be encountered in many other cases above
K4
, as we showed in Remark
19
. Passing to Barr-exactness, the picture changes drastically: there are exactly five logics
L
extending
S4
and such that
L\textbf{{KFr}}_{\textit{lf}}
is Barr exact. These five logics are all locally finite and of little interest (the category of their locally finite Kripke frames is equivalent to the topos of the actions of a finite small monoid). Still, one may ask whether Barr exactness is so rare everywhere: in particular a classification over
K4
cannot include only trivial examples, as witnessed by Theorem
46
.

Given that Barr exactness is rare, one may investigate almost Barr exactness, where a category is said to be
almost Barr exact
if and only if it is regular and effective descent morphisms coincide with regular epimorphisms. Almost Barr exactness, in fact, turns out to be equivalent to effectiveness of special kinds of equivalence relations
[
11
]
, so that there is a concrete chance that it should hold for some relevant categories of locally finite Kripke frames.
