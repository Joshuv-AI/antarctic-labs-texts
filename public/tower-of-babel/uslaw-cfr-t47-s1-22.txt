47 C.F.R. § 1.22 — Authority for representation.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.22
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person, in a representative capacity, transacting business with the Commission, may be required to show his authority to act in such capacity.
