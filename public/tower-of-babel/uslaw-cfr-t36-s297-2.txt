36 C.F.R. § 297.2 — Scope and application.
Citation: us:cfr:36:297.2
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-297.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 297 (Wild And Scenic Rivers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules apply to Federal assistance in the construction of water resources projects affecting Wild and Scenic Rivers or Study Rivers administered in whole or part by the Secretary of Agriculture.
