Abstract
S H 0 H_{0} ES 2016-2022 H ​ V ​ I HVI data for classical Cepheids in the keystone galaxy NGC4258 yield doubly discordant Wesenheit Leavitt functions: Δ ​ W 0 , H − V ​ I = − 0.13 ± 0 m ​ .02 \Delta W_{0,H-VI}=-0.13\pm 0^{m}.02 ( − 0 m ​ .17 -0^{m}.17 unweighted) and that is paired with a previously noted Δ ​ W 0 , I − V ​ I ≃ − 0 m ​ .3 \Delta W_{0,I-VI}\simeq-0^{m}.3 , which in concert with complimentary evidence suggest the 2016 S H 0 H_{0} ES NGC4258-anchored H 0 ± σ H 0 H_{0}\pm\sigma_{H_{0}} warrants scrutiny (i.e., σ H 0 / H 0 ≳ 6 \sigma_{H_{0}}/{H_{0}}\gtrsim 6 %). Lastly, during the analysis it was identified that the 2022 S H 0 H_{0} ES database relays incorrect SMC Cepheid photometry.
