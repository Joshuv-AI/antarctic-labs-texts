Abstract
. Let B n B_{n} be the unit ball in the complex vector space ℂ n \mathbb{C}^{n} , and let φ : B n → B n \varphi:B_{n}\rightarrow B_{n} be a holomorphic mapping. In this paper, we characterize those symbols φ \varphi such that composition operators C φ C_{\varphi} are bounded or compact on the general Hardy type space H p , q , s ​ ( B n ) H^{p,q,s}(B_{n}) . These results extend the relevant results on Hardy space and some other classical function spaces.

1.
Introduction

Suppose that
\mathbb{C}^{n}
is the
n
-dimensional complex vector space. For
w=(w_{1},\cdots,w_{n})
and
u=(u_{1},\cdots,u_{n})
in
\mathbb{C}^{n}
, let

\langle w,u\rangle=w_{1}\overline{u_{1}}+\cdots+w_{n}\overline{u_{n}}
, and let
B_{n}=\{w=(w_{1},\cdots,w_{n})\in\mathbb{C}^{n}:|w|=\sqrt{\langle w,w\rangle}<1\}
be the unit ball in
\mathbb{C}^{n}
. Suppose that
H(B_{n})
is the set of all holomorphic functions on
B_{n}
, and
H^{\infty}(B_{n})
is the set of
all bounded holomorphic functions on
B_{n}
.
Let
dv
be the normalized volume measure on
B_{n}
, and
d\sigma
be the normalized rotation invariant measure
on
S_{n}
, where
S_{n}
is the boundary of
B_{n}
.

Definition 1.1
For
p>0
, if
h\in H(B_{n})
and

||h||_{p}=\sup_{u\in B_{n}}(1-|u|^{2})^{p}\ |h(u)|<\infty,

then
h
is said to belong to the holomorphic growth space

H^{\infty}_{p}(B_{n})
.

Definition 1.2
For
p>0
and
q>0
, the weighted Hardy space
H_{q}^{p}(B_{n})
consists of all holomorphic functions
h
on
B_{n}
such that

||h||_{p,q}=\sup_{0\leq r<1}\left\{(1-r^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|h(r\eta)|^{p}\ d\sigma(\eta)\right\}^{\frac{1}{p}}<\infty.

Definition 1.3
For
\beta>-1
and
p>0
, the function
h
is said to belong to
the weighted Bergman space
A_{\beta}^{p}(B)
if
h\in H(B_{n})
and

||h||_{A_{\beta}^{p}}=\left(\int_{B}|h(u)|^{p}\ dv_{\beta}(u)\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<\infty,

where
dv_{\beta}(u)=c_{\beta}(1-|u|^{2})^{\beta}\ dv(u)
, the
constant
c_{\beta}
such that
v_{\beta}(B_{n})=1
.

For
w\in B_{n}
, let
\varphi_{w}
be the holomorphic automorphism of
B_{n}
with
\varphi_{w}(0)=w
,
\varphi_{w}(w)=0

and
\varphi_{w}^{-1}=\varphi_{w}
.

Definition 1.4
For
p>0
,
s\geq 0
,
q+n\geq 0
,
q+s\geq 0
, if
f
is a Lebesgue measurable function on
B_{n}

and
||f||_{p,q,s}=\displaystyle{\sup_{0\leq r<1}}M_{p,q,s}(r,f)<\infty
,
where

\displaystyle M_{p,q,s}(r,f)=\sup_{a\in B_{n}}\left\{(1-r^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|f(r\xi)|^{p}\ (1-|\varphi_{a}(r\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)\right\}^{\frac{1}{p}},

then
f
is said to belong to the general Lebesgue space
L^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

The space
L^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
is a Banach space under the norm

||.||_{p,q,s}
when
p\geq 1
. If
0<p<1
, then
L^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
is
a complete metric space under the distance

\rho(f,g)=||f-g||^{p}_{p,q,s}
.
In particular,
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})=L^{p,q,s}(B_{n})\bigcap H(B_{n})
is called
the general Hardy type space. In fact,
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
is just the Hardy space

H^{p}(B_{n})
when
q=s=0
. Therefore,
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
is a generalization of
H^{p}(B_{n})
.
The space
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
comes from some practical applications (for example, [1-3]).
In [4], we formally defined this function class as the general Hardy type space on the unit ball. The space

H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
contains several
classical function spaces by taking different parameters
q
and
s
, for examples,
H^{\infty}(B_{n})
(
s\geq n=-q
),

H^{\infty}_{\frac{q+n}{p}}(B_{n})
(
s\geq n>-q
),
H^{p}_{q}(B_{n})
(
q>0=s
) etc. If
0<s<n
, then
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})

is a new holomorphic function space, different from the Hardy space
H^{k}(B_{n})
and the weight Hardy space
H^{k}_{t}(B_{n})
for any
0<k\leq\infty
and
0<t<\infty
(see [4]).

Definition 1.5
Let
\varphi:B_{n}\rightarrow B_{n}
be a holomorphic mapping. The
\varphi
induces a operator
C_{\varphi}
on
H(B_{n})
:
C_{\varphi}g=g\circ\varphi
(
g\in H(B_{n}
)). The operator
C_{\varphi}
is called composition operator.

Composition type operators between various holomorphic function spaces
on the unit disc or ball have been
studied for a long time, and there have been a lot of research results (for examples, [1]-[23]).
The main purpose of this paper is to discuss the boundedness and compactness of
C_{\varphi}
on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
. These results extend the relevant results on Hardy space and some other classical function spaces.

If there exists constant
c>0
such that
E_{1}\geq cE_{2}

( or
E_{1}\leq cE_{2}
), then we write
``E_{1}\gtrsim E_{2}"
( or
``E_{1}\lesssim E_{2}"
). If
``E_{1}\gtrsim E_{2}"
and
``E_{1}\lesssim E_{2}"
, then we call
``E_{1}\asymp E_{2}"
.

2.
Some Lemmas

Lemma 2.1 ([4])
For
p>0
,
s\geq 0
,
q+s\geq 0
,
q+n\geq 0
, if
h\in H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
, then

|h(z)|\lesssim\frac{||h||_{p,q,s}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{\frac{q+n}{p}}}\ \ \mbox{for all $z\in B_{n}$}.

This result comes from Lemma 2.3 in [4].

Lemma 2.2 ([24])
For
\beta>-1
, if
h\in A_{\beta}^{1}(B_{n})
, then

h(z)=\int_{B_{n}}\frac{h(w)\ dv_{\beta}(w)}{(1-\langle z,w\rangle)^{n+1+\beta}}\ \ (z\in B_{n}).

This result comes from Theorem 2.2 in [24].

Lemma 2.3 ([26])
Let
c>0
and
\delta>-1
. Then for all
w\in B_{n}
,

\int_{S_{n}}\frac{d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle w,\xi\rangle|^{n+c}}\asymp\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-|u|^{2})^{\delta}\ dv(u)}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{n+1+\delta+c}}\asymp\frac{1}{(1-|w|^{2})^{c}}.

This result comes from Proposition 1.4.10 in [26].

For two points in
B_{n}
, there are the following
integral bidirectional estimates. These results come from Proposition 3.1 in [25].

Lemma 2.4 ([25])
For
t>0
and
\delta>0
, let

\displaystyle{I_{w,u}=\int_{S_{n}}\frac{d\sigma(\eta)}{|1-\langle\eta,w\rangle|^{\delta}\ |1-\langle\eta,u\rangle|^{l}}\ \ (w,u\in B_{n}).}

Then there are the following bidirectional estimates:

(1)
\displaystyle{I_{w,u}\asymp\frac{1}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{\delta+l-n}}\ }
when
\delta+l>n
and
\max\{\delta,l\}<n
.

(2)
\displaystyle{I_{w,u}\asymp\frac{1}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{l}}\log\frac{e}{|1-\langle w,\varphi_{w}(u)\rangle|}}
when
\delta=n>l
.

(3)
\displaystyle{I_{w,u}\asymp\frac{1}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{n}}\log\frac{e}{1-|\varphi_{w}(u)|^{2}}}
when
\delta=n=l
.

(4)
\displaystyle{I_{w,u}\asymp\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{n-\delta}}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{l}}+\frac{(1-|u|^{2})^{n-l}}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{\delta}}}
when
\delta>n
and
l>n
.

(5)
\displaystyle{I_{w,u}\asymp\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{n-\delta}}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{n}}+\frac{\log\frac{e}{1-|\varphi_{u}(w)|^{2}}}{|1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{\delta}}}
when

\delta>n=l
.

(6)
\displaystyle{I_{w,u}\asymp\frac{1}{(1-|w|^{2})^{\delta-n}\ |1-\langle w,u\rangle|^{l}}}
when
\delta>n>l
.

Lemma 2.5 ([23])
For
p>0
and
0<p_{0}\leq 1
, then

(1-\rho^{2})^{\frac{(1-p_{0})n}{p_{0}}}\int_{S_{n}}|h(\rho^{2}\zeta)|^{p}\ d\sigma(\zeta)\leq\left\{\int_{S_{n}}|h(\rho\zeta)|^{pp_{0}}\ d\sigma(\zeta)\right\}^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}

for all
h\in H(B_{n})
and
0\leq\rho<1
.

The result comes from Lemma 2.3 in [23].

3.
Main Results

If
s\geq n
, then
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})=B^{\frac{q+n}{p}}(B_{n})
, the Bloch type space. If
s=0
, then
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
is the weighted Hardy space or the Hardy space. The boundedness and compactness of
C_{\varphi}
on the Bloch type space or the weighted Hardy space have been solved. Therefore, we only need to consider the case
0<s<n
.

Theorem 3.1
Let
p>0
,
0<s<n
,
q+s\geq 0
,
q+n\geq 0
. Suppose that
\varphi:\ B_{n}\rightarrow B_{n}
is a holomorphic mapping.

(1) If
\varphi
is an automorphism, then
C_{\varphi}
is bounded on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

(2) If
q+n>0
and
C_{\varphi}
is compact on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
, then

\displaystyle\lim_{|w|\rightarrow 1^{-}}\frac{1-|w|^{2}}{1-|\varphi(w)|^{2}}=0.

(3.1)

(3) If
||\varphi||_{\infty}<1
, then
C_{\varphi}
is compact on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

(4) If
C_{\varphi}
is bounded on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
, then for any
t>0
there is

\displaystyle\sup_{0\leq\rho<1}\sup_{a,w\in B_{n}}(1-\rho^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{t}(1-|\varphi_{a}(\rho\xi)|^{2})^{s}}{|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),w\rangle|^{q+n+t}}\ d\sigma(\xi)<\infty.

(3.2)

(5) When
q+s>0
, for any
\beta>\{q+\max(s,pn)\}\min(1/p,1)-1
, if

\displaystyle\sup_{\eta\in S_{n}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-|a|^{2})^{s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{(1-|\varphi(a)|^{2})^{s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),r\eta\rangle|^{n+1+\beta}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\lesssim\frac{1}{(1-r\rho)^{\beta+1}}

(3.3)

for all
a\in B_{n}
and
0\leq r,\rho<1
, then
C_{\varphi}
is bounded on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

(6) When
q+s>0
, if (3.3) holds, then
C_{\varphi}
is a compact operator on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
if and only if (3.1) holds.

Proof
For the following proof, we select constant
\alpha>\max\{(q+n)/p-1,\ (q+s)/p-1,\ (q+s+n)/p-n-1\}
.

(1) This result comes from Theorem 5.1 in [31].

(2) If
||\varphi||_{\infty}<1
, then (3.1) is obvious. When
||\varphi||_{\infty}=1
, let
\{w^{j}\}\subset B_{n}
be any sequence such that

|\varphi(w^{j})|\rightarrow 1
when
j\rightarrow\infty
. We take

g_{j}(w)=\frac{(1-|\varphi(w^{j})|^{2})^{\frac{|q|}{p}+1}}{(1-\langle w,\varphi(w^{j})\rangle)^{\frac{q+n+|q|}{p}+1}}\ \ \ (w\in B_{n}).

Then
\{g_{j}\}
converges to 0 uniformly on any compact set of
B_{n}
. Otherwise,
it follows from Lemma 2.4(4-6) and
\displaystyle{\sup_{0<x\leq 1}x^{\varepsilon}\log\frac{e}{x}}<\infty
(
\varepsilon>0
) (case
2s=n
) that

\sup_{0\leq\rho<1}\sup_{a\in B_{n}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-r^{2})^{q+s}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}(1-|\varphi(w^{j})|^{2})^{|q|+p}}{|1-\langle r\xi,\varphi(w^{j})\rangle|^{q+n+|q|+p}|1-\langle r\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\ d\sigma(\xi)\lesssim 1

for all
j=1,2,\cdots
by means of three cases
0\leq 2s<n
,
2s=n
and
2s>n
. Therefore, it follows from Lemma 2.1 that

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1-|w^{j}|^{2}}{1-|\varphi(w^{j})|^{2}}\right)^{\frac{q+n}{p}}=(1-|w^{j}|^{2})^{\frac{q+n}{p}}|C_{\varphi}g_{j}(w^{j})|

\displaystyle\lesssim||C_{\varphi}g_{j}||_{p,q,s}\rightarrow 0\ \ (j\rightarrow\infty).

(3.4)

This implies that (3.1) holds.

(3) When
||\varphi||_{\infty}<1
, for any sequence
\{h_{k}\}
which converges to 0 uniformly on any compact set of
B_{n}
and
\displaystyle{\sup_{k\in\{1,2,\cdots\}}}||h_{k}||_{p,q,s}\leq 1
, it follows from Lemma 2.3 that

\displaystyle\sup_{0\leq\rho<1}\sup_{a\in B_{n}}(1-\rho^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|h_{k}[\varphi(\rho\xi)]|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{a}(\rho\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)

\displaystyle\leq\max_{|w|\leq||\varphi||_{\infty}}|h_{k}(w)|^{p}\sup_{0\leq\rho<1}\sup_{a\in B_{n}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\max_{|w|\leq||\varphi||_{\infty}}|h_{k}(w)|^{p}\ \rightarrow 0\ \ (k\rightarrow\infty)\ \Rightarrow\ \mbox{$C_{\varphi}$ is compact on $H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})$.}

(4) For any
w\in B_{n}
and
t>0
, let

f_{w}(z)=\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{\frac{t}{p}}}{(1-\langle z,w\rangle)^{\frac{q+n+t}{p}}}\ \ (z\in B_{n}).

By Lemma 2.4(1-6) and
\displaystyle{\sup_{0<x<2}x^{\varepsilon}\log\frac{e}{x}}<\infty
(
\varepsilon>0
), we have that

\sup_{0\leq\rho<1}\sup_{a\in B_{n}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}(1-|w|^{2})^{t}}{|1-\langle\rho\xi,w\rangle|^{q+n+t}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\ d\sigma(\xi)\lesssim 1

for all
w\in B_{n}
. It follows from the boundedness of
C_{\varphi}
on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})

that

\displaystyle||C_{\varphi}||\gtrsim||C_{\varphi}||.||f_{w}||_{p,q,s}\geq||C_{\varphi}f_{w}||_{p,q,s}

\displaystyle=\sup_{0\leq\rho<1}\sup_{a\in B_{n}}(1-\rho^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{t}(1-|\varphi_{a}(\rho\xi)|^{2})^{s}}{|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),w\rangle|^{q+n+t}}\ d\sigma(\xi)

for all
w\in B_{n}
. This shows that (3.2) holds.

(5) For any
g\in H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
and
a\in B_{n}
, we take

G_{a,g}(w)=\frac{g(w)}{(1-\langle w,\varphi(a)\rangle)^{\frac{2s}{p}}}\ \ (w\in B_{n}).

It follows from
\alpha>(q+n)/p-1
and Lemmas 2.1-2.2 that

G_{a,g}(w)=\int_{B_{n}}\frac{G_{a,g}(u)\ dv_{\alpha}(u)}{(1-\langle w,u)^{n+1+\alpha}}\ \ (w\in B_{n}).

This means that

\displaystyle|G_{a,g}[\varphi(z)]|\leq\int_{B_{n}}\frac{|G_{a,g}(u)|\ dv_{\alpha}(u)}{|1-\langle\varphi(z),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}}

\displaystyle=\int_{B_{n}}\frac{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{-\frac{2s}{p}}|g(u)|\ dv_{\alpha}(u)}{|1-\langle\varphi(z),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}}\ \ (z\in B_{n}).

(3.5)

If
p>1
, then we may choose
\max\{\alpha,\ q+s-1\}<\alpha_{1}<\min\{p(\alpha+1)-1,\ q+s+\alpha\}
such that
(p\alpha-\alpha_{1})/(p-1)>-1
and
\alpha_{1}-\alpha>0
. By (3.5), Hölder inequality, Lemma 2.3,
(1-|z|^{2})/(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})\leq(1+|\varphi(0)|)/(1-|\varphi(0)|)
, we have that

\displaystyle|G_{a,g}[\varphi(z)]|^{p}\lesssim\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha_{1}}|g(u)|^{p}\ dv(u)}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}|1-\langle\varphi(z),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}}

\displaystyle\times\left\{\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-|u|^{2})^{\frac{p\alpha-\alpha_{1}}{p-1}}\ dv(u)}{|1-\langle\varphi(z),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}}\right\}^{p-1}

\displaystyle\asymp(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha-\alpha_{1}}\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha_{1}}|g(u)|^{p}\ dv(u)}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}|1-\langle\varphi(z),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha-\alpha_{1}}\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha_{1}}|g(u)|^{p}\ dv(u)}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}|1-\langle\varphi(z),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}}.

(3.6)

It follows from the selection of
\alpha
and
\alpha_{1}
that there are
\alpha_{1}-q-s>-1
and
q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}>0
. When (3.3) holds, for any
0\leq\rho<1
and
\xi\in S_{n}
, by (3.6),
Lemma 1.8 in [24], (3.3), Lemma 6 in [27], we have that

\displaystyle(1-\rho^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|g[\varphi(\rho\xi)]|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{a}(\rho\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)

\displaystyle=(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}\int_{S_{n}}|G_{a,g}[\varphi(\rho\xi)]|^{p}\frac{|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}}{|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\ d\sigma(\xi)

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{S_{n}}\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}}|g(u)|^{p}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha_{1}}\ dv(u)d\sigma(\xi)}{(1-|a|^{2})^{-s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}}

\displaystyle=\int_{B_{n}}\frac{|g(u)|^{p}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha_{1}}}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)dv(u)}{(1-|a|^{2})^{-s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{1}(1-r^{2})^{\alpha_{1}-q-s}\int_{S_{n}}(1-r^{2})^{q}|g(r\eta)|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{\varphi(a)}(r\eta)|^{2})^{s}

\displaystyle\times\left\{\frac{(1-|a|^{2})^{s}}{(1-|\varphi(a)|^{2})^{s}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),r\eta|^{n+1+\alpha}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\right\}d\sigma(\eta)dr

\displaystyle\lesssim(1-\rho)^{q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}}||g||_{p,q,s}^{p}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1-r)^{\alpha_{1}-q-s}}{(1-r\rho)^{\alpha+1}}\ dr\asymp||g||_{p,q,s}^{p}.

This means that
C_{\varphi}
is a bounded operator on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

If
0<p\leq 1
, then we let
\alpha=(\alpha^{\prime}+n+1)/p-n-1
. It follows from
\alpha>(q+s+n)/p-n-1
and
\alpha>(q+n)/p-1
that
\alpha^{\prime}>q+\max\{s,pn\}-1
.
When (3.3) holds, by (3.5), Lemma 2.15 in [24],

\displaystyle(1-\rho^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|g[\varphi(\rho\xi)]|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{a}(\rho\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{S_{n}}\int_{B_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s}|g(u)|^{p}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha^{\prime}}\ dv(u)d\sigma(\xi)}{(1-|a|^{2})^{-s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha^{\prime}}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}}

\displaystyle=\int_{B_{n}}\frac{|g(u)|^{p}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha^{\prime}}}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)dv(u)}{(1-|a|^{2})^{-s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha^{\prime}}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}

\displaystyle\lesssim(1-\rho^{2})^{q+s}\int_{0}^{1}(1-r^{2})^{\alpha^{\prime}-q-s}\int_{S_{n}}(1-r^{2})^{q}|g(r\eta)|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{\varphi(a)}(r\eta)|^{2})^{s}

\displaystyle\times\left\{\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-|a|^{2})^{s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{(1-|\varphi(a)|^{2})^{s}|1-\langle\varphi(\rho\xi),r\eta\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha^{\prime}}|1-\langle\rho\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\right\}d\sigma(\eta)dr

\displaystyle\lesssim(1-\rho)^{q+s}||g||_{p,q,s}^{p}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1-r)^{\alpha^{\prime}-q-s}}{(1-r\rho)^{\alpha^{\prime}+1}}\ dr\asymp||g||_{p,q,s}^{p}.

This means that
C_{\varphi}
is a bounded operator on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

(6) Let
\{h_{k}\}
be any sequence which converges to 0 uniformly on any compact set of
B_{n}
and
||h_{k}||_{p,q,s}\leq 1
for all
k\in\{1,2,\cdots\}
. Let
1/2<\rho<1
and

M_{\rho}=\sup_{\rho<|z|<1}\frac{1-|z|^{2}}{1-|\varphi(z)|^{2}}\ \ (z\in B_{n}).

When
p>1
, by (3.5), Hölder inequality,
(1-|r\xi|^{2})/(1-|\varphi(r\xi)|^{2})\leq M_{\rho}
for any
\rho<r<1
,
Fubini theorem, Lemma 1.8 in [24], (3.3), Lemma 6 in [27], we have that

\displaystyle\sup_{0\leq r<1}(1-r^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|h_{k}[\varphi(r\xi)]|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{a}(r\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)\lesssim\max_{|u|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(u)]|^{p}

\displaystyle+\sup_{\rho<r<1}(1-r^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}(1-|\varphi_{a}(r\xi)|^{2})^{s}|G_{a,h_{k}}[\varphi(r\xi)]|^{p}|1-\langle\varphi(r\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)

\displaystyle\lesssim\max_{|u|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(u)]|^{p}+M_{\rho}^{\alpha_{1}-\alpha}\sup_{\rho<r<1}(1-r^{2})^{q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}

\displaystyle\times\int_{B_{n}}\frac{|h_{k}(u)|^{p}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha_{1}}}{|1-\langle\varphi(a),u\rangle|^{2s}}\left\{\int_{S_{n}}\frac{|1-\langle\varphi(r\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle\varphi(r\xi),u\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha}|1-\langle r\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\right\}dv(u)

\displaystyle\lesssim\max_{|u|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(u)]|^{p}+M_{\rho}^{\alpha_{1}-\alpha}\sup_{\rho<r<1}(1-r^{2})^{q+s+\alpha-\alpha_{1}}

\displaystyle\times\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1-t)^{\alpha_{1}-q-s}}{(1-rt)^{\alpha+1}}\left\{\int_{S_{n}}(1-t^{2})^{q}|h_{k}(t\eta)|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{\varphi(a)}(t\eta)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\eta)\right\}dt

\displaystyle\lesssim\max_{|u|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(u)]|^{p}+M_{\rho}^{\alpha_{1}-\alpha}\rightarrow M_{\rho}^{\alpha_{1}-\alpha}\ \ (k\rightarrow\infty).

When
0<p\leq 1
, we choose
0<\varepsilon<\min\{q+s,1+\alpha^{\prime}-q-\max\{s,pn\}\}
. By (3.5), Lemma 2.15 in [24], (3.3), Lemma 1.8 in [24], Lemma 6 in [27], we have that

\displaystyle\sup_{0\leq r<1}(1-r^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|h_{k}[\varphi(r\xi)]|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{a}(r\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)

\displaystyle\lesssim\sup_{0\leq r\leq\rho}(1-r^{2})^{q}\int_{S_{n}}|h_{k}[\varphi(r\xi)]|^{p}(1-|\varphi_{a}(r\xi)|^{2})^{s}\ d\sigma(\xi)+(1-|a|^{2})^{s}

\displaystyle\times\sup_{\rho<r<1}\int_{B_{n}}\frac{|h_{k}(u)|^{p}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha^{\prime}}}{|1-\overline{\varphi(a)}u|^{2s}}\int_{S_{n}}\frac{(1-r^{2})^{q+s}|1-\langle\varphi(r\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(r\xi)\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha^{\prime}}|1-\langle r\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}dv(u)

\displaystyle\lesssim\sup_{|w|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(w)]|^{p}+\sup_{\rho<r<1}M_{\rho}^{\varepsilon}(1-r^{2})^{q+s-\varepsilon}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}

\displaystyle\times\ \int_{B_{n}}\frac{|h_{k}(u)|^{p}(1-|u|^{2})^{\alpha^{\prime}}}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}}\left\{\int_{S_{n}}\frac{|1-\langle\varphi(r\xi),\varphi(a)\rangle|^{2s}\ d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle u,\varphi(r\xi)\rangle|^{n+1+\alpha^{\prime}-\varepsilon}|1-\langle r\xi,a\rangle|^{2s}}\right\}dv(u)

\displaystyle\lesssim\sup_{|w|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(w)]|^{p}+M_{\rho}^{\varepsilon}\sup_{\rho<r<1}(1-r)^{q+s-\varepsilon}||h_{k}||_{p,q,s}^{p}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1-t)^{\alpha^{\prime}-q-s}}{(1-rt)^{1+\alpha^{\prime}-\varepsilon}}\ dt

\displaystyle\lesssim\sup_{|w|\leq\rho}|h_{k}[\varphi(w)]|^{p}+M_{\rho}^{\varepsilon}\rightarrow M_{\rho}^{\varepsilon}\ \ (k\rightarrow\infty).

It follows from (3.1) that
C_{\varphi}
is a compact operator on
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

This proof is complete.
\Box

Theorem 3.2
Let
p>0
,
0<s<n
,
q+s\geq 0
. Suppose that
\varphi:\ B_{n}\rightarrow B_{n}
is a holomorphic mapping.
If
C_{\varphi}
is a compact operator from
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
to
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
, then (3.1) holds. If
C_{\varphi}
is a compact operator on
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
, then
C_{\varphi}
is a compact operator from
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
to
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

Proof
If
C_{\varphi}
is compact from
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
to
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
, then it follows from (3.4) that (3.1) holds. Conversely,
C_{\varphi}
is a compact operator on
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
means that
C_{\varphi}
is a bounded operator on
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
. Let
\{h_{k}\}
be any sequence which converges to 0 uniformly on any compact set of
B_{n}
and
||h_{k}||_{\frac{pn}{q+n}}\leq 1
for all
k\in\{1,2,\cdots\}
.

When
-n<q<0
, by Hölder’s
inequality, Lemmas 2.3, the boundedness of
C_{\varphi}
on
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
, we have that

\displaystyle||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||_{p,q,s}^{p}\leq\sup_{0\leq r<1,a\in B_{n}}(1-r^{2})^{q+s}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}

\displaystyle\times\left\{\int_{S_{n}}|h_{k}[\varphi(r\xi)]|^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}\ d\sigma(\xi)\right\}^{\frac{q+n}{n}}\left\{\int_{S_{n}}\frac{d\sigma(\xi)}{|1-\langle r\xi,a\rangle|^{\frac{2sn}{-q}}}\right\}^{\frac{-q}{n}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sup_{0\leq r<1,a\in B_{n}}\frac{(1-r^{2})^{q+s}(1-|a|^{2})^{s}||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||^{p}_{\frac{pn}{q+n}}}{(1-r^{2}|a|^{2})^{q+2s}}\lesssim||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||^{p}_{\frac{pn}{q+n}}.

When
q\geq 0
, let
p_{0}=n/(q+n)
. It follows from Lemma 2.6 that

||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||_{p,q,s}^{p}\leq\sup_{0\leq r<1}\frac{(1-r^{2})^{q}}{(1-r^{2})^{\frac{(1-p_{0})n}{p_{0}}}}\left\{\int_{S_{n}}|h_{k}[\varphi(\sqrt{r}\xi)]|^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}\ d\sigma(\xi)\right\}^{\frac{q+n}{n}}=||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||_{\frac{pn}{q+n}}^{p}.

If
C_{\varphi}
is compact on
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
, then we have that

\displaystyle{\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}}||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||_{\frac{pn}{q+n}}=0\ \Rightarrow\ \lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}||C_{\varphi}h_{k}||_{p,q,s}=0.

This shows that
C_{\varphi}
is compact from
H^{\frac{pn}{q+n}}(B_{n})
to
H^{p,q,s}(B_{n})
.

This proof is complete.
\Box
