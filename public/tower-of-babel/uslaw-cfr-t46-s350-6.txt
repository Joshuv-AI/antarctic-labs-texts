46 C.F.R. § 350.6 — Unauthorized sale, manufacture, possession or display.
Citation: us:cfr:46:350.6
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-350.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 350 (Seamen'S Service Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sale, manufacture, possession or display of any Merchant Marine decoration, or colorable imitations thereof, by anyone other than an authorized vendor is prohibited by law and subject to fine and imprisonment.
