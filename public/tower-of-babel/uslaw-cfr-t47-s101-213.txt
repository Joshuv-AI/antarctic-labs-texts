47 C.F.R. § 101.213 — Station identification.
Citation: us:cfr:47:101.213
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-101.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 101 (Fixed Microwave Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Stations in these services are exempt from the requirement to identify transmissions by call sign or any other station identifier.
