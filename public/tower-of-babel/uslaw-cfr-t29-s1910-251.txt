29 C.F.R. § 1910.251 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1910 (Occupational Safety And Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart:
(a) Welder and welding operator mean any operator of electric or gas welding and cutting equipment.
(b) Approved means listed or approved by a nationally recognized testing laboratory. Refer to § 1910.155(c)(3) for definitions of listed and approved, and § 1910.7 for nationally recognized testing laboratory.
