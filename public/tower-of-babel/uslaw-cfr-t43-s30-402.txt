43 C.F.R. § 30.402 — Does property purchased at probate remain in trust or restricted status?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.402
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The property interests purchased at probate must remain in trust or restricted status.
