7 C.F.R. § 210.27 — Educational prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:210.27
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-210.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 210 (National School Lunch Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In carrying out the provisions of the Act, the Department shall not impose any requirements with respect to teaching personnel, curriculum, instructions, methods of instruction, or materials of instruction in any school as a condition for participation in the Program.
