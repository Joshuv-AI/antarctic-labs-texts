34 C.F.R. § 361.2 — Eligibility for a grant.
Citation: us:cfr:34:361.2
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-361.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 361 (State Vocational Rehabilitation Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any State that submits to the Secretary a vocational rehabilitation services portion of the Unified or Combined State Plan that meets the requirements of section 101(a) of the Act and this part is eligible for a grant under this program.
