42 U.S.C. § 15006 — State control of operations
Citation: us:usc:42:15006
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15006
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 144 (Developmental Disabilities Assistance And Bill Of Rights); Subchapter I (Programs For Individuals With Developmental Disabilities); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise specifically provided, nothing in this subchapter shall be construed as conferring on any Federal officer or employee the right to exercise any supervision or control over the administration, personnel, maintenance, or operation of any programs, services, and supports for individuals with developmental disabilities with respect to which any funds have been or may be expended under this subchapter.
