16 C.F.R. § 1027.6 — Written decision.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1027.6
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1027.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1027 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing official shall issue a final written opinion no later than 60 days after the filing of the petition.
(b) The written opinion will include: A statement of the facts presented to demonstrate the nature and origin of the alleged debt; the hearing official's analysis, findings, and conclusions; the amount and validity of the debt; and the repayment schedule.
