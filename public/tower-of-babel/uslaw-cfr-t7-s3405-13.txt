7 C.F.R. § 3405.13 — When and where to submit a proposal.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3405.13
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3405.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3405 (Higher Education Challenge Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The program announcement will provide the deadline date for submitting a proposal, the number of copies of each proposal that must be submitted, and the address to which proposals must be submitted.
