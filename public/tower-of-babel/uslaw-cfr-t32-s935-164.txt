32 C.F.R. § 935.164 — Boat operations.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.164
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.164
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator of each boat used at Wake Island shall conform to the limitations on its operations as the Commander may prescribe in the public interest.
