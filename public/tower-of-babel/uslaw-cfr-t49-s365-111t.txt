49 C.F.R. § 365.111T — T Appeals to rejections of the application.
Citation: us:cfr:49:365.111T
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-365.111T
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 365 (Rules Governing Applications For Operating Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An applicant has the right to appeal rejection of the application. The appeal must be filed at the FMCSA within 10 days of the date of the letter of rejection.
(b) If the appeal is successful and the filing is found to be proper, the application shall be deemed to have been properly filed as of the decision date of the appeal.
