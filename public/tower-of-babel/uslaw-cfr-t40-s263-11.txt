40 C.F.R. § 263.11 — EPA identification number.
Citation: us:cfr:40:263.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-263.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 263 (Standards Applicable To Transporters Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A transporter must not transport hazardous wastes without having received an EPA identification number from the Administrator.
(b) A transporter who has not received an EPA identification number may obtain one by applying to the Administrator using EPA Form 8700-12. Upon receiving the request, the Administrator will assign an EPA identification number to the transporter.
