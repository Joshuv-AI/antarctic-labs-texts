16 U.S.C. § 2644 — Relationship to other authority
Citation: us:usc:16:2644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2644
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 46 (Public Utility Regulatory Policies); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit or affect any authority of the Secretary or the Commission under any other provision of law.
