18 C.F.R. § 740.12 — Amendments.
Citation: us:cfr:18:740.12
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-740.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 740 (State Water Management Planning Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council may amend all or portions of these guidelines in accordance with established procedures. If it does, it will:
(a) Consult with appropriate advisory groups;
(b) Publish such proposed rulemaking in the Federal Register; and
(c) Simultaneously provide a copy of such proposed changes to each designated agency.
