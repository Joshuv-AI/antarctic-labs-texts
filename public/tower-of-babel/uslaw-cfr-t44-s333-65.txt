44 C.F.R. § 333.65 — Compliance conflicts.
Citation: us:cfr:44:333.65
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-333.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 333 (Emergency Management Priorities And Allocations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If compliance with any provision of the Defense Production Act and other applicable statutes, this part, or an official action would prevent a person from filling a rated order or from complying with another provision of the Defense Production Act, this part, or an official action, the person must immediately notify FEMA for resolution of the conflict.
