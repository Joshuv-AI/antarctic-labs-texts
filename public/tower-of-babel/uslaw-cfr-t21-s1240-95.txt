21 C.F.R. § 1240.95 — Sanitation of water boats.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1240.95
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1240.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1240 (Control Of Communicable Diseases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No vessel engaged in interstate traffic shall obtain water for drinking and culinary purposes from any water boat unless the tanks, piping, and other appurtenances used by the water boat in the loading, transportation, and delivery of such drinking and culinary water, have been approved by the Commissioner of Food and Drugs.
