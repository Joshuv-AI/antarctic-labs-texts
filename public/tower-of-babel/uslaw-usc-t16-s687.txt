16 U.S.C. § 687 — Grand Canyon Game Preserve included in park
Citation: us:usc:16:687
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section687
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 6 (Game And Bird Preserves; Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such parts of the Grand Canyon National Game Preserve, designated under authority of section 684 of this title, as are by this Act included with 1 the Grand Canyon National Park are excluded and eliminated from said game preserve.
