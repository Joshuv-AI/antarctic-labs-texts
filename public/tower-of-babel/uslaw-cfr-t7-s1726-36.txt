7 C.F.R. § 1726.36 — Documents subject to RUS approval.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1726.36
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1726.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1726 (Electric System Construction Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise indicated, the borrower shall make all contracts and amendments that are subject to RUS approval effective only upon RUS approval.
