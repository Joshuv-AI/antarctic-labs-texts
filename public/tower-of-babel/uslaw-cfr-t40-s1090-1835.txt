40 C.F.R. § 1090.1835 — Alternative procedures for certified pentane blenders.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.1835
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.1835
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An auditor must perform the procedures specified in this section instead of or in addition to the applicable procedures in § 1090.1810 for a certified pentane blender that blends certified pentane into PCG under § 1090.1320(b).
(b) An auditor must apply the procedures in § 1090.1830 by substituting “pentane” for “butane” in all cases.
