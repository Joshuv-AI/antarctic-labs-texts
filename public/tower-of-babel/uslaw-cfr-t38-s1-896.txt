38 C.F.R. § 1.896 — Publicizing vacancies.
Citation: us:cfr:38:1.896
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-1.896
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 1 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When applicants from outside the Federal service are desired, part-time vacancies may be publicized through various recruiting means, such as:
(a) Federal Job Information Centers.
(b) State Employment offices.
(c) VA Recruiting Bulletins.
