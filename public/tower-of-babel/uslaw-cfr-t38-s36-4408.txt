38 C.F.R. § 36.4408 — Submission of proof to the Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:38:36.4408
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-36.4408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 36 (Loan Guaranty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may, at any time, require submission of such proof of costs and other matters as the Secretary deems necessary.
