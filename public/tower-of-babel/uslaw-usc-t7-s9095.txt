7 U.S.C. § 9095 — Tracking of benefits
Citation: us:usc:7:9095
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section9095
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 115 (Agricultural Commodity Policy And Programs); Subchapter V (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after February 7, 2014, the Secretary may track the benefits provided, directly or indirectly, to individuals and entities under titles I and II and the amendments made by those titles.
