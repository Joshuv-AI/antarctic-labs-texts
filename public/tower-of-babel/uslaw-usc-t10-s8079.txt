10 U.S.C. § 8079 — Appointment of chiropractors in the Medical Service Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:8079
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8079
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 809 (Bureaus; Office Of The Judge Advocate General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Chiropractors who are qualified under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Navy may be appointed as commissioned officers in the Medical Service Corps of the Navy.
