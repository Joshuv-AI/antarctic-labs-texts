21 C.F.R. § 161.136 — Olympia oysters.
Citation: us:cfr:21:161.136
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-161.136
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 161 (Fish And Shellfish). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Olympia oysters, raw Olympia oysters, shucked Olympia oysters, are of the species Ostrea lurida and conform to the definition and standard of identity prescribed for oysters in § 161.130.
