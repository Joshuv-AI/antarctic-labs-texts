40 C.F.R. § 471.100 — Applicability; description of the powder metals subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:471.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-471.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 471 (Nonferrous Metals Forming And Metal Powders Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges of pollutants to waters of the United States, and introductions of pollutants into publicly owned treatment works from the process operations of the metal powders subcategory.
