48 C.F.R. § 2933.209 — 2933.209 Suspected fraudulent claims.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2933.209
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2933.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2933 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer must refer all matters relating to suspected fraudulent claims by a contractor under the conditions in FAR 33.209 to the Office of the Inspector General for further action or investigation.
