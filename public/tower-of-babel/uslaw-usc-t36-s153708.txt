36 U.S.C. § 153708 — Principal office
Citation: us:usc:36:153708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153708
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1537 (National Woman’S Relief Corps, Auxiliary To The Grand Army Of The Republic). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the corporation shall be in Springfield, Illinois. However, the activities of the corporation are not confined to Springfield but may be conducted throughout the States of the United States and the District of Columbia.
