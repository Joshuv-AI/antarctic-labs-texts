33 U.S.C. § 3855 — Warnings, detentions, dismissals, exclusion
Citation: us:usc:33:3855
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3855
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 51 (Clean Hulls); Subchapter IV (Action On Violation, Penalties, And Referrals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
If a vessel is detected to be in violation of the Convention, this chapter, or any regulation prescribed under this chapter, the Secretary may warn, detain, dismiss, or exclude the vessel from any port or offshore terminal under the jurisdiction of the United States.
(b) Notifications
If action is taken under subsection (a), the Secretary, in consultation with the Secretary of State, shall make the notifications required by the Convention.
