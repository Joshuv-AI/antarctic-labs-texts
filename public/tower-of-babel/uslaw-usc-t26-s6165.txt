26 U.S.C. § 6165 — Bonds where time to pay tax or deficiency has been extended
Citation: us:usc:26:6165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6165
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 62 (Time And Place For Paying Tax); Subchapter B (Extensions Of Time For Payment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the event the Secretary grants any extension of time within which to pay any tax or any deficiency therein, the Secretary may require the taxpayer to furnish a bond in such amount (not exceeding double the amount with respect to which the extension is granted) conditioned upon the payment of the amount extended in accordance with the terms of such extension.
