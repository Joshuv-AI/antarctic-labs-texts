49 C.F.R. § 214.519 — Floors, decks, stairs, and ladders of on-track roadway maintenance machines.
Citation: us:cfr:49:214.519
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-214.519
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 214 (Railroad Workplace Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Floors, decks, stairs, and ladders of on-track roadway maintenance machines shall be of appropriate design and maintained to provide secure access and footing, and shall be free of oil, grease, or any obstruction which creates a slipping, falling, or fire hazard.
