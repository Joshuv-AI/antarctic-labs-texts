40 C.F.R. § 417.90 — Applicability; description of the oleum sulfonation and sulfation subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:417.90
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-417.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 417 (Soap And Detergent Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of sulfonic acid and sulfuric acid esters by means of sulfonation and sulfation of raw materials, including but not limited to petroleum derived alkyls, employing oleum in either continuous or batch processes.
