7 C.F.R. § 501.4 — Conformity with signs and emergency directions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:501.4
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-501.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 501 (Conduct On U.S. Meat Animal Research Center, Clay Center, Nebraska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons in and on property of the Research Center shall comply with official signs of a prohibitory or directory nature, and with the directions of authorized individuals.
