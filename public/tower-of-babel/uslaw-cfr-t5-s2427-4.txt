5 C.F.R. § 2427.4 — Submissions from interested parties.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2427.4
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2427.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2427 (General Statements Of Policy Or Guidance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to issuance of a general statement of policy or guidance the Authority, as it deems appropriate, will afford an opportunity to interested parties to express their views orally or in writing.
