7 C.F.R. § 985.25 — Alternate members.
Citation: us:cfr:7:985.25
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-985.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 985 (Marketing Order Regulating The Handling Of Spearmint Oil Produced In The Far West). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alternate for a member shall act in the place of such member (a) in the member's absence, (b) in the event of the member's death, removal, resignation, or disqualification, until a successor for the member's unexpired term has been selected and has qualified, or (c) when requested and designated by the member.
