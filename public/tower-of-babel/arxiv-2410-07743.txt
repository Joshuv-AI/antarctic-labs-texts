Abstract
In this article, we show that conjugacy classes of classical Weyl groups W ⁡ ( B n ) W(B_{n}) and W ⁡ ( D n ) W(D_{n}) are of type D . Consequently, we obtain that Nichols algebras of irreducible Yetter-Drinfeld modules over the classical Weyl groups 𝕎 n \mathbb{W}_{n} ( n ≥ 5 n\geq 5 ) are infinite dimensional. Keywords: Rack; classical Weyl groups; Nichols algebras; infinite dimensional 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 16W50; 16T05

Introduction

The theory of Nichols algebras is motivated by the Hopf algebra theory.
It has interesting applications to other research fields such as Kac-Moody Lie superalgebras, and conformal field theory and quantum groups. In particular, the field of quantum groups and pointed Hopf
algebras over finite abelian groups has seen great progress due to the
work of Andruskiewitsch, Schneider, Heckenberger, see
[
5

[
10

In

7

(\mathcal{O},\rho)
such that the associated Nichols algebra

which imply

Nichols algebras of braided vector spaces

In

Preliminaries

Let

\displaystyle\delta(g_{i}v)=t_{i}\otimes g_{i}v,\qquad h\cdot(g_{i}v)=g_{j}(\nu_{i}(h)\cdot v),

(2.1)

where
hg_{i}=g_{j}\nu_{i}(h)
, for
unique
1\leq j\leq m
and
\nu_{i}(h)\in G^{s}

the braiding is then given by

C(g_{i}v\otimes g_{j}w)=t_{i}\cdot(g_{j}w)\otimes g_{i}v=g_{j^{\prime}}(\nu_{j}(t_{i})\cdot w)\otimes g_{i}v

(2.2)

for any

. Let

denote

M({\mathcal{O}}_{s},\rho)
is a simple YD module (see
[
6
]
).

A rack
is a pair
(X,\rhd)
, where
X\neq\emptyset,\rhd:X\times X\rightarrow X
is an operation such that

x\rhd(y\rhd z)=(x\rhd y)\rhd(x\rhd z)

and the map
\phi_{x}:y\rightarrow x\rhd y

is invertible, for any
x,y\in X.

For example,
({\mathcal{O}}_{s}^{G},\rhd)
is a rack with

Assume

and
S
are two subracks of

with
R\cup S=X
,
R\cap S=\emptyset
. For any

, if

, then

is called a
decomposition of subracks
of

. Moreover, if there exist
\sigma\in R
,
\tau\in S
such that

, then

is called to be of
type D
. Notice that if
X
is a subrack of a rack

, and

is of
type D
, then

is also of
type D
.
If
G
is a finite group and

is of
type D
, then
{\rm dim}~\mathfrak{B}(\mathcal{O}_{\sigma}^{G},\rho)=\infty
for any
\rho\in\widehat{G^{\sigma}}
(see
[
2

).
See
[
4
]
,
[
2
]
for more details.
