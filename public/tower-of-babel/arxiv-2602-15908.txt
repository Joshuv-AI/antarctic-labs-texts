Abstract
The purpose of this paper is to give an explicit and elementary construction for the Lie algebras of type G 2 ​ ( K ) G_{2}(K) of dimension 14 14 , over the field K K of characteristic 2 2 . We say an elementary construction on the account that we use not more than little naive linear algebra notions.
