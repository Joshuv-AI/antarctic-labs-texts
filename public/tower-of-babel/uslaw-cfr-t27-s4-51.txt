27 C.F.R. § 4.51 — Exhibiting certificates to Government officials.
Citation: us:cfr:27:4.51
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-4.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 4 (Labeling And Advertising Of Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any bottler or packer holding an original or duplicate original of a certificate of label approval or a certificate of exemption shall, upon demand, exhibit such certificate to a duly authorized representative of the United States Government.
