42 C.F.R. § 478.26 — Delegation of the reconsideration function.
Citation: us:cfr:42:478.26
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-478.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 478 (Reconsiderations And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A QIO may delegate the authority to reconsider an initial determination to a nonfacility subcontractor, including the organization that made the initial determination as a QIO subcontractor.
