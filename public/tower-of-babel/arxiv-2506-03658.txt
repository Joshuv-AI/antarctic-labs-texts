Time discretization of a semi-discrete scheme for 3D Chemotaxis-Navier-stokes system driven by transport noise
Erika Hausenblas a , Boris Jidjou Moghomye a and Paul André Razafimandimby b

Abstract
. This work is devoted to the convergence of a time-discrete numerical scheme of a semi-discretization model arising from biology, consisting of a chemotaxis equation coupled with a Galerkin approximation of Navier-Stokes system driven by transport noise in a three-dimensional bounded and convex domain. We propose a semi-implicit Euler numerical scheme approximating the infinite dimensional model, for which we study the well-posedness and derive some uniform estimates for the discrete variables.

\begin{split}I_{N}(\theta)&:=\int_{\theta}^{T-\theta}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}^{4}ds\\

&=\left[\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-2}\left(\int_{t_{\ell}}^{t_{\ell+1}-\theta}+\int^{t_{\ell+1}}_{t_{\ell+1}-\theta}\right)+\int_{t_{N-1}}^{t_{N}-\theta}\right]\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}^{4}ds.\end{split}

We note that

\mathbb{E}\sup_{0<\theta\leq T}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(I_{N}(\theta\right))^{\frac{1}{4}}\leq\mathbb{E}\sup_{0<\theta\leq h}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(I_{N}(\theta\right))^{\frac{1}{4}}+\mathbb{E}\sup_{h<\theta\leq T}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(I_{N}(\theta\right))^{\frac{1}{4}}.

We then distinguish two cases: the case
0<\theta\leq h
and the case
h<\theta\leq T
.

Case (a):
0<\theta\leq h
.

We recall that for any
\ell\in\{0,1,...,N-1\}
,

\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(t)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}\leq\frac{\left|t-s\right|}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|_{0,2},\ \forall t,s\in[t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1}].

Let us fix
\ell\in\{0,...,N-1\}
and take
s\in[t_{\ell+1}-\theta,t_{\ell+1}]\subset[t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1}]
. Then
|t_{\ell+1}-s|\leq\theta
. Moreover,
s+\theta\in[t_{\ell+1},t_{\ell+1}+\theta]\subset[t_{\ell+1},t_{\ell+2}]
and
|(s+\theta)-t_{\ell+1}|\leq\theta
. Hence,

\begin{split}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}&\leq\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}\right\|_{0,2}+\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}\\
&\leq\frac{\theta}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}^{\ell+2}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}\right\|_{0,2}+\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(t_{\ell+1})-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}\\
&\leq\frac{\theta}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}^{\ell+2}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}\right\|_{0,2}+\frac{\left|t_{\ell+1}-s\right|}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|_{0,2}\\
&\leq\frac{\theta}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}^{\ell+2}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}\right\|_{0,2}+\frac{\theta}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|_{0,2}.\end{split}

This implies that (since
\theta\leq h
)

\begin{split}&\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-2}\int^{t_{\ell+1}}_{t_{\ell+1}-\theta}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}^{4}ds\\
&\leq\mathcal{R}\left(\frac{\theta}{h}\right)^{4}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-2}\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}^{\ell+2}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}+\mathcal{R}\left(\frac{\theta}{h}\right)^{4}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-2}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}\\
&\leq\mathcal{R}\left(\frac{\theta}{h}\right)^{4}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}\frac{\theta}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}.\end{split}

Accordingly, now let
s\in[t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1}-\theta]\subset[t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1}]
. Then also
s+\theta\in[t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1}]
and hence

\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}\leq\frac{\theta}{h}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|_{0,2}.

Consequently, by using (
4.3
), we have
I_{N}(\theta)\leq\mathcal{R}\left(\frac{\theta}{h}\right)^{4}h\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}
. Thus,

\sup_{0<\theta\leq h}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(I_{N}(\theta\right))^{\frac{1}{4}}\leq\mathcal{R}\sup_{0<\theta\leq h}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\frac{\theta}{h}\left(h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}.

By using the Hölder inequality, the first inequality of Lemma
3.6
with
j=1
(so that
t_{j}=t_{1}=h
) and the fact that
\theta\leq h
, we get

\mathbb{E}\sup_{0<\theta\leq h}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(I_{N}(\theta\right))^{\frac{1}{4}}\leq\mathcal{R}\sup_{0<\theta\leq h}\frac{\theta^{\frac{1}{2}}}{h}\left(\mathbb{E}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}\leq\mathcal{R}_{m}\sup_{0<\theta\leq h}\frac{\theta^{\frac{1}{2}}}{h^{\frac{1}{2}}}\leq\mathcal{R}_{m}.

Case (b):
h<\theta\leq T
.
In this case, for any
\theta>h
, we can find
1\leq j\leq N-1
and
\eta\in(0,1)
such that
\theta=h(j+\eta)
. For any
\ell\in\{0,1,...,N-1\}
, let
s\in[t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1}]\cap[0,T-\theta]
. Then, by the triangle inequality we have

\begin{split}&\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}\\
&\leq\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+\theta)-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+t_{j})\right\|_{0,2}+\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(s+t_{j})-\mathbf{u}_{N}(t_{\ell+j})\right\|_{0,2}\\
&\qquad+\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(t_{\ell+j})-\mathbf{u}_{N}(t_{\ell})\right\|_{0,2}+\left\|\mathbf{u}_{N}(t_{\ell})-\mathbf{u}_{N}(s)\right\|_{0,2}\\
&:=I+II+III+IV.\end{split}

We may proceed as in the case (a) to control the terms I, II and IV to arrive at

I_{N}(\theta)\leq 3\times 4^{3}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}+4^{3}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-j}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+j}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}.

By using the Hölder inequality and the first inequality of Lemma
3.6
as well as the fact that
t_{1}=h
,
h<\theta
and
\theta=t_{j}+h\eta>t_{j}
, with
\eta\in(0,1)
, we get

\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\sup_{h<\theta\leq T}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(I_{N}(\theta\right))^{\frac{1}{4}}&\leq\mathcal{R}\sup_{h<\theta\leq T}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(\mathbb{E}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-j}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+1}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}+\mathbb{E}h\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-j}\left\|\mathbf{u}^{\ell+j}-\mathbf{u}^{\ell}\right\|^{4}_{0,2}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}\\
&\leq\mathcal{R}_{m}\sup_{h<\theta\leq T}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(t_{1}^{2}+t_{j}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}\\
&\leq\mathcal{R}_{m}\sup_{h<\theta\leq T}\theta^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(\theta^{2}+\theta^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}=\mathcal{R}_{m},\end{split}

which completes the proof of Lemma
4.3
.
