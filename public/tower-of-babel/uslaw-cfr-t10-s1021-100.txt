10 C.F.R. § 1021.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1021.100
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1021.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1021 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish certain procedures that the Department of Energy (DOE) shall use to comply with the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4332(2)), as amended. This part applies to all Departmental Elements.
