14 C.F.R. § 137.59 — Inspection authority.
Citation: us:cfr:14:137.59
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-137.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 137 (Agricultural Aircraft Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each holder of an agricultural aircraft operator certificate shall allow the Administrator at any time and place to make inspections, including on-the-job inspections, to determine compliance with applicable regulations and his agricultural aircraft operator certificate.
