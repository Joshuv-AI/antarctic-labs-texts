10 C.F.R. § 50.53 — Jurisdictional limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:50.53
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-50.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 50 (Domestic Licensing Of Production And Utilization Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No license under this part shall be deemed to have been issued for activities which are not under or within the jurisdiction of the United States.
