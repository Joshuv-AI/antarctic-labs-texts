42 C.F.R. § 512.235 — Included RT services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:512.235
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-512.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 512 (Standard Provisions For Mandatory Innovation Center Models And Specific Provisions For Certain Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Only the following RT services furnished using an included modality identified at § 512.240 for an included cancer type are included RT services that are paid for by CMS under § 512.265:
(1) Treatment planning;
(2) Technical preparation and special services;
(3) Treatment delivery; and,
(4) Treatment management.
(b) All other RT services furnished by an RO participant during the Model performance period are subject to Medicare FFS payment rules.
