19 U.S.C. § 2014 — Termination of proclamations
Citation: us:usc:19:2014
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2014
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 8 (Automotive Products); Subchapter II (Basic Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized at any time to terminate, in whole or in part, any proclamation issued pursuant to section 2011 or 2012 of this title.
