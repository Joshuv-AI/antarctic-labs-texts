16 U.S.C. § 4605 — Authority to execute contracts
Citation: us:usc:16:4605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4605
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 66 (Take Pride In America Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to enter into contracts and cooperative agreements and generally to do any and all lawful acts necessary or appropriate to further the purposes of the TPIA Program.
