Fisheries of the Exclusive Economic Zone Off Alaska; Reallocation 
of Atka Mackerel in the Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands Management Area

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Temporary rule; reallocation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: NMFS is reallocating the projected unused amount of the 2026 
Atka mackerel incidental catch allowance (ICA) for the Bering Sea 
subarea and Eastern Aleutian district (BS/EAI) to the Amendment 80 
cooperative allocation for the BS/EAI in the Bering Sea and Aleutian 
Islands management area (BSAI). This action is necessary to allow the 
2026 total allowable catch (TAC) of Atka mackerel in the BS/EAI to be 
fully harvested.

DATES: Effective October 16, 2026 through 2400 hours, A.l.t., December 
31, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Steve Whitney, 907-586-7228.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: NMFS manages the groundfish fishery in the

[[Page 65695]]

BSAI according to the Fishery Management Plan for Groundfish of the 
Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands Management Area (FMP) prepared and 
recommended by the North Pacific Fishery Management Council under 
authority of the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery Conservation and Management 
Act (Magnuson-Stevens Act). Regulations governing fishing by U.S. 
vessels in accordance with the FMP appear at subpart H of 50 CFR part 
600 and 50 CFR part 679.
    The 2026 Atka mackerel ICA for the BSAI trawl limited access sector 
and the non-trawl gear sector for the BS/EAI is 800 metric tons (mt) 
and the 2026 Atka mackerel TAC allocated to the Amendment 80 sector for 
the BS/EAI is 32,819 mt as established by the final 2026 and 2027 
harvest specifications for groundfish in the BSAI (91 FR 11750, March 
10, 2026). For 2026, all Amendment 80 vessels are part of the sole 
Amendment 80 cooperative, so the TAC allocated to the Amendment 80 
sector is allocated entirely to the sole Amendment 80 cooperative and 
no TAC is assigned to the Amendment 80 limited access fishery.
    The Regional Administrator, Alaska Region, NMFS has determined that 
700 mt of the Atka mackerel ICA for the BS/EAI will not be harvested. 
Therefore, in accordance with Sec.  679.91(f), NMFS reallocates 700 mt 
of Atka mackerel from the BS/EAI ICA to the BS/EAI Amendment 80 
cooperative allocation in the BS/EAI. In accordance with Sec.  
679.91(f), NMFS will reissue the cooperative quota permit for the 
reallocated Atka mackerel following the procedures set forth in Sec.  
679.91(f)(3).
    The harvest specifications for Atka mackerel included in the final 
2026 and 2027 harvest specifications for groundfish in the BSAI (91 FR 
11750, March 10, 2026) are revised as follows: 100 mt of Atka mackerel 
for the BS/EAI ICA and 33,519 mt of Atka mackerel for the Amendment 80 
cooperative allocation for the BS/EAI. Table 7 is revised and

    The Regional Administrator considered the following factors in 
reaching this decision: (1) the current catch of the Atka mackerel ICA 
in the BS/EAI, and (2) the harvest capacity and stated intent on future 
harvesting patterns of the Amendment 80 cooperative that participates 
in this BS/EAI fishery.

Classification

    NMFS issues this action pursuant to section 305(d) of the Magnuson-
Stevens Act. This action is authorized by 50 CFR part 679, which was 
issued pursuant to section 304(b) of the Magnuson-Stevens Act, and is 
exempt from review under Executive Order 12866.
    Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B), there is good cause to waive prior 
notice and an opportunity for public comment on this action, as notice 
and comment would be impracticable and contrary to

[[Page 65696]]

the public interest, as it would prevent NMFS from responding to the 
most recent fisheries data on Atka mackerel catch in the BS/EAI in a 
timely fashion and would not allow for the full harvest of the Atka 
mackerel TAC in the BS/EAI fishery by the sector with harvesting 
capacity. NMFS was unable to publish a notice providing time for public 
comment because the most recent, relevant data on the catch of Atka 
mackerel in the BS/EAI only became available as of October 12, 2026.
    There is good cause under 5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3) to waive the 30-day 
delay in the effective date of this action. This finding is based upon 
the reasons provided above for waiver of prior notice and opportunity 
for public comment.
