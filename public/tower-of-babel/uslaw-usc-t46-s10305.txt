46 U.S.C. § 10305 — Manner of signing agreement
Citation: us:usc:46:10305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10305
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 103 (Foreign And Intercoastal Voyages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The agreement required by section 10302 of this title shall be signed—
(1)
first by the master and dated at that time, after which each seaman shall sign; and
(2)
in the presence of the master or individual in charge.
