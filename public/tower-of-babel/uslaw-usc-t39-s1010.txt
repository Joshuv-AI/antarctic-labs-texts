39 U.S.C. § 1010 — Administration of oaths related to postal inspection matters
Citation: us:usc:39:1010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section1010
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 10 (Employment Within The Postal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Officers and employees of the Postal Service performing duties related to the inspection of postal matters may administer oaths required or authorized by law or regulation with respect to any matter coming before them in the performance of their official duties.
