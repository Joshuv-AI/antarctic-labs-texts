22 U.S.C. § 285m — Withdrawal rights covering United States Special Resources
Citation: us:usc:22:285m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section285m
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XIV (Asian Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall have the right to withdraw all or part of the United States Special Resources and any accrued resources derived therefrom under the procedures provided for in section 8.03 of the Special Funds Rules and Regulations of the Bank.
