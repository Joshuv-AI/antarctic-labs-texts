42 U.S.C. § 2385 — Installations transferable
Citation: us:usc:42:2385
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2385
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter VII (Municipalities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All municipal installations are authorized to be transferred under this subchapter, but shall not include property which the Commission determines to be needed for its own use.
