7 U.S.C. § 65 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:7:65
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section65
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 2 (Cotton Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
