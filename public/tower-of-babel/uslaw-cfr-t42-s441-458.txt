42 C.F.R. § 441.458 — Involuntary disenrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:441.458
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-441.458
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 441 (Services: Requirements And Limits Applicable To Specific Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) States must specify the conditions under which a participant may be involuntarily disenrolled from the self-directed PAS option.
(b) CMS must approve the State's conditions under which a participant may be involuntarily disenrolled.
(c) The State must specify in the section 1915(j) State plan amendment the safeguards that are in place to ensure continuity of services during the transition from self-directed PAS.
