7 U.S.C. § 4916 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:4916
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4916
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 80 (Watermelon Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter, except that the funds so appropriated shall not be available for the payment of any expenses or expenditures of the Board in administering any provision of any plan issued under authority of this chapter.
