20 C.F.R. § 404.1821 — Garnishment of payments after disbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.1821
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.1821
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Payments that are covered by section 207 of the Social Security Act and made by direct deposit are subject to 31 CFR part 212, Garnishment of Accounts Containing Federal Benefit Payments.
(b) This section may be amended only by a rulemaking issued jointly by the Department of Treasury and the agencies defined as a “benefit agency” in 31 CFR 212.3.
