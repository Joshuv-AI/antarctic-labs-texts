6 U.S.C. § 674 — No private right of action
Citation: us:usc:6:674
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section674
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XVIII (Cybersecurity And Infrastructure Security Agency); Part B (Critical Infrastructure Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part may be construed to create a private right of action for enforcement of any provision of this chapter.
