48 C.F.R. § 2942.101 — 2942.101 Contract audit responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2942.101
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2942.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2942 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall insert the clause at DOLAR 2952.242-70, Access to Contractor Business Systems, in all solicitations and contracts that include a covered contractor system, which is a system that is owned by, or operated by or for, a contractor that processes, stores, or transmits Federal information.
