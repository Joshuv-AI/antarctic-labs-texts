18 U.S.C. § 3047 — Multiple warrants unnecessary
Citation: us:usc:18:3047
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3047
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 203 (Arrest And Commitment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When two or more charges are made, or two or more indictments are found against any person, only one writ or warrant shall be necessary to commit him for trial. It shall be sufficient to state in the writ the name or general character of the offenses, or to refer to them only in general terms.
