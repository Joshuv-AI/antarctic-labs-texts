33 U.S.C. § 3851 — Criminal enforcement
Citation: us:usc:33:3851
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3851
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 51 (Clean Hulls); Subchapter IV (Action On Violation, Penalties, And Referrals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who knowingly violates paragraph (2), (3), (4), or (5) of section 3841(a) of this title or section 3841(b) of this title shall be fined under title 18 or imprisoned not more than 6 years, or both.
