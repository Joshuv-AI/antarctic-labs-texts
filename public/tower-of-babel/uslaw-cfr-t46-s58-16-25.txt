46 C.F.R. § 58.16-25 — -25 Odorization.

(a) All liquefied petroleum gases must be effectively odorized by an agent of such character as to indicate positively by a distinctive odor, the presence of gas down to concentration in air of not over one-fifth the lower limit of combustibility.
