36 U.S.C. § 150509 — Headquarters and principal office
Citation: us:usc:36:150509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150509
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1505 (National Conference Of State Societies, Washington, District Of Columbia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The headquarters and principal office of the corporation shall be in the District of Columbia. However, the activities of the corporation are not confined to the District of Columbia but may be conducted throughout the States, territories, and possessions of the United States.
