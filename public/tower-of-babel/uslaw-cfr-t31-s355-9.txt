31 C.F.R. § 355.9 — What should I do if I recover a check reported as lost, stolen, destroyed, or not received?
Citation: us:cfr:31:355.9
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-355.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 355 (Regulations Governing Fiscal Agency Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you recover the original check you must notify us in writing. If we have not yet issued a replacement check, we will remove the stop payment order against the original check. If we have already issued a replacement check, you must return the original check to us.
