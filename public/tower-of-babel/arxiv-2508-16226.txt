Universal quantum control over Majorana zero modes
Zhu-yao Jin, Jun Jing

Abstract
Majorana zero mode (MZM) exhibits inherent resilience to local parametric fluctuations in quantum information processing, due to a topological protection mechanism in the non-Abelian braiding statistics of the anyonic quasiparticles. In this paper, we construct the braiding operations between arbitrary pair of three MZMs under the framework of universal quantum control (UQC). Largely detuned driving fields on the mediator, a local defect of lattice, enable indirect and tunable exchange interaction among arbitrary two MZMs. The braiding operations can then present through the evolution along the universal nonadiabatic passages, whose robustness against the driving-field errors can be substantially enhanced by rapid modulation of the passage-dependent global phase. Moreover, a chiral population transfer on MZMs along the universal passage can be perfectly demonstrated in both clockwise and counterclockwise manners. Our scale-free protocol therefore provides an avenue towards the universal quantum control over MZMs, which is fundamental and essential for topological quantum computation.

Introduction

Certain problems
Bravyi et al. 2018
can show provable advantages of quantum computer over its classical counterpart, i.e., Shor’s algorithm for factoring large numbers
Calderbank and Shor 1996
;
Ekert and Jozsa 1996
;
Kivlichan et al. 2020
and Grover’s algorithm for amplifying probability of target states
Grover 1997
;
Nagib et al. 2025
. However, practical quantum computer demands large-scale quantum systems with extensive gate operations
Gidney and Ekerå 2019
. Each elementary operation can introduce systematic errors that accumulate exponentially, posing significance of error correction schemes, such as the Shor’s code
Shor 1995
. That scheme encodes a single logical qubit into nine physical qubits and can detect and correct single-qubit errors. Another paradigm for error correction is topological quantum computation
Nayak et al. 2008
;
Freedman 1998
;
Kitaev 2001
;
Kitaev 2003
;
Das Sarma et al. 2005
;
Stern and Halperin 2006
;
Bonderson et al. 2006
, in which qubits are encoded on the non-Abelian anyonic quasiparticles
Nayak et al. 2008
. Quantum information encoded in those non-local degrees of freedom is believed to maintain intrinsic fault-tolerance against local parametric fluctuations.

Anyons fundamentally bifurcate into the Abelian
Wilczek 1990
and non-Abelian categories
Read and Green 2000
;
Ivanov 2001
;
Alicea et al. 2011
;
Sarma et al. 2015
. The unitary evolutions resulted from braiding Abelian anyons yield the accumulation of phases: the wave function of multiple Abelian anyons acquires a fractional phase factor
e^{i\theta}
,
\theta\neq 0,\pi
, on the exchange of quasiparticles. Bosons (
\theta=0
) and fermions (
\theta=\pi
) are the limiting cases
Wilczek 1990
. Besides the phase accumulation, the braid group
Ivanov 2001
;
Alicea et al. 2011
;
Sarma et al. 2015
associated with the non-Abelian anyons enables nontrivial unitary operations among the degenerate many-quasiparticle states. Due to the fact that the qubits encoded in the global degenerate states are robust against the local systematic errors
Witzel and Das Sarma 2006
, the non-Abelian anyons can serve as the primitive units of topological quantum computation.

Majorana zero mode (MZM), first theoretically predicted in the Kitaev chain model
Kitaev 2001
, represents the most elementary realization of the non-Abelian anyons. The Kitaev chain models a one-dimensional system of spinless fermions and was once regarded as a somewhat unphysical toy model
Elliott and Franz 2015
. Nevertheless, the spinless fermions behavior can be simulated through experimentally accessible proxy systems, such as the spin-orbit-coupling electron gases under Zeeman field, as suggested by the subsequent theoretical studies
Elliott and Franz 2015
;
Fu and Kane 2008
;
Sau et al. 2010
;
Alicea 2010
;
Lutchyn et al. 2010
;
Oreg et al. 2010
;
Nadj-Perge et al. 2013
;
Pientka et al. 2013
;
Gao et al. 2024
. Experimental researches for MZM have been conducted in multiple physical platforms, including the superconducting systems
Nadj-Perge et al. 2014
;
Kim et al. 2018
;
Jiao et al. 2018
;
Schneider et al. 2020
;
Yang et al. 2021
and hybrid magnetic-superconductor systems
Schneider et al. 2022
. Recently, indirect evidences for MZMs have been witnessed in the hybrid semiconductor-superconductor system
Quantum 2025
, exhibiting a zero-bias conductance peak
Das et al. 2012
;
Deng et al. 2012
;
Finck et al. 2013
;
Churchill et al. 2013
and the topological protection against the variations of local parameters
Jiao et al. 2018
;
Yang et al. 2021
;
Quantum 2025
. The braiding of MZMs, however, remains the gold-standard signature for definitively establishing their non-Abelian statistics

.

Conventional braiding operations of MZMs were proposed under the adiabatic condition
Ivanov 2001
;
Stone and Chung 2006
;
Read 2009
;
Sau et al. 2011
;
van Heck et al. 2012
;
Kraus et al. 2013
and governed by the Berry-Wilczek-Zee holonomy
Wilczek and Zee 1984
;
Cheng et al. 2011
. In practice, the implementation of topological quantum computation based on the adiabatic braiding operations is constrained by the extended operation time. First, the prolonged exposure to the environment can induce ruinable decoherence effect on the quantum systems
Carmichael 1999
. Second, while the exact adiabatic solution is established within infinite evolution time, the realistic operation with a finite clock speed unavoidably entails nonzero non-adiabatic errors
Nayak et al. 2008
. Such errors will lead to the leakage from the subspace of degenerate ground states to the external Hilbert space. Third, the long operation time of topological quantum computer will increase the possibility of the quasiparticle poisoning
Catelani et al. 2011
;
Karzig et al. 2021
;
Liu et al. 2024
, i.e., a quasiparticle enters the collection of MZMs and changes the parity of the states. Although the measurement-based tools
Knapp et al. 2016
have been used to mitigate unwanted transition errors during adiabatic braid operations
Bonderson et al. 2008
;
Bomantara and Gong 2020
, a fast and high-fidelity nonadiabatic braiding operation remains desired for MZMs.

Inspired by transitionless quantum driving
Berry 2009
, the shortcuts to non-Abelian braiding
Karzig et al. 2015
;
Song et al. 2025
exploit the time-dependent and direct exchange interaction between MZMs as the counterdiabatic driving fields. But practical implementation of such interactions in semiconductor-superconductor systems is fundamentally difficult
Amundsen et al. 2024
. In analog to nonadiabatic holonomic quantum computation
Sjöqvist et al. 2012
;
Liu et al. 2019
, the nonadiabatic braiding operations
Yu et al. 2025
have been demonstrated between two MZMs mediated by the lattice defect (LD). However, the braiding operations under the parallel-transport condition
Jing et al. 2017
is sensitive to the systematic errors of the local driving fields on LD.

We here construct the braiding operations between any pair of three MZMs mediated with LD under local driving fields by universal quantum control (UQC)
Jin and Jing 2025a
;
Jin and Jing 2025b
;
Jin and Jing 2025c
;
Jin and Jing 2025d
. It is a theoretical framework that unifies the shortcuts to adiabaticity and holonomic quantum transformation and is beyond the parallel-transport condition. The degree of freedom of LD can be eliminated
Jin and Jing 2025d
;
James and Jerke 2007
when the driving fields are largely detuned; and then it enables indirect exchange interaction among MZMs. Consequently, the braiding operations of arbitrary two MZMs can be performed along the universal passages of the system. Our protocol presents fault-tolerant braiding operations that are robust against significant fluctuations in both transition frequency of LD and Rabi frequency of driving field. In addition, the clockwise and counterclockwise population transfers of three MZMs can be demonstrated by the time evolution along the passage that is superposed by LD excited state and MZMs.

The rest part of this paper is structured as follows. In Sec.
II.1
, we introduce a general model that is consisted of three MZMs coupled to a lattice defect and the full Hamiltonian. In Sec.
II.2
, we derive the effective Hamiltonian by using the local largely-detuned driving fields on LD, based on which the braiding operations can be established via universal nonadiabatic passages. In Sec
II.3
, we employ a rapidly-varying global phase to correct systematic errors. Fault-tolerant, fast, and high-fidelity braiding operations can then be constructed for MZMs. Section
III
devotes to designing the chiral population transfer of three MZMs in both clockwise and counterclockwise manners. The whole work is concluded in Sec.
IV
. Appendix
A
presents the UQC theory in the parametric space with ideal control setting, followed by an error-correction extension in Appendix
B
.

IV
Conclusion

In the framework of universal quantum control theory, we construct robust, fast, and high-fidelity braiding operations for Majorana zero modes that are coupled to a common two-level local defect. Also, we propose a protocol to realize the perfect chiral population transfer among three MZMs. The braiding operation between an arbitrary pair of MZMs is based on the mediated exchange interaction by using the largely detuned driving fields at LD and the subsequent nonadiabatic passage. Moreover, our universal passage could employ a rapidly-varying global phase instead of extra driving fields to significantly suppress the systematic errors in LD and the driving fields on it. In the large splitting regime of the local defect, we can construct an effective Hamiltonian for the excited state of LD and the three MZMs, by which the population can propagate in both clockwise and counterclockwise manners among three MZMs with a unit fidelity. Our protocol demonstrates fault-tolerant, nonadiabatic and scalable braiding operations and the chiral population transfer with no time-reversal-symmetry broken Hamiltonian, which are fundamental and essential for the topological quantum computation and quantum information.
