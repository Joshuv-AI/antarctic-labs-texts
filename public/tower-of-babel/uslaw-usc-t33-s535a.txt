33 U.S.C. § 535a — Congressional consent to State agreements with Canada and Mexico; Secretary of State’s approval of agreements
Citation: us:usc:33:535a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section535a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter IV (International Bridges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The consent of Congress is hereby granted for a State or a subdivision or instrumentality thereof to enter into agreements—
(1)
with the Government of Canada, a Canadian Province, or a subdivision or instrumentality of either, in the case of a bridge connecting the United States and Canada, or
(2)
with the Government of Mexico, a Mexican State, or a subdivision or instrumentality of either, in the case of a bridge connecting the United States and Mexico,
for the construction, operation, and maintenance of such bridge in accordance with the applicable provisions of this subchapter. The effectiveness of such agreement shall be conditioned on its approval by the Secretary of State.
