36 C.F.R. § 1225.10 — What Federal records must be scheduled?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1225.10
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1225.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1225 (Scheduling Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Federal records, including those created or maintained for the Government by a contractor, must be covered by a NARA-approved agency disposition authority, SF 115, Request for Records Disposition Authority, or the NARA General Records Schedules.
