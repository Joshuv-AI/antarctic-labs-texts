6 C.F.R. § 11.5 — Administrative wage garnishment.
Citation: us:cfr:6:11.5
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-11.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 11 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS may collect debts from a debtor's wages by means of administrative wage garnishment in accordance with the requirements of 31 U.S.C. 3720D under the procedures established in 31 CFR 285.11.
