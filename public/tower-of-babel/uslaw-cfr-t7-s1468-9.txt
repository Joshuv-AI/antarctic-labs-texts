7 C.F.R. § 1468.9 — Assignments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1468.9
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1468.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1468 (Agricultural Conservation Easement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person, legal entity, Indian Tribe, eligible entity, or other party entitled to any cash payment under this program may assign the right to receive such cash payments, in whole or in part.
