25 C.F.R. § 273.157 — What is the process for requesting technical assistance and/or training?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.157
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.157
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Existing contracting parties may request technical assistance and/or training by addressing the request in writing to the BIE Director.
(b) The BIE Director, or designee, will acknowledge receipt of a request for technical assistance and/or training.
(c) No later than 30 days after receiving the original request, the BIE Director will identify a point of contact and begin the process of providing technical assistance and/or training. The BIE Director and requesting contracting party will work together to identify the form, substance, and timeline for the assistance.
