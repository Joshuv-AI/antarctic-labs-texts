12 U.S.C. § 4247 — Promulgation of regulations
Citation: us:usc:12:4247
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4247
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 43 (Actions Against Persons Committing Bank Fraud Crimes); Subchapter III (Use Of Private Legal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may promulgate any rules, regulations, or guidelines that, in the Attorney General’s judgment, are necessary and appropriate to the effective administration of this subchapter.
