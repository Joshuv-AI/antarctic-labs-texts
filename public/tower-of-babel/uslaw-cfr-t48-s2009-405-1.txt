48 C.F.R. § 2009.405-1 — 2009.405-1 Continuation of current contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2009.405-1
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2009.405-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2009 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Head of the Contracting Activity is authorized to make the determination to continue contracts or subcontracts in existence at the time the contractor was debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment in accordance with FAR 9.405-1.
