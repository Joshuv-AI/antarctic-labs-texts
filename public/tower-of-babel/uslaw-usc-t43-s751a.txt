43 U.S.C. § 751a — Survey system extended to Alaska
Citation: us:usc:43:751a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section751a
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 18 (Survey Of Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The system of public land surveys is extended to the Territory of Alaska.
