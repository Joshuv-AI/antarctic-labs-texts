9 C.F.R. § 81.6 — Federal preemption of State and local laws and regulations with respect to CWD.
Citation: us:cfr:9:81.6
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-81.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 81 (Chronic Wasting Disease In Deer, Elk, And Moose). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State and local laws and regulations on farmed or captive deer, elk, or moose with respect to CWD that are more restrictive than the regulations in this part are not preempted by this part, except as described in § 81.5.
