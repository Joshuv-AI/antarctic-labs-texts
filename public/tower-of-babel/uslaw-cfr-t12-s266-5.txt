12 C.F.R. § 266.5 — Criminal penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:12:266.5
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-266.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 266 (Limitations On Activities Of Former Members And Employees Of The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any former member or employee of the Board who engages in actions in contravention of paragraph (a) or (b) of § 266.3 may be subject to criminal penalties for violation of section 207 of the United States Criminal Code (18 U.S.C. 207).
