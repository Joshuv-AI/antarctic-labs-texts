16 C.F.R. § 660.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:16:660.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-660.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 660 (Duties Of Furnishers Of Information To Consumer Reporting Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to furnishers of information to consumer reporting agencies that are motor vehicle dealers as defined by § 660.2 of this part (referred to as “furnishers”).
