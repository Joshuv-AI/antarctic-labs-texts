46 C.F.R. § 109.107 — Designation of master or person in charge.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.107
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner of a unit or his agent shall designate an individual to be the master or person in charge of the unit.
