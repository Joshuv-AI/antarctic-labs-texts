15 U.S.C. § 6151 — National Do-Not-Call Registry
Citation: us:usc:15:6151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6151
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 87A (National Do-Not-Call Registry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority
The Federal Trade Commission is authorized under section 6102(a)(3)(A) of this title to implement and enforce a national do-not-call registry.
(b) Ratification
The do-not-call registry provision of the Telemarketing Sales Rule (16 C.F.R. 310.4(b)(1)(iii)), which was promulgated by the Federal Trade Commission, effective March 31, 2003, is ratified.
