46 C.F.R. § 190.20-15 — -15 Construction.
Citation: us:cfr:46:190.20-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-190.20-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 190 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All crew spaces are to be constructed and arranged in a manner suitable to the purpose for which they are intended and so they can be kept in a clean, workable and sanitary condition.
