32 C.F.R. § 1665.8 — Systems of records exempted from certain provisions of this act.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1665.8
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1665.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1665 (Privacy Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SSS will not provide requesters information exempt from disclosure pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552a(k), (e.g., the SSS will not reveal to the suspected violator the informant's name or other identifying information relating to the informant).
