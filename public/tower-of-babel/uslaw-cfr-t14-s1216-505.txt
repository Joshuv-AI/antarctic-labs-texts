14 C.F.R. § 1216.505 — Publication of the environmental documents.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1216.505
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1216.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1216 (Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NASA will publish the entire environmental impact statement and record of decision on a public-facing NASA website. During the process of preparing the environmental impact statement, NASA may publish such draft, pre-decisional materials as in its judgment may assist in fulfilling its responsibilities under NEPA and this subpart. NASA will also file with EPA as required under § 1216.701.
