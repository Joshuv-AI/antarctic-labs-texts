48 C.F.R. § 1237.7001 — 1237.7001 Certification of data.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1237.7001
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1237.7001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1237 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Towards fulfilling DOT's policy at 1237.7000(a), contracting officers shall request information from prospective contractors for certification purposes. The type of information requested is dependent upon the criticality of the service and/or any unique or essential qualification requirements.
