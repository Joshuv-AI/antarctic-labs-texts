Gromov-Hausdorff limits of Aspherical Manifolds
Xiaochun Rong

Abstract
Let X X be a compact Gromov-Hausdorff limit space of a collapsing sequence of compact n n -manifolds, M i M_{i} , of Ricci curvature Ric M i ≥ − ( n − 1 ) \operatorname{Ric}_{M_{i}}\geq-(n-1) and all points in M i M_{i} are ( δ , ρ ) (\delta,\rho) -local rewinding Reifenberg points, or sectional curvature sec M i ≥ − 1 \operatorname{sec}_{M_{i}}\geq-1 , respectively. We conjecture that if M i M_{i} is an aspherical manifold of fundamental group satisfying a certain condition (e.g., a nilpotent group), then X X is a differentiable, or topological aspherical manifold, respectively. A main result in this paper asserts that if M i M_{i} a diffeomorphic or homeomorphic to a nilmanifold, then X X is diffeomorphic or homeomorphic to a nilmanifold, respectively.

1.1. Equivariant Gromov-Hausdorff convergence

Let
X,Y
be compact metric spaces. For
\epsilon>0
, a map,
f:X\to Y

\epsilon

is an
\epsilon
-isometry i.e.,
|d_{Y}(f(x),f(x^{\prime}))-d_{X}(x,x^{\prime})|<\epsilon
and
f(X)\subseteq Y
is
\epsilon
-dense.

We say that a sequence of compact metric spaces,
X_{i}
, Gromov-Hausdorff (briefly, GH) converges to a compact metric space, denoted by
X_{i}@>\operatorname{GH}>>X
, if there is a sequence of
\epsilon_{i}
-GHA,
f_{i}:X_{i}\to X
,
\epsilon_{i}\to 0
.

A sequence of compact metric spaces,
X_{i}
, contains a GH-convergent subsequence if the following Ascoli type precompactness conditions are satisfied:

(1.1.1) (Uniform bound) the diameter,
\operatorname{diam}(X_{i})\leq d
,

(1.1.2) (Equi-continuous) For
\epsilon>0
, the number of points in an
\epsilon
-net of
X_{i}
,
|X_{i}(\epsilon)|\leq c(\epsilon)
, a constant depending on
\epsilon
(an
\epsilon
-net of
X
is a subset of points that are
\epsilon
-separated and
\epsilon
-dense).

There is a natural metric on the isometry group of
X
, for
\alpha,\beta\in\operatorname{Isom}(X)
,

d(\alpha,\beta)=\max\{d(\alpha(x),\beta(x)),\,x\in X\}
, and it is easy to check
that
(\operatorname{Isom}(X),d)
is a compact metric space.

For a sequence of pairs,
(X_{i},\Gamma_{i})
, and a pair

are

equivariant GH-convergent to
