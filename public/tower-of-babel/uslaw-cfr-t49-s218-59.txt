49 C.F.R. § 218.59 — Responsibilities of railroads.
Citation: us:cfr:49:218.59
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-218.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 218 (Railroad Operating Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any railroad that operates a train when the controlling locomotive of a train is equipped with a disabled safety device is subject to a civil penalty. See FRA's website at https://railroads.dot.gov/ for a statement of agency civil penalty policy.
