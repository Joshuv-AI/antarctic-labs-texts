2 U.S.C. § 1930 — Applicable pay rate upon appointment
Citation: us:usc:2:1930
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1930
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part B (Compensation And Other Personnel Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the rate of basic pay payable to an individual upon appointment to a position with the Capitol Police shall be at a rate within the minimum and maximum pay rates applicable to the position.
(b) Effective date
This section shall apply to fiscal year 2003 and each fiscal year thereafter.
