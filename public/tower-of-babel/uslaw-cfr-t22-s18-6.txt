22 C.F.R. § 18.6 — Authority to prohibit appearances.
Citation: us:cfr:22:18.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-18.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 18 (Regulations Concerning Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 18 U.S.C 207(j), if the Director General finds, after notice and opportunity for a hearing, that a former officer or employee of the Department has violated 18 U.S.C. 207(a), (b) or (c), the Director General in his/her discretion may prohibit that person from engaging in practice before the Department for a period not to exceed five years, or may take other appropriate disciplinary action.
