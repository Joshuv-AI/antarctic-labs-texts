{resume}

Notre étude consiste à établir le lien entre l’ensemble

{abstr}

We investigate the relationship between the set
S
of Pisot numbers and the set
T
of Salem numbers. Salem first established that : " every Pisot number is an accumulation point of the set
T
". Building on Boyd’s method, we show that every accumulation point of
T
belongs to
S
. Together, these results imply that the union
S\cup T
forms a closed subset of the real half-line
\left]1,+\infty\right[
. Consequently, this settles Boyd’s conjecture while disproving Lehmer’s conjecture.

Dans cet article, nous proposons d’étudier les liens entre deux ensembles d’entiers algébriques, celui
