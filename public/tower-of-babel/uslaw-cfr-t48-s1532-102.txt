48 C.F.R. § 1532.102 — 1532.102 Description of contract financing methods.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1532.102
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1532.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1532 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion are authorized for use as a payment method under EPA contracts or subcontracts for construction and alteration or repair of buildings, structures, or other real property.
