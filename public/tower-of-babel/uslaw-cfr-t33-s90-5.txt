33 C.F.R. § 90.5 — Lights for moored vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:90.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-90.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 90 (Inland Rules: Interpretative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel at anchor includes a vessel made fast to one or more mooring buoys or other similar device attached to the ocean floor. Such vessels may be lighted as a vessel at anchor in accordance with Rule 30, or may be lighted on the corners in accordance with 33 CFR 88.30(h) through (l).
