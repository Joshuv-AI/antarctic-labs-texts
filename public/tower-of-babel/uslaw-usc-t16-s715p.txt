16 U.S.C. § 715p — Cooperation of State in enforcement of provisions
Citation: us:usc:16:715p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section715p
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter III (Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When any State shall, by suitable legislation, make provision adequately to enforce the provisions of this subchapter and all regulations promulgated thereunder, the Secretary of the Interior may so certify, and then and thereafter said State may cooperate with the Secretary of the Interior in the enforcement of this subchapter and the regulations thereunder.
