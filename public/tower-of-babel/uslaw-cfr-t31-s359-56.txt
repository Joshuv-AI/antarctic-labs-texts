31 C.F.R. § 359.56 — How can I find out what my book-entry Series I savings bonds are worth?
Citation: us:cfr:31:359.56
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-359.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 359 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Redemption values. You may access redemption values for your book-entry bonds through your New Treasury Direct account.
(b) Redemption penalty. Redemption values shown in your New Treasury Direct account reflect the three-month interest penalty applied to bonds redeemed prior to five years from the date of issue.
