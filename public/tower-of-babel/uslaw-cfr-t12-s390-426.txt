12 C.F.R. § 390.426 — Sales of securities at an office of a State savings association.
Citation: us:cfr:12:390.426
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-390.426
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 390 (Regulations Transferred From The Office Of Thrift Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sales of securities of a State savings association or its affiliates at an office of a State savings association may only be made in accordance with the provisions of § 390.340.
