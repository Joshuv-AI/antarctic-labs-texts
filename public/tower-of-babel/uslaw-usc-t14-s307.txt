14 U.S.C. § 307 — Vice admirals and admirals, continuity of grade
Citation: us:usc:14:307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section307
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 3 (Composition And Organization); Subchapter II (Positions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The continuity of an officer’s precedence on the active duty promotion list, date of rank, grade, pay, and allowances as a vice admiral or admiral shall not be interrupted by the termination of an appointment for the purpose of reappointment to another position as a vice admiral or admiral.
