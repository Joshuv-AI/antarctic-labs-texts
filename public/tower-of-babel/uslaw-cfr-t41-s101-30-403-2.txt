41 C.F.R. § 101-30.403-2 — -30.403-2 Management codes.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-30.403-2
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-30.403-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-30 (30—Federal Catalog System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For internal use within an agency, alphabetic codes excluding letters “I” and “O” may be prefixed or suffixed to the national stock number as CM7520-00-123-4567 or 7520-00-123-4567CM, as required for supply management operations. Numeric codes shall not be affixed immediately adjacent to or as a part of the national stock number, nor shall codes be intermingled in the national stock number.
