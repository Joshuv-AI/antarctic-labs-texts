41 C.F.R. § 101-1.106 — -1.106 Applicability of FPMR.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-1.106
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-1.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FPMR apply to all Federal agencies to the extent specified in the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949 or other applicable law.
