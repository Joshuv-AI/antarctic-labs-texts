41 C.F.R. § 101-6.2105 — -6.2105 What is the Administrator's obligation with respect to Federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-6.2105
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-6.2105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-6 (6—Miscellaneous Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected Federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and GSA regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
