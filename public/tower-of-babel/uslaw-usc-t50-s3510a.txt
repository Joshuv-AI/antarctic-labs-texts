50 U.S.C. § 3510a — Availability of appropriations for construction projects
Citation: us:usc:50:3510a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3510a
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 46 (Central Intelligence Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During the current fiscal year and thereafter, funds appropriated for construction projects of the Central Intelligence Agency, which are transferred to another Agency for execution, shall remain available until expended.
