Global boundedness of solutions of degenerate and non-uniform parabolic equations
Thuyen Dang, Duong Minh Duc

Abstract
Let 2 ≤ N ∈ ℕ 2\leq N\in{\mathbb{N}} , Ω \Omega be a bounded open in ℝ N {\mathbb{R}}^{N} , T ∈ ( 0 , ∞ ) T\in(0,\infty) , Q = Ω × ( 0 , T ) Q=\Omega\times(0,T) , u u be a weak solution of parabolic equation ∂ u ∂ t − L ​ u = f \displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}-Lu=f , where L L is an elliptic operator on a space of functions on Q Q . The coefficients of L L may be not bounded, not strictly nor uniformly elliptic, and not of Muckenhoupt type. We obtain global boundedness of u u . Our result can be applied to u u , which may vanish on ( A × ( 0 , T ) ) ∪ ( Ω × { 0 } ) (A\times(0,T))\cup(\Omega\times\{0\}) of the boundary of Q Q and is free outside this set.
