49 C.F.R. § 568.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:568.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-568.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 568 (Vehicles Manufactured In Two Or More Stages—All Incomplete, Intermediate And Final-Stage Manufacturers Of Vehicles Manufactured In Two Or More Stages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe the method by which manufacturers of vehicles manufactured in two or more stages shall ensure conformity of those vehicles with the Federal motor vehicle safety standards (“standards”) and other regulations issued under the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act, as amended (49 U.S.C. § 30115) and the Motor Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act, as amended (49 U.S.C. 32504 and 33108(c)).
