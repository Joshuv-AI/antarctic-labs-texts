47 C.F.R. § 95.3331 — Permissible 76-81 GHz Band Radar Service uses.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.3331
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.3331
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Radar systems operating in the 76-81 GHz band may operate as vehicular radars, or as fixed or mobile radars in airport air operations areas, including but not limited to FOD detection radars and aircraft-mounted radars for ground use only.
