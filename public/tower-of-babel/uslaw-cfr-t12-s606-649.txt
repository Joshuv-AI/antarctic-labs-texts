12 C.F.R. § 606.649 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:12:606.649
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-606.649
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 606 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Farm Credit Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 606.650, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
