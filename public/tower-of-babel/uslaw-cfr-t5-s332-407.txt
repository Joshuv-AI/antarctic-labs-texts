5 C.F.R. § 332.407 — Restriction of consideration to one sex.
Citation: us:cfr:5:332.407
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-332.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 332 (Recruitment And Selection Through Competitive Examination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appointing officer may not restrict his consideration of eligibles or employees for competitive appointment or appointment by noncompetitive action to a position in the competitive service to one sex, except in unusual circumstances when OPM finds the action justified.
