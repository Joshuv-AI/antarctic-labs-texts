15 U.S.C. § 7806 — Limitation
Citation: us:usc:15:7806
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7806
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 104 (Sports Agent Responsibility And Trust). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prohibit an individual from seeking any remedies available under existing Federal or State law or equity.
