45 C.F.R. § 1631.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1631.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1631.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1631 (Purchasing And Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set standards for purchasing, leasing, using, and disposing of LSC-funded personal property and real estate and using LSC funds to contract for services.
