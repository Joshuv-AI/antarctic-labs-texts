29 C.F.R. § 1955.46 — Finality for purposes of judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1955.46
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1955.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1955 (Procedures For Withdrawal Of Approval Of State Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only a final decision by the Secretary under § 1955.44 shall be deemed final agency action for purposes of judicial review. A decision of an administrative law judge which becomes final for lack of appeal is not deemed final agency action for purposes of 5 U.S.C. 704.
