15 U.S.C. § 9553 — Report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:15:9553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9553
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter III (Rural Minority Business Center Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after November 15, 2021, the Under Secretary shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees a report that includes—
(1)
a summary of the efforts of the Under Secretary to provide services to minority business enterprises located in States that lack an MBDA Business Center, as of November 15, 2021, and especially in those States that have significant minority populations; and
(2)
recommendations for extending the outreach of the Agency to underserved areas.
