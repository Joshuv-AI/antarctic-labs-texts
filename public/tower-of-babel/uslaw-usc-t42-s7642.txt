42 U.S.C. § 7642 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:7642
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7642
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 85 (Air Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter IV (Noise Pollution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such amount, not to exceed $30,000,000, as may be necessary for the purposes of this subchapter.
