12 C.F.R. § 381.12 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:12:381.12
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-381.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 381 (Resolution Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board and Corporation may jointly enforce an order jointly issued by the Board and Corporation under § 381.9(a) or (c). The Board, in consultation with the Corporation, may take any action to address any violation of this part by a covered company under section 8 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1818).
