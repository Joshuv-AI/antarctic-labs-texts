43 C.F.R. § 3190.0-4 — -4 Objective.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3190 (Delegation Of Authority, Cooperative Agreements And Contracts For Oil And Gas Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective of this part is to assure that delegations of authority, cooperative agreements and contracts as provided for under the Federal Oil and Gas Royalty Management Act are carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Act and this title.
