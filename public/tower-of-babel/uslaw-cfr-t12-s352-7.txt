12 C.F.R. § 352.7 — Accessibility of programs and activities: Existing facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:12:352.7
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-352.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 352 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC shall operate each of the programs or activities set forth in § 352.2 of this part so that when viewed in its entirety, the program or activity is readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities.
