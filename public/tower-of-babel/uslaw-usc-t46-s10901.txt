46 U.S.C. § 10901 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:10901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10901
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 109 (Proceedings On Unseaworthiness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies to a vessel of the United States except a fishing or whaling vessel or a yacht.
