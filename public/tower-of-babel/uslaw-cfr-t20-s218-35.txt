20 C.F.R. § 218.35 — When an employee age annuity ends.
Citation: us:cfr:20:218.35
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-218.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 218 (Annuity Beginning And Ending Dates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee annuity based on age ends with the last day of the month before the month in which the employee dies.
