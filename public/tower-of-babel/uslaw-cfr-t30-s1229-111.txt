30 C.F.R. § 1229.111 — Materials furnished to States necessary to perform delegation.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1229.111
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1229.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1229 (Delegation To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ONRR shall provide to the State all reports, files, and supporting materials within its possession necessary to allow the State to effectively carry out the terms of the delegation specified in § 1229.104.
