43 C.F.R. § 3905.10 — Oil shale lease exchanges.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3905.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3905.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3900 (Oil Shale Management—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To facilitate the recovery of oil shale, the BLM may consider land exchanges where appropriate and feasible to consolidate land ownership and mineral interest into manageable areas. Exchanges are covered under part 2200 of this chapter.
