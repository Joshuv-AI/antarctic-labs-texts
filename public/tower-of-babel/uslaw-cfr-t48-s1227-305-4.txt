48 C.F.R. § 1227.305-4 — 1227.305-4 Protection of invention disclosures.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1227.305-4
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1227.305-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1227 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Solicitations and contracts that include a patent rights clause must provide the contractor the means to report inventions made during contract performance and at contract completion. This requirement may be fulfilled by requiring the contractor to submit a Department of Defense DD Form 882, Report of Inventions and Subcontracts.
