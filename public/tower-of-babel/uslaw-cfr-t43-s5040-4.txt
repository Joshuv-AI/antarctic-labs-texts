43 C.F.R. § 5040.4 — What is the effect of designating sustained-yield units?
Citation: us:cfr:43:5040.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5040.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5040 (Sustained-Yield Forest Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Designating new sustained-yield forest units abolishes previous O. and C. master unit or sustained-yield forest unit designations. Until new sustained-yield forest units are designated for the first time in accordance with 43 CFR part 5040, the current master unit designations will continue to be in effect.
