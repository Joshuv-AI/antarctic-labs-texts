32 C.F.R. § 1701.4 — Privacy Act responsibilities/policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1701.4
Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1701.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1701 (Administration Of Records Under The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ODNI will administer records about individuals consistent with statutory, administrative, and program responsibilities. Subject to exemptions authorized by the Act, ODNI will collect, maintain and disclose records as required and will honor subjects' rights to view and amend records and to obtain an accounting of disclosures.
