34 C.F.R. § 75.703 — Obligation of funds during the grant period.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.703
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee may use grant funds only for obligations it makes during the grant period.
