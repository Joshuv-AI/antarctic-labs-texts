2 C.F.R. § 1500.5 — Supersession.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1500.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1500.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1500 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective December 26, 2014, this part supersedes the following regulations under Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations:
(a) 40 CFR part 30, “Uniform Administrative Requirements for Grants and Agreements with Institutions of Higher Education, Hospitals, and other Non-profit Organizations.”
(b) 40 CFR part 31, “Uniform Administrative Requirements for Grants and Cooperative Agreements to State and Local Governments.”
