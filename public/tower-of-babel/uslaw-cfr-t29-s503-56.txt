29 C.F.R. § 503.56 — Retention of official record.
Citation: us:cfr:29:503.56
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-503.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 503 (Enforcement Of Obligations For Temporary Nonimmigrant Non-Agricultural Workers Described In The Immigration And Nationality Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official record of every completed administrative hearing provided by the regulations in this part will be maintained and filed under the custody and control of the Chief ALJ, or, where the case has been the subject of administrative review, the ARB.
