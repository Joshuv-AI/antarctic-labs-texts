10 U.S.C. § 591 — Reference to chapters 1205 and 1207
Citation: us:usc:10:591
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section591
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 34 (Appointments As Reserve Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of law relating to appointments of reserve officers other than warrant officers are set forth in chapter 1205 of this title (beginning with section 12201). Provisions of law relating to appointments and promotion of reserve warrant officers are set forth in chapter 1207 (beginning with section 12241).
