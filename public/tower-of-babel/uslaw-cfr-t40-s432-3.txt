40 C.F.R. § 432.3 — General limitation or standard for pH.
Citation: us:cfr:40:432.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-432.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 432 (Meat And Poultry Products Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any discharge subject to BPT, BCT, or NSPS limitations or standards in this part must remain within the pH range of 6 to 9.
