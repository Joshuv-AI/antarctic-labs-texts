40 C.F.R. § 469.21 — Compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:40:469.21
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-469.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 469 (Electrical And Electronic Components Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The compliance date for the BAT fluoride limitation is as soon as possible as determined by the permit writer but in no event later than November 8, 1985. The compliance date for PSES for total toxic organics (TTO) is July 1, 1984 and for arsenic is November 8, 1985.
