21 C.F.R. § 801.122 — Medical devices for processing, repacking, or manufacturing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:801.122
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-801.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 801 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A device intended for processing, repacking, or use in the manufacture of another drug or device shall be exempt from section 502(f)(1) of the act if its label bears the statement “Caution: For manufacturing, processing, or repacking”.
