21 U.S.C. § 379d–5 — Guidance document regarding product promotion using the Internet
Citation: us:usc:21:379d–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section379d–5
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter VII (General Authority); Part A (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 2 years after July 9, 2012, the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall issue guidance that describes Food and Drug Administration policy regarding the promotion, using the Internet (including social media), of medical products that are regulated by such Administration.
