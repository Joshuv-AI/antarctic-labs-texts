16 C.F.R. § 306.2 — Who is covered.
Citation: us:cfr:16:306.2
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-306.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 306 (Automotive Fuel Ratings, Certification And Posting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You are covered by this rule if you are a refiner, importer, producer, distributor, or retailer of automotive fuel.
