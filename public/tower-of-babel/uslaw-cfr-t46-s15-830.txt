46 C.F.R. § 15.830 — Radio Officers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:15.830
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-15.830
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 15 (Manning Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Radio Officers are required on certain merchant vessels of the United States. The determination of when a Radio Officer is required is based on the Federal Communications Commission requirements as found in 47 CFR parts 13 and 80.
