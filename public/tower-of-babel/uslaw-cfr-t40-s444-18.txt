40 C.F.R. § 444.18 — Pretreatment standards for new sources (PSNS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:444.18
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-444.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 444 (Waste Combustors Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7, any source that introduces wastewater pollutants into a POTW must comply with 40 CFR part 403 and achieve the following pretreatment standards: Standards for arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, lead, mercury, silver, titanium and zinc are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 444.16.
