46 C.F.R. § 64.19 — External pressure.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A tank without a vacuum breaker must be designed to withstand an external pressure of 7 1/2 psig or more.
(b) A tank with a vacuum breaker must be designed to withstand an external pressure of 3 psig or more.
