20 C.F.R. § 340.7 — Deduction in computation of death benefit.
Citation: us:cfr:20:340.7
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-340.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 340 (Recovery Of Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing the residual lump sum provided for in part 234, subpart D, of this chapter, the Board shall include in the benefits to be deducted from the gross residual all amounts recoverable under this part, but not recovered, including amounts where recovery was waived, that were paid to the individual or paid to others as benefits accrued to the individual but not paid at death.
