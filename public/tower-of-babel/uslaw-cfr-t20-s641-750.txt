20 C.F.R. § 641.750 — Will there be performance-related incentives?
Citation: us:cfr:20:641.750
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-641.750
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 641 (Provisions Governing The Senior Community Service Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department is authorized by OAA secs. 502(e)(2)(B)(iv) and 517(c)(1) to use recaptured SCSEP funds to provide incentive awards. The Department will exercise this authority at its discretion.
