29 C.F.R. § 2204.401 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2204.401
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2204.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2204 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Proceedings Before The Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award, or any accompanying documentation related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in accordance with §§ 2200.7 and 2200.8 of this chapter, except as provided in § 2204.302(b) for confidential financial information.
