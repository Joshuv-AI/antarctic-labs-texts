22 U.S.C. § 284q — Special Facility for Sub-Saharan Africa
Citation: us:usc:22:284q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section284q
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XIII (International Development Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to the Special Facility for Sub-Saharan Africa, administered by the Association, amounts appropriated pursuant to subsection (b).
(b)
For purposes of the United States contribution provided for in subsection (a), there are authorized to be appropriated, without fiscal year limitation, $225,000,000.
