43 U.S.C. § 911 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:43:911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section911
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 21 (Grants In Aid Of Railroads And Wagon Roads). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall prescribe such rules and regulations as will be necessary to the carrying out of the provisions of sections 908 to 910 of this title.
