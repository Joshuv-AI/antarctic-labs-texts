46 C.F.R. § 154.401 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.401
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in §§ 154.440 and 154.447:
“σY” means the minimum yield strength of the tank material, including weld metal, at room temperature.
“σB” means minimum tensile strength of the tank material, including weld metals, at room temperature.
