40 C.F.R. § 158.210 — Experimental use permit data requirements for product chemistry.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-158.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 158 (Data Requirements For Pesticides). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All product chemistry data, as described in § 158.310, must be submitted to support a request for an experimental use permit.
