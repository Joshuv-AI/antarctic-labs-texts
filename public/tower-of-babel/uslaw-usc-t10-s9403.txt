10 U.S.C. § 9403 — Aviation cadets and aviation students: schools
Citation: us:usc:10:9403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9403
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 951 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force shall establish and maintain—
(1)
one or more schools for the training and instruction of aviation cadets; and
(2)
courses of instruction for aviation students at one or more established flying schools.
