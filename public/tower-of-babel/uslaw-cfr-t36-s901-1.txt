36 C.F.R. § 901.1 — Title and office.
Citation: us:cfr:36:901.1
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-901.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 901 (Bylaws Of The Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Title. The name of the Corporation is the Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation.
(b) Office. The office of the Corporation shall be in the city of Washington, District of Columbia.
