22 U.S.C. § 2429a–1 — Annual report on nuclear transfer activities
Citation: us:usc:22:2429a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2429a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter III (General And Administrative Provisions); Part III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Beginning with the fiscal year 1983 and for each fiscal year thereafter, the President shall prepare and transmit to the Congress, as part of the presentation materials for foreign assistance programs proposed for that fiscal year, a classified report describing the nuclear programs and related activities of any country for which a waiver of section 2799aa or 2799aa–1 of this title is in effect, including an assessment of—
(1)
the extent and effectiveness of International Atomic Energy Agency safeguards at that country’s nuclear facilities; and
(2)
the capability, actions, and intentions of the government of that country with respect to the manufacture or acquisition of a nuclear explosive device.
