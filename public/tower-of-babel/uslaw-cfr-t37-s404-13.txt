37 C.F.R. § 404.13 — Transfer of custody.
Citation: us:cfr:37:404.13
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-404.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 404 (Licensing Of Government-Owned Inventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal agency having custody of a federally owned invention may transfer custody and administration, in whole or in part, to another Federal agency, of the right, title, or interest in such invention.
