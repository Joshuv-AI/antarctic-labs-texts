41 C.F.R. § 301-31.5 — -31.5 Allowable subsistence expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-31.5
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-31.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-31 (31—Threatened Law Enforcement/Investigative Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may only pay lodging costs. However, the agency may pay for meals and laundry/cleaning expenses if:
(a) The temporary living accommodations do not have kitchen or laundry facilities; or
(b) The agency determines that other extenuating circumstances exist which necessitate payment of these expenses.
