42 U.S.C. § 4761 — Declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:4761
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4761
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to provide for the general administration of subchapters I, II, III, and IV of this chapter (hereinafter referred to as “this chapter”), and to provide for the establishment of certain advisory committees.
