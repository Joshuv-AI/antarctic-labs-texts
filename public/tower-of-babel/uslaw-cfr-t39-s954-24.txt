39 C.F.R. § 954.24 — Official record.
Citation: us:cfr:39:954.24
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-954.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 954 (Denial, Suspension, Or Revocaton Of Periodicals Mail Privileges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The pleadings, orders, exhibits, transcript of testimony, briefs, decisions and other documents filed in the proceeding constitute the official record of the proceeding.
