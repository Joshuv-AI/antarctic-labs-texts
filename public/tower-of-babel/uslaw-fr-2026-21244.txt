October 14, 2026.
    Pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 
(``Act''),\1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ notice is hereby given that 
on October 1, 2026, 24X National Exchange LLC (``24X'' or the

proposed rule change from interested persons.

I. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement of the Terms of Substance 
of the Proposed Rule Change

    The Exchange proposes to make certain amendments to its warrant 
performance incentive program. The proposed rule change is available on 
the Exchange's website at https://equities.24exchange.com/regulation 
and at the principal office of the Exchange.

II. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement of the Purpose of, and 
Statutory Basis for, the Proposed Rule Change

    In its filing with the Commission, the self-regulatory organization

A. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement of the Purpose of, and the 
Statutory Basis for, the Proposed Rule Change

1. Purpose
    The Exchange adopted a warrant performance incentive program 
(``Program'') to allow Members of the Exchange who participate in the 
Program (``Participants'') \3\ to earn the right to purchase Non-Voting 
Common Units \4\ of 24X US Holdings LLC (``24X US Holdco''), the 
Exchange's parent company.\5\ As described in the Warrant Program 
Release, each Member of the Exchange may become a Participant in the 
Program by prepaying $500,000 in Exchange fees (``Prepayment Fee'') and 
satisfying the Program eligibility requirements.\6\ Upon joining the 
Program, each Participant will receive a warrant that vests based on 
the Participant's achievement of certain minimum trading volumes 
(``Target Volume'') \7\ on the Exchange during each designated pre-
determined period in which the Program is in effect (``Measurement 
Period'') \8\ and the Exchange's achievement of a minimum market share 
during such Measurement Periods (``24X Minimum Overall Market 
Share'').\9\ When the warrants vest, Participants will have the right 
to exercise the warrants to purchase a certain number of 24X US Holdco 
Non-Voting Common Units.

    The Exchange has determined to revise the Program to make 
additional Non-Voting Common Units available to be purchased by 
Participants starting in the first Measurement Period of 2027

the Non-Voting Common Units currently available to be purchased through

Additional Equity would extend through two additional Measurement

aspects of the Program would remain the same as described in the 
Warrant Program Releases.

    As described below, the Exchange proposes that depending on which 
Prepayment Fee they elect to pay, Participants of the Program may 
become eligible to earn either (i) the right to purchase both Original 
Equity and Additional Equity or (ii) the right to purchase Additional 
Equity only.
ii. Prepayment Fee
    The Exchange proposes that current Participants of the Program who 
have already paid the $500,000 Prepayment Fee will be automatically 
eligible to earn the right to purchase Additional Equity for any 
Measurement Period in which the Program thresholds are met, as long as 
those Participants continue to meet the other Program eligibility 
requirements. The Exchange also proposes that any Participant who joins 
the Program after the proposed rule change goes into effect can either 
(i) prepay $500,000 in Exchange fees to become eligible to earn the 
right to purchase both Original Equity and Additional Equity, or (ii) 
prepay a reduced amount of $250,000 in Exchange fees to become eligible 
to earn the right to purchase Additional Equity only. The Prepayment 
Fee must be tendered by the first day of each quarter of the Program 
Period to participate in the Program as of such quarter until April 1, 
2028.
    The following are examples of the timing of participation in the 
Program:
     If Participant A joined the Program by tendering the 
$500,000 Prepayment Fee and executing the required documentation on 
October 14, 2025 (the date trading commenced on the Exchange), then 
Participant A's trading starting on October 14, 2025 would be counted 
toward the Target Volume for Year 1 and thereafter until the end of the 
Program Period. Participant A would be eligible to earn the right to 
purchase both Original Equity and Additional Equity for the entire 
Program Period.
     If Participant B joined the Program by tendering the 
$500,000 Prepayment Fee and executing the required documentation on 
October 22, 2026, then Participant B's trading starting on January 1, 
2027 would be counted toward the Target Volume for the first quarter of 
Year 3 (i.e., the first Measurement Period for Year 3) and thereafter 
until the end of the Program Period. Participant B would be eligible to 
earn the right to purchase both Original Equity and Additional Equity 
starting with the Measurement Period that begins on January 1, 2027 
throughout the end of the Program Period.
     If Participant C joined the Program by tendering the 
$250,000 Prepayment Fee and executing the required documentation on 
October 22, 2026, then Participant C's trading starting on January 1, 
2027 would be counted toward the Target Volume for the first quarter of 
Year 3 (i.e., the first Measurement Period for Year 3) and thereafter 
until the end of the Program Period. Participant C would be eligible to 
earn the right to purchase Additional Equity only starting with the 
Measurement Period that begins on January 1, 2027 throughout the end of 
the Program Period.
     If Participant D joined the Program by tendering the 
$500,000 Prepayment Fee and executing the required documentation on 
October 1, 2027, then Participant D's trading starting on October 1, 
2027 would be counted toward the Target Volume for the fourth quarter 
of Year 3 (i.e., the last Measurement Period in Year 3) and the first 
quarter of Year 4 (i.e., the only Measurement Period in Year 4). 
Participant D would be eligible to earn both the right to purchase 
Original Equity for the last Measurement Period in Year 3 and the right 
to purchase Additional Equity for the last Measurement Period in Year 3 
and the only two Measurement Periods in Year 4. No Member may become a 
Participant that is eligible to earn Original Equity after October 1, 
2027.
     If Participant E joined the Program by tendering the 
$250,000 Prepayment Fee and executing the required documentation on 
April 1, 2028, then Participant E's trading starting on April 1, 2028 
would be counted toward the Target Volume for the second quarter of 
Year 4 (i.e., the second Measurement Period in Year 4). Participant E 
would be eligible to earn the right to purchase Additional Equity for 
the second Measurement Period in Year 4. No Member may become a 
Participant after April 1, 2028.

iii. Target Volume Calculation
    As described in the Warrant Program Release, the Target Volume that 
Participants must meet in order for their warrants to vest is 5% of the 
average daily trading volume on the Exchange, where the daily trading 
volume is calculated based on total aggregated average daily volume 
traded over each Measurement Period.\12\ The Exchange proposes that 
with respect to Additional Equity only, Target Volume for each

[[Page 66185]]

Measurement Period will be calculated based solely on Participants' 
maker volume (i.e., trading volume which adds liquidity to the 
Exchange). Target Volume will continue to be calculated based on total 
trading volume with respect to Original Equity.

    Pursuant to the Program requirements, the Non-Voting Common Units 
subject to the Program would be allocated among Participants who have

b. Proposed Changes With Respect to Both Original Equity and Additional 
Equity
i. Elimination of 24X Minimum Overall Market Share Threshold
    As described in the Warrant Program Releases, the 24X Minimum 
Overall Market Share is a designated target percentage of the CADV for 
all NMS Stocks eligible for trading on 24X for each relevant 
Measurement Period.\14\ The CADV is calculated based on the volume 
reported by all exchanges and trade reporting facilities to a 
consolidated transaction reporting plan for the relevant Measurement 
Period.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \14\ See Warrant Program Release at 46439.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    In order to incentivize participation in the Program and bring 
greater volume and liquidity to the Exchange, the Exchange proposes to 
eliminate the 24X Minimum Overall Market Share requirement with respect 
to Original Equity, and to not implement a 24X Minimum Overall Market 
Share requirement with respect to Additional Equity. Elimination of the 
requirement would make Program outcomes more predictable for 
Participants because Exchange market share depends on the trading 
activity of all Members and on changes in overall U.S. equity market 
volume, rather than the individual actions of each Participant. By 
allowing Participants to determine whether their warrants will vest 
solely by choosing to meet the Target Volume and not having to rely on 
other factors outside their control, the proposed revised Program would 
both attract more new Participants and encourage existing Participants 
to increase the amount of liquidity they contribute to the Exchange. 
The Exchange also notes that another national securities exchange 
offers a warrant program that does not include an exchange market share 
threshold as a warrant vesting condition.\15\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

2026), 91 FR 16044 (Mar. 31, 2026) (SR-TXSE-2026-003).

significant percentage of consolidated trading volume has occurred 
during the closing auctions on the primary listing exchanges. Because

Participants, they artificially increased consolidated market volume

trading volume on 24X. The Exchange therefore proposes to exclude the 
above dates from the calculation of Target Volume given that their 
inclusion would create a diluting effect that would make it more 
difficult for

2. Statutory Basis
    The Exchange believes that the proposed rule change is consistent 
with Section 6(b) of the Exchange Act \16\ in general, and furthers the 
objectives of Section 6(b)(5) of the Exchange Act \17\ in particular, 
in that it is designed to prevent fraudulent and manipulative acts and 
practices, to promote just and equitable principles of trade, to foster 
cooperation and coordination with persons engaged in facilitating 
transactions in securities, to remove impediments to and perfect the 
mechanisms of a free and open market and a national market system and, 
in general, to protect investors and the public interest. Additionally, 
the Exchange believes the proposed rule change is consistent with the 
Section 6(b)(5) of the Exchange Act \18\ requirement that the rules of 
an exchange not be designed to permit unfair discrimination between 
customers, issuers, brokers, or dealers. The Exchange also believes the 
proposed rule change is consistent with Section 6(b)(4) of the Exchange 
Act,\19\ which requires that Exchange rules provide for the equitable 
allocation of reasonable dues, fees and other charges among its members 
and other persons using its facilities.

    The Exchange believes that the proposed changes to the Program are 
consistent with the Act. Making the Additional Equity available for 
purchase through the Program at a reduced Prepayment Fee and based on 
maker volume only would both encourage additional Participant 
enrollment in the Program, and encourage Participants to provide 
additional volume and liquidity to the Exchange. The Exchange believes 
that an increase in volume and liquidity would benefit all market 
participants by providing more trading opportunities and tighter 
spreads, even to those market participants that do not participate in 
the Program, thereby perfecting the mechanism for a free and open 
market and national market system.
    Moreover, the elimination of the 24X Minimum Overall Market Share 
requirement and exclusion of certain dates from the calculation of 
Target Volume with respect to both Original Equity and Additional 
Equity would make it more likely that Participants' warrants would vest 
in any given Measurement Period. As described above, elimination of the 
24X Minimum Overall Market Share requirement would allow Participants 
to have more control over whether their warrants vest in any given 
Measurement Period,\20\ and exclusion of certain dates from the 
calculation of Target Volume would remove a potential diluting effect 
that otherwise would hamper Participants' ability to achieve Target 
Volume through ordinary trading activity. These incentives would 
promote the long-term interests of the Exchange by attracting new 
Participants and encouraging existing Participants to contribute to the 
growth and success of the Exchange via actively providing liquidity on 
the 24X market, and to provide additional investment and funding which 
could be used for the regulation and operation of the Exchange. The 
Exchange believes that the additional funds provided as a result of 
these changes would enable the Exchange to have greater capacity to 
carry out the purposes of the Act and to comply with the provisions of 
the Act, the rules and regulations thereunder, and the rules of the 
Exchange, which, in turn, would protect investors and the public 
interest.

to purchase both Original Equity and Additional Equity on the same 
terms and conditions. The additional Measurement Period, Prepayment Fee 
schedule, and Target Volume modification with respect to Additional 
Equity are objective and the same for all Participants. Likewise, the 
elimination of the 24X Minimum Overall Market Share requirement and 
exclusion of certain dates from the calculation of Target Volume with 
respect to both Additional Equity and Original Equity are based on 
objective standards and would have the same effect for all 
Participants.

B. Self-Regulatory Organization's Statement on Burden on Competition

    The Exchange does not believe that the proposed rule change will 
impose any burden on competition that is not necessary or appropriate 
in furtherance of the purposes of the Exchange Act. The Exchange 
believes that the Program as amended by this proposed rule change would 
increase both intermarket and intramarket competition by incentivizing 
Participants to add liquidity to the Exchange, which will enhance the 
quality of quoting and increase the volume of securities traded on the 
Exchange. To the extent that there is an additional competitive burden 
on non-Participants, the Exchange believes that such burden is 
appropriate because the Program should incentivize Participants to add 
liquidity to the Exchange and thus enhance the volume of securities 
traded on the Exchange. To the extent that this purpose is achieved, 
the Exchange believes that all of the Exchange's market participants 
would benefit from the improved market liquidity.
    Given the robust competition for volume among equities markets, 
implementing a program to attract liquidity addition like the one 
proposed in this filing is consistent with the abovementioned goals of 
the Exchange Act. This is especially true for the smaller exchange 
markets, such as 24X, which are competing for volume with much larger 
exchanges that dominate the equities trading industry. As a new 
exchange, 24X will likely continue to have a nominal percentage of the 
average daily trading volume in equities in the near term, so it is 
unlikely that the Program could cause any competitive harm to the 
equities market or to market participants. Rather, the Program is a 
modest attempt to attract order volume away from larger competitors by 
adopting an innovative pricing strategy. The Exchange notes that if the 
Program results in a modest percentage increase in the average daily 
trading volume on 24X, while such percentage would represent a large 
volume increase for 24X, it would represent a minimal reduction in 
volume of its larger competitors in the industry. The Exchange believes 
that the Program will help further competition, because market 
participants will have yet another option in determining where to post 
liquidity if they factor the benefits of 24X equity participation into 
the determination.

    Interested persons are invited to submit written data, views, and 
arguments concerning the foregoing, including whether the proposed rule 
change is consistent with the Act. Comments may be submitted by any of 
the following methods:

Electronic Comments

     Use the Commission's internet comment form (https://www.sec.gov/rules/sro.shtml); or
     Send an email to [email protected]. Please include 
file number SR-24X-2026-26 on the subject line.

Paper Comments

     Send paper comments in triplicate to Secretary, Securities 
and Exchange Commission, 100 F Street NE, Washington, DC 20549-1090.

All submissions should refer to file number SR-24X-2026-26. This file 
number should be included on the subject line if email is used. To help 
the Commission process and review your comments more efficiently, 
please use only one method. The Commission will post all comments on 
the Commission's internet website (https://www.sec.gov/rules/sro.shtml). Copies of the filing will be available for inspection and 
copying at the principal office of the Exchange. Do not include 
personal identifiable information in submissions; you should submit 
only information that you wish to make available publicly. We may 
redact in part or withhold entirely from publication submitted material 
that is obscene or subject to copyright protection. All submissions 
should refer to file number SR-24X-2026-26 and should be submitted on 
or before November 9, 2026.
