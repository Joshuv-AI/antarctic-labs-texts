25 U.S.C. § 391a — Sale for town site; removal of restriction
Citation: us:usc:25:391a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section391a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 12 (Lease, Sale, Or Surrender Of Allotted Or Unallotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of allowing any Indian allottee to sell for townsite purposes any portion of the lands allotted to him, the Secretary of the Interior may, by order, remove restrictions upon the alienation of such lands and issue fee-simple patents therefor under such rules and regulations as he may prescribe.
