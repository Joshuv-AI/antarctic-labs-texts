7 C.F.R. § 985.30 — Expenses and compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:985.30
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-985.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 985 (Marketing Order Regulating The Handling Of Spearmint Oil Produced In The Far West). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members of the Committee, their alternates, subcommittees including any special subcommittees, shall serve without compensation but shall receive such allowances for necessary expenses, incurred in performing their duties, as may be approved by the Committee.
