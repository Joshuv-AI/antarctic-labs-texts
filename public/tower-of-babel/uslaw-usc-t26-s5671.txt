26 U.S.C. § 5671 — Penalty and forfeiture for evasion of beer tax and fraudulent noncompliance with requirements
Citation: us:usc:26:5671
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5671
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part III (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Applicable To Beer And Brewing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever evades or attempts to evade any tax imposed by section 5051, or with intent to defraud the United States fails or refuses to keep and file true and accurate records and returns as required by section 5415 and regulations issued pursuant thereto, shall be fined not more than $5,000, or imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both, for each such offense, and shall forfeit all beer made by him or for him, and all the vessels, utensils, and apparatus used in making the same.
