31 C.F.R. § 256.14 — What happens if I submit an incomplete request for payment?
Citation: us:cfr:31:256.14
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-256.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 256 (Obtaining Payments From The Judgment Fund And Under Private Relief Bills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fiscal Service may return, without action, any request for payment that is incomplete. If a request for payment is returned for lack of necessary information, the submitting agency may resubmit the request for payment once all the required information is available.
