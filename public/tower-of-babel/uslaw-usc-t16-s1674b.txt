16 U.S.C. § 1674b — Sustainable Forestry Outreach Initiative
Citation: us:usc:16:1674b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1674b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter III (Extension Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a program, to be known as the “Sustainable Forestry Outreach Initiative”, to educate landowners concerning the following:
(1)
The value and benefits of practicing sustainable forestry.
(2)
The importance of professional forestry advice in achieving sustainable forestry objectives.
(3)
The variety of public and private sector resources available to assist the landowners in planning for and practicing sustainable forestry.
