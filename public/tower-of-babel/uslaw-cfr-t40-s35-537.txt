40 C.F.R. § 35.537 — Application requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.537
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.537
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for a Performance Partnership Grant must contain:
(a) A list of the environmental programs and the amount of funds from each program to be combined in the Performance Partnership Grant;
(b) A consolidated budget;
(c) A consolidated work plan that addresses each program being combined in the grant and which meets the requirements of § 35.507.
