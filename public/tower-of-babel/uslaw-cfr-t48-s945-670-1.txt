48 C.F.R. § 945.670-1 — 945.670-1 Plant clearance function.
Citation: us:cfr:48:945.670-1
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-945.670-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 945 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the plant clearance function has not been formally delegated to another Federal agency, the contracting officer shall assume all responsibilities of the plant clearance officer identified in FAR 2.101.
