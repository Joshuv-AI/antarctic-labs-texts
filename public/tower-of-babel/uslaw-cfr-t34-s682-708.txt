34 C.F.R. § 682.708 — Evidence of mailing and receipt dates.
Citation: us:cfr:34:682.708
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-682.708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 682 (Federal Family Education Loan (Ffel) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All mailing dates and receipt dates referred to in this subpart must be substantiated by the original receipts from the U.S. Postal Service.
(b) If a lender or third-party servicer refuses to accept a notice mailed under this subpart, the Secretary considers the notice as being received on the date that the lender or servicer refuses to accept the notice.
