48 C.F.R. § 1430.202-2 — 1430.202-2 Impracticality of submission.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1430.202-2
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1430.202-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1430 (Cost Accounting Standards Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CO shall prepare any request to award a contract without the required contractor submission of the Form No. CASB-DS-1, Disclosure Statement, and submit it through the HCA, to the Director, PAM, and to the Secretary for approval. The Secretary, without the power of redelegation, must file a report to CASB within 30 days in accordance with 48 CFR 9903.202-2 (FAR Appendix).
