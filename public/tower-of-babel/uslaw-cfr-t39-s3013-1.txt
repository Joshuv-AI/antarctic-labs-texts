39 C.F.R. § 3013.1 — Scope and applicability of other parts of this title.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3013.1
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3013.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3013 (Procedures For Compelling Production Of Information By The Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The rules in this part govern the procedures for compelling the production of information by the Postal Service pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 504(f).
(b) Subparts A, B, and D to part 3010 of this chapter apply unless otherwise stated in this part or otherwise ordered by the Commission.
