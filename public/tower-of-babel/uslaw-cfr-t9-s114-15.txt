9 C.F.R. § 114.15 — Disposal of unsatisfactory products and byproducts.
Citation: us:cfr:9:114.15
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-114.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 114 (Production Requirements For Biological Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All biological products found to be unsatisfactory for marketing, all biological products which have become worthless subsequent to the expiration date, all refuse, other materials deemed unsatisfactory for production purposes, all carcasses (part or whole) of production or test animals, and any undesirable byproducts of manufacture shall be disposed of as may be required by the Administrator.
