21 C.F.R. § 1140.12 — Additional responsibilities of manufacturers.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1140.12
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1140.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1140 (Cigarettes, Smokeless Tobacco, And Covered Tobacco Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the other responsibilities under this part, each manufacturer shall remove from each point of sale all self-service displays, advertising, labeling, and other items that the manufacturer owns that do not comply with the requirements under this part.
