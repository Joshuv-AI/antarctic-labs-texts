5 C.F.R. § 511.615 — Temporary compliance authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:511.615
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-511.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 511 (Classification Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may use temporary or conditional compliance action, e.g., a temporary promotion or a temporary reassignment when available, if:
(a) A position has been certified by the Office under either section 5110 or 5112 of title 5, United States Code;
(b) The certificate has not been suspended; and,
(c) The agency or employee has requested reconsideration.
