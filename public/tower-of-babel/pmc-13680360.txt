Author Correction: BRAHMA represses STOP1-NRT1.1 module to control plant rhizosphere alkalization and acid stress adaptation
Authors: Jia Yuan Ye, Wen Hao Tian, De Rui Zhang, Qian Yi Yu, Ji Ming Xu, Wo Na Ding, Gui Xin Li, Jing Ying Yan, Chong Wei Jin, Moussa Benhamed, Shao Jian Zheng, Zhong Jie Ding
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69905-z, published online 23 February 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors in Figs 3d, 4c, 4d and 6a, Supplementary Fig. 14, the associated Source Data file and Reporting Summary.

For Fig. 3d, the original data inadvertently included samples from a different experiment. For Figs. 4c and 4d and Supplementary Fig. 14, in several cases, average values were inadvertently duplicated and pasted into cells that should have been empty due to data exclusions. This resulted in errors in some of the reported mean values. For Fig. 6a, the reported mean values, included an extra datapoint which was generated due to copy-paste errors. In each case, the incorrect data has been replaced. The reporting summary has been updated to specify data exclusions. These errors have now been corrected in the HTML and PDF version of the article.
