Partial

-DAGs

In
[
2
]
, Hunter and Kreutzer introduced the Kelly-width of a digraph. In this section, we follow their paper.

A
DAG
is an acyclic digraph, that is, a digraph with no directed cycles. Let
T
be a DAG. If
i
and
j
are two distinct nodes of
T
, we write
i\prec j
if there is a directed walk in
T
from
i
to
j
. If
i
and
j
are nodes of
T
, we write
i\preceq j
if either
i=j
or
i\prec j
.
Let
D
be a digraph, and let
W,X\subseteq V(D)
. We say that
X

guards

W
if
W\cap X=\emptyset
, and for all
uv\in E(D)
, if
u\in W
, then
v\in W\cup X
.

A
Kelly-decomposition
of a digraph
D
is a triple
(T,\mathcal{W},\mathcal{X})
, where
T
is a DAG, and
\mathcal{W}=\{W_{i}\}_{i\in V(T)}
and
\mathcal{X}=\{X_{i}\}_{i\in V(T)}
are families of subsets of
V(D)
such that

•

\mathcal{W}
is a partition of
V(D)
,

•

for all nodes
i\in V(T)
,
X_{i}
guards
W_{\preceq i}
, and

•

for each node
i\in V(T)
, the children of
i
can be enumerated as
j_{1},\ldots,j_{s}
so that for each
j_{q}
,
X_{j_{q}}\subseteq W_{i}\cup X_{i}\cup\bigcup_{p<q}W_{\preceq j_{p}}
. Also, the roots of
T
can be enumerated as
r_{1},r_{2},\ldots
such that for each root
r_{q}
,
W_{r_{q}}\subseteq\bigcup_{p<q}W_{\preceq r_{p}}
.

The
width
of a Kelly-decomposition
(T,\mathcal{W},\mathcal{X})
is defined as
\max\{|W_{i}\cup X_{i}|:i\in V(T)\}
. The
Kelly-width
of
D
is the minimum width over all possible Kelly-decompositions of
D
.

The class of
k
-DAGs is defined recursively as follows:

(1)

A complete digraph with
k
vertices is a
k
-DAG.

(2)

A
k
-DAG with
n+1
vertices can be constructed from a
k
-DAG
H
with
n
vertices by adding a vertex
v
and arcs satisfying:

•

There are at most
k
arcs from
v
to
H
, and

•

If
X
is the set of endpoints of the edges added in the previous subcondition, an arc is added from
u\in V(H)
to
v
if
uw
for all
w\in X\setminus\{u\}
. If
X=\emptyset
, then this condition is true by default for all
u\in V(H)
.

A
partial
k
-DAG
is a spanning subdigraph of a
k
-DAG.

Let
D=(V,A)
be a digraph. A
directed elimination ordering

\lhd
is a linear ordering on
V
. If
\lhd=(v_{1},v_{2},\ldots,v_{n})
is a directed elimination ordering of
D
, define

•

D_{1}^{\lhd}:=D
, and

•

D_{i+1}^{\lhd}
is obtained from
D_{i}^{\lhd}
by deleting
v_{i}
and adding new arcs
uv
if
uv_{i},v_{i}v\in A(D_{i}^{\lhd})
and
u\not=v
.

The
width
of a directed elimination ordering is the maximum over all

of the out-degree of
v_{i}
in

.

Theorem 11
.

[
2
]

Let

be a digraph. Then equivalent are:

(1)

D
has Kelly-width
\leq k+1
,

(2)

D
has a directed elimination ordering of width
\leq k
, and

(3)

is a partial
k
-DAG.

Kintali and Zhang

proved the following lemma.

Lemma 12

If a digraph

has Kelly-width
\leq m
and

\leq m

In this paper, we prove that the class of digraphs
