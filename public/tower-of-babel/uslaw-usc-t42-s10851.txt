42 U.S.C. § 10851 — Construction of subchapters I and II; “individual with mental illness” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:10851
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10851
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 114 (Protection And Advocacy For Individuals With Mental Illness); Subchapter III (Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Subchapters I and II shall not be construed as establishing any new rights for individuals with mental illness.
(b)
For purposes of this section, the term “individual with mental illness” has the same meaning as in section 10802(3) 1 of this title.
