42 U.S.C. § 7300 — Report to Congress; effect on personnel
Citation: us:usc:42:7300
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7300
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VII (Transitional, Savings, And Conforming Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Civil Service Commission shall, as soon as practicable but not later than one year after October 1, 1977, prepare and transmit to the Congress a report on the effects on employees of the reorganization under this chapter which shall include—
(1)
an identification of any position within the Department or elsewhere in the executive branch, which it considers unnecessary due to consolidation of functions under this chapter;
(2)
a statement of the number of employees entitled to pay savings by reason of the reorganization under this chapter;
(3)
a statement of the number of employees who are voluntarily or involuntarily separated by reason of such reorganization;
(4)
an estimate of the personnel costs associated with such reorganization;
(5)
the effects of such reorganization on labor management relations; and
(6)
such legislative and administrative recommendations for improvements in personnel management within the Department as the Commission considers necessary.
