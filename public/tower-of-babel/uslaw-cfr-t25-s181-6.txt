25 C.F.R. § 181.6 — How are applicants informed of the results?
Citation: us:cfr:25:181.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-181.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 181 (Indian Highway Safety Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA will send a letter to all applicants notifying them of their selection or non-selection for participation in the Indian Highway Safety Program for the upcoming fiscal year. BIA will explain to each applicant not selected for participation the reason(s) for non-selection.
