24 C.F.R. § 206.109 — Amount of mortgagee share of premium.
Citation: us:cfr:24:206.109
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-206.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 206 (Home Equity Conversion Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Using the factors provided by the Commissioner, the amount of the mortgagee share of the premium shall be determined for each mortgage based upon the age of the youngest borrower or Eligible Non-Borrowing Spouse and the expected average mortgage interest rate.
