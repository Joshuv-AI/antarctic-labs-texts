45 C.F.R. § 1605.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1605.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1605.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1605 (Appeals On Behalf Of Clients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appeal means any appellate proceeding in a civil action as defined by law or usage in the jurisdiction in which the action is filed.
