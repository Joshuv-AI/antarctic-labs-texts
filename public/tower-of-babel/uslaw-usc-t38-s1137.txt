38 U.S.C. § 1137 — Wartime presumptions for certain veterans
Citation: us:usc:38:1137
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1137
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Service-Connected Disability Or Death); Subchapter IV (Peacetime Disability Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter and subchapter V of this chapter and notwithstanding the provisions of sections 1132 and 1133 of this subchapter, the provisions of sections 1111, 1112, and 1113 of this chapter shall be applicable in the case of any veteran who served in the active military, naval, air, or space service after December 31, 1946.
