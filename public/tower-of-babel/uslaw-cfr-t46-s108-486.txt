46 C.F.R. § 108.486 — Helicopter decks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.486
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.486
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At least two of the accesses to the helicopter landing deck must each have a fire hydrant on the unit's fire main system located next to them.
