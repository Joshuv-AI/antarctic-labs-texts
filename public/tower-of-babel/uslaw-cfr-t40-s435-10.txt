40 C.F.R. § 435.10 — Applicability; description of the offshore subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:435.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-435.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 435 (Oil And Gas Extraction Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to those facilities engaged in field exploration, drilling, well production, and well treatment in the oil and gas industry which are located in waters that are seaward of the inner boundary of the territorial seas (“offshore”) as defined in section 502(g) of the Clean Water Act.
