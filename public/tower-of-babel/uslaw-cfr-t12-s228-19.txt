12 C.F.R. § 228.19 — Areas for eligible community development loans, community development investments, and community development services.
Citation: us:cfr:12:228.19
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-228.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 228 (Community Reinvestment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may consider a bank's community development loans, community development investments, and community development services provided outside of its facility-based assessment areas, as provided in this part.
