40 U.S.C. § 6122 — Designation of members of the Supreme Court Police
Citation: us:usc:40:6122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6122
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 61 (United States Supreme Court Building And Grounds); Subchapter III (Policing Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under the general supervision and direction of the Chief Justice of the United States, the Marshal of the Supreme Court may designate employees of the Supreme Court as members of the Supreme Court Police, without additional compensation.
