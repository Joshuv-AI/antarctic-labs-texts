14 C.F.R. § 22.160 — Visibility.
Citation: us:cfr:14:22.160
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-22.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 22 (Design, Production, And Airworthiness Requirements For Non-Type Certificated Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The aircraft must be designed and constructed so that the pilot has—
(a) Sufficient visibility of controls, instruments, equipment, and placards; and
(b) Sufficient visibility outside the aircraft necessary to conduct safe aircraft operations.
