Abstract
. The orbit spaces of free 𝕊 0 \mathbb{S}^{0} -actions on the mod 2 cohomology product of three spheres, 𝕊 n × 𝕊 m × 𝕊 l , 1 ≤ n ≤ m ≤ l , \mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},1\leq n\leq m\leq l, have been determined in [ 6 ] . In this paper, we extend these findings to free 𝕊 1 \mathbb{S}^{1} -actions on the rational cohomology product of three spheres. This extension also builds upon the work of Dotzel et al. [ 7 ] , who studied free circle actions on the rational cohomology product of two spheres. Additionally, we establish Borsuk-Ulam type theorems.

Preliminaries

In this section, we recall some basic key results that are used throughout the paper.
Let

(Borel space) obtained by diagonal action of
G

We recall some results of the Leray-Serre spectral sequence associated with Borel fibration

For proofs, we refer
[
3
,
10
]
.

Proposition 2.1
.

(
[
10
]
)
Suppose that the system of local coefficients on
B_{G}
is simple. Then the homomorphisms
i^{*}:H^{*}(X_{G})\rightarrow H^{*}(X)
and
\pi^{*}:H^{*}(B_{G})\rightarrow H^{*}(X_{G})
are the edge homomorphisms,

H^{k}(B_{G})=E_{2}^{k,0}\rightarrow E_{3}^{k,0}\rightarrow\cdots E_{k}^{k,0}\rightarrow E_{k+1}^{k,0}=E_{\infty}^{k,0}\subset H^{k}(X_{G}),
and
H^{i}(X_{G})\rightarrow E_{\infty}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow E_{l+1}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow E_{l}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow\cdots\hookrightarrow E_{3}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow E_{2}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow H^{i}(X),
respectively.

Proposition 2.2
.

(
[
10
]
)
Let
G=\mathbb{S}^{1}
act on a finitistic space
X
and
\pi_{1}(B_{G})
acts trivially on
H^{*}(X).
Then the system of local coefficients on
B_{G}
is simple and

E^{k,i}_{2}=H^{k}(B_{G})\otimes H^{i}(X),~k,i\geq 0.

Proposition 2.3
.

(
[
10
]
)
Let
G=\mathbb{S}^{1}
act freely on a finitistic space
X.
Then the Borel space

is homotopy equivalent to the orbit space

Proposition 2.4
.

(
[
3
]
)
Let

act freely on a finitistic space
X.
If
H^{i}(X;\mathbb{Q})=0~\forall~i>n,
then
H^{i}(X/G;\mathbb{Q})=0~\forall~i>n.

Recall that

H^{*}(\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l};\mathbb{Q})=\mathbb{Q}[a,b,c]/<{a^{2},b^{2},c^{2}}>,
where deg

deg

and deg

3.
The Cohomology Algebra of The Orbit Space of Free Circle Actions on
\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l}

In this section, we determine the cohomology ring of orbit space of free
G=\mathbb{S}^{1}
actions on finitistic spaces
X
having rational cohomology of the product of three spheres, that is,
X\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},
where
1\leq n\leq m\leq l.

Let
\{E_{r}^{*,*},d_{r}\}
be the Leray-Serre spectral sequence of the Borel fibration
X\hookrightarrow X_{G}\rightarrow B_{G}.
As
G
act freely on a finitistic space
X\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},
we get the spectral sequence
\{E_{r}^{*,*},d_{r}\}
must be nondegenerate, that is,
E_{2}^{*,*}\neq E^{*,*}_{\infty}.
Also, as
\pi_{1}(B_{G})
is trivial, by Proposition
2.2
, we get

E_{2}^{k,i}=H^{k}(B_{G})\otimes H^{i}(X)~\forall~k,i\geq 0.

Thus
E_{2}^{k,i}\cong\mathbb{Q},
for
k=0,n,m,l,n+m,n+l,m+l,n+m+l,
and
\forall~i\geq 0.

Let

be generators of

The non-triviality of differentials

or

occurs under the following conditions:

•

if
d_{r_{1}}(1\otimes a)\neq 0,
then
r_{1}=n+1
&
n
is odd,

•

if
d_{r_{2}}(1\otimes b)\neq 0,
then
r_{2}=m-n+1
&

is odd or
r_{2}=m+1
&
m
is odd, and

•

is odd,
r_{3}=l-m+1
&
l-m
is odd,
r_{3}=l-n+1
&

is odd or
r_{3}=l+1
&
l
is odd.

We have proved the following theorems under conditions that at least one differential
