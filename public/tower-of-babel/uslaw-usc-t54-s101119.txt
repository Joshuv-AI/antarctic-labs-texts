54 U.S.C. § 101119 — Liability of United States
Citation: us:usc:54:101119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101119
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1011 (Donations); Subchapter II (National Park Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall not be liable for any debts, defaults, acts, or omissions of the National Park Foundation.
