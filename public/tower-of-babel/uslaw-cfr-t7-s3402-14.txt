7 C.F.R. § 3402.14 — Budget and budget narrative.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3402.14
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3402.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3402 (Food And Agricultural Sciences National Needs Graduate And Postgraduate Fellowship Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants must prepare the Budget, Form NIFA-2004, and a budget narrative identifying all costs associated with the application. Instructions for completing the Budget are provided with the form.
