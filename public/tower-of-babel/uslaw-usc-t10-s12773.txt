10 U.S.C. § 12773 — Limitation on accrual of increased pay or benefits
Citation: us:usc:10:12773
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12773
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1225 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless otherwise provided by law, no person is entitled to increased pay or other benefits because of sections 12771 and 12772 of this title.
