33 C.F.R. § 115.01 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:115.01
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-115.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 115 (Bridge Locations And Clearances; Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part states the requirements for applying for a permit to construct or modify bridges crossing the navigable waters of the United States. It also sets forth the procedures by which the application is processed by the Coast Guard.
