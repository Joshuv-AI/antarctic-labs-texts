25 U.S.C. § 2714 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:25:2714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2714
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 29 (Indian Gaming Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Decisions made by the Commission pursuant to sections 2710, 2711, 2712, and 2713 of this title shall be final agency decisions for purposes of appeal to the appropriate Federal district court pursuant to chapter 7 of title 5.
