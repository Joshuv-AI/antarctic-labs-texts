16 U.S.C. § 773k — Location of office space and other facilities on or near University of Washington campus in State of Washington
Citation: us:usc:16:773k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section773k
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 10 (Northern Pacific Halibut Fishing); Subchapter IV (Northern Pacific Halibut Act Of 1982). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the Secretary of State to provide for fiscal year 1983 and beyond, by contract, grant, or otherwise, facilities for office and any other necessary space for the Commission. Such facilities shall be located on or near the campus of the University of Washington in the State of Washington and shall be provided without regard to the cost-sharing provisions in the Convention.
