5 C.F.R. § 1206.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1206.1
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1206.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1206 (Open Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe the procedures by which the Board will conduct open meetings in accordance with the Government in the Sunshine Act (5 U.S.C. 552b) (“the Act”).
