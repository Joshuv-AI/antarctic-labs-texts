43 C.F.R. § 3833.31 — What is a transfer of interest?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3833.31
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3833.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3833 (Recording Mining Claims And Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A transfer of interest is a sale, assignment, transfer through inheritance, or conveyance of total or partial ownership or legal interest in a mining claim or site.
