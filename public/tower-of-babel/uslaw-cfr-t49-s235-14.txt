49 C.F.R. § 235.14 — Notice.
Citation: us:cfr:49:235.14
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-235.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 235 (Instructions Governing Applications For Approval Of A Discontinuance Or Material Modification Of A Signal System Or Relief From The Requirements Of Part 236). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FRA will publish notice of the filing of an application or a request for reconsideration of an application in the Federal Register and a copy of such notice will be available at the U.S. Department of Transportation, Docket Operations (M-30), West Building Ground Floor, Room W12-140, 1200 New Jersey Avenue, SE., Washington, DC 20590, and on the Federal Docket Management System's Web site at http://www.regulations.gov.
