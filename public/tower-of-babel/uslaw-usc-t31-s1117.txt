31 U.S.C. § 1117 — Exemption
Citation: us:usc:31:1117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1117
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 11 (The Budget And Fiscal, Budget, And Program Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget may exempt from the requirements of sections 1115 and 1116 of this title and section 306 of title 5, any agency with annual outlays of $20,000,000 or less.
