23 C.F.R. § 650.109 — Public involvement.
Citation: us:cfr:23:650.109
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-650.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 650 (Bridges, Structures, And Hydraulics). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures which have been established to meet the public involvement requirements of 23 CFR part 771 shall be used to provide opportunity for early public review and comment on alternatives which contain encroachments.
