41 C.F.R. § 102-2.135 — -2.135 How do agencies obtain forms prescribed by the FMR?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-2.135
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-2.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-2 (2—Federal Management Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For copies of the forms prescribed by in the FMR, do any of the following:
(a) Write to us at: General Services Administration, National Forms and Publications Center (7CPN), Warehouse 4, Dock No. 1, 501 West Felix Street, Fort Worth, TX 76115.
(b) Send e-mail messages to: NFPC@gsa-7FDepot.
(c) Visit our web site at: www.gsa.gov/forms/forms.htm.
