42 U.S.C. § 3232 — Authorization of appropriations for defense conversion activities
Citation: us:usc:42:3232
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3232
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter VII (Funding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
In addition to amounts made available under section 3231 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out section 3149(c)(1) of this title, to remain available until expended.
(b) Pilot projects
Funds made available under subsection (a) may be used for activities including pilot projects for privatization of, and economic development activities for, closed or realigned military or Department of Energy installations.
