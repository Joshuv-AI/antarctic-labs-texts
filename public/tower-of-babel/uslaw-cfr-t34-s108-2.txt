34 C.F.R. § 108.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:34:108.2
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-108.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 108 (Equal Access To Public School Facilities For The Boy Scouts Of America And Other Designated Youth Groups). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any public elementary school, public secondary school, local educational agency, or State educational agency that has a designated open forum or limited public forum and that receives funds made available through the Department.
