32 C.F.R. § 70.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:70.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-70.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 70 (Discharge Review Board (Drb) Procedures And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part 70 apply to the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD) and the Military Departments. The terms, “Military Services,” and “Armed Forces,” as used herein, refer to the Army, Navy, Air Force and Marine Corps.
