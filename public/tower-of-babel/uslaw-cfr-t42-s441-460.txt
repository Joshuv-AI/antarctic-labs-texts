42 C.F.R. § 441.460 — Participant living arrangements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:441.460
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-441.460
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 441 (Services: Requirements And Limits Applicable To Specific Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Self-directed PAS are not available to an individual who resides in a home or property that is owned, operated, or controlled by a PAS provider who is not related to the individual by blood or marriage.
(b) States may specify additional restrictions on a participant's living arrangements if they have been approved by CMS.
