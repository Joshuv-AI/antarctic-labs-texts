16 U.S.C. § 1646 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:1646
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1646
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter II (Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated annually such sums as may be needed to implement this subchapter. Funds appropriated under this subchapter shall remain available until expended.
