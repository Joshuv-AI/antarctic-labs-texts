42 C.F.R. § 512.270 — Treatment of add-on payments under existing Medicare payment systems.
Citation: us:cfr:42:512.270
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-512.270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 512 (Standard Provisions For Mandatory Innovation Center Models And Specific Provisions For Certain Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) CMS does not make separate Medicare FFS payments to RO participants for any included RT services that are furnished to an RO beneficiary during an RO episode.
(b) An RO participant may receive Medicare FFS payment for items and services furnished to an RO beneficiary during an RO episode, provided that any such other item or service is not an included RT service.
