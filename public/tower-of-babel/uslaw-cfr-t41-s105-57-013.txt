41 C.F.R. § 105-57.013 — -57.013 Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-57.013
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-57.013
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-57 (57—Administration Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a hearing official, at a hearing held pursuant to § 105-57.005 of this part, determines that a debt is not legally due and owing to the United States, GSA will promptly refund any amount collected by means of administrative wage garnishment.
(b) Unless required by Federal law or contract, refunds under this part will not bear interest.
