16 U.S.C. § 1167 — Leases, permits, agreements, and contracts with public or private agencies or persons
Citation: us:usc:16:1167
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1167
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 24 (Conservation And Protection Of North Pacific Fur Seals); Subchapter II (Administration Of Pribilof Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to enter into agreements or contracts or leases with, or to issue permits to, any public or private agency or person for carrying out the provisions of the Convention or this chapter.
