49 C.F.R. § 216.21 — Notice of track conditions.

Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-216.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 216 (Special Notice And Emergency Order Procedures: Railroad Track, Locomotive And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When an FRA Track Inspector or State Track Inspector finds track conditions which may require the issuance of an Emergency order removing the track from service under section 203, Public Law 91-458, 84 Stat. 972, the Inspector may issue a notice to the railroad owning the track. The notice sets out and describes the conditions found by the Inspector and specifies the location of defects on the affected track segment. The Inspector provides a copy to the FRA Track and Structures Division Staff Director.
(b) In the event the railroad immediately commences repairs on the affected track and so advises the FRA Track and Structures Division Staff Director, the Staff Director assigns an Inspector to reinspect the track immediately on the completion of repairs. If upon reinspection the Inspector determines that necessary repairs have been completed, the Inspector withdraws the Notice of Track Conditions.
