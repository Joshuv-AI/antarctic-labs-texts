36 U.S.C. § 70914 — Distribution of assets on dissolution or final liquidation
Citation: us:usc:36:70914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70914
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 709 (Future Farmers Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On dissolution or final liquidation of the FFA, any assets remaining after the discharge of all liabilities shall be used by the board of directors for the benefit of students of agricultural education or be transferred to a recognized educational foundation.
