26 C.F.R. § 20.6314-1 — -1 Duplicate receipts for payment of estate taxes.
Citation: us:cfr:26:20.6314-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-20.6314-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 20 (Estate Tax; Estates Of Decedents Dying After August 16, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The internal revenue officer with whom the estate tax return is filed will, upon request, give to the person paying the tax duplicate receipts, either of which will be sufficient evidence of such payment and entitle the executor to be credited with the amount by any court having jurisdiction to audit or settle his accounts.
