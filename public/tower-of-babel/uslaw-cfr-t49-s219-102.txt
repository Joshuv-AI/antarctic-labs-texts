49 C.F.R. § 219.102 — Prohibition on abuse of controlled substances.
Citation: us:cfr:49:219.102
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-219.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 219 (Control Of Alcohol And Drug Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No regulated employee may use a controlled substance at any time, whether on duty or off duty, except as permitted by § 219.103.
