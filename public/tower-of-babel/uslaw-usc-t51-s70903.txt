51 U.S.C. § 70903 — International Space Station research
Citation: us:usc:51:70903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70903
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 709 (International Space Station). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall—
(1)
carry out a program of microgravity research consistent with section 40904 of this title; and
(2)
consider the need for a life sciences centrifuge and any associated holding facilities.
