45 C.F.R. § 97.10 — What is a consolidated grant?
Citation: us:cfr:45:97.10
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-97.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 97 (Consolidation Of Grants To The Insular Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, a consolidated grant means a grant award to an insular area, the funds of which are derived from the allocations under two or more of the programs specified in § 97.12.
