Site preference of chalcogen atoms in 1T ′ M ​ X 2 ​ ( 1 − x ) ​ Y 2 ​ x MX_{2(1-x)}Y_{2x} ( M = M= Mo and W; X , Y = X,Y= S, Se, and Te)
Shota Ono, Ryotaro Ohse

Abstract
The insulator-metal transition, accompanying the structural phase transition from 2H to 1T ′ structure, has been reported in two-dimensional W-S-Te and W-Se-Te systems. It is also reported that Te atoms tend to occupy a specific site of the 1T ′ structure. Here, we study the site preference of chalcogen atoms in M ​ X 2 ​ ( 1 − x ) ​ Y 2 ​ x MX_{2(1-x)}Y_{2x} ( M = M= Mo and W; X , Y = X,Y= S, Se, and Te; 0 ≤ x ≤ 1 0\leq x\leq 1 ) using first-principles approach. We demonstrate that the site preference of chalcogen atoms explains the universal correlation between the formation energy and the Peierls-like distortion amplitude in the 1T ′ phase. The impact of the site preference on the linear elastic properties is strong, whereas its impact is weak in the non-linear regime. This establishes the structure-property relationships in M ​ X 2 ​ ( 1 − x ) ​ Y 2 ​ x MX_{2(1-x)}Y_{2x} systems.

II
Computational details

We considered 2H and 1T
′
structures and calculated the total energies of
MX_{2(1-x)}Y_{2x}
(
0\leq x\leq 1
) monolayer assuming a
2\times 1
supercell of the rectangular unit cell (see Fig.

qe
. The generalized-gradient approximation (GGA) parametrized by Perdew, Burke, and Ernzerhof (PBE)

was used for the exchange-correlation energy functional. The pslibrary
pslibrary
was used for the pseudopotentials of W, Te, and S atoms (Mo.pbe-spn-, W.pbe-spfn-, S.pbe-nl-, Se.pbe-n-, and Te.pbe-n-rrkjus_psl.1.0.0.UPF). Spin-polarized calculations were performed. The energy cutoff for wavefunctions and charge density was set to be 60 and 480 Ry, respectively. Convergence thresholds for the total energy in the self-consistent field calculations was set to be
10^{-6}
Ry, and those for the total energy and forces for structure optimization were set to be
10^{-4}
Ry and
10^{-3}
a.u., respectively. We assumed the smearing parameter of 0.01 Ry
smearingMV
and
k
-point distance
\Delta k
smaller than 0.15 Å
-1
. The orthorhombic unit cell was used and the length of the
c
axis was fixed to 20 Å to avoid the spurious interaction between slabs along the out-of-plane direction. Crystal structures were visualized using VESTA
vesta
.

IV
Conclusion

We have studied the structural properties of 1T
′
-structured
MX_{2(1-x)}Y_{2x}
(
M=
Mo and W;
X,Y=
S, Se, and Te;
0\leq x\leq 1
) using first-principles approach. We have shown that (i) a universal correlation exists between the formation energy and the distortion amplitude of the 1T
′
structure and (ii) the site preference of Te atoms plays an important role in stabilizing the 1T
′
phase. Such preference influences the linear elastic constants, while no such correlations are observed in the non-linear regime. The present work indicates that the structure-elastic property relationship is valid within the linear elastic regime.
