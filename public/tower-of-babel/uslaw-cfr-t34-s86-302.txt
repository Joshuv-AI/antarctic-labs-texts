34 C.F.R. § 86.302 — What are the procedures used by the Secretary for providing information or technical assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.302
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary provides information or technical assistance to an IHE in writing, through site visits, or by other means.
(b) The IHE shall inform the Secretary of any corrective action it has taken within a period specified by the Secretary.
