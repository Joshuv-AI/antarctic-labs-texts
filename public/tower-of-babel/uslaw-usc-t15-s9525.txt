15 U.S.C. § 9525 — Minimizing disruptions to existing MBDA Business Center program
Citation: us:usc:15:9525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9525
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter I (Existing Initiatives); Part B (Minority Business Development Agency Business Center Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Under Secretary shall ensure that each Federal assistance award made under the Business Centers program of the Agency, as is in effect on the day before November 15, 2021, is carried out in a manner that, to the greatest extent practicable, prevents disruption of any activity carried out under that award.
