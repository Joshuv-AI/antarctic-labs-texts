16 U.S.C. § 1272 — Congressional declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:16:1272
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1272
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 28 (Wild And Scenic Rivers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to implement the policy set out in section 1271 of this title by instituting a national wild and scenic rivers system, by designating the initial components of that system, and by prescribing the methods by which and standards according to which additional components may be added to the system from time to time.
