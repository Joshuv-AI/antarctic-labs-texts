46 C.F.R. § 294.25 — National security modifications.
Citation: us:cfr:46:294.25
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-294.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 294 (Tanker Security Program (Tsp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant agrees to the installation onboard its TSP Fleet Vessel of militarily useful features for national defense purposes as approved by U.S. Coast Guard and the vessel's classification society.
