43 C.F.R. § 3273.17 — When may BLM reassess the annual rent for my site license?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3273.17
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3273.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM may reassess the rent for lands covered by the license, beginning with the 10th year and every 10 years after that.
