14 C.F.R. § 450.7 — Duration of a vehicle operator license.
Citation: us:cfr:14:450.7
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-450.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 450 (Launch And Reentry License Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vehicle operator license is valid for the period of time determined by the Administrator as necessary to conduct the licensed activity but may not exceed 5 years from the issuance date.
