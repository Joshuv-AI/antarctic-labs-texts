48 U.S.C. § 1421e — Duty on articles
Citation: us:usc:48:1421e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1421e
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All articles coming into the United States from Guam shall be subject to or exempt from duty as provided for in section 1301a 1 of title 19.
