10 C.F.R. § 1022.17 — Follow-up.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1022.17
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1022.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1022 (Compliance With Floodplain And Wetland Environmental Review Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For those DOE actions taken in a floodplain or wetland, DOE shall verify that the implementation of the selected alternative, particularly with regard to any adopted mitigation measures, is proceeding as described in the floodplain or wetland assessment and the floodplain statement of findings.
