16 U.S.C. § 45d — Exclusive privileges within park prohibited
Citation: us:usc:16:45d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section45d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter VI (Sequoia And Yosemite National Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No exclusive privilege shall be granted within said park, or on or over the roads and trails therein, except upon ground leased for the erection of buildings or camps thereon.
