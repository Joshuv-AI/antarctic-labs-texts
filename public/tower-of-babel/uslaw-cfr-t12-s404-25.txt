12 C.F.R. § 404.25 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:12:404.25
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-404.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 404 (Information Disclosure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the Privacy Act.
