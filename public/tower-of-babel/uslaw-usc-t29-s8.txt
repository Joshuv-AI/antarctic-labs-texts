29 U.S.C. § 8 — Unemployment data relating to Americans of Spanish origin or descent
Citation: us:usc:29:8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section8
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 1 (Labor Statistics); Subchapter I (Bureau Of Labor Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Labor, in cooperation with the Department of Commerce, shall develop methods for improving and expanding the collection, analysis, and publication of unemployment data relating to Americans of Spanish origin or descent.
