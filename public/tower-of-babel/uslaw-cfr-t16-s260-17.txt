16 C.F.R. § 260.17 — Source reduction claims.
Citation: us:cfr:16:260.17
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-260.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 260 (Guides For The Use Of Environmental Marketing Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is deceptive to misrepresent, directly or by implication, that a product or package has been reduced or is lower in weight, volume, or toxicity. Marketers should clearly and prominently qualify source reduction claims to the extent necessary to avoid deception about the amount of the source reduction and the basis for any comparison.
