31 C.F.R. § 560.419 — U.S. employment of persons ordinarily resident in Iran.
Citation: us:cfr:31:560.419
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-560.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 560 (Iranian Transactions And Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions in § 560.201 make it unlawful to hire an Iranian national ordinarily resident in Iran to come to the United States solely or for the principal purpose of engaging in employment on behalf of an entity in Iran or as the employee of a U.S. person, unless authorized pursuant to § 560.505. See also § 560.418 with respect to the release of technology and software.
