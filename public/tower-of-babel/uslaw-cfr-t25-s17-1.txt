25 C.F.R. § 17.1 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:25:17.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-17.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 17 (Action On Wills Of Osage Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When used in the regulations in this part the following words or terms shall have the meaning shown below:
(a) Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
(b) Commissioner means the Commissioner of Indian Affairs.
(c) Superintendent means the superintendent of the Osage Indian Agency.
(d) Special attorney means the special attorney for Osage Indians, or other legal officer designated by the Commissioner.
