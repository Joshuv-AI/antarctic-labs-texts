5 C.F.R. § 1639.13 — Antialienation of funds in Thrift Savings Plan participant accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1639.13
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1639.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1639 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 U.S.C. 8437, net assets available for Thrift Savings Plan benefits will not be used to satisfy a debt owed by a participant to an agency under the regulations of this part or under the debt collection regulations of any agency.
