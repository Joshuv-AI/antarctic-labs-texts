43 C.F.R. § 404.59 — If the Secretary recommends a project for construction, is that a promise of Federal funding?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.59
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Congress must first authorize the project for construction and Federal funding is subject to the availability of appropriations.
