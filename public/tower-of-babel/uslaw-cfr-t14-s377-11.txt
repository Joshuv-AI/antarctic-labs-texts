14 C.F.R. § 377.11 — Processing of defective renewal applications.
Citation: us:cfr:14:377.11
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-377.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 377 (Continuance Of Expired Authorizations By Operation Of Law Pending Final Determination Of Applications For Renewal Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Department determines that a renewal application does not comply with the requirements of this part, or that it does not relate to a license with reference to an activity of a continuing nature, it will so notify the applicant. The applicant may amend his application to cure the deficiency as a matter of right at any time prior to the date when the application was due pursuant to § 377.10(c).
