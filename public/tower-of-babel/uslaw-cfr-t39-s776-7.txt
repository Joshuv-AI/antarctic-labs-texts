39 C.F.R. § 776.7 — Lease, easement, right-of-way, or disposal of property to non-federal parties.
Citation: us:cfr:39:776.7
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-776.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 776 (Floodplain And Wetland Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When postal property in floodplains is proposed for lease, easement, right-of-way, or disposal to non-federal public or private parties, the Postal Service shall:
(a) Reference in the conveyance document that the parcel is located in a floodplain and may be restricted in use pursuant to federal, state, or local floodplain regulations; or
(b) Withhold the property from conveyance.
