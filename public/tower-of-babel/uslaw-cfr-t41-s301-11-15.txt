41 C.F.R. § 301-11.15 — -11.15 Reimbursement for voluntary return during TDY assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.15
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee voluntarily returns home or to their official station on non-workdays during a TDY assignment, the maximum reimbursement for round-trip transportation and subsistence expenses is limited to what would have been allowed had the employee remained at the TDY location.
