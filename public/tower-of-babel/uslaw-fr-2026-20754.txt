    Title of Collection: Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System 
(IPEDS) 2026-27 through 2028-29.
    OMB Control Number: 1850-0582.

    Respondents/Affected Public: Private Sector; State, Local, and

    Abstract: The National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) is 
seeking clearance, including revisions, for the 2026-27, 2027-28, and 
2028-29 Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS) data 
collections. The currently active OMB clearance for IPEDS (1850-0582, 
v. 33) was granted on August 05, 2024, approved with change on December 
16, 2025, and extends through August 31, 2027.
    IPEDS is a web-based data collection system designed to collect 
basic data from all postsecondary institutions in the United States and 
the other jurisdictions. The IPEDS data collection enables NCES to 
report on key dimensions of postsecondary education such as admission 
and enrollments, degrees and other awards earned, tuition and fees, 
average net price, student financial aid, graduation rates, student 
outcomes, revenues and expenditures, faculty salaries, and staff 
employed. IPEDS data are available to the public through multiple 
websites, including the College Navigator and IPEDS Use the Data 
websites. The reported data is also used for research and analysis to 
inform decisionmakers at institutions, state and federal government, 
and other stake holding parties. All Title IV institutions are required 
to respond to IPEDS (Section 490 of the Higher Education Amendments of 
1992 [Pub. L. 102-325]). IPEDS allows other (non-Title IV) institutions 
to participate on a voluntary basis.
    In Version 34 of this clearance, NCES proposes changes only to the 
Admissions and Consumer Transparency Supplement (ACTS). Specifically, 
we propose: (1) a change in the eligibility criteria for the ACTS 
components; (2) improvements in the ACTS aggregator tool; (3) removal 
of variables that, despite their potential analytic utility, were 
determined during the disclosure avoidance process to reduce the 
utility of the ACTS data collection overall; and (4) clarifications to 
instructions and item definitions based on feedback from institutions 
and practitioners provided prior to a recent Technical Review Panel 
(TRP) held in 2026. All other IPEDS survey components are unchanged 
from the previously approved collection.
    NCES encourages the public to thoroughly review and comment upon 
the contents of this package.
