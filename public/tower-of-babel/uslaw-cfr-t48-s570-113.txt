48 C.F.R. § 570.113 — 570.113 Disclosure of mistakes after award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:570.113
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-570.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 570 (Acquiring Leasehold Interests In Real Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a mistake in a lessor's offer is discovered after award, the contracting officer should process it substantially in accordance with FAR 14.407-4 and GSAM 514.407-4.
