32 C.F.R. § 174.8 — Screening for properties covered by the Base Closure Community Redevelopment and Homeless Assistance Act of 1994, cross-reference.
Citation: us:cfr:32:174.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-174.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 174 (Revitalizing Base Closure Communities And Addressing Impacts Of Realignment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Departments of Defense and Housing and Urban Development have promulgated regulations to address state and local screening and approval of redevelopment plans for installations covered by the Base Closure Community Redevelopment and Homeless Assistance Act of 1994 (Pub. L. 103-421). The Department of Defense regulations can be found at part 176 of this title. The Department of Housing and Urban Development regulations can be found at 24 CFR part 586.
