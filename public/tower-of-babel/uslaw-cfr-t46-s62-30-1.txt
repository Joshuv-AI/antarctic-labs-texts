46 C.F.R. § 62.30-1 — -1 Failsafe.
Citation: us:cfr:46:62.30-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-62.30-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 62 (Vital System Automation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The failsafe state must be evaluated for each subsystem, system, or vessel to determine the least critical consequence.
(b) All automatic control, remote control, safety control, and alarm systems must be failsafe.
