25 U.S.C. § 2807 — Uniform allowance
Citation: us:usc:25:2807
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2807
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 30 (Indian Law Enforcement Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the limitation in section 5901(a) of title 5, the Secretary may provide a uniform allowance for uniformed law enforcement officers under section 2803 of this title of not more than $400 a year.
