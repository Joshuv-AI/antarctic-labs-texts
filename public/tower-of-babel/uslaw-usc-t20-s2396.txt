20 U.S.C. § 2396 — Federal laws guaranteeing civil rights
Citation: us:usc:20:2396
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2396
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 44 (Career And Technical Education); Subchapter II (General Provisions); Part A (Federal Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to be inconsistent with applicable Federal law prohibiting discrimination on the basis of race, color, sex, national origin, age, or disability in the provision of Federal programs or services.
