50 C.F.R. § 14.112 — Other applicable provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:14.112
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-14.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 14 (Importation, Exportation, And Transportation Of Wildlife). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the provisions of §§ 14.101-14.111, the requirements of §§ 14.121-14.172 applicable for particular groups of animals shall be met for all shipments of wild mammals and birds covered by this part.
