10 U.S.C. § 20263 — Entitlement of officers discharged or retired under this chapter to separation pay or retired pay
Citation: us:usc:10:20263
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20263
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2005 (Officers); Subchapter V (Continuation On Active Duty And Selective Early Retirement; Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Separation Pay.—
An officer who is discharged under this chapter is entitled, if eligible therefor, to separation pay under section 1174 of this title.
(b) Retired Pay.—
An officer who is retired under this chapter is entitled to retired pay computed under chapter 71 or 1223 of this title, as applicable.
