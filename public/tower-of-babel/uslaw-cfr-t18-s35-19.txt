18 C.F.R. § 35.19 — Submission of information by reference.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 35 (Filing Of Rate Schedules And Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If all or any portion of the information called for in this part has already been submitted to the Commission, substantially in the form prescribed above, specific reference thereto may be made in lieu of re-submission in response to the requirements of this part.
