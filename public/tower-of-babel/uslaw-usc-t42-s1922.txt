42 U.S.C. § 1922 — Establishment of medals for character and service; condition governing awards
Citation: us:usc:42:1922
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1922
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 18 (Youth Medals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Justice shall also honor by an appropriate medal such American boy or girl citizens, eighteen years old or under, who, in the opinion of the said Department of Justice, shall have achieved outstanding or unusual recognition for character and service during any given year.
