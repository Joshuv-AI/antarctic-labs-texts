39 C.F.R. § 3022.21 — Investigator.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3022.21
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3022.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3022 (Rules For Complaints). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may appoint an investigator to examine issues raised by the complaint and responses thereto. The investigator will use appropriate due diligence under the circumstances and provide a public, written report to the Commission.
