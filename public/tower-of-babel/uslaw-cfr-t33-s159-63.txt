33 C.F.R. § 159.63 — Access to parts.
Citation: us:cfr:33:159.63
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-159.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 159 (Marine Sanitation Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each part of the device that is required by the manufacturer's instructions to be serviced routinely must be readily accessible in the installed position of the device recommended by the manufacturer.
