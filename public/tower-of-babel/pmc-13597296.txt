Author Correction: Impaired cAMP–PKA–CREB1 signalling drives mitochondrial dysfunction in skeletal muscle during cancer cachexia

Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Metabolism https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-025-01397-5, published online 12 November 2025.

In this correction, we report the replacement of the source data and graphs for Fig. 6e, 6h and 6i due to inadvertent data assignment errors identified during a re-analysis of the original respirometry datasets. The corrected data do not affect any of the results or statements reported in the article, nor the conclusions drawn from them. The figure and Source Data are now updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. For comparison, the original Fig. 6 is available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment.
