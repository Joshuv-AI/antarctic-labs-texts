21 C.F.R. § 876.5470 — Ureteral dilator.
Citation: us:cfr:21:876.5470
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-876.5470
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 876 (Gastroenterology-Urology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A ureteral dilator is a device that consists of a specially shaped catheter or bougie and is used to dilate the ureter at the place where a stone has become lodged or to dilate a ureteral stricture.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
