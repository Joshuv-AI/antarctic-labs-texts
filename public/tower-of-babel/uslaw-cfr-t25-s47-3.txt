25 C.F.R. § 47.3 — How does a Bureau-operated school find out how much funding it will receive?
Citation: us:cfr:25:47.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-47.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 47 (Uniform Direct Funding And Support For Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Indian Education Programs (OIEP) will notify each Bureau-operated school in writing of the annual funding amount it will receive as follows:
(a) No later than July 1 OIEP will let the Bureau-operated school know the amount that is 80 percent of its funding; and
(b) No later than September 30 OIEP will let the Bureau-operated school know the amount of the remaining 20 percent.
