Fibred sets within a predicative and constructive effective topos
Cipriano Junior Cioffo, Maria Emilia Maietti, Samuele Maschio

Abstract
We describe the fibrational structure of sets within the predicative variant 𝐩𝐄𝐟𝐟 \mathbf{pEff} of Hyland’s Effective Topos 𝐄𝐟𝐟 {\mathbf{Eff}} previously introduced in Feferman’s predicative theory of non-iterative fixpoints I ​ D 1 ^ \widehat{ID_{1}} . Our structural analysis can be carried out in constructive and predicative variants of 𝐄𝐟𝐟 {\mathbf{Eff}} within extensions of Aczel’s Constructive Zermelo-Fraenkel Set Theory. All this shows that the full subcategory of discrete objects of Hyland’s Effective topos 𝐄𝐟𝐟 {\mathbf{Eff}} contains already a fibred predicative topos validating the formal Church’s thesis, even when both are formalized in a constructive metatheory.

Our predicative meta-theory.

2.1
A classical predicative metatheory l̀a Feferman:

\widehat{ID_{1}}
.

Feferman’s theory of non-iterative fixpoints
\widehat{ID_{1}}

[
Fef82
]
is a first-order classical theory with equality containing
0
,
succ
, the functional symbols for the (definitions of) primitive recursive functions and a unary predicate symbol

for every admissible second-order formula

(see
[

,
MM16
,
MM21
]
for more details).

The axioms of
\widehat{ID_{1}}
include those of Peano arithmetic (even with those defining equations for primitive recursive functions) plus the
induction principle
for every formula of
\widehat{ID_{1}}
and a
fixpoint schema
:
P_{\varphi}(x)\leftrightarrow\varphi[P_{\varphi}/X]
for every admissible second-order formula
\varphi
, where
\varphi[P_{\varphi}/X]
is the result of substituting in
\varphi
all the subformulas of the form
t\,\epsilon\,X
with
P_{\varphi}(t)
.

We follow the notation for recursive functions in
\widehat{ID_{1}}
in
[
MM21
]
.

2.2
A constructive and predicative meta-theory: extensions of
CZF

P. Aczel introduced
\mathbf{CZF}
as a constructive theory of sets (see
[
AR01
]
for its axioms). In this paper we are interested in two extensions of it used in
[
MMR21
]
and
[
MMR22
]
to prove the consistency of the Minimalist Foundation with inductive and coinductive topological generation, respectively. The first extension adds to
\mathbf{CZF}
the so-called regular extension axiom
\mathbf{REA}
which states that every set is a subset of a regular set. The second extension adds to
\mathbf{CZF}
the axiom
\bigcup-\mathbf{REA}
, stating that every set is contained in a
\bigcup
-regular set, and the axiom of relativized dependent choice (
\mathbf{RDC}
). In
[
MMR21
]
and
[
MMR22
]
one can see how these axioms play a crucial role in interpreting universes and inductive and coinductive topological generation. Further details on these theories can be found in
[
AR01
]
.

In what follows we will write
\mathcal{T}
as a metavariable for any of the theories
\widehat{ID_{1}}
,
\mathbf{CZF}+\mathbf{REA}
or
\mathbf{CZF}+\mathbf{RDC}+\bigcup-\mathbf{REA}
.

Let

.

The functor

is a first-order hyperdoctrine equivalent to the subobjects doctrine
\mathbf{pEff}_{prop}\cong\mathbf{Sub}_{\mathbf{pEff}}
.
∎

We now provide a similar correspondence for

regarding suitable subobjects. We will denote with

the doctrine which sends an object

to the poset of subobjects of

over

which have a representative in

. This is a well-defined functor thanks to Lemma

. We will refer to these objects as

Lemma 8.2
.

Let
(\mathsf{A},\mathsf{R})
be an object of
pEff
. Given an element

if and only if

.

Proof.

This follows from Lemma
7.3
, and the fact that an arrow

in
\mathbf{pEff}
with codomain
(\mathsf{A},[\mathsf{R}])
is a monomorphism if and only if it is a monomorphism in
\mathbf{pEff}/(\mathsf{A},[\mathsf{R}])
seen as an arrow from

to

. Indeed,
id_{(\mathsf{A},[\mathsf{R}])}
is isomorphic to
\mathbf{K}(1,\top,!)
.
∎

Proposition 8.3
.

The functor
\mathbf{pEff}_{props}
is equivalent to the small subobjects doctrine
\mathbf{Sub}_{\mathbf{pEff}}^{\mathbf{s}}
.

Proof.

Given an object
(\mathsf{A},[\mathsf{R}])\in\mathbf{pEff}
, consider a subobject over it which has a representative of the form
\mathbf{K}(\mathsf{B},[\mathsf{S}],\sigma)
where
(\mathsf{B},[\mathsf{S}],\sigma)\in Dep_{\mathbf{pEff}_{set}}(\mathsf{A},\mathsf{R})
. We can associate to it the element
[\mathsf{B}]\in\overline{{\mathbf{Prop}^{r}_{\mathbf{s}}}}(\mathsf{A})
. Thanks to
\sigma
, it follows that
[\mathsf{B}]\in\mathbf{pEff}_{props}(\mathsf{A},[\mathsf{R}])
. Moreover, for two monomorphisms of this form, it holds that
\mathbf{K}(\mathsf{B},[\mathsf{S}],\sigma)\leq\mathbf{K}(\mathsf{B}^{\prime},[\mathsf{S}^{\prime}],\sigma^{\prime})
if and only if
[\mathsf{B}]\leq[\mathsf{B}^{\prime}]
in
\mathbf{pEff}_{props}(\mathsf{A},[\mathsf{R}])
. Conversely, given a

and a representative

(whose existence is guaranteed by (

)), we associate the equivalence class of
\mathbf{K}(\mathsf{P},\top,\sigma)
which is a monomorphism thanks to Lemma

. This provides the desired equivalence.
∎

Applying the same argument in

Theorem 8.4

There exists an object

.
∎

11
Conclusions

We have shown here that
the full subcategory of discrete objects of Hyland’s Effective topos
\mathbf{Eff}
contains already a
fibred predicative topos
validating the formal Church’s thesis, even when both are formalized in a constructive metatheory. We did so by enucleating a fibred structure for
\mathbf{pEff}
introduced in

[
MM21
]
that would be enough to model both levels of
MF
and its extensions with inductive and conductive predicates in
[
MMR21
,
MMR22
,
MS23
,
Sab24
]
.

Furthermore, thanks to the equiconsistency of both levels of
MF
with their classical version shown in
[
MS24
]
, we could use
\mathbf{pEff}
as a computational model for the classical version of
MF
, too. And, since both levels of
MF
can be interpreted in
\mathbf{HoTT}
in a compatible way
as shown in
[
CM24
]
, it would be interesting to investigate whether
\mathbf{pEff}
is somewhat related to computational models of Homotopy Type theory, for example to that in
[
SA21
]
.

In the future, we intend to investigate abstract notions of
fibred predicative topos
and of
predicative tripos-to-topos construction
of which the fibred structure of
\mathbf{pEff}
enucleated here would provide a key example.
