41 C.F.R. § 101-26.800 — -26.800 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-26.800
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-26.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes a uniform system for reporting discrepancies or deficiencies in material or shipments and processing requests for or documenting adjustments in billings from or directed by GSA or Department of Defense (DOD) activities.
