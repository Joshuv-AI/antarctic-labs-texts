33 C.F.R. § 150.210 — What are the restrictions on serving in more than one position?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.210
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may serve in more than one of the essential management positions outlined in the operations manual at any one time.
