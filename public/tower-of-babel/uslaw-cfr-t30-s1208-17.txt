30 C.F.R. § 1208.17 — Suspensions for national emergencies.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1208.17
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1208.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1208 (Sale Of Federal Royalty Oil). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Department of the Interior, upon a recommendation by the Secretary of Defense or the Secretary of Energy and with the approval of the President, may suspend operations under these regulations and suspend royalty oil contracts during a national emergency declared by the Congress or the President.
