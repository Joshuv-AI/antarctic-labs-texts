49 C.F.R. § 350.219 — MCSAP administration: Award of funds under a continuing resolution or an extension of FMCSA's authorization.
Citation: us:cfr:49:350.219
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-350.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 350 (Motor Carrier Safety Assistance Program (Mcsap) And High Priority Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of a continuing resolution or an extension of FMCSA's authorization, subject to the availability of funding, FMCSA may first issue grants to States that have the lowest percent of undelivered obligations of the previous Federal fiscal year's funding, or as otherwise determined by the Administrator.
