39 U.S.C. § 3212 — Congressional Record under frank of Members of Congress
Citation: us:usc:39:3212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3212
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 32 (Penalty And Franked Mail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Members of Congress may send the Congressional Record as franked mail.
(b)
Members of Congress may send, as franked mail, any part of, or a reprint of any part of, the Congressional Record, including speeches or reports contained therein, if such matter is mailable as franked mail under section 3210 of this title.
