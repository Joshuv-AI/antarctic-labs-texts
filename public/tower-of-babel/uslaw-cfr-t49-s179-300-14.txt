49 C.F.R. § 179.300-14 — -14 Attachments not otherwise specified.
Citation: us:cfr:49:179.300-14
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-179.300-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Siphon pipes and their couplings on the inside of the tank head and lugs on the outside of the tank head for attaching the valve protective housing must be fusion-welded in place prior to postweld heat treatment. All other fixtures and appurtenances, except as specifically provided for, are prohibited.
