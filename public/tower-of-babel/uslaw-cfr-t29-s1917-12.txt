29 C.F.R. § 1917.12 — Slippery conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1917.12
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1917.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1917 (Marine Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employer shall eliminate, to the extent possible, conditions causing slippery working and walking surfaces in immediate work areas used by employees.
