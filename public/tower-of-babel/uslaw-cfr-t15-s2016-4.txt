15 C.F.R. § 2016.4 — Information open to public inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:15:2016.4
Creator: Office Of The United States Trade Representative
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-2016.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 2016 (Procedures To Petition For Withdrawal Or Suspension Of Country Eligibility Or Duty-Free Treatment Under The Andean Trade Preference Act (Atpa), As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With the exception of information subject to § 2016.5, any person may, on request, inspect in the USTR Reading Room:
(a) Any written petition, comments, or other submission of information made pursuant to this part; and
(b) Any stenographic record of any public hearings held pursuant to this part.
