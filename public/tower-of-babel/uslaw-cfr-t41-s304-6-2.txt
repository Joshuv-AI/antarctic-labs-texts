41 C.F.R. § 304-6.2 — -6.2 Partial payment handling.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-6.2
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-6.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-6 (6—Payment Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a non-Federal source provides partial payment for travel expenses, the agency should specify on the travel authorization that the employee will be reimbursed for the difference between the full allowances and the payment from the non-Federal source.
