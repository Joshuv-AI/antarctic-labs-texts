10 U.S.C. § 1214 — Right to full and fair hearing
Citation: us:usc:10:1214
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1214
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 61 (Retirement Or Separation For Physical Disability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No member of the armed forces may be retired or separated for physical disability without a full and fair hearing if the member demands it. The Secretary concerned may require submission of a statement of contention as part of the demand.
