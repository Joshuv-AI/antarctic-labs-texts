49 C.F.R. § 556.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:556.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-556.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 556 (Exemption For Inconsequential Defect Or Noncompliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth procedures, pursuant to section 157 of the Act, for exempting manufacturers of motor vehicles and replacement equipment from the Act's notice and remedy requirements when a defect or noncompliance is determined to be inconsequential as it relates to motor vehicle safety.
