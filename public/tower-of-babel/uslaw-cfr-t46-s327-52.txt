46 C.F.R. § 327.52 — Notice of claim acceptance or denial.
Citation: us:cfr:46:327.52
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MARAD will give prompt notice in writing of the acceptance or denial of each claim in whole or in part, by mail to the last known address of, or by personal delivery to, the claimant or the claimant's legal representative. In the case of denial, such notice will contain a brief statement of the reason for such a denial.
