27 C.F.R. § 21.118 — Methyl n-butyl ketone.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.118
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Acidity (as acetic acid). 0.02 percent by weight, maximum.
(b) Color. Colorless.
(c) Odor. Characteristic odor.
(d) Refractive index at 20 °C. 1.396 to 1.404.
(e) Specific gravity at 20 °/20 °C. 0.800 to 0.835.
(f) Distillation range. No distillate should come over below 123 °C. and none above 129 °C.
