42 U.S.C. § 300ff–37 — State HIV testing programs established prior to or after May 20, 1996
Citation: us:usc:42:300ff–37
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300ff–37
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXIV (Hiv Health Care Services Program); Part B (Care Grant Program); Subpart ii (Provisions Concerning Pregnancy And Perinatal Transmission Of Hiv). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to disqualify a State from receiving grants under this subchapter if such State has established at any time prior to or after May 20, 1996, a program of mandatory HIV testing.
