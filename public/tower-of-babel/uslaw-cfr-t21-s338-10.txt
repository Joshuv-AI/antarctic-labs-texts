21 C.F.R. § 338.10 — Nighttime sleep-aid active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:338.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-338.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 338 (Nighttime Sleep-Aid Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any of the following when used within the dosage limits established for each ingredient in § 338.50(d):
(a) Diphenhydramine hydrochloride.
(b) Diphenhydramine citrate.
