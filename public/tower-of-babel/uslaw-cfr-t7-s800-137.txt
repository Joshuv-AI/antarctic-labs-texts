7 C.F.R. § 800.137 — Who shall perform appeal inspection services.
Citation: us:cfr:7:800.137
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-800.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 800 (General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Appeal. Appeal inspection services shall be performed by the field office responsible for the area in which the original inspection was performed.
(b) Board appeal. Board appeal inspection services shall be performed only by the Board of Appeals and Review. The field office that performed the appeal inspection service will act as a liaison between the Board of Appeals and Review and the applicant.
