34 C.F.R. § 300.112 — Individualized education programs (IEP).
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.112
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must ensure that an IEP, or an IFSP that meets the requirements of section 636(d) of the Act, is developed, reviewed, and revised for each child with a disability in accordance with §§ 300.320 through 300.324, except as provided in § 300.300(b)(3)(ii).
