11 C.F.R. § 102.15 — Commingled funds (52 U.S.C. 30102(b)(3)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:102.15
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-102.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 102 (Registration, Organization, And Recordkeeping By Political Committees (52 U.S.C. 30103)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All funds of a political committee shall be segregated from, and may not be commingled with, any personal funds of officers, members or associates of that committee, or with the personal funds of any other individual. See also 11 CFR 103.3 and part 114 and 52 U.S.C. 30118.
