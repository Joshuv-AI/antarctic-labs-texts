FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the 
agreement to the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, 
Federal Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 
20573. Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received 
within 12 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
and the Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited review. Copies of agreements are 
available through the Commission's website (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of General Counsel at (202) 523-5740 or 
[email protected].

    Agreement No.: 201159-003.
    Agreement Name: Memorandum of Settlement of Local Conditions in the 
Port of New York and New Jersey.
    Parties: International Longshoremen's Association, AFL-CIO; and 
Shipping Association of New York and New Jersey.
    Filing Party: Richard Ciampi, The Lambos Firm, LLP.
    Synopsis: The Amendment establishes local conditions for the Port 
of New York--New Jersey covering the period from October 1, 2024, 
through September 30, 2030.
    Proposed Effective Date: 10/21/2026.

    Agreement No.: 201446-002.
    Agreement Name: CMA CGM/COSCO North Europe--USEC/USWC Service Space 
Charter Agreement.
    Parties: CMA CGM S.A.; and COSCO SHIPPING LINES Co. Ltd.
    Filing Party: Draughn Arbona, CMA CGM (America) LLC.
    Synopsis: The Amendment would revise the allocation chartered by 
CMA CGM to COSCO to 250 TEUs per vessel sailing.
    Proposed Effective Date: 12/6/2026.

    Agreement No.: 201464-002.
    Agreement Name: CMA CGM/Neoline North Europe--USEC Service Space 
Charter Agreement.
    Parties: CMA CGM S.A.; and Neoline Armateur.
    Filing Party: Draughn Arbona, CMA CGM (America) LLC.
    Synopsis: The Amendment would revise the geographic scope of the 
Agreement by adding Spain, Portugal and Germany.
    Proposed Effective Date: 12/5/2026.


    Dated: October 23, 2026
Jennifer Everling,
Assistant Secretary.
