25 U.S.C. § 4168 — Public availability of information
Citation: us:usc:25:4168
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4168
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter IV (Compliance, Audits, And Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each recipient shall make any housing plan, policy, or annual report prepared by the recipient available to the general public.
