27 C.F.R. § 771.111 — Proceedings involving violations not within the division of issuance of license or permit.
Citation: us:cfr:27:771.111
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-771.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 771 (Rules Of Practice In Explosive License And Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event violations occurred at a place not within the field division where the licensee or permittee is located, the Director of Industry Operations of the field division where the licensee or permittee is located will take jurisdiction over any proceeding and will take appropriate action in accordance with this subpart, including issuing the relevant notice.
