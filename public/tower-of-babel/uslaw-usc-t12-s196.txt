12 U.S.C. § 196 — Expenses
Citation: us:usc:12:196
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section196
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XIII (Receivership). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All expenses of any preliminary or other examinations into the condition of any association shall be paid by such association. All expenses of any receivership shall be paid out of the assets of such association before distribution of the proceeds thereof.
