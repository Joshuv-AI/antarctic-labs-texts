42 U.S.C. § 10270 — Termination of Board
Citation: us:usc:42:10270
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10270
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter V (Nuclear Waste Technical Review Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall cease to exist not later than 1 year after the date on which the Secretary begins disposal of high-level radioactive waste or spent nuclear fuel in a repository.
