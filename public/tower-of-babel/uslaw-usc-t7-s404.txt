7 U.S.C. § 404 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section404
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 16 (Bureau Of Dairy Industry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this chapter and the activities of the Bureau of Dairy Industry, such sums of money as Congress may deem necessary are authorized to be appropriated.
