2 U.S.C. § 1537 — Pilot program on small government flexibility
Citation: us:usc:2:1537
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1537
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 25 (Unfunded Mandates Reform); Subchapter II (Regulatory Accountability And Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Director of the Office of Management and Budget, in consultation with Federal agencies, shall establish pilot programs in at least 2 agencies to test innovative, and more flexible regulatory approaches that—
(1)
reduce reporting and compliance burdens on small governments; and
(2)
meet overall statutory goals and objectives.
(b) Program focus
The pilot programs shall focus on rules in effect or proposed rules, or a combination thereof.
