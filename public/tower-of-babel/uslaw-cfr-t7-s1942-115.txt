7 C.F.R. § 1942.115 — Reasonable project costs.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1942.115
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1942.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1942 (Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants are responsible for determining that prices paid for property rights, construction, equipment, and other project development are reasonable and fair. RD may require an appraisal by an independent appraiser or Agency employee.
