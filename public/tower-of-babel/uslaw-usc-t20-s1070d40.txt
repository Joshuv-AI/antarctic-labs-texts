20 U.S.C. § 1070d–40 — Construction of needs provisions
Citation: us:usc:20:1070d–40
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1070d–40
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part A (Grants To Students In Attendance At Institutions Of Higher Education); Subpart 6 (Robert C. Byrd Honors Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 1087kk of this title, nothing in this subpart, or any other Act, shall be construed to permit the receipt of a scholarship under this subpart to be counted for any needs test in connection with the awarding of any grant or the making of any loan under this chapter or any other provision of Federal law relating to educational assistance.
