7 C.F.R. § 53.17 — Advance information concerning service rendered.
Citation: us:cfr:7:53.17
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-53.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 53 (Livestock (Grading, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of any applicant, all or any part of the contents of any certificate issued to him under the regulations, or other notification concerning the determination of class, grade, other quality, or compliance of livestock for such applicant may be transmitted by telegraph or telephone to him, or to any person designated by him, at his expense.
