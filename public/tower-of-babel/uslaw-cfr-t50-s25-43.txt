50 C.F.R. § 25.43 — Who may terminate or revoke a permit and why?
Citation: us:cfr:50:25.43
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-25.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 25 (Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The refuge manager may terminate or revoke a permit at any time for noncompliance with the terms of the permit or of the regulations in this subchapter C; for nonuse; for violation of any law, regulation, or order applicable to the refuge; or to protect public health or safety or the resources of a national wildlife refuge.
