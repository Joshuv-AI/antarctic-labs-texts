7 C.F.R. § 762.159 — Pledging of guarantee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:762.159
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-762.159
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 762 (Guaranteed Farm Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lender may pledge all or part of the guaranteed or unguaranteed portion of the loan as security to a Federal Home Loan Bank, a Federal Reserve Bank, a Farm Credit System Bank, or any other funding source determined acceptable by the Agency.
