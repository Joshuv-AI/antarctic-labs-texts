48 C.F.R. § 533.211 — 533.211 Contracting officer's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:533.211
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-533.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 533 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer's written decision must include the paragraph at FAR 33.211(a)(4)(v). The contracting officer shall state in the decision that a contractor's notice of appeal to the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals (CBCA) should include a copy of the contracting officer's decision.
