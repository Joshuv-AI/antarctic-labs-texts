20 C.F.R. § 211.8 — Displacement allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:20:211.8
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-211.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 211 (Creditable Railroad Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An allowance paid to an employee because he has been displaced to a lower paying position is creditable compensation.
