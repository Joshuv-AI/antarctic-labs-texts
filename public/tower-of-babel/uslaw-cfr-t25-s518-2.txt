25 C.F.R. § 518.2 — Who will administer the self-regulation program for the Commission?
Citation: us:cfr:25:518.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-518.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 518 (Self-Regulation Of Class Ii Gaming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The self-regulation program will be administered by the Office of Self-Regulation. The Chair shall appoint a Director to administer the Office of Self-Regulation.
