49 U.S.C. § 20147 — Warning of civil liability
Citation: us:usc:49:20147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20147
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 201 (General); Subchapter II (Particular Aspects Of Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall encourage railroad carriers to warn the public about potential liability for violation of regulations related to vandalism of railroad signs, devices, and equipment and to trespassing on railroad property.
