40 C.F.R. § 436.150 — Applicability; description of the sodium sulfate subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:436.150
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-436.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 436 (Mineral Mining And Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to the processing of sodium sulfate. Sodium sulfate obtained from brine lakes is regulated in the salines from brine lakes subcategory (subpart L of this part).
