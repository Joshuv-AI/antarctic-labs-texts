21 C.F.R. § 814.120 — Temporary suspension of approval of an HDE.
Citation: us:cfr:21:814.120
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-814.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 814 (Premarket Approval Of Medical Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An HDE or HDE supplement may be temporarily suspended for the same reasons and in the same manner as prescribed for PMA's in § 814.47.
