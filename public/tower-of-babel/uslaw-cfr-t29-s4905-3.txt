29 C.F.R. § 4905.3 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4905.3
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4905.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4905 (Appearances In Certain Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No PBGC employee or former employee may appear in any proceeding to which this part applies to testify and/or produce documents or other material unless authorized under this part.
