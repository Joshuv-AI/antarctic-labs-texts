45 C.F.R. § 1149.13 — When may the NEA issue a complaint?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.13
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NEA may issue a complaint:
(a) If the Attorney General, or his/her designee, approves the referral of the allegations for adjudication in a written statement; and
(b) In a case of submission of false claims, if the amount of money or the value of property or services demanded or requested in a false claim, or a group of related claims submitted at the same time, does not exceed $150,000.
