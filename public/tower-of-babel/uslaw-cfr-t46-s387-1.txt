46 C.F.R. § 387.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:387.1
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-387.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 387 (Utilization And Disposal Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Development Or Operation Of A Port Facility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is applicable to Surplus Property that is recommended by the Secretary as being needed for the development or operation of a Port Facility and is appropriate for being assigned to, or that has been assigned to the Secretary for conveyance as provided for in Public Law 103-160 and 40 U.S.C. 471 et seq.
