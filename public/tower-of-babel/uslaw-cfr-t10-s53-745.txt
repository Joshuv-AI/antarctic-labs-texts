10 C.F.R. § 53.745 — Operator license requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:53.745
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-53.745
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 53 (Risk-Informed, Technology-Inclusive Regulatory Framework For Commercial Nuclear Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person must be authorized by a license issued by the Commission to perform the function of an operator, senior operator, or generally licensed reactor operator as defined in this part.
