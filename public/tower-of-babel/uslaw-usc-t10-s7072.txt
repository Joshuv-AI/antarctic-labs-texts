10 U.S.C. § 7072 — Judge Advocate General’s Corps
Citation: us:usc:10:7072
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7072
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 707 (The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is a Judge Advocate General’s Corps in the Army. The Judge Advocate General’s Corps consists of—
(1)
the Judge Advocate General;
(2)
the Assistant Judge Advocate General;
(3)
commissioned officers of the Regular Army appointed therein; and
(4)
other members of the Army assigned thereto by the Secretary of the Army.
