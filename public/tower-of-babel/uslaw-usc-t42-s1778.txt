42 U.S.C. § 1778 — Nonprofit programs
Citation: us:usc:42:1778
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1778
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 13A (Child Nutrition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The food and milk service programs in schools and nonprofit institutions receiving assistance under this chapter shall be conducted on a nonprofit basis.
