8 C.F.R. § 231.3 — Exemptions for private vessels and aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:8:231.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-231.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 231 (Arrival And Departure Manifests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provision of this part relating to the presentation of arrival and departure manifests shall not apply to a private vessel or private aircraft. Private aircraft as defined in 19 CFR 122.1(h) are subject to the arrival and departure manifest presentation requirements set forth in 19 CFR 122.22.
