48 C.F.R. § 2801.404-70 — 2801.404-70 Requests for class deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2801.404-70
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2801.404-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2801 (Department Of Justice Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for approval of class deviations from the FAR or the JAR, for any solicitation that will result in multiple awards, shall be forwarded to the SPE. Such requests will be signed by the Bureau Procurement Chief (BPC).
