2 U.S.C. § 140 — Employees; fitness
Citation: us:usc:2:140
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section140
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All persons employed in and about said Library of Congress under the Librarian shall be appointed solely with reference to their fitness for their particular duties.
