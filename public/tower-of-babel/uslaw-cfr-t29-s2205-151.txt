29 C.F.R. § 2205.151 — Program accessibility: new construction and alterations.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2205.151
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2205.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2205 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission And In Accessibility Of Commission Electronic And Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each building or part of a building that is constructed or altered by, on behalf of, or for the use of the agency shall be designed, constructed, or altered so as to be readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities. The definitions, requirements, and standards of the Architectural Barriers Act (42 U.S.C. 4151-4157), as established in 41 CFR 102-76.60 to 102-76.95, apply to buildings covered by this section.
