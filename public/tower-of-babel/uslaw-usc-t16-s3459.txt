16 U.S.C. § 3459 — Supplemental authority of the Secretary
Citation: us:usc:16:3459
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3459
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 54 (Resource Conservation); Subchapter V (Resource Conservation And Development Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the Secretary under this subchapter to assist councils in the development and implementation of area plans shall be supplemental to, and not in lieu of, any authority of the Secretary under any other provision of law.
