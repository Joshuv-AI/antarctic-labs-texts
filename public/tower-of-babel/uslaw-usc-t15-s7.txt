15 U.S.C. § 7 — “Person” or “persons” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 1 (Monopolies And Combinations In Restraint Of Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “person”, or “persons”, wherever used in sections 1 to 7 of this title shall be deemed to include corporations and associations existing under or authorized by the laws of either the United States, the laws of any of the Territories, the laws of any State, or the laws of any foreign country.
