40 C.F.R. § 1060.301 — Manufacturer testing.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1060.301
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1060.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1060 (Control Of Evaporative Emissions From New And In-Use Nonroad And Stationary Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Using good engineering judgment, you must evaluate production samples to verify that equipment or components you produce are as specified in the certificate of conformity. This may involve testing using certification procedures or other measurements.
(b) You must give us records to document your evaluation if we ask for them.
