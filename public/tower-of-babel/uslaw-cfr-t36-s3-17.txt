36 C.F.R. § 3.17 — What regulations apply to swimming areas and beaches?
Citation: us:cfr:36:3.17
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-3.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 3 (Boating And Water Use Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The superintendent may designate areas as swimming areas or swimming beaches in accordance with §§ 1.5 and 1.7 of this chapter.
(b) Within designated swimming areas, the use of a surfboard or similar rigid device is prohibited.
(c) The superintendent may prohibit the use or possession of flotation devices, glass containers, kites, or incompatible activities in swimming areas or swimming beaches in accordance with §§ 1.5 and 1.7 of this chapter.
