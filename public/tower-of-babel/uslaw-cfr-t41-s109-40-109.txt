41 C.F.R. § 109-40.109 — -40.109 Utilization of special contracts and agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-40.109
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-40.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-40 (40—Transportation And Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

From time to time special transportation agreements are entered into on a Government-wide or DOE-wide basis and are applicable, generally, to DOE shipments. The HQ DOE Manager, Transportation Operations and Traffic, will distribute information on such agreements to field offices as it becomes available.
