40 C.F.R. § 425.60 — Applicability; description of the through-the-blue subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:425.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-425.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 425 (Leather Tanning And Finishing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to process wastewater discharges resulting from any tannery which processes raw or cured cattle or cattle-like hides through the blue tanned state by hair pulp unhairing and chrome tanning; no retan-wet finishing is performed.
