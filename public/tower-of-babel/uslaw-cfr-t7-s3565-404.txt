7 C.F.R. § 3565.404 — Transfer of loans or mortgage servicing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3565.404
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3565.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3565 (Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transfer of servicing is prohibited unless the Agency determines that circumstances warrant such action, the proposed lender is an eligible lender approved by the Agency, and the transfer of servicing is approved by the Agency in advance.
