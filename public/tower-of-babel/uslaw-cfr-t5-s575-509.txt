5 C.F.R. § 575.509 — Is an extended assignment incentive considered basic pay for any purpose?
Citation: us:cfr:5:575.509
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-575.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 575 (Recruitment, Relocation, And Retention Incentives; Supervisory Differentials; And Extended Assignment Incentives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, an extended assignment incentive is not considered part of an employee's rate of basic pay for any purpose, nor is it included for the purpose of calculating a lump-sum payment for annual leave under 5 CFR 550.1205.
