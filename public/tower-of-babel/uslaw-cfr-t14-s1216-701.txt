14 C.F.R. § 1216.701 — Filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1216.701
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1216.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1216 (Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NASA will file environmental impact statements together with comments and responses with the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), for publication in the Federal Register.
