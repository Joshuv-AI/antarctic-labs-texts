27 C.F.R. § 11.39 — Seasonal dealers.
Citation: us:cfr:27:11.39
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-11.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 11 (Consignment Sales). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Industry members may accept the return of products from retail dealers who are only open a portion of the year, if the products are likely to spoil during the off season. These returns will be for cash or for credit against outstanding indebtedness.
