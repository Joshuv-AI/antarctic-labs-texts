17 C.F.R. § 230.120 — Inspection of registration statements.
Citation: us:cfr:17:230.120
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-230.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 230 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for material contracts or portions thereof accorded confidential treatment pursuant to § 230.406, all registration statements are available for public inspection, during business hours, at the principal office of the Commission in Washington, D.C. Electronic registration statements made through the Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval system are publicly available through the Commission's Web site (http://www.sec.gov).
