47 C.F.R. § 87.73 — Transmitter adjustments and tests.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.73
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A general radiotelephone operator must directly supervise and be responsible for all transmitter adjustments or tests during installation, servicing or maintenance of a radio station. A general radiotelephone operator must be responsible for the proper functioning of the station equipment.
