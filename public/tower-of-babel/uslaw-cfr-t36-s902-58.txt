36 C.F.R. § 902.58 — Reports of financial institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:36:902.58
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-902.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 902 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any material contained in or related to any examination, operating, or condition report prepared by, on behalf of, or for the use of, any agency responsible for the regulation or supervision of financial institutions is within the statutory exemption.
