The presented article is an announced continuation of the work
[

for quadrinomial

Polynomial
P(z)
with real coefficients is called reciprocal if
P(z)=\pm z^{N}P(\frac{1}{z})
(additionally called symmetric or antisymmetric depending on the sign). The reciprocal polynomial has zeros located symmetrically with respect to the unit circle, i.e., if
z_{0}
is a nontrivial root of a reciprocal polynomial, then
\frac{1}{z_{0}}
is also a root of this polynomial. In particular, if
z_{0}
lies on the unit circle then
\frac{1}{z_{0}}
also lies on the unit circle.

A significant number of works are devoted to polynomials whose zeros all lie on the unit circle–see, for example,
[
7
,
3
,
8
,
9
,
10
]
where the importance of studying the properties of such polynomials is noted.

The classical criterion of A. Cohn for the membership of all zeros of a polynomial on the unit circle is well known.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
4
]
. All zeros of the polynomial
\displaystyle P(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{N}a_{j}z^{N-j}
with
a_{0}\neq 0
lie on the unit circle if and only if the polynomial
P(z)
is reciprocal and all zeros of the polynomial
P^{\prime}(z)
belong to the closed central unit disc
\overline{\mathbb{D}}=\{z:\left|z\right|\leq 1\}
.

Notice that polynomials
p(z)
and
q(z)
are reciprocal (
p(z)
is symmetric,
q(z)
is antisymmetric) and all their zeros lie on the unit circle. In the extreme cases
\left(\left|\kappa\right|=\frac{N}{N-2},\ \left|\kappa\right|=1\right)
these polynomials are related by simple relations with the Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind
U_{N}(x)
and their first derivatives through their zeros
[
5
]
.

In this paper, we consider a more general case of a reciprocal antisymmetric polynomial of the form
\displaystyle P(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\gamma_{j}\left(z^{j}+z^{N+s+1-j}\right)
with
(\gamma_{0}=1)
. The case of a symmetric polynomial can be considered similarly.

The purpose of this article is:

•

obtaining the necessary conditions under which all zeros of the polynomial
P(z)
lie on the unit circle, in the form of estimates for the coefficients
\gamma_{j}\ (j=1,\ldots,s)
;

•

confirmation of the unimprovability of these estimates in the general case in the form of constructing extremal polynomials;

•

factorization of extremal polynomials through binomials with zeros on the unit circle, which are associated with derivatives of order
s\ (0\leq s\leq N)
of polynomials
U_{N}(x)
.

The obtained extremal properties of reciprocal polynomials are apparently new compared to the known ones (see, for example,
[
2
,
14
,
13
]
).

As a consequence, we additionally obtain formulas representing the derivatives of Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind through linear combinations of the Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind. In
[
15
,
12
,
1
]
it is noted that the exploration of such internal connections between Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind and their derivatives is an important and interesting task, and moreover, in the opinion of the authors, the results in this direction are clearly insufficient.

2
Auxiliary results

For every natural number
N\geq 0
the family of Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind
U_{N}(x)
are defined as follows (see, for example,
[
15
]
):

U_{N}(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{\left[\frac{N}{2}\right]}(-1)^{j}{N-j\choose j}(2z)^{N-2j},

(1)

where we define

\left[\frac{N}{2}\right]=\begin{cases}\frac{N}{2},&\mbox{ if }N\mbox{ is even}\\
\frac{N-1}{2},&\mbox{ if }N\mbox{ is odd}\end{cases}.

Let us note that
\frac{(N-j)!}{j!(N-2j)!}={N-j\choose j}
. For
N<0
the family of Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind can be defined by the formula
U_{-N}(z)=-U_{N-2}(z)
.

Formula (
1
) allows us to define polynomials
U_{N}(z)
for all complex numbers
z
. We obtain explicit formulas for derivatives of order
s

\left(0\leq s\leq N\right)
the Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind:

U_{N}^{(s)}(z)=s!\sum_{j=0}^{\left[\frac{N-s}{2}\right]}(-1)^{j}{N-j\choose j}{N-2j\choose s}2^{N-2j}z^{N-2j-s}.

(2)

Another explicit formula for the derivatives
U_{N}^{(s)}(z)
was obtained on the basis of classical formulas of combinatorics in
[
12
]
:

U_{N}^{(s)}(z)=\frac{2^{s}}{(s-1)!}\sum_{j=0}^{\left[\frac{N-s}{2}\right]}\frac{(N-j)!(j+s-1)!}{j!(N-j-s+1)!}(N-s-2j+1)U_{N-s-2j}(z).

(3)

Next, let’s consider the polynomial
\displaystyle z^{\frac{N-s}{2}}U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)
. In the case when
s=N
notice that
\displaystyle z^{\frac{N-s}{2}}U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)=2^{N}N!
, as can be easily verified from (
2
). Thus, we turn our attention to the case when
s<N
.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\displaystyle\left\{\nu_{j}^{(s)}\right\}_{j=1}^{\left[\frac{N-s}{2}\right]}
be the set of positive zeros of
U_{N}^{(s)}(z)
. Then, we have

\displaystyle z^{\frac{N-s}{2}}U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{N!2^{s}}{(N-s)!}\begin{cases}(1+z)\prod_{j=1}^{\frac{N-s-1}{2}}\left[z^{2}+1+2z\left(1-2\left(\nu_{j}^{(s)}\right)^{2}\right)\right],&N-s\mbox{ is odd}\\
\prod_{j=1}^{\frac{N-s}{2}}\left[z^{2}+1+2z\left(1-2\left(\nu_{j}^{(s)}\right)^{2}\right)\right],&N-s\mbox{ is even}\end{cases}

Proof.

Let
N-s
be an even number. Since

\displaystyle U_{N}^{(s)}(z)=\frac{N!2^{N}}{(N-s)!}z^{N-s}+\cdots

we have that

\displaystyle U_{N}^{(s)}(z)=\frac{N!}{(N-s)!}\prod_{j=1}^{\frac{N-s}{2}}\left[z^{2}-\left(\nu_{j}^{(s)}\right)^{2}\right]
.
Substituting

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)

in place of
z
we obtain the desired formula after multiplying by
z^{\frac{N-s}{2}}
. The case when the number
N-s
is odd is shown similarly. The lemma is proved.
∎

From Lemma
2.1
it follows that the polynomial

\displaystyle z^{\frac{N-s}{2}}U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)
has degree
N-s
, is reciprocal, and all its zeros lie on the circle. Indeed,
\left(\nu_{j}^{(s)}\right)^{2}\leq 1,
therefore
|1-2\left(\nu_{j}^{(s)}\right)^{2}|\leq 1,
while the polynomial
z^{2}+2bz+1
has all its zeros on the circle if
|b|\leq 1
.

Lemma 2.2
.

The following formulas hold:

\displaystyle z^{\frac{N}{2}}U_{N}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1+z+z^{2}+\cdots+z^{N}

\displaystyle z^{\frac{N-1}{2}}U_{N}^{\prime}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(j+1)(N-j)z^{j}.

Proof.

By definition, for any whole
N\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle U_{N}\left(\cos\left(\frac{t}{2}\right)\right)=\frac{\sin\left(\frac{(N+1)t}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{t}{2}\right)}
where

U_{N}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(e^{i\frac{t}{2}}+e^{-i\frac{t}{2}}\right)\right)=\frac{e^{i\frac{(N+1)t}{2}}-e^{-i\frac{(N+1)t}{2}}}{e^{i\frac{t}{2}}+e^{-i\frac{t}{2}}}=\frac{e^{-i\frac{(N+1)t}{2}}\left(e^{i(N+1)t}-1\right)}{e^{-i\frac{t}{2}}\left(e^{it}-1\right)}=e^{-i\frac{Nt}{2}}\frac{1-e^{i(N+1)t}}{1-e^{it}}.

Thus, we have the following formula

U_{N}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)=z^{-\frac{N}{2}}\frac{1-z^{N+1}}{1-z}=z^{-\frac{N}{2}}\left(1+z+z^{2}+\cdots+z^{N}\right).

holds for all
z\neq 1
on the unit circle. Therefore, the two polynomials are equal for all
z\notin\{0,1\}
.

From this, we obtain the following

\displaystyle U_{N}^{\prime}(x)\Big|_{x=\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dz}\left(z^{-\frac{N}{2}}\frac{1-z^{N+1}}{1-z}\right)\frac{1}{\frac{d}{dz}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2z^{-\frac{N-1}{2}}\frac{N(1-z^{N+2})-(N+2)\left(z-z^{N+1}\right)}{(1-z)^{3}},

where

U_{N}^{\prime}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)=2z^{-\frac{N-1}{2}}\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(j+1)(N-j)z^{j}
.
The lemma is proved.
∎

Main Results

Theorem 3.1
.

The following formula holds.

z^{\frac{N-s}{2}}U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)={2^{s}}{s!}\sum_{j=0}^{N-s}{N-j\choose s}{s+j\choose s}z^{j}.

(4)

Proof.

For
s=0,1
, formula (
4
) is true (Lemma
2.2
). We proceed by induction. Let us denote

A_{N,s,j}=(s!)^{2}{N-j\choose s}{s+j\choose s}=\frac{(N-j)!(j+s)!}{j!(N-j-s)!}.

Hence,

A_{N,s+1,j}=A_{N,s,j}(N-j-s)(j+s+1)=A_{N,s,j+1}(j+1)(N-j).

(5)

Further, let us note that

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dz}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)=\frac{1}{4}z^{-\frac{3}{2}}(z-1)
. Let (
4
) be satisfied for some
s

\left(1\leq s<N\right)
. Then, for
s+1
:

\displaystyle U_{N}^{(s+1)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{d}{dz}\left(U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)\right)\right)\cdot\frac{1}{\frac{d}{dz}\left[\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right]}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2^{s}}{s!}\left(\frac{d}{dz}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{N-s}A_{N,s,j}\right)z^{j-\frac{N-s}{2}}\right)\frac{4z^{\frac{3}{2}}}{z-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2^{s}}{s!}\frac{4z^{\frac{3}{2}}}{z-1}\sum_{j=0}^{N-s}A_{N,s,j}\left(j-\frac{N-s}{2}\right)z^{j-\frac{N-s}{2}-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2^{s+1}}{(s+1)!}\frac{z^{-\frac{N-s-1}{2}}}{z-1}\sum_{j=0}^{N-s}A_{N,s,j}(2j-N+s)(s+1)z^{j}.

It is necessary to check the identity

(z-1)\sum_{j=0}^{N-s-1}A_{N,s+1,j}z^{j}\equiv\sum_{j=0}^{N-s}A_{N,s,j}(2j-N+s)(s+1)z^{j}.

To do this, we compare coefficients on powers of
z
. Using (
5
) yields the following for powers of
z
:

•

z^{0}
:
-A_{N,s+1,0}\equiv A_{N,s,0}(-N+s)(s+1)
;

•

z^{N-s}
:
A_{N,s+1,N-s-1}\equiv A_{N,s,N-s}(N-s)(s+1)
;

•

z^{j}

\left(j=1,\ldots,N-s-1\right)
:

\displaystyle A_{N,s+1,j-1}-A_{N,s+1,j}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle A_{N,s,j}(j(N-j+1)-(j+s+1)(N-j-s))

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle A_{N,s,j}(2j-N+s)(s+1).

By Strong Induction, the theorem is proved.
∎

\displaystyle\frac{s!s!(N+s+1)!}{(N-s)!}\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\frac{(N-j)!}{j!(s-j)!(N+s+1-j)!}\left(z^{j}-z^{N+s+1-j}\right).

, where

is the coefficient on

Note that

A_{p}=(-1)^{p}\frac{N!s!(2s+1)!}{p!(N-s)!(2s+1-p)!}\cdot\phantom{}_{3}{F}_{2}\left(\left[-p,s+1,-N+s\right];\left[-N,2s+2-p\right];1\right),

where

is a generalized hypergeometric function in

,

Here

4
Extremal properties of polynomials with zeros on the unit circle

Let us consider an antisymmetric reciprocal polynomial of the form

P(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\gamma_{j}\left(z^{j}-z^{N+s+1-j}\right),

(12)

where
\gamma_{0}=1
for
0\leq s\leq N
. Obviously,
P(1)=0
,
P(-1)=0
, where
N-s
is odd.

Lemma 4.1
.

If the inequality
\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{s}\left|\gamma_{j}\right|\leq 1
is satisfied, then all of the zeros of the polynomial
P(z)
are located on the unit circle.

Proof.

Let’s define the derivative

P^{\prime}(z)=-(N+s+1)z^{N+s}+\sum_{j=1}^{s}(-1)^{j}\gamma_{j}\left(jz^{j-1}-(N+s+1-j)z^{N+s-j}\right)

If the sum of the absolute values of the coefficients of the polynomial
\displaystyle\frac{-1}{N+s+1}P^{\prime}(z)
(excluding the coefficient for the highest power of
z
) does not exceed 1, Rouché’s Theorem tells us that all zeros of this polynomial belong to the closed central unit disc
\overline{\mathbb{D}}=\left\{z\ :\ \left|z\right|\leq 1\right\}
. Therefore, by Theorem
1.1
, all of the zeros of the polynomial
P(z)
are located on the unit circle. The lemma is proved.
∎

Theorem 4.2
.

If all zeros of a polynomial
P(z)
are located on the unit circle, then

\left|\gamma_{j}\right|\leq{s\choose j}{N+s+1\choose j}\left({N\choose j}\right)^{-1},\ j=1,\ldots,s.

(13)

Moreover, these estimates cannot be improved in general.

Proof.

Let us define the derivative of order
s+1

\displaystyle P^{(s+1)}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(-\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\gamma_{j}z^{N+s+1-j}\right)^{(s+1)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{(N+s+1)!}{N!}z^{N-s}\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\gamma_{j}\frac{N!}{(N+s+1)!}\frac{(N+s+1-j)!}{(N-j)!}z^{s-j}.

If all zeros of a polynomial
P(z)
are located on the unit circle, then all of the zeros of the polynomial
P^{\prime}(z)
lie inside the disc
\overline{\mathbb{D}}
. Since the zeros of the derivative of a polynomial lie inside the closed convex hull of the zeros of this polynomial, then all the zeros of the polynomial
P^{(s+1)}(z)
must lie inside the disc
\overline{\mathbb{D}}
. From Vieta’s theorem it follows that the coefficients of the polynomial
\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\gamma_{j}\frac{N!}{(N+s+1)!}\frac{(N+s+1-j)!}{(N-j)!}z^{s-j}
must satisfy the constraints:

\displaystyle|\gamma_{j}|\frac{N!}{(N+s+1)!}\frac{(N+s+1-j)!}{(N-j)!}\leq{s\choose s-j}
for
j=1,\ldots,s
, which are equivalent to conditions (
13
). Generally speaking, it is impossible to reduce the validity of even one of the inequalities (
13
), as shown by the example from the Corollary
3.3
.
The theorem is proved.
∎

Thus, the set in the space of coefficients of the polynomial (
12
), for which all its zeros lie on the unit circle, is contained in the parallelepiped

\hat{\Pi}=\left\{\gamma_{1},\ldots,\gamma_{s}\ :\ \left|\gamma_{j}\right|\leq{s\choose j}{N+s+1\choose j}\left({N\choose j}\right)^{-1},\ j=1,\ldots,s\right\}

and contains a parallelepiped (cube)

\tilde{\Pi}=\left\{\gamma_{1},\ldots,\gamma_{s}\ :\ \sum_{j=1}^{s}\left|\gamma_{j}\right|\leq 1\right\}.

Moreover, without additional assumptions on the coefficients, neither the sides of the parallelepiped
\hat{\Pi}
nor the edges of the cube
\tilde{\Pi}
can be reduced or increased. For the latter case, an example would be a polynomial
P(z)=\left(1-z^{N+3}\right)+\gamma_{2}\left(z^{2}-z^{N+1}\right)
. Then,
\gamma_{2}\leq 1
for odd
N
and
\gamma_{2}\geq-1
for even
N

[
5
]
.

Formula (

Theorem 6.1
.

The representation below is valid:

\displaystyle U_{N}^{(s)}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-1)^{s}2^{-s}\frac{s!(N+s+1)!}{(N-s)!}\frac{1}{(1-z^{2})^{s}}

(14)

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\frac{(N-j)!}{j!(s-j)!(N+s+1-j)!}U_{N+s-2j}(z).

Proof.

We write formula (
9
) in the form

\displaystyle U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2^{s}\frac{s!(N+s+1)!}{(N-s)!}\frac{1}{(1-z)^{2s+1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\frac{(N-j)!}{j!(s-j)!(N+s+1-j)!}\left(z^{j-\frac{N}{2}+\frac{s}{2}}-z^{\frac{N}{2}+\frac{3s}{2}+1-j}\right).

Notice that

\displaystyle(1-z)^{2s+1}=z^{s+\frac{1}{2}}\left(z^{-\frac{1}{2}}-z^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2s+1}
. Then

\displaystyle U_{N}^{(s)}\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(z^{\frac{1}{2}}+z^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2^{s}\frac{s!(N+s+1)!}{(N-s)!}\frac{1}{\left(z^{-\frac{1}{2}}-z^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2s}}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}\frac{(N-j)!}{j!(s-j)!(N+s+1-j)!}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\left(\frac{z^{j-\frac{N}{2}-\frac{s}{2}-\frac{1}{2}}-z^{\frac{N}{2}+\frac{s}{2}+\frac{1}{2}-j}}{z^{-\frac{1}{2}}-z^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right).

Make the substitution
