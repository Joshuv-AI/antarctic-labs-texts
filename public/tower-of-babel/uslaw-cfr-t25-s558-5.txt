25 C.F.R. § 558.5 — Submission of notices.
Citation: us:cfr:25:558.5
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-558.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 558 (Gaming Licenses For Key Employees And Primary Management Officials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All notices under this part shall be provided to the Commission through the appropriate Regional office.
(b) Should a tribe wish to submit notices electronically, it should contact the appropriate Regional office for guidance on acceptable document formats and means of transmission.
