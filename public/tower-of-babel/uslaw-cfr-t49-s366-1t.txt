49 C.F.R. § 366.1T — T Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:366.1T
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-366.1T
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 366 (Designation Of Process Agent). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These rules, relating to the filing of designations of persons upon whom court process may be served, govern motor carriers and brokers and, as of the moment of succession, their fiduciaries (as defined at 49 CFR 387.319(a)).
