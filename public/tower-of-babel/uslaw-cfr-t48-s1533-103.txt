48 C.F.R. § 1533.103 — 1533.103 Protests to the Agency.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1533.103
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1533.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1533 (Protests, Disputes And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Protests to the Agency are processed pursuant to the requirements of FAR 33.103. Contracting Officers must include in every solicitation the provision at 1552.233-70, Notice of Filing Requirements for Agency Protests.
