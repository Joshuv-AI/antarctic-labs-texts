36 C.F.R. § 1202.8 — How does NARA handle records that are in Government-wide Privacy Act systems?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1202.8
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1202.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1202 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records in the custody of NARA in a Government-wide Privacy Act system are the primary responsibility of another agency, e.g., the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) or the Office of Government Ethics (OGE). These records are governed by the regulations established by that agency pursuant to the Privacy Act. NARA provides access using that agency's regulations.
