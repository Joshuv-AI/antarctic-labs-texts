14 U.S.C. § 105 — Secretary defined
Citation: us:usc:14:105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section105
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 1 (Establishment And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the respective department in which the Coast Guard is operating.
