27 C.F.R. § 29.51 — Failure to give notice; penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:27:29.51
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-29.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 29 (Stills And Miscellaneous Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to give notice of manufacture of still or notice of setup of still when required to do so is punishable by a fine of not more than $1,000 or imprisonment for not more than one year, or both, and any still, boiler (double or pot still), condenser, or other distilling apparatus to be used for the purpose of distilling which is removed or set up without the required notice having been given is forfeitable to the Government.
