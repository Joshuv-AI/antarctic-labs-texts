43 C.F.R. § 16.5 — Bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:43:16.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-16.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 16 (Conservation Of Helium). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant shall be required to submit a bond in such amount and in such form as the Secretary may prescribe to secure the faithful performance of the terms of any agreement made.
