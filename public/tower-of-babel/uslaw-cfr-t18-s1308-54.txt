18 C.F.R. § 1308.54 — Requests to quash or modify.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1308.54
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1308.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1308 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The person served with a subpoena (or a party, if the person served is a party's employee) may request the Hearing Officer to quash or modify a subpoena. Such requests shall be made and determined in accordance with the time limits and principles of Rule 45(a), (b) and (d) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
