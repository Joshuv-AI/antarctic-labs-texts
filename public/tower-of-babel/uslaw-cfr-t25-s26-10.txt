25 C.F.R. § 26.10 — When will I find out if I have been selected for Job Placement and Training assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your servicing office will notify you in writing within 30 calendar days once it receives a completed job training application request; or
(b) Your servicing office will notify you within 5 business days once it has received a completed Job Placement application and written job offer.
