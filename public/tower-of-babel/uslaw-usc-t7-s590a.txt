7 U.S.C. § 590a — Estimates of apple production
Citation: us:usc:7:590a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section590a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 25 (Export Standards For Apples). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 18, 1986, no funds available to the Department of Agriculture shall be available to publish estimates of apple production for other than the commercial crop.
