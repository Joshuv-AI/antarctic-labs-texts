14 C.F.R. § 101.7 — Hazardous operations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:101.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-101.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 101 (Moored Balloons, Kites, Amateur Rockets, And Unmanned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may operate any moored balloon, kite, amateur rocket, or unmanned free balloon in a manner that creates a hazard to other persons, or their property.
(b) No person operating any moored balloon, kite, amateur rocket, or unmanned free balloon may allow an object to be dropped therefrom, if such action creates a hazard to other persons or their property.
