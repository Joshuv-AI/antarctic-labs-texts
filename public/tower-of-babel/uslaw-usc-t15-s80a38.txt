15 U.S.C. § 80a–38 — Procedure for issuance of rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:15:80a–38
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80a–38
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter I (Investment Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the provisions of chapter 15 of title 44 and regulations prescribed under the authority thereof, the rules and regulations of the Commission under this subchapter, and amendments thereof, shall be effective upon publication in the manner which the Commission shall prescribe, or upon such later date as may be provided in such rules and regulations.
