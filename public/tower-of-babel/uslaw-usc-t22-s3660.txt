22 U.S.C. § 3660 — Review and adjustment of classifications, grades, and pay level
Citation: us:usc:22:3660
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3660
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 2 (Employees); Subpart ii (Wage And Employment Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An employee may request at any time that the employee’s agency—
(1)
review the classification of the employee’s position or the grade or pay level for the employee’s position, or both; and
(2)
revise or adjust that classification, grade or pay level, or both, as the case may be.
The request for review and revision or adjustment shall be submitted and adjudicated in accordance with the regularly established appeals procedures of the agency.
