5 C.F.R. § 178.103 — Claim filed by a claimant's representative.
Citation: us:cfr:5:178.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-178.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 178 (Procedures For Settling Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim filed by a claimant's representative must be supported by a duly executed power of attorney or other documentary evidence of the representative's right to act for the claimant.
