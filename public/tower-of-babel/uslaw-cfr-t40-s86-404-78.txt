40 C.F.R. § 86.404-78 — -78 Section numbering.
Citation: us:cfr:40:86.404-78
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-86.404-78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 86 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Highway Vehicles And Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The year of initial applicability of a section is indicated by its section number. The two digits following the hyphen designate the first model year for which a section is effective. A section remains effective until superseded.
(b) A reference to a section without a year designation implies the appropriate model year.
