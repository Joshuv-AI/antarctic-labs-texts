A short note on a definite integral of a general form
Robert Reynolds

Abstract
. In this note we derive some interesting definite integrals involving Malmsten logarithm forms, reciprocal logarithm forms and Kölbig type integrals in terms of special functions.

4.
Main theorem and evaluations

In this section we evaluate the main theorem and analyze special cases of known integral forms.

Theorem 4.1
.

(4.1)

\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{m-1}\log^{k}(ax)}{(b+x)(x+\gamma)^{n}}\,dx\\
=b^{m-1}(-1)^{m+1}(2i\pi)^{k+1}(\gamma-b)^{-n}\Phi\left(e^{2im\pi},-k,-\frac{i(i\pi+\log(a)+\log(b))}{2\pi}\right)\\
+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\sum_{p=0}^{j}\sum_{l=0}^{p}\sum_{h=0}^{p-l}\frac{(-1)^{j-l}(-1)^{m}m^{p-l-h}(2i\pi)^{1-h+k}\gamma^{m-1}(\gamma-b)^{-n}\left(\frac{\gamma-b}{\gamma}\right)^{j}\binom{p}{l}}{j!}\\
\times\binom{-l+p}{h}(1-h+k)_{h}\Phi\left(e^{2im\pi},h-k,-\frac{i(i\pi+\log(a)+\log(\gamma))}{2\pi}\right)S_{j}^{(p)}

where
0<Re(m)<1,0<Im(m)<1
.

Proof.

This formula is achieved by observing the right-hand side of equation (
3.1
) is equal to the addition of the right-hand sides of equations (
3.2

3.3
) relative to equation (
1.2
). The Pochhammer symbol is applied to simplify the right-hand side.
