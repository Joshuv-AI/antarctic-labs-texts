Correction: Frontline daratumumab, lenalidomide, and dexamethasone in patients older than 80 years with newly diagnosed multiple myeloma: treatment durability, infectious complications, and nutritional vulnerability
Authors: Yuichi Horigome, Koichi Ehata, Noriyuki Tadera, Kei Hayama, Hirotoshi Kamata, Takahiro Suzuki, Koji Miyazaki
Published: 2026

Correction: Blood Res 61, 47 (2026)

https://doi.org/10.1007/s44313-026-00165-y

Following publication of this article [1], an error was identified in the Materials and Methods section regarding the expansion of the abbreviation “IgRT”.

The published text reads:

“Image-guided radiation therapy (IgRT) was not routinely administered but was considered at the discretion of the treating physician for patients with severe hypogammaglobulinemia and recurrent infections.”

The correct text should read:

“Immunoglobulin replacement therapy (IgRT) was not routinely administered but was considered at the discretion of the treating physician for patients with severe hypogammaglobulinemia and recurrent infections.”

The authors would like to apologize for any inconvenience caused to the journal and its readers.
