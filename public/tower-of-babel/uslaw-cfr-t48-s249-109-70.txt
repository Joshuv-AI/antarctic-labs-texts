48 C.F.R. § 249.109-70 — 249.109-70 Limitation on pricing of the terminated effort.
Citation: us:cfr:48:249.109-70
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-249.109-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 249 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When there is a termination for convenience (partial or whole) or a change that reduces scope, follow the procedures at PGI 249.109-70 for limitation on pricing of the terminated or reduced effort.
