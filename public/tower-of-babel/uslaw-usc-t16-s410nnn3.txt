16 U.S.C. § 410nnn–3 — Land acquisition
Citation: us:usc:16:410nnn–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410nnn–3
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–LL (Palo Alto Battlefield National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within the historical park, the Secretary is authorized to acquire lands and interest in lands by donation, purchase with donated or appropriated funds, or exchange. Lands or interests in lands owned by the State of Texas or political subdivisions thereof may be acquired only by donation.
