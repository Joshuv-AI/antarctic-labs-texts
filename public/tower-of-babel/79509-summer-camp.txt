A whistle shrilled, echoing across the crystal-clear lake.

Fifty young girls splashed and squealed their way out of the water.

As the last wet little swimmer ran into the bathhouse, counselor Peggy
Matthews climbed to the diving platform and moved gracefully along the
board, her tanned limbs gleaming in the July sunlight.

She stood statue-still, arms stretched behind her, chin held high, feet

jackknifed into the water.

Seconds later she emerged, shook her blond ponytail, and with smooth,

Noticing her approach, the girl in the boat oared to meet Peggy, who
surface-dived just before she reached the craft and frog-kicked under

“Mind if I join you?” she spluttered from the other side.

dragged oars while Peggy clambered aboard. “For a campcraft counselor,”
