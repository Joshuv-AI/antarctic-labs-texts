10 U.S.C. § 2346 — Crediting of receipts
Citation: us:usc:10:2346
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2346
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 138 (Cooperative Agreements With Nato Allies And Other Countries); Subchapter I (Acquisition And Cross-Servicing Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any receipt of the United States as a result of an agreement entered into under this subchapter shall be credited, at the option of the Secretary of Defense, to (1) the appropriation, fund, or account used in incurring the obligation, or (2) an appropriate appropriation, fund, or account currently available for the purposes for which the expenditures were made.
