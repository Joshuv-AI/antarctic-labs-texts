25 U.S.C. § 1667d — Use of predoctoral psychology and psychiatry interns
Citation: us:usc:25:1667d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1667d
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter V–A (Behavioral Health Programs); Part B (Indian Youth Suicide Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall carry out such activities as the Secretary determines to be necessary to encourage Indian tribes, tribal organizations, and other mental health care providers to obtain the services of predoctoral psychology and psychiatry interns—
(1)
to increase the quantity of patients served by the Indian tribes, tribal organizations, and other mental health care providers; and
(2)
for purposes of recruitment and retention.
