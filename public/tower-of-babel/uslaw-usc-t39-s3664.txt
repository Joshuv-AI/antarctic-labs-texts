39 U.S.C. § 3664 — Enforcement of orders
Citation: us:usc:39:3664
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3664
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 36 (Postal Rates, Classes, And Services); Subchapter V (Postal Services, Complaints, And Judicial Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The several district courts have jurisdiction specifically to enforce, and to enjoin and restrain the Postal Service from violating, any order issued by the Postal Regulatory Commission.
