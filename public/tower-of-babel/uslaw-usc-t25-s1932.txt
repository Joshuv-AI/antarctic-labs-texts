25 U.S.C. § 1932 — Grants for off-reservation programs for additional services
Citation: us:usc:25:1932
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1932
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 21 (Indian Child Welfare); Subchapter II (Indian Child And Family Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is also authorized to make grants to Indian organizations to establish and operate off-reservation Indian child and family service programs which may include, but are not limited to—
(1)
a system for regulating, maintaining, and supporting Indian foster and adoptive homes, including a subsidy program under which Indian adoptive children may be provided support comparable to that for which they would be eligible as Indian foster children, taking into account the appropriate State standards of support for maintenance and medical needs;
(2)
the operation and maintenance of facilities and services for counseling and treatment of Indian families and Indian foster and adoptive children;
(3)
family assistance, including homemaker and home counselors, day care, afterschool care, and employment, recreational activities, and respite care; and
(4)
guidance, legal representation, and advice to Indian families involved in child custody proceedings.
