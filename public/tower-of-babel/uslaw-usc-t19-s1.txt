19 U.S.C. § 1 — Organization of customs service
Citation: us:usc:19:1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1 (Collection Districts, Ports, And Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as hereinafter provided the reorganization of the customs service made by the President and communicated to Congress under date of March 3, 1913, shall, until otherwise provided by Congress, constitute the permanent organization of the customs service.
