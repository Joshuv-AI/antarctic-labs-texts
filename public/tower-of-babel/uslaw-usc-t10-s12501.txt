10 U.S.C. § 12501 — Reserve components: detail of members of regular and reserve components to assist
Citation: us:usc:10:12501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12501
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1213 (Special Appointments, Assignments, Details, And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary concerned shall detail such members of the regular and reserve components under his jurisdiction as are necessary to effectively develop, train, instruct, and administer those reserve components.
