27 C.F.R. § 17.21 — Registration.
Citation: us:cfr:27:17.21
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-17.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 17 (Drawback On Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Used In Manufacturing Nonbeverage Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person claiming drawback under this part must register annually as a nonbeverage domestic drawback claimant. Registration will be accomplished when the claimant submits the first drawback claim for each year along with the supporting data required under subpart G of this part. No registration is required for any year in which the claimant does not file a claim for drawback.
