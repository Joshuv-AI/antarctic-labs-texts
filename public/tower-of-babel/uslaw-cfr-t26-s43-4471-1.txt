26 C.F.R. § 43.4471-1 — -1 Imposition of tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:43.4471-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-43.4471-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 43 (Excise Tax On Transportation By Water). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. Section 4471 imposes a tax of $3 per passenger on a covered voyage as is defined in section 4472.
(b) By whom paid. The tax is imposed on the person providing the covered voyage (the operator of the vessel).
