19 C.F.R. § 127.21 — Time of sale.
Citation: us:cfr:19:127.21
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-127.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 127 (General Order, Unclaimed, And Abandoned Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All unclaimed and abandoned merchandise will be sold at the first regular sale held after the merchandise becomes subject to sale, unless a deferment of its sale is authorized by the port director. Regular sales shall be made once every year or more often at the discretion of the port director.
