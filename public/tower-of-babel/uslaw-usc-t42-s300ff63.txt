42 U.S.C. § 300ff–63 — Applicability of requirements regarding confidentiality, informed consent, and counseling
Citation: us:usc:42:300ff–63
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300ff–63
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXIV (Hiv Health Care Services Program); Part C (Early Intervention Services); Subpart ii (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not make a grant under this part unless the applicant for the grant agrees that, with respect to testing for HIV/AIDS, any such testing carried out by the applicant with funds appropriated through this chapter will be carried out in accordance with conditions described in sections 300ff–61 and 300ff–62 of this title.
