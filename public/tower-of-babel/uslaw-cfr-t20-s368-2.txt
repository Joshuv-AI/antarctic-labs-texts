20 C.F.R. § 368.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:20:368.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-368.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 368 (Prohibition Of Cigarette Sales To Minors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part—
Federal property includes any building and real property occupied and maintained by the Board.
Minor means an individual under the age of 18 years.
Tobacco product means cigarettes, cigars, little cigars, pipe tobacco, smokeless tobacco, snuff, and chewing tobacco.
