36 C.F.R. § 1224.3 — What standards are used as guidance for this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1224.3
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1224.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1224 (Records Disposition Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations conform with guidance provided in ISO 15489-1:2001, Information and documentation—Records management. Paragraphs 7.1 (Principles of records management programmes), 8.3.7 (Retention and disposition), 8.5 (Discontinuing records systems), and 9.9 (Implementing disposition) apply to records disposition.
