16 U.S.C. § 7152 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:7152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7152
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 90 (Secure Rural Schools And Community Self-Determination); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
