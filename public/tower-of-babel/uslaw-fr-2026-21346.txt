SUMMARY: The Department of the Interior (DOI), acting through the 
Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM), is correcting citations in 30 
CFR part 560. DOI issued two rules in 2008 and 2016 where some of the 
finalized amendments were never incorporated into the CFR. This 
document corrects 30 CFR part 560 to reflect the finalized amendments.

DATES: Effective on October 19, 2026.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background

    On October 7, 2008, the Minerals Management Service (MMS), BOEM's 
predecessor agency, issued Royalty Relief for Deepwater Outer 
Continental Shelf Oil and Gas Leases--Conforming Regulations to Court 
Decision final rule (73 FR 58467), which, among other things, 
eliminated specific part 260 sections. The final rule removed sections 
260.117 and 260.114(c), (d), and (e) from the CFR. However, later 
amendments to part 260 did not reflect the eliminated sections. Cross-
references to eliminated sections 260.117 and 260.114(d) in part 260 
remained.
    On October 18, 2011, DOI published the Reorganization of Title 30: 
Bureaus of Safety and Environmental Enforcement and Ocean Energy 
Management final rule (76 FR 64432), which recodified 30 CFR part 260 
regulations into the new 30 CFR chapter V, part 560. During this 
recodification, the invalid references to eliminated part 260 sections 
were carried forward into part 560.
    On March 30, 2016, BOEM published the Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and 
Gas in the Outer Continental Shelf final rule (81 FR 18112). In the 
final rule, BOEM redesignated several sections in subparts A and B of 
part 560 and revised their titles to align with the numbering structure 
used throughout the regulation. However, BOEM did not include complete 
instructions to update references to reflect the redesignation. As a 
result, any references to the former section numbers that were not 
removed are invalid.
    This document corrects invalid references in part 560.

II. Summary of Corrections

A. Sections 560.203(a) and (e)

    Sections 560.203(a) and (e) currently cite section 560.110. In the 
Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and Gas in the Outer Continental Shelf final 
rule (81 FR 18112), BOEM redesignated section 560.110 as section 
560.202. This document replaces the incorrect citation with the correct 
reference to section 560.202.

B. Section 560.210

    The introductory paragraph of section 560.210 currently references 
section 560.113 and sections 560.113 through 560.117.
    The final rule Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and Gas in the Outer 
Continental Shelf (81 FR 18112) redesignated sections 560.113 through 
560.116 as sections 560.211 through 560.214. This document corrects the 
references.
    In Royalty Relief for Deepwater Outer Continental Shelf Oil and Gas 
Leases--Conforming Regulations to Court Decision (73 FR 58467), MMS 
removed section 260.117 from the CFR. However, later amendments to part 
260 did not reflect that deletion.
    Reorganization of Title 30: Bureaus of Safety and Environmental 
Enforcement and Ocean Energy Management (76 FR 64432) recodified part 
260 regulations

[[Page 65983]]

into the new 30 CFR chapter V, part 560. During this recodification, an 
invalid reference to former section 260.117 was carried forward as 
section 560.117. This document removes the obsolete citation.

C. Section 560.211

    Section 560.211(a) currently includes a reference to section 
560.110 (g). The final rule Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and Gas in the 
Outer Continental Shelf final rule (81 FR 18112) redesignated section 
560.110 as section 560.202. This document corrects the section to 
reference section 560.202(g).

D. Section 560.220

    The introductory paragraph of section 560.220 currently references 
sections 560.121 through 560.124. In the Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and 
Gas in the Outer Continental Shelf final rule (81 FR 18112), BOEM 
redesignated sections 560.121 through 560.124 as sections 560.221 
through section 560.224. Accordingly, this document corrects section 
560.220 to reference sections 560.221 through 560.224.

E. Section 560.222

    Section 560.222(b)(3) currently includes references to sections 
560.120 through 560.124. The final rule Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and 
Gas in the Outer Continental Shelf (81 FR 18112) redesignated sections 
560.120 through 560.124 as sections 560.220 through 560.224. This 
document corrects section 560.222(b)(3) to reference sections 560.220 
through 560.224.

F. Section 560.224

    Section 560.224(b)(1) currently references section 560.114(d). As 
explained earlier, DOI previously recodified the regulations in section 
260.114 to section 560.114, and earlier, the MMS Royalty Relief for 
Deepwater Outer Continental Shelf Oil and Gas Leases--Conforming 
Regulations to Court Decision final rule (73 FR 58467) removed sections 
260.114(c), (d), and (e). The subsequent regulatory revisions did not 
account for this removal, and an incorrect reference to former section 
260.114(d) remained. It was carried forward as section 560.114(d). This 
document corrects that error by eliminating the reference to 
560.114(d). The regulatory text is clear as written and reference to 
another regulatory citation is unnecessary.

G. Section 560.230

    The introductory paragraph of section 560.230 currently cites 
section 560.110. The Leasing of Sulfur or Oil and Gas in the Outer 
Continental Shelf final rule (81 FR 18112) redesignated section 560.110 
as section 560.202. This document corrects section 560.110 to reference 
to section 560.202.
