49 C.F.R. § 229.71 — Clearance above top of rail.
Citation: us:cfr:49:229.71
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-229.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 229 (Railroad Locomotive Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No part or appliance of a locomotive except the wheels, flexible nonmetallic sand pipe extension tips, and trip cock arms may be less than 2 1/2 inches above the top of rail.
