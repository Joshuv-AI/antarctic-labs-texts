25 U.S.C. § 3664 — No offset
Citation: us:usc:25:3664
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3664
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 38A (Indian Tribal Justice Technical And Legal Assistance); Subchapter I (Training And Technical Assistance, Civil And Criminal Legal Assistance Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No Federal agency shall offset funds made available pursuant to this chapter for Indian tribal court membership organizations or Indian legal services organizations against other funds otherwise available for use in connection with technical or legal assistance to tribal justice systems or members of Indian tribes.
