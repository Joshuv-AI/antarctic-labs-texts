28 U.S.C. § 675 — Law clerks and secretaries
Citation: us:usc:28:675
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section675
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 45 (Supreme Court). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chief Justice of the United States, and the associate justices of the Supreme Court may appoint law clerks and secretaries whose salaries shall be fixed by the Court.
