48 U.S.C. § 1826 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:48:1826
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1826
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 17 (Northern Mariana Islands); Subchapter II (Judicial Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this subchapter.
