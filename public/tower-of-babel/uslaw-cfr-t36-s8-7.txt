36 C.F.R. § 8.7 — Record keeping.
Citation: us:cfr:36:8.7
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-8.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 8 (Labor Standards Applicable To Employees Of National Park Service Concessioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Concessioners shall for a period of 3 years keep records of the name, age, address, and occupation of each of their employees, the rate of pay and the amount paid to each employee each pay day, the hours worked each day and each work week by each employee and such other information concerning employees as the Director may require.
