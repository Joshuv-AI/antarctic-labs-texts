48 C.F.R. § 3401.604-70 — 3401.604-70 Contract clause.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3401 (Ed Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers must insert a clause substantially the same as the clause at 3452.201-70 (Contracting Officer's Representative (COR)), in all solicitations and contracts for which a COR will be (or is) appointed.
