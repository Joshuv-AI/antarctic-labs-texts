24 C.F.R. § 943.146 — What impact does the use of a subsidiary, affiliate, or joint venture have on financial accountability to HUD and the Federal government?
Citation: us:cfr:24:943.146
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-943.146
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 943 (Public Housing Agency Consortia And Joint Ventures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

None; the subsidiary, affiliate, or joint venture is subject to the same authority of HUD, HUD's Inspector General, and the Comptroller General to audit its conduct.
