10 U.S.C. § 7324 — Forty years or more: Army officers
Citation: us:usc:10:7324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7324
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 741 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Except as provided in section 1186 of this title, a commissioned officer of the Army who has at least 40 years of service computed under section 7326 of this title shall be retired upon his request.
(b)
Any warrant officer of the Army who has at least 40 years of service computed under section 7326(a) of this title shall be retired upon his request.
