22 U.S.C. § 1928c — Report to the Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:1928c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1928c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24 (Mutual Security Program); Subchapter III (Economic Assistance); Part D (Special Assistance And Other Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Group of the NATO Parliamentary Assembly shall submit to the Congress a report for each fiscal year for which an appropriation is made, including its expenditures under such appropriation.
