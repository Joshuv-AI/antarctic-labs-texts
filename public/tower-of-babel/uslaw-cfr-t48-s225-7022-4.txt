48 C.F.R. § 225.7022-4 — 225.7022-4 Exceptions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibition at 225.7022-3 does not apply to—
(a) Purchases under the micro-purchase threshold made using the Governmentwide commercial purchase card; or
(b) Purchases using the SF 44 in accordance with 213.306.
