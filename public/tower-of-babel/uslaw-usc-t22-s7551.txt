22 U.S.C. § 7551 — Requirement to comply with procedures relating to the prohibition on assistance to drug traffickers
Citation: us:usc:22:7551
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7551
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 82 (Afghanistan Freedom Support); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Assistance provided under this chapter shall be subject to the same provisions as are applicable to assistance under the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 [22 U.S.C. 2151 et seq.] and the Arms Export Control Act [22 U.S.C. 2751 et seq.] under section 487 of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (relating to the prohibition on assistance to drug traffickers; 22 U.S.C. 2291f), and the applicable regulations issued under that section.
