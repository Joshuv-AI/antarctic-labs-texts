49 C.F.R. § 390.209 — Pre-authorization safety audit.
Citation: us:cfr:49:390.209
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-390.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 390 (Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A non-North America-domiciled motor carrier seeking to provide transportation of property or passengers in interstate commerce within the United States must pass the pre-authorization safety audit under § 385.607(c) of this subchapter as a condition for receiving registration under this part.
