31 C.F.R. § 208.3 — Payment by electronic funds transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:31:208.3
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-208.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 208 (Management Of Federal Agency Disbursements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to § 208.4, and notwithstanding any other provision of law, all Federal payments made by an agency shall be made by electronic funds transfer. This requirement does not apply to payments under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
