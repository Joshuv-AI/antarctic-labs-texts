33 C.F.R. § 2.26 — Inland waters.
Citation: us:cfr:33:2.26
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-2.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 2 (Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inland waters means the waters shoreward of the territorial sea baseline.
