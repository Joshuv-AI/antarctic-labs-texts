41 C.F.R. § 302-3.207 — -3.207 Travel to another overseas location (instead of the U.S.).
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.207
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If travel is to another overseas location (instead of the U.S.), the employee will be reimbursed only if their actual residence is within that country in which they are taking their leave, and then they will only be reimbursed for their authorized travel and transportation expenses.
