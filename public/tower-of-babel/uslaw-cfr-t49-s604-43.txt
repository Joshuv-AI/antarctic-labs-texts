49 C.F.R. § 604.43 — Offer of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:49:604.43
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-604.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 604 (Charter Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party whose evidence has been excluded by a ruling of the PO, during a hearing in which the respondent had an opportunity to respond to the offer of proof, may offer the evidence on the record when filing an appeal.
