46 C.F.R. § 131.940 — Marine sanitation device.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.940
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.940
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel with installed toilet facilities must have a marine sanitation device in compliance with 33 CFR part 159.
