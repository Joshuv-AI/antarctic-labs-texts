48 U.S.C. § 1403b — Bond liability of United States
Citation: us:usc:48:1403b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1403b
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 7 (Virgin Islands); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Bonds or other obligations issued pursuant to sections 1403 to 1403b of this title shall not be a debt of the United States, nor shall the United States be liable thereon.
