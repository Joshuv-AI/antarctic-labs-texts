35 U.S.C. § 12 — Copies of patents and applications for public libraries
Citation: us:usc:35:12
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section12
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part I (United States Patent And Trademark Office); Chapter 1 (Establishment, Officers And Employees, Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may supply copies of specifications and drawings of patents and published applications for patents in printed or electronic form to public libraries in the United States which shall maintain such copies for the use of the public, at the rate for each year’s issue established for this purpose in section 41(d).
