41 C.F.R. § 301-11.8 — -11.8 Computation of daily lodging rate for long-term lodging.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.8
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When obtaining lodging on a long-term basis (e.g., weekly or monthly), the daily lodging rate is computed by dividing the total lodging cost by the number of days of occupancy for which the employee is entitled to subsistence expense reimbursement for lodging. The daily rate may not exceed the daily per diem rate for the TDY location.
