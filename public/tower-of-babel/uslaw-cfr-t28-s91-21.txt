28 C.F.R. § 91.21 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:28:91.21
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-91.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 91 (Grants For Correctional Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth requirements and procedures to award grants to Indian Tribes for purposes of constructing jails on tribal lands for the incarceration of offenders subject to tribal jurisdiction.
