43 C.F.R. § 4120.3-5 — -5 Assignment of range improvements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4120.3-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4120.3-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4100 (Grazing Administration—Exclusive Of Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer shall not approve the transfer of a grazing preference under § 4110.2-3 of this title or approve use by the transferee of existing range improvements, unless the transferee has agreed to compensate the transferor for his/her interest in the authorized improvements within the allotment as of the date of the transfer.
