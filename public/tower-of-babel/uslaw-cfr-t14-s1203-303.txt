14 C.F.R. § 1203.303 — Distribution controls.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1203.303
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1203.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1203 (Information Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NASA shall establish controls over the distribution of classified information to ensure that it is dispersed only to organizations or individuals eligible for access to such information and with a need-to-know the information.
