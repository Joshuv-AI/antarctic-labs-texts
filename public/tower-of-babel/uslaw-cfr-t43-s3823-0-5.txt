43 C.F.R. § 3823.0-5 — -5 Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3823.0-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3823.0-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3820 (Areas Subject To Special Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart the term National Forest Wilderness means an area or part of an area of National Forest lands designated by the Wilderness Act as a wilderness area within the National Wilderness Preservation System.
