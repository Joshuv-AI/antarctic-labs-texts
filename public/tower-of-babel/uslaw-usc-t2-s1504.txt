2 U.S.C. § 1504 — Agency assistance
Citation: us:usc:2:1504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1504
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 25 (Unfunded Mandates Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each agency shall provide to the Director such information and assistance as the Director may reasonably request to assist the Director in carrying out this chapter.
