8 U.S.C. § 1353c — Immigration officials; service in foreign contiguous territory
Citation: us:usc:8:1353c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1353c
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter II (Immigration); Part IX (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in section 209 of title 18 relative to augmenting salaries of Government officials from outside sources shall prevent receiving reimbursements for services of immigration officials incident to the inspection of aliens in foreign contiguous territory and such reimbursement shall be credited to the appropriation, “Immigration and Naturalization Service—Salaries and Expenses.”
