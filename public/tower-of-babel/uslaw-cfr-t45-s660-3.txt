45 C.F.R. § 660.3 — What programs and activities of the Foundation are subject to these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:45:660.3
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-660.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 660 (Intergovernmental Review Of The National Science Foundation Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director publishes in the Federal Register a list of the Foundation's programs and activities that are subject to these regulations.
