44 U.S.C. § 2308 — Tax exemption for gifts
Citation: us:usc:44:2308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2308
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 23 (National Archives Trust Fund Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Gifts and bequests received by the Board under this chapter, and the income from them are exempt from taxes.
