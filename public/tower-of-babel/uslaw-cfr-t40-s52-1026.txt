40 C.F.R. § 52.1026 — Review of new sources and modifications.
Citation: us:cfr:40:52.1026
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.1026
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The program to review operation and construction of new and modified major stationary sources in non-attainment areas is approved as meeting the requirements of part D as amended by the CAAA of 1990.
