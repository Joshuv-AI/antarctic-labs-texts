40 C.F.R. § 124.202 — How do I as a facility owner or operator apply for a standardized permit?
Citation: us:cfr:40:124.202
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-124.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 124 (Procedures For Decisionmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must follow the requirements in this subpart as well as those in § 124.31, 40 CFR 270.10, and 40 CFR part 270, subpart J.
(b) You must submit to the Director a written Notice of Intent to operate under the standardized permit. You must also include the information and certifications required under 40 CFR part 270, subpart J.
