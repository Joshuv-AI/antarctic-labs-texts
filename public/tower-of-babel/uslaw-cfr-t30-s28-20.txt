30 C.F.R. § 28.20 — Certificates of approval; scope of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:30:28.20
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-28.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 28 (Fuses For Use With Direct Current In Providing Short-Circuit Protection For Trailing Cables In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) MSHA shall issue certificates of approval pursuant to the provisions of this subpart only for individual, completely fabricated fuses which have been examined, inspected, and tested as specified in § 28.10, and have been evaluated by MSHA to ensure that they meet the minimum requirements prescribed in this part.
(b) MSHA shall not issue an informal notification of approval.
