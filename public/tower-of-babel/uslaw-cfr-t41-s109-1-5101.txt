41 C.F.R. § 109-1.5101 — -1.5101 Official use of personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-1.5101
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-1.5101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Personal property shall be used only in the performance of official work of the United States Government, except:
(a) In emergencies threatening loss of life or property as authorized by law;
(b) As otherwise authorized by law and approved by the Office of Management; Program Secretarial Officer (PSO) for their respective organizations; or a contracting officer for contractor-held property.
