33 U.S.C. § 666 — Noting conditions of navigable channels
Citation: us:usc:33:666
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section666
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 14 (California Debris Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The said commission shall from time to time note the conditions of the navigable channels of said river systems, by cross-section surveys or otherwise, in order to ascertain the effect therein of such hydraulic mining operations as may be permitted by its orders and such as is caused by erosion, natural or otherwise.
