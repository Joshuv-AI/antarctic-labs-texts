7 C.F.R. § 3011.5 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3011.5
Creator: Office Of The Chief Financial Officer, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3011.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3011 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person whose initial request is denied in whole or in part may appeal that denial, in accordance with 7 CFR 1.6(e) and 1.8, to the Director, Office of Finance and Management, USDA, Room 117-W, Administration Building, 14th and Independence Ave., Washington, DC 20250-9000.
