31 C.F.R. § 256.20 — How does an agency indicate that a debt is to be offset from a Judgment Fund payment?
Citation: us:cfr:31:256.20
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-256.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 256 (Obtaining Payments From The Judgment Fund And Under Private Relief Bills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The submitting agency must identify on the appropriate Judgment Fund form any known debt owed to the United States that Fiscal Service is expected to collect by setoff against the award. Such a debt will be offset pursuant to the provisions of 31 U.S.C. 3728.
