25 C.F.R. § 173.5 — Plans, approval thereof.
Citation: us:cfr:25:173.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-173.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 173 (Concessions, Permits And Leases On Lands Withdrawn Or Acquired In Connection With Indian Irrigation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No building or other structure shall be erected by permittee except in accordance with plans, specifications and locations approved by the project engineer. All premises and appurtenances shall be kept in a sanitary, safe and sightly condition.
