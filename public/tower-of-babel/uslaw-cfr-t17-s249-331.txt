17 C.F.R. § 249.331 — Form N-CSR, certified shareholder report.
Citation: us:cfr:17:249.331
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-249.331
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 249 (Forms, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used by registered management investment companies to file reports pursuant to § 270.30b2-1(a) of this chapter not later than 10 days after the transmission to stockholders of any report that is required to be transmitted to stockholders under § 270.30e-1 of this chapter.
