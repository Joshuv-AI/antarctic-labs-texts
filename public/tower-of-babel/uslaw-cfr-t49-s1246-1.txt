49 C.F.R. § 1246.1 — Monthly report of number of railroad employees.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1246.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1246.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1246 (Number Of Railroad Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Class I railroad shall file a Monthly Report of Number of Railroad Employees (Form C) each month. The report should be submitted, in paper or electronically, to the Office of Economics, Surface Transportation Board, Washington, DC, by the end of the month to which it applies.
