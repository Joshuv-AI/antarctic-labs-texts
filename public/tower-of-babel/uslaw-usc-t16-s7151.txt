16 U.S.C. § 7151 — Regulations

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 90 (Secure Rural Schools And Community Self-Determination); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of the Interior shall issue regulations to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
