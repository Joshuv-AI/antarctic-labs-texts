7 C.F.R. § 503.3 — Preservation of property.
Citation: us:cfr:7:503.3
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-503.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 503 (Conduct On Plum Island Animal Disease Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The willful destruction, damage to or removal of property or any part thereof from the Government-owned buildings, grounds, and vessels in or on the PIADC is prohibited.
