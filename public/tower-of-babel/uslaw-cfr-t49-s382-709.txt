49 C.F.R. § 382.709 — Drivers' access to information in the Clearinghouse.
Citation: us:cfr:49:382.709
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-382.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 382 (Controlled Substances And Alcohol Use And Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A driver may review information in the Clearinghouse about himself or herself, except as otherwise restricted by law or regulation. A driver must register with the Clearinghouse before accessing his or her information.
