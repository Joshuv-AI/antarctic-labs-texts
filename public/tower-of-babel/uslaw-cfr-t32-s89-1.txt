32 C.F.R. § 89.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:89.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-89.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 89 (Interstate Compact On Educational Opportunity For Military Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with the sense of Congress as set forth in section 539 of Public Law 111-84, this part establishes policy, assigns responsibilities, and provides procedures to implement the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children (referred to in this part as the “Compact”) within the DoD.
