2 C.F.R. § 902.500 — Who in the DOE determines that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:902.500
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-902.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 902 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Department of Energy and the Secretary's designee or designees are authorized to make the determinations under 2 CFR 182.500 for DOE, including NNSA.
