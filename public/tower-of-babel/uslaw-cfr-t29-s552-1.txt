29 C.F.R. § 552.1 — Terms used in regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:29:552.1
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-552.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 552 (Application Of The Fair Labor Standards Act To Domestic Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Administrator means the Administrator of the Wage and Hour Division, U.S. Department of Labor, or the Administrator's authorized representative.
(b) Act means the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938, as amended.
