36 U.S.C. § 200102 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:36:200102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section200102
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2001 (Society Of American Florists And Ornamental Horticulturists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are to educate members of the florist industry and the public, and to promote scientific development, in floriculture and horticulture.
