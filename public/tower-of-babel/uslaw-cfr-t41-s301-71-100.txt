41 C.F.R. § 301-71.100 — -71.100 Purpose of the travel authorization process.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-71.100
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-71.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-71 (71—Agency Travel Accountability Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The travel authorization process serves to:
(a) Inform employees about authorized expenses.
(b) Provide travel service vendors with documentation for travel programs.
(c) Generate financial information for budgetary planning.
(d) Identify the specific purpose of travel.
