34 C.F.R. § 300.213 — Records regarding migratory children with disabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.213
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The LEA must cooperate in the Secretary's efforts under section 1308 of the ESEA to ensure the linkage of records pertaining to migratory children with disabilities for the purpose of electronically exchanging, among the States, health and educational information regarding those children.
