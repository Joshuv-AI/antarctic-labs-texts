1 C.F.R. § 18.7 — Signature.
Citation: us:cfr:1:18.7
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-18.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 18 (Preparation And Transmittal Of Documents Generally). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The original and each duplicate original document must be signed in ink, with the name and title of the official signing the document typed or stamped beneath the signature. Initialed or impressed signatures will not be accepted. Documents submitted under § 18.4(c) may be authenticated as original documents by digital signatures.
