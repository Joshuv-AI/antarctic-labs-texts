41 C.F.R. § 302-17.44 — -17.44 Income tax liability to the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands or any other territory or possession of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-17.44
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-17.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-17 (17—Taxes On Relocation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee is relocated to, from, or within the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands or any territory or possession of the United States, the agency will have to determine the tax rules of that locality and then include those taxes in the RITA calculation, as applicable.
