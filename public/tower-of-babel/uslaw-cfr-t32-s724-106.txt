32 C.F.R. § 724.106 — Presiding Officer, NDRB Panel.
Citation: us:cfr:32:724.106
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-724.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 724 (Naval Discharge Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The senior member of an NDRB Panel shall normally be the Presiding Officer. He/she shall convene, recess and adjourn the NDRB Panel as appropriate.
