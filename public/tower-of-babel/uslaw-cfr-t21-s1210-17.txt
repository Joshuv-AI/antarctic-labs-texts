21 C.F.R. § 1210.17 — Authority to sample and inspect.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1210.17
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1210.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1210 (Regulations Under The Federal Import Milk Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspectors engaged in the enforcement of the Federal Import Milk Act are empowered to test for temperature, to take samples of milk or cream, and to use such means as may be necessary for these purposes.
