2 U.S.C. § 5325 — Performance of duties by clerical assistants of dead or resigned Member of House
Citation: us:usc:2:5325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5325
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter II (Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any clerical assistants who continue on the House pay rolls under the provisions of section 5324 of this title shall, while so continued, perform their duties under the direction of the Clerk of the House, and he is authorized and directed to remove from such pay rolls any such clerks who are not attending to the duties for which their services are continued.
