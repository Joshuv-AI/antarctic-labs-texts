28 U.S.C. § 131 — Wyoming
Citation: us:usc:28:131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section131
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 5 (District Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Wyoming and those portions of Yellowstone National Park situated in Montana and Idaho constitute one judicial district.
Court shall be held at Casper, Cheyenne, Evanston, Lander, Jackson, and Sheridan.
