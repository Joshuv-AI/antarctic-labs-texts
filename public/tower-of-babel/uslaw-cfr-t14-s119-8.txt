14 C.F.R. § 119.8 — Safety Management Systems.
Citation: us:cfr:14:119.8
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-119.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 119 (Certification: Air Carriers And Commercial Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certificate holders authorized to conduct operations under part 121 or 135 of this chapter must have a safety management system that meets the requirements of part 5 of this chapter.
