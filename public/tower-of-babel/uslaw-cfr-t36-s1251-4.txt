36 C.F.R. § 1251.4 — May employees provide records or give testimony in response to a demand without authorization?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1251.4
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1251.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1251 (Testimony By Nara Employees Relating To Agency Information And Production Of Records In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, except as otherwise permitted by § 1251.14 of this part, no employee may produce records and information or provide any testimony relating to agency information in response to a demand, or other legal request, without the prior, written approval of the General Counsel.
