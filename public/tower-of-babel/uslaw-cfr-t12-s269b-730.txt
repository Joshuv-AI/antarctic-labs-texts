12 C.F.R. § 269b.730 — b.730 Number of copies; form.
Citation: us:cfr:12:269b.730
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-269b.730
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 269b (PART 269b—CHARGES OF UNFAIR LABOR PRACTICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in the regulations in this subchapter, any documents or papers shall be filed with four copies in addition to the original. All matters filed shall be printed, typed, or otherwise legibly duplicated; carbon copies of typewritten matter will be accepted if they are clearly legible.
