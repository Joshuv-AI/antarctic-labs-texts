20 C.F.R. § 602.20 — Organization.
Citation: us:cfr:20:602.20
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-602.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 602 (Quality Control In The Federal-State Unemployment Insurance System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State shall establish a QC unit independent of, and not accountable to, any unit performing functions subject to evaluation by the QC unit. The organizational location of this unit shall be positioned to maximize its objectivity, to facilitate its access to information necessary to carry out its responsibilities, and to minimize organizational conflict of interest.
