7 C.F.R. § 1774.19 — Applications determined ineligible.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1774.19
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1774.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1774 (Special Evaluation Assistance For Rural Communities And Households Program (Search)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If at any time an application is determined ineligible, the processing office will notify the applicant in writing of the reasons. The notification to the applicant will state that an appeal of this decision may be made by the applicant under 7 CFR part 11.
