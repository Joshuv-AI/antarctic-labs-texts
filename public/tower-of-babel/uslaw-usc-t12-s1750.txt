12 U.S.C. § 1750 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:12:1750
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1750
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter X (National Defense Housing Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter, the terms “mortgage”, “first mortgage”, “mortgagee”, “mortgagor”, “maturity date”, and “State” shall have the same meaning as in section 1707 of this title.
