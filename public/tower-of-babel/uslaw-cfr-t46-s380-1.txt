46 C.F.R. § 380.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:380.1

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 380 (Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe procedure to be followed for filing applications submitted to the Maritime Subsidy Board/Maritime Administration pursuant to the provisions of section 805(a), Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended.
