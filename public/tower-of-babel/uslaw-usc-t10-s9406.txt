10 U.S.C. § 9406 — Service schools: leaves of absence for instructors
Citation: us:usc:10:9406
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9406
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 951 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The officer in charge of an Air Force or Space Force service school may grant a leave of absence for the period of the suspension of the ordinary academic studies, without deduction of pay or allowances, to any officer on duty exclusively as an instructor at the school.
