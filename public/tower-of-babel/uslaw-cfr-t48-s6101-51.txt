48 C.F.R. § 6101.51 — 6101.51 Alternative procedures [Rule 51].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6101.51
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6101.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6101 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appellant in an eligible case may elect the small claims procedure under Rule 52 (48 CFR 6101.52) or the accelerated procedure under Rule 53 (48 CFR 6101.53). Parties may jointly elect alternative dispute resolution under Rule 54 (48 CFR 6101.54).
