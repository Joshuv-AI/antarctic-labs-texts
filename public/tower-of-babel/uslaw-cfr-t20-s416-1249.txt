20 C.F.R. § 416.1249 — Exclusion of payments received as restitution for misuse of benefits by a representative payee.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1249
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1249
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining the resources of an individual (and spouse, if any), the unspent portion of any payment received by the individual as restitution for title II, title VIII or title XVI benefits misused by a representative payee under § 404.2041, § 408.641 or § 416.641, respectively, is excluded for 9 months following the month of receipt.
