32 C.F.R. § 1624.7 — Expiration of deferment or exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1624.7
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1624.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1624 (Inductions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director shall issue an Order to Report for Induction to a registrant who is liable for induction whenever his deferment or exemption expires.
