29 C.F.R. § 778.4 — Reliance on interpretations.

Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 778 (Overtime Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The interpretations of the law contained in this part 778 are official interpretations which may be relied upon as provided in section 10 of the Portal-to-Portal Act of 1947 (61 Stat. 84).
