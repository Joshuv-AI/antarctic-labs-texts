2 U.S.C. § 1386 — Disposition of surplus or obsolete personal property
Citation: us:usc:2:1386
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1386
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 24 (Congressional Accountability); Subchapter III (Office Of Congressional Workplace Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Executive Director may, within the limits of available appropriations, dispose of surplus or obsolete personal property by interagency transfer, donation, or discarding.
