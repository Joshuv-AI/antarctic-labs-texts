29 C.F.R. § 515.10 — Amendments and repeal.
Citation: us:cfr:29:515.10
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-515.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 515 (Utilization Of State Agencies For Investigations And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part may be amended or repealed by appropriate joint regulations issued by the Secretary of Labor and the Administrator: Provided, however, That no such amendment or repeal shall be effective as to any agreement previously entered into by a State agency without its consent thereto.
