7 C.F.R. § 4290.410 — Changes in Control of RBIC (through change in ownership or otherwise).
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.410
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must obtain the Agency's prior written approval for any proposed transaction or event that results in Control by any Person(s) not previously approved by the Agency.
