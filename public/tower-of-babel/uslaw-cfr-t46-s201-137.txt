46 C.F.R. § 201.137 — Rights of parties as to presentation of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.137
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every party shall have the right to present his case or defense by oral or documentary evidence, to submit rebuttal evidence, and to conduct such cross-examination as may be required for a full and true disclosure of the facts.
