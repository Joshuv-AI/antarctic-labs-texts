51 U.S.C. § 40101 — Definition of institution of higher education
Citation: us:usc:51:40101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40101
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 401 (Aeronautics); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “institution of higher education” has the meaning given the term by section 101 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1001).
