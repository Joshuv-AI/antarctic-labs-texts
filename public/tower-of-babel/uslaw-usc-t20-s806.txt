20 U.S.C. § 806 — Authorization of appropriations; availability of funds
Citation: us:usc:20:806
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section806
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 23 (Training And Fellowship Programs For Community Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated for the purpose of making grants and providing fellowships under this chapter, without fiscal year limitation, not to exceed $30,000,000, which amount shall be increased by $3,500,000 on July 1, 1974, and by $3,500,000 on July 1, 1975. Any amounts appropriated under this section shall remain available until expended.
