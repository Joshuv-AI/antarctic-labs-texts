37 C.F.R. § 360.20 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:37:360.20
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-360.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 360 (Filing Of Claims To Royalty Fees Collected Under Compulsory License). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes procedures whereby an interested copyright party, as defined in 17 U.S.C. 1001(7), claiming to be entitled to royalty payments made for the importation and distribution in the United States, or the manufacture and distribution in the United States, of digital audio recording devices and media (DART) pursuant to 17 U.S.C. 1006, shall file claims with the Copyright Royalty Board.
