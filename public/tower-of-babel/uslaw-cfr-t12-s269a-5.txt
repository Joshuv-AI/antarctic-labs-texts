12 C.F.R. § 269a.5 — a.5 Hearing officer.
Citation: us:cfr:12:269a.5
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-269a.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 269a (PART 269a—DEFINITIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term hearing officer means the officer designated by the panel to conduct hearings pursuant to § 269b.420 et seq. of this subchapter and whose duties and power are enumerated in § 269b.442 of this subchapter.
