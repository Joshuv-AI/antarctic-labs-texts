16 U.S.C. § 470aaa–4 — Curation of resources
Citation: us:usc:16:470aaa–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section470aaa–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1C (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any paleontological resource, and any data and records associated with the resource, collected under a permit, shall be deposited in an approved repository. The Secretary may enter into agreements with non-Federal repositories regarding the curation of these resources, data, and records.
