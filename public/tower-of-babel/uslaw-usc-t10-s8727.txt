10 U.S.C. § 8727 — Cooperative or unit plans in the naval petroleum reserves
Citation: us:usc:10:8727
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8727
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 869 (Naval Petroleum Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, with the consent of the President, may make agreements, with respect to lands inside the naval petroleum reserves, of the same type as the Secretary of the Interior may make under section 17(m) of the Act of February 25, 1920 (30 U.S.C. 226(m)). No such agreement made by the Secretary may extend the term of any lease unless the agreement so provides.
