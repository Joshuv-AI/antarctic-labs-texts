12 C.F.R. § 741.221 — Suretyship and guaranty requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:741.221
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-741.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 741 (Requirements For Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any credit union, which is insured pursuant to title II of the Act, must adhere to the requirements in § 701.20 of this chapter. State-chartered, NCUSIF-insured credit unions may only enter into suretyship and guaranty agreements to the extent authorized under state law.
