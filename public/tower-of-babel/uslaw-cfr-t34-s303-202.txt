34 C.F.R. § 303.202 — Certification regarding financial responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:34:303.202
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-303.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 303 (Early Intervention Program For Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each application must include a certification to the Secretary that the arrangements to establish financial responsibility for the provision of part C services among appropriate public agencies under § 303.511 and the lead agency's contracts with EIS providers regarding financial responsibility for the provision of part C services both meet the requirements in subpart F of this part (§§ 303.500 through 303.521) and are current as of the date of submission of the certification.
