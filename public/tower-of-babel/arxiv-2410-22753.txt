Nowhere vanishing 1 1 -forms on varieties admitting a good minimal model
Benjamin Church

Abstract
. We prove several conjectures relating the existence of nonvanishing 1 1 -forms to smooth morphisms over abelian varieties, assuming the existence of good minimal models. The proof involves a decomposition result for a family of Calabi-Yau varieties equipped with a surjective map to an abelian scheme. In the uniruled case, supposing the MRC base admits a good minimal model, we also achieve a structure theorem for those varieties admitting nowhere vanishing 1 1 -forms.

Definition 5.3

Let

form a basis and

if we apply proper shifts to give a

-equivariant structure:

Remark 5.4

Since

, where

Moreover,

computes the associated graded of the direct image for the trivial Hodge module

Furthermore, the cohomology “commutes” with

also computes the graded parts of the decomposition of
