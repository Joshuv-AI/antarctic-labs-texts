14 C.F.R. § 16.105 — Failure to resolve informally.
Citation: us:cfr:14:16.105
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-16.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 16 (Rules Of Practice For Federally-Assisted Airport Enforcement Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the matters addressed in the FAA notices are not resolved informally, the FAA may issue a Director's Determination under § 16.31.
