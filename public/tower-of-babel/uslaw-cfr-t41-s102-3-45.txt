41 C.F.R. § 102-3.45 — -3.45 Requirements for establishing and terminating advisory committees.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-3.45
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-3.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-3 (3—Federal Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requirements for establishing and terminating advisory committees vary depending on the establishing entity and the source of authority for the advisory committee. This subpart covers the procedures associated with the establishment, renewal, reestablishment, merger, and termination of advisory committees.
