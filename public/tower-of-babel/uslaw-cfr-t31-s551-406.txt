31 C.F.R. § 551.406 — Offshore transactions involving blocked property.
Citation: us:cfr:31:551.406
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-551.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 551 (Somalia Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibitions in § 551.201 on transactions or dealings involving blocked property, as defined in § 551.303, apply to transactions by any U.S. person in a location outside the United States.
