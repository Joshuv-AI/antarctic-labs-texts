27 C.F.R. § 19.348 — Formula requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.348
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.348
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor must obtain approval of a formula on form TTB F 5100.51 as provided in §§ 5.192 and 5.193 of this chapter before a proprietor may:
(a) Blend, mix, purify, refine, compound, or treat spirits in any manner which results in a change of character, composition, class, or type of the spirits, including redistillation as provided in § 19.314; or
(b) Produce gin or vodka by other than original and continuous distillation.
