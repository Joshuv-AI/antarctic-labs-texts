13 C.F.R. § 108.1120 — General eligibility requirement for Leverage.
Citation: us:cfr:13:108.1120
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-108.1120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 108 (New Markets Venture Capital (“Nmvc”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for Leverage, you must be in compliance with the Act, the regulations in this part, and your Participation Agreement.
