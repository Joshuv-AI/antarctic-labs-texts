29 U.S.C. § 3344 — Reduction of reporting burdens and requirements
Citation: us:usc:29:3344
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section3344
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 32 (Workforce Innovation And Opportunity); Subchapter III (General Provisions); Part A (Workforce Investment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to simplify reporting requirements and reduce reporting burdens, the Secretary of Labor, the Secretary of Education, and the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall establish procedures and criteria under which a State board and local board may reduce reporting burdens and requirements under this Act (including the amendments made by this Act).
