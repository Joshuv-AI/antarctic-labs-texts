40 C.F.R. § 370.44 — To whom must I submit the inventory information?
Citation: us:cfr:40:370.44
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-370.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 370 (Hazardous Chemical Reporting: Community Right-To-Know). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit the required inventory information to your SERC, LEPC, and the fire department with jurisdiction over your facility.
