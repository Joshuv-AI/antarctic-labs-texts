15 U.S.C. § 1511 — Bureaus in Department
Citation: us:usc:15:1511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1511
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 40 (Department Of Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following named bureaus, administrations, services, offices, and programs of the public service, and all that pertains thereto, shall be under the jurisdiction and subject to the control of the Secretary of Commerce:
(1)
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration;
(2)
United States Travel and Tourism Administration;
(3)
National Institute of Standards and Technology;
(4)
United States Patent and Trademark Office 1
(5)
Bureau of the Census; and
(6)
such other bureaus or other organizational units as the Secretary of Commerce may from time to time establish in accordance with law.
