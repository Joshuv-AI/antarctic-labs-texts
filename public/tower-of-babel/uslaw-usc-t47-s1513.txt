47 U.S.C. § 1513 — No additional funds authorized
Citation: us:usc:47:1513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1513
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 14 (Making Opportunities For Broadband Investment And Limiting Excessive And Needless Obstacles To Wireless). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No additional funds are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter, or the amendments made by this title.1 This chapter, and the amendments made by this title,1 shall be carried out using amounts otherwise authorized.
