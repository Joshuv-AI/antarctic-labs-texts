45 C.F.R. § 90.11 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:45:90.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-90.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 90 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to set forth the prohibitions against age discrimination and the exceptions to those prohibitions.
