15 U.S.C. § 1336 — Authority of Federal Trade Commission; unfair or deceptive acts or practices
Citation: us:usc:15:1336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1336
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 36 (Cigarette Labeling And Advertising). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter (other than the requirements of section 1333 of this title) shall be construed to limit, restrict, expand, or otherwise affect the authority of the Federal Trade Commission with respect to unfair or deceptive acts or practices in the advertising of cigarettes.
