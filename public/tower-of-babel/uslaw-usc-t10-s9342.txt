10 U.S.C. § 9342 — Higher grade for service in special positions
Citation: us:usc:10:9342
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9342
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 943 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon retirement, any permanent professor of the United States Air Force Academy whose grade is below brigadier general, and whose service as such a professor has been long and distinguished, may, in the discretion of the President, be retired in the grade of brigadier general.
