30 C.F.R. § 556.603 — How may I maintain my sulfur lease beyond the primary term?
Citation: us:cfr:30:556.603
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-556.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 556 (Leasing Of Sulfur Or Oil And Gas And Financial Assurance Requirements In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may maintain your sulfur lease after the primary term as long as you are producing sulfur in paying quantities, conducting drilling, well reworking or plant construction, or other operations for the production of sulfur or you are granted a suspension by BSEE; or your lease is subject to a suspension directed by BSEE for reasons other than gross negligence or a willful violation of a provision of your lease or governing regulations.
