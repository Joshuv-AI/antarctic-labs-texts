16 C.F.R. § 1.89 — Effect on prior actions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1.89
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1 (General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Commission to apply these procedures to the fullest extent possible to proceedings which are already in progress.
