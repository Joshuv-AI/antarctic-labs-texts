36 C.F.R. § 1270.24 — When the Archivist may act for a President.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1270.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1270.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1270 (Presidential Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a President specifies restrictions on access to Presidential records under 44 U.S.C. 2204(a), but has not made a designation under § 1270.22 at the time of their death or disability, the Archivist exercises the President's discretion or authority under 44 U.S.C. 2204, except as limited by 44 U.S.C. 2208 and § 1270.48.
