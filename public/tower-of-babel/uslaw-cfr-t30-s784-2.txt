30 C.F.R. § 784.2 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:30:784.2
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-784.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 784 (Underground Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements For Reclamation And Operation Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objectives of this part are to ensure that the regulatory authority is provided with comprehensive and reliable information on proposed underground mining activities, and to ensure that those activities are allowed to be conducted only in compliance with the Act, this chapter, and the regulatory program.
