40 C.F.R. § 464.10 — Applicability; description of the aluminum casting subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:464.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-464.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 464 (Metal Molding And Casting Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges to waters of the United States and to the introduction of pollutants into publicly owned treatment works resulting from aluminum casting operations as defined in § 464.02(a).
