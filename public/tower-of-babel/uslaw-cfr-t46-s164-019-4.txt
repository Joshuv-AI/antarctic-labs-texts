46 C.F.R. § 164.019-4 — -4 Component requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:164.019-4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-164.019-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 164 (Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) PFDs may be constructed only with Coast Guard-accepted PFD components meeting the requirements of this subchapter.
(b) PFD components may be used in the construction of PFDs only in accordance with their Use Codes.
