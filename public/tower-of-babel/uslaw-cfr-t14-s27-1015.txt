14 C.F.R. § 27.1015 — Oil tank tests.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.1015
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.1015
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each oil tank must be designed and installed so that it can withstand, without leakage, an internal pressure of 5 p.s.i., except that each pressurized oil tank used with a turbine engine must be designed and installed so that it can withstand, without leakage, an internal pressure of 5 p.s.i., plus the maximum operating pressure of the tank.
