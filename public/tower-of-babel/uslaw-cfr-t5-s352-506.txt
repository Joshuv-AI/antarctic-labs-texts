5 C.F.R. § 352.506 — Application for reinstatement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.506
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who desires reinstatement shall apply for reinstatement, in writing, no later than 30 days after his appointment under authority of section 233(d) or section 625(b) of the Act is terminated, unless arrangement has been made for his reinstatement without a break in service under § 352.505(b).
