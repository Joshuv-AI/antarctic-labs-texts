34 C.F.R. § 685.400 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:34:685.400
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 685 (William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth the provisions under which a borrower defense to repayment may be asserted and applies to borrower defense applications pending with the Secretary on July 1, 2023, or received by the Secretary on or after July 1, 2023.
