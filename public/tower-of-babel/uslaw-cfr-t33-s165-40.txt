33 C.F.R. § 165.40 — Restricted waterfront areas.
Citation: us:cfr:33:165.40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-165.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 165 (Regulated Navigation Areas And Limited Access Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commandant, may direct the COTP to prevent access to waterfront facilities, and port and harbor areas, including vessels and harbor craft therein. This section may apply to persons who do not possess the credentials outlined in § 125.09 of this chapter when certain shipping activities are conducted that are outlined in § 125.15 of this chapter.
