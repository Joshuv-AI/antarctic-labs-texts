42 C.F.R. § 136.418 — What should the IHS do if an individual has been charged with an offense but the charge is pending or no disposition has been made by a court?
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.418
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The IHS may deny the applicant employment until the charge has been resolved.
(b) The IHS may deny the employee any on-the-job contact with children until the charge is resolved.
(c) The IHS may detail or reassign the employee to other duties that do not involve regular contact with children.
(d) The IHS may place the employee on indefinite suspension, in accordance with statutory and regulatory requirements, until the court has disposed of the charge.
