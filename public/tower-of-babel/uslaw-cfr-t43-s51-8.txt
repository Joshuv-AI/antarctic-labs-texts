43 C.F.R. § 51.8 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:43:51.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-51.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 51 (Subsistence Management Regulations For Public Lands In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are convicted of violating any provision of this part or 36 CFR part 242, you may be punished by a fine or by imprisonment in accordance with the penalty provisions applicable to the public land where the violation occurred.
