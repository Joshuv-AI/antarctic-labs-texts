11 C.F.R. § 111.42 — Will the enforcement file be made available to the public?
Citation: us:cfr:11:111.42
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-111.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 111 (COMPLIANCE PROCEDURE (52 U.S.C. 30109, 30107(a))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes; the Commission shall make the enforcement file available to the public.
(b) If neither the Commission nor the respondent commences a civil action, the Commission shall make the enforcement file available to the public pursuant to 11 CFR 4.4(a)(3).
(c) If a civil action is commenced, the Commission shall make the enforcement file available pursuant to 11 CFR 111.20(c).
