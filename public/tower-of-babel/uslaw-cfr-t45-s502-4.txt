45 C.F.R. § 502.4 — Incorporation by reference.
Citation: us:cfr:45:502.4
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-502.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 502 (Public Information-Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, matter which is reasonably available to the class of persons affected thereby will be deemed published in the Federal Register when incorporated by reference therein with the approval of the Director of the Federal Register.
