13 C.F.R. § 114.106 — What if my claim exceeds $5,000?
Citation: us:cfr:13:114.106
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-114.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 114 (Administrative Claims Under The Federal Tort Claims Act And Representation And Indemnification Of Sba Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The District Counsel or Disaster Area Counsel, as appropriate, must review and investigate your claim and forward it with a report and recommendation to the Associate General Counsel for Litigation, who may approve or deny an award, compromise, or settlement of claims in excess of $5,000, but not exceeding $25,000.
