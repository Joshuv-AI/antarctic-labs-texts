34 C.F.R. § 86.102 — What is required of an IHE that the Secretary selects for annual review?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.102
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary selects an IHE for review under § 86.101, the IHE shall provide the Secretary access to personnel, records, documents and any other necessary information requested by the Secretary to review the IHE's adoption and implementation of its drug prevention program.
