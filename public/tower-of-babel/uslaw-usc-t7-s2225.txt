7 U.S.C. § 2225 — Employment of temporary personnel
Citation: us:usc:7:2225
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2225
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Agriculture may employ persons or organizations, on a temporary basis, by contract or otherwise: Provided, That no expenditures for such temporary employment shall be made unless provision is made therefor in the applicable appropriation and the cost thereof is not in excess of limitations prescribed therein.
