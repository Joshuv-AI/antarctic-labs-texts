33 U.S.C. § 531 — International bridges
Citation: us:usc:33:531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section531
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter III (General Bridge Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall not be construed to authorize the construction of any bridge which will connect the United States, or any Territory or possession of the United States, with any foreign country.
