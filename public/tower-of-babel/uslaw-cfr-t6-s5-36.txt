6 C.F.R. § 5.36 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:6:5.36
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-5.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 5 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart will be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the Privacy Act or JRA.
