Determination of Regulatory Review Period for Purposes of Patent 
Extension; Correction of Notices--ANDEMBRY; ANZUPGO; ANKTIVA; BIZENGRI; 
BRINSUPRI; DAYVIGO

AGENCY: Food and Drug Administration, HHS.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is announcing 
corrections to the notices of Determination of Regulatory Review Period 
for Purposes of Patent Extension for ANDEMBRY; ANZUPGO; ANKTIVA; 
BIZENGRI; BRINSUPRI; and DAYVIGO. The original determinations were 
announced in the Federal Registers of August 10, 2026 (BRINSUPRI, 
DAYVIGO), August 17, 2026 (BIZENGRI), and September 9, 2026 (ANDEMBRY, 
ANZUPGO, ANKTIVA). Those notices correctly identified the dates on 
which the testing and approval phases began and ended but incorrectly 
stated the length of the regulatory review period due to calculation 
errors. No other changes are made.

DATES: All due dates for submission of comments, redetermination 
requests, and submission of petitions for due diligence as well as the 
dates used to determine the regulatory review periods for the products 
noted above remain the same as originally published.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Patrick Clouser, Office of the 
Commissioner, Food and Drug Administration, 240-402-5276.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: FDA announced the regulatory review period 
determination for BRINSUPRI in the Federal Register of August 10, 2026
