34 C.F.R. § 75.622 — Definition of “project materials.”
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.622
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.622
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in §§ 75.620 through 75.621, “project materials” means a copyrightable work developed with funds from a grant of the Department. (See 2 CFR 200.307 and 200.315.)
