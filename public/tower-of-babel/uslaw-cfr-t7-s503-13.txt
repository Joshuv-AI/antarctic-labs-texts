7 C.F.R. § 503.13 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:7:503.13
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-503.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 503 (Conduct On Plum Island Animal Disease Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person while in or on the PIADC shall carry firearms or other dangerous or deadly weapons or explosives either openly or concealed, except when authorized to do so for official purposes by the Director, PIADC, or his authorized representative.
