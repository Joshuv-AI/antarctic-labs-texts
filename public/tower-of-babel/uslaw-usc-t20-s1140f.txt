20 U.S.C. § 1140f — Purpose

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part D (Programs To Provide Students With Disabilities With A Quality Higher Education); Subpart 2 (Transition Programs For Students With Intellectual Disabilities Into Higher Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subpart to support model demonstration programs that promote the successful transition of students with intellectual disabilities into higher education.
