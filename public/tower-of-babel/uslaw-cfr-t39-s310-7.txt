39 C.F.R. § 310.7 — Amendment of regulations.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 310 (Enforcement Of The Private Express Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Amendments of the regulations in this part and in part 320 may be made only in accordance with the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act.
