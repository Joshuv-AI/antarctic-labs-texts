41 C.F.R. § 105-60.1004 — -60.1004 Production or disclosure prohibited unless approved by the Appropriate Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-60.1004
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-60.1004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-60 (60—Public Availability Of Agency Records And Informational Materals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No current or former GSA employee shall, in response to a demand, produce any material or disclose, through testimony or other means, any information covered by this subpart, without prior approval of the Appropriate Authority.
