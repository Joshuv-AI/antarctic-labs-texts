46 C.F.R. § 67.43 — Evidence of citizenship.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.43
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When received by the Coast Guard, a properly completed original Application for Initial Issue, Exchange, or Replacement of Certificate of Documentation; or Redocumentation (form CG-1258) establishes a rebuttable presumption that the applicant is a United States citizen.
