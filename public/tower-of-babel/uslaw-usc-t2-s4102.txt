2 U.S.C. § 4102 — Purchase of supplies for Senate and House
Citation: us:usc:2:4102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4102
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 41 (Congressional Officers And Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Supplies for use of the Senate and the House of Representatives may be purchased in accordance with the schedule of contract articles and prices of the Administrator of General Services.
