DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

Drug Enforcement Administration


Mark Allen, D.D.S.; Rescission of Final Agency Action and 
Withdrawal of Order To Show Cause

2026, at 91 FR 13986 (2026). Additionally, I withdraw the Order to Show 
Cause issued to Mark Allen, D.D.S., of Edmond, Oklahoma on November 24, 
2025.
