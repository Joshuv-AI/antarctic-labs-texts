25 C.F.R. § 2.405 — How will the reviewing official notify interested parties of the decision on a request for an appeals bond?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.405
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the reviewing official decides whether to require an appeal bond, she or he will provide the interested parties with written notice of the decision
