42 U.S.C. § 18443 — Eligibility for service of individual currently serving as Administrator of NASA
Citation: us:usc:42:18443
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18443
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 159 (Space Exploration, Technology, And Science); Subchapter XI (Other Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The individual serving in the position of Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration as of October 11, 2010, comes from civilian life and is therefore eligible to serve in such position, in conformance with section 20111 of title 51.
