31 C.F.R. § 566.302 — Correspondent account.
Citation: us:cfr:31:566.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-566.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 566 (Hizballah Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term correspondent account means an account established to receive deposits from, make payments on behalf of, or handle other financial transactions related to a foreign financial institution.
