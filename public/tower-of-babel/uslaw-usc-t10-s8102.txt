10 U.S.C. § 8102 — Regular Navy: retired flag officers on active duty
Citation: us:usc:10:8102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8102
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 811 (Distribution In Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except in time of war or national emergency, not more than ten retired flag officers of the Regular Navy may be on active duty.
