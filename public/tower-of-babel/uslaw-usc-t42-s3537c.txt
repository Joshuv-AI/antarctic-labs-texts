42 U.S.C. § 3537c — Prohibition of lump-sum payments
Citation: us:usc:42:3537c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3537c
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 44 (Department Of Housing And Urban Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In providing relocation assistance in connection with any program administered by the Department of Housing and Urban Development, the Secretary may not make lump-sum payments to any displaced residential tenant, except where necessary to cover—
(1)
moving expenses;
(2)
a downpayment on the purchase of a replacement residence, including a condominium unit or membership in a cooperative housing association; or
(3)
any incidental expenses related to paragraph (1) or (2).
