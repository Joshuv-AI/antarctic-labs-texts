SARNEN, the capital of the western half (or Obwalden) of
the Swiss canton of Unterwalden. It stands 1558 ft. above sea-level,
at the north end of the lake of Sarnen (3 sq. m. in extent)
and on the river Aa. Pop. (1900) 3949. It has a large parish
church and two convents. In the archives is preserved the
famous MS. known from the colour of its binding as the White
Book of Sarnen, which contains one of the earliest known versions
of the Tell legend (see Tell Tell, William). Sarnen is a station on the Brünig
Railway, being 42 m. from Alpnachstad, its port on the lake of
Lucerne. William Augustus Brevoort Coolidge W. A. B. C.
