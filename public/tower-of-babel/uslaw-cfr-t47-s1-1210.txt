47 C.F.R. § 1.1210 — Prohibition on solicitation of presentations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.1210
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.1210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall solicit or encourage others to make any improper presentation under the provisions of this section.
