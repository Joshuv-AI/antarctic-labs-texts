42 U.S.C. § 2371 — Transfer of utilities
Citation: us:usc:42:2371
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2371
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter VI (Utilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is authorized to transfer to one or more of the entities specified in this subchapter such utilities as in the judgment of the Commission will be appropriate to enable the transferee to meet the needs of the residents of the community for adequate utility services of the kind to be transferred.
