29 C.F.R. § 408.7 — Terminal trusteeship financial report.
Citation: us:cfr:29:408.7
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-408.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 408 (Labor Organization Trusteeship Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each labor organization which has assumed trusteeship over a subordinate labor organization shall file within 90 days after the termination of such trusteeship on behalf of the subordinate labor organization a terminal financial report, and one copy, with the Office of Labor-Management Standards, on Form LM-2 Long Form or Form LM-2 and in conformance with the requirements of part 403 of this chapter.
