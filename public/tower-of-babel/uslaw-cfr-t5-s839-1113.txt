5 C.F.R. § 839.1113 — If I elect to change my retirement coverage under the FERCCA, can I change my decision about making a deposit or redeposit for civilian or military service?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.1113
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.1113

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, you or your survivor will have a new opportunity to decide whether to pay any deposits or redeposits.
