41 C.F.R. § 302-4.200 — -4.200 Per diem rate for employee and immediate family members for en route relocation travel within CONUS.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-4.200
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-4.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-4 (4—Allowances For Subsistence And Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The per diem rate for en route relocation travel between the old and new official stations will be at the standard CONUS rate (see applicable FTR Per Diem Rate Bulletins available at https://www.gsa.gov/perdiem). Reimbursement will be in accordance with §§ 301-11.19 through 301-11.22 of this subtitle.
