45 C.F.R. § 1643.5 — Recipient policies and recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1643.5
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1643.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1643 (Restriction On Assisted Suicide, Euthanasia, And Mercy Killing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The recipient shall adopt written policies to guide its staff in complying with this part and shall maintain records sufficient to document the recipient's compliance with this part.
