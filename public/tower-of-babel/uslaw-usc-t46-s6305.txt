46 U.S.C. § 6305 — Reports of investigations
Citation: us:usc:46:6305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section6305
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Marine Casualties); Chapter 63 (Investigating Marine Casualties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall prescribe regulations about the form and manner of reports of investigations conducted under this part.
(b)
Reports of investigations conducted under this part shall be made available to the public. This subsection does not require the release of information described by section 552(b) of title 5 or protected from disclosure by another law of the United States.
