15 U.S.C. § 1460 — Savings provisions
Citation: us:usc:15:1460
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1460
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 39 (Fair Packaging And Labeling Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed to repeal, invalidate, or supersede—
(a)
the Federal Trade Commission Act [15 U.S.C. 41 et seq.] or any statute defined therein as an antitrust Act;
(b)
the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act [21 U.S.C. 301 et seq.]; or
(c)
the Federal Hazardous Substances Labeling Act [15 U.S.C. 1261 et seq.].
