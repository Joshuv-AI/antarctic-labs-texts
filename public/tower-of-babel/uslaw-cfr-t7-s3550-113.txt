7 C.F.R. § 3550.113 — Rates and terms (loans only).
Citation: us:cfr:7:3550.113
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3550.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3550 (Direct Single Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Interest rate. The interest rate for all section 504 loans will be 1 percent.
(b) Loan term. The repayment period for all section 504 loans will be 20 years.
