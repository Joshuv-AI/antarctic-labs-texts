45 C.F.R. § 5.21 — Who can file a FOIA request?
Citation: us:cfr:45:5.21
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-5.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 5 (Freedom Of Information Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual, partnership, corporation, association, or public or private organization other than a Federal agency, regardless of nationality, may submit a FOIA request to us. This includes state and local governments.
