46 C.F.R. § 141.310 — Stowage of survival craft.
Citation: us:cfr:46:141.310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-141.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 141 (Lifesaving). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Survival craft must be stowed in accordance with the requirements of § 199.130 of this chapter, as far as is practicable on existing towing vessels.
