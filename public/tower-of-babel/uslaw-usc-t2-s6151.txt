2 U.S.C. § 6151 — Compensation of Secretaries for Senate Majority and Minority
Citation: us:usc:2:6151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6151
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter IV (Majority And Minority Secretaries, Conferences, And Policy Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary for the Majority of the Senate (other than the incumbent holding office on April 1, 1977) and the Secretary for the Minority of the Senate shall each be paid at an annual rate of compensation of $39,500.
