FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreement under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may submit 
comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the agreement to 
the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, Federal 
Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 20573. 
Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received within 12 
days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, and the 
Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited review. Copies of agreements are 
available through the Commission's website (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of General Counsel at (202) 523-5740 or 
[email protected].
    Agreement No.: 201315-003.
    Agreement Name: NYSA-ILA Assessment Agreement.
    Parties: International Longshoremen's Association, AFL-CIO; and New 
York Shipping Association, Inc.
    Filing Party: Richard Ciampi, The Lambos Firm, LLP.
    Synopsis: The Amendment simplifies reporting and auditing by 
eliminating the measurement ton concept and moving to a weight ton 
basis. The Amendment also clarifies assessments on certain new from the 
manufacturer vehicles under 15,000 pounds (autos, trucks and RVs) which 
are to be assessed on a unit rate versus used vehicles and other 
machinery which are to be assessed on a weight ton basis. Loaded Mafis 
are to be assessed on a unit basis.
    Proposed Effective Date: 10/14/2026.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/eAgreementsSP/Public/AgreementHistory/22427.

    Dated: October 16, 2026.
Jennifer Everling,
Assistant Secretary.
