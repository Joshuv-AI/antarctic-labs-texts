12 C.F.R. § 150.560 — When may the OCC revoke my fiduciary powers?
Citation: us:cfr:12:150.560
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-150.560
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 150 (Fiduciary Powers Of Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OCC may revoke your fiduciary powers if it determines that you have done any of the following:
(a) Exercised those fiduciary powers unlawfully or unsoundly.
(b) Failed to exercise those fiduciary powers for five consecutive years.
(c) Otherwise failed to follow the requirements of this part.
