43 U.S.C. § 439 — Cultivation requirement as to entrymen
Citation: us:usc:43:439
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section439
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter VI (Water Right Applications And Land Entries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The entryman upon lands to be irrigated shall, in addition to compliance with the homestead laws, reclaim at least one-half of the total irrigable area of his entry for agricultural purposes.
