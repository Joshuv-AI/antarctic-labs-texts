29 C.F.R. § 4233.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4233.1
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4233.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4233 (Partitions Of Eligible Multiemployer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe rules governing applications for partition under section 4233 of ERISA, and related notice requirements.
