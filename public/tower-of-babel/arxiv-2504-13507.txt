On ℓ − \ell- regular and 2 − 2- color partition triples modulo powers of 3 3
B. Hemanthkumar 1, D. S. Gireesh 2

Abstract
. Let T ℓ ​ ( n ) T_{\ell}(n) denote the number of ℓ − \ell- regular partition triples of n n and let p ℓ , 3 ​ ( n ) p_{\ell,3}(n) enumerates the number of 2–color partition triples of n n where one of the colors appear only in parts that are multiples of ℓ \ell . In this paper, we prove several infinite families of congruences modulo powers of 3 for T ℓ ​ ( n ) T_{\ell}(n) and p ℓ , 3 ​ ( n ) p_{\ell,3}(n) , where ℓ ≥ 1 \ell\geq 1 and ≡ 0 ( mod 3 k ) \equiv 0\pmod{3^{k}} , and ≡ ± 3 k ( mod 3 k + 1 ) \equiv\pm 3^{k}\pmod{3^{k+1}} .

1.
Introduction

Let
p_{3}(n)
denotes the number of partitions of
n
in three colors, given by

(1.1)

\sum_{n\geq 0}p_{3}(n)q^{n}=\frac{1}{E(q)^{3}}.

Here and throughout the paper, we set

E(q^{r}):=(q^{r};q^{r})_{\infty}=\prod\limits_{m=1}^{\infty}(1-q^{rm}).

In
[
3
]
, Hirschhorn showed that for
\alpha\geq 0,

(1.2)

\sum_{n\geq 0}p_{3}\left(3^{2\alpha+1}n+\frac{5\times 3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{n}=\sum_{j\geq 1}x_{2\alpha+1,j}q^{j-1}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12j-3}}{E(q)^{12j}}

and

(1.3)

\sum_{n\geq 0}p_{3}\left(3^{2\alpha+2}n+\frac{7\times 3^{2\alpha+2}+1}{8}\right)q^{n}=\sum_{j\geq 1}x_{2\alpha+2,j}q^{j-1}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12j}}{E(q)^{12j+3}}.

where the coefficient vectors
\mathbf{x}_{k}=(x_{k,1},x_{k,2},\dots)
are given by

\mathbf{x}_{1}=\left(x_{1,1},x_{1,2},\dots\right)=\left(9,0,0,\dots\right),

and for
k\geq 1
,

x_{k+1,i}=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\sum_{j\geq 1}x_{k,j}\,m_{4i,i+j},&\text{if $k$ is odd},\\
\displaystyle\sum_{j\geq 1}x_{k,j}\,m_{4i+1,i+j},&\text{if $k$ is even},\end{cases}

where the first five rows of
M=\left(m_{i,j}\right)_{i,j\geq 1}
are

\begin{bmatrix}3^{2}&0&0&0&0&0&\dots\\
2\times 3&3^{5}&0&0&0&0&\dots\\
1&3^{5}&3^{8}&0&0&0&\dots\\
0&2\times 3^{2}\times 5&2^{2}\times 3^{7}&3^{11}&0&0&\dots\\
0&3\times 5&2^{2}\times 3^{5}\times 5&3^{10}\times 5&3^{14}&0&\dots\end{bmatrix}

and for
i>3
,
m_{i,1}=0
, and for
j\geq 2
,

(1.4)

m_{i,j}=27m_{i-1,j-1}+9m_{i-2,j-1}+m_{i-3,j-1}.

For any positive integer
\ell
, a partition is called
\ell
-regular if none of its parts are divisible by
\ell
. Let
T_{\ell}(n)
enumerate the number of
\ell
-regular partition triples of
n
. It is given by

(1.5)

\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{\ell}(n)q^{n}=\frac{E(q^{\ell})^{3}}{E(q)^{3}}.

Recently, Gireesh and Naika
[
2
]
found infinite family of congruences for
T_{3}(n)
. They showed that for
\beta\geq 0
and
n\geq 0

(1.6)

T_{3}\left(3^{2\beta+1}n+\frac{3^{2\beta+2}-1}{4}\right)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+2}}.

Baruah and Das
[
1
]
extented these results to 9 and 27–regular partition triples. For each
\beta,n\geq 0
and
r\in\{7,11\},
they proved that

(1.7)

\displaystyle T_{9}(3^{\beta+1}(n+1)-1)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+2}},

(1.8)

\displaystyle T_{9}(3^{\beta+2}n+2\cdot 3^{\beta+1}-1)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+3}},

(1.9)

\displaystyle T_{27}\left(3^{2\beta+3}n+\frac{3^{2\beta+4}-13}{4}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+5}},

and

(1.10)

T_{27}\left(3^{2\beta+4}n+\frac{r\cdot 3^{2\beta+3}-13}{4}\right)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+7}}.

Further, they conjectured that for all
\ell,n\geq 0
and
k\geq 1
,

(1.11)

T_{3^{2k}}\left(3^{\ell+2k-1}n+3^{\ell+2k-1}-\frac{3^{2k}-1}{8}\right)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{3k+2\ell-1}}

and

(1.12)

T_{3^{2k-1}}\left(3^{2\ell+2k-1}n+\frac{2\cdot 3^{2\ell+2k}-3^{2k-1}+1}{8}\right)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{3k+2\ell-1}}.

The
2-
color partition triple function is defined by

(1.13)

\sum_{n\geq 0}p_{\ell,3}(n)q^{n}=\frac{1}{E(q)^{3}E(q^{\ell})^{3}}.

In
[
4
]
, Tang proved congruences modulo powers of 3 for 2–color partition triples. In particular, he proved that

(1.14)

\displaystyle p_{3,3}\left(3^{\beta+1}+\frac{3^{\beta+1}+1}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{3^{\beta+2}},

(1.15)

\displaystyle p_{9,3}\left(3^{2\beta+1}n+\frac{3^{2\beta+1}+5}{4}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+2}},

and

(1.16)

p_{9,3}\left(3^{2\beta+2}n+\frac{3^{2\beta+3}+5}{4}\right)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2\beta+4}}.

2.
Preliminaries

To establish the main results of this paper, we first introduce the necessary notation and lemmas. We first introduce a huffing operator
H
, which is defined as

H\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}q^{n}\right)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{3n}q^{3n}.

Lemma 2.1
.

[
3
, (2.23)]

Let
\zeta=\frac{E(q)^{3}}{qE(q^{9})^{3}}
and
T=\frac{E(q^{3})^{12}}{q^{3}E(q^{9})^{12}}
. Then

(2.1)

H\left(\frac{1}{\zeta^{i}}\right)=\sum_{j\geq 1}\frac{m_{i,j}}{T^{j}}.

For a positive integer
n
, let
\pi(n)
be the highest power of
3
that divides
n
, and define
\pi(0)=+\infty
.

Lemma 2.2

(
[
3
]
and
[
4
]
)
.

For each
i,j\geq 1
, we have

(2.2)

\pi\left(m_{i,j}\right)\geq\left\lfloor\frac{9j-3i-1}{2}\right\rfloor.

Proof.

It follows easily from (
1.4
) and induction.
∎

Lemma 2.3
.

[
3
, (4.6) and (4.7)]

For any integers
\alpha\geq 0
and
j\geq 1
, we have

(2.3)

\pi({x_{2\alpha+1,j}})\geq 3\alpha+2+\left\lfloor\frac{9j-10}{2}\right\rfloor+\delta_{j,1}

and

(2.4)

\pi({x_{2\alpha+2,j}})\geq 3\alpha+4+\left\lfloor\frac{9j-10}{2}\right\rfloor+\delta_{j,1},

where

\delta_{j,1}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if $j=1$},\\
0&\text{if $j\neq 1$}.\end{cases}

Lemma 2.4
.

For all
i\geq 1
, we have

(2.5)

\displaystyle H\left(q^{i-3}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12i}}{E(q)^{12i}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 1}m_{4i,i+j}\,q^{3j-3}\frac{E(q^{9})^{12j}}{E(q^{3})^{12j}},

(2.6)

\displaystyle H\left(q^{i-2}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12i-3}}{E(q)^{12i+3}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 1}m_{4i+1,i+j}\,q^{3j-3}\frac{E(q^{9})^{12j-3}}{E(q^{3})^{12j+3}},

(2.7)

\displaystyle H\left(q^{i-3}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12i-9}}{E(q)^{12i-9}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 1}m_{4i-3,i+j-1}\,q^{3j-3}\frac{E(q^{9})^{12j-3}}{E(q^{3})^{12j-3}},

(2.8)

\displaystyle H\left(q^{i-1}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12i-3}}{E(q)^{12i-3}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 1}m_{4i-1,i+j-1}\,q^{3j-3}\frac{E(q^{9})^{12j-9}}{E(q^{3})^{12j-9}},

We can rewrite (
2.1
) as

From (

for

, we see that

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=i+1}^{\infty}m_{4i,j}\,q^{3j}\frac{E(q^{9})^{12j}}{E(q^{3})^{12j}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}m_{4i,j+i}\,q^{3j+3i}\frac{E(q^{9})^{12j+12i}}{E(q^{3})^{12j+12i}}

which yields (

2.9

∎

Theorem 3.1
.

For each
\alpha\geq 0
and
\beta\geq 0
, we have

(3.1)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}\left(3^{2\alpha+2\beta+1}n+\frac{2\cdot 3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}-3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{n}=\sum_{j\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{2\beta+1,j}q^{j-1}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12j-3}}{E(q)^{12j-3}},

(3.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}\left(3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}n+\frac{2\cdot 3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}-3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{n}=\sum_{j\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{2\beta+2,j}q^{j-1}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12j-9}}{E(q)^{12j-9}}.

where the coefficient vectors are defined as follows:

(3.3)

and

(3.4)

\displaystyle\sum_{i\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{\mu,i}m_{4i-3,i+j-1}\,\,\ &\text{if $\mu$ is even},\end{cases}

for all
\mu,j\geq 1
.

Proof.

From (
1.5
), we have

\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}(n)q^{n}=E\left(q^{3^{2\alpha+1}}\right)^{3}\sum_{n\geq 0}p_{3}(n)q^{n}.

Extracting the terms involving

on both sides of (

) and dividing throughout by

, we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}\left(3^{2\alpha+1}n+\frac{5\times 3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{3^{2\alpha+1}n}=E\left(q^{3^{2\alpha+1}}\right)^{3}\sum_{n\geq 0}p_{3}\left(3^{2\alpha+1}n+\frac{5\times 3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{3^{2\alpha+1}n}.

If we replace

by
q
and use (
1.2
), we get

(3.7)

\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}\left(3^{2\alpha+1}n+\frac{5\times 3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{n}=\sum_{j\geq 1}x_{2\alpha+1,j}q^{j-1}\frac{E(q^{3})^{12j-3}}{E(q)^{12j-3}},

which is the case
\beta=0
of (
3.1
).

We now assume that (
3.1
) is true for some integer
\beta\geq 0
. Applying the operator
H
to both sides, by (
2.8
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}\left(3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}n+\frac{2\cdot 3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}-3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{3n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{2\beta+1,i}\sum_{j\geq 1}m_{4i-1,i+j-1}q^{3j-3}\left(\frac{E(q^{9})^{3}}{E(q^{3})^{3}}\right)^{4j-3}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 1}\left(\sum_{i\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{2\beta+1,i}m_{4i-1,i+j-1}\right)q^{3j-3}\left(\frac{E(q^{9})^{3}}{E(q^{3})^{3}}\right)^{4j-3}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{2\beta+2,j}q^{3j-3}\left(\frac{E(q^{9})^{3}}{E(q^{3})^{3}}\right)^{4j-3}.

If we now replace
q^{3}
by
q
, we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}T_{3^{2\alpha+1}}\left(3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}n+\frac{2\cdot 3^{2\alpha+2\beta+2}-3^{2\alpha+1}+1}{8}\right)q^{n-2}=\sum_{j\geq 1}r^{(2\alpha+1)}_{2\beta+2,j}q^{j-3}\left(\frac{E(q^{3})^{3}}{E(q)^{3}}\right)^{4j-3},

which is (
3.2
).

Now suppose (
3.2
) holds. If we operate
H
on both sides of the equation, by (
