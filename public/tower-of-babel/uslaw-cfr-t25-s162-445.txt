25 C.F.R. § 162.445 — May the parties amend a business lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.445
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.445
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The parties may amend a business lease by obtaining:
(a) The lessee's signature;
(b) The Indian landowners' consent under the requirements in § 162.446; and
(c) BIA approval of the amendment under §§ 162.447 and 162.448.
