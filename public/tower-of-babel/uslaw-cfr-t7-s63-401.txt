7 C.F.R. § 63.401 — Use of information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:63.401
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-63.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 63 (National Sheep Industry Improvement Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information from records or reports required pursuant to this part shall be made available to the Secretary as is appropriate for the administration or enforcement of the Act or rules and regulation issued thereunder.
