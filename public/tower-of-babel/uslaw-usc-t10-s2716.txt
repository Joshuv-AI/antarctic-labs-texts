10 U.S.C. § 2716 — Budget justification document for funding relating to perfluoroalkyl substances and polyfluoroalkyl substances
Citation: us:usc:10:2716
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2716
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 160 (Environmental Restoration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall submit to Congress, concurrent with the submission to Congress of the budget of the President for each fiscal year pursuant to section 1105(a) of title 31, a separate budget justification document that consolidates all information pertaining to activities of the Department of Defense relating to perfluoroalkyl substances or polyfluoroalkyl substances, including funding for and descriptions of—
(1)
research and development efforts;
(2)
testing;
(3)
remediation;
(4)
contaminant disposal; and
(5)
community outreach.
