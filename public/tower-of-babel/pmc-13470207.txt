Emotional words evoke region- and valence-specific patterns of concurrent neuromodulator release in human thalamus and cortex

There are two typos in the STAR Methods section under the section heading “rodent electrochemistry and optical stimulation” under the subheading “in vivo data acquisition.” The error specifically applies to coordinates for viral injections used for our supplemental in vivo electrochemical validation data in mice. The affected text is included below:

“…in midbrain dopaminergic cells (from bregma, AP: –3.16mm, ML: ±1.02mm, DV: –4.5mm). An optic fiber (400μm, Doric) was positioned 75μM above the site of viral infusion to enable light delivery to activate this opsin. DBH-Cre mice (Figures S1M–S1P, N = 3) were injected with an AAV to drive Cre-recombinase-dependent expression of ChR2 (ChR2, AAV-DIO-hChR2(H134R)-EYFP, Addgene viral prep # 26973-AAV9) in noradrenergic locus coeruleus (LC) cells (from bregma, AP: –1.34mm, ML: ±0.3mm, DV: –2.1mm).”

The correct coordinates used for these studies are AP: –3.16mm, ML: ±1.35mm, and DV: –4.2mm for midbrain dopaminergic cells and AP: −5.40mm, ML: ± 0.95mm, and DV: −3.7mm for noradrenergic cells of the locus coeruleus.

Because of the time that has passed since this paper’s initial publication, the typos cannot be corrected in the article itself, but this notice will serve as a correction to the record and as a resource to readers. These typos have no impact on the manuscript’s data, analyses, results, or figures in the main text or supplementals. The authors apologize for the error.
