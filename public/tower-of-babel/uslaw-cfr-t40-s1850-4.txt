40 C.F.R. § 1850.4 — Public reading room.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1850.4
Creator: Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1850.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1850 (Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council maintains an electronic public reading room on its Web site, http://www.restorethegulf.gov, which contains the records FOIA requires the Council to make available for public inspection and copying, as well as additional records of interest to the public.
