42 U.S.C. § 256f — Designated dental health professional shortage area
Citation: us:usc:42:256f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section256f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part D (Primary Health Care); Subpart x (Primary Dental Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subpart, the term “designated dental health professional shortage area” means an area, population group, or facility that is designated by the Secretary as a dental health professional shortage area under section 254e of this title or designated by the applicable State as having a dental health professional shortage.
