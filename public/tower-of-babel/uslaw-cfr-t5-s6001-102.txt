5 C.F.R. § 6001.102 — Agency designees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:6001.102
Creator: Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-6001.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 6001 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Department Of Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of 5 CFR part 2635, the following Department of Transportation officials are agency designees within the meaning of 5 CFR 2635.102(b):
(a) The Designated Agency Ethics Official;
(b) The Alternate Agency Ethics Official;
(c) The Deputy Ethics Officials; and
(d) As designated by Deputy Ethics Officials, legal counsel in regional and other offices.
