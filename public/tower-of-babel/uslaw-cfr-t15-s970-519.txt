15 C.F.R. § 970.519 — Resource conservation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:15:970.519
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-970.519
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 970 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of conservation of natural resources, each license issued under this part will contain, as needed, terms, conditions and restrictions which have due regard for the prevention of waste and the future opportunity for the commercial recovery of the unrecovered balance of the hard mineral resources in the license area. The Administrator will establish these pursuant to § 970.603.
