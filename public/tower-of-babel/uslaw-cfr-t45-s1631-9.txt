45 C.F.R. § 1631.9 — Applicability of part 1630 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1631.9
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1631.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1631 (Purchasing And Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All purchases and leases of personal property and contracts for services made with LSC funds must comply with the provisions of 45 CFR part 1630 (Cost Standards and Procedures).
