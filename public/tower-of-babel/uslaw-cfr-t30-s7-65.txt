30 C.F.R. § 7.65 — Critical characteristics.
Citation: us:cfr:30:7.65
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-7.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 7 (Testing By Applicant Or Third Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following critical characteristics shall be inspected or tested on each blasting unit to which an approval marking is affixed:
(a) The output current.
(b) The voltage cut-off time.
(c) The components that control voltage and current through each making and breaking electric contact.
(d) Operation of the visual indicator and the firing switch.
