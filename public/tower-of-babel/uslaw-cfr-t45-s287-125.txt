45 C.F.R. § 287.125 — What supportive and job retention services may be provided under the NEW Program?
Citation: us:cfr:45:287.125
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-287.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 287 (The Native Employment Works (New) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NEW Program grantee may provide, pay for or reimburse expenses for supportive services, including but not limited to transportation, child care, traditional or cultural work related services, and other work or family sufficiency related expenses that the Tribe determines are necessary to enable a client to participate in the program.
