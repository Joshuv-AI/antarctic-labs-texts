On a family of sums of powers of the floor function and their links with generalized Dedekind sums
Steven Brown

Abstract
In this paper we are concerned with a family of sums involving the floor function. With r r a non negative integer and n n and m m positive integers we consider the sums 𝐒 r ​ ( n , m ) := ∑ k = 1 n − 1 ⌊ k ​ m n ⌋ r \mathbf{S}_{r}\left(n,m\right):=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}{\left\lfloor\frac{km}{n}\right\rfloor}^{r} While a formula for 𝐒 1 \mathbf{S}_{1} is well known, we provide closed-form formulas for 𝐒 2 \mathbf{S}_{2} and 𝐒 3 \mathbf{S}_{3} as well as the reciprocity laws they satisfy. Additionally, one can find a closed-form formula for the classical Dedekind sum using the Euclidean algorithm. Finally, we provide a general formula for 𝐒 r \mathbf{S}_{r} showing its dependency on generalized Dedekind sums.

u,v,w\geq 0\end{subarray}}\binom{r}{u,v,w}a^{u}(-1)^{v}\left(\frac{a-1}{2}\right)^{w}\frac{2^{u+v}b^{u+v-1}}{2^{u+v}b^{u+v-1}}\sum_{k=1}^{b-1}\left(\!\!\!\left(\frac{k}{b}\right)\!\!\!\right)^{u}\left(\!\!\!\left(\frac{ka}{b}\right)\!\!\!\right)^{v}
