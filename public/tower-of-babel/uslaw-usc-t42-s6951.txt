42 U.S.C. § 6951 — Functions
Citation: us:usc:42:6951
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6951
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter V (Duties Of Secretary Of Commerce In Resource And Recovery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce shall encourage greater commercialization of proven resource recovery technology by providing—
(1)
accurate specifications for recovered materials;
(2)
stimulation of development of markets for recovered materials;
(3)
promotion of proven technology; and
(4)
a forum for the exchange of technical and economic data relating to resource recovery facilities.
