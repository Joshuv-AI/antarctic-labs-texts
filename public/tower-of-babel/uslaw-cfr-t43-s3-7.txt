43 C.F.R. § 3.7 — Permit to become void.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3 (Preservation Of American Antiquities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to begin work under a permit within 6 months after it is granted, or failure to diligently prosecute such work after it has been begun, shall make the permit void without any order or proceeding by the Secretary having jurisdiction.
