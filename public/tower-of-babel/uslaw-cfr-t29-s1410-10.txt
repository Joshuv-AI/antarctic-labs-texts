29 C.F.R. § 1410.10 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1410.10
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1410.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1410 (Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who knowingly and willfully requests or obtains any record concerning an individual from the Service under false pretenses shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and fined not more than $5,000.
