26 U.S.C. § 4904 — Liability in case of different businesses of same ownership and location
Citation: us:usc:26:4904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4904
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 40 (General Provisions Relating To Occupational Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever more than one of the pursuits or occupations described in this subtitle are carried on in the same place by the same person at the same time, except as otherwise provided in this subtitle, the tax shall be paid for each according to the rates severally prescribed.
