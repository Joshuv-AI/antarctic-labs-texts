9 C.F.R. § 354.72 — Packaging.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.72
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No container which bears or may bear any official identification or any abbreviation or copy or representation thereof may be filled in whole or in part except with edible products which were inspected and certified and are, at the time of such filing, sound, wholesome, and fit for human food. All such filling of containers shall be under the supervision of an inspector.
