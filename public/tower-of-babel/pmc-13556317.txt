Sodium-ion-responsive injectable supramolecular hydrogel delivers engineered Salmonella for safer and enhanced tumor immunotherapy
Authors: Wenliang Yu, Wei Tang, Tao Zhang, Mengyue Gao, Lina Liu, Xiaoran Zhou, Zeyang Liu, Ziyi Tan, Yao Li, Yuning Meng, Siyu Li, Chenyang Li, Congrui Guo, Kaiting Zheng, Zichun Hua

In the originally published version of this article, there were errors present in the figures that occurred during the figure polishing and formatting process. In Figures 4G, 4H, and 8E, the scale bars were incorrectly labeled as 5 μm; the correct unit should be 5 mm (millimeters). In Figure 8B, the label “VNP-TNF-α nbP” should be “VNP-TNF-α nb.” In Figures 8C and 8L, the y-axis unit was mistakenly labeled as cm3; the correct unit should be mm3 (cubic millimeters).

Additionally, in the “Ethics statement and patient consent” section, one ethics approval was omitted. The corrected statement should read as follows:

“All animal experiments were approved by the Affidavit of Approval of Animal Ethical and Welfare of Nanjing University (approval number: IACUC-D2403138) and the Xinxiang Medical University Ethics Committee (approval number: XYLL-20250399) and were carried out in compliance with all relevant ethical regulations.”

These errors have been corrected in the article online, and these corrections do not affect the description, interpretation, or conclusions of the paper. The authors apologize for these errors.
