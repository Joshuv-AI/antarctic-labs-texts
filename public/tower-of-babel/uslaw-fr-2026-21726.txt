Agency Information Collection Activities; 30 CFR 822--Special

AGENCY: Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of information collection; request for comment.

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, the 
Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement (OSM) is proposing 
to renew an information collection. The information collected is used 
to verify that alluvial valley floors are protected in accordance with 
statutory requirements.

    Abstract: This information collection, which is entitled, ``30 CFR 
822--Special Permanent Program Performance Standards-Operations in 
Alluvial Valley Floors'' (OMB Control Number 1029-0049) implements 
sections 510(b)(5) and 515(b)(10)(F) of the Surface Mining Control and 
Reclamation Act of 1977 (SMCRA), which protect alluvial valley floors 
(AVFs) west of the 100th meridian from the adverse impacts of surface 
coal mining operations. The information collected is used to verify 
that AVFs are protected in accordance with SMCRA requirements. Under 30 
CFR 822.13, permittees within these areas must install, maintain, and 
operate a monitoring system to safeguard these unique hydrologic areas. 
The regulatory authority is responsible for reviewing the monitoring 
data the permittee submits.
