20 U.S.C. § 6064 — Contraceptive devices
Citation: us:usc:20:6064
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6064
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 68 (National Education Reform); Subchapter X (Miscellaneous); Part A (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Health and Human Services and the Department of Education shall ensure that all federally funded programs which provide for the distribution of contraceptive devices to unemancipated minors develop procedures to encourage, to the extent practical, family participation in such programs.
