36 U.S.C. § 170307 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:170307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section170307
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1703 (Pearl Harbor Survivors Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its regional districts and local branches have the exclusive right to use the name “Pearl Harbor Survivors Association” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
