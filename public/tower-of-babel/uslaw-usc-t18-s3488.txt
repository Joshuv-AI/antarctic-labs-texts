18 U.S.C. § 3488 — Intoxicating liquor in Indian country as evidence of unlawful introduction
Citation: us:usc:18:3488
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3488
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 223 (Witnesses And Evidence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The possession by a person of intoxicating liquors in Indian country where the introduction is prohibited by treaty or Federal statute shall be prima facie evidence of unlawful introduction.
