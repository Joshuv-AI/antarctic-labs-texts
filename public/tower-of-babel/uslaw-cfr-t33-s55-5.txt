33 C.F.R. § 55.5 — Who is eligible for child development services?
Citation: us:cfr:33:55.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-55.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 55 (Child Development Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Coast Guard members and civilian Coast Guard employees are eligible for the child developmental services described in this subpart. As space is available, members of the other Armed Forces and other Federal civilian employees are also eligible.
