20 C.F.R. § 366.5 — Consideration of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:20:366.5
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-366.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 366 (Collection Of Debts By Federal Tax Refund Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence submitted by the debtor will be considered only by officials or employees of the Board and a determination that an amount of such debt is past-due and legally enforceable will be made only by such officials or employees.
