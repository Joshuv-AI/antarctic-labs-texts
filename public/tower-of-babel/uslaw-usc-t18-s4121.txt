18 U.S.C. § 4121 — Federal Prison Industries; board of directors
Citation: us:usc:18:4121
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section4121
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part III (Prisons And Prisoners); Chapter 307 (Employment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

“Federal Prison Industries”, a government corporation of the District of Columbia, shall be administered by a board of six directors, appointed by the President to serve at the will of the President without compensation.
The directors shall be representatives of (1) industry, (2) labor, (3) agriculture, (4) retailers and consumers, (5) the Secretary of Defense, and (6) the Attorney General, respectively.
