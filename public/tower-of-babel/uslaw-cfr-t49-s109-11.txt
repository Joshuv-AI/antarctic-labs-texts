49 C.F.R. § 109.11 — Assistance of properly qualified personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:49:109.11
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-109.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 109 (Department Of Transportation Hazardous Material Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agent may authorize properly qualified personnel to assist in the activities conducted under this part if the agent is not properly qualified to perform a function that is essential to the agent's exercise of authority under this part or when safety might otherwise be compromised by the agent's performance of such a function.
