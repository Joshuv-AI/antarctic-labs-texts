30 C.F.R. § 35.23 — Performance required for certification.
Citation: us:cfr:30:35.23
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-35.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 35 (Fire-Resistant Hydraulic Fluids). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To qualify as fire-resistant under the regulations of this part, a hydraulic fluid shall meet each performance requirement and stated in §§ 35.20(d), 35.21(d), and 35.22(d).
