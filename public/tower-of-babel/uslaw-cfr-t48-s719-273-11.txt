48 C.F.R. § 719.273-11 — 719.273-11 Solicitation provision and contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:719.273-11
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-719.273-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 719 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The contracting officer shall insert the provision at (48 CFR) AIDAR 752.219-70 in all unrestricted solicitations exceeding $550,000 ($1,000,000 for construction) that offer subcontracting opportunities.
(b) The contracting officer shall insert the clause at (48 CFR) AIDAR 752.219-71 in all contracts where the prime contractor has signed a Mentor-Protégé Agreement with USAID.
