36 U.S.C. § 70704 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:70704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70704
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 707 (Frederick Douglass Memorial And Historical Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend bylaws for the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
employ persons the corporation considers necessary;
(4)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property;
(5)
sue and be sued; and
(6)
do any other act to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
