28 C.F.R. § 545.30 — Funds due deceased inmates.
Citation: us:cfr:28:545.30
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-545.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 545 (Work And Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds due a deceased inmate for work performed and not yet paid shall be made to a legal representative of the inmate's estate or in accordance with the laws of descent and distribution of the state of the inmate's domicile.
