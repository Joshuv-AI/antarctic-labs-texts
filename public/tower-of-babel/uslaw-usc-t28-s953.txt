28 U.S.C. § 953 — Administration of oaths and acknowledgments
Citation: us:usc:28:953
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section953
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 57 (General Provisions Applicable To Court Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each clerk of court and his deputies may administer oaths and affirmations and take acknowledgments.
