30 C.F.R. § 56.20011 — Barricades and warning signs.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.20011
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.20011
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Areas where health or safety hazards exist that are not immediately obvious to employees shall be barricaded, or warning signs shall be posted at all approaches. Warning signs shall be readily visible, legible, and display the nature of the hazard and any protective action required.
