2 C.F.R. § 180.610 — What procedures does a Federal agency use in suspension and debarment actions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:180.610
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-180.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 180 (Omb Guidelines To Agencies On Government-Wide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In deciding whether to suspend or debar you, a Federal agency handles the actions as informally as practicable, consistent with principles of fundamental fairness.
(a) For suspension actions, a Federal agency uses the procedures in this subpart and subpart G.
(b) For debarment actions, a Federal agency uses the procedures in this subpart and subpart H.
