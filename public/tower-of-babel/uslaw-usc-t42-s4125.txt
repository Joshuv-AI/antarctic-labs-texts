42 U.S.C. § 4125 — Finality of certain financial transactions
Citation: us:usc:42:4125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4125
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 50 (National Flood Insurance); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law—
(1)
any financial transaction authorized to be carried out under this chapter, and
(2)
any payment authorized to be made or to be received in connection with any such financial transaction,
shall be final and conclusive upon all officers of the Government.
