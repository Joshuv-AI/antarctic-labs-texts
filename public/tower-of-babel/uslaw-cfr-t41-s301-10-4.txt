41 C.F.R. § 301-10.4 — -10.4 Liability for unauthorized or indirect travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.4
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees will be reimbursed for only the constructive cost of traveling to their destination using the authorized method of transportation and by the usually traveled route, unless their agency authorizes a different route as officially necessary. Any additional expenses incurred will be borne by the employee.
