30 U.S.C. § 503 — Reservations required by law; atomic energy materials
Citation: us:usc:30:503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section503
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 11 (Mining Claims On Lands Subject To Mineral Leasing Laws). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rights under any mining claim given force and effect by this chapter shall also be subject to the reservation to the United States specified in section 5(b)(7) of the Atomic Energy Act of 1946, as amended, and, in addition, any reservation or reservations required by any other provision or provisions of law; and any patent issued for such mining claim shall contain such reservations.
