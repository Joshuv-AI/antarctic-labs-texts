11 U.S.C. § 558 — Defenses of the estate
Citation: us:usc:11:558
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section558
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 5 (Creditors, The Debtor, And The Estate); Subchapter III (The Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The estate shall have the benefit of any defense available to the debtor as against any entity other than the estate, including statutes of limitation, statutes of frauds, usury, and other personal defenses. A waiver of any such defense by the debtor after the commencement of the case does not bind the estate.
