7 U.S.C. § 3319h — Funds for research facilities
Citation: us:usc:7:3319h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3319h
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 64 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Teaching); Subchapter X (Funding And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fiscal year 2003 and thereafter, funds may be received from any State, other political subdivision, organization, or individual for the purpose of establishing any research facility of the Agricultural Research Service, as authorized by law.
