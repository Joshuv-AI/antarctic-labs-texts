7 C.F.R. § 1944.265 — Reserve for supplemental adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1944.265
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1944.265
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1944 (Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary concerned may reserve funds subject to section 802(o). Requests to utilize supplemental funds by the grantee shall be transmitted to the Secretary concerned in such form as may be required.
