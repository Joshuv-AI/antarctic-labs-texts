7 U.S.C. § 5605 — Independence of authorities
Citation: us:usc:7:5605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5605
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each authority granted under this chapter shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any authority granted to the Secretary or the Commodity Credit Corporation under any other provision of law.
