43 C.F.R. § 5473.1 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5473.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5473.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5470 (Contract Modification—Extension—Assignment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to be considered, written requests for extension shall be delivered to the appropriate BLM office prior to the expiration of the time for cutting and removal.
