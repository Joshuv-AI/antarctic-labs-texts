21 U.S.C. § 360ddd–2 — Inapplicability of drug fees to designated medical gases
Citation: us:usc:21:360ddd–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section360ddd–2
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter V (Drugs And Devices); Part G (Medical Gases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A designated medical gas, alone or in combination with another designated gas or gases (as medically appropriate) deemed under section 360ddd–1 of this title to have in effect an approved application shall not be assessed fees under section 379h(a) or 379j–12(a) of this title on the basis of such deemed approval.
