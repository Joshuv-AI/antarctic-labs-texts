46 C.F.R. § 9.10 — Waiting time.
Citation: us:cfr:46:9.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-9.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 9 (Extra Compensation For Overtime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The same construction should be given the act when charging for waiting time as governs the charge for services actually rendered. No charge should be made unless after having reported for duty the waiting time amounts to at least one hour.
