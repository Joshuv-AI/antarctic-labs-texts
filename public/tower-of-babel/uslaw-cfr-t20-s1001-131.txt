20 C.F.R. § 1001.131 — Secretary's annual report to Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:20:1001.131
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1001.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1001 (Services For Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall report, after the end of each program year, on the success of the Department and State agencies in carrying out the provisions of this part.
