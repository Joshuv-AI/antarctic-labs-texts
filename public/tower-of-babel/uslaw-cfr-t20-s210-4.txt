20 C.F.R. § 210.4 — Year of service.
Citation: us:cfr:20:210.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-210.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 210 (Creditable Railroad Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A year of service is twelve months of reported or deemed service, consecutive or not consecutive. A fraction of a year of service is taken at its actual value.
(b) The term years of service means the total number of years an employee is credited with service as defined in § 210.2 of this part.
