15 U.S.C. § 9022 — Flexibility in paying reimbursement
Citation: us:usc:15:9022
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9022
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 116 (Coronavirus Economic Stabilization (Cares Act)); Subchapter II (Unemployment Insurance Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor may issue clarifying guidance to allow States to interpret their State unemployment compensation laws in a manner that would provide maximum flexibility to reimbursing employers as it relates to timely payment and assessment of penalties and interest pursuant to such State laws.
