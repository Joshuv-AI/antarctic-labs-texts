30 C.F.R. § 937.779 — Surface mining permit applications—minimum requirements for information on environmental resources.
Citation: us:cfr:30:937.779
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-937.779
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 937 (Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 779 of this chapter, Surface Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements for Information on Environmental Resources, shall apply to any person who makes application to conduct surface mining and reclamation operations.
