21 U.S.C. § 1045 — Limitation on entry of eggs and egg products and other materials into official plants
Citation: us:usc:21:1045
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section1045
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 15 (Egg Products Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may limit the entry of eggs and egg products and other materials into official plants under such conditions as he may prescribe to assure that allowing the entry of such articles into such plants will be consistent with the purposes of this chapter.
