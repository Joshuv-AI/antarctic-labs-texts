30 C.F.R. § 942.845 — Civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:30:942.845
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-942.845
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 942 (Tennessee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 845 of this chapter, Civil Penalties, shall apply to the assessment of civil penalties for violations on coal exploration and surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
