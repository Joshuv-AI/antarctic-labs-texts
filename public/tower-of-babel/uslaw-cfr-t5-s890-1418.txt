5 C.F.R. § 890.1418 — No continuation of FEHB enrollment in compensationer status past 365 days.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.1418
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.1418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribal employee who is not also a Federal employee who becomes eligible for one of the Department of Labor's disability compensation programs may not continue FEHB coverage in leave without pay status past 365 days.
