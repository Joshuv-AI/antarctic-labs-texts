18 C.F.R. § 1304.401 — Marine sanitation devices.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1304.401
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1304.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1304 (Approval Of Construction In The Tennessee River System And Regulation Of Structures And Other Alterations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person operating a commercial boat dock permitted under this part shall allow the mooring at such permitted facility of any watercraft or floating structure equipped with a marine sanitation device (MSD) unless such MSD is in compliance with all applicable statutes and regulations, including the FWPCA and regulations issued thereunder, and, where applicable, statutes and regulations governing “no discharge” zones.
