Design and practice of the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model in teaching of surgical nursing
Authors: Meichun Tan, Huyan Zhou, Xiumei Wei, Yunxia Ding, Caiyu Shen, Yutong Wu
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundSurgical Nursing is a compulsory course for nursing major and plays a crucial role in cultivating nursing professionals. Currently, with the rapid development of educational technology, blended learning that deeply integrates information technology in surgical nursing teaching has become the normality.The study aimed to design and implementation of “Bridge-in、0bjective、Pre-assessment、Participatory Learning、Post-assessment、Summary (BOPPPS) + Presentation-Assimilation-Discussion(PAD)” blended Learning model in Surgical Nursing teaching, and evaluate Its effectiveness.MethodsA total of 363 undergraduate nursing students enrolled in 2021 and 2022 were selected as research subjects by using convenience sampling. The 188 students enrolled in 2021 formed the control group, while the 175 students enrolled in 2022 constituted the experimental group.For the control group students, instructors delivered surgical nursing instruction using traditional blended learning model which centered on classroom instruction supplemented by online resources. For the experimental group students, instructors implemented surgical nursing instruction through the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended teaching model. After completing the course, learning outcomes and course satisfaction were compared between the two groups.ResultsThe course overal grades for students in the control group and experimental group were 78.00 ± 7.41 and 83.66 ± 9.42, respectively (t = -3.03, p < 0.01); The self-directed learning ability scores were 91.53 ± 16.08 and 103.81 ± 15.48, respectively (t = -4.97, p < 0.001); The critical thinking ability scores were 344.84 ± 43.78 and 376.84 ± 72.33, respectively (t = -3.35, p = 0.001); Active collaborative learning ability scores were 16.06 ± 4.57 and 18.56 ± 3.05, respectively (t = -3.67, P < 0.001). All results showed statistically significant differences. Course satisfaction among experimental group students was higher than that of the control group (P < 0.05), indicating a statistically significant difference.ConclusionThe implementation of the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model in Surgical Nursing teaching enhances students' academic performance and course satisfaction, while fostering their self-directed learning abilities, critical thinking abilities, and active collaborative learning capabilities.Keywords: BOPPPS, PAD, Blended learning model, Surgical nursing

Introduction

The BOPPPS model was first developed by Douglas Kerr’s team at the University of British Columbia in 1978 [1]. The BOPPPS teaching model is an education-goal-oriented, student-centered teaching approach which was designed to enhance classroom teaching efficiency and promote active learning. Many current studies have demonstrated the effectiveness of this teaching method. Studies by Yong Yang, Xiang Ma, Huangfu Shi Yi, and others [2–4] have shown that the BOPPPS teaching method has achieved certain results in improving students' subjective initiative, teaching satisfaction, critical thinking ability. Studies by Na Liang, Yongfang Gong, and others [5, 6] have also shown that this method has significant effects on improving students' learning motivation, self-directed learning ability, and academic performance.

The PAD teaching model, formally known as the “Split-Classroom Approach,” is an original teaching methodology proposed by Professor Xuexin Zhang of the Department of Psychology at Fudan University in 2014 [7]. The core concept of PAD is to divide classroom time into two parts: half allocated for teacher-led instruction, and the other half dedicated to interactive learning through student discussions. This study adheres to its core principle. But the implementation process “was modified to follow the sequence of Discussion–Assimilation–Presentation because of research requirements. This teaching model is widely used in the teaching of nursing courses and has significant effects. Research by Tongyu Lv, Tingting Zhang, and others [8, 9] has shown that the PAD teaching method can help improve the critical thinking ability, scientific research thinking ability, and teaching satisfaction of students. Studies by Jibo Feng, Yanxia Guo, and others [10, 11] have shown that this teaching method has a certain effect on improving students' learning effectiveness, self-learning ability, scientific research thinking ability, and teaching satisfaction.

Currently, with the rapid development of educational technology, blended learning has become the normality. Many studies have validated the effectiveness of BOPPPS or PAD models in single online or offline teaching, and there are also many studies reporting the effectiveness of blended teaching based on BOPPPS or PAD models.

However, there are still some shortcomings in existing research: firstly, the depth of integration of teaching modes is insufficient. Most blended teaching only use online resources as a supplement or information transmission tool for offline teaching, and there is still a sense of disconnection between "online" and "offline", failing to form a coherent and mutually supportive learning loop; Secondly, there is insufficient attention paid to students' deep participation and cognitive internalization process in the fusion mode. This study relies on first-class online courses constructed in the early stage and online resources of the course (online resources are classified by chapter, each chapter includes guidance, teaching objectives, knowledge points, chapter quizzes (pre-test and post-test), and teaching videos) to construct and implement the "BOPPPS + PAD" blended teaching mode. This model is not simply a combination of "BOPPPS" and "PAD", but a carefully designed teaching process that closely links online learning resources with offline teaching content, interlocking and deeply integrating them. This teaching mode always revolves around case-based teaching. In online teaching, students discover and analyze problems around the case, and initially propose solutions. In offline teaching, students further think about problems through group discussions and teacher summaries, comprehensively analyze and solve problems. This study focuses on case teaching and carefully designs teaching elements based on the BOPPPS and PAD teaching models. It deeply integrates online and offline teaching, fully stimulates students' learning autonomy, guides students to participate deeply in teaching throughout the process, and aims to cultivate their critical thinking and ability to solve complex clinical problems, thereby improving the teaching effectiveness of the course and talent cultivation quality.

Materials and methods

Research subjects

Three hundred sixty-three undergraduate nursing students enrolled in 2021 and 2022 were selected as the study subjects by using convenience sampling. The 188 students enrolled in 2021 served as the control group, comprising 33 males and 155 females with an average age of 20.78 ± 3.95 years. Their average score of completed courses was 80.95 ± 6.88; the 175 students enrolled in 2022 served as the experimental group, comprising 44 males and 131 females with an average age of 20.13 ± 2.74 years. Their average score of completed courses was 80.17 ± 7.03.There were no statistically significant differences between the two groups of students in terms of general characteristics such as gender and age, as well as completed course grades. This study adheres to the Declaration of Helsinki, all subjects signed an informed consent form before participating in the study. The study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Jiangxi Institute of Technology (review approval number: A099-20240915–003).

Study methods

The curriculum was divided into two semesters. In the fourth semester, students completed 56 credit hours of theoretical instruction and 8 credit hours of practical training. In the fifth semester, students completed 48 credit hours of theoretical instruction and 16 credit hours of practical training. This study adopted a quasi experimental design, with undergraduate nursing students registered in 2021 as the control group, From March 2023 to December 2023, the course team used traditional blended teaching methods to carry out surgical nursing course teaching; Undergraduate nursing students registered in 2022 were the experimental group, From February 2024 to December 2024, the course team used a blended online and offline teaching model of "BOPPPS + PAD" to carry out surgical nursing course teaching. The members of the teaching teams for the two groups were the same, and all members are guided by the undergraduate nursing talent training program and carry out teaching according to the same syllabus. Compare the differences in course grades, course satisfaction, critical thinking ability, and other indicators between two groups of students after the course ends. Before study, all research subjects were informed of the research purpose and content, and expressed their willingness to participate in this study. The specific intervention methods are as follows:

Control group

The control group conducted instruction primarily through offline methods, supplemented by online approaches, as detailed below:

Before class, teachers assign preparatory tasks through the online platform. Students complete these preparatory tasks and target assessments on the online learning platform. In the classroom, the teacher first clearly states the learning objectives and requirements. Next, they systematically and methodically explain all content, incorporating some interaction with students as needed during instruction, though the primary focus remains on teacher-led lectures. Finally, the teacher concludes the class with a summary. After class, teachers assign homework through the online platform, and students complete chapter quizzes on the online platform. The implementation path of this teaching model is shown in Fig. 1. This teaching model primarily utilizes the classroom as the main teaching venue and relies on teacher-led instruction as the primary teaching method. In practice, neither instructors nor students make the most of the online resources for Surgical Nursing. Instructors incorporated only pre- and post-tests into teaching management, while students lacked motivation for online learning due to unclear objectives. Multiple factors resulted in online instruction serving merely as a supplement to in-person teaching. Students remained passive recipients during the process, lacking active critical thinking. Consequently, this teaching model fails to cultivate students' Self-directed learning ability and clinical nursing thinking.

Implementation path for Blended learing

Experimental group

From February 2023 to February 2024, the teaching team continuously refined key concepts, expanded the test question bank, case library, and assignment collection. They produced several 10-min instructional videos based on these concepts, created micro-lessons, list key points into mind maps, and summarize teaching priorities and challenges. Before the lesson begins, the teacher divides each class into 5–6 groups, with each group consisting of one group leader and 4–5 members. The teaching team members collaborate with defined roles: one clinical nursing expert is responsible for collecting and organizing teaching cases; one member is responsible for online instruction implementation and management, including assigning learning tasks, addressing student inquiries, analyzing online learning data, summarizing student learning outcomes, and promptly identifying issues in online teaching. Three members oversee in-person instruction, organizing classroom activities such as discussions and presentations around teaching cases using the PAD teaching method.

The experimental group adopted “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model with the implementation path shown in Fig. 2. One week prior to the course commencement, instructors released learning tasks such as case studies or instructional videos online, along with supplementary learning materials or resources which guiding students self-directed learning. Concurrently, learning objectives were published online to direct students' learning direction. Students take the pre-test online one day before the course begins. Teachers promptly summarize the pre-test results and provide timely feedback to the instructors responsible for in-person teaching. In-person classes, teachers organize students into small groups to conduct case-centered discussions. Following this, students engage in cross-group exchanges and discussions, group presentations at last. During case discussions, students are encouraged to actively participate in class, fostering their initiative to gain a deep understanding and internalize knowledge. The instructor addresses questions and clarifies issues revealed during discussions and presentations, providing explanations for concepts that students find challenging. At the end of the class, the instructor reviews key points once more. This study adopted the core principles of the PAD teaching method, dividing classroom time into two same segments: half allocated for teacher instruction and half for student engagement. The classroom begins with student discussion, interaction, and presentation followed by teacher-led Q&A and knowledge consolidation. To be precise, the classroom instruction follows the Discussion-Assimilation-Presentation sequence. After class, teachers assign quizzes through the online platform. Upon completing the quizzes, students can discuss learning challenges and insights with teachers and peers in the platform's discussion forum. Students can also reflect on shortcomings in their learning, summarize their experiences, and propose more effective methods. Teachers provide timely feedback, concluding with a chapter summary.

This teaching model cultivates students' self-directed learning abilities, clinical nursing thinking, and proactive collaborative learning capabilities with team members. Observation indicators

Overal grade of surgical nursing

Upon completing the course, students will take a final exam, after which their overal course grade will be calculated. The specific components and their respective weightings are as follows: Pre-assessment 10% 、Course participation (including various online and offline participation forms such as group discussions, intergroup exchanges, group presentations, and online teaching resource learning) 20% 、Post-assessment 10% and Final exam 60%.

Assessment scale for self-directed learning abilities among nursing students

This indicator is one of the main evaluation indicators.it directly measures the effectiveness of "internalization and absorption" in PAD and "online self-learning" in BOPPPS. It is a key indicator of whether the blended learning model can successfully stimulate students' intrinsic motivation and achieve personalized deep learning. The self-directed learning ability assessment scale for nursing students developed by Professors Lin Yi and Jiang Anli of the Second Military Medical University [12] was adopted. The scale demonstrated reliability and validity coefficients of 0.835 and 0.8632 respectively. We recalculated the Cronbach's alpha coefficient in the sample of this study to confirm its reliability in this specific student population. The results showed that the overall Cronbach's alpha of this scale in this study was 0.872.

This scale comprises three dimensions with 28 items: Self-Management Competence (10 items), Learning Collaboration Competence (7 items), and Information Competence (11 items).The scale adopts a 5-point Likert scale: “Strongly Agree,” “Somewhat Agree,” “Neutral,” “Somewhat Disagree,” and “Strongly Disagree.” Positive statements are scored as follows: 5 (strongly agree), 4 (Somewhat Agree), 3 (Neutral), 2 (somewhat disagree), and 1 (Strongly Disagree). Reversed items are scored in the opposite manner. The total score ranges from 28 to 140 points. The higher the score, the better the self-directed learning ability.

Critical thinking abilities assessment scale

This indicator is one of the main evaluation indicators. This indicator can directly reflect the effectiveness of "Participatory Learning" in BOPPPS and "Discussion and Q&A" in PAD. The critical thinking ability measurement scale adopted was developed by experts including Peng Meici and Wang Guocheng from the School of Nursing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, based on the Chinese adaptation of the California Critical Thinking Dispositions Inventory (CCTDI) [13]. In this study, we fully adopted the validated Chinese version of the original question items without any modifications. The Cronbach's alpha coefficient of the scale is 0.9,content validity index is 0.89.This scale comprises seven dimensions with 70 items: Truth-Seeking (10 items), Open-Mindedness (10 items), Analytical Ability (10 items), Systematization Ability (10 items), Confidence in Critical Thinking (10 items), Intellectual Curiosity (10 items), and Cognitive Maturity (10 items).The scale adopts Likert's six-level scoring method, the scores from high to low are 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1, representing respectively" “Strongly agree,” “Somewhat agree,” “agree,” “Somewhat disagree,” and “Strongly disagree”. The total score of this scale ranges from 70 to 420. the higher the score, the better the critical thinking ability.

Active collaborative learning capabilities

This indicator is one of the main evaluation indicators. This indicator comprehensively evaluates the synergistic effect generated by BOPPPS' participatory learning and PAD's group discussion. It directly reflects the practical effect of the teaching mode in building a learning community, and cultivating teamwork and communication skills. Referencing the National Survey of Student Engagement-China (NSSE-China) developed by Tsinghua University's Institute of Education, this scale encompasses five dimensions: Academic Challenge, Active Collaborative Learning, Faculty-Student Interaction, Richness of Educational Experiences, and Campus Environment Support [14]. This study employs only the dimension of Active and Collaborative Learning. This dimension has a Cronbach's α of 0.87 and consists of 6 items. Using a 4-point Likert scale, the scores from high to low are 4, 3, 2, and 1, representing respectively "Excellent, Good, Average, Poor. The total score of this scale ranges from 6 to 24.the higher the score, the stronger levels of active collaborative learning among students. To ensure the adaptability and reliability of the scale, we recalculated the Cronbach's alpha coefficient in the sample of this study. The results showed that the overall Cronbach's alpha of the scale in this study was 0.853.

Course satisfaction

To evaluate the effectiveness of the blended learning model, we developed a questionnaire and conducted a survey through the Wenjuanxing platform. The questionnaire covered three dimensions (three items): satisfaction with teaching, satisfaction with learning outcomes, and overall evaluation. Each item offers four response options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Dissatisfied, and Very Dissatisfied. The questionnaire achieved a Cronbach's alpha coefficient of 0.742. Prior to the formal survey, researchers randomly selected 10 students for a pre-survey. Feedback indicated that the pre-survey instructions and questionnaire items were easy to understand.

Data analysis

SPSS(Statistical Package for the Social Sciences) version 26.0 is used to analyze the date. General information use descriptive analysis. Quantitative datas that follow normal distribution are described by “mean ± standard deviation( ± S), and the comparison between groups is made by two independent samples T-test. Count data were described using frequency and proportion. Comparisons between groups of ordered classification data were performed using the Mann–Whitney U test (Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test).A p value of < 0.05 is considered statistically significant.

Discussion

The “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model has a positive effect on improving students' course comprehensive grades

The study showed that the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended teaching model enhances students’ academic performance(Table 1). This finding aligns with research by Zhang Qing, Aida Bazrgar, and others [15–17]. The “BOPPPS + PAD” teaching model emphasizes student-centered, participatory instruction, aiming to enable students to fully exercise their initiative and engagement in the classroom Table 2 shows that the experimental group's course participation scores were significantly higher than those of the control group, with post-test scores, final exam scores, and course comprehensive grades all significantly higher than the control group (p < 0.01). Furthermore, linear correlation analysis revealed positive correlations between course participation and post-assessment(r = 0.286, p = 0.001), final exam scores (r = 0.302, p = 0.001), and overall course grades (r = 0.370, p < 0.001). This indicates that increasing course participation can enhance performance on post-assessment, final exams, and overall course grades.In summary, the "BOPPPS + PAD" blended teaching method, through structured teaching stage design and strengthened internalization and discussion, not only enhances students' classroom participation, but also significantly improves their stage and overall academic performance.

The “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model has a positive effect on enhancing students' self-directed learning ability

The study reports that the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended teaching method helps enhance students' Self-directed learning abilities(Table 3). This finding aligns with the research results of Siying Lu, Anyan Duan [18, 19], and others.Traditional blended learning requires students to complete assigned tasks before class, yet they often lack clarity on learning objectives. This results in students having unclear goals and motivation for online learning, while classroom instruction remains teacher-centered, with students primarily passively absorbing knowledge [20]. Under the guidance of the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model, instructors publish learning objectives through the online platform prior to class and provide learning materials such as instructional videos and case studies. Guided by these objectives, students independently select relevant content through the online platform for self-directed learning. When encountering difficulties during study, they promptly engage in discussions with peers and instructors to ultimately achieve the learning goals.In the classroom, teachers adhere to the principles of the “PAD” teaching method, dedicating half the class time to student-centered activities. Students engage in discussions with their group members based on learning objectives or assigned tasks, exchange ideas with peers from other groups, and finally report their insights to classmates or the teacher. They may also raise questions during this process. The teacher's role is to guide students in deepening their thinking step by step and promptly address any doubts that arise.Whether online or offline, students' autonomy in learning is fully exercised and cultivated under the guidance of learning objectives or driven by learning tasks, As a result, their self-directed learning abilities have been significantly enhanced.

The “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model has a positive effect on cultivating students' critical thinking ability

Critical thinking is the process of reflecting on and questioning one's own or others' viewpoints, and it is also the process of conducting independent analytical thinking.In the face of increasingly complex nursing environments and growing care demands, cultivating students' critical thinking ability is crucial for enhancing nursing quality [21]. As shown in Table 4, the experimental group's scores for critical thinking ability were significantly higher than those of the control group. This finding aligns with the research results of Zhang Guangyu, Jiang Tao, et al. [22, 23]. In traditional blended learning, after teachers deliver key content in lectures, students must immediately engage in discussions due to limited class time. However, at this stage, students have not yet fully understood or assimilated the knowledge, making it difficult for them to conduct deep thinking and comprehensive analysis. Consequently, this blended learning model is not conducive to cultivating critical thinking ability.Following the blended learning model of “BOPPPS + PAD,” instructors consistently adhere to the principle of student-centered learning [24]. Before class, teachers upload resources such as learning objectives, tasks, and key concepts to the online learning platform. Students proactively access the platform to study based on these objectives or tasks. When encountering challenges during their learning journey, they are encouraged to engage in deep thinking, explore solutions, and seek answers. They may also initiate discussions on the platform or share their questions and insights during offline class discussions.Teachers then provide targeted guidance based on the questions raised, helping students overcome challenges. This interactive learning approach transforms students from passive recipients into active participants in their learning journey, fostering a high level of interest in learning and enhancing their critical thinking ability [25].

The “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model has a positive effect on students' active collaborative learning abilities

The modern healthcare environment emphasizes multidisciplinary teamwork (MDT). Only through effective collaboration among nurses, physicians, rehabilitation specialists, nutritionists, and other healthcare professionals can effectively resolve complex clinical health issues, ensure patient safety, and provide high-quality holistic care [26]. Therefore, cultivating undergraduate nursing students' ability for active collaborative learning is key to developing composite nursing professionals who can adapte to future challenges.Following the blended learning model of “BOPPPS + PAD,” the teacher divided the class into 5–6 groups before the lesson, with each group consisting of one leader and 4–5 members. During the in-person instruction, the teacher required students to engage in learning, discussion, and exchange within their respective groups.To better achieve learning objectives, the team leader must assign tasks reasonably based on each member's strengths and capabilities. Team members must collaborate closely and communicate promptly and effectively to accomplish shared goals. When challenges arise, the group must harness its team wisdom to analyze problems, engage in in-depth discussions, and exchange insights to resolve issues efficiently and effectively.In group-based case study discussions, students' active collaborative learning abilities are fully developed.This study demonstrates that the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model significantly enhances students' active collaborative learning abilities compared to traditional blended learning models (P < 0.001), as detailed in Table 5.

The “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model has a positive effect on students' course satisfaction

This study showed that The “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model has a positive effect on students' course satisfaction (P < 0.05), The specific evaluation are shown in Table 6. This result is consistent with the findings of Deng Fengyuan, Xia Ningning, and others [27, 28]. Following the “BOPPPS + PAD” blended learning model, the teaching team refined online resources including instructional videos, teaching cases, and key learning points before students begin this course. The abundance of online resources enables students to utilize fragmented time for learning, better meeting their learning needs; Students can participate in learning activities both online and offline. In online discussion forums, they can engage in real-time interaction with teachers and peers. In offline classrooms, half of the class time is dedicated to student-centered discussions and exchanges. When encountering difficulties, students can receive timely and effective assistance, enabling them to resolve issues more efficiently and effectively. This model effectively addresses students' individual learning needs, enhances their learning experience and academic performance, thereby significantly increasing their satisfaction with classroom instruction.

Conclusion

This study explored and practiced the "BOPPPS + PAD" blended teaching mode in the course of "Surgical Nursing" through a quasi experimental design. The main advantage of this study is that it does not simply overlap the BOPPPS and PAD teaching modes, but rather integrates and restructures the process organically (such as adjusting to DAP sequence), emphasizing the deep integration and linkage of online resources and offline activities, forming a coherent learning loop centered on cases, with clear goal orientation and deep student participation. The results showed that compared to the traditional blended teaching approach of "offline as the main approach and online as a supplement", the experimental group using the "BOPPPS + PAD" model showed significantly better overall course scores, self-learning ability, critical thinking ability, active cooperative learning ability, and course teaching satisfaction than the control group. However, this study also has certain limitations. Firstly, the sample comes from the same institution, which may limit the representativeness of the sample. Secondly, the interventions of two groups were not conducted at the same time,some interfering factors may have a certain impact on the results.In the future, research will further expand the sample size and carry out multi center cooperation to comprehensively and deeply verify the effectiveness and promotional value of this blended teaching model.
