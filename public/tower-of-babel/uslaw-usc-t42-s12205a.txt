42 U.S.C. § 12205a — Rule of construction regarding regulatory authority
Citation: us:usc:42:12205a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12205a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority to issue regulations granted to the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, the Attorney General, and the Secretary of Transportation under this chapter includes the authority to issue regulations implementing the definitions of disability in section 12102 of this title (including rules of construction) and the definitions in section 12103 of this title, consistent with the ADA Amendments Act of 2008.
