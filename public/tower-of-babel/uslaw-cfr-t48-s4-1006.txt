48 C.F.R. § 4.1006 — 4.1006 Modifications.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 4 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When a new item (such as an increased quantity) is added to the procurement instrument, assign a new line item number.
(b) If the modification relates to existing line items, the modification shall refer to those items.
