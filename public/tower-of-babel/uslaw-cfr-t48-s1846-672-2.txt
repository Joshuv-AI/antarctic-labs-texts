48 C.F.R. § 1846.672-2 — 1846.672-2 Consolidated shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1846.672-2
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1846.672-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1846 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When individual shipments are held at the contractor's plant for authorized transportation consolidation to a single destination on a single bill of lading, the applicable DD Forms 250 may be prepared at the time of CQA or acceptance prior to the time of actual shipment (see Block 3).
