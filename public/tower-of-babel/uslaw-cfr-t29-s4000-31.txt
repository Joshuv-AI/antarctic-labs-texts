29 C.F.R. § 4000.31 — Is my issuance untimely if I miss a few participants or beneficiaries?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4000.31
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4000.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4000 (Filing, Issuance, Computation Of Time, And Record Retention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The PBGC will not treat your issuance as untimely based on your failure to provide the issuance to a participant or beneficiary in a timely manner if—
(a) The failure resulted from administrative error;
(b) The failure involved only a de minimis percentage of intended recipients; and
(c) You resend the issuance to the intended recipient promptly after discovering the error.
