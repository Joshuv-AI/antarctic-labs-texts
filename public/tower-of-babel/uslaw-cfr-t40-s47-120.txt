40 C.F.R. § 47.120 — Solicitation notice and proposal procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:40:47.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-47.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 47 (National Environmental Education Act Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each fiscal year the Administrator shall publish a solicitation for environmental education grant proposals. The solicitation notice shall prescribe the information to be included in the proposal and other information sufficient to permit EPA to assess the project.
