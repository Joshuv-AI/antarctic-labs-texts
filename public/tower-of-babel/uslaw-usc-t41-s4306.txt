41 U.S.C. § 4306 — Applicability of regulations to subcontractors
Citation: us:usc:41:4306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section4306
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 43 (Allowable Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The regulations referred to in sections 4304 and 4305(a) and (b) of this title shall require prime contractors of a covered contract, to the maximum extent practicable, to apply the provisions of those regulations to all subcontractors of the covered contract.
