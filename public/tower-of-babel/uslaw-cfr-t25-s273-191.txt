25 C.F.R. § 273.191 — How may a contract be renewed for Johnson-O'Malley funding?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.191
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An awarding official will notify the existing contracting party in advance of the contract's expiration and ask if the existing contracting party wants to renew the contract. The renewal must be in writing from the existing contracting party and the awarding official.
