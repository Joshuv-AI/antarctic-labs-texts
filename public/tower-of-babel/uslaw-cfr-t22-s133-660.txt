22 C.F.R. § 133.660 — Recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:22:133.660
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-133.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 133 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipient means any individual, corporation, partnership, association, unit of government (except a Federal agency) or legal entity, however organized, that receives an award directly from a Federal agency.
