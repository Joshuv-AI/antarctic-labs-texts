25 C.F.R. § 175.125 — How do I request and receive service?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.125
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you need electrical service in an area where we provide service, you must contact our utility in that service area.
(b) To receive service, you must enter into an Agreement with that utility after it has determined that you have met its requirements.
