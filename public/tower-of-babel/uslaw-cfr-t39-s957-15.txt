39 C.F.R. § 957.15 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:39:957.15
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-957.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 957 (Debarment And Suspension From Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A designated period of time under the rules in this part excludes the day the period begins, and includes the last day of the period unless the last day is a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, in which event the period runs until the close of business on the next business day.
