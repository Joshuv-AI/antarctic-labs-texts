39 C.F.R. § 3013.16 — Enforcement of subpoenas.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3013.16
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3013.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3013 (Procedures For Compelling Production Of Information By The Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of contumacy or failure to obey a subpoena issued under this subpart, the Commission may apply for an order to enforce its subpoena as permitted by 39 U.S.C. 504(f)(3).
