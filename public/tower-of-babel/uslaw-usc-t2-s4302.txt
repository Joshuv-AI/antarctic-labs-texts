2 U.S.C. § 4302 — Maximum annual rate of compensation of Chief of Staff of Joint Committee on Taxation
Citation: us:usc:2:4302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4302
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 43 (Congressional Committees); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The maximum annual rate of compensation of the Chief of Staff of the Joint Committee on Taxation shall not exceed the greater of $173,900 or the rate of pay in effect for such position under an order issued by the Speaker of the House of Representatives pursuant to the authority of section 4532 of this title.
