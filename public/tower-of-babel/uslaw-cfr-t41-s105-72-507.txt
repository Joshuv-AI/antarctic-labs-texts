41 C.F.R. § 105-72.507 — -72.507 Contract administration.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-72.507
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-72.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-72 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A system for contract administration shall be maintained to ensure contractor conformance with the terms, conditions and specifications of the contract and to ensure adequate and timely follow up of all purchases. Recipients shall evaluate contractor performance and document, as appropriate, whether contractors have met the terms, conditions and specifications of the contract.
