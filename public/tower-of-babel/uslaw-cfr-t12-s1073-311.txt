12 C.F.R. § 1073.311 — Non-waiver of rights by payment.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1073.311
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1073.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1073 (Procedures For Bureau Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any portion of a debt being collected under 5 U.S.C. 5514 shall not be construed as a waiver of any rights which the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law, unless there are statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary.
