25 U.S.C. § 5376 — Application of other provisions
Citation: us:usc:25:5376
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5376
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 46 (Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance); Subchapter IV (Tribal Self-Governance—Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 314 of the Department of the Interior and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 1991 (Public Law 101–512; 104 Stat. 1959), shall apply to compacts and funding agreements entered into under this subchapter.
