36 C.F.R. § 1250.70 — When may I appeal NARA's FOIA determination?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1250.70
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1250.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1250 (Nara Records Subject To Foia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may appeal when there is any adverse determination, including:
(a) Refusal to release a record, either in whole or in part;
(b) Determination that a record does not exist or cannot be found;
(c) Determination that the record you sought was not subject to the FOIA;
(d) Denial of a request for expedited processing;
(e) Denial of a fee waiver request; or
(f) Fee category determination.
