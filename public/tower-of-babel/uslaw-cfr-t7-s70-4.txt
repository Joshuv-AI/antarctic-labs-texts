7 C.F.R. § 70.4 — Services available.
Citation: us:cfr:7:70.4
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-70.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 70 (Voluntary Grading Of Poultry Products And Rabbit Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part provide for the following kinds of service; and any one or more of the different services applicable to official plants may be rendered in an official plant:
(a) Grading of ready-to-cook poultry and rabbits in an official plant or at other locations with adequate facilities.
(b) Grading of specified poultry food products in official plants.
