47 C.F.R. § 302.1 — Purpose.

Creator: National Telecommunications And Information Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 302 (Connecting Minority Communities Pilot Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes uniform application, approval, award, financial and administrative requirements for the Connecting Minority Communities pilot program authorized under Division N, Title IX, section 902, of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021.
