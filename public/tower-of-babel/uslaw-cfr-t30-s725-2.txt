30 C.F.R. § 725.2 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:30:725.2
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-725.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 725 (Reimbursements To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objectives of assistance under this part are:
(a) To assist the States in meeting the increased costs of administering the initial performance standards.
(b) To encourage the States to build strong reclamation and enforcement programs.
