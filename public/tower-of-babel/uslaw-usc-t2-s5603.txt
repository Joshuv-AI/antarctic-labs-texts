2 U.S.C. § 5603 — Symbol of office of Sergeant at Arms
Citation: us:usc:2:5603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5603
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter VIII (Sergeant At Arms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The symbol of his office shall be the mace, which shall be borne by him while enforcing order on the floor.
