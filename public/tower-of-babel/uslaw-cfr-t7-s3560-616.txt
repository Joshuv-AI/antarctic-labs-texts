7 C.F.R. § 3560.616 — Rates and terms.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3560.616
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3560.616
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3560 (Direct Multi-Family Housing Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The interest rate for on-farm labor housing loans will be 1 percent.
(b) The term of the on-farm labor housing loan will not exceed 33 years.
(c) Loan amortization for on-farm labor housing may be on a monthly or an annual basis.
