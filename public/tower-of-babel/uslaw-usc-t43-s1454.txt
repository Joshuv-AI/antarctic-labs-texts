43 U.S.C. § 1454 — Duties of Assistant Secretary and assistant to Secretary
Citation: us:usc:43:1454
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1454
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 31 (Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary of the Interior shall perform such duties in the Department of the Interior as shall be prescribed by the Secretary, or may be required by law. The assistant to the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to sign such official papers and documents as the Secretary may direct.
