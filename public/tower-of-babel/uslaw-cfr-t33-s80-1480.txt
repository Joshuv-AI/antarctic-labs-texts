33 C.F.R. § 80.1480 — Hilo Harbor, Hawaii, HI.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.1480
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.1480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A line drawn from the seaward extremity of the Hilo Breakwater 265° true (as an extension of the seaward side of the breakwater) to the shoreline 0.2 nautical mile north of Alealea Point.
