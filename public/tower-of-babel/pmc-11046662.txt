Clinical utility of specialist respiratory outpatient review for all patients diagnosed with acute pulmonary embolism
Authors: Sarah Shuker
Published: 2023

Introduction and objective

Pulmonary embolism (PE) is a common condition, usually diagnosed and managed as part of the acute medical take. Subsequent specialist outpatient review is not included in the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) recommendations1 and not routinely undertaken, despite advice from the British Lung Foundation.2 We sought to evaluate clinical outcomes in a dedicated PE clinic.

Methods

Records and outcomes of patients diagnosed with PE between January 2016 and August 2021 were reviewed.

Results

Of 995 patients identified, the diagnosis of PE alone was confirmed in 889. Significant management changes were made in 106: 33 had the diagnosis of PE removed, based on inconsistent history/investigations; 24 had PE confirmed, but with additional diagnoses requiring significant management changes (including sleep apnoea, obstructive airways disease and various neuromuscular disorders). 49 patients (5%) were diagnosed with chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH) and referred to the National Pulmonary Hypertension Service, Cambridge, for further assessment and definitive management.

Conclusions

Routine specialist review of all patients diagnosed with PE is clinically justifiable, with significant clinical management changes occurring in 11% of cases. The incorrect diagnosis of PE has significant implications for treatment, including unnecessary anticoagulation and misleading future risk assessment scores. Identification and treatment of additional symptomatic pathologies, often incorrectly attributed to PE, is also important. The prevalence of CTEPH identified in our clinic supports recent estimates from specialist centres,3 which are higher than previous estimates,4 accurately reflect the wider case-mix in secondary care, and further justifies the provision of routine specialist review of all cases of acute PE.
