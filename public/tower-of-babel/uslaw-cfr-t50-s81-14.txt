50 C.F.R. § 81.14 — Comprehensive plan alternative.
Citation: us:cfr:50:81.14
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-81.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 81 (Conservation Of Endangered And Threatened Species Of Fish, Wildlife, And Plants—Cooperation With The States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that the State elects to operate under a comprehensive fish and wildlife resource planning system, the Cooperative Agreement will be an attachment to the plan. No Application for Federal Assistance will be required since the documentation will be incorporated in the plan. However, the continued existence of the comprehensive plan, and Federal financing thereunder, will be contingent upon the continued existence of the Cooperative Agreement described in § 81.3, above.
