29 C.F.R. § 2.15 — Protection of witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2 (General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A witness has the right, prior to or during his testimony, to exclude audiovisual coverage of his testimony in any hearing being covered audiovisually.
