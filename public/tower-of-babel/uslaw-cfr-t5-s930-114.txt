5 C.F.R. § 930.114 — Reports required.
Citation: us:cfr:5:930.114
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-930.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 930 (Programs For Specific Positions And Examinations (Miscellaneous)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency will submit to OPM, on request (a) a copy of agency orders and directives issued in compliance with this subpart; and (b) such other reports as OPM may require for adequate administration and evaluation of the motor vehicle operator program.
