25 C.F.R. § 150.101 — What is the purpose of the record of title?
Citation: us:cfr:25:150.101
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-150.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 150 (Record Of Title To Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The record of title provides the BIA with a record of title documents to Indian land and provides constructive notice that the title documents exist.
