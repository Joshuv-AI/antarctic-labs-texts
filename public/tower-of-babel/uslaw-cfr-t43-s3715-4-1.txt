43 C.F.R. § 3715.4-1 — -1 What happens after I give BLM written notification of my existing occupancy?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3715.4-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3715.4-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3710 (Public Law 167; Act Of July 23, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BLM will visit your site during the normal course of inspection to obtain the information described in § 3715.3-2. After the visit, BLM will make a determination of concurrence or non-concurrence.
(b) You must provide the information described in § 3715.3-2 to BLM. You may provide it either in writing or verbally during a site visit by BLM field staff.
