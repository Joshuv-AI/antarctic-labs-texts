12 C.F.R. § 611.1200 — Applicability of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:12:611.1200
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-611.1200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 611 (Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to each bank and association that desires to terminate its System institution status and become chartered as a bank, savings association, or other financial institution.
