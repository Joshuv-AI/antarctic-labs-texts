7 C.F.R. § 505.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:505.1
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-505.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 505 (National Agricultural Library Fees For Loans And Copying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations establish fees for loans, copying, or reproduction of materials in the collections of the National Agricultural Library (NAL) within the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA).
