16 C.F.R. § 1750.4 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1750.4
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1750.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1750 (Standard For Devices To Permit The Opening Of Household Refrigerator Doors From The Inside). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Household refrigerators shall be equipped with a device enabling the doors thereof to be opened easily from the inside, either by the application of an outwardly directed force to the inside of the door or by the rotation of a knob similar to a conventional doorknob. The device shall not render the refrigerator unsatisfactory for the preservation of food under any or all normal conditions of use.
