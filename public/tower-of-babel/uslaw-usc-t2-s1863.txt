2 U.S.C. § 1863 — Funds out of Contingent Expenses, Architect of the Capitol Appropriation
Citation: us:usc:2:1863
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1863
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter IV (Appropriations And Expenditures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any expenditures required to implement the provisions of section 1818 of this title shall be paid from the appropriation “Contingent Expenses, Architect of the Capitol” and any funds appropriated under this head shall hereafter be available for such purpose.
