Correction for Closson et al., Context-dependent variability of HIF heterodimers influences interactions with macromolecular and small molecule partners
Published: 2026

Correction for “Context-dependent variability of HIF heterodimers influences interactions with macromolecular and small molecule partners,” by Joseph D. Closson, Xingjian Xu, Meiling Zhang, Tarsisius T. Tiyani, Leandro Pimentel Marcelino, Eta A. Isiorho, Jason S. Nagati, Joseph A. Garcia, and Kevin H. Gardner, which published April 28, 2026; 10.1073/pnas.2514042123 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 123, e2514042123).

The authors note that an error occurred in the ninth paragraph of Results and in SI Appendix, Fig. S7: “HIF-1α residue Cys 139 was originally erroneously referred to as Cys 118 in the main text and SI Appendix, Fig. S7 due to a residue numbering error in PDB entry 9OFU. This has since been corrected in the main text, SI Appendix, and PDB. This in no way affects the conclusions drawn from our research, and we sincerely apologize for this error.” The published paper and the SI Appendix have been updated.
