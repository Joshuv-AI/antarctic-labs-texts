20 U.S.C. § 9583 — Removal
Citation: us:usc:20:9583
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9583
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter I (Education Sciences Reform); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Presidential
The Director, each member of the Board, and the Commissioner for Education Statistics may be removed by the President prior to the expiration of the term of each such appointee.
(b) Director
Each Commissioner appointed by the Director pursuant to section 9517 of this title may be removed by the Director prior to the expiration of the term of each such Commissioner.
