37 U.S.C. § 901 — Wartime pay of officer of armed force exercising command higher than his grade
Citation: us:usc:37:901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section901
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 17 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of war, an officer of an armed force who is serving with troops operating against an enemy and who exercises, under assignment in orders issued by competent authority, a command above that pertaining to his grade, is entitled to the pay and allowances (not above that of pay grade O–7) appropriate to the command so exercised.
