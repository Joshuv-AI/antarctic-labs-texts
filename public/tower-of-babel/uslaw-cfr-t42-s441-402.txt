42 C.F.R. § 441.402 — State plan requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:441.402
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-441.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 441 (Services: Requirements And Limits Applicable To Specific Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a State that is eligible to provide community supported living arrangements services as an optional Medicaid service to developmentally disabled individuals provides such services, the State plan must specify that it complies with the minimum protection requirements in § 441.404.
