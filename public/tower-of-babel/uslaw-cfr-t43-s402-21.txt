43 C.F.R. § 402.21 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:43:402.21
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-402.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 402 (Sale Of Lands In Federal Reclamation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to the sale of small tracts of public and acquired lands on the Gila Project, Arizona, that are subject to the reclamation laws and that may be sold to actual settlers or farmers under the Act of July 30, 1947 (61 Stat. 628; 43 U. S. C. 613-613e).
