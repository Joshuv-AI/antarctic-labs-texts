24 C.F.R. § 964.330 — Grant set-aside assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:24:964.330
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-964.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 964 (Tenant Participation And Tenant Opportunities In Public Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may make available five percent (5%) of any amounts available in each fiscal year (subsequent to the first funding cycle) available to eligible HAs to supplement grants previously awarded under this program. These supplemental grants would be awarded if the HA demonstrates that the funds cannot otherwise be obtained and are needed to maintain adequate levels of services to residents.
