10 U.S.C. § 1376 — Temporary disability retired lists
Citation: us:usc:10:1376
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1376
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 69 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary concerned shall maintain a temporary disability retired list containing the names of members of the armed forces under his jurisdiction placed thereon under sections 1202 and 1205 of this title.
