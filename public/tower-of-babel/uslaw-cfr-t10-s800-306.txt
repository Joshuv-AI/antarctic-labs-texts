10 C.F.R. § 800.306 — Noninterference with other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:10:800.306
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-800.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 800 (Loans For Bid Or Proposal Preparation By Minority Business Enterprises Seeking Doe Contracts And Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this regulation shall be construed to modify requirements imposed on the borrower by Federal, State and local government agencies in connection with permits, licenses, or other authorizations to conduct or finance its business.
