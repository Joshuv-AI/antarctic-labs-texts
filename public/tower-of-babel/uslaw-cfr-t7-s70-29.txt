7 C.F.R. § 70.29 — Who may obtain grading service.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-70.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 70 (Voluntary Grading Of Poultry Products And Rabbit Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for grading service may be made by any interested person, including, but not being limited to any authorized agent of the United States, 3any State, county, municipality, or common carrier.
