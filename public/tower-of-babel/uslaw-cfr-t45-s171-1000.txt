45 C.F.R. § 171.1000 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:171.1000
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-171.1000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 171 (Information Blocking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth disincentives that an appropriate agency may impose on a health care provider that OIG determines has committed information blocking, and certain procedures related to those disincentives.
