40 C.F.R. § 799.19 — Chemical imports and exports.
Citation: us:cfr:40:799.19
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-799.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 799 (Identification Of Specific Chemical Substance And Mixture Testing Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons who export or who intend to export chemical substances or mixtures listed in subpart B, subpart C, or subpart D of this part are subject to the requirements of 40 CFR part 707.
