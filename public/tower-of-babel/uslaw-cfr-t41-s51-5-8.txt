41 C.F.R. § 51-5.8 — -5.8 Violations by entities of the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:41:51-5.8
Creator: Chapter 51—Committee For Purchase From People Who Are Blind Or Severely Disabled
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-51-5.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 51-5 (5—Contracting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any alleged violations of the JWOD Act or these regulations by entities of the Government shall be investigated by the Committee, which shall notify the entity and afford it an opportunity to submit a statement.
