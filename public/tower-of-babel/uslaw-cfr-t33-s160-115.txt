33 C.F.R. § 160.115 — Withholding of clearance.
Citation: us:cfr:33:160.115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-160.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 160 (Ports And Waterways Safety—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each District Commander or Captain of the Port may request the Secretary of the Treasury, or the authorized representative thereof, to withhold or revoke the clearance required by 46 U.S.C. App. 91 of any vessel, the owner or operator of which is subject to any penalties under 46 U.S.C. 70036.
