16 C.F.R. § 5.54 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:16:5.54
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-5.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 5 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person against whom the Commission has issued an order imposing disciplinary action under this part may seek judicial review of the Commission's determination in the United States District Court for the District of Columbia by filing a petition for such review within 60 days of receipt of notice of the Commission's final decision.
