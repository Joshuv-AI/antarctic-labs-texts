34 U.S.C. § 10561 — Grant authorization
Citation: us:usc:34:10561
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10561
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXVII (Paul Coverdell Forensic Sciences Improvement Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall award grants to States and units of local government in accordance with this subchapter.
