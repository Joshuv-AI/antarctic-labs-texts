40 C.F.R. § 13.36 — Administrative charges.
Citation: us:cfr:40:13.36
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-13.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 13 (Claims Collection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with § 13.11, all administrative charges incurred in connection with the referral of a debt to the IRS shall be assessed on the debt and thus increase the amount of the offset.
