The star edge coloring of cubic Halin graphs with star chromatic index 5 5
Xingxing Hu, Yunfang Tang

Abstract
The star chromatic index of a graph G G , denoted by χ s ​ t ′ ​ ( G ) \chi^{\prime}_{st}(G) , is the minimum number of colors needed to properly color the edges of G G such that no path or cycle of length four is bi-colored. Casselgren et al. and Hou et al. independently proved that the star chromatic index of a cubic Halin graph, except a special graph, is at most 6 6 . It remains an open problem to determine which of such graphs have star chromatic index 5 5 . In this paper, we show that if G ≠ N e 2 G\neq N_{e_{2}} is a cubic Halin graph whose tree is a caterpillar or a complete tree, then χ s ​ t ′ ​ ( G ) = 5 \chi^{\prime}_{st}(G)=5 . Keywords: star chromatic index; caterpillar; complete tree; Halin graph
