25 C.F.R. § 518.12 — Which investigative or enforcement powers of the Commission are inapplicable to self-regulating tribes?
Citation: us:cfr:25:518.12
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-518.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 518 (Self-Regulation Of Class Ii Gaming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During any time in which a tribe has a certificate of self-regulation, the powers of the Commission, as set forth in 25 U.S.C. 2706(b)(1)-(4), shall be inapplicable.
