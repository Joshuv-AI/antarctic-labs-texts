48 C.F.R. § 829.000 — 829.000 Scope of part.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 829 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part states the policies and procedures for the following:
(a) Exemptions of alcohol products purchased for use by the VA medical care program from Federal excise taxes.
(b) Specified refund procedures for State and local taxes.
