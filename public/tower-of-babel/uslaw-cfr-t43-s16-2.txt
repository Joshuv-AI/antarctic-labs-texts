43 C.F.R. § 16.2 — Applications for helium disposition agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:16.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-16.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 16 (Conservation Of Helium). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The application for a helium disposition agreement need not be in any particular form, but must contain information sufficient to enable the Secretary to determine that the proposal will conserve helium that will otherwise be wasted, drained, or lost to Federal ownership or use, and to evaluate the suitability of the proposal.
