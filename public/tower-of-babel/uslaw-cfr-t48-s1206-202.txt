48 C.F.R. § 1206.202 — 1206.202 Establishing or maintaining alternative sources.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1206.202
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1206.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1206 (Competition Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The head of the contracting activity (HCA) is delegated authority to exclude a particular source from a contract action in order to establish or maintain an alternative source under the conditions listed in FAR 6.202(a).
(b) The HCA is also delegated authority to approve a Determination and Findings (D&F) in support of a contract action awarded under the authority of FAR 6.202(a).
