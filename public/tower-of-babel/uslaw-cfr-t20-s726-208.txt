20 C.F.R. § 726.208 — Report by carrier of issuance of policy or endorsement.
Citation: us:cfr:20:726.208
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-726.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 726 (Black Lung Benefits; Requirements For Coal Mine Operator'S Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each carrier shall report to the Office each policy and endorsement issued, canceled, or renewed by it to an operator. The report shall be made in such manner and on such form as the Office may require.
