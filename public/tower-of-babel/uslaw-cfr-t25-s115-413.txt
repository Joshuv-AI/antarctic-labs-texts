25 C.F.R. § 115.413 — Who may receive funds from a minor's supervised account?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.413
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A custodial parent, a legal guardian, a person who has been recognized by the BIA as having control and custody of the minor, or an emancipated minor may be eligible to withdraw funds from a minor's supervised account if there is an authorized disbursement request that is based upon the terms of a BIA-approved distribution plan.
