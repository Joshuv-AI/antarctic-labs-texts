40 C.F.R. § 125.56 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:125.56
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-125.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 125 (Criteria And Standards For The National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the criteria to be applied by EPA in acting on section 301(h) requests for modifications to the secondary treatment requirements. It also establishes special permit conditions which must be included in any permit incorporating a section 301(h) modification of the secondary treatment requirements (“section 301(h) modified permit”).
