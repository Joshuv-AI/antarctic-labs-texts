22 U.S.C. § 288c — Exemption from property taxes
Citation: us:usc:22:288c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section288c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVIII (Privileges And Immunities Of International Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

International organizations shall be exempt from all property taxes imposed by, or under the authority of, any Act of Congress, including such Acts as are applicable solely to the District of Columbia or the Territories.
