42 U.S.C. § 12701 — National housing goal
Citation: us:usc:42:12701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12701
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter I (General Provisions And Policies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress affirms the national goal that every American family be able to afford a decent home in a suitable environment.
