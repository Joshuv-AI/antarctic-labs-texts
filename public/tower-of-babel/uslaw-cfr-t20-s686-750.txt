20 C.F.R. § 686.750 — Are graduates provided with transition allowances?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.750
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.750
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, graduates receive post-separation transition allowances according to policies and procedures established by the Secretary. Transition allowances are incentive-based to reflect a graduate's attainment of academic credentials and those associated with career technical training such as industry-recognized credentials.
