43 C.F.R. § 3425.1-5 — -5 Leasing outside coal production regions.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3425.1-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3425.1-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3420 (Competitive Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lease sale may be held in response to an application under this subpart if the application covers coal deposits which are outside coal production regions identified under § 3400.5 of this title.
