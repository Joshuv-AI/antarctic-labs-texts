28 C.F.R. § 35.203 — Equivalent facilitation.
Citation: us:cfr:28:35.203
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-35.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In State And Local Government Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart prevents the use of designs, methods, or techniques as alternatives to those prescribed, provided that the alternative designs, methods, or techniques result in substantially equivalent or greater accessibility and usability of the web content or mobile app.
