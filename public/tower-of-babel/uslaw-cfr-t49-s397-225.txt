49 C.F.R. § 397.225 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:49:397.225
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-397.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 397 (Transportation Of Hazardous Materials; Driving And Parking Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party to a proceeding under § 397.205(a), § 397.213(a), or § 397.223(a) may seek review by the appropriate district court of the United States of the decision of the Administrator under such proceeding only by filing a petition with such court within 60 days after the final agency decision.
