49 C.F.R. § 1177.2 — To whom documents should be submitted for recordation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1177.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1177.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1177 (Recordation Of Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Documents to be recorded shall be submitted in person, via the Board's website, or by mail addressed to the Chief of Case Administration, Office of Chief Counsel, Surface Transportation Board, Washington, DC 20423-0001. All documents submitted by mail should clearly state “Documents for Recordation” on the envelope.
