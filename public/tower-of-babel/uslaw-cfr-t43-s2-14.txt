43 C.F.R. § 2.14 — In what order are responses usually made?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2 (Freedom Of Information Act; Records And Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The bureau ordinarily will respond to requests according to their order of receipt within their processing track.
