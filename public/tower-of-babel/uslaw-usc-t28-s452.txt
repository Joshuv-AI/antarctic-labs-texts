28 U.S.C. § 452 — Courts always open; powers unrestricted by expiration of sessions
Citation: us:usc:28:452
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section452
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 21 (General Provisions Applicable To Courts And Judges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All courts of the United States shall be deemed always open for the purpose of filing proper papers, issuing and returning process, and making motions and orders.
The continued existence or expiration of a session of a court in no way affects the power of the court to do any act or take any proceeding.
