42 C.F.R. § 475.107 — QIO contract awards.
Citation: us:cfr:42:475.107
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-475.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 475 (Quality Improvement Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the provisions of § 475.105, CMS will—
(a) Ensure that all awardees meet the requirements of §§ 475.101 through 475.103, as applicable; and
(b) Award the contract to the selected organization for a specific QIO area for a period of 5 years.
