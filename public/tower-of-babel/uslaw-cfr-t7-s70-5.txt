7 C.F.R. § 70.5 — Nondiscrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:70.5
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-70.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 70 (Voluntary Grading Of Poultry Products And Rabbit Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The conduct of all services and the licensing of graders under these regulations shall be accomplished without regard to race, color, national origin, religion, age, sex, disability, political beliefs, sexual orientation, or marital or family status.
