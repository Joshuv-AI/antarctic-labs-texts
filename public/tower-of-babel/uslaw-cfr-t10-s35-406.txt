10 C.F.R. § 35.406 — Brachytherapy sources accountability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:35.406
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-35.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 35 (Medical Use Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A licensee shall maintain accountability at all times for all brachytherapy sources in storage or use.
(b) As soon as possible after removing sources from a patient or a human research subject, a licensee shall return brachytherapy sources to a secure storage area.
(c) A licensee shall maintain a record of the brachytherapy source accountability in accordance with § 35.2406.
