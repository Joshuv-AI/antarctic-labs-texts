40 C.F.R. § 426.60 — Applicability; description of the automotive glass tempering subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:426.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-426.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 426 (Glass Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges of pollutants resulting from the processes in which glass is cut and then passed through a series of processes that grind and polish the edges, bend the glass, and then temper the glass to produce side and back windows for automobiles.
