48 C.F.R. § 215.371-6 — -6 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:215.371-6
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-215.371-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 215 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the provision at 252.215-7007, Notice of Intent to Resolicit, in competitive solicitations that will be solicited for fewer than 30 days, unless an exception at 215.371-4 applies or the requirement is waived in accordance with 215.371-5.
