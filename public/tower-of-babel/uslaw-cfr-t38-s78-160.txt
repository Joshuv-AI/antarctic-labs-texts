38 C.F.R. § 78.160 — Withholding, suspension, deobligation, termination, and recovery of funds by VA.
Citation: us:cfr:38:78.160
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-78.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 78 (Staff Sergeant Parker Gordon Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will enforce this part through such actions as may be appropriate. Appropriate actions include withholding, suspension, deobligation, termination, recovery of funds by VA, and actions in accordance with 2 CFR part 200.
