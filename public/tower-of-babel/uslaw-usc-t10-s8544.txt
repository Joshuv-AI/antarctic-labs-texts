10 U.S.C. § 8544 — Civilian teachers: number; compensation
Citation: us:usc:10:8544
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8544
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 855 (United States Naval Postgraduate School). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may employ as many civilians as he considers necessary to serve at the Naval Postgraduate School under the direction of the President of the school as senior professors, professors, associate professors, assistant professors, and instructors. The Secretary shall prescribe the compensation of those persons.
