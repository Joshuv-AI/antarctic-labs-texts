30 C.F.R. § 585.631 — When must I initiate activities under an approved COP?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.631
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.631
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After your COP is approved, you are expected to commence construction on the OCS in accordance with the construction schedule included as a part of your approved COP, unless you notify BOEM in advance of a deviation from your schedule.
