46 U.S.C. § 42103 — No preference to Government-owned vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:42103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section42103
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Actions To Address Foreign Practices); Chapter 421 (Regulations Affecting Shipping In Foreign Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A regulation may not give a vessel owned by the United States Government a preference over a vessel owned by citizens of the United States and documented under the laws of the United States.
