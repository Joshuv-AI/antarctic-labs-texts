29 C.F.R. § 1471.980 — Participant.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.980
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.980
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participant means any person who submits a proposal for or who enters into a covered transaction, including an agent or representative of a participant.
