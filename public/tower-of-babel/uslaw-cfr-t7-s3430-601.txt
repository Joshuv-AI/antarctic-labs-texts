7 C.F.R. § 3430.601 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3430.601
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3430.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3430 (Competitive And Noncompetitive Non-Formula Federal Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the Beginning Farmer and Rancher Development Program (BFRDP) is to establish a beginning farmer and rancher development program that provides local and regional training, education, outreach, and technical assistance initiatives for beginning farmers and ranchers.
