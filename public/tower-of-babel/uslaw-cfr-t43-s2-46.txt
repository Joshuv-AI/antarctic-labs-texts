43 C.F.R. § 2.46 — When may you ask the bureau for a fee waiver?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2.46
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2 (Freedom Of Information Act; Records And Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You should request a fee waiver when your request is first submitted to the bureau (see § 2.6 of this part).
(b) You may submit a fee waiver request at a later time if the bureau has not yet completed processing your request.
