7 C.F.R. § 8.5 — Revocation of present authorizations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:8.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-8.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 8 (4-H Club Name And Emblem). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective September 16, 1985, authorization permits for the use of the 4-H Club Name and Emblem presently in effect will be revoked. However, such authorizations may be renewed upon written request.
