2 C.F.R. § 1600.215 — Which nonprocurement transactions are not covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1600.215
Creator: US International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1600.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1600 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the nonprocurement transactions which are not covered transactions under 2 CFR 180.215, any nonprocurement transaction entered into under a primary tier nonprocurement transaction that does not require DFC explicit prior consent is not a covered transaction under 2 CFR 180.215(g)(2).
