42 U.S.C. § 629l — Public access to State plans
Citation: us:usc:42:629l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section629l
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Aid And Services To Needy Families With Children And For Child-Welfare Services); Part B (Child And Family Services); Subpart 2 (Promoting Safe And Stable Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall—
(1)
create a standardized format for State plans required under sections 622 and 629b of this title used to monitor compliance with those sections;
(2)
produce comparisons and analyses of trends in State plans to inform future technical assistance and policy development;
(3)
make the State plans available on a public website; and
(4)
include on the website aggregated national summaries of State submissions as the Secretary deems appropriate.
