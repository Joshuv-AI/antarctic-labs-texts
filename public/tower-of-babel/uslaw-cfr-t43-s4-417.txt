43 C.F.R. § 4.417 — Coordination with judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.417
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.417
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon motion or on its own initiative after notice to the parties, the Board may suspend consideration or dismiss an appeal when the decision on appeal has been challenged in Federal court.
