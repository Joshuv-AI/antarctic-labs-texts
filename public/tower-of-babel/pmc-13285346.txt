Letter to the Editor Regarding “Safety and Efficacy of Magnetically Controlled Growing Rods for Early-Onset Scoliosis in the Era of Disease-Modifying Therapies: Mid-Term Results in a Homogeneous Cohort of SMA-I Patients”
Authors: Prudhvi Kodali, David Skaggs
Published: 2026

To the Editor,

We read with great interest “Safety and Efficacy of Magnetically Controlled Growing Rods for Early-Onset Scoliosis in the Era of Disease-Modifying Therapies: Mid-term Results in a Homogeneous Cohort of SMA-I Patients” [1].

We have encountered spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) patients who lost ambulatory status following growing rods to the pelvis. Conversely, we have not observed comparable losses in patients whose constructs were stopped short of the pelvis. We are aware of many patients at other institutions with similar experiences.

Did any patients in your study lose ambulation when fused to the pelvis?

Author contributions

Prudhvi Kodali: Writing – original draft, Writing – review & editing. David Skaggs: Conceptualization, Writing – review & editing.
