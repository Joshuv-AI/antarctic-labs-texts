29 U.S.C. § 554 — Assistants to Secretary
Citation: us:usc:29:554
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section554
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 12 (Department Of Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department of Labor not more than two assistants to the Secretary, who shall be appointed by the President and shall perform such duties as may be prescribed by the Secretary of Labor or required by law.
