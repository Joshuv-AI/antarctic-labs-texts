33 C.F.R. § 105.135 — Equivalents.
Citation: us:cfr:33:105.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-105.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 105 (Maritime Security: Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For any measure required by this part, the facility owner or operator may propose an equivalent as provided in § 101.130 of this subchapter.
