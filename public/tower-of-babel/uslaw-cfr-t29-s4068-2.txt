29 C.F.R. § 4068.2 — Definitions.

Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4068.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4068 (Lien For Liability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following terms are defined in § 4001.2 of this chapter: ERISA, PBGC, person, plan, and termination date.
Collective net worth of persons subject to liability in connection with a plan termination has the meaning in § 4062.2.
