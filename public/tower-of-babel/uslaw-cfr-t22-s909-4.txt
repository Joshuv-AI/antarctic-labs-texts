22 C.F.R. § 909.4 — Other decision.
Citation: us:cfr:22:909.4
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-909.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 909 (Decisionmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the Board's decision requires no action by an Agency, the decision shall be forwarded to the grievant. A copy of the decision shall be supplied to each party.
