21 C.F.R. § 870.3935 — Prosthetic heart valve holder.
Citation: us:cfr:21:870.3935
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-870.3935
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 870 (Cardiovascular Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A prosthetic heart valve holder is a device used to hold a replacement heart valve while it is being sutured into place.
(b) Classification. Class I. The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter.
