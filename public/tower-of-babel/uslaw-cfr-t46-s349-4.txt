46 C.F.R. § 349.4 — Decision on application.
Citation: us:cfr:46:349.4
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-349.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 349 (Reemployment Rights Of Certain Merchant Seamen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MARAD will issue or deny certification (accompanied by an explanation in writing) to each applicant not later than 20 days after receipt of an application for certification.
