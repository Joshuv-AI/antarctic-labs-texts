38 C.F.R. § 21.3103 — Failure to cooperate.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.3103
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.3103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will not act further on an eligible person's application for assistance under 38 U.S.C. chapter 35 when counseling is required for him or her and the eligible person—
(a) Fails to report;
(b) Fails to cooperate in the counseling process; or
(c) Does not complete counseling to the extent required under § 21.3102.
