31 C.F.R. § 8.38 — Notaries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:8.38
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-8.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 8 (Practice Before The Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco And Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No attorney, certified public accountant, or enrolled practitioner may, with respect to any matter administered by the Bureau, take acknowledgements, administer oaths, certify papers, or perform any official act in connection with matters in which he or she is employed as counsel, attorney, or practioner, or in which he or she may be in any way interested before the Bureau.
