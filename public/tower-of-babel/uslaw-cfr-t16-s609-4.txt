16 C.F.R. § 609.4 — Timing of electronic credit monitoring notices.
Citation: us:cfr:16:609.4
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-609.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 609 (Free Electronic Credit Monitoring For Active Duty Military). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice required in § 609.3(a) must be provided within 48 hours of any material additions or modifications to a consumer's file.
