30 C.F.R. § 1227.104 — What will ONRR do when it receives a State's delegation proposal?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1227.104
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1227.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1227 (Delegation To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When ONRR receives your delegation proposal, it will record the receipt date. ONRR will notify you in writing within 15 business days whether your proposal is complete. If it is not complete, ONRR will identify any missing items § 1227.103 requires. Once you submit all required information, ONRR will notify you of the date your application is complete.
