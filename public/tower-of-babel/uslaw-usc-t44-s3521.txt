44 U.S.C. § 3521 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:44:3521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3521
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 35 (Coordination Of Federal Information Policy); Subchapter I (Federal Information Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs to carry out the provisions of this subchapter, and for no other purpose, $8,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, and 2001.
