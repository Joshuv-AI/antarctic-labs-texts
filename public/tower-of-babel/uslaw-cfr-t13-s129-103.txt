13 C.F.R. § 129.103 — What are the applicable performance requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:13:129.103
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-129.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 129 (Contracts For Small Businesses Located In Disaster Areas, And Surplus Personal Property For Small Businesses Located In Disaster Areas, Puerto Rico, And Covered Territory Businesses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The performance requirements of § 125.6 of this chapter apply to small and socioeconomic set-asides under this part. A similarly situated entity as that term is used in § 125.6 of this chapter must qualify as a concern located in a disaster area.
