50 C.F.R. § 660.516 — Exempted fishing.
Citation: us:cfr:50:660.516

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-660.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 660 (Fisheries Off West Coast States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. In the interest of developing an efficient and productive fishery for CPS, the Regional Administrator may issue exempted fishing permits (EFP) for the harvest of CPS that otherwise would be prohibited.
(b) No exempted fishing for CPS may be conducted unless authorized by an EFP issued for the participating vessel in accordance with the criteria and procedures specified in § 600.745 of this chapter.
