20 U.S.C. § 92 — Admissions to marine biological station for pursuit of investigations
Citation: us:usc:20:92
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section92
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 5 (Government Collections And Institutions For Research, And Material For Educational Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The professors, instructors, and students of the several land-grant, agricultural, and mechanical colleges of the United States shall be admitted to the marine biological station on the Gulf of Mexico on the coast of Florida, to pursue such investigation in fish culture and biology as may be practicable, without cost to the Government, under such rules and regulations as may be from time to time prescribed by the Secretary of Interior.
