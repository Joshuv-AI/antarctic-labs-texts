40 C.F.R. § 63.9890 — What emission limitations must I meet?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.9890
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.9890
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must meet each emission limit in Table 1 to this subpart that applies to you.
(b) For each wet scrubber applied to meet any particulate matter, particulate matter less than 10 microns (PM10), chlorine, hydrochloric acid, or dioxins/furans emission limit in Table 1 to this subpart, you must maintain the hourly average pressure drop and scrubber liquid flow rate at or above the minimum level established during the initial or subsequent performance test.
