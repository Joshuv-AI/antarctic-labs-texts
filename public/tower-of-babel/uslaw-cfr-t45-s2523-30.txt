45 C.F.R. § 2523.30 — Must Federal agencies meet the requirements imposed on grantees under parts 2521 and 2522 of this chapter?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2523.30
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2523.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2523 (Agreements With Other Federal Agencies For The Provision Of Americorps Program Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, except as provided in § 2523.90. Federal agency programs must meet the same requirements and serve the same purposes as all other applicants seeking support under part 2522 of this chapter.
