16 U.S.C. § 730 — Violations of law or regulations; punishment
Citation: us:usc:16:730
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section730
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 8 (Upper Mississippi River National Wildlife And Fish Refuge). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who shall violate or fail to comply with any provision of or any regulation made pursuant to this chapter shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor, and upon conviction thereof shall be fined not more than $500 or be imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
