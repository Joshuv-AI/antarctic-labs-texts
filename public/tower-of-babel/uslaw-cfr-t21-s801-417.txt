21 C.F.R. § 801.417 — Chlorofluorocarbon propellants.
Citation: us:cfr:21:801.417
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-801.417
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 801 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of chlorofluorocarbon in devices as propellants in self-pressurized containers is generally prohibited except as provided in § 2.125 of this chapter.
