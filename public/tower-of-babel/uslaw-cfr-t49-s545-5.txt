49 C.F.R. § 545.5 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:545.5
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-545.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 545 (Federal Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Standard Phase-In And Small-Volume Line Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Production year means the 12-month period between September 1 of 2006 and August 31, 2007, inclusive.
Small-volume line means a line with an annual production of not more than 3,500 vehicles.
