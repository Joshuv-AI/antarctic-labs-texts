29 C.F.R. § 1955.18 — Provision for written comments.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1955.18
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1955.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1955 (Procedures For Withdrawal Of Approval Of State Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who is not a party may submit a written statement of position with 4 copies to either the Assistant Secretary or the State at any time during the proceeding which statement shall be made available to all parties and may be introduced into evidence by a party. Mere statements of approval or opposition to the plan without any documentary support shall not be considered as falling within this provision.
