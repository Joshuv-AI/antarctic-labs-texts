31 C.F.R. § 551.301 — African Union Mission in Somalia; AMISOM.
Citation: us:cfr:31:551.301
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-551.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 551 (Somalia Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term African Union Mission in Somalia or AMISOM means the mission authorized by the United Nations Security Council in Resolution 1744 of February 20, 2007, and reauthorized in subsequent resolutions, and includes its agencies, instrumentalities, and controlled entities.
