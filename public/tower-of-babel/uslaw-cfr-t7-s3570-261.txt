7 C.F.R. § 3570.261 — Environmental and intergovernmental review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3570.261
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3570.261
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3570 (Community Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All grants awarded under this subpart are subject to the environmental requirements of 7 CFR part 1940, subpart G. Technical Assistance under this program is categorically excluded unless extraordinary circumstances exist.
