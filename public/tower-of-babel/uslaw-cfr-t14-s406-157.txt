14 C.F.R. § 406.157 — Expert or opinion witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:14:406.157
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-406.157
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 406 (Investigations, Enforcement, And Administrative Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee of the FAA may not be called as an expert or opinion witness for any party other than the agency, in any proceeding governed by this part. An employee of a respondent may not be called as an expert or opinion witness for the complainant in any proceeding governed by this part to which the respondent is a party.
