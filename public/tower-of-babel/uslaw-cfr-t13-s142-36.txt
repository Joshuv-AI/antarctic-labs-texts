13 C.F.R. § 142.36 — Can I obtain judicial review?
Citation: us:cfr:13:142.36
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-142.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 142 (Administrative False Claims Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the initial decision is appealed, the decision of the Administrator is the final decision of SBA and is not subject to judicial review unless the defendant files a petition for judicial review within 60 days after the Administrator serves the defendant with a copy of the final decision.
