16 U.S.C. § 4607 — Slogan and logo
Citation: us:usc:16:4607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4607
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 66 (Take Pride In America Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The “Take Pride in America” slogan and logo, which are registered by the Department of the Interior, and the goodwill associated with such slogan and logo, shall be administered pursuant to the TPIA Program.
