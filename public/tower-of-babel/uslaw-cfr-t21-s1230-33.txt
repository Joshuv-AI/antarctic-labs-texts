21 C.F.R. § 1230.33 — Investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1230.33
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1230.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1230 (Regulations Under The Federal Caustic Poison Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorized agents in the employ of the Department of Health and Human Services may make investigations, including the inspection of premises where dangerous caustic and corrosive substances subject to the act are manufactured, packed, stored, or held for sale or distribution, and make examinations of freight and other transportation records.
