20 C.F.R. § 680.710 — What are the requirements for on-the-job training contracts for employed workers?
Citation: us:cfr:20:680.710
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-680.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 680 (Adult And Dislocated Worker Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OJT contracts may be written for eligible employed workers when:
(a) The employee is not earning a self-sufficient wage or wages comparable to or higher than wages from previous employment, as determined by Local WDB policy;
(b) The requirements in § 680.700 are met; and
(c) The OJT relates to the introduction of new technologies, introduction to new production or service procedures, upgrading to new jobs that require additional skills, workplace literacy, or other appropriate purposes identified by the Local WDB.
