45 C.F.R. § 63.33 — Treatment of animals.
Citation: us:cfr:45:63.33
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-63.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 63 (Grant Programs Administered By The Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Planning And Evaluation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If animals are utilized in any project receiving assistance, the applicant for such assistance shall provide assurances satisfactory to the Assistant Secretary that such animals will be provided with proper care and humane treatment; in accordance with the Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. 2131 et seq.) and regulations set forth in (9 CFR parts 1, 2, 3, 4).
