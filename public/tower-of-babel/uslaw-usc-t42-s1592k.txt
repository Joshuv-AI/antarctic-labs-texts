42 U.S.C. § 1592k — Determination of fair rentals and classes of occupants by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:42:1592k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1592k
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter IX (Defense Housing And Community Facilities And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall fix fair rentals based on the value thereof as determined by the Secretary which shall be charged for housing accommodations operated under this subchapter and may prescribe the class or classes of persons who may occupy such accommodations, preferences, or priorities in the rental thereof, and the terms, conditions, and period of such occupancy.
