24 C.F.R. § 3280.601 — Scope.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3280.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3280 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart G of this standard covers the plumbing materials, fixtures, and equipment installed within or on manufactured homes. It is the intent of this subpart to assure water supply, drain, waste and vent systems which permit satisfactory functioning and provide for health and safety under all conditions of normal use.
