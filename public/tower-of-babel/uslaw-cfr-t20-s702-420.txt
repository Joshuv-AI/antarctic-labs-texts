20 C.F.R. § 702.420 — Issuance of authorization; binding effect upon insurance carrier.
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.420
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The issuance of an authorization for treatment by the employer shall bind his insurance carrier to furnish and pay for such care and services.
