50 C.F.R. § 216.86 — Local regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:50:216.86
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-216.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 216 (Regulations Governing The Taking And Importing Of Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Local regulations will be published from time to time and will be brought to the attention of local residents and persons assigned to duty on the Islands by posting in public places and brought to the attention of tourists by personal notice.
