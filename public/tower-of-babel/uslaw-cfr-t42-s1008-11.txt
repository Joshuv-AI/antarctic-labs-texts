42 C.F.R. § 1008.11 — Who may submit a request.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1008.11
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1008.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1008 (Advisory Opinions By The Oig). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual or entity may submit a request to the OIG for an advisory opinion regarding an existing arrangement or one which the requestor in good faith specifically plans to undertake. The requestor must be a party to the arrangement, or proposed arrangement, that is the subject of the request.
