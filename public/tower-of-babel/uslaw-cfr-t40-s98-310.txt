40 C.F.R. § 98.310 — Definition of the source category.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.310
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The titanium dioxide production source category consists of facilities that use the chloride process to produce titanium dioxide.
