48 U.S.C. § 794 — Official reports
Citation: us:usc:48:794
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section794
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter II (The Executive And Government Officials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All reports required by law to be made by the governor or heads of departments to any official of the United States shall be made to an executive department of the Government of the United States to be designated by the President, and the President is authorized to place all matters pertaining to the government of Puerto Rico in the jurisdiction of such department.
