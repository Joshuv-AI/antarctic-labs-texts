48 C.F.R. § 8.405 — 8.405 Ordering procedures for Federal Supply Schedules.
Citation: us:cfr:48:8.405
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-8.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 8 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ordering activities shall use the ordering procedures of this section when placing an order or establishing a BPA for supplies or services. The procedures in this section apply to all schedules. For establishing BPAs and for orders under BPAs see 8.405-3.
