49 C.F.R. § 801.57 — Records compiled for law enforcement purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:49:801.57
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-801.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 801 (Public Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(7), any records compiled for law or regulatory enforcement are exempt from public disclosure to the extent that disclosure would interfere with enforcement, would be an unwarranted invasion of privacy, would disclose the identity of a confidential source, would disclose investigative procedures and practices, or would endanger the life or security of law enforcement personnel.
