21 U.S.C. § 468 — Cost of inspection; overtime
Citation: us:usc:21:468
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section468
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 10 (Poultry And Poultry Products Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The cost of inspection rendered under the requirements of this chapter, shall be borne by the United States, except the cost of overtime and holiday pay paid pursuant to the 1 section 2219a of title 7.
