32 C.F.R. § 150.25 — Suspension of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:32:150.25
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-150.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 150 (Courts Of Criminal Appeals Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For good cause shown, the Court acting as a whole or in panel may suspend the requirements or provisions of any of this part in a particular case on petition of a party or on its own motion and may order proceedings in accordance with its direction.
