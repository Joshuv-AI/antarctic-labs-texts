31 C.F.R. § 548.318 — Structured.
Citation: us:cfr:31:548.318
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-548.318
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 548 (Belarus Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term structured, with respect to a transaction, has the meaning given the term “structure (structuring)” in 31 CFR 1010.100(xx) (or any corresponding similar regulation or ruling).
