25 C.F.R. § 173.0 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:25:173.0
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-173.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 173 (Concessions, Permits And Leases On Lands Withdrawn Or Acquired In Connection With Indian Irrigation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are promulgated governing the granting of concessions, business, agricultural and grazing leases or permits on reservoir sites, reserves for canals or flowage areas, and other lands withdrawn or otherwise acquired in connection with the San Carlos, Fort Hall, Flathead and Duck Valley or Western Shoshone irrigation projects.
