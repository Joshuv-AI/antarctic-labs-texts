39 C.F.R. § 3021.1 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3021.1
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3021.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3021 (Rules For Appeals Of Postal Service Determinations To Close Or Consolidate Post Offices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following definitions apply in this part:
(a) Final determination means the written determination and findings required by 39 U.S.C. 404(d)(3).
(b) Petitioner means a person who files a Petition for Review.
(c) Petition for Review means a written document that the Commission accepts as an appeal of a post office closing or consolidation.
