44 U.S.C. § 2113 — Depository for agreements between States
Citation: us:usc:44:2113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2113
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 21 (National Archives And Records Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Archivist may receive duplicate originals or authenticated copies of agreements or compacts entered into under the Constitution and laws of the United States, between States of the Union, and take necessary actions for their preservation and servicing.
