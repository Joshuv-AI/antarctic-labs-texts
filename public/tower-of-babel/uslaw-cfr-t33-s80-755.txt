33 C.F.R. § 80.755 — Anclote, FL to the Suncoast Keys, FL.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.755
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.755
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except inside lines specifically described in this section, the 72 COLREGS shall apply on the bays, bayous, creeks, marinas, and rivers from Anclote to the Suncoast Keys.
(b) A north-south line drawn at longitude 82°38.3′ W. across the Chassahowitzka River Entrance.
