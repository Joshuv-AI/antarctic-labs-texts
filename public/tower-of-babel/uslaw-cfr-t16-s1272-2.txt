16 C.F.R. § 1272.2 — Prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1272.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1272.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1272 (Marking Of Toy, Look-Alike, And Imitation Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall manufacture, enter into commerce, ship, transport, or receive any toy, look-alike, or imitation firearm (“device”) covered by this part as set forth in § 1272.1 unless such device contains, or has affixed to it, one of the markings set forth in § 1272.3, or unless this prohibition has been waived by § 1272.4.
