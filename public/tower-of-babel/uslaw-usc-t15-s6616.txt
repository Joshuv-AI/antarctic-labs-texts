15 U.S.C. § 6616 — Admissible evidence ultimate issue in State courts
Citation: us:usc:15:6616
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6616
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 92 (Year 2000 Computer Date Change). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any party to a Y2K action in a State court in a State that has not adopted a rule of evidence substantially similar to Rule 704 of the Federal Rules of Evidence may introduce in such action evidence that would be admissible if Rule 704 applied in that jurisdiction.
