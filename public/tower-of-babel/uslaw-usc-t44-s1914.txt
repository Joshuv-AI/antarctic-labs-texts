44 U.S.C. § 1914 — Implementation of depository library program by Director of the Government Publishing Office
Citation: us:usc:44:1914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1914
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 19 (Depository Library Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office, with the approval of the Joint Committee on Printing, as provided by section 103 of this title, may use any measures he considers necessary for the economical and practical implementation of this chapter.
