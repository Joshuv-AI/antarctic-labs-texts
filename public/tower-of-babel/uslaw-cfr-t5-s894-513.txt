5 C.F.R. § 894.513 — Do I have to elect FEDVIP coverage each year in order to remain covered?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.513
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.513
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. If you do not change or cancel your enrollment, and if your enrollment does not terminate pursuant to this part, then your current enrollment will continue into the next year. Before open season, you should review the plan brochure for any changes in benefits and premiums for the next year.
