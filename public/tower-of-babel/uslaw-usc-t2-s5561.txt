2 U.S.C. § 5561 — Reporters for House of Representatives
Citation: us:usc:2:5561
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5561
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter IV (Clerk). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person shall be employed as a reporter for the House of Representatives without the approval of the Speaker.
