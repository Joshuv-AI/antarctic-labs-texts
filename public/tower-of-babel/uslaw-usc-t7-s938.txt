7 U.S.C. § 938 — Full faith and credit of the United States
Citation: us:usc:7:938
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section938
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter III (Rural Electric And Telephone Direct Loan Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any contract of insurance or guarantee executed by the Secretary under this subchapter shall be an obligation supported by the full faith and credit of the United States and incontestable except for fraud or misrepresentation of which the holder had actual knowledge at the time it became a holder.
