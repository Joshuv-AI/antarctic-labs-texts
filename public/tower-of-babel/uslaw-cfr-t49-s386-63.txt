49 C.F.R. § 386.63 — Decision on review.
Citation: us:cfr:49:386.63
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-386.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 386 (Rules Of Practice For Fmcsa Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon review of a decision, the Agency Decisionmaker may adopt, modify, or set aside the administrative law judge's findings of fact and conclusions of law. He/she may also remand proceedings to the administrative law judge with instructions for such further proceedings as he/she deems appropriate. If not remanded, the Agency Decisionmaker shall issue a final order disposing of the proceedings, and serve it on all parties.
