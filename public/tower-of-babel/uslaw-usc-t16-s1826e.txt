16 U.S.C. § 1826e — Negotiations
Citation: us:usc:16:1826e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1826e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 38 (Fishery Conservation And Management); Subchapter III (Foreign Fishing And International Fishery Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, on behalf of the United States, shall seek to enhance the implementation and effectiveness of the United Nations General Assembly resolutions and decisions regarding the moratorium on large-scale driftnet fishing on the high seas through appropriate international agreements and organizations.
