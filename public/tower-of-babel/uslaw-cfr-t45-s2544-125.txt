45 C.F.R. § 2544.125 — Who has the authority to solicit and accept or reject a donation?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2544.125
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2544.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2544 (Solicitation And Acceptance Of Donations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Corporation has the authority to solicit, accept, or reject a donation offered to the Corporation and to make the determinations described in § 2544.130 (c) and (d). The CEO may delegate this authority in writing to other officials of the Corporation.
