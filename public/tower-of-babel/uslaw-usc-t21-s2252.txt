21 U.S.C. § 2252 — Compliance with international agreements
Citation: us:usc:21:2252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2252
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 27 (Food Safety Modernization); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act (or an amendment made by this Act) shall be construed in a manner inconsistent with the agreement establishing the World Trade Organization or any other treaty or international agreement to which the United States is a party.
