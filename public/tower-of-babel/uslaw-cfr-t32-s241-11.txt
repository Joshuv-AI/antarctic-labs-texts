32 C.F.R. § 241.11 — Numerical limitation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:241.11
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-241.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 241 (Pilot Program For Temporary Exchange Of Information Technology Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ITEP Pilot is an opportunity for the exchange of knowledge, experience and skills between DoD and the private sector. The DoD has the flexibility to send their employees to the private sector or receive private sector employees, or participate in a one-for-one exchange. In no event may more than 10 employees participate in assignments under this section at any given time.
