42 C.F.R. § 64.3 — Who is eligible for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:42:64.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-64.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 64 (National Library Of Medicine Training Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise prohibited by law, any public or private nonprofit entity is eligible for a training grant.
