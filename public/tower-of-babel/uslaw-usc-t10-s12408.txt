10 U.S.C. § 12408 — National Guard in Federal service: physical examination
Citation: us:usc:10:12408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12408
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1211 (National Guard Members In Federal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Under regulations prescribed by the President, each member of the National Guard called into Federal service under section 12301(a), 12302, or 12304 of this title shall be examined as to physical fitness, without further commission or enlistment.
(b)
Immediately before such a member is mustered out of Federal service, he shall be examined as to physical fitness. The record of this examination shall be retained by the United States.
