7 C.F.R. § 2710.2 — Public inspection and copying.

Creator: Office Of Information Resources Management, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2710 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Background. 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2) required that each agency make certain kinds of records available for public inspection and copying.
(b) Procedure. Persons wishing to gain access to OIRM records should contact the Information Access & Disclosure Officer by writing to the address shown in 2710.4(b)(2).
