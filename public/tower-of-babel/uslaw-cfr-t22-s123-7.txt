22 C.F.R. § 123.7 — Exports to warehouses or distribution points outside the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:22:123.7
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-123.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 123 (Licenses For The Export And Temporary Import Of Defense Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the exemption under § 123.16(b)(1) is used, a license is required to export defense articles to a warehouse or distribution point outside the United States for subsequent resale and will normally be granted only if an agreement has been approved pursuant to § 124.14 of this subchapter.
