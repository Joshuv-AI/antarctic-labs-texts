16 U.S.C. § 2440 — Jurisdiction of courts
Citation: us:usc:16:2440
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2440
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 44A (Antarctic Marine Living Resources Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any case or controversy arising under the provisions of this chapter or of any regulation promulgated under this chapter.
