7 C.F.R. § 319.75-8 — -8 Ports of entry.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.75-8
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.75-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any regulated article shall be imported only at a Customs designated port of entry indicated in 19 CFR 101.3(b)(1) and found by the Administrator and specified on the permit issued pursuant to § 319.75-3 to have a nongovernmental fumigator available at the port to treat such regulated article pursuant to § 319.75-4. It is the responsibility of the importer to arrange with the nongovernmental fumigator for treatment of the article.
