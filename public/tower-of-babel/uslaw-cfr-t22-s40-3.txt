22 C.F.R. § 40.3 — Entry into areas under U.S. administration.
Citation: us:cfr:22:40.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-40.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 40 (Regulations Pertaining To Both Nonimmigrants And Immigrants Under The Immigration And Nationality Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An immigrant or nonimmigrant seeking to enter an area which is under U.S. administration but which is not within the “United States”, as defined in INA 101(a)(38), is not required by the INA to be documented with a visa unless the authority contained in INA 215 has been invoked.
