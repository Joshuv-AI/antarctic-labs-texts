42 C.F.R. § 457.1222 — Provider-enrollee communication.
Citation: us:cfr:42:457.1222
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-457.1222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 457 (Allotments And Grants To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must ensure, through its contracts, that each MCO, PIHP, and PAHP protects communications between providers and enrollees in accordance with the terms of § 438.102 of this chapter.
