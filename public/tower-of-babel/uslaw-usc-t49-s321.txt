49 U.S.C. § 321 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 3 (General Duties And Powers); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, “aeronautics”, “air commerce”, and “air navigation facility” have the same meanings given those terms in section 40102(a) of this title.
