12 C.F.R. § 313.122 — Notification of debt to FMS.
Citation: us:cfr:12:313.122
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-313.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 313 (Procedures For Collection Of Corporate Debt, Criminal Restitution Debt, And Civil Money Penalty Debt). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC shall notify FMS of the amount of any past due, legally enforceable non-tax debt owed to it by a person, for the purpose of collecting such debt by tax refund offset. Notification and referral to FMS of such debts does not preclude FDIC's use of any other debt collection procedures, such as wage garnishment, either separately or in conjunction with tax refund offset.
