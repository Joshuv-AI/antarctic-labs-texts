40 C.F.R. § 415.600 — Applicability; description of the stannic oxide production subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:415.600
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-415.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 415 (Inorganic Chemicals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the production of stannic oxide by the reaction of tin metal with air or oxygen.
