47 C.F.R. § 69.414 — Lifeline assistance expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.414
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Expenses allocated to the interstate jurisdiction pursuant to § 36.741 shall be assigned to the Carrier Common Line element until March 31, 1989. Beginning April 1, 1989, such expenses shall be assigned to the Lifeline Assistance element.
