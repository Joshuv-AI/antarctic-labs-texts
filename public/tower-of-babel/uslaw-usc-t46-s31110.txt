46 U.S.C. § 31110 — Subpoenas to officers or members of crew
Citation: us:usc:46:31110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31110
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 311 (Suits Involving Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer or member of the crew of a public vessel may not be subpoenaed in a civil action under this chapter without the consent of—
(1)
the Secretary of the department or the head of the independent establishment having control of the vessel at the time the cause of action arose; or
(2)
the master or commanding officer of the vessel at the time the subpoena is issued.
