25 C.F.R. § 101.8 — Environmental and Flood Disaster Acts.
Citation: us:cfr:25:101.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-101.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 101 (Loans To Indians From The Revolving Loan Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loans will not be approved until there is assurance of compliance with any applicable provisions of the Flood Disaster Protection Act of 1973 (Pub. L. 93-234, 87 Stat. 975), the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (Pub. L. 91-190), (42 U.S.C. 4321) and Executive Order 11514.
