38 U.S.C. § 8133 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:38:8133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8133
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 81 (Acquisition And Operation Of Hospital And Domiciliary Facilities; Procurement And Supply; Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property); Subchapter III (State Home Facilities For Furnishing Domiciliary, Nursing Home, And Hospital Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this subchapter. Sums appropriated pursuant to this section shall be used for making grants to States which have submitted, and have had approved by the Secretary, applications for carrying out the purposes and meeting the requirements of this subchapter.
(b)
Sums appropriated pursuant to subsection (a) of this section shall remain available until expended.
