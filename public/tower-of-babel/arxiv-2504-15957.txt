Kato-Milne cohomology group over rational function fields in characteristic 2, II
Ahmed Laghribi, Trisha Maiti

Abstract
. Our aim in this paper is to prove in the setting of Kato-Milne cohomology in characteristic 2 2 an exact sequence which is analogue to the Milnor-Scharlau sequence [ 8 , Theorem 6.2] . This is an extension of the Milnor exact sequence proved in [ 6 ] .

Let
F

For
