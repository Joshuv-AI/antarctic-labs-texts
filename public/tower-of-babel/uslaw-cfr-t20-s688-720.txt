20 C.F.R. § 688.720 — What environmental protection laws apply to the YouthBuild program?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.720
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

YouthBuild program grantees are required, where applicable, to comply with all environmental protection statutes and regulations.
