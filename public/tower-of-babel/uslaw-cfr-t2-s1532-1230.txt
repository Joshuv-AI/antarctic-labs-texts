2 C.F.R. § 1532.1230 — How will the EPA debarring official notify me of the reinstatement decision?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1532.1230
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1532.1230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1532 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The EPA debarring official will notify you of the reinstatement decision in writing, using the same methods for communicating debarment or suspension action notices under 2 CFR 180.615.
