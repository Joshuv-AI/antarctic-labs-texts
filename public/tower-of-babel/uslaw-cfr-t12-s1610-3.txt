12 C.F.R. § 1610.3 — Treatment of collected information.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1610.3
Creator: Office Of Financial Research, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1610.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1610 (Regulatory Data Collections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office will treat any financial transaction data or position data submitted to the Data Center under this part in accordance with the relevant provisions of law, including 12 U.S.C. 5343(b) and 5344(b).
