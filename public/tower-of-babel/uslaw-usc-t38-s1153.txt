38 U.S.C. § 1153 — Aggravation
Citation: us:usc:38:1153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1153
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Service-Connected Disability Or Death); Subchapter VI (General Compensation Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A preexisting injury or disease will be considered to have been aggravated by active military, naval, air, or space service, where there is an increase in disability during such service, unless there is a specific finding that the increase in disability is due to the natural progress of the disease.
