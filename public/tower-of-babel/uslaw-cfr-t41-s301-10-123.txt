41 C.F.R. § 301-10.123 — -10.123 Compensation for voluntarily vacating a seat.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.123
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Employees may keep airline compensation for voluntarily vacating a seat under two conditions:
(1) Voluntarily vacating the seat will not interfere with performing official duties; and
(2) Any additional travel expenses resulting from vacating the seat are personally borne and not reimbursed by the Government.
(b) If volunteering causes travel delays during duty hours, the agency will charge the employee annual leave for the additional hours.
