6 U.S.C. § 969 — Revalidation
Citation: us:usc:6:969
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section969
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 3 (Security And Accountability For Every Port); Subchapter II (Security Of The International Supply Chain); Part B (Customs–Trade Partnership Against Terrorism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, acting through the Commissioner, shall develop and implement—
(1)
a revalidation process for Tier 2 and Tier 3 participants;
(2)
a framework based upon objective criteria for identifying participants for periodic revalidation not less frequently than once during each 4-year period following the initial validation; and
(3)
an annual plan for revalidation that includes—
(A)
performance measures;
(B)
an assessment of the personnel needed to perform the revalidations; and
(C)
the number of participants that will be revalidated during the following year.
