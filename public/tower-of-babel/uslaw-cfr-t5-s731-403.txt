5 C.F.R. § 731.403 — Answer.
Citation: us:cfr:5:731.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-731.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 731 (Suitability And Fitness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A respondent may answer the charges in writing and furnish documentation and/or affidavits in support of the answer. To be timely, a written answer must be submitted no more than 30 days after the date of the notice of proposed action.
