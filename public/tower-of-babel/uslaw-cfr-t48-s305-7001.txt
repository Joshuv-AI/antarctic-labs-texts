48 C.F.R. § 305.7001 — 305.7001 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:48:305.7001
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-305.7001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 305 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to appropriations acts, this subpart prescribes requirements for posting presolicitation and award notices for actions funded in whole or in part from the Prevention and Public Health Fund (PPHF). The requirements of this subpart enhance transparency to the public.
