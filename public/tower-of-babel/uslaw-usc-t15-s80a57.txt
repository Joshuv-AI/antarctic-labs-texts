15 U.S.C. § 80a–57 — Changes in investment policy
Citation: us:usc:15:80a–57
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80a–57
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter I (Investment Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No business development company shall, unless authorized by the vote of a majority of its outstanding voting securities or partnership interests, change the nature of its business so as to cease to be, or to withdraw its election as, a business development company.
