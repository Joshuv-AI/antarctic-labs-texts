49 C.F.R. § 40.89 — What are the adulterant cutoff concentrations for initial and confirmation urine tests?
Citation: us:cfr:49:40.89
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-40.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 40 (Procedures For Transportation Workplace Drug And Alcohol Testing Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As a laboratory, you must use the cutoff concentrations for the initial and confirmation adulterant testing as required by the HHS Mandatory Guidelines and you must use two separate aliquots—one for the initial test and another for the confirmation test.
(b) As a laboratory, you must report results at or above the cutoffs (or for pH, at or above or below the values, as appropriate) as adulterated and provide the numerical value that supports the adulterated result.
