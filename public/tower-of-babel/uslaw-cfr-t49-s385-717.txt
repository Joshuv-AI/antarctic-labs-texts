49 C.F.R. § 385.717 — Applicability of safety fitness and enforcement procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:49:385.717
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-385.717
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 385 (Safety Fitness Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At all times during which a non-North America-domiciled motor carrier is subject to the safety monitoring system in this subpart, it is also subject to the general safety fitness procedures established in subpart A of this part and to compliance and enforcement procedures applicable to all carriers regulated by the FMCSA.
