41 C.F.R. § 105-1.000-50 — -1.000-50 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-1.000-50
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-1.000-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part describes the method by which the General Services Administration (GSA) implements and supplements the Federal Property Management Regulations (FPMR) and implements certain regulations prescribed by other agencies. It contains procedures that implement and supplement part 101-1 of the FPMR.
