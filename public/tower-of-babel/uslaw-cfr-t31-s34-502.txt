31 C.F.R. § 34.502 — Allocation of funds—Spill Impact Component.
Citation: us:cfr:31:34.502
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-34.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 34 (Resources And Ecosystems Sustainability, Tourist Opportunities, And Revived Economies Of The Gulf Coast States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council will allocate amounts to the Gulf Coast States based on the Act and regulations promulgated by the Council. The Council will make allocated funds available through grants for activities described in a State Expenditure Plan approved by the Council.
