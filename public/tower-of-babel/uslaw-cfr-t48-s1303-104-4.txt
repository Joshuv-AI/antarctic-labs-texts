48 C.F.R. § 1303.104-4 — 1303.104-4 Disclosure, protection and marking of contractor bid or proposal information and source selection information.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1303.104-4
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1303.104-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1303 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contractor bid or proposal information and source selection information must be protected from unauthorized disclosure in accordance with FAR Parts 3, 14 and 15, and CAM 1315.3.
