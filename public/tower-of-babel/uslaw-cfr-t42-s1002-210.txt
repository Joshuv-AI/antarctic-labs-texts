42 C.F.R. § 1002.210 — General authority.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1002.210
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1002.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1002 (Program Integrity—State-Initiated Exclusions From Medicaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State agency must have administrative procedures in place that enable it to exclude an individual or entity for any reason for which the Secretary could exclude such individual or entity under parts 1001 or 1003 of this chapter. The period of such exclusion is at the discretion of the State agency.
