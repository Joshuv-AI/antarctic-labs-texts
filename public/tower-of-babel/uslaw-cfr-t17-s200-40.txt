17 C.F.R. § 200.40 — Joint disposition of business by Commission meeting.
Citation: us:cfr:17:200.40
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-200.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 200 (Organization; Conduct And Ethics; And Information And Requests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any meeting of the Commission that is subject to the provisions of the Government in the Sunshine Act, 5 U.S.C. 552b, shall be held in accordance with subpart I of this part. The Commission's Secretary shall prepare and maintain a Minute Record reflecting the official action taken at such meetings.
