14 U.S.C. § 1921 — Corps of Cadets authorized strength
Citation: us:usc:14:1921
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1921
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 19 (Coast Guard Academy); Subchapter II (Cadets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The number of cadets appointed annually to the Academy shall be as determined by the Secretary but the number appointed in any one year shall not exceed six hundred.
