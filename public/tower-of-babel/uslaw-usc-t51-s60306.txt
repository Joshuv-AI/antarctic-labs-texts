51 U.S.C. § 60306 — Education
Citation: us:usc:51:60306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60306
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 603 (Remote Sensing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall establish an educational outreach program to increase awareness at institutions of higher education and State, local, regional, and tribal agencies of the potential applications of remote sensing and other geospatial information and awareness of the need for geospatial workforce development.
