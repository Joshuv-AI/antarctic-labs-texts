49 U.S.C. § 47527 — Liability of the United States Government for noise damages
Citation: us:usc:49:47527
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section47527
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Airport Development And Noise); Chapter 475 (Noise); Subchapter II (National Aviation Noise Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a proposed airport noise or access restriction is disapproved under this subchapter, the United States Government shall assume liability for noise damages only to the extent that a taking has occurred as a direct result of the disapproval. The United States Court of Federal Claims has exclusive jurisdiction of a civil action under this section.
