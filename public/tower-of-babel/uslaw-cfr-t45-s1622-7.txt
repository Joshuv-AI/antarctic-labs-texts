45 C.F.R. § 1622.7 — Certification by the General Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1622.7
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1622.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1622 (Public Access To Meetings Under The Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a meeting or portion thereof is closed, the General Counsel shall publicly certify that, in his opinion, the meeting may be so closed to the public and shall state each relevant exemption. A copy of the certification, together with a statement from the presiding officer of the meeting setting forth the time and place of the meeting and the persons present, shall be retained by the Corporation.
