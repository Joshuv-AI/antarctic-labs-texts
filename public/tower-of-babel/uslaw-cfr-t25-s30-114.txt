25 C.F.R. § 30.114 — What should a tribal governing body or school board include in a proposal for alternative requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:25:30.114
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-30.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 30 (Standards, Assessments, And Accountability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proposals for alternative requirements must include an explanation of how the alternative proposal meets the requirements of section 1111 of the Act, taking into consideration the unique circumstances and needs of BIE-funded schools and the students served at such schools.
