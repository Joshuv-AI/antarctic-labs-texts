25 C.F.R. § 243.6 — Which sales or transfers of Alaskan reindeer do not require a permit?
Citation: us:cfr:25:243.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-243.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 243 (Reindeer In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following transfers do not require a permit:
(a) Sale or transfer by Alaska Natives of dead reindeer or reindeer products; and
(b) Sale of transfer of live reindeer between Alaska Natives or Native reindeer organizations.
