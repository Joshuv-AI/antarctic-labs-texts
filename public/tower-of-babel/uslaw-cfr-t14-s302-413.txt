14 C.F.R. § 302.413 — Evidence of previous violations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:302.413
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-302.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence of previous violations by any person or of any provision of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII or any requirement thereunder found by the Department or a court in any other proceeding or criminal or civil action may, if relevant and material, be admitted in any enforcement proceeding involving such person.
