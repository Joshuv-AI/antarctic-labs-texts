48 C.F.R. § 647.305-71 — 647.305-71 Shipping instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:647.305-71
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-647.305-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 647 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 652.247-71, Shipping Instructions, in solicitations and contracts with a source in the United States if overseas shipment of supplies is required.
