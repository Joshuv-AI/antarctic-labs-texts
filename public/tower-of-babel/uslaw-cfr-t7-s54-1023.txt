7 C.F.R. § 54.1023 — Denial or withdrawal of appeal service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:54.1023
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-54.1023
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 54 (Meats, Prepared Meats, And Meat Products (Grading, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for appeal service may be rejected or such service may be otherwise denied to or withdrawn from any person in accordance with the procedure set forth in § 54.1013(a), if it appears that the person or product involved is not eligible for appeal service under § 54.1020, or that the identity of the piece of equipment, portion of a piece of equipment, or utensil has been lost; or for any of the causes set forth in § 54.1032.
