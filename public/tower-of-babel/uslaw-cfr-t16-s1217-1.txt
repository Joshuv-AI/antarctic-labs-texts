16 C.F.R. § 1217.1 — Scope, application, and effective date.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1217 (Safety Standard For Toddler Beds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part 1217 establishes a consumer product safety standard for toddler beds manufactured or imported on or after October 20, 2011.
