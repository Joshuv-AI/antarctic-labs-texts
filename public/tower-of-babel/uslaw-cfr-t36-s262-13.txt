36 C.F.R. § 262.13 — Removal of obstructions.
Citation: us:cfr:36:262.13
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-262.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 262 (Law Enforcement Support Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Forest Officer may remove or have removed a vehicle or other object on National Forest System lands that is abandoned or vandalized or that poses an impediment or hazard to the safety, convenience, or comfort of National Forest System visitors.
