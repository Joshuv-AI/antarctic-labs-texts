29 C.F.R. § 457.17 — Administrative Law Judge.
Citation: us:cfr:29:457.17
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-457.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 457 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Administrative Law Judge means the Chief Administrative Law Judge or any Administrative Law Judge designated by the Chief Administrative Law Judge to conduct a hearing in cases under 5 U.S.C. 7120 or 22 U.S.C. 4117 as implemented by part 458 of this subchapter and such other matters as may be assigned.
