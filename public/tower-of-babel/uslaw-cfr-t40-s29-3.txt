40 C.F.R. § 29.3 — What programs and activities of the Environmental Protection Agency are subject to these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:40:29.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-29.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 29 (Intergovernmental Review Of Environmental Protection Agency Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator publishes in the Federal Register a list of the EPA programs and activities that are subject to these regulations and identifies which of these are subject to the requirements of section 204 of the Demonstration Cities and Metropolitan Development Act.
