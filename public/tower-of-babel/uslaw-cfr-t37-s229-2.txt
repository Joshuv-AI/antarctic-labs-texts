37 C.F.R. § 229.2 — Final determination certification.
Citation: us:cfr:37:229.2
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-229.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 229 (Records And Publication). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon a party's written request to the Board and payment of the appropriate fee pursuant to 37 CFR 201.3, the Board will provide a certified copy of a proceeding's final or amended final determination. A party who wishes to engage in this service should contact the Board for further instructions on how to make this request.
