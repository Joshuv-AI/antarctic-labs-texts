39 C.F.R. § 263.4 — Records disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:39:263.4
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-263.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 263 (Records Retention And Disposition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All disposals of records containing sensitive information, i.e. transfers to records storage centers, destruction, transfers external to the USPS, and maintenance of accounting records regarding such disposal, must be accomplished in accordance with procedures issued by the Records Office.
