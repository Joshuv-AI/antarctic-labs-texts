41 C.F.R. § 302-17.5 — -17.5 When an expense is considered completed in a specific tax year.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-17.5
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-17.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-17 (17—Taxes On Relocation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A reimbursement, allowance, or direct payment to a vendor is considered completed in a specific tax year if the money was actually disbursed to the employee or vendor during the tax year in question.
