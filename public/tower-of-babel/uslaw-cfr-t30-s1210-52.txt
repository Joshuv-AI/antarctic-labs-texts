30 C.F.R. § 1210.52 — What royalty reports must I submit?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1210.52
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1210.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1210 (Forms And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit a completed Form ONRR-2014, Report of Sales and Royalty Remittance, to ONRR with:
(a) All royalty payments; and
(b) Rents on nonproducing leases, where specified in the lease.
