42 C.F.R. § 84.112 — Canisters and cartridges in parallel; resistance requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.112
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where two or more canisters or cartridges are used in parallel, their resistance to airflow shall be essentially equal.
