5 C.F.R. § 297.302 — Time limits.
Citation: us:cfr:5:297.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-297.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 297 (Privacy Procedures For Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The system manager should acknowledge receipt of an amendment request within 10 working days and issue a determination as soon as practicable. This timeframe begins when the request is received by the proper Office or agency official.
