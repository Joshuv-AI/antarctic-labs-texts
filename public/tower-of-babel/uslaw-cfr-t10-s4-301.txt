10 C.F.R. § 4.301 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:4.301
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-4.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 4 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to set forth NRC policies and procedures under the Age Discrimination Act of 1975 which prohibits discrimination on the basis of age in programs or activities receiving Federal financial assistance.
