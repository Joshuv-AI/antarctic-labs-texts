40 C.F.R. § 463.30 — Applicability; description of the finishing water subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:463.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-463.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 463 (Plastics Molding And Forming Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges of pollutants from processes in the finishing water subcategory to waters of the United States and the introduction of such pollutants into publicly owned treatment works. Processes in the finishing water subcategory are processes where water comes in contact with the plastic product during finishing.
