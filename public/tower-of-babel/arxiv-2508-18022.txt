Precision Measurements of the Electroweak Mixing Angle in the Region of the Z pole
Arie Bodek, Hyon-San Seo, Un-Ki Yang

Abstract
We review the current status and techniques used in precision measurements of the effective leptonic weak mixing angle sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} (a fundamental parameter of the Standard Model (SM)) in the region of the Z pole with emphasis on hadron colliders. We also build on these techniques to extract the most precise single measurement to date of sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} from a new analysis of the published forward-backward asymmetry ( A FB A_{\rm FB} ) in Drell-Yan dielpton production in proton-proton collisions at a center of mass energy of 13 TeV measured by the CMS collaboration at the large hadron collider. The uncertainty in sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} published by CMS is dominated by uncertainties in Parton Distribution Functions (PDFs), which are reduced by PDF profiling using the dilepton mass dependence of A FB A_{\rm FB} . Our new extraction of sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} from the CMS values of A FB A_{\rm FB} includes profiling with additional new CMS measurements of the W W -boson decay lepton asymmetry, and W/Z cross section ratio at 13 TeV. We obtain the most precise single measurement of sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} to date of 0.23153 ± \pm 0.00023, which is in excellent agreement with the SM prediction of 0.23161 ± \pm 0.00004. We also discuss outlook for future measurements at the LHC including more precise measurements of sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} , a measurement of sin 2 ⁡ θ eff ℓ \sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff} for b-quarks in the initial state, and a measurement of the running of sin 2 ⁡ θ MS ¯ ​ ( μ ) \sin^{2}\theta^{\overline{\rm MS}}(\mu) up to 3 TeV.



1
Introduction

The weak mixing angle
\sin^{2}\theta_{\rm W}
is a fundamental parameter of the Standard Model (SM) of particle physics. In the on-shell scheme,
\sin^{2}\theta_{\rm W}
= 1-
M^{2}_{W}/M^{2}_{Z}
, where

M_{W}
and
M_{Z}
are the masses of the
W
and
Z
bosons, respectively. The effective leptonic weak mixing angle
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
(which can be expressed in terms of
\sin^{2}\theta_{\rm W}
with a radiative correction factor) is the quantity more directly extracted from experiment.

In the SM
\sin^{2}\theta_{\rm W}
,
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
and
M_{W}
are predicted to very high accuracy by using the experimental values of
M_{Z}
,
the Fermi constant, the fine structure constant, the mass of Higgs boson, the quark masses,
and the value of the Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) strong coupling (
\alpha_{s}
) at
M_{Z}
. The predictions of the 2025 SM fit
[
1
,
2
]
are

\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
=0.23161
\pm
0.00004, and
M_{W}
= 80.3560
\pm
0.0050 GeV.
The two most precise measurements of
M_{W}

are in disagreement with each other. The measurement by the CDF collaboration
[
3
]
of 80.4335
\pm
0.0094 GeV is about 9 standard deviations lower than the SM value, and the measurement
by the CMS collaboration
[
4
]
of 80.3602
\pm
0.0099 GeV is in good agreement with the SM. The CDF measurement of
M_{W}
can be accommodated by introducing new physics processes beyond the SM. For example,
using the CDF measurement of
M_{W}
as input a Two Higgs Doublet Model
[
5
]
(2HDM) predicts
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
= 0.23110
\pm
0.00010.

The weak mixing angle has been measured in several experiments including atomic parity violation, electron scattering, and neutrino experiments. However, the most precise are from measurements of asymmetries in the region of the
Z
pole in
e^{+}e^{-}
and hadron colliders.

2
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
measurements in
e^{+}e^{-}
colliders

The two most precise measurements
[
6
]
of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
in
e^{+}e^{-}
colliders differ by 3.2 standard deviations. A value of
0.23221
\pm
0.00029 from the measurements of the b-quark forward-backward asymmetry
A^{0,b}_{FB}
at the LEP experiments at CERN, and a value of 0.23098
\pm
0.00026 from the measurement of the asymmetry of Z boson production cross sections for left-handed and right-handed electrons (
A_{LR}
)
by the SLD experiment at SLAC. It is interesting to note that the SLD
A_{LR}
measurement of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
is lower than the SM prediction and is more consistent with the predictions of 2HDM, while the
LEP measurement from the b-quark forward-backward asymmetry (
A^{o,b}_{FB}
) is higher than the SM prediction. However, the average of all six different measurements of

\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
at LEP and SLD (shown in
Fig.
1
) is 0.23153
\pm
0.00016 which is in excellent agreement with the SM value. In addition to
A_{LR}
and
A^{0,b}_{FB}
the other four measurements shown in Fig.
1
are extracted from:
A^{0,l}_{FB}
(which is the average from

A^{0,e}_{FB}
,

A^{0,\mu}_{FB}
, and
A^{0,\tau}_{FB}
),
P_{\tau}

(parity violation in
\tau
decays),
A^{0,c}_{FB}
and
A^{had}_{FB}
.

The measured asymmetry of b-quarks in the final state is a product of quark and electron asymmetries
A_{FB}^{0,b}=A_{e}A_{b}
. Here
A_{e}
is sensitive to

\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
while
A_{b}
is rather insensitive (by a factor of 100) to SM parameters. The deviation of
A_{FB}^{0,b}
from the SM prediction is either a fluctuation, or the consequence of new physics
[
6
]
.
Therefore, new precision measurements
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
in processes involving both light and heavy quarks in the initial or final state are an important tool in searches for physics beyond the SM.

3
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
measurements in hadron colliders

With recent advances in experimental and analysis techniques
[
7
,
8
,
9
]
, measurements of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
at hadron colliders extracted from the forward-backward asymmetry in Drell-Yan dilepton events have become competitive with the published measurements by
e^{+}e^{-}
collider experiments.
In this paper we report on the extraction of the world’s best measurements of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
from an updated analysis of published asymmetries of Drell-Yan dilepton events at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) as described below.
In hadron collisions the Drell-Yan measurements are dominated by quark-antiquark annihilation of light quarks in the initial state.

In Drell-Yan production of dilepton events in proton-proton collisions at the LHC the positive z direction is defined as the direction of the rapidity of the dilepton pair.
The angular distribution of the negative lepton (integrated over all
Z
boson transverse momenta) in the Collins-Soper frame
[
10
]
is given by the following equation:

\frac{d\sigma}{d\cos\theta}\propto 1+\cos^{2}\theta+\frac{A_{0}}{2}(1-3\cos^{2}\theta)+A_{4}\cos\theta,

(1)

where
A_{0}
and
A_{4}
are functions of the rapidity (
y
) and mass (
M
) of the dilepton pair.
When integrating over
\cos\theta
(full acceptance)
A^{full}_{FB}=(3/8)A_{4}
.

4
The CMS measurement of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
at 13 TeV

Recently, the CMS collaboration at the LHC reported
[
11
]
on a measurement of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
(0.23152
\pm
0.00031) extracted from the forward-backward asymmetry
A_{FB}
in Drell–Yan dilepton production in proton-proton collisions at a center of mass energy
\sqrt{s}=
13 TeV with an integrated luminosity of 138 fb
-1
. The statistical error in this measurement is
\pm
0.00010.
The experimental systematic error is small because of the precise lepton momentum calibration
[
7
]
and the use of the angular weighted
[
8
]
forward-backward asymmetry
A^{w}_{\rm FB}
. The experimental systematic errors in acceptance and efficiencies mostly cancel in the angular weighted
A^{w}_{\rm FB}
, which can be regarded as the detector-level equivalent of full-acceptance asymmetry.

The dominant source of error in the CMS measurements originates from uncertainties in the Parton Distribution Functions (PDFs) of the proton.
The three major PDF sets are from the CTEQ
[
12
,
13
]
(Coordinated Theoretical-Experimental Project on QCD) collaboration, NNPDF
[
14
,
15
]
(Neural Network Parton Distribution Function) collaboration and
MSHT
[
16
,
17
]
(Mass Scheme Hessian Tolerance) collaboration. The CMS value of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
was extracted using the QCD next-to-next to-leading-order (nnlo)
CT18Znnlo
PDF set
[
12
]
. The PDF uncertainties in each PDF set, as well as the differences between
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
values extracted using different PDF sets, are significantly reduced by using the
A_{4}
PDF re-weighting/profiling technique (first proposed in
[
9
]
) to constrain PDFs as described below.
In the CMS analysis the
CT18Znnlo

[
12
]
PDF was chosen as the nominal PDF because the extracted value of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
was in the middle of the extracted values with other PDF sets.

5
PDF re-weighting/profiling

The PDF re-weighting/profiling technique to constrain PDFs
[
9
]
primarily relies on including the rapidity and dilepton mass dependence of
A_{4}
to constrain PDFs in the analysis. This is because at the region of the
Z
pole,
A_{4}
is sensitive to the vector couplings, which depend on
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
, and also to PDFs. At higher and lower dilepton mass
A_{4}
is sensitive to PDFs, but much less sensitive to
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
. Consequently,
A_{4}
measurements in the high and low mass regions provides additional constraints on PDFs.

When PDF replicas are used in the analysis, the PDF constraints are implemented by the re-weighting of a large number of PDF replicas. PDF replicas with predictions of
A_{4}
which are in disagreements with the measurements in the high and low mass regions are assigned lower weights. The re-weighting implementation was first used to reduce PDF errors in the measurement of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
by the CDF experiment at the Tevatron
[
19
,
20
,
21
]
, and then by the CMS experiment at 8 TeV
[
22
]
.
When Hessian PDFs are used, the technique is implemented by profiling the eigenvectors of the Hessian PDFs. The profiling implementation was used in the preliminary measurement of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
by the ATLAS experiment at 8 TeV
[
23
]
, and in the recent CMS measurement of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
at 13 TeV
[
11
]
.
The two implementations are equivalent and Hessian PDFs can readily be used to generate replica PDFs.

6
A new analysis of the published CMS 13 TeV measurements of
A_{4}

In this section, we present a new analysis based on the published CMS measurements of
A_{4}
at 13 TeV. As a starting point, we reproduce the original CMS analysis, where the dilepton rapidity and mass dependence of
A_{4}
was used to profile PDFs and extract
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
.
Then, we extend this approach by including additional CMS measurements of
W
and
Z
boson production at 13 TeV in the profiling.
Additionally, we also increase the number of PDF sets which are investigated from 14 to 19.

6.1
Reproduction of the CMS 13 TeV analysis

As described in the CMS publication
[
11
]
the value of
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
is determined in a profiling analysis, by minimizing the
\chi^{2}
function

\chi^{2}(\beta_{\rm exp},\beta_{\rm th})=\frac{(\sigma_{i}^{\rm exp}+\sum_{j}\Gamma_{ij}^{\rm exp}\beta_{j,{\rm exp}}-\sigma_{i}^{\rm th}-\sum_{k}\Gamma_{ik}^{\rm th}\beta_{k,{\rm th}})^{2}}{\Delta_{i}^{2}}+\sum_{j}\beta^{2}_{j,{\rm exp}}+\sum_{k}\beta^{2}_{k,{\rm th}}.

(2)

The correlated experimental (theoretical) uncertainties are included in the nuisance vector
\beta_{\rm exp}
(
\beta_{\rm th}
)
and their impact on the measured distributions (theory predictions)

\Gamma^{\rm exp}
(

The index

The measurements and the uncorrelated experimental uncertainties are represented
by

and

whereas the theoretical predictions are denoted by
\sigma_{i}^{\rm th}

The information in the experimental covariance matrix of the double-differential

The theoretical prediction is obtained with the

2.0 event generator at multi-scale improved next-to-next-to-leading order (

The theoretical uncertainty includes contributions from the missing higher-order QCD and Electroweak (EW) corrections,
as evaluated with
POWHEG-Z-ew
and PDF uncertainties by using grids generated at NLO with

MadGraph5-aMC@nlo

[
27

and

[

\Gamma^{\rm th}

and the

parameter itself, which is left free in the fit. The impact of missing higher-order QCD corrections is estimated by repeating the fit for each of the six scale variations, and the maximum deviation from the central result is taken as the uncertainty.

6.2
Including
W
decay lepton asymmetry and
W/Z
cross section ratio in PDF profiling

The uncertainty in
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
is significantly reduced after profiling with
A_{4}
, but some differences between PDF sets remain. As shown in
[
9
]
, including the
W
boson decay lepton asymmetry measurements (
W_{\rm asym}
) in the profiling provides additional constraints on the
d/u
ratio, leading to a further reduction of the PDF uncertainty in
\sin^{2}\theta^{\ell}_{\rm eff}
. Moreover, some CT18 PDF sets lack sufficient constraints on the strange-quark distribution at high energy scales, resulting in systematically lower strange-quark densities compared to other PDFs and thereby predicting a larger effective weak mixing angle. Incorporating the
W/Z
cross section ratio can provide further constraints on the strange-quark distribution, helping to reduce these discrepancies.

Therefore, we perform an updated analysis of the published CMS 13 TeV
A_{4}
data (63 data points) and also include the recent
CMS
W_{asym}
measurement
[
18
