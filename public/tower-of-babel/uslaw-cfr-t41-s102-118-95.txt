41 C.F.R. § 102-118.95 — -118.95 Prompt payment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.95
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must advise the TSP using a statement of difference of any adjustments made, either electronically or in writing, within 7 days of receipt of the bill, as required by the Prompt Payment Act (31 U.S.C. 3901, et seq.).
