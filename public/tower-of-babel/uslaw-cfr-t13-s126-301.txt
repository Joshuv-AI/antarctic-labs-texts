13 C.F.R. § 126.301 — Is there any other way for a concern to obtain certification?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.301
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. SBA certification is the only way to qualify for HUBZone program status.
