43 U.S.C. § 43 — Copies to Senators, Representatives, and Delegates
Citation: us:usc:43:43
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section43
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 2 (United States Geological Survey). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

One copy of each map and atlas shall be sent to each Senator and each Representative and Delegate in Congress, if published within his term; and a second copy shall be placed at the disposal of each such Senator, Representative and Delegate
