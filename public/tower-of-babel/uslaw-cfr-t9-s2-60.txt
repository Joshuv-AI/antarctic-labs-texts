9 C.F.R. § 2.60 — Prohibition on the purchase, sale, use, or transportation of stolen animals.
Citation: us:cfr:9:2.60
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-2.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 2 (Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall buy, sell, exhibit, use for research, transport, or offer for transportation, any stolen animal.
