15 U.S.C. § 8008 — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:15:8008
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 106 (Pool And Spa Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter 1 is applicable to the United States and its territories, including American Samoa, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, Guam, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, and the United States Virgin Islands.
