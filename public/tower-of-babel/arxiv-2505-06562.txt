Abstract
. In this paper, we introduce and explore a new class of topological spaces termed as S ​ C ∗ \displaystyle SC^{*} -normal spaces, defined via S ​ C ∗ \displaystyle SC^{*} -open sets. The concept of S ​ C ∗ \displaystyle SC^{*} -normality is analyzed in relation to classical notions such as normal spaces and g \displaystyle g -normal spaces. We further define and examine generalized forms of S ​ C ∗ \displaystyle SC^{*} -closed functions, including S ​ C ∗ \displaystyle SC^{*} -generalized closed mappings, and investigate their fundamental properties. Several characterizations of S ​ C ∗ \displaystyle SC^{*} -normal spaces are established, and various preservation theorems under different types of functions are also presented.
