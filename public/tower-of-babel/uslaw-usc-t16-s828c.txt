16 U.S.C. § 828c — Applicability of this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:16:828c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section828c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter IV (State And Municipal Water Conservation Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as herein provided, the provisions of this subchapter shall not be construed as repealing or affecting any of the provisions of the Federal Power Act [16 U.S.C. 791a et seq.].
