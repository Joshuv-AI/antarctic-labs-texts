5 C.F.R. § 9301.16 — Requests for copies of records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9301.16
Creator: Special Inspector General For Afghanistan Reconstruction
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9301.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9301 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rules governing requests for copies of records are the same as those for the granting of access to the records (including verification of identity) outlined in § 9301.14. (See also § 9301.19 for rules regarding fees.)
