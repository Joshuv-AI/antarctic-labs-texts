17 C.F.R. § 201.53 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:17:201.53
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-201.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 201.55.
