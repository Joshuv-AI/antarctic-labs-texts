49 C.F.R. § 670.29 — Advisories.
Citation: us:cfr:49:670.29
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-670.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 670 (Public Transportation Safety Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any instance in which the Administrator determines there are hazards or risks to public transportation, the Administrator may issue an advisory which recommends corrective actions, inspections, conditions, limitations or other actions to avoid or mitigate any hazards or risks. The Administrator will issue notice to recipients of an advisory in the Federal Register.
