28 U.S.C. § 3703 — Injunctions
Citation: us:usc:28:3703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3703
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 178 (Professional And Amateur Sports Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A civil action to enjoin a violation of section 3702 may be commenced in an appropriate district court of the United States by the Attorney General of the United States, or by a professional sports organization or amateur sports organization whose competitive game is alleged to be the basis of such violation.
