31 C.F.R. § 360.57 — When a certifying officer may not certify.
Citation: us:cfr:31:360.57
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-360.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 360 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certifying officers may not certify the requests for payment or reissue of bonds, or appropriate Fiscal Service transaction forms if, in their own right or in a representative capacity, they:
(a) Have an interest in the bonds; or
(b) Will, by virtue of the requests being certified, acquire an interest in the bonds.
