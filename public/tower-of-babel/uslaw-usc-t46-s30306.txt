46 U.S.C. § 30306 — Foreign cause of action
Citation: us:usc:46:30306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30306
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 303 (Death On The High Seas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a cause of action exists under the law of a foreign country for death by wrongful act, neglect, or default on the high seas, a civil action in admiralty may be brought in a court of the United States based on the foreign cause of action, without abatement of the amount for which recovery is authorized.
