33 U.S.C. § 607a — Appropriations; separate project
Citation: us:usc:33:607a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section607a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter IV (Particular Work Or Improvements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after July 3, 1930, direct allotments from appropriations for maintenance and improvement of existing river and harbor works or other available appropriation may be made by the Secretary of the Army for the collection and removal of drift in New York Harbor and its tributary waters, and this work on and after July 3, 1930, shall be carried as a separate and distinct project.
