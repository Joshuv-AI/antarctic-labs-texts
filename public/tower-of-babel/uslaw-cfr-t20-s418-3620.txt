20 C.F.R. § 418.3620 — What is the effect of an initial determination?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3620
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3620
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An initial determination is binding unless you request an appeal within the time period stated in § 418.3630(a) or we revise it as provided in § 418.3678.
