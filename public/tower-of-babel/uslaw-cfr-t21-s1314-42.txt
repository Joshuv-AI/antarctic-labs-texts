21 C.F.R. § 1314.42 — Self-certification fee; time and method of fee payment.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1314.42
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1314.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1314 (Retail Sale Of Scheduled Listed Chemical Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A regulated seller must pay a fee for each self-certification. For each initial application to self-certify, and for the renewal of each existing self-certification, a regulated seller shall pay a fee of $21.
(b) The fee for self-certification shall be waived for any person holding a current, DEA registration in good standing as a pharmacy to dispense controlled substances.
(c) A regulated seller shall pay the fee at the time of self-certification.
(d) Payment shall be made by credit card.
(e) The self-certification fee is not refundable.
