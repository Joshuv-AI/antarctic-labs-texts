16 C.F.R. § 260.14 — Refillable claims.
Citation: us:cfr:16:260.14
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-260.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 260 (Guides For The Use Of Environmental Marketing Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is deceptive to misrepresent, directly or by implication, that a package is refillable. A marketer should not make an unqualified refillable claim unless the marketer provides the means for refilling the package. The marketer may either provide a system for the collection and refill of the package, or offer for sale a product that consumers can purchase to refill the original package.
