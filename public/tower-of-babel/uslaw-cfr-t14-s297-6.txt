14 C.F.R. § 297.6 — Foreign cooperative shippers association as agent.
Citation: us:cfr:14:297.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-297.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 297 (Foreign Air Freight Forwarders And Foreign Cooperative Shippers Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A foreign cooperative shippers association may act as agent of a shipper, or of a direct air carrier that has authorized such agency, if it expressly reserves the option to do so when the shipment is accepted. A cooperative shippers association shall not act as an agent of any direct air carrier with respect to shipments accepted in its capacity as an indirect air carrier.
