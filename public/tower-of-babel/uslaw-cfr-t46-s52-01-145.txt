46 C.F.R. § 52.01-145 — -145 Manufacturer's data report forms (modifies PG-112 and PG-113).
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.01-145
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.01-145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The manufacturer's data report forms required by PG-112 and PG-113 of Section I of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 52.01-1) must be made available to the marine inspector for review. The Authorized Inspector's National Board commission number must be included on the manufacturer's data report forms.
