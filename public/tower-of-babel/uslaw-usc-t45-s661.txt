45 U.S.C. § 661 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:45:661
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section661
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 15 (Emergency Rail Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter—
(1)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of Transportation.
(2)
“Board” means the Surface Transportation Board.
(3)
“Railroad” means any rail carrier subject to part A of subtitle IV of title 49.
(4)
“Certificate” means certificates issued by trustees of a railroad pursuant to subsection 77(c)(3) of the Bankruptcy Act, as amended.
