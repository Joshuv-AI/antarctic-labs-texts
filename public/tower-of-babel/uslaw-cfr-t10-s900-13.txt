10 C.F.R. § 900.13 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:900.13
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-900.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 900 (Coordination Of Federal Authorizations For Electric Transmission Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. Should a court hold any provision(s) to be stayed or invalid, such action shall not affect any other provision of this part and the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.
