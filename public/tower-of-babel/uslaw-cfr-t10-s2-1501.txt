10 C.F.R. § 2.1501 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.1501
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.1501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Demonstrative information means physical things, not constituting documentary information.
Documentary information means information, ordinarily contained in documents or electronic files, but may also include photographs and digital audio files.
