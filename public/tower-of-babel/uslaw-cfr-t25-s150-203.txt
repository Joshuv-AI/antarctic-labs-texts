25 C.F.R. § 150.203 — Who may submit a title document for recording?
Citation: us:cfr:25:150.203
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-150.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 150 (Record Of Title To Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only an Agency, Region, or OHA may submit title documents to the LTRO for recording. All other government offices and individuals must submit title documents to the Agency, Region, or OHA, as appropriate, for that Agency, Region, or OHA to submit to the LTRO.
