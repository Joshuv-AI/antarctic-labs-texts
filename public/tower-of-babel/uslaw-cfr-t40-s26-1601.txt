40 C.F.R. § 26.1601 — To what does this subpart apply?
Citation: us:cfr:40:26.1601
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-26.1601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 26 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to both of the following:
(a) Reviews by EPA and by the Human Studies Review Board of proposals to conduct new research subject to § 26.1125.
(b) Reviews by EPA on or after April 15, 2013 and, to the extent required by § 26.1604, by the Human Studies Review Board of reports of completed research subject to § 26.1701.
