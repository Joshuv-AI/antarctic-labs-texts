7 C.F.R. § 623.20 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:623.20
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-623.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 623 (Emergency Wetlands Reserve Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant in the EWRP may obtain a review of any administrative determination concerning land eligibility, development of a WRPO, or any adverse determination under this part in accordance with the administrative appeal regulations provided in part 614 of this title.
