16 U.S.C. § 410aaa–43 — Transfer of lands
Citation: us:usc:16:410aaa–43
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410aaa–43
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–Y (California Desert Lands Parks, Preserve, And Off-Highway Vehicle Recreation Areas); Part C (Mojave National Preserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On October 31, 1994, the Secretary shall transfer the lands under the jurisdiction of the Bureau of Land Management depicted on the maps described in section 410aaa–42 of this title, without consideration, to the administrative jurisdiction of the Director of the National Park Service. The boundaries of the public lands shall be adjusted accordingly.
