46 U.S.C. § 505 — Penalty for violating regulation or order
Citation: us:usc:46:505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section505
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 5 (Other General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person convicted of knowingly and willfully violating a regulation or order of the Federal Maritime Commission or the Secretary of Transportation under subtitle IV or V of this title, for which no penalty is expressly provided, shall be fined not more than $500. Each day of a continuing violation is a separate offense.
