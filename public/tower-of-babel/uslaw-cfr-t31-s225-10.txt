31 C.F.R. § 225.10 — Other agency practices and authorities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:225.10
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-225.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 225 (Acceptance Of Bonds Secured By Government Obligations In Lieu Of Bonds With Sureties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Agency practices. Nothing in this part shall be construed as modifying the existing practices or duties of agencies in handling bonds, except to the extent made necessary under the terms of this part by reason of the acceptance of bonds secured by Government obligations.
(b) Agency authorities. Nothing contained in this part shall affect the authority of agencies to receive Government obligations for security in cases authorized by other provisions of law.
