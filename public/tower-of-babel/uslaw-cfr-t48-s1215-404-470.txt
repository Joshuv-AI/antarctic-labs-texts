48 C.F.R. § 1215.404-470 — 1215.404-470 Payment of profit or fee.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1215.404-470
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1215.404-470
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1215 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall not pay profit or fee on undefinitized contracts or undefinitized contract modifications. Any profit or fee earned shall be paid after the contract or modification is definitized.
