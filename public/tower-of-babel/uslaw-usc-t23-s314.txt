23 U.S.C. § 314 — Relief of employees in hazardous work
Citation: us:usc:23:314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title23-section314
Context: United States Code, Title 23 (Highways); Chapter 3 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized in an emergency to use appropriations to the Department of Transportation for carrying out the provisions of this title for medical supplies, services, and other assistance necessary for the immediate relief of employees of the Federal Highway Administration engaged in hazardous work.
