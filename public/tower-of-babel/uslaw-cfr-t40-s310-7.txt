40 C.F.R. § 310.7 — Can more than one local agency or government be reimbursed for response to the same incident?
Citation: us:cfr:40:310.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-310.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 310 (Reimbursement To Local Governments For Emergency Response To Hazardous Substance Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. EPA will accept only one reimbursement request for a single response. A single response includes all of the temporary emergency measures that ALL local governments or agencies conduct in response to a single hazardous substance release. If more than one local government or agency responds, you must decide among yourselves who will request reimbursement on behalf of all.
