5 C.F.R. § 839.401 — What can I elect if I was put in CSRS or CSRS Offset by mistake?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.401
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you were placed in CSRS or CSRS Offset due to a qualifying retirement coverage error and you should have been in FERS, you may elect CSRS Offset or FERS. If you were placed in CSRS or CSRS Offset due to a qualifying retirement coverage error and you should have been in Social Security-Only, you may elect CSRS Offset or Social Security-Only.
