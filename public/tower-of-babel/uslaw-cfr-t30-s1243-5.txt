30 C.F.R. § 1243.5 — May another person post a bond or other surety instrument or demonstrate financial solvency on my behalf?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1243.5
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1243.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1243 (Suspensions Pending Appeal And Bonding—Office Of Natural Resources Revenue). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any other person, including a designee, payor, or affiliate, may post a bond or other surety instrument or demonstrate financial solvency under this part on behalf of an appellant required to post a bond or other surety instrument under § 1243.4(a)(1).
