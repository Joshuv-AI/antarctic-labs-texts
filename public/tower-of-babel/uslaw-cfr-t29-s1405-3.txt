29 C.F.R. § 1405.3 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1405.3
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1405.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1405 (Part-Time Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part-time career employment means regularly scheduled work of from 16 to 32 hours per week performed by employees in competitive or excepted appointments in tenure groups I or II.
