21 C.F.R. § 1301.01 — Scope of this part 1301.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1301.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1301.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1301 (Registration Of Manufacturers, Distributors, And Dispensers Of Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures governing the registration of manufacturers, distributors, dispensers, importers, and exporters of controlled substances pursuant to sections 301-304 and 1007-1008 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 821-824 and 957-958) are set forth generally by those sections and specifically by the sections of this part.
