5 C.F.R. § 875.105 — May OPM correct errors?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM may order correction of administrative errors after reviewing evidence and finding that it would be against equity and good conscience not to do so.
