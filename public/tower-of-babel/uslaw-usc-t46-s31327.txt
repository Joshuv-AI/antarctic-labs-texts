46 U.S.C. § 31327 — Forfeiture of mortgagee interest
Citation: us:usc:46:31327
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31327
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 313 (Commercial Instruments And Maritime Liens); Subchapter II (Commercial Instruments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The interest of a mortgagee in a documented vessel or a vessel covered by a preferred mortgage under section 31322(d) of this title may be terminated by a forfeiture of the vessel for a violation of a law of the United States only if the mortgagee authorized, consented, or conspired to do the act, failure, or omission that is the basis of the violation.
