40 C.F.R. § 19.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:19.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-19.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 19 (Adjustment Of Civil Monetary Penalties For Inflation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to each statutory provision under the laws administered by the Environmental Protection Agency concerning the civil monetary penalties which may be assessed in either civil judicial or administrative proceedings.
