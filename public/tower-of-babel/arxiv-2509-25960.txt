Abstract
We study correlation numbers in Virasoro minimal string [ 1 ] . Using analytic properties of correlation functions in spacelike and timelike Liouville theories, we verify exact expressions for correlation numbers for the four punctured sphere and the once punctured torus conjectured in [ 1 ] .

There are several ways to define Liouville field theory (LFT)

2.1

Consider the correlation function of

. This action does not produce singularities in

or

with

2.3.4

Here we consider a transformation of

\displaystyle P_{1}\to\tilde{P}_{1}=\frac{P_{1}+P_{2}+P_{3}+P_{4}}{2}\mathchar 59\relax

(2.59)

\displaystyle\sigma=(24)\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax}\,(243):

2.4.1
Analytic continuation of OPE and degenerate fields

Operator product expansion (

This prescription fixes the problem, because similarly to (

Boundary terms.

When using the above formulas in the integral over the moduli space the terms that are exact forms can be reduced to boundary terms using Stokes’ theorem. The boundary consists of small circles around the marked points and infinity. As these circles shrink, the only terms that produce finite contributions are those that behave like
1/z
or
1/\bar{z}
. Terms that do not behave like that either vanish, or require analytic continuation from the domain where they vanish. This analytic continuation was described in
3.2
.

In the neighborhood of the marked point
z_{k}
the integrand contains the factor
|z-z_{k}|^{2\hat{P}^{2}}
from the timelike LFT correlation function. The spectrum in timelike LFT is continuous and never contains discreet terms. Because of that, the integrand never behaves like
1/z
or
1/\bar{z}
and thus boundary terms from the marked points do not contribute.

It would be possible to obtain the desired behavior in the integrand that corresponds to a circle around infinity, if there was a term that contained both
\partial\bar{\partial}
and logarithmic fields. Then, after differentiating the logarithm we would get
1/z
or
1/\bar{z}
. However, in (

\partial\bar{\partial}
we have the field

with the conformal dimension
\Delta_{1}+\hat{\Delta}_{1}=1-mn
. Since the total conformal dimension does not depend on
P_{1}
, we do not obtain logarithmic terms here.

Therefore, the boundary terms do not contribute to the integral over the moduli space.

5
Conclusion

In this paper we have studied correlation numbers in Virasoro minimal string
[
1
]
. We were able to analytically confirm the formula for the volumes
V_{0\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax}4}
and
V_{1\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax}1}
proposed in
[
1
]
. Unfortunately, our proof is not complete since it relies on the assumption of polynomial boundedness of the volumes – an assumption that we cannot deduce from first principles.

When trying to generalize our method to a more general case of
V_{g\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax}n}
, certain problems arise. First, it is necessary to prove that the volumes defined by the formula (
3.5
) have no singularities. The absence of singularities at
P_{k}\rightarrow P_{m\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax}n}
can be proved using HEM, similarly to what was done in subsection
3.4
. On the other hand, the poles corresponding to the screening conditions in spacelike Liouville CFT (
2.53
) have a completely different nature and in the absence of a triality equation this type of poles cannot be reduced to the previous one. It would be interesting to come up with an argument why the residues in such poles correspond to exact forms. We will consider this issue in a future publication.
