49 C.F.R. § 10.41 — Requests for correction of records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:10.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-10.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 10 (Maintenance Of And Access To Records Pertaining To Individuals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who desires to have a record pertaining to that person corrected shall submit a written request detailing the correction and the reasons the record should be so corrected. Requests for correction of records shall be submitted to the System Manager.
