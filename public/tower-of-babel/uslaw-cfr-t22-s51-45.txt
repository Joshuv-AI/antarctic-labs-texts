22 C.F.R. § 51.45 — Department discretion to require evidence of U.S. citizenship or non-citizen nationality.
Citation: us:cfr:22:51.45
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-51.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 51 (Passports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may require an applicant to provide any evidence that it deems necessary to establish that he or she is a U.S. citizen or non-citizen national, including evidence in addition to the evidence specified in 22 CFR 51.42 through 51.44.
