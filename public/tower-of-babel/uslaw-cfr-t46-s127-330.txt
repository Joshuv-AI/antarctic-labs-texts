46 C.F.R. § 127.330 — Guards in dangerous places.
Citation: us:cfr:46:127.330
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-127.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 127 (Construction And Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Suitable hand covers, guards, or rails must be installed on each exposed and dangerous place, such as gears of rotating machinery, and hot surfaces.
