12 U.S.C. § 5013 — Variation by agreement
Citation: us:usc:12:5013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5013
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 50 (Check Truncation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Section 5007
Any provision of section 5007 of this title may be varied by agreement of the banks involved.
(b) No other provisions may be varied
Except as provided in subsection (a), no provision of this chapter may be varied by agreement of any person or persons.
