54 U.S.C. § 300310 — Local government
Citation: us:usc:54:300310
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300310
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “local government” means a city, county, township, municipality, or borough, or any other general purpose political subdivision of any State.
