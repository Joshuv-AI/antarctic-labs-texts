9 C.F.R. § 202.120 — Rule 20: Official notice.
Citation: us:cfr:9:202.120
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-202.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 202 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Official notice shall be taken of such matters as are judicially noticed by the courts of the United States and of any other matter of technical or scientific fact of established character: Provided, That the parties shall be given notice of matters so noticed, and shall be given adequate opportunity to show that such facts are erroneously noticed.
