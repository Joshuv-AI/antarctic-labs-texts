48 C.F.R. § 3401.000 — 3401.000 Scope of part.

Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3401 (Ed Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes a system of Department of Education (Department) acquisition regulations, referred to as the Education Acquisition Regulation (EDAR), for the codification and publication of policies and procedures of the Department that implement and supplement the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR).
