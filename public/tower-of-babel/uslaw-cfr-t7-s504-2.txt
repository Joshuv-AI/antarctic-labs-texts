7 C.F.R. § 504.2 — Fees for deposit and requisition of microbial cultures.
Citation: us:cfr:7:504.2
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-504.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 504 (User Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Depositors of microbial cultures must pay a one-time $670 user fee for each culture deposited on or after December 1, 2015.
(b) For cultures deposited on or after December 1, 2015, requestors must pay a $40 user fee for each culture distributed.
