40 C.F.R. § 408.160 — Applicability; description of the Alaskan hand-butchered salmon processing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:408.160
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-408.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 408 (Canned And Preserved Seafood Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the hand-butchering of salmon in Alaska.
