41 C.F.R. § 301-10.420 — -10.420 Use of taxi, TNC, innovative mobility technology company, shuttle service, or other courtesy transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.420
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When authorized and approved by the agency, employee transportation expenses in the performance of official travel are reimbursable for the usual fare plus a tip which the agency determines to be reasonable for use of a taxi, TNC, innovative mobility technology company, shuttle service, or other courtesy transportation (if charges result). When selecting a TNC, first consideration should be given to the General Services Administration's Ridehail/Rideshare program.
