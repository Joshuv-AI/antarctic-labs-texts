8 C.F.R. § 337.4 — When requests for change of name granted.
Citation: us:cfr:8:337.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-337.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 337 (Oath Of Allegiance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the court has granted the petitioner's change of name request, the petitioner shall subscribe his or her new name to the written oath of allegiance.
