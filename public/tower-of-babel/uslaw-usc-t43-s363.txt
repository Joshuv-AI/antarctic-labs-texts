43 U.S.C. § 363 — Rules and regulations by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:43:363
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section363
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 11 (Discovery, Development, And Marking Of Water Holes, Etc., By Government). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to perform any and all acts and make such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the purpose of carrying the provisions of this chapter into full force and effect.
