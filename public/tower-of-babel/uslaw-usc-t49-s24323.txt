49 U.S.C. § 24323 — Prohibition on smoking on Amtrak trains
Citation: us:usc:49:24323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section24323
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Passenger Transportation); Chapter 243 (Amtrak). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition.—
Beginning on the date of enactment of this section, Amtrak shall prohibit smoking, including the use of electronic cigarettes, onboard all Amtrak trains.
(b) Electronic Cigarette Defined.—
In this section, the term “electronic cigarette” means a device that delivers nicotine or other substances to a user of the device in the form of a vapor that is inhaled to simulate the experience of smoking.
