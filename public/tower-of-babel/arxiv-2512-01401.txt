{\color[rgb]{0,0.5,0}\mathcal{U}}:=\left\{\{e,f\}\in\binom{E(G)}{2}:\text{$e$ and $f$ do not share an endpoint and are non-adjacent}\right\}.

Then

|\mathcal{U}|\leqslant\frac{ct(t-1)^{3}}{8}.

(5)

For any two distinct edges
e,f\in E(G)
that do not share an endpoint, if
e,f\in M
, then
e,f\in X
, and thus:

\displaystyle\mathbb{P}(e,f\in M\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})

\displaystyle=\mathbb{P}(e,f\in M\text{ and }e,f\in X\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell}).

By
Sections

2
and
11.2
,

\displaystyle\mathbb{P}(e,f\in M\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})

\displaystyle=\frac{\mathbb{P}(e,f\in M\text{ and }e,f\in X\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})}{\mathbb{P}(e,f\in X\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})}\cdot\mathbb{P}(e,f\in X\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})

\displaystyle=\mathbb{P}(e,f\in M\mid e,f\in X\text{ and }X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})\cdot\mathbb{P}(e,f\in X\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})

\displaystyle\leqslant p^{2}\cdot\frac{1}{q(ct-1)(ct-3)}.

(6)

From
Sections

2
and
5
, it follows that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}(\text{number of non-adjacent pairs of edges in $M$}\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})

\displaystyle=\sum_{\{e,f\}\,\in\,\mathcal{U}}\mathbb{P}(e,f\in M\mid X\in\mathcal{A}_{\ell})

\displaystyle\leqslant\frac{p^{2}ct(t-1)^{3}}{8q(ct-1)(ct-3)}.

This implies that there is a matching
M
of
G
with
|M|=t
such that the number of non-adjacent pairs of edges is at most
\frac{p^{2}ct(t-1)^{3}}{8q(ct-1)(ct-3)}
, as desired.
∎

Observation 12

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(\frac{1}{\frac{1}{2}\left(c^{\prime}-1\right)-\ell}\right)^{2}c^{\prime}t\left(t-1\right)^{3}}{8\left(1-\frac{c^{\prime}}{\ell^{2}t}\right)\left(c^{\prime}t-1\right)\left(c^{\prime}t-3\right)}
