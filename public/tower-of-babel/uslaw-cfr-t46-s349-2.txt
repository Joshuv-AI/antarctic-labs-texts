46 C.F.R. § 349.2 — Application for certification.
Citation: us:cfr:46:349.2
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-349.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 349 (Reemployment Rights Of Certain Merchant Seamen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 46 App. U.S.C. 1132, an individual may submit an application to MARAD not later than 45 days after the date the individual completes the period of employment described in § 349.3 of this part.
