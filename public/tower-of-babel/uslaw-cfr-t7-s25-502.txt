7 C.F.R. § 25.502 — Nominations by State-chartered economic development corporations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:25.502
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-25.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 25 (Rural Empowerment Zones And Enterprise Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any rural area nominated by an economic development corporation chartered by a State and qualified to do business in the State in which it is located shall be treated as nominated by a State and local government.
