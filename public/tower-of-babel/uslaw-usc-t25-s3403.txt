25 U.S.C. § 3403 — Integration of services authorized
Citation: us:usc:25:3403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3403
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 36 (Indian Employment, Training And Related Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, after approving a plan submitted by an Indian tribe in accordance with section 3407 of this title, authorize the Indian tribe to, in accordance with the plan—
(1)
integrate the programs and Federal funds received by the Indian tribe in accordance with waiver authority granted under section 3406(d) of this title; and
(2)
coordinate the employment, training, and related services provided with those funds in a consolidated and comprehensive tribal plan.
