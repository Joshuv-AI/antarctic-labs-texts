38 C.F.R. § 26.80 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:38:26.80
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-26.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 26 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart outlines the relationship between VA and Federal, Tribal, State, and local environmental agencies; identifies the role of VA in actions initiated by other Federal agencies and the role other Federal agencies may play in VA actions; and describes the role of applicants in VA's NEPA process.
