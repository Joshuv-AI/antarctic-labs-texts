28 C.F.R. § 202.204 — Biometric identifiers.
Citation: us:cfr:28:202.204
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-202.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 202 (Access To U.S. Sensitive Personal Data And Government-Related Data By Countries Of Concern Or Covered Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term biometric identifiers means measurable physical characteristics or behaviors used to recognize or verify the identity of an individual, including facial images, voice prints and patterns, retina and iris scans, palm prints and fingerprints, gait, and keyboard usage patterns that are enrolled in a biometric system and the templates created by the system.
