16 U.S.C. § 558d — Incidental expenses
Citation: us:usc:16:558d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section558d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretaries are authorized to provide for incidental expenses, such as transportation, uniforms, lodging, training, equipment, and subsistence.
