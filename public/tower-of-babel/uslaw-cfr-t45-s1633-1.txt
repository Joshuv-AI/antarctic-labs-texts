45 C.F.R. § 1633.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1633.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1633.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1633 (Restriction On Representation In Certain Eviction Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is designed to ensure that in certain public housing eviction proceedings recipients refrain from defending persons charged with or convicted of illegal drug activities.
