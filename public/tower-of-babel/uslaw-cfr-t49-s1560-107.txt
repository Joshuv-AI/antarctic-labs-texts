49 C.F.R. § 1560.107 — Use of watch list matching results by covered aircraft operators.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1560.107
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1560.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1560 (Secure Flight Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered aircraft operator must not use any watch list matching results provided by TSA for purposes other than those provided in § 1560.105 and other security purposes.
