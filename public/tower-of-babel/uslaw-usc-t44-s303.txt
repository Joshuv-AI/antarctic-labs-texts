44 U.S.C. § 303 — Director of the Government Publishing Office and Deputy Director of the Government Publishing Office: pay
Citation: us:usc:44:303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section303
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 3 (Government Publishing Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual rate of pay for the Director of the Government Publishing Office shall be a rate which is equal to the rate for level II of the Executive Schedule under subchapter II of chapter 53 of title 5. The annual rate of pay for the Deputy Director of the Government Publishing Office shall be a rate which is equal to the rate for level III of such Executive Schedule.
