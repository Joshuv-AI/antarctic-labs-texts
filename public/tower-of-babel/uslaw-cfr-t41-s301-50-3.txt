41 C.F.R. § 301-50.3 — -50.3 Consequences of not using ETS, TMS, or TMC.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-50.3
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-50.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-50 (50—Arranging For Travel Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee is responsible for any additional costs resulting from the failure to use the ETS or their agency's TMS or TMC if they do not have an exception to use. In addition, the agency may take appropriate disciplinary actions.
