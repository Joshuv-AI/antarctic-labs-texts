42 U.S.C. § 6250f — Limit on amount of petroleum distillate
Citation: us:usc:42:6250f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6250f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter I (Domestic Supply Availability); Part D (Northeast Home Heating Oil Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 6250 of this title, for fiscal year 2012 and hereafter, the [Northeast Home Heating Oil] Reserve shall contain no more than 1 million barrels of petroleum distillate.
