45 C.F.R. § 1152.13 — May the Chairman waive any provision of these regulations?

Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1152 (Intergovernmental Review Of National Endowment For The Arts Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
