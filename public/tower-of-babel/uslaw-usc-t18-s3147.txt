18 U.S.C. § 3147 — Penalty for an offense committed while on release
Citation: us:usc:18:3147
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3147
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 207 (Release And Detention Pending Judicial Proceedings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person convicted of an offense committed while released under this chapter shall be sentenced, in addition to the sentence prescribed for the offense, to—
(1)
a term of imprisonment of not more than ten years if the offense is a felony; or
(2)
a term of imprisonment of not more than one year if the offense is a misdemeanor.
A term of imprisonment imposed under this section shall be consecutive to any other sentence of imprisonment.
