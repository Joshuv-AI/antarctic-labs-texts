24 C.F.R. § 203.681 — Authority of HUD Field Office Managers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:203.681
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-203.681
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 203 (Single Family Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Field Office Managers shall act for the Secretary in all matters relating to assignment and occupied conveyance determinations. The decision of the Field Office Manager under § 203.677 will be final and not be subject to further administrative review.
