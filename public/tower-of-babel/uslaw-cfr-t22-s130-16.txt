22 C.F.R. § 130.16 — Other reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:22:130.16
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-130.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 130 (Political Contributions, Fees And Commissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The submission of reports under this part does not relieve any person of any requirements to furnish information to any federal, state, or municipal agency, department or other instrumentality as required by law, regulation or contract.
