48 U.S.C. § 1756 — Lack of effect on covenant
Citation: us:usc:48:1756
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1756
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 16 (Delegates To Congress); Subchapter III (Northern Mariana Islands Delegate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No provision of this subchapter shall be construed to alter, amend, or abrogate any provision of the covenant referred to in section 1751 of this title except section 901 of the covenant.
