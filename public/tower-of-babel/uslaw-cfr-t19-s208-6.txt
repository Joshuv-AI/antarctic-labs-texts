19 C.F.R. § 208.6 — Time for determinations, reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:19:208.6
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-208.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 208 (Investigations Of United States-Mexico Cross-Border Long-Haul Trucking Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Determinations. (1) The Commission will make its determination with respect to the petition, request, or resolution no later than 120 days after the date on which an investigation is initiated under section 322(a) of the Act, except that:
(2) If the Commission determines, before the 100th day after an investigation is initiated under section 322(a) of the Act, that the investigation is extraordinarily complicated, the Commission will make its determination within 150 days after the date on which an investigation is initiated.
(b) Reporting. The Commission will submit its report to the President not later than the date that is 60 days after the date on which the determination is made under section 322(a) of the Act.
