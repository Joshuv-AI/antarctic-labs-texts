46 C.F.R. § 120.430 — Portable lights.
Citation: us:cfr:46:120.430
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-120.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 120 (Electrical Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must be equipped with at least two operable portable battery lights. One of these lights must be located at the operating station and the other at the access to the propulsion machinery space.
