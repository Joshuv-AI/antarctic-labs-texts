The corresponding author disagreed with PLOS’ findings of vertical discontinuities in Figs 5 and 6A. The underlying data provided for Fig 6A (S1 File) do appear to support the published results, but the resolution is too low to fully resolve the concerns. The corresponding author’s response did not resolve the issues raised with Fig 6B. The original data underlying the Fig 5 and 6B panels were not provided for editorial review. In the absence of these data, the concerns with Figs 5 and 6 cannot be fully resolved.

In light of the concerns raised the PLOS ONE Editors issue this Expression of Concern to notify readers of the above concerns and to relay the available data provided by the corresponding author.
