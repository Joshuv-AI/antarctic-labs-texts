25 C.F.R. § 179.4 — When does a life estate terminate?
Citation: us:cfr:25:179.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-179.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 179 (Life Estates And Future Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A life estate terminates upon relinquishment or upon the death of the measuring life.
