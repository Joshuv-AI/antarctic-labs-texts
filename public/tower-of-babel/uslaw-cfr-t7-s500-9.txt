7 C.F.R. § 500.9 — Photographs for news or advertising.
Citation: us:cfr:7:500.9
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-500.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 500 (National Arboretum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Photographs for news purposes may be taken at the USNA without prior permission. Photographs for advertising and other commercial purposes may be taken, but only with the prior approval of the Director, USNA and fees may be charged pursuant to § 500.23.
