ETH-monotonicity and the black hole singularity
Nilakash Sorokhaibam

Abstract
ETH-monotonicity is a recently discovered quantum advantage in quantum chaotic many-body systems which reinforces the Kelvin statement of the second law of thermodynamics over and above the universal entropic contribution. We show that the boundary conformal field theory in AdS/CFT correspondence possesses ETH-monotonicity. This leads to our proposal that the black hole singularity is a microstate for which ETH-monotonicity dominates the entropic factor.

Introduction and Summary

It would not be an overstatement to state that the black hole singularity (and, in general, other spacetime singularities) has been the greatest mystery in physics. This singularity is found abundantly in physics. So, we expect that its physics underpinning should be robust and universal. In this paper, we provide a promising lead towards resolving this mystery.

In a seemingly unrelated front, quantum chaos and the related topic of thermalization in closed quantum systems have been subjects of great interest for the last few decades

The singularity at the centre of a white hole is again a microstate with ETH-monotonicity dominance but at negative inverse temperature. A small perturbation will lead to the lost of a larger amount of energy of the system, more than what one expects from a canonical density matrix.

3
Conclusions

In this work, we show that boundary CFT of BTZ black hole satisfies ETH-monotonicity, assuming ETH ansatz. This result leads to our proposal that the black hole singularity is a microstate for which ETH-monotonicity dominates the entropic factor. So when perturbed, the singularity will absorb much more energy than what one expects from entropic consideration. The equivalence of ensembles is strongly violated by the microstates of the singularity.
