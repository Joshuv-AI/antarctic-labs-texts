10 C.F.R. § 218.12 — Pricing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:218.12
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-218.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 218 (Standby Mandatory International Oil Allocation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The price for oil subject to a supply order issued pursuant to this subpart shall be based on the price conditions prevailing for comparable commercial transactions at the time the supply order is served.
