44 C.F.R. § 295.2 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:44:295.2
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-295.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 295 (Cerro Grande Fire Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is our policy to provide for the expeditious resolution of meritorious claims through a process that is administered with sensitivity to the burdens placed upon Claimants by the Cerro Grande Fire.
