46 C.F.R. § 169.821 — Patrol person.
Citation: us:cfr:46:169.821
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-169.821
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 169 (Sailing School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The master shall designate a member of the ship's company to be a roving patrol person, whenever the vessel is operational.
(b) The roving patrol person shall frequently visit all areas to ensure that safe conditions are being maintained.
