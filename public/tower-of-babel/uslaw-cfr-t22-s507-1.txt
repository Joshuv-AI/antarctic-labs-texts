22 C.F.R. § 507.1 — General policies.
Citation: us:cfr:22:507.1
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-507.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 507 (Rules For Implementing Open Meetings Under The Sunshine Act For The Broadcasting Board Of Governors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Broadcasting Board of Governors will provide the public with the fullest practical information regarding its decision making process while protecting the rights of individuals and its abilities to carry out its responsibilities.
