27 C.F.R. § 28.221 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.221
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Beer brewed or produced in the United States and on which the internal revenue tax has been paid may, subject to this part, be:
(a) Exported;
(b) Delivered for use as supplies on the vessels and aircraft described in § 28.21; or
(c) Transferred to and deposited in a foreign-trade zone for exportation or for storage pending exportation.
