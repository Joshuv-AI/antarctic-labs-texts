22 C.F.R. § 72.30 — Provisions in a will or advanced directive regarding disposition of remains.
Citation: us:cfr:22:72.30
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-72.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 72 (Deaths And Estates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

United States state law regarding advance directives, deaths and estates include provisions regarding a person's right to direct disposition of remains. Host country law may or may not accept such directions, particularly if the surviving spouse/next-of-kin disagree with the wishes of the testator/affiant.
