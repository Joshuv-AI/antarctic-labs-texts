19 U.S.C. § 2494 — Progress reports
Citation: us:usc:19:2494
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2494
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter VII (Tariff Treatment Of Products Of, And Other Sanctions Against, Uncooperative Major Drug Producing Or Drug-Transit Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall include as a part of the annual report required under section 2291h of title 22 an evaluation of progress that each major drug producing country and each major drug-transit country has made during the reporting period in achieving the objectives set forth in section 2492(b) of this title.
