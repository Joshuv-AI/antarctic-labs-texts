7 C.F.R. § 3403.9 — Submission of proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3403.9
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3403.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3403 (Small Business Innovation Research Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SBIR program solicitation for Phase I proposals and the correspondence requesting Phase II proposals will provide the deadline date for submitting proposals, and instructions for submitting the proposal to NIFA for funding consideration.
