48 C.F.R. § 1631.205-81 — 1631.205-81 Inferred reasonableness.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1631.205-81
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1631.205-81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1631 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the carrier follows the notification and consent requirements of paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) of 1652.244-70, and subsequently obtains the Contracting officer's consent or ratification, then the reasonableness of the subcontract's costs shall be inferred.
