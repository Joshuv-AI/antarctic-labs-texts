1 C.F.R. § 15.4 — Reproduction and certification of copies of acts and documents.
Citation: us:cfr:1:15.4
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-15.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 15 (Services To Federal Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Federal Register shall furnish to requesting agencies, at cost, reproductions or certified copies of original acts and documents filed with that Office that are needed for official use unless funds are appropriated for that purpose.
