46 C.F.R. § 177.960 — Guards for exposed hazards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:177.960
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-177.960
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 177 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exposed hazard, such as gears or rotating machinery, must be properly protected by a cover, guard, or rail.
