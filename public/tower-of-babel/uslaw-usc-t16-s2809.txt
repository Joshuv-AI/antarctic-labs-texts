16 U.S.C. § 2809 — Authorizations for appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:2809
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2809
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 48 (National Aquaculture Policy, Planning, And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of carrying out the provisions of this chapter, there are authorized to be appropriated—
(1)
to the Department of Agriculture, $1,000,000 for each of fiscal years 1991 through 2023;
(2)
to the Department of Commerce, $1,000,000 for each of fiscal years 1991 through 2023; and
(3)
to the Department of 1 Interior, $1,000,000 for each of fiscal years 1991 through 2023.
Funds authorized by this section shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, funds authorized by any other Act.
