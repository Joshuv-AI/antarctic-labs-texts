16 U.S.C. § 460s–14 — Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore boundary adjustment
Citation: us:usc:16:460s–14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460s–14
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXVII (Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The boundary of Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore is hereby modified as depicted on the map entitled “Area Proposed for Addition to Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore”, numbered 625–80,043A, and dated July 1992.
