16 C.F.R. § 1306.1 — Scope and application.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1306.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1306.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1306 (Ban Of Hazardous Lawn Darts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In this part 1306, the Commission declares lawn darts, described in § 1306.3, to be banned hazardous products.
(b) Lawn darts and similar products that are articles intended for use by children are not covered by this ban, but are banned under the Federal Hazardous Substances Act at 16 CFR 1500.18(a)(4).
