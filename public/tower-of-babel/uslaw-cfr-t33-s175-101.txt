33 C.F.R. § 175.101 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:175.101
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-175.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 175 (Equipment Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to boats on the coastal waters of the United States and on the high seas beyond the territorial seas for boats owned in the United States.
