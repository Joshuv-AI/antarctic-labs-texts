36 C.F.R. § 1150.91 — Record for decision.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1150.91
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1150.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1150 (Practice And Procedures For Compliance Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony, exhibits and all papers, documents and requests filed in the proceeding, including briefs and proposed findings and conclusions, shall constitute the record for decision.
