14 C.F.R. § 93.133 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:93.133
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-93.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 93 (Special Air Traffic Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 93.130, the provisions of §§ 93.123 and 93.125 do not apply to—
(a) The Newark Airport, Newark, NJ;
(b) The Kennedy International Airport, New York, NY, except during the hours from 3 p.m. through 7:59 p.m., local time; and
(c) O'Hare International Airport from 9:15 p.m. to 6:44 a.m., local time.
