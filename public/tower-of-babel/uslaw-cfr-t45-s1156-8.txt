45 C.F.R. § 1156.8 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1156.8
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1156.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1156 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The recipient of Federal financial assistance bears the burden of proving that an age distinction or other action falls within the exceptions outlined in § 1156.7.
