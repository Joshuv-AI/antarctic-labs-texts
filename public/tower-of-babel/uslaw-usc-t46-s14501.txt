46 U.S.C. § 14501 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:14501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section14501
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 145 (Regulatory Measurement); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies to the following:
(1)
A vessel not measured under chapter 143 of this title if the application of an international agreement or other law of the United States to the vessel depends on the vessel’s tonnage.
(2)
A vessel measured under chapter 143 of this title if the owner requests that the vessel also be measured under this chapter as provided in section 14305 of this title.
