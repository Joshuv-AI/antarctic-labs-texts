21 C.F.R. § 133.145 — Granular cheese for manufacturing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:133.145
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-133.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 133 (Cheeses And Related Cheese Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Granular cheese for manufacturing conforms to the definition and standard of identity prescribed for granular cheese by § 133.144, except that the dairy ingredients are not pasteurized and curing is not required.
