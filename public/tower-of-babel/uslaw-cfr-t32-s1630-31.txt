32 C.F.R. § 1630.31 — Class 3-A-S: Registrant deferred because of hardship to dependents (separated).
Citation: us:cfr:32:1630.31
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1630.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1630 (Classification Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any registrant who has been separated from active military service by reason of dependency or hardship shall be placed in Class 3-A-S unless his period of military service qualifies him for Class 4-A or 1-D-E. No registrant shall be retained in Class 3-A-S for more than six months.
