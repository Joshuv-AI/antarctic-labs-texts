27 C.F.R. § 7.222 — Conditions of basic permit.
Citation: us:cfr:27:7.222
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-7.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 7 (Labeling And Advertising Of Malt Beverages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A basic permit is conditioned upon compliance with the requirements of 27 U.S.C. 205, including the labeling and advertising provisions of this part. A willful violation of the conditions of a basic permit provides grounds for the revocation or suspension of the permit, as applicable, as set forth in part 1 of this chapter.
