30 C.F.R. § 1218.307 — How do I pay royalties due for my existing leases that qualify for near-term production incentives under BLM regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1218.307
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1218.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1218 (Collection Of Royalties, Rentals, Bonuses, And Other Monies Due The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you qualify for a production incentive under BLM regulations at 43 CFR subpart 3212, your royalty due on the production BLM determines to be qualified for a production incentive under 43 CFR 3212.23 and 3212.24 is 50 percent of the amount of the total royalty that would otherwise be due under 30 CFR part 1206, subpart H.
