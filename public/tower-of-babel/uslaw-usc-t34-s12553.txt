34 U.S.C. § 12553 — Establishment of Office of the Police Corps and Law Enforcement Education
Citation: us:usc:34:12553
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12553
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter VII (Police Corps And Law Enforcement Officers Training And Education); Part A (Police Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the Department of Justice, under the general authority of the Attorney General, an Office of the Police Corps and Law Enforcement Education.
