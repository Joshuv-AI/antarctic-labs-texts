21 C.F.R. § 1003.5 — Effect of regulations on other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1003.5
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1003.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1003 (Notification Of Defects Or Failure To Comply). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The remedies provided for in this subchapter shall be in addition to and not in substitution for any other remedies provided by law and shall not relieve any person from liability at common law or under statutory law.
