48 U.S.C. § 1713 — Qualifications for Office of Delegate
Citation: us:usc:48:1713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1713
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 16 (Delegates To Congress); Subchapter I (Guam And Virgin Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To be eligible for the Office of Delegate a candidate must—
(a)
be at least twenty-five years of age on the date of the election,
(b)
have been a citizen of the United States for at least seven years prior to the date of the election,
(c)
be an inhabitant of the territory from which he is elected, and
(d)
not be, on the date of the election, a candidate for any other office.
