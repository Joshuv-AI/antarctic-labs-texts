46 C.F.R. § 46.10-40 — -40 Nonsubmergence subdivision load line (Great Lakes).
Citation: us:cfr:46:46.10-40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-46.10-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 46 (Subdivision Load Lines For Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Passenger vessels on the Great Lakes of 150 gross tons or over shall not submerge the subdivision load line applicable to the voyage.
