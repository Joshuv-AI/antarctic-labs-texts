7 C.F.R. § 3565.353 — Affirmative fair housing marketing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3565.353
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3565.353
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3565 (Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a condition of the guarantee, the lender must ensure that the lender and borrower are in compliance with the approved Affirmative Fair Housing Marketing Plan. This plan must be reviewed annually by the lender to ensure that the borrower remains in compliance and to recommend modifications, as necessary.
