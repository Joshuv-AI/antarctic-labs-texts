10 C.F.R. § 35.2092 — Records of decay-in-storage.
Citation: us:cfr:10:35.2092
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-35.2092
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 35 (Medical Use Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensee shall maintain records of the disposal of licensed materials, as required by § 35.92, for 3 years. The record must include the date of the disposal, the survey instrument used, the background radiation level, the radiation level measured at the surface of each waste container, and the name of the individual who performed the survey.
