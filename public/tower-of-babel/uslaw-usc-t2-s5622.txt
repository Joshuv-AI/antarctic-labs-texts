2 U.S.C. § 5622 — Lump sum allowance for Corrections Calendar Office
Citation: us:usc:2:5622
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5622
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter IX (Corrections Calendar Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be a lump sum allowance of $300,000 per fiscal year for the salaries and expenses of the Corrections Calendar Office, established by section 5621 of this title. Such amount shall be allocated between the majority party and the minority party as determined by the Speaker, in consultation with the minority leader.
