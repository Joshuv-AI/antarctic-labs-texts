41 C.F.R. § 304-3.16 — -3.16 Penalties for unauthorized non-Federal source payment acceptance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.16
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If an employee accepts a payment from a non-Federal source in violation of this part, they may be required, in addition to any other penalty provided by law and applicable regulations, to pay the general fund of the Treasury, an amount equal to any payment accepted; and
(b) For reimbursement under paragraph (a) of this section, an employee will not be entitled to any reimbursement from the Government for travel expenses that the payment was intended to cover.
