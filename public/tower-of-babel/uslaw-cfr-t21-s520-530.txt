21 C.F.R. § 520.530 — Cythioate oral liquid.
Citation: us:cfr:21:520.530
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-520.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 520 (Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each milliliter contains 15 milligrams of cythioate.
(b) Sponsor. See Nos. 054771 and 058198 in § 510.600 of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use—(1) Amount. 15 milligrams cythioate per 10 pounds of body weight every third day or twice a week.
(2) Indications for use. Dogs, for control of fleas.
(3) Limitations. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
