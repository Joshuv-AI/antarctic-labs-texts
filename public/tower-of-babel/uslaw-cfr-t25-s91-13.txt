25 C.F.R. § 91.13 — Health, sanitation, and sewerage disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:25:91.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-91.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 91 (Government Of Indian Villages, Osage Reservation, Oklahoma). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Health, sanitation, and sewerage disposal problems within the village reserves shall be subject to and controlled by applicable County and State laws.
