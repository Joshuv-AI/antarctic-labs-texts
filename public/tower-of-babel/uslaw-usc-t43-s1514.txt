43 U.S.C. § 1514 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:43:1514
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1514
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32 (Colorado River Basin Project); Subchapter II (Investigations And Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are required to carry out the purposes of this subchapter.
