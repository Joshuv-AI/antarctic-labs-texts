43 C.F.R. § 3934.30 — License terminations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3934.30
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3934.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3930 (Management Of Oil Shale Exploration And Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BLM may terminate an exploration license if:
(a) The BLM issued it in violation of any law or regulation, or if there are substantive factual errors, such as a lack of title;
(b) The licensee does not comply with the terms and conditions of the exploration license; or
(c) The licensee does not comply with the approved exploration plan.
