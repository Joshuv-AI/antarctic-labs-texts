20 C.F.R. § 261.3 — Change of legal interpretation or administrative ruling.
Citation: us:cfr:20:261.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-261.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 261 (Administrative Finality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A change of legal interpretation or administrative ruling upon which a decision is based does not render a decision erroneous and does not provide a basis for reopening.
