5 C.F.R. § 1840.1 — Service of subpoenas by mail.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1840.1
Creator: Office Of Special Counsel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1840.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1840 (Subpoenas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to all other methods of authorized service, an Office of Special Counsel subpoena may be served by mailing a copy to the person at his or her residence or place of business by certified or registered mail.
