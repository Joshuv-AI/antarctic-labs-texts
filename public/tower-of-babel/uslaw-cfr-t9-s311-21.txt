9 C.F.R. § 311.21 — Mange or scab.
Citation: us:cfr:9:311.21
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-311.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 311 (Disposal Of Diseased Or Otherwise Adulterated Carcasses And Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carcasses of livestock affected with mange or scab in advanced stages, showing cachexia or extensive inflammation of the flesh, shall be condemned. When the disease is slight, the carcass may be passed after removal of the affected portion.
