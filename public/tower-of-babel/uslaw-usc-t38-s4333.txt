38 U.S.C. § 4333 — Outreach
Citation: us:usc:38:4333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section4333
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 43 (Employment And Reemployment Rights Of Members Of The Uniformed Services); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, the Secretary of Defense, and the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall take such actions as such Secretaries determine are appropriate to inform persons entitled to rights and benefits under this chapter and employers of the rights, benefits, and obligations of such persons and such employers under this chapter.
