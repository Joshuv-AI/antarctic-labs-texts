29 C.F.R. § 1602.36 — Schools exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1602.36
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1602.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1602 (Recordkeeping And Reporting Requirements Under Title Vii, The Ada, Gina, And The Pwfa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The recordkeeping and report-filing requirements of subparts I and J of this part shall not apply to State or local educational institutions or to school districts or school systems or any other educational functions. The previous sentence of this section shall not act to bar jurisdiction which otherwise would attach under § 1602.30.
