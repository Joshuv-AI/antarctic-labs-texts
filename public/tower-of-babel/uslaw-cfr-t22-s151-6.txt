22 C.F.R. § 151.6 — Authorized insurer.
Citation: us:cfr:22:151.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-151.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 151 (Compulsory Liability Insurance For Diplomatic Missions And Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The insurance must be issued by an insurer licensed or otherwise authorized by applicable law to do business in the jurisdiction where the motor vehicle, vessel or aircraft is principally garaged, berthed or kept.
