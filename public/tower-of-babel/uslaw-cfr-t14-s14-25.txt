14 C.F.R. § 14.25 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:14:14.25
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-14.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 14 (Rules Implementing The Equal Access To Justice Act Of 1980). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant and agency counsel may agree on a proposed settlement of the award before final action on the application, either in connection with a settlement of the underlying proceeding, or after the underlying proceeding has been concluded. If a prevailing party and agency counsel agree on a proposed settlement of an award before an application has been filed, the application shall be filed with the proposed settlement.
