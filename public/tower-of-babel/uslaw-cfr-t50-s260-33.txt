50 C.F.R. § 260.33 — Compliance with statutory and regulatory requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:50:260.33
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-260.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 260 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Approved Establishments shall comply with all statutory and regulatory requirements and provisions pertaining to the production of fish and fishery products and other marine ingredients for human or animal consumption.
