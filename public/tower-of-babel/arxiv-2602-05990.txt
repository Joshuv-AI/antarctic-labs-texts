Categories graded by group homomorphisms
Jonathan Davies

Abstract
We generalise to a group homomorphism τ \tau the χ \chi -graded categories of Sözer and Virelizier. These are categories in which both morphisms and objects have compatible degrees. We give a ‘half-enriched’ Yoneda lemma, a structure theorem for semisimple τ \tau -graded categories, and an alternative picture of τ \tau -graded categories in terms of pseudofunctors into ℂ ​ at \mathrm{\mathbb{C}at} . † † Date: 5 February 2026 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 18N10, 18A50, 18D20 Key words and phrases: 2-category, graded category, enriched category, module category
