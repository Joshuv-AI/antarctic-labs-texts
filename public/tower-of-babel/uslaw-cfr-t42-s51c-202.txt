42 C.F.R. § 51c.202 — c.202 Application.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51c.202
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51c.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51c (PART 51c—GRANTS FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be approved by the Secretary under this subpart, an application for a grant must, in addition to meeting the requirements of § 51c.104 of subpart A, contain information sufficient to enable the Secretary to determine that the project for which the grant is sought will meet the requirements of § 51c.203.
