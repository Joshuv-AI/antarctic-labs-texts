20 U.S.C. § 4361 — Supervision of public business of Gallaudet University
Citation: us:usc:20:4361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4361
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 55 (Education Of The Deaf); Subchapter II (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Education is charged with the supervision of public business relating to Gallaudet University.
