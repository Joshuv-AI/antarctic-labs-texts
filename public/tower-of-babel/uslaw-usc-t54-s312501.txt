54 U.S.C. § 312501 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:54:312501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section312501
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3125 (Preservation Of Historical And Archeological Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “State” includes a State, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands.
