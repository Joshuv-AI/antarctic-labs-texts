24 C.F.R. § 180.220 — Separation of functions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:180.220
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-180.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 180 (Consolidated Hud Hearing Procedures For Civil Rights Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No officer, employee, or agent of the Federal Government engaged in the performance of investigative, conciliatory, or prosecutorial functions in connection with the proceeding shall, in that proceeding or any factually related proceeding under this part, participate or advise in the decision of the ALJ, except as a witness or counsel during the proceedings or in its appellate review.
