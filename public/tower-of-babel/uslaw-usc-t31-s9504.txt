31 U.S.C. § 9504 — Review and recommendations
Citation: us:usc:31:9504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section9504
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 95 (Government Pension Plan Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When necessary or when requested by either House of Congress or a committee of Congress, the Comptroller General shall—
(1)
review financial and actuarial statements provided under section 9503 of this title to decide whether the reporting requirements of section 9503 are adequate to carry out section 9501 of this title; and
(2)
submit to Congress recommendations for legislation necessary to carry out section 9501 of this title.
