48 C.F.R. § 928.101-1 — 928.101-1 Policy on use.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 928 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the restriction on use of bid guarantees in 48 CFR 28.101-1(a), a bid guarantee may be required only for fixed price or unit price contracts entered into as a result of sealed bidding. They may not be required for negotiated contracts.
