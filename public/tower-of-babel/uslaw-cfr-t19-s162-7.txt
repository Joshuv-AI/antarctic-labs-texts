19 C.F.R. § 162.7 — Search of vehicles, persons, or beasts.
Citation: us:cfr:19:162.7
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-162.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 162 (Inspection, Search, And Seizure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Customs officer may stop, search, and examine any vehicle, person, or beast, or search any trunk or envelope wherever found, in accordance with section 3061 of the Revised Statutes (19 U.S.C. 482).
