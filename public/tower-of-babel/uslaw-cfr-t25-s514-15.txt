25 C.F.R. § 514.15 — May tribes submit fingerprint cards to the Commission for processing?
Citation: us:cfr:25:514.15
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-514.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 514 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribes may submit fingerprint cards to the Commission for processing by the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the Commission may charge a fee to process fingerprint cards on behalf of the tribes.
