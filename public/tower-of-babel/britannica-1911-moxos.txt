MOXOS, Mojos, Mohos, a tribe of South American Indians
living around the head-waters of the Maidera river, northern
Bolivia, particularly on both sides on the Mamoré. They
submitted to Inca domination, but in 1564 gallantly repulsed the
Spaniards. A century later, however, the Jesuits were welcomed,
and the Moxos became devout Catholics. They number some
30,000.
