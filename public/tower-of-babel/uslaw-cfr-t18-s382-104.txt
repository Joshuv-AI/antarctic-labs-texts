18 C.F.R. § 382.104 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:18:382.104
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-382.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 382 (Annual Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may refuse to process any petition, application, or other filing submitted by or on the behalf of any person that does not pay the annual charge assessed when due, or may take any other appropriate action permitted by law.
