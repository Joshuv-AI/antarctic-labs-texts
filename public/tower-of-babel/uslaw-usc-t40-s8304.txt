40 U.S.C. § 8304 — Priority projects
Citation: us:usc:40:8304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8304
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 83 (Washington Metropolitan Region Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the policy pursuant to section 8303 of this title for the attainment of the objective established by section 8302 of this title, priority should be given to the solution, on a unified metropolitan basis, of the problems of water supply, sewage disposal, and water pollution and transportation.
