48 C.F.R. § 6101.31 — 6101.31 Payment of award [Rule 31].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6101.31
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6101.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6101 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When permitted by law, Board awards under contracts may be paid from the permanent indefinite judgment fund under 31 U.S.C. 1304 and 31 CFR part 256. An EAJA award is paid from funds of the respondent.
