7 C.F.R. § 707.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:707.1
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-707.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 707 (Payments Due Persons Who Have Died, Disappeared, Or Have Been Declared Incompetent). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all programs in title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations which are administered by the Farm Service Agency under which payments are made to eligible program participants. This part also applies to all other programs to which this part is applicable by the individual program regulations.
