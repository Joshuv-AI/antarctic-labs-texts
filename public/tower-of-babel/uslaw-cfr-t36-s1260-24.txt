36 C.F.R. § 1260.24 — Who is responsible for declassification of foreign government information in NARA's holdings?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1260.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1260.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1260 (Declassification Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The agency that received or classified the information is responsible for its declassification.
(b) In the case of a defunct agency, NARA is responsible for declassification of foreign government information, as defined in section 6.1(s) of the Order, in its holdings and will consult with the agencies having equity in the records before making declassification determinations.
