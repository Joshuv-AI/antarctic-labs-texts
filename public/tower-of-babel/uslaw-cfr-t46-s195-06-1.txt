46 C.F.R. § 195.06-1 — -1 Lifesaving appliances and arrangements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:195.06-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-195.06-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 195 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All lifesaving appliances and arrangements shall be in accordance with the requirements for special purpose vessels in subchapter W (Lifesaving Appliances and Arrangements) of this chapter.
