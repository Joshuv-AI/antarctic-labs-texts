17 C.F.R. § 4.11 — Exemption from section 4n(3)(B).
Citation: us:cfr:17:4.11
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-4.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 4 (Commodity Pool Operators And Commodity Trading Advisors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of section 4n(3)(B) of the Act shall not apply to any commodity pool operator or commodity trading advisor that is registered under the Act as such or that is exempt from such registration.
