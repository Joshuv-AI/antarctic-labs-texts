48 C.F.R. § 52.249-14 — 52.249-14 Excusable Delays.
Citation: us:cfr:48:52.249-14
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-52.249-14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 52 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 49.505(b), insert the following clause in solicitations and contracts for supplies, services, construction, and research and development on a fee basis whenever a cost-reimbursement contract is contemplated. Also insert the clause in time-and-material contracts, and labor-hour contracts. When used in construction contracts, substitute the words “completion time” for “delivery schedule” in the last sentence of the clause.
