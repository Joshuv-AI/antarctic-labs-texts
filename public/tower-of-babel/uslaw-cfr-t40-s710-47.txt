40 C.F.R. § 710.47 — When to submit substantiation or information on previous substantiation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:710.47
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-710.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 710 (Compilation Of The Tsca Chemical Substance Inventory). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All persons required to substantiate a confidentiality claim pursuant to § 710.43(a) or (b)(1) must submit their substantiation not later than November 1, 2020.
(b) All persons who seek an exemption under § 710.43(b)(2) must submit the information specified in § 710.43(b)(2)(ii) not later than November 1, 2020.
