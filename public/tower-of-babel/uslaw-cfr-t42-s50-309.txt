42 C.F.R. § 50.309 — Recordkeeping requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:50.309
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-50.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 50 (Policies Of General Applicability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Programs or projects to which this subpart applies must maintain copies of the certifications and documentation specified in §§ 50.304 and 50.306 for three years pursuant to the retention and custodial requirements for records at 2 CFR 200.334.
