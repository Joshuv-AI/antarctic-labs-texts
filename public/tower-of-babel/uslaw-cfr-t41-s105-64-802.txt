41 C.F.R. § 105-64.802 — -64.802 Can I appeal a decision to a privacy complaint?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-64.802
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-64.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-64 (64—Gsa Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may file an appeal within 30 workdays of a denial of a privacy complaint by writing to: GSA Privacy Act Officer (CIB), General Services Administration, 1800 F Street NW., Washington, DC 20405. Mark both the envelope and appeal letter “Privacy Act Complaint appeal”.
