16 U.S.C. § 6109 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:6109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6109
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 80 (Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $6,500,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2028.
(b) Use of funds
Of the amounts made available under subsection (a) for each fiscal year, not less than 75 percent shall be expended for projects carried out at a location outside of the United States.
