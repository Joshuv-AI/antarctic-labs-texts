21 U.S.C. § 205 — Display of license in pharmacy
Citation: us:usc:21:205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section205
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 7 (Practice Of Pharmacy And Sale Of Poisons In Consular Districts In China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every license to practice pharmacy shall be conspicuously displayed by the person to whom the same has been issued in the pharmacy, drug store, or place of business, if any, of which the said person is the owner or part owner or manager.
