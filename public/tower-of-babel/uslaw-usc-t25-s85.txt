25 U.S.C. § 85 — Contracts relating to tribal funds or property
Citation: us:usc:25:85
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section85
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 3 (Agreements With Indians); Subchapter II (Contracts With Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No contract made with any Indian, where such contract relates to the tribal funds or property in the hands of the United States, shall be valid, nor shall any payment for services rendered in relation thereto be made unless the consent of the United States has previously been given.
