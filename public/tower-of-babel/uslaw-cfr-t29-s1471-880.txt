29 C.F.R. § 1471.880 — What factors may influence the debarring official during reconsideration?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.880
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.880
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The debarring official may reduce or terminate your debarment based on—
(a) Newly discovered material evidence;
(b) A reversal of the conviction or civil judgment upon which your debarment was based;
(c) A bona fide change in ownership or management;
(d) Elimination of other causes for which the debarment was imposed; or
(e) Other reasons the debarring official finds appropriate.
