10 C.F.R. § 1004.12 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1004.12
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1004.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1004 (Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise noted, in computing any period of time prescribed or allowed by this part, the day of the event from which the designated period of time begins to run is not to be included; the last day of the period so computed is to be included; and Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays are excepted.
