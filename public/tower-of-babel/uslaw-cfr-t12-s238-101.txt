12 C.F.R. § 238.101 — Authority and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:238.101
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-238.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 238 (Savings And Loan Holding Companies (Regulation Ll)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 10(f) of HOLA which requires savings associations with holding companies to provide the Board not less than 30 days' notice of a proposed declaration of a dividend. This subpart applies to all declarations of dividends by a subsidiary savings association of a savings and loan holding company.
