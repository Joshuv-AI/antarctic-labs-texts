13 U.S.C. § 212 — Refusal or neglect of employees to perform duties
Citation: us:usc:13:212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section212
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 7 (Offenses And Penalties); Subchapter I (Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being an employee referred to in subchapter II of chapter 1 of this title, and having taken and subscribed the oath of office, neglects or refuses, without justifiable cause, to perform the duties enjoined on such employee by this title, shall be fined not more than $500.
