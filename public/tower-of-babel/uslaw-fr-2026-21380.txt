DATES: Comments are encouraged and will be accepted until December 18,

ADDRESSES: You may submit comments on the Federal Rulemaking Portal:

viewing at https://www.regulations.gov as they are received without 
change, including any personal identifiers or contact information.

Background

    The FPA Portal is an enrollee notification and eligibility 
verification system that will support the OPM requirement under the 
Federal Employees Health Benefits (FEHB) Protection Act of 2025 (Sec. 
90101, Pub. L. 119-21, 139 Stat. 362) to conduct a comprehensive audit 
regarding family members who are covered in the FEHB Program. It will 
provide a secure and consistent process to ensure that only eligible 
individuals are enrolled in the FEHB Program. This will strengthen 
enrollment integrity and ensure ineligible family members are not 
participating in the FEHB Program. The FPA Portal will involve 
enrollees whose family member's eligibility has not yet been verified, 
OPM personnel, and a contracted vendor. Employing agencies, payroll 
offices, and participating health insurance carriers will be 
incorporated in subsequent phases. Enrollees whose family member's 
eligibility has not been verified will receive FPA Portal generated 
emails and mailed notices. These notices will direct enrollees to 
securely sign in to the FPA Portal enabling them to provide 
documentation to allow verification of the eligibility of their family 
members.

[[Page 66173]]

The FPA Portal will send status updates and reminders when a response 
or additional action is required. The enrollee will use online forms 
and document-upload features within the FPA Portal to enter requested 
information and submit supporting eligibility documents. The documents 
required depend on the family member's relationship to the enrollee and 
include records used to establish a spouse's or child's eligibility. 
The list of these records can be found in the FEHB Handbook at https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/reference-materials/reference/family-members/. The FPA Portal will use automated document-
verification capabilities to review the submitted information and 
documentation and determine whether the submission is sufficient to 
support an eligibility decision. If a case cannot be resolved through 
the automated review process, it will be referred to the contracted 
vendor and OPM. The results of that review will be returned to the FPA 
Portal for continued processing.
