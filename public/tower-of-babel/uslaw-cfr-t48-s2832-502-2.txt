48 C.F.R. § 2832.502-2 — 2832.502-2 Contract finance office clearance.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2832 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before taking any of the actions prescribed in FAR 32.502-2, the contracting officer shall obtain advice and assistance from the bureau's Chief Financial Officer.
