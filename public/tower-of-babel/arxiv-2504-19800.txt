Long time asymptotics of a perturbed modified KdV equation
Gong Chen, Jiaqi Liu, Yuanhong Tian

Abstract
. We derive full asymptotics of the modified KdV equation (mKdV) with a higher-order perturbative term. We make use of the perturbative theory of infinite-dimensional integrable systems developed by P. Deift and X. Zhou [ 7 ] , and some new and simpler proofs of certain L ∞ L^{\infty} bounds and L p L^{p} a priori estimates developed recently in [ 3 ] . We show that the perturbed equation exhibits the same long-time behavior as the completely integrable mKdV.



1.
Introduction

It is well-known that wave motions in complex media are often influenced by additional energy introduced through related processes. Accordingly, the mathematical models describing such motion are frequently formulated as evolution equations incorporating source-like terms or external forcing. We refer to
[
10
,
11
]
for details on physical backgrounds and derivations for the KdV models as concrete examples.
In this paper we are interested in the long time asymptotics
of perturbed (forced) modified KdV equations

(1.1)

u_{t}+u_{xxx}-6u^{2}u_{x}=\varepsilon F(u),\qquad(x,t)\in(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R}^{+}).

We recall the standard defocussing modified KdV equation (mKdV)

(1.2)

u_{t}+u_{xxx}-6u^{2}u_{x}=0.

Important conserved quantities of mKdV are the mass
M
, energy
H
, and momentum
P
:

(1.3)

\displaystyle M=\int_{\mathbb{R}}u^{2}dx,\quad H=\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|\partial_{x}u\right|^{2}+|u|^{4}dx,\quad P=\int_{\mathbb{R}}udx.

For concreteness and simplicity, in this article, we consider the model with an external force is given by a power nonlinearity of the dependent variable:

(1.4)

u_{t}+u_{xxx}-6u^{2}u_{x}=\varepsilon u^{\ell},\qquad(x,t)\in(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R}^{+}).

Here
0<\varepsilon\ll 1
,
\ell
is some positive constant.
It is easy to verify that the

term on the RHS of (
1.4
) is a dissipative term if

is an odd number. In this paper we will not emphasize whether the perturbative term is dissipative or not. It will become clear in our proof that the long time asymptotics of (
1.4

1.2.
The inverse scattering for the mKdV

To describe our approach, we recall that (
1.2
)
is the compatibility condition for the following commutator:

\displaystyle\left[\partial_{x}-\mathcal{U},\partial_{t}-\mathcal{W}\right]=0,

\displaystyle=-iz\sigma+\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}0&u\\

\displaystyle=(-4iz^{3}-2izu^{2})\sigma+\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}0&4z^{2}u+2izu_{x}-u_{xx}+2u^{3}\\

Thus (

(1.8)

\frac{d}{dx}\Psi=-iz\sigma\Psi+U(x)\Psi

where

U(x)=\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}0&{u(x)}\\
{{u(x)}}&0\end{array}\right).

This is a standard AKNS system. If

, Equation (
1.8
) admits bounded
solutions for

of (

\lim_{x\to\pm\infty}\Psi^{\pm}(x,z)e^{-ixz\sigma}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}{1}&0\\
0&{1}\end{array}\right),

and there is a matrix

, the scattering matrix, with

\Psi^{+}(x,z)=\Psi^{-}(x,z)S(z)

The matrix

takes the form

(1.9)

S(z)=\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}{a(z)}&{\breve{b}(z)}\\[10.0pt]
{b(z)}&{\breve{a}(z)}\end{array}\right)

and the determinant relation gives

a(z)\breve{a}(z)-b(z)\breve{b}(z)=1.

Combining this with the symmetry relations

(1.10)

\displaystyle\breve{a}(z)=\overline{a(\overline{z})},\quad\breve{b}(z)=\overline{b(z)}

we arrive at

|a(z)|^{2}-|b(z)|^{2}=1

and conclude that
a(z)
is zero-free. By the standard inverse scattering theory, we formulate the reflection coefficient:

(1.11)

r(z)=\breve{b}(z)/a(z),\quad z\in\mathbb{R}.

In
[
19
]

(1.12)

\left\|f\right\|_{H^{i,j}(\mathbb{R})}=\left(\left\|(1+|x|^{j})f\right\|_{2}^{2}+\left\|f^{(i)}\right\|_{2}^{2}\right)^{1/2}

and the map

is Lipschitz continuous. Since we are dealing with the defocussing mKdV, only the reflection coefficient

Theorem 1.1
.
