30 C.F.R. § 254.24 — What information must I include in the “Equipment inventory” appendix?
Citation: us:cfr:30:254.24
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-254.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 254 (Oil-Spill Response Requirements For Facilities Located Seaward Of The Coast Line). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your “Equipment inventory appendix” must include:
(a) An inventory of spill-response materials and supplies, services, equipment, and response vessels available locally and regionally. You must identify each supplier and provide their locations and telephone numbers.
(b) A description of the procedures for inspecting and maintaining spill-response equipment in accordance with § 254.43.
