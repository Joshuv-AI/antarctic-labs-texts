44 C.F.R. § 362.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:44:362.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-362.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 362 (Criteria For Acceptance Of Gifts, Bequests, Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes criteria for determining whether the Administrator may accept gifts, bequests, or donations of services, money or property for the National Earthquake Hazards Reduction Program (NEHRP), under section 9 of the National Earthquake Hazards Reduction Program Reauthorization Act, 42 U.S.C. 7705c.
