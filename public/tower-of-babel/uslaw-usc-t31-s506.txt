31 U.S.C. § 506 — Office of Federal Procurement Policy
Citation: us:usc:31:506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section506
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 5 (Office Of Management And Budget); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of Federal Procurement Policy, established under section 1101(a) of title 41, is an office in the Office of Management and Budget.
