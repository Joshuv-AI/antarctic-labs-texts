28 C.F.R. § 29.11 — Sale or other transfer of an enrolled vehicle.
Citation: us:cfr:28:29.11
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-29.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 29 (Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the transferral of ownership of an enrolled vehicle, the transferring owner must completely remove the program decals, change the license plate(s) if necessary, and is encouraged to notify the participating agency in writing of the transfer of ownership of the vehicle.
