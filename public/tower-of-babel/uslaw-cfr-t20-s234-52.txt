20 C.F.R. § 234.52 — Effect of payment on other benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:20:234.52
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-234.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 234 (Lump-Sum Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lump-sum refund payment is deductible from the RLS; however, it has no effect on the payment of other benefits.
