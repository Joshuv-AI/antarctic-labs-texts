46 C.F.R. § 151.56-1 — -1 Prohibited materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.56-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.56-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When one of the following paragraphs of this section is referenced in table 151.05, the materials listed in that paragraph may not be used in components that contact the cargo or its vapor:
(a) Aluminum or aluminum alloys.
(b) Copper or copper alloys.
(c) Zinc, galvanized steel, or alloys having more than 10 percent zinc by weight.
(d) Magnesium.
(e) Lead.
(f) Silver or silver alloys.
(g) Mercury.
