27 C.F.R. § 19.679 — Duration of permit.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.679
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.679
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proprietor of an alcohol fuel plant may conduct the operations authorized by the permit on a continuing basis unless:
(a) The proprietor voluntarily surrenders the permit;
(b) TTB suspends or revokes the permit pursuant to § 19.697; or
(c) The permit is automatically terminated under its own terms or in accordance with § 19.684.
