7 U.S.C. § 1704c — Payments by Secretary of Defense in liquidation of amount due for foreign currencies
Citation: us:usc:7:1704c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1704c
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter II (Economic Assistance And Food Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall pay to the Commodity Credit Corporation an amount not to exceed $6,000,000 per year until the amount due for foreign currencies used for housing constructed or acquired under title II of the Food for Peace Act (7 U.S.C. 1721–1726) has been liquidated.
