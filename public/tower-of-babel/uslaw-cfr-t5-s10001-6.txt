5 C.F.R. § 10001.6 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:10001.6
Creator: National Council On Disability
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-10001.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 10001 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will not charge a fee for search or review of records requested under this part, or for the correction of records. If you request copies of records, we may charge a fee of $.10 per page.
