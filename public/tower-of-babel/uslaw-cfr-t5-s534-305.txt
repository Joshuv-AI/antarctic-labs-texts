5 C.F.R. § 534.305 — Pay periods and computation of pay.
Citation: us:cfr:5:534.305
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-534.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 534 (Pay Under Other Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements of 5 U.S.C. 5504, must be applied to employees of temporary organizations. This includes requirements for biweekly pay periods and requirements for converting an annual rate of basic pay to a basic hourly, daily, weekly, or biweekly rate.
(b) Employees of temporary organizations must receive basic pay on an hourly basis.
