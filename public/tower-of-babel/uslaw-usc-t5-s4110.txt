5 U.S.C. § 4110 — Expenses of attendance at meetings
Citation: us:usc:5:4110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4110
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 41 (Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations available to an agency for travel expenses are available for expenses of attendance at meetings which are concerned with the functions or activities for which the appropriation is made or which will contribute to improved conduct, supervision, or management of the functions or activities.
