25 C.F.R. § 26.19 — Will I be required to contribute financially to my employment and training goals?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.19
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the Job Placement and Training Program clients are required to seek other funding, including the use of personal resources as a condition of their ISP.
