15 U.S.C. § 1141n — Rights of extension of protection
Citation: us:usc:15:1141n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1141n
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter IV (The Madrid Protocol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a United States registration and a subsequently issued certificate of extension of protection to the United States are owned by the same person, identify the same mark, and list the same goods or services, the extension of protection shall have the same rights that accrued to the registration prior to issuance of the certificate of extension of protection.
