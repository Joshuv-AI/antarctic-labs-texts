30 C.F.R. § 755.14 — Amendments.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 755 (Tribal-Federal Intergovernmental Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agreement that has been approved pursuant to this part may be amended by mutual agreement of the Secretary and the officers of the tribe.
