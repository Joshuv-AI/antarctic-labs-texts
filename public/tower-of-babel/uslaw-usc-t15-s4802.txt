15 U.S.C. § 4802 — Council established
Citation: us:usc:15:4802
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4802
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 74 (Competitiveness Policy Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established the Competitiveness Policy Council (hereafter in this chapter referred to as the “Council”), an advisory committee under the provisions of chapter 10 of title 5.
