26 C.F.R. § 31.3102-2 — -2 Manner and time of payment of employee tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:31.3102-2
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-31.3102-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 31 (Employment Taxes And Collection Of Income Tax At Source). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee tax is payable to the district director in the manner and at the time prescribed in Subpart G of the regulations in this part. For provisions relating to the payment by an employee of employee tax in respect of tips, see paragraph (d) of § 31.3102-3.
