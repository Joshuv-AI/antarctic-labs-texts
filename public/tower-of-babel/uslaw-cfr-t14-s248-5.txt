14 C.F.R. § 248.5 — Withholding from public disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:14:248.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-248.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 248 (Submission Of Audit Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The special reports required to be filed by § 248.2 shall be withheld from public disclosure, until further order of the BTS, if such treatment is requested by the air carrier at the time of filing.
