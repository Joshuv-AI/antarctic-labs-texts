16 U.S.C. § 3119 — Cooperative agreements
Citation: us:usc:16:3119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3119
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 51 (Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation); Subchapter II (Subsistence Management And Use). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may enter into cooperative agreements or otherwise cooperate with other Federal agencies, the State, Native Corporations, other appropriate persons and organizations, and, acting through the Secretary of State, other nations to effectuate the purposes and policies of this subchapter.
