40 C.F.R. § 62.14462 — Where must I keep the records?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14462
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14462
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must maintain all records specified under § 62.14460 onsite in either paper copy or computer-readable format, unless an alternative format is approved by the EPA Administrator.
