21 C.F.R. § 213.63 — Equipment design, size, and location.
Citation: us:cfr:21:213.63
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-213.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 213 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medical Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Equipment used in the manufacture, processing, packing, or holding of a medical gas shall be of appropriate design and adequate size, and be suitably located to facilitate operations for its intended use and any necessary cleaning and maintenance.
