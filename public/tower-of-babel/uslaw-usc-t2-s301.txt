2 U.S.C. § 301 — Preservation of existing appointing authorities
Citation: us:usc:2:301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section301
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 10 (Classification Of Employees Of House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not be held or considered to change or otherwise affect—
(1)
any authority to establish positions under the House of Representatives which are not within the purview of this chapter, or
(2)
any authority to make appointments to positions under the House of Representatives, irrespective of whether such positions are within the purview of this chapter.
