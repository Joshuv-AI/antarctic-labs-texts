40 C.F.R. § 374.5 — Copy of complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:40:374.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-374.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 374 (Prior Notice Of Citizen Suits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the time of filing an action under this Act, the plaintiff must provide a copy of the complaint to the Attorney General of the United States and to the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.
