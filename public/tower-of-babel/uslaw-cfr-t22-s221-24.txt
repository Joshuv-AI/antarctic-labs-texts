22 C.F.R. § 221.24 — Subrogation of A.I.D.
Citation: us:cfr:22:221.24
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-221.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 221 (Israel Loan Guarantee Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of payment by A.I.D. to a Noteholder under this Guarantee, A.I.D. shall be subrogated to the extent of such payment to all of the rights of such Noteholder against the Borrower under the related Note.
