36 U.S.C. § 154103 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:154103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section154103
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1541 (Naval Sea Cadet Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in this chapter, eligibility for membership in the corporation and the rights, privileges, and designation of classes of members are as provided in the constitution and bylaws of the corporation.
