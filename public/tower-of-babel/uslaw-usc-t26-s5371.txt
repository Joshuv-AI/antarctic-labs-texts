26 U.S.C. § 5371 — Insurance coverage, etc.
Citation: us:usc:26:5371
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5371
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter F (Bonded And Taxpaid Wine Premises); Part II (Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any remission, abatement, refund, or credit of, or other relief from, taxes on wines or wine spirits authorized by law shall be allowed only to the extent that the claimant is not indemnified or recompensed for the tax.
