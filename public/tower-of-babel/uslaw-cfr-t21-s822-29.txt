21 C.F.R. § 822.29 — May I request a waiver of a specific requirement of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:21:822.29
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-822.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 822 (Postmarket Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may request that we waive any specific requirement of this part. You may submit your request, with supporting documentation, separately or as a part of your postmarket surveillance submission to the address in § 822.8.
