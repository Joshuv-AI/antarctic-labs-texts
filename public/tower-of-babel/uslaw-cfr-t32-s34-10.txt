32 C.F.R. § 34.10 — Purpose of financial and program management.
Citation: us:cfr:32:34.10
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-34.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 34 (Administrative Requirements For Grants And Agreements With For-Profit Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 34.11 through 34.17 prescribe standards for financial management systems; methods for making payments; and rules for cost sharing and matching, program income, revisions to budgets and program plans, audits, allowable costs, and fee and profit.
