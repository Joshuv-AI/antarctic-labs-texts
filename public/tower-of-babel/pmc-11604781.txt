In this article the affiliation details for Author Polyanna da Conceição Bispo were incorrectly given as ‘6Department of Geography, School of Environment, Education and Development, University of Manchester, Manchester, United Kingdom. 7Biota Projetos e Consultoria Ambiental Ltda. Rua 86-C, 64, Setor Sul, CEP 74083-360, Goiânia, Goiás, Brasil.’ but should have been ‘6Department of Geography, School of Environment, Education and Development, University of Manchester, Manchester, United Kingdom’. The original article has been corrected.
