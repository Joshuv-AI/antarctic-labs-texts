22 U.S.C. § 9681 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter VI (Transitional Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) Agency
The term “agency” includes any entity, organizational unit, program, or function.
(2) Transition period
The term “transition period” means the period—
(A)
beginning on October 5, 2018; and
(B)
ending on the effective date of the reorganization plan required by section 9682(e) of this title.
