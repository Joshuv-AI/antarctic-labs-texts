25 C.F.R. § 44.104 — How can a grant be terminated?
Citation: us:cfr:25:44.104
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-44.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 44 (Grants Under The Tribally Controlled Schools Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grant can be terminated only by one of the following methods:
(a) Retrocession;
(b) Revocation of eligibility by the Secretary; or
(c) Reassumption by the Secretary.
