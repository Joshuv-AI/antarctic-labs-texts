25 C.F.R. § 582.6 — When will I receive a copy of the record on which the Chair relied?
Citation: us:cfr:25:582.6
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-582.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 582 (Appeals Of Disapprovals Of Gaming Ordinances, Resolutions, Or Amendments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 10 days of the filing of a notice of appeal, or as soon thereafter as practicable, the record on which the Chair relied will be transmitted to the tribe.
