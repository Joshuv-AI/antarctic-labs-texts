Asymptotics of the shifted finite differences of the overpatition function and a problem of Wang–Xie–Zhang
Gargi Mukherjee

Abstract
. Let p ¯ ​ ( n ) \overline{p}(n) denote the overpartition function, and for j ∈ ℕ j\in\mathbb{N} , Δ j r \Delta^{r}_{j} denote the r r -fold applications of the shifted difference operator Δ j \Delta_{j} defined by Δ j ​ ( a ) ​ ( n ) := a ⁡ ( n ) − a ⁡ ( n − j ) \Delta_{j}(a)(n):=a(n)-a(n-j) . The main goal of this paper is to derive an asymptotic expansion of Δ j r ​ ( p ¯ ) ​ ( n ) \Delta^{r}_{j}(\overline{p})(n) with an effective error bound which subsequently gives an answer to a problem of Wang, Xie, and Zhang. 1.
Introduction

An overpartition of a positive integer
n
, introduced by Corteel and Lovejoy
[
6
]
, is a non-increasing sequence of positive integers whose sum is
n
in which the first occurrence of a number may be overlined,
\overline{p}(n)
denotes the number of overpartitions of
n
, and
\overline{p}(0):=1
. For instance, there are
8
overpartitions of
3
enumerated by

3,\overline{3},2+1,\overline{2}+1,2+\overline{1},\overline{2}+\overline{1},1+1+1,\overline{1}+1+1.

Analogous to the celebrated Hardy-Ramanujan-Rademacher formula for the partition function
p(n)
(see
[
13
,
24
]
), Zuckerman’s
[
27
]
formula for
\overline{p}(n)
states that

\overline{p}(n)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\underset{2\nmid k}{\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}}\sqrt{k}\underset{(h,k)=1}{\sum_{h=0}^{k-1}}\dfrac{\omega(h,k)^{2}}{\omega(2h,k)}e^{-\frac{2\pi inh}{k}}\dfrac{d}{dn}\left(\dfrac{\sinh\frac{\pi\sqrt{n}}{k}}{\sqrt{n}}\right),

(1.1)

where

\omega(h,k)=\text{exp}\left(\pi i\sum_{r=1}^{k-1}\dfrac{r}{k}\left(\dfrac{hr}{k}-\left\lfloor\dfrac{hr}{k}\right\rfloor-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\right)

for
(h,k)\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\times\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}
. Similar to the work done by Lehmer
[
16
]
for
p(n)
, Engel
[
8
]
estimated an error term for
\overline{p}(n)
as

\overline{p}(n)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\underset{2\nmid k}{\sum_{k=1}^{N}}\sqrt{k}\underset{(h,k)=1}{\sum_{h=0}^{k-1}}\dfrac{\omega(h,k)^{2}}{\omega(2h,k)}e^{-\frac{2\pi inh}{k}}\dfrac{d}{dn}\left(\dfrac{\sinh\frac{\pi\sqrt{n}}{k}}{\sqrt{n}}\right)+R_{2}(n,N),

(1.2)

with

\left|R_{2}(n,N)\right|<\dfrac{N^{5/2}}{\pi n^{3/2}}\sinh\left(\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{n}}{N}\right).

(1.3)

Let
\Delta
be the forward difference operator defined by
\Delta(a)(n):=a(n)-a(n-1)
for a sequence
(a(n))_{n\geq 0}
and
\Delta^{r}
be the
r
-fold applications of
\Delta
defined as
\Delta^{r}a(n):=\Delta\left(\Delta^{r-1}a(n)\right)
for
r\in\mathbb{N}
. In 1977, Good
[
10
]
proposed a problem on
\Delta^{r}(p)(n)
which states that for
r\geq 3
,
\left(\Delta^{r}(p)(n)\right)_{n\geq 1}
is a sequence of alternating terms for a certain range
1\leq n\leq n_{0}(r)
and then become positive. Using Hardy-Ramanujan-Rademacher
[
13
,
24
]
formula for
p(n)
, Gupta proved that
\Delta^{r}(p)(n)>0
for all
n>n_{0}(r)
but without any explicit estimate for
n_{0}(r)
and conjectured that
n_{0}(r)\sim r^{3}
as
r\rightarrow\infty
. Odlyzko
[
22
]
settled the Good-Gupta conjecture by proving that
(-1)^{n}\Delta^{r}(p)(n)\geq 0
for all
1\leq n<n_{0}(r)
and
\Delta^{r}(p)(n)\geq 0
for
n\geq n_{0}(r)
with
n_{0}(r)\sim\frac{6}{\pi^{2}}r^{2}(\log r)^{2}
as
r\rightarrow\infty
. Remarkably, an exact equation for
n_{0}(r)
was given by Knessl and Keller
[
15
]
which subsequently refined the asymptotic estimation of
n(r)
by Odlyzko mentioned before. Later Almkvist
[
1
]
obtained an exact formula for
\Delta^{r}(p)(n)
. Recently, Gomez, Males, and Rolen
[
9
]
considered a generalized version of the difference operator
\Delta
and defined
j
-shifted difference
by
\Delta_{j}(a)(n):=a(n)-a(n-j)
(note that, for
j=1
, we retrieve the classical
\Delta
operator) and obtained an asymptotic expansion (up to first 4 terms) of
\Delta^{r}(p)(n)
along with an explicit estimation of the error term (cf.
[
9
, Theorem 1.1]
) and consequently proved that
\Delta^{2}_{j}(p)(n)>0
for all
n>16j^{2}+1/24
(cf.
[
9
, Theorem 1.2]
). A combinatorial proof of
\Delta^{2}_{j}p(n)>0
is given independently by Gomez, Males, and Rolen
[
9
]
and Banerjee
[
4
]
. Banerjee
[
2
, Section 7.2]
studied the asymptotic expansion of
\Delta^{r}_{j}(p)(n)
but without any estimation of the error bound. Regarding the logarithmic variation of finite differences of the partition function, denoted by
(-1)^{r-1}\Delta^{r}(\log p)(n)
, the positivity phenomena similar to the difference of partition function, first studied in detail by Chen, Wang, and Xie
[
5
, Theorem 3.1 and Theorem 4.1]
and settled a conjecture of DeSalvo and Pak
[
7
, Conjecture 4.3]
. Banerjee
[
3
, Theorem 4.9]
obtained an asymptotic expansion for
(-1)^{r-1}\Delta^{r}(\log p)(n)
.

In context of the overpartition function, Wang, Xie, and Zhang
[
25
]
studied the positivity of the higher order differences
\Delta^{r}(\overline{p})(n)
(cf.
[
25
, Theorems 2.1 and 2.3]
) and its logarithmic variation
(-1)^{r-1}\Delta^{r}(\log\overline{p})(n)
(cf.
[
25
, Theorem 3.1 and Theorem 4.1]
). The author
[
20
]
settled a problem of Wang, Xie, and Zhang
[
25
, Problem 3.4]
by proving an asymptotic lower bound of
(-1)^{r-1}\Delta^{r}(\log\overline{p})(n)
. A combinatorial proof of
\Delta^{2}_{j}(\overline{p})(n)>0
is given in
[
4
]
. The key motivation of this paper arises from the following problem of Wang, Xie, and Zhang
[
25
, Problem 2.4]
. Before we state the problem, let us state the result on
\Delta^{r}(\overline{p})(n)
by Wang, Xie, and Zhang.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
25
, Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 2.3]

For
r\geq 1
, there exists a positive integer
n(r)
such that for all
n\geq n(r)
,

0<\Delta^{r}\left(\overline{p}\right)(n)<2^{r-3}\left(1-2^{-\frac{3}{2}}\right)\zeta\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n+r}}}{n+r},

(1.4)

where
\zeta
is the Riemann zeta function.

This result led Wang, Xie, and Zhang to propose the following problem.

Problem 1.2
.

[
25
, Problem 2.4]

Find a sharp lower bound of
\Delta^{r}(\overline{p})(n)
.

In other words, it is equivalent to find the asymptotic growth of
\Delta^{r}(\overline{p})(n)
as
n\rightarrow\infty
.

In attempting to give an affirmative answer to this question, we first derive an asymptotic expansion of
\overline{p}(n+k)
for any
k\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}
in Theorem
1.3
. Using Theorem
1.3
, we obtain the asymptotic expansion of the
r
-fold applications of the
j
-shifted differences of the overpartition function in Theorem
1.4
which in turn gives a sharper lower and upper bounds for
\Delta^{r}_{j}(\overline{p})(n-j)
.

Before we state the main results, for
(N,j,r)\in\mathbb{N}^{3}
and
k\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}
, let us define the following constants depending on parameters
N,j,r,k
which will be used in stating the main results.

\displaystyle N(m)

\displaystyle:=\begin{cases}9,&\quad\text{if}\ m=1,\\
10m\log m-m\log\log m,&\quad\text{if}\ m\geq 2,\end{cases}

(1.5)

\displaystyle N_{0}(m)

\displaystyle:=\left\lceil\Bigl(\frac{N(m)}{\pi}\Bigr)^{2}\right\rceil,

(1.6)

\displaystyle N_{1}(m,k)

\displaystyle:=\underset{k\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}}{\min}\{N_{0}(m)-k\},

(1.7)

\displaystyle n(N,k)

\displaystyle:=\begin{cases}\max\Bigl\{N_{0}(N+1),\ 4k,\ k^{2},\ \Bigl\lceil\frac{k^{2}\pi^{2}}{4}\Bigr\rceil\Bigr\},&\quad\text{if}\ k>0,\\
\max\Bigl\{N_{0}(N+1),\ 4|k|\Bigr\},&\quad\text{if}\ k<0,\end{cases}

(1.8)

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{1}(N,k)

\displaystyle:=\frac{2|k|^{\frac{N}{2}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{|k|})}{\pi^{3/2}}\Bigl({1+\sqrt{2(N+1)}}\Bigr),

(1.9)

\displaystyle\widetilde{E}_{2}(N,k)

\displaystyle:=\frac{2|k|^{\frac{N}{2}}\cosh(\pi\sqrt{|k|})\sqrt{2(N+2)}}{\pi^{3/2}},

(1.10)

\displaystyle\overline{E}_{1}(N,k)

\displaystyle:=\widetilde{E}_{1}(N,k)+\widetilde{E}_{2}(N,k),

(1.11)

\displaystyle\widehat{E}_{2}(N)

\displaystyle=\widehat{E}_{2}(N,k):=\begin{cases}\frac{1+\pi}{\pi^{N+1}},&\quad\text{if}\ k>0,\\
\frac{2^{\frac{N+3}{2}}}{\pi^{N+1}},&\quad\text{if}\ k<0,\end{cases}

(1.12)

\displaystyle E(N,k)

\displaystyle:=\overline{E}_{1}(N,k)+\widehat{E}_{2}(N),

(1.13)

\displaystyle n(N,r,j)

\displaystyle:=\underset{0\leq m\leq r}{\max}\{n(N,-(m+1)j)\}

(1.14)

\displaystyle E(N,r,j)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{m=0}^{r}\binom{r}{m}E\left(N,-(m+1)j\right).

(1.15)

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
n(N,k)
,
E(N,k)
be as in (
1.8
) and (
1.13
) respectively.. For
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
k\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}
, and
n\geq n(N,k)
, we have

\overline{p}(n+k)=\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}\Biggl(\sum_{t=0}^{N}\frac{A_{k}(t)}{n^{\frac{t}{2}}}+O_{\leq E(N,k)}\left(n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}\right)\Biggr),

(1.16)

where

A_{k}(t)=\Bigl(\frac{k}{2}\Bigr)^{t}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor\frac{t+1}{2}\rfloor}\frac{(-1)^{\ell}(t+1-\ell)}{(t+1-2\ell)!}\binom{t+1}{\ell}\frac{\pi^{t-2\ell}}{k^{\ell}}.

(1.17)

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
n(N,r,j)
and
E(N,r,j)
be as defined in (
1.14
) and (
1.15
) respectively. For all
j,r\in\mathbb{N}
,
N\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq r}
and
n\geq n(N,r,j)
we have

\Delta^{r}_{j}\left(\overline{p}\right)(n-j)=\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}\Biggl(\sum_{t=r}^{N}\frac{A_{j}(t,r)}{n^{\frac{t}{2}}}+O_{\leq E(N,r,j)}(n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}})\Biggr),

(1.18)

where

A_{j}(t,r)=\Bigl(-\frac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{t}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor\frac{t+1}{2}\rfloor}\binom{t+1}{\ell}\frac{j^{t-\ell}\pi^{t-2\ell}(t+1-\ell)}{(t+1-2\ell)!}\sum_{m=0}^{r}(-1)^{m}\binom{r}{m}(m+1)^{t-\ell}.

(1.19)

Remark 1.5
.

In particular, we see that

A_{j}(r,r)=\left(\frac{\pi j}{2}\right)^{r}.

(1.20)

From Theorem
1.4
, we have the following asymptotic growth of
\Delta^{r}_{j}(\overline{p})(n)
.

Corollary 1.6
.

We have

\Delta^{r}_{j}\left(\overline{p}\right)(n)\sim\left(\frac{\pi j}{2}\right)^{r}\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8\ n^{\frac{r}{2}+1}}\ \ \text{as}\ \ n\to\infty.

Setting
j=1
in Corollary
1.6
, we get the asymptotic major term of
\Delta^{r}(\overline{p})(n)
stated below.

Corollary 1.7
.

We have

\Delta^{r}\left(\overline{p}\right)(n)\sim\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)^{r}\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8\ n^{\frac{r}{2}+1}}\ \ \text{as}\ \ n\to\infty.

2.
Preliminary lemmas

Following the sketch done in
[
19
, Lemma 2.1.]
, we have the folowing lemma.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
N(m)
be as defined in (
1.5
). Then for any integer
m\geq 1
,

10x^{m}e^{-\frac{x}{2}}<1,\ \ \text{for}\ x\geq N(m).

For
m=1
, it is clear that
N(m)=9
. For
m\geq 2
, define
f(x):=\frac{x}{2}-m\log x-\log 10
. Now,
f^{{}^{\prime}}(x)=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{m}{x}>0
implies that
f(x)
is increasing for
x>2m
. In order to show
f(x)>0
for all
x\geq N(m)
, it is enough to show
f(x)>f(N(m))>0
for all
x\geq N(m)>2m
; i.e., we first show that (i)
N(m)>2m
and then we show (ii)
f(N(m))>0
. It is easy to verify that
N(m)>2m
, that is,
10m\log m-m\log\log m>2m
for
m\geq 2
. Next, in order to prove
f(N(m))>0
, it is equivalent to show that

5m\log m-\frac{m\log\log m}{2}-m\log(10m\log m-m\log\log m)-\log 10>0,

which in turn, amounts to prove

\log m>\frac{2m+1}{8m}\log 10+\frac{3}{8}\log\log m+\frac{1}{8}\log\Bigl(1-\frac{\log\log m}{10\log m}\Bigr).

Since
\frac{2m+1}{8m}>\frac{1}{4}
and
\log\left(1-\frac{\log\log m}{10\log m}\right)<0
for
m\geq 3
, we have
m>10^{\frac{1}{4}}\log m^{\frac{3}{8}}
which is equivalent to the inequality stated before.
∎

Set
\overline{\mu}(n):=\pi\sqrt{n}
.

.

Let
N_{0}(m)

). Then for all

,

(2.1)

From

, we have

\overline{p}(n)=\frac{e^{\overline{\mu}(n)}}{8n}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n)}+\tilde{T}(n)\Biggr)

with

\tilde{T}(n)=\Biggl(1+\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n)}\Biggr)e^{-2\overline{\mu}(n)}+\frac{8n}{e^{\overline{\mu}(n)}}R_{2}(n,2),

and
R_{2}(n,2)
is the error term of ((
1.2
)), given in ((

By

\mid\tilde{T}(n)\mid<10e^{-\frac{1}{2}\overline{\mu}(n)}.

Using Lemma
2.1
we get for all

and

; i.e, for

\overline{p}(n)=\frac{e^{\overline{\mu}(n)}}{8n}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n)}+O_{\leq 1}(\overline{\mu}(n)^{-m})\Biggr).

(2.2)

Thus applying (

To derive the coefficient sequence arising from the Taylor expansion of the asymptotic main term
\frac{e^{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}}{8(n+k)}\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}\Bigr)
after extracting out the factor
\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}
, we closely follow the methodology carried out in
[
23
, Proposition 4.4]
.

Lemma 2.3
.

For
k\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}
, we have

\frac{e^{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}}{8(n+k)}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}\Biggr)=\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}\sum_{t\geq 0}\frac{A_{k}(t)}{n^{\frac{t}{2}}},

with

A_{k}(t):=\left(\frac{\pi k}{2}\right)^{t}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor\frac{t+1}{2}\rfloor}\left(-\frac{1}{\pi^{2}k}\right)^{\ell}\frac{(t-\ell+1)}{(t+1-2\ell)!}\binom{t+1}{\ell}.

We first estimate
\frac{e^{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}}{8(n+k)}\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}\Bigr)
up to some truncation point say
N\in\mathbb{N}
.

Setting
z:=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}
and
c:=\pi
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{e^{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}}{8(n+k)}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{\overline{\mu}(n+k)}\Biggr)

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}\Biggl(\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}\Bigl(\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{n}}-1\Bigr)}}{(1+\frac{k}{n})}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{\pi\sqrt{n}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{n}}}\Biggr)\Biggr)

(2.3)

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}\Biggl[e^{\pi\sqrt{n}\Bigl(\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{n}}-1\Bigr)}\Biggl(\frac{1}{1+\frac{k}{n}}-\frac{1}{\pi\sqrt{n}(1+\frac{k}{n})^{3/2}}\Biggr)\Biggr]

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}}{8n}\Biggl[\exp\Bigl(\frac{c}{z}(\sqrt{1+kz^{2}}-1)\Bigr)\Biggl(\frac{1}{1+kz^{2}}-\frac{1}{\frac{c}{z}(1+kz^{2})^{3/2}}\Biggr)\Biggr].

Let
\alpha:=c\sqrt{n}
,
x:=kz^{2}=\frac{k}{n}
. Considering the left hand side of (
2.3
) as a formal power series in
x
, we get

\exp(\alpha(\sqrt{x+1}-1))\Biggl(\frac{1}{1+x}-\frac{1}{\alpha(1+x)^{3/2}}\Biggr)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\xi_{j}(\alpha)x^{j}.

(2.4)

Integrating (
2.4
) twice with respect to
x
yields

\xi_{j}(\alpha)=(j+1)(j+2)[x^{j+2}]\frac{4}{\alpha^{2}}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\frac{\alpha^{\ell}}{\ell!}(\sqrt{1+x}-1)^{\ell}.

Let
y:=\sqrt{1+x}
and
\mathcal{B}_{-1}(x)
be the generalized binomial series (see
[
11
, page 203]
). Then for all
m\in\mathbb{C}
with
m\notin\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
, it follows that

\Bigl(\frac{1+y}{2}\Bigr)^{m}=\Bigl(\frac{1+\sqrt{1+x}}{2}\Bigr)^{m}={\mathcal{B}_{-1}}\Bigl(\frac{x}{4}\Bigr)^{m}=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{m}{m-j}\binom{m-j}{j}\frac{x^{j}}{4^{j}}.

(2.5)

Therefore,

\displaystyle\xi_{j}(\alpha)

\displaystyle=(j+1)(j+2)\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\Bigl(\frac{\alpha}{2}\Bigr)^{\ell-2}\frac{1}{\ell!}[x^{j+2-\ell}]\Bigl(\frac{1+y}{2}\Bigr)^{-\ell}

\displaystyle=(j+1)(j+2)\sum_{\ell=0}^{j+1}\Bigl(\frac{\alpha}{2}\Bigr)^{j-\ell}\frac{1}{(j+2-\ell)!}[x^{\ell}]\Bigl(\frac{1+y}{2}\Bigr)^{-(j+2-\ell)}

\displaystyle=(j+1)\sum_{\ell=0}^{j+1}\Bigl(\frac{\alpha}{2}\Bigr)^{j-\ell}\frac{1}{(j+2-\ell)!}\frac{j+2-\ell}{j+2}\binom{-j-2}{\ell}\frac{1}{4^{\ell}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=0}^{j+1}\frac{(j+1)\alpha^{j-\ell}(-1)^{\ell}}{2^{j+\ell}(j+\ell+1)!}\binom{j+\ell+1}{\ell},

where in the second step, we have used (
2.5
) along with the fact that

\Bigl(\frac{1+y}{2}\Bigr)^{-(j+2-\ell)}=0\ \ \text{for}\ \ \ell\geq{j+2}\ \ \text{as}\ \ \binom{m-j-1}{j-1}=0\ \text{for }\ \left\lfloor\frac{m}{2}\right\rfloor+1\leq j\leq m-1.

Since, in (
2.4
),
\xi_{j}(\alpha)x^{j}
contains the term
\alpha^{j-\ell}x^{j}={\pi}^{j-\ell}k^{j}n^{-\frac{j+\ell}{2}}
, thus we have

\displaystyle A_{k}(t)

\displaystyle=\underset{\ell\leq j+1}{\sum_{j+\ell=t}}\frac{(-1)^{\ell}(j+1){\pi}^{j-\ell}k^{j}}{2^{j+\ell}(j+1-\ell)!}\binom{j+\ell+1}{\ell}=\sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor\frac{t+1}{2}\rfloor}\frac{(-1)^{\ell}(t-\ell+1)\pi^{t-2\ell}k^{t-\ell}}{2^{t}(t+1-2\ell)!}\binom{t+1}{\ell},

as
j+\ell=t
and
\ell\leq j+1
, we have
t-j\leq j+1\Leftrightarrow j\geq\frac{t-1}{2}
which implies
\ell\leq t-j\leq\frac{t+1}{2}
and this finally concludes the proof.
∎

\displaystyle A_{k}(2t)

\displaystyle=\Bigl(-\frac{k}{4}\Bigr)^{t}\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{t}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{2t+1}{t-\ell}(t+\ell+1)\frac{(\pi\sqrt{k})^{2\ell}}{(2\ell+1)!},

c(t,\ell):=\binom{2t+1}{t-\ell}(t+\ell+1)

\frac{c(t,\ell)}{c(t,0)}=\frac{\prod_{j=1}^{\ell}(t-j+1)}{\prod_{j=1}^{\ell}(t+j)}\leq 1,

which implies that
c(t,\ell)\leq c(t,0)
for all
0\leq\ell\leq t
. Consequently, using (
3.1
) and the fact that
\binom{2t}{t}\leq\frac{4^{t}}{\sqrt{\pi t}}
, we have

\displaystyle\left|A_{k}(2t)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\pi\sqrt{k}}\Bigl(\frac{k}{4}\Bigr)^{t}\binom{2t+1}{t}(t+1)\left|\sum_{\ell=0}^{t}\frac{(-1)^{\ell}(\pi\sqrt{k})^{2\ell+1}}{(2\ell+1)!}\right|\leq\frac{k^{t-1/2}(2t+1)}{\pi^{3/2}\sqrt{t}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{k}),

and so

\displaystyle\sum_{t\geq\frac{N+1}{2}}\frac{\left|A_{k}(2t)\right|}{n^{t}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{k^{-\frac{1}{2}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}\sum_{t\geq\frac{N+1}{2}}\frac{2t+1}{\sqrt{t}}\frac{k^{t}}{n^{t}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{k^{\frac{N}{2}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}\sum_{t\geq 0}\Biggl(2\sqrt{\frac{N+1}{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{2t}{N+1}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t+\frac{N+1}{2}}}\Biggr)\Bigl(\frac{k}{n}\Bigr)^{t}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{k^{\frac{N}{2}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}\sum_{t\geq 0}\Biggl(2\sqrt{\frac{N+1}{2}}\sqrt{1+t}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t+1}}\Biggr)\Bigl(\frac{k}{n}\Bigr)^{t}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{k^{\frac{N}{2}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}\Bigl({1+\sqrt{2(N+1)}}\Bigr)\sum_{t\geq 0}\Bigl(\frac{2k}{n}\Bigr)^{t}

\displaystyle\leq\Biggl(\frac{2k^{\frac{N}{2}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}\Bigl({1+\sqrt{2(N+1)}}\Bigr)\Biggr)n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}=\widetilde{E}_{1}(N,k)n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}},

(3.3)

using the fact
\sqrt{t+1}\leq 2^{t}
for
t>0
in the fourth step and
n\geq n(N,k)\geq 4k
in the second last step and (
1.9
) in the final step.

Similarly, we estimate

\left|A_{k}(2t+1)\right|\leq\frac{k^{t}(t+1)}{4^{t}2\pi}\binom{2t+2}{t+1}\cosh(\pi\sqrt{k})\leq\frac{2k^{t}\sqrt{t+1}}{\pi^{3/2}}\cosh(\pi\sqrt{k}),

and consequently using (
3.2
), (
1.10
), and
n\geq n(N,k)\geq 4k
, it follows that

\displaystyle\sum_{t\geq\frac{N}{2}}\frac{\left|A_{k}(2t+1)\right|}{n^{t+\frac{1}{2}}}

\displaystyle\leq n^{-\frac{1}{2}}\frac{2\cosh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}\sum_{t\geq\frac{N}{2}}\sqrt{t+1}\frac{k^{t}}{n^{t}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2k^{N/2}\cosh(\pi\sqrt{k})}{\pi^{3/2}}n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}\sum_{t\geq 0}\frac{k^{t}}{n^{t}}\sqrt{t+\frac{N}{2}+1}\leq\widetilde{E}_{2}(N,k)n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}.

(3.4)

Combining (
3.3
) and (
3.4
), we get

\displaystyle\left|\sum_{t\geq N+1}\frac{A_{k}(t)}{n^{\frac{t}{2}}}\right|=\left|\sum_{t\geq\frac{N+1}{2}}\frac{A_{k}(2t)}{n^{t}}+\sum_{t\geq\frac{N}{2}}\frac{A_{k}(2t+1)}{n^{t+\frac{1}{2}}}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{t\geq\frac{N+1}{2}}\frac{\left|A_{k}(2t)\right|}{n^{t}}+\sum_{t\geq\frac{N}{2}}\frac{\left|A_{k}(2t+1)\right|}{n^{t+\frac{1}{2}}}

\displaystyle=\overline{E}_{1}(N,k)n^{-\frac{N+1}{2}}.

(3.5)

This concludes the proof of for the case
k>0
. Next, we proceed to the case where
k<0
.

Let
k=-m
,

; i.e,

. Then we have

A_{-m}(t)=\frac{(-m)^{t}}{2^{t}}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\left\lfloor\frac{t+1}{2}\right\rfloor}\frac{(t+1-\ell)}{(t+1-2\ell)!}\binom{t+1}{\ell}\frac{\pi^{t-2\ell}}{m^{\ell}}.

In a similar way like in the case

, here we have the following bounds

\left|A_{-m}(2t+1)\right|\leq\frac{2m^{t}\sqrt{t+1}}{\pi^{3/2}}\cosh(\pi\sqrt{m}),\ \left|A_{-m}(2t)\right|\leq\frac{m^{t-\frac{1}{2}}(2t+1)}{\pi^{3/2}\sqrt{t}}\sinh(\pi\sqrt{m}),

which consequently implies that for

Analogous to the estimation done in (

which concludes the proof.
∎

4.
Conclusion

We conclude this paper by raising the following questions with a brief background on each of the problem.

(1)

Following up the work of Odlyzko

Problem 4.1
.

What is the asymptotic growth of

as
r\rightarrow\infty
such that for all

as in Almkvist, Knessl and Keller’s work
[
1
,
15
]
for the overpartition function?

(2)

Let

denotes the plane partition function introduced by MacMahon
[
18
]
, and the generating function is given by

Recently, thanks to the seminal work of Ono, Pujahari and Rolen

Problem 4.2
.

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, what is the asymptotic growth of
\Delta^{r}_{j}(\textnormal{PL})(n)
as

?
