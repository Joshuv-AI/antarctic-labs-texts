38 U.S.C. § 8510 — Finality of decisions
Citation: us:usc:38:8510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8510
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 85 (Disposition Of Deceased Veterans’ Personal Property); Subchapter I (Property Left On Department Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Decisions by the Secretary under this subchapter shall not be reviewable administratively by any other officer of the United States.
