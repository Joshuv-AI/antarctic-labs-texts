49 C.F.R. § 1110.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1110.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1110.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1110 (Procedures Governing Informal Rulemaking Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains general rulemaking procedures that apply to the issuance, amendment, and repeal of rules, general policy statement, or other interpretation of rules or law of the Surface Transportation Board, adopted under the procedures of section 553 of title 5 of the United States Code (the Administrative Procedure Act).
