15 C.F.R. § 905.3 — Access to information.
Citation: us:cfr:15:905.3
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-905.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 905 (Use In Enforcement Proceedings Of Information Collected By Voluntary Fishery Data Collectors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Information collected by a voluntary fishery data collector:
(a) Is subject to disclosure to both the Secretary and the public, to the extent required or authorized by law; and
(b) Is subject to discovery by any party to an enforcement proceeding, to the extent required or authorized by law.
