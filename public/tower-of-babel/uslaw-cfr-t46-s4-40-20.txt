46 C.F.R. § 4.40-20 — -20 Cause or probable cause determinations from Board investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:4.40-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-4.40-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 4 (Marine Casualties And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After an investigation conducted by the Board under § 4.40-15, the Board determines cause or probable cause and issues a report of that determination.
