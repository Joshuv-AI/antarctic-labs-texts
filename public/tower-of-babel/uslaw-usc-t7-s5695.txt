7 U.S.C. § 5695 — Authorization of appropriations

Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter V (Foreign Agricultural Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.
