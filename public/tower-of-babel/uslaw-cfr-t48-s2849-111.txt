48 C.F.R. § 2849.111 — 2849.111 Review of proposed settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2849.111
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2849.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2849 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA or designee may establish procedures for the review and approval of settlement agreements at a level above the contracting officer. In addition, all proposed termination settlements shall be reviewed by legal counsel.
