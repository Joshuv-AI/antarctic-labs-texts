26 U.S.C. § 2601 — Tax imposed
Citation: us:usc:26:2601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2601
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 13 (Tax On Generation-Skipping Transfers); Subchapter A (Tax Imposed). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A tax is hereby imposed on every generation-skipping transfer (within the meaning of subchapter B).
