44 U.S.C. § 1118 — Documents beyond scope of ordinary departmental business
Citation: us:usc:44:1118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1118
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 11 (Executive And Judiciary Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A book or document not having to do with the ordinary business transactions of the executive departments may not be printed on the requisition of a department unless expressly authorized by Congress.
