44 U.S.C. § 1717 — Documents and reports for foreign legations
Citation: us:usc:44:1717
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1717
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 17 (Distribution And Sale Of Public Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Documents and reports may be furnished to foreign legations to the United States upon request stating those desired and requisition upon the Director of the Government Publishing Office by the Secretary of State. Gratuitous distribution may only be made to legations whose Governments furnish to legations from the United States copies of their printed and legislative documents desired.
