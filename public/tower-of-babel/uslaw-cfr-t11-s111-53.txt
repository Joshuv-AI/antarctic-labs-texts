11 C.F.R. § 111.53 — Litigation by the Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:11:111.53
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-111.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 111 (COMPLIANCE PROCEDURE (52 U.S.C. 30109, 30107(a))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart C precludes the Commission from filing suit in the appropriate court to enforce compliance with a conciliation agreement under 52 U.S.C. 30109(a)(5)(D), seek a civil money penalty under 52 U.S.C. 30109(a)(6), petition the court for a contempt order under 52 U.S.C. 30109(a)(11), or otherwise exercise its authority to enforce or administer the statutes specified in 11 CFR 111.51(a).
