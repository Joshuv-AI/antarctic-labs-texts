31 C.F.R. § 598.509 — Payment of legal fees and expenses at public expense.
Citation: us:cfr:31:598.509
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-598.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 598 (Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

U.S. persons that are attorneys, law firms, or legal services organizations are authorized to receive payment of professional fees and reimbursement of incurred expenses from public funds for the provision of legal services authorized by § 598.507(a).
