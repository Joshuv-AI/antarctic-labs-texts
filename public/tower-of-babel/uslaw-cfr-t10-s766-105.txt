10 C.F.R. § 766.105 — Payment procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:10:766.105
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-766.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 766 (Uranium Enrichment Decontamination And Decommissioning Fund; Procedures For Special Assessment Of Domestic Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE shall specify payment details and instructions in all Special Assessment invoices. Each domestic utility shall make payments to the Fund by wire transfer to the Department of Treasury.
