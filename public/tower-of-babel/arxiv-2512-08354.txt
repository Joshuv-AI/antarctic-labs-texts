Expansion formula of one-loop Einstein-Yang-Mills integrand
Yi-Jian Du, Chongsi Xie

Abstract
Building upon the algebraic consistency construction of one-loop Bern-Carrasco-Johansson (BCJ) numerators for Yang-Mills (YM) and Yang-Mills-scalar (YMS) theories, we explore the expansion formula of one-loop Einstein-Yang-Mills (EYM) integrands (with a gluon loop) in terms of conventional one-loop YM integrands with quadratic propagators. We first express the EYM integrand by tree-level amplitudes according to the forward limit approach. Employing a two-step expansion strategy, the gluon-loop EYM integrand is decomposed into tree-level YM amplitudes under the forward limit, which are subsequently expanded into tree-level bi-adjoint scalar (BS) ones. We then prove that when the kinematic coefficients in both expansion steps satisfy the one-loop consistency conditions, the EYM integrand is finally expanded as a combination of YM integrands with quadratic propagators. The coefficients in this expansion formula coincide exactly with those in the expansion formula for YMS integrands. This correspondence highlights a shared kinematic structure, providing the proper foundation for constructing BCJ numerators in both YMS and EYM theories at one loop.

Introduction

Colour-kinematic duality
Bern:2008qj
;
Bern:2010ue
demonstrates that colour-dressed Yang-Mills (YM) amplitudes can be arranged as sums over trivalent graphs, each associated with a colour factor and a kinematic factor that shares the same algebraic identity as its colour counterpart. When each colour factor in a YM amplitude is replaced by another copy of the kinematic factor, the amplitude then turns into a gravitational (GR) amplitude.
This duality provides a deep insight into the connection between YM and GR.
How to construct the kinematic numerators (BCJ numerators) satisfying the algebraic identities is the main problem of the colour-kinematic duality.

5
Conclusions

In this work, we provided a concrete proof of the statement that the gluon-loop single-trace EYM integrands at one-loop level (with quadratic propagators) satisfy expansion relations, by which an EYM integrand is expanded in terms of quadratic propagator expressed one-loop YM integrands. The expansion coefficients in this formula have the same form as those in the corresponding expansion formula for scalar-loop YMS integrands. This proof was based on the forward limit approach of the EYM integrand as well as the consistency conditions of one-loop expansion coefficients. Since the existence of consistent coefficients (for YM and YMS) has been proven in
Du:2025yxz
, any expansion formula of YMS integrands with consistent coefficients implies the corresponding EYM expansion formula. Hence, the explicit formula for YMS integrands
Du:2025yxz
with up to three gluons precisely results in the EYM expansion formula with up to three gravitons (the coefficients are displayed in appendix
C
). This work may provide more hints for understanding the BCJ duality at one-loop level.
