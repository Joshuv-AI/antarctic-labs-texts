10 U.S.C. § 4975 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:10:4975
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4975
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart I (Defense Industrial Base); Chapter 389 (Loan Guarantee Programs); Subchapter I (Defense Export Loan Guarantees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1)
The terms “defense article”, “defense services”, and “design and construction services” have the meanings given those terms in section 47 of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2794).
(2)
The term “cost”, with respect to a loan guarantee, has the meaning given that term in section 502 of the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control Act of 1974 (2 U.S.C. 661a).
