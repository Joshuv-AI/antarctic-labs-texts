33 C.F.R. § 173.79 — Expiration of Coast Guard certificate of number.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.79
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificate of number issued by the Coast Guard expires 3 years from the date it is issued.
