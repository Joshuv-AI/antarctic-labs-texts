44 C.F.R. § 206.402 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:44:206.402
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-206.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 206 (Federal Disaster Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient of disaster assistance under the Stafford Act must document for the Regional Administrator its compliance with this subpart following the completion of any repair or construction activities.
