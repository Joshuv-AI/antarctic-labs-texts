10 U.S.C. § 8749a — Civil service mariners of Military Sealift Command: alcohol testing
Citation: us:usc:10:8749a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8749a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 871 (Civilian Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may prescribe regulations establishing a program to conduct on-duty reasonable suspicion alcohol testing and post-accident alcohol testing of civil service mariners of the Military Sealift Command who are assigned to vessels.
