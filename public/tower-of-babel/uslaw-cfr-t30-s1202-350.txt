30 C.F.R. § 1202.350 — Scope and definitions.

Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1202 (Royalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart is applicable to all geothermal resources produced from Federal geothermal leases issued pursuant to the Geothermal Steam Act of 1970, as amended (30 U.S.C. 1001 et seq.).
(b) The definitions in § 1206.351 are applicable to this subpart.
