48 C.F.R. § 52.247-62 — 52.247-62 Specific Quantities Unknown.
Citation: us:cfr:48:52.247-62
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-52.247-62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 52 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 47.305-16(d)(2), insert the following clause in solicitations and contracts when total requirements and destinations to which shipments will be made are known, but the specific quantity to be shipped to each destination cannot be predetermined. This clause protects the interests of both the Government and the contractor during the course of the performance of the contract.
