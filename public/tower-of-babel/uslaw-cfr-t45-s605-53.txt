45 C.F.R. § 605.53 — Drug and alcohol addicts.
Citation: us:cfr:45:605.53
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-605.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 605 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient to which this subpart applies that operates a general hospital or outpatient facility may not discriminate in admission or treatment against a drug or alcohol abuser or alcoholic who is suffering from a medical condition, because of the person's drug or alcohol abuse or alcoholism.
