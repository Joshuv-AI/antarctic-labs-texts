2 U.S.C. § 1853 — Exemption of officers and employees of Architect of Capitol from certain Federal pay provisions
Citation: us:usc:2:1853
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1853
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter III (Personnel); Part B (Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The classes of employees whose compensation is authorized by section 3 of the Legislative Pay Act of 1929, as amended (46 Stat. 38; 55 Stat. 615), to be fixed by the Architect of the Capitol without regard to the Classification Act of 1923, as amended, are authorized to be compensated without regard to chapter 51 and subchapter III of chapter 53 of title 5.
