36 C.F.R. § 296.11 — Appeals relating to permits.
Citation: us:cfr:36:296.11
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-296.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 296 (Protection Of Archaeological Resources: Uniform Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any affected person may appeal permit issuance, denial of permit issuance, suspension, revocation, and terms and conditions of a permit through existing administrative appeal procedures, or through procedures which may be established by the Federal land manager pursuant to section 10(b) of the Act and this part.
