48 C.F.R. § 1425.1001 — 1425.1001 Waiver of right to examination of records.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1425.1001
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1425 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, PAM is authorized to make the determinations prescribed in FAR 25.1001(b). Determinations shall be prepared by the CO and submitted through the HCA to the Director, PAM.
