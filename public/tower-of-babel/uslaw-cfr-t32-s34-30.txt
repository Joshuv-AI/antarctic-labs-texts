32 C.F.R. § 34.30 — Purpose of procurement standards.
Citation: us:cfr:32:34.30
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-34.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 34 (Administrative Requirements For Grants And Agreements With For-Profit Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 34.31 sets forth requirements necessary to ensure:
(a) Compliance of recipients' procurements that use Federal funds with applicable Federal statutes and executive orders.
(b) Proper stewardship of Federal funds used in recipients' procurements.
