33 C.F.R. § 165.101 — Kittery, Maine—regulated navigation area.
Citation: us:cfr:33:165.101
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-165.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 165 (Regulated Navigation Areas And Limited Access Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following is a regulated navigation area—Waters within the boundaries of a line beginning at 43°04′50″ N, 70°44′52″ W; then to 43°04′52″ N, 70°44′53″ W; then to 43°04′59″ N, 70°44′46″ W; then to 43°05′05″ N, 70°44′32″ W; then to 43°05′03″ N, 70°44′30″ W; then to the beginning point.
(b) Regulations. No vessel may operate in this area at a speed in excess of five miles per hour.
