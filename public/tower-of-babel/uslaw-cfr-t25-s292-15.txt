25 C.F.R. § 292.15 — May a tribe apply for a Secretarial Determination for lands not yet held in trust?
Citation: us:cfr:25:292.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-292.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 292 (Gaming On Trust Lands Acquired After October 17, 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A tribe can apply for a Secretarial Determination under § 292.13 for land not yet held in trust at the same time that it applies under part 151 of this chapter to have the land taken into trust.
