43 C.F.R. § 2370.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2370.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2370.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2370 (Restorations And Revocations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this part 2370 apply to lands and interests in lands withdrawn or reserved from the public domain, except lands reserved or dedicated for national forest or national park purposes, which are no longer needed by the agency for which the lands are withdrawn or reserved.
