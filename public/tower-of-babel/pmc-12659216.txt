In this article [1], Fig. 1 appeared incorrectly and have now been corrected in the original publication. For completeness and transparency, the incorrect and correct versions of Fig. 1 are displayed below.

Cell-type specific expression of IGF system genes in normal and COVID-19 human lungs and in normal mouse lungs obtained from scRNA-seq studies. Dot plots show expression patterns of IGF system genes across annotated lung cell types and conditions in: A control and lethal COVID-19 human lungs (GSE171524; Melms et al., 2021 [26]), and B young adult mouse lungs (GSE109774; Tabula Muris Consortium, 2018 [27]). Dot size indicates the per-centage of cells with detected expression per cell type on each group; dot color represents the gene-wise scaled mean expression among expressing cells. Only subgroups with detected gene expression in ≥ 1% of cells are included. See Supplementary methods in Additional File 1 for more details on data filtering and visualization
