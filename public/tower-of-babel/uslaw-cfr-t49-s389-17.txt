49 C.F.R. § 389.17 — Participation by interested persons.
Citation: us:cfr:49:389.17
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-389.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 389 (Rulemaking Procedures—Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any interested person may participate in rule making proceedings by submitting comments in writing containing information, views, or arguments.
(b) In his/her discretion, the Administrator may invite any interested person to participate in the rule making procedures described in § 389.25.
