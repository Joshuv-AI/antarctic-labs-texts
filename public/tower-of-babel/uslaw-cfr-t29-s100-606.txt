29 C.F.R. § 100.606 — No private rights created.
Citation: us:cfr:29:100.606
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-100.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 100 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The failure of the NLRB to include in this part any provision of the Federal Collections Claim Standards (FCCS), 31 CFR chapter IX parts 900-904, does not prevent the NLRB from applying these provisions.
(b) A debtor may not use the failure of the NLRB to comply with any provision of this part or of the FCCS as a defense.
