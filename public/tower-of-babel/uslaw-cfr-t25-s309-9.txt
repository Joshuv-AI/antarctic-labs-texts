25 C.F.R. § 309.9 — When can non-Indians make and sell products in the style of Indian arts and crafts?
Citation: us:cfr:25:309.9
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-309.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 309 (Protection Of Indian Arts And Crafts Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A non-Indian can make and sell products in the style of Indian art or craft products only if the non-Indian or other seller does not falsely suggest to consumers that the products have been made by an Indian.
