33 C.F.R. § 158.520 — Following the instruction manual.
Citation: us:cfr:33:158.520
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-158.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 158 (Reception Facilities For Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, And Garbage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The person in charge shall ensure that the instruction manual under § 158.330(b) is followed during the transfer of any NLS.
