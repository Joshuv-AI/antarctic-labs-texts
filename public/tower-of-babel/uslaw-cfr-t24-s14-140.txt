24 C.F.R. § 14.140 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:24:14.140
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-14.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 14 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over another agency of the United States that participates in a proceeding before the Department and takes a position that is not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
