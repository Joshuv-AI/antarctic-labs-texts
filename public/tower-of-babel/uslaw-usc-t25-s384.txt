25 U.S.C. § 384 — Employment of superintendents of irrigation
Citation: us:usc:25:384
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section384
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 11 (Irrigation Of Allotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of Indian Affairs, under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior, may employ superintendents of irrigation who shall be skilled irrigation engineers, not to exceed seven in number.
