22 C.F.R. § 1508.980 — Participant.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1508.980
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1508.980
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1508 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participant means any person who submits a proposal for or who enters into a covered transaction, including an agent or representative of a participant.
