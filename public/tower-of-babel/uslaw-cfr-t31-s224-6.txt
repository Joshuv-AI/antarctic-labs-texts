31 C.F.R. § 224.6 — Where can I find a sample power of attorney form?
Citation: us:cfr:31:224.6
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-224.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 224 (Federal Process Agents Of Surety Corporations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Surety Bond Branch provides a sample form on its Web page located at: http://www.fiscal.treasury.gov/c570. While use of the sample form is not required, any power of attorney provided should be substantially the same as the sample form.
