5 C.F.R. § 847.415 — OASDI coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.415
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who elects CSRS coverage under § 847.411 is prospectively subject to both the Old Age, Survivors, and Disability Insurance (OASDI) tax and CSRS as described in subpart J of part 831 of this chapter, known as CSRS Offset, effective from the first day of the next pay period after the employing agency receives the employee's election under § 847.411(a).
