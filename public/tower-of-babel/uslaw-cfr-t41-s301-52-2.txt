41 C.F.R. § 301-52.2 — -52.2 Travel claim filing format.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-52.2
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-52.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-52 (52—Claiming Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must use the format prescribed by ETS to file travel claims, unless the agency has been granted, or has granted the employee, an exception from required use of the ETS in accordance with § 301-50.2 of this subchapter.
