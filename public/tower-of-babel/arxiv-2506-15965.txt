Smooth hybrid inflation in light of ACT DR6 data Preprint: EPHOU-25-011
Nobuchika Okada, Osamu Seto

Abstract
Smooth hybrid inflation is a hybrid inflation model which is free from topological defects and predicts the density perturbation with the spectral index of about 0.97 0.97 . We show that the prediction on the spectral index is robust regardless the power of nonrenormalizable terms and meets with the latest results reported by Atacama Cosmology Telescope. Viable scenarios for baryogenesis and dark matter are also discussed.

Introduction

The inflationary cosmology has been the standard paradigm of modern cosmology.
It solves various problems in the standard big bang

The scalar perturbation is generated from the inflaton’s quantum fluctuation

The scalar spectral index has been reported as

Recently,

II
Smooth hybrid inflation

The superpotential for a smooth hybrid inflation is given by

W=S\left(-\mu^{2}+\frac{(\bar{\Psi}\Psi)^{m}}{M^{2m-2}}\right),

(1)

where this model is based on certain gauge group
G
under which
S
is a singlet,
and
\Psi
and
\bar{\Psi}
are a pair of a vector-like representation.
We may consider
U(1)_{B-L}
as an example.
While
m=2
is assumed in the original paper
Lazarides:1995vr
, we generalize
that
m
is an integer (
\geq 2
) as in Refs.
Yamaguchi:2004tn
;
Kawasaki:2006zv
.
With the minimal Kahler potential, the scalar potential in supergravity in the Planck unit is

\displaystyle V=

\displaystyle e^{|S|^{2}+|\Psi|^{2}+|\bar{\Psi}|^{2}}\left[\left|-\mu^{2}+\frac{(\bar{\Psi}\Psi)^{m}}{M^{2m-2}}\right|^{2}(1-|S|^{2}+|S|^{4})\right.

\displaystyle+|S|^{2}\left|m\bar{\Psi}\frac{(\bar{\Psi}\Psi)^{m-1}}{M^{2m-2}}+\Psi^{*}\left(-\mu^{2}+\frac{(\bar{\Psi}\Psi)^{m}}{M^{2m-2}}\right)\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\left.+|S|^{2}\left|m\Psi\frac{(\bar{\Psi}\Psi)^{m-1}}{M^{2m-2}}+\bar{\Psi}^{*}\left(-\mu^{2}+\frac{(\bar{\Psi}\Psi)^{m}}{M^{2m-2}}\right)\right|^{2}\right]+D{\rm-terms}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\left(-\mu^{2}+\frac{\psi^{2m}}{M^{2m-2}}\right)^{2}\left(1+\frac{|S|^{4}}{2}+2\psi^{2}\right)+2|S|^{2}\psi^{2}\left(m\frac{\psi^{2m-2}}{M^{2m-2}}-\mu^{2}\right)^{2}.

(2)

Here, in the last expression,
the D-flat condition (
\Psi=\bar{\Psi}^{*}\equiv\psi
) are imposed, and
the third terms in the F-term of
\Psi
and
\bar{\Psi}
are neglected compared to the first terms,
and the lowest order supergravity corrections for
S
and
\psi
only are kept in the F-term of
S
,
since we are interested in the field region
|S|^{2},\psi^{2}\ll 1
.
The supersymmetric global minimum is located at

\left(S,\psi\right)=\left(0,\mu^{\frac{1}{m}}M^{\frac{m-1}{m}}\right),

(3)

where the mass of inflatons is

m_{S}^{2}=m_{\psi}^{2}\simeq 2m^{2}\mu^{2}\left(\frac{\mu}{M}\right)^{\frac{2(m-1)}{m}}.

(4)

The stationary condition for
\psi
is

V,_{\psi}\simeq 4\psi\left(-\mu^{2}+m\frac{\psi^{2m-2}}{M^{2m-2}}\right)\left(-\mu^{2}+\frac{\psi^{2m}}{M^{2m-2}}+m(2m-1)|S|^{2}\frac{\psi^{2m-2}}{M^{2m-2}}\right),

(5)

and we find a nontrivial local minimum

|S|^{2}\left(\frac{\psi}{M}\right)^{2m-2}=\frac{\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)},

(6)

for a large
|S|
as

|S|^{2}>\frac{\psi^{2}}{m(2m-1)}.

(7)

In this region, inflationary expansion can be realized due to the false vacuum energy
V\simeq\mu^{4}
.
For this minimum for
\psi
, we can see

V_{,\psi\psi}\simeq\frac{8\mu^{2}}{(2m-1)|S|^{2}}\left[(m-1)\mu^{2}+mM^{2}\left(\frac{\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)|S|^{2}}\right)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\right]\gg\mu^{4}.

(8)

for
|S|\ll 1
.
Hence, we consider that the
\psi
field can trace the instantaneous minimum
Eq. (
6
).
By substituting Eq. (
6
) into Eq. (
2
),
we obtain the potential for only
the canonical inflaton
\sigma=|S|/\sqrt{2}
as

V\simeq\mu^{4}\left(1-\frac{2M^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\frac{m-1}{2m-1}\left(\frac{2\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)}\right)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\sigma^{-\frac{2m}{m-1}}+\frac{\sigma^{4}}{8}\right).

(9)

Yamaguchi and Yokoyama verified this procedure
Yamaguchi:2005qm
.
The final term in the potential comes from a supergravity effect.
The slow roll parameters are defined by

\displaystyle\epsilon:=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{V_{,\sigma}}{V}\right)^{2},

(10)

\displaystyle\eta:=

\displaystyle\frac{V_{,\sigma\sigma}}{V},

(11)

where the derivatives of the potential are given as

\displaystyle\frac{V_{,\sigma}}{V}\simeq\frac{2M^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\frac{2m}{2m-1}\left(\frac{2\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)}\right)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\sigma^{-\frac{2m}{m-1}-1}+\frac{\sigma^{3}}{2},

(12)

\displaystyle\frac{V_{,\sigma\sigma}}{V}\simeq-\frac{2M^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\frac{2m}{2m-1}\frac{3m-1}{m-1}\left(\frac{2\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)}\right)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}\sigma^{-\frac{2m}{m-1}-2}+\frac{3}{2}\sigma^{2}.

(13)

We define the transition point
\sigma_{t}
in the potential
from Eq. (
12
) as

\sigma_{t}^{\frac{2m}{m-1}+4}=\frac{4M^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\frac{2m}{2m-1}\left(\frac{2\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)}\right)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}.

(14)

While the potential slope is dominantly given
by the
\sigma^{-\frac{2m}{m-1}}
term for
\sigma<\sigma_{t}
,
the supergravity effect is significant for
\sigma>\sigma_{t}
.
The critical point where the slow roll condition breaks down
as
\eta(\sigma_{c})=-1
is given by

\sigma_{c}^{\frac{2m}{m-1}+2}=\frac{2M^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\frac{2m}{2m-1}\frac{3m-1}{m-1}\left(\frac{2\mu^{2}}{m(2m-1)}\right)^{\frac{m}{m-1}}.

(15)

The number of e-folds during inflation is expressed as

N=\frac{3m-1}{4m-2}\left(\frac{\sigma}{\sigma_{c}}\right)^{2\frac{2m-1}{m-1}},

(16)

as long as

\sigma(N)<\sigma_{t}

(17)

is satisfied.
This constrains the parameters of the model
as shown in Fig.
1
where each contours are of
\sigma(N)/\sigma_{t}

for
N=60
and the gray shaded region is not compatible with the condition (
17
).
If the condition (
17

and the resultant
n_{s}
exceeds
1

Panagiotakopoulos:1997qd
;
Linde:1997sj

hence not compatible with observation.

The amplitude of the density perturbation is estimated as

{\cal P}_{\zeta}=\frac{\mu^{4}}{12\pi^{2}}\left(\frac{m-1}{3m-1}\sigma_{c}\right)^{-2}\left(\frac{4m-2}{3m-1}N\right)^{\frac{3m-1}{2m-1}},

(18)

and the spectral index is expressed as

n_{s}=1-\frac{3m-1}{2m-1}\frac{1}{N}.

(19)

One can immediately realize that, for
m\geq 2
, the spectral index is more red tilted than that
of the standard supersymmetric hybrid inflation with a logarithmic correction
in the potential.
The tensor to scalar ratio is given by

r=16\epsilon,

(20)

with

\displaystyle\epsilon=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{m-1}{3m-1}\left(\frac{\sigma_{c}}{\sigma}\right)^{\frac{3m-1}{m-1}}\sigma_{c}\right)^{2}.

(21)

In addition, the mass of the inflaton in the vacuum is given by

m_{\sigma}^{2}=2M^{2}\left(\frac{\mu}{M}\right)^{2/m}\left[-\mu^{2}+m\left(\frac{\mu}{M}\right)^{2-2/m}\right]^{2}.

(22)

II.3
Post-inflationary evolution

After inflation, the inflaton fields oscillate around the minimum (
3
).
The inflaton decay may be caused through the superpotential
W=ySH_{u}H_{d}

where
y
is a coupling constant,
and
H_{u}
and
H_{d}
are superfields of the up-type and down-type Higgs
doublet fields, respectively.
The reheating temperature after inflation
T_{R}
is controlled by the magnitude of
y
.
If
\Psi
and
\bar{\Psi}
are charged under
U(1)_{B-L}
,

\Psi
or
\bar{\Psi}
can couple with right-handed neutrino superfield
N
, for instance,
as

W=y_{N}\Psi NN.

(26)

If
T_{R}
is large enough, the baryon asymmetry could be generated by thermal leptogenesis
Fukugita:1986hr
;
Davidson:2008bu
.
On the other hand, if
T_{R}
is not so high,
the baryon asymmetry can be generated by Affleck-Dine mechanism
Affleck:1984fy
.
If the superpotential (
26
) exists,
nonthermal leptogenesis by the inflaton decay is also possible
Asaka:1999yd
.
In fact, Jeannerot et al. studied nonthermal leptogenesis in
the original smooth hybrid inflation
Jeannerot:2001qu
.
As is suggested by the Higgs boson mass of
125
GeV,
soft SUSY breaking mass parameters seem to be
large
Okada:1990vk
;
Okada:1990gg
;
Ellis:1990nz
;
Haber:1990aw
;
Ellis:1991zd
.
Thus, the promising candidate of dark matter in this scenario would be
Higgsino with the mass of about
1
TeV left due to thermal freeze out
Cirelli:2007xd
.

III
Conclusion

We revisited smooth hybrid inflation model whose
spectral index of the density perturbation is about
0.97
,
which well agrees with the latest results reported by ACT.
By treating the power of non-renormalizable terms as a free parameter,
we have found that the predicted
n_{s}
lies
within the uncertainty of the latest ACT results for any value of its power
m
.
The scenario seems to be compatible with various baryogenesis mechanism
and Higgsino-like neutralino dark matter.
