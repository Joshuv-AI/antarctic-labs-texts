5 C.F.R. § 630.1115 — Limitations on the use of donated annual leave.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.1115
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.1115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Donated annual leave transferred to a leave recipient under this subpart may not be—
(a) Included in a lump-sum payment under 5 U.S.C. 5551 or 5552;
(b) Recredited to a former employee who is reemployed by a Federal agency; or
(c) Used to establish initial eligibility for immediate retirement or acquire eligibility to continue health benefits into retirement under 5 U.S.C. 6302(g).
