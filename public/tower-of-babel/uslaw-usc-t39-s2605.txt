39 U.S.C. § 2605 — Suits to recover wrongful or fraudulent payments
Citation: us:usc:39:2605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section2605
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part III (Modernization And Fiscal Administration); Chapter 26 (Debts And Collection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service shall request the Attorney General to bring a suit to recover with interest any payment made from moneys of, or credit granted by, the Postal Service as a result of—
(1)
mistake;
(2)
fraudulent representations;
(3)
collusion; or
(4)
misconduct of an officer or employee of the Postal Service.
