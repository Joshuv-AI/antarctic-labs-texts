12 U.S.C. § 1749aaa–3 — Regulations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter IX–B (Mortgage Insurance For Group Practice Facilities And Medical Practice Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter, after consulting with the Secretary of Health and Human Services with respect to any health or medical aspects of the program under this subchapter which may be involved in such regulations.
