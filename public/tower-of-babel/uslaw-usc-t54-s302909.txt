54 U.S.C. § 302909 — Prohibited use of grant amounts
Citation: us:usc:54:302909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section302909
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3029 (Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No part of any grant made under this subdivision shall be used to compensate any person intervening in any proceeding under this division.
