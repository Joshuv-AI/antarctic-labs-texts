20 C.F.R. § 364.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 364 (Use Of Penalty Mail To Assist In The Location And Recovery Of Missing Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations, which implement 39 U.S.C. 3220, provide the standards and guidelines for the use of Board penalty mail in the location and recovery of missing children.
