33 C.F.R. § 151.1024 — Display of number.
Citation: us:cfr:33:151.1024
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-151.1024
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 151 (Vessels Carrying Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, Garbage, Municipal Or Commercial Waste, And Ballast Water). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The owner or operator of each vessel under this subpart must ensure that the vessel number stated on the conditional permit issued under § 151.1015 is displayed so that it—
(1) Is clearly legible;
(2) Has a contrasting background;
(3) Is readily visible from either side of the vessel; and
(4) Is in block figures that are at least 18 inches in height.
(b) No person may tamper with or falsify a number required under this section.
