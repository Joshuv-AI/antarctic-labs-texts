47 C.F.R. § 1.10013 — How do I check the status of my application after I file it?
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.10013
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.10013
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You can check the status of your application through the “Search Tools” on the ICFS homepage. The ICFS homepage is located at https://www.fcc.gov/icfs.
