45 C.F.R. § 2516.840 — By what standards will the Corporation evaluate individual Learn and Serve America programs?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.840
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.840
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corporation will evaluate programs based on the following: (a) The extent to which the program meets the objectives established and agreed to by the grantee and the Corporation before the grant award.
(b) The extent to which the program is cost-effective.
(c) Other criteria as determined and published by the Corporation.
