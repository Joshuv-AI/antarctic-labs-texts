22 U.S.C. § 9906 — Improving awareness of United States Government tools and services to support United States businesses overseas
Citation: us:usc:22:9906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9906
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 106 (Championing American Business Through Diplomacy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State and the Secretary of Commerce shall take actions to improve the awareness of United States businesses with respect to United States Government tools and services to assist such businesses overseas, especially small and medium-sized enterprises, including by coordinating with State trade agencies, Export Assistance Centers, and Small Businesses Development Centers.
