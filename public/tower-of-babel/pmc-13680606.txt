Author Correction: SESAME-catalyzed H3T11 phosphorylation inhibits Dot1-catalyzed H3K79me3 to regulate autophagy and telomere silencing
Authors: Fei He, Qi Yu, Min Wang, Rongsha Wang, Xuanyunjing Gong, Feng Ge, Xilan Yu, Shanshan Li
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-35182-9, published online 6 December 2022

In the version of this article initially published, in Fig. 4b and associated Source Data, data for ATG7, ATG15 and ATG23 were incorrect; in Fig. 6l and associated Source Data, data for the 16K-Replicate 3 were inadvertent duplicates of 25K-Replicate 3 data; in Supplementary Fig. 8c and associated Source Data, data for UBA2 and FAR3 from Replicate 3 were inadvertently duplicated into Replicate 2. For comparison, the original figures are available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment. The figures and Source Data are now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
