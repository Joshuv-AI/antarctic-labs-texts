6 U.S.C. § 644 — Assistance to non-Federal entities
Citation: us:usc:6:644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section644
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter XVII (Anti-Trafficking Training For Department Of Homeland Security Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide training curricula to any State, local, or tribal government or private organization to assist the government or organization in establishing a program of training to identify human trafficking, upon request from the government or organization.
