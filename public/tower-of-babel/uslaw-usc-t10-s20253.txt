10 U.S.C. § 20253 — Retirement: retirement for years of service
Citation: us:usc:10:20253
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20253
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2005 (Officers); Subchapter IV (Failure Of Selection For Promotion And Involuntary Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 633 through 636 of this title shall apply to the retirement of officers on the Space Force officer list in the same manner as to officers of the Regular Air Force.
