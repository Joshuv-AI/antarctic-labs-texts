43 C.F.R. § 402.1 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:43:402.1
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-402.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 402 (Sale Of Lands In Federal Reclamation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to the sale of certain classes of lands that are subject to the reclamation laws and that may be sold under one of the following statutes:
(a) The Act of May 20, 1920 (41 Stat. 605; 43 U.S.C. 375);
(b) The Act of May 16, 1930 (46 Stat. 367; 43 U.S.C. 424 through 424e); or
(c) The Act of March 31, 1950 (64 Stat. 39; 43 U.S.C. Sup. 375b through 375f).
