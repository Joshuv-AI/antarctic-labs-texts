22 U.S.C. § 9226 — Travel sanctions
Citation: us:usc:22:9226
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9226
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 99 (North Korea Sanctions And Policy Enhancement); Subchapter II (Sanctions Against North Korean Proliferation, Human Rights Abuses, And Illicit Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State may deny a visa to, and the Secretary of Homeland Security may deny entry into the United States of, any alien who is—
(1)
a designated person;
(2)
a corporate officer of a designated person; or
(3)
a principal shareholder with a controlling interest in a designated person.
