15 U.S.C. § 1826 — Notice of violations to Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:15:1826
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1826
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 44 (Protection Of Horses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary believes that a willful violation of this chapter has occurred and that prosecution is needed to obtain compliance with this chapter, he shall inform the Attorney General and the Attorney General shall take such action with respect to such matter as he deems appropriate.
