15 U.S.C. § 208 — Variations

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter III (Standard Gauge For Iron And Steel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the practical use and application of the standard gauge esablished 1 in section 206 of this title a variation of 2½ percent, either way may be allowed.
