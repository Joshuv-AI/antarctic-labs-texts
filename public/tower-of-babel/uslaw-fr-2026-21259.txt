FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE:  October 27, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. EST.

PLACE:  This meeting will be held at the Federal Maritime Commission 
(Commission), 800 North Capitol St. NW, 1st Floor Hearing Room, 
Washington, DC 20573. The meeting will also be streamed live through a 
link that will be published on the Commission's website, www.fmc.gov.

STATUS:  Parts of this meeting will be open to the public. The rest of 
the meeting will be closed to the public. Visitors will be subject to 
security checks. If technical issues prevent the Commission from 
streaming live, the Commission will post a recording of the public 
portion of the meeting on the Commission's YouTube channel following 
the meeting.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED: 

Portions Open to the Public

1. Staff Briefing from the Bureau of Enforcement, Investigations, and 
Compliance
2. Staff Briefing from the Office of Consumer Affairs and Dispute 
Resolution Services
3. Staff briefing from the Office of the General Counsel

Portions Closed to the Public

1. Staff Briefing from the Office of Information Technology
2. Staff Briefing from the Office of Consumer Affairs and Dispute 
Resolution Services
3. Staff briefing from the Office of the General Counsel
