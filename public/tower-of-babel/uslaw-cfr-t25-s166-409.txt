25 C.F.R. § 166.409 — How is my grazing rental payment determined?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.409
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grazing rental payment is the total of the grazing rental rate multiplied by the number of AUMs or acres covered by the permit.
