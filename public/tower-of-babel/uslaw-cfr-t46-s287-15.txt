46 C.F.R. § 287.15 — Time for making deposits.
Citation: us:cfr:46:287.15
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-287.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 287 (Establishment Of Construction Reserve Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Proceeds of sale or indemnification. Deposits of amounts representing proceeds of the sale or indemnification for loss of a vessel or share therein must be made within 60 days after receipt by the taxpayer.
(b) Earnings and receipts. Earnings and receipts for the taxable year may be deposited at any time. (See § 287.14.)
