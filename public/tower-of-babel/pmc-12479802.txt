Trends in the Prevalence of Diabetes, 2013–2022
Published: 2024

The prevalence of diabetes among Korean adults aged ≥19 years was 11.2% in men and 6.9% in women in 2022 (Figure 1). The prevalence of diabetes increased with age in both sexes. The prevalence in men was higher than that in women before the age of 70 (Figure 2).
