25 C.F.R. § 581.3 — How do I file a motion to intervene in appeals?
Citation: us:cfr:25:581.3
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-581.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 581 (Motions In Appeal Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Motions to intervene in appeals before a presiding official are governed by § 584.5 of this subchapter. Motions to intervene in appeals before the Commission are governed by § 585.5 of this subchapter.
