28 U.S.C. § 1585 — Powers in law and equity
Citation: us:usc:28:1585
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1585
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 95 (Court Of International Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Court of International Trade shall possess all the powers in law and equity of, or as conferred by statute upon, a district court of the United States.
