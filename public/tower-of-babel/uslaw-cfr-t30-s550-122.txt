30 C.F.R. § 550.122 — What effect does subsurface storage have on the lease term?
Citation: us:cfr:30:550.122
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-550.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 550 (Oil And Gas And Sulfur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you use a lease area for subsurface storage of gas, it does not affect the continuance or expiration of the lease.
