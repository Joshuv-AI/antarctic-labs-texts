18 U.S.C. § 16 — Crime of violence defined
Citation: us:usc:18:16
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section16
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “crime of violence” means—
(a)
an offense that has as an element the use, attempted use, or threatened use of physical force against the person or prop­erty of another, or
(b)
any other offense that is a felony and that, by its nature, involves a substantial risk that physical force against the person or property of another may be used in the course of committing the offense.
