(x|a_{2})^{(\overline{n})}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{F}(n,k)(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\underline{k})},

(18)

where
(x|a_{2})^{(\overline{n})}=\prod_{i=0}^{i=n-1}(x+ia_{2})
and
(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\underline{k})}=\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}(x-a_{0}-a_{2}-ia_{1}).

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{E}_{n}
be the set of paths enumerating the
\big({F}(n,k)\big)_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
of length
n

\pi\in\mathcal{E}_{n},
define
v(\pi)=(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\overline{h(\pi)})}
,
h(\pi)
being the height of
\pi

|\mathcal{E}_{n}|_{v}=\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal{E}_{n}}(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\underline{h(\pi)})}=\sum_{k\geq 0}\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal{E}_{n,k}}(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\underline{k})}=\sum_{k\geq 0}{F}(n,k)(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\underline{k})}.

Considering the two ways of going from
n-1

n

\displaystyle|\mathcal{E}_{n}|_{v}=

\displaystyle\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal{E}_{n}}(x-a_{0}-a_{2}|a_{1})^{(\underline{h(\pi)})}=\sum_{u\in\mathcal{E}_{n-1}}m\big(x-a_{0}-a_{2}-a_{1}(h(u)+1)\big)v(u)+\big(a_{2}n+a_{1}(h(u)+1)+a_{0}\big)v(u)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(x+a_{2}(n-1))|\mathcal{E}_{n-1}|_{v}.

Noting that
|\mathcal{E}_{0}|_{w}=1

∎
