39 C.F.R. § 3040.150 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3040.150
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3040.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3040 (Product Lists And The Mail Classification Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Users of the mail, by filing a request with the Commission, may propose a modification to the market dominant product list or the competitive product list. For purposes of this part, modification shall be defined as adding a product to a list, removing a product from a list, or transferring a product from one list to the other list.
