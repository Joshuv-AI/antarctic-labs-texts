32 C.F.R. § 310.11 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:32:310.11
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-310.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 310 (Protection Of Privacy And Access To And Amendement Of Individual Records Under The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the Privacy Act.
