48 U.S.C. § 1421c — Certain laws continued in force; modification or repeal of laws
Citation: us:usc:48:1421c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1421c
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The laws of Guam in force on August 1, 1950, except as amended by this chapter, are continued in force, subject to modification or repeal by the Congress of the United States or the Legislature of Guam, and all laws of Guam inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter are repealed to the extent of such inconsistency.
