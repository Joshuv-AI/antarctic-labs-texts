47 C.F.R. § 42.2 — Designation of a supervisory official.
Citation: us:cfr:47:42.2
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-42.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 42 (Preservation Of Records Of Communication Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each carrier subject to the regulations in this part shall designate one or more officials to supervise the preservation of its records.
