14 C.F.R. § 404.17 — Additional rulemaking proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:14:404.17
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-404.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 404 (Petition And Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAA may initiate other rulemaking proceedings, if necessary or desirable. For example, it may invite interested people to present oral arguments, participate in conferences, appear at informal hearings, or participate in any other proceedings.
