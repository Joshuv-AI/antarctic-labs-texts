42 U.S.C. § 9167 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:42:9167
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9167
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 99 (Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or any application thereof is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of the chapter, or any other application, shall not be affected thereby.
