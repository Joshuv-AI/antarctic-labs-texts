10 U.S.C. § 1221 — Effective date of retirement or placement of name on temporary disability retired list
Citation: us:usc:10:1221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1221
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 61 (Retirement Or Separation For Physical Disability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 8301 of title 5, the Secretary concerned may specify an effective date for the retirement of any member of the armed forces under this chapter, or for the placement of his name on the temporary disability retired list, that is earlier than the date provided for in that section.
