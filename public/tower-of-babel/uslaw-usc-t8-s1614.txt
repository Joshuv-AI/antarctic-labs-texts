8 U.S.C. § 1614 — Notification and information reporting
Citation: us:usc:8:1614
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1614
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 14 (Restricting Welfare And Public Benefits For Aliens); Subchapter I (Eligibility For Federal Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Federal agency that administers a program to which section 1611, 1612, or 1613 of this title applies shall, directly or through the States, post information and provide general notification to the public and to program recipients of the changes regarding eligibility for any such program pursuant to this subchapter.
