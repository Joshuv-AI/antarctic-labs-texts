45 C.F.R. § 153.540 — Compliance with risk corridors standards.
Citation: us:cfr:45:153.540
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-153.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 153 (Standards Related To Reinsurance, Risk Corridors, And Hhs Risk Adjustment Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HHS or its designee may audit a QHP issuer to assess its compliance with the requirements of this subpart. HHS will conduct an audit in accordance with the procedures set forth in § 158.402(a) through (e) of this subchapter.
