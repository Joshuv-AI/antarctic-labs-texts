7 C.F.R. § 1430.222 — Death, incompetency, or disappearance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1430.222
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1430.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1430 (Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of death, incompetency, disappearance or dissolution of a producer that is eligible to receive benefits under this part, such persons as are specified in part 707 of this title may receive such benefits, as determined appropriate by FSA.
