42 U.S.C. § 5515 — Use of publicly assisted housing by Secretary in demonstrations
Citation: us:usc:42:5515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5515
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 71 (Solar Energy); Subchapter I (Heating And Cooling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall make appropriate use of publicly assisted housing and particularly low-rent housing assisted under the United States Housing Act of 1937 [42 U.S.C. 1437 et seq.] in demonstrating solar heating systems and combined solar heating and cooling systems under this subchapter.
