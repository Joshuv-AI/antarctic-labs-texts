37 C.F.R. § 1.1024 — The description.
Citation: us:cfr:37:1.1024
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-1.1024
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 1 (Rules Of Practice In Patent Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An international design application designating the United States must include a specification as prescribed by 35 U.S.C. 112 and preferably include a brief description of the reproduction pursuant to Rule 7(5)(a) describing the view or views of the reproductions.
