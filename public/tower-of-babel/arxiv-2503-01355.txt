Mean curvature flow for principal orbits of Hermann actions on rank two symmetric spaces
Naoyuki Koike, Sakura Nakaoka

Abstract
In this paper, we illustrate the behaviour of the mean curvature flows starting from principal orbits of any commuting Hermann action of cohomogeneity two on irreducible rank two Riemannian symmetric spaces of compact type by using Mathematicae. In more detail, we illustrate the velocity vector fields of the curves (determined by the flows) on the orbit space (which is a 2-simplex) of the Hermann action by using Mathematcae. Also, we calculate the position of the point of the orbit space corresponding to the only minimal principal orbit of the Hermann action by using Mathematicae.

As a generalized notion of compact isoparametric hypersurfaces in a sphere and a hyperbolic space,

According to results in [C], [HPTT] and [Kol], all equifocal submanifolds of cohomogeneity greater than one

\lx@sectionsign 3.
The minimal orbits of Hermann actions of cohomogeneity two

In this section, we use the notations in Subsection 1.2 and Section 2.
Let
H\curvearrowright G/K
be a commuting Hermann action of cohomogeneity two on an irreducible rank two Riemannian symmetric
space of compact type. It is known that the only minimal principal
H
-orbit
H(\exp_{eK}(Z_{0}))
(
Z_{0}\in C^{\prime}
) and
the only minimal singular
H
-orbit
H(\exp_{eK}(Z^{\sigma}))
(
Z^{\sigma}\in\sigma
) through the edge
\exp_{eK}(\sigma)

for each edge
\sigma
of
\partial C^{\prime}
, where the “minimality” means that the orbit is a minimal submanifold in
G/K
.
Let
Z_{0}=x_{1}^{0}\bm{e}_{1}+x_{2}^{0}\bm{e}_{2}
and
Z^{\sigma}=x_{1}^{\sigma}\bm{e}_{1}+x_{2}^{\sigma}\bm{e}_{2}
, where
(\bm{e}_{1},\bm{e}_{2})
is an orthonormal basis
of
\mathfrak{b}
as stated in Section 2.
It is clear that
\bm{X}_{Z_{0}}={\bf 0}
and
(\bm{X}^{\sigma})_{Z^{\sigma}}={\bf 0}
holds. Hence, from the descriptions of
\bm{X}

in Table 2.3, we can calculate
(x^{0}_{1},x^{0}_{2})
by using Mathematicae. Also, we can describe
\bm{X}^{\sigma}
’s explicitly
by using the description
(2.2)
of
\bm{X}^{\sigma}
and, from the explicit descriptions of
\bm{X}^{\sigma}
, we can calculate

(x^{\sigma}_{1},x^{\sigma}_{2})
by using Mathematicae.

X_{x_{1}e_{1}+x_{2}e_{2}}
as
X_{(x_{1},x_{2})}
.

\rho_{1}(SO(3))\curvearrowright SU(3)/SO(3)
.

\bm{X}
in Table 2.3, we obtain
(x^{0}_{1},x^{0}_{2})=(\frac{\pi}{6},0)
.
According to Table 2.2, we have
\Delta_{+}=\{\underset{(1)}{\alpha},\underset{(1)}{\beta},\underset{(1)}{\alpha+\beta}\}

\mathfrak{a}_{2}
-type),

\sigma
