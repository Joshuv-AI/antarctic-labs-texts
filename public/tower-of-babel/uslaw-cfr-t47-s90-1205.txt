47 C.F.R. § 90.1205 — Permissible operations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.1205
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.1205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Unattended and continuous operation is permitted.
(b) Voice, data and video operations are permitted.
(c) Aeronautical mobile operations are prohibited.
