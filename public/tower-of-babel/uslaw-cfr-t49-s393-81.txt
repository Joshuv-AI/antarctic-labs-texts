49 C.F.R. § 393.81 — Horn.
Citation: us:cfr:49:393.81
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-393.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 393 (Parts And Accessories Necessary For Safe Operation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every bus, truck, truck-tractor, and every driven motor vehicle in driveaway-towaway operations shall be equipped with a horn and actuating elements which shall be in such condition as to give an adequate and reliable warning signal.
