40 C.F.R. § 713.21 — Electronic filing.
Citation: us:cfr:40:713.21
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-713.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 713 (Reporting Requirements For The Tsca Inventory Of Mercury Supply, Use, And Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must use the Mercury Electronic Reporting (MER) application to complete and submit required information as set forth in § 713.17. Submissions may only be made as set forth in this section.
(b) Submissions must be sent electronically to EPA via CDX.
(c) Access MER and instructions, as follows:
(1) By website. Access MER via the CDX homepage at https://cdx.epa.gov/ and follow the appropriate links.
(2) By phone or email. Contact the EPA TSCA Hotline at (202) 554-1404 or TSCA-Hotline@epa.gov.
