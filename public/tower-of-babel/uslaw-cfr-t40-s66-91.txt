40 C.F.R. § 66.91 — Applicability of supplemental rules.
Citation: us:cfr:40:66.91
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-66.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 66 (Assessment And Collection Of Noncompliance Penalties By Epa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Supplemental Rules in this subpart, in conjunction with the Consolidated Rules of Practice (40 CFR part 22) shall govern all hearings held under this part. To the extent that the provisions of these Supplemental Rules or any other provision of this part are inconsistent with the Consolidated Rules, the provisions of this part shall govern.
