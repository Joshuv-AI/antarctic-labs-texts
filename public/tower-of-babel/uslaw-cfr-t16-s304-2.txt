16 C.F.R. § 304.2 — General requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:16:304.2
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-304.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 304 (Rules And Regulations Under The Hobby Protection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Imitation political or numismatic items subject to the Act shall be marked in conformity with the requirements of the Act and the regulations promulgated thereunder. Any violation of these regulations shall constitute a violation of the Act and of the Federal Trade Commission Act.
