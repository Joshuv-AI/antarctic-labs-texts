46 U.S.C. § 30103 — Liability of master, mate, engineer, and pilot
Citation: us:usc:46:30103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30103
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 301 (General Liability Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person may bring a civil action against a master, mate, engineer, or pilot of a vessel, and recover damages, for personal injury or loss caused by the master’s, mate’s, engineer’s, or pilot’s—
(1)
negligence or willful misconduct; or
(2)
neglect or refusal to obey the laws governing the navigation of vessels.
