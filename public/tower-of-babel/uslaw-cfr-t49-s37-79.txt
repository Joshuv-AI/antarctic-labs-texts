49 C.F.R. § 37.79 — Purchase or lease of new rail vehicles by public entities operating rapid or light rail systems.
Citation: us:cfr:49:37.79
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-37.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 37 (Transportation Services For Individuals With Disabilities (Ada)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each public entity operating a rapid or light rail system making a solicitation after August 25, 1990, to purchase or lease a new rapid or light rail vehicle for use on the system shall ensure that the vehicle is readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities, including individuals who use wheelchairs.
