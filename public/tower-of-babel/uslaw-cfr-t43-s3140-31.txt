43 C.F.R. § 3140.31 — Conversion applications.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3140.31
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3140.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3140 (Leasing In Special Tar Sand Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A plan of operations to convert an existing oil and gas lease or valid claim based on a mineral location to a combined hydrocarbon lease must have been filed on or before November 15, 1983, or prior to the expiration of the oil and gas lease, whichever was earlier, except as provided in § 3140.12.
