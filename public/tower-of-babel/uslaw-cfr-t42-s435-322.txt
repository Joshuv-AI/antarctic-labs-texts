42 C.F.R. § 435.322 — Medically needy coverage of the blind in States that cover individuals receiving SSI.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.322
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.322
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the agency provides Medicaid to individuals receiving SSI and elects to cover the medically needy, it may provide Medicaid to blind individuals who meet—
(a) The requirements for blindness, as specified in §§ 435.530 and 435.531; and
(b) The income and resource requirements of subpart I of this part.
