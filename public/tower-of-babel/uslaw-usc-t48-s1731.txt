48 U.S.C. § 1731 — Delegate to House of Representatives from American Samoa
Citation: us:usc:48:1731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1731
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 16 (Delegates To Congress); Subchapter II (American Samoa). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Territory of American Samoa shall be represented in the United States Congress by a nonvoting Delegate to the House of Representatives, elected as hereinafter provided.
