7 C.F.R. § 1944.63 — Authorized use of grant funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1944.63
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1944.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1944 (Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grant funds may only be used to reimburse a packager for delivered packages. Payment will be made for each complete package received and accepted in accordance with exhibit C of this subpart.
