46 C.F.R. § 35.25-15 — -15 Carrying of excess steam—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:35.25-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-35.25-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 35 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the duty of the Chief Engineer of any tank vessel to see that a steam pressure is not carried in excess of that allowed by the certificate of inspection, and to see that the safety valves, once set by the inspector, are in no way tampered with or made inoperative.
