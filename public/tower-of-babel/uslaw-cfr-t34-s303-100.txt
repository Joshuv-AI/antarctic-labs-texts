34 C.F.R. § 303.100 — General authority.
Citation: us:cfr:34:303.100
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-303.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 303 (Early Intervention Program For Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, in accordance with part C of the Act, makes grants to States (from their allotments under section 643 of the Act) to assist each State to maintain and implement a statewide, comprehensive, coordinated, multidisciplinary, interagency system to provide early intervention services for infants and toddlers with disabilities and their families.
