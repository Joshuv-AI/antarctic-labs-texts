VIII
Conclusions

In this paper we have calculated Jones polynomials
V(H_{r(m)},t)
for
a family of alternating knots and links with arbitrarily many
crossings
r(m)=4m+2
,
m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{+}
, by computing the Tutte
polynomials
T(G_{+}(H_{r(m)}),x,y)
for the associated graphs

G_{+}(H_{r(m)})=S_{m}
. Our results further elucidate how, with this
method, one can calculate Jones polynomials for an infinite family of
this type in a systematic manner, circumventing the generic
property that the calculation of the Jones polynomial of knots and
links becomes intractably difficult for sufficiently many
crossings. We have studied the zeros of
V(H_{r(m)},t)
in the complex

t
plane and the accumulation set
{\cal B}(H_{\infty})
. These results
involve a fruitful confluence of knot theory, graph theory, and
complex analysis.
