25 C.F.R. § 173.6 — Stock grazing.
Citation: us:cfr:25:173.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-173.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 173 (Concessions, Permits And Leases On Lands Withdrawn Or Acquired In Connection With Indian Irrigation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Permittees may graze upon lands covered by such permits, such stock as may be required in connection with the purposes for which the permit is issued subject to such restrictions and limitations as may be prescribed by the project engineer.
