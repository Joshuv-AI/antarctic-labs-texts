7 C.F.R. § 1435.317 — Revisions of allocations and proportionate shares.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1435.317
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1435.317
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1435 (Sugar Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Vice President, CCC, may modify any processor's allocation or any producer's proportionate share on the same basis as the initial allocation or proportionate share was required to be established.
