29 C.F.R. § 520.508 — What records, in addition to those required by Part 516 of this chapter and section 520.203 of this part, must I keep when student-learners are employed?
Citation: us:cfr:29:520.508
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-520.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 520 (Employment Under Special Certificate Of Messengers, Learners (Including Student-Learners), And Apprentices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any worker employed as a student-learner shall be identified as such on the payroll records, with each student-learner's occupation and rate of pay being shown. Notations should be made in the employer's records when additional hours are worked by reason of school not being in session.
