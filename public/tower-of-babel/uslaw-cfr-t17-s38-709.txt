17 C.F.R. § 38.709 — Final decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.709
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each designated contract market must establish rules setting forth when a decision rendered pursuant to this section will become the final decision of such designated contract market.
