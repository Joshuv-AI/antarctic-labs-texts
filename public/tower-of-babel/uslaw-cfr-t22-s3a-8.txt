22 C.F.R. § 3a.8 — a.8 Change in status.
Citation: us:cfr:22:3a.8
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-3a.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 3a (PART 3a—ACCEPTANCE OF EMPLOYMENT FROM FOREIGN GOVERNMENTS BY MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that an applicant's foreign government employment approved under this part is to be materially changed, either by a substantial change in duties from those described in the request upon which the original approval was based, or by a change of employer, the applicant must obtain further approval in accordance with this part for such changed employment.
