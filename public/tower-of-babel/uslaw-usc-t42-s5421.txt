42 U.S.C. § 5421 — Prohibition on waiver of rights
Citation: us:usc:42:5421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5421
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 70 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rights afforded manufactured home purchasers under this chapter may not be waived, and any provision of a contract or agreement entered into after August 22, 1974, to the contrary shall be void.
