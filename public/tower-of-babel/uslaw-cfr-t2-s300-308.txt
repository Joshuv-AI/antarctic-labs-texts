2 C.F.R. § 300.308 — Revision of budget and program plans.

In addition to the requirements of 2 CFR 200.308(f) a recipient or subrecipient must also request approval from the Federal agency or pass-through entity for the inclusion of research patient care costs in research awards made for the performance of research work.
