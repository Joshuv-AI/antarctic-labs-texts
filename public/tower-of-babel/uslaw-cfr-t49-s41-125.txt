49 C.F.R. § 41.125 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:49:41.125
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-41.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 41 (Seismic Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part is intended to create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law by a party against the DOT, its Operating Administrations, its officers, or any person.
