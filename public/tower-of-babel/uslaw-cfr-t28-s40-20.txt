28 C.F.R. § 40.20 — Contemplated change in certified procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:28:40.20
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-40.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 40 (Standards For Inmate Grievance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proposed change in a certified procedure must be submitted to the Attorney General thirty days in advance of its proposed effective date. The Attorney General shall review such proposed change and notify the applicant in writing before the effective date of the proposed change if such change will result in suspension or withdrawal of the certification of the grievance procedure.
