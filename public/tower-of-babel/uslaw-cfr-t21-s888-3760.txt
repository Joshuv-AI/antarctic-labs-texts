21 C.F.R. § 888.3760 — Wrist joint carpal scaphoid polymer prosthesis.
Citation: us:cfr:21:888.3760
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-888.3760
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 888 (Orthopedic Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A wrist joint carpal scaphoid polymer prosthesis is a one-piece device made of silicone elastomer intended to be implanted to replace the carpal scaphoid bone of the wrist.
(b) Classification. Class II.
