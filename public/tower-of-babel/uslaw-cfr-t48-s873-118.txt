48 C.F.R. § 873.118 — 873.118 Debriefings.
Citation: us:cfr:48:873.118
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-873.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 873 (Simplified Procedures For Health-Care Resources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Offerors whose proposals are not accepted under a competitive request for proposals (RFP) may submit a written request for a debriefing to the contracting officer. Without regard to FAR 15.505, preaward debriefings may be conducted by the contracting officer when determined to be in the best interest of the Government. Post-award debriefings shall be conducted in accordance with FAR 15.506. (38 U.S.C. 8153)
