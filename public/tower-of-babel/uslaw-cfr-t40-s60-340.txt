40 C.F.R. § 60.340 — Applicability and designation of affected facility.
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.340
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart are applicable to each rotary lime kiln used in the manufacture of lime.
(b) The provisions of this subpart are not applicable to facilities used in the manufacture of lime at kraft pulp mills.
(c) Any facility under paragraph (a) of this section that commences construction or modification after May 3, 1977, is subject to the requirements of this subpart.
