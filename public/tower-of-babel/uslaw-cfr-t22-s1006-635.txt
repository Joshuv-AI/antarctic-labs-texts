22 C.F.R. § 1006.635 — May the Inter-American Foundation settle a debarment or suspension action?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1006.635
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1006.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1006 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, we may settle a debarment or suspension action at any time if it is in the best interest of the Federal Government.
