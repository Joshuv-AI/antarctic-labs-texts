7 C.F.R. § 28.37 — Exposing of samples for classification.
Citation: us:cfr:7:28.37
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-28.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 28 (Cotton Classing, Testing, And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Classification shall not proceed until the samples, after being delivered to the Program, shall have been exposed for such length of time as in the judgment of the Area Director or Quality Assurance Director shall be sufficient to put them in proper condition for the purpose.
