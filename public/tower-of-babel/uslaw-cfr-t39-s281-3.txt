39 C.F.R. § 281.3 — Postal inspector responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:39:281.3
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-281.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 281 (Firm Mailings Damaged Or Destroyed Through Transportation Accidents Or Catastrophes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The postal inspector investigating the incident should assure that the processing postal officials are fulfilling their notification responsibilities on a timely basis. Should the situation arise where no postal officials are involved in processing affected mail, then the investigating postal inspector will take necessary action to insure that appropriate notification is made.
