40 C.F.R. § 417.110 — Applicability; description of the SO3 solvent and vacuum sulfonation subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:417.110
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-417.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 417 (Soap And Detergent Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the operations in which undiluted SO3 and organic reactant are fed through a mixing nozzle into a vacuum reactor where the sulfonation of the organic reactant takes place.
