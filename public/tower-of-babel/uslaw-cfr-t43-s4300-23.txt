43 C.F.R. § 4300.23 — After I file my application, can I use the land before BLM issues my permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.23
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. You cannot use the land until BLM issues you a permit. Generally, BLM will issue a permit within 120 days after receiving an application and will keep you informed if there are delays in meeting that timeframe.
