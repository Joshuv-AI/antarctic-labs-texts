31 U.S.C. § 3305 — Audits of depositaries
Citation: us:usc:31:3305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3305
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 33 (Depositing, Keeping, And Paying Money); Subchapter I (Deposits And Depositaries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury, or an officer, employee, or agent designated by the Secretary, may audit a depositary of public money. For uniformity and accuracy in accounts and safety of public money, an individual conducting an audit shall audit a depositary’s—
(1)
books;
(2)
accounts;
(3)
returns; and
(4)
public money on hand and the way the money is kept.
