41 C.F.R. § 105-70.043 — -70.043 Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-70.043
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-70.043
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-70 (70—Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 3806 and 3808(b) of title 31, United States Code, authorize action for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
