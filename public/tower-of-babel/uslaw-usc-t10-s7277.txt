10 U.S.C. § 7277 — Medal of honor; distinguished-service cross; distinguished-service medal; silver star: replacement
Citation: us:usc:10:7277
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7277
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 737 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any medal of honor, distinguished-service cross, distinguished-service medal, or silver star, or any bar, ribbon, rosette, or other device issued for wear with or in place of any of them, that is stolen, lost, or destroyed, or becomes unfit for use, without fault or neglect of the person to whom it was awarded, shall be replaced without charge.
