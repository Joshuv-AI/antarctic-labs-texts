29 C.F.R. § 4221.11 — Waiver of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4221.11
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4221.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4221 (Arbitration Of Disputes In Multiemployer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party that fails to object in writing in a timely manner to any deviation from any provision of this part is deemed to have waived the right to interpose that objection thereafter.
