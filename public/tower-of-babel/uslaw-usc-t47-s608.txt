47 U.S.C. § 608 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:47:608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section608
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
