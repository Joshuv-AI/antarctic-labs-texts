14 C.F.R. § 11.23 — Does FAA follow the same procedures in issuing all types of rules?
Citation: us:cfr:14:11.23
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-11.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 11 (General Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, in general, FAA follows the same procedures for all rule types. There are some differences as to which FAA official has authority to issue each type, and where you send petitions for FAA to adopt, amend, or repeal each type. Assume that the procedures in this subpart apply to all rules, except where we specify otherwise.
