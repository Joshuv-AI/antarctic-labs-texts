Privacy Act of 1974; Matching Program

AGENCY: Office of the Secretary, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of a new matching program.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974, as amended, and 
Office of Management and Budget guidance on computer matching, the U.S. 
Department of the Interior (DOI) is providing notice of the 
establishment of a new matching program. The matching program will 
enable the 20 DOI programs listed in the appendix to compare records 
maintained in applicable DOI systems of records, including INTERIOR/
DOI-85, Payroll, Attendance, Retirement, and Leave Records; INTERIOR/
DOI-86, Accounts Receivable: FBMS; INTERIOR/DOI-87, Acquisition of 
Goods and Services: FBMS; and INTERIOR/DOI-89, Grants and Cooperative 
Agreements: FBMS, with records maintained in the U.S. Department of the 
Treasury's Do Not Pay Working System. DOI will use the matching program 
to verify prepayment or pre-award eligibility, identify and prevent 
improper payments, and conduct related recovery activities.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background

    The Computer Matching and Privacy Protection Act of 1988 amended 
the Privacy Act to establish procedural protections for certain 
computerized comparisons. The Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 
established the Do Not Pay Initiative.
    Pursuant to the Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019, the 
Secretary of the Treasury, after consultation with the Director of the 
Office of Management and Budget, authorized a four-year waiver of the 
matching-agreement requirement under 5 U.S.C. 552a(o) for the class of 
Do Not Pay matching programs that meet the criteria established in OMB 
Memorandum M-25-32. The waiver is effective from September 10, 2025, 
through September 10, 2029.
    DOI, in consultation with the U.S. Department of the Treasury, 
Bureau of the Fiscal Service, determined that the Do Not Pay matching 
program described in this notice is eligible for the waiver described 
in OMB Memorandum M-25-32. For purposes of this notice, the matching 
activities conducted between the Federal benefit programs listed in the 
appendix to this document and the Do Not Pay Working System constitute 
a single agency-wide DOI matching program implementing Do Not Pay for 
DOI's listed programs.\1\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ The statutory definition of the term ``matching program'' 
means ``any computerized comparison of--(i) two or more automated 
systems of records or a system of records with non-Federal records'' 
for certain enumerated purposes. 5 U.S.C. 552a(a)(8). There is a 
separate statutory definition for ``Federal benefit program.'' See 
OMB Memorandum M-25-32 at Appendix II, page 2, sec. a.3.iii.1 
(recognizing that a single agency matching program may consist of 
multiple systems of records). Thus, this notice applies to the DNP 
matching program for multiple Federal benefits programs and 
associated systems of records within DOI.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

II. Participating Agencies

    The U.S. Department of the Interior programs listed in the 
appendix, administered by the Bureau of Indian Affairs and the U.S. 
Fish and Wildlife Service, will compare records with the Do Not Pay 
Working System, which is administered by the U.S. Department of the 
Treasury, Bureau of the Fiscal Service.

III. Authority for Conducting the Matching Program

    The Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019, 31 U.S.C. 3351 et 
seq.; Executive Order 13520, Reducing Improper Payments (74 FR 62201); 
Executive Order 14249, Protecting America's Bank Account Against Fraud, 
Waste, and Abuse (90 FR 14011); and OMB Memorandum M-25-32, Preventing 
Improper Payments and Protecting Privacy Through Do Not Pay. Additional 
information regarding the authorities for the collection and 
maintenance of information for each of the DOI programs that will 
conduct matches with the Do Not Pay Working System are contained within 
the systems of records notices listed in the appendix below.

IV. Purpose(s)

    The purpose of the matching program is to identify and prevent 
improper payments and support related recovery activities by verifying 
prepayment or pre-award eligibility through the Do Not Pay Working 
System. Data elements that are necessary for eligibility determinations 
for a relevant DOI program that are contained in records from DOI 
systems of records will be compared with records in Do Not Pay Working 
System.
    When the Do Not Pay Working System identifies a potential match, it 
will notify the submitting DOI program and identify the database or 
data source containing the potential match. The DOI program will 
independently verify the information before taking any action that 
could affect an individual, applicant, awardee, recipient, vendor, or 
payee. If the Do Not Pay Working System identifies no match, it will 
return a no-match response.

V. Categories of Individuals

    Applicants for or recipients of Federal funds administered through 
the DOI programs listed in the appendix, including individual awardees, 
vendors, payees, and sole proprietors whose records are submitted to 
the Do Not Pay

[[Page 62743]]

Working System for prepayment or pre-award eligibility verification.

VI. Categories of Records

    The categories of records involved in the matching program include 
identifying, contact, award, payment, and financial information 
maintained by DOI, and identifying, eligibility, payment, financial, 
and other payment-verification information maintained in the U.S. 
Department of the Treasury's Do Not Pay Working System. DOI data 
elements disclosed for the computerized comparison include Taxpayer 
Identification Number (TIN), which may include Social Security number 
(SSN), Employer Identification Number (EIN), or Individual Taxpayer 
Identification Number (ITIN); awardee/payee name; awardee/payee 
physical address, including city, state, and ZIP Code; and awardee/
payee bank account information, including account number and financial 
institution routing and transit number. Records returned by the Do Not 
Pay Working System may include information identifying a potential 
match and the database or data source in which the potential match was 
identified.
    Records involved in the matching program may also include 
information returned by the Do Not Pay Working System concerning a 
potential match and records used or generated by DOI to independently 
verify the match and conduct any necessary follow-up before taking an 
action affecting an individual, applicant, awardee, recipient, vendor, 
or payee. DOI will independently verify information resulting from a 
potential match before taking such action.

VII. System(s) of Records

    DOI records used in the matching program are maintained, as 
applicable to each participating program identified in the appendix, in 
the following systems of records:
     INTERIOR/DOI-85, Payroll, Attendance, Retirement, and 
Leave Records, 83 FR 34156 (July 19, 2018), modified at 91 FR 10622 
(March 4, 2026);
     INTERIOR/DOI-86, Accounts Receivable: FBMS, 73 FR 43772 
(July 28, 2008), modified at 86 FR 50156 (September 7, 2021) and 91 FR 
10622 (March 4, 2026);
     INTERIOR/DOI-87, Acquisition of Goods and Services: FBMS, 
73 FR 43766 (July 28, 2008), modified at 86 FR 50156 (September 7, 
2021) and 91 FR 10622 (March 4, 2026); and
     INTERIOR/DOI-89, Grants and Cooperative Agreements: FBMS, 
73 FR 43755 (July 28, 2008), modified at 86 FR 50156 (September 7, 
2021) and 91 FR 10622 (March 4, 2026).
    Records in the Do Not Pay Working System are maintained in 
Department of the Treasury, Bureau of the Fiscal Service .017--Do Not 
Pay Payment Verification Records, 85 FR 11776 (February 27, 2020), 
including databases designated for inclusion under applicable law and 
OMB designation authority.
