36 C.F.R. § 702.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:36:702.1
Creator: Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-702.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 702 (Conduct On Library Premises). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules and regulations in this part apply to all Federal property under the charge and control of the Librarian of Congress and to all persons entering in or on such property.
