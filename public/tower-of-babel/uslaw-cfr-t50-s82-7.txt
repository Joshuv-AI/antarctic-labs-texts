50 C.F.R. § 82.7 — Coordination with States.
Citation: us:cfr:50:82.7
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-82.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 82 (Administrative Procedures For Grants-In-Aid (Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the proposed project is to be conducted within the territorial limits of a state, the Secretary shall not enter into an agreement with a non-Federal interest other than a State without first consulting with the State agency.
