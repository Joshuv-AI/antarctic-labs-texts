7 U.S.C. § 2281 — Congressional declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:7:2281
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2281
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55A (Department Of Agriculture Advisory Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are to—
(1)
require strict financial and program accounting by advisory committees of the Department of Agriculture;
(2)
assure balance and objectivity in the membership of such advisory committees; and
(3)
prevent the formation or continuation of unnecessary advisory committees by the Department of Agriculture.
