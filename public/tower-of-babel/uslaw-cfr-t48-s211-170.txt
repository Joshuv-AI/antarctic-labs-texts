48 C.F.R. § 211.170 — 211.170 Use of proprietary specifications or standards.
Citation: us:cfr:48:211.170
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-211.170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 211 (Describing Agency Needs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A justification and approval is required to use proprietary specifications and standards—
(1) When using sealed bidding or negotiated acquisition procedures (see 206.302-1(S-70) for justification requirements); or
(2) When using the simplified procedures for certain commercial products or commercial services at FAR 13.5 (see 213.501(a)(ii) for justification requirements).
