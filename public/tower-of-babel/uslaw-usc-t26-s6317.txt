26 U.S.C. § 6317 — Payments of Federal unemployment tax for calendar quarter
Citation: us:usc:26:6317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6317
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter B (Receipt Of Payment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payment of Federal unemployment tax for a calendar quarter or other period within a calendar year pursuant to section 6157 shall be considered payment on account of the tax imposed by chapter 23 of such calendar year.
