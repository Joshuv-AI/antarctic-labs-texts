32 C.F.R. § 1605.27 — Minutes of meetings.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1605.27
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1605.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1605 (Selective Service System Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Selective Service compensated employee will keep the minutes of each appeal board meeting. In the absence of a compensated employee the minutes will be kept by an appeal board member.
