28 C.F.R. § 42.723 — Compliance information.
Citation: us:cfr:28:42.723
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-42.723
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 42 (Nondiscrimination; Equal Employment Opportunity; Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon request by the Department, a recipient shall make available to the Department information necessary to determine whether the recipient is complying with this subpart.
(b) Each recipient shall permit reasonable access by the Department to the recipient's facilities, books, records and other sources of information concerning the recipient's compliance with this subpart.
