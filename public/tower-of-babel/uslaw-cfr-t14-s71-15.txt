14 C.F.R. § 71.15 — Designation of jet routes and VOR Federal airways.
Citation: us:cfr:14:71.15
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-71.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 71 (Designation Of Class A, B, C, D, And E Airspace Areas; Air Traffic Service Routes; And Reporting Points). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified, the place names appearing in the descriptions of airspace areas designated as jet routes in subpart A of FAA Order JO 7400.11M, and as VOR Federal airways in subpart E of FAA Order JO 7400.11M, are the names of VOR or VORTAC navigation aids. FAA Order JO 7400.11M is incorporated by reference in § 71.1.
