25 C.F.R. § 152.30 — Bidding by employees.
Citation: us:cfr:25:152.30
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-152.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 152 (Issuance Of Patents In Fee, Certificates Of Competency, Removal Of Restrictions, And Sale Of Certain Indian Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as authorized by the provisions of part 140 of this chapter, no person employed in Indian Affairs shall directly or indirectly bid, make, or prepare any bid, or assist any bidder in preparing his bid. Sales between Indians, either of whom is an employee of the U.S. Government, are governed by the provisions of part 140 of this chapter (see 25 U.S.C. 68 and 441).
