28 C.F.R. § 12.40 — Public examination.
Citation: us:cfr:28:12.40
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-12.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 12 (Registration Of Certain Persons Having Knowledge Of Foreign Espionage, Counterespionage, Or Sabotage Matters Under The Act Of August 1, 1956). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registration statements shall be available for public examination at the offices of the Registration Unit, Department of Justice, Washington, DC, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on each official business day, except to the extent that the Attorney General having due regard for national security and public interest may withdraw such statements from public examination.
