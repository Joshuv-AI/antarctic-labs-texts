16 U.S.C. § 838e — Acquisition of property
Citation: us:usc:16:838e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section838e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12G (Pacific Northwest Federal Transmission System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the provisions of section 838c of this title the Administrator may purchase or lease or otherwise acquire and hold such real and personal property in the name of the United States as he deems necessary or appropriate to carry out his duties pursuant to law.
