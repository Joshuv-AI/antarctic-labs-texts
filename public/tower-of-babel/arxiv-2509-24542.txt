Theoretical uncertainties in Gamow-Teller transitions of 76 Ge Preprint: APS/123-QED
Hao Zhou, Long-Jun Wang, Yang Sun

Abstract
We present a novel shell-model approach to investigate possible theoretical uncertainties in the description of Gamow-Teller (GT) transitions regarding the β \beta decays of heavy nuclei. Our method systematically accounts for key factors including nuclear force or collectivity (intrinsic deformation), quantum many-body correlations (e.g., configuration space truncation), and contributions from transition operators (e.g., chiral two-body currents). Using the neutrinoless double β \beta -decay candidate 76 Ge as a benchmark, we compare our calculations with the charge-exchange reaction data, demonstrating that increased deformation tends to a reduction in the GT transition strengths of 76 Ge. Furthermore, the inclusion of higher-order quasiparticle configurations provides a more realistic description of nuclear structure, revealing two key effects: (1) a rapid increase in nuclear level density with excitation energy, and (2) fragmentation of GT strengths, shifting their distribution toward higher-energy states. Additionally, chiral two-body currents induce a 5 − 15 % 5-15\% quenching in the transition strength, with the exact magnitude depending on the choice of coupling constants.

In summary, the calculation of nuclear GT transition strengths is indispensable for understanding a large number of problems in nuclear physics, particle physics, nuclear astrophysics and nuclear technology. The theoretical uncertainties in calculating and predicting the GT transition strengths must be evaluated. We present a novel shell-model approach with projection techniques and large configuration space to investigate possible theoretical uncertainties for heavy nuclei, including nuclear deformation, configuration space truncation and the chiral two-body currents. For
76
Ge, our calculations show that both nuclear deformation and large configuration space are very important and show large uncertainties in describing the GT transition strengths. The effect of the chiral two-body currents on the uncertainties of nuclear GT transition strengths is about
5-15\%
quenching, which is actually independent of the excitation-energy region. The ongoing future works includes studying the similar theoretical uncertainties for double
\beta
decay and developing a projected shell model with chiral force.
