45 C.F.R. § 155.1304 — Definitions.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-155.1304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 155 (Exchange Establishment Standards And Other Related Standards Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this subpart:
Complete application means an application that has been submitted and for which the Secretary and the Secretary of the Treasury, as applicable, have made a preliminary determination that it includes all required information and satisfies all requirements that are described in § 155.1308(f).
Public notice means a notice issued by a government agency or legislative body that contains sufficient detail to notify the public at large of a proposed action consistent with § 155.1312.
Section 1332 waiver means a Waiver for State Innovation under section 1332 of the Affordable Care Act.
