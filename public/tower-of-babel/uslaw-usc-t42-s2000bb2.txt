42 U.S.C. § 2000bb–2 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:2000bb–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000bb–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21B (Religious Freedom Restoration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
(1)
the term “government” includes a branch, department, agency, instrumentality, and official (or other person acting under color of law) of the United States, or of a covered entity;
(2)
the term “covered entity” means the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, and each territory and possession of the United States;
(3)
the term “demonstrates” means meets the burdens of going forward with the evidence and of persuasion; and
(4)
the term “exercise of religion” means religious exercise, as defined in section 2000cc–5 of this title.
