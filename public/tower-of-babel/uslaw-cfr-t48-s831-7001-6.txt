48 C.F.R. § 831.7001-6 — 831.7001-6 Reimbursement for other supplies and services.
Citation: us:cfr:48:831.7001-6
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-831.7001-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 831 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will provide reimbursement for other services and assistance that may be authorized under applicable provisions of 38 U.S.C. chapter 31 regulations, including, but not limited to, employment and self-employment services, initial and extended evaluation services, and independent living services.
