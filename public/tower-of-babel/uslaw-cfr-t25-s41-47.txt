25 C.F.R. § 41.47 — Are Tribal colleges or universities eligible for endowments?
Citation: us:cfr:25:41.47
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-41.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 41 (Grants To Tribal Colleges And Universities And Diné College). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Tribal colleges and universities are eligible for endowments under a signed agreement between the Tribal college and university and the Secretary as described in 25 U.S.C. 1832. Endowments must be invested in a trust fund and the Tribal college or university may only use the interest deposited for the purpose of defraying expenses associated with the operation of the Tribal college or university (25 U.S.C. 1833).
