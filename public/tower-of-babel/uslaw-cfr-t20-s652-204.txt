20 C.F.R. § 652.204 — Must funds authorized under the Wagner-Peyser Act Governor's Reserve flow through the one-stop delivery system?
Citation: us:cfr:20:652.204
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-652.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 652 (Establishment And Functioning Of State Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, sec. 7(b) of the Wagner-Peyser Act provides that 10 percent of the State's allotment under the Wagner-Peyser Act is reserved for use by the Governor for performance incentives, supporting exemplary models of service delivery, professional development and career advancement of SWA officials as applicable, and services for groups with special needs. However, these funds may flow through the one-stop delivery system.
