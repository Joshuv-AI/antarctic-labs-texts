46 C.F.R. § 142.231 — Exception for portable and semi-portable fire extinguishers required for existing towing vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:142.231
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-142.231
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 142 (Fire Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Previously installed fire extinguishers with extinguishing capacities smaller than what is required by § 142.230 of this part need not be replaced and may be continued in service so long as they are maintained in good condition to the satisfaction of the OCMI.
(b) All new equipment and installations must meet the applicable requirements in this part for new vessels.
