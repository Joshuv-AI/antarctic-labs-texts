40 C.F.R. § 204.1 — General applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:204.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-204.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 204 (Noise Emission Standards For Construction Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to all products for which regulations have been published under this part and which are manufactured after the effective date of such regulations.
