5 C.F.R. § 1210.4 — Waiver of MSPB regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1210.4
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1210.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1210 (Practices And Procedures For An Appeal Of A Removal Or Transfer Of A Senior Executive Service Employee By The Secretary Of The Department Of Veterans Affairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may waive any MSPB regulation in order to provide for the expedited review of an appeal covered by this part. Public Law 113-146, section 707(b)(3).
