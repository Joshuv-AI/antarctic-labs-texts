30 C.F.R. § 773.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:773.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-773.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 773 (Requirements For Permits And Permit Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides minimum requirements for permits and permit processing and covers obtaining and reviewing permits; coordinating with other laws; public participation; permit decision and notification; permit conditions; and permit term and right of renewal.
