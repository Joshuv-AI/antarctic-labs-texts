7 C.F.R. § 1000.3 — Route disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1000.3
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1000.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1000 (General Provisions Of Federal Milk Marketing Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Route disposition means a delivery to a retail or wholesale outlet (except a plant), either directly or through any distribution facility (including disposition from a plant store, vendor, or vending machine) of a fluid milk product in consumer-type packages or dispenser units classified as Class I milk.
