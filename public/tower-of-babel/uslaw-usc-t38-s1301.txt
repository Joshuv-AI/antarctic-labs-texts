38 U.S.C. § 1301 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:38:1301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1301
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 13 (Dependency And Indemnity Compensation For Service-Connected Deaths); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
The term “veteran” includes a person who died in the active military, naval, air, or space service.
