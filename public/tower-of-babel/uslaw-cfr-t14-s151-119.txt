14 C.F.R. § 151.119 — Advance planning proposals: Procedures; funding.
Citation: us:cfr:14:151.119
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-151.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 151 (Federal Aid To Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The funding information required by § 151.23, except the last sentence, also is required in connection with an advance planning proposal. The sponsor's share of estimated proposal costs may not consist of or include the value of donated labor, materials, or equipment.
