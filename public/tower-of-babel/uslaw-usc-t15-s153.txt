15 U.S.C. § 153 — Dividends
Citation: us:usc:15:153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section153
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 4 (China Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Dividends declared by a China Trade Act corporation shall be derived wholly from the surplus profits of its business.
