29 C.F.R. § 500.141 — Concurrent actions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:500.141
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-500.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 500 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The taking of any one of the actions referred to in § 500.140 shall not be a bar to the concurrent taking of any other action authorized by the Act and these regulations.
