2 U.S.C. § 1834 — Heating and ventilating Senate wing
Citation: us:usc:2:1834
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1834
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter III (Personnel); Part A (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All engineers and others who are engaged in heating and ventilating the Senate wing of the Capitol shall be subject to the orders and in all respects under the direction of the Architect of the Capitol, subject to the approval of the Senate Committee on Rules and Administration.
