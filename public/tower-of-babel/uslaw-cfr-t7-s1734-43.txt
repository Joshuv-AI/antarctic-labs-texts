7 C.F.R. § 1734.43 — Maximum and minimum amounts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1734.43
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1734.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1734 (Distance Learning And Telemedicine Loan And Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for loans under this subpart will be subject to limitations on the proposed amount of loans. The Administrator will establish the maximum amount of a loan available to an applicant under this subpart.
