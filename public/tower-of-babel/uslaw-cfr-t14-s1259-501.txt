14 C.F.R. § 1259.501 — Responsibilities.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1259 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All institutions that receive Space Grant fellowships shall use the awards to increase the pool of graduate students in fields related to space.
(b) The overall fellowship program shall be cognizant of the importance of achieving institutional and geographical diversity.
