49 C.F.R. § 270.301 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:49:270.301
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-270.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 270 (System Safety Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The system safety program and its implementation shall be assessed internally by the passenger rail operation and audited externally by FRA or FRA's designee.
