42 C.F.R. § 137.22 — May the Secretary consider uncorrected significant and material audit exceptions identified regarding centralized financial and administrative functions?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.22
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if the Indian Tribe chooses to centralize its self-determination or self-governance financial and administrative functions with non-self-determination or non-self-governance financial and administrative functions, such as personnel, payroll, property management, etc., the Secretary may consider uncorrected significant and material audit exceptions related to the integrity of a cross-cutting centralized function in determining the Indian Tribe's eligibility for participation in the self-governance program.
