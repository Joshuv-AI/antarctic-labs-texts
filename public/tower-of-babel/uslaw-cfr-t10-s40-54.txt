10 C.F.R. § 40.54 — Requirements for license to initially transfer source material for use under the ‘small quantities of source material’ general license.
Citation: us:cfr:10:40.54
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-40.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 40 (Domestic Licensing Of Source Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for a specific license to initially transfer source material for use under § 40.22, or equivalent regulations of an Agreement State, will be approved if:
(a) The applicant satisfies the general requirements specified in § 40.32; and
(b) The applicant submits adequate information on, and the Commission approves the methods to be used for quality control, labeling, and providing safety instructions to recipients.
