34 C.F.R. § 225.2 — Who is eligible to receive a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:225.2
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-225.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 225 (Credit Enhancement For Charter School Facilities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are eligible to receive a grant under this part:
(a) A public entity, such as a State or local governmental entity;
(b) A private nonprofit entity; or
(c) A consortium of entities described in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section.
