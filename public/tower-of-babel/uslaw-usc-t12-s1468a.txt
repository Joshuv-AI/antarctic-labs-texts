12 U.S.C. § 1468a — Advertising
Citation: us:usc:12:1468a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1468a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 12 (Savings Associations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No savings association shall carry on any sale, plan, or practices, or any advertising, in violation of regulations promulgated by a Federal banking agency.
