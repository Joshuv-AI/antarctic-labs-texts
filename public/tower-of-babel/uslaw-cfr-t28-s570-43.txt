28 C.F.R. § 570.43 — Inmates requiring a high degree of control and supervision.
Citation: us:cfr:28:570.43
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-570.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 570 (Community Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only the Regional Director may approve a non-medical escorted trip (either emergency or non-emergency) for an inmate determined to require a high degree of control and supervision.
