Spin density matrix of baryon-antibaryon pairs in electron-positron annihilation with P P and C ​ P CP violation including electron mass
Chun-Qiu Zhao, Xu Cao, Jian-Ping Dai

Abstract
The spin density matrix for octet baryon-antibaryon pairs produced in electron-positron annihilation is unambiguously given within the one-photon exchange in the centre-of-mass frame. Both the parity ( P P ) and combined parity and charge-conjugation ( C ​ P CP ) violation effects in the intermediate photon coupling to the baryon pairs are simultaneously considered. The electron mass is explicitly included in the general formulae presented in this paper. The whole framework is build upon the spin projection operator.

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha^{2}\beta}{4q^{6}}L_{\mu\nu}\biggl[H^{\mu\nu}_{EM}+H^{\mu\nu}_{A}+H^{\mu\nu}_{T}+2\mathfrak{Re}H^{\mu\nu}_{EMA}

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{A}=J_{A}^{\mu}(J_{A}^{\nu})^{*}\,,

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{T}=J_{T}^{\mu}(J_{T}^{\nu})^{*}\,,

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{EMA}=J_{EM}^{\mu}(J_{A}^{\nu})^{*}\,,

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{EMT}=J_{EM}^{\mu}(J_{T}^{\nu})^{*}\,,

\displaystyle p_{1}=(E,0,0,|\mbox{\boldmath$p\!\!$ \unboldmath}_{1}|)

\displaystyle;

\displaystyle\>k_{1}=(E,-|\mbox{\boldmath$k\!\!$ \unboldmath}_{1}|\sin\theta,0,|\mbox{\boldmath$k\!\!$ \unboldmath}_{1}|\cos\theta)

After a Lorentz boost of the polarization unit vector

\displaystyle s_{0}=\frac{1}{M}\mbox{\boldmath$p\!\!$ \unboldmath}\cdot\mbox{\boldmath$\xi\!\!$ \unboldmath}\>;\>\mbox{\boldmath$s\!\!$ \unboldmath}=\mbox{\boldmath$\xi\!\!$ \unboldmath}+\frac{\mbox{\boldmath$p\!\!$ \unboldmath}(\mbox{\boldmath$p\!\!$ \unboldmath}\cdot\mbox{\boldmath$\xi\!\!$ \unboldmath})}{M(M+E)}\,.

\begin{split}P_{y}(\bar{Y})&=\frac{1}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\biggl[\mathfrak{Im}\left[G_{M}G_{E}^{*}\right]\\
&\quad-(\tau-1)\mathfrak{Re}\left[H_{T}F_{A}^{*}\right]\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(12)

\begin{split}P_{y}(Y)&=\frac{1}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\biggl[\mathfrak{Im}\left[G_{M}G_{E}^{*}\right]\\

\begin{split}P_{x}(\bar{Y})&=\frac{\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\tau}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\\

(15)

\begin{split}P_{z}(\bar{Y})&=\frac{2\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\biggl[\bigl(\sin^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\bigr)\mathfrak{Im}\left[G_{E}H_{T}^{*}\right]\\

\begin{split}P_{z}(Y)&=\frac{2\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\biggl[\Bigl(-\sin^{2}\theta-\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)\mathfrak{Im}\left[G_{E}H_{T}^{*}\right]\\

IV
Double-spin polarization observables

The double-spin polarization observables represent spin correlation between the final baryon and antibaryon.
By inserting the spin projector operator of baryon and antibaryon defined above simultaneously into Eq. (
4

\begin{split}C_{xx}&=\frac{1}{D\tau}\biggl[\tau\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)\sin^{2}\theta|G_{M}|^{2}\\

&\quad-\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)(\tau-1)\sin^{2}\theta|F_{A}|^{2}\\
&\quad-\tau(\tau-1)\Bigl(\sin^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|H_{T}|^{2}\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(18)

\begin{split}C_{yy}=\frac{1}{D\tau}\biggl[&\Bigl(\sin^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|G_{E}|^{2}\\
&-\tau\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)\sin^{2}\theta|G_{M}|^{2}\\
&+\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)(\tau-1)\sin^{2}\theta|F_{A}|^{2}\\
&-\tau(\tau-1)\Bigl(\sin^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|H_{T}|^{2}\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(19)

\begin{split}C_{zz}&=\frac{1}{D\tau}\biggl[\tau\Bigl(1+\cos^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|G_{M}|^{2}\\
&\quad-\Bigl(\sin^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|G_{E}|^{2}\\
&\quad+(\tau-1)\Bigl(1+\cos^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|F_{A}|^{2}\\
&\quad-\tau(\tau-1)\Bigl(\sin^{2}\theta+\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)|H_{T}|^{2}\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(20)

\begin{split}C_{xy}&=\frac{2\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\biggl[\Bigl(\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}+\sin^{2}\theta\Bigr)\mathfrak{Re}\left[G_{E}H_{T}^{*}\right]\\
&\quad-\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)\sin^{2}\theta\mathfrak{Im}\left[G_{M}F_{A}^{*}\right]\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(21)

\begin{split}C_{yx}&=\frac{2\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\biggl[-\Bigl(\tfrac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\tau_{e}}+\sin^{2}\theta\Bigr)\mathfrak{Re}\left[G_{E}H_{T}^{*}\right]\\
&\quad-\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\Bigr)\sin^{2}\theta\mathfrak{Im}\left[G_{M}F_{A}^{*}\right]\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(22)

\begin{split}C_{xz}&=\frac{1}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\\
&\quad\times\biggl[-\mathfrak{Re}\left[G_{M}G_{E}^{*}\right]+(\tau-1)\mathfrak{Im}\left[H_{T}F_{A}^{*}\right]\biggr]\,,\end{split}

(23)

\begin{split}C_{zx}&=\frac{1}{D\sqrt{\tau}}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\\

(24)

\begin{split}C_{yz}&=\frac{\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\tau}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\\

(25)

\begin{split}C_{zy}&=\frac{\sqrt{\tau-1}}{D\tau}\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\tau_{e}}\right)\sin 2\theta\\

V
Spin density matrix

We explicitly write down the spin density matrix
C_{\mu\nu}
of baryon-antibaryon pair because it is convenient to calculate the whole angular analysis of sequential decays in the helicity formalism
29
;
58
;
61
;
39
;

.
By combining in single and double polarization observables in Sec.
III
and Section
IV
, one has

C_{\mu\nu}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}C_{00}&P_{x}&P_{y}&P_{z}\\
\bar{P}_{x}&C_{xx}&C_{xy}&C_{xz}\\
\bar{P}_{y}&C_{yx}&C_{yy}&C_{yz}\\
\bar{P}_{z}&C_{zx}&C_{zy}&C_{zz}\\
\end{array}\right)\,,

(28)

with
C_{00}
being the normalized unpolarized cross section in Eq. (
9

\bar{P}_{x,y,z}=P_{x,y,z}(\bar{Y})
.
The relative phases are defined as:

\displaystyle\Delta\Phi_{EM}

\displaystyle=\arg(G_{E}/G_{M})\,,

(29)

\displaystyle\Delta\Phi_{AT}

\displaystyle=\arg(F_{A}/H_{T})\,,

\displaystyle\Delta\Phi_{AE}

\displaystyle=\arg(F_{A}/G_{E})\,,

\displaystyle\Delta\Phi_{TM}

\displaystyle=\arg(H_{T}/G_{M})\,,

\displaystyle\Delta\Phi_{AM}

\displaystyle=\arg(F_{A}/G_{M})\,,

\displaystyle\Delta\Phi_{TE}

\displaystyle=\arg(H_{T}/G_{E}),

which correspond to the relative phases between the electric and magnetic form factors,

- and

violating strengths,

violating strength and electric form factor,

violating strength and magnetic form factor,
P
-violating strength and magnetic form factor, and

Note that only three relative phases among six are independent.
We decompose
C_{\mu\nu}
into 12 matrices as follows:

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=C_{\mu\nu}(EM)+C_{\mu\nu}(AT)+C_{\mu\nu}(AE)

(30)

\displaystyle+C_{\mu\nu}(TM)+C_{\mu\nu}(AM)+C_{\mu\nu}(TE)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{\tau_{e}}\left[C_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}(EM)+C_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}(AT)+C_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}(AE)\right.

\displaystyle\left.+C_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}(TM)+C_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}(AM)+C_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}(TE)\right]\,,

including the effect of finite electron and positron mass.
The letters in parentheses denote the involved form factors and the superscript

These matrices are categorized according to distinct combinations of the electric, magnetic,

-violating and

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}(EM)

\displaystyle=\xi_{EM}\times\begin{pmatrix}1+\alpha_{EM}\cos^{2}\theta&0&\beta_{EM}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0\\
0&\sin^{2}\theta&0&-\gamma_{EM}\sin\theta\cos\theta\\
\beta_{EM}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&-\alpha_{EM}\sin^{2}\theta&0\\
0&-\gamma_{EM}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&\alpha_{EM}+\cos^{2}\theta\end{pmatrix},

(31)

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}(AT)

\displaystyle=\xi_{AT}(\tau-1)\times\begin{pmatrix}1-\alpha_{AT}\cos^{2}\theta&0&\gamma_{AT}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0\\
0&-\sin^{2}\theta&0&\beta_{AT}\sin\theta\cos\theta\\
-\gamma_{AT}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&-\alpha_{AT}\sin^{2}\theta&0\\
0&-\beta_{AT}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&-\alpha_{AT}+\cos^{2}\theta\end{pmatrix},

(32)

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}(AE)

\displaystyle=\xi_{AE}\frac{\sqrt{\tau-1}}{\sqrt{\tau}}\times\begin{pmatrix}0&\gamma_{AE}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&0\\
\gamma_{AE}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&0&0\\
0&0&0&\beta_{AE}\sin\theta\cos\theta\\
0&0&\beta_{AE}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0\end{pmatrix},

(33)

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}(TM)

\displaystyle=\xi_{TM}\sqrt{\tau(\tau-1)}\times\begin{pmatrix}0&-\beta_{TM}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&0\\
\beta_{TM}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0&0&0\\
0&0&0&\gamma_{TM}\sin\theta\cos\theta\\
0&0&-\gamma_{TM}\sin\theta\cos\theta&0\end{pmatrix},

(34)

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}(AM)

\displaystyle=-\xi_{AM}\sqrt{\tau-1}\times\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0&\gamma_{AM}(1+\cos^{2}\theta)\\
0&0&\beta_{AM}\sin^{2}\theta&0\\
0&\beta_{AM}\sin^{2}\theta&0&0\\
\gamma_{AM}(1+\cos^{2}\theta)&0&0&0\end{pmatrix},

(35)

\displaystyle C_{\mu\nu}(TE)

\displaystyle=\xi_{TE}\sqrt{\tau-1}\times\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0&-\beta_{TE}\sin^{2}\theta\\
0&0&\gamma_{TE}\sin^{2}\theta&0\\

VI
Summary

This paper explicitly gives the spin density matrix for spin half baryon-antibaryon pairs produced through one-photon exchange in electron-positron annihilation.
The
P
and
CP
violation in the vertex of virtual photon coupling to the baryon pairs are considered and the electron mass correction is included.
The calculation of polarization and correlation is performed by the method of spin projection operator and the final results are obtained in the centre-of-mass frame.
The whole spin density matrix is written down in a compact way for later calculation of the full angular distributions of sequential decays within the helicity formalism.

The parameters of
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi\to\Lambda(p\pi^{-})\bar{\Lambda}(\overline{p}\pi^{+})
in our prescription are infered from recent BESIII data.
Our framework would be also used to study the
P
and
CP
violation in
\tau
lepton pair production
21
;
50
.
Our results are obtained for the unpolarized electron and positron beams and portable to the cases of longitudinal and transversely polarized beams
67
;
61
;
28
.
