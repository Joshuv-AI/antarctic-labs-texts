48 C.F.R. § 225.770-3 — 225.770-3 Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.770-3
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.770-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The prohibition in 225.770-2 does not apply to items acquired—
(a) In connection with a visit to the People's Republic of China by a vessel or an aircraft of the U.S. armed forces;
(b) For testing purposes; or
(c) For the purpose of gathering intelligence.
