Antibody evasion and receptor binding of SARS-CoV-2 LP.8.1.1, NB.1.8.1, XFG, and related subvariants
Authors: Ian A Mellis, Madeline Wu, Hsiang Hong, Chih-Chen Tzang, Anthony Bowen, Qian Wang, Carmen Gherasim, Virginia M Pierce, Jayesh G Shah, Lawrence J Purpura, Michael T Yin, Aubree Gordon, Yicheng Guo, David D Ho
Published: 2025

In the originally published version of this article, in the mutation summary in Figure 1A, the JN.1 spike was incorrectly noted to have N679, LP.8.1 was incorrectly noted to have R679, and LP.8.1.1 was incorrectly noted to have K679. The spikes of JN.1 and LP.8.1 in fact have K679, while the subvariant LP.8.1.1 has a substitution leading to R679. N679 is in fact the wild-type pre-Omicron virus’s genotype at that position; none of the tested viruses in this report have N679. Throughout the rest of the manuscript, in the main text and in the supplemental information, the correct amino acid identities at position 679 are noted accurately; the error only appears in Figure 1A. This error in Figure 1A was a transcription error introduced during figure assembly and did not affect the accuracy of experiments in later figures. All experimental results are based on plasmids encoding the correct amino acids, verified before experiments.
