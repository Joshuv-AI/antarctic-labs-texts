42 U.S.C. § 1397m–5 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:42:1397m–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1397m–5
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XX (Block Grants And Programs For Social Services And Elder Justice); Part II (Programs To Promote Elder Justice). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this division shall be construed as—
(1)
limiting any cause of action or other relief related to obligations under this division that is available under the law of any State, or political subdivision thereof; or
(2)
creating a private cause of action for a violation of this division.
