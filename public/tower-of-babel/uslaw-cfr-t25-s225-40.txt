25 C.F.R. § 225.40 — Government employees cannot acquire minerals agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:25:225.40
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-225.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 225 (Oil And Gas, Geothermal, And Solid Minerals Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

U.S. Government employees are prevented from acquiring any interest(s) in minerals agreements by the provisions of 25 CFR part 140 and 43 CFR part 20 pertaining to conflicts of interest and ownership of an interest in trust land.
