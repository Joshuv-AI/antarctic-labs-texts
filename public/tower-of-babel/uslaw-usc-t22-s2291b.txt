22 U.S.C. § 2291b — Prohibition on use of foreign assistance for reimbursements for drug crop eradications
Citation: us:usc:22:2291b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2291b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part VIII (International Narcotics Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds made available to carry out this chapter may not be used to reimburse persons whose illicit drug crops are eradicated.
