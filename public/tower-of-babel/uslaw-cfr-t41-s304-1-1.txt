41 C.F.R. § 304-1.1 — -1.1 Authority for accepting non-Federal source travel expense payments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-1.1
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-1.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-1 (1—Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the authority of this part and 31 U.S.C. 1353, employees may accept payment of travel expenses from a non-Federal source on behalf of their agency, but not personally, when specifically authorized to do so by their agency and only for official travel to a meeting. Except as provided in § 304-3.11 of this subchapter, the agency must approve acceptance of such payments in advance of travel.
