13 C.F.R. § 124.702 — What types of assistance are available through the 7(j) program?
Citation: us:cfr:13:124.702
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-124.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 124 (8(a) BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT/SMALL DISADVANTAGED BUSINESS STATUS DETERMINATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Through its private sector service providers, SBA may provide a wide variety of management and technical assistance to eligible individuals or concerns to meet their specific needs, including:
(a) Counseling and training in the areas of financing, management, accounting, bookkeeping, marketing, and operation of small business concerns; and
(b) The identification and development of new business opportunities.
