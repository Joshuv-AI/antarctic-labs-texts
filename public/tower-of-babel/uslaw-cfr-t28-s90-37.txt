28 C.F.R. § 90.37 — Ineligible expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:28:90.37
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-90.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 90 (Violence Against Women). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating Tribes are not permitted to request reimbursement for the following:
(a) Planning: Expenses associated with planning to exercise STCJ, such as code drafting.
(b) Training, including costs for training criminal justice personnel, court personnel, or others.
(c) Any expenses not incurred in, relating to, or associated with exercising STCJ.
