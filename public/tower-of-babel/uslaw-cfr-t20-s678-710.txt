20 C.F.R. § 678.710 — How are infrastructure costs funded?
Citation: us:cfr:20:678.710
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-678.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 678 (Description Of The One-Stop Delivery System Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Infrastructure costs are funded either through the local funding mechanism described in § 678.715 or through the State funding mechanism described in § 678.730.
