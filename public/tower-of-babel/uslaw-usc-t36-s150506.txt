36 U.S.C. § 150506 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:150506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150506
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1505 (National Conference Of State Societies, Washington, District Of Columbia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the powers provided in its bylaws and articles of incorporation filed in the State in which it is incorporated, including the power to—
(1)
adopt bylaws and regulations for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
adopt emblems and badges;
(4)
choose officers, managers, and agents as the activities of the corporation require;
(5)
make contracts;
(6)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(7)
borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property;
(8)
publish a magazine, newspaper, and other publications consistent with the purposes of the corporation;
(9)
sue and be sued; and
(10)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
