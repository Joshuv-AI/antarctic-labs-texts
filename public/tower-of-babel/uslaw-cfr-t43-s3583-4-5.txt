43 C.F.R. § 3583.4-5 — -5 Lease by competitive bidding.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3583.4-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3583.4-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3580 (Special Leasing Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases may be offered competitively for any lands applied for under this subpart without regard to the quantity or quality of the mineral deposit that may be present therein.
