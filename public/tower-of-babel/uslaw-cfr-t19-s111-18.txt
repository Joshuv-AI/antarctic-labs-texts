19 C.F.R. § 111.18 — Reapplication for license.
Citation: us:cfr:19:111.18
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-111.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 111 (Customs Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant who has been denied a license may reapply at any time by complying with the provisions of § 111.12 and addressing how deficiencies have been remedied.
