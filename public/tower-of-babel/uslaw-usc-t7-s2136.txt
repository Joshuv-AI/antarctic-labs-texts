7 U.S.C. § 2136 — Registration of research facilities, handlers, carriers and unlicensed exhibitors
Citation: us:usc:7:2136
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2136
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 54 (Transportation, Sale, And Handling Of Certain Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every research facility, every intermediate handler, every carrier, and every exhibitor not licensed under section 2133 of this title shall register with the Secretary in accordance with such rules and regulations as he may prescribe.
