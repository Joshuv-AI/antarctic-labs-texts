10 U.S.C. § 2010 — Requirements with respect to motorcycle safety training
Citation: us:usc:10:2010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2010
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 101 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretaries of the military departments shall ensure that all beginner motorcycle safety training provided to members of the Armed Forces meets the motorcycle safety training requirements for licensing of the State in which the permanent station of the member receiving the training is located.
