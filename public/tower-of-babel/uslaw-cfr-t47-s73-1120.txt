47 C.F.R. § 73.1120 — Station location.
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.1120
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.1120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each AM, FM, TV and Class A TV broadcast station will be licensed to the principal community or other political subdivision which it primarily serves. This principal community (city, town or other political subdivision) will be considered to be the geographical station location.
