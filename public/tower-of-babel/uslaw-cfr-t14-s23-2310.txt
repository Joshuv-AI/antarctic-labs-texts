14 C.F.R. § 23.2310 — Buoyancy for seaplanes and amphibians.
Citation: us:cfr:14:23.2310
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-23.2310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 23 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Airplanes intended for operations on water, must—
(a) Provide buoyancy of 80 percent in excess of the buoyancy required to support the maximum weight of the airplane in fresh water; and
(b) Have sufficient margin so the airplane will stay afloat at rest in calm water without capsizing in case of a likely float or hull flooding.
