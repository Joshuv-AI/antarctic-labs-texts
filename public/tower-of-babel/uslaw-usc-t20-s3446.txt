20 U.S.C. § 3446 — Transfers from Department of Housing and Urban Development
Citation: us:usc:20:3446
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3446
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter III (Transfers Of Agencies And Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are transferred to the Secretary all functions relating to college housing loans of the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development and of the Department of Housing and Urban Development under title IV of the Housing Act of 1950 [12 U.S.C. 1749 et seq.].
