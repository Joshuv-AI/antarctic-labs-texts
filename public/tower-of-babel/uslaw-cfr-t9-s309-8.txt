9 C.F.R. § 309.8 — Cattle affected with anasarca and generalized edema.
Citation: us:cfr:9:309.8
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-309.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 309 (Ante-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All cattle found on ante-mortem inspection to be affected with anasarca in advanced stages and characterized by an extensive and generalized edema shall be identified as U.S. Condemned and disposed of in accordance with § 309.13.
