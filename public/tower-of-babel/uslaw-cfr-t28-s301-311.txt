28 C.F.R. § 301.311 — Expenses associated with appearance at committee hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:28:301.311
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-301.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 301 (Inmate Accident Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal Prison Industries, Inc., may not assume responsibility for any expenses incurred by the claimant, claimant's representative, or any witness appearing on behalf of the claimant in connection with attendance at the hearing, as well as any other costs relating to any representative, witnesses, or evidence associated with a hearing before the Committee.
