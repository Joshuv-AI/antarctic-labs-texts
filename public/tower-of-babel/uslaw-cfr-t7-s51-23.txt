7 C.F.R. § 51.23 — Report of inspection results prior to issuance of formal report.
Citation: us:cfr:7:51.23
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-51.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 51 (Fresh Fruits, Vegetables, And Other Products (Inspection, Certification, And Standards)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request of an applicant, all or any part of the contents of a certificate covering an inspection requested by him may be telegraphed or telephoned to him, or to any person designated by him, at his expense. If the application for such information is received after the certificate has been issued, it will be considered as an application for an extra copy of the certificate, and the fees prescribed in § 51.41 shall apply.
