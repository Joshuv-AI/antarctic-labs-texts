22 U.S.C. § 5502 — Coordinator for Counterterrorism
Citation: us:usc:22:5502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5502
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 64 (United States Response To Terrorism Affecting Americans Abroad). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coordinator for Counterterrorism shall be responsible for the coordination of international aviation security for the Department of State.
