32 C.F.R. § 202.5 — Creating a mission statement.
Citation: us:cfr:32:202.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-202.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 202 (Restoration Advisory Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The installation and community co-chair, in conjunction with the RAB members, shall determine the RAB mission statement in accordance with guidance provided by the DoD Components.
