39 C.F.R. § 3021.41 — Due date for participant statement.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3021.41
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3021.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3021 (Rules For Appeals Of Postal Service Determinations To Close Or Consolidate Post Offices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statement or brief of petitioner and of any other participant seeking to have the Commission return the entire matter to the Postal Service for further consideration, shall be filed not more than 20 days after the filing of the administrative record.
