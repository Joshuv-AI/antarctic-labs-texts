42 U.S.C. § 2222 — Condemnation of real property
Citation: us:usc:42:2222
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2222
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XIV (Compensation For Private Property Acquired). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Proceedings for condemnation shall be instituted pursuant to the provisions of section 3113 of title 40, and section 1403 of title 28. Sections 3114 to 3116 and 3118 of title 40 shall be applicable to any such proceedings.
