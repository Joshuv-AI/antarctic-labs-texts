46 C.F.R. § 380.3 — Processing of application.
Citation: us:cfr:46:380.3
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-380.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 380 (Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All applications under section 805(a) shall be referred to the Chief, Office of Government Aid, Maritime Administration, for consideration and such further action as may be appropriate.
