14 C.F.R. § 183.27 — Designated aircraft maintenance inspectors.
Citation: us:cfr:14:183.27
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-183.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 183 (Representatives Of The Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A designated aircraft maintenance inspector (DAMI) may approve maintenance on civil aircraft used by United States military flying clubs in foreign countries.
