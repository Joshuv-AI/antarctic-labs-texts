41 C.F.R. § 304-3.2 — -3.2 Types of acceptable non-Federal source payments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.2
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies or employees may accept payments other than cash from a non-Federal source for all official travel expenses to attend a meeting of mutual interest, or any portion of those travel expenses mutually agreed upon between the agency and the non-Federal source. Agencies or employees may not accept payments for non-meeting travel under this part. However, agencies or employees may be able to accept payments under other authorities (see § 304-3.17).
