42 U.S.C. § 16162 — Technology transfer
Citation: us:usc:42:16162
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16162
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter VIII (Hydrogen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the Secretary shall carry out programs that—
(1)
provide for the transfer of critical hydrogen and fuel cell technologies to the private sector;
(2)
accelerate wider application of those technologies in the global market;
(3)
foster the exchange of generic, nonproprietary information; and
(4)
assess technical and commercial viability of technologies relating to the production, distribution, storage, and use of hydrogen energy and fuel cells.
