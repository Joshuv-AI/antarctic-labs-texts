Weak quasi contact metric manifolds and new characteristics of K-contact and Sasakian manifolds
Vladimir Rovenski

Abstract
Quasi-contact metric manifolds (introduced by Y. Tashiro and then studied by several authors) are a natural extension of contact metric manifolds. Weak almost-contact metric manifolds, i.e., where the linear complex structure on the contact distribution is replaced by a nonsingular skew-symmetric tensor, have been defined by the author and R. Wolak. In this paper, we study a weak analogue of quasi-contact metric manifolds. Our main results generalize some well-known theorems and provide new criterions for K-contact and Sasakian manifolds in terms of conditions on the curvature tensor and other geometric objects associated with the weak quasi-contact metric structure. Keywords : quasi contact metric manifold, K-contact manifold, Sasakian manifold. Mathematics Subject Classifications (2010) 53C15, 53C25, 53D15

The following condition is stronger than

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X}\,Q)\,Y=0\quad(X,Y\in\ker\eta).

The exterior derivative of

\displaystyle=({1}/{3})\,\big\{X\,\Phi(Y,Z)+Y\,\Phi(Z,X)+Z\,\Phi(X,Y)

According to Lemma

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X}\,\eta)(QY)-(\nabla_{Y}\,\eta)(QX)+(\nabla_{fX}\,\eta)(fY)-(\nabla_{fY}\,\eta)(fX)=-4\,g(fX,Y).

Conclusions

This paper contains substantial new mathematics that successfully
