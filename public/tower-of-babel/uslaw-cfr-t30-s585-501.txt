30 C.F.R. § 585.501 — What deposits must I submit for a competitively issued lease, ROW grant, or RUE grant?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.501
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For a competitively issued lease or grant, BOEM may require a bid deposit before the auction as established in the FSN.
(b) The provisional winner of a lease must pay the balance of its accepted bid in accordance with § 585.225.
