24 C.F.R. § 92.257 — Equal participation of faith-based organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:92.257
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-92.257
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 92 (Home Investment Partnerships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HUD program requirements in § 5.109 apply to the HOME program, including the requirements regarding disposition and change in use of real property by a faith-based organization.
