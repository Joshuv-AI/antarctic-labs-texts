43 C.F.R. § 3809.553 — May I post a financial guarantee for a part of my operations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3809.553
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3809.553
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes, BLM may authorize you to provide a financial guarantee covering a part of your operations if—
(1) Your operations do not go beyond what is specifically covered by the partial financial guarantee; and
(2) The partial financial guarantee covers all reclamation costs within the incremental area of operations.
(b) BLM will review the amount and terms of the financial guarantee for each increment of your operations at least annually.
