49 C.F.R. § 655.34 — Use following an accident.
Citation: us:cfr:49:655.34
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-655.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 655 (Prevention Of Alcohol Misuse And Prohibited Drug Use In Transit Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employer shall prohibit alcohol use by any covered employee required to take a post-accident alcohol test under § 655.44 for eight hours following the accident or until he or she undergoes a post-accident alcohol test, whichever occurs first.
