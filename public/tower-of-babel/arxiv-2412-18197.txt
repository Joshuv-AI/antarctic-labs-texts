The symbol of the normal operator for the d d -plane transform on the Euclidean space Thanks: Supported by the JSPS Grant-in-Aid for Scientific Research #23K03186.
Hiroyuki Chihara

Abstract
. We directly compute the symbol of the normal operator for the d d -plane transform on the Euclidean space. We show that this symbol is the product of the symbol of the power of the Laplacian of order − d / 2 -d/2 and a constant given by an invariant integral over excess-dimensional spaces. This leads to an alternative derivation of the filtered backprojection formula for the d d -plane transform.
