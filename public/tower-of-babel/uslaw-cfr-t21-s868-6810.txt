21 C.F.R. § 868.6810 — Tracheobronchial suction catheter.
Citation: us:cfr:21:868.6810
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-868.6810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 868 (Anesthesiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A tracheobronchial suction catheter is a device used to aspirate liquids or semisolids from a patient's upper airway.
(b) Classification. Class 1 (general controls). The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to § 868.9.
