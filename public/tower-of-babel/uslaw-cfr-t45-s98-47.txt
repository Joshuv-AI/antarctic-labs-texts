45 C.F.R. § 98.47 — List of providers.
Citation: us:cfr:45:98.47
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-98.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 98 (Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Lead Agency does not have a registration process for child care providers who are unlicensed or unregulated under State, local, or tribal law, it is required to maintain a list of the names and addresses of unlicensed or unregulated providers of child care services for which assistance is provided under this part.
