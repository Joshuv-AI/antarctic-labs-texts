5 U.S.C. § 5352 — Stipends
Citation: us:usc:5:5352
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5352
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 53 (Pay Rates And Systems); Subchapter V (Student-Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of each agency, and the District of Columbia Council with respect to the government of the District of Columbia, shall fix the stipends of its student-employees. The stipend may not exceed the applicable maximum prescribed by the Office of Personnel Management.
