10 C.F.R. § 110.126 — Protection of classified information.
Citation: us:cfr:10:110.126
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-110.126
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 110 (Export And Import Of Nuclear Equipment And Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall relieve any person from safeguarding classified information as required by law and rules, regulations or orders of any Government agency.
