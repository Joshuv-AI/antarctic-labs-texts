7 C.F.R. § 762.104 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:762.104
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-762.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 762 (Guaranteed Farm Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A decision made by the lender adverse to the borrower is not a decision by the Agency, whether or not concurred in by the Agency, and may not be appealed.
(b) The lender or Agency may request updated information from the borrower to implement an appeal decision.
(c) Appeals will be handled in accordance with parts 11 and 780 of this title.
