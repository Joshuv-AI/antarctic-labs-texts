7 U.S.C. § 621 — Machinery belting processed from cotton; exemption from tax
Citation: us:usc:7:621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section621
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 26 (Agricultural Adjustment); Subchapter III (Commodity Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 616 of this title, shall not apply to articles of machinery belting processed wholly or in chief value from cotton, if such processing was completed prior to January 1, 1930.
