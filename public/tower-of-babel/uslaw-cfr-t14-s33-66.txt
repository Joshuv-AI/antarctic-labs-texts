14 C.F.R. § 33.66 — Bleed air system.
Citation: us:cfr:14:33.66
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-33.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 33 (Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The engine must supply bleed air without adverse effect on the engine, excluding reduced thrust or power output, at all conditions up to the discharge flow conditions established as a limitation under § 33.7(c)(11). If bleed air used for engine anti-icing can be controlled, provision must be made for a means to indicate the functioning of the engine ice protection system.
