49 U.S.C. § 21107 — Maximum duty hours and subjects of collective bargaining
Citation: us:usc:49:21107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section21107
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 211 (Hours Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The number of hours established by this chapter that an employee may be required or allowed to be on duty is the maximum number of hours consistent with safety. Shorter hours of service and time on duty of an employee are proper subjects for collective bargaining between a railroad carrier and its employees.
