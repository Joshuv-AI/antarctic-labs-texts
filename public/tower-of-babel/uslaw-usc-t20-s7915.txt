20 U.S.C. § 7915 — Rulemaking
Citation: us:usc:20:7915
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7915
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part F (Uniform Provisions); Subpart 2 (Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall issue regulations under this chapter only to the extent that such regulations are necessary to ensure that there is compliance with the specific requirements and assurances required by this chapter.
