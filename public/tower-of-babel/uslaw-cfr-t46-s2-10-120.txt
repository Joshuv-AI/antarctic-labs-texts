46 C.F.R. § 2.10-120 — -120 Overseas inspection and examination fees.
Citation: us:cfr:46:2.10-120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-2.10-120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 2 (Vessel Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In addition to any other fee required by this subpart, an overseas inspection and examination fee of $4,585 must be paid for each vessel inspection and examination conducted outside the United States and its territories. This fee does not apply to vessel inspections and examinations conducted in Canada, Mexico, or the British Virgin Islands.
(b) The overseas inspection and examination fee for each vessel must be received before an overseas inspection or examination is conducted.
