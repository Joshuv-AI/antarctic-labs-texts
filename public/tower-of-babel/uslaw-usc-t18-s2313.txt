18 U.S.C. § 2313 — Sale or receipt of stolen vehicles
Citation: us:usc:18:2313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2313
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113 (Stolen Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Whoever receives, possesses, conceals, stores, barters, sells, or disposes of any motor vehicle, vessel, or aircraft, which has crossed a State or United States boundary after being stolen, knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.
(b)
For purposes of this section, the term “State” includes a State of the United States, the District of Columbia, and any commonwealth, territory, or possession of the United States.
