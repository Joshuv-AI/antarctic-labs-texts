10 C.F.R. § 862.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 862 (Restrictions On Aircraft Landing And Air Delivery At Department Of Energy Nuclear Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth Department of Energy, hereinafter “DOE”, security policy regarding aircraft and air delivery on nuclear sites under the jurisdiction of DOE pursuant to the Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended (42 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.).
