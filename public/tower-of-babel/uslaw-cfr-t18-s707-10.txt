18 C.F.R. § 707.10 — Scoping.
Citation: us:cfr:18:707.10
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-707.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 707 (Compliance With The National Environmental Policy Act (Nepa)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Scoping will be used to determine the extent of issues to be addressed by the EIS and to identify significant issues related to the proposed action. Scoping will be conducted as described by the CEQ NEPA regulations, §§ 1501.7 and 1508.25.
