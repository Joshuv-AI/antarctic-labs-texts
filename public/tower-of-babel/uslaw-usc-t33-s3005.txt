33 U.S.C. § 3005 — Number of authorized commissioned officers
Citation: us:usc:33:3005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3005
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The total number of authorized commissioned officers on the lineal list of the commissioned officer corps of the Administration shall not exceed 500.
(b) Positions of importance and responsibility
Officers serving in positions designated under section 3028 of this title and officers recalled from retired status or detailed to an agency other than the Administration—
(1)
may not be counted in determining the total number of authorized officers on the lineal list under this section; and
(2)
may not count against such number.
