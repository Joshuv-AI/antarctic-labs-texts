43 C.F.R. § 3281.7 — What documents must a unit operator submit to BLM before we will approve a unit agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3281.7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3281.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After BLM approves a unit area designation, a unit operator must submit the following information in order for BLM to approve a unit agreement:
(a) Documentation of tract commitment (see §§ 3281.8 and 3281.9);
(b) The unit agreement (see § 3281.15);
(c) The map required by § 3281.2(a)(2), if any modifications have occurred since the unit area was designated;
(d) The list required by § 3281.2(a)(3) indicating whether each lease or tract is committed to the unit agreement; and
(e) The plan of development.
