17 C.F.R. § 205.7 — No private right of action.
Citation: us:cfr:17:205.7
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-205.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 205 (Standards Of Professional Conduct For Attorneys Appearing And Practicing Before The Commission In The Representation Of An Issuer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Nothing in this part is intended to, or does, create a private right of action against any attorney, law firm, or issuer based upon compliance or noncompliance with its provisions.
(b) Authority to enforce compliance with this part is vested exclusively in the Commission.
