31 C.F.R. § 50.114 — Data call authority.
Citation: us:cfr:31:50.114
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-50.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 50 (Terrorism Risk Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of determining initial or recalculated PRLPs, Treasury may issue a data call to insurers for insured loss information, seeking information in addition to any information provided to Treasury under subparts F and H of this part.
