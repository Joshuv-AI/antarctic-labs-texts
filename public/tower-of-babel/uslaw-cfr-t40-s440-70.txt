40 C.F.R. § 440.70 — Applicability; description of the nickel ore subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:440.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-440.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 440 (Ore Mining And Dressing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart G are applicable to discharges from (a) mines that produce nickel ore and (b) mills that process nickel ore.
