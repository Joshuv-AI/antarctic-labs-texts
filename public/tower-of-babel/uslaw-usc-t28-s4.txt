28 U.S.C. § 4 — Precedence of associate justices
Citation: us:usc:28:4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section4
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 1 (Supreme Court). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Associate justices shall have precedence according to the seniority of their commissions. Justices whose commissions bear the same date shall have precedence according to seniority in age.
