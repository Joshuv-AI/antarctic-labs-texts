47 U.S.C. § 763c — Space segment capacity of the GMDSS
Citation: us:usc:47:763c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section763c
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 6 (Communications Satellite System); Subchapter VI (Communications Competition And Privatization); Part B (Federal Communications Commission Licensing Criteria: Privatization Criteria). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States shall preserve the space segment capacity of the GMDSS. This section is not intended to alter the status that the GMDSS would otherwise have under United States laws and regulations of the International Telecommunication Union with respect to spectrum, orbital locations, or other operational parameters, or to be a barrier to competition for the provision of GMDSS services.
