26 U.S.C. § 6316 — Payment by foreign currency
Citation: us:usc:26:6316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6316
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter B (Receipt Of Payment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized in his discretion to allow payment of taxes in the currency of a foreign country under such circumstances and subject to such conditions as the Secretary may by regulations prescribe.
