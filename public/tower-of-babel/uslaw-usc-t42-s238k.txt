42 U.S.C. § 238k — Contract authority
Citation: us:usc:42:238k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section238k
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter I (Administration And Miscellaneous Provisions); Part B (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the Secretary to enter into contracts under this chapter shall be effective for any fiscal year only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance by appropriation Acts.
