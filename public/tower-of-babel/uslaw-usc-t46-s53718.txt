46 U.S.C. § 53718 — Annual report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:46:53718
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53718
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 537 (Loans And Guarantees); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall report to Congress annually on the loan guarantee program under this chapter. Each report shall include—
(1)
the size, in dollars, of the portfolio of loans guaranteed;
(2)
the size, in dollars, of projects in the portfolio facing financial difficulties;
(3)
the number and type of projects covered;
(4)
a profile of pending loan applications;
(5)
the amount of appropriations available for new guarantees;
(6)
a profile of each project approved since the last report; and
(7)
a profile of any defaults since the last report.
