24 C.F.R. § 10.12 — Additional rulemaking proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:24:10.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-10.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 10 (Rulemaking: Policy And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may invite interested persons to present oral arguments, appear at informal hearings, or participate in any other procedure affording opportunity for oral presentation of views. The transcript or minutes of such meetings, as appropriate, will be kept and filed in the Rules Docket.
