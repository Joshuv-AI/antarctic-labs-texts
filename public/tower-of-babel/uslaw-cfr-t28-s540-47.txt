28 C.F.R. § 540.47 — Media visits.
Citation: us:cfr:28:540.47
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-540.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 540 (Contact With Persons In The Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requirements for media visits are governed by the provisions on contact with news media (see subpart E of this part). A media representative who wishes to visit outside his or her official duties, however, must qualify as a regular visitor or, if applicable, a special visitor.
