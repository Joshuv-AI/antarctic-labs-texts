2 C.F.R. § 1138.900 — Purpose of SUB Article IX.

Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1138.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1138 (Requirements Related To Subawards: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SUB Article IX addresses national policy requirements that recipients must include in their subaward terms and conditions. It thereby partially implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.331(a)(2).
