41 C.F.R. § 105-10.107 — -10.107 Special benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-10.107
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-10.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-10 (10—Nondiscrimination In Programs Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual shall not be deemed subjected to discrimination by reason of his exclusion from benefits limited by Federal law to individuals of a particular race, color, or national origin different from his.
