20 U.S.C. § 9163 — State and local initiatives
Citation: us:usc:20:9163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9163
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 72 (Museum And Library Services); Subchapter II (Library Services And Technology); Part 3 (Administrative Provisions); Subpart b (Federal Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to interfere with State and local initiatives and responsibility in the conduct of library services. The administration of libraries, the selection of personnel and library books and materials, and insofar as consistent with the purposes of this subchapter, the determination of the best uses of the funds provided under this subchapter, shall be reserved for the States and their local subdivisions.
