5 C.F.R. § 352.804 — Maximum period of entitlement to reemployment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.804
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Entitlement to reemployment terminates at the end of 6 years and 30 days, following the date employment commences in the Institute unless exercised or otherwise terminated before that time as provided in this subpart.
