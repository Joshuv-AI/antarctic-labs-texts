28 U.S.C. § 1697 — Service in multiparty, multiforum actions
Citation: us:usc:28:1697
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1697
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 113 (Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the jurisdiction of the district court is based in whole or in part upon section 1369 of this title, process, other than subpoenas, may be served at any place within the United States, or anywhere outside the United States if otherwise permitted by law.
