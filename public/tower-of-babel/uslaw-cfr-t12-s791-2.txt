12 C.F.R. § 791.2 — Number of votes required for board action.
Citation: us:cfr:12:791.2
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-791.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 791 (Rules Of Ncua Board Procedure; Promulgation Of Ncua Rules And Regulations; Public Observation Of Ncua). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agreement of at least two of the three Board members is required for any action by the Board.
