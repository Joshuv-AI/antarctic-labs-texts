28 C.F.R. § 905.1 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:28:905.1
Creator: National Crime Prevention And Privacy Compact Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-905.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 905 (National Fingerprint File (Nff) Program Qualification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“National Fingerprint File” means a database of fingerprints, or other uniquely personal identifying information, relating to an arrested or charged individual maintained by the FBI to provide positive identification of record subjects indexed in the III System.
