33 C.F.R. § 25.709 — Assessment limitation on claims.
Citation: us:cfr:33:25.709
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-25.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 25 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim is permitted in any amount; however, this subpart prohibits any assessment that exceeds one-half of one month's basic pay against the pay of any offender.
