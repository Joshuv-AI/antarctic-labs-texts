DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-47-2025]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 84; Withdrawal of Notification of 
Proposed Production Activity; Recodeal Energy Inc; (Galvanized Steel); 
Houston, Texas

    Notice is hereby given of the withdrawal of the notification of 
proposed production activity submitted by Recodeal Energy Inc for its 
facilities in Houston, Texas, within FTZ 84. The notification was 
docketed on September 24, 2025 (90 FR 55847, December 4, 2025). The 
withdrawal was requested by Recodeal Energy Inc on February 13, 2026.

    Dated: February 17, 2026.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
