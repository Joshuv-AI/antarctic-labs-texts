10 U.S.C. § 2892b — Prohibition on requirement to disclose personally identifiable information in requests for certain maintenance
Citation: us:usc:10:2892b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2892b
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 169 (Military Construction And Military Family Housing); Subchapter V (Oversight Of Landlords And Protections And Responsibilities For Tenants Of Privatized Military Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A landlord responsible for a housing unit may not require the disclosure of personally identifiable information as a part of the submission of a request for maintenance regarding a housing unit or common area when the disclosure of personally identifiable information is not needed to identify the location at which such maintenance will be performed.
