25 C.F.R. § 170.133 — May a Tribe or BIA use TTP funds as matching funds?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.133
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

TTP funds may be used to meet matching or cost participation requirements for any Federal or non-Federal transit grant or program.
