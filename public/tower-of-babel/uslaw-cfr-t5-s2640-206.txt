5 C.F.R. § 2640.206 — Existing agency exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2640.206
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2640.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2640 (Interpretation, Exemptions And Waiver Guidance Concerning 18 U.S.C. 208 (Acts Affecting A Personal Financial Interest)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who, prior to January 17, 1997, acted in an official capacity in a particular matter in which he had a financial interest, will be deemed to have acted in accordance with applicable regulations if he acted in reliance on an exemption issued by his employing Government agency pursuant to 18 U.S.C. 208(b)(2), as in effect prior to November 30, 1989.
