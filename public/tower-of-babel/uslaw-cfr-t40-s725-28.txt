40 C.F.R. § 725.28 — Notice that submission is not required.
Citation: us:cfr:40:725.28
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-725.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 725 (Reporting Requirements And Review Processes For Microorganisms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When EPA receives a MCAN or exemption request, EPA will review it to determine whether the microorganism is subject to the requirements of this part. If EPA determines that the microorganism is not subject to these requirements, EPA will notify the submitter that section 5 of the Act does not prevent the manufacture, import, or processing of the microorganism and that the submission is not needed.
