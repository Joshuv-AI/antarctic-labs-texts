19 C.F.R. § 4.71 — Inspection of livestock.
Citation: us:cfr:19:4.71
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-4.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 4 (Vessels In Foreign And Domestic Trades). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proper export inspection certificate issued by the Veterinary Services, Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, Department of Agriculture, shall be filed before the clearance of a vessel carrying horses, mules, asses, cattle, sheep, swine, or goats (9 CFR part 91)
