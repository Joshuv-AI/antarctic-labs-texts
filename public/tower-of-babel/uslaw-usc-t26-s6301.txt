26 U.S.C. § 6301 — Collection authority

Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 64 (Collection); Subchapter A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall collect the taxes imposed by the internal revenue laws.
