Adopting Categorical Exclusions Under the National Environmental 
Policy Act; Notice Of Adoption Of Categorical Exclusions Under The 
National Environmental Policy Act

    On July 3, 2025, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. CX25-
2-000, pursuant to section 109 of the National Environmental Policy Act 
(NEPA), 42 U.S.C. 4336c, adopting two categorical exclusions from the 
US Bureau of Reclamation regarding actions at hydropower projects. 
Adopting Categorical Exclusions Under the National Environmental Policy 
Act, 192 FERC ] 61,019 (2025).
    The adoption becomes effective 31 days after the issuance date of 
the Commission's order, i.e., on August 4, 2025.
