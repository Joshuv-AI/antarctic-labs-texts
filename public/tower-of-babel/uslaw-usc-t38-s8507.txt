38 U.S.C. § 8507 — Payment of small shipping charges
Citation: us:usc:38:8507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8507
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 85 (Disposition Of Deceased Veterans’ Personal Property); Subchapter I (Property Left On Department Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon receipt of a proper claim for such property under the provisions of this subchapter the Secretary is hereby authorized, in the Secretary’s discretion and in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Secretary, to pay mailing or shipping charges not to exceed $25 in the case of each deceased veteran as hereinabove defined.
