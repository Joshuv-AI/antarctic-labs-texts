48 C.F.R. § 53.103 — 53.103 Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:53.103
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-53.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 53 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall not (a) alter a standard form prescribed by this regulation, or (b) use for the same purpose any form other than the standard form prescribed by this regulation without receiving in advance an exception to the form.
