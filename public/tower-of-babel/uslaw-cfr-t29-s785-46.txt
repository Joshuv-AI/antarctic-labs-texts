29 C.F.R. § 785.46 — Applicable regulations governing keeping of records.
Citation: us:cfr:29:785.46
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-785.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 785 (Hours Worked). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 11(c) of the Act authorizes the Secretary to promulgate regulations requiring the keeping of records of hours worked, wages paid and other conditions of employment. These regulations are published in part 516 of this chapter. Copies of the regulations may be obtained on request.
