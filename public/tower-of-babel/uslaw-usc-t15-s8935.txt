15 U.S.C. § 8935 — Collaboration
Citation: us:usc:15:8935
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8935
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 115 (Perfluoroalkyl And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances And Emerging Contaminants); Subchapter III (Usgs Performance Standard). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the Director shall collaborate with—
(1)
appropriate Federal and State regulators;
(2)
institutions of higher education;
(3)
research institutions; and
(4)
other expert stakeholders.
