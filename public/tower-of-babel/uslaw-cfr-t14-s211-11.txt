14 C.F.R. § 211.11 — Verification.
Citation: us:cfr:14:211.11
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-211.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 211 (Applications For Permits To Foreign Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications shall be verified and subscribed and sworn to before a Notary Public or other officer authorized to administer oaths in the jurisdiction in which the application is executed. An application verified before a United States consular officer meets the requirements of this section.
