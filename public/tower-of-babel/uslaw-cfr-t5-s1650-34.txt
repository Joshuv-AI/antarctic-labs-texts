5 C.F.R. § 1650.34 — Uniqueness of loans and in-service withdrawals.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1650.34
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1650.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1650 (Methods Of Withdrawing Funds From The Thrift Savings Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An outstanding TSP loan cannot be converted into an in-service withdrawal or vice versa. Funds distributed as an in-service withdrawal cannot be returned or repaid.
