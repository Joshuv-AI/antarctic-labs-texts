25 C.F.R. § 247.4 — How can eligible users be identified?
Citation: us:cfr:25:247.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-247.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 247 (Use Of Columbia River Treaty Fishing Access Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In order to use these sites you must posses an identification card issued by your tribe identifying you as a member of that tribe.
(b) You must exhibit the identification upon request of authorized Federal, State, local or tribal officials.
