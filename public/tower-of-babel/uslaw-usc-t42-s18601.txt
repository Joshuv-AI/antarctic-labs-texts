42 U.S.C. § 18601 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:18601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18601
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 161 (Department Of Energy Research And Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Department
The term “Department” means the Department of Energy.
(2) Director
The term “Director” means the Director of the Office of Science of the Department, except as otherwise indicated.
(3) National Laboratory
The term “National Laboratory” has the meaning given that term in section 15801 of this title.
(4) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
