48 C.F.R. § 736.570 — 736.570 Disability standards in construction contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:736.570
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-736.570
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 736 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers must insert the clause at 752.236-70, Standards for Accessibility for the Disabled in USAID Construction Contracts, in all solicitations and contracts for construction or renovations made using program funds.
