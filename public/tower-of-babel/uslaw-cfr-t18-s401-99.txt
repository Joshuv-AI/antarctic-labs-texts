18 C.F.R. § 401.99 — Suspension or modification of penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:18:401.99
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-401.99
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 401 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may postpone the imposition of a penalty or provide for reconsideration of the penalty amount imposed pending correction of the condition that gave rise to the violation or pending a satisfactory resolution of any adverse consequences that resulted from the violation.
