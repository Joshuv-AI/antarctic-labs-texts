Investigation of the ratio σ r F 2 ​ ( Q 2 / s , Q 2 ) \frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(Q^{2}/s,Q^{2}) in the momentum-space approach
S.Fathinejad, G.R. Boroun

Abstract
We present a calculation of the ratio σ r F 2 ​ ( x , Q 2 ) \frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(x,Q^{2}) in momentum-space approach using the Block-Durand-Ha (BDH) parameterization of the proton structure function F 2 ​ ( x , Q 2 ) F_{2}(x,Q^{2}) . The results are compared with H1 data and extended to high inelasticity. We also examine the ratio σ r F 2 ​ ( Q 2 s , Q 2 ) \frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(\frac{Q^{2}}{s},Q^{2}) obtained at a fixed s \sqrt{s} and Q 2 Q^{2} to the minimum value of x x given by Q 2 / s Q^{2}/s , comparing them with both the HERA data and the color dipole model bounds. These results and comparisons with HERA data demonstrate that the suggested method for the ratio σ r F 2 \frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}} can be applied in analyses of the Large Hadron Collider and Future Circular Collider projects.



I
Introduction

The proton structure functions from the HERA collider summarize
experimental efforts
H1
;
H2
;
H3

observed. These violations are attributed to a high gluon density
within the proton. Measurements of the inclusive deep-inelastic
lepton-nucleon scattering (DIS) cross section have been essential
for testing Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) and provide us with an
understanding of strong interaction dynamics.

The reduced cross section of the measured reaction

e^{+}p{\rightarrow}e^{+}X
depends on the proton structure
functions as:

\displaystyle\sigma_{r}(x,Q^{2}){\equiv}F_{2}(x,Q^{2})-\frac{y^{2}}{1+(1-y)^{2}}F_{L}(x,Q^{2}).

(1)

This dependence is on two independent kinematic variables,
specifically chosen to be
x
and
Q^{2}
, as well as on the center
of mass energy squared
s
, with the inelasticity variable

y=Q^{2}/sx
. The new kinematic region with small values of the
Bjorken variable
x
corresponds to the high energy (or Regge)
limit of QCD extended to the Large Hadron electron Collider (LHeC)

LHeC
. At LHeC, measurements are extended to much lower
values of
x
and high
Q^{2}
with

\sqrt{s}{\simeq}1.3~\mathrm{TeV}
, which is about 4 times the
center-of-mass (COM) energy range of ep collisions at HERA. At low
values of
x
, the DIS structure functions, which follow the
relation
0{\leq}F_{L}{\leq}F_{2}
due to the positivity of the
cross sections for longitudinally and transversely polarized
photons scattering off protons, are defined solely by the singlet
quark
xf_{s}(x,Q^{2})
and gluon density
xf_{g}(x,Q^{2})
as

\displaystyle F_{k}(x,Q^{2})=<e^{2}>\sum_{a=s,g}\bigg[B_{k,a}(x){\otimes}xf_{a}(x,Q^{2})\bigg],~~~k=2,L,

(2)

where
<e^{2}>={\sum_{i=1}^{n_{f}}e_{i}^{2}}/{n_{f}}

{n_{f}}
which
{n_{f}}
denotes the

densities satisfy the renormalization group evolution equations.
The symbol
\otimes
indicates convolution over the variable
x

in the usual form,
f(x){\otimes}g(x)=\int_{x}^{1}\frac{dz}{z}f(z,\alpha_{s})g(x/z)

\displaystyle\frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(x,Q^{2})=1-\frac{y^{2}}{1+(1-y)^{2}}\frac{F_{L}}{F_{2}}(x,Q^{2}),

(3)

\displaystyle\frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(x,Q^{2}){\approx}1,

\displaystyle\frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(Q^{2}/s,Q^{2})|_{y=1}=1-\frac{F_{L}}{F_{2}}(Q^{2}/s,Q^{2}).

(5)

\displaystyle\rho(x,Q^{2})=\frac{\sigma_{T}^{\gamma^{*}p}(W^{2},Q^{2})}{2\sigma_{L}^{\gamma^{*}p}(W^{2},Q^{2})}.

In this paper, we present the ratio
{\sigma_{r}}/{F_{2}}
in a
momentum-space approach using the proton structure function
measurable in deeply inelastic scattering based on the
Block-Durand-Ha (BDH) parameterization
Martin1

applies to large and small

and small

parameterization provides a better fit to experimental data,
particularly at low values of the Bjorken variable

improved fit is crucial for accurately describing the behavior of
the proton structure function in regions where data is sparse.
Additionally, the BDH parameterization aligns well with
theoretical predictions, such as the Froissart bound, which
describes the asymptotic behavior of hadron-hadron cross sections.
By avoiding the need for a specific factorization scheme, the BDH
parameterization simplifies the theoretical calculations involved
in deep inelastic scattering processes, making them more efficient
and accessible.

II
Method

In this paper, the ratio
{\sigma_{r}}/{F_{2}}
is determined
directly in terms of observable quantities, specifically the
proton structure function in DIS utilizing the BDH
parameterization. The structure functions
F_{2}
and
F_{L}
for
deeply inelastic scattering into the Parton Distribution Functions
(PDFs) are defined in Ref.
Lappi
by the following forms

\displaystyle F_{2}(x,Q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle<e^{2}>\bigg\{C^{(0)}_{2,s}+\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg[C^{(1)}_{2,s}-\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}C^{(0)}_{2,s}{\otimes}P_{qq}\bigg]\bigg\}{\otimes}x\Sigma(x,\mu_{r}^{2})

(9)

\displaystyle+2\sum_{i=1}^{n_{f}}e_{i}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg[C^{(1)}_{2,g}-\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}C^{(0)}_{2,g}{\otimes}P_{qg}\bigg]{\otimes}xg(x,\mu_{r}^{2}),

and

\displaystyle F_{L}(x,Q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle<e^{2}>\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg\{C^{(1)}_{L,s}+\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg[C^{(2)}_{L,s}-\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}C^{(1)}_{L,s}{\otimes}P_{qq}-2n_{f}\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}C^{(1)}_{L,g}{\otimes}P_{gq}\bigg]\bigg\}{\otimes}x\Sigma(x,\mu_{r}^{2})

(10)

\displaystyle+2\sum_{i=1}^{n_{f}}e_{i}^{2}\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg\{C^{(1)}_{L,g}+\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg[C^{(2)}_{L,g}-\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}C^{(1)}_{L,s}{\otimes}P_{qg}-\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}C^{(1)}_{L,g}{\otimes}P_{gg}\bigg]\bigg\}{\otimes}xg(x,\mu_{r}^{2})

\displaystyle+<e^{2}>\bigg(\frac{\alpha_{s}(\mu_{r}^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg)^{2}\bigg[b_{0}\ln{\bigg(}\frac{\mu_{r}^{2}}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}\bigg]\bigg[C^{(1)}_{L,s}{\otimes}x\Sigma(x,\mu_{r}^{2})+2n_{f}C^{(1)}_{L,g}{\otimes}xg(x,\mu_{r}^{2})\bigg],

where
C_{ij}(i=2,L;j=s,g)
denotes the scheme-dependent Wilson
coefficient functions. The quark singlet and gluon PDFs can be
expressed in terms of the DIS structure functions, therefore one
of the explicit forms of the evolution equation is reported by the
following form
Lappi
:

\displaystyle\frac{dF_{2}(x,Q^{2})}{d{\ln}Q^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}{2\pi}x\bigg\{\frac{1}{4}\bigg(\frac{2}{x}-\frac{d}{dx}\bigg)\frac{2\pi}{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}F_{L}(x,Q^{2})+\frac{1}{2}\int_{x}^{1}\frac{dz}{z^{2}}\frac{2\pi}{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}F_{L}(z,Q^{2})

(11)

\displaystyle+C_{F}\bigg[\frac{1}{x}F_{2}(x,Q^{2})-2\int_{x}^{1}\frac{dz}{z^{2}}F_{2}(z,Q^{2})+\frac{2}{x}\int_{x}^{1}dz\frac{1}{(1-z)_{+}}F_{2}(\frac{x}{z},Q^{2})\bigg]\bigg\},

with the color factor
C_{F}=4/3
associated with the color group
SU(3). The plus function is defined as

\displaystyle\int_{x}^{1}dz\frac{f(z)}{(1-z)_{+}}=\int_{x}^{1}dz\frac{f(z)-f(1)}{1-z}+f(1)\ln(1-x).

(12)

In Ref.
BH1
, the authors developed a method to obtain the
longitudinal structure function,
F^{BH}_{L}
, in the proton
structure function and its derivative using a Laplace-transform
method detailed in
Martin1
;
Martin3
;
Martin4
;
Martin5
;
Martin6
. Then in Ref.
BH2
, the authors modified the
equation to significantly improve convergence for increasing
numbers of terms in the series. Therefore, the ratio

{\sigma_{r}}/{F_{2}}
is found to be

\displaystyle\frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(x,Q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1-\frac{1}{F^{BDH}_{2}(x,Q^{2})}\bigg\{4\int_{x}^{1}\frac{d{F}^{BDH}_{2}(z,Q^{2})}{d{\ln}Q^{2}}(\frac{x}{z})^{3/2}\bigg[\cos{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{z}{x}{\bigg)}-\frac{\sqrt{7}}{7}\sin{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{z}{x}{\bigg)}\bigg]\frac{dz}{z}-4C_{F}\frac{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}{2\pi}

(13)

\displaystyle{\times}\int_{x}^{1}F^{BDH}_{2}(z,Q^{2})(\frac{x}{z})^{3/2}\bigg[(1.6817+2\psi(1))\cos{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{z}{x}{\bigg)}+(2.9542-2\frac{\sqrt{7}}{7}\psi(1))\sin{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{z}{x}{\bigg)}\bigg]\frac{dz}{z}

\displaystyle+8C_{F}\frac{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg[\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\bigg(\frac{2(m-4)}{m(m^{2}-3m+4)}-\frac{2}{m^{2}}\bigg)\int_{x}^{1}F_{2}^{BDH}(z,Q^{2})(\frac{x}{z})^{m}\frac{dz}{z}

\displaystyle+\int_{x}^{1}F_{2}^{BDH}(z,Q^{2})\bigg({\rm Li}_{2}(\frac{x}{z})-\ln(1-\frac{x}{z})\bigg)\frac{dz}{z}\bigg]\bigg\},

where the maximum value of
m
in the series is chosen to be
approximately
50
, with an accuracy of
1/10^{4}
, which is
sufficient for the present purpose (please refer to the Appendix
in Ref.
BH2
). The structure function
F^{BDH}_{2}(x,Q^{2})

has the following explicit expression:

\displaystyle F^{\mathrm{BDH}}_{2}(x,Q^{2})=D(Q^{2})(1-x)^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{2}A_{m}(Q^{2})L^{m},

(14)

with

\displaystyle D(Q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{Q^{2}(Q^{2}+{\lambda}M^{2})}{(Q^{2}+M^{2})^{2}},~A_{0}=a_{00}+a_{01}L_{2},~A_{i}(Q^{2})=\sum_{k=0}^{2}a_{ik}L_{2}^{k},~(i=1,2),

\displaystyle L=\ln(1/x)+L_{1},~L_{1}={\ln}\frac{Q^{2}}{Q^{2}+\mu^{2}},~L_{2}={\ln}\frac{Q^{2}+\mu^{2}}{\mu^{2}},

where the effective parameters are summarized in
Ref.
Martin1
and are given in Table I.

In the limit where
x_{\mathrm{min}}=Q^{2}/s
and it is indicated
that the longitudinal polarization of the virtual photon at
y=1

is zero, we can conclude that the ratio

\frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(Q^{2}/s,Q^{2})|_{y=1}{\rightarrow}1
.
Therefore, we find that this ratio is defined by the following
form:

\displaystyle\frac{\sigma_{r}}{F_{2}}(Q^{2}/s,Q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1-\frac{1}{F^{BDH}_{2}(Q^{2}/s,Q^{2})}\bigg\{4\int_{Q^{2}/s}^{1}\frac{d{F}^{BDH}_{2}(z,Q^{2})}{d{\ln}Q^{2}}(\frac{Q^{2}}{sz})^{3/2}\bigg[\cos{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{sz}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}-\frac{\sqrt{7}}{7}\sin{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{sz}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}\bigg]\frac{dz}{z}

(16)

\displaystyle-4C_{F}\frac{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}{2\pi}\int_{Q^{2}/s}^{1}F^{BDH}_{2}(z,Q^{2})(\frac{Q^{2}}{sz})^{3/2}\bigg[(1.6817+2\psi(1))\cos{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{sz}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}

\displaystyle+(2.9542-2\frac{\sqrt{7}}{7}\psi(1))\sin{\bigg(}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}{\ln}\frac{sz}{Q^{2}}{\bigg)}\bigg]\frac{dz}{z}+8C_{F}\frac{\alpha_{s}(Q^{2})}{2\pi}\bigg[\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\bigg(\frac{2(m-4)}{m(m^{2}-3m+4)}-\frac{2}{m^{2}}\bigg)

\displaystyle{\times}\int_{Q^{2}/s}^{1}F_{2}^{BDH}(z,Q^{2})(\frac{Q^{2}}{sz})^{m}\frac{dz}{z}+\int_{Q^{2}/s}^{1}F_{2}^{BDH}(z,Q^{2})\bigg({\rm Li}_{2}(\frac{Q^{2}}{sz})-\ln(1-\frac{Q^{2}}{sz})\bigg)\frac{dz}{z}\bigg]\bigg\}.
