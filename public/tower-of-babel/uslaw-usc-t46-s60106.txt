46 U.S.C. § 60106 — State inspection laws
Citation: us:usc:46:60106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section60106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 601 (Arrival And Departure Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When State law requires a certificate of inspection for goods carried on a vessel, a vessel transporting the goods may not be cleared until the certificate is produced.
