5 C.F.R. § 891.105 — Correction of errors.
Citation: us:cfr:5:891.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-891.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 891 (Retired Federal Employees Health Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM may order correction of administrative errors at any time upon a showing satisfactory to OPM that it would be against equity and good conscience not to do so.
