49 C.F.R. § 1554.205 — Nondisclosure of certain information.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1554.205
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1554.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1554 (Aircraft Repair Station Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In connection with the procedures under this subpart, TSA does not disclose classified information, as defined in Executive Order 12968, section 1.1(d), or any successor order, and TSA reserves the right not to disclose any other information or material not warranting disclosure or protected from disclosure under law or regulation.
