46 C.F.R. § 42.07-15 — -15 Zones and seasonal areas.
Citation: us:cfr:46:42.07-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-42.07-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 42 (Domestic And Foreign Voyages By Sea). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A vessel subject to 46 U.S.C. 5101-5116 or the 1966 Convention shall comply, as applicable, with the requirements regarding the zones and seasonal areas described in subpart 42.30.
(b) A port located on the boundary line between two zones or areas shall be regarded as within the zone or seasonal area from or into which the vessel arrives or departs.
