THARANDT, a town of Germany, in the kingdom of Saxony,
romantically situated on the Wilde Weisseritz, 9 m. S.W. of
Dresden, on the Dresden-Reichenbach railway. Pop. (1905)
2967. It has a Protestant church, a hydropathic establishment,
and the oldest academy of forestry in Germany (founded
by Heinrich Cotta in 1811) with about sixty students. Tharandt
is a favourite summer resort of the people of Dresden, one of
its principal charms being the magnificent beech woods which
surround it.

See Donner, Tharandt (Tharandt, 1890).
