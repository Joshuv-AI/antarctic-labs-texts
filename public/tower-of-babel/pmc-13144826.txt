Expression of Concern on ‘Control of small inhibitory RNA levels and RNA interference by doxycycline induced activation of a minimal RNA polymerase III promoter’
Published: 2026

This is an expression of concern about: Lahouari Amar, Mathieu Desclaux, Nicole Faucon-Biguet, Jacques Mallet, Roland Vogel, Control of small inhibitory RNA levels and RNA interference by doxycycline induced activation of a minimal RNA polymerase III promoter, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 34, Issue 5, 1 March 2006, Page e37, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkl034

In November 2025, the Editors were notified of concerns regarding apparent duplications in two figures: phase contrast images in Figure 3A, and actin bands in Figures 4A and 4C. These concerns were also raised on PubPeer (https://pubpeer.com/publications/DF338654AD2C365B16169D040BE3C5).

The Editors have contacted the authors and are investigating all the issues raised in line with COPE guidance; however, at the time of publication, the authors have not responded to the Editors’ correspondence. In the interim, the Editors are publishing this Expression of Concern to alert readers while the outcome of the investigation is pending and advise readers to examine the details of this study with particular care.
