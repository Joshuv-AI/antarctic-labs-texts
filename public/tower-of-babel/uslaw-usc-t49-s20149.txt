49 U.S.C. § 20149 — Coordination with the Department of Labor
Citation: us:usc:49:20149
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20149
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 201 (General); Subchapter II (Particular Aspects Of Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall consult with the Secretary of Labor on a regular basis to ensure that all applicable laws affecting safe working conditions for railroad employees are appropriately enforced to ensure a safe and productive working environment for the railroad industry.
