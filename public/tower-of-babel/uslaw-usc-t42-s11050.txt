42 U.S.C. § 11050 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:11050
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11050
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 116 (Emergency Planning And Community Right-To-Know); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for fiscal years beginning after September 30, 1986, such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter.
