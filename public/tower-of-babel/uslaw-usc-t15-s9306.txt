15 U.S.C. § 9306 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:15:9306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9306
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 118 (Sustainable Chemistry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to alter or amend any State law or action with regard to sustainable chemistry, as defined by the State.
