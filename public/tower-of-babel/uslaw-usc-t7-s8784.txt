7 U.S.C. § 8784 — Assignment of payments
Citation: us:usc:7:8784
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8784
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 113 (Agricultural Commodity Support Programs); Subchapter V (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The provisions of section 590h(g) of title 16, relating to assignment of payments, shall apply to payments made under this chapter.
(b) Notice
The producer making the assignment, or the assignee, shall provide the Secretary with notice, in such manner as the Secretary may require, of any assignment made under this section.
