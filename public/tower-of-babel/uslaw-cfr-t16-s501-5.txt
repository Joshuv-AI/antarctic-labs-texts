16 C.F.R. § 501.5 — Paper table covers, bedsheets, pillowcases.
Citation: us:cfr:16:501.5
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-501.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 501 (Exemptions From Requirements And Prohibitions Under Part 500). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Table covers, bedsheets, and pillowcases, fabricated from paper, are exempt from the requirements of § 500.12 of this chapter which specifies the expression of measurement of bidimensional commodities: Provided, That such commodities shall clearly present their actual length and width in terms of inches.
