48 C.F.R. § 15.000 — 15.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:15.000
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-15.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 15 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes policies and procedures governing competitive and noncompetitive negotiated acquisitions. A contract awarded using other than sealed bidding procedures is a negotiated contract (see 14.101).
