SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is correcting a 
notice that was published in the Federal Register on April 24, 2026, 
regarding Accelerated Decommissioning Partners Crystal River Unit 3, 
LLC; Crystal River Unit 3 Nuclear Generating Plant; License Termination 
Plan, public meeting notice and request for comment. This action is 
necessary to correct the NRC Docket ID.
