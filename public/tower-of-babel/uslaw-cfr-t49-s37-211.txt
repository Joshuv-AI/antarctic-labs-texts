49 C.F.R. § 37.211 — Effect of NHTSA and FHWA safety rules.
Citation: us:cfr:49:37.211
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-37.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 37 (Transportation Services For Individuals With Disabilities (Ada)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OTRB operators are not required to take any action under this subpart that would violate an applicable National Highway Traffic Safety Administration or Federal Highway Administration safety rule.
