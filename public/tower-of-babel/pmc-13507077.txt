Retraction Note: MHF-UIE a multi-task hybrid fusion method for real-world underwater image enhancement
Authors: Jian Xu, Miss Laiha Mat Kiah, Rafidah Md Noor, Lip Yee Por, Yanyong Wu
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-02942-8, published online 24 May 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Following publication, concerns were raised about substantial overlap with previously published work by different authors1. Specifically, the methodological pipeline, formulaic components, and experimental setup appear to be highly similar to this previous study. These concerns were corroborated by post-publication peer review by experts. The Editors therefore consider this Article redundant.

Jian Xu and Lip Yee Por disagree with this retraction. Rafidah Md Noor has not explicitly stated whether they agree or disagree with this retraction. Miss Laiha Mat Kiah and Yanyong Wu did not respond to correspondence from the Editors about this retraction.
