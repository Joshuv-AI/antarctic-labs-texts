22 C.F.R. § 712.210 — Reporting.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 712 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
