22 U.S.C. § 5493 — Administrative expenses of Agency for International Development
Citation: us:usc:22:5493
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5493
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 63 (Support For East European Democracy (Seed)); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of paying administrative expenses incurred in connection with carrying out its functions under this chapter, the Agency for International Development may use up to $500,000 each fiscal year of the funds made available to the Agency under this chapter.
