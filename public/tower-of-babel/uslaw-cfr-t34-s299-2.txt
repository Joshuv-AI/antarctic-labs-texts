34 C.F.R. § 299.2 — What general administrative regulations apply to ESEA programs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:299.2
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-299.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 299 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Title 2 of the CFR, part 200, as adopted at 2 CFR part 3474, applies to all ESEA programs except for title VII programs (Impact Aid) (in addition to any other specific implementing regulations).
