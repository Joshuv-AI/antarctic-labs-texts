22 U.S.C. § 2226 — Reports on international organizations
Citation: us:usc:22:2226
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2226
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part III (International Organizations And Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual reports to the Congress under section 262a of this title, shall be submitted within nine months after the end of the fiscal year to which they relate.
