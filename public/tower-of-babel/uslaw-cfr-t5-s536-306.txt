5 C.F.R. § 536.306 — Limitation on retained rates.
Citation: us:cfr:5:536.306
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-536.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 536 (Grade And Pay Retention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A retained rate may not at any time exceed the rate payable for level IV of the Executive Schedule.
(b) When an employee's retained rate is limited under this section, an agency may not apply this subpart or the provisions of any other law or regulation to the rate of basic pay the employee would have received but for this limitation.
