31 C.F.R. § 316.3 — Governing regulations.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 316 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series E). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in 31 CFR part 315 apply to definitive Series E bonds that have not been converted to book-entry bonds.
(b) The regulations in 31 CFR part 363 apply to definitive Series E bonds that have been converted to book-entry bonds through New Treasury Direct.
