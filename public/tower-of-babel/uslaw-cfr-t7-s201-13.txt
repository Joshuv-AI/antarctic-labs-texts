7 C.F.R. § 201.13 — Lot number or other identification.
Citation: us:cfr:7:201.13
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-201.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 201 (Federal Seed Act Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lot number or other identification shall be shown on the label and shall be the same as that used in the records pertaining to the same lot of seed.
