7 U.S.C. § 3158 — Next generation agriculture technology challenge
Citation: us:usc:7:3158
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3158
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 64 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Teaching); Subchapter III (Agricultural Research And Education Grants And Fellowships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall establish a next generation agriculture technology challenge competition to provide an incentive for the development of innovative mobile technology that removes barriers to entry in the marketplace for beginning farmers and ranchers (as defined in subsection (a) of section 2279 of this title).
(b) Amount
The Secretary may award not more than $1,000,000 in the aggregate to 1 or more winners of the competition under subsection (a).
