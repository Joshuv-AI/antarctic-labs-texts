26 U.S.C. § 7343 — Definition of term “person”
Citation: us:usc:26:7343
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7343
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 75 (Crimes, Other Offenses, And Forfeitures); Subchapter D (Miscellaneous Penalty And Forfeiture Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “person” as used in this chapter includes an officer or employee of a corporation, or a member or employee of a partnership, who as such officer, employee, or member is under a duty to perform the act in respect of which the violation occurs.
