5 C.F.R. § 9701.355 — Setting pay upon movement to a different occupational cluster.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9701.355
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System (Department Of Homeland Security—Office Of Personnel Management)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9701.355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9701 (Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS will issue implementing directives regarding how to set an employee's pay when he or she moves voluntarily or involuntarily to a position in a different occupational cluster, including rules for determining whether such a movement is to a higher or lower band for the purpose of setting pay upon promotion or demotion under §§ 9701.353 and 9701.354, respectively.
