16 U.S.C. § 392b — Conveyance of added lands to United States by Governor
Citation: us:usc:16:392b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section392b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XLI (Hawaii National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Governor of the Territory of Hawaii is authorized to convey to the United States any and all lands and interests in lands acquired by the Territorial Government under the provisions of sections 391b, 391b–1, 392c, 396, 396a of this title.
