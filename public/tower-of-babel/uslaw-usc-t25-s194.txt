25 U.S.C. § 194 — Trial of right of property; burden of proof
Citation: us:usc:25:194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section194
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all trials about the right of property in which an Indian may be a party on one side, and a white person on the other, the burden of proof shall rest upon the white person, whenever the Indian shall make out a presumption of title in himself from the fact of previous possession or ownership.
