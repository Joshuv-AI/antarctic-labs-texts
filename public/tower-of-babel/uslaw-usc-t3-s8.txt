3 U.S.C. § 8 — Manner of voting
Citation: us:usc:3:8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section8
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 1 (Presidential Elections And Vacancies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The electors shall vote for President and Vice President, respectively, in the manner directed by the Constitution.
