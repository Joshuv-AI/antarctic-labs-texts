33 U.S.C. § 3047 — Retired rank and pay held pursuant to other laws unaffected
Citation: us:usc:33:3047
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3047
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter III (Separation And Retirement Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall prevent an officer from being placed on the retired list with the highest rank and with the highest retired pay to which the officer is entitled under any other provision of law.
