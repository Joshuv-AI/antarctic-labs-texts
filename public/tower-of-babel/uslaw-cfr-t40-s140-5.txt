40 C.F.R. § 140.5 — Analytical procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:40:140.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-140.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 140 (Marine Sanitation Device Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining the composition and quality of effluent discharge from marine sanitation devices, the procedures contained in 40 CFR part 136, “Guidelines Establishing Test Procedures for the Analysis of Pollutants,” or subsequent revisions or amendments thereto, shall be employed.
