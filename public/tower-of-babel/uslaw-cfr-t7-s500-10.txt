7 C.F.R. § 500.10 — Pets.
Citation: us:cfr:7:500.10
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-500.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 500 (National Arboretum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pets brought upon USNA property must have proper vaccinations and, except assistance trained animals, must be kept on leash at all times. The release or abandonment of fish, plants, and animals of any kind on USNA grounds is prohibited.
