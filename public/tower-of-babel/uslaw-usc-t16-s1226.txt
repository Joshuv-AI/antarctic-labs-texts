16 U.S.C. § 1226 — Federal agency authority to carry out Federal project within an estuary unaffected
Citation: us:usc:16:1226
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1226
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 26 (Estuarine Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect the authority of any Federal agency to carry out any Federal project heretofore or hereafter authorized within an estuary.
