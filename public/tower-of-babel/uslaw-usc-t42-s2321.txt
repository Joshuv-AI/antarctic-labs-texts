42 U.S.C. § 2321 — Lots; establishment of boundaries
Citation: us:usc:42:2321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2321
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter II (Lots, Appraisals, And Prices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is authorized to plat each community immediately upon passage of this chapter, or immediately upon the inclusion of the community within the provisions of this chapter. The Commission may establish lot boundaries, and realine, divide, or enlarge existing tracts as it deems appropriate.
