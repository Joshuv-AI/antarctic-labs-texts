18 U.S.C. § 1349 — Attempt and conspiracy
Citation: us:usc:18:1349
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1349
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 63 (Mail Fraud And Other Fraud Offenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who attempts or conspires to commit any offense under this chapter shall be subject to the same penalties as those prescribed for the offense, the commission of which was the object of the attempt or conspiracy.
