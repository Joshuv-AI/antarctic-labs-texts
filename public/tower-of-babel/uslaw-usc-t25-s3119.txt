25 U.S.C. § 3119 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:25:3119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3119
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 33 (National Indian Forest Resources Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application of any provision of this chapter to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the application of such provision or circumstance and the remainder of this chapter shall not be affected thereby.
