25 U.S.C. § 2014 — Recruitment of Indian educators
Citation: us:usc:25:2014
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2014
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 22 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall institute a policy for the recruitment of qualified Indian educators and a detailed plan to promote employees from within the Bureau. Such plan shall include opportunities for acquiring work experience prior to actual work assignment.
