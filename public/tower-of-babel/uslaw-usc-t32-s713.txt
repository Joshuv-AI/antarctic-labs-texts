32 U.S.C. § 713 — Official mail: free transmission
Citation: us:usc:32:713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section713
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 7 (Service, Supply, And Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Units and headquarters of the National Guard, whether or not in Federal service, have the same privilege of free mailing of official matter as the Department of Defense.
