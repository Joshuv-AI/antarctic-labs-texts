5 U.S.C. § 5733 — Expeditious travel
Citation: us:usc:5:5733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5733
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 57 (Travel, Transportation, And Subsistence); Subchapter II (Travel And Transportation Expenses; New Appointees, Student Trainees, And Transferred Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The travel of an employee shall be by the most expeditious means of transportation practicable and shall be commensurate with the nature and purpose of the duties of the employee requiring such travel.
