47 C.F.R. § 0.506 — FOIA and Privacy Act requests.
Citation: us:cfr:47:0.506
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-0.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 0 (Commission Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for declassification that are submitted under the provisions of the Freedom of Information Act, as amended, (See § 0.461), of the Privacy Act of 1974, (See § 0.554) shall be processed in accordance with the provisions of those Acts.
