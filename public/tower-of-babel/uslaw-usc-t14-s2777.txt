14 U.S.C. § 2777 — Clothing for destitute shipwrecked individuals
Citation: us:usc:14:2777
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2777
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter III (Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard may furnish clothing and subsistence to destitute shipwrecked individuals, and the Coast Guard may reimburse, in cash or in kind, Coast Guard personnel who furnish clothing and subsistence to destitute shipwrecked individuals.
