26 C.F.R. § 36.3121(l)(10)-4 — (l)(10)-4 Payment of amounts equivalent to tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:36.3121(l)(10)-4
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-36.3121(l)(10)-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 36 (Contract Coverage Of Employees Of Foreign Subsidiaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A domestic corporation which has entered into an agreement as provided in § 36.3121(l)(1)-1 is not required to make deposits with an authorized financial institution of any amount for which liability is incurred under its agreement.
