2 C.F.R. § 1401.120 — Does this part affect the Federal contracts that I receive?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1401.120
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1401.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1401 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It will affect future contract awards indirectly if you are debarred or suspended for a violation of the requirements of this part, as described in § 1401.610(c). However, this part does not directly apply to procurement contracts. The portion of the Drug-Free Workplace Act of 1988 that applies to Federal procurement contracts is carried out through the Federal Acquisition Regulation in 48 CFR part 23, subpart 23.5.
