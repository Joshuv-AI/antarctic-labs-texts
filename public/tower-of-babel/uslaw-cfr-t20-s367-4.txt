20 C.F.R. § 367.4 — Notification to another agency.
Citation: us:cfr:20:367.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-367.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 367 (Recovery Of Debts Owed To The United States Government By Administrative Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Board refers a debt under this part to another agency forcollection by means of administrative offset, the Board shall provide a written certification to the other agency stating that the debtor owes the debt (including the amount) and that the provisions of this part have been fully complied with.
