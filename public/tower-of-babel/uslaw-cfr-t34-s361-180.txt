34 C.F.R. § 361.180 — When is a State subject to a financial sanction under the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act?
Citation: us:cfr:34:361.180
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-361.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 361 (State Vocational Rehabilitation Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State will be subject to financial sanction under WIOA sec. 116(f) if it fails to:
(a) Submit the State annual performance report required under WIOA sec. 116(d)(2); or
(b) Meet adjusted levels of performance for the primary indicators of performance in accordance with sec. 116(f) of WIOA.
