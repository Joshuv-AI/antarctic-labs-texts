36 C.F.R. § 1120.11 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1120.11
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1120.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1120 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
