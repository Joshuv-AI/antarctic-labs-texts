38 U.S.C. § 303 — Secretary of Veterans Affairs
Citation: us:usc:38:303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section303
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 3 (Department Of Veterans Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is a Secretary of Veterans Affairs, who is the head of the Department and is appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate. The Secretary is responsible for the proper execution and administration of all laws administered by the Department and for the control, direction, and management of the Department.
