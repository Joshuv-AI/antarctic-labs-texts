41 C.F.R. § 102-37.385 — -37.385 SASP coordination with GSA.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.385
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.385
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must coordinate with GSA before selling or demanding payment of the fair market or fair rental value of donated property that is:
(a) Subject to any special handling condition or use limitation imposed by GSA; or
(b) Not properly used within 1 year of donation or which ceases to be properly used within 1 year of being placed in use.
