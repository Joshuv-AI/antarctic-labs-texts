46 C.F.R. § 131.920 — Level of manning.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.920
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must carry the personnel required by the Certificate of Inspection, as determined by the cognizant OCMI, based on an evaluation under part 15 of this chapter.
