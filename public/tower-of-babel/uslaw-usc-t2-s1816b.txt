2 U.S.C. § 1816b — Architect of the Capitol, authority for personal services contracts with legal entities
Citation: us:usc:2:1816b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1816b
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Architect of the Capitol is authorized to contract for personal services with any firm, partnership, corporation, association, or other legal entity in the same manner as he is authorized to contract for personal services with individuals under the provisions of section 6101 of title 41.
