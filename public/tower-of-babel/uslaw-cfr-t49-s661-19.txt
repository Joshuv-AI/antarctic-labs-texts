49 C.F.R. § 661.19 — Sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:661.19
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-661.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 661 (Buy America Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A willful refusal to comply with a certification by a successful bidder or offeror may lead to the initiation of debarment or suspension proceedings under part 29 of this title.
