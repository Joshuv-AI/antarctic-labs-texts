25 C.F.R. § 43.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:25:43.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-43.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 43 (Maintenance And Control Of Student Records In Bureau Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains the regulations of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, U.S. Department of the Interior, governing the maintenance, control, and accessibility of student records. This part will apply to all educational institutions under the jurisdiction of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, whether operated under contract or otherwise.
