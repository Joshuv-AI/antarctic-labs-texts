14 U.S.C. § 1942 — Permanent commissioned teaching staff; composition
Citation: us:usc:14:1942
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1942
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 19 (Coast Guard Academy); Subchapter III (Faculty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The permanent commissioned teaching staff at the Academy shall consist of professors, associate professors, assistant professors and instructors, in such numbers as the needs of the Service require. They shall perform duties as prescribed by the Commandant, and exercise command only in the academic department of the Academy.
