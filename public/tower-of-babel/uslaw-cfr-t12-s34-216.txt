12 C.F.R. § 34.216 — Information to be presented to the Appraisal Subcommittee by participating States.
Citation: us:cfr:12:34.216
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-34.216
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 34 (Real Estate Lending And Appraisals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State electing to register AMCs for purposes of permitting AMCs to provide appraisal management services relating to covered transactions in the State must submit to the Appraisal Subcommittee the information required to be submitted by Appraisal Subcommittee regulations or guidance concerning AMCs that operate in the State.
