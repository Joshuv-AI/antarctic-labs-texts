22 U.S.C. § 9624 — Termination

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 103 (Better Utilization Of Investments Leading To Development); Subchapter II (Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The authorities provided under this subchapter terminate on December 31, 2031.
(b) Termination of Corporation
The Corporation shall terminate on the date on which the portfolio of the Corporation is liquidated.
