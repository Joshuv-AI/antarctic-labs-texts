2 U.S.C. § 1102 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:2:1102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1102
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 22 (John C. Stennis Center For Public Service Training And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “Center” means the John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development established under section 1103(a) of this title.
(2)
The term “Board” means the Board of Trustees of the John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development established under section 1103(b) of this title.
(3)
The term “fund” means the John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development Trust Fund provided for under section 1105 of this title.
