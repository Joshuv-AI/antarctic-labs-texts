21 C.F.R. § 821.4 — Imported devices.
Citation: us:cfr:21:821.4
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-821.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 821 (Medical Device Tracking Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, the importer of a tracked device shall be considered the manufacturer and shall be required to comply with all requirements of this part applicable to manufacturers. Importers must keep all information required under this part in the United States.
