40 U.S.C. § 11703 — Procurement procedures
Citation: us:usc:40:11703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section11703
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 117 (Additional Information Resources Management Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the maximum extent practicable, the Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council shall ensure that the process for acquisition of information technology is a simplified, clear, and understandable process that specifically addresses the management of risk, incremental acquisitions, and the need to incorporate commercial information technology in a timely manner.
