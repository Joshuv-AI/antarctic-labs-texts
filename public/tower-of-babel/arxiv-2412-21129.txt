Considering the main term, we conclude

\displaystyle\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)\gg X^{3}c_{f}(\log\log X+\psi_{f}),

\displaystyle 8\pi^{3}\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+24\pi^{3}\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)+8\pi^{3}\rho^{3}\Phi^{\prime\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)\ll X^{4}c_{f}(\log\log X+\psi_{f}).

Thus, for

, there exists a constant
C_{2}
for sufficiently large

, yielding

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1}{3}\mathfrak{w}|\Theta|^{3}(8\pi^{3}\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+24\pi^{3}\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)+8\pi^{3}\rho^{3}\Phi^{\prime\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho))\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{2}\Theta^{2}X^{3}c_{f}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})(\log X)^{-1/4}\leq C_{2}\Theta^{2}X^{3}c_{f}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\pi^{2}\Theta^{2}(\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)).

Hence,

\displaystyle\mathrm{Re}(\Lambda_{f}(\rho,\Theta))\leq\Phi_{f}(\rho)-\pi^{2}\Theta^{2}(\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)).

For
|\Theta|\geq X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}
, there exist a positive constant
C_{3}
such that

\displaystyle\mathrm{Re}(\Lambda_{f}(\rho,\Theta))\leq\Phi_{f}(\rho)-C_{3}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})

Therefore, the contribution of the integrand in (
6.4
) yields

(6.5)

\displaystyle\int_{X^{-3/2}(\log\log X)^{-1}\leq|\Theta|\leq\tau}\exp(\Phi_{f}(\rho e(\Theta)))e(-n\Theta)d\Theta\ll\Psi_{f}(\rho)X^{-C_{3}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})}\ll\Psi_{f}(\rho)n^{-B_{2}}.

Now it remains to deal with the integrand for the interval

\displaystyle\left[-X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1},X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}\right].

For
\Theta
belonging to the above interval we have

\displaystyle|\Theta|^{3}\left(8\pi^{3}\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+12\pi^{3}\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)+8\pi^{3}\rho^{3}\Phi^{\prime\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)\right)

\displaystyle\ll

\displaystyle X^{-\frac{9}{2}}(\log\log X)^{-3}X^{4}\log\log X=X^{-\frac{1}{2}}(\log\log X)^{-2}.

From Lemma
6.1
; recall that
n=x\asymp X^{2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})
. Thus

\displaystyle X^{\frac{1}{2}}(\log\log X)^{-2}=

\displaystyle\left(X^{2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}(\log\log X)^{-2}\gg n(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{\frac{1}{2}}\gg n^{1-\varepsilon}.

Then

\displaystyle|\Theta|^{3}\left(8\pi^{3}\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+12\pi^{3}\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)+8\pi^{3}\rho^{3}\Phi^{\prime\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)\right)\ll n^{-1+\varepsilon},

and

\displaystyle\exp\left(|\Theta|^{3}\left(8\pi^{3}\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+12\pi^{3}\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)+8\pi^{3}\rho^{3}\Phi^{\prime\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)\right)\right)=1+O\left(n^{-1+\varepsilon}\right).

By definition
\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho)=\rho\Phi^{\prime}_{f}(\rho)+\rho^{2}\Phi^{\prime\prime}_{f}(\rho)
. Then we have

\displaystyle\int_{-X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}^{X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}\exp(\Phi_{f}(\rho e(\Theta)))e(-n\Theta)d\Theta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{-X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}^{X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}\exp(\Lambda_{f}(\rho,\Theta))d\Theta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(1+O(n^{-1+\varepsilon}))\Psi_{f}(\rho)\int_{-X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}^{X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}\exp(-2\pi\Theta^{2}\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho))d\Theta.

Recall that
X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho)\gg X^{\varepsilon}
. By performing a standard polar coordinates integration, we obtain

\displaystyle\left(\int_{-X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}^{X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}\exp\left(-\Theta^{2}2\pi^{2}\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho)d\Theta\right)\right)^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho)}\left(1-\exp(-X^{-3}(\log\log X+\psi_{f}))^{-2}2\pi^{2}\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho))\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho)}\left(1+O\left(\frac{1}{\log X}\right)\right).

Therefore,

\displaystyle\int_{-X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}^{X^{-3/2}(\log\log X+\psi_{f})^{-1}}\exp(\Phi_{f}(\rho e(\Theta)))e(-n\Theta)d\Theta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Psi_{f}(\rho)}{\sqrt{2\pi\Phi_{f,(2)}(\rho)}}\left(1+O\left(n^{-1+\varepsilon}\right)\right)\left(1+O\left(\frac{1}{\log X}\right)\right).

Combining the aforementioned expression with (
6.4
) and (

) leads to the completion of the proof of the theorem.
∎
