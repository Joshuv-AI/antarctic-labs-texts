14 C.F.R. § 1266.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1266.100
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1266.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1266 (Cross-Waiver Of Liability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this Part is to ensure that consistent cross-waivers of liability are included in NASA agreements for activities related to the ISS and for NASA's science or space exploration activities unrelated to the ISS that involve a launch.
