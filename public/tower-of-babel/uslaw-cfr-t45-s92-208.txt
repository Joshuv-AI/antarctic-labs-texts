45 C.F.R. § 92.208 — Prohibition on sex discrimination related to marital, parental, or family status.
Citation: us:cfr:45:92.208
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-92.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 92 (Nondiscrimination In Health Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining whether an individual satisfies any policy or criterion regarding access to its health programs or activities, a covered entity must not take an individual's sex, as defined in § 92.101(a)(2), into account in applying any rule concerning an individual's current, perceived, potential, or past marital, parental, or family status.
