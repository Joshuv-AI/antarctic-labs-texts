40 C.F.R. § 246.202-7 — -7 Recommended procedures: Establishment of purchase contract.
Citation: us:cfr:40:246.202-7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-246.202-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 246 (Source Separation For Materials Recovery Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Formal bids should be requested for purchase of the recovered materials, such bids being solicited in conformance with bidding procedures established for the responsible agency. Contracts should include the buyer's quality specifications, transportation agreements, a guarantee that the material will be accepted for one year or more and a guaranteed minimum purchase price.
