48 C.F.R. § 970.2208 — 970.2208 Equal employment opportunity.
Citation: us:cfr:48:970.2208
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-970.2208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 970 (Doe Management And Operating Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The equal employment opportunity provisions of 48 CFR subpart 22.8 and subpart 922.8 of this chapter, including Executive Order 11246 and 41 CFR part 60, are applicable to DOE management and operating contracts.
