21 C.F.R. § 170.245 — Part 5 of a GRAS notice: Experience based on common use in food before 1958.
Citation: us:cfr:21:170.245
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-170.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 170 (Food Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the statutory basis for your conclusion of GRAS status is through experience based on common use in food, in Part 5 of your GRAS notice you must include evidence of a substantial history of consumption of the notified substance for food use by a significant number of consumers prior to January 1, 1958.
