49 C.F.R. § 198.31 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:198.31
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-198.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 198 (Regulations For Grants To Aid State Pipeline Safety Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements parts of the pipeline safety laws (49 U.S.C. 60101 et seq.), which direct the Secretary to require each State to adopt a one-call damage prevention program as a condition to receiving a full grant-in-aid for its pipeline safety compliance program.
