25 C.F.R. § 1200.32 — How often can funds be returned?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1200.32
Creator: Office Of The Special Trustee For American Indians, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1200.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1200 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribes may return all or part of withdrawn funds no more than twice a year, beginning no sooner than six months after date of withdrawal, except with approval of the Secretary.
