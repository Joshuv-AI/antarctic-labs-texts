41 C.F.R. § 109-45.902-2 — -45.902-2 Abandonment or destruction without notice.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-45.902-2
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-45.902-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-45 (45—Sale, Abandonment, Or Destruction Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of the field organization shall designate an official to make the findings justifying abandonment or destruction without public notice of personal property. The OPMO/PA shall review and coordinate on the findings.
