44 C.F.R. § 206.67 — Requirement when limitation is exceeded.
Citation: us:cfr:44:206.67
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-206.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 206 (Federal Disaster Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the limitation described in § 206.66 is exceeded, the Administrator must report to the Congress on the nature and extent of continuing emergency assistance requirements and shall propose additional legislation if necessary.
