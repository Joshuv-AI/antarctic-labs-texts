28 C.F.R. § 74.8 — Notification of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:28:74.8
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-74.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 74 (Civil Liberties Act Redress Provision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall, when funds are appropriated for payment, notify an eligible individual in writing of his or her eligibility for payment. Section 104 of the Act limits any appropriation to not more than $500,000,000 for any fiscal year.
