25 C.F.R. § 514.2 — When will the annual rates of fees be published?
Citation: us:cfr:25:514.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-514.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 514 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission shall adopt the rates of fees no later than November 1st of each year.
(b) The Commission shall publish the rates of fees in a notice in the Federal Register.
