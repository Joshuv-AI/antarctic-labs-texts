28 C.F.R. § 94.111 — Eligible crime victim assistance programs.
Citation: us:cfr:28:94.111
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-94.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 94 (Crime Victim Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SAAs may award VOCA funds only to crime victim assistance programs that meet the requirements of VOCA, at 34 U.S.C. 20103(b)(1), and this subpart. Each such program shall abide by any additional criteria or reporting requirements established by the SAA.
