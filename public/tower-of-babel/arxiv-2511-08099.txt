Combining this with Lemma
5.3
, in the case of even indices, we get

(5.4)

\frac{2\cdot 0.495b^{-0.5}c^{0.5}}{\log(2\cdot 0.495b^{-0.5}c^{0.5}+1)}<6.543\cdot 10^{15}\log^{2}c,

and in the case of odd indices, we get the inequality

(5.5)

\frac{2\cdot 0.0625^{0.5}b^{-0.5}c^{0.25}+1}{\log(2\cdot 0.0625^{0.5}b^{-0.5}c^{0.25}+2)}<6.543\cdot 10^{15}\log^{2}c.

The solutions obtained from these inequalities are summarized in the following lemma.

Lemma 5.4
.

For the
D(4)
-triples
\{a,ka,c_{3}^{\pm}\}
with
a=a_{p}^{(k)}

(p\geq 1)
defined in (
2.4
)-(
2.9
), if
z=v_{2m}=w_{2n}
has a solution
(m,n)
, then
p\leq E_{k}
and

where

for

respectively. However, if

has a solution

and

Now, for the remaining small values of

, by using Lemma

we get that
