20 C.F.R. § 261.8 — Time and place to request review of a revised decision.
Citation: us:cfr:20:261.8
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-261.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 261 (Administrative Finality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party to a revised decision may request, as appropriate, further review of the decision in accordance with the rules set forth in part 260 of this chapter.
