33 U.S.C. § 1484 — Interpretation and administration; other right, duty, privilege, or immunity and other remedy unaffected
Citation: us:usc:33:1484
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1484
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 28 (Pollution Casualties On The High Seas: United States Intervention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall be interpreted and administered in a manner consistent with the convention, the protocol, and other international law. Except as specifically provided, nothing in this chapter may be interpreted to prejudice any otherwise applicable right, duty, privilege, or immunity or deprive any country or person of any remedy otherwise applicable.
