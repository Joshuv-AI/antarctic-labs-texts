10 C.F.R. § 1040.8 — Effect of employment opportunity.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1040.8
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1040.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1040 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Due to limited opportunities in the past, certain protected groups may be underrepresented in some occupations or professions. A recipient's obligation to comply with this part is not alleviated by use of statistical information which reflects limited opportunities in those occupations or professions.
