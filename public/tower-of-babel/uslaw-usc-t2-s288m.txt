2 U.S.C. § 288m — Contingent fund
Citation: us:usc:2:288m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section288m
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9D (Office Of Senate Legal Counsel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The expenses of the Office shall be paid from the contingent fund of the Senate in accordance with section 6503 of this title, and upon vouchers approved by the Counsel.
