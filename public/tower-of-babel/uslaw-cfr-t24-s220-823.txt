24 C.F.R. § 220.823 — Claim computation; items deducted.
Citation: us:cfr:24:220.823
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-220.823
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 220 (Mortgage Insurance And Insured Improvement Loans For Urban Renewal And Concentrated Development Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the lender is to receive payment in cash, there shall be deducted from the total of the added items in § 220.822 the following:
(a) Any balance of the loan not advanced to the borrower;
(b) Any cash held by the lender or its agents or to which it is entitled; including deposits made for the account of the borrower and which have not been applied in reduction of the principal obligation under the note and security instrument or instruments.
