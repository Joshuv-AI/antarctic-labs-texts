31 C.F.R. § 592.302 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:31:592.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-592.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 592 (Rough Diamonds Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term effective date refers to the effective date of the applicable prohibitions and directives contained in this part as follows:
(a) With respect to all provisions of this part except for § 592.301(a)(3), 12:01 a.m., eastern daylight time, July 30, 2003; and
(b) With respect to § 592.301(a)(3), September 23, 2004.
