Correction: LncRNA H19 promotes the differentiation of bovine skeletal muscle satellite cells by suppressing Sirt1/FoxO1
Authors: Xiaochun Xu, Shengyue Ji, Weili Li, Bao Yi, Hengxin Li, Hongfu Zhang, Wenping Ma
Published: 2026

Correction: Cellular & Molecular Biology Letters (2017) 22: 10

https://doi.org/10.1186/s11658-017-0040-6

In this article [1], the wrong figure appeared in 3 H, 3I, 4 F, 4G the figure should displayed as shown below.

Incorrect Fig. 3:

Correct Fig. 3:

Knockdown of H19 suppressed the differentiation of bovine tibia skeletal muscle (sk-muscle) satellite and C2C12 cells. a The immunofluorescence results for C2C12 cells and satellite cells on day 4 of differentiation. b The silencing efficiency of pLenti-H19. Cells with pLenti-NTC were the negative control and wild-type cells were the blank control (control). c The differentiation rate of C2C12 cells and the satellite cells on days 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5. (d, e, f and g). Detection of myoblast marker and myoblast inhibitory genes based on the protein level after H19 was silenced by pLenti-H19 vector transfection in bovine sk-muscle satellite cells (d, e) and C2C12 cells (f, g). h, i RT-PCR results for bovine sk-muscle satellite cells (h) and C2C12 cells (i) after H19 was silenced by pLenti-H19 vector transfection. j, k. mRNA level of bovine sk-muscle satellite cells (j) and C2C12 cells (k) after H19 was silenced by pLenti-H19 vector transfection. pLenti-NTC was the negative control. The blank controlwas the mRNA of satellite and C2C12 cells without any treatment, with cultivation for the same number of days. GAPDH was internal control. *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01

Incorrect Fig. 4:

Correct Fig. 4:

Sirt1/FoxO1 overexpression neutralized myoblast inhibition by H19. a The overexpression efficiency of H19. Cells without H19 transfection (scrambled) were the negative control and wild-type cells were the blank control (control). The protein levels of MyoG, MyHC, Sirt1 and FoxO1 in bovine tibia skeletal muscle (sk-muscle) satellite cells (b and d) and C2C12 cells (c and e) with H19 overexpression. f, g RT-PCR results for bovine sk-muscle satellite cells (f) and C2C12 cells (g) with H19 overexpression. h, i Real-time qPCR results for bovine sk-muscle satellite cells (h) and C2C12 cells (i) with H19 overexpression. The cells transfected with the vector without H19 (scrambled) were the control and GAPDH was the internal control. The protein and mRNA abundance was normalized to the GAPDH gene, n = 3, *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01
