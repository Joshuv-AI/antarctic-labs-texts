37 C.F.R. § 233.1 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 233 (Limitation On Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes rules pertaining to the management of the Copyright Claims Board's (Board's) docket and prevention of abuse of the Board's proceedings.
