38 U.S.C. § 1513 — Veterans 65 years of age and older
Citation: us:usc:38:1513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1513
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 15 (Pension For Non-Service-Connected Disability Or Death Or For Service); Subchapter II (Veterans’ Pensions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall pay to each veteran of a period of war who is 65 years of age or older and who meets the service requirements of section 1521 of this title (as prescribed in subsection (j) of that section) pension at the rates prescribed by section 1521 of this title and under the conditions (other than the permanent and total disability requirement) applicable to pension paid under that section.
(b)
If a veteran is eligible for pension under both this section and section 1521 of this title, pension shall be paid to the veteran only under section 1521 of this title.
