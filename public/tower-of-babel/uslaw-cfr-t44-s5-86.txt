44 C.F.R. § 5.86 — Records involved in litigation or other judicial process.
Citation: us:cfr:44:5.86
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-5.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 5 (Production Or Disclosure Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpoenas duces tecum issued pursuant to litigation or any other adjudicatory proceeding in which the United States is a party shall be referred to the Chief Counsel.
