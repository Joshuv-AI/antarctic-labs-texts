5 U.S.C. § 5521 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:5:5521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5521
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 55 (Pay Administration); Subchapter III (Advancement, Allotment, And Assignment Of Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter—
(1)
“agency” means—
(A)
an Executive agency;
(B)
the judicial branch;
(C)
the Library of Congress;
(D)
the Government Publishing Office; and
(E)
the government of the District of Columbia;
(2)
“employee” means an individual employed in or under an agency;
(3)
“head of each agency” means—
(A)
the Director of the Administrative Office of the United States Courts with respect to the judicial branch; and
(B)
the Mayor of the District of Columbia with respect to the government of the District of Columbia; and
(4)
“United States”, when used in a geographical sense, means the several States and the District of Columbia.
