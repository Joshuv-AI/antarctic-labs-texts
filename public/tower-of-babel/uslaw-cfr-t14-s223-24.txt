14 C.F.R. § 223.24 — Transportation of empty mail bags.
Citation: us:cfr:14:223.24
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-223.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 223 (Free And Reduced-Rate Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any carrier authorized to engage in foreign air transportation may transport in foreign air transportation empty air mail bags from any country to the country of origin of such bags, free of charge, on a voluntary space-available basis.
