Abstract
The Tree Augmentation Problem (TAP) is given a tree T = ( V , E T ) T=(V,E_{T}) and additional set of links E E on V × V V\times V , find F ⊆ E F\subseteq E such that T ∪ F T\cup F is 2 2 -edge-connected, and | F | |F| is minimum. The problem is APX-hard [ 29 ] even in if links are only between leaves [ 29 ] . The best known approximation ratio for TAP is 1.393 1.393 , due to Traub and Zenklusen [ 6 ] J. ACM, 2025 using the relative greedy technique [ 43 ] . We introduce a new technique called the deferred local ratio technique . In this technique, the disjointness of the local-ratio primal-dual type does not hold. The technique applies Set Cover problem under certain conditions (see Section 4 ). We use it provide a 4 / 3 4/3 approximation algorithm for TAP. It is possible this technique will find future applications. The running time is O ⁡ ( m ⋅ n ) O(m\cdot\sqrt{n}) time [ 33 ] , [ 42 ] .
