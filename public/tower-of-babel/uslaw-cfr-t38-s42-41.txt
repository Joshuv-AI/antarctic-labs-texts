38 C.F.R. § 42.41 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:38:42.41
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-42.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 42 (Standards Implementing The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the Secretary.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the Secretary.
