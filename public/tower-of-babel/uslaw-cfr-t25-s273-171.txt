25 C.F.R. § 273.171 — Can a contractor make changes to a program approved by an Indian Education Committee?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.171
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No program contracted may be changed from the time of its original approval by the Indian Education Committee to the end of the contract period without the prior approval, in writing, of the Indian Education Committee.
