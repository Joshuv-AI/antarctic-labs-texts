47 C.F.R. § 97.7 — Control operator required.
Citation: us:cfr:47:97.7
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-97.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 97 (Amateur Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When transmitting, each amateur station must have a control operator. The control operator must be a person:
(a) For whom an amateur operator/primary station license grant appears on the ULS consolidated licensee database, or
(b) Who is authorized for alien reciprocal operation by § 97.107 of this part.
