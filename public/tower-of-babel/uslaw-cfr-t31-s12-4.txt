31 C.F.R. § 12.4 — Distribution of free samples of tobacco products prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:31:12.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-12.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 12 (Restriction Of Sale And Distribution Of Tobacco Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The distribution of free samples of tobacco products in or around any Federal building under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Treasury is prohibited, except in areas designated pursuant to § 12.5 of this part.
