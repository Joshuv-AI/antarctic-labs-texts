30 C.F.R. § 90.210 — Status change reports.
Citation: us:cfr:30:90.210
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-90.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 90 (Mandatory Health Standards—Coal Miners Who Have Evidence Of The Development Of Pneumoconiosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is a change in the status of a part 90 miner (such as entering a terminated, injured, or ill status, or returning to work), the operator shall report the change in the status of the part 90 miner to the MSHA District Office or to any other MSHA office designated by the District Manager. Status changes shall be reported in writing or by electronic means within 3 working days after the status change has occurred.
