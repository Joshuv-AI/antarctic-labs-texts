12 C.F.R. § 1073.309 — Interest, penalties, and administrative costs.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1073.309
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1073.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1073 (Procedures For Bureau Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the Bureau is the creditor agency, it shall assess interest, penalties, and administrative costs pursuant to the procedures set forth in § 1073.206 and in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3717 and 31 CFR parts 900 through 904.
