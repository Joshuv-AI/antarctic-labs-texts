48 C.F.R. § 941.201-71 — 941.201-71 Use of subcontracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:941.201-71
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-941.201-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 941 (Acquisition Of Utility Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Utility services for the furnishing of electricity, gas (natural or manufactured), steam, water and/or sewerage at facilities owned or leased by DOE shall not be acquired under a subcontract arrangement, except as provided for at 970.4102-1 or if the prime contract is with a utility company.
