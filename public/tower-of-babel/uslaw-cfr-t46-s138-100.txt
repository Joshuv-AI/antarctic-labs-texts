46 C.F.R. § 138.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:138.100
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-138.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 138 (Towing Safety Management System (Tsms)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe requirements for owners or managing operators of towing vessels who adopt a Towing Safety Management System (TSMS) under this subchapter.
