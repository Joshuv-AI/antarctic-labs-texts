16 C.F.R. § 1117.8 — Effect of reports on liability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1117.8
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1117.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1117 (Reporting Of Choking Incidents Involving Marbles, Small Balls, Latex Balloons And Other Small Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A report by a manufacturer, distributor, retailer, or importer under this part shall not be interpreted, for any purpose, as an admission of liability or of the truth of the information contained in the report.
