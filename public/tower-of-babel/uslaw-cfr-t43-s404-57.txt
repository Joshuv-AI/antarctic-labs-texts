43 C.F.R. § 404.57 — Does this rule have any affect on state water law?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.57
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Neither the Act nor this rule preempts or affects state water law or any interstate compact governing water. Reclamation will comply with state water laws in carrying out this rule.
