10 U.S.C. § 1092a — Persons entering the armed forces: baseline health data
Citation: us:usc:10:1092a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1092a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 55 (Medical And Dental Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Program Required.—
The Secretary of Defense shall carry out a program—
(1)
to collect baseline health data from each person entering the armed forces, at the time of entry into the armed forces; and
(2)
to provide for computerized compilation and maintenance of the baseline health data.
(b) Purposes.—
The program under this section shall be designed to achieve the following purposes:
(1)
To facilitate understanding of how subsequent exposures related to service in the armed forces affect health.
(2)
To facilitate development of early intervention and prevention programs to protect health and readiness.
