48 C.F.R. § 911.600 — 911.600 Scope of subpart.

Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 911 (Describing Agency Needs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements and supplements 48 CFR part subpart 11.6, Priorities and Allocations, and implements the regulations and procedures of the Defense Priorities and Allocations System (DPAS) in solicitations and contracts in support of authorized national defense programs. (See 15 CFR part 700).
