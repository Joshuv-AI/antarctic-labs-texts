29 C.F.R. § 541.702 — Exempt and nonexempt work.
Citation: us:cfr:29:541.702
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-541.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 541 (Defining And Delimiting The Exemptions For Executive, Administrative, Professional, Computer And Outside Sales Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “exempt work” means all work described in §§ 541.100, 541.101, 541.200, 541.300, 541.301, 541.302, 541.303, 541.304, 541.400 and 541.500, and the activities directly and closely related to such work. All other work is considered “nonexempt.”
