36 C.F.R. § 1250.4 — Who can file a FOIA request?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1250.4
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1250.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1250 (Nara Records Subject To Foia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual, partnership, corporation, association, or public or private organization other than a Federal agency, regardless of nationality, may file a FOIA request with NARA. The Administrative Procedure Act, 5 U.S.C. 551(2), excludes Federal agencies from filing FOIA requests. However, state and local governments may file FOIA requests.
