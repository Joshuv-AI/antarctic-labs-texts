Deformed Bivariate q q -Appell Polynomials
Ronald Orozco López

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce bivariate polynomial sets of deformed q q -Appell type, and we study the algebraic properties of these sets. We show the relation between deformed bivariate q q -Appell polynomials and deformed homogeneous polynomials. Next, we give some of their characterizations and algebraic structure. Then, we introduce the deformed q q -Appell operators and obtain Mehler’s and Rogers-type formulas of quasi- q q -Appell polynomials. Finally, some examples of polynomial sequences of deformed q q -Appell type are given: Bernoulli, Euler, and Genocchi types.

Definition 1
.

Let
\alpha
be an arbitrary complex number. The
u
-deformed
q
-Appell polynomials of order
\alpha
are defined by

(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t))^{\alpha}\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx,u)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x;u)\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!},

(10)

where the determining function is

(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t))^{\alpha}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}^{(\alpha)}\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!},

(11)

with
a_{0}^{(\alpha)}\neq 0,\ \mathcal{A}_{q}(0)\neq 0.

Theorem 1
.

\displaystyle\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x;u)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{n-k}{2}}a_{k}^{(\alpha)}x^{n-k}.

(12)

Proof.

\displaystyle(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t))^{\alpha}\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx,u)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}^{(\alpha)}\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{x^{n}t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{n-k}{2}}a_{k}^{(\alpha)}x^{n-k}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}

∎

At
x=0
,
\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(0;u)=a_{n}^{(\alpha)}
.

Definition 2
.

\alpha

Theorem 2
.

For all
\alpha\in\mathbb{C}

\displaystyle\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{n-k}{2}}\mathrm{P}_{k,q}^{(\alpha)}(x)y^{n-k}.

(15)

\displaystyle\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}\mathrm{P}_{k,q}^{(\alpha)}(y;u)x^{n-k}.

(16)

Proof.

From Theorem
1

\displaystyle(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t))^{\alpha}\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx)\mathrm{e}_{q}(ty,u)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}^{(\alpha)}\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{y^{n}t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}a_{k}^{(\alpha)}x^{n-k}\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{y^{n}t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{y^{n}t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{n-k}{2}}\mathrm{P}_{k,q}^{(\alpha)}(x)y^{n-k}\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)\frac{t^{n}}{[n]_{q}!}.

Therefore, Eq.(
15

(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t))^{\alpha}\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx)\mathrm{e}_{q}(ty,u)=(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t)^{\alpha}\mathrm{e}_{q}(ty,u))\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx).

∎

From above theorem,
\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(0,x;u)=\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x;u)

\displaystyle\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}a_{k}^{(\alpha)}\mathrm{R}_{n-k}(x,y;u|q).

Proof.

\displaystyle(\mathcal{A}_{q}(t))^{\alpha}\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx)\mathrm{e}_{q}(ty,u)

Theorem 4

\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)=\mathrm{T}(yD_{q}|u)\{\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x)\}

Theorem 5

\mathrm{T}(yD_{q}|u)\{\mathcal{A}_{q}^{\alpha}(t)\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx)\}=\mathcal{A}_{q}^{\alpha}(t)\mathrm{e}_{q}(tx)\mathrm{e}_{q}(ty,u).

\displaystyle D_{q,x}\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)

Theorem 7

\displaystyle D_{q,x}^{k}\mathrm{P}_{n,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u)

\displaystyle=\frac{[n]_{q}!}{[n-k]_{q}!}\mathrm{P}_{n-k,q}^{(\alpha)}(x,y;u).
