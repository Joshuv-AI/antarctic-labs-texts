49 C.F.R. § 37.181 — Applicability dates.
Citation: us:cfr:49:37.181
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-37.181
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 37 (Transportation Services For Individuals With Disabilities (Ada)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all private entities that operate OTRBs. The requirements of the subpart begin to apply to large operators beginning October 30, 2000 and to small operators beginning October 29, 2001.
