45 C.F.R. § 261.55 — Under what circumstances will we reduce the amount of the penalty for not properly imposing penalties on individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:45:261.55
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-261.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 261 (Ensuring That Recipients Work). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We will reduce the amount of the penalty based on the degree of the State's noncompliance.
(b) In determining the size of any reduction, we will consider objective evidence of:
(1) Whether the State has established a control mechanism to ensure that the grants of individuals are appropriately reduced for refusing to engage in required work; and
(2) The percentage of cases for which the grants have not been appropriately reduced.
