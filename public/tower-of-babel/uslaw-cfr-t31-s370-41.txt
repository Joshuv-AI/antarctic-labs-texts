31 C.F.R. § 370.41 — What limitations exist on liability?
Citation: us:cfr:31:370.41
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-370.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 370 (Electronic Transactions And Funds Transfers Relating To United States Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In no instance does our liability extend beyond the amount of the transaction.
