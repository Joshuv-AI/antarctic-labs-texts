7 U.S.C. § 6524 — Organically produced food
Citation: us:usc:7:6524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6524
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 94 (Organic Certification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a food certified under the national organic program established under the Organic Foods Production Act of 1990 (7 U.S.C. 6501 et seq.), the certification shall be considered sufficient to make a claim regarding the absence of bioengineering in the food, such as “not bioengineered”, “non-GMO”, or another similar claim.
