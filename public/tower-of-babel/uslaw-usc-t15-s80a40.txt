15 U.S.C. § 80a–40 — Hearings by Commission
Citation: us:usc:15:80a–40
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80a–40
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter I (Investment Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Hearings may be public and may be held before the Commission, any member or members thereof, or any officer or officers of the Commission designated by it, and appropriate records thereof shall be kept.
