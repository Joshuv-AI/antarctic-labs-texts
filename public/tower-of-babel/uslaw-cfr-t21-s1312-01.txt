21 C.F.R. § 1312.01 — Scope of part 1312.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1312.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1312.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1312 (Importation And Exportation Of Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures governing the importation, exportation, transshipment and intransit shipment of controlled substances pursuant to section 1002, 1003, and 1004 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 952, 953, and 954) are governed generally by those sections and specifically by the sections of this part.
