22 U.S.C. § 1464 — Voice of America/Europe
Citation: us:usc:22:1464
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1464
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter V (Dissemination Abroad Of Information About The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As part of its duties and programs under this subchapter, Voice of America/Europe shall—
(1)
target news and features in accordance with the findings and recommendations of the Young European Survey;
(2)
conduct periodic audience evaluations and measurements; and
(3)
promote and advertise Voice of America/Europe.
