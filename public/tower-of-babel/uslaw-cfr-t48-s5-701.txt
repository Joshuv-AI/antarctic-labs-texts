48 C.F.R. § 5.701 — 5.701 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:48:5.701
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-5.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 5 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes posting requirements for presolicitation and award notices for actions funded in whole or in part by the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (Pub. L. 111-5) (Recovery Act). The requirements of this subpart enhance transparency to the public.
