12 C.F.R. § 238.103 — Filing requirement.

Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-238.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 238 (Savings And Loan Holding Companies (Regulation Ll)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Filing. A subsidiary savings association of a savings and loan holding company must file a notice with the appropriate Reserve Bank on the designated form at least 30 days before the proposed declaration of a dividend by its board of directors.
(b) Schedules. A notice may include a schedule proposing dividends over a specified period, not to exceed 12 months.
