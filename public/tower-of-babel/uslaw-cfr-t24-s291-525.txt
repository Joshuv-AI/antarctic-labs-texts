24 C.F.R. § 291.525 — Eligible teachers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:291.525
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-291.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 291 (Disposition Of Hud-Acquired And -Owned Single Family Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person qualifies as a teacher for the purposes of the GNND Sales Program if the person is:
(a) Employed as a full-time teacher by a state-accredited public school or private school that provides direct services to students in grades pre-kindergarten through 12; and
(b) The full-time employment in paragraph (a) of this section must, in the normal course of business, serve students from the locality where the home is located.
