41 C.F.R. § 301-71.1 — -71.1 Purpose of agency travel accounting system.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-71.1
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-71.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-71 (71—Agency Travel Accountability Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency travel accounting system is designed to:
(a) Pay authorized and allowable travel expenses of employees.
(b) Provide standard data necessary for managing official travel.
(c) Ensure comprehensive accounting for all travel and transportation expenses related to official travel.
