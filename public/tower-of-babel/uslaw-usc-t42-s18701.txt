42 U.S.C. § 18701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:18701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18701
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 162 (Energy Infrastructure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division:
(1) Department
The term “Department” means the Department of Energy.
(2) Indian Tribe
The term “Indian Tribe” has the meaning given the term in section 5304 of title 25.
(3) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
