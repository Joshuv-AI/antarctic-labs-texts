46 U.S.C. § 70109 — Notifying foreign authorities
Citation: us:usc:46:70109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section70109
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 701 (Port Security); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Unless the Secretary finds that a port in a foreign country maintains effective antiterrorism measures, the Secretary shall notify the appropriate authorities of the government of the foreign country of the finding and recommend the steps necessary to improve the antiterrorism measures in use at the port.
(b) Training Program.—
The Secretary, in cooperation with the Secretary of State, shall operate a port security training program for ports in foreign countries that are found under section 70108 to lack effective antiterrorism measures.
