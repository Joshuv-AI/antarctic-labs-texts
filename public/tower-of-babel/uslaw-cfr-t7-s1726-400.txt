7 C.F.R. § 1726.400 — Final contract amendment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1726.400
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1726.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1726 (Electric System Construction Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As needed, a final contract amendment will be prepared and processed in accordance with § 1726.24(b) prior to or in conjunction with the closeout of the contract.
