A Note on Lower Bounds of Concurrence for Arbitrary Dimensional Bipartite Quantum States
Zhi-Bo Chen, Shao-Ming Fei

Abstract
Quantification of quantum entanglement plays a crucial role in the study of quantum information tasks. We present analytical lower bounds for both concurrence and 2-concurrence based on the correlation matrices of bipartite quantum states. Compared with other related lower bounds, our approach provides a better estimation of the entanglement, particularly for states with large purity. Keywords: concurrence, entanglement measure, q-concurrence



Quantum entanglement is a remarkable property of quantum systems, serving as fundamental physical resources in quantum information processing, including quantum cryptography, quantum teleportation and quantum computation

However, most entanglement measures have no explicit analytical formulas for general quantum mixed states, due to the optimization involved in the definition with convex roof extension.

Based on the generalized Bloch representation, the concurrence of a pure state is given by the Frobenius norm of the correlation matrix.

Theorem 1

For any bipartite pure state
\ket{\psi}\in\mathbb{C}^{d_{1}}\otimes\mathbb{C}^{d_{2}}
, we have

\displaystyle C(\ket{\psi})

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\frac{2(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{d_{1}+d_{2}}\left({\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right)},

\displaystyle C_{2}(\ket{\psi})

\displaystyle=\frac{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{d_{1}+d_{2}}\left({\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right),

where
T
is correlation matrix of
\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}
.

Proof

For a pure state
\rho=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}
, from Lemma 1 and Eq.(
3
) we have

\displaystyle|R|^{2}+|S|^{2}+{\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}=\frac{d_{1}d_{2}-1}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}.

(4)

Since
Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})=Tr({\rho_{B}}^{2})
for pure state
QCQI
, by Lemma 1 we have

|R|^{2}=\frac{d_{1}Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})-1}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)},~\ |S|^{2}=\frac{d_{2}Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})-1}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}.

From Eq.(
4
) we have

\displaystyle{\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1

\displaystyle=\frac{d_{1}d_{2}-1}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}-|R|^{2}-|S|^{2}-1

\displaystyle=\frac{d_{1}d_{2}-1-(d_{1}Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})-1)-(d_{2}Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})-1)-(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}

\displaystyle=\frac{d_{1}+d_{2}}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}\left(1-Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})\right).

Therefore,
1-Tr({\rho_{A}}^{2})=\frac{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{d_{1}+d_{2}}\left({\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right)
, which proves the theorem.
\Box

For general bipartite mixed states, we have the following conclusion.

Theorem 2

For any bipartite state
\rho\in\mathbb{C}^{d_{1}}\otimes\mathbb{C}^{d_{2}}
, we have

C(\rho)\geq\frac{\sqrt{2}(\sqrt{K+1}+1)}{K}\left(\left\|T\right\|_{F}-1\right),

C_{2}(\rho)\geq\frac{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{d_{1}+d_{2}}\left({\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right),

where
T
is the correlation matrix of
\rho
,
K=\frac{d_{1}+d_{2}}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}
.

Proof

Since
\sqrt{2}>C(\ket{\psi})\geq 0
, by Theorem 1 we have
2>\frac{2(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{d_{1}+d_{2}}\left({\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right)\geq 0
. Hence, the correlation matrix of a pure state always satisfies

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{d_{1}+d_{2}}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}+1}>\left\|T\right\|_{F}\geq 1.

(5)

It is easy to verify that if
a\geq x\geq 1
, then
\sqrt{x^{2}-1}\geq\sqrt{\frac{a+1}{a-1}}(x-1)
. Take
a=\sqrt{K+1}
, where
K=\frac{d_{1}+d_{2}}{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}
, by (
5

\displaystyle\sqrt{{\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1}\geq\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{K+1}+1}{\sqrt{K+1}-1}}(\left\|T\right\|_{F}-1).

From Theorem 1 we have

\displaystyle C(\ket{\psi})=\sqrt{\frac{2}{K}\left({\left\|T\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right)}\geq\sqrt{\frac{2}{K}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{K+1}+1}{\sqrt{K+1}-1}}(\left\|T\right\|_{F}-1).

(6)

Next consider the optimal decomposition
\rho=\sum_{i}P_{i}\ket{\varphi_{i}}\bra{\varphi_{i}}
for the concurrence. Using (
6

\displaystyle C(\rho)=\sum_{i}P_{i}C(\ket{\varphi_{i}})

\displaystyle\geq\sqrt{\frac{2(\sqrt{K+1}+1)}{K(\sqrt{K+1}-1)}}\sum_{i}P_{i}\left(\left\|T_{i}\right\|_{F}-1\right)

\displaystyle\geq\sqrt{\frac{2(\sqrt{K+1}+1)}{K(\sqrt{K+1}-1)}}\left(\left\|T\right\|_{F}-1\right)=\frac{\sqrt{2}(\sqrt{K+1}+1)}{K}\left(\left\|T\right\|_{F}-1\right),

where
T_{i}
is correlation matrix of the state
\ket{\varphi_{i}}\bra{\varphi_{i}}

For the second inequality in Theorem 2, consider the optimal decomposition
\rho=\sum_{i}P_{i}\ket{\psi_{i}}\bra{\psi_{i}}

\displaystyle C_{2}(\rho)=\sum_{i}P_{i}C_{2}(\ket{\psi_{i}})=\frac{(d_{1}-1)(d_{2}-1)}{d_{1}+d_{2}}\sum_{i}P_{i}\left({\left\|T_{i}\right\|_{F}}^{2}-1\right),

\Box
