29 C.F.R. § 417.23 — Report to the Director.
Citation: us:cfr:29:417.23
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-417.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 417 (Procedure For Removal Of Local Labor Organization Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following completion of the hearing and vote, the Director's Representative shall file a report with the Director setting out the results of the balloting; and pertinent details of the hearing and vote. Notice thereof shall be given to the membership of such labor organization promptly and copies shall be furnished to all interested parties.
