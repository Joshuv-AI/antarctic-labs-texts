45 C.F.R. § 1225.14 — Consolidation of complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1225.14
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1225.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1225 (Member And Volunteer Discrimination Complaint Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The EEOP Director may consolidate the complaint if it involves the same or sufficiently similar allegations as those contained in a previous complaint filed on behalf of the same class which is pending in the agency or which had been resolved or decided by the agency.
