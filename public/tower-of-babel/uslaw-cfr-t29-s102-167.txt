29 C.F.R. § 102.167 — Prior provision of rights with respect to debt.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.167
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.167
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that the rights of the debtor in relation to the same debt have been previously provided for under some other statutory or regulatory authority, the Agency is not required to duplicate those efforts before effecting administrative offset.
