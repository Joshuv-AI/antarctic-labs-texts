41 C.F.R. § 101-25.110-2 — -25.110-2 Tires obtained through Federal Supply Schedules or regional term contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-25.110-2
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-25.110-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-25 (25—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When tire manufacturers ship tires direct against orders placed under Federal Supply Schedules, the tire manufacturer will record the name and address of the purchaser and the identification numbers of the tires involved.
