14 C.F.R. § 303.03 — Requirement to file application.
Citation: us:cfr:14:303.03
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-303.03
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 303 (Review Of Air Carrier Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who seeks approval of a section 41309 transaction must file the application with the Docket Operations Office, M-30, U.S. Department of Transportation, 1200 New Jersey Avenue SE, Washington, DC 20590, or by electronic submission at http://www.regulations.gov. The application must conform to the requirements set forth in §§ 303.04 and 303.05.
