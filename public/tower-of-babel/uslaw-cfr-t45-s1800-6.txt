45 C.F.R. § 1800.6 — Request for correction or amendment to the record.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1800.6
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1800.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1800 (Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The individual should submit a request to the Deputy Executive Secretary of the Foundation which states the individual's desire to correct or to amend his or her record. This request is to be made in accord with the provisions of § 1800.4.
