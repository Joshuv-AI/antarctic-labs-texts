24 C.F.R. § 234.274 — Certificate of tax assessment.
Citation: us:cfr:24:234.274
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-234.274
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 234 (Condominium Ownership Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The mortgagee shall certify, as of the date of filing for record of the deed or assignment of the mortgage to the Commissioner, that the family unit is assessed and subject to assessment for taxes pertaining only to that unit.
