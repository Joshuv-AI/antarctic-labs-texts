20 C.F.R. § 368.5 — Free tobacco samples.
Citation: us:cfr:20:368.5
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-368.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 368 (Prohibition Of Cigarette Sales To Minors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The distribution of free samples of tobacco products is prohibited in or around Federal property occupied and maintained by the Railroad Retirement Board.
