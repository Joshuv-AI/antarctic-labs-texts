42 U.S.C. § 5189b — Date of eligibility; expenses incurred before date of disaster
Citation: us:usc:42:5189b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5189b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV (Major Disaster Assistance Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Eligibility for Federal assistance under this subchapter shall begin on the date of the occurrence of the event which results in a declaration by the President that a major disaster exists; except that reasonable expenses which are incurred in anticipation of and immediately preceding such event may be eligible for Federal assistance under this chapter.
