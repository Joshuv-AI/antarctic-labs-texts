6 C.F.R. § 15.49 — Program accessibility; discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:6:15.49
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-15.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 15 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Department Of Homeland Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 15.50, no qualified individual with a disability shall, because the Department's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with a disability, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the Department.
