14 U.S.C. § 2181 — Physical fitness of officers
Citation: us:usc:14:2181
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2181
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe regulations under which the physical fitness of officers to perform their duties shall be periodically determined.
