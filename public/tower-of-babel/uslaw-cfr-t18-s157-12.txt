18 C.F.R. § 157.12 — Dismissal of application.
Citation: us:cfr:18:157.12
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-157.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 157 (Applications For Certificates Of Public Convenience And Necessity And For Orders Permitting And Approving Abandonment Under Section 7 Of The Natural Gas Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for good cause shown, failure of an applicant to go forward on the date set for hearing and present its full case in support of its application will constitute ground for the summary dismissal of the application and the termination of the proceedings.
