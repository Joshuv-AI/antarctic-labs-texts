25 C.F.R. § 224.114 — How may the Tribe address a petition in its written response?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.114
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to responding to the petitioner's claims, the Tribe may also:
(a) Include its interpretation of relevant provisions of the TERA and other legal requirements;
(b) Discuss whether the petitioner is an interested party;
(c) State whether the petitioner has exhausted Tribal remedies, and if so, how; and
(d) Propose to cure or otherwise resolve the claims within the time frame in § 224.113(b).
