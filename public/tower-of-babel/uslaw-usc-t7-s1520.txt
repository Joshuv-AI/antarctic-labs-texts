7 U.S.C. § 1520 — Producer eligibility
Citation: us:usc:7:1520
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1520
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 36 (Crop Insurance); Subchapter I (Federal Crop Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this subchapter, a producer shall not be denied insurance under this subchapter if—
(1)
for purposes of catastrophic risk protection coverage, the producer is a “person” (as defined by the Secretary); and
(2)
for purposes of any other plan of insurance, the producer is 18 years of age and has a bona fide insurable interest in a crop as an owner-operator, landlord, tenant, or sharecropper.
