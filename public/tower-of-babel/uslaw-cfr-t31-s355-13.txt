31 C.F.R. § 355.13 — Are there any additional requirements related to fiscal agency checks?
Citation: us:cfr:31:355.13
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-355.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 355 (Regulations Governing Fiscal Agency Checks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may require an indemnification agreement, with or without surety. You must provide any additional evidence we consider necessary. We will require any information necessary for the protection of the interests of the United States.
