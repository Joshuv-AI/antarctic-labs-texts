31 C.F.R. § 16.42 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:31:16.42
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-16.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 16 (Regulations Implementing The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 3805 of title 31, United States Code, authorizes judicial review by an appropriate United States District Court of a final decision of the authority head imposing penalties or assessments under this part and specifies the procedures for such review.
