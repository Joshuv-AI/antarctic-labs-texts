November 18, 2025.
    On the dates indicated in Table 1 below, New York Stock Exchange 
LLC, NYSE American LLC, NYSE Arca, Inc., NYSE National, Inc., and NYSE 
Texas, Inc. filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission 
(``Commission''), pursuant to Section 19(b)(1) of the Securities 
Exchange Act of 1934 (``Act'') \1\ and Rule 19b-4 thereunder,\2\ the 
proposed rule changes described in Table 1 concerning hardware 
procurement services and managed services in those exchanges' co-
location facilities.

NYSENAT-2025-19, and SR-NYSETEX-2025-28) are deemed to have been
