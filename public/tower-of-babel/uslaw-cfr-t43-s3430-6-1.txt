43 C.F.R. § 3430.6-1 — -1 Lease terms.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3430.6-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3430.6-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3430 (Noncompetitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each preference right lease shall be subject to the terms provided for Federal coal leases established in part 3470 of this title.
