12 C.F.R. § 1209.10 — Authority of the Director.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1209.10
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1209.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1209 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may, at any time during the pendency of a proceeding, perform, direct the performance of, or waive performance of any act that could be done or ordered by the presiding officer.
