7 C.F.R. § 1430.417 — Assignments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1430.417
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1430.417
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1430 (Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any producer may assign a payment to be made under this subpart in accordance with part 1404 of this chapter or successor regulations as designated by the Secretary or as allowed by the Deputy Administrator in writing.
