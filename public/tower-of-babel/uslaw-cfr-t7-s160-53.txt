7 C.F.R. § 160.53 — Stopping inspection by suspended inspector.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.53
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An accredited processor upon receipt of notice of the suspension or revocation of a license shall discontinue the use of the licensee as an official inspector, and the marking of his products to indicate official inspection, until the suspension is lifted, or another inspector is licensed or assigned.
