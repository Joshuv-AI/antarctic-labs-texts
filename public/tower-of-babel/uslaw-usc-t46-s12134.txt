46 U.S.C. § 12134 — Evidentiary uses of documentation
Citation: us:usc:46:12134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12134
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 121 (Documentation Of Vessels); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A certificate of documentation is—
(1)
conclusive evidence of nationality for international purposes, but not in a proceeding conducted under the laws of the United States;
(2)
conclusive evidence of qualification to engage in a specified trade; and
(3)
not conclusive evidence of ownership in a proceeding in which ownership is in issue.
