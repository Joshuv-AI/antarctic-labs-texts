49 C.F.R. § 596.9 — General description.
Citation: us:cfr:49:596.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-596.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 596 (Automatic Emergency Braking Test Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The vehicle test device provides a sensor representation of a passenger motor vehicle.
(b) The rear view of the vehicle test device contains representations of the vehicle silhouette, a rear window, a high-mounted stop lamp, two taillamps, a rear license plate, two rear reflex reflectors, and two tires.
