43 U.S.C. § 1577 — Existing Federal laws not modified
Citation: us:usc:43:1577
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1577
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32A (Colorado River Basin Salinity Control); Subchapter I (Programs Downstream From Imperial Dam). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to modify the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 [42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.], the Federal Water Pollution Control Act, as amended [33 U.S.C. 1251 et seq.], or, except as expressly stated herein, the provisions of any other Federal law.
