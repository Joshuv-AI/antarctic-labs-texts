31 C.F.R. § 101.4 — Extraction of gold bullion from the counterfeit coins.
Citation: us:cfr:31:101.4
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-101.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 101 (Mitigation Of Forfeiture Of Counterfeit Gold Coins). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the petition is approved, the Assistant Secretary shall then forward the gold coins to the Bureau of the Mint where, if economically feasible, the gold bullion will be extracted from the counterfeit coins. The Bureau of the Mint will then return the bullion to the Assistant Secretary.
