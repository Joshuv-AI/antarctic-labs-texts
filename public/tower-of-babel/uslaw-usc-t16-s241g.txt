16 U.S.C. § 241g — Change in name of Theodore Roosevelt National Memorial Park
Citation: us:usc:16:241g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section241g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXVI (Theodore Roosevelt National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The area formerly known as the “Theodore Roosevelt National Memorial Park”, established by the Act of April 25, 1947 (61 Stat. 52) [16 U.S.C. 241 et seq.], shall henceforth be known as the “Theodore Roosevelt National Park”.
