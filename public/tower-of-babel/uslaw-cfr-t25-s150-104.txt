25 C.F.R. § 150.104 — How does the LTRO maintain the record of title?
Citation: us:cfr:25:150.104
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-150.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 150 (Record Of Title To Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The LTRO maintains the record of title electronically. However, certain title documents may exist only as physical copies and not electronically.
