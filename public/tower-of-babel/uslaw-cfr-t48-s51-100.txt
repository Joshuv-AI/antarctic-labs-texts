48 C.F.R. § 51.100 — 51.100 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:51.100
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-51.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 51 (Use Of Government Sources By Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for the use of Government supply sources (see 51.102(c)) by contractors. In this subpart, the terms contractors and contracts include subcontractors and subcontracts.
