5 C.F.R. § 837.501 — Refund of retirement deductions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:837.501
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-837.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 837 (Reemployment Of Annuitants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A reemployed annuitant who separates from reemployment without title to either a supplemental annuity or a redetermined annuity under this subpart is entitled to have any retirement deductions withheld from pay during the period of reemployment refunded without interest.
