34 U.S.C. § 40705 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:34:40705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40705
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 407 (Dna Identification); Subchapter I (Collection And Analysis Of Samples). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Attorney General to carry out this Act (including to reimburse the Federal judiciary for any reasonable costs incurred in implementing such Act, as determined by the Attorney General) such sums as may be necessary.
