7 C.F.R. § 1436.18 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1436.18
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1436.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1436 (Farm Storage Facility Loan Program Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appeal, reconsideration, or review of all determinations made under this part, except for provisions for which there are no appeal rights because they are determined rules of general applicability, must be in accordance with parts 11 and 780 of this title.
