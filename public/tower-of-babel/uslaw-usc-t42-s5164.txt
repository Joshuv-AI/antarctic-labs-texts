42 U.S.C. § 5164 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:42:5164
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5164
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter III (Major Disaster And Emergency Assistance Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may prescribe such rules and regulations as may be necessary and proper to carry out the provisions of this chapter, and may exercise, either directly or through such Federal agency as the President may designate, any power or authority conferred to the President by this chapter.
