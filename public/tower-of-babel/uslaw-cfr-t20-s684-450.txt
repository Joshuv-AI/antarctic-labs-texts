20 C.F.R. § 684.450 — How will supplemental youth services be provided?
Citation: us:cfr:20:684.450
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-684.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 684 (Indian And Native American Programs Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) INA program grantees may offer supplemental services to youth throughout the school year, during the summer vacation, and/or during other breaks during the school year at their discretion.
(b) The Department encourages INA program grantees to work with local educational agencies to provide academic credit for youth activities whenever possible.
(c) INA program grantees may provide participating youth with the activities referenced in § 684.310(e).
