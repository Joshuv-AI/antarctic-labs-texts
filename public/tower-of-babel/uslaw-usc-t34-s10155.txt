34 U.S.C. § 10155 — Rules
Citation: us:usc:34:10155
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10155
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter V (Bureau Of Justice Assistance Grant Programs); Part A (Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall issue rules to carry out this part. The first such rules shall be issued not later than one year after the date on which amounts are first made available to carry out this part.
