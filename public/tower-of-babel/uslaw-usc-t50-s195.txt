50 U.S.C. § 195 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:50:195
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section195
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 12 (Vessels In Territorial Waters Of United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this Act:
(1) United states.—
The term “United States” includes all territory and waters, continental or insular, subject to the jurisdiction of the United States.
(2) Territorial waters.—
The term “territorial waters of the United States” includes all waters of the territorial sea of the United States as described in Presidential Proclamation 5928 of December 27, 1988.
