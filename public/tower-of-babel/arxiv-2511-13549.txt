Precision tests of bulk entanglement: A ​ d ​ S 3 AdS_{3} vectors.
Rayirth Bhat a , Justin R. David a , Semanti Dutta b

Abstract
We consider single particle excitations of the massive Chern-Simons field of mass M M in A ​ d ​ S 3 AdS_{3} and evaluate their contribution at the first sub-leading order in G N G_{N} to the entanglement entropy across the Ryu-Takayanagi surface. Quantizing the Chern-Simons field in A ​ d ​ S 3 AdS_{3} , we evaluate the corrections to the holographic entanglement entropy using the Faulkner-Lewkowycz-Maldacena formula. The massive Chern-Simons field also obeys the equations of motion of a massive vector in A ​ d ​ S 3 AdS_{3} . The lowest energy single particle excitation of this field is dual to the primary operator of conformal dimensions M + 1 M+1 with spin one in the dual CFT, all other single particle excitations are dual to its global descendants. We compare the result for the entanglement entropy from the Faulkner-Lewkowycz-Maldacena formula to the single interval entanglement entropy in large charge holographic CFT obtained using the replica trick for the primary and its tower of holomorphic descendants. The two results agree precisely in the leading and sub-leading terms of the short interval expansion. On taking the massless limit the result coincides with the contribution of a U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) current to the single interval entanglement entropy.

3.1

The action of the massive Chern-Simons theory in

\displaystyle S=\int d^{3}x{\cal L}=-\int d^{3}x\sqrt{-g}\left(\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho}A_{\mu}\partial_{\nu}A_{\rho}+MA_{\mu}A^{\mu}\right).

\displaystyle\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho}=\frac{\tilde{\epsilon}^{\mu\nu\rho}}{\sqrt{-g}},\qquad\tilde{\epsilon}^{t\rho\varphi}=1.

Here we have chosen

It is clear that the Chern-Simons term is independent of the metric.

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-(1+r^{2})dt^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{1+r^{2}}+r^{2}d\varphi^{2}.

\displaystyle\epsilon^{\mu\alpha\beta}\partial_{\alpha}A_{\beta}=-MA^{\mu}.

(26)

\displaystyle\frac{g_{tt}}{\sqrt{-g}}\Big(\partial_{r}A_{\varphi}-\partial_{\varphi}A_{r}\Big)=-MA_{t},

(27)

\displaystyle\frac{g_{rr}}{\sqrt{-g}}\Big(\partial_{\varphi}A_{t}-\partial_{t}A_{\varphi}\Big)=-MA_{r},

\displaystyle\frac{g_{\varphi\varphi}}{\sqrt{-g}}\Big(\partial_{t}A_{r}-\partial_{r}A_{t}\Big)=-MA_{\varphi}.

Conclusions

We have quantized the massive Chern-Simons fields both in global
AdS_{3}
and Rindler BTZ.
We have also evaluated the Bogoliubov coefficients relating the creation(annihilation) operators of these fields in
global
AdS_{3}
to that in the BTZ.
Using the results of this quantization, we extended the approach of

Belin:2018juv
;

to obtain the entanglement entropy
of perturbative vector excitations in

.
We considered the massive Chern-Simons fields which is dual to the a state of spin

and conformal dimensions

.
We showed that the quantum corrections to the entanglement entropy of a single interval given by the FLM formula precisely agrees with that evaluated in large

CFT.

On taking the massless limit we showed that the leading and sub-leading contribution to the entanglement entropy
using the FLM formula coincides with the exact expression obtained using methods of topological
Chern-Simons theory in the bulk

.
We should point out that those methods relied on using edge modes on the ‘entanglement cut’ or the
Ryu-Takayanagi surface to evaluate
S_{\rm bulk}
.
Our approach is different, we turn on the mass. The massive Chern-Simons theory does not
have gauge symmetry and therefore there is no issue of super-selection sectors or edge modes
as in the case theories with gauge symmetry.
We perform the calculation with the mass and in the end set the mass to zero.
The fact that the leading terms agree with that of the methods in
Belin:2019mlt
of dealing with the massless Chern-Simons
theory is satisfying. We also mention that the analysis in this paper was done with

of the massive Chern-Simons field will be dual to the primary of dimension

.

In fact this approach of turning on the mass, to break gauge symmetry and evaluate entanglement without having to
worry of edge modes or superselection sectors is interesting.
It is worth re-examining the calculations of the entanglement entropy of the Maxwell field as well as the graviton
across a spherical surface in
d=4

done in
Soni:2016ogt
;
David:2022jfd
with this approach and check if on taking the massless limit, the coefficient of the logarithmic term
coincides with or without the inclusion of the edge modes.

Finally in
