7 C.F.R. § 1700.103 — Eligible communities.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1700.103
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1700.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1700 (General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible community is a community that:
(a) Is located on Trust land;
(b) May be served by an RUS administered program; and
(c) Is determined by the Administrator as having a high need for benefits of an eligible program.
