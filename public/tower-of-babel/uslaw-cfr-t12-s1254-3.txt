12 C.F.R. § 1254.3 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1254.3
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1254.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1254 (Validation And Approval Of Credit Score Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, each time period begins on the day after the relevant event occurs (e.g., the day after a submission is made) and continues through the last day of the relevant period. When the last day is a Saturday, Sunday, or Federal holiday, the period runs until the end of the next business day.
