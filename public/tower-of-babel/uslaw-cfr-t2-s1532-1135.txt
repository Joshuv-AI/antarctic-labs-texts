2 C.F.R. § 1532.1135 — Does CAA or CWA disqualification mean that I must remain ineligible?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1532.1135
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1532.1135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1532 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must remain ineligible until the EPA debarring official certifies that the condition giving rise to your conviction has been corrected. If you desire to have your disqualification terminated, you must submit a written request for reinstatement to the EPA debarring official and support your request with persuasive documentation. For information about the process for reinstatement see §§ 1532.1205 and 1532.1300.
