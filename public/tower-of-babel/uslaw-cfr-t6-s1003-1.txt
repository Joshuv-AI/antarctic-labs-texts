6 C.F.R. § 1003.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:6:1003.1
Creator: Privacy And Civil Liberties Oversight Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-1003.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 1003 (Implementation Of The Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this part implement the provisions of the Sunshine Act.
(b) Requests for all records other than those described in § 1003.9, shall be governed by the Board's Freedom of Information Act procedures at 6 CFR part 1001.
