30 U.S.C. § 1322 — Fellowship recipients
Citation: us:usc:30:1322
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1322
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 25 (Surface Mining Control And Reclamation); Subchapter IX (Energy Resource Graduate Fellowships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Recipients of fellowships under this subchapter shall be—
(a)
persons who have been accepted by an institution of higher education for graduate study leading to an advanced degree or for a professional degree, and
(b)
persons who plan a career in the field of energy resources, production, or utilization.
