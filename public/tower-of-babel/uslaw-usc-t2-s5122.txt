2 U.S.C. § 5122 — Personal services in office of Speaker; payments
Citation: us:usc:2:5122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5122
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 51 (House Of Representatives Leadership); Subchapter II (The Speaker). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be paid from the applicable accounts of the House of Representatives until otherwise provided by law, for personal services in the office of the Speaker of the House, an additional basic sum of $10,000 per annum.
