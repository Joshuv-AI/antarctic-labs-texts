42 U.S.C. § 292 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:292
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section292
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter V (Health Professions Education); Part A (Student Loans); Subpart i (Insured Health Education Assistance Loans To Graduate Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subpart is to enable the Secretary to provide a Federal program of student loan insurance for students in (and certain former students of) eligible institutions (as defined in section 292o of this title).
