24 C.F.R. § 50.21 — Aggregation.
Citation: us:cfr:24:50.21
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-50.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 50 (Protection And Enhancement Of Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Activities which are geographically related and are logical parts of a composite of contemplated HUD projects shall be evaluated together.
