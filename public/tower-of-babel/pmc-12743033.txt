Correction: Prediction models for different types of leukemia: a systematic review and critical appraisal
Authors: Ayizhati Tuerxun, Yingzi Yang, Xinqi Cai, Xinyu Chen, Zhuoya Zhao, Yang Zhao, Zinuo Lin, Shengfeng Wang
Published: 2025

Correction to: Journal of Cancer Research and Clinical Oncology (2025) 151:268 10.1007/s00432-025-06314-7

In this article, the order in which the authors appeared in the author list was incorrectly given as "Yingzi Yang, Ayizhati Tuerxun, Xinqi Cai, Xinyu Chen, Zhuoya Zhao, Yang Zhao, Zinuo Lin & Shengfeng Wang" where it should have been “Ayizhati Tuerxun, Yingzi Yang, Xinqi Cai, Xinyu Chen, Zhuoya Zhao, Yang Zhao, Zinuo Lin & Shengfeng Wang”.

The original article has been corrected.
