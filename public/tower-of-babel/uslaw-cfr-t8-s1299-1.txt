8 C.F.R. § 1299.1 — Use of immigration forms.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1299.1
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1299.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1299 (Immigration Review Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to forms prepared by the Executive Office for Immigration Review, the Executive Office for Immigration Review, immigration judges, the Board of Immigration Appeals, and administrative law judges use forms listed under 8 CFR chapter I, part 299.
