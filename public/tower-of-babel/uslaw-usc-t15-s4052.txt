15 U.S.C. § 4052 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:4052
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4052
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 66 (Promotion Of Export Trade); Subchapter III (Export Promotion Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Department of Commerce to carry out export promotion programs such sums as are necessary for fiscal years 1995 and 1996.
