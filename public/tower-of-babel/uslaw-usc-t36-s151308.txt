36 U.S.C. § 151308 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:36:151308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151308
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1513 (National Fallen Firefighters Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall have a designated agent to receive service of process for the corporation.
