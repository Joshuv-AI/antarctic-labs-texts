40 U.S.C. § 8165 — Services for Office of Personnel Management
Citation: us:usc:40:8165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8165
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 81 (Administrative); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For carrying out the work of the Director of the Office of Personnel Management and the examinations provided for in sections 3304 and 3305 of title 5, the Administrator of General Services shall—
(1)
assign or provide suitable and convenient rooms and accommodations, which are furnished, heated, and lighted, in Washington, D.C.;
(2)
supply necessary stationery and other articles; and
(3)
arrange for or provide necessary printing.
