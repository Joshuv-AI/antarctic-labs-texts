35 U.S.C. § 211 — Relationship to antitrust laws
Citation: us:usc:35:211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section211
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 18 (Patent Rights In Inventions Made With Federal Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to convey to any person immunity from civil or criminal liability, or to create any defenses to actions, under any antitrust law.
