24 C.F.R. § 103.35 — Is there a time limit on when I can file?
Citation: us:cfr:24:103.35
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-103.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 103 (Fair Housing—Complaint Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, you must notify us within one year that you are a victim of discrimination. If you indicate that there is more than one act of discrimination, or that the discrimination is continuing, we must receive your information within one year of the last incident of discrimination.
