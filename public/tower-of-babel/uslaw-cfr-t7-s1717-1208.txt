7 C.F.R. § 1717.1208 — Government's rights under loan documents.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1717.1208
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1717.1208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1717 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Electric Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart limits, modifies, or otherwise affects the rights of the government under loan documents executed with borrowers, or under law or equity.
