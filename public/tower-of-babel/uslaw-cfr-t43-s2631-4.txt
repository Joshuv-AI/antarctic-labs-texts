43 C.F.R. § 2631.4 — Patents.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2631.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2631.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2630 (Railroad Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If all be found regular and in conformity with the governing law and regulations, patent shall be issued in the name of the grantee under the railroad grant, the carrier paying the costs of preparation and issuance of the patent.
