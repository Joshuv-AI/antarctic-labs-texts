2 C.F.R. § 930.120 — Nonprocurement debarment and suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:2:930.120
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-930.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 930 (Other Transaction Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Nonprocurement debarment and suspension requirements in 2 CFR part 180, as adopted and supplemented by 2 CFR part 901, are applicable to all other transaction agreements.
