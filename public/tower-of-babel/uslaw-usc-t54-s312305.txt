54 U.S.C. § 312305 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:54:312305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section312305
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3123 (Commission For The Preservation Of America’S Heritage Abroad). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after the end of each fiscal year, the Commission shall transmit to the President a report that includes—
(1)
a detailed statement of the activities and accomplishments of the Commission during the fiscal year; and
(2)
any recommendations of the Commission for legislation and administrative actions.
