15 U.S.C. § 8116 — Authorization of appropriations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 107 (Protection Of Intellectual Property Rights); Subchapter I (Coordination And Strategic Planning Of Federal Effort Against Counterfeiting And Infringement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) 1 In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
