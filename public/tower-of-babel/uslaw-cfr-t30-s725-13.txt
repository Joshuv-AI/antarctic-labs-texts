30 C.F.R. § 725.13 — Amount of grants.
Citation: us:cfr:30:725.13
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-725.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 725 (Reimbursements To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office shall pay up to 100 percent of the costs to the agency in excess of the base program for administering and enforcing the performance standards during the initial regulatory program.
