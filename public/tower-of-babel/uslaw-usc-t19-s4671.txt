19 U.S.C. § 4671 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:19:4671
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4671
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter VI (Labor Monitoring And Enforcement); Part C (Independent Mexico Labor Expert Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established a board, to be known as the “Independent Mexico Labor Expert Board”, to be responsible for monitoring and evaluating the implementation of Mexico’s labor reform and compliance with its labor obligations. The Board shall also advise the Interagency Labor Committee with respect to capacity-building activities needed to support such implementation and compliance.
