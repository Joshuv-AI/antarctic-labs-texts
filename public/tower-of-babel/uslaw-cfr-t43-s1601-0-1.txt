43 C.F.R. § 1601.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1601.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1601.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1600 (Planning, Programming, Budgeting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to establish in regulations a process for the development, approval, maintenance, amendment and revision of resource management plans, and the use of existing plans for public lands administered by the Bureau of Land Management.
