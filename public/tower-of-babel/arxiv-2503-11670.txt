Motivated by the works of Ramanujan, Richmond and Szekeres

explored the vanishing coefficients in the following infinite products and proved that if

\displaystyle{}\alpha(4n+3)=0

\displaystyle{}\beta(4n+2)=0.

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\gamma(n)q^{n}:=\frac{\left(q^{5};q^{12}\right)_{\infty}\left(q^{7};q^{12}\right)_{\infty}}{\left(q;q^{12}\right)_{\infty}\left(q^{11};q^{12}\right)_{\infty}}\quad\text{ and }\quad\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\delta(n)q^{n}:=\frac{\left(q;q^{12}\right)_{\infty}\left(q^{11};q^{12}\right)_{\infty}}{\left(q^{5};q^{12}\right)_{\infty}\left(q^{7};q^{12}\right)_{\infty}},

\displaystyle{}\gamma(6n+5)=0

\displaystyle{}\delta(6n+3)=0

\displaystyle{}\displaystyle f(a,b)
