5 U.S.C. § 1206 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:5:1206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1206
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part II (Civil Service Functions And Responsibilities); Chapter 12 (Merit Systems Protection Board, Office Of Special Counsel, And Employee Right Of Action); Subchapter I (Merit Systems Protection Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall submit an annual report to the President and the Congress on its activities, which shall include a description of significant actions taken by the Board to carry out its functions under this title. The report shall also review the significant actions of the Office of Personnel Management, including an analysis of whether the actions of the Office of Personnel Management are in accord with merit system principles and free from prohibited personnel practices.
