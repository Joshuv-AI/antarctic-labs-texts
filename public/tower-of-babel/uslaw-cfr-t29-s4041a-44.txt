29 C.F.R. § 4041A.44 — A.44 Cessation of withdrawal liability.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4041A.44
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4041A.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4041A (Part 4041A—Termination Of Multiemployer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The obligation of an employer to make payments of initial withdrawal liability and mass withdrawal liability shall cease on the date on which the plan's assets are distributed in full satisfaction of all nonforfeitable benefits provided by the plan.
