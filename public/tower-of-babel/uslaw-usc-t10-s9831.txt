10 U.S.C. § 9831 — Custody of departmental records and property
Citation: us:usc:10:9831
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9831
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 983 (Accountability And Responsibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force has custody and charge of all books, records, papers, furniture, fixtures, and other property under the lawful control of the executive part of the Department of the Air Force.
