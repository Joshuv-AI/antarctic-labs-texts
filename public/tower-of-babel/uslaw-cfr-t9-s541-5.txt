9 C.F.R. § 541.5 — Official detention marks and devices.
Citation: us:cfr:9:541.5
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-541.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 541 (Marks, Marking And Labeling Of Products And Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official mark for shipments of articles and fish detained under this subchapter is the designation “U.S. Detained,” and the official device for applying the mark is the official “U.S. Detained” tag (FSIS Form 8400-2) as prescribed in 9 CFR 329.2 or otherwise by the Administrator.
