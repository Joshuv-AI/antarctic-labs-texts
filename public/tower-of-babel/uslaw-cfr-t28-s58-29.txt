28 C.F.R. § 58.29 — Renewal for an additional one year period.
Citation: us:cfr:28:58.29
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-58.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 58 (Regulations Relating To The Bankruptcy Reform Acts Of 1978 And 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an approved provider's application for an additional one year period is approved, such renewal period shall begin to run from the later of:
(a) The day after the expiration date of the immediately preceding approval period; or
(b) The actual date of approval of such renewal by the United States Trustee.
