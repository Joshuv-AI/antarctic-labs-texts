25 U.S.C. § 5140 — Mortgaged property governed by State law
Citation: us:usc:25:5140
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5140
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45 (Protection Of Indians And Conservation Of Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Trust or restricted tribal or tribal corporation property mortgaged pursuant to sections 5136 to 5143 of this title shall be subject to foreclosure and sale or conveyance in lieu of foreclosure, free of such trust or restrictions, in accordance with the laws of the State in which the property is located.
