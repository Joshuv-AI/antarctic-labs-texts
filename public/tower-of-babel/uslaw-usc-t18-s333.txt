18 U.S.C. § 333 — Mutilation of national bank obligations
Citation: us:usc:18:333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section333
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 17 (Coins And Currency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever mutilates, cuts, defaces, disfigures, or perforates, or unites or cements together, or does any other thing to any bank bill, draft, note, or other evidence of debt issued by any national banking association, or Federal Reserve bank, or the Federal Reserve System, with intent to render such bank bill, draft, note, or other evidence of debt unfit to be reissued, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.
