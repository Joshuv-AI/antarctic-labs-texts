22 C.F.R. § 151.9 — Evidence of insurance required for diplomatic license plates and waiver of fees.
Citation: us:cfr:22:151.9
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-151.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 151 (Compulsory Liability Insurance For Diplomatic Missions And Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of State will not endorse on behalf of any person subject to the Act or any mission any application for diplomatic motor vehicle license plates or any application for waiver of motor vehicle registration fees without prior receipt of satisfactory evidence from the Chief of Mission or other duly authorized official that the required insurance is in effect.
