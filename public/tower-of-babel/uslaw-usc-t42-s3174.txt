42 U.S.C. § 3174 — Provision of comprehensive economic development strategies to Regional Commissions
Citation: us:usc:42:3174
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3174
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter IV (Economic Development Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any part of an economic development district is in a region covered by 1 or more of the Regional Commissions, the economic development district shall ensure that a copy of the comprehensive economic development strategy of the district is provided to the affected Regional Commission.
