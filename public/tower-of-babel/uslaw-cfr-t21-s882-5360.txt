21 C.F.R. § 882.5360 — Cranioplasty plate fastener.
Citation: us:cfr:21:882.5360
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-882.5360
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 882 (Neurological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A cranioplasty plate fastener is a screw, wire, or other article made of tantalum, vitallium, or stainless steel used to secure a plate to the patient's skull to repair a skull defect.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
