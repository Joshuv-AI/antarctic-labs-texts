7 C.F.R. § 623.19 — Assignments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:623.19
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-623.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 623 (Emergency Wetlands Reserve Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any participant entitled to any cash payment under this program may assign the right to receive such cash payments, in whole or in part.
