21 C.F.R. § 573.300 — Choline xanthate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:573.300
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-573.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 573 (Food Additives Permitted In Feed And Drinking Water Of Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Choline xanthate may be safely used as a component of animal feed as an added source of choline to supplement the diets of poultry, ruminants, and swine in accordance with good feeding practice.
