18 C.F.R. § 281.301 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:18:281.301
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-281.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 281 (Natural Gas Curtailment Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to determine the economic practicability and reasonable availability of alternative fuels, as prescribed in section 401(b) of the Natural Gas Policy Act of 1978 for use by essential agricultural use establishments that seek priority 2 entitlements for natural gas.
