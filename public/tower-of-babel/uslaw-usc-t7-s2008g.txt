7 U.S.C. § 2008g — Payment of interest as condition of loan servicing for borrowers
Citation: us:usc:7:2008g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2008g
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not reschedule or reamortize a loan for a borrower under this chapter who has not requested consideration under section 1981d(e) of this title unless the borrower pays a portion, as determined by the Secretary, of the interest due on the loan.
