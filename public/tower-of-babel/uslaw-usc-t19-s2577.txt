Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 13 (Trade Agreements Act Of 1979); Subchapter II (Technical Barriers To Trade (Standards)); Part E (Standards And Measures Under The North American Free Trade Agreement); Subpart 3 (Part Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 2571 of this title, for purposes of this part—
(1) NAFTA
The term “NAFTA” means the North American Free Trade Agreement.
(2) State
The term “State” means any of the several States, the District of Columbia, and the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.
