31 C.F.R. § 100.4 — Gold coin and gold certificates in general.
Citation: us:cfr:31:100.4
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-100.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 100 (Exchange Of Paper Currency And Coin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Gold coins, and gold certificates of the type issued before January 30, 1934, are exchangeable, as provided in this part, into other currency or coin which may be lawfully issued.
