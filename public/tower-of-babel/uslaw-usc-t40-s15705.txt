40 U.S.C. § 15705 — Biannual meetings of representatives of all Commissions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section15705
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 157 (Administrative Provisions); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Representatives of each Commission, the Appalachian Regional Commission, and the Denali Commission shall meet biannually to discuss issues confronting regions suffering from chronic and contiguous distress and successful strategies for promoting regional development.
(b) Chair of Meetings.—
The chair of each meeting shall rotate among the Commissions, with the Appalachian Regional Commission to host the first meeting.
