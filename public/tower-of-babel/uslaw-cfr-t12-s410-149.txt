12 C.F.R. § 410.149 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:12:410.149
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-410.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 410 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By Export-Import Bank Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 410.150, no qualified handicapped person shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by handicapped persons, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
