36 C.F.R. § 52.25 — What is the term of a commercial services contract?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.25
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A commercial services contract will generally be awarded for a set term or for a base term plus option years, with the total term not to exceed 10 years.
