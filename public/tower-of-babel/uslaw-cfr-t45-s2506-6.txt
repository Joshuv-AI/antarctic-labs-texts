45 C.F.R. § 2506.6 — What is a claim or debt?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2506.6
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2506.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2506 (Collection Of Debts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim or debt is an amount of money, funds, or property that has been determined by an agency official to be due the United States from any person, organization, or entity except another Federal agency (see § 2506.3).
