16 U.S.C. § 4751 — Environmental impact analyses
Citation: us:usc:16:4751
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4751
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 67 (Aquatic Nuisance Prevention And Control); Subchapter V (Cooperative Environmental Analyses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, in consultation with the Council on Environmental Quality, is encouraged to enter into negotiations with the governments of Canada and Mexico to provide for reciprocal cooperative environmental impact analysis of major Federal actions which have significant transboundary effects on the quality of the human environment in the United States, Canada, and Mexico.
