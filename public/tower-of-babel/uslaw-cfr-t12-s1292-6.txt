12 C.F.R. § 1292.6 — Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1292.6
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1292.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1292 (Community Investment Cash Advance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each Bank annually shall provide to FHFA, on or before January 31, a Targeted Community Lending Plan.
(b) Each Bank shall provide such other reports concerning its CICA programs as FHHA may request from time to time.
