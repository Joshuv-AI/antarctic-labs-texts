49 C.F.R. § 384.213 — State penalties for drivers of CMVs.
Citation: us:cfr:49:384.213
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-384.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 384 (State Compliance With Commercial Driver'S License Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must impose on drivers of CMVs appropriate civil and criminal penalties that are consistent with the penalties prescribed under part 383, subpart D, of this subchapter.
