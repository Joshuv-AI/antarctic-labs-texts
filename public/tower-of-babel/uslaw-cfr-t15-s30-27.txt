15 C.F.R. § 30.27 — Return of exported cargo to the United States prior to reaching its final destination.
Citation: us:cfr:15:30.27
Creator: Bureau Of The Census, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-30.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 30 (Foreign Trade Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When goods reported as exported from the United States are not exported or are returned without having been entered into a foreign destination, the filer shall cancel the EEI.
