45 C.F.R. § 2517.110 — Who may apply for a subgrant from a Corporation grantee?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2517.110
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2517.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2517 (Community-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Entities that may apply for a subgrant from a State Commission or grantmaking entity are qualified organizations that have entered into a local partnership with one or more—
(a) Local educational agencies (LEAs);
(b) Other qualified organizations; or
(c) Both.
