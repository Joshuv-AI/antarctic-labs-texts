47 C.F.R. § 68.6 — Telephones with volume control.
Citation: us:cfr:47:68.6
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-68.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 68 (Connection Of Terminal Equipment To The Telephone Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As of January 1, 2000, all telephones, including cordless telephones, as defined in § 15.3(j) of this chapter, manufactured in the United States (other than for export) or imported for use in the United States, must have volume control in accordance with § 68.317. Secure telephones, as defined by § 68.3 are exempt from this section, as are telephones used with public mobile services or private radio services.
