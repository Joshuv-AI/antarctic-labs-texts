34 C.F.R. § 200.65 — Determining equitable participation of teachers and families of participating private school children.
Citation: us:cfr:34:200.65
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-200.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 200 (Title I—Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) From the proportional share reserved for equitable services under § 200.77(d), an LEA shall ensure that teachers and families of participating private school children participate on an equitable basis in services and activities under this subpart.
(b) After consultation with appropriate private school officials, the LEA must provide services and activities under paragraph (a) of this section either—
(1) In conjunction with the LEA's services and activities for teachers and families; or
(2) Independently.
