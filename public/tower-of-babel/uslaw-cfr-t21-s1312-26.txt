21 C.F.R. § 1312.26 — Records required of exporter.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1312.26
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1312.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1312 (Importation And Exportation Of Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to any other records required by this chapter, the exporter must keep a record of any serial numbers that might appear on packages of narcotic drugs in quantities of one ounce or more in such a manner as will identify the foreign consignee, along with an official record of the export permit, in accordance with part 1304 of this chapter.
