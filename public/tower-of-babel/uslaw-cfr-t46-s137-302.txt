46 C.F.R. § 137.302 — Documenting compliance for the Coast Guard inspection option.
Citation: us:cfr:46:137.302
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-137.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 137 (Vessel Compliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The managing owner or managing operator of a towing vessel, who has selected the Coast Guard inspection option, must make their vessel available for the Coast Guard to conduct the examinations required by this subpart in accordance with the intervals prescribed in § 137.300.
