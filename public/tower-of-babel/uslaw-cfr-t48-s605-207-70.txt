48 C.F.R. § 605.207-70 — 605.207-70 Acquisitions available from only one responsible source.
Citation: us:cfr:48:605.207-70
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-605.207-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 605 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the information required at FAR 5.207, each notice of a proposed acquisition from only one responsible source shall include descriptions of the specific qualifications or capabilities required to perform the work and the information a potential source must submit.
