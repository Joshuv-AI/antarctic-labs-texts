28 C.F.R. § 5.301 — Exemption under section 3(a) of the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:28:5.301
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-5.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 5 (Administration And Enforcement Of Foreign Agents Registration Act Of 1938, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A consular officer of a foreign government shall be considered duly accredited under section 3(a) of the Act whenever he has received formal recognition as such, whether provisionally or by exequatur, from the Secretary of State.
(b) The exemption provided by section 3(a) of the Act to a duly accredited diplomatic or consular officer is personal and does not include within its scope an office, bureau, or other entity.
