31 C.F.R. § 6.2 — When the Act applies.
Citation: us:cfr:31:6.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-6.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 6 (Applications For Awards Under The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Act applies to any adversary adjudication pending before an agency at any time between October 1, 1981 and September 30, 1984. This includes proceedings begun before October 1, 1981, if final agency action has not been taken before that date, and proceedings pending on September 30, 1984, regardless of when they were initiated or when final agency action occurs.
