DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


2025-2026 IACC Summary of Advances

AGENCY: National Institutes of Health, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Interagency Autism Coordinating Committee (IACC) is 
preparing its Summary of Advances, a statutorily mandated report that 
highlights the most consequential autism research of the past year. The 
Committee is seeking suggested research articles to highlight for this 
report. On behalf of the IACC, the National Institute of Mental Health 
(NIMH) Office of Autism Research Coordination (OARC) is collecting 
research article suggestions from members of the public.

DATES: Responses to this notice are voluntary and the public comment 
period will be open from October 9, 2026, through October 23, 2026.

ADDRESSES: All comments must be submitted electronically via the web-
based form at: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/g/zvrk5fMKsw.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Specific questions about this Request 
for Public Comment should be directed to: Ms. Rebecca Martin by phone 
at 301-435-0886 or email at [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The IACC http://www.iacc.hhs.gov/ is a 
federal advisory committee composed of federal and public members that 
provides advice to the Secretary of Health and Human Services on 
autism. The

[[Page 64936]]

Committee is authorized under the Autism CARES Act of 2024, Public Law 
118-180. The IACC is preparing its Summary of Advances, a statutorily 
mandated report that highlights the most consequential autism research 
of the past year. This edition will cover research published in 2025 
and 2026. The Committee invites researchers, clinicians, autistic 
people, families, organizations, and other members of the public to 
suggest studies for its consideration.
    Submission Information: The Committee seeks suggestions of which 
research articles to highlight for this report. Respondents may suggest 
up to five studies published between January 1, 2025, and the response 
deadline, in any of the following topic areas:

1. Foundational Science
     Genetic and environmental factors
     Biology of the brain and body
2. Population Understanding & Surveillance
     Prevalence and population characterization
     Prevalence data specific to profound autism and 
developmental regression
3. Identification & Early Trajectory
     Early screening, diagnosis, and identification of co-
occurring conditions
     Developmental regression
4. Translation to Practice
     Treatment
     Interventions, including school- and community-based 
interventions
5. Services and Supports
     Efficacy
     Access

    Nominated articles must be peer-reviewed and represent significant 
advances in autism research. The following types of articles are 
typically not included, although the IACC can discuss and decide 
whether the insights gained warrant inclusion:

 Editorials & opinion pieces
 Preliminary studies and/or studies of small sample size
 Literature reviews, systematic reviews
 Expert recommendations & reports from panels/taskforces/
organizations/agencies
 Articles not solely/primarily focused on autism

    Respondents may also briefly identify important gaps in current 
autism research (optional). Responses will be reviewed by the Committee 
alongside nominations from Committee members. Suggestions are advisory; 
the Committee will make final selections.
    Instructions: All comments must be submitted through the web form 
at https://forms.cloud.microsoft/g/zvrk5fMKsw. Individuals submitting 
comments will receive an onscreen confirmation acknowledging receipt of 
the comment, but commenters will not receive individualized responses 
or feedback from the IACC or OARC except in the cases of requests for 
technical assistance with the survey, etc.
    A valid email address is required for submission, and only one 
submission per email address will be accepted. If duplicate submissions 
are received (i.e., form letters or email campaigns), only one example 
of such a submission will be included in the final set of materials 
provided to the committee.
    Comments are subject to redaction in accordance with federal 
policies and the IACC's public comment guidelines and privacy policy. 
To view the IACC's public comment guidelines and privacy policy, visit: 
https://iacc.hhs.gov/meetings/public-comments/guidelines/. All comments 
or summaries of comments received will be made publicly available on 
the IACC website https://www.iacc.hhs.gov within 90 days of the closing 
deadline for this notice. Email addresses associated with comments will 
not be included as part of the public disclosure. After the closing 
deadline, responses cannot be edited or withdrawn. No basis for claims 
against the U.S. Government shall arise as a result of a response to 
this request for information or from the Government's use of such 
information.

Beth Tignor,
