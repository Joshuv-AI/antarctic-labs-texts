29 U.S.C. § 1904 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:29:1904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1904
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 21 (Helen Keller National Center For Youths And Adults Who Are Deaf-Blind). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this chapter such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1999 through 2003. Such sums shall remain available until expended.
(b)
Any appropriation Act containing any appropriation authorized by subsection (a) shall contain a statement of the specific amount being made available to the Center.
