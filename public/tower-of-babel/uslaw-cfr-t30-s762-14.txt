30 C.F.R. § 762.14 — Applicability to lands designated as unsuitable by Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:30:762.14
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-762.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 762 (Criteria For Designating Areas As Unsuitable For Surface Coal Mining Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to appropriate petitions, lands listed in § 761.11 of this chapter are subject to designation as unsuitable for all or certain types of surface coal mining operations under this part and parts 764 and 769 of this chapter.
