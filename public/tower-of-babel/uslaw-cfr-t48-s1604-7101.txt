48 C.F.R. § 1604.7101 — 1604.7101 Filing health benefit claims/court review of disputed claims.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1604.7101
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1604.7101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1604 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Guidelines for a Federal Employees Health Benefit (FEHB) Program covered individual to file a claim for payment or service and for legal actions on disputed health benefit claims are found at 5 CFR 890.105 and 890.107, respectively. The contract clause at 1652.204-72 of this chapter, reflecting this guidance, must be inserted in all FEHB Program contracts.
