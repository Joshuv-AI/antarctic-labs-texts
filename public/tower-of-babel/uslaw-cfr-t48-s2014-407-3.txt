48 C.F.R. § 2014.407-3 — 2014.407-3 Other mistakes disclosed before award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2014.407-3
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2014.407-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2014 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Division of Contracts and Property Management, is delegated the authority to make the determinations concerning mistakes in bids, including those with obvious clerical errors, discovered prior to award. These determinations will be concurred in by legal counsel prior to notification of the bidder.
