50 C.F.R. § 90.17 — Compliance with other regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:50:90.17
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-90.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 90 (Feeding Depredating Migratory Waterfowl). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to supersede or modify any regulations relating to the hunting of migratory game birds, nor to permit the transportation, installation or use of grain contrary to any applicable Federal, State, or local laws or regulations.
