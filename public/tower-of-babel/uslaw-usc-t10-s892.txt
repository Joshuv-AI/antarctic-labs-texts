10 U.S.C. § 892 — Art. 92. Failure to obey order or regulation
Citation: us:usc:10:892
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section892
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who—
(1)
violates or fails to obey any lawful general order or regulation;
(2)
having knowledge of any other lawful order issued by a member of the armed forces, which it is his duty to obey, fails to obey the order; or
(3)
is derelict in the performance of his duties;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
