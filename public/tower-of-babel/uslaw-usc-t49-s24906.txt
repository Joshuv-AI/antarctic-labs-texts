49 U.S.C. § 24906 — Eliminating highway at-grade crossings
Citation: us:usc:49:24906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section24906
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Passenger Transportation); Chapter 249 (Northeast Corridor Improvement Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Plan.—
In consultation with the States on the main line of the Northeast Corridor, the Secretary of Transportation shall develop a plan not later than September 30, 1993, to eliminate all highway at-grade crossings of the main line by not later than December 31, 1997. The plan may provide that eliminating a crossing is not required if—
(1)
impracticable or unnecessary; and
(2)
using the crossing is consistent with conditions the Secretary considers appropriate to ensure safety.
(b) Amtrak’s Share of Costs.—
Amtrak shall pay 20 percent of the cost of eliminating each highway at-grade crossing under the plan.
