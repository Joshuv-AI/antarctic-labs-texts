25 U.S.C. § 4024 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:25:4024
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4024
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 42 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform); Subchapter II (Indian Trust Fund Management Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall—
(1)
directly or by contract, provide Indian tribes with technical assistance in developing, implementing, and managing Indian trust fund investment plans; and
(2)
among other things, ensure that legal, financial, and other expertise of the Department of the Interior has been made fully available in an advisory capacity to the Indian tribes to assist in the development, implementation, and management of investment plans.
