34 C.F.R. § 86.2 — What Federal programs are covered by this part?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal programs covered by this part include—
(a) All programs administered by the Department of Education under which an IHE may receive funds or any other form of Federal financial assistance; and
(b) All programs administered by any other Federal agency under which an IHE may receive funds or any other form of Federal financial assistance.
