14 C.F.R. § 49.21 — True copy of original conveyance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:49.21
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-49.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 49 (Recording Of Aircraft Titles And Security Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person submitting a conveyance for recording may submit a true copy in lieu of the original, except where an original is specifically required. The true copy must be in a form and manner acceptable to the Administrator, to which is attached a certificate, or other acceptable attestation, of the person submitting the conveyance stating that the copy has been compared with the original and that it is a true copy.
