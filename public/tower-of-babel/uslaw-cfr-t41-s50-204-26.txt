41 C.F.R. § 50-204.26 — -204.26 Exemptions for radioactive materials packaged for shipment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-204.26
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-204.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-204 (204—Safety And Health Standards For Federal Supply Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Radioactive materials packaged and labeled in accordance with regulations of the Department of Transportation shall be exempt from the labeling and posting requirements during shipment, provided that the inside containers are labeled in accordance with the provisions of § 50-204.24.
