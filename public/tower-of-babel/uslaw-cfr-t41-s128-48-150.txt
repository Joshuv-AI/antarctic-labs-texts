41 C.F.R. § 128-48.150 — -48.150 Determination of type of property.
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-48.150
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-48.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-48 (48—Utilization, Donation, Or Disposal Of Abandoned And Forfeited Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a bureau is unable to determine whether the personal property in its custody is abandoned or voluntarily abandoned, the bureau shall contact the regional office of the General Services Administration for the region in which the property is located for such a determination.
