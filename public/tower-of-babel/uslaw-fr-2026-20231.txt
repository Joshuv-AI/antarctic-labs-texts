DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


21st Century Energy Workforce Advisory Board

AGENCY: Office of Policy, Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice announces a virtual, open meeting of the 21st 
Century Energy Workforce Advisory Board (EWAB). The Federal Advisory 
Committee Act requires that public notice of these meetings be 
announced in the Federal Register.

DATES: Monday, November 2, 2026, from 2 to 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET).

ADDRESSES: Virtual meeting.
    Register to attend at: https://events.gcc.teams.microsoft.com/event/625684d3-34b1-4fe6-b433-98a46b4ef2d8@6b183ecc-4b55-4ed5-b3f8-7f64be1c4138.
    The meeting information will be posted on the EWAB website at: 
https://www.energy.gov/policy/21st-century-energy-workforce-advisory-board-ewab and can also be obtained by emailing [email protected].

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Angela Dayton, Designated Federal 
Officer (DFO), EWAB; Telephone: (240) 449-0652; or Email: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Purpose of the Board: EWAB advises the Secretary of Energy in 
developing a strategy for the Department of Energy to support and 
develop a skilled energy workforce to meet the changing needs of the 
U.S. energy system. It was established pursuant to Section 40211 of the 
Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (IIJA), Public Law 117-58 (42 
U.S.C. 18744) in accordance with the provisions of the Federal Advisory 
Committee Act (FACA), as amended, 5 U.S.C. 1001 et seq. This is the 
eleventh meeting of the EWAB.
    Tentative Agenda: The preliminary agenda for the meeting includes a 
roll call of the Board, a presentation on the history of the EWAB, 
deliberation of topics for the biennial report, and an opportunity for 
public comments.
    Public Participation: The meeting is open to the public via a 
virtual meeting option. Individuals who would like to attend must 
register for the meeting here: https://events.gcc.teams.microsoft.com/event/625684d3-34b1-4fe6-b433-98a46b4ef2d8@6b183ecc-4b55-4ed5-b3f8-7f64be1c4138.
    It is the policy of the EWAB to accept written public comments up 
to five (5) pages and to accommodate oral public comments, whenever 
possible. The EWAB expects that public statements presented at its 
meetings will not be repetitive of previously provided oral or 
submitted written statements. The public comment period for this 
meeting will take place on November 2, 2026, at a time specified in the 
meeting agenda. This public comment period is designed only for 
substantive commentary on the EWAB's work, not for business marketing 
purposes. The DFO will

[[Page 62722]]

conduct the meeting to facilitate the orderly conduct of business.
    Oral Comments: To be considered for the meeting's public speaker 
list, interested parties must register to speak by emailing 
[email protected] no later than 3 p.m. ET on October 27, 2026. To 
accommodate as many speakers as possible, the time for public comments 
will be limited to three (3) minutes per person, with a total public 
comment period of up to 15 minutes. If more speakers register than 
there is space available on the agenda, the EWAB will select speakers 
on a first-come, first-served basis from those who registered. Those 
not able to present oral comments may always file written comments with 
the EWAB.
    Written Comments: Although written comments are accepted 
continuously, written comments relevant to the subjects of the meeting 
should be submitted to [email protected] no later than 3 p.m. ET on 
October 27, 2026, so that the comments can be made available to the 
EWAB members prior to this meeting for their consideration. Please note 
that because EWAB operates under the provisions of FACA, all public 
comments and related materials will be treated as public documents and 
will be made available for public inspection, including being posted on 
the EWAB website.
    Minutes: The minutes of this meeting will be available on the EWAB 
website at: https://www.energy.gov/policy/21st-century-energy-workforce-advisory-board-ewab or by contacting the DFO at 
[email protected].
