Cohomological obstructions to equivariant unirationality
Yuri Tschinkel, Zhijia Zhang

Abstract
. We study cohomological obstructions to equivariant unirationality, with special regard to actions of finite groups on del Pezzo surfaces and Fano threefolds.



1.
Introduction

Let
X
be a smooth projective
rational variety with a generically free regular action of a finite group
G
. We say that
X
is
G
-unirational
, respectively,
projectively
G
-unirational
, if there exists a dominant
G
-equivariant rational map

{\mathbb{P}}(V)\dashrightarrow X,

where
V
is a representation of
G
, respectively, of a central extension of
G
. In the analogy between geometry over nonclosed fields and equivariant geometry, this should be viewed as being dominated by projective space, i.e., unirationality, versus being dominated by a Brauer-Severi variety.

An obvious obstruction to rationality of
X
over a field
k
is the absence of
k
-rational points – a birational invariant. In the equivariant context,
existence of
G
-fixed points is not an equivariant birational invariant – only the existence of fixed points upon restriction to abelian subgroups is; we refer to this as Condition
(A)
.

In general, unirationality and
G
-unirationality are difficult to establish or exclude, when the obvious obstructions, such as the absence of
k
-rational points, respectively, failure of Condition
(A)
, vanish.
In this note, we explore new cohomological obstructions to (projective) unirationality, and apply them to del Pezzo surfaces and Fano threefolds.
The obstructions arise from considerations of the
G
-action on the Picard group
\mathrm{Pic}(X)
.
Concretely, the Leray spectral sequence yields homomorphisms in group cohomology

{\mathrm{H}}^{j-2}(G,\mathrm{Pic}(X))\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\delta_{j}}}{{\longrightarrow}}{\mathrm{H}}^{j}(G,k^{\times}),\quad j=2,3,

where
G
acts trivially on
k^{\times}
. The images of
\delta_{j}
are stable birational invariants. Moreover, they vanish in the presence of
G
-fixed points and
for
G
-unirational
X
, see Section
2
.

Similar homomorphisms exist in the framework of birational geometry over nonclosed fields
k
, for Galois cohomology. However, their role in that context is limited: the images are trivial in presence of
k
-points, which is a (stable) birational invariant and an obvious necessary condition for
k
-unirationality. In the equivariant context, the invariants are quite subtle and informative – this highlights a stark difference between birational geometry over
k
and over the stack
BG
.

In this paper, we classify generically free regular actions of finite groups
G
on smooth projective
X
such that

•

X^{A}\neq\emptyset
, for all abelian
A\subseteq G
, and

•

\mathrm{Am}^{3}(X,H)\neq 0
, for
some
H\subseteq G
,

when
X
is a del Pezzo surface (see Theorem
4
) or a Kummer
quartic double solid, i.e., a
double cover of
{\mathbb{P}}^{3}
, ramified in a Kummer surface arising from the
Jacobian of a genus 2 curve with maximal automorphisms
(see Theorem
5
).
In all cases, we find that the obstruction arises from

\mathrm{Q}_{8},

the quaternion group of order 8.
As a corollary, such varieties are not
G
-unirational, nor projectively
G
-unirational.

2.
Cohomological obstructions

Let
k
be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and

X
a smooth projective rational variety over
k
, with a regular action of a finite group
G
. The Leray spectral sequence for
G
-actions yields an exact sequence (see, e.g.,
[
10
, Section 3]
):

\displaystyle\begin{split}0&\to\mathrm{Hom}(G,k^{\times})\to\mathrm{Pic}(X,G)\to\mathrm{Pic}(X)^{G}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\delta_{2}}}{{\longrightarrow}}{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,k^{\times})\\
&\qquad\quad\!\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\gamma}}{{\longrightarrow}}\mathrm{Br}([X/G])\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\beta}}{{\longrightarrow}}{\mathrm{H}}^{1}(G,\mathrm{Pic}(X))\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\delta_{3}}}{{\longrightarrow}}{\mathrm{H}}^{3}(G,k^{\times}),\end{split}

(2.1)

where
\mathrm{Pic}(X,G)
is the group of isomorphism classes of
G
-linearized line bundles on
X
,
and
\mathrm{Br}([X/G])
is the Brauer group of the quotient stack.
This gives rise to the following invariants:

•

\mathrm{Am}^{2}(X,G):=\mathrm{Im}(\delta_{2})
, the
Amitsur group
, and

•

\mathrm{Am}^{3}(X,G):=\mathrm{Im}(\delta_{3})
.

The
Amitsur
group

\mathrm{Am}(X,G)=\mathrm{Am}^{2}(X,G),

defined in
[
1
, Section 6]
as the image of
G
-invariant divisor classes

\mathrm{Pic}(X)^{G}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\delta_{2}}}{{\longrightarrow}}{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,k^{\times}),

is a stable
G
-birational invariant. The same holds for the higher Amitsur group
\mathrm{Am}^{3}(X,G)
, see
[
10
, Section 3]
.

We note in passing that basic results in group cohomology allow to reduce the study of these invariants to
p
-Sylow subgroups: vanishing of
\mathrm{Am}^{j}(X,H)
for
p
-Sylow subgroups
H
of
G
, for all
p
, implies the vanishing for all subgroups of
G
.
Furthermore, both groups vanish when
G
has a fixed point on
X
, by functoriality. They vanish for
G
-varieties
which are stably linearizable, or when
G
is cyclic.
We record the following refinement:

Proposition 1
.

Let
Y\to X
be a
G
-equivariant morphism of smooth projective varieties
with regular
G
-actions. Then

\mathrm{Am}^{j}(X,G)\subseteq\mathrm{Am}^{j}(Y,G),\quad j=2,3.

We are now in the position to formulate necessary conditions for

•

G

–

Condition
(A)
: for all abelian
H\subseteq G
, one has
X^{H}\neq\emptyset
.

–

H\subseteq G

\mathrm{Am}^{j}(X,H)=0

•

–

\mathrm{Am}^{2}(X,H)
is cyclic.

–

Amitsur: for all
H\subseteq G
, one has

\mathrm{Am}^{3}(X,H)=0

Indeed, for

\mathrm{Am}^{2}(X,G)=0,

since, by definition, the generator of
\mathrm{Pic}({\mathbb{P}}(V))
is

actions, the Amitsur group is cyclic.
Furthermore, for linear and projectively linear actions, we have

{\mathrm{H}}^{1}(G,\mathrm{Pic}({\mathbb{P}}(V)))=0,

which implies the vanishing

\mathrm{Am}^{3}(X,G)=0.

3.
Del Pezzo surfaces

In this section, we study cohomological obstructions to (projective) unirationality of regular, generically free,
G
-actions on smooth del Pezzo surfaces
X
, over algebraically closed fields
k
of characteristic zero. Let

\deg(X):=(-K_{X})^{2}\in[9,\ldots,1]

be the degree of
X
.
We begin with a summary of known results:

•

G
-actions are known, in principle
[
7
]
.

•

The groups

\mathrm{Am}^{2}(X,G)

have been determined
[
1
, Proposition 6.7]
; these are trivial when
\mathrm{rk}\,\mathrm{Pic}(X)^{G}=1
and
\deg(X)\leq 6
.

•

The groups
{\mathrm{H}}^{1}(G,\mathrm{Pic}(X))
have been
determined, starting with
[
13
]
,
[
17
]
; these are trivial when
\deg(X)\geq 5
.
There are algorithms to compute them when
G
is cyclic, in

[
2
]
,
[
15
]
, and also for general

[

E.g., for a del Pezzo surface with an involution fixing a (necessarily unique) smooth curve of genus

•

There is an algorithm to compute
\mathrm{Br}([X/G])

[
10
]
and
[
12
]
.
It is based on the stabilizer stratification of the
G

When

[

]
.

•

When
X

\mathrm{Am}^{j}(X,H)=0,\quad\forall H\subseteq G,\quad j=2,3.

•

G
-unirationality of del Pezzo surfaces of degree
\geq 3

[
8
]

(A)

We complement these results by analyzing

\mathrm{Am}^{3}(X,G),

for all del Pezzo surfaces
X

In particular, we classify all actions with Amitsur obstructions to (projective)
