43 C.F.R. § 100.302 — May I request Departmental review of an appraisal even if a qualified appraiser completed the appraisal or valuation?
Citation: us:cfr:43:100.302
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-100.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 100 (Waiving Departmental Review Of Appraisals And Valuations Of Indian Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you do not specifically request waiver of Departmental review and approval under § 100.300(a)(1), the Department will review the appraisal or valuation.
