40 C.F.R. § 258.22 — Disease vector control.
Citation: us:cfr:40:258.22
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-258.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 258 (Criteria For Municipal Solid Waste Landfills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Owners or operators of all MSWLF units must prevent or control on-site populations of disease vectors using techniques appropriate for the protection of human health and the environment.
(b) For purposes of this section, disease vectors means any rodents, flies, mosquitoes, or other animals, including insects, capable of transmitting disease to humans.
