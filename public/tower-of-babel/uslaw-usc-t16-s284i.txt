16 U.S.C. § 284i — Authorization of additional appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:284i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section284i
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXXV (Wolf Trap National Park For The Performing Arts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated not more than $17,000,000 to carry out sections 284c and 284d of this title. No authority under this subchapter to enter into contracts or to make payments shall be effective except to the extent and in such amounts as provided in advance in appropriations Acts.
