45 C.F.R. § 150.465 — Collection and use of penalty funds.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.465
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.465
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any funds collected under § 150.461 are paid to CMS.
(b) The funds are available without appropriation until expended.
(c) The funds may be used only for the purpose of enforcing the PHS Act requirements for which the penalty was assessed.
