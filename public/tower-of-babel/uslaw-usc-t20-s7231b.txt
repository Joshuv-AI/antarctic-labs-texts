20 U.S.C. § 7231b — Program authorized

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter IV (21St Century Schools); Part D (Magnet Schools Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in accordance with this part, is authorized to award grants to eligible local educational agencies, and consortia of such agencies where appropriate, to carry out the purpose of this part for magnet schools that are—
(1)
part of an approved desegregation plan; and
(2)
designed to bring students from different social, economic, ethnic, and racial backgrounds together.
