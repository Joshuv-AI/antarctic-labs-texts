29 C.F.R. § 1988.115 — Special circumstances; waiver of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1988.115
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1988.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1988 (Procedures For Handling Retaliation Complaints Under Section 31307 Of The Moving Ahead For Progress In The 21St Century Act (Map-21)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In special circumstances not contemplated by the provisions of these rules, or for good cause shown, the ALJ or the ARB on review may, upon application, after three-days' notice to all parties, waive any rule or issue such orders that justice or the administration of MAP-21 requires.
