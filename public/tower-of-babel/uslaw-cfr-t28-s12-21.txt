28 C.F.R. § 12.21 — Time within which registration statement must be filed.
Citation: us:cfr:28:12.21
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-12.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 12 (Registration Of Certain Persons Having Knowledge Of Foreign Espionage, Counterespionage, Or Sabotage Matters Under The Act Of August 1, 1956). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person who is or becomes subject to the registration provisions of the act after its effective date shall file a registration statement within fifteen days after the obligation to register arises.
