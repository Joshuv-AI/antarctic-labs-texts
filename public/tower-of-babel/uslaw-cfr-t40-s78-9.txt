40 C.F.R. § 78.9 — Notice of the filing of petition for administrative review.
Citation: us:cfr:40:78.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-78.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 78 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator will publish in the Federal Register a notice stating that a petition for administrative review of a decision of the Administrator has been filed and specifying any request in the petition for an evidentiary hearing.
