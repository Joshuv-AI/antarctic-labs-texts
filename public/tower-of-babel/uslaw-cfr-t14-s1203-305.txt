14 C.F.R. § 1203.305 — Restricted data.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1203.305
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1203.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1203 (Information Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Restricted Data or Formerly Restricted Data is so classified when originated or by operation of the law, as required by the Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended. Specific guidance for the classification of Restricted Data and Formerly Restricted Data is provided in “Classification Guides” published by the Department of Energy and or Department of Defense.
