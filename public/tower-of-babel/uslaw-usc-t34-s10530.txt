34 U.S.C. § 10530 — Patrick Leahy Bulletproof Vest Partnership Grant Program
Citation: us:usc:34:10530
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10530
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXIV (Matching Grant Program For Law Enforcement Armor Vests). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The program under this subchapter shall be known as the “Patrick Leahy Bulletproof Vest Partnership Grant Program”.
