41 C.F.R. § 302-8.102 — -8.102 Allowable costs for storage.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-8.102
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-8.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-8 (8—Allowances For Extended Storage Of Household Goods (Hhg)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Allowable costs for storage include the cost of:
(a) Necessary packing;
(b) Crating;
(c) Unpacking;
(d) Uncrating;
(e) Transportation to and from place of storage;
(f) Charges while in storage; and
(g) Other necessary charges directly relating to the storage as approved by the agency.
