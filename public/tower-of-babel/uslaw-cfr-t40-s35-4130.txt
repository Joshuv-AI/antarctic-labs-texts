40 C.F.R. § 35.4130 — What must be included in my group's budget?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4130
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your budget must clearly show how:
(a) You will spend the money and how the spending meets the objectives of the TAG project;
(b) Your group will provide the required cash and/or in-kind contributions; and
(c) Your group derived the figures included in the budget.
