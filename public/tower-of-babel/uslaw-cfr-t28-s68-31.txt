28 C.F.R. § 68.31 — Separation of functions.
Citation: us:cfr:28:68.31
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-68.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 68 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Immigration-Related Administrative Hearings Before Ocaho Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No officer, employee, or agent of the Federal Government engaged in the performance of investigative or prosecutorial functions in connection with any proceeding shall, in that proceeding or a factually related proceeding, participate or advise in the decision of the Administrative Law Judge, except as a witness or counsel in the proceedings.
