5 U.S.C. § 578 — Authority of the arbitrator
Citation: us:usc:5:578
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section578
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 5 (Administrative Procedure); Subchapter IV (Alternative Means Of Dispute Resolution In The Administrative Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An arbitrator to whom a dispute is referred under this subchapter may—
(1)
regulate the course of and conduct arbitral hearings;
(2)
administer oaths and affirmations;
(3)
compel the attendance of witnesses and production of evidence at the hearing under the provisions of section 7 of title 9 only to the extent the agency involved is otherwise authorized by law to do so; and
(4)
make awards.
