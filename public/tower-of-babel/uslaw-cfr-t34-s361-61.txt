34 C.F.R. § 361.61 — Limitation on use of funds for construction expenditures.
Citation: us:cfr:34:361.61
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-361.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 361 (State Vocational Rehabilitation Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No more than 10 percent of a State's allotment for any fiscal year under section 110 of the Act may be spent on the construction of facilities for community rehabilitation program purposes.
