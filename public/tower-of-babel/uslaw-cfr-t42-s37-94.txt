42 C.F.R. § 37.94 — Respiratory assessment form.
Citation: us:cfr:42:37.94
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-37.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 37 (Specifications For Medical Examinations Of Coal Miners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As part of the spirometry testing and concurrent with it, personnel at the facility must complete a Respiratory Assessment Form (CDC/NIOSH 2.13).
