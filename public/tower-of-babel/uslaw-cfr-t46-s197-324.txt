46 C.F.R. § 197.324 — Diver's safety harness.
Citation: us:cfr:46:197.324
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-197.324
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 197 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each safety harness used in surface-supplied diving must have—
(a) A positive buckling device; and
(b) An attachment point for the umbilical life line that—
(1) Distributes the pulling force of the umbilical over the diver's body; and
(2) Prevents strain on the mask or helmet.
