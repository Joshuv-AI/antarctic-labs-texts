Abstract
During this work, it is considered a binary system of supermassive rotating black holes; first, it is employed the concept of weak field limit to develop a metric tensor g μ ​ ν g_{\mu\nu} that describes the geometry of the spacetime, it introduced an approximation in which the second black hole is coupled to the system through a perturbation tensor f + ∗ f^{*}_{+} , consequently, Ricci scalar curvature is determined at a distance of 120 AU and whose value is − 1.97 ⋅ 10 − 34 -1.97\cdot 10^{-34} m − 2 m^{-2} , it is employed a black hole type Sagittarius A* to make the numerical calculations; the negative Ricci scalar curvature states that the tensor f + ∗ f^{*}_{+} does not change the topological properties of Kerr’s solution.
