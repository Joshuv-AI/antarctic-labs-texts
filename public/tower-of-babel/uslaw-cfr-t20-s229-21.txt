20 C.F.R. § 229.21 — When a spouse is eligible for an increase under the overall minimum.
Citation: us:cfr:20:229.21
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-229.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 229 (Social Security Overall Minimum Guarantee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Normally, only the employee annuity receives the amount of the overall minimum increase. However, a spouse annuity may be increased under the O/M in cases in which the O/M benefit amount exceeds the total amount of the employee and spouse annuity.
