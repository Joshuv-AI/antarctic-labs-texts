19 C.F.R. § 212.22 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:19:212.22
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-212.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 212 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the adversary adjudication, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 212.25.
