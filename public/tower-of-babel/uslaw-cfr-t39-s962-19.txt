39 C.F.R. § 962.19 — (Rule 19) Transcript of proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:39:962.19
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-962.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 962 (Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Presiding Officer will arrange for a transcript of the hearing to be made, unless ordered otherwise. The transcript provided by the Presiding Officer is the official record of the hearing, and the Presiding Officer will provide the parties with a copy after the hearing.
