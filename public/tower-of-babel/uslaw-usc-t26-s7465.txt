26 U.S.C. § 7465 — Provisions of special application to transferees
Citation: us:usc:26:7465
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7465
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter C (The Tax Court); Part II (Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(1)
For rules of burden of proof in transferee proceedings, see section 6902(a).
(2)
For authority of Tax Court to prescribe rules by which a transferee of property of a taxpayer shall be entitled to examine books, records and other evidence, see section 6902(b).
