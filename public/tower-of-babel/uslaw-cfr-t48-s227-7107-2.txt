48 C.F.R. § 227.7107-2 — 227.7107-2 Contracts for construction supplies and research and development work.
Citation: us:cfr:48:227.7107-2
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-227.7107-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 227 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the provisions and clauses required by 227-7103-6 and 227.7203-6 when the acquisition is limited to—
(a) Construction supplies or materials;
(b) Experimental, developmental, or research work, or test and evaluation studies of structures, equipment, processes, or materials for use in construction; or
(c) Both.
