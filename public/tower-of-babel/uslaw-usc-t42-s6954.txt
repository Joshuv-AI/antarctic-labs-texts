42 U.S.C. § 6954 — Technology promotion
Citation: us:usc:42:6954
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6954
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter V (Duties Of Secretary Of Commerce In Resource And Recovery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce is authorized to evaluate the commercial feasibility of resource recovery facilities and to publish the results of such evaluation, and to develop a data base for purposes of assisting persons in choosing such a system.
