19 U.S.C. § 2575 — General
Citation: us:usc:19:2575
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2575
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 13 (Trade Agreements Act Of 1979); Subchapter II (Technical Barriers To Trade (Standards)); Part E (Standards And Measures Under The North American Free Trade Agreement); Subpart 1 (Sanitary And Phytosanitary Measures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subpart may be construed—
(1)
to prohibit a Federal agency or State agency from engaging in activity related to sanitary or phytosanitary measures to protect human, animal, or plant life or health; or
(2)
to limit the authority of a Federal agency or State agency to determine the level of protection of human, animal, or plant life or health the agency considers appropriate.
