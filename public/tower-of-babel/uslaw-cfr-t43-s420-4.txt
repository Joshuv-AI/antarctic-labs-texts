43 C.F.R. § 420.4 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:43:420.4
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-420.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 420 (Off-Road Vehicle Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part will be enforced to the extent of Bureau authority, including entering into cooperative agreements with Federal, State, county, or local law enforcement officials.
