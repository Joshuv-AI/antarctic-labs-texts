48 U.S.C. § 50f — Disposal of miscellaneous revenues from schools, hospitals, and other Indian Service facilities
Citation: us:usc:48:50f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section50f
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 2 (Alaska). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After February 20, 1942, miscellaneous revenues derived from schools, hospitals, and other facilities maintained and operated by the Indian Service for the benefit of Indians and natives of Alaska shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States under the provisions of section 155 of title 25.
