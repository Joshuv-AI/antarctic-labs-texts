29 C.F.R. § 0.737-5 — -5 Appointment of Examiner.
Citation: us:cfr:29:0.737-5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-0.737-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 0 (Ethics And Conduct Of Department Of Labor Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a notice of alleged violation has been issued and a hearing requested, the Under Secretary shall provide for the selection of a Department of Labor Administrative Law Judge, appointed in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 3105, to act as the Examiner with respect to the matter.
