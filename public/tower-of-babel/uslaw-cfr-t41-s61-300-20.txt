41 C.F.R. § 61-300.20 — -300.20 How will DOL determine whether a contractor or subcontractor is complying with the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:41:61-300.20
Creator: Chapter 61—Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-61-300.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 61-300 (300—Annual Report From Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the course of a compliance evaluation, OFCCP may determine whether a contractor or subcontractor has submitted its VETS-4212 Report(s) as required by this part.
