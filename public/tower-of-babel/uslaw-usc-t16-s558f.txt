16 U.S.C. § 558f — Promotion of volunteer opportunities
Citation: us:usc:16:558f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section558f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretaries shall promote volunteer opportunities in areas administered by the Secretaries.
