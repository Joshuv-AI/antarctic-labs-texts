7 C.F.R. § 633.10 — Modifications.
Citation: us:cfr:7:633.10
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-633.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 633 (Water Bank Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NRCS may approve modifications to the agreement or associated conservation plan after consultation with the Conservation District. Any modification must meet WBP program objectives, and must be in compliance with this part.
