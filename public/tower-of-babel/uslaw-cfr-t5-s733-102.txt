5 C.F.R. § 733.102 — Exclusion of employees in the Criminal Division and National Security Division of the United States Department of Justice.
Citation: us:cfr:5:733.102
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-733.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 733 (Political Activity—Federal Employees Residing In Designated Localities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees in the Criminal Division and National Security Division in the Department of Justice (except employees appointed by the President by and with the advice and consent of the Senate) specifically are excluded from coverage under the provisions of this part.
