40 C.F.R. § 247.4 — Contracting officer requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:247.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-247.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 247 (Comprehensive Procurement Guideline For Products Containing Recovered Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within one year after the effective date of each item designation, contracting officers shall require that vendors:
(a) Certify that the percentage of recovered materials to be used in the performance of the contract will be at least the amount required by applicable specifications or other contractual requirements, and
(b) Estimate the percentage of total material utilized for the performance of the contract which is recovered materials.
