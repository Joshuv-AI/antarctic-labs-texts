34 U.S.C. § 11190 — Issuance of rules
Citation: us:usc:34:11190
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11190
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter II (Programs And Offices); Part F (General And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall issue rules to carry out this subchapter, including rules that establish procedures and methods for making grants and contracts, and distributing funds available, to carry out this subchapter.
