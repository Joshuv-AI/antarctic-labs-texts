45 C.F.R. § 1602.7 — Index of records.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1602 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Information Under The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

LSC will maintain and make available for public inspection in an electronic format a current index identifying any matter within the scope of § 1602.4 and § 1602.5(b).
