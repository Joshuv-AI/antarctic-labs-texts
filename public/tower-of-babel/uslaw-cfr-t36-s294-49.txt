36 C.F.R. § 294.49 — List of designated Colorado Roadless Areas.
Citation: us:cfr:36:294.49
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-294.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 294 (Special Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All National Forest System lands within the State of Colorado listed in this section are hereby designated as Colorado Roadless Areas. An “X” in the third column indicates that some or all of that CRA contains upper tier acres.
