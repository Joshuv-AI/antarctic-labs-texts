43 C.F.R. § 2933.12 — Where can I obtain a Recreation Use Permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2933.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2933.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2930 (Permits For Recreation On Public Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may obtain a permit at self-service pay stations, from personnel at the site, or at other specified locations. Because these locations may vary from site to site, you should contact the local BLM office with jurisdiction over the area or site in advance for permit information.
