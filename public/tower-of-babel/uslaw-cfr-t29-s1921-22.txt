29 C.F.R. § 1921.22 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1921.22

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1921.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1921 (Rules Of Practice In Enforcement Proceedings Under Section 41 Of The Longshoremen'S And Harbor Workers' Compensation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sundays and holidays shall be included in computing the time allowed for filing any document or paper under this part. When such time expires on a Sunday or legal holiday, such period shall be extended to include the next following business day.
