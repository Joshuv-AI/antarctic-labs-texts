25 C.F.R. § 522.9 — Publication of class III ordinance and approval.
Citation: us:cfr:25:522.9
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-522.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 522 (Submission Of Gaming Ordinance Or Resolution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chair shall publish notice of approval of class III tribal gaming ordinances or resolutions in the Federal Register, along with the Chair's approval thereof.
