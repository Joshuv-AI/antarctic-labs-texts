47 C.F.R. § 1.10010 — Do I need to send paper copies with my electronic applications?
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.10010
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.10010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When you file electronically through ICFS, the electronic record is the official record. You do not need to submit paper copies of your application.
