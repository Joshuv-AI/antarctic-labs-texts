25 C.F.R. § 547.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:547.1
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-547.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 547 (Minimum Technical Standards For Class Ii Gaming Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Indian Gaming Regulatory Act, 25 U.S.C. 2703(7)(A)(i), permits the use of electronic, computer, or other technologic aids in connection with the play of Class II games. This part establishes the minimum technical standards governing the use of such aids.
