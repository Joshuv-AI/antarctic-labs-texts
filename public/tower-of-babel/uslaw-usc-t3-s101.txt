3 U.S.C. § 101 — Commencement of term of office
Citation: us:usc:3:101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section101
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 2 (Office And Compensation Of President). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term of four years for which a President and Vice President shall be elected, shall, in all cases, commence on the 20th day of January next succeeding the day on which the votes of the electors have been given.
