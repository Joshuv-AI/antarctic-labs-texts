46 U.S.C. § 7506 — Convention tonnage for licenses, certificates, and documents
Citation: us:usc:46:7506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7506
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 75 (General Procedures For Licensing, Certification, And Documentation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any provision of section 14302(c) or 14305 of this title, the Secretary may—
(1)
evaluate the service of an individual who is applying for a license, a certificate of registry, or a merchant mariner’s document by using the tonnage as measured under chapter 143 of this title for the vessels on which that service was acquired, and
(2)
issue the license, certificate, or document based on that service.
