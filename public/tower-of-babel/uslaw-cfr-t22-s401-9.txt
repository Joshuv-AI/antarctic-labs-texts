22 C.F.R. § 401.9 — Suspension or amendment of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:22:401.9
Creator: International Joint Commission, United States And Canada
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-401.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 401 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The commission may suspend, repeal, or amend all or any of the rules of procedure at any time, with the concurrence of at least four commissioners. Both Governments shall be informed forthwith of any such action.
