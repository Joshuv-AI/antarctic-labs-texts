16 U.S.C. § 6908 — Territorial participation
Citation: us:usc:16:6908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6908
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 88 (Western And Central Pacific Fisheries Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall ensure participation in the Commission and its subsidiary bodies by American Samoa, Guam, and the Northern Mariana Islands to the same extent provided to the territories of other nations.
