49 C.F.R. § 821.13 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:49:821.13
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-821.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 821 (Rules Of Practice In Air Safety Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Waivers of any rights provided by statute or regulation shall either be in writing or by stipulation made at the hearing and entered into the record, and shall set forth the precise terms and conditions of the waiver.
