29 C.F.R. § 500.231 — Appearances; representation of the Department of Labor.
Citation: us:cfr:29:500.231
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-500.231
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 500 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Associate Solicitor, Division of Fair Labor Standards, and such other counsel, as designated, shall represent the Secretary in any proceeding under these regulations.
