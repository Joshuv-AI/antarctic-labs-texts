40 C.F.R. § 61.250 — Designation of facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:40:61.250
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-61.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 61 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to owners or operators of facilities licensed to manage uranium byproduct materials during and following the processing of uranium ores, commonly referred to as uranium mills and their associated tailings. This subpart does not apply to the disposal of tailings.
