50 C.F.R. § 13.44 — Display of permit.
Citation: us:cfr:50:13.44
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-13.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 13 (General Permit Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any permit issued under this part shall be displayed for inspection upon request to the Director or his agent, or to any other person relying upon its existence.
