40 C.F.R. § 63.11144 — What definitions apply to this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.11144
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.11144
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) On and before April 17, 2012, the terms used in this subpart are defined in the Clean Air Act; §§ 61.02 and 61.61 of this chapter; and § 63.2 for terms used in the applicable provisions of subpart A of this part, as specified in § 63.11143(b).
(b) After April 17, 2012, terms used in this subpart are defined in the Clean Air Act; § 63.2; and § 63.12005.
