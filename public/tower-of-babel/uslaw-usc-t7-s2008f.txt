7 U.S.C. § 2008f — Crop insurance requirement
Citation: us:usc:7:2008f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2008f
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
As a condition of obtaining any benefit (including a direct loan, loan guarantee, or payment) described in subsection (b), a borrower must obtain at least catastrophic risk protection insurance coverage under section 1508 of this title for the crop and crop year for which the benefit is sought, if the coverage is offered by the Corporation.
(b) Applicable benefits
Subsection (a) shall apply to—
(1)
a farm ownership loan (FO) under section 1923 of this title;
(2)
an operating loan (OL) under section 1942 of this title; and
(3)
an emergency loan (EM) under section 1961 of this title.
