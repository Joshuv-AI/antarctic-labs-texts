27 C.F.R. § 771.136 — Availability.
Citation: us:cfr:27:771.136
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-771.136
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 771 (Rules Of Practice In Explosive License And Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A copy of the record shall be available for inspection or copying by the parties to the proceedings during business hours at the office of the administrative law judge or the Director of Industry Operations or, pending administrative review, at the Office of the Director.
