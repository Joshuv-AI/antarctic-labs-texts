1 C.F.R. § 21.35 — OMB control numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:1:21.35
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-21.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 21 (Preparation Of Documents Subject To Codification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To display OMB control numbers in agency regulations, those numbers shall be placed parenthetically at the end of the section or displayed in a table or codified section.
