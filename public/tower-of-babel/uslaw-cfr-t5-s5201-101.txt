5 C.F.R. § 5201.101 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:5:5201.101
Creator: Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-5201.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 5201 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Department Of Labor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 CFR 2635.105, the regulations in this part apply to employees of the Department of Labor (Department) and supplement the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch contained in 5 CFR part 2635.
