QCD analysis of B → π ​ π B\to\pi\pi form factors and the | V u ​ b | |V_{ub}| extraction from B l ​ 4 B_{l4} decays
Shan Cheng

Abstract
The B l ​ 4 B_{l4} decays offer an independent method for determining the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix element | V u ​ b | |V_{ub}| , with a QCD analysis of the underlying B → π ​ π B\to\pi\pi form factors serving as a crucial component. We present a comprehensive QCD analysis of the B → π ​ π B\to\pi\pi form factors within the framework of light-cone sum rules, employing two-pion light-cone distribution amplitudes. For the first time, we derive the twist-three 2 ​ π 2\pi DAs explicitly by exploiting the conformal symmetry of massless QCD. This breakthrough enables the first systematic calculation of higher-power corrections to the B → π ​ π B\to\pi\pi -type transitions. Leveraging the recently measured partial decay fractions of B + → π + ​ π − ​ l + ​ ν l B^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}l^{+}\nu_{l} by the Belle detector, we obtain | V u ​ b | = ( 4.27 ± 0.49 | data ± 0.55 | LCSRs ) × 10 − 3 |V_{ub}|=\left(4.27\pm 0.49|_{\rm data}\pm 0.55|_{\rm LCSRs}\right)\times 10^{-3} within the dominant region of the ρ \rho resonance. We clarify that the relatively small value of | V u ​ b | |V_{ub}| extracted from B → ρ ​ l ​ ν B\to\rho l\nu , when compared to the golden channel B → π ​ l ​ ν B\to\pi l\nu , arises due to the finite width and nonresonant effects. We anticipate future measurements of purely isovector and isoscalar B l ​ 4 B_{l4} decays, which will aid in reducing uncertainties by enhancing our understanding of the π ​ π \pi\pi phase shifts in the two-pion distribution amplitudes through heavy flavor decays.

Two-pion light-cone distribution amplitudes.
–The most general nonperturbative quantity
in hard exclusive processes with two energetic pions in the final state is provided by the

These amplitudes offer a systematic framework for disentangling resonant (from different partial-waves)

The

the one-pion momentum fraction along the light-cone direction

Further details on the matrix element definition and twist clarification for
2\pi

Here, we focus on the double expansion formalism. At the parton level, the momentum distributions, charactered by the variable

are solved via a local collinear conformal expansion and expressed in terms of Gegenbauer polynomials
C_{n}^{3/2}(2u-1)

At the hadron level, the kinematic distributions encode the angle momentum of the produced pion pair.
They are decomposed into partial waves and expanded in another set of Gegenbauer polynomials,
C_{l}^{1/2}(2\zeta-1)

which is identical to the Legendre polynomials. In practice, the variable

\displaystyle\Phi\propto\sum_{n}^{\infty}\sum_{l}^{n+1}B_{nl}(\mu,k^{2})C_{n}^{3/2}(2u-1)C_{l}^{1/2}(2\zeta-1)

(3)

\displaystyle+\frac{k^{4}}{\pi}\int_{4m_{\pi}^{2}}^{\infty}ds\frac{\delta_{l}(s)}{s^{2}(s-k^{2}-i0)}\Big].

The instanton vacuum is modeled as a grand canonical ensemble,
with the partition function expressed in terms of the collective coordinates.
Within this framework, the mean values of these coordinates are determined

A key element of this derivation is the introducing of a dimensionless parameter to incorporates the argument of the inverse charge

Final-state interactions between meson pairs, especially critical in the

In exclusive QCD processes, power-suppressed contributions arise from three sources:
the ”bad” component of the quark field with an incorrect spin projector,
transverse momentum in the leading-twist components of the
q{\bar{q}}

and higher Fock states such as

These contributions are encoded in subleading-twist LCDAs.
For

Conclusions.
–In this work, we perform the comprehensive calculation of the
B\to\pi\pi
FFs using LCSRs,
incorporating an investigation of the subleading twist-three
2\pi
DAs.
We demonstrate that these previously neglected contributions play a significant role, particularly in the timelike- and longitudinal-helicity FFs,
highlighting the importance of higher-twist effects in multi-body
B
-meson decays.
Given the well-understood predominance of the
P
-wave contribution in the
B_{l4}
decay
B^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}l^{+}\nu_{l}
,
we extract the CKM matrix element
|V_{ub}|
via the
B_{l4}
decays.
Our result,
|V_{ub}|=\left(4.27\pm 0.49|_{\rm data}\pm 0.55|_{\rm LCSRs}\right)\times 10^{-3}
,
is in consistent with that obtained from the golden channel
B\to\pi l\nu_{l}
.
This consistency resolves the puzzle regarding the determination of
|V_{ub}|
from different exclusive channels,
particularly in light of finite width and nonresonant effects.
The uncertainties in our determination can be further reduced through advancements in both theoretical frameworks and experimental precision.
It is also feasible to perform a next-to-leading-order (NLO) calculation for the
B\to\pi\pi
form factors
which would significantly enhance the accuracy of the QCD predictions
[
65
,
66
,
67
]
.
Consequently,
B_{l4}
decays offer a robust and independent method to address the
|V_{ub}|
puzzle,
providing a critical cross-check for existing determinations.

\rho

Our sum rules for the

This key difference stems from the choice of interpolating currents.
Employing the current

B

In the

between the on-shell
B
-meson state and the vacuum, involving two bilocal currents,

\displaystyle F_{\mu\nu}=i\int dx^{4}e^{ik\cdot x}\langle 0|{\bf T}\{j^{\prime}_{\mu}(x),j_{\nu}(0)\}|{\bar{B}}^{0}(q+k)\rangle.

(S9)

Considering, for example, the vector component of the weak current,

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{3}F_{\perp}^{(l=1)}(q^{2},k^{2})}{\sqrt{k^{2}}\sqrt{\lambda}}=\frac{g_{\rho\pi\pi}V(q^{2})}{(m_{B}+m_{\rho})\left[m_{\rho}^{2}-k^{2}-i\sqrt{k^{2}}\Gamma_{\rho}(k^{2})\right]}+\cdots,
