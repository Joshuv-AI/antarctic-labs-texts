7 C.F.R. § 81.12 — Inspection of premises.
Citation: us:cfr:7:81.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-81.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 81 (Prune/Dried Plum Diversion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The producer must permit authorized representatives of USDA or the Committee, at any reasonable time, to have access to their premises to inspect and examine the orchard block where trees were removed and records pertaining to the orchard to determine compliance with the provisions of this part.
