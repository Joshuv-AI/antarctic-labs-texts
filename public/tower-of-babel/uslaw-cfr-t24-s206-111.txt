24 C.F.R. § 206.111 — Due date of MIP.
Citation: us:cfr:24:206.111
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-206.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 206 (Home Equity Conversion Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Initial MIP. The mortgagee shall pay the initial MIP to the Commissioner within fifteen days of closing and as a condition to the endorsement of the mortgage for insurance.
(b) Monthly MIP. Each monthly MIP shall be due to the Commissioner on the first business day of each month except the month in which the mortgage is closed.
