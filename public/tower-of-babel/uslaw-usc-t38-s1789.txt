38 U.S.C. § 1789 — Mental health services for members of the reserve components of the Armed Forces
Citation: us:usc:38:1789
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1789
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter VIII (Health Care Of Persons Other Than Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in consultation with the Secretary of Defense, may furnish mental health services to members of the reserve components of the Armed Forces.
