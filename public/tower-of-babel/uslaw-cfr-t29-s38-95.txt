29 C.F.R. § 38.95 — Enforcement when voluntary compliance cannot be secured.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.95
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Director concludes that compliance cannot be secured by voluntary means, the Director must either:
(a) Issue a Final Determination;
(b) Refer the matter to the Attorney General with a recommendation that an appropriate civil action be instituted; or
(c) Take such other action as may be provided by law.
