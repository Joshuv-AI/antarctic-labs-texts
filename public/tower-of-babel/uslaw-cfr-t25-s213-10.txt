25 C.F.R. § 213.10 — Lessor's signature.
Citation: us:cfr:25:213.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-213.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 213 (Leasing Of Restricted Lands Of Members Of Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma, For Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any Indian who cannot write his name will be required to sign all official papers by making a distinct thumbprint which shall be designated as “right” or “left” thumbmark. Such signatures must be witnessed by two persons, one of whom must be a U.S. Government employee (such as field clerk, postmaster, U.S. Commissioner, etc.).
