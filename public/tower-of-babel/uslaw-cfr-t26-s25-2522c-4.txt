26 C.F.R. § 25.2522(c)-4 — (c)-4 Disallowance of double deduction in the case of qualified terminable interest property.
Citation: us:cfr:26:25.2522(c)-4
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-25.2522(c)-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 25 (Gift Tax; Gifts Made After December 31, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No deduction is allowed under section 2522 for the transfer of an interest in property if a deduction is taken from the total amount of gifts with respect to that property by reason of section 2523(f). See § 25.2523(h)-1.
