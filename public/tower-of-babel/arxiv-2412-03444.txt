A new fidelity of quantum channel evolution and its geometric interpretation
Xiaojing Yan, Xiao Sun, Mingming Du, Jiashan Tang

Abstract
Fidelity is crucial for characterizing transformations of quantum states under various quantum channels, which can be served as a fundamental tool in resource theories. Firstly, we define an α \alpha - z z -fidelity as a significant quantity in quantum information theory and give the properties of the fidelity with orders α \alpha and z z . Secondly, by analyzing the α \alpha - z z -fidelity under the evolution of different types of quantum channels (single orbit, all quantum channels, unitary quantum channels, and mixed unitary quantum channels), we propose a limit formula for the maximum and the minimum of the α \alpha - z z -fidelity. In addition, we have extended the α \alpha - z z -Rényi relative entropy, providing new insights into its relevance for resource quantification. Finally, we offer a geometric interpretation for measuring the distance between quantum states, contributing to the broader understanding of the operational and transformative power of dynamical quantum resources across various physical settings.

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{m^{z}n^{1-\alpha}}\dim(S_{m}\cap S_{n}).
