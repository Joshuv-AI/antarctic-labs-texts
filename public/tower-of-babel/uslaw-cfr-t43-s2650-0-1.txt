43 C.F.R. § 2650.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2650.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2650.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2650 (Alaska Native Selections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to provide procedures for orderly and timely implementation of those provisions of the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act of December 18, 1971 (43 U.S.C. 1601) which pertain to selections of lands and interests in lands in satisfaction of the land selections conferred by said Act upon Alaska Natives and Alaska Native corporations.
