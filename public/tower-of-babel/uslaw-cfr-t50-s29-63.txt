50 C.F.R. § 29.63 — What plugging and reclamation requirements apply to my pre-existing operations?
Citation: us:cfr:50:29.63
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-29.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 29 (Land Use Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon completion of your production operation, you are subject to the reclamation standards in § 29.117(d). You must obtain an operations permit in accordance with §§ 29.90 through 29.97, Operations Permit: Application, and all applicable requirements of this subpart, prior to plugging your well and conducting site reclamation.
