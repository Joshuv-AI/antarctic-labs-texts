15 U.S.C. § 5206 — National Aeronautics and Space Administration
Citation: us:usc:15:5206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5206
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 78 (Superconductivity And Competitiveness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration shall utilize existing programs in technology transfer, aeronautics and space technology, and space commercialization to promote the commercial applications of high-temperature superconductors, including applications relating to thin film technology, communications technology, sensors, space power, and propulsion.
