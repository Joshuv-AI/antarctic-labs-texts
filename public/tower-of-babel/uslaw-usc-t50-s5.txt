50 U.S.C. § 5 — Reports of subordinate bodies and committees; unvouchered expenditures
Citation: us:usc:50:5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section5
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 1 (Council Of National Defense). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Reports shall be submitted by all subordinate bodies and by the advisory commission to the council, and from time to time the council shall report to the President or to the heads of executive departments upon special inquiries or subjects appropriate thereto. When deemed proper the President may authorize, in amounts stipulated by him, unvouchered expenditures.
