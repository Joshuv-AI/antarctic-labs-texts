28 C.F.R. § 541.33 — Release from the SHU.
Citation: us:cfr:28:541.33
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-541.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 541 (Inmate Discipline And Special Housing Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Administrative detention status. You will be released from administrative detention status when the reasons for your placement no longer exist.
(b) Disciplinary segregation status. You will be released from disciplinary segregation status after satisfying the sanction imposed by the DHO. The SRO may release you earlier if it is determined you no longer require disciplinary segregation status.
