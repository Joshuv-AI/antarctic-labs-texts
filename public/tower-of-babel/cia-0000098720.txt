Weapons

Many nonnuclear ASW weapons have been exported
to the WP, including RBU rocket Jaunchers and
munitions, torpedoes, and mines. The exported weap-
onry is almost always less effective than that in the
current Soviet inventory. Weapons effectiveness re-
quires not only proper maintenance and practice, but
also proper target location and fire control. The
transferred sensors cannot perform the necessary tar-
get definition with sufficient accuracy or in a timely
manner. Therefore, the weapons pose no threat to
NATO submarines. Some of the weaponry actually
