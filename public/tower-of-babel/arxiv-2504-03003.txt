III
Decays of the hybrid meson

In this section we consider decays of the hybrid charmonium
H_{\mathrm{c}}
. There are two-body processes

,
D^{0}\overline{D}^{\ast}(2007)^{0}

which are kinematically allowed modes of
H_{\mathrm{c}}
. In fact, it is not difficult to see that the mass
m
of

exceeds the two-meson thresholds for these decays. The partial widths of
these channels apart from other factors are determined by the strong
couplings
g_{i}
at the corresponding
H_{\mathrm{c}}
-meson-meson
vertices. To evaluate
g_{i}
, we employ the three-point SR method enabling
us to extract information on the strong form factors
g_{i}(q^{2})
which at
the relevant mass shells
q^{2}=m_{D}^{2}
become equal to the couplings of
interest.

III.1
H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{\ast}(2010)^{-}
,
D^{-}D^{\ast}(2010)^{+}
and
D^{0}\overline{D}^{\ast}(2007)^{0}

Here, we are going to analyze in expanded form the process
H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{\ast}(2010)^{-}
and calculate its partial width
\Gamma[H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{\ast}{}^{-}]
. The
partial width of the second decay is equal to
\Gamma[H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{\ast}{}^{-}]
as well. The quark content
\overline{u}c+(\overline{c}u)^{\ast}
of the mode
H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{0}\overline{D}^{\ast}(2007)^{0}
after replacement
u\rightarrow d
becomes
\overline{d}c+(\overline{c}d)^{\ast}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{\ast}{}^{-}
. In
the present work we adopt an approximation
m_{u}=m_{d}=0
, and use the same
decay constants for the charged and neutral
D^{(\ast)}
particles. The
differences between the masses of the
D^{0}
,
\overline{D}^{\ast}(2007)^{0}
and
D^{+}
,
D^{\ast}(2010)^{-}
mesons are very small and
can be safely neglected. Therefore, with high accuracy the partial width of
the mode
H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{0}\overline{D}^{\ast}(2007)^{0}
is
equal to that of the first decay.

The coupling
g_{1}
that describes strong interaction of particles at the
vertex
H_{\mathrm{c}}D^{+}D^{\ast-}
can be evaluated using the
three-point correlation function. First, we find the sum rule for the form
factor
g_{1}(q^{2})
and consider, to this end, the correlation function

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}(p,p^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle i^{2}\int d^{4}xd^{4}ye^{ip^{\prime}x}e^{iqy}\langle 0|\mathcal{T}\{J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast}}(x)

(29)

\displaystyle\times J^{D}(y)J_{\alpha\beta}^{\dagger}(0)\}|0\rangle.

Here,
J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast}}(x)
and
J^{D}(x)
are interpolating currents of
the vector
D^{\ast}(2010)^{-}
and pseudoscalar
D^{+}
mesons,
respectively. They are defined as

J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast}}(x)=\overline{c}_{i}(x)\gamma_{\mu}d_{i}(x),\ J^{D}(x)=\overline{d}_{j}(x)i\gamma_{5}c_{j}(x),

(30)

with
i
and
j
being the color indices.

To determine the phenomenological side
\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})
of the sum rule, we rewrite Eq. (
29
) in
terms of the particles’ parameters. By taking into account only
contributions of the ground-state particles, we recast the correlator
\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}(p,p^{\prime})
into the form

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})=\frac{\langle 0|J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast}}|D^{\ast-}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon)\rangle}{p^{\prime 2}-m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}}\frac{\langle 0|J^{D}|D^{+}(q)\rangle}{q^{2}-m_{D}^{2}}

\displaystyle\times\langle D^{\ast-}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon)D^{+}(q)|H_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)\rangle\frac{\langle H_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)|J_{\alpha\beta}^{\dagger}|0\rangle}{p^{2}-m^{2}}+\cdots,

(31)

where
m_{D^{\ast}}=(2010.26\pm 0.05)~\mathrm{MeV}
and
m_{D}=(1869.5\pm 0.4)~\mathrm{MeV}
are the masses of the mesons
D^{\ast-}
and
D^{+}

PDG:2024
, respectively. Above, we denote by
\varepsilon_{\mu}(p^{\prime})
the polarization vector of the meson
D^{\ast}{}^{-}
.

To simplify further Eq. (
31
), we introduce the following matrix
elements

\displaystyle\langle 0|J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast}}|D^{\ast-}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon)\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{D^{\ast}}m_{D^{\ast}}\varepsilon_{\mu}(p^{\prime}),

\displaystyle\langle 0|J^{D}|D^{+}(q)\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{f_{D}m_{D}^{2}}{m_{c}},

(32)

where
f_{D^{\ast}}=(223.5\pm 8.4)~\mathrm{MeV}
and
f_{D}=(212.0\pm 0.7)~\mathrm{MeV}
are decay constants of the mesons. We have also to fix the
matrix element
\langle D^{\ast-}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon)D(q)|H_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)\rangle
that is given by the expression

\displaystyle\langle D^{\ast-}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon)D(q)|H_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle g_{1}(q^{2})\epsilon^{\rho\sigma}(p)q_{\rho}\varepsilon_{\sigma}(p^{\prime}).

As a result, for
\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})

we get

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})=g_{1}(q^{2})\frac{\Lambda f_{D^{\ast}}m_{D^{\ast}}f_{D}m_{D}^{2}}{m_{c}\left(p^{2}-m^{2}\right)(p^{\prime 2}-m_{D^{\ast}}^{2})}

\displaystyle\times\frac{1}{(q^{2}-m_{D}^{2})}\left[\frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\alpha}p_{\beta}^{\prime}-\frac{(m^{2}+m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}-q^{2})^{2}}{12m^{2}m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}}g_{\alpha\beta}p_{\mu}^{\prime}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\text{other structures}\right]+\cdots.

(34)

For
\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p,p^{\prime})
one finds

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p,p^{\prime})=-i\int d^{4}xd^{4}ye^{ip^{\prime}x}e^{iqy}g_{s}\frac{{\lambda}_{ab}^{n}}{2}G_{\rho\alpha}^{n}(0)

\displaystyle\times\mathrm{Tr}\left[\gamma_{\beta}S_{d}^{ij}(x-y)\gamma_{5}S_{c}^{jb}(y)\gamma_{5}\sigma_{\mu}^{\rho}S_{c}^{ai}(-x)\right],

(35)

where

is the light quark propagator
Agaev:2020zad
.

\Pi_{1}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p^{2},p^{\prime 2})

(36)

In Eq. (
36

is the

\Pi_{1}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p^{2},p^{\prime 2})

\displaystyle\times e^{-s/M_{1}^{2}-s^{\prime}/M_{2}^{2}}.

The two-point spectral density has the components
\rho_{1}^{\mathrm{pert.}}(s,s^{\prime},q^{2})

\displaystyle\rho_{1}^{\mathrm{pert.}}(s,s^{\prime})=\frac{g_{s}^{2}m_{c}^{2}}{32\pi^{2}}\int_{0}^{1}d\alpha\int_{0}^{1-\alpha}\frac{d\beta\theta(N)}{\alpha\beta^{3}(\alpha+\beta-1)}

In Eqs. (

To determine the width of the decay

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\alpha\beta}(p,p^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})=\frac{\langle 0|J^{D}|D^{+}(p^{\prime})\rangle}{p^{\prime 2}-m_{D}^{2}}\frac{\langle 0|J^{D^{-}}|D^{-}(q)\rangle}{q^{2}-m_{D}^{2}}

Then, the correlator becomes equal to

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})=\frac{\widetilde{g}_{1}(q^{2})\widetilde{\Lambda}f_{D}^{2}m_{D}^{4}}{m_{c}^{2}\left(p^{2}-\widetilde{m}^{2}\right)(p^{\prime 2}-m_{D}^{2})}

\displaystyle\times\frac{1}{(q^{2}-m_{D}^{2})}\left\{\frac{\widetilde{\lambda}^{2}}{3}g_{\alpha\beta}+\left[\frac{m_{D}^{2}}{\widetilde{m}^{2}}+\frac{2\widetilde{\lambda}^{2}}{3\widetilde{m}^{2}}\right]p_{\alpha}p_{\beta}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+p_{\alpha}^{\prime}p_{\beta}^{\prime}-\frac{\widetilde{m}^{2}+m_{D}^{2}-q^{2}}{2\widetilde{m}^{2}}(p_{\alpha}p_{\beta}^{\prime}+p_{\beta}p_{\alpha}^{\prime})\right\},

(63)

where
\widetilde{\lambda}=\lambda(\widetilde{m},m_{D},q)
.

In terms of the quark-gluon propagators the correlation function
\widetilde{\Pi}_{\alpha\beta}(p,p^{\prime})
reads

\displaystyle\Pi_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p,p^{\prime})=\int d^{4}xd^{4}ye^{ip^{\prime}x}e^{iqy}g_{s}\frac{{\lambda}_{ab}^{n}}{2}\widetilde{G}_{\rho\alpha}^{n}(0)

\displaystyle\times\mathrm{Tr}\left[\gamma_{5}S_{d}^{ij}(x-y)\gamma_{5}S_{c}^{jb}(y)\gamma_{5}\sigma_{\beta}^{\rho}S_{c}^{ai}(-x)\right].

(64)

The SR for the form factor
\widetilde{g}_{1}(q^{2})
is derived by
employing invariant amplitudes
\widetilde{\Pi}_{1}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p^{2},p^{\prime 2})
and
\widetilde{\Pi}_{1}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p^{2},p^{\prime 2})
which correspond to terms
g_{\alpha\beta}
in the
correlation functions

\displaystyle\widetilde{g}_{1}(q^{2})=\frac{3m_{c}^{2}(q^{2}-m_{D}^{2})}{\widetilde{\Lambda}f_{D}^{2}m_{D}^{4}\widetilde{\lambda}^{2}}e^{m^{2}/M_{1}^{2}}e^{m_{D}^{2}/M_{2}^{2}}

\displaystyle\times\widetilde{\Pi}_{1}(\mathbf{M}^{2},\mathbf{s}_{0}).

(65)

Here,
\widetilde{\Pi}_{1}(\mathbf{M}^{2},\mathbf{s}_{0})
is the amplitude

\widetilde{\Pi}_{1}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p^{2},p^{\prime 2})
after the Borel
transformations and subtractions.

For the Borel and continuum subtraction parameters in the
D^{-}
channel,
we employ

M_{2}^{2}\in[1.5,3]~\mathrm{GeV}^{2},\ s_{0}^{\prime}\in[5,5.2]~\mathrm{GeV}^{2}.

(66)

The strong coupling
\widetilde{g}_{1}
is fixed at the
D^{-}
meson mass
shell
q^{2}=m_{D}^{2}
by means of the extrapolating function
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{1}(Q^{2})
with parameters
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{1}^{0}=10.23,

\widetilde{c}_{1}^{1}=1.91,
and
\widetilde{c}_{1}^{2}=-0.54
(see, Fig.
4
). It is worth noting that the
functions
\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{i}(Q^{2})
are given by Eq. (
42
) with replacement
m\rightarrow\widetilde{m}
.

As a result, we get

\widetilde{g}_{1}\equiv\widetilde{\mathcal{G}}_{1}(-m_{D}^{2})=(7.24\pm 1.41)\ \mathrm{GeV}^{-1}.

(67)

The partial width of the decay
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{-}
is equal to

\Gamma\left[\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{-}\right]=\frac{\widetilde{g}_{1}^{2}\widetilde{\lambda}_{1}}{960\pi\widetilde{m}^{2}}\left(\widetilde{m}^{2}-4m_{D}^{2}\right)^{2},

(68)

and
\widetilde{\lambda}_{1}=\lambda(\widetilde{m},m_{D},m_{D})
. Then it
is not difficult to evaluate

\Gamma\left[\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{-}\right]=(42.5\pm 12.1)~\mathrm{MeV}.

(69)

The width of the process
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{0}\overline{D}^{0}
amounts to Eq. (
68
) because the quark content
of the mesons
D^{0}\overline{D}^{0}
can be obtained from
D^{+}D^{-}

after replacement
d\rightarrow u
. Note that we ignore the small mass gap
between
D^{\pm}
and
D^{0}(\overline{D}^{0})
mesons.

Here, we consider the process

the SR for the strong form
factor
\widetilde{g}_{2}(q^{2})
at the vertex
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}D^{\ast+}D^{\ast-}
can be obtained from the correlation function

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}(p,p^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle i^{2}\int d^{4}xd^{4}ye^{ip^{\prime}y}e^{iqx}\langle 0|\mathcal{T}\{J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast+}}(x)

(70)

\displaystyle\times J_{\nu}^{D^{\ast}}(y)\widetilde{J}_{\alpha\beta}^{\dagger}(0)\}|0\rangle,

where

and
J_{\nu}^{D^{\ast}}(x)
are the

D^{\ast}(2010)^{+}

), whereas for

J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast+}}(x)=\overline{d}_{i}(x)\gamma_{\mu}c_{i}(x).

(71)

\widetilde{\Pi}_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}(p,p^{\prime})
in terms of the physical parameters of the particles

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})=\frac{\langle 0|J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast+}}|D^{\ast+}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon_{1})\rangle}{p^{\prime 2}-m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}}\frac{\langle 0|J_{\nu}^{D^{\ast}}|D^{\ast-}(q,\varepsilon_{2})\rangle}{q^{2}-m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}}

\displaystyle\times\langle D^{\ast+}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon_{1})D^{\ast-}(q,\varepsilon_{2})|\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)\rangle\frac{\langle\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)|\widetilde{J}_{\alpha\beta}^{\dagger}|0\rangle}{p^{2}-\widetilde{m}^{2}}+\cdots,

where
\varepsilon_{1\mu}
and
\varepsilon_{2\mu}
are the polarization
vectors of the
\ D^{\ast+}
and
D^{\ast-}
mesons, respectively.

The matrix elements which are used are

\displaystyle 0|J_{\mu}^{D^{\ast}+}|D^{\ast+}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon_{1})\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{D^{\ast}}m_{D^{\ast}}\varepsilon_{1\mu}(p^{\prime}),

\displaystyle 0|J_{\nu}^{D^{\ast}}|D^{\ast-}(q,\varepsilon_{2})\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{D^{\ast}}m_{D^{\ast}}\varepsilon_{2\nu}(p^{\prime}),

(73)

where
m_{D^{\ast}}=(1869.5\pm 0.4)\ \mathrm{MeV}
and
f_{D^{\ast}}=(252.2\pm 22.66)\ \mathrm{MeV}
are the mass and decay constant of the
mesons
D^{\ast\pm}
. The vertex
\langle D^{\ast+}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon_{1})D^{\ast-}(q,\varepsilon_{2})|\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)\rangle
has the following form Ref

\displaystyle\langle D^{\ast+}(p^{\prime},\varepsilon_{1})D^{\ast-}(q,\varepsilon_{2})|\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}(p,\epsilon)\rangle=\widetilde{g}_{2}(q^{2})\epsilon_{\tau\rho}\left[\varepsilon_{1}^{\ast}\cdot q\right.

\displaystyle\left.\times\varepsilon_{2}^{\tau\ast}p^{\prime\rho}+\varepsilon_{2}^{\ast}\cdot p^{\prime}\varepsilon_{1}^{\ast\tau}q^{\rho}-p^{\prime}\cdot q\varepsilon_{1}^{\tau\ast}\varepsilon_{2}^{\rho\ast}-\varepsilon_{1}^{\ast}\cdot\varepsilon_{2}^{\ast}p^{\prime\tau}q^{\rho}\right].

(74)

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{Phys}}(p,p^{\prime})=\frac{\widetilde{g}_{2}(q^{2})\widetilde{\Lambda}f_{D^{\ast}}^{2}m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}}{\left(p^{2}-\widetilde{m}^{2}\right)(p^{\prime 2}-m_{D^{\ast}}^{2})(q^{2}-m_{D^{\ast}}^{2})}

\displaystyle\times\left\{\frac{m_{D^{\ast}}^{4}-2m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}(2\widetilde{m}^{2}+q^{2})+(\widetilde{m}^{2}-q^{2})(3\widetilde{m}^{2}-q^{2})}{12\widetilde{m}^{2}}\right.

\displaystyle\times g_{\mu\nu}g_{\alpha\beta}+\frac{1}{3}g_{\alpha\beta}\left(\frac{m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}}{\widetilde{m}^{2}}p_{\mu}p_{\nu}+2p_{\mu}^{\prime}p_{\nu}^{\prime}\right)-\frac{1}{6\widetilde{m}^{2}}g_{\alpha\beta}

\displaystyle\times\left[(m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}+3\widetilde{m}^{2}-q^{2})p_{\mu}p_{\nu}^{\prime}+(m_{D^{\ast}}^{2}+\widetilde{m}^{2}-q^{2})p_{\mu}^{\prime}p_{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle\left.+\text{other structures}\right\}.

(75)

For the QCD side of the sum rule, we obtain

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{OPE}}(p,p^{\prime})=i^{2}\int d^{4}xd^{4}ye^{ip^{\prime}x}e^{iqy}g_{s}\frac{{\lambda}_{ab}^{n}}{2}\widetilde{G}_{\rho\alpha}^{n}(0)

\displaystyle\times\mathrm{Tr}\left[\gamma_{\mu}S_{d}^{ij}(x-y)\gamma_{\nu}S_{c}^{jb}(y)\gamma_{5}\sigma_{\beta}^{\rho}S_{c}^{ai}(-x)\right].

V
Concluding notes

In the present article, we have evaluated the parameters of the tensor
hybrid charmonia
H_{\mathrm{c}}
and
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}
. Our
predictions for the masses of these states
m=(4.16\pm 0.14)~\mathrm{GeV}

and
\widetilde{m}=(4.5\pm 0.1)~\mathrm{GeV}
demonstrate that these
structures are unstable against strong decays to conventional meson pairs.

We have also estimated the full width of these hybrid charmonia. In the case
of the structure
H_{\mathrm{c}}
we have studied the processes
H_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{(\pm)}D^{\ast}{}^{(\mp)}
,
D^{0}\overline{D}^{\ast}{}^{0}
, and
D_{s}^{(\pm)}D_{s}^{\ast}{}^{(\mp)}
. The full width of
H_{\mathrm{c}}
saturated by these decay channels amounts to
\Gamma\left[H_{\mathrm{c}}\right]=(160\pm 23)~\mathrm{MeV}
. The width of the hybrid
state
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}
is equal to
\Gamma\left[\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\right]=(206\pm 25)~\mathrm{MeV}
which have been evaluated by
taking into account the kinematically allowed processes
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow D^{+}D^{-}
,
D^{0}\overline{D}^{0}
,
D^{\ast+}D^{\ast-}
,
D^{\ast 0}\overline{D}^{\ast 0}
,
D_{s}^{+}D_{s}^{-}
and
D_{s}^{\ast+}D_{s}^{\ast-}
. Clearly, both of these hypothetical hybrid
charmonia are broad structures.

The masses of the tensor hybrid charmonia
H_{\mathrm{c}}
and
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}
were computed in numerous publications. In the framework of
the sum rule method these particles were investigated in Refs.
Chen:2013zia
;
Wang:2024hvp
. In the first paper, the authors predicted for
relevant masses
(4.04\pm~0.23)~\mathrm{GeV}
and
(4.45\pm~0.27)~\mathrm{GeV}
, respectively. Analysis made in Ref.
Wang:2024hvp
led to the
results
(4.31\pm~0.08)~\mathrm{GeV}
and
(4.85\pm~0.06)~\mathrm{GeV}
.
As is seen,
m
is larger than prediction of Ref.
Chen:2013zia
, but
\widetilde{m}
is in nice agreement with the result of the same paper. The
masses extracted in Ref.
Wang:2024hvp
exceed our predictions for
both
m
and
\widetilde{m}
.

It is interesting to compare our results with predictions obtained using
alternative methods. Thus, the mass range
4.046-4.394~\mathrm{GeV}
for the
hybrid
H_{\mathrm{c}}
and
(4.232\pm 0.030)~\mathrm{GeV}
for the
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}
were predicted by the Born-Oppenheimer Effective
Field Theory
Soto:2023lbh
. In the context of the lattice simulations
these hybrids have approximately the masses
4.46~\mathrm{GeV}
and
4.62~\mathrm{GeV}

Ryan:2020iog
, respectively. Our result for
m
is
smaller than prediction of Ref.
Ryan:2020iog
, and comparable with
one from Ref.
Soto:2023lbh
. The parameter
\widetilde{m}
is
compatible with the result found in the context of the lattice computations,
whereas overshoots estimate made in Ref.
Soto:2023lbh
. Evidently,
there ia a necessity to continue relevant studies to improve agreement
between these predictions.

Another important measurable parameter of the hybrid mesons is widths of
these structures. Information on decay pattern and full width of the hybrid
states may allow one to distinguish them from other exotic mesons. It should
be noted that there is a deficiency of information about decays of the heavy
hybrid mesons in literature. In this article, for the first time, we
calculated widths
\Gamma\left[\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}\right]
and
\Gamma\left[H_{\mathrm{c}}\right]
of the tensor hybrids in the context
of the SR method. We saturated their full widths by five and six decay
channels, respectively. It turned out, that
H_{\mathrm{c}}
and
\widetilde{H}_{\mathrm{c}}
are rather broad structures. Predictions for these
parameters can be refined by including into consideration their other decay
modes. Computation of the full widths of different heavy hybrid mesons
should also be on agenda of researches as an important source of valuable
theoretical information.
