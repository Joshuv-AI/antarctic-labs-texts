28 C.F.R. § 33.53 — Participation by faith-based organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:28:33.53
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-33.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 33 (Bureau Of Justice Assistance Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The funds provided under this part shall be administered in compliance with the standards set forth in part 38 (Equal Treatment for Faith-based Organizations) of this chapter.
