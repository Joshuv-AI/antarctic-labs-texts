29 C.F.R. § 458.3 — Application of LMRDA labor organization reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:29:458.3
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-458.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 458 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reporting provisions of parts 402, 403, and 408 of this chapter shall apply to labor organizations subject to the requirements of the CSRA or FSA.
