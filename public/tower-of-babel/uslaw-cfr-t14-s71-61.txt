14 C.F.R. § 71.61 — Class D airspace.
Citation: us:cfr:14:71.61
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-71.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 71 (Designation Of Class A, B, C, D, And E Airspace Areas; Air Traffic Service Routes; And Reporting Points). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Class D airspace areas listed in subpart D of FAA Order JO 7400.11M (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) consist of specified airspace within which all aircraft operators are subject to operating rules and equipment requirements specified in part 91 of this chapter. Each Class D airspace area designated for an airport in subpart D of FAA Order JO 7400.11M (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) contains at least one primary airport around which the airspace is designated.
