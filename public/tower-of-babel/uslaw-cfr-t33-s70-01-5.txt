33 C.F.R. § 70.01-5 — -5 Penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:33:70.01-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-70.01-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 70 (Interference With Or Damage To Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person violating the provisions of this section shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and be subject to a fine not exceeding the sum of $500 for each offense, and each day during which such violation shall continue shall be considered a new offense.
