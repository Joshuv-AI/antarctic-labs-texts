21 C.F.R. § 607.7 — Establishment registration and product listing of blood banks and other firms manufacturing human blood and blood products.
Citation: us:cfr:21:607.7
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-607.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 607 (Establishment Registration And Product Listing For Manufacturers Of Human Blood And Blood Products And Licensed Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All owners or operators of establishments that engage in the manufacturing of blood products are required to register, pursuant to section 510 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. Registration and listing of blood products must comply with this part. Registration does not permit any blood bank or similar establishment to ship blood products in interstate commerce.
