20 C.F.R. § 408.922 — When will we adjust your SVB payments to recover an overpayment?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.922
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.922
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you do not refund your overpayment to us, and waiver of recovery is not applicable, we will adjust any SVB payments due you to recover the overpayment. Adjustment will generally be accomplished by withholding each month the amount set forth in § 408.923 from the benefit payable to you.
