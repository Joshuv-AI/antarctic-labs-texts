Inconsistency of Reinhardt Cardinals with 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF}
Rupert 𝐌 𝐜 ​ 𝐂𝐚𝐥𝐥𝐮𝐦 \mathbf{M^{c}Callum}

Abstract
. A proof will be presented that the existence of a non-trivial Σ 1 \Sigma_{1} -elementary embedding j : V λ + 3 ≺ V λ + 3 j:V_{\lambda+3}\prec V_{\lambda+3} is inconsistent with ZF . Sections 1 and 2 shall review various important contributions from the literature, notably including [ 5 ] , [ 10 ] , and [ 11 ] , the latter reference being where the crucial forcing construction is presented. Section 3 shall introduce some new large cardinal properties, of consistency strength intermediate between 𝖨 𝟥 \mathsf{I_{3}} and 𝖨 𝟤 \mathsf{I_{2}} , and greater than 𝖨 𝟣 \mathsf{I_{1}} , respectively. The proof of the inconsistency with 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF} of the existence of a non-trivial Σ 1 \Sigma_{1} -elementary embedding j : V λ + 3 ≺ V λ + 3 j:V_{\lambda+3}\prec V_{\lambda+3} shall be given in Section 4. The claims of Sections 2 and 4 are provable in ZF ; those of Section 3, with the exception of the last two theorems, in ZFC .

1.
Introduction

In
[
1

, Joan Bagaria, Peter Koellner, and Hugh Woodin describe how set theory is “at a crossroads” and facing two possible futures, in one of which various conjectures are confirmed which would imply the inconsistency of the choiceless large cardinal axioms, and in the other of which we gain confidence that the choiceless large cardinal axioms are consistent. In that work the spectrum of choiceless large cardinal axioms is described, up to and including Reinhardt cardinals, super-Reinhardt cardinals, Berkeley cardinals, club Berkeley cardinals, and limit club Berkeley cardinals. Rafaella Cutolo in
[

ZF

In the opening sections of what follows, we shall build on work of Gabriel Goldberg and Farmer Schultzenberg in

to show that

+“There exists a non-trivial

A corollary will be that almost all of the hierarchy of the choiceless cardinals is inconsistent with
