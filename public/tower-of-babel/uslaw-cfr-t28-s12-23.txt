28 C.F.R. § 12.23 — Deficient registration statement.
Citation: us:cfr:28:12.23
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-12.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 12 (Registration Of Certain Persons Having Knowledge Of Foreign Espionage, Counterespionage, Or Sabotage Matters Under The Act Of August 1, 1956). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A registration statement which is determined to be incomplete, inaccurate, misleading, or false, by the Chief Registration Unit, may be returned by him to the registrant as being unacceptable for filing under the terms of the act.
