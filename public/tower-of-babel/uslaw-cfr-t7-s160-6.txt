7 C.F.R. § 160.6 — Standard designations for turpentine.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.6
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Spirits of turpentine within the meaning of the act and the provisions in this part shall be designated as “gum spirits of turpentine,” “steam distilled wood turpentine,” “destructively distilled wood turpentine,” or “sulphate wood turpentine,” as the case may be.
