12 C.F.R. § 1081.300 — Public hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1081.300
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1081.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1081 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudication Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All hearings in adjudication proceedings shall be public unless a confidentiality order is entered by the hearing officer pursuant to § 1081.119 or unless otherwise ordered by the Director on the grounds that holding an open hearing would be contrary to the public interest.
