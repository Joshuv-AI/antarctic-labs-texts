10 U.S.C. § 7278 — Medal of honor; distinguished-service cross; distinguished-service medal; silver star: availability of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:10:7278
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7278
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 737 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army may spend, from any appropriation for contingent expenses of the Department of the Army, amounts necessary to provide medals and devices under sections 7271, 7272, 7273, 7274, 7276, 7277, and 7282 of this title.
