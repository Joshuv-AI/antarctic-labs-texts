10 U.S.C. § 8895 — Stay extended or shortened
Citation: us:usc:10:8895
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8895
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 885 (Stay Of Judicial Proceedings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy, when a stay under this chapter is in effect, may file with the court, or a judge of the court, a certificate extending or shortening the time stated in the prior certificate. The filing of such a new certificate extends or shortens the stay to the period specified in the new certificate or terminates the stay if the new certificate so states.
