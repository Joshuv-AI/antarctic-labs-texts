30 C.F.R. § 707.10 — Information collection.
Citation: us:cfr:30:707.10
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-707.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 707 (Exemption For Coal Extraction Incident To Government-Financed Highway Or Other Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Since the information collection requirement contained in 30 CFR 707.12 consists only of expenditures on information collection activities that would be incurred by persons in the normal course of their activities, it is exempt from the requirements of the Paperwork Reduction Act (44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq.) and does not require clearance by OMB.
