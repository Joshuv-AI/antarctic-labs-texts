Retraction Note: Antiphospholipid antibodies inhibit the migration and invasion of trophoblast cells by suppressing the JNK/C-Jun/MMP1 signaling pathway
Authors: Qingchen Wang, Weimin Feng, Yuan Tan, Jiao Qiao, Hongchao Liu, Qi Liu, He Wang, Qian Zhang, Jingjin Tao, Zhongxin Li, Boxin Yang, Zhen Xu, Chong Wang, Shuo Yang, Liyan Cui
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Journal of Translational Medicine (2025) 23:581

10.1186/s12967-025-06596-y

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. After publication, the authors contacted the publisher requesting a correction to address image duplication between Figs. 4A and 6B (migration APS and APS+Anisomycin, and invasion APS images). The authors provided the underlying raw data for validation; however, further checks by the publisher found additional overlaps between original images representing different samples or assays. The Editor-in-Chief therefore has lost confidence in the data presented in the article.

On behalf of all authors, Liyan Cui stated that they agree to this retraction.
