16 U.S.C. § 7141 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:7141
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7141
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 90 (Secure Rural Schools And Community Self-Determination); Subchapter III (County Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter:
(1) County funds
The term “county funds” means all funds an eligible county elects under section 7112(d) of this title to reserve for expenditure in accordance with this subchapter.
(2) Participating county
The term “participating county” means an eligible county that elects under section 7112(d) of this title to expend a portion of the Federal funds received under section 7112 of this title in accordance with this subchapter.
