46 C.F.R. § 25.10-1 — -1 Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 25 (Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to vessel manufacturers, distributors, and dealers installing navigation lights on all uninspected commercial vessels, except those completed before November 7, 2002.
