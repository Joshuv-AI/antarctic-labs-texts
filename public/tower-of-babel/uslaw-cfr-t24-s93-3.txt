24 C.F.R. § 93.3 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:93.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-93.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 93 (Housing Trust Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD may, upon a determination of good cause and subject to statutory limitations, waive any provision of this part and delegate this authority in accordance with section 106 of the Department of Housing and Urban Development Reform Act of 1989 (42 U.S.C. 3535(q)).
