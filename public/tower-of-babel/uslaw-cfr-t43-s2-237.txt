43 C.F.R. § 2.237 — Requests for access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2.237
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2.237
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2 (Freedom Of Information Act; Records And Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Privacy Act permits individuals, upon request, to gain access to their records or to any information pertaining to them which is contained in a system and to review the records and have a copy made of all or any portion thereof in a form comprehensive to them. 5 U.S.C. 552a(d)(1). A request for access shall be submitted in accordance with the procedures in this subpart.
