33 U.S.C. § 3911 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:33:3911
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3911
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 52 (Water Infrastructure Finance And Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary or the Administrator, as applicable, may promulgate such regulations as the Secretary or Administrator determines to be appropriate to carry out this chapter.
