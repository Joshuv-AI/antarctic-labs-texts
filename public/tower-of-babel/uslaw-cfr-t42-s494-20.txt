42 C.F.R. § 494.20 — Condition: Compliance with Federal, State, and local laws and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:42:494.20
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-494.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 494 (Conditions For Coverage For End-Stage Renal Disease Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The facility and its staff must operate and furnish services in compliance with applicable Federal, State, and local laws and regulations pertaining to licensure and any other relevant health and safety requirements.
