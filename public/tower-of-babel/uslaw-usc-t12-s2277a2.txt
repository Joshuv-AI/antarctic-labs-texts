12 U.S.C. § 2277a–2 — Board of Directors

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter V (Farm Credit Administration Organization); Part E (Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Establishment
The Corporation shall be managed by a Board of Directors that shall consist of the members of the Farm Credit Administration Board.
(b) Chairman
The Board of Directors shall be chaired by any Board member other than the Chairman of the Farm Credit Administration Board.
