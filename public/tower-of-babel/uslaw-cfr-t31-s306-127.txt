31 C.F.R. § 306.127 — Preservation of existing rights.
Citation: us:cfr:31:306.127
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-306.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 306 (General Regulations Governing U.S. Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in these regulations shall limit or restrict existing rights which holders of securities heretofore issued may have acquired under the circulars offering such securities for sale or under the regulations in force at the time of acquisition.
