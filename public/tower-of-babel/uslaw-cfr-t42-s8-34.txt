42 C.F.R. § 8.34 — Court review of final administrative action; exhaustion of administrative remedies.
Citation: us:cfr:42:8.34
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-8.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 8 (Medications For The Treatment Of Opioid Use Disorder). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before any legal action is filed in court challenging the suspension, proposed revocation, or adverse action, respondent shall exhaust administrative remedies provided under this subpart, unless otherwise provided by Federal law. The reviewing official's decision, under § 8.28(e) or § 8.33(a), constitutes final agency action as of the date of the decision.
