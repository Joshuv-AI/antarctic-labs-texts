49 C.F.R. § 40.51 — What materials are used to send oral fluid specimens to the laboratory?
Citation: us:cfr:49:40.51
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-40.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 40 (Procedures For Transportation Workplace Drug And Alcohol Testing Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, you must use a shipping container that adequately protects the specimen bottles from damage in the transport of specimens from the collection site to the laboratory.
(b) You are not required to use a shipping container if a laboratory courier hand-delivers the specimens from the collection site to the laboratory.
