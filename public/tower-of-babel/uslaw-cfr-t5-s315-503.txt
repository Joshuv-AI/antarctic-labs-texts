5 C.F.R. § 315.503 — Acquisition of competitive status.
Citation: us:cfr:5:315.503
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-315.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 315 (Career And Career-Conditional Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who was serving probation when he was appointed under § 315.501 acquires a competitive status automatically on completion of probation.
