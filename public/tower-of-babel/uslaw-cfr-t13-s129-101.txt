13 C.F.R. § 129.101 — What contracting preferences are available for small business concerns located in disaster areas?
Citation: us:cfr:13:129.101
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-129.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 129 (Contracts For Small Businesses Located In Disaster Areas, And Surplus Personal Property For Small Businesses Located In Disaster Areas, Puerto Rico, And Covered Territory Businesses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers may set aside solicitations for emergency response contracts to allow only small businesses located in the disaster area to compete.
