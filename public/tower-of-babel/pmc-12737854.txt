Exosome Therapy for Chronic Wound Healing – Retraction
Authors: Lucas Basamage, Hee Jin Ahn, Ho-Sung Choi, Carlos Roberto Antonio, Alessandro Louza Alarcão, Shirley Nogueira Silva, Zeisa Teixeira Hohl, Jovian Wan, Kyu-Ho Yi
Published: 2025

At the request of the authors, the editors and publisher retract the article “Exosome Therapy for Chronic Wound Healing” by Basamage et al, published in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Global Open (Volume 13, Issue 10, October 3, 2025).

The rationale for the retraction is as follows: the authors discovered an error regarding the source of exosomes used in the procedure. Adipose-derived stem-cell exosomes were reported, whereas the actual material used was edelweiss callus-derived exosomes. This discrepancy compromises the validity of the findings and conclusions.
