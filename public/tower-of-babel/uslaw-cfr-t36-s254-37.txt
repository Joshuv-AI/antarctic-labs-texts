36 C.F.R. § 254.37 — Conveyance of parcels 40 acres or less that no longer meet National Forest System objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:36:254.37
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-254.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 254 (Landownership Adjustments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart allows conveyance of parcels of 40 acres or less that are determined by Forest Service officials to:
(1) be physically isolated from other Federal land; or
(2) be inaccessible; or
(3) have lost National Forest character.
