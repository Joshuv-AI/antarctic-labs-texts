20 C.F.R. § 220.28 — How long the impairment must last.
Citation: us:cfr:20:220.28
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-220.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 220 (Determining Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the claimant's impairment is expected to result in death, it must have lasted or must be expected to last for a continuous period of at least 12 months. This is known as the duration requirement.
