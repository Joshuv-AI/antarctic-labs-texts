14 C.F.R. § 414.41 — Hearings in safety element approval actions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:414.41
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-414.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 414 (Safety Element Approvals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The FAA will give the safety element approval applicant or holder, as appropriate, written notice stating the reason for issuing a denial or for modifying, suspending, or revoking a safety element approval under this part.
(b) A safety element approval applicant or holder is entitled to a determination on the record after an opportunity for a hearing.
