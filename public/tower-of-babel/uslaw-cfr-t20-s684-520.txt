20 C.F.R. § 684.520 — Must Indian and Native American program grantees give preference to Indian and Native American entities in the selection of contractors or service providers?
Citation: us:cfr:20:684.520
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-684.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 684 (Indian And Native American Programs Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, INA program grantees must give as much preference as possible to Indian organizations and to Indian-owned economic enterprises, as defined in sec. 3 of the Indian Financing Act of 1974 (25 U.S.C. 1452), when awarding any contract or subgrant.
