30 C.F.R. § 22.0 — Compliance with the requirements necessary for obtaining approval.
Citation: us:cfr:30:22.0
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-22.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 22 (Portable Methane Detectors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive approval of MSHA for any portable methane detectors a manufacturer must comply with the requirements specified in this part.
