5 U.S.C. § 9009 — Cost accounting standards
Citation: us:usc:5:9009
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section9009
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 90 (Long-Term Care Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The cost accounting standards issued pursuant to section 1502(a) and (b) of title 41 shall not apply with respect to a long-term care insurance contract under this chapter.
