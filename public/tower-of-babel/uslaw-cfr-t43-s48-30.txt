43 C.F.R. § 48.30 — How does the Secretary determine if Congressional approval is unnecessary?
Citation: us:cfr:43:48.30
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-48.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 48 (Amendments To The Hawaiian Homes Commission Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will determine that Congressional approval is unnecessary if the proposed amendment meets none of the criteria in § 48.20.
