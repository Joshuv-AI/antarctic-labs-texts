36 U.S.C. § 210307 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:210307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section210307
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2103 (369Th Veterans’ Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the exclusive right to use the name “369th Veterans’ Association” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts. This section does not affect any vested rights.
