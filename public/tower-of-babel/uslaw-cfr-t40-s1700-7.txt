40 C.F.R. § 1700.7 — No-discharge zones.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1700.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Defense; Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1700.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1700 (Uniform National Discharge Standards For Vessels Of The Armed Forces). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For this part, a no-discharge zone is a waterbody, or portion thereof, where one or more discharges incidental to the normal operation of Armed Forces vessels, whether treated or not, are prohibited. A no-discharge zone is established either by State prohibition using the procedures in § 1700.9, or by EPA prohibition, upon application of a State, using the procedures in § 1700.10.
