40 C.F.R. § 86.401-2006 — -2006 General applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:86.401-2006
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-86.401-2006
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 86 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Highway Vehicles And Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to 1978 and later model year, new, gasoline-fueled motorcycles built after December 31, 1977, and to 1990 and later model year, new methanol-fueled motorcycles built after December 31, 1989 and to 1997 and later model year, new natural gas-fueled and liquefied petroleum gas-fueled motorcycles built after December 31, 1996 and to 2006 and later model year new motorcycles, regardless of fuel.
