2 U.S.C. § 4312 — Regulations governing availability of appropriations for House committee employees
Citation: us:usc:2:4312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4312
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 43 (Congressional Committees); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriations for committee employees shall be available in such amounts and under such regulations as may be approved by the Committee on House Oversight for compensation of employees of the standing committees of the House of Representatives, except the Committee on Appropriations.
