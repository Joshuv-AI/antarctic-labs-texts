29 C.F.R. § 1926.1439 — Dedicated pile drivers.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1926.1439
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1926.1439
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1926 (Safety And Health Regulations For Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of subpart CC apply to dedicated pile drivers, except as specified in this section.
(b) Section 1926.1416(d)(3) (Anti two-blocking device) does not apply.
(c) Section 1926.1416(e)(4) (Load weighing and similar devices) applies only to dedicated pile drivers manufactured after November 8, 2011.
(d) In § 1926.1433, only §§ 1926.1433(d) and (e) apply to dedicated pile drivers.
