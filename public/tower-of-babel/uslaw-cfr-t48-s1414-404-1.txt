48 C.F.R. § 1414.404-1 — 1414.404-1 Cancellation of invitations after opening.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1414.404-1
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1414.404-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1414 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CCO is authorized to make the written determination to cancel the IFB before award but after bid opening.
