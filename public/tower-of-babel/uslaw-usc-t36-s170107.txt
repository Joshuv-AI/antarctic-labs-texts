36 U.S.C. § 170107 — Headquarters and principal place of business
Citation: us:usc:36:170107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section170107
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1701 (Paralyzed Veterans Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The headquarters and principal place of business of the corporation shall be in the District of Columbia. However, the activities of the corporation are not confined to the District of Columbia but may be conducted throughout the States, territories, and possessions of the United States.
