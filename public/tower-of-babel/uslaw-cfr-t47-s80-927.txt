47 C.F.R. § 80.927 — Antenna radio frequency indicator.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.927
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.927
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transmitter must be equipped with a device which provides visual indication whenever the transmitter is supplying power to the antenna.
