12 C.F.R. § 1510.6 — What must the Funding Corporation do with surplus funds?
Citation: us:cfr:12:1510.6
Creator: Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1510.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1510 (Resolution Funding Corporation Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Funding Corporation has funds that are not needed for current interest payments on obligations, it must invest the funds in obligations of the United States issued by the Secretary, in accordance with an investment policy approved by the Secretary.
