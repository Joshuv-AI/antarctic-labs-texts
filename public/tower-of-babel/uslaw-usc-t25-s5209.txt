25 U.S.C. § 5209 — Rules and regulations; repeals
Citation: us:usc:25:5209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5209
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45A (Oklahoma Indian Welfare). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to prescribe such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter. All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this chapter are repealed.
