25 C.F.R. § 290.13 — Under what conditions may an Indian tribe distribute per capita payments?
Citation: us:cfr:25:290.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-290.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 290 (Tribal Revenue Allocation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may make per capita payments only after the ABO approves your tribal revenue allocation plan.
