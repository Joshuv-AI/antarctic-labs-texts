12 C.F.R. § 1206.7 — Delinquent payment.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1206.7
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1206.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1206 (Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may assess interest and penalties on any delinquent semiannual payment or other payment assessed under this part in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3717 (interest and penalty on claims) and part 1704 of this title (debt collection).
