22 U.S.C. § 4313 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:22:4313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4313
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 53 (Authorities Relating To The Regulation Of Foreign Missions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of this chapter and the application of such provision to any other person or circumstance shall not be affected thereby.
