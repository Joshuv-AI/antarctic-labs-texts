40 C.F.R. § 459.10 — Applicability; description of the photographic processing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:459.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-459.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 459 (Photographic Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to point source discharges resulting from the development or printing of paper prints, slides, negatives, enlargements, movie film, and other sensitized materials except that facilities processing 150 sq. meters (1600 sq. feet) per day or less are not covered. Both commercial and military facilities are covered by this subpart.
