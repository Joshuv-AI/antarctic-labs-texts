46 C.F.R. § 8.250 — Acceptance of standards and functions delegated under existing regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:8.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-8.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 8 (Vessel Inspection Alternatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Classification society class rules will only be accepted as equivalent to Coast Guard regulatory standards when that classification society has received authorization to conduct a related delegated function.
(b) A recognized classification society may not conduct any delegated function under this title until it receives a separate written authorization from the Commandant to conduct that specific function.
