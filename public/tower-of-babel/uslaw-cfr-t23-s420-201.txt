23 C.F.R. § 420.201 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:23:420.201
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-420.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 420 (Planning And Research Program Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to prescribe requirements for research, development, and technology transfer (RD&T) activities, programs, and studies undertaken by State DOTs and their subrecipients with FHWA planning and research funds.
