29 C.F.R. § 570.127 — Homeworkers engaged in the making of evergreen wreaths.
Citation: us:cfr:29:570.127
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-570.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 570 (Child Labor Regulations, Orders And Statements Of Interpretation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FLSA section 13(d) provides an exemption from the child labor provisions, as well as the minimum wage and overtime provisions, for homeworkers engaged in the making of wreaths composed principally of natural holly, pine, cedar, or other evergreens (including the harvesting of the evergreens or other forest products used in making such wreaths).
