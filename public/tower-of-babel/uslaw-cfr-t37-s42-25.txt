37 C.F.R. § 42.25 — Default filing times.
Citation: us:cfr:37:42.25
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-42.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 42 (Trial Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A motion may only be filed according to a schedule set by the Board. The default times for acting are:
(1) An opposition is due one month after service of the motion; and
(2) A reply is due one month after service of the opposition.
(b) A party should seek relief promptly after the need for relief is identified. Delay in seeking relief may justify a denial of relief sought.
