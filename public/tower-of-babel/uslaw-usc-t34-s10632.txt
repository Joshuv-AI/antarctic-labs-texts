34 U.S.C. § 10632 — State reentry project evaluation
Citation: us:usc:34:10632
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10632
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXXI (Offender Reentry And Community Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Evaluation
The Attorney General shall evaluate the demonstration projects authorized by section 10631 of this title to determine their effectiveness.
(b) Report
Not later than April 30, 2005, the Attorney General shall submit a report to the Committees on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives and the Senate containing—
(1)
the findings of the evaluation required by subsection (a); and
(2)
any recommendations the Attorney General has with regard to expanding, changing, or eliminating the demonstration projects.
