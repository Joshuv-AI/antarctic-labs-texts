32 C.F.R. § 1803.31 — Requirements as to time and form.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1803.31
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1803.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1803 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Section 3.6 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appeals of decisions must be received by the Coordinator within forty-five (45) days of the date of mailing of NACIC's initial decision. It shall identify with specificity the documents or information to be considered on appeal and it may, but need not, provide a factual or legal basis for the appeal.
