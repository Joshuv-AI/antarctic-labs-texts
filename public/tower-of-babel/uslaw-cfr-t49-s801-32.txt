49 C.F.R. § 801.32 — Accident reports.
Citation: us:cfr:49:801.32
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-801.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 801 (Public Availability Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The NTSB will report the facts, conditions, circumstances, and its determination of the probable causes of U.S. civil transportation accidents, in accordance with 49 U.S.C. 1131(e).
(b) These reports will be made available on the NTSB electronic reading room.
