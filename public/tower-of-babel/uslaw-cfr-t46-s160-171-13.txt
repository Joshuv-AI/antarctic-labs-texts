46 C.F.R. § 160.171-13 — -13 Storage case.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.171-13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.171-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each suit must have a storage case made of vinyl coated cloth or material that provides an equivalent measure of protection to the suit.
(b) Each storage case must be designed so that it is still useable after two seconds contact with a gasoline fire.
