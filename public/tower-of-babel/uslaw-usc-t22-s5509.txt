22 U.S.C. § 5509 — Official Department of State recognition
Citation: us:usc:22:5509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5509
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 64 (United States Response To Terrorism Affecting Americans Abroad). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 60 days after November 16, 1990, the Secretary of State shall promulgate guidelines for appropriate ceremonies or other official expressions of respect and support for the families of United States citizens who are killed through acts of terrorism abroad.
