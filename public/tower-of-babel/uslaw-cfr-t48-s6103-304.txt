48 C.F.R. § 6103.304 — 6103.304 Reply to the audit division and agency responses [Rule 304].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6103.304
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6103.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6103 (Transportation Rate Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claimant may file a reply to the Audit Division and agency responses within 30 calendar days after receiving the responses. The claimant shall provide a copy of the response to the Audit Division and the agency. To expedite proceedings, if the claimant does not wish to respond, the claimant should so notify the Board, the Audit Division, and the agency.
