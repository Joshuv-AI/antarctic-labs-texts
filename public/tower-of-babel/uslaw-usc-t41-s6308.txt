41 U.S.C. § 6308 — Contracts for transportation of Federal Government securities
Citation: us:usc:41:6308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section6308
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 63 (General Contract Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When practicable, a contract for transporting bullion, cash, or securities of the Federal Government shall be awarded to the lowest responsible bidder after notice to all parties with means of transportation.
