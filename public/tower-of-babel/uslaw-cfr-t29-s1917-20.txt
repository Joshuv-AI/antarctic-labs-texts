29 C.F.R. § 1917.20 — Interference with communications.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1917.20
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1917.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1917 (Marine Terminals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cargo handling operations shall not be carried on when noise-producing, maintenance, construction or repair work interferes with the communication of warnings or instructions.
