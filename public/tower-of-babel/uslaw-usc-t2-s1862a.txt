2 U.S.C. § 1862a — Use of construction project funds to reimburse Capitol Police for related overtime costs
Citation: us:usc:2:1862a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1862a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter IV (Appropriations And Expenditures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Payment of overtime costs
The Architect of the Capitol shall transfer amounts made available for construction projects during a fiscal year to the applicable appropriations accounts of the United States Capitol Police in order to reimburse the Capitol Police for overtime costs incurred in connection with such projects.
(b) Effective date
This section shall apply with respect to fiscal year 2013 and each succeeding fiscal year.
