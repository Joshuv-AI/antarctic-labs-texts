32 C.F.R. § 935.60 — Wake Island Judicial Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.60
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The judicial authority under this part is vested in the Wake Island Court and the Wake Island Court of Appeals.
(b) The Wake Island Court and the Wake Island Court of Appeals shall each have a seal approved by the General Counsel.
(c) Judges and Clerks of the Courts may administer oaths.
