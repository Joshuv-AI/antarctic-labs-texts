33 C.F.R. § 20.804 — Objections and offers of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:33:20.804
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-20.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 20 (Rules Of Practice, Procedure, And Evidence For Formal Administrative Proceedings Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any party objecting to the admission or exclusion of evidence shall concisely state the grounds. A ruling on every objection must appear in the record. No party may raise an objection to the admission or exclusion of evidence on appeal unless he or she raised it before the ALJ.
(b) Whenever evidence is objected to, the party offering it may make an offer of proof, which must appear in the record.
