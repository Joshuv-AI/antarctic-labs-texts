13 C.F.R. § 126.202 — Who does SBA consider to control a HUBZone small business concern?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.202
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Control means both the day-to-day management and long-term decision-making authority for the HUBZone small business concern. Many persons may share control of a concern, including each of those occupying the following positions: officer, director, general partner, managing partner, managing member and manager.
