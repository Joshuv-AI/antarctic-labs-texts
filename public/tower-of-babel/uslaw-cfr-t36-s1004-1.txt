36 C.F.R. § 1004.1 — Applicability and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1004.1
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1004.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1004 (Vehicles And Traffic Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicability of the regulations in this part is described in § 1001.2 of this chapter. The regulations in this part also apply, regardless of land ownership, on all roadways and parking areas within the boundaries of the area administered by the Presidio Trust that are open to public traffic and that are under the legislative jurisdiction of the United States.
