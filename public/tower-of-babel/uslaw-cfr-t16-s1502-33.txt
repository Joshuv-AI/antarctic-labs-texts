16 C.F.R. § 1502.33 — Official notice.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1502.33
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1502.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1502 (Procedures For Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Official notice may be taken of such matters as might be judicially noticed by the courts of the United States or of any other matter peculiarly within the general knowledge of CPSC as an expert agency.
(b) If official notice is taken of a material fact not appearing in the evidence of record, a participant, on timely request, will be afforded an opportunity to show the contrary.
