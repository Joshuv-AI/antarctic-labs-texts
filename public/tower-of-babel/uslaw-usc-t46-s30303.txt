46 U.S.C. § 30303 — Amount and apportionment of recovery
Citation: us:usc:46:30303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 303 (Death On The High Seas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The recovery in an action under this chapter shall be a fair compensation for the pecuniary loss sustained by the individuals for whose benefit the action is brought. The court shall apportion the recovery among those individuals in proportion to the loss each has sustained.
