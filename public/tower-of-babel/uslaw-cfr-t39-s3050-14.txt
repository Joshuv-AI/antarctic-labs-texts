39 C.F.R. § 3050.14 — Format of the Postal Service's section 3652 report.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3050.14
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3050.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3050 (Periodic Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service's Cost and Revenue Analysis (CRA) report shall be presented in a format reflecting the classification structure in the Mail Classification Schedule.
