41 C.F.R. § 301-71.105 — -71.105 Internal policies for travel authorization.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-71.105
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-71.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-71 (71—Agency Travel Accountability Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must establish clear guidelines for using different types of travel authorizations, consistent with the regulations in this chapter, and specific criteria identifying who is authorized to sign travel authorizations.
