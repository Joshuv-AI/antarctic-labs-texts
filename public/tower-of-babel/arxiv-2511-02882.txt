Asymptotic analysis of a stochastic SVEIS epidemic model using Black-Karasinski process
Lahcen Khammich, Driss Kiouach

Abstract
In this paper, we present a stochastic SVEIS epidemic model perturbed by a Black–Karasinski process. Using a Lyapunov functional approach, we derive a sufficient condition, ℛ 0 s > 1 \mathcal{R}_{0}^{s}>1 for the existence of a stationary distribution, which indicates disease persistence. Additionally, we theoretically demonstrate that the disease will die out at an exponential rate if ℛ 0 e < 1 \mathcal{R}_{0}^{e}<1 . Our results show that random fluctuations will facilitate disease outbreak.



keywords
Stochastic SVEIS epidemic model, Stationary distribution, Extinction, Black–Karasinski process.

I\end{array}\right]+\frac{I}{V_{e}}\frac{\omega_{1}S^{0}\bar{\beta}}{m+\sigma+\xi}|e^{z}-1|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\frac{\omega_{1}}{m+\sigma+\xi}E+\frac{\omega_{2}}{m+\gamma+\eta}I}\left(\sqrt{\mathcal{R}_{0}}-1\right)\left(\omega_{1}E+\omega_{2}I\right)+\frac{I}{\frac{\omega_{1}}{m+\sigma+\xi}E+\frac{\omega_{2}}{m+\gamma+\eta}I}\frac{\omega_{1}S^{0}\bar{\beta}}{m+\sigma+\xi}|e^{z}-1|

\displaystyle\leq\min\{m+\sigma+\xi,m+\gamma+\eta\}\left(\sqrt{\mathcal{R}_{0}}-1\right)+\frac{\sigma S^{0}\bar{\beta}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{R}_{0}}(m+\sigma+\xi)}|e^{z}-1|.

By the ergodicity of
\{z(t)\}_{t\geq 0}
and the Hölder’s inequality, we have :

\displaystyle\lim_{t\rightarrow\infty}\frac{1}{t}\int_{0}^{t}\left|e^{z(s)}-1\right|ds=\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|e^{z}-1\right|\pi(z)dz\leq

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left(e^{z}-1\right)^{2}\pi(z)dz\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{e^{\frac{\delta^{2}}{\theta}}-2e^{\frac{\delta^{2}}{4\theta}}+1},\quad\text{ a.s. }

Integrating from
0
to
t
and then dividing by
t
on both sides, and combining, we get

\displaystyle\limsup_{t\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\ln\left(\frac{\varpi_{1}}{m+\sigma+\xi}E+\frac{\omega_{2}}{m+\gamma+\eta}I\right)}{t}\leq

\displaystyle\min\{m+\sigma+\xi,m+\gamma+\eta\}\left(\sqrt{\mathcal{R}_{0}}-1\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{\sigma S^{0}\bar{\beta}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{R}_{0}}(m+\sigma+\xi)}\sqrt{e^{\frac{\delta^{2}}{\theta}}-2e^{\frac{\delta^{2}}{4\theta}}+1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\min\{m+\sigma+\xi,m+\gamma+\eta\}\left(\mathcal{R}_{0}^{e}-1\right).

Therefore, if
\mathcal{R}_{0}^{e}<1
, then the disease will go to extinction. this completes the proof.
∎

5
Conclusion

This work focuses on a stochastic SVEIS epidemic model
3
, where the Black-Karasinski process is introduced to represent the random effects in disease transmission. We define two critical values
\mathcal{R}_{0}^{s}

and
\mathcal{R}_{0}^{e}
, to analyze the asymptotic behavior of the system. Specifically, we demonstrate the existence of a stationary distribution when
\mathcal{R}_{0}^{s}>1
