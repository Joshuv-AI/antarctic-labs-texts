40 C.F.R. § 60.2010 — Does this subpart apply to my incineration unit?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2010
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, this subpart applies if your incineration unit meets all the requirements specified in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section:
(a) Your incineration unit is a new incineration unit as defined in § 60.2015;
(b) Your incineration unit is a CISWI as defined in § 60.2265, or an ACI as defined in § 60.2265; and
(c) Your incineration unit is not exempt under § 60.2020.
