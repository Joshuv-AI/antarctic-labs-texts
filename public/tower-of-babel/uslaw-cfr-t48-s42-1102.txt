48 C.F.R. § 42.1102 — 42.1102 Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 42 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all contracts for supplies or services other than construction contracts, and Federal Supply Schedule contracts. See part 37, especially subpart 37.6, regarding surveillance of contracts for services.
