36 U.S.C. § 220105 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:220105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section220105
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2201 (United Service Organizations, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has all the powers necessary and proper to carry out the purposes stated in section 220102 of this title, including the power—
(1)
to adopt and amend bylaws and regulations for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
to adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
to adopt and alter emblems and marks;
(4)
to establish and maintain offices to conduct the affairs of the corporation;
(5)
to choose officers, representatives, and agents as the activities of the corporation require;
(6)
to make contracts;
(7)
to acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(8)
to borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property;
(9)
to publish a magazine, newspaper, and other publications;
(10)
to establish, regulate, and terminate councils, organizations, chapters, or affiliates as needed to carry out the purposes stated in section 220102 of this title;
(11)
to solicit funds;
(12)
to sue and be sued; and
(13)
to do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the purposes stated in section 220102 of this title.
