9 U.S.C. § 203 — Jurisdiction; amount in controversy
Citation: us:usc:9:203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section203
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 2 (Convention On The Recognition And Enforcement Of Foreign Arbitral Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An action or proceeding falling under the Convention shall be deemed to arise under the laws and treaties of the United States. The district courts of the United States (including the courts enumerated in section 460 of title 28) shall have original jurisdiction over such an action or proceeding, regardless of the amount in controversy.
