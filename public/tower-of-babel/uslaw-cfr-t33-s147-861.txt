33 C.F.R. § 147.861 — Interim Big Foot TLP Construction Site safety zone.

(a) Description. The Big Foot Tension Leg Platform (TLP) construction site is in the deepwater area of the Gulf of America at Walker Ridge 29. The Big Foot TLP construction site outermost points are located at:
NW Corner 26-56-18.85 N, 090-31-26.44 W
NE Corner 26-56-18.85 N, 090-30-53.06 W
SE Corner 26-55-46.76 N, 090-30-53.06 W
SW Corner 26-55-46.76 N, 090-31-26.44 W,
(b) Regulation. No vessel may enter or remain in this safety zone except the following:
(1) An attending vessel;
(2) A vessel authorized by the Commander, USCG Heartland District or a designated representative.
