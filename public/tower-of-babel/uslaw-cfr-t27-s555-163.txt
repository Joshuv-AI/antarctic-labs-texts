27 C.F.R. § 555.163 — False entry in record.
Citation: us:cfr:27:555.163
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-555.163
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 555 (Commerce In Explosives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any licensed importer, licensed manufacturer, licensed dealer, or permittee who knowingly makes any false entry in any record required to be kept under subpart G of this part, shall be fined not more than $10,000 or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.
