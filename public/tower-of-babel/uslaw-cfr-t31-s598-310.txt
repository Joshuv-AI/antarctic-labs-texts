31 C.F.R. § 598.310 — Narcotics trafficking.
Citation: us:cfr:31:598.310
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-598.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 598 (Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term narcotics trafficking means any illicit activity to cultivate, produce, manufacture, distribute, sell, finance, or transport narcotic drugs, controlled substances, or listed chemicals, or otherwise endeavor or attempt to do so, or to assist, abet, conspire, or collude with others to do so.
