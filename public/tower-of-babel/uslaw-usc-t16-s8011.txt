16 U.S.C. § 8011 — Coordination with international organizations
Citation: us:usc:16:8011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8011
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 99 (Maritime Security And Fisheries Enforcement); Subchapter I (Programs To Combat Iuu Fishing And Increase Maritime Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce, shall coordinate with Regional Fisheries Management Organizations and the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, and may coordinate with other relevant international governmental or nongovernmental organizations, or the private sector, as appropriate, to enhance regional responses to IUU fishing and related transnational organized illegal activities.
