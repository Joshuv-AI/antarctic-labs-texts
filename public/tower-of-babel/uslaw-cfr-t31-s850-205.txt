31 C.F.R. § 850.205 — Contingent equity interest.
Citation: us:cfr:31:850.205
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-850.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 850 (Provisions Pertaining To U.S. Investments In Certain National Security Technologies And Products In Countries Of Concern). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term contingent equity interest means a financial interest (including debt) that currently does not constitute an equity interest but is convertible into, or provides the right to acquire, an equity interest upon the occurrence of a contingency or defined event or at the discretion of the U.S. person that holds the financial interest.
