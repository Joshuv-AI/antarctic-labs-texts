41 C.F.R. § 102-33.260 — -33.260 Federal aircraft cost and utilization data.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.260
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must report costs and flight hours for each Federal aircraft (including UAS), including both Federal and CAS expenses. For data element details, see the U.S. Government Aircraft CAG at www.gsa.gov/aviationpolicy.
(b) All executive agencies—excluding the Armed Forces and Intelligence Community—must report Federal aircraft cost and utilization data.
(c) Report data for loaned aircraft only if Federal funds were used.
