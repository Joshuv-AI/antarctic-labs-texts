41 C.F.R. § 101-25.404-1 — -25.404-1 Limitation.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-25.404-1
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-25.404-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-25 (25—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothwithstanding the provisions in § 101-25.404, agencies shall limit acquisition of new office furniture to essential requirements as provided in § 101-25.104. Replacement of correspondence filing cabinets will be governed by the provisions of § 101-26.308.
