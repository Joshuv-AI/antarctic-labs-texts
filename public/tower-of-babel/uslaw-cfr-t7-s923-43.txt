7 C.F.R. § 923.43 — Contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:923.43
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-923.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 923 (Sweet Cherries Grown In Designated Counties In Washington). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The committee may accept voluntary contributions but these shall only be used to pay expenses incurred pursuant to § 923.45. Furthermore, such contributions shall be free from any encumbrances by the donor and the committee shall retain complete control of their use.
