7 C.F.R. § 1755.502 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1755.502
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1755.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1755 (Telecommunications Policies On Specifications, Acceptable Materials, And Standard Contract Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Sections 1755.503 through 1755.510 cover approved methods of making service installations at customer access locations in telecommunications systems of RUS borrowers.
(b) Requirements in §§ 1755.503 through 1755.510 cover facilities of the type described in the FCC rules in 47 CFR part 68 for one and multi-party customer owned premises wiring.
