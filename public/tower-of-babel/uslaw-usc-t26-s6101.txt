26 U.S.C. § 6101 — Period covered by returns or other documents
Citation: us:usc:26:6101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6101
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 61 (Information And Returns); Subchapter B (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When not otherwise provided for by this title, the Secretary may by regulations prescribe the period for which, or the date as of which, any return, statement, or other document required by this title or by regulations, shall be made.
