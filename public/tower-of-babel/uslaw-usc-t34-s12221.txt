34 U.S.C. § 12221 — Grant authorization
Citation: us:usc:34:12221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12221
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter II (Crime Prevention); Part F (Community-Based Justice Grants For Prosecutors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Attorney General may make grants to State, Indian tribal, or local prosecutors for the purpose of supporting the creation or expansion of community-based justice programs.
(b) Consultation
The Attorney General may consult with the Ounce of Prevention Council in making grants under subsection (a).
