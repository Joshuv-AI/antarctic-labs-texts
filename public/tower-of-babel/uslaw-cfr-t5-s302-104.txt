5 C.F.R. § 302.104 — Applicability of regulations to applicants and employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:302.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-302.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 302 (Employment In The Excepted Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall follow the provisions of this part relating to examination, rating, and selection for appointment of an applicant when a qualified preference eligible or person entitled to priority consideration applies for appointment to a position covered by this part. Each agency, in its discretion, may follow these provisions when no preference eligible or person entitled to priority consideration applies.
