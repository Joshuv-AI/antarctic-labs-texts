2 U.S.C. § 6536 — Compensation of Financial Clerk of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6536
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6536
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter II (Secretary Of The Senate); Part A (Organization And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Financial Clerk of the Senate may be paid at a maximum annual rate of compensation not to exceed $39,000.
