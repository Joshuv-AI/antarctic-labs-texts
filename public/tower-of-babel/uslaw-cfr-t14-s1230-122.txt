14 C.F.R. § 1230.122 — Use of Federal funds.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1230.122
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1230.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1230 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funds administered by a Federal department or agency may not be expended for research involving human subjects unless the requirements of this policy have been satisfied.
