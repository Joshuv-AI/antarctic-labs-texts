46 C.F.R. § 153.1500 — Venting system rupture disks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:153.1500
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-153.1500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 153 (Ships Carrying Bulk Liquid, Liquefied Gas, Or Compressed Gas Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master shall ensure that a relief valve exposed to a cargo after the failure of a rupture disk or breaking pin is cleaned and operates properly before the next cargo is loaded into the tank.
