40 U.S.C. § 18101 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 181 (Telecommunications Accessibility For Hearing-Impaired And Speech-Impaired Individuals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1) Federal agency.—
The term “federal agency” has the same meaning given that term in section 102 of this title.
(2) TTY.—
The term “TTY” means a text-telephone used in the transmission of coded signals through the nationwide telecommunications system.
