40 C.F.R. § 280.240 — General requirement for all UST systems.
Citation: us:cfr:40:280.240
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-280.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 280 (Technical Standards And Corrective Action Requirements For Owners And Operators Of Underground Storage Tanks (Ust)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Not later than October 13, 2018, all owners and operators of UST systems must ensure they have designated Class A, Class B, and Class C operators who meet the requirements of this subpart.
