48 C.F.R. § 219.706 — 219.706 Responsibilities of the cognizant administrative contracting officer.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 219 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a)(i) The contract administration office also is responsible for reviewing, evaluating, and approving master subcontracting plans.
(ii) The small business specialist supports the administrative contracting officer in evaluating a contractor's performance and compliance with its subcontracting plan.
