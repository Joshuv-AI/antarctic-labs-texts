21 C.F.R. § 175.230 — Hot-melt strippable food coatings.
Citation: us:cfr:21:175.230
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-175.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 175 (Indirect Food Additives: Adhesives And Components Of Coatings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hot-melt strippable food coatings may be safely applied to food, subject to the provisions of this section.
(a) The coatings are applied to and used as removable coatings for food.
(b) The coatings may be prepared, as mixtures, from the following substances:
(1) Substances generally recognized as safe in food.
(2) Substances identified in this subparagraph.
