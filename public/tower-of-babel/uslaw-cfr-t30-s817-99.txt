30 C.F.R. § 817.99 — Slides and other damage.
Citation: us:cfr:30:817.99
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-817.99
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 817 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—Underground Mining Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time a slide occurs which may have a potential adverse effect on public, property, health, safety, or the environment, the person who conducts the underground mining activities shall notify the regulatory authority by the fastest available means and comply with any remedial measures required by the regulatory authority.
