40 U.S.C. § 6905 — Service on board of directors of Union Station Redevelopment Corporation
Citation: us:usc:40:6905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6905
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 69 (Union Station Redevelopment); Subchapter I (Union Station Complex). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To further the rehabilitation, redevelopment, and operation of the Union Station complex, the Secretary of Transportation and the Administrator of the Federal Railroad Administration may serve as ex officio members of the board of directors of the Union Station Redevelopment Corporation.
