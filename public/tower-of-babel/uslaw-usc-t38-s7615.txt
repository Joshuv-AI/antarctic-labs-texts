38 U.S.C. § 7615 — Status of participants
Citation: us:usc:38:7615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7615
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 76 (Health Professionals Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter II (Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Participants in the Scholarship Program shall not by reason of their participation in such program (1) be considered to be employees of the Federal Government, or (2) be counted against any personnel ceiling affecting the Veterans Health Administration.
