33 C.F.R. § 174.123 — Annual report of vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:174.123
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-174.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 174 (State Numbering And Casualty Reporting Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before March 1 of each year, each State that has an approved numbering system must prepare and submit Coast Guard Form CGHQ-3923 to the Coast Guard.
