27 C.F.R. § 21.120 — Nitropropane, mixed isomers of.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.120
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Nitropropane content. A minimum of 94 percent by weight.
(b) Total nitroparaffin content. A minimum of 99 percent by weight.
(c) Distillation range. 119° to 113 °C.
(d) Specific gravity at 20°/20 °C. 0.992 to 1.003.
