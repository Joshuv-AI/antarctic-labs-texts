7 C.F.R. § 4284.554 — Multi-year award.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4284.554
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4284.554
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4284 (Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency may provide a multi-year funding opportunity to previous Recipients. If the Agency provides a multi-year funding opportunity, the application requirements and award process will be included in the annual notification.
