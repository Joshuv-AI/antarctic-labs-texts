40 C.F.R. § 158.270 — Experimental use permit data requirements for residue chemistry.
Citation: us:cfr:40:158.270
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-158.270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 158 (Data Requirements For Pesticides). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All residue chemistry data, as described in § 158.1410, are required for an experimental use permit for which a temporary tolerance under FFDCA section 408(r) is sought. Residue chemistry data are not required for an experimental use permit issued on a crop-destruct basis.
