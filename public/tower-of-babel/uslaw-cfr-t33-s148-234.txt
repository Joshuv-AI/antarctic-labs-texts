33 C.F.R. § 148.234 — What are the limits of an Administrative Law Judge (ALJ)'s jurisdiction?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.234
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.234
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An ALJ's jurisdiction begins upon assignment to a proceeding.
(b) An ALJ's jurisdiction ends after the recommended findings are filed with the Commandant (CG-5P) or immediately after the ALJ issues a notice of withdrawal from the proceeding.
