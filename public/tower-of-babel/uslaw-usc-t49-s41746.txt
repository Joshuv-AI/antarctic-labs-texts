49 U.S.C. § 41746 — Tracking service
Citation: us:usc:49:41746
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41746
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 417 (Operations Of Carriers); Subchapter II (Small Community Air Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall require a carrier that provides essential air service to an eligible place and that receives compensation for such service under this subchapter to report not less than semiannually—
(1)
the percentage of flights to and from the place that arrive on time as defined by the Secretary; and
(2)
such other information as the Secretary considers necessary to evaluate service provided to passengers traveling to and from such place.
