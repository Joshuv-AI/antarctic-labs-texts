43 C.F.R. § 3514.40 — What if I am a bona fide purchaser and my lease is subject to cancellation?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3514.40
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3514.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you are a bona fide purchaser, BLM will not cancel your lease or your interest in a lease based on your predecessor's actions. However, you must be sure that the lease is in compliance with the terms and conditions required by BLM.
(b) BLM will promptly take action to dismiss any party who shows they are a bona fide purchaser from any legal proceedings to cancel the lease.
