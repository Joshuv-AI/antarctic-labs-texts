10 C.F.R. § 780.30 — Filing of application.
Citation: us:cfr:10:780.30
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-780.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 780 (Patent Compensation Board Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for a license pursuant to section 153b(2) of the Act, under a patent which the Department has declared to be affected with the public interest, shall file an application with the Board in accordance with § 780.5. The Board will docket the application and serve notice of the docketing upon all parties.
