Correction for You et al., p53-dependent inhibition of FKHRL1 in response to DNA damage through protein kinase SGK1
Published: 2026

Correction for “p53-dependent inhibition of FKHRL1 in response to DNA damage through protein kinase SGK1,” by Han You, YingJu Jang, Annick Itie You-Ten, Hitoshi Okada, Jennifer Liepa, Andrew Wakeham, Kathrin Zaugg, and Tak W. Mak, which published September 21, 2004; 10.1073/pnas.0406286101 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 101, 14057–14062).

The authors note, “There appears to be image splicing present in Figs. 1A, 3B, and 4C. We believe these concerns are most likely the result of the removal of unrelated lanes during figure preparation. In addition, the AKT-p blot in Fig. 2A was inadvertently duplicated as an actin blot in Fig. 3A, which was likely due to mislabeling of the related blots. The original data are no longer available due to the age of the study.”

The authors believe the overall conclusions are unaffected by these errors and apologize to the readers. The editors have reviewed and approved the correction.
