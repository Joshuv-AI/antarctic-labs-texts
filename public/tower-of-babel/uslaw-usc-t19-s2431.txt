19 U.S.C. § 2431 — Exception of products of certain countries or areas
Citation: us:usc:19:2431
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2431
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter IV (Trade Relations With Countries Not Receiving Nondiscriminatory Treatment); Part 1 (Trade Relations With Certain Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this subchapter, the President shall continue to deny nondiscriminatory treatment to the products of any country, the products of which were not eligible for the rates set forth in rate column numbered 1 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States on January 3, 1975.
