16 U.S.C. § 410jj–8 — Reevaluation of management, etc., policies
Citation: us:usc:16:410jj–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410jj–8
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–H (Kalaupapa National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At such time when there is no longer a resident patient community at Kalaupapa, the Secretary shall reevaluate the policies governing the management, administration, and public use of the park in order to identify any changes deemed to be appropriate.
