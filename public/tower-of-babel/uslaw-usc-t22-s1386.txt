22 U.S.C. § 1386 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:1386
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1386
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 15 (The Republic Of The Philippines); Subchapter V (Property Retained By The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter the term “Philippine Government” shall mean “Government of the Commonwealth of the Philippines” until the date of independence, and thereafter it shall mean the “Government of the Republic of the Philippines”.
