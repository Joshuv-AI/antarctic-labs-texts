20 C.F.R. § 216.64 — What is required for payment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:216.64
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-216.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 216 (Eligibility For An Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An eligible widow(er), surviving divorced spouse, or remarried widow(er) must:
(a) Apply to be entitled for an annuity; and
(b) Submit evidence requested by the Board to support his or her application.
