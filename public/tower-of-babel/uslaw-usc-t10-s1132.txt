10 U.S.C. § 1132 — Presentation of decorations: prohibition on entering correctional facilities for presentation to prisoners convicted of serious violent felonies
Citation: us:usc:10:1132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1132
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 57 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition.—
A member of the armed forces may not enter a Federal, State, local, or foreign correctional facility to present a decoration to a person who is incarcerated due to conviction of a serious violent felony.
(b) Definitions.—
In this section:
(1)
The term “decoration” means any decoration or award that may be presented or awarded to a member of the armed forces.
(2)
The term “serious violent felony” has the meaning given that term in section 3559(c)(2)(F) of title 18.
