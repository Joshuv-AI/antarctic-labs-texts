12 C.F.R. § 1015.6 — Assisting and facilitating.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1015.6
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1015.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1015 (Mortgage Assistance Relief Services (Regulation O)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is a violation of this rule for a person to provide substantial assistance or support to any mortgage assistance relief service provider when that person knows or consciously avoids knowing that the provider is engaged in any act or practice that violates this rule.
