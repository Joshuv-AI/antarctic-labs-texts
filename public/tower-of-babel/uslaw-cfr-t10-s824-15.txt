10 C.F.R. § 824.15 — Collection of civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:10:824.15
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-824.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 824 (Procedural Rules For The Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Classified Information Security Violations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any person fails to pay an assessment of a civil penalty after it has become a final order or after the appropriate District Court has entered final judgment for DOE under § 824.14, DOE shall institute an action to recover the amount of such penalty in an appropriate District Court of the United States.
