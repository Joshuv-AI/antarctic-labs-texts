36 C.F.R. § 331.3 — Hunting and trapping.
Citation: us:cfr:36:331.3
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-331.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 331 (Regulations Governing The Protection, Use And Management Of The Falls Of The Ohio National Wildlife Conservation Area, Kentucky And Indiana). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless authorized in writing by the District Engineer:
(a) The hunting, trapping, catching, molesting, killing, or having in possession any wild animal or bird, or taking the eggs of any such bird, is prohibited.
(b) Possession of equipment (including, but not limited to, firearms, ammunition, traps, projectile firing devices including bow and arrow) which could be used for hunting, trapping, or the taking of wildlife, is prohibited.
