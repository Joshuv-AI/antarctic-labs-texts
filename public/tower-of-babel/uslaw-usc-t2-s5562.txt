2 U.S.C. § 5562 — Preservation of reports, statements, or documents filed with Clerk of House
Citation: us:usc:2:5562
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5562
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter IV (Clerk). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
If the Clerk of the House of Representatives is required under any law, rule, or regulation to make available for public inspection a report, statement, or other document filed with the Office of the Clerk, the Clerk shall preserve the report, statement, or document—
(1)
for a period of 6 years from the date on which the document is filed; or
(2)
if the law, rule, or regulation so provides, the period required under such law, rule, or regulation.
(b)
Subsection (a) shall apply with respect to reports, statements, and documents filed before, on, or after December 8, 2004.
