42 U.S.C. § 8146 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:8146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8146
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 90 (Neighborhood And City Reinvestment, Self-Help And Revitalization); Subchapter III (Livable Cities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for carrying out the purposes of this subchapter not to exceed $5,000,000 for fiscal year 1979, and not to exceed $5,000,000 for fiscal year 1980. Any amounts appropriated under this section shall remain available until expended.
