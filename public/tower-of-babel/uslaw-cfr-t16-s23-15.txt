16 C.F.R. § 23.15 — Misuse of the term “blue white.”
Citation: us:cfr:16:23.15
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-23.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 23 (Guides For The Jewelry, Precious Metals, And Pewter Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unfair or deceptive to use the term “blue white” or any representation of similar meaning to describe any diamond that under normal, north daylight or its equivalent shows any color or any trace of any color other than blue or bluish.
