46 C.F.R. § 113.27-1 — -1 Engineers' assistance-needed alarm.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.27-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.27-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each self-propelled ocean, Great Lakes, or coastwise vessel must have a manually-operated engineers' assistance-needed alarm that is:
(a) Operated from:
(1) The engine control room, if the vessel has an engine control room; or
(2) The maneuvering platform, if the vessel has no engine control room;
(b) Audible in the engineers' accommodation spaces; and
(c) Powered from the general alarm power source.
