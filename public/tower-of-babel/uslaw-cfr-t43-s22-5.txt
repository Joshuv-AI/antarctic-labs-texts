43 C.F.R. § 22.5 — Payment of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:43:22.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-22.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 22 (Administrative Claims Under The Federal Tort Claims Act And Indemnification Of Department Of The Interior Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When an award of $2,500 or less is made, the voucher signed by the claimant shall be transmitted for payment to the appropriate Bureau or Office of the Department. When an award over $2,500 is made, transmittal for payment will be made as prescribed by § 14.10 of the regulations (28 CFR part 14).
(b) Prior to payment appropriate releases shall be obtained as provided in said section.
