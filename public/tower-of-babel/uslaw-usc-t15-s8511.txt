15 U.S.C. § 8511 — Public safety priority
Citation: us:usc:15:8511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8511
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 111 (Weather Research And Forecasting Innovation); Subchapter I (United States Weather Research And Forecasting Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In conducting research, the Under Secretary shall prioritize improving weather data, modeling, computing, forecasting, and warnings for the protection of life and property and for the enhancement of the national economy.
