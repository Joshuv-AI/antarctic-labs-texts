36 C.F.R. § 1121.3 — Procedures for requests pertaining to individuals' records in a records system.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1121.3
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1121.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1121 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual or authorized representative shall submit a written request to the Administrative Officer to determine if a system of records named by the individual contains a record pertaining to the individual. The individual or authorized representative shall submit a written request to the Executive Director of the ATBCB which states the individual's desire to review his or her record.
