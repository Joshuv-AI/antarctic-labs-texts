36 U.S.C. § 40307 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:40307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section40307
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 403 (Civil Air Patrol). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall submit an annual report to Congress on the activities of the corporation during the prior fiscal year.
