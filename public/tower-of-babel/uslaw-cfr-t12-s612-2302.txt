12 C.F.R. § 612.2302 — Notification of board of directors and bonding company.
Citation: us:cfr:12:612.2302
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-612.2302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 612 (Standards Of Conduct And Referral Of Known Or Suspected Criminal Violations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The institution's board of directors shall be promptly notified of any criminal referral by the institution, except that if the criminal referral involves a member of the board of directors, discretion may be exercised in notifying such member of the referral.
(b) The institution involved shall promptly make all required notifications under any applicable surety bond or other contract for protection.
