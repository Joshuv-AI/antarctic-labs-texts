Decoding the structure near the 𝝅 + ​ 𝝅 − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} mass threshold in 𝝍 ⁡ ( 𝟑𝟔𝟖𝟔 ) → 𝑱 / 𝝍 ​ 𝝅 + ​ 𝝅 − \psi(3686)\rightarrow J/\psi\pi^{+}\pi^{-} decays
Yun-Hua Chen, Xiang-Kun Dong, Feng-Kun Guo, Christoph Hanhart, Bastian Kubis

Abstract
In light of recent high-precision data taken by the BESIII Collaboration, we reconsider the dipion transition ψ ⁡ ( 3686 ) → J / ψ ​ π + ​ π − \psi(3686)\rightarrow J/\psi\pi^{+}\pi^{-} . The strong pion–pion final-state interactions are taken into account model-independently by using dispersion theory. We find that we can reproduce the substructure near the π + ​ π − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} threshold observed experimentally without introducing an extra resonance state. While a helicity-flip amplitude plays an important role for the formation of the dip in the invariant-mass distribution, virtual exchange of the charmoniumlike exotic Z c ​ ( 3900 ) Z_{c}(3900) state improves the fit quality significantly.



I
Introduction

The dipion transitions between heavy quarkonia are important for understanding both their dynamics and low-energy QCD. Because the heavy quarkonia
are expected to be nonrelativistic and compact, the method of the
QCD multipole expansion
Voloshin and Zakharov 1980
;
Novikov and Shifman 1981
;
Kuang and Yan 1981
is
often used to study these transitions, in which the pions are produced due to the hadronization of emitted soft gluons. The QCD multipole expansion maps onto the chiral effective Lagrangian for dipion transitions between heavy quarkonia
Brown and Cahn 1975
;
Mannel and Urech 1997
, which has been used successfully together with subsequent pion–pion final-state interactions (FSI) to study the decays
\Upsilon(2S,3S,4S,5S)\to\Upsilon(1S,2S,3S)\pi\pi

Chen et al. 2016
;
Chen et al. 2017
;
Chen and Guo 2019
;
Baru et al. 2021

and
\psi(3686)\to J/\psi\pi\pi

Chen 2019
;
Dong et al. 2021
.

Very recently, based on
2712.4(14.4)\times 10^{6}

\psi(3686)\equiv\psi^{\prime}
events, BESIII presented a high-precision study of the
\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
mass spectrum in
\psi^{\prime}\rightarrow\pi^{+}\pi^{-}J/\psi
decays.
A clear resonance-like structure near the
\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
mass threshold was observed for the first time
Ablikim et al. 2025
. In the BESIII analysis, the structure was best described by a Breit–Wigner parameterization with a mass of
(282.6\pm 0.4_{\mathrm{sta}}\pm 2.5_{\mathrm{sys}})\,
MeV and a width of
(17.3\pm 0.8_{\mathrm{sta}}\pm 0.4_{\mathrm{sys}})\,
MeV
Ablikim et al. 2025
.
However, a resonance with such a low mass, which is much lower than the mass of the
f_{0}(500)
(for a review, see Ref.
Peláez 2016
), would be at odds with the chiral structure of QCD that suppresses two-pion interactions at low energies. Thus, the new data set necessitates a reanalysis of the reaction
\psi^{\prime}\to J/\psi\pi\pi
.
Note that the dipion mass spectra in
\Upsilon(3S)\to\Upsilon(1S)\pi\pi
, which show a similar two-hump behavior, can be explained naturally by the virtual exchange
of the isovector bottomonium exotics
Z_{b}(10610)/Z_{b}(10650)

Chen et al. 2016
(see Refs.
Anisovich et al. 1995
;
Guo et al. 2005
for earlier studies); and similar double-bump structures were also observed in
\Upsilon(4S)\to\Upsilon(1S,2S)\pi\pi
decays as discussed in Ref.
Guo et al. 2007
.
The analogous isovector charmoniumlike structure
Z_{c}(3900)^{\pm}

was discovered in the
J/\psi\,\pi
invariant-mass spectra by the BESIII and Belle Collaborations in 2013 in the process
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi\pi^{+}\pi^{-}

Ablikim et al. 2013
;
Liu et al. 2013
.
Therefore, we study the decay
\psi^{\prime}\to J/\psi\pi\pi
, considering effects of the virtual

Z_{c}(3900)
-exchange mechanism.

It is necessary to account for the
two-pion FSI properly. In this article, we use dispersion theory to treat the
\pi\pi
FSI
García-Martín and Moussallam 2010
;
Kubis and Plenter 2015
;
Kang et al. 2014
;
Dai and Pennington 2014
;
Chen et al. 2016
. Instead of the chiral unitary approach
Oller and Oset 1997
that was used in the literature to describe
\pi\pi
FSI for the process of interest
Guo et al. 2005
;
Guo et al. 2006
, in dispersion theory it is treated in a model-independent way consistent with
\pi\pi
scattering data. We will provide a simultaneous description of the experimental data for the dipion invariant-mass distribution and the helicity angular
distribution of
\psi^{\prime}\to J/\psi\pi\pi
, which help reveal the nature of the structure near the
\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
threshold.

II
Theoretical framework

For the contact
\psi^{\prime}\to J/\psi\pi\pi
interaction, the effective
Lagrangian to leading order in the chiral as well as the heavy-quark
nonrelativistic expansion reads
Mannel and Urech 1997
;
Chen et al. 2016
;
Chen et al. 2017

\displaystyle\mathscr{L}_{\psi^{\prime}\psi\Phi\Phi}=

\displaystyle g_{1}\langle\psi^{\prime\alpha}\psi^{\dagger}_{\alpha}\rangle\langle u_{\mu}u^{\mu}\rangle+h_{1}\langle\psi^{\prime\alpha}\psi^{\dagger}_{\alpha}\rangle\langle u_{\mu}u_{\nu}\rangle v^{\mu}v^{\nu}

\displaystyle+j_{1}\langle\psi^{\prime\mu}\psi^{\dagger}_{\nu}\rangle\langle u_{\mu}u^{\nu}\rangle+\mathrm{H.c.}\,,

(1)

where
v^{\mu}=(1,\mathbf{0})
is the velocity of the heavy quark,

\psi^{\alpha}
and
\psi^{\prime\alpha}
denote the
J/\psi
and

\psi^{\prime}
fields, respectively,
and
u_{\mu}
collects the Goldstone bosons of the spontaneous breaking of
chiral symmetry
Gasser and Leutwyler 1984
;
Gasser and Leutwyler 1985
.
The leading-order

Z_{c}\psi^{\prime}\pi
and
Z_{c}J/\psi\pi
interaction Lagrangians
are proportional to the pion energy
Cleven et al. 2011
,

\displaystyle\mathscr{L}_{Z_{c}\psi^{\prime}\pi}

\displaystyle=C_{Z_{c}\psi^{\prime}\pi}\psi^{\prime i}\langle{Z^{i}_{c}}^{\dagger}u_{\mu}v^{\mu}\rangle+\mathrm{H.c.}\,,

\displaystyle\mathscr{L}_{Z_{c}\psi\pi}

\displaystyle=C_{Z_{c}\psi\pi}\psi^{i}\langle{Z^{i}_{c}}^{\dagger}u_{\mu}v^{\mu}\rangle+\mathrm{H.c.}\,.

(2)

We define
C_{\psi^{\prime}\psi}^{Z_{c}}\equiv C_{Z_{c}\psi^{\prime}\pi}C_{Z_{c}\psi\pi}
as the product
of the coupling constants for the exchange of the
Z_{c}
.

The doubly differential decay width for the process
\psi^{\prime}(p_{a})\rightarrow J/\psi(p_{b})\pi(p_{c})\pi(p_{d})
can be written as

\displaystyle\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}\Gamma}{{\text{d}}\sqrt{s}\,{\text{d}}\cos\theta}=\frac{\sqrt{s}\kappa(s)}{32(2\pi)^{3}M_{\psi^{\prime}}^{3}}S_{0}(s)\frac{1}{3}\sum_{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}\left|\mathscr{M}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}(s,t,u)\right|^{2}\,,

(3)

where the Mandelstam variables are

s=(p_{c}+p_{d})^{2}
,
t=(p_{a}-p_{c})^{2}
, and
u=(p_{a}-p_{d})^{2}
, and
\theta
is defined as the angle between
\pi^{+}
and
\psi^{\prime}
in the
\pi\pi
c.m. frame,

\kappa(s)=\sqrt{1-4m_{\pi}^{2}/s}\lambda^{1/2}(M_{\psi^{\prime}}^{2},s,M_{\psi}^{2})
, and

\lambda(a,b,c)=a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-2(ab+ac+bc)
is the Källén triangle function.
In order to take the near-threshold Coulomb enhancement of the
\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
pair into account, we employ the Sommerfeld factor
Sommerfeld 1931

\displaystyle S_{0}(s)=\frac{2\pi x}{1-\text{exp}(-2\pi x)},\qquad x=\frac{\alpha\,m_{\pi}}{\sqrt{s-4m_{\pi}^{2}}},

(4)

where
\alpha=e^{2}/(4\pi)\approx 1/137
is the fine-structure constant.

\mathscr{M}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}(s,t,u)\equiv\mathscr{M}_{\mu\nu}(s,t,u)\epsilon_{\lambda^{\prime}}^{\mu}\epsilon_{\lambda}^{\ast\nu}
is the helicity amplitude, and
\lambda^{\prime}
and
\lambda
denote the helicities of
\psi^{\prime}
and
J/\psi
, respectively.
To proceed, we perform the
s
-channel partial-wave decomposition of the amplitude

\displaystyle\mathscr{M}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}(s,t,u)=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}d_{\lambda-\lambda^{\prime},0}^{l}(\theta)\left[H^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)+\hat{H}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)\right],

(5)

where
l
denotes the relative orbital angular momentum of the pions, and
d_{\lambda-\lambda^{\prime},0}^{l}(\theta)
is the Wigner-
d
function.
H^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)
is
the right-hand-cut part and represents the
s
-channel
\pi\pi
rescattering. The “hat function”
\hat{H}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)
encodes the left-hand-cut contribution
derived from the partial-wave projection of the
Z_{c}
-exchange amplitude obtained from Eq. (
2
). Charge-parity conservation requires that
l
must be even, and we only take the
S
- and
D
-wave components into account in this study.

In the regime of elastic
\pi\pi
rescattering, the partial-wave unitarity relations read

\displaystyle\text{Im}H^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)=\big[H^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)+\hat{H}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)\big]\sin\delta_{l}^{0}(s)e^{-i\delta_{l}^{0}(s)}\,,

(6)

where
\delta_{l}^{0}(s)
is the
\pi\pi
isoscalar phase shift of angular momentum
l
.

The dispersive solution to Eq. (
6
) is
Anisovich and Leutwyler 1996

\displaystyle H^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)=

\displaystyle\,\Omega_{l}^{0}(s)\bigg\{M^{\chi,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)

\displaystyle+\frac{s^{n}}{\pi}\int_{4m_{\pi}^{2}}^{\infty}\frac{{\text{d}}x}{x^{n}}\frac{\hat{H}^{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(x)\sin\delta_{l}^{0}(x)}{|\Omega_{l}^{0}(x)|(x-s)}\bigg\}\,,

(7)

where the subtraction terms
M^{\chi,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},l}(s)
are matched to the partial-wave projection of the low-energy chiral amplitudes obtained from Eq. (
1
).
The single-channel Omnès function reads
Omnès 1958

\Omega_{l}^{0}(s)=\exp\bigg\{\frac{s}{\pi}\int^{\infty}_{4m_{\pi}^{2}}\frac{{\text{d}}x}{x}\frac{\delta_{l}^{0}(x)}{x-s}\bigg\}\,.

(8)

For the
S
-wave, we use the phase of the nonstrange pion
scalar form factor as determined in Ref.
Hoferichter et al. 2012
. For the
D
-wave,
we employ the parametrization for
\delta_{2}^{0}
given in
Ref.
García-Martín et al. 2011
. A diagrammatic representation of all contributions is
given in Fig.
1
.

Note that the BESIII paper
Ablikim et al. 2025
alternatively presents the folded
\pi\pi

\pi\pi

In our Fits I and Ib, the enhancement near the
\pi\pi
threshold can be described by the chiral contact terms and FSI.
In particular, no additional
2
\pi
resonance is required. Note that Ref.
Ablikim et al. 2025
claims that chiral perturbation theory supplemented with a leading-order unitarization as used in Ref.
Guo et al. 2005
fail to reproduce this threshold enhancement. We find that in Ref.
Ablikim et al. 2025
, the
E_{\pi^{+}}E_{\pi^{-}}
and
(p_{\pi^{+}}^{\mu}p_{\pi^{-}}^{\nu}+p_{\pi^{+}}^{\nu}p_{\pi^{-}}^{\mu})

\pi\pi

S
- and
D
-waves, respectively, where
E_{\pi^{(\pm)}}
and
p_{\pi^{(\pm)}}
are the energies and the four-momenta of the pions, while both of these actually contribute to both
S
- and
D
-waves
Yan et al. 1999
;
Guo et al. 2005
. Also note that Ref.
Ablikim et al. 2025
considers the FSI within the chiral unitary approach
Oller and Oset 1997
, which yields a dynamical generation of the
scalar mesons. In contrast to the present study, the very precise information available on pion–pion phase shifts is not strictly implemented there.

Comparing Fit I with Fits II and III, one finds that including the
Z_{c}
exchange improves the fit quality significantly, especially in the low-energy region of the
\pi\pi
spectra and for the angular distribution. Furthermore, comparing Fits II and III, one observes that although a smaller
Z_{c}
mass leads to a smaller
\chi^{2}
, the change of fit quality is tiny. Therefore we conclude that a virtual intermediate
Z_{c}
state indeed can play a relevant role in
\psi^{\prime}\to J/\psi\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
.

IV
Conclusions

We used dispersion theory to study the FSI in the decay

\psi^{\prime}\to J/\psi\pi\pi
. Through fitting the data of the
\pi\pi
mass spectra and
the angular
\cos\theta
distributions, we find that the structure near the
\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
mass threshold observed by
Ablikim et al. 2025
can be described well without introducing a new resonance. We find that the helicity-flip amplitude plays an important role in the low-energy
\pi\pi
mass spectrum and the angular distributions. Also we find that the virtual
Z_{c}(3900)
-exchange mechanism improves the agreement between the theoretical prediction and the experimental data.

became aware of Ref.
Wang et al. 2025
addressing the
same problem and explaining the structure near threshold as the result from the
interference and superposition of two decay mechanisms. We point out that the second decay mechanism in Fig. 1 of Ref.
Wang et al. 2025
in practice
refers to a contribution for Goldstone-boson singlets, i.e., it
exclusively gives a contribution to the
\eta\eta
channel,
for which they need to include non-octet parts of the
\eta
. In every
other coupled-channel analysis of the
I=0

S
-wave, the
\eta\eta
channel is
tiny compared to the
\pi\pi
–
K\bar{K}
channel coupling
Kaiser 1998
;
García-Martín et al. 2011
;
Briceño et al. 2018
; indeed, from the fit results in Table I of Ref.
Wang et al. 2025
, in order for this mechanism
to have any effect, the corresponding coupling
V_{2}
is found to be larger by five orders of magnitude than that for the octet meson pair. Also, the complex phase
\phi
