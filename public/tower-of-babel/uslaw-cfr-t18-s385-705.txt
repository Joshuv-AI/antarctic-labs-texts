18 C.F.R. § 385.705 — Additional powers of presiding officer with respect to briefs (Rule 705).
Citation: us:cfr:18:385.705
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-385.705
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 385 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Limitations on briefs. A presiding officer, with due regard to the nature of the proceeding, may limit the length of any brief to be filed under Rule 706.
(b) Additional briefs and other filings. If appropriate, the presiding officer may permit or require briefs or other filings in addition to those provided for in Rule 706.
