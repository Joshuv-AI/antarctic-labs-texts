29 C.F.R. § 7.17 — Variations in procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:29:7.17
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-7.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 7 (Practice Before The Administrative Review Board With Regard To Federal And Federally Assisted Construction Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon reasonable notice to the parties or interested persons, the Board may vary the procedures specified in this part in particular cases.
