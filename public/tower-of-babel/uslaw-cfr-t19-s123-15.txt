19 C.F.R. § 123.15 — Vehicles of foreign origin used between communities of the United States and Canada or Mexico.
Citation: us:cfr:19:123.15
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-123.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 123 (Cbp Relations With Canada And Mexico). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vehicles of foreign origin which are used for commercial purposes between adjoining or neighboring communities of the United States and Canada or Mexico, such as delivery, peddlers', and service trucks, or wagons, are subject to duty on first arrival, but may thereafter be admitted without formal entry or the payment of duty so long as they are continuously employed in such service.
