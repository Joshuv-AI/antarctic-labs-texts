10 U.S.C. § 7239 — Enlisted members: officers not to use as servants
Citation: us:usc:10:7239
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7239
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 729 (Miscellaneous Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer of the Army may use an enlisted member of the Army as a servant.
