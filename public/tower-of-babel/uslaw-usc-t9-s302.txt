9 U.S.C. § 302 — Incorporation by reference
Citation: us:usc:9:302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section302
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 3 (Inter-American Convention On International Commercial Arbitration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 202, 203, 204, 205, and 207 of this title shall apply to this chapter as if specifically set forth herein, except that for the purposes of this chapter “the Convention” shall mean the Inter-American Convention.
