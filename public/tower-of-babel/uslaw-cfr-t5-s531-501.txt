5 C.F.R. § 531.501 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:5:531.501
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-531.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 531 (Pay Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains regulations of the Office of Personnel Management to carry out section 5336 of title 5, United States Code, which authorizes the head of an agency, or another official to whom such authority is delegated, to grant quality step increases.
