25 C.F.R. § 227.9 — Acreage limitation: Leases on noncontiguous tracts.
Citation: us:cfr:25:227.9
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-227.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 227 (Leasing Of Certain Lands In Wind River Indian Reservation, Wyoming, For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person, firm, or corporation will be allowed to lease for oil and gas more than 10,240 acres in the aggregate. The land contained in the lease shall be described by legal subdivisions, and leases may be executed to cover only adjoining or contiguous subdivisions. In case a lessee is a successful bidder for two or more tracts of land which are not contiguous, separate leases shall be executed.
