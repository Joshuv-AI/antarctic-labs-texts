7 C.F.R. § 766.51 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.51
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) DSA is available to borrowers with program loans who suffered losses as a result of a natural disaster.
(b) DSA is not intended to circumvent other servicing available under this part.
(c) Non-program loans may be serviced under this subpart for borrowers who also have program loans.
