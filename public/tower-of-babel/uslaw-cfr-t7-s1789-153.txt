7 C.F.R. § 1789.153 — Borrower funding.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1789.153
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1789.153
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1789 (Use Of Consultants Funded By Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers shall use their general funds for the purposes of funding consultant services hereunder. Borrowers may not use the proceeds of loans made or guaranteed under the RE Act for costs incurred by Borrowers pursuant to the funding of consultant services for RUS.
