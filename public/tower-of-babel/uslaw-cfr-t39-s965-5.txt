39 C.F.R. § 965.5 — Initial submissions by parties.
Citation: us:cfr:39:965.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-965.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 965 (Mail Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after receipt of the Recorder's notice, each party shall file via the Judicial Officer electronic filing system (https://usps-judicialoffice.journaltech.com) a sworn statement of the facts supporting its claim to receipt of the mail together with a copy of each document on which it relies in making such claim, and any arguments supporting its claim. Unless the presiding officer otherwise permits, all documents relative to this proceeding must be filed using the electronic filing system.
