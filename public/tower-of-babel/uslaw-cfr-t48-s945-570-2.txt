48 C.F.R. § 945.570-2 — 945.570-2 Disposition of motor vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:48:945.570-2
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-945.570-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 945 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The contractor shall dispose of DOE-owned motor vehicles as directed by the contracting officer.
(b) DOE-owned motor vehicles may be disposed of as exchange/sale items when directed by the contracting officer; however, a designated DOE official must execute the Title Transfer forms (SF-97).
