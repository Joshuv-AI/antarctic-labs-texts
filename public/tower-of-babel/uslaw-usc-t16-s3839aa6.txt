16 U.S.C. § 3839aa–6 — Duties of the Secretary
Citation: us:usc:16:3839aa–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3839aa–6
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 58 (Erodible Land And Wetland Conservation And Reserve Program); Subchapter IV (Agricultural Resources Conservation Program); Part IV (Environmental Quality Incentives Program And Conservation Stewardship Program); Subpart a (Environmental Quality Incentives Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the extent appropriate, the Secretary shall assist a producer in achieving the conservation and environmental goals of a program plan by—
(1)
providing payments for developing and implementing 1 or more practices, as appropriate; and
(2)
providing the producer with information and training to aid in implementation of the plan.
