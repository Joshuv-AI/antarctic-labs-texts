20 C.F.R. § 702.213 — Notice; by whom given.
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.213
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notice shall be given by the injured employee or someone on his behalf, or in the case of death, by the deceased employee's beneficiary or someone on his behalf.
