42 U.S.C. § 274i–3 — Medals not treated as valuable consideration
Citation: us:usc:42:274i–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section274i–3
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part H–1 (Stephanie Tubbs Jones Gift Of Life Medal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A medal under this part shall not be treated as valuable consideration for purposes of section 274e(a) of this title.
