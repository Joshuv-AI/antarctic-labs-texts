21 C.F.R. § 874.3760 — Sacculotomy tack (Cody tack)
Citation: us:cfr:21:874.3760
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-874.3760
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 874 (Ear, Nose, And Throat Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A sacculotomy tack (Cody tack) is a device that consists of a pointed stainless steel tack intended to be implanted to relieve the symptoms of vertigo. The device repetitively ruptures the utricular membrane as the membrane expands under increased endolymphatic pressure.
(b) Classification. Class II.
