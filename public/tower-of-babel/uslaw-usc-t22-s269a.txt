22 U.S.C. § 269a — Central Bureau of the International Map of the World on the Millionth Scale; authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:269a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section269a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, an annual sum of $50 as a contribution on the part of the United States toward the expenses incurred by the Central Bureau of the International Map of the World on the Millionth Scale.
