42 U.S.C. § 8840 — Termination of authorities
Citation: us:usc:42:8840
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8840
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 96 (Biomass Energy And Alcohol Fuels); Subchapter II (Municipal Waste Biomass Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No financial assistance may be committed to or made under this subchapter after September 30, 1984. This section shall not be construed to affect the authority of the Secretary of Energy to spend funds after such date pursuant to any award of financial assistance made on or before that date.
