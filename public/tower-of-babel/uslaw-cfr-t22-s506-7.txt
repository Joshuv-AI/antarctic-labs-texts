22 C.F.R. § 506.7 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:506.7
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-506.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 506 (Part-Time Career Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Board and the Associate Director for Management may except positions from inclusion in this program as necessary to carry out the mission of the Board.
