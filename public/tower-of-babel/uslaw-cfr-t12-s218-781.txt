12 C.F.R. § 218.781 — Exemption from the definition of “broker” for banks for a limited period of time.
Citation: us:cfr:12:218.781
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-218.781
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 218 (Exceptions For Banks From The Definition Of Broker In The Securities Exchange Act Of 1934 (Regulation R)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bank is exempt from the definition of the term “broker” under section 3(a)(4) of the Act (15 U.S.C. 78c(a)(4)) until the first day of its first fiscal year commencing after September 30, 2008.
