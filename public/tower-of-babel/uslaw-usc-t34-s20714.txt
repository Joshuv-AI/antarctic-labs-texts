34 U.S.C. § 20714 — Training of tribal law enforcement and prosecutorial personnel
Citation: us:usc:34:20714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section20714
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 207 (Combating Domestic Trafficking In Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General, in consultation with the Director of the Office of Tribal Justice, shall carry out a program under which tribal law enforcement officials may receive technical assistance and training to pursue a victim-centered approach to investigating and prosecuting severe forms of trafficking in persons (as defined in section 7102 of title 22).
