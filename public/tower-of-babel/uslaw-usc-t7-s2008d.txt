7 U.S.C. § 2008d — Recordkeeping of loans by borrower’s gender
Citation: us:usc:7:2008d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2008d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall classify, by gender, records of applicants for loans and loan guarantees under this chapter.
