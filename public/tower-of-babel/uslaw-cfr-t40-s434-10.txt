40 C.F.R. § 434.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:434.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-434.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 434 (Coal Mining Point Source Category Bpt, Bat, Bct Limitations And New Source Performance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to discharges from any coal mine at which the extraction of coal is taking place or is planned to be undertaken and to coal preparation plants and associated areas.
