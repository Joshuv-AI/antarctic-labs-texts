23 C.F.R. § 260.401 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:260.401
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-260.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 260 (Education And Training Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe policy and implement procedures for the administration of Federal-aid funds for education and training of State and local highway department employees.
