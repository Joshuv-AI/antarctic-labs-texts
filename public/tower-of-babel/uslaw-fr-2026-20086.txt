Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records

AGENCY: Office of Public and Indian Housing.

ACTION: Notice of a new system of records.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the provisions of the Privacy Act of 1974, as 
amended, the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), Office 
of Public and Indian Housing, is issuing a public notice of its intent 
to establish a Privacy Act system of records titled Enterprise Voucher 
Management System (eVMS). The Enterprise Voucher Management System 
(eVMS) is used for managing funds for the Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) 
Program. eVMS automates monthly Housing Assistance Payment (HAP) 
calculations using family-level data derived from the Information 
Management System/Public and Indian Housing Information Center (IMS/
PIC), provides authorized users with daily online access to those 
calculations and provides visibility into funding

[[Page 62544]]

balances. This information is provided through the eVMS front-end user 
interface and does not require back-end access to the calculations. The 
system supports financial oversight and payment integrity by 
calculating monthly payments using near real-time HAP eligibility at 
the participant level. Any changes in HAP eligibility are promptly 
reflected in the next payment cycle, ensuring timely and accurate 
adjustments. As a result, payments are aligned with participants' 
current needs, reducing the risk of overpayments, waste, and potential 
abuse within the Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) Program.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: HUD, PIH is establishing a Privacy Act 
system of records titled Enterprise Voucher Management System (eVMS). 
eVMS displays the full Head of Household (HOH) name and Social Security 
Number (SSN) to authorize HUD and Public Housing Authority (PHA) users 
to identify the Family Report records that comprise Housing Assistance 
Payment (HAP) calculations. The system provides a daily online view 
that enables authorized users to monitor program financial activity, 
including monthly HAP calculations, historical payment statements, and 
available budget authority balances through the eVMS user interface 
without requiring back-end system access. eVMS also provides a report 
that allows HUD and PHA users to export details of monthly HAP 
disbursements and retrieve historical versions of those reports. The 
ability to track HAP disbursements at the family level supports 
compliance with the Payment Integrity Act and strengthens program 
oversight and accountability. Head of Household names and Social 
Security Numbers are stored within eVMS (under the HUD WASS/Secure 
Systems environment). SSNs are maintained in an unmasked format to 
enable authorized HUD and PHA personnel to accurately identify families 
associated with HAP calculations and disbursements.

PURPOSES OF THE SYSTEM:
    The purpose of the eVMS will be to meet the Treasury cash 
management requirements. Federal funds are disbursed to housing 
agencies based on immediate need. eVMS aggregates HAP eligibilities 
from all active participants, restricting disbursements to the minimum 
amount required to pay owners. This reduces excess unspent HAP funds 
and ensures accurate monthly adjustments for changes in eligibility. 
eVMS stores the applicable parts of the Family Report data in eVMS. 
Transitioning to the use of IMS/PIC (Family Report) participant-level 
HAP eligibility data enables month-to-month reconciliation of payments, 
enhances traceability for audit and monitoring purposes, and 
strengthens overall administration of Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) 
Program subsidies. eVMS enhances the efficiency of the HCV Program by 
automating key aspects of the cash management process, while 
significantly strengthening financial oversight and payment integrity. 
The system supports ongoing monitoring by providing detailed, traceable 
reporting that allows program administrators to identify discrepancies, 
unusual payment patterns, and potential compliance issues. These 
capabilities facilitate early detection of errors, as well as potential 
fraud, waste, and abuse. eVMS enables Public Housing Agencies (PHAs) 
and HUD to track individual participant families and the exact Housing 
Assistance Payments (HAP) allocated for each. Updated daily, this 
system provides both agencies with enhanced transparency regarding 
federal fund disbursements before monies are officially issued. In 
addition, automated reconciliations and validation checks help ensure 
that payments are consistent with reported participant data, reducing 
the likelihood of overpayments or improper disbursements.

CATEGORIES OF INDIVIDUALS COVERED BY THE SYSTEM:
    Categories of individuals covered by the system include: Housing 
Choice Voucher (HCV) participants, household members, and owners 
(meaning the owner of the property a HCV participant is leased in).

CATEGORIES OF RECORDS IN THE SYSTEM:
    Categories of records in the system include Head of Household full 
name, Social Security Number (SSN), Housing Assistance Payment (HAP) 
amounts, administrative fee data, landlord identifiers, PHA codes, and 
financial reconciliation data.

RECORD SOURCE CATEGORIES:
    eVMS receives records from other HUD information systems, including 
IMS/PIC, the Public Housing Data Warehouse (PIHDW), the Financial Data 
Mart (FDM), the HUD Central Accounting and Program System (HUDCAPS), 
and the Voucher Management System (VMS). User provisioning and access 
authorization information is provided through DIAMS.

POLICIES AND PRACTICES FOR STORAGE OF RECORDS:
    Records are maintained in electronic format only. Security is HUD 
Web Access Security Subsystem (WASS) for all applications and other HUD 
security IT protocols. PII is stored on a centralized HUD server. 
Methods to ensure only authorized personnel have access to PII are 
implemented. PHAs must apply for a user ID to access the system. 
Technical controls include: role-based access controls, firewall, 
Personal Identity Verification (PIV) card, and user identification and 
password.

POLICIES AND PRACTICES FOR RETRIEVAL OF RECORDS:
    Records are primarily retrieved by PHA code and related 
programmatic identifiers. However, authorized users may also access 
participant-level records associated with Head of Household names and 
Social Security Numbers (SSNs) in connection with Housing Assistance 
Payment (HAP) calculations, reconciliations, reporting, and payment 
validation activities.

POLICIES AND PRACTICES FOR RETENTION AND DISPOSAL OF RECORDS:
    Under General Records Schedule (GRS) authority: DAA-2013-0003-0001: 
Temporary. Destroy six (6) years after final payment or cancellation, 
but longer retention is authorized if required for business use.

ADMINISTRATIVE, TECHNICAL, AND PHYSICAL SAFEGUARDS:
    The eVMS system incorporates a comprehensive set of security 
controls designed to protect data and ensure compliance with applicable 
federal information security requirements. Access is restricted to 
authorized users based on a demonstrated need-to-know and is governed 
by role-based access controls and strong authentication mechanisms, 
including multi-factor authentication. Data is protected and system 
activities are logged, monitored, and regularly reviewed to support 
auditing, accountability, and oversight.
    Additional safeguards include user accountability requirements, 
such as acknowledgment of Rules of Behavior and confidentiality 
obligations prior to being granted access. Access to system 
infrastructure, including servers, storage, and backup environments, is

[[Page 62546]]

restricted and controlled through administrative and technical 
safeguards, with monitoring in place to prevent unauthorized access.
    All users must be properly authorized and authenticated before 
accessing system resources, and electronic records are maintained 
within secure, access-controlled environments. External partners and 
authorized entities are required to adhere to applicable access and 
security requirements consistent with federal standards. Collectively, 
these measures ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability 
of information, while aligning with federal information security 
frameworks and supporting the protection of sensitive data.
