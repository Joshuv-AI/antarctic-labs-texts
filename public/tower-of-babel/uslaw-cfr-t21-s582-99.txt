21 C.F.R. § 582.99 — Adjuvants for pesticide chemicals.
Citation: us:cfr:21:582.99
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-582.99
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 582 (Substances Generally Recognized As Safe). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adjuvants, identified and used in accordance with 40 CFR 180.910 and 180.920, which are added to pesticide use dilutions by a grower or applicator prior to application to the raw agricultural commodity, are exempt from the requirement of tolerances under section 409 of the act.
