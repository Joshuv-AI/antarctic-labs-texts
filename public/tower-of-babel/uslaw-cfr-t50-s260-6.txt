50 C.F.R. § 260.6 — Application for inspection service.
Citation: us:cfr:50:260.6
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-260.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 260 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prospective service participants must submit an application for inspection service per the Application for Inspection Services procedures in the SIP Manual. To be considered for approval, applications for inspection service must be complete and conform to all SIP inspection service requirements as specified in the SIP Manual.
