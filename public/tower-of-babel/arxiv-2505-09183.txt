Ordinary and spectral extremal problems on vertex disjoint copies of even fans
Yiting Cai, Bo Zhou

Abstract
Let ex ⁡ ( n , F ) \mathrm{ex}(n,F) and spex ⁡ ( n , F ) \mathrm{spex}(n,F) be the maximum size and spectral radius among all F F -free graphs with fixed order n n , respectively. A fan is a graph P 1 ∨ P s P_{1}\vee P_{s} (join of a vertex and a path of order s s ) for s ≥ 3 s\geq 3 , and it is called an even fan if s s is even. In this paper, we study ex ⁡ ( n , t ⁡ ( P 1 ∨ P 2 ​ k ) ) \mathrm{ex}(n,t(P_{1}\vee P_{2k})) , spex ⁡ ( n , t ⁡ ( P 1 ∨ P 2 ​ k ) ) \mathrm{spex}(n,t(P_{1}\vee P_{2k})) with t ≥ 1 t\geq 1 and k ≥ 3 k\geq 3 and characterize the corresponding extremal graphs for sufficiently large n n . Keywords: graph size, spectral radius, extremal graphs

\displaystyle\mathbf{x}^{\top}(\rho(G)-\rho(A_{r}))\mathbf{z}
