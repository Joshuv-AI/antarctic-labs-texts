49 C.F.R. § 1.43 — Delegations to the Director of the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1.43
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1 (Organization And Delegation Of Powers And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization is delegated authority to:
(a) Exercise departmental responsibility for the implementation and execution of functions and duties under sections 2[8] and 2[15] of the Small Business Act (Pub. L. 85-836) [15 U.S.C. 637 and 644].
(b) Carry out the functions vested in the Secretary by 49 U.S.C. 332 (Minority Resource Center).
