27 C.F.R. § 72.32 — Interest claimed.
Citation: us:cfr:27:72.32
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-72.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 72 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person claiming an interest in property, including carriers, seized by alcohol, tobacco and firearms officers as subject to administrative forfeiture may file a petition addressed to the Director, for remission or mitigation of the forfeiture of such property.
