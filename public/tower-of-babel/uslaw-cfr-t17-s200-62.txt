17 C.F.R. § 200.62 — Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:17:200.62
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-200.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 200 (Organization; Conduct And Ethics; And Information And Requests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All proceedings required to be determined by the Commission on the record shall be determined by the members solely upon the record and the arguments of the parties or their counsel properly made in the regular course of such proceeding. A member shall at all times comply with the Commission's Code of Behavior governing ex parte communications between persons outside the Commission and decisional employees, § 200.110 et seq.
