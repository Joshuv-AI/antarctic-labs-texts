36 C.F.R. § 34.7 — Cultivation of controlled substances.
Citation: us:cfr:36:34.7
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-34.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 34 (El Portal Administrative Site Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the provisions of § 2.35 of this chapter, the planting, cultivating, harvesting, drying or processing of a controlled substance, or any part thereof, is prohibited.
