48 C.F.R. § 326.607-4 — 326.607-4 Challenge Not Timely.
Citation: us:cfr:48:326.607-4
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-326.607-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 326 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a CO receives an untimely filed challenge of a representation, the CO must notify the challenger that the challenge cannot be considered on the instant acquisition but will be considered in any future actions. However, the CO may question at any time, before or after award, the representation of an IEE.
