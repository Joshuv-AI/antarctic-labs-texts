44 U.S.C. § 1313 — Education, Commissioner of: report
Citation: us:usc:44:1313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1313
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 13 (Particular Reports And Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to the usual number of the report of the Commissioner of Education, thirty-five thousand copies shall be printed: five thousand for the Senate, ten thousand for the House of Representatives, and twenty thousand for distribution by the Commissioner of Education.
