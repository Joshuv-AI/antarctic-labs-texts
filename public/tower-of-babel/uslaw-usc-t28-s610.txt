28 U.S.C. § 610 — Courts defined
Citation: us:usc:28:610
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section610
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 41 (Administrative Office Of United States Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter the word “courts” includes the courts of appeals and district courts of the United States, the United States District Court for the District of the Canal Zone, the District Court of Guam, the District Court of the Virgin Islands, the United States Court of Federal Claims, and the Court of International Trade.
