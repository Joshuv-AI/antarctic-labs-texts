10 U.S.C. § 6279 — Scope of authority to carry out plant projects
Citation: us:usc:10:6279
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6279
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 607 (Budget And Financial Management Matters); Subchapter I (Recurring National Security Authorization Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out programs necessary for national security, the authority of the Secretary of Energy to carry out plant projects includes authority for maintenance, restoration, planning, construction, acquisition, modification of facilities, and the continuation of projects authorized in prior years, and land acquisition related thereto.
