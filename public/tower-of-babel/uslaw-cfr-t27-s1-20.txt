27 C.F.R. § 1.20 — Importers.
Citation: us:cfr:27:1.20
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-1.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 1 (Basic Permit Requirements Under The Federal Alcohol Administration Act, Nonindustrial Use Of Distilled Spirits And Wine, Bulk Sales And Bottling Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person, except pursuant to a basic permit issued under the Act, shall:
(a) Engage in the business of importing into the United States distilled spirits, wine, or malt beverages; or
(b) While so engaged, sell, offer or deliver for sale, contract to sell, or ship, in interstate or foreign commerce, directly or indirectly or through an affiliate, distilled spirits, wine, or malt beverages so imported.
