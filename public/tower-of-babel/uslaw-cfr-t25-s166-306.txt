25 C.F.R. § 166.306 — Can the BIA adjust the grazing capacity?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.306
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. In consultation with the Indian landowners or in the BIA's discretion based on good cause, we may adjust the grazing capacity using the best evaluation method(s) relevant to the ecological region.
