Ivan Gadjev 1, Parvan Parvanov 2, Rumen Uluchev 3

Abstract
. A new Goodman-Sharma modification of the Baskakov operator is presented for approximation of bounded and continuous on [ 0 , ∞ ) [0,\,\infty) functions. In our study on the approximation error of the proposed operator we prove direct and strong converse theorems with respect to a related K-functional. This operator is linear but not positive. However it has the advantage of a higher order of approximation compared to the Goodman-Sharma variant of the Baskakov operator defined in 2005 by Finta.

1.
Introduction

In 1957, Baskakov
[
2
]
suggested the linear positive operators

(1.1)

B_{n}(f,x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\,f\Big(\frac{k}{n}\Big)P_{n,k}(x)

for approximation of bounded and continuous on
[0,\,\infty)
functions
f
, where

P_{n,k}(x)=\binom{n+k-1}{k}x^{k}(1+x)^{-n-k},\qquad k=0,1,\ldots,

can be considered as Baskakov basis functions.

Following the Goodman and Sharma modification
[
6
]
of the Bernstein polynomials,
Finta
[
4
]
introduced a variant of the operators
B_{n}
for functions
f
which are Lebesgue
measurable on
(0,\infty)
with a finite limit
f(0)
as
x\to 0
:

(1.2)

\begin{gathered}V_{n}(f,x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\,v_{n,k}(f)P_{n,k}(x),\\
v_{n,0}(f)=f(0),\qquad v_{n,k}(f)=(n+1)\int_{0}^{\infty}P_{n+2,k-1}(t)f(t)\,dt,\ \ k\in\mathbb{N},\end{gathered}

Finta proved a strong converse result of Type B (in the terminology of
[
3
]
) for
V_{n}
.
The research on the operators (
1.2
) were continued in
[
5
,
7
,
8
]
.

Later Ivanov and Parvanov
[
9
]
investigated the uniform weighted approximation error
of the Baskakov-type operators
V_{n}
for weights of the form

\big(\frac{x}{1+x}\big)^{\gamma_{0}}(1+x)^{\gamma_{\infty}}
,
\gamma_{0},\gamma_{\infty}\in[-1,0]
,
by establishing direct and strong converse theorems in terms of the weighted K-functional.

Recently Jabbar and Hassan
[
10
]
studied a family of Baskakov-type operators, where the
Baskakov basis functions
P_{n,k}
in
B_{n}f
are replaced by linear combinations of Baskakov
basis functions of lower degree with coefficients being polynomials of appropriate degree.
The benefit is obtaining a better order of approximation than the classical Baskakov operator.
Jabar and Hassan proved certain direct results on the approximation rate of these operators and
attached results of numerical experiments.

The ideas presented in
[
10
]
suggested the authors of the current paper to define and
explore new operators with higher order of approximation.

As usual,
Df(x)=\frac{df(x)}{dx}=f^{\prime}(x)
,
D^{2}f(x)=\frac{d^{2}f(x)}{dx^{2}}=f^{\prime\prime}(x)
, and we denote
by

\psi(x)=x(1+x)

the weight function which is naturally related to the second order differential operator for the
Baskakov type operators. Also, we set

(1.3)

\widetilde{D}f(x):=\psi(x)f^{\prime\prime}(x),

and

\widetilde{D}^{2}f:=\widetilde{D}\widetilde{D}f,\qquad\widetilde{D}^{r+1}f:=\widetilde{D}\widetilde{D}^{r}f,\qquad r=2,3\ldots\,.

Our study is on the operators explicitly defined by

(1.4)

\widetilde{V}_{n}(f,x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}v_{n,k}(f)\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x),\qquad x\in[0,\infty),

with basis functions

(1.5)

\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)=P_{n,k}(x)-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}P_{n,k}(x)

The operators
\widetilde{V}_{n}
relate to operators
V_{n}
in the same manner as some operators in

[
10
]
relate to the classical Baskakov operators (
1.1
).

By
C[0,\infty)
we denote the space of all continuous on
[0,\infty)
functions, and

L_{\infty}[0,\infty)
stands for the space of all Lebesgue measurable and essentially bounded on

[0,\infty)
functions equipped with the uniform norm
\|\cdot\|
.
Let us set

W^{2}(\psi)=\big\{g\,:\,g,g^{\prime}\in AC_{loc}(0,\infty),\ \widetilde{D}g\in L_{\infty}[0,\infty)\big\},

where
AC_{loc}(0,\infty)
consists of the functions which are absolutely continuous on
[a,b]
for
every
[a,b]\subset(0,\infty)
.

By
W^{2}_{0}(\psi)
we denote the subspace of
W^{2}(\psi)
of functions
g
satisfying the additional
boundary condition

\lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\widetilde{D}g(x)=0.

For functions
f\in C[0,\infty)
and
t>0
we define the K-functional

(1.6)

K(f,t)=\inf\big\{\|f-g\|+t\|\widetilde{D}^{2}g\|\,:\,g\in W^{2}_{0}(\psi),\ \widetilde{D}g\in W^{2}(\psi)\big\}.

Below we investigate the error rate for functions
f\in C[0,\infty)
approximated by the
Goodman-Sharma modification of the Baskakov operator (
1.4
). Direct and strong converse
theorems are proved in means of the above K-functional
K(f,t)
and we summarize our main results
in the following statements.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
\,n\geq 2
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
. Then for all
f\in C[0,\infty)
, there holds

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|\leq 3K\Big(f,\frac{1}{n^{2}}\Big).

Theorem 1.2
.

There exist constants
C,L>0
such that for all
f\in C[0,\infty)
and
\ell,n\in\mathbb{N}

with
\ell\geq Ln
there holds

K\Big(f,\frac{1}{n^{2}}\Big)\leq C\,\frac{\ell^{2}}{n^{2}}\,\big(\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|+\big\|\widetilde{V}_{\ell}f-f\big\|\big).

In particular,

K\Big(f,\frac{1}{n^{2}}\Big)\leq C\,\big(\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|+\big\|\widetilde{V}_{Ln}f-f\big\|\big).

The constants
C
are independent of the function
f
,
\ell
and
n
.

The article is organized in the following way. Section 1 is an introduction to the topic. We give
notations, define a new modification of the Baskakov operator and claim our main results. Section 2
includes preliminary and auxiliary statements. In Section 3 we present an estimation of the norm
of the operator
\widetilde{V}_{n}
, a Jackson type inequality and a proof of the direct theorem. The converse
result for the modified Baskakov operator (
1.4
) is discussed in Section 4. Inequalities of
the Voronovskaya type and Bernstein type for
\widetilde{V}_{n}
are proved using the differential
operator
\widetilde{D}
, defined in (
1.3
). Theorem
1.2
represents a strong converse
inequality of Type B in the Ditzian-Ivanov classification
[
3
]
. Finally, a proof of the
converse theorem is given.

Preliminaries and Auxiliary Results

The central moments of the Baskakov operator are defined by

\mu_{n,j}(x)=B_{n}\big((t-x)^{j},x\big)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{k}{n}-x\Big)^{j}P_{n,k}(x),\qquad j=0,1,\ldots\,.

We set
P_{n,k}:=0
if
k<0
. The next proposition summarizes several well known relations and
formulae for the Baskakov basis polynomials.

Proposition 2.1

(see, e.g.
[
9

)
.

(a) The following identities are valid:

(2.1)

\displaystyle kP_{n,k}(x)=nxP_{n+1,k-1}(x),\qquad(n+k)P_{n,k}(x)=n(1+x)P_{n+1,k}(x),\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt

(2.2)

\displaystyle k(k-1)P_{n,k}(x)=n(n+1)x^{2}P_{n+2,k-2}(x),\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt

(2.3)

\displaystyle(n+k)(n+k+1)P_{n,k}(x)=n(n+1)(1+x)^{2}P_{n+2,k}(x),\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt

(2.4)

\displaystyle\tfrac{x}{1+x}\,P_{n,k}(x)=\tfrac{k+1}{n+k}\,P_{n,k+1}(x),\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt

(2.5)

\displaystyle\tfrac{1+x}{x}\,P_{n,k}(x)=\tfrac{n+k-1}{k}\,P_{n,k-1}(x),\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt

(2.6)

\displaystyle P_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)=n\big[P_{n+1,k-1}(x)-P_{n+1,k}(x)\big],\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt

(2.7)

\displaystyle P_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)=n(n+1)\big[P_{n+2,k-2}(x)-2P_{n+2,k-1}(x)+P_{n+2,k}(x)\big].

(b) For the moments
\mu_{n,j}(x)
,
j=0,1,...,4
, we have:

\displaystyle\mu_{n,0}(x)

\displaystyle=B_{n}\big((t-x)^{0},x\big)=1,

\displaystyle\mu_{n,1}(x)

\displaystyle=B_{n}\big((t-x),x\big)=0,

\displaystyle\mu_{n,2}(x)

\displaystyle=B_{n}\big((t-x)^{2},x\big)=\frac{\psi(x)}{n},

\displaystyle\mu_{n,3}(x)

\displaystyle=B_{n}\big((t-x)^{3},x\big)=\frac{(1+2x)\psi(x)}{n^{2}},

\displaystyle\mu_{n,4}(x)

\displaystyle=B_{n}\big((t-x)^{4},x\big)=\frac{3(n+2)\psi^{2}(x)}{n^{3}}+\frac{\psi(x)}{n^{3}}.

The operators

\widetilde{V}_{n}

Proposition 2.2
.

If the operators

\widetilde{V}_{n}

(c)

(f)
\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\widetilde{V}_{n}f=f

(g)
\big\|\widetilde{D}V_{n}f\big\|\leq\big\|\widetilde{D}f\big\|

Proof.

For the proof of (a), see
[
9
, Theorem 2.5]
.

We have

\displaystyle\widetilde{V}_{n}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}v_{n,k}(f)\widetilde{P}_{n,k}

\displaystyle=v_{n,0}(f)\Big(P_{n,0}-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}P_{n,0}\Big)+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}v_{n,k}(f)\Big(P_{n,k}-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}P_{n,k}\Big)

\displaystyle=v_{n,0}(f)P_{n,0}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}v_{n,k}(f)P_{n,k}-\frac{\psi}{n}\bigg(v_{n,0}(f)D^{2}P_{n,0}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}v_{n,k}(f)D^{2}P_{n,k}\bigg)

\displaystyle=V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\psi D^{2}V_{n}f=V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}f.

Then from (a) we obtain

\widetilde{V}_{n}f=V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}f=V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,V_{n}\widetilde{D}f=V_{n}\Big(f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}f\Big),

which proves (b).

Now, commutative properties (c) and (d) follow from (b) and (a):

\widetilde{D}\widetilde{V}_{n}f=\widetilde{D}V_{n}\Big(f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}f\Big)=V_{n}\Big(\widetilde{D}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}\widetilde{D}f\Big)=\widetilde{V}_{n}(\widetilde{D}f),

and

\displaystyle V_{n}\widetilde{V}_{n}f

\displaystyle=V_{n}\Big(V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}f\Big)=V_{n}V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,V_{n}\widetilde{D}V_{n}f

\displaystyle=V_{n}V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}V_{n}f=V_{n}(V_{n}f)-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}(V_{n}f)=\widetilde{V}_{n}V_{n}f.

The operators
\widetilde{V}_{n}
commute in the sense of (e), since

\displaystyle\widetilde{V}_{m}\widetilde{V}_{n}f

\displaystyle=\widetilde{V}_{m}\Big(V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}f\Big)

\displaystyle=V_{m}\Big(V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}f\Big)-\frac{1}{m}\widetilde{D}V_{m}\Big(V_{n}f-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}V_{n}f\Big)

\displaystyle=V_{m}V_{n}\Big(f-\frac{m+n}{mn}\,\widetilde{D}f+\frac{1}{mn}\,\widetilde{D}^{2}f\Big).

The same expression in the last line we obtain for
\widetilde{V}_{n}\widetilde{V}_{m}f
because of properties
(a), (b) and
V_{m}V_{n}f=V_{n}V_{m}f

In

Proposition 2.5
.

Let

\lambda(n):=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,,\qquad\theta(n):=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{2}(k+1)^{2}}\,,\qquad n\in\mathbb{N}.

Then, for
n\geq 2
the next inequalities are satisfied:

(2.14)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{3n^{2}}\leq\lambda(n)\leq\frac{1}{n^{2}}\,,

(2.15)

\displaystyle\theta(n)\leq\frac{4}{9n^{3}}\,.

Proof.

For
\lambda(n)
we have

\frac{1}{3n^{2}}\leq\frac{1}{2n(n+1)}=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)(k+2)}<\lambda(n)<\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k-1)k(k+1)}=\frac{1}{2n(n-1)}\leq\frac{1}{n^{2}}\,.

For the upper estimate of
\theta(n)
we obtain

\theta(n)<\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k-1)k(k+1)(k+2)}=\frac{1}{3n(n^{2}-1)}\leq\frac{4}{9n^{3}}\,.

∎

3.
A Direct Theorem

First we estimate from above the norm of the operator
\widetilde{V}_{n}
defined in (
1.4
).

Lemma 3.1
.

If
\,n\geq 2
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
f\in C[0,\infty)
, then

(3.1)

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f\big\|\leq 2\,\|f\|.

Proof.

From (
1.5
) and Proposition
2.3
(a) we have

\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)=P_{n,k}(x)-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}P_{n,k}(x)=\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\Big)P_{n,k}(x).

Taking into account that
|v_{n,k}(f)|\leq\|f\|
we get for
x\in[0,\infty)
,

\displaystyle\big|\widetilde{V}_{n}(f,x)\big|

\displaystyle=\left|\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}v_{n,k}(f)\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\right|\leq\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|v_{n,k}(f)|\,\big|\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|

\displaystyle\leq\|f\|\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big|\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|=\|f\|\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big|1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\Big|P_{n,k}(x).

Then, the Cauchy inequality yields

\big|\widetilde{V}_{n}(f,x)\big|\leq\|f\|\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\Big)^{2}P_{n,k}(x)}\,\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}P_{n,k}(x)}.

Since
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}P_{n,k}(x)=1
identically, by Proposition
2.3
(b) with
\alpha=1
we find

\big|\widetilde{V}_{n}(f,x)\big|\leq\sqrt{3+\frac{2}{n}}\,\|f\|<2\,\|f\|,\qquad x\in[0,\infty).

Hence, inequality (
3.1
) follows.
∎

Now we prove a Jackson type inequality.

Lemma 3.2
.

If
\,n\geq 2
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
f\in W^{2}_{0}(\psi)
and
\widetilde{D}f\in W^{2}(\psi)
, then

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|\leq\frac{1}{n^{2}}\|\widetilde{D}^{2}f\|.

Proof.

According to
[
9
, Lemma 2.2]
we have

V_{k}f-V_{k+1}f=\frac{1}{k(k+1)}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}f.

Combining the latter with Proposition
2.2
(a)–(b) we obtain

\displaystyle\widetilde{V}_{k}f-\widetilde{V}_{k+1}f

\displaystyle=V_{k}f-\frac{1}{k}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}f-V_{k+1}f+\frac{1}{k+1}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k+1}f

\displaystyle=\Big(\frac{1}{k}-\frac{1}{k+1}\Big)\widetilde{D}V_{k}f+\frac{1}{k+1}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k+1}f-\frac{1}{k}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}f

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{k+1}\big(\widetilde{D}V_{k}f-\widetilde{D}V_{k+1}f\big)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{k+1}\big(V_{k}\widetilde{D}f-V_{k+1}\widetilde{D}f\big)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{k+1}\cdot\frac{1}{k(k+1)}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}\widetilde{D}f,

i.e.

\widetilde{V}_{k}f-\widetilde{V}_{k+1}f=-\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}\widetilde{D}f.

Therefore for every
s>n
we have

\displaystyle\widetilde{V}_{n}f-\widetilde{V}_{s}f=\sum_{k=n}^{s-1}\big(\widetilde{V}_{k}f-\widetilde{V}_{k+1}f\big)=-\sum_{k=n}^{s-1}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}\widetilde{D}f.

Letting
s\rightarrow\infty
by Proposition
2.2
(a) and (f) we obtain

(3.2)

\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f=-\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,\widetilde{D}V_{k}\widetilde{D}f=-\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,V_{k}\widetilde{D}^{2}f.

Then Proposition
2.2
(g) yields

\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\|\leq\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}V_{k}\widetilde{D}f\big\|\leq\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|

and from (
2.14
) we conclude

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|\leq\frac{1}{n^{2}}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|.

∎

Based on both lemmas above we prove a direct result for the approximation rate of functions

f\in C[0,\infty)
by the operators (
1.4
) in means of the K-functional defined in
(
1.6
).

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.

Let
g
be arbitrary function such that
g\in W^{2}_{0}(\psi)
and
\widetilde{D}g\in W^{2}(\psi)
. Then by
Lemma
3.1
and Lemma
3.2
we have

\displaystyle\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|

\displaystyle\leq\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-\widetilde{V}_{n}g\big\|+\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}g-g\big\|+\|g-f\|

\displaystyle\leq 3\|f-g\|+\frac{1}{n^{2}}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}g\big\|

\displaystyle\leq 3\Big(\|f-g\|+\frac{1}{n^{2}}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}g\big\|\Big).

Taking infimum over all functions
g\in W^{2}_{0}(\psi)
with
\widetilde{D}g\in W^{2}(\psi)
we obtain

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f\big\|\leq 3\,K\Big(f,\frac{1}{n}\Big).

∎

4.
A Strong Converse Inequality

First, we will prove a Voronovskaya type result for the operator
\widetilde{V}_{n}
.

Lemma 4.1
.

If
\,n\geq 2
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\displaystyle\,\lambda(n)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}
,

\displaystyle\,\theta(n)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{2}(k+1)^{2}}
, and
f\in C[0,\infty)
is such that

f\in W^{2}_{0}(\psi)
,
\widetilde{D}^{2}f\in W^{2}(\psi)
, then

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f+\lambda(n)\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|\leq\theta(n)\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{3}f\big\|.

Proof.

We have from (
3.2
) that

\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f+\lambda(n)\widetilde{D}^{2}f=-\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{V_{k}\widetilde{D}^{2}f}{k(k+1)^{2}}+\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{\widetilde{D}^{2}f}{k(k+1)^{2}}=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{\widetilde{D}^{2}f-V_{k}\widetilde{D}^{2}f}{k(k+1)^{2}}.

Then

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f+\lambda(n)\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|\leq\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}f-V_{k}\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|.

Using (
2.8
) with
\widetilde{D}^{2}f
replacing
f
we obtain

\big\|\widetilde{V}_{n}f-f+\lambda(n)\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|\leq\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)^{2}}\cdot\frac{1}{k}\,\big\|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{D}^{2}f\big\|=\theta(n)\,\big\|\widetilde{D}^{3}f\big\|.

∎

We need the next inequality of Bernstein type.

Lemma 4.2
.

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
f\in C[0,\infty)
. Then for
n\geq 17
the inequality

\|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{V}_{n}f\|\leq\widetilde{C}\,n\|f\|

holds true, where
\widetilde{C}=6+4\sqrt{3}
.

Proof.

Since

\big|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{V}_{n}(f,x)\big|\leq\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|v_{n,k}(f)|\,\big|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|\leq\|f\|\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|,

it is sufficient to prove that

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big|\psi(x)\widetilde{P}_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)\big|\leq(6+4\sqrt{3})\,n,\qquad n\geq 17.

Remind that by (
2.12
) we have

\widetilde{D}P_{n,k}(x)=T_{n,k}(x)P_{n,k}(x),

hence

\displaystyle\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle=P_{n,k}(x)-\frac{1}{n}\,\widetilde{D}P_{n,k}(x)=\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\!\Big)P_{n,k}(x),

\displaystyle\widetilde{P}_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)P_{n,k}(x)-\frac{2}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)P_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)+\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\!\Big)P_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x).

Then,

\displaystyle\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle=-\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)P_{n,k}(x)-\frac{2\psi(x)}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)P_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)+\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\!\Big)\psi(x)P_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)

\displaystyle=-\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)P_{n,k}(x)-\frac{2\psi(x)}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)P_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)+\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\!\Big)T_{n,k}(x)P_{n,k}(x).

By using Proposition
2.4
(a)–(c) we obtain

-\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)P_{n,k}^{\prime}(x)=T_{n+1,k-1}(x)P_{n+1,k-1}(x)+T_{n+1,k}(x)P_{n+1,k}(x)+\frac{\psi(x)}{n}T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)P_{n,k}(x)

and then

\displaystyle\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\,T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)P_{n,k}(x)+2\big[T_{n+1,k-1}(x)P_{n+1,k-1}(x)+T_{n+1,k}(x)P_{n+1,k}(x)\big]

\displaystyle\quad+\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\!\Big)T_{n,k}(x)P_{n,k}(x).

Therefore

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|

\displaystyle\leq a_{n}(x)+b_{n}(x)+c_{n}(x),

where

\displaystyle a_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big|T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)\big|P_{n,k}(x),

\displaystyle b_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|T_{n+1,k-1}(x)|P_{n+1,k-1}(x)+2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|T_{n+1,k}(x)|P_{n+1,k}(x),

\displaystyle c_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big|\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\!\Big)T_{n,k}(x)\Big|P_{n,k}(x).

(i) Estimation of
a_{n}(x)
.

We will estimate
a_{n}(x)
separately in the intervals
\big(0,\frac{1}{n}\big]
and

\big[\frac{1}{n},\infty\big)
.

Let
x\in\big(0,\frac{1}{n}\big]
. Then, from (
2.11
),
T_{n,0}^{\prime\prime}(x)<0
and
T_{n,1}^{\prime\prime}(x)<0
.
Since
\big(\frac{1+x}{x}\big)^{3}
is a decreasing function for
x\in\big(0,\frac{1}{n}\big]
and

\frac{(n+k)(n+k+1)}{k(k-1)}
decreases for
k\geq 2
, we have

\Big(\frac{1+x}{x}\Big)^{3}>(n+1)^{3}>\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2}\geq\frac{(n+k)(n+k+1)}{k(k-1)}.

Hence

T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)=2\Big(\frac{k(k-1)}{x^{3}}-\frac{(n+k)(n+k+1)}{(1+x)^{3}}\Big)>0,\qquad k\geq 2.

Applying the latter inequalities, Proposition
2.4
(c) and (
2.1
) we obtain

\displaystyle a_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)|P_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\bigg[\!\frac{n(n+1)}{(1+x)^{3}}\frac{1}{(1+x)^{n}}+\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}{(1+x)^{3}}\frac{nx}{(1+x)^{n+1}}+\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}T_{n,k}^{\prime\prime}(x)P_{n,k}(x)\!\bigg]

\displaystyle=2\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\bigg[\frac{n(n+1)}{(1+x)^{n+3}}+\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)x}{(1+x)^{n+4}}\bigg]+\frac{\psi(x)}{n}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}T_{n,k}(x)P_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle=2(n+1)\,\frac{x}{(1+x)^{n+2}}+2n(n+1)(n+2)\frac{x^{2}}{(1+x)^{n+3}}

\displaystyle\quad+2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\big[(n+k-1)P_{n+1,k-2}(x)-(k+1)P_{n+1,k+1}(x)\big]

\displaystyle=2(n+1)\,\frac{x}{(1+x)^{n+2}}+2n(n+1)(n+2)\,\frac{x^{2}}{(1+x)^{n+3}}

\displaystyle\quad+2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(n+1)(1+x)P_{n+2,k-1}(x)-2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(n+1)xP_{n+2,k}(x)

\displaystyle=2(n+1)\frac{x}{(1+x)^{n+2}}+2n(n+1)(n+2)\,\frac{x^{2}}{(1+x)^{n+3}}+2(n+1)(1+x)-2(n+1)x

\displaystyle<\frac{2(n+1)}{n}+\frac{2(n+1)(n+2)}{n^{2}}+2(n+1).

Therefore,

(4.1)

a_{n}(x)<3n,\qquad\text{for all}\ \ n\geq 8,\ \ x\in\big[0,\tfrac{1}{n}\big).

Now, let
x\in\big[\frac{1}{n},\infty\big)
. By the Cauchy inequality we have

\displaystyle a_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{2\psi(x)}{n}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big|\frac{k(k-1)}{x^{3}}-\frac{(n+k)(n+k+1)}{(1+x)^{3}}\Big|P_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2\psi(x)}{n}\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{k(k-1)}{x^{3}}-\frac{(n+k)(n+k+1)}{(1+x)^{3}}\Big)^{\!2}P_{n,k}(x)}\,\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}P_{n,k}(x)}

and then

(4.2)

a_{n}^{2}(x)=\frac{4\psi^{2}(x)}{n^{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{k(k-1)}{x^{3}}-\frac{(n+k)(n+k+1)}{(1+x)^{3}}\Big)^{\!2}P_{n,k}(x).

From (
2.1
) and (
2.2
) we get

(4.3)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}k^{2}(k-1)^{2}P_{n,k}(x)=n(n+1)(n+2)\Big((n+3)x^{4}+4x^{3}+\frac{2x^{2}}{n+2}\Big),

(4.4)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}k(k-1)(n+k)(n+k+1)P_{n,k}(x)=n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)x^{2}(1+x)^{2},

(4.5)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(n+k)^{2}(n+k+1)^{2}P_{n,k}(x)=n(n+1)(n+2)(1+x)^{2}

\displaystyle\hskip 170.71652pt\times\Big(\!(n+3)(1+x)^{2}-4(1+x)+\frac{2}{n+2}\Big).

After straightforward computations (
4.2
)–(
4.5
) yields

a^{2}_{n}(x)=\frac{4(n+1)(n+2)}{n}\bigg[n+3+4\bigg(\frac{(1+x)^{2}}{x}-\frac{x^{2}}{1+x}\bigg)+\frac{2}{n+2}\bigg(\frac{(1+x)^{2}}{x^{2}}-\frac{x^{2}}{(1+x)^{2}}\bigg)\bigg].

Observe that functions
g_{1}(x)=\frac{(1+x)^{2}}{x}-\frac{x^{2}}{1+x}
and

g_{2}(x)=\frac{(1+x)^{2}}{x^{2}}-\frac{x^{2}}{(1+x)^{2}}
are decreasing for
x\in(0,\infty)
. Hence, for

x\in\big[\frac{1}{n},\infty)
,

g_{1}(x)\leq g_{1}\Big(\frac{1}{n}\Big)=\frac{(n+1)^{2}}{n}-\frac{1}{n(n+1)},\qquad g_{2}(x)\leq g_{2}\Big(\frac{1}{n}\Big)=(n+1)^{2}+\frac{1}{(n+1)^{2}}.

Then

\displaystyle a^{2}_{n}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4(n+1)(n+2)}{n}\bigg[n+3+\frac{4(n+1)^{2}}{n}+\frac{4(n+1)^{2}}{n+2}+\frac{2}{(n+1)^{2}(n+2)}-\frac{4}{n(n+1)}\bigg]

\displaystyle<\frac{4(n+1)(n+2)}{n}\bigg[n+3+\frac{4(n+1)^{2}}{n}+\frac{4(n+1)^{2}}{n+2}\bigg]

\displaystyle<36n^{2},

i.e.

(4.6)

a_{n}(x)<6n,\qquad\text{for all}\ \ n\geq 17,\ \ x\in\big[\tfrac{1}{n},\infty\big).

Now from (
4.1
) and (
4.6
) we get

(4.7)

a_{n}(x)\leq 6\,n,\qquad\text{for all}\ \ n\geq 17,\ \ x\in[0,\infty).

(ii) Estimation of
b_{n}(x)
.

By the Cauchy inequality and Proposition
2.3
(b) with
\alpha=0
we obtain

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left|T_{n,k}(x)\right|P_{n,k}(x)\leq\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}T_{n,k}^{2}(x)P_{n,k}(x)}\,\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}P_{n,k}(x)}=\sqrt{\Phi(0)n^{2}}=n\sqrt{2+\frac{2}{n}}<\sqrt{3}\,n,

and hence for all
n\geq 2
,

(4.8)

b_{n}(x)\leq 2\sqrt{3}\,n,\qquad x\in[0,\infty).

(iii) Estimation of
c_{n}(x)
.
Similarly, by applying the Cauchy inequality and
Proposition
2.3
(b) with
\alpha=0
and
\alpha=1
:

\displaystyle c_{n}(x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big|T_{n,k}(x)\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\Big)\Big|P_{n,k}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}T_{n,k}^{2}(x)P_{n,k}(x)}\,\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Big(1-\frac{1}{n}\,T_{n,k}(x)\Big)^{2}P_{n,k}(x)}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\Phi(0)\,n^{2}}\,\sqrt{\Phi(1)}=n\sqrt{2+\frac{2}{n}}\,\sqrt{3+\frac{2}{n}}\,.

Then for all
n\geq 2
,

(4.9)

c_{n}(x)\leq 2\sqrt{3}\,n,\qquad x\in[0,\infty).

From (
4.7
)–(
4.9
) we obtain that for all
n\geq 17
,

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\big|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{P}_{n,k}(x)\big|\leq a_{n}(x)+b_{n}(x)+c_{n}(x)\leq\widetilde{C}\,n,\qquad x\in[0,\infty),

i.e.

\big\|\widetilde{D}\widetilde{V}_{n}f\big\|\leq\widetilde{C}n\|f\|,\qquad\widetilde{C}=6+4\sqrt{3}\,.

∎

Now we are ready to prove a strong converse inequality of Type B according to Ditzian-Ivanov
classification, following their approach in

]
.

Proof of Theorem
1.2
.

Let

,
n\geq 2
,

and
\lambda(n)
,

2.5
. We apply the Voronovskaya type inequality in Lemma

for the

. Then

\displaystyle\lambda(\ell)\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f\big\|

\displaystyle=\big\|\widetilde{V}_{\ell}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f-\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f+\lambda(\ell)\widetilde{D}^{2}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f-\widetilde{V}_{\ell}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f+\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f\big\|

4.2

\displaystyle\leq\widetilde{C}\,n\,\theta(\ell)\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{2}(f-\widetilde{V}_{n}f)\big\|+\widetilde{C}\,n\,\theta(\ell)\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f\big\|+8\big\|\widetilde{V}_{\ell}f-f\big\|.

Now, application of the Bernstein type inequality Lemma
4.2
twice for

\frac{1}{2\ell^{2}}\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f\big\|\leq\frac{4\widetilde{C}^{3}n^{3}}{9\ell^{3}}\big\|f-\widetilde{V}_{n}f\big\|+8\big\|\widetilde{V}_{\ell}-f\big\|+\frac{4\widetilde{C}n}{9\ell^{3}}\big\|\widetilde{D}^{2}\widetilde{V}_{n}^{3}f\big\|.

, for all integers
