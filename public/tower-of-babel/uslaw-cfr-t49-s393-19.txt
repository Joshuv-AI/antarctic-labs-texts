49 C.F.R. § 393.19 — Hazard warning signals.
Citation: us:cfr:49:393.19
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-393.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 393 (Parts And Accessories Necessary For Safe Operation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hazard warning signal operating unit on each commercial motor vehicle shall operate independently of the ignition or equivalent switch, and when activated, cause all turn signals required by § 393.11 to flash simultaneously.
