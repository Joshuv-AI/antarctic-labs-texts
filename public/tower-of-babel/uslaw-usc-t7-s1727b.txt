7 U.S.C. § 1727b — Grant programs
Citation: us:usc:7:1727b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1727b
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter III–A (Food For Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To carry out the policies and accomplish the objectives described in section 1691 of this title, the Administrator may negotiate and execute agreements with least developed countries to provide commodities to such countries on a grant basis either through the Commodity Credit Corporation or through private trade channels.
