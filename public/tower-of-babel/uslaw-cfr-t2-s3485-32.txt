2 C.F.R. § 3485.32 — What policies and procedures must I follow?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3485.32
Creator: Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3485.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3485 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department's policies and procedures that you must follow are the policies and procedures specified in this part and in Subparts A through I of 2 CFR part 180. The contracts that are covered transactions, for example, are specified in § 3485.220. Section 180.205 of this title does not require supplementation, so it is not included in the table of contents for this part and is not separately stated in this part.
