27 C.F.R. § 72.66 — Purchaser entitled to bill of sale.
Citation: us:cfr:27:72.66
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-72.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 72 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each purchaser of administratively forfeited property, including carriers, shall be entitled to receive a suitable and authentic bill of sale on a form to be provided for the purpose.
