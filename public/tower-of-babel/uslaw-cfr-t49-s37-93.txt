49 C.F.R. § 37.93 — One car per train rule.
Citation: us:cfr:49:37.93
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-37.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 37 (Transportation Services For Individuals With Disabilities (Ada)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The definition of accessible for purposes of meeting the one car per train rule is spelled out in the applicable subpart for each transportation system type in part 38 of this title.
(b) Each person providing intercity rail service and each commuter rail authority shall ensure that, as soon as practicable, but in no event later than July 26, 1995, that each train has one car that is readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities, including individuals who use wheelchairs.
(c) Each public entity providing light or rapid rail service shall ensure that each train, consisting of two or more vehicles, includes at least one car that is readily accessible to and usable by individuals with disabilities, including individuals who use wheelchairs, as soon as practicable but in no case later than July 25, 1995.
