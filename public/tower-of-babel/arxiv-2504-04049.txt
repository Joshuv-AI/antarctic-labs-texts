The Multiple Riordan Group and the Multiple Riordan Type Arrrays
Tian-Xiao He

Abstract
This is the first paper of a sequence papers on the multiple Riordan group and the multiple Riordon type arrays. We give a comprehensive discussion of the multiple Riordan arrays and characterize them by an A A -sequence and multiple Z Z -sequences. The multiple Riordan group and some of its subgroups are defined. In addition, we give compressions of multiple Riordan arrays and their sequence characterizations. The total positivity of the compressions of multiple Riordan arrays is studied. AMS Subject Classification: 05A15, 05A05, 11B39, 11B73, 15B36, 15A06, 05A19, 11B83. Key Words and Phrases: multiple Riordan arrays, the multiple Riordan group, generating function, sequence characterization, production matrix, compressions of multiple Riordan arrays, and total positivity.

3 Sequence characterization of Riordan type arrays

Definition 3.1
.

Let
(d_{n,k})_{n,k\in\mbox{\scriptsize${\mathbb{N}}$}}=(g,f)

g,f\in\mbox{$\mathcal{F}$}_{0}

It is clear that

d_{n,k}=\tilde{d}_{n+k,k}\quad\mbox{and}\quad\tilde{d}_{n,k}=d_{n-k,k}.

(9)

Hence, we have the following sequence characterization of Riordan type arrays.

Let

\left[a_{i-j}\right]_{i,j\geq 0}=\left[\begin{array}[]{lllll}a_{0}&&&&\\

(a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{n})

(a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{n},0,\ldots)

{{\mathbb{N}}}

\sum_{n\geq 0}a_{n}t^{n}=Ct^{k}e^{\gamma t}\frac{\Pi_{j\geq 0}(1+\alpha_{j}t)}{\Pi_{j\geq 0}(1-\beta_{j}t)},

\beta_{j}

\gamma\geq 0

\sum_{j\geq 0}(\alpha_{j}+\beta_{j})<\infty
