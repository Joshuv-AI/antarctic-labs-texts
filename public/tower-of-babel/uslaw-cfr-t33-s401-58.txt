33 C.F.R. § 401.58 — Pleasure craft scheduling.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.58
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At the U.S. locks, the transit of pleasure craft shall be scheduled by the traffic controller or the officer in charge of a lock and may be delayed in order to avoid interference with other vessels; and
(b) Every pleasure craft seeking to transit Canadian locks shall first make a reservation on the Seaway website according to the available schedule.
