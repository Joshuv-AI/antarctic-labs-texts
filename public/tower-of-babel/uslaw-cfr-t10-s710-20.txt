10 C.F.R. § 710.20 — Purpose of administrative review.
Citation: us:cfr:10:710.20
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-710.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 710 (Procedures For Determining Eligibility For Access To Classified Matter And Special Nuclear Material Or Eligibility To Hold A Sensitive Position). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These procedures govern the conduct of the administrative review of questions concerning an individual's eligibility for access authorization when it is determined that such questions cannot be favorably resolved by consultation or other action.
