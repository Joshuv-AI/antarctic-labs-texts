32 C.F.R. § 536.51 — Collateral source rule.
Citation: us:cfr:32:536.51
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-536.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 536 (Claims Against The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where permitted by applicable state or maritime law, damages recovered from collateral sources are payable under subparts D and H, but not under subparts C, E, F, or J of this part. For further discussion see DA Pam 27-162, paragraph 2-57.
