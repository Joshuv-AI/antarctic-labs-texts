43 U.S.C. § 1066 — Permission of Secretary to sue
Citation: us:usc:43:1066
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1066
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 25 (Unlawful Inclosures Or Occupancy; Obstructing Settlement Or Transit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where the alleged unlawful inclosure includes less than one hundred and sixty acres of land, no suit shall be brought under the provisions of this chapter without authority from the Secretary of the Interior.
