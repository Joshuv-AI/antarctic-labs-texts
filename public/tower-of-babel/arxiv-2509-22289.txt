Beyond the Euler–Mascheroni Constant: A Family of Functionals
Ken Nagai

Abstract
We introduce a family of regularized functionals g n ​ ( x ) g_{n}(x) that generalize the Euler–Mascheroni constant γ \gamma . They arise from a weighted regularization of Clausen-type trigonometric sums, and admit explicit integral representations, differential and ladder relations, together with an umbral generating function.

Core identities

Proposition 1

(Integral representation)
.

For
n\geq 1
and
x>0
,

g_{n}(x)=H_{n}-\log(2\pi x)-n\int_{0}^{1}(1-u)^{n-1}\,\log\!\bigl(2\sin(\pi xu)\bigr)\,du.

Corollary 1

(Small-
x
normalization)
.

For
n\geq 1
,

g_{n}(x)=O(x^{2})\hskip 18.49988pt(x\to 0^{+}).

Proposition 2

(Differential form)
.

For
n\geq 1
and
x>0
,

\displaystyle x\,g_{n}^{\prime}(x)

\displaystyle=2n!\sum_{m\geq 1}\frac{(2m)!}{(2m+n)!}\,\zeta(2m)\,x^{2m}-1

\displaystyle=-\,n\int_{0}^{1}(1-u)^{n-1}\,\pi xu\,\cot(\pi xu)\,du\;-\;1.

Remark 1

(Cotangent connection)
.

The derivative formula shows that
g_{n}
is governed by cotangent averages,
linking it with classical Bernoulli expansions of
\pi\cot(\pi z)
.

Proposition 3

(Ladder in
n
)
.

For
n\geq 1
and
x>0
,

g_{n+1}(x)-g_{n}(x)=\frac{1}{n+1}-\int_{0}^{1}\!\bigl[(n+1)(1-u)^{n}-n(1-u)^{n-1}\bigr]\,\log(2\sin(\pi xu))\,du.

Corollary 2

(Large-
n
decay)
.

For fixed
x>0
,

g_{n}(x)=O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right)\hskip 18.49988pt(n\to\infty).

3
Bernoulli connections

The cotangent expansion

\pi\cot(\pi z)=\frac{1}{z}+\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{m}\frac{2^{2m}B_{2m}}{(2m)!}\,(\pi z)^{2m-1}

implies that the integrands appearing in the differential form of
g_{n}(x)

are closely tied to Bernoulli numbers
B_{2m}
.
Indeed, expanding
\pi xu\cot(\pi xu)
and integrating termwise shows that
the coefficients in the defining series of
g_{n}(x)
match precisely the
Bernoulli–zeta relation

\zeta(2m)=(-1)^{m+1}\frac{(2\pi)^{2m}B_{2m}}{2(2m)!}.

Remark 2

(Bernoulli connection)
.

Thus
g_{n}(x)
interpolates between the Euler–Mascheroni constant
and higher Bernoulli data, reflecting the classical bridge
between Clausen sums and even zeta values.

Remark 3

(Clausen origin)
.

In the cosine–Clausen expansion one encounters

\operatorname{Cl}^{(c)}_{2m+1}(\theta)=\sum_{r=0}^{m-1}\zeta(2m+1-2r)\,\frac{(-1)^{r}\theta^{2r}}{(2r)!}-\frac{(-1)^{m}\theta^{2m}}{(2m)!}\,g_{2m}\!\Bigl(\tfrac{|\theta|}{2\pi}\Bigr)+\cdots,

where the divergent central contribution involving
\zeta(1)

is naturally replaced by
g_{2m}(|\theta|/2\pi)
.
This indicates that the family
g_{n}(x)
has its origin
in the same mechanism that produces the Euler–Mascheroni constant
in the simplest case
m=0
.

4
Umbral generating function

Proposition 4

(Generating function)
.

For
|z|<1
,

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 1}g_{n}(x)\,z^{n}

\displaystyle=-\,\frac{z}{1-z}\,\log(2\pi x)-\frac{\log(1-z)}{1-z}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\int_{0}^{1}\log\!\bigl(2\sin(\pi xu)\bigr)\,\frac{z}{\bigl(1-z(1-u)\bigr)^{2}}\,du.

Remark 4

(Umbral perspective)
.

The generating function realizes
g_{n}(x)

as coefficients of an operator-valued series
acting on
\log(2\sin(\pi xu))
.
This matches the umbral perspective

]
,
where moments are encoded by a single parent functional.

5
Conclusion

We close with a brief sketch of proof for the Main Theorem,
together with some outlook remarks.

Sketch of proof.

The defining moment representation of
g_{n}(x)

leads directly to the integral identity in Proposition 2.1.
Differentiating under the integral sign yields
the differential and ladder relations.
Finally, the umbral generating function provides a
unified operator perspective, from which the Main Theorem follows
by a straightforward synthesis.
∎

Beyond this, the umbral/Heisenberg perspective suggests
further connections with spectral theory and operator calculus.
In particular, the moment–functional interpretation points toward
an operator-theoretic framework in which
g_{n}(x)

appears naturally alongside Bernoulli-type structures.
We leave such perspectives for future investigation.
