10 C.F.R. § 1050.401 — Prohibition against use of appropriated funds.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1050.401
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1050.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1050 (Foreign Gifts And Decorations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No appropriated funds other than funds from the “Emergencies in the Diplomatic and Consular Service” account of the Department of State may be used to purchase any tangible gift of more than minimal value for any foreign individual unless such gift has been approved by the Congress.
