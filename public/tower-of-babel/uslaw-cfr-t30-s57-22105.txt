30 C.F.R. § 57.22105 — Smoking and open flames (IV mines).
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.22105
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-57.22105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 57 (Safety And Health Standards—Underground Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Smoking or open flames shall not be permitted in a face or raise, or during release of gas from a borehole until tests have been conducted in accordance with § 57.22226 and the methane level has been determined to be below 0.5 percent.
