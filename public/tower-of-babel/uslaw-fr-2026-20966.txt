    Title: Qualifying Advanced Energy Project Credit--Notice 2023-18.
    OMB Control Number: 1545-2151.
    Regulation Project Number: Notice 2023-18, Notice 2024-36.
    Abstract: IRS Notice 2023-18, as clarified and modified by 
subsequent guidance, establishes the Sec.  48C(e) Qualifying Advanced 
Energy Project Credit Allocation Program. The program authorizes the 
allocation of $10 billion in tax credits for qualified investments in 
eligible advanced energy projects, including at least $4 billion 
reserved for projects located in designated energy communities. 
Eligible projects generally include investments to establish, expand, 
or re-equip industrial or manufacturing facilities that produce or 
recycle clean energy technologies, reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 
at least 20 percent, or process, refine, or recycle critical materials. 
To participate in the Sec.  48C(e) program, taxpayers must submit 
project information to the Department of Energy (DOE) for technical 
review and recommendation and to the IRS for credit allocation and 
certification. Required submissions include a concept paper and Sec.  
48C(e) application, as applicable, as well as documentation 
demonstrating that an allocated project satisfies the program's 
certification requirements and notification when the project is placed 
in service. Notice 2023-18 has been clarified and modified by 
subsequent IRS guidance, including Notice 2024-36, which established 
procedures for the second allocation round and updated the program's 
technical and administrative requirements.
