51 U.S.C. § 51105 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:51:51105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section51105
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 511 (Space Transportation Infrastructure Matching Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not more than $10,000,000 may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation to make grants under this chapter. Amounts appropriated under this section remain available until expended.
