Correction: Genome-wide identification and expression profiling of the MADS-box gene family in Lavandula angustifolia
Authors: Yongguang Li, Jishan Xiang, Xiaoxing Wang, Jiandong Ren, Dafeng Liu, Dingguo Zhang, Danli Guo, Bin Ma
Published: 2025

Correction: BMC Genomics 26, 911 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-025-12120-9

Following publication of the original article, it was reported that there were errors in the captions of Figs. 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6.

The incorrect and corrected captions are given below, and the original article has been corrected.

Corrected captions.

Figure 1 Phylogenetic tree of type I MADS-box genes in A. thaliana, O. sativa, and L. angustifolia. The neighbor-joining tree was constructed using MEGA 7.0 with bootstrap analysis of 1000 replicates. Bootstrap support values are shown at nodes (only values ≥ 50% are displayed). Green, red, and blue circles represent MADS-box genes from A. thaliana, O. sativa, and L. angustifolia, respectively. Subgroups are marked by different-colored lines on the tree.

Figure 2 Phylogenetic tree of type II MADS-box genes in A. thaliana, O. sativa and L. angustifolia. The neighbor-joining tree was constructed using MEGA 7.0 with bootstrap analysis of 1000 replicates. Bootstrap support values are shown at nodes (only values ≥ 50% are displayed). Green, red, and blue circles represent MADS-box genes from A. thaliana, O. sativa, and L. angustifolia, respectively. Subgroups are marked by different-colored lines on the tree.

Figure 3 Phylogenetic relationships, protein motifs, and gene structure in MADS-box genes from L. angustifolia. a Phylogenetic analysis of 173 MADS-box genes from L. angustifolia. b Distribution characteristics of the conserved motifs of MADS-box proteins. c Exon-intron distribution of MADS-box genes.

Figure 5 Analysis of cis-acting elements of MADS-box gene family in L. angustifolia. The numbers in the heatmap indicate the counts of the corresponding cis-regulatory elements.

Figure 6 The expression patterns of L. angustifolia type II MADS-box at different tissues. a The RNA-seq results of type II MADS-box expression at different tissues. The genes analyzed by qPCR are shown in red font. b The qPCR results of twelve MADS-box genes. Different lowercase letters in the figure indicate statistically significant differences (P < 0.05).
