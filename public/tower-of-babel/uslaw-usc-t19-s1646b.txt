19 U.S.C. § 1646b — Random customs inspections for stolen automobiles being exported
Citation: us:usc:19:1646b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1646b
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of U.S. Customs and Border Protection shall direct customs officers to conduct at random inspections of automobiles, and of shipping containers that may contain automobiles that are being exported, for purposes of determining whether such automobiles were stolen.
