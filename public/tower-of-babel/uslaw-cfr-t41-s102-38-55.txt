41 C.F.R. § 102-38.55 — -38.55 Fixed priced sales to State agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.55
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before offering to the public, you may offer the property at fixed prices through the State Agency for Surplus Property (SASP) to any States, territories, possessions, political subdivisions, or tax-supported agencies, which have expressed an interest in obtaining the property.
