7 C.F.R. § 780.12 — Appeals of penalties assessed under the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978.
Citation: us:cfr:7:780.12
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-780.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 780 (Appeal Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requests for appeals of penalties assessed under the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978 must be addressed to: Administrator, Farm Service Agency, Stop 0572, 1400 Independence Avenue, SW., Washington, DC 20250-0572.
(b) Decisions in appeals under this section are not subject to reconsideration and are administratively final.
