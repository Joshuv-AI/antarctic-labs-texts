15 U.S.C. § 315 — Changes or assignment to duty
Citation: us:usc:15:315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section315
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 9 (National Weather Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce is authorized to make such changes or assignment to duty in the personnel or detailed force of the National Weather Service for limiting or reducing expenses as he may deem necessary.
