12 C.F.R. § 335.201 — Securities exempted from registration.
Citation: us:cfr:12:335.201
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-335.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 335 (Securities Of State Nonmember Banks And State Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons subject to registration requirements under Exchange Act section 12 and subject to this part shall follow the applicable and currently effective SEC regulations relative to exemptions from registration issued under sections 3 and 12 of the Exchange Act as codified at 17 CFR part 240.
