40 U.S.C. § 17103 — Commercial standards for passenger safety devices
Citation: us:usc:40:17103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section17103
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 171 (Safety Standards For Motor Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services shall prescribe and publish in the Federal Register commercial standards for passenger safety devices the Administrator requires under section 17102 of this title. Changes in the standards take effect one year and 90 days after the publication of the standards in the Federal Register.
