46 C.F.R. § 9.4 — Waiting time; actual report for duties.
Citation: us:cfr:46:9.4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-9.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 9 (Extra Compensation For Overtime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Extra compensation for waiting time will not be allowed unless and until an officer or employee actually reports for duty.
