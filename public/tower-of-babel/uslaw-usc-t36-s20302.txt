36 U.S.C. § 20302 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:36:20302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section20302
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 203 (American Academy Of Arts And Letters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of the corporation is to further the interests of literature and the fine arts.
