14 C.F.R. § 45.16 — Marking of life-limited parts.
Citation: us:cfr:14:45.16
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-45.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 45 (Identification And Registration Marking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When requested by a person required to comply with § 43.10 of this chapter, the holder of a type certificate or design approval for a life-limited part must provide marking instructions, or must state that the part cannot be practicably marked without compromising its integrity.
