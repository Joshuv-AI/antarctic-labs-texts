12 C.F.R. § 1015.8 — Waiver not permitted.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1015.8
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1015.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1015 (Mortgage Assistance Relief Services (Regulation O)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is a violation of this rule for any person to obtain, or attempt to obtain, a waiver from any consumer of any protection provided by or any right of the consumer under this rule.
