18 C.F.R. § 385.914 — Commission action (Rule 914).
Citation: us:cfr:18:385.914
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-385.914
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 385 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will upon consideration of the entire record, issue a final order affirming, modifying, or vacating the contested order or directing other appropriate relief. The Commission will serve the final order on the participants.
