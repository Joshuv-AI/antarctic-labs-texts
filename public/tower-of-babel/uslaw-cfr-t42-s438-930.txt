42 C.F.R. § 438.930 — Compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:42:438.930
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-438.930
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 438 (Managed Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In general, contracts with MCOs, PIHPs, and PAHPs offering Medicaid State plan services to enrollees, and those entities, must comply with the requirements of this subpart no later than October 2, 2017.
