24 C.F.R. § 965.306 — Energy conservation equipment and practices.
Citation: us:cfr:24:965.306
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-965.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 965 (Pha-Owned Or Leased Projects—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In purchasing original or, when needed, replacement equipment, PHAs shall acquire only equipment that meets or exceeds the minimum efficiency requirements established by the U.S. Department of Energy. In the operation of their facilities, PHAs shall follow operating practices directed to maximum energy conservation.
