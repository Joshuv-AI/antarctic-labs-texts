47 C.F.R. § 97.403 — Safety of life and protection of property.
Citation: us:cfr:47:97.403
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-97.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 97 (Amateur Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision of these rules prevents the use by an amateur station of any means of radiocommunication at its disposal to provide essential communication needs in connection with the immediate safety of human life and immediate protection of property when normal communication systems are not available.
