15 U.S.C. § 278o — User fees
Citation: us:usc:15:278o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section278o
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 7 (National Institute Of Standards And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Institute shall not implement a policy of charging fees with respect to the use of Institute research facilities by research associates in the absence of express statutory authority to charge such fees.
