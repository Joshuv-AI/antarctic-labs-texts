5 C.F.R. § 2610.109 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2610.109
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2610.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2610 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over another agency of the United States that participates in a proceeding before the Office of Government Ethics and takes a position that is not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
