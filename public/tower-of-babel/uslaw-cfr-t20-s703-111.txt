20 C.F.R. § 703.111 — Submission of new forms of policies for approval; other endorsements.
Citation: us:cfr:20:703.111
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-703.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 703 (Insurance Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No new forms of policies or modification of existing forms of policies shall be used by an insurer authorized by the Office under the regulations in this part to write insurance under said Act except after submission to and approval by the Office. No endorsement altering any provisions of a policy approved by the Office shall be used except after submission to and approval by the Office.
