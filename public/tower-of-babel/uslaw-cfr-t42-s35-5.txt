42 C.F.R. § 35.5 — Entitlement to care after discharge or transfer by reason of noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:42:35.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-35.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 35 (Hospital And Station Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person otherwise entitled to care, treatment, or hospitalization at Service facilities, or in other facilities at the expense of the Service, shall be denied such care or treatment by reason of his prior discharge or transfer from any such facility under the provisions of § 35.4.
