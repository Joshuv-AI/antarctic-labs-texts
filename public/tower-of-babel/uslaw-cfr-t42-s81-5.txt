42 C.F.R. § 81.5 — Use of personal and medical information.
Citation: us:cfr:42:81.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-81.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 81 (Guidelines For Determining Probability Of Causation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Determining probability of causation may require the use of the following personal and medical information provided to DOL by claimants under DOL regulations 20 CFR part 30:
(a) Year of birth.
(b) Cancer diagnosis (by ICD-10-CM code) for primary and secondary cancers.
(c) Date of cancer diagnosis.
(d) Gender.
(e) Race/ethnicity (if the claim is for skin cancer or a secondary cancer for which skin cancer is a likely primary cancer).
(f) Smoking history (if the claim is for lung cancer or a secondary cancer for which lung cancer is a likely primary cancer).
