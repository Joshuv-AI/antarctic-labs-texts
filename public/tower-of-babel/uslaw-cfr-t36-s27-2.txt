36 C.F.R. § 27.2 — Commercial and industrial activities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:27.2
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-27.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 27 (Cape Cod National Seashore; Zoning Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No commercial or industrial districts may be established within the Cape Cod National Seashore.
