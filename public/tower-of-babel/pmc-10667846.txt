Organometal Halide Perovskite‐Based Photoelectrochemical Module Systems for Scalable Unassisted Solar Water Splitting (Adv. Sci. 33/2023)
Authors: Hojoong Choi, Sehun Seo, Chang Jae Yoon, Jae‐Bin Ahn, Chan‐Sol Kim, Yoonsung Jung, Yejoon Kim, Francesca M. Toma, Heejoo Kim, Sanghan Lee
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
Photoelectrochemical Water Splitting In article number 2303106, Heejoo Kim, Sanghan Lee, and co‐workers report organometal halide perovskite (OHP)‐based photoelectrochemical (PEC) module systems for scalable unassisted solar water splitting. The scalability of OHP‐based PEC systems is significantly limited by resistive losses and intrinsic defects in devices. In this respect, the OHP‐based PEC module systems overcome scalability issues with a simple but effective design, providing a promising strategy for their future practical application and commercialization.
