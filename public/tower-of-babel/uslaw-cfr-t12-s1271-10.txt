12 C.F.R. § 1271.10 — Transfer of funds between Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1271.10
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1271.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1271 (Miscellaneous Federal Home Loan Bank Operations And Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inter-Bank borrowing shall be through unsecured deposits bearing interest at rates negotiated between Banks.
