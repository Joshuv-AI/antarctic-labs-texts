28 C.F.R. § 5.305 — Exemption under section 3(f) of the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:28:5.305
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-5.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 5 (Administration And Enforcement Of Foreign Agents Registration Act Of 1938, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exemption provided by section 3(f) of the Act shall not be available unless the President has, by publication in the Federal Register, designated for the purpose of this section the country the defense of which he deems vital to the defense of the United States.
