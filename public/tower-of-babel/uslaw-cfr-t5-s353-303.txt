5 C.F.R. § 353.303 — Restoration rights of TAPER employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:353.303
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-353.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 353 (Restoration To Duty From Uniformed Service Or Compensable Injury). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee serving in the competitive service under a temporary appointment pending establishment of a register (TAPER) under § 316.201 of this chapter (other than an employee serving in a position classified above GS-15), is entitled to be restored to the position he or she left or an equivalent one in the same commuting area.
