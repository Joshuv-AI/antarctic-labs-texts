46 C.F.R. § 98.33-11 — -11 Smoking.
Citation: us:cfr:46:98.33-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-98.33-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 98 (Special Construction, Arrangement, And Other Provisions For Certain Dangerous Cargoes In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may smoke when—
(a) Within 50 feet of a portable tank or IBC containing a combustible liquid; and
(b) On the deck where the tank or IBC is stowed.
