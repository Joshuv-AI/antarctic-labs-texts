54 U.S.C. § 102502 — Definition of museum object
Citation: us:usc:54:102502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section102502
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1025 (Museums). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) In general.—
The term “museum object” means an object that—
(A)
typically is movable; and
(B)
is eligible to be, or is made part of, a museum, library, or archive collection through a formal procedure, such as accessioning.
(2) Inclusions.—
The term “museum object” includes a prehistoric or historic artifact, work of art, book, document, photograph, or natural history specimen.
