16 U.S.C. § 544a — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:16:544a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section544a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter II (Scenic Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of sections 544 to 544p of this title are—
(1)
to establish a national scenic area to protect and provide for the enhancement of the scenic, cultural, recreational, and natural resources of the Columbia River Gorge; and
(2)
to protect and support the economy of the Columbia River Gorge area by encouraging growth to occur in existing urban areas and by allowing future economic development in a manner that is consistent with paragraph (1).
