48 C.F.R. § 509.306 — 509.306 Solicitation requirements.

Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-509.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 509 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The clauses at FAR 52.209-3 and 52.209-4 do not cover all the solicitation requirements described in FAR 9.306. If a solicitation contains a testing and approval requirement, the contracting officer must address the requirements in FAR 9.306(d) and (f) through (j) in the solicitation's Section H, special contract requirements.
