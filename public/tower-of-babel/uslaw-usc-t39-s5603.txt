39 U.S.C. § 5603 — Transportation of mail by vessel as freight or express
Citation: us:usc:39:5603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section5603
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part V (Transportation Of Mail); Chapter 56 (Transportation Of Mail By Vessel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may require that mail be transported by freight or express when—
(1)
there is no competition on a water route and the rate or compensation asked is excessive; or
(2)
no proposal is received.
A common carrier by water that fails or refuses to transport the mail when required to do so under this section shall be fined not more than $500 for each day of refusal.
