Surfaces with quadratic support function of harmonic type
Armando M. V. Corro, Carlos M. C. Riveros, Jose L. Teruel Carretero

Abstract
. In this paper, we study oriented surfaces S S in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} , called Surfaces with quadratic support function of harmonic type (in short HQSF-surfaces), these surfaces generalize the QSF-surfaces. We obtain a Weierstrass type representation for the HQSF-surfaces which depends on three holomorphic functions. Moreover, classify the HQSF-surfaces of rotation.

1.
Introduction

Let
S\subset\mathbb{R}^{3}
be a surface oriented by its normal Gauss map
N
. The functions
\Psi,\Lambda:S\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{3}
given by
\Psi(p)=\langle p,N(p)\rangle,\,\Lambda(p)=\langle p,p\rangle
,
p\in S
, where
\langle,\rangle
denotes the Euclidean scalar product in
\mathbb{R}^{3}
, are called
support function
and
quadratic distance function
, respectively.

Appell in
[
2
]
, studied a class of oriented surfaces in
\mathbb{R}^{3}
associated with area preserving transformations in the sphere.
In
[
8
]
, the authors showed that these surfaces are such that the mean curvature
H
, the Gaussian curvature
K
and the support function
\Psi
satisfy
H+\Psi K=0.
Tzitzéica in
[
17
]
estudied oriented hyperbolic surfaces such that there exist a nonzero constant
c\in\mathbb{R}
for which the following relation is satisfied
K+c^{2}\Psi^{4}=0
.

A surface
S\subset\mathbb{R}^{3}
is a
Laguerre minimal surface
if

\bigtriangleup_{III}\left(\frac{H}{K}\right)=0,

where
H
is the mean curvature,
K
is the Gaussian curvature and
III
is the third fundamental form of
S
. Laguerre minimal surfaces have been studied by several authors, for example see
[
1
]
,
[
10
]
,
[
11
]
,
[
12
]
,
[
15
]
,
[
16
]
and
[
18
]
.

In
[
14
]
, the authors classify the Laguerre minimal surfaces with planar curvature lines. In addition, they define the spherical type hypersurfaces, as one of the generalizations of the Laguerre minimal surfaces for larger dimensions.

In
[
7
]
, the authors motivated by the works
[
2
]
and
[
17
]
they introduce the class of oriented surfaces
S
in
\mathbb{R}^{3}

satisfying a relation of the form
A(p)+B(p)H(p)+C(p)K(p)=0,p\in S,
where
A,B,C:S\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
are differentiable
functions depending on the support function and the quadratic distance function. These surfaces are called surfaces of generalized Weingarten surfaces depending on the support and distance functions (in short, DSGW-surfaces). The linear Weingarten surfaces, Appell’s surfaces and Tzitzeica’s surfaces are all DSGW-surfaces. In this context, the authors study a special class of oriented surfaces
S\subset\mathbb{R}^{3}
that satisfy a relation of the form
2\Psi H+\Lambda K=0
, this surfaces are called
EDSW-surfaces
. They show that these surfaces are invariant by dilations and inversions. Moreover, they obtain a Weierstrass type representation depending on two holomorphic functions.

A generalization of the DSGW-surfaces is to consider the functions
A,B,C:S\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
depending on the support function, the quadratic distance function and harmonic functions with respect to the third quadratic form, these surfaces are called of generalized Weingarten surfaces depending on the support and distance functions of harmonic type (in short DSGWH-surfaces). The Laguerre minimal surfaces is a class of the DSGWH-surfaces, since
H
and
K
satify

H-\mu K=0,

where
\mu
is a harmonic function with respect to the third quadratic form.

In
[
9
]
, the authors introduce the class of surfaces in Euclidean space motivated by a problem posed by Élie Cartan. This class of
surfaces are called
Ribaucour surfaces
and are defined as surfaces where all the medial spheres intercept a fixed sphere along a large circle, these surfaces satisfy a relation of the form

2\Psi H+(1+\Lambda)K=0.

The authors obtain holomorphic data for these surfaces and discuss the relation with minimal surfaces.

In
[
5
]
, the authors study the Ribaucour surfaces of harmonic type (in short HR-surfaces), these surfaces satisfy

2\Psi H+(ce^{2\mu}+\Lambda)K=0,

where
c
is a nonzero real constant,
\mu
a harmonic function with respect to the third fundamental form. These surfaces generalize the Ribaucour surfaces studied in
[
9
]
.

Corro and Mendez in
[
6
]
, study the Ribaucour-type surfaces (in short RT-surfaces) what satisfy

2\Psi H+(\Lambda+\Psi^{2})K=0.

In
[
4
]
, the authors study the surfaces with quadratic support function (in short QSF-surfaces), these surfaces satisfy

2\Psi H+(\Lambda-\Psi^{2})K=0.

They obtain a Weierstrass type representation for the QSF-surfaces which depends on two holomorphic functions.

In this paper, we study the surfaces with quadratic support function of harmonic type (in short HQSF-surfaces), these surfaces satisfy

2\Psi H+(c\Psi e^{2\mu}+\Lambda-\Psi^{2})K=0,

where
\mu
is a harmonic function with respect to the third fundamental form.

This class of surfaces generalize the QSF-surfaces studied in
[
4
]
. We obtain a Weierstrass type representation for the HQSF-surfaces which depends on three holomorphic functions. Moreover, classify the HQSF-surfaces of rotation.

2.
Preliminaries

In this section we present the definitions and results that will be used in the work. In this paper the inner
produt
\langle,\rangle:\mathbb{C}\times\mathbb{C}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is defined by

\langle f,g\rangle=f_{1}g_{1}+f_{2}g_{2},\,\,\mbox{where}\,\,f=f_{1}+if_{2},\,\,g=g_{1}+ig_{2},

are holomorphic functions.

In the computation we use the following properties: if
f,g:\mathbb{C}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}

are holomorphic functions of
z=u_{1}+iu_{2}
, then

\langle f,g\rangle_{,1}=\langle f^{\prime},g\rangle+\langle f,g^{\prime}\rangle,\quad\langle f,g\rangle_{,2}=\langle if^{\prime},g\rangle+\langle f,ig^{\prime}\rangle,

(2.1)

\langle f,g\rangle=\langle 1,\bar{f}g\rangle,\quad\langle 1,f\rangle^{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left[|f|^{2}+\langle 1,f^{2}\rangle\right]

Here
\langle f,g\rangle_{,i}
denotes the derivative of
\langle f,g\rangle
with respect to
u_{i},\;i=1,2
.

The following lemma was obtained in
[
5
]

Lemma 1
.

If
f_{1},f_{2},g_{1},g_{2}:\mathbb{C}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}

\,\langle f_{1},g_{1}\rangle+\langle f_{2},g_{2}\rangle=0,

(2.2)

\,\,g_{1}=ic_{1}f_{1}-\overline{z}_{1}f_{2},\,\,g_{2}=z_{1}f_{1}+ic_{2}f_{2},

3.
Surfaces with quadratic support function of harmonic type

Definition 1
.

We say that an oriented surface
S\subset\mathbb{R}^{3}
is a Surface with quadratic support function of harmonic type (in short, HQSF-surface) if the mean curvature
H
, the Gaussian curvature
K
, the support function
\Psi
and quadratic distance
\Lambda
satisfy

(3.1)

2\Psi H+(c\Psi e^{2\mu}+\Lambda-\Psi^{2})K=0.

The following Theorem provides a Weierstrass type representation for HQSF-surfaces which depends on three holomorphic functions.

Theorem 2
.

Let
S\subset\mathbb{R}^{3}
be a connected orientable Riemann surface. Then
S
is a HQSF-surface if,
and only if, there exist holomorphic functions
g,f_{1},f_{2}:S\rightarrow\mathbb{C}_{\infty}
, with
|f_{1}f^{\prime}_{2}-f_{2}f^{\prime}_{1}|\neq 0
such that
X(S)
is locally parametrized by

(3.2)

\displaystyle X(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2(|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2})}{T^{2}}\left(2T\left(f_{1}\overline{\left(\frac{f_{1}^{\prime}}{g^{\prime}}\right)}+cf_{2}\overline{\left(\frac{f_{2}^{\prime}}{g^{\prime}}\right)}\right)-g\left(|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2}\right)\right),0\right)

\displaystyle-\frac{2R}{T}(g,1),

where

(3.3)

R=\frac{(|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2})}{T^{2}}\left(4T\left\langle 1,\frac{(\overline{f_{1}}f_{1}^{\prime}+c\overline{f_{2}}f_{2}^{\prime})g}{g^{\prime}}\right\rangle-(3|g|^{2}+1)(|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2})\right),

c\in\mathbb{R},|g^{\prime}|\neq 0,\,T=1+|g|^{2}
.

Proof.

From Theorem 1, we have that

(3.4)

\frac{H}{K}=-\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{T\Delta h}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}-4R\right),

(3.5)

\Psi=\frac{2h}{T},\,\,\Lambda=\frac{|\nabla h|^{2}}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}-4R\frac{h}{T},\,L_{11}=\frac{4|g^{\prime}|^{2}}{T^{2}},\,T=1+|g|^{2}.

Using (
3.5
) in (
3.4
) we obtain

(3.6)

\frac{H}{K}=-\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{T\Delta h}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}+\frac{T}{h}\left(\Lambda-\frac{|\nabla h|^{2}}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}\right)\right)=-\frac{1}{2\Psi}\left(\frac{h\Delta h-|\nabla h|^{2}}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}+\Lambda\right).

Now, let
h=\displaystyle\frac{A^{2}}{T}
where
A
is a differentiable function.

We can show that

(3.7)

h\Delta h-|\nabla h|^{2}=\frac{2A^{2}}{T^{2}}\left(A\Delta A-|\nabla A|^{2}\right)-\frac{A^{4}}{T^{4}}\left(T\Delta T-|\nabla T|^{2}\right).

Considering (
3.5
) we obtain

(3.8)

T\Delta T-|\nabla T|^{2}=4|g^{\prime}|^{2},\,\,\Psi=\frac{2A^{2}}{T^{2}}.

Using (
3.7
) and (
3.8
) in (
3.6
)

(3.9)

\frac{H}{K}=-\frac{1}{2\Psi}\left(\Psi\left(\frac{A\Delta A-|\nabla A|^{2}}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}\right)+\Lambda-\Psi^{2}\right).

From (
3.1
) and (
3.9
)
X
is a HQSF-surface if and only if

(3.10)

\frac{A\Delta A-|\nabla A|^{2}}{|g^{\prime}|^{2}}=ce^{2\mu}.

Now, considering
A=|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2}
, where
f_{1}
and
f_{2}
are holomorphic functions, we obtain

(3.11)

A\Delta A-|\nabla A|^{2}=4c|f_{1}f^{\prime}_{2}-f_{2}f^{\prime}_{1}|^{2}.

From (
3.10
) and (
3.11
) it follows

(3.12)

\mu=\ln\left(\frac{2|f_{1}f^{\prime}_{2}-f_{2}f^{\prime}_{1}|}{|g^{\prime}|}\right).

Therefore, the function
h
is given by

(3.13)

h=\frac{(|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2})^{2}}{1+|g|^{2}}.

Using (
3.13
) we obtain

(3.14)

\nabla h=2h\left(\frac{2(f_{1}\overline{f_{1}^{\prime}}+cf_{2}\overline{f_{2}^{\prime}})}{|f_{1}|^{2}+c|f_{2}|^{2}}-\frac{g\overline{g^{\prime}}}{1+|g|^{2}}\right).

Using (
3.14
) in (
2.4
) we obtain (
3.2
). The proof is complete.
