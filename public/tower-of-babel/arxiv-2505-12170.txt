Extending Pólya’s random walker beyond probability I. Complex weights
M. Klazar (UK, Praha), R. Horský (VŠE, Praha)

Abstract
Working in combinatorial model W co ​ ( d ) \mathrm{W_{co}}(d) , d = 1 , 2 , … d=1,2,\dots , of Pólya’s random walker in ℤ d \mathbb{Z}^{d} , we prove two theorems on recurrence to a vertex. We obtain an effective version of the first theorem if d = 2 d=2 . Using a semi-formal approach to generating functions, we extend both theorems beyond probability to a more general model W ℂ \mathrm{W_{\mathbb{C}}} with complex weights. We relate models W co ​ ( d ) \mathrm{W_{co}}(d) to standard models W Ma ​ ( d ) \mathrm{W_{Ma}}(d) based on Markov chains. The follow-up article will treat non-Archimedean models W fo ​ ( k ) \mathrm{W_{fo}}(k) in which weights are formal power series in ℂ ⁡ [ [ x 1 , x 2 , … , x k ] ] \mathbb{C}[[x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{k}]] .

\displaystyle\mathrm{Pr}(X_{i+1}=\overline{u}_{i+1}\,|\,X_{0}=\overline{u}_{0}\wedge\dots\wedge X_{i}=\overline{u}_{i})\cdot\mathrm{Pr}(X_{0}=\overline{u}_{0}\wedge\dots\wedge X_{i}=\overline{u}_{i})

\displaystyle=\mathrm{Pr}(X_{i+1}=\overline{u}_{i+1}\,|\,X_{i}=\overline{u}_{i})\cdot(2d)^{-i}

\displaystyle=(2d)^{-1}\cdot(2d)^{-i}=(2d)^{-i-1}\;.

In the first line we use the formula for the conditional
probability and the inductive assumption that

\mathrm{Pr}(X_{0}=\overline{u}_{0}\wedge X_{1}=\overline{u}_{1}\wedge\dots\wedge X_{i}=\overline{u}_{i})=(2d)^{-i}>0\,.

We use this assumption also in the second line, together with part 1 of Definition

In the third line we use part 3 of
Definition
1.6
and the fact that

.

Let

be in

If

. By the monotonicity of probability,

If

. For

\displaystyle\mathrm{Pr}(X_{0}=\overline{u}_{0}\wedge\dots\wedge X_{j+1}=\overline{u}_{j+1})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Pr}(X_{j+1}=\overline{u}_{j+1}\,|\,C)\cdot\mathrm{Pr}(C)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Pr}(X_{j+1}=\overline{u}_{j+1}\,|\,X_{j}=\overline{u}_{j})\cdot(2d)^{-j}

\displaystyle 0\cdot(2d)^{-j}=0

In the first line we use the formula for the conditional probability and the fact proved in the first case
that

we use this fact too, and part 1 of

\Box

Lemma 5.3

Let

{\textstyle\big|s-\sum_{x\in Z}h(x)\big|\leq\varepsilon\,.}

Proof.

A_{0,h}(1)=C_{0,h}(1)D_{h}(1)
and the stated formula follows.

3. Suppose that
h
is nonnegative,
D_{h}(1)=+\infty
and
v\in V(h)
.
The shortest walk of length
m
joining
1
and
v
in
G(h)
shows that
(c_{m}^{h})^{\prime}>0
. Since by part 1 of
Proposition
5.20
we have
(a_{n}^{h})^{\prime}=\sum_{j=0}^{n}(c_{j}^{h})^{\prime}d_{n-j}^{h}
for every
n\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, the stated inequality
follows. Hence

.

\Box

The fourth and last main result in this section is
the second extension of Theorem

on

-recurrence to

Theorem 5.22

Suppose that

2.

(In this case

6
Concluding remarks

Since this paper is already long, we decided to move several concrete
examples in the follow-up paper
[
16
]
. There we obtain
analogues of the four main theorems in the previous section for weights

h\colon\mathbb{N}_{2}\to\mathbb{C}[[x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{k}]]\,.

The algebras
\mathbb{C}[[x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{k}]]
of formal power series are endowed
with non-Archimedean norms.
Non-Archimedean infinite series are simpler than the complex ones we
dealt with above.
Weighting objects with polynomials and formal power series is a basic
technique in enumerative combinatorics.
