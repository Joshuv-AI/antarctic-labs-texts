14 C.F.R. § 23.2230 — Limit and ultimate loads.
Citation: us:cfr:14:23.2230
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-23.2230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 23 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must determine—
(a) The limit loads, which are equal to the structural design loads unless otherwise specified elsewhere in this part; and
(b) The ultimate loads, which are equal to the limit loads multiplied by a 1.5 factor of safety unless otherwise specified elsewhere in this part.
