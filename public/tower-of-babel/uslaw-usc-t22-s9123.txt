22 U.S.C. § 9123 — Consultations with foreign governments
Citation: us:usc:22:9123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9123
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 98 (International Child Abduction Prevention And Return); Subchapter II (Actions By The Secretary Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after the Secretary of State makes a determination under section 9121 of this title in response to a failure to resolve unresolved abduction cases or the Secretary takes an action under subsection (d) or (e) of section 9122 of this title, based on a pattern of noncompliance, the Secretary shall request consultations with the government of such country regarding the situation giving rise to such determination.
