48 U.S.C. § 1405 — Geographical application of subchapter; land and waters included in term “Virgin Islands”
Citation: us:usc:48:1405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1405
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 7 (Virgin Islands); Subchapter II (Civil Government). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this subchapter, and the name “the Virgin Islands” as used in this subchapter, shall apply to and include the territorial domain, lands and waters acquired by the United States through cession of the Danish West Indian Islands by the convention between the United States of America and His Majesty the King of Denmark entered into August 4, 1916, and ratified by the Senate on September 7, 1916 (39 Stat. L. 1706).
