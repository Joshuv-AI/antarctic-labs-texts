10 C.F.R. § 9.62 — Records under control of another Government agency.
Citation: us:cfr:10:9.62
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-9.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 9 (Public Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests received by NRC pertaining to records under the control of another Government agency will be returned to the requester with the name of the controlling Government agency, if known, within ten working days after receipt by the NRC.
