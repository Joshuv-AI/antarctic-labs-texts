49 C.F.R. § 1103.24 — Use of adverse witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1103.24
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1103.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1103 (Practitioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A practitioner shall not be deterred from seeking information from a witness connected with or reputed to be biased in favor of an adverse party, if the ascertainment of the truth requires that such a person be called as a witness in a proceeding.
