43 C.F.R. § 2611.1-5 — -5 Priority of Carey Act applications.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2611.1-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2611.1-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2610 (Carey Act Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Properly filed applications under § 2611.1-1 or § 2611.1-3 of this title shall have priority over any subsequently filed agricultural applications for lands within the project boundaries. However, the rejection of a Carey Act application will not preclude subsequent agricultural development under another authority.
