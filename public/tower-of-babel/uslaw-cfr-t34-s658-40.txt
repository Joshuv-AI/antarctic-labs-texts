34 C.F.R. § 658.40 — What are the limitations on allowable costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:658.40
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-658.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 658 (Undergraduate International Studies And Foreign Language Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Equipment costs may not exceed five percent of the grant amount; and
(b) No more than ten percent of the total amount of grant funds awarded to a grantee under this part may be used for the activity described in § 658.11(j).
