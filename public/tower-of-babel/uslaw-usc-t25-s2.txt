25 U.S.C. § 2 — Duties of Commissioner
Citation: us:usc:25:2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of Indian Affairs shall, under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior, and agreeably to such regulations as the President may prescribe, have the management of all Indian affairs and of all matters arising out of Indian relations.
