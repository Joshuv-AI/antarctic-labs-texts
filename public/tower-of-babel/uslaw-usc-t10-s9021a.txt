10 U.S.C. § 9021a — Air Force Reserve Forces Policy Committee
Citation: us:usc:10:9021a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9021a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 903 (Department Of The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is in the Office of the Secretary of the Air Force an Air Force Reserve Forces Policy Committee. The functions, membership, and organization of that committee are set forth in section 10305 of this title.
