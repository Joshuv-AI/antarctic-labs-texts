49 C.F.R. § 588.6 — Record retention.
Citation: us:cfr:49:588.6
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-588.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 588 (Child Restraint Systems Recordkeeping Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer, or manufacturer's designee, shall maintain the information specified in § 588.5 of this part for a registered restraint system for a period of not less than six years from the date of manufacture of that restraint system.
