28 C.F.R. § 513.67 — Fees for Freedom of Information Act requests.
Citation: us:cfr:28:513.67
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-513.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 513 (Access To Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fees for copies of records disclosed under the FOIA, including fees for a requester's own records, may be charged in accordance with Department of Justice regulations contained in 28 CFR 16.10.
