10 C.F.R. § 590.305 — Informal discovery.
Citation: us:cfr:10:590.305
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-590.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 590 (Administrative Procedures With Respect To The Import And Export Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The parties to a proceeding may conduct discovery through use of procedures such as written interrogatories or production of documents. In response to a motion by a party, the Assistant Secretary or presiding official may determine the procedures to be utilized for discovery if the parties cannot agree on such procedures.
