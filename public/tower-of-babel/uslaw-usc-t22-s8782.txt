22 U.S.C. § 8782 — Applicability to certain intelligence activities
Citation: us:usc:22:8782
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8782
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter VI (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act or the amendments made by this Act shall apply to the authorized intelligence activities of the United States.
