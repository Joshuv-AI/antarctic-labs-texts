5 C.F.R. § 880.205 — Determinations of death.
Citation: us:cfr:5:880.205
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-880.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 880 (Retirement And Insurance Benefits During Periods Of Unexplained Absence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM does not make findings of presumed death. A claimant for CSRS, FERS, or FEGLI death benefits (other than payments under § 880.302) or an individual seeking an adjustment of accounts under § 880.207 must submit a death certificate or other legal certification of death issued by an authorized institution.
