48 C.F.R. § 2928.203 — 2928.203 Individual sureties.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2928 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers must refer evidence of possible criminal or fraudulent activities by an individual surety to the Office of Inspector General.
