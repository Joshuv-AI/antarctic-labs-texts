42 U.S.C. § 10002 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:10002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10002
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 107 (Consumer-Patient Radiation Health And Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to—
(1)
provide for the establishment of minimum standards by the Federal Government for the accreditation of education programs for persons who administer radiologic procedures and for the certification of such persons; and
(2)
insure that medical and dental radiologic procedures are consistent with rigorous safety precautions and standards.
