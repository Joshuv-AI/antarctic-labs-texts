2 U.S.C. § 196 — Senate resolutions for investigations; limit of cost
Citation: us:usc:2:196
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section196
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 6 (Congressional And Committee Procedure; Investigations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Senate resolutions providing for inquiries and investigations shall contain a limit of cost of such investigation, which limit shall not be exceeded except by vote of the Senate authorizing additional amounts.
