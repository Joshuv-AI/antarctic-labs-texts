1 C.F.R. § 21.40 — General requirements: Authority citations.
Citation: us:cfr:1:21.40
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-21.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 21 (Preparation Of Documents Subject To Codification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each section in a document subject to codification must include, or be covered by, a complete citation of the authority under which the section is issued, including—
(a) General or specific authority delegated by statute; and
(b) Executive delegations, if any, necessary to link the statutory authority to the issuing agency.
