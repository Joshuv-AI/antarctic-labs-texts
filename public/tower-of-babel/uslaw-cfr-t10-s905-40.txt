10 C.F.R. § 905.40 — Technical assistance.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 905 (Energy Planning And Management Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Western shall establish a program that provides technical assistance to customers to conduct integrated resource planning, implement applicable IRPs and small customer plans, and otherwise comply with the requirements of these regulations.
