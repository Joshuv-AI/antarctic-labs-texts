40 C.F.R. § 463.3 — Monitoring and reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:463.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-463.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 463 (Plastics Molding And Forming Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The “monthly average” regulatory values shall be the basis for the monthly average effluent limitations guidelines and standards in direct discharge permits. Compliance with the monthly average effluent limitations guidelines and standards is required regardless of the number of samples analyzed and averaged.
