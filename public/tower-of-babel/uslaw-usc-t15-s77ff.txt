15 U.S.C. § 77ff — Accounts and annual balance sheet of Corporation; audits
Citation: us:usc:15:77ff
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77ff
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter II (Foreign Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The board of directors shall cause accounts to be kept of all matters relating to or connected with the transactions and business of the Corporation, and cause a general account and balance sheet of the Corporation to be made out in each year, and cause all accounts to be audited by one or more auditors who shall examine the same and report thereon to the board of directors.
