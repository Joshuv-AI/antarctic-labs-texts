Spectroscopy and Radiative Decays of Ω c ​ c ​ c \Omega_{ccc} and Ω b ​ b ​ b \Omega_{bbb} Baryons in a Quark–Diquark Model
Chaitanya Anil Bokade, Bhaghyesh

Abstract
We present a comprehensive study of the spectra and radiative decays of the triply heavy baryons Ω c ​ c ​ c \Omega_{ccc} and Ω b ​ b ​ b \Omega_{bbb} within a quark–diquark framework using a relativistic screened potential model. The analysis is carried out by solving a relativized Hamiltonian for a two–body bound system: the diquark masses are first determined, after which each baryon is treated as a composite of the diquark and the third quark. Employing the obtained wave functions, we calculate electromagnetic transitions using the E1 and M1 operators. We report complete N d ​ L d ​ n q ​ l q N_{d}L_{d}n_{q}l_{q} spectra together with E1/M1 decay widths for radially and orbitally excited states, and systematically compare our results with those from other theoretical approaches.

III
Radiative Transition

Beyond the mass spectra, examining the decay behavior of heavy baryons is essential, particularly for aiding their identification in experimental observations. Due to the limited phase space available for hadronic decays, electromagnetic transitions are expected to significantly contribute to the decay mechanisms of low-lying heavy baryon states. V
Conclusion

Using a relativistic screened potential in the quark–diquark picture, we have analyzed the low–lying as well as radially and orbitally excited spectra of
\Omega_{ccc}
and
\Omega_{bbb}
baryons and computed their leading E1/M1 radiative decay widths. Our predicted ground–state masses,
M(\Omega_{ccc})=4.660
Gev and
M(\Omega_{ccc})=14.200
GeV, are consistent with other model estimates. The
S
-,
P
-, and
D
-wave states with internal diquark excitations
(L_{d})
are consistently found to lower mass comapred to the excitations dominated by quark–diquark orbital motion
(L_{q})
, highlighting the phenomenological importance of explicit diquark dynamics in triply heavy systems. This hierarchy is visible in our level schemes and in the comparative plots with other theoretical approaches. The radiative decay analysis further reveals several experimentally relevant features. Transitions that convert
Sp\rightarrow Ps
or
Sd\rightarrow Ds
are among the most prominent channels, whereas pure spin–flip transitions within the same radial level carry the smallest widths. In addition, the decay widths of
\Omega_{bbb}
are suppressed by roughly three orders of magnitude relative to
\Omega_{ccc}
, a direct consequence of the smaller magnetic moment of the
b
quark. Direct E1/M1 transition to the
1S1s(3/2^{+})
ground state are suppressed, implying that higher multi-pole processes could provide valuable insight into the internal structure and charge distribution of these baryons. Our results provide a comprehensive and coherent picture of the spectra and radiative properties of triply heavy baryons, offering a useful reference for future experimental searches.
