15 U.S.C. § 720i — Sense of Congress concerning use of steel manufactured in North America and negotiation of a project labor agreement
Citation: us:usc:15:720i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section720i
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15D (Alaska Natural Gas Pipeline). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that—
(1)
an Alaska natural gas transportation project would provide significant economic benefits to the United States and Canada; and
(2)
to maximize those benefits, the sponsors of the Alaska natural gas transportation project should make every effort to—
(A)
use steel that is manufactured in North America; and
(B)
negotiate a project labor agreement to expedite construction of the pipeline.
