30 C.F.R. § 7.30 — Post-approval product audit.
Citation: us:cfr:30:7.30
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-7.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 7 (Testing By Applicant Or Third Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request by MSHA but no more than once a year except for cause, the approval-holder shall supply to MSHA at no cost up to fifty feet of each approved design of brattice cloth and ventilation tubing for audit.
