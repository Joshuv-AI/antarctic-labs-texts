7 C.F.R. § 1709.9 — Grant awards and advance of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1709.9
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1709.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1709 (Assistance To High Energy Cost Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grantee must execute a grant agreement that is acceptable to the Agency. The grantee must sign and return the grant agreement to the Agency, within the time specified, before any grant funds will be advanced.
