25 C.F.R. § 1000.335 — What are the Secretary's responsibilities upon a decision not to award a planning or negotiation grant?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.335
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary must communicate in writing the reasons for denying a planning or negotiation grant, and offer the Tribe/Consortium any technical assistance that might make an award possible.
