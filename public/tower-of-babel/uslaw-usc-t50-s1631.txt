50 U.S.C. § 1631 — Declaration of national emergency by Executive order; authority; publication in Federal Register; transmittal to Congress
Citation: us:usc:50:1631
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1631
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 34 (National Emergencies); Subchapter III (Exercise Of Emergency Powers And Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the President declares a national emergency, no powers or authorities made available by statute for use in the event of an emergency shall be exercised unless and until the President specifies the provisions of law under which he proposes that he, or other officers will act. Such specification may be made either in the declaration of a national emergency, or by one or more contemporaneous or subsequent Executive orders published in the Federal Register and transmitted to the Congress.
