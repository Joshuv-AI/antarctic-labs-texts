47 C.F.R. § 80.61 — Commission inspection of stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.61
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All stations and required station records must be made available for inspection by authorized representatives of the Commission.
