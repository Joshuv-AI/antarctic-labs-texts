48 C.F.R. § 613.302-5-70 — 613.302-5-70 DOSAR clauses.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 613 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the appropriate FAR clauses, each purchase order shall incorporate all DOSAR clauses required for or applicable to the acquisition. The DOSAR clauses may be incorporated by reference.
