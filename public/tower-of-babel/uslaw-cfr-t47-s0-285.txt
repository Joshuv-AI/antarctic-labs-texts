47 C.F.R. § 0.285 — Record of actions taken.
Citation: us:cfr:47:0.285
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-0.285
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 0 (Commission Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The history card, the station file, and other appropriate files are designated to be the official records of action taken by the Chief of the Media Bureau. The official records of action are maintained by the Reference Information Center.
