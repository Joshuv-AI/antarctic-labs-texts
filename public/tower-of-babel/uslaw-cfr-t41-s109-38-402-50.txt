41 C.F.R. § 109-38.402-50 — -38.402-50 Prompt disposal of replaced motor vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-38.402-50
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-38.402-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-38 (38—Motor Equipment Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A replaced motor vehicle shall be removed from service and disposed of prior to or as soon as practicable after delivery of the replacement motor vehicle to avoid concurrent operation of both motor vehicles.
