33 C.F.R. § 165.10 — Regulated navigation areas.
Citation: us:cfr:33:165.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-165.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 165 (Regulated Navigation Areas And Limited Access Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A regulated navigation area is a water area within a defined boundary for which regulations for vessels navigating within the area have been established under this part.
