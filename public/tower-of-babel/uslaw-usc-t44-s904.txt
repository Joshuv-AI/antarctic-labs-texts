44 U.S.C. § 904 — Congressional Record: maps; diagrams; illustrations
Citation: us:usc:44:904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section904
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 9 (Congressional Record). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Maps, diagrams, or illustrations may not be inserted in the Record without the approval of the Joint Committee on Printing.
