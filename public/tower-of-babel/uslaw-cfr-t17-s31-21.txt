17 C.F.R. § 31.21 — Leverage contracts entered into prior to April 13, 1984; subsequent transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:17:31.21
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-31.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 31 (Leverage Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in these regulations shall be construed to affect any lawful activities that occurred prior to April 13, 1984. All leverage contracts offered or entered into on or after April 13, 1984 shall be subject to the terms and conditions of these regulations.
