47 C.F.R. § 90.19 — Nationwide Public Safety Broadband Network.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.19
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to the Middle Class Tax Relief and Job Creation Act of 2012, Public Law 112-96, 126 Stat. 156 (2012), the 758-769 MHz and 788-799 MHz bands are allocated for use by the First Responder Network Authority to deploy a nationwide public safety broadband network as prescribed by statute.
