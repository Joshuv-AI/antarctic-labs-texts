7 C.F.R. § 97.176 — Fees payable in advance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:97.176
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-97.176
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 97 (Plant Variety And Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fees and charges shall be paid at the time of making application or at the time of submitting a request for any action by the Office for which a fee or charge is payable and established in this part.
