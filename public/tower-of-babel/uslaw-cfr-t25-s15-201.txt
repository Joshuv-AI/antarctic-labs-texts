25 C.F.R. § 15.201 — What will the agency do with the documents that I provide?
Citation: us:cfr:25:15.201
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-15.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 15 (Probate Of Indian Estates, Except For Members Of The Osage Nation And The Five Civilized Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After we receive notice of the death of a person owning trust or restricted land or trust personalty, we will examine the documents provided under §§ 15.104 and 15.105, and other documents and information provided to us to prepare a complete probate file. We may consult with you and other individuals or entities to obtain additional information to complete the probate file. Then we will transfer the probate file to OHA.
