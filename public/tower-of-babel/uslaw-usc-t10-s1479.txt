10 U.S.C. § 1479 — Death gratuity: delegation of determinations, payments
Citation: us:usc:10:1479
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1479
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 75 (Deceased Personnel); Subchapter II (Death Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of making immediate payments under section 1475 of this title, the Secretary concerned shall—
(1)
authorize the commanding officer of a territorial command, installation, or district in which a survivor of a person covered by that section is residing to determine the beneficiary eligible for the death gratuity; and
(2)
authorize a disbursing or certifying official of each of those commands, installations, or districts to make the payments to the beneficiary, or certify the payments due them, as the case may be.
