41 C.F.R. § 105-10.102 — -10.102 Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-10 (10—Nondiscrimination In Programs Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate the provisions of title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (hereinafter referred to as the “Act”) to the end that no person in the United States shall, on the ground of race, color, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to discrimination under any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance from GSA.
