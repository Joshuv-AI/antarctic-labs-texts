28 C.F.R. § 600.10 — No creation of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:28:600.10
Creator: Offices Of Independent Counsel, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-600.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 600 (General Powers Of Special Counsel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are not intended to, do not, and may not be relied upon to create any rights, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or equity, by any person or entity, in any matter, civil, criminal, or administrative.
