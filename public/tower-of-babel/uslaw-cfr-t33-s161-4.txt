33 C.F.R. § 161.4 — Requirement to carry the rules.
Citation: us:cfr:33:161.4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-161.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 161 (Vessel Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each VTS User shall carry on board and maintain for ready reference a copy of these rules.
