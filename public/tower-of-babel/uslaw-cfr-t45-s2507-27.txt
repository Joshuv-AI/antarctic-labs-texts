45 C.F.R. § 2507.27 — Rights and services qualified by the FOIA statute.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2507.27
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2507.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2507 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part may be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
