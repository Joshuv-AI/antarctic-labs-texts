33 C.F.R. § 49.10-15 — -15 Final accounting by trustee.
Citation: us:cfr:33:49.10-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-49.10-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 49 (Payment Of Amounts Due Mentally Incompetent Coast Guard Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The trustee or trustees, when payments, hereunder are terminated, shall file a final account with the said Commandant. Thereupon, the trustee or trustees will be discharged and the surety released. In event of death or disability of the trustee, the final accounting will be filed by his legal representative.
