42 U.S.C. § 1437m — Payment of non-Federal share
Citation: us:usc:42:1437m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1437m
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8 (Low-Income Housing); Subchapter I (General Program Of Assisted Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any of the following may be used as the non-Federal share required in connection with activities undertaken under Federal grant-in-aid programs which provide social, educational, employment, and other services to the tenants in a project assisted under this chapter, other than under section 1437f of this title;
(1)
annual contributions under this chapter for operation of the project; or
(2)
rental or use-value of buildings or facilities paid for, in whole or in part, from development, modernization, or operation cost financed under this chapter.
