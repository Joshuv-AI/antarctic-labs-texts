25 C.F.R. § 103.9 — Who applies to BIA under the Program?
Citation: us:cfr:25:103.9
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-103.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 103 (Loan Guaranty, Insurance, And Interest Subsidy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The lender is responsible for determining whether it will require a BIA guaranty or insurance coverage, based upon the loan application it receives from an eligible borrower. If the lender requires a BIA guaranty or insurance coverage, the lender is responsible for completing and submitting a guaranty application or complying with a loan insurance agreement under the Program.
