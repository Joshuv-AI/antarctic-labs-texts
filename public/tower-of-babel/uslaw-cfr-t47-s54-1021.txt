47 C.F.R. § 54.1021 — Record retention for the 5G Fund.
Citation: us:cfr:47:54.1021
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-54.1021
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 54 (Universal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient authorized to receive 5G Fund support and its agents are required to retain any documentation prepared for, or in connection with, the award of the 5G Fund support for a period of not less than ten (10) years after the date on which the recipient receives its final disbursement of 5G Fund support.
