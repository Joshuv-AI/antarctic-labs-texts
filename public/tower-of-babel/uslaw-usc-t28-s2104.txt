28 U.S.C. § 2104 — Reviews of State court decisions
Citation: us:usc:28:2104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2104
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 133 (Review—Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A review by the Supreme Court of a judgment or decree of a State court shall be conducted in the same manner and under the same regulations, and shall have the same effect, as if the judgment or decree reviewed had been rendered in a court of the United States.
