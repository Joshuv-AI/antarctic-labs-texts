25 C.F.R. § 307.10 — Period of license.
Citation: us:cfr:25:307.10
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-307.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 307 (Navajo All-Wool Woven Fabrics; Use Of Government Certificate Of Genuineness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each license shall be in effect from the date of execution thereof and until 1 year thereafter, unless sooner surrendered or canceled in accordance with the foregoing provisions.
