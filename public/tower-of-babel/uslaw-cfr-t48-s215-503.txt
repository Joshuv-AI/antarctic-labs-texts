48 C.F.R. § 215.503 — 215.503 Notifications to unsuccessful offerors.
Citation: us:cfr:48:215.503
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-215.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 215 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Government exercises the authority provided in 239.7305(d), the notifications to unsuccessful offerors, either preaward or postaward, shall not reveal any information that is determined to be withheld from disclosure in accordance with 10 U.S.C. 3252 (see subpart 239.73).
