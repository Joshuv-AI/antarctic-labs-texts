29 C.F.R. § 1206.3 — Age of authorization cards.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1206.3
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1206.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1206 (Handling Representation Disputes Under The Railway Labor Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorizations must be signed and dated in the employee's own handwriting or witnessed mark. No authorizations will be accepted by the National Mediation Board in any employee representation dispute which bear a date prior to one year before the date of the application for the investigation of such dispute.
