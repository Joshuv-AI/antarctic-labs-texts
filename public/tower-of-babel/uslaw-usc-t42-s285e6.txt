42 U.S.C. § 285e–6 — Dissemination of research results
Citation: us:usc:42:285e–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285e–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 5 (National Institute On Aging). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Institute shall disseminate the results of research conducted under section 285e–5 of this title and this section to appropriate professional entities and to the public.
