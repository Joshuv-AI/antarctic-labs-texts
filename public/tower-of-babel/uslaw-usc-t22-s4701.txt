22 U.S.C. § 4701 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:22:4701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4701
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 57 (United States Scholarship Program For Developing Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish an undergraduate scholarship program designed to bring students of limited financial means from developing countries to the United States for study at United States institutions of higher education.
