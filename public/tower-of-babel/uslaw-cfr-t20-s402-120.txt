20 C.F.R. § 402.120 — The FOIA Exemption 2: Internal personnel rules and practices.
Citation: us:cfr:20:402.120
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-402.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 402 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exemption 2 authorizes our agency to withhold records that are related solely to the internal personnel rules and practices of an agency. For example, we may withhold personnel rules and practices dealing with employee relations or human resources.
