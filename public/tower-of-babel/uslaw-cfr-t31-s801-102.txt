31 C.F.R. § 801.102 — Effect on other law.
Citation: us:cfr:31:801.102
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-801.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 801 (Pilot Program To Review Certain Transactions Involving Foreign Persons And Critical Technologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise indicated, nothing in this part shall be construed as altering or affecting any other authority, process, regulation, investigation, enforcement measure, or review provided by or established under any other provision of federal law, including the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701-1706), or any other authority of the President or the Congress under the Constitution of the United States.
