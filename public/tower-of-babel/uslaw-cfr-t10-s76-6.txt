10 C.F.R. § 76.6 — Interpretations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:76.6
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-76.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 76 (Certification Of Gaseous Diffusion Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as specifically authorized by the Commission in writing, no interpretation of the meaning of the regulations in this part by any officer or employee of the Commission other than a written interpretation by the General Counsel will be recognized to be binding upon the Commission.
