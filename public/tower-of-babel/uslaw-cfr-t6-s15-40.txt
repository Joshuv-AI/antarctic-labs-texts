6 C.F.R. § 15.40 — Employment.
Citation: us:cfr:6:15.40
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-15.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 15 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Department Of Homeland Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified individual with a disability shall, on the basis of that disability, be subjected to discrimination in employment under any program or activity conducted by the Department. The definitions, requirements and procedures of section 501 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C. 791), as established by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission in 29 CFR part 1614, shall apply to employment of Federally conducted programs or activities.
