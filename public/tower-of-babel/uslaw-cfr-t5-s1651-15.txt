5 C.F.R. § 1651.15 — Claims referred to the Board.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1651.15
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1651.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1651 (Death Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Contested claims. Any challenge to a proposed death benefit payment must be filed in writing with the TSP record keeper before payment. All contested claims will be referred to the Board. The Board may also consider issues on its own.
(b) Payment deferred. No payment will be made until the Board has resolved the claim.
