42 C.F.R. § 136a.51 — a.51 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136a.51
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136a.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136a (PART 136a—INDIAN HEALTH). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart is applicable to the use of Federal funds in providing health services to Indians in accordance with the provisions of subparts A, B, C, H, I and J of this part.
