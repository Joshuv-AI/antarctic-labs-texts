43 C.F.R. § 8365.1 — Public lands—general.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8365.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8365.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8360 (Visitor Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subsection shall apply to use and occupancy of all public lands under the jurisdiction of the Bureau of Land Management. Additional rules for developed sites and areas are found in § 8365.2 of this title.
