46 U.S.C. § 56501 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:46:56501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section56501
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Control Of Merchant Marine Capabilities); Chapter 565 (Essential Vessels Affected By Neutrality Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “essential vessel” means a vessel that is—
(1)
(A)
security for a mortgage indebtedness to the United States Government; or
(B)
constructed under this subtitle or required by a contract under this subtitle to be operated on a certain essential foreign trade route; and
(2)
necessary in the interests of commerce and national defense to be maintained in condition for prompt use.
