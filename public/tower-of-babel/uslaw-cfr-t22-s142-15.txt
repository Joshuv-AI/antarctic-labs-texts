22 C.F.R. § 142.15 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:22:142.15
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-142.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 142 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified handicapped person shall, because a recipient's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by handicapped persons, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity to which the part applies.
