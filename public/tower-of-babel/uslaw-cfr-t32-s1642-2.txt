32 C.F.R. § 1642.2 — The claim for classification in Class 3-A.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1642.2
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1642.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1642 (Classification Of Registrants Deferred Because Of Hardship To Dependents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim for classification in Class 3-A must be made by the registrant in writing. Prior to the consideration of the claim, the registrant shall submit supporting documentation, such documents being placed in his file folder.
