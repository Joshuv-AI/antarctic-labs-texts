48 C.F.R. § 514.203-1 — 514.203-1 Transmittal to prospective bidders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:514.203-1
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-514.203-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 514 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prospective bidders, as used in FAR 14.203-1, include both the following:
(a) The incumbent contractor, except when its written response to the notice of contract action under FAR subpart 5.2 states a negative interest.
(b) Bidders that responded to recent solicitations for the same or similar items.
