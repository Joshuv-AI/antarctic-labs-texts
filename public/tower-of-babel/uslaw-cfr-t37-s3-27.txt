37 C.F.R. § 3.27 — Mailing address for submitting documents to be recorded.
Citation: us:cfr:37:3.27
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-3.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 3 (Assignment, Recording And Rights Of Assignee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Documents and cover sheets submitted by mail for recordation should be addressed to Mail Stop Assignment Recordation Services, Director of the United States Patent and Trademark Office, P.O. Box 1450, Alexandria, Virginia 22313-1450, unless they are filed together with new applications.
