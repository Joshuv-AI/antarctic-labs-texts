46 C.F.R. § 503.12 — Effect of nonpublication.
Citation: us:cfr:46:503.12
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-503.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 503 (Public Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except to the extent that a person has actual and timely notice of the terms thereof, no person shall in any manner be required to resort to, or be adversely affected by any matter required to be published in the Federal Register and not so published.
