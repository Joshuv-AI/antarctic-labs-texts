15 U.S.C. § 1295 — “Persons” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:1295
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1295
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 32 (Telecasting Of Professional Sports Contests). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, “persons” means any individual, partnership, corporation, or unincorporated association or any combination or association thereof.
