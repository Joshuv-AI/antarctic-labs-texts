31 U.S.C. § 777 — Annuity increases
Citation: us:usc:31:777
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section777
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter V (Annuities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An annuity payable under this subchapter shall be increased at the same time that, and by the same percent as the percentage by which, annuities are increased under section 8340(b) of title 5.
(b)
An annuity under section 772 of this title may not be more than the basic pay of the Comptroller General. A surviving spouse’s annuity may be increased under this section without regard to any limitation set forth in section 774(e) of this title.
