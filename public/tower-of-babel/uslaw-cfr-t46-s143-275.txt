46 C.F.R. § 143.275 — Bilge pumps or other dewatering capability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:143.275
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-143.275
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 143 (Machinery And Electrical Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There must be an installed or portable bilge pump for emergency dewatering. Any portable pump must have sufficient hose length and pumping capability. All installed bilge piping must have a check/foot valve in each bilge suction that prevents unintended backflooding through bilge piping.
