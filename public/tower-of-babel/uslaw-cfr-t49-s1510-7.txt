49 C.F.R. § 1510.7 — Air transportation advertisements and solicitations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1510.7
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1510.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1510 (Passenger Civil Aviation Security Service Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A direct air carrier and foreign air carrier must identify the security service fee imposed by this part as “September 11th Security Fee” in all its advertisements and solicitations for air transportation.
