48 U.S.C. § 1955 — Audit certification
Citation: us:usc:48:1955
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1955
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 18 (Micronesia, Marshall Islands, And Palau); Subchapter II (Palau); Part B (Implementation Of Compact). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The chief officer of any agency conducting an audit pursuant to paragraph (1) of sections 1902(c) and 1903(m) of this title and section 1931(d)(1)(C) of this title shall certify that audit.
