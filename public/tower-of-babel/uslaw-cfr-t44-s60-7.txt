44 C.F.R. § 60.7 — Revisions of criteria for flood plain management regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:44:60.7
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-60.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 60 (Criteria For Land Management And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

From time to time part 60 may be revised as experience is acquired under the Program and new information becomes available. Communities will be given six months from the effective date of any new regulation to revise their flood plain management regulations to comply with any such changes.
