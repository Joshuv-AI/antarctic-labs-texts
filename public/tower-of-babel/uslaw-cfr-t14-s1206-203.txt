14 C.F.R. § 1206.203 — Incorporation by reference.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1206.203
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1206.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1206 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records reasonably available to the members of the public affected thereby shall be deemed published in the Federal Register when incorporated by reference in material published in the Federal Register (pursuant to the Federal Register regulation on incorporation by reference, 1 CFR Part 51).
