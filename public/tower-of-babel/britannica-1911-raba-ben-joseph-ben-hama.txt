RABA BEN JOSEPH BEN ḤAMA (c. 280–352), Babylonian
rabbi or amora. He is closely associated in his studies with
Abaye. The latter was head of the Academy at Pumbeditha.
Raba founded a new school at Maḥuza, which eventually became
so long as Raba lived the only academy in Babylonia
(Persia). The development of Talmudic Law (or Halakhah)
was much indebted to this rabbi, whose influence in all branches of Jewish learning was supreme. His friendship with the King Shapur II. enabled Raba to secure a relaxation of the oppressive laws enacted against the Jews of Persia.
