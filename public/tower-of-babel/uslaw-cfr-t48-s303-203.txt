48 C.F.R. § 303.203 — 303.203 Reporting suspected violations of the Gratuities clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:303.203
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-303.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 303 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HHS personnel shall report suspected violations of the clause at FAR 52.203-3, Gratuities, to the contracting officer, who will in turn report the matter to the Office of General Counsel (OGC), Ethics Division for disposition.
