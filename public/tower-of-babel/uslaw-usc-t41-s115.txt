41 U.S.C. § 115 — Supplies
Citation: us:usc:41:115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section115
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 1 (Definitions); Subchapter I (Subtitle Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subtitle, the term “supplies” has the same meaning as the terms “item” and “item of supply”.
