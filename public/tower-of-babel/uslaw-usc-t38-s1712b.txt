38 U.S.C. § 1712B — Counseling for former prisoners of war
Citation: us:usc:38:1712B
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1712B
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter II (Hospital, Nursing Home, Or Domiciliary Care And Medical Treatment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may establish a program under which, upon the request of a veteran who is a former prisoner of war, the Secretary, within the limits of Department facilities, furnishes counseling to such veteran to assist such veteran in overcoming the psychological effects of the veteran’s detention or internment as a prisoner of war.
