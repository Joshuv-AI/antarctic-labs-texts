22 U.S.C. § 2682 — Liquidation and disposal of broadcasting facilities
Citation: us:usc:22:2682
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2682
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Secretary finds that the operation of the facilities authorized by sections 2681 to 2683 of this title to be transferred is no longer necessary or desirable, he shall report such fact to Congress with his recommendations for the disposition of such facilities.
