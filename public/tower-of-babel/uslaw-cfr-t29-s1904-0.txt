29 C.F.R. § 1904.0 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1904.0
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1904.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1904 (Recording And Reporting Occupational Injuries And Illnesses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule (part 1904) is to require employers to record and report work-related fatalities, injuries, and illnesses.
