14 C.F.R. § 15.105 — Filing of requests for indemnification.
Citation: us:cfr:14:15.105
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-15.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 15 (Administrative Claims Under Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for indemnification under this part—
(a) May be filed by—
(1) A publisher described in § 15.101 of this part; or
(2) The publisher's duly authorized agent or legal representative;
(b) Shall be filed with the Chief Counsel, Federal Aviation Administration, 800 Independence Avenue SW., Washington, DC 20591; and
(c) Shall state the basis for the publisher's assertion that indemnification under this part is required.
