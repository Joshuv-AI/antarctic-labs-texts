47 C.F.R. § 27.1320 — Notification to white space database administrators.
Citation: us:cfr:47:27.1320
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-27.1320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 27 (Miscellaneous Wireless Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive interference protection, 600 MHz licensees shall notify one of the white space database administrators of the areas where they have commenced operation pursuant to §§ 15.713(j)(10) and 15.715(n) of this chapter.
