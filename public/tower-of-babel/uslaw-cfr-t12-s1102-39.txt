12 C.F.R. § 1102.39 — Duty to cooperate.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1102.39
Creator: Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1102.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1102 (Appraiser Regulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the course of the investigations and proceedings, the ASC (and its staff, with appropriate authorization) must provide parties or persons ample opportunity to work out problems by consent, by settlement, or in some other manner.
