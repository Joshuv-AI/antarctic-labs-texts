43 U.S.C. § 1579 — Fish and wildlife habitat; mitigation of losses
Citation: us:usc:43:1579
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1579
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32A (Colorado River Basin Salinity Control); Subchapter I (Programs Downstream From Imperial Dam). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 1979, and to such extent and in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriate 1 Acts, in order to provide measures determined by the Secretary of the Interior to be appropriated to mitigate loss of fish and wildlife habitat associated with other measures taken under this subchapter:
(a) Appropriation of funds; acquisition and disposal of lands; facilities undertakings; funds restriction for non-Federal facilities
The Secretary is authorized to—
(1)
acquire lands by purchase, eminent domain, or exchange;
(2)
dispose of land, facilities, and equipment;
(3)
construct, operate, maintain, and make replacements of facilities: Provided, however, That no funds will be provided for operation, maintenance, or replacement of non-Federal facilities.
(b) Nonreimbursable costs
All costs authorized by this section are nonreimbursable.
