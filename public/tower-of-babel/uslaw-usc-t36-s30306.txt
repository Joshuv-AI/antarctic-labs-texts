36 U.S.C. § 30306 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:30306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section30306
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 303 (Blinded Veterans Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its authorized regional groups and other local subdivisions have the exclusive right to use the name “Blinded Veterans Association” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
