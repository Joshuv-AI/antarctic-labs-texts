20 C.F.R. § 220.48 — If the claimant fails to submit medical or other evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:20:220.48
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-220.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 220 (Determining Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may request a claimant to submit medical or other evidence. If the claimant does not submit that evidence, the Board will make a decision on other evidence which is either already available in the claimant's case or which the Board may develop from other sources, including reports of consultative examinations.
