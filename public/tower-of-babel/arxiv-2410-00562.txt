Forbidden Minor Characterization of 3-fold-3-splitting of Binary Gammoid


Abstract
. The r-fold-n-point-splitting operation is an important operation in Graph Theory defined by Slater [ 15 ] . Later, Ghafari [ 6 ] extended 3-fold-n-point-splitting operation in binary matroids and obtained the result for Eulerian matroids whose 3-fold is Eulerian. In this paper, we give another approach to extend 3-fold-3-point-splitting in binary matroids in terms of splitting and haracterize binary gammoid whose 3-fold-3-point spitting is binary gammoid.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
S
be a binary gammoid such that
S_{Y}
has
M(F)
a minor and

|Y|\geq 2

S
has a minor

i)
R_{Y}\cong M(F)
.

ii)
R_{Y}/Y^{\prime}\cong M(F)
, where

.

iii)
R
has a minor
M(F)
.

iv)
R
is one element extension of some minimal minor in the collection
\mathcal{GF}_{k-1}
.

Proof.

Let
S
be a binary gammoid such that
S_{Y}
has a minor
M(F)
. Thus
S_{Y}\backslash Y_{1}/Y_{2}\cong M(F)
for some subsets
Y_{1}
and
Y_{2}
of
E(S)
. Let
Y_{i}^{\prime}=Y\cap Y_{i}
and
Y_{i}^{\prime\prime}=Y_{i}-Y_{i}^{\prime}
, for
i=1,2
, thus
Y_{i}^{\prime}\subseteq Y
and
Y_{i}=Y_{i}^{\prime}\cup Y_{i}^{\prime\prime}
for

. Then,
S_{Y}\backslash Y_{1}/Y_{2}=S_{Y}\backslash(Y_{1}^{\prime}\cup Y_{1}^{\prime\prime})/(Y_{2}^{\prime}\cup Y_{2}^{\prime\prime})=[(S\backslash Y_{1}^{\prime\prime}/Y_{2}^{\prime\prime})_{Y}]\backslash Y_{1}^{\prime}/Y_{2}^{\prime}
. Let
R=S\backslash Y_{1}^{\prime\prime}/Y_{2}^{\prime\prime}
, hence
R
is a minor of
S
. Thus
R_{Y}\backslash Y_{1}^{\prime}/Y_{2}^{\prime}\cong M(F)
.

If
Y_{1}^{\prime}=\phi
and
Y_{2}^{\prime}=\phi
, then (i) holds.

If
Y_{1}^{\prime}=\phi
and
Y_{2}^{\prime}\neq\phi
, then (ii) holds.

If

, as

,

. If

, then

and

. Thus

. Hence

has a minor
M(F)
, (iii) holds. If

, let

,

and

is single element extension of

∎
