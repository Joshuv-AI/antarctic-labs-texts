27 C.F.R. § 11.3 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:27:11.3
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-11.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 11 (Consignment Sales). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The regulations in this part apply to transactions between industry members and trade buyers.
(b) Transactions involving State agencies. The regulations in this part apply to transactions involving State agencies operating as retailers or wholesalers.
