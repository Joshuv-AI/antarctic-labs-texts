17 C.F.R. § 148.8 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:17:148.8
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-148.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 148 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Covered Adjudicatory Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over another agency of the United States that participates in an adjudicatory proceeding before the Commission and takes a postion that is not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
