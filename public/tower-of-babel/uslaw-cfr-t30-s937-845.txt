30 C.F.R. § 937.845 — Civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:30:937.845
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-937.845
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 937 (Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 845 of this chapter, Civil Penalties, shall apply when civil penalties are assessed for violations on surface mining and reclamation operations.
