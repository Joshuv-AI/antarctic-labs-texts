10 C.F.R. § 490.801 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:490.801
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-490.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 490 (Alternative Fuel Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 514 of the Act (42 U.S.C. 13263a) which permits States and alternative fuel providers to petition for alternative compliance waivers from the alternative fueled vehicle acquisition requirements in subparts C and D of this part, respectively.
