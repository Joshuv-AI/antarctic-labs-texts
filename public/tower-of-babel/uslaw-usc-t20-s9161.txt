20 U.S.C. § 9161 — Services for Native Americans
Citation: us:usc:20:9161
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9161
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 72 (Museum And Library Services); Subchapter II (Library Services And Technology); Part 3 (Administrative Provisions); Subpart b (Federal Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

From amounts reserved under section 9131(a)(1)(A) of this title for any fiscal year the Director shall award grants to Indian tribes and to organizations that primarily serve and represent Native Hawaiians (as the term is defined in section 7517 of this title) to enable such tribes and organizations to carry out the activities described in section 9141 of this title.
