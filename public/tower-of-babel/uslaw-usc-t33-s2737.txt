33 U.S.C. § 2737 — Limitation
Citation: us:usc:33:2737
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2737
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 40 (Oil Pollution); Subchapter II (Prince William Sound Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other law, tank vessels that have spilled more than 1,000,000 gallons of oil into the marine environment after March 22, 1989, are prohibited from operating on the navigable waters of Prince William Sound, Alaska.
