5 C.F.R. § 451.202 — Payment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:451.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-451.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 451 (Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A Presidential award is paid by the agency(ies) primarily benefiting from the employee contribution.
(b) A Presidential award may be in addition to an agency award under subpart A of this part.
