31 U.S.C. § 6306 — Authority to vest title in tangible personal property for research
Citation: us:usc:31:6306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6306
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 63 (Using Procurement Contracts And Grant And Cooperative Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of an executive agency may vest title in tangible personal property in a nonprofit institution of higher education or in a nonprofit organization whose primary purpose is conducting scientific research—
(1)
when the property is bought with amounts provided under a procurement contract, grant agreement, or cooperative agreement with the institution or organization to conduct basic or applied scientific research;
(2)
when the head of the agency decides the vesting furthers the objectives of the agency;
(3)
without further obligation to the United States Government; and
(4)
under conditions the head of the agency considers appropriate.
