14 U.S.C. § 2510 — Sea service letters

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2510
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 25 (Personnel; General Provisions); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
The Secretary shall provide a sea service letter to a member or former member of the Coast Guard who—
(1)
accumulated sea service on a vessel of the Armed Forces (as such term is defined in section 527(e)); and
(2)
requests such letter.
(b) Deadline.—
Not later than 30 days after receiving a request for a sea service letter from a member or former member of the Coast Guard under subsection (a), the Secretary shall provide such letter to such member or former member if such member or former member satisfies the requirement under subsection (a)(1).
