15 U.S.C. § 1095 — Registration on principal register not precluded
Citation: us:usc:15:1095
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1095
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter II (The Supplemental Register). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Registration of a mark on the supplemental register, or under the Act of March 19, 1920, shall not preclude registration by the registrant on the principal register established by this chapter. Registration of a mark on the supplemental register shall not constitute an admission that the mark has not acquired distinctiveness.
