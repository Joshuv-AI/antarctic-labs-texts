16 U.S.C. § 2611 — Purposes

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2611
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 46 (Public Utility Regulatory Policies); Subchapter I (Retail Regulatory Policies For Electric Utilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are to encourage—
(1)
conservation of energy supplied by electric utilities;
(2)
the optimization of the efficiency of use of facilities and resources by electric utilities; and
(3)
equitable rates to electric consumers.
