27 C.F.R. § 18.18 — Execution under penalties of perjury.
Citation: us:cfr:27:18.18
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-18.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 18 (Production Of Volatile Fruit-Flavor Concentrate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a form or other document called for under this part is required to be executed under penalties of perjury, it will be so executed, as defined in § 18.11, and signed by an authorized person.
