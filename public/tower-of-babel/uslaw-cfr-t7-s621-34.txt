7 C.F.R. § 621.34 — Recipient responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:621.34
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-621.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 621 (River Basin Investigations And Surveys). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating local and State governments work with USDA and the Department of the Army representatives in developing objectives, collecting data, analyzing problems, planning and formulating proposals, and considering financial plans. Active public participation is solicited in the planning process through means such as questionnaires, public meetings, citizen advisory boards, and technical committees.
