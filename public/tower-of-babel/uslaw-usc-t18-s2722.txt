18 U.S.C. § 2722 — Additional unlawful acts

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 123 (Prohibition On Release And Use Of Certain Personal Information From State Motor Vehicle Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Procurement for Unlawful Purpose.—
It shall be unlawful for any person knowingly to obtain or disclose personal information, from a motor vehicle record, for any use not permitted under section 2721(b) of this title.
(b) False Representation.—
It shall be unlawful for any person to make false representation to obtain any personal information from an individual’s motor vehicle record.
