Classification for smooth manifolds looking like ℂ ​ ℙ 3 × S 7 \mathbb{CP}^{3}\times S^{7}
Wen Shen

Abstract
. In this paper, we classify simply connected closed smooth 13 13 -dimensional manifolds whose cohomology ring is isomorphic to that of ℂ ​ ℙ 3 × S 7 \mathbb{CP}^{3}\times S^{7} , up to diffeomorphism, homeomorphism, and homotopy equivalence. Furthermore, if such a manifold satisfies certain conditions, either itself or its connected sum with an exotic 13 13 -sphere Σ 13 \Sigma^{13} admits a Riemannian metric of non-negative sectional curvature. As an additional application of our classification, we classify the diagonal S 1 S^{1} -actions on S 7 × S 7 S^{7}\times S^{7} .
