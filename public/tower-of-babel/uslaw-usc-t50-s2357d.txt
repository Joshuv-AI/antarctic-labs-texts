50 U.S.C. § 2357d — Administrative support
Citation: us:usc:50:2357d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2357d
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 40 (Defense Against Weapons Of Mass Destruction); Subchapter IV–A (Nonproliferation Assistance Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All United States departments and agencies shall provide, to the extent permitted by law, such information and assistance as may be requested by the coordination mechanism established pursuant to section 2357b of this title, in carrying out its functions and activities under this subchapter.
