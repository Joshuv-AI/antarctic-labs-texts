29 C.F.R. § 1918.34 — Other decks.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1918.34
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1918.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1918 (Safety And Health Regulations For Longshoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Cargo shall not be worked on decks that were not designed to support the load being worked.
(b) Grated decks shall be properly placed, supported, maintained and designed to support employees.
