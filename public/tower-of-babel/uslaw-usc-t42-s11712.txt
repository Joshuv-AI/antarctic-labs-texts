42 U.S.C. § 11712 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:42:11712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11712
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 122 (Native Hawaiian Health Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to restrict the authority of the State of Hawaii to license health practitioners.
