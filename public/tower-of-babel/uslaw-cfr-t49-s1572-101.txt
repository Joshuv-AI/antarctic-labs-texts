49 C.F.R. § 1572.101 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1572.101
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1572.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1572 (Credentialing And Security Threat Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to applicants who hold or are applying to obtain or renew an HME or TWIC, or transfer an HME. Applicants for an HME also are subject to safety requirements issued by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration under 49 CFR part 383 and by the State issuing the HME, including additional immigration status and criminal history standards.
